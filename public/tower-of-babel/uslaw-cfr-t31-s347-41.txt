31 C.F.R. § 347.41 — Additional requirements; bond of indemnity.
Citation: us:cfr:31:347.41
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-347.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 347 (Regulations Governing Retirement Savings Bonds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commissioner of the Fiscal Service may require:
(a) Such additional evidence to support a requested action as he or she may consider necessary or advisable; or
(b) A bond of indemnity, with or without surety, in any case in which he or she may consider such a bond necessary for the protection of the interests of the United States.
