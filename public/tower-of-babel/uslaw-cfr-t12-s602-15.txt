12 C.F.R. § 602.15 — Interest on unpaid fees.
Citation: us:cfr:12:602.15
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-602.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 602 (Releasing Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you fail to pay fees on time, FCA may charge you interest starting on the 31st calendar day following the date we bill you. We will charge you interest at the rate allowed by law (31 U.S.C. 3717) on the billing date.
