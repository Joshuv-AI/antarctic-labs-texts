22 C.F.R. § 12.1 — No cognizance taken of complaint.
Citation: us:cfr:22:12.1
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-12.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 12 (Complaints Against Employees By Alleged Creditors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department of State will take no cognizance of a complaint against an employee by an alleged creditor, so far as the complainant is concerned, beyond acknowledging receipt of his communication.
