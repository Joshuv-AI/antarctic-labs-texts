5 C.F.R. § 890.505 — Recurring premium payments to carriers.
Citation: us:cfr:5:890.505
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-890.505
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 890 (Federal Employees Health Benefits Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The procedures for payment of premiums, contingency reserve, and interest distribution to FEHB Program carriers shall be those contained in 48 CFR subpart 1632.170.
