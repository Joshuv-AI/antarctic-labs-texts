31 C.F.R. § 357.43 — Liability for transfers to and from Legacy Treasury Direct ®.
Citation: us:cfr:31:357.43
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-357.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 357 (Regulations Governing Book-Entry Treasury Bonds, Notes And Bills Held In Treasury/Reserve Automated Debt Entry System (Trades) And Legacy Treasury Direct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A depository institution or other entity that transfers to, or receives, a security from Legacy Treasury Direct is deemed to be acting as agent for its customer and agrees thereby to indemnify the United States and the Federal Reserve Banks for any claim, liability, or loss resulting from the transaction.
