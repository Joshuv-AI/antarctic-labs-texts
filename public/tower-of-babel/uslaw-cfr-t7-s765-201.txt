7 C.F.R. § 765.201 — General policy.
Citation: us:cfr:7:765.201
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 765 (Direct Loan Servicing—Regular). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All Agency servicing actions regarding preservation and protection of Agency security will be consistent with the covenants and agreements contained in all loan agreements and security instruments.
