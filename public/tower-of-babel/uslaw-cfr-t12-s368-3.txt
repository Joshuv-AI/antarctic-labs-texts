12 C.F.R. § 368.3 — Business conduct.
Citation: us:cfr:12:368.3
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-368.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 368 (Government Securities Sales Practices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A bank that is a government securities broker or dealer shall observe high standards of commercial honor and just and equitable principles of trade in the conduct of its business as a government securities broker or dealer.
