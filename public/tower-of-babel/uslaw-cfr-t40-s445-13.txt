40 C.F.R. § 445.13 — Effluent limitations representing the degree of effluent reduction attainable by the application of best available technology economically achievable (BAT).
Citation: us:cfr:40:445.13
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-445.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 445 (Landfills Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in 40 CFR 125.30 through 125.32, any existing point source subject to this subpart must achieve the following effluent limitations which represent the application of BAT: Limitations for ammonia (as N), a-terpineol, aniline, benzoic acid, naphthalene, p-cresol, phenol, pyridine, arsenic, chromium and zinc are the same as the corresponding limitations specified in § 445.11.
