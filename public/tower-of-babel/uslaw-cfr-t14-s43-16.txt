14 C.F.R. § 43.16 — Airworthiness limitations.
Citation: us:cfr:14:43.16
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-43.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 43 (Maintenance, Preventive Maintenance, Rebuilding, And Alteration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each person performing an inspection or other maintenance specified in an Airworthiness Limitations section of a manufacturer's maintenance manual or Instructions for Continued Airworthiness shall perform the inspection or other maintenance in accordance with that section, or in accordance with operations specifications approved by the Administrator under part 121 or 135, or an inspection program approved under § 91.409(e).
