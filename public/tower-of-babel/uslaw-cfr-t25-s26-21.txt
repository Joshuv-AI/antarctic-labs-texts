25 C.F.R. § 26.21 — Can this program be combined with other similar programs for maximum benefit?
Citation: us:cfr:25:26.21
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-26.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 26 (Job Placement And Training Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, combining this program with other programs is encouraged, to the extent that laws governing program services permit partnering with similar programs and resources.
