46 C.F.R. § 173.075 — Subdivision requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:46:173.075
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-173.075
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 173 (Special Rules Pertaining To Vessel Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each oceanographic vessel must comply with the subdivision requirements in §§ 171.070, 171.072, and 171.073 of this subchapter as if it were a passenger vessel carrying 400 or less passengers.
(b) Each vessel must have a collision bulkhead.
