39 U.S.C. § 3213 — Seeds and reports from Department of Agriculture
Citation: us:usc:39:3213
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title39-section3213
Context: United States Code, Title 39 (Postal Service); Part IV (Mail Matter); Chapter 32 (Penalty And Franked Mail). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Seeds and agricultural reports emanating from the Department of Agriculture may be mailed—
(1)
as penalty mail by the Secretary of Agriculture; and
(2)
during the 90-day period immediately following the expiration of their terms of office, as franked mail by Members of Congress.
