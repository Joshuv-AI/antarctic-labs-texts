26 U.S.C. § 27 — Taxes of foreign countries and possessions of the United States
Citation: us:usc:26:27
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section27
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 1 (Normal Taxes And Surtaxes); Subchapter A (Determination Of Tax Liability); Part IV (Credits Against Tax); Subpart B (Other Credits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The amount of taxes imposed by foreign countries and possessions of the United States shall be allowed as a credit against the tax imposed by this chapter to the extent provided in section 901 1
