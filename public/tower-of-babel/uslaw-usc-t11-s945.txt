11 U.S.C. § 945 — Continuing jurisdiction and closing of the case
Citation: us:usc:11:945
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section945
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 9 (Adjustment Of Debts Of A Municipality); Subchapter III (The Plan). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The court may retain jurisdiction over the case for such period of time as is necessary for the successful implementation of the plan.
(b)
Except as provided in subsection (a) of this section, the court shall close the case when administration of the case has been completed.
