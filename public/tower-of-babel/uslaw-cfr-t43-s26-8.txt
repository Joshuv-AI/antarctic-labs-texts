43 C.F.R. § 26.8 — Program reporting requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:43:26.8
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-26.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 26 (Grants To States For Establishing Youth Conservation Corps Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Monitoring and reporting of program performance will be in accordance with Attachment I of OMB Circular A-102.
(b) The reporting and/or recordkeeping requirements contained herein have been approved by the Office of Management and Budget in accordance with the Federal Reports Act of 1942.
