9 C.F.R. § 306.5 — Appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:9:306.5
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-306.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 306 (Assignment And Authorities Of Program Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any appeal from a decision of any Program employee shall be made to his/her immediate supervisor having jurisdiction over the subject matter of the appeal, except as otherwise provided in the applicable rules of practice.
