40 C.F.R. § 1.1 — Creation and authority.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1 (Statement Of Organization And General Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Reorganization Plan 3 of 1970, established the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) in the Executive branch as an independent Agency, effective December 2, 1970.
