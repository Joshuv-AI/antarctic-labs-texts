30 C.F.R. § 721.14 — Failure to give notice and lack of reasonable belief.
Citation: us:cfr:30:721.14
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-721.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 721 (Federal Inspections). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No notice of violation or cessation order may be vacated by reason of failure to give notice required by the Act or these regulations prior to the inspection; or by reason of a subsequent determination that prior to the inspection the Office did not have information sufficient to create a reasonable belief that a violation had occurred.
