25 C.F.R. § 256.15 — When will I hear if I have received funding?
Citation: us:cfr:25:256.15
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-256.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 256 (Housing Improvement Program (Hip)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Your servicing housing office will inform you whether you will receive funds in writing within 45 days after it completes the list required by § 256.14(c).
(a) If funding is available, the office will send you complete information on how to obtain HIP services.
(b) If funding is not available, the office will send you instructions on how to update your application for funding for the next available program year.
