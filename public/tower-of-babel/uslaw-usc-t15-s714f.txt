15 U.S.C. § 714f — Use of funds
Citation: us:usc:15:714f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section714f
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 15 (Economic Recovery); Subchapter II (Commodity Credit Corporation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Corporation is authorized to use in the conduct of its business all its funds and other assets, including capital and net earnings therefrom, and all funds and other assets which have been or may hereafter be transferred or allocated to, borrowed by, or otherwise acquired by it.
