15 U.S.C. § 63 — Acquisition of stock of export trade corporation
Citation: us:usc:15:63
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section63
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 2 (Federal Trade Commission; Promotion Of Export Trade And Prevention Of Unfair Methods Of Competition); Subchapter II (Promotion Of Export Trade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing contained in section 18 of this title shall be construed to forbid the acquisition or ownership by any corporation of the whole or any part of the stock or other capital of any corporation organized solely for the purpose of engaging in export trade, and actually engaged solely in such export trade, unless the effect of such acquisition or ownership may be to restrain trade or substantially lessen competition within the United States.
