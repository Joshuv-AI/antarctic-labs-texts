49 U.S.C. § 13909 — Availability of information
Citation: us:usc:49:13909
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section13909
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part B (Motor Carriers, Water Carriers, Brokers, And Freight Forwarders); Chapter 139 (Registration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall make information relating to registration and financial security required by this chapter publicly available on the Internet, including—
(1)
the names and business addresses of the principals of each entity holding such registration;
(2)
the status of such registration; and
(3)
the electronic address of the entity’s surety provider for the submission of claims.
