14 C.F.R. § 91.853 — Final compliance: Civil subsonic airplanes.
Citation: us:cfr:14:91.853
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-91.853
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 91 (General Operating And Flight Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in § 91.873, after December 31, 1999, no person shall operate to or from any airport in the contiguous United States any airplane subject to § 91.801(c), unless that airplane has been shown to comply with Stage 3, Stage 4, or Stage 5 noise levels.
