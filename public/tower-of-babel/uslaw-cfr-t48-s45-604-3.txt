48 C.F.R. § 45.604-3 — 45.604-3 Proceeds from sales of surplus property.
Citation: us:cfr:48:45.604-3
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-45.604-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 45 (Government Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Proceeds of any sale are to be credited to the Treasury of the United States as miscellaneous receipts, unless otherwise authorized by statute or the contract or any subcontract thereunder authorizes the proceeds to be credited to the price or cost of the work (40 U.S.C. 571 and 574).
