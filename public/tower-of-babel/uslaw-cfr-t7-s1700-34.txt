7 C.F.R. § 1700.34 — Assistance to High Energy Cost Rural Communities.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1700.34
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1700.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1700 (General Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

RUS, through the Electric Program, makes grants and loans to assist high energy cost rural communities. The Assistant Administrator, Electric Program, directs and coordinates the assistance to high energy cost rural communities program and serves as the primary point of contact for applicants, grantees, and borrowers.
