41 C.F.R. § 102-192.45 — -192.45 Agency mail managers.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-192.45
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-192.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-192 (192—Mail Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every agency, as defined in § 102-192.10, must have an agency mail manager at a managerial level that enables them to speak for their agency on mail management as outlined in this part.
