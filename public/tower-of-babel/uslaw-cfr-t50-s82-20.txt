50 C.F.R. § 82.20 — Civil rights.
Citation: us:cfr:50:82.20
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-82.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 82 (Administrative Procedures For Grants-In-Aid (Marine Mammal Protection Act Of 1972)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each cooperative agreement shall be supported by a statement of assurance executed by the grantee providing that the project will be carried out in accordance with title VI, non-discrimination in federally assisted programs, of the Civil Rights Act of 1964, 42 U.S.C. 2000d-2000d-4, and with the Secretary's regulations promulgated thereunder, 43 CFR part 17.
