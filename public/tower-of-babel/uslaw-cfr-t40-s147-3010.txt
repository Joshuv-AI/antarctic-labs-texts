40 C.F.R. § 147.3010 — Mechanical integrity tests.
Citation: us:cfr:40:147.3010
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-147.3010
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 147 (State, Tribal, And Epa-Administered Underground Injection Control Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The monitoring of annulus pressure listed in § 146.8(b)(1) of this chapter will only be acceptable if preceded by a pressure test, using liquid or gas that clearly demonstrates that mechanical integrity exists at the time of the pressure test.
