36 C.F.R. § 702.11 — Smoking in Library buildings.
Citation: us:cfr:36:702.11
Creator: Library Of Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-702.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 702 (Conduct On Library Premises). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Smoking in Library areas is prohibited except in those areas specifically designated for this purpose.
