10 U.S.C. § 7314 — Twenty to thirty years: enlisted members
Citation: us:usc:10:7314
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7314
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 741 (Retirement For Length Of Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Under regulations to be prescribed by the Secretary of the Army, an enlisted member of the Army who has at least 20, but less than 30, years of service computed under section 7325 of this title may, upon his request, be retired.
