32 C.F.R. § 203.16 — Record retention and audits.
Citation: us:cfr:32:203.16
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-203.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 203 (Technical Assistance For Public Participation (Tapp) In Defense Environmental Restoration Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The recipient technical assistance providers shall keep and preserve detailed records in connection with the contract reflecting acquisitions, work progress, reports, expenditures and commitments, and indicate the relationship to established costs and schedules.
