Abstract

Question 1
.

Can a surface group act properly on a locally compact product of trees?

A folklore candidate action, first considered by Long and Reid in the 1990s, attempted to produce an explicit example of this. Let
\Sigma
denote the orbifold which is a torus with a single cone point of order 2. Its fundamental group is given by the presentation
\pi_{1}(\Sigma)=\langle a,b\mid[a,b]^{2}=1\rangle
. Magnus
[

considers the
\mathsf{PGL}_{2}
character variety of this group. An element of
\mathsf{PGL}_{2}
has order 2 if and only if it has trace 0, and so we seek two matrices whose commutator has trace zero to obtain a representation of this group. Supposing the first matrix is
a=\begin{pmatrix}t&0\\
0&1\end{pmatrix}
, the second matrix
b
must satisfy
2b_{11}b_{22}=(t+t^{-1})b_{12}b_{21}

\left\langle\begin{pmatrix}t&0\\
0&1\end{pmatrix},\begin{pmatrix}1+t^{2}&2\\
t&1\end{pmatrix}\right\rangle\leq\mathsf{PGL}_{2}\left(\mathbb{Z}[t]\left[\frac{1}{t},\frac{1}{t-1}\right]\right).

Any choice of real parameter
t>1

0&1\end{pmatrix},\frac{1}{8}\begin{pmatrix}82&2\\
9&1\end{pmatrix}\right\rangle\leq\mathsf{PSL}_{2}\left(\mathbb{Z}\left[\frac{1}{6}\right]\right).
