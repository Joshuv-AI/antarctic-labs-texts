31 C.F.R. § 598.313 — Significant foreign narcotics trafficker.
Citation: us:cfr:31:598.313
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-598.313
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 598 (Foreign Narcotics Kingpin Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term significant foreign narcotics trafficker means any foreign person that plays a significant role in international narcotics trafficking that the President has determined to be appropriate for sanctions and has publicly identified under section 804(b) or section 804(h)(1) of the Foreign Narcotics Kingpin Designation Act.
