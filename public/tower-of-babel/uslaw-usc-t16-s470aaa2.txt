16 U.S.C. § 470aaa–2 — Public awareness and education program
Citation: us:usc:16:470aaa–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section470aaa–2
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1C (Paleontological Resources Preservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall establish a program to increase public awareness about the significance of paleontological resources.
