21 C.F.R. § 211.115 — Reprocessing.
Citation: us:cfr:21:211.115
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-211.115
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 211 (Current Good Manufacturing Practice For Finished Pharmaceuticals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Written procedures shall be established and followed prescribing a system for reprocessing batches that do not conform to standards or specifications and the steps to be taken to insure that the reprocessed batches will conform with all established standards, specifications, and characteristics.
(b) Reprocessing shall not be performed without the review and approval of the quality control unit.
