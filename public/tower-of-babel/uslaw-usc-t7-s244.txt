7 U.S.C. § 244 — Quality and value standards
Citation: us:usc:7:244
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section244
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 10 (Warehouses). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If standards for the evaluation or determination of the quality or value of an agricultural product are not established under another Federal law, the Secretary may establish standards for the evaluation or determination of the quality or value of the agricultural product under this chapter.
