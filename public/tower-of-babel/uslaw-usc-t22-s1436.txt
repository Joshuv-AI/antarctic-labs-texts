22 U.S.C. § 1436 — Restriction on disclosure of information
Citation: us:usc:22:1436
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1436
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 18 (United States Information And Educational Exchange Programs); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall authorize the disclosure of any information or knowledge in any case in which such disclosure (1) is prohibited by any other law of the United States, or (2) is inconsistent with the security of the United States.
