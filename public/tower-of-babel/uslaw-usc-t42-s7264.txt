42 U.S.C. § 7264 — Seal of Department
Citation: us:usc:42:7264
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section7264
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 84 (Department Of Energy); Subchapter VI (Administrative Provisions); Part C (General Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall cause a seal of office to be made for the Department of such design as he shall approve and judicial notice shall be taken of such seal.
