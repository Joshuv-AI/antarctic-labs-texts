20 C.F.R. § 411.610 — When should a beneficiary receive information on the procedures for resolving disputes?
Citation: us:cfr:20:411.610
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-411.610
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 411 (The Ticket To Work And Self-Sufficiency Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each EN that is not a State VR agency must inform each beneficiary seeking services under the Ticket to Work program of the procedures for resolving disputes when—
(a) The EN and the beneficiary complete and sign the IWP;
(b) Services in the beneficiary's IWP are reduced, suspended or terminated; and
(c) A dispute arises related to the services spelled out in the beneficiary's IWP or to the beneficiary's participation in the program.
