10 C.F.R. § 1013.41 — Stay pending appeal.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1013.41
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1013.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1013 (Administrative False Claims And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An initial decision is stayed automatically pending disposition of a motion for reconsideration or of an appeal to the authority head.
(b) No administrative stay is available following a final decision of the authority head.
