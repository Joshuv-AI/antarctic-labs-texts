42 U.S.C. § 254c–18 — Telemedicine; incentive grants regarding coordination among States
Citation: us:usc:42:254c–18
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section254c–18
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter II (General Powers And Duties); Part D (Primary Health Care); Subpart i (Health Centers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The Secretary may make grants to State professional licensing boards to carry out programs under which such licensing boards of various States cooperate to develop and implement State policies that will reduce statutory and regulatory barriers to telemedicine.
(b) Authorization of appropriations
For the purpose of carrying out subsection (a), there are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary for each of the fiscal years 2002 through 2006.
