43 U.S.C. § 421d — Effect on existing laws
Citation: us:usc:43:421d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section421d
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 12 (Reclamation And Irrigation Of Lands By Federal Government); Subchapter III (Institution And Construction Of Projects). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as otherwise provided in sections 421a to 421h of this title, the provisions of the Federal reclamation laws, and Acts amendatory thereto, are continued in full force and effect.
