25 U.S.C. § 45 — Preference to Indians qualified for duties
Citation: us:usc:25:45
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section45
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 2 (Officers Of Indian Affairs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In all cases of the appointments of interpreters or other persons employed for the benefit of the Indians, a preference shall be given to persons of Indian descent, if such can be found, who are properly qualified for the execution of the duties.
