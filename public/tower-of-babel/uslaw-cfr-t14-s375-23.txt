14 C.F.R. § 375.23 — Maximum allowable weights.
Citation: us:cfr:14:375.23
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-375.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 375 (Navigation Of Foreign Civil Aircraft Within The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Foreign civil aircraft that are permitted to navigate in the United States on the basis of foreign airworthiness certificates must conform to the limitations on maximum certificated weights prescribed or authorized for the particular variation of the aircraft type, and for the particular category of use, by the country of manufacture of the aircraft type involved.
