21 C.F.R. § 1316.10 — Administrative probable cause.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1316.10
Creator: Drug Enforcement Administration, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1316.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1316 (Administrative Functions, Practices, And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the judge or magistrate is satisfied that “administrative probable cause,” as defined in section 510(d)(1) of the Act (21 U.S.C. 880(d)(1)) exists, he shall issue an administrative warrant. Administrative probable cause shall not mean criminal probable cause as defined by Federal statute or case law.
