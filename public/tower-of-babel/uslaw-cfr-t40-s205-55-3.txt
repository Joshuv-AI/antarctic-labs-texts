40 C.F.R. § 205.55-3 — -3 Configuration identification.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 205 (Transportation Equipment Noise Emission Controls). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A separate vehicle configuration shall be determined by each combination of the following parameters:
(1) Exhaust system configuration. (i) Single vertical.
(ii) Dual vertical.
(iii) Single horizontal.
(iv) Dual horizontal.
(2) Air induction system (engine). (i) Natural.
(ii) Turbocharged.
(3) Fan. (i) Diameter.
(ii) Drive.
(a) Direct.
(b) Thermostatic.
(iii) Max fan rpm.
(4) Engine manufacturer's horsepower rating.
(5) Cab characteristic. (i) Sleeper.
(ii) Non sleeper.
(6) Category parameters listed in § 205.55-2.
