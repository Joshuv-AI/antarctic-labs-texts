34 C.F.R. § 34.5 — Contents of a notice of proposed garnishment.
Citation: us:cfr:34:34.5
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-34.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 34 (Administrative Wage Garnishment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In a notice of proposed garnishment, we inform you of—
(a) The nature and amount of the debt;
(b) Our intention to collect the debt through deductions from pay until the debt and all accumulated interest, penalties, and collection costs are paid in full; and
(c) An explanation of your rights, including those in § 34.6, and the time frame within which you may exercise your rights.
