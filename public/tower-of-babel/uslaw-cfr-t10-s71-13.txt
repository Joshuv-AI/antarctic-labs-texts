10 C.F.R. § 71.13 — Exemption of physicians.
Citation: us:cfr:10:71.13
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-71.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 71 (Packaging And Transportation Of Radioactive Material). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any physician licensed by a State to dispense drugs in the practice of medicine is exempt from § 71.5 with respect to transport by the physician of licensed material for use in the practice of medicine. However, any physician operating under this exemption must be licensed under 10 CFR part 35 or the equivalent Agreement State regulations.
