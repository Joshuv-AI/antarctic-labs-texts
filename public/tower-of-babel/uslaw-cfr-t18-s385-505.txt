18 C.F.R. § 385.505 — Right of participants to present evidence (Rule 505).
Citation: us:cfr:18:385.505
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-385.505
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 385 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Consistent with the provisions of this part, a participant has the right to present such evidence, including rebuttal evidence, to make such objections and arguments, and to conduct such cross-examination, as may be necessary to assure true and full disclosure of the facts.
