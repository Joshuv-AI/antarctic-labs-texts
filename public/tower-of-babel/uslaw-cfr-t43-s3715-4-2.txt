43 C.F.R. § 3715.4-2 — -2 What if I do not notify BLM of my existing occupancy?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3715.4-2
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3715.4-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3710 (Public Law 167; Act Of July 23, 1955). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you do not provide the written notice required in § 3715.4, you will be subject to the enforcement actions of § 3715.7-1, the civil remedies of § 3715.7-2, and the criminal penalties of § 3715.8.
