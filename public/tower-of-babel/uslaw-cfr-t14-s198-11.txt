14 C.F.R. § 198.11 — Change in status of aircraft.
Citation: us:cfr:14:198.11
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-198.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 198 (Aviation Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the event of sale, lease, confiscation, requisition, total loss, or other change in the status of an aircraft or insurable items covered by insurance under this part, the insured party must notify the office administering the Aviation Insurance Program before, or as soon as practicable after, the change in status.
