48 C.F.R. § 1601.103 — 1601.103 Applicability.

Creator: Chapter 16—Office Of Personnel Management Federal Employees Health Benefits Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1601.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1601 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The FAR is generally applicable to contracts negotiated in the FEHBP pursuant to 5 U.S.C. chapter 89. The FEHBAR implements and supplements the FAR where necessary to identify basic and significant acquisition policies unique to the FEHBP.
