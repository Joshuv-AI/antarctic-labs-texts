22 U.S.C. § 7501 — Definition
Citation: us:usc:22:7501
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section7501
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 82 (Afghanistan Freedom Support). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter, the term “Government of Afghanistan” includes—
(1)
the government of any political subdivision of Afghanistan; and
(2)
any agency or instrumentality of the Government of Afghanistan.
