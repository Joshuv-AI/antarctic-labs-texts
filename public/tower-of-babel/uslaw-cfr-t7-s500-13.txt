7 C.F.R. § 500.13 — Nondiscrimination.
Citation: us:cfr:7:500.13
Creator: Agricultural Research Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-500.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 500 (National Arboretum). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The USNA is subject to the policy of nondiscrimination in programs or activities conducted by the United States Department of Agriculture as set forth in 7 CFR part 15d.
