34 U.S.C. § 40704 — Conditions of release generally
Citation: us:usc:34:40704
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section40704
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 407 (Dna Identification); Subchapter I (Collection And Analysis Of Samples). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If the collection of a DNA sample from an individual on probation, parole, or supervised release is authorized pursuant to section 40702 or 40703 of this title or section 1565 of title 10, the individual shall cooperate in the collection of a DNA sample as a condition of that probation, parole, or supervised release.
