24 C.F.R. § 17.95 — Written decision following a hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:24:17.95
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-17.95
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 17 (Administrative Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Written decisions provided after a request for a hearing will include:
(a) A statement of the facts presented to support the nature and origin of the alleged debt;
(b) The hearing officer's analysis, findings, and conclusions, in light of the hearing, concerning the employee's or the Department's grounds;
(c) The amount and validity of the alleged debt; and
(d) The repayment schedule, if applicable.
