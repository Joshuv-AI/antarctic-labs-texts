36 C.F.R. § 1260.26 — Who is responsible for issuing special procedures for declassification of records pertaining to intelligence activities and intelligence sources or methods, or of classified cryptologic records in NARA's holdings?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1260.26
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1260.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1260 (Declassification Of National Security Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Director of National Intelligence is responsible for issuing special procedures for declassification of classified records pertaining to intelligence activities and intelligence sources and methods.
(b) The Secretary of Defense is responsible for issuing special procedures for declassification of classified cryptologic records.
