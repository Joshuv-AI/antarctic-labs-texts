19 C.F.R. § 147.46 — Voluntary abandonment or destruction.
Citation: us:cfr:19:147.46
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-147.46
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 147 (Trade Fairs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At any time before or within 3 months after the closing date of the fair any article entered for a fair may be abandoned to the Government or destroyed under Customs supervision, upon compliance with § 158.43 of this chapter.
