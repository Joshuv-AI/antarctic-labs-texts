On the abstract wrapped Floer setups
Hayato Morimura

Abstract
. The wrapped Fukaya category of a Liouville sector is defined via an axiomatic construction from the associated abstract wrapped Floer setup. In this paper, we propose a modified axiomatic construction, removing the irrelevant choices and the factorization axiom from the abstract wrapped Floer setup. Based on our modification, we reformulate and then prove a conjecture which essentially claims that the wrapped Fukaya category is obtained as the ∞ \infty -categorical localization along continuation maps. This amounts to compare the two axiomatic constructions.



1.
Introduction

In their seminal works

[
GPS1
,
GPS2
]
,
Ganatra–Pardon–Shende proposed a covariantly functorial construction of the (partially) wrapped Fukaya category of a (stopped) Liouville sector.
Based on the covariant functoriality,
they developed various
interesting
and
useful
theories.
One example is sectorial descent,
which provides us with a powerful local-to-global method to compute the wrapped Fukaya category of a Weinstein sector.
It also has applications in proving homological mirror symmetry

[
GJ25
,
GL22
,
Mor
]
.
See

[
GS20
,
GS23
]

for the corresponding gluing method via

[
GPS3
]

in microlocal sheaf theory.
Another is Viterbo restriction,
with respect to which the wrapped Fukaya category of a Weinstein sector exhibits Zariski-type descent

[
PS1
,
PS2
,
PS3
,
MSZ
]
,
playing a key role in the proof of homological mirror symmetry.
See

[
Kuw20
]

for the corresponding gluing method via

[
GPS3
]

in microlocal sheaf theory.

Their construction of the wrapped Fukaya category is twofold.
First,
to a stopped Liouville sector

(X,\mathfrak{f})
,
one associates a collection of data

(\mathcal{S}_{(X,\mathfrak{f})},H\mathcal{F}^{\text{env}}_{\mathcal{S}_{(X,\mathfrak{f})}},\tilde{C}_{(X,\mathfrak{f})},\{\tilde{R}^{(X,\mathfrak{f})}_{L}\}_{L})
,
called an abstract wrapped Floer setup,
so that
the assignments

\displaystyle(X,\mathfrak{f})\mapsto(\mathcal{S}_{(X,\mathfrak{f})},H\mathcal{F}^{\text{env}}_{\mathcal{S}_{(X,\mathfrak{f})}},\tilde{C}_{(X,\mathfrak{f})},\{\tilde{R}^{(X,\mathfrak{f})}_{L}\}_{L})

are covariantly functorial with respect to suitable morphisms.
Next,
to an abstract wrapped Floer setup

(\mathcal{S},H\mathcal{F}^{\text{env}}_{\mathcal{S}},\tilde{C},\{\tilde{R}_{L}\}_{L})
,
one associates a cofibrant strictly unital
A_{\infty}
-category

\mathcal{W}_{P_{\text{univ}}(\mathcal{S}),\tilde{C}}

so that
the assignments

\displaystyle(\mathcal{S},H\mathcal{F}^{\text{env}}_{\mathcal{S}},\tilde{C},\{\tilde{R}_{L}\}_{L})\mapsto\mathcal{W}_{P_{\text{univ}}(\mathcal{S}),\tilde{C}}

are covariantly functorial with respect to suitable morphisms.
The former step is geometric
while the latter is axiomatic.
In

[
GPS2
, Rem. 2.12]
,
Ganatra–Pardon–Shende conjectured the existence of a weaker axiomatic framework.
More specifically,
they expected that
one can remove from the latter step
the choices of
envelope

H\mathcal{F}^{\text{env}}_{\mathcal{S}}

for

H\mathcal{F}^{\text{pre}}_{\mathcal{S}}

and
wrapping categories

\tilde{R}_{L}

for all objects

L

of the
A_{\infty}
-pre-category

\mathcal{F}^{\text{pre}}_{\mathcal{S}}

associated with

\mathcal{S}
.

One of the two goals of this paper is to give an affirmative answer to the above conjecture.
Careful tracing of the original construction naturally leads us to a minimal collection of data

(w\mathcal{S},C)
,
which we call a

weak abstract wrapped Floer setup
.
After refining the axiomatic composability

[
GPS2
, Def. 2.10(ii)]
to obtain a modification

w\mathcal{S}

of

\mathcal{S}
,
this amounts to couple

w\mathcal{S}

with a certain subset

C\subset H^{0}\mathcal{F}_{w\mathcal{S}}

of the morphisms in canonical envelope

H\mathcal{F}_{w\mathcal{S}}

for

H\mathcal{F}^{\text{pre}}_{w\mathcal{S}}
.
Note that
by definition
we have

H\mathcal{F}_{w\mathcal{S}}\subset H\mathcal{F}^{\text{env}}_{\mathcal{S}}

as
\mathbb{Z}
-linear graded categories.
The weak abstract wrapped Floer setup

(w\mathcal{S},C)

is free from not only the irrelevant choices but also the factorization axiom

[
GPS2
, Def. 2.11(iv)]
.
As desired,
also for weak abstract wrapped Floer setups there is a covariantly functorial construction of a cofibrant strictly unital
A_{\infty}
-category
whose cohomology recovers the wrapped Donaldson–Fukaya-type category.

Theorem 1.1
.

There exist assignments

(1.1)

\displaystyle w\mathcal{S}\mapsto\mathcal{F}^{\text{univ}}_{w\mathcal{S}}

to cofibrant strictly unital
A_{\infty}
-categories with the following properties.

(i)

When coupled with the set

C\subset H^{0}\mathcal{F}_{w\mathcal{S}}

of continuation maps,
the assignments

(
1.1
)

extend to

(1.2)

\displaystyle(w\mathcal{S},C)\mapsto\mathcal{F}^{\text{univ}}_{w\mathcal{S}}[C^{-1}_{\text{univ}}]

for some set

C_{\text{univ}}\subset H^{0}\mathcal{F}^{\text{univ}}_{w\mathcal{S}}

uniquely determined by

C

up to unique isomorphism.

(ii)

The assignments

(
1.2
)

define an
\infty
-functor

(1.3)

\displaystyle N(\operatorname{wAWFS})\to\mathcal{C}at^{s}_{A_{\infty}},\ (w\mathcal{S},C)\mapsto\mathcal{W}_{w\mathcal{S},C}=\mathcal{F}^{\text{univ}}_{w\mathcal{S}}[C^{-1}_{\text{univ}}].

Here,

N(\operatorname{wAWFS})

denotes the nerve of the category of weak abstract wrapped Floer setups
and

\mathcal{C}at^{s}_{A_{\infty}}

denotes the
\infty
-category of strictly unital
A_{\infty}
-categories.

(iii)

There exist canonical equivalences

\displaystyle H\mathcal{W}_{w\mathcal{S},C}\simeq H\mathcal{W}_{\operatorname{DF},w\mathcal{S},C}

to the weak wrapped Donaldson–Fukaya categories in the sense of Section

4.3
,
which are compatible with the
\infty
-functor

(
1.3
)
.

Remark 1.2
.

As we will explain in Section

4.3
,
essentially we do not add anything to the original definition.
Hence,
even after our modification,
usual geometric data still give sufficient input to define the wrapped Fukaya categories.

The other goal is to give an affirmative answer to the following conjecture.

Conjecture 1.3

(
[
GPS2
, Conj. 2.23]
)
.

For
any
A_{\infty}
-category

\mathcal{C}

and
suitably defined

\operatorname{Fun}(\mathcal{F}^{\text{pre}}_{\mathcal{S}},\mathcal{C})
,
the restriction functor

\displaystyle\operatorname{Fun}(\mathcal{W}_{P_{\text{univ}}(\mathcal{S}),\tilde{C}},\mathcal{C})\to\operatorname{Fun}(\mathcal{F}^{\text{pre}}_{\mathcal{S}},\mathcal{C})

is fully faithful
and
its essential image consists of those functors
which send continuation maps to isomorphisms in

\mathcal{C}
.

Suppose that

\mathcal{F}^{\text{pre}}_{\mathcal{S}}

was a cofibrant unital
A_{\infty}
-category
and

\mathcal{W}_{P_{\text{univ}}(\mathcal{S}),\tilde{C}}

was its
\infty
-categorical localization

\mathcal{F}^{\text{pre}}_{\mathcal{S}}[\tilde{C}^{-1}_{\circ}]

along the set

\tilde{C}_{\circ}\subset H^{0}\mathcal{F}^{\text{pre}}_{\mathcal{S}}

of continuation maps.
Then,
due to

[
OT25
]
,
for any unital
A_{\infty}
-category

\mathcal{D}

the pullback would give a unital
A_{\infty}
-functor

\displaystyle\operatorname{Fun}^{u}_{A_{\infty}}(\mathcal{W}_{P_{\text{univ}}(\mathcal{S}),\tilde{C}},\mathcal{D})\to\operatorname{Fun}^{u}_{A_{\infty}}(\mathcal{F}^{\text{pre}}_{\mathcal{S}},\mathcal{D})

between unital
A_{\infty}
-categories of unital
A_{\infty}
-functors with universal property:
it is fully faithful
and
its essential image consists of those functors
which send all representatives of any element in

\tilde{C}_{\circ}

to isomorphisms in

\mathcal{D}
.
As

\mathcal{W}_{P_{\text{univ}}(\mathcal{S}),\tilde{C}}

is the
\infty
-categorical localization

\mathcal{O}_{P_{\text{univ}}(\mathcal{S})}[I^{-1}_{P_{\text{univ}}(\mathcal{S}),\tilde{C}}]
,
Conjecture

1.3

would be trivial
if we set

\displaystyle\operatorname{Fun}(\mathcal{F}^{\text{pre}}_{\mathcal{S}},\mathcal{C})\coloneqq\operatorname{Fun}^{u}_{A_{\infty}}(\mathcal{O}_{P_{\text{univ}}(\mathcal{S})},\mathcal{C})

and
assume

\mathcal{C}

to be unital.
Here,

I_{P_{\text{univ}}(\mathcal{S}),\tilde{C}}\subset H^{0}\mathcal{O}_{P_{\text{univ}}(\mathcal{S})}

is obtained by
somehow universally duplicating

\tilde{C}
.
In other words,

\mathcal{W}_{P_{\text{univ}}(\mathcal{S}),\tilde{C}}

would be the
\infty
-categorical localization along universal duplicates of continuation maps.

Now,
one can consider the same thing as in the above paragraph replacing

\mathcal{F}^{\text{pre}}_{\mathcal{S}},\mathcal{W}_{P_{\text{univ}}(\mathcal{S}),\tilde{C}}

with

\mathcal{F}^{\text{univ}}_{w\mathcal{S}},\mathcal{W}_{w\mathcal{S},C}
,
where here

\mathcal{W}_{w\mathcal{S},C}

is the
\infty
-categorical localization of the cofibrant (strictly) unital
A_{\infty}
-category

\mathcal{F}^{\text{univ}}_{w\mathcal{S}}

along

C_{\text{univ}}
.
Hence it remains to compare the two axiomatic constructions.
If we
set

\operatorname{Fun}(\mathcal{F}^{\text{pre}}_{\mathcal{S}},\mathcal{C})\coloneqq\operatorname{Fun}^{u}_{A_{\infty}}(\mathcal{F}^{\text{univ}}_{w\mathcal{S}},\mathcal{C})

and
assume

\mathcal{C}

to be unital,
then Conjecture

1.3

could be reformulated as

Theorem 1.4
.

Let

(w\mathcal{S},C)

be
a weak abstract wrapped Floer setup
and

(\mathcal{S},H\mathcal{F}^{\text{env}}_{\mathcal{S}},\tilde{C},\{\tilde{R}_{L}\}_{L})

an abstract wrapped Floer setup with

\tilde{C}|_{H^{0}\mathcal{F}_{w\mathcal{S}}}=C
.
Then there exists a quasi-equivalence

\displaystyle\bar{\tau}\colon\mathcal{W}_{P_{\text{univ}}(\mathcal{S}),\tilde{C}}\xrightarrow{qeq}\mathcal{W}_{w\mathcal{S},C}

such that
for any unital
A_{\infty}
-category

\mathcal{D}

the pullback induces a zigzag of unital
A_{\infty}
-functors

\displaystyle\operatorname{Fun}^{u}_{A_{\infty}}(\mathcal{W}_{P_{\text{univ}}(\mathcal{S}),\tilde{C}},\mathcal{D})\xleftarrow{qeq}\operatorname{Fun}^{u}_{A_{\infty}}(\mathcal{W}_{w\mathcal{S},C},\mathcal{D})\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Fun}^{u}_{A_{\infty}}(\mathcal{F}^{\text{univ}}_{w\mathcal{S}},\mathcal{D})

with universal property:
it is fully faithful
and
its essential image consists of those functors
which send all representatives of any element in

C

to isomorphisms in

\mathcal{D}
.

Remark 1.5
.

The existence of some noncanonical fully faithful functor

\operatorname{Fun}^{u}_{A_{\infty}}(\mathcal{W}_{P_{\text{univ}}(\mathcal{S}),\tilde{C}},\mathcal{D})\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Fun}^{u}_{A_{\infty}}(\mathcal{F}^{\text{univ}}_{w\mathcal{S}},\mathcal{D})

easily follows from

[
Tan
, Prop. 3.47]

once we find an equivalence

H\mathcal{W}_{\operatorname{DF},\mathcal{S},\tilde{C}}\simeq H\mathcal{W}_{\operatorname{DF},w\mathcal{S},C}
.
A little more nontrivial part is to guarantee the property of its essential image.

Passing to the
\infty
-category

\mathcal{C}at^{u}_{A_{\infty}}

of unital
A_{\infty}
-categories,
which by

[
COS
, Thm. A]

is canonically equivalent to
\mathcal{C}at^{s}_{A_{\infty}}
,
we obtain the following refinement.

Corollary 1.6
.

In the same setting as in
Theorem
1.4
,
there exists a canonical equivalence

\displaystyle\bar{\tau}\colon\mathcal{W}_{P_{\text{univ}}(\mathcal{S}),\tilde{C}}\xrightarrow{\sim}\mathcal{W}_{w\mathcal{S},C}

in

\mathcal{C}at^{u}_{A_{\infty}}

such that
for any

\mathcal{D}\in\mathcal{C}at^{u}_{A_{\infty}}

the pullback induces a canonical
\infty
-functor

\displaystyle\hom_{\mathcal{C}at^{u}_{A_{\infty}}}(\mathcal{W}_{P_{\text{univ}}(\mathcal{S}),\tilde{C}},\mathcal{D})\xrightarrow{\sim}\hom_{\mathcal{C}at^{u}_{A_{\infty}}}(\mathcal{W}_{w\mathcal{S},C},\mathcal{D})\hookrightarrow\hom_{\mathcal{C}at^{u}_{A_{\infty}}}(\mathcal{F}^{\text{univ}}_{w\mathcal{S}},\mathcal{D})

with universal property:
it is fully faithful
and
its essential image consists of those functors
which send all representatives of any element in

C

to equivalence in

\mathcal{D}
.

Finally,
the sense becomes clear in
which the wrapped Fukaya category is obtained as the localization along continuation maps.
It might be an interesting problem to
construct the
B
-side counterparts of

\mathcal{F}^{\text{univ}}_{w\mathcal{S}}

and
continuation maps to lift homological mirror symmetry.

2.
\infty
-categorical quotients and localizations of
A_{\infty}
-categories

In this section,
we review the construction of

\infty
-categorical
quotients
and
localizations
of
A_{\infty}
-categories.
We denote by

A_{\infty}Cat

the ordinary category of small
\mathbb{Z}
-linear

A_{\infty}
-categories
and

A_{\infty}
-functors.
For the rest of the paper,
we will assume that
all

A_{\infty}
-categories
and

A_{\infty}
-functors
belong to

A_{\infty}Cat
.

2.1.
Unitalities

One advantage to work within the
\infty
-category of
A_{\infty}
-categories is the equivalence of various unitalities.
Recall that
an
A_{\infty}
-category

\mathcal{A}

is

strictly unital

if it admits a closed degree

0

element

\operatorname{id}_{X}\in\mathcal{A}(X,X)

such that
the chain maps

\displaystyle\mu^{2}_{\mathcal{A}}(\operatorname{id}_{X},-)\colon\mathcal{A}(Y,X)\to\mathcal{A}(Y,X),\ \mu^{2}_{\mathcal{A}}(-,\operatorname{id}_{X})\colon\mathcal{A}(X,Y)\to\mathcal{A}(X,Y)

are the identities
for all

X,Y\in\mathcal{A}

and

\displaystyle\mu^{k}_{\mathcal{A}}(\operatorname{id}_{X}\otimes\cdots)=\cdots=\mu^{k}_{\mathcal{A}}(\cdots\otimes\operatorname{id}_{X}\otimes\cdots)=\cdots=\mu^{k}_{\mathcal{A}}(\cdots\otimes\operatorname{id}_{X})=0

whenever

k>1

for all

X\in\mathcal{A}
.
We call such an element

strict unit
.
An
A_{\infty}
-functor

F\colon\mathcal{A}\to\mathcal{B}

is

strictly unital

if

\mathcal{A},\mathcal{B}

are strictly unital
and
we have

\displaystyle F^{1}(\operatorname{id}_{X})=\operatorname{id}_{F^{0}(X)},\ F^{k}(\operatorname{id}_{X}\otimes\cdots)=\cdots=F^{k}(\cdots\otimes\operatorname{id}_{X}\otimes\cdots)=\cdots=F^{k}(\cdots\otimes\operatorname{id}_{X})=0

for all

X\in\mathcal{A}

and

k>1
.

Recall that
an
A_{\infty}
-category

\mathcal{A}

is

cohomologically unital

if its cohomology category

H\mathcal{A}

is unital.
We call a closed degree

0

element

e_{X}\in\mathcal{A}(X,X)

representing the unit in

H\mathcal{A}(X,X)

a

cohomological unit

of

X
.
An
A_{\infty}
-functor

F\colon\mathcal{A}\to\mathcal{B}

is

cohomologically unital

if

\mathcal{A},\mathcal{B}

are cohomologically unital
and

HF

sends units to units.
Cohomologically unital

A_{\infty}
-categories
and

A_{\infty}
-functors
have the following refinements.

Definition 2.1

(
[
Lyu03
, Def. 7.3, 8.1, Prop. 7.4, 8.2]
)
.

An
A_{\infty}
-category

\mathcal{A}

is

unital

if
it is cohomologically unital
and
the chain maps

\displaystyle\mu^{2}_{\mathcal{A}}(e_{X},-)\colon\mathcal{A}(Y,X)\to\mathcal{A}(Y,X),\ \mu^{2}_{\mathcal{A}}(-,e_{X})\colon\mathcal{A}(X,Y)\to\mathcal{A}(X,Y)

are homotopic to the identities for all

X,Y\in\mathcal{A}
.
An
A_{\infty}
-functor

F\colon\mathcal{A}\to\mathcal{B}

is

unital

if

\mathcal{A},\mathcal{B}

are unital
and

HF

sends units to units.

In fact,
over a filed,
an
A_{\infty}
-category

\mathcal{A}

is unital
if and only if
it is cohomologically unital

[
COS
, Rem. 1.11]
.
We denote by

A_{\infty}Cat^{s},A_{\infty}Cat^{c}

and

the subcategories of

formed by

A_{\infty}
-categories
and

-functors
which are

There are canonical faithful inclusions

(2.1)

\displaystyle A_{\infty}Cat^{s}\to A_{\infty}Cat^{u}\hookrightarrow A_{\infty}Cat^{c}

where the last one is also full.
Let

\mathbf{w}_{s},\mathbf{w}_{u}

and

\mathbf{w}_{c}

be the subcategories of

and

For

\operatorname{Fun}^{u}_{A_{\infty}}(\mathcal{A}^{\prime},\mathcal{B})\to\operatorname{Fun}^{u}_{A_{\infty}}(\mathcal{A},\mathcal{B})

between unital
A_{\infty}
-categories of unital
A_{\infty}
-functors induces an equivalence in

\mathcal{C}at^{u}_{A_{\infty}}
.

Lemma 2.5

(
[
Tan
, Prop. 3.47]
)
.

Suppose that
there is an equivalence

H\mathcal{A}\simeq H\mathcal{A}^{\prime}

of cohomology categories for cofibrant unital
A_{\infty}
-categories

\mathcal{A},\mathcal{A}^{\prime}
.
Then it lifts to a unital
A_{\infty}
-functor

\mathcal{A}\to\mathcal{A}^{\prime}

inducing an equivalence in

\mathcal{C}at^{u}_{A_{\infty}}
.

Remark 2.6

(
[
OT25
, Rmk. 1.5]
,
[
Tan
, Rmk 1.6]
)
.

Functor
A_{\infty}
-categories are not necessarily invariant under quasi-equivalences in the domain variable.
Specifically,
if either of

\mathcal{A}

or

\mathcal{A}^{\prime}

is not cofibrant,
then in general the pullback does not induce a bijection on homotopy classes of
A_{\infty}
-functors.

2.3.
Quotients and localizations

Another advantage to work within

\mathcal{C}at^{*}_{A_{\infty}}

is the rigorous universal property of
quotients
and
localizations of
A_{\infty}
-categories.
Let

\mathcal{B}\hookrightarrow\mathcal{A}

be a fully faithful inclusion of unital
A_{\infty}
-categories.
We denote by

\mathbf{0}

the zero category,
i.e.
the category with
a single object
and
the zero morphism.
Then the

quotient
A_{\infty}
-category

\mathcal{A}/\mathcal{B}

is defined in

[
OT25
, Def. 4.1]

to be the pushout in

\mathcal{C}at^{u}_{A_{\infty}}

of the diagram

\mathbf{0}\leftarrow\mathcal{B}\hookrightarrow\mathcal{A}
.
Passing via the canonical equivalence to

\mathcal{C}at_{dg}
,
one sees that

\mathcal{C}at^{u}_{A_{\infty}}

is presentable.
In particular,

\mathcal{C}at^{u}_{A_{\infty}}

admits all colimits.
Hence

\mathcal{A}/\mathcal{B}

always exists.
Moreover,
by

[
OT25
, Prop. 4.5]

it exhibits universal property
when

\mathcal{A}

is in addition cofibrant.
Namely,
for any unital
A_{\infty}
-category

\mathcal{D}

the pullback

\displaystyle\hom_{\mathcal{C}at^{u}_{A_{\infty}}}(\mathcal{A}/\mathcal{B},\mathcal{D})\to\hom_{\mathcal{C}at^{u}_{A_{\infty}}}(\mathcal{A},\mathcal{D})

is fully faithful
and
its essential image consists of those functors
which are contractible along

\mathcal{B}

[
OT25
, Def. 4.2, Rmk. 4.3]
.
Due to the following result,
for any unital
A_{\infty}
-functor

i\colon\mathcal{B}\to\mathcal{A}

one may define the

quotient
A_{\infty}
-category

\mathcal{A}/\mathcal{B}

along

i

as

\mathcal{A}/\mathcal{B}^{\#}
,
where

\mathcal{B}^{\#}\subset\mathcal{A}

denotes the full subcategory spanned by the essential image of

i
.

Lemma 2.7

(
[
OT25
, Prop. 4.7]
)
.

For any unital
A_{\infty}
-functor

i\colon\mathcal{B}\to\mathcal{A}
,
the pushout in

\mathcal{C}at_{A_{\infty}}

of the diagram

\bf 0\leftarrow\mathcal{B}\xrightarrow{i}\mathcal{A}

is equivalent to

\mathcal{A}/\mathcal{B}^{\#}
.

One can construct localizations of
A_{\infty}
-categories via quotients.
Let

\mathcal{A}

be a unital
A_{\infty}
-category
and
fix a subset

W\subset H^{0}\mathcal{A}

of cohomology classes of some morphisms in

\mathcal{A}
.
In

[
OT25
, Def. 5.2]

a

localization

of

\mathcal{A}

along

W

is defined to be a unital
A_{\infty}
-functor

\iota\colon\mathcal{A}\to\mathcal{L}

if any of its cofibrant replacements

\tilde{\iota}\colon\tilde{\mathcal{A}}\to\tilde{\mathcal{L}}

satisfies the following universal property in the sense of

[
OT25
, Prop. 5.1(a)]
:
for every unital
A_{\infty}
-category

\mathcal{D}
,
the pullback

\displaystyle\tilde{\iota}^{*}\colon\hom_{\mathcal{C}at^{u}_{A_{\infty}}}(\tilde{\mathcal{L}},\mathcal{D})\to\hom_{\mathcal{C}at^{u}_{A_{\infty}}}(\tilde{\mathcal{A}},\mathcal{D})

is fully faithful
and
its essential image consists of those functors
which send all representatives of any element in

W

to equivalences in

\mathcal{D}
.
We denote by

\mathcal{A}[W^{-1}]

the codomain of such a morphism as

\iota
,
which is also called the

localization

of

\mathcal{A}

along

W
.
Let

\mathcal{B}_{W}\subset\mathcal{A}

be the full subcategory spanned by objects
which arise as mapping cones of representatives of elements in

W
.
The quotient
A_{\infty}
-category

(\operatorname{Tw}\mathcal{A})/\mathcal{B}_{W}

receives the canonical functor

\mathcal{A}\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Tw}\mathcal{A}\to(\operatorname{Tw}\mathcal{A})/\mathcal{B}_{W}
.
We denote by

\mathcal{L}_{W}

the full subcategory spanned by its essential image.
Due to the following result,
it is a localization of

\mathcal{A}

along

W

when

\mathcal{A}

is in addition cofibrant.

Theorem 2.8

(
[
OT25
, Thm. 5.9]
)
.

When

\mathcal{A}

is cofibrant,
the unital
A_{\infty}
-functor

\mathcal{A}\to\mathcal{L}_{W}

satisfies the universal property in the sense of

[
OT25
, Prop. 5.1(a)]
.

3.1.
Abstract Floer setups

Definition 3.1

(
[
GPS2
, Def. 2.9]
)
.

An

abstract Floer setup

\mathcal{S}

consists of the following data.

(i)

A set

\mathcal{L}

of
Lagrangians
.

(ii)

Subsets

\mathcal{L}_{k}\subset\mathcal{L}^{k+1}

of

composable tuples

for integers

k\geq 1

which are closed under
passing to subsequences:
if

(L_{0},\ldots,L_{k})\in\mathcal{L}_{k}

then

(L_{i_{0}},\ldots,L_{i_{l}})\in\mathcal{L}_{l}

for every

0\leq i_{0}<\cdots<i_{l}\leq k
.

(iii)

Graded modules

CF(L,K)

for all

(L,K)\in\mathcal{L}_{1}
.

(iv)

A contravariant functor

D

defined by specifying
sets

D(L_{0},\ldots,L_{k})

of

Floer data

for all

(L_{0},\ldots,L_{k})\in\mathcal{L}_{k}

and
restriction maps

D(L_{0},\ldots,L_{k})\to D(L_{i_{0}},\ldots,L_{i_{l}})

along inclusions of subsequences for every

0\leq i_{0}<\cdots<i_{l}\leq k

with

l\geq 1
.

(v)

Contractibility of Floer data:
the map from

D(L_{0},\ldots,L_{k})

to the limit of

D

applied to all proper subsequences of

(L_{0},\ldots,L_{k})

must be surjective.
In particular,

D(L_{0},L_{1})

is nonempty
as

D(L)

consists of a single point for every

L\in\mathcal{L}
.

(vi)

For every element

\delta(L_{0},\ldots,L_{k})\in D(L_{0},\ldots,L_{k})

a linear map

\displaystyle\mu^{k}_{\delta(L_{0},\ldots,L_{k})}\colon CF(L_{0},L_{1})\otimes\cdots\otimes CF(L_{k-1},L_{k})\to CF(L_{0},L_{k})[2-k]

satisfying the

A_{\infty}
-relations with respect to the restriction maps on

D
.
In particular,

(CF(L_{0},L_{1}),\mu^{1}_{\delta(L,K)})

is a cochain complex for every

(L,K)\in\mathcal{L}_{1}

and

\delta(L,K)\in D(L,K)
.
We require each such complex to be cofibrant in the sense of

[
GPS1
, Def. 3.2]
.

(vii)

Sets

D^{\prime}(L,K),D^{\prime\prime}(L,K)

and

D^{\prime\prime\prime}_{i}(L_{0},L_{1},L_{2})

for

i=0,1,2

together with surjective maps respectively to

D(L,K)\times D(L,K),D^{\prime}(L,K)\times_{D(L,K)\times D(L,K)}(D^{\prime}(L,K)\times_{D(L,K)}D^{\prime}(L,K))

and

\displaystyle\begin{gathered}D(L_{0},L_{1},L_{2})\times_{D(L_{0},L_{1})\times D(L_{1},L_{2})\times D(L_{0},L_{2})}(D(L_{0},L_{1},L_{2})\times_{D(L_{0},L_{1})}D^{\prime}(L_{0},L_{1})),\\
D(L_{0},L_{1},L_{2})\times_{D(L_{0},L_{1})\times D(L_{1},L_{2})\times D(L_{0},L_{2})}(D(L_{0},L_{1},L_{2})\times_{D(L_{1},L_{2})}D^{\prime}(L_{1},L_{2})),\\
D(L_{0},L_{1},L_{2})\times_{D(L_{0},L_{1})\times D(L_{1},L_{2})\times D(L_{0},L_{2})}(D(L_{0},L_{1},L_{2})\times_{D(L_{0},L_{2})}D^{\prime}(L_{0},L_{2})).\end{gathered}

(viii)

For every

\delta^{\prime}(L,K)\in D^{\prime}(L,K)

a chain map

\displaystyle\alpha_{\delta^{\prime}(L,K)}\colon CF(L,K)\to CF(L,K)

where the
domain
and
codomain
are equipped with the differentials associated with the image of

\delta^{\prime}(L,K)

under the above surjective map from

D^{\prime}(L,K)
.
For every

\delta^{\prime\prime}(L,K)\in D^{\prime\prime}(L,K)

a chain homotopy

\displaystyle\beta_{\delta^{\prime\prime}(L,K)}\colon CF(L,K)\to CF(L,K)[-1]

between an

\alpha

map
and
the composition of two

\alpha

maps associated with the image of

\delta^{\prime\prime}(L,K)

under the above surjective map from

D^{\prime\prime}(L,K)
.
For every

\delta^{\prime\prime\prime}(L_{0},L_{1},L_{2})\in D^{\prime\prime\prime}_{i}(L_{0},L_{1},L_{2})

a chain homotopy

\displaystyle\gamma_{\delta^{\prime\prime\prime}(L_{0},L_{1},L_{2})}\colon CF(L_{0},L_{1})\otimes CF(L_{1},L_{2})\to CF(L_{0},L_{2})[-1]

between a

\mu^{2}

map
and
the composition of an

\alpha

with a

\mu^{2}

map associated with the image of

\delta^{\prime\prime\prime}(L_{0},L_{1},L_{2})

under the above surjective map from

D^{\prime\prime\prime}(L_{0},L_{1},L_{2})
.

(ix)

A map

f\colon D(L,K)\to D^{\prime}(L,K)

whose composition with the above surjective map from

D^{\prime}(L,K)

is the diagonal
and
such that
every map

\alpha_{f(\delta(L,K))}

is the identity.

We denote by

\mathcal{F}^{\text{pre}}_{\mathcal{S}}

the
A_{\infty}
-pre-category specified by data
(i)-(iv)
and
(vi).
As mentioned in

[
GPS2
, Rem. 2.7]
,
data
(i), (ii), (iv)
correspond to the semisimplicial set from

[
GPS2
, Def. 2.6]
.
Note that
datum
(v)
allows one to construct a compatible collection of Floer data inductively.
The other data force all possibilities of such collection to define a single cohomology pre-category

H\mathcal{F}^{\text{pre}}_{\mathcal{S}}

up to unique equivalence.

(
[
GPS2

\mathcal{L}

\mathcal{S}

\mathcal{S}

\mathcal{S}

as follows.

\tilde{C}

of

For all

the

corresponding to

.

Definition 3.4

(
[
GPS2
, Def. 2.13]
)
.

The

wrapped Donaldson–Fukaya category

H\mathcal{W}_{\operatorname{DF},\mathcal{S},\tilde{C}}

of an abstract wrapped Floer setup

(\mathcal{S},H\mathcal{F}^{\text{env}}_{\mathcal{S}},\tilde{C},\{\tilde{R}_{L}\}_{L})

is the full subcategory of

\operatorname{Pro}H\mathcal{F}^{\text{env}}_{\mathcal{S}}

spanned by

\tilde{R}_{L}

for all

L\in\mathcal{L}
.
In other words,
the objects of

H\mathcal{W}_{\operatorname{DF},\mathcal{S},\tilde{C}}

are Lagrangians in

\mathcal{L}

from

\mathcal{S}

and
for every

L,K\in\mathcal{L}

the morphisms are

\displaystyle H\mathcal{W}_{\operatorname{DF},\mathcal{S},\tilde{C}}(L,K)=\varprojlim_{K^{w}\in\tilde{R}_{K}}\varinjlim_{L^{w}\in\tilde{R}_{L}}H\mathcal{F}^{\text{env}}_{\mathcal{S}}(L^{w},K^{w})=HW_{\mathcal{S},\tilde{C}}(L,K)

with the evident notion of composition.

There is a canonical functor

H\mathcal{F}^{\text{env}}_{\mathcal{S}}\to H\mathcal{W}_{\operatorname{DF},\mathcal{S},\tilde{C}}

induced by the maps

\displaystyle H\mathcal{F}^{\text{env}}_{\mathcal{S}}(L,K)\to H\mathcal{F}^{\text{env}}_{\mathcal{S}}(L^{w},K)\to HW_{\mathcal{S},\tilde{C}}(L,K)

for
all

L,K\in\mathcal{L}

and
any

L^{w}\in\tilde{R}_{L}
.
Since

HW_{\mathcal{S},\tilde{C}}(L,K)

satisfy the right locality property,
the maps are compatible with composition.

(
[
GPS2
, Lem. 2.14]
)
.

For any category

\mathcal{C}
,
the pullback along the canonical functor

\displaystyle\operatorname{Fun}(H\mathcal{W}_{\operatorname{DF},\mathcal{S},\tilde{C}},\mathcal{C})\to\operatorname{Fun}(H\mathcal{F}^{\text{env}}_{\mathcal{S}},\mathcal{C})

between ordinary functor categories
is fully faithful

3.3.
Universal decorated posets

Definition 3.6

(
[
GPS2
, Def. 2.8]
)
.

A

decorated poset

\eta\colon P\to\mathcal{F}^{\text{pre}}_{\mathcal{S}}

for an abstract Floer setup

\mathcal{S}

is a poset

P

together with a map

\eta

from the nerve of

P

to the semisimplicial set associated with

\mathcal{F}^{\text{pre}}_{\mathcal{S}}
.
The map

\eta

sends each totally ordered tuple

p_{k}>\cdots>p_{0}

in

P

to a pair of
a composable tuple

(L_{p_{k}},\ldots,L_{p_{0}})\in\mathcal{L}_{k}

and
an element

\eta(p_{k},\cdots,p_{0})\in D(L_{p_{k}},\ldots,L_{p_{0}})
.

In particular,

\eta

sends each element

p\in P

to a Lagrangian

L_{p}\in\mathcal{L}
.
We denote by

\mathcal{O}_{P}

the cofibrant strictly unital
A_{\infty}
-category
whose objects are elements

p\in P

and
for every

p,q\in P

whose morphisms are

\displaystyle\mathcal{O}_{P}(p,q)=\begin{cases}CF(L_{p},L_{q})&p>q,\\
\mathbb{Z}\langle 1_{p}\rangle&p=q,\\
0&\text{else}.\end{cases}

Here,
the
A_{\infty}
-operations for totally ordered tuples

p_{k}>\cdots>p_{0}

are

\mu^{k}_{\eta(p_{k},\ldots,p_{0})}

and
we require all

1_{p}

to be a strict unit.
Let

I_{P,\tilde{C}}

be the set of morphisms in

H^{0}\mathcal{O}_{P}

determined by

P

and
the set

\tilde{C}\subset H^{0}\mathcal{F}^{\text{env}}_{\mathcal{S}}

of continuation maps.
We denote by

\mathcal{W}_{P,\tilde{C}}

the localization

\mathcal{O}_{P}[I^{-1}_{P,\tilde{C}}]
.

Definition 3.7
.

A decorated poset

P

has

no duplicates

if

P^{\leq p},P^{\leq q}

are not isomorphic as decorated posets
whenever

p\neq q
.
An

inclusion

P\subset P^{\prime}

of decorated posets is an inclusion of underlying posets
such that
the decoration

\eta

on

P

is the restriction of that

\eta^{\prime}

on

P^{\prime}
.
It is

downward closed

if in addition

P^{\prime\leq p}=P^{\leq p}

for all

p\in P
.

Given any two decorated posets with no duplicates

P,P^{\prime}
,
there is at most one downward closed inclusion

P\subset P^{\prime}
.
Recall that
a poset

P

is

cofinite

if

P^{\leq p}

is finite for all

p\in P
.
Let

\operatorname{Pos}_{\mathcal{S}}

be the ordinary category
whose objects are countable cofinite decorated posets for

\mathcal{S}

with no duplicates
and
whose morphisms are downward closed inclusions.
In

[
GPS1
, Lem. 3.42]

Ganatra–Pardon–Shende introduced the universal decorated poset

\displaystyle\eta_{\text{univ}}(\mathcal{S})\colon P_{\text{univ}}(\mathcal{S})\to\mathcal{F}^{\text{pre}}_{\mathcal{S}}

which corresponds to the full subcategory of

\operatorname{Pos}_{\mathcal{S}}

spanned by objects of the form

P^{\leq p}
.
By construction for every

p\in P_{\text{univ}}(\mathcal{S})

there exists some

P^{\leq p}\in\operatorname{Pos}_{\mathcal{S}}

with

P_{\text{univ}}(\mathcal{S})^{\leq p}=P^{\leq p}
.
According to

[
GPS2
, Sec. 2.3]
,
each

p\in P_{\text{univ}}(\mathcal{S})

corresponds to an isomorphism class

[P^{\leq p}]

of countable cofinite decorated posets with no duplicates.
Moreover,
every

P\in\operatorname{Pos}_{\mathcal{S}}

admits a unique downward closed inclusion

P\subset P_{\text{univ}}(\mathcal{S})
.

Remark 3.8
.

Here,
the isomorphism class

[P^{\leq p}]

must mean that
any two representatives have isomorphic underlying posets each pair of
whose corresponding nerves map to the same image.
Otherwise,
the image of

\eta_{\text{univ}}(\mathcal{S})

would not cover
distinct Lagrangians

L,K\in\mathcal{L}

nor a composable tuple

(L_{0},\cdots,L_{k})\in\mathcal{L}_{k}

with distinct Floer data

\delta(L_{0},\ldots,L_{k}),\delta^{\prime}(L_{0},\ldots,L_{k})\in D(L_{0},\ldots,L_{k})
.
Note that
two distinctive decorated posets with a common underlying poset are not isomorphic in

\operatorname{Pos}_{\mathcal{S}}
,
as by definition of inclusions of decorated posets the restriction of the decoration on one must coincide with that on the other.

Caution 3.9
.

As mentioned above,
for any

p\in P_{\text{univ}}(\mathcal{S})

we have

P^{\leq p}_{\text{univ}}(\mathcal{S})=P^{\leq p}

for some

P^{\leq p}\in\operatorname{Pos}_{\mathcal{S}}
.
Let

P^{\leq p}_{\circ}\subset P^{\leq p}

be a decorated subposet obtained by removing one of the minimum elements,
which makes sense
as

P_{\text{univ}}(\mathcal{S})

is cofinite.
While the canonical inclusion

P^{\leq p}\subset P^{\leq p}_{\text{univ}}(\mathcal{S})

is downward closed,
the one

P^{\leq p}_{\circ}\subset P^{\leq p}_{\text{univ}}(\mathcal{S})

is not
since

P^{\leq p}_{\text{univ}}(\mathcal{S})

contains the removed element.

(

The

wrapped Fukaya category

of an abstract wrapped Floer setup

3.4.
Wrapping sequences

Definition 3.11

(
[
GPS2
, Def. 2.15]
)
.

Let

\eta\colon P\to\mathcal{F}^{\text{pre}}_{\mathcal{S}}

be a decorated poset for

\mathcal{S}
.
For an abstract wrapped Floer setup

(\mathcal{S},H\mathcal{F}^{\text{env}}_{\mathcal{S}},\tilde{C},\{\tilde{R}_{L}\}_{L})

a

P
-wrapping sequence

is a cofinal sequence

p_{0}<p_{1}<\cdots

in

P

along with elements of

I_{P,\tilde{C}}

in

H^{0}\mathcal{O}_{P}(p_{i+1},p_{i})=H^{0}\mathcal{F}^{\text{env}}_{\mathcal{S}}(L_{p_{i+1}},L_{p_{i}})

such that
the canonical map

\displaystyle\varinjlim_{i}H\mathcal{F}^{\text{env}}_{\mathcal{S}}(L_{p_{i}},K)\to\varinjlim_{i}HW_{\mathcal{S},\tilde{C}}(L_{p_{i}},K)=HW_{\mathcal{S},\tilde{C}}(L_{p_{0}},K)

is an isomorphism for every

K\in\mathcal{L}
.
A decorated poset

P

for

\mathcal{S}

is

sufficiently wrapped

with respect to

(\mathcal{S},H\mathcal{F}^{\text{env}}_{\mathcal{S}},\tilde{C},\{\tilde{R}_{L}\}_{L})

if every

p\in P

belongs to a
P
-wrapping sequence for

(\mathcal{S},H\mathcal{F}^{\text{env}}_{\mathcal{S}},\tilde{C},\{\tilde{R}_{L}\}_{L})
.

Given any isomorphism

L_{p}\xrightarrow{\sim}L_{q}

in

H\mathcal{W}_{\operatorname{DF},\mathcal{S},\tilde{C}}

and
a

P
-wrapping sequence

p=p_{0}<p_{1}<\cdots
,
there is an element of

I_{P,\tilde{C}}

in

H^{0}\mathcal{O}_{P}(p_{i},q)

for some

p_{i}\in P

which is sent to the isomorphism.
Hence

L_{p}\cong L_{q}

in

H\mathcal{W}_{\operatorname{DF},\mathcal{S},\tilde{C}}

implies that

p

belongs to a
P
-wrapping sequence for

(\mathcal{S},H\mathcal{F}^{\text{env}}_{\mathcal{S}},\tilde{C},\{\tilde{R}_{L}\}_{L})

if and only if
so does

q
.

Lemma 3.12

(
[
GPS2
, Lem. 2.16]
)
.

Let

p_{0}<p_{1}<\cdots

be any
P
-wrapping sequence for

(\mathcal{S},H\mathcal{F}^{\text{env}}_{\mathcal{S}},\tilde{C},\{\tilde{R}_{L}\}_{L})
.
Then the canonical map

\displaystyle\varinjlim_{i}H\mathcal{O}_{P}(p_{i},q)\to\varinjlim_{i}H\mathcal{W}_{P,\tilde{C}}(p_{i},q)

Lemma 3.14

(
[
GPS2
, Lem. 2.18]
)
.

Every countable cofinite decorated poset

P

for

\mathcal{S}

admits a downward closed inclusion into a countable cofinite decorated poset

P^{\prime}

which is sufficiently wrapped with respect to

(\mathcal{S},H\mathcal{F}^{\text{env}}_{\mathcal{S}},\tilde{C},\{\tilde{R}_{L}\}_{L})
.

Proof.

Let

P^{\prime}=P\sqcup\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}

be a countable cofinite poset defined as follows.
The ordering on

P

is the given one
and
that on

\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}

is the usual one.
Choose a sequence of finite downward closed subposets

S_{0}\subset S_{1}\subset\cdots P

whose union is

P
.
Add an ordering
such that
each

i\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}

is larger than all elements in

S_{i}
.
This additional ordering guarantees that

P^{\prime\leq i}

are finite for all

i\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}
.
For each

p\in P

choose a cofinal sequence

L^{0}_{p}\leadsto L^{1}_{p}\leadsto\cdots

in

\tilde{R}_{L_{p}}
.
Choose a map

\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}\to P

whose fiber

i_{p,0}<i_{p,1}<\cdots

over every

p\in P

is infinite.
Assign to

i_{p,k}

the Lagrangian

H^{k}_{p}

arising from a choice of factorization

L^{k}_{p}\leadsto H^{k}_{p}\leadsto L^{k+1}_{p}

in

\tilde{R}_{L_{p}}
.
One chooses these factorizations by induction on

i\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}
,
using datum
(iv)
in Definition

3.3

so that
all totally ordered tuples of

P^{\prime}

correspond to composable Lagrangians.
Now,
every

p\in S_{j}\subset P\subset P^{\prime}

belongs to a sequence

p<i_{p,k}<i_{p,k+1}<\cdots

in

P^{\prime}

for some

k\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}

with

j<i_{p,k}
,
which is cofinal.
Together with elements in

H^{0}\mathcal{O}_{P}(i_{p,k},p)=H^{0}\mathcal{F}^{\text{env}}_{\mathcal{S}}(H^{k}_{p},L_{p})

and

H^{0}\mathcal{O}_{P}(i_{p,k+1},i_{p,k})=H^{0}\mathcal{F}^{\text{env}}_{\mathcal{S}}(H^{k+1}_{p},H^{k}_{p})

associated with the sequence

L_{p}\leadsto H^{k}_{p}\leadsto H^{k+1}_{p}\leadsto\cdots

in

\tilde{R}_{L_{p}}
,
it defines a
P^{\prime}
-wrapping sequence.
Similarly,
every

i\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}

belongs to some

P^{\prime}
-wrapping sequence.
Finally,
choose additional Floer data for all totally ordered tuples of

P^{\prime}

by induction on the skeleta of the nerve of

P^{\prime}
.
∎

4.
Modified construction

In this section,
we modify the abstract wrapped Floer setups.
This amounts to specifying a suitable set of continuation maps.
After introducing the universal envelope,
we define the wrapped Fukaya category as its localization along the continuation maps.

4.1.
Axiomatic composability

Definition 4.1

(cf.
[
GPS2
, Def. 2.9]
)
.

A

weak abstract Floer setup

w\mathcal{S}

is an abstract Floer setup

\mathcal{S}

with its datum
(ii)
imposed additional axiomatic composability condition.
Namely,
we replace it with the following datum.

(ii)

Subsets

\mathcal{L}_{k}\subset\mathcal{L}^{k+1}

of

composable tuples

for integers

k\geq 1

which are closed under
passing to subsequences
and
permuting Lagrangians:
if

(L_{0},\ldots,L_{k})\in\mathcal{L}_{k}

then

(L_{i_{0}},\ldots,L_{i_{l}})\in\mathcal{L}_{l}

and

(L_{\sigma(0)},\ldots,L_{\sigma(k)})\in\mathcal{L}_{k}

for every

0\leq i_{0}<\cdots<i_{l}\leq k

and

\sigma\in\mathfrak{S}_{k}
.
Moreover,
every element

(L_{0},\ldots,L_{k})\in\mathcal{L}_{k}

consists of pairwise distinct Lagrangians.
In particular,

(L,L)\notin\mathcal{L}_{1}

for all

L\in\mathcal{L}
.

Remark 4.2
.

Different from the original definition,
here in datum
(ii)
we impose the additional conditions on the
closedness under permutations
and
pairwise distinction.
Geometrically,
a tuple

(L_{0},\ldots,L_{k})

is composable
if it is mutually transverse

[
GPS2
, Def. 2.25]
.
Hence our additional conditions should not affect the theory.

Remark 4.3
.

Perhaps one might wonder
why here we add the closedness under permutations,
as technically it will play almost no role in the sequel.
Moreover,
the original

\mathcal{S}

seems more flexible than our

w\mathcal{S}

in the sense that
one can simultaneously have

(L,K)\in\mathcal{L}_{1},(K,L)\notin\mathcal{L}_{1}

for some Lagrangians

L,K\in\mathcal{L}
,
although it is a geometrically imaginary situation.
However,
that actually means

\mathcal{S}

being more directed rather than flexible:
there are no nontrivial representatives of elements in

I_{P,\tilde{C}}(q,p)

for

p,q\in P

with

\xi(p)=L,\xi(q)=K

and
hence

I_{P,\tilde{C}}(q,p)=0

for all

P
,

H^{0}\mathcal{F}^{\text{env}}_{\mathcal{S}}

and

\tilde{C}
.
In particular,
since all morphisms between non-composable pairs (if exist) will be thrown away,
essentially the original

\tilde{C}

has more constraints than our

C

coming from the geometrically imaginary hypothesis

(L,K)\in\mathcal{L}_{1},(K,L)\notin\mathcal{L}_{1}

on

\mathcal{S}
.
We package the information on such directions into

C

together with
the irrelevant choices
and
the factorization axiom
which will be removed from the original

(\mathcal{S},H\mathcal{F}^{\text{env}}_{\mathcal{S}},\tilde{C},\{\tilde{R}_{L}\}_{L})
.

We denote by

\mathcal{F}^{\text{pre}}_{w\mathcal{S}}

the
A_{\infty}
-pre-category specified by data
(i)-(iv)
and
(vi)
in Definition

4.1
.
From

\mathcal{F}^{\text{pre}}_{w\mathcal{S}}

we obtain the

weak Donaldson–Fukaya pre-category

h\mathcal{F}^{\text{pre}}_{\operatorname{DF},w\mathcal{S}}

of

w\mathcal{S}

in the same way as Definition

3.2
.

4.2.
Canonical envelopes

Fix a compatible collection

\delta

of Floer data.
Specifically,
choose one element

\delta(L,K)\in D(L,K)

for each

(L,K)\in\mathcal{L}_{1}
.
By datum
(v)
in
Definition

one can find an element

-relation with

Definition 4.4
.

The

for

-category

whose objects are Lagrangians

and
for every

whose morphisms are

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}_{w\mathcal{S},\delta}(L,K)=\begin{cases}CF(L,K)&(L,K)\in\mathcal{L}_{1},\\
\mathbb{Z}\langle 1_{L}\rangle&L=K,\\

Here,
the

Requiring all

By

∎

From the above proof one sees that

for

Remark 4.6
.

In view of

4.2
,

sends

to a naive inclusion

\mathcal{W}_{E_{n},C}\hookrightarrow\mathcal{W}_{E_{n+1},C}
.

be any vertex

(v)
in Definition
