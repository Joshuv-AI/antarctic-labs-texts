40 C.F.R. § 149.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:40:149.1
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-149.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 149 (Sole Source Aquifers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this subpart is to provide criteria for identifying critical aquifer protection areas, pursuant to section 1427 of the Safe Drinking Water Act (SDWA).
