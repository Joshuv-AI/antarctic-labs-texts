40 C.F.R. § 45.145 — Allocability and allowability of costs.
Citation: us:cfr:40:45.145
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-45.145
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 45 (Training Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Allocability and allowability of costs will be determined in accordance with 2 CFR part 200, subpart E.
(b) Costs incurred for the purchase of land or the construction of buildings are not allowable.
