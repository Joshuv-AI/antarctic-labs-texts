40 C.F.R. § 415.656 — Pretreatment standards for new sources (PSNS).
Citation: us:cfr:40:415.656
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-415.656
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 415 (Inorganic Chemicals Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in 40 CFR 403.7, any new source subject to this subpart which introduces pollutants into a publicly owned treatment works must comply with 40 CFR part 403 and achieve the following pretreatment standards for new sources (PSNS): The limitations for cobalt (T), copper (T), and nickel (T), are the same as specified in § 415.654.
