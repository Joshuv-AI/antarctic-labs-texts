33 U.S.C. § 702a–6 — Drainage necessitated by floodway levees
Citation: us:usc:33:702a–6
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section702a–6
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 15 (Flood Control). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States shall provide the drainage made necessary by the construction of floodway levees included in the modified project.
