49 C.F.R. § 227.11 — Responsibility for compliance.
Citation: us:cfr:49:227.11
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-227.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 227 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Although the duties imposed by this part are generally stated in terms of the duty of a railroad, any person, including a contractor for a railroad, who performs any function covered by this part must perform that function in accordance with this part.
