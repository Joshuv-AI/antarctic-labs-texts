20 C.F.R. § 901.53 — Notice of suspension, termination or resignation of enrollment.
Citation: us:cfr:20:901.53
Creator: Joint Board For The Enrollment Of Actuaries
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-901.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 901 (Regulations Governing The Performance Of Actuarial Services Under The Employee Retirement Income Security Act Of 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon the resignation or the issuance of a final order suspending or terminating the enrollment of an actuary, the Executive Director shall give notice thereof to appropriate officers and employees of the Department of the Treasury, the Department of Labor, the Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation, and to other interested departments and agencies of the Federal Government.
