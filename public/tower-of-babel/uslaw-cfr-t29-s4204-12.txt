29 C.F.R. § 4204.12 — De minimis transactions.
Citation: us:cfr:29:4204.12
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4204.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4204 (Variances For Sale Of Assets). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The criterion under this section is that the amount of the bond or escrow does not exceed the lesser of $250,000 or two percent of the average total annual contributions made by all employers to the plan, for the purposes of section 304(b)(3)(A) of ERISA and section 431(b)(3)(A) of the Code, for the three most recent plan years ending before the date of determination.
