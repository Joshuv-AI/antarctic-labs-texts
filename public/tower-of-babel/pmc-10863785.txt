Correction to: Prognostic Utility of Cardiovascular Magnetic Resonance–Based Phenotyping in Patients With Muscular Dystrophy
Published: 2023

In the article by Niharika Kashyap et al, “Prognostic Utility of Cardiovascular Magnetic Resonance–Based Phenotyping in Patients With Muscular Dystrophy,” which published on November 6, 2023 (J Am Heart Assoc. 2023;12:e030229. DOI: 10.1161/JAHA.123.030229) and was included in the November 7, 2023 issue of the journal, a correction was needed.

The legend to Figure 3 was incorrect. It has been corrected to read as follows: “Regional American Heart Association 16‐segmental distribution of minimum principal strain amplitude values for representative subjects from the study cohort. Respective mean global values for a healthy control (HC) (A), patient with dystrophinopathy (Dys) (B), limb‐girdle muscular dystrophy (LGMD) (C), and type 1 myotonic dystrophy (DM1) (D) are −29.2%, −19.8%, −24.7%, and −26.8%.”

The authors regret the error.

The online version of the article has been updated and is available here: https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/10.1161/JAHA.123.030229.
