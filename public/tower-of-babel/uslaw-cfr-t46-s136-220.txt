46 C.F.R. § 136.220 — Posting.
Citation: us:cfr:46:136.220
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-136.220
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 136 (Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The original COI must be framed under glass or other transparent material and posted in a conspicuous place onboard the towing vessel.
(b) If posting is impracticable, the COI must be kept on board in a weathertight container and must be readily available.
