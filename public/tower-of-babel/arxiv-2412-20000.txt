Schouten like metrics on five dimensional nilpotents Lie groups.


Abstract
The prescribed Ricci curvature problem consists in finding a Riemannian metric g g to satisfy the equation R ​ i ​ c ​ ( g ) = T Ric(g)=T , for some fixed symmetric ( 0 , 2 ) (0,2) -tensor field T T on a differential manifold M M . In this paper, we define Schouten like metric as a particular solution of a prescribed Ricci curvature problem and we classify them on five dimensional nilpotent Lie groups by establishing a link with algebraic Schouten solitons. This also permit us to make a classification of five dimensional nilsoliton.
