19 C.F.R. § 122.91 — Application.
Citation: us:cfr:19:122.91
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-122.91
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 122 (Air Commerce Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to the transportation in bond of merchandise arriving in the U.S. by aircraft and entered:
(a) For immediate transportation to another airport without appraisement; or
(b) For transportation through the U.S. and later exportation by aircraft.
