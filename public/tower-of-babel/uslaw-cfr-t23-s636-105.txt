23 C.F.R. § 636.105 — Is the FHWA requiring the use of design-build?
Citation: us:cfr:23:636.105
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-636.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 636 (Design-Build Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No, the FHWA is neither requiring nor promoting the use of the design-build contracting method. The design-build contracting technique is optional.
