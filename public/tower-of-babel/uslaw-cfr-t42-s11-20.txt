42 C.F.R. § 11.20 — Who must submit clinical trial registration information?
Citation: us:cfr:42:11.20
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-11.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 11 (Clinical Trials Registration And Results Information Submission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The responsible party for an applicable clinical trial specified in § 11.22 must submit clinical trial registration information for that clinical trial.
