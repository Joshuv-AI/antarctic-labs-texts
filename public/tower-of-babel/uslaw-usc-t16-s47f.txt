16 U.S.C. § 47f — Inapplicability of certain laws to lands acquired under section 47e
Citation: us:usc:16:47f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section47f
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter VI (Sequoia And Yosemite National Parks). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of the Federal Power Act, as amended [16 U.S.C. 791a et seq.], shall not apply to any of the lands added to the Yosemite National Park pursuant to the provisions of section 47e of this title.
