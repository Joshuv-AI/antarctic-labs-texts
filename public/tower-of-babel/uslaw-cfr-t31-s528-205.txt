31 C.F.R. § 528.205 — Exempt transactions.
Citation: us:cfr:31:528.205
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-528.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 528 (International Criminal Court-Related Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) International Emergency Economic Powers Act. The prohibitions contained in this part do not apply to any transactions that are exempt pursuant to section 203(b) of the International Emergency Economic Powers Act (50 U.S.C. 1702(b)).
(b) Official business. The prohibitions contained in § 528.201(a) do not apply to transactions for the conduct of the official business of the United States government by employees, grantees, or contractors thereof.
