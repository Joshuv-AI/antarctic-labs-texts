9 C.F.R. § 98.12 — General prohibitions.
Citation: us:cfr:9:98.12
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-98.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 98 (Importation Of Certain Animal Embryos And Animal Semen). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Ruminant and swine embryos may not be imported from regions where foot-and-mouth disease exists except in accordance with this subpart.
(b) Ruminant and swine embryos may not be imported into the United States from any region other than the region in which they were conceived and collected.
