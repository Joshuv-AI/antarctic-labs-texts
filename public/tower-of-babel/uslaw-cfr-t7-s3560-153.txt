7 C.F.R. § 3560.153 — Calculation of household income and assets.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3560.153
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3560.153
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3560 (Direct Multi-Family Housing Loans And Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Annual income will be calculated in accordance with 24 CFR 5.609(a) and (b).
(b) Adjusted income will be calculated in accordance with 24 CFR 5.611.
(c) Net family assets will be calculated in accordance with 24 CFR 5.603(b).
