26 U.S.C. § 5243 — Sale of abandoned spirits for denaturation without collection of tax
Citation: us:usc:26:5243
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section5243
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 51 (Distilled Spirits, Wines, And Beer); Subchapter C (Operation Of Distilled Spirits Plants); Part II (Operations On Bonded Premises); Subpart D (Denaturation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, any distilled spirits abandoned to the United States may be sold, in such cases as the Secretary may by regulation provide, to the proprietor of any distilled spirits plant for denaturation, or redistillation and denaturation, without the payment of the internal revenue tax thereon.
