33 C.F.R. § 150.540 — What are the restrictions on fueling aircraft?
Citation: us:cfr:33:150.540
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-150.540
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 150 (Deepwater Ports: Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the deepwater port is not equipped with a permanent fueling facility, the Sector Commander, or the MSU Commander with COTP and OCMI authority's approval is necessary before aircraft may be fueled at the port.
