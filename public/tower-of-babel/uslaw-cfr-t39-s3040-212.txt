39 C.F.R. § 3040.212 — Limitations applicable to competitive mail matter.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3040.212
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3040.212
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3040 (Product Lists And The Mail Classification Schedule). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Postal Service shall notify the Commission of updates to size and weight limitations for competitive mail matter pursuant to subpart E of this part.
