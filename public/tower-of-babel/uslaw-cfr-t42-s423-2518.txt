42 C.F.R. § 423.2518 — Intermediate sanctions for the LI NET sponsor.
Citation: us:cfr:42:423.2518
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-423.2518
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 423 (Voluntary Medicare Prescription Drug Benefit). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the event it is determined that the LI NET sponsor violated its contract, CMS may impose intermediate sanctions as outlined in subpart O of this part.
