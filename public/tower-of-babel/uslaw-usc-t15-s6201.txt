15 U.S.C. § 6201 — Disclosure to foreign antitrust authority of antitrust evidence
Citation: us:usc:15:6201
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section6201
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 88 (International Antitrust Enforcement Assistance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In accordance with an antitrust mutual assistance agreement in effect under this chapter, subject to section 6207 of this title, and except as provided in section 6204 of this title, the Attorney General of the United States and the Federal Trade Commission may provide to a foreign antitrust authority with respect to which such agreement is in effect under this chapter, antitrust evidence to assist the foreign antitrust authority—
(1)
in determining whether a person has violated or is about to violate any of the foreign antitrust laws administered or enforced by the foreign antitrust authority, or
(2)
in enforcing any of such foreign antitrust laws.
