18 U.S.C. § 4103 — Applicability of United States laws
Citation: us:usc:18:4103
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section4103
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part III (Prisons And Prisoners); Chapter 306 (Transfer To Or From Foreign Countries). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All laws of the United States, as appropriate, pertaining to prisoners, probationers, parolees, and juvenile offenders shall be applicable to offenders transferred to the United States, unless a treaty or this chapter provides otherwise.
