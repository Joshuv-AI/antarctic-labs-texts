Correction: Multimodal sequencing identifies synergistic mechanisms driving resistance to neoadjuvant nivolumab treatment in hepatocellular carcinoma
Authors: Fanhong Zeng, Jiaxin Guo, Zheng Zeng, Vanilla Xin Zhang, Yu-Man Tsui, Po-Man Li, Abdullah Husain, Qingyang Zhang, Joyce Man-Fong Lee, Tan-To Cheung, Daniel Wai-Hung Ho, Irene Oi-Lin Ng
Published: 2026

Correction: Mol Cancer 25, 190 (2026)

https://doi.org/10.1186/s12943-026-02682-x

Following publication of the original article [1], the authors reported that during their review, they found that the supplementary figures and legends are missing from the online version, and that several figure legends, one cell-line designation in Table 3, and four abbreviation entries require correction.

The requested changes are listed below.
