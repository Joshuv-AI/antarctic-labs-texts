Abstract
. For an integer m ≥ 2 m\geq 2 , we aim to investigate the realizability of types of metacyclic-nonmodular groups, whose abelianization is ℤ / 2 ​ ℤ × ℤ / 2 m ​ ℤ \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}\times\mathbb{Z}/2^{m}\mathbb{Z} , as the Galois group of the maximal unramified 2 2 -extension (resp. pro- 2 2 -extension) of certain number fields of 2 2 -power degree (resp. cyclotomic ℤ 2 \mathbb{Z}_{2} -extensions). Furthermore, we show up some new techniques for studying Greenberg’s conjecture for some number fields. In particular, the reader can find results concerning the real quadratic fields F = ℚ ⁡ ( η ​ q ​ r ​ s ) F=\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{\eta qrs}) , the real biquadratic fields K = ℚ ⁡ ( η ​ q , r ​ s ) K=\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{\eta q},\sqrt{rs}) , with η ∈ { 1 , 2 } \eta\in\{1,2\} , and the Fröhlich multiquadratic fields of the form 𝔽 = ℚ ⁡ ( q , r , s ) \mathbb{F}=\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{q},\sqrt{r},\sqrt{s}) , where q q , r r and s s are odd primes numbers.

Lemma 2.4

To compute the unit index

is generated by the elements of

,

and

Lemma 3.2
.

Let
q\equiv 3\pmod{4}
and
r\equiv s\equiv 5\pmod{8}
be prime numbers such that
\left(\frac{q}{r}\right)=\left(\frac{q}{s}\right)=(-1)^{\delta_{\eta,2}}
and

\left(\frac{r}{s}\right)=1
. Let
\rho\in\{1,2\}
be such that
\rho\not=\eta
.

1)

Let
a
and
b
be the integers such that

\varepsilon_{\eta qrs}=\gamma+\gamma^{\prime}\sqrt{\eta qrs}
. Then out of the three numbers
2^{\delta_{\eta,1}}q(\gamma-1)
,
2r(\gamma+(-1)^{\delta_{\eta,2}})
and
2s(\gamma+(-1)^{\delta_{\eta,2}})
, exactly one of them is a square in
\mathbb{N}
. Moreover, we have:

a)

If
2^{\delta_{\eta,1}}q(\gamma-1)
is a square in
\mathbb{N}
, then
\sqrt{\eta\varepsilon_{\eta qrs}}=\gamma_{1}\sqrt{q}+\gamma_{2}\sqrt{\eta rs}\text{ and }\eta=-q\gamma_{1}^{2}+\eta rs\gamma_{2}^{2},

b)

If
2r(\gamma+(-1)^{\delta_{\eta,2}})
is a square in
\mathbb{N}
, then
\sqrt{\eta\varepsilon_{\eta qrs}}=\gamma_{1}\sqrt{\eta r}+\gamma_{2}\sqrt{qs}
and
\eta=(-1)^{\delta_{\eta,2}}\eta r\gamma_{1}^{2}+(-1)^{\delta_{\eta,1}}qs\gamma_{2}^{2},

c)

If
2s(\gamma+(-1)^{\delta_{\eta,2}})
is a square in
\mathbb{N}
, then
\sqrt{\eta\varepsilon_{\eta qrs}}=\gamma_{1}\sqrt{\eta s}+\gamma_{2}\sqrt{qr}
and
\eta=(-1)^{\delta_{\eta,2}}\eta s\gamma_{1}^{2}+(-1)^{\delta_{\eta,1}}qr\gamma_{2}^{2}

Here
\gamma_{1}
and
\gamma_{2}
are two integers.

2)

Let

and

be the integers such that

\varepsilon_{\rho qrs}=x+y\sqrt{\rho qrs}

\mathbb{N}
. Moreover, we have:

a)

If

is a square in
\mathbb{N}
, then
\sqrt{2\varepsilon_{\rho qrs}}=y_{1}\sqrt{\rho q}+y_{2}\sqrt{rs}\text{ and }2=\rho qy_{1}^{2}-rsy_{2}^{2}.

b)

If
2^{{\delta_{\rho,2}}}q(x-1)
is a square in
\mathbb{N}
, then
\sqrt{2\varepsilon_{\rho qrs}}=y_{1}\sqrt{\rho q}+y_{2}\sqrt{rs}\text{ and }2=-\rho qy_{1}^{2}+rsy_{2}^{2}.

Here
y_{1}
and
y_{2}
are two integers.

Proof.

Let us first assume that
\eta=1

(
i.e.
\rho=2)
.

1)

As
N(\varepsilon_{qrs})=1
, by the unique factorization of
\gamma^{2}-1=qrs\gamma^{\prime 2}
in
\mathbb{Z}
, and Lemmas
3.1
, there exist
\gamma_{1}
and
\gamma_{2}
in
\mathbb{Z}
such that we have one of the following systems:

(1):\ \left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\gamma\pm 1=2q\gamma_{1}^{2}\\
\gamma\mp 1=2rs\gamma_{2}^{2},\end{array}\right.\quad(2):\ \left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\gamma\pm 1=2r\gamma_{1}^{2}\\
\gamma\mp 1=2qs\gamma_{2}^{2},\end{array}\right.\quad(3):\ \left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\gamma\pm 1=2s\gamma_{1}^{2}\\
\gamma\mp 1=2qr\gamma_{2}^{2},\end{array}\right.

(4):\ \left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\gamma\pm 1=q\gamma_{1}^{2}\\
\gamma\mp 1=rs\gamma_{2}^{2},\end{array}\right.\quad(5):\ \left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\gamma\pm 1=r\gamma_{1}^{2}\\
\gamma\mp 1=qs\gamma_{2}^{2}\end{array}\right.\text{ or }(6):\ \left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\gamma\pm 1=s\gamma_{1}^{2}\\
\gamma\mp 1=qr\gamma_{2}^{2}.\end{array}\right.

\bullet

Assume that the system
\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\gamma+1=2q\gamma_{1}^{2}\\
\gamma-1=2rs\gamma_{2}^{2},\end{array}\right.
holds. We have:

\left(\dfrac{2}{q}\right)=\left(\dfrac{2rs\gamma_{1}^{2}}{q}\right)=\left(\dfrac{\gamma-1}{q}\right)=\left(\dfrac{\gamma+1-2}{q}\right)=\left(\dfrac{2q\gamma_{1}^{2}-2}{q}\right)=\left(\dfrac{-2}{q}\right)=-\left(\dfrac{2}{q}\right),

which is absurd. So this system can not hold.

\bullet

Assume that the system
\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\gamma\pm 1=q\gamma_{1}^{2}\\
\gamma\mp 1=rs\gamma_{2}^{2}\end{array}\right.
holds. We have:

1=\left(\dfrac{q\gamma_{1}^{2}}{s}\right)=\left(\dfrac{\gamma\pm 1}{s}\right)=\left(\dfrac{\gamma\mp 1\pm 2}{s}\right)=\left(\dfrac{2}{s}\right)=-1,

which is absurd. So this system also can not hold.

We similarly eliminate all systems except the following:

(1):\ \left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\gamma-1=2q\gamma_{1}^{2}\\
\gamma+1=2rs\gamma_{2}^{2},\end{array}\right.\quad\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\gamma+1=2r\gamma_{1}^{2}\\
\gamma-1=2qs\gamma_{2}^{2}\end{array}\right.\quad\text{ and }\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\gamma+1=2s\gamma_{1}^{2}\\
\gamma-1=2qr\gamma_{2}^{2}.\end{array}\right.

By subtracting and summing the equations of each system, we get the result of our first item for
\eta=1
.

2)

For the second item, we proceed similarly and eliminate all systems except
\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}x\pm 1=2qy_{1}^{2}\\
x\mp 1=rsy_{2}^{2},\end{array}\right.
with
y_{1}
and
y_{2}
are two integers such that
y=y_{1}y_{2}
. So gives the second item for
\eta=1

(
i.e.
\rho=2)
.

The proof is analogous for the case
\eta=2
.
