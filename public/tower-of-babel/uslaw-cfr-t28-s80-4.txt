28 C.F.R. § 80.4 — Issuer or domestic concern.
Citation: us:cfr:28:80.4
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-80.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 80 (Foreign Corrupt Practices Act Opinion Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The request must be submitted by an issuer or domestic concern within the meaning of 15 U.S.C. 78dd-1 and 78dd-2, respectively, that is also a party to the transaction which is the subject of the request.
