21 C.F.R. § 112.180 — Under what circumstances may FDA modify or revoke an approved variance?
Citation: us:cfr:21:112.180
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-112.180
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 112 (Standards For The Growing, Harvesting, Packing, And Holding Of Produce For Human Consumption). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We may modify or revoke a variance if we determine that such variance is not reasonably likely to ensure that the produce is not adulterated under section 402 of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act and to provide the same level of public health protection as the requirements of this part.
