17 C.F.R. § 239.20 — Form S-20, for standardized options.
Citation: us:cfr:17:239.20
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-239.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 239 (Forms Prescribed Under The Securities Act Of 1933). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This form may be used to register standardized options under the Securities Act of 1933 where the issuer undertakes not to issue, clear, guarantee or accept an option registered on Form S-20 unless there is a definitive options disclosure document meeting the requirements of Rule 9b-1 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934.
