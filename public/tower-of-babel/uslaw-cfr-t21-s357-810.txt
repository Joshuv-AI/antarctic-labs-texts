21 C.F.R. § 357.810 — Active ingredients for deodorant drug products for internal use.
Citation: us:cfr:21:357.810
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-357.810
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 357 (Miscellaneous Internal Drug Products For Over-The-Counter Human Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The active ingredient of the product consists of either of the following when used within the dosage limits established for each ingredient in § 357.850(d):
(a) Bismuth subgallate.
(b) Chlorophyllin copper complex.
