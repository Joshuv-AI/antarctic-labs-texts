45 C.F.R. § 50.8 — Compliance.
Citation: us:cfr:45:50.8
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-50.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 50 (U.S. Exchange Visitor Program—Request For Waiver Of The Two-Year Foreign Residence Requirement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an alien physician acquires H-1B nonimmigrant status following approval by the INS of a request for waiver, then he or she becomes subject not only to the terms and conditions of the waiver, but also the terms and conditions of the H-1B nonimmigrant status. Failure to comply with those conditions will make that physician subject to removal from the United States by the INS.
