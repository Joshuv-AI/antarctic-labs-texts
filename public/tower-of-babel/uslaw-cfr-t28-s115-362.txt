28 C.F.R. § 115.362 — Agency protection duties.
Citation: us:cfr:28:115.362
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-115.362
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 115 (Prison Rape Elimination Act National Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When an agency learns that a resident is subject to a substantial risk of imminent sexual abuse, it shall take immediate action to protect the resident.
