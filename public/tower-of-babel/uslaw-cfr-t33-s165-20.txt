33 C.F.R. § 165.20 — Safety zones.
Citation: us:cfr:33:165.20
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-165.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 165 (Regulated Navigation Areas And Limited Access Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Safety Zone is a water area, shore area, or water and shore area to which, for safety or environmental purposes, access is limited to authorized persons, vehicles, or vessels. It may be stationary and described by fixed limits or it may be described as a zone around a vessel in motion.
