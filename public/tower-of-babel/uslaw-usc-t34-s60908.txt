34 U.S.C. § 60908 — Withholding information
Citation: us:usc:34:60908
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section60908
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 609 (Homicide Victims’ Families’ Rights). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall require an agency to provide information that would endanger the safety of any person, unreasonably impede an ongoing investigation, violate a court order, or violate legal obligations regarding privacy.
