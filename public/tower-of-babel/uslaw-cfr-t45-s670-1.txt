45 C.F.R. § 670.1 — Purpose of regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:45:670.1
Creator: National Science Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-670.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 670 (Conservation Of Antarctic Animals And Plants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of the regulations in this part is to conserve and protect the native mammals, birds, plants, and invertebrates of Antarctica and the ecosystem upon which they depend and to implement the Antarctic Conservation Act of 1978, Public Law 95-541, as amended by the Antarctic Science, Tourism, and Conservation Act of 1996, Public Law 104-227.
