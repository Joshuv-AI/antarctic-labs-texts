14 C.F.R. § 374a.5 — a.5 Exemption authority.
Citation: us:cfr:14:374a.5
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-374a.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 374a (PART 374a—EXTENSION OF CREDIT BY AIRLINES TO FEDERAL POLITICAL CANDIDATES). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Air carriers are exempt from the following provisions of 49 U.S.C. Subtitle VII:
(a) Section 41510.
(b) Section 41310, and any and all other provisions of 49 U.S.C. Subtitle VII, to the extent necessary to enable air carriers to comply with the provisions of this part.
