16 U.S.C. § 3912 — Transfers to Migratory Bird Conservation Fund
Citation: us:usc:16:3912
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section3912
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 59 (Wetlands Resources); Subchapter II (Revenues For Refuge Operations And The Migratory Bird Conservation Fund). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, an amount equal to the amount of all import duties collected on arms and ammunition, as specified in chapter 93 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States, shall, beginning with the next fiscal year quarter after November 10, 1986, be paid quarterly into the migratory bird conservation fund 1 established under section 718d of this title.
