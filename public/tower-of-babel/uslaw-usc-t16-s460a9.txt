16 U.S.C. § 460a–9 — Part of Blue Ridge Parkway; administration and maintenance of parkway extension
Citation: us:usc:16:460a–9
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section460a–9
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LXV (National Parkways). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The parkway extension herein authorized shall be a part of the Blue Ridge Parkway and shall be administered and maintained by the Secretary of the Interior in accordance with the laws and regulations applicable thereto, including section 460a–4 of this title.
