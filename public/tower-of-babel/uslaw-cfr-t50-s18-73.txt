50 C.F.R. § 18.73 — Burden of proof.
Citation: us:cfr:50:18.73
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-18.73
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 18 (Marine Mammals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The proponents of the proposed regulations and waiver must demonstrate that any taking or importation of any marine mammal under such proposed regulations and waiver would be consistent with the act.
