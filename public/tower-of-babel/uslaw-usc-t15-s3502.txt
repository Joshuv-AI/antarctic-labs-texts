15 U.S.C. § 3502 — Price fixing agreements, horizontal restraints of trade, or group boycotts
Citation: us:usc:15:3502
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section3502
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 61 (Soft Drink Interbrand Competition). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to legalize the enforcement of provisions described in section 3501 of this title in trademark licensing contracts or agreements described in that section by means of price fixing agreements, horizontal restraints of trade, or group boycotts, if such agreements, restraints, or boycotts would otherwise be unlawful.
