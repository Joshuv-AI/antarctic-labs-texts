43 C.F.R. § 4.1393 — Status of decision pending administrative review.
Citation: us:cfr:43:4.1393
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4.1393
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4 (Department Of The Interior Hearings And Appeals Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Determinations of the Office of Surface Mining under 30 U.S.C. 1272(e) will not be effective during the time in which a person or entity adversely affected may file a notice of appeal. When the public interest requires or to protect trust resources, however, the Board may provide that a decision, or any part of a decision, will be effective immediately.
