16 C.F.R. § 1702.5 — Failure to supply adverse information.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1702.5
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1702.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1702 (Petitions For Exemptions From Poison Prevention Packaging Act Requirements; Petition Procedures And Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Failure to obtain and provide the Commission with all reasonably available information that the petitioner knows is unfavorable or could reasonably expect to be unfavorable to the petition shall result in the denial of the petition.
