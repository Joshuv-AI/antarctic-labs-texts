5 C.F.R. § 3801.105 — Personal use of Government property.
Citation: us:cfr:5:3801.105
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-3801.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 3801 (Supplemental Standards Of Ethical Conduct For Employees Of The Department Of Justice). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees are prohibited by part 2635 of this title from using Government property for other than authorized purposes. The Department rule authorizing limited personal use of Department of Justice office and library equipment and facilities by its employees is at 28 CFR 45.4.
