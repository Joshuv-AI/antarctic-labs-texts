42 U.S.C. § 6864e — Hiring
Citation: us:usc:42:6864e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section6864e
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 81 (Energy Conservation And Resource Renewal); Subchapter III (Energy Conservation And Renewable-Resource Assistance For Existing Buildings); Part A (Weatherization Assistance For Low-Income Persons). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may, as the Secretary determines appropriate, encourage entities receiving funding from the Federal Government or from a State through a weatherization program under section 6863 of this title or section 6864 of this title, to prioritize the hiring and retention of employees who are individuals described in section 6864d(a)(5) of this title.
