31 C.F.R. § 601.3 — Distinctive paper.
Citation: us:cfr:31:601.3
Creator: Bureau Of Engraving And Printing, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-601.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 601 (Distinctive Paper And Distinctive Counterfeit Deterrents For United States Federal Reserve Notes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The distinctive paper is a cream-white currency note paper with fibers, colored red and blue, evenly distributed throughout the currency note paper. The distinctive paper shall contain distinctive counterfeit deterrents in the currency note paper denominations prescribed by the Secretary of the Treasury.
