16 C.F.R. § 24.1 — Deception (general).
Citation: us:cfr:16:24.1
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-24.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 24 (Guides For Select Leather And Imitation Leather Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is unfair or deceptive to misrepresent, directly or by implication, the kind, grade, quality, quantity, material content, thickness, finish, serviceability, durability, price, origin, size, weight, ease of cleaning, construction, manufacture, processing, distribution, or any other material aspect of an industry product.
