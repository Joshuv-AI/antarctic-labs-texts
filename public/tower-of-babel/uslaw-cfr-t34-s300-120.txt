34 C.F.R. § 300.120 — Monitoring activities.
Citation: us:cfr:34:300.120
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-300.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 300 (Assistance To States For The Education Of Children With Disabilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The SEA must carry out activities to ensure that § 300.114 is implemented by each public agency.
(b) If there is evidence that a public agency makes placements that are inconsistent with § 300.114, the SEA must—
(1) Review the public agency's justification for its actions; and
(2) Assist in planning and implementing any necessary corrective action.
