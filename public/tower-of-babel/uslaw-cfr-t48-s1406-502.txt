48 C.F.R. § 1406.502 — 1406.502 Duties and responsibilities.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1406.502
Creator: Chapter 14—Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1406.502
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1406 (Competition Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

PAM is responsible for preparing and submitting the annual report required by FAR 6.502(b)(2). Bureau Competition Advocates shall furnish certain information, as may be required, to assist PAM in preparing the report.
