41 C.F.R. § 102-38.90 — -38.90 Sales contract award recipient.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-38.90
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-38.90
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-38 (38—Sale Of Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You will award the sales contract to the bidder with the highest responsive bid, unless a determination is made to reject the bid.
