6 U.S.C. § 256 — Sense of Congress regarding construction of fencing near San Diego, California
Citation: us:usc:6:256
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section256
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 1 (Homeland Security Organization); Subchapter IV (Border, Maritime, And Transportation Security); Part D (Immigration Enforcement Functions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the sense of the Congress that completing the 14-mile border fence project required to be carried out under section 102(b) of the Illegal Immigration Reform and Immigrant Responsibility Act of 1996 (8 U.S.C. 1103 note) should be a priority for the Secretary.
