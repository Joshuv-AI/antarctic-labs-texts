42 U.S.C. § 11505 — Coordination with CDBG and UDAG programs
Citation: us:usc:42:11505
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section11505
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 120 (Enterprise Zone Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the policy of the Congress that amounts provided under the community development block grant and urban development action grant programs under title I of the Housing and Community Development Act of 1974 [42 U.S.C. 5301 et seq.] shall not be reduced in any fiscal year in which the provisions of this chapter are in effect.
