41 C.F.R. § 302-10.400 — -10.400 Establishment of policies for authorizing transportation of a mobile home.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-10.400
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-10.400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-10 (10—Allowances For Transportation Of Mobile Homes And Boats Used As A Primary Residence). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies must establish policies for authorizing transportation of a mobile home that implements this part including when:
(a) It is considered in the best interest of the Government to assume direct responsibility for preparing and transporting an employee's mobile home; and
(b) To authorize an advance of funds for a commercial carrier transporting an employee's mobile home based on constructive or estimated cost when the employee assumes direct responsibility for payment.
