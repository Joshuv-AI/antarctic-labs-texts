5 C.F.R. § 302.203 — Standard and criteria for determining fitness for employment in an Excepted Service position.
Citation: us:cfr:5:302.203
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-302.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 302 (Employment In The Excepted Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The minimum standard and criteria for determining fitness for employment based on character and conduct are prescribed in part 731, subpart B, of this chapter.
(b) Agencies may prescribe additional factors to protect the integrity and promote the efficiency of the service when job-related and consistent with business necessity.
