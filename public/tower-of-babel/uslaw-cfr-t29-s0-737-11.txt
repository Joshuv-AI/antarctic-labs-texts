29 C.F.R. § 0.737-11 — -11 Judicial review.
Citation: us:cfr:29:0.737-11
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-0.737-11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 0 (Ethics And Conduct Of Department Of Labor Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person found to have participated in a violation of 18 U.S.C. 207(a), (b), or (c) or the regulations at 5 CFR part 737 may seek judicial review of the administrative determination in an appropriate United States district court.
