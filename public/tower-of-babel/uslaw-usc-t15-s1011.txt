15 U.S.C. § 1011 — Declaration of policy
Citation: us:usc:15:1011
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1011
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 20 (Regulation Of Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Congress hereby declares that the continued regulation and taxation by the several States of the business of insurance is in the public interest, and that silence on the part of the Congress shall not be construed to impose any barrier to the regulation or taxation of such business by the several States.
