5 C.F.R. § 9301.5 — Accessing records without request
Citation: us:cfr:5:9301.5
Creator: Special Inspector General For Afghanistan Reconstruction
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-9301.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 9301 (Disclosure Of Records And Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Certain SIGAR records, including the agency's Quarterly Report, audit reports, testimony, oversight plans, press releases, other public issuances, and records that are required by 5 U.S.C. 552(a)(2) to be made publicly available are available electronically from SIGAR's homepage at http://www.sigar.mil. SIGAR encourages requesters to visit its Web site before making a request for records under § 9301.6.
