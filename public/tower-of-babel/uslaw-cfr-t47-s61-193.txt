47 C.F.R. § 61.193 — Vacation of suspension order; supplements announcing same; etc.
Citation: us:cfr:47:61.193
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-61.193
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 61 (Tariffs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Commission vacates a suspension order, the affected carrier must issue a supplement or revised page stating the Commission's action as well as the lawful schedules.
