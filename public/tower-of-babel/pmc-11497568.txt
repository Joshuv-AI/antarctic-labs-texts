Professor Dr. Aishah Adam: a legacy of excellence
Authors: Nor Amlizan Ramli, Mizaton Hazizul Hasan
Published: 2024

Professor Dr. Aishah Adam, a distinguished scholar and leader in pharmaceutical education and research, passed away on 22 September 2024. Her untimely departure has left a profound void in the Faculty of Pharmacy at Universiti Teknologi MARA (UiTM) and the wider pharmacy community of Malaysia.

Prof. Aishah began her academic journey at Tunku Kurshiah College, where she demonstrated early academic promise. She subsequently earned a Bachelor of Pharmacy (Hons) from Zagazig University, Egypt, and a PhD in Biochemical Toxicology from London University.

Prof. Aishah’s contributions to pharmacy education were significant. She was instrumental in establishing the Bachelor of Pharmacy program at Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia and subsequently served as Dean of the Faculty of Pharmacy at UiTM for a decade. Her dedication to advancing pharmacy education was evident in her continuous efforts to develop and improve the curriculum.

In addition to her academic pursuits, Prof. Aishah was a passionate researcher. She secured substantial funding for various research projects, supervised numerous postgraduate students, and mentored countless scientists and academics. Her research in oxidative stress, antioxidants, efficacy, and safety studies was highly regarded, and she authored numerous peer-reviewed journal articles and presented her findings at conferences and seminars.

Prof Aishah played a vital role in fostering academic rigor by serving as external examiner across several institutions and reviewer of multiple publications.

Beyond her academic achievements, Prof. Aishah was a respected and admired leader. Her approach, described as both strict and motherly, fostered respect and admiration among students and colleagues alike. She was known for her kindness, compassion, and willingness to help others.

Prof. Aishah’s passing is an immeasurable loss to all who knew her, but her legacy will live on through the countless lives she touched. We extend our heartfelt sympathy to her loved ones. May her soul rest in peace.

Al Fatihah.
