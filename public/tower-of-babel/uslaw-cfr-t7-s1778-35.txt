7 C.F.R. § 1778.35 — Subsequent grants.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1778.35
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1778.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1778 (Emergency And Imminent Community Water Assistance Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subsequent grants will be processed in accordance with the requirements set forth in this part. The initial and subsequent grants made to complete a previously approved project must comply with the maximum grant requirements set forth in § 1778.11.
