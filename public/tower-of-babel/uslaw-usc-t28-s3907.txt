28 U.S.C. § 3907 — Other judicial review prohibited
Citation: us:usc:28:3907
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section3907
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part VI (Particular Proceedings); Chapter 179 (Judicial Review Of Certain Actions By Presidential Offices). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as expressly authorized by this chapter and chapter 5 of title 3, the compliance or noncompliance with the provisions of chapter 5 of title 3, and any action taken pursuant to chapter 5 of title 3, shall not be subject to judicial review.
