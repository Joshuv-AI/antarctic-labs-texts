45 C.F.R. § 156.255 — Rating variations.
Citation: us:cfr:45:156.255
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-156.255
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 156 (Health Insurance Issuer Standards Under The Affordable Care Act, Including Standards Related To Exchanges). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Rating areas. A QHP issuer, including an issuer of a multi-State plan, may vary premiums by the geographic rating area established under section 2701(a)(2) of the PHS Act.
(b) Same premium rates. A QHP issuer must charge the same premium rate without regard to whether the plan is offered through an Exchange, or whether the plan is offered directly from the issuer or through an agent.
