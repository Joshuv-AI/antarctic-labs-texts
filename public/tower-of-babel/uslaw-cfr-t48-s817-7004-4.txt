48 C.F.R. § 817.7004-4 — 817.7004-4 Final price negotiation—profit.

Creator: Chapter 8—Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 817 (Special Contracting Methods). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before the final price of a UCA is negotiated, contracting officers shall ensure the profit agreed to and documented in the contract negotiation memorandum reflects consideration of any risks incurred in performance of the work under the UCA.
