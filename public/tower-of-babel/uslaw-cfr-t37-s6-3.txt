37 C.F.R. § 6.3 — Schedule for certification marks.
Citation: us:cfr:37:6.3
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-6.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 6 (Classification Of Goods And Services Under The Trademark Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In applications for registration of certification marks based on sections 1 and 44 of the Trademark Act and registrations resulting from such applications, goods and services are classified in two classes as follows:
A. Goods.
B. Services.
