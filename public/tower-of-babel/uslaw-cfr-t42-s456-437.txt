42 C.F.R. § 456.437 — Notification of adverse decision.
Citation: us:cfr:42:456.437
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-456.437
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 456 (Utilization Control). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The UR plan must provide that written notice of any adverse final decision on the need for continued stay under § 456.436 (g) through (i) is sent to—
(a) The ICF administrator;
(b) The attending physician;
(c) The qualified Intellectual Disability professional, if applicable;
(d) The Medicaid agency;
(e) The beneficiary; and
(f) If possible, the next of kin or sponsor.
