Correction: Efficacy of the preformulated irrigation solution Bactisure® in acute periprosthetic joint infection debridement surgery: study protocol for a randomized controlled trial
Authors: Rafael Oleo-Taltavull, Matías Vicente Gomà-Camps, Nayana Joshi Jubert, Pablo S Corona
Published: 2024

Correction: Trials 25, 775 (2024)

https://doi.org/10.1186/s13063-024–08637-1

Following the publication of the original article [1], we were notified an error in the Methods section of the abstract.

The text originally stated: “Prospective single-centre randomized controlled trial involving patients with acute haematogenous PJI who received standard DAIR surgery from December 2022 to December 2024.”

The corrected version should read: “Prospective single-centre randomized controlled trial involving patients with acute or haematogenous PJI who received standard DAIR surgery from December 2022 to December 2024.”

The same omission occured on page 2, in the "Design and setting" section, where the text originally stated: "This study is a prospective single-centre randomized controlled trial involving patients with acute haematogenous PJI who receive standard DAIR surgery…"

The corrected version should read:

This study is a prospective single-centre randomized controlled trial involving patients with acute or haematogenous PJI who receive standard DAIR surgery…"

The original article has been corrected.
