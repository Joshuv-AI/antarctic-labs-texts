10 U.S.C. § 7434 — Command and supervision
Citation: us:usc:10:7434
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7434
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training); Chapter 753 (United States Military Academy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The supervision and charge of the Academy is in the Department of the Army, under officers of the Army detailed to that duty by the Secretary of the Army.
(b)
The immediate government of the Academy is under the Superintendent, who is also the commanding officer of the Academy and of the military post at West Point.
(c)
The Commandant of Cadets is the immediate commander of the Corps of Cadets, and is in charge of the instruction of the Corps in tactics.
(d)
The permanent professors and the director of admissions exercise command only in the academic department of the Academy.
