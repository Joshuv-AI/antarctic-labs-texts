33 U.S.C. § 883d — Improvement of methods, instruments, and equipments; investigations and research
Citation: us:usc:33:883d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section883d
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 17 (National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration); Subchapter II (Surveys). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To improve the efficiency of the National Ocean Survey and to increase engineering and scientific knowledge, the Secretary of Commerce is authorized to conduct developmental work for the improvement of surveying and cartographic methods, instruments, and equipments; and to conduct investigations and research in geophysical sciences (including geodesy, oceanography, seismology, and geomagnetism).
