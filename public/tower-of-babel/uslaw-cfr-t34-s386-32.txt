34 C.F.R. § 386.32 — What are allowable costs?
Citation: us:cfr:34:386.32
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-386.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 386 (Rehabilitation Training: Rehabilitation Long-Term Training). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to those allowable costs established in the Education Department General Administrative Regulations in 34 CFR 75.530 through 75.562, the following items are allowable under long-term training projects:
(a) Student stipends.
(b) Tuition and fees.
(c) Books and supplies.
(d) Student travel in conjunction with training assignments.
