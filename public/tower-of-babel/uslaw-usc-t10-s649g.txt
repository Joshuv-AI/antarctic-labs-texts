10 U.S.C. § 649g — Retirement: retirement for years of service; selective early retirement
Citation: us:usc:10:649g
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section649g
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 36 (Promotion, Separation, And Involuntary Retirement Of Officers On The Active-Duty List); Subchapter VI (Alternative Promotion Authority For Officers In Designated Competitive Categories). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Retirement for Years of Services.—
Sections 633 through 636 of this title shall apply to the retirement of officers in competitive categories of officers designated for purposes of this subchapter.
(b) Selective Early Retirement.—
Sections 638 and 638a of this title shall apply to the retirement of officers described in subsection (a).
