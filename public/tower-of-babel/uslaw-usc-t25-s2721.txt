25 U.S.C. § 2721 — Severability
Citation: us:usc:25:2721
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section2721
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 29 (Indian Gaming Regulation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the event that any section or provision of this chapter, or amendment made by this chapter, is held invalid, it is the intent of Congress that the remaining sections or provisions of this chapter, and amendments made by this chapter, shall continue in full force and effect.
