PANTÓN, a town of north-western Spain, in the province of
Lugo; in a mountainous district, watered by the rivers Miño
and Cabe. Pop. (1900), 12,088. Livestock is extensively reared,
and large quantities of wheat, wine, oats and potatoes are
produced. The other industries are distilling and linen
manufacture. The nearest railway station is 6 m. east, at
Montforte.
