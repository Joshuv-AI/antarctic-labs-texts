41 C.F.R. § 302-5.9 — -5.9 Advance of funds for househunting trip expenses.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-5.9
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-5.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-5 (5—Allowance For Househunting Trip Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies may authorize an advance of funds, in accordance with § 302-2.8 of this chapter, for the househunting trip expenses. The agency may not advance funds in excess of the sum of the anticipated transportation costs and either the maximum amount allowable under part 302-4 of this subchapter for the location and duration of the househunting trip or the lump sum househunting trip subsistence expenses payment, whichever applies.
