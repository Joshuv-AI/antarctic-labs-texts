25 C.F.R. § 166.817 — What happens if I do not pay the assessed penalties, damages and costs?
Citation: us:cfr:25:166.817
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-166.817
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 166 (Grazing Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless otherwise provided by applicable tribal law:
(a) We will refuse to issue you a permit for use, development, or occupancy of Indian agricultural lands; and
(b) We will forward your case for appropriate legal action.
