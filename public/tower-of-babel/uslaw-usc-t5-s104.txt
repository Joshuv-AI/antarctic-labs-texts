5 U.S.C. § 104 — Independent establishment
Citation: us:usc:5:104
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section104
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part I (The Agencies Generally); Chapter 1 (Organization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of this title, “independent establishment” means—
(1)
an establishment in the executive branch (other than the United States Postal Service or the Postal Regulatory Commission) which is not an Executive department, military department, Government corporation, or part thereof, or part of an independent establishment; and
(2)
the Government Accountability Office.
