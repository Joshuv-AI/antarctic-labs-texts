32 C.F.R. § 1630.15 — Class 1-H: Registrant not subject to processing for induction.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1630.15
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1630.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1630 (Classification Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In Class 1-H shall be placed any registrant who is not eligible for Class 1-A and is not currently subject to processing for induction.
