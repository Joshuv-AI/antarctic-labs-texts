42 U.S.C. § 7383i — Definitions of national laboratory and nuclear weapons production facility
Citation: us:usc:42:7383i
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section7383i
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 84 (Department Of Energy); Subchapter XV (Matters Relating To Safeguards, Security, And Counterintelligence). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of this subchapter:
(1)
The term “national laboratory” means any of the following:
(A)
The Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory, Livermore, California.
(B)
The Los Alamos National Laboratory, Los Alamos, New Mexico.
(C)
The Sandia National Laboratories, Albuquerque, New Mexico and Livermore, California.
(2)
The term “nuclear weapons production facility” means any of the following:
(A)
The Kansas City Plant, Kansas City, Missouri.
(B)
The Pantex Plant, Amarillo, Texas.
(C)
The Y–12 Plant, Oak Ridge, Tennessee.
(D)
The tritium operations at the Savannah River Site, Aiken, South Carolina.
(E)
The Nevada Test Site, Nevada.
