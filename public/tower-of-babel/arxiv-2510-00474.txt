Remotely Almost Periodic Solutions of Scalar Differential Equations
David Cheban

Abstract
. The aim of this paper is to study the problem of existence of remotely almost periodic solutions for the scalar differential equation x ′ = f ⁡ ( t , x ) , x^{\prime}=f(t,x), where f : ℝ × ℝ → ℝ f:\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R} is a continuous, monotone in x x and remotely almost periodic in t t function. We prove that every solution φ \varphi of this equation bounded on the semi-axis ℝ + \mathbb{R}_{+} is remotely almost periodic. This statement is a generalization of the well-known Opial’s theorem for remotely almost periodic scalar differential equations. We also establish a similar statement for scalar difference equations.

In the relation with Corollary

Is there Opial’s theorem for asymptotically

Unfortunately, the answer to this question, in general, is

Example 4.14
.

Consider the differential equation (
18
) with the right
hand side

defined

(23)

for all

conditions of Theorem
4.7
and it is asymptotically almost
periodic (in fact asymptotically stationary) in time uniformly
w.r.t.

because

\sup\limits_{x\in\mathbb{R}}|f(t,x)|=\frac{2t|\cos(t^{2}+\pi^{3})^{1/3}|}{3(t^{2}+\pi^{3})^{2/3}}\leq\frac{2t}{3(t^{2}+\pi^{3})^{2/3}}\to 0

Let

be a solution of the equation

|\varphi(t+\tau,x_{0},f)-\varphi(t,x_{0},f)|=|\sin(\pi^{3}+(t+\tau)^{2})^{1/3}-\sin(\pi^{3}+t^{2})^{1/3}|=

2|\sin\frac{(\pi^{3}+(t+\tau)^{2})^{1/3}-(\pi^{3}+t^{2})^{1/3}}{2}\cos\frac{(\pi^{3}+(t+\tau)^{2})^{1/3}+(\pi^{3}+t^{2})^{1/3}}{2}|=

\frac{|\tau(t+\tau)|}{(\pi^{3}+(t+\tau)^{2})^{2/3}+(\pi^{3}+(t+\tau)^{2})^{1/3}(\pi^{3}+t^{2})^{1/3}+(\pi^{3}+t^{2})^{2/3}}\to 0

.
