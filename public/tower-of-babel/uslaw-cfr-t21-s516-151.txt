21 C.F.R. § 516.151 — Granting a request for addition to the index.
Citation: us:cfr:21:516.151
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-516.151
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 516 (New Animal Drugs For Minor Use And Minor Species). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) FDA will grant the request for addition of a new animal drug to the index if none of the reasons described in § 516.149 for denying such a request applies.
(b) When a request for addition of a new animal drug to the index is granted, FDA will notify the requestor in accordance with § 516.153.
