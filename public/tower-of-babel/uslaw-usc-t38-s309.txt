38 U.S.C. § 309 — Chief Financial Officer
Citation: us:usc:38:309
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section309
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part I (General Provisions); Chapter 3 (Department Of Veterans Affairs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall designate the Assistant Secretary whose functions include budgetary and financial functions as the Chief Financial Officer of the Department. The Chief Financial Officer shall advise the Secretary on financial management of the Department and shall exercise the authority and carry out the functions specified in section 902 of title 31.
