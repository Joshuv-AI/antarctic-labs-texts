45 C.F.R. § 1705.9 — Disclosure of record to a person other than the individual to whom the record pertains.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1705.9
Creator: National Commission On Libraries And Information Science
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1705.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1705 (Privacy Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An individual to whom a record is to be disclosed in person may have a person of his or her own choosing accompany the individual when the record is disclosed.
