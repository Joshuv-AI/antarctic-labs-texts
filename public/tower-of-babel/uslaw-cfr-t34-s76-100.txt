34 C.F.R. § 76.100 — Effect of this subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:34:76.100
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-76.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 76 (State-Administered Formula Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart establishes general requirements that a State must meet to apply for a grant under a program covered by this part. Additional requirements are in applicable statutes and regulations for the program.
