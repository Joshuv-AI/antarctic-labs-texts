7 U.S.C. § 8785 — Tracking of benefits
Citation: us:usc:7:8785
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section8785
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 113 (Agricultural Commodity Support Programs); Subchapter V (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As soon as practicable after the date of enactment of this Act, the Secretary may track the benefits provided, directly or indirectly, to individuals and entities under titles I and II and the amendments made by those titles.
