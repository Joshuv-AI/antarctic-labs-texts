31 C.F.R. § 536.512 — Official business of the United States Government.
Citation: us:cfr:31:536.512
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-536.512
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 536 (Narcotics Trafficking Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All transactions prohibited by this part that are for the conduct of the official business of the United States Government by employees, grantees, or contractors thereof are authorized.
