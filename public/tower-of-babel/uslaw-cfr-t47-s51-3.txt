47 C.F.R. § 51.3 — Applicability to negotiated agreements.
Citation: us:cfr:47:51.3
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-51.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 51 (Interconnection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To the extent provided in section 252(e)(2)(A) of the Act, a state commission shall have authority to approve an interconnection agreement adopted by negotiation even if the terms of the agreement do not comply with the requirements of this part.
