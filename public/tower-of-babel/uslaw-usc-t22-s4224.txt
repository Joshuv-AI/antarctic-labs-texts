22 U.S.C. § 4224 — Fees; accounting; stamps
Citation: us:usc:22:4224
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section4224
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 52 (Foreign Service); Subchapter XIV (Powers, Duties And Liabilities Of Consular Officers Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of sections 1196 1 and 4223 of this title, relative to official fees and the method of accounting therefor shall apply to diplomatic officers below the grade of minister and to consular officers.
