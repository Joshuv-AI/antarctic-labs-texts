45 U.S.C. § 1213 — Conflict with other laws
Citation: us:usc:45:1213
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2024
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title45-section1213
Context: United States Code, Title 45 (Railroads); Chapter 21 (Alaska Railroad Transfer). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of this chapter shall govern if there is any conflict between this chapter and any other law.
