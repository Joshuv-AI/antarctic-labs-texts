49 C.F.R. § 228.315 — Vermin control.
Citation: us:cfr:49:228.315
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-228.315
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 228 (Passenger Train Employee Hours Of Service; Recordkeeping And Reporting; Sleeping Quarters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Camp cars shall be constructed, equipped, and maintained to prevent the entrance or harborage of rodents, insects, or other vermin. A continuing and effective extermination program shall be instituted where the presence of vermin is detected.
