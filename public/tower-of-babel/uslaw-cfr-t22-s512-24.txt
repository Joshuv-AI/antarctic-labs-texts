22 C.F.R. § 512.24 — Non-waiver of rights by payments.
Citation: us:cfr:22:512.24
Creator: United States Agency For Global Media
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-512.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 512 (Collection Of Debts Under The Debt Collection Act Of 1982). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee's voluntary payment of all or part of a debt being collected under 5 U.S.C. 5514 shall not be construed as a waiver of any rights which the employee may have under 5 U.S.C., or any other provision of contract or law, unless statutory or contractual provisions provide to the contrary.
