42 U.S.C. § 5054 — Distribution of benefits between rural and urban areas
Citation: us:usc:42:5054
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section5054
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 66 (Domestic Volunteer Services); Subchapter IV (Administration And Coordination). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director shall adopt appropriate administrative measures to assure that the benefits of and services under this chapter will be distributed equitably between residents of rural and urban areas.
