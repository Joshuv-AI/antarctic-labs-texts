15 C.F.R. § 19.3 — Do these regulations adopt the Federal Claims Collection Standards (FCCS)?
Citation: us:cfr:15:19.3
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-19.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 19 (Commerce Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part adopts and incorporates all provisions of the FCCS (31 CFR Chapter IX parts 900-904). This part also supplements the FCCS by prescribing procedures consistent with the FCCS, as necessary and appropriate for Commerce Department operations.
