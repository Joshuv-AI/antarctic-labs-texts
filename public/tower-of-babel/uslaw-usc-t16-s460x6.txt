16 U.S.C. § 460x–6 — Taxing power
Citation: us:usc:16:460x–6
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section460x–6
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LXXXII (Sleeping Bear Dunes National Lakeshore). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subchapter shall be construed as prohibiting any governmental jurisdiction in the State of Michigan from assessing taxes upon any interest in real estate retained under the provisions of section 460x–9 of this title to the owner of such interest.
