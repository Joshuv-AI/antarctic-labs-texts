20 C.F.R. § 686.710 — What career transition services are provided for Job Corps enrollees?
Citation: us:cfr:20:686.710
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-686.710
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 686 (The Job Corps Under Title I Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Job Corps career transition services focus on placing program graduates in:
(a) Full-time jobs that are related to their career technical training and career pathway that lead to economic self-sufficiency;
(b) Postsecondary education;
(c) Advanced training programs, including registered apprenticeship programs; or
(d) The Armed Forces.
