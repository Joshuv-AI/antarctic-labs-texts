34 C.F.R. § 222.13 — What overpayments are not eligible for forgiveness under section 8012 of the Act?
Citation: us:cfr:34:222.13
Creator: Office Of Elementary And Secondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-222.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 222 (Impact Aid Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary does not consider as eligible for forgiveness under section 8012 of the Act any overpayment caused by an LEA's failure to expend or account for funds properly under the following laws and regulations:
(a) Section 8003(d) of the Act (implemented in subpart D of this part) for certain federally connected children with disabilities.
(b) Section 8007 of the Act for construction.
