The nonexistence of positive solutions for Dirichlet problems like (

Recently, Abbebe and Chhetri in

Our procedure is based on an energy analysis, which involves computing the derivative of the energy function (see equation (
10

However, since our solutions do not possess second-order derivatives at all points, we are compelled to compute the derivative using the very definition of the energy function. This paper is structured as follows. Section 2 defines the notions of solution and energy associated with the problem. It also establishes the monotonicity properties of this energy.
