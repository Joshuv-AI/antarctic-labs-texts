43 U.S.C. § 316i — Assignment of leases
Citation: us:usc:43:316i
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section316i
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 8A (Grazing Lands); Subchapter II (Alaska). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The lessee may, with the approval of the Secretary, assign in whole or in part any lease, and to the extent of such assignment be relieved from any liability in respect of such lease, accruing subsequent to the effective date of such assignment.
