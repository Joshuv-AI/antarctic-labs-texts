16 U.S.C. § 3824 — Fairness of compliance
Citation: us:usc:16:3824
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section3824
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 58 (Erodible Land And Wetland Conservation And Reserve Program); Subchapter III (Wetland Conservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If the actions of an unrelated person or public entity, outside the control of, and without the prior approval of, the landowner or tenant result in a change in the characteristics of cropland that would cause the land to be determined to be a wetland, the affected land shall not be considered to be wetland for purposes of this subchapter.
