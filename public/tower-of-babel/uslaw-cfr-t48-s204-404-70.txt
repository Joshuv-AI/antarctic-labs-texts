48 C.F.R. § 204.404-70 — 204.404-70 Additional contract clauses.

Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-204.404-70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 204 (Administrative And Information Matters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Use the clause at 252.204-7000, Disclosure of Information, in solicitations and contracts when the contractor will have access to or generate unclassified information that may be sensitive and inappropriate for release to the public.
(b) Use the clause at 252.204-7003, Control of Government Personnel Work Product, in all solicitations and contracts.
