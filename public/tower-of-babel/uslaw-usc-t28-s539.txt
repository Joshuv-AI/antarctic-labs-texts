28 U.S.C. § 539 — Counterintelligence official reception and representation expenses
Citation: us:usc:28:539
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section539
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part II (Department Of Justice); Chapter 33 (Federal Bureau Of Investigation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of the Federal Bureau of Investigation may use funds available to the Federal Bureau of Investigation for counterintelligence programs to pay the expenses of hosting foreign officials in the United States under the auspices of the Federal Bureau of Investigation for consultation on counterintelligence matters.
