49 C.F.R. § 371.10 — Duties and obligations of brokers.
Citation: us:cfr:49:371.10
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-371.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 371 (Brokers Of Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where the broker acts on behalf of a person bound by law or the FMCSA regulation as to the transmittal of bills or payments, the broker must also abide by the law or regulations which apply to that person.
