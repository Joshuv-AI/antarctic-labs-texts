41 U.S.C. § 3707 — Antitrust violations
Citation: us:usc:41:3707
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title41-section3707
Context: United States Code, Title 41 (Public Contracts); Chapter 37 (Awarding Of Contracts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If the agency head considers that a bid or proposal evidences a violation of the antitrust laws, the agency head shall refer the bid or proposal to the Attorney General for appropriate action.
