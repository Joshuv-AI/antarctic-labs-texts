46 C.F.R. § 3.01-1 — -1 Purpose of regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:46:3.01-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-3.01-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 3 (Designation Of Oceanographic Research Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of the regulations in this part is to establish standard procedures for the designation of certain vessels as oceanographic research vessels as defined in 46 U.S.C. 2101.
