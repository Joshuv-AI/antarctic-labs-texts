7 U.S.C. § 7491 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:7:7491
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section7491
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 101 (Agricultural Promotion); Subchapter V (Popcorn). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as are necessary to carry out this subchapter. Amounts made available under this section or otherwise made available to the Department, and amounts made available under any other marketing or promotion order, may not be used to pay any administrative expense of the Board.
