10 U.S.C. § 9284 — Medal of honor: duplicate medal
Citation: us:usc:10:9284
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9284
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 937 (Decorations And Awards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A person awarded a medal of honor shall, upon written application of that person, be issued, without charge, one duplicate medal of honor with ribbons and appurtenances. Such duplicate medal of honor shall be marked, in such manner as the Secretary of the Air Force may determine, as a duplicate or for display purposes only.
