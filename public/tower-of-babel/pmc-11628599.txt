Author Correction: Percutaneous transluminal angioplasty is safe and feasible for reinsertion of tunneled cuffed catheters in the right internal jugular vein
Authors: Daoyang Wang, Yanchun Guan, Li Zhuo, Zheng Chen, Xuxiang Ma, Yuping Huang
Published: 2024

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-024-73154-9, published online 14 October 2024

The original version of this Article contained errors.

The patient age data and the study’s date range were incorrect in the original version of this article.

As a result, the Abstract

“Clinical data from 21 hemodialysis patients with dysfunctional vascular access who underwent PTA for reinsertion of TCC in the RIJVs from July 2020 to June 2023 at the First and Second Affiliated Hospitals of Bengbu Medical College were retrospectively analyzed.”

now reads,

“Clinical data from 21 hemodialysis patients with dysfunctional vascular access who underwent PTA for reinsertion of TCC in the RIJVs from July 2020 to July 2023 at the First and Second Affiliated Hospitals of Bengbu Medical College were retrospectively analyzed.”

Furthermore, in the Materials and methods section, under the subheading ‘General information’

“Clinical data of patients who underwent the placement of TCC in the RIJV due to dysfunction of the hemodialysis vascular access at The First and Second Affiliated Hospital of Bengbu Medical College from July 2020 to June 2023 were collected and analyzed. The study included 13 female and 8 male patients, aged 33 to 85 years, with an average age of (64.29 ± 14.06) years.”

now reads,

“Clinical data of patients who underwent the placement of TCC in the RIJV due to dysfunction of the hemodialysis vascular access at The First and Second Affiliated Hospital of Bengbu Medical College from July 2020 to July 2023 were collected and analyzed. The study included 13 female and 8 male patients, aged 33 to 85 years, with an average age of (63.86 ± 13.86) years.”

And under the subheading ‘Treatment methods’

“Post-dilation angiography showed the opening of stenotic lesions, and a TCC catheter was smoothly placed through the RIJV, as shown in Fig. 1. Figure 1 shows the procedures of Case No. 5, a 62-year-old female on maintenance hemodialysis for 13 years due to obstructive nephropathy.”

now reads,

“Post-dilation angiography showed the opening of stenotic lesions, and a TCC catheter was smoothly placed through the RIJV, as shown in Fig. 1. Figure 1 shows the procedures of Case No. 5, a 60-year-old female on maintenance hemodialysis for 13 years due to obstructive nephropathy.”
