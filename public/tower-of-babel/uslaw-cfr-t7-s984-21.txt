7 C.F.R. § 984.21 — Handler inventory.
Citation: us:cfr:7:984.21
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-984.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 984 (Walnuts Grown In California). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Handler inventory as of any date means all walnuts, inshell or shelled, wherever located, then held by a handler or for his or her account.
