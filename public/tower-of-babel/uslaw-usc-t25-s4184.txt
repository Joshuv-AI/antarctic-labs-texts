25 U.S.C. § 4184 — Effect on HOME Investment Partnerships Act
Citation: us:usc:25:4184
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section4184
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 43 (Native American Housing Assistance And Self-Determination); Subchapter V (Termination Of Assistance For Indian Tribes Under Incorporated Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter or an amendment made by this chapter prohibits or prevents any participating jurisdiction (within the meaning of the HOME Investment Partnerships Act (42 U.S.C. 12721 et seq.)) from providing any amounts made available to the participating jurisdiction under that Act (42 U.S.C. 12721 et seq.) to an Indian tribe or a tribally designated housing entity for use in accordance with that Act (42 U.S.C. 12721 et seq.).
