10 U.S.C. § 8821 — Definitions

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 881 (Claims). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
In this chapter “vessel in the naval service” means—
(1)
any vessel of the Navy, manned by the Navy, or chartered on bareboat charter to the Navy; or
(2)
when the Coast Guard is operating as a service in the Navy, any vessel of the Coast Guard, manned by the Coast Guard, or chartered on bareboat charter to the Coast Guard.
(b)
In this chapter “settle” means consider, ascertain, adjust, determine, and dispose of a claim, whether by full or partial allowance or by disallowance.
