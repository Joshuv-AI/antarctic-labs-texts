48 C.F.R. § 14.203-2 — 14.203-2 Dissemination of information concerning invitations for bids.

Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-14.203-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 14 (Sealed Bidding). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Procedures concerning display of invitations for bids in a public place, information releases to newspapers and trade journals, paid advertisements, and synopsizing through the Governmentwide point of entry (GPE) are set forth in 5.101 and Subpart 5.2.
