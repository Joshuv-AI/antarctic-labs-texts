17 C.F.R. § 37.300 — Core Principle 3—Swaps not readily susceptible to manipulation.
Citation: us:cfr:17:37.300
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-37.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 37 (Swap Execution Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The swap execution facility shall permit trading only in swaps that are not readily susceptible to manipulation.
