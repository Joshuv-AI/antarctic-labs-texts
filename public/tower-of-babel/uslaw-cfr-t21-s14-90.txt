21 C.F.R. § 14.90 — Ad hoc advisory committee members.
Citation: us:cfr:21:14.90
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-14.90
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 14 (Public Hearing Before A Public Advisory Committee). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In selecting members of an ad hoc advisory committee, the Commissioner may use the procedures in §§ 14.82 and 14.84 or any other procedure deemed appropriate.
