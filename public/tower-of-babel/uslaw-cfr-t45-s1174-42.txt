45 C.F.R. § 1174.42 — Deposit in Treasury of the United States.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1174.42
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1174.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1174 (Program Fraud Civil Remedies Act Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The authority shall deposit all amounts collected pursuant to this part as miscellaneous receipts in the Treasury of the United States, except as provided in 31 U.S.C. 3806(g).
