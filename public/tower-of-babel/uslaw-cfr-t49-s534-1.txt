49 C.F.R. § 534.1 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:49:534.1
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-534.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 534 (Rights And Responsibilities Of Manufacturers In The Context Of Changes In Corporate Relationships). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part defines the rights and responsibilities of manufacturers in the context of changes in corporate relationships for purposes of the fuel economy and fuel consumption programs established by 49 U.S.C. chapter 329.
