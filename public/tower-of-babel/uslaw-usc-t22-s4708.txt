22 U.S.C. § 4708 — J. William Fulbright Foreign Scholarship Board
Citation: us:usc:22:4708
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section4708
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 57 (United States Scholarship Program For Developing Countries). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The J. William Fulbright Foreign Scholarship Board shall advise and assist the President in the discharge of the scholarship program carried out pursuant to this chapter, in accordance with the guidelines set forth in section 4704 of this title. The President may provide for such additional secretarial and staff assistance for the Board as may be required to carry out this chapter.
