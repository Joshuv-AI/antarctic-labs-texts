19 C.F.R. § 151.84 — Determination of staple length.
Citation: us:cfr:19:151.84
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-151.84
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 151 (Examination, Sampling, And Testing Of Merchandise). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Center director shall have one or more samples of each sampled bale of cotton stapled by a qualified Customs officer, or a qualified employee of the Department of Agriculture designated by the Commissioner of Customs for the purpose, and shall promptly mail the importer a notice of the results determined.
