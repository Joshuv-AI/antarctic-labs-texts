19 U.S.C. § 3523 — Duty free treatment for octadecyl isocyanate and 5-Chloro-2-(2,4-dichloro-phenoxy)phenol
Citation: us:usc:19:3523
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section3523
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 22 (Uruguay Round Trade Agreements); Subchapter I (Approval Of, And General Provisions Relating To, Uruguay Round Agreements); Part B (Tariff Modifications). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President—
(1)
shall proclaim duty-free entry for octadecyl isocyanate and 5-Chloro-2-(2,4-dichloro-phenoxy)phenol, to be effective on the effective date of the proclamation issued by the President under section 3513(a) of this title to carry out Schedule XX, and
(2)
shall take such actions as are necessary to reflect such tariff treatment in Schedule XX.
