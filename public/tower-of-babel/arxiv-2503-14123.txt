Introduction

Let

The wave trace Poisson summation formula on

[

\displaystyle{\rm Tr}(e^{it\sqrt{\Delta}})

\displaystyle\sim

\displaystyle a_{\,0,-n+1}(t+i0)^{-n+1}+a_{\,0,-n+2}(t+i0)^{-n+2}+\cdots

(1.2)

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle\sum_{L>0}\Biggl(a_{\,L,-1}(t-(L+i0))^{-1}\biggr.

\displaystyle\hskip 28.45274pt+\ \ \left.\sum_{j\geq 0}a_{\,L,j}(t-(L+i0))^{j}\log(t-(L+i0))\right).

2.2
Vertical Lagrangian distributions and FIOs

A vertical (fibrewise vertically-embedded) conic Lagrangian fibration in
T^{*}_{M}\backslash 0
is a closed conic Lagrangian submanifold
\Lambda
of
T^{*}_{M}\backslash 0
which is the total space of a conic subfibration
\lambda:\Lambda\rightarrow B
of the cotangent bundle along the fibres

(minus the zero section). Thus,
\lambda:\Lambda\rightarrow B
has fibre
at
b\in B
a conic Lagrangian submanifold

\lambda^{-1}(b)=\Lambda_{b}\subset T^{*}_{M_{b}}\backslash 0.

One has

\dim T^{*}_{M}\backslash 0=2q+2\beta

and by the Lagrangian condition

\dim\Lambda=q+\beta,

where

q:=\dim M_{b}=\dim\Lambda_{b}=\frac{1}{2}\dim T^{*}_{M_{b}},\ \ \ \ \beta=\dim B.

T^{*}_{M/B}\backslash 0
(a subbundle of
T^{*}_{M}\backslash 0
) has dimension
2q+\beta
and is considered here as a fibration over
B
with fibre at
b\in B
the cotangent bundle
T^{*}_{M_{b}}\backslash 0
of dimension
2q
of
M_{b}
.
\Lambda
is not (in general) a Lagrangian submanifold of the (in general) non-symplectic manifold
T^{*}_{M/B}\backslash 0
, although the fibres of
\Lambda
are Lagrangian submanifolds of the fibres of
T^{*}_{M/B}\backslash 0
.
\Lambda
is, on the other hand, assumed to be a Lagrangian subfibration of
T^{*}_{M}\backslash 0
when the latter is considered as a fibration over
B
with fibre

over
b\in B
.

Any vertical conic Lagrangian fibrations is parametrised locally by non-degenerate vertical phase functions. Using local triviality of
\pi
and the corresponding classical fact for conic Lagrangian submanifolds it follows that a
q+\beta
dimensional subfibration

is a a vertical Lagrangian fibration if and only if every

has a fibred conic neighbourhood
\Gamma=\cup_{b}\Gamma_{b}
such that
\Gamma_{b}\cap L=\Lambda_{\phi_{b}}
some nondegenerate vertical phase function
\phi
.
For such local considerations one may use fibrewise charts defined relative to a local trivialization
M_{|W}\cong W\times M_{b}
of
\pi
over a chart

and fibre
M_{b}=\pi^{-1}(b)
with
b\in W
and local chart

with

.

To a smooth family of Schwartz class functions
f:S^{1}\rightarrow{\mathcal{S}}(\mathbb{R}^{1}),\,b\mapsto f_{b}
, we associate the vertical (fibrewise) FIO kernel
K_{{\mathcal{A}}_{b}}(p,q)=\int^{\infty}_{-\infty}e^{i(p-q)\zeta}f_{b}(\zeta)\ d\zeta
for
p,q\in\pi^{-1}(b)
.

K_{{\mathcal{A}}_{b}}(p,q)
is fibrewise
4\pi\mathbb{Z}
invariant (
A.2
) and so pushes-down to
2S^{1}
as the vertical family
\textsf{K}^{f}
of kernels
K^{f_{b}}(\overline{p},\overline{q})=\sum_{n}K_{{\mathcal{A}}_{b}}(p,q+4\pi n)
of a vertical FIO
\textsf{A}^{2S^{1}}
on
2S^{1}
, where
\tau(p)=\overline{p},\tau(q)=\overline{q}\in 2S^{1}_{b}.

Each
K^{f_{b}}
has just four possible values on
2S^{1}_{b}\times 2S^{1}_{b}

K^{f_{b}}(\sqrt{b},\sqrt{b}),\ K^{f_{b}}(\sqrt{b},-\sqrt{b}),\ K^{f_{b}}(-\sqrt{b},\sqrt{b}),\ K^{f_{b}}(-\sqrt{b},-\sqrt{b}),

(A.12)

and is
\mathbb{Z}_{2}
-invariant so
K^{f_{b}}(\sqrt{b},\sqrt{b})=K^{f_{b}}(-\sqrt{b},-\sqrt{b})
and equal to
\sum_{n}F(f_{b})(4\pi n)
while
K^{f_{b}}(\sqrt{b},-\sqrt{b})=K^{f_{b}}(-\sqrt{b},\sqrt{b})=\sum_{n}F(f_{b})(2\pi(2n+1))
.
Thus, integrating over the fibre, (
A.11
) is in this case the vertical trace formula

{\rm Tr}_{2S^{1}/S^{1}}(\textsf{A}^{2S^{1}})(b)=K^{f_{b}}(\sqrt{b},\sqrt{b})+K^{f_{b}}(-\sqrt{b},-\sqrt{b})=2\sum_{n}F(f_{b})(4\pi n).

On the other hand, in view of
\mathbb{Z}_{2}
invariance,
\textsf{K}^{f}
in turn pushes-down from
2S^{1}
to the null-fibration
id:S^{1}\rightarrow S^{1}
by (
A.7
) as

K_{S^{1}}(b)=K^{f_{b}}(\sqrt{b},\sqrt{b})+K^{f_{b}}(\sqrt{b},-\sqrt{b})=\sum_{n}F(f_{b})(2\pi n)

which is the usual Poisson sum in (
A.1
) (note comment following (
A.11
)).

The push-down to the
k
-fold cover
kS^{1}\rightarrow S^{1}
is entirely similar, with the above sums replaced by a sum over the
{\rm k}^{th}
roots of unity. Likewise, discrete quotients of higher dimensional rank vector bundles yield trace formulae over fibrations of tori.

Theorem
1.1
and Theorem
1.2
provide generalizations of such vertical families Poisson summation formulae to arbitrary compact Riemannian fibrations, coinciding with the generalization of (
1.2
) to (
A.1
) in the case that
B
is a single point manifold.

Appendix B
Proof of Proposition
2.3

We shall show the dual identification for tangent bundles since the proof is perhaps a little more transparent, though the argument is entirely dualizable to give directly the result for cotangent bundles. There is a commutative diagram

{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces M\times_{B}N}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces M}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces N}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces B}
\scriptstyle{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mu}
\scriptstyle{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\hskip 14.22636pt\ s}
\scriptstyle{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\nu}
\scriptstyle{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\pi}
\scriptstyle{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\sigma}

from which one has (since
s=\pi\circ\mu=\sigma\circ\nu
) the exact sequence

0\rightarrow T_{M\times_{B}N}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\mu^{*}d\mu+\nu^{*}d\nu}}{{\longrightarrow}}\mu^{*}T_{M}+\nu^{*}T_{N}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle s^{*}d\pi\mu^{\#}-s^{*}d\sigma\nu^{\#}}}{{\longrightarrow}}s^{*}T_{B}\rightarrow 0,

(B.1)

where
\beta^{\#}:\beta^{*}E\rightarrow E
is the covering bundle map for the pull-back of a vector bundle
E\rightarrow Y
and smooth map
\beta:X\rightarrow Y
. Using (
2.16
) to write this as

0\rightarrow T_{\mbox{\tiny{$(M\times_{B}N)/B$}}}+s^{*}T_{B}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\mu^{*}d\mu+\nu^{*}d\nu}}{{\longrightarrow}}\mu^{*}T_{\mbox{\tiny{M/B}}}+s^{*}T_{B}+\nu^{*}T_{\mbox{\tiny{N/B}}}+s^{*}T_{B}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle s^{*}d\pi\mu^{\#}-s^{*}d\sigma\nu^{\#}}}{{\longrightarrow}}s^{*}T_{B}\rightarrow 0,

(B.2)

(
B.1
) is seen to split into the direct sum of the two short exact sequences

0\rightarrow T_{\mbox{\tiny{$(M\times_{B}N)/B$}}}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\mu^{*}d\mu+\nu^{*}d\nu}}{{\longrightarrow}}\mu^{*}T_{\mbox{\tiny{M/B}}}+\nu^{*}T_{\mbox{\tiny{N/B}}}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle s^{*}d\pi\mu^{\#}-s^{*}d\sigma\nu^{\#}}}{{\longrightarrow}}0,

(B.3)

and

0\rightarrow s^{*}T_{B}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\mu^{*}d\mu+\nu^{*}d\nu}}{{\longrightarrow}}s^{*}T_{B}+s^{*}T_{B}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle s^{*}d\pi\mu^{\#}-s^{*}d\sigma\nu^{\#}}}{{\longrightarrow}}s^{*}T_{B}\rightarrow 0

(B.4)

with
s^{*}d\pi\mu^{\#}
restricting on
\mu^{*}T_{\mbox{\tiny{M/B}}}
to the zero map, as
T_{\mbox{\tiny{M/B}}}=\mbox{\rm ker}(d\pi)
, and likewise
s^{*}d\sigma\nu^{\#}
to the zero map on
\nu^{*}T_{\mbox{\tiny{N/B}}}
, hence the final arrow of (
B.3
). On the other hand,
\mu^{*}d\mu
and
\nu^{*}d\nu
are complementary linear projection operators to the vertical tangent spaces over
M
and
N
respectively, defining a bijection for the non-zero arrow of (
B.3
). (
B.3
) is thus a vector bundle isomorphism

T_{\mbox{\tiny{$(M\times_{B}N)/B$}}}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\mu^{*}d\mu+\nu^{*}d\nu}}{{\longrightarrow}}\mu^{*}T_{\mbox{\tiny{M/B}}}+\nu^{*}T_{\mbox{\tiny{N/B}}}.

(B.5)

The fibrewise projections
\mu^{*}d\mu
and
\nu^{*}d\nu
both reduce on the pull-back bundle
s^{*}T_{B}
to the identity operator, so the first non-zero arrow of (
B.4
) is the diagonal map
\xi\mapsto(\xi,\xi)
and the second is
(\xi,\eta)\mapsto\xi-\eta
. Taking duals of (
B.5
) gives the first identity of (
2.32
).

T^{*}_{\mbox{\tiny{M/B}}}=\cup_{b}T^{*}_{M_{b}}
has a canonical vertical(fibrewise)-symplectic form
\omega_{\mbox{\tiny{M/B}}}
–
restricting to the standard symplectic two form
\omega_{M_{b}}
on each
T^{*}_{M_{b}}
, but which is degenerate on the manifold
T^{*}_{\mbox{\tiny{M/B}}}
. Globally, to the projection

p:T^{*}_{\mbox{\tiny{M/B}}}\rightarrow M
one assigns the 1-form
\alpha
on
T^{*}_{\mbox{\tiny{M/B}}}
by
\alpha_{m,\xi}=\xi\circ dp_{(m,\xi)}
, with
\xi\in T^{*}_{m}M_{b}
(i.e
\pi(m)=b
) considered as in
T^{*}_{M}
via inclusion (extending by zero) (
2.16
). Then the (degenerate) 2-form
\omega_{\mbox{\tiny{M/B}}}=d\alpha
coincides on restriction to each
T^{*}_{M_{b}}
with the standard non-degenerate symplectic form
\omega_{M_{b}}
. From the first identity of (
2.32
) we obtain the vertical-symplectic form
\mu^{*}\omega_{\mbox{\tiny{M/B}}}+\nu^{*}\omega_{\mbox{\tiny{N/B}}}
on
\mu^{*}T^{*}_{\mbox{\tiny{M/B}}}+\nu^{*}T^{*}_{\mbox{\tiny{N/B}}}
which by convention is usually written as
\omega_{\mbox{\tiny{M/B}}}+\omega_{\mbox{\tiny{N/B}}}
on
T^{*}_{\mbox{\tiny{$(M\times_{B}N)/B$}}}
.

The final identification of (
2.32
) is the twist fibrewise diffeomorphism defined by the map which acts by
-I
on the fibres of
T^{*}_{\mbox{\tiny{N/B}}}
, given in local fibrewise coordinates by
(b,x,y,\xi,\eta)\mapsto(b,x,\xi,y,-\eta)
, taking the vertical-symplectic form
\mu^{*}\omega_{\mbox{\tiny{M/B}}}+\nu^{*}\omega_{\mbox{\tiny{N/B}}}
on
T^{*}_{\mbox{\tiny{$(M\times_{B}N)/B$}}}
to the vertical-symplectic form
\omega_{\mbox{\tiny{M/B}}}-\omega_{\mbox{\tiny{N/B}}}
on
T^{*}_{\mbox{\tiny{M/B}}}\times_{B}T^{*}_{\mbox{\tiny{N/B}}}
; that is, to the degenerate 2-form which coincides on restriction to the fibre
T^{*}_{M_{b}}\times T^{*}N_{b}
with the (non-degenerate) symplectic 2-form
\omega_{M_{b}}-\omega_{N_{b}}
.
