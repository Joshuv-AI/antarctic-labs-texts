17 U.S.C. § 305 — Duration of copyright: Terminal date
Citation: us:usc:17:305
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title17-section305
Context: United States Code, Title 17 (Copyrights); Chapter 3 (Duration Of Copyright). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All terms of copyright provided by sections 302 through 304 run to the end of the calendar year in which they would otherwise expire.
