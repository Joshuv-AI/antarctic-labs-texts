25 C.F.R. § 10.7 — Where do I find help or receive technical assistance in complying with the policies and standards?
Citation: us:cfr:25:10.7
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-10.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 10 (Indian Country Detention Facilities And Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The BIA has a trained Detention Specialist on the staff of the Office of Law Enforcement Services, Albuquerque, New Mexico, who is available to conduct evaluations and provide technical assistance or guidance in all facets of Indian country detention programs.
