23 C.F.R. § 658.7 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:23:658.7
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-658.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 658 (Truck Size And Weight, Route Designations—Length, Width And Weight Limitations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as limited in § 658.17(a) the provisions of this part are applicable to the National Network and reasonable access thereto. However, nothing in this regulation shall be construed to prevent any State from applying any weight and size limits to other highways, except when such limits would deny reasonable access to the National Network.
