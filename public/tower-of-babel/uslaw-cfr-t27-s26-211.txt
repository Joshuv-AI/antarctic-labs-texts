27 C.F.R. § 26.211 — Warehouse receipts covering distilled spirits.
Citation: us:cfr:27:26.211
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-26.211
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 26 (Liquors And Articles From Puerto Rico And The Virgin Islands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The sale of warehouse receipts for distilled spirits is equivalent to the sale of distilled spirits. Accordingly, every person bringing distilled spirits into the United States from the Virgin Islands who sells, or offers for sale, warehouse receipts for distilled spirits stored in warehouses, or elsewhere, must register and keep records as a dealer in liquors at the place where the warehouse receipts are sold, or offered for sale, in accordance with part 31 of this chapter.
