48 C.F.R. § 1203.405 — 1203.405 Misrepresentations or violations of the Covenant Against Contingent Fees.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1203.405
Creator: Chapter 12—Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1203.405
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1203 (Improper Business Practices And Personal Conflicts Of Interest). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The same procedures contained in 1203.203 shall be followed for reporting the attempted or actual exercise of improper influence, misrepresentation of a contingent fee arrangement, or other violation of the Covenant Against Contingent Fees (see FAR 52.203-5), except reports of misrepresentation or violations of the Covenant Against Contingent Fees shall be coordinated with DOT legal counsel for referral to the Department of Justice, if deemed appropriate.
