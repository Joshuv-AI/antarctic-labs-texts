42 U.S.C. § 300aa–32 — Judicial review
Citation: us:usc:42:300aa–32
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section300aa–32
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter XIX (Vaccines); Part 2 (National Vaccine Injury Compensation Program); Subpart d (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A petition for review of a regulation under this part may be filed in a court of appeals of the United States within 60 days from the date of the promulgation of the regulation or after such date if such petition is based solely on grounds arising after such 60th day.
