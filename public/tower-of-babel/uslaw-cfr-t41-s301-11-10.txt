41 C.F.R. § 301-11.10 — -11.10 Reimbursement for prepaid lodging expenses.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-11.10
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-11.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-11 (11—Subsistence Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a temporary duty assignment is curtailed, canceled, or interrupted for official purposes or reasons beyond the employee's control and acceptable to the agency, the employee may be reimbursed for pre-paid expenses that are not refundable, including a forfeited rental deposit, provided the employee sought to obtain a refund or took steps to minimize costs.
