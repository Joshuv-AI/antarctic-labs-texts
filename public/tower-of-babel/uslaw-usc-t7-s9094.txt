7 U.S.C. § 9094 — Assignment of payments
Citation: us:usc:7:9094
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section9094
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 115 (Agricultural Commodity Policy And Programs); Subchapter V (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The provisions of section 590h(g) of title 16, relating to assignment of payments, shall apply to payments made under this chapter.
(b) Notice
The producer making the assignment, or the assignee, shall provide the Secretary with notice, in such manner as the Secretary may require, of any assignment made under this section.
