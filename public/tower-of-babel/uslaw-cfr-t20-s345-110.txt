20 C.F.R. § 345.110 — Reports of compensation of employees.
Citation: us:cfr:20:345.110
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-345.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 345 (Employers' Contributions And Contribution Reports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of part 209 of this chapter shall be applicable to the reporting of compensation under the Railroad Unemployment Insurance Act to the same extent and in the same manner as they are applicable to the reporting of compensation under the Railroad Retirement Act.
