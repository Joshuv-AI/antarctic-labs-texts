7 C.F.R. § 772.17 — Equal opportunity and non-discrimination requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:7:772.17
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-772.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 772 (Servicing Minor Program Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

With respect to any aspect of a credit transaction, the Agency will comply with the requirements of the Equal Credit Opportunity Act and the Department's civil rights policy in 7 CFR part 15d.
