17 U.S.C. § 1330 — Common law and other rights unaffected
Citation: us:usc:17:1330
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title17-section1330
Context: United States Code, Title 17 (Copyrights); Chapter 13 (Protection Of Original Designs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall annul or limit—
(1)
common law or other rights or remedies, if any, available to or held by any person with respect to a design which has not been registered under this chapter; or
(2)
any right under the trademark laws or any right protected against unfair competition.
