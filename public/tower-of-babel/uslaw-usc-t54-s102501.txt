54 U.S.C. § 102501 — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:54:102501
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section102501
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 1025 (Museums). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of this chapter is to increase the public benefits from museums established within System units as a means of informing the public concerning the areas and preserving valuable objects and relics relating to the areas.
