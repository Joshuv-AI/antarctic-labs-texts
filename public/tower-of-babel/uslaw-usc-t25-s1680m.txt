25 U.S.C. § 1680m — Results of demonstration projects
Citation: us:usc:25:1680m
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section1680m
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 18 (Indian Health Care); Subchapter VI (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall provide for the dissemination to Indian tribes of the findings and results of demonstration projects conducted under this chapter.
