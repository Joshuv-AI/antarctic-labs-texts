7 U.S.C. § 2443 — Initial appeal
Citation: us:usc:7:2443
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2443
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 57 (Plant Variety Protection); Subchapter II (Protectability Of Plant Varieties And Certificates Of Protection); Part F (Examinations; Response Time; Initial Appeals). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When an application for plant variety protection has been refused by the Plant Variety Protection Office, the applicant may appeal to the Secretary. The Secretary shall seek the advice of the Plant Variety Protection Board on all appeals, before deciding the appeal.
