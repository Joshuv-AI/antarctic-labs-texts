10 C.F.R. § 430.45 — Hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:10:430.45
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-430.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 430 (Energy Conservation Program For Consumer Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary may hold a public hearing, and publish notice in the Federal Register of the date and location of the hearing, when he determines that such a hearing is necessary and likely to result in a timely and effective resolution of the issues. A transcript shall be kept of any such hearing.
