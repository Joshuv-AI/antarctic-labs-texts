46 C.F.R. § 190.05-1 — -1 Application.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 190 (Construction And Arrangement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The provisions of this subpart shall apply to all vessels, except as noted otherwise in this subpart.
(b) Non-self-propelled vessels of less than 300 gross tons shall not be subject to the provisions of this subpart.
