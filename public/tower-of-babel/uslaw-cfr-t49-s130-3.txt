49 C.F.R. § 130.3 — General requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:49:130.3
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-130.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 130 (Oil Spill Prevention And Response Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person may offer or accept for transportation or transport oil subject to this part unless that person—
(a) Complies with this part; and
(b) Has been instructed on the applicable requirements of this part.
