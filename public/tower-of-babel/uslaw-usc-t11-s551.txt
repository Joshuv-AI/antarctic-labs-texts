11 U.S.C. § 551 — Automatic preservation of avoided transfer
Citation: us:usc:11:551
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section551
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 5 (Creditors, The Debtor, And The Estate); Subchapter III (The Estate). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any transfer avoided under section 522, 544, 545, 547, 548, 549, or 724(a) of this title, or any lien void under section 506(d) of this title, is preserved for the benefit of the estate but only with respect to property of the estate.
