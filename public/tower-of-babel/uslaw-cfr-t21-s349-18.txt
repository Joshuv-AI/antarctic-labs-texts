21 C.F.R. § 349.18 — Ophthalmic vasoconstrictors.
Citation: us:cfr:21:349.18
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-349.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 349 (Ophthalmic Drug Products For Over-The-Counter Human Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The active ingredient of the product consists of one of the following, within the established concentration for each ingredient:
(a) Ephedrine hydrochloride, 0.123 percent.
(b) Naphazoline hydrochloride, 0.01 to 0.03 percent.
(c) Phenylephrine hydrochloride, 0.08 to 0.2 percent.
(d) Tetrahydrozoline hydrochloride, 0.01 to 0.05 percent.
