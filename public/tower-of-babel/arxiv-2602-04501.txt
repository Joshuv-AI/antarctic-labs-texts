Abstract

Here we present the simple example of an Exotic Invariant with just two chiral electron supermultiplets
{\widehat{E}}
and
{\widehat{P}}
. In this example we include a mass term, and that means that there is a constraint on the Exotic Invariant. The constraint is easily solved for this simple case. Here we also exhibit a simple conjecture for the Completion Terms. This simple example is very useful, because the constraint that arises in the case of the XM, in E6, is just as easy to solve, and the Completion Terms there are also very similar to those here. So this simple EP model is very useful for understanding the XM, which is what results from adding an Exotic Invariant to the rather complicated Supersymmetric Standard Model.
