22 C.F.R. § 238.9 — No acceleration of Eligible Notes.
Citation: us:cfr:22:238.9
Creator: Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-238.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 238 (Hashemite Kingdom Of Jordan Loan Guarantees Issued Under The Department Of State, Foreign Operations, And Related Programs Appropriations Act, 2015—Standard Terms And Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Eligible Notes shall not be subject to acceleration, in whole or in part, by USAID, the Noteholder or any other party. USAID shall not have the right to pay any amounts in respect of the Eligible Notes other than in accordance with the original payment terms of such Eligible Notes.
