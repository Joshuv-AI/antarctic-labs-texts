48 C.F.R. § 235.070-2 — 235.070-2 Indemnification under contracts involving both research and development and other work.
Citation: us:cfr:48:235.070-2
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-235.070-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 235 (Research And Development Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These contracts may provide for indemnification under the authority of both 10 U.S.C. 3861 and Public Law 85-804. Public Law 85-804 will apply only to work to which 10 U.S.C. 3861 does not apply. Actions under Public Law 85-804 must also comply with FAR 50.104-3.
