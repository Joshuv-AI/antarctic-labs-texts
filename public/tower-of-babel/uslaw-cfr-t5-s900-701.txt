5 C.F.R. § 900.701 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:5:900.701
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-900.701
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 900 (Intergovernmental Personnel Act Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to effectuate section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973, to eliminate discrimination on the basis of handicap in any program or activity receiving Federal financial assistance from the Office of Personnel Management (OPM).
