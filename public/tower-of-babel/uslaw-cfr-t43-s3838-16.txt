43 C.F.R. § 3838.16 — What happens if the surface owner of the SRHA lands changes?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3838.16
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3838.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3838 (Special Procedures For Locating And Recording Mining Claims And Tunnel Sites On Stockraising Homestead Act (Srha) Lands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the surface owner transfers all or part of the surface to a new owner after you have recorded a NOITL and served it on the surface owner, you do not have to serve a copy of the NOITL on the new surface owners.
