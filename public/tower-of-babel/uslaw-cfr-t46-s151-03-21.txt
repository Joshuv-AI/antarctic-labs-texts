46 C.F.R. § 151.03-21 — -21 Filling density.
Citation: us:cfr:46:151.03-21
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-151.03-21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 151 (Barges Carrying Bulk Liquid Hazardous Material Cargoes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The ratio, expressed as a percentage, of the weight of cargo that may be loaded into a tank compared to the weight of water that the tank will hold at 60 °F. The weight of a gallon of water at 60 °F in air shall be 8.32828 pounds.
