48 U.S.C. § 1421k — Designation of naval or military reservations; closed port
Citation: us:usc:48:1421k
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1421k
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 8A (Guam); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing contained in this chapter shall be construed as limiting the authority of the President to designate parts of Guam as naval or military reservations, nor to restrict his authority to treat Guam as a closed port with respect to the vessels and aircraft of foreign nations.
