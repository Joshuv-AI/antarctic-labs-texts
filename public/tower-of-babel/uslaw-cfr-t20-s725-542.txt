20 C.F.R. § 725.542 — When waiver of adjustment or recovery may be applied.
Citation: us:cfr:20:725.542
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-725.542
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 725 (Claims For Benefits Under Part C Of Title Iv Of The Federal Mine Safety And Health Act, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

There shall be no adjustment or recovery of an overpayment in any case where an incorrect payment has been made with respect to an individual:
(a) Who is without fault, and where
(b) Adjustment or recovery would either:
(1) Defeat the purpose of title IV of the Act, or
(2) Be against equity and good conscience.
