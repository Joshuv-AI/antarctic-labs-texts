33 C.F.R. § 80.1114 — San Pedro Bay—Anaheim Bay, CA.
Citation: us:cfr:33:80.1114
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-80.1114
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 80 (Colregs Demarcation Lines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A line drawn across the seaward extremities of the Anaheim Bay Entrance Jetties; thence to Long Beach Breakwater East End Light 1.
(b) A line drawn from Long Beach Channel Entrance Light 2 to Long Beach Light.
(c) A line drawn from Los Angeles Main Entrance Channel Light 2 to Los Angeles Light.
