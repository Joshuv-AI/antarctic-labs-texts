DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

Internal Revenue Service


Open Meeting of the Taxpayer Advocacy Panel's Special Projects 
Committee; Correction

AGENCY: Internal Revenue Service (IRS) Treasury.

ACTION: Notice of meeting: correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In the Federal Register that was originally published on June 
11, 2025, the Designated Federal Officer

[[Page 31580]]

signing this document is being changed to John A. Lipold. All other 
meeting details remain unchanged. This meeting will be held as a 
virtual video conference via the Microsoft Teams platform.

DATES: The meeting will be held Thursday, July 10, 2025, at 11:00 a.m. 
Eastern Time.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ann Tabat at 1-888-912-1227, (602) 
636-9143, or by email at [email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Notice is hereby given pursuant to Section 
10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory Committee Act, 5 U.S.C. App. (1988), 
that an open meeting of the Taxpayer Advocacy Panel Special Projects 
Project Committee will be held on Thursday, July 10, 2025, at 11:00 
a.m. Eastern Time.
    The public is invited to attend the meeting virtually, or by phone, 
and may provide oral comments or submit written statements for 
consideration. Due to meeting structure and time limitations, advance 
registration is required to attend or make public comments during the 
meeting. To register and receive meeting access information, please 
contact Ann Tabat at the contact information above no later than 
Thursday, July 3, 2025.
    Meeting materials, including the agenda and any handouts, will be 
made available prior to the meeting at www.improveirs.org.
    The agenda will include a committee discussion of new and 
continuing issues and other activities related to the new TAP year.

    Dated: July 7, 2025.
John A. Lipold,
Designated Federal Official, Taxpayer Advocacy Panel.
