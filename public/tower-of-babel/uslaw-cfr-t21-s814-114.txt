21 C.F.R. § 814.114 — Timeframes for reviewing an HDE.
Citation: us:cfr:21:814.114
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-814.114
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 814 (Premarket Approval Of Medical Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within 75 days after receipt of an HDE that is accepted for filing and to which the applicant does not submit a major amendment, FDA shall send the applicant an approval order, an approvable letter, a not approvable letter (under § 814.116), or an order denying approval (under § 814.118).
