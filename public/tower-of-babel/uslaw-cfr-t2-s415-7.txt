2 C.F.R. § 415.7 — Federal interagency coordination.
Citation: us:cfr:2:415.7
Creator: Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-415.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 415 (General Program Administrative Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary, to the extent practicable, consults with and seeks advice from all other substantially affected Federal departments and agencies in an effort to assure full coordination between such agencies and the Department regarding programs and activities covered under these regulations.
