48 C.F.R. § 3028.311-1 — 3028.311-1 Contract clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:3028.311-1
Creator: Chapter 30—Department Of Homeland Security, Homeland Security Acquisition Regulation (Hsar)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-3028.311-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 3028 (Bonds And Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Insert a clause substantially similar to (HSAR) 48 CFR 3052.228-70, “Insurance,” in all solicitations and contracts that contain the clause at (FAR) 48 CFR 52.228-7, unless waived by an official one level above the contracting officer.
