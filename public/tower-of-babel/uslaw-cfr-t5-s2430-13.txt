5 C.F.R. § 2430.13 — Exceptions to Administrative Law Judge's decision; briefs; action of Authority.
Citation: us:cfr:5:2430.13
Creator: Federal Labor Relations Authority, General Counsel Of The Federal Labor Relations Authority And Federal Service Impasses Panel
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-2430.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 2430 (Awards Of Attorney Fees And Other Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Procedures before the Authority, including the filing of exceptions to the administrative law judge's decision rendered pursuant to § 2430.12, and action by the Authority, shall be in accordance with §§ 2423.26(c), 2423.27, and 2423.28 of these rules. The Authority's review of the matter shall be in accordance with § 2423.29(a).
