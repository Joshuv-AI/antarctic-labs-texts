24 C.F.R. § 200.81 — Disbursement of mortgage proceeds.
Citation: us:cfr:24:200.81
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-200.81
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 200 (Introduction To Fha Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The mortgagee shall be obligated, as a part of the mortgage transaction, to disburse the principal amount of the mortgage to the:
(a) Mortgagor or mortgagor's account;
(b) Mortgagor's creditors for the mortgagor's account, subject to the mortgagor's consent.
