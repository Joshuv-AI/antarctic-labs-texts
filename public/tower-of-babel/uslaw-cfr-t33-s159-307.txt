33 C.F.R. § 159.307 — Untreated sewage.
Citation: us:cfr:33:159.307
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-159.307
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 159 (Marine Sanitation Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person shall discharge any untreated sewage from a cruise vessel into the applicable waters of Alaska.
