FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Kenneth Gatten III (202-708-1447),

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Effective May 1, 2025, the Commission

schedule as follows: the staff report will be placed in the nonpublic 
record on November 26, 2025; comments are due on December 2, 2025.
    For further information concerning this proceeding, see the 
Commission's notice cited above and the Commission's Rules of Practice 
and Procedure, part 201, subparts A and B (19 CFR part 201), and part 
207, subparts A, D, E, and F (19 CFR part 207).
    Authority: This proceeding is being conducted under authority of 
title VII of the Tariff Act of 1930; this notice is published pursuant 
to section 207.62 of the Commission's rules.
