12 C.F.R. § 308.168 — Effective date of and payment under an order to pay.
Citation: us:cfr:12:308.168
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-308.168
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 308 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Unless otherwise provided in the Notice of Assessment of Liability, payment of the assessment shall be due on or before the 21st day after service of the Assessment of Liability, under the terms of the schedule for payment set forth therein.
(b) All payments collected shall be paid to the Corporation.
(c) Failure to request a hearing as prescribed herein shall render the order to pay final and unappealable.
