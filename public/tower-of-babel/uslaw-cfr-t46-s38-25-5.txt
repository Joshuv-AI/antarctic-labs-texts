46 C.F.R. § 38.25-5 — -5 Removal of defective tanks—TB/ALL.
Citation: us:cfr:46:38.25-5
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-38.25-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 38 (Liquefied Flammable Gases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a tank fails to pass the tests prescribed in this subpart, it shall be removed from service unless otherwise authorized by the Commandant.
