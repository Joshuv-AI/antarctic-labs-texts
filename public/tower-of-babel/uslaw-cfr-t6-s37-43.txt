6 C.F.R. § 37.43 — Physical security of DMV production facilities.
Citation: us:cfr:6:37.43
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security, Office Of The Secretary
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-6/section-37.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 6 (Domestic Security), Part 37 (Real Id Driver'S Licenses And Identification Cards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) States must ensure the physical security of facilities where driver's licenses and identification cards are produced, and the security of document materials and papers from which driver's licenses and identification cards are produced or manufactured.
(b) States must describe the security of DMV facilities as part of their security plan, in accordance with § 37.41.
