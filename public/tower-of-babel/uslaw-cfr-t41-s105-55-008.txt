41 C.F.R. § 105-55.008 — -55.008 No private rights created.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-55.008
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-55.008
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-55 (55—Collection Of Claims Owed The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The standards in this part do not create any right or benefit, substantive or procedural, enforceable at law or in equity by a party against the United States, its agencies, its officers, or any other person, nor shall the failure of the General Services Administration to comply with any of the provisions of this part be available to any debtor as a defense.
