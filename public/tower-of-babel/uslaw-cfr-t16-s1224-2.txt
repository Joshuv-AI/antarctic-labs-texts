16 C.F.R. § 1224.2 — Requirements for portable bed rails.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1224.2
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1224.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1224 (Safety Standard For Portable Bed Rails). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each portable bed rail as defined in ASTM F2085-19, Standard Consumer Safety Specification for Portable Bed Rails, approved on November 1, 2019, must comply with all applicable provisions of ASTM F2085-19. The Director of the Federal Register approves the incorporation by reference listed in this section in accordance with 5 U.S.C. 552(a) and 1 CFR part 51.
