24 C.F.R. § 700.160 — Eligibility and priority for 1978 Act recipients.
Citation: us:cfr:24:700.160
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Housing And Urban Development (Housing Assistance Programs And Public And Indian Housing Programs)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-700.160
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 700 (Congregate Housing Services Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Grantees funded initially under 42 U.S.C. 8001 shall be eligible to receive continued, non-competitive funding subject to its availability. These grantees will be eligible to receive priority funding under this part if they comply with the regulations in this part and with the requirements of any NOFA issued in a particular fiscal year.
