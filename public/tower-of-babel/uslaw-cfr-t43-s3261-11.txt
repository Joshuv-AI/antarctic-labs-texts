43 C.F.R. § 3261.11 — How do I apply for approval of drilling operations and well pad construction?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3261.11
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3261.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3200 (Geothermal Resource Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Send to BLM:
(1) A completed and signed drilling permit application, Form 3260-2;
(2) A complete operations plan (§ 3261.12);
(3) A complete drilling program (§ 3261.13); and
(4) An acceptable bond (§ 3261.18).
(b) Do not start any drilling operations until after BLM approves the permit.
