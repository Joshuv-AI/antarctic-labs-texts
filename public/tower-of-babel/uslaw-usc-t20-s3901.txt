20 U.S.C. § 3901 — Statement of purpose
Citation: us:usc:20:3901
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section3901
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 52 (Education For Economic Security). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the purpose of this chapter to improve the quality of mathematics and science teaching and instruction in the United States.
