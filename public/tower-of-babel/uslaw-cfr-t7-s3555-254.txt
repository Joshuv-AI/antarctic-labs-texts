7 C.F.R. § 3555.254 — Final payments.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3555 (Guaranteed Rural Housing Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Lenders may release security instruments only after payment for the satisfaction of the full debt, including any recapture, has been received and verified.
