25 C.F.R. § 224.71 — What standards will the Secretary use to decide to approve a final proposed TERA?
Citation: us:cfr:25:224.71
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-224.71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 224 (Tribal Energy Resource Agreements Under The Indian Tribal Energy Development And Self Determination Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary must approve a final proposed TERA unless:
(a) The Tribe does not meet the definition of a “qualified Tribe” in § 224.30;
(b) A provision of the TERA violates applicable Federal law (including regulations) or a treaty applicable to the Tribe; or
(c) The TERA fails to include the provisions required by § 224.63.
