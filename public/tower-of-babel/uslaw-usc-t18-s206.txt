18 U.S.C. § 206 — Exemption of retired officers of the uniformed services
Citation: us:usc:18:206
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section206
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 11 (Bribery, Graft, And Conflicts Of Interest). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Sections 203 and 205 of this title shall not apply to a retired officer of the uniformed services of the United States while not on active duty and not otherwise an officer or employee of the United States, or to any person specially excepted by Act of Congress.
