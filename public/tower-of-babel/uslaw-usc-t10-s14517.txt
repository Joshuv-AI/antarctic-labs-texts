10 U.S.C. § 14517 — Entitlement of officers discharged under this chapter to separation pay
Citation: us:usc:10:14517
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section14517
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Promotion And Retention Of Officers On The Reserve Active-Status List); Chapter 1407 (Failure Of Selection For Promotion And Involuntary Separation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An officer who is discharged under section 14513, 14514, or 14515 of this title is entitled to separation pay under section 1174 of this title if otherwise eligible under that section.
