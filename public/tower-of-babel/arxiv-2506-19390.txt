Alexis Bouthier, David Kazhdan, Yakov Varshavsky

Abstract
. Using techniques of [ BKV ] , we construct a perverse t t -structure on the ∞ \infty -category of ℓ \ell -adic ℒ ⁡ G \cL G -equivariant sheaves on the regular-semisimple bounded locus of the loop group ℒ ⁡ G \cL G and prove that the derived τ \tau -coinvariants of affine Grothendieck–Springer sheaves are perverse. Our main new ingredient is a theorem of Yun [ Yun2 ] on compatibility of actions.

Affine character sheaves.
(a) Perverse sheaves

(b) Likewise we expect that perverse sheaves

Relation to local Langlands conjectures and stability.

The local Langlands conjecture predicts that the set of isomorphism classes of smooth irreducible representations of
G(\mathbb{F}_{q}((t)))
has a natural partition into finite subsets
\Pi_{\lambda}
, called
L
-packets.

Moreover, it is expected that the linear span
\operatorname{Span}\{\chi_{\pi}\}_{\pi\in\Pi_{\lambda}}
of characters of
\pi\in\Pi_{\lambda}
has another basis
\{\chi_{\lambda}^{\kappa}\}_{\kappa}
such that each
\chi_{\lambda}^{\kappa}
is “
\cal E_{\lambda,\kappa}
-stable”. Furthermore, it is expected that each
\chi_{\lambda}^{\kappa}
is obtained from a perverse sheaf on
[\cL G/\cL G]
by the “sheaf-function correspondence”.

Using results of
[
BV
]
, the perversity of
\mathcal{S}_{\cL,\bullet,\tau}
implies a version of the above conjecture for
L
-packets
of cuspidal Deligne–Lusztig representations, introduced in
[
KV
]
.

Plan of the paper.
The paper is organized as follows:

In the Section 1 we carry out geometric preliminaries:
First, in Section 1.1, we recall various notions from
[
BKV
]
, including placid
\infty
-stacks, placidly stratified
\infty
-stacks
and small morphisms. Next, in Section 1.2, we introduce
\Gamma
-constructible and essentially constructible sheaves on
\infty
-stacks
and study their properties. We also introduce a subclass of placid
\infty
-stacks, which we call
admissible
.
Then, in Section 1.3, we recall a construction of perverse
t
-structures on placidly stratified
\infty
-stacks, introduced and studied
in
[
BKV
]
, and give several criteria we need later. Finally, in Section 1.4, we show several simple properties of quasi-coherent sheaves, which play a central role in the proof of our main theorem.

In Section 2, we prove group analogs of some of the results of
[
BKV
]
: First, in Section 2.1, we show flatness of the Chevalley map for
truncated arc spaces, deducing it from its analog for Lie algebras. Next, in Section 2.2, we introduce a GKM-stratification and
give a proof of a formula for the codimension of strata, which is much shorter than the original one. Finally, in Section 2.3, we study basic properties of the affine Grothendieck–Springer fibration, essentially mimicking the corresponding assertions for Lie algebras.

In Section 3, we introduce affine Grothendieck–Springer sheaves
\mathcal{S}_{\cL,\bullet}
and study their properties.
First, in Section 3.1, we show that each
\mathcal{S}_{\cL,\bullet}
is perverse, equipped with a natural
\widetilde{W}
-action and
that the induced action on homologies of affine Springer fibers coincides with the action constructed by Lusztig. Finally, in Section 3.2,
we show the main result of this work asserting that sheaves of
\tau
-coinvariants
\mathcal{S}_{\cL,\bullet,\tau}
are perverse.
For completeness, we also show the corresponding assertion for Lie algebras, whose proof is almost identical.

1.2.1

Sheaves on
\infty
-prestacks
(compare
[
BKV
, Sections 5.2.1, 5.2.2, 5.3.1]
).
Let
\ell
be a prime, different from the characteristic of
k
.

(a) For an affine scheme
Y\in\Aff^{\operatorname{ft}}_{k}
, we denote by
\mathcal{D}_{c}(Y):=\mathcal{D}_{c}(Y,\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{l})
the stable
\infty
-category
of constructible sheaves on
Y
. Then, for an affine scheme
X
over
k
, we set
\mathcal{D}_{c}(X):=\colim_{X\to Y}\mathcal{D}_{c}(Y)
, where the colimit is taken over all morphisms

X\to Y
with
Y\in\Aff^{\operatorname{ft}}_{k}
, and the transition maps are
!
-pullbacks. Next, we set
\mathcal{D}(X):=\Ind\mathcal{D}_{c}(X)
.

(b) For an
\infty
-prestack
\cal X
over
k
, we set
\mathcal{D}_{c}(\cal X):=\lim_{X\to\cal X}\mathcal{D}_{c}(X)
and
\mathcal{D}(\cal X):=\lim_{X\to\cal X}\mathcal{D}(X)
, where the limits are taken over all morphisms
X\to\cal X
with
X
affine, and the transition maps are
!
-pullbacks. Notice that
\mathcal{D}_{c}(\cal X)\subseteq\mathcal{D}(\cal X)
is a full
\infty
-subcategory, and we call objects of
\mathcal{D}_{c}(\cal X)

constructible
.
By construction, the
\infty
-category
\mathcal{D}(\cal X)
is equipped with a
!
-tensor product.

(c) Notice that for every
Y\in\Sch^{\operatorname{ft}}_{k}
or, more generally,

the
\infty
-category

is naturally identifies with the
\infty

We will need the following generalization of constructible sheaves.

1.2.2
.

Essentially constructible sheaves.

(a) For an affine
k
-scheme
X
, we denote by
\mathcal{D}_{\operatorname{ess}-c}(X)\subseteq\mathcal{D}(X)
the smallest full
\infty
-subcategory, which contains

\mathcal{D}_{c}(X)
and is closed under retracts, finite colimits and tensor products
-\otimes_{\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}}V
, where
V
is a
\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}
-vector space.

(b) For a morphism
f:X\to Y
of affine
k
-schemes, the pullback
f^{!}
preserves the
\infty
-subcategories
\mathcal{D}_{\operatorname{ess}-c}(-)\subseteq\mathcal{D}(-)
, so
for an arbitrary
\infty
-prestack
\mathcal{X}
, we can form a full
\infty
-subcategory

\mathcal{D}_{\operatorname{ess}-c}(\mathcal{X}):=\lim_{X\to\cal X}\mathcal{D}_{\operatorname{ess}-c}(X)\subseteq\mathcal{D}(\cal X),

and call objects of
\mathcal{D}_{\operatorname{ess}-c}(\mathcal{X})

essentially constructible
.

(c) By construction, for every morphism
f:\mathcal{X}\to\mathcal{Y}
of
\infty
-prestacks, the pullback
f^{!}
preserves the class of essentially constructible objects.

Lemma 1.2.3
.

Let
X\in\Sch^{\operatorname{ft}}_{k}
and
K\in\mathcal{D}(X)
. Then we have
K\in\mathcal{D}_{\operatorname{ess}-c}(X)
if and only if there exists a finite constructible stratification
\{X_{\alpha}\}_{\alpha}
of
X
such that for each index
\alpha
,

\bullet
only finitely many of cohomologies
\cal H^{i}(\eta_{\alpha}^{!}(K))
’s are nonzero.

\bullet
each
\cal H^{i}(\eta_{\alpha}^{!}(K))
is a finite extension of
L\otimes_{\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}}V
, where
L
is an irreducible constructible local system
on
X
, and
V
is a
\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}
-vector space.

Moreover, we can further assume that each
X_{\alpha}
is connected smooth and affine, while each embedding
\eta_{\alpha}:X_{\alpha}\to X
is weakly equidimensional.

Proof.

Let
\mathcal{D}^{\prime}_{\operatorname{ess}-c}(X)
be the collection of all
K\in\mathcal{D}(X)
such that there exists a finite constructible stratification
\{X_{\alpha}\}_{\alpha}
of
X
satisfying the conditions of the lemma.
Then
\mathcal{D}^{\prime}_{\operatorname{ess}-c}(X)
contains
\mathcal{D}_{c}(X)
, closed under retracts, finite colimits and tensor products
-\otimes_{\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}}V
and therefore contains
\mathcal{D}_{\operatorname{ess}-c}(X)
.

It remains to show that every
K\in\mathcal{D}^{\prime}_{\operatorname{ess}-c}(X)
belongs to
\mathcal{D}_{\operatorname{ess}-c}(X)
. Since
K
is a finite extension of the

(\eta_{\alpha})_{*}\eta_{\alpha}^{!}K
’s, it suffices to show that each
(\eta_{\alpha})_{*}\eta_{\alpha}^{!}(K)
is essentially constructible.
Next, since each
(\eta_{\alpha})_{*}
preserve constructibility, finite colimits, retracts and commute with
-\otimes_{\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{l}}V
, it preserves essentially constructibility. So it suffices to show that each
\eta_{\alpha}^{!}(K)
is essentially constructible. But this follows from the fact that
each
\eta_{\alpha}^{!}(K)
is a finite extension of its shifted cohomologies
\mathcal{H}^{i}(\eta_{\alpha}^{!}(K))[-i]
and definition of
\mathcal{D}^{\prime}_{\operatorname{ess}-c}(X)
.

Finally, to show the “moreover” assertion we argue similarly but use the fact that every constructible stratification
\{X^{\prime}_{\beta}\}_{\beta}
has a refinement
\{X_{\alpha}\}_{\alpha}
such that each
X_{\alpha}
is connected smooth and affine, while each embedding
\eta_{\alpha}:X_{\alpha}\to X
is weakly equidimensional.
∎

1.2.4
.

Admissible
\infty
-stacks.

(a) We call

(b) Following
[

(c) It follows from

, is fully faithful.

(d) We call a placid
\infty
-stack

(e) An argument of
[
BKV
, Lemma 1.3.6(b)]
shows that
f:X\to Y
is an fp-morphism of
k
-schemes such that

Lemma 1.2.5
.

Let
X
be an affine
k
-scheme with an admissible presentation

. Then every
K\in\mathcal{D}_{\operatorname{ess}-c}(X)

is a pullback of some
K_{\alpha}\in\mathcal{D}_{\operatorname{ess}-c}(X_{\alpha})

Proof.

We denote by
\mathcal{D}^{\prime}_{\operatorname{ess}-c}(X)\subseteq\mathcal{D}_{\operatorname{ess}-c}(X)
the full
\infty
-subcategory, consisting of objects, which are pullbacks
of some

. By definition,
\mathcal{D}^{\prime}_{\operatorname{ess}-c}(X)
contains
\mathcal{D}_{c}(X)
and is closed under tensor

-\otimes_{\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{l}}V
. It remains to show that
\mathcal{D}^{\prime}_{\operatorname{ess}-c}(X)
is closed under finite colimits and retracts.
But this follows from the fact that the limit

is filtered and each

∎

1.2.6
.

Categories of
\Gamma
-equivariant objects.

(a) Let
\Gamma
be a (discrete) group, and let
B\Gamma
be the classifying space of
\Gamma
. For an
\infty
-category
\mathcal{C}
, we denote by

\mathcal{C}^{B\Gamma}
the
\infty
-category of functors
B\Gamma\to\mathcal{C}
, and call it the
\infty
-category of
\Gamma
-equivariant objects
in
\mathcal{C}
. Explicitly, objects of
\mathcal{C}^{B\Gamma}
are objects of
\mathcal{C}
equipped with an action of
\Gamma
.

(b) The assignment
\mathcal{C}\mapsto\mathcal{C}^{B\Gamma}
is functorial in
\Gamma
. In particular, for every homomorphism of groups
\alpha:\Gamma_{1}\to\Gamma_{2}
, we have a restriction functor
\res_{\Gamma_{2}}^{\Gamma_{1}}=\alpha^{*}:\mathcal{C}^{B\Gamma_{2}}\to\mathcal{C}^{B\Gamma_{1}}
,
whose left adjoint we denote by
\ind_{\Gamma_{1}}^{\Gamma_{2}}=\alpha_{!}
(if exists).
Notice that
\alpha_{!}
always exists, if
\mathcal{C}
is
cocomplete
, that is, has all small colimits.

(c) An important particular case is when
\alpha
is the projection
\Gamma\to\{1\}
. In this case,
\alpha_{!}
is the functor of coinvariants
\coinv_{\Gamma}
. Another important particular case is when
\alpha
is the embedding
\{1\}\to\Gamma
. In this case,
\alpha^{*}
is the forgetful functor
\For
, and
\alpha_{!}
is the functor
\operatorname{Free}
.

(d) The assignment
\cal C\to\cal C^{B\Gamma}
is functorial in
\cal C
. In particular, every functor
f:\cal C_{1}\to\cal C_{2}
of
\infty
-categories
naturally lifts to a functor
f:\cal C^{B\Gamma}_{1}\to\cal C^{B\Gamma}_{2}
. Moreover,
f
commutes with functors
\alpha^{*}
from part (b). Furthermore,

f
commutes with functors
\alpha_{!}
, if
\cal C_{1}
and
\cal C_{2}
are cocomplete and
f
preserves all small colimits.

.

Let
\cal C
be the derived
\infty
-category
\Vect
of
\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{l}
-vector spaces. In this case, the
\infty
-category

\cal C^{B\Gamma}
is naturally identified with the derived
\infty
-category
\cal D(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{l}[\Gamma])
of (left)
\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{l}[\Gamma]
-modules or, what is the same,

\tau

(a) Let
\cal C

-tensored stable

(b) Assume in addition that

(a) Let

1.2.10

The case of a trivial action.
Let
\mathcal{X}
be an
\infty
-stack.

(a) For every group
\Gamma
, we equip
\mathcal{X}
with the trivial action of
\Gamma
. Then, using standard bar-resolution argument, there is a natural equivalence between the
\infty

\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{X})^{B\Gamma}
of
\Gamma

\mathcal{D}(X)
and the
\infty

\mathcal{D}([\cal X/\Gamma])

\mathcal{X}

(b) A group homomorphism

induces a morphism of

Lemma 1.2.11
.

Let
\cal X
be an
\infty
-stack equipped with an action of a group
\Gamma
, and let
p
be the projection
\cal X\to\cal Y:=[\cal X/\Gamma]
.
Then

(a) For every
K\in\mathcal{D}(\cal Y)
, the object
p_{!}p^{!}K\in\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{Y})
has a natural lift
\widetilde{K}\in\mathcal{D}(\cal Y)^{B\Gamma}
and satisfies

\coinv_{\Gamma}(\widetilde{K})\simeq K\in\mathcal{D}(\cal Y).

(b) Moreover, for every representation
\tau\in\Rep_{\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{l}}(\Gamma)
, we have a natural isomorphism

\coinv_{\tau}(\widetilde{K})\simeq A_{\tau}\overset{!}{\otimes}K,

where
A_{\tau}=\pr^{!}(\tau)
,
\pr:[\mathcal{X}/\Gamma]\to[\pt/\Gamma]
is the canonical projection corresponding to the projection
\mathcal{X}\to\pt
, and we identify
\tau
with the corresponding object
of
\mathcal{D}([\pt/\Gamma])

Proof.

(a) Projection

\begin{CD}\mathcal{X}@>{p}>{}>\mathcal{Y}\\
@V{p}V{}V@V{}V{p}V\\
\mathcal{Y}=[\mathcal{X}/\Gamma]@>{\overline{p}}>{}>[\mathcal{Y}/\Gamma],\end{CD}

where
\Gamma
acts on
\mathcal{Y}
trivially, and
\overline{p}
is induced by
p
. Then, by base change, we get a canonical isomorphism
p_{!}p^{!}K\simeq p^{!}(\overline{p}_{!}K)
, thus
\widetilde{K}:=\overline{p}_{!}K\in\cal D([\mathcal{Y}/\Gamma])\simeq\cal D(\mathcal{Y})^{B\Gamma}
is a lift of
p_{!}p^{!}K
.

Moreover, under the identification
\cal D([\mathcal{Y}/\Gamma])\simeq\cal D(\mathcal{Y})^{B\Gamma}
, the functor
\coinv_{\Gamma}
corresponds to the push-forward

\pi_{!}:\mathcal{D}([\mathcal{Y}/\Gamma])\to\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{Y})
, corresponding to the projection
\pi:[\mathcal{Y}/\Gamma]\to\mathcal{Y}
.
Since
\pi\circ\overline{p}\simeq\Id
, the isomorphism
\coinv_{\Gamma}(\widetilde{K})=\coinv_{\Gamma}(\overline{p}_{!}K)\simeq K
follows.

(b) By definitions, we have
\coinv_{\tau}(\widetilde{K})\simeq\coinv_{\Gamma}(\tau\otimes_{\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{l}}\widetilde{K})
,
and
\tau\otimes_{\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{l}}\widetilde{K}\simeq\overline{\pr}^{!}(\tau)\overset{!}{\otimes}\widetilde{K}
, where

\overline{\pr}:[\mathcal{Y}/\Gamma]\to[\pt/\Gamma]
is the projection induced by the projection
\mathcal{Y}\to\pt
. Moreover, we have

\overline{\pr}^{!}(\tau)\overset{!}{\otimes}\widetilde{K}=\overline{\pr}^{!}(\tau)\overset{!}{\otimes}\overline{p}_{!}(K)\simeq\overline{p}_{!}(\overline{p}^{!}\overline{\pr}^{!}(\tau))\overset{!}{\otimes}K)\simeq\overline{p}_{!}(\pr^{!}(\tau)\overset{!}{\otimes}K)=\overline{p}_{!}(A_{\tau}\overset{!}{\otimes}K)

by the projection formula. Hence, as in part (a), we conclude that

\coinv_{\tau}(\widetilde{K})\simeq\coinv_{\Gamma}(\overline{p}_{!}(A_{\tau}\overset{!}{\otimes}K))\simeq A_{\tau}\overset{!}{\otimes}K.

∎

1.2.12
.

\Gamma
-constructible sheaves.
Assume that we are in the situation of Section

.

(a) For every

, we denote by

\infty

\infty

of affine schemes, the pullback

preserves

\mathcal{D}_{\Gamma,c}(-)\subseteq\mathcal{D}^{\Gamma}(-)
.

(b) The assignment
\mathcal{C}\mapsto\mathcal{C}^{B\Gamma}
commutes with limits. Therefore for every
\infty
-stack
\cal X
, the
\infty
-category
\mathcal{D}^{\Gamma}(\cal X)
is naturally identified with a limit
\lim_{X\to\cal X}\mathcal{D}^{\Gamma}(X)
, taken over maps from affine schemes

. Hence, using part (a), we define the full
\infty

\mathcal{D}_{\Gamma,c}(\cal X):=\lim_{X\to\cal X}\mathcal{D}_{\Gamma,c}(X)\subseteq\mathcal{D}(\cal X).

Lemma 1.2.15
.

Let
\mathcal{X}
be an
\infty
-stack, let
\Gamma
be a group, and let
K\in\mathcal{D}^{\Gamma}(\mathcal{X})
.

(a) Let
\{\mathcal{X}_{\alpha}\}_{\alpha}
be a bounded constructible stratification of
\mathcal{X}
. Then we have
K\in\mathcal{D}_{\Gamma,c}(\mathcal{X})
if and only if
\eta_{\alpha}^{!}(K)\in\mathcal{D}_{\Gamma,c}(\mathcal{X}_{\alpha})
for all
\alpha
.

(b) Let
f:\mathcal{Y}\to\mathcal{X}
be a covering of
\infty
-stacks. Then we have
K\in\mathcal{D}_{\Gamma,c}(\mathcal{X})
if and only if
f^{!}K\in\mathcal{D}_{\Gamma,c}(\mathcal{Y})
.

(c) If
K\in D_{\Gamma,c}(\cal X)
, then for every
\tau\in\Rep_{\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{l}}(\Gamma)
, we have
K_{\tau}\in\mathcal{D}_{\operatorname{ess}-c}(\cal X)
. Moreover, we have

K_{\tau}\in\mathcal{D}_{c}(\cal X)
, if
\tau
is finite-dimensional.

Proof.

(a) The “only if” assertion follows from the fact that the
!
-pullbacks preserve
\mathcal{D}_{\Gamma,c}
.
For the converse, choose a presentation
\cal X=\colim_{U}\cal X_{U}
as in Section
1.1.3
(c). Since every morphism
X\to\mathcal{X}
from
an affine scheme
X
factors through some
\cal X_{U}
, we conclude that
K\in\mathcal{D}_{\Gamma,c}(\mathcal{X})
if and only if
K|_{\mathcal{X}_{U}}\in\mathcal{D}_{\Gamma,c}(\mathcal{X}_{U})
for all
U
. Thus, replacing
\cal X
and
K
by
\mathcal{X}_{U}
and
K|_{\mathcal{X}_{U}}
, we can assume that the stratification is finite.

Next, taking pullback to an affine scheme, we can assume that
X
is an affine scheme. Since
K
is a finite extension of the
(\eta_{\alpha})_{*}\eta_{\alpha}^{!}(K)
’s, it remains to show that functors
(\eta_{\alpha})_{*}
preserve
\Gamma
-constructibility.
If
X_{\alpha}
is affine, the assertion follows from the fact that
(\eta_{\alpha})_{*}
preserve compact objects. To show the general case,
choose a finite constructible stratification
\{X_{\beta}\}_{\beta}
of
X_{\alpha}
by affine schemes and
let
\eta_{\beta}
be the composition
X_{\beta}\hookrightarrow X_{\alpha}\hookrightarrow X
. Then the assertion for
(\eta_{\alpha})_{*}
follows from that for the
(\eta_{\beta})_{*}
’s.

(b) By construction, the
!
-pullbacks preserves
\mathcal{D}_{\Gamma,c}
. To show the converse assertion, notice that for every morphism

X\to\cal X
from an affine scheme
X
there exists an étale covering
Y\to X
such that the composition
Y\to X\to\mathcal{X}
has a lift
Y\to\mathcal{Y}
. Thus, it suffices to show the assertion when
f:Y\to X
is an étale covering of affine schemes. Then
f
is finitely-presented,
so there exists a finite constructible stratification
\{X_{\alpha}\}_{\alpha}
of
X
such that each
Y_{\alpha}:=f^{-1}(X_{\alpha})\to X_{\alpha}
is finite
étale. Thus, by part (a), we can assume that
f
is finite étale. In this case, the assertion follows from the fact that
K
is a
retract of
f_{!}f^{!}(K)
.

(c) Since
\coinv_{\tau}
commutes with
!
-pullbacks, we can assume that
\mathcal{X}
is an affine scheme and

K=\Free(K^{\prime})
for some
K^{\prime}\in\mathcal{D}_{c}(X)
. Using isomorphisms
\tau\otimes_{\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{l}}\Free(K^{\prime})\simeq\Free(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{l}^{\dim\tau}\otimes_{\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{l}}K)
and

\coinv_{\Gamma}\circ\Free\simeq\Id
, we conclude that in this case we have
\coinv_{\tau}(K)\simeq\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{l}^{\dim\tau}\otimes_{\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{l}}K
,
from which the assertion follows.
∎

Lemma 1.2.16
.

Let
f:X\to S
a morphism of schemes, let
\Gamma
be a group acting on
X
over
S
such that
Y:=[X/\Gamma]
is an algebraic space, fp-proper over
S
. Then the pushforward
f_{!}:\mathcal{D}(X)\to\mathcal{D}(S)
exists, and for every
K\in\mathcal{D}(Y)
, the object
f_{!}(p^{!}K)\in\mathcal{D}(S)
has a natural lift to
\mathcal{D}^{\Gamma}(S)
. Moreover, this lift
belongs to
\mathcal{D}_{\Gamma,c}(S)
if
K\in\mathcal{D}_{c}(Y)
.

Proof.

Morphism
f
gives rise to a Cartesian diagram

\begin{CD}X@>{f}>{}>S\\
@V{p}V{}V@V{}V{p}V\\
Y=[X/\Gamma]@>{\overline{f}}>{}>[S/\Gamma],\end{CD}

where
\Gamma
acts on
S
trivially. Moreover, morphisms
f
and
\overline{f}
decomposes as
X\overset{p}{\to}Y\overset{g}{\to}S
and

Y\overset{g}{\to}S\overset{p}{\to}[S/\Gamma]
, respectively, and morphism
g:Y\to S
is fp-proper. By
[
BKV
, Proposition 5.2.5]
, our assumption on
g
implies that
g^{!}
has a left adjoint
g_{!}
, from which the existence of
f_{!}
and
\overline{f}_{!}
follow.

Moreover, since
p
is étale, we have an isomorphism

f_{!}(p^{!}K)\simeq p^{!}(\overline{f}_{!}K)
, thus
f_{!}(p^{!}K)\in\mathcal{D}(S)
has a natural lift
\overline{f}_{!}K\in\mathcal{D}([S/\Gamma])\simeq\mathcal{D}^{\Gamma}(S)
.
Finally, if

, then

, thus
\overline{f}_{!}K=p_{!}(g_{!}K)\in\mathcal{D}_{\Gamma,c}(S)
.
∎

Proposition 1.2.17
.

Let
\mathcal{X}
be an admissible
\infty
-stack, let
\Gamma
be a group such that
\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{l}[\Gamma]
is Noetherian of finite cohomological dimension, and let
K\in\mathcal{D}^{\Gamma}(\mathcal{X})
. Then we have
K\in\mathcal{D}_{\Gamma,c}(\mathcal{X})
if and only if we have
\For(K)\in\mathcal{D}_{\operatorname{ess}-c}(\mathcal{X})

and
\iota_{x}^{!}K\in\mathcal{D}_{\Gamma,c}(\pt)=\mathcal{D}_{\perf}(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{l}[\Gamma])
for every
x\in\mathcal{X}(k)
.

Proof.

By Lemma
1.2.15
(b), we can assume that
\mathcal{X}
is an admissible affine scheme
X
.

Assume first
X
is of finite type over
k
. If
K\in\mathcal{D}_{\Gamma,c}(X)
, then
\For(K)\in\mathcal{D}_{\operatorname{ess}-c}(X)
by Section
1.2.14
(b), and each
\iota_{x}^{!}K\in\mathcal{D}_{\Gamma,c}(\pt)
because
\iota_{x}^{!}
preserves
\Gamma
-constructibility. Conversely, assume that
\overline{K}:=\For(K)\in\mathcal{D}_{\operatorname{ess}-c}(X)
and
\iota_{x}^{!}K\in\mathcal{D}_{\perf}(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{l}[\Gamma])
for every
x\in X(k)
.

By Lemma
1.2.15
(a), it suffices to show that there exists a finite constructible stratification
\{X_{\alpha}\}_{\alpha}
of
X
such that

\eta_{\alpha}^{!}K\in\mathcal{D}_{\Gamma,c}(X_{\alpha})
for all
\alpha
. Thus, by Lemma
1.2.3
, we can replace
K
by
\eta_{\alpha}^{!}(K)
, thus assuming that
X
is smooth connected,
\overline{K}\in\mathcal{D}^{b}(X)
and each
\mathcal{H}^{i}(\overline{K})
is a finite extension of the
L\otimes_{\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{l}}V
’s, where
L
is an irreducible constructible local system on
X
and
V
is a
\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{l}
-vector space.

Since
\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{l}[\Gamma]
is Noetherian of finite cohomological dimension, we have
\iota_{x}^{!}K\in\mathcal{D}_{\perf}(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{l}[\Gamma])
if and only if

\bigoplus_{i}H^{i}(\iota_{x}^{!}K)
is a finitely generated
\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{l}[\Gamma]
-module. Since
K
is a finite extension of its shifted cohomologies, and
\iota_{x}^{!}[-2\dim X]\simeq\iota^{*}_{x}
is exact, we can replace
K
by
\bigoplus_{i}\cal H^{i}(K)
, thus assuming that
\overline{K}=\For(K)
is a finite extension of the
L\otimes_{\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{l}}V
’s, where
L
is a simple (constructible) local system on
X
and
V
is a
\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{l}
-vector space.

In this case,
\overline{K}
has a canonical finite increasing filtration
\{\overline{K}_{\leq i}\}_{i}
, defined by the property that
\overline{K}_{\leq i+1}/\overline{K}_{\leq i}
is the largest semisimple local subsystem of
\For(K)/\For(K)_{\leq i}
. In particular, each
\overline{K}_{\leq i}
is
\Gamma
-invariant, so filtration
\{\overline{K}_{\leq i}\}_{i}
gives rise to a filtration
\{K_{\leq i}\}_{i}
of
K
.

Replacing
K
by its graded piece
\gr^{i}(K)
, we can assume that
\overline{K}
is semisimple and has only many simple factors. In this case,
K
decomposes as a finite direct sum
\bigoplus_{L}K_{L}
, where
L
runs over the set of isomorphism classes of simple local systems
L
on
X
, and
K_{L}:=\Hom(L,\overline{K})\otimes_{\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{l}}L
. Now our assumption that each
H^{i}(\iota_{x}^{!}K)
is a finitely generated
\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{l}[\Gamma]
-module
implies that each
\Hom(L,\overline{K})
is a finitely generated
\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{l}[\Gamma]
-module. Thus
K_{L}\in\mathcal{D}_{\Gamma,c}(X)
for all
L
, hence
K\in\mathcal{D}_{\Gamma,c}(X)
.

In the general case, choose an admissible presentation
X\simeq\lim_{\alpha}X_{\alpha}
. Then
\For(K)
is a pullback of
\For(K)_{\alpha}\in\mathcal{D}_{\operatorname{ess}-c}(X_{\alpha})\subseteq\mathcal{D}(X_{\alpha})
for some
\alpha
(by Lemma
1.2.5
). Next, using the fact that the pullback
\pi_{\alpha}^{!}:\mathcal{D}(X_{\alpha})\to\mathcal{D}(X)
is fully faithful, we conclude that the natural functor

\mathcal{D}^{\Gamma}(X_{\alpha})\to\mathcal{D}(X_{\alpha})\times_{\mathcal{D}(X)}\mathcal{D}^{\Gamma}(X)

is an equivalence. Thus,
K
is a pullback of a unique object
K_{\alpha}\in\mathcal{D}^{\Gamma}(X_{\alpha})
such that

\For(K_{\alpha})\simeq\For(K)_{\alpha}\in\mathcal{D}_{\operatorname{ess}-c}(X_{\alpha}).

Since presentation
X\simeq\lim_{\alpha}X_{\alpha}
is admissible, the projection
\pi_{\alpha}:X(k)\to X_{\alpha}(k)
is surjective. Hence,
our assumption implies that
\iota_{x_{\alpha}}^{!}(K_{\alpha})\in\mathcal{D}_{\Gamma,c}(\pt)
for every
x_{\alpha}\in X_{\alpha}(k)
. Then, we get
K_{\alpha}\in\mathcal{D}_{\Gamma,c}(X_{\alpha})
(by the particular case shown above), hence
K=\pi_{\alpha}^{!}(K_{\alpha})\in\mathcal{D}_{\Gamma,c}(X)
.
∎

1.4.
Lemmas on quasi-coherent sheaves

Motivated by a nice trick explained to us by Zhiwei Yun, in this section we will establish simple properties of quasi-coherent sheaves, playing a central role in the proof of Proposition
3.2.3
.

Lemma 1.4.1
.

Let
i:X\hookrightarrow Y
be a closed embedding of codimension
d
between smooth connected schemes over
k
. Then

(a) the derived pullback functor
i^{*}:D^{+}(\operatorname{QCoh}(Y))\to D^{+}(\operatorname{QCoh}(X))
sends
D^{\geq 0}
to
D^{\geq-d}
;

(b) for every non-zero sheaf
K\in\operatorname{QCoh}(Y)
, which is scheme-theoretically supported on
X
, we have
\mathcal{H}^{-d}(i^{*}K)\neq 0
.

Proof.

(a) follows from the Koszul resolution of
i_{*}\mathcal{O}_{X}
.

(b) By assumption, one has
K=i_{*}M
for some
M\in\operatorname{QCoh}(X)
. Thus, by the projection formula, we have
i^{*}i_{*}M\simeq M\otimes_{\mathcal{O}_{X}}i^{*}i_{*}\mathcal{O}_{X}
, and the assertion follows from the Koszul resolution for
i_{*}\mathcal{O}_{X}
as well.
∎

Corollary 1.4.2
.

In the situation of Lemma
1.4.1
, let
K\in D^{\geq 0}(\operatorname{QCoh}(Y))
be such that
\mathcal{H}^{0}(K)
has a non-zero subsheaf
K^{\prime}
, which is scheme-theoretically supported on
X
. Then
\mathcal{H}^{-d}(i^{*}K)\neq 0
.

Proof.

Consider the fiber sequence

i^{*}\mathcal{H}^{0}(K)\to i^{*}K\to i^{*}\tau^{>0}K.

Since
i^{*}\tau^{>0}K
belongs to
D^{\geq-d+1}
by Lemma
1.4.1
(a), we conclude that
\mathcal{H}^{-d}(i^{*}\mathcal{H}^{0}(K))\simeq\mathcal{H}^{-d}(i^{*}K)
. Thus, replacing
K
by
\mathcal{H}^{0}(K)
, we can assume that
K\in\operatorname{QCoh}(Y)
. Then, using Lemma
1.4.1
(a) for
i^{*}(K/K^{\prime})
, we obtain that the map

\mathcal{H}^{-d}(i^{*}K^{\prime})\to\mathcal{H}^{-d}(i^{*}K)

is injective. Since
\mathcal{H}^{-d}(i^{*}K^{\prime})\neq 0
by Lemma
1.4.1
(b), the assertion follows.
∎

Proposition 1.4.3
.

Let
\eta_{1},\dots,\eta_{m}:X\to Y
be closed embeddings of smooth connected
k
-schemes, let
p_{X}:X\times Y\to X
and

p_{Y}:X\times Y\to Y
be projections, and let
K\in D^{+}(\operatorname{QCoh}(X\times Y))
be such that

(i)
K
is set-theoretically supported on the union of the graphs of the
\eta_{a}
’s;

(ii) we have
K\otimes p_{X}^{*}A\in D^{\geq 0}
for all
A\in\operatorname{QCoh}(X)
.

Then we have
K\otimes p_{Y}^{*}B\in D^{\geq\dim X-\dim Y}
for all
B\in\operatorname{QCoh}(Y)
.

Proof.

Let
n
be the minimal integer
j
such that
\mathcal{H}^{j}(K\otimes p_{Y}^{*}B)\neq 0
. We want to show that
n\geq\dim X-\dim Y
. By assumption (i), there exists a nonzero subsheaf
K^{\prime}\subseteq\mathcal{H}^{n}(K\otimes p_{Y}^{*}B)
, which is scheme-theoretically supported on the graph of some
\eta_{a}
.

Consider morphism

. It is a closed embedding of smooth connected
k
-schemes of codimension

.
Thus, by Corollary
1.4.2
, we have

Using isomorphisms

and Lemma

(a), it suffices to prove that

.
By Lemma

(a), we have

(a) The isomorphism

(b) By assumption, there is a Cartesian diagram

Corollary 3.1.4

(a) For a local system
\cL
on
