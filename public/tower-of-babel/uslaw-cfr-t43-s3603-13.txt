43 C.F.R. § 3603.13 — What price does BLM charge under materials sales contracts for mineral materials from community pits and common use areas?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3603.13
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3603.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3600 (Mineral Materials Disposal). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

BLM will sell mineral materials from community pits or common use areas under materials sales contracts for not less than fair market value.
