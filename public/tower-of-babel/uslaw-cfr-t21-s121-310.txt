21 C.F.R. § 121.310 — Additional requirements applying to the food defense plan.
Citation: us:cfr:21:121.310
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-121.310
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 121 (Mitigation Strategies To Protect Food Against Intentional Adulteration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The owner, operator, or agent in charge of the facility must sign and date the food defense plan:
(a) Upon initial completion; and
(b) Upon any modification.
