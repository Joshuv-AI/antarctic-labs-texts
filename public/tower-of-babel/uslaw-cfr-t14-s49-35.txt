14 C.F.R. § 49.35 — Eligibility for recording: ownership requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:14:49.35
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-49.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 49 (Recording Of Aircraft Titles And Security Documents). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the seller of an aircraft is not shown on the records of the FAA as the owner of the aircraft, a conveyance, including a contract of conditional sale, submitted for recording under this subpart must be accompanied by bills of sale or similar documents showing consecutive transfers from the last registered owner, through each intervening owner, to the seller.
