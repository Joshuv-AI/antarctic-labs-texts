16 U.S.C. § 251c — Administration of acquired lands
Citation: us:usc:16:251c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section251c
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter XXVII (Olympic National Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Lands acquired pursuant to the exchange authority contained herein shall be administered as a part of Olympic National Park in accordance with the laws and regulations applicable to the park.
