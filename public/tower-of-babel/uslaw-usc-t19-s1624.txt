19 U.S.C. § 1624 — General regulations
Citation: us:usc:19:1624
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section1624
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 4 (Tariff Act Of 1930); Part V (Enforcement Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In addition to the specific powers conferred by this chapter the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized to make such rules and regulations as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this chapter.
