Correction: NRF2 signaling plays an essential role in cancer progression through the NRF2-GPX2-NOTCH3 axis in head and neck squamous cell carcinoma
Authors: Xiaoye Jin, Xiayuan Lou, Haoxiang Qi, Chao Zheng, Bo Li, Xuerong Siwu, Ren Liu, Qiaoli Lv, An Zhao, Jian Ruan, Ming Jiang
Published: 2026

Correction to: Oncogenesis

https://doi.org/10.1038/s41389-024-00536-z published online 27 September 2024

Following the publication of this article, the authors noted an error in Figure 2F. Specifically, an incorrect representative Transwell invasion image was inadvertently used for the sgCtrl group in Figure 2F during figure preparation. We have replaced the incorrect image in Figure 2F with the correct representative image from the original experiment, as shown below. This correction is limited to the representative image and does not affect the underlying quantitative data, statistical analyses, interpretation, and conclusions of the article. The authors apologise for the error.

The original article has been corrected.
