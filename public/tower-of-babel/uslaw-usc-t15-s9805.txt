15 U.S.C. § 9805 — Data on domestic travel and tourism
Citation: us:usc:15:9805
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section9805
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 122 (Travel And Tourism). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Assistant Secretary of Commerce for Travel and Tourism, subject to the availability of appropriations, shall collect and make public aggregate data on domestic travel and tourism trends.
