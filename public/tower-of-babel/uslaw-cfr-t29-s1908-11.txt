29 C.F.R. § 1908.11 — Exclusions.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1908.11
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1908.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1908 (Consultation Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Cooperative Agreement under this part will not restrict in any manner the authority and responsibility of the Assistant Secretary under sections 8, 9, 10, 13, and 17 of the Act, or any corresponding State authority.
