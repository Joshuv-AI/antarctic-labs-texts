19 U.S.C. § 1622 — Foreign landing certificates
Citation: us:usc:19:1622
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section1622
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 4 (Tariff Act Of 1930); Part V (Enforcement Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Treasury may by regulations require the production of landing certificates in respect of merchandise exported from the United States, or in respect of residue cargo, in cases in which he deems it necessary for the protection of the revenue, or to comply with international obligations.
