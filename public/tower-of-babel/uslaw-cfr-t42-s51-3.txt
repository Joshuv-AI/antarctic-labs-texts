42 C.F.R. § 51.3 — Formula for determining allotments.
Citation: us:cfr:42:51.3
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-51.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 51 (Requirements Applicable To The Protection And Advocacy For Individuals With Mental Illness Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary shall make allotments to eligible Systems from amounts apportioned each year under the Act on the basis of a formula prescribed by the Secretary in accordance with the requirements of sections 112 and 113 of the Act (42 U.S.C. 10822 and 10823).
