40 C.F.R. § 1066.120 — Measurement instruments.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1066.120
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1066.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1066 (Vehicle-Testing Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The measurement instrument requirements in 40 CFR part 1065, subpart C, apply with the following exceptions:
(a) The provisions of § 1066.125 apply instead of 40 CFR 1065.202.
(b) The provisions of 40 CFR 1065.210 and 1065.295 do not apply.
