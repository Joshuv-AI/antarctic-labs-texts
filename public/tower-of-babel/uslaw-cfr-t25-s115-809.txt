25 C.F.R. § 115.809 — May a tribe recommend to OTFM how to invest the tribe's trust funds?
Citation: us:cfr:25:115.809
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-115.809
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 115 (Trust Funds For Tribes And Individual Indians). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Tribes may recommend certain investments to OTFM, but the recommendations must be in accordance with the statutory requirements set forth in 25 U.S.C. §§ 161a and 162a. The OTFM will make the final investment decision based on prudent investment practices.
