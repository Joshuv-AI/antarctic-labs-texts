25 C.F.R. § 224.130 — What is the purpose of this subpart?
Citation: us:cfr:25:224.130
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-224.130
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 224 (Tribal Energy Resource Agreements Under The Indian Tribal Energy Development And Self Determination Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart describes how the Secretary and a Tribe will develop and perform the periodic review and evaluation required by the Act and by a TERA.
