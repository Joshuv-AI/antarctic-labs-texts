40 C.F.R. § 46.225 — Equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:40:46.225
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-46.225
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 46 (Fellowships). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If EPA authorizes you to purchase equipment (see § 46.140(b)) and the equipment retains a fair market value of more than $5,000, you must request disposition instructions from the EPA project officer when you no longer need it for the work under the fellowship.
(b) If you purchase an item with an acquisition cost of $5,000 or less, the item belongs to you.
