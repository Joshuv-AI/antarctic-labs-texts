5 U.S.C. § 1205 — Transmittal of information to Congress
Citation: us:usc:5:1205
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section1205
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part II (Civil Service Functions And Responsibilities); Chapter 12 (Merit Systems Protection Board, Office Of Special Counsel, And Employee Right Of Action); Subchapter I (Merit Systems Protection Board). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law or any rule, regulation or policy directive, any member of the Board, or any employee of the Board designated by the Board, may transmit to the Congress on the request of any committee or subcommittee thereof, by report, testimony, or otherwise, information and views on functions, responsibilities, or other matters relating to the Board, without review, clearance, or approval by any other administrative authority.
