15 U.S.C. § 4808 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:15:4808
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section4808
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 74 (Competitiveness Policy Council). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated for each of the fiscal years 1991 and 1992 such sums as may be necessary not to exceed $5,000,000 to carry out the provisions of this chapter.
