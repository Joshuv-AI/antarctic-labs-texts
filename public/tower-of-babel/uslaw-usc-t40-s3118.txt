40 U.S.C. § 3118 — Right of taking as addition to existing rights
Citation: us:usc:40:3118
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section3118
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part A (General); Chapter 31 (General); Subchapter II (Acquiring Land). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The right to take possession and title in advance of final judgment in condemnation proceedings as provided by section 3114 of this title is in addition to any right, power, or authority conferred by the laws of the United States or of a State, territory, or possession of the United States under which the proceeding may be conducted, and does not abrogate, limit, or modify that right, power, or authority.
