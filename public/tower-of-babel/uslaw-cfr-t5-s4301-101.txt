5 C.F.R. § 4301.101 — Prior approval for outside employment.
Citation: us:cfr:5:4301.101
Creator: Us International Development Finance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-4301.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 4301 (Supplemental Standards Of Ethical Conduct For Employees Of The Us International Development Finance Corporation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any employee of the US International Development Finance Corporation (DFC) who is interested in engaging in outside employment must first obtain approval from the Designated Agency Ethics Official before engaging in such employment activity. For this purpose, employment has the meaning set forth in § 2635.603(a) of this title.
