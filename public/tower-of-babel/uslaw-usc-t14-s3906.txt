14 U.S.C. § 3906 — Membership in other organizations
Citation: us:usc:14:3906
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section3906
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 39 (Coast Guard Auxiliary). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Members of the Auxiliary may be appointed or enlisted in the Reserve, pursuant to applicable regulations, and membership in the Auxiliary shall not be a bar to membership in any other naval or military organization.
