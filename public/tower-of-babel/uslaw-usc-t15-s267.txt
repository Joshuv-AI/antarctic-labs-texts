15 U.S.C. § 267 — “State” defined
Citation: us:usc:15:267
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section267
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 6 (Weights And Measures And Standard Time); Subchapter IX (Standard Time). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this Act, the term “State” includes the District of Columbia, the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico, Guam, the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands, or any possession of the United States.
