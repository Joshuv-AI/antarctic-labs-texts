7 U.S.C. § 5941 — Purchase of cultures for soil and fertilizer investigations
Citation: us:usc:7:5941
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section5941
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 88 (Research); Subchapter VII (Miscellaneous Research Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Agriculture may purchase from applicable appropriations cultures in the open market for use in connection with soil and fertilizer investigations.
