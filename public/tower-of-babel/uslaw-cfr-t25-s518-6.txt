25 C.F.R. § 518.6 — What are the responsibilities of the Office of Self-Regulation in the certification process?
Citation: us:cfr:25:518.6
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-518.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 518 (Self-Regulation Of Class Ii Gaming). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Office of Self-Regulation shall be responsible for directing and coordinating the certification process. It shall provide a written report and recommendation to the Commission as to whether a certificate of self-regulation should be issued or denied, and a copy of the report and recommendation to the petitioning tribe.
