40 U.S.C. § 5103 — Restrictions on public use of United States Capitol Grounds
Citation: us:usc:40:5103
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section5103
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part B (United States Capitol); Chapter 51 (United States Capitol Buildings And Grounds). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Public travel in, and occupancy of, the United States Capitol Grounds is restricted to the roads, walks, and places prepared for that purpose.
