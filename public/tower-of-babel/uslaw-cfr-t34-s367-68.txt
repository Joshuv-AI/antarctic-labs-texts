34 C.F.R. § 367.68 — What notice must be given about the Client Assistance Program (CAP)?
Citation: us:cfr:34:367.68
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-367.68
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 367 (Independent Living Services For Older Individuals Who Are Blind). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The DSA and all other service providers under this part shall use formats that are accessible to notify individuals seeking or receiving services under this part about—
(a) The availability of CAP authorized by section 112 of the Act;
(b) The purposes of the services provided under the CAP; and
(c) How to contact the CAP.
