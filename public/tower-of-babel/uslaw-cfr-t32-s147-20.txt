32 C.F.R. § 147.20 — Exception to periods of coverage.
Citation: us:cfr:32:147.20
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-147.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 147 (Adjudicative Guidelines For Determining Eligibility For Access To Classified Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Some elements of standards specify a period of coverage (e.g. seven years). Where appropriate, such coverage may be shortened to the period from the subject's eighteenth birthday to the present or to two years, whichever is longer.
