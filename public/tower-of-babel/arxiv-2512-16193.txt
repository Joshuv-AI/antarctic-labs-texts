Emergence of higher-order interactions in systems of coupled Kuramoto oscillators with time delay
Narumi Fujii, Keisuke Taga, Riccardo Muolo, Bob Rink, Hiroya Nakao

Abstract
Understanding the mechanisms that govern collective synchronization is a paramount task in nonlinear dynamics. While higher-order (many-body) interactions have recently emerged as a powerful framework for capturing collective behaviors, real-world examples regarding dynamics remain scarce. Here, we show that higher-order interactions naturally emerge from time-delayed pairwise coupling in Kuramoto oscillators. By expanding the delay term up to second order in the coupling strength, we derive an effective Kuramoto model featuring both two-body and three-body interactions, but without delay, hence, easier to be analyzed. Numerical simulations show that this reduced model can reproduce the bistability and synchronization transitions of the original time-delayed system. Furthermore, applying the Ott–Antonsen ansatz, we obtain a stability diagram for incoherent and synchronized states that closely matches the results of the original model. Our findings reveal that time delays can be effectively recast in the form of higher-order interactions, offering a new perspective on how delayed interactions shape the dynamics.

\sin x=\frac{x}{1!}-\frac{x^{3}}{3!}+\cdots
, we derive the higher-order Kuramoto model as

\displaystyle\dot{\theta}_{j}(t)=

\displaystyle\ \omega_{j}+\frac{\epsilon}{N}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}k=1\\
k\neq j\end{subarray}}^{N}\sin\left(\theta_{k}(t)-\theta_{j}(t)-\omega_{k}\tau\right)

\displaystyle+\frac{\epsilon^{2}}{N^{2}}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}k=1\\
k\neq j\end{subarray}}^{N}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}l=1\\
l\neq k\end{subarray}}^{N}\cos\left(\theta_{k}(t)-\theta_{j}(t)-\omega_{k}\tau\right)\left\{-\sin\left(\theta_{l}(t)-\theta_{k}(t)-\omega_{l}\tau\right)\frac{\sin\left((\omega_{l}-\omega_{k})\tau\right)}{{\omega_{l}-\omega_{k}}}\right.

\displaystyle\left.+\cos\left(\theta_{l}(t)-\theta_{k}(t)-\omega_{l}\tau\right)\frac{1-\cos\left((\omega_{l}-\omega_{k})\tau\right)}{\omega_{l}-\omega_{k}}\right\}+\mathcal{O}(\epsilon^{3}).

(19)

A.2
Derivation of Eq. (
8
)

We derive Eq. (
8
) from Eq. (
3
) under
the assumption
\gamma=\mathcal{O}(\epsilon)
and
N\gg 1
.
From Eq. (
3
),

\displaystyle\dot{\theta}_{j}(t)

\displaystyle=\omega_{j}+\frac{\epsilon}{N}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}k=1\\
k\neq j\end{subarray}}^{N}\frac{e^{i(\theta_{k}(t)-\theta_{j}(t)-\omega_{k}\tau)}-e^{-i(\theta_{k}(t)-\theta_{j}(t)-\omega_{k}\tau)}}{2i}

\displaystyle\hskip 9.24994pt+\frac{\epsilon^{2}}{N^{2}}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}k=1\\
k\neq j\end{subarray}}^{N}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}l=1\\
l\neq k\end{subarray}}^{N}\frac{e^{i(\theta_{k}(t)-\theta_{j}(t)-\omega_{k}\tau)}+e^{-i(\theta_{k}(t)-\theta_{j}(t)-\omega_{k}\tau)}}{2}\left(\frac{e^{i(\theta_{l}(t)-\theta_{k}(t)-\omega_{l}\tau)}}{2}\frac{1-e^{-i(\omega_{l}-\omega_{k})\tau}}{\omega_{l}-\omega_{k}}\right.

\displaystyle\hskip 9.24994pt\left.+\frac{e^{-i(\theta_{l}(t)-\theta_{k}(t)-\omega_{l}\tau)}}{2}\frac{1-e^{i(\omega_{l}-\omega_{k})\tau}}{\omega_{l}-\omega_{k}}\right).

(20)

Under the assumption that
\gamma=\mathcal{O}(\epsilon)
, we suppose that
\omega_{k}-\omega_{j}=\mathcal{O}(\epsilon)
holds for the majority of oscillator pairs
k
and
j
. Consequently,
\frac{1-e^{\pm i(\omega_{l}-\omega_{k})\tau}}{\omega_{l}-\omega_{k}}=\mp i\tau+\mathcal{O}(\epsilon)
. Hence, we obtain

\displaystyle\dot{\theta}_{j}(t)

\displaystyle\simeq\omega_{j}+\frac{\epsilon}{N}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}k=1\\
k\neq j\end{subarray}}^{N}\frac{e^{i(\theta_{k}(t)-\theta_{j}(t)-\omega_{k}\tau)}-e^{-i(\theta_{k}(t)-\theta_{j}(t)-\omega_{k}\tau)}}{2i}

\displaystyle\hskip 9.24994pt-\frac{\epsilon^{2}\tau}{N^{2}}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}k=1\\
k\neq j\end{subarray}}^{N}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}l=1\\
l\neq k\end{subarray}}^{N}\frac{e^{i(\theta_{k}(t)-\theta_{j}(t)-\omega_{k}\tau)}+e^{-i(\theta_{k}(t)-\theta_{j}(t)-\omega_{k}\tau)}}{2}\frac{e^{i(\theta_{l}(t)-\theta_{k}(t)-\omega_{l}\tau)}-e^{-i(\theta_{l}(t)-\theta_{k}(t)-\omega_{l}\tau)}}{2i}

\displaystyle=\omega_{j}+\frac{\epsilon}{N}\left\{\sum_{k=1}^{N}\frac{e^{i(\theta_{k}(t)-\theta_{j}(t)-\omega_{k}\tau)}-e^{-i(\theta_{k}(t)-\theta_{j}(t)-\omega_{k}\tau)}}{2i}-\frac{e^{-i\omega_{j}\tau}-e^{i\omega_{j}\tau}}{2i}\right\}

\displaystyle\hskip 9.24994pt-\frac{\epsilon^{2}\tau}{N^{2}}\left[\sum_{k=1}^{N}\frac{e^{i(\theta_{k}(t)-\theta_{j}(t)-\omega_{k}\tau)}+e^{-i(\theta_{k}(t)-\theta_{j}(t)-\omega_{k}\tau)}}{2}\left\{\sum_{l=1}^{N}\frac{e^{i(\theta_{l}(t)-\theta_{k}(t)-\omega_{l}\tau)}-e^{-i(\theta_{l}(t)-\theta_{k}(t)-\omega_{l}\tau)}}{2i}-\frac{e^{-i\omega_{k}\tau}-e^{i\omega_{k}\tau}}{2i}\right\}\right.

\displaystyle\hskip 9.24994pt-\left.\frac{e^{-i\omega_{j}\tau}+e^{i\omega_{j}\tau}}{2}\left\{\sum_{l=1}^{N}\frac{e^{i(\theta_{l}(t)-\theta_{j}(t)-\omega_{l}\tau)}-e^{-i(\theta_{l}(t)-\theta_{j}(t)-\omega_{l}\tau)}}{2i}-\frac{e^{-i\omega_{j}\tau}-e^{i\omega_{j}\tau}}{2i}\right\}\right].

(21)

By using general complex order parameters in Eq. (
9
),

\displaystyle\dot{\theta}_{j}(t)

\displaystyle\simeq\omega_{j}+\epsilon\left\{\frac{e^{-i\theta_{j}(t)}z_{1}-e^{i\theta_{j}(t)}z_{1}^{*}}{2i}+\mathcal{O}(N^{-1})\right\}

\displaystyle\hskip 9.24994pt-\epsilon^{2}\tau\left[\sum_{k=1}^{N}\left\{\frac{e^{-i(\theta_{j}(t)+\omega_{j}\tau)}e^{i(\omega_{j}-\omega_{k})\tau}z_{1}-e^{-i(\theta_{j}(t)-\omega_{j}\tau)}e^{2i(\theta_{k}(t)-\omega_{k}\tau)}e^{-i(\omega_{j}-\omega_{k})\tau}z_{1}^{*}}{4iN}\right.\right.

\displaystyle\hskip 9.24994pt\left.\left.+\frac{e^{i(\theta_{j}(t)-\omega_{j}\tau)}e^{-2i(\theta_{k}(t)-\omega_{k}\tau)}e^{i(\omega_{j}-\omega_{k})\tau}z_{1}-e^{i(\theta_{j}(t)+\omega_{j}\tau)}e^{-i(\omega_{j}-\omega_{k})\tau}z_{1}^{*}}{4iN})\right\}+\mathcal{O}(N^{-1})\right],

(22)

where
e^{\pm i(\omega_{j}-\omega_{k})\tau}=1+\mathcal{O}(\epsilon)
, then

\displaystyle\dot{\theta}_{j}(t)

\displaystyle\simeq\omega_{j}+\epsilon\left\{\frac{e^{-i\theta_{j}(t)}z_{1}-e^{i\theta_{j}(t)}z_{1}^{*}}{2i}+\mathcal{O}(N^{-1})\right\}

\displaystyle\hskip 9.24994pt-\epsilon^{2}\tau\left\{\sum_{k=1}^{N}\frac{e^{-i(\theta_{j}(t)+\omega_{j}\tau)}z_{1}-e^{-i(\theta_{j}(t)-\omega_{j}\tau)}e^{2i(\theta_{k}(t)-\omega_{k}\tau)}z_{1}^{*}+e^{i(\theta_{j}(t)-\omega_{j}\tau)}e^{-2i(\theta_{k}(t)-\omega_{k}\tau)}z_{1}-e^{i(\theta_{j}(t)+\omega_{j}\tau)}z_{1}^{*}}{4iN}+\mathcal{O}(N^{-1})\right\}

\displaystyle=\omega_{j}+\epsilon\left\{\frac{e^{-i\theta_{j}(t)}z_{1}-e^{i\theta_{j}(t)}z_{1}^{*}}{2i}+\mathcal{O}(N^{-1})\right\}

\displaystyle\hskip 9.24994pt-\epsilon^{2}\tau\left\{\frac{e^{-i(\theta_{j}(t)+\omega_{j}\tau)}z_{1}-e^{-i(\theta_{j}(t)-\omega_{j}\tau)}z_{1}^{*}z_{2}+e^{i(\theta_{j}(t)-\omega_{j}\tau)}z_{1}z_{2}^{*}-e^{i(\theta_{j}(t)+\omega_{j}\tau)}z_{1}^{*}}{4i}+\mathcal{O}(N^{-1})\right\}.

(23)

Under the assumption of
N\gg 1
, we derive

\displaystyle\dot{\theta}_{j}(t)

\displaystyle\simeq\omega_{j}+\frac{e^{-i\theta_{j}(t)}H_{j}-e^{i\theta_{j}(t)}H_{j}^{*}}{2i},

(24)

where
H_{j}=\epsilon z_{1}(t)-\frac{\epsilon^{2}\tau}{2}\left(e^{-i\omega_{j}\tau}z_{1}-e^{i\omega_{j}\tau}z_{1}^{*}z_{2}\right)
.

A.3
Ott-Antonsen ansatz

In the
N\rightarrow\infty
limit, the general complex order parameter in Eq. (
9
) is redefined as

\displaystyle z_{m}(t)=\int_{0}^{2\pi}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{im(\theta^{\prime}-\omega^{\prime}\tau)}P(\theta^{\prime},\penalty\ \omega^{\prime},\penalty\ t)g(\omega^{\prime})d\omega^{\prime}d\theta^{\prime},

(25)

and the time evolution of
P(\theta,\omega,t)
is given from Eq. (
8
) as

\displaystyle\frac{\partial P}{\partial t}

\displaystyle=-\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta}\left(\dot{\theta}P\right)

\displaystyle=-\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta}\left[\left\{\omega+\frac{1}{2i}\left(e^{-i\theta}H-e^{i\theta}H^{*}\right)\right\}P\right],

(26)

where
H=\epsilon z_{1}(t)-\frac{\epsilon^{2}\tau}{2}\left(e^{-i\omega\tau}z_{1}-e^{i\omega\tau}z^{*}_{1}z_{2}\right)
.

First, we expand
P(\theta,\omega,t)
in Fourier series as

\displaystyle P(\theta,\omega,t)=\frac{1}{2\pi}\sum_{m=-\infty}^{\infty}P_{m}(\omega,t)e^{im\theta},

(27)

where
P_{m}
is the Fourier coefficient.
Note that
P_{0}(\omega,t)=1
by the normalization condition for
P(\theta,\omega,t)
, i.e.,

\int_{0}^{2\pi}P(\theta,\omega,t)d\theta=1
, and
P_{m}(\omega,t)={P}^{*}_{-m}(\omega,t)
since
P(\theta,\omega,t)
is real.
Substituting Eq. (
27
) into Eq. (
26
), we obtain the equation for
m=1
as

\displaystyle\frac{\partial P_{1}(\omega,t)}{\partial t}

\displaystyle=-im\omega P_{1}(\omega,t)-\frac{1}{2}\left(P_{2}(\omega,t)G-G^{*}\right),

(28)

where

\displaystyle G

\displaystyle=\left(\epsilon-\frac{\epsilon^{2}\tau}{2}e^{-i\omega\tau}\right)\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-i\omega^{\prime}\tau}P_{-1}(\omega^{\prime},t)g(\omega^{\prime})d\omega^{\prime}+\frac{\epsilon^{2}\tau}{2}e^{i\omega\tau}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{i\omega^{\prime}\tau}P_{1}(\omega^{\prime},t)g(\omega^{\prime})d\omega^{\prime}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-2i\omega^{\prime}\tau}P_{-2}(\omega^{\prime},t)g(\omega^{\prime})d\omega^{\prime}.

(29)

We now introduce the Ott-Antonsen ansatz and assume that the Fourier coefficient
P_{m}
can be represented as

\displaystyle P_{m}(\omega,\penalty\ t)=\alpha(\omega,\penalty\ t)^{m},\penalty\ \penalty\ (m=1,\penalty\ 2,\penalty\ \cdots).

(30)

The coefficient
\alpha(\omega,t)
then obeys

\displaystyle\frac{\partial\alpha(\omega,\penalty\ t)}{\partial t}

\displaystyle=-i\omega\alpha(\omega,\penalty\ t)-\frac{1}{2}\left[\alpha(\omega,\penalty\ t)^{2}\left\{\left(\epsilon-\frac{\epsilon^{2}\tau}{2}e^{-i\omega\tau}\right)z_{1}(t)+\frac{\epsilon^{2}\tau}{2}e^{i\omega\tau}{z}^{*}_{1}(t)z_{2}(t)\right\}\right.

\displaystyle\quad\left.-\left\{\left(\epsilon-\frac{\epsilon^{2}\tau}{2}e^{i\omega\tau}\right){z}^{*}_{1}(t)+\frac{\epsilon^{2}\tau}{2}e^{-i\omega\tau}z_{1}(t){z}^{*}_{2}(t)\right\}\right],

(31)

where

\displaystyle z_{1}(t)

\displaystyle=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\alpha(\omega^{\prime},\penalty\ t)^{-1}e^{-i\omega^{\prime}\tau}g(\omega^{\prime})d\omega^{\prime},

(32)

\displaystyle z_{2}(t)

\displaystyle=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\alpha(\omega^{\prime},\penalty\ t)^{-2}e^{-2i\omega^{\prime}\tau}g(\omega^{\prime})d\omega^{\prime},

(33)

from Eq. (
25
) and (
30
).
Under the assumption that
g(\omega)
follows the Lorentzian distribution, we obtain

\displaystyle z_{1}(t)

\displaystyle=\overline{\alpha(\omega_{0}-i\gamma,\penalty\ t)}e^{-i(\omega_{0}-i\gamma)\tau},

(34)

\displaystyle z_{2}(t)

\displaystyle=\overline{\alpha(\omega_{0}-i\gamma,\penalty\ t)}^{2}e^{-2i(\omega_{0}-i\gamma)\tau}=\left\{z_{1}(t)\right\}^{2},

(35)

by applying the residue theorem, where

\alpha(\omega,\penalty\ t)
is analytically continued to the complex plane.
Then, Eq. (
31
) is rewritten as

\displaystyle\frac{d{z}_{1}(t)}{dt}

\displaystyle=\left\{i\omega_{0}-\gamma+\frac{\epsilon}{2}e^{-i(\omega_{0}+i\gamma)\tau}-\frac{\epsilon^{2}\tau}{4}e^{-2i(\omega_{0}+i\gamma)\tau}-\left(\frac{\epsilon}{2}e^{i(\omega_{0}+i\gamma)\tau}-\frac{\epsilon^{2}\tau}{4}-\frac{\epsilon^{2}\tau}{4}e^{2i(\omega_{0}+i\gamma)\tau}\right)|{z}_{1}(t)|^{2}-\frac{\epsilon^{2}\tau}{4}|{z}_{1}(t)|^{4}\right\}{z}_{1}(t).

(36)

For the case of identical oscillators, we consider the limit with
\gamma\rightarrow 0
and derive

\displaystyle\frac{d{z}_{1}(t)}{dt}

\displaystyle=\left\{i\omega_{0}+\frac{\epsilon}{2}e^{-i\omega_{0}\tau}-\frac{\epsilon^{2}\tau}{4}e^{-2i\omega_{0}\tau}-\left(\frac{\epsilon}{2}e^{i\omega_{0}\tau}-\frac{\epsilon^{2}\tau}{4}-\frac{\epsilon^{2}\tau}{4}e^{2i\omega_{0}\tau}\right)|{z}_{1}(t)|^{2}-\frac{\epsilon^{2}\tau}{4}|{z}_{1}(t)|^{4}\right\}{z}_{1}(t).

(37)
