27 C.F.R. § 479.92 — Transportation of firearms to effect transfer.
Citation: us:cfr:27:479.92
Creator: Bureau Of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms, And Explosives, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-479.92
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 479 (Machine Guns, Destructive Devices, And Certain Other Firearms). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notwithstanding any provision of § 478.28 of this chapter, it shall not be required that authorization be obtained from the Director for the transportation in interstate or foreign commerce of a firearm in order to effect the transfer of a firearm authorized under the provisions of this subpart.
