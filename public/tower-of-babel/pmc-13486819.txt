Correction: Cue labeling reduces cigarette craving and associated neural activity
Authors: Golnaz Tabibnia, Dara G Ghahremani, Hilary A Tindle, J David Creswell, Cecilia Westbrook, Thomas E Kraynak, Erica Julson, Edythe D London
Published: 2026

Correction to: Neuropsychopharmacology 10.1038/s41386-025-02297-8, published online 17 December 2025

In the final words of the penultimate sentence of the abstract, the phrase “than cue matching” should be corrected to “than gender labeling,” and the corresponding p-value should be updated from “0.01” to “0.001.” The corrected sentence ending should read: “...activity in this region was lower during cue labeling than gender labeling (Z = −3.9, p-corrected<0.001)”.

The manuscript does not contain text anywhere else that refers to these errors and that would need to be revised.

The original article has been corrected.
