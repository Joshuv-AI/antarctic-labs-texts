21 C.F.R. § 211.89 — Rejected components, drug product containers, and closures.
Citation: us:cfr:21:211.89
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-211.89
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 211 (Current Good Manufacturing Practice For Finished Pharmaceuticals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Rejected components, drug product containers, and closures shall be identified and controlled under a quarantine system designed to prevent their use in manufacturing or processing operations for which they are unsuitable.
