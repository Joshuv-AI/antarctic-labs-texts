22 C.F.R. § 72.4 — Notifications of death.
Citation: us:cfr:22:72.4
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-72.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 72 (Deaths And Estates). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The consular officer should make best efforts to notify the next of kin (or legal guardian), if any, and the legal representative (if any, and if different from the next of kin), of the death of a United States citizen or non-citizen national by telephone as soon as possible, and then should follow up with a written notification of death.
