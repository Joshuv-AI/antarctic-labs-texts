42 C.F.R. § 81.22 — General guidelines for use of NIOSH-IREP.
Citation: us:cfr:42:81.22
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 81 (Guidelines For Determining Probability Of Causation Under The Energy Employees Occupational Illness Compensation Program Act Of 2000). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

DOL will use procedures specified in the NIOSH-IREP Operating Guide to calculate probability of causation estimates under EEOICPA. The guide provides current, step-by-step instructions for the operation of IREP. The procedures include entering personal, diagnostic, and exposure data; setting/confirming appropriate values for variables used in calculations; conducting the calculation; and, obtaining, evaluating, and reporting results.
