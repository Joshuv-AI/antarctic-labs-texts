45 C.F.R. § 287.160 — What reports must a grantee file regarding financial operations?
Citation: us:cfr:45:287.160
Creator: Office Of Family Assistance (Assistance Programs), Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-287.160
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 287 (The Native Employment Works (New) Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Grantees will use SF-269A to make an annual financial report of expenditures for program activities and services.
(b) Two annual financial reports will be due to the appropriate Regional Office. The interim SF-269A is due no later than July 30, i.e., 30 days after the end of the obligation period. The final SF-269A is due 90 days after the end of the liquidation period.
