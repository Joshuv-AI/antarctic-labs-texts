41 C.F.R. § 302-14.2 — -14.2 Eligibility to receive a home marketing incentive payment.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-14.2
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-14.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-14 (14—Home Marketing Incentive Payments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies have the discretion to offer a home marketing incentive (HMI) payment. An employee is eligible if their agency offers the HMI to them.
