31 U.S.C. § 327 — Advancements and reimbursements for services
Citation: us:usc:31:327
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section327
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 3 (Department Of The Treasury); Subchapter II (Administrative). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
In this section, “service” includes service provided in—
(1)
disbursing and receiving amounts.
(2)
servicing bonds.
(3)
making accounts.
(4)
maintaining bank accounts.
(b)
When the Secretary of the Treasury provides a service for an agency (except the Department of the Treasury) for which amounts have not been appropriated to the Department, the agency may advance for credit or reimburse the Department the amounts necessary to provide the service. Notwithstanding section 3302 of this title, amounts advanced or reimbursed may be credited to the appropriation of the Department that is current when the service is provided.
