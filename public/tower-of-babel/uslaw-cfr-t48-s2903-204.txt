48 C.F.R. § 2903.204 — 2903.204 Treatment of violations.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2903.204
Creator: Chapter 29—Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2903.204
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2903 (Improper Business Practices And Personal Conflicts Of Interest). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any suspected violations of FAR subpart 3.2 and the clause at FAR 52.203-3, Gratuities, must be reported to the Office of the Inspector General. The authority to determine whether a violation of the Gratuities clause by the contractor, its agent, or another representative has occurred, and the appropriate remedies, are delegated to the HCA.
