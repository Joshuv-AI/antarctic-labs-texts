2 C.F.R. § 2424.1155 — What is the effect of a limited denial of participation on a suspension or a debarment?
Citation: us:cfr:2:2424.1155
Creator: Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-2424.1155
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 2424 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The imposition of a limited denial of participation does not affect the right of the Department to suspend or debar any person under this part.
