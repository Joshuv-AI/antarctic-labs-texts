42 U.S.C. § 2000g–3 — Reports to Congress
Citation: us:usc:42:2000g–3
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2000g–3
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 21 (Civil Rights); Subchapter VIII (Community Relations Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Subject to the provisions of sections 2000a–4 and 2000g–2(b) of this title, the Director shall, on or before January 31 of each year, submit to the Congress a report of the activities of the Service during the preceding fiscal year.
