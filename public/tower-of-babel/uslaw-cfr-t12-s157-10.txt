12 C.F.R. § 157.10 — What authorities govern the issuance of deposit accounts by Federal savings associations?
Citation: us:cfr:12:157.10
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-157.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 157 (Deposits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Federal savings association (“you”) may raise funds through accounts and may issue evidence of accounts under section 5(b)(1) of the HOLA (12 U.S.C. 1464(b)(1)), your charter, and this part. Additionally, 12 CFR parts 204 and 230 apply to your deposit activities.
