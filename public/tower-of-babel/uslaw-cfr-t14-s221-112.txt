14 C.F.R. § 221.112 — Rejected tariff is void and must not be used.
Citation: us:cfr:14:221.112
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-221.112
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 221 (Tariffs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A tariff rejected by the Department is void and is without any force or effect whatsoever. Such rejected tariff must not be used.
