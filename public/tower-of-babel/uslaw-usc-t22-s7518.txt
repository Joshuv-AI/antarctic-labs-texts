22 U.S.C. § 7518 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:22:7518
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section7518
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 82 (Afghanistan Freedom Support); Subchapter I (Economic And Democratic Development Assistance For Afghanistan). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
There is authorized to be appropriated to the President to carry out this subchapter (other than section 7513(c) of this title) such sums as may be necessary for each of the fiscal years 2005 and 2006.
(b) Availability
Amounts appropriated pursuant to the authorization of appropriations under subsection (a) are—
(1)
authorized to remain available until expended; and
(2)
in addition to funds otherwise available for such purposes, including, with respect to food assistance under section 7513(a)(1) of this title, funds available under title II of the Food for Peace Act [7 U.S.C. 1721 et seq.], section 1736o of title 7, and section 1431(b) of title 7.
