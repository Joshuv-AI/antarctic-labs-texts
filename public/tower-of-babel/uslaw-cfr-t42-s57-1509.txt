42 C.F.R. § 57.1509 — Forms of credit and security instruments.
Citation: us:cfr:42:57.1509
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-57.1509
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 57 (Grants For Construction Of Teaching Facilities, Educational Improvements, Scholarships And Student Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each loan with respect to which a guarantee is made or interest subsidies are paid under this subpart shall be evidenced by a credit instrument and secured by a security instrument in such forms as may be acceptable to the Secretary.
