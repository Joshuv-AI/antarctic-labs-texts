29 C.F.R. § 70.44 — Other rights and services.
Citation: us:cfr:29:70.44
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-70.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 70 (Production Or Disclosure Of Information Or Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this subpart will be construed to entitle any person, as of right, to any service or to the disclosure of any records to which such person is not entitled under the FOIA.
