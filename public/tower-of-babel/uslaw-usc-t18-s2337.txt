18 U.S.C. § 2337 — Suits against Government officials
Citation: us:usc:18:2337
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section2337
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 113B (Terrorism). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No action shall be maintained under section 2333 of this title against—
(1)
the United States, an agency of the United States, or an officer or employee of the United States or any agency thereof acting within his or her official capacity or under color of legal authority; or
(2)
a foreign state, an agency of a foreign state, or an officer or employee of a foreign state or an agency thereof acting within his or her official capacity or under color of legal authority.
