40 C.F.R. § 1039.815 — What provisions apply to confidential information?
Citation: us:cfr:40:1039.815
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1039.815
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1039 (Control Of Emissions From New And In-Use Nonroad Compression-Ignition Engines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of 40 CFR 1068.10 apply for information you consider confidential.
