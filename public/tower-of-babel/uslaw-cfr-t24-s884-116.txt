24 C.F.R. § 884.116 — Establishment of income limit schedules; 30 percent occupancy by very-low income families.

Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary For Housing-Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development (Section 8 Housing Assistance Programs, Section 202 Direct Loan Program, Section 202 Supportive Housing For The Elderly Program And Section 811 Supportive Housing For Persons With Disabilities Program)

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 884 (Section 8 Housing Assistance Payments Program, New Construction Set-Aside For Section 515 Rural Rental Housing Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) HUD will establish schedules of Income limits for determining whether families qualify as Low-Income Families and Very Low-Income Families.
(b) In the leasing of units, the Owner shall comply with HUD requirements concerning the permissible income levels of families, as prescribed in part 5 of this title.
