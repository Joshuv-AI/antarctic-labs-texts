2 U.S.C. § 21 — Oath of Senators
Citation: us:usc:2:21
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section21
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 2 (Organization Of Congress). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The oath of office shall be administered by the President of the Senate to each Senator who shall be elected, previous to his taking his seat.
