24 C.F.R. § 35.1300 — Purpose and applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:24:35.1300
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-35.1300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 35 (Lead-Based Paint Poisoning Prevention In Certain Residential Structures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this subpart R is to provide standards and methods for evaluation and hazard reduction activities required in subparts B, C, D, and F through M of this part.
