24 C.F.R. § 970.31 — Replacement units.
Citation: us:cfr:24:970.31
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-970.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 970 (Public Housing Program—Demolition Or Disposition Of Public Housing Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, replacement public housing units may be built on the original public housing location or in the same neighborhood as the original public housing location if the number of the replacement public housing units is significantly fewer than the number of units demolished. Such development must comply with 24 CFR part 905, Public Housing Capital Fund Program, as well as 24 CFR part 941.
