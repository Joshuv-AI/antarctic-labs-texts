45 C.F.R. § 1618.3 — Complaints.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1618.3
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1618.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1618 (Enforcement Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A complaint of a violation by a recipient or an employee of a recipient may be made to the recipient, the State Advisory Council, or the Corporation.
