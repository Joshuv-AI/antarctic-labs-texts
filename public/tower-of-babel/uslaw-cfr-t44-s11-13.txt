44 C.F.R. § 11.13 — Investigations.
Citation: us:cfr:44:11.13
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-11.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 11 (Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

FEMA may investigate, or may request any other Federal agency to investigate, a claim filed under this part.
