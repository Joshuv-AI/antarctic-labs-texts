33 C.F.R. § 159.115 — Temperature range test.
Citation: us:cfr:33:159.115
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-159.115
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 159 (Marine Sanitation Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The device must be held at a temperature of 60 °C or higher for a period of 16 hours.
(b) The device must be held at a temperature of −40 °C or less for a period of 16 hours following winterization in accordance with manufacturers' instructions.
