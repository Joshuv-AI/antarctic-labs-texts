25 U.S.C. § 4061 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:25:4061
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section4061
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 42 (American Indian Trust Fund Management Reform); Subchapter IV (Authorization Of Appropriations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this chapter.
