30 C.F.R. § 1202.557 — Must I pay royalty on insurance compensation for unavoidably lost gas?
Citation: us:cfr:30:1202.557
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1202.557
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1202 (Royalties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you receive insurance compensation for unavoidably lost gas, you must pay royalties on the amount of that compensation. This paragraph does not apply to compensation through self-insurance.
