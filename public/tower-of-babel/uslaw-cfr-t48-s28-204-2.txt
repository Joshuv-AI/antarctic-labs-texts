48 C.F.R. § 28.204-2 — 28.204-2 Certified or cashiers checks, bank drafts, money orders, or currency.
Citation: us:cfr:48:28.204-2
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-28.204-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 28 (Bonds And Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person required to furnish a bond has an option to furnish a certified or cashier's check, bank draft, Post Office money order, or currency, in an amount equal to the penal sum of the bond, instead of furnishing surety or sureties on the bonds. Those furnishing checks, drafts, or money orders shall draw them to the order of the appropriate Federal agency.
