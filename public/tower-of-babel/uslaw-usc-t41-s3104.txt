41 U.S.C. § 3104 — Small business concerns
Citation: us:usc:41:3104
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title41-section3104
Context: United States Code, Title 41 (Public Contracts); Chapter 31 (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the policy of Congress that a fair proportion of the total purchases and contracts for property and services for the Federal Government shall be placed with small business concerns.
