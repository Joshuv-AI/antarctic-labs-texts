Abstract
. Let S S be a fixed set of primes and let ( X l ) l ≥ 1 (X_{l})_{l\geq 1} be the X X -coordinates of the positive integer solutions ( X , Y ) (X,Y) of the Pell equation X 2 − d ​ Y 2 = 1 X^{2}-dY^{2}=1 corresponding to a non-square integer d > 1 d>1 . We show that there are only a finite number of non-square integers d > 1 d>1 such that there are at least two different elements of the sequence ( X l ) l ≥ 1 (X_{l})_{l\geq 1} that can be represented as a sum of S S -units with a fixed number of terms. Furthermore, we solve explicitly a particular case in which two of the X X -coordinates are product of power of two and power of three.
