Représentations des quaternions de norme 1 1
Guy Henniart and Marie-France Vignéras

Abstract
. Let p p be a prime number, F F a local field with finite residue field of characteristic p p , D D the quaternion division algebra with centre F F , and R R an algebraically closed field of any characteristic char R \operatorname{char}_{R} . We classify the smooth irreducible R R -representations π \pi of the group D 1 D^{1} of elements of D ∗ D^{*} with reduced norm 1 1 to F F . Such a π \pi occurs in the restriction of a smooth irreducible R R -representation Π \Pi of D ∗ D^{*} . When the dimension of Π \Pi is > 1 >1 , following our previous work in the case of S ​ L 2 ​ ( F ) SL_{2}(F) , we show that the restriction of Π \Pi to D 1 D^{1} is irreducible or the sum of two irreducible representations. When char R ≠ p \operatorname{char}_{R}\neq p , that restriction is the sum of two irreducible equivalent representations if and only if the representation of G ​ L 2 ​ ( F ) GL_{2}(F) corresponding to Π \Pi via the Jacquet-Langlands correspondence restricts to S ​ L 2 ​ ( F ) SL_{2}(F) as a sum of four inequivalent irreducible representations (this is never the case if char R ≠ 2 \operatorname{char}_{R}\neq 2 ).
