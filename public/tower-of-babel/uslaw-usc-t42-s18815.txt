42 U.S.C. § 18815 — Report
Citation: us:usc:42:18815
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section18815
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 162 (Energy Infrastructure); Subchapter V (Energy Efficiency And Building Infrastructure); Part C (Smart Manufacturing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary annually shall submit to Congress and make publicly available a report on the progress made in advancing smart manufacturing in the United States.
