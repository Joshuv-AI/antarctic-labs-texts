24 C.F.R. § 35.84 — Effective dates.
Citation: us:cfr:24:35.84
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-35.84
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 35 (Lead-Based Paint Poisoning Prevention In Certain Residential Structures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirements in this subpart take effect in the following manner:
(a) For owners of more than four residential dwellings, the requirements shall take effect on September 6, 1996.
(b) For owners of one to four residential dwellings, the requirements shall take effect on December 6, 1996.
