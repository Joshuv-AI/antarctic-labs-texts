41 C.F.R. § 301-75.3 — -75.3 Allowable pre-employment interview travel expenses.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-75.3
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-75.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-75 (75—Pre-Employment Interview Travel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies may pay expenses consistent with those allowed for employees traveling on temporary duty, as specified in this subtitle with the exception of:
(a) Communication services for purposes other than communication directly related to travel arrangements for the Government interview.
(b) Hire of a room at a hotel or other place to transact official business.
