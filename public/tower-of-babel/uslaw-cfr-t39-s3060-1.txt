39 C.F.R. § 3060.1 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3060.1
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3060.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3060 (Accounting Practices And Tax Rules For The Theoretical Competitive Products Enterprise). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules in this part are applicable to the Postal Service's theoretical competitive products enterprise developed pursuant to 39 U.S.C. 2011 and 3634 and to the Postal Service's obligation to calculate annually an assumed Federal income tax on competitive products income and transfer annually any such assumed Federal income tax due from the Competitive Products Fund to the Postal Service Fund.
