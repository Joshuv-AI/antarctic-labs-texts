33 C.F.R. § 164.30 — Charts, publications, and equipment: General.
Citation: us:cfr:33:164.30
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-164.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 164 (Navigation Safety Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person may operate or cause the operation of a vessel unless the vessel has the marine charts, publications, and equipment as required by §§ 164.33 through 164.41 of this part.
