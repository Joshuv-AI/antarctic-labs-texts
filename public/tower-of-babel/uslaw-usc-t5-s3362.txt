5 U.S.C. § 3362 — Promotion; effect of incentive award
Citation: us:usc:5:3362
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section3362
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart B (Employment And Retention); Chapter 33 (Examination, Selection, And Placement); Subchapter V (Promotion). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An agency, in qualifying and selecting an employee for promotion, shall give due weight to an incentive award under chapter 45 of this title. For the purpose of this section, “agency” and “employee” have the meanings given them by section 4501 of this title.
