5 C.F.R. § 1312.12 — Security Program Review Committee.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1312.12
Creator: Office Of Management And Budget
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1312.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1312 (Classification, Downgrading, Declassification And Safeguarding Of National Security Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Associate Director (or Assistant Director) for Administration will chair the OMB Security Program Review Committee, which will act on suggestions and complaints about the OMB security program.
