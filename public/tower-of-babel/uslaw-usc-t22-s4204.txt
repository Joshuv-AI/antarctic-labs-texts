22 U.S.C. § 4204 — Restriction as to certificate for goods from countries adjacent to United States
Citation: us:usc:22:4204
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section4204
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 52 (Foreign Service); Subchapter XIV (Powers, Duties And Liabilities Of Consular Officers Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No consular officer of the United States shall grant a certificate for goods, wares, or merchandise shipped from countries adjacent to the United States which have passed a consulate after purchase for shipment.
