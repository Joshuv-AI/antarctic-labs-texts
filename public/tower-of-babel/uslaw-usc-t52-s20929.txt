52 U.S.C. § 20929 — Limitation on rulemaking authority
Citation: us:usc:52:20929
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title52-section20929
Context: United States Code, Title 52 (Voting And Elections); Chapter 209 (Election Administration Improvement); Subchapter II (Commission); Part A (Establishment And General Organization); Subpart 1 (Election Assistance Commission). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commission shall not have any authority to issue any rule, promulgate any regulation, or take any other action which imposes any requirement on any State or unit of local government, except to the extent permitted under section 20508(a) of this title.
