32 C.F.R. § 1903.18 — Distribution of materials.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1903.18
Creator: Central Intelligence Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1903.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1903 (Conduct On Agency Installations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Distributing, posting, or affixing materials, such as pamphlets, handbills, or flyers, on any Agency installation is prohibited except as authorized by § 1903.17(b), or by other authorization from the CIA Director of Security, or from his or her designee.
