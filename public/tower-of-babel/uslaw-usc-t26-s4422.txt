26 U.S.C. § 4422 — Applicability of Federal and State laws
Citation: us:usc:26:4422
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section4422
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 35 (Taxes On Wagering); Subchapter C (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The payment of any tax imposed by this chapter with respect to any activity shall not exempt any person from any penalty provided by a law of the United States or of any State for engaging in the same activity, nor shall the payment of any such tax prohibit any State from placing a tax on the same activity for State or other purposes.
