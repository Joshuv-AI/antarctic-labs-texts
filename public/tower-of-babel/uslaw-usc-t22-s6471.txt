22 U.S.C. § 6471 — Use of Annual Report
Citation: us:usc:22:6471
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section6471
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 73 (International Religious Freedom); Subchapter IV (Refugee, Asylum, And Consular Matters). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Annual Report, together with other relevant documentation, shall serve as a resource for immigration judges and consular, refugee, and asylum officers in cases involving claims of persecution on the grounds of religion. Absence of reference by the Annual Report to conditions described by the alien shall not constitute the sole grounds for a denial of the alien’s claim.
