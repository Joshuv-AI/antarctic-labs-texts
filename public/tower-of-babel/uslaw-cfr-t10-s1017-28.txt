10 C.F.R. § 1017.28 — Processing on Automated Information Systems (AIS).
Citation: us:cfr:10:1017.28
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1017.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1017 (Identification And Protection Of Unclassified Controlled Nuclear Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

UCNI may be processed or produced on any AIS that complies with the guidance in OMB Circular No. A-130, Revised, Transmittal No. 4, Appendix III, “Security of Federal Automated Information Resources,” or is certified for classified information.
