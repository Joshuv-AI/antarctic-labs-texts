41 C.F.R. § 302-15.7 — -15.7 Service agreement requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-15.7
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-15.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-15 (15—Allowance For Property Management Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an agency is paying for property management services under this part and the employee's service agreement expires, the employee must sign a new service agreement to continue to receive this benefit.
