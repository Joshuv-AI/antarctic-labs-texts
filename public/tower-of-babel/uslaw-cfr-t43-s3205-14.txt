43 C.F.R. § 3205.14 — May I amend my application for a direct use lease?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3205.14
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3205.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3200 (Geothermal Resource Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may amend your application for a direct use lease at any time before we issue the lease, provided your amended application meets the requirements in this subpart and does not add lands. To add lands, you must file a new application.
