28 C.F.R. § 524.40 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:28:524.40
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-524.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 524 (Classification Of Inmates). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Bureau of Prisons maintains current information on each inmate through progress reports completed by staff. The progress report summarizes information relating to the inmate's adjustment during confinement, program participation, and readiness for release.
