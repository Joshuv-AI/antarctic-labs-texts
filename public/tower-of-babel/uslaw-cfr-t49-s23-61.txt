49 C.F.R. § 23.61 — Can recipients use quotas or set-asides as part of their ACDBE programs?
Citation: us:cfr:49:23.61
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-23.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 23 (Participation Of Disadvantaged Business Enterprise In Airport Concessions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must not use quotas or set-asides for ACDBE participation in your program.
