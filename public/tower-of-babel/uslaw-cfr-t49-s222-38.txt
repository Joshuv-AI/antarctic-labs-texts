49 C.F.R. § 222.38 — Can a quiet zone be created in the Chicago Region?
Citation: us:cfr:49:222.38
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-222.38
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 222 (Use Of Locomotive Horns At Public Highway-Rail Grade Crossings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Public authorities that are eligible to establish quiet zones under this part may create New Quiet Zones or New Partial Quiet Zones in the Chicago Region, provided the New Quiet Zone or New Partial Quiet Zone does not include any highway-rail grade crossing described in § 222.3(c) of this part.
