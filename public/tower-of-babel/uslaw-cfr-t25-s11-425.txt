25 C.F.R. § 11.425 — Persistent non-support.
Citation: us:cfr:25:11.425
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-11.425
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 11 (Courts Of Indian Offenses And Law And Order Code). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person commits a misdemeanor if he or she persistently fails to provide support which he or she can provide and which he or she knows he or she is legally obliged to provide to a spouse, child or other dependent.
