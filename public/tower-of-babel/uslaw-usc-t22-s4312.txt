22 U.S.C. § 4312 — Presidential guidelines
Citation: us:usc:22:4312
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section4312
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 53 (Authorities Relating To The Regulation Of Foreign Missions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The authorities granted to the Secretary pursuant to the provisions of this chapter shall be exercised in accordance with procedures and guidelines approved by the President.
