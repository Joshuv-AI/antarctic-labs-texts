7 U.S.C. § 2222 — Details of law clerks
Citation: us:usc:7:2222
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2222
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 55 (Department Of Agriculture). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Law clerks may be detailed by the Secretary of Agriculture for service in or out of Washington.
