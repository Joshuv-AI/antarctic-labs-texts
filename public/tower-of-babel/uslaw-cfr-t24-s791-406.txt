24 C.F.R. § 791.406 — Competition.
Citation: us:cfr:24:791.406
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Housing And Urban Development (Housing Assistance Programs And Public And Indian Housing Programs)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-791.406
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 791 (Allocations Of Housing Assistance Funds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) All budget authority allocated pursuant to § 791.403(b)(2) shall be reserved and obligated pursuant to a competition. Any such competition shall be conducted pursuant to specific criteria for the selection of recipients of assistance. These criteria shall be contained in a regulation promulgated after notice and public comment or, to the extent authorized by law, a notice published in the Federal Register.
(b) This section shall not apply to assistance referred to in §§ 791.403(b)(1) and 791.407.
