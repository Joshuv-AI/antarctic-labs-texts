40 U.S.C. § 8123 — Record of transfer of jurisdiction between Director of National Park Service and Mayor of District of Columbia
Citation: us:usc:40:8123
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section8123
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part D (Public Buildings, Grounds, And Parks In The District Of Columbia); Chapter 81 (Administrative); Subchapter II (Jurisdiction). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When in accordance with law or mutual legal agreement, spaces or portions of public land are transferred between the jurisdiction of the Director of the National Park Service, as established by the Act of July 1, 1898 (ch. 543, 30 Stat. 570), and the Mayor of the District of Columbia, the letters of transfer and acceptance exchanged between them are sufficient authority for the necessary change in the official maps and for record when necessary.
