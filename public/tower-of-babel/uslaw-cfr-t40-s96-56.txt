40 C.F.R. § 96.56 — Account error.
Citation: us:cfr:40:96.56
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-96.56
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 96 (Nox Budget Trading Program And Cair Nox And So2 Trading Programs For State Implementation Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator may, at his or her sole discretion and on his or her own motion, correct any error in any NOX Allowance Tracking System account. Within 10 business days of making such correction, the Administrator will notify the NOX authorized account representative for the account.
