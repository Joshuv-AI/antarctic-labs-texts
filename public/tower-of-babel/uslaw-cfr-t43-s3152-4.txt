43 C.F.R. § 3152.4 — Relinquishment of exploration permit.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3152.4
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3152.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3150 (Onshore Oil And Gas Geophysical Exploration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subject to the continued obligations of the permittee and the surety to comply with the terms and conditions of the exploration permit and the regulations, the permittee may relinquish an exploration permit for all or any portion of the lands covered by it. Such relinquishment shall be filed with the District Manager of the proper BLM office.
