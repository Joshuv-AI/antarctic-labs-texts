49 U.S.C. § 20150 — Positive train control system progress report
Citation: us:usc:49:20150
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section20150
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Safety); Chapter 201 (General); Subchapter II (Particular Aspects Of Safety). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Transportation shall submit a report to the Congress on the development, deployment, and demonstration of positive train control systems by December 31, 1995.
