34 C.F.R. § 628.44 — When and for what purposes may a grantee use the endowment fund corpus?
Citation: us:cfr:34:628.44
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-628.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 628 (Endowment Challenge Grant Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a)(1) During the grant period, a grantee may not withdraw or spend any part of the endowment fund corpus.
(2) If, during the grant period, a grantee withdraws or spends all or part of the endowment fund corpus, it must repay to the Secretary an amount equal of 50 percent of the amount withdrawn or spent plus the income earned on that amount.
(b) At the end of the grant period, the institution may use the endowment fund corpus for any educational purpose.
