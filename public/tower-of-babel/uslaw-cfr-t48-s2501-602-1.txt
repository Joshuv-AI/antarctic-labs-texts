48 C.F.R. § 2501.602-1 — 2501.602-1 Authority.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2501.602-1
Creator: Chapter 25—National Science Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2501.602-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2501 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

NSF Contracting Officers have authority to enter into, administer, or terminate contracts and make related determinations and findings to the extent of the authority delegated to them in writing by the NSF Procurement Executive.
