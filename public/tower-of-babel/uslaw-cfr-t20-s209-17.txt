20 C.F.R. § 209.17 — Use of payroll records as returns of compensation.
Citation: us:cfr:20:209.17
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-209.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 209 (Railroad Employers' Reports And Responsibilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Payroll records of employers which have permanently ceased operations may be accepted in lieu of prescribed reports provided that there is no official of the employer available to prepare and certify to the accuracy of such reports and, provided further that any employer and employee tax liability incurred under the Railroad Retirement Tax Act has been discharged.
