Forged Steel Fittings From India and South Korea; Cancellation of 
Hearing for Antidumping and Countervailing Duty Reviews

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

DATES: October 5, 2026.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Caitlyn Costello (202-205-2058), 
Office of Investigations, U.S. International Trade Commission, 500 E 
Street SW, Washington, DC 20436. Hearing-impaired individuals are 
advised that information on this matter can be obtained by contacting 
the Commission's TDD terminal on 202-205-1810. Persons with mobility 
impairments who will need special assistance in gaining access to the

General information concerning the Commission may also be obtained by 
accessing its internet server (https://www.usitc.gov). The public 
record for these investigations may be viewed on the Commission's 
electronic docket (EDIS) at https://edis.usitc.gov.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: On June 2, 2026, the Commission established 
a schedule for the reviews of the subject antidumping and 
countervailing duty orders (91 FR 34248, June 5, 2026). On September 
23, 2026, counsel for Bonney Forge Corporation and the United Steel, 
Paper and Forestry, Rubber, Manufacturing, Energy, Allied Industrial 
and Service Workers International Union (collectively, ``Domestic 
Interested Parties'') filed a request that, inter alia, the Commission 
cancel the scheduled hearing for this proceeding and indicated a 
willingness to respond to any Commission questions in lieu of an actual 
hearing. On October 1, 2026, Domestic Interested Parties filed a 
conditional request to appear at the hearing if one occurred. No other 
parties submitted a request to appear at the hearing. On October 2, 
2026, counsel for Domestic Interested Parties filed a request that the 
Commission cancel the hearing and reiterated their intent to respond to 
any questions posed by the Commission in lieu of a hearing. The 
Commission considers Domestic Interested Parties' letters of September 
23, 2026 and October 2, 2026 to be a request under 19 CFR 201.12 to 
take action with respect to these investigations, and that cancellation 
is appropriate because a public hearing is not necessary to address the 
legal and factual issues these investigations present. Consequently, 
the Commission has cancelled the public hearing in connection with this 
proceeding, scheduled to begin at 9:30 a.m. on October 6, 2026. Parties 
to this proceeding should respond to any written questions posed by the 
Commission in their posthearing briefs, which are due to be filed on 
October 13, 2026.
    For further information concerning this proceeding, see the 
Commission's notice cited above and the Commission's Rules of Practice 
and Procedure, part 201, subparts A and B (19 CFR part 201), and part 
207, subparts A and C (19 CFR part 207).
    Authority: This proceeding is being conducted under authority of 
title VII of the Tariff Act of 1930; this notice is published pursuant 
to Sec.  207.21 of the Commission's rules.
