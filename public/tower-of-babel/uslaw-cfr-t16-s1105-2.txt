16 C.F.R. § 1105.2 — Factors.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1105.2
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1105.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1105 (Contributions To Costs Of Participants In Development Of Consumer Product Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission may agree to contribute to the cost of a participant who participates with the Commission in developing a standard in any case in which the Commission determines:
(a) That a contribution is likely to result in a more satisfactory standard than would be developed without a contribution; and
(b) That the participant to whom a contribution is made is financially responsible.
