7 U.S.C. § 954 — Grades and standards for classification
Citation: us:usc:7:954
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section954
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 32 (Peanut Statistics). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to establish and promulgate grades and standards for the classification of peanuts, whenever in his discretion he may see fit.
