40 C.F.R. § 97.927 — Account error.
Citation: us:cfr:40:97.927
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-97.927
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 97 (Federal Nox Budget Trading Program, Cair Nox And So2 Trading Programs, Csapr Nox And So2 Trading Programs, And Texas So2 Trading Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator may, at his or her sole discretion and on his or her own motion, correct any error in any Allowance Management System account. Within 10 business days of making such correction, the Administrator will notify the authorized account representative for the account.
