10 C.F.R. § 830.5 — Enforcement.
Citation: us:cfr:10:830.5
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-830.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 830 (Nuclear Safety Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirements in this part are DOE Nuclear Safety Requirements and are subject to enforcement by all appropriate means, including the imposition of civil and criminal penalties in accordance with the provisions of 10 CFR part 820.
