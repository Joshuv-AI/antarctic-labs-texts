5 C.F.R. § 178.107 — Finality of claim settlements.
Citation: us:cfr:5:178.107
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-178.107
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 178 (Procedures For Settling Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The OPM settlement is final; no further administrative review is available within OPM.
(b) Nothing is this subpart limits the right of a claimant to bring an action in an appropriate United States court.
