29 U.S.C. § 561 — Records and papers and furniture transferred to Department
Citation: us:usc:29:561
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section561
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 12 (Department Of Labor). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The official records and papers on file in and pertaining exclusively to the business of any bureau, office, department, or branch of the public service in this Act transferred to the Department of Labor, together with the furniture in use in such bureau, office, department, or branch of the public service, are transferred to the Department of Labor.
