42 U.S.C. § 285 — Purpose of Institute
Citation: us:usc:42:285
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section285
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter III (National Research Institutes); Part C (Specific Provisions Respecting National Research Institutes); Subpart 1 (National Cancer Institute). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The general purpose of the National Cancer Institute (hereafter in this subpart referred to as the “Institute”) is the conduct and support of research, training, health information dissemination, and other programs with respect to the cause, diagnosis, prevention, and treatment of cancer, rehabilitation from cancer, and the continuing care of cancer patients and the families of cancer patients.
