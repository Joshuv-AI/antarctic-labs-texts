36 C.F.R. § 901.6 — Seal.
Citation: us:cfr:36:901.6
Creator: Pennsylvania Avenue Development Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-901.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 901 (Bylaws Of The Corporation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Corporation may adopt a corporate seal which shall have the name of the Corporation and year of incorporation printed upon it. The seal may be used by causing it or a facsimile thereof to be impressed, affixed, or reproduced.
