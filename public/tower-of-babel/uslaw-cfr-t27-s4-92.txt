27 C.F.R. § 4.92 — Alternative names permitted for temporary use.
Citation: us:cfr:27:4.92
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-4.92
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 4 (Labeling And Advertising Of Wine). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following alternative names shown in the left column may be used as the type designation for American wine in lieu of the name of the grape variety shown in the right column. Alternative names listed in the left column may only be used for wine bottled prior to the date indicated.
(a) Wines bottled prior to January 1, 1997.
(b) Wines bottled prior to January 1, 1999.
(c) Wines bottled prior to January 1, 2006.
(d) Wines bottled prior to October 29, 2012.
