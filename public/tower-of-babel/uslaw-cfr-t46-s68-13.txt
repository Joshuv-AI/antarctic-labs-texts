46 C.F.R. § 68.13 — Privileges conferred—documentation of vessel.
Citation: us:cfr:46:68.13
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-68.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 68 (Documentation Of Vessels: Exceptions To Coastwise Qualification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The special citizenship status created by the Act entitles the 883-1 corporation to document certain vessels for certain limited purposes:
(a) An 883-1 corporation may document under the Act only vessels which are qualified for employment in the coastwise trade and which are either non-self propelled or, if self-propelled, are of less than 500 gross tons.
(b) A vessel owned by an 883-1 corporation and meeting the criteria in paragraph (a) of this section may be documented only for use in the coastwise trade subject to the restrictions in § 68.17.
(c) Section 68.19 contains details concerning the documentation of vessels by an 883-1 corporation.
