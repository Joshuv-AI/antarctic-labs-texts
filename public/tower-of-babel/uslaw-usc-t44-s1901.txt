44 U.S.C. § 1901 — Definition of Government publication
Citation: us:usc:44:1901
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section1901
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 19 (Depository Library Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

“Government publication” as used in this chapter, means informational matter which is published as an individual document at Government expense, or as required by law.
