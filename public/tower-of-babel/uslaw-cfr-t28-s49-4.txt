28 C.F.R. § 49.4 — Deputy custodians.
Citation: us:cfr:28:49.4
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-49.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 49 (Antitrust Civil Process Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Deputy custodians may perform such of the duties assigned to the custodian as may be authorized or required by the Assistant Attorney General.
