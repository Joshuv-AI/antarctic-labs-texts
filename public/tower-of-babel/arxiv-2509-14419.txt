On Hilbert series of Koszul operads and a classification result for set-operads
Paul Laubie

Abstract
Motivated by numerous examples in the literature, we state a conjecture on the Hilbert series of Koszul symmetric operads generated by one element of arity 2 2 . We prove this conjecture for all Koszul symmetric set-operads generated by one element of arity 2 2 by explicitly classifying those. There are 11 11 such operads; 4 4 of them are new.



1
Introduction

The Koszul duality and the Koszul property were originally introduced by Priddy in 1970
[
20
]
for algebras in the non-homogenous case, namely the quadratic-linear case.
They were then extended to the homogenous case for quadratic operads generated by elements of arity
2
by Ginzburg and Kapranov in 1994
[
11
]
and for general quadratic operads by Getzler and Jones in 1994
[
10
]
.
Koszul duality and Koszul property are very useful tools in homological algebra; indeed, they allow, for example, the computation of manageable resolutions for algebras and operads. In
[
19
, Conjecture 7.1]
, Polishchuk and Positselski proposed the following remarkable conjecture:

Conjecture
.

Let
A
be a finitely generated Koszul algebra, then the Hilbert series of
A
is a rational function.

This conjecture is still open and can be extended to symmetric operads following the insight of Khoroshkin and Piontkovski
[
12
]
:

Conjecture
.

Let
\mathcal{P}
a finitely generated Koszul symmetric operad, then the Hilbert series of
\mathcal{P}
is differential algebraic over
\mathbb{Z}[t]
.

However, in the case of Koszul operads generated by one element of arity
2
, one may expect a stronger result to hold. The number of examples of Koszul symmetric operads generated by one element of arity
2
appearing in the literature grows, and in most cases the Hilbert series of these operads can be computed; see
[
22
,
23
]
.
In all the examples we know, the Hilbert series of these operads are differential algebraic of order
1
over
\mathbb{Q}[t]
. We conjecture that this is always the case:

Conjecture
.

3.2

Let
\mathcal{P}
a Koszul symmetric operad generated by one element in arity
2
, then the Hilbert series of
\mathcal{P}
is differential algebraic of order
1
over
\mathbb{Z}[t]
.

A classification of Koszul symmetric operads generated by one element of arity
2
would be enough to prove or disprove this conjecture.
Some classification results on Koszul operads under some hypothesis already exist; indeed, Bremner and Dotsenko
[
3
]
classified all the Koszul operads of associator-dependent algebras generated by one element of arity
2
, which all satisfy Conjecture
3.2
.
In this paper, we classify all the Koszul symmetric set-operads generated by one element of arity
2
and check our conjecture on those operads. We get the following classification:

Theorem
.

7.5

Let
\mathcal{P}
a Koszul set-operad generated by one element of arity
2
, then
\mathcal{P}
is either isomorphic to one of the
7
operads
\mathrm{Mag}
,
\mathrm{NAP}
,
\mathrm{Ass}
,
\mathrm{Perm}
,
\mathrm{LieAdm}^{!}
,
\mathrm{ComMag}
or
\mathrm{Com}
, or to one of the
4
following operads:

•

\mathcal{P}_{10}
which is built from
\mathrm{ComMag}
and
\mathrm{SkewNil_{2}}
with the relation
[a.b,c]=0
;

•

\mathcal{P}_{2;2}
which is built from
\mathrm{ComMag}
and
\mathrm{SkewNil_{2}}
with the relation
[a,b].c=0
;

•

\mathcal{P}_{11}
which is the connected sum of
\mathrm{ComMag}
and
\mathrm{SkewMag}
;

•

\mathcal{P}_{2;10}
which is the connected sum of
\mathrm{ComMag}
and
\mathrm{SkewNil_{2}}
.

Four new Koszul symmetric set-operads generated by one element of arity
2
are discovered, all satisfying our conjecture.

2
Koszul property

The Koszul property is a well studied property on operads; see
[
16
]
for an introduction to operads and Koszul duality for operads. Most of the usual operads like
\mathrm{Ass}
,
\mathrm{Lie}
, or
\mathrm{Com}
are known to be Koszul. It is a well known fact that Koszul operads are quadratic. However, there is no general method to determine if an operad is Koszul or not.

The main tool to prove that a quadratic operad is not Koszul is the Ginzburg-Kapranov criterion using Hilbert series of the operad.

Proposition 2.1
.

Let
\mathcal{P}
a Koszul operad generated by elements of arity
2
, and
\mathcal{P}^{!}
its Koszul dual, then:

f_{\mathcal{P}^{!}}(-f_{\mathcal{P}}(-t))=t.

In particular, if the compositional reverse of
-f_{\mathcal{P}}(-t)
has a nonpositive term, then
\mathcal{P}
is not Koszul.

Thus any operad generated in arity
2
such that their Hilbert series is one of the series given in Table
1
of the
Appendix
is not Koszul.

The main tool we will use to prove that an operad is Koszul is the following property:

Definition 2.2
.

Let
\mathcal{P}
be an operad;
\mathcal{P}
is
monomial
if it admits a presentation:

\mathcal{P}\simeq\mathcal{F}(x^{1},\dots,x^{n})/\langle\mathcal{R}\rangle,

such that
\mathcal{R}
is a set of monomials.
\mathcal{P}
is
quadratic monomial
if
\mathcal{R}
is a set of quadratic monomials.

Proposition 2.3
.

Let
\mathcal{P}
be a quadratic monomial operad, then
\mathcal{P}
is Koszul.

This fact is a direct consequence of
[
1
, Theorem 6.3.3.2]
or
[
16
, Theorem 8.3.1]
.
However, if one inspects the proof of those theorems, the above proposition is, in fact, one of the first intermediate results of those proofs.

This proposition already allows us to prove the Koszulness of some natural to consider operads
\mathrm{Mag}
,
\mathrm{ComMag}
and
\mathrm{SkewMag}
the free operads over respectively one generator of arity
2
without symmetries, one commutative generator of arity
2
and one antisymmetric generator of arity
2
, and their Koszul dual
\mathrm{Nil_{2}}
,
\mathrm{SkewNil_{2}}
and
\mathrm{ComNil_{2}}
.

3
Koszul symmetric operads generated by one element of arity
2

The conjecture expected in
[
12
]
is the following:

Conjecture 3.1
.

Let
\mathcal{P}
a finitely generated Koszul symmetric operad, then the Hilbert series of
\mathcal{P}
is differential algebraic over
\mathbb{Z}[t]
.

It states that the Hilbert series of any finitely generated Koszul symmetric operad should satisfy a non-trivial differential algebraic equation over
\mathbb{Z}[t]
.
It can be experimentally checked on examples appearing in the literature; however, most of the examples are operads generated by elements of arity
2
and are often generated by one element.
We have already introduced the Koszul operads
\mathrm{Mag}
,
\mathrm{ComMag}
,
\mathrm{SkewMag}
,
\mathrm{Nil_{2}}
,
\mathrm{SkewNil_{2}}
and
\mathrm{ComNil_{2}}
which are all generated by one element of arity
2
.
In order to be as exhaustive as possible, we looked through
Operadia
[
22
]
which is an under-construction database of operads and their properties.
One can find
17
other Koszul operads generated by one element of arity
2
in
[
22
]
; a summary table of those operads and their Hilbert series is given in Table
2
of the
Appendix
, and the sequence of arity-wise dimensions of the operads are given in the OEIS; they might be shifted by
1
or have a non-zero first term.

One may check that all their Hilbert series satisfy differential algebraic identities.
Moreover, those identities are of
order
1
, meaning that only
f
and
f^{\prime}
appear and no higher differential of
f
.
It leads us to the following conjecture:

Conjecture 3.2
.

Let
\mathcal{P}
a Koszul symmetric operad generated by one element of arity
2
, then the Hilbert series of
\mathcal{P}
is differential algebraic of order
1
over
\mathbb{Z}[t]
. Equivalently,
f_{\mathcal{P}}
and
f_{\mathcal{P}}^{\prime}
are algebraically dependent over
\mathbb{Z}[t]
.

Remark 3.3
.

One cannot expect this conjecture to be true for Koszul symmetric operads generated by finitely many elements of arity
2
since one can define the operad
\mathrm{Com}\circ\mathrm{Com}
by generators and relations:

\mathrm{Com}\circ\mathrm{Com}=\mathcal{F}(c_{1},c_{2})/\langle c_{1}\circ_{1}c_{1}-c_{1}\circ_{2}c_{1},\;c_{2}\circ_{1}c_{2}-c_{2}\circ_{2}c_{2},\;c_{1}\circ c_{2}\rangle

where the action of
\mathfrak{S}_{2}
on
\{c_{1},c_{2}\}
is given by
c_{i}.(1\;2)=c_{i}
. This operad is Koszul by the theory of distributed law
[
17
]
; moreover, its Hilbert series is
\exp(\exp(t)-1)-1
, which is not algebraically dependent with its differential over
\mathbb{Z}[t]
.

More generally this construction allow us to build a Koszul operad
\mathcal{P}
with
n
generators such that its Hilbert series is not algebraically dependent with its first
n-1
differentials over
\mathbb{Z}[t]
.

4
The magmatic Operad

As stated in the abstract, a goal of this paper is to classify set-operads generated by one element of arity
2
that are Koszul. The case with a symmetric generator will not be developed here since the only quadratic set-operads generated by one symmetric element of arity
2
are
\mathrm{Com}
and
\mathrm{ComMag}
, and are both known to be Koszul.

The following lemma allows us to reduce the study of Koszul set-operads generated by one element of arity
2
to the study of a finite number of operads.

Lemma 4.1
.

Let
\mathcal{P}
be a Koszul set-operad generated by one element of arity
2
, then
\mathcal{P}
is a quotient of
\mathrm{Mag}
by an equivariant equivalence relation on the monomials of
\mathrm{Mag}(3)
.

Proof.

Since
\mathcal{P}
is generated by one element of arity
2
, it is a quotient of
\mathrm{Mag}
, moreover since
\mathcal{P}
is Koszul,
\mathcal{P}
is quadratic, hence the relations are quadratic. Moreover, since
\mathcal{P}
is set-theoretical, the relations are given by an equivariant equivalence relation on the monomials.
∎

Some Koszul set-operads generated by one element of arity
2
are well known:

•

The magmatic operad on one generator without symmetries,

•

The non-associative permutative operad,

•

The associative operad,

•

The permutative operad,

•

The Koszul dual of the Lie admissible operad (which is indeed a set-operad when
considering the non-symmetric generator).

We will add four new operads to this list.
Namely, the connected sum of
\mathrm{ComMag}
and
\mathrm{SkewMag}
, the connected sum of
\mathrm{ComMag}
and
\mathrm{SkewNil_{2}}
, and two other operads built over
\mathrm{ComMag}
and
\mathrm{SkewNil_{2}}
.
One may notice that neither
\mathrm{SkewMag}
,
\mathrm{ComNil_{2}}
, nor
\mathrm{SkewNil_{2}}
are set-operads; however, they are the building blocks of the
4
new Koszul set-operads we will construct.

Let
\mathrm{Mag}
be the magmatic operad on one generator without symmetries
x
, and let
y=x.(1\;2)
.
The operad
\mathrm{Mag}
admits a non-trivial automorphism given by
x\mapsto y
, let us call it the
reverse automorphism
.
The operad
\mathrm{Mag}
is Koszul and is in fact the first example of Koszul set-operad generated by one element of arity
2
.
By Lemma
4.1
, we are left to consider quotients of
\mathrm{Mag}
by an equivariant equivalence relation on the monomials of
\mathrm{Mag}(3)
.
Let us study the action of
\mathfrak{S}_{3}
on the set of monomials of
\mathrm{Mag}(3)
.
They can be represented by all possible ways to parenthesize the product of three elements,
a
,
b
, and
c
, with
a
,
b
, and
c
in any order.
Let us represent it this way:

a(bc)

b(ac)

b(ca)

a(cb)

c(ab)

c(ba)

(ab)c

(ac)b

(ca)b

(ba)c

(bc)a

(cb)a

Black
edges represent the action of the transposition
(1\;2)
,
red
edges represent the action of the transposition
(2\;3)
,
blue
edges represent the action of the transposition
(1\;3)
, and
green
edges represent the action of the
3
-cycles.

As we can see, the action of
\mathfrak{S}_{3}
on the monomials of
\mathrm{Mag}(3)
has
2

\mathrm{Mag}
.

Proposition 4.2
.

Let
\mathcal{R}
an equivariant equivalence relation on the monomials of
\mathrm{Mag}(3)
, then
\mathcal{R}
satisfies exactly one of the following property:

1.

all equivalence classes are either subsets of the left orbit or subsets of the right orbit;

2.

all equivalence classes contain elements of both orbits.

In the first case, the relation is entirely determined by the class of
(ab)c
and the class of
a(bc)
. In the second case, the class of
(ab)c
is enough to determine the relation.

The first case can be refined into four subcases:

Proposition 4.3
.

Let
\mathcal{R}
an equivariant equivalence relation on the monomials of
\mathrm{Mag}(3)
satisfying Property
1
of Proposition
4.2
, then
\mathcal{R}
satisfies exactly one of the following property:

1.1

the relation is trivial (equivalence classes are singletons);

1.2

equivalence classes of the left orbit are reduced to singletons, and equivalence classes of the right are not;

1.3

equivalence classes of the right orbit are reduced to singletons, and equivalence classes of the left are not;

1.4

no equivalence class is reduced to a singleton.

The first subcase gives rise to the operad
\mathrm{Mag}
. The second and third subcases are equivalent by the reverse automorphism. The last subcase is the same as giving one equivalence relation on the left orbit and another one on the right orbit.

The second case can be refined into two subcases:

Proposition 4.4
.

Let
\mathcal{R}
an equivariant equivalence relation on the monomials of
\mathrm{Mag}(3)
satisfying Property
2
of Proposition
4.2
, then
\mathcal{R}
satisfies exactly one of the following property:

2.1

all equivalence classes contain exactly two elements;

2.2

all equivalence classes contain strictly more than two elements (namely
4
,
6
, or
12
elements).

We will study each subcase of Proposition
4.3
and
4.4
in the following section.
This approach leads us to consider
57

5
Construction of all the quadratic set-operads generated by one element of arity
2

Let us first study Subcase
1.2
. By equivariance, a relation of Subcase
1.2
is entirely determined by the class of
(ab)c
.
Moreover, the equivalence class of
(ab)c
contains either
2
,
3
, or
6
elements.
We have a priori
5

•

(ab)c\sim(ac)b
;

•

(ab)c\sim(ba)c
;

•

(ab)c\sim(bc)a
;

•

(ab)c\sim(ca)b
;

•

(ab)c\sim(cb)a
.

However, since the relation is equivariant, the relation generated by
(ab)c\sim(ca)b
is the same as the relation generated by
(ab)c\sim(bc)a
. Moreover, this relation is the only one such that the equivalence class of
(ab)c
contains
3
elements.
We have only one possibility for a relation such that the equivalence class of
(ab)c
contains
6

(ab)c\sim(cb)a\sim(ca)b\sim(ba)c\sim(ac)b\sim(bc)a

Hence we have
5

5

•

\mathrm{RR}_{1}=\{(ab)c=(ac)b\}
,

•

\mathrm{RR}_{2}=\{(ab)c=(ba)c\}
,

•

\mathrm{RR}_{3}=\{(ab)c=(cb)a\}
,

•

\mathrm{RR}_{4}=\{(ab)c=(bc)a=(ca)b\}
,

•

\mathrm{RR}_{5}=\{(ab)c=(cb)a=(ca)b=(ba)c=(ac)b=(bc)a\}
,

Let
\mathcal{P}_{i}=\mathrm{Mag}/\langle\mathrm{RR}_{i}\rangle

Let us study Subcase
1.4
. We need to study the compatibility between the relations on the left and right orbits. We have
5

•

\mathrm{LL}_{1}=\{a(bc)=b(ac)\}
,

•

\mathrm{LL}_{2}=\{a(bc)=a(cb)\}
,

•

\mathrm{LL}_{3}=\{a(bc)=c(ba)\}
,

•

\mathrm{LL}_{4}=\{a(bc)=b(ca)=c(ab)\}
,

•

\mathrm{LL}_{5}=\{a(bc)=b(ca)=c(ab)=a(cb)=b(ac)=c(ba)\}
,

We have
25

\mathrm{RR}_{i}
and
\mathrm{LL}_{j}
; however, by the reverse automorphism, we only need to study those with
i\leq j
, and thus we have
15
possibilities. Let
\mathcal{P}_{i;j}=\mathrm{Mag}/\langle\mathrm{RR}_{i};\mathrm{LL}_{j}\rangle
.

Let us study equivalence relations involving exactly one term of the left and one of right orbits. As previously, knowing the equivalence class of
(ab)c

6

•

\mathrm{RL}_{6}=\{(ab)c=a(bc)\}
,

•

\mathrm{RL}_{7}=\{(ab)c=a(cb)\}
,

•

\mathrm{RL}_{8}=\{(ab)c=b(ac)\}
,

\mathrm{RL}_{9}=\{(ab)c=b(ca)\}

\mathrm{RL}_{10}=\{(ab)c=c(ab)\}
,

\mathrm{RL}_{11}=\{(ab)c=c(ba)\}

Moreover, none of those relations are equivalent. We get the

Let us finally study Subcase

, or

.
Any relation of Subcase

Let
\mathcal{P}

.

The couples of operads
(\mathcal{P}_{1;6},\mathcal{P}_{1;7})
,
(\mathcal{P}_{1;8},\mathcal{P}_{1;10})

Proposition 6.2
.

The operad
\mathcal{P}_{3;3}
is not Koszul.

Proof.

The method consisting of finding negative coefficients in
\rev(-f_{\mathcal{P}}(-t))
does not seem to work in this case. However, one can notice that this operad is self-dual (in the sense of Koszul duality). We recall that
x

x\circ_{1}x-y\circ_{2}y\qquad;\qquad y\circ_{1}y-x\circ_{2}x

And those relations are the same as the one of the operad
\mathcal{P}_{3;3}^{!}
with
x\mapsto x_{*}

The explicit method to compute a Koszul dual is given in
[
16

Let us compute the first dimensions of the operad
\mathcal{P}_{3;3}

[

(1,2,6,20,60,182,546,?,?,\dots)

f_{\mathcal{P}_{3;3}}(-f_{\mathcal{P}_{3;3}}(-t))=t-\frac{7}{12}t^{7}+\mathcal{O}(t^{8})

Which shows that
\mathcal{P}_{3;3}
is not Koszul.
∎

Proposition 6.3
.

The operad
\mathcal{P}_{5;5}
is not Koszul.

Proof.

Once again the generating series method does not seem to work in this case. Let us polarize the relations of
\mathcal{P}_{5;5}
by defining
[a,b]=ab-ba
and
a.b=ab+ba
and rewriting the relations of
\mathcal{P}_{5;5}
using those:

•

(a.b).c=a.(b.c)
,

•

[a.b,c]=-[a,b.c]
,

•

[a,b].c=0
,

•

[[a,b],c]=0
.

From this presentation we can see that
\mathcal{P}_{5;5}
is graded by
[\cdot,\cdot]
and the dimensions can be easily computed. We get
(1,1+u,1+u,1,1,1,\dots)
, where
u
is the generator of the grading.
This case looks very much like
[
3
, Proposition 3.6]
(however, this is not the same operad). We have that:

f_{\mathcal{P}_{5;5}}=\exp(t)-1+\frac{u}{2}t^{2}+\frac{u}{6}t^{3}

The Ginzburg-Kapranov criterion can be used on this series and the first negative term is

\frac{-1983044460002323872u^{2}}{20!}t^{20}

which is the exact same term as in
[
3
, Proposition 3.6]
since the power series are the same. Thus
\mathcal{P}_{5;5}
is not Koszul.
∎

Proposition 6.4
.

The operad
\mathcal{P}_{4;6}
is not Koszul.

Proof.

Let us polarize the relations. We get:

•

[a,[b,c]]=0
,

•

[a,b].c=a.[b,c]
,

•

[a,b.c]=0
,

•

(a.b).c=a.(b.c)

We recognize the operad of
[
3
, Proposition 3.6]
which is not Koszul.
∎

Proposition 6.5
.

The operad
\mathcal{P}_{4;9}
is not Koszul.

Proof.

Let us polarize the relations. We get:

•

[a,[b,c]]=-[[a,b],c]
,

•

[a,b].c=0
,

•

[a,b.c]=0
,

•

(a.b).c=a.(b.c)
.

From this presentation, we can notice that
\mathcal{P}_{4;9}
is graded by
[\cdot,\cdot]
, and the dimensions can be easily computed.
We get
(1,1+u,1+u^{2},1,1,1,\dots)
, where
u
is the generator of the grading. We have that:

f_{\mathcal{P}_{4;9}}=\exp(t)-1+\frac{u}{2}t^{2}+\frac{u^{2}}{6}t^{3}

The Ginzburg-Kapranov criterion can be used on this series and the first negative term is
\frac{-35u^{5}}{6!}t^{6}
which shows that this operad is not
Koszul.
∎

7
The Koszul operads

We have
4
operads left to check, namely the operads
\mathcal{P}_{2,2}
,
\mathcal{P}_{10}
,
\mathcal{P}_{11}
, and
\mathcal{P}_{2,10}
. Let us show that they are Koszul.

Theorem 7.1
.

The operad
\mathcal{P}_{2;2}
is Koszul and self-dual.

Proof.

Let us polarize the relations of
\mathcal{P}_{2;2}
. We get:

•

[a,[b,c]]=0
,

•

[a,b].c=0
,

By Proposition
2.3
,
\mathcal{P}_{2;2}
is Koszul. Moreover, its generating series can be computed and is
2-t-\sqrt{1-2t}
. Computation of its Koszul dual shows that it is self-dual.
∎

Theorem 7.2
.

The operad
\mathcal{P}_{10}
is Koszul and is self-dual.

Proof.

Let us polarize the relations of
\mathcal{P}_{10}
. We get::

•

[a,[b,c]]=0
,

•

[a,b.c]=0
,

By Proposition
2.3
,
\mathcal{P}_{10}
is Koszul. Moreover, its generating series can be computed and is

1-\sqrt{1-2t-t^{2}}
. Computation of its Koszul dual shows that it is self-dual.
∎

Theorem 7.3
.

The operad
\mathcal{P}_{11}
is Koszul.

Proof.

Let us polarize the relations of
\mathcal{P}_{11}
. We get:

•

a.[b,c]=0
,

•

[a,b.c]=0
,

We get the connected sum of the magmatic operad over a symmetric generator and the magmatic operad over a skew-symmetric generator. By Proposition
2.3
, it is Koszul. Moreover, its generating series can be computed and is
2-t-\sqrt{1-2t}
.
∎

Theorem 7.4
.

The operad
\mathcal{P}_{2;10}
is Koszul.

Proof.

Let us polarize the relations. We get:

•

[[a,b],c]=0
,

•

[a,b].c=0
,

•

[a,b.c]=0
.

One may recognize the connected sum of
\mathrm{ComMag}
and
\mathrm{SkewNil_{2}}
. By Proposition
2.3
,
\mathcal{P}_{2;10}
is Koszul. Moreover, its generating series can be computed and is
1-\sqrt{1-2t}+\frac{1}{2}t^{2}
.
∎

All the cases have been checked; we can now state the main theorem of this paper:

Theorem 7.5
.

Let
\mathcal{P}
a Koszul set-operad generated by one element of arity
2
, then
\mathcal{P}
is isomorphic to one of the
11
following operads:

•

\mathrm{Mag}
the magmatic operad, and
f_{\mathcal{P}}(t)=\frac{1}{2}(1-\sqrt{1-4t})
;

•

\mathrm{NAP}
the non-associative permutative operad, and
f_{\mathcal{P}}
is the Euler tree function;

•

\mathcal{P}_{10}
which is built from
\mathrm{ComMag}
and
\mathrm{SkewNil_{2}}
with the relation
[a.b,c]=0
, and
f_{\mathcal{P}}(t)=1-\sqrt{1-2t-t^{2}}
;

•

\mathcal{P}_{2;2}
which is built from
\mathrm{ComMag}
and
\mathrm{SkewNil_{2}}
with the relation
[a,b].c=0
, and
f_{\mathcal{P}}(t)=2-t-\sqrt{1-2t}
;

•

\mathcal{P}_{11}
which is the connected sum of
\mathrm{ComMag}
and
\mathrm{SkewMag}
, and
f_{\mathcal{P}}(t)=2-t-\sqrt{1-2t}
;

•

\mathrm{Ass}
the associative operad, and
f_{\mathcal{P}}(t)=\frac{t}{1-t}
;

•

\mathcal{P}_{2;10}
which is the connected sum of
\mathrm{ComMag}
and
\mathrm{SkewNil_{2}}
, and
f_{\mathcal{P}}(t)=1-\sqrt{1-2t}+\frac{1}{2}t^{2}
;

•

\mathrm{Perm}
the permutative operad and
f_{\mathcal{P}}(t)=t\exp(t)
;

•

\mathrm{LieAdm}^{!}
the Koszul dual of the Lie admissible operad, and
f_{\mathcal{P}}(t)=\exp(t)-1+\frac{t^{2}}{2}
;

•

\mathrm{ComMag}
the commutative magmatic operad, and
f_{\mathcal{P}}(t)=(1-\sqrt{1-2t})
;

•

\mathrm{Com}
the commutative operad, and
f_{\mathcal{P}}(t)=\exp(t)-1
.

Moreover, only
\mathrm{Ass}
,
\mathcal{P}_{2;2}
and
\mathcal{P}_{10}
are self-dual. And only
\mathrm{NAP}
and
\mathrm{Perm}
do not inherit the reverse automorphism from
\mathrm{Mag}
.

All those operads satisfy Conjecture
3.2
.

Corollary 7.6
.

The Hilbert series of a Koszul symmetric set-operad generated by one element of arity
2
is differential algebraic of order
1
over
\mathbb{Z}[t]
.
