25 U.S.C. § 1660e — Expanded program authority for urban Indian organizations
Citation: us:usc:25:1660e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section1660e
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 18 (Indian Health Care); Subchapter IV (Health Services For Urban Indians). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter, the Secretary, acting through the Service, is authorized to establish programs, including programs for awarding grants, for urban Indian organizations that are identical to any programs established pursuant to sections 1621q, 1665a, and 1665g(g) of this title.
