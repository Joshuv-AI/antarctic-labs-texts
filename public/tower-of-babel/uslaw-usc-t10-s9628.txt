10 U.S.C. § 9628 — Airplane parts and accessories: civilian flying schools
Citation: us:usc:10:9628
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9628
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Procurement); Chapter 969 (Sale Of Serviceable Material). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Air Force may sell, to civilian flying schools at which personnel of the Department of the Air Force or the Department of the Army are receiving flight training under contracts requiring these schools to maintain and repair airplanes of the Air Force furnished to them for flight training, the spare parts and accessories needed for those repairs.
