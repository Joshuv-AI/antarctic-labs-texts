7 C.F.R. § 160.89 — Medicinal preparations.
Citation: us:cfr:7:160.89
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-160.89
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 160 (Regulations And Standards For Naval Stores). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A compound or mixture containing spirits of turpentine or rosin, or both, with other drugs, when sold for medicinal purposes, is not subject to the provisions of the Naval Stores Act or of the provisions in this part.
