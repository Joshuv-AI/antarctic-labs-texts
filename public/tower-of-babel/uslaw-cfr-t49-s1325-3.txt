49 C.F.R. § 1325.3 — Federal office.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1325.3
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1325.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1325 (Extension Of Credit To Candidates For Federal Office Or Their Representatives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the purposes of this section, Federal office means the office of President or Vice President of the United States; or of Senator or Representative in, or Delegate or Resident Board Member to, the Congress of the United States.
