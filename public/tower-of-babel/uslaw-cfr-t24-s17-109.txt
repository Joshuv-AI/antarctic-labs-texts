24 C.F.R. § 17.109 — Nonwaiver of rights.
Citation: us:cfr:24:17.109
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-17.109
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 17 (Administrative Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

So long as there are no statutory or contractual provisions to the contrary, no employee involuntary payment (of all or a portion of a debt) collected under these regulations will be interpreted as a waiver of any rights that the employee may have under 5 U.S.C. 5514 or any other provision of contract or law.
