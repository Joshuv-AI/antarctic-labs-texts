28 U.S.C. § 2105 — Scope of review; abatement
Citation: us:usc:28:2105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section2105
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part V (Procedure); Chapter 133 (Review—Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There shall be no reversal in the Supreme Court or a court of appeals for error in ruling upon matters in abatement which do not involve jurisdiction.
