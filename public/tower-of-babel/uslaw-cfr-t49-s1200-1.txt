49 C.F.R. § 1200.1 — Financial statements released by carriers.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1200.1
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1200.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1200 (General Accounting Regulations Under The Interstate Commerce Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Carriers desiring to do so may prepare and publish financial statements in reports to stockholders and others, except in reports to this Board, based on generally accepted accounting principles for which there is authoritative support, provided that any variance from this Board's prescribed accounting rules contained in such statements is clearly disclosed in footnotes to the statements.
