22 C.F.R. § 50.4 — Application for passport.
Citation: us:cfr:22:50.4
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-50.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 50 (Nationality Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A claim to U.S. nationality in connection with an application for passport shall be determined by posts abroad in accordance with the regulations contained in part 51 of this chapter.
