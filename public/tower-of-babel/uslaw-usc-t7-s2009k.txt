7 U.S.C. § 2009k — Rural development interagency working group
Citation: us:usc:7:2009k
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2009k
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 50 (Agricultural Credit); Subchapter V (Rural Community Advancement Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The Secretary shall provide leadership within the Executive branch for, and assume responsibility for, establishing an interagency working group chaired by the Secretary.
(b) Duties
The working group shall establish policy for, coordinate, make recommendations with respect to, and evaluate the performance of, all Federal rural development efforts.
