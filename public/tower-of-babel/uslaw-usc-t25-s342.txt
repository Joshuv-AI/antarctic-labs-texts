25 U.S.C. § 342 — Removal of Southern Utes to new reservation
Citation: us:usc:25:342
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section342
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 9 (Allotment Of Indian Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this act shall be so construed as to prevent the removal of the Southern Ute Indians from their present reservation in southwestern Colorado to a new reservation by and with the consent of a majority of the adult male members of said tribe.
