48 C.F.R. § 242.7100 — 242.7100 General.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 242 (Contract Administration And Audit Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A voluntary refund is a payment or credit (adjustment under one or more contracts or subcontracts) to the Government from a contractor or subcontractor that is not required by any contractual or other legal obligation. Follow the procedures at PGI 242.7100 for voluntary refunds.
