46 C.F.R. § 150.170 — Right of appeal.
Citation: us:cfr:46:150.170
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-150.170
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 150 (Compatibility Of Cargoes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person directly affected by a decision or action taken under this part, by or on behalf of the Coast Guard, may appeal therefrom in accordance with subpart 1.03 of this chapter.
