40 C.F.R. § 14.5 — Who may file a claim.
Citation: us:cfr:40:14.5
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-14.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 14 (Employee Personal Property Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A claim may be filed by an employee or by his/her authorized agent or legal representative. If a claim is otherwise allowable under this part, a claim can be filed by a surviving spouse, child, parent, brother or sister of a deceased employee.
