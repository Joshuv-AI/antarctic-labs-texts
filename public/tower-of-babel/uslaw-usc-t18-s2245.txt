18 U.S.C. § 2245 — Offenses resulting in death

Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 109A (Sexual Abuse). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) 1 In General.—
A person who, in the course of an offense under this chapter, or section 1591, 2251, 2251A, 2260, 2421, 2422, 2423, or 2425, murders an individual, shall be punished by death or imprisoned for any term of years or for life.
