42 C.F.R. § 5.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:42:5.1
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-5.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 5 (Designation Of Health Professional(S) Shortage Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These regulations establish criteria and procedures for the designation of geographic areas, population groups, medical facilities, and other public facilities, in the States, as health professional(s) shortage areas.
