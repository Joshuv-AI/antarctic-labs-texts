45 C.F.R. § 2521.90 — If I am a new or replacement legal applicant for an existing program, what will my matching requirements be?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2521.90
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2521.90
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2521 (Eligible Americorps Subtitle C Program Applicants And Types Of Grants Available For Award). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If your organization is a new or replacement legal applicant for an existing program, you must provide matching resources at the level the previous legal applicant had reached at the time you took over the program.
