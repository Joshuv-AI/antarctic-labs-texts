17 C.F.R. § 274.51 — Form N-18F-1, for notification of election pursuant to § 270.18f-1 of this chapter.

Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 274 (Forms Prescribed Under The Investment Company Act Of 1940). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) This form shall be filed with the Commission in triplicate as the notification of election pursuant to § 270.18f-1 of this chapter by a registered open-end investment company to commit itself to pay in cash all redemptions requested by a shareholder of record as provided in said section.
