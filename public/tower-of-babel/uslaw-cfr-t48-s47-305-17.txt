48 C.F.R. § 47.305-17 — 47.305-17 Returnable cylinders.
Citation: us:cfr:48:47.305-17
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-47.305-17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 47 (Transportation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.247-66, Returnable Cylinders, in a solicitation and contract whenever the contract involves the purchase of gas in contractor-furnished returnable cylinders and the contractor retains title to the cylinders.
