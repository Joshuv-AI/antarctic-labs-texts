10 C.F.R. § 55.21 — Medical examination.
Citation: us:cfr:10:55.21
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-55.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 55 (Operators' Licenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An applicant for a license shall have a medical examination by a physician. A licensee shall have a medical examination by a physician every two years. The physician shall determine that the applicant or licensee meets the requirements of § 55.33(a)(1).
