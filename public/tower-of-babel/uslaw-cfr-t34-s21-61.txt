34 C.F.R. § 21.61 — Release.
Citation: us:cfr:34:21.61
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-21.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 21 (Equal Access To Justice). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an applicant, its agent, or its attorney accepts payment of any award or settlement in conjunction with an application under this part, that acceptance—
(a) Is final and conclusive with respect to that application; and
(b) Constitutes a complete release of any further claim against the United States with respect to that application.
