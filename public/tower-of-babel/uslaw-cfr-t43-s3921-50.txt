43 C.F.R. § 3921.50 — Determining the geographic area for receiving applications to lease.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3921.50
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3921.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3920 (Oil Shale Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After analyzing expressions of leasing interest received under § 3921.30 and complying with the procedures at § 3921.40 of this chapter, the State Director may determine a geographic area for receiving applications to lease. The BLM may also include additional geographic areas available for lease in addition to lands identified in expressions of interest to lease.
