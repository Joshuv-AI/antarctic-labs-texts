18 U.S.C. § 11 — Foreign government defined
Citation: us:usc:18:11
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section11
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 1 (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The term “foreign government”, as used in this title except in sections 112, 878, 970, 1116, and 1201, includes any government, faction, or body of insurgents within a country with which the United States is at peace, irrespective of recognition by the United States.
