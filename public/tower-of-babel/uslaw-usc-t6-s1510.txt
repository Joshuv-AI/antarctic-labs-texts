6 U.S.C. § 1510 — Effective period
Citation: us:usc:6:1510
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section1510
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 6 (Cybersecurity); Subchapter I (Cybersecurity Information Sharing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
Except as provided in subsection (b), this subchapter and the amendments made by this subchapter shall be effective during the period beginning on December 18, 2015 and ending on December 11, 2026.
(b) Exception
With respect to any action authorized by this subchapter or information obtained pursuant to an action authorized by this subchapter, which occurred before the date on which the provisions referred to in subsection (a) cease to have effect, the provisions of this subchapter shall continue in effect.
