3.1.

The core goal of this section is to establish an algebra isomorphism between the novel quantum affine algebra

(defined in Section 2) and its Drinfeld realization

(defined in Section 3). To achieve this, we first state three key theorems, where Theorem 3.5 (surjective homomorphism) and Theorem 3.6 (inverse mapping) imply Isomorphism Theorem 3.4. We present the detailed proof of Theorem 3.5 below, and omit that of Theorem 3.6.

Theorem 3.4
.

Let

denote the Drinfeld realization of

. Then we have an algebraic isomorphism:

Theorem 3.5

Proof of Theorem 3.5

To prove Theorem 3.5, we only need to verify that the mapping
\Psi

\Psi

is obvious, since the image of
\Psi
exactly constitutes the generator set of

is an algebra homomorphism.

We rely on commutation relations of root vectors to check that
\Psi

is an algebra homomorphism.
Before starting the proof, we first state several identities (all obtainable via induction).

\displaystyle(\mathcal{T}_{1}\Phi)^{n}(e_{1})=\mathcal{E}_{n\delta+\alpha_{1}},\quad(\mathcal{T}_{1}\Phi)^{-n}(e_{1})=-k^{-n+1}_{1}k^{-n}_{2}\mathcal{F}_{(n-1)\delta+\alpha_{2}},

Lemma 3.7
.

For
n,m\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}
,
n\geq m
, the commutation relations between
\mathcal{E}_{n\delta+\alpha_{1}}
and
\mathcal{E}_{m\delta+\alpha_{1}}

\displaystyle\mathcal{E}_{n\delta+\alpha_{1}}

\displaystyle\mathcal{E}_{m\delta+\alpha_{1}}+(-1)^{n+m}q^{-2}\mathcal{E}_{m\delta+\alpha_{1}}\mathcal{E}_{n\delta+\alpha_{1}}

\displaystyle=q^{-2}\mathcal{E}_{(n-1)\delta+\alpha_{1}}\mathcal{E}_{(m+1)\delta+\alpha_{1}}+(-1)^{n+m}\mathcal{E}_{(m+1)\delta+\alpha_{1}}\mathcal{E}_{(n-1)\delta+\alpha_{1}}.

Proof
.

We prove the lemma for indices
n
and
m
by double induction. First, we verify that the base case
P(n,0)
holds. Second, we assume that the lemma
P(n,i)
is true for all positive integers
i
satisfying
i<m
, then proceed to prove that
P(n,m)
holds.

(I) When
m=0
, let us consider the relations

(3.1)

\displaystyle\mathcal{E}_{n\delta+\alpha_{1}}e_{1}+(-1)^{n}q^{-2}e_{1}\mathcal{E}_{n\delta+\alpha_{1}}=q^{-2}\mathcal{E}_{(n-1)\delta+\alpha_{1}}\mathcal{E}_{\delta+\alpha_{1}}+(-1)^{n}\mathcal{E}_{\delta+\alpha_{1}}\mathcal{E}_{(n-1)\delta+\alpha_{1}}.

We first examine the case of
n=1
, then extend the result to general
n\geq 1
.

Substituting
n=1
and
m=0
into (3.1), we need to prove

\mathcal{E}_{\delta+\alpha_{1}}e_{1}=q^{-2}e_{1}\mathcal{E}_{\delta+\alpha_{1}}.
It is now straightforward to verify that

\mathcal{E}_{\delta+\alpha_{1}}e_{1}-q^{-2}e_{1}\mathcal{E}_{\delta+\alpha_{1}}=\mathcal{T}_{1}\Phi\left(-e_{1}k^{-1}_{2}f_{2}+q^{-2}k^{-1}_{2}f_{2}e_{1}\right).

We use
(A2)
and
(A3)
to get that

\mathcal{E}_{\delta+\alpha_{1}}e_{1}=q^{-2}e_{1}\mathcal{E}_{\delta+\alpha_{1}}
.

Assume relation (3.1) holds for
n=k\,(k>1)
. To confirm it holds for
n=k+1
, we need to use the following identities

(3.2)

\displaystyle[2]_{q}\mathcal{E}_{(t+1)\delta+\alpha_{1}}=\mathcal{E}_{\delta}\mathcal{E}_{t\delta+\alpha_{1}}+\mathcal{E}_{t\delta+\alpha_{1}}\mathcal{E}_{\delta},\quad\forall t\geq 0,\qquad(\text{by Theorem 2.10})

and use induction hypothesis to get

(3.3)

\displaystyle\mathcal{E}_{s\delta+\alpha_{1}}e_{1}+(-1)^{s}q^{-2}e_{1}\mathcal{E}_{s\delta+\alpha_{1}}=q^{-2}\mathcal{E}_{(s-1)\delta+\alpha_{1}}\mathcal{E}_{\delta+\alpha_{1}}+(-1)^{s}\mathcal{E}_{\delta+\alpha_{1}}\mathcal{E}_{(s-1)\delta+\alpha_{1}},

Applying (3.2) and (3.3), it is straightforward to verify that

\displaystyle[2]_{q}\mathcal{E}_{(k+1)\delta+\alpha_{1}}e_{1}

\displaystyle=\Bigl\{\mathcal{E}_{\delta}\mathcal{E}_{k\delta+\alpha_{1}}+\mathcal{E}_{k\delta+\alpha_{1}}\mathcal{E}_{\delta}\Bigr\}e_{1}

\displaystyle\quad+(-1)^{k}\Bigl\{[2]_{q}\mathcal{E}_{2\delta+\alpha_{1}}-\mathcal{E}_{\delta+\alpha_{1}}\mathcal{E}_{\delta}\Bigr\}\mathcal{E}_{(k-1)\delta+\alpha_{1}}+[2]_{q}\mathcal{E}_{k\delta+\alpha_{1}}\mathcal{E}_{\delta+\alpha_{1}}

\displaystyle\quad-\Bigl\{(-1)^{k-1}q^{-2}e_{1}\mathcal{E}_{k\delta+\alpha_{1}}+q^{-2}\mathcal{E}_{(k-1)\delta+\alpha_{1}}\mathcal{E}_{\delta+\alpha_{1}}+(-1)^{k}\mathcal{E}_{\delta+\alpha_{1}}\mathcal{E}_{(k-1)\delta+\alpha_{1}}\Bigr\}\mathcal{E}_{\delta}

\displaystyle=[2]_{q}\Bigl\{(-1)^{k}q^{-2}e_{1}\mathcal{E}_{(k+1)\delta+\alpha_{1}}+q^{-2}\mathcal{E}_{k\delta+\alpha_{1}}\mathcal{E}_{\delta+\alpha_{1}}+(-1)^{k+1}\mathcal{E}_{\delta+\alpha_{1}}\mathcal{E}_{k\delta+\alpha_{1}}

\displaystyle\quad+\Bigl(\mathcal{E}_{k\delta+\alpha_{1}}\mathcal{E}_{\delta+\alpha_{1}}+(-1)^{k+1}q^{-2}\mathcal{E}_{\delta+\alpha_{1}}\mathcal{E}_{k\delta+\alpha_{1}}-q^{-2}\mathcal{E}_{(k-1)\delta+\alpha_{1}}\mathcal{E}_{2\delta+\alpha_{1}}

\displaystyle\quad+(-1)^{k}\mathcal{E}_{2\delta+\alpha_{1}}\mathcal{E}_{(k-1)\delta+\alpha_{1}}\Bigr)\Bigr\}

\displaystyle=[2]_{q}\Bigl\{(-1)^{k}q^{-2}e_{1}\mathcal{E}_{(k+1)\delta+\alpha_{1}}+q^{-2}\mathcal{E}_{k\delta+\alpha_{1}}\mathcal{E}_{\delta+\alpha_{1}}+(-1)^{k+1}\mathcal{E}_{\delta+\alpha_{1}}\mathcal{E}_{k\delta+\alpha_{1}}

\displaystyle\quad+(\mathcal{T}_{1}\Phi)\Bigl(\mathcal{E}_{(k-1)\delta+\alpha_{1}}e_{1}+(-1)^{k+1}q^{-2}e_{1}\mathcal{E}_{(k-1)\delta+\alpha_{1}}-q^{-2}\mathcal{E}_{(k-2)\delta+\alpha_{1}}\mathcal{E}_{\delta+\alpha_{1}}

\displaystyle\quad+(-1)^{k}\mathcal{E}_{\delta+\alpha_{1}}\mathcal{E}_{(k-2)\delta+\alpha_{1}}\Bigr)\Bigr\}.

We ultimately obtain

\displaystyle\mathcal{E}_{(k+1)\delta+\alpha_{1}}e_{1}+(-1)^{k+1}q^{-2}e_{1}\mathcal{E}_{(k+1)\delta+\alpha_{1}}=q^{-2}\mathcal{E}_{k\delta+\alpha_{1}}\mathcal{E}_{\delta+\alpha_{1}}+(-1)^{k+1}\mathcal{E}_{\delta+\alpha_{1}}\mathcal{E}_{k\delta+\alpha_{1}}.

Thus, relation (3.1) holds for all
n>0

(II) When
m=t
,
n>t>0

\displaystyle\mathcal{E}_{n\delta+\alpha_{1}}\mathcal{E}_{t\delta+\alpha_{1}}+(-1)^{n+t}

\displaystyle q^{-2}\mathcal{E}_{t\delta+\alpha_{1}}\mathcal{E}_{n\delta+\alpha_{1}}

(3.4)

\displaystyle=q^{-2}\mathcal{E}_{(n-1)\delta+\alpha_{1}}\mathcal{E}_{(t+1)\delta+\alpha_{1}}+(-1)^{n+t}\mathcal{E}_{(t+1)\delta+\alpha_{1}}\mathcal{E}_{(n-1)\delta+\alpha_{1}}.

To prove the claim, applying the automorphism
\mathcal{T}_{1}\Phi
to relation (3.4), we obtain

\displaystyle\mathcal{E}_{(n+1)\delta+\alpha_{1}}\mathcal{E}_{(t+1)\delta+\alpha_{1}}+

\displaystyle(-1)^{n+t}q^{-2}\mathcal{E}_{(t+1)\delta+\alpha_{1}}\mathcal{E}_{(n+1)\delta+\alpha_{1}}
