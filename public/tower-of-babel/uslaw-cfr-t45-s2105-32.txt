45 C.F.R. § 2105.32 — Will a submitter be notified of a FOIA lawsuit?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2105.32
Creator: Commission Of Fine Arts
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2105.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2105 (Requirements For Compliance With The Freedom Of Information Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you file a lawsuit seeking to compel the disclosure of confidential information, the Agency must promptly notify the submitter.
