29 C.F.R. § 1405.10 — Effect on employment ceilings.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1405.10
Creator: Federal Mediation And Conciliation Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1405.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1405 (Part-Time Employment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Effective October 1, 1980, part-time employees will be counted on the basis of the fractional part of the 40-hour week actually worked. For example two employees each working twenty hours a week will count as one employee.
