16 C.F.R. § 309.21 — Recordkeeping.
Citation: us:cfr:16:309.21
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-309.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 309 (Labeling Requirements For Alternative Fuels And Alternative Fueled Vehicles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Manufacturers required to comply this subpart shall establish, maintain, and retain copies of all data, reports, records, and procedures used to meet the requirements of this subpart for three years after the end of the model year to which they relate. They must be available for inspection by Federal Trade Commission staff members, or by people authorized by the Federal Trade Commission.
