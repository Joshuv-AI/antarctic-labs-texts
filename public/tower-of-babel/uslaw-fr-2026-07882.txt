DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice: 13002]


Title: Cancellation of Shipping Coordination Committee Public 
Meeting in Preparation for International Maritime Organization MEPC84 
Session

    The subject public meeting scheduled for April 15, 2026, and 
noticed in 91 FR 12038 and 91 FR 18522 was cancelled due to the lapse 
in appropriations impacting the Department of Homeland Security and the 
United States Coast Guard. All those who RSVP'ed were contacted via 
email to inform of the cancellation. For further information, contact 
[email protected].

Coast Guard Liaison Officer, Office of Ocean and Polar Affairs,
