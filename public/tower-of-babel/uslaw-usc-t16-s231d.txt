16 U.S.C. § 231d — Repeal of inconsistent laws
Citation: us:usc:16:231d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section231d
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter XXV (Jean Lafitte National Historical Park And Preserve); Part B (Chalmette Unit). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All Acts or parts of Acts inconsistent with this subchapter are repealed to the extent of such inconsistency.
