16 U.S.C. § 1339 — Limitation of authority
Citation: us:usc:16:1339
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section1339
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 30 (Wild Horses And Burros: Protection, Management, And Control). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to authorize the Secretary to relocate wild free-roaming horses or burros to areas of the public lands where they do not presently exist.
