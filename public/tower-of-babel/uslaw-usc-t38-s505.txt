38 U.S.C. § 505 — Opinions of Attorney General
Citation: us:usc:38:505
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section505
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part I (General Provisions); Chapter 5 (Authority And Duties Of The Secretary); Subchapter I (General Authorities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may require the opinion of the Attorney General on any question of law arising in the administration of the Department.
