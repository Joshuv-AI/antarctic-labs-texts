21 C.F.R. § 884.4260 — Hygroscopic Laminaria cervical dilator.
Citation: us:cfr:21:884.4260
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-884.4260
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 884 (Obstetrical And Gynecological Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. A hygroscopic Laminaria cervical dilator is a device designed to dilate (stretch open) the cervical os by cervical insertion of a conical and expansible material made from the root of a seaweed (Laminaria digitata or Laminaria japonica). The device is used to induce abortion.
(b) Classification. Class II (performance standards).
