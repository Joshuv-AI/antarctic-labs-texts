42 U.S.C. § 6978 — Payments
Citation: us:usc:42:6978
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section6978
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 82 (Solid Waste Disposal); Subchapter VII (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) General rule
Payments of grants under this chapter may be made (after necessary adjustment on account of previously made underpayments or overpayments) in advance or by way of reimbursement, and in such installments and on such conditions as the Administrator may determine.
(b) Prohibition
No grant may be made under this chapter to any private profitmaking organization.
