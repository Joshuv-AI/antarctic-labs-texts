7 U.S.C. § 452 — Supervision of division of cooperative marketing
Citation: us:usc:7:452
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section452
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 18 (Cooperative Marketing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The division of cooperative marketing shall be under the direction and supervision of the Secretary of Agriculture.
