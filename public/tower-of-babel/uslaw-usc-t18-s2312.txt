18 U.S.C. § 2312 — Transportation of stolen vehicles
Citation: us:usc:18:2312
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section2312
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 113 (Stolen Property). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever transports in interstate or foreign commerce a motor vehicle, vessel, or aircraft, knowing the same to have been stolen, shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than 10 years, or both.
