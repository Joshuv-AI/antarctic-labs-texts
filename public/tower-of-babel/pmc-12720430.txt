Correction: New Miocene litoptern remains from Colombia and ecological structure of American Neogene herbivore guilds
Authors: Andrew J McGrath, Darin A Croft, Juan D Carrillo, M Gabriela Suárez, Andres Vanegas, Siobhan B Cooke, Andres Link
Published: 2025

Correction to: BMC Zoology (2025) 10:17

10.1186/s40850-025-00232-4

Following publication of the original article [1], the authors reported an error that the figure caption of Fig. 3 was repeated in Figs. 6 and 7.

The correct captions of Figs. 6 and 7 are provided below.

Fig 6 Mandibles of Mesolicaphrium sanalfonense. VPPLT 1697: right and left external incisors, dp1-4, and erupting m1 in A. occlusal and B. left lateral (flipped) views. VPPLT 1118: mandible preserving right p2–m3 and left p1-m3 in C. occlusal and D. left lateral (flipped) views. Scale bar equals 1 cm.

Fig 7 Mandibular fragments and isolated teeth of Mesolicaphrium sananfonense. VPPLT 1605, mandibular fragment with partial right p3 and p4-m3 in A. occlusal and B. lateral views. LT-0059, mandibular fragments with C. right p1-3, D. left dp4-m1. E. LT-0060, right m1-2 with erupting p4. F. VPPLT 1264, isolated right M3. Scale bar equals 1 cm.

The original article [1] has been corrected.
