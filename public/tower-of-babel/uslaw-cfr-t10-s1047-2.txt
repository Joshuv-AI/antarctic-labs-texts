10 C.F.R. § 1047.2 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1047.2
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1047.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1047 (Limited Arrest Authority And Use Of Force By Protective Force Officers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to DOE and DOE contractor protective force personnel armed pursuant to section 161.k. of the Atomic Energy Act of 1954 (42 U.S.C. 2011 et seq.) to protect nuclear weapons, special nuclear material, clasified matter, nuclear facilities, and related property.
