42 C.F.R. § 34.1 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:42:34.1
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-34.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 34 (Medical Examination Of Aliens). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this part shall apply to the medical examination of:
(a) Aliens applying for a visa at an embassy or consulate of the United States;
(b) Aliens arriving in the United States;
(c) Aliens required by DHS to have a medical examination in connection with the determination of their admissibility into the United States; and
(d) Aliens applying for adjustment of status.
