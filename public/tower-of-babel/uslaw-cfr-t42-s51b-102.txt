42 C.F.R. § 51b.102 — b.102 Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:42:51b.102
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-51b.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 51b (PART 51b—PROJECT GRANTS FOR PREVENTIVE HEALTH SERVICES). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in these regulations:
Act means the Public Health Service Act, as amended.
Secretary means the Secretary of Health and Human Services (HHS) or any other officer or employee of that Department to whom the authority involved has been delegated.
State means one of the 50 States, the District of Columbia, Guam, the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico, the Northern Mariana Islands, the Virgin Islands, American Samoa, and the Trust Territory of the Pacific Islands.
