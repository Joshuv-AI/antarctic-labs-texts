Retraction Note: An autocatalytic multicomponent DNAzyme nanomachine for tumor-specific photothermal therapy sensitization in pancreatic cancer
Authors: Jiaqi Yan, Xiaodong Ma, Danna Liang, Meixin Ran, Dongdong Zheng, Xiaodong Chen, Shichong Zhou, Weijian Sun, Xian Shen, Hongbo Zhang

Retraction Note: Nature Communications 10.1038/s41467-023-42740-2, published online 30 October 2023

The authors have retracted this article. After publication, the authors identified the following errors in the figures:Fig. 8a duplicated 24 h and 48 h images;Figure 8g, 9c, and 9g image errors and mislabelling;Figure 8h and 9d incorrect data in the plots.
