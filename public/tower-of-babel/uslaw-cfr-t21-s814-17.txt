21 C.F.R. § 814.17 — Service of orders.
Citation: us:cfr:21:814.17
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-814.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 814 (Premarket Approval Of Medical Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Orders issued under this part will be served in person by a designated officer or employee of FDA on, or by registered mail to, the applicant or the designated agent at the applicant's or designated agent's last known address in FDA's records.
