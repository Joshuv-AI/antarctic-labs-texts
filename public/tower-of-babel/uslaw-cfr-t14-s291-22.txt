14 C.F.R. § 291.22 — Aircraft accident liability insurance requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:14:291.22
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-291.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 291 (Cargo Operations In Interstate Air Transportation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No air carrier shall operate all-cargo aircraft or provide all-cargo air transportation unless such carrier has and maintains in effect aircraft accident liability coverage that meets the requirements of part 205 of this chapter.
