33 U.S.C. § 2201 — “Secretary” defined
Citation: us:usc:33:2201
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section2201
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 36 (Water Resources Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of this Act, the term “Secretary” means the Secretary of the Army.
