10 C.F.R. § 706.11 — Consent elections.
Citation: us:cfr:10:706.11
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-706.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 706 (Security Policies And Practices Relating To Labor-Management Relations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In accordance with the recommendation of the President's Commission on Labor Relations in the Atomic Energy Installations, it is the policy of DOE to encourage every effort by management and labor at DOE installations to determine bargaining units and representatives by agreement and consent elections in preference to contested proceedings before the National Labor Relations Board.
