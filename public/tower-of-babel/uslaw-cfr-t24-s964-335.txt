24 C.F.R. § 964.335 — Grant agreement.
Citation: us:cfr:24:964.335
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-964.335
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 964 (Tenant Participation And Tenant Opportunities In Public Housing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) General. HUD will enter into a grant agreement with the recipients of a Family Investment Centers grant which defines the legal framework for the relationship between HUD and a HA.
(b) Term of grant agreement. A grant will be for a term of three to five years depending upon the tasks undertaken, as defined under this subpart.
