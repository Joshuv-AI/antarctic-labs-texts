2 U.S.C. § 288f — Immunity proceedings
Citation: us:usc:2:288f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section288f
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 9D (Office Of Senate Legal Counsel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When directed to do so pursuant to section 288b(d) of this title, the Counsel shall serve as the duly authorized representative of the Senate or a committee or subcommittee of the Senate in requesting a United States district court to issue an order granting immunity pursuant to section 6005 of title 18.
