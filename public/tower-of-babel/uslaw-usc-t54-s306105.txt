54 U.S.C. § 306105 — Agency programs and projects
Citation: us:usc:54:306105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section306105
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3061 (Program Responsibilities And Authorities); Subchapter I (In General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Consistent with the agency’s missions and mandates, each Federal agency shall carry out agency programs and projects (including those under which any Federal assistance is provided or any Federal license, permit, or other approval is required) in accordance with the purposes of this division and give consideration to programs and projects that will further the purposes of this division.
