7 C.F.R. § 925.50 — Marketing policy.
Citation: us:cfr:7:925.50
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-925.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 925 (Grapes Grown In A Designated Area Of Southeastern California). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each season prior to making any recommendation pursuant to § 925.51 the committee shall submit to the Secretary a report setting forth its marketing policy for the ensuing marketing season. Such marketing policy report shall contain information relative to:
(a) The estimated total shipments of grapes produced within the production area;
(b) The expected general quality of grapes in the production area;
(c) The expected demand conditions for grapes;
(d) The probable prices for grapes;
(e) Supplies of competing commodities, including foreign produced grapes;
(f) Trend and level of consumer income;
(g) Other factors having a bearing on the marketing of grapes; and
(h) The type of regulations expected to be recommended during the marketing season.
