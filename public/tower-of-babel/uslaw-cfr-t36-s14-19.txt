36 C.F.R. § 14.19 — Severability.
Citation: us:cfr:36:14.19
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-14.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 14 (Rights-Of-Way). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a court holds any provisions of the regulations in this part or their applicability to any person or circumstances invalid, the remainder of these rules and their applicability to other people or circumstances will not be affected.
