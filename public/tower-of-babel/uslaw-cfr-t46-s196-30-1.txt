46 C.F.R. § 196.30-1 — -1 Repairs to boilers and pressure vessels.
Citation: us:cfr:46:196.30-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-196.30-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 196 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Before making any repairs to boilers or unfired pressure vessels, the Chief Engineer shall submit a report covering the nature of the repairs to the Officer in Charge, Marine Inspection, at or nearest to the U.S. port where the repairs are to be made.
