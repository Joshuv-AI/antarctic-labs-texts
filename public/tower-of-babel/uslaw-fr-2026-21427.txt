DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION


Notice Announcing From Seedlings to Scale and Transformative 
Research in Special Education Grants Programs

AGENCY: Department of Education.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Institute of Education Sciences (IES) at the U.S. 
Department of Education (ED) is soliciting applications for new awards 
of the Fiscal Year (FY) 2027 From Seedlings to Scale and Transformative 
Research in Special Education grants programs, Assistance Listing 
Numbers (ALNs) 84.305J and 84.324T.

DATES: Complete proposals must be submitted electronically through the 
Grants.gov ``APPLY'' function by 11:59:59 p.m. Eastern time December 
11, 2026.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: 
    Akilah Nelson for the 84.305J From Seedlings to Scale Grants 
Program. Telephone: (202) 804-7493. Email. [email protected].
    Courtney Pollack for the 84.324T Transformative Research in Special 
Education. Telephone: (202) 987-0071. [email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The FY 2027 application notices and 
instructions include program foci, requirements, and scientific peer 
review criteria for each competition listed below. IES is announcing 
the following competitions:
    National Center for Education Research (NCER) Competition: From 
Seedlings to Scale Grants Program (ALN 84.305J). Under this 
competition, NCER will consider only Phase One applications that 
address the ``C-STEP--Coordinating Systems to Translate Evidence into 
Policy and Practice'' focus area.
    National Center for Special Education Research (NCSER) Competition: 
Transformative Research in Special

[[Page 66321]]

Education (ALN 84.324T). Under this competition, NCSER will consider 
only Phase One applications that propose to research and develop 
transformative solutions to dramatically improve preservice teacher 
education systems so they can better attract, train, and retain preK-12 
general and special education teachers who are prepared to provide 
high-quality instruction and substantially improve outcomes for 
students with or at risk for disabilities.
    Maximum Award and Duration: For the From Seedlings to Scale Grants 
Program (ALN 84.305J), the maximum Phase One award is $500,000 for a 
one-year project period. For the Transformative Research in Special 
Education (ALN 84.324T) program, the maximum Phase One award is 
$3,750,000 for a three-year project.
    Eligible Applicants: Institutions that have the ability and 
capacity to conduct scientifically valid research are eligible to 
apply. Eligible applicants include, but are not limited to, 
institutions of higher education and non-profit, for-profit, public, or 
private organizations and entities.
    Program Authority: 20 U.S.C. 9501 et seq.
