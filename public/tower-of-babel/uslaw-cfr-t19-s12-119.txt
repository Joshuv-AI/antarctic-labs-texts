19 C.F.R. § 12.119 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:19:12.119
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-12.119
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 12 (Special Classes Of Merchandise). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Sections 12.120 through 12.127 apply to the importation into the customs territory of the United States of:
(a) Chemical substances in bulk form and as part of a mixture under TSCA;
(b) TSCA-excluded chemicals; and
(c) Articles containing a chemical substance or mixture if so required by the Administrator by specific rule under TSCA.
