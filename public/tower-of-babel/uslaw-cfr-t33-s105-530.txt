33 C.F.R. § 105.530 — Qualifications of screeners.
Citation: us:cfr:33:105.530
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-105.530
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 105 (Maritime Security: Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to the requirements for facility personnel with security duties contained in § 105.210, screening personnel at cruise ship terminals must—
(a) Have a combination of education and experience that the FSO has determined to be sufficient for the individual to perform the duties of the position; and
(b) Be capable of using all screening methods and equipment needed to perform the duties of the position.
