Non-archimedean topological mirror symmetry for SL n \mathrm{SL}_{n} and PGL n \mathrm{PGL}_{n} Higgs bundles
Elsa Maneval

Abstract
. The Hausel–Thaddeus conjectures concern topological mirror symmetry between moduli spaces of SL n \mathrm{SL}_{n} and PGL n \mathrm{PGL}_{n} Higgs bundles on a curve. A non-archimedean approach was introduced by Groechenig, Wyss and Ziegler, proving the conjecture for coprime rank and degree. This article is concerned with its generalisation to the non-coprime case. We treat both the classical ( D = K D=K ) and meromorphic ( D > K D>K ) settings. We prove an equality of p p -adic volumes twisted by gerbes between moduli spaces of SL n \mathrm{SL}_{n} and PGL n \mathrm{PGL}_{n} Higgs bundles of arbitrary degree. In the meromorphic case, building on results of Maulik and Shen, we show that these twisted p p -adic volumes are related to intersection cohomology. We also conjecture a connection between these p p -adic volumes and B ​ P ​ S BPS cohomology.

The purpose of this article is to establish certain equalities of

Let

1.3.
Interpretations of
p
-adic integrals

In general, it still remains to interpret

(Corollary

)

Let

E(M_{\mathrm{SL}_{n}}^{L})=E_{\textnormal{st},\alpha_{N}^{d^{\prime}}}(M_{\mathrm{PGL}_{n}}^{e}).

It extends
[

, Theorem 7.21]
where the degree of

)

1.4.
About the proof

The proof of our main theorem relies on a generalisation to arbitrary fields of

, Propositions 3.2 and 3.6]

-type mirror symmetries. In

As in

.

1.5.
Organisation of the article.

In
section 2
we explain the construction of the gerbes and some background for the proofs of
SYZ
-type dualities.

The moduli stack of
\mathrm{SL}_{n}
-Higgs bundles can be seen as a
\mu_{n}
-gerbe
\alpha_{n}
over its rigidification. However, the
SYZ
duality is better described in terms of the induced
n
-torsion
\mathbb{G}_{m}
-gerbe
\alpha
, which is also naturally a moduli stack of Higgs bundles.

For the
\mathrm{PGL}_{n}
-side, the moduli stack is constructed as a quotient by the finite group
\Gamma=\mathcal{P}ic^{0}_{\mathcal{C}/S}[n]\simeq(\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z})^{2g}
of the
\mathrm{SL}_{n}
stack. The action of
\mathcal{P}ic^{0}_{\mathcal{C}/S}
by tensorisation on
\mathrm{GL}_{n}
-bundles restricts to an action of
\Gamma
on
\mathrm{SL}_{n}
-bundles. Often one defines the
\Gamma
-action directly on the rigidified stack, where scalar automorphisms do not appear. However, rigidifying line bundles in
\Gamma
, we get a
\Gamma
-action at the level of the non-rigidified stack. It gives the gerbe
\alpha
a structure of
\Gamma
-equivariant
\mathbb{G}_{m}
-gerbe, hence makes it a gerbe over the
\mathrm{PGL}_{n}
moduli space. This construction requires the choice of a degree
1
line bundle
N
over
\mathcal{C}
, which replaces the base point
c\in\mathcal{C}
in
[
HT03
]
. We explain in Appendix
A
the precise relation to their setup. To characterize the trivialisations of the
\Gamma
-equivariant gerbe, we study a relative group scheme
\Gamma_{N}\longrightarrow\mathcal{C}
, which acts on Higgs bundles corresponding to
\Gamma
-equivariant sections of the gerbe
\alpha
.

In
section 3
, we generalise the
SYZ
-type duality of
[
HT03
]
to arbitrary fields and arbitrary determinant line bundle
L
.

The main theorem is proven in
section 4
. The proof uses the
p
-adic measure constructed in
[
COW24
]
and the arguments of
[
GWZ20
]
. Namely, we interpret gerbe functions in terms of Tate duality pairings when integrating against abelian varieties.

Finally,
section 5
is devoted to completing the proof of topological mirror symmetry in the coprime case with odd degree
D
and to the interpretation of
p
-adic integrals in terms of intersection and
BPS
cohomologies.
