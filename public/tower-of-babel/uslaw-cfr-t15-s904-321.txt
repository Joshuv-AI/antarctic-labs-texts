15 C.F.R. § 904.321 — Reinstatement of permit.
Citation: us:cfr:15:904.321
Creator: National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-904.321
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 904 (Civil Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A permit suspended for a specified period of time will be reinstated automatically at the end of the period.
(b) A permit suspended until stated requirements are met will be reinstated only by order of NOAA.
