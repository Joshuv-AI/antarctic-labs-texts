5 C.F.R. § 850.202 — Survivor elections.
Citation: us:cfr:5:850.202
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-850.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 850 (Electronic Retirement Processing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A survivor election under subsection (j) or (k) of section 8339, or under section 8416, 8417, or 8420 of title 5, United States Code, which is otherwise required to be in writing may be effected in such form as the Director prescribes under § 850.104.
