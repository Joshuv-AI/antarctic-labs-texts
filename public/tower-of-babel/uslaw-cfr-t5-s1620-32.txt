5 C.F.R. § 1620.32 — Employees who move to a NAF instrumentality on or after August 10, 1996.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1620.32
Creator: Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1620.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1620 (Expanded And Continuing Eligibility). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any employee who moves from a CSRS- or FERS-covered position to a NAF instrumentality on or after August 10, 1996, and who elects to continue to be covered by CSRS or FERS, will be eligible to contribute to the TSP as determined in accordance with 5 CFR part 1600.
