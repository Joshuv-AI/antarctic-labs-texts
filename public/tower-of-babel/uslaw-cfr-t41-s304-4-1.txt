41 C.F.R. § 304-4.1 — -4.1 Alternative authorities for accepting non-Federal source travel expense payments.
Citation: us:cfr:41:304-4.1
Creator: Chapter 304—Payment Of Travel Expenses From A Non-Federal Source
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-304-4.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 304-4 (4—Authority). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies may accept payment for travel expenses to events other than meetings from a non-Federal source pursuant to an agency gift statute or similar statutory authority. However, this part is the only authority agencies may use to accept (or authorize an employee to accept on the agency's behalf) payment for travel expenses from a non-Federal source to attend a meeting.
