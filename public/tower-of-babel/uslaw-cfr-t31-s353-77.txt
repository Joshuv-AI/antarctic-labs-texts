31 C.F.R. § 353.77 — Exchanges by fiduciaries.
Citation: us:cfr:31:353.77
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-353.77
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 353 (Regulations Governing Definitive United States Savings Bonds, Series Ee And Hh). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Fiduciaries are authorized to request an exchange of bonds of one series for those of another, pursuant to any applicable Department of the Treasury offering. A living coowner of beneficiary named on the bonds submitted in exchange may be retained in the same capacity on the new bonds.
