24 U.S.C. § 18 — Rules and regulations for Army and Navy Hospital
Citation: us:usc:24:18
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title24-section18
Context: United States Code, Title 24 (Hospitals And Asylums); Chapter 1 (Navy Hospitals, Army And Navy Hospital, And Hospital Relief For Seamen And Others). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Army and Navy General Hospital at Hot Springs, Arkansas, shall be subject to such rules, regulations, and restrictions as shall be provided by the President of the United States and shall remain under the jurisdiction and control of the Department of the Army.
