31 C.F.R. § 360.0 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:31:360.0
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-360.0
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 360 (Regulations Governing Definitive United States Savings Bonds, Series I). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The regulations in this part apply to definitive (paper) Series I savings bonds that have not been converted to book-entry bonds through TreasuryDirect®.
(b) The regulations in 31 CFR part 363 apply to:
(1) book-entry Series I savings bonds that were originally issued as book-entry bonds in TreasuryDirect®; and
(2) definitive Series I savings bonds that have been converted to book-entry bonds through TreasuryDirect®.
