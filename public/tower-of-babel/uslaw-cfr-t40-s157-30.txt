40 C.F.R. § 157.30 — Voluntary use of child-resistant packaging.
Citation: us:cfr:40:157.30
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-157.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 157 (Packaging Requirements For Pesticides And Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A registrant whose product is not required to be in child-resistant packaging may distribute or sell his pesticide product in child-resistant packaging. If he does so, that packaging must meet the standards for child-resistant packaging stated in § 157.32. The registrant must certify to this effect in accordance with § 157.34, and must retain the records required by § 157.36.
