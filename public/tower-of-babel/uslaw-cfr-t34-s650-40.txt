34 C.F.R. § 650.40 — What institutional agreements are needed?
Citation: us:cfr:34:650.40
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-650.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 650 (Jacob K. Javits Fellowship Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Students enrolled in an otherwise eligible institution of higher education may receive fellowships only if the institution enters into an agreement with the Secretary to comply with the provisions of this part.
