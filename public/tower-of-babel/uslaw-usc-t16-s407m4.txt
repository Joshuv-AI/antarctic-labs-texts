16 U.S.C. § 407m–4 — Erection of replica of Graff House; maintenance
Citation: us:usc:16:407m–4
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section407m–4
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LI (Independence National Historical Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is further authorized to erect on the site aforesaid, with donated funds, a replica of the Graff House and to furnish and maintain the same.
