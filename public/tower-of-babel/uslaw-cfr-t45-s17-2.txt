45 C.F.R. § 17.2 — Basic policy.
Citation: us:cfr:45:17.2
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-17.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 17 (Release Of Adverse Information To News Media). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All adverse information release to news media shall be factual in content and accurate in description. Disparaging terminology not essential to the content and purpose of the publicity shall be avoided.
