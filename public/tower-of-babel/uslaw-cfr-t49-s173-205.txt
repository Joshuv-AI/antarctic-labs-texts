49 C.F.R. § 173.205 — Specification cylinders for liquid hazardous materials.
Citation: us:cfr:49:173.205
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-173.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 173 (Shippers—General Requirements For Shipments And Packagings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When § 172.101 of this subchapter specifies that a hazardous material must be packaged under this section, the use of any specification or UN cylinder, except those specified for acetylene, is authorized. Cylinders used for toxic materials in Division 6.1 or 2.3 must conform to the requirements of § 173.40.
