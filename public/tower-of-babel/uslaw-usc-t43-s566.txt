43 U.S.C. § 566 — Maintenance of public reservations and conveyance to municipality
Citation: us:usc:43:566
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section566
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 12 (Reclamation And Irrigation Of Lands By Federal Government); Subchapter XV (Town Sites, Parks, Playgrounds, And School Sites). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The public reservations in such town sites shall be improved and maintained by the town authorities at the expense of the town; and upon the organization thereof as municipal corporations the said reservations shall be conveyed to such corporations by the Secretary of the Interior, subject to the condition that they shall be used forever for public purposes.
