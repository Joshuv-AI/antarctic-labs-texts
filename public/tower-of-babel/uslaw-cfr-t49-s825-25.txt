49 C.F.R. § 825.25 — Oral argument.
Citation: us:cfr:49:825.25
Creator: National Transportation Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-825.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 825 (Rules Of Procedure For Merchant Marine Appeals From Decisions Of The Commandant, U.S. Coast Guard). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If any party desires to argue a case orally before the Board, he should request leave to make such argument in his brief filed pursuant to § 825.20.
(b) Oral argument before the Board will normally not be granted unless the Board finds good cause for such argument. If granted, the parties will be advised of the date.
