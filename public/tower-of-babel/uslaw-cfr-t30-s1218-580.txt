30 C.F.R. § 1218.580 — When do I submit Form ONRR-4444?
Citation: us:cfr:30:1218.580
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1218.580
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1218 (Collection Of Royalties, Rentals, Bonuses, And Other Monies Due The Federal Government). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Initially, you must submit Form ONRR-4444 by November 29, 2006, and subsequently, within 2 weeks of any change of your address.
