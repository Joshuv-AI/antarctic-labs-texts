48 C.F.R. § 653.200 — 653.200 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:48:653.200
Creator: Chapter 6—Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-653.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 653 (Forms). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes or references optional and DOS forms for use in acquisition. Consistent with FAR 53.200, this subpart is arranged by subject matter, in the same order as and keyed to the parts of the DOSAR in which the form usage requirements are addressed.
