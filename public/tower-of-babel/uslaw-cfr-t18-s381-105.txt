18 C.F.R. § 381.105 — Method of payment.
Citation: us:cfr:18:381.105
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-381.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 381 (Fees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Fee payment shall be made by check or money order payable to the Treasurer of the United States. The check should state the nature of the filing and the docket number where applicable so that the fee category for which the check is being submitted is clearly identifiable.
