2 U.S.C. § 4713 — Reporting payments made to witnesses before Committee on Standards of Official Conduct
Citation: us:usc:2:4713
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section4713
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 47 (Congressional Ethics); Subchapter II (House Of Representatives). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law or any other rule or regulation, any information on payments made by the Committee on Standards of Official Conduct of the House of Representatives to an individual for attendance as a witness before the Committee in executive session during a Congress shall be reported not later than the second semiannual report filed under section 5535 of this title in the following Congress.
