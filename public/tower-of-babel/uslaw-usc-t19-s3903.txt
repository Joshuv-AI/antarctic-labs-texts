19 U.S.C. § 3903 — Measures for the importation and exportation of rough diamonds
Citation: us:usc:19:3903
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section3903
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 25 (Clean Diamond Trade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Prohibition
The President shall prohibit the importation into, or exportation from, the United States of any rough diamond, from whatever source, that has not been controlled through the Kimberley Process Certification Scheme.
(b) Waiver
The President may waive the requirements set forth in subsection (a) with respect to a particular country for periods of not more than 1 year each, if, with respect to each such waiver—
(1)
the President determines and reports to the appropriate congressional committees that such country is taking effective steps to implement the Kimberley Process Certification Scheme; or
(2)
the President determines that the waiver is in the national interests of the United States, and reports such determination to the appropriate congressional committees, together with the reasons therefor.
