22 U.S.C. § 10807 — Prohibition on investment by United States persons in the Russian Federation
Citation: us:usc:22:10807
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section10807
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 115 (Sanctions With Respect To The Russian Federation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Effective on the date that is 30 days after September 18, 2026, the following are prohibited:
(1)
New investment in the Russian Federation by a United States person, wherever located.
(2)
The exportation, reexportation, sale, or supply, directly or indirectly, from the United States, or by a United States person, wherever located, of any category of services identified by the Secretary of the Treasury, in consultation with the Secretary of State, to any person located in the Russian Federation.
(3)
Any approval, financing, facilitation, or guarantee by a United States person, wherever located, of a transaction by a foreign person if the transaction by that foreign person would be prohibited by this section if performed by a United States person or within the United States.
