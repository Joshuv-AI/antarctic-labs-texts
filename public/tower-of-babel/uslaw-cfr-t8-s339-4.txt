8 C.F.R. § 339.4 — Binding of naturalization records.
Citation: us:cfr:8:339.4
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-339.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 339 (Functions And Duties Of Clerks Of Court Regarding Naturalization Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever a volume of petitions for naturalization, applications to take the oath of allegiance, declarations of intention, orders of court, or other documents affecting or relating to the naturalization of persons is completed, it shall be bound and locked by the clerk of court.
