40 C.F.R. § 1700.8 — Discharges for which no-discharge zones can be established.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1700.8
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency And Department Of Defense; Uniform National Discharge Standards For Vessels Of The Armed Forces
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1700.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1700 (Uniform National Discharge Standards For Vessels Of The Armed Forces). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A no-discharge zone may be established by State prohibition for any discharge listed in § 1700.4 or § 1700.5 following the procedures in § 1700.9. A no-discharge zone established by a State using these procedures may apply only to those discharges that have been preempted from other State or local regulation pursuant to § 1700.6.
(b) A no-discharge zone may be established by EPA prohibition for any discharge listed in § 1700.4 or § 1700.5 following the procedures in § 1700.10.
