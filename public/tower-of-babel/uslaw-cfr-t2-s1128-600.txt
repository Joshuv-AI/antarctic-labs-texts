2 C.F.R. § 1128.600 — Purpose of FMS Article VI.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1128.600
Creator: Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1128.600
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1128 (Recipient Financial And Program Management: General Award Terms And Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

FMS Article VI sets forth requirements concerning recipients' cost sharing or matching under awards. It thereby implements OMB guidance in:
(a) 2 CFR 200.306 and 200.308(c)(1)(vii); and
(b) 2 CFR 200.434, in conjunction with FMS Article III in appendix C to this part.
