50 C.F.R. § 38.13 — Speed limits.
Citation: us:cfr:50:38.13
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-38.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 38 (Midway Atoll National Wildlife Refuge). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person on Midway Atoll National Wildlife Refuge will exceed the speed limit for automobiles, trucks, bicycles, motorcycles, or other vehicles. Unless otherwise posted, the speed limit throughout the Midway Atoll National Wildlife Refuge is 15 miles per hour.
