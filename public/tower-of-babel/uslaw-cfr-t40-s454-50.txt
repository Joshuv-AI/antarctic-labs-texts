40 C.F.R. § 454.50 — Applicability; description of the essential oils subcategory.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 454 (Gum And Wood Chemicals Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges resulting from the manufacture of essential oils.
