16 U.S.C. § 430mm — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:430mm
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section430mm
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LX (National Military Parks). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For development of the Wilson’s Creek National Battlefield, there are authorized to be appropriated not more than $5,640,000. There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary to carry out section 430kk(b) of this title.
