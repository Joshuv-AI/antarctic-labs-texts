31 C.F.R. § 82.1 — Prohibitions.
Citation: us:cfr:31:82.1
Creator: Monetary Offices, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-82.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 82 (5-Cent And One-Cent Coin Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as specifically authorized by the Secretary of the Treasury (or designee) or as otherwise provided in this part, no person shall export, melt, or treat:
(a) Any 5-cent coin of the United States; or
(b) Any one-cent coin of the United States.
