10 U.S.C. § 9432 — Departments and professors: titles
Citation: us:usc:10:9432
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9432
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training); Chapter 953 (United States Air Force Academy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Air Force may prescribe the titles of each of the departments of instruction and the professors of the Academy. However, the change of the title of a department or officer does not affect the status, rank, or eligibility for promotion or retirement of, or otherwise prejudice, a professor at the Academy.
