26 U.S.C. § 6414 — Income tax withheld
Citation: us:usc:26:6414
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section6414
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 65 (Abatements, Credits, And Refunds); Subchapter B (Rules Of Special Application). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the case of an overpayment of tax imposed by chapter 24, or by chapter 3 or 4, refund or credit shall be made to the employer or to the withholding agent, as the case may be, only to the extent that the amount of such overpayment was not deducted and withheld by the employer or withholding agent.
