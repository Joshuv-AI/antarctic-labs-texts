20 C.F.R. § 654.1 — Purpose of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:20:654.1
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-654.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 654 (Special Responsibilities Of The Employment Service System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart implements the responsibilities of the Secretary of Labor in classifying labor surplus areas in accordance with Executive Order 12073 (Federal Procurement in Labor Surplus Areas). The Secretary of Labor has delegated responsibilities to the Assistant Secretary, Employment and Training Administration.
