26 U.S.C. § 8001 — Authorization
Citation: us:usc:26:8001
Creator: United States Congress

Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section8001
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 91 (Organization And Membership Of The Joint Committee). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There shall be a joint congressional committee known as the Joint Committee on Taxation (hereinafter in this subtitle referred to as the “Joint Committee”).
