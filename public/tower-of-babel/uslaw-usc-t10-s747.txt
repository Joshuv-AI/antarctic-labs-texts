10 U.S.C. § 747 — Command: when different commands of Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Space Force, and Coast Guard join
Citation: us:usc:10:747
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section747
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 43 (Rank And Command). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When different commands of the Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Space Force, and Coast Guard join or serve together, the officer highest in rank in the Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Space Force, or Coast Guard on duty there, who is otherwise eligible to command, commands all those forces unless otherwise directed by the President.
