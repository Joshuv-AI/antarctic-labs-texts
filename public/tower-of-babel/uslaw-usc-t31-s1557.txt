31 U.S.C. § 1557 — Authority for exemptions in appropriation laws
Citation: us:usc:31:1557
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section1557
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 15 (Appropriation Accounting); Subchapter IV (Closing Accounts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A provision of an appropriation law may exempt an appropriation from the provisions of this subchapter and fix the period for which the appropriation remains available for expenditure.
