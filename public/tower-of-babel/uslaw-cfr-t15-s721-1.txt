15 C.F.R. § 721.1 — Inspection of records.
Citation: us:cfr:15:721.1
Creator: Bureau Of Industry And Security, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-721.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 721 (Inspection Of Records And Recordkeeping). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon request by BIS or any other agency of competent jurisdiction, you must permit access to and copying of any record relating to compliance with the requirements of the CWCR. This requires that you make available the equipment and, if necessary, knowledgeable personnel for locating, reading, and reproducing any record.
