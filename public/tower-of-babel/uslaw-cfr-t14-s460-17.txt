14 C.F.R. § 460.17 — Verification program.
Citation: us:cfr:14:460.17
Creator: Commercial Space Transportation, Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-460.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 460 (Human Space Flight Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An operator must successfully verify the integrated performance of a vehicle's hardware and any software in an operational flight environment before allowing any space flight participant on board during a flight. Verification must include flight testing.
