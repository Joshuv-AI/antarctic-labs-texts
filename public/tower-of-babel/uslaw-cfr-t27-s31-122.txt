27 C.F.R. § 31.122 — Incorporation of business.
Citation: us:cfr:27:31.122
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-31.122
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 31 (Alcohol Beverage Dealers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where an individual or a firm engaged in business requiring registration under this part forms a corporation to take over and conduct the business, the corporation, as a separate legal entity, must register in its own name as a new business.
