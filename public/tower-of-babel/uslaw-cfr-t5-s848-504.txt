5 C.F.R. § 848.504 — Non-eligibility for annuity supplement.
Citation: us:cfr:5:848.504
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-848.504
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 848 (Phased Retirement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A phased retiree is not eligible to receive an annuity supplement under 5 U.S.C. 8421.
