49 C.F.R. § 538.1 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:49:538.1
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-538.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 538 (Manufacturing Incentives For Alternative Fuel Vehicles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part establishes minimum driving range criteria to aid in identifying passenger automobiles that are dual-fueled automobiles. It also establishes gallon equivalent measurements for gaseous fuels other than natural gas.
