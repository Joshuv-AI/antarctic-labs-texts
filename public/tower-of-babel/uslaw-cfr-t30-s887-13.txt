30 C.F.R. § 887.13 — Grant period.
Citation: us:cfr:30:887.13
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-887.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 887 (Subsidence Insurance Program Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The grant funding period must not exceed 8 years from the time we approve the grant. You must return any unexpended funds remaining at the end of any grant period to us according to 43 CFR part 12.
