22 C.F.R. § 51.6 — Verification of passports and release of information from passport records.
Citation: us:cfr:22:51.6
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-51.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 51 (Passports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Verification. When required by a foreign government, a consular officer abroad may verify a U.S. passport.
(b) Release of information. Information in passport records is subject to the provisions of the Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) and the Privacy Act. Release of this information may be requested in accordance with part 171 or part 172 of this title.
