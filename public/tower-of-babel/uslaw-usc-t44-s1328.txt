44 U.S.C. § 1328 — Merchant vessels of the United States
Citation: us:usc:44:1328
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section1328
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 13 (Particular Reports And Documents). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Five thousand copies of the annual list of merchant vessels of the United States may be printed for distribution by the Coast Guard.
