32 C.F.R. § 1609.2 — Citizenship.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1609.2
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1609.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1609 (Uncompensated Personnel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person shall be appointed to any uncompensated position in the Selective Service System who is not a citizen of the United States.
