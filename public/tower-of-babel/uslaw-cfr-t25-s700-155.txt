25 C.F.R. § 700.155 — Expenses in searching for replacement dwelling—residential move.
Citation: us:cfr:25:700.155
Creator: The Office Of Navajo And Hopi Indian Relocation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-700.155
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 700 (Commission Operations And Relocation Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A certified eligible head of household is entitled to actual reasonable expenses incurred in the search for a replacement dwelling.
(b) Transportation, meals and lodging when required to be away from home by the Commission, computed at prevailing federal per diem and mileage allowance schedules.
