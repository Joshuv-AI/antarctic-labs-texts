45 C.F.R. § 400.76 — Criteria for exemption from registration for employment services, participation in employability service programs, and acceptance of appropriate offers of employment.
Citation: us:cfr:45:400.76
Creator: Office Of Refugee Resettlement, Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-400.76
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 400 (Refugee Resettlement Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

States and local resettlement agencies operating a public/private RCA program, as well as States operating a publicly-administered RCA program, may determine what specific exemptions, if any, are appropriate for recipients of a time-limited RCA program in their State.
