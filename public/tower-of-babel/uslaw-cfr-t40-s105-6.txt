40 C.F.R. § 105.6 — What are the Awards Program categories for which I may be eligible?
Citation: us:cfr:40:105.6
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-105.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 105 (Recognition Awards Under The Clean Water Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

EPA will publish from time to time, a notice in the Federal Register announcing the current Awards Program categories. EPA also may subsequently discontinue, combine, or rename categories by notice published in the Federal Register.
