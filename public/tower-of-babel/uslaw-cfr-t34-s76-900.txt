34 C.F.R. § 76.900 — Waiver of regulations prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:34:76.900
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-76.900
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 76 (State-Administered Formula Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) No official, agent, or employee of the Department may waive any regulation that applies to a Department program unless the regulation specifically provide that it may be waived.
(b) No act or failure to act by an official, agent, or employee of the Department can affect the authority of the Secretary to enforce regulations.
