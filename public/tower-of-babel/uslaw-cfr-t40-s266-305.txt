40 C.F.R. § 266.305 — What does the transportation and disposal conditional exemption do?
Citation: us:cfr:40:266.305
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-266.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 266 (Standards For The Management Of Specific Hazardous Wastes And Specific Types Of Hazardous Waste Management Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This conditional exemption exempts your waste from the regulatory definition of hazardous waste in 40 CFR 261.3 if your waste meets the eligibility criteria under § 266.310, and you meet the conditions in § 266.315.
