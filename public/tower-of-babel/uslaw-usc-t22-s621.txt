22 U.S.C. § 621 — Reports to Congress
Citation: us:usc:22:621
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section621
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 11 (Foreign Agents And Propaganda); Subchapter II (Registration Of Foreign Propagandists). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General shall every six months report to the Congress concerning administration of this subchapter, including registrations filed pursuant to the subchapter, and the nature, sources and content of political propaganda disseminated and distributed.
