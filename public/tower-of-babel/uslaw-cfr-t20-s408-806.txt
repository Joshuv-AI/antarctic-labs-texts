20 C.F.R. § 408.806 — What happens to your SVB payments if you are no longer residing outside the United States?
Citation: us:cfr:20:408.806
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-408.806
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 408 (Special Benefits For Certain World War Ii Veterans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Suspension effective date. We will suspend your SVB payments effective the first full calendar month you are no longer residing outside the United States.
(b) Resumption of payments. If otherwise payable, we will resume your SVB payments effective with the first full calendar month you are again residing outside the United States.
