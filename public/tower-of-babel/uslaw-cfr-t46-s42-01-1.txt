46 C.F.R. § 42.01-1 — -1 Authority for regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:46:42.01-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-42.01-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 42 (Domestic And Foreign Voyages By Sea). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The statutory authority to prescribe rules and regulations requiring certain vessels to have and display load line marks indicating the maximum amidship draft to which such vessels may be safely loaded and certification thereof by the assigning authority is in 46 U.S.C. 5101-5116.
