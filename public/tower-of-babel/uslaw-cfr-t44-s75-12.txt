44 C.F.R. § 75.12 — Application by a State for exemption.
Citation: us:cfr:44:75.12
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-75.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 75 (Exemption Of State-Owned Properties Under Self-Insurance Plan). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Application for exemption made pursuant to this part shall be made by the Governor or other duly authorized official of the State accompanied by sufficient supporting documentation which certifies that the plan of self-insurance upon which the application for exemption is based meets or exceeds the standards set forth in § 75.11.
