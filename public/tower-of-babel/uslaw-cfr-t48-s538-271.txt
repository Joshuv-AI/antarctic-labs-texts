48 C.F.R. § 538.271 — 538.271 FSS contract awards.
Citation: us:cfr:48:538.271
Creator: Chapter 5—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-538.271
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 538 (Federal Supply Schedule Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) FSS awards will be for commercial products and commercial services.
(b) Before awarding any FSS contract, determine that the offered prices are fair and reasonable (see FAR subpart 15.4 and 538.270). Document the negotiation and your determination using FAR 15.406-3 as guidance.
