33 C.F.R. § 183.570 — Fuel filters and strainers: Installation.
Citation: us:cfr:33:183.570
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-183.570
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 183 (Boats And Associated Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each fuel filter and strainer must be supported on the engine or boat structure independent from its fuel line connections, unless the fuel filter or strainer is inside a fuel tank.
