21 C.F.R. § 1.1375 — What information submitted in a petition requesting modified requirements or an exemption, or information in comments on such a petition, is publicly available?
Citation: us:cfr:21:1.1375
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1.1375
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1 (General Enforcement Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

FDA will presume that information submitted in a petition requesting modified requirements or an exemption, as well as information in comments submitted on such a petition, does not contain information exempt from public disclosure under part 20 of this chapter and will be made public as part of the docket associated with the petition.
