11 U.S.C. § 1525 — Cooperation and direct communication between the court and foreign courts or foreign representatives
Citation: us:usc:11:1525
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section1525
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 15 (Ancillary And Other Cross-Border Cases); Subchapter IV (Cooperation With Foreign Courts And Foreign Representatives). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Consistent with section 1501, the court shall cooperate to the maximum extent possible with a foreign court or a foreign representative, either directly or through the trustee.
(b)
The court is entitled to communicate directly with, or to request information or assistance directly from, a foreign court or a foreign representative, subject to the rights of a party in interest to notice and participation.
