9 C.F.R. § 302.3 — Livestock and products entering official establishments.
Citation: us:cfr:9:302.3
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-302.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 302 (Application Of Inspection And Other Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All livestock and all products entering any official establishment and all products prepared, in whole or in part, therein, shall be inspected, handled, stored, prepared, packaged, marked, and labeled as required by the regulations in this subchapter.
