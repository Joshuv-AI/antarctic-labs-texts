46 U.S.C. § 50503 — Oceanographic research vessels
Citation: us:usc:46:50503
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section50503
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part A (General); Chapter 505 (Other General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An oceanographic research vessel (as defined in section 2101 of this title) is deemed not to be engaged in trade or commerce.
