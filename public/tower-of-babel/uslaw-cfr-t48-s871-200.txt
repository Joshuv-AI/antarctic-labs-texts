48 C.F.R. § 871.200 — 871.200 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:48:871.200
Creator: Chapter 8—Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-871.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 871 (Vocational Rehabilitation And Employment Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart establishes policy and procedures for the vocational rehabilitation and employment services as it pertains to the following:
(a) Contracts for training and rehabilitation services.
(b) Approval of institutions (including rehabilitation facilities), training establishments, and employers under 38 U.S.C. chapter 31.
(c) Contracts for counseling services under 38 U.S.C. chapters 30, 31, 32, 35, and 36.
