16 U.S.C. § 825s–3 — Southwestern area sale at uniform systemwide rates of electric power over transmission lines constructed with appropriated funds or used under contractual arrangements
Citation: us:usc:16:825s–3
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section825s–3
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 12 (Federal Regulation And Development Of Power); Subchapter III (Licensees And Public Utilities; Procedural And Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Power and energy marketed by the Southwestern Power Administration pursuant to section 825s of this title, shall be sold at uniform systemwide rates, without discrimination between customers to whom the Southwestern Power Administration delivers such power and energy by means of transmission lines or facilities constructed with appropriated funds, and customers to whom the Southwestern Power Administration delivers such power and energy by means of transmission lines or facilities, the use of which is acquired by lease, wheeling, or other contractual arrangements.
