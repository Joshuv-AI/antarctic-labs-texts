18 U.S.C. § 2112 — Personal property of United States
Citation: us:usc:18:2112
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section2112
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 103 (Robbery And Burglary). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever robs or attempts to rob another of any kind or description of personal property belonging to the United States, shall be imprisoned not more than fifteen years.
