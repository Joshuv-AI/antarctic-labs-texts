49 C.F.R. § 180.602 — Alternative qualification and maintenance.
Citation: us:cfr:49:180.602
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-180.602
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 180 (Continuing Qualification And Maintenance Of Packagings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-28 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notwithstanding the applicability of § 180.601 for the continuing qualification, maintenance, or periodic testing of portable tanks, and subject to conditions and limitations set forth in § 173.252 of this subchapter, the NBIC (IBR, see § 171.7 of this subchapter), must be used for the continuing qualification, maintenance, and periodic testing (i.e., continued service) of UN T75 cryogenic portable tanks constructed to ASME Code Section XII in accordance with § 178.278 of this subchapter.
