31 C.F.R. § 353.50 — Change of name.
Citation: us:cfr:31:353.50
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-353.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 353 (Regulations Governing Definitive United States Savings Bonds, Series Ee And Hh). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An owner, coowner, or beneficiary whose name is changed by marriage, divorce, annulment, order of court, or in any other legal manner after the issue of the bond should submit the bond with a request for reissue to substitute the new name for the name inscribed on the bond. Documentary evidence may be required in any appropriate case.
