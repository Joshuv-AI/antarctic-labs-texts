43 C.F.R. § 2569.402 — Do I need to fill out a special application form?
Citation: us:cfr:43:2569.402
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2569.402
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2560 (Alaska Occupancy And Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. You must complete and sign the BLM Form No. AK-2569-1004-0216, “Alaska Native Vietnam-Era Veteran Land Allotment Application.”
