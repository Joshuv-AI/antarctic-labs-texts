33 U.S.C. § 607 — Removal of drift from New York Harbor
Citation: us:usc:33:607
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section607
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 12 (River And Harbor Improvements Generally); Subchapter IV (Particular Work Or Improvements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

So much as may be necessary of any appropriations made for specific portions of New York Harbor and its immediate tributaries may be allotted by the Secretary of the Army for the maintenance of these waterways by the collection and removal of drift.
