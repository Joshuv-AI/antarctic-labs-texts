26 U.S.C. § 1243 — Loss of small business investment company
Citation: us:usc:26:1243
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section1243
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 1 (Normal Taxes And Surtaxes); Subchapter P (Capital Gains And Losses); Part IV (Special Rules For Determining Capital Gains And Losses). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the case of a small business investment company operating under the Small Business Investment Act of 1958, if—
(1)
a loss is on stock received pursuant to the conversion privilege of convertible debentures acquired pursuant to section 304 of the Small Business Investment Act of 1958, and
(2)
such loss would (but for this section) be a loss from the sale or exchange of a capital asset,
then such loss shall be treated as an ordinary loss.
