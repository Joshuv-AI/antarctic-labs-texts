24 C.F.R. § 200.11 — Audit requirements for State and local governments as mortgagees.
Citation: us:cfr:24:200.11
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-200.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 200 (Introduction To Fha Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Requirements set forth in 2 CFR part 200, subpart F, apply to State and local governments (as defined at 2 CFR 200.90 and 200.64, respectively) that receive mortgage insurance as mortgagees.
