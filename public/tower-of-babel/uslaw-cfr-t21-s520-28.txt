21 C.F.R. § 520.28 — Acetazolamide.
Citation: us:cfr:21:520.28
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-520.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 520 (Oral Dosage Form New Animal Drugs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Specifications. A powder containing acetazolamide sodium, USP equivalent to 25 percent acetazolamide activity.
(b) Sponsor. See No. 054771 in § 510.600(c) of this chapter.
(c) Conditions of use in dogs—(1) Amount. Administer orally at a dosage of 5 to 15 milligrams per pound of body weight daily.
(2) Indications for use. As an aid in the treatment of mild congestive heart failure and for rapid reduction of intraocular pressure.
(3) Limitations. Federal law restricts this drug to use by or on the order of a licensed veterinarian.
