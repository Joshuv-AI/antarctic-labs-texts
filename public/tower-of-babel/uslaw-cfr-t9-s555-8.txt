9 C.F.R. § 555.8 — Loading or unloading of fish products in sealed transport conveyances.
Citation: us:cfr:9:555.8
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-555.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 555 (Transportation Of Fish Products In Commerce). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirements in 9 CFR 325.17 governing the unloading of any meat or meat food product from an officially sealed railroad car, truck, or other means of conveyance containing any unmarked product or loading any means of conveyance after the product leaves an official establishment are applicable to fish and fish products.
