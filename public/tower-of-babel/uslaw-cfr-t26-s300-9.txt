26 C.F.R. § 300.9 — Renewal of enrollment of enrolled retirement plan agent fee.
Citation: us:cfr:26:300.9
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-300.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 300 (User Fees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Applicability. This section applies to the renewal of enrollment of enrolled retirement plan agents with the IRS pursuant to 31 CFR 10.5(b).
(b) Fee. The fee for renewal of enrollment as an enrolled retirement plan agent with the IRS is $140.
(c) Person liable for the fee. The person liable for the renewal of enrollment fee is the person renewing enrollment as an enrolled retirement plan agent with the IRS.
(d) Applicability date. This section is applicable beginning October 31, 2022.
