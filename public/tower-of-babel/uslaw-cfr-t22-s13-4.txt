22 C.F.R. § 13.4 — False certificate as to ownership of property.
Citation: us:cfr:22:13.4
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-13.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 13 (Personnel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If any consul of vice consul falsely and knowingly certifies that property belonging to foreigners is property belonging to citizens of the United States, he or she shall be punishable by imprisonment for not more than three years, and by a fine of not more than $10,000 (22 U.S.C. 4218).
