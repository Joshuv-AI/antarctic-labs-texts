31 C.F.R. § 801.207 — Investment fund.
Citation: us:cfr:31:801.207
Creator: Office Of Investment Security, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-801.207
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 801 (Pilot Program To Review Certain Transactions Involving Foreign Persons And Critical Technologies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term investment fund means any entity that is an “investment company,” as defined in section 3(a) of the Investment Company Act of 1940 (15 U.S.C. 80a-1 et seq.), or would be an “investment company” but for one or more of the exemptions provided in section 3(b) or 3(c) thereunder.
