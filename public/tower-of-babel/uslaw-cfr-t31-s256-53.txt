31 C.F.R. § 256.53 — How does the submitting agency know when payment is made?
Citation: us:cfr:31:256.53
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-256.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 256 (Obtaining Payments From The Judgment Fund And Under Private Relief Bills). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Fiscal Service will e-mail the agency contact when payment is disbursed, if the agency contact has provided an email address on the appropriate Judgment Fund form. Also, Fiscal Service maintains an on-line payment status system that the submitting agency can access to determine the status of a payment. The payment reporting system can be accessed from the Judgment Fund Web site at http://www.fiscal.treasury.gov/judgefund.
