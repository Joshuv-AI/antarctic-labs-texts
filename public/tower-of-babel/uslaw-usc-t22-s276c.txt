22 U.S.C. § 276c — Designation of Senate delegates to Conferences of the Interparliamentary Union
Citation: us:usc:22:276c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section276c
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On and after June 30, 1958, Senate delegates to Conferences of the Interparliamentary Union shall be designated by the Presiding Officer of the Senate. Not less than two Senators so designated shall be members of the Committee on Foreign Relations.
