9 C.F.R. § 72.1 — Interstate movement of infested or exposed animals prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:9:72.1
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-72.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 72 (Bovine Babesiosis). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No animals infested with ticks [Boophilus annulatus or Rhipicephalus (Boophilus) annulatus, Margaropus annulatus, Boophilus microplus or Rhipicephalus (Boophilus) microplus, or Babesiasis (Babesiosis) species vectors of Margaropus, Rhipicephalus, Amblyomma, Demacentor, or Ixodes] or exposed to tick infestation may be moved interstate, except as provided in this part.
