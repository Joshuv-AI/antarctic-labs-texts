39 C.F.R. § 3025.102 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3025.102
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3025.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3025 (Procedures Related To Commission Views Submitted To The Secretary Of State). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules in this part are intended to facilitate public participation in, and promote the transparency of, the development of Commission views.
