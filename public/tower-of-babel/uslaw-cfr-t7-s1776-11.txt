7 C.F.R. § 1776.11 — Revolving loan fund.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1776.11
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1776.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1776 (Rural Decentralized Water Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The grant recipient shall establish and maintain a revolving loan fund for the purposes set forth in § 1776.12. All loans made to loan recipients shall be drawn from the revolving loan fund. The loans shall be serviced, and the revolving loan fund shall be maintained, as set forth in § 1776.17.
