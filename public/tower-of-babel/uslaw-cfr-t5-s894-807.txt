5 C.F.R. § 894.807 — Can an active duty member enroll or be covered under a FEDVIP vision plan?
Citation: us:cfr:5:894.807
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-894.807
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 894 (Federal Employees Dental And Vision Insurance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No, a uniformed services member on active duty is not a TEI-V and may not enroll or be covered under a FEDVIP vision plan. However, an active duty member is a sponsor, therefore their TEI family members may be eligible to enroll in a vision plan. See definition of TEI for FEDVIP vision benefits (TEI-V) in § 894.101.
