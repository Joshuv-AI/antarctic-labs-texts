43 C.F.R. § 8365.2-5 — -5 Public health, safety and comfort.
Citation: us:cfr:43:8365.2-5
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-8365.2-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 8360 (Visitor Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

On developed recreation sites and areas, unless otherwise authorized, no person shall:
(a) Discharge or use firearms, other weapons, or fireworks; or
(b) Bring an animal, except a Seeing Eye or Hearing Ear dog, to a swimming area.
