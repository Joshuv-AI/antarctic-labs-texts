33 C.F.R. § 157.106 — Letter of acceptance.
Citation: us:cfr:33:157.106
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-157.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 157 (Rules For The Protection Of The Marine Environment Relating To Tank Vessels Carrying Oil In Bulk). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Coast Guard informs the submitter by letter that the plans submitted under § 157.100 or § 157.102 are accepted if:
(a) The plans submitted show that the COW system meets this subpart; or
(b) The plans submitted and the scale model under § 157.104 show that the COW system meets this subpart.
