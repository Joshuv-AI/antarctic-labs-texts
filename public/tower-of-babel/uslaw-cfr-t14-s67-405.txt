14 C.F.R. § 67.405 — Medical examinations: Who may perform?
Citation: us:cfr:14:67.405
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-67.405
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 67 (Medical Standards And Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) First-class. Any aviation medical examiner who is specifically designated for the purpose may perform examinations for the first-class medical certificate.
(b) Second- and third-class. Any aviation medical examiner may perform examinations for the second-or third-class medical certificate.
