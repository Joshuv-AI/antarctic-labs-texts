18 C.F.R. § 270.203 — Determinations by jurisdictional agencies.
Citation: us:cfr:18:270.203
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-270.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 270 (Determination Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A jurisdictional agency must make determinations to which this part applies in accordance with procedures applicable to it under the law of its jurisdiction for making such determinations or for making comparable determinations.
