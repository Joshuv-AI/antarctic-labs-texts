29 C.F.R. § 501.31 — Written notice of determination required.
Citation: us:cfr:29:501.31
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-501.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 501 (Enforcement Of Contractual Obligations For Temporary Agricultural Workers Admitted Under Section 218 Of The Immigration And Nationality Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever the WHD Administrator decides to assess a civil money penalty, debar, increase a surety bond, or proceed administratively to enforce contractual obligations, or obligations under 8 U.S.C. 1188, 20 CFR part 655, subpart B, or this part, including for the recovery of the monetary relief, the person against whom such action is taken shall be notified in writing of such determination.
