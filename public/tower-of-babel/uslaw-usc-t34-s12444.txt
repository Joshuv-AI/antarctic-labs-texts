34 U.S.C. § 12444 — Grant conditions
Citation: us:usc:34:12444
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section12444
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 121 (Violent Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Subchapter III (Violence Against Women); Part I (Privacy Protections For Victims Of Domestic Violence, Dating Violence, Sexual Violence, And Stalking). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Applicants described in paragraph (1) and paragraphs (3) through (6) shall demonstrate that they have entered into a significant partnership with a State, tribal, territorial, or local victim service or advocacy organization or condition in order to develop safe, confidential, and effective protocols, procedures, policies, and systems for protecting personally identifying information of victims.
