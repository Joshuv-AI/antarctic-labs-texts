21 C.F.R. § 830.210 — Eligibility for use of FDA as an issuing agency.
Citation: us:cfr:21:830.210
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-830.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 830 (Unique Device Identification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When FDA acts as an issuing agency, any labeler will be permitted to use FDA's unique device identification system, regardless of whether the labeler is considered a small business.
