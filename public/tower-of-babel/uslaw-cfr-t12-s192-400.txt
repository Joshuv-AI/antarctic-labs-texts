12 C.F.R. § 192.400 — Time period for completion of sale of stock.
Citation: us:cfr:12:192.400
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-192.400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 192 (Conversions From Mutual To Stock Form). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A savings association must complete all sales of its stock within 45 calendar days after the last day of the subscription period, unless the offering is extended under § 192.405.
