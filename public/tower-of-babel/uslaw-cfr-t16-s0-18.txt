16 C.F.R. § 0.18 — Bureau of Economics.
Citation: us:cfr:16:0.18
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-0.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 0 (Organization). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Bureau aids and advises the Commission concerning the economic aspects of all of its functions, and is responsible for the preparation of various economic reports and surveys. The Bureau provides economic and statistical assistance to the enforcement Bureaus in the investigation and trial of cases.
