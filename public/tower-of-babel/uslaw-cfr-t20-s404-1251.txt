20 C.F.R. § 404.1251 — Final reports—for wages paid prior to 1987.
Citation: us:cfr:20:404.1251
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-404.1251
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 404 (Federal Old-Age, Survivors And Disability Insurance (1950- )). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a political subdivision is legally dissolved, the State shall file a final report on that entity. The report shall include each coverage group whose existence ceases with that of the entity. It shall:
(a) Be marked “final report”;
(b) Cover the period during which final payment of wages subject to the agreement is made; and
(c) Indicate the last date wages were paid.
