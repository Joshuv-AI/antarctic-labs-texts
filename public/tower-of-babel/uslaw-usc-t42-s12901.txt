42 U.S.C. § 12901 — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:42:12901
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section12901
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 131 (Housing Opportunities For Persons With Aids). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of this chapter 1 is to provide States and localities with the resources and incentives to devise long-term comprehensive strategies for meeting the housing needs of persons with acquired immunodeficiency syndrome and families of such persons.
