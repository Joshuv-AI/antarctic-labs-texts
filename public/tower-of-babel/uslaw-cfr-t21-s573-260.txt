21 C.F.R. § 573.260 — Calcium silicate.
Citation: us:cfr:21:573.260
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-573.260
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 573 (Food Additives Permitted In Feed And Drinking Water Of Animals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Calcium silicate, including synthetic calcium silicate, may be safely used as an anticaking agent in animal feed, provided that the amount of calcium silicate does not exceed 2 percent.
