48 C.F.R. § 3412.203 — 3412.203 Procedures for solicitation, evaluation, and award.
Citation: us:cfr:48:3412.203
Creator: Chapter 34—Department Of Education Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-3412.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 3412 (Acquisition Of Commercial Products And Commercial Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As specified in 3413.003, simplified acquisition procedures for commercial products and commercial services may be used without regard to any dollar or timeframe limitations described in FAR subpart 13.5 when acquired by the FSA and used for its purposes.
