7 C.F.R. § 22.306 — Financing rural development planning.
Citation: us:cfr:7:22.306
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-22.306
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 22 (Rural Development Coordination). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

States will be required to finance rural development planning through their own resources, revenue-sharing allocations, or the Department of Housing and Urban Development planning and management assistance program or other available Federal planning programs.
