42 U.S.C. § 1395j — Establishment of supplementary medical insurance program for aged and disabled
Citation: us:usc:42:1395j
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1395j
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 7 (Social Security); Subchapter XVIII (Health Insurance For Aged And Disabled); Part B (Supplementary Medical Insurance Benefits For Aged And Disabled). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is hereby established a voluntary insurance program to provide medical insurance benefits in accordance with the provisions of this part for aged and disabled individuals who elect to enroll under such program, to be financed from premium payments by enrollees together with contributions from funds appropriated by the Federal Government.
