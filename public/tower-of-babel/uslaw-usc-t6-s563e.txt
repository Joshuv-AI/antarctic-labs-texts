6 U.S.C. § 563e — Consistency with the Federal Acquisition Regulation and departmental policies and directives
Citation: us:usc:6:563e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section563e
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 1 (Homeland Security Organization); Subchapter XII–A (Transportation Security); Part B (Transportation Security Administration Acquisition Improvements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator shall execute the responsibilities set forth in this part in a manner consistent with, and not duplicative of, the Federal Acquisition Regulation and the Department’s policies and directives.
