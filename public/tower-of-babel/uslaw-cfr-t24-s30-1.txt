24 C.F.R. § 30.1 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:24:30.1
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-30.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 30 (Civil Money Penalties: Certain Prohibited Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless provided for elsewhere in this title or under separate authority, this part implements HUD's civil money penalty provisions. The procedural rules for hearings under this part are those applicable to hearings in accordance with the Administrative Procedure Act, as set forth in 24 CFR part 26.
