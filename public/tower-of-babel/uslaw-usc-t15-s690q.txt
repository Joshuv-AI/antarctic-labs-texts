15 U.S.C. § 690q — Termination
Citation: us:usc:15:690q
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section690q
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 14B (Small Business Investment Program); Subchapter III (Investment Division Programs); Part C (Renewable Fuel Capital Investment Pilot Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The program under this part shall terminate at the end of the second full fiscal year after the date that the Administrator establishes the program under this part.
