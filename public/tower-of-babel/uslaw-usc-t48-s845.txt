48 U.S.C. § 845 — Income tax laws; modification or repeal by legislature
Citation: us:usc:48:845
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section845
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 4 (Puerto Rico); Subchapter III (The Legislature). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Puerto Rican Legislature shall have power by due enactment to amend, alter, modify, or repeal the income tax laws in force in Puerto Rico.
