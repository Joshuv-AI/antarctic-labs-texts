33 C.F.R. § 67.10-30 — -30 Withdrawal of approval.
Citation: us:cfr:33:67.10-30
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-67.10-30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 67 (Aids To Navigation On Artificial Islands And Fixed Structures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Coast Guard may withdraw approval of a sound signal if it fails to meet the requirements of § 67.10-1 (a), (b), and (c).
