45 C.F.R. § 900.107 — Role of lead and cooperating agencies.
Citation: us:cfr:45:900.107
Creator: Denali Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-900.107
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 900 (National Environmental Policy Act Implementing Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In accordance with § 900.104, the Commission may defer the lead agency role to other federal agencies in accordance with 40 CFR 1501.5, and the Commission will then exercise its role as either a joint lead or a cooperating agency in accordance with 40 CFR 1501.6.
