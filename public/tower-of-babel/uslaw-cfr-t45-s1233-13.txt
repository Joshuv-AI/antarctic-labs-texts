45 C.F.R. § 1233.13 — May the Director waive any provision of these regulations?
Citation: us:cfr:45:1233.13
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1233.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1233 (Intergovernmental Review Of Action Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In an emergency, the Director may waive any provision of these regulations.
