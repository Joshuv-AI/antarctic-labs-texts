15 C.F.R. § 1180.5 — Timeliness.
Citation: us:cfr:15:1180.5
Creator: National Technical Information Service, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-1180.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 1180 (Transfer By Federal Agencies Of Scientific, Technical And Engineering Information To The National Technical Information Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A single copy of a final product or summary described in § 1180.3 must be transferred to NTIS within fifteen days of the date it is first made available for public dissemination through any distribution channel, and, whenever practical, as soon as it has been approved by the agency for final printing or other reproduction, unless the agency and the Director have otherwise agreed.
