16 U.S.C. § 6107 — Report to Congress
Citation: us:usc:16:6107
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section6107
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 80 (Neotropical Migratory Bird Conservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 2 years after October 17, 2006, the Secretary shall submit to Congress a report on the results and effectiveness of the program carried out under this chapter, including recommendations concerning how the chapter might be improved and whether the program should be continued.
