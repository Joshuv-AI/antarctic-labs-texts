41 C.F.R. § 302-14.1 — -14.1 Purpose of a home marketing incentive payment when offering a “homesale program”.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-14.1
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-14.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-14 (14—Home Marketing Incentive Payments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A “homesale program” is a program offered by an agency through a contractual arrangement with a relocation services company. The relocation services company purchases a transferred employee's residence at fair market (appraised) value and then independently markets and sells the residence.
