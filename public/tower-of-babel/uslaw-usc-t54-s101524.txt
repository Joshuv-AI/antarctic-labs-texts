54 U.S.C. § 101524 — Special rule for service contract to provide transportation services
Citation: us:usc:54:101524
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section101524
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 1015 (Transportation); Subchapter III (Public Transportation Programs For System Units). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, a service contract entered into by the Secretary for the provision solely of transportation services in a System unit shall be not more than 10 years in length, including a base period of 5 years and annual extensions for up to an additional 5 years based on satisfactory performance and approval by the Secretary.
