33 U.S.C. § 451a — Harbors subject to this subchapter
Citation: us:usc:33:451a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section451a
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 9 (Protection Of Navigable Waters And Of Harbor And River Improvements Generally); Subchapter III (New York Harbor, Harbor Of Hampton Roads, And Harbor Of Baltimore). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The following harbors shall be subject to this subchapter:
(1)
The harbor of New York.
(2)
The harbor of Hampton Roads.
(3)
The harbor of Baltimore.
