34 U.S.C. § 10704 — Equitable distribution of funds
Citation: us:usc:34:10704
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section10704
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 101 (Justice System Improvement); Subchapter XXXVIII (Comprehensive Opioid Abuse Grant Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In awarding grants under this subchapter, the Attorney General shall distribute funds in a manner that—
(1)
equitably addresses the needs of underserved populations, including rural and tribal communities; and
(2)
focuses on communities that have been disproportionately impacted by opioid abuse as evidenced in part by—
(A)
high rates of primary treatment admissions for heroin and other opioids;
(B)
high rates of drug poisoning deaths from heroin and other opioids; and
(C)
a lack of accessibility to treatment providers and facilities and to emergency medical services.
