12 C.F.R. § 238.71 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:12:238.71
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-238.71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 238 (Savings And Loan Holding Companies (Regulation Ll)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart implements 12 U.S.C. 1831i, which requires certain savings and loan holding companies to notify the Board before appointing or employing directors and senior executive officers.
