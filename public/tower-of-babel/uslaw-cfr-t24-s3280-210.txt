24 C.F.R. § 3280.210 — Fire testing.
Citation: us:cfr:24:3280.210
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-3280.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 3280 (Manufactured Home Construction And Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All fire testing conducted in accordance with this subpart shall be performed by nationally recognized testing laboratories which have expertise in fire technology. In case of dispute, the Secretary shall determine if a particular agency is qualified to perform such fire tests.
