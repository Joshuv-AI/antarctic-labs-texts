43 C.F.R. § 4.760 — How can a final decision be challenged?
Citation: us:cfr:43:4.760
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4.760
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4 (Department Of The Interior Hearings And Appeals Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A party in interest adversely affected by a final decision, or the Project Director, may file either a petition for reconsideration with the presiding officer or an appeal with the Board under § 4.783, but not both.
