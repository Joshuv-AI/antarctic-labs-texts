19 U.S.C. § 3736 — Donation of air traffic control equipment to eligible sub-Saharan African countries
Citation: us:usc:19:3736
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section3736
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 23 (Extension Of Certain Trade Benefits To Sub-Saharan Africa); Subchapter III (Economic Development Related Issues). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the sense of the Congress that, to the extent appropriate, the United States Government should make every effort to donate to governments of sub-Saharan African countries determined to be eligible under section 3703 of this title air traffic control equipment that is no longer in use, including appropriate related reimbursable technical assistance.
