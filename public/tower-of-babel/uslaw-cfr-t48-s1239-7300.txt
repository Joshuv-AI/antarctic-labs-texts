48 C.F.R. § 1239.7300 — 1239.7300 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1239.7300
Creator: Chapter 12—Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1239.7300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1239 (Acquisition Of Information Technology). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes policies and procedures for incorporating technology modernization, upgrades, and refreshment into acquisitions involving information technology products or services supporting the development of applications, information systems, or system software.
