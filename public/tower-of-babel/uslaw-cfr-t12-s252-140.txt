12 C.F.R. § 252.140 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:12:252.140
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-252.140
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 252 (Enhanced Prudential Standards (Regulation Yy)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to foreign banking organizations with average total consolidated assets of $100 billion or more, but average combined U.S. assets of less than $100 billion.
