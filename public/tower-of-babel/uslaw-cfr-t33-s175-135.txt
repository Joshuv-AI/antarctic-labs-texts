33 C.F.R. § 175.135 — Existing equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:33:175.135
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-175.135
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 175 (Equipment Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Launchers manufactured before 1 January, 1981, which do not have approval numbers are acceptable for use with meteor or parachute signals listed in Table 175.130 under § 175.130 as long as they remain in serviceable condition.
