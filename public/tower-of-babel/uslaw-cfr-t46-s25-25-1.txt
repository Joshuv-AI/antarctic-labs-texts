46 C.F.R. § 25.25-1 — -1 Application.
Citation: us:cfr:46:25.25-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-25.25-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 25 (Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to each vessel to which this part applies, except:
(a) Vessels used for noncommercial use; and
(b) Vessels leased, rented, or chartered to another for the latter's noncommercial use.
