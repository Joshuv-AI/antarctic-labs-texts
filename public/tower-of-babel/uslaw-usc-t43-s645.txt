43 U.S.C. § 645 — Additional arid lands available to Colorado, Idaho, Nevada, and Wyoming for reclamation
Citation: us:usc:43:645
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section645
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 14 (Grants Of Desert Lands To States For Reclamation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An additional one million acres of arid lands within each of the States of Colorado, Idaho, Nevada, and Wyoming is made available and subject to the terms of section 641 of this title, and the States of Colorado, Nevada, Idaho, and Wyoming are allowed under the provisions of said section said additional area or so much thereof as may be necessary for the purposes and under the provisions of said section.
