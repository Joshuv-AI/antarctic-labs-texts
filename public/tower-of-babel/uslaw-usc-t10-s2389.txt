10 U.S.C. § 2389 — Ensuring safety regarding insensitive munitions
Citation: us:usc:10:2389
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2389
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Property); Chapter 141 (Miscellaneous Provisions Relating To Property). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Defense shall ensure, to the extent practicable, that insensitive munitions under development or procurement are safe throughout development and fielding when subject to unplanned stimuli.
