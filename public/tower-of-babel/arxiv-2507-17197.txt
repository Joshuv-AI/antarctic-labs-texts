Asymptotic stability of the 2 ​ D 2D temperature-dependent tropical climate model with the sharp decay rates
Hyunjin In, Dong-ha Kim, Junha Kim

Abstract
. We investigate the asymptotic stability of a tropical climate model posed on ℝ 2 \mathbb{R}^{2} , with temperature-dependent diffusion in the barotropic mode u u and linear damping in the first baroclinic mode v v . We consider two distinct cases for the barotropic component: one with linear damping and one without. For both cases, we prove the small data global existence of smooth solutions. Furthermore, we establish sharp temporal decay estimates for solutions in arbitrary Sobolev norms H m ​ ( ℝ 2 ) H^{m}(\mathbb{R}^{2}) , m ≥ 0 m\geq 0 .

Frierson–Majda–Pauluis

(1.1)

\begin{cases}u_{t}+(u\cdot\nabla)u+\nabla p=-\operatorname{div}(v\otimes v),\\

Here,

Since the system (

Physically, the atmospheric dynamics in the Earth’s tropics are largely characterized by the first baroclinic mode, where winds in the lower and upper troposphere are of equal magnitude but opposite sign. While this mode captures the primary vertical structure, a comprehensive study of phenomena like tropical-extratropical interactions requires accounting for the momentum exchange between the baroclinic component and the barotropic mode (the vertical average). Therefore, retaining both the barotropic and baroclinic modes is essential for a physically complete model (see
[
7

(Gagliardo–Nirenberg)

satisfying the following:

(a)

(Undamped case) In case of
\alpha=0
, if there hold
0<\epsilon<\varepsilon^{*}
and

(3.1)

\|u_{0}\|_{H^{s}}+\|v_{0}\|_{H^{s}}+\|\theta_{0}\|_{H^{s}}<\epsilon,

then the Cauchy problem (
1.2
) has a unique global solution
(u,v,\theta)\in\mathcal{C}([0,\infty),H^{s}(\mathbb{R}^{2}))
satisfying

(3.2)

\sup_{t\in[0,\infty)}\left(\|u(t)\|_{H^{s}}+\|v(t)\|_{H^{s}}+\|\theta(t)\|_{H^{s}}\right)+\lambda\left(\int_{0}^{\infty}\|\nabla u(t)\|_{H^{s}}^{2}+\|v(t)\|_{H^{s}}^{2}+\|\nabla\theta(t)\|_{H^{s-1}}^{2}\,\textnormal{d}t\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}<2\epsilon.

(b)

(Damped case) In case of
\alpha>0
, if there hold
0<\epsilon<\varepsilon^{*}
and

(3.3)

\|u_{0}\|_{\dot{H}^{s}\cap\dot{H}^{1}}+\|v_{0}\|_{H^{s}}+\|\theta_{0}\|_{H^{s}}<\epsilon,

then the Cauchy problem (
1.2
) has a unique global solution
(u,v,\theta)\in\mathcal{C}([0,\infty),H^{s}(\mathbb{R}^{2}))
satisfying

(3.4)

\sup_{t\in[0,\infty)}\left(\|u(t)\|_{\dot{H}^{s}\cap\dot{H}^{1}}+\|v(t)\|_{H^{s}}+\|\theta(t)\|_{H^{s}}\right)+\lambda\left(\int_{0}^{\infty}\|\nabla u(t)\|_{H^{s}}^{2}+\|v(t)\|_{H^{s}}^{2}+\|\nabla\theta(t)\|_{H^{s-1}}^{2}\,\textnormal{d}t\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}<2\epsilon.

Here,
\lambda
is as defined in (
1.3
).
Let us further assume that
s\in(1,2)
. Then, in both cases, there exists a positive constant
C>0
, depending on
s,\alpha,\beta,\underline{\mu},\|\mu^{\prime}\|_{C^{s-1}}
, and
\|(u_{0},v_{0},\theta_{0})\|_{H^{s}}
such that

(3.5)

\lVert\Lambda^{s-1}u(t)\rVert_{L^{2}}+\lVert\Lambda^{s-1}v(t)\rVert_{L^{2}}+\lVert\Lambda^{s-1}\theta(t)\rVert_{L^{2}}\leq C(1+t)^{-\frac{s-1}{2}},\qquad\text{for all }t>0.

These decay rates (
3.5
) are not sharp and can be improved (see (
1.6
) and (
1.9
)).

The proof of this proposition will be given in subsection
3.3
.

3.1.
An a priori estimate for stability

The goal of this section is to state and prove the Lemma
3.2
, which concerns an a priori estimate. This will be used to establish Proposition
3.1
.

We define a number
{\delta_{1}}
by

(3.6)

{\delta_{1}}=\begin{cases}0&\text{if }\alpha=0,\\
1&\text{if }\alpha>0.\end{cases}

We introduce the number
{\delta_{1}}
to describe the main stability results all at once, regardless of whether the damping parameter
\alpha\geq 0
vanishes or not.

We multiply
\Lambda^{2s}u
,
\Lambda^{2s}v
, and
\Lambda^{2s}\theta
on the both sides of
\eqref{E11}_{1}
-
\eqref{E11}_{3}
respectively and integrate over
\mathbb{R}^{2}
. Then, we do similar process with
\Lambda^{2\delta_{1}}u
,
v
, and
\theta
on
\eqref{E11}_{1}
-
\eqref{E11}_{3}
. By summing these two results and using
\underline{\mu}\leq\mu
, we have

(3.7)

\begin{split}&\frac{1}{2}\frac{\textnormal{d}}{\textnormal{d}t}\left(\|\Lambda^{s}u\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\|\Lambda^{s}v\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\|\Lambda^{s}\theta\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\|\Lambda^{\delta_{1}}u\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\|v\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\|\theta\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\right)\\
&\quad+\left(\alpha\|\Lambda^{s}u\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\underline{\mu}\|\Lambda^{s}\nabla u\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\beta||\Lambda^{s}v\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\alpha\|\Lambda^{\delta_{1}}u\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\underline{\mu}\|\Lambda^{\delta_{1}}\nabla u\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\beta\|v\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\right)\\
&\leq-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{s}((u\cdot\nabla)u)\cdot\Lambda^{s}u+\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{s}(v\otimes v):\Lambda^{s}\nabla u-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{s}((u\cdot\nabla)v)\cdot\Lambda^{s}v-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{s}((v\cdot\nabla)u)\cdot\Lambda^{s}v\\
&\quad-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{\delta_{1}}((u\cdot\nabla)u)\cdot\Lambda^{\delta_{1}}u+\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{\delta_{1}}(v\otimes v):\Lambda^{\delta_{1}}\nabla u-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}(v\cdot\nabla)u\cdot v-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{s}(u\cdot\nabla\theta)\Lambda^{s}\theta\\
&\quad-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\left(\Lambda^{s}(\mu(\theta)\nabla u)-\mu(\theta)\Lambda^{s}\nabla u\right):\Lambda^{s}\nabla u-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\left(\Lambda^{\delta_{1}}(\mu(\theta)\nabla u)-\mu(\theta)\Lambda^{\delta_{1}}\nabla u\right):\Lambda^{\delta_{1}}\nabla u.\end{split}

One difficulty in obtaining the stability result is the terms containing
\|\Lambda^{s}\theta\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{2})}
.
To control these, we consider the
cross term
with respect to
v,\theta
as follows.

We multiply
\Lambda^{2(s-1)}\nabla\theta
on the second equation of (
1.2
) and
\Lambda^{2(s-1)}\operatorname{div}v
on the third equation of (
1.2
). Then, we integrate over
\mathbb{R}^{2}
to obtain

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}v_{t}\Lambda^{2(s-1)}\nabla\theta+\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}(u\cdot\nabla)v\cdot\Lambda^{2(s-1)}\nabla\theta+\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}(v\cdot\nabla)u\cdot\Lambda^{2(s-1)}\nabla\theta+\beta\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}v\cdot\Lambda^{2(s-1)}\nabla\theta=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}|\Lambda^{s}\theta|^{2},

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{2(s-1)}\theta_{t}\operatorname{div}v+\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{2(s-1)}(u\cdot\nabla\theta)\operatorname{div}v=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}|\Lambda^{s-1}\operatorname{div}v|^{2}.

By subtracting these two equations, we deduce that

(3.8)

\begin{split}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}|\Lambda^{s}\theta|^{2}&-\beta\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{s-1}v\cdot\Lambda^{s-1}\nabla\theta-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}|\Lambda^{s-1}\operatorname{div}v|^{2}-\frac{\textnormal{d}}{\textnormal{d}t}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{s-1}v\cdot\Lambda^{s-1}\nabla\theta\\
&=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{s-1}(u\cdot\nabla)v\cdot\Lambda^{s-1}\nabla\theta+\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{s-1}(v\cdot\nabla)u\cdot\Lambda^{s-1}\nabla\theta-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{s-1}(u\cdot\nabla\theta)\cdot\Lambda^{s-1}\operatorname{div}v.\\
\end{split}

By Young’s inequality, this leads to

(3.9)

\begin{split}\frac{1}{2}\|\Lambda^{s}\theta\|_{L^{2}}^{2}&-\left(1+\frac{\beta^{2}}{2}\right)\|v\|_{H^{s}}^{2}-\frac{\textnormal{d}}{\textnormal{d}t}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{s-1}v\cdot\Lambda^{s-1}\nabla\theta\\
&\leq\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{s-1}(u\cdot\nabla)v\cdot\Lambda^{s-1}\nabla\theta+\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{s-1}(v\cdot\nabla)u\cdot\Lambda^{s-1}\nabla\theta-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{s-1}(u\cdot\nabla\theta)\cdot\Lambda^{s-1}\operatorname{div}v.\\
\end{split}

Similarly, we take
s=1
and apply the integration by parts to obtain

(3.10)

\begin{split}\frac{1}{2}\|\nabla\theta\|_{L^{2}}^{2}&-\left(1+\frac{\beta^{2}}{2}\right)\|v\|_{H^{1}}^{2}-\frac{\textnormal{d}}{\textnormal{d}t}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}v\cdot\nabla\theta\\
&\leq\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}(u\cdot\nabla)v\cdot\nabla\theta+\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}(v\cdot\nabla)u\cdot\nabla\theta-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}(u\cdot\nabla\theta)\cdot\operatorname{div}v=2\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}(v\cdot\nabla)u\cdot\nabla\theta.\\
\end{split}

Finally, we set
\eta
to satisfy
0<\eta\leq\beta/(4+2\beta^{2})
, which is always less than
\frac{1}{4}
.
We multiply (
3.9
) and (
3.10
) by a sufficiently small constant
\eta>0
and add it to (
3.7
), which yields

(3.11)

\begin{split}&\frac{1}{2}\frac{\textnormal{d}}{\textnormal{d}t}\left(\|\Lambda^{s}u\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\|\Lambda^{s}v\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\|\Lambda^{s}\theta\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\|\Lambda^{\delta_{1}}u\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\|v\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\|\theta\|_{L^{2}}^{2}-\eta\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{s-1}v\cdot\Lambda^{s-1}\nabla\theta-\eta\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}v\cdot\nabla\theta\right)\\
&\quad+\left(\alpha\|\Lambda^{s}u\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\underline{\mu}\|\Lambda^{s}\nabla u\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\frac{\beta}{2}||\Lambda^{s}v\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\alpha\|\Lambda^{\delta_{1}}u\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\underline{\mu}\|\Lambda^{\delta_{1}}\nabla u\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\frac{\beta}{2}\|v\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\right)+\frac{\eta}{2}\|\nabla\theta\|_{H^{s-1}}^{2}\\
&\leq-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{s}((u\cdot\nabla)u)\cdot\Lambda^{s}u+\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{s}(v\otimes v):\Lambda^{s}\nabla u-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{s}((u\cdot\nabla)v)\cdot\Lambda^{s}v-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{s}((v\cdot\nabla)u)\cdot\Lambda^{s}v\\
&\quad-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{\delta_{1}}((u\cdot\nabla)u)\cdot\Lambda^{\delta_{1}}u+\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{\delta_{1}}(v\otimes v):\Lambda^{\delta_{1}}\nabla u-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}(v\cdot\nabla)u\cdot v-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{s}(u\cdot\nabla\theta)\Lambda^{s}\theta\\
&\quad-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\left(\Lambda^{s}(\mu(\theta)\nabla u)-\mu(\theta)\Lambda^{s}\nabla u\right):\Lambda^{s}\nabla u-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\left(\Lambda^{\delta_{1}}(\mu(\theta)\nabla u)-\mu(\theta)\Lambda^{\delta_{1}}\nabla u\right):\Lambda^{\delta_{1}}\nabla u\\
&\quad+\eta\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{s-1}(u\cdot\nabla)v\cdot\Lambda^{s-1}\nabla\theta+\eta\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{s-1}(v\cdot\nabla)u\cdot\Lambda^{s-1}\nabla\theta-\eta\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{s-1}(u\cdot\nabla\theta)\cdot\Lambda^{s-1}\operatorname{div}v\\
&\quad+2\eta\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}(v\cdot\nabla)u\cdot\nabla\theta\\
&=:\sum_{k=1}^{14}\mathcal{I}_{k}.\end{split}

Lemma 3.2
.

Let
\alpha\geq 0
and
s>1
.
Let
\delta_{1}
and
\lambda
as defined in (
3.6
) and in (
1.3
), respectively.
Let
(u,v,\theta)
be a smooth solution of (
1.2
) on
[0,T]\times\mathbb{R}^{2}
. For each time
t>0
, we define
A_{s}(t)
and
B_{s}(t)
by

A_{s}(t):=\left(\|\Lambda^{s}u(t)\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\|\Lambda^{\delta_{1}}u(t)\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\|v(t)\|^{2}_{H^{s}}+\|\theta(t)\|^{2}_{H^{s}}-\eta\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{s-1}v\cdot\Lambda^{s-1}\nabla\theta-\eta\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}v\cdot\nabla\theta\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}

and

(3.12)

B_{s}(t):=\lambda\left(\|\nabla u(t)\|_{H^{s}}^{2}+\|v(t)\|^{2}_{H^{s}}+\|\nabla\theta(t)\|_{H^{s-1}}^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}.

Then, there exists a constant
C>0
which depends on
\alpha,\beta,s,
and
\underline{\mu}
such that

(3.13)

\frac{1}{2}\frac{\textnormal{d}}{\textnormal{d}t}A_{s}^{2}+B_{s}^{2}\leq C\,(A_{s}+A_{s}^{s+1})\,B_{s}^{2}.

Remark 7
.

Since
\eta
is chosen to be less than
\frac{1}{4}
, we have

\frac{3}{4}A_{s}(t)^{2}\leq\left(\|\Lambda^{s}u(t)\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\|\Lambda^{\delta_{1}}u(t)\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\|v(t)\|_{H^{s}}^{2}+\|\theta(t)\|_{H^{s}}^{2}\right)\leq\frac{5}{4}A_{s}(t)^{2}.

Moreover, we have

B_{s}(t)^{2}\leq\left(\alpha\|\Lambda^{s}u\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\underline{\mu}\|\Lambda^{s}\nabla u\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\frac{\beta}{2}||\Lambda^{s}v\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\alpha\|\Lambda^{\delta_{1}}u\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\underline{\mu}\|\Lambda^{\delta_{1}}\nabla u\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\frac{\beta}{2}\|v\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\right)+\frac{\eta}{2}\|\nabla\theta\|_{H^{s-1}}^{2},

for the both cases
\alpha>0
or
\alpha=0
. This simplicity motivates the definition of
\lambda
.

Proof of Lemma
3.2
.

Due to (
3.11
), it only remains to bound nonlinear terms
\sum_{k=1}^{13}\mathcal{I}_{k}
.
We consider two cases, either
\alpha=0
or
\alpha>0
. This simplicity is the motivation for the definition of
\lambda
.

Let us consider the undamped case, i.e.,
\alpha=0
. In this case, we have
\delta_{1}=0
. By using integration by parts, we can easily obtain

\mathcal{I}_{5}=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}(u\cdot\nabla u)\cdot u=0,\quad\text{and}\quad\mathcal{I}_{6}+\mathcal{I}_{7}=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}(v\otimes v):\nabla u-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}(v\cdot\nabla)u\cdot v=0.

Moreover, it is clear that
\mathcal{I}_{10}=0
.

The estimates for the rest terms follow from a combination of (
2.3
), Lemma
A.1
, Lemma
2.3
, and the divergence-free condition
\operatorname{div}u=0
. Thus, we obtain

\displaystyle\mathcal{I}_{1}

\displaystyle=\left|\,\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}(\Lambda^{s}((u\cdot\nabla)u)-(u\cdot\nabla)\Lambda^{s}u)\cdot\Lambda^{s}u\,\right|\lesssim\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}u\rVert_{L^{4}}^{2}\lesssim\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{s+\frac{1}{2}}u\rVert_{L^{2}}^{2}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{L^{2}}(\lVert\Lambda^{s+1}u\rVert_{L^{2}}^{\frac{1}{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}u\rVert_{L^{2}}^{\frac{1}{2}})^{2}\lesssim\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{s+1}u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lesssim A_{s}B_{s}B_{s},

\displaystyle\mathcal{I}_{2}

\displaystyle=\left|\,\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{s}(v\otimes v):\Lambda^{s}\nabla u\,\right|\lesssim\lVert\Lambda^{s}v\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert v\rVert_{L^{\infty}}\lVert\Lambda^{s+1}u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lesssim\lVert\Lambda^{s}v\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert v\rVert_{H^{s}}\lVert\Lambda^{s+1}u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lesssim A_{s}B_{s}B_{s},

\displaystyle\mathcal{I}_{3}

\displaystyle=\left|\,\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}(\Lambda^{s}((u\cdot\nabla)v)-(u\cdot\nabla)\Lambda^{s}v)\cdot\Lambda^{s}v\,\right|

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{L^{\infty}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}v\rVert_{L^{2}}^{2}+\begin{cases}\lVert\Lambda^{s}u\rVert_{L^{\frac{2}{s-1}}}\lVert\nabla v\rVert_{L^{\frac{2}{2-s}}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}v\rVert_{L^{2}}&\quad\text{if}\ 1<s<2,\\
\lVert\Lambda^{s}u\rVert_{L^{4}}\lVert\nabla v\rVert_{L^{4}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}v\rVert_{L^{2}}&\quad\text{if}\ s\geq 2,\end{cases}

\displaystyle\lesssim\|\nabla u\|_{H^{s}}\|\Lambda^{s}v\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\begin{cases}\lVert\Lambda^{2}u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}v\rVert_{L^{2}}^{2}&\quad\text{if}\ 1<s<2,\\
\lVert\Lambda^{s+\frac{1}{2}}u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{\frac{3}{2}}v\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}v\rVert_{L^{2}}&\quad\text{if}\ s\geq 2,\end{cases}

\displaystyle\lesssim\|\nabla u\|_{H^{s}}\|v\|_{H^{s}}\|\Lambda^{s}v\|_{L^{2}}\lesssim B_{s}A_{s}B_{s},

\displaystyle\mathcal{I}_{4}

\displaystyle=\left|\,\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{s}((v\cdot\nabla)u)\cdot\Lambda^{s}v\,\right|

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert\Lambda^{s}v\rVert_{L^{2}}^{2}\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{L^{\infty}}+\lVert v\rVert_{L^{\infty}}\lVert\Lambda^{s+1}u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}v\rVert_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert\Lambda^{s}v\rVert_{L^{2}}^{2}\|\nabla u\|_{H^{s}}+\lVert v\rVert_{H^{s}}\lVert\Lambda^{s+1}u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}v\rVert_{L^{2}}\lesssim A_{s}B_{s}B_{s},

\displaystyle\mathcal{I}_{8}

\displaystyle=\left|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}(\Lambda^{s}(u\cdot\nabla\theta)-(u\cdot\nabla)\Lambda^{s}\theta)\,\Lambda^{s}\theta\,\right|

\displaystyle\lesssim\|\nabla u\|_{L^{\infty}}\|\Lambda^{s}\theta\|^{2}_{L^{2}}+\begin{cases}\|\Lambda^{s}u\|_{L^{\frac{2}{s-1}}}\|\nabla\theta\|_{L^{\frac{2}{2-s}}}\|\Lambda^{s}\theta\|_{L^{2}}&\quad\text{if }1<s<2,\\
\|\Lambda^{s}u\|_{L^{4}}\|\nabla\theta\|_{L^{4}}\|\Lambda^{s}\theta\|_{L^{2}}&\quad\text{if }s\geq 2,\\
\end{cases}

\displaystyle\lesssim\|\nabla u\|_{H^{s}}\|\Lambda^{s}\theta\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\begin{cases}\lVert\Lambda^{2}u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}^{2}&\,\text{ if}\ 1<s<2,\\
\lVert\Lambda^{s+\frac{1}{2}}u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{\frac{3}{2}}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}\|\Lambda^{s}\theta\|_{L^{2}}&\,\text{ if}\ s\geq 2,\end{cases}

\displaystyle\lesssim\|\theta\|_{H^{s}}\|\Lambda^{s}\theta\|_{L^{2}}\|\nabla u\|_{H^{s}}\lesssim A_{s}B_{s}B_{s}.

Moreover, by using Lemma
2.3
, (
2.6
), and (
2.7
), it follows that

\displaystyle\mathcal{I}_{9}

\displaystyle=\left|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\left(\Lambda^{s}(\mu(\theta)\nabla u)-\mu(\theta)\Lambda^{s}\nabla u\right):\Lambda^{s}\nabla u\,\right|

\displaystyle=\left|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\left(\Lambda^{s}((\mu(\theta)-\mu(0))\nabla u)-(\mu(\theta)-\mu(0))\Lambda^{s}\nabla u\right):\Lambda^{s}\nabla u\,\right|

\displaystyle\lesssim\|\Lambda^{s}(\mu(\theta)-\mu(0))\|_{L^{2}}\|\nabla u\|_{L^{\infty}}\|\Lambda^{s+1}u\|_{L^{2}}+\begin{cases}\|\Lambda^{s}u\|_{L^{\frac{2}{s-1}}}\|\nabla\mu(\theta)\|_{L^{\frac{2}{2-s}}}\|\Lambda^{s+1}u\|_{L^{2}}&\,\text{ if }1<s<2,\\
\|\Lambda^{s}u\|_{L^{4}}\|\nabla\mu(\theta)\|_{L^{4}}\|\Lambda^{s+1}u\|_{L^{2}}&\,\text{ if }s\geq 2,\\
\end{cases}

\displaystyle\lesssim(1+\|\nabla\theta\|_{L^{2}}^{\lceil s-1\rceil})\|\Lambda^{s}\theta\|_{L^{2}}\|\nabla u\|_{H^{s}}\|\Lambda^{s+1}u\|_{L^{2}}+\|\theta\|_{H^{s}}\|\nabla u\|_{H^{s}}\|\Lambda^{s+1}u\|_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle\lesssim(\|\theta\|_{H^{s}}^{s+1}+\|\theta\|_{H^{s}})\|\nabla u\|_{H^{s}}\|\Lambda^{s+1}u\|_{L^{2}}\lesssim A_{s}^{s+1}B_{s}^{2}+A_{s}B_{s}^{2}.

The last three terms can be estimated by

\displaystyle\mathcal{I}_{11}=\left|\,\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{s-1}(u\cdot\nabla)v\cdot\Lambda^{s-1}\nabla\theta\,\right|

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert u\rVert_{L^{\infty}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}v\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}+\begin{cases}\lVert\Lambda^{s-1}u\rVert_{L^{\frac{2}{s-1}}}\lVert\nabla v\rVert_{L^{\frac{2}{2-s}}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}&\,\text{if}\ 1<s<2,\\
\lVert\Lambda^{s-1}u\rVert_{L^{4}}\lVert\nabla v\rVert_{L^{4}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}&\,\text{if}\ s\geq 2,\end{cases}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert u\rVert_{H^{s}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}v\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}+\begin{cases}\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}v\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}&\,\text{if}\ 1<s<2,\\
\lVert\Lambda^{s-\frac{1}{2}}u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{\frac{3}{2}}v\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}&\,\text{if}\ s\geq 2,\end{cases}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert u\rVert_{H^{s}}\lVert v\rVert_{H^{s}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}\lesssim A_{s}B_{s}B_{s},

\displaystyle\mathcal{I}_{12}=\left|\,\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{s-1}(v\cdot\nabla)u\cdot\Lambda^{s-1}\nabla\theta\,\right|

\displaystyle\lesssim\left(\lVert\Lambda^{s-1}v\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{L^{\infty}}+\lVert v\rVert_{L^{\infty}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}u\rVert_{L^{2}}\right)\lVert\Lambda^{s}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert v\rVert_{H^{s}}\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{H^{s}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}\lesssim A_{s}B_{s}B_{s},

and

\displaystyle\mathcal{I}_{13}=\left|\,\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{s-1}(u\cdot\nabla\theta)\cdot\Lambda^{s-1}\operatorname{div}v\,\right|

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert u\rVert_{L^{\infty}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}v\rVert_{L^{2}}+\begin{cases}\lVert\Lambda^{s-1}u\rVert_{L^{\frac{2}{s-1}}}\lVert\nabla\theta\rVert_{L^{\frac{2}{2-s}}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}v\rVert_{L^{2}}&\,\text{if}\ 1<s<2,\\
\lVert\Lambda^{s-1}u\rVert_{L^{4}}\lVert\nabla\theta\rVert_{L^{4}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}v\rVert_{L^{2}}&\,\text{if}\ s\geq 2,\end{cases}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert u\rVert_{H^{s}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}v\rVert_{L^{2}}+\begin{cases}\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}v\rVert_{L^{2}}&\,\text{if}\ 1<s<2,\\
\lVert\Lambda^{s-\frac{1}{2}}u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{\frac{3}{2}}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}v\rVert_{L^{2}}&\,\text{if}\ s\geq 2,\end{cases}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert u\rVert_{H^{s}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}v\rVert_{L^{2}}+\lVert\theta\rVert_{H^{s}}\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{H^{s}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}v\rVert_{L^{2}}\lesssim A_{s}B_{s}B_{s}.

Finally, we have

\mathcal{I}_{14}=\left|\,2\eta\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}(v\cdot\nabla)u\cdot\nabla\theta\,\right|\lesssim\lVert v\rVert_{L^{\infty}}\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\nabla\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}\lesssim\lVert v\rVert_{H^{s}}\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\nabla\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}\lesssim B_{s}A_{s}B_{s}.

Combining all these estimates, we conclude that all the terms

\mathcal{I}_{k}
are bounded by
C(A_{s}B_{s}^{2}+A_{s}^{s+1}B_{s}^{2})
, where
C
is a constant depending only on
\beta>0
,
\underline{\mu}>0
, and
s>1
. This completes the estimate (
3.13
) in the undamped case.

Next, let us consider the damped case, i.e.
\alpha>0
. In this case, we have
\delta_{1}=1
. For the terms
\mathcal{I}_{1}-\mathcal{I}_{4}
,
\mathcal{I}_{8}
,
\mathcal{I}_{9}
,
\mathcal{I}_{12}
, and
\mathcal{I}_{14}
, the same estimate as the undamped case can be used.
By using (
2.3
), Lemma
A.1
, and
\operatorname{div}u=0
, we see that

\displaystyle\mathcal{I}_{5}

\displaystyle=\left|\,\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}(\Lambda((u\cdot\nabla)u)-(u\cdot\nabla)\Lambda u)\cdot\Lambda u\,\right|

\begin{split}\mathcal{I}_{11}+\mathcal{I}_{13}&=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\left(\Lambda^{s-1}(u_{i}\partial_{i}v_{j})-u_{i}\partial_{i}\Lambda^{s-1}v_{j}\right)\partial_{j}\Lambda^{s-1}\theta-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\left(\Lambda^{s-1}(u_{i}\partial_{i}\theta)-u_{i}\partial_{i}\Lambda^{s-1}\theta\right)\Lambda^{s-1}\partial_{j}v_{j}\\

\begin{split}N_{1}&\lesssim\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{L^{\infty}}\lVert\Lambda^{s-1}v\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}+\begin{cases}\lVert\Lambda^{s-1}u\rVert_{L^{\frac{2}{s-1}}}\lVert\nabla v\rVert_{L^{\frac{2}{2-s}}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}&\quad\text{if }1<s<2,\\

\begin{split}N_{2}&\lesssim\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{L^{\infty}}\lVert\Lambda^{s-1}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}v\rVert_{L^{2}}+\begin{cases}\lVert\Lambda^{s-1}u\rVert_{L^{\frac{2}{s-1}}}\lVert\nabla\theta\rVert_{L^{\frac{2}{2-s}}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}v\rVert_{L^{2}}&\quad\text{if }1<s<2,\\

and

We combine (

Thus, we have established (

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert\Lambda^{m}v\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert v\rVert_{L^{\infty}}\lVert\Lambda^{m+1}u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lesssim\lVert\Lambda^{m}v\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert v\rVert_{H^{s}}\lVert\Lambda^{m+1}u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lesssim A_{m}B_{s}B_{m},

\displaystyle\mathcal{J}_{3}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{L^{\infty}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}v\rVert_{L^{2}}^{2}+\lVert\Lambda^{m}u\rVert_{L^{\frac{2}{s-1}}}\lVert\nabla v\rVert_{L^{\frac{2}{2-s}}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}v\rVert_{L^{2}}\lesssim\|\nabla u\|_{H^{s}}\|\Lambda^{m}v\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\lVert\Lambda^{m-s+2}u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}v\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}v\rVert_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle\lesssim\|\nabla u\|_{H^{s}}\|\Lambda^{m}v\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{H^{m}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}v\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}v\rVert_{L^{2}}\lesssim B_{s}B_{m}A_{m}+B_{m}B_{s}A_{m},

\displaystyle\mathcal{J}_{4}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert\Lambda^{m}v\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{L^{\infty}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}v\rVert_{L^{2}}+\lVert v\rVert_{L^{\infty}}\lVert\Lambda^{m+1}u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}v\rVert_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert\Lambda^{m}v\rVert_{L^{2}}\|\nabla u\|_{H^{s}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}v\rVert_{L^{2}}+\lVert v\rVert_{H^{s}}\lVert\Lambda^{m+1}u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}v\rVert_{L^{2}}\lesssim B_{m}B_{s}A_{m}+B_{s}B_{m}A_{m},

and

\displaystyle\mathcal{J}_{8}=\left|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\left(\Lambda^{m}(u\cdot\nabla\theta)-(u\cdot\nabla)\Lambda^{m}\theta\right):\Lambda^{m}\theta\,\right|

\displaystyle\lesssim\|\nabla u\|_{L^{\infty}}\|\Lambda^{m}\theta\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\|\Lambda^{m}u\|_{L^{\frac{2}{s-1}}}\|\nabla\theta\|_{L^{\frac{2}{2-s}}}\|\Lambda^{m}\theta\|_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle\lesssim\|\nabla u\|_{H^{s}}\|\Lambda^{m}\theta\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\|\Lambda^{m-s+2}u\|_{L^{2}}\|\Lambda^{s}\theta\|_{L^{2}}\|\Lambda^{m}\theta\|_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle\lesssim\|\nabla u\|_{H^{s}}\|\Lambda^{m}\theta\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\|\nabla u\|_{H^{m}}\|\Lambda^{s}\theta\|_{L^{2}}\|\Lambda^{m}\theta\|_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle\lesssim B_{s}A_{m}B_{m}+B_{m}B_{s}A_{m}.

We apply Young’s inequality together with Lemma
2.3
, (
2.6
), and (
2.7
) to obtain

\displaystyle\mathcal{J}_{9}

\displaystyle=\bigg|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\left(\Lambda^{m}(\mu(\theta)\nabla u)-\mu(\theta)\Lambda^{m}\nabla u\right):\Lambda^{m}\nabla u\bigg|

\displaystyle=\bigg|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\left(\Lambda^{m}((\mu(\theta)-\mu(0))\nabla u)-(\mu(\theta)-\mu(0))\Lambda^{m}\nabla u\right):\Lambda^{m}\nabla u\bigg|

\displaystyle\lesssim\|\Lambda^{m}(\mu(\theta)-\mu(0))\|_{L^{2}}\|\nabla u\|_{L^{\infty}}\|\Lambda^{m+1}u\|_{L^{2}}+\|\nabla\mu(\theta)\|_{L^{\frac{2}{2-s}}}\|\Lambda^{m}u\|_{L^{\frac{2}{s-1}}}\|\Lambda^{m+1}u\|_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle\lesssim(1+\|\nabla\theta\|_{L^{2}}^{\lceil m-1\rceil})\|\Lambda^{m}\theta\|_{L^{2}}\|\nabla u\|_{H^{s}}\|\Lambda^{m+1}u\|_{L^{2}}+\|\nabla\theta\|_{L^{\frac{2}{2-s}}}\|\Lambda^{m-s+2}u\|_{L^{2}}\|\Lambda^{m+1}u\|_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle\lesssim(1+\|\theta\|^{\lceil m-1\rceil}_{H^{s}})\|\Lambda^{m}\theta\|_{L^{2}}\|\nabla u\|_{H^{s}}\|\Lambda^{m+1}u\|_{L^{2}}+\|\Lambda^{s}\theta\|_{L^{2}}\|\Lambda^{m-s+2}u\|_{L^{2}}\|\Lambda^{m+1}u\|_{L^{2}}.

Now, we use Young’s inequality with
\|\Lambda^{m-s+2}u\|_{L^{2}}\lesssim\|\Lambda^{m}u\|_{L^{2}}^{s-1}\|\Lambda^{m+1}u\|_{L^{2}}^{2-s}
to see that

\displaystyle\|\Lambda^{s}\theta\|_{L^{2}}\|\Lambda^{m-s+2}u\|_{L^{2}}\|\Lambda^{m+1}u\|_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle\lesssim(\|\Lambda^{s}\theta\|_{L^{2}}\|\Lambda^{m}u\|_{L^{2}})^{s-1}\|\Lambda^{s}\theta\|_{L^{2}}^{2-s}\|\Lambda^{m+1}u\|_{L^{2}}^{3-s}

\displaystyle\leq C(\|\Lambda^{s}\theta\|_{L^{2}}\|\Lambda^{m}u\|_{L^{2}})^{2}+\frac{\underline{\mu}}{4}\|\Lambda^{s}\theta\|_{L^{2}}^{\frac{4-2s}{3-s}}\|\Lambda^{m+1}u\|_{L^{2}}^{2}

\displaystyle\leq C(\|\Lambda^{s}\theta\|_{L^{2}}\|\Lambda^{m}u\|_{L^{2}})^{2}+\frac{\underline{\mu}}{4}\epsilon^{\frac{4-2s}{3-s}}\|\Lambda^{m+1}u\|_{L^{2}}^{2}

\displaystyle\leq C(\|\Lambda^{s}\theta\|_{L^{2}}\|\Lambda^{m}u\|_{L^{2}})^{2}+\frac{\underline{\mu}}{4}\|\Lambda^{m+1}u\|_{L^{2}}^{2}.

Hence, the term
\mathcal{J}_{9}
can be bounded by

\mathcal{J}_{9}\leq C(A_{m}B_{s}B_{m})+C(B_{s}A_{m})^{2}+\frac{1}{4}B_{m}^{2}.

On the other hands, by using
\nabla\mu(\theta)=\mu^{\prime}(\theta)\nabla\theta
,

\displaystyle\mathcal{J}_{10}

\displaystyle=\left|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\left(\Lambda(\mu(\theta)\nabla u)-\mu(\theta)\Lambda\nabla u\right):\Lambda\nabla u\right|

\displaystyle\lesssim\|\nabla\theta\|_{L^{2}}\|\nabla u\|_{L^{\infty}}\|\Lambda^{2}u\|_{L^{2}}\lesssim\|\theta\|_{H^{m}}\|\nabla u\|_{H^{s}}\|\nabla u\|_{H^{m}}\lesssim A_{m}B_{s}B_{m},

\displaystyle\mathcal{J}_{12}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert\Lambda^{m-1}v\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{L^{\infty}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}+\lVert v\rVert_{L^{\infty}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert v\rVert_{H^{m}}\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{H^{s}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}+\lVert v\rVert_{H^{s}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}\lesssim A_{m}B_{s}B_{m}+B_{s}A_{m}B_{m}.

To estimate
\mathcal{J}_{11}
and
\mathcal{J}_{13}
terms, we use cancellation properties and integration by parts, similarly to (
3.14
). It follows that

(4.6)

\displaystyle\mathcal{J}_{11}+\mathcal{J}_{13}

\displaystyle=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\left(\Lambda^{m-1}(u_{i}\partial_{i}v_{j})-u_{i}\partial_{i}\Lambda^{m-1}v_{j}\right)\partial_{j}\Lambda^{m-1}\theta-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\left(\Lambda^{m-1}(u_{i}\partial_{i}\theta)-u_{i}\partial_{i}\Lambda^{m-1}\theta\right)\Lambda^{m-1}\partial_{j}v_{j}

\displaystyle+\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}(\partial_{i}\Lambda^{m-1}\theta)(\partial_{j}u_{i})(\Lambda^{m-1}v_{j})=:M_{1}+M_{2}+M_{3}.

By using (
2.1
), (
2.2
), and (
2.5
), one has

\displaystyle M_{1}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{L^{\infty}}\lVert\Lambda^{m-1}v\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}+\lVert\Lambda^{m-1}u\rVert_{L^{\frac{2}{s-1}}}\lVert\nabla v\rVert_{L^{\frac{2}{2-s}}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{H^{s}}\lVert v\rVert_{H^{m}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}+\lVert\Lambda^{m-s+1}u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}v\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{H^{s}}\lVert v\rVert_{H^{m}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}+\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{H^{m}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}v\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}\lesssim B_{s}A_{m}B_{m}+B_{m}B_{s}A_{m},

\displaystyle M_{2}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{L^{\infty}}\lVert\Lambda^{m-1}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}v\rVert_{L^{2}}+\lVert\Lambda^{m-1}u\rVert_{L^{\frac{2}{s-1}}}\lVert\nabla\theta\rVert_{L^{\frac{2}{2-s}}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}v\rVert_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{H^{s}}\lVert\theta\rVert_{H^{m}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}v\rVert_{L^{2}}+\lVert\Lambda^{m-s+1}u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}v\rVert_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{H^{s}}\lVert\theta\rVert_{H^{m}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}v\rVert_{L^{2}}+\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{H^{m}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}v\rVert_{L^{2}}\lesssim B_{s}A_{m}B_{m}+B_{m}B_{s}A_{m},

\displaystyle M_{3}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert\Lambda^{m}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{L^{\infty}}\lVert\Lambda^{m-1}v\rVert_{L^{2}}\lesssim\lVert\Lambda^{m}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{H^{s}}\lVert v\rVert_{H^{m}}\lesssim A_{m}B_{s}B_{m}.

It is easy to see that

\mathcal{J}_{14}=\left|\,2\eta\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}(v\cdot\nabla)u\cdot\nabla\theta\,\right|\lesssim\lVert v\rVert_{L^{\infty}}\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\nabla\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}\lesssim\lVert v\rVert_{H^{m}}\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\theta\rVert_{H^{m}}\lesssim B_{m}B_{s}A_{m}.

We combine all these estimates and finally use Young’s inequality to conclude that

\sum_{j=1}^{13}\mathcal{J}_{k}\leq C(B_{s}A_{m}B_{m}+B_{s}^{2}A_{m}^{2})+\frac{1}{4}B_{m}(t)^{2}\leq C(B_{s}^{2}A_{m}^{2})+\frac{1}{2}B_{m}(t)^{2}.

We can hide the term
B_{m}(t)^{2}
on the right-hand side, and (
4.4
) is shown.
The proof is complete.
∎

4.2.
Temporal decay estimates

In this section, we derive temporal decay estimates for solutions in Sobolev spaces of higher regularity. We shall use the temporal decay estimates derived in Section
3.2
.

Proposition 4.2
.

Let
\alpha\geq 0
and
s\in(1,2)
, and suppose
that the initial data
(u_{0},v_{0},\theta_{0})
belong to
\mathcal{C}^{\infty}_{0}(\mathbb{R}^{2})

, the global solution
(u,v,\theta)
of the Cauchy problem (

) constructed in Proposition

enjoys the estimate:

\lVert\Lambda^{m-1}u(t)\rVert_{L^{2}}+\lVert\Lambda^{m-1}v(t)\rVert_{L^{2}}+\lVert\Lambda^{m-1}\theta(t)\rVert_{L^{2}}\leq C(1+t)^{-\frac{m-1}{2}}\quad\text{for all }t\geq 0,

where the positive constant
C>0
which depends only on
s,m,\alpha,\beta,\underline{\mu},\|\mu^{\prime}\|_{C^{m-1}}
, and
\|(u_{0},v_{0},\theta_{0})\|_{H^{m}}
.

Proof.

We remark that the solution
(u,v,\theta)
is smooth due to Proposition
4.1
.
The following identity is obtained by substituting
m
for

s
in the expression previously used in (
3.18
).

(4.8)

\begin{split}&\frac{1}{2}\frac{\textnormal{d}}{\textnormal{d}t}\left(\|\Lambda^{m}u\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\|\Lambda^{m}v\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\|\Lambda^{m}\theta\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\|\Lambda^{m-1}u\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\|\Lambda^{m-1}v\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\|\Lambda^{m-1}\theta\|_{L^{2}}^{2}-\kappa\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{m-1}v\cdot\Lambda^{m-1}\nabla\theta\,\right)\\
&\quad+\left(\alpha\|\Lambda^{m}u\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\underline{\mu}\|\Lambda^{m}\nabla u\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\frac{\beta}{2}||\Lambda^{m}v\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\alpha\|\Lambda^{m-1}u\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\underline{\mu}\|\Lambda^{m-1}\nabla u\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\beta\|\Lambda^{m-1}v\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\right)+\frac{\kappa}{2}\|\Lambda^{m}\theta\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\\
&\leq-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{m}((u\cdot\nabla)u)\cdot\Lambda^{m}u+\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{m}(v\otimes v):\Lambda^{m}\nabla u-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{m}((u\cdot\nabla)v)\cdot\Lambda^{m}v-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{m}((v\cdot\nabla)u)\cdot\Lambda^{m}v\\
&\quad-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{m-1}((u\cdot\nabla)u)\cdot\Lambda^{m-1}u+\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{m-1}(v\otimes v):\Lambda^{m-1}\nabla u-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{m-1}((u\cdot\nabla)v)\cdot\Lambda^{m-1}v\\
&\quad-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{m-1}((v\cdot\nabla)u)\cdot\Lambda^{m-1}v-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{m}(u\cdot\nabla\theta)\Lambda^{m}\theta-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\left(\Lambda^{m}(\mu(\theta)\nabla u)-\mu(\theta)\Lambda^{m}\nabla u\right):\Lambda^{m}\nabla u\\
&\quad-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{m-1}(u\cdot\nabla\theta)\Lambda^{m-1}\theta-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\left(\Lambda^{m-1}(\mu(\theta)\nabla u)-\mu(\theta)\Lambda^{m-1}\nabla u\right):\Lambda^{m-1}\nabla u\\
&\quad+\kappa\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{m-1}(u\cdot\nabla)v\cdot\Lambda^{m-1}\nabla\theta+\kappa\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{m-1}(v\cdot\nabla)u\cdot\Lambda^{m-1}\nabla\theta-\kappa\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{m-1}(u\cdot\nabla\theta)\cdot\Lambda^{m-1}\operatorname{div}v\\
&=:\sum_{k=1}^{15}\mathcal{J}_{k}.\end{split}

For each time

, we define

and

by

and

Y_{m}(t):=\lambda\left(\|\Lambda^{m+1}u\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\|\Lambda^{m}u\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\alpha\|\Lambda^{m-1}u\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\|\Lambda^{m}v\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\|\Lambda^{m-1}v\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\|\Lambda^{m}\theta\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}(t).

We claim that there holds

(4.9)

\frac{\textnormal{d}}{\textnormal{d}t}X_{m}^{2}+Y_{m}^{2}\leq\frac{C_{1}}{(1+t)^{m}}+\frac{C_{2}}{(1+t)^{\delta}}Y_{m}^{2},

for some fixed positive real number

.
To prove the claim, we estimate the nonlinear terms

in (

).
To this end, we use the temporal decay estimates of (

), (

), and several interpolations for the case

\displaystyle\|\Lambda^{s-1}(u,v,\theta)\|_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle\leq C(1+t)^{-(s-1)/2},

\displaystyle\|\Lambda^{s}(u,v,\theta)\|_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle\leq C(1+t)^{-(s-1)/2},

\displaystyle\|\nabla(u,v,\theta)\|_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle\leq C\left(\|\Lambda^{s-1}(u,v,\theta)\|_{L^{2}}+\|\Lambda^{s}(u,v,\theta)\|_{L^{2}}\right)\leq C(1+t)^{-(s-1)/2},

\displaystyle\|(u,v,\theta)\|_{L^{\infty}}

\displaystyle\leq C\left(\|\Lambda^{s-1}(u,v,\theta)\|_{L^{2}}+\|\Lambda^{s}(u,v,\theta)\|_{L^{2}}\right)\leq C(1+t)^{-(s-1)/2}.

For simplicity, we restrict our attention to the undamped case in this proof, noting that the same argument carries over to the damped case.

Now, let us begin with the terms

, and

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}u\rVert_{L^{4}}^{2}\lesssim\left(\lVert\Lambda^{s-1}u\rVert_{L^{2}}+\lVert\Lambda^{s}u\rVert_{L^{2}}\right)\lVert\Lambda^{m+1}u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lesssim(1+t)^{-(s-1)/2}Y_{m}^{2},

\displaystyle\mathcal{J}_{2}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert\Lambda^{m}v\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert v\rVert_{L^{\infty}}\lVert\Lambda^{m+1}u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lesssim\lVert\Lambda^{m}v\rVert_{L^{2}}(\lVert\Lambda^{s-1}v\rVert_{L^{2}}+\lVert\Lambda^{s}v\rVert_{L^{2}})\lVert\Lambda^{m+1}u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lesssim(1+t)^{-(s-1)/2}Y_{m}^{2},

\displaystyle\mathcal{J}_{6}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert\Lambda^{m-1}v\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert v\rVert_{L^{\infty}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lesssim\lVert\Lambda^{m-1}v\rVert_{L^{2}}(\lVert\Lambda^{s-1}v\rVert_{L^{2}}+\lVert\Lambda^{s}v\rVert_{L^{2}})\lVert\Lambda^{m}u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lesssim(1+t)^{-(s-1)/2}Y_{m}^{2}.

To estimate the term
\mathcal{J}_{3},\mathcal{J}_{4},\mathcal{J}_{9}
, and
\mathcal{J}_{10}
, we use Lemma

to obtain

Hence, for the term

\displaystyle\mathcal{J}_{3}

\displaystyle\mathcal{J}_{4}

Using an analogous computation, we arrive at the following conclusion:

\displaystyle\mathcal{J}_{7}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{m-1}v\rVert_{L^{4}}^{2}+\lVert\Lambda^{m-1}u\rVert_{L^{4}}\lVert\nabla v\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{m-1}v\rVert_{L^{4}}\leq C(1+t)^{-m}+\frac{1}{8}Y_{m}^{2},

\displaystyle\mathcal{J}_{8}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{m-1}v\rVert_{L^{4}}^{2}+\lVert v\rVert_{L^{\infty}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{m-1}v\rVert_{L^{2}}\leq C(1+t)^{-m}+\frac{1}{8}Y_{m}^{2}+C(1+t)^{-(s-1)/2}Y_{m}^{2},

\displaystyle\mathcal{J}_{11}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{m-1}\theta\rVert_{L^{4}}\lVert\Lambda^{m-1}\theta\rVert_{L^{4}}+\lVert\Lambda^{m-1}u\rVert_{L^{4}}\lVert\nabla\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{m-1}\theta\rVert_{L^{4}}\leq C(1+t)^{-m}+\frac{1}{8}Y_{m}^{2}.

To bound

, and

where we denote

.
For the term
\mathcal{J}_{12},
we apply (
2.6
) and (
2.7
) to obtain

\displaystyle\mathcal{J}_{12}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert\nabla\mu(\theta)\rVert_{L^{\frac{2}{2-s}}}\lVert\Lambda^{m-1}u\rVert_{L^{\frac{2}{s-1}}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}u\rVert_{L^{2}}+\lVert\Lambda^{m-1}\mu(\theta)\rVert_{L^{\frac{2}{s-1}}}\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{L^{\frac{2}{2-s}}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}u\rVert_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert\nabla\theta\rVert_{L^{\frac{2}{2-s}}}\lVert\Lambda^{m-s+1}u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}u\rVert_{L^{2}}+\lVert\Lambda^{m-s+1}(\mu(\theta)-\mu(0))\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}u\rVert_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert\Lambda^{s}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{m-s+1}u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}u\rVert_{L^{2}}+(1+\|\nabla\theta\|_{L^{2}}^{\lceil m-s\rceil})\lVert\Lambda^{m-s+1}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{s}u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}u\rVert_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle\lesssim(\lVert\nabla\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}^{a_{4}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}^{1-a_{4}})(\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{L^{2}}^{1-a_{4}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}u\rVert_{L^{2}}^{a_{4}})\lVert\Lambda^{m}u\rVert_{L^{2}}+(\lVert\nabla\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}^{1-a_{4}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}^{a_{4}})(\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{L^{2}}^{a_{4}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}u\rVert_{L^{2}}^{1-a_{4}})\lVert\Lambda^{m}u\rVert_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle\lesssim(1+t)^{-(s-1)/2}Y_{m}^{2}.

Similarly, it follows that

\mathcal{J}_{14}\lesssim\lVert v\rVert_{L^{\infty}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}u\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}+\lVert\Lambda^{m-1}v\rVert_{L^{\frac{2}{s-1}}}\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{L^{\frac{2}{2-s}}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}\lesssim(1+t)^{-(s-1)/2}Y_{m}^{2}.

The term
\mathcal{J}_{13}+\mathcal{J}_{15}
can be represented, similarly to (
3.23
), as follows:

\displaystyle\mathcal{J}_{13}+\mathcal{J}_{15}=

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\left(\Lambda^{m-1}(u_{i}\partial_{i}v_{j})-u_{i}\partial_{i}\Lambda^{m-1}v_{j}\right)\partial_{j}\Lambda^{m-1}\theta-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\left(\Lambda^{m-1}(u_{i}\partial_{i}\theta)-u_{i}\partial_{i}\Lambda^{m-1}\theta\right)\Lambda^{m-1}\partial_{j}v_{j}

\displaystyle\quad+\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}(\partial_{i}\Lambda^{m-1}\theta)(\partial_{j}u_{i})(\Lambda^{m-1}v_{j})=:R_{1}+R_{2}+R_{3}.

Then, these terms can also be computed similarly by

\displaystyle R_{1}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{L^{\frac{2}{2-s}}}\lVert\Lambda^{m-1}v\rVert_{L^{\frac{2}{s-1}}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}+\lVert\Lambda^{m-1}u\rVert_{L^{\frac{2}{s-1}}}\lVert\nabla v\rVert_{L^{\frac{2}{2-s}}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}\lesssim(1+t)^{-(s-1)/2}Y_{m}^{2},

\displaystyle R_{2}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{L^{\frac{2}{2-s}}}\lVert\Lambda^{m-1}\theta\rVert_{L^{\frac{2}{s-1}}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}v\rVert_{L^{2}}+\lVert\Lambda^{m-1}u\rVert_{L^{\frac{2}{s-1}}}\lVert\nabla\theta\rVert_{L^{\frac{2}{2-s}}}\lVert\Lambda^{m}v\rVert_{L^{2}}\lesssim(1+t)^{-(s-1)/2}Y_{m}^{2},

\displaystyle R_{3}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert\Lambda^{m}\theta\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert\nabla u\rVert_{L^{\frac{2}{2-s}}}\lVert\Lambda^{m-1}v\rVert_{L^{\frac{2}{s-1}}}\lesssim(1+t)^{-(s-1)/2}Y_{m}^{2}.

Combining these together, the nonlinear terms
\sum_{k=1}^{15}\mathcal{J}_{k}
are bounded in the form of
\frac{C_{1}}{(1+t)^{m}}+\frac{C_{2}}{(1+t)^{\delta}}Y_{m}^{2}+\frac{1}{2}Y_{m}^{2}
, and thus the claim (
4.9
) is shown.

Now, we are ready to establish the temporal decay estimates (
4.7
).
Assuming (
4.9
), i.e.

\frac{\textnormal{d}}{\textnormal{d}t}X_{m}^{2}+Y_{m}^{2}\leq\frac{C_{1}}{(1+t)^{m}}+\frac{C_{2}}{(1+t)^{\delta}}Y_{m}^{2}\quad\text{for some }\delta>0,

we choose a time
T_{1}>0
such that
\frac{C_{2}}{(1+T_{1})^{\delta}}<\frac{1}{2}
.
By using Gagliardo–Nirenberg inequality in Lemma
2.1
, it can be easily shown that

2C_{0}(X_{m}^{2})^{\frac{m}{m-1}}\leq Y_{m}^{2},

for some positive constant
C_{0}
.
The choice of
T_{1}
leads to that

\frac{\textnormal{d}}{\textnormal{d}t}X_{m}^{2}+C_{0}(X_{m}^{2})^{\frac{m}{m-1}}\leq\frac{C_{1}}{(1+t)^{m}},\quad\text{if }t\geq T_{1}.

To simplify notation, we set
y(t):=X_{m}(t)^{2}
.
Note that

\displaystyle\left((1+t)^{m}y(t)\right)^{\prime}

\displaystyle=m(1+t)^{m-1}y(t)+(1+t)^{m}\left(y^{\prime}(t)+C_{0}(y(t))^{\frac{m}{m-1}}\right)-C_{0}(1+t)^{m}y(t)^{\frac{m}{m-1}}

\displaystyle\leq(1+t)^{m}y(t)\left(\frac{m}{1+t}-C_{0}(y(t))^{\frac{1}{m-1}}\right)+C_{1}.

Now, we divide into two cases:

(i)

\frac{m}{1+t}-C_{0}(y(t))^{\frac{1}{m-1}}\geq 0
: Then, this implies that
y(t)\leq\left(\frac{m}{C_{0}(1+t)}\right)^{m-1}
and this yields that

\left((1+t)^{m}y(t)\right)^{\prime}\leq(1+t)^{m}y(t)\left(\frac{m}{1+t}-C_{0}(y(t))^{\frac{1}{m-1}}\right)+C_{1}\leq\frac{m^{m}}{C_{0}^{m-1}}+C_{1}.

(ii)

\frac{m}{1+t}-C_{0}(y(t))^{\frac{1}{m-1}}<0
: Then, it is clear that

\left((1+t)^{m}y(t)\right)^{\prime}\leq C_{1}.

On the other hand, we can obtain the uniform bound of
X_{m}(t)
in
[0,T_{1}]
by

\sup_{t\in[0,T_{1}]}X_{m}(t)\lesssim\sup_{t\in[0,\infty)}A_{m}(t)<C_{3}\epsilon.

Combining these, we conclude that

X_{m}(t)^{2}\leq C_{4}(1+t)^{-m+1},

where the constant
C_{4}
can be explicitly bounded by
\max\left(\frac{m^{m}}{C_{0}^{m-1}}+C_{1}+(C_{3}\epsilon)^{2},\,(C_{3}\epsilon)^{2}(1+T_{1})^{m-1}\right)
. Notice that the constant
C_{4}
depends solely on the parameters
s,\alpha,\beta,\underline{\mu},\|\mu^{\prime}\|_{C^{m-1}}
and
\|(u_{0},v_{0},\theta_{0})\|_{H^{m}}
.

We arrive at

\lVert\Lambda^{m-1}u(t)\rVert_{L^{2}}+\lVert\Lambda^{m-1}v(t)\rVert_{L^{2}}+\lVert\Lambda^{m-1}\theta(t)\rVert_{L^{2}}\leq 2X_{m}(t)\leq C(1+t)^{-\frac{m-1}{2}}\quad\text{for all }t\geq 0.

∎

We close this section by stating a simple but useful lemma.

Lemma 4.3
.

Let
y(t)
be a nonnegative differentiable function that satisfies

y^{\prime}(t)+ky(t)\leq\frac{C}{(1+t)^{a}},

for some positive constants
k,a
and
C
.
Then, there holds

y(t)\lesssim\frac{1}{(1+t)^{a}}.

Proof.

By Grönwall’s inequality, one can simply show that

y(t)\leq e^{-kt}y(0)+\int_{0}^{t}e^{-k(t-\tau)}\frac{C}{(1+\tau)^{a}}\textnormal{d}\tau.

Then, the result comes from

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{t}e^{-k(t-\tau)}\frac{C}{(1+\tau)^{a}}\textnormal{d}\tau

\displaystyle=\int_{0}^{t/2}e^{-k(t-\tau)}\frac{C}{(1+\tau)^{a}}\textnormal{d}\tau+\int_{t/2}^{t}e^{-k(t-\tau)}\frac{C}{(1+\tau)^{a}}\textnormal{d}\tau

\displaystyle\leq e^{-kt/2}\int_{0}^{t/2}\frac{C}{(1+\tau)^{a}}\textnormal{d}\tau+\frac{C}{(1+\frac{t}{2})^{a}}\int_{t/2}^{t}e^{-k(t-\tau)}\textnormal{d}\tau=\mathcal{O}\left(\frac{1}{(1+t)^{a}}\right).

∎

Lemma A.1

(
L^{\infty}
-bound,
\Lambda^{s}
-type interpolation.)
.

Let
d
be a positive integer.

(i)

Let
s_{1},s_{2}
and
s
be nonnegative real numbers satisfying
0\leq s_{1}<s<s_{2}
.
Suppose

. Then, it holds that

\|\Lambda^{s}u\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\leq\|\Lambda^{s_{1}}u\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}^{\frac{s_{2}-s}{s_{2}-s_{1}}}\|\Lambda^{s_{2}}u\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}^{\frac{s-s_{1}}{s_{2}-s_{1}}}.

(ii)

Let
s\geq 0
, and let
s_{1},s_{2}
be two nonnegative real numbers satisfying
0\leq s_{1}<\frac{d}{2}
and
\frac{d}{2}+s<s_{2}

Suppose

. Then, there exists a constant

\|\Lambda^{s}u\|_{L^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\leq C\|\Lambda^{s_{1}}u\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}^{\frac{s_{2}-s-\frac{d}{2}}{s_{2}-s_{1}}}\|\Lambda^{s_{2}}u\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}^{\frac{\frac{d}{2}+s-s_{1}}{s_{2}-s_{1}}}.

(i)

We define the exponents
p
and
q
by

\frac{1}{p}=\frac{s_{2}-s}{s_{2}-s_{1}},\quad\frac{1}{q}=\frac{s-s_{1}}{s_{2}-s_{1}}.

Then the following relations hold:

\frac{1}{p}+\frac{1}{q}=1,\quad\frac{s_{1}}{p}+\frac{s_{2}}{q}=s.

Applying Hölder’s inequality, we deduce

\int_{\mathbb{R}^{d}}|\xi|^{2s}|\hat{u}(\xi)|^{2}\,\textnormal{d}\xi=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{d}}\left(|\xi|^{\frac{2s_{1}}{p}}|\hat{u}|^{\frac{2}{p}}\right)\left(|\xi|^{\frac{2s_{2}}{q}}|\hat{u}|^{\frac{2}{q}}\right)\,\textnormal{d}\xi\leq\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{d}}|\xi|^{2s_{1}}|\hat{u}|^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{d}}|\xi|^{2s_{2}}|\hat{u}|^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}.

(ii)

As a direct consequence of [Lemma 2.1,
[
28
]
], we obtain that for
s\geq 0
and

\lVert\Lambda^{s}u\rVert_{L^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\leq C\lVert|\xi|^{s}\hat{u}\rVert_{L^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\leq C\lVert|\xi|^{s+\frac{d}{2}+p}\hat{u}\rVert_{L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}^{\frac{q}{p+q}}\lVert|\xi|^{s+\frac{d}{2}-q}\hat{u}\rVert_{L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}^{\frac{p}{p+q}}.

∎

Lemma A.2
