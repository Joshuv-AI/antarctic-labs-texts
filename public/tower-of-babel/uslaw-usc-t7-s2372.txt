7 U.S.C. § 2372 — Payment of plant variety protection fees; return of excess amounts
Citation: us:usc:7:2372
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2372
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 57 (Plant Variety Protection); Subchapter I (Plant Variety Protection Office); Part C (Plant Variety Protection Fees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All fees shall be paid to the Secretary, and the Secretary may refund any sum paid by mistake or in excess of the fee required.
