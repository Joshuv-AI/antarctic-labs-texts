30 C.F.R. § 580.3 — What requirements must I follow when I conduct prospecting or research activities?
Citation: us:cfr:30:580.3
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-580.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 580 (Prospecting For Minerals Other Than Oil, Gas, And Sulphur On The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must conduct G&G prospecting activities or scientific research activities under this part according to:
(a) The Act;
(b) The regulations in this part;
(c) Orders of the Director/Regional Director (RD); and
(d) Other applicable statutes, regulations, and amendments.
