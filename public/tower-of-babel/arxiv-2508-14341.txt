Abstract
In this paper, we classify the homotopy types of the total spaces of S 2 ​ k − 1 S^{2k-1} -bundles (or fibrations) over S 2 ​ k S^{2k} for 2 ≤ k ≤ 6 2\leq k\leq 6 by listing the attaching map of the top cell of these as CW-complexes. The method is using the new necessary and sufficient conditions we gave in our previous work to determine whether a CW complex has the homotopy type of total space of these sphere bundles.
