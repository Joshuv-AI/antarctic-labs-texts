30 C.F.R. § 57.22205 — Doors on main fans (I-A, II-A, III, and V-A mines).
Citation: us:cfr:30:57.22205
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-57.22205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 57 (Safety And Health Standards—Underground Metal And Nonmetal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In mines ventilated by multiple main fans, each main fan installation shall be equipped with noncombustible doors. Such doors shall automatically close to prevent air reversal through the fan. The doors shall be located so that they are not in direct line with explosive forces which could come out of the mine.
