Computational hardness of estimating quantum entropies via binary entropy bounds
Yupan Liu

Abstract
We investigate the computational hardness of estimating the quantum α \alpha -Rényi entropy S α 𝚁 ​ ( ρ ) = ln ⁡ Tr ⁡ ( ρ α ) 1 − α \mathrm{S}^{\mathtt{R}}_{\alpha}(\rho)=\frac{\ln\mathrm{Tr}(\rho^{\alpha})}{1-\alpha} and the quantum q q -Tsallis entropy S q 𝚃 ​ ( ρ ) = 1 − Tr ⁡ ( ρ q ) q − 1 \mathrm{S}^{\mathtt{T}}_{q}(\rho)=\frac{1-\mathrm{Tr}(\rho^{q})}{q-1} , both converging to the von Neumann entropy as the order approaches 1 1 . The promise problems Quantum α \alpha -Rényi Entropy Approximation ( RényiQEA α ) and Quantum q q -Tsallis Entropy Approximation ( TsallisQEA q ) ask whether S α 𝚁 ​ ( ρ ) \mathrm{S}^{\mathtt{R}}_{\alpha}(\rho) or S q 𝚃 ​ ( ρ ) \mathrm{S}^{\mathtt{T}}_{q}(\rho) , respectively, is at least τ 𝚈 \tau_{\mathtt{Y}} or at most τ 𝙽 \tau_{\mathtt{N}} , where τ 𝚈 − τ 𝙽 \tau_{\mathtt{Y}}-\tau_{\mathtt{N}} is typically a positive constant. Previous hardness results cover only the von Neumann entropy (order 1 1 ) and some cases of the quantum q q -Tsallis entropy, while existing approaches do not readily extend to other orders. We establish that for all positive real orders, the rank- 2 2 variants Rank2RényiQEA α and Rank2TsallisQEA q are BQP -hard. Combined with prior (rank-dependent) quantum query algorithms in Wang, Guan, Liu, Zhang, and Ying (TIT 2024) , Wang, Zhang, and Li (TIT 2024) , and Liu and Wang (SODA 2025) , our results imply: • For all real orders α > 0 \alpha>0 and 0 < q ≤ 1 0<q\leq 1 , LowRankRényiQEA α and LowRankTsallisQEA q are BQP -complete, where both are restricted versions of RényiQEA α and TsallisQEA q with ρ \rho of polynomial rank. • For all real order q > 1 q>1 , TsallisQEA q is BQP -complete. Our hardness results stem from reductions based on new inequalities relating the α \alpha -Rényi or q q -Tsallis binary entropies of different orders, where the reductions differ substantially from previous approaches, and the inequalities are also of independent interest.

Previous approaches to establishing computational hardness

Before presenting the proof techniques underlying

Our method is guided by two key observations. The first observation is the following identity: the quantum

1.4
Discussion and open problems

Perhaps the most intriguing open problem is the following – what are the limitations of our new approach for establishing the computational hardness of estimating quantum entropies? In particular, can one prove the hardness of the

\alpha

\alpha

8

2.1

The

2.1
Binary entropies.

The
q
-Tsallis binary entropy
\mathrm{H}^{\mathtt{T}}_{q}(x)
and the
\alpha
-Rényi binary entropy

are defined by: for any

\displaystyle\mathrm{H}^{\mathtt{T}}_{q}(x)

\displaystyle\coloneqq\frac{1-x^{q}-(1-x)^{q}}{q-1}=-x^{q}\ln_{q}(x)-(1-x)^{q}\ln_{q}(1-x),

\displaystyle\mathrm{H}^{\mathtt{R}}_{\alpha}(x)

\displaystyle\coloneq\frac{\ln\left\lparen x^{\alpha}+(1-x)^{\alpha}\right\rparen}{1-\alpha}.

The (Shannon) binary entropy arises as a limiting case of both the

-Tsallis binary entropy and the

\mathrm{H}^{\mathtt{T}}_{1}(x)=\mathrm{H}^{\mathtt{R}}_{1}(x)=\mathrm{H}(x)\coloneqq-x\ln{x}-(1-x)\ln(1-x),

and
\mathrm{H}^{\mathtt{R}}_{1}(x)\coloneqq\lim_{\alpha\rightarrow 1}\mathrm{H}^{\mathtt{R}}_{\alpha}(x)
.
The min binary entropy also arises as a limiting case of the
\alpha
-Rényi binary entropy as

\mathrm{H}^{\mathtt{R}}_{\infty}(x)=\mathrm{H}_{\infty}(x)\coloneqq-\ln\left\lparen\max\{x,1-x\}\right\rparen,\;\;\text{where}\;\;\mathrm{H}^{\mathtt{R}}_{\infty}(x)\coloneqq\lim_{\alpha\to\infty}\mathrm{H}^{\mathtt{R}}_{\alpha}(x).

The following holds:

(1)

2.7
Sign conditions for generalized binomial coefficients.

For any real number
a>0
and integer

, the generalized binomial coefficient
\binom{a}{2k}\geq 0
if and only if the integer
\max\{0,2k-\lceil a\rceil\}
is even.

Noting that
