34 C.F.R. § 76.652 — Consultation with representatives of private school students.
Citation: us:cfr:34:76.652
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-76.652
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 76 (State-Administered Formula Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A subgrantee must consult with appropriate private school officials in accordance with the requirements in § 299.7.
