36 U.S.C. § 153303 — Powers
Citation: us:usc:36:153303
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section153303
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1533 (National Society Of The Sons Of The American Revolution). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation may—
(1)
adopt and amend a constitution, bylaws, and regulations for the admission, government, suspension, and expulsion of its members;
(2)
adopt and alter a seal;
(3)
provide for the election of its officers and define their duties;
(4)
provide for State societies or chapters with regulations for their conduct, and regulate and provide for the management, safe-keeping, and protection of their property and funds;
(5)
acquire, own, lease, encumber, and transfer property as necessary or convenient to carry out the purposes of the corporation; and
(6)
sue and be sued.
