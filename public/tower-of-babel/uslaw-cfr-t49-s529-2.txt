49 C.F.R. § 529.2 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:49:529.2
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-529.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 529 (Manufacturers Of Multistage Automobiles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to incomplete automobile manufacturers, intermediate manufacturers, and final-stage manufacturers of automobiles that are manufactured in two or more stages.
