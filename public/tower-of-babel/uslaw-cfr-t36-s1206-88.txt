36 C.F.R. § 1206.88 — Does the NHPRC have any liability under a grant?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1206.88
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1206.88
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1206 (National Historical Publications And Records Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No, NARA and the Commission cannot assume any liability for accidents, illnesses, or claims arising out of any work undertaken with the assistance of the grant.
