33 C.F.R. § 150.605 — What are the procedures for reporting a possible workplace safety or health violation at a deepwater port?
Citation: us:cfr:33:150.605
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-150.605
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 150 (Deepwater Ports: Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person may notify the Sector Commander, or the MSU Commander, with COTP and OCMI authority verbally or in writing of:
(a) A possible violation of a regulation in this part; or
(b) A hazardous or unsafe working condition on any deepwater port.
