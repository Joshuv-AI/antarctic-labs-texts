42 U.S.C. § 9820 — Grants to plan economic development and cooperative programs
Citation: us:usc:42:9820
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section9820
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 105 (Community Services Programs); Subchapter I (Community Economic Development); Part D (Supportive Programs And Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In order to facilitate the purposes of this subchapter, the Secretary is authorized to provide financial assistance to any public or private nonprofit agency or organization for planning of community economic development programs and cooperative programs under this subchapter.
