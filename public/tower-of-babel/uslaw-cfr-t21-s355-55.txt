21 C.F.R. § 355.55 — Principal display panel of all fluoride rinse drug products.
Citation: us:cfr:21:355.55
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-355.55
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 355 (Anticaries Drug Products For Over-The-Counter Human Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to the statement of identity required in § 355.50, the following statement shall be prominently placed on the principal display panel: “IMPORTANT: Read directions for proper use.”
