10 U.S.C. § 9307 — Aviation cadets: discharge
Citation: us:usc:10:9307
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9307
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 939 (Separation For Various Reasons). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Air Force may discharge an aviation cadet at any time.
