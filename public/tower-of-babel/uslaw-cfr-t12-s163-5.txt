12 C.F.R. § 163.5 — Securities: Statement of non-insurance.
Citation: us:cfr:12:163.5
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-163.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 163 (Savings Associations—Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every security issued by a Federal savings association must include in its provisions a clear statement that the security is not insured by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation.
