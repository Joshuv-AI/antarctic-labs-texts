36 U.S.C. § 702 — Definition
Citation: us:usc:36:702
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section702
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part A (Observances And Ceremonies); Chapter 7 (Federal Participation In Carl Garner Federal Lands Cleanup Day). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of this chapter, “Federal land management agency” includes—
(1)
the Forest Service of the Department of Agriculture;
(2)
the Bureau of Land Management of the Department of the Interior;
(3)
the National Park Service of the Department of the Interior;
(4)
the Fish and Wildlife Service of the Department of the Interior;
(5)
the Bureau of Reclamation of the Department of the Interior; and
(6)
the Army Corps of Engineers.
