8 C.F.R. § 212.10 — Section 212(k) waiver.
Citation: us:cfr:8:212.10
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-212.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 212 (Documentary Requirements: Nonimmigrants; Waivers; Admission Of Certain Inadmissible Aliens; Parole). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any applicant for admission who is in possession of an immigrant visa, and who is inadmissible under section 212(a)(5)(A) or 212(a)(7)(A)(i) of the Act, may apply at the port of entry for a waiver under section 212(k) of the Act. If the application for waiver is denied, the application may be renewed in removal proceedings before an immigration judge as provided in 8 CFR part 1240.
