24 C.F.R. § 964.14 — HUD policy on partnerships.
Citation: us:cfr:24:964.14
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-964.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 964 (Tenant Participation And Tenant Opportunities In Public Housing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

HUD promotes partnerships between residents and HAs which are an essential component to building, strengthening and improving public housing. Strong partnerships are critical for creating positive changes in lifestyles thus improving the quality of life for public housing residents, and the surrounding community.
