32 C.F.R. § 1804.12 — Designations of authority to hear requests.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1804.12
Creator: National Counterintelligence Center
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1804.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1804 (Access By Historical Researchers And Former Presidential Appointees Pursuant To Section 4.5 Of Executive Order 12958). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director, NACIC has designated the Coordinator, as the NACIC authority to decide requests for historical and former Presidential appointee access under Executive Order 12958 (or successor Orders) and this part.
