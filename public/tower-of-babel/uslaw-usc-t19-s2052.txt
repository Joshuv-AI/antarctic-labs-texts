19 U.S.C. § 2052 — Assistance from other Federal agencies; facilities and personnel
Citation: us:usc:19:2052
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section2052
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 9 (Visual And Auditory Materials Of Educational, Scientific, And Cultural Character). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Agencies of the Federal Government are authorized to furnish facilities and personnel for the purpose of assisting the agency or agencies designated by the President in carrying out the provisions of the Agreement.
