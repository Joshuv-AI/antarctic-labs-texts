11 U.S.C. § 1167 — Collective bargaining agreements
Citation: us:usc:11:1167
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section1167
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 11 (Reorganization); Subchapter IV (Railroad Reorganization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding section 365 of this title, neither the court nor the trustee may change the wages or working conditions of employees of the debtor established by a collective bargaining agreement that is subject to the Railway Labor Act except in accordance with section 6 of such Act.
