34 U.S.C. § 10305 — Discontinuation for unsatisfactory conduct or progress
Citation: us:usc:34:10305
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section10305
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 101 (Justice System Improvement); Subchapter XI (Public Safety Officers’ Death Benefits); Part B (Educational Assistance To Dependents Of Civilian Federal Law Enforcement Officers Killed Or Disabled In Line Of Duty). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General may discontinue assistance under this part when the Attorney General finds that, according to the regularly prescribed standards and practices of the educational institution, the recipient fails to maintain satisfactory progress as described in section 1091(c) of title 20.
