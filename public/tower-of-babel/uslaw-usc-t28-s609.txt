28 U.S.C. § 609 — Courts’ appointive power unaffected
Citation: us:usc:28:609
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section609
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part III (Court Officers And Employees); Chapter 41 (Administrative Office Of United States Courts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The authority of the courts to appoint their own administrative or clerical personnel shall not be limited by any provisions of this chapter.
