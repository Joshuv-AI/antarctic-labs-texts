18 U.S.C. § 2382 — Misprision of treason
Citation: us:usc:18:2382
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section2382
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 115 (Treason, Sedition, And Subversive Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever, owing allegiance to the United States and having knowledge of the commission of any treason against them, conceals and does not, as soon as may be, disclose and make known the same to the President or to some judge of the United States, or to the governor or to some judge or justice of a particular State, is guilty of misprision of treason and shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than seven years, or both.
