16 U.S.C. § 161b — Designation for purposes of administration, promotion, development, and support
Citation: us:usc:16:161b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section161b
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter XX (Glacier National Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of administration, promotion, development, and support by appropriations that part of the said Waterton-Glacier International Peace Park within the territory of the United States shall be designated as the Glacier National Park.
