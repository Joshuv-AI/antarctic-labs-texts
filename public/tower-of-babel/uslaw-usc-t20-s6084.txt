20 U.S.C. § 6084 — Preemption
Citation: us:usc:20:6084
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section6084
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 68 (National Education Reform); Subchapter X (Miscellaneous); Part B (Environmental Tobacco Smoke). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this part is intended to preempt any provision of law of a State or political subdivision of a State that is more restrictive than a provision of this part.
