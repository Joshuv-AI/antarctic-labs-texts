19 U.S.C. § 81d — Customs officers and guards
Citation: us:usc:19:81d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section81d
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 1A (Foreign Trade Zones). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Treasury shall assign to the zone the necessary customs officers and guards to protect the revenue and to provide for the admission of foreign merchandise into customs territory.
