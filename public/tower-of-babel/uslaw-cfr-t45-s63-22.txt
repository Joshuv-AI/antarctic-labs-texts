45 C.F.R. § 63.22 — Cost sharing.
Citation: us:cfr:45:63.22
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-63.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 63 (Grant Programs Administered By The Office Of The Assistant Secretary For Planning And Evaluation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Policy Research funds shall not be used to pay any recipient of a grant for the conduct of a research project an amount equal to as much as the entire cost of the project.
