13 C.F.R. § 121.602 — At what point in time must a 8(a) BD applicant be small?
Citation: us:cfr:13:121.602
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-121.602
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 121 (Small Business Size Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A 8(a) BD applicant must be small for its primary industry at the time SBA certifies it for admission into the program.
