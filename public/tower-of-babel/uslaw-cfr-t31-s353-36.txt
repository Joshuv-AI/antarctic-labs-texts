31 C.F.R. § 353.36 — Payment during life of sole owner.
Citation: us:cfr:31:353.36
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-353.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 353 (Regulations Governing Definitive United States Savings Bonds, Series Ee And Hh). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A savings bond registered in single ownership form (i.e., without a coowner or beneficiary) will be paid to the owner during his or her lifetime upon surrender with an appropriate request.
