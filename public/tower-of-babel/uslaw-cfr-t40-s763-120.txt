40 C.F.R. § 763.120 — What is the purpose of this subpart?
Citation: us:cfr:40:763.120
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-763.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 763 (Asbestos). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart protects certain State and local government employees who are not protected by the Asbestos Standards of the Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA). This subpart applies the OSHA Asbestos Standards in 29 CFR 1910.1001 and 29 CFR 1926.1101 to these employees.
