22 U.S.C. § 9125 — Termination of actions by the Secretary of State
Citation: us:usc:22:9125
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section9125
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 98 (International Child Abduction Prevention And Return); Subchapter II (Actions By The Secretary Of State). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any specific action taken under this Act or any amendment made by this Act with respect to a foreign country shall terminate on the date on which the Secretary of State submits a written certification to Congress that the government of such country—
(1)
has resolved any unresolved abduction case that gave rise to such specific action; or
(2)
has taken substantial and verifiable steps to correct such country’s persistent pattern of noncompliance that gave rise to such specific action, as applicable.
