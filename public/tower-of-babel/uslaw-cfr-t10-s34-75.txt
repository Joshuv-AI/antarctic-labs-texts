10 C.F.R. § 34.75 — Records of alarm system and entrance control checks at permanent radiographic installations.
Citation: us:cfr:10:34.75
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-34.75
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 34 (Licenses For Industrial Radiography And Radiation Safety Requirements For Industrial Radiographic Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each licensee shall maintain records of alarm system and entrance control device tests required under § 34.33 and retain each record for 3 years after it is made.
