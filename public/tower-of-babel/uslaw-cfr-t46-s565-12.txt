46 C.F.R. § 565.12 — Stay, postponement, discontinuance or suspension of action.
Citation: us:cfr:46:565.12
Creator: Federal Maritime Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-565.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 565 (Controlled Carriers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission may, on its own motion or upon petition, postpone, discontinue, or suspend any and all actions taken by it under the provisions of this part. The Commission shall immediately stay the effect of any order issued under this part as requested by the President pursuant to § 565.11.
