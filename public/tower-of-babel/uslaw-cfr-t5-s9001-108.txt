5 C.F.R. § 9001.108 — Prohibited recommendations.
Citation: us:cfr:5:9001.108
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-9001.108
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 9001 (Supplemental Standards Of Ethical Conduct For Employees Of The Federal Housing Finance Agency). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees shall not make any recommendation or suggestion, directly or indirectly, concerning the acquisition, sale, or divestiture of securities of a regulated entity.
