49 C.F.R. § 601.42 — Emergency relief docket.
Citation: us:cfr:49:601.42
Creator: Federal Transit Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-601.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 601 (Organization, Functions, And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) By January 31st of each year, FTA shall establish an Emergency Relief Docket in https://www.regulations.gov.
(b) FTA shall publish a notice in the Federal Register identifying, by docket number, the Emergency Relief Docket for that calendar year. A notice shall also be published in the previous year's Emergency Relief Docket identifying the new docket number.
(c) If the Administrator, or his/her designee, determines that an emergency event has occurred, or in anticipation of such an event, FTA shall place a message on its web page indicating the Emergency Relief Docket has been opened and including the docket number.
