Introduction

We consider simple connected graphs in this paper. We generally follow the book
[
17
]
of Stiebitz et al. for notation and terminologies.
Let
G=(V(G),E(G))
be a simple graph, where
V(G)
is the vertex set, and
E(G)
is the edge set of
G
.
Denote by
\Delta(G)
and
\delta(G)
(or simple
\Delta
and
\delta
) respectively the maximum degree and minimum degree of graph
G
, and by
[k]
the set of first
k
consecutive positive integers
\{1,\dots,k\}
. A (proper)
k
-edge-coloring
of a graph
G
is a map
\varphi:E(G)\to[k]
that assigns to every edge
e
of
G
a color
\varphi(e)\in[k]
such that no two adjacent edges of
G
receive the same color.
Denote by
\mathcal{C}^{k}(G)
the set of all
k
-edge-colorings of
G
.
The
chromatic index

\chi^{\prime}(G)
is the least integer
k
such that
\mathcal{C}^{k}(G)\neq\emptyset
.
A graph
G
is
edge-chromatic critical
if
\chi^{\prime}(H)<\chi^{\prime}(G)
for every proper subgraph
H
of
G
. Furthermore, we call
G
a
\Delta
-critical
graph if
G
is edge-chromatic critical and
\chi^{\prime}(G)=\Delta(G)+1
.

In 1960¡¯s, Vizing
[
19
]
and, independently, Gupta
[
8
]
proved that
\Delta(G)\leq\chi^{\prime}(G)\leq\Delta(G)+1
, which leads to a natural classification of graphs.
Following Fiorini and Wilson
[
7
]
, if
\chi^{\prime}(G)=\Delta(G)
, then
G
is of
Class 1
; otherwise, it is of
Class 2
. If a graph
G
has so many edges with
\left|E(G)\right|>\Delta(G)\lfloor|V(G)|/2\rfloor
, then we need to give the
|E(G)|
edges an edge-coloring that uses at least
\Delta+1
colors.
Such graphs are called
overfull
.
Holyer
[
10
]
proved that it is NP-complete to determine whether an arbitrary graph is of Class
1
.
However, by a conjecture of Chetwynd and Hilton from 1986, there is a polynomial-time algorithm to determine the chromatic index for graphs
G
with
\Delta(G)\textgreater\frac{|V(G)|}{3}
. The conjecture involves the overfullness of graphs. In fact, a number of longstanding conjecture listed in
Twenty Pretty Edge Coloring Conjecture
in
[
17
]
lie in deciding when a graph is overfull. The conjecture of Chetwynd and Hilton in 1986 is stated as follows.

Conjecture 1

(Overfull Conjecture)
.

Let
G
be a graph of Class
2
with
\Delta(G)\textgreater\frac{|V(G)|}{3}
. Then
G
contains an overfull subgraph
H
with
\Delta(H)=\Delta(G)
.

Note that for the graph
P^{*}
obtained from the Petersen graph by deleting one vertex,
\chi^{\prime}(P^{*})=4
and
\Delta(P^{*})=\frac{|V(P^{*})|}{3}
but with no overfull subgraphs.
Thus the degree condition of
\Delta(G)\textgreater\frac{|V(G)|}{3}
in the conjecture above is best possible.
Seymour
[
14
]
showed that whether a graph
G
contains an overfull subgraph with maximum degree
\Delta(G)
can be determined in polynomial time.
Independently, Niessen
[
18
]
in 2001 showed that for graphs
G
with
\Delta(G)\textgreater\frac{|V(G)|}{3}
, there are at most three induced overfull subgraphs with maximum degree
\Delta(G)
, and it is possible to find one in polynomial time in
(|V(G)|)
.
And when
\Delta(G)\geq\frac{|V(G)|}{2}
, there is at most one induced overfull subgraph with maximum degree
\Delta(G)
, and it is possible find one in linear time in
(|V(G)|+|E(G)|)
.
Hence if the Overfull Conjecture is true, then the NP-complete problem of determining the chromatic index becomes polynomial-time solvable for graphs
G
with
\Delta(G)\textgreater\frac{|V(G)|}{3}
.
Furthermore, the Overfull Conjecture can also imply several other longstanding conjectures such as the Just Overfull Conjecture
[
17
]
, the Vertex-splitting Conjecture
[
9
]
, and Vizing’s 2-factor Conjecture, Independence Conjecture and Average Degree Conjecture
[
20
,
21
]
when restricted to graphs
G
with
\Delta(G)\textgreater\frac{|V(G)|}{3}
.

Although the Overfull Conjecture is so important, there are not many direct research results on it.
Chetwynd and Hilton
[
6
]
proved that the conjecture holds when
\Delta(G)\geq|V(G)|-3
.
Recently, Shan
[
16
]
confirmed that for any
0<\varepsilon\leq\frac{1}{14}
, there exists a positive integer
n_{0}
such that if
G
is a graph with order
|V(G)|\geq n_{0}
and
\Delta(G)\geq(1-\varepsilon)|V(G)|
, then the conjecture holds.
There are some other results on the conjecture focusing on minimum degree condition or both of minimum degree and maximum degree conditions.
In 2004, Plantholt
[
11
]
showed that a graph
G
with even order and minimum degree
\delta(G)\geq\frac{\sqrt{7}|V(G)|}{3}
is overfull. In 2022, Plantholt
[
12
]
also obtained the result for large even order
G
with
\delta(G)\geq\frac{2}{3}|V(G)|
. Recently, Plantholt and Shan
[
13
]
showed that for large even order
G
, if its minimum degree is arbitrarily close to
\frac{1}{2}|V(G)|
from above, then the Overfull Conjecture holds; and this result has been extended by Shan
[
15
]
to the same class of graphs with odd order.
Since every Class
2
graph with maximum degree
\Delta
has a
\Delta
-critical subgraph, the Overfull Conjecture is equivalent to stating that
every
\Delta
-critical graph
G
with
\Delta(G)\textgreater\frac{|V(G)|}{3}
is overfull
. Note that
\delta(G)\geq 2
for
\Delta
-critical graph
G
.
In 2022, Cao, Chen, Jing and Shan
[
2
]
demonstrated that the conjecture holds for
n
-vertex
\Delta
-critical graphs when
\Delta(G)-\frac{7\delta(G)}{4}\geq\frac{3n-17}{4}
, which implies the conditions
\Delta(G)\geq\frac{3n-3}{4}
and
\delta(G)\leq\frac{n+1}{7}
(given
\Delta(G)\leq n-4
and
\delta(G)\geq 2
). In this paper, we confirm the result as follows.

Theorem 1
.

Let
G
be an
n
-vertex
\Delta
-critical graph. If
\Delta(G)-\frac{5\delta(G)}{3}\geq\frac{2n-7}{3}
, then
G
is overfull.

Notice that the condition
\Delta(G)-\frac{5\delta(G)}{3}\geq\frac{2n-7}{3}
implies
\Delta(G)\geq\frac{2n+3}{3}
and
\delta(G)\leq\frac{n-5}{5}
(also given
\Delta(G)\leq n-4
and
\delta(G)\geq 2
), which is closer to the Overfull Conjecture’s maximum degree condition, and our result in this paper provides new techniques and ideas for further research on the Overfull Conjecture.

Let

be a graph. For

\chi^{\prime}(G-e)<\chi^{\prime}(G)
. Clearly, if

is edge-chromatic critical. For

, let

in

.

Let

be a graph with an edge

, and an edge coloring
\varphi\in\mathcal{C}^{k}(G-e)
for some integer

. For a vertex

, let
\varphi(u)
denote the set of colors assigned to edges incident with

, and
\overline{\varphi}(u)=[k]\setminus\varphi(u)
, i.e., the set of colors not assigned to any edge incident with

. We call
\varphi(u)
the set of colors
present
at
u
and
\overline{\varphi}(u)
the set of colors
missing
at
u
. Clearly,

|\varphi(u)|+|\overline{\varphi}(u)|=k
for each vertex

.
For a vertex set
X\subseteq V(G)

\overline{\varphi}(X)=\bigcup_{u\in X}\overline{\varphi}(u)
.
The set

is called elementary with respect to
\varphi
or simply
\varphi

\overline{\varphi}(u)\cap\overline{\varphi}(v)=\emptyset
for every two distinct vertices

For a color

, let

denote the set of edges colored with
\alpha
.
Let
\alpha,\beta\in[k]
be two distinct colors, and

be the spanning subgraph induced by
E_{\varphi,\alpha}(G)
and
E_{\varphi,\beta}(G)
.
Clearly, every component of
H
is either a path or an even cycle which are referred as
(\alpha,\beta)
-chains
of
G
.
If we interchange the colors
\alpha
and
\beta
on
(\alpha,\beta)
-chain

, then we obtain a new (proper)
k
-edge-coloring of
G
, which is also in
\mathcal{C}^{k}(G-e)
.
This operation is called a

.
Furthermore, we say that a chain

has endvertices
u
and
v
if
C
is a path joining vertices
u
and
v
.
For a vertex
u
of
G
, we denote by

or simply

the unique
(\alpha,\beta)
-chain containing the vertex

.

For two vertices

,

, the two chains

and

are either identical or disjoint
, which is an important fact in our proofs.
More generally, for an
(\alpha,\beta)
-chain, if it is a path and it contains two vertices

a
and
b
, we let
P_{[a,b]}(\alpha,\beta,\varphi)
be its subchain with endvertices
a
and
b
.
The operation of interchanging colors
\alpha
and
\beta
on the subchain
P_{[a,b]}(\alpha,\beta,\varphi)
is still called a Kempe change, but the resulting coloring may no longer be a proper edge coloring.
In particular, we may change the color of one edge in our proof and adopt the notation
uv:\alpha\rightarrow\beta
to mean ¡°recolor the edge
uv
from
\alpha
to
\beta
¡±.
If
x,y\in P_{u}(\alpha,\beta,\varphi)
with

positioned between

and

on the path

meets

.

Definition 1

(Multi-fan)
.

Let

be a graph with an edge

, and an edge coloring
\varphi\in\mathcal{C}^{k}(G-e)

.
A multi-fan at

\varphi
is a sequence

with

F1.

The edges

Let

3
The proof of Theorem 1

Proof.

Since the Overfull Conjecture is true for
\Delta(G)\geq n-3
, we assume that
\Delta(G)\leq n-4
.
Since
\Delta(G)-\frac{5\delta(G)}{3}\geq\frac{2n-7}{3}
, it follows that
\delta(G)\leq\frac{n-5}{5}
.
Note that
\delta(G)\geq 2

\Delta
-critical. Then we have

.
Firstly, we select one vertex

with

, and then let

.
Thus, for every

First we suppose that there exist two distinct vertices

.

, both of

and
