20 C.F.R. § 801.304 — Business hours.
Citation: us:cfr:20:801.304
Creator: Benefits Review Board, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-801.304
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 801 (Establishment And Operation Of The Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The office of the Clerk of the Board at Washington, DC shall be open from 8:30 a.m.-5:00 p.m. on all days, except Saturdays, Sundays, and legal holidays, for the purpose of receiving notices of appeal, petitions for review, other pleadings, motions, and other papers.
