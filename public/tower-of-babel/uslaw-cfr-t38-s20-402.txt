38 C.F.R. § 20.402 — Rule 402. Time limits for filing Notice of Disagreement in simultaneously contested claims.
Citation: us:cfr:38:20.402
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-20.402
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 20 (Board Of Veterans' Appeals: Rules Of Practice). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In simultaneously contested claims, the Notice of Disagreement from the person adversely affected must be filed within 60 days from the date of mailing of the notification of the determination to him or her; otherwise, that determination will become final. The date of mailing of the letter of notification will be presumed to be the same as the date of that letter for purposes of determining whether a Notice of Disagreement has been timely filed.
