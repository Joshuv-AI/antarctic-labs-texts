40 C.F.R. § 267.76 — What notifications must I make?
Citation: us:cfr:40:267.76
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-267.76
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 267 (Standards For Owners And Operators Of Hazardous Waste Facilities Operating Under A Standardized Permit). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before transferring ownership or operation of a facility during its operating life, you must notify the new owner or operator in writing of the requirements of this part and 40 CFR part 270, subpart J.
