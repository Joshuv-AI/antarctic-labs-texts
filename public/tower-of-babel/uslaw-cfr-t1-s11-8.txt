1 C.F.R. § 11.8 — LSA (List of CFR Sections Affected).
Citation: us:cfr:1:11.8
Creator: Administrative Committee Of The Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-1/section-11.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 1 (General Provisions), Part 11 (Subscriptions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-23 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The annual subscription price for the monthly LSA (List of CFR Sections Affected), purchased separately, in paper form, is $30. The price excludes postage. The prevailing postal rates will be applied to orders according to the delivery method requested.
