Following publication of the original article [1], captions for Figs. 1 and 3; Table 3 have been updated.

The caption for Figure 1 has been updated from:

Fig. 1 The number of accumulated counts from 50 patient scans, each consisting of four10 min. scans; scan 1, 2,3 and 4.

To:

Fig. 1 Striatal SBR for the 10-, 20- and 30- min. scans (from left to right) versus 40-min. scans of patients (blue) and controls (red). Data from a healthy control with SBR > 6 has been removed to allow for a better data separation. The black line is the line of identity. The dotted black line represents the fitted linear equation for the patient group.

The caption for Figure 3 has been updated from:

Fig. 3 Mean SBR-difference for striatum, putamen and caudatus nucleus with 95% confidence intervals (CI) for all subjects

To:

Fig 3 Representative examples for two patients with 10-, 20-,30-, and 40 min. scans. Top row: The 10 min scan was rated as insufficient due to the noisy background and having the barbell-shape of the striatum. The 20, 30, and 40 min. scans were rated as sufficient, demonstrating the typical normal comma-shape with well-deliniated borders. Bottom row: The 10-, 20-, and 30-minute scans were rated as insufficient due to noisy background activity and barell shape of the striatum. The 40-minute scan was rated as sufficient, showing mild asymmetric dot-shape pattern with homogeneous background activity.

To:
