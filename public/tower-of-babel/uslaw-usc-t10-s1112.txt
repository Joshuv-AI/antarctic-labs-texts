10 U.S.C. § 1112 — Assets of Fund
Citation: us:usc:10:1112
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1112
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 56 (Department Of Defense Medicare-Eligible Retiree Health Care Fund). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There shall be deposited into the Fund the following, which shall constitute the assets of the Fund:
(1)
Amounts paid into the Fund under section 1116 of this title.
(2)
Any amount appropriated to the Fund.
(3)
Any return on investment of the assets of the Fund.
(4)
Amounts paid into the Fund pursuant to section 1111(c) of this title.
