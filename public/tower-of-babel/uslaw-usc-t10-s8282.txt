10 U.S.C. § 8282 — Insane members of the naval service
Citation: us:usc:10:8282
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8282
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 833 (Hospitalization And Medical Care). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A member of the naval service who becomes insane may be placed in the hospital for the insane that, in the opinion of the Secretary of the Navy, is most convenient and will provide the most beneficial treatment.
