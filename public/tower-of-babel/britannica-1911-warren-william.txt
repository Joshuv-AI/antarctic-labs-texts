WARREN, WILLIAM (1812–1888), American actor, was born
in Philadelphia on the 17th of November 1812, the son of an
English actor (1767–1832) of the same name. His first stage
appearance was made there as Young Norval in Home’s Douglas in 1832. A dozen years of wandering theatrical life followed, giving him a wide experience in every kind of part, the last few
in comedy in a company headed by his brother-in-law, J. B. Rice. In 1846 he made his first appearance in Boston as Sir Lucius
O’Trigger in The Rivals at the Howard Athenaeum, and in the next season he became a member of the Boston Museum, in which stock company he remained for thirty-five years. Here he held his “Golden Jubilee” on the 28th of October 1882, He died on the 21st of September 1888.
