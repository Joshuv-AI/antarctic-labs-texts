22 U.S.C. § 9654 — Publicly available project information
Citation: us:usc:22:9654
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section9654
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 103 (Better Utilization Of Investments Leading To Development); Subchapter IV (Monitoring, Evaluation, And Reporting). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Corporation shall—
(1)
maintain a user-friendly, publicly available, machine-readable database with detailed project-level information, as appropriate and to the extent practicable, including a description of the support provided by the Corporation under subchapter II of this chapter, which shall include, to the greatest extent feasible for each project—
(A)
the information included in the report to Congress under section 9653 of this title;
(B)
project-level performance metrics; and
(C)
a description of the development impact of the project, including anticipated impact prior to initiation of the project and assessed impact during and after the completion of the project; and
(2)
include a clear link to information about each project supported by the Corporation under subchapter II of this chapter on the internet website of the Department of State, “ForeignAssistance.gov”, or a successor website or other online publication.
