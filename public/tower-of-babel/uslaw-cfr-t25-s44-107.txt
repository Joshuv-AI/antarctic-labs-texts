25 C.F.R. § 44.107 — Under what circumstances may the Secretary reassume a program?
Citation: us:cfr:25:44.107
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-44.107
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 44 (Grants Under The Tribally Controlled Schools Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary may only reassume a program in compliance with 25 U.S.C. 450m and 25 CFR part 900, subpart P. The tribe or school board shall have a right to appeal the reassumption pursuant to 25 CFR part 900, subpart L.
