2 U.S.C. § 6111 — Compensation of President pro tempore of Senate
Citation: us:usc:2:6111
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section6111
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 61 (Senate Leadership); Subchapter II (Vice President And President Pro Tempore). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whenever there is no Vice President, the President of the Senate for the time being is entitled to the compensation provided by law for the Vice President.
