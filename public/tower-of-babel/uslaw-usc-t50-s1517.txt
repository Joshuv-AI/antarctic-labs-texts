50 U.S.C. § 1517 — Immediate disposal when health or safety are endangered
Citation: us:usc:50:1517
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section1517
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 32 (Chemical And Biological Warfare Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing contained in this section 1 [50 U.S.C. 1512, 1513–1515, 1517] shall be deemed to restrict the transportation or disposal of research quantities of any lethal chemical or any biological warfare agent, or to delay or prevent, in emergency situations either within or outside the United States, the immediate disposal together with any necessary associated transportation, of any lethal chemical or any biological warfare agent when compliance with the procedures and requirements of this section 1 would clearly endanger the health or safety of any person.
