25 C.F.R. § 31.2 — Use of Federal school facilities.
Citation: us:cfr:25:31.2
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-31.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 31 (Federal Schools For Indians). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Federal Indian school facilities may be used for community activities and for adult education activities upon approval by the superintendent or officer in charge.
