48 C.F.R. § 214.209 — 214.209 Cancellation of invitations before opening.
Citation: us:cfr:48:214.209
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-214.209
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 214 (Sealed Bidding). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an invitation for bids allowed fewer than 30 days for receipt of offers, and resulted in only one offer, the contracting officer shall cancel and resolicit, allowing an additional period of at least 30 days for receipt of offers, as provided in 215.371.
