12 C.F.R. § 1013.8 — Record retention.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1013.8
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1013.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1013 (Consumer Leasing (Regulation M)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A lessor shall retain evidence of compliance with the requirements imposed by this part, other than the advertising requirements under § 1013.7, for a period of not less than two years after the date the disclosures are required to be made or an action is required to be taken.
