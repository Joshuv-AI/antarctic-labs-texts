41 C.F.R. § 302-17.2 — -17.2 Eligibility for the WTA and the RITA.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-17.2
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-17.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-17 (17—Taxes On Relocation Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees are eligible for the WTA and the RITA if-
(a) WTA and RITA are listed under their type of move at §§ 302-3.1 and 302-3.100 of this chapter;
(b) They are relocating in the interest of the Government; and
(c) The agency's reimbursements to the employee for relocation expenses result in the employee being liable for additional income taxes.
