36 C.F.R. § 218.15 — Information collection requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:36:218.15
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-218.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 218 (Project-Level Predecisional Administrative Review Process). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules of this part specify the information that objectors must provide in an objection to a proposed project (see § 218.8). As such, these rules contain information collection requirements as defined in 5 CFR part 1320. These information requirements are assigned OMB Control Number 0596-0172.
