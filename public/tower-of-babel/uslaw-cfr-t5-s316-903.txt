5 C.F.R. § 316.903 — Qualifications.
Citation: us:cfr:5:316.903
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-316.903
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 316 (Temporary And Term Employment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies must evaluate eligible post-secondary students using the government-wide OPM prescribed qualification standard or an OPM-approved agency-specific qualification standard for the position being filled.
