42 U.S.C. § 9312 — Authorization of appropriations; contract authority
Citation: us:usc:42:9312
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section9312
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 101 (Magnetic Fusion Energy Engineering). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
There is hereby authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary, for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1981, such sums as are provided in the annual authorization Act pursuant to section 7270 of this title.
(b)
In carrying out the provisions of this chapter, the Secretary is authorized to enter into contracts only to such extent or in such amounts as may be provided in advance in appropriations Acts.
