48 C.F.R. § 47.305-14 — 47.305-14 Mode of transportation.
Citation: us:cfr:48:47.305-14
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-47.305-14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 47 (Transportation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Generally, solicitations shall not specify a particular mode of transportation or a particular carrier. If the use of particular types of carriers is necessary to meet program requirements, the solicitation shall provide that only offers involving the specified types of carriers will be considered. The contracting officer shall obtain all specifications for mode, route, delivery, etc., from the transportation office.
