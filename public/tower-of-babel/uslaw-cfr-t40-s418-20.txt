40 C.F.R. § 418.20 — Applicability; description of the ammonia subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:418.20
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-418.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 418 (Fertilizer Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges resulting from the manufacture of ammonia. Discharges attributable to shipping losses and cooling tower blowdown are excluded.
