Abstract
. In this paper, we introduce three invariants of virtual knotoids. Two of them are smoothing invariants ℱ \mathcal{F} and ℒ \mathcal{L} which take values in a free ℤ \mathbb{Z} -module generated by non-oriented flat virtual knotoids. We prove that ℱ \mathcal{F} and ℒ \mathcal{L} are both Vassiliev invariants of order one. Moreover we construct a universal Vassiliev invariant 𝒢 \mathcal{G} of order one of virtual knotoids. We demonstrate that 𝒢 \mathcal{G} is stronger than ℱ \mathcal{F} and ℒ \mathcal{L} . To prove this result, we extend the singular based matrix invariant for singular virtual strings introduced by Turaev and Henrich to singular virtual open strings corresponding to singular flat virtual knotoids with one singular crossing.

1.
Introduction

In 2012, Turaev introduced knotoid diagram, defining it as an open-ended topological diagram in
S^{2}
(or
\mathbb{R}^{2}
), which is a generic immersion of the unit interval with finitely many double points. Knotoids are defined to be equivalent classes of knotoid diagrams modulo the Reidemeister moves applied away from the endpoints
[
Tur1
]
. Knotoid theory can be considered as a generalization of classical knot theory. Gügümcü and Kauffman later generalized knotoids to virtual knotoids by adding virtual crossings in
[
GK
]
. In recent years, there have been numerous studies on invariants of knotoids, see, for example,
[
Tar
,
FLV
]
.

In 2013, Kauffman defined the affine index polynomial invariant of virtual knots in
[
Kau
]
. Kim further defined the affine index polynomial invariant for flat virtual knots in
[
Kim
]
. In 2017, Gügümcü and Kauffman defined the affine index polynomial invariants for knotoids and virtual knotoids in
[
GK
]
.

A history and theory of Vassiliev invariants are well-presented in
[
CDM
]
. We only recall that a notion of a finite type invariant was introduced by Vassiliev in the end of the 1980’s, see
[
Vas1
,
Vas2
]
. In
[
BL
]
Birman and Lin realized Vassiliev’s approach, established the relation between the Jones polynomial and finite type invariants and emphasized the algebra of chord diagrams (called circular configurations in the paper). Bar-Natan described the theory of knot invariants of finite type (Vassiliev invariants) in
[
BN
]
. Goussarov, Polyak and Viro discussed finite type invariants of classical and virtual knots in
[
GPV
]
. It is known
[
CDM
, Ch 3.3]
that no non-trivial Vassiliev invariants of order one exist for classical knots. This property fails in the case of virtual knots, since Sawollek
[
Saw
]
constructed Vassiliev invariants of order one distinguishing virtual knots from their inverses. Im, Kim, and Lee
[
IKL
]
introduced a Vassiliev invariant for virtual knots and flat virtual knots. Subsequent studies have also been conducted on the Vassiliev invariants of virtual knots. Moltmaker and Kauffman discussed Vassiliev invariants for virtual knots in
[
MK
]
.

In
[
Tch
]
, Tchernov introduced the universal Vassiliev invariant of order one of knots. Subsequently, Henrich
[
Hen
]
and Petit
[
Pet
]
extended finite-type invariants of order one and their universality to virtual knots, and framed virtual knots, respectively. In particular, Henrich provided a construction giving a “gluing invariant” that is the universal Vassiliev invariant of order one for virtual knots. As noted in
[
MLK
, Remark 7.9]
, one can adapt this construction for knotoids by considering the virtual closure of the knotoid.

Cheng
[
Che
]
established the chord index axiom, assigning integers to classical crossings via arc weights to generate invariants for virtual knots. In
[
CGX
]
, Cheng, Gao and Xu defined chord indices via smoothing classical crossings, yielding Vassiliev invariants valued in flat virtual knot diagrams. Moreover, Gill, Ivanov, Prabhakar and Vesnin introduced weight function, which may be thought of as a generalization of the chord index axioms in
[
GIPV
]
.

In this paper we study Vassiliev invariants for virtual knotoids. In Section
3
we construct two polynomials
\mathcal{F}
and
\mathcal{L}
of virtual knotoids, which are valued in the free
\mathbb{Z}
-module generated by flat virtual non-orientable knotoids and prove that
\mathcal{F}
and
\mathcal{L}
are virtual knotoid invariants, see Theorem
3.1
. Furthermore, we prove that
\mathcal{F}
and
\mathcal{L}
are Vassiliev invariants of order one, see Theorem
3.3
and Theorem
3.4
. Moreover, we construct another invariant
\mathcal{G}
of virtual knotoids using the flat singular virtual knotoids and prove that
\mathcal{G}
is a universal invariant of order one, see Theorem
3.5
. Then, we prove that
\mathcal{G}
is stronger than
\mathcal{F}
and
\mathcal{L}
, see Theorem
3.6
.
