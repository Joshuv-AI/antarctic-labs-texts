43 U.S.C. § 2101 — Findings
Citation: us:usc:43:2101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section2101
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 39 (Abandoned Shipwrecks). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Congress finds that—
(a)
States have the responsibility for management of a broad range of living and nonliving resources in State waters and submerged lands; and
(b)
included in the range of resources are certain abandoned shipwrecks, which have been deserted and to which the owner has relinquished ownership rights with no retention.
