32 C.F.R. § 631.7 — Composition of boards.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 631 (Armed Forces Disciplinary Control Boards And Off-Installation Liaison And Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Boards should be structured according to the needs of the command, with consideration given to including representatives from the following functional areas—
(1) Law enforcement.
(2) Legal counsel.
(3) Health.
(4) Environmental protection.
(5) Public affairs.
(6) Equal opportunity.
(7) Fire and safety.
(8) Chaplains' service.
(9) Alcohol and drug abuse.
(10) Personnel and community activities.
(11) Consumer affairs.
(b) Sponsoring commanders will designate a board president, and determine by position which board members will be voting members. Such designations will be included in a written agreement establishing the board.
