30 U.S.C. § 28i — Failure to pay
Citation: us:usc:30:28i
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section28i
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 2 (Mineral Lands And Regulations In General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Failure to pay the claim maintenance fee or the location fee as required by sections 28f to 28l of this title shall conclusively constitute a forfeiture of the unpatented mining claim, mill or tunnel site by the claimant and the claim shall be deemed null and void by operation of law.
