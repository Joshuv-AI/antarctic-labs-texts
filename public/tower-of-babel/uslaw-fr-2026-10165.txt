State of Indiana: NRC Staff Assessment of a Proposed Agreement 
Between the Nuclear Regulatory Commission and the State of Indiana; 
Correction

AGENCY: Nuclear Regulatory Commission.

ACTION: Proposed state agreement; request for comment; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) is correcting a 
notice that was published in the Federal Register on May 15, 2026, 
regarding the State of Indiana: NRC Staff Assessment of a Proposed 
Agreement Between the Nuclear Regulatory Commission and the State of 
Indiana, proposed state agreement and request for comment. This action 
is necessary to change the designation of the Draft Staff Assessment of 
the Proposed Indiana Program, dated April 10, 2026, from non-publicly 
available to publicly available.

DATES: The correction takes effect on May 21, 2026.
