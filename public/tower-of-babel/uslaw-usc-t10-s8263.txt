10 U.S.C. § 8263 — Emergency shore duty: advance of funds
Citation: us:usc:10:8263
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8263
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 831 (Miscellaneous Rights And Benefits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Under such regulations as the President approves, the Secretary of the Navy may, to meet necessary expenses, advance funds to members of the naval service detailed on emergency shore duty. The funds advanced may not exceed the reasonable estimate of expenses to be incurred for which reimbursement is authorized.
