54 U.S.C. § 308905 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:54:308905
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section308905
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3089 (Save America’S Treasures Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out this chapter $50,000,000 for each fiscal year, to remain available until expended.
