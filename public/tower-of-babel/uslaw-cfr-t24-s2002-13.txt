24 C.F.R. § 2002.13 — Fee schedule, advance payment, interest charges, and waiving or reducing fees.
Citation: us:cfr:24:2002.13
Creator: Office Of Inspector General, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-2002.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 2002 (Availability Of Information To The Public). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The OIG will charge for processing requests under the FOIA in accordance with § 15.106 of this title, except where those provisions conflict with provisions of this part; more specifically, where § 15.106 references § 15.103 of this title replace such reference with § 2002.15.
