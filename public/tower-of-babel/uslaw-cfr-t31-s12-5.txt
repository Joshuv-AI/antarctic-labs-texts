31 C.F.R. § 12.5 — Prohibitions not applicable in areas designated by the Secretary of the Treasury.
Citation: us:cfr:31:12.5
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-12.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 12 (Restriction Of Sale And Distribution Of Tobacco Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The prohibitions set forth in this part shall not apply in areas designated by the Secretary as exempt from the prohibitions, but all designated areas must prohibit the presence of minors.
