31 C.F.R. § 9.9 — Report.
Citation: us:cfr:31:9.9
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-9.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 9 (Effects Of Imported Articles On The National Security). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A report will be made and published in the Federal Register upon the disposition of each request, application or motion under § 9.3. Copies of the report will be available at the Office of the Assistant Secretary (Enforcement, Operations, and Tariff Affairs), Department of the Treasury.
