34 C.F.R. § 395.34 — Application for permits.
Citation: us:cfr:34:395.34
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-395.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 395 (Vending Facility Program For The Blind On Federal And Other Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applications for permits for the operation of vending facilities other than cafeterias shall be made in writing on the appropriate form, and submitted for the review and approval of the head of the Federal property managing department, agency, or instrumentality.
