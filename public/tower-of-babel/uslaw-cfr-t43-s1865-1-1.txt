43 C.F.R. § 1865.1-1 — -1 Filing of application.
Citation: us:cfr:43:1865.1-1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-1865.1-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 1860 (Conveyances, Disclaimers And Correction Documents). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Any claimant asserting ownership of lands described in and based upon a patent or other document of conveyance containing an alleged error may file an application to correct the alleged error.
(b) An application shall be filed in writing with the proper Bureau of Land Management office as listed in § 1821.2-1(d) of this title.
