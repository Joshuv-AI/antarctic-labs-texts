32 U.S.C. § 103 — Branches and organizations
Citation: us:usc:32:103
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title32-section103
Context: United States Code, Title 32 (National Guard); Chapter 1 (Organization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Army National Guard of each State, the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico, the District of Columbia, Guam, and the Virgin Islands includes such members of the staff corps corresponding to the staff corps of the Army as the Secretary of the Army may authorize.
