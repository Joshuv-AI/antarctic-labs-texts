18 C.F.R. § 376.104 — Functions.
Citation: us:cfr:18:376.104
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-376.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 376 (Organization, Mission, And Functions; Operations During Emergency Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The functions of the Commission include:
(a) All functions vested in the Commission under the DOE Act;
(b) All functions delegated to the Commission by the Secretary of Energy in accordance with the DOE Act; and
(c) All functions vested in the Commission by statute.
