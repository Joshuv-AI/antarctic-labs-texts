19 C.F.R. § 152.11 — Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States.
Citation: us:cfr:19:152.11
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-152.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 152 (Classification And Appraisement Of Merchandise). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Merchandise shall be classified in accordance with the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (19 U.S.C. 1202) as interpreted by administrative and judicial rulings.
