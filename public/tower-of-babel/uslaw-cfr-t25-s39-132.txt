25 C.F.R. § 39.132 — Can a school integrate Language Development programs into its regular instructional program?
Citation: us:cfr:25:39.132
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-39.132
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 39 (The Indian School Equalization Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A school may offer Language Development programs to students as part of its regular academic program. Language Development does not have to be offered as a stand-alone program.
