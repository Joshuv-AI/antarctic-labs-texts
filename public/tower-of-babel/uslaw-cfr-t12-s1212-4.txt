12 C.F.R. § 1212.4 — Waiver.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1212.4
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1212.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1212 (Post-Employment Restriction For Senior Examiners). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At the written request of a senior examiner or former senior examiner, the Director may waive the post-employment restriction in § 1212.3 if he or she certifies, in writing, and on a case-by-case basis, that granting a waiver of such restriction does not affect the integrity of the supervisory program of FHFA.
