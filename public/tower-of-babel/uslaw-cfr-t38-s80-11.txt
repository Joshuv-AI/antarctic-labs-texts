38 C.F.R. § 80.11 — Payments under the grant.
Citation: us:cfr:38:80.11
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-80.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 80 (Veteran And Spouse Transitional Assistance Grant Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Payments. Grantees are to be paid in accordance with the timeframes and manner set forth in the NOFO.
(b) Availability of grant funds. Federal financial assistance will become available subsequent to the effective date of the grant as set forth in the grant agreement. Recipients will not be reimbursed for costs resulting from obligations incurred before the effective date of the grant.
