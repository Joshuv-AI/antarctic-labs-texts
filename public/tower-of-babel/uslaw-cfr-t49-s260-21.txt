49 C.F.R. § 260.21 — Eligibility.
Citation: us:cfr:49:260.21
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-260.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 260 (Regulations Governing Loans And Loan Guarantees Under The Railroad Rehabilitation And Improvement Financing Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator may make a direct loan to an Applicant, or guarantee the payment of the principal balance and any interest of an obligation of an Applicant prior to, on, or after the date of execution or the date of disbursement of such obligation, if the proceeds of such direct loan or obligation shall be, or have been, used by the Applicant for the eligible purposes listed in § 260.5(a)(1), (2), and (3).
