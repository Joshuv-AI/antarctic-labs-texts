48 C.F.R. § 8.103 — 8.103 Information on available excess personal property.
Citation: us:cfr:48:8.103
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-8.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 8 (Required Sources Of Supplies And Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Information regarding the availability of excess personal property can be obtained through—
(a) Reviewing and requesting available excess personal property in GSAXcess® (see https://gsaxcess.gov); and
(b) Personal contact with GSA or the activity holding the property.
