10 C.F.R. § 1045.145 — Who must review output from a classified IT system that is marked as RD, FRD, or TFNI?
Citation: us:cfr:10:1045.145
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1045.145
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1045 (Nuclear Classification And Declassification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the output is a final product that has been reviewed by a person with appropriate authority, and is properly marked, or is a working paper that is properly marked, no additional review is required. Otherwise, the output must be reviewed in accordance with § 1045.30.
