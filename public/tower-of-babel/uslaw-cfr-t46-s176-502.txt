46 C.F.R. § 176.502 — Certificate of Inspection: Conditions of validity.
Citation: us:cfr:46:176.502
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-176.502
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 176 (Inspection And Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To maintain a valid Certificate of Inspection, you must complete your annual inspection within the periods specified in § 176.500(b)(1) and your Certificate of Inspection must be endorsed.
