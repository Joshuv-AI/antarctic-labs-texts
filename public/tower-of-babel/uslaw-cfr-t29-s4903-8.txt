29 C.F.R. § 4903.8 — When will PBGC compromise a debt owed to PBGC?
Citation: us:cfr:29:4903.8
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4903.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4903 (Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If PBGC cannot collect the full amount of a debt owed to PBGC, PBGC may compromise the debt in accordance with the provisions of 31 CFR part 902.
