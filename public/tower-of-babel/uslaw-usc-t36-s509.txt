36 U.S.C. § 509 — Penalty
Citation: us:usc:36:509
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section509
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part A (Observances And Ceremonies); Chapter 5 (Presidential Inaugural Ceremonies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A person violating a regulation prescribed under this chapter shall be fined under title 18 or imprisoned for not more than 30 days. A separate violation occurs under this section for each day the violation continues.
