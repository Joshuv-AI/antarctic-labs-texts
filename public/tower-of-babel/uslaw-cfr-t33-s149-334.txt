33 C.F.R. § 149.334 — Who must ensure compliance with the requirements for unmanned deepwater ports?
Citation: us:cfr:33:149.334
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-149.334
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 149 (Deepwater Ports: Design, Construction, And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The owner or operator of an unmanned deepwater port must ensure that applicable requirements are complied with on that deepwater port.
