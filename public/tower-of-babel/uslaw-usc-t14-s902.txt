14 U.S.C. § 902 — Employment of draftsmen and engineers
Citation: us:usc:14:902
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section902
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 9 (Administration); Subchapter I (Real And Personal Property). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Coast Guard may employ temporarily, at the seat of government, draftsmen and engineers for the preparation of plans and specifications for vessels, lighthouses, aids to navigation, and other projects for the Coast Guard that may be authorized or appropriated for by Congress, to be paid from the appropriations applicable to such projects.
