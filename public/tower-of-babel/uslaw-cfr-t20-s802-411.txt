20 C.F.R. § 802.411 — Certification of record for judicial review.
Citation: us:cfr:20:802.411
Creator: Benefits Review Board, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-802.411
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 802 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The record of a case including the record of proceedings before the Board shall be transmitted to the appropriate court pursuant to the rules of such court.
