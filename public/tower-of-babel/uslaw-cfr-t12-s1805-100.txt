12 C.F.R. § 1805.100 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1805.100
Creator: Community Development Financial Institutions Fund, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1805.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1805 (Community Development Financial Institutions Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of the Community Development Financial Institutions (CDFI) Program is to promote economic revitalization and community development through investment in and assistance to Community Development Financial Institutions.
