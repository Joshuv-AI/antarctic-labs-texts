28 U.S.C. § 1357 — Injuries under Federal laws
Citation: us:usc:28:1357
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1357
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part IV (Jurisdiction And Venue); Chapter 85 (District Courts; Jurisdiction). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The district courts shall have original jurisdiction of any civil action commenced by any person to recover damages for any injury to his person or property on account of any act done by him, under any Act of Congress, for the protection or collection of any of the revenues, or to enforce the right of citizens of the United States to vote in any State.
