49 U.S.C. § 80301 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:49:80301
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section80301
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Chapter 803 (Contraband). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter—
(1)
“aircraft” means a contrivance used, or capable of being used, for transportation in the air.
(2)
“vehicle” means a contrivance used, or capable of being used, for transportation on, below, or above land, but does not include aircraft.
(3)
“vessel” means a contrivance used, or capable of being used, for transportation in water, but does not include aircraft.
