22 U.S.C. § 4862 — Prohibition on use of funds for facilities in Israel, Jerusalem, or West Bank
Citation: us:usc:22:4862
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section4862
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 58 (Diplomatic Security); Subchapter IV (Diplomatic Security Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

None of the funds authorized to be appropriated by this Act may be obligated or expended for site acquisition, development, or construction of any facility in Israel, Jerusalem, or the West Bank.
