43 C.F.R. § 3275.18 — May BLM require me to test for byproducts associated with geothermal resource production?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3275.18
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3275.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3200 (Geothermal Resource Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must conduct any tests we require, including tests for byproducts, if we find it necessary to require such tests for a given operation.
