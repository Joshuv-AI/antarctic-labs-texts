16 U.S.C. § 718g — Violations
Citation: us:usc:16:718g
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section718g
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 7 (Protection Of Migratory Game And Insectivorous Birds); Subchapter IV (Hunting And Conservation Stamp Tax). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person that violates or fails to comply with any provision of this subchapter (including a regulation promulgated under this subchapter) shall be subject to the penalties described in section 707 of this title.
