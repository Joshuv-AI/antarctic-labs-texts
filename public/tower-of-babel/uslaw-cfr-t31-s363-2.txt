31 C.F.R. § 363.2 — What agency administers TreasuryDirect ®?
Citation: us:cfr:31:363.2
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-363.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 363 (Regulations Governing Securities Held In Treasurydirect). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Bureau of the Fiscal Service (Fiscal Service), Department of the Treasury (Treasury) is responsible for administering TreasuryDirect. Fiscal Service may delegate authority to process certain transactions in TreasuryDirect to Federal Reserve Banks and Branches as fiscal agents of the United States.
