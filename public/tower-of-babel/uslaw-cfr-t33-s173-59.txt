33 C.F.R. § 173.59 — Where to submit report.
Citation: us:cfr:33:173.59
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-173.59
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 173 (Vessel Numbering And Casualty And Accident Reporting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A report required by § 173.55 must be submitted to:
(a) The reporting authority listed in appendix A of this part where the vessel number was issued, or, if the vessel has no number, where the vessel is principally used; or
(b) The reporting authority where the casualty or accident occurred, if it occurred outside the State where the vessel is numbered or principally used.
