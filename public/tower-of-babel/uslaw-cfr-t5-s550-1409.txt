5 C.F.R. § 550.1409 — Inapplicability of premium pay and aggregate pay caps.
Citation: us:cfr:5:550.1409
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-550.1409
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 550 (Pay Administration (General)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Accrued compensatory time off under this subpart is not considered in applying the premium pay limitations established under 5 U.S.C. 5547 and 5 CFR 550.105 through 550.107 or the aggregate limitation on pay established under 5 U.S.C. 5307 and 5 CFR part 530, subpart B.
