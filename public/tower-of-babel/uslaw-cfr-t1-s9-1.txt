1 C.F.R. § 9.1 — Publication required.
Citation: us:cfr:1:9.1
Creator: Administrative Committee Of The Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-1/section-9.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 1 (General Provisions), Part 9 (The United States Government Manual). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-23 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Director publishes a special edition of the Federal Register called “The United States Government Manual” as authorized by the Administrative Committee.
(b) The Director may update the Manual when such supplementation is considered to be in the public interest.
