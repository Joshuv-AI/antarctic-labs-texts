20 U.S.C. § 107f — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:20:107f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section107f
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 6A (Vending Facilities For Blind In Federal Buildings). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary for carrying out the provisions of this chapter.
