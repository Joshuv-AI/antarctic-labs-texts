16 C.F.R. § 1205.31 — Effective date.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1205.31
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1205.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1205 (Safety Standard For Walk-Behind Power Lawn Mowers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any walk-behind rotary power mower manufactured after December 31, 1981, must meet the standard and must be certified as complying with the standard in accordance with this rule.
