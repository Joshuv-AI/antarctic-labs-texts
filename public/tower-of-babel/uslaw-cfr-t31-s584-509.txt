31 C.F.R. § 584.509 — Emergency medical services.
Citation: us:cfr:31:584.509
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-584.509
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 584 (Magnitsky Act Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provision and receipt of nonscheduled emergency medical services that are otherwise prohibited by this part or any Executive orders or further Presidential action relating to the Magnitsky Act are authorized.
