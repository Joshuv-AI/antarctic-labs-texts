7 C.F.R. § 42.134 — Disposition of rejected portions of production.
Citation: us:cfr:7:42.134
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-42.134
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 42 (Standards For Condition Of Food Containers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Rejected portions of production from the same basic inspection period may be reworked, combined together to form a lot, and resubmitted for inspection under the criteria for tightened inspection using stationary lot sampling procedures described in subpart B of this part.
