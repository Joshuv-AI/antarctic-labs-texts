42 C.F.R. § 600.500 — Basis, scope, and applicability.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 600 (Administration, Eligibility, Essential Health Benefits, Performance Standards, Service Delivery Requirements, Premium And Cost Sharing, Allotments, And Reconcilation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Statutory basis. This subpart implements section 1331(a) of the Affordable Care Act, which sets forth provisions regarding the establishment of the BHP and requirements regarding monthly premiums and cost sharing for enrollees.
(b) Scope and applicability. This subpart consists of provisions relating to the imposition of monthly premiums and cost-sharing under all state BHPs.
