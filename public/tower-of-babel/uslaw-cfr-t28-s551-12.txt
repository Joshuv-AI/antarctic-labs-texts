28 C.F.R. § 551.12 — Eligibility to marry.
Citation: us:cfr:28:551.12
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-551.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 551 (Miscellaneous). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An inmate's request to marry shall be approved provided:
(a) The inmate is legally eligible to marry;
(b) The inmate is mentally competent;
(c) The intended spouse has verified, ordinarily in writing, an intention to marry the inmate; and
(d) The marriage poses no threat to institution security or good order, or to the protection of the public.
