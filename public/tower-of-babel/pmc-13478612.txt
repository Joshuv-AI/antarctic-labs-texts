Research on supporting mechanism and application of new ductile thin spraying technology in coal mine roadway
Authors: Ruijie Wang, Rui Wu, Jinhai Xu, Huaiguo Wu, Keke Wang
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
The safety and stability of the roadway is the fundamental guarantee for safe, green, high-yield and efficient production in coal mines, and the surface support of the roadway surrounding rock is crucial for the stability and effectiveness of the entire roadway support system. However, currently most coal mines use traditional shotcrete technology for surface protection, which has disadvantages such as poor ductileness, easy detachment and cracking, high transportation and labor costs, environmental pollution and high rebound; Only a few mines have adopted the new ductile thin spraying technology, but the support mechanism, applicable conditions, and application effects are still unclear and in the stage of engineering experience. This article first provides a detailed theoretical analysis of the support mechanism of ductile thin spray coatings, including surface protection and cooperative support. Secondly, numerical simulations and comparative analysis were conducted using finite difference software FLAC3D and discrete element software 3DEC to study the effectiveness of new ductile thin spray materials and traditional sprayed concrete materials in roadway support. The simulation results show that ductile thin spray materials are more conducive to improving the stress environment of shallow rock layers in roadway surrounding rock, enhancing the bearing capacity of roadway surrounding rock itself, suppressing the displacement and sliding of loose rock blocks between anchor rods, optimizing the stress state of the entire support system, and greatly ensuring the stability of the roadway support system. They are particularly suitable for application in two types of coal rock roadways: “poor quality+medium hard rock/hard rock” and “general quality+soft rock/medium hard rock”. Finally, the new ductile thin spraying technology was industrially tested in the bottom extraction roadway of Changping Coal Mine. The on-site measurement results showed that the technology effectively controlled the overall deformation of the roadway surrounding rock, improved the stress state of the roadway support components, and had good application effects. The research results of this article have good reference and guidance significance for the understanding of the importance of roadway surrounding rock surface support and the promotion and application of new ductile thin spraying technology.

Introduction

In recent years, with the layout and planning of the national energy strategy, China’s coal resources have been further integrated and optimized, resulting in a large number of “safe, green, high-yield and high-efficiency” large-scale mines. Obviously, the rapid excavation and safe stability of roadways are the fundamental guarantees for the safe and efficient production of coal mines1–3. As of now, with the vigorous promotion and popularization of anchor net cable support technology in most coal mines in China, the overall support problem of coal mine roadways has been basically solved and improved. However, the problem of timely sealing and surface protection of roadway surrounding rock has become increasingly prominent.

Relevant research results show that the following problems exist in the timely sealing of surrounding rock of coal mine roadway for surface protection4–10:, As a result, the metal mesh can not closely fit with the surrounding rock surface, so that some of the surrounding rock is in a two-way stress state, which can not play a real active support role, and can not effectively control the harmful deformation of the roadway surrounding rock at the initial stage. At the same time, the mesh laying process increases the labor intensity, and restricts the roadway excavation speed to a certain extent; (2) For the traditional shotcrete surface protection, due to the low ductileness of the shotcrete layer, poor adhesion with the surrounding rock, and easy to fall off and crack, the concrete shotcrete layer has poor water separation effect, large thickness, large amount, large auxiliary transportation volume, high rebound rate, and large dust in the operating environment, which are contrary to the production concept of “green, safe, high-yield and efficient”.

Therefore, based on the above outstanding problems, continue to seek and research a new type of surface protection and support material that can replace the traditional metal mesh or concrete shotcrete, which is green, energy efficient and has better comprehensive performance.

In recent years, extensive research and practical results have shown that a new type of ductile thin spray material (TSL) specifically used for sealing and protecting the surface of coal mine roadway surrounding rock in countries such as Canada and Australia has a good effect on protecting the surface of roadway surrounding rock. Compared with traditional surface protection technologies (metal mesh and concrete spraying), TSL has the following main advantages: (1) good ductileness, strong adhesion with surrounding rock, and can better adapt to the uneven surface of roadway surrounding rock, while not easily cracking or falling off. It can truly play an active supporting role in sealing and protecting the surrounding rock in a timely manner. TSL has good surface protection effect and can effectively solve problems such as gas leakage, weathering of surrounding rock, and rusting of supporting components in coal mine roadway; (2) The ductile thin spray coating on the surface of the surrounding rock in the roadway can form a good cooperative support effect with components such as anchor cables, allowing the pre stress of the anchor cables to fully diffuse, improving the stress environment on the surface of the roadway surrounding rock, enhancing the bearing capacity of the surrounding rock itself, and improving the stability of the support system; (3) The ductileness thin spray coating has a small thickness, low usage, low labor intensity and auxiliary transportation volume, a green and environmentally friendly working environment, no rebound, and fast construction speed.

Numerous domestic and foreign researchers have conducted extensive research on the new ductileness thin spray technology and achieved a series of results. Representative scholars include:

In the study of mechanical properties and failure modes of thin sprayed materials, Dube11 believes that the important mechanism by which thin spray coatings play a supporting role is to promote the interlocking between rock blocks and improve their own bearing capacity; Shan et al.13, compared the strength and support effect of samples reinforced with thin spray coating and metal mesh, and the results showed that thin spray coating can improve the load-bearing capacity and surface damage resistance of the samples; Zhang Nong et al.17, conducted tensile and block bearing capacity tests on sprayed flexible film materials, indicating that sprayed film materials have the characteristics of fast bearing response, large deformation capacity, and strong block bearing capacity. At the same time, combined with the characteristics of coal mine roadway engineering and the mechanical properties of sprayed flexible film, their application principles and applicable conditions were pointed out; Gunner and Tananat18,19 used a combination of two-dimensional discrete element software PFC2D and experimental methods to study the mechanical properties and mechanism of thin spray coatings, providing a new approach for the research of thin spray coatings; Taesang20 and Lee21 studied the thermal insulation and water barrier effects of thin spray coatings on cement concrete through numerical simulations. The existing research lacks systematic comparative analysis of the ductile thin spray material and the traditional concrete shotcrete under the same conditions, especially the quantitative comparison from the perspective of improving the surrounding rock stress environment and optimizing the stress state of supporting components. In the research on the cooperation of thin spray coatings as a part of the support system, Kuijpers et al.12, believe that the support performance of thin spray coatings is mainly reflected in their role as a part of the entire support system, working together with other support components; Wei Qun et al.22,23, used the two-dimensional discrete element software UDEC to simulate the support effect of spray film under anchor rod support system. The results showed that the bonding performance between spray film and rock mass is very important, which is the key to the spray film’s surface support effect. At the same time, they pointed out that the spray film and anchor rod play a cooperative support role, greatly reducing the subsidence of the roof rock coatings and improving the bearing capacity of the roadway roof. Most researches focus on the material properties or two-dimensional simplified simulation, and the research on the cooperative support mechanism of thin spray coatings and anchor (cable) under three-dimensional conditions is insufficient. In the field application and performance test research of thin spray technology, Archibald and Katsabanis14,15 conducted extensive experimental research on the support effect of thin spray coatings under dynamic pressure under different support methods, the results showed that the performance of thin spray coatings was not inferior to other support methods; Dong Shan et al.16, summarized the main functions of thin spray flexible film in roadway support, including adhesive effect, diaphragm effect, and penetration reinforcement effect. The existing industrial test and effect evaluation of thin spray coatings are not systematic and quantitative enough, and lack of long-term monitoring data support.

To sum up, the existing research has not yet formed a clear consensus on the cooperative mechanism of the ductile thin spray coatings in roadway support, especially the comparison effect with the traditional shotcrete under the framework of three-dimensional numerical simulation. Therefore, this paper aims to deeply study the support mechanism of ductile thin spray coatings and its advantages over traditional shotcrete through detailed theoretical analysis, three-dimensional finite difference (FLAC3D) and discrete element (3DEC) numerical simulation, and verify its application effect through field industrial test, so as to clarify its application conditions and engineering value.

Engineering background

Geological conditions

The sixth panel of Changping Coal Mine, located in Shanxi Province, mainly mines No.3 coal seam. Due to the low strength, high gas content, poor coal permeability and rapid gas drainage attenuation of No.3 coal seam within the mine field, the gas drainage effect of this coal seam is not ideal. To solve the problem of gas drainage and accelerate the connection of mining and excavation working faces, the comprehensive mining working face adopts the bottom drainage roadway for gas drainage.

The design length of the north roadway of gangue removal in panel 6 is 1530 m. The normal section has a clear width of 4.8 m and a clear height of 3.2 m. The north lane of gangue removal in panel 6 is located between K6 limestone and K7 fine sandstone. The K7 sandstone is high. The rock stratum between K6 limestone and K7 sandstone is sandy mudstone with a thickness of about 3.0 m. The design height of the bottom drainage roadway is 3.2 m, and the roadway is driven along the K6 roof. Therefore, it is necessary to break the K7 sandstone of the roof with a height of about 0.2 m. Above the K7 sandstone is a 3 m thick sandy mudstone layer, as shown in Fig. 1.

Existing problems

Due to the thickness change of K7 fine-grained sandstone, roadwaying disturbance, roadway crossing structure, layer crossing and other factors, it is easy to dig through and expose the sandy mudstone above K7 sandstone in some areas, and even in some areas, the top of K7 sandstone can not be found after passing structure or layer crossing. The top plate is directly sandy mudstone, which is unstable, and is prone to weathering, deformation and damage, and gradually expand the broken area of surrounding rock to the deep stable surrounding rock. So, the roadway gradually converges and deforms, and even local collapse and roof fall occur, resulting in the need for multiple repairs of the roadway, and even serious personal safety accidents. Although the traditional shotcrete can play a certain role in preventing the weathering of the surrounding rock of the roadway, there are problems such as large pollution, large material consumption, high transportation and labor costs, high rebound rate, unstable quality, easy to fall off and crack, etc. in the shotcrete operation process, and the construction speed is slow, which is difficult to meet the requirements of rapid roadway excavation and timely closure of the roadway.

Therefore, to solve the above problems, this article proposes a new ductileness thin spraying technology based on green environmental protection and high-performance surface protection materials.

Mechanism of the supporting effect of ductile thin spray technology

A large number of engineering practices and research results have shown11,12,16,17,24–27 that ductile thin spray coatings play two main roles in coal mine roadway support according to their main functions, namely, surface protection of roadway surrounding rock and cooperative support with anchor cables. The former mainly reflects the closed anti weathering, anti gas or goaf gas leakage, and anti rust function of the ductile thin spray coating on the surface of the surrounding rock of the roadway, while the latter mainly reflects its timely and active support for the shallow surrounding rock of the roadway, and the formation of a systematic support system from the surface to the inside with anchor cables in space.

Mechanism of surface protection

A thin spray coating with good ductileness and bonding strength can form a continuous and complete “physical diaphragm” on the surface of the roadway surrounding rock, as shown in Fig. 2, tightly wrapping the loose blocks on the surface of the roadway surrounding rock and the nearby intact rock blocks together. The ductile thin spray coating can not only prevent and suppress the movement and sliding of loose rock blocks in the shallow part of the roadway surrounding rock, but also form a “physical diaphragm” on the surface of the surrounding rock, blocking the entry of moisture and gas from the air into the cracks of the surrounding rock. The protective effect of ductile thin spray coating largely depends on the shear strength, tensile strength, and bonding strength between the coating and the surrounding rock surface. The protective effect of the ductile thin spray coating generally goes through several main processes from the beginning to failure (as shown in Fig. 3): (1) In the static bearing stage, the thin spray coating is tightly adhered to the surface of the surrounding rock, and the coating at the gap between the stable surrounding rock and the loose surrounding rock mainly bears the combined effect of shear and tension. At this time, the coating is in the elastic stage and there is no damage phenomenon; (2) In the debonding stage, when the gravity of the loose surrounding rock exceeds the bonding force between the coating and the surface of the surrounding rock, the coating begins to slowly detach from the surface of the surrounding rock, generally expanding from the boundary between stable and loose surrounding rock to both sides. The detached coating mainly bears tensile action; (3) During the tensile bearing stage, as the debonding process continues, the detachment of the coating gradually stops when it extends to the point where the support component is subjected to force (near the anchor cable tray). At this point, the detached coating mainly bears the tensile action between the support component and the loose surrounding rock at the point of force application; (4) During the tensile failure stage, when the applied force exceeds the tensile strength of the coating itself, the coating gradually undergoes tensile failure until the entire coating fractures and falls off, ultimately leading to failure. From this, it can be seen that the protective effect of the ductile thin spray coating mainly has two failure modes, namely tensile shear failure and adhesive failure failure, as shown in Fig. 4.

Cooperative support mechanism

In addition to providing surface protection for the surrounding rock of the roadway, the ductile thin spray coating can also interact with supporting components such as anchor rods and cables to improve the stress environment of the surrounding rock and construct the entire support system of the roadway.

The principle diagram of the cooperative support effect of the ductile thin spray coating is shown in Fig. 5. After the initial support of the roadway excavation, a certain thickness (10–50 mm) of ductile thin spray coating with high compressive, tensile, and shear strength forms a closed “physical protective layer” on the surface of the roadway surrounding rock in a timely manner (as shown in the yellow part in the Fig. 5). On the one hand, this protective layer can prevent the weathering and damage of air and moisture in the roadway to the shallow surrounding rock, suppress and limit the displacement and sliding of loose blocks between anchor rods (cables); On the other hand, the discontinuous and unstable “point line support” formed by the original anchor rods, anchor cables and other supporting components on the surface of the roadway can be converted into continuous and stable “surface support”, so as to form a continuous overall structure on the surface of the roadway surrounding rock, thereby improving the stress environment of the shallow surrounding rock. At the same time, connect the relatively isolated compressive stress zone formed by the anchor rod of the shallow surrounding rock (as shown in the green part of the Fig. 5) into a relatively continuous compressive stress zone, and then deform together with the “bearing layer” of this compressive stress zone under the pressure of the surrounding rock, so that the surface surrounding rock of the roadway and the shallow surrounding rock form a continuous deformation bearing structure in space. Finally, use the “anchoring layer” formed by the anchor cable in the deep surrounding rock (as shown in the blue part of the Fig. 5) to suspend the previous continuous bearing structure in the stable rock layer in the deep part. At this time, the “physical protection layer”, “shallow bearing layer” and “deep anchoring layer” of the roadway surrounding rock can jointly form a complete “surface to interior” structure. The continuous effective compressive stress zone (as shown in the red box inside the Fig. 5) forms a relatively stable and reliable cooperative support system in space. This greatly increases the stability and effectiveness of the entire roadway support system.

From Figs. 20 and 21, it can be seen that in the roadway support system, when only anchor cables are used, two anchor cables on the leftmost side of the roof are sheared and broken, and six anchor cables are sheared and broken (blue segment in the Fig. 20). Moreover, the average axial force value of all anchor cables is very large, and the force is extremely uneven; When there are anchor cables and sprayed concrete, one anchor cable on the leftmost side of the roof is sheared and broken, three anchor cables are sheared and broken, and three anchor cables on both sides are stretched and broken (yellow segment in the Fig. 20). Moreover, the average axial force value of all anchor cables is relatively large, and the force is uneven; When there are anchor cables and ductile thin spray materials, only 2 anchor rods on the roof undergo shear fracture, and the average axial force value of all anchor cables is relatively small, with relatively uniform force distribution.

Therefore, in terms of the force state of the roadway anchor cables, ductile thin sprayed materials are more conducive to improving the force state of the anchor cables than sprayed concrete materials, fully exerting the timely active support effect of the anchor cables, better coordinating with the anchor cables for support, and more conducive to ensuring the reliability and stability of the entire support system of the roadway.

Engineering applications

Based on the theoretical and numerical simulation analysis results in the previous section, a new type of ductile thin spray material with better performance should be preferred for roadway surface protection materials. Therefore, based on the specific geological conditions of the Liupan area of Changping Coal Mine in Shanxi Province (see Chap. 2 for details), the new ductileness thin spraying technology will be industrially tested in its bottom extraction roadway.

Monitoring method of roadway surface displacement

Conclusion

This paper points out the problems in timely sealing and surface support of coal mine roadway surrounding rock, compares the advantages and disadvantages of roadway surrounding rock surface protection materials, and briefly summarizes the research progress and current shortcomings of domestic and foreign scholars on ductile thin spray materials. On the basis of analyzing the main problems and engineering geological conditions of the bottom extraction roadway in Changping Coal Mine, a new type of ductile thin spraying technology was proposed, and the main supporting mechanisms of the ductile thin spraying coating, namely surface protection and cooperative support, were theoretically analyzed in detail. Numerical simulations and comparative analysis were conducted using finite difference software FLAC3D and discrete element software 3DEC to investigate the effectiveness of new ductileness thin spray materials and traditional shotcrete materials in roadway support. The simulation results show that ductile thin spray materials are more conducive to improving the stress environment of shallow rock layers in roadway surrounding rock, enhancing the bearing capacity of roadway surrounding rock itself, suppressing the displacement and sliding of loose rock blocks between anchor cable, optimizing the force state of the entire support system, and greatly ensuring the stability of the roadway support system. They are particularly suitable for application in two types of coal rock roadways: “poor quality+medium hard rock/hard rock” and “general quality+soft rock/medium hard rock”. The new ductileness thin spraying technology was tested industrially and the spraying support effect was evaluated in the bottom extraction roadway of the Liupan area of Changping Coal Mine. The on-site measured mining pressure data shows that the new surface protection technology effectively controls the overall deformation of the roadway surrounding rock and improves the stability of the support system. The industrial application effect is good, significant economic and social benefits, further verifying the rationality and correctness of the new ductileness thin spraying technology.
