7 C.F.R. § 981.36 — Vacancy.
Citation: us:cfr:7:981.36
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-981.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 981 (Almonds Grown In California). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To fill any vacancy occasioned by the death, removal, resignation, or disqualification of any member or alternate of the Board, a successor for his unexpired term shall be selected by the Secretary after consideration of recommendations which may be submitted by members of the group for which such vacancy exists, unless such selection is deemed unnecessary by the Secretary.
