18 C.F.R. § 33.7 — Verification.
Citation: us:cfr:18:33.7
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-33.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 33 (Applications Under Federal Power Act Section 203). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The original application must be signed by a person or persons having authority with respect thereto and having knowledge of the matters therein set forth, and must be verified under oath.
