48 C.F.R. § 37.501 — 37.501 Definition.
Citation: us:cfr:48:37.501
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-37.501
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 37 (Service Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Best practices, as used in this subpart, means techniques that agencies may use to help detect problems in the acquisition, management, and administration of service contracts. Best practices are practical techniques gained from experience that agencies may use to improve the procurement process.
