42 C.F.R. § 423.2130 — Effect of the Council's decision.
Citation: us:cfr:42:423.2130
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-423.2130
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 423 (Voluntary Medicare Prescription Drug Benefit). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Council's decision is final and binding unless a Federal District Court issues a decision modifying the Council's decision or the decision is revised as the result of a reopening in accordance with § 423.1980. An enrollee may file an action in a Federal District Court within 60 calendar days after the date the enrollee receives written notice of the Council's decision.
