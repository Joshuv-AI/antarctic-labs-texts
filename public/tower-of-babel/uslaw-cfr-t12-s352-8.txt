12 C.F.R. § 352.8 — Program accessibility: New construction and alterations.
Citation: us:cfr:12:352.8
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-352.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 352 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Disability). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each building or part of a building, whether newly constructed, or substantially altered, in which FDIC programs or activities will be conducted, shall be designed, constructed or altered so as to be readily accessible to, and usable by, individuals with disabilities.
