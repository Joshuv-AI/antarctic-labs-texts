10 U.S.C. § 1454 — Correction of administrative errors
Citation: us:usc:10:1454
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1454
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 73 (Annuities Based On Retired Or Retainer Pay); Subchapter II (Survivor Benefit Plan). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Authority.—
The Secretary concerned may, under regulations prescribed under section 1455 of this title, correct or revoke any election under this subchapter when the Secretary considers it necessary to correct an administrative error.
(b) Finality.—
Except when procured by fraud, a correction or revocation under this section is final and conclusive on all officers of the United States.
