10 C.F.R. § 1705.10 — Fees.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1705.10
Creator: Defense Nuclear Facilities Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1705.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1705 (Privacy Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A fee will not be charged for search or review of requested records, or for correction of records. When a request is made for copies of records, a copying fee will be charged at the same rate established for FOIA requests. See 10 CFR 1703.107. However, the first 100 pages of copying will be free of charge.
