50 C.F.R. § 31.15 — Public hunting and fishing programs.
Citation: us:cfr:50:31.15
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-31.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 31 (Wildlife Species Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The privilege of hunting and fishing may be extended to the general public under the provisions of regulations cited in parts 32 and 33 of this subchapter.
