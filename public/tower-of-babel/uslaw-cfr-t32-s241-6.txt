32 C.F.R. § 241.6 — Length of details.
Citation: us:cfr:32:241.6
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-241.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 241 (Pilot Program For Temporary Exchange Of Information Technology Personnel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A detail shall be for a period of not less than 3 months and not more than 1 year, and may be extended in 3-month increments for a total of not more than 1 additional year by DoD Components and private sector organizations authorized approving officials.
(b) This extension may be granted in 3-month increments not to exceed 1 year. No assignment may commence after September 30, 2018.
