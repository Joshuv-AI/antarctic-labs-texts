27 C.F.R. § 771.108 — Director of Industry Operations' decision.
Citation: us:cfr:27:771.108
Creator: Bureau Of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms, And Explosives, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-771.108
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 771 (Rules Of Practice In Explosive License And Permit Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the Director of Industry Operations issues an initial decision in accordance with § 771.77 or § 771.107 the decision shall become a part of the record. The decision shall consist of:
(a) A brief statement of the issues involved in the proceedings;
(b) The Director of Industry Operations' findings and conclusions, as well as the reasons therefor; and
(c) The Director of Industry Operations' determination on the record.
