14 C.F.R. § 382.80 — May carriers impose additional restrictions on the transport of service animals?
Citation: us:cfr:14:382.80
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-382.80
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 382 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Disability In Air Travel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Carriers are not permitted to establish additional restrictions on the transport of service animals outside of those specifically permitted by the provisions in this part, unless required by applicable FAA, TSA, or other Federal requirements or a foreign carrier's government.
