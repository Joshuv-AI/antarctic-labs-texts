46 C.F.R. § 52.01-115 — -115 Feedwater supply (modifies PG-61).
Citation: us:cfr:46:52.01-115
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-52.01-115
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 52 (Power Boilers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Boiler feedwater supply must meet the requirements of PG-61 of Section I of the ASME BPVC (incorporated by reference; see § 52.01-1) and § 56.50-30 of this subchapter.
