Local and global patterns of rank 3 G G -fans of totally-infinite type
Tomoki Nakanishi

Abstract
. We focus on the G G -fans associated with cluster patterns whose initial exchange matrices are of infinite type. We study the asymptotic behavior of the g g -vectors around the initial G G -cone under the alternating mutations for two indices of infinite type. In the rank 3 case, we classify them into several patterns. As an application, the incompleteness of the G G -fans of infinite type is proved. We observed that the local pattern of a rank 3 G G -fan of totally-infinite type classified by the above types correlates with its global pattern. Following the classification of the local patterns (together with the Markov constant), we present several prototypical examples of the global patterns of the rank 3 G G -fans of totally-infinite type, many of which are new in the literature.

2.1.
Chebyshev polynomials

The
Chebyshev polynomials
U_{n}(t)

(n\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq-2})
of the second kind

are defined by the following recursion relation:

(2.4)

\displaystyle U_{-2}(t)=-1,\quad U_{-1}(t)=0,

(2.5)

\displaystyle U_{n}(t)=2tU_{n-1}(t)-U_{n-2}(t)\quad(n\geq 0).

For example, we have

(2.6)

\displaystyle U_{0}(t)=1,\quad U_{1}(t)=2t,\quad U_{2}(t)=4t^{2}-1,\quad U_{3}(t)=8t^{3}-4t.

The following properties are well-known.

(2.7)

\displaystyle U_{n}(t)>0\quad(n\geq 0,\,t\geq 1),

(2.8)

\displaystyle U_{n}(t)^{2}-U_{n-1}(t)U_{n+1}(t)=1\quad(n\geq-1),

(2.9)

\displaystyle\frac{U_{n}(t)}{U_{n-1}(t)}>\frac{U_{n+1}(t)}{U_{n}(t)}\quad(n\geq 1),

(2.10)

\displaystyle U_{n}(t)=\frac{(t+\sqrt{t^{2}-1})^{n+1}-(t-\sqrt{t^{2}-1})^{n+1}}{2\sqrt{t^{2}-1}}\quad(n\geq-2),

(2.11)

\displaystyle\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\frac{U_{n+1}(t)}{U_{n}(t)}=t+\sqrt{t^{2}-1}\quad(|t|\geq 1).
