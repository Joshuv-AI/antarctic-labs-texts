20 U.S.C. § 2392 — Authority to make payments
Citation: us:usc:20:2392
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section2392
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 44 (Career And Technical Education); Subchapter II (General Provisions); Part A (Federal Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any authority to make payments or to enter into contracts under this chapter shall be available only to such extent or in such amounts as are provided in advance in appropriation Acts.
