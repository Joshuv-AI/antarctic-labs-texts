40 C.F.R. § 121.16 — When the Administrator certifies.
Citation: us:cfr:40:121.16
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-121.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 121 (State Certification Of Activities Requiring A Federal License Or Permit). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Certification or waiver by the Administrator is required where no state, Tribe, or interstate agency has authority to give such a certification.
(b) When acting pursuant to this section, the Administrator shall comply with the requirements of Clean Water Act section 401 and this part.
