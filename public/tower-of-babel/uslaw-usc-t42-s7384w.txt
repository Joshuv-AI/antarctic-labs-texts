42 U.S.C. § 7384w — Subpoenas; oaths; examination of witnesses
Citation: us:usc:42:7384w
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section7384w
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 84 (Department Of Energy); Subchapter XVI (Energy Employees Occupational Illness Compensation Program); Part B (Program Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Labor, with respect to any matter under this part, may—
(1)
issue subpoenas for and compel the attendance of witnesses;
(2)
administer oaths;
(3)
examine witnesses; and
(4)
require the production of books, papers, documents, and other evidence.
