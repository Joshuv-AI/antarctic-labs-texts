24 C.F.R. § 50.41 — EIS policy.
Citation: us:cfr:24:50.41
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-50.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 50 (Protection And Enhancement Of Environmental Quality). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

EIS's will be prepared and considered in program determinations pursuant to the general environmental policy stated in § 50.3 and 40 CFR 1505.2 (b) and (c).
