47 C.F.R. § 76.939 — Truthful written statements and responses to requests of franchising authority.
Citation: us:cfr:47:76.939
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-76.939
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 76 (Multichannel Video And Cable Television Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Cable operators shall comply with franchising authorities' and the Commission's requests for information, orders, and decisions. Any information submitted to a franchising authority or the Commission in making a rate determination pursuant to an FCC form filing is subject to the provisions of § 1.17 of this chapter.
