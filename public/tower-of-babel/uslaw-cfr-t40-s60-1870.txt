40 C.F.R. § 60.1870 — When must I submit the initial report?
Citation: us:cfr:40:60.1870
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.1870
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 60 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As specified in § 60.7(c), submit your initial report by 180 days after your final compliance date.
