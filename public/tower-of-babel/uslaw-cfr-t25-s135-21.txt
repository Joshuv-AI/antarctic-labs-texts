25 C.F.R. § 135.21 — Time of payment.
Citation: us:cfr:25:135.21
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-135.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 135 (Construction Assessments, Crow Indian Irrigation Project). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The amount of each annual installment, payable under the private landowner contracts, determined as provided in this part shall be paid by the landowners to the United States, on or before November 15 of each year commencing with the calendar year 1951.
