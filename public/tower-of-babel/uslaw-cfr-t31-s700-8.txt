31 C.F.R. § 700.8 — Soliciting, vending, debt collection and distribution of handbills.
Citation: us:cfr:31:700.8
Creator: Federal Law Enforcement Training Center, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-700.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 700 (Regulations Governing Conduct In Or On The Federal Law Enforcement Training Center (Fletc) Buildings And Grounds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The unauthorized soliciting for charity and contributions, commercial soliciting and vending of all kinds, the display or distribution of commercial advertising, or the collecting of private debts, other than legal service of process, in or on the property, is prohibited. This prohibition does not apply to Federal Law Enforcement Training Center concessions or notices posted by authorized employees on the bulletin boards. Distribution of material such as pamphlets, handbills, and flyers is prohibited without prior approval of the Director.
