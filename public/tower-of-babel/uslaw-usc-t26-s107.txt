26 U.S.C. § 107 — Rental value of parsonages
Citation: us:usc:26:107
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section107
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 1 (Normal Taxes And Surtaxes); Subchapter B (Computation Of Taxable Income); Part III (Items Specifically Excluded From Gross Income). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the case of a minister of the gospel, gross income does not include—
(1)
the rental value of a home furnished to him as part of his compensation; or
(2)
the rental allowance paid to him as part of his compensation, to the extent used by him to rent or provide a home and to the extent such allowance does not exceed the fair rental value of the home, including furnishings and appurtenances such as a garage, plus the cost of utilities.
