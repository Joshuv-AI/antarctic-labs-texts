Emerging Photon Jets in the Hadronic Calorimeter: A Novel Signature of Neutral Long-Lived Particles at the LHC
Jinheung Kim, Dongjoo Kim, Soojin Lee, Jeonghyeon Song

Abstract
We propose a novel collider signature for neutral long-lived particles (LLPs): the emerging photon jet in the hadronic calorimeter (HCAL). This signature arises when a neutral LLP decays into photons within the HCAL, producing an electromagnetic shower without associated charged tracks or energy deposits in the electromagnetic calorimeter (ECAL). To demonstrate the viability of this approach, we consider the fermiophobic Higgs boson h f h_{\rm f} in the Type-I two-Higgs-doublet model as a representative scenario. In the ultralight regime ( m h f < 1 m_{h_{\rm f}}<1 GeV), h f h_{\rm f} decays exclusively into a photon pair via loop-induced processes, resulting in a suppressed width and consequently a long lifetime. Focusing on the golden channel p ​ p → H ± ​ h f → W ± ​ h f ​ h f pp\to H^{\pm}h_{\rm f}\to W^{\pm}h_{\rm f}h_{\rm f} , we analyze the exotic final state in which one h f h_{\rm f} decays in the ECAL and appears as a highly collimated photon jet (reconstructed as a single photon), while the other decays within the HCAL, producing an emerging photon jet. Through a detailed signal-to-background analysis incorporating realistic detector effects via fast simulation, we demonstrate that this signature achieves discovery-level sensitivity at the HL-LHC across a broad region of parameter space consistent with theoretical and experimental constraints. While our study focuses on the fermiophobic Higgs, the emerging photon jet in the HCAL constitutes a broadly applicable and previously unexplored strategy for detecting neutral LLPs decaying into photons, opening a new avenue in LLP searches at colliders.

by performing a full signal-to-background analysis at the detector level.
A crucial step is identifying a promising production channel for
h_{\rm f}
. Since
h_{\rm f}
is fermiophobic, it cannot be produced via gluon fusion through quark loops. Instead, the dominant production modes involve associated production with other BSM Higgs bosons,
pp\to W^{*}\to h_{\rm f}H^{\pm}
and
pp\to Z^{*}\to h_{\rm f}A

[
150
,
134
]
. Among these, the
W

The production cross sections for the signal process
pp\to h_{\rm f}H^{\pm}
are sizable at the 14 TeV LHC. For benchmark cases with
M_{H^{\pm}}=100,200,300{\;{\rm GeV}}
and fixed
t_{\beta}=10
(corresponding to
c_{\beta-\alpha}=0.995
in the fermiophobic Type-I scenario), we compute the parton-level cross sections using
MadGraph5_aMC@NLO

[

]
version 3.5.6 with the
NNPDF31_nlo_as_0118

\displaystyle\sigma(pp\to h_{\rm f}H^{\pm})\big|_{14{\;{\rm TeV}}}=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}2.79\times 10^{3}{\;{\rm fb}}&\hbox{if }M_{H^{\pm}}=100{\;{\rm GeV}},\\
2.18\times 10^{2}{\;{\rm fb}}&\hbox{if }M_{H^{\pm}}=200{\;{\rm GeV}},\\
5.20\times 10^{1}{\;{\rm fb}}&\hbox{if }M_{H^{\pm}}=300{\;{\rm GeV}}.\end{array}\right.

We observe that the signal cross section decreases rapidly with increasing charged Higgs boson mass, illustrating the strong sensitivity of the production rate to

.
Although the above calculation assumes a fixed

is relatively mild. Since the cross section scales with

, its

. Given that

is tightly constrained to be above 0.95 by Higgs precision data, variations in

We now turn to the final-state topology of the signal process
pp\to h_{\rm f}H^{\pm}
. Given that
H^{\pm}\to h_{\rm f}W^{\pm}
proceeds with nearly 100% branching ratio, the process yields two
h_{\rm f}
bosons in the final state. We focus on the scenario in which one
h_{\rm f}
decays within the ECAL and is reconstructed as
\gamma_{\rm col}
, while the other decays inside the HCAL and gives rise to an
J^{\gamma}_{\rm HCAL}
. This leads to our golden channel:

\displaystyle pp\to H^{\pm}h_{\rm f}\to W^{\pm}h_{\rm f}h_{\rm f}\to\ell^{\pm}\nu\,\gamma_{\rm col}\,J^{\gamma}_{\rm HCAL},

(18)

where
\ell^{\pm}=e^{\pm},\mu^{\pm}
. The resulting final state consists of a charged lepton, missing transverse energy (
{E_{T}^{\rm miss}}
), a single photon, and an emerging photon jet in the HCAL.

For the background, we consider the

pp\to\ell^{\pm}\nu j\gamma.

(19)

This background includes both the hard scattering process
pp\to W^{\pm}(\to\ell^{\pm}\nu)j\gamma

pp\to\ell^{\pm}\nu j

Among bremsstrahlung sources, radiation from electrons is particularly significant due to their small mass, which enhances collinear photon emission

Although the photon origins differ, the resulting final states are experimentally indistinguishable and must be treated as a single irreducible background in the analysis.

For the signal process with final-state particles as in

and the other as

\gamma_{\rm col}

h_{\rm f}
must decay before reaching the outer boundary of the ECAL. Conversely, for
h_{\rm f}
to be reconstructed as
J^{\gamma}_{\rm HCAL}

h_{\rm f}

Let us consider a signal event in which the two
h_{\rm f}
bosons have transverse momenta
p_{T}^{(1)}
and
p_{T}^{(2)}
, denoted by
h_{\rm f}^{(1)}
and
h_{\rm f}^{(2)}

is given by
d_{\rm rad}^{(i)}=c\tau p_{T}^{(i)}/m_{h_{\rm f}}

h_{\rm f}^{(i)}
is reconstructed as

or

\displaystyle P(h_{\rm f}^{(i)}\to\gamma_{\rm col})

\displaystyle=1-\exp\left(-\frac{L_{\rm ECAL}}{d_{\rm rad}^{(i)}}\right),

(20)

\displaystyle P(h_{\rm f}^{(i)}\to J^{\gamma}_{\rm HCAL})

\displaystyle=\exp\left(-\frac{L_{\rm HCAL}^{i}}{d_{\rm rad}^{(i)}}\right)-\exp\left(-\frac{L_{\rm HCAL}^{f}}{d_{\rm rad}^{(i)}}\right),

is the radial distance to the outer boundary of the ECAL,

marks the inner boundary of the HCAL, and
L_{\rm HCAL}^{f}
its outer boundary.

IV
Conclusion

We have proposed a novel collider signature for neutral long-lived particles: the emerging photon jet in the hadronic calorimeter (HCAL). This distinctive signal arises when a collimated photon pair, produced from the decay of an ultralight scalar, initiates an electromagnetic shower deep in the HCAL, without associated charged tracks or ECAL deposits. To our knowledge, this is the first dedicated study of such a signature.

As a concrete realization, we examined the ultralight fermiophobic Higgs boson
h_{\rm f}
in the Type-I two-Higgs-doublet model. In the golden channel
pp\to H^{\pm}h_{\rm f}\to W^{\pm}h_{\rm f}h_{\rm f}
, we focused on events featuring one
h_{\rm f}
decaying in the ECAL (as a single photon) and the other in the HCAL (as an emerging photon jet). Our detector-level analysis shows that this signature provides discovery-level sensitivity across a wide region of theoretically and experimentally allowed parameter space.

While demonstrated in the context of the fermiophobic Type-I 2HDM, the emerging photon jet in the HCAL offers a broadly applicable and previously unexplored strategy for probing neutral LLPs decaying into photons—highlighting a promising new direction in long-lived particle searches at collider experiments.
