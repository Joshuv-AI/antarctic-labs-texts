5 C.F.R. § 2472.10 — Reports.
Citation: us:cfr:5:2472.10
Creator: Federal Labor Relations Authority, General Counsel Of The Federal Labor Relations Authority And Federal Service Impasses Panel
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-2472.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 2472 (Impasses Arising Pursuant To Agency Determinations Not To Establish Or To Terminate Flexible Or Compressed Work Schedules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a report is issued after a hearing conducted pursuant to § 2472.8 and 2472.9, it normally shall be in writing and shall be submitted to the Panel, with a copy to each party, within a period normally not to exceed 30 calendar days after the close of the hearing and receipt of briefs, if any.
