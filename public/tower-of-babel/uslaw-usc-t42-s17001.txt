42 U.S.C. § 17001 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:42:17001
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section17001
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 152 (Energy Independence And Security). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this Act:
(1) Department
The term “Department” means the Department of Energy.
(2) Institution of higher education
The term “institution of higher education” has the meaning given the term in section 1001(a) of title 20.
(3) Secretary
The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of Energy.
