33 C.F.R. § 117.461 — Lacassine Bayou.
Citation: us:cfr:33:117.461
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-117.461
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 117 (Drawbridge Operation Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The draws of the S14 bridge, mile 17.0, and the Southern Pacific railroad bridge, mile 20.4, both near Hayes, shall open on signal if at least 24 hours notice is given.
