25 C.F.R. § 2.816 — May I appeal the Decision on my Objection to the Statement of Performance?
Citation: us:cfr:25:2.816
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-2.816
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 2 (Appeals From Administrative Decisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. The Decision issued by the decision-maker is appealable to the reviewing official, who is the Director, BTFA.
