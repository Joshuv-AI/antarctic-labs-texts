28 C.F.R. § 104.32 — Eligibility review.
Citation: us:cfr:28:104.32
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-104.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 104 (September 11Th Victim Compensation Fund). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any claimant deemed ineligible by the Claims Evaluator may appeal that decision to the Special Master or her designee by filing an eligibility appeal within 30 days on forms created by the office of the Special Master.
