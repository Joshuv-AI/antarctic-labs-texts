42 C.F.R. § 38.9 — Federal audits.
Citation: us:cfr:42:38.9
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-38.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 38 (Disaster Assistance For Crisis Counseling And Training). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary, the Administrator, and the Comptroller General of the United States, or their duly authorized representatives shall have access to any books, documents, papers, and records that pertain to Federal funds, equipment, and supplies received under this part for the purpose of audit and examination.
