7 C.F.R. § 1.800 — Words in the singular form.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1.800
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1.800
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1 (Administrative Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Words in this subpart in the singular form shall be deemed to import the plural, and vice versa, as the context may require.
