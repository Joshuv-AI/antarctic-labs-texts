34 C.F.R. § 396.32 — What additional factors does the Secretary consider in making awards?
Citation: us:cfr:34:396.32
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-396.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 396 (Training Of Interpreters For Individuals Who Are Deaf Or Hard Of Hearing And Individuals Who Are Deaf-Blind). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to the selection criteria listed in § 396.31 and 34 CFR 75.210, the Secretary, in making awards under this part, considers the geographical distribution of projects throughout the country, as appropriate, in order to best carry out the purposes of this program. To accomplish this, the Secretary may in any fiscal year make awards of regional or national scope.
