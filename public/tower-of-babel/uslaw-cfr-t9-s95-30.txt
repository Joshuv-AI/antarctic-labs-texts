9 C.F.R. § 95.30 — Animal stomachs.
Citation: us:cfr:9:95.30
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-95.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 95 (Sanitary Control Of Animal Byproducts (Except Casings), And Hay And Straw, Offered For Entry Into The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Stomachs or portions of the stomachs of ruminants or swine, other than those imported for food purposes under the meat-inspection regulations of the Department, shall not be imported without permission from the Deputy Administrator, Veterinary Services. Importations permitted shall be subject to such restrictions as the Deputy Administrator, Veterinary Services may deem necessary in each instance.
