21 U.S.C. § 821 — Rules and regulations
Citation: us:usc:21:821
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section821
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 13 (Drug Abuse Prevention And Control); Subchapter I (Control And Enforcement); Part C (Registration Of Manufacturers, Distributors, And Dispensers Of Controlled Substances). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General is authorized to promulgate rules and regulations and to charge reasonable fees relating to the registration and control of the manufacture, distribution, and dispensing of controlled substances and to listed chemicals.
