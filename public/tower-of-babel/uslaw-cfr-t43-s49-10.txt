43 C.F.R. § 49.10 — Does this part affect existing authorities?
Citation: us:cfr:43:49.10
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-49.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 49 (Paleontological Resources Preservation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. This part preserves the authority of the Secretary of the Interior and the bureaus under this and other laws and regulations to preserve, manage, and protect paleontological resources on Federal land.
