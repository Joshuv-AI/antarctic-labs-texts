42 C.F.R. § 495.350 — State Medicaid agency attestations.
Citation: us:cfr:42:495.350
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-495.350
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 495 (Standards For The Electronic Health Record Technology Incentive Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The State must provide assurances to HHS that amounts received with respect to sums expended that are attributable to payments to a Medicaid provider for the adoption of EHR are paid directly to such provider, or to an employer or facility to which such provider has assigned payments, without any deduction or rebate.
