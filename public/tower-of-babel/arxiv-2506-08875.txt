Extremal Zagreb indices of bicyclic hypergraphs
Hong Zhou, Changjiang Bu

Abstract
The Zagreb index of a hypergraph is defined as the sum of the squares of the degrees of its vertices. A connected k k -uniform hypergraph with n n vertices and m m edges is called bicyclic if n = m ⁡ ( k − 1 ) − 1 n=m(k-1)-1 . In this paper, we determine the hypergraphs with the maximum and minimum Zagreb indices among all linear bicyclic uniform hypergraphs.

Lemma 2.1

For

be a linear
k
-uniform hypergraph with

. Let

be the edges incident with
u
,
v\notin e_{i}

d_{\mathcal{H}}(v)>d_{\mathcal{H}}(u)-t

e^{\prime}_{i}=(e_{i}\setminus\{u\})\bigcup\{v\}
for each
i\in[t]

\mathcal{H}^{{}^{\prime}}
be the hypergraph with

Then

\mathcal{H}
by moving

edges from

to

and

Proof.

By the definition of the Zagreb index, we have

\displaystyle M(\mathcal{H}^{\prime})-M(\mathcal{H})

\displaystyle=d^{2}_{\mathcal{H}^{\prime}}(v)+d^{2}_{\mathcal{H}^{\prime}}(u)-d^{2}_{\mathcal{H}}(v)-d^{2}_{\mathcal{H}}(u)

\displaystyle=(d_{\mathcal{H}}(v)+t)^{2}+(d_{\mathcal{H}}(u)-t)^{2}-d_{\mathcal{H}}^{2}(v)-d_{\mathcal{H}}^{2}(u)

\displaystyle=2t(t+d_{\mathcal{H}}(v)-d_{\mathcal{H}}(u))>0.

∎

Theorem 3.2

Proof.

M(\mathcal{H})=\sum\limits_{t=1}^{\Delta_{\mathcal{H}}}((t-1)(t-2)+3t-2)n_{t}=\sum\limits_{t=1}^{\Delta_{\mathcal{H}}}(t-1)(t-2)n_{t}+3km-2n.\\
