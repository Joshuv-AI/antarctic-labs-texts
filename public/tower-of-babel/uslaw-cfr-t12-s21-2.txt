12 C.F.R. § 21.2 — Designation of security officer.
Citation: us:cfr:12:21.2
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-21.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 21 (Minimum Security Devices And Procedures, Reports Of Suspicious Activities, And Bank Secrecy Act Compliance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within 30 days after the opening of a new bank, the Bank's board of directors shall designate a security officer who shall have the authority, subject to the approval of the board of directors, for immediately developing and administering a written security program to protect each banking office from robberies, burglaries, and larcenies and to assist in identifying and apprehending persons who commit such acts.
