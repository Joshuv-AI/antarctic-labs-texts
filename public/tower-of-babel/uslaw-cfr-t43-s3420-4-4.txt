43 C.F.R. § 3420.4-4 — -4 Consultation with Indian tribes.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3420.4-4
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3420.4-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3420 (Competitive Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary shall consult with any Indian tribe which may be affected by the adoption of the proposed regional lease sale schedule. The Secretary shall give the tribe 30 days in which to comment prior to adopting a lease sale schedule.
