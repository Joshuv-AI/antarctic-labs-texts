21 C.F.R. § 15.40 — Administrative record.
Citation: us:cfr:21:15.40
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-15.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 15 (Public Hearing Before The Commissioner). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The administrative record of a public hearing before the Commissioner consists of the following:
(1) All relevant Federal Register notices, including any documents to which they refer.
(2) All written submissions under § 15.25.
(3) The transcript of the oral hearing.
(b) The record of the administrative proceeding will be closed at the time specified in § 15.25.
