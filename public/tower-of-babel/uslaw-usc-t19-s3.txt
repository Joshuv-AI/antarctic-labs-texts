19 U.S.C. § 3 — Superintendence of collection of import duties
Citation: us:usc:19:3
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section3
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 1 (Collection Districts, Ports, And Officers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Treasury shall direct the superintendence of the collection of the duties on imports as he shall judge best.
