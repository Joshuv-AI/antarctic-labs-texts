9 C.F.R. § 202.119 — Rule 19: Fees of witnesses.
Citation: us:cfr:9:202.119
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Fair Trade Practices Program), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-202.119
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 202 (Rules Of Practice Governing Proceedings Under The Packers And Stockyards Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Witnesses subpoenaed before the presiding officer, and witnesses whose depositions are taken, shall be entitled to the same fees and mileage as are paid for like services in the courts of the United States. Fees and mileage shall be paid by the party at whose instance the witness appears or the deposition is taken.
