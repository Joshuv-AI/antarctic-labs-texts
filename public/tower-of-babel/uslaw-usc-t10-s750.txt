10 U.S.C. § 750 — Command: retired officers
Citation: us:usc:10:750
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section750
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 43 (Rank And Command). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A retired officer has no right to command except when on active duty.
