33 C.F.R. § 401.85 — Reporting of impairment or other hazard by vessels intending to transit the Seaway.
Citation: us:cfr:33:401.85
Creator: Great Lakes St. Lawrence Seaway Development Corporation, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-401.85
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 401 (Seaway Regulations And Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The master of any vessel which intends to transit the Seaway shall report to the nearest Seaway Station, prior to entering the Seaway, any of the conditions set out in paragraphs (a) through (d) of § 401.84.
