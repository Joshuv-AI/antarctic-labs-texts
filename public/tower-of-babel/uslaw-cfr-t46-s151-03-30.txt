46 C.F.R. § 151.03-30 — -30 Hazardous material.
Citation: us:cfr:46:151.03-30
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-151.03-30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 151 (Barges Carrying Bulk Liquid Hazardous Material Cargoes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In this part hazardous material means a liquid material or substance that is—
(a) Flammable or combustible;
(b) Designated a hazardous substance under section 311(b) of the Federal Water Pollution Control Act (33 U.S.C. 1321); or
(c) Designated a hazardous material under 49 U.S.C. 5103.
