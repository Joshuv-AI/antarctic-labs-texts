49 C.F.R. § 366.2T — T Form of designation.
Citation: us:cfr:49:366.2T
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-366.2T
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 366 (Designation Of Process Agent). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Designations shall be made on Form BOC-3, Designation of Agent for Service of Process. Only one completed current form may be on file. It must include all States for which agent designations are required. One copy must be retained by the carrier or broker at its principal place of business.
