28 C.F.R. § 41.54 — Employment criteria.
Citation: us:cfr:28:41.54
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-41.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 41 (Implementation Of Executive Order 12250, Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap In Federally Assisted Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A recipient may not use employment tests or criteria that discriminate against handicapped persons and shall ensure that employment tests are adapted for use by persons who have handicaps that impair sensory, manual, or speaking skills.
