48 C.F.R. § 19.502-7 — 19.502-7 Inclusion of Federal Prison Industries, Inc.
Citation: us:cfr:48:19.502-7
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-19.502-7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 19 (Small Business Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When using competitive procedures in accordance with 8.602(a)(4), agencies shall include Federal Prison Industries, Inc. (FPI), in the solicitation process and consider a timely offer from FPI.
