24 C.F.R. § 247.5 — Inapplicability to substantial rehabilitation or demolition.
Citation: us:cfr:24:247.5
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-247.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 247 (Evictions From Certain Subsidized And Hud-Owned Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart shall not apply in any case in which the landlord terminates the occupancy of a tenant as a direct result of a determination, concurred in by HUD, to substantially rehabilitate or demolish the project or to dispose of the project to a purchaser who purchases for the purpose of substantial rehabilitation or demolition.
