34 U.S.C. § 12572 — Allotment
Citation: us:usc:34:12572
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section12572
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 121 (Violent Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Subchapter VII (Police Corps And Law Enforcement Officers Training And Education); Part B (Law Enforcement Scholarship Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

From amounts appropriated under section 14119 of title 42, the Director shall allot—
(1)
80 percent of such amounts to States on the basis of the number of law enforcement officers in each State compared to the number of law enforcement officers in all States; and
(2)
20 percent of such amounts to States on the basis of the shortage of law enforcement personnel and the need for assistance under this part in the State compared to the shortage of law enforcement personnel and the need for assistance under this part in all States.
