40 C.F.R. § 300.100 — Duties of President delegated to federal agencies.
Citation: us:cfr:40:300.100
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-300.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 300 (National Oil And Hazardous Substances Pollution Contingency Plan). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In Executive Orders 12580 and 12777, the President delegated certain functions and responsibilities vested in him by the CWA, CERCLA, and the OPA.
