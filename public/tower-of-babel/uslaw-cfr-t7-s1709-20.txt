7 C.F.R. § 1709.20 — Member delegate clause.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1709.20
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1709.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1709 (Assistance To High Energy Cost Communities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each grant agreement under this part shall provide that no member of Congress shall be admitted to any share or part of a grant program or any benefit that may arise there from, but this provision shall not be construed to bar as a contractor under a grant a publicly held corporation whose ownership might include a member of Congress.
