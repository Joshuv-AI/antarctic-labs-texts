40 C.F.R. § 35.295 — Maximum federal share.
Citation: us:cfr:40:35.295
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-35.295
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 35 (State And Local Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Regional Administrator may provide State agencies up to 50 percent of the approved costs for the development and implementation of radon program activities.
