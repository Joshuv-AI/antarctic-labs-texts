23 C.F.R. § 656.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:23:656.1
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-656.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 656 (Carpool And Vanpool Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this regulation is to prescribe policies and general procedures for administering a program of ridesharing projects using Federal-aid primary, secondary, and urban system funds.
