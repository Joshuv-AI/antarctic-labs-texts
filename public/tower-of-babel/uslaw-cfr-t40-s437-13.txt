40 C.F.R. § 437.13 — Effluent limitations attainable by the application of the best available technology economically achievable (BAT).
Citation: us:cfr:40:437.13
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-437.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 437 (The Centralized Waste Treatment Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except as provided in 40 CFR 125.30 through 125.32 or § 437.10(b), any existing point source subject to this subpart must achieve the following effluent limitations representing the application of BAT: Limitations for antimony, arsenic, cadmium, chromium, cobalt, copper, lead, mercury, nickel, silver, tin, titanium, vanadium, and zinc are the same as the corresponding limitation specified in § 437.11(a).
(b) In-plant standards for cyanide are the same as the limitations specified in § 437.11(b).
