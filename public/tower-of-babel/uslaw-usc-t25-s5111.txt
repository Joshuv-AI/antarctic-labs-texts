25 U.S.C. § 5111 — Allotments or holdings outside of reservations
Citation: us:usc:25:5111
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section5111
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 45 (Protection Of Indians And Conservation Of Resources). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing contained in this Act shall be construed to relate to Indian holdings of allotments or homesteads upon the public domain outside of the geographic boundaries of any Indian reservation now existing or established hereafter.
