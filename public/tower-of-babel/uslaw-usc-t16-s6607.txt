16 U.S.C. § 6607 — Report to Congress
Citation: us:usc:16:6607
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section6607
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 85 (Marine Turtle Conservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than October 1, 2005, the Secretary shall submit to the Congress a report on the results and effectiveness of the program carried out under this chapter, including recommendations concerning how this chapter might be improved and whether the Fund should be continued in the future.
