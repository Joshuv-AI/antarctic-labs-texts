12 C.F.R. § 16.31 — Escrow requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:12:16.31
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-16.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 16 (Securities Offering Disclosure Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The OCC may require that any funds received in connection with an offer or sale of securities be held in an independent escrow account at an unrelated insured depository institution when the use of an escrow account is in the best interests of shareholders.
