21 C.F.R. § 1313.25 — Foreign import restrictions.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1313.25
Creator: Drug Enforcement Administration, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1313.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1313 (Importation And Exportation Of List I And List Ii Chemicals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any export from the United States in violation of the law of the country to which the chemical is exported is subject to the penalties of Title 21 United States Code 960(d).
