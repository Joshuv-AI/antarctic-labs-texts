25 C.F.R. § 83.8 — May the deadlines in this part be extended?
Citation: us:cfr:25:83.8
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-83.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 83 (Procedures For Federal Acknowledgment Of Indian Tribes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The AS-IA may extend any of the deadlines in this part upon a finding of good cause.
(b) For deadlines applicable to the Department, AS-IA may extend the deadlines upon the consent of the petitioner.
(c) If AS-IA grants a time extension, it will notify the petitioner and those listed in § 83.22(d).
