31 C.F.R. § 553.304 — Entity.
Citation: us:cfr:31:553.304
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-553.304
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 553 (Central African Republic Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term entity means a partnership, association, trust, joint venture, corporation, group, subgroup, or other organization.
