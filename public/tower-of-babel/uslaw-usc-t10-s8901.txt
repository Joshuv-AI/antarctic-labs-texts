10 U.S.C. § 8901 — Composition

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 887 (Naval Militia). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Naval Militia consists of the Naval Militia of the States, the District of Columbia, Guam, and the Virgin Islands.
