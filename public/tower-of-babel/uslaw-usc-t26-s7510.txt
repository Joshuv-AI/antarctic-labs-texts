26 U.S.C. § 7510 — Exemption from tax of domestic goods purchased for the United States
Citation: us:usc:26:7510
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section7510
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 77 (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The privilege existing by provision of law on December 1, 1873, or thereafter of purchasing supplies of goods imported from foreign countries for the use of the United States, duty free, shall be extended, under such regulations as the Secretary may prescribe, to all articles of domestic production which are subject to tax by the provisions of this title.
