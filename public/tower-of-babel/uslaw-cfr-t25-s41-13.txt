25 C.F.R. § 41.13 — For what activities can financial assistance to Tribal colleges and universities be used?
Citation: us:cfr:25:41.13
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-41.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 41 (Grants To Tribal Colleges And Universities And Diné College). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Tribal colleges and universities may use financial assistance under this subpart to defray expenditures for academic, educational, and administrative purposes and for the operation and maintenance of the college or university.
