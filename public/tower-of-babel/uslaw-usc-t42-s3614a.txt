42 U.S.C. § 3614a — Rules to implement subchapter
Citation: us:usc:42:3614a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section3614a
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 45 (Fair Housing); Subchapter I (Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may make rules (including rules for the collection, maintenance, and analysis of appropriate data) to carry out this subchapter. The Secretary shall give public notice and opportunity for comment with respect to all rules made under this section.
