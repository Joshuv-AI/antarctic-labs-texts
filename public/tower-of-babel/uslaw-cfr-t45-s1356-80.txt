45 C.F.R. § 1356.80 — Scope of the National Youth in Transition Database.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1356.80
Creator: Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1356.80
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1356 (Requirements Applicable To Title Iv-E). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirements of the National Youth in Transition Database (NYTD) §§ 1356.81 through 1356.86 of this part apply to the agency in any State, the District of Columbia, or Territory, that administers, or supervises the administration of the Chafee Foster Care Independence Program (CFCIP) under section 477 of the Social Security Act (the Act).
