5 C.F.R. § 2601.102 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:5:2601.102
Creator: Office Of Government Ethics
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-2601.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 2601 (Implementation Of Office Of Government Ethics Statutory Gift Acceptance Authority). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to establish guidelines governing the implementation of OGE's gift authority by defining its scope and application, by prescribing the policies, standards and procedures that govern the solicitation, acceptance and use of gifts, and by setting forth accounting requirements related to the use of this authority.
