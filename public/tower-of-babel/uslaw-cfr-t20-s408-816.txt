20 C.F.R. § 408.816 — When does SVB entitlement end due to death?
Citation: us:cfr:20:408.816
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-408.816
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 408 (Special Benefits For Certain World War Ii Veterans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Your SVB entitlement ends with the month in which you die. Payments are terminated effective with the month after the month of death.
