42 U.S.C. § 3057i — Grants authorized
Citation: us:usc:42:3057i
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section3057i
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 35 (Programs For Older Americans); Subchapter X (Grants For Native Americans); Part B (Native Hawaiian Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Assistant Secretary may make grants to public and nonprofit private organizations to pay all of the costs for the delivery of supportive services and nutrition services to older Native Hawaiians.
