Topological quantization of vector meson anomalous couplings
Chao-Qiang Geng, Chia-Wei Liu, Yue-Liang Wu

Abstract
We uncover a new anomalous term in hidden local symmetry that enforces the topological quantization of vector-meson anomalous couplings. Unlike existing formulations in the literature, which introduce several unquantized coefficients, our term removes this freedom by fixing the couplings to quantized, topologically determined values. We further conjecture that it saturates the anomaly, explaining the success of vector-meson dominance while pinpointing where saturation must fail. High-precision measurements of η ( ′ ) → π + π − γ ∗ \eta^{(\prime)}\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-}\gamma^{*} form factors at BESIII and the Super τ \tau -Charm Facility can provide a definitive experimental discriminator of this quantized picture.



I
Introduction

At low energies, the anomalous interactions of pseudoscalar mesons are encoded in the Wess–Zumino–Witten (WZW) action
Wess:1971yu
;
Witten:1983tw
. Its overall coefficient is fixed by anomaly matching and is quantized so that the path integral is defined unambiguously. Moreover, the corresponding anomalous couplings are protected from higher-order corrections by the Adler–Bardeen theorem
Adler:1969er
;
Adler:1969gk
, making the anomalous sector a rare case where a coefficient
of the chiral perturbation theory (
\chi
PT)
Weinberg:1978kz
;
Gasser:1983yg
;
Gasser:1984gg
;
Leutwyler:1993iq

is both theoretically controlled and directly tied to short-distance physics
Bai:2024lpq
.

Beyond the anomaly, most interactions in chiral effective theories are parameterized by low-energy constants (LECs), which encode short-distance QCD dynamics and must be determined from experiment or nonperturbative inputs
Bernard:1990kw
;
Bernard:1991zc
;
Meissner:1993ah
. Their number proliferates at higher orders
Bijnens:1994ie
;
Bijnens:1999hw
;
Bijnens:1999sh
;
Ebertshauser:2001nj
;
Li:2024ghg
, and interpreting individual LECs is often nontrivial. A complementary perspective is therefore provided by resonance realizations of chiral dynamics, in which LECs arise as Wilson coefficients after integrating out heavier states
Ecker:1988te
. For vector mesons, the hidden local symmetry (HLS) framework offers a systematic and unified description and leads to nontrivial relations among couplings
Bando:1984ej
;
Harada:2003jx
;
Harada:2011xx
;
Geng:2025fuc
. These relations reduce the number of independent parameters and are closely connected to the phenomenological success of vector-meson dominance (VMD)
Sakurai:1960ju
in radiative decays and transition form factors.

In this work, we consider the HLS framework supplemented by a WZW-like action that bridges these two viewpoints. We show that the newly introduced interaction can obey a quantization condition, and we study its phenomenological implications for anomalous radiative decays and transition form factors. In Sec.
II
, we present the formalism of the new action. In Sec.
III
, we examine the constraints from current data. In Sec.
IV
, we conclude.

IV
Conclusion

In this work, we studied the HLS framework supplemented by a WZW-like action and showed that the resulting anomalous interactions can satisfy a quantization condition once the winding numbers of
U
and
\xi_{L,R}
are no longer tied by a field identity. This construction generates the complete set of odd-intrinsic-parity vertices and motivates a quantized-saturation prediction that can partially account for the phenomenological success of VMD through cancellations in photon-related couplings. In particular, it implies the quantized values of
c_{i}
shown in Eq. (
11
). Confronting the framework with current data, we found that both the
\pi^{0}\to\gamma\gamma^{*}
slope and the
\eta
transition form factor are consistent with
N_{h}/N_{c}=2
, i.e., with the quantized expectation within present uncertainties.

From this perspective, quantized vector-meson anomalous couplings point to dynamics beyond the original HLS setup, such as extensions in which axial-vector mesons are incorporated through
\xi_{m}
so that
U
and
\xi_{L}^{\dagger}\xi_{R}
are topologically independent. We further identified the form factors of
\eta^{(\prime)}\to e^{+}e^{-}\mu^{+}\mu^{-}
and
\eta^{(\prime)}\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-}\ell^{+}\ell^{-}
as particularly sensitive probes that can provide a decisive experimental test of the quantized-HLS picture. Future measurements at BESIII and the Super Tau-Charm Facility
Achasov:2023gey
may determine whether the saturation hypothesis holds.
