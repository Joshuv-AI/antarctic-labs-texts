45 C.F.R. § 160.418 — Penalty not exclusive.
Citation: us:cfr:45:160.418
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-160.418
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 160 (General Administrative Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise provided by 42 U.S.C. 1320d-5(b)(1) and 42 U.S.C. 299b-22(f)(3), a penalty imposed under this part is in addition to any other penalty prescribed by law.
