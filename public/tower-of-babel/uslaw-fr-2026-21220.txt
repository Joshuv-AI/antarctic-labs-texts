DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE


Department of Defense Wage Committee (DoDWC); Notice of Federal 
Advisory Committee Meetings

AGENCY: Under Secretary of Defense for Personnel and Readiness 
(USD(P&R)), Department of Defense (DoD).

ACTION:  Notice of Closed Federal Advisory Committee meetings.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY:  The Department of Defense (referred to herein as ``the 
Department'', ``Department of War'' or ``DoW'') is publishing this 
notice to announce the following Federal Advisory Committee meetings of 
the DoW will take place.

DATES: 
    Tuesday, September 29, 2026, from 10:00 a.m. to 11:00 a.m. was 
closed to the public.
    Tuesday, October 13, 2026, from 10:00 a.m. to 11:30 a.m. was closed 
to the public.
    Tuesday, October 27, 2026, from 10:00 a.m. to 10:30 a.m. will be 
closed to the public.
    Tuesday, November 10, 2026, from 10:00 a.m. to 11:00 a.m. will be 
closed to the public.
    Tuesday, November 24, 2026, from 10:00 a.m. to 11:00 a.m. will be 
closed to the public.
    Tuesday, December 8, 2026, from 10:00 a.m. to 11:00 a.m. will be 
closed to the public.
    Tuesday, December 15, 2026, from 10:00 a.m. to 11:30 a.m. will be 
closed to the public.

ADDRESSES: Meetings will be held via Microsoft Teams.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Mr. Karl Fendt, Designated Federal 
Officer (DFO) (571) 372-1618 (voice), [email protected]. 
(email), 4800 Mark Center Drive, Suite 05G21, Alexandria, Virginia 
22350 (mailing address). Any agenda updates can be found at the DoDWC's 
official website: https://wageandsalary.dcpas.osd.mil/BWN/DODWC/.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: These meetings are held under the provisions 
of chapter 10 of 5, United States Code (U.S.C.) (commonly known as the 
``Federal Advisory Committee Act'' or ``FACA''), 5 U.S.C. 552b(c) and 
41 Code of Federal Regulations (CFR) parts 102-3.140 and 102-3.155.
    Purpose of the Meeting: The purpose of these meetings is to provide 
independent advice and recommendations on matters relating to the 
conduct of wage surveys and the establishment of wage schedules for all 
appropriated fund and non-appropriated fund areas of blue-collar 
employees within the DoW.

    Closing Remarks by Chair, Mr. Eric Clayton.
    Meeting Accessibility: Pursuant to 5 U.S.C. 552b(c)(4), the DoW has 
determined that the meetings shall be closed to the public. The 
USD(P&R), in consultation with the DoW Office of General Counsel, has 
determined in writing that each of these meetings is likely to disclose 
trade secrets and commercial or financial information obtained from a 
person and is privileged or confidential.
    Written Statements: Pursuant to 5 U.S.C. 1009(a)(3) and 41 CFR part 
102-3.140, interested persons may submit written statements to the DFO 
for the DoDWC at any time. Written statements should be submitted to 
the DFO at the email or mailing address listed above in FOR FUTHER 
INFORMATION CONTACT. If statements pertain to a specific topic being 
discussed at a planned meeting, then these statements must be submitted 
no later than five (5) business days prior to the meeting in question. 
Written statements received after this date may not be provided to or 
considered by the DoDWC until its next meeting. The DFO will review all 
timely submitted written statements and provide copies to all the 
committee members before the meetings that are the subject of this 
notice.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Due to negative impacts to pay for Federal 
Wage System employees that directly support national defense, the 7-day 
notice period was unable to be met for the September 29th and October 
13th meetings. The delay was unavoidable for the lead agency and 
additional delays would have increased the severity of negative 
implications for all agencies that employ Federal Wage System 
employees. Due to circumstances, beyond the control of the DFO and the 
DoW, the DoDWC was unable to provide public notification required by 41 
CFR. part 102-3.150(a) concerning its September 29, 2026, and October 
13, 20206 meetings. Accordingly, the Advisory Committee Management 
Officer for the DoW, pursuant to 41 CFR part 102-3.150(b), waives the 
public notification requirement.
