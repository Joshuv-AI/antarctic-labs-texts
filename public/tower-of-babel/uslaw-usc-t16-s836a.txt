16 U.S.C. § 836a — Rules governing issuance of license
Citation: us:usc:16:836a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section836a
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 12E (Niagara Power Project). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The license issued under the terms of this chapter shall be granted in conformance with Rules of Practice and Procedure of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, but in the event of any conflict, the provisions of this chapter shall govern in respect of the project herein authorized.
