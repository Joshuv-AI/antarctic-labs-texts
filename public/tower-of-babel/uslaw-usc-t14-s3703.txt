14 U.S.C. § 3703 — Coast Guard Reserve Boards
Citation: us:usc:14:3703
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section3703
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 37 (Coast Guard Reserve); Subchapter I (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Secretary shall convene a Coast Guard Reserve Policy Board at least annually to consider, recommend, and report to the Secretary on Reserve policy matters. At least one-half of the members of the Board shall be Reserve officers.
(b)
The Secretary may convene any other Reserve Board the Secretary considers necessary.
