39 C.F.R. § 230.39 — Submission of claims.
Citation: us:cfr:39:230.39
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-230.39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 230 (Office Of Inspector General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Claims submitted pursuant to this subpart must be submitted on Postal Service Form 1503, which may be obtained from the Executive Special Agent in Charge who has custody of the property.
