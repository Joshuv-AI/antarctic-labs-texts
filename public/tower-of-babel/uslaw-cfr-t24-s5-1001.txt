24 C.F.R. § 5.1001 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:24:5.1001
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-5.1001
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 5 (General Hud Program Requirements; Waivers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to all applicants for HUD grants, cooperative agreements, capital fund or operating fund subsidy, capital advance, or other assistance under HUD programs, including grant programs that are classified by OMB as including formula grant programs or activities, but excluding FHA insurance and loan guarantees that are not associated with a grant program or grant award.
