36 U.S.C. § 20704 — Membership
Citation: us:usc:36:20704
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section20704
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 207 (American Council Of Learned Societies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as provided in this chapter, eligibility for membership in the corporation and the rights and privileges of members are as provided in the constitution and bylaws of the corporation.
