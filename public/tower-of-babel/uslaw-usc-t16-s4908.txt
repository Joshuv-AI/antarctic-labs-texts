16 U.S.C. § 4908 — Call for information
Citation: us:usc:16:4908
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section4908
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 69 (Wild Exotic Bird Conservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Within one month after October 23, 1992, the Secretary shall issue a call for information on the wild bird conservation program of each country that exports exotic birds, by—
(1)
publishing a notice in the Federal Register requesting submission of such information to the Secretary by all interested persons; and
(2)
submitting a written request for such information through the Secretary of State to each country that exports exotic birds.
