10 U.S.C. § 2211 — Reimbursement for equipment, material, or services furnished members of the United Nations
Citation: us:usc:10:2211
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2211
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Property); Chapter 131 (Planning And Coordination). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Amounts paid by members of the United Nations for equipment or materials furnished, or services performed, in joint military operations shall be credited to appropriate appropriations of the Department of Defense in the manner authorized by section 632(d) of the Foreign Assistance Act of 1961 (22 U.S.C. 2392(d)).
