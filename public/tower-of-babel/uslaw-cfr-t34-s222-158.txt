34 C.F.R. § 222.158 — What procedures apply to the Secretary's review of an initial decision?
Citation: us:cfr:34:222.158
Creator: Office Of Elementary And Secondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-222.158
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 222 (Impact Aid Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the Secretary reviews an initial decision, the Secretary—
(a) Notifies the applicant in writing that it may file a written statement or comments; and
(b) Mails to each party written notice of the Secretary's final decision.
