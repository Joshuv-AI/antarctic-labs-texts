40 C.F.R. § 63.9918 — How do I demonstrate initial compliance with the operation and maintenance requirements that apply to me?
Citation: us:cfr:40:63.9918
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-63.9918
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 63 (National Emission Standards For Hazardous Air Pollutants For Source Categories). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must demonstrate initial compliance by certifying in your notification of compliance status that you have met the requirements in paragraphs (a) and (b) of this section.
(a) You have prepared the operation and maintenance plan according to the requirements in § 63.9910; and
(b) You will operate each control device according to the procedures in the plan.
