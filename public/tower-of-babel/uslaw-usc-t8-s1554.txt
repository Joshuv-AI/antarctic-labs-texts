8 U.S.C. § 1554 — Special immigrant inspectors at Washington
Citation: us:usc:8:1554
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title8-section1554
Context: United States Code, Title 8 (Aliens And Nationality); Chapter 13 (Immigration And Naturalization Service); Subchapter I (Organization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Special immigrant inspectors, not to exceed three, may be detailed for duty in the service at Washington.
