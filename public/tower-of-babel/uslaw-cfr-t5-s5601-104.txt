5 C.F.R. § 5601.104 — Prohibited outside employment.
Citation: us:cfr:5:5601.104
Creator: Postal Rate Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-5601.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 5601 (Supplemental Standards Of Ethical Conduct For Employees Of The Postal Regulatory Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee shall not engage in outside employment, either on a paid or unpaid basis, with or for an entity on the prohibited securities list described in § 5601.102(b)(1)(i) through (vi).
