10 C.F.R. § 708.40 — Notice of program requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:10:708.40
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-708.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 708 (Doe Contractor Employee Protection Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employers who are covered by this part must inform their employees about these regulations by posting notices in conspicuous places at the work site. These notices must include the name, address, telephone number, and website or email address of the DOE office where employees can file complaints under this part.
