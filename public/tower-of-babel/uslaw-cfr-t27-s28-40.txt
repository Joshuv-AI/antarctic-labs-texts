27 C.F.R. § 28.40 — Evidence of exportation: distilled spirits and wine.
Citation: us:cfr:27:28.40
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-28.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 28 (Exportation Of Alcohol). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The exportation of any shipment of distilled spirits or wine may be evidenced by:
(a) A copy of the export bill of lading (§ 28.250); or
(b) A copy of the railway express receipt (§ 28.251); or
(c) A copy of the air express receipt (§ 28.252); or
(d) A copy of the through bill of lading where exportation is to a contiguous foreign country (§ 28.250); or
(e) A certificate by the export carrier, as provided for in § 28.253.
