7 U.S.C. § 2563 — Injunction
Citation: us:usc:7:2563
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2563
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 57 (Plant Variety Protection); Subchapter III (Plant Variety Protection And Rights); Part L (Remedies For Infringement Of Plant Variety Protection, And Other Actions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The several courts having jurisdiction of cases under this subchapter may grant injunctions in accordance with the principles of equity to prevent the violation of any right hereunder on such terms as the court deems reasonable.
