46 C.F.R. § 58.25-45 — -45 Buffers.
Citation: us:cfr:46:58.25-45
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-58.25-45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 58 (Main And Auxiliary Machinery And Related Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For each vessel on an ocean, coastwise, or Great Lakes voyage, steering gear other than hydraulic must be designed with suitable buffering arrangements to relieve the gear from shocks to the rudder.
