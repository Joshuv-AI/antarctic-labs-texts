Abstract
. We show that the color restriction map Op h ​ ( 𝒮 ​ 𝒞 m , 𝒮 ​ 𝒞 n ) → Op h ​ ( ℰ m − 1 , ℰ n − 1 ) \mathrm{Op}^{h}({\mathcal{SC}}_{m},{\mathcal{SC}}_{n})\to\mathrm{Op}^{h}({\mathcal{E}}_{m-1},{\mathcal{E}}_{n-1}) from the derived mapping space of Swiss cheese operads to that of little discs operads, is a weak homotopy equivalence. We explain how this can help in the study of disc concordance embedding spaces.



1.
Introduction

The manifold functor calculus was invented by T. Goodwillie and M. Weiss in order to study embedding spaces
[
12
,
24
]
. In its heart this approach replaces embedding spaces by induced maps of configuration spaces.
In its modern formulations the calculus approximations are described in terms of module maps over the little discs operad
[
1
,
3
,
16
,
22
]

The approach can be generalized to spaces of
proper
embeddings (that send boundary to boundary), for which instead of modules over the little discs operad one would need to use modules over the Swiss cheese operad.
(To the best of our knowledge
such generalization(s) have not been written anywhere yet.) In other words, one would need to look at the configuration spaces of points both in the interior and in the boundary of the source and target manifolds, whose interaction is encoded
by the Swiss Cheese operad. The main result of the paper is not a generalization of any previous result, but rather a precoursor and a key for such generalizations, for example, for all findings from
[
1
,
4
,
5
,
8
]

as it is outlined below.

2.1.
The Fulton-MacPherson version of the Swiss cheese operad

The Fulton-MacPherson operad
\mathcal{F}_{m}
will be used as a model of the little discs operad
{\mathcal{E}}_{m}
. Its

j

\mathcal{F}_{m}(j)
is defined as a compactification of the configuration

j

{\mathbb{R}}^{m}
modulo translations and postitive rescalings:
C(j,{\mathbb{R}}^{m})/{\mathbb{R}}_{+}\ltimes{\mathbb{R}}^{m}
.
One has
\mathcal{F}_{m}(0)=\mathcal{F}_{m}(1)=*
, while each
\mathcal{F}_{m}(j)
,
j\geq 2
, is a smooth (and semi-algebraic) compact
manifold with corners of dimension
mj-m-1
. The strata of
\mathcal{F}_{m}(j)
are in one-to-one correspondence
with rooted trees whose leaves are bijectively labelled by
1,2,\ldots,j
and whose all internal vertices are
of arity
\geq 2
. For more details, see
[
9
]
.

For a model of the Swiss cheese operad
{\mathcal{SC}}_{m}
, we will use its similarly defined Fulton-MacPherson version,
that we denote by
{\mathcal{FS}}_{m}
, see
[
23
,
26
]
, where this operad was introduced and studied. Its second output components are defined as follows:

{\mathcal{FS}}_{m}(i,j)_{2}=\begin{cases}{\mathcal{F}}_{m}(j),&\text{if }i=0;\\
\emptyset&\text{if }i\geq 1.\end{cases}

(In particular,
({\mathcal{FS}}_{m})_{2}={\mathcal{F}}_{m}
.) Its each first output component
{\mathcal{FS}}_{m}(i,j)_{1}
is defined as a compactification of
the configuration space of
i
labelled points in
{\mathbb{R}}^{m-1}\times\{0\}
and
j
points in
{\mathbb{R}}^{m-1}\times(0,+\infty)
quotiented out by translations and rescalings:

\left.\Bigl(C(i,{\mathbb{R}}^{m-1}\times\{0\})\times C(j,{\mathbb{R}}^{m-1}\times(0,+\infty))\Bigr)\right/{\mathbb{R}}_{+}\ltimes{\mathbb{R}}^{m-1}.

One has
{\mathcal{FS}}_{m}(0,0)_{1}={\mathcal{FS}}_{m}(1,0)_{1}={\mathcal{FS}}_{m}(0,1)_{1}=*
and
({\mathcal{FS}}_{m})_{1}={\mathcal{F}}_{m-1}
, while for
i+2j\geq 2
, each component
{\mathcal{FS}}_{m}(i,j)_{1}
is a smooth (and semi-algebraic) compact manifold with corners
of dimension
(m-1)i+mj-m
. For example,
{\mathcal{FS}}_{m}(1,1)_{1}=D^{m-1}
. We agree to label the strata of
({\mathcal{FS}}_{m})_{1}={\mathcal{F}}_{m-1}
by trees with solid edges, while those of
({\mathcal{FS}}_{m})_{2}={\mathcal{F}}_{m}
by trees with dashed edges.
The strata of
{\mathcal{FS}}_{m}(i,j)_{1}
,
j\geq 1
, are labelled by trees with two types of edges: solid and dashed, and satisfying the following properties: output is solid; there are
i
solid inputs and
j
dashed inputs
labelled by
1,\ldots,i
and
1,\ldots,j
, respectively; arity of each internal vertex is
\geq 1
; if the arity of such vertex is one, then its incoming edge is dashed and its outgoing edge is solid; if a vertex has a solid incoming edge, then its outgoing edge must also be solid.

As example,
{\mathcal{FS}}_{m}(0,2)_{1}
is homeomorphic to
S^{m-1}\times[0,+\infty]
and consists of five strata. The interior is labelled by

. The boundary part
S^{m-1}\times\{+\infty\}
is labelled by

.
The part

is a union of two
(m-1)
-discs

and

(m-2)
-sphere

.
This homeomorphism and its general version in the following lemma are crucial for the proof of

Lemma 2.1
.

For
j\geq 2
, the pair
\bigl({\mathcal{FS}}_{m}(0,j)_{1},{\mathcal{FS}}_{m}(0,1)_{1}\circ_{1}{\mathcal{FS}}_{m}(0,j)_{2}\bigr)
is
\Sigma_{j}
-equivariantly homeomorphic to
\bigl({\mathcal{F}}_{m}(j)\times[0,+\infty],{\mathcal{F}}_{m}(j)\times\{+\infty\}\bigr)
.

Idea of the proof.

The map
{\mathcal{FS}}_{m}(0,j)_{1}\to{\mathcal{F}}_{m}(j)
on the first factor is the closure of the map of interiors induced by the obvious inclusion
C(j,{\mathbb{R}}^{m-1}\times(0,+\infty))\to C(j,{\mathbb{R}}^{m})
. The map

{\mathcal{FS}}_{m}(0,j)_{1}\to[0,+\infty]
is induced by the map
C(j,{\mathbb{R}}^{m-1}\times(0,+\infty))\to(0,+\infty)
sending

(x_{1},\ldots,x_{j})\mapsto\frac{\underset{1\leq\ell\leq j}{\min}(x_{\ell})_{m}}{\underset{1\leq\ell_{1}<\ell_{2}\leq j}{\max}\bigl(|x_{\ell_{2}}-x_{\ell_{1}}|\bigr)}.

One can easily check that the maps extend continuously on the boundary of
{\mathcal{FS}}_{m}(0,j)_{1}
. The resulting map

{\mathcal{FS}}_{m}(0,j)_{1}\to{\mathcal{F}}_{m}(j)\times[0,+\infty]

is a continuous bijection between Hausdorff compact spaces and therefore is a homeomorphism.
∎

2.2.
Homotopy setting

We work in the Reedy model category
\mathrm{Op}
of reduced topological operads and
{\mathrm{T}}_{k}\mathrm{Op}
of
k
-truncated reduced operads. We usually deal with two-colored operads. By a

k
-truncated operad
\mathcal{P}
we mean a collection of components
\mathcal{P}(i,j)_{r}
,
i+j\leq k
,
r\in\{1,2\}
, with the composition operations of usual operads whenever such maps are defined.
For the usual one-colored ones, these model category structures were constructed and studied in
[
6
]
. In particular, it was proved in
[
7
]
that the derived mapping space of (truncated) operads
is the same for the Reedy model structure and the usual projective one
[
2
]
. We denote by
\mathrm{Op}(-,-)
,
{\mathrm{T}}_{k}\mathrm{Op}(-,-)
,
\mathrm{Op}^{h}(-,-)
,
{\mathrm{T}}_{k}\mathrm{Op}^{h}(-,-)
the corresponding mapping spaces and derived mapping spaces, respectively.

One has a natural truncation functor
{\mathrm{T}}_{k}\colon\mathrm{Op}\to{\mathrm{T}}_{k}\mathrm{Op}
that forgets the components of combined arity
>k
. To simplify notation instead of
{\mathrm{T}}_{k}\mathrm{Op}({\mathrm{T}}_{k}\mathcal{P},{\mathrm{T}}_{k}\mathcal{Q})
we will be writing
{\mathrm{T}}_{k}\mathrm{Op}(\mathcal{P},\mathcal{Q})
.
We will follow the same convention for the derived mapping spaces.

A possibly truncated operad
\mathcal{F}
is Reedy cofibrant if and only if its suboperad
\mathcal{F}^{>0}
of positive arity components is projectively cofibrant, see
[
6
]
. For example,
\mathcal{F}_{m}
and
{\mathcal{FS}}_{m}
are Reedy
cofibrant.

For any component
\mathcal{P}(i,j)_{r}
of a 2-colored operad, one can define an
(i+j)
-cubical diagram
\mathcal{P}(i-\bullet,j-\bullet)_{r}
obtained by composing
\mathcal{P}(i,j)_{r}
with the arity zero operations. Its matching object
{\mathrm{M}}\mathcal{P}(i,j)_{r}
is defined as the limit of the subcubical diagram obtained from
\mathcal{P}(i-\bullet,j-\bullet)_{r}
by removing its initial object
\mathcal{P}(i,j)_{r}
.
An operad
\mathcal{P}
is Reedy fibrant if for all
i,j\geq 0
,
r\in\{1,2\}
, the map
\mathcal{P}(i,j)_{r}\to{\mathrm{M}}\mathcal{P}(i,j)_{r}
is a Serre fibration. For any Reedy fibrant operad,
{\mathrm{M}}\mathcal{P}(i,j)_{r}
is equivalent to the homotopy limit of the corresponding
subcubical part of
\mathcal{P}(i-\bullet,j-\bullet)_{r}
. We refer to
[
8
]
, where these facts are stated and proved in the case of one-colored operads. The two-colored generalization is straightforward.

For our proof we also need to consider the Reedy model category
{\mathrm{T}}_{k-1,\ell}\mathrm{Op}
of
(k-1,\ell)
-truncated operads in which components have either combined arity
\leq k-1
or have arity
k
with
\leq\ell
inputs of the second color.
By construction, the truncation functors
{\mathrm{T}}_{k}\colon\mathrm{Op}\to{\mathrm{T}}_{k}\mathrm{Op}
and
{\mathrm{T}}_{k-1,\ell}\colon\mathrm{Op}\to{\mathrm{T}}_{k-1,\ell}\mathrm{Op}
preserve fibrant objects. The former one preserves cofibrant objects, while the latter preserves cofibrant objects

\mathcal{F}
in which
\mathcal{F}(1,0)_{2}=\emptyset
(and hence, by composing with the arity zero operations, all
\mathcal{F}(i,j)_{2}=\emptyset
,
i\geq 1
).
