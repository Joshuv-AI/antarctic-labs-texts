43 C.F.R. § 3179.92 — Size of production equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3179.92
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3179.92
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3170 (Onshore Oil And Gas Production). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Production and processing equipment must be of sufficient size to accommodate the volumes of production expected to occur at the lease site.
