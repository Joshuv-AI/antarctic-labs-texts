46 C.F.R. § 168.15-5 — -5 Location of crew spaces.
Citation: us:cfr:46:168.15-5
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-168.15-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 168 (Civilian Nautical School Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Quarters must be located so that sufficient fresh air and light are obtainable compatible with accepted practice or good arrangement and construction.
(b) Unless approved by the Commandant, quarters, must not be located forward of the collision bulkhead, nor may such section or sections of any deck head occupied by quarters be below the deepest load line.
