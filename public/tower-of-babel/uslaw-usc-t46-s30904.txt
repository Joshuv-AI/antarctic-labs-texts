46 U.S.C. § 30904 — Exclusive remedy
Citation: us:usc:46:30904
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section30904
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 309 (Suits In Admiralty Against The United States). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If a remedy is provided by this chapter, it shall be exclusive of any other action arising out of the same subject matter against the officer, employee, or agent of the United States or the federally-owned corporation whose act or omission gave rise to the claim.
