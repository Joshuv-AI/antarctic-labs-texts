36 U.S.C. § 151102 — Purposes

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section151102
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1511 (National Education Association Of The United States). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purposes of the corporation are—
(1)
to elevate the character and advance the interests of the profession of teaching; and
(2)
to promote the cause of education in the United States.
