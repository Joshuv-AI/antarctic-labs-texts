45 C.F.R. § 1800.5 — Access to requested information to the individual.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1800.5
Creator: Harry S. Truman Scholarship Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1800.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1800 (Privacy Act Of 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon verification of identity the Foundation shall disclose to the individual the information contained in the record which pertains to that individual.
