16 U.S.C. § 241f — Extension of exchange authority
Citation: us:usc:16:241f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section241f
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter XXVI (Theodore Roosevelt National Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The land exchange authority relating to Theodore Roosevelt National Park prescribed by sections 241b and 243 of this title shall be applicable also to the lands described in section 241c of this title.
