19 C.F.R. § 220.10 — Commission publication and public availability of petitions.
Citation: us:cfr:19:220.10
Creator: United States International Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-220.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 220 (Process For Consideration Of Petitions For Duty Suspensions And Reductions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Not later than 30 days after expiration of the 60-day period for filing petitions for duty suspensions and reductions, the Commission will publish on its website the petitions for duty suspensions and reductions submitted under § 220.3 that were timely filed and contain the information required under § 220.5. When circumstances allow, the Commission may post such petitions on its website earlier than 30 days after expiration of the 60-day period for filing petitions.
