7 U.S.C. § 1765d — Acquisition of property
Citation: us:usc:7:1765d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1765d
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 43 (Foreign Market Development); Subchapter II (United States Agricultural Trade Offices). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Upon the request of the Secretary of Agriculture, the Secretary of State may use the authorities contained in the Foreign Service Buildings Act, 1926 [22 U.S.C. 292 et seq.], to acquire sites and buildings, including living quarters, for the purpose of establishing United States Agricultural Trade Offices.
