43 U.S.C. § 1555 — Federal Power Act inapplicable to Colorado River between Hoover Dam and Glen Canyon Dam
Citation: us:usc:43:1555
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section1555
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 32 (Colorado River Basin Project); Subchapter V (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Part I of the Federal Power Act [16 U.S.C. 791a et seq.] shall not be applicable to the reaches of the main stream of the Colorado River between Hoover Dam and Glen Canyon Dam until and unless otherwise provided by Congress.
