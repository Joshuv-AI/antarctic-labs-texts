36 C.F.R. § 13.1008 — Solid waste disposal.
Citation: us:cfr:36:13.1008
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-13.1008
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 13 (National Park System Units In Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A solid waste disposal site may accept non-National Park Service solid waste generated within the boundaries of the park area.
(b) A solid waste disposal site may be located within one mile of facilities as defined by this part so long as it does not degrade natural or cultural resources of the park area.
