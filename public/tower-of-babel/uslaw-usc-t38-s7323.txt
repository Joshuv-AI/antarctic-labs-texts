38 U.S.C. § 7323 — Required consultations with nurses
Citation: us:usc:38:7323
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section7323
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part V (Boards, Administrations, And Services); Chapter 73 (Veterans Health Administration—Organization And Functions); Subchapter II (General Authority And Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Under Secretary for Health shall ensure that—
(1)
the director of a geographic service area, in formulating policy relating to the provision of patient care, shall consult regularly with a senior nurse executive or senior nurse executives; and
(2)
the director of a medical center shall include a registered nurse as a member of any committee used at that medical center to provide recommendations or decisions on medical center operations or policy affecting clinical services, clinical outcomes, budget, or resources.
