44 C.F.R. § 4.3 — What programs and activities of FEMA are subject to these regulations?
Citation: us:cfr:44:4.3
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-4.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 4 (Intergovernmental Review Of Federal Emergency Management Agency (Fema) Programs And Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator publishes in the Federal Register a list of FEMA's programs and activities that are subject to these regulations and identifies which of these are subject to the requirements of section 204 of the Demonstration Cities and Metropolitan Development Act.
