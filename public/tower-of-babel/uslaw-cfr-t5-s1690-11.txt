5 C.F.R. § 1690.11 — Plan year.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1690.11
Creator: Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1690.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1690 (Thrift Savings Plan). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Thrift Savings Plan's plan year is established on a calendar-year basis for all purposes, except where another applicable provision of law requires that a fiscal year or other basis be used. As used in this section, the term “calendar-year basis” means a twelve-month period beginning on January 1 and ending on December 31 of the same year.
