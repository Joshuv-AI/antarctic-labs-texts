25 C.F.R. § 36.95 — What sanitary standards must homeliving programs meet?
Citation: us:cfr:25:36.95
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-36.95
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 36 (Minimum Academic Standards For The Basic Education Of Indian Children And National Criteria For Dormitory Situations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each homeliving program must meet all of the following standards:
(a) Restrooms, showers, and common areas must be cleaned daily;
(b) Rooms must be cleaned daily;
(c) Linens must be changed and cleaned weekly;
(d) Linens are to be provided;
(e) Basic Toiletries must be provided; and
(f) Functional washing machines and dryers must be provided.
