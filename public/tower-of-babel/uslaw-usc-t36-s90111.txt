36 U.S.C. § 90111 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:36:90111
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section90111
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 901 (Help America Vote Foundation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to the foundation for carrying out the purposes of this chapter—
(1)
$5,000,000 for fiscal year 2003; and
(2)
such sums as may be necessary for each succeeding fiscal year.
