50 C.F.R. § 92.2 — Authority.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 92 (Migratory Bird Subsistence Harvest In Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).
