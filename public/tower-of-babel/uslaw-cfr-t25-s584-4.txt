25 C.F.R. § 584.4 — Are motions permitted?
Citation: us:cfr:25:584.4
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-584.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 584 (Appeals Before A Presiding Official). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. Motions to intervene under § 584.5 are permitted. Motions for an extension of time that are filed before the appointment of a presiding official shall be decided by the Commission. All other motions may be scheduled and heard at the discretion of the presiding official.
