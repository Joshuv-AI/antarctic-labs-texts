8 C.F.R. § 236.17 — Eligibility for Federal financial assistance programs.
Citation: us:cfr:8:236.17
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-236.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 236 (Apprehension And Detention Of Inadmissible And Deportable Aliens; Removal Of Aliens Ordered Removed). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An alien granted Family Unity Program benefits based on a relationship to a legalized alien as defined in § 236.11 is ineligible for public welfare assistance in the same manner and for the same period as the legalized alien who is ineligible for such assistance under section 245A(h) or 210(f) of the Act, respectively.
