46 U.S.C. § 55115 — Supplies on fish processing vessels
Citation: us:usc:46:55115
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section55115
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part D (Promotional Programs); Chapter 551 (Coastwise Trade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Section 55102 of this title does not apply to supplies aboard a United States documented fish processing vessel that are necessary and used for processing or assembling fishery products aboard such a vessel.
