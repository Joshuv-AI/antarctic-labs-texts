16 U.S.C. § 4007 — Voluntary payments
Citation: us:usc:16:4007
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section4007
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 60 (Fish And Seafood Promotion). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person may make voluntary payments to assist the National Council to carry out its annual marketing and promotion plan and annual budget. The Secretary shall deposit such payments into the Fund.
