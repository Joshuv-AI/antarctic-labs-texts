46 U.S.C. § 10319 — Costs of a criminal conviction
Citation: us:usc:46:10319
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section10319
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part G (Merchant Seamen Protection And Relief); Chapter 103 (Foreign And Intercoastal Voyages). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In a proceeding about a seaman’s wages, if it is shown that the seaman was convicted during the voyage of an offense by a competent tribunal and sentenced by the tribunal, the court hearing the case may direct that a part of the wages due the seaman, but not more than $15, be applied to reimburse the master for costs properly incurred in procuring the conviction and sentence.
