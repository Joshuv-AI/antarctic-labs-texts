28 U.S.C. § 2642 — Analysis of imported merchandise
Citation: us:usc:28:2642
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section2642
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part VI (Particular Proceedings); Chapter 169 (Court Of International Trade Procedure). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Court of International Trade may order an analysis of imported merchandise and reports thereon by laboratories or agencies of the United States or laboratories accredited by the Customs Service under section 499(b) of the Tariff Act of 1930.
