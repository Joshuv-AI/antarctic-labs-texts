29 C.F.R. § 102.131 — Solicitation of prohibited communications.
Citation: us:cfr:29:102.131
Creator: National Labor Relations Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-102.131
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 102 (Rules And Regulations, Series 8). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person may knowingly and willfully solicit the making of an unauthorized ex parte communication by any other person.
