42 C.F.R. § 440.225 — Optional services.
Citation: us:cfr:42:440.225
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-440.225
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 440 (Services: General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any of the services defined in subpart A of this part that are not required under §§ 440.210 and 440.220 may be furnished under the State plan at the State's option.
