22 U.S.C. § 3310a — Commercial personnel at American Institute of Taiwan
Citation: us:usc:22:3310a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section3310a
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 48 (Taiwan Relations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The American Institute of Taiwan shall employ personnel to perform duties similar to those performed by personnel of the United States and Foreign Commercial Service. The number of individuals employed shall be commensurate with the number of United States personnel of the Commercial Service who are permanently assigned to the United States diplomatic mission to South Korea.
