5 C.F.R. § 846.704 — Irrevocability of an election of FERS coverage.
Citation: us:cfr:5:846.704
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-846.704
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 846 (Federal Employees Retirement System—Elections Of Coverage). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An election to be covered by FERS becomes irrevocable on the date it becomes effective.
(b) If, during the 1998 open enrollment period, an employee files an election on an SF 3109 to remain covered by CSRS, the employee may revoke such an election by filing another election during the 1998 open enrollment period.
