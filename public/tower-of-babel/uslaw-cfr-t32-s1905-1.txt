32 C.F.R. § 1905.1 — Scope and purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1905.1
Creator: Central Intelligence Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1905.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1905 (Production Of Official Records Or Disclosure Of Official Information In Proceedings Before Federal, State Or Local Governmental Entities Of Competent Jurisdiction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth the policy and procedures with respect to the production or disclosure of (a) material contained in the files of CIA, (b) information relating to or based upon material contained in the files of CIA, and (c) information acquired by any person while such person was an employee of CIA as part of the performance of that person's official duties or because of that person's association with CIA.
