34 C.F.R. § 637.41 — What are the cost restrictions on design project grants?
Citation: us:cfr:34:637.41
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-637.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 637 (Minority Science And Engineering Improvement Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For design project grants funds may not be used to pay more than fifty percent of the academic year salaries of faculty members involved in the project.
