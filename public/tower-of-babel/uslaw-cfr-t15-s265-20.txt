15 C.F.R. § 265.20 — Towing of improperly parked vehicles.
Citation: us:cfr:15:265.20
Creator: National Institute Of Standards And Technology, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-265.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 265 (Regulations Governing Traffic And Conduct On The Grounds Of The National Institute Of Standards & Technology, Gaithersburg, Maryland, And Boulder And Fort Collins, Colorado). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any motor vehicle that is parked in violation of these regulations may be towed away or otherwise moved if a determination is made by a uniformed guard that it is a nuisance or hazard. A reasonable amount for the moving service and for the storage of the vehicle, if any, may be charged, and the vehicle is subject to a lien for that charge.
