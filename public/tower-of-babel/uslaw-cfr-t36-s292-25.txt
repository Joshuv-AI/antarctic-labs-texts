36 C.F.R. § 292.25 — Information requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:36:292.25
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-292.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 292 (National Recreation Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The information required by § 292.24 of this subpart in order for a landowner to obtain a determination of compliance constitutes an information requirement as defined in the Paperwork Reduction Act (44 U.S.C. 3507) and has been approved for use by the Office of Management and Budget and assigned control number 0596-0135.
