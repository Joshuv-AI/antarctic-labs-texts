21 C.F.R. § 211.196 — Distribution records.
Citation: us:cfr:21:211.196
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-211.196
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 211 (Current Good Manufacturing Practice For Finished Pharmaceuticals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Distribution records shall contain the name and strength of the product and description of the dosage form, name and address of the consignee, date and quantity shipped, and lot or control number of the drug product.
