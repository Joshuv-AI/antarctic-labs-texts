50 C.F.R. § 13.47 — Inspection requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:50:13.47
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-13.47
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 13 (General Permit Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person holding a permit under this subchapter B shall allow the Director's agent to enter his premises at any reasonable hour to inspect any wildlife or plant held or to inspect, audit, or copy any permits, books, or records required to be kept by regulations of this subchapter B.
