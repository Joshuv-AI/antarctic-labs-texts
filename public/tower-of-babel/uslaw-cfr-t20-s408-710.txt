20 C.F.R. § 408.710 — What must your report include?
Citation: us:cfr:20:408.710
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-408.710
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 408 (Special Benefits For Certain World War Ii Veterans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When you make a report, you must tell us—
(a) The name and social security number of the person to whom the report applies;
(b) The event you are reporting and the date it happened; and
(c) Your name if you are not the person to whom the report applies.
