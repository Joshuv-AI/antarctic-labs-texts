On the methods of reduction of some types of Marczerwski-Burstin measurable functions to continuous functions on products of perfect sets
Waldemar Hołubowski, Sławomir Kusiński

Abstract
In this paper we provide product-wise generalizations of certain Marczewski-Burstin bases such as sets with (s)-property and completely Ramsey sets. For each of those we prove - as an analog of classical Luzin’s and Eggleston’s theorems - that functions measurable with respect to those families can be reduced to continuous functions on a product of perfect sets. Moreover we also provide a way to reduce sequences of such functions to continuity, providing generalizations to Laver’s extension of Halpern-Lauchli and Harrington theorems.

f_{n}|_{P}
is continuous.

Proof:

Let the sets
R_{m,0}\subseteq X_{m}
be such
that the function
f_{0}
is continuous on the product
\prod\limits_{m\in\omega}R_{m,0}
.
Let
P_{0,(0)}
and
P_{0,(1)}
be two disjoint relative base sets in
R_{0,0}
of length at least
1
.

Assume inductively that for some
n\in\omega

we have defined the sets
R_{m,n}
for
m>n

and
P_{m,t}
for
m\leq n
and
t\in 2^{n+1}

such that the functions
f_{k}
for
k\leq n
are continuous on the set

\prod\limits_{m\leq n}\bigcup\limits_{t\in 2^{n+1}}P{m,t}\times\prod\limits_{m>n}R_{m,n}.

As there is finitely many sets
P_{0,t_{0}}\times\ldots\times P_{n,t_{n}}\times\prod\limits_{m>n}R_{m,n}

we can choose the sets
R_{m,n+1}\subseteq R_{m,n}
for
m>n

as well as

for
m\leq n
and
t\in 2^{n+1}

\prod\limits_{m\leq n}\bigcup\limits_{t\in 2^{n+1}}Q_{m,t}\times\prod\limits_{m>n}R_{m,n+1}.

In each set

P_{m,t^{\smallfrown}0},P_{m,t^{\smallfrown}1}

n+1

and in the set

Clearly each set
P_{m}

Theorem 8
.

be
(v^{\omega})
-measurable functions.
Then there exists a Silver cube
Q=\prod\limits_{m\in\omega}Q_{m}

such that each

is continuous.

Proof:

Q_{m,0}=2^{\omega}

i_{0,0}=0
.

is continuous for

(2^{n})^{n}=\{\alpha_{k}\colon k\in 2^{n^{2}}\}

k\in 2^{n}

and

,
where
