2 U.S.C. § 1965a — Prohibition on use of funds for installation of permanent fencing on Capitol Grounds
Citation: us:usc:2:1965a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1965a
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 29 (Capitol Police); Subchapter II (Powers And Duties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

None of the funds made available in this or any other Act in prior fiscal years, this fiscal year, or any fiscal year thereafter may be used to install permanent, above-ground fencing around the perimeter, or any portion thereof, of the United States Capitol Grounds, as described in section 5102 of title 40.
