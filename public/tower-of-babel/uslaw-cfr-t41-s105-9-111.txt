41 C.F.R. § 105-9.111 — -9.111 Age distinctions contained in General Services Administration regulations generally.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-9.111
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-9.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-9 (9—Discrimination Prohibited On The Basis Of Age). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any age distinctions contained in a rule or regulation issued by GSA are presumed to be necessary to the achievement of a statutory objective of the program or activity to which the rule or regulation applies.
