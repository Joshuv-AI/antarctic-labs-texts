9 C.F.R. § 50.22 — Claims not allowed.
Citation: us:cfr:9:50.22
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-50.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 50 (Animals Destroyed Because Of Tuberculosis). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department will not allow claims for payment if the claimant has failed to comply with any of the requirements of this subpart, or there is substantial evidence, as determined by the Administrator, that the claimant has been responsible for any attempt to obtain payment funds for such cattle or other dairy property unlawfully or improperly.
