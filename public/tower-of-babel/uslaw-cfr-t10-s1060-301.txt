10 C.F.R. § 1060.301 — Government employees.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1060.301
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1060.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1060 (Payment Of Travel Expenses Of Persons Who Are Not Government Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this part shall be interpreted as being applicable to authorization or approval of payment of travel expenses of Government employees, including DOE employees.
