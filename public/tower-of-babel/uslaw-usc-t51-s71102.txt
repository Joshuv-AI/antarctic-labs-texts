51 U.S.C. § 71102 — Requests for information
Citation: us:usc:51:71102
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section71102
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 711 (Near-Earth Objects). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator shall issue requests for information on—
(1)
a low-cost space mission with the purpose of rendezvousing with, attaching a tracking device,1 and characterizing the Apophis asteroid; and
(2)
a medium-sized space mission with the purpose of detecting near-Earth objects equal to or greater than 140 meters in diameter.
