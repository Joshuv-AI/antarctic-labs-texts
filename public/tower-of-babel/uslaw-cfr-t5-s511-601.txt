5 C.F.R. § 511.601 — Applicability of regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:5:511.601
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-511.601
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 511 (Classification Under The General Schedule). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to a request from an employee or an agency for the Office to review the classification of a position subject to chapter 51 of title 5, United States Code, or for the Office to determine whether a position is subject to that chapter.
