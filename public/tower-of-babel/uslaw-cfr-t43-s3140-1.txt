43 C.F.R. § 3140.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3140.1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3140.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3140 (Leasing In Special Tar Sand Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this subpart is to provide for the conversion of existing oil and gas leases and valid claims based on mineral locations within Special Tar Sand Areas to combined hydrocarbon leases.
