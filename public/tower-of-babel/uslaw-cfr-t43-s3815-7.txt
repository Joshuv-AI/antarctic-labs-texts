43 C.F.R. § 3815.7 — Mining claims subject to stock driveway withdrawals.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3815.7
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3815.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3810 (Lands And Minerals Subject To Location). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Mining claims on lands within stock driveway withdrawals, located prior to May 4, 1929, and subsequent to the date of the withdrawal, may be held and perfected subject to the provisions and regulations in this section.
