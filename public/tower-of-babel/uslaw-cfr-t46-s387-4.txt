46 C.F.R. § 387.4 — Applications.
Citation: us:cfr:46:387.4
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-387.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 387 (Utilization And Disposal Of Surplus Federal Real Property For Development Or Operation Of A Port Facility). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Application forms for conveyance of Surplus Property can be obtained from the Maritime Administration, Division of Ports, 400 Seventh Street, SW, Washington, DC 20590. The applicant shall identify on the application form the requested property, agree to the terms/conditions of the conveyance and shall also submit a Port Facility Redevelopment Plan (PFRP) which details the plan of use for the property and the associated economic development plan.
