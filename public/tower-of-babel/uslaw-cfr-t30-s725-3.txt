30 C.F.R. § 725.3 — Authority.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 725 (Reimbursements To States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 502(e)(4) of the Surface Mining Control and Reclamation Act of 1977 (30 U.S.C. 1201) authorizes the Secretary to reimburse States for costs of enforcing the performance standards of the initial regulatory program.
