14 C.F.R. § 1271.210 — Reporting.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1271 (New Restrictions On Lobbying). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No reporting is required with respect to payments of reasonable compensation made to regularly employed officers or employees of a person.
