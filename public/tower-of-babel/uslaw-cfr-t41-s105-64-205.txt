41 C.F.R. § 105-64.205 — -64.205 Who will provide access to my record?
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-64.205
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-64.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-64 (64—Gsa Privacy Act Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The system manager will make a record available to you on request, unless special conditions apply, such as for medical, law enforcement, and security records.
