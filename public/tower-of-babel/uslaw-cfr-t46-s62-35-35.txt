46 C.F.R. § 62.35-35 — -35 Starting systems for internal-combustion engines.
Citation: us:cfr:46:62.35-35
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-62.35-35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 62 (Vital System Automation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The starting systems for propulsion engines and for prime movers of ships' service generators required to start automatically must meet sections 4-6-5/9.5 and 4-8-2/11.11 of the ABS Marine Vessel Rules (incorporated by reference; see § 62.05-1).
