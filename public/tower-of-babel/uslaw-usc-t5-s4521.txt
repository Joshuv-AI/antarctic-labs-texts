5 U.S.C. § 4521 — Definition
Citation: us:usc:5:4521
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section4521
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart C (Employee Performance); Chapter 45 (Incentive Awards); Subchapter III (Award To Law Enforcement Officers For Foreign Language Capabilities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of this subchapter, the term “law enforcement officer” means—
(1)
a law enforcement officer within the meaning of section 5541(3) and to whom the provisions of chapter 51 apply;
(2)
a member of the United States Secret Service Uniformed Division;
(3)
a member of the United States Park Police;
(4)
a special agent in the Diplomatic Security Service;
(5)
a probation officer (referred to in section 3672 of title 18); and
(6)
a pretrial services officer (referred to in section 3153 of title 18).
