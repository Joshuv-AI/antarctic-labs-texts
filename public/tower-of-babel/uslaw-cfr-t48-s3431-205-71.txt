48 C.F.R. § 3431.205-71 — 3431.205-71 Noncontractor travel.
Citation: us:cfr:48:3431.205-71
Creator: Chapter 34—Department Of Education Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-3431.205-71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 3431 (Contract Cost Principles And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer may insert the clause at 3452.231-71 (Invitational travel costs) in solicitations and contracts when travel by other than Federal or contractor personnel will be required in performance of the contract.
