14 C.F.R. § 13.129 — Post-investigation action.
Citation: us:cfr:14:13.129
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-13.129
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 13 (Investigative And Enforcement Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A decision on whether to initiate subsequent action must be made on the basis of the record developed during the formal investigation and any other information in the possession of the Administrator.
