25 C.F.R. § 247.10 — What will happen if I abandon property?
Citation: us:cfr:25:247.10
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-247.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 247 (Use Of Columbia River Treaty Fishing Access Sites). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you abandon property at a site, it may be removed without your consent and disposed of at your expense, if the Area Director approves.
