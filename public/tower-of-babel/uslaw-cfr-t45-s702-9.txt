45 C.F.R. § 702.9 — Cross-examination at public session.
Citation: us:cfr:45:702.9
Creator: Commission On Civil Rights
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-702.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 702 (Rules On Hearings, Reports, And Meetings Of The Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Commission determines that oral testimony of a witness at a public session tends to defame, degrade, or incriminate any person, such person, or through counsel, shall be permitted to submit questions to the Commission in writing, which, in the discretion of the Commission, may be put to such witness by the Chairperson or by authorized Commission staff personnel.
