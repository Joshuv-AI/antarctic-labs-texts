16 U.S.C. § 7803 — Authority and responsibility of the Secretary of State
Citation: us:usc:16:7803
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section7803
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 97 (Convention On The Conservation And Management Of High Seas Fishery Resources In The South Pacific). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of State may—
(1)
receive and transmit, on behalf of the United States, reports, requests, recommendations, proposals, decisions, and other communications of and to the Commission;
(2)
in consultation with the Secretary, act upon, or refer to other appropriate authority, any communication pursuant to paragraph (1); and
(3)
with the concurrence of the Secretary, and in accordance with the South Pacific Fishery Resources Convention, object to decisions of the Commission.
