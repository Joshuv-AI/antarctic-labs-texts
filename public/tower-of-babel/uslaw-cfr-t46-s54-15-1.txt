46 C.F.R. § 54.15-1 — -1 General (modifies UG-125 through UG-137).

Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 54 (Pressure Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) All pressure vessels must be provided with protective devices as indicated in UG-125 through UG-136 Section VIII of the ASME BPVC (incorporated by reference; see § 54.01-1) except as noted otherwise in this subpart.
(b) The markings must be in accordance with this chapter for devices covered by § 54.15-10.
