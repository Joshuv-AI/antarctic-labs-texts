14 C.F.R. § 372.10 — Exemption.
Citation: us:cfr:14:372.10
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-372.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 372 (Overseas Military Personnel Charters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Charter operators are hereby relieved from the provisions of section 41102 of 49 U.S.C. Subtitle VII only if and so long as they comply with the provisions of this part and the conditions imposed herein, and to the extent necessary to permit them to organize and arrange overseas military personnel charters.
