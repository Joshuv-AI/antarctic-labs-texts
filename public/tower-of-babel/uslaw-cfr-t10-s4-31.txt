10 C.F.R. § 4.31 — Cooperation and assistance.
Citation: us:cfr:10:4.31
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-4.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 4 (Nondiscrimination In Federally Assisted Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance From The Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The responsible NRC official shall to the fullest extent practicable seek the cooperation of recipients in obtaining compliance with this subpart and shall provide assistance and guidance to recipients to help them comply voluntarily with this subpart.
