Correction: Dual-responsive nanoparticles targeting ACE-II senescence for therapeutic mitigation of acute lung injury
Authors: Linlin Gao, Fushuang Zheng, Zhiling Fu, Wei Wang
Published: 2025

Correction: Journal of Nanobiotechnology (2025) 23:339 10.1186/s12951-025-03382-2

In this article Figs 1 and 2 appeared incorrectly and have now been corrected in the original publication. For completeness and transparency, the correct and incorrect versions are displayed below.

After further internal verification and repeated experiments, the authors confirmed that the previously published TEM images were mistakenly taken from incorrect archived files due to a technical error in laboratory’s data management system.

The authors sincerely apologize for the inconvenience caused.
