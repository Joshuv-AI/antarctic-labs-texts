Test Schwartz functions at p p for theta lifts of Hida families
Zheng Liu

Abstract
. We construct Hida families of theta lifts from definite orthogonal and unitary groups. A major ingredient of the construction is the choice of test Schwartz functions at places dividing p p . We select a special type of Schwartz functions endowed with the equivariance property for the action of 𝕌 p \mathbb{U}_{p} -operators.

Introduction

Hida families are families of ordinary automorphic forms parameterized by finite covers of the spectrums of Iwasawa algebras. Since being introduced by Hida in the 1980’s
[
HidaFamily
]
, they have found broad applications in studying arithmetic properties of automorphic forms and Galois representations. When implementing Hida families in those applications, one aspect that is often essential is to construct certain explicit Hida families. For
\GL_{2}

[
WiMC
]
and CM fields
[
HiTi93
,
HiTi94
]
. They are also a crucial ingredient in the construction of various
p

-adic
L
-functions
[
Hi88
,
HiFRankin
]
and anti-cyclotomic
p
-adic
L
-functions for modular forms and their Rankin–Selberg products. Explicit Eisenstein families on groups of higher ranks are constructed and utilized in
[

i.e.
families of theta lifts, on groups of higher ranks.

It follows that $\mathcal{F}^{\prime}\equiv 0\mod\mathcal{P}_{\underline{\tau}}$ for infinitely many $\underline{\tau}$, which implies that $\mathcal{F}^{\prime}=0$. This proves \eqref{eq:incl}. To finish the proof, it remains to showing that
\begin{equation}\mathpzc{M}^{G^{\prime},n-m}_{P\text{-ord}}(K^{G^{\prime},p}_{f},\mathcal{O}_{L})=\mathpzc{M}^{G^{\prime},n-m}_{P\text{-ord}}(K^{G^{\prime},p}_{f},\mathcal{O}_{L})\otimes_{\Lambda_{P}}{\rm Frac}(\Lambda_{P})\cap\mathpzc{M}^{G^{\prime},n-m}_{P\text{-ord},q\text{-}\mathrm{exp}}(K^{G^{\prime},p}_{f},\mathcal{O}_{L}).\end{equation}
Let $\mathpzc{C}\subset\mathpzc{V}^{G^{\prime}}_{P\text{-ord}}\big(K^{G^{\prime},p}_{f},\mathcal{O}_{L}\big)^{*}$ be the $\Lambda_{P}$-submodule generated by elements obtained as taking coefficients of $q$-expansions at cusps inside the ordinary locus. Denoting by $\mathfrak{m}$ the maximal ideal of $\Lambda_{P}$ and $\overline{\mathpzc{C}}$ the image of $\mathpzc{C}$ inside $\mathpzc{V}^{G^{\prime}}_{P\text{-ord}}\big(K^{G^{\prime},p}_{f},\mathcal{O}_{L}\big)^{*}\otimes(\Lambda_{P}/\mathfrak{m})=\Hom_{\mathbb{Z}_{p}}(\mathpzc{V}^{G^{\prime},n-m}_{P}(K^{G^{\prime},p}_{f},\mathcal{O}_{L})[\mathfrak{m}],\mathbb{Q}_{p}/\mathbb{Z}_{p})$. It follows from the $q$-expansion principle that
$$\ker(\overline{\mathpzc{C}})=\{v\in\mathpzc{V}^{G^{\prime},n-m}_{P}(K^{G^{\prime},p}_{f},\mathcal{O}_{L})[\mathfrak{m}]:\eta(v)=0\text{ for all }\eta\in\mathpzc{C}\}=0.$$
Since $\mathpzc{V}^{G^{\prime}}_{P\text{-ord}}\big(K^{G^{\prime},p}_{f},\mathcal{O}_{L}\big)^{*}$ is a finite $\Lambda_{P}$-module, $\mathpzc{V}^{G^{\prime},n-m}_{P}(K^{G^{\prime},p}_{f},\mathcal{O}_{L})[\mathfrak{m}]$ is finite dimensional over $\Lambda_{P}/\mathfrak{m}$, and $\ker(\overline{\mathpzc{C}})=0$ implies that $\overline{\mathpzc{C}}=\mathpzc{V}^{G^{\prime}}_{P\text{-ord}}\big(K^{G^{\prime},p}_{f},\mathcal{O}_{L}\big)^{*}\otimes(\Lambda_{P}/\mathfrak{m})$. By Nakayama's Lemma, we know $\mathpzc{C}=\mathpzc{V}^{G^{\prime}}_{P\text{-ord}}\big(K^{G^{\prime},p}_{f},\mathcal{O}_{L}\big)^{*}$, which implies \eqref{eq:Mqexp}.
\par\par\par\qed\end@proof
\par\par\par\par\begin{rmk}One main feature of the Hida family ${\bm{\theta}}(\bm{\varphi})$ constructed in Theorem~\ref{thm:family} lies in its explicit specialization formula \eqref{eq:theta-dagger}. Such a formula is expected to be useful for applying it to study the family of Galois representations associated to $\bm{\varphi}$. When $\bm{\varphi}$ is a Hecke eigen-family with associated Hecke eigensystem $\lambda_{\bm{\varphi}}$, based on results on theta correspondence for automorphic representations, it is easy to directly write down a Hecke eigensystem $\theta(\lambda_{\bm{\varphi}})$ (in a manner like in \cite[cite]{[\@@bibref{}{pLF-F}{}{}, Section~4.1.3]}), and deduce that $\left(\mathpzc{M}^{G^{\prime},n-m}_{P\text{-ord}}(K^{G^{\prime},p}_{f},\mathcal{O}_{L})\otimes_{\Lambda_{P}}\mathbb{I}_{\bm{\varphi}}\right)[\theta(\lambda_{\bm{\varphi}})]$ has rank $\geq 1$ over $\mathbb{I}_{\bm{\varphi}}$. Any element in this family can be viewed as a theta lift of the family $\bm{\varphi}$, but it might be hard to pin down its specializations without a scalar ambiguity.
\par\par\end{rmk}
