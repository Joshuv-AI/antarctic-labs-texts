34 C.F.R. § 371.3 — What types of projects are authorized under this program?
Citation: us:cfr:34:371.3
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-371.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 371 (American Indian Vocational Rehabilitation Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The American Indian Vocational Rehabilitation Services program provides financial assistance for the establishment and operation of tribal vocational rehabilitation services programs for American Indians with disabilities who reside on or near Federal or State reservations.
