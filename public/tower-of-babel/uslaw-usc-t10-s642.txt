10 U.S.C. § 642 — Entitlement of officers discharged or retired under this chapter to separation pay or retired pay
Citation: us:usc:10:642
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section642
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 36 (Promotion, Separation, And Involuntary Retirement Of Officers On The Active-Duty List); Subchapter V (Additional Provisions Relating To Promotion, Separation, And Retirement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
An officer who is discharged under this chapter is entitled, if eligible therefor, to separation pay under section 1174 of this title.
(b)
An officer who is retired under this chapter is entitled to retired pay computed under chapter 71 of this title.
