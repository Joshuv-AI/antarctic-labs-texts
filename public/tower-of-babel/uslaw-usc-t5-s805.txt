5 U.S.C. § 805 — Judicial review
Citation: us:usc:5:805
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section805
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part I (The Agencies Generally); Chapter 8 (Congressional Review Of Agency Rulemaking). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No determination, finding, action, or omission under this chapter shall be subject to judicial review.
