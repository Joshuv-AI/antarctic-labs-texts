8 C.F.R. § 1245.4 — Documentary requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:8:1245.4
Creator: Executive Office For Immigration Review, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-1245.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 1245 (Adjustment Of Status To That Of Person Admitted For Permanent Residence). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of part 1211 of this chapter relating to the documentary requirements for immigrants shall not apply to an applicant under this part.
