46 C.F.R. § 168.15-10 — -10 Construction.
Citation: us:cfr:46:168.15-10
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-168.15-10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 168 (Civilian Nautical School Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The accommodations provided must be securely constructed, properly lighted, heated, drained, ventilated, equipped, located, arranged, and insulated from undue noise, heat, and odors.
(b) All accommodations must be constructed and arranged so that they can be kept in a clean, workable, and sanitary condition.
