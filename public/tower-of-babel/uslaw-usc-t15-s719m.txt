15 U.S.C. § 719m — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:15:719m
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section719m
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 15C (Alaska Natural Gas Transportation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is hereby authorized to be appropriated beginning in fiscal year 1978 and each fiscal year thereafter, such sums as may be necessary to carry out the functions of the Federal inspector appointed by the President with the advice and consent of the Senate under section 719e of this title.
