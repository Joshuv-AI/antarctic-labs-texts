26 U.S.C. § 5847 — Effect on other laws
Citation: us:usc:26:5847
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section5847
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 53 (Machine Guns, Destructive Devices, And Certain Other Firearms); Subchapter B (General Provisions And Exemptions); Part I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed as modifying or affecting the requirements of section 38 of the Arms Export Control Act (22 U.S.C. 2778), as amended, with respect to the manufacture, exportation, and importation of arms, ammunition, and implements of war.
