25 C.F.R. § 1200.42 — Who can provide technical assistance?
Citation: us:cfr:25:1200.42
Creator: Office Of The Special Trustee For American Indians, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-1200.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 1200 (American Indian Trust Fund Management Reform Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A sample of competent providers includes any of the following entities with the appropriate skills and capabilities: available DOI or OST staff; intertribal organizations; public agencies; and contracted private investment firms.
