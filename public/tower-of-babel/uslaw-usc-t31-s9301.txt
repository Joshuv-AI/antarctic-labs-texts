31 U.S.C. § 9301 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:31:9301
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section9301
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 93 (Sureties And Surety Bonds). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter—
(1)
“person” means an individual, a trust, an estate, a partnership, and a corporation.
(2)
“eligible obligation” means any security designated as acceptable in lieu of a surety bond by the Secretary of the Treasury.
