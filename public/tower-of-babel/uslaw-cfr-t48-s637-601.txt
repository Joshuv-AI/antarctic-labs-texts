48 C.F.R. § 637.601 — 637.601 General.
Citation: us:cfr:48:637.601
Creator: Chapter 6—Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-637.601
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 637 (Service Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is the Department's policy that all new service contracts be performance-based, with clearly defined deliverables and performance standards. Any deviations from this policy shall be fully justified in writing and approved by the Departmental Advocate for Competition.
