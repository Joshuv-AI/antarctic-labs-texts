14 C.F.R. § 305.9 — Rights of witnesses.
Citation: us:cfr:14:305.9
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-305.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 305 (Rules Of Practice In Informal Nonpublic Investigations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person required to testify or to submit documentary evidence shall be entitled to procure, on payment of lawfully prescribed costs, a copy of any document produced by such person and of his or her own testimony as stenographically reported. Any person compelled to testify or to produce documentary evidence may be accompanied, represented, and advised by counsel.
