16 U.S.C. § 460hhh–7 — Cooperative agreements
Citation: us:usc:16:460hhh–7
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section460hhh–7
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter CXIX (Spring Mountains National Recreation Area). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In order to encourage unified and cost-effective management and interpretation of natural and cultural resources in southern Nevada, the Secretary may enter into cooperative agreements with other Federal, State, and local agencies, and with nonprofit entities, that provide for the management and interpretation of natural and cultural resources.
