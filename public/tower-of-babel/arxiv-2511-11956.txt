Introduction

Let
(X_{t})_{t}
be a Langevin diffusion process in
\mathbb{R}^{d}

\mathrm{d}X_{t}=\nabla\log p_{t}(X_{t})\mathrm{d}t+\mathrm{d}W_{t}

(1)

where

denotes standard Brownian motion and
(p_{t})_{t}
is the Lebesgue density of a time-dependent probability distribution
\pi_{t}

1

[
3
]
. We assume that the family

satisfies the following properties.

Assumption 1
.

•

Differentiability:
p_{t}(x)\in C^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{d}\times(0,\infty))
.

•

Uniform Lipschitz continuity:
\nabla p_{t}(x)
is Lipschitz continuous with respect to
x
uniformly in
t
, that is, there exists
L>0
such that

|\nabla p_{t}(x)-\nabla p_{t}(y)|\leq L|x-y|,\quad t>0,\;x,y\in\mathbb{R}^{d}.

•

Growth of the time-derivative: There exists
c>0
such that

|\partial_{t}p_{t}(x)|\leq c|x|^{2}.

•

Uniform dissipativity: There exist
a,b>0
such that

-x\cdot\nabla\log p_{t}(x)\geq a|x|^{2}-b,\quad t>0,\;x\in\mathbb{R}^{d}.

By standard theory under these assumptions (
1

(\mu_{t})_{t}
denotes the distribution of
(X_{t})_{t}

\mu_{t}
satisfies in the weak sense the partial differential equation (PDE)

\partial_{t}\mu_{t}=-\nabla\cdot(\mu_{t}\nabla\log p_{t})+\Delta\mu_{t}.

(2)

By
[
2
, 6.3.2 Corollary]
it follows that
\mu_{t}
admits a density with respect to the Lebesgue measure, that is,
\mu_{t}=q_{t}\mathrm{d}x
for some
q\in L^{1}_{loc}(\mathbb{R}^{d}\times(0,\infty))
. Moreover,
q
is continuous and strictly positive on
\mathbb{R}^{d}\times(0,\infty)
and
q_{t}\in W^{1,p}_{loc}(\mathbb{R}^{d})
for all
p>d+2
(see in particular the intro to
[
1
, Section 9.4]
). In addition, the (weak) solution to (
2
) is unique (
cf.

[
2
, Theorem 9.4.8]
with the trivial choice
V(x)=|x|^{2})
leading to a one-to-one correspondence between the Fokker-Planck equation and the SDE.

Main result

Recently, in

4

3

Indeed, invoking (

Under
