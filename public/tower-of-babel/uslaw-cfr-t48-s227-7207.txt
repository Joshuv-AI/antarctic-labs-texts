48 C.F.R. § 227.7207 — 227.7207 Contractor data repositories.
Citation: us:cfr:48:227.7207
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-227.7207
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 227 (Patents, Data, And Copyrights). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Follow 227.7108 when it is in the Government's interests to have a data repository include computer software or to have a separate computer software repository. Contractual instruments establishing the repository requirements must appropriately reflect the repository manager's software responsibilities.
