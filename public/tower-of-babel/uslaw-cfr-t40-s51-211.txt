40 C.F.R. § 51.211 — Emission reports and recordkeeping.
Citation: us:cfr:40:51.211
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-51.211
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 51 (Requirements For Preparation, Adoption, And Submittal Of Implementation Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The plan must provide for legally enforceable procedures for requiring owners or operators of stationary sources to maintain records of and periodically report to the State—
(a) Information on the nature and amount of emissions from the stationary sources; and
(b) Other information as may be necessary to enable the State to determine whether the sources are in compliance with applicable portions of the control strategy.
