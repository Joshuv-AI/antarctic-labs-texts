10 C.F.R. § 202.24 — Final action by the appropriate DOE official.
Citation: us:cfr:10:202.24
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-202.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 202 (Production Or Disclosure Of Material Or Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the General Counsel approves a demand for the production of material or disclosure of information, he shall so notify the Regional Counsel and such other persons as circumstances may warrant.
