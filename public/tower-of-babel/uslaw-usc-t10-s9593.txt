10 U.S.C. § 9593 — Quarters: heat and light
Citation: us:usc:10:9593
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9593
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Procurement); Chapter 967 (Utilities And Services). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The heat and light necessary for the authorized quarters of members of the Air Force and members of the Space Force shall be furnished at the expense of the United States.
