40 C.F.R. § 205.171-3 — -3 Test motorcycle sample selection.
Citation: us:cfr:40:205.171-3
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-205.171-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 205 (Transportation Equipment Noise Emission Controls). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A test motorcycle to be used for selective enforcement audit testing of exhaust systems must be a motorcycle of the subject class which has been assembled using the manufacturer's normal production process, in stock configuration including exhaust system, and sold or offered for sale in commerce.
