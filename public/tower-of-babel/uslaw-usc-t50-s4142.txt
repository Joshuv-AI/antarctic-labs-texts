50 U.S.C. § 4142 — Notification to claimants
Citation: us:usc:50:4142
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section4142
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 51 (War Claims); Subchapter II (Title Ii Of War Claims Act Of 1948). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each award or denial of a claim by the Commission, whether rendered before or after a hearing, shall include a specific statement of the facts and of the reasoning of the Commission in support of its conclusion.
