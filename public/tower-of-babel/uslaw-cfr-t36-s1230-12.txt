36 C.F.R. § 1230.12 — What are the penalties for unlawful or accidental removal, defacing, alteration, or destruction of records?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1230.12
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1230.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1230 (Unlawful Or Accidental Removal, Defacing, Alteration, Or Destruction Of Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The penalties for the unlawful or accidental removal, defacing, alteration, or destruction of Federal records or the attempt to do so, include a fine, imprisonment, or both (18 U.S.C. 641 and 2071).
