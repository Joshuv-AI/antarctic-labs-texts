18 U.S.C. § 1261 — Enforcement, regulations, and scope
Citation: us:usc:18:1261
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1261
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 59 (Liquor Traffic). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
1 The Attorney General—
(1)
shall enforce the provisions of this chapter; and
(2)
has the authority to issue regulations to carry out the provisions of this chapter.
