7 C.F.R. § 54.8 — When request for service deemed made.
Citation: us:cfr:7:54.8
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-54.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 54 (Meats, Prepared Meats, And Meat Products (Grading, Certification, And Standards)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A request for service is considered made when received by the designated office as identified on the Application for Service form. Records showing the date and time of the request shall be made and maintained in the designated office.
