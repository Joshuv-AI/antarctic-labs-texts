42 C.F.R. § 57.1518 — Modification of loans.
Citation: us:cfr:42:57.1518
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-57.1518
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 57 (Grants For Construction Of Teaching Facilities, Educational Improvements, Scholarships And Student Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No official of the Department of Health and Human Services will approve any proposal to modify the terms of a loan guaranteed under title VII of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 293 et seq.) and this subpart which would permit the use of the guaranteed loan (or the guarantee) as collateral for an issue of tax-exempt securities.
