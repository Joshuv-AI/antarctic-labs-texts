31 C.F.R. § 592.310 — Rough diamond.
Citation: us:cfr:31:592.310
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-592.310
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 592 (Rough Diamonds Control Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term rough diamond means any diamond that is unworked or simply sawn, cleaved, or bruted and classifiable under subheading 7102.10, 7102.21, or 7102.31 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States.
