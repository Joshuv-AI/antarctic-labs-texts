44 C.F.R. § 206.9 — Nonliability.
Citation: us:cfr:44:206.9
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-206.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 206 (Federal Disaster Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Federal Government shall not be liable for any claim based upon the exercise or performance of, or the failure to exercise or perform a discretionary function or duty on the part of a Federal agency or an employee of the Federal Government in carrying out the provisions of the Stafford Act.
