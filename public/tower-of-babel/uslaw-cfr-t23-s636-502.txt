23 C.F.R. § 636.502 — Why should I use discussions?
Citation: us:cfr:23:636.502
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-636.502
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 636 (Design-Build Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You should use discussions to maximize your ability to obtain the best value, based on the requirements and the evaluation factors set forth in the solicitation.
