6 C.F.R. § 158.602 — Compensation system.
Citation: us:cfr:6:158.602
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security, Office Of The Secretary
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-6/section-158.602
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 6 (Domestic Security), Part 158 (Cybersecurity Talent Management System (Ctms)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Secretary or designee establishes and administers a compensation system based on:
(1) The compensation strategy in § 158.601;
(2) Information from strategic talent planning described in § 158.401(c);
(3) Generally recognized compensation principles and practices; and
(4) Strategic talent priorities.
(b) The compensation system comprises:
(1) The salary system described in § 158.610; and
(2) Additional compensation described in § 158.603.
