31 C.F.R. § 601.5 — Penalty for Unauthorized Control or Possession.
Citation: us:cfr:31:601.5
Creator: Bureau Of Engraving And Printing, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-601.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 601 (Distinctive Paper And Distinctive Counterfeit Deterrents For United States Federal Reserve Notes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Control or possession of distinctive paper and/or distinctive counterfeit deterrents adopted in §§ 601.3 and 601.4 require authorization by the Secretary of the Treasury.
(b) The penalty for unauthorized control and/or possession of distinctive paper and/or distinctive counterfeit deterrents adopted in §§ 601.3 and 601.4 is found at 18 U.S.C. 474A.
