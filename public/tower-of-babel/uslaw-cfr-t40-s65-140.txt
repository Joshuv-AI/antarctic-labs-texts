40 C.F.R. § 65.140 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:40:65.140
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-65.140
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 65 (Consolidated Federal Air Rule). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart and of subpart A of this part (including the startup, shutdown, and malfunction provisions in § 65.6) apply to routing emissions to processes, fuel gas systems, closed vent systems, control devices, and recovery devices where another subpart expressly references the use of this subpart.
