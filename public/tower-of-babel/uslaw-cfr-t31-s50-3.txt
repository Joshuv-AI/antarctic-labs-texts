31 C.F.R. § 50.3 — Mandatory participation in program.
Citation: us:cfr:31:50.3
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-50.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 50 (Terrorism Risk Insurance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any entity that meets the definition of an insurer under the Act is required to participate in the Program.
