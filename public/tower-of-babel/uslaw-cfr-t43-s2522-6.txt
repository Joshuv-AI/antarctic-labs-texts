43 C.F.R. § 2522.6 — Service fees.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2522.6
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2522.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2520 (Desert-Land Entries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All applications for extension of time made under the Acts of March 28, 1908, April 30, 1912, or February 25, 1925, must be accompanied by an application service fee of $10 which will not be returnable.
