30 C.F.R. § 810.1 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:30:810.1
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-810.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 810 (Permanent Program Performance Standards—General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subchapter sets forth the minimum performance standards and design requirements to be adopted and implemented under a regulatory program for coal exploration and surface coal mining and reclamation operations.
