25 C.F.R. § 39.203 — When does OIEP calculate a school's allotment?
Citation: us:cfr:25:39.203
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-39.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 39 (The Indian School Equalization Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

OIEP calculates a school's allotment no later than July 1. Schools must submit final ADM enrollment figures no later than June 15.
