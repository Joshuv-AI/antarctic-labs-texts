48 C.F.R. § 1413.306 — 1413.306 Standard Form 44, Purchase order-invoice-voucher.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1413.306
Creator: Chapter 14—Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1413.306
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1413 (Simplified Acquisition Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

HCAs are responsible for establishing bureau procedures to control the use of the SF 44 and accounting for all purchases made using the form. Bureau procedures shall include instructions covering:
(a) Maintenance of a list of designated individuals authorized to make purchases using the form;
(b) Controls for issuing the form to authorized individuals; and
(c) Review of purchase transactions using the form to assure compliance with authorized procedures.
