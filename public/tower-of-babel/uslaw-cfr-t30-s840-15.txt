30 C.F.R. § 840.15 — Public participation.
Citation: us:cfr:30:840.15
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-840.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 840 (State Regulatory Authority: Inspection And Enforcement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each State program shall provide for public participation in enforcement of the State program consistent with that provided by 30 CFR parts 842, 843 and 845 and 43 CFR part 4.
