34 C.F.R. § 648.33 — What priorities and absolute preferences does the Secretary establish?
Citation: us:cfr:34:648.33
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-648.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 648 (Graduate Assistance In Areas Of National Need). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) For each application period, the Secretary establishes as an area of national need and gives absolute preference to one or more of the general disciplines and sub-disciplines listed as priorities in the appendix to this part or the resulting interdisciplines.
(b) The Secretary announces the absolute preferences in a notice published in the Federal Register.
