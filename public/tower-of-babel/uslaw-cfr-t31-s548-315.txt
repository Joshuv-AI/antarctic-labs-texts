31 C.F.R. § 548.315 — Potassium chloride (potash) sector of the Belarus economy.
Citation: us:cfr:31:548.315
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-548.315
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 548 (Belarus Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term potassium chloride (potash) sector of the Belarus economy includes the extraction, mining, harvesting, production, procurement, refinement, manufacturing, testing, financing, distribution, or transport to, from, or involving Belarus, of potassium chloride (potash) or potash products; and any related activities, including the provision or receipt of goods, services, or technology to, from, or involving the potash sector of the Belarus economy.
