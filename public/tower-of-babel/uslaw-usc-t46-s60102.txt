46 U.S.C. § 60102 — Production of certificate on entry
Citation: us:usc:46:60102
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section60102
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 601 (Arrival And Departure Requirements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On entry of a vessel documented under chapter 121 of this title, the master or other individual in charge of the vessel shall produce the certificate of documentation to the customs officer at the place where the vessel is entered. If the certificate is not produced, the vessel is not entitled to the privileges of a documented vessel.
