27 C.F.R. § 20.143 — Receipt.
Citation: us:cfr:27:20.143
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-20.143
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 20 (Distribution And Use Of Denatured Alcohol And Rum). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless completely denatured alcohol received in bulk conveyances or by pipeline is to be used immediately, it shall be deposited in storage tanks, stored in the tank cars or tank trucks in which received, or drawn into packages which shall be marked or labeled as required by this subpart.
