49 C.F.R. § 553.3 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:49:553.3
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-553.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 553 (Rulemaking Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Administrator means the Administrator of the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration or a person to whom he has delegated final authority in the matter concerned.
Rule includes any order, regulation, or Federal motor vehicle safety standard issued under Title 49.
Title 49 means 49 U.S.C. 30101, et seq.
