36 C.F.R. § 4.1 — Applicability and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:36:4.1
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-4.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 4 (Vehicles And Traffic Safety). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The applicability of the regulations in this part is described in § 1.2 of this chapter. The regulations in this part also apply, regardless of land ownership, on all roadways and parking areas within a park area that are open to public traffic and that are under the legislative jurisdiction of the United States.
