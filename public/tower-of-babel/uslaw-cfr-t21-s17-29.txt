21 C.F.R. § 17.29 — Fees.
Citation: us:cfr:21:17.29
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-17.29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 17 (Civil Money Penalties Hearings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The party requesting a subpoena shall pay the cost of the fees and mileage of any witness subpoenaed in the amounts that would be payable to a witness in a proceeding in a United States District Court. A check for witness fees and mileage shall accompany the subpoena when served.
