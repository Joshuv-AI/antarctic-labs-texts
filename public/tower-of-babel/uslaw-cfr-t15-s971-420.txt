15 C.F.R. § 971.420 — Resource conservation requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:15:971.420
Creator: National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-971.420
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 971 (Deep Seabed Mining Regulations For Commercial Recovery Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the purpose of conservation of natural resources, each permit issued under this part will contain, as needed, TCRs which have due regard for the prevention of waste and the future opportunity for the commercial recovery of the unrecovered balance of the hard mineral resources in the recovery area. The Administrator will establish these requirements pursuant to § 971.502.
