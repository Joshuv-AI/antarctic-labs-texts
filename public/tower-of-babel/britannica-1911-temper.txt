TEMPER (from Lat. temperare, to mingle or compound in due proportion, to qualify, rule, regulate, to be moderate,
formed from tempus, time, fit or due season), to blend, modify, or qualify by mixing, to combine in due proportions, hence to
restrain, calm. A specific application of the word is to the
bringing of steel or other metal to a proper hardness and
elasticity (see Metal and Iron and Steel). The word is also
used as a substantive, especially in the transferred sense of
disposition or frame of mind, generally with some qualifying
epithet, but when used absolutely signifying a hasty, passionate
temper, or display of such.
