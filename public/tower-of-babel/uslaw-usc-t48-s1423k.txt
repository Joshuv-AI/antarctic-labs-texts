48 U.S.C. § 1423k — Right of petition
Citation: us:usc:48:1423k
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1423k
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 8A (Guam); Subchapter III (The Legislature). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The legislature or any person or group of persons in Guam shall have the unrestricted right of petition. It shall be the duty of all officers of the government to receive and without delay to act upon or forward, as the case may require, any such petition.
