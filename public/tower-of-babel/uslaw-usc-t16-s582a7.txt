16 U.S.C. § 582a–7 — “State” defined
Citation: us:usc:16:582a–7
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section582a–7
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 3 (Forests; Forest Service; Reforestation; Management); Subchapter III (Research Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The term “State” as used in this subchapter shall include Puerto Rico, the Virgin Islands, and Guam.
