Geochemical dataset of laterites soils in Koubou gold district (Zone A) East Cameroon: Implication for identification of gold pathfinder elements
Authors: Amaya Adama, Bokanda Ekoko Eric, Bisse Salomon Bertrant, Boroh Andre William, Emmanuel Eseya Mengu, Ngwang Nfor Bertrand
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
AbstractThe study zone (A) located in the Koubou Gold district is situated between latitudes 4° 36’ to 4° 42’ N and longitudes 14° 08’ to 14° 11’ E, composing of mostly Gneiss and Granodiorite bed rocks. To determine the relationship between gold and selected pathfinder elements (As, Mo, Ag, Sn, Sb, Te, W, Bi, Ni, Cu, Sb, Nb, Ta, Fe, Mn, Cu, Zn, Ni, and Pb) in the laterite soils of the Koubou gold district, East region of Cameroon, a lithogeochemical dataset from the laterites were utilized. The lithogeochemical dataset were realized through the inductively coupled plasma mass spectrometry (ICP-MS) method, while multi-statistical methods of factor analyses, principal component analyses and hierarchical clustering employed to show the relationship between the gold mineral and selected pathfinder minerals. The gold (Au) has a close relation with Bismuth (Bi) while also been related to other elements such as Mn, Ni, Nb, Sn and TiKeywords: Multivariate analyses, Koubou, Lithogeochemistry, Pathfinder elements, Gold, East Cameroon

Background

The Koubou gold district is composed of the Pan Africano-Braziliano mobile belt, which enclosed the Central African mobile belt, a combination of Precambrian terrains with a long complex crustal history. The Central African Republic Yade Massif, the Lom schist belt, remnants of metasedimentary rocks, orthogneis, and felsic plutons make up the Adamawa-Yade domain composing of mineral lineation and a foliation of first event deformation [1]. Underlying the Koubou gold district are granitoids formed from tonalitic affinity I-type parental magmas.

The article used multivariate statistical approach methodology used in the research article [2]. The choice of the selected pathfinder elements to be used in this work were gotten from the literature of Ref [3].This dataset is compose of the chemistry of laterite soil obtained from 55 pits in Koubou gold district, East Cameroon. The data set aims to reveal the chemical content of the soil to unveil their relationship with gold using multi-statistical methods. The selected metals are consider pathfinder minerals for gold and are widely used for gold exploration. The usage of this specific pathfinder elements of gold include As, Mo, Ag, Sn, Sb, Te, W, Bi, Ni, Cu, Nb, Ta, Sn Fe, Mn, Cu, Zn, Ni, and Pb is significant innovation in mineral exploration and the usage of multivariate statistical methods such as Principal component analysis(PCA), parsons correction, Factor analysis, and cluster diagrams. Additionally, the presence of these Pathfinder elements in the soil provides a lead way gold mineral exploration. The multi-statistical method will provide clear evidence of the relationship that exist between gold and the other pathfinder elements.

Data Description

Fifty five (55) soil samples were collected from fifty five (55) different pits in the Koubou gold district, East Cameroon (Fig. 1). The laterites were carefully collected and placed in a sealed sampling bag to avoid contamination. All the samples collected after field observations were pre-treated. The criteria of description are color, and texture. All these features differ from one sample to another. The color variation and the texture the different laterites reflect the conditions of formation (Table 1). Most of the samples (85%) show a clayey texture while 15% of the samples show laterite duricrust and laterite gravel texture (Table 1). 85% of the samples display a dark orange and light orange brown colour with the remaining 15% showing a dark brown colour (Table 1). The concentrations of selected pathfinder elements were determined using the inductively couple plasma mass spectrometry method (ICP-MS) technique (Table i). in all the analysed laterites Fe show the highest proportion followed by Cu, Ni Pb and Zn while the other elements show ppm values <5 on average (Table i).

Experimental Design, Materials and Methods

Fifty five (55) unique samples were collected from the study area (Koubou Gold district) located between latitudes 4° 36’ to 4° 42’ N and longitudes 14° 08’ to 14° 11’ E. All the 55 analyse samples were collected from 55 different pits since in each pit only the laterite layer was sampled for this work. The collected samples weighing 2kg were sealed in an airtight plastic bag before being transported to the MIPROMALO laboratory in Cameroon for pulverisation. At MIPROMALO, the samples were air dried and powdered using the DMP-50 grinding machine. For each powdered sample, 30g were measured using an electronic balance. The weight samples were place in a smaller size plastic bag and shipped to Acme Analytical Laboratories Ltd., Vancouver, Canada, for lithogeochemical analyses using the inductively couple plasma mass spectrometry (ICP-MS) method. This method reveal the presence of selected gold pathfinder elements used in this work. The analysed samples from the laboratories were reported under certified codes BA1-BA55. All raw and treated and raw data (ICP- MS) are at https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/9nx6ypbr5n/1, (Table i).
