31 C.F.R. § 525.307 — Government of Burma.
Citation: us:cfr:31:525.307
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-525.307
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 525 (Burma Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term Government of Burma means the Government of Burma, any political subdivision, agency, or instrumentality thereof, including the Central Bank of Myanmar, and any person owned or controlled by, or acting for or on behalf of, the Government of Burma.
