47 U.S.C. § 763a — Specific criteria for INTELSAT

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 47 (Telecommunications); Chapter 6 (Communications Satellite System); Subchapter VI (Communications Competition And Privatization); Part B (Federal Communications Commission Licensing Criteria: Privatization Criteria). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In securing the privatizations required by section 763 of this title, the following additional criteria with respect to INTELSAT privatization shall be applied as licensing criteria for purposes of part A:
(1) 1 Technical coordination under intelsat agreements.—
Technical coordination shall not be used to impair competition or competitors, and shall be conducted under International Telecommunication Union procedures and not under Article XIV(d) of the INTELSAT Agreement.
