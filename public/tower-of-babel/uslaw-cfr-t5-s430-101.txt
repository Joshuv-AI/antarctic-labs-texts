5 C.F.R. § 430.101 — Authority.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 430 (Performance Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Chapter 43 of title 5, United States Code, provides for the performance appraisal of Federal employees. This subpart supplements and implements this portion of the law.
