19 C.F.R. § 159.43 — Duties contingent upon foreign export duties, charges, or restrictions.
Citation: us:cfr:19:159.43
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-159.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 159 (Liquidation Of Duties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

U.S. Note 1 to Section X, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (19 U.S.C. 1202), provides for the imposition under certain conditions of additional duties on merchandise covered thereby. The assessment of these additional duties is dependent upon action by the President, and notice of such action, if taken, will be published in the Customs Bulletin.
