37 C.F.R. § 353.4 — Filing deadline.
Citation: us:cfr:37:353.4
Creator: Copyright Royalty Board, Library Of Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-353.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 353 (Rehearing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A motion for rehearing must be filed within 15 days after the date on which the Copyright Royalty Judges issue an initial determination.
