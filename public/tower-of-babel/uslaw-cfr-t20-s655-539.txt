20 C.F.R. § 655.539 — Effective date and validity of filed attestations for locations in Alaska.
Citation: us:cfr:20:655.539
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-655.539
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 655 (Temporary Employment Of Foreign Workers In The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An attestation is filed and effective as of the date it is accepted and signed by the Certifying Officer. Such attestation is valid for the 12-month period beginning on the date of acceptance for filing, unless suspended or invalidated pursuant to § 655.540 of this part. The filed attestation expires at the end of the 12-month period of validity.
