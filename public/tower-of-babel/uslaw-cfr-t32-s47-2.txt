32 C.F.R. § 47.2 — Applicability and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:32:47.2
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-47.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 47 (Active Duty Service For Civilian Or Contractual Groups). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part:
(a) Applies to the Office of the Secretary of Defense (OSD), the Military Departments, and by agreement with the Department of Transportation (DoT), the U.S. Coast Guard.
(b) Applies to any group application considered under Public Law 95-202 after September 11, 1989 and to any individual who applies for discharge documents as a member of a group recognized by the Secretary of the Air Force.
