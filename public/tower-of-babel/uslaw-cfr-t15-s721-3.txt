15 C.F.R. § 721.3 — Destruction or disposal of records.
Citation: us:cfr:15:721.3
Creator: Bureau Of Industry And Security, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-721.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 721 (Inspection Of Records And Recordkeeping). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If BIS or other authorized U.S. government agency makes a formal or informal request for a certain record or records, such record or records may not be destroyed or disposed of without the written authorization of the requesting entity.
