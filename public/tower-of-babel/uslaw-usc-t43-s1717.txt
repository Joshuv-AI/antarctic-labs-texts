43 U.S.C. § 1717 — Qualifications of conveyees
Citation: us:usc:43:1717
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section1717
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 35 (Federal Land Policy And Management); Subchapter II (Land Use Planning And Land Acquisition And Disposition). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No tract of land may be disposed of under this Act, whether by sale, exchange, or donation, to any person who is not a citizen of the United States, or in the case of a corporation, is not subject to the laws of any State or of the United States.
