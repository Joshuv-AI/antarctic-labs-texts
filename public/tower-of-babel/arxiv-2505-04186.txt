Cutoff Sobolev inequalities on the Vicsek and Sierpiński fractals
Meng Yang

Abstract
We present a direct proof of the cutoff Sobolev inequalities on the Vicsek set, the Sierpiński gasket and the Sierpiński carpet, which has long been regarded as highly non-trivial in the context of heat kernel estimates.

Introduction

Let us recall the following classical result. On a complete non-compact Riemannian manifold, it was independently discovered by Grigor’yan
[
20
]
and Saloff-Coste
[
29
,
30

that the following two-sided Gaussian estimate of the heat kernel

\frac{C_{1}}{V(x,\sqrt{t})}\exp\left(-C_{2}\frac{d(x,y)^{2}}{t}\right)\leq p_{t}(x,y)\leq\frac{C_{3}}{V(x,\sqrt{t})}\exp\left(-C_{4}\frac{d(x,y)^{2}}{t}\right),

HK(2)

where

is the geodesic distance,

is the Riemannian measure of the open ball

\displaystyle\frac{C_{1}}{V(x,t^{1/{d_{w}}})}\exp\left(-C_{2}\left(\frac{d(x,y)}{t^{1/d_{w}}}\right)^{\frac{d_{w}}{d_{w}-1}}\right)

\displaystyle\leq p_{t}(x,y)\leq\frac{C_{3}}{V(x,t^{1/d_{w}})}\exp\left(-C_{4}\left(\frac{d(x,y)}{t^{1/d_{w}}}\right)^{\frac{d_{w}}{d_{w}-1}}\right),

HK(
d_{w}
)

where
d_{w}
is a new parameter called the walk dimension, which is always strictly greater than 2 on fractals. For
d_{w}=2
,
HK(

d
w

)
is exactly
HK(2)
.

For general

, Barlow and Bass
[
6
]
introduced the well-known cutoff Sobolev inequality and proved that
HK(

d
w

)
is equivalent to the conjunction of the volume doubling condition, the Poincaré inequality and the cutoff Sobolev inequality. The cutoff Sobolev inequality was later extended to more general settings, see
[
7
,
1
,
22
,
14
,
15
,
21
]
, and the references therein.

Roughly speaking, the cutoff Sobolev inequality provides a family of cutoff functions with controlled energy behavior, but it is significantly stronger than the capacity condition. While the capacity condition is relatively easy to verify on many fractals, it has long been believed that establishing the cutoff Sobolev inequality is complicated and difficult. This is partly due to the fact that existing proofs typically construct the desired cutoff functions using Green’s functions with certain estimates. To the best of the author’s knowledge, there has been no
direct
verification of the cutoff Sobolev inequality on any fractal.

The main motivation of this paper is to give a direct proof on three typical fractals, that is, the Vicsek set, the Sierpiński gasket and the Sierpiński carpet. Although all three fractals lie in the strongly recurrent setting and the proofs will follow the same general approach, each exhibits distinct properties, as detailed below.

A key property of the Vicsek set is that it is an
\mathbb{R}
-tree, which allows for a form of first-order differential calculus and makes the proof analogous to the case of
\mathbb{R}
. A similar argument can give a direct proof of the cutoff Sobolev inequality on the
\mathbb{R}
-trees introduced in
[
27
]
, thus the verification of other equivalent conditions can be avoided. Indeed, we used the same technique to prove a
p
-version of the cutoff Sobolev inequality in
[
32
]
.

The Sierpiński gasket is a prototypical finitely ramified fractal and a standard example of a p.c.f. self-similar set, as described in
[
24
]
, where the so-called compatible condition holds. Our proof would be transparent, and similarly transparent proofs can be carried out for a broad class of fractals satisfying the compatible condition.

The Sierpiński carpet is a typical infinitely ramified fractal, on which the aforementioned compatible condition is not available. Our proof will intrinsically rely on certain resistance estimates, which may render it less direct than in the case of the Sierpiński gasket, but it will reveal the key mechanism of our approach. Technically, the argument is based on a Morrey-Sobolev inequality and a capacity upper bound, both of which follow from the resistance estimates.
