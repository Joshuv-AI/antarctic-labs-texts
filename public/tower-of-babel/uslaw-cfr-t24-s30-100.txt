24 C.F.R. § 30.100 — Settlement of a civil money penalty action.
Citation: us:cfr:24:30.100
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-30.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 30 (Civil Money Penalties: Certain Prohibited Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The officials listed at subpart B of this part, or their designees (or the Mortgagee Review Board, or designee, for violations under § 30.35), are authorized to enter into settlement agreements resolving civil money penalty actions that may be brought under part 30.
