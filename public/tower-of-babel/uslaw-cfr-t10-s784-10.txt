10 C.F.R. § 784.10 — Record of waiver determinations.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 784 (Patent Waiver Regulation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Assistant General Counsel for Technology Transfer and Intellectual Property shall maintain and periodically update a publicly available record of waiver determinations.
