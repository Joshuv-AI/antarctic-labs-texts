12 U.S.C. § 4638 — Private rights of action
Citation: us:usc:12:4638
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section4638
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 46 (Government Sponsored Enterprises); Subchapter III (Enforcement Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter shall not create any private right of action on behalf of any person against a regulated entity, or any director or executive officer of a regulated entity, or impair any existing private right of action under other applicable law.
