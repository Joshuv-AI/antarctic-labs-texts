29 C.F.R. § 1201.6 — Representatives.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1201.6
Creator: National Mediation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1201.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1201 (Definitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term representative means any person or persons, labor union, organization, or corporation designated either by a carrier or group of carriers or by its or their employees, to act for it or them.
