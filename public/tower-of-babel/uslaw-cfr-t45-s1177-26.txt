45 C.F.R. § 1177.26 — Consideration of tax consequences to the Government.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1177.26
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1177.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1177 (Claims Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In negotiating a compromise, NEH will consider the tax consequences to the Government. In particular, NEH will consider requiring a waiver of the debtor's tax-loss-carry-forward and tax-loss-carry-back rights. For information on discharge of indebtedness reporting requirements, see § 1177.32.
