22 U.S.C. § 7846 — Sense of Congress on humanitarian coordination related to the Korean Peninsula
Citation: us:usc:22:7846
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section7846
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 85 (North Korean Human Rights); Subchapter III (Protecting North Korean Refugees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the sense of Congress that—
(1)
any instability on the Korean Peninsula could have significant humanitarian and strategic impact on the region and for United States national interests; and
(2)
as such, the United States Government should work with countries sharing a land or maritime border with North Korea to develop long-term whole-of-government plans to coordinate efforts related to humanitarian assistance and human rights promotion and to effectively assimilate North Korean defectors.
