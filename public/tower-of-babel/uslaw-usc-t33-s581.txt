33 U.S.C. § 581 — Hiring special means of transportation
Citation: us:usc:33:581
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section581
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 12 (River And Harbor Improvements Generally); Subchapter II (Pay And Allowances; Traveling Expenses And Subsistence). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In their execution and inspection of river and harbor improvement work, at points beyond easy reach of ordinary regular transportation lines, Engineer officers are authorized to hire and use such transportation as they may consider desirable and advantageous to the progress of work.
