20 C.F.R. § 355.44 — Right to administrative offset.
Citation: us:cfr:20:355.44
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-355.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 355 (Regulations Under The Administrative False Claims Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The amount of any penalty or assessment which has become final, or for which a judgment has been entered under § 355.42 or § 355.43, or any amount agreed upon in a compromise or settlement under § 355.46, may be collected by administrative offset under 31 U.S.C. 3716, except that an administrative offset may not be made under this section against a refund of an overpayment of Federal taxes, then or later owing by the United States to the respondent.
