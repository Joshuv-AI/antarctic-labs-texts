14 C.F.R. § 150.3 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:150.3
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-150.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 150 (Airport Noise Compatibility Planning). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to the airport noise compatibility planning activities of the operators of “public use airports,” including heliports, as that term is used in section 47501(2) as amended (49 U.S.C. 47501 et seq.) and as defined in section 47102(17) of 49 U.S.C.
