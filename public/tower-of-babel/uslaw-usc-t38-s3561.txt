38 U.S.C. § 3561 — Authority and duties of Secretary
Citation: us:usc:38:3561
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section3561
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part III (Readjustment And Related Benefits); Chapter 35 (Survivors’ And Dependents’ Educational Assistance); Subchapter VI (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Secretary may provide the educational and vocational counseling authorized under section 3520 of this title, and may provide additional counseling if the Secretary deems it to be necessary to accomplish the purposes of this chapter.
(b)
Where any provision of this chapter authorizes or requires any function, power, or duty to be exercised by a State, or by any officer or agency thereof, such function, power, or duty shall, with respect to the Republic of the Philippines, be exercised by the Secretary.
