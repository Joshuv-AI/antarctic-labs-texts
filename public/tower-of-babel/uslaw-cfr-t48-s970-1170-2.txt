48 C.F.R. § 970.1170-2 — 970.1170-2 Contract provision.
Citation: us:cfr:48:970.1170-2
Creator: Chapter 9—Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-970.1170-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 970 (Doe Management And Operating Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Contracting Officer shall insert the clause at 970.5211-1, Work authorization, in each solicitation and contract for the management and operation of a DOE site or facility and in other contracts designated by the DOE or NNSA Senior Procurement Executive.
