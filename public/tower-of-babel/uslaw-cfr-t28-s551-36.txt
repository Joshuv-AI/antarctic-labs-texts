28 C.F.R. § 551.36 — Funding.
Citation: us:cfr:28:551.36
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-551.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 551 (Miscellaneous). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Bureau of Prisons may fund approved activities of inmate organizations or organization requests for purchase of equipment or services for all inmates subject to the availability of designated funds.
