49 C.F.R. § 374.317 — Identification—bus and driver.
Citation: us:cfr:49:374.317
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-374.317
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 374 (Passenger Carrier Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each bus and driver providing service shall be identified in a manner visible to passengers. The driver may be identified by name or company number.
