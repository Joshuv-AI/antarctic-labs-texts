On the m m -graph of a finite Abelian Group
Ayman Badawi

Abstract
. Let H H be a finite abelian (commutative) group of order n ≥ 2 n\geq 2 , and m > 1 m>1 be an integer. We define the m m -graph of H H , denoted by m − G ⁡ ( H ) m-G(H) , as a simple undirected graph with vertex set H H , and two distinct vertices, a , b ∈ H a,b\in H , are connected by an edge if and only if a m = b a^{m}=b or b m = a b^{m}=a . Several results regarding the properties of the m m - G ⁡ ( H ) G(H) have been established.
