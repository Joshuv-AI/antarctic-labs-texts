20 U.S.C. § 5206 — Report to Congress
Citation: us:usc:20:5206
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section5206
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 63 (Eisenhower Exchange Fellowship Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For any fiscal year for which Eisenhower Exchange Fellowships, Incorporated, receive funds pursuant to section 5203(a) of this title, Eisenhower Exchange Fellowships, Incorporated, shall prepare and transmit to the President and the Congress a report of its activities for such fiscal year.
