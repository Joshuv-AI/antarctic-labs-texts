18 U.S.C. § 928 — Separability
Citation: us:usc:18:928
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section928
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 44 (Firearms). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this chapter or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of the chapter and the application of such provision to other persons not similarly situated or to other circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
