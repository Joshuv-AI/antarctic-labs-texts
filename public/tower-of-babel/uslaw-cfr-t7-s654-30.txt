7 C.F.R. § 654.30 — Responsibility for operation and maintenance.
Citation: us:cfr:7:654.30
Creator: Natural Resources Conservation Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-654.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 654 (Operation And Maintenance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The land user is responsible for O&M of soil and water conservation measures installed with NRCS assistance provided through soil, water, and other conservation districts.
