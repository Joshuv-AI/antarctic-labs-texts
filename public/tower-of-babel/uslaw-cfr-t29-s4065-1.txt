29 C.F.R. § 4065.1 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:29:4065.1
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4065.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4065 (Annual Report). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to specify the form and content of the Annual Report required by section 4065 of ERISA. This part applies to all plans covered by title IV of ERISA.
