31 U.S.C. § 7106 — Delegation of supervision of assistance
Citation: us:usc:31:7106
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section7106
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 71 (Joint Funding Simplification). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

With the approval of the President, the head of an executive agency may delegate or otherwise arrange to have another executive agency carry out or supervise a project or class of projects jointly financed under this chapter. A delegation—
(1)
shall be made under conditions ensuring that duties and powers delegated are exercised consistent with law; and
(2)
may not relieve the head of an executive agency of responsibility for the proper and efficient management of a project for which the agency provides assistance.
