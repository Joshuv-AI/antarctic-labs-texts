22 U.S.C. § 5491 — Suspension of SEED assistance
Citation: us:usc:22:5491
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section5491
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 63 (Support For East European Democracy (Seed)); Subchapter VII (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President should suspend all assistance to an East European country pursuant to this chapter if the President determines, and reports to the Congress, that—
(1)
that country is engaged in international activities directly and fundamentally contrary to United States national security interests;
(2)
the president or any other government official of that country initiates martial law or a state of emergency for reasons other than to respond to a natural disaster or a foreign invasion; or
(3)
any member who was elected to that country’s parliament has been removed from that office or arrested through extraconstitutional processes.
