10 U.S.C. § 3156 — Mission readiness exercises
Citation: us:usc:10:3156
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section3156
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part V (Acquisition); Subpart A (General); Chapter 209 (Operational Contract Support); Subchapter I (Joint Policies On Requirements Definition, Contingency Program Management, And Contingency Contracting). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The joint policy required by section 3151 of this title shall also provide for the incorporation of contractors and contract operations in mission readiness exercises for operations that will include contracting and contractor support.
