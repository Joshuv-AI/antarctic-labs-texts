48 C.F.R. § 3025.7100 — 3025.7100 Scope of subpart.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 3025 (Foreign Acquisition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart contains restrictions on the acquisition of certain personal protective equipment (PPE) imposed by the Make PPE in America Act (Pub. L. 117-58), and they apply to all types of actions, orders, option exercises, and contracts entered into on or after February 14, 2022.
