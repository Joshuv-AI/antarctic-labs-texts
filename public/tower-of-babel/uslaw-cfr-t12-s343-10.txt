12 C.F.R. § 343.10 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:12:343.10
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-343.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 343 (Consumer Protection In Sales Of Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part establishes consumer protections in connection with retail sales practices, solicitations, advertising, or offers of any insurance product or annuity to a consumer by:
(a) Any institution; or
(b) Any other person that is engaged in such activities at an office of the institution or on behalf of the institution.
