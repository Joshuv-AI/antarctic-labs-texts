10 C.F.R. § 1303.116 — Other rights and services.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1303.116
Creator: Nuclear Waste Technical Review Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1303.116
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1303 (Public Information And Requests). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this part shall be construed to entitle any person, as a right, to any service or to the disclosure of any record to which such person is not entitled under the FOIA.
