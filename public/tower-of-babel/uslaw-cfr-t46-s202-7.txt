46 C.F.R. § 202.7 — Supplemental briefs.
Citation: us:cfr:46:202.7
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-202.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 202 (Procedures Relating To Review By Secretary Of Transportation Of Actions By Maritime Subsidy Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an order taking review is entered by the Secretary, further briefs supplementing the arguments set forth in the petitions and replies may be requested in cases where the Secretary deems such to be appropriate and desirable.
