7 C.F.R. § 301.89-4 — -4 Planting.
Citation: us:cfr:7:301.89-4
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-301.89-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 301 (Domestic Quarantine Notices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any wheat, durum wheat, or triticale that originates within a regulated area must be tested and found free from bunted wheat kernels and spores before it may be used as seed within or outside a regulated area.
