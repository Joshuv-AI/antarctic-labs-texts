48 C.F.R. § 4.1902 — 4.1902 Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:48:4.1902
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-4.1902
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 4 (Administrative And Information Matters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to all acquisitions, including acquisitions of commercial products or commercial services, other than commercially available off-the-shelf items, when a contractor's information system may contain Federal contract information.
