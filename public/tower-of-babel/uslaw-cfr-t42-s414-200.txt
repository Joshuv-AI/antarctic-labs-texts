42 C.F.R. § 414.200 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:42:414.200
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-414.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 414 (Payment For Part B Medical And Other Health Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart implements sections 1834(a), (h) and (i) of the Act by specifying how payments are made for the purchase or rental of new and used durable medical equipment, prosthetic and orthotic devices, and surgical dressings for Medicare beneficiaries.
