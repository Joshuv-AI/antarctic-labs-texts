2 U.S.C. § 931 — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:2:931
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section931
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 20A (Statutory Pay-As-You-Go). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of this chapter is to reestablish a statutory procedure to enforce a rule of budget neutrality on new revenue and direct spending legislation.
