25 C.F.R. § 700.469 — Unallowable program and project costs.
Citation: us:cfr:25:700.469
Creator: The Office Of Navajo And Hopi Indian Relocation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-700.469
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 700 (Commission Operations And Relocation Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Costs for program or project operating expenses are not allowable except in the following cases—
(a) An application for an annual contract for services under § 700.457 or 700.459 may include necessary operating expenses; and
(b) An application for a demonstration effort under § 700.459 may include costs relating to the operation of the demonstration.
