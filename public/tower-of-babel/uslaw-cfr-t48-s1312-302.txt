48 C.F.R. § 1312.302 — 1312.302 Tailoring of provisions and clauses for the acquisition of commercial items.

Creator: Chapter 13—Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1312 (Acquisition Of Commercial Items). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The authority for approving a request for waiver to tailor a clause, or otherwise include any additional terms or conditions in a solicitation or contract in a manner that is inconsistent with customary commercial practice, is set forth in CAM 1301.70.
