46 C.F.R. § 201.156 — Requests for extension of time for filing briefs.
Citation: us:cfr:46:201.156
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-201.156
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 201 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Requests for extension of time within which to file briefs shall conform to the requirements of § 201.53. Except for good cause shown, such requests shall be filed and served not later than five (5) days before the expiration of the time fixed for the filing of briefs.
