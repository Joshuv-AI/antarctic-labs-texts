40 C.F.R. § 1066.915 — Enclosures; auxiliary systems and equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1066.915
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1066.915
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1066 (Vehicle-Testing Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Enclosures for evaporative and refueling emissions must be equipped with fans, blowers, and measurement and data recording equipment as described in 40 CFR 86.107-98(b) through (h) and (j).
