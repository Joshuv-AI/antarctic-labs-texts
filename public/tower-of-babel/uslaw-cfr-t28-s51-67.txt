28 C.F.R. § 51.67 — Disposition of petition.
Citation: us:cfr:28:51.67
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-51.67
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 51 (Procedures For The Administration Of Section 5 Of The Voting Rights Act Of 1965, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Attorney General shall promptly consider and dispose of a petition under this subpart and give notice of the disposition, accompanied by a simple statement of the reasons, to the petitioner.
