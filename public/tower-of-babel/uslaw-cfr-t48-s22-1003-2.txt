48 C.F.R. § 22.1003-2 — 22.1003-2 Geographical coverage of the Service Contract Labor Standards statute.
Citation: us:cfr:48:22.1003-2
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-22.1003-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 22 (Application Of Labor Laws To Government Acquisitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Service Contract Labor Standards statute applies to service contracts performed in the United States (see 22.1001). The Service Contract Labor Standards statute does not apply to contracts performed outside the United States.
