14 C.F.R. § 139.7 — Methods and procedures for compliance.
Citation: us:cfr:14:139.7
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-139.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 139 (Certification Of Airports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Certificate holders must comply with requirements prescribed by subparts C and D of this part in a manner authorized by the Administrator. FAA Advisory Circulars contain methods and procedures for compliance with this part that are acceptable to the Administrator.
