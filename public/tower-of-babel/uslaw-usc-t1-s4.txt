1 U.S.C. § 4 — “Vehicle” as including all means of land transportation
Citation: us:usc:1:4
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title1-section4
Context: United States Code, Title 1 (General Provisions); Chapter 1 (Rules Of Construction). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The word “vehicle” includes every description of carriage or other artificial contrivance used, or capable of being used, as a means of transportation on land.
