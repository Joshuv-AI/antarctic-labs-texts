15 U.S.C. § 504 — Powers of Foundation
Citation: us:usc:15:504
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section504
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 13 (Textile Foundation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation—
(a)
Shall have perpetual succession;
(b)
May sue and be sued;
(c)
May adopt a corporate seal and alter it at pleasure;
(d)
May adopt and alter bylaws;
(e)
May appoint officers and agents;
(f)
May acquire by purchase, devise, bequest, gift, or otherwise, and hold, encumber, convey, or otherwise dispose of, such real and personal property as may be necessary or appropriate for its corporate purposes;
(g)
May invest and reinvest the principal and interest of its funds; and
(h)
Generally, may do any and all lawful acts necessary or appropriate to carry out the purposes for which the corporation is created.
