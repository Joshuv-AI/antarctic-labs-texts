10 U.S.C. § 14001 — Applicability of this part
Citation: us:usc:10:14001
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section14001
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Promotion And Retention Of Officers On The Reserve Active-Status List); Chapter 1401 (Applicability And Reserve Active-Status Lists). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter and chapters 1403 through 1411 of this title apply, as appropriate, to all reserve officers of the Army, Navy, Air Force, and Marine Corps except warrant officers.
