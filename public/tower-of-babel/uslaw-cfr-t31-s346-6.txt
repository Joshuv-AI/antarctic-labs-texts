31 C.F.R. § 346.6 — Nontransferability.
Citation: us:cfr:31:346.6
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-346.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 346 (Regulations Governing United States Individual Retirement Bonds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

United States Individual Retirement Bonds are not transferable, and may not be sold, discounted or pledged as collateral for a loan or as security for the performance of an obligation, or for any other purpose.
