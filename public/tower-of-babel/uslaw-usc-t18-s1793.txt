18 U.S.C. § 1793 — Trespass on Bureau of Prisons reservations and land
Citation: us:usc:18:1793
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1793
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 87 (Prisons). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever, without lawful authority or permission, goes upon a reservation, land, or a facility of the Bureau of Prisons shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than six months, or both.
