18 U.S.C. § 753 — Rescue to prevent execution
Citation: us:usc:18:753
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section753
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 35 (Escape And Rescue). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever, by force, sets at liberty or rescues any person found guilty in any court of the United States of any capital crime, while going to execution or during execution, shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than twenty-five years, or both.
