7 C.F.R. § 631.13 — Disputes and appeals for matters other than contract violations.
Citation: us:cfr:7:631.13
Creator: Natural Resources Conservation Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-631.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 631 (Great Plains Conservation Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applicants or participants may appeal decisions regarding matters other than contract disputes under this part in accordance with part 614 of this title.
