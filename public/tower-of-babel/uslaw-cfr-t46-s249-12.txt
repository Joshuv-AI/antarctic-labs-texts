46 C.F.R. § 249.12 — Waivers.
Citation: us:cfr:46:249.12
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-249.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 249 (Approval Of Underwriters For Marine Hull Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provision of this part may be waived in writing, for special circumstances and good cause shown, provided the procedures adopted are consistent with the Act and with the intent of these regulations.
