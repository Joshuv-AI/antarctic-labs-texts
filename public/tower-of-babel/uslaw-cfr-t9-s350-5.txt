9 C.F.R. § 350.5 — Application for service.
Citation: us:cfr:9:350.5
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-350.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 350 (Special Services Relating To Meat And Other Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person who desires to receive service under the regulations in this part for meat or other product eligible therefor under such regulations may make application for service to the Administrator, upon an application form which will be furnished by the Administrator upon request.
