37 C.F.R. § 205.4 — Relationship of this part to the Federal Rules of Civil and Criminal Procedure.
Citation: us:cfr:37:205.4
Creator: U.S. Copyright Office, Library Of Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-205.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 205 (Legal Processes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this part waives any requirement under the Federal Rules of Civil or Criminal Procedure.
