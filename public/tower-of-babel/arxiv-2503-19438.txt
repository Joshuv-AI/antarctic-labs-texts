Introduction

) that

\displaystyle M_{k+1}

\displaystyle\leq C\,\bigl\|t^{-\nu}|D_{x}|^{\gamma-\frac{1}{m+2}}(t^{\alpha}F(u_{k}))\bigr\|_{L^{p_{0}}([1,\infty)\times\mathbb{R}^{2})}+M_{0}

(3.6)

\displaystyle\leq C\,\bigl\|t^{\alpha-\nu}|D_{x}|^{\gamma-\frac{1}{m+2}}F(u_{k})\bigr\|_{L^{p_{0}}([1,\infty)\times\mathbb{R}^{2})}+M_{0}

\displaystyle\leq C\,\|t^{(p-1)\nu}F^{\prime}(u_{k})\|_{L^{\frac{m+3}{2(\nu+1)}}([1,\infty)\times\mathbb{R}^{2})}\bigl\|t^{\nu}|D_{x}|^{\gamma-\frac{1}{m+2}}u_{k}\bigr\|_{L^{q_{0}}([1,\infty)\times\mathbb{R}^{2})}+M_{0}

\displaystyle\leq C\|t^{(p-1)\nu}F^{\prime}(u_{k})\|_{L^{\frac{m+3}{2(\nu+1)}}([1,\infty)\times\mathbb{R}^{2})}M_{k}+M_{0}.

On the other hand, by Hölder’s inequality, (

) and the smallness of
\epsilon_{0}
, we arrive at

\|t^{(p-1)\nu}F^{\prime}(u_{k})\|_{L^{\frac{m+3}{2(\nu+1)}}([1,\infty)\times\mathbb{R}^{2})}\leq C\,\|t^{\nu}u_{k}\|_{L^{r}([1,\infty)\times\mathbb{R}^{2})}^{p-1}\leq CM_{k}^{p-1}\leq C\epsilon_{0}^{p-1}\leq\frac{1}{2}.

(3.7)

This, together with (
3.6
) and (

Therefore,

holds for any

by the induction method.

Let
N_{k}=\|t^{\nu}(u_{k}-u_{k-1})\|_{L^{q_{0}}([1,\infty)\times\mathbb{R}^{2})}
. Then by Lemma
2.5
with
q=q_{0}
and
\gamma=\frac{1}{m+2}
,
we have that for small

,

\displaystyle N_{k+1}

\displaystyle=\|t^{\nu}(u_{k+1}-u_{k})\|_{L^{q_{0}}([1,\infty)\times\mathbb{R}^{2})}\leq\|t^{\alpha-\nu}\left(F(u_{k})-F(u_{k-1})\right)\|_{L^{p_{0}}([1,\infty)\times\mathbb{R}^{2})}

(3.8)

\displaystyle\leq\bigl(\|t^{\nu}u_{k}\|_{L^{r}([1,\infty)\times\mathbb{R}^{2})}+\|t^{\nu}u_{k-1}\|_{L^{r}([1,\infty)\times\mathbb{R}^{2})}\bigr)^{p-1}\|t^{\nu}(u_{k}-u_{k-1})\|_{L^{q_{0}}([1,\infty)\times\mathbb{R}^{2})}

\displaystyle\leq(M_{k}+M_{k-1})^{p-1}\|t^{\nu}(u_{k}-u_{k-1})\|_{L^{q_{0}}([1,\infty)\times\mathbb{R}^{2})}

\displaystyle\leq C\epsilon_{0}^{p-1}\|t^{\nu}(u_{k}-u_{k-1})\|_{L^{q_{0}}([1,\infty)\times\mathbb{R}^{2})}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{2}\,\|t^{\nu}(u_{k}-u_{k-1})\|_{L^{q_{0}}([1,\infty)\times\mathbb{R}^{2})}=\frac{1}{2}N_{k}

such that
u_{k}\rightarrow u
in
L^{q_{0}}_{loc}([1,\infty)\times\mathbb{R}^{2})
and hence in

\mathcal{D}^{\prime}([1,\infty)\times\mathbb{R}^{2})
.
On the other hand, for any fixed compact set

K\Subset[1,\infty)\times\mathbb{R}^{2}
, one has

\displaystyle\|t^{\alpha}F(u_{k})-t^{\alpha}F(u)\|_{L^{1}(K)}

\displaystyle\leq C_{K}\|t^{\alpha-\nu}\left(F(u_{k})-F(u)\right)\|_{L^{p_{0}}(K)}

(3.9)

\displaystyle\leq C_{K}(\|t^{\nu}u_{k}\|_{L^{r}(K)}+\|t^{\nu}u\|_{L^{r}(K)})^{p-1}\|t^{\nu}(u_{k}-u)\|_{L^{q_{0}}(K)}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{K}\epsilon_{0}^{p-1}\|t^{\nu}(u_{k}-u)\|_{L^{q_{0}}(K)}\rightarrow 0\quad as\quad k\rightarrow\infty.

This means
t^{\alpha}F(u_{k})\rightarrow t^{\alpha}F(u)
in
L^{1}_{loc}([1,\infty)\times\mathbb{R}^{2})
and

u
is a weak solution of (
1.17
).

By
u_{k}\rightarrow u
in
L^{q_{0}}_{loc}([1,\infty)\times\mathbb{R}^{2})
,
then there exists a subsequence, which is
still denoted by
\{u_{k}\}
, such that
u_{k}\rightarrow u
a.e.
Together with
\|t^{\nu}u_{k}\|_{L^{r}([1,\infty)\times\mathbb{R}^{2})}\leq 2M_{0}
and Fatou’s
lemma, this yields

\|t^{\nu}u\|_{L^{r}([1,\infty)\times\mathbb{R}^{2})}\leq 2M_{0}.

Therefore
u_{k}\rightarrow u\in L^{r}([1,+\infty)\times\mathbb{R}^{2})
holds.

Until now we have proved the global existence of (
1.17
) for

r=\frac{m+3}{2(\nu+1)\left(p-1\right)}\geq q_{0}=\frac{2m+6}{m+1-2\nu}
or equivalently

.
Note that under the condition

,
when

, the restricted condition

in Lemma
2.1
is satisfied.
Furthermore, for the choice of

. Thus the global existence of (

) can be shown when

.
∎

For the solution

(3.14)

For the solution

Based on (

Let
