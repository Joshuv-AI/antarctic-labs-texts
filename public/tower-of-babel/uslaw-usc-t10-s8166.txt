10 U.S.C. § 8166 — Precedence accorded commanding officers
Citation: us:usc:10:8166
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8166
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 821 (Officers In Command). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The commanding officer of a vessel or of a naval station takes precedence over all officers under his command.
