38 C.F.R. § 26.0 — Purpose of this subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:38:26.0
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-26.0
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 26 (National Environmental Policy Act Implementing Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart provides the procedures by which the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) considers the environmental effects of its actions in carrying out the VA mission. This subpart also sets forth the scope and terminology of this part, and the responsibilities for implementing the requirements of the National Environmental Policy Act (NEPA) and this part.
