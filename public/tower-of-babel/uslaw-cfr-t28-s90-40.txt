28 C.F.R. § 90.40 — Other sources of funding.
Citation: us:cfr:28:90.40
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-90.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 90 (Violence Against Women). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If there are other sources of federal funding available to pay for a particular cost associated with the exercise of STCJ, participating Tribes must expend funds from those sources before seeking reimbursement from this program. Examples include existing Department of Justice grant funds, Medicare/Medicaid, and Bureau of Indian Affairs funding.
