7 C.F.R. § 1423.10 — Exceptions for United States Warehouse Act licensed warehouses.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1423.10
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1423.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1423 (Commodity Credit Corporation Approved Warehouses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The financial requirements, net worth alternatives and examination provisions of this part do not apply if the warehouse operator is licensed under the U.S. Warehouse Act (USWA) for such commodities, but an examination under this part will be made of such a warehouse whenever CCC determines such action is necessary to protect its interests.
