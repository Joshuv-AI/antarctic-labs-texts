27 C.F.R. § 25.64 — Maintenance of notice file.
Citation: us:cfr:27:25.64
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-25.64
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 25 (Beer). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The brewer shall maintain the approved Brewer's Notice, Form 5130.10, and all incorporated documents at the brewery premises, in complete and current condition, readily available for inspection by an appropriate TTB officer.
