47 C.F.R. § 76.1715 — Sponsorship identification.
Citation: us:cfr:47:76.1715
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-76.1715
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 76 (Multichannel Video And Cable Television Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever sponsorship announcements are omitted pursuant to § 76.1615(f) of subpart T, the cable television system operator shall observe the following conditions:
(a) Maintain a list showing the name, address, and (where available) the telephone number of each advertiser;
(b) Make this list available to members of the public who have a legitimate interest in obtaining the information contained in the list.
