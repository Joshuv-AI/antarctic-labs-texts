49 U.S.C. § 45304 — Maximum fees for private person services
Citation: us:usc:49:45304
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section45304
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Air Commerce And Safety); Subpart iii (Safety); Chapter 453 (Fees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator of the Federal Aviation Administration may establish maximum fees that private persons may charge for services performed under a delegation to the person under section 44702(d) of this title.
