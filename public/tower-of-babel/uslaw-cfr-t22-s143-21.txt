22 C.F.R. § 143.21 — General responsibilities.
Citation: us:cfr:22:143.21
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-143.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 143 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Age In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each agency recipient has primary responsibility to ensure that its programs and activities are in compliance with the Act, the government-wide regulations, and these regulations.
