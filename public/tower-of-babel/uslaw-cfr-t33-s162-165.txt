33 C.F.R. § 162.165 — Buffalo and Rochester Harbors, New York.
Citation: us:cfr:33:162.165
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-162.165
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 162 (Inland Waterways Navigation Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In Buffalo and Rochester Harbors, no vessel may exceed 6 miles per hour, except in the outer harbors where no vessel may exceed 10 miles per hour.
