42 U.S.C. § 655a — Provision for reimbursement of expenses
Citation: us:usc:42:655a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section655a
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 7 (Social Security); Subchapter IV (Grants To States For Aid And Services To Needy Families With Children And For Child-Welfare Services); Part D (Child Support And Establishment Of Paternity). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of section 655 of this title, expenses incurred to reimburse State employment offices for furnishing information requested of such offices—
(1)
pursuant to section 49b(b) of title 29, or
(2)
by a State or local agency charged with the duty of carrying a State plan for child support approved under this part,
shall be considered to constitute expenses incurred in the administration of such State plan.
