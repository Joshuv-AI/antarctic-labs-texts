7 U.S.C. § 1585 — Certain seeds not adapted for general agricultural use
Citation: us:usc:7:1585
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1585
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 37 (Seeds); Subchapter III (Foreign Commerce). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whenever the Secretary of Agriculture, after a public hearing, determines that seed of alfalfa or red clover from any foreign country is not adapted for general agricultural use in the United States, the Secretary shall publish the determination and the reasons for the determination.
