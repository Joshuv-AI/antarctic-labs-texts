47 C.F.R. § 95.2933 — Prohibited use of PLBs and MSLDs.
Citation: us:cfr:47:95.2933
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-95.2933
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 95 (Personal Radio Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) PLBs must not be used for any purpose other than transmission of distress and safety of life communications.
(b) Use of MSLDs on land is not authorized.
