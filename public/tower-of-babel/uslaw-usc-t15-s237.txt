15 U.S.C. § 237 — Standard barrels for lime
Citation: us:usc:15:237
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section237
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 6 (Weights And Measures And Standard Time); Subchapter VI (Standard Barrels). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is established a large and a small barrel of lime, the large barrel to consist of two hundred and eighty pounds and the small barrel to consist of one hundred and eighty pounds, net weight.
