38 U.S.C. § 1733 — Supervision of program by the President
Citation: us:usc:38:1733
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section1733
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 17 (Hospital, Nursing Home, Domiciliary, And Medical Care); Subchapter IV (Hospital Care And Medical Treatment For Veterans In The Republic Of The Philippines). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President, or any officer of the United States to whom the President may delegate authority under this section, may from time to time prescribe such rules and regulations and impose such conditions on the receipt of financial aid as may be necessary to carry out this subchapter.
