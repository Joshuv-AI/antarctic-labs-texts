44 U.S.C. § 4104 — Definition
Citation: us:usc:44:4104
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section4104
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 41 (Access To Federal Electronic Information). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this chapter, the term “Federal electronic information” means Federal public information stored electronically.
