21 C.F.R. § 870.1670 — Syringe actuator for an injector.
Citation: us:cfr:21:870.1670
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-870.1670
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 870 (Cardiovascular Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. A syringe actuator for an injector is an electrical device that controls the timing of an injection by an angiographic or indicator injector and synchronizes the injection with the electrocardiograph signal.
(b) Classification. Class II (performance standards).
