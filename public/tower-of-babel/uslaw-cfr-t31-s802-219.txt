31 C.F.R. § 802.219 — Foreign government.
Citation: us:cfr:31:802.219
Creator: Office Of Investment Security, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-802.219
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 802 (Regulations Pertaining To Certain Transactions By Foreign Persons Involving Real Estate In The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term foreign government means any government or body exercising governmental functions, other than the U.S. Government or a subnational government of the United States. The term includes, but is not limited to, national and subnational governments, including their respective departments, agencies, and instrumentalities.
