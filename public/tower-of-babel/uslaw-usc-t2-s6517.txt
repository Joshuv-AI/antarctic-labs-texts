2 U.S.C. § 6517 — Receipts from sale of used or surplus furniture and furnishings of Senate
Citation: us:usc:2:6517
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section6517
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 65 (Senate Officers And Administration); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On and after October 1, 1982, receipts from the sale of used or surplus furniture and furnishings shall be deposited in the United States Treasury for credit to the appropriation for “Senate Office Buildings” under the heading “Architect of the Capitol.”
