36 C.F.R. § 264.12 — Use without permission.
Citation: us:cfr:36:264.12
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-264.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 264 (Property Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Business or calling cards commercially prepared at employee expense for employees assigned to the Volcanic Monument may depict the official Monument symbol without special permission from Forest Service officials.
