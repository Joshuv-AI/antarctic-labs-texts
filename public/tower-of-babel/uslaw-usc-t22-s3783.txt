22 U.S.C. § 3783 — Disposition of property of United States
Citation: us:usc:22:3783
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section3783
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 51 (Panama Canal); Subchapter I (Administration And Regulations); Part 5 (Public Property). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No property of the United States located in the Republic of Panama may be disposed of except pursuant to law enacted by the Congress.
