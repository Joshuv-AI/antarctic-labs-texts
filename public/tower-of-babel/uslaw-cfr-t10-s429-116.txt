10 C.F.R. § 429.116 — Additional certification testing requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:10:429.116
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-429.116
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 429 (Certification, Compliance, And Enforcement For Consumer Products And Commercial And Industrial Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pursuant to § 429.102(b)(2), if DOE determines that independent, third-party testing is necessary to ensure a manufacturer's compliance with the rules of this part, part 430, or part 431, a manufacturer must base its certification of a basic model under subpart B of this part on independent, third-party laboratory testing.
