Weak Fano bundles of rank 2 2 over hyperquadrics Q n Q^{n} of dimension n ≥ 5 n\geq 5
Yuta Takahashi

Abstract
. A vector bundle whose projectivization becomes a weak Fano variety is called a weak Fano bundle. We present classification results for rank 2 weak Fano bundles on higher-dimensional quadrics Q n Q^{n} of dimension ≥ 5 \geq 5 .

Introduction

In this paper, we study weak Fano bundles over a smooth quadric hypersurface

defined over an algebraically closed field

A vector bundle

on a smooth projective variety

is a Fano variety. Szurek and Wiśniewski showed that the base space

of a Fano bundle

[
21

Fano bundles on

and studied rank
2
Fano bundles on

[
21

and

[
1

After that, Sols, Szurek and Wiśniewski completed the classification of rank
2
Fano bundles on

in
[
20
]
. More generally, Muñoz, Occhetta and Solá Conde completely classified rank

Fano bundles on smooth Fano varieties

with

in
[
14
,
15
,
16
]
.

On the other hand, Langer introduced a

as a natural generalization of Fano bundles in
[
9
]
. A vector bundle
{\mathcal{E}}
on a smooth projective variety

is a

if the projectivization of

is a weak Fano variety. Until now rank

weak Fano bundles were classified when the base space is the projective space
{\mathbb{P}}^{n}

[
23
]
or a cubic hypersurface in
{\mathbb{P}}^{4}
, i.e., a del Pezzo 3-fold of degree three
[

]
or a del Pezzo 3-fold

[

,
4

,
17
]

As opposed to Fano bundles, rank
2
weak Fano bundles on quadric hypersurfaces

where

have not yet been classified. Our main purpose of this paper is to prove the following theorem.

Theorem 1.1
.

Let
{\mathcal{E}}
be a weak Fano bundle of rank
2
over

where

. Then
{\mathcal{E}}
is a direct sum of line bundles or the Cayley bundle on

up to twist with a line bundle (see Definition

To prove this theorem, we follow the idea of
[
1
]
. The approach to classify Fano bundles of rank

on

in
[

is based on two key ingredients. The first ingredient is to show that

is globally generated for a normalized Fano bundle

of rank

on

of

]

Assume that

\displaystyle-K_{X}

\displaystyle=2\xi_{{\mathcal{E}}}+(2k-c_{1}({\mathcal{E}}))H

\displaystyle=2\xi_{{\mathcal{E}}(k+1)}-(c_{1}({\mathcal{E}})+2)H.

{\mathcal{E}}(k+1)
is ample.

Proof.

Since

and

\displaystyle(-K_{X}+(c_{1}({\mathcal{E}})+2)H)\cdot L

Let

\displaystyle(-K_{X}+(c_{1}({\mathcal{E}})+2)H)\cdot C

is unstable by Proposition

\displaystyle c_{2}({\mathcal{F}})

\displaystyle=c_{2}({\mathcal{E}})+(n-1)c_{1}({\mathcal{E}})+(n-1)^{2}

\displaystyle<(n-1)c_{1}({\mathcal{E}})+(n-1)^{2}+1

\displaystyle=(n-1)(c_{1}({\mathcal{F}})-2(n-1))+(n-1)^{2}+1

\displaystyle=(n-1)c_{1}({\mathcal{F}})-(n-1)^{2}+1

\displaystyle\leq(n-2)(c_{1}({\mathcal{F}})-n+2)+n-3+c_{1}({\mathcal{E}})

By Proposition

\displaystyle\frac{4}{3}m^{2}<\displaystyle\frac{71}{4{\rm sin}^{2}\left(\displaystyle\frac{\pi}{m}\right)}
. In other words, it is enough to show that

\displaystyle\frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}m<\displaystyle\frac{\sqrt{71}}{2{\rm sin}\left(\displaystyle\frac{\pi}{m}\right)}\hskip 5.69054pt{\rm for}\hskip 2.84526ptm\geq 11.

Set

f(x):=\frac{\sqrt{213}}{4}-\frac{1}{x}\sin(\pi x)\quad\left(0<x\leq\frac{1}{11}\right).

We show that
f(x)>0
on this interval. A direct calculation gives

f^{\prime}(x)=\frac{\sin(\pi x)-\pi x\cos(\pi x)}{x^{2}}.

Put

g(x):=\sin(\pi x)-\pi x\cos(\pi x).

Then

g^{\prime}(x)=\pi^{2}x\sin(\pi x)>0

for
0<x\leq 1/11
, and
\displaystyle\lim_{x\to+0}g(x)=0
. Hence

g(x)>0
for
0<x\leq 1/11
. Therefore
f^{\prime}(x)>0
, and so

f(x)
is increasing. Moreover,

\lim_{x\to+0}f(x)=\frac{\sqrt{213}}{4}-\pi>0.

Thus
f(x)>0
for every
0<x\leq 1/11
.
In other words,
\displaystyle\frac{4}{3}(n-1)^{2}<\displaystyle\frac{71}{4{\rm sin}^{2}\left(\displaystyle\frac{\pi}{n-1}\right)}
for
n\geq 12
. This is a contradiction.

Definition 4.3
.

The Cayley bundle
{\mathcal{E}}
over
Q^{5}
is defined as a stable vector bundle of rank
2
with Chern classes
(c_{1}({\mathcal{E}}),c_{2}({\mathcal{E}}))=(-1,1)
.

Proposition 4.4
.

Let
{\mathcal{E}}
be a weak Fano bundle of rank
2
over
Q^{n}
where
5\leq n\leq 11
. If
c_{1}({\mathcal{E}})^{2}<4c_{2}({\mathcal{E}})
, then
n=5
and
{\mathcal{E}}
is a Cayley bundle up to a twist.

Proof.

Assume that
{\mathcal{E}}
is normalized. Write
{\mathcal{E}}_{0}:={\mathcal{E}}\left(\frac{n-c_{1}({\mathcal{E}})}{2}\right)
. By Lemma
2.12
,
{\mathcal{E}}_{0}
is nef. Applying
[
1
, Proposition 1.4]
, we have
c_{1}({\mathcal{E}}_{0})^{2}\geq\alpha c_{2}({\mathcal{E}}_{0})
with

\alpha=\begin{cases}3&(n=5,6)\\
2+\sqrt{2}&(n=7,8)\\
\frac{5}{2}+\frac{\sqrt{5}}{2}&(n=9,10)\\
2+\sqrt{3}&(n\geq 11).\end{cases}

Since
c_{1}({\mathcal{E}}_{0})^{2}=(c_{1}({\mathcal{E}})+(n-c_{1}({\mathcal{E}})))^{2}=n^{2}
and

\alpha c_{2}({\mathcal{E}}_{0})=\alpha\left(c_{2}\left({\mathcal{E}}\right)-\frac{1}{4}c_{1}({\mathcal{E}})^{2}+\frac{1}{4}n^{2}\right),

we have
n^{2}\geq\alpha\left(c_{2}\left({\mathcal{E}}\right)-\frac{1}{4}c_{1}({\mathcal{E}})^{2}+\frac{1}{4}n^{2}\right)
.

Claim 4.5
.

c_{2}({\mathcal{E}})\leq 2
, if
n\neq 6
and

n=6
.

Proof.

Consider the case where

and

c_{2}({\mathcal{E}})\leq 2
. Other cases can be calculated similarly.

Firstly we consider the case of
c_{2}=3
.

If
n=6
and

, then

c_{1}\left(\mathcal{E}\left(\frac{7}{2}\right)\right)=6,\qquad c_{2}\left(\mathcal{E}\left(\frac{7}{2}\right)\right)=\frac{47}{4}.

Thus Lemma 2.11 gives

\displaystyle s_{6}\left(\mathcal{E}\left(\frac{7}{2}\right)\right)

\displaystyle=2c_{1}^{6}\left(\mathcal{E}\left(\frac{7}{2}\right)\right)-10c_{1}^{4}\left(\mathcal{E}\left(\frac{7}{2}\right)\right)c_{2}\left(\mathcal{E}\left(\frac{7}{2}\right)\right)

\displaystyle+12c_{1}^{2}\left(\mathcal{E}\left(\frac{7}{2}\right)\right)c_{2}^{2}\left(\mathcal{E}\left(\frac{7}{2}\right)\right)-2c_{2}^{3}\left(\mathcal{E}\left(\frac{7}{2}\right)\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{32}(-82223)<0.

This contradicts for Lemma
2.12
. Next we consider the case of

\displaystyle=2+\frac{55}{24}(-2)+\frac{5}{24}\cdot 8

\displaystyle=-\frac{11}{2}\notin{\mathbb{Z}}

by Lemma
2.10
. Similarly, if

2.10
