22 C.F.R. § 506.4 — Annual goals and timetables.
Citation: us:cfr:22:506.4
Creator: United States Agency For Global Media
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-506.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 506 (Part-Time Career Employment Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Board-wide plan for promoting part-time employment opportunities will be developed annually by the Office of Personnel after consultation with the operating elements. This plan will establish annual goals and set deadlines for achieving these goals.
