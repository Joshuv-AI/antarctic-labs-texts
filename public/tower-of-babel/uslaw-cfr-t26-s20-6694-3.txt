26 C.F.R. § 20.6694-3 — -3 Penalty for understatement due to willful, reckless, or intentional conduct.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 20 (Estate Tax; Estates Of Decedents Dying After August 16, 1954). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In general. A person who is a tax return preparer of any return or claim for refund of estate tax under chapter 11 of subtitle B of the Internal Revenue Code (Code) shall be subject to penalties under section 6694(b) of the Code in the manner stated in § 1.6694-3 of this chapter.
(b) Effective/applicability date. This section is applicable to returns and claims for refund filed, and advice provided, after December 31, 2008.
