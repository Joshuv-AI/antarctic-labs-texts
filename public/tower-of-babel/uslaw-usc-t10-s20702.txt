10 U.S.C. § 20702 — Modifications to career fields and codes
Citation: us:usc:10:20702
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section20702
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Space Force); Chapter 2015 (Reports). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 60 days before the date on which a change is made to the career fields or mission occupational specialty codes for the Space Force, the Secretary of the Air Force shall submit to the Committees on Armed Forces of the Senate and the House of Representatives a report that includes—
(1)
a description of the changes intended to be made to such career fields or mission occupational specialty codes; and
(2)
the plan of the Secretary to maintain the Space Force competencies and comply with requirements in law with respect to Space Force career fields and duty assignments.
