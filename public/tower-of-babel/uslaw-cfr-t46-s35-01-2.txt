46 C.F.R. § 35.01-2 — -2 Preemptive effect.
Citation: us:cfr:46:35.01-2
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-35.01-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 35 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part have preemptive effect over State or local regulations in the same field.
