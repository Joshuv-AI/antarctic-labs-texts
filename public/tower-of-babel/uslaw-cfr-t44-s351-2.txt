44 C.F.R. § 351.2 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:44:351.2
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-351.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 351 (Radiological Emergency Planning And Preparedness). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The emergency planning and preparedness responsibilities covered by this part relate to consequences and activities which extend beyond the boundaries of any fixed nuclear facility with a potential for serious consequences and the area affected by a transportation accident involving radioactive materials.
