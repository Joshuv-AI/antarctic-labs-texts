30 C.F.R. § 28.30 — Quality control plans; filing requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:30:28.30
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-28.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 28 (Fuses For Use With Direct Current In Providing Short-Circuit Protection For Trailing Cables In Coal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As a part of each application for approval or modification of approval submitted pursuant to this part, each applicant shall file with MSHA a proposed quality control plan which shall be designed to assure the quality of short-circuit protection provided by the fuse for which approval is sought.
