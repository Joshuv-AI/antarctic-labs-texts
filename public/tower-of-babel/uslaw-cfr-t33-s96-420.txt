33 C.F.R. § 96.420 — What authority may an organization ask for under this regulation?
Citation: us:cfr:33:96.420
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-96.420
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 96 (Rules For The Safe Operation Of Vessels And Safety Management Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An organization may request authorization to conduct safety management audits and to issue the following certificates:
(1) Safety Management Certificate;
(2) Document of Compliance certificate;
(3) Interim Safety Management Certificate; and
(4) Interim Document of Compliance certificate.
