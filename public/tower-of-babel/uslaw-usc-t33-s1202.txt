33 U.S.C. § 1202 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:33:1202
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section1202
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 24 (Vessel Bridge-To-Bridge Communication). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of this chapter—
(1)
“Secretary” means the Secretary of the Department in which the Coast Guard is operating;
(2)
“power-driven vessel” means any vessel propelled by machinery; and
(3)
“towing vessel” means any commercial vessel engaged in towing another vessel astern, alongside, or by pushing ahead.
