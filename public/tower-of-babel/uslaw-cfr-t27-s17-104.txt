27 C.F.R. § 17.104 — Deposit of collateral.
Citation: us:cfr:27:17.104
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-17.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 17 (Drawback On Taxpaid Distilled Spirits Used In Manufacturing Nonbeverage Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise provided by law or regulations, bonds or notes of the United States, or other obligations which are unconditionally guaranteed as to both interest and principal by the United States, may be pledged and deposited by principals as collateral security in lieu of bonds obtained from surety companies. Deposit of collateral security is governed by the provisions of 31 U.S.C. 9303, and 31 CFR part 225.
