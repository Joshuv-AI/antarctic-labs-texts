Request for Public Comment: 60-Day Notice for Reinstated 
Collection: Division of Urban Indian Affairs Uniform Data System and 
Budget Formulation

ACTION: Notice and request for comments. Request for a reinstated 
collection.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In compliance with the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995, the 
Indian Health Service (IHS) invites the public to comment on the 
information collection titled, ``Division of Urban Indian Affairs 
(DUIA) Uniform Data System and Budget Formulation,'' Office of 
Management and Budget (OMB) Control Number 0917-0007. The IHS is 
requesting OMB to approve a reinstated collection.

DATES: Comment Due Date: December 22, 2026. Your comments regarding 
this

received within 60 days of the date of this publication.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Reinstated: Title: Division of Urban Indian 
Affairs Uniform Data System and Budget Formulation. Type of Information 
Collection Request: Request for data collection to ensure contract 
compliance and to report to Congress and policymakers on program 
accomplishments. Reporting formats are contained in the Uniform Data 
System (UDS) Reporting Guidance Manual. Need and Use of Information 
Collection: The UDS and Budget Formulation (BF) contain the annual 
reporting requirements for the cluster of ambulatory, limited 
ambulatory, and case management/outreach and referral urban Indian 
organizations (UIOs) funded by the IHS. The authorizing statute is 
Title V of Public Law 94-437, of the Indian Health Care Improvement 
Act, as amended. The IHS will collect data in the UDS and BF, which 
will be used to ensure contract compliance and for reports to Congress 
and policymakers on program accomplishments. To meet these objectives, 
the DUIA will collect a core set of data annually that is appropriate 
for monitoring and evaluating performance and reporting on annual 
trends. Affected Public: Title V-funded Urban Indian health programs. 
Type of Respondents: Urban Indian Organizations.

    Requests for Comments: Your written comments and/or suggestions are 
invited on one or more of the following points:
    (a) whether the information collection activity is necessary to

    (b) whether the agency processes the information collected in a
