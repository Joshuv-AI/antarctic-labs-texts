24 C.F.R. § 5.857 — When must I prohibit admission of alcohol abusers?
Citation: us:cfr:24:5.857
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-5.857
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 5 (General Hud Program Requirements; Waivers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must establish standards that prohibit admission to federally assisted housing if you determine you have reasonable cause to believe that a household member's abuse or pattern of abuse of alcohol interferes with the health, safety, or right to peaceful enjoyment of the premises by other residents.
