41 C.F.R. § 50-205.9 — -205.9 Inspections by the Department of Labor.
Citation: us:cfr:41:50-205.9
Creator: Chapter 50—Public Contracts, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-50-205.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 50-205 (205—Enforcement Of Safety And Health Standards By State Officers And Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director may conduct such inspections as he may find appropriate to assure compliance with the safety and health provisions of the Act or whenever he may find that a safety and health inspection should be carried out along with investigation under other provisions of the Act or the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938. Whenever an inspection by the Director discloses apparent violations of State safety and health requirements, the Director shall report such disclosures to the State agency.
