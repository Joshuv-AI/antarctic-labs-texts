28 U.S.C. § 536 — Positions in excepted service
Citation: us:usc:28:536
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section536
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part II (Department Of Justice); Chapter 33 (Federal Bureau Of Investigation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All positions in the Federal Bureau of Investigation are excepted from the competitive service, and the incumbents of such positions occupy positions in the excepted service.
