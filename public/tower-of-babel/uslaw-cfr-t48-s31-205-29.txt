48 C.F.R. § 31.205-29 — 31.205-29 Plant protection costs.
Citation: us:cfr:48:31.205-29
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-31.205-29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 31 (Contract Cost Principles And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Costs of items such as (a) wages, uniforms, and equipment of personnel engaged in plant protection, (b) depreciation on plant protection capital assets, and (c) necessary expenses to comply with military requirements, are allowable.
