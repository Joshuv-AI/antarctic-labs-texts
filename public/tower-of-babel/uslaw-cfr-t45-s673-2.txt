45 C.F.R. § 673.2 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:45:673.2
Creator: National Science Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-673.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 673 (Antarctic Non-Governmental Expeditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirements in this part apply to non-governmental expeditions to or within Antarctica for which the United States is required to give advance notice under Paragraph (5) of Article VII of the Antarctic Treaty.
