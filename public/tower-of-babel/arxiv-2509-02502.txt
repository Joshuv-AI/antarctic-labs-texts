Investigating charm-quark dynamics in the QGP via the charm-hadron elliptic flow in Pb–Pb collisions with ALICE
\firstname Chuntai \lastname Wu \fnsep

Abstract
In these proceedings, we report the measurement of the elliptic flow ( v 2 v_{\rm 2} ) of prompt and non-prompt D 0 \mathrm{D^{0}} , prompt D + \mathrm{D^{+}} and D s + \mathrm{D_{s}^{+}} mesons, and, for the first time ever at the LHC, prompt and non-prompt Λ c + \Lambda_{\mathrm{c}}^{+} baryons in Pb–Pb collisions at s NN = 5.36 ​ TeV \sqrt{s_{\mathrm{NN}}}=5.36\ \mathrm{TeV} collected by the ALICE experiment during the LHC Run 3. The analysis is performed at midrapidity ( | y | < 0.8 |y|<0.8 ) in different centrality classes (30–40%, 40–50%, and 60–80% for D 0 \mathrm{D^{0}} , D + \mathrm{D^{+}} , and D s + \mathrm{D_{s}^{+}} ; 30–50% for Λ c + \Lambda_{\mathrm{c}}^{+} ). The candidates are reconstructed from hadronic decay channels, and the v 2 v_{\rm 2} is extracted via the scalar-product method. The measurements are compared to light-flavor results and several predictions of models that differently describe heavy-quark transport and hadronization processes in the QGP.



1
Introduction

Heavy quarks (charm and beauty) are excellent probes for investigating the properties of the quark–gluon plasma (QGP) generated in ultra-relativistic heavy-ion collisions. Their participation in the collective motion of the medium can be studied by measuring the elliptic flow of charm hadrons. In non-central heavy-ion collisions, the elliptic flow originates mainly from the initial-state spatial asymmetry
Liu:2012ax
. The elliptic flow
v_{\rm 2}
is the second-order coefficient of the Fourier decomposition of the particle azimuthal distribution. The harmonic coefficients, which quantify the flow anisotropy (
v_{\rm n}
), are calculated as
v_{\rm n}=\langle\cos[n(\phi-\Psi_{n})]\rangle
, where
\phi
is the particle’s azimuthal angle and
\Psi_{n}
denotes the symmetry-plane angle for the
n^{\mathrm{th}}
harmonic
Poskanzer:1998yz
. These measurements provide essential inputs to constrain theoretical models describing the charm-quark transport in the QGP, being sensitive to charm thermalization, heavy-quark diffusion coefficients, path-length dependence of parton energy loss, initial-state spatial anisotropy, and hadronization mechanisms. In particular, comparisons between charm meson and baryon
v_{\rm 2}
can provide further insights into medium-induced phenomena, such as the interplay of radial flow and the charm-quark hadronization via coalescence. Comparisons between charm and beauty also allow exploration of the mass dependence of relaxation times.

2
Data sample and analysis procedure

In these proceedings, we present measurements of the elliptic flow
v_{\rm 2}
of
\mathrm{D^{0}}
,
\mathrm{D^{+}}
, and
\mathrm{D_{s}^{+}}
mesons, and, for the first time ever at LHC,
\Lambda_{\mathrm{c}}^{+}
baryons, in
Pb–Pb
collisions at
\sqrt{s_{\mathrm{NN}}}=\ 5.36\ \mathrm{TeV}
, within the rapidity interval
|y|<0.8
. The analysis is based on the data samples collected with the ALICE detector in 2023. After the LHC Long Shutdown 2, the ALICE detector underwent a major upgrade, as detailed in Ref.
ALICE:2023udb
. The integrated luminosity of this minimum-bias data sample in 2023 reached approximately 1.5
\mathrm{nb}^{-1}
. The
v_{\rm 2}
of prompt
\mathrm{D^{0}}
,
\mathrm{D^{+}}
, and
\mathrm{D_{s}^{+}}
mesons is measured in the 30–50%, 30–40%, 40–50%, and 60–80% centrality classes. The
v_{\rm 2}
of the prompt and non-prompt
\Lambda_{\mathrm{c}}^{+}
baryons, as well as the non-prompt
\mathrm{D^{0}}
meson, is measured in the 30–50% centrality class.
The candidates are reconstructed by combining couples or triplets of tracks that pass some quality cuts, with the correct charge combination. To reduce the combinatorial background and separate the prompt and non-prompt contributions, a multi-class Machine Learning (ML) model, implemented as Boosted Decision Trees (BDT)
Chen:2016btl
, was trained, following the training strategy described in Ref.
ALICE:2023gjj
. Signal candidates were selected using the BDT-based selections. The inclusive
v_{\rm 2}
was extracted using simultaneous fits to the invariant mass spectrum and the
v_{\rm 2}
as a function of mass, and the feed-down fraction was estimated using a data-driven method
ALICE:2023gjj
. A linear fit to the inclusive
v_{\rm 2}
as a function of the feed-down fraction was used to evaluate the prompt and non-prompt
v_{\rm 2}
components.

5
Summary

In these proceedings, the first measurements of D-meson and
\Lambda_{\mathrm{c}}^{+}
-baryon
v_{\rm 2}
performed with ALICE using the 2023
Pb–Pb
data collected in Run 3 were reported.
In combination with future measurements of
R_{\mathrm{AA}}
in Run 3, the comparison of D-meson and
\Lambda_{\mathrm{c}}^{+}
-baryon
v_{\rm 2}
will constrain the description of the hadronization process and its impact on
v_{\rm 2}
, allowing for a more precise estimate of the heavy-quark diffusion coefficient.
Overall, with the inclusion of 2024 and 2025 data, we expect to analyze a data sample with a factor of 3 larger than the current one.
