9 C.F.R. § 351.16 — Certificate required for shipments of technical animal fat.
Citation: us:cfr:9:351.16
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-351.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 351 (Certification Of Technical Animal Fats For Export). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No certified plant shall export any certified technical animal fat unless the shipment is accompanied by a certificate issued under § 351.3.
