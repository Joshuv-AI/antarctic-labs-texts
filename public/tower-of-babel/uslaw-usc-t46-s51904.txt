46 U.S.C. § 51904 — Presentation to representatives
Citation: us:usc:46:51904
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section51904
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part B (Merchant Marine Service); Chapter 519 (Merchant Marine Awards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If an individual to be issued an award under this chapter is unable to accept the award personally, the Secretary of Transportation may present the award to an appropriate representative.
