14 C.F.R. § 13.103 — Order of investigation.
Citation: us:cfr:14:13.103
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-13.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 13 (Investigative And Enforcement Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The order of investigation—
(a) Defines the scope of the investigation by describing the information sought in terms of its subject matter or its relevancy to specified FAA functions;
(b) Sets forth the form of the investigation which may be either by individual deposition or investigative proceeding or both; and
(c) Names the official who is authorized to conduct the investigation and serve as the presiding officer.
