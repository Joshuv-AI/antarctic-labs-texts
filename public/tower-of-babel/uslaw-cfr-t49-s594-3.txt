49 C.F.R. § 594.3 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:49:594.3
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-594.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 594 (Schedule Of Fees Authorized By 49 U.S.C. 30141). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to any person who applies to NHTSA to be granted the status of Registered Importer under part 592 of this chapter, to any person who has been granted such status, to any manufacturer not a Registered Importer who petitions the Administrator for a determination pursuant to part 593 of this chapter, and to any person who imports a motor vehicle into the United States pursuant to such determination.
