7 C.F.R. § 16.5 — Severability.
Citation: us:cfr:7:16.5
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-16.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 16 (Equal Opportunity For Religious Organizations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To the extent that any provision of this regulation is declared invalid by a court of competent jurisdiction, USDA intends for all other provisions that are capable of operating in the absence of the specific provision that has been invalidated to remain in effect.
