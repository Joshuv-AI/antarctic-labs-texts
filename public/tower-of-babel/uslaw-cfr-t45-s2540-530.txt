45 C.F.R. § 2540.530 — Are there instances where an insignia may be used without getting the approval of the Corporation?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2540.530
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2540.530
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2540 (General Administrative Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All uses of the national service insignia require the written approval of the Corporation.
