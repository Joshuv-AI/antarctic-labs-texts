2 U.S.C. § 6117 — Stationery allowance for President of Senate
Citation: us:usc:2:6117
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section6117
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 61 (Senate Leadership); Subchapter II (Vice President And President Pro Tempore). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Effective April 1, 1975, and each fiscal year thereafter, the annual allowance for stationery for the President of the Senate shall be $8,000.
