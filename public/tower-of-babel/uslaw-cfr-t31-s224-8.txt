31 C.F.R. § 224.8 — When must a surety corporation appoint a new process agent?
Citation: us:cfr:31:224.8
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-224.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 224 (Federal Process Agents Of Surety Corporations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The surety corporation must immediately appoint a new process agent whenever the authority of a process agent is terminated by reason of revocation, disability, removal from the district, or any other cause.
