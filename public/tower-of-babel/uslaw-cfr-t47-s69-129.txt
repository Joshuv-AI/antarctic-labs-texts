47 C.F.R. § 69.129 — Signalling for tandem switching.
Citation: us:cfr:47:69.129
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-69.129
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 69 (Access Charges). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A charge that is expressed in dollars and cents shall be assessed upon the purchasing entity by a local telephone company for provision of signalling for tandem switching.
