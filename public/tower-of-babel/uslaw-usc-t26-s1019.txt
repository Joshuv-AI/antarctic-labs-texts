26 U.S.C. § 1019 — Property on which lessee has made improvements
Citation: us:usc:26:1019
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section1019
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 1 (Normal Taxes And Surtaxes); Subchapter O (Gain Or Loss On Disposition Of Property); Part II (Basis Rules Of General Application). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Neither the basis nor the adjusted basis of any portion of real property shall, in the case of the lessor of such property, be increased or diminished on account of income derived by the lessor in respect of such property and excludable from gross income under section 109 (relating to improvements by lessee on lessor’s property).
