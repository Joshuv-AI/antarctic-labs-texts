17 C.F.R. § 249.444 — Form SE, form for submission of paper format exhibits by electronic filers.
Citation: us:cfr:17:249.444
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-249.444
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 249 (Forms, Securities Exchange Act Of 1934). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This form shall be used by an electronic filer for the submission of any paper format document relating to an otherwise electronic filing, as provided in Rule 311 of Regulation S-T (§ 232.311 of this chapter).
