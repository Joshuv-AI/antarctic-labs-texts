7 C.F.R. § 1948.55 — Source of funds.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1948.55
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Rural Business-Cooperative Service, And Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1948.55
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1948 (Rural Development). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Grants will be awarded from appropriate funds specifically allocated for this program.
(b) Grants made for growth management and housing planning may equal but will not exceed 10 percent of the total amount of funds appropriated for and allocated to this program.
