31 C.F.R. § 28.540 — Advertising.
Citation: us:cfr:31:28.540
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-28.540
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 28 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Sex In Education Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A recipient shall not in any advertising related to employment indicate preference, limitation, specification, or discrimination based on sex unless sex is a bona fide occupational qualification for the particular job in question.
