49 C.F.R. § 1021.6 — Method of claim payment.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1021.6
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1021.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1021 (Administrative Collection Of Enforcement Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Debtors shall be required to settle claims by:
(a) Payment via the Board's electronic payment system found on the Board's website.
(b) Installment payments via the Board's electronic payment system found on the Board's website after the execution of a promissory note containing an agreement for judgment.
