19 C.F.R. § 171.42 — Time limit for filing petition for restoration.
Citation: us:cfr:19:171.42
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-171.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 171 (Fines, Penalties, And Forfeitures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A petition for the restoration of proceeds of sale under section 613, Tariff Act of 1930, as amended (19 U.S.C. 1613) must be filed within 3 months after the date of the sale.
