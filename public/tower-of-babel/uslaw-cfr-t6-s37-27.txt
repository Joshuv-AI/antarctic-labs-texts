6 C.F.R. § 37.27 — Driver's licenses and identification cards issued during the age-based enrollment period.
Citation: us:cfr:6:37.27
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security, Office Of The Secretary
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-6/section-37.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 6 (Domestic Security), Part 37 (Real Id Driver'S Licenses And Identification Cards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Driver's licenses and identification cards issued to individuals prior to a DHS determination that the State is materially compliant may be renewed or reissued pursuant to current State practices, and will be accepted for official purposes until the validity dates described in § 37.5.
