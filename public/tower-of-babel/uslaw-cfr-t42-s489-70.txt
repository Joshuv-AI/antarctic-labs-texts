42 C.F.R. § 489.70 — Effect of payment by the Surety.
Citation: us:cfr:42:489.70
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-489.70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 489 (Provider Agreements And Supplier Approval). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Surety's payment to CMS under a bond for an unpaid claim or an unpaid civil money penalty or assessment, constitutes—
(a) Collection of the unpaid claim or unpaid civil money penalty or assessment (to the extent the Surety's payment on the bond covers such unpaid claim, civil money penalty, or assessment); and
(b) A basis for termination of the HHA's provider agreement under § 489.53(a)(1).
