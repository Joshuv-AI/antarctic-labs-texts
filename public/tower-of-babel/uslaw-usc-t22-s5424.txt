22 U.S.C. § 5424 — Peace Corps programs in Poland and Hungary
Citation: us:usc:22:5424
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section5424
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 63 (Support For East European Democracy (Seed)); Subchapter II (Private Sector Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out programs in Poland and Hungary under the Peace Corps Act [22 U.S.C. 2501 et seq.], $6,000,000 for the 3-year period beginning October 1, 1989, in addition to amounts otherwise available for such purposes. Such programs shall include the use of Peace Corps volunteers—
(1)
to provide English language training, and
(2)
to extend the technical skills described in section 5423(a) of this title to the people of Poland and Hungary, using the Associate Volunteer Program to the extent practicable.
