27 C.F.R. § 71.87 — Oath of reporter.
Citation: us:cfr:27:71.87
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-71.87
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 71 (Rules Of Practice In Permit Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The reporter making the stenographic record shall subscribe an oath before the administrative law judge, to be filed in the record of the case, that he will truly and correctly report the oral testimony and proceedings at such hearing and accurately transcribe the same to the best of his ability.
