7 C.F.R. § 766.60 — Canceling a Disaster Set-Aside.
Citation: us:cfr:7:766.60
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-766.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 766 (Direct Loan Servicing—Special). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Agency will cancel a DSA if:
(a) The Agency takes any primary loan servicing action on the loan;
(b) The borrower pays the current market value buyout in accordance with § 766.113; or
(c) The borrower pays the set-aside installment.
