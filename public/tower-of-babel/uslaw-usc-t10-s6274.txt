10 U.S.C. § 6274 — General plant projects
Citation: us:usc:10:6274
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section6274
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part VI (Elements Of Department Of Defense And Other Matters); Subpart B (Atomic Energy Defense); Chapter 607 (Budget And Financial Management Matters); Subchapter I (Recurring National Security Authorization Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Plant or construction projects for which amounts are made available under this and subsequent appropriation Acts 1 with a current estimated cost of less than $10,000,000 are considered for purposes of section 6273 as a plant project for which the approved total estimated cost does not exceed the minor construction threshold and for purposes of section 6275 as a construction project with a current estimated cost of less than a minor construction threshold.
