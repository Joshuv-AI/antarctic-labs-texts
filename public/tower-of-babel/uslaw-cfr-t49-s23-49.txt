49 C.F.R. § 23.49 — What is the base for a recipient's goal for car rentals?
Citation: us:cfr:49:23.49
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-23.49
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 23 (Participation Of Disadvantaged Business Enterprise In Airport Concessions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except in the case where you use the alternative goal approach of § 23.51(c)(5)(ii), the base for your goal is the total gross receipts of car rental operations at your airport. You do not include gross receipts of other concessions in this base.
