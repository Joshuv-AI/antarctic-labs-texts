Tunneling magnetoresistance in a junction made of X X -wave magnets with X = p , d , f , g , i X=p,d,f,g,i
Motohiko Ezawa

Abstract
We investigate the tunneling magnetoresistance (TMR) of a bilayer system made of X X -wave magnets with X = p , d , f , g , i X=p,d,f,g,i , where X = d , g , i X=d,g,i corresponds to altermagnets. A universal analytic formula is derived for the TMR ratio. It is proportional to | J | / ( N X ​ Γ ) \left|J\right|/\left(N_{X}\Gamma\right) for small Γ \Gamma , where N X N_{X} is the number of the nodes of the X X -wave magnet, J J is the strength of the X X -wave magnet, and Γ \Gamma is the self-energy. It is contrasted with the TMR ratio made of ferromagnets, where it is proportional to J 2 / Γ 2 J^{2}/\Gamma^{2} for small Γ \Gamma . Therefore, the TMR ratio is larger in ferromagnets for | J | > Γ \left|J\right|>\Gamma . However, the X X -wave magnets are expected to achieve high-speed and ultra-dense memory owing to the zero net magnetization.

\frac{G_{\text{AP}}}{4e\pi^{3}}=\pi^{2}\sum_{\mathbf{k}}\delta\left(\varepsilon_{+}\right)\delta\left(\varepsilon_{-}\right)=\pi^{2}\sum_{\mathbf{k}}\frac{\delta\left(k_{x}-k_{+}\right)}{\left|\frac{\partial\varepsilon_{+}}{\partial k_{x}}\right|}\delta\left(\varepsilon_{-}\right)

(49)

\frac{\partial\varepsilon_{+}}{\partial k_{x}}=\frac{\hbar^{2}k_{x}}{m}+Ja,

(50)

k_{\pm}=-aJ\frac{m}{\hbar^{2}}\pm\sqrt{2m\mu+a^{2}J^{2}\frac{m^{2}}{\hbar^{2}}-k_{y}^{2}}.

(51)

It is further calculated as

\frac{G_{\text{AP}}}{4e\pi^{3}}=\sum_{k_{y},\pm}\frac{\pi^{2}\delta\left(k_{y}\pm\sqrt{2m\mu}\right)}{\left|\left.\frac{\partial\varepsilon_{+}}{\partial k_{x}}\right|_{k_{x}=k_{\pm}}\right|\left|\left.\frac{\partial\varepsilon_{-}}{\partial k_{y}}\right|_{k_{x}=k_{\pm}}\right|}=\sqrt{\frac{2m}{\mu}}\frac{\pi^{2}}{\hbar a\left|J\right|}.

By requiring the Lorentzian form, we recover
\Gamma
in this equation as

\frac{G_{\text{AP}}}{4e\pi^{3}}=\sqrt{\frac{2m}{\mu}}\frac{\pi^{2}}{\hbar\sqrt{a^{2}J^{2}+\frac{\hbar^{2}}{2m\mu}\Gamma^{2}}},

where we have determined the coefficient of
\Gamma
to recover the result
with

V
Discussion

We have analytically derived a formula of the TMR ratio based on the
X
-wave magnet, which is proportional to
\left|J\right|/\left(N_{X}\Gamma\right)
. On the other hand, the TMR ratio based on
ferromagnets is proportional to
J^{2}/\Gamma^{2}
. Hence, the TMR ratio is
larger in ferromagnets for
\left|J\right|>\Gamma
. However, the
X
-wave magnets are expected to achieve high-speed and ultra-dense memory
owing to the zero net magnetization.
