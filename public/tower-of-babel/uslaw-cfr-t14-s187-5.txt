14 C.F.R. § 187.5 — Duplicates of licenses.
Citation: us:cfr:14:187.5
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-187.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 187 (Fees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The fee for furnishing to a person entitled thereto a replacement, duplicate, or facsimile of a certificate or other document evidencing a license, for which a fee is not specifically provided elsewhere in this chapter, is $2.
