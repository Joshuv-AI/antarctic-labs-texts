42 U.S.C. § 3030g — Criteria
Citation: us:usc:42:3030g
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section3030g
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 35 (Programs For Older Americans); Subchapter III (Grants For State And Community Programs On Aging); Part C (Nutrition Services); Subpart ii (Home Delivered Nutrition Services). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Assistant Secretary, in consultation with recognized experts in the fields of nutrition science, dietetics, meal planning and food service management, and aging, shall develop minimum criteria of efficiency and quality for the furnishing of home delivered meal services for projects described in section 3030f of this title.
