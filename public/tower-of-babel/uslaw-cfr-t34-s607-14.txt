34 C.F.R. § 607.14 — How many applications for a development grant may an institution submit?
Citation: us:cfr:34:607.14
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-607.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 607 (Strengthening Institutions Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In any fiscal year, an institution of higher education that meets the eligibility requirements under sections 311, 316, and 317 of the HEA may—
(a) Submit an application for a development grant authorized under sections 311, 316, and 317 of the HEA; and
(b) Be part of a cooperative arrangement application.
