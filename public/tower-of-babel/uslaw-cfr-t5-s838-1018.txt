5 C.F.R. § 838.1018 — Settlements.
Citation: us:cfr:5:838.1018
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-838.1018
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 838 (Court Orders Affecting Retirement Benefits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The former spouse may request that an amount be withheld from the retirement benefits that is less than the amount stipulated in the court order. This lower amount will be deemed a complete fulfillment of the obligation of OPM for the period in which the request is in effect.
