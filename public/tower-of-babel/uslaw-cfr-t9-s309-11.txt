9 C.F.R. § 309.11 — Vaccine livestock.
Citation: us:cfr:9:309.11
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-309.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 309 (Ante-Mortem Inspection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Vaccine livestock with unhealed lesions of vaccinia, accompanied with fever, which have not been exposed to any other infectious or contagious disease, are not required to be slaughtered and may be released for removal from the premises.
