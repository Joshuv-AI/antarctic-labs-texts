22 C.F.R. § 903.5 — Service of documents.
Citation: us:cfr:22:903.5
Creator: Foreign Service Grievance Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-903.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 903 (Initiation And Documentation Of Cases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any party submitting documents to the Board in connection with a case shall send a copy to the other parties and to the exclusive representative, if any. The Board shall send copies of its correspondence concerning the case to the parties and the exclusive representative, if any.
