32 C.F.R. § 1908.32 — Receipt, recording, and tasking.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1908.32
Creator: Central Intelligence Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1908.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1908 (Public Requests For Mandatory Declassification Review Of Classified Information Pursuant To Sec. 3.5 Of Executive Order 13526). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Coordinator shall promptly record each appeal received under this part, acknowledge receipt to the requester, and task the originator and other interested parties. Additional taskings, as required during the review process, shall be accomplished within ten (10) days of notification.
