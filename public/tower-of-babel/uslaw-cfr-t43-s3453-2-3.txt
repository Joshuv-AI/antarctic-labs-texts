43 C.F.R. § 3453.2-3 — -3 Filing location and fee.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3453.2-3
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3453.2-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3450 (Management Of Existing Leases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Instruments of transfer and requests for approval shall be filed in the Bureau of Land Management office having jurisdiction over the leased lands proposed for transfer (see 43 CFR subpart 1821). Each instrument of transfer shall be accompanied by a nonrefundable filing fee (see 43 CFR 3473.2).
