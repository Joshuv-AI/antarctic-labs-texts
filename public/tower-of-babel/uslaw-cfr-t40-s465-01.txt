40 C.F.R. § 465.01 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:40:465.01
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-465.01
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 465 (Coil Coating Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to any coil coating facility or to any canmaking facility that discharges pollutants to waters of the United States or that introduces pollutants to a publicly owned treatment works.
