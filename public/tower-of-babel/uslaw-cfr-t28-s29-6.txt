28 C.F.R. § 29.6 — Limited participation by states and localities permitted.
Citation: us:cfr:28:29.6
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-29.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 29 (Motor Vehicle Theft Prevention Act Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A State or locality need not authorize the stopping of motor vehicles under all sets of conditions specified under the program in order to participate in the program.
