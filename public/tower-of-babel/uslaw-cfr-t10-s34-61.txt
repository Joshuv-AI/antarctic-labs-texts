10 C.F.R. § 34.61 — Records of the specific license for industrial radiography.
Citation: us:cfr:10:34.61
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-34.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 34 (Licenses For Industrial Radiography And Radiation Safety Requirements For Industrial Radiographic Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each licensee shall maintain a copy of its license, license conditions, documents incorporated by reference, and amendments to each of these items until superseded by new documents approved by the Commission, or until the Commission terminates the license.
