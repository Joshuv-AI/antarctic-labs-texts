32 C.F.R. § 234.14 — Posting of materials.
Citation: us:cfr:32:234.14
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-234.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 234 (Conduct On The Pentagon Reservation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Posting or affixing materials, such as pamphlets, handbills, or fliers on the Pentagon Reservation is prohibited except as provided by § 234.13(b) or when conducted as part of activities approved by the applicable Building Management Office or Installation Commander under § 234.3(d).
