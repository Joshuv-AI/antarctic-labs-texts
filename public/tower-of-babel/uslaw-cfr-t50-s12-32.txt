50 C.F.R. § 12.32 — When may the Service or the Solicitor obtain administrative forfeiture of my property?
Citation: us:cfr:50:12.32
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-12.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 12 (Seizure And Forfeiture Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If your fish, wildlife, plant or other property is subject to forfeiture under any Act listed in § 12.2, and it is also property subject to administrative forfeiture, the Service or the Solicitor may initiate an administrative forfeiture proceeding of the property under the forfeiture procedures described in this subpart.
