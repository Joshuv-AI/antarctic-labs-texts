42 U.S.C. § 9209 — Utilization of capabilities and facilities
Citation: us:usc:42:9209
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section9209
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 100 (Wind Energy Systems). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall utilize the technological and management capabilities, equipment, and facilities of the National Aeronautics and Space Administration to the maximum extent practicable in carrying out his duties under this chapter, and shall enter into such additional agreements with the Administrator of such Administration as may be necessary for this purpose.
