42 C.F.R. § 426.468 — Decision to not appeal an ALJ's decision.
Citation: us:cfr:42:426.468
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-426.468
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 426 (Review Of National Coverage Determinations And Local Coverage Determinations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Failure to timely appeal without good cause shown waives the right to challenge any part(s) of the ALJ's decision under § 426.465.
(b) Unless the Board finds good cause shown for late filing, an untimely appeal is dismissed.
(c) If a party does not timely appeal any part(s) of the ALJ's decision on an LCD review to the Board, as provided in this subpart, then the ALJ's decision is final and not subject to further review.
