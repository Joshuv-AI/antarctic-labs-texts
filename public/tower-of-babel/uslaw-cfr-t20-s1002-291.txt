20 C.F.R. § 1002.291 — What actions may an individual take if the complaint is not resolved by VETS?
Citation: us:cfr:20:1002.291
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary For Veterans' Employment And Training Service, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-1002.291
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 1002 (Regulations Under The Uniformed Services Employment And Reemployment Rights Act Of 1994). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an individual receives a notification from VETS of an unsuccessful effort to resolve his or her complaint relating to a State or private employer, the individual may request that VETS refer the complaint to the Attorney General.
