Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 19 (Insurance); Subchapter II (United States Government Life Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purposes of this subchapter, the term “insurance” means United States Government life insurance.
