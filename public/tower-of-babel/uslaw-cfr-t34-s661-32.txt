34 C.F.R. § 661.32 — What priorities may the Secretary establish?
Citation: us:cfr:34:661.32
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-661.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 661 (Business And International Education Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Secretary may each year establish priorities for funding from the activities described in § 661.10.
(b) The Secretary announces any priorities in the application notice published in the Federal Register.
