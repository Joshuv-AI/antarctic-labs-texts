20 C.F.R. § 655.47 — Referrals of U.S. workers.
Citation: us:cfr:20:655.47
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-655.47
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 655 (Temporary Employment Of Foreign Workers In The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

SWAs may only refer for employment individuals who have been apprised of all the material terms and conditions of employment and who are qualified and will be available for employment.
