47 C.F.R. § 3.53 — FCC notification of refusal to provide telecommunications service to U.S. registered vessel(s).
Citation: us:cfr:47:3.53
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-3.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 3 (Authorization And Administration Of Accounting Authorities In Maritime And Maritime Mobile-Satellite Radio Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An accounting authority must inform the FCC immediately should it receive notice from any source that a foreign administration or facility is refusing or plans to refuse legitimate public correspondence to or from any U.S. registered vessel.
