40 C.F.R. § 273.1 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:40:273.1
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-273.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 273 (Standards For Universal Waste Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) This part establishes requirements for managing the following:
(1) Batteries as described in 40 CFR 273.2;
(2) Pesticides as described in § 273.3;
(3) Mercury-containing equipment as described in § 273.4;
(4) Lamps as described in § 273.5; and
(5) Aerosol cans as described in § 273.6.
(b) This part provides an alternative set of management standards in lieu of regulation under 40 CFR parts 260 through 272.
