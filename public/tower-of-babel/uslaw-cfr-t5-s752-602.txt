5 C.F.R. § 752.602 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:5:752.602
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-752.602
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 752 (Adverse Actions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In this subpart—
Career appointee, limited term appointee, and limited emergency appointee have the meaning given in 5 U.S.C. 3132(a).
Day means calendar day.
Suspension has the meaning given in 5 U.S.C. 7501(2).
