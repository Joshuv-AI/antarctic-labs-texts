10 U.S.C. § 1074c — Medical care: authority to provide a wig
Citation: us:usc:10:1074c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1074c
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 55 (Medical And Dental Care). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A person entitled to medical care under this chapter who has alopecia resulting from the treatment of a malignant disease may be furnished a wig if the person has not previously been furnished one at the expense of the United States.
