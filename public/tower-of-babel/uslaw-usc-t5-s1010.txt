5 U.S.C. § 1010 — Availability of transcripts

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section1010
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part I (The Agencies Generally); Chapter 10 (Federal Advisory Committees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Definition of Agency Proceeding.—
In this section, the term “agency proceeding” has the meaning given the term in section 551 of this title.
(b) Availability.—
Agencies and advisory committees shall make available to any person, at actual cost of duplication, copies of transcripts of any agency proceeding or advisory committee meeting.
