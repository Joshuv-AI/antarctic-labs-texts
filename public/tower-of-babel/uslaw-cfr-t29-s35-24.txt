29 C.F.R. § 35.24 — Designation of responsible employee.
Citation: us:cfr:29:35.24
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-35.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 35 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Age In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance From The Department Of Labor). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each recipient shall designate at least one employee to coordinate its compliance activities under the Act and this part, including investigation of any complaints that the recipient receives alleging any actions that are prohibited by the Act or this part.
