25 U.S.C. § 1 — Commissioner of Indian Affairs
Citation: us:usc:25:1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section1
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 1 (Bureau Of Indian Affairs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There shall be in the Department of the Interior a Commissioner of Indian Affairs, who shall be appointed by the President, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate.
