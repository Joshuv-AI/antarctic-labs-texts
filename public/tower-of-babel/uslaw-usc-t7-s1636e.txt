7 U.S.C. § 1636e — Voluntary reporting
Citation: us:usc:7:1636e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1636e
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 38 (Distribution And Marketing Of Agricultural Products); Subchapter II (Livestock Mandatory Reporting); Part E (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall encourage voluntary reporting by packers (as defined in section 191 of this title) to which the mandatory reporting requirements of this subchapter do not apply.
