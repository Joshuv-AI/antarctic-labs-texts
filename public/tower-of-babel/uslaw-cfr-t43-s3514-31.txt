43 C.F.R. § 3514.31 — May BLM waive cancellation or forfeiture?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3514.31
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3514.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3500 (Leasing Of Solid Minerals Other Than Coal And Oil Shale). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, but our waiver of any particular cause of forfeiture will not prevent us from canceling and forfeiting the lease for any other cause or for the same cause occurring at any other time.
