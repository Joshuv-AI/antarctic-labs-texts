5 U.S.C. § 6340 — Inapplicability of certain provisions
Citation: us:usc:5:6340
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section6340
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart E (Attendance And Leave); Chapter 63 (Leave); Subchapter III (Voluntary Transfers Of Leave). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except to the extent that the Office of Personnel Management may prescribe regulations, nothing in section 7351 shall apply with respect to a solicitation, donation, or acceptance of leave under this subchapter.
