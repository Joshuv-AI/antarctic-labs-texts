7 U.S.C. § 224 — Attorney General to institute court proceedings for enforcement
Citation: us:usc:7:224
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section224
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 9 (Packers And Stockyards); Subchapter V (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may report any violation of this chapter to the Attorney General of the United States, who shall cause appropriate proceedings to be commenced and prosecuted in the proper courts of the United States without delay.
