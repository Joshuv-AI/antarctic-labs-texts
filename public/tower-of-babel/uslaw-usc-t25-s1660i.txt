25 U.S.C. § 1660i — Deeming an Urban Indian organization and employees thereof to be a part of the public health service for the purposes of certain claims for personal injury
Citation: us:usc:25:1660i
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section1660i
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 18 (Indian Health Care); Subchapter IV (Health Services For Urban Indians). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Section 5321(d) of this title shall apply—
(1)
to an Urban Indian organization to the same extent and in the same manner as such section applies to an Indian tribe, a tribal organization, and an Indian contractor; and
(2)
to the employees of an Urban Indian organization to the same extent and in the same manner as such section applies to employees of an Indian tribe, a tribal organization, or an Indian contractor.
