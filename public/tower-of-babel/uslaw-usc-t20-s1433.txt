20 U.S.C. § 1433 — General authority
Citation: us:usc:20:1433
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1433
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 33 (Education Of Individuals With Disabilities); Subchapter III (Infants And Toddlers With Disabilities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall, in accordance with this subchapter, make grants to States (from their allotments under section 1443 of this title) to assist each State to maintain and implement a statewide, comprehensive, coordinated, multidisciplinary, interagency system to provide early intervention services for infants and toddlers with disabilities and their families.
