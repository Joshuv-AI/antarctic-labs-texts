33 C.F.R. § 164.02 — Applicability exception for foreign vessels.
Citation: us:cfr:33:164.02
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-164.02
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 164 (Navigation Safety Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except for § 164.46(c), none of the requirements of this part apply to foreign vessels that:
(1) Are not destined for, or departing from, a port or place subject to the jurisdiction of the United States; and
(2) Are in:
(i) Innocent passage through the territorial sea of the United States; or
(ii) Transit through navigable waters of the United States which form a part of an international strait.
