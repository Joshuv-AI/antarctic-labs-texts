32 C.F.R. § 21.460 — What is the extent of grants officers' and agreements officers' authority?
Citation: us:cfr:32:21.460
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-21.460
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 21 (DoD GRANTS AND AGREEMENTS—GENERAL MATTERS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Grants officers and agreements officers may bind the Government only to the extent of the authority delegated to them in their written statements of appointment (see § 21.445).
