20 C.F.R. § 228.19 — Reduction for a social security benefit.
Citation: us:cfr:20:228.19
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-228.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 228 (Computation Of Survivor Annuities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The tier I annuity component is reduced for the amount of any social security benefit to which the survivor annuitant is entitled.
