Abstract
. Extending earlier results of the authors on minimal polynomials of p p -elements of finite groups of Lie type in cross-characteristic representations, this paper focuses on the case where Sylow p p -subgroups are cyclic and p p is distinct from the representation field characteristic.

The problem on the minimal polynomial degree of

[

. We state it here for

Hall-Higman Theorem

Let G be a finite p-solvable irreducible linear group over a field of characteristic

g\in G
an element of order

, the degree of the minimal polynomial of g, is at least
(p-1)p^{k-1}

This is an important result which was used in an essential way in the classification of finite simple groups. It has many refinements and is largely discussed in the literature (see for instance
[
48

and
[
25
, Ch. VII, §10]

that a similar result holds for an arbitrary ground field provided the
p
-element
in question does not lie in any abelian normal subgroup of

and

is
p
-solvable
(see
[
25
, Ch. VII, Theorem 10.2]
for a slightly weaker result). The problem of obtaining a sharp lower bound for the minimal polynomial degree of
p
-elements in arbitrary groups attracted a lot of attention too.
The bulk of the problem is to study quasisimple
G
, where groups of Lie type in characteristic
p
are excluded so that one can expect a
lower bound similar to that of Hall and Higman.

The project of extending the Hall–Higman theorem
to arbitrary finite groups was started in
[
79
]
and significantly developed further in
[
16
]
and
[
70
]
.
(If

[

deal with quasi-simple classical groups

in almost all situations.
On many occasions the degree in question is equal to the order of

in

. Moreover, Sylow

-subgroups of

are cyclic in most cases where the degree remains undetermined. The quasisimple groups of Lie type of small rank are considered in

In the current paper we focus on the case where Sylow

-subgroups of

Note that for

Although obtaining Theorem

Lemma 2.2

Lemma 3.1

Let

Let

Lemma 4.1

Lemma 4.2

Lemma 5.4
.

Let E be a group of extraspecial type and A an abelian subgroup of E. Let
\lambda\in\mathop{\rm Irr}\nolimits_{\ell}(E)
. Suppose that
\dim\lambda>1
and

. Then
\lambda|_{A}
is a multiple of
\rho_{A}^{reg}
, the regular representation of A. In particular,

is a constituent of

.

Proof.

Let

be the Brauer character of

. Since

, it follows that

Lemma 5.5
.

Let
G={\rm Spin}^{-}_{8}(q)
,

. Let

Proof.

Suppose the contrary, that

\langle g\rangle

-subgroup of
G

As mentioned in Lemma
5.2
,

Let

be a nontrivial irreducible constituent of

. Then

is of dimension

Note that

has exactly two irreducible Brauer characters of degree

. Let

be a

is not

Let

.

, there is a parabolic subgroup

of

of

For this observe that the action of

(of order

Lemma 6.9
.
