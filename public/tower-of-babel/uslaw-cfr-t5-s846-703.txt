5 C.F.R. § 846.703 — Effective date of FERS coverage.
Citation: us:cfr:5:846.703
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-846.703
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 846 (Federal Employees Retirement System—Elections Of Coverage). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An election under this subpart is effective on the later of—
(a) The first day of the pay period beginning after the date the election and any required supporting documentation is received by the employing office; or
(b) The first day of the pay period beginning after July 1, 1998.
