Radio Broadcasting Services; Enterprise, Utah

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document requests comments on a petition for rule making 
filed SSR Communications, Inc., proposing to amend the Table of FM 
Allotments, by allotting Channel 226C3 at Enterprise, Utah, as the 
community's second local service and first competing FM local service. 
Channel 226C3 can be allotted to Enterprise consistent with the minimum 
distance separation requirements of the Federal Communications 
Commission (Commission) rules, with a site restriction of 20 kilometers 
(12.4 miles) north of the community at reference coordinates 37-45-04 
NL and 113-46-26 WL.

DATES: Comments must be filed on or before October 6, 2025, and reply 
comments on or before October 21, 2025.
