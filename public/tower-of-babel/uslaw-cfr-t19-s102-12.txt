19 C.F.R. § 102.12 — Fungible goods.
Citation: us:cfr:19:102.12
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-102.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 102 (Rules Of Origin). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When fungible goods of different countries of origin are commingled the country of origin of the goods:
(a) Is the countries of origin of those commingled goods; or
(b) If the good is fungible, has been commingled, and direct physical identification of the origin of the commingled good is not practical, the country or countries of origin may be determined on the basis of an inventory management method.
