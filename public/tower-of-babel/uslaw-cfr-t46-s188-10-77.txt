46 C.F.R. § 188.10-77 — -77 Vessel.
Citation: us:cfr:46:188.10-77
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-188.10-77
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 188 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where the word “vessel” is used in this subchapter, it will be considered to include all inspected and certificated oceanographic research vessels as listed in column 6 of table 2.01-7(a) in § 2.01-7(a) of this chapter.
