36 C.F.R. § 1235.10 — What records do agencies transfer to the National Archives of the United States?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1235.10
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1235.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1235 (Transfer Of Records To The National Archives Of The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies must transfer to the National Archives of the United States records that have been scheduled as permanent on an SF 115, Request for Records Disposition Authority, records that are designated as permanent in a GRS; and, when appropriate, records that are accretions to holdings (continuations of series already accessioned.)
