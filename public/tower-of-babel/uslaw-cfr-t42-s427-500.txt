42 C.F.R. § 427.500 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:42:427.500
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-427.500
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 427 (Medicare Part B Drug Inflation Rebate Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in this subpart, date of receipt is the calendar day following the day on which a report of a rebate amount (as set forth in §§ 427.501(b) through (d) and 427.502(b) and (c) is made available to the manufacturer of a Part B rebatable drug by CMS.
