32 C.F.R. § 1633.3 — Submission of claims.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1633.3
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1633.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1633 (Administration Of Classification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise expressly provided by the Director, no document relating to any registrant's claims or potential claims will be retained by the Selective Service System and no file relating to a registrant's possible classification status will be established prior to that registrant being ordered to report for induction.
