21 C.F.R. § 369.9 — General warnings re accidental ingestion by children.
Citation: us:cfr:21:369.9
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-369.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 369 (Interpretative Statements Re Warnings On Drugs And Devices For Over-The-Counter Sale). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 369.20 includes under certain items, but not all medicines, the statement: “Keep this and all medicines out of children's reach. In case of overdose, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away,” or “Keep out of reach of children.” However, in view of the possibility of accidental ingestion of drugs, it is not only suggested but is recommended that one of these statements be used on the label of all drug products.
