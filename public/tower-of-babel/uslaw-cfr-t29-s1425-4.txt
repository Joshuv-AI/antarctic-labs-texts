29 C.F.R. § 1425.4 — Duty of parties.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1425.4
Creator: Federal Mediation And Conciliation Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1425.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1425 (Mediation Assistance In The Federal Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It shall be the duty of the parties to participate fully and promptly in any meetings arranged by the Service for the purpose of assisting in the settlement of a negotiation dispute.
