12 C.F.R. § 705.6 — Terms and conditions for technical assistance grants.
Citation: us:cfr:12:705.6
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-705.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 705 (Community Development Revolving Loan Fund Access For Credit Unions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Participating Credit Unions must comply with the terms and conditions for technical assistance grants specified for each funding opportunity offered under a Notice of Funding Opportunity.
(b) NCUA will establish applicable funding limits for technical assistance grants in the Notice of Funding Opportunity.
