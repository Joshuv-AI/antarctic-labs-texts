5 C.F.R. § 894.304 — Am I eligible to enroll if I'm retired or receiving workers' compensation?
Citation: us:cfr:5:894.304
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-894.304
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 894 (Federal Employees Dental And Vision Insurance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you are retired, receiving workers' compensation, or are a survivor annuitant, you are eligible if you meet the definition of annuitant in 5 U.S.C. 8901(3).
