22 U.S.C. § 8566 — Exception relating to importation of goods
Citation: us:usc:22:8566
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section8566
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 92 (Comprehensive Iran Sanctions, Accountability, And Divestment); Subchapter V (Masih Alinejad Harassment And Unlawful Targeting). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
Notwithstanding any other provision of this subchapter, the authorities and requirements to impose sanctions under this subchapter shall not include the authority or a requirement to impose sanctions on the importation of goods.
(b) Good defined
In this section, the term “good” means any article, natural or manmade substance, material, supply or manufactured product, including inspection and test equipment, and excluding technical data.
