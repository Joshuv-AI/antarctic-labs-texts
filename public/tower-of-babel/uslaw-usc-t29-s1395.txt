29 U.S.C. § 1395 — Plan notification to corporation of potentially significant withdrawals
Citation: us:usc:29:1395
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section1395
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 18 (Employee Retirement Income Security Program); Subchapter III (Plan Termination Insurance); Part 1 (Employer Withdrawals). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation may, by regulation, require the plan sponsor of a multiemployer plan to provide notice to the corporation when the withdrawal from the plan by any employer has resulted, or will result, in a significant reduction in the amount of aggregate contributions under the plan made by employers.
