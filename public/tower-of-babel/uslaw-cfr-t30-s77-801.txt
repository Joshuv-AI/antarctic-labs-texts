30 C.F.R. § 77.801 — Grounding resistors.
Citation: us:cfr:30:77.801
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-77.801
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 77 (Mandatory Safety Standards, Surface Coal Mines And Surface Work Areas Of Underground Coal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The grounding resistor, where required, shall be of the proper ohmic value to limit the voltage drop in the grounding circuit external to the resistor to not more than 100 volts under fault conditions. The grounding resistor shall be rated for maximum fault current continuously and insulated from ground for a voltage equal to the phase-to-phase voltage of the system.
