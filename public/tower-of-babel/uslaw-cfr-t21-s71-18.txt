21 C.F.R. § 71.18 — Petition for exemption from certification.
Citation: us:cfr:21:71.18
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-71.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 71 (Color Additive Petitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A manufacturer, packer, or distributor of a color additive or color additive mixture may petition for an exemption from certification pursuant to part 10 of this chapter. Any such petition shall show why such certification is not necessary for the protection of public health.
