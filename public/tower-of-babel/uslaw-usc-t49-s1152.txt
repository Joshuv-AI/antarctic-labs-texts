49 U.S.C. § 1152 — Joinder and intervention in aviation proceedings
Citation: us:usc:49:1152
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section1152
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Chapter 11 (National Transportation Safety Board); Subchapter IV (Enforcement And Penalties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A person interested in or affected by a matter under consideration in a proceeding or a civil action to enforce section 1132, 1134(b) or (f)(1) (related to an aircraft accident), or 1155(a) of this title, or a regulation prescribed or order issued under any of those sections, may be joined as a party or permitted to intervene in the proceeding or civil action.
