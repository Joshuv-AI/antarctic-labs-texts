19 U.S.C. § 4643 — Enforcement priorities
Citation: us:usc:19:4643
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section4643
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 29 (United States–Mexico–Canada Agreement Implementation); Subchapter VI (Labor Monitoring And Enforcement); Part A (Interagency Labor Committee For Monitoring And Enforcement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Interagency Labor Committee shall—
(1)
review the list of priority sectors under Annex 31–A of the USMCA and suggest to USTR additional sectors for review by the USMCA countries as appropriate;
(2)
establish and annually update a list of priority subsectors within such priority sectors to be the focus of the enforcement efforts of the Committee, the first of which shall consist of—
(A)
auto assembly;
(B)
auto parts;
(C)
aerospace;
(D)
industrial bakeries;
(E)
electronics;
(F)
call centers;
(G)
mining; and
(H)
steel and aluminum; and
(3)
review priority facilities within such priority subsectors for monitoring and enforcement.
