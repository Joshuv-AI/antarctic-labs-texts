49 C.F.R. § 1108.13 — Additional parties per side.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1108.13
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1108.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1108 (Arbitration Of Certain Disputes Subject To The Statutory Jurisdiction Of The Surface Transportation Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where an arbitration complaint is filed by more than one complainant in a particular arbitration proceeding against, or is answered or counterclaimed by, more than one respondent, these arbitration rules will apply to the complainants as a group and the respondents as a group in the same manner as they will apply to individual opposing parties.
