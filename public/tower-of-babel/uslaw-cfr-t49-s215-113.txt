49 C.F.R. § 215.113 — Defective plain bearing wedge.
Citation: us:cfr:49:215.113
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-215.113
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 215 (Railroad Freight Car Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A railroad may not place or continue in service a car, if a plain bearing wedge on that car is—
(a) Missing;
(b) Cracked;
(c) Broken; or
(d) Not located in its design position.
