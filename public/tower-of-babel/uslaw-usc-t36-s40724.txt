36 U.S.C. § 40724 — Priority of youth participation
Citation: us:usc:36:40724
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section40724
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 407 (Corporation For The Promotion Of Rifle Practice And Firearms Safety); Subchapter II (Civilian Marksmanship Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In carrying out the Civilian Marksmanship Program, the corporation shall give priority to activities that benefit firearms safety, training, and competition for youth and that reach as many youth participants as possible.
