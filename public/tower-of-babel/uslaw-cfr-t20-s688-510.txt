20 C.F.R. § 688.510 — How may grantees provide services under the YouthBuild program?
Citation: us:cfr:20:688.510
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-688.510
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 688 (Provisions Governing The Youthbuild Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each recipient of a grant under the YouthBuild program may provide the services and activities described in these regulations either directly or through subgrants, contracts, or other arrangements with local educational agencies, postsecondary educational institutions, State or local housing development agencies, other public agencies, including agencies of Indian tribes, or private organizations.
