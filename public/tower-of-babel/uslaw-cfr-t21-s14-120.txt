21 C.F.R. § 14.120 — Establishment of the Technical Electronic Product Radiation Safety Standards Committee (TEPRSSC).
Citation: us:cfr:21:14.120
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-14.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 14 (Public Hearing Before A Public Advisory Committee). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Technical Electronic Product Radiation Safety Standards Committee (TEPRSSC), consisting of 15 members, is established in accordance with the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (21 U.S.C. 360kk(f)(1)(A)) to provide consultation before the Commissioner prescribes any performance standard for an electronic product.
