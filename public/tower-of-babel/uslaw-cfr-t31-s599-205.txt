31 C.F.R. § 599.205 — Exempt transactions.
Citation: us:cfr:31:599.205
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-599.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 599 (Illicit Drug Trade Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Personal communications. The prohibitions contained in this part do not apply to any postal, telegraphic, telephonic, or other personal communication that does not involve the transfer of anything of value.
