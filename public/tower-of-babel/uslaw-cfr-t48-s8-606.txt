48 C.F.R. § 8.606 — 8.606 Evaluating FPI performance.
Citation: us:cfr:48:8.606
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-8.606
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 8 (Required Sources Of Supplies And Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies shall evaluate FPI contract performance in accordance with subpart 42.15. Performance evaluations do not negate the requirements of 8.602 and 8.604, but they may be used to support a waiver request in accordance with 8.604.
