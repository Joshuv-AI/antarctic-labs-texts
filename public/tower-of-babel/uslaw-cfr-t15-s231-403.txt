15 C.F.R. § 231.403 — Severability.
Citation: us:cfr:15:231.403
Creator: National Institute Of Standards And Technology, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-231.403
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 231 (Clawbacks Of Chips Funding). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If any provision of this part or its application to any person, act, or practice is held invalid, the remainder of the part or the application of its provisions to any person, act, or practice shall not be affected thereby.
