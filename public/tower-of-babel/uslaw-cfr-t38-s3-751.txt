38 C.F.R. § 3.751 — Statutory awards; retired service personnel.
Citation: us:cfr:38:3.751
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-3.751
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 3 (Adjudication). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Retired Regular and Reserve officers and enlisted personnel are not entitled to statutory awards of disability compensation from the Department of Veterans Affairs in addition to their retirement pay. However, under § 3.750(c), eligible persons may waive an amount equal to the basic disability compensation and any statutory award otherwise payable by the Department of Veterans Affairs.
