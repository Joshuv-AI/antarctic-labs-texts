45 C.F.R. § 510.2 — Time for filing.
Citation: us:cfr:45:510.2
Creator: Foreign Claims Settlement Commission Of The United States, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-510.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 510 (Filing Of Claims And Procedures Therefor). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Claims for payments under the Guam World War II Loyalty Recognition Act, Title XVII, Public Law 114-328 (the “Act”), must be filed not later than one year after the date on which the Commission publishes the notice described in section 1705(b)(2)(B) of the Act.
