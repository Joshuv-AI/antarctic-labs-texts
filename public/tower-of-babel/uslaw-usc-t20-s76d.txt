20 U.S.C. § 76d — Donations of works of art from Government agencies
Citation: us:usc:20:76d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section76d
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 3 (Smithsonian Institution, National Museums And Art Galleries); Subchapter IV (Smithsonian Gallery Of Art). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator of General Services and other agencies of the Government are authorized to donate to the Gallery any works of art now or hereafter under their control.
