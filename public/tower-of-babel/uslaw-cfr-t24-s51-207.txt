24 C.F.R. § 51.207 — Special circumstances.
Citation: us:cfr:24:51.207
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-51.207
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 51 (Environmental Criteria And Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary or the Secretary's designee may, on a case-by-case basis, when circumstances warrant, require the application of this subpart C with respect to a substance not listed in appendix I to this subpart C that would create thermal or overpressure effect in excess of that listed in § 51.203.
