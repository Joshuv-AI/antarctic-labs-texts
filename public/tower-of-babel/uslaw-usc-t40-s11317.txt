40 U.S.C. § 11317 — Significant deviations
Citation: us:usc:40:11317
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section11317
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Chapter 113 (Responsibility For Acquisitions Of Information Technology); Subchapter II (Executive Agencies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The head of each executive agency shall identify in the strategic information resources management plan required under section 3506(b)(2) of title 44 any major information technology acquisition program, or any phase or increment of that program, that has significantly deviated from the cost, performance, or schedule goals established for the program.
