37 C.F.R. § 7.29 — Effect of replacement on U.S. registration.
Citation: us:cfr:37:7.29
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-7.29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 7 (Rules Of Practice In Filings Pursuant To The Protocol Relating To The Madrid Agreement Concerning The International Registration Of Marks). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A U.S. registration that has been replaced by a registered extension of protection under section 74 of the Act and § 7.28 will remain in force, unless cancelled, expired or surrendered, as long as:
(a) The owner of the replaced U.S. registration continues to file affidavits or declarations of use in commerce or excusable nonuse under section 8 of the Act; and
(b) The replaced U.S. registration is renewed under section 9 of the Act.
