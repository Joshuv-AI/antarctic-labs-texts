32 C.F.R. § 2700.34 — Downgrading authority.
Citation: us:cfr:32:2700.34
Creator: Office For Micronesian Status Negotiations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-2700.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 2700 (Security Information Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Security Officer, OMSN is authorized to downgrade OMSN originated documents after consultation with the staff member who is charged with functional responsibility for the subject matter under question.
