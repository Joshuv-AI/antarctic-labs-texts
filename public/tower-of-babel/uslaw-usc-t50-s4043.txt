50 U.S.C. § 4043 — Preservation of remedies
Citation: us:usc:50:4043
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section4043
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 50 (Servicemembers Civil Relief); Subchapter VIII (Civil Liability). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in section 4041 or 4042 of this title shall be construed to preclude or limit any remedy otherwise available under other law, including consequential and punitive damages.
