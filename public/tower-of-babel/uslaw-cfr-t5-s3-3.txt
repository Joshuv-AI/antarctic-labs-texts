5 C.F.R. § 3.3 — Conversion of appointments.
Citation: us:cfr:5:3.3
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-3.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 3 (Noncompetitive Acquisition Of Status (Rule Iii)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person who acquires a competitive status under this part shall have his appointment converted to career-conditional appointment unless he meets the service requirement for career appointment prescribed under § 2.2(a) of this subchapter.
