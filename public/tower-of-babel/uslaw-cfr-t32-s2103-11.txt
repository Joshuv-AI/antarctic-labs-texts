32 C.F.R. § 2103.11 — Basic policy.
Citation: us:cfr:32:2103.11
Creator: National Security Council
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-2103.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 2103 (Regulations To Implement E.O. 12065—Including Procedures For Public Access To Documents That May Be Declassified). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is the policy of the National Security Council Staff to make available to the public as much information concerning its activities as is possible, consistent with its responsibility to protect the national security.
