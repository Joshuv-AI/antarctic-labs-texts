25 C.F.R. § 214.12 — Time of payment of royalties.
Citation: us:cfr:25:214.12
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-214.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 214 (Leasing Of Osage Reservation Lands, Oklahoma, For Mining, Except Oil And Gas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Royalties on all minerals produced in any quarter (January-March, April-June, July-September, October-December) shall be paid on or before the 25th day of the month next succeeding, and the remittance shaIl be accompanied by sworn reports covering all operations, whether there has been production or not. Annual advance rentals shall be paid within 10 days after the beginning of the lease year.
