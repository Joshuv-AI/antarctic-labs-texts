25 U.S.C. § 3055 — Forest products for traditional and cultural purposes
Citation: us:usc:25:3055
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section3055
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 32A (Cultural And Heritage Cooperation Authority). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
Notwithstanding section 472a of title 16, the Secretary may provide free of charge to Indian tribes any trees, portions of trees, or forest products from National Forest System land for traditional and cultural purposes.
(b) Prohibition
Trees, portions of trees, or forest products provided under subsection (a) may not be used for commercial purposes.
