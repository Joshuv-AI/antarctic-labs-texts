46 C.F.R. § 32.35-5 — -5 Installation of internal combustion engines—TB/ALL.
Citation: us:cfr:46:32.35-5
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-32.35-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 32 (Special Equipment, Machinery, And Hull Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each internal combustion engine located on the weather deck shall be provided with a ventilated metal hood or, where space permits, with a well-ventilated metal housing of sufficient size to allow for proper operation and maintenance.
