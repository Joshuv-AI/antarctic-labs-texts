7 U.S.C. § 6111 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:7:6111
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section6111
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 90 (Mushroom Promotion, Research, And Consumer Information). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
There are authorized to be appropriated for each fiscal year such sums as are necessary to carry out this chapter.
(b) Administrative expenses
The funds so appropriated shall not be available for payment of the expenses or expenditures of the Council in administering any provision of an order issued under this chapter.
