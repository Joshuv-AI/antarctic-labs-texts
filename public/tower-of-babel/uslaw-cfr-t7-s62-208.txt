7 C.F.R. § 62.208 — Publication of assessment status.
Citation: us:cfr:7:62.208
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-62.208
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 62 (Agricultural Marketing Service Audit Verification And Accreditation Programs (Avaap)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Approved programs shall be posted for public reference on: https://www.ams.usda.gov. Such postings shall include:
(a) Program name and contact information;
(b) Products or services covered under the scope of approval;
(c) Effective dates of approval;
(d) Control numbers of official assessments, as appropriate; and
(e) Any other information deemed necessary by the Administrator.
