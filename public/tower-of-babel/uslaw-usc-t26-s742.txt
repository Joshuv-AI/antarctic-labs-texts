26 U.S.C. § 742 — Basis of transferee partner’s interest
Citation: us:usc:26:742
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section742
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 1 (Normal Taxes And Surtaxes); Subchapter K (Partners And Partnerships); Part II (Contributions, Distributions, And Transfers); Subpart C (Transfers Of Interests In A Partnership). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The basis of an interest in a partnership acquired other than by contribution shall be determined under part II of subchapter O (sec. 1011 and following).
