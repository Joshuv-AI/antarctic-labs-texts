43 U.S.C. § 485j — Effect on existing laws
Citation: us:usc:43:485j
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section485j
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 12 (Reclamation And Irrigation Of Lands By Federal Government); Subchapter X (Payment Of Construction Charges). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of previous Acts of Congress not inconsistent with the provisions of this subchapter shall remain in full force and effect.
