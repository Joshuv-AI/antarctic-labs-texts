19 C.F.R. § 4.68 — Federal Maritime Commission certificates for certain passenger vessels.
Citation: us:cfr:19:4.68
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-4.68
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 4 (Vessels In Foreign And Domestic Trades). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No vessel having berth or stateroom accommodations for 50 or more passengers and embarking passengers at U.S. ports will be granted a clearance at the port or place of departure from the United States unless it is established that the vessel has valid certificates issued by the Federal Maritime Commission.
