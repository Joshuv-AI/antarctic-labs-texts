42 C.F.R. § 405.2102 — Definitions.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 405 (Federal Health Insurance For The Aged And Disabled). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in this subpart, the following definitions apply:
Network, ESRD. All Medicare-approved ESRD facilities in a designated geographic area specified by CMS.
Network organization. The administrative governing body to the network and liaison to the Federal government.
