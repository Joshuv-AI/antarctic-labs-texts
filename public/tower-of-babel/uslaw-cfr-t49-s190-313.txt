49 C.F.R. § 190.313 — Initiation of rulemaking.
Citation: us:cfr:49:190.313
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-190.313
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 190 (Pipeline Safety Enforcement And Regulatory Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator initiates rulemaking on his or her own motion; however, in so doing, the Administrator may use discretion to consider the recommendations of other agencies of the United States or of other interested persons including those of any technical advisory body established by statute for that purpose.
