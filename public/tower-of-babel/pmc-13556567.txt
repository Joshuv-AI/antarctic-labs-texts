477. Antidepressant action of short-acting psychedelics
Authors: J Ramaekers
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
AbstractBackgroundShort-acting psychedelics such as 5-MeO-DMT and DMT may induce rapid relief of depressive symptoms after brief but intense psychedelic experiences.Aims & ObjectivesThis presentation examines their clinical pharmacology and therapeutic potential in depression.MethodReview of clinical data.ResultsBoth compounds primarily target serotonergic receptors, notably 5-HT1A and 5-HT2A. Early small-scale trials indicate that short interventions (15–30 minutes) are safe, well tolerated, and can yield significant antidepressant effects within 24 hours. Although long-term data are limited, findings suggest sustained benefits.Discussion & ConclusionsAdvantages include single-day dosing, reduced reliance on integrative therapy, and straightforward clinical implementation. Key challenges remain, including determining effect duration, maintaining treatment response, optimizing delivery, and clarifying mechanisms of action. Broader acceptance will require large, randomized, placebo-controlled trials demonstrating replicable and durable antidepressant efficacy.
