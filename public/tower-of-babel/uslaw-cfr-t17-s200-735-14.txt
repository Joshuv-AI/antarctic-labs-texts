17 C.F.R. § 200.735-14 — -14 Employees on leave of absence.
Citation: us:cfr:17:200.735-14
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-200.735-14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 200 (Organization; Conduct And Ethics; And Information And Requests). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of the rules in this subpart relative to employees of the Commission are applicable to employees on a leave with pay or a leave without pay status other than extended military service.
