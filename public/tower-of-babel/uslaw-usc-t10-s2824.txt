10 U.S.C. § 2824 — Authorization for acquisition of existing family housing in lieu of construction
Citation: us:usc:10:2824
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2824
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Property); Chapter 169 (Military Construction And Military Family Housing); Subchapter II (Military Family Housing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
In lieu of constructing any family housing units authorized by law to be constructed, the Secretary concerned may acquire sole interest in existing family housing units that are privately owned or that are held by the Department of Housing and Urban Development, except that in foreign countries the Secretary concerned may acquire less than sole interest in existing family housing units.
(b)
When authority provided by law to construct military family housing units is used to acquire existing family housing units under subsection (a), the authority includes authority to acquire interests in land.
(c)
The net floor area of a family housing unit acquired under the authority of this section may not exceed the applicable limitation specified in section 2826 of this title. The Secretary concerned may waive the limitation set forth in the preceding sentence to family housing units acquired under this section during the five-year period beginning on February 10, 1996.
(d)
Family housing units may not be acquired under this section through the exercise of eminent domain authority.
