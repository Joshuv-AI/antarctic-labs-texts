32 C.F.R. § 1900.45 — Notification of decision and right of judicial review.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1900.45
Creator: Central Intelligence Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1900.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1900 (Public Access To Cia Records Under The Freedom Of Information Act (Foia)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Executive Secretary of the ARP shall promptly prepare and communicate the final Agency decision to the requester. With respect to any adverse Agency determination, that correspondence shall state the reasons for the decision, and include a notice of a right to judicial review.
