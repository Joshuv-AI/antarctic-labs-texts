48 C.F.R. § 15.403-2 — 15.403-2 Other circumstances where certified cost or pricing data are not required.
Citation: us:cfr:48:15.403-2
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-15.403-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 15 (Contracting By Negotiation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The exercise of an option at the price established at contract award or initial negotiation does not require submission of certified cost or pricing data.
(b) Certified cost or pricing data are not required for proposals used solely for overrun funding or interim billing price adjustments.
