14 C.F.R. § 298.12 — Duration of exemption.
Citation: us:cfr:14:298.12
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-298.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 298 (Exemptions For Air Taxi And Commuter Air Carrier Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The exemption from any provision of 49 U.S.C. Subtitle VII provided by this part shall continue in effect only until such time as the Department shall find that enforcement of that provision would be in the public interest, at which time the exemption shall terminate or be conditioned with respect to the person, class of persons, or service (e.g., limited-entry foreign air transportation market) subject to the finding.
