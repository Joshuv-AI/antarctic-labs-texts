20 C.F.R. § 25.100 — What general provisions does OWCP apply to the Special Schedule?
Citation: us:cfr:20:25.100
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-25.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 25 (Compensation For Disability And Death Of Noncitizen Federal Employees Outside The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The definitions of terms in the FECA, as amended, shall apply to terms used in this subpart.
(b) The provisions of the FECA, unless modified by this subpart or otherwise inapplicable, shall be applied whenever possible in the application of this subpart.
(c) The provisions of the regulations for the administration of the FECA, as amended or supplemented from time to time by instructions applicable to this subpart, shall apply in the administration of compensation under this subpart, whenever they can reasonably be applied.
