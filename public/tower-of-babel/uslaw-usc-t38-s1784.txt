38 U.S.C. § 1784 — Humanitarian care
Citation: us:usc:38:1784
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section1784
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 17 (Hospital, Nursing Home, Domiciliary, And Medical Care); Subchapter VIII (Health Care Of Persons Other Than Veterans). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may furnish hospital care or medical services as a humanitarian service in emergency cases, but the Secretary shall charge for such care and services at rates prescribed by the Secretary.
