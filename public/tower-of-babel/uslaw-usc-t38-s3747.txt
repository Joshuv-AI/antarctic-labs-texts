38 U.S.C. § 3747 — Eligible financial institutions
Citation: us:usc:38:3747
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section3747
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part III (Readjustment And Related Benefits); Chapter 37 (Housing And Small Business Loans); Subchapter IV (Small Business Loans). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may not guarantee under this subchapter a loan made by an entity not subject to examination and supervision by an agency of the United States or of a State.
