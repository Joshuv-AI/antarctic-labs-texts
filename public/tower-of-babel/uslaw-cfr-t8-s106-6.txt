8 C.F.R. § 106.6 — DHS severability.
Citation: us:cfr:8:106.6
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-106.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 106 (Uscis Fee Schedule). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this part are separate and severable from one another. If any provision is stayed or determined to be invalid, or held unenforceable as to any person or circumstance, the remaining provisions and applications will continue in effect.
