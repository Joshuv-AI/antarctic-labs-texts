28 U.S.C. § 998 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:28:998
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section998
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part III (Court Officers And Employees); Chapter 58 (United States Sentencing Commission). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this chapter—
(a)
“Commission” means the United States Sentencing Commission;
(b)
“Commissioner” means a member of the United States Sentencing Commission;
(c)
“guidelines” means the guidelines promulgated by the Commission pursuant to section 994(a) of this title; and
(d)
“rules and regulations” means rules and regulations promulgated by the Commission pursuant to section 995 of this title.
