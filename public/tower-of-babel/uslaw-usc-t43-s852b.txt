43 U.S.C. § 852b — Survey of lands prior to transfer; time for survey; availability of funds; lands suitable for transfer
Citation: us:usc:43:852b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section852b
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 20 (Reservations And Grants To States For Public Purposes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Prior to issuance of an instrument of transfer, lands must be surveyed. The Secretary of the Interior shall within five years, subject to the availability of funds, survey the exterior boundaries of lands approved as suitable for transfer to the State.
