9 U.S.C. § 201 — Enforcement of Convention
Citation: us:usc:9:201
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title9-section201
Context: United States Code, Title 9 (Arbitration); Chapter 2 (Convention On The Recognition And Enforcement Of Foreign Arbitral Awards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Convention on the Recognition and Enforcement of Foreign Arbitral Awards of June 10, 1958, shall be enforced in United States courts in accordance with this chapter.
