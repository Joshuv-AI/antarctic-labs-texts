41 C.F.R. § 105-67.100 — -67.100 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-67.100
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-67.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-67 (67—Sale Of Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes policies and procedures governing the debarment or suspension of contractors from purchases of Federal personal property (see FPMR part 101-45).
