40 C.F.R. § 310.8 — Can EPA reimburse the entire cost of my response?
Citation: us:cfr:40:310.8
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-310.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 310 (Reimbursement To Local Governments For Emergency Response To Hazardous Substance Releases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Possibly not. EPA can only reimburse you for temporary emergency measures you take in response to releases of hazardous substances, pollutants, or contaminants. The statute allows reimbursement for only certain costs, and by statute, the total amount of the reimbursement may not exceed $25,000 for a single response.
