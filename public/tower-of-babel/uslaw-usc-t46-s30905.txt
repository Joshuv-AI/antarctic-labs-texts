46 U.S.C. § 30905 — Period for bringing action
Citation: us:usc:46:30905
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section30905
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 309 (Suits In Admiralty Against The United States). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A civil action under this chapter must be brought within 2 years after the cause of action arose.
