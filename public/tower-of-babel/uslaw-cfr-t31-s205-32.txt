31 C.F.R. § 205.32 — What Federal assistance programs are subject to this subpart B?
Citation: us:cfr:31:205.32
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-205.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 205 (Rules And Procedures For Efficient Federal-State Funds Transfers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart B applies to all Federal assistance programs listed in the Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance that are not subject to subpart A of this part.
