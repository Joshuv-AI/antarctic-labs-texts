15 U.S.C. § 795 — Report to Congress by January 31, 1975
Citation: us:usc:15:795
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section795
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 16C (Energy Supply And Environmental Coordination). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency shall report to Congress not later than January 31, 1975, on the implementation of sections 3 through 7 of the Energy Supply and Environmental Coordination Act of 1974.
