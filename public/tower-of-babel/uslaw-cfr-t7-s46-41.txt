7 C.F.R. § 46.41 — Licensee's responsibility for acts of employees and agents.
Citation: us:cfr:7:46.41
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-46.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 46 (Requirements (Other Than Administrative Procedures) Under The Perishable Agricultural Commodities Act, 1930). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In construing and enforcing the provisions of the Act and the regulations in this part, the act, omission, or failure of any agent, officer, or other person acting for or employed by a licensee, within the scope of his employment or office, shall in every case be deemed the Act, omission, or failure of the licensee.
