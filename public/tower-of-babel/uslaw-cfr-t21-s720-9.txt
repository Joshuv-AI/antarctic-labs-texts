21 C.F.R. § 720.9 — Misbranding by reference to filing or to statement number.
Citation: us:cfr:21:720.9
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-720.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 720 (Voluntary Filing Of Cosmetic Product Ingredient Composition Statements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The filing of Form FDA 2512 or assignment of a number to the statement does not in any way denote approval by the Food and Drug Administration of the firm or the product. Any representation in labeling or advertising that creates an impression of official approval because of such filing or such number will be considered misleading.
