13 C.F.R. § 126.205 — May participants in other SBA programs be certified as HUBZone small business concerns?
Citation: us:cfr:13:126.205
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-126.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 126 (Hubzone Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Participants in other SBA programs may be certified as HUBZone small business concerns if they meet all of the requirements set forth in this part.
