46 C.F.R. § 404.108 — Ratemaking step 8: Calculate revised base rates.
Citation: us:cfr:46:404.108
Creator: Coast Guard (Great Lakes Pilotage), Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-404.108
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 404 (Great Lakes Pilotage Ratemaking). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director calculates revised base rates for each area by dividing the initial base rate from § 404.106 (step 6) by the average weighting factor from § 404.107 (step 7) to produce a revised base rate for each area.
