15 C.F.R. § 265.21 — Improper use of roads as thoroughfares.
Citation: us:cfr:15:265.21
Creator: National Institute Of Standards And Technology, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-265.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 265 (Regulations Governing Traffic And Conduct On The Grounds Of The National Institute Of Standards & Technology, Gaithersburg, Maryland, And Boulder And Fort Collins, Colorado). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise provided herein, no person shall drive a motor vehicle or bicycle onto the site for the sole purpose of using the roads of the site as a thoroughfare between roads bordering the site. This section shall not apply to bicyclists using officially approved bike paths on the site.
