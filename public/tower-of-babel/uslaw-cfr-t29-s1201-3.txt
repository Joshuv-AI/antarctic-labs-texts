29 C.F.R. § 1201.3 — Determination as to electric lines.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1201.3
Creator: National Mediation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1201.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1201 (Definitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Interstate Commerce Commission is hereby authorized and directed upon request of the Mediation Board or upon complaint of any part interested to determine after hearing whether any line operated by electric power falls within the terms of this part.
