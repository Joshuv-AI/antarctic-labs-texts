46 U.S.C. § 12306 — Safety certificates
Citation: us:usc:46:12306
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section12306
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part H (Identification Of Vessels); Chapter 123 (Numbering Undocumented Vessels). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When a State is the authority issuing a number under this chapter, it may require that the individual in charge of a numbered vessel have a valid safety certificate issued under conditions set by the issuing authority, except when the vessel is subject to manning requirements under part F of this subtitle.
