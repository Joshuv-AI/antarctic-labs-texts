45 C.F.R. § 81.11 — Appearance.
Citation: us:cfr:45:81.11
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-81.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 81 (Practice And Procedure For Hearings Under Part 80 Of This Title). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A party may appear in person or by counsel and participate fully in any proceeding. A State agency or a corporation may appear by any of its officers or by any employee it authorizes to appear on its behalf. Counsel must be members in good standing of the bar of a State, Territory, or possession of the United States or of the District of Columbia or the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico.
