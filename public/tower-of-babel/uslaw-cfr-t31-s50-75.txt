31 C.F.R. § 50.75 — Determination of affiliations.
Citation: us:cfr:31:50.75
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-50.75
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 50 (Terrorism Risk Insurance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the purposes of this subpart, an insurer's affiliates for any calendar year shall be determined by the circumstances existing on the date of the act which is the Program Trigger Event for that calendar year.
