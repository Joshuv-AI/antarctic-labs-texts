9 C.F.R. § 320.4 — Access to and inspection of records, facilities and inventory; copying and sampling.
Citation: us:cfr:9:320.4
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-320.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 320 (Records, Registration, And Reports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Representatives of the Secretary afforded access to a business specified in § 320.1 of this part (see § 300.6(b)(2) of this chapter) also must be afforded any necessary facilities (other than reproduction equipment) for the examination and copying of records and for the examination and sampling of inventory.
