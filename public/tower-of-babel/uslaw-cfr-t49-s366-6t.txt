49 C.F.R. § 366.6T — T Cancellation or change.
Citation: us:cfr:49:366.6T
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-366.6T
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 366 (Designation Of Process Agent). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A designation may be canceled or changed only by a new designation except that, where a carrier or broker ceases to be subject to § 366.4T in whole or in part for 1 year, designation is no longer required and may be canceled without making another designation.
