30 C.F.R. § 872.20 — What will OSM do with unappropriated AML funds currently allocated to the Rural Abandoned Mine Program ?
Citation: us:cfr:30:872.20
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-872.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 872 (Moneys Available To Eligible States And Indian Tribes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Under section 402(h)(4)(B) of SMCRA, we will make available any moneys that remain allocated to RAMP and that were not appropriated or moved to other allocations before December 20, 2006, for possible transfer to the three United Mine Workers of America (UMWA) health care plans described in section 402(h)(2) of SMCRA.
