17 C.F.R. § 38.850 — Core Principle 16.
Citation: us:cfr:17:38.850
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-38.850
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 38 (Designated Contract Markets). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The board of trade shall establish and enforce rules:
(a) To minimize conflicts of interest in the decision-making process of the contract market; and
(b) To establish a process for resolving conflicts of interest described in paragraph (a) of this section.
