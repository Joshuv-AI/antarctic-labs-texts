16 C.F.R. § 1209.40 — Certification responsibility, multiple parties.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1209.40
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1209.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1209 (Interim Safety Standard For Cellulose Insulation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If there is more than one party (i.e., manufacturer, private labeler, or importer) otherwise subject to the requirements of this subpart B of part 1209 for certain cellulose insulation, only the party closest to the consumer in the distribution chain is required to issue a certificate.
