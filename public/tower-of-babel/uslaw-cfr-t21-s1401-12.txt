21 C.F.R. § 1401.12 — Fees to be charged—miscellaneous provisions.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1401.12
Creator: Office Of National Drug Control Policy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1401.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1401 (Public Availability Of Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Payment for FOIA services may be made by check or money order made payable to the Treasury of the United States.
(b) ONDCP may require advance payment where the estimated fee exceeds $250, or a requester previously failed to pay within 30 days of the billing date.
(c) ONDCP may assess interest charges beginning the 31st day of billing. Interest will be at the rate prescribed in section 3717 of title 31 of the United States Code and will accrue from the date of the billing.
(d) ONDCP may assess search charges where records are not located or where records are exempt from disclosure.
(e) ONDCP may aggregate individual requests for fee purposes in accordance with 1401.16.
