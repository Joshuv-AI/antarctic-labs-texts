7 U.S.C. § 1517 — Separability
Citation: us:usc:7:1517
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1517
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 36 (Crop Insurance); Subchapter I (Federal Crop Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The sections of this subchapter and subdivisions of sections are declared to be separable, and in the event any one or more sections or parts of the same of this subchapter be held to be unconstitutional, the same shall not affect the validity of other sections or parts of sections of this subchapter.
