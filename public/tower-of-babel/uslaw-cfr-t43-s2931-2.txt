43 C.F.R. § 2931.2 — What kinds of permits does BLM issue for recreation-related uses of public lands?
Citation: us:cfr:43:2931.2
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2931.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2930 (Permits For Recreation On Public Lands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part establish permit and fee systems for:
(a) Special Recreation Permits for commercial use, organized group activities or events, competitive use, and for use of special areas; and
(b) Recreation use permits for use of fee areas such as campgrounds and day use areas.
