13 C.F.R. § 108.640 — Requirement to file portfolio financing reports (SBA Form 1031).
Citation: us:cfr:13:108.640
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-108.640
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 108 (New Markets Venture Capital (“Nmvc”) Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For each Financing you make (excluding guarantees), you must submit a Portfolio Financing Report on SBA Form 1031 within 30 days of the closing date.
