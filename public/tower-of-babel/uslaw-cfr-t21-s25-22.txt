21 C.F.R. § 25.22 — Actions requiring the preparation of an environmental impact statement.
Citation: us:cfr:21:25.22
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-25.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 25 (Environmental Impact Considerations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) There are no categories of agency actions that routinely significantly affect the quality of the human environment and that therefore ordinarily require the preparation of an EIS.
(b) EIS's are prepared for agency actions when evaluation of data or information in an EA or otherwise available to the agency leads to a finding by the responsible agency official that a proposed action may significantly affect the quality of the human environment.
