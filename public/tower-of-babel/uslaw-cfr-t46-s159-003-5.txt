46 C.F.R. § 159.003-5 — -5 Approval by the Coast Guard under a Mutual Recognition Agreement (MRA).
Citation: us:cfr:46:159.003-5
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-159.003-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 159 (Approval Of Equipment And Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Manufacturers must specify in writing that foreign approval under an MRA is requested.
(b) The Coast Guard Certificate of Approval will clearly identify as specified in the MRA that the product is approved to the foreign requirements under the MRA.
