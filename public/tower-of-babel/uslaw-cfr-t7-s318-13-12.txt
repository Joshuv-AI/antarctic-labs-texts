7 C.F.R. § 318.13-12 — -12 Movement by the U.S. Department of Agriculture.
Citation: us:cfr:7:318.13-12
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-318.13-12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 318 (State Of Hawaii And Territories Quarantine Notices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notwithstanding any other restrictions of this subpart, regulated articles may be moved if they are moved by the U.S. Department of Agriculture for experimental or scientific purposes and are moved under conditions found by the Administrator to be adequate to prevent the spread of plant pests and diseases.
