30 C.F.R. § 36.31 — Fire extinguisher.
Citation: us:cfr:30:36.31
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-36.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 36 (Approval Requirements For Permissible Mobile Diesel-Powered Transportation Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each unit of mobile diesel-powered transportation equipment shall be fitted with a fire extinguisher carried in a location easily accessible to the operator and protected by position from external damage. Liquid carbon dioxide extinguishers shall contain an active charge of not less than 4 pounds. Pressurized dry chemical extinguishers shall contain an active charge of not less than 2 1/2 pounds.
