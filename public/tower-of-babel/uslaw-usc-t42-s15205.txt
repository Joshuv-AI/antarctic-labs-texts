42 U.S.C. § 15205 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:42:15205
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section15205
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 145 (Public Safety Officer Medal Of Valor And Tributes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Attorney General such sums as may be necessary to carry out this chapter.
