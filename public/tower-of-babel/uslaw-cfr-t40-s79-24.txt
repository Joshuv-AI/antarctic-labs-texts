40 C.F.R. § 79.24 — Termination of registration of additives.
Citation: us:cfr:40:79.24
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-79.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 79 (Registration Of Fuels And Fuel Additives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Registration may be terminated by the Administrator if the additive manufacturer requests such termination in writing.
(b) Registration for an additive that has its purpose-in-use identified as a deposit control additive for use in gasoline pursuant to the requirements of § 79.21(d) may be terminated by the Administrator if the EPA determines that the detergent additive is not compliant with the gasoline deposit control requirements of 40 CFR 1090.260.
