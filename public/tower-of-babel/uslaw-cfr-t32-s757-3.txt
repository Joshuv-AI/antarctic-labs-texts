32 C.F.R. § 757.3 — Regulatory authority.
Citation: us:cfr:32:757.3
Creator: Department Of The Navy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-757.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 757 (Affirmative Claims Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations published in 31 CFR Chapter IX control the collection and settlement of affirmative claims. This section supplements the material contained in those regulations. Where this section conflicts with the materials and procedure published in 31 CFR Chapter IX, the latter controls.
