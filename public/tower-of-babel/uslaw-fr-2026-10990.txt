FERC Form 580, Interrogatories on Fuel and Energy Purchase 
Practices; Notice of Request for Partial Waiver

    Take notice that on May 22, 2026, pursuant to Rule 207(a)(5) of the 
Federal Energy Regulatory Commission's (Commission) Rules of Practice 
and Procedure,\1\ Sierra Pacific Power Company submitted a request for 
a partial waiver of the requirement to respond to the 2026 FERC Form 
580 Interrogatory on Fuel and Energy Purchase Practices, as more fully 
explained in the request.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ 18 CFR 385.207.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
