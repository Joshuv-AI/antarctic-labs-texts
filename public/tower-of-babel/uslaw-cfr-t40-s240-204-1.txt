40 C.F.R. § 240.204-1 — -1 Requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:40:240.204-1
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-240.204-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 240 (Guidelines For The Thermal Processing Of Solid Wastes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All waters discharged from the facility shall be sufficiently treated to meet the most stringent of applicable water quality standards, established in accordance with or effective under the provisions of the Federal Water Pollution Control Act, as amended.
