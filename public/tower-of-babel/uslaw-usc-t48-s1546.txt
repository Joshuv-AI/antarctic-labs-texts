48 U.S.C. § 1546 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:48:1546
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1546
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 12 (Virgin Islands [1954]); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated annually by the Congress of the United States such sums as may be necessary and appropriate to carry out the provisions and purposes of this chapter.
