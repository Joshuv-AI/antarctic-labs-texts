43 C.F.R. § 3481.4-3 — -3 Does a temporary interruption in coal severance affect the diligence requirements applicable to my lease or LMU?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3481.4-3
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3481.4-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3480 (Coal Exploration And Mining Operations Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No, a temporary interruption in coal severance covered by §§ 3481.4-1 to 3481.4-4 does not change the diligence requirements of subpart 3483 applicable to your lease or LMU.
