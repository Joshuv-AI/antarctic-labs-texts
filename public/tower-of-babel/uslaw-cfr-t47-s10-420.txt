47 C.F.R. § 10.420 — Message elements.
Citation: us:cfr:47:10.420
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-10.420
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 10 (Wireless Emergency Alerts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A WEA Alert Message processed by a Participating CMS Provider shall include five mandatory CAP elements—Event Type; Area Affected; Recommended Action; Expiration Time (with time zone); and Sending Agency. This requirement does not apply to National Alerts.
