46 C.F.R. § 154.1330 — Liquid level alarm system: Independent tank type C.
Citation: us:cfr:46:154.1330
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-154.1330
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 154 (Safety Standards For Self-Propelled Vessels Carrying Bulk Liquefied Gases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Independent tanks type C need not have the high liquid level alarm system under § 154.1325 if:
(a) The tank volume is less than 200 m 3 (7,060 ft. 3); or
(b) The tank can withstand the maximum possible pressure during loading, that pressure is below the relief valve setting, and overflow of the tank cannot occur.
