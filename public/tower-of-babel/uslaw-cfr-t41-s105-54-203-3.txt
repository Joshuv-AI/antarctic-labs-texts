41 C.F.R. § 105-54.203-3 — -54.203-3 Submission to Library of Congress.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-54.203-3
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-54.203-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-54 (54—Advisory Committee Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The GSA Committee Management Officer furnishes a copy of each charter to the Library of Congress when or shortly after copies are filed with the requisite committees of the Congress. Copies for the Library are addressed: Library of Congress, Exchange and Gift Division, Federal Documents Section, Federal Advisory Committee Desk, Washington, DC 20540.
