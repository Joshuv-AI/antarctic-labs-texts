6 U.S.C. § 513 — Federal air marshal program
Citation: us:usc:6:513
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section513
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 1 (Homeland Security Organization); Subchapter X (Construction). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(1) Sense of Congress
It is the sense of Congress that the Federal air marshal program is critical to aviation security.
(2) Limitation on statutory construction
Nothing in this chapter, including any amendment made by this chapter, shall be construed as preventing the Under Secretary of Transportation for Security from implementing and training Federal air marshals.
