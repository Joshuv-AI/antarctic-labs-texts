7 C.F.R. § 981.15 — Almond product.
Citation: us:cfr:7:981.15
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-981.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 981 (Almonds Grown In California). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Almond product means any edible preparation other than those included under the definition of “shelled almonds,” manufactured entirely or partially from raw shelled almonds, and nut mixtures containing shelled or unshelled almonds.
