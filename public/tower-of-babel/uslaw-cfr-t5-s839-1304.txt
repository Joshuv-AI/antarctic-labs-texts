5 C.F.R. § 839.1304 — Is there anything else I can do if I am not satisfied with the way my error was corrected?
Citation: us:cfr:5:839.1304
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-839.1304
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 839 (Correction Of Retirement Coverage Errors Under The Federal Erroneous Retirement Coverage Corrections Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except for claims under subpart L (see § 839.1303), and after exhausting your administrative remedies as set out in this subpart, you may bring a claim against the Government under section 1346(b) or chapter 171 of title 28, United States Code.
(b) You may also bring a claim against the Government under any other provision of law if your claim is for amounts not otherwise provided for under these rules.
