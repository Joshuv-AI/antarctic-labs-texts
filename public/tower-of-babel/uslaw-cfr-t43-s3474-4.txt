43 C.F.R. § 3474.4 — Qualified sureties.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3474.4
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3474.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3470 (Coal Management Provisions And Limitations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A list of companies holding certificates of authority from the Secretary of the Treasury under the Act of July 30, 1947 (6 U.S.C. 6-14) as acceptable sureties on Federal bonds is published annually in the Federal Register.
