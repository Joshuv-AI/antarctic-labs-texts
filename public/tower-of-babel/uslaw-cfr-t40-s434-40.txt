40 C.F.R. § 434.40 — Applicability; description of the alkaline mine drainage subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:434.40
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-434.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 434 (Coal Mining Point Source Category Bpt, Bat, Bct Limitations And New Source Performance Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to alkaline mine drainage from an active mining area resulting from the mining of coal of any rank including, but not limited to, bituminous, lignite, and anthracite.
