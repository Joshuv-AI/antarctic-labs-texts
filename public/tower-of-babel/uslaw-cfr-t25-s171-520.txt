25 C.F.R. § 171.520 — How will I receive my bill and when do I pay it?
Citation: us:cfr:25:171.520
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-171.520
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 171 (Irrigation Operation And Maintenance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You will receive your bill in the mail at the address of record you provide us.
(b) You should pay your bill no later than the due date stated on your bill.
(c) You will not receive a bill for supplemental water. You must pay us in advance at the supplemental water rate established for you project published annually in the Federal Register.
