34 C.F.R. § 75.683 — Health or safety standards for facilities.
Citation: us:cfr:34:75.683
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-75.683
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 75 (Direct Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A grantee shall comply with any Federal health or safety requirements that apply to the facilities that the grantee uses for the project.
