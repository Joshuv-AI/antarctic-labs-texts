41 C.F.R. § 301-31.2 — -31.2 Agency discretion in paying expenses.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-31.2
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-31.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-31 (31—Threatened Law Enforcement/Investigative Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agency is not required to pay transportation and subsistence expenses. The decision to pay depends on the agency's assessment of the threat against the employee's or immediate family member's life.
