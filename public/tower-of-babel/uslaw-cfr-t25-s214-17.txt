25 C.F.R. § 214.17 — Use of timber from restricted lands.
Citation: us:cfr:25:214.17
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-214.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 214 (Leasing Of Osage Reservation Lands, Oklahoma, For Mining, Except Oil And Gas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Lessees will not be permitted to use any timber from any Osage lands not relieved of restrictions upon alienation except under written agreement with the owner approved by the officer in charge.
