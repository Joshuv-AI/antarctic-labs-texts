12 C.F.R. § 745.5 — Accounts held by executors or administrators.
Citation: us:cfr:12:745.5
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-745.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 745 (Share Insurance And Appendix). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Funds of a decedent held in the name of the decedent or in the name of the executor or administrator of the decedent's estate and deposited in one or more accounts shall be insured up to the SMSIA in the aggregate for all such accounts, separately from the individual accounts of the beneficiaries of the estate or of the executor or administrator.
