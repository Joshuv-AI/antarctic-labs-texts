Comments on ‘Comment on Aurélien Drezet’s defense of relational quantum mechanics’ by Jay Lawrence, Marcin Markiewicz and Marek Źukowski.
Aurélien Drezet

Abstract
We respond briefly to the recent comment by Jay Lawrence, Marcin Markiewicz and Marek Źukowski [arXiv:2210.09025 and Found. Phys. 54 , 45 (2024)] regarding our work defending RQM against their previous assessment. We refute the analysis proposed by the authors and rephrase our previous study in order to clarify the remaining ambiguities in our rebuttal.
