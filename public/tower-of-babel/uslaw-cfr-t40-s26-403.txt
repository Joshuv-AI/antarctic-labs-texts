40 C.F.R. § 26.403 — IRB duties.
Citation: us:cfr:40:26.403
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-26.403
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 26 (Protection Of Human Subjects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to other responsibilities assigned to IRBs under this part, each IRB shall review observational research covered by this subpart and approve only research that satisfies the conditions of all applicable sections of this subpart.
