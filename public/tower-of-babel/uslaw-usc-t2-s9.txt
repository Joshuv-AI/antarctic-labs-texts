2 U.S.C. § 9 — Voting for Representatives
Citation: us:usc:2:9
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section9
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 1 (Election Of Senators And Representatives). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All votes for Representatives in Congress must be by written or printed ballot, or voting machine the use of which has been duly authorized by the State law; and all votes received or recorded contrary to this section shall be of no effect.
