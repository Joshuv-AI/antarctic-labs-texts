25 C.F.R. § 30.125 — What is required for the Bureau to meet its reporting responsibilities?
Citation: us:cfr:25:30.125
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-30.125
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 30 (Standards, Assessments, And Accountability System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Bureau is required to prepare and disseminate widely to the public an annual report card for the BIE-funded school system as a whole, and also report cards for individual BIE-funded schools, consistent with the requirements of section 1111(h) of the Act. The BIE's annual report card will be made available on the internet along with all BIE-funded school report cards.
