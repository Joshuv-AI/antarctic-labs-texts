30 U.S.C. § 1444 — Disclaimer of obligation to pay compensation
Citation: us:usc:30:1444
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section1444
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 26 (Deep Seabed Hard Mineral Resources); Subchapter II (Transition To International Agreement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Sections 1441 and 1442 of this title do not create or express any legal or moral obligation on the part of the United States Government to compensate any person for any impairment of the value of that person’s investment in any operation for exploration or commercial recovery under subchapter I which might occur in connection with the entering into force of an international agreement with respect to the United States.
