45 C.F.R. § 708.13 — Non-waiver of rights by payments.
Citation: us:cfr:45:708.13
Creator: Commission On Civil Rights
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-708.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 708 (Collection By Salary Offset From Indebted Current And Former Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee's involuntary payment of all or any part of a debt collected under the regulations in this part will not be construed as a waiver of any rights that employee may have under 5 U.S.C. 5514 or any other provision of contract or law unless there are statutory or contractual provisions to the contrary.
