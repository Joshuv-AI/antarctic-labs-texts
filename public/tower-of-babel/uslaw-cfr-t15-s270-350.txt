15 C.F.R. § 270.350 — Freedom of Information Act.
Citation: us:cfr:15:270.350
Creator: National Institute Of Standards And Technology, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-270.350
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 270 (National Construction Safety Teams). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As permitted by section 7(b) of the Act, the following information will not be released:
(a) Information described by section 552(b) of Title 5, United States Code, or protected from disclosure by any other law of the United States; and
(b) Copies of evidence collected, information created, or other investigation documents submitted or received by NIST, a Team, or any other investigation participant, until the final investigation report is issued.
