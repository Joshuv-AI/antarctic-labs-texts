2 C.F.R. § 180.115 — What do subparts A through I of this part do?
Citation: us:cfr:2:180.115
Creator: Office Of Management And Budget Government-Wide Guidance For Federal Financial Assistance
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-180.115
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 180 (Omb Guidelines To Agencies On Government-Wide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subparts A through I provide for the reciprocal exclusion of persons who have been excluded under the Federal Acquisition Regulations and provide for the consolidated listing of all persons who are excluded, or disqualified by statute, executive order or other legal authority.
