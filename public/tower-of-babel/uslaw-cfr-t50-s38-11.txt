50 C.F.R. § 38.11 — Prostitution and lewd behavior.
Citation: us:cfr:50:38.11
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-38.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 38 (Midway Atoll National Wildlife Refuge). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person on Midway Atoll National Wildlife Refuge will:
(a) Engage in prostitution. “Prostitution” means the giving or receiving of the body for sexual intercourse for hire; or
(b) Commit any lewd act in a public place which is likely to be observed by others who would be affronted or alarmed.
