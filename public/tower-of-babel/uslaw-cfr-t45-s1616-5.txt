45 C.F.R. § 1616.5 — Preference to local applicants.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1616.5
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1616.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1616 (Attorney Hiring). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When equally qualified applicants are under consideration for an attorney position, a recipient shall give preference to an applicant residing in the community to be served.
