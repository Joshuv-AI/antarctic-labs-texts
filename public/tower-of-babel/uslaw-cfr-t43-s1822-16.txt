43 C.F.R. § 1822.16 — Where do I file an application that involves lands under the jurisdiction of more than one BLM State Office?
Citation: us:cfr:43:1822.16
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-1822.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 1820 (Application Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may file your application with any BLM State Office having jurisdiction over the subject lands. You should consult the regulations of the particular BLM resource program involved for more specific information.
