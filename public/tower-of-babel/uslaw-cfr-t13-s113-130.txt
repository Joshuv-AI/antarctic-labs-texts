13 C.F.R. § 113.130 — Effect of employment opportunities.
Citation: us:cfr:13:113.130
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-113.130
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 113 (Nondiscrimination In Financial Assistance Programs Of Sba—Effectuation Of Policies Of Federal Government And Sba Administrator). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The obligation to comply with these Title IX regulations is not obviated or alleviated because employment opportunities in any occupation or profession are or may be more limited for members of one sex than for members of the other sex.
