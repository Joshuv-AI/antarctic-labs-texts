28 U.S.C. § 2715 — Publication
Citation: us:usc:28:2715
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section2715
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part VI (Particular Proceedings); Chapter 173 (Attachment In Postal Suits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The marshal shall cause publication of an executed warrant of attachment—
(1)
for two months in case of an absconding debtor, and
(2)
for four months in case of a nonresident debtor
in a newspaper published in the district where the property is situated pursuant to the details of the order under which the warrant is issued.
