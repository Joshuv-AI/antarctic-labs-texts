50 U.S.C. § 3204 — Regulations
Citation: us:usc:50:3204
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section3204
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 44 (National Security); Subchapter VIII (Education In Support Of National Intelligence); Part B (Foreign Languages Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The Secretary of Defense and the Director of National Intelligence shall jointly prescribe regulations to carry out the Foreign Languages Program.
(b) Elements of the intelligence community
The head of each covered element of the intelligence community shall prescribe regulations to carry out sections 3202 and 3203 of this title with respect to that element including the following:
(1)
Procedures to be utilized for the acceptance of voluntary services under section 3203 of this title.
(2)
Procedures and requirements relating to the installation of equipment under section 3203(f) of this title.
