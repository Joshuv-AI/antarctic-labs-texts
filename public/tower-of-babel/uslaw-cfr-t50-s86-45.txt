50 C.F.R. § 86.45 — If the Service does not select my grant application for funding, can I apply for the same project the following year?
Citation: us:cfr:50:86.45
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-86.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 86 (Boating Infrastructure Grant Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. If we do not select your BIG grant application for funding, you can apply for the same project the following year or in later years.
