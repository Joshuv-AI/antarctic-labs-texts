15 U.S.C. § 689j — Reporting requirement
Citation: us:usc:15:689j
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section689j
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 14B (Small Business Investment Program); Subchapter III (Investment Division Programs); Part B (New Markets Venture Capital Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each New Markets Venture Capital company that participates in the program established under this part shall provide to the Administrator such information as the Administrator may require, including—
(1)
information related to the measurement criteria that the company proposed in its program application; and
(2)
in each case in which the company under this part makes an investment in, or a loan or grant to, a business that is not located in a low-income geographic area, a report on the number and percentage of employees of the business who reside in such areas.
