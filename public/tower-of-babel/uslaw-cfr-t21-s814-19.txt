21 C.F.R. § 814.19 — Product development protocol (PDP).
Citation: us:cfr:21:814.19
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-814.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 814 (Premarket Approval Of Medical Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A class III device for which a product development protocol has been declared completed by FDA under this chapter will be considered to have an approved PMA.
