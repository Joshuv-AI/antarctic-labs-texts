21 C.F.R. § 530.4 — Advertising and promotion.
Citation: us:cfr:21:530.4
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-530.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 530 (Extralabel Drug Use In Animals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this part shall be construed as permitting the advertising or promotion of extralabel uses in animals of approved new animal drugs or approved human drugs.
