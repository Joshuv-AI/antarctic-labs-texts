36 C.F.R. § 1005.2 — Alcoholic beverages; sale of intoxicants.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1005.2
Creator: Presidio Trust
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1005.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1005 (Commercial And Private Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The sale of alcoholic, spirituous, vinous, or fermented liquor, containing more than 1 percent of alcohol by weight, shall conform with all applicable Federal, State, and local laws and regulations. (See also § 1002.35 of this chapter.)
