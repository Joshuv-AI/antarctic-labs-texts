7 U.S.C. § 1446a–1 — Use of Commodity Credit Corporation funds for purchases of dairy products requirements for school and other programs
Citation: us:usc:7:1446a–1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1446a–1
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 35A (Price Support Of Agricultural Commodities); Subchapter III (Nonbasic Agricultural Commodities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Agriculture is hereby authorized to use funds of the Commodity Credit Corporation to purchase sufficient supplies of dairy products at market prices to meet the requirements of any programs for the schools (other than fluid milk in the case of schools), domestic relief distribution, community action, and such other programs as are authorized by law, when there are insufficient stocks of dairy products in the hands of Commodity Credit Corporation available for these purposes.
