36 C.F.R. § 1254.64 — Will NARA certify copies?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1254.64
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1254.64
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1254 (Using Records And Donated Historical Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, the responsible director of a unit, or any of his or her superiors, the Director of the Federal Register, and their designees may certify copies of documents as true copies for a fee. The fee is found at § 1258.12(a).
