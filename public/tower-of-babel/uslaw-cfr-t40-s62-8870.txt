40 C.F.R. § 62.8870 — Identification of plan-delegation of authority.
Citation: us:cfr:40:62.8870
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-62.8870
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 62 (Approval And Promulgation Of State Plans For Designated Facilities And Pollutants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

On June 16, 2025, the Ohio Environmental Protection Agency (Ohio EPA), submitted a letter requesting delegation of authority from EPA to implement and enforce the Federal plan requirements for existing municipal solid waste landfills as required under subpart OOO of this part. The Federal plan will be administered by both Ohio EPA and EPA.
