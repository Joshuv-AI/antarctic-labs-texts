16 C.F.R. § 1238.2 — Requirements for stationary activity centers.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1238.2
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1238.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1238 (Safety Standard For Stationary Activity Centers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each stationary activity center shall comply with all applicable provisions of ASTM F2012-24, Standard Consumer Safety Performance Specification for Stationary Activity Centers, approved on November 1, 2024. The Director of the Federal Register approves this incorporation by reference in accordance with 5 U.S.C. 552(a) and 1 CFR part 51.
