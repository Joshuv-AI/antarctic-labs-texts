11 U.S.C. § 1163 — Appointment of trustee
Citation: us:usc:11:1163
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section1163
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 11 (Reorganization); Subchapter IV (Railroad Reorganization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As soon as practicable after the order for relief the Secretary of Transportation shall submit a list of five disinterested persons that are qualified and willing to serve as trustees in the case. The United States trustee shall appoint one of such persons to serve as trustee in the case.
