45 C.F.R. § 1233.5 — What is the Director's obligation with respect to federal interagency coordination?
Citation: us:cfr:45:1233.5
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1233.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1233 (Intergovernmental Review Of Action Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director, to the extent practicable, consults with and seeks advice from all other substantially affected federal departments and agencies in an effort to assure full coordination between such agencies and ACTION regarding programs covered under these regulations.
