42 U.S.C. § 1394 — Payments to States; adjustments; advances or reimbursement; installments; conditions
Citation: us:usc:42:1394
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1394
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 7 (Social Security); Subchapter XVII (Grants For Planning Comprehensive Action To Combat Mental Retardation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Payment of grants under this subchapter may be made (after necessary adjustment on account of previously made underpayments or overpayments) in advance or by way of reimbursement, and in such installments and on such conditions, as the Secretary may determine.
