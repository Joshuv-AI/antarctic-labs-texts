36 C.F.R. § 293.10 — Jurisdiction over wildlife and fish.
Citation: us:cfr:36:293.10
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-293.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 293 (Wilderness—Primitive Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in the regulations in this part shall be construed as affecting the jurisdiction or responsibility of the several States with respect to wildlife and fish in the National Forests.
