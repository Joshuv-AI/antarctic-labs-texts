5 U.S.C. § 8126 — Subpenas; oaths; examination of witnesses
Citation: us:usc:5:8126
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section8126
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart G (Insurance And Annuities); Chapter 81 (Compensation For Work Injuries); Subchapter I (Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Labor, on any matter within his jurisdiction under this subchapter, may—
(1)
issue subpenas for and compel the attendance of witnesses within a radius of 100 miles;
(2)
administer oaths;
(3)
examine witnesses; and
(4)
require the production of books, papers, documents, and other evidence.
