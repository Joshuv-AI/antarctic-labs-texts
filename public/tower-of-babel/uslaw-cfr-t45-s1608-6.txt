45 C.F.R. § 1608.6 — Prohibitions applicable to attorneys and to staff attorneys.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1608.6
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1608.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1608 (Prohibited Political Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

While engaged in legal assistance activities supported under the act, no attorney shall engage in
(a) Any political activity,
(b) Any activity to provide voters with transportation to the polls, or to provide similar assistance in connection with an election, or
(c) Any voter registration activity.
