46 C.F.R. § 68.25 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:46:68.25
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-68.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 68 (Documentation Of Vessels: Exceptions To Coastwise Qualification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart contains citizen ownership requirements and procedures to allow documentation of vessels which do not meet the requirements of part 67 of this chapter. The requirements are for the limited purposes of training for, implementing, and supporting oil spill cleanup operations.
