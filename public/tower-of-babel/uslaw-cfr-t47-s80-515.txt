47 C.F.R. § 80.515 — Limitations on use.
Citation: us:cfr:47:80.515
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-80.515
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 80 (Stations In The Maritime Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A private coast station or marine utility station using telephony must:
(a) Not be used for public correspondence;
(b) Not be used to transmit program material for radio broadcasting; and
(c) Not be used to transmit press material or news items which are not required to serve the needs of ships.
