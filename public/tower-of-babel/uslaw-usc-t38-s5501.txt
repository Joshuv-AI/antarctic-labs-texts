38 U.S.C. § 5501 — Commitment actions
Citation: us:usc:38:5501
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section5501
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part IV (General Administrative Provisions); Chapter 55 (Minors, Incompetents, And Other Wards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may incur necessary court costs and other expenses incident to proceedings for the commitment of mentally incompetent veterans to a Department hospital or domiciliary when necessary for treatment or domiciliary purposes.
