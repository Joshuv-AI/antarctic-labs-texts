44 U.S.C. § 103 — Joint Committee on Printing: remedial powers
Citation: us:usc:44:103
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section103
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 1 (Joint Committee On Printing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Joint Committee on Printing may use any measures it considers necessary to remedy neglect, delay, duplication, or waste in the public printing and binding and the distribution of Government publications.
