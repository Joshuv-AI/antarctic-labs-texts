45 C.F.R. § 17.5 — Context to be reflected.
Citation: us:cfr:45:17.5
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-17.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 17 (Release Of Adverse Information To News Media). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The authority for and the character of the information shall be made clear, where appropriate, the release shall explain the nature of any studies performed, the sources of relevant data, the areas in which administrative findings of fact were made, and whether the information is based on allegations subject to subsequent adjudication.
