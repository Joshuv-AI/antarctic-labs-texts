42 U.S.C. § 1005 — Benefit amount
Citation: us:usc:42:1005
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1005
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 7 (Social Security); Subchapter VIII (Special Benefits For Certain World War Ii Veterans). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The benefit under this subchapter payable to a qualified individual for any month shall be in an amount equal to 75 percent of the Federal benefit rate under subchapter XVI for the month, reduced by the amount of the qualified individual’s benefit income for the month.
