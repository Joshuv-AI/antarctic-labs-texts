40 C.F.R. § 721.40 — Recordkeeping.
Citation: us:cfr:40:721.40
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-721.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 721 (Significant New Uses Of Chemical Substances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person subject to the requirements of this part must retain documentation of information contained in that person's significant new use notice. This documentation must be maintained for a period of 5 years from the date of the submission of the significant new use notice.
