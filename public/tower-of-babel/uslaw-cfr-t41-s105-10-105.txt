41 C.F.R. § 105-10.105 — -10.105 Discrimination prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-10.105
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-10.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-10 (10—Nondiscrimination In Programs Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person in the United States shall, on the ground of race, color, or national origin, be excluded from participation in, be denied the benefits of, or be otherwise subjected to discrimination under any program to which this part applies.
