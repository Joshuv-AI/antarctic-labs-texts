23 C.F.R. § 645.101 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:23:645.101
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-645.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 645 (Utilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To prescribe the policies, procedures, and reimbursement provisions for the adjustment and relocation of utility facilities on Federal-aid and direct Federal projects.
