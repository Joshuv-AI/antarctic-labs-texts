34 U.S.C. § 11242 — Grants for technical assistance and training
Citation: us:usc:34:11242
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section11242
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 111 (Juvenile Justice And Delinquency Prevention); Subchapter III (Runaway And Homeless Youth); Part D (Coordinating, Training, Research, And Other Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may make grants to statewide and regional nonprofit organizations (and combinations of such organizations) to provide technical assistance and training to public and private entities (and combinations of such entities) that are eligible to receive grants under this subchapter, for the purpose of carrying out the programs, projects, or activities for which such grants are made.
