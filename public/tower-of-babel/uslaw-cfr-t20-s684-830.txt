20 C.F.R. § 684.830 — Does the Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act administrative cost limit for States and local areas apply to WIOA grants?
Citation: us:cfr:20:684.830
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-684.830
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 684 (Indian And Native American Programs Under Title I Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No, under § 683.205(b) of this chapter, limits on administrative costs for sec. 166 grants will be negotiated with the grantee and identified in the grant award document.
