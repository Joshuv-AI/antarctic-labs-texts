13 C.F.R. § 126.608 — Are there HUBZone contract opportunities at or below the simplified acquisition threshold or micropurchase threshold?
Citation: us:cfr:13:126.608
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-126.608
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 126 (Hubzone Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A CO may make a requirement available as a HUBZone set-aside or sole source award if it is at or below the simplified acquisition threshold. In addition, a CO may award a requirement as a HUBZone contract to a certified HUBZone small business concern at or below the micropurchase threshold.
