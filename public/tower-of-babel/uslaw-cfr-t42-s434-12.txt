42 C.F.R. § 434.12 — Contracts with private nonmedical institutions.
Citation: us:cfr:42:434.12
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-434.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 434 (Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contracts with private nonmedical institutions must—
(a) Meet the requirements of § 434.6;
(b) Specify a capitation fee based on the cost of the services provided, in accordance with the reimbursement requirements prescribed in part 447 of this chapter; and
(c) Specify when the capitation fee must be paid.
