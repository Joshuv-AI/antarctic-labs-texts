20 U.S.C. § 1755 — Additional priority of remedies after finding of de jure segregation
Citation: us:usc:20:1755
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1755
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 39 (Equal Educational Opportunities And Transportation Of Students); Subchapter II (Assignment And Transportation Of Students). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, after June 30, 1974 no court of the United States shall order the implementation of any plan to remedy a finding of de jure segregation which involves the transportation of students, unless the court first finds that all alternative remedies are inadequate.
