42 C.F.R. § 137.132 — How does the Indian Tribe submit a final offer?
Citation: us:cfr:42:137.132
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-137.132
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 137 (Tribal Self-Governance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A written final offer should be submitted:
(1) During negotiations to the agency lead negotiator or
(2) Thereafter to the Director.
(b) The document should be separate from the compact, funding agreement, or amendment and clearly identified as a “Final Offer.”
