47 C.F.R. § 80.1179 — On-board repeater limitations.

Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-80.1179
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 80 (Stations In The Maritime Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When an on-board repeater is used, the following limitations must be met:
(a) The on-board repeater antenna must be located no higher than 3 meters (10 feet) above the vessel's highest working deck.
(b) Each on-board repeater must have a timer that deactivates the transmitter if the carrier remains on for more than 3 minutes.
