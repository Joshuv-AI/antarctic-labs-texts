46 C.F.R. § 144.710 — Overnight accommodations.
Citation: us:cfr:46:144.710
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-144.710
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 144 (Construction And Arrangement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Overnight accommodations must be provided for crewmembers if it is operated more than 12 hours in a 24-hour period, unless the crew is put ashore and the vessel is provided with a new crew.
