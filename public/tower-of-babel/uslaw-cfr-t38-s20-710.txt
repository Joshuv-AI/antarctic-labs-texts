38 C.F.R. § 20.710 — Rule 710. Expenses of appellants, representatives, and witnesses incident to hearings not reimbursable by the Government.
Citation: us:cfr:38:20.710
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-20.710
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 20 (Board Of Veterans' Appeals: Rules Of Practice). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No expenses incurred by an appellant, representative, or witness incident to attendance at a hearing may be paid by the Government.
