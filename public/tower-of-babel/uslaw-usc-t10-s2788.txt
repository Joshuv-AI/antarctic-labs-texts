10 U.S.C. § 2788 — Property accountability: regulations
Citation: us:usc:10:2788
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2788
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Property); Chapter 165 (Accountability And Responsibility). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of a military department may prescribe regulations for the accounting for the property of that department and the fixing of responsibility for that property.
