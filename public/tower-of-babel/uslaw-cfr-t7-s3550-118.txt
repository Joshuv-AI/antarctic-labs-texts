7 C.F.R. § 3550.118 — Grant restrictions.

Creator: Rural Housing Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3550.118
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3550 (Direct Single Family Housing Loans And Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Maximum grant. Lifetime assistance to any individual for initial or subsequent Section 306C WWD grants may not exceed a cumulative total of $5,000.
(b) Limitation on use of grant funds. WWD grant funds may not be used to:
(1) Pay any debt or obligation of the grantees other than obligations incurred for purposes listed in § 3550.117.
(2) Pay individuals for their own labor.
