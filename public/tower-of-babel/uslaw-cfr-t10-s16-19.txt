10 C.F.R. § 16.19 — Statute of limitations.
Citation: us:cfr:10:16.19
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-16.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 16 (Salary Offset Procedures For Collecting Debts Owed By Federal Employees To The Federal Government). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a debt has been outstanding for more than 10 years after the agency's right to collect the debt first accrued, the agency may not collect by salary offset unless facts material to the Government's right to collect were not known and could not reasonably have been known by the NRC official or officials who were charged with the responsibility for discovery and collection of the debts.
