7 C.F.R. § 1425.5 — Confidentiality.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1425.5
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1425.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1425 (Cooperative Marketing Associations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Information submitted to CCC related to trade secrets, financial or commercial operations, or the financial condition of a CMA, whether for initial approval or continued approval, shall be kept confidential by the officers, agents, and employees of CCC and the Department of Agriculture except as required to be disclosed by law.
