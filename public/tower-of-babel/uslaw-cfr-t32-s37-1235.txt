32 C.F.R. § 37.1235 — Award-specific audit.
Citation: us:cfr:32:37.1235
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-37.1235
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 37 (Technology Investment Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An audit of a single TIA, usually done at the cognizant agreements officer's request, to help resolve issues that arise during or after the performance of the research project. An award-specific audit of an individual award differs from a periodic audit of a participant (as defined in § 37.1325).
