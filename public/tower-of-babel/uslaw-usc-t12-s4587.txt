12 U.S.C. § 4587 — Notice of service
Citation: us:usc:12:4587
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section4587
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 46 (Government Sponsored Enterprises); Subchapter I (Supervision And Regulation Of Enterprises); Part B (Additional Authorities Of The Director); Subpart 3 (Enforcement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any service required or authorized to be made by the Director under this subpart may be made by registered mail or in such other manner reasonably calculated to give actual notice, as the Director may by regulation or otherwise provide.
