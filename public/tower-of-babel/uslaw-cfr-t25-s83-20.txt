25 C.F.R. § 83.20 — How does an entity request Federal acknowledgment?
Citation: us:cfr:25:83.20
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-83.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 83 (Procedures For Federal Acknowledgment Of Indian Tribes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any entity that believes it can satisfy the criteria in this part may submit a documented petition under this part to: Department of the Interior, Office of the Assistant Secretary—Indian Affairs, Attention: Office of Federal Acknowledgment, Mail Stop 4071 MIB, 1849 C Street NW, Washington, DC 20240.
