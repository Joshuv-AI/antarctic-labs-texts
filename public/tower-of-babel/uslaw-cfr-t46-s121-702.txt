46 C.F.R. § 121.702 — Pollution prevention equipment and procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:46:121.702
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-121.702
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 121 (Vessel Control And Miscellaneous Systems And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A vessel must comply with the applicable design, equipment, personnel, procedures, and record requirements of 33 CFR parts 151, 155, and 156.
