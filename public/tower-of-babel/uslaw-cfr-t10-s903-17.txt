10 C.F.R. § 903.17 — Informal public meetings for minor rate adjustments.
Citation: us:cfr:10:903.17
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-903.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 903 (Power And Transmission Rates). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In lieu of public information or comment forums in conjunction with a minor rate adjustment, informal public meetings may be held if deemed appropriate by the Administrator. Such informal meetings will not require a Notice or a transcription.
