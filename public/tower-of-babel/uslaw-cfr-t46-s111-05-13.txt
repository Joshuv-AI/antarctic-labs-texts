46 C.F.R. § 111.05-13 — -13 Grounding connection.
Citation: us:cfr:46:111.05-13
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-111.05-13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 111 (Electric Systems—General Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each grounded system must have only one point of connection to ground regardless of the number of power sources operating in parallel in the system.
