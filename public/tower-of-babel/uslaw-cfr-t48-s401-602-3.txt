48 C.F.R. § 401.602-3 — 401.602-3 Ratification of unauthorized commitments.
Citation: us:cfr:48:401.602-3
Creator: Chapter 4—Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-401.602-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 401 (Agriculture Acquisition Regulation System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Ratification means the signed, documented action taken by an authorized official to approve and sanction a previously unauthorized commitment. Unauthorized commitment means an agreement made by a Government representative who lacked the authority to enter into a contract on behalf of the Government.
(b) Procedures are in accordance with the USDA Contracting Desk Book, Part 401.602-3.
