15 U.S.C. § 46a — Concurrent resolution essential to authorize investigations
Citation: us:usc:15:46a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section46a
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 2 (Federal Trade Commission; Promotion Of Export Trade And Prevention Of Unfair Methods Of Competition); Subchapter I (Federal Trade Commission). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

After June 16, 1933, no new investigations shall be initiated by the Commission as the result of a legislative resolution, except the same be a concurrent resolution of the two Houses of Congress.
