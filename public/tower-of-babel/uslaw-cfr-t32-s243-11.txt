32 C.F.R. § 243.11 — Appeals of USTRANSCOM Contracting Officer Decisions regarding rates.
Citation: us:cfr:32:243.11
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-243.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 243 (Department Of Defense Ratemaking Procedures For Civil Reserve Air Fleet Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If resolution of ratemaking issues cannot be made by the USTRANSCOM contracting officer, concerned parties shall contact the USTRANSCOM Ombudsman appointed to hear and facilitate the resolution of such concerns. In the event a ratemaking issue is not resolved through the ombudsman process, the carrier may request a final agency decision from the Director of Acquisition, USTRANSCOM.
