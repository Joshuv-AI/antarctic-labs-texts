9 C.F.R. § 351.13 — Inspectors to have access to certified plants at all times.
Citation: us:cfr:9:351.13
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-351.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 351 (Certification Of Technical Animal Fats For Export). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the purpose of administering the regulations in this part, inspectors shall have access at all times by day or night to every part of a certified plant.
