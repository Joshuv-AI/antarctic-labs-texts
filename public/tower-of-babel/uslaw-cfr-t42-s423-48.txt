42 C.F.R. § 423.48 — Information about Part D.
Citation: us:cfr:42:423.48
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-423.48
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 423 (Voluntary Medicare Prescription Drug Benefit). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each Part D plan must provide, on an annual basis, and in a format and using standard terminology that CMS may specify in guidance, the information necessary to enable CMS to provide to current and potential Part D eligible individuals the information they need to make informed decisions among the available choices for Part D coverage.
