33 C.F.R. § 150.508 — What are the maintenance and repair requirements for inflatable rescue boats?
Citation: us:cfr:33:150.508
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-150.508
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 150 (Deepwater Ports: Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The operator must perform the maintenance and repair of inflatable rescue boats according to the manufacturer's instructions.
