7 C.F.R. § 764.105 — General personal property security requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:7:764.105
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-764.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 764 (Direct Loan Making). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The same personal property may be pledged as security for more than one direct or guaranteed loan.
