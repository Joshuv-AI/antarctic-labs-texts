49 U.S.C. § 31316 — Limitation on statutory construction
Citation: us:usc:49:31316
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section31316
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part B (Commercial); Chapter 313 (Commercial Motor Vehicle Operators). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter does not affect the authority of the Secretary of Transportation to regulate commercial motor vehicle safety involving motor vehicles with a gross vehicle weight rating of less than 26,001 pounds or a lesser gross vehicle weight rating the Secretary decides is appropriate under section 31301(4)(A) of this title.
