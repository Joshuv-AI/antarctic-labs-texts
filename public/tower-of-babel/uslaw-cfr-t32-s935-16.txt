32 C.F.R. § 935.16 — Emergency authority.
Citation: us:cfr:32:935.16
Creator: Department Of The Air Force
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-935.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 935 (Wake Island Code). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

During the imminence and duration of any emergency declared by him, the Commander may perform or direct any acts necessary to protect life and property.
