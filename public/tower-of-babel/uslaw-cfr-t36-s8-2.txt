36 C.F.R. § 8.2 — Basis and purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:36:8.2
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-8.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 8 (Labor Standards Applicable To Employees Of National Park Service Concessioners). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The public using the national parks is better served when the employees of the concessioners enjoy the benefits of fair labor standards and when, in this respect, they are treated at least as well as those employed in similar occupations outside such areas, but within the same State. This principle is the basis of the regulations in this part and their purpose is its implementation.
