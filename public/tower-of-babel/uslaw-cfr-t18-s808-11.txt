18 C.F.R. § 808.11 — Duty to comply.
Citation: us:cfr:18:808.11
Creator: Susquehanna River Basin Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-808.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 808 (Hearings And Enforcement Actions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It shall be the duty of any person to comply with any provision of the compact, or the Commission's rules, regulations, orders, approvals, docket conditions, staff directives or any other requirement of the Commission.
