21 C.F.R. § 601.93 — Postmarketing safety reporting.
Citation: us:cfr:21:601.93
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-601.93
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 601 (Licensing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Biological products approved under this subpart are subject to the postmarketing recordkeeping and safety reporting applicable to all approved biological products.
