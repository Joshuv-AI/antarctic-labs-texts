20 C.F.R. § 411.566 — May an EN use outcome or milestone payments to make payments to the beneficiary?
Citation: us:cfr:20:411.566
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-411.566
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 411 (The Ticket To Work And Self-Sufficiency Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, an EN may use milestone or outcome payments to make payments to a beneficiary.
