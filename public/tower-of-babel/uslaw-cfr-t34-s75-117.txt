34 C.F.R. § 75.117 — Information needed for a multi-year project.
Citation: us:cfr:34:75.117
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-75.117
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 75 (Direct Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An applicant that proposes a multi-year project shall include in its application:
(a) Information that shows why a multi-year project is needed; and
(b) A budget narrative accompanied by a budget form prescribed by the Secretary, that provides budget information for each budget period of the proposed project period.
