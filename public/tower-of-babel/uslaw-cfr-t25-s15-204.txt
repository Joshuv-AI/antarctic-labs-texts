25 C.F.R. § 15.204 — When is a probate file complete?
Citation: us:cfr:25:15.204
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-15.204
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 15 (Probate Of Indian Estates, Except For Members Of The Osage Nation And The Five Civilized Tribes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A probate file is complete for transfer to OHA when a BIA approving official includes a certification that:
(a) States that the probate file includes all information listed in § 15.202 that is available; and
(b) Lists all sources of information BIA queried in an attempt to locate information listed in § 15.202 that is not available.
