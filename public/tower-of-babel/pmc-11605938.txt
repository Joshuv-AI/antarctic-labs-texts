The caption legends for Fig. 7 and Fig. 8 were updated from “Data labels are provided only for results with p-values < 0.05 (**p ≤ 0.01, *p ≤ 0.05).” to “Data labels are provided only for results with p-values < 0.05 (***p ≤ 0.01, **p ≤ 0.05).”

The original article [1] has been updated.
