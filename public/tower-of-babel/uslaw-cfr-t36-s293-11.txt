36 C.F.R. § 293.11 — Water rights.
Citation: us:cfr:36:293.11
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-293.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 293 (Wilderness—Primitive Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in the regulations in this part constitutes an expressed or implied claim or denial on the part of the Department of Agriculture as to exemption from State water laws.
