22 C.F.R. § 204.32 — Change in agreements.
Citation: us:cfr:22:204.32
Creator: Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-204.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 204 (Housing Guaranty Standard Terms And Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Neither the Lender nor any Assignee will consent to any change or waiver of any provision of any document contemplated by this Guaranty without the prior written consent of A.I.D.
