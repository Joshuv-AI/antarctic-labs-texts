32 C.F.R. § 1605.60 — Area.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1605.60
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1605.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1605 (Selective Service System Organization). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Director of Selective Service shall prescribe the number of area offices to be established and shall define the boundaries thereof.
(b) The area office shall be an office of record and responsible for all administrative and operational support of the one or more local boards within its jurisdiction.
