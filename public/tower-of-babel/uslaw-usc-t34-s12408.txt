34 U.S.C. § 12408 — Inclusion in National Incident-Based Reporting System
Citation: us:usc:34:12408
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section12408
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 121 (Violent Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Subchapter III (Violence Against Women); Part F (National Stalker And Domestic Violence Reduction). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 2 years after September 13, 1994, the Attorney General, in accordance with the States, shall compile data regarding domestic violence and intimidation (including stalking) as part of the National Incident-Based Reporting System (NIBRS).
