32 C.F.R. § 770.47 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:32:770.47
Creator: Department Of The Navy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-770.47
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 770 (Rules Limiting Public Access To Particular Installations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To promulgate regulations and procedures governing entry upon Puget Sound Naval Shipyard, and to prevent the interruption of the functions and operations of Puget Sound Naval Shipyard by the presence of any unauthorized person within the boundaries of the Puget Sound Naval Shipyard.
