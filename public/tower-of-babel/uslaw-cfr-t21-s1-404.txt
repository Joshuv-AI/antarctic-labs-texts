21 C.F.R. § 1.404 — Who serves as the presiding officer for an appeal and for an informal hearing?
Citation: us:cfr:21:1.404
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1.404
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1 (General Enforcement Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The presiding officer for an appeal, and for an informal hearing, must be an Office of Regulatory Affairs Program Director or another FDA official senior to an FDA Division Director.
