25 U.S.C. § 1651 — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:25:1651
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section1651
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 18 (Indian Health Care); Subchapter IV (Health Services For Urban Indians). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of this subchapter is to establish programs in urban centers to make health services more accessible to urban Indians.
