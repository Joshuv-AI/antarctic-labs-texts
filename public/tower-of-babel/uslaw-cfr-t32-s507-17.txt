32 C.F.R. § 507.17 — Authority to manufacture.
Citation: us:cfr:32:507.17
Creator: Department Of The Army
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-507.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 507 (Manufacture, Sale, Wear, And Quality Control Of Heraldic Items). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Secretary of Defense has designated the Secretary of the Army to grant certificates of authority for the manufacture and commercial sale of Service flags and Service lapel buttons.
(b) Any person, firm, or corporation that wishes to manufacture the Service flag or lapel button must apply for a certificate of authority to manufacture from TIOH.
