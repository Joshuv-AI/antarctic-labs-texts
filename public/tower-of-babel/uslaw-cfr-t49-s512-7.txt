49 C.F.R. § 512.7 — Where should I send the information for which I am requesting confidentiality?
Citation: us:cfr:49:512.7
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-512.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 512 (Confidential Business Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A claim for confidential treatment must be submitted in accordance with the provisions of this regulation to the Chief Counsel of the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, 1200 New Jersey Avenue, SE., West Building W41-227, Washington, DC 20590.
