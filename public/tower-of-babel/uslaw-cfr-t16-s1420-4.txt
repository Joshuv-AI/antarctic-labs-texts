16 C.F.R. § 1420.4 — Restrictions on three-wheel ATVs.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1420.4
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1420.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1420 (Requirements For All Terrain Vehicles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Until a mandatory consumer product safety standard applicable to three-wheel ATVs promulgated pursuant to the Consumer Product Safety Act is in effect, new three wheel ATVs may not be imported into or distributed in commerce in the United States.
