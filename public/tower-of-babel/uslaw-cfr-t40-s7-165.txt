40 C.F.R. § 7.165 — Special benefits for children and the elderly.
Citation: us:cfr:40:7.165
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-7.165
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 7 (Nondiscrimination In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Assistance From The Environmental Protection Agency). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a recipient operating a program which serves the elderly or children in addition to persons of other ages, provides special benefits to the elderly or to children the provision of those benefits shall be presumed to be voluntary affirmative action provided that it does not have the effect of excluding otherwise eligible persons from participation in the program.
