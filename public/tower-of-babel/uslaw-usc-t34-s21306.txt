34 U.S.C. § 21306 — Period of increase
Citation: us:usc:34:21306
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section21306
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 213 (Rape Survivor Child Custody). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The Attorney General shall provide an increase in the amount provided to a State under the covered formula grants under this chapter for a 2-year period.
(b) Limit
The Attorney General may not provide an increase in the amount provided to a State under the covered formula grants under this chapter more than 4 times.
