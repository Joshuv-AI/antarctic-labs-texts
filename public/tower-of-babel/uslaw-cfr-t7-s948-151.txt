7 C.F.R. § 948.151 — Colorado Potato Committee membership.
Citation: us:cfr:7:948.151
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-948.151
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 948 (Irish Potatoes Grown In Colorado). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Colorado Potato Committee shall be comprised of six members and alternates selected by the Secretary. Three members and three alternates shall be selected from nominations of Area 2 committee members or alternates, and three members and three alternates shall be selected from nominations of Area 3 committee members or alternates.
