49 C.F.R. § 179.302 — Alternative requirements for the design, construction, inspection, and testing of multi-unit tank car tanks.
Citation: us:cfr:49:179.302
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-179.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 179 (Specifications For Tank Cars). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-28 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notwithstanding the requirements of this subpart, Class DOT-106A and 110AW multi-unit tank car tanks may be designed, constructed, inspected, tested, and certified in accordance with ASME Code Section XII (IBR, see § 171.7 of this subchapter) in conjunction with the NBIC (IBR, see § 171.7 of this subchapter), and in accordance with the conditions and limitations of § 173.252 of this subchapter.
