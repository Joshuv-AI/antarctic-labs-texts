15 C.F.R. § 286.9 — Maintaining recognized status.
Citation: us:cfr:15:286.9
Creator: National Institute Of Standards And Technology, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-286.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 286 (National Voluntary Conformity Assessment System Evaluation (Nvcase) Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each program participant remaining in the program shall continuously meet all program requirements and cooperate with NIST in the conduct of all surveillance and reassessment activities. Participants shall reimburse NIST for expenses incurred for these purposes.
