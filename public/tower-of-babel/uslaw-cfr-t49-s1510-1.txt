49 C.F.R. § 1510.1 — Applicability and purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1510.1
Creator: Transportation Security Administration, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1510.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1510 (Passenger Civil Aviation Security Service Fees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes a uniform fee to be paid by passengers of direct air carriers and foreign air carriers in air transportation, foreign air transportation, and intrastate air transportation originating at airports in the United States.
