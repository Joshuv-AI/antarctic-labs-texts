42 U.S.C. § 12781 — Authority
Citation: us:usc:42:12781
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section12781
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 130 (National Affordable Housing); Subchapter II (Investment In Affordable Housing); Part C (Other Support For State And Local Housing Strategies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall, insofar as is feasible through contract with eligible organizations, develop the capacity of participating jurisdictions, State and local housing finance agencies, nonprofit organizations and for-profit corporations, working in partnership, to identify and meet needs for an increased supply of decent, affordable housing.
