38 U.S.C. § 8509 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:38:8509
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section8509
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part VI (Acquisition And Disposition Of Property); Chapter 85 (Disposition Of Deceased Veterans’ Personal Property); Subchapter I (Property Left On Department Facility). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The term “facility” or “Department facility” as used in this subchapter means those facilities over which the Department has direct and exclusive administrative jurisdiction, including hospitals or other facilities on property owned or leased by the United States while operated by the Department.
