28 C.F.R. § 540.49 — Transportation assistance.
Citation: us:cfr:28:540.49
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-540.49
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 540 (Contact With Persons In The Community). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Warden shall ensure that directions for transportation to and from the institution are provided for the approved visitor (see § 540.51(b)(4)). Directions for transportation to and from the institution and pay phone service, with commercial transportation phone numbers posted, are also to be made available at the institution to assist visitors.
