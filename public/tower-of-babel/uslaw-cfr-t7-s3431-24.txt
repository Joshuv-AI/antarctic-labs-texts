7 C.F.R. § 3431.24 — Reporting requirements, monitoring, and close-out.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3431.24
Creator: National Institute Of Food And Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3431.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3431 (Veterinary Medicine Loan Repayment Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

VMLRP participants will be required to submit periodic reports per the terms and conditions of their service agreements. In addition, the Secretary is responsible for ensuring that a VMLRP participant is complying with the terms and conditions of their service agreement, including any additional reporting or close-out requirements.
