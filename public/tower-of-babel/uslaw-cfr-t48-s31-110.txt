48 C.F.R. § 31.110 — 31.110 Indirect cost rate certification and penalties on unallowable costs.
Citation: us:cfr:48:31.110
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-31.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 31 (Contract Cost Principles And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Certain contracts require certification of the indirect cost rates proposed for final payment purposes. See 42.703-2 for administrative procedures regarding the certification provisions and the related contract clause prescription.
(b) If unallowable costs are included in final indirect cost settlement proposals, penalties may be assessed. See 42.709 for administrative procedures regarding the penalty assessment provisions and the related contract clause prescription.
