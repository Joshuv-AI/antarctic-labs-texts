26 U.S.C. § 4907 — Federal agencies or instrumentalities
Citation: us:usc:26:4907
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section4907
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 40 (General Provisions Relating To Occupational Taxes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any special tax imposed by this subtitle, except the tax imposed by section 4411, shall apply to any agency or instrumentality of the United States unless such agency or instrumentality is granted by statute a specific exemption from such tax.
