49 C.F.R. § 236.837 — Valve, electro-pneumatic.
Citation: us:cfr:49:236.837
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026

A valve electrically operated which, when operated, will permit or prevent passage of air.
