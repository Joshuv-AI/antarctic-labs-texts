40 C.F.R. § 16.10 — Penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:40:16.10
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-16.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 16 (Implementation Of Privacy Act Of 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Act provides, in pertinent part: “Any person who knowingly and willfully requests or obtains any record concerning an individual from an agency under false pretenses shall be guilty of a misdemeanor and fined not more than $5,000.” (5 U.S.C. 552a(i)(3))
