46 C.F.R. § 57.04-1 — -1 Test specimen requirements and definition of ranges (modifies QW 202, QW 210, QW 451, and QB 202).
Citation: us:cfr:46:57.04-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-57.04-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 57 (Welding And Brazing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The type and number of specimens that must be tested to qualify an automatic, semiautomatic, or manual procedure specification must be in accordance with QW 202, QW 210, or QB 202 of Section IX of the ASME BPVC (incorporated by reference; see § 57.02-1) as applicable, except as supplemented by §§ 57.03-1(b) and (d).
