42 C.F.R. § 435.324 — Medically needy coverage of the disabled in States that cover individuals receiving SSI.
Citation: us:cfr:42:435.324
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-435.324
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 435 (Eligibility In The States, District Of Columbia, The Northern Mariana Islands, And American Samoa). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the agency provides Medicaid to individuals receiving SSI and elects to cover the medically needy, it may provide Medicaid to disabled individuals who meet—
(a) The requirements for disability, as specified in §§ 435.540 and 435.541; and
(b) The income and resource requirements of subpart I of this part.
