46 C.F.R. § 221.71 — Hearing Officer referral.
Citation: us:cfr:46:221.71
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-221.71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 221 (Regulated Transactions Involving Documented Vessels And Other Maritime Interests). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If, pursuant to § 221.67(b)(2) of this subpart, a Party elects to have the matter referred to a Hearing Officer, the Vessel Transfer Officer may—
(a) Decide not to proceed with penalty action, close the case, and notify the Party in writing that the case has been closed; or
(b) Refer the matter to a Hearing Officer with the case file and a record of any prior violations by the Party.
