16 U.S.C. § 2202b — Payment limitation
Citation: us:usc:16:2202b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section2202b
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 42 (Emergency Conservation Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The maximum payment made under the emergency conservation program to an agricultural producer under sections 2201 and 2202 of this title shall not exceed $500,000.
