10 U.S.C. § 8763 — Useless ordnance material: disposition of proceeds on sale
Citation: us:usc:10:8763
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8763
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 875 (Disposal Of Obsolete Or Surplus Material). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The net proceeds of sales of useless ordnance material by the Department of the Navy shall be covered into the Treasury.
