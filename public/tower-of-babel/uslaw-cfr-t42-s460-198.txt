42 C.F.R. § 460.198 — Disclosure of compliance deficiencies.
Citation: us:cfr:42:460.198
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-460.198
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 460 (Programs Of All-Inclusive Care For The Elderly (Pace)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

CMS may require a PACE organization to disclose to its PACE participants or potential PACE participants the PACE organization's performance and contract compliance deficiencies in a manner specified by CMS.
