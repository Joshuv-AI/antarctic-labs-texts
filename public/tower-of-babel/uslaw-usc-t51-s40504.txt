51 U.S.C. § 40504 — Establishment of electronic data archive
Citation: us:usc:51:40504
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section40504
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 405 (Biomedical Research In Space). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator shall create and maintain a national electronic data archive for biomedical research data obtained from space-based experiments.
