22 C.F.R. § 133.515 — Are there any exceptions to those actions?
Citation: us:cfr:22:133.515
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-133.515
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 133 (Governmentwide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Procurement Executive may waive with respect to a particular award, in writing, a suspension of payments under an award, suspension or termination of an award, or suspension or debarment of a recipient if the Procurement Executive determines that such a waiver would be in the public interest. This exception authority cannot be delegated to any other official.
