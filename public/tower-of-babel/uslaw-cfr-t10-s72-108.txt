10 C.F.R. § 72.108 — Spent fuel, high-level radioactive waste, or reactor-related greater than Class C waste transportation.
Citation: us:cfr:10:72.108
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-72.108
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 72 (Licensing Requirements For The Independent Storage Of Spent Nuclear Fuel, High-Level Radioactive Waste, And Reactor-Related Greater Than Class C Waste). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The proposed ISFSI or MRS must be evaluated with respect to the potential impact on the environment of the transportation of spent fuel, high-level radioactive waste, or reactor-related GTCC waste within the region.
