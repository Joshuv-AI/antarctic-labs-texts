45 C.F.R. § 160.502 — Definitions.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 160 (General Administrative Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in this subpart, the following term has the following meaning:
Board means the members of the HHS Departmental Appeals Board, in the Office of the Secretary, who issue decisions in panels of three.
