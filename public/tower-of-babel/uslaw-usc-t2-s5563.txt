2 U.S.C. § 5563 — Index to House daily calendar
Citation: us:usc:2:5563
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section5563
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 55 (House Of Representatives Officers And Administration); Subchapter IV (Clerk). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The index to the daily calendar of business of the House of Representatives shall be printed only on Monday of each week.
