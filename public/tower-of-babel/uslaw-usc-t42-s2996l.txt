42 U.S.C. § 2996l — Reservation of right to repeal, alter, or amend
Citation: us:usc:42:2996l
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2996l
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 34 (Economic Opportunity Program); Subchapter X (Legal Services Corporation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The right to repeal, alter, or amend this subchapter at any time is expressly reserved.
