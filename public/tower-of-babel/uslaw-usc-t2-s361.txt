2 U.S.C. § 361 — Publication of recommendations
Citation: us:usc:2:361
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section361
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 11 (Citizens’ Commission On Public Service And Compensation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The recommendations of the President which take effect shall be printed in the Statutes at Large in the same volume as public laws and shall be printed in the Federal Register and included in the Code of Federal Regulations.
