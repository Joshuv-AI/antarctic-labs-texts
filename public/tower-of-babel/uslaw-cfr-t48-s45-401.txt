48 C.F.R. § 45.401 — 45.401 Title to Government-furnished property.
Citation: us:cfr:48:45.401
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-45.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 45 (Government Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Government retains title to all Government-furnished property until properly disposed of, as authorized by law or regulation. Property that is leased by the Government and subsequently furnished to the contractor for use shall be considered Government-furnished property under the clause 52.245-1, Government Property.
