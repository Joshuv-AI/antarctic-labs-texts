54 U.S.C. § 100501 — Areas included in System
Citation: us:usc:54:100501
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section100501
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 1005 (Areas Of National Park System). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The System shall include any area of land and water administered by the Secretary, acting through the Director, for park, monument, historic, parkway, recreational, or other purposes.
