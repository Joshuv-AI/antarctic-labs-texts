46 C.F.R. § 90.10-30 — -30 Pilot boarding equipment and point of access.
Citation: us:cfr:46:90.10-30
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-90.10-30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 90 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Pilot Boarding Equipment means a pilot ladder, accommodation ladder, pilot hoist, or combination of them as required by this subchapter.
(b) Point of Access means the place on deck of a vessel where a person steps onto or off of pilot boarding equipment.
