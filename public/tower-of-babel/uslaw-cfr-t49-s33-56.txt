49 C.F.R. § 33.56 — Changes or cancellations of an allocation order.
Citation: us:cfr:49:33.56
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-33.56
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 33 (Transportation Priorities And Allocation System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An allocation order may be changed or canceled by an official action of the Department of Transportation.
