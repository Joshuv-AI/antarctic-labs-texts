40 U.S.C. § 17707 — Reports
Citation: us:usc:40:17707
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section17707
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Chapter 177 (Alaska Communications Disposal). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Defense shall report to the Congress and the President—
(1)
in January of each year, the actions taken under this chapter during the preceding 12 months; and
(2)
not later than 90 days after completion of each transfer under this chapter, a full account of that transfer.
