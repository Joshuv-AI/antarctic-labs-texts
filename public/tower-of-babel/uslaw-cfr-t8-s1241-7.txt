8 C.F.R. § 1241.7 — Self-removal.
Citation: us:cfr:8:1241.7
Creator: Executive Office For Immigration Review, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-1241.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 1241 (Apprehension And Detention Of Aliens Ordered Removed). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any alien who has departed from the United States while an order of deportation or removal is outstanding shall be considered to have been deported, excluded and deported, or removed, except that an alien who departed before the expiration of the voluntary departure period granted in connection with an alternate order of deportation or removal shall not be considered to be so deported or removed.
