49 C.F.R. § 592.2 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:49:592.2
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-592.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 592 (Registered Importers Of Vehicles Not Originally Manufactured To Conform To The Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to provide content and format requirements for persons who wish to register with the Administrator as importers of motor vehicles not originally manufactured to conform to all applicable Federal motor vehicle safety standards, to provide procedures for the registration f importers and for the suspension, revocation and reinstatement of registration, and to set forth the duties required of Registered Importers.
