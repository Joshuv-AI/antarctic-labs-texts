16 U.S.C. § 2410 — Jurisdiction of district courts
Citation: us:usc:16:2410
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section2410
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 44 (Antarctic Conservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The district courts of the United States shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any case or controversy arising under the provisions of this chapter or of any regulation prescribed, or permit issued, under this chapter.
