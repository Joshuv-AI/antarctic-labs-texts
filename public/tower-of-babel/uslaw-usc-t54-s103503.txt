54 U.S.C. § 103503 — Summary to Congress
Citation: us:usc:54:103503
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section103503
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 1035 (National Park Centennial Challenge Fund). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall provide with the submission of the President’s annual budget a summary of the status and funding of signature projects and programs.
