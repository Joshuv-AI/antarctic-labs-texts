Enhancing Clinical Relevance of GLIM Criteria in Acute Pancreatitis: A Call for Integrated Practice [Response to Letter]
Authors: Hao Fu
Published: 2024

Dear editor

Thanks to Dr. Zhu and Dr. Liu for their interest in our article entitled “Comparative Study of Different Inflammation Definition Methods of GLIM in the Diagnosis of Malnutrition in Patients with Acute Pancreatitis”.1 The authors made five comments.2 The first point, there are many issues that need to be addressed to develop a comprehensive set of clinical guidelines based on GLIM criteria. The GLIM criteria for reduced muscle mass and inflammation have not yet been standardized, and as for the timing of nutritional therapy, choice of nutritional support, and disease heterogeneity all need to be addressed in large-scale RCTs. As for the second to fifth points, the authors talked about some details of clinical implementation and some future research directions, which are also on our mind, and we agree with the above points.
