34 U.S.C. § 10515 — Restrictions on use of funds
Citation: us:usc:34:10515
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section10515
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 101 (Justice System Improvement); Subchapter XXIII (Dna Identification Grants). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Federal share
The Federal share of a grant, contract, or cooperative agreement made under this subchapter may not exceed 75 percent of the total costs of the project described in the application submitted for the fiscal year for which the project receives assistance.
(b) Administrative costs
A State or unit of local government may not use more than 10 percent of the funds it receives from 1 this subchapter for administrative expenses.
