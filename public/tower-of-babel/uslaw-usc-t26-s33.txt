26 U.S.C. § 33 — Tax withheld at source on nonresident aliens and foreign corporations
Citation: us:usc:26:33
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section33
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 1 (Normal Taxes And Surtaxes); Subchapter A (Determination Of Tax Liability); Part IV (Credits Against Tax); Subpart C (Refundable Credits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There shall be allowed as a credit against the tax imposed by this subtitle the amount of tax withheld at source under subchapter A of chapter 3 (relating to withholding of tax on nonresident aliens and on foreign corporations).
