42 U.S.C. § 4346b — Expenditures in support of international activities
Citation: us:usc:42:4346b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section4346b
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 55 (National Environmental Policy); Subchapter II (Council On Environmental Quality). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Council may make expenditures in support of its international activities, including expenditures for: (1) international travel; (2) activities in implementation of international agreements; and (3) the support of international exchange programs in the United States and in foreign countries.
