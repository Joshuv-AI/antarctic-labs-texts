5 C.F.R. § 10.3 — OPM authority to review personnel management programs and practices.
Citation: us:cfr:5:10.3
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-10.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 10 (Agency Accountability Systems; Opm Authority To Review Personnel Management Programs (Rule X)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Office of Personnel Management may review the human resources management programs and practices of any agency and report to the head of the agency and the President on the effectiveness of these programs and practices, including whether they are consistent with the merit system principles.
