Astrophysical S S factor and reaction rate of the direct C 12 ​ ( p , γ ) 13 ​ N {}^{12}{\rm C}(p,\gamma)^{13}{\rm N} capture process within a potential model approach
E.M. Tursunov, S.A. Turakulov, A.S. Kadyrov

Abstract
The astrophysical direct nuclear capture reaction C 12 ​ ( p , γ ) 13 ​ N {}^{12}{\rm C}(p,\gamma)^{13}{\rm N} is studied within the framework of a potential model. Parameters of the nuclear p − 12 p-^{12} C interaction potentials of the Woods-Saxon form are adjusted to reproduce experimental p − 12 p-^{12} C scattering phase shifts, as well as the binding energies and empirical values of the asymptotic normalization coefficient (ANC) for the 13 N(1/2 - ) ground state from the literature. The reaction rates are found to be very sensitive to the description of the value of the ANC of the 13 N( 1 / 2 − 1/2^{-} ) ground state and width of the 13 N( 1 / 2 + 1/2^{+} ) resonance at the E x = 2.365 E_{x}=2.365 MeV excitation energy. The potential model, which yields the ANC value of 1.63 fm -1/2 for the 13 N( 1 / 2 − 1/2^{-} ) ground state and a value Γ \Gamma =39 keV for the 13 N( 1 / 2 + 1/2^{+} ) resonance width, is able to reproduce the astrophysical S S factor in the energy interval up to 2 MeV, the empirical values of the reaction rates in the temperature region up to T = 10 10 T=10^{10} K of the LUNA Collaboration and the results of the R-matrix fit. The astrophysical factor S ⁡ ( 0 ) = 1.35 S(0)=1.35 keV b is found using the asymptotic expansion method of D. Baye. The obtained value is in a good agreement with the Solar Fusion II result. At the same time, the calculated value of 1.44 keV b of the astrophysical S S factor at the Solar Gamow energy is consistent with the result of the R-matrix fit of S ⁡ ( 25 ​ keV ) = 1.48 ± 0.09 S(25~\rm{keV})=1.48\pm 0.09 keV b by Kettner et al. , but slightly less than the result of S ⁡ ( 25 ​ keV ) = 1.53 ± 0.06 S(25~\rm{keV})=1.53\pm 0.06 keV b the LUNA Collaboration.

Introduction

Over the recent years, the direct nuclear capture reaction
{}^{12}{\rm C}(p,\gamma)^{13}{\rm N}

for the description of nuclear astrophysics processes in the stellar

47

(8)

where
\sigma(E)
is the calculated cross-section of the process,

k_{\text{B}}
is the Boltzmann coefficient,
T
is the
temperature,
N_{A}=6.0221\times 10^{23}\,\text{mol}^{-1}
is the
Avogadro number.

V
Conclusion

The potential model was developed to study the astrophysical direct
nuclear capture process
12
C(
p,\gamma)^{13}
N. The Woods-Saxon
form of the phase-equivalent potentials for the
p-^{12}
C
interaction have been examined for their ability to describe the
experimental data on the astrophysical
S
factor and the reaction
rates of the process. The parameters of the
p-^{12}
C potentials
are fitted to reproduce the bound state properties (binding energy
and the ANC), as well as the experimental phase shifts in the
scattering channels.

It was shown found, that the reaction rates of the process are very
sensitive to the description of the ANC of the
13
N(1/2
-
)
ground state and
S
- wave N(
1/2^{+}
) resonance width at the
excitation energy
E_{x}=2.365
MeV. The potential model which
reproduce the empirical value of ANC,
C=1.63
fm
-1/2
and the
width
\Gamma=39
keV of the N
(1/2^{+})
resonance, yields a very
good description of the new experimental data for the astrophysical

S
factor and the empirical reaction rates of the LUNA
Collaboration. The calculated values of the astrophysical
S
factor
at the solar Gamow energy are in good agreement with the result of

S(25~\rm{keV})=1.48\pm 0.09
keV b obtained by Kettner
et al.

[
10
]

using the R-matrix fit, but slightly lower than the result of the
LUNA Collaboration
[
6
,
4
]
of
S(25~\rm{keV})=1.53\pm 0.06
keV b.
