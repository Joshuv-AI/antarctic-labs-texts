40 U.S.C. § 3706 — Limitations, variations, tolerances, and exemptions
Citation: us:usc:40:3706
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section3706
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part A (General); Chapter 37 (Contract Work Hours And Safety Standards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Labor may provide reasonable limitations to, and may prescribe regulations allowing reasonable variations to, tolerances from, and exemptions from, this chapter that the Secretary may find necessary and proper in the public interest to prevent injustice or undue hardship or to avoid serious impairment of the conduct of Federal Government business.
