27 C.F.R. § 18.23 — Registry of stills.
Citation: us:cfr:27:18.23
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-18.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 18 (Production Of Volatile Fruit-Flavor Concentrate). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of subpart C of part 29 of this chapter are applicable to stills or distilling apparatus located on concentrate plant premises used for the production of concentrate. As provided under § 29.55, the listing of a still in the application, and approval of the application, constitutes registration of the still.
