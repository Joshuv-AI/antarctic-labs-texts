10 U.S.C. § 9653 — Ordnance and ordnance stores: District of Columbia high schools
Citation: us:usc:10:9653
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9653
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Procurement); Chapter 971 (Issue Of Serviceable Material Other Than To Armed Forces). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Air Force, under regulations to be prescribed by him, may issue to the high schools of the District of Columbia ordnance and ordnance stores required for military instruction and practice. The Secretary shall require a bond in double the value of the property issued under this section, for the care and safekeeping of that property and, except for property properly expended, for its return when required.
