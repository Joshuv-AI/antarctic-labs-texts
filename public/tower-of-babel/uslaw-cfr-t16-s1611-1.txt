16 C.F.R. § 1611.1 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1611.1
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1611.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1611 (Standard For The Flammability Of Vinyl Plastic Film). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this standard is to promulgate a minimum standard for flammability of vinyl plastic film which are subject to the requirements of the Flammable Fabrics Act.
