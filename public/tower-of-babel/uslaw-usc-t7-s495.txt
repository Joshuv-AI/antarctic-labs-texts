7 U.S.C. § 495 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:7:495
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section495
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 20 (Dumping Or Destruction Of Interstate Produce). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is authorized to be appropriated, out of any moneys in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, such sums as may be necessary after the fiscal year beginning July 1, 1927 to carry out the purposes of this chapter.
