12 C.F.R. § 12.8 — Waivers.
Citation: us:cfr:12:12.8
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-12.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 12 (Recordkeeping And Confirmation Requirements For Securities Transactions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A national bank may file a written request with the OCC for waiver of one or more of the requirements set forth in §§ 12.2 through 12.7, either in whole or in part. The OCC may grant a waiver from the requirements of this part to any national bank, or any class of national banks, with regard to a specific transaction or a specific class of transactions.
