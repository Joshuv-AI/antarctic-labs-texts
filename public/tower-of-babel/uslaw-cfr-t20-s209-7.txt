20 C.F.R. § 209.7 — Employers' supplemental reports of service.
Citation: us:cfr:20:209.7
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-209.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 209 (Railroad Employers' Reports And Responsibilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each employer shall furnish the Board a report of the current year service of each employee who ceases work for the purpose of retiring under the provisions of the Railroad Retirement Act.
