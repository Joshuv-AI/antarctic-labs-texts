31 C.F.R. § 1010.940 — Photographic or other reproductions of Government obligations.
Citation: us:cfr:31:1010.940
Creator: Financial Crimes Enforcement Network, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-1010.940
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 1010 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing herein contained shall require or authorize the reproduction of:
(a) Currency or other obligation or security of the United States as defined in 18 U.S.C. 8, or
(b) Any obligation or other security of any foreign government, the reproduction of which is prohibited by law.
