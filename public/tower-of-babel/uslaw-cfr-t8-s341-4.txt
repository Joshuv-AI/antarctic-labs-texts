8 C.F.R. § 341.4 — Surrender of immigration documents.
Citation: us:cfr:8:341.4
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-341.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 341 (Certificates Of Citizenship). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each claimant shall surrender any immigration identification and permanent resident cards in his or her possession.
