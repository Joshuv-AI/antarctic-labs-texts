20 C.F.R. § 679.350 — What criteria will be used to establish the membership of the Local Workforce Development Board?
Citation: us:cfr:20:679.350
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-679.350
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 679 (Statewide And Local Governance Of The Workforce Development System Under Title I Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Local WDB is appointed by the chief elected official(s) in the local area in accordance with State criteria established under WIOA sec. 107(b), and is certified by the Governor every 2 years, in accordance with WIOA sec. 107(c)(2).
