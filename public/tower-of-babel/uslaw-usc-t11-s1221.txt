11 U.S.C. § 1221 — Filing of plan
Citation: us:usc:11:1221
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section1221
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 12 (Adjustment Of Debts Of A Family Farmer Or Fisherman With Regular Annual Income); Subchapter II (The Plan). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The debtor shall file a plan not later than 90 days after the order for relief under this chapter, except that the court may extend such period if the need for an extension is attributable to circumstances for which the debtor should not justly be held accountable.
