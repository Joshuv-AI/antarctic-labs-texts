30 C.F.R. § 553.21 — How can I use self-insurance as OSFR evidence?
Citation: us:cfr:30:553.21
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-553.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 553 (Oil Spill Financial Responsibility For Offshore Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If you use self-insurance to satisfy all or part of your obligation to demonstrate OSFR, you must annually pass either a net worth test under § 553.25 or an unencumbered net asset test under § 553.28.
(b) To establish the amount of self-insurance allowed, you must submit evidence of your net worth under § 553.23 or evidence of your unencumbered assets under § 553.26.
(c) You must identify a U.S. agent for service of process.
