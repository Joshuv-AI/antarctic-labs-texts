42 C.F.R. § 435.310 — Medically needy coverage of parents and other caretaker relatives.
Citation: us:cfr:42:435.310
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-435.310
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 435 (Eligibility In The States, District Of Columbia, The Northern Mariana Islands, And American Samoa). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the agency provides Medicaid for the medically needy, it may provide Medicaid to parents and other caretaker relatives who meet:
(a) The definition of “caretaker relative” at § 435.4, or are the spouse of a parent or caretaker relative; and
(b) The medically needy income and resource requirements at subpart I of this part.
