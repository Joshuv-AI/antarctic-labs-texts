12 C.F.R. § 361.2 — Why does the FDIC have this outreach program?
Citation: us:cfr:12:361.2
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-361.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 361 (Minority And Women Outreach Program Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is the policy of the FDIC that minorities and women, and businesses owned by them have the maximum practicable opportunity to participate in contracts awarded by the FDIC.
