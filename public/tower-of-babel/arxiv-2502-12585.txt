Two-sided Remotely Almost Periodic Solutions of Ordinary Differential Equations in Banach Spaces
David Cheban

Abstract
. The aim of this paper is studying the two-sided remotely almost periodic solutions of ordinary differential equations in Banach spaces of the form x ′ = A ⁡ ( t ) ​ x + f ⁡ ( t ) + F ⁡ ( t , x ) x^{\prime}=A(t)x+f(t)+F(t,x) with two-sided remotely almost periodic coefficients if the linear equation x ′ = A ⁡ ( t ) ​ x x^{\prime}=A(t)x satisfies the condition of exponential trichotomy and nonlinearity F F is ”small”.
