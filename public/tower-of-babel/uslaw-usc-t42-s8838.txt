42 U.S.C. § 8838 — Jurisdiction of Department of Energy and Environmental Protection Agency
Citation: us:usc:42:8838
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section8838
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 96 (Biomass Energy And Alcohol Fuels); Subchapter II (Municipal Waste Biomass Energy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of section 5920(c) 1 of this title, relating to the responsibilities of the Environmental Protection Agency and the Department of Energy, shall apply with respect to actions under this subchapter to the same extent and in the same manner as such provisions apply to actions under section 5920 1 of this title.
