41 C.F.R. § 102-118.10 — -118.10 Applicable entities.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-118.10
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-118.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-118 (118—Transportation Payment And Audit). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to all agencies (including the Department of Defense (DoD)) and TSPs defined in § 102-118.15, and wholly owned Government corporations as defined in 31 U.S.C. 101, et seq., and 31 U.S.C. 9101(3). Agencies are required to incorporate this part into their internal regulations.
