47 C.F.R. § 87.477 — Condition of grant for radionavigation land stations.
Citation: us:cfr:47:87.477
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-87.477
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 87 (Aviation Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Radionavigation land stations may be designated by the FAA as part of the National Airspace System. Stations so designated will be required to serve the public under IFT conditions. This condition of grant is applicable to all radionavigation land stations.
