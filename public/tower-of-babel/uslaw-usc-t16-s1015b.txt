16 U.S.C. § 1015b — Effect of chapter
Citation: us:usc:16:1015b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section1015b
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 18A (Cooperative Watershed Management Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter affects the applicability of any Federal, State, or local law with respect to any watershed group.
