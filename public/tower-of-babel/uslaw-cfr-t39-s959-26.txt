39 C.F.R. § 959.26 — Motion for reconsideration.
Citation: us:cfr:39:959.26
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-959.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 959 (Private Express Statutes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A party may file a motion for reconsideration of a final decision of the Postal Service within 10 days after receiving it, or within such longer period as the Judicial Officer may fix. Each motion for reconsideration shall be accompanied by a brief clearly setting forth the points of fact and of law relied upon in support of said motion.
