24 C.F.R. § 232.550 — Accumulation of next premium.
Citation: us:cfr:24:232.550
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-232.550
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 232 (Mortgage Insurance For Nursing Homes, Intermediate Care Facilities, Board And Care Homes, And Assisted Living Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The security instrument shall provide for payments by the borrower to the lender on each interest payment date of an amount sufficient to accumulate in the hands of the lender one payment period prior to its due date the next annual insurance premium payable by the lender to the Commissioner.
