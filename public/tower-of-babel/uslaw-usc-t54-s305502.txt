54 U.S.C. § 305502 — Cooperative agreement to operate museum
Citation: us:usc:54:305502
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section305502
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3055 (National Building Museum). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To provide a national center to commemorate and encourage the building arts and to preserve and maintain a nationally significant building that exemplifies the great achievements of the building arts in the United States, the Secretary and the Administrator of General Services shall enter into a cooperative agreement with the Committee for the operation of a National Building Museum in the Federal building located in the block bounded by Fourth Street, Fifth Street, F Street, and G Street, Northwest in Washington, District of Columbia. The cooperative agreement shall include provisions that—
(1)
make the site available to the Committee without charge;
(2)
provide, subject to available appropriations, such maintenance, security, information, janitorial, and other services as may be necessary to ensure the preservation and operation of the site; and
(3)
prescribe reasonable terms and conditions by which the Committee can fulfill its responsibilities under this division.
