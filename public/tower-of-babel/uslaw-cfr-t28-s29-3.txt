28 C.F.R. § 29.3 — Administration by the Bureau of Justice Assistance.
Citation: us:cfr:28:29.3
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-29.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 29 (Motor Vehicle Theft Prevention Act Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director of the Bureau of Justice Assistance shall administer this Program and shall issue guidelines governing the operational aspects of it, including the design and production of a standardized, universally recognizable MVTPA reflective decal, as well as model consent and registration forms.
