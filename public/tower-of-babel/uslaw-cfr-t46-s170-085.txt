46 C.F.R. § 170.085 — Information required before a stability test.
Citation: us:cfr:46:170.085
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-170.085
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 170 (Stability Requirements For All Inspected Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a stability test is to be performed, a stability test procedure that contains the information prescribed in § 170.185(g) must be submitted to the Coast Guard Marine Safety Center at least two weeks before the test.
