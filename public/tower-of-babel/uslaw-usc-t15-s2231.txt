15 U.S.C. § 2231 — Cooperative agreements with Federal facilities
Citation: us:usc:15:2231
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section2231
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 49 (Fire Prevention And Control). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator shall make publicly available, including through the Internet, information on procedures for establishing cooperative agreements between State and local fire and emergency services and Federal facilities in their region relating to the provision of fire and emergency services.
