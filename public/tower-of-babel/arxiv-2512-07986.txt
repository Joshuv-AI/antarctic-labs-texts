On germs of mappings ℂ 2 → ℂ 2 \mathbb{C}^{2}\to\mathbb{C}^{2}
S. Yu. Orevkov

Abstract

The main context

Let
U
be an open ball in
\mathbb{C}^{2}

C
be
a curve
u^{d_{1}}=v^{d_{2}}

\text{\rm gcd}(d_{1},d_{2})=1
,
\min(d_{1},d_{2})>1

Let
F:\widetilde{U}\to U

C

Let
\widetilde{C}\subset\widetilde{U}

(the set of points

Suppose that the following conditions hold.

(A1)

\widetilde{U}
is a domain in
\mathbb{C}^{2}

is analytic, and
F(0)=0
;

(A2)

the restriction of
F
to each irreducible component of
f^{-1}(C)

(in particular, to
\widetilde{C}
) is bijective.

Let

along
\widetilde{C}
.
This means that the divisor of the Jacobian of

(n-1)\widetilde{C}
.

Let

be a

simple normal crossing model
(SNC-model) of

in the sense that

\sigma:X\to U
and
\widetilde{\sigma}:\widetilde{X}\to\widetilde{U}
is a composition of blowups of the origin
and its infinitely near points,

\sigma^{-1}(C)
is a simple normal crossing curve (SNC-curve), and

is a holomorphic mapping such that
F\circ\widetilde{\sigma}=\sigma\circ f
.
We assume that the SNC-model is

, i.e.
E:=\sigma^{-1}(0)
does not contain
(-1)
-curves
whose valence in
\sigma^{-1}(C)
is

2

\widetilde{E}:=\widetilde{\sigma}^{-1}(0)

(-1)
-curves contracted by

It is well known that a minimal SNC-model exists and is unique up to equivalence.
It can be obtained as follows. First, we construct an embedded SNC-resolution of the singularity of
C
,
and then resolve the indeterminacy points of
F
by blowups on
\widetilde{U}
.
We keep the notation

and
\widetilde{C}
for the proper transforms of

and
\widetilde{C}
on
X

\widetilde{X}

Given a compact SNC-curve

We say that

D_{i}\cdot D_{j}=0

.
Then we call

and

Easy to see that

E_{0}
be the component of
E
that intersects
C
, and let
E_{1}
and
E_{2}

E\setminus E_{0}
(they are also rational linear chains).
Denote the intersection points as follows:
p_{0}=E_{0}\cap C
,
p_{i}=E_{0}\cap E_{i}
,
i=1,2
.
It is well known (see, e.g.,
[

,

(recall that

Belyi functions and Zannier’s theorem

Denote the Riemann sphere
\mathbb{C}\cup\{\infty\}
by
\widehat{\mathbb{C}}
.
We need the following result.

Theorem 1
.

(Zannier
[
11

.)

Let
\alpha=(\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{k})
and
\beta=(\beta_{1},\dots,\beta_{l})

be two tuples of positive integers such that
\sum\alpha_{i}=\sum\beta_{j}=N

n:=k+l-1\leq N
, and

\text{\rm gcd}(\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{k},\,\beta_{1},\dots,\beta_{l})=1

Then there exists a rational function
g:\widehat{\mathbb{C}}\to\widehat{\mathbb{C}}
with three critical values
2
2

2

Such functions are called
Belyi functions.

with the sequences of multiplicities
\alpha,\beta
, and
(n,1,\dots,1)

at their preimages.

This theorem is proven in
[
11

in terms of products of permutations (see also
[
3
]

A simple and elegant proof based on ‘‘dessins d’enfants’’ is given in

[

Remark 1
.

In
[
9
]
, Pakovich and Zvonkin found all the pairs

in Theorem

which define

uniquely up
to automorphisms of
\widehat{\mathbb{C}}
. In
[

Remark 2
.

Under the hypothesis of Theorem
1
, one can choose coordinates on
\widehat{\mathbb{C}}
such that

(n,1,...,1)

Such pairs of polynomials
