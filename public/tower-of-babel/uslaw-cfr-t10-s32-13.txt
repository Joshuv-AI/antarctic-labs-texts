10 C.F.R. § 32.13 — Same: Prohibition of introduction.
Citation: us:cfr:10:32.13
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-32.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 32 (Specific Domestic Licenses To Manufacture Or Transfer Certain Items Containing Byproduct Material). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person may introduce byproduct material into a product or material knowing or having reason to believe that it will be transferred to persons exempt under § 30.14 of this chapter or equivalent regulations of an Agreement State, except in accordance with a license issued under § 32.11.
