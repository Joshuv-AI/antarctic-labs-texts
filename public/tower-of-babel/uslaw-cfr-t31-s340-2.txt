31 C.F.R. § 340.2 — Denominations and exchanges.
Citation: us:cfr:31:340.2
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-340.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 340 (Regulations Governing The Sale Of Treasury Bonds Through Competitive Bidding). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Bearer bonds with interest coupons attached, and bonds registered as to principal and interest, will be available in denominations of $500, $1,000, $5,000, $10,000, $100,000, and $1,000,000. Provisions will be made for the interchange of bonds of different denominations and of bearer and registered bonds, and for the transfer of registered bonds.
