14 U.S.C. § 2746 — Award of other medals
Citation: us:usc:14:2746
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2746
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 27 (Pay, Allowances, Awards, And Other Rights And Benefits); Subchapter II (Awards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Coast Guard personnel, notwithstanding the provisions of this chapter, may be awarded medals, bars, emblems, or insignia to which such personnel may be entitled under other provisions of law.
