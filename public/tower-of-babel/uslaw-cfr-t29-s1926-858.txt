29 C.F.R. § 1926.858 — Removal of steel construction.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1926.858
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1926.858
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1926 (Safety And Health Regulations For Construction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) When floor arches have been removed, planking in accordance with § 1926.855(b) shall be provided for the workers engaged in razing the steel framing.
(b) Cranes, derricks, and other hoisting equipment. Employers must meet the requirements specified in subparts N and CC of this part.
(c) Steel construction shall be dismantled column length by column length, and tier by tier (columns may be in two-story lengths).
(d) Any structural member being dismembered shall not be overstressed.
