25 C.F.R. § 26.32 — What constitutes a complete Job Training Program application?
Citation: us:cfr:25:26.32
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-26.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 26 (Job Placement And Training Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A request for training includes:
(a) Intake and application data;
(b) Feasible, comprehensive ISP;
(c) Tribal affiliation document;
(d) Selective Service registration;
(e) Selected place of training;
(f) Statement of financial need;
(g) Statement of eligibility; and
(h) Applicant assessment or other documents as required by the servicing agency.
