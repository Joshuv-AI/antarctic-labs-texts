18 C.F.R. § 5.30 — Critical energy infrastructure information.
Citation: us:cfr:18:5.30
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-5.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 5 (Integrated License Application Process). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If any action required by this part requires a potential Applicant or Applicant to reveal Critical Energy Infrastructure Information, as defined by § 388.113(c) of this chapter, to the public, the Applicant must follow the procedures set out in § 4.32(k) of this chapter.
