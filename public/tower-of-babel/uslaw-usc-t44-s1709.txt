44 U.S.C. § 1709 — Blank forms: printing and sale to public
Citation: us:usc:44:1709
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section1709
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 17 (Distribution And Sale Of Public Documents). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of the Government Publishing Office may print for sale by the Superintendent of Documents to the public, upon prepayment, additional copies of approved Government blank forms.
