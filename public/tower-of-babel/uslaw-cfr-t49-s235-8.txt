49 C.F.R. § 235.8 — Relief from the requirements of part 236 of this title.
Citation: us:cfr:49:235.8
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-235.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 235 (Instructions Governing Applications For Approval Of A Discontinuance Or Material Modification Of A Signal System Or Relief From The Requirements Of Part 236). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Relief from the requirements of the rules, standards and instructions contained in part 236 of this title will be granted upon an adequate showing by an individual carrier. Relief heretofore granted to any carrier shall constitute relief to the same extent as relief granted under the requirements of this part.
