18 U.S.C. § 3625 — Inapplicability of the Administrative Procedure Act
Citation: us:usc:18:3625
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section3625
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part II (Criminal Procedure); Chapter 229 (Postsentence Administration); Subchapter C (Imprisonment). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of sections 554 and 555 and 701 through 706 of title 5, United States Code, do not apply to the making of any determination, decision, or order under this subchapter.
