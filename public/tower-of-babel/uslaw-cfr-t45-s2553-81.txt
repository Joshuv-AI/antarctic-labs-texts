45 C.F.R. § 2553.81 — Under what conditions may an agency or organization sponsor an RSVP project without AmeriCorps funding?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2553.81
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2553.81
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2553 (The Retired And Senior Volunteer Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An eligible agency or organization who wishes to sponsor an RSVP project without AmeriCorps funding must make an application through the designated grants management system which is approved by AmeriCorps and documented through the Notice of Grant Agreement (NGA).
