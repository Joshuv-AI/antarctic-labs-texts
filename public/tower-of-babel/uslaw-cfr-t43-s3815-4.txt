43 C.F.R. § 3815.4 — Protection of stock.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3815.4
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3815.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3810 (Lands And Minerals Subject To Location). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All excavations and other mining work and improvements made in prospecting and mining operations shall be fenced or otherwise protected to prevent the same from being a menace to stock on the land.
