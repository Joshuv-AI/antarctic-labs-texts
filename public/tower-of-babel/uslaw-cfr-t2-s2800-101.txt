2 C.F.R. § 2800.101 — Adoption of 2 CFR part 200.
Citation: us:cfr:2:2800.101
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-2800.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 2800 (Uniform Administrative Requirements, Cost Principles, And Audit Requirements For Federal Awards By The Department Of Justice). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Under the authority listed above, the Department of Justice adopts the Office of Management and Budget (OMB) Guidance in 2 CFR part 200, except as otherwise may be provided by this Part. Unless expressly provided otherwise, any reference in this part to any provision of law not in this part shall be understood to constitute a general reference and thus to include any subsequent changes to the provision.
