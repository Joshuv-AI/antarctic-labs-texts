43 C.F.R. § 28.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:43:28.1
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-28.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 28 (Fire Protection Emergency Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to provide criteria for agencies in the Department to render fire protection emergency assistance to fire organizations not within the Department.
