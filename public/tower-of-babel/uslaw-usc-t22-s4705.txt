22 U.S.C. § 4705 — Authority to enter into agreements
Citation: us:usc:22:4705
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section4705
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 57 (United States Scholarship Program For Developing Countries). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may enter into agreements with foreign governments in furtherance of the purposes of this chapter. Such agreements may provide for the creation or continuation of binational or multinational educational and cultural foundations and commissions for the purposes of administering programs under this chapter.
