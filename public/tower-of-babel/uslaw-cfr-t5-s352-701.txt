5 C.F.R. § 352.701 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:5:352.701
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-352.701
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 352 (Reemployment Rights). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart governs reemployment rights authorized by section 105(i) of the Indian Self-Determination Act (88 Stat. 2210; Pub. L. 93-638, the Act) and E.O. 11899 after service in an Indian tribal organization under the Act.
