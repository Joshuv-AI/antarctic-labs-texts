12 C.F.R. § 1808.100 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1808.100
Creator: Community Development Financial Institutions Fund, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1808.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1808 (Community Development Financial Institutions Bond Guarantee Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of the Community Development Financial Institutions (CDFI) Bond Guarantee Program is to support CDFI lending by providing Guarantees for Bonds issued as part of a Bond Issue for Eligible Community or Economic Development Purposes, as authorized by sections 1134 and 1703 of the Small Business Jobs Act of 2010 (Pub. L. 111-240; 12 U.S.C. 4713a).
