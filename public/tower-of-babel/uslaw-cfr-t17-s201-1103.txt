17 C.F.R. § 201.1103 — Notice of proposed plan and opportunity for comment by non-parties.
Citation: us:cfr:17:201.1103
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-201.1103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 201 (Rules Of Practice). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notice of a proposed plan of disgorgement or a proposed Fair Fund plan shall be published on the SEC website and in such other publications as the Commission or the hearing officer may require. The notice shall specify how copies of the proposed plan may be obtained and shall state that persons desiring to comment on the proposed plan may submit their views, in writing, to the Commission.
