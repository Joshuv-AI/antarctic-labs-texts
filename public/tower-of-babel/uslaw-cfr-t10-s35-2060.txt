10 C.F.R. § 35.2060 — Records of calibrations of instruments used to measure the activity of unsealed byproduct material.
Citation: us:cfr:10:35.2060
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-35.2060
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 35 (Medical Use Of Byproduct Material). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A licensee shall maintain a record of instrument calibrations required by § 35.60 for 3 years. The records must include the model and serial number of the instrument, the date of the calibration, the results of the calibration, and the name of the individual who performed the calibration.
