15 C.F.R. § 280.322 — Transfer or amendment of the certificate of recordal.
Citation: us:cfr:15:280.322
Creator: National Institute Of Standards And Technology, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-280.322
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 280 (Fastener Quality). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The certificate of recordal cannot be transferred or assigned.
(b) The certificate of recordal may be amended only to show a change of name or change of address.
