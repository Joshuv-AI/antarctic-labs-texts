10 U.S.C. § 12740 — Eligibility: denial upon certain punitive discharges or dismissals
Citation: us:usc:10:12740
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section12740
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel Generally); Chapter 1223 (Retired Pay For Non-Regular Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A person who—
(1)
is convicted of an offense under the Uniform Code of Military Justice (chapter 47 of this title) and whose sentence includes death; or
(2)
is separated pursuant to sentence of a court-martial with a dishonorable discharge, a bad conduct discharge, or (in the case of an officer) a dismissal,
is not eligible for retired pay under this chapter.
