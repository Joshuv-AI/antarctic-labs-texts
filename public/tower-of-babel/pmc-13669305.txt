Author Correction: Versatile molecular tools enabling customizable DNA methylation editing in Arabidopsis
Authors: Li He, Yao Yao, Yin You, Xiaofei Wei, Yu Ma, Wei Yuan, Zhaobo Lang, Jian-Kang Zhu
Published: 2026

Correction to: Nature Communications https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-66933-z, published online 6 December 2025

In the version of this article initially published, in the Fig. 7b Source Data and associated figure panel, several control plant labels were incorrectly assigned owing to an autofill error. The underlying qPCR measurements were correct, but “nrpe1-6” and “nrpe1-7” were mistakenly shown as “nrpe1-4” and “nrpe1-5”, respectively, while “nrpe1-1” and “nrpe1-2” were mislabeled as “nrpe1-6” and “nrpe1-7.” The Source Data, figure and legend have been updated. In Figs. 2b and 6c Source Data and associated figure panels, data from separate qPCR experiments that used different control plants were inadvertently combined within a single presentation. The figures have been revised to display the experimental batches separately, and the relevant legends have been updated to clarify the identity and handling of the control plants. In the Source Data associated with Supplementary Figs. 5b, 14b, 23b, and 34b, some values appeared identical because they had been rounded to two decimal places. The Source Data files have been replaced with the corresponding unrounded raw values.

Figures 2, 6 and 7 and the Source Data have been updated in the HTML and PDF versions of the article. For comparison, the original and corrected figure panels are available as Supplementary Information accompanying this amendment.
