14 U.S.C. § 2165 — Relief of retired officer promoted while on active duty
Citation: us:usc:14:2165
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2165
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 21 (Personnel; Officers); Subchapter II (Discharges; Retirements; Revocation Of Commissions; Separation For Cause). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any regular officer on the retired list recalled to active duty who during such active duty is advanced to a higher grade under an appointment shall, upon relief from active duty, if his performance of duty under such appointment has been satisfactory, be advanced on the retired list to the highest grade held while on such active duty.
