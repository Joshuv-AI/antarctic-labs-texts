34 U.S.C. § 10105 — Inclusion of Indian tribes
Citation: us:usc:34:10105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section10105
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 101 (Justice System Improvement); Subchapter I (Office Of Justice Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of sections 10103 and 10104 of this title, the term “State” includes an Indian tribal government.
