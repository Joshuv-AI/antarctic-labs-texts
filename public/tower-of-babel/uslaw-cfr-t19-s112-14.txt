19 C.F.R. § 112.14 — Discontinuance of carrier bonds.
Citation: us:cfr:19:112.14
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-112.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 112 (Carriers, Cartmen, And Lightermen). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Carrier bonds may be discontinued at any time by the Commissioner of Customs or by the director of the port where the bond is filed. Authorized carriers desiring to terminate such bonds shall make application therefor to such port director.
