50 U.S.C. § 3074 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:50:3074
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section3074
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 44 (National Security); Subchapter II (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary and appropriate to carry out the provisions and purposes of this chapter (other than the provisions and purposes of sections 3023, 3025, 3035, 3038 of this title and subchapters III, IV, and V).
