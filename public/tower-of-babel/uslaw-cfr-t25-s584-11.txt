25 C.F.R. § 584.11 — Will the hearing be transcribed?
Citation: us:cfr:25:584.11
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-584.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 584 (Appeals Before A Presiding Official). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. Hearings under this part that involve oral presentations shall be recorded verbatim and transcripts thereof shall be provided to parties upon request. Each party shall pay its own fees for transcripts.
