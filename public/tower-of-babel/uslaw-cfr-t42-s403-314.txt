42 C.F.R. § 403.314 — Evaluation of State systems.
Citation: us:cfr:42:403.314
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-403.314
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 403 (Special Programs And Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

CMS will evaluate all State applications for approval of State systems and notify the State of its determination within 60 days.
