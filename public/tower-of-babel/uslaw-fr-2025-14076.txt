NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION


Committee on Equal Opportunities in Science & Engineering; 
Cancellation of Meeting

AGENCY: National Science Foundation.

ACTION: Notice; cancellation of meeting date.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    The National Science Foundation published a notice in the Federal 
Register June 23, 2025, in FR Doc. 2025-11435 at 90 FR 26618-26619, 
concerning a meeting of the Committee on Equal Opportunities in Science 
& Engineering. The meeting scheduled for Thursday, October 30, 2025, at 
1 p.m. (ET) is cancelled.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Please contact Crystal Robinson 
[email protected] or 703-292-8687.
