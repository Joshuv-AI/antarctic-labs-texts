25 U.S.C. § 327 — Application for grant by department or agency
Citation: us:usc:25:327
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section327
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 8 (Rights-Of-Way Through Indian Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Rights-of-way for the use of the United States may be granted under sections 323 to 328 of this title upon application by the department or agency having jurisdiction over the activity for which the right-of-way is to be used.
