32 C.F.R. § 263.5 — Inspection of license and registration.
Citation: us:cfr:32:263.5
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-263.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 263 (Traffic And Vehicle Control On Certain Defense Mapping Agency Sites). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person may operate any motor vehicle on the site without a valid, current operator's license, nor may any person, if operating a motor vehicle on the site, refuse to exhibit for inspection, upon request of a uniformed guard, his operator's license or proof of registration of the vehicle under his control at time of operation.
