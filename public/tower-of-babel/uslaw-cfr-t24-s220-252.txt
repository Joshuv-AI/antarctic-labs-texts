24 C.F.R. § 220.252 — Forbearance of foreclosure and assignment of mortgage.
Citation: us:cfr:24:220.252
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-220.252
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 220 (Mortgage Insurance And Insured Improvement Loans For Urban Renewal And Concentrated Development Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All of the provisions of §§ 203.340 through 203.342, 203.350, 203.352 and 203.353 of this chapter shall apply to mortgages insured under this subpart, except that the provisions relating to forbearance of foreclosure, recasting of the mortgage and assignment of a defaulted mortgage, shall be applicable only to a mortgage covering a property having not more than four dwelling units.
