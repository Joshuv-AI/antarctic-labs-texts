33 C.F.R. § 125.05 — Captain of the Port.
Citation: us:cfr:33:125.05
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-125.05
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 125 (Identification Credentials For Persons Requiring Access To Waterfront Facilities Or Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term Captain of the Port means the officer of the Coast Guard, under the command of a District Commander, so designated by the Commandant for the purpose of giving immediate direction to Coast Guard law enforcement activities within the general proximity of the port in which he is situated.
