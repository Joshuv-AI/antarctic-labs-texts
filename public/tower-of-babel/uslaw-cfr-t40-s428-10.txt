40 C.F.R. § 428.10 — Applicability; description of the tire and inner tube plants subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:428.10
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-428.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 428 (Rubber Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges of process wastewater pollutants resulting from the production of pneumatic tires and inner tubes in tire and inner tube plants.
