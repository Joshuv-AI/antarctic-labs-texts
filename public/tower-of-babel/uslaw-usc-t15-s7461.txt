15 U.S.C. § 7461 — Definitions

Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 100A (Cybersecurity Enhancement); Subchapter IV (Advancement Of Cybersecurity Technical Standards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this subchapter:
(1) Director
The term “Director” means the Director of the National Institute of Standards and Technology.
(2) Institute
The term “Institute” means the National Institute of Standards and Technology.
