23 C.F.R. § 630.701 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:23:630.701
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-630.701
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 630 (Preconstruction Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this subpart is to prescribe procedures for advancing the construction of Federal-aid highway projects without obligating Federal funds apportioned or allocated to the State.
