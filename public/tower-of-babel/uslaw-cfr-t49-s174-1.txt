49 C.F.R. § 174.1 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:49:174.1
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-174.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 174 (Carriage By Rail). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes requirements in addition to those contained in parts 171, 172, 173, and 179 of this subchapter, to be observed with respect to the transportation of hazardous materials in or on rail cars.
