Note on the induced metric on power sets of groups
Tatsuhiko Yagasaki

Abstract
. In this note we clarify general properties of the Hausdorff-like metric on the power sets 𝒮 ⁡ ( G ) {\mathcal{S}}(G) of a group G G induced from word length norm and obtain some results on quasi-isometries between some subspaces of 𝒮 ⁡ ( G ) {\mathcal{S}}(G) and 𝒮 ⁡ ( H ) {\mathcal{S}}(H) for a group epimorphism f : G → H f:G\to H , when 𝒮 ⁡ ( Ker ​ f ) {\mathcal{S}}({\rm Ker}\,f) is metrically bounded.

Our goal in this subject is a complehensive study of boundedness of bundle diffeomorphism groups and diffeomorphism groups of manifold pairs.
In

we have studied the cases of bundle diffeomorphism groups over a circle and diffeomorphism groups of manifold pairs, in which
the submanifold is a finite disjoint union of circles, and are in preparation of successive papers in the higher dimensional cases.
As a preliminary of these works, in this note we try to make a framework and a foundation under a general setting and clarify general properties on the extended metric on the power set
{\mathcal{S}}(G)
of a group
G
,
which is induced from the word lengths with respect to subsets of
G
.
Here, the power set

and
we treat the wide class of extended (additive/multiplicative) assymmetric metrics with values in

Each

on

The usual symmetric metrics

We list formal properties of these extended metrics.
In this general setting, one of main problems is to understand relations as metric spaces among (some subspaces of) the power sets

,
{\mathcal{S}}(H)
and
{\mathcal{S}}(K)
for a group epimorphism

We obtain some weak results on quasi-isometries between some subspaces of

Although the materials in this note are seemed to be essentially known for the authors in

As an advantage of this framework

We also extend this framework to the cases of group actions and sets with binary operations.

Let

Definition 2.1
.

For a function

(i)

(\mbox{${}^{\forall}$}x,y,z\in X)

We say that the function

1)

an asymmetric metric on

2)

nondegenerate if it satisfies (i)
′

3)

a psuedo metric on

4)

a metric on

′
