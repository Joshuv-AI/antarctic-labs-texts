33 C.F.R. § 142.42 — Safety belts and lifelines.
Citation: us:cfr:33:142.42
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-142.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 142 (Workplace Safety And Health). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except when moving from one location to another, personnel engaged in an activity where there is a hazard of falling 10 or more feet shall wear a safety belt or harness secured by a lanyard to a lifeline, drop line, or fixed anchorage.
(b) Each safety belt, harness, lanyard, lifeline, and drop line must meet the specifications of ANSI A10.14.
