5 C.F.R. § 317.1001 — OPM authority for corrective action.
Citation: us:cfr:5:317.1001
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-317.1001
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 317 (Employment In The Senior Executive Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If OPM finds that an agency has taken an action contrary to law or regulation under this part, it may require the agency to take appropriate corrective action.
