48 U.S.C. § 912 — Authority to appoint commissioners; powers of authorities
Citation: us:usc:48:912
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section912
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 4 (Puerto Rico); Subchapter VII (Low Rent Housing Projects And Elimination Of Sub-Standard Housing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Legislature of Puerto Rico may provide for the appointment and terms of the commissioners of such authorities, and for the powers of such authorities, except that such authorities shall be given no power of taxation, and may authorize the commissioners of such authorities to fix the salaries of employees.
