43 C.F.R. § 4200.1 — Authority for grazing privileges.
Citation: us:cfr:43:4200.1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4200.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4200 (Grazing Administration; Alaska; Livestock). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The BLM is authorized under the Alaska Livestock Grazing Act (Act of March 4, 1927, 43 U.S.C. 316, 316a-316o) to lease to qualified applicants the grazing privileges on the grazing districts established in Alaska.
