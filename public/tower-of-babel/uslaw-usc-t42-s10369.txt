42 U.S.C. § 10369 — Research agreement authority
Citation: us:usc:42:10369
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section10369
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 109B (Secure Water). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may enter into contracts, grants, or cooperative agreements, for periods not to exceed 5 years, to carry out research within the Bureau of Reclamation.
