Correction: Genome-wide identification, structural characterization and expression profiling of AP2/ERF gene family in bayberry (Myrica rubra)
Authors: Yumeng Liu, Linqi Cai, Jiali Zhu, Yue Lin, Minghui Chen, Huiling Zhang, Xiurun Fan, Kai Xu, Boping Wu
Published: 2024

Correction: BMC Plant Biol 24, 1139 (2024)

10.1186/s12870-024-05847-2

Following publication of the original article [1], the authors identified minor error in the designation of equal contributors. Author, Minghui Chen, was mistakenly added as equal contributors in the published version. The correct designation is given below:

Incorrect designation: †Yumeng Liu, Linqi Cai and Minghui Chen contributed equally to this work.

Correct designation †Yumeng Liu, Linqi Cai and Jiali Zhu contributed equally to this work and are co-first authors.

Additionaly, another error was also spotted in the funding grant number of the Funding section. The correct number is highlighted below in bold.

Incorrect Funding: This work was funded by the Natural Science Foundation of Zhejiang province (Q21C150002).

Current Funding: This work was funded by the Natural Science Foundation of Zhejiang province (LQ21C150001).

The corrections do not compromise the validity of the conclusions and the overall content of the article. The original article [1] has been updated.
