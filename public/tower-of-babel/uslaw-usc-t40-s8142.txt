40 U.S.C. § 8142 — Rent of other buildings
Citation: us:usc:40:8142
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section8142
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part D (Public Buildings, Grounds, And Parks In The District Of Columbia); Chapter 81 (Administrative); Subchapter III (Services For Facilities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An executive department of the Federal Government renting a building for public use in the District of Columbia may rent a different building instead if it is in the public interest to do so. This section does not authorize an increase in the number of buildings in use or in the amount paid for rent.
