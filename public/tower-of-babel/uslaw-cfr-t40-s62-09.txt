40 C.F.R. § 62.09 — Revision of plans by Administrator.
Citation: us:cfr:40:62.09
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-62.09
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 62 (Approval And Promulgation Of State Plans For Designated Facilities And Pollutants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After notice and opportunity for public hearing in each affected State, the Administrator may revise any provision of an applicable plan if:
(a) The provision was promulgated by the Administrator and
(b) The plan, as revised, will be consistent with the Act and with the requirements of part 60, subpart B of this chapter.
