48 C.F.R. § 2452.237-82 — 2452.237-82 Access to controlled unclassified information (CUI).
Citation: us:cfr:48:2452.237-82
Creator: Chapter 24—Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2452.237-82
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2452 (Solicitation Provisions And Contract Clauses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As prescribed in HUDAR 2437.110(e)(7), the Contracting Officer shall insert provision 2452.237-82 in Section L of solicitations when controlled unclassified information (CUI), as defined in the provision, will be provided to potential offerors for the purpose of preparing offers.
