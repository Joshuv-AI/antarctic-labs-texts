16 C.F.R. § 1310.3 — Banned hazardous product.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1310.3
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1310.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1310 (Ban Of Inclined Sleepers For Infants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any inclined sleeper for infants, as defined in section 1310.2, regardless of the date of manufacture, is a banned hazardous product under section 8 of the Consumer Product Safety Act (15 U.S.C. 2057).
