7 U.S.C. § 7918 — Period of effectiveness
Citation: us:usc:7:7918
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section7918
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 106 (Commodity Programs); Subchapter I (Direct Payments And Counter-Cyclical Payments). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This subchapter shall be effective beginning with the 2002 crop year of each covered commodity through the 2007 crop year.
