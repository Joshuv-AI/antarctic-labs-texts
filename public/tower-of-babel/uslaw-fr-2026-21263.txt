DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE


Notice of Lodging of Proposed Consent Decree Under the Clean Air 
Act

    On October 9, 2026, the Department of Justice lodged a proposed 
Consent Decree with the United States District Court for the Southern 
District of Ohio in the lawsuit entitled United States and State of 
Ohio v. Renergy, Inc., Dovertail Energy, LLC, and Emerald Bioenergy, 
LLC, Civil Action No. 2:26-CV-01286.
    The United States and the State of Ohio filed this action under the 
Clean Air Act. The complaint seeks injunctive relief and civil 
penalties for violations at Defendants' biogas facilities located at 
1156 Herr Road in Fairborn, Ohio (Dovetail Facility) and at 2279 County 
Road 156 in Cardington, Ohio (Emerald Facility). The consent decree 
requires Defendants to perform injunctive relief and pay a $15,000 
civil penalty, which is based on Defendants' demonstrated limited 
ability to pay a penalty.
    The publication of this notice opens a period for public comment on 
the proposed Consent Decree. Comments should be addressed to the 
Principal Deputy Assistant Attorney General, Energy and Natural 
Resources Division, and should refer to United States and State of Ohio 
v. Renergy, Inc., Dovertail Energy, LLC, and Emerald Bioenergy, LLC, 
D.J. Ref. No. 90-5-2-1-12791. All comments must be submitted no later 
than thirty (30) days after the publication date of this notice. 
Comments may be submitted either by email or by mail:
