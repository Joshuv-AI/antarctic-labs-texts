9 C.F.R. § 166.4 — Storage of garbage.
Citation: us:cfr:9:166.4
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-166.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 166 (Swine Health Protection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Untreated garbage at a treatment facility shall be stored in covered and leakproof containers until treated.
(b) Treated garbage shall be transported to a feeding area from the treatment facility only in (1) containers used only for such treated garbage; (2) containers previously used for garbage which have been cleaned and disinfected in accordance with § 166.14 of this part; or (3) containers in which the garbage was treated.
