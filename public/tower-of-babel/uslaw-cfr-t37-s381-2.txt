37 C.F.R. § 381.2 — Definition of public broadcasting entity.
Citation: us:cfr:37:381.2
Creator: Copyright Royalty Board, Library Of Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-381.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 381 (Use Of Certain Copyrighted Works In Connection With Noncommercial Educational Broadcasting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in this part, the term public broadcasting entity means a noncommercial educational broadcast station as defined in section 397 of title 47 and any nonprofit institution or organization engaged in the activities described in 17 U.S.C. 118(c).
