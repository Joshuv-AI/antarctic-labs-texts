2 C.F.R. § 901.30 — What policies and procedures must I follow?
Citation: us:cfr:2:901.30
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-901.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 901 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The DOE policies and procedures that you must follow are the policies and procedures specified in each applicable section of the OMB guidance in subparts A through I of 2 CFR part 180 and any supplemental policies and procedures set forth in this part.
