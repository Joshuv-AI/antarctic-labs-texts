7 C.F.R. § 927.5 — Size.
Citation: us:cfr:7:927.5
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-927.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 927 (Pears Grown In Oregon And Washington). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Size means the number of pears which can be packed in a 44-pound net weight standard box or container equivalent, or as “size” means the greatest transverse diameter of the pear taken at right angles to a line running from the stem to the blossom end, or such other specifications more specifically defined in a regulation issued under this part.
