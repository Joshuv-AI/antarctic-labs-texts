19 U.S.C. § 3906 — Statement of policy
Citation: us:usc:19:3906
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section3906
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 25 (Clean Diamond Trade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Congress supports the policy that the President shall take appropriate steps to promote and facilitate the adoption by the international community of the Kimberley Process Certification Scheme implemented under this chapter.
