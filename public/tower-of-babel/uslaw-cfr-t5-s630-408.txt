5 C.F.R. § 630.408 — Records on the use of sick leave.
Citation: us:cfr:5:630.408
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-630.408
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 630 (Absence And Leave). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An agency must maintain records of the amount of sick leave used by an employee for family care purposes and to make arrangements for or attend the funeral of a family member under § 630.401(a)(3) and (4). The records must be sufficient to ensure that an employee does not exceed the limitations in § 630.401(b) and (c).
