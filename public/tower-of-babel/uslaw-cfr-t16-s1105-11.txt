16 C.F.R. § 1105.11 — Compensable costs.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1105.11
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1105.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1105 (Contributions To Costs Of Participants In Development Of Consumer Product Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission may compensate participants for any or all of the following costs:
(a) Salaries for participants or employees of participants;
(b) Fees for consultants, experts, contractural services, and attorneys that are incurred by participants;
(c) Transportation costs;
(d) Travel-related costs such as lodging, meals, tipping, telephone calls; and
(e) All other reasonable costs incurred, such as document reproduction, postage, baby-sitting, and the like.
