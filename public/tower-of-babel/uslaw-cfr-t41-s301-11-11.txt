41 C.F.R. § 301-11.11 — -11.11 Subsistence expense calculations when traveling across the international dateline (IDL).
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-11.11
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-11.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-11 (11—Subsistence Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When crossing the IDL, actual elapsed travel time will be used to compute an employee's subsistence entitlement rather than calendar days.
