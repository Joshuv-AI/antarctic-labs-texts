15 C.F.R. § 230.5 — Terms and shipping.
Citation: us:cfr:15:230.5
Creator: National Institute Of Standards And Technology, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-230.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 230 (Standard Reference Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Prices are given in the SP 260 supplement. These prices are subject to revision and orders will be billed for prices in effect at the time of shipment. No discounts are given on purchases of SRM's.
(b) Payment need not accompany a purchase order. Payment is due within 30 days of receipt of an invoice.
(c) SRM's are shipped in the most expeditious manner that complies with transportation and postal laws and regulations.
