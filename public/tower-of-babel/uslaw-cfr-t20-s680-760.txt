20 C.F.R. § 680.760 — What is customized training?
Citation: us:cfr:20:680.760
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-680.760
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 680 (Adult And Dislocated Worker Activities Under Title I Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Customized training is training:
(a) That is designed to meet the special requirements of an employer (including a group of employers);
(b) That is conducted with a commitment by the employer to employ an individual upon successful completion of the training; and
(c) For which the employer pays for a significant cost of the training, as determined by the Local WDB in accordance with the factors identified in WIOA sec. 3(14).
