41 C.F.R. § 102-41.5 — -41.5 Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-41.5

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-41 (41—Disposition Of Seized, Forfeited, Voluntarily Abandoned, And Unclaimed Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part covers the disposition of seized, forfeited, voluntarily abandoned, and unclaimed personal property under the custody of any Federal agency located in the United States, the U.S. Virgin Islands, American Samoa, Guam, the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico, the Northern Mariana Islands, the Federated States of Micronesia, the Marshall Islands, and Palau.
