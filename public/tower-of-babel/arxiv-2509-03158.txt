fractional integral on Hardy spaces on product domains
Yiyu Tang

Abstract
. By using the vector-valued theory of singular integrals, we prove a Hardy–Littlewood–Sobolev inequality on product Hardy spaces H prod p H^{p}_{\rm{prod}} , which is a parallel result of the classical Hardy–Littlewood–Sobolev inequality. The same technique shows the H prod p H^{p}_{\rm{prod}} -boundedness of the iterated Hilbert transform. As a byproduct, new proofs of several recently discovered Hardy type inequalities on product Hardy spaces are obtained, which avoid complicated Calderón–Zygmund theory on product domain, rendering them considerably simpler than the original proofs.

It is known that many results of

1.2.

Differences between

One way to describe the differences between

In Krantz’s proof of (

This is not the only difficulty. Krantz’s proof is essentially the same as that of the boundedness of singular integral operator (i.e., the Calderón–Zygmund theory) on

Theorem 1.1
.

Let
I_{(\alpha,d)}
be the product form of the fractional integral operator. We have the Hardy–Littlewood–Sobolev inequality in product form:

\|I_{(\alpha,d)}f\|_{H_{\mathrm{prod}}^{q}}\lesssim\|f\|_{H_{\mathrm{prod}}^{p}},\quad\frac{1}{q}=\frac{1}{p}-\frac{\alpha}{d},

as long as
0<p<q<\infty
.

Also, based on the same technique, new easier (both conceptually and technically) proofs of the main results of a recent paper
[
5
]
by Dyachenko, Nursultanov, Tikhonov and Weisz are obtained. An example is the
H^{p}_{\rm{prod}}
Hardy–Littlewood inequality

(1.5)

\bigg(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{d}}\frac{|\widehat{f}(\xi)|^{p}}{|\xi_{1}\xi_{2}\cdots\xi_{d}|^{2-p}}\,\mathrm{d}\xi\bigg)^{\frac{1}{p}}\lesssim_{d,p}\|f\|_{H^{p}_{\mathrm{prod}}(\mathbb{R}^{d})},\text{ where }0<p\leq 1.

(1.6)

\bigg(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{d}}\frac{|\widehat{f}(\xi)|^{p}}{|\xi|^{(2-p)d}}\,\mathrm{d}\xi\bigg)^{\frac{1}{p}}\lesssim_{d,p}\|f\|_{H^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{d})},\text{ where }0<p\leq 1.

]

Proof.
