31 C.F.R. § 35.7 — Pensions.
Citation: us:cfr:31:35.7
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-35.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 35 (Pandemic Relief Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A recipient (other than a Tribal government) may not use funds for deposit into any pension fund.
