7 C.F.R. § 766.458 — Reversal of DBSA.
Citation: us:cfr:7:766.458
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-766.458
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 766 (Direct Loan Servicing—Special). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Agency determines that the borrower received an unauthorized DBSA, the Agency will reverse the DBSA agreement after all appeals are concluded.
