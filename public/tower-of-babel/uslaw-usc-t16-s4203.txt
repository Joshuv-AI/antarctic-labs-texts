16 U.S.C. § 4203 — Statement of policy
Citation: us:usc:16:4203
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section4203
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 62 (African Elephant Conservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the policy of the United States—
(1)
to assist in the conservation and protection of the African elephant by supporting the conservation programs of African countries and the CITES Secretariat; and
(2)
to provide financial resources for those programs.
