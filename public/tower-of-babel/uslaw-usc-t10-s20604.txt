10 U.S.C. § 20604 — Enlisted members: computation of years of service for voluntary retirement
Citation: us:usc:10:20604
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section20604
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Space Force); Chapter 2013 (Voluntary Retirement For Length Of Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Years of Active Service.—
For the purpose of determining whether an enlisted member of the Space Force may be retired under section 20603 of this title, the member’s years of service are computed by adding all active service in the armed forces.
(b) Reference to Section Excluding Counting of Certain Service Required to Be Made up.—
Time required to be made up under section 972(a) of this title may not be counted in computing years of service under subsection (a).
