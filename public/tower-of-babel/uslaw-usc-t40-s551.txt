40 U.S.C. § 551 — Donations to American Red Cross
Citation: us:usc:40:551
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section551
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Chapter 5 (Property Management); Subchapter III (Disposing Of Property). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator of General Services, in the Administrator’s discretion and under regulations that the Administrator may prescribe, may donate to the American National Red Cross for charitable purposes property that the American National Red Cross processed, produced, or donated and that has been determined to be surplus property.
