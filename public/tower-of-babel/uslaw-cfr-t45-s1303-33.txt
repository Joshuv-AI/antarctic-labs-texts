45 C.F.R. § 1303.33 — Termination of delegate agencies.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1303.33
Creator: Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1303.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1303 (Financial And Administrative Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If a grant recipient shows cause why termination is appropriate or demonstrates cost effectiveness, the grant recipient may terminate a delegate agency's contract.
(b) The grant recipient's decision to terminate must not be arbitrary or capricious.
(c) The grant recipient must establish a process for defunding a delegate agency, including an appeal of a defunding decision and must ensure the process is fair and timely.
(d) The grant recipient must notify the responsible HHS official about the appeal and its decision.
