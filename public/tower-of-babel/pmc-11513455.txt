Artificial intelligence-based power market price prediction in smart renewable energy systems: Combining prophet and transformer models
Authors: Cheng Huang, Tianhui Zhao, Di Huang, Bingcheng Cen, Qian Zhou, Wenjia Chen
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
AbstractWith the increasing integration of smart renewable energy systems and power electronic converters, electricity market price prediction is particularly important. It is not only crucial for the interests of power suppliers and market regulators but also plays a key role in ensuring the reliable and flexible operation of the power system, particularly during extreme weather events or abnormal conditions. This study develops a hybrid time series forecasting model that combines Prophet and Transformer, which takes advantage of deep learning to provide a new solution for electricity market price forecasting. By introducing the Stacking optimization strategy, this study improves the accuracy and stability of electricity market price sequence prediction. In addition, the study tries to integrate traditional time series forecasting methods (such as the Prophet model) with deep learning models (such as the Transformer model), aiming to make full use of their respective advantages to achieve more accurate and stable predictions. Through experimental evaluation on four electricity market data sets, this study finds that the hybrid forecast model exhibits significant performance improvements in enhancing the accuracy and stability of electricity market price predictions. This method not only provides a more accurate tool for power market price prediction, but also provides solid technical support for the efficient operation and sustainable development of smart renewable energy systems. Experimental results also show that the combination of deep learning models with traditional time series methods and the introduction of Stacking strategies is crucial to improving the performance of power market price prediction, and also helps us better understand and design smart renewable energy systems, price and energy management strategies, thereby providing an effective method for achieving efficient and reliable power and energy transmission.Keywords: Renewable energy systems; Deep learning; Electricity market; Price forecasting,; Multi-source data; Data analysis; Artificial intelligence

Introduction

With the transformation of the global energy structure, smart renewable energy systems have gradually become a core component of the power system. In this context, accurate electricity market price forecasting is of great significance for maintaining the balance of energy supply and demand, optimizing grid operation, and promoting the effective use of renewable energy [1]. However, due to the volatility of renewable energy such as wind and solar energy, electricity market prices often present a high degree of complexity and uncertainty, and traditional forecasting methods face many challenges in dealing with these dynamic changes [2], [3]. Therefore, how to build a more accurate and stable forecasting model has become a hot issue in current research [4].

The electricity market price forecasting not only needs to consider the historical data of the market, but also must integrate multi-source heterogeneous data such as weather and power generation [5]. Although traditional time series models can process one-dimensional time data, they often fail to achieve ideal results when faced with complex multi-dimensional features and long sequence dependencies [6]. In order to solve these problems, the application of artificial intelligence technology, especially deep learning models, has become an important breakthrough [7].

This paper proposes a model framework combining Prophet and Transformer for the forecasting and trend analysis of electricity market prices. The Prophet model is good at capturing long-term trends and seasonal fluctuations in time series, especially when dealing with exogenous events such as holidays [8], [9]. On the other hand, the Transformer model has significant advantages in modeling complex time series dependencies and can effectively capture the nonlinear dynamic characteristics of price data [10]. However, relying on a single model may have certain limitations, so this article further introduces the Stacking model to optimize model performance and improve the final prediction accuracy by integrating the output of the Prophet and Transformer models.

The key contributions of this research can be summarized as follows:

•An innovative hybrid time series forecasting model is proposed, which combines the Prophet and Transformer models, and introduces the Stacking optimization strategy to solve the power market price forecasting problem.

Related work

Deep learning in smart grid monitoring

As an important part of modern power system, the research focus of smart grid is on how to improve the stability and flexibility of power grid through efficient data processing and intelligent decision-making [11], [12]. In smart grid, CNN and GCN are gradually applied to the state monitoring and prediction of power system. CNN performs well in processing images and time series data in power system, especially in identifying complex spatial features. For example, CNN is very successful in the application of power equipment fault detection, which can quickly identify abnormal signals from monitoring images [13]. However, CNN is subject to certain limitations when processing non-image structured data, and it is difficult to capture the topological relationship in the power network [14], [15].

In contrast, GCN can effectively model complex power transmission networks in smart grids by utilizing the network structure of the power system. For example, in power load forecasting and power grid state estimation, GCN is widely used to capture complex dependencies between power nodes [16]. The advantage of this method is that it can make full use of the graph structure characteristics of the power grid and improve the prediction accuracy of the model. However, GCN performs worse than time series models when dealing with long time series dependencies [17]. Therefore, it is often necessary to combine other time series models, such as LSTM and Seq2Seq, in the dynamic prediction of smart grids to enhance its time series modeling capabilities.

Electricity price forecasting with deep learning

Electricity market price prediction is an important topic in smart grid research. In recent years, time series models based on deep learning have made significant progress in this field [18]. LSTM and Seq2Seq are commonly used models in price prediction. LSTM is good at processing time series data with long-term dependencies, and can effectively capture long-term and short-term trends in historical prices through memory and forgetting mechanisms. In practical applications, LSTM is widely used to predict electricity market prices and can better cope with complex fluctuations in the electricity market. However, LSTM has limited flexibility when faced with multi-dimensional and multi-variable data [19], [20].

The Seq2Seq model is particularly suitable for multi-step prediction tasks. The application of Seq2Seq in electricity market price prediction improves the modeling ability of long-term series dependencies by introducing an attention mechanism [21]. For example, some studies use Seq2Seq combined with the Transformer model to show higher accuracy and stability in multi-step prediction tasks of electricity market prices [22]. However, the Seq2Seq model relies heavily on data during the training process, takes a long time to train, and has high model complexity, which may pose challenges to scenarios with limited computing resources.

In order to further improve the accuracy of electricity price prediction, Bidirectional Gated Recurrent Units (BiGRU) have also been introduced into price prediction tasks in recent years. BiGRU can handle both forward and reverse time dependencies, and has certain advantages in capturing complex timing relationships [23], [24]. However, BiGRU still needs to be improved when dealing with multi-source data fusion and handling long-term time dependencies, so it is often used in combination with other models to improve prediction effects.

Deep reinforcement learning in power systems

Deep reinforcement learning (DRL), as a technology that has emerged in recent years, has been increasingly used in smart grids and power markets [25], [26]. Typical DRL applications include optimizing power dispatch through DQNs to improve the utilization efficiency of renewable energy. For example, the application of DRL in power load management and real-time pricing can dynamically adjust power prices according to market supply and demand conditions to reduce fluctuations and improve market efficiency [27], [28].

DRL performs poorly when processing high-dimensional continuous action spaces and is prone to falling into local optimal solutions [29]. In order to cope with these problems, researchers have proposed hybrid models combined with deep learning, such as combining DRL with Transformer for complex power market pricing optimization, thereby improving prediction accuracy and accelerating model convergence [30]. However, the generalization ability and stability of DRL are still the focus of current research. How to build a more robust DRL model in a changing power market environment is one of the current important challenges.

Various deep learning models such as CNN, GCN, LSTM, Seq2Seq, BiGRU and DRL have shown different advantages and disadvantages in related applications. However, when faced with a complex power market environment, these models still have problems such as insufficient fusion of multi-source heterogeneous data, inaccurate modeling of long sequence dependencies, and high model training costs. The Prophet-Transformer model proposed in this paper, by introducing Stacking optimization, can not only combine Prophet to capture basic trends and seasonal characteristics, but also use Transformer and Stacking to improve the modeling capabilities of complex time series dependencies, thereby providing a more accurate and stable solution for power market price forecasting.

Methodology

Overview of the network

This paper proposes a power market price forecasting model that combines Prophet and Transformer. The model is processed in two stages: first, the Prophet model is used to capture the long-term trends and seasonal fluctuations in the power market, and then the Transformer model is used to model complex time series dependencies, thereby improving the accuracy and stability of the forecast. In order to further improve the performance of the model, this paper also introduces the Stacking model to integrate the outputs of Prophet and Transformer to optimize the final forecasting effect.

As shown in Fig. 1, the overall process of the model starts with the input data set, including the division of training and test sets. First, the Prophet model is used to initialize network parameters and process outliers and missing data in the data. The Prophet model is good at capturing long-term trends and seasonal characteristics in the data, ensuring that a relatively stable basic feature is provided for the Transformer in the initial stage. Based on the data processed by the Prophet model, the Transformer model further corrects the network parameters and performs complex time series modeling. The Transformer model can capture short-term fluctuations and long-term series dependencies in the power market through its powerful self-attention mechanism. During the model training process, the network parameters are dynamically adjusted until the error value of the model meets the requirements. Finally, the output of the model will be verified and the forecast results of the power market price will be generated. The two are effectively combined through the Stacking optimization module to form a multi-model integrated forecasting system, which improves the accuracy and robustness of the forecast.

Prophet module

The Prophet model is a powerful tool for time series data prediction proposed by Facebook. It is especially good at processing time series data with significant trends, periodicity and abnormal events [31]. Prophet models provide accurate forecasts for future points in time by modeling long-term trends, seasonal changes, and holiday effects in time series. In electricity market price forecasting, market prices often have significant cyclical characteristics, such as day and night price fluctuations and seasonal demand changes. Therefore, this paper selects the Prophet model as the preliminary time series processing module responsible for capturing these long-term and cyclical trends.

The main advantages of the Prophet model are its robustness to data outliers and flexible interpretability. Electricity market data are often affected by abnormal events (such as equipment failures, extreme weather), and the Prophet model can well identify these anomalies and correct them by adjusting model parameters [32]. In addition, the Prophet model has strong interpretability, and the model can clearly give the specific impact of trends, seasonality and holiday effects, which is important for analyzing the causes of price fluctuations in the power market.

In the overall framework of this article, the main role of the Prophet module is to provide processed preliminary prediction results for the Transformer module. Through the Prophet model, the system can effectively model long-term trends and seasonal fluctuations in market prices, and the Transformer module further captures complex time series dependencies in price data. The two work together, with the Prophet model responsible for providing stable trend predictions at a global level, while the Transformer model models short-term fluctuations in detail.

Transformer model

Unlike traditional RNN or LSTM models, Transformer does not rely on sequence order, but uses a multi-head attention mechanism to capture global dependencies in the data [33]. This enables it to have stronger parallel computing capabilities and faster training speed when processing long-term series dependencies. This paper introduces the Transformer module for power market price forecasting, aiming to further capture the complex short-term fluctuations and time series dependencies in the data [34].

The Transformer module plays a key role in the overall model of this paper. Transformer uses its global attention mechanism to capture the complex dependencies between various time points in the power market, especially when dealing with long-term dependencies and nonlinear fluctuations. In addition, Transformer's multi-head attention mechanism and position encoding enable it to efficiently model multi-dimensional feature data and provide more accurate price predictions.

As illustrated in Fig. 3, the Transformer module receives time series data with positional encoding, which preserves the temporal information of the data. The core architecture of the Transformer is composed of an encoder and decoder, where each component integrates a multi-head attention mechanism and a feed forward network. To maintain model stability and efficiency, the “Add & Norm” operation is applied throughout the process.

Stacking optimization model

The Stacking optimization model is an integral part of the Prophet-Transformer combination model. It is mainly responsible for improving the accuracy and robustness of electricity market price prediction [35]. The core of this process is to utilize various data characteristics and patterns captured by different models and effectively fuse this information to form the final prediction. Within the model, Stacking works by integrating the respective outputs of the Prophet and Transformer models [36].

The Prophet model focuses on capturing trends and cyclical changes in price data, while the Transformer model is good at capturing complex serial dependencies, such as short-term fluctuations and non-linear characteristics. The Stacking model treats these outputs as different feature combinations and gives them different importance through the weight parameters obtained through training, so that the final prediction result can not only reflect long-term trends, but also respond quickly to instant changes in the market.

The core advantage of the Stacking model lies in its synthesis and optimization of predictions from different sources. By assigning appropriate weights to each model, Stacking can balance between various models, reduce the bias that a single model may introduce, and at the same time improve the generalization ability of the overall prediction system. This approach enables the system to maintain robust performance and optimize prediction results when faced with different data sets and market conditions. In order to ensure the efficiency and adaptability of the Stacking process, this article carefully designs the interaction of each component. According to the performance of different models on specific data sets, the weight parameters are dynamically adjusted through the adaptive mechanism, which improves the adaptability of the model when facing unknown data and also enhances the prediction stability in various market fluctuations.

The above electricity market data are particularly suitable for prediction by the Prophet-Transformer model, mainly because they have the following key characteristics: First, time dependence, the electricity market price data has obvious time series characteristics, such as cyclical changes (Within days, weeks, seasonal changes) and trend changes, these are the advantages of the Prophet model. Conclusion

This study aims to address the problem of electricity market price prediction, which is crucial for electricity market participants and policymakers. We propose an innovative hybrid time series forecasting method that combines the Prophet model with the Transformer model and integrates their outputs through the Stacking optimization method. This method not only effectively captures the basic trends and complex correlations of prices, but also achieves significant performance improvements and provides more accurate power market price predictions. This contribution provides power market participants with a powerful tool for better decision-making and resource planning. Through extensive experiments, the study verifies the superior performance of the Prophet-Transformer model on real electricity market datasets in different geographical regions and markets. The experimental results clearly show the significant advantages of this model in performance indicators such as MAE, MAPE, RMSE and MSE. These analytical results highlight the effectiveness and potential of the approach in the field of electricity market price prediction.

This research provides power market participants with a powerful tool to better cope with market fluctuations, optimize resource allocation, and improve the operating efficiency of the power market. Through more accurate price predictions, participants can make more informed decisions, improve their decision-making processes, and improve resource utilization to achieve more sustainable electricity market operations. For example, by combining the advantages of Prophet and Transformer, the model significantly improves the accuracy and stability of price predictions, allowing market participants to quickly adjust strategies in the face of emergencies, thereby reducing economic losses. In addition, accurate forecasts support power producers and suppliers in optimizing resource allocation decisions, especially in rationally arranging renewable energy power generation plans, helping to balance supply and demand and reduce energy waste. This kind of optimization not only enhances the market competitiveness of enterprises, but also promotes the healthy development of the entire market. More importantly, improving forecast accuracy can also help governments and regulatory agencies monitor market dynamics more effectively, formulate reasonable electricity price policies and energy subsidies, and ensure the fairness and economy of power supply.

Despite the satisfactory results achieved in this study, there remains room for improvement. First, the model could be further enhanced in handling abnormal data and extreme events, which would improve its robustness. Second, the computational complexity of the models, especially the Transformer model, may limit their practical applications. Therefore, future research could focus on model lightweighting and hardware acceleration methods to improve computational efficiency. Additionally, there are plans to expand the application scope of the model, such as optimizing power market trading strategies and load dispatching in power systems. Furthermore, the integration of multi-source data, including weather data and market transaction data, deserves further exploration to advance the field of electricity market price prediction.
