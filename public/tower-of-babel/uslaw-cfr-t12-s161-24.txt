12 C.F.R. § 161.24 — Immediate family.
Citation: us:cfr:12:161.24
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-161.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 161 (Definitions For Regulations Affecting All Savings Associations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term immediate family of any natural person means the following (whether by the full or half blood or by adoption):
(a) Such person's spouse, father, mother, children, brothers, sisters, and grandchildren;
(b) The father, mother, brothers, and sisters of such person's spouse; and
(c) The spouse of a child, brother, or sister of such person.
