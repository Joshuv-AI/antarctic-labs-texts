21 U.S.C. § 213 — Fraudulent representations to evade or defeat restrictions
Citation: us:usc:21:213
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section213
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 7 (Practice Of Pharmacy And Sale Of Poisons In Consular Districts In China). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No person, firm, or corporation whose permanent allegiance is due to the United States seeking to procure in the consular districts of the United States in China any substance the sale of which is regulated by the provisions of this chapter shall make any fraudulent representations so as to evade or defeat the restrictions herein imposed.
