25 U.S.C. § 16 — Transportation of Indians in Bureau vehicles
Citation: us:usc:25:16
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section16
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 1 (Bureau Of Indian Affairs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On and after October 12, 1984, passenger carrying motor vehicles of the Bureau of Indian Affairs may be used for the transportation of Indians.
