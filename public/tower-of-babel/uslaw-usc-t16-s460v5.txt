16 U.S.C. § 460v–5 — Ashley National Forest; addition of lands of Flaming Gorge National Recreation Area
Citation: us:usc:16:460v–5
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section460v–5
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LXXX (Flaming Gorge National Recreation Area). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The boundaries of the Ashley National Forest are hereby extended to include all of the lands not presently within such boundaries lying within the recreation area as described in accordance with sections 460v and 460v–2 of this title.
