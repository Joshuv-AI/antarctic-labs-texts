28 C.F.R. § 107.80 — Relief void if procured by fraud.
Citation: us:cfr:28:107.80
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-107.80
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 107 (Relief From Disabilities Under The Gun Control Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any relief granted to an applicant pursuant to this part shall be void ab initio if the applicant willfully subscribed as true any material matter that the applicant did not believe to be true or willfully omitted any material requested information.
