50 U.S.C. § 2362 — Transfers of allocations among cooperative threat reduction programs
Citation: us:usc:50:2362
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section2362
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 40 (Defense Against Weapons Of Mass Destruction); Subchapter V (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Congress finds that—
(1)
the various Cooperative Threat Reduction programs are being carried out at different rates in the various countries covered by such programs; and
(2)
it is necessary to authorize transfers of funding allocations among the various programs in order to maximize the effectiveness of United States efforts under such programs.
