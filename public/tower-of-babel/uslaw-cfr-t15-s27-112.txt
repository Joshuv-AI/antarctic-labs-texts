15 C.F.R. § 27.112 — Review by Institution
Citation: us:cfr:15:27.112
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-27.112
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 27 (Protection Of Human Subjects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Research covered by this policy that has been approved by an IRB may be subject to further appropriate review and approval or disapproval by officials of the institution. However, those officials may not approve the research if it has not been approved by an IRB.
