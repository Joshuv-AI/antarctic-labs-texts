2 C.F.R. § 2205.306 — Cost sharing or matching.
Citation: us:cfr:2:2205.306
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-2205.306
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 2205 (Implementation Of And Exemptions To 2 Cfr). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Shared costs or matching funds must meet the criteria of 2 CFR 200.306(b), with the exception of 2 CFR 200.306(b)(5). Federal funds from other agencies may be used as match or cost sharing as authorized by 42 U.S.C. 12571(e) under the national service laws.
