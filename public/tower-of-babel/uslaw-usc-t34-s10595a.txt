34 U.S.C. § 10595a — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:34:10595a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section10595a
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 101 (Justice System Improvement); Subchapter XXIX (Grants For Family-Based Substance Abuse Treatment). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out this subchapter $10,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2019 through 2023.
(b) Use of amounts
Of the amount made available to carry out this subchapter in any fiscal year, not less than 5 percent shall be used for grants to Indian Tribes.
