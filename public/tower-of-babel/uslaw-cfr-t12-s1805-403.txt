12 C.F.R. § 1805.403 — Authority to sell.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1805.403
Creator: Community Development Financial Institutions Fund, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1805.403
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1805 (Community Development Financial Institutions Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The CDFI Fund may, at any time, sell its equity investments and loans, provided the CDFI Fund shall retain the authority to enforce the provisions of the Assistance Agreement until the performance goals specified therein have been met.
