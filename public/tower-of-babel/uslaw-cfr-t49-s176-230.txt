49 C.F.R. § 176.230 — Stowage of Division 2.1 (flammable gas) materials.
Citation: us:cfr:49:176.230
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-176.230
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 176 (Carriage By Vessel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Division 2.1 (flammable gas) materials transported in Specification 106A or 110A multi-unit car tanks must be stowed on deck only, and must be protected from sources of heat.
