31 C.F.R. § 19.430 — How do I check to see if a person is excluded or disqualified?
Citation: us:cfr:31:19.430
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-19.430
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 19 (Governmentwide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You check to see if a person is excluded or disqualified in two ways:
(a) You as an agency official must check the EPLS when you take any action listed in § 19.425.
(b) You must review information that a participant gives you, as required by § 19.335, about its status or the status of the principals of a transaction.
