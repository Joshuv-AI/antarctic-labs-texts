24 C.F.R. § 3282.551 — Scope and purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:24:3282.551
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-3282.551
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 3282 (Manufactured Home Procedural And Enforcement Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart describes the reports which shall be submitted by manufacturers, PIAs and SAAs as part of the system of enforcement established under these regulations. Additional reports described in subpart I are required when corrective actions are taken under that subpart.
