ASTRO’s Advances in Radiation Oncology Top Reviewers for 2025
Authors: Rachel B Jimenez
Published: 2026

This year, I am thrilled to honor some of the outstanding radiation oncologists, physicists, biologists, and statisticians who have generously and expertly reviewed manuscripts for our journal. As Advances in Radiation Oncology witnesses continued growth in submission numbers and scientific rigor, our reviewers have met the challenge. Many of you have given your precious time and ample skill to rigorously evaluate the novel research emerging from our field. While I can only acknowledge a small subset of our talented reviewers with this accolade, please know how grateful I am to every reviewer who has helped us sustain and elevate Advances in Radiation Oncology and the work of the talented researchers who chose to publish with us.

This year’s top reviewers remain a diverse and talented group, representing experts from multiple continents, practice settings, and specialties. I am gratified that, despite their differences, they share a selfless commitment to academic excellence and to supporting their colleagues around the globe. I hope you will recognize some of your peers among this list of valued contributors to Advances in Radiation Oncology and consider joining them as they steward valuable research toward publication.
