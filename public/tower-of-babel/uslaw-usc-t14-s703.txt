14 U.S.C. § 703 — Treasury Department
Citation: us:usc:14:703
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section703
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 7 (Cooperation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Commissioned, warrant, and petty officers of the Coast Guard are deemed to be officers of the customs and when so acting shall, insofar as performance of the duties relating to customs laws are concerned, be subject to regulations issued by the Secretary of the Treasury governing officers of the customs.
