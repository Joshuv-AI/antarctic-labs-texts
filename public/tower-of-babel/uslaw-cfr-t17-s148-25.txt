17 C.F.R. § 148.25 — Settlement.
Citation: us:cfr:17:148.25
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-148.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 148 (Implementation Of The Equal Access To Justice Act In Covered Adjudicatory Proceedings Before The Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The applicant may propose settlement of the award to the Commission before final action on the application, either in connection with a settlement of the adjudicatory proceeding, or after the adjudicatory proceeding has been concluded, in either case in accordance with § 10.108 of this chapter. If a prevailing party offers a proposed settlement of an award before an application has been filed, the application shall be filed with the proposed settlement.
