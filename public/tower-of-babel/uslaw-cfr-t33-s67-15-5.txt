33 C.F.R. § 67.15-5 — -5 Seismographic and surveying operations.
Citation: us:cfr:33:67.15-5
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-67.15-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 67 (Aids To Navigation On Artificial Islands And Fixed Structures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All stakes, casings, pipes, and buoys, except bamboo poles and wooden stakes less than 2 inches in diameter, placed in the water to facilitate seismographic or surveying operations shall be marked, in the manner prescribed by the District Commander, for the safety of navigation.
