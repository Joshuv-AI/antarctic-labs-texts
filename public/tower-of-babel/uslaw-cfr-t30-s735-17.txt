30 C.F.R. § 735.17 — Grant periods.
Citation: us:cfr:30:735.17
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-735.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 735 (Grants For Program Development And Administration And Enforcement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director or his authorized designee shall normally approve a grant for a period of one year or less. Consecutive grants shall be awarded to fund approved programs.
