2 U.S.C. § 608 — Lump-sum payments to enhance staff recruitment and to reward exceptional performance
Citation: us:usc:2:608
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section608
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 17 (Congressional Budget Office). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Director of the Congressional Budget Office shall have the authority to make lump-sum payments to enhance staff recruitment and to reward exceptional performance by an employee or a group of employees.
(b)
Subsection (a) shall apply with respect to fiscal years beginning after September 30, 1999.
