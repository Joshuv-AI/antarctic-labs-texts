32 C.F.R. § 842.113 — Reconsideration of a waiver for undue hardship.
Citation: us:cfr:32:842.113
Creator: Department Of The Air Force
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-842.113
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 842 (Administrative Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A settlement authority may reconsider its previous action on a request for waiver or compromise whether requested or not. Reconsideration is normally on the basis of new evidence or discovery of errors in the waiver submission or settlement, but can be based upon a re-evaluation of the claim by the settlement authority.
