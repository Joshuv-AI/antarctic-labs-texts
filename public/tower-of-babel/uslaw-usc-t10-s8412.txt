10 U.S.C. § 8412 — Aviation cadets: benefits
Citation: us:usc:10:8412
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8412
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Education And Training); Chapter 851 (Officer Procurement Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as provided in section 402(a) and (b) of title 37, aviation cadets or their beneficiaries are entitled to the same allowances, pensions, gratuities, and other benefits as are provided for enlisted members in pay grade E–4. While on active duty, an aviation cadet is entitled to uniforms, clothing, and equipment at the expense of the United States.
