20 C.F.R. § 340.12 — Waiver not a matter of right.
Citation: us:cfr:20:340.12
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-340.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 340 (Recovery Of Benefits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A waiver under § 340.10 or § 340.11 is not a matter of right, but is at all times within the judgment of the Board.
