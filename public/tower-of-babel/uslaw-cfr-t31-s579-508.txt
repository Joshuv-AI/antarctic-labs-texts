31 C.F.R. § 579.508 — Emergency medical services.
Citation: us:cfr:31:579.508
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-579.508
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 579 (Foreign Interference In U.S. Elections Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provision and receipt of nonscheduled emergency medical services that are otherwise prohibited by this part or any further Executive orders relating to the national emergency declared in Executive Order 13848 of September 12, 2018, are authorized.
