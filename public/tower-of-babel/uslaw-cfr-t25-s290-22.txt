25 C.F.R. § 290.22 — How does the Indian tribe ensure compliance with its tribal revenue allocation plan?
Citation: us:cfr:25:290.22
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-290.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 290 (Tribal Revenue Allocation Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must utilize or establish a tribal court system, forum or administrative process in the tribal revenue allocation plan for reviewing expenditures of net gaming revenues and explain how you will correct deficiencies.
