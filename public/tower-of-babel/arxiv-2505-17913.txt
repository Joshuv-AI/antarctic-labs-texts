Abstract
. We identify which conditions on an open normal subgroupoid S S of a locally compact, Hausdorff, étale groupoid G G with twist ℰ \mathcal{E} are necessary and sufficient for the subgroupoid’s reduced twisted C ∗ C^{*} -algebra C r ∗ ​ ( S , ℰ S ) C^{*}_{r}(S;\mathcal{E}_{S}) to be a C ∗ C^{*} -diagonal in the ambient groupoid C ∗ C^{*} -algebra C r ∗ ​ ( G , ℰ ) C^{*}_{r}(G;\mathcal{E}) . We do so by first giving an explicit description of the Weyl groupoid and Weyl twist associated to any non-traditional Cartan subalgebra, that is, a Cartan subalgebra that is induced from a non-trivial open normal subgroupoid, as studied in [ DWZ25 ] . We then combine this description with Kumjian-Renault theory to establish the necessary and sufficient conditions to get a C ∗ C^{*} -diagonal.

Introduction

This paper is concerned with open normal subgroupoids
S
of étale groupoids
G
, and the inclusions of twisted
C^{*}
-algebras
C^{*}_{r}(S;\mathcal{E}_{S})\subset C^{*}_{r}(G;\mathcal{E})
that they induce. Our focus is on the situation in which the subalgebra is Cartan, and we will determine necessary and sufficient conditions on

for this subalgebra to even be a
C^{*}
-diagonal. To motivate this endeavor, let us start by discussing the case where
S=G^{(0)}
is the groupoid’s unit space, which is the ‘traditional’ situation that was solved by Kumjian and Renault.

Since

is étale,
G^{(0)}
is an open subset, and so the map “extension by zero”
extends from

Renault showed in
[

that this subalgebra is maximal abelian in

if and only if

is effective, i.e., the interior of the isotropy is nothing more than the unit space

[

]

furthermore has the

if and only if
G
is even principal, meaning that the unit space is the entire isotropy rather than just its interior. In summary, certain topological and algebraic properties of the groupoid

are reflected in the inclusion of
C_{0}(G^{(0)})
in
C^{*}_{r}(G;\mathcal{E})

What is most remarkable about Kumjian and Renault’s work, however, is that it provides a recipe for how to reconstruct the groupoid

and twist

from the masa inclusion
C_{0}(G^{(0)})\subset C^{*}_{r}(G;\mathcal{E})

is masa, but that it satisfies two additional properties: (2) there exists a faithful conditional expectation

, induced by the map that restricts

-functions on
\mathcal{E}
to
G^{(0)}
; and (3)
C_{0}(G^{(0)})
is regular in
C^{*}_{r}(G;\mathcal{E})
, meaning that its normalizers generate the ambient groupoid
C^{*}
-algebra. These three properties combined make
C_{0}(G^{(0)})\subset C^{*}_{r}(G;\mathcal{E})
a
Cartan inclusion
, and Kumjian–Renault theory allows you to start with such a Cartan inclusion
B\subset A
and ‘reconstruct’ the twisted groupoid
\mathcal{E}\to G
that realizes
A
as its
C^{*}
-algebra and
B
as the traditional Cartan subalgebra,
C_{0}(G^{(0)})
. This paper will use this reconstruction recipe, but applied to

Cartan subalgebras:

When

is not effective, meaning that it contains an open abelian subgroupoid

that is strictly larger than

[

For each

We define a representation

The completion of

in

Example 2.6
.

Of course, the trivial twist

-algebra

-algebra

We remind the reader of a typical source for twists that is more general than the above but still tractable:

Example 2.7
.

Any (continuous,
\mathbb{T}

2

,

,

It is well known that a continuous section of a twist

realizes the twist as isomorphic to that coming from a
2

[

[

Lemma 2.8
.

Suppose there exists a continuous (set-theoretic) section
\mathfrak{s}\colon G\to\mathcal{E}
of
\pi
which satisfies
\mathfrak{s}(u)=u
for all
u\in\mathcal{U}
. For

be the unique element such that

(1)

The map
\operatorname{\mathsf{c}}_{\mathfrak{s}}\colon G^{(2)}\to\mathbb{T}
determined by the equality

\iota\bigl(\operatorname{\mathsf{c}}_{\mathfrak{s}}(g,h),u\bigr)=\mathfrak{s}(g)\mathfrak{s}(h)\mathfrak{s}(gh)^{-1}\text{ for }u=r(g)

is a continuous, normalized
2

(2.2)

\operatorname{\mathsf{c}}_{\mathfrak{s}}\bigl(\pi(e),\pi(f)\bigr)=\overline{z_{e}}\overline{z_{f}}z_{ef}.

(2)

\mathbb{T}\times_{\mathfrak{s}}G

is an isomorphism of topological groupoids with inverse

In particular,
\mathcal{E}
is abelian if and only if

is symmetric. Moreover, a twist is trivializable if and only if

Example 2.9
.

If a
2
-cocycle
\operatorname{\mathsf{c}}
is a coboundary, meaning that

is a homomorphism, so that the twist
\mathbb{T}\times_{\operatorname{\mathsf{c}}}G
is trivializable.

2.2.

Definition 2.13
