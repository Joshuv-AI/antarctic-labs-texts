7 C.F.R. § 1948.93 — Appeal procedure.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1948.93
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Rural Business-Cooperative Service, And Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1948.93
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1948 (Rural Development). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any grantee or applicant for Rural Development assistance under this subpart who has been directly and adversely affected by an administrative decision by Rural Development may appeal such decision in accordance with RD Instruction 1900-B.
