20 C.F.R. § 320.20 — Powers of hearings officer.
Citation: us:cfr:20:320.20
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-320.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 320 (Initial Determinations Under The Railroad Unemployment Insurance Act And Reviews Of And Appeals From Such Determinations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the development of an appeal, the hearings officer shall have the power to hold hearings, require and compel the attendance of witnesses, administer oaths, take testimony, and make all necessary investigations.
