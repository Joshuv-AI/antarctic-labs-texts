25 C.F.R. § 115.713 — When does money in a trust account start earning interest?
Citation: us:cfr:25:115.713
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-115.713
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 115 (Trust Funds For Tribes And Individual Indians). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Funds must remain on deposit at least one business day before interest is earned. Interest earnings of less than one cent are not credited to any account.
