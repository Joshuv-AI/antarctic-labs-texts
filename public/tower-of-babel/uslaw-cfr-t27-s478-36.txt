27 C.F.R. § 478.36 — Transfer or possession of machine guns.
Citation: us:cfr:27:478.36
Creator: Bureau Of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms, And Explosives, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-478.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 478 (Commerce In Firearms And Ammunition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person shall transfer or possess a machine gun except:
(a) A transfer to or by, or possession by or under the authority of, the United States, or any department or agency thereof, or a State, or a department, agency, or political subdivision thereof. (See Part 479 of this chapter); or
(b) Any lawful transfer or lawful possession of a machine gun that was lawfully possessed before May 19, 1986 (See Part 479 of this chapter).
