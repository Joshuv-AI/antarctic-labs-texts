2 U.S.C. § 1a — Election to be certified by governor
Citation: us:usc:2:1a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1a
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 1 (Election Of Senators And Representatives). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It shall be the duty of the executive of the State from which any Senator has been chosen to certify his election, under the seal of the State, to the President of the Senate of the United States.
