46 C.F.R. § 199.07 — Additional equipment and requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:46:199.07
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-199.07
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 199 (Lifesaving Systems For Certain Inspected Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The OCMI may require a vessel to carry specialized or additional lifesaving equipment other than as required in this part if the OCMI determines that the conditions of a voyage present uniquely hazardous circumstances that are not adequately addressed by existing requirements.
