41 C.F.R. § 102-40.30 — -40.30 Transfer and donation.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-40.30
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-40.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-40 (40—Utilization And Disposition Of Personal Property With Special Handling Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Personal property requiring special handling is generally available for transfer or donation. Surplus personal property identified as hazardous material not required for transfer as excess personal property to Federal agencies should normally be made available for donation.
