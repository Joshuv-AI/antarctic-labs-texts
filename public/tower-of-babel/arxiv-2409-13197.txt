I = 2 I=2 π ​ π \pi\pi s s -wave scattering length from lattice QCD
Ziwen Fu, Wang Jun

Abstract
The I = 2 I=2 π ​ π \pi\pi elastic s s -wave scattering phase shift is measured by lattice QCD with N f = 3 N_{f}=3 flavors of the Asqtad-improved staggered fermions. The lattice-calculated energy-eigenvalues of π ​ π \pi\pi systems at one center of mass frame and some moving frames using the moving wall source technique are utilized to secure phase shifts by Lüscher’s formula. Our computations are fine enough to obtain threshold parameters: scattering length a a , effective range r r , and shape parameter P P , which can be extrapolated at the physical point by NLO in chiral perturbation theory, and our relevant NNLO predictions from expanding NPLQCD’s works are novelly considered as the systematic uncertainties. Our outcomes are consistent with Roy equation determinations, newer experimental data, and lattice estimations. Numerical computations are performed with a coarse ( a ≈ 0.12 a\approx 0.12 fm, L 3 ​ T = 32 3 ​ 64 L^{3}T=32^{3}64 ), two fine ( a ≈ 0.09 a\approx 0.09 fm, L 3 ​ T = 40 3 ​ 96 L^{3}T=40^{3}96 ) and a superfine ( a ≈ 0.06 a\approx 0.06 fm, L 3 ​ T = 48 3 ​ 144 L^{3}T=48^{3}144 ) lattice ensembles at four pion masses of m π ∼ 247 ​ MeV m_{\pi}\sim 247~{\rm MeV} , 249 ​ MeV 249~{\rm MeV} , 275 ​ MeV 275~{\rm MeV} , and 384 ​ MeV 384~{\rm MeV} , respectively.

II.1
Center of mass frame

In the center-of-mass (CoM) frame, the energy levels of two free pions are provided by

E=2\sqrt{m_{\pi}^{2}+|{\mathbf{p}}|^{2}},

where
{\mathbf{p}}=\tfrac{2\pi}{L}{\mathbf{n}}
, and
{\mathbf{n}}\in\mathbb{Z}^{3}
.
The lowest energy
E
for
{\mathbf{n}}\neq 0

is beyond the
t
-channel cut, which starts at
{k^{2}}={m_{\pi}^{2}}

[
9
]
.

The energy levels of
\pi\pi
system are shifted
by the hadronic interaction from
E
to
\overline{E}
,

\overline{E}=2\sqrt{m_{\pi}^{2}+k^{2}},\quad k=\frac{2\pi}{L}q,

where the dimensionless scattering momentum
q\in\mathbb{R}
.
It is the Lüscher formula that relates the energy
\overline{E}

to the
s
-wave
\pi\pi
scattering phase

\delta

[
47
,
48
]
,

k\cot\delta(k)=\frac{2}{L\sqrt{\pi}}{\mathcal{Z}_{00}(1;q^{2})},

(2)

where the zeta function is formally defined by

\mathcal{Z}_{00}(s;q^{2})=\frac{1}{\sqrt{4\pi}}\sum_{{\mathbf{n}}\in\mathbb{Z}^{3}}\frac{1}{\left(|{\mathbf{n}}|^{2}-q^{2}\right)^{s}}.

(3)

The zeta function
\mathcal{Z}_{00}(s;q^{2})

can be efficiently evaluated by the method described in Refs.
[
57
,
58
]
.
