46 C.F.R. § 167.01-10 — -10 Effective date of regulations.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 167 (Public Nautical School Ships). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The regulations in this part shall be in effect on and after July 1, 1951: Provided, That amendments, revisions, or additions shall become effective 90 days after the date of publication in the Federal Register unless the Commandant shall fix a different time.
(b) Amendments to regulations in this part will not be retroactive in effect unless specifically made so at the time the amendments are issued.
