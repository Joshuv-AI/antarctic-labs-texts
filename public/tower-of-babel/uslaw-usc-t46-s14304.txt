46 U.S.C. § 14304 — Remeasurement
Citation: us:usc:46:14304
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section14304
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part J (Measurement Of Vessels); Chapter 143 (Convention Measurement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
To the extent necessary, the Secretary shall remeasure a vessel to which this chapter applies if—
(1)
the Secretary or the owner alleges an error in its measurement; or
(2)
the vessel or the use of its space is changed in a way that substantially affects its tonnage.
(b)
Except as provided in this chapter or section 14504 of this title, a vessel that has been measured does not have to be remeasured to obtain another document or endorsement under chapter 121 of this title.
