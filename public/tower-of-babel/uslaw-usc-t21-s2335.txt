21 U.S.C. § 2335 — Exception relating to importation of goods
Citation: us:usc:21:2335
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section2335
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 28 (Sanctions With Respect To Foreign Traffickers Of Illicit Synthetic Opioids); Subchapter II (Other Matters). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The authorities and requirements to impose sanctions under this chapter shall not include the authority or a requirement to impose sanctions on the importation of goods.
(b) Good defined
In this section, the term “good” means any article, natural or manmade substance, material, supply, or manufactured product, including inspection and test equipment, and excluding technical data.
