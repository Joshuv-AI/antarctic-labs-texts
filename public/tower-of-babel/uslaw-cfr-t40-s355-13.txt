40 C.F.R. § 355.13 — How do I calculate the quantity of an extremely hazardous substance present in mixtures?
Citation: us:cfr:40:355.13
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-355.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 355 (Emergency Planning And Notification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an EHS is present in a mixture in a particular container, determine the quantity (in pounds) of the EHS in that container by multiplying the concentration of the EHS (in weight percent) by the weight (in pounds) of the mixture in the container.
