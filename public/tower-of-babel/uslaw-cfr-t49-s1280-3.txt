49 C.F.R. § 1280.3 — Authority to classify.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1280.3
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1280.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1280 (Handling Of National Security Information And Classified Material). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board does not have authority of its own to classify any of its internally generated documents. The only documents handled by the Board which are classified as confidential, secret, or top secret are those generated by Executive Branch Agencies with original classification authority.
