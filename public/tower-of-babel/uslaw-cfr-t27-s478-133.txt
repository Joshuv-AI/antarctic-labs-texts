27 C.F.R. § 478.133 — Records of transactions in semiautomatic assault weapons.
Citation: us:cfr:27:478.133
Creator: Bureau Of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms, And Explosives, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-478.133
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 478 (Commerce In Firearms And Ammunition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The evidence specified in § 478.40(c), relating to transactions in semiautomatic assault weapons, shall be retained in the permanent records of the manufacturer or dealer and in the records of the licensee to whom the weapons are transferred.
