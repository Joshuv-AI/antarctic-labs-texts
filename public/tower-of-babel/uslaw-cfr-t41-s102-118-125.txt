41 C.F.R. § 102-118.125 — -118.125 Controlling GBL and GTR forms.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-118.125
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-118.125
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-118 (118—Transportation Payment And Audit). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies—
(a) Are responsible for the physical control, use, and accountability of GBLs and GTRs and must have procedures in place to track, manage, and account for these documents when necessary.
(b) Must assign each form a unique sequential tracking number.
(c) Must hold employees accountable for the issuance and use of the forms.
