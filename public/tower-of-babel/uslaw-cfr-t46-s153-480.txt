46 C.F.R. § 153.480 — Stripping quantity for Category B and C NLS tanks on ships built after June 30, 1986: Categories B and C.
Citation: us:cfr:46:153.480
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-153.480
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 153 (Ships Carrying Bulk Liquid, Liquefied Gas, Or Compressed Gas Hazardous Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless waived under § 153.491, Category B and C NLS cargo tanks on each ship built after June 30, 1986 must have stripping quantities determined under § 153.1604 that are less than—
(a) 0.15 m 3 if Category B; and
(b) 0.35 m 3 if Category C.
