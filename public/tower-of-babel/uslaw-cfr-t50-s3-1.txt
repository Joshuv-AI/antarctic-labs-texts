50 C.F.R. § 3.1 — Discrimination prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:50:3.1
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-3.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 3 (Nondiscrimination—Contracts, Permits, And Use Of Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person shall, on the ground of race, color, creed, or national origin, be excluded from participation in, be denied the benefits of, or be otherwise subjected to any form of discrimination or segregation under any program or activity, or in the use of any facility or accommodation of the Service.
