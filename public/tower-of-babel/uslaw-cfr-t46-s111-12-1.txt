46 C.F.R. § 111.12-1 — -1 Prime movers.
Citation: us:cfr:46:111.12-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-111.12-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 111 (Electric Systems—General Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Prime movers must meet § 58.01-5 and subpart 58.10 of this chapter except that those for mobile offshore drilling units must meet 6-1-3/3.3 and 6-1-3/3.5 of the ABS MOU Rules (incorporated by reference; see § 110.10-1 of this subchapter). Further requirements for emergency generator prime movers are in subpart 112.50 of this subchapter.
