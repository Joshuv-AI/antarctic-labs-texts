23 C.F.R. § 490.801 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:23:490.801
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-490.801
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 490 (National Performance Management Measures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this subpart is to implement the requirements of 23 U.S.C. 150(c)(5)(B) to establish performance measures for State DOTs and the MPOs to use in assessing on-road mobile source emissions.
