43 C.F.R. § 2720.4 — Issuance of document of conveyance.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2720.4
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2720.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2720 (Conveyance Of Federally-Owned Mineral Interests). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon receipt of the payment required by § 2720.3 of this subpart, if any is required, the authorized officer shall issue the necessary document conveying to the applicant the mineral interests of the United States in the land covered by the application.
