15 C.F.R. § 930.80 — Federal permitting agency responsibility.
Citation: us:cfr:15:930.80
Creator: National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-930.80
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 930 (Federal Consistency With Approved Coastal Management Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Following receipt of a State agency objection to a consistency certification related to federal license or permit activities described in detail in an OCS plan, the Federal agency shall not issue any of such licenses or permits except as provided in subpart H of this part.
