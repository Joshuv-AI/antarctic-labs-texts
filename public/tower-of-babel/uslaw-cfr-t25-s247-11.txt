25 C.F.R. § 247.11 — What other restrictions apply to use of the sites?
Citation: us:cfr:25:247.11
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-247.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 247 (Use Of Columbia River Treaty Fishing Access Sites). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Area Director may prescribe and post at the sites regulations covering:
(a) Camping;
(b) Picnicking;
(c) Use of alcoholic beverages;
(d) Setting or use of fires;
(e) Use of the sites for cleaning fish;
(f) Deposit of garbage, paper, cans, bottles, or rubbish of any kind; or
(g) Use of the sites for any commercial activity (including commercial purchase of fish).
