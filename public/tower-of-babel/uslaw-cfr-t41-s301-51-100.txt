41 C.F.R. § 301-51.100 — -51.100 Payment methods to procure common carrier transportation.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-51.100
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-51.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-51 (51—Paying Travel Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees must use a Government contractor-issued individually billed travel charge card, centrally billed account, GTR, or other method of payment authorized in accordance with their agency's internal policy to procure common carrier transportation.
