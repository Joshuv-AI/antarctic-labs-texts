24 C.F.R. § 1000.208 — What happens if an Indian tribe had two IHAs as of September 30, 1996?
Citation: us:cfr:24:1000.208
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-1000.208
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 1000 (Native American Housing Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Indian tribes which had established and were operating two IHAs as of September 30, 1996, under the 1937 Act shall be allowed to form and operate two TDHEs under NAHASDA. Nothing in this section shall affect the allocation of funds otherwise due to an Indian tribe under the formula.
