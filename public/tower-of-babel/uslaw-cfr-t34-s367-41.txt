34 C.F.R. § 367.41 — How does the Secretary evaluate an application for a discretionary grant?
Citation: us:cfr:34:367.41
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-367.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 367 (Independent Living Services For Older Individuals Who Are Blind). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Secretary evaluates an application for a discretionary grant based on the selection criteria chosen from the general selection criteria found in EDGAR regulations at 34 CFR 75.210.
(b) In addition to the selection criteria, the Secretary considers the geographic distribution of projects in making an award.
