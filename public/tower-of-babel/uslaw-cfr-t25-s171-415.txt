25 C.F.R. § 171.415 — Can I place an obstruction on a BIA irrigation project?
Citation: us:cfr:25:171.415
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-171.415
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 171 (Irrigation Operation And Maintenance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. You may not place obstructions on BIA irrigation projects.
(a) If you do so, we will notify you in writing that you must remove it.
(b) If you do not remove your obstruction in compliance with our notice, we will remove it and we will bill you for our costs.
(c) We can remove your obstruction without notice because of an urgency we have identified.
