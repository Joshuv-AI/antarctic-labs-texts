33 C.F.R. § 127.1403 — Inspections.
Citation: us:cfr:33:127.1403
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-127.1403
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 127 (Waterfront Facilities Handling Liquefied Natural Gas And Liquefied Hazardous Gas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each operator of a waterfront facility handling LHG must conduct a visual inspection for defects of each pressure relief device not capable of being tested.
(b) The operator must conduct the inspection required by paragraph (a) of this section at least once each calendar year, with intervals between inspections not exceeding 15 months.
