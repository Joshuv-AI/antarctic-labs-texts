31 U.S.C. § 3514 — Discontinuing certain accounts maintained by the Comptroller General
Citation: us:usc:31:3514
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section3514
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 35 (Accounting And Collection); Subchapter II (Accounting Requirements, Systems, And Information). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Comptroller General may discontinue an agency appropriation, expenditure, limitation, receipt, or personal ledger account maintained by the Comptroller General when the Comptroller General believes that the accounting system and internal controls of the agency will allow the Comptroller General to carry out the functions related to the account.
