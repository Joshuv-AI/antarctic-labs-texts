48 C.F.R. § 243.205-71 — 243.205-71 Requests for equitable adjustment.
Citation: us:cfr:48:243.205-71
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-243.205-71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 243 (Contract Modifications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Use the clause at 252.243-7002, Requests for Equitable Adjustment, in solicitations and contracts, including solicitations and contracts using FAR part 12 procedures for the acquisition of commercial products and commercial services, that are estimated to exceed the simplified acquisition threshold.
