40 C.F.R. § 61.192 — Standard.
Citation: us:cfr:40:61.192
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-61.192
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 61 (National Emission Standards For Hazardous Air Pollutants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No source at a Department of Energy facility shall emit more than 20 picocuries per square meter per second (pCi/(m 2-sec)) (1.9 pCi/(ft 2-sec)) of radon-222 as an average for the entire source, into the air. This requirement will be part of any Federal Facilities Agreement reached between Environmental Protection Agency and Department of Energy.
