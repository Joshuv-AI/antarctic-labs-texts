14 C.F.R. § 323.10 — Time for filing objections.
Citation: us:cfr:14:323.10
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-323.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 323 (Terminations, Suspensions, And Reductions Of Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Objections shall be filed not later than:
(1) 12 days from the date of filing of a 30-day notice;
(2) 15 days from the date of filing of a 60-day notice; or
(3) 20 days from the date of filing of a 90-day notice.
(b) The Department may accept late-filed objections, upon motion, for good cause shown.
(c) Whenever a notice has been filed earlier than required under § 323.5, the Department may extend the time for filing an objection to that notice.
