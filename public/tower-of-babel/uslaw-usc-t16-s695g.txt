16 U.S.C. § 695g — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:695g
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section695g
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 6 (Game And Bird Preserves; Protection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are hereby authorized to be appropriated such funds, not to exceed $400,000, for construction of necessary works to supply water for State and federally owned and operated waterfowl management areas in the San Joaquin Valley to carry out the purposes of sections 695d to 695j–1 of this title.
