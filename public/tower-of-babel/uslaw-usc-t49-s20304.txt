49 U.S.C. § 20304 — Assumption of risk by employees
Citation: us:usc:49:20304
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section20304
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Safety); Chapter 203 (Safety Appliances). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An employee of a railroad carrier injured by a vehicle or train used in violation of section 20302(a)(1)(A), (2), (4), or (5)(A) of this title does not assume the risk of injury resulting from the violation, even if the employee continues to be employed by the carrier after learning of the violation.
