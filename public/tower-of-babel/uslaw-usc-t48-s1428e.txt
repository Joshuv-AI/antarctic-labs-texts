48 U.S.C. § 1428e — Audit of books and records of agency, or agencies, administering loan funds
Citation: us:usc:48:1428e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1428e
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 8A (Guam); Subchapter VI (Guam Development Fund). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Comptroller General of the United States, or any of his duly authorized representatives, shall have access, for the purpose of audit and examination, to the books, documents, papers, and records of the agency, or agencies, of the government of Guam administering the plan that are pertinent to the funds received under this subchapter.
