43 U.S.C. § 2246 — Report
Citation: us:usc:43:2246
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section2246
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 40 (Reclamation States Emergency Drought Relief); Subchapter III (General And Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There shall be included as part of the President’s annual budget submittal to the Congress a detailed report on past and proposed expenditures and accomplishments under this chapter.
