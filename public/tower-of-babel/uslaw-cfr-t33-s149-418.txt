33 C.F.R. § 149.418 — What fire protection system must a helicopter fueling facility have?
Citation: us:cfr:33:149.418
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-149.418
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 149 (Deepwater Ports: Design, Construction, And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to the portable fire extinguishers required under Table 149.409 of this part, each helicopter fueling facility must have a fire protection system complying with 46 CFR 108.489.
