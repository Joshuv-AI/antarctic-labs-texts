Correction
Published: 2025

Bu, L. L., Yu, G. T., Deng, W. W., Mao, L., Liu, J. F., Ma, S. R., … Sun, Z. J. (2016). Targeting STAT3 signaling reduces immunosuppressive myeloid cells in head and neck squamous cell carcinoma. OncoImmunology, 5(5). https://doi.org/10.1080/2162402X.2015.1130206

Following a thorough review of the published article, the authors identified that there was an inadvertent misuse of images in the publication. Specifically, the immunohistochemical staining in Figure 3 G of p-STAT3 in the S3I-201 treatment group was incorrectly included. This error occurred during the preparation of the manuscript and unfortunately went unnoticed prior to publication. The corrected figure is displayed below.

S3I-201-induced STAT3 signaling inhibition delays tumorigenesis in Tgfbr1/Pten2cKO mice. (G) Immunohistochemical staining indicated increased p-STAT3Tyr705 expression in Tgfbr1/Pten2cKO mice compared with S3I-201 treatment group.

The authors have confirmed that this mistake does not affect the conclusions of this article and apologise for any confusion this error may have caused.
