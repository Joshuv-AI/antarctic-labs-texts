16 U.S.C. § 3506 — Certification of compliance
Citation: us:usc:16:3506
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section3506
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 55 (Coastal Barrier Resources). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Regulations
Not later than 1 year after November 25, 2024, the head of each Federal agency affected by this chapter shall revise or issue regulations and guidance as necessary to ensure compliance with the provisions of this chapter.
(b) Certification
The head of each Federal agency affected by this chapter shall report and certify that each such agency is in compliance with the provisions of this chapter. Such reports and certifications shall be submitted annually to the Committees and the Secretary.
