36 U.S.C. § 80103 — Constitution and bylaws
Citation: us:usc:36:80103
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section80103
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 801 (General Federation Of Women’S Clubs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation shall have a constitution and may adopt bylaws for the admission and qualifications of members, the management of its property, and the regulation of its affairs. The corporation may amend its constitution and bylaws.
