Abstract
We have examined the cosmological actions of LRS ( Locally Rationally Symmetric ) Bianchi type-I universe model in f ⁡ ( R , T ψ ) f(R,T^{\psi}) gravity. For this, we have estimate Hubble parameter, effective equation of state parameter ( ω eff \omega^{\text{eff}} ) and potential of scalar field as a function of time using equation H = W ⁡ ( ψ ) H=W(\psi) . The graphical representation of potential function V ⁡ ( ψ ) V(\psi) with respect to cosmic time t t is described. This study explores the dynamical properties of a Bianchi Type-I universe by utilizing Bayesian statistical techniques to constrain the model parameters and evaluate the viability of anisotropic cosmology under extended matter-geometry couplings. Also, we have applied Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) mechanism on derived H ⁡ ( z ) H(z) model by using observational Hubble data (OHD), Baryon Acoustic Oscillation (BAO) dataset and Pantheon dataset. From the confidence–level contours and best–fit parameter values obtained, along with the corresponding reduced χ 2 \chi^{2} , it is evident that the model aligns strongly with observational data, demonstrating statistical stability and consistency in describing late–time cosmic acceleration. Likewise, the error analyses presented in this research, including a comparison between the Λ \Lambda CDM cosmology and the reconstructed H ⁡ ( z ) H(z) model, confirm the model’s compatibility with current observations by yielding a reliable and accurate account of the universe’s expansion history.
