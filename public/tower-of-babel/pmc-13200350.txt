Correction: Repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation influences cognitive-motor dual-task performance in children with cerebral palsy: a randomized controlled study
Authors: Jingyi Zhao, Tingting Peng, Liru Liu, Lu He, Jingbo Zhang, Yuan Zhang, Qingfen Hou, Hongyu Zhou, Xubo Yang, Kaishou Xu
Published: 2026

Correction: Journal of NeuroEngineering and Rehabilitation (2025) 22:169

10.1186/s12984-025-01702-z

A sentence in the “Experimental design and blinding” heading under the Methods section of our article [1].

The current sentence beginning with “Participants to group assignment and … approximately the same time daily.” is incorrect. The corrected sentence should read:

“Participants were randomized to groups and received the same stimulation protocol for 3 consecutive days, with each stimulation implemented at approximately the same time daily.”

Also, in the “Results” section, under the heading “The effects of rTMS on dual-task performance,” there is an error in the following sentence:

“Post hoc tests used Bonferroni correction ………. day 1 (F = 2.451, corrected p = 0.366).”

The corrected sentence should read:

“Post hoc tests used Bonferroni correction showed differences on day 2 (F = 6.617, corrected p = 0.036) and day 3 (F = 11.451, corrected p = 0.003), but not on day 1 (F = 2.451, corrected p = 0.366).”

The original article has been corrected.
