18 U.S.C. § 1011 — Federal land bank mortgage transactions
Citation: us:usc:18:1011
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1011
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 47 (Fraud And False Statements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever, being a mortgagee, knowingly makes any false statement in any paper, proposal, or letter, relating to the sale of any mortgage, to any Federal land bank; or
Whoever, being an appraiser, willfully over-values any land securing such mortgage—
Shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than one year, or both.
