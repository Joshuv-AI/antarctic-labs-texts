25 U.S.C. § 58 — Limitation on number and kind of employment
Citation: us:usc:25:58
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section58
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 2 (Officers Of Indian Affairs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The number and kind of employees at each agency shall be prescribed by the Secretary of the Interior and none other shall be employed.
