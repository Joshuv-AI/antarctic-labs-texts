16 C.F.R. § 1502.39 — Examination of record.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1502.39
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1502.39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1502 (Procedures For Formal Evidentiary Public Hearing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in § 1502.3, documents in the record will be publicly available. Documents available for examination or copying will be placed on public display in the Office of the Secretary promptly upon receipt in that office.
