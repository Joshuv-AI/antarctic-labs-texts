40 C.F.R. § 57.505 — Amendments of the NSO.
Citation: us:cfr:40:57.505
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-57.505
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 57 (Primary Nonferrous Smelter Orders). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An NSO shall be amended within three months of submission of any report required under § 57.504 so as to require additional fugitive emission control measures if such report establishes that such additional measures are necessary to assure that the NAAQS will be maintained with a reasonable degree of reliability.
