2 U.S.C. § 5 — Nominations for Representatives at large
Citation: us:usc:2:5
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section5
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 1 (Election Of Senators And Representatives). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Candidates for Representative or Representatives to be elected at large in any State shall be nominated in the same manner as candidates for governor, unless otherwise provided by the laws of such State.
