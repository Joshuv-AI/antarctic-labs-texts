45 C.F.R. § 2525.210 — For what purposes may an education award be used?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2525.210
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2525.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2525 (National Service Trust). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An education award may be used to pay educational expenses and/or to repay qualified student loans, as defined in § 2525.2;
(b) An education award is divisible and may be applied to any combination of loans, costs, or expenses described in paragraph (a) of this section.
