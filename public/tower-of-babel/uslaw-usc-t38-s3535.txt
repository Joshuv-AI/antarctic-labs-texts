38 U.S.C. § 3535 — Approval of courses
Citation: us:usc:38:3535
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section3535
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part III (Readjustment And Related Benefits); Chapter 35 (Survivors’ And Dependents’ Educational Assistance); Subchapter IV (Payments To Eligible Persons). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An eligible person shall receive the benefits of this chapter while enrolled in a course of education offered by an educational institution only if such course (1) is approved in accordance with the provisions of subchapter I 1 of chapter 36 of this title, or (2) is approved for the enrollment of the particular individual under the provisions of section 3536 of this title.
