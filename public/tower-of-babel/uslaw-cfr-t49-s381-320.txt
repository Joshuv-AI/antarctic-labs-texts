49 C.F.R. § 381.320 — How long will it take the agency to respond to my application for an exemption?
Citation: us:cfr:49:381.320
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-381.320
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 381 (Waivers, Exemptions, And Pilot Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agency will attempt to issue a final decision within 180 days of the date it receives your application. However, if you leave out important details or other information necessary for the FMCSA to prepare a meaningful request for public comments, the agency will attempt to issue a final decision within 180 days of the date it receives the additional information.
