54 U.S.C. § 311105 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:54:311105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section311105
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3111 (Preserve America Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out this chapter $25,000,000 for each fiscal year, to remain available until expended.
