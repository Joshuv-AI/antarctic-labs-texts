42 U.S.C. § 300x–61 — Solicitation of views of certain entities
Citation: us:usc:42:300x–61
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section300x–61
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter XVII (Block Grants); Part B (Block Grants Regarding Mental Health And Substance Use); Subpart iii (General Provisions).
