20 C.F.R. § 369.2 — Authority to grant written permission for use of the seal.
Citation: us:cfr:20:369.2
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-369.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 369 (Use Of The Seal Of The Railroad Retirement Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board hereby delegates authority to grant written permission for the use of the seal of the Railroad Retirement Board to the Director of Administration.
