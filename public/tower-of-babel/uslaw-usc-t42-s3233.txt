42 U.S.C. § 3233 — Authorization of appropriations for disaster economic recovery activities
Citation: us:usc:42:3233
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section3233
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 38 (Public Works And Economic Development); Subchapter VII (Funding). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
In addition to amounts made available under section 3231 of this title, there are authorized to be appropriated such sums as are necessary to carry out section 3149(c)(2) of this title, to remain available until expended.
(b) Federal share
The Federal share of the cost of activities funded with amounts made available under subsection (a) shall be up to 100 percent.
