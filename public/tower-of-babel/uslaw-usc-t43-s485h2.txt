43 U.S.C. § 485h–2 — Amendments to existing contracts
Citation: us:usc:43:485h–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section485h–2
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 12 (Reclamation And Irrigation Of Lands By Federal Government); Subchapter X (Payment Of Construction Charges). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to negotiate amendments to existing contracts entered into pursuant to subsection (e) of section 485h of this title to conform said contracts to the provisions of sections 485h–1 to 485h–5 of this title.
