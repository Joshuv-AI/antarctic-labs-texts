5 U.S.C. § 10302 — Consulting services for the Department of State
Citation: us:usc:5:10302
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section10302
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart I (Miscellaneous); Chapter 103 (Department Of State). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any consulting service obtained by the Department of State through procurement contract pursuant to section 3109 of title 5, United States Code, shall be limited to those contracts with respect to which expenditures are a matter of public record and available for public inspection, except if otherwise provided under existing law, or under existing Executive order issued pursuant to existing law.
