29 C.F.R. § 1917.158 — Prohibited operations.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1917.158
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1917.158
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1917 (Marine Terminals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Spray painting and abrasive blasting operations shall not be conducted in the vicinity of cargo handling operations.
(b) Welding and burning operations shall not be conducted in the vicinity of cargo handling operations unless such hot work is part of the cargo operation.
