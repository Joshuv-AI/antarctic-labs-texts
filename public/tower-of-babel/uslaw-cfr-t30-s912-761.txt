30 C.F.R. § 912.761 — Areas designated unsuitable for surface coal mining by act of Congress.
Citation: us:cfr:30:912.761
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-912.761
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 912 (Idaho). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Part 761 of this chapter, Areas Designated by Act of Congress, shall apply to surface coal mining and reclamation operations.
