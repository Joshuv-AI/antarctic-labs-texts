40 C.F.R. § 60.2982 — Is a state plan required for all States?
Citation: us:cfr:40:60.2982
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.2982
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 60 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No, you are not required to submit a state plan if there are no existing incineration units that are an OSWI unit as defined in §§ 60.2992 and 60.3078 or air curtain incinerators subject to this subpart as described in § 60.2994 in your state and you submit a negative declaration letter in place of the state plan.
