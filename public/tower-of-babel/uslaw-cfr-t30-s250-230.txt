30 C.F.R. § 250.230 — When are you required to submit an I3P Report?
Citation: us:cfr:30:250.230
Creator: Bureau Of Safety And Environmental Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-250.230
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 250 (Oil And Gas And Sulphur Operations In The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must submit to BSEE any I3P reports required in § 250.232 for any equipment identified in your New or Unusual Technology Barrier Equipment Conceptual Plan and when required by the Regional Supervisor. BSEE will not approve your associated Conceptual Plan until BSEE reviews the required I3P Reports.
