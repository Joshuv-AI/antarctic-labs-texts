19 C.F.R. § 176.0 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:19:176.0
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-176.0
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 176 (Proceedings In The Court Of International Trade). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part deals with service of summons and notice of appeal in actions before the Court of International Trade, the transmission of records to the court, statements of agreed facts, and Customs procedures following a decision by the court.
