15 C.F.R. § 995.27 — Format validation software testing.
Citation: us:cfr:15:995.27
Creator: National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-995.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 995 (Certification Requirements For Distributors Of Noaa Hydrographic Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Tests shall be performed verifying, as far as reasonable and practicable, that CEVAD's data testing software performs the checks, as specified by CEVAD, for verifying that the converted data conforms to its own proprietary product specification. These tests may be combined with testing of the conversion software.
