20 U.S.C. § 2412 — Prohibition on use of funds to induce out-of-State relocation of businesses
Citation: us:usc:20:2412
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section2412
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 44 (Career And Technical Education); Subchapter II (General Provisions); Part B (State Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No funds provided under this chapter shall be used for the purpose of directly providing incentives or inducements to an employer to relocate a business enterprise from one State to another State if such relocation will result in a reduction in the number of jobs available in the State where the business enterprise is located before such incentives or inducements are offered.
