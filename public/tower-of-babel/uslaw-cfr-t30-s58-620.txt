30 C.F.R. § 58.620 — Drill dust control.
Citation: us:cfr:30:58.620
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-58.620
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 58 (Health Standards For Metal And Nonmetal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Holes shall be collared and drilled wet, or other effective dust control measures shall be used, when drilling non-water-soluble material. Effective dust control measures shall be used when drilling water-soluble materials.
