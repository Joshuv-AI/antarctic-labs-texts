29 C.F.R. § 570.8 — Issuance of a Federal certificate of age.
Citation: us:cfr:29:570.8
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-570.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 570 (Child Labor Regulations, Orders And Statements Of Interpretation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Federal certificate of age which shall have the effect specified in § 570.5 shall be issued by a person authorized by the Administrator of the Wage and Hour Division and shall be issued in accordance with the provisions of §§ 570.6 and 570.7.
