7 U.S.C. § 2710 — Applicability of provisions to amendments to orders
Citation: us:usc:7:2710
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2710
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 60 (Egg Research And Consumer Information). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of this chapter applicable to orders shall be applicable to amendments to orders.
