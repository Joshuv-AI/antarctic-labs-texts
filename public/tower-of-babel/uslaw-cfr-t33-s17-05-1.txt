33 C.F.R. § 17.05-1 — -1 Gifts.
Citation: us:cfr:33:17.05-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-17.05-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 17 (United States Coast Guard General Gift Fund). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The gifts or bequests may be in money or negotiable instrument form. If in the form of a money order, check, etc., it should be made payable to the Treasurer of the United States.
