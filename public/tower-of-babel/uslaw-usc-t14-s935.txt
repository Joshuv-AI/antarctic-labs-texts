14 U.S.C. § 935 — Coast Guard band recordings for commercial sale
Citation: us:usc:14:935
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section935
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 9 (Administration); Subchapter II (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Coast Guard band may produce recordings for commercial sale.
(b)
Amounts received as proceeds from the sale of any such recordings may be credited to applicable appropriations of the Coast Guard for expenses of the Coast Guard band.
(c)
The Secretary shall prescribe regulations governing the accounting of such proceeds.
