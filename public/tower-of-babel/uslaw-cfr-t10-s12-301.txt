10 C.F.R. § 12.301 — Filing and service of documents.
Citation: us:cfr:10:12.301
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-12.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 12 (Implementation Of The Equal Access To Justice Act In Agency Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any application for an award or other pleading or document related to an application shall be filed and served on all parties to the proceeding in the same manner as other pleadings in the proceeding, except as provided in § 12.202(b) for confidential financial information.
