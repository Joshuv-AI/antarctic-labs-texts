21 C.F.R. § 522.1289 — Lufenuron.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 522 (Implantation Or Injectable Dosage Form New Animal Drugs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Specifications. Each milliliter of suspension contains 100 milligrams (mg) of lufenuron.
(b) Sponsor. See No. 058198 in § 510.600(c) of this chapter.
(c) Conditions of use in cats—(1) Amount. 10 mg per kilogram (4.5 mg per pound) of body weight every 6 months, by subcutaneous injection.
(2) Indications for use. For control of flea populations in cats 6 weeks of age and older.
(3) Limitations. Federal law restricts this drug to use by or on the order of a licensed veterinarian.
