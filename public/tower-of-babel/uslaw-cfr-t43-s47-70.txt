43 C.F.R. § 47.70 — How does the Chairman complete the exchange once approved?
Citation: us:cfr:43:47.70
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-47.70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 47 (Land Exchange Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Chairman completes the exchange in accordance with the requirements of State law.
(b) The Chairman shall provide a title report to the Secretary as evidence of the completed exchange.
