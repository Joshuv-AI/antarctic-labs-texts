50 U.S.C. § 47d — Hearings; rules and regulations; conclusiveness of determinations of Attorney General
Citation: us:usc:50:47d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section47d
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 4C (Atomic Weapons And Special Nuclear Materials Information Rewards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Attorney General is authorized to hold such hearings and make, promulgate, issue, rescind, and amend such rules and regulations as may be necessary to carry out the purposes of this chapter.
(b)
A determination made by the Attorney General under section 47b of this title shall be final and conclusive and no court shall have power or jurisdiction to review it.
