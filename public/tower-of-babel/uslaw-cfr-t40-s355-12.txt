40 C.F.R. § 355.12 — What quantities of extremely hazardous substances trigger emergency planning requirements?
Citation: us:cfr:40:355.12
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-355.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 355 (Emergency Planning And Notification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any EHS present at your facility in an amount equal to or greater than its TPQ triggers the emergency planning requirements of this subpart. The TPQs are listed in Appendices A and B of this part in the column labeled “threshold planning quantity.”
