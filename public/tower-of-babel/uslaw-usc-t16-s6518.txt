16 U.S.C. § 6518 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:6518
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section6518
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 84 (Healthy Forest Restoration); Subchapter I (Hazardous Fuel Reduction On Federal Land). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is authorized to be appropriated $660,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2019 through 2023 to carry out—
(1)
activities authorized by this subchapter; and
(2)
other hazardous fuel reduction activities of the Secretary, including making grants to States, local governments, Indian tribes, and other eligible recipients for activities authorized by law.
