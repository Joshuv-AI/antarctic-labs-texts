33 C.F.R. § 169.225 — Which Application Service Providers may a ship use?
Citation: us:cfr:33:169.225
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-169.225
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 169 (Ship Reporting Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A ship may use an Application Service Provider (ASP) recognized by its Administration. Some Communication Service Providers may also serve as an ASP.
