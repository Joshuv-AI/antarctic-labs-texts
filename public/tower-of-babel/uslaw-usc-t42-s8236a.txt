42 U.S.C. § 8236a — Technical assistance
Citation: us:usc:42:8236a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section8236a
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 91 (National Energy Conservation Policy); Subchapter II (Residential Energy Conservation); Part D (Residential Energy Efficiency Rating Guidelines). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 2 years after October 24, 1992, the Secretary shall establish a program to provide technical assistance to State and local organizations to encourage the adoption of and use of residential energy efficiency rating systems consistent with the voluntary guidelines issued under section 8236 of this title.
