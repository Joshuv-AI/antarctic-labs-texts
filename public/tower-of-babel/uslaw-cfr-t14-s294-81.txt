14 C.F.R. § 294.81 — Local traffic prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:14:294.81
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-294.81
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 294 (Canadian Charter Air Taxi Operators). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except as set forth in paragraph (b) of this section or § 294.60, a registrant shall not carry passengers, cargo, or mail between two or more United States points for compensation or hire.
(b) A registrant may grant stopover privileges at any point or points in the United States to passengers and their accompanied baggage as part of a single continuous operation to or from Canada.
