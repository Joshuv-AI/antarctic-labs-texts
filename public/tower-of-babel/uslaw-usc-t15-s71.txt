15 U.S.C. § 71 — “Person” defined
Citation: us:usc:15:71
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section71
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 2 (Federal Trade Commission; Promotion Of Export Trade And Prevention Of Unfair Methods Of Competition); Subchapter VI (Prevention Of Unfair Methods Of Competition). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When used in this subchapter the term “person” includes partnerships, corporations, and associations.
