24 C.F.R. § 40.4 — Standards.
Citation: us:cfr:24:40.4
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-40.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 40 (Accessibility Standards For Design, Construction, And Alteration Of Publicly Owned Residential Structures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Residential structures subject to this part shall be designed, constructed or altered to ensure that physically handicapped persons have access to, and use of, these structures. This requirement is satisfied by using the Uniform Federal Accessibility Standards (UFAS).
