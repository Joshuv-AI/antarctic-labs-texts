22 U.S.C. § 1974 — Inapplicability of chapter to certain seizures
Citation: us:usc:22:1974
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1974
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 25 (Protection Of Vessels On The High Seas And In Territorial Waters Of Foreign Countries). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of this chapter shall not apply with respect to a seizure made by a country at war with the United States or a seizure made in accordance with the provisions of any applicable convention or treaty, if that treaty or convention was made with advice and consent to 1 the Senate and was in force and effect for the United States and the seizing country at the time of the seizure.
