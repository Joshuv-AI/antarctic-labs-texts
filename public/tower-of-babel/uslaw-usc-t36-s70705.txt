36 U.S.C. § 70705 — Management of homestead and erection of monument
Citation: us:usc:36:70705
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section70705
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 707 (Frederick Douglass Memorial And Historical Association). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

After the corporation has acquired any part of the property occupied by the late Frederick Douglass as his homestead, commonly called Cedar Hill, in the village of Anacostia, District of Columbia, the corporation may—
(1)
manage, repair, and improve the property to carry out the purposes of the corporation; and
(2)
erect on the property a monument to the memory of the late Frederick Douglass.
