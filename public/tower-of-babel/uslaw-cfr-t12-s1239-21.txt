12 C.F.R. § 1239.21 — Compensation of Enterprise board members.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1239.21
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1239.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1239 (Responsibilities Of Boards Of Directors, Corporate Practices, And Corporate Governance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each Enterprise may pay its directors reasonable and appropriate compensation for the time required of them, and their necessary and reasonable expenses, in the performance of their duties.
