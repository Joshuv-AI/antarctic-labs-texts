14 C.F.R. § 187.17 — Failure by applicant to pay prescribed fees.
Citation: us:cfr:14:187.17
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-187.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 187 (Fees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an applicant fails to pay fees agreed to under appendix C of this part, the FAA may suspend or deny any application for service and may suspend or revoke any production certification-related approval granted.
