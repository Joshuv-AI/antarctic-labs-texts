28 U.S.C. § 1444 — Foreclosure action against United States
Citation: us:usc:28:1444
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1444
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part IV (Jurisdiction And Venue); Chapter 89 (District Courts; Removal Of Cases From State Courts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any action brought under section 2410 of this title against the United States in any State court may be removed by the United States to the district court of the United States for the district and division in which the action is pending.
