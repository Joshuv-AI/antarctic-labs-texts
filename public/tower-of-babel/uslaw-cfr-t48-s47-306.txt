48 C.F.R. § 47.306 — 47.306 Transportation factors in the evaluation of offers.
Citation: us:cfr:48:47.306
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-47.306
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 47 (Transportation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When evaluating offers, contracting officers shall consider transportation and transportation-related costs as well as the offerors' shipping and receiving facilities.
