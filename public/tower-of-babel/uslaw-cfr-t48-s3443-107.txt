48 C.F.R. § 3443.107 — 3443.107 Contract clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:3443.107
Creator: Chapter 34—Department Of Education Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-3443.107
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 3443 (Contract Modifications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer must insert a clause substantially the same as the clause at 3452.243-70 (Key personnel) in all solicitations and contracts in which it will be essential for the contracting officer to be notified that a change of designated key personnel is to take place by the contractor.
