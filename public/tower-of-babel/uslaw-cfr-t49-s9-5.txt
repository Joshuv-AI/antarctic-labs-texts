49 C.F.R. § 9.5 — General prohibition of production or disclosure in legal proceedings.
Citation: us:cfr:49:9.5
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-9.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 9 (Testimony Of Employees Of The Department And Production Of Records In Legal Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No employee of the Department may provide testimony or produce any material contained in the files of the Department, or disclose any information relating to, or based upon, material contained in the files of the Department, or disclose any information or produce any material acquired as part of the performance of that employee's official duties or because of that employee's official status unless authorized in accordance with this part, or by other applicable law.
