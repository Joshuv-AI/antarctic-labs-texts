31 U.S.C. § 5304 — Regulations
Citation: us:usc:31:5304
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section5304
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 53 (Monetary Transactions); Subchapter I (Credit And Monetary Expansion). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

With the approval of the President, the Secretary of the Treasury may prescribe regulations—
(1)
to carry out section 5301 of this title; and
(2)
the Secretary considers necessary to carry out section 5302 of this title.
