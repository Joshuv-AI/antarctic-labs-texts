33 C.F.R. § 174.29 — Temporary certificate of number.
Citation: us:cfr:33:174.29
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-174.29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 174 (State Numbering And Casualty Reporting Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A State may issue a temporary certificate of number that is effective for not more than 60 days.
