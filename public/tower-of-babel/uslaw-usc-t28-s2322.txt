28 U.S.C. § 2322 — United States as party

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part VI (Particular Proceedings); Chapter 157 (Surface Transportation Board Orders; Enforcement And Review). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All actions specified in section 2321 of this title shall be brought by or against the United States.
