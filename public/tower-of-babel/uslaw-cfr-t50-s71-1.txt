50 C.F.R. § 71.1 — Opening of national fish hatcheries to hunting and sport fishing.
Citation: us:cfr:50:71.1
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-71.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 71 (Hunting And Sport Fishing On National Fish Hatcheries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

National fish hatchery areas may be opened to hunting or sport fishing when such activity is not detrimental to the propagation and distribution of fish or other aquatic wildlife.
