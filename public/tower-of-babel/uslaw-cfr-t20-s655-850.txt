20 C.F.R. § 655.850 — Who has custody of the administrative record?
Citation: us:cfr:20:655.850
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-655.850
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 655 (Temporary Employment Of Foreign Workers In The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The official record of every completed administrative hearing procedure provided by subparts H and I of this part shall be maintained and filed under the custody and control of the Chief Administrative Law Judge. Upon receipt of a complaint seeking review of the final agency action in a United States District Court, the Chief Administrative Law Judge shall certify the official record and shall transmit such record to the clerk of the court.
