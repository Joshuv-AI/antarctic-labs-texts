10 U.S.C. § 272 — Use of military equipment and facilities
Citation: us:usc:10:272
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section272
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And General Military Powers); Chapter 15 (Military Support For Civilian Law Enforcement Agencies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Defense may, in accordance with other applicable law, make available any equipment (including associated supplies or spare parts), base facility, or research facility of the Department of Defense to any Federal, State, or local civilian law enforcement official for law enforcement purposes.
