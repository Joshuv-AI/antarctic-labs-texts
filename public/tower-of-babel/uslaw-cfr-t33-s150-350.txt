33 C.F.R. § 150.350 — What are the rules of navigation for support vessels in the safety zone or area to be avoided?
Citation: us:cfr:33:150.350
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-150.350
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 150 (Deepwater Ports: Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A support vessel must not anchor in the safety zone or area to be avoided, except:
(a) In an anchorage area; or
(b) For vessel maintenance, which, in the case of a manned deepwater port, must be cleared by the port's person in charge of vessel operations.
