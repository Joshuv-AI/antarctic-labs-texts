10 U.S.C. § 9011 — Organization
Citation: us:usc:10:9011
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9011
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization); Chapter 903 (Department Of The Air Force). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Department of the Air Force is separately organized under the Secretary of the Air Force. It operates under the authority, direction, and control of the Secretary of Defense.
