34 C.F.R. § 300.100 — Eligibility for assistance.
Citation: us:cfr:34:300.100
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-300.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 300 (Assistance To States For The Education Of Children With Disabilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A State is eligible for assistance under Part B of the Act for a fiscal year if the State submits a plan that provides assurances to the Secretary that the State has in effect policies and procedures to ensure that the State meets the conditions in §§ 300.101 through 300.176.
