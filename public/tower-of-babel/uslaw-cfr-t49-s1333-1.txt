49 C.F.R. § 1333.1 — Demurrage defined.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1333.1
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1333.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1333 (Demurrage Liability). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Demurrage is a charge that both compensates rail carriers for the expenses incurred when rail cars are detained beyond a specified period of time (i.e., free time) for loading or unloading, and serves as a penalty for undue car detention to encourage the efficient use of rail cars in the rail network.
