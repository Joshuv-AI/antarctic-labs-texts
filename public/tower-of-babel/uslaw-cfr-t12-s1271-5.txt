12 C.F.R. § 1271.5 — Operations.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1271.5
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1271.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1271 (Miscellaneous Federal Home Loan Bank Operations And Authorities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Bank may utilize the services of a Federal Reserve Bank and may become a member or use the services of a clearinghouse, public or private financial institution, or agency in the exercise of any powers or functions under this subpart.
