34 C.F.R. § 222.183 — How does an LEA apply for a grant?
Citation: us:cfr:34:222.183
Creator: Office Of Elementary And Secondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-222.183
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 222 (Impact Aid Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) To apply for funds under this program, an LEA may submit only one application for one educational facility for each competition.
(b) An application must—
(1) Contain the information required in §§ 222.184 through 222.186, as applicable, and in any application notice that the Secretary may publish in the Federal Register; and
(2) Be timely filed in accordance with the provisions of the Secretary's application notice.
