48 U.S.C. § 1842 — Covering into Commonwealth treasury of tax proceeds collected pursuant to Covenant
Citation: us:usc:48:1842
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1842
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 17 (Northern Mariana Islands); Subchapter III (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall take such steps as are necessary to ensure that the proceeds of taxes collected under the provisions of sections 601, 602, 603, and 604 of the Covenant (Public Law 94–241) are covered directly upon collection into the treasury of the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands.
