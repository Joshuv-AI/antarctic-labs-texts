22 U.S.C. § 297 — Acquisition of property by lease
Citation: us:usc:22:297
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section297
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 8 (Foreign Service Buildings). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The authority granted to acquire sites and buildings by purchase or otherwise shall include authority to acquire leaseholds.
