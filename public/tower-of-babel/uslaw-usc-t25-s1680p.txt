25 U.S.C. § 1680p — Annual budget submission
Citation: us:usc:25:1680p
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section1680p
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 18 (Indian Health Care); Subchapter VI (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Effective beginning with the submission of the annual budget request to Congress for fiscal year 2011, the President shall include, in the amount requested and the budget justification, amounts that reflect any changes in—
(1)
the cost of health care services, as indexed for United States dollar inflation (as measured by the Consumer Price Index); and
(2)
the size of the population served by the Service.
