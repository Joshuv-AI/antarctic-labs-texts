25 C.F.R. § 171.555 — What additional costs will I incur if I am granted a Payment Plan?
Citation: us:cfr:25:171.555
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-171.555
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 171 (Irrigation Operation And Maintenance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You will incur the following costs:
(a) An administrative fee to process your Payment Plan, as required by 31 CFR 901.9.
(b) Interest, accrued on your unpaid balance, in accordance with § 171.545.
