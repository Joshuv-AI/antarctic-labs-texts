30 C.F.R. § 912.780 — Surface mining permit applications—minimum requirements for reclamation and operation plan.
Citation: us:cfr:30:912.780
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-912.780
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 912 (Idaho). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Part 780 of this chapter, Surface Mining Permit Applications—Minimum Requirements for Reclamation and Operation Plan, shall apply to any person who makes application to conduct surface coal mining and reclamation operations.
