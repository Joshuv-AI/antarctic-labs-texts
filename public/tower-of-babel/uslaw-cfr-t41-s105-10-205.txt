41 C.F.R. § 105-10.205 — -10.205 Periodic compliance reviews.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-10.205
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-10 (10—Nondiscrimination In Programs Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The responsible GSA official or designee shall from time to time review the practices of recipients to determine whether they are complying with this regulation.
