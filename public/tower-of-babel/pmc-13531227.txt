Correction to: Structure-informed machine learning for drug discovery: a task-centric perspective
Published: 2026

This is a correction to: Yi Li, Rong-hui Zhan, Jingxin Rao, Mengting Liu, Peng Sang, Xin Zeng, Mingyue Zheng, Xutong Li, Liquan Yang, Structure-informed machine learning for drug discovery: a task-centric perspective, Briefings in Bioinformatics, Volume 27, Issue 1, January 2026, bbag081, https://doi.org/10.1093/bib/bbag081

In the originally published version of this manuscript, Formula 1 and Formula 5 contained errors.

In the originally published version of this manuscript, there were omissions and errors in Table 3 that have now been corrected.

The code for ‘SurfDock’ has been added, and the entries for ‘RosettaVS’ and ‘HydraScreen’ have been separated accordingly and given the correct code, level, classes, and benchmarks.

Additionally, the names of two co-folding models have been corrected to read: “AlphaFold3” and “Chai-1” instead of “AphaFold3” and “Chai-I”.
