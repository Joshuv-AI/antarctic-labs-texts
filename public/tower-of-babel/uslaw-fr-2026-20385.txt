DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES


Advisory Council on Alzheimer's Research, Care, and Services; 
Meeting

AGENCY: Assistant Secretary for Planning and Evaluation, HHS.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice announces the public meeting of the Advisory 
Council on Alzheimer's Research, Care, and Services (Advisory Council) 
and the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services Alzheimer's 
Disease and Related Dementias Summit. The Advisory Council provides 
advice on how to prevent or reduce the burden of Alzheimer's disease 
and related dementias on people living with the disease and their 
caregivers. During the Summit, attendees will hear updates on the 
coordinated national response to Alzheimer's disease and related 
dementias, including panel presentations on care, services, and 
supports, as well as research, innovation, and prevention. During the 
fourth and final Advisory Council meeting of 2026, the Advisory Council 
will hear from a panel organized by the clinical care subcommittee, 
receive budget updates from the National Institutes of Health, and hear 
a presentation on innovation in Alzheimer's disease interventions using 
focused ultrasound technology.

DATES: The events will be held on Monday, October 19, 2026. The Summit 
will take place from 9:30 a.m. to noon ET and the Advisory Council 
meeting will be held from 1:00 to 5:00 p.m. ET.

ADDRESSES: The Summit will be held in person, and the Advisory Council 
meeting will be a hybrid of in-person and virtual. Both events will be 
held in the Great Hall of the Hubert H. Humphrey Building, 200 
Independence Avenue SW, Washington, DC 20201. They will also stream 
live at www.hhs.gov/live.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: 
    Maria-Theresa Okafor, 771-223-7102, [email protected].

    Comments: Time will be allocated on the agenda to hear public 
comments from 3:30 p.m. to 3:55 p.m. Oral comments will be limited to 
two (2) minutes per individual. Individuals who wish to provide an oral 
public comment must register using the registration form no later than 
Monday, October 12: https://forms.cloud.microsoft/g/QLYF2dvykW.
    Registered commenters participating virtually will receive a 
meeting link and dial-in number and may use either option to join the 
meeting. Individuals may also request that NAPA staff read their 
comments during the public comment session. To make this request, 
please select the applicable response on the registration form and 
email the comment to [email protected] with the subject line ``October 19 
Public Comments Reading Request.'' Comments to be read by NAPA staff 
must be received by Monday, October 12. Note: There may be a 30-45 
second delay in the livestream video presentation of the meeting. For 
this reason, if you have pre-registered to submit a public comment, it 
is important to connect to the meeting by 2:55 p.m., to ensure that you 
do not miss your name and allotted time when called. If you miss your 
name and allotted time to speak, you may not be able to make your 
public comment. All participant audio lines will be muted for the 
duration of the meeting and only unmuted by the Host at the time of the 
participant's public comment. Should you have questions during the 
meeting, email [email protected] and someone will respond to your message as 
quickly as possible. To ensure accuracy, please submit a written copy 
of oral comments for the record by emailing [email protected] by Wednesday, 
October 21, 2026. Please attach the comments as a Microsoft Word 
(.docx) file so they can be compiled for publication. You may also 
attach a PDF copy for reference, but a Word version is required. These 
comments will be shared on the website and reflected in the meeting 
summary.
