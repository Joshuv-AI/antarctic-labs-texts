29 C.F.R. § 511.18 — Publication and effective date of wage order.
Citation: us:cfr:29:511.18
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-511.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 511 (Wage Order Procedure For American Samoa). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Promptly after receipt of the committee report the Administrator shall publish the committee recommendations in the Federal Register and shall provide by order that the recommendations contained in such report shall take effect upon the expiration of 15 days after the date of such publication.
