48 C.F.R. § 247.371 — 247.371 DD Form 1653, Transportation Data for Solicitations.
Citation: us:cfr:48:247.371
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-247.371
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 247 (Transportation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The transportation specialist prepares the DD Form 1653 to accompany requirements for the acquisition of supplies. The completed form should contain recommendations for suitable f.o.b. terms and other suggested transportation provisions for inclusion in the solicitation.
