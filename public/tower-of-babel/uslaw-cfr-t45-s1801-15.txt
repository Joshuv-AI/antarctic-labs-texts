45 C.F.R. § 1801.15 — Submission of application to the Foundation.

Creator: Harry S. Truman Scholarship Foundation
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1801 (Harry S. Truman Scholarship Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To nominate a student for the competition, the Faculty Representative must submit the completed nomination packet to the Foundation as provided in § 1801.16. The Foundation does not accept nominations packets directly from students.
