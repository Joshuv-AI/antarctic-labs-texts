21 C.F.R. § 344.12 — Ear drying aid active ingredient.
Citation: us:cfr:21:344.12
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-344.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 344 (Topical Otic Drug Products For Over-The-Counter Human Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The active ingredient of the product consists of isopropyl alcohol 95 percent in an anhydrous glycerin 5 percent base.
