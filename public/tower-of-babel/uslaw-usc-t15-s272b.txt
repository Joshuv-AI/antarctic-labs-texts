15 U.S.C. § 272b — Annual budget submission
Citation: us:usc:15:272b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section272b
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 7 (National Institute Of Standards And Technology). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The National Institute of Standards and Technology shall annually submit to the Congress, at the time of the release of the President’s budget, a three year budget estimate for the Institute, including funding estimates for each major account and new initiative.
