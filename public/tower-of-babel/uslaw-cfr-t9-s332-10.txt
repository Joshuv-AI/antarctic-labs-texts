9 C.F.R. § 332.10 — Deselection of ineligible establishments.
Citation: us:cfr:9:332.10
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-332.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 332 (Selected Establishments; Cooperative Program For Interstate Shipment Of Carcasses, Parts Of Carcasses, Meat, And Meat Food Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Administrator will deselect a selected establishment that becomes ineligible to participate in a cooperative interstate shipment program for any reason listed under § 332.3(c) of this part.
(b) An establishment that has been deselected must transition to become an official establishment as provided in § 332.11 of this part.
