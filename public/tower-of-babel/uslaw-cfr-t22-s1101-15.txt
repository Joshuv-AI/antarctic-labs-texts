22 C.F.R. § 1101.15 — Judicial review.
Citation: us:cfr:22:1101.15
Creator: International Boundary And Water Commission, United States And Mexico, United States Section
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1101.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1101 (Privacy Act Of 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After having exhausted all administrative remedies set forth in § 1101.7(g)(3) or § 1101.14, a requester may bring a civil action against the Section, in a United States District Court of proper venue, within two years of the final administrative decision which the requester seeks to challenge.
