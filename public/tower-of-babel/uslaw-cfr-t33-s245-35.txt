33 C.F.R. § 245.35 — Judgments to require removal.
Citation: us:cfr:33:245.35
Creator: Corps Of Engineers, Department Of The Army, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-245.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 245 (Removal Of Wrecks And Other Obstructions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the owner or responsible party has been identified, and refuses or fails to take prompt action toward removal, the District Engineer may seek a judgment by the district court requiring removal.
