41 C.F.R. § 109-50.104 — -50.104 Equipment which may be granted.
Citation: us:cfr:41:109-50.104
Creator: Chapter 109—Department Of Energy Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-109-50.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 109-50 (50—Special Doe Disposal Authorities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Generally, equipment items classified in FSCG 66, Instruments and Laboratory Equipment, are eligible for granting under this program. Other selected items designated by the Office of Workforce Development for Teachers and Scientists (WDTS) and approved by the OPMO, are made available under the program.
