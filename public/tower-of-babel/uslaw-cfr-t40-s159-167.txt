40 C.F.R. § 159.167 — Discontinued studies.
Citation: us:cfr:40:159.167
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-159.167
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 159 (Statements Of Policies And Interpretations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The fact that a study has been discontinued before the planned termination must be reported to EPA, with the reason for termination, if submission of information concerning the study is, or would have been, required under this part.
