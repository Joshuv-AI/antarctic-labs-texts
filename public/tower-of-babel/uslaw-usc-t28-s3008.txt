28 U.S.C. § 3008 — Proceedings before United States magistrate judges
Citation: us:usc:28:3008
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section3008
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part VI (Particular Proceedings); Chapter 176 (Federal Debt Collection Procedure); Subchapter A (Definitions And General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A district court of the United States may assign its duties in proceedings under this chapter to a United States magistrate judge to the extent not inconsistent with the Constitution and laws of the United States.
