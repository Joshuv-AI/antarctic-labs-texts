ACT-Consistent 𝑩 − 𝑳 B-L Higgs Inflation in Supergravity


Abstract
We consider a renormalizable extension of the minimal supersymmetric standard model (MSSM) endowed by an R and a gauged B − L B-L symmetry. The model incorporates chaotic inflation driven by a quartic potential, associated with the Higgs superfields which lead to a spontaneous breaking of U ​ ( 1 ) B − L U(1)_{B-L} . Consistency with the ACT data is achieved by considering a fractional shift-symmetric Kähler potential which includes two free parameters ( p , N ) (p,N) constrained in the ranges 1.355 ≤ p ≤ 6.7 1.355\leq p\leq 6.7 and 6 ⋅ 10 − 5 ≤ N ≤ 0.7 6\cdot 10^{-5}\leq N\leq 0.7 . An explanation of the μ \mu term of the MSSM is also provided, under the condition that a related parameter in the superpotential is somewhat small. Baryogenesis occurs via non-thermal leptogenesis which is also realized by the inflaton’s decay to the lightest and/or next-to-lightest right-handed neutrinos for normal ordered light neutrino masses. Published in PoS CORFU 2025 , 139 (2026).

4.1
Generation of the
\mu
Term of MSSM

The origin of the
\mu
term of MSSM can be explained if we
combine the three first terms of Eq. (
2b
) working at the level
of SUSY. In fact, the total low energy potential is

V_{\rm tot}=V_{\rm SUSY}+V_{\rm soft},

(26)

where the first term includes the SUSY limit
V_{\rm SUSY}
of

V_{\rm pHI}
in Eq. (
9
) which is given by

V_{\rm SUSY}=K_{\rm SUSY}^{{\alpha}{\bar{\beta}}}W_{\rm B{\alpha}}W^{*}_{\rm B{\bar{\beta}}}.

(27a)

Here
K_{\rm SUSY}
is the limit of the
K
Eq. (
3
) for

m_{\rm P}\rightarrow\infty
, which is

K_{\rm SUSY}=Np\left(|\Phi|^{2}+|\bar{\Phi}|^{2}-\bar{\Phi}\Phi-\bar{\Phi}^{*}\Phi^{*}\right)+|X^{\alpha}|^{2}.

(27b)

Upon substitution of
K_{\rm SUSY}
into Eq. (
27a
) we obtain

\displaystyle V_{\rm SUSY}=\lambda^{2}|\bar{\Phi}\Phi-M^{2}/2|^{2}+\lambda^{2}|S|^{2}\left(|\bar{\Phi}|^{2}+|\Phi|^{2}\right)/Np+\cdots,

where the ellipsis includes terms which vanish at the SUSY vacuum
lying along the D-flat direction
|\bar{\Phi}|=|\Phi|
with

\langle{X^{\alpha}}\rangle=0\>\>\mbox{and}\>\>|\langle{\Phi}\rangle|=|\langle{\bar{\Phi}}\rangle|=M/\sqrt{2}\,.

(28)

As a consequence,
\langle{\Phi}\rangle
and
\langle{\bar{\Phi}}\rangle
break
spontaneously
U(1)_{B-L}
down to
\mathbb{Z}^{B-L}_{2}
. Since

U(1)_{B-L}
is already broken during pHI, no cosmic strings are
formed.

The contributions from the soft SUSY-breaking terms, although
negligible during pHI, since these are much smaller than
\phi
,
may shift slightly
\langle{S}\rangle
from zero in Eq. (
28
). Indeed, the
relevant potential terms are

V_{\rm soft}=\left(\lambda A_{\lambda}S\bar{\Phi}\Phi+\lambda_{\mu}A_{\mu}S{H_{u}}{H_{d}}+\lambda_{iN^{c}}A_{iN^{c}}\Phi\widetilde{N}^{c2}_{i}-{\rm a}_{S}S\lambda M^{2}/2+{\rm h.c.}\right)+m_{{\alpha}}^{2}\left|X^{\alpha}\right|^{2},

(29)

where
m_{{\alpha}},A_{\lambda},A_{\mu},A_{iN^{c}}
and
{\rm a}_{S}
are soft SUSY
breaking mass parameters. Rotating
S
in the real axis by an
appropriate
R
-transformation, choosing conveniently the phases
of
A_{\lambda}
and
{\rm a}_{S}
so as the potential in Eq. (
26
) to be
minimized – see Eq. (
27
) – and substituting in
V_{\rm soft}

\langle{\Phi}\rangle
and
\langle{\bar{\Phi}}\rangle
from Eq. (
28
) we get

\langle{V_{\rm tot}(S)}\rangle=\lambda^{2}{M^{2}S^{2}}/{Np}-2\lambda\href{http://www-spires.slac.stanford.edu/spires/find/hep/www?j=ANMAA\%2C$m_{3/2}$\%2C^}{{\it Ann.\ Math.\ }{\bf$m_{3/2}$} (M) ^}2S,~~\mbox{where}~~\href{http://www-spires.slac.stanford.edu/spires/find/hep/www?j=ANMAA\%2C=\%2C/}{{\it Ann.\ Math.\ }{\bf=} ((|A_$\lambda$| + |{\rm a}_{S}|)) /}{2m_{3/2}}>0

(30a)

is a parameter of order unity which parameterizes our ignorance
for the dependence of
|A_{\lambda}|
and
|{\rm a}_{S}|
on
m_{3/2}
and
we take into account that
m_{S}\ll M
. The minimization condition
for the total potential in Eq. (
30a
) w.r.t
S
leads to a non
vanishing
\langle{S}\rangle
as follows

\frac{d}{dS}\langle{V_{\rm tot}(S)}\rangle=0~~\Rightarrow~~\langle{S}\rangle\simeq 2Np\ \href{http://www-spires.slac.stanford.edu/spires/find/hep/www?j=ANMAA\%2C$m_{3/2}$\%2C$\lambda$}{{\it Ann.\ Math.\ }{\bf$m_{3/2}$} (/) $\lambda$}.

(30b)

The generated
\mu
term from the third term in Eq. (
2b
) is

\mu=\lambda_{\mu}\langle{S}\rangle\simeq 2\lambda_{\mu}Np\ \href{http://www-spires.slac.stanford.edu/spires/find/hep/www?j=ANMAA\%2C$m_{3/2}$\%2C$\lambda$}{{\it Ann.\ Math.\ }{\bf$m_{3/2}$} (/) $\lambda$}.

(31)

It can be comparable to
m_{3/2}
for
p
and
N
of order unity, if

\lambda_{\mu}
is of the same order of magnitude with
\lambda
. Since
\lambda\sim 10^{-6}
, as inferred by Eq. (
19
), the required
\lambda_{\mu}
can
become compatible with Eq. (
14
) assuring, thereby, the stability
of the path in Eq. (
8
) during
p
HI.

4.2
Baryogenesis,
\widetilde{G}
Abundance and Neutrino
Masses

When pHI is over, the inflaton continues to roll down towards the
SUSY vacuum, Eq. (
28
). Soon after, it settles into a phase of
damped oscillations around the minimum of
V_{\rm pHI}
. The (canonically
normalized) inflaton,

\widehat{\delta\phi}=\langle{J}\rangle\delta\phi\>\>\>\mbox{with}\>\>\>\delta\phi=\phi-M\>\>\>\mbox{and}\>\>\>\langle{J}\rangle\simeq\sqrt{2Np}

(32)

acquires mass, at the SUSY vacuum in Eq. (
28
), which is given
by

\widehat{m}_{\rm\delta\phi}=\left\langle V_{\rm pHI,\widehat{\phi}\widehat{\phi}}\right\rangle^{1/2}=\left\langle V_{\rm pHI,\phi\phi}/J^{2}\right\rangle^{1/2}\simeq{\lambda M}/{\sqrt{Np}}

(33)

and lies in the intermediate energy scale. E.g., for
p=2
we find

0.14\lesssim\widehat{m}_{\rm\delta\phi}/{\mbox{\rm ZeV}}\lesssim 3.8~~\mbox{with}~~8\gtrsim N\gtrsim 0.1.

(34)

Obviously
\widehat{m}_{\rm\delta\phi}
increases as
N
decreases. Recall that

1~{\mbox{\rm ZeV}}=10^{12}~{\mbox{\rm GeV}}
.

During the phase of its oscillations at the SUSY vacuum,
\widehat{\delta\phi}

decays pertubatively reheating the Universe at a reheat
temperature given by
[
5
]

T_{\rm rh}=\left({40\over\pi^{2}g_{\rm rh*}}\right)^{1/4}\left(\widehat{\Gamma}_{\rm\delta\phi}m_{\rm P}\right)^{1/2}\>\>\>\mbox{with}\>\>\>\widehat{\Gamma}_{\rm\delta\phi}=\widehat{\Gamma}_{\delta\phi\rightarrow N_{i}^{c}N_{i}^{c}}+\widehat{\Gamma}_{\delta\phi\rightarrow{H_{u}}{H_{d}}}\,.

(35)

Also
g_{*}=228.75
counts the MSSM effective number of
relativistic degrees of freedom. To compute
T_{\rm rh}
we take into
account the following decay widths:

\displaystyle\widehat{\Gamma}_{\delta\phi\rightarrow N_{i}^{c}N_{i}^{c}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{g_{iN^{c}}^{2}}{16\pi}\widehat{m}_{\rm\delta\phi}\left(1-\frac{4M_{[N^{c}}i]^{2}}{\widehat{m}_{\rm\delta\phi}^{2}}\right)^{3/2}\>\>\mbox{with}\>\>g_{iN^{c}}=\sqrt{2}\lambda_{iN^{c}}/{\langle{J}\rangle},

(36a)

\displaystyle\widehat{\Gamma}_{\delta\phi\rightarrow{H_{u}}{H_{d}}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{2}{8\pi}g_{H}^{2}\widehat{m}_{\rm\delta\phi}\>\>\>\mbox{with}\>\>g_{H}={\lambda_{\mu}}/{\sqrt{2}}

(36b)

which arise from the lagrangian
terms

\displaystyle{\cal L}_{\widehat{\delta\phi}\rightarrow N^{c}_{i}N^{c}_{i}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{2}e^{K/2m_{\rm P}^{2}}W_{{\rm B},N_{i}^{c}N^{c}_{i}}N^{c}_{i}N^{c}_{i}\ +\ {\rm h.c.}=g_{iN^{c}}\widehat{\delta\phi}\ \left(N^{c}_{i}N^{c}_{i}\ +\ {\rm h.c.}\right)+\cdots,

(37a)

\displaystyle{\cal L}_{\widehat{\delta\phi}\rightarrow{H_{u}}{H_{d}}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-e^{K/m_{\rm P}^{2}}K^{SS^{*}}\left|W_{{\rm B},S}\right|^{2}=-g_{H}\widehat{m}_{\rm\delta\phi}\widehat{\delta\phi}\left(H_{u}^{*}H_{d}^{*}\ +\ {\rm h.c.}\right)+\cdots

(37b)

describing
\widehat{\delta\phi}
decay into a pair of
N^{c}_{j}
and
{H_{u}}
and

{H_{d}}
respectively. Note that
N^{c}_{j}
acquire masses

M_{[N^{c}}j]=\sqrt{2}\lambda_{jN^{c}}M
and we work in the so-called

N^{c}_{i}
-basis
, where
M_{[N^{c}}j]
is diagonal, real and
positive.

For
T_{\rm rh}<M_{[N^{c}}i]
, the out-of-equilibrium decay of
N^{c}_{i}

generates a lepton-number asymmetry (per
N^{c}_{i}
decay),

\varepsilon_{i}
. The resulting lepton-number asymmetry is partially
converted through sphaleron effects into a yield of the observed
BAU
[
5
]

Y_{B}=-0.35\cdot{3\over 2}\ {T_{\rm rh}\over\widehat{m}_{\rm\delta\phi}}\mbox{$\sum_{i}$}{\widehat{\Gamma}_{\delta\phi\rightarrow N_{i}^{c}N_{i}^{c}}\over\widehat{\Gamma}_{\rm\delta\phi}}\varepsilon_{i},

(38)

where
\varepsilon_{i}
are calculated as analyzed in Ref.
[
9
]
. The
expression above has to match with the observational value

[
6
]

Y_{B}=\left(8.75\pm 0.085\right)\cdot 10^{-11}.

(39)

The validity of Eq. (
38
) requires that the
\widehat{\delta\phi}
decay into a
pair of
N^{c}_{i}
’s is kinematically allowed for at least one species
of the
N^{c}_{i}
’s and also that there is no erasure of the produced

Y_{L}
due to
N^{c}_{1}
mediated inverse decays and
\Delta L=1

scatterings. These prerequisites are ensured if we impose

10T_{\rm rh}\lesssim M_{[N^{c}}1]\lesssim\widehat{m}_{\rm\delta\phi}/2.

(40)

The quantity
\varepsilon_{i}
can be expressed in terms of the Dirac masses
of
\nu_{i}
,
m_{[\rm D}i]
, arising from the third term of
W
in
Eq. (
2b
). Employing the seesaw formula we can then obtain the
light-neutrino mass matrix
m_{\nu}
in terms of
m_{[\rm D}i]
and

M_{[N^{c}}i]
. As a consequence, nTL can be nicely linked to low
energy neutrino data
[
14
]
.

The required
T_{\rm rh}
in Eq. (
38
) must be compatible with
constraints on the gravitino (
\widetilde{G}
) abundance,
Y_{3/2}
, at the
onset of
nucleosynthesis
(
BBN
), which is
estimated to be
[
13
]

Y_{3/2}\simeq 1.9\cdot 10^{-13}~T_{\rm rh}/{\mbox{\rm EeV}},

(41)

where we take into account only thermal production of
\widetilde{G}
, and
assume that
\widetilde{G}
is much heavier than the MSSM gauginos.
Avoidance any disturbance to BBN entails
[
13
]

Y_{3/2}\lesssim\left\{\begin{matrix}10^{-14}\hfill\cr 10^{-13}\hfill\cr\end{matrix}\right.\>\>\>\mbox{for}\>\>\>m_{3/2}\simeq\left\{\begin{matrix}0.69~{\mbox{\rm TeV}}\hfill\cr 10.6~{\mbox{\rm TeV}}\hfill\cr\end{matrix}\right.\>\>\>\mbox{implying}\>\>\>T_{\rm rh}\lesssim 5.3\cdot\left\{\begin{matrix}0.01~{\mbox{\rm EeV}}\,,\hfill\cr 0.1~{\mbox{\rm EeV}}\,,\hfill\cr\end{matrix}\right.

(42)

for the typical case where
\widetilde{G}
decays with a tiny hadronic
branching ratio. The bounds above can be somehow relaxed in the
case of a short or very short-lived
\widetilde{G}
– cf., e.g.,
Ref.
[
15
]

We investigated the realization of pHI and nTL in the framework of
a
B-L
extension of MSSM endowed with the condition that the GUT
scale is determined by the running of the three gauge coupling
