26 C.F.R. § 44.6696-1 — -1 Claims for credit or refund by tax return preparers.
Citation: us:cfr:26:44.6696-1
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-44.6696-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 44 (Taxes On Wagering; Effective January 1, 1955). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In general. For rules for claims for credit or refund by a tax return preparer who prepared a return or claim for refund for tax on wagers under sections 4401 or 4411, the rules under § 1.6696-1 of this chapter will apply.
(b) Effective/applicability date. This section is applicable to returns and claims for refund filed, and advice provided, after December 31, 2008.
