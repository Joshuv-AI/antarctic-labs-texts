18 U.S.C. § 3585 — Calculation of a term of imprisonment
Citation: us:usc:18:3585
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part II (Criminal Procedure); Chapter 227 (Sentences); Subchapter D (Imprisonment). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Commencement of Sentence.—
A sentence to a term of imprisonment commences on the date the defendant is received in custody awaiting transportation to, or arrives voluntarily to commence service of sentence at, the official detention facility at which the sentence is to be served.
(b) Credit for Prior Custody.—
A defendant shall be given credit toward the service of a term of imprisonment for any time he has spent in official detention prior to the date the sentence commences—
(1)
as a result of the offense for which the sentence was imposed; or
(2)
as a result of any other charge for which the defendant was arrested after the commission of the offense for which the sentence was imposed;
that has not been credited against another sentence.
