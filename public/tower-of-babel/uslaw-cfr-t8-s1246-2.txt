8 C.F.R. § 1246.2 — Allegations admitted; no answer filed; no hearing requested.
Citation: us:cfr:8:1246.2
Creator: Executive Office For Immigration Review, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-1246.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 1246 (Rescission Of Adjustment Of Status). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the answer admits the allegations in the notice, or if no answer is filed within the thirty-day period, or if no hearing is requested within such period, the district director or asylum office director shall rescind the adjustment of status previously granted, and no appeal shall lie from his decision.
