13 C.F.R. § 123.505 — What if you are both an essential employee and the owner of the small business and you started active service before applying for a Military Reservist EIDL?
Citation: us:cfr:13:123.505
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-123.505
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 123 (Disaster Loan Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you are both an essential employee and the owner of the small business and you started active service before applying for an Military Reservist EIDL, a person who has a power of attorney with the authority to borrow and make other related commitments on your behalf, may complete and submit the EIDL loan application package for you.
