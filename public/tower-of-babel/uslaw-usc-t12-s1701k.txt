12 U.S.C. § 1701k — Right to redeem property on which United States has lien
Citation: us:usc:12:1701k
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section1701k
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 13 (National Housing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The right to redeem provided for by section 2410(c) of title 28, shall not arise in any case in which the subordinate lien or interest of the United States derives from the issuance of insurance under the National Housing Act, as amended [12 U.S.C. 1701 et seq.].
