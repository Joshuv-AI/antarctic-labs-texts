22 C.F.R. § 65.3 — Reference of applications.
Citation: us:cfr:22:65.3
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-65.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 65 (Foreign Students). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary of State of the Department of State shall refer the applications to the proper department or agency of the Government for advice as to what reply should be made to the application.
