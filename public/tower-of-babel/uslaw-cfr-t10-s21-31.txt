10 C.F.R. § 21.31 — Procurement documents.
Citation: us:cfr:10:21.31
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-21.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 21 (Reporting Of Defects And Noncompliance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each individual, corporation, partnership, dedicating entity, or other entity subject to the regulations in this part shall ensure that each procurement document for a facility, or a basic component issued by him, her or it on or after January 6, 1978, specifies, when applicable, that the provisions of 10 CFR part 21 apply.
