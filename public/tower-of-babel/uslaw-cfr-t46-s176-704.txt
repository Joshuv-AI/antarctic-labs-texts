46 C.F.R. § 176.704 — Breaking of safety valve seals.
Citation: us:cfr:46:176.704
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-176.704
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 176 (Inspection And Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The owner, managing operator, or master shall notify the cognizant OCMI as soon as practicable after the seal on a boiler safety valve on a vessel is broken.
