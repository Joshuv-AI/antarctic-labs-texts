46 U.S.C. § 57108 — Consideration of ballast and equipment in determining selling price
Citation: us:usc:46:57108
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section57108
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part F (Government-Owned Merchant Vessels); Chapter 571 (General Authority). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Maritime Administration may not sell a vessel until its ballast and equipment have been inventoried and their value considered in determining the selling price of the vessel.
