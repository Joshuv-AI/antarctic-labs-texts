20 U.S.C. § 1226c–1 — Availability of education reports, etc., to Congressional committees
Citation: us:usc:20:1226c–1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1226c–1
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 31 (General Provisions Concerning Education); Subchapter II (Appropriations And Evaluations); Part 2 (Planning And Evaluation Of Federal Education Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any evaluation report or data or information collected in preparation of such report, which is paid for with appropriated funds, shall be made available, upon request, within 4 days to the chairman and ranking minority member of the Committee on Education and Labor of the House of Representatives and of the Committee on Labor and Human Resources of the Senate.
