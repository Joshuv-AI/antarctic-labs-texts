34 C.F.R. § 655.10 — What kinds of projects does the Secretary assist?
Citation: us:cfr:34:655.10
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-655.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 655 (International Education Programs—General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subpart A of 34 CFR parts 656, 657, and 669 and subpart B of 34 CFR parts 658, 660, 661 describe the kinds of projects that the Secretary assists under the International Education Programs.
