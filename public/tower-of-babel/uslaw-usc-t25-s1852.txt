25 U.S.C. § 1852 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:25:1852
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section1852
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 20 (Tribally Controlled Colleges And Universities Assistance); Subchapter III (Tribal Economic Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated for grants under this subchapter, such sums as may be necessary for fiscal year 2009 and such sums as may be necessary for each of the five succeeding fiscal years.
