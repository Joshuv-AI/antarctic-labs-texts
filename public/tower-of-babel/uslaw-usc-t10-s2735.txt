10 U.S.C. § 2735 — Settlement: final and conclusive
Citation: us:usc:10:2735
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2735
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Property); Chapter 163 (Military Claims). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the settlement of a claim under section 2733, 2733a, 2734, 2734a, 2734b, or 2737 of this title is final and conclusive.
