Integral Kirwan surjectivity
Daniel Pomerleano and Constantin Teleman

Abstract
. We refine Kirwan’s surjectivity and formality theorems for a Hamiltonian G G -action on a compact symplectic manifold M M . For a regular value of the moment map, we show that the Kirwan map is surjective and additively split after inverting the orders of stabilizers in the reduction. In particular, for a free quotient, it is surjective integrally. We generalize this to a splitting of M ​ U MU -module spectra. We also give a stable version of Kirwan’s equivariant formality theorem: after inverting the torsion primes for G G , the G G -action on M ​ U ∧ M + MU\wedge M_{+} is trivalizable. The novel idea is to exploit the Atiyah-Bott argument in Morava K K -theory, then return to bordism and cohomology.

6.
Spectral formality

We start with a quick refresher on (naive) equivariance for spectra.

6.1.

-actions on spectra

A

-action for short) on a spectrum

is an

-action
of

to a local system

of spectra with fiber

over the classifying space

(understood as the
geometric realization of the simplicial

[

]
for background). An equivalent
but more abstract datum is the promotion of

to a left module over the suspension spectrum

\Sigma^{\infty}G_{+}
, which the multiplication in
G
turns into an associative ring spectrum
(the group ring of
G
over the stable sphere). Of course, strict

-actions define homotopy
actions.

The
homotopy fixed-point spectrum

F^{hG}
is the space of sections of
\mathcal{F}
over
BG
.
For the cohomology theory
X\mapsto\pi_{*}F(X)
represented by

,
the homotopy groups of the spectra

give the naive equivariant

-homologies and cohomologies of

. When the
G
-action on

is trivial,
the latter spectrum agrees with

.

6.2.
Extensions.

A
fibration sequence

of

-spectra is
a fiber-wise fibration sequence for the associated bundles over
BG
. Its relative delooping over
BG

\Sigma\mathcal{F}^{\prime}\rightarrowtail\Sigma\mathcal{F}\twoheadrightarrow\Sigma\mathcal{F}^{\prime\prime}
,
defines a fiber-wise action of
\mathcal{F}^{\prime\prime}\cong\Omega\Sigma\mathcal{F}^{\prime\prime}
on
\Sigma\mathcal{F}^{\prime}
,
in particular, a map
\mathcal{F}^{\prime\prime}\to\Sigma\mathcal{F}^{\prime}
, after acting on the base-point section of

\ \mathcal{F}^{\prime}
. As usual, its homotopy class determines the
hG
-fibration sequence up to homotopy,
identifying it with the pull-back from the tautological
\mathcal{F}^{\prime}\rightarrowtail*\twoheadrightarrow\Sigma\mathcal{F}^{\prime}
: a homotopy of classifying maps leads to an equivalence of fibration sequences over
BG
.
In particular, the sequence is split when the classifying map is null-homotopic.
In our case, when the

and the fibration maps are

-modules, the classifying map for the extension
is

-linear.

Remark 6.1
.

Our base

is a finite type

of fiber-wise homotopy theory over compact bases, as in

fiber-wise stable homotopy category.
