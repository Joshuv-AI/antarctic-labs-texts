17 C.F.R. § 37.402 — Additional requirements for physical-delivery swaps.
Citation: us:cfr:17:37.402
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-37.402
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 37 (Swap Execution Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For physical-delivery swaps, the swap execution facility shall demonstrate that it:
(a) Monitors a swap's terms and conditions as they relate to the underlying commodity market; and
(b) Monitors the availability of the supply of the commodity specified by the delivery requirements of the swap.
