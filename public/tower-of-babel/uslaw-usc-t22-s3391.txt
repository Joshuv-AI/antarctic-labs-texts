22 U.S.C. § 3391 — Rule of construction
Citation: us:usc:22:3391
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section3391
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 48A (Taiwan Enhanced Resilience); Subchapter V (Rules Of Construction). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter may be construed—
(1)
to restore diplomatic relations with the Republic of China; or
(2)
to alter the United States Government’s position with respect to the international status of the Republic of China.
