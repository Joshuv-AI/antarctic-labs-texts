1 C.F.R. § 8.9 — Form of citation.
Citation: us:cfr:1:8.9
Creator: Administrative Committee Of The Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-1/section-8.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 1 (General Provisions), Part 8 (Code Of Federal Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-23 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Code of Federal Regulations may be cited by title and section, and the short form “CFR” may be used for “Code of Federal Regulations.” For example, “1 CFR 10.2” refers to title 1, Code of Federal Regulations, part 10, section 2.
