15 C.F.R. § 280.103 — Laboratory accreditation.
Citation: us:cfr:15:280.103
Creator: National Institute Of Standards And Technology, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-280.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 280 (Fastener Quality). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A laboratory may be accredited by any laboratory accreditation program that may be established by any entity or entities, which have affirmed to the Director, NIST, under § 280.102 of this subpart, or by the National Voluntary Laboratory Accreditation Program for fasteners, established by the Director, NIST, under part 285 of this chapter.
