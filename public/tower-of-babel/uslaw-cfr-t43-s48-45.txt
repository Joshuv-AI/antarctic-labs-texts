43 C.F.R. § 48.45 — When is a proposed amendment deemed effective?
Citation: us:cfr:43:48.45
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-48.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 48 (Amendments To The Hawaiian Homes Commission Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If the Secretary determines that a proposed amendment meets none of the criteria in § 48.20, the effective date of the proposed amendment is the date of the notification letter to the Congressional Committee Chairmen.
(b) If the Secretary determines that the proposed amendment requires congressional approval then the effective date of the proposed amendment is the date that Congress's approval becomes law.
