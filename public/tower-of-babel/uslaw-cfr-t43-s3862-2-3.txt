43 C.F.R. § 3862.2-3 — -3 Trustee to disclose nature of trust.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3862.2-3
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3862.2-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3860 (Mineral Patent Applications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any party applying for patent as trustee must disclose fully the nature of the trust and the name of the cestui que trust; and such trustee, as well as the beneficiaries, must furnish satisfactory proof of citizenship; and the names of beneficiaries, as well as that of the trustee, must be inserted in the final certificate of entry.
