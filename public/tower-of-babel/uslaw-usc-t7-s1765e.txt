7 U.S.C. § 1765e — Location of offices
Citation: us:usc:7:1765e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1765e
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 43 (Foreign Market Development); Subchapter II (United States Agricultural Trade Offices). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

United States Agricultural Trade Offices shall be centrally located in the cities of assignment to facilitate foreign trade meetings and foreign trade reliance on such offices for assistance in marketing activities.
