7 C.F.R. § 460.17 — Funding available for ADD PAY II.
Citation: us:cfr:7:460.17
Creator: Federal Crop Insurance Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-460.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 460 (Disaster And Other Additional Payments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The total funding available for ADD PAY II is $30 million.
(b) Funds from Title I of Division B of the American Relief Act, 2025 (Pub. L. 118-158) will be used for ADD PAY II.
