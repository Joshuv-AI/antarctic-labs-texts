33 U.S.C. § 2284 — Benefits and costs attributable to environmental measures
Citation: us:usc:33:2284
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section2284
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 36 (Water Resources Development); Subchapter V (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the evaluation by the Secretary of benefits and costs of a water resources project, the benefits attributable to measures included in a project for the purpose of environmental quality, including improvement of the environment and fish and wildlife enhancement, shall be deemed to be at least equal to the costs of such measures.
