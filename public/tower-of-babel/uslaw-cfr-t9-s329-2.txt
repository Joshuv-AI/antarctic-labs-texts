9 C.F.R. § 329.2 — Method of detention; form of detention tag.
Citation: us:cfr:9:329.2
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-329.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 329 (Detention; Seizure And Condemnation; Criminal Offenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An authorized representative of the Secretary shall detain any article or livestock to be detained under this part, by affixing an official “U.S. Detained” tag (FSIS Form 8400-2) to such article or livestock.
