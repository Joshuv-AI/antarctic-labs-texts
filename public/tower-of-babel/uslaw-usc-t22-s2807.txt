22 U.S.C. § 2807 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:22:2807
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2807
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 40 (International Expositions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated such sums, not to exceed $200,000 in any fiscal year, as may be necessary to carry out the purposes of this chapter.
