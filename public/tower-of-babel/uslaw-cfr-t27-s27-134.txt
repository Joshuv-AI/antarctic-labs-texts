27 C.F.R. § 27.134 — Proprietors of qualified premises.
Citation: us:cfr:27:27.134
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-27.134
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 27 (Importation Of Distilled Spirits, Wines, And Beer). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Importing operations conducted by proprietors of premises qualified under the provisions of this chapter shall be recorded and reported in accordance with the regulations governing the operations of each such premises.
