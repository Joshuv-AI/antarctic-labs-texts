47 C.F.R. § 0.1 — The Commission.
Citation: us:cfr:47:0.1
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-0.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 0 (Commission Organization). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Federal Communications Commission is composed of five (5) members who are appointed by the president subject to confirmation by the Senate. Normally, one Commissioner is appointed or reappointed each year, for a term of five (5) years.
