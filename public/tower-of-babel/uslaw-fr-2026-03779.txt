DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services

42 CFR Part 438


Managed Care

CFR Correction

    This rule is being published by the Office of the Federal Register 
to correct an editorial or technical error that appeared in the most 
recent annual revision of the Code of Federal Regulations.
    In Title 42 of the Code of Federal Regulations, Parts 430 to 481, 
revised as of October 1, 2025, in section 438.72, reinstate paragraph 
(a) to read as follows:


Sec.  438.72   Additional requirements for long-term services and 
supports.

    (a) Nursing facility services and services delivered in 
intermediate care facilities for individuals with intellectual 
disabilities (ICFs/IID). The State must comply with the requirements in 
Sec.  442.43 for nursing facility and ICF/IID services.
