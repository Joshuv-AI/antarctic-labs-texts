5 C.F.R. § 532.509 — Pay for Sunday work.
Citation: us:cfr:5:532.509
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-532.509
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 532 (Prevailing Rate Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A wage employee whose regular work schedule includes a period of service of up to 8 hours which is not overtime work, a part of which is on Sunday, is entitled to additional pay under the provisions of section 5544 of title 5, United States Code.
