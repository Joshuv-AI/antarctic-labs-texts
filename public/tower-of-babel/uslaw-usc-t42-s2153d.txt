42 U.S.C. § 2153d — Authority to continue agreements for cooperation entered into prior to March 10, 1978
Citation: us:usc:42:2153d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2153d
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 23 (Development And Control Of Atomic Energy); Subchapter X (International Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The amendments to section 2153 of this title made by this Act shall not affect the authority to continue cooperation pursuant to agreements for cooperation entered into prior to March 10, 1978.
(b)
Nothing in this Act shall affect the authority to include dispute settlement provisions, including arbitration, in any agreement made pursuant to an Agreement for Cooperation.
