45 C.F.R. § 1206.1-10 — -10 Modification of procedures by consent.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1206.1-10
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1206.1-10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1206 (Grants And Contracts—Suspension And Termination And Denial Of Application For Refunding). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The responsible Corporation official or the presiding officer of a termination hearing may alter, eliminate or modify any of the provisions of this subpart with the consent of the recipient and, in the case of a termination hearing, with the consent of all agencies that have a right to participate in the hearing pursuant to § 1206.1-5(e). Such consent must be in writing or be recorded in the hearing transcript.
