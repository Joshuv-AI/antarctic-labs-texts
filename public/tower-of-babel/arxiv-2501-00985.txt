On the population of electron-positron pairs in the magnetosphere of SgrA*
Zaza N. Osmanov

Abstract




Abstract

In this study we consider the possibility of the Schwinger pair creation driven by the centrifugal mechanism in the magnetosphere of SgrA*. In particular, it is shown that the magneto-centrifugal effects become extremely efficient due to the vortex driven magnetic field, which is by several orders of magnitude bigger than previously thought. Dynamics of the magneto-centrifugally accelerated charged particles leads to charge-separation, parametrically inducing the Langmuir waves. The corresponding electric field exponentially amplifies and after reaching the Schwinger threshold, the efficient pair production occurs, that is eventually saturated by the annihilation process.

It is clear that to a large extent the processes taking place in the vicinity of SgrA* are determined by the magnetosphere, therefore it is very important to study the latter. For example, it is believed that many processes taking place in the magnetospheres of giant black holes are determined by the presence of electron-positron pairs. It is generally accepted that mainly two mechanisms can be the cause of their generation. According to one mechanism, high-energy photons scatter off relatively soft photons, produced in the accretion disks, and generate electron-positron pairs
[
5
]

[

Recently, it has been found that this is not the only mechanism by which pairs can be formed. In particular, the works
[
7
,
8

The essence of the mechanism lies in the following: a strong magnetic field in the magnetosphere of a Kerr-type black hole/pulsar causes charged particles to follow magnetic field lines, which in turn are characterised by rotation. These two factors determine the magneto-centrifugal acceleration of particles, which might reach very high energies (VHE). Eventually, magneto-centrifugal effects lead to the exponentially amplified effect of charge separation, when the electrostatic field might reach the so-called Schwinger threshold

3
Conclusions

It has been shown that the central black hole of our galaxy, SgrA*, is an efficient engine for acceleration of particles by means of the magneto-centrifugal mechanism.

Together with the conventional mechanism of generation of the magnetic field we have examined the vortex-driven magnetic field, which exceeds the previous one by many orders of magnitude and it was shown that the centrifugally accelerated particles induce exponentially increasing electric field.

In due course, the mentioned field might approach the Schwinger threshold, when efficient pair creation takes place and it has been shown that the Schwinger mechanism will provide very high values of electron-positron number densities, leading to annihilation emission lines.
