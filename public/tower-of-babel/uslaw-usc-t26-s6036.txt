26 U.S.C. § 6036 — Notice of qualification as executor or receiver
Citation: us:usc:26:6036
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section6036
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 61 (Information And Returns); Subchapter A (Returns And Records); Part III (Information Returns); Subpart A (Information Concerning Persons Subject To Special Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Every receiver, trustee in a case under title 11 of the United States Code, assignee for benefit of creditors, or other like fiduciary, and every executor (as defined in section 2203), shall give notice of his qualification as such to the Secretary in such manner and at such time as may be required by regulations of the Secretary. The Secretary may by regulation provide such exemptions from the requirements of this section as the Secretary deems proper.
