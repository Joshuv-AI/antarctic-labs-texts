22 U.S.C. § 276dd — Income from property covered into Treasury; disbursement and investment
Citation: us:usc:22:276dd
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section276dd
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter III (Kermit Roosevelt Fund). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The income from any property held or administered by the board, as and when collected, shall be deposited in the Treasury of the United States to the credit of the trust fund established pursuant to section 276bb of this title, and it shall be and remain subject to investment, reinvestment, and disbursement by the board for the uses and purposes set forth herein.
