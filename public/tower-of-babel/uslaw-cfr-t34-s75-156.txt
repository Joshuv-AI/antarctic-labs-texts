34 C.F.R. § 75.156 — When an applicant under § 75.155 must submit its application to the State; proof of submission.
Citation: us:cfr:34:75.156
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-75.156
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 75 (Direct Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each applicant under a program covered by § 75.155 shall submit a copy of its application to the State on or before the deadline date for submitting its application to the Department.
(b) The applicant shall attach to its application a copy of its letter that requests the State to comment on the application.
