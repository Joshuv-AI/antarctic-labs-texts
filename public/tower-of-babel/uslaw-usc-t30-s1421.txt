30 U.S.C. § 1421 — Prevention of interference with other uses of the high seas
Citation: us:usc:30:1421
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section1421
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 26 (Deep Seabed Hard Mineral Resources); Subchapter I (Regulation Of Exploration And Commercial Recovery By United States Citizens). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each license and permit issued under this subchapter shall include such restrictions as may be necessary and appropriate to ensure that exploration or commercial recovery activities conducted by the licensee or permittee do not unreasonably interfere with the interests of other states in their exercise of the freedoms of the high seas, as recognized under general principles of international law.
