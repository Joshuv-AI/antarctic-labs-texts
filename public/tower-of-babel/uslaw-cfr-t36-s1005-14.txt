36 C.F.R. § 1005.14 — Prospecting, mining, and mineral leasing.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1005.14
Creator: Presidio Trust
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1005.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1005 (Commercial And Private Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Prospecting, mining, and the location of mining claims under the general mining laws and leasing under the mineral leasing laws are prohibited in the area administered by the Presidio Trust except as authorized by law.
