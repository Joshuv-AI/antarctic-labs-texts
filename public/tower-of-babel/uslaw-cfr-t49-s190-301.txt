49 C.F.R. § 190.301 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:49:190.301
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-190.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 190 (Pipeline Safety Enforcement And Regulatory Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes general rulemaking procedures for the issue, amendment, and repeal of Pipeline Safety Program regulations of the Pipeline and Hazardous Materials Safety Administration of the Department of Transportation.
