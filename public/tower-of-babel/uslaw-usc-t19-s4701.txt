19 U.S.C. § 4701 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:19:4701
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section4701
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 29 (United States–Mexico–Canada Agreement Implementation); Subchapter VII (Environment Monitoring And Enforcement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this subchapter:
(1) Environmental law
The term “environmental law” has the meaning given the term in article 24.1 of the USMCA.
(2) Environmental obligations
The term “environmental obligations” means obligations relating to the environment under—
(A)
chapter 1 of the USMCA (relating to initial provisions and general definitions); and
(B)
chapter 24 of the USMCA (relating to environment).
