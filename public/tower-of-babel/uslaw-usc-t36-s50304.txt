36 U.S.C. § 50304 — Powers
Citation: us:usc:36:50304
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section50304
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 503 (Disabled American Veterans). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation may—
(1)
adopt a constitution, bylaws, and regulations to carry out the purposes of the corporation;
(2)
adopt and alter a corporate seal;
(3)
adopt emblems and badges;
(4)
establish and maintain offices to conduct its activities;
(5)
establish State and territorial organizations and local chapter or post organizations;
(6)
acquire, own, lease, encumber, and transfer property as necessary to carry out the purposes of the corporation;
(7)
publish a newspaper and other publications devoted to the purposes of the corporation;
(8)
sue and be sued; and
(9)
do any other act necessary or proper to carry out the purposes of the corporation.
