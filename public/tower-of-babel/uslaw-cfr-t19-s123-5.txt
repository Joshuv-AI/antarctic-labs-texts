19 C.F.R. § 123.5 — Certification and filing of inward foreign manifest.
Citation: us:cfr:19:123.5
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-123.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 123 (Cbp Relations With Canada And Mexico). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The manifest listing baggage and other merchandise, certified by the master of the vessel or the person in charge of the vehicle, shall be presented to the Customs officer at the time the report of arrival is made. It shall be filed in the original only, unless additional copies are required in this part.
