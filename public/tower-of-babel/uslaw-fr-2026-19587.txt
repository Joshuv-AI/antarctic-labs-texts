DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Foreign-Trade Zones Board

[B-48-2025]


Foreign-Trade Zone 183; Withdrawal of Application for Subzone; 
Applied Materials, Inc.; Austin and Pflugerville, Texas

    Notice is hereby given of the withdrawal of the application 
submitted by the Foreign Trade Zone of Central Texas, Inc., grantee of 
Foreign-Trade Zone 183, requesting subzone status for the facilities of 
Applied Materials, Inc., in Austin and Pflugerville, Texas.
    The application was docketed on December 3, 2025 (90 FR 56130, 
December 5, 2025). The withdrawal was requested by the applicant due to 
outstanding issues that need to be addressed. The case has been closed 
without prejudice.

    Dated: September 21, 2026.
Colin Agostisi,
Executive Secretary.
