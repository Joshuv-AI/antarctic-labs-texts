7 C.F.R. § 1780.25 — Exception authority.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1780.25
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1780.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1780 (Water And Waste Loans And Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator may, in individual cases, make an exception to any requirement or provision of this part which is not inconsistent with the authorizing statute or other applicable law and is determined to be in the Government's interest.
