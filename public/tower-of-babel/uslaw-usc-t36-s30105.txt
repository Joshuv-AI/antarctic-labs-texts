36 U.S.C. § 30105 — Powers
Citation: us:usc:36:30105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section30105
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 301 (Big Brothers—Big Sisters Of America). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation may—
(1)
adopt and amend a constitution and bylaws for the management of its property and the regulation of its affairs;
(2)
adopt and alter a corporate seal;
(3)
choose officers, managers, agents, and employees as the activities of the corporation require;
(4)
make contracts;
(5)
acquire, own, lease, encumber, and transfer property as necessary or convenient to carry out the purposes of the corporation;
(6)
borrow money, issue instruments of indebtedness, and secure its obligations by granting security interests in its property; and
(7)
sue and be sued.
