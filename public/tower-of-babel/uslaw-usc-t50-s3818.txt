50 U.S.C. § 3818 — Effective date
Citation: us:usc:50:3818
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section3818
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 49 (Military Selective Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter shall become effective immediately; except that unless the President, or the Congress by concurrent resolution, declares a national emergency after June 24, 1948, no person shall be inducted or ordered into active service without his consent under this chapter within ninety days after June 24, 1948.
