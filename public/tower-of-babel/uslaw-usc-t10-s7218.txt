10 U.S.C. § 7218 — Duties: warrant officers; limitations
Citation: us:usc:10:7218
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7218
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 723 (Special Appointments, Assignments, Details, And Duties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Under regulations prescribed by the President, a warrant officer may be assigned to perform duties that necessarily include those normally performed by a commissioned officer.
