31 C.F.R. § 224.3 — When may a surety corporation provide a bond without appointing a process agent?
Citation: us:cfr:31:224.3
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-224.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 224 (Federal Process Agents Of Surety Corporations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A surety corporation may provide a bond without appointing a process agent when the State where the bond is filed, the State where the principal resides, and the State where the surety corporation is incorporated are the same.
