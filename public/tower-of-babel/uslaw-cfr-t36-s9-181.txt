36 C.F.R. § 9.181 — What enforcement actions can the NPS take?
Citation: us:cfr:36:9.181
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-9.181
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 9 (Minerals Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you engage in a prohibited act described in § 9.180:
(a) You may be subject to a fine or imprisonment, or both, in accordance with 36 CFR 1.3;
(b) The Superintendent may suspend your operations; or
(c) The Regional Director may revoke your approved temporary access permit or operations permit.
