7 U.S.C. § 1738n — Consultations with Congress
Citation: us:usc:7:1738n
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1738n
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 41 (Food For Peace); Subchapter VI (Enterprise For The Americas Initiative). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President shall consult with the appropriate congressional committees on a periodic basis to review the operation of the Facility under this subchapter and the eligibility of countries for benefits from the Facility under this subchapter.
