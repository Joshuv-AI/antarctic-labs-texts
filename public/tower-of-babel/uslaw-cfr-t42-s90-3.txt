42 C.F.R. § 90.3 — Procedures for requesting health assessments.
Citation: us:cfr:42:90.3
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-90.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 90 (Administrative Functions, Practices, And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) ATSDR will accept requests to perform health assessments for a particular facility or release from any person or group of persons.
(b) All requests to ATSDR to perform health assessments should be addressed to: Assistant Administrator, Agency for Toxic Substances and Disease Registry, 1600 Clifton Road NE., Atlanta, GA 30333.
