52 U.S.C. § 21123 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:52:21123
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title52-section21123
Context: United States Code, Title 52 (Voting And Elections); Chapter 209 (Election Administration Improvement); Subchapter V (Help America Vote College Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In addition to any funds authorized to be appropriated to the Commission under section 20930 of this title, there are authorized to be appropriated to carry out this subchapter—
(1)
$5,000,000 for fiscal year 2003; and
(2)
such sums as may be necessary for each succeeding fiscal year.
