40 C.F.R. § 1054.401 — General provisions.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1054.401
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1054 (Control Of Emissions From New, Small Nonroad Spark-Ignition Engines And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We may perform in-use testing of any engines or equipment subject to the standards of this part. We will consult with you as needed for information or special equipment related to testing your engines.
