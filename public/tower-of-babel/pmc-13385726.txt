We, the authors, regretfully identified an unintentional error in Figure S1B of the originally published article. Specifically, the entire set of images (brightfield, fluorescence, and merged) for cell line AOS-13 was inadvertently replaced with the images belonging to AOS-14. The images for AOS-15 and AOS-16 in the original Figure S1B are correct.

See here the corrected Figure S1B, in which we have replaced the erroneous images with the proper images for AOS-13. The corrected figure now accurately represents all three intended cell lines (AOS-13, AOS-15, and AOS-16).

The error is purely a figure placement mistake made during the final figure assembly and does not affect any of the results, data interpretation, or conclusions of the study. The underlying raw data remain accurate and unchanged.
