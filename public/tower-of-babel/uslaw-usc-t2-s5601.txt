2 U.S.C. § 5601 — Sergeant at Arms of House; additional compensation
Citation: us:usc:2:5601
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section5601
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 55 (House Of Representatives Officers And Administration); Subchapter VIII (Sergeant At Arms). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Sergeant at Arms of the House of Representatives shall receive, directly or indirectly, no fees or other compensation or emolument whatever for performing the duties of the office, or in connection therewith, otherwise than the salary prescribed by law.
