7 U.S.C. § 1032a — Disbursing and certifying officers; exemption from liability for advances to defense relocation corporations
Citation: us:usc:7:1032a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1032a
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 33 (Farm Tenancy); Subchapter IV (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Comptroller General of the United States is authorized and directed to allow credit in the accounts of disbursing and certifying officers for advances made in good faith on behalf of the Department of Agriculture to defense relocation corporations and land purchasing associations.
