F-Term Hybrid Inflation, Metastable Cosmic Strings and Low Reheating in View of ACT


Abstract
We consider the formation of metastable cosmic strings in a left-right unified theory. The produced monopoles are diluted by a stage of F-term hybrid inflation (FHI) which is realized consistently with the SUSY breaking and a global U ⁡ ( 1 ) U(1) R R symmetry in the context of a U ​ ( 1 ) R × U ​ ( 1 ) B − L U(1)_{\rm R}\times U(1)_{B-L} extension of MSSM. The hidden sector Kähler manifold enjoys an enhanced S ​ U ​ ( 1 , 1 ) / U ⁡ ( 1 ) SU(1,1)/U(1) symmetry with the scalar curvature determined by the achievement of a SUSY-breaking de Sitter vacuum without ugly tuning. FHI turns out to be compatible with data – including the recent ACT results –, provided that the magnitude of the emergent soft tadpole term is confined in the range ( 0.1 − 70 ) (0.1-70) TeV, and it is accompanied with the production of cosmic strings. Their dimensionless tension G ​ μ cs ≃ ( 1 − 11 ) ⋅ 10 − 8 {G\mu_{\rm cs}}\simeq(1-11)\cdot 10^{-8} interprets the present observations from PTA experiments on the stochastic background of gravitational waves. The μ \mu parameter of MSSM arises by appropriately adapting the Giudice-Masiero mechanism and facilitates the out-of-equilibrium decay of the R R saxion at a reheat temperature lower than about 34 34 GeV. The SUSY mass scale turns out to lie in the PeV region. Published in PoS CORFU 2024 , 206 (2025)

4.1

The minimization of the F–term (tree level) SUGRA scalar

[

\left|\langle{\Phi}\rangle\right|=\left|\langle{\bar{\Phi}}\rangle\right|=M,~~\langle{\sigma}\rangle\simeq 2^{(1-\nu)/2}\left(m-\lambda m_{\rm P}\right)\ z^{\nu}/m_{\rm P}^{\nu}~~\mbox{and}~~\langle{z}\rangle=2\sqrt{2/3}|\nu|m_{\rm P}

(24)

with
\langle{\theta}\rangle=\langle{\theta_{S}}\rangle=0

the absence of IS
[
19

\langle{V_{\rm F}}\rangle=\left(\frac{16\nu^{4}}{9}\right)^{\nu}\left(\frac{\lambda M^{2}-mm_{\rm P}}{\kappa m_{\rm P}^{2}}\right)^{2}\omega^{N}m_{\rm P}^{2}\left(\lambda m_{\rm P}-m\right)^{2}~~\mbox{with}~~\omega\simeq\frac{2(3-2\nu)}{3}.

(25)

Tuning
\lambda
to a value
\lambda\sim m/m_{\rm P}\simeq 10^{-12}

identify
\langle{V_{\rm F}}\rangle
with the DE energy density
[
4
]

\langle{V_{\rm F}}\rangle\simeq 10^{-120}m_{\rm P}^{4}.

(26)

Therefore, we obtain a dS vacuum with
\langle{\sigma}\rangle\simeq 0
which
resolves without extensive tuning the notorious DE problem. At
this vacuum we can estimate the masses of the gravitino, the
sgoldstino (or
R
saxion), the pseudo-sgoldstino (or
R
axion)
and the IS which are respectively
[

\displaystyle m_{3/2}

\displaystyle\simeq

\displaystyle 2^{\nu}3^{-\nu/2}|\nu|^{\nu}m\omega^{N/2},~m_{z}\simeq\frac{3\omega}{2\nu}m_{3/2},~m_{\theta}\simeq 12k\omega^{3/2}m_{3/2}

(27a)

\displaystyle\mbox{and}~~m_{\rm I}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle e^{{K_{\rm H}}/{2m_{\rm P}^{2}}}\sqrt{2}\left(\kappa^{2}M^{2}+(4\nu^{2}/3)^{\nu}(1+4M^{2}/m_{\rm P}^{2})m^{2}\right)^{1/2}.

4.2
Low Reheating

Soon after FHI, the IS and
\widehat{\delta z}
enter into an oscillatory phase

z

\rho_{z\rm It}

\rho_{z\rm I}\sim m_{z}^{2}\langle{z}\rangle^{2}\sim m_{z}^{2}m_{\rm P}^{2}~~\mbox{and}~~\rho_{z\rm It}=3m_{\rm P}^{2}H_{z\rm I}^{2}\simeq 3m_{\rm P}^{2}m_{z}^{2}.

z

[
23
,
24
]

. This fact jeopardizes the

[

6
Conclusions

We analyzed the production of CSs within a
\mathbb{G}_{\rm L1R}
-invariant model
which incorporates FHI and SUSY breaking consistently within an
approximate
R
symmetry. The CSs may be metastable due to
preinflationary GUT genealogy – see Eq. (
5
). The model
offers the following interesting, in our opinion, achievements:

•

Observationally acceptable FHI adjusting the tadpole
parameter
{\rm a}_{S}
and the
\mathbb{G}_{\rm L1R}
breaking scale
M
as shown in
Eq. (
23
);

•

A prediction of the SUSY-mass scale
\widetilde{m}
which turns out
to be of the order of PeV;

•

Generation of the
\mu
term of MSSM with
|\mu|\sim m_{3/2}
;

•

An interpretation of the DE problem without extensive
tuning;

•

Compatibility of the reheating temperature
T_{\rm rh}
with BBN;

•

An explanation of the NG15 via the decay of the CSs.

Due to the low
T_{\rm rh}
our proposal faces difficulties with the
following issues:

•

Baryogenesis. An interesting possibility is to take
advantage from the non-thermal decay of sgoldstino within some
modified version of MSSM – cf. Ref.
[
30
]
.

•

Cold-dark-matter abundance. The relic density of the
lightest neutralino although in the
PeV
range may be inadequate
to account for the dark-matter abundance. Non-thermal contribution

[
31
]
from gravitino decay may be welcome for this aim.
