43 C.F.R. § 8344.1 — Permit requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:43:8344.1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-8344.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 8340 (Off-Road Vehicles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Permits are required for certain types of ORV use and shall be issued in accordance with the special recreation permit procedures under part 2930 of this chapter.
