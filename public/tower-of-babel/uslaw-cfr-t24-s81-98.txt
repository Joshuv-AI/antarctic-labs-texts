24 C.F.R. § 81.98 — Liability of GSEs and Federal Reserve Banks.
Citation: us:cfr:24:81.98
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-81.98
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 81 (The Secretary Of Hud'S Regulation Of The Federal National Mortgage Association (Fannie Mae) And The Federal Home Loan Mortgage Corporation (Freddie Mac)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A GSE and the Federal Reserve Banks may rely on the information provided in a Transfer Message, and are not required to verify the information. A GSE and the Federal Reserve Banks shall not be liable for any action taken in accordance with the information set out in a Transfer Message, or evidence submitted in support thereof.
