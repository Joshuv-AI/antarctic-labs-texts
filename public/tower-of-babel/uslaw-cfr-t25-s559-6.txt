25 C.F.R. § 559.6 — May the Chair require a tribe to submit applicable and available Indian lands or environmental and public health and safety documentation regarding any gaming place, facility, or location where gaming will occur?
Citation: us:cfr:25:559.6
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-559.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 559 (Facility License Notifications And Submissions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A tribe shall provide applicable and available Indian lands or environmental and public health and safety documentation requested by the Chair.
