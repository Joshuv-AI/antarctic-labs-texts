7 C.F.R. § 1787.4 — Unmanufactured articles, materials, and supplies.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1787.4
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1787.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1787 (The “Buy American” Requirement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Buy American requirement also applies to unmanufactured articles, materials, and supplies to be financed with RUS loan funds, and will be considered domestic if mined or produced in the United States or in an eligible country.
