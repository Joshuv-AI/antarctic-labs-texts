7 C.F.R. § 766.457 — Canceling a DBSA agreement.
Citation: us:cfr:7:766.457
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-766.457
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 766 (Direct Loan Servicing—Special). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Agency will cancel a DBSA agreement if the Agency takes any PLS action on the loan.
(b) The Agency will cancel a DBSA agreement if the borrower pays the:
(1) Current market value buyout in accordance with § 766.113; or
(2) The set-aside installment.
