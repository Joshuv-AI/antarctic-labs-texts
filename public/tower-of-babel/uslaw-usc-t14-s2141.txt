14 U.S.C. § 2141 — Revocation of commissions during first five years of commissioned service
Citation: us:usc:14:2141
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2141
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 21 (Personnel; Officers); Subchapter II (Discharges; Retirements; Revocation Of Commissions; Separation For Cause). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary, under such regulations as he may prescribe, may revoke the commission of any regular officer on active duty who, at the date of such revocation, has had less than five years of continuous service as a commissioned officer in the Regular Coast Guard.
