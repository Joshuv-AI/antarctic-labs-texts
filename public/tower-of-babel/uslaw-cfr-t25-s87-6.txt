25 C.F.R. § 87.6 — Extension of period for submitting plans.
Citation: us:cfr:25:87.6
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-87.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 87 (Use Or Distribution Of Indian Judgment Funds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An extension of the one hundred and eighty (180) day period, not to exceed ninety (90) days, may be requested by the Secretary or by the governing body of any affected tribe or group submitting such request to both Congressional Committees through the Secretary, and any such request shall be subject to the approval of both Congressional Committees.
