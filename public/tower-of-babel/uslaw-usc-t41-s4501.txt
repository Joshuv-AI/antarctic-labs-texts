41 U.S.C. § 4501 — Authority of executive agency
Citation: us:usc:41:4501
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title41-section4501
Context: United States Code, Title 41 (Public Contracts); Chapter 45 (Contract Financing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An executive agency may—
(1)
make advance, partial, progress or other payments under contracts for property or services made by the agency; and
(2)
insert in solicitations for procurement of property or services a provision limiting to small business concerns advance or progress payments.
