Error in Figure

In the original publication, there was a mistake in Figure 1B(b,c) as published. Upon thorough examination of the original data, we found that this was due to an error in selecting the correct images for assembly using Photoshop software, and there was a lack of careful checking in the later stages. The corrected, revised Figure 1 appears below. The authors state that the scientific conclusions are unaffected. This correction was approved by the Academic Editor.
