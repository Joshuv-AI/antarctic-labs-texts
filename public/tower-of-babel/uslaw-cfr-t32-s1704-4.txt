32 C.F.R. § 1704.4 — MDR program feedback.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1704.4
Creator: Office Of The Director Of National Intelligence
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1704.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1704 (Mandatory Declassification Review Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The ODNI welcomes suggestions for improving the administration of our MDR program in accordance with Executive Order 13526. Suggestions should identify the specific purpose and the items for consideration. The ODNI will respond to all communications and take such actions as determined feasible and appropriate.
