15 U.S.C. § 8533 — Unnecessary duplication
Citation: us:usc:15:8533
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section8533
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 111 (Weather Research And Forecasting Innovation); Subchapter II (Weather Satellite And Data Innovation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In meeting the requirements under this subchapter, the Under Secretary shall avoid unnecessary duplication between public and private sources of data and the corresponding expenditure of funds and employment of personnel.
