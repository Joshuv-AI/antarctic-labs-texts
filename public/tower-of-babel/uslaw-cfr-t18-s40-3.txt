18 C.F.R. § 40.3 — Availability of Reliability Standards.
Citation: us:cfr:18:40.3
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-40.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 40 (Mandatory Reliability Standards For The Bulk-Power System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Electric Reliability Organization must post on its Web site the currently effective Reliability Standards as approved and enforceable by the Commission. The effective date of the Reliability Standards must be included in the posting.
