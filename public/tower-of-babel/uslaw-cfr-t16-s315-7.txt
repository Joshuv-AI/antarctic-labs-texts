16 C.F.R. § 315.7 — Content of advertisements and other representations.
Citation: us:cfr:16:315.7
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-315.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 315 (Contact Lens Rule). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person who engages in the manufacture, processing, assembly, sale, offering for sale, or distribution of contact lenses may not represent, by advertisement, sales presentation, or otherwise, that contact lenses may be obtained without a prescription.
