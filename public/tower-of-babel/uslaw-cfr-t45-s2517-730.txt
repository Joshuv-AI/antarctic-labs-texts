45 C.F.R. § 2517.730 — May an applicant submit more than one application to the Corporation for the same project at the same time?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2517.730
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2517.730
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2517 (Community-Based Service-Learning Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. The Corporation will reject an application for a project if an application for funding or educational awards for the same project is already pending before the Corporation.
