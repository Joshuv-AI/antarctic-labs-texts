54 U.S.C. § 320303 — Regulations
Citation: us:usc:54:320303
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section320303
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3203 (Monuments, Ruins, Sites, And Objects Of Antiquity). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary, the Secretary of Agriculture, and the Secretary of the Army shall make and publish uniform regulations for the purpose of carrying out this chapter.
