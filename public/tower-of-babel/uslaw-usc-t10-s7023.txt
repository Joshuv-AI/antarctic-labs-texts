10 U.S.C. § 7023 — Chief of Legislative Liaison
Citation: us:usc:10:7023
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7023
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization); Chapter 703 (Department Of The Army). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
There is a Chief of Legislative Liaison in the Department of the Army.
(b)
The Chief of Legislative Liaison shall perform legislative affairs functions as specified for the Office of the Secretary of the Army by section 7014(c)(1)(F) of this title.
