16 C.F.R. § 802.60 — Acquisitions by securities underwriters.
Citation: us:cfr:16:802.60
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-802.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 802 (Exemption Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An acquisition of voting securities by a person acting as a securities underwriter, in the ordinary course of business, and in the process of underwriting, shall be exempt from the requirements of the act.
