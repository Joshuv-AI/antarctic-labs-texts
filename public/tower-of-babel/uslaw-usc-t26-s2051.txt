26 U.S.C. § 2051 — Definition of taxable estate
Citation: us:usc:26:2051
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section2051
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 11 (Estate Tax); Subchapter A (Estates Of Citizens Or Residents); Part IV (Taxable Estate). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of the tax imposed by section 2001, the value of the taxable estate shall be determined by deducting from the value of the gross estate the deductions provided for in this part.
