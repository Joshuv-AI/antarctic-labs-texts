49 C.F.R. § 89.13 — Documentary evidence of compromise.
Citation: us:cfr:49:89.13
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-89.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 89 (Implementation Of The Federal Claims Collection Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A compromise of any claim is not final or binding on the United States unless it is in writing, signed by an officer or employee authorized to compromise that claim.
