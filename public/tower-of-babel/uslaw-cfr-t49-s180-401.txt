49 C.F.R. § 180.401 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:49:180.401
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-180.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 180 (Continuing Qualification And Maintenance Of Packagings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes requirements, in addition to those contained in parts 107, 171, 172, 173 and 178 of this subchapter, applicable to any person responsible for the continuing qualification, maintenance or periodic testing of a cargo tank.
