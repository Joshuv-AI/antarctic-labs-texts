29 U.S.C. § 2636 — Termination
Citation: us:usc:29:2636
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section2636
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 28 (Family And Medical Leave); Subchapter II (Commission On Leave). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commission shall terminate 30 days after the date of the submission of the report of the Commission to Congress.
