45 C.F.R. § 641.13 — Right of action
Citation: us:cfr:45:641.13
Creator: National Science Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-641.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 641 (Environmental Assessment Procedures For Proposed National Science Foundation Actions In Antarctica). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The procedures set forth in this part establish internal procedures to be followed by NSF in considering the potential environmental effects of actions taken in Antarctica. Nothing in this part shall be construed to create a cause of action.
