48 U.S.C. § 869 — Fees payable by United States out of revenue of Puerto Rico
Citation: us:usc:48:869
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section869
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 4 (Puerto Rico); Subchapter IV (The Judiciary). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Such fees and expenses as are payable by the United States if earned or incurred in connection with a district court of the United States shall be paid from the revenue of Puerto Rico if earned or incurred in connection with the United States District Court for the District of Puerto Rico.
