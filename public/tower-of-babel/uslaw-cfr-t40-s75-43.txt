40 C.F.R. § 75.43 — Accessibility criteria.
Citation: us:cfr:40:75.43
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-75.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 75 (Continuous Emission Monitoring). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To demonstrate accessibility equal to or better than the continuous emission monitoring system, the owner or operator shall provide reports and onsite records of emission data to demonstrate that the alternative monitoring system provides data meeting the requirements of subparts F and G of this part.
