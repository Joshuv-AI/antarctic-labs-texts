16 U.S.C. § 460k–4 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:460k–4
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section460k–4
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LXVIII (National Conservation Recreational Areas). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is authorized to be appropriated such funds as may be necessary to carry out the purposes of this subchapter, including the construction and maintenance of public recreational facilities.
