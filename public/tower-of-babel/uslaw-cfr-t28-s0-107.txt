28 C.F.R. § 0.107 — Representation on committee for visit-exchange.
Citation: us:cfr:28:0.107
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-0.107
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 0 (Organization Of The Department Of Justice). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commissioner of Immigration and Naturalization shall be a member of the committee which represents the Department of Justice in the development and implementation of plans for exchanging visits between the Iron Curtain countries and the United States and shall have authority to designate an alternate to serve on such committee.
