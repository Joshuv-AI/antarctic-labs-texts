5 C.F.R. § 2422.22 — What are objections and exceptions concerning the conduct of the hearing?
Citation: us:cfr:5:2422.22
Creator: Federal Labor Relations Authority, General Counsel Of The Federal Labor Relations Authority And Federal Service Impasses Panel
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-2422.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 2422 (Representation Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Objections. Objections are oral or written complaints concerning the conduct of a hearing.
(b) Exceptions to rulings. There are automatic exceptions to all adverse rulings.
