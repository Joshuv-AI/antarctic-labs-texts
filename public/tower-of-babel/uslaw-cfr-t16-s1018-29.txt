16 C.F.R. § 1018.29 — Appeals under the Freedom of Information Act.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1018.29
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1018.29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1018 (Advisory Committee Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Appeals from the denial of access to advisory committee documents shall be considered in accordance with the Commission's Procedures for Disclosure or Production of Information under the Freedom of Information Act (16 CFR part 1015).
