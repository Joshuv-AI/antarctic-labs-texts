22 C.F.R. § 1506.3 — Do these regulations adopt the Federal Claims Collection Standards (FCCS)?
Citation: us:cfr:22:1506.3
Creator: African Development Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1506.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1506 (Collection Of Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part adopts and incorporates all provisions of the FCCS. Except as otherwise provided by law, USADF will conduct administrative actions to collect claims (including offset, compromise, suspension termination, disclosure, and referral) in accordance with the FCCS.
