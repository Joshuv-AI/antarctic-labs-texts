30 C.F.R. § 900.14 — Abandoned mine land programs.
Citation: us:cfr:30:900.14
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-900.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 900 (Introduction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Programs for reclamation of abandoned mine lands are codified under the applicable part for the State. The date of submittal and approval and the addresses at which copies of the program are available appear below in the applicable part for each State.
