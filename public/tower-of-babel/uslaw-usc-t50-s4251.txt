50 U.S.C. § 4251 — Exclusion of claims
Citation: us:usc:50:4251
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section4251
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 52 (Restitution For World War Ii Internment Of Japanese-Americans And Aleuts); Subchapter III (Territory Or Property Claims Against United States). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law or of this chapter, nothing in this chapter shall be construed as recognition of any claim of Mexico or any other country or any Indian tribe (except as expressly provided in this chapter with respect to the Aleut tribe of Alaska) to any territory or other property of the United States, nor shall this chapter be construed as providing any basis for compensation in connection with any such claim.
