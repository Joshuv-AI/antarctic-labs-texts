46 C.F.R. § 179.340 — Trunks.
Citation: us:cfr:46:179.340
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-179.340
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 179 (Subdivision, Damage Stability, And Watertight Integrity). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where a trunk (i.e., an enclosed passageway through a deck or bulkhead) is installed, it must comply with the requirements of § 179.360(a)(1) and with the requirements of § 171.113 in subchapter S of this chapter.
