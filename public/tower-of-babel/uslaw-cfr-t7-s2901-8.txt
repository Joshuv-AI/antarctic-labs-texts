7 C.F.R. § 2901.8 — Judicial review.
Citation: us:cfr:7:2901.8
Creator: Office Of Energy Policy And New Uses, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-2901.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 2901 (Administrative Procedures For Adjustments Of Natural Gas Curtailment Priority). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person aggrieved or adversely affected by a final agency action taken on a request for an adjustment under this section may obtain judicial review in accordance with section 506 of the Natural Gas Policy Act of 1978.
