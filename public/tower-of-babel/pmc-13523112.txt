Article title: Propranolol Enhances the Oncolytic Effect of Newcastle Disease Virus on Canine Mammary Tumor Cell by Modulating the IFN-I-Mediated JAK-STAT Signaling Pathway

Authors: Zhu, Y., Xu, M. & Li, M

Journal: TVEQ: Veterinary Quarterly

Bibliometrics: Volume 46, Number 01, pages 1—16

DOI: http://doi.org/10.1080/01652176.2026.2648283

This article was first published with errors in several panels across Figures 1–6.

The phrase: “These findings suggest that NDV induces IFN-I production in both CMT-U27 and MDCK cells at 24 h.p.i. However, while IFN-I levels in MDCK cells returned to baseline by 48 h.p.i, expression remained elevated in CMT-U27 cells.” Is also corrected.

Also, some P values in section 3.3 has been updated in the article.

The article has been republished with errors corrected.
