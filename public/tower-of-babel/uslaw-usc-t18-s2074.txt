18 U.S.C. § 2074 — False weather reports
Citation: us:usc:18:2074
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section2074
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 101 (Records And Reports). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever knowingly issues or publishes any counterfeit weather forecast or warning of weather conditions falsely representing such forecast or warning to have been issued or published by the Weather Bureau, United States Signal Service, or other branch of the Government service, shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than ninety days, or both.
