48 C.F.R. § 609.404 — 609.404 System for Award Management Exclusions.
Citation: us:cfr:48:609.404
Creator: Chapter 6—Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-609.404
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 609 (Contractor Qualifications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A/OPE shall accomplish the agency responsibilities prescribed in FAR 9.404(c)(1) through (6). The authority to establish procedures prescribed in FAR 9.404(c)(7) is delegated, without power of redelegation, to the head of the contracting activity.
