Published: 2025

Author withdrawal to: The EMBO Journal (2023) 42:e111980. 10.15252/embj.2022111980 | Published online 27 March 2023

Author statement

We discovered that some of the key materials used in this study, namely plasmids and seed stocks, all provided by one of the authors, are incorrect. Furthermore, the primary source data generated using these materials have been deleted. To date, no statement has been provided by the author to clarify this situation.

We contacted EMBO Press about this matter and agreed to their proposal to investigate the manuscript for potential scientific misconduct. Their review revealed unacceptable image manipulations in several figures. Considering these findings and the problems with the materials, we no longer have confidence in the integrity of the results presented in this work.

Due to the lack of the proper material, we cannot repeat the experiments to prove or disprove the results in a reasonable time frame. As a result, we have made the decision to withdraw the paper.

We sincerely apologise for any confusion or negative impact this publication may have caused.

Image manipulations detected by EMBO Press data integrity check

Figure 4C, panel “Mock” is identical to Fig. EV5, panel “24 h CiPt”. However, these are supposed to be different reporters (RMI1-GFP vs RMI1-RFP) in different treatments (Mock vs 24 hrs CiPt).

Figure 6C, the same pictures were used for different genotypes, i.e. kno1 RMI1-RFP and agt5 RMI1-RFP; the pictures are magnifications from the time course in EV5, which represents atg5 RMI1-RFP. Note that the 24 hrs CiPt magnification in Fig. 6C is flipped horizontally.

Figure 7A, 2nd input blot matches Fig. EV4, 1st input blot; however the images have been horizontally flipped and differently scaled.

Figure 7C, 3rd input blot matches Fig. EV4, 2nd input blot, however the images have been horizontally flipped, differently scaled and cut.

Figure EV1B, Dapi stained nuclei number 1, 4 and 5 as well as the merged nucleus number 4 have been taken from a previous publication (PMID:30500810, Fig. 9C). These Dapi stained nuclei represent the correct genotypes/treatments, however they are from a different experiment, i.e. these pictures do not show the same nuclei as the anti-gH2AX or the anti-RAD51 antibody staining below.

Figure EV1B, antiRAD51 stained nucleus number 3 has been taken from a different publication (PMID:30500810, Fig. 9C) and used for a different genotype and treatment (WT MMC treated vs. ubp21 BLM treated).

Figure 9B, line 1, pictures 5 and 6. The same Y2H growth control picture has been used for different constructs (RMI1deltaOB2 and HsRMI1).

NDW, GDJ, MI, MH, and AS agree to this withdrawal. No response was received from PC.
