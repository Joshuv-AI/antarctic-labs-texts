41 C.F.R. § 105-70.013 — -70.013 Parties to the hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-70.013
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-70.013
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-70 (70—Implementation Of The Program Fraud Civil Remedies Act Of 1986). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The parties to the hearing shall be the defendant and the Authority.
(b) Pursuant to 31 U.S.C. 3730(c)(5), a private plaintiff under the False Claims Act may participate in these proceedings to the extent authorized by the provisions of that Act.
