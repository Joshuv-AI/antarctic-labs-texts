Equivalence between Gromov-Witten invariants: Domain dependent perturbation and Kuranishi structure approaches
Yuguo Qin

Abstract
We prove that the homology class induced by the rational pseudocycle constructed via domain-dependent perturbations by Cieliebak and Mohnke [ 2 ] coincides with the homology class induced by the virtual fundamental class defined through Kuranishi structures by Fukaya, Oh, Ohta, and Ono [ 9 , 6 ] .



1
Introduction

Let
(M,\omega)
be a closed symplectic manifold with a compatible almost complex structure
J
. The Gromov-Witten invariants of
(M,\omega)
, which encode counts of
J
-holomorphic curves, can be constructed through several different approaches. These include the Kuranishi structure framework established by Fukaya, Ono, Oh, and Ohta
[
9
,
6
]
; domain-dependent perturbation techniques, applied to genus zero curves by Cieliebak and Mohnke
[
2
]
and extended to higher genus curves by Gerstenberger
[
10
]
and Ionel, Parker
[
15
]
; the polyfold approach pioneered by Hofer, Wysocki, and Zehnder
[
13
]
; and the homotopy sheaf approach developed by Pardon
[
18
]
. This paper establishes the equivalence between the domain-dependent perturbation method and the Kuranishi structure approach.

For a homology class
A\in H_{2}(M;\mathbb{Z})
and nonnegative integer
k
, the moduli space
\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{0,k}(J,A)
of stable
J
-holomorphic genus zero curves with
k
marked points consists of equivalence classes
[u,z_{1},\ldots,z_{k}]
, where
u\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}\Sigma_{T}\to M
is a
J
-holomorphic map from a genus zero nodal curve
\Sigma_{T}
modeled on a tree
T
to
M
and represents the class
A
,
z_{i}\in\Sigma_{T}
are marked points. The evaluation maps
\ev_{i}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{0,k}(J,A)\to M
is defined by
\ev_{i}([u,z_{1},\ldots,z_{k}])=u(z_{i})
.

In general,
\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{0,k}(J,A)
may not be a smooth manifold, making it challenging to define a fundamental class. To address this issue, Cieliebak and Mohnke
[
2
]
use domain-dependent almost complex structures
\mathcal{J}_{\ell+1}
that depend on the universal curve
\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{0,\ell+1}
of genus zero curves with
\ell
marked points, combined with a Donaldson hypersurface
Y\subset M
of sufficiently large degree
D
. For a regular choice
K\in\mathcal{J}_{\ell+1}
and
\ell=D\omega(A)
, they show that the evaluation map

\prod_{i=1}^{k}\ev_{i}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}\mathcal{M}_{0,k+\ell}(K,A,Y)\to M^{k}

defines a pseudocycle, where
\mathcal{M}_{0,k+\ell}(K,A,Y)
is the moduli space of stable
K
-holomorphic spheres with
k+\ell
marked points and the last
\ell
marked points are constrained to lie on the Donaldson hypersurface
Y
. Moreover, they prove that the rational pseudocycle
\frac{1}{\ell!}(\prod_{i=1}^{k}\ev_{i})
is independent of the auxiliary data
K
and
Y
.

In contrast, Fukaya and Ono
[
9
]
construct a Kuranishi structure on
\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{0,k}(J,A)
by providing local finite-dimensional smooth manifolds around the moduli space with obstruction bundles. Using multisections of these bundles, they define a virtual fundamental class
[s^{{}^{\prime}-1}(0)]
whose pushforward

(\prod_{i=1}^{k}\ev_{i})_{*}([s^{{}^{\prime}-1}(0)])

gives a well-defined homology class in
M^{k}
.

Our main result establishes the equivalence between these two approaches:

Theorem A

(Theorem
4.33
)
.

For
K\in\mathcal{J}_{\ell+1}^{reg*}
and
J_{0}\in B^{*}\cap\mathcal{J}_{\ell+1}^{*}(M,Y;J,\theta_{1})
connected by a path
\{K_{t}\}_{t\in[0,1]}\subset\mathcal{J}_{\ell+1}^{*}(M,Y;J,\theta_{1})
with
K_{0}=J_{0}
,
K_{1}=K
, take a generic multisection
s^{{}^{\prime}}=\{s^{{}^{\prime}}_{w}\}
on the Kuranishi structure

\{(V_{w},E_{J_{0},w},\Gamma_{w},\psi_{w},s_{w})\}_{w\in\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{0,k}(J_{0},A)}.

Then the homology class given by the pseudocycle

{\bf ev}_{k}\coloneqq\prod_{i=1}^{k}\ev_{i}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}\mathcal{M}_{0,k+\ell}(K,A,Y)\rightarrow M^{k}

is equal to
\ell!
times the homology class
{\bf ev}_{k*}([s^{{}^{\prime}-1}(0)])
.

1.

We construct a Kuranishi structure on

\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{0,k+\ell}(K,A,Y)

3.

For the
K
above and domain independent
J_{0}

\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{0,k+\ell}(J_{0},A,Y)

5.

Pseudocycle by domain-dependent perturbation via Donaldson hypersurface

In this section, we introduce the pseudocycle construction given by Cieliebak and Mohnke in
[
2

\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{0,k+1}

\mathbf{J}_{\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{0,k+1}}

{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mathbf{J}_{\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{0,k+1}}}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mathbf{J}}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{0,k+1}\times M}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces M}

\mathbf{J}\to M
is the bundle whose fiber at

is the space of
\omega
-compatible complex structures on the tangent space

.

The space

lacks the Banach manifold structure needed for transversality arguments. Following
[
2

\mathcal{J}_{k+1}

\mathcal{J}_{k+1}\coloneqq\exp_{J}(\Coh(\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{0,k+1},T_{J}\mathcal{J}))\subset\mathcal{J}_{\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{0,k+1}},

where

is the tangent space of

at

,

consists of coherent maps from
\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{0,k+1}
to

with finite
\epsilon
-Floer norm and
\exp_{J}
is the exponential map. The space
\mathcal{J}_{k+1}
forms a Banach manifold.

The singular curves arising from the Gromov compactification of domain stable spheres may not remain domain stable, and thus may no longer be elements of
\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{0,k+1}

[

use the Donaldson hypersurface.

Assume that
(M,\omega)
is of dimension

with

. For sufficiently large integers

, there exist symplectic hypersurfaces

that are
\bar{J}
-holomorphic for some
\omega
-compatible almost complex structure
\bar{J}
arbitrarily

-close to
J

[

,
2
]
.

Definition 2.1

(
[

, Rmk. 8.10, Def. 9.2]
)
.

A

of degree

consisting of an

-compatible almost complex structure

,
\omega
,

, and
\theta_{0}
, and

For a Donaldson pair

and a constant

\omega
-compatible almost complex structure

such that

is
K

\mathcal{J}(M,Y;J,\theta)\coloneqq\{K\in\mathcal{J}\mid K(TY)=TY,\|K(m)-J(m)\|<\theta,m\in M\}

is the space of
\omega
-compatible almost complex structures on

. For

-holomorphic maps
f\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}S^{2}\rightarrow M
and
K
-complex hypersurface
