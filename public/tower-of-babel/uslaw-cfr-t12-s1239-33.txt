12 C.F.R. § 1239.33 — Dividends.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1239.33
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1239.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1239 (Responsibilities Of Boards Of Directors, Corporate Practices, And Corporate Governance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Bank's board of directors may not declare or pay a dividend based on projected or anticipated earnings and may not declare or pay a dividend if the par value of the Bank's stock is impaired or is projected to become impaired after paying such dividend.
