29 C.F.R. § 38.114 — Distribution of WIOA Title I financial assistance to an alternate recipient.
Citation: us:cfr:29:38.114
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-38.114
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 38 (Implementation Of The Nondiscrimination And Equal Opportunity Provisions Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the Department withholds funds from a recipient or grant applicant under these regulations, the Secretary may disburse the withheld funds directly to an alternate recipient. In such case, the Secretary will require any alternate recipient to demonstrate:
(a) The ability to comply with these regulations; and
(b) The ability to achieve the goals of the nondiscrimination and equal opportunity provisions of WIOA.
