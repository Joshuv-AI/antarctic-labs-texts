7 C.F.R. § 550.118 — Peer review.
Citation: us:cfr:7:550.118
Creator: Agricultural Research Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-550.118
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 550 (General Administrative Policy For Non-Assistance Cooperative Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon request of the REE Agency, Cooperators may be required to provide documentation in support of peer review activities, and Cooperator's personnel may be requested to participate in peer review forums to assist the REE Agency with their reviews.
