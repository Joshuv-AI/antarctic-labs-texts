31 C.F.R. § 501.747 — Procedures on remand of decisions.
Citation: us:cfr:31:501.747
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-501.747
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 501 (Reporting, Procedures And Penalties Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Either an Administrative Law Judge or a Secretary's designee, as appropriate, shall reconsider any Department decision on judicial remand to the Department. The rules of practice contained in this subpart shall apply to all proceedings held on judicial remand.
