30 C.F.R. § 581.14 — OCS mining area identification.
Citation: us:cfr:30:581.14
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-581.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 581 (Leasing Of Minerals Other Than Oil, Gas, And Sulphur In The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary, after considering the available OCS mineral resources and environmental data and information, the recommendation of any joint State/Federal task force established pursuant to § 581.13 of this part, and the comments received from interested parties, shall select the tracts to be considered for offering for lease. The selected tracts will be considered in the environmental analysis conducted for the proposed lease offering.
