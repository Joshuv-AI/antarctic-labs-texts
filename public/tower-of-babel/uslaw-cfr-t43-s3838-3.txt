43 C.F.R. § 3838.3 — What rules must I follow to explore for minerals and locate mining claims on SRHA lands?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3838.3
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3838.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3838 (Special Procedures For Locating And Recording Mining Claims And Tunnel Sites On Stockraising Homestead Act (Srha) Lands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The regulations in this part describe how to notify the surface owner before exploring for minerals or locating a mining claim on the mineral estate of SRHA lands.
(b) If you own the surface estate of SRHA lands and want to explore for minerals or locate a mining claim on the Federally-reserved mineral estate, you do not need to follow the requirements in this part, but you must follow the requirements in parts 3832, 3833, 3834 and 3835 of this chapter.
