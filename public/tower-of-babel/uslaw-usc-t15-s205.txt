15 U.S.C. § 205 — Metric system defined
Citation: us:usc:15:205
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section205
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 6 (Weights And Measures And Standard Time); Subchapter I (Weights, Measures, And Standards Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The metric system of measurement shall be defined as the International System of Units as established in 1960, and subsequently maintained, by the General Conference of Weights and Measures, and as interpreted or modified for the United States by the Secretary of Commerce.
