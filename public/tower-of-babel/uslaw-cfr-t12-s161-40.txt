12 C.F.R. § 161.40 — Public unit.
Citation: us:cfr:12:161.40
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-161.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 161 (Definitions For Regulations Affecting All Savings Associations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term public unit means the United States, any state of the United States, the District of Columbia, any territory of the United States, Puerto Rico, the Virgin Islands, any county, any municipality or any political subdivision thereof.
