49 C.F.R. § 1017.13 — Nonwaiver of rights.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1017.13
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1017.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1017 (Debt Collection—Collection By Offset From Indebted Government And Former Government Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee's involuntary payment of all or any part of a debt collected under these regulations will not be construed as a waiver of any rights that employee may have under 5 U.S.C. 5514 or any other provision of law.
