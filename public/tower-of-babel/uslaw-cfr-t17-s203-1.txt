17 C.F.R. § 203.1 — Application of the rules of this part.
Citation: us:cfr:17:203.1
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-203.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 203 (Rules Relating To Investigations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules of this part apply only to investigations conducted by the Commission. They do not apply to adjudicative or rulemaking proceedings.
