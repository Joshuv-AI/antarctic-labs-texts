51 U.S.C. § 70709 — Security clearances for Commission members and staff
Citation: us:usc:51:70709
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section70709
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 707 (Human Space Flight Independent Investigation Commission). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The appropriate Federal agencies or departments shall cooperate with a Commission in expeditiously providing to the Commission members and staff appropriate security clearances to the extent possible pursuant to existing procedures and requirements. No person shall be provided with access to classified information under this chapter without the appropriate security clearances.
