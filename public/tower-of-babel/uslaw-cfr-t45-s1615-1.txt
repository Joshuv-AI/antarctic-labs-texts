45 C.F.R. § 1615.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1615.1
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1615.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1615 (Restrictions On Actions Collaterally Attacking Criminal Convictions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prohibits the provision of legal assistance in an action in the nature of habeas corpus seeking to collaterally attack a criminal conviction.
