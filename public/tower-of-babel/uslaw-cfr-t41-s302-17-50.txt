41 C.F.R. § 302-17.50 — -17.50 Requirement to provide tax information to the agency to make the RITA calculation possible under the one-year process.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-17.50
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-17.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-17 (17—Taxes On Relocation Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees should provide the information their agency requires to make the RITA calculation. This will include tax information for any Federal and State tax returns filed for the year that the employee received covered taxable relocation expenses. Employees should submit this information as soon as they receive their relocation orders, or as soon as they file their tax returns for the most recent tax year, whichever occurs later.
