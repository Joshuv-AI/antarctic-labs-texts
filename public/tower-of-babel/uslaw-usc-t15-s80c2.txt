15 U.S.C. § 80c–2 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:15:80c–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section80c–2
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 2E (Omnibus Small Business Capital Formation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For fiscal year 1982, and for each of the three succeeding fiscal years, there are hereby authorized to be appropriated such amounts as may be necessary and appropriate to carry out the provisions and purposes of this chapter. Any sums so appropriated shall remain available until expended.
