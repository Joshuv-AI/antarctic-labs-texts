45 C.F.R. § 510.3 — Applicability of administrative provisions concerning claims under the International Claims Settlement Act of 1949.
Citation: us:cfr:45:510.3
Creator: Foreign Claims Settlement Commission Of The United States, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-510.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 510 (Filing Of Claims And Procedures Therefor). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To the extent they are not inconsistent with the provisions of the Act, the following provisions of subchapter C of this chapter shall be applicable to claims under this subchapter: §§ 509.2, 509.3, 509.4, 509.5, and 509.6.
