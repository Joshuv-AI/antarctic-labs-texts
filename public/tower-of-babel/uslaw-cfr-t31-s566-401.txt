31 C.F.R. § 566.401 — Reference to amended sections.
Citation: us:cfr:31:566.401
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-566.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 566 (Hizballah Financial Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise provided in this part, reference to any provision in or appendix to this part or chapter or to any regulation, ruling, order, instruction, directive, or license issued pursuant to this part refers to the same as currently amended.
