21 C.F.R. § 16.62 — Right to counsel.
Citation: us:cfr:21:16.62
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-16.62
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 16 (Regulatory Hearing Before The Food And Drug Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any party to a hearing under this part has the right at all times to be advised and accompanied by counsel.
