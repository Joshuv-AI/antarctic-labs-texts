15 C.F.R. § 256.5 — Duration of projects.
Citation: us:cfr:15:256.5
Creator: National Institute Of Standards And Technology, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-256.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 256 (Research Associate Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The work of a Research Associate is generally conducted on a full-time basis. Typically, Research Associates are in residence at NIST for 6 to 18 months; longer-term programs may be carried on by a succession of Research Associates. Agreements provide for cancellation by any of the parties.
