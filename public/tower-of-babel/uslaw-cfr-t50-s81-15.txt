50 C.F.R. § 81.15 — Audits.
Citation: us:cfr:50:81.15
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-81.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 81 (Conservation Of Endangered And Threatened Species Of Fish, Wildlife, And Plants—Cooperation With The States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The State is required to conduct an audit at least every two years in accordance with the provisions of Attachment P of OMB Circular A-102. Failure to conduct audits as required may result in withholding of grant payments or such other sanctions as the Secretary may deem appropriate.
