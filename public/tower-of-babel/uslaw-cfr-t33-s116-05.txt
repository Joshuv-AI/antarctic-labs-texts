33 C.F.R. § 116.05 — Complaints.
Citation: us:cfr:33:116.05
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-116.05
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 116 (Alteration Of Unreasonably Obstructive Bridges). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person, company, or other entity may submit to the District Commander of the Coast Guard district in which a bridge over a navigable water of the United States is located, a complaint that a bridge unreasonably obstructs navigation. The complaint must be in writing and include specific details to support the allegation.
