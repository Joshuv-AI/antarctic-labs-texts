Influence of dark photon on magnetized and charged particle orbits around static spherically symmetric black hole
Marek Rogatko, Paritosh Verma

Abstract
We elaborate the problem of magnetized particle motion in the spacetime of a static, spherically symmetric black hole influenced by weak magnetic fields stemming from visible and dark matter sectors. The Wald’s procedure for obtaining the weakly magnetized solution, generalized to the case of dark photon - Einstein-Maxwell gravity was implemented. The collision process analysis of two particles in the background of the black hole has been studied in order to find the signature of dark matter presence in the nearby of the object in question.

II
The Model of dark photon

In this section we shortly describe the
dark photon
model and propose a transformation of underlying gauge fields in order to simply
the underlying action, i.e., dispose of
kinetic mixing
term. In the process of this we obtain new gauge fields, being the mixture of the starting ones, with adequate factors
comprising
\alpha
-coupling constant. Next, one derives the equation of motion for charged particle affected by two gauge fields.

The action describing two coupled, massless gauge field is given by

S_{M-dark~photon}=\int d^{4}x\Big(-F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}-B_{\mu\nu}B^{\mu\nu}-{\alpha}F_{\mu\nu}B^{\mu\nu}\Big),

(1)

where
\alpha
is taken as a coupling constant.

To get rid of the
kinetic mixing
term we define new gauge fields. They can be written as

\displaystyle\tilde{A}_{\mu}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\sqrt{2-\alpha}}{2}\Big(A_{\mu}-B_{\mu}\Big),

(2)

\displaystyle{\tilde{B}}_{\mu}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\sqrt{2+\alpha}}{2}\Big(A_{\mu}+B_{\mu}\Big).

(3)

As a result one leaves with only modified gauge fields, i.e.,

F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}+B_{\mu\nu}B^{\mu\nu}+\alpha F_{\mu\nu}B^{\mu\nu}\Longrightarrow\tilde{F}_{\mu\nu}\tilde{F}^{\mu\nu}+{\tilde{B}}_{\mu\nu}{\tilde{B}}^{\mu\nu},

(4)

where we set
\tilde{F}_{\mu\nu}=2\nabla_{[\mu}\tilde{A}_{\nu]}
, and respectively
{\tilde{B}}_{\mu\nu}=2\nabla_{[\mu}{\tilde{B}}_{\nu]}
. Just the action can be rewritten as

S_{M-dark~photon}=\int d^{4}x\Big(-\tilde{F}_{\mu\nu}\tilde{F}^{\mu\nu}-{\tilde{B}}_{\mu\nu}{\tilde{B}}^{\mu\nu}\Big).

(5)

Variation of the action (
5
) with respect to
\tilde{A}_{\mu}
and
{\tilde{B}}_{\mu}
reveals the following equations of motion for Maxwell
dark matter
system:

\nabla_{\mu}\tilde{F}^{\mu\nu}=0,\qquad\nabla_{\mu}{\tilde{B}}^{\mu\nu}=0.

(6)

According to the relations (
5
) and (
6
), we shall search for the equation of motion of charged particle in the background of the modified gauge fields.
However, the consistency of the approach requires the appropriate redefinitions of the charges coupled to original fields.
The resulting action of massive charged particle influenced by both visible and
dark matter
sectors is assumed to be

S=-\int m\sqrt{-ds^{2}}+\tilde{e}_{A}\int\tilde{A}_{\mu}dx^{\mu}+\tilde{e}_{B}\int{\tilde{B}}_{\mu}dx^{\mu},

(7)

where for transformed charges are defined as

\displaystyle\tilde{e}_{A}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\sqrt{2-\alpha}}{2}\Big(e-e_{d}\Big),

(8)

\displaystyle\tilde{e}_{B}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\sqrt{2+\alpha}}{2}\Big(e+e_{d}\Big).

(9)

In the above equations
e
stands for the Maxwell charge, while
e_{d}
is connected with
dark sector
one.
In our considerations we assume that the charges
e
and
e_{d}
(as well as
\tilde{e}_{A}
and
\tilde{e}_{B}
) are positive.

The standard calculation leads to the following equation of motion

m~\frac{Du^{\mu}}{d\tau}=\Big(\tilde{e}_{A}\tilde{F}^{\mu\nu}+\tilde{e}_{B}{\tilde{B}}^{\mu\nu}\Big)u_{\nu},

(10)

where
\frac{D}{d\tau}
denotes in general case the covariant derivative with respect to the proper time.

As a result the four-momentum of the massive particle subject to two gauge fields
may be written as

{\tilde{p}}_{\mu}=mu_{\mu}+\tilde{e}_{A}\tilde{A}_{\mu}+\tilde{e}_{B}{\tilde{B}}_{\mu}.

(11)

We can notice that transforming the charges and fields back one arrives at

{\tilde{p}}_{\mu}=mu_{\mu}+eA_{\mu}+e_{d}B_{\mu}+\frac{\alpha}{2}\Big(e_{d}A_{\mu}+eB_{\mu}\Big).

(12)

Having in mind the action (
1
),
the above equation (
12
) provides the consistency in choosing transformed charges (
8
)-(
9
) and fields (
2
) and (
3
).

It can be remarked that for the special case, when
e_{d}=0
, the above relation reduces to the following:

{\tilde{p}}_{\mu}=mu_{\mu}+e\Big(A_{\mu}+\frac{\alpha}{2}B_{\mu}\Big).

(13)

This shows the modifications of the kinetic momentum by dark sector gauge field.

VII
Conclusions

In this paper we have considered motion of magnetized and charged particles
in the spacetime of a static spherically symmetric weakly magnetized black hole. We take into account the Einstein-Maxwell gravity with

dark matter
sector, using the so-called
dark photon
theory, where for the
invisible sector
is responsible auxiliary
U(1)
-gauge field coupled to the ordinary Maxwell one.
The weakly magnetized black hole solution has been found by means of Wald’s procedure
[
58
]
, utilizing the formal resemblance of equations of motion
for Maxwell-
dark photon
fields (in Lorentz gauge) with the the relations for Killing vector fields.

Using Hamilton-Jacobi equations for magnetized particle and restricting our attention to the linear terms (weak magnetic field assumption), one can show
that the magnetic coupling parameter
\beta
, responsible for the strength of external magnetic effects, is influenced by
dark matter sector
magnetic field and

\alpha
-coupling constant. These factors also effect the energy of the particle
{\cal E}
and its angular momentum
l
.

The inspection of the particle collision process in the nearby of the aforementioned weakly magnetized black hole was also taken into account. Namely,
we have considered
magnetized/charged particle with energies
{\cal E}_{i}
, angular momenta
l_{i}
,
i=1,~2,
which motions have been subject to magnetic
\beta_{(F,B)}
and charge
w^{(F,B)}
parameters.
One examines the repulsive and attractive Lorentz force pointed respectively outward and toward spherically symmetric black hole.

It happens that
for the neutral and magnetized particle collision, one achieves the description which is qualitatively the same as for the ordinary Schwarzschild black hole spacetime,
but quantitatively the values of
{\cal E}
and
l
will depend on
dark matter sector
.

For the case of charged and magnetized particles, having the same energy and
charge due to Maxwell and
dark photon
fields, with the magnetic parameter is equal to
0.5
, we studied the
dependence of
{\cal E}_{CM}
on
r
, for various values of
\alpha
-coupling constant. Due to the very small value of coupling constant,
we consider the
\alpha
-values to have the clear qualitative view of what happens if the coupling constant of
visible
and
dark sectors
varies.
It turns out that the larger
\alpha
one takes into account the bigger value of

{\cal E}_{CM}
we achieve.

The most interesting case one encounters during examination of
particles (
l_{1}=-l_{2}
) under the assumption that one has greater concentration of
dark matter
, where
w^{(B)}>w^{(F)}
.
The bunch of curves, for different values of
\alpha
-coupling constant, cuts in one point
r_{c}
(isosbestic point),
For the distances before and after it the results are quantitatively different, i.e.,
for the distance
r>r_{c}
one achieves the larger
{\cal E}_{CM}
for bigger values of coupling constant, but for
r<r_{c}
the situation is opposite.
Consequently, for the high values of the Maxwell fields, there are no difference among the curves connected with different values of
\alpha
-coupling constant.

On the other hand,
the neutral and magnetized particle collision description is qualitatively the same as for the ordinary Schwarzschild black hole spacetime.
However, quantitatively we have the different values of
{\cal E}
and
l
, because of the fact that magnetic field stemming from
dark matter sector
are considered.

Moreover the studies of charged particles in magnetic field configurations (homogeneous, dipolar and parabolic) reveal several interesting features:

1. the bigger value of the
\alpha
-coupling constant one implies, the higher value of
U_{eff}
is achieved,

2. the bigger value of
dark photon
magnetic field is consider, the larger separation distances among curves devoted to the adequate values of
\alpha
we get,

3. if the Maxwell magnetic field is greater than
dark photon
one, we obtain

U_{eff}
curve for specific value of
\alpha
, with very small separation distances (almost one curve), which divides into several one for large distances from
black hole event horizon,

4. for the case when
B^{(F)}_{0}=B^{(B)}_{0}
, the situation is in between of those described above, and has the clear separation among the curves, as well as,
the above tendencies are maintained.

Because of the fact that we suppose that
e_{d}=0
, the
dark photon
current loops do not influence on the behavior of the particle motion. However
this fact can serve as the ’possible observational experiment’ , i.e.,
if future observations show the different than for Maxwell field result, it will be a strong suspicion that ’new physics’ bounded with the
dark photon

version of the
hidden sector
is spotted.
