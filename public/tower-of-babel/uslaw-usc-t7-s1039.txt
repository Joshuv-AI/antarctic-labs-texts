7 U.S.C. § 1039 — Time for filing purchase applications
Citation: us:usc:7:1039
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1039
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 33 (Farm Tenancy); Subchapter IV (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No application for the purchase of mineral interests under sections 1033 to 1039 of this title shall be filed until ninety days after September 6, 1950.
