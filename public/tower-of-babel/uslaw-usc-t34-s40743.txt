34 U.S.C. § 40743 — Expungement of profiles
Citation: us:usc:34:40743
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section40743
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 407 (Dna Identification); Subchapter III (Dna Arrestee Collection Processes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The expungement requirements under section 12592(d) of this title shall apply to any DNA profile or DNA data collected pursuant to this subchapter for purposes of inclusion in the National DNA Index System.
