10 C.F.R. § 429.10 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:10:429.10
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-429.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 429 (Certification, Compliance, And Enforcement For Consumer Products And Commercial And Industrial Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart sets forth the procedures for manufacturers to certify that their covered products and covered equipment comply with the applicable energy conservation standards.
