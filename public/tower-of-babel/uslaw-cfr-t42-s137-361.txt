42 C.F.R. § 137.361 — Does the Secretary have any other opportunities to approve planning or design documents prepared by the Self-Governance Tribe?
Citation: us:cfr:42:137.361
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-137.361
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 137 (Tribal Self-Governance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, but only if there is an amendment to the construction project agreement that results in a significant change in the original scope of work.
