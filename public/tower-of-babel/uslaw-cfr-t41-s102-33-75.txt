41 C.F.R. § 102-33.75 — -33.75 Federal aircraft management responsibilities.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-33.75
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-33.75
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-33 (33—Management Of Government Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must:
(a) Establish agency-specific Flight Program Standards;
(b) Account for the cost of acquiring, operating, and supporting your aircraft;
(c) Account for the use of your aircraft;
(d) Maintain and account for aircraft parts;
(e) Report inventory, cost, and utilization data; and
(f) Properly dispose of aircraft and parts.
