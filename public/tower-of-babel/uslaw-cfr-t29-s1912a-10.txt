29 C.F.R. § 1912a.10 — a.10 Presence of OSHA officer or employee.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1912a.10
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1912a.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1912a (PART 1912a—NATIONAL ADVISORY COMMITTEE ON OCCUPATIONAL SAFETY AND HEALTH). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The meetings of all advisory committees shall be in the presence of an officer or employee of the Federal Government referred to in § 1912a.4. Such officer or employee shall be empowered to adjourn any meeting whenever he determines adjournment to be in the public interest.
