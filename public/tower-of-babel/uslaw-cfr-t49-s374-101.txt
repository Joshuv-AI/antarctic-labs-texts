49 C.F.R. § 374.101 — Discrimination prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:49:374.101
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-374.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 374 (Passenger Carrier Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No motor carrier of passengers subject to 49 U.S.C. subtitle IV, part B shall operate a motor vehicle in interstate or foreign commerce on which the seating of passengers is based upon race, color, creed, or national origin.
