22 U.S.C. § 1642i — Certification of awards
Citation: us:usc:22:1642i
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1642i
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 21 (Settlement Of International Claims); Subchapter IV (Claims Against Czechoslovakia). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commission shall certify to the Secretary of the Treasury, in terms of United States currency, each award made pursuant to this subchapter.
