34 C.F.R. § 390.40 — What are the matching requirements?
Citation: us:cfr:34:390.40
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-390.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 390 (Rehabilitation Short-Term Training). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A grantee must contribute to the cost of a project under this program in an amount satisfactory to the Secretary. The part of the costs to be borne by the grantee is determined by the Secretary at the time of the award.
