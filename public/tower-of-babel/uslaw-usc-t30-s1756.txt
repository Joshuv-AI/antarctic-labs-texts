30 U.S.C. § 1756 — Expanded royalty obligations
Citation: us:usc:30:1756
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section1756
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 29 (Oil And Gas Royalty Management); Subchapter III (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any lessee is liable for royalty payments on oil or gas lost or wasted from a lease site when such loss or waste is due to negligence on the part of the operator of the lease, or due to the failure to comply with any rule or regulation, order or citation issued under this chapter or any mineral leasing law.
