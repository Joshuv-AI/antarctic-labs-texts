7 U.S.C. § 5925d — International organic research collaboration
Citation: us:usc:7:5925d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section5925d
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 88 (Research); Subchapter VII (Miscellaneous Research Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary, acting through the Agricultural Research Service (including the National Agricultural Library) and the Economic Research Service, shall facilitate access by research and extension professionals, farmers, and other interested persons in the United States to, and the use by those persons of, organic research conducted outside the United States.
