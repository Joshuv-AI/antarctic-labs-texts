26 U.S.C. § 741 — Recognition and character of gain or loss on sale or exchange
Citation: us:usc:26:741
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section741
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 1 (Normal Taxes And Surtaxes); Subchapter K (Partners And Partnerships); Part II (Contributions, Distributions, And Transfers); Subpart C (Transfers Of Interests In A Partnership). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the case of a sale or exchange of an interest in a partnership, gain or loss shall be recognized to the transferor partner. Such gain or loss shall be considered as gain or loss from the sale or exchange of a capital asset, except as otherwise provided in section 751 (relating to unrealized receivables and inventory items).
