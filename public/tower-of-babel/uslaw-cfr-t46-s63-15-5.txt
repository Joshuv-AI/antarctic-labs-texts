46 C.F.R. § 63.15-5 — -5 Strainers.
Citation: us:cfr:46:63.15-5
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-63.15-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 63 (Automatic Auxiliary Boilers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Strainers must be installed in the fuel supply line. Each strainer must be self-cleaning, fitted with a bypass, or be capable of being cleaned without interrupting the fuel oil supply.
(b) The strainer must not allow a quantity of air to be trapped inside which would affect the rate of fuel flow to the burner or reduce the effective area of the straining element.
(c) The strainer must meet the requirements for strainers found in UL 296 (incorporated by reference, see § 63.05-1) and the requirements for fluid conditioner fittings found in § 56.15-5 of this subchapter.
