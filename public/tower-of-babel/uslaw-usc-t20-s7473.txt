20 U.S.C. § 7473 — Preference for Indian applicants
Citation: us:usc:20:7473
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section7473
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 70 (Strengthening And Improvement Of Elementary And Secondary Schools); Subchapter VI (Indian, Native Hawaiian, And Alaska Native Education); Part A (Indian Education); Subpart 4 (Federal Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In making grants and entering into contracts or cooperative agreements under subpart 2 or subpart 3 of this part, the Secretary shall give a preference to Indian tribes, organizations, and institutions of higher education under any program with respect to which Indian tribes, organizations, and institutions are eligible to apply for grants, contracts, or cooperative agreements.
