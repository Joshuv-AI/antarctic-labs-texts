7 C.F.R. § 764.202 — Eligibility requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:7:764.202
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-764.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 764 (Direct Loan Making). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The applicant must:
(a) Comply with the general eligibility requirements established at § 764.101 and the FO eligibility requirements of § 764.152; and
(b) Be a beginning farmer, socially disadvantaged farmer, or veteran farmer.
