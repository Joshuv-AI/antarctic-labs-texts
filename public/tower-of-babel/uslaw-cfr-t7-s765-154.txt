7 C.F.R. § 765.154 — Distribution of payments.
Citation: us:cfr:7:765.154
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-765.154
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 765 (Direct Loan Servicing—Regular). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Agency applies both regular and extra payments to each loan in the following order, as applicable:
(a) Recoverable costs and protective advances plus interest;
(b) Accrued deferred interest;
(c) Interest accrued to date of payment; and
(d) Loan principal.
