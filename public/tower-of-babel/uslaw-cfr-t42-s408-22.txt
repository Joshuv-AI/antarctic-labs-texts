42 C.F.R. § 408.22 — Increased premiums for late enrollment and for reenrollment.
Citation: us:cfr:42:408.22
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-408.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 408 (Premiums For Supplementary Medical Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For an individual who enrolls after expiration of his or her initial enrollment period or reenrolls after termination of a coverage period, the standard monthly premium determined under § 408.20 is increased by ten percent for each full twelve months in the periods specified in §§ 408.24 and 408.25.
