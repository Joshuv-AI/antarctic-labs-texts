46 C.F.R. § 5.5 — Purpose of administrative actions.
Citation: us:cfr:46:5.5
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-5.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 5 (Marine Investigation Regulations—Personnel Action). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The administrative actions against a license, certificate, merchant mariner credential, endorsement, or document are remedial and not penal in nature. These actions are intended to help maintain standards for competence and conduct essential to the promotion of safety at sea.
