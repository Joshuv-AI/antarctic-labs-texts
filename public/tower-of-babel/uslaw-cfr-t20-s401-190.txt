20 C.F.R. § 401.190 — Deceased persons.
Citation: us:cfr:20:401.190
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-401.190
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 401 (Privacy And Disclosure Of Official Records And Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We do not consider the disclosure of information about a deceased person to be a clearly unwarranted invasion of that person's privacy. However, in disclosing information about a deceased person, we follow the principles in § 401.115 to insure that the privacy rights of a living person are not violated.
