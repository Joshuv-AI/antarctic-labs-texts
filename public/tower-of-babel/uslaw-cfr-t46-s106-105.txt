46 C.F.R. § 106.105 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:46:106.105
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-106.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 106 (Requirements For Nonqualified Vessels That Perform Certain Aquaculture Support Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part apply to a documented vessel with only a registry endorsement or a foreign-flagged vessel that has been issued an Aquaculture Support Operations Waiver by the Department of Transportation (DOT) under 46 U.S.C. 12102(d)(1), for the purpose of conducting aquaculture support operations.
