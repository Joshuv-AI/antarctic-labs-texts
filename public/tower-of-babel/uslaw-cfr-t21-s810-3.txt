21 C.F.R. § 810.3 — Computation of time.
Citation: us:cfr:21:810.3
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-810.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 810 (Medical Device Recall Authority). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In computing any period of time prescribed or allowed by this part, the day of the act or event from which the designated period of time begins to run shall not be included. The computation of time is based only on working days.
