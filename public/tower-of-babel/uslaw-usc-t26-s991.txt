26 U.S.C. § 991 — Taxation of a domestic international sales corporation
Citation: us:usc:26:991
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section991
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 1 (Normal Taxes And Surtaxes); Subchapter N (Tax Based On Income From Sources Within Or Without The United States); Part IV (Domestic International Sales Corporations); Subpart A (Treatment Of Qualifying Corporations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of the taxes imposed by this subtitle upon a DISC (as defined in section 992(a)), a DISC shall not be subject to the taxes imposed by this subtitle.
