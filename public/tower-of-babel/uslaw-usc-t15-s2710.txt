15 U.S.C. § 2710 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:15:2710
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 54 (Automotive Propulsion Research And Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out the purposes of this chapter, in addition to any amounts made available for such purposes pursuant to title I of this Act, the sum of $12,500,000 for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1978.
