29 U.S.C. § 114 — Separability
Citation: us:usc:29:114
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section114
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 6 (Jurisdiction Of Courts In Matters Affecting Employer And Employee). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this chapter or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held unconstitutional or otherwise invalid, the remaining provisions of this chapter and the application of such provisions to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
