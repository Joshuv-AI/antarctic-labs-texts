31 U.S.C. § 3544 — Rights and remedies of the United States Government reserved
Citation: us:usc:31:3544
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section3544
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 35 (Accounting And Collection); Subchapter IV (Collection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This subchapter does not affect a right or remedy the United States Government has by law to recover a tax, debt, or demand.
