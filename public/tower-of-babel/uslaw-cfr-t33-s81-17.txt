33 C.F.R. § 81.17 — Certificate of Alternative Compliance: Termination.
Citation: us:cfr:33:81.17
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-81.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 81 (72 Colregs: Implementing Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Certificate of Alternative Compliance terminates if the information supplied under § 81.5(a) or the Certificate issued under § 81.9 is no longer applicable to the vessel.
