Proposed Extension Without Change of Existing Collection; Comment 
Request; Application for Continuation of Death Benefit for Student (LS-
266); Correction

AGENCY: Office of Workers' Compensation Programs, Division of Longshore 
and Harbor Workers' Compensation (OWCP/DLHWC), Department of Labor.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This is a correction of a notice published June 9, 2026, which 
in error referenced Administration of the Longshore and Harbor Workers' 
Compensation Act. The collection of information is for the Application 
of Continuation of Death Benefit for Student (LS-266).

DATES: The OMB will consider all written comments that the agency 
receives on or before August 10, 2026.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Contact Anjanette Suggs, Office of 
Workers' Compensation Programs, OWCP by telephone at 202-354-9660 or by 
email at [email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Correction

    In the Federal Register of June 9, 2026 (91 FR 34838), make the 
following correction:
    On page 34838, in the first column, correct the subject heading to 
read, ``Proposed Extension Without Change of Existing Collection; 
Comment Request; Application for Continuation of Death Benefit for 
Student (LS-266)''.
    Authority: 33 U.S.C. 902(18) and 939(a).
