33 C.F.R. § 62.54 — Ownership identification.
Citation: us:cfr:33:62.54
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-62.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 62 (United States Aids To Navigation System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Ownership identification on private or state aids to navigation is permitted so long as it does not change or hinder an understanding of the meaning of the aid to navigation.
