48 C.F.R. § 36.209 — 36.209 Construction contracts with architect-engineer firms.
Citation: us:cfr:48:36.209
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-36.209
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 36 (Construction And Architect-Engineer Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No contract for the construction of a project shall be awarded to the firm that designed the project or its subsidiaries or affiliates, except with the approval of the head of the agency or authorized representative.
