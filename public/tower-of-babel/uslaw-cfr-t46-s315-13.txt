46 C.F.R. § 315.13 — Vessel deactivation procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:46:315.13
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-315.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 315 (Agency Agreements And Appointment Of Agents). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When an agent is responsible as vessel operator to decommission and deliver a vessel to the NDRF, that agent must observe all the terms, procedures, and requirements prescribed by MARAD.
