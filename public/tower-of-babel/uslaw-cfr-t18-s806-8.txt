18 C.F.R. § 806.8 — Waiver/modification.
Citation: us:cfr:18:806.8
Creator: Susquehanna River Basin Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-806.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 806 (Review And Approval Of Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission may, in its discretion, waive or modify any of the requirements of this or any other part of its regulations if the essential purposes set forth in § 806.2 continue to be served.
