13 C.F.R. § 127.605 — What are the procedures for appealing an EDWOSB or WOSB status protest decision?
Citation: us:cfr:13:127.605
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-127.605
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 127 (Women-Owned Small Business Federal Contract Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The protested concern, the protestor, or the contracting officer may file an appeal of a WOSB or EDWOSB status protest determination with SBA's Office of Hearings and Appeals (OHA) in accordance with part 134 of this chapter.
