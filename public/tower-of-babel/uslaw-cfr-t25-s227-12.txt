25 C.F.R. § 227.12 — Mineral reserves in nonmineral entries.
Citation: us:cfr:25:227.12
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-227.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 227 (Leasing Of Certain Lands In Wind River Indian Reservation, Wyoming, For Oil And Gas Mining). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where lands have been leased under authority of said act of August 21, 1916 (39 Stat. 519), and nonmineral entry is subsequently lawfully made for such lands with a view to obtaining a restricted patent therefor, all such subsequently allowed nonmineral entries shall be with the mineral reservation prescribed by the act of July 17, 1914 (38 Stat. 509).
