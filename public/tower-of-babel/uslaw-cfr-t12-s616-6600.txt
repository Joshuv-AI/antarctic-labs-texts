12 C.F.R. § 616.6600 — Leasing limit.
Citation: us:cfr:12:616.6600
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-616.6600
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 616 (Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All leases made by Farm Credit System institutions shall be subject to the lending and leasing limit in subpart J of part 614 of this chapter.
