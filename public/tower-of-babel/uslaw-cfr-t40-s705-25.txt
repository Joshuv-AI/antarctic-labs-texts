40 C.F.R. § 705.25 — Recordkeeping requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:40:705.25
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-705.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 705 (Reporting And Recordkeeping Requirements For Certain Per- And Polyfluoroalkyl Substances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each person who is subject to the reporting requirements of this part must retain records that document any information reported to EPA. Relevant records must be retained for a period of 5 years beginning on the last day of the submission period.
