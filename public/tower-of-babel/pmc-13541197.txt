The corresponding author confirmed that the Fig S3 CCDC6wt GFP and Hoechst panels were incorrect as a result of a figure preparation error and provided replacement panels.

Regarding the concerns with Figs 4B and 4C, the corresponding author stated that the CREB1 panels serve as a shared loading control for these figures derived from the same master lysate mix run on the same gel. They clarified that the Fig 4C CREB1 panel was spliced, such that the first lane of the Fig 4C CREB1 panel corresponds to the first lane of the Fig 4B CREB1 panel. They stated that the uncropped blots underlying the Figs 4B and 4C results are no longer available.

The corresponding author’s response to the concerns with Fig 1C and Fig 3C did not resolve the editorial concerns.

In light of the above unresolved concerns, the PLOS One editors retract this article.

AC did not agree with the retraction. CL, FM, VL, SP, DS, and AF either did not respond directly or could not be reached.
