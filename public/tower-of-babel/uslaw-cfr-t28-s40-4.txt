28 C.F.R. § 40.4 — Accessibility.
Citation: us:cfr:28:40.4
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-40.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 40 (Standards For Inmate Grievance Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each inmate shall be entitled to invoke the grievance procedure regardless of any disciplinary, classification, or other administrative or legislative decision to which the inmate may be subject. The institution shall ensure that the procedure is accessible to impaired and handicapped inmates.
