15 U.S.C. § 714a — Location of offices
Citation: us:usc:15:714a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section714a
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 15 (Economic Recovery); Subchapter II (Commodity Credit Corporation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Corporation may establish offices in such place or places as it may deem necessary or desirable in the conduct of its business.
