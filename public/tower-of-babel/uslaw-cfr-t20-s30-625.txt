20 C.F.R. § 30.625 — What does “coordination of benefits” mean under Part E of EEOICPA?
Citation: us:cfr:20:30.625
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-30.625
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 30 (Claims For Compensation Under The Energy Employees Occupational Illness Compensation Program Act Of 2000, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In general, “coordination of benefits” under Part E of the Act occurs when compensation to be received under Part E is reduced by OWCP, pursuant to section 7385s-11 of EEOICPA, to reflect certain benefits the beneficiary receives under a state workers' compensation program for the same covered illness.
