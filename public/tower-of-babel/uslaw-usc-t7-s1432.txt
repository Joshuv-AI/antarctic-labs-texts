7 U.S.C. § 1432 — Extension of price support on long staple cotton seeds and products
Citation: us:usc:7:1432
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1432
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 35A (Price Support Of Agricultural Commodities); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any price support program in effect on cottonseed or any of its products shall be extended to the same seed and products of the cottons defined under section 1347(a) of this title.
