14 C.F.R. § 437.17 — Rights not conferred by an experimental permit.
Citation: us:cfr:14:437.17
Creator: Commercial Space Transportation, Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-437.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 437 (Experimental Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Issuance of an experimental permit does not relieve a permittee of its obligation to comply with any requirement of law that applies to its activities.
