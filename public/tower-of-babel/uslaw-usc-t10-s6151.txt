10 U.S.C. § 6151 — Authority to conduct program relating to fissile materials
Citation: us:usc:10:6151
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section6151
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part VI (Elements Of Department Of Defense And Other Matters); Subpart B (Atomic Energy Defense); Chapter 603 (Proliferation Matters). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Energy may conduct programs designed to improve the protection, control, and accountability of fissile materials in Russia.
