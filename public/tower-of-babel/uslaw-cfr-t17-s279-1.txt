17 C.F.R. § 279.1 — Form ADV, for application for registration of investment adviser and for amendments to such registration statement.
Citation: us:cfr:17:279.1
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-279.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 279 (Forms Prescribed Under The Investment Advisers Act Of 1940). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This form shall be filed pursuant to Rule 203-1 (§ 275.203-1 of this chapter) as an application for registration of an investment adviser pursuant to sections 203(c) or 203(g) of the Investment Advisers Act of 1940, and also as an amendment to registration pursuant to Rule 204-1 (§ 275.204-1 of this chapter).
