16 C.F.R. § 5.50 — Submission of information.
Citation: us:cfr:16:5.50
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-5.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 5 (Standards Of Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person may submit a written statement to the FTC's General Counsel, the Designated Agency Ethics Official (DAEO), or the Inspector General, where appropriate, setting forth information indicating a possible violation of subparts A-B or D of this part or 18 U.S.C. 207.
