43 C.F.R. § 14.3 — Consideration of petitions.
Citation: us:cfr:43:14.3
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-14.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 14 (Petitions For Rulemaking). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The petition will be given prompt consideration and the petitioner will be notified promptly of action taken.
