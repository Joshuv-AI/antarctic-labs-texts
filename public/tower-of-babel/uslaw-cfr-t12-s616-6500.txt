12 C.F.R. § 616.6500 — Investment in leased assets.
Citation: us:cfr:12:616.6500
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-616.6500
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 616 (Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An institution may acquire property to be leased that is consistent with current or planned leasing programs.
