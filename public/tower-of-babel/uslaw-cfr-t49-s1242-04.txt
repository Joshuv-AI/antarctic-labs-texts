49 C.F.R. § 1242.04 — Special tests.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1242.04
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1242.04
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1242 (Separation Of Common Operating Expenses Between Freight Service And Passenger Service For Railroads 1). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the separation of common expense accounts between freight and passenger services is based upon special tests or service unit factors, such tests shall be made at sufficiently frequent intervals to represent actual operating conditions. The service unit factors used are those of the reporting period.
