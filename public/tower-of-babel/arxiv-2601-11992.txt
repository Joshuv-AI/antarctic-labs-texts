Abstract
We investigate a class of non-quasi-homogeneous free divisors in the sense of Saito. These divisors are defined by equations of the form D := { h = 0 } D:=\{h=0\} on ℂ p \mathbb{C}^{p} , where the polynomial h h is specific linear combination of monomials involving the product of coordinates. For this class, we explicitly construct a Saito basis for the module of logarithmic vector fields D ​ e ​ r ​ ( l ​ o ​ g ​ D ) Der(logD) . This construction is then applied to the setting of logarithmic Poisson geometry. Focusing on the example defined by h = x ​ y + x 2 ​ y 2 + x 3 ​ y 3 h=xy+x^{2}y^{2}+x^{3}y^{3} on the Poisson algebra ( 𝒜 = ℂ ⁡ [ x , y ] , { − , − } h ) (\mathcal{A}=\mathbb{C}[x,y],\{-,-\}_{h}) , where the Poisson bracket is induced by the bivector π = h ​ ∂ x ∧ ∂ y \pi=h\partial x\wedge\partial y . We define the associated Koszul bracket on the module of logarithmic 1-forms. This enables us to prove that π \pi endows the sheaf of logarithmic 1-forms Ω 1 ​ ( l ​ o ​ g ​ D ) \Omega^{1}(logD) with a Lie-Rinehart algebra structure. Furthermore, we introduce and provide explicit descriptions for the resulting cohomology theory, which we term the logarithmic Poisson cohomology H l ​ o ​ g ∙ H_{log}^{\bullet} of { − , − } h \{-,-\}_{h} . As a related and foundational computation, we also calculate the corresponding logarithmic De Rham cohomology H D ​ R ∙ H^{\bullet}_{DR} for the divisor D D and we make a generalization in dimension 2.
