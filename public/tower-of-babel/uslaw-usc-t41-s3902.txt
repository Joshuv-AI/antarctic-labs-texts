41 U.S.C. § 3902 — Severable services contracts for periods crossing fiscal years

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 41 (Public Contracts); Chapter 39 (Specific Types Of Contracts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Authority To Enter Into Contract.—
The head of an executive agency may enter into a contract for the procurement of severable services for a period that begins in one fiscal year and ends in the next fiscal year if (without regard to any option to extend the period of the contract) the contract period does not exceed one year.
(b) Obligation of Funds.—
Funds made available for a fiscal year may be obligated for the total amount of a contract entered into under the authority of this section.
