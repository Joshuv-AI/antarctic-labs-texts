Retraction: A Simple Nomogram for Predicting Extended High-Frequency Hearing Loss in Pilots Despite Normal Audiometry: A Retrospective Study
Published: 2026

The original article titled "A Simple Nomogram for Predicting Extended High-Frequency Hearing Loss in Pilots Despite Normal Audiometry: A Retrospective Study", published in Noise & Health, on pages 112-122, Volume 27, Issue 125, 2025[1], is being retracted as the authors identified an unintended and critical error in the underlying dataset/data processing that led to deviations in some analytical results from the true situation. Although the overall trend of the core conclusions remains unchanged, some of the data presented is not sufficiently accurate. To maintain the integrity of the scientific record, the authors proactively requested that the article be withdrawn.

Plagiarism, fabrication, unethical or redundant publication violates the editorial policy of Noise & Health, which follows best practice guidelines given by the International Committee of Medical Journal Editors (ICMJE) and Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE) mentioned on the Information for Authors and as codified in the signed statements made by the authors regarding the copyright of their work.

This article is being retracted on request of the Authors, Editor-in-Chief and Editorial Board of the journal.
