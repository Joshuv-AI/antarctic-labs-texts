28 C.F.R. § 40.6 — Remedies.
Citation: us:cfr:28:40.6
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-40.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 40 (Standards For Inmate Grievance Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The grievance procedure shall afford a successful grievant a meaningful remedy. Although available remedies may vary among institutions, a reasonable range of meaningful remedies in each institution is necessary.
