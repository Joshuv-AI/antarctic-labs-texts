16 U.S.C. § 410aaa–55 — Construction of visitor center
Citation: us:usc:16:410aaa–55
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section410aaa–55
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LIX–Y (California Desert Lands Parks, Preserve, And Off-Highway Vehicle Recreation Areas); Part C (Mojave National Preserve). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to construct a visitor center in the preserve for the purpose of providing information through appropriate displays, printed material, and other interpretive programs, about the resources of the preserve.
