41 C.F.R. § 102-33.65 — -33.65 Military FSCAP requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-33.65
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-33.65
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-33 (33—Management Of Government Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When acquiring FSCAP you must:
(a) Accept FSCAP only if traceable to the original manufacturer and marked with the DoD Criticality Code; and
(b) Not install untraceable FSCAP unless inspected and recertified by the OEM or an FAA-approved facility.
