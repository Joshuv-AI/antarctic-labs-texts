34 C.F.R. § 222.67 — How may a State aid program affect a local educational agency's eligibility for assistance under section 8003(b)(2)?
Citation: us:cfr:34:222.67
Creator: Office Of Elementary And Secondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-222.67
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 222 (Impact Aid Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary determines that an LEA is not eligible for financial assistance under section 8003(b)(2) if—
(a) The LEA is in a State that has an equalized program of State aid that meets the requirements of section 8009; and
(b) The State, in determining the LEA's eligibility for or amount of State aid, takes into consideration the portion of the LEA's payment under section 8003(b)(2) that exceeds what the LEA would receive under section 8003(b)(1).
