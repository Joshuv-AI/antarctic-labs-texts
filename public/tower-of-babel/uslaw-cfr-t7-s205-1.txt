7 C.F.R. § 205.1 — Meaning of words.
Citation: us:cfr:7:205.1
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-205.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 205 (National Organic Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the purpose of the regulations in this subpart, words in the singular form shall be deemed to impart the plural and vice versa, as the case may demand.
