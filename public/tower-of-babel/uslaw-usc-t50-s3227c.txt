50 U.S.C. § 3227c — Continued applicability of the Federal Advisory Committee Act to the Board of Visitors
Citation: us:usc:50:3227c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section3227c
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 44 (National Security); Subchapter VIII (Education In Support Of National Intelligence); Part D (National Intelligence University). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Federal Advisory Committee Act (5 U.S.C. App.) 1 shall continue to apply to the Board of Visitors of the National Intelligence University on and after the transfer date.
