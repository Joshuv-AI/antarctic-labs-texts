48 U.S.C. § 1684 — Expenditure of funds for administration of Trust Territory of the Pacific Islands
Citation: us:usc:48:1684
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1684
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 14 (Trust Territory Of The Pacific Islands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

After June 30, 1954, no funds appropriated by any Act and no funds which are available or which may become available from any source whatever shall be used for administration of the Trust Territory of the Pacific Islands, except as may be specifically authorized by law.
