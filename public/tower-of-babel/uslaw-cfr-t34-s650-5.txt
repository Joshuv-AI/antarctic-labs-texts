34 C.F.R. § 650.5 — What does a fellowship award include?
Citation: us:cfr:34:650.5
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-650.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 650 (Jacob K. Javits Fellowship Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary awards fellowships consisting of the following:
(a) A stipend paid to the fellow, based upon an annual determination of the fellow's financial need, as described in § 650.42.
(b) An annual payment made to the institution in which the fellow is enrolled as described in § 650.41.
