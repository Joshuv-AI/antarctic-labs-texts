42 C.F.R. § 8.23 — Limitation on issues subject to review.
Citation: us:cfr:42:8.23
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-8.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 8 (Medications For The Treatment Of Opioid Use Disorder). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The scope of this informal review shall be limited to the facts relevant to any suspension, or proposed revocation, or adverse action, the necessary interpretations of the facts, the regulations in this subpart, and other relevant law.
