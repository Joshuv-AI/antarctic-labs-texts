48 C.F.R. § 546.704 — 546.704 Authority for use of warranties.
Citation: us:cfr:48:546.704
Creator: Chapter 5—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-546.704
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 546 (Quality Assurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

FAR clause 52.246-21, Warranty of Construction, is approved by the agency for use in solicitations and contracts when a fixed-price construction contract is contemplated.
