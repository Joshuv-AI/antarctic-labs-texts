40 U.S.C. § 1311 — Authority of President to obtain release
Citation: us:usc:40:1311
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section1311
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Chapter 13 (Public Property). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the use or benefit of the Federal Government, the President may obtain from an individual or officer to whom land has been or will be conveyed a release of the individual’s or officer’s interest to the Government.
