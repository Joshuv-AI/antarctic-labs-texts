17 C.F.R. § 171.40 — Notice of the commencement of a member responsibility action.
Citation: us:cfr:17:171.40
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-171.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 171 (Rules Relating To Review Of National Futures Association Decisions In Disciplinary, Membership Denial, Registration And Member Responsibility Actions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The notice of a Member Responsibility Action provided by the National Futures Association pursuant to its rules shall advise the affected parties of their right to petition the Commission pursuant to § 171.41 to stay the effective date of the action pending a hearing before the National Futures Association on the factual issues relevant to the suspension, restriction or remedial action ordered.
