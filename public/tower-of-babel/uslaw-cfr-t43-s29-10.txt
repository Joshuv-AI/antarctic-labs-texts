43 C.F.R. § 29.10 — Subrogation.
Citation: us:cfr:43:29.10
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-29.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 29 (Trans-Alaska Pipeline Liability Fund). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Fund pays compensation to any claimant, the Fund shall be subrogated to all rights, claims, and causes of action which that claimant has to the extent permitted by law.
