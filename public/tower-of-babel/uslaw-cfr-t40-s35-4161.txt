40 C.F.R. § 35.4161 — Does the TAG application process affect the schedule for work at my site?
Citation: us:cfr:40:35.4161
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-35.4161
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 35 (State And Local Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No, the schedule for response activities at your site is not affected by the TAG process.
