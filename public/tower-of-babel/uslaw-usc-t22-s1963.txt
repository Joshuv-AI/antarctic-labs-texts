22 U.S.C. § 1963 — United Nations Emergency Force
Citation: us:usc:22:1963
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1963
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 24A (Middle East Peace And Stability). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President should continue to furnish facilities and military assistance, within the provisions of applicable law and established policies, to the United Nations Emergency Force in the Middle East, with a view to maintaining the truce in that region.
