36 C.F.R. § 291.4 — Preservation of existing authorities.
Citation: us:cfr:36:291.4
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-291.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 291 (Paleontological Resources Preservation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part do not alter or diminish the authority of the Forest Service under any other law to manage, preserve, and protect paleontological resources on National Forest System lands in addition to the protection provided under the Act or this part.
