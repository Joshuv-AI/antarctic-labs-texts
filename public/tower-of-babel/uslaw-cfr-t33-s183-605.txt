33 C.F.R. § 183.605 — Definitions.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 183 (Boats And Associated Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in this subpart:
“Fuel” means gasoline.
“Open to the atmosphere” means a compartment that has at least 15 square inches of open area directly exposed to the atmosphere for each cubic foot of net compartment volume.
