29 C.F.R. § 778.122 — Computation of overtime for commission employees on established basic rate.
Citation: us:cfr:29:778.122
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-778.122
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 778 (Overtime Compensation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Overtime pay for employees paid wholly or partly on a commission basis may be computed on an established basic rate, in lieu of the method described above. See § 778.400 and part 548 of this chapter.
