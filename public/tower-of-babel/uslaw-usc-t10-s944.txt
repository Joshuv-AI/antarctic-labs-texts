10 U.S.C. § 944 — Art. 144. Procedure
Citation: us:usc:10:944
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section944
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 47 (Uniform Code Of Military Justice); Subchapter XII (United States Court Of Appeals For The Armed Forces). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States Court of Appeals for the Armed Forces may prescribe its rules of procedure and may determine the number of judges required to constitute a quorum.
