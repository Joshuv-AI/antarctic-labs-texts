19 U.S.C. § 2547 — Consultations with representatives of domestic interests
Citation: us:usc:19:2547
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section2547
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 13 (Trade Agreements Act Of 1979); Subchapter II (Technical Barriers To Trade (Standards)); Part B (Functions Of Federal Agencies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In carrying out the functions for which responsible under this subchapter, the Trade Representative and the Secretary concerned shall solicit technical and policy advice from the committees, established under section 2155 of this title, that represent the interests concerned, and may solicit advice from appropriate State agencies and private persons.
