29 C.F.R. § 1960.54 — Training of top management officials.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1960.54
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1960.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1960 (Basic Program Elements For Federal Employee Occupational Safety And Health Programs And Related Matters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each agency shall provide top management officials with orientation and other learning experiences which will enable them to manage the occupational safety and health programs of their agencies. Such orientation should include coverage of section 19 of the Act, Executive Order 12196, the requirements of this part, and the agency safety and health program.
