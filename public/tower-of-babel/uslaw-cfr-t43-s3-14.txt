43 C.F.R. § 3.14 — Examinations by field officer.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3.14
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3 (Preservation Of American Antiquities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The field officer in charge may at all times examine the permit of any person or institution claiming privileges granted in accordance with the act and this part, and may fully examine all work done under such permit.
