52 U.S.C. § 20103 — Selection of registration facilities
Citation: us:usc:52:20103
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title52-section20103
Context: United States Code, Title 52 (Voting And Elections); Chapter 201 (Voting Accessibility For The Elderly And Handicapped). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Each State or political subdivision responsible for registration for Federal elections shall provide a reasonable number of accessible permanent registration facilities.
(b)
Subsection (a) does not apply to any State that has in effect a system that provides an opportunity for each potential voter to register by mail or at the residence of such voter.
