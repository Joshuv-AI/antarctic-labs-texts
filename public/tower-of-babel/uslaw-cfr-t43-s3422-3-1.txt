43 C.F.R. § 3422.3-1 — -1 Bidding systems.

Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3420 (Competitive Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The provisions of 10 CFR part 378 1 are not applicable to this part.
(b) The Department may conduct lease sales using cash bonus—fixed royalty bidding systems or any other bidding system adopted through rulemaking procedures.
