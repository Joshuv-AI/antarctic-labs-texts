10 C.F.R. § 205.288 — Interim and ancillary orders.
Citation: us:cfr:10:205.288
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-205.288
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 205 (Administrative Procedures And Sanctions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director of the Office of Hearings and Appeals or his designee may issue any interim or ancillary orders, or make any rulings or determinations to ensure that refund proceedings, including the actions of the administrator and the custodian of the funds involved in a refund proceeding, are conducted in an appropriate manner and are not unduly delayed.
