7 C.F.R. § 1.658 — When will the hearing record close?
Citation: us:cfr:7:1.658
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1.658
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1 (Administrative Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The hearing record will close when the ALJ closes the hearing, unless he or she directs otherwise.
(b) Evidence may not be added after the hearing record is closed, but the transcript may be corrected under § 1.656(b).
