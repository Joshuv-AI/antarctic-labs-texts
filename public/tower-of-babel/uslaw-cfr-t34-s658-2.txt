34 C.F.R. § 658.2 — Who is eligible to apply for assistance under this program?
Citation: us:cfr:34:658.2
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-658.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 658 (Undergraduate International Studies And Foreign Language Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following are eligible to apply for assistance under this part:
(a) Institutions of higher education.
(b) Consortia of institutions of higher education.
(c) Partnerships between nonprofit educational organizations and institutions of higher education.
(d) Public and private nonprofit agencies and organizations, including professional and scholarly associations.
