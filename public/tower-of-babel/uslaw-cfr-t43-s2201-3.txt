43 C.F.R. § 2201.3 — Appraisals.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2201.3
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2201.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2200 (Exchanges: General Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Federal and non-Federal parties to an exchange shall comply with the appraisal standards set forth in §§ 2201.3-1 through 2201.3-4 of this part and, to the extent appropriate, with the Department of Justice “Uniform Appraisal Standards for Federal Land Acquisitions” when appraising the values of the Federal and non-Federal lands involved in an exchange.
