30 C.F.R. § 47.73 — Providing labels and MSDSs to customers.
Citation: us:cfr:30:47.73
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-47.73
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 47 (HAZARD COMMUNICATION (HazCom)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For a hazardous chemical produced at the mine, the operator must provide customers, upon request, with the chemical's label or a copy of the label information, and the chemical's MSDS.
