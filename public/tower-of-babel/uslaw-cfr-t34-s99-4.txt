34 C.F.R. § 99.4 — What are the rights of parents?
Citation: us:cfr:34:99.4
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-99.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 99 (Family Educational Rights And Privacy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An educational agency or institution shall give full rights under the Act to either parent, unless the agency or institution has been provided with evidence that there is a court order, State statute, or legally binding document relating to such matters as divorce, separation, or custody that specifically revokes these rights.
