12 C.F.R. § 52.1 — Authority and purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:12:52.1
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-52.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 52 (Regulatory Reporting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Authority. This part is issued pursuant to 12 U.S.C. 93a, 161, 1463(a), 1464(v), and 1817(a)(12).
(b) Purpose. This part establishes a reduced reporting requirement for a covered depository institution making its reports of condition for the first and third calendar quarters of a year.
