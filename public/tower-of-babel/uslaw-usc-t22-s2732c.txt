22 U.S.C. § 2732c — Submission of federally funded research and development center reports to Congress
Citation: us:usc:22:2732c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2732c
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 38 (Department Of State). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 30 days after receiving a report or other written product provided to the Department by federally funded research and development centers (FFRDCs) and consultant groups that were supported by funds congressionally appropriated to the Department, the Secretary shall provide the appropriate congressional committees—
(1)
the report or written product, including the original proposal for the report;
(2)
the amount provided by the Department to the FFRDC; and
(3)
a detailed description of the value the Department derived from the report.
