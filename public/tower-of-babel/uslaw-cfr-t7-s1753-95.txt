7 C.F.R. § 1753.95 — Advance of loan funds.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1753.95
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1753.95
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1753 (Telecommunications System Construction Policies And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Advance of loan funds needed to meet the certification borrower's current financial obligations are to be requested on RUS Form 481 for construction and engineering items supported by appropriate RUS Forms 158 and 159. For items other than construction or engineering, other supporting data shall be submitted. (See 7 CFR part 1744 subpart C.)
