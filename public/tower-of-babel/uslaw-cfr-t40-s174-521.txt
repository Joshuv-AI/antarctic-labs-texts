40 C.F.R. § 174.521 — Neomycin phosphotransferase II; exemption from the requirement of a tolerance.
Citation: us:cfr:40:174.521
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-174.521
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 174 (Procedures And Requirements For Plant-Incorporated Protectants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Residues of the neomycin phosphotransferase II (NPTII) enzyme are exempted from the requirement of a tolerance in all food commodities when used as a plant-incorporated protectant inert ingredient.
