20 U.S.C. § 7803 — Applicability to Bureau of Indian Education operated schools
Citation: us:usc:20:7803
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section7803
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 70 (Strengthening And Improvement Of Elementary And Secondary Schools); Subchapter VIII (General Provisions); Part A (Definitions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of any competitive program under this chapter—
(1)
a consortium of schools operated by the Bureau of Indian Education;
(2)
a school operated under a contract or grant with the Bureau of Indian Education in consortium with another contract or grant school or a tribal or community organization; or
(3)
a Bureau of Indian Education school in consortium with an institution of higher education, a contract or grant school, or a tribal or community organization,
shall be given the same consideration as a local educational agency.
