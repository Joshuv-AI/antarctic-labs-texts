18 U.S.C. § 229C — Individual self-defense devices
Citation: us:usc:18:229C
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section229C
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 11B (Chemical Weapons). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to prohibit any individual self-defense device, including those using a pepper spray or chemical mace.
