20 U.S.C. § 80r–8 — Consideration of recommendations of Presidential Commission
Citation: us:usc:20:80r–8
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section80r–8
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 3 (Smithsonian Institution, National Museums And Art Galleries); Subchapter XIV (National Museum Of African American History And Culture). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In carrying out their duties under this subchapter, the Council and the Board of Regents shall take into consideration the reports and plans submitted by the National Museum of African American History and Culture Plan for Action Presidential Commission under the National Museum of African American History and Culture Plan for Action Presidential Commission Act of 2001 (Public Law 107–106).
