20 C.F.R. § 216.94 — Entitlement to more than one divorced spouse annuity.
Citation: us:cfr:20:216.94
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-216.94
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 216 (Eligibility For An Annuity). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an individual is entitled to more than one annuity as a divorced spouse, only the larger annuity will be paid. However, if the individual so chooses, he or she can receive the smaller annuity rather than the larger annuity.
