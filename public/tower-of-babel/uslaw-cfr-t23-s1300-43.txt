23 C.F.R. § 1300.43 — Continuing requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:23:1300.43
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-1300.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 1300 (Uniform Procedures For State Highway Safety Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notwithstanding the expiration of an annual grant application, the provisions in 2 CFR parts 200 and 1201 and 23 CFR part 1300, including but not limited to equipment and audit, continue to apply to the grant funds authorized under 23 U.S.C. Chapter 4 and Section 1906.
