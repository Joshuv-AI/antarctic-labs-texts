48 C.F.R. § 1201.301-72 — 1201.301-72 Transportation Acquisition Circular numbering.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1201.301-72
Creator: Chapter 12—Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1201.301-72
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1201 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Transportation Acquisition Circulars (TACs) will be numbered consecutively on a fiscal year basis beginning with number “01” prefixed by the last two digits of the fiscal year (e.g., TACs 21-01 and 21-02 indicate the first two TACs issued in fiscal year 2021).
