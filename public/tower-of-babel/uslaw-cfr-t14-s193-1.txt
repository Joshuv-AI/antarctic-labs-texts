14 C.F.R. § 193.1 — What does this part cover?
Citation: us:cfr:14:193.1
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-193.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 193 (Protection Of Voluntarily Submitted Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part describes when and how the FAA protects from disclosure safety and security information that you submit voluntarily to the FAA. This part carries out 49 U.S.C. 40123, protection of voluntarily submitted information.
