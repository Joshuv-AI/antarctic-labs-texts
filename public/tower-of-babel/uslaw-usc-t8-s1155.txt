8 U.S.C. § 1155 — Revocation of approval of petitions; effective date
Citation: us:usc:8:1155
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title8-section1155
Context: United States Code, Title 8 (Aliens And Nationality); Chapter 12 (Immigration And Nationality); Subchapter II (Immigration); Part I (Selection System). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Homeland Security may, at any time, for what he deems to be good and sufficient cause, revoke the approval of any petition approved by him under section 1154 of this title. Such revocation shall be effective as of the date of approval of any such petition.
