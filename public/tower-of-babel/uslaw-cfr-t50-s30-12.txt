50 C.F.R. § 30.12 — Disposition of feral animals.
Citation: us:cfr:50:30.12
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-30.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 30 (Range And Feral Animal Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Feral animals taken on wildlife refuge areas may be disposed of by sale on the open market, gift or loan to public or private institutions for specific purposes, and as otherwise provided in section 401 of the act of June 15, 1935 (49 Stat. 383, 16 U.S.C. 715s).
