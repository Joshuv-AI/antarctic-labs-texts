32 C.F.R. § 274.5 — Notice of intent to bid.
Citation: us:cfr:32:274.5
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-274.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 274 (Regulations Governing Competitive Bidding On U.S. Government Guaranteed Military Export Loan Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any individual or organization, syndicates, or other group which intends to submit a bid, must, when required by the notice, give written notice of such intent on the appropriate form at the place and within the time specified in the public notice. Such notice, which shall be given to the Federal Reserve Bank of New York, 33 Liberty Street, New York, NY 10045, will not constitute a commitment to bid.
