12 C.F.R. § 1041.14 — Severability.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1041.14
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1041.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1041 (Payday, Vehicle Title, And Certain High-Cost Installment Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this part are separate and severable from one another. If any provision is stayed or determined to be invalid, the remaining provisions shall continue in effect.
