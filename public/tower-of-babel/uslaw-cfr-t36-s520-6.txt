36 C.F.R. § 520.6 — Nuisances.
Citation: us:cfr:36:520.6
Creator: Smithsonian Institution
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-520.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 520 (Rules And Regulations Governing The Buildings And Grounds Of The National Zoological Park Of The Smithsonian Institution). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The use of loud, abusive, or otherwise improper language; unwarranted loitering, sleeping or assembly; the creation of any hazard to persons or things; improper disposal of rubbish; spitting; prurient prying; the commission of any obscene or indecent act, or any other unseemly or disorderly conduct on the premises; throwing articles of any kind on the premises, or climbing upon any part of the building is prohibited.
