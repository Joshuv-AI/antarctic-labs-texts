5 C.F.R. § 842.1101 — Purpose.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 842 (Federal Employees Retirement System—Basic Annuity). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart contains regulations of the Office of Personnel Management (OPM) to supplement chapter 34 of title 22, United States Code, concerning FERS retirement service credit eligibility for satisfactory Peace Corps volunteer and volunteer leader service.
