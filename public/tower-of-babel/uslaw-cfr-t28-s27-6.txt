28 C.F.R. § 27.6 — Extensions of time.
Citation: us:cfr:28:27.6
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-27.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 27 (Whistleblower Protection For Federal Bureau Of Investigation Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director may extend, for extenuating circumstances, any of the time limits provided in these regulations relating to proceedings before him and to requests for review by the Deputy Attorney General.
