29 C.F.R. § 4043.22 — Amendment decreasing benefits payable.

Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4043.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4043 (Reportable Events And Certain Other Notification Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Reportable event. A reportable event occurs when an amendment to a plan is adopted under which the retirement benefit payable from employer contributions with respect to any participant may be decreased.
(b) Waiver. Notice is waived for this event.
