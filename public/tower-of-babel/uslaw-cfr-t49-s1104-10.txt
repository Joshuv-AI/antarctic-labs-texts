49 C.F.R. § 1104.10 — Rejection of a deficient document.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1104.10
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1104.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1104 (Filing With The Board-Copies-Verification-Service-Pleadings, Generally). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Board may reject a document, submitted for filing if the Board finds that the document does not comply with the rules.
(b) The Board may either return the material unfiled or tentatively accept the material for filing and advise the person tendering it of the deficiency and require that the deficiency be corrected.
