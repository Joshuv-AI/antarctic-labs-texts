2 C.F.R. § 180.200 — What is a covered transaction?
Citation: us:cfr:2:180.200
Creator: Office Of Management And Budget Government-Wide Guidance For Federal Financial Assistance
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-180.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 180 (Omb Guidelines To Agencies On Government-Wide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A covered transaction is a nonprocurement or procurement transaction subject to this part's prohibitions. It may be a transaction at:
(a) The primary tier, between a Federal agency and a person (see Appendix to this part); or
(b) A lower tier between a participant in a covered transaction and another person.
