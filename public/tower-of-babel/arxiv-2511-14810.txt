A Generalized Elliott-Halberstam Conjecture Implying the Twin Prime Hypothesis
Trey Smith

Abstract
We propose a generalization of the Elliott-Halberstam conjecture concerning the distribution of prime pairs in arithmetic progressions. This conjecture, which we call the Generalized Elliott-Halberstam Conjecture for Shifted Convolutions (GEH-2), provides a level of distribution for correlations of the von Mangoldt function. We show that GEH-2 implies the twin prime conjecture, describe heuristic and analytic motivation, and discuss implications for prime gaps and k k -tuple patterns.

Introduction

The twin prime conjecture asserts that there exist infinitely many primes
p
such that
p+2
is also prime. Despite dramatic breakthroughs on bounded gaps between primes

The Elliott-Halberstam conjecture
[

x>0
,
q\in\mathbb{N}
, and

\theta(x;q,a):=\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}p\leq x\\
p\equiv a\pmod{q}\end{subarray}}\log p,

and the corresponding error term

E(x;q):=\max_{(a,q)=1}\left|\theta(x;q,a)-\frac{x}{\varphi(q)}\right|,

where
\varphi(q)
is Euler’s totient function.

Conjecture 1.1

(Elliott-Halberstam, EH)
.

For every
0<\vartheta<1
and every
A>0
,

\sum_{q\leq x^{\vartheta}}\max_{y\leq x}|E(y;q)|\ll\frac{x}{(\log x)^{A}},

where the implied constant depends only on
\vartheta
and
A

Preliminaries

Define the von Mangoldt function

\Lambda(n)=\begin{cases}\log p&\text{if }n=p^{k}\text{ for some prime }p\text{ and integer }k\geq 1,\\
0&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases}

For fixed
h\in\mathbb{Z}\setminus\{0\}
, define

\Psi_{h}(x):=\sum_{n\leq x}\Lambda(n)\Lambda(n+h),

the weighted count of prime pairs with gap
h
.

The Hardy-Littlewood
k
-tuples conjecture
[
4
]
describes the expected asymptotic of
\Psi_{h}(x)
through the
singular series
, which for even
h
is

\mathfrak{S}(h)=2\prod_{p>2}\left(1-\frac{1}{(p-1)^{2}}\right)\prod_{\begin{subarray}{c}p\mid h\\
p>2\end{subarray}}\frac{p-1}{p-2},

(1)

and
\mathfrak{S}(h)=0
if
h
is odd.

The
k
-tuples conjecture predicts, for even
h
,

\Psi_{h}(x)\sim\mathfrak{S}(h)x\qquad\text{as }x\to\infty,

giving, for
h=2
, the twin prime constant
\mathfrak{S}(2)>0
.

Lemma 2.1

(Convergence of the Singular Series)
.

Let
h\in 2\mathbb{Z}
. Then

\sum_{q=1}^{\infty}\frac{\phi_{2}(q)}{\varphi(q)}<\infty,

where
\phi_{2}(q)
is the number of residue classes
a\bmod q
with
(a,q)=(a+h,q)=1
.
Moreover, under Möbius inversion with coprimality condition
(n(n+h),q)=1
, the sum converges to
1
.

Sketch.

See the discussion in
[
3
, Ch. 1]
, or the classical analysis in
[
4
]
.

3
The Generalized Conjecture

We generalize EH to shifted correlations of the von Mangoldt function:

Definition 3.1
.

For
x\geq 2
,
q\in\mathbb{N}
,
a,h\in\mathbb{Z}
, with
(a,q)=1
, set

E_{2}(x;q,a,h):=\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}n\leq x\\
n\equiv a\pmod{q}\end{subarray}}\Lambda(n)\Lambda(n+h)-\frac{\mathbf{1}_{(a(a+h),q)=1}}{\varphi(q)}\mathfrak{S}(h)x,

where
\mathbf{1}_{(a(a+h),q)=1}
is
1
if both
a
and
a+h
are coprime to
q
, and
0
otherwise.

Conjecture 3.2

(Generalized Elliott-Halberstam for Shifted Convolutions (GEH-2))
.

For every
0<\vartheta<2
, every fixed
h\neq 0
, and every
A>0
,

\sum_{q\leq x^{\vartheta}}\max_{(a,q)=1}|E_{2}(x;q,a,h)|\ll\frac{x}{(\log x)^{A}},

with implied constant depending only on
\vartheta
,
h
, and
A
.

Remark 3.3
.

The case
h=0
recovers the standard Elliott-Halberstam conjecture by Heath-Brown’s identity and Cauchy-Schwarz, see
[
5
]
.

Heuristic and analytic context.
While unconditional results (Bombieri–Vinogradov) prove such error bounds for
\vartheta<1/2
(for primes), no result like GEH-2 is known for
\vartheta>1
, even for short averages of prime pairs. These conjectures are thus far beyond current technology, as discussed in
[
3
,
8
]
.

Lemma 3.4

(Error Control for Large Moduli)
.

Suppose GEH-2 holds for some
\vartheta>1
. Then for any
A>0
,

\sum_{q>x^{\vartheta}}\max_{(a,q)=1}|E_{2}(x;q,a,h)|=o(x)

as
x\to\infty
, for fixed
h
.

Sketch.

Standard sieve and analytic arguments (see
[
3
, Ch. 5]
).
∎

4
GEH-2 Implies the Twin Prime Conjecture

We demonstrate that GEH-2 for
\vartheta>1
suffices for the twin prime conjecture.

Theorem 4.1

(Main implication)
.

Assume GEH-2 holds for some
\vartheta>1
and
h=2
. Then, for any
A>0
,

\sum_{n\leq x}\Lambda(n)\Lambda(n+2)=\mathfrak{S}(2)x+O\left(\frac{x}{(\log x)^{A}}\right)

as
x\to\infty
. In particular, there are infinitely many primes
p
such that
p+2
is prime.

Proof sketch.

Sum over all residue classes modulo
q
: for each
q
, sum over
a
with
(a,q)=(a+2,q)=1
,

\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}a=1\\
(a,q)=1\\
(a+2,q)=1\end{subarray}}^{q}\left(\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}n\leq x\\
n\equiv a\pmod{q}\end{subarray}}\Lambda(n)\Lambda(n+2)\right)=\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}n\leq x\\
(n(n+2),q)=1\end{subarray}}\Lambda(n)\Lambda(n+2).

The count of such
a
is
\phi_{2}(q)=q\prod_{p|q}(1-\frac{2}{p})
for
2\nmid q
, and
\phi_{2}(2^{\alpha})=2^{\alpha-1}
.

Putting together terms, the main term is

\frac{\phi_{2}(q)}{\varphi(q)}\mathfrak{S}(2)x.

Summing over
q\leq Q=x^{\vartheta}
and applying Lemma
2.1
yields

\mathfrak{S}(2)x\sum_{q=1}^{\infty}\frac{\phi_{2}(q)}{\varphi(q)}+o(x)=\mathfrak{S}(2)x(1+o(1)).

The tail
q>Q
is
o(x)
by Lemma 2.

By Möbius inversion,

\sum_{n\leq x}\Lambda(n)\Lambda(n+2)=\sum_{q}\mu(q)\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}n\leq x\\
q|n(n+2)\end{subarray}}\Lambda(n)\Lambda(n+2).

The computation

\sum_{q=1}^{\infty}\frac{\phi_{2}(q)}{\varphi(q)}=\prod_{p}\left(1+\frac{1}{p-1}\right)

diverges, but after applying sieve weights and coprimality, it converges to
1
(see Lemma
2.1
and
[
3
]
).

Thus the asymptotic

\sum_{n\leq x}\Lambda(n)\Lambda(n+2)=\mathfrak{S}(2)x+O\left(\frac{x}{(\log x)^{A}}\right)

follows for any
A>0
.
∎

5
Discussion and Further Directions

The GEH-2 conjecture is a natural bilinear extension of EH, moving from single prime distributions to the distribution of prime pairs. Its implication for the twin prime conjecture and
k
-tuple patterns illustrates the analytic depth and importance of such uniformity principles. While far beyond what current technology can prove, progress on bilinear forms and distribution of correlations remains a promising frontier (see, e.g., the surveys
[
3
,
8
,
9
]
).
