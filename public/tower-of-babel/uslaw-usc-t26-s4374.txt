26 U.S.C. § 4374 — Liability for tax
Citation: us:usc:26:4374
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section4374
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 34 (Taxes On Certain Insurance Policies); Subchapter A (Policies Issued By Foreign Insurers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The tax imposed by this chapter shall be paid, on the basis of a return, by any person who makes, signs, issues, or sells any of the documents and instruments subject to the tax, or for whose use or benefit the same are made, signed, issued, or sold. The United States or any agency or instrumentality thereof shall not be liable for the tax.
