42 U.S.C. § 10250 — Termination of Office
Citation: us:usc:42:10250
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section10250
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 108 (Nuclear Waste Policy); Subchapter IV (Nuclear Waste Negotiator). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Office shall cease to exist not later than 30 days after the date 7 years after December 22, 1987.
