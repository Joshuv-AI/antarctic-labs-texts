10 C.F.R. § 63.23 — Elimination of repetition.
Citation: us:cfr:10:63.23
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-63.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 63 (Disposal Of High-Level Radioactive Wastes In A Geologic Repository At Yucca Mountain, Nevada). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In its application or environmental impact statement, DOE may incorporate, by reference, information contained in previous applications, statements, or reports filed with the Commission, if the references are clear and specific and copies of the information incorporated are made available to the public locations near the site of the proposed geologic repository, as specified in § 63.22(d).
