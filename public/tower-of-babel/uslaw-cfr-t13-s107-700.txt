13 C.F.R. § 107.700 — Compliance with size standards in part 121 of this chapter as a condition of Assistance.
Citation: us:cfr:13:107.700
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-107.700
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 107 (Small Business Investment Companies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You are permitted to provide financial assistance and management services only to a Small Business. To determine whether an applicant is a Small Business, you may use either the financial size standards in § 121.301(c)(2) of this chapter or the industry standard covering the industry in which the applicant is primarily engaged, as set forth in § 121.301(c)(1) of this chapter.
