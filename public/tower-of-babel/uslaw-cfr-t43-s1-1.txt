43 C.F.R. § 1.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:43:1.1
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-1.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 1 (Practices Before The Department Of The Interior). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part governs the participation of individuals in proceedings, both formal and informal, in which rights are asserted before, or privileges sought from, the Department of the Interior.
