46 U.S.C. § 51328 — Student support
Citation: us:usc:46:51328
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section51328
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part B (Merchant Marine Service); Chapter 513 (United States Merchant Marine Academy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Maritime Administrator shall—
(1)
require a biannual survey of midshipmen, faculty, and staff of the Academy assessing the environment of the Academy; and
(2)
require an annual survey of faculty and staff of the Academy assessing the Sea Year program.
