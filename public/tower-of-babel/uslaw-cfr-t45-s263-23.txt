45 C.F.R. § 263.23 — How does a State prevent a recipient from using the IDA account for unqualified purposes?
Citation: us:cfr:45:263.23
Creator: Office Of Family Assistance (Assistance Programs), Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-263.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 263 (Expenditures Of State And Federal Tanf Funds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To prevent recipients from using the IDA account improperly, States may do the following:
(a) Count withdrawals as earned income in the month of withdrawal (unless already counted as income);
(b) Count withdrawals as resources in determining eligibility; or
(c) Take such other steps as the State has established in its State plan or written State policies to deter inappropriate use.
