22 C.F.R. § 1003.8 — Exemptions.
Citation: us:cfr:22:1003.8
Creator: Inter-American Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1003.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1003 (Rules Safeguarding Personal Information In Iaf Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No Inter-American Foundation records system or systems as such are exempted from the provisions of 5 U.S.C. 552a as permitted under certain conditions by 5 U.S.C. 552a (j) and (k).
