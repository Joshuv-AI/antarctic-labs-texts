20 C.F.R. § 1001.125 — Standards of performance governing complaints of veterans and eligible persons.
Citation: us:cfr:20:1001.125
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary For Veterans' Employment And Training Service, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-1001.125
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 1001 (Services For Veterans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each SDP shall display information on the various complaint systems to advise veterans and eligible persons about procedures for filing employment service, Federal contractor, equal opportunity, and other complaints.
