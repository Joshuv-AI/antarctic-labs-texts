14 C.F.R. § 39.27 — What do I do if the airworthiness directive conflicts with the service document on which it is based?
Citation: us:cfr:14:39.27
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-39.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 39 (Airworthiness Directives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In some cases an airworthiness directive incorporates by reference a manufacturer's service document. In these cases, the service document becomes part of the airworthiness directive. In some cases the directions in the service document may be modified by the airworthiness directive. If there is a conflict between the service document and the airworthiness directive, you must follow the requirements of the airworthiness directive.
