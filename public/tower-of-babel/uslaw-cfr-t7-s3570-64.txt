7 C.F.R. § 3570.64 — Applications determined ineligible.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3570.64
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3570.64
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3570 (Community Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If, at any time, an application is determined ineligible, the processing office will notify the applicant in writing of the reasons. The applicant will be advised that it may appeal the decision. (See 7 CFR part 11.)
