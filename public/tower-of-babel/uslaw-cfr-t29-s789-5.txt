29 C.F.R. § 789.5 — “* * * acquired * * * in good faith * * * for value without notice * * *.”
Citation: us:cfr:29:789.5
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 789 (GENERAL STATEMENT ON THE PROVISIONS OF SECTION 12(a) AND SECTION 15(a)(1) OF THE FAIR LABOR STANDARDS ACT OF 1938, RELATING TO WRITTEN ASSURANCES). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 12(a) and section 15(a)(1) of the Act provide that a purchaser must acquire the goods in good faith in reliance on the specified written assurance in order to be accorded the statutory protection.
