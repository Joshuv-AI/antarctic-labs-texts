5 U.S.C. § 105 — Executive agency
Citation: us:usc:5:105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section105
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part I (The Agencies Generally); Chapter 1 (Organization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of this title, “Executive agency” means an Executive department, a Government corporation, and an independent establishment.
