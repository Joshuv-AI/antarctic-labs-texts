Characterizations of ellipsoids by means of the strong intersection property
E. Morales-Amaya

Abstract
. Let E 1 , E 2 ⊂ ℝ n E_{1},E_{2}\subset\mathbb{R}^{n} be two homothetic solid ellipsoids, n ≥ 3 n\geq 3 , with center at the origin O O of a system coordinates of ℝ n \mathbb{R}^{n} , and E 1 ⊂ int E 2 E_{1}\subset\operatorname*{int}E_{2} . Then there exists a O O -symmetric ellipsoid E 3 E_{3} such that E 3 E_{3} is homothetic to E 1 E_{1} and, for all x ∈ bd E 2 x\in\operatorname*{bd}E_{2} , there exists an hyperplano Π ⁡ ( x ) \Pi(x) , O ∈ Π ⁡ ( x ) O\in\Pi(x) , such that the relation (1) S ⁡ ( E 1 , x ) ∩ S ⁡ ( E 1 , − x ) = Π ⁡ ( x ) ∩ E 3 . \displaystyle S(E_{1},x)\cap S(E_{1},-x)=\Pi(x)\cap E_{3}. holds, where S ⁡ ( E 1 , x ) S(E_{1},x) and S ⁡ ( E 1 , − x ) S(E_{1},-x) are the supporting cones of E 1 E_{1} with apex x x and − x -x , respectively. In this work we prove that aforesaid condition characterizes the ellipsoid. In fact, we prove that if K , S , G ⊂ ℝ n K,S,G\subset\mathbb{R}^{n} are three convex bodies, n ≥ 3 n\geq 3 , O ∈ int K O\in\operatorname*{int}K , K ⊂ int G ⊂ int S K\subset\operatorname*{int}G\subset\operatorname*{int}S and G G strictly convex and, for all x ∈ bd S x\in\operatorname*{bd}S , there exists y ∈ bd S y\in\operatorname*{bd}S , O O in the line defined by x , y x,y , an hyperplane Π ⁡ ( x ) \Pi(x) , O ∈ Π ⁡ ( x ) O\in\Pi(x) , such that the relation (2) S ⁡ ( K , x ) ∩ S ⁡ ( K , y ) = Π ⁡ ( x ) ∩ bd G . \displaystyle S(K,x)\cap S(K,y)=\Pi(x)\cap\operatorname*{bd}G. holds, where S ⁡ ( K , x ) S(K,x) and S ⁡ ( K , y ) S(K,y) are the supporting cones of K K with apex x x and y y , respectively, then G , K G,K and S S are O O -symmetric homothetic ellipsoids. Let

Let
