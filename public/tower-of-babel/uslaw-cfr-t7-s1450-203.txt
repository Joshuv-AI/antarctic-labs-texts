7 C.F.R. § 1450.203 — Eligible persons and legal entities.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1450.203
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1450.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1450 (Biomass Crop Assistance Program (Bcap)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In order to be eligible to enter into a BCAP contract for this subpart, a person or legal entity must be an owner, operator, or tenant of eligible land within a project area, as defined in § 1450.204 and be the person or entity with the ability to perform under the terms of the contract.
