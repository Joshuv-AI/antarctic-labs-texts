29 C.F.R. § 35.23 — Assurances required.
Citation: us:cfr:29:35.23
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-35.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 35 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Age In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance From The Department Of Labor). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A recipient or applicant for Federal financial assistance from DOL shall sign a written assurance, in a form specified by DOL, that the program or activity will be operated in compliance with the Act and this part. In subsequent applications to DOL, an applicant may incorporate this assurance by reference.
