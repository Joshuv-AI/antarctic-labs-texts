20 C.F.R. § 685.390 — When may eligible migrant and seasonal farmworkers receive related assistance?
Citation: us:cfr:20:685.390
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-685.390
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 685 (National Farmworker Jobs Program Under Title I Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Eligible MSFWs may receive related assistance services when the grantee identifies and documents the need for the related assistance, which may include a statement by the eligible MSFW.
