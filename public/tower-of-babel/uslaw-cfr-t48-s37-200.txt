48 C.F.R. § 37.200 — 37.200 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:48:37.200
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-37.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 37 (Service Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes policies and procedures for acquiring advisory and assistance services by contract. The subpart applies to contracts, whether made with individuals or organizations, that involve either personal or nonpersonal services.
