14 C.F.R. § 302.21 — Appearances.
Citation: us:cfr:14:302.21
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-302.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 302 (Rules Of Practice In Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Any party to a proceeding may appear and be heard in person or by a designated representative.
(b) No register of persons who may practice before the Department is maintained and no application for admission to practice is required.
(c) Any person practicing or desiring to practice before the Department may, upon hearing and good cause shown, be suspended or barred from practicing.
