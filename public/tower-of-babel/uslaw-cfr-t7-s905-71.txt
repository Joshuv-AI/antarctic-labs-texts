7 C.F.R. § 905.71 — Other information.
Citation: us:cfr:7:905.71
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-905.71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 905 (Oranges, Grapefruit, Tangerines, And Pummelos Grown In Florida). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon request of the committee, made with the approval of the Secretary, every handler shall furnish the committee, in such manner and at such times as it prescribes, such other information as will enable it to perform its duties under this subpart.
