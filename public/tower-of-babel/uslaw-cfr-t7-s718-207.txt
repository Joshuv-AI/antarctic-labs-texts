7 C.F.R. § 718.207 — Determining base acres when reconstitution is made by combination.
Citation: us:cfr:7:718.207
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-718.207
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 718 (Provisions Applicable To Multiple Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) When two or more farms or tracts are combined for a year, that year's base acres, with respect to the combined farm or tract, as required by applicable program regulations, will not be greater than the sum of the base acres for each of the farms or tracts comprising the combination, subject to the provisions of § 718.204.
