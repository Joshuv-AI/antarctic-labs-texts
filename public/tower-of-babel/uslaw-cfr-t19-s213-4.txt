19 C.F.R. § 213.4 — Disclosure of receipt of technical assistance.
Citation: us:cfr:19:213.4
Creator: United States International Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-213.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 213 (Trade Remedy Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An eligible small business that has received technical assistance from the Office must state that it has received technical assistance from the Trade Remedy Assistance Office in any resulting petition, complaint or application which is filed with the Commission or any other agency which administers the trade law under which remedies or benefits are sought.
