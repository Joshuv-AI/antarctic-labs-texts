49 C.F.R. § 392.12 — Highway-rail crossings; safe clearance.
Citation: us:cfr:49:392.12
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-392.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 392 (Driving Of Commercial Motor Vehicles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No driver of a commercial motor vehicle shall drive onto a highway-rail grade crossing without having sufficient space to drive completely through the crossing without stopping.
