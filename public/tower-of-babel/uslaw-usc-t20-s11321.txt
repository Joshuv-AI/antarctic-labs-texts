20 U.S.C. § 1132–1 — Special rule
Citation: us:usc:20:1132–1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1132–1
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 28 (Higher Education Resources And Student Assistance); Subchapter VI (International Education Programs); Part D (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may waive or reduce the non-Federal share required under this subchapter for institutions that—
(1)
are eligible to receive assistance under part A or B of subchapter III or under subchapter V; and
(2)
have submitted a grant application under this section that demonstrates a need for a waiver or reduction, as determined by the Secretary.
