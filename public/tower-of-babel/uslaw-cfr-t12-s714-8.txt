12 C.F.R. § 714.8 — Are the early payment provisions, or interest rate provisions, applicable in leasing arrangements?
Citation: us:cfr:12:714.8
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-714.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 714 (Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You are not subject to the early payment provisions set forth in § 701.21(c)(6) of this chapter. You are also not subject to the interest rate provisions in § 701.21(c)(7).
