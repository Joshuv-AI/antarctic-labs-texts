2 U.S.C. § 2211 — Establishment
Citation: us:usc:2:2211
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section2211
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 31 (Capitol Visitor Center); Subchapter II (Office Of The Capitol Visitor Center). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is established within the Office of the Architect of the Capitol the Office of the Capitol Visitor Center (in this chapter referred to as the “Office”), to be headed by the Chief Executive Officer for Visitor Services (in this chapter referred to as the “Chief Executive Officer”).
