40 C.F.R. § 17.23 — Comments by other parties.
Citation: us:cfr:40:17.23
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-17.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 17 (Implementation Of The Equal Access To Justice Act In Epa Administrative Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any party to a proceeding other than the applicant and EPA counsel may file comments on an application within 30 calendar days after it is served or on an answer within 15 calendar days after it is served.
