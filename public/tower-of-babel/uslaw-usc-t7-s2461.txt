7 U.S.C. § 2461 — Appeals
Citation: us:usc:7:2461
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2461
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 57 (Plant Variety Protection); Subchapter II (Protectability Of Plant Varieties And Certificates Of Protection); Part G (Appeals To Courts And Other Review). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

From the decisions made under sections 2404, 2443, 2501, and 2568 of this title appeal may, within sixty days or such further times as the Secretary allows, be taken under the Federal Rules of Appellate Procedure. The United States Court of Appeals for the Federal Circuit shall have jurisdiction of any such appeal.
