21 C.F.R. § 343.3 — Definitions.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 343 (Internal Analgesic, Antipyretic, And Antirheumatic Drug Products For Over-The-Counter Human Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in this part:
Analgesic—antipyretic drug. An agent used to alleviate pain and to reduce fever.
Cardiovascular drug. An agent used to prevent ischemic events.
Rheumatologic drug. An agent used for the treatment of rheumatologic disorders.
