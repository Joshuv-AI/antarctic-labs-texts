40 C.F.R. § 98.510 — Definition of the source category.
Citation: us:cfr:40:98.510
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-98.510
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 98 (Mandatory Greenhouse Gas Reporting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This source category includes any facility that produces caprolactam, glyoxal, or glyoxylic acid. This source category excludes the production of glyoxal through the LaPorte process (i.e., the gas-phase catalytic oxidation of ethylene glycol with air in the presence of a silver or copper catalyst).
