5 C.F.R. § 2418.6 — When will the FLRA allow a debtor to pay an FLRA debt in installments instead of one lump sum?
Citation: us:cfr:5:2418.6
Creator: Federal Labor Relations Authority, General Counsel Of The Federal Labor Relations Authority And Federal Service Impasses Panel
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-2418.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 2418 (Flra Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a debtor is financially unable to pay the debt in one lump sum, then the FLRA may accept payment of an FLRA debt in regular installments, in accordance with 31 CFR 901.8.
