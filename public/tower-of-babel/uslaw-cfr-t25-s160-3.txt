25 C.F.R. § 160.3 — Leases to include description of lands.
Citation: us:cfr:25:160.3
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-160.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 160 (Inclusion Of Liens In All Patents And Instruments Executed). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is important, also, for superintendents in leasing irrigable lands to present to the project engineer lists containing descriptions of the lands involved for his approval of the irrigable acreage and for checking as to whether or not such lands are in fact irrigable under existing works. Strict compliance with this section is required for the purpose of avoiding error.
