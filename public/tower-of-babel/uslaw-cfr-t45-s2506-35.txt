45 C.F.R. § 2506.35 — Will the collection of a debt by salary offset act as a waiver of my rights to dispute the claimed debt?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2506.35
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2506.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2506 (Collection Of Debts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No, your involuntary payment of all or any portion of a debt under this subpart will not be construed as a waiver of any rights that you may have under 5 U.S.C. 5514 or other provisions of a law or written contract, unless there are statutory or contractual provisions to the contrary.
