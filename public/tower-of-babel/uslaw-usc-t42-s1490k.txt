42 U.S.C. § 1490k — FHA insurance
Citation: us:usc:42:1490k
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1490k
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 8A (Slum Clearance, Urban Renewal, And Farm Housing); Subchapter III (Farm Housing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to act as an agent of the Secretary of Housing and Urban Development to recommend insurance of any mortgage meeting the requirements of section 1709 of title 12.
