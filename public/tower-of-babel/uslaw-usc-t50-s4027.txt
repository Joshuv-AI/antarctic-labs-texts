50 U.S.C. § 4027 — Guarantee of residency for spouses of servicemembers
Citation: us:usc:50:4027
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section4027
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 50 (Servicemembers Civil Relief); Subchapter VII (Further Relief). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purposes of establishing the residency of a spouse of a servicemember for any purpose (including the registration of a business), the spouse of a servicemember may elect to use the same residence as the servicemember regardless of the date on which the marriage of the spouse and the servicemember occurred.
