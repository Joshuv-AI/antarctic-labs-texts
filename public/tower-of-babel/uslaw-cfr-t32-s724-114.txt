32 C.F.R. § 724.114 — Applicant.
Citation: us:cfr:32:724.114
Creator: Department Of The Navy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-724.114
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 724 (Naval Discharge Review Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A former member of the naval service who has been discharged administratively in accordance with the directives of the naval service or by sentence of a special court-martial under title 10 U.S.C. 801 et seq. (Uniform Code of Military Justice) and, in accordance with statutory and regulatory provisions:
(a) Whose case is considered by the NDRB at the request of the former member, of, if authorized under § 724.113, the surviving spouse, next-of-kin or legal representative, or
(b) Whose case is considered on the NDRB's own motion.
