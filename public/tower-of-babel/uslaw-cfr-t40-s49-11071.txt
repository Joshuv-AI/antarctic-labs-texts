40 C.F.R. § 49.11071 — Identification of plan.
Citation: us:cfr:40:49.11071
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-49.11071
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 49 (Indian Country: Air Quality Planning And Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This section and §§ 49.11072 through 49.11100 contain the implementation plan for the Confederated Tribes of the Warm Springs Reservation. This plan consists of a combination of Tribal rules and measures and Federal regulations and measures which apply within the Warm Springs Reservation.
