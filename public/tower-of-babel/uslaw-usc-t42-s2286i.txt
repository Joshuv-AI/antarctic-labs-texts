42 U.S.C. § 2286i — Annual authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:42:2286i
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2286i
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 23 (Development And Control Of Atomic Energy); Subchapter XVII–A (Defense Nuclear Facilities Safety Board). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Authorizations of appropriations for the Board for fiscal years beginning after fiscal year 1989 shall be provided annually in authorization Acts.
