17 C.F.R. § 37.703 — Monitoring for financial soundness.
Citation: us:cfr:17:37.703
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-37.703
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 37 (Swap Execution Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A swap execution facility shall monitor its members to ensure that they continue to qualify as eligible contract participants as defined in section 1a(18) of the Act.
