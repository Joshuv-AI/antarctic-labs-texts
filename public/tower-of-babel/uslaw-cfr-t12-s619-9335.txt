12 C.F.R. § 619.9335 — Supplemental retirement plan or supplemental executive retirement plan.
Citation: us:cfr:12:619.9335
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-619.9335
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 619 (Definitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A nonqualified retirement plan that provides benefits in addition to those covered by other retirement plans for all employees and funded in whole or part by a Farm Credit institution.
