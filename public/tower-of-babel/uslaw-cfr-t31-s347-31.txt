31 C.F.R. § 347.31 — Crediting of retirement savings bond.
Citation: us:cfr:31:347.31
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-347.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 347 (Regulations Governing Retirement Savings Bonds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each retirement savings bond issued to an Auto-IRA custodian must be credited to an IRA under the state Auto-IRA program with the custodian.
