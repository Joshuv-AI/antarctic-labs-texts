48 C.F.R. § 23.400 — 23.400 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:48:23.400
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-23.400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 23 (Environment, Sustainable Acquisition, And Material Safety). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes policies and procedures for—
(a) Obtaining information needed for Government compliance with right-to-know laws and pollution prevention requirements;
(b) Contractor compliance with environmental management systems; and
(c) Ensuring waste reduction at Federal facilities.
