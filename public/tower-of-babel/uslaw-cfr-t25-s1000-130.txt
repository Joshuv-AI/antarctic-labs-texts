25 C.F.R. § 1000.130 — What additional information may be submitted to the Secretary to facilitate negotiations?
Citation: us:cfr:25:1000.130
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary, Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-1000.130
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 1000 (Annual Funding Agreements Under The Tribal Self-Government Act Amendments To The Indian Self-Determination And Education Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At the option of the Tribe/Consortium, a Tribe/Consortium may identify BIA and non-BIA programs that the Tribe/Consortium may wish to subsequently negotiate for inclusion in a funding agreement. The inclusion of PSFAs in a funding agreement is not limited by the provision of this additional information.
