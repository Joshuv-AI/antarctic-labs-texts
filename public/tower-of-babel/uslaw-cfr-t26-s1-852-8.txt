26 C.F.R. § 1.852-8 — -8 Information returns.
Citation: us:cfr:26:1.852-8
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.852-8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 1 (Income Taxes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in §§ 1.852-6 and 1.852-7 shall be construed to relieve regulated investment companies or their shareholders from the duty of filing information returns required by regulations prescribed under the provisions of subchapter A, chapter 61 of the Code.
