46 C.F.R. § 116.960 — Guards for exposed hazards.
Citation: us:cfr:46:116.960
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-116.960
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 116 (Construction And Arrangement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An exposed hazard, such as gears or rotating machinery, must be protected by a cover, guard, or rail.
