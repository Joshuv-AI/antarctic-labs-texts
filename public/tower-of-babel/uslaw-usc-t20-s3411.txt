20 U.S.C. § 3411 — Establishment of Department; appointment of Secretary
Citation: us:usc:20:3411
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section3411
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 48 (Department Of Education); Subchapter II (Establishment Of The Department). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is established an executive department to be known as the Department of Education. The Department shall be administered, in accordance with the provisions of this chapter, under the supervision and direction of a Secretary of Education. The Secretary shall be appointed by the President, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate.
