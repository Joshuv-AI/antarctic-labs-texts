47 C.F.R. § 26.101 — Eligibility.
Citation: us:cfr:47:26.101
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-26.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 26 (Space Launch Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following entities are eligible for Space Launch Services licenses:
(a) A non-Federal entity that conducts space launch operations; or
(b) A parent of such entity or a subsidiary of such entity if either conducts space launch operations.
