46 C.F.R. § 183.550 — General alarm systems.
Citation: us:cfr:46:183.550
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-183.550
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 183 (Electrical Installation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All vessels with overnight accommodations must be equipped with a general alarm system. The public address system required by § 184.610 of this chapter may be used to sound the general alarm signal.
