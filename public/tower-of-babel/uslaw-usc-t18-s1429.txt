18 U.S.C. § 1429 — Penalties for neglect or refusal to answer subpena
Citation: us:usc:18:1429
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1429
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 69 (Nationality And Citizenship). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person who has been subpenaed under the provisions of subsection (d) of section 336 of the Immigration and Nationality Act to appear at the final hearing of an application for naturalization, and who shall neglect or refuse to so appear and to testify, if in the power of such person to do so, shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than five years, or both.
