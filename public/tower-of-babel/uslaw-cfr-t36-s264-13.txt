36 C.F.R. § 264.13 — Unauthorized use.
Citation: us:cfr:36:264.13
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-264.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 264 (Property Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in §§ 264.11 and 264.12, whoever manufactures, sells, or possesses the official symbol of the Mount St. Helens National Volcanic Monument may be subject to criminal penalty under 18 U.S.C. 701.
