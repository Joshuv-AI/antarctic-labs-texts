7 C.F.R. § 1000.70 — Producer-settlement fund.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1000.70
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Milk), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1000.70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1000 (General Provisions Of Federal Milk Marketing Orders). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The market administrator shall establish and maintain a separate fund known as the producer-settlement fund into which the market administrator shall deposit all payments made by handlers pursuant to §§ ____.71, ____.76, and ____.77 of each Federal milk order and out of which the market administrator shall make all payments pursuant to §§ ____.72 and ____.77 of each Federal milk order. Payments due any handler shall be offset by any payments due from that handler.
