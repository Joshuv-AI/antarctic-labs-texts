46 U.S.C. § 53512 — FIFO and LIFO withdrawals
Citation: us:usc:46:53512
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section53512
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part C (Financial Assistance Programs); Chapter 535 (Capital Construction Funds). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) FIFO.—
Except as provided in subsection (b), an amount withdrawn from an account under this chapter shall be treated as withdrawn on a first-in-first-out basis.
(b) LIFO.—
An amount withdrawn from an account under this chapter shall be treated as withdrawn on a last-in-first-out basis if it is—
(1)
a nonqualified withdrawal for research, development, and design expenses incident to new and advanced vessel design, machinery, and equipment; or
(2)
an amount treated as a nonqualified withdrawal under section 53510(d) of this title.
