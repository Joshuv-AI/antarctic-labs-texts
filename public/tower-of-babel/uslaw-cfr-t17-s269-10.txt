17 C.F.R. § 269.10 — Form TH—Notification of reliance on temporary hardship exemption.
Citation: us:cfr:17:269.10
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-269.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 269 (Forms Prescribed Under The Trust Indenture Act Of 1939). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Form TH shall be filed by any electronic filer who submits to the Commission, pursuant to a temporary hardship exemption, a document in paper format that otherwise would be required to be submitted electronically, as prescribed by Rule 201(a) of Regulation S-T (§ 232.201(a) of this chapter).
