30 U.S.C. § 531 — Approval of United States officials
Citation: us:usc:30:531
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section531
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 12 (Multiple Mineral Development Of The Same Tracts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to waive, amend, or repeal the requirement of any provision of any law for approval of any official of the United States whose approval prior to prospecting, exploring, or mining would be required.
