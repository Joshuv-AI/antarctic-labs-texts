16 U.S.C. § 7144 — Termination of authority
Citation: us:usc:16:7144
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section7144
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 90 (Secure Rural Schools And Community Self-Determination); Subchapter III (County Funds). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The authority to initiate projects under this subchapter terminates on September 30, 2028.
(b) Availability
Any county funds not obligated by September 30, 2029, shall be returned to the Treasury of the United States.
