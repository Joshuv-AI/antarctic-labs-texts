Magnetic excitations in the 1/3 plateau state in InCu 3 (OH) 6 Cl 3
Moyu Kato 1, Hiroyuki K. Yoshida 1, R. Kumar 1, Yoshihiko Ihara 1

Abstract
Magnetic dynamics in InCu 3 (OH) 6 Cl 3 was investigated from the NMR relaxation rate measurement. In InCu 3 (OH) 6 Cl 3 , the magnetization isotherm shows a plateau at the 1/3 of full-saturation magnetization, characterizing the 1/3 plateau state. As the 1/3 plateau state appears above 7 T upto 14 T, the microscopic magnetic properties were investigated with the NMR measurement in steady fields. The temperature and field dependence of 1 / T 1 1/T_{1} measurement reveals a gap in the magnetic excitation spectrum and its evolution with field in the 1/3 plateau state. The field dependence of spin gap provides an important information to understand the microscopic origin of 1/3 plateau state in the kagome antiferromagnets.

The sample used for the NMR experiment was prepared through the hydrothermal synthesis.
Cu chloride and In nitride as ingredients, lithium hydroxide as a catalyst were dissolved to the distilled water in a 25 ml Teflon-lined stainless steel autoclave, which was kept at 220

Then, light blue polycrystalline samples were obtained as reported in Ref.

The crystal structure (Space group

The Cl sites, our target of NMR measurement, are classified into two groups;

(0.3288,0.3288,0.8425)

(0.6610,0.6610,0.1675)

(1/3,2/3,0.0071)

The site symmetry of Cl-3 and Cl-4 sites are

For the NMR analyses, Cl-1 and Cl-2 sites on the mirror plane are labeled as Cl-m sites and Cl-3 and Cl-4 sites on the 3-fold axis are labeled as Cl-T sites.

For the
35

Its dimension is
\phi 3

Knight shift measurements at high temperature were performed in a fixed magnetic field of approximately 13 T.
The field strength was calibrated by the

At low temperatures, the NMR spectra were obtained by the field-sweep method, for which the NMR intensity at a fixed frequency was recorded during field sweep at a constant ramping rate.
The nuclear spin lattice relaxation rate

To explore the magnetic dynamics, the temperature dependence of
1/T_{1}
was measured at four different fields across the onset of 1/3 plateau state.
At high temperatures above 50 K,
1/T_{1}
is nearly independent of both temperature and field showing a constant value of
1/T_{1}\simeq 40

This is a typical behavior for a localized spin system at temperature higher than the energy scale of exchange interaction.
[

From the constant

J/k_{B}\simeq 5

In 4.0 T a peak was observed in the temperature dependence of

at
T_{\rm N}\simeq 2

The peak in

The field dependence of

is consistent with the previous study by heat capacity.

When the LRO transition is suppressed by fields, the magnetic fluctuations above

is also suppressed as evident from the decrease in

at 8.6 T starting below 10 K.
The moderate temperature dependence in 8.6 T becomes steeper in the 1/3 plateau state at 12.7 T,
where

shows the thermal excitation-type exponential behavior.
This result clearly suggests that the magnetic excitations are gapped in the 1/3 plateau state.
The gap size is estimated to be
\Delta/k_{B}=11

The coefficient

C=120

It is worth noting that the gap size is smaller than the Zeeman gap for an electron spin with

To reveal the origin of field-induced gap in the 1/3 plateau state, the detailed field dependence of

The deviation from the exponential behavior at low fields is ascribed to the LRO ground state.
The exponential field dependence in the plateau state suggests that the gap in low-energy magnetic excitations evolves linearly with magnetic fields.
By assuming a conventional Zeeman gap

The absolute value of

To fit the experimental data as shown by red dashed line in Fig.

To summarize, we have measured the temperature and field dependence of
1/T_{1}
around the 1/3 plateau state from the
35
Cl-NMR measurement on the powder sample of InCu
3
(OH)
6
Cl
3
.
The temperature dependence of
1/T_{1}
in the 1/3 plateau state is characterized by the exponential behavior suggesting a spin gap in the magnetic excitation spectrum.
To consistently explain the temperature and field dependence, we found that the spin gap appears around the onset of 1/3 plateau state and evolves linearly with field.
The static magnetic structure in the 1/3 plateau state has not been investigated from the broad powder spectra.
To reveal the static magnetic configuration in the 1/3 plateau state, a single crystalline sample should be measured in future.
