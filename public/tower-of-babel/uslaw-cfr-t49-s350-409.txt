49 C.F.R. § 350.409 — High Priority Program: Responses to applications.

Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 350 (Motor Carrier Safety Assistance Program (Mcsap) And High Priority Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Approval. If FMCSA awards a grant or cooperative agreement, the applicant will receive a grant agreement to execute.
(b) Denial. If FMCSA denies the grant or cooperative agreement, the applicant will receive a notice of denial.
