46 C.F.R. § 298.9 — Prioritization of applications.
Citation: us:cfr:46:298.9
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-298.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 298 (Vessel And Shipyard Financing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

MARAD will prioritize processing Applications for Vessels determined by the Secretary of War as suitable for service as a United States naval or military auxiliary in time of war or national emergency and that meet a shortfall in sealift capacity or capability, followed by Vessels designated as Vessels of National Interest pursuant to § 298.37 of this part.
