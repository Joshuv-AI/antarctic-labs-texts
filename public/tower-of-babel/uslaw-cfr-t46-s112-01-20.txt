46 C.F.R. § 112.01-20 — -20 Final emergency power source.
Citation: us:cfr:46:112.01-20
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-112.01-20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 112 (Emergency Lighting And Power Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A final emergency power source is one that automatically supplies power to the emergency loads under § 112.15-5 and automatically transfers the temporary emergency loads under § 112.15-1 when the potential of the final emergency source reaches 85 to 95% of normal value.
