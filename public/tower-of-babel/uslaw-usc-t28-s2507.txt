28 U.S.C. § 2507 — Calls and discovery
Citation: us:usc:28:2507
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section2507
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part VI (Particular Proceedings); Chapter 165 (United States Court Of Federal Claims Procedure). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The United States Court of Federal Claims may call upon any department or agency of the United States or upon any party for any information or papers, not privileged, for purposes of discovery or for use as evidence. The head of any department or agency may refuse to comply with a call issued pursuant to this subsection when, in his opinion, compliance will be injurious to the public interest.
(b)
Without limitation on account of anything contained in subsection (a) of this section, the court may, in accordance with its rules, provide additional means for the discovery of any relevant facts, books, papers, documents or tangible things, not privileged.
(c)
The Court of Federal Claims may use all recorded and printed reports made by the committees of the Senate or House of Representatives.
