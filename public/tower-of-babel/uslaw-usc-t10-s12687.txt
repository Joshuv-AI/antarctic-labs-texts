10 U.S.C. § 12687 — Reserves under confinement by sentence of court-martial: separation after six months confinement
Citation: us:usc:10:12687
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section12687
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel Generally); Chapter 1221 (Separation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as otherwise provided in regulations prescribed by the Secretary of Defense, a Reserve sentenced by a court-martial to a period of confinement for more than six months may be separated from that Reserve’s armed force at any time after the sentence to confinement has become final under chapter 47 of this title and the Reserve has served in confinement for a period of six months.
