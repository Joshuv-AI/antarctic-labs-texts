42 U.S.C. § 300l–1 — Requirement regarding medicaid
Citation: us:usc:42:300l–1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section300l–1
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter XIII (Preventive Health Measures With Respect To Breast And Cervical Cancers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may not make a grant under section 300k of this title for a program in a State unless the State plan under title XIX of the Social Security Act [42 U.S.C. 1396 et seq.] for the State includes the screening procedures specified in subparagraphs (A) and (B) of section 300m(a)(2) of this title as medical assistance provided under the plan.
