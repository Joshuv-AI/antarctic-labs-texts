41 C.F.R. § 102-2.15 — -2.15 What is the authority for the FMR system?
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-2.15
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-2.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-2 (2—Federal Management Regulation System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator of General Services prescribes and issues the FMR under the authority of the Federal Property and Administrative Services Act of 1949, as amended, 40 U.S.C. 486(c), as well as other applicable Federal laws and authorities.
