36 U.S.C. § 2306 — Insurance for Museum
Citation: us:usc:36:2306
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section2306
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (United States Government Organizations Involved With Observances And Ceremonies); Chapter 23 (United States Holocaust Memorial Museum). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Museum shall maintain insurance on the memorial museum to cover such risks, in such amount, and containing such terms and conditions as the Museum deems necessary.
