25 C.F.R. § 20.301 — What is the goal of General Assistance?
Citation: us:cfr:25:20.301
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-20.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 20 (Financial Assistance And Social Services Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The goal of the General Assistance program is to increase self-sufficiency. Each General Assistance recipient must work with the social services worker to develop and sign an Individual Self-Sufficiency Plan (ISP). The plan must outline the specific steps the individual will take to increase independence by meeting the goal of employment.
