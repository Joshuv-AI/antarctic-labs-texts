47 C.F.R. § 80.763 — Effective antenna height.
Citation: us:cfr:47:80.763
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-80.763
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 80 (Stations In The Maritime Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The effective height of the antenna is the vertical distance between the center of the radiating system above the mean sea level and the average terrain elevation.
