2 C.F.R. § 1132.3 — Exceptions from requirements of this part.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1132.3
Creator: Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1132.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1132 (Recipient Procurement Procedures: General Award Terms And Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Exceptions are permitted from the administrative requirements in this part only as follows:
(a) As described in 2 CFR 1126.3, and
(b) Based on any language in 2 CFR 200.110(a) regarding the applicability of the procurement standards in 2 CFR part 200.
