6 U.S.C. § 346 — Consolidation and co-location of offices
Citation: us:usc:6:346
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section346
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 1 (Homeland Security Organization); Subchapter VII (Management). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 1 year after November 25, 2002, the Secretary shall develop and submit to Congress a plan for consolidating and co-locating—
(1)
any regional offices or field offices of agencies that are transferred to the Department under this chapter, if such officers 1 are located in the same municipality; and
(2)
portions of regional and field offices of other Federal agencies, to the extent such offices perform functions that are transferred to the Secretary under this chapter.
