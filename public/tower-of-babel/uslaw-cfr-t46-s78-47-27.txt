46 C.F.R. § 78.47-27 — -27 Self-contained breathing apparatus.

Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 78 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Lockers or spaces containing self-contained breathing apparatus shall be marked “SELF-CONTAINED BREATHING APPARATUS.”
