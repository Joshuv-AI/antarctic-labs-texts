5 C.F.R. § 837.601 — Generally.
Citation: us:cfr:5:837.601
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-837.601
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 837 (Reemployment Of Annuitants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise provided by this subpart, when an annuitant who is reemployed under circumstances that provide for continuation of annuitant status during reemployment dies, death benefits are payable under CSRS or FERS as if the individual died as an annuitant, and not as employee.
