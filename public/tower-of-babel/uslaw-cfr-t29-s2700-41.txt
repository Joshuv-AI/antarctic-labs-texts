29 C.F.R. § 2700.41 — Time to file.
Citation: us:cfr:29:2700.41
Creator: Federal Mine Safety And Health Review Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-2700.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 2700 (Procedural Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Secretary. A discrimination or interference complaint shall be filed by the Secretary within 30 days after the Secretary's written determination that a violation has occurred.
(b) Miner, representative of miners, or applicant for employment. A discrimination or interference complaint may be filed by a complaining miner, representative of miners, or applicant for employment within 30 days after receipt of a written determination by the Secretary that no violation has occurred.
