6 C.F.R. § 13.12 — Notice of hearing.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 6 (Domestic Security), Part 13 (Administrative Remedies For False Claims And Statements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) When the Presiding Officer receives the Complaint and answer, the Presiding Officer will promptly serve a notice of hearing upon the Defendant in the manner prescribed by § 13.8.
(b) Such notice will include:
(1) The tentative time and place, and the nature of the hearing;
(2) The legal authority and jurisdiction under which the hearing is to be held;
(3) The matters of fact and law to be asserted;
(4) A description of the procedures for the conduct of the hearing;
(5) The name, address, and telephone number of the Representative of the Government and of the Defendant, if any; and
(6) Such other matters as the Presiding Officer deems appropriate.
