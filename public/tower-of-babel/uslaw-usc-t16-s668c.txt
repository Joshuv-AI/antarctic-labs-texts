16 U.S.C. § 668c — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:16:668c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section668c
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 5A (Protection And Conservation Of Wildlife); Subchapter II (Protection Of Bald And Golden Eagles). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this subchapter “whoever” includes also associations, partnerships, and corporations; “take” includes also pursue, shoot, shoot at, poison, wound, kill, capture, trap, collect, molest or disturb; “transport” includes also ship, convey, carry, or transport by any means whatever, and deliver or receive or cause to be delivered or received for such shipment, conveyance, carriage, or transportation.
