43 C.F.R. § 2864.24 — Do I always have to use Standard Form 299 when submitting my application for a Communications Uses authorization?
Citation: us:cfr:43:2864.24
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2864.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2860 (Communications Uses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must file an application for communications uses using Standard Form 299.
