51 U.S.C. § 60608 — Space weather benchmarks
Citation: us:usc:51:60608
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section60608
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 606 (Space Weather). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The interagency working group established under section 60601(c) shall periodically review and update the benchmarks described in the report of the National Science and Technology Council entitled “Space Weather Phase 1 Benchmarks” and dated June 2018, as necessary, based on—
(1)
any significant new data or advances in scientific understanding that become available; or
(2)
the evolving needs of entities impacted by space weather phenomena.
