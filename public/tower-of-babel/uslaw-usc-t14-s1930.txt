14 U.S.C. § 1930 — Cadets: charges and fees for attendance; limitation
Citation: us:usc:14:1930
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section1930
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 19 (Coast Guard Academy); Subchapter II (Cadets). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Prohibition.—
Except as provided in subsection (b), no charge or fee for tuition, room, or board for attendance at the Academy may be imposed unless the charge or fee is specifically authorized by a law enacted after October 5, 1994.
(b) Exception.—
The prohibition specified in subsection (a) does not apply with respect to any item or service provided to cadets for which a charge or fee is imposed as of October 5, 1994. The Secretary shall notify Congress of any change made by the Academy in the amount of a charge or fee authorized under this subsection.
