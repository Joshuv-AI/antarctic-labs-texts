42 C.F.R. § 136a.54 — a.54 Life of the mother would be endangered.
Citation: us:cfr:42:136a.54
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-136a.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 136a (PART 136a—INDIAN HEALTH). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Federal funds are available for an abortion when a physician has found and so certified in writing to the appropriate tribal or other contracting organization, or service unit or area director, that “on the basis of my professional judgement the life of the mother would be endangered if the fetus were carried to term.” The certification must contain the name and address of the patient.
