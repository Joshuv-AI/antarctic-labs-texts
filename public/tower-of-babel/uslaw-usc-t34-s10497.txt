34 U.S.C. § 10497 — Reports
Citation: us:usc:34:10497
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section10497
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 101 (Justice System Improvement); Subchapter XXII (Support For Law Enforcement Officers And Families). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A State or local law enforcement agency or organization that receives a grant under this subchapter shall submit to the Attorney General an annual report that includes—
(1)
program descriptions;
(2)
the number of staff employed to administer programs;
(3)
the number of individuals who participated in programs; and
(4)
an evaluation of the effectiveness of grant programs.
