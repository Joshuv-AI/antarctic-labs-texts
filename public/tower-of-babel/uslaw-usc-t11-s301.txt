11 U.S.C. § 301 — Voluntary cases
Citation: us:usc:11:301
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section301
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 3 (Case Administration); Subchapter I (Commencement Of A Case). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
A voluntary case under a chapter of this title is commenced by the filing with the bankruptcy court of a petition under such chapter by an entity that may be a debtor under such chapter.
(b)
The commencement of a voluntary case under a chapter of this title constitutes an order for relief under such chapter.
