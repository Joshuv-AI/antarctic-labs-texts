42 U.S.C. § 17002 — Relationship to other law
Citation: us:usc:42:17002
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section17002
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 152 (Energy Independence And Security). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except to the extent expressly provided in this Act or an amendment made by this Act, nothing in this Act or an amendment made by this Act supersedes, limits the authority provided or responsibility conferred by, or authorizes any violation of any provision of law (including a regulation), including any energy or environmental law or regulation.
