9 C.F.R. § 590.404 — Erasures or alterations made on official certificates.
Citation: us:cfr:9:590.404
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-590.404
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 590 (Inspection Of Eggs And Egg Products (Egg Products Inspection Act)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Erasures or alterations shall be initialed by the issuing inspector on the original certificate and any copy thereof. All certificates made useless through clerical error or otherwise and all certificates canceled for whatever cause shall be voided and initialed and the original and all other copies shall be forwarded as prescribed by the Administrator.
