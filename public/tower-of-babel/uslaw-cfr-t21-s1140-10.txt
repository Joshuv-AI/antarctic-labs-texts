21 C.F.R. § 1140.10 — General responsibilities of manufacturers, distributors, and retailers.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1140.10
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1140.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1140 (Cigarettes, Smokeless Tobacco, And Covered Tobacco Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each manufacturer, distributor, importer, and retailer is responsible for ensuring that the cigarettes, smokeless tobacco, or covered tobacco products it manufactures, labels, advertises, packages, distributes, imports, sells, or otherwise holds for sale comply with all applicable requirements under this part.
