38 U.S.C. § 8527 — Notice of provisions of subchapter
Citation: us:usc:38:8527
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section8527
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part VI (Acquisition And Disposition Of Property); Chapter 85 (Disposition Of Deceased Veterans’ Personal Property); Subchapter II (Death While Patient Of Department Facility). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall prescribe a form of application for hospital treatment and domiciliary care which shall include notice of the provisions of this subchapter.
