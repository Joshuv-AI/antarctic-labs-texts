40 C.F.R. § 437.36 — Pretreatment standards for new sources (PSNS).

Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 437 (The Centralized Waste Treatment Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in 40 CFR 403.7 or § 437.30(b), any new source subject to this subpart must achieve the following pretreatment standards: Standards for o-cresol, p-cresol, 2,4,6-trichlorophenol are the same as the corresponding limitation specified in § 437.31.
