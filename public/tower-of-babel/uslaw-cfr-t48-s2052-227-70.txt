48 C.F.R. § 2052.227-70 — 2052.227-70 Drawings, designs, specifications, and other data.

Creator: Chapter 20—Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2052.227-70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2052 (Solicitation Provisions And Contract Clauses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As prescribed at 2027.305-70, the contracting officer shall insert the following clause in all solicitations and contracts in which drawings, designs, specifications, and other data will be developed and the NRC must retain full rights to them (except for the contractor's right to retain a copy for its own use). When any of the clauses prescribed at FAR 27.409 are included in the solicitation and contract, this clause will not be used.
