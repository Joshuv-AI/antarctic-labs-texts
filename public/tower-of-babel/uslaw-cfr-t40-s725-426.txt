40 C.F.R. § 725.426 — Applicability of the Tier I exemption.
Citation: us:cfr:40:725.426
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-725.426
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 725 (Reporting Requirements And Review Processes For Microorganisms). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Tier I exemption under § 725.424 applies only to a manufacturer or importer of a new microorganism that certifies that the microorganism will be used in all cases in compliance with §§ 725.420, 725.421, and 725.422.
