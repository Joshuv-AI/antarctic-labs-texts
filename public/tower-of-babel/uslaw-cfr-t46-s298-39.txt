46 C.F.R. § 298.39 — Default and liquidation.
Citation: us:cfr:46:298.39
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-298.39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 298 (Vessel And Shipyard Financing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon default under the terms of any Note, Financing Documents, Mortgage, Guarantee, or other security document, MARAD will take remedial actions including, but not limited to, where appropriate, seizure or arrest of collateral, foreclosure, restructuring, debarment, referral for debt collection, or liquidation to protect the interest of the United States.
