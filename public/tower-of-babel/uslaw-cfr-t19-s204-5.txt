19 C.F.R. § 204.5 — Reports.
Citation: us:cfr:19:204.5
Creator: United States International Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-204.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 204 (Investigations Of Effects Of Imports On Agricultural Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After completion of its investigation, the Commission will transmit to the President a report of the results thereof, including its findings and recommendations based thereon, and a statement of the steps taken in the investigation, together with a transcript of the evidence submitted at the hearing. A copy of such report will be transmitted to the Secretary of Agriculture.
