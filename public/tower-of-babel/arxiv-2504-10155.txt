A Buium–Coleman Bound for the Mordell–Lang conjecture
Netan Dogra, Sudip Pandit

Abstract
. For X X a hyperbolic curve of genus g g with good reduction at p ≥ 2 ​ g p\geq 2g , we give an explicit bound on the Mordell–Lang locus X ⁡ ( ℂ ) ∩ Γ X(\mathbb{C})\cap\Gamma , when Γ ⊂ J ⁡ ( ℂ ) \Gamma\subset J(\mathbb{C}) is the divisible hull of a subgroup of J ⁡ ( ℚ p nr ) J(\mathbb{Q}_{p}^{\mathrm{nr}}) of rank less than g g . Without any assumptions on the rank (but with all the other assumptions) we show that X ⁡ ( ℂ ) ∩ Γ X(\mathbb{C})\cap\Gamma is unramified at p p , and bound the size of its image in X ⁡ ( 𝔽 ¯ p ) X(\overline{\mathbb{F}}_{p}) . As a corollary, we obtain a new proof that Mordell implies Mordell–Lang for curves.

Introduction

Let
p
be a prime number. Let
R
be the ring of integers of the maximal unramified extension
\mathbb{Q}_{p}^{\mathrm{nr}}
of
\mathbb{Q}_{p}
,

, and

Let

be a subgroup such that

such that the map

is surjective (or equivalently

is contained in the divisible hull of

). In this paper we prove the following theorem.

Theorem 1
.

(1)

Let
\Gamma<J(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{p})
be a subgroup such that
\dim_{\mathbb{Q}}\Gamma\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}}\mathbb{Q}=r
. If
\Gamma\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}}\mathbb{Q}
has a basis defined over
\mathbb{Q}_{p}^{\mathrm{nr}}
, then

\#\mathrm{red}(X(\mathbb{Q}_{p}^{\mathrm{nr}})\cap\Gamma)\leq p^{3g+r}3^{g}[p(2g-2)+6g]g!.

where

\mathrm{red}:X(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{p})\to X(\overline{\mathbb{F}}_{p})

denotes the reduction map.

(2)

If
p\geq 2g
, then

X(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{p})\cap J(\mathbb{Q}_{p}^{\mathrm{nr}})_{\mathrm{div}}\subset X(\mathbb{Q}_{p}^{\mathrm{nr}}).

(3)

If
p\geq 2g
,
\Gamma
is as in part (1), and

, then

\#X(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{p})\cap\Gamma\leq p^{3g+r}3^{g}[p(2g-2)+6g]g!+2r.

As an application, we deduce the following.

Corollary 1
.

Let
K
be a number field and
X/K
a smooth projective geometrically irreducible curve of genus
g>1
with a
K
-rational point
b
. Let
\Gamma<J(\overline{K})
be a subgroup such that

such that

\iota(X(\overline{K}))\cap\Gamma\subset\iota(X(L)).

Of course this corollary is a special case of Faltings’ proof of the Mordell–Lang conjecture
[

]
.
Corollary

[

]
. Our proof has some common features with Raynaud’s proof. He also proves finiteness of the image of

in

, and that all but finitely many points of

(in fact, he proves a considerably more general statement

). One possible novelty of our approach is that we make minimal use of the Galois action on torsion points of

. We hope that this will make the results amenable to generalisation to other cases of the Zilber–Pink conjecture.

After Faltings’ proof of Mordell–Lang it was shown in several works how to bound the size of the set in terms of invariants of the curve. The state of the art is the work of Dimitrov–Gao–Habegger

]

(with no condition on the reduction at

or the field of definition of

). However the constant

seems hard to make explicit.

In the case

, part (3) of the theorem reduces to the Buium–Coleman’s explicit Manin–Mumford bound
[

]

[

]
. In fact, the exponent

. On the other hand, if we considered the intersection of

that when

) are unramified. The main idea of this paper (and the proof of part (3) of the theorem) is that one can combine these three ideas (Buium’s bound on unramified torsion, Coleman’s bound on ramified torsion, and Coleman’s effective Chabauty bound) to get an explicit Mordell–Lang bound.

Definition 1
