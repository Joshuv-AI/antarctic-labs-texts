50 U.S.C. § 3005 — Department of Defense
Citation: us:usc:50:3005
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 44 (National Security). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except to the extent inconsistent with the provisions of this chapter or other provisions of law, the provisions of title 5 shall be applicable to the Department of Defense.
