49 U.S.C. § 6310 — Limitations on statutory construction
Citation: us:usc:49:6310
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section6310
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Chapter 63 (Bureau Of Transportation Statistics). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter—
(1)
authorizes the Bureau to require any other Federal agency to collect data; or
(2)
alters or diminishes the authority of any other officer of the Department to collect and disseminate data independently.
