43 C.F.R. § 404.51 — Are proposed projects under the Rural Water Supply Program reviewed by the Administration?
Citation: us:cfr:43:404.51
Creator: Bureau Of Reclamation, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-404.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 404 (Reclamation Rural Water Supply Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. The Administration will review all projects proposed for funding under the Reclamation's Rural Water Supply Program. This includes review under Executive Order 12322 to determine whether the project is consistent with the policies and programs of the President. This review will occur before the feasibility report is finalized.
