25 C.F.R. § 1000.1160 — Is technical assistance available to a Tribe/Consortium to overcome the objections stated in the Secretary's rejection of a final offer?
Citation: us:cfr:25:1000.1160
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary, Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-1000.1160
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 1000 (Annual Funding Agreements Under The Tribal Self-Government Act Amendments To The Indian Self-Determination And Education Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, the Secretary must provide technical assistance to overcome the objection stated in the notification of the rejection of the final offer.
