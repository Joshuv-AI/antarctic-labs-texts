42 U.S.C. § 5911 — Acquisition of essential materials
Citation: us:usc:42:5911
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section5911
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 74 (Nonnuclear Energy Research And Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The President may, by rule or order, require the allocation of, or the performance under contracts or orders (other than contracts of employment) relating to, supplies of materials and equipment if he finds that—
(1)
such supplies are scarce, critical, and essential to carry out the purposes of this chapter; and
(2)
such supplies cannot reasonably be obtained without exercising the authority granted by this section.
(b)
A rule or order under subsection (a) shall be considered to be a major rule subject to chapter 8 of title 5.
