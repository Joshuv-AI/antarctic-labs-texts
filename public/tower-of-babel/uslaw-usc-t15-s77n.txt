15 U.S.C. § 77n — Contrary stipulations void
Citation: us:usc:15:77n
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section77n
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 2A (Securities And Trust Indentures); Subchapter I (Domestic Securities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any condition, stipulation, or provision binding any person acquiring any security to waive compliance with any provision of this subchapter or of the rules and regulations of the Commission shall be void.
