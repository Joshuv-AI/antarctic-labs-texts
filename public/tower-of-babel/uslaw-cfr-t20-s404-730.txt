20 C.F.R. § 404.730 — When evidence of a parent or child relationship is needed.
Citation: us:cfr:20:404.730
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-404.730
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 404 (Federal Old-Age, Survivors And Disability Insurance (1950- )). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you apply for parent's or child's benefits, we will ask for evidence showing your relationship to the insured person. What evidence we will ask for depends on whether you are the insured person's natural parent or child; or whether you are the stepparent, stepchild, grandchild, stepgrandchild, adopting parent or adopted child.
