29 C.F.R. § 788.9 — “Preparing * * * logs.”
Citation: us:cfr:29:788.9
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-788.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 788 (Forestry Or Logging Operations In Which Not More Than Eight Employees Are Employed). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Preparing logs includes, where appropriate, removing the limbs and top, cutting them into lengths, removing the bark, and splitting or facing them when done at the felling site, but does not include such operations when done at a mill. Employees engaged in sawmill, tie mill, and other operations in connection with the processing of logs, such as the production of lumber, are not exempt.
