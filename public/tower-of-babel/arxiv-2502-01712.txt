Summing large Pomeron loops in the saturation region: dipole-nucleus collision beyond nonlinear equations.
Eugene Levin

Abstract
In this paper we found the dipole-nucleus scattering amplitude at high energies by summing large Pomeron loops. It turns out that the energy dependence of this amplitude is the same as for dipole-dipole scattering. It means that the Balitsky-Kovchegov (BK) equation, which has been derived to describe this scattering, can be trusted only in the limited range of energies: z ≤ 2 ​ κ ​ c ​ A 1 / 6 z\,\leq\,2\sqrt{\,\kappa\,c}A^{1/6}

IV
Conclusions

In this paper we summed the large BFKL Pomeron loops in the framework of the BFKL Pomeron calculus for the scattering of dipole with nucleus target.The main result is that this scattering amplitude has the same energy dependence as the dipole-dipole amplitude. It means that the non-linear BK equation cannot describe the contribution of the large Pomeron loops and can only be used in the limited range of energy for the dipole-nucleus scattering:

z^{\prime}\,\leq\,2\sqrt{\,\kappa\,c}A^{1/6}
. From Eq. (
34
) for
z^{\prime}
one can see that our statement about energy actually related to the kinematic region where
\bar{\alpha}_{S}\frac{\chi\left(\bar{\gamma}\right)}{\bar{\gamma}}\,Y\,\,\gg\,\,\xi_{r,r^{\prime}}
. For scattering with nuclei
\xi_{r,r^{\prime}}\,\approx\,\ln\left(\frac{r^{2}\,r^{\prime 2}}{R^{4}_{A}}\right)\approx\ln\left(\frac{R_{N}^{2}}{Q^{2}\,R^{4}_{A}}\right)
, where
R_{N}
is the nucleon radius,
Q^{2}
is the photon virtuality in deep inelastc scattering and
R_{A}
is the radius of a nucleus. Therefore, we can reach the asymptotic behaviour at sufficiently large values of
Y
:
\bar{\alpha}_{S}\frac{\chi\left(\bar{\gamma}\right)}{\bar{\gamma}}\,Y\,\,>\,2\sqrt{\,\kappa\,c}A^{1/6}+\ln\left(Q^{2}\,R^{2}_{N}\right)+\frac{4}{3}\ln A\approx\,18+\ln\left(Q^{2}\,R^{2}_{N}\right)
for
A\sim 200
and
c\sim 1
. Hence, practically we expect that we can trust BK equation for accessible energies.

We wish to emphasize that the estimate for
z^{\prime}
were made using several assumptions which we cannot prove. First, it is assumed that the smooth function of
z
for the interaction with
j
-nucleons is equal
C^{J}\left(z\right)
. Second, we used cylindrical model to evaluate the cross sections.
Third, we actually assumed that
C^{j}\left(z\right)
tends to constant at
z\to\infty
. Bearing this in mind we are aware that our estimates are not very reliable. The result, that does not depends on additional assumptions, is that dipole-nucleus scattering amplitude at ultrahigh energy behaves as the amplitude of the dipole-dipole scattering.

The result has been expected since it is shown in Refs.
[
22
,
23
]

that BK equation violates the s-channel unitarity. The kind of surprise is the resulting scattering amplitude coincides with the dipole-dipole scattering at high energy.

We hope that this paper will contribute to the further study of the Pomeron calculus in QCD.
