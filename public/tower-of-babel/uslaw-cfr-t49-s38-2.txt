49 C.F.R. § 38.2 — Equivalent facilitation.
Citation: us:cfr:49:38.2
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-38.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 38 (Americans With Disabilities Act (Ada) Accessibility Specifications For Transportation Vehicles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Departures from particular technical and scoping requirements of these guidelines by use of other designs and technologies are permitted where the alternative designs and technologies used will provide substantially equivalent or greater access to and usability of the vehicle. Departures are to be considered on a case-by-case basis under procedures set forth in § 37.7 of this title.
