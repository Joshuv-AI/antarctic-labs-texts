13 C.F.R. § 101.407 — May the Administrator waive these regulations?
Citation: us:cfr:13:101.407
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-101.407
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 101 (Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator may waive any provision of §§ 101.400 through and including 101.406 in an emergency.
