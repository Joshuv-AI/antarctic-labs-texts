41 C.F.R. § 301-10.301 — -10.301 Reimbursement for advantageous POV use.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-10.301
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-10.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-10 (10—Transportation Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees will be reimbursed an applicable mileage rate based on the type of POV actually used, including privately owned airplane, automobile, or motorcycle. These rates will be published in an FTR bulletin and displayed on the General Services Administration website at https://www.gsa.gov/mileage.
