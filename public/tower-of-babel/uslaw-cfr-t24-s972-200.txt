24 C.F.R. § 972.200 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:24:972.200
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-972.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 972 (Conversion Of Public Housing To Tenant-Based Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart implements section 22 of the United States Housing Act of 1937 (42 U.S.C. 1437t). The purposes of this subpart are to:
(a) Require PHAs to perform an assessment which considers developments for which conversion of public housing may be appropriate; and
(b) Provide a basis for a PHA to take action for conversion on a voluntary basis.
