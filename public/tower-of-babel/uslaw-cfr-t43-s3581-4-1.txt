43 C.F.R. § 3581.4-1 — -1 Lease terms.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3581.4-1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3581.4-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3580 (Special Leasing Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The lease shall be issued for a period of 20 years with a preference right in the lessee to renew for a 10-year term at the end of the initial term and at the end of each 10-year period thereafter.
