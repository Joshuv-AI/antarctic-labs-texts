3 U.S.C. § 303 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:3:303
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title3-section303
Context: United States Code, Title 3 (The President); Chapter 4 (Delegation Of Functions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this chapter, the term “function” embraces any duty, power, responsibility, authority, or discretion vested in the President or other officer concerned, and the terms “perform” and “performance” may be construed to mean “exercise”.
