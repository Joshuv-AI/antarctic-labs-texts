22 C.F.R. § 906.9 — Failure of party to appear.
Citation: us:cfr:22:906.9
Creator: Foreign Service Grievance Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-906.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 906 (Hearings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The hearing may proceed in the absence of any party who, after due notice and without good cause, fails to be present or obtain an adjournment.
