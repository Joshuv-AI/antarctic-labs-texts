42 U.S.C. § 1962d–8 — Reports on Delmarva Peninsula hydrologic study
Citation: us:usc:42:1962d–8
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1962d–8
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 19B (Water Resources Planning); Subchapter IV (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

During the course of the study and investigation authorized by sections 1962d–7 to 1962d–11 of this title, the Secretary may submit to the President for transmission to the Congress such interim reports as the Secretary may consider desirable. The Secretary shall submit a final report to the President for transmission to the Congress not more than six years after October 4, 1966.
