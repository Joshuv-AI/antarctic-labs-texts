7 C.F.R. § 1410.53 — Executed CRP contract not in conformity with this part.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1410.53
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1410.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1410 (Conservation Reserve Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If, after a CRP contract is approved by CCC, it is discovered that such CRP contract is found to contain material errors of fact or is not in conformity with this part, CCC may terminate or offer to modify the CRP contract in whole or in part.
