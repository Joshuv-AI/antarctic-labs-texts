40 U.S.C. § 305 — Electronic Government and information technologies
Citation: us:usc:40:305
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section305
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Chapter 3 (Organization Of General Services Administration); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator of General Services shall consult with the Administrator of the Office of Electronic Government on programs undertaken by the General Services Administration to promote electronic Government and the efficient use of information technologies by Federal agencies.
