25 C.F.R. § 103.22 — How does BIA determine the amount of interest subsidy?
Citation: us:cfr:25:103.22
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-103.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 103 (Loan Guaranty, Insurance, And Interest Subsidy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Interest subsidy payments should equal the difference between the lender's rate of interest and the rate determined in accordance with 25 U.S.C. 1464. BIA will fix the amount of interest subsidy as of the date it approves the interest subsidy request.
