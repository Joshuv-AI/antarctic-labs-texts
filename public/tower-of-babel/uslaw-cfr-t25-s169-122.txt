25 C.F.R. § 169.122 — Who does the grantee pay if there is a life estate on the tract?
Citation: us:cfr:25:169.122
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-169.122
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 169 (Rights-Of-Way Over Indian Land). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The grantee must pay compensation directly to the life tenant under the terms of the right-of-way unless the whereabouts of the life tenant are unknown, in which case we may collect compensation on behalf of the life tenant.
