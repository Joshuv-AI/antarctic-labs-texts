40 C.F.R. § 17.4 — Applicability to EPA proceedings.
Citation: us:cfr:40:17.4
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-17.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 17 (Implementation Of The Equal Access To Justice Act In Epa Administrative Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Act applies to an adversary adjudication pending before EPA at any time between October 1, 1981 and September 30, 1984. This includes proceedings begun before October 1, 1981 if final EPA action has not been taken before that date, and proceedings pending on September 30, 1984.
