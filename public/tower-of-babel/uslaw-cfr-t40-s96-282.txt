40 C.F.R. § 96.282 — CAIR designated representative.
Citation: us:cfr:40:96.282
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-96.282
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 96 (Nox Budget Trading Program And Cair Nox And So2 Trading Programs For State Implementation Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any CAIR SO2 opt-in unit, and any unit for which a CAIR opt-in permit application is submitted and not withdrawn and a CAIR opt-in permit is not yet issued or denied under this subpart, located at the same source as one or more CAIR SO2 units shall have the same CAIR designated representative and alternate CAIR designated representative as such CAIR SO2 units.
