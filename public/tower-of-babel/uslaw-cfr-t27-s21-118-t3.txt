27 C.F.R. § 21.118-T3 — -T3 Natural gasoline.
Citation: us:cfr:27:21.118-T3
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-21.118-T3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 21 (Formulas For Denatured Alcohol And Rum). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Natural gasoline is a mixture of various alkanes including butane, pentane, and hexane hydrocarbons extracted from natural gas. It has a distillation range wherein no more than 10 percent by volume of the sample may distill below 97 °F; at least 50 percent by volume shall distill at or below 156 °F; and at least 90 percent by volume shall distill at or below 209 °F.
