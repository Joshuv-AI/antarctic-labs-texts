12 C.F.R. § 1807.502 — CMF Award limits.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1807.502
Creator: Community Development Financial Institutions Fund, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1807.502
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1807 (Capital Magnet Fund). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An eligible Applicant and its Subsidiaries and Affiliates may not be awarded more than 15 percent of the aggregate funds available for the CMF Awards during any year.
