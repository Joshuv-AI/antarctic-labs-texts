33 C.F.R. § 1.01-90 — -90 Commissioned, warrant, and petty officers.
Citation: us:cfr:33:1.01-90
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-1.01-90
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 1 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any commissioned, warrant, or petty officer of the United States Coast Guard may be authorized to carry out the functions delegated to superior officials under §§ 1.01-1, 1.01-20, 1.01-30, 1.01-70, and 1.07-80, or redelegated under § 1.01-85, within the jurisdiction of the cognizant official. They will do so under the supervision and general direction of that official.
