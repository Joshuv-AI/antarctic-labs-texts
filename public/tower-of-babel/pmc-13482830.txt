A novel cascade neural network with heuristic computational analysis for thermal dynamics of rectangular fin model with surface stretching/shrinking
Authors: Zirwa Khan, Iftikhar Ahmad, Hira Ilyas
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
The present study designs a new computational approach to study the thermal performance of a shrinking or stretching longitudinal rectangular fin under both convective and radiative conditions. A dimensionless mathematical model is developed to describe the thermal behavior of the fin, including parameters such as Peclet number, convective and radiative coefficients, temperature ratios, and stretching/shrinking parameters. The hybrid neurocomputing method used to solve the nonlinear system of differential equations consists of Cascaded Neural Networks (CNN) along with Genetic Algorithm hybridized with Sequential Quadratic Programming (GA-SQP). CNN modeling offers an approximate solution through a piecewise continuous representation, whereas GA-SQP improves convergence by optimal network weights. A detailed parametric study is done in six scenarios to assess the fin’s temperature distribution and tip characteristics. The accuracy and stability of the solver are validated using statistical metrics such as RMSE, MAE, E-VAF, and E-NSE. The outcome shows that the Peclet number increases enhance the fin tip temperature up-to 15% as the temperature ratio increases, the fin tip temperature increases by 7–10%. The increase in the the radiation coefficient reduces the temperature by 8%. Various statistical measures confirm its reliability and effectiveness, making it a strong tool for handling complex ODEs and PDEs. Histogram and boxplot analysis show accuracy ranges up to 10− 9, indicating that almost 90% of total iterations achieved the required accuracy tolerance range. Fitness evaluation attains high rate of convergence shows that CNN-GA-SQP scheme is a fast-converging and accurate computational intelligence approach for solving stiff nonlinear fin heat transfer models.

Introduction

The process by which thermal energy moves from one place to another due to a temperature difference is known as heat transfer. Heat transformation occurs when two bodies are in contact, and the process continues until they accomplish thermal equilibrium. The energy of the system is changed due to heat transfer, and it depends on the system temperature1. These days, higher energy density electronic devices demand effective and efficient cooling systems to enhance their performance2. The power dissipation in industrial machines generates excessive heat that lowers their efficiency, though it affects their running and performance. Almost every production system is planned to work inside a temperature range with unique limits, exceeding these limits through too much-heated consequence failure of the system. To solve this difficulty of the unwanted heat era, electronic components require a bypass via a heat-resistant complicated system. The passive method is mostly preferred for power electronics and electronic devices because of its low budget, which gives noiseless solutions, improving thermal performance, and reducing thermally-induced failures3, 4. The passive method utilizes an extended surface (fin), a treated surface (surface’s texture is changed), and a rough surface. In extended surfaces, expanding the area of the surface for heat transformation effectively is done, and fins are those surfaces that exploit both conduction and convection happens in this case at the same time5.

The performance of porous fin heat sink having FGM (functionally graded material) is analyzed using supervised learning techniques such as Levenberg-Marquardt propagation6. An assessment of heat transfer in an inclined longitudinal porous fin of various profiles is done by using a cascade neural network7. A comparative study of movable exponential and trapezoidal fins was done by Din with various non-linearities using the shooting technique for introducing a unique idea of increasing the fin’s efficiency, effectiveness, and heat transfer8. The behavior of a longitudinal fin having porous material with an exponential profile witted by a hybrid Nano-composite fluid is investigated using an integrated analytical method such as residual power series method with Pade approximation9.

The heat transfer in a stretching/shrinking radial fin is studied; results show that while convection, radiation, and stretching have a substantial impact on temperature distribution and fin tip behaviour, thermal performance and efficiency greatly improve under shrinking with radiation10. An exponential-profiled entirely wetted stretching/shrinking longitudinal fin’s thermal performance is examined to demonstrate that using a shrinking mechanism successfully reduces the negative effects of motion and internal heat production, providing helpful information for realistic fin design11. Temperature distribution, tip temperature, and efficiency in a fully wet trapezoidal fin with internal heat generation are examined, indicating that although stretching the fin raises temperature and decreases efficiency, decreasing the fin lowers temperature and boosts efficiency. Thus, thermal performance is enhanced by shrinking trapezoidal fins12. The Levenberg-Marquard propagation technique is used to study the efficiency of a moving triangular shape porous material fin and check the accuracy and efficiency of the suggested technique13. The thermal hydraulic performance of heat exchanger double pipe having twisted angled fins is investigated conclusion drown that twisted angle geometry significantly improves performance over smooth pipes14. The convective heat transfer enhancement in a heated cubical cavity using porous fins is examined, results shows that there is more heat transformation in fin cavity then the other finless cavity15. The Taguchi method is applied to examine fin arrays on a cylinder under natural convection, results highlight that the fin having functionally graded aluminum–copper material have more heat transformation16. An investigation of heat transfer in wattad spine fin of convex shape is carried out and an Operational Matrix with Charlier Polynomial Collocation Method (OMCCM) technique is applied ans its thermal performance is examined17. Heat transfer in inclined pyramidal spine fins having porous medium is studied and a semi-analytical Shifted Vieta–Pell Polynomial Collocation Method (SVP-PCM) is applied on it and results compared with a numerical technique18.

In the recent period, electronic components have become more compact, and the 21st-century demands better heat transfer for best performance, so, to facilitate better heat transfer from mechanical and electronic devices, a huge study all over the world has been conducted at a high rate, for fins are introduced in the system19. Fins are used in heat exchanging, air conditioners, electronics turbines, electronics apparatuses, super-fast vehicles, aero-engines, and cooling gadgets20. Different types of fins and profiles have been studied and inserted to make the system more effective and efficient. Some types and profiles of fins, such as rectangular, triangular, convex, exponential, circular, pin fin, solid, perforated, and porous have been studied to make the heat transfer more perfect and more effective in a system.

Filtering motionless packing, isolation, grease, synergistic bed reactors, and increasing natural gas and oil production are some important applications of fins, which is why they are an effective and efficient way of heat transfer. Moreover, the various fins have different industrial applications such as heat exchangers, cooling devices, and solar radiation collectors21, 22. The specific fin that is a rectangular shaped fin is widely used to enhance the heart transformation rate. With the stretching and shrinking of the rectangular fin the geometry changes its length, thickness and surface area changes that play a major key role in enhancing its performance and efficiency these types of fins are widely used in heat sinks for power electronics, CPUs and AC/DC inverters, radiators, condensers HAVAC systems, cooling of turbines, generators, and LNG plants.

Thoroughly studying literature, one can without much of a stretch say that several analytical methods, optimization techniques and various numerical approach have been exploited so far on thermal profile investigation of fins with various materials, physical phenomenon and geometrical characteristics23, 24, 25, 26. More specifically on a rectangular shaped fin with surface stretching and shrinking is also under consideration and various techniques are used on it to improve the design efficiency27, 28 However, these analytical and numerical approaches generally faces limitations as compared to the machine learning based predicted models. The computational techniques based on neural network models are new in this field. Neural network models are extensively utilized in numerous domains, such as bio-medicine29, material science30, finance31, optics study32, fluid dynamics and geology33, 34, for the purpose of solving differential equations. These techniques have gained widespread adoption due to their impressive and robust performance model, which is independent of diverse datasets, making them suitable for tackling a variety of real-world problems. Various neural networking methods has been used by researchers in different aspects on a huge criteria to accomplish an estimated solution of several types of problems in the range of applied sciences such as malaria-endemic model35, reactive transport model (RTM)36, FRAPPE model, corneal shape model37, 38, 39, Bateman-Burger equation40, Tomas-Fermi equation models41, Painlevé equations42, Maxwell fluid flow43, computer virus models44, 45, 46, dengue and SVIS-epidemic models47, 48, neno-fluid flow models49, 50, COVID-19 model51, Lane–Emden equation52, Degasperis–Procesi and Benjamin–Bona–Mahony equation52, fractional differential system53, Tuberculosis disease model54, SEIRC model55, MHD blood flow models56, fractional cyber warfare modeling57, Parkinson’s disease model58.
