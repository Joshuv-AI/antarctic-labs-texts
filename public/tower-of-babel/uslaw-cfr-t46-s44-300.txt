46 C.F.R. § 44.300 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:46:44.300
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-44.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 44 (Special Service Limited Domestic Voyages). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to each self-propelled hopper dredge—
(a) For which a working freeboard assignment is desired after January 1, 1990; and
(b) That operates with a working freeboard assigned under this subpart.
