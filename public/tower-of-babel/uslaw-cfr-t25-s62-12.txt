25 C.F.R. § 62.12 — Special instructions.

Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 62 (Enrollment Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To facilitate the work of the Director, the Assistant Secretary may issue special instructions not inconsistent with the regulations in this part 62.
