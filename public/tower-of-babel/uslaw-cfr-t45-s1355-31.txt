45 C.F.R. § 1355.31 — Elements of the child and family services review system.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1355 (General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Scope. Sections 1355.32 through 1355.37 of this part apply to reviews of child and family services programs under subparts 1 and 2 of title IV-B of the Act, and reviews of foster care and adoption assistance programs under title IV-E of the Act.
