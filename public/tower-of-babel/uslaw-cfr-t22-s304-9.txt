22 C.F.R. § 304.9 — Referral to the Department of Justice.
Citation: us:cfr:22:304.9
Creator: Peace Corps
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-304.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 304 (Claims Against Government Under Federal Tort Claims Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When Department of Justice approval or consultation is required under § 304.8, the referral or request shall be transmitted to the Department of Justice by the General Counsel.
