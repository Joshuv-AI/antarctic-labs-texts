40 C.F.R. § 152.117 — Notification to applicant.
Citation: us:cfr:40:152.117
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-152.117
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 152 (Pesticide Registration And Classification Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Agency will notify the applicant of the approval of his application by a Notice of Registration for new registration, or by a letter in the case of an amended registration.
