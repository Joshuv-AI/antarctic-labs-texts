33 C.F.R. § 110.79b — b Millers Bay, Lake Winnebago, Oshkosh, WI.
Citation: us:cfr:33:110.79b
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-110.79b
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 110 (Anchorage Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The area adjacent to Menominee Park in Millers Bay within the following boundaries: beginning at latitude 44°01′47″ N., longitude 88°31′05″ W.; thence to latitude 44°01′46″ N., longitude 88°31′00″ W.; thence to latitude 44°01′34″ N., longitude 88°31′04″ W.; thence to latitude 44°01′36″ N., longitude 88°31′08″ W.; thence to point of beginning.
