46 U.S.C. § 4705 — Liability of barge removal contractors
Citation: us:usc:46:4705
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section4705
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part B (Inspection And Regulation Of Vessels); Chapter 47 (Abandonment Of Vessels); Subchapter I (Barges). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
A barge removal contractor and its subcontractor are not liable for damages that result from actions taken or omitted to be taken in the course of removing a barge under this subchapter.
(b)
Subsection (a) does not apply—
(1)
with respect to personal injury or wrongful death; or
(2)
if the contractor or subcontractor is grossly negligent or engages in willful misconduct.
