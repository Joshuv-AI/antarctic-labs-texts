10 U.S.C. § 8902 — Appointment and enlistment in reserve components
Citation: us:usc:10:8902
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8902
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 887 (Naval Militia). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the discretion of the Secretary of the Navy, any member of the Naval Militia may be appointed or enlisted in the Navy Reserve or the Marine Corps Reserve in the grade for which he is qualified.
