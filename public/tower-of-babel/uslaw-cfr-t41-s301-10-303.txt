41 C.F.R. § 301-10.303 — -10.303 Reimbursement with multiple POV travelers.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-10.303
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-10.303
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-10 (10—Transportation Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If another employee travels with the employee on the same trip in the same privately owned vehicle, mileage is payable to only one traveler. No deduction will be made from the mileage allowance if other passengers contribute to defraying expenses.
