33 C.F.R. § 118.10 — Interference or obstruction prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:33:118.10
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-118.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 118 (Bridge Lighting And Other Signals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person shall obstruct or interfere with any lights or signals maintained in accordance with the regulations prescribed in this part.
