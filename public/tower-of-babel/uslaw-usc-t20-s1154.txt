20 U.S.C. § 1154 — Contract authority
Citation: us:usc:20:1154
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1154
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 28 (Higher Education Resources And Student Assistance); Subchapter VIII (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The authorization to enter into contracts or other obligations under the Act, as amended by this Act, shall be effective for fiscal year 1981 and any succeeding fiscal year only to the extent or in such amounts as are provided in advance in appropriation Acts.
