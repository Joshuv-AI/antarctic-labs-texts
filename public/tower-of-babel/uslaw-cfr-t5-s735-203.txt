5 C.F.R. § 735.203 — What are the restrictions on conduct prejudicial to the Government?
Citation: us:cfr:5:735.203
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-735.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 735 (Employee Responsibilities And Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee shall not engage in criminal, infamous, dishonest, immoral, or notoriously disgraceful conduct, or other conduct prejudicial to the Government.
