20 C.F.R. § 408.305 — Why do you need to file an application to receive benefits?
Citation: us:cfr:20:408.305
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-408.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 408 (Special Benefits For Certain World War Ii Veterans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to meeting other requirements, you must file an application to become entitled to SVB. If you believe you may be entitled to SVB, you should file an application. Filing an application will—
(a) Permit us to make a formal decision on whether you qualify for SVB;
(b) Assure that you receive SVB for any months you are entitled to receive payments; and
(c) Give you the right to appeal if you are dissatisfied with our determination.
