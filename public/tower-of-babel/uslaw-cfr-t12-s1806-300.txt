12 C.F.R. § 1806.300 — Eligible Activities.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1806.300
Creator: Community Development Financial Institutions Fund, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1806.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1806 (Bank Enterprise Award Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Recipients of BEA Program Awards must use their payments for the following Eligible Activities:
(a) CDFI Related Activities;
(b) Distressed Community Financing Activities; and
(c) Service Activities, and to comply with the Uniform Administrative Requirements as further described in the applicable NOFA and the Award Agreement.
