37 C.F.R. § 2.115 — Amendment of pleadings in a cancellation proceeding.
Citation: us:cfr:37:2.115
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-2.115
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 2 (Rules Of Practice In Trademark Cases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pleadings in a cancellation proceeding may be amended in the same manner and to the same extent as in a civil action in a United States district court.
