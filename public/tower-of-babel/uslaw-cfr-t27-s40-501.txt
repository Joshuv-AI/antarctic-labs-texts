27 C.F.R. § 40.501 — Retention of permit and supporting documents.
Citation: us:cfr:27:40.501
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-40.501
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 40 (Manufacture Of Tobacco Products, Cigarette Papers And Tubes, And Processed Tobacco). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The manufacturer must retain the permit, together with the copy of the application and supporting documents returned with the permit, at the same place where the records required by this subpart are kept. The permit and supporting documents must be made available for inspection by any appropriate TTB officer upon request.
