21 U.S.C. § 1043 — Rules and regulations; administration and enforcement
Citation: us:usc:21:1043
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section1043
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 15 (Egg Products Inspection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall promulgate such rules and regulations as he deems necessary to carry out the purposes or provisions of this chapter, and shall be responsible for the administration and enforcement of this chapter except as otherwise provided in section 1034(d) of this title.
