30 C.F.R. § 47.31 — Requirement for a HazCom program.
Citation: us:cfr:30:47.31
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-47.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 47 (HAZARD COMMUNICATION (HazCom)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each operator must—
(a) Develop and implement a written HazCom program,
(b) Maintain it for as long as a hazardous chemical is known to be at the mine, and
(c) Share relevant HazCom information with other on-site operators whose miners can be affected.
