14 C.F.R. § 258.6 — Effective and compliance dates.
Citation: us:cfr:14:258.6
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-258.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 258 (Disclosure Of Change-Of-Gauge Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) This Part is effective as of August 25, 1999.
(b) Compliance with the following sections is mandatory as of August 25, 1999: §§ 258.1, 258.2, 258.3, 258.4, 258.5(a), 258.5(b), and 258.6.
(c) Compliance with § 258.5(c) is mandatory as of March 15, 2000.
