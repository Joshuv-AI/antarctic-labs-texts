32 C.F.R. § 203.6 — Cost principles.
Citation: us:cfr:32:203.6
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-203.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 203 (Technical Assistance For Public Participation (Tapp) In Defense Environmental Restoration Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Non-profit contractors must comply with the cost principles in OMB Circular A-122. Copies of the circular may be obtained from EOP Publications, 725 17th NW, NEOB, Washington, DC 20503.
(b) For-profit contractors and subcontractors must comply with the cost principles in the FAR (48 CFR part 31).
