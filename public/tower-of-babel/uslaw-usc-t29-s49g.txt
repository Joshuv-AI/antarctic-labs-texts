29 U.S.C. § 49g — State plans
Citation: us:usc:29:49g
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section49g
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 4B (Federal Employment Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any State desiring to receive assistance under section 49e of this title shall prepare and submit to, and have approved by, the Secretary and the Secretary of Education, a State plan in accordance with section 3112 or 3113 of this title.
