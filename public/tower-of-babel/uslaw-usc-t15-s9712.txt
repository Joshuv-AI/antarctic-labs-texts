15 U.S.C. § 9712 — Availability to the public of flood-related data
Citation: us:usc:15:9712
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section9712
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 121 (Flood Level Observation, Operations, And Decision Support). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The Administrator shall make flood-related data available to the public on the website of the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration.
(b) Cost
The Administrator may make the data under subsection (a) freely accessible or available at a cost that does not exceed the cost of preparing the data.
