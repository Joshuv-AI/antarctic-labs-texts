48 C.F.R. § 53.107 — 53.107 Obtaining forms.
Citation: us:cfr:48:53.107
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-53.107
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 53 (Forms). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Executive agencies shall obtain standard and optional forms from the General Services Administration (GSA) Forms Library at https://www.gsa.gov/forms. Agency forms are available from the prescribing agency.
