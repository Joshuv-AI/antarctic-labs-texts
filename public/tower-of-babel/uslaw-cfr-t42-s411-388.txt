42 C.F.R. § 411.388 — When advisory opinions are not admissible evidence.
Citation: us:cfr:42:411.388
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-411.388
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 411 (Exclusions From Medicare And Limitations On Medicare Payment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The failure of a party to seek or to receive an advisory opinion may not be introduced into evidence to prove that the party either intended or did not intend to violate the provisions of sections 1128, 1128A or 1128B of the Act.
