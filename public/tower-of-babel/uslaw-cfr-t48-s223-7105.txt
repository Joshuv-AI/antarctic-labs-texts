48 C.F.R. § 223.7105 — 223.7105 Reimbursement.
Citation: us:cfr:48:223.7105
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-223.7105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 223 (Environment, Sustainable Acquisition, And Material Safety). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary of Defense may assess a charge for any storage or disposal provided under this subpart. If a charge is to be assessed, then such assessment shall be identified in the contract with payment to the Government on a reimbursable cost basis.
