7 C.F.R. § 28.960 — Confidential information.
Citation: us:cfr:7:28.960
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-28.960
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 28 (Cotton Classing, Testing, And Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No information concerning individual tests under the regulations in this subpart shall be published or communicated in such a way as to disclose to others the identity of the owners of cotton represented by samples submitted for testing, except with the written permission of such owners.
