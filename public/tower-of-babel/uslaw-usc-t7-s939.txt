7 U.S.C. § 939 — Loan terms and conditions
Citation: us:usc:7:939
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section939
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 31 (Rural Electrification And Telephone Service); Subchapter III (Rural Electric And Telephone Direct Loan Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Loans made from or insured through the fund shall be for the same purposes and on the same terms and conditions as are provided for loans in subchapters I and II of this chapter except as otherwise provided in sections 933 to 938 inclusive.
