48 C.F.R. § 919.503 — 919.503 Setting aside a class of acquisitions for small business.
Citation: us:cfr:48:919.503
Creator: Chapter 9—Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-919.503
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 919 (Small Business Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

By agreement with Small Business Administration (SBA), the DOE has established a class set-aside for construction acquisitions not exceeding $3 million, including new construction and repair and alteration of structures. Lists of other class set-asides shall be maintained by all DOE contracting offices. These lists shall be updated at least annually.
