16 U.S.C. § 5206 — Rule of construction
Citation: us:usc:16:5206
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section5206
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 72 (Recreational Hunting Safety). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to impair a right guaranteed to a person under the first article of amendment to the Constitution or limit any legal remedy for forceful interference with a person’s lawful participation in speech or peaceful assembly.
