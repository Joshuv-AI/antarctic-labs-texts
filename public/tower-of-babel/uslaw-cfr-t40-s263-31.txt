40 C.F.R. § 263.31 — Discharge clean up.
Citation: us:cfr:40:263.31
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-263.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 263 (Standards Applicable To Transporters Of Hazardous Waste). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A transporter must clean up any hazardous waste discharge that occurs during transportation or take such action as may be required or approved by Federal, State, or local officials so that the hazardous waste discharge no longer presents a hazard to human health or the environment.
