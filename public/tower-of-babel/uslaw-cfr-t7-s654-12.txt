7 C.F.R. § 654.12 — Financing operations and maintenance.
Citation: us:cfr:7:654.12
Creator: Natural Resources Conservation Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-654.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 654 (Operation And Maintenance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Sources of funds needed to operate and maintain project measures for the duration of the O&M agreement shall be identified in the watershed or RC&D measure plan.
