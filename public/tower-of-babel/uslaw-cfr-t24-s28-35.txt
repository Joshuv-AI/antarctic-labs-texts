24 C.F.R. § 28.35 — Statute of limitations.
Citation: us:cfr:24:28.35
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-28.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 28 (Implementation Of The Program Fraud Civil Remedies Act Of 1986). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The statute of limitations for commencing hearings under this part shall be tolled:
(a) If the hearing is commenced in accordance with 31 U.S.C. 3803(d)(2)(B) within 6 years after the date on which the claim or statement is made; or
(b) If the parties agree to such tolling.
