38 U.S.C. § 905 — Use of facilities and services of other law enforcement agencies
Citation: us:usc:38:905
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section905
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part I (General Provisions); Chapter 9 (Security And Law Enforcement On Property Under The Jurisdiction Of The Department). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

With the permission of the head of the agency concerned, the Secretary may use the facilities and services of Federal, State, and local law enforcement agencies when it is economical and in the public interest to do so.
