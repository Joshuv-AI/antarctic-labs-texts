33 C.F.R. § 333.38 — Unique identification numbers.
Citation: us:cfr:33:333.38
Creator: Corps Of Engineers, Department Of The Army, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-333.38
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 333 (Processing Of Department Of The Army Permits And 33 U.S.C. 408 Permissions, National Environmental Policy Act Implementing Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For all environmental documents, the District Engineer will provide a unique identification number for tracking purposes, which the District Engineer will reference on all associated environmental review documents prepared for the proposed agency action and in any database or tracking system for such documents. The District Engineer will coordinate with the CEQ and other Federal agencies to ensure uniformity of such identification numbers across Federal agencies.
