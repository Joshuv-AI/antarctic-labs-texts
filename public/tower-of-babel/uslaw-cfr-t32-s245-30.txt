32 C.F.R. § 245.30 — ESCAT test procedures restrictions.
Citation: us:cfr:32:245.30
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-245.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 245 (Plan For The Emergency Security Control Of Air Traffic (Escat)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Aircraft will not be grounded or diverted.
(b) Test messages will not be broadcast over air/ground frequencies.
(c) Radio communications will not be interrupted.
(d) Navigation Aids will not be affected.
