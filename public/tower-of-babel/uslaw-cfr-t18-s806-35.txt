18 C.F.R. § 806.35 — Fees.
Citation: us:cfr:18:806.35
Creator: Susquehanna River Basin Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-806.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 806 (Review And Approval Of Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Project sponsors shall have an affirmative duty to pay such fees as established by the Commission to cover its costs of administering the regulatory program established by this part, including any extraordinary costs associated with specific projects.
