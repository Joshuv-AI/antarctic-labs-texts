Multiplicity results for a subcritical Hamiltonian system with concave-convex nonlinearities
Oscar Agudelo, Bernhard Ruf, Carlos Vélez

Abstract

Let us consider the elliptic system

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{aligned} -\Delta u&=\lambda|v|^{r-1}v+|v|^{p-1}v\qquad&\hbox{in}\ \ \Omega,\\

The approach to this problem is as follows: we invert the (odd and monotone increasing) nonlinearity in the second equation, and obtain

The existence and multiplicity results are obtained via variational methods for the energy functional associated to this fourth order quasilinear equation. We now give a detailed review of some of the known results for such systems, and state our result in more generality (see Theorem

\displaystyle\frac{q}{q+1}\left(1-\delta\right)|\theta|^{\frac{q+1}{q}}\leq

\displaystyle\Psi(\mu,\theta)\leq\frac{q}{q+1}\left(1+\delta\right)|\theta|^{\frac{q+1}{q}}.

Using (

\displaystyle I_{n}=

\displaystyle\int_{\{|\Delta w_{n}|\geq D\}}\left(\Psi(\mu,\Delta w_{n})-\frac{q}{q+1}\psi(\mu,\Delta w_{n})\Delta w_{n}\right)dx

(5.5)

\displaystyle+\int_{\{|\Delta w_{n}|<D\}}\left(\Psi(\mu,\Delta w_{n})-\frac{q}{q+1}\psi(\mu,\Delta w_{n})\Delta w_{n}\right)dx

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\frac{-2q\delta}{q+1}\int_{\{|\Delta w_{n}|\geq{D}\}}|\Delta w_{n}|^{\frac{q+1}{q}}dx

\displaystyle+\int_{\{|\Delta w_{n}|<{D}\}}\left(\Psi(\mu,\Delta w_{n})-\frac{q}{q+1}\psi(\mu,\Delta w_{n})\Delta w_{n}\right)dx

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\frac{-2q\delta}{q+1}\int_{\Omega}|\Delta w_{n}|^{\frac{q+1}{q}}dx-\mathcal{K}_{\mu,q,s,\delta,\Omega}

for some constant
\mathcal{K}_{\mu,q,s,\delta,\Omega}>0
. Proceeding similarly as above, and taking
\mathcal{K}_{\mu,q,s,\delta,\Omega}>0
larger if necessary,

I_{n}\leq\frac{2q\delta}{q+1}\int_{\Omega}|\Delta w_{n}|^{\frac{q+1}{q}}dx+\mathcal{K}_{\mu,q,s,\delta,\Omega}.

(5.6)

As for the integral
II_{n}
we find that

II_{n}+\lambda\frac{1-rq}{(r+1)(q+1)}\int_{\Omega}(w_{n})_{+}^{r+1}dx=\frac{pq-1}{(p+1)(q+1)}\int_{\Omega}(w_{n})_{+}^{p+1}dx.

(5.7)

Assume for the moment

), making use of (

), (

) and (

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}(w_{n})_{+}^{p+1}dx\leq

\displaystyle C+\delta\frac{q}{q+1}\|w_{n}\|_{W}+\lambda\frac{(p+1)|1-rq|}{(r+1)(qp-1)}\|w_{n}\|^{r+1}_{L^{r+1}(\Omega)}

(5.8)

\displaystyle+\frac{2\delta q(p+1)}{|qp-1|}\|w_{n}\|^{\frac{q+1}{q}}_{W},

where the constant

Next, from (
5.2
), (

) and the Sobolev embeddings in (

) and (

(5.9)

Assume for the moment
\mu>0

3.1
) and (
3.3

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}\psi(\mu,\Delta w_{n})\Delta w_{n}dx=

\displaystyle\int_{\{|\Delta w_{n}|\geq\theta_{\mu}\}}\psi(\mu,\Delta w_{n})\Delta w_{n}dx+\int_{\{|\Delta w_{n}|<\theta_{\mu}\}}\psi(\mu,\Delta w_{n})\Delta w_{n}dx

(5.10)

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\hat{C}_{q,s}\int_{\{|\Delta w_{n}|\geq\theta_{\mu}\}}|\Delta w_{n}|^{\frac{q+1}{q}}dx+

\displaystyle+\underbrace{\mu^{-\frac{1}{s}}c_{q,s}\int_{\{|\Delta w_{n}|<\theta_{\mu}\}}|\Delta w_{n}|^{\frac{s+1}{s}}dx}_{\geq 0}

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\hat{C}_{q,s}\int_{\{|\Delta w_{n}|\geq\theta_{\mu}\}}|\Delta w_{n}|^{\frac{q+1}{q}}dx+\hat{C}_{q,s}\int_{\{|\Delta w_{n}|<\theta_{\mu}\}}|\Delta w_{n}|^{\frac{q+1}{q}}dx

\displaystyle-\hat{C}_{q,s}\int_{\{|\Delta w_{n}|<\theta_{\mu}\}}|\Delta w_{n}|^{\frac{q+1}{q}}dx

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\hat{C}_{q,s}\int_{\Omega}|\Delta w_{n}|^{\frac{q+1}{q}}dx-\hat{C}_{q,s}\int_{\{|\Delta w_{n}|<\theta_{\mu}\}}|\Delta w_{n}|^{\frac{q+1}{q}}dx

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\hat{C}_{q,s}\|w_{n}\|^{\frac{q+1}{q}}_{W}-\hat{C}_{q,s}{C}_{q,s}\mu^{\frac{q+1}{q-s}}{\rm meas}(\Omega).

Remark 5.1

Lemma 5.2
.

Lemma 5.3
.

\Delta w_{n}\to\Delta w
strongly in

\displaystyle 0\leq\int_{\Omega}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\underbrace{\int_{\Omega}\psi(\mu,\Delta w_{n})\Delta(w_{n}-w)dx}_{I_{n}}-\underbrace{\int_{\Omega}\psi(\mu,\Delta w)\Delta(w_{n}-w)dx}_{II_{n}}.

strongly in

, from Vainberg’s Lemma or the continuity of Charatheodory operators,

\displaystyle\lim\limits_{n\to\infty}\int_{\Omega}f_{+}(\lambda,w_{n})(w_{n}-w)dx=

\displaystyle 0.

As for the integral term
