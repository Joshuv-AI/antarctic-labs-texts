41 C.F.R. § 105-71.105 — -71.105 Additions and exceptions.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-71.105
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-71 (71—Uniform Administrative Requirements For Grants And Cooperative Agreements With State And Local Governments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) For classes of grants and grantees subject to this part, Federal agencies may not impose additional administrative requirements except in codified regulations published in the Federal Register.
(b) Exceptions for classes of grants or grantees may be authorized only by OMB.
(c) Exceptions on a case-by-case basis and for subgrantees may be authorized by the affected Federal agencies.
