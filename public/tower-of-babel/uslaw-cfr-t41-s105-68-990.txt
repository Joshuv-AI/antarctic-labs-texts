41 C.F.R. § 105-68.990 — -68.990 Preponderance of the evidence.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-68.990
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-68.990
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-68 (68—Governmentwide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Preponderance of the evidence means proof by information that, compared with information opposing it, leads to the conclusion that the fact at issue is more probably true than not.
