7 C.F.R. § 4279.206 — Agency representation.
Citation: us:cfr:7:4279.206
Creator: Rural Business-Cooperative Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-4279.206
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 4279 (Guaranteed Loanmaking). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notwithstanding any other provision of this subpart and 7 CFR part 4287, subpart D, the Agency reserves the right to be represented by the U.S. Department of Justice in any litigation where the Agency is named as a party.
