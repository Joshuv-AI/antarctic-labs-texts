20 U.S.C. § 7944 — Applicability
Citation: us:usc:20:7944
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section7944
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 70 (Strengthening And Improvement Of Elementary And Secondary Schools); Subchapter VIII (General Provisions); Part F (Uniform Provisions); Subpart 3 (Teacher Liability Protection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This subpart shall only apply to States that receive funds under this chapter, and shall apply to such a State as a condition of receiving such funds.
