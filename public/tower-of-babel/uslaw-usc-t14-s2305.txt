14 U.S.C. § 2305 — Voluntary retirement after thirty years’ service
Citation: us:usc:14:2305
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2305
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 23 (Personnel; Enlisted). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any enlisted member who has completed thirty years’ service may, upon his own application, in the discretion of the Commandant, be retired from active service.
