42 U.S.C. § 9708 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:42:9708
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section9708
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 104 (Nuclear Safety Research, Development, And Demonstration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary to carry out this chapter such sums as may be authorized by legislation hereafter enacted.
