12 C.F.R. § 1510.8 — What are the audit requirements for the Funding Corporation?
Citation: us:cfr:12:1510.8
Creator: Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1510.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1510 (Resolution Funding Corporation Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Funding Corporation must obtain an audit of its books and records by an independent external auditor at least annually.
