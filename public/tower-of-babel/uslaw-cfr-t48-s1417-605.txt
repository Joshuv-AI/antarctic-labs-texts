48 C.F.R. § 1417.605 — 1417.605 Award, renewal and extension.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1417.605
Creator: Chapter 14—Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1417.605
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1417 (Special Contracting Methods). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The CO shall review each management and operating contract prior to any extension or exercise of a renewal option. Any extension or renewal of a management and operating contract shall first be authorized as required in 1417.602.
