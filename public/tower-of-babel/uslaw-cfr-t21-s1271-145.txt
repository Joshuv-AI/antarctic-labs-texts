21 C.F.R. § 1271.145 — Prevention of the introduction, transmission, or spread of communicable diseases.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1271.145
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1271.145
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1271 (Human Cells, Tissues, And Cellular And Tissue-Based Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must recover, process, store, label, package, and distribute HCT/Ps, and screen and test cell and tissue donors, in a way that prevents the introduction, transmission, or spread of communicable diseases.
