33 C.F.R. § 144.10-10 — -10 Other lifesaving equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:33:144.10-10
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-144.10-10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 144 (Lifesaving Appliances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any lifesaving equipment on an unmanned platform that is not required in § 144.10-1 must meet the standards contained in Subpart 144.01 of this part.
