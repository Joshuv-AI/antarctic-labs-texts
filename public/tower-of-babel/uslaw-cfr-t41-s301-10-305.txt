41 C.F.R. § 301-10.305 — -10.305 Reimbursement when using an unauthorized method of transportation.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-10.305
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-10.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-10 (10—Transportation Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Reimbursement is limited to the constructive cost of the authorized transportation method, which is the sum of travel and transportation expenses the employee would reasonably have incurred had they traveled by the method deemed most advantageous to the Government. The calculation involves assumptions and may include expenses such as: taxi and TNC fares, baggage fees, rental car costs, tolls, ferry fees, and parking charges.
