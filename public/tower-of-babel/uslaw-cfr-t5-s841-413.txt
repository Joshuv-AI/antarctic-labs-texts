5 C.F.R. § 841.413 — Determinations of amount due from each agency.
Citation: us:cfr:5:841.413
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-841.413
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 841 (Federal Employees Retirement System—General Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) For each pay period, each agency will determine the total amount of basic pay paid to employees in each category of employees.
(b) For each category of employees, the amount due from each agency for a pay period is the product of—
(1) The total amount of basic pay of employees in that category of employees in that agency; and
(2) The normal cost percentage.
