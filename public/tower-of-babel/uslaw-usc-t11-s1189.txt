11 U.S.C. § 1189 — Filing of the plan

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 11 (Reorganization); Subchapter V (Small Business Debtor Reorganization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Who May File a Plan.—
Only the debtor may file a plan under this subchapter.
(b) Deadline.—
The debtor shall file a plan not later than 90 days after the order for relief under this chapter, except that the court may extend the period if the need for the extension is attributable to circumstances for which the debtor should not justly be held accountable.
