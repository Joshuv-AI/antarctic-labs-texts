42 U.S.C. § 12645e — Audits and reports
Citation: us:usc:42:12645e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section12645e
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 129 (National And Community Service); Subchapter I (National And Community Service State Grant Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Corporation shall comply with applicable audit and reporting requirements as provided in the Chief Financial Officers Act of 1990 (31 U.S.C. 901 note; Public Law 101–576) and chapter 91 of title 31 (commonly known as the “Government Corporation Control Act”). The Corporation shall report to the authorizing committees any failure to comply with such requirements.
