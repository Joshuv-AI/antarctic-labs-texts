45 C.F.R. § 617.14 — Remedial action by recipients.
Citation: us:cfr:45:617.14
Creator: National Science Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-617.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 617 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Age In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance From Nsf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where the Director finds that a recipient has discriminated on the basis of age, the recipient shall take any remedial action the Director may require to overcome the effects of the discrimination. If another recipient exercises control over the recipient that has discriminated, the Director may require both recipients to take remedial action.
