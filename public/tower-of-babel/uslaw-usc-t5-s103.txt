5 U.S.C. § 103 — Government corporation
Citation: us:usc:5:103
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section103
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part I (The Agencies Generally); Chapter 1 (Organization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of this title—
(1)
“Government corporation” means a corporation owned or controlled by the Government of the United States; and
(2)
“Government controlled corporation” does not include a corporation owned by the Government of the United States.
