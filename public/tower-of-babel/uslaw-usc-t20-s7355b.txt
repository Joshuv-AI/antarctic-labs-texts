20 U.S.C. § 7355b — Rule of construction
Citation: us:usc:20:7355b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section7355b
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 70 (Strengthening And Improvement Of Elementary And Secondary Schools); Subchapter V (Flexibility And Accountability); Part B (Rural Education Initiative); Subpart 3 (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this part shall be construed to prohibit a local educational agency that enters into cooperative arrangements with other local educational agencies for the provision of special, compensatory, or other education services, pursuant to State law or a written agreement, from entering into similar arrangements for the use, or the coordination of the use, of the funds made available under this part.
