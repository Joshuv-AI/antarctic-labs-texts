Joint Conference of ISEH, ICEPH, and ISEG on Environment and Health Successfully Held in Galway
Authors: Chaosheng Zhang
Published: 2024

For the first time, three closely related international conference series on environment and health, International Symposium on Environment and Health (ISEH), International Conference on Environmental Pollution and Health (ICEPH) and Environmental Symposium on Environmental Geochemistry (ISEG), came together as a joint conference (https://www.universityofgalway.ie/iseh-iceph), which was successfully held on campus of University of Galway, Ireland during August 11–18, 2024 (Figure 1).

Quadrangle Building, University of Galway (Photo: Chaosheng Zhang).

The original plan was to have the conference held in 2020. It was postponed to 2024 due to the outbreak of COVID-19 to ensure it was held in the format of in-person! The joint conference provided a historical opportunity for international experts working in environment, health, and related areas including GIS and agriculture, to meet and share the latest understanding of the ever-growing challenges between human and our changing environment. As a joint conference, delegates were allowed and encouraged to attend any sessions of the conference and to extend their academic networks. This approach of conference organization maintained the traditional identities of ISEH, ICEPH and ISEG conference series while providing a new opportunity of networking for all delegates.

Located in the western coast of Ireland, Galway is the “2020 European Capital of Culture.” It attracts more than 1 million international tourists annually. Thanks to the warm Gulf Stream and North Atlantic Current, the landscape of Ireland remains green all year round under a mild marine temperate climate. Delegates of the conference have enjoyed the well-maintained traditional Irish culture and unique natural beauty of Ireland.

This conference was linked to several Sustainable Development Goals of the United Nations: Good Health and Well-being; Quality Education; Clean Water and Sanitation; Responsible Consumption and Production; Climate Action; Life below Water; Life on Land; and Partnerships for the Goals.

This conference was jointly organized by University of Galway, Jinan University, and the Geographical Society of China, and chaired by Prof. Chaosheng Zhang. The sole sponsor of this conference was Failte Ireland (The National Tourism Development Authority, FS012275). There were two exhibitors: Carbon Research and Biochar (journals), and PerkinElmer.

A total number of 504 participants from 31 countries/regions, plus 59 visitors of a student group attended the conference. There were 14 plenary talks, 314 oral presentations arranged in 64 parallel sessions, and 100 poster presentations.

The themes of the conference included the most challenging issues that human beings are currently facing, ranging from natural environment to anthropogenic influences, from exposure to hazardous materials to diseases, from laboratory experiments to field investigations, with the data analytical tools from conventional statistics to artificial intelligence.

ISEH 2026 will be held in Beijing, China, during summer 2026. ISEG 2025 will be held in Aveiro, Portugal, at the end of July and early August 2025. Details will be announced.
