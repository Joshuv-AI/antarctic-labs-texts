25 C.F.R. § 558.1 — Scope of this part.
Citation: us:cfr:25:558.1
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-558.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 558 (Gaming Licenses For Key Employees And Primary Management Officials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless a tribal-state compact assigns responsibility to an entity other than a tribe, the licensing authority for class II or class III gaming is a tribal authority. The procedures and standards of this part apply only to licenses for primary management officials and key employees. This part does not apply to any license that is intended to expire within 90 days of issuance.
