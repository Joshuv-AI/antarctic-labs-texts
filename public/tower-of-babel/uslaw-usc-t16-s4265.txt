16 U.S.C. § 4265 — Acceptance and use of donations
Citation: us:usc:16:4265
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section4265
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 62A (Asian Elephant Conservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may accept and use donations to provide assistance under section 4264 of this title. Amounts received by the Secretary in the form of donations shall be transferred to the Secretary of the Treasury for deposit into the Fund.
