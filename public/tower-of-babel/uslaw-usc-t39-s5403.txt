39 U.S.C. § 5403 — Fines
Citation: us:usc:39:5403
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title39-section5403
Context: United States Code, Title 39 (Postal Service); Part V (Transportation Of Mail); Chapter 54 (Transportation Of Mail By Air). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Postal Service may impose or remit fines on carriers transporting mail by air on routes extending beyond the borders of the United States for—
(1)
unreasonable or unnecessary delay to mail; and
(2)
other delinquencies in the transportation of the mail.
