46 C.F.R. § 194.05-17 — -17 Poisonous articles as chemical stores—Detail requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:46:194.05-17
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-194.05-17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 194 (Handling, Use, And Control Of Explosives And Other Hazardous Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Poisonous articles as chemical stores and reagents shall be governed by subparts 194.15 and 194.20.
(b) Other poisonous articles shall be regulated by the appropriate portions of 49 CFR parts 172, 173, and 176 or part 147 of Subchapter N (Dangerous Cargoes) of this chapter.
