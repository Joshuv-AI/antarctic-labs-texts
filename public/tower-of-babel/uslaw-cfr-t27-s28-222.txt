27 C.F.R. § 28.222 — Claim, Form 1582-B (5130.6).
Citation: us:cfr:27:28.222
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-28.222
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 28 (Exportation Of Alcohol). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Claim for allowance of drawback of internal revenue taxes on beer brewed or produced in the United States shall be prepared on Form 1582-B (5130.6), in quadruplicate, as required by this part. Each Form 1582-B (5130.6) shall be given, by the person initiating the form, a serial number beginning with “1” for the first day of January of each year and running consecutively thereafter to December 31, inclusive.
