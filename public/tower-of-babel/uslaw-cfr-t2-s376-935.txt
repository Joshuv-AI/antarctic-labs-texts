2 C.F.R. § 376.935 — Disqualified. (HHS supplement to government-wide definition at 2 CFR 180.935).
Citation: us:cfr:2:376.935
Creator: Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-376.935
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 376 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Disqualified means persons prohibited from participating in specified federal procurement and nonprocurement transactions pursuant to the statutes listed in 2 CFR 180.935, and pursuant to Title XI of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1320a-7, 1320a-7a, 1320c-5, and 1395ccc) as enforced by the HHS Office of the Inspector General.
