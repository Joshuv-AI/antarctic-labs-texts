The spectral Sullivan conjecture
Ishan Levy

Abstract
We show that any map from an infinite loop space to a p p -complete nilpotent finite dimensional space factors canonically through a union of p p -adic tori. This is proven via bootstrapping from the case of B ​ ℤ / p ​ ℤ B\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z} , which is the key case of the Sullivan conjecture proven by Miller. The main step in our proof is to show that the subcategory of spectra generated by the reduced suspension spectrum of B ​ ℤ / p ​ ℤ B\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z} under colimits and extensions agrees with that of a Moore spectrum.

Introduction

There is a dichotomy in homotopy theory of the types of spaces that are often studied. On one hand, there are spaces that are equivalent to finite dimensional CW-complexes such as many spaces coming from manifolds, varieties. On the other hand, there are spaces that are used as ‘homotopy theoretic moduli spaces’, i.e maps into them classify some kind of homotopy theoretic data associated to the source. Spaces of this form include

(see also

Connective Bousfield classes

The goal of this section is to prove

The following is a stable analog of a result of Bousfield
[

Lemma 2.1
.

Let
Z
be a connective spectrum. Then for any

\langle Z\rangle^{s}=\langle\Sigma^{n}Z\oplus Z\otimes\mathbb{Z}\rangle^{s}

Proof.

Clearly
\langle Z\rangle^{s}\supset\langle\Sigma^{n}Z\oplus Z\otimes\mathbb{Z}\rangle^{s}
. For the other inclusion, it is clear that by induction on
n
, it suffices to prove it for

Z\to Z\otimes\mathbb{Z}
is in
\langle\Sigma Z\rangle^{s}
. But this is
Z\otimes\tau_{\geq 1}\mathbb{S}
, and
\tau_{\geq 1}\mathbb{S}
is
1

\Sigma\mathbb{S}
.
∎

The goal of this section is to prove
Theorem

1.5
. We begin by reformulating connective Bousfield classes in terms of localizations:

Definition 2.2
.

Let
C
be a presentable category and let

L_{f}:C\to L_{f}C
to be the presentable localization that inverts the morphism

. In the case that

Lemma 2.3

Let

be a presentable stable category. Then the collection
\{Z|P_{X}Z=0\}
is the smallest subcategory generated under colimits and extensions by

Proof.

It is easy to see that the collection of objects with

is closed under extensions and colimits and contains

can be computed using a small object argument, by transfinitely pushing out along homotopy classes of maps from
\Sigma^{n}X\to*

∎

We use

In

The transfer realizes

Using a homology decomposition, we see that
