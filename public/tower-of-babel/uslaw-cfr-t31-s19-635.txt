31 C.F.R. § 19.635 — May the Department of the Treasury settle a debarment or suspension action?
Citation: us:cfr:31:19.635
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-19.635
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 19 (Governmentwide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, we may settle a debarment or suspension action at any time if it is in the best interest of the Federal Government.
