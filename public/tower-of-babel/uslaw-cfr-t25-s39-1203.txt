25 C.F.R. § 39.1203 — Future consideration of contract school operation and maintenance funding.
Citation: us:cfr:25:39.1203
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-39.1203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 39 (The Indian School Equalization Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Assistant Secretary shall arrange for full funding for operation and maintenance of contract schools by fiscal year 1981.
