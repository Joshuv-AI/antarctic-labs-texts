46 C.F.R. § 184.502 — Requirements of the Federal Communications Commission.
Citation: us:cfr:46:184.502
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-184.502
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 184 (Vessel Control And Miscellaneous Systems And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A vessel must comply with the applicable requirements for any radio and Electronic Position Indicating Radiobeacon (EPIRB) installations, including the requirements for a station license and installation certificates to be issued by the Federal Communications Commission, as set forth in 47 CFR part 80.
