7 U.S.C. § 6813 — Regulations
Citation: us:usc:7:6813
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section6813
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 97 (Fresh Cut Flowers And Fresh Cut Greens Promotion And Information). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may issue such regulations as are necessary to carry out this chapter and the powers vested in the Secretary by this chapter, including regulations relating to the assessment of late payment charges and interest.
