12 C.F.R. § 1254.10 — Withdrawal of application.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1254.10
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1254.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1254 (Validation And Approval Of Credit Score Models). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At any time during the validation and approval process, an applicant may withdraw its application by notifying an Enterprise. The Enterprise may, in its sole discretion, determine whether to return any portion of the application fee paid by the applicant.
