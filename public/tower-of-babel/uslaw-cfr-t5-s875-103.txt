5 C.F.R. § 875.103 — Do I need to authorize release of my medical records when I file a claim?
Citation: us:cfr:5:875.103
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-875.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 875 (Federal Long Term Care Insurance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, if you file a claim for benefits, the Carrier needs to have a valid authorization from you to release your medical records.
