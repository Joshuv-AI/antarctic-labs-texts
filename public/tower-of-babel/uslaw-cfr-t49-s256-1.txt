49 C.F.R. § 256.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:49:256.1
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-256.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 256 (Financial Assistance For Railroad Passenger Terminals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to establish procedures for implementing subsection 4(i) of the Department of Transportation Act as amended (49 U.S.C. 1653(i)) with respect to all financial assistance provided under that subsection.
