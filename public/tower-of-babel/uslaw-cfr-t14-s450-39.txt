14 C.F.R. § 450.39 — Use of safety element approval.
Citation: us:cfr:14:450.39
Creator: Commercial Space Transportation, Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-450.39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 450 (Launch And Reentry License Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an applicant proposes to use any vehicle, safety system, process, service, or personnel for which the FAA has issued a safety element approval under part 414 of this chapter, the FAA will not reevaluate that safety element during a license application evaluation to the extent its use is within its approved scope.
