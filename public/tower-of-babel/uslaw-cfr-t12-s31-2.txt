12 C.F.R. § 31.2 — Insider lending restrictions and reporting requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:12:31.2
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-31.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 31 (Extensions Of Credit To Insiders And Transactions With Affiliates). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) General rule. National banks, Federal savings associations, and their insiders shall comply with the provisions contained in 12 CFR part 215 (Regulation O).
(b) Enforcement. The Comptroller of the Currency administers and enforces insider lending standards and reporting requirements as they apply to national banks, Federal savings associations, and their insiders.
