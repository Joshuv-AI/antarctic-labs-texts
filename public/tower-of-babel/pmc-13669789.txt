Correction to: DeepDrugs: a mechanism-aware tri-linear attention framework for synergistic drug-combination prediction
Published: 2026

This is a correction to: Gaojia Xin, Yanhao Zhu, Qiuyu Li, Jiyun Han, Zeyu Xu, Hua Wang, Juntao Liu, DeepDrugs: a mechanism-aware tri-linear attention framework for synergistic drug-combination prediction, Briefings in Bioinformatics, Volume 27, Issue 2, March 2026, bbag205, https://doi.org/10.1093/bib/bbag205

In the originally published version of this manuscript, the affiliation for co-author Hua Wang erroneously read “Department of Neurosurgery, Shandong Provincial Hospital Affiliated to Shandong First Medical University, Jinan 250021, China”.

This has been corrected to read “Department of Neurology, Shandong Provincial Hospital Affiliated to Shandong First Medical University, Jinan 250021, China”.
