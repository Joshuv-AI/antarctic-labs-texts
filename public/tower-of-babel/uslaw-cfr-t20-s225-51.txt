20 C.F.R. § 225.51 — PIA's that are subject to recomputation.
Citation: us:cfr:20:225.51
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-225.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 225 (Primary Insurance Amount Determinations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following PIA's are subject to recomputation—
(a) Tier I PIA;
(b) Survivor Tier I PIA;
(c) Overall Minimum PIA;
(d) Employee RIB PIA; and
(e) Residual Lump-Sum PIA.
