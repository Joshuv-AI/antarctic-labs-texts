8 U.S.C. § 1738 — Identification documents for certain newly admitted aliens
Citation: us:usc:8:1738
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title8-section1738
Context: United States Code, Title 8 (Aliens And Nationality); Chapter 15 (Enhanced Border Security And Visa Entry Reform); Subchapter III (Visa Issuance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 180 days after May 14, 2002, the Attorney General shall ensure that, immediately upon the arrival in the United States of an individual admitted under section 1157 of this title, or immediately upon an alien being granted asylum under section 1158 of this title, the alien will be issued an employment authorization document. Such document shall, at a minimum, contain the fingerprint and photograph of such alien.
