45 C.F.R. § 2508.20 — What are the restrictions regarding the release of mailing lists?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2508.20
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2508.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2508 (Implementation Of The Privacy Act Of 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An individual's name and address may not be sold or rented by the Corporation unless such action is specifically authorized by law. This section does not require the withholding of names and addresses otherwise permitted to be made public.
