31 C.F.R. § 19.135 — May the Department of the Treasury exclude a person who is not currently participating in a nonprocurement transaction?
Citation: us:cfr:31:19.135
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-19.135
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 19 (Governmentwide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Given a cause that justifies an exclusion under this part, we may exclude any person who has been involved, is currently involved, or may reasonably be expected to be involved in a covered transaction.
