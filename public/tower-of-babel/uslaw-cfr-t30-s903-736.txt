30 C.F.R. § 903.736 — Permit fees.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 903 (Arizona). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 736.25 of this chapter, Permit fees applies to any person who makes application for a permit to conduct surface coal mining and reclamation operations in Arizona.
