43 U.S.C. § 617q — Effect on authority of States to control waters within own borders
Citation: us:usc:43:617q
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section617q
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 12A (Boulder Canyon Project); Subchapter I (Boulder Canyon Project Act). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing herein shall be construed as interfering with such rights as the States had on December 21, 1928, either to the waters within their borders or to adopt such policies and enact such laws as they deem necessary with respect to the appropriation, control, and use of waters within their borders, except as modified by the Colorado River compact or other interstate agreement.
