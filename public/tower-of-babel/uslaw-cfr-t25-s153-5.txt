25 C.F.R. § 153.5 — Children of competent Indians.
Citation: us:cfr:25:153.5
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-153.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 153 (Determination Of Competency: Crow Indians). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Children of competent Indians who have attained or upon attaining their majority shall automatically become competent except any such Indian who is declared incompetent by a court of competent jurisdiction or who is incompetent under the laws of the State within which he resides.
