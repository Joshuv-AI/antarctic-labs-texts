43 C.F.R. § 3834.22 — How will I know that BLM has adjusted location and maintenance fees?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3834.22
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3834.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3834 (Required Fees For Mining Claims Or Sites). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

BLM will publish a notice in the Federal Register about the adjustment on or before July 1st of a given year in order to make the adjusted fees due on September 1st of the same year.
