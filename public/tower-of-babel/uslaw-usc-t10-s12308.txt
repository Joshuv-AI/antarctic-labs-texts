10 U.S.C. § 12308 — Retention after becoming qualified for retired pay
Citation: us:usc:10:12308
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section12308
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel Generally); Chapter 1209 (Active Duty). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person who has qualified for retired pay under chapter 1223 of this title may, with his consent and by order of the Secretary concerned, be retained on active duty, or in service in a reserve component other than that listed in section 12732(b) of this title. A member so retained shall be credited with that service for all purposes.
