42 C.F.R. § 460.172 — Documentation of disenrollment.
Citation: us:cfr:42:460.172
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-460.172
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 460 (Programs Of All-Inclusive Care For The Elderly (Pace)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A PACE organization must meet the following requirements:
(a) Have a procedure in place to document the reasons for all voluntary and involuntary disenrollments.
(b) Make documentation available for review by CMS and the State administering agency.
(c) Use the information on voluntary disenrollments in the PACE organization's internal quality improvement program.
