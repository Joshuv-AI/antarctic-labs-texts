Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 9a (PART 9a—SECURITY INFORMATION REGULATIONS APPLICABLE TO CERTAIN INTERNATIONAL ENERGY PROGRAMS; RELATED MATERIAL). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These regulations apply to all information and material classified by the United States under the provisions of E.O. 11932, dated August 4, 1976 entitled “Classification of Certain Information and Material Obtained From Advisory Bodies Created To Implement The International Energy Program.”
