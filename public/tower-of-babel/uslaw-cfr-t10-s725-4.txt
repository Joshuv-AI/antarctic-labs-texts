10 C.F.R. § 725.4 — Interpretations.
Citation: us:cfr:10:725.4
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-725.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 725 (Permits For Access To Restricted Data). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as specifically authorized by the Director, Office of Environment, Health, Safety and Security in writing no interpretation of the meaning of the regulations in this part by any officer or employee of DOE other than a written interpretation by the General Counsel will be recognized to be binding upon DOE.
