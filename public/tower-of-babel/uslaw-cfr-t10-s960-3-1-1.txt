10 C.F.R. § 960.3-1-1 — -1-1 Diversity of geohydrologic settings.
Citation: us:cfr:10:960.3-1-1
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-960.3-1-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 960 (General Guidelines For The Preliminary Screening Of Potential Sites For A Nuclear Waste Repository). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Consideration shall be given to a variety of geohydrologic settings in which sites for the development of repositories may be located. To the extent practicable, sites recommended as candidate sites for characterization shall be located in different geohydrologic settings.
