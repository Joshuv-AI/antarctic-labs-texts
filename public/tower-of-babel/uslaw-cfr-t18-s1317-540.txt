18 C.F.R. § 1317.540 — Advertising.
Citation: us:cfr:18:1317.540
Creator: Tennessee Valley Authority
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-1317.540
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 1317 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Sex In Education Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A recipient shall not in any advertising related to employment indicate preference, limitation, specification, or discrimination based on sex unless sex is a bona fide occupational qualification for the particular job in question.
