16 U.S.C. § 460jj–4 — Application of State water laws
Citation: us:usc:16:460jj–4
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section460jj–4
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter XCIV (Arapaho National Recreation Area). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The jurisdiction of the State of Colorado and the United States over waters of any stream included in the Arapaho National Recreation Area shall be determined by established principles of law. Nothing in this subchapter shall constitute an express or implied claim or denial on the part of the Federal Government as to exemption from State water laws.
