37 U.S.C. § 425 — United States Navy Band; United States Marine Corps Band: allowances while on concert tour
Citation: us:usc:37:425
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title37-section425
Context: United States Code, Title 37 (Pay And Allowances Of The Uniformed Services); Chapter 7 (Allowances Other Than Travel And Transportation Allowances). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

While on concert tours approved by the President, the members of the United States Navy Band and the United States Marine Corps Band do not forfeit allowances.
