45 C.F.R. § 87.4 — Severability.
Citation: us:cfr:45:87.4
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-87.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 87 (Equal Treatment For Faith-Based Organizations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To the extent that any provision of this part is declared invalid by a court of competent jurisdiction, the Department intends for all other provisions that are capable of operating in the absence of the specific provision that has been invalidated to remain in effect.
