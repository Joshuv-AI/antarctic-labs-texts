14 C.F.R. § 1261.102 — Maximum amount.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1261.102
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1261.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1261 (Processing Of Monetary Claims (General)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

From October 1, 1982, to October 30, 1988, the maximum amount that may be paid under the Military Personnel and Civilian Employees' Claim Act of 1964, as amended (31 U.S.C. 3721) is $25,000, and on or after October 31, 1988, the maximum amount is $40,000 (Pub. L. 100-565, 102 Stat. 2833, October 31, 1988).
