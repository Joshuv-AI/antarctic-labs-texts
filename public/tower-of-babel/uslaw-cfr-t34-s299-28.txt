34 C.F.R. § 299.28 — Continuation of a bypass.
Citation: us:cfr:34:299.28
Creator: Office Of Elementary And Secondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-299.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 299 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary continues a bypass until the Secretary determines, in consultation with the relevant agency, consortium, or entity and representatives of the affected private school children, teachers or other educational personnel, or families, that there will no longer be any failure or inability on the part of the agency, consortium, or entity to meet the requirements for providing services.
