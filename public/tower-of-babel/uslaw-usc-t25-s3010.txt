25 U.S.C. § 3010 — Special relationship between Federal Government and Indian tribes and Native Hawaiian organizations
Citation: us:usc:25:3010
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section3010
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 32 (Native American Graves Protection And Repatriation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter reflects the unique relationship between the Federal Government and Indian tribes and Native Hawaiian organizations and should not be construed to establish a precedent with respect to any other individual, organization or foreign government.
