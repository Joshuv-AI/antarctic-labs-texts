45 C.F.R. § 1701.1 — Statement of policy.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1701.1
Creator: National Commission On Libraries And Information Science
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1701.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1701 (Disclosure Of Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The records of the National Commission on Libraries and Information Science shall be available to the fullest extent possible consistent with the terms and policies of 5 U.S.C. section 552 and on request will be promptly furnished to any member of the public.
