36 U.S.C. § 80105 — Principal office and meetings
Citation: us:usc:36:80105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section80105
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 801 (General Federation Of Women’S Clubs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Principal Office.—
The principal office of the corporation shall be in the District of Columbia.
(b) Meetings.—
The corporation may hold its meetings at places outside the District of Columbia.
