17 C.F.R. § 270.10e-1 — e-1 Death, disqualification, or bona fide resignation of directors.
Citation: us:cfr:17:270.10e-1
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-270.10e-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 270 (Rules And Regulations, Investment Company Act Of 1940). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a registered investment company, by reason of the death, disqualification, or bona fide resignation of any director, does not meet any requirement of the Act or any rule or regulation thereunder regarding the composition of the company's board of directors, the operation of the relevant subsection of the Act, rule, or regulation will be suspended as to the company:
(a) For 90 days if the vacancy may be filled by action of the board of directors; or
(b) For 150 days if a vote of stockholders is required to fill the vacancy.
