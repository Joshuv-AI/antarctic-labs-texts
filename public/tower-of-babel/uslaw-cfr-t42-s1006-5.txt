42 C.F.R. § 1006.5 — Enforcement of a subpoena.
Citation: us:cfr:42:1006.5
Creator: Office Of Inspector General-Health Care, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-1006.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 1006 (Investigational Inquiries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A subpoena to appear at an investigational inquiry is enforceable through the District Court of the United States and the district where the subpoenaed person is found, resides or transacts business.
