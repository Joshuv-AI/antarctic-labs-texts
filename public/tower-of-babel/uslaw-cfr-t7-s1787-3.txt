7 C.F.R. § 1787.3 — Products constituting a portion of a purchase order or contract.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1787.3
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1787.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1787 (The “Buy American” Requirement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where a supplier or contractor offers or furnishes several products under a purchase order or contract, the provisions of this part apply to each product individually.
