Regulatory Amendment 39 (Sunsetting of the Spawning Special Management 
Zones)

    In 2016, Amendment 36 established five spawning special management 
zones (SSMZs): South Cape Lookout, North Carolina; Georgetown/Devil's 
Hole, Area 51 and Area 53, South Carolina; and Warsaw Hole, Florida. 
The goal of these areas was to protect spawning fish and their habitat. 
Three of the SSMZs, South Cape Lookout, Georgetown/Devil's Hole, and 
Warsaw Hole had a sunset provision on the regulations put in place 
through this amendment. The sunset provision allowed 10 years to 
conduct research and monitoring in the SSMZs to determine if they 
provided protection for spawning fish. The Council is now considering 
extending the sunset provision for an additional 10 years to allow more 
time for additional research and monitoring.
