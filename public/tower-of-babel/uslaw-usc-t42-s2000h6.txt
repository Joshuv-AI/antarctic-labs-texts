42 U.S.C. § 2000h–6 — Separability
Citation: us:usc:42:2000h–6
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2000h–6
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 21 (Civil Rights); Subchapter IX (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this Act or the application thereof to any person or circumstances is held invalid, the remainder of the Act and the application of the provision to other persons not similarly situated or to other circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
