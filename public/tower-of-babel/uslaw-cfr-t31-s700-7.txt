31 C.F.R. § 700.7 — Alcoholic beverages, narcotics, and drugs.
Citation: us:cfr:31:700.7
Creator: Federal Law Enforcement Training Center, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-700.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 700 (Regulations Governing Conduct In Or On The Federal Law Enforcement Training Center (Fletc) Buildings And Grounds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Entering or being on the property, or operating a motor vehicle thereon, by a person under the influence of alcoholic beverages, narcotics, hallucinogenic or dangerous drugs, or marijuana is prohibited. The possession or use of any unlawful drug or substance contrary to the provisions of Federal, State, or local law in or on the property is prohibited.
