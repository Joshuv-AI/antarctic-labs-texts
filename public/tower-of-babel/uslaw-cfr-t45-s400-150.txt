45 C.F.R. § 400.150 — General eligibility requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:45:400.150
Creator: Office Of Refugee Resettlement, Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-400.150
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 400 (Refugee Resettlement Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Eligibility for refugee social services is limited to those refugees who—
(a) Meet immigration status and identification requirements in Subpart D of this part;
(b) Meet the other eligibility requirements and conditions in this subpart.
