9 C.F.R. § 350.1 — Meaning of words.
Citation: us:cfr:9:350.1
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-350.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 350 (Special Services Relating To Meat And Other Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Words used in this part in the singular form shall be deemed to import the plural, and vice versa, as the case may demand.
