9 C.F.R. § 73.11 — Treatment of means of conveyance and premises having contained scabby cattle.
Citation: us:cfr:9:73.11
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-73.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 73 (Scabies In Cattle). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Means of conveyance, yards, pens, sheds, chutes, or other premises or facilities which have contained cattle of a consignment in which scabies is found shall be treated within 72 hours of use and prior to further use in the required concentration with a permitted dip listed in § 73.10 under supervision of a State or Federal inspector or an accredited veterinarian.
