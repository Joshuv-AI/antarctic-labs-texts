20 U.S.C. § 1132–4 — Evaluation, outreach, and information
Citation: us:usc:20:1132–4
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1132–4
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 28 (Higher Education Resources And Student Assistance); Subchapter VI (International Education Programs); Part D (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may use not more than one percent of the funds made available under this subchapter to carry out program evaluation, national outreach, and information dissemination activities relating to the programs authorized under this subchapter.
