20 C.F.R. § 614.20 — Information to ex-servicemembers.
Citation: us:cfr:20:614.20
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-614.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 614 (Unemployment Compensation For Ex-Servicemembers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At the time of discharge or release from Federal military service, each Federal military agency shall furnish to each ex-servicemember information explaining rights and responsibilities under the UCX Program and 18 U.S.C. 1919, and military documents necessary for filing claims for UCX.
