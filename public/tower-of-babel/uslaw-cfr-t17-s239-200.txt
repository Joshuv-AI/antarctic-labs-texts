17 C.F.R. § 239.200 — Form 1-E, notification under Regulation E.
Citation: us:cfr:17:239.200
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-239.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 239 (Forms Prescribed Under The Securities Act Of 1933). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This form shall be used for notification pursuant to Rule 604 (§ 230.604 of this chapter) of Regulation E (§§ 230.601-230.610a of this chapter) by a small business investment company or business development company described in Rule 602 (§ 230.602 of this chapter).
