16 C.F.R. § 240.15 — Cost justification.
Citation: us:cfr:16:240.15
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-240.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 240 (Guides For Advertising Allowances And Other Merchandising Payments And Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is no defense to a charge of unlawful discrimination in the payment of an allowance or the furnishing of a service for a seller to show that such payment or service could be justified through savings in the cost of manufacture, sale or delivery.
