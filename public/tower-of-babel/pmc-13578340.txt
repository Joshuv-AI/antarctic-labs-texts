Retraction Note: Design, synthesis and biological evaluation of novel chalcone-derived thioxopyridine and pyrazolopyridine compounds as antimicrobial agents
Authors: Gaber Algaber, P. Shyamala, Zaccaria Dammag, Mohammed Al-haddad
Published: 2026

Retraction of: Scientific Reports https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-51574-z, published online 10 May 2026

The Editors have retracted this Article.

After publication, information was brought to the Editors' attention indicating that the data reported in this Article were used without the authorisation of the data owners. In addition, the authorship and contribution statements do not correctly reflect the roles and contributions of the individuals involved in the work. As a result, the provenance of the data and the attribution of responsibility for the work cannot be reliably established. The Editors therefore no longer have confidence in this Article.

All Authors agree with this retraction.
