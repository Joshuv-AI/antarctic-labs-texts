40 C.F.R. § 303.31 — Assurance of claimant confidentiality.
Citation: us:cfr:40:303.31
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-303.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 303 (Citizen Awards For Information On Criminal Violations Under Superfund). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person, except as authorized by the Agency's Office of Enforcement and Compliance Monitoring to have this knowledge, shall be given access to the identity of, or information that would lead to the identity of, a claimant who has requested anonymity prior to disclosing information to the Agency.
