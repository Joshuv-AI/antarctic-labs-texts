29 C.F.R. § 1404.8 — Freedom of choice.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1404.8
Creator: Federal Mediation And Conciliation Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1404.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1404 (Arbitration Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing contained in this part should be construed to limit the rights of parties who use FMCS arbitration services to jointly select any arbitrator or arbitration procedure acceptable to them. Once a request is made to OA, all parties are subject to the procedures contained in this part.
