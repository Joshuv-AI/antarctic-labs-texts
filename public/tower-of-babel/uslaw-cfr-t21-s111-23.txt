21 C.F.R. § 111.23 — Under this subpart C, what records must you make and keep?
Citation: us:cfr:21:111.23
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-111.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 111 (Current Good Manufacturing Practice In Manufacturing, Packaging, Labeling, Or Holding Operations For Dietary Supplements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You must make and keep records required under this subpart C in accordance with subpart P of this part.
(b) You must make and keep records of the written procedures for cleaning the physical plant and for pest control.
(c) You must make and keep records that show that water, when used in a manner such that the water may become a component of the dietary supplement, meets the requirements of § 111.15(e)(2).
