12 C.F.R. § 601.100 — Cross-references to employee ethical conduct standards and financial disclosure regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:12:601.100
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-601.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 601 (Employee Responsibilities And Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Board members, officers, and other employees of the Farm Credit Administration are subject to the Standards of Ethical Conduct for Employees of the Executive Branch at 5 CFR part 2635, the Farm Credit Administration regulation at 5 CFR part 4101, which supplements the Executive Branch-wide Standards, and the executive branch-wide financial disclosure regulations at 5 CFR part 2634.
