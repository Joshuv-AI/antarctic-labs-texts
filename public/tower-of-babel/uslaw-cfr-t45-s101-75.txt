45 C.F.R. § 101.75 — Compliance conflicts.
Citation: us:cfr:45:101.75
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-101.75
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 101 (Health Resources Priorities And Allocations System (Hrpas)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If compliance with any provision of the DPA and other applicable statutes, this part, or an official action would prevent a person from filling a rated order or from complying with another provision of the DPA and other applicable statutes, this part, or an official action, the person must immediately notify HHS, as specified in § 101.93, for resolution of the conflict.
