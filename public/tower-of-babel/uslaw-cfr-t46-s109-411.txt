46 C.F.R. § 109.411 — Notice and reporting of casualty.
Citation: us:cfr:46:109.411
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-109.411
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 109 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The owner, operator, or person in charge of a mobile offshore drilling unit (MODU) regulated under this part must provide notice and report marine casualties in accordance with 46 CFR part 4.
