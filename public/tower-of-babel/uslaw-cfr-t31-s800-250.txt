31 C.F.R. § 800.250 — Unaffiliated TID U.S. business.
Citation: us:cfr:31:800.250
Creator: Office Of Investment Security, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-800.250
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 800 (Regulations Pertaining To Certain Investments In The United States By Foreign Persons). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term unaffiliated TID U.S. business means, with respect to a foreign person, a TID U.S. business in which that foreign person does not directly hold more than 50 percent of the outstanding voting interest or have the right to appoint more than half of the members of the board of directors or equivalent governing body.
