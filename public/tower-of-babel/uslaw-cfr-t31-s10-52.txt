31 C.F.R. § 10.52 — Violations subject to sanction.
Citation: us:cfr:31:10.52
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-10.52
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 10 (Practice Before The Internal Revenue Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A practitioner may be sanctioned under § 10.50 if the practitioner—
(1) Willfully violates any of the regulations (other than § 10.33) contained in this part; or
(2) Recklessly or through gross incompetence (within the meaning of § 10.51(a)(13)) violates § 10.34, § 10.35, § 10.36 or § 10.37.
(b) Effective/applicability date. This section is applicable to conduct occurring on or after September 26, 2007.
