17 C.F.R. § 148.2 — When the Act applies.
Citation: us:cfr:17:148.2
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-148.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 148 (Implementation Of The Equal Access To Justice Act In Covered Adjudicatory Proceedings Before The Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Act applies to any covered adjudicatory proceeding pending before the Commission on or after October 1, 1981. This includes proceedings begun before October 1, 1981, if final Commission action has not been taken before that date. Awards may be sought for fees and other expenses incurred before October 1, 1981, in any such covered proceeding.
