46 C.F.R. § 69.201 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:46:69.201
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-69.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 69 (Measurement Of Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes the procedures for measuring a vessel under the Simplified Regulatory Measurement System described in 46 U.S.C. chapter 145, subchapter III.
