Introduction

We show how to build models of the system presented in

At the same time, we report a mistake,

This mistake was found by the second author, and a solution for the general case will be presented in forthcoming work.

Interestingly, the problem involves the notion of

In the paper

]

1.1
Levelwise Problem

One way to understand this problem is the following. The algebra
L[X]
in
sets

If

This problem can be summarized informally by stating that we may not have the equality

1.2
Strict Coherence Problem

The second issue is now a strict coherence problem. Even in the decidable case,
