10 C.F.R. § 61.15 — Financial information.
Citation: us:cfr:10:61.15
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-61.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 61 (Licensing Requirements For Land Disposal Of Radioactive Waste). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The financial information must be sufficient to demonstrate that the financial qualifications of the applicant are adequate to carry out the activities for which the license is sought and meet other financial assurance requirements as specified in subpart E of this part.
