7 C.F.R. § 1c.124 — c.124 Conditions.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1c.124
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1c.124
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1c (PART 1c—PROTECTION OF HUMAN SUBJECTS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

With respect to any research project or any class of research projects the department or agency head of either the conducting or the supporting Federal department or agency may impose additional conditions prior to or at the time of approval when in the judgment of the department or agency head additional conditions are necessary for the protection of human subjects.
