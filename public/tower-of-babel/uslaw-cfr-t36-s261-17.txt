36 C.F.R. § 261.17 — Recreation fees.
Citation: us:cfr:36:261.17
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-261.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 261 (Prohibitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Failure to pay any recreation fee is prohibited. Notwithstanding 18 U.S.C. 3571(e), the fine imposed for the first offense of nonpayment shall not exceed $100.
