46 C.F.R. § 154.1816 — Cargo location plan.
Citation: us:cfr:46:154.1816
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-154.1816
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 154 (Safety Standards For Self-Propelled Vessels Carrying Bulk Liquefied Gases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The master shall ensure that:
(a) A cargo location plan is prepared that gives:
(1) The location and number of each cargo tank; and
(2) The name of the cargo in each tank;
(b) One cargo location plan is kept with the sets of cargo information cards required under § 154.1814; and
(c) The cargo names in the cargo location plan do not differ from the names of the cargoes listed in Table 4.
