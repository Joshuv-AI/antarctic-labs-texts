19 C.F.R. § 190.0a — a Claims filed under NAFTA and USMCA.
Citation: us:cfr:19:190.0a
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-190.0a
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 190 (Modernized Drawback). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Claims for drawback filed under the provisions of part 181 or part 182 of this chapter must be filed separately from claims filed under the provisions of this part.
