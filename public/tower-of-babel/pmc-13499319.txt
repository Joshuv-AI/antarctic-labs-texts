Correction: Multi-functional self-assembly nanoparticles originating from small molecule natural product for oral insulin delivery through modulating tight junctions
Authors: Xiaohui Jia, Zhihua Yuan, Yuqin Yang, Xuemei Huang, Nana Han, Xiaojing Liu, Xiaoyu Lin, Tao Ma, Bing Xu, Penglong Wang, Haimin Lei
Published: 2026

Correction: Journal of Nanobiotechnology (2022) 20:116

10.1186/s12951-022-01260-9

During a careful post-publication review, the authors noticed an error in Fig. 6D, the H&E staining of heart for the Model group in Fig. 6D was inadvertently duplicated from the H&E staining for the INS@BA-Al NPs group – that is, the same image was used to represent both groups. This error occurred during the figure preparation process and was not detected prior to publication. For completeness and transparency, the old and new versions of Fig. 6 are displayed below.
