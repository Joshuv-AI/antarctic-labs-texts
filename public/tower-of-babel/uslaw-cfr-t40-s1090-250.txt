40 C.F.R. § 1090.250 — Certified butane standards.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1090.250
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1090.250
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1090 (Regulation Of Fuels, Fuel Additives, And Regulated Blendstocks). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Butane designated as certified butane under § 1090.1000(e) for use under the butane blending provisions of § 1090.1320(b) must meet the following per-gallon standards:
(a) Butane content. Minimum 85 volume percent.
(b) Benzene content. Maximum 0.03 volume percent.
(c) Sulfur content. Maximum 10 ppm.
(d) Chemical composition. Be composed solely of carbon, hydrogen, oxygen, nitrogen, and sulfur.
