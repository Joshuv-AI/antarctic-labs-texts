48 C.F.R. § 1631.205-78 — 1631.205-78 FEHBP printed material costs.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1631.205-78
Creator: Chapter 16—Office Of Personnel Management Federal Employees Health Benefits Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1631.205-78
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1631 (Contract Cost Principles And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless OPM determines that it is in the best interest of the FEHBP to do otherwise, if a carrier orders printed material that is available from the Government Printing Office (GPO) under the “rider system” from another source, the allowable contract charges shall be the lesser of the amount actually paid or the cost that would have been incurred had the carrier ridden OPM's GPO order.
