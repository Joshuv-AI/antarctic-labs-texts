48 C.F.R. § 1316.203-4 — 1316.203-4 Contract clauses.

Creator: Chapter 13—Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1316.203-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1316 (Types Of Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contracting officers shall use an economic price adjustment clause based on cost indexes of labor or material after obtaining approval for use of the clause from the head of the contracting office.
