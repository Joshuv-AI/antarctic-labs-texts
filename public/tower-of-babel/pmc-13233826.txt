Author Correction: Hospital information system based psychological nursing improves maternal and neonatal outcomes in cesarean section patients
Authors: Yuping Ye, Hongmei Cai, Dan Xiao, Yongmei Yang, Hua Liang, Runzuan Li
Published: 2026

Correction to: Scientific reports 10.1038/s41598-025-30605-1, published online 17 December 2025

The original version of this Article contained errors. In the Ethical approval section, the approval number and date were incorrectly stated as "FWCH-2021-IRB-089, dated December 15, 2021", and in Fig. 2 they were incorrectly given as "FSWH-2021-IRB-089, dated December 15, 2021". The correct approval details are "FSFY MEC 2022 041, dated 17 February 2022".

Overall study design flowchart.

As a result, in the Ethical approval section,

“This study was approved by the Ethics Committee of Foshan Women and Children Hospital (Ethics Approval Number: FWCH-2021-IRB-089, dated December 15, 2021). ”

now reads

“This study was approved by the Ethics Committee of Foshan Women and Children Hospital (Ethics Approval Number: FSFY-MEC-2022-041, dated February 17, 2022). ”

The corresponding ethical approval information in Fig. 2 has also been updated. The original Fig. 2 and accompanying legends appear below.

The original Article has been corrected.
