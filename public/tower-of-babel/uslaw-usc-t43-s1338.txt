43 U.S.C. § 1338 — Disposition of revenues
Citation: us:usc:43:1338
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section1338
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 29 (Submerged Lands); Subchapter III (Outer Continental Shelf Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All rentals, royalties, and other sums paid to the Secretary or the Secretary of the Navy under any lease on the outer Continental Shelf for the period from June 5, 1950, to date, and thereafter shall be deposited in the Treasury of the United States and credited to miscellaneous receipts.
