49 C.F.R. § 586.3 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:49:586.3
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-586.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 586 (Replica Motor Vehicles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to low-volume manufacturers that wish to register with NHTSA as a replica motor vehicle manufacturer, and to manufacturers registered as replica motor vehicle manufacturers.
