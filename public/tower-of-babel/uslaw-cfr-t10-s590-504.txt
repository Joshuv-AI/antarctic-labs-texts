10 C.F.R. § 590.504 — Denial by operation of law.
Citation: us:cfr:10:590.504
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-590.504
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 590 (Administrative Procedures With Respect To The Import And Export Of Natural Gas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless the Assistant Secretary acts upon the application for rehearing within thirty (30) days after it is filed, it is deemed to be denied. Such denial shall constitute final agency action for the purpose of judicial review.
