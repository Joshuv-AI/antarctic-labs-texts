46 C.F.R. § 164.009-25 — -25 Marking.
Citation: us:cfr:46:164.009-25
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-164.009-25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 164 (Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The manufacturer must mark each shipping container for an approved noncombustible material with the approval number and date of approval of the material.
