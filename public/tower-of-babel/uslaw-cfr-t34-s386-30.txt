34 C.F.R. § 386.30 — What are the matching requirements?
Citation: us:cfr:34:386.30
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-386.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 386 (Rehabilitation Training: Rehabilitation Long-Term Training). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The grantee is required to contribute at least ten percent of the total cost of a project under this program. However, if the grantee can demonstrate that it has insufficient resources to contribute the entire match but that it can fulfill all other requirements for receiving an award, the Secretary may waive part of the non-Federal share of the cost of the project after negotiations with Department staff.
