40 C.F.R. § 22.2 — Use of number and gender.
Citation: us:cfr:40:22.2
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-22.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 22 (Consolidated Rules Of Practice Governing The Administrative Assessment Of Civil Penalties And The Revocation/Termination Or Suspension Of Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in these Consolidated Rules of Practice, words in the singular also include the plural and words in the masculine gender also include the feminine, and vice versa, as the case may require.
