34 C.F.R. § 303.230 — Standard for disapproval of an application.
Citation: us:cfr:34:303.230
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-303.230
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 303 (Early Intervention Program For Infants And Toddlers With Disabilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary does not disapprove an application under this part unless the Secretary determines, after notice and opportunity for a hearing in accordance with the procedures in §§ 303.231 through 303.236, that the application fails to comply with the requirements of this part.
