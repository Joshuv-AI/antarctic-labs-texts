Editorial Expression of Concern: Lacking of palladin leads to multiple cellular events changes which contribute to NTD
Authors: Juan Tan, Xue-Jiao Chen, Chun-Ling Shen, Hong-Xin Zhang, Ling-Yun Tang, Shun-Yuan Lu, Wen-Ting Wu, Ying Kuang, Jian Fei, Zhu-Gang Wang
Published: 2026

Editorial Expression of Concern: Neural Development (2017) 12:4 https://doi.org/10.1186/s13064-017-0081-6

The Editor would like to alert the readers that concerns have been raised regarding the data presented in Fig. 4D of this article [1]. Specifically, the 8.5 h wt and Palladin-/- images appear to overlap with slight rotation and different magnification. Due to the age of the article, the raw data is not available. Additionally, the statement in the Figure legend that “The proportion of BrdU-stained progenitors is higher in Palladin-/- neural progenitors at each time point (right panel) compared with wt (left panel)” does not appear to be supported by the presented data.

Readers are therefore advised to interpret these results with caution.

Authors Juan Tan, Chun-Ling Shen, and Ling-Yun Tang agree to this Editorial Expression of Concern. Authors Xue-Jiao Chen, Hong-Xin Zhang, Shun-Yuan Lu, Wen-Ting Wu, Ying Kuang, Jian Fei, and Zhu-Gang Wang have not responded to any correspondence from the editor or publisher about this Editorial Expression of Concern.
