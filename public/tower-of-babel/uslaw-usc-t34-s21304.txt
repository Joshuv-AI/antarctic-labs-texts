34 U.S.C. § 21304 — Application
Citation: us:usc:34:21304
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section21304
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 213 (Rape Survivor Child Custody). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A State seeking an increase in the amount provided to the State under the covered formula grants shall include in the application of the State for each covered formula grant such information as the Attorney General may reasonably require, including information about the law described in section 21303 of this title.
