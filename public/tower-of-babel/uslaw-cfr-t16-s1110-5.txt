16 C.F.R. § 1110.5 — Finished products required to be certified.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1110.5
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1110.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1110 (Certificates Of Compliance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Finished products subject to a consumer product safety rule under the CPSA, or similar rule, ban, standard, or regulation under any other law enforced by the Commission, which are imported for consumption or warehousing, or are distributed in commerce, must be accompanied by a finished product certificate.
