7 C.F.R. § 301.48-7 — -7 Nonliability of the Department.
Citation: us:cfr:7:301.48-7
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-301.48-7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 301 (Domestic Quarantine Notices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The U.S. Department of Agriculture disclaims liability for any costs incident to inspections or compliance with the provisions of the quarantine and regulations in this subpart other than for the services of the inspector.
