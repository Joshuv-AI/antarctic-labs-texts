36 C.F.R. § 327.13 — Explosives, firearms, other weapons and fireworks.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 327 (Rules And Regulations Governing Public Use Of Water Resource Development Projects Administered By The Chief Of Engineers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The possession of loaded firearms, ammunition, loaded projectile firing devices, bows and arrows, crossbows, or other weapons is prohibited unless:
(1) In the possession of a Federal, state or local law enforcement officer;
(2) Being used for hunting or fishing as permitted under § 327.8, with devices being unloaded when transported to, from or between hunting and fishing sites;
(3) Being used at authorized shooting ranges; or
(4) Written permission has been received from the District Commander.
(b) Possession of explosives or explosive devices of any kind, including fireworks or other pyrotechnics, is prohibited unless written permission has been received from the District Commander.
