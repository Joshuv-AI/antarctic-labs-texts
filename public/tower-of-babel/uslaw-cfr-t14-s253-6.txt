14 C.F.R. § 253.6 — Explanation of incorporated terms.
Citation: us:cfr:14:253.6
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-253.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 253 (Notice Of Terms Of Contract Of Carriage). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each air carrier shall ensure that any passenger can obtain from any location where its tickets are sold within the United States a concise and immediate explanation of any terms incorporated by reference, concerning the subjects listed in § 253.5(b).
