33 U.S.C. § 10 — Waters in Louisiana Purchase as public highways
Citation: us:usc:33:10
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section10
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 1 (Navigable Waters Generally); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All the navigable rivers and waters in the former Territories of Orleans and Louisiana shall be and forever remain public highways.
