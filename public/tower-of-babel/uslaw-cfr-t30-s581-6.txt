30 C.F.R. § 581.6 — Appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:30:581.6
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-581.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 581 (Leasing Of Minerals Other Than Oil, Gas, And Sulphur In The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any party adversely affected by a decision of a BOEM official made pursuant to the provisions of this part shall have the right of appeal pursuant to 30 CFR part 590, except as provided otherwise in § 581.21 of this part.
