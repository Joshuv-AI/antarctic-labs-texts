26 C.F.R. § 48.4073-2 — -2 Exemption of tires with internal wire fastening.
Citation: us:cfr:26:48.4073-2
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-48.4073-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 48 (Manufacturers And Retailers Excise Taxes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The tax does not apply to sales of tires of any size or dimension manufactured from extruded tiring that is fastened or held together by means of internal wire or other metallic material.
