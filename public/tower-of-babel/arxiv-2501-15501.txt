Non-perturbative corrections in the semi-classical limit of double-scaled SYK
Kazumi Okuyama

Abstract
We study the disk partition function of double-scaled SYK model (DSSYK) in the small λ \lambda limit, where λ = − log ⁡ q \lambda=-\log q is the coupling of DSSYK. We find that the partition function receives non-perturbative corrections in λ \lambda , which can be resummed by the cubic power of the Dedekind eta function in a certain low temperature limit. We also discuss a possible bulk interpretation of our findings.

2
Disk partition function of DSSYK

In this section, we review the exact result of the disk partition function
of DSSYK
[
1
]
.
The SYK model is defined by the
p
-body Hamiltonian for
the
N
Majorana fermions
\psi_{i}~(i=1,\cdots,N)

[
21
,
22
,
23

\displaystyle H=\mathrm{i}^{p/2}\sum_{1\leq i_{1}<\cdots<i_{p}\leq N}J_{i_{1}\cdots i_{p}}\psi_{i_{1}}\cdots\psi_{i_{p}},

(1)

with the random coupling
J_{i_{1}\cdots i_{p}}
.
This coupling is assumed to be Gaussian random with zero mean
and the variance is given by

\displaystyle\Bigl\langle J_{i_{1}\cdots i_{p}}^{2}\Bigr\rangle=\frac{\mathcal{J}^{2}}{\lambda\binom{N}{p}}.

(2)

DSSYK is defined by taking the limit

\displaystyle p,N\to\infty\quad\text{with}\quad\lambda=\frac{2p^{2}}{N}:~\text{fixed}.

(3)

In this scaling limit, the computation of the moment
\langle\tr H^{k}\rangle

boils down to the counting problem of the intersection numbers of the
chord diagrams.
As shown in
[
1
]
,
this counting problem can be exactly solved by introducing the
transfer matrix
T=a+a^{\dagger}

acting on the chord Hilbert space, where
a,a^{\dagger}

are the
q
-deformed oscillators with
q=e^{-\lambda}
.
Using this technique,
we can write down the exact form of the disk partition function of DSSYK

\displaystyle Z(\beta)

\displaystyle=\int_{0}^{\pi}\frac{d\theta}{2\pi}\mu(\theta)e^{-\beta E(\theta)},

(4)

where
E(\theta)
is the eigenvalue of the transfer matrix

\displaystyle E(\theta)=-E_{0}\cos\theta,\quad E_{0}=\frac{2\mathcal{J}}{\sqrt{\lambda(1-q)}},

(5)

and the measure factor
\mu(\theta)
is given by

1

\displaystyle(a;q)_{\infty}=\prod_{k=0}^{\infty}(1-aq^{k}),\quad(a_{1},\cdots,a_{s};q)_{\infty}=\prod_{i=1}^{s}(a_{i};q)_{\infty},\quad(e^{\pm 2\mathrm{i}\theta};q)_{\infty}=(e^{2\mathrm{i}\theta},e^{-2\mathrm{i}\theta};q)_{\infty}.

(6)

\displaystyle\mu(\theta)=(q,e^{\pm 2\mathrm{i}\theta};q)_{\infty}.

(7)

4
Discussion

In this paper, we have studied the semi-classical
\lambda\to 0
limit
of the disk partition function of DSSYK.
We found that the non-perturbative corrections in
\lambda

can be resummed by the cubic power of the Dedekind eta function
in the low energy limit (
37
)
and the low temperature limit (
46
).
In this concluding section, we discuss a possible bulk interpretation
of our result (
37
).

We have seen in (
33
) that
in the low energy, small
\lambda
limit,
the partition function of DSSYK reduces to that of JT gravity with negative
cosmological constant.
As discussed in
[
27
]
,
the partition function
of JT gravity
depends on the boundary value
of the dilaton
\Phi

\displaystyle\Phi\big|_{\partial\mathcal{M}}=\frac{\gamma}{\varepsilon}\quad(\varepsilon\to 0),

(49)

where
\mathcal{M}
is the bulk spacetime and
\partial\mathcal{M}
is the
boundary of the asymptotic
AdS_{2}
part of
\mathcal{M}

at
z=\varepsilon
in the Poincaré coordinate.
Note that
\gamma
plays the role of the coupling of Schwarzian mode.
Including the
\gamma
-dependence, the disk partition function of
JT gravity becomes

\displaystyle Z_{\text{JT}}(\beta_{\text{JT}})\propto\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\beta_{\text{JT}}^{3}}}e^{\frac{2\pi^{2}\gamma}{\beta_{\text{JT}}}}.

(50)

Here the proportionality constant is regularization dependent
[
26
,
27
]
.
For our purpose, it is convenient choose this constant as

\displaystyle Z_{\text{JT}}(\beta_{\text{JT}},\gamma)=\frac{\sqrt{\gamma}}{\sqrt{2\pi\beta_{\text{JT}}^{3}}}e^{\frac{2\pi^{2}\gamma}{\beta_{\text{JT}}}}.

(51)

Then, our result (
37
) of the low energy limit of

Z(\beta)
is written as

\displaystyle Z(\beta)=2\pi\lambda^{2}\mathcal{C}e^{\beta E_{0}}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}(-1)^{j}\tilde{q}^{\frac{\gamma_{j}}{8}}Z_{\text{JT}}(\beta_{\text{JT}},\gamma_{j}),

(52)

with
\gamma_{j}
being

\displaystyle\gamma_{j}=(2j+1)^{2}.

(53)

This expression (
52
)
suggests that DSSYK is holographically dual to a
superposition of the boundary conditions of the dilaton
in JT gravity with negative cosmological constant,
at least at low energy in the semi-classical limit.
This is formally realized by inserting the
\delta
-function

\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}(-1)^{j}\tilde{q}^{\frac{\gamma_{j}}{8}}\delta\Bigl(\Phi|_{\partial\mathcal{M}}-\frac{\gamma_{j}}{\varepsilon}\Bigr)

(54)

into the JT gravity path integral.
It is not clear to us how this picture is related to the sine dilaton gravity.
In
[
30
]
, the low temperature limit of DSSYK is
discussed from the viewpoint of Schwarzian theory.
It would be interesting to study the relation between
the result in
[
30
]
and our (
52
).

We can improve (
52
) by including the contribution
from
\theta=\pi
. As we have seen in (
46
),
the contribution
from
\theta=\pi
is the analytic continuation
\beta\to-\beta

of the contribution from
\theta=0
