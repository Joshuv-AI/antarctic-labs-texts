12 C.F.R. § 380.61 — Appointment of receiver and trustee for covered broker or dealer.
Citation: us:cfr:12:380.61
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-380.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 380 (Orderly Liquidation Authority). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon the appointment of the Corporation as receiver for a covered broker or dealer, the Corporation shall appoint SIPC to act as trustee for the covered broker or dealer.
