Abstract

An
r
-colored Dyck path is a Dyck path with all
\mathbf{d}
-steps having one of
r
colors in
[r]=\{1,2,\dots,r\}
. In this paper, we consider several statistics on the set
\mathcal{A}_{n,0}^{(r)}
of
r
-colored Dyck paths of length
2n
with no two consecutive
\mathbf{d}
-steps having the same colors. Precisely, the paper studies the statistics “number of points” at level
\ell
, “number of
\mathbf{u}
-steps” at level
\ell+1
, “number of peaks” at level
\ell+1
and “number of
\mathbf{udu}
-steps” on the set
\mathcal{A}_{n,0}^{(r)}
. The counting formulas of the first three statistics are established by Riordan arrays related to
S(a,b;x)
, the weighted generating function of
(a,b)
-Schröder paths. By a useful and surprising relations satisfied by
S(a,b;x)
, several identities related to these counting formulas are also described.

Keywords
: Dyck path; Riordan array;
(a,b)
-Schröder path;
(a,b)
-Schröder number.

A

r
-colored Dyck path
is a Dyck path with all
\mathbf{d}
-steps having one of

[r]=\{1,2,\dots,r\}

A

(0,0)
to
(n,0)
in the first quadrant of the
XY
-plane and consists of up-steps

\mathbf{d}=(1,-1)

\mathbf{d}=(1,-1)

An
(a,b)
-Dyck path

\mathbf{u}
-steps weighted by
1
,
\mathbf{d}
-steps in peaks (called
\mathbf{ud}
-steps) weighted by

-steps weighted by

. An
(a,b)
-Motzkin path

\mathbf{h}
-steps weighted by

\mathbf{d}
-steps weighted by

The

. The weight of a set

(1.1)

\displaystyle C_{n}(a,b)\hskip-6.25963pt

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\hskip-6.25963pt\sum_{k=1}^{n}\frac{1}{n}\binom{n}{k-1}\binom{n}{k}a^{k}b^{n-k},

\displaystyle M_{n}(a,b)\hskip-6.25963pt

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\hskip-6.25963pt\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{2k}C_{k}a^{n-2k}b^{k},

(1.2)

\displaystyle S_{n}(a,b)\hskip-6.25963pt

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\hskip-6.25963pt\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n+k}{2k}C_{k}a^{n-k}b^{k},

\displaystyle C(a,b;x)\hskip-6.25963pt

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\hskip-6.25963pt\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}C_{n}(a,b)x^{n}=\frac{1-(a-b)x-\sqrt{(1-(a-b)x)^{2}-4bx}}{2bx},

\displaystyle M(a,b;x)\hskip-6.25963pt

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\hskip-6.25963pt\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}M_{n}(a,b)x^{n}=\frac{1-ax-\sqrt{(1-ax)^{2}-4bx^{2}}}{2bx^{2}},

\displaystyle S(a,b;x)\hskip-6.25963pt

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\hskip-6.25963pt\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}S_{n}(a,b)x^{n}=\frac{1-ax-\sqrt{(1-ax)^{2}-4bx}}{2bx},

\displaystyle C(a,b;x)\hskip-6.25963pt

\displaystyle\hskip-6.25963pt1+(a-b)xC(a,b;x)+bxC(a,b;x)^{2},

\displaystyle M(a,b;x)\hskip-6.25963pt

\displaystyle\hskip-6.25963pt1+axM(a,b;x)+bx^{2}M(a,b;x)^{2},

(1.4)

\displaystyle S(a,b;x)\hskip-6.25963pt

2.
r
-Colored Dyck paths

An
r
-colored Dyck path is a Dyck path with all
\mathbf{d}
-steps having one of
r
colors in
[r]=\{1,2,\dots,r\}
.
Let
\mathcal{A}_{n,k}^{(r)}
denote the set of
r
-colored Dyck paths
\mathbf{P}
of length
2n
such that
\mathbf{P}
exactly has
k

\mathbf{dd}
-steps with the same color. Set
\mathcal{A}_{k}^{(r)}=\bigcup_{n\geq k}\mathcal{A}_{n,k}^{(r)}
. Let
A_{n,k}^{(r)}
be the cardinality of
\mathcal{A}_{n,k}^{(r)}
and
A_{r}(x,y)
be the bivariate generating function of
A_{n,k}^{(r)}
, i.e.,

A_{r}(x,y)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}A_{n}^{(r)}(y)x^{n}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{k=0}^{n}A_{n,k}^{(r)}y^{k}x^{n}.

Denote by
A_{r,i}(x,y)
to be the bivariate generating function of the number of paths
\mathbf{P}\in\mathcal{A}_{n,k}^{(r)}
according to
n
and
k
such that the last return step of
\mathbf{P}
is colored by
i
. Such nonempty paths
\mathbf{P}
can be written as
\mathbf{P}=\mathbf{P}_{1}\mathbf{u}\mathbf{P}_{2}\mathbf{d}_{i}
, according to the last return decomposition, where
\mathbf{P}_{1}\in\mathcal{A}_{n_{1},k_{1}}^{(r)},\mathbf{P}_{2}\in\mathcal{A}_{n_{2},k_{2}}^{(r)}
for some nonnegative integers
n_{1},n_{2},k_{1}
and
k_{2}
such that
n_{1}+n_{2}=n-1
and
k_{1}+k_{2}=k-1\ (k_{1}+k_{2}=k)
if the color of the last return step of
\mathbf{P}_{2}
is (not)
i
, and
\mathbf{d}_{i}
denotes the last return step of
\mathbf{P}
with the color
i
. Moreover, once the last return step of
\mathbf{P}_{2}
is colored by
i
, it contributes one additional
\mathbf{dd}
-step with the same color to
\mathbf{P}
. Then we deduce the following relations:

\displaystyle A_{r}(x,y)\hskip-7.11317pt

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\hskip-7.11317pt1+\sum_{i=1}^{r}A_{r,i}(x,y),

\displaystyle A_{r,i}(x,y)\hskip-7.11317pt

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\hskip-7.11317ptx\big(A_{r}(x,y)+(y-1)A_{r,i}(x,y)\big)A_{r}(x,y).

These produce a recursive relation for
A_{r}(x,y)
, namely,

\displaystyle A_{r}(x,y)\hskip-7.11317pt

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\hskip-7.11317pt1+x(1-y)A_{r}(x,y)+x(y+r-1)A_{r}(x,y)^{2}.

By solving it, we have

\displaystyle A_{r}(x,y)\hskip-7.11317pt

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\hskip-7.11317pt\frac{1-(1-y)x-\sqrt{(1-(1-y)x)^{2}-4(y+r-1)x}}{2(y+r-1)x},

which generates that
A_{0}^{(r)}(y)=1
and

\displaystyle A_{n}^{(r)}(y)\hskip-7.11317pt

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\hskip-7.11317pt\sum_{k=0}^{n}A_{n,k}^{(r)}y^{k}=C_{n}(r,y+r-1)=S_{n}(1-y,y+r-1)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\hskip-7.11317pt\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}\frac{1}{n}\binom{n}{\ell-1}\binom{n}{\ell}r^{\ell}(y+r-1)^{n-\ell}

for
n\geq 1
. Taking the coefficient of
y^{k}
, we obtain the following result.

Theorem 2.1
.

The set of
r
-colored Dyck paths of length
2n
with exact
k

\mathbf{dd}
-steps having the same color is counted by

(2.1)

\displaystyle A_{n,k}^{(r)}\hskip-7.11317pt

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\hskip-7.11317pt\sum_{\ell=1}^{n-k}\frac{1}{n}\binom{n}{\ell-1}\binom{n}{\ell}\binom{n-\ell}{k}r^{\ell}(r-1)^{n-\ell-k}.

In the
k=0
case
A_{n,0}^{(r)}=S_{n}(1,r-1)
counts the number of
r
-colored Dyck paths of length
2n
with no
\mathbf{dd}
-steps having the same color. More precisely, the number of
2
-colored Dyck paths of length
2n
with no
\mathbf{dd}
-steps having the same color is enumerated by the large Schröder number
S_{n}

[
45
]
.

The formula (
2.1
) has a direct combinatorial interpretation as follows. It is well known that the Narayana number
\frac{1}{n}\binom{n}{\ell-1}\binom{n}{\ell}
counts the Dyck paths
\mathbf{P}
of length
2n
with exact
\ell
peaks. If one colors the
\ell

\mathbf{d}
-steps in peaks by one of the
r
colors, there are
r^{\ell}
ways. Then one has
\binom{n-\ell}{k}
ways to choose
k

\mathbf{d}
-steps from the left
n-\ell

\mathbf{d}
-steps such that each of the
k

\mathbf{d}
-steps has the same color as the
\mathbf{d}
-step right ahead of it. Finally, each of the rest
n-\ell-k

\mathbf{d}
-steps has different colors as the
\mathbf{d}
-step immediately ahead of it, there are
(r-1)^{n-\ell-k}
ways to do this. In conclusion, we derive (
2.1
).

The Lagrange inversion formula

\displaystyle[x^{n}]\Psi(f(x))\hskip-6.25963pt

for all

Lemma 2.2
.

\displaystyle Z_{m}(a,b;x)\hskip-7.11317pt

Corollary 2.3
.

For any integers

and any parameters

\displaystyle S_{n}\big(-a,\frac{b^{2^{m+1}}}{a^{2^{m+1}-1}}\big)\hskip-6.25963pt

, one has

\displaystyle\big(rS_{r}(x)^{2},\ x(r-1)S_{r}(x)^{2}\big)\frac{1}{(1+x)^{m+1}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\hskip-6.25963ptrS_{r}(x)^{2}\frac{1}{(1+x(r-1)S_{r}(x)^{2})^{m+1}}=\frac{rS_{r}(x)^{1-m}}{(1-x)^{m+1}}.

By using the Lagrange inversion formula to
S_{r}(x)=1+xS_{r}(x)+x(r-1)S_{r}(x)^{2}
, that is to set
\alpha=S_{r}(x)-1=x(\alpha+1)((r-1)(\alpha+1)+1)
, for
n\geq\ell\geq 0
, one has

\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=0}^{n}(-1)^{\ell}\binom{m+\ell}{\ell}p_{n,\ell}^{(r)}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\hskip-6.25963pt[x^{n}]\left\{\big(rS_{r}(x)^{2},\ x(r-1)S_{r}(x)^{2}\big)\frac{1}{(1+x)^{m+1}}\right\}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\hskip-6.25963pt[x^{n}]\frac{rS_{r}(x)^{1-m}}{(1-x)^{m+1}}=\sum_{\ell=0}^{n}\binom{m+\ell}{\ell}[x^{n-\ell}]rS_{r}(x)^{1-m}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\hskip-6.25963pt\sum_{\ell=0}^{n}\sum_{j=0}^{n-\ell}\binom{m+\ell}{\ell}\binom{n-\ell}{j}\frac{(1-m)r}{n-\ell+j-m+1}\binom{n-\ell+j-m+1}{n-\ell}(r-1)^{j},

as desired.

Specially, one could verify easily the results when
m=0
and
m=1
.
\Box

Theorem 6.1
.

For any integers
n>\ell\geq 0
and
r\geq 2
, we deduce

(6.3)

\displaystyle T_{n,\ell}^{(r)}=\binom{n-1}{\ell}T_{n-\ell,0}^{(r)}r^{\ell}

with
T_{0,0}^{(r)}=1
, where

(6.4)

\displaystyle T_{n,0}^{(r)}=\sum_{j=[\frac{n}{2}]}^{n-1}\binom{n-1}{j}\frac{1}{j+1}\binom{j+1}{n-j}r^{n-j}(r-1)^{j},\ (n\geq 1),

is determinated by

(6.5)

\displaystyle T_{0}(x)=\sum_{n\geq 0}T_{n,0}^{(r)}x^{n}=\frac{1+(r-1)x-\sqrt{(1+(r-1)x)^{2}-4(r-1)(1+rx)x}}{2(r-1)x},

which satisfies the recurrence
T_{0}(x)=1+rx-(r-1)xT_{0}(x)+(r-1)xT_{0}(x)^{2}
.

Proof.
First we consider
\mathcal{T}_{n,0}^{(r)}
, the subset of
r
-colored Dyck paths of length
2n
with no
\mathbf{udu}
-steps (called
\mathbf{udu}
-avoiding).
For any
\mathbf{D}\in\mathcal{T}_{n,0}^{(r)}
,
\mathbf{D}
has one of the following three forms

\varepsilon,\ \mathbf{ud}_{j}\ \mbox{or}\ \mathbf{u}\mathbf{D}_{1}\mathbf{d}_{j}\mathbf{D}_{2},

where both
\mathbf{D}_{1}(\neq\varepsilon)
and
\mathbf{D}_{2}
are
\mathbf{udu}
-avoiding,
\mathbf{d}_{j}
has color
j
for some
j\in[r]
, so
\mathbf{d}_{j}
has
r
choices for colors in the second case and has
r-1
choices in the third case since
\mathbf{D}_{1}
is nonempty. Let
T_{0}(x)=\sum_{n\geq 0}T_{n,0}^{(r)}x^{n}
. Then

\displaystyle T_{0}(x)=1+rx+(r-1)x(T_{0}(x)-1)T_{0}(x).

By solving it, one obtains (
6.5
).

By using the Lagrange inversion formula to
T_{0}(x)=1+rx+(r-1)x(T_{0}(x)-1)T_{0}(x)
, that is to set
\beta=T_{0}(x)-1=x(r+(r-1)\beta(\beta+1))
, for
n>0
, one has

\displaystyle T_{n,0}^{(r)}\hskip-6.25963pt

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\hskip-6.25963pt[x^{n}](T_{0}(x)-1)=\frac{1}{n}[\beta^{n-1}]\big(r+(r-1)\beta(\beta+1)\big)^{n}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\hskip-6.25963pt\sum_{j=[\frac{n}{2}]}^{n-1}\binom{n-1}{j}\frac{1}{j+1}\binom{j+1}{n-j}r^{n-j}(r-1)^{j},

which leads to (
6.4
).

For any
\mathbf{D}\in\mathcal{T}_{n,\ell}^{(r)}
, we can get a
\mathbf{D}^{*}\in\mathcal{T}_{n-\ell,0}^{(r)}
by deleting the
\ell

\mathbf{d}_{j}\mathbf{u}
-steps once they form
\mathbf{udu}
-steps, where
\mathbf{d}_{j}
has color
j
for some
j\in[r]
. Conversely, for any
\mathbf{D}^{*}\in\mathcal{T}_{n-\ell,0}^{(r)}
there are exact
n-\ell
endpoints of
\mathbf{u}
-steps, one can repeatedly select
\ell
endpoints and insert
\ell

\mathbf{d}_{j}\mathbf{u}
-steps to form
\ell

\mathbf{u}\mathbf{d}\mathbf{u}
-steps. Since there are
\binom{n-1}{\ell}
ways to select repeatedly
\ell

n-\ell

\mathbf{d}_{j}
has one of
r
colors, then we obtain that

\displaystyle T_{n,\ell}^{(r)}=\binom{n-1}{\ell}T_{n-\ell,0}^{(r)}r^{\ell},

which proves (
6.3
).

Theorem 6.2
.

For any integers
n\geq 1
and
r\geq 2
, we deduce

\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}\ell T_{n+1,\ell}^{(r)}\hskip-6.25963pt

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\hskip-6.25963ptrnS_{n}^{(r)}.

Proof.
Let
T_{r}(x,y)=1+\sum_{n\geq 1}\big(\sum_{\ell=0}^{n-1}T_{n,\ell}^{(r)}y^{\ell}\big)x^{n}=1+\sum_{n\geq 0}\big(\sum_{\ell=0}^{n}T_{n+1,\ell}^{(r)}y^{\ell}\big)x^{n+1}
, similar to obtaining the relation for
T_{0}(x)
in Theorem 6.1, one has

\displaystyle T_{r}(x,y)=1+rx+rxy(T_{r}(x,y)-1)+(r-1)x(T_{r}(x,y)-1)T_{r}(x,y),

which, when
y=0
or
1
, generates respectively that
T_{r}(x,0)=T_{0}(x)
and
T_{r}(x,1)=S_{r}(x)
.

Note that

\displaystyle\frac{\partial T_{r}(x,y)}{\partial y}\Big|_{y=1}=\frac{rx(T_{r}(x,1)-1)}{1-x-2(r-1)xT_{r}(x,1)}=rx^{2}\frac{\partial T_{r}(x,1)}{\partial x}.

Hence, we deduce

\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}\ell T_{n+1,\ell}^{(r)}\hskip-6.25963pt

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\hskip-6.25963pt[x^{n+1}]\frac{\partial T_{r}(x,y)}{\partial y}\Big|_{y=1}=[x^{n+1}]rx^{2}\frac{\partial T_{r}(x,1)}{\partial x}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\hskip-6.25963pt[x^{n+1}]rx^{2}\frac{\partial S_{r}(x)}{\partial x}=rnS_{n}^{(r)},

as desired.
\Box
