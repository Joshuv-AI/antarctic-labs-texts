24 C.F.R. § 886.305 — Disclosure and verification of Social Security and Employer Identification Numbers by owners.
Citation: us:cfr:24:886.305
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary For Housing-Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development (Section 8 Housing Assistance Programs, Section 202 Direct Loan Program, Section 202 Supportive Housing For The Elderly Program And Section 811 Supportive Housing For Persons With Disabilities Program)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-886.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 886 (Section 8 Housing Assistance Payments Program—Special Allocations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To be eligible to become an owner of housing assisted under this subpart, the owner must meet the disclosure and verification requirements for Social Security and Employer Identification Numbers, as provided by part 5, subpart B, of this title.
