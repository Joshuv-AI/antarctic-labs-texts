7 C.F.R. § 1580.505 — Appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1580.505
Creator: Foreign Agricultural Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1580.505
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1580 (Trade Adjustment Assistance For Farmers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A producer adversely affected by a determination with respect to their application for trade adjustment assistance under § 1580.301 of this part or with respect to the receipt of technical assistance or payments under § 1580.302 of this part may file a notice of appeal within 30 days of the date that the notification of the adverse determination was sent.
(b) A producer may not seek judicial review of any adverse decision under this paragraph without receiving a final determination pursuant to this paragraph.
