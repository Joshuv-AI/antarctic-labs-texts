34 U.S.C. § 41301 — Report to Congress on sexual exploitation of children
Citation: us:usc:34:41301
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section41301
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 413 (Crime Reports And Statistics). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Beginning one hundred and twenty days after May 21, 1984, and every year thereafter, the Attorney General shall report to the Congress on prosecutions, convictions, and forfeitures under chapter 110 of title 18.
