42 U.S.C. § 15206 — National Medal of Valor Office
Citation: us:usc:42:15206
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section15206
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 145 (Public Safety Officer Medal Of Valor And Tributes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is established within the Department of Justice a National Medal of Valor Office. The Office shall provide staff support to the Board to establish criteria and procedures for the submission of recommendations of nominees for the Medal of Valor and for the final design of the Medal of Valor.
