40 C.F.R. § 29.13 — May the Administrator waive any provision of these regulations?
Citation: us:cfr:40:29.13
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-29.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 29 (Intergovernmental Review Of Environmental Protection Agency Programs And Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In an emergency, the Administrator may waive any provision of these regulations.
