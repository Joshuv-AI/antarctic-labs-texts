Flipped S ​ U ​ ( 5 ) SU(5) GUT with conformal gravity from a single supermultiplet
David Chester 1, Alessio Marrani 2, Michael Rios 3 and Klee Irwin 1

Abstract
The Grassmann envelope is used to find the 𝒩 = 1 \mathcal{N}=1 ‘superquasiconformal’ algebra in D = 10 + 1 D=10+1 . The adjoint representation of this algebra is found to contain 𝔰 ​ 𝔲 2 , 2 ⊕ 𝔲 1 ⊕ 𝔰 ​ 𝔲 5 \mathfrak{su}_{2,2}\oplus\mathfrak{u}_{1}\oplus\mathfrak{su}_{5} as a submaximal subalgebra, giving a spectrum of conformal gravity with flipped S ​ U 5 × U 1 SU_{5}\times U_{1} GUT. Combining Yang-Mills theory with MacDowell-Mansouri gravity over the conformal group is found to recover the Einstein-Hilbert action with a cosmological constant. An action for the theory is presented, which contains the gauge bosons of gravity and GUT, three generations of fermions in an efficient manner, and a new Higgs sector. By using the superalgebra for the entire multiplet, the theory gauges a non-supersymmetric subalgebra without introducing superpartners.
