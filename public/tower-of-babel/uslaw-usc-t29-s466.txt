29 U.S.C. § 466 — Additional rights and remedies; exclusive jurisdiction of district court; res judicata
Citation: us:usc:29:466
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section466
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 11 (Labor-Management Reporting And Disclosure Procedure); Subchapter IV (Trusteeships). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The rights and remedies provided by this subchapter shall be in addition to any and all other rights and remedies at law or in equity: Provided, That upon the filing of a complaint by the Secretary the jurisdiction of the district court over such trusteeship shall be exclusive and the final judgment shall be res judicata.
