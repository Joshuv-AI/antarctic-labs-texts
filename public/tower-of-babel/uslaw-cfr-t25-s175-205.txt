25 C.F.R. § 175.205 — When are BIA rates and fees reviewed?
Citation: us:cfr:25:175.205
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-175.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 175 (Electric Power Utilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We review our rates and fees at least annually to:
(a) Determine if our financial requirements are being met to ensure the reliable operation of the utility serving you; and
(b) Determine if revenues are sufficient to meet the statutory requirements.
