45 C.F.R. § 2506.31 — May I ask the Corporation to waive an overpayment that otherwise would be collected by offsetting my salary as a Federal employee?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2506.31
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2506.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2506 (Collection Of Debts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, the regulations in this subpart do not preclude you from requesting waiver of an overpayment under 5 U.S.C. 5584 or 8346(b), 10 U.S.C. 2774, 32 U.S.C. 716, or other statutory provisions pertaining to the particular debts being collected.
