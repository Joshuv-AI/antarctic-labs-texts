25 C.F.R. § 502.23 — Facility license.
Citation: us:cfr:25:502.23
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-502.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 502 (Definitions Of This Chapter). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Facility license means a separate license issued by a tribe to each place, facility, or location on Indian lands where the tribe elects to allow class II or III gaming.
