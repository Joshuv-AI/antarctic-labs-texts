40 C.F.R. § 792.61 — Equipment design.
Citation: us:cfr:40:792.61
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-792.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 792 (Good Laboratory Practice Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Equipment used in the generation, measurement, or assessment of data and equipment used for facility environmental control shall be of appropriate design and adequate capacity to function according to the protocol and shall be suitably located for operation, inspection, cleaning, and maintenance.
