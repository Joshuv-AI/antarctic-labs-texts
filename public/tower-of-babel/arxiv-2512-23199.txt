Introduction

Throughout the paper,
G
stands for a graph on
n
vertices without any loops and multiple-edges. As usual, we denote the vertex set and the edge set of
G
by
V(G)
and
E(G)
, respectively. Two adjacent vertices
v_{i}
and
v_{j}
are represented as
v_{i}\sim v_{j}
, or
i\sim j
. The degree of a vertex
v_{i}
in
G
is denoted as
d_{G}(v_{i})/d(v_{i}).
The vertex connectivity
\kappa
of
G
is the cardinality of a minimum vertex cut set. The vertex
k
-partiteness of
G
,
v_{k}(G)
is the minimum number of vertices whose removal results in a
k
-partite graph. Topological indices, a well-known class of graph invariants, are numerical values derived from the structure of a graph. In molecular graph theory, these indices are widely used as they often reflect the physicochemical properties of the corresponding molecules. For some recent studies on topological indices, see
[
4
,
6
]
. The atom-bond sum connectivity index, commonly known as the
\mathcal{ABS}
index, is a recently introduced topological invariant that has quickly attracted significant attention. It is defined as

\mathcal{ABS}(G)=\sum\limits_{uv\in E(G)}\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{d(u)+d(v)}}
.

The chemical relevance of the
\mathcal{ABS}
index is explored in
[
2
]
, while its extremal values and bounds are investigated in
[
7
,
8
]
.

Recently, in the review paper
[
1
]
, Ali et al. listed known bounds and extremal results related to the
\mathcal{ABS}
index. They also stated several new extremal results that follow easily from existing general findings. Furthermore, the authors posed several open problems concerning extremal graphs with respect to the
\mathcal{ABS}
index. These problems involve characterizing graphs that attain the maximum
\mathcal{ABS}
index within specific graph classes, including: (i) connected graphs with
n
vertices and
p
cut-vertices; (ii) connected graphs of order
n
with vertex
k
-partiteness
v_{k}(G)=r
; and (iii) connected bipartite graphs of order
n
with a fixed vertex connectivity
\kappa
. Motivated by these works, in this paper, we provide complete solutions to all of these problems.

.

\mathcal{ABS}(G)-\mathcal{ABS}(G^{\prime})<

\displaystyle+\sum_{\underset{y^{\prime}\notin V(H_{2})}{y^{\prime}:y^{\prime}\sim y}}\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{d_{G}(y^{\prime})+d_{G}(y)}}-\sum_{u:u\in V(H_{1})\backslash\{x,u_{1}\}}\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{d_{G^{\prime}}(u)+d_{G^{\prime}}(z)}}

Thus,
\mathcal{ABS}(G)<\mathcal{ABS}(G^{\prime})

\displaystyle\displaystyle\sum_{w:w\in\{x,z\}}\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{d_{G}(w)+d_{G}(y)}}-\sum_{u:u\in V(H_{1})\backslash\{x\}}\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{d_{G^{\prime}}(u)+d_{G^{\prime}}(z)}}

\displaystyle+\sum_{\underset{y^{\prime}\notin V(H_{2})}{y^{\prime}:y^{\prime}\sim y}}\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{d_{G}(y^{\prime})+d_{G}(y)}}-\sum_{\underset{y^{\prime}\notin V(H_{2})}{y^{\prime}:y^{\prime}\sim y}}\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{d_{G^{\prime}}(y^{\prime})+d_{G^{\prime}}(y)}}

(2.1)

Theorem 2.3
.

Let

Then
\mathcal{ABS}(G)\leq\mathcal{ABS}(\mathbb{K}_{n}^{p})
,

Proof.

Suppose
G

\mathcal{ABS}
index. If

, then there is nothing to prove. Otherwise,
G\ncong P_{n}
, and by Lemma
2.2
,

(with a length of at least 1) to a vertex

of the complete graph

(with vertex labels

, where

\mathcal{ABS}
index and vertex
k
-partiteness in graphs

A complete multipartite graph with
k
-partition sets of order
n_{1},n_{2},\ldots,n_{k}
is denoted as
K_{n_{1},n_{2},\ldots,n_{k}}
.
The Tur
\acute{\text{a}}
n graph
T(n,k)
is the complete
k
-partite graph on
n
vertices given by
T(n,k)\cong K_{\underbrace{t,t,\dots,t}_{(k-s)},\underbrace{t+1,t+1,\dots,t+1}_{s}}
, where
n=kt+s
and
s\geq 0
.

We now show that the Tur
\acute{\text{a}}
n graph has maximum
\mathcal{ABS}
index among all complete
k
-partite graphs.

Theorem 3.1
.

Let
G
be a complete k-partite graph on n vertices. Then
\mathcal{ABS}(G)\leq\mathcal{ABS}(T(n,k))
. Equality holds if and only if
G\cong T(n,k)
.

Proof.

Let
\Gamma\cong K_{t_{1},t_{2},\dots,t_{k}}
be a complete
k
-partite graph with maximum
\mathcal{ABS}
index in the class of complete
k
-partite graphs. Suppose
|t_{i}-t_{j}|\geq 2
for some
1\leq i<j\leq k
. Then without loss of generality, we can assume that
t_{1}-t_{2}\geq 2
. Let
\Gamma^{\prime}=K_{t_{1}-1,t_{2}+1,\dots,t_{k}}
. By direct calculation,

\displaystyle\mathcal{ABS}(\Gamma)-\mathcal{ABS}(\Gamma^{\prime})

\displaystyle=t_{1}t_{2}\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{2n-(t_{1}+t_{2})}}-(t_{1}-1)(t_{2}+1)\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{2n-(t_{1}+t_{2})}}

\displaystyle+\sum\limits_{i=3}^{k}t_{1}t_{i}\left(\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{2n-t_{1}-t_{i}}}-\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{2n-t_{i}-t_{1}+1}}\right)

\displaystyle+\sum\limits_{i=3}^{k}t_{2}t_{i}\left(\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{2n-t_{2}-t_{i}}}-\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{2n-t_{2}-t_{i}-1}}\right)

\displaystyle+\sum_{i=3}^{k}t_{i}\left(\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{2n-t_{i}-t_{1}+1}}-\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{2n-t_{i}-t_{2}-1}}\right).

Since
t_{1}\geq t_{2}+2
,

\displaystyle\mathcal{ABS}(\Gamma)-\mathcal{ABS}(\Gamma^{\prime})

\displaystyle<\sum\limits_{i=3}^{k}t_{2}t_{i}\left(\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{2n-t_{1}-t_{i}}}-\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{2n-t_{i}-t_{1}+1}}\right.

\displaystyle\left.+\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{2n-t_{2}-t_{i}}}-\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{2n-t_{2}-t_{i}-1}}\right).

(3.1)

Consider
\zeta(x)=\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{x-t_{i}}}-\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{x-t_{i}+1}}
. Employing the first derivative test, we see that the function
\zeta(x)
is increasing. So,
\zeta(2n-t_{2}-1)\geq\zeta(2n-t_{1})
as
t_{1}\geq t_{2}+2
, and thus from inequality (
3
),
\mathcal{ABS}(\Gamma)<\mathcal{ABS}(\Gamma^{\prime})
.
This is a contradiction. Hence
\Gamma\cong T(n,k)
.
∎

\Gamma^{\prime}=K_{r}\vee K_{t_{1}-1,t_{2}+1,\dots,t_{k}}

\mathcal{ABS}(\Gamma)-\mathcal{ABS}(\Gamma^{\prime})=

\displaystyle~rt_{1}\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{2n-(t_{1}+1)}}+rt_{2}\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{2n-(t_{2}+1)}}+\sum\limits_{i=2}^{k}t_{1}t_{i}\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{2n-(t_{1}+t_{i})}}

\displaystyle+\sum\limits_{i=3}^{k}t_{2}t_{i}\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{2n-(t_{2}+t_{i})}}-r(t_{1}-1)\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{2n-t_{1}}}-r(t_{2}+1)\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{2(n-1)-t_{2}}}

\displaystyle-(t_{1}-1)(t_{2}+1)\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{2n-(t_{1}+t_{2})}}-\sum\limits_{i=3}^{k}(t_{1}-1)t_{i}\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{2n-(t_{1}+t_{i})+1}}

\displaystyle-\sum\limits_{i=3}^{k}(t_{2}+1)t_{i}\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{2n-(t_{2}+t_{i})-1}}.

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{i=3}^{k}t_{2}t_{i}\left(\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{2n-t_{1}-t_{i}}}-\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{2n-t_{i}-t_{1}+1}}+\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{2n-t_{2}-t_{i}}}-\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{2n-t_{2}-t_{i}-1}}\right)

\displaystyle-rt_{2}\left(\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{2n-t_{1}}}-\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{2n-t_{1}-1}}-\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{2n-1-t_{2}}}+\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{2n-t_{2}-2}}\right)

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{i=3}^{k}\big(t_{2}t_{i}(\zeta(2n-t_{1})-\zeta(2n-t_{2}-1))\big)-rt_{2}\left(\zeta_{1}(t_{1})-\zeta_{1}(t_{2}+1)\right),

where

Remark 4.11
.

Let
G
be a graph with maximum
\mathcal{ABS}
index in
\mathcal{BG}_{n.\kappa}
and let
S
be a minimum vertex cut set of
G
. If
G-S
is an empty graph, then
S
is a partition set of
G
and
G\cong K_{\kappa,n-\kappa}
(because addition of an edge increases
\mathcal{ABS}
index).

The main result of this section is presented below.

Theorem 4.12
.

Let
G\in\mathcal{BG}_{n,\kappa}
with
n\geq 7
.

(i)

For
\kappa\in\left\{\dfrac{n-2}{2},\dfrac{n-1}{2},\dfrac{n}{2}\right\}
,
\mathcal{ABS}(G)\leq\kappa(n-\kappa)\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{n}}
. Equality holds if and only if
G\cong K_{\kappa,n-\kappa}
.

(ii)

For
n
even and
1\leq k\leq\dfrac{n-4}{2}
,

\mathcal{ABS}(G)\leq\dfrac{n(n-2k-2)}{4}\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{n-1}}+\dfrac{nk}{2}\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{n}}+k\sqrt{1-\dfrac{4}{2k+n+2}}.

Equality holds if and only if
G\cong\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{\kappa}\left[\dfrac{n}{2},\dfrac{n-2k-2}{2}\right]
.

(iii)

For
n
odd and
1\leq k\leq\dfrac{n-3}{2}
,

\mathcal{ABS}(G)\leq\dfrac{(n-1)(n-2k-1)}{4}\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{n-1}}+\dfrac{(n-1)k}{2}\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{n}}+k\sqrt{1-\dfrac{4}{2k+n+1}}.

Equality holds if and only if
G\cong\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{\kappa}\left[\dfrac{n-1}{2},\dfrac{n-2k-1}{2}\right]
.

Proof.

Let
G\in\mathcal{BG}_{n,\kappa}
with maximum
\mathcal{ABS}
index and let
S
be a minimum vertex cut set of
G
. Let
\kappa\in\left\{\dfrac{n-1}{2},\dfrac{n}{2}\right\}
. If
G-S
has a non-trivial component, then by Theorem
4.6
,
G\cong\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{\kappa}[x,y]
with
x\geq 1
and
y\geq\kappa
. So,
|G|=x+y+\kappa+1>n
, a contradiction. Hence
G-S
is an empty graph, and so by Remark
4.11
,
G\cong\mathcal{K}_{\kappa,n-\kappa}
.

Let
\kappa=\dfrac{n-2}{2}
. Then from Remark
4.11
and by Theorems
4.6
and
4.10
, we get,
G\cong K_{\dfrac{n-2}{2},\dfrac{n+2}{2}}
or
\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{\kappa}\left[\dfrac{n-2}{2},1\right]
. Since
\mathcal{ABS}\left(K_{\dfrac{n-2}{2},\dfrac{n+2}{2}}\right)>\mathcal{ABS}\left(\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{\kappa}\left[\dfrac{n-2}{2},1\right]\right)
,
G\cong K_{\dfrac{n-2}{2},\dfrac{n+2}{2}}
.
Assume that
1\leq k\leq\dfrac{n-3}{2}
. If
n
is even, then from Remark
4.11
and by Theorems
4.6
and
4.10
,
G\cong K_{\kappa,n-\kappa}
or
G\cong\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{\kappa}\left[\dfrac{n}{2},\dfrac{n-2k-2}{2}\right]
.
Since
{\small\mathcal{ABS}\left(\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{\kappa}\left[\dfrac{n}{2},\dfrac{n-2k-2}{2}\right]\right)}\\
\geq\mathcal{ABS}\left(\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{\kappa}\left[n-k-2,1\right]\right)>\mathcal{ABS}\left(K_{\kappa,n-\kappa}\right)
,
G\cong\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{\kappa}\left[\dfrac{n}{2},\dfrac{n-2k-2}{2}\right]
. Otherwise
n
is odd. Similar to the case
n
is even, we get
G\cong\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{\kappa}\left[\dfrac{n-1}{2},\dfrac{n-2k-1}{2}\right]
.
∎
