Some estimates on stable minimal hypersurfaces in Euclidean space
Luen-Fai Tam

Abstract
. We derive some estimates for stable minimal hypersurfaces in ℝ n + 1 \mathbb{R}^{n+1} . The estimates are related to recent proofs of Bernstein theorems for complete stable minimal hypersurfaces in ℝ n + 1 \mathbb{R}^{n+1} for 3 ≤ n ≤ 5 3\leq n\leq 5 by Chodosh-Li [ 3 ] , Chodosh-Li-Minter-Stryker [ 4 ] and Mazet [ 6 ] . In particular, the estimates indicate that the methods in their proofs may not work for n = 6 n=6 , which is observed also by Antonelli-Xu [ 2 ] and Mazet [ 7 ] . The method of derivation in this work might also be applied to other problems.

2.
Basic facts

Recall the following, see
[
5
, Theorem 7.30]
. Let
(N,g)
be a Riemannian manifold. Consider the conformal metric
\widetilde{g}=e^{2f}g
. Let
\widetilde{\mathrm{Rm}},{\mathrm{Rm}}
be the curvature tensors of
\widetilde{g},g
respectively. Then

(2.1)

\widetilde{\mathrm{Rm}}=e^{2f}\left[\mathrm{Rm}-\nabla^{2}_{g}f\owedge g+(df\otimes df)\owedge g-\frac{1}{2}|df|^{2}_{g}g\owedge g\right].

Here
\owedge
be the Kulkarni-Nomizu product. Namely, for symmetric
(0,2)
tensors
S,T
on a manifold,

(2.2)

\begin{split}(S\owedge T)(X,Y,Z,W):=&S(X,W)T(Y,Z)+S(Y,Z)T(X,W)\\
&-S(X,Z)T(Y,W)-S(Y,W)T(X,Z)\end{split}

for tangent vectors
X,Y,Z,W
.

Let
G
be the Euclidean metric on
\mathbb{R}^{n+1}
. Let
s=\log r
and let

(2.3)

\overline{G}=e^{-2s}G=r^{-2}G

where
r
is the Euclidean distance to the origin.
Let
M^{n}\subset\mathbb{R}^{n+1}
be a hypersurface in
\mathbb{R}^{n+1}
with induced metric
g,\overline{g}
from
G,\overline{G}
respectively. Denote:

•

D,\overline{D}
, the covariant derivatives of
G,\overline{G}
;

•

\nabla,{\overline{\nabla}}
the covariant derivatives of
g,\overline{g}
.

.

Lemma 2.1
.

Let
{\overline{\mathrm{Rm}}}
be the curvature tensor of
\overline{G}
. Then

{\overline{\mathrm{Rm}}}=\frac{1}{2}\overline{G}\owedge\overline{G}-(ds\otimes ds)\owedge\overline{G}.

Proof.

Let
\mathrm{Rm}
be the curvature tensor of
G
, then

{\overline{\mathrm{Rm}}}=e^{-2s}\left[\mathrm{Rm}+D^{2}s\owedge G+(ds\otimes ds)\owedge G-\frac{1}{2}|ds|^{2}_{G}G\owedge G\right].

Now,
\mathrm{Rm}=0
,
D^{2}s=e^{-2s}G-2ds\otimes ds
,
|ds|^{2}_{G}=|Ds|^{2}_{G}=e^{-2s}
.
So

\begin{split}{\overline{\mathrm{Rm}}}=&e^{-2s}\left[\left(e^{-2s}G-2ds\otimes ds\right)\owedge G+(ds\otimes ds)\owedge G-\frac{1}{2}e^{-2s}G\owedge G\right]\\
=&\frac{1}{2}e^{-4s}G\owedge G-e^{-2s}(ds\otimes ds)\owedge G\\
=&\frac{1}{2}\overline{G}\owedge\overline{G}-(ds\otimes ds)\owedge\overline{G}.\end{split}

∎

Lemma 2.2
.

Let
M^{n}
be a hypersurface in
\mathbb{R}^{n+1}
with unit normal
\mathbf{n}
with respect to
G
, and let
\overline{\mathbf{n}}=e^{s}\mathbf{n}
be the unit normal with respect to
\overline{G}
. Let
\mathcal{K},\overline{\mathcal{K}}
be the second fundamental forms with respect to
G,\overline{G}
. Then

\mathcal{K}=e^{s}\left(\overline{\mathcal{K}}+\overline{\mathbf{n}}(s)\overline{g}\right).

In particular, if
H,\overline{H}
are the mean curvatures with respect to
G,\overline{G}
, then

H=e^{-s}(\overline{H}+n\overline{\mathbf{n}}(s)).

If
M
is minimal with respect to
G
, then
\overline{H}=-n\overline{\mathbf{n}}(s)=:-n\omega
.

Lemma 2.3
.

Suppose
M^{n}
is stably minimal in
(\mathbb{R}^{n+1},G)
, then for any
\psi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(M)
, we have

\int_{M}|{\overline{\nabla}}\psi|^{2}d\mu_{\overline{g}}\geq\int_{M}W\psi^{2}d\mu_{\overline{g}}

where

W=:|\overline{\mathcal{K}}|_{\overline{g}}^{2}-\frac{n^{2}+4n-4}{4}\omega^{2}-\left(\frac{n-2}{2}\right)^{2}

with
\omega^{2}\leq 1
.

Proof.

This follows from
[
4
]
and Lemma
2.2
. In fact, by
[
4
, Proposition 3.10]
,

\int_{M}|{\overline{\nabla}}\psi|^{2}d\mu_{\overline{g}}\geq\int_{M}\left[(r^{2}|\mathcal{K}|_{g}^{2}-\frac{n(n-2)}{2}+\left(\frac{n(n-2)}{2}-\frac{(n-2)^{2}}{4}\right)|\nabla r|^{2}_{g}\right]\psi^{2}d\mu_{\overline{g}}

By Lemma
2.2

|\mathcal{K}|^{2}_{g}=e^{-4s}|\mathcal{K}|^{2}_{\overline{g}}=e^{-2s}|\overline{\mathcal{K}}+\overline{\mathbf{n}}(s)\overline{g}|^{2}_{\overline{g}}=e^{-2s}(|\overline{\mathcal{K}}|^{2}_{\overline{g}}-\frac{1}{n}\overline{H}^{2})=e^{-2s}(|\overline{\mathcal{K}}|^{2}_{\overline{g}}-n\omega^{2}).

Also

|\nabla r|^{2}_{g}=|\nabla e^{s}|^{2}_{g}=|{\overline{\nabla}}s|^{2}_{\overline{g}}=1-(\overline{\mathbf{n}}(s))^{2}=1-\omega^{2}.

Here we have used the fact that
|\overline{D}s|^{2}=1
and
|\overline{D}s|^{2}=|{\overline{\nabla}}s|^{2}_{\overline{g}}+(\overline{\mathbf{n}}(s))^{2}
.
From these the result follows.
∎

Recall that in a Riemannian manifold, the bi-Ricci curvature is defined as:

(3.2)

\mathrm{BiRic}(e_{1},e_{2})=\text{\rm Ric}(e_{1},e_{1})+\text{\rm Ric}(e_{2},e_{2})-R(e_{1},e_{2},e_{2},e_{1})

for any orthonormal pair

.

Lemma 3.3
.

With same notation as in Lemma
3.4
, let
e_{1},\cdots,e_{n-1}
be orthonormal in
\Sigma
. Then

\overline{\text{\rm Ric}}^{M}({\vec{\nu}},{\vec{\nu}})=\overline{\mathrm{BiRic}}^{M}({\vec{\nu}},e_{1})-\text{\rm Ric}^{\Sigma}(e_{1},e_{1})+\sum_{j=2}^{n-1}(A_{11}A_{jj}-A_{1j}^{2})

Here
\overline{\mathrm{BiRic}}^{M}
is the bi-Ricci curvature of
M
,
{\vec{\nu}}
is a unit normal of
\Sigma
in
M
and
A
is the second fundamental form with respect to
{\vec{\nu}}
.

Proof.

\begin{split}\text{\rm Ric}^{\Sigma}(e_{1},e_{1})=&\sum_{j=2}^{n-1}R^{\Sigma}_{ijj1}\\
=&\sum_{j=2}^{n-1}{\overline{R}}^{M}_{1jj1}+\sum_{j=2}^{n-1}(A_{11}A_{jj}-A_{1j}^{2})\\
=&\overline{\text{\rm Ric}}^{M}(e_{1},e_{1})-{\overline{R}}^{M}(e_{1},{\vec{\nu}},{\vec{\nu}},e_{1})+\sum_{j=2}^{n-1}(A_{11}A_{jj}-A_{1j}^{2})\\
=&\overline{\text{\rm Ric}}^{M}(e_{1},e_{1})+\overline{\text{\rm Ric}}^{M}({\vec{\nu}},{\vec{\nu}})-{\overline{R}}^{M}(e_{1},{\vec{\nu}},{\vec{\nu}},e_{1})-\overline{\text{\rm Ric}}^{M}({\vec{\nu}},{\vec{\nu}})+\sum_{j=2}^{n-1}(A_{11}A_{jj}-A_{1j}^{2})\end{split}

So

\overline{\text{\rm Ric}}^{M}({\vec{\nu}},{\vec{\nu}})=\overline{\mathrm{BiRic}}^{M}(e_{1},{\vec{\nu}})+\sum_{j=2}^{n-1}(A_{11}A_{jj}-A_{1j}^{2})-\text{\rm Ric}^{\Sigma}(e_{1},e_{1}).

∎

Lemma 3.4
.

For any
0\leq{\alpha}\leq 1
, and let

be an orthonormal frame on
\Sigma

be as in Lemma

\begin{split}E\geq&aW+{\alpha}\overline{\mathrm{BiRic}}^{M}(e_{1},{\vec{\nu}}))+(1-{\alpha})\overline{\text{\rm Ric}}^{M}({\vec{\nu}},{\vec{\nu}})-{\alpha}\text{\rm Ric}^{\Sigma}(e_{1},e_{1})+Q\\
\end{split}

where

is a quadratic form defined in Theorem
1.1
.

Proof.

By Lemma
3.1
,
{\vec{\nu}}(\log w)=a^{-1}(h-H)
, write

\overline{\text{\rm Ric}}^{M}({\vec{\nu}},{\vec{\nu}})={\alpha}\overline{\text{\rm Ric}}^{M}({\vec{\nu}},{\vec{\nu}})+(1-{\alpha})\overline{\text{\rm Ric}}^{M}({\vec{\nu}},{\vec{\nu}}).

By Lemma
3.4
,

\begin{split}E\geq&aW+{\alpha}\overline{\mathrm{BiRic}}^{M}(e_{1},{\vec{\nu}}))+(1-{\alpha})\overline{\text{\rm Ric}}^{M}({\vec{\nu}},{\vec{\nu}})-{\alpha}\text{\rm Ric}^{\Sigma}(e_{1},e_{1})\\
&+{\alpha}\sum_{j=2}^{n-1}(A_{11}A_{jj}-A_{1j}^{2})+|A|^{2}+\frac{1}{a}(H-h)^{2}-H(H-h)-|{\overline{\nabla}}h|\\
\geq&aW+{\alpha}\overline{\mathrm{BiRic}}^{M}(e_{1},{\vec{\nu}}))+(1-{\alpha})\overline{\text{\rm Ric}}^{M}({\vec{\nu}},{\vec{\nu}})-{\alpha}\text{\rm Ric}^{\Sigma}(e_{1},e_{1})\\

\end{split}

Now:

\begin{split}{\alpha}(A_{11}H-\sum_{j=1}^{n}A_{1j}^{2})+|A|^{2}\geq&{\alpha}A_{11}H+\sum_{j=2}^{n-1}A_{jj}^{2}+(1-{\alpha})A_{11}^{2}\\
\geq&{\alpha}A_{11}H+\frac{1}{n-2}(H-A_{11})^{2}+(1-{\alpha})A_{11}^{2}\\
\geq&\frac{1}{n-2}H^{2}+(1-{\alpha}+\frac{1}{n-2})A_{11}^{2}+({\alpha}-\frac{2}{n-2})A_{11}H.\end{split}

\begin{split}E\geq&aW+{\alpha}\overline{\mathrm{BiRic}}^{M}(e_{1},{\vec{\nu}}))+(1-{\alpha})\overline{\text{\rm Ric}}^{M}({\vec{\nu}},{\vec{\nu}})-{\alpha}\text{\rm Ric}^{\Sigma}(e_{1},e_{1})\\

Lemma 4.1
.

For

\geq&(n-2)\left({\alpha}-\frac{(n-2)a}{4}\right)+P\\
\end{split}

where

is a quadratic form as defined in Theorem

Proof.

Let
\overline{e}_{i},1\leq i\leq n
be an orthonormal basis for
M
with respect to
\overline{g}
.
Let
M
be a hypersurface with second fundamental form
\overline{\mathcal{K}}
with respect to
\overline{G}
unit normal
\overline{\mathbf{n}}
(then
\overline{\mathbf{n}}=e^{s}\mathbf{n}
where
\mathbf{n}
is the unit normal with respect to
G
). Let
\omega=\overline{\mathbf{n}}(s)=-\frac{1}{n}\overline{H}
. By Lemma
2.1
, for
1\leq i,j\leq n
, with

, the curvature with respect to
\overline{G}
is given by:

{\overline{R}}(\overline{e}_{i},\overline{e}_{j},\overline{e}_{j},\overline{e}_{i})=1-\overline{e}_{i}^{2}(s)-\overline{e}_{j}^{2}(s).

By the Gauss equation:

1-\overline{e}_{i}^{2}(s)-\overline{e}_{j}^{2}(s)={\overline{R}}^{M}_{ijji}-\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{ii}\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{jj}+\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{ij}^{2}.

and so

(4.1)

{\overline{R}}^{M}_{ijji}=1-\overline{e}_{i}^{2}(s)-\overline{e}_{j}^{2}(s)+\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{ii}\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{jj}-\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{ij}^{2}.

So the bi-Ricci of
M
is:

\begin{split}\overline{\mathrm{BiRic}}^{M}(\overline{e}_{1},\overline{e}_{2})=&\overline{\text{\rm Ric}}^{M}(\overline{e}_{1},\overline{e}_{1})+\overline{\text{\rm Ric}}^{M}(\overline{e}_{2},\overline{e}_{2})-{\overline{R}}^{M}(\overline{e}_{i},\overline{e}_{j},\overline{e}_{j},\overline{e}_{i})\\
=&\sum_{j\neq 1,1\leq j\leq n}\left(1-\overline{e}_{1}^{2}(s)-\overline{e}_{j}^{2}(s)+\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{11}\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{jj}-\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{1j}^{2}\right)\\
&+\sum_{j\neq 2,1\leq j\leq n}\left(1-\overline{e}_{2}^{2}(s)-\overline{e}_{j}^{2}(s)+\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{22}\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{jj}-\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{2j}^{2}\right)\\
&-(1-\overline{e}_{1}^{2}(s)-\overline{e}_{2}^{2}(s)+\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{11}\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{22}-\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{12}^{2}).\end{split}

Now

\begin{split}\sum_{j\neq 1,1\leq j\leq n}\left(1-\overline{e}_{1}^{2}(s)-\overline{e}_{j}^{2}(s)\right)=&(n-1)-(n-2)\overline{e}_{1}^{2}(s)-\sum_{j=1}^{n}\overline{e}_{j}^{2}(s)\\
=&(n-1)-(n-2)\overline{e}_{1}^{2}(s)-|{\overline{\nabla}}s|^{2}.\end{split}

\sum_{j\neq 1,1\leq j\leq n}\left(\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{11}\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{jj}-\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{1j}^{2}\right)=\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{11}\overline{H}-\sum_{j=1}^{n}\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{1j}^{2}.

Similarly,

\begin{split}\sum_{j\neq 2,1\leq j\leq n}\left(1-\overline{e}_{2}^{2}(s)-\overline{e}_{j}^{2}(s)\right)=&(n-1)-(n-2)\overline{e}_{2}^{2}(s)-\sum_{j=1}^{n}\overline{e}_{j}^{2}(s)\\
=&(n-1)-(n-2)\overline{e}_{2}^{2}(s)-|{\overline{\nabla}}s|^{2}.\end{split}

\sum_{j\neq 2,1\leq j\leq n}\left(\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{22}\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{jj}-\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{2j}^{2}\right)=\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{22}\overline{H}-\sum_{j=1}^{n}\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{2j}^{2}.

Hence

\begin{split}\overline{\mathrm{BiRic}}^{M}(\overline{e}_{1},\overline{e}_{2})=&(2n-3)-(n-3)(\overline{e}_{1}^{2}(s)+\overline{e}_{2}^{2}(s))-2|{\overline{\nabla}}s|^{2}_{\overline{g}}+(\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{11}+\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{22})\overline{H}\\
&-\sum_{j=1}^{n}(\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{1j}^{2}+\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{2j}^{2})-\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{11}\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{12}+\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{12}^{2}\\
\end{split}

Similarly,

\begin{split}\overline{\text{\rm Ric}}^{M}(\overline{e}_{1},\overline{e}_{1})=&\sum_{j\geq 2}\overline{R}^{M}_{1jj1}\\
=&\sum_{j\geq 2}\left(1-\overline{e}_{1}^{2}(s)-\overline{e}_{j}^{2}(s)+\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{11}\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{jj}-\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{1j}^{2}\right)\\
=&(n-1)-(n-2)\overline{e}_{1}^{2}-|{\overline{\nabla}}s|^{2}+\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{11}\overline{H}-\sum_{j=1}^{n}\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{1j}^{2}.\end{split}

So

\begin{split}&{\alpha}\overline{\mathrm{BiRic}}^{M}(\overline{e}_{1},\overline{e}_{2})+(1-{\alpha})\overline{\text{\rm Ric}}(\overline{e}_{1},\overline{e}_{1})\\
=&{\alpha}(2n-3)+(1-{\alpha})(n-1)-{\alpha}(n-3)(\overline{e}_{1}^{2}(s)+\overline{e}_{2}^{2}(s))-(1-{\alpha})(n-2)\overline{e}_{1}^{2}(s)\\
&-(2{\alpha}+(1-{\alpha}))|{\overline{\nabla}}s|^{2}+(\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{11}+{\alpha}\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{22})\overline{H}-\sum_{j=1}^{n}(\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{1j}^{2}+{\alpha}\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{2j}^{2})-{\alpha}(\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{11}\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{22}-\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{12}^{2})\\
\geq&{\alpha}(2n-3)+(1-{\alpha})(n-1)-(n-1)|{\overline{\nabla}}s|^{2}\\
&+(\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{11}+{\alpha}\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{22})\overline{H}-\sum_{j=1}^{n}(\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{1j}^{2}+{\alpha}\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{2j}^{2})-{\alpha}(\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{11}\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{22}-\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{12}^{2})\\
=&{\alpha}(n-2)+(n-1)\omega^{2}-n(\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{11}+{\alpha}\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{22})\omega-\sum_{j=1}^{n}(\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{1j}^{2}+{\alpha}\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{2j}^{2})-{\alpha}(\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{11}\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{22}-\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{12}^{2})\end{split}

because
0\leq{\alpha}\leq 1
and
|{\overline{\nabla}}s|^{2}=|\overline{D}s|^{2}-(\overline{\mathbf{n}}(s))^{2}=1-\omega^{2}
. By the definition of
W
in Lemma
2.3
, since
a\geq 1
,

, we have

\begin{split}&aW+{\alpha}\overline{\mathrm{BiRic}}^{M}(\overline{e}_{1},\overline{e}_{2})+(1-{\alpha})\overline{\text{\rm Ric}}^{M}(\overline{e}_{1},\overline{e}_{1})\\
\geq&a\left(|\overline{\mathcal{K}}|^{2}-\frac{n^{2}+4n-4}{4}\omega^{2}-\left(\frac{n-2}{2}\right)^{2}\right)\\
&+{\alpha}(n-2)+(n-1)\omega^{2}-n(\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{11}+{\alpha}\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{22})\omega-\sum_{j=1}^{n}(\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{1j}^{2}+{\alpha}\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{2j}^{2})-{\alpha}(\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{11}\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{22}-\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{12}^{2})\\
=&{\alpha}(n-2)-a\left(\frac{n-2}{2}\right)^{2}+\left(n-1-\frac{a(n^{2}+4n-4)}{4}\right)\omega^{2}\\
&+a\sum_{i=3}^{n}\sum_{j=1}^{n}\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{ij}^{2}+(a-1)\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{11}^{2}+(a-{\alpha})\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{22}^{2}-{\alpha}\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{11}\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{22}-n(\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{11}+{\alpha}\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{22})\omega\end{split}

Let

\begin{split}\sum_{i=3}^{n}\sum_{j=1}^{n}\overline{\mathcal{K}}_{ij}^{2}\geq&\frac{1}{n-2}(\overline{H}-x-y)^{2}=\frac{1}{n-2}(\overline{H}^{2}-2\overline{H}(x+y)+(x+y)^{2})\\
=&\frac{n^{2}}{n-2}\omega^{2}+2\frac{n}{n-2}(x+y)\omega+\frac{1}{n-2}(x^{2}+2xy+y^{2})\end{split}

Hence, for

&+(a-1+\frac{a}{n-2})x^{2}+(a-{\alpha}+\frac{a}{n-2})y^{2}+(\frac{2a}{n-2}-{\alpha})xy\\
&+n\left((\frac{2a}{n-2}-1)x+(\frac{2a}{n-2}-{\alpha})y\right)\omega\\
\end{split}
