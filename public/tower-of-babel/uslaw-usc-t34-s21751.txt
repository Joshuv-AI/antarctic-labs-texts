34 U.S.C. § 21751 — Model power of attorney legislation
Citation: us:usc:34:21751
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section21751
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 217 (Elder Abuse Prevention And Prosecution); Subchapter V (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General shall publish model power of attorney legislation for the purpose of preventing elder abuse.
