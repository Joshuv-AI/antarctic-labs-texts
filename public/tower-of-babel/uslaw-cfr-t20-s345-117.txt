20 C.F.R. § 345.117 — When fractional part of cent may be disregarded.
Citation: us:cfr:20:345.117
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-345.117
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 345 (Employers' Contributions And Contribution Reports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the payment of employers' contributions to the Board a fractional part of a cent shall be disregarded unless it amounts to one-half cent or more, in which case it shall be increased to one cent.
