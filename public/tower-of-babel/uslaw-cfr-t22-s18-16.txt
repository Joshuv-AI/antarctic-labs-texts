22 C.F.R. § 18.16 — Evidence.
Citation: us:cfr:22:18.16
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-18.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 18 (Regulations Concerning Post Employment Conflict Of Interest). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules of evidence prevailing in courts of law and equity are not controlling in hearings under this part. However, the hearing examiner shall exclude evidence which is irrelevant, immaterial, or unduly repetitious.
