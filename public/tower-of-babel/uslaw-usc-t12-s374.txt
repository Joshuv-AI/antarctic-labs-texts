12 U.S.C. § 374 — Acting as agent for nonmember bank in getting discounts from reserve bank
Citation: us:usc:12:374
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section374
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 3 (Federal Reserve System); Subchapter X (Powers And Duties Of Member Banks). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No member bank shall act as the medium or agent of a nonmember bank in applying for or receiving discounts from a Federal reserve bank under the provisions of this chapter, except by permission of the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System.
