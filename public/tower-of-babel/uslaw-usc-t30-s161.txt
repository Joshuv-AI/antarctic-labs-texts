30 U.S.C. § 161 — Entry of building-stone lands; previous law unaffected
Citation: us:usc:30:161
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section161
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 3 (Lands Containing Coal, Oil, Gas, Salts, Asphaltic Materials, Sodium, Sulphur, And Building Stone); Subchapter VIII (Building Stone Or Saline Land Entries Under Placer-Mining Laws). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person authorized to enter lands under the mining laws of the United States may enter lands that are chiefly valuable for building stone under the provisions of the law in relation to placer mineral claims. Lands reserved for the benefit of the public schools or donated to any State shall not be subject to entry under this section. Nothing contained in this section shall be construed to repeal section 471 of title 16 relating to the establishment of national forests.
