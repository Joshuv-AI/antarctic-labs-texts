26 U.S.C. § 531 — Imposition of accumulated earnings tax
Citation: us:usc:26:531
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section531
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 1 (Normal Taxes And Surtaxes); Subchapter G (Corporations Used To Avoid Income Tax On Shareholders); Part I (Corporations Improperly Accumulating Surplus). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In addition to other taxes imposed by this chapter, there is hereby imposed for each taxable year on the accumulated taxable income (as defined in section 535) of each corporation described in section 532, an accumulated earnings tax equal to 20 percent of the accumulated taxable income.
