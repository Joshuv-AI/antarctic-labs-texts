34 C.F.R. § 395.10 — The maintenance and replacement of vending facility equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:34:395.10
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-395.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 395 (Vending Facility Program For The Blind On Federal And Other Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The State licensing agency shall maintain (or cause to be maintained) all vending facility equipment in good repair and in an attractive condition and shall replace or cause to be replaced worn-out and obsolete equipment as required to ensure the continued successful operation of the facility.
