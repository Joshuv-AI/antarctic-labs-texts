38 C.F.R. § 26.20 — Purpose of this subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:38:26.20
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-26.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 26 (National Environmental Policy Act Implementing Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart outlines the requirements and process for NEPA compliance to inform VA decision-making. Section 26.21 provides context by explaining how VA will integrate NEPA with VA planning. Remaining §§ 26.22 through 26.73 lay out NEPA analysis options and NEPA implementation methods specific to those options.
