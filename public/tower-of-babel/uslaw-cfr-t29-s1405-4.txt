29 C.F.R. § 1405.4 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1405.4
Creator: Federal Mediation And Conciliation Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1405.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1405 (Part-Time Employment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations cover permanent positions which are deemed by management to be appropriately structured on a part-time basis. The regulations do not apply to positions at GS-16 (or equivalent) and above.
