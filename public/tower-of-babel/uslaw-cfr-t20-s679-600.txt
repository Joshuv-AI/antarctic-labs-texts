20 C.F.R. § 679.600 — What is the purpose of the general statutory and regulatory waiver authority in the Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act?
Citation: us:cfr:20:679.600
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-679.600
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 679 (Statewide And Local Governance Of The Workforce Development System Under Title I Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The purpose of the general statutory and regulatory waiver authority provided at sec. 189(i)(3) of the WIOA is to provide flexibility to States and local areas and enhance their ability to improve the statewide workforce development system to achieve the goals and purposes of WIOA.
(b) A waiver may be requested to address impediments to the implementation of a Unified or Combined State Plan, including the continuous improvement strategy, consistent with the purposes of title I of WIOA as identified in § 675.100 of this chapter.
