49 C.F.R. § 845.4 — Determination to hold hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:49:845.4
Creator: National Transportation Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-845.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 845 (Rules Of Practice In Transportation: Investigative Hearings; Meetings, Reports, And Petitions For Reconsideration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Board may order an investigative hearing as part of an investigation whenever a hearing is deemed necessary in the public interest.
(b) If a quorum of the Board is not immediately available in the event of a catastrophic accident, the determination to hold an investigative hearing may be made by the Chairman of the Board.
