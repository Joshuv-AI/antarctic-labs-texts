25 C.F.R. § 150.202 — Must I check with any other governmental office to find title documents for Indian land?
Citation: us:cfr:25:150.202
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-150.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 150 (Record Of Title To Indian Land). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

LTRO maintains current and historical title documents to Indian land but in certain circumstances, due diligence may require examination of other Federal, State, and local records of title.
