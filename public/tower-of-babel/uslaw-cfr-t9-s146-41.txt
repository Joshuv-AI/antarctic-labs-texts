9 C.F.R. § 146.41 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:9:146.41
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-146.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 146 (National Poultry Improvement Plan For Commercial Poultry). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Meat-type turkey. A domesticated turkey grown for the primary purpose of producing meat.
Meat-type turkey slaughter plant. A meat-type turkey slaughter plant that is federally inspected or under State inspection that the Food Safety Inspection Service has recognized as equivalent to federal inspection.
