16 U.S.C. § 943b — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:16:943b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section943b
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 15C (Great Lakes Fish And Wildlife Tissue Bank). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter—
(1)
“Secretary” means the Secretary of the Interior, acting through the Director of the United States Fish and Wildlife Service.
(2)
“Great Lakes fish and wildlife” means fauna, fish, and invertebrates dependent on Great Lakes resources, and located within the Great Lakes Basin.
