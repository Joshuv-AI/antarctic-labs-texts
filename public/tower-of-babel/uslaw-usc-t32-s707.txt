32 U.S.C. § 707 — Use of public buildings for offices by instructors
Citation: us:usc:32:707
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title32-section707
Context: United States Code, Title 32 (National Guard); Chapter 7 (Service, Supply, And Procurement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whenever practicable, instructors of the National Guard shall use State armories or other public buildings for offices.
