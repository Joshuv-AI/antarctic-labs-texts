42 U.S.C. § 8855 — Forest Service leases and permits
Citation: us:usc:42:8855
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section8855
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 96 (Biomass Energy And Alcohol Fuels); Subchapter III (Rural, Agricultural, And Forestry Biomass Energy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the intent of the Congress that the Secretary of Agriculture shall process applications for leases of National Forest System lands and for permits to explore, drill, and develop resources on land leased from the Forest Service, notwithstanding the current status of any plan being prepared under section 1604 of title 16.
