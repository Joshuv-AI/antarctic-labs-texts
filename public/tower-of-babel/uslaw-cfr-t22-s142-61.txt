22 C.F.R. § 142.61 — Application of this subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:22:142.61
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-142.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 142 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subpart E applies to health, welfare, social and other programs or activities that receive Federal financial assistance and to recipients that operate, or that receive or benefit from Federal financial assistance for the operation of such programs or activities.
