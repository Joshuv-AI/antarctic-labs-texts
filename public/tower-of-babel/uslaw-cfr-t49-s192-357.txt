49 C.F.R. § 192.357 — Customer meters and regulators: Installation.
Citation: us:cfr:49:192.357
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-192.357
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 192 (Transportation Of Natural And Other Gas By Pipeline: Minimum Federal Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each meter and each regulator must be installed so as to minimize anticipated stresses upon the connecting piping and the meter.
(b) When close all-thread nipples are used, the wall thickness remaining after the threads are cut must meet the minimum wall thickness requirements of this part.
(c) Connections made of lead or other easily damaged material may not be used in the installation of meters or regulators.
(d) Each regulator that might release gas in its operation must be vented to the outside atmosphere.
