7 C.F.R. § 1223.12 — Market or marketing.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1223.12
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Miscellaneous Commodities), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1223.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1223 (Pecan Promotion, Research, And Information Order). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Marketing means the sale or other disposition of pecans in any channel of commerce.
(b) To market means to sell or otherwise dispose of pecans in interstate, foreign, or intrastate commerce.
