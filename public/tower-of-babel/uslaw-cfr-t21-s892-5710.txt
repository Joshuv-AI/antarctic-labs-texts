21 C.F.R. § 892.5710 — Radiation therapy beam-shaping block.
Citation: us:cfr:21:892.5710
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-892.5710
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 892 (Radiology Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. A radiation therapy beam-shaping block is a device made of a highly attenuating material (such as lead) intended for medical purposes to modify the shape of a beam from a radiation therapy source.
(b) Classification. Class II.
