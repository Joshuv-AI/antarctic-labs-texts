31 C.F.R. § 370.5 — How can I appoint a financial institution to receive payments on my behalf?
Citation: us:cfr:31:370.5
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-370.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 370 (Electronic Transactions And Funds Transfers Relating To United States Securities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must name a financial institution to receive payments through credit entries using the ACH method. You also must identify the deposit account to which payments are to be made. To do this, you must use a form approved by us.
