11 C.F.R. § 100.35 — Writing, written.
Citation: us:cfr:11:100.35
Creator: Federal Election Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-100.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 100 (Scope And Definitions (52 U.S.C. 30101)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Written, in writing, or a writing means consisting of letters, words, numbers, or their equivalent set down in any medium or form, including paper, email or other electronic message, computer file, or digital storage device.
