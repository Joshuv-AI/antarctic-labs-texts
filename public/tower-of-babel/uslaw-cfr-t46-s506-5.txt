46 C.F.R. § 506.5 — Application of increase to violations.
Citation: us:cfr:46:506.5
Creator: Federal Maritime Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-506.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 506 (Civil Monetary Penalty Inflation Adjustment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any adjustment in a civil monetary penalty under this part shall apply only to civil monetary penalties, including those whose associated violation predated such increase, which are assessed after the date the adjustment takes effect.
