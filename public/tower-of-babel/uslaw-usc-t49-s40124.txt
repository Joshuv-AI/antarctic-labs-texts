49 U.S.C. § 40124 — Interstate agreements for airport facilities
Citation: us:usc:49:40124
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section40124
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Air Commerce And Safety); Subpart i (General); Chapter 401 (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Congress consents to a State making an agreement, not in conflict with a law of the United States, with another State to develop or operate an airport facility.
