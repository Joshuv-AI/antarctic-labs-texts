47 U.S.C. § 922 — National spectrum allocation planning
Citation: us:usc:47:922
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title47-section922
Context: United States Code, Title 47 (Telecommunications); Chapter 8 (National Telecommunications And Information Administration); Subchapter II (Transfer Of Auctionable Frequencies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Assistant Secretary and the Chairman of the Commission shall meet, at least biannually, to conduct joint spectrum planning with respect to the following issues:
(1)
the extent to which licenses for spectrum use can be issued pursuant to section 309(j) of this title to increase Federal revenues;
(2)
the future spectrum requirements for public and private uses, including State and local government public safety agencies;
(3)
the spectrum allocation actions necessary to accommodate those uses; and
(4)
actions necessary to promote the efficient use of the spectrum, including spectrum management techniques to promote increased shared use of the spectrum that does not cause harmful interference as a means of increasing commercial access.
