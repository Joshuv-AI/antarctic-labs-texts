29 C.F.R. § 4903.7 — When will PBGC allow a debtor to pay a debt owed to PBGC in installments instead of a lump sum?
Citation: us:cfr:29:4903.7
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4903.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4903 (Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a debtor is financially unable to pay the debt in a lump sum, PBGC may accept payment of a debt in regular installments, in accordance with the provisions of 31 CFR 901.8.
