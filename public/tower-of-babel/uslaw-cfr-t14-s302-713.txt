14 C.F.R. § 302.713 — DOT analysis of data for submission of answers thereto.
Citation: us:cfr:14:302.713
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-302.713
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 302 (Rules Of Practice In Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After a careful analysis of these data, the DOT employees will, in most cases, send the air carrier a statement of exceptions showing areas of differences. Where practicable, the air carrier may submit an answer to these exceptions. Conferences will then be scheduled to resolve the issues and facts in accordance with sound ratemaking principles.
