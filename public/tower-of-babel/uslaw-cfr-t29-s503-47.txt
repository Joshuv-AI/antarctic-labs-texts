29 C.F.R. § 503.47 — Caption of proceeding.
Citation: us:cfr:29:503.47
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-503.47
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 503 (Enforcement Of Obligations For Temporary Nonimmigrant Non-Agricultural Workers Described In The Immigration And Nationality Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each administrative proceeding instituted under 8 U.S.C. 1184(c)(14), INA section 214(c)(14) and the regulations in this part will be captioned in the name of the person requesting such hearing, and will be styled as follows:
In the Matter of __________, Respondent.
(b) For the purposes of such administrative proceedings the Administrator, WHD will be identified as plaintiff and the person requesting such hearing will be named as respondent.
