42 C.F.R. § 57.1501 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:42:57.1501
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-57.1501
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 57 (Grants For Construction Of Teaching Facilities, Educational Improvements, Scholarships And Student Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations of this subpart are applicable to loan guarantees and interest subsidy payments made pursuant to section 729 of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 293i) to assist nonprofit private entities which are eligible for grants under subpart B of this part in carrying out projects for construction of teaching facilities for health professions personnel.
