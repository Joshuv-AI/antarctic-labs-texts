30 C.F.R. § 560.211 — When does an eligible lease qualify for a royalty suspension volume?
Citation: us:cfr:30:560.211
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-560.211
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 560 (Outer Continental Shelf Oil And Gas Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Your eligible lease will receive a royalty suspension volume as specified in the Act. The bidding system in § 560.202(g) applies.
(b) Your eligible lease may receive a royalty suspension volume only if your entire lease is west of 87 degrees, 30 minutes West longitude.
