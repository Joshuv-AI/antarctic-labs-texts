25 U.S.C. § 2906 — Use of English
Citation: us:usc:25:2906
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section2906
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 31 (Native American Languages). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed as precluding the use of Federal funds to teach English to Native Americans.
