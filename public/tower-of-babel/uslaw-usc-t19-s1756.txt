19 U.S.C. § 1756 — Regulations
Citation: us:usc:19:1756
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section1756
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 6 (Trade Fair Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Treasury may prescribe such regulations as may be necessary or appropriate to carry out the provisions of this chapter (other than section 1751 thereof).
