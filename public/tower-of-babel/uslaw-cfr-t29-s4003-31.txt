29 C.F.R. § 4003.31 — Who may request reconsideration.
Citation: us:cfr:29:4003.31
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4003.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4003 (Rules For Administrative Review Of Agency Decisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person aggrieved by an initial determination of PBGC to which this subpart applies may request reconsideration of the initial determination.
