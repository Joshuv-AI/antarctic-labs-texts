45 C.F.R. § 261.25 — Do we count Tribal families in calculating the work participation rate?
Citation: us:cfr:45:261.25
Creator: Office Of Family Assistance (Assistance Programs), Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-261.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 261 (Ensuring That Recipients Work). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At State option, we will include families with a work-eligible individual that are receiving assistance under an approved Tribal family assistance plan or under a Tribal work program in calculating the State's participation rates under §§ 261.22 and 261.24.
