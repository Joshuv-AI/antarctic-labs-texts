36 C.F.R. § 4.13 — Obstructing traffic.
Citation: us:cfr:36:4.13
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-4.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 4 (Vehicles And Traffic Safety). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following are prohibited:
(a) Stopping or parking a vehicle upon a park road, except as authorized by the superintendent, or in the event of an accident or other condition beyond the control of the operator.
(b) Operating a vehicle so slowly as to interfere with the normal flow of traffic.
