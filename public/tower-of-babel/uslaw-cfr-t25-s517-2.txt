25 C.F.R. § 517.2 — Public reading room.
Citation: us:cfr:25:517.2
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-517.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 517 (Freedom Of Information Act Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Records that are required to be maintained by the Commission shall be available for public inspection and copying at 90 K Street NE, Suite 200, Washington, DC 20002. Reading room records created on or after November 1, 1996, shall be made available electronically via the Commission's website.
