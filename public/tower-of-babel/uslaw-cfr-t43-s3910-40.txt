43 C.F.R. § 3910.40 — Exploration license requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3910.40
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3910.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3910 (Oil Shale Exploration Licenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The licensee must comply with all applicable Federal, state, and local laws and regulations, the terms and conditions of the license, and the approved exploration plan. The operator or licensee must notify the BLM of any change of address or operator or licensee name.
