2 C.F.R. § 1108.355 — Subaward.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1108.355
Creator: Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1108.355
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1108 (Definitions Of Terms Used In Subchapters A Through F Of This Chapter). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subaward means a legal instrument by which a recipient or subrecipient at any tier transfers—for performance by an entity at the next lower tier—a portion of the substantive program for which the DoD Component made an award.
