12 U.S.C. § 74 — Vacancies
Citation: us:usc:12:74
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section74
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 2 (National Banks); Subchapter III (Directors). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any vacancy in the board shall be filled by appointment by the remaining directors, and any director so appointed shall hold his place until the next election.
