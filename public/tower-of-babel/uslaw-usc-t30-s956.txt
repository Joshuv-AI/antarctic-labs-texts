30 U.S.C. § 956 — Applicability of administrative procedure provisions
Citation: us:usc:30:956
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section956
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 22 (Mine Safety And Health); Subchapter V (Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as otherwise provided in this chapter, the provisions of sections 551 to 559 and sections 701 to 706 of title 5 shall not apply to the making of any order, notice, or decision made pursuant to this chapter, or to any proceeding for the review thereof.
