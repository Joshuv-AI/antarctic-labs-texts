41 C.F.R. § 301-11.604 — -11.604 When to file the required tax information for extended TDY.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-11.604
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-11.604
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-11 (11—Subsistence Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees should provide the information their agency requires to make the ETTRA calculation. This will include tax information for any Federal and State tax returns filed for the year that the employee was on a taxable extended TDY assignment. Employees should submit this information at the beginning of the extended TDY assignment, or as soon as the employee or agency realizes the assignment will incur taxes.
