10 U.S.C. § 6179 — Public participation in planning for defense environmental cleanup
Citation: us:usc:10:6179
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section6179
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part VI (Elements Of Department Of Defense And Other Matters); Subpart B (Atomic Energy Defense); Chapter 604 (Defense Environmental Cleanup Matters); Subchapter I (Defense Environmental Cleanup). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Energy shall consult with the Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency, the Attorney General, Governors and attorneys general of affected States, appropriate representatives of affected Indian tribes, and interested members of the public in any planning conducted by the Secretary for defense environmental cleanup activities at Department of Energy defense nuclear facilities.
