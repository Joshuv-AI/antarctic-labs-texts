14 C.F.R. § 135.12 — Previously trained crewmembers.
Citation: us:cfr:14:135.12
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-135.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 135 (Operating Requirements: Commuter And On Demand Operations And Rules Governing Persons On Board Such Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A certificate holder may use a crewmember who received the certificate holder's training in accordance with subparts E, G, and H of this part before March 19, 1997 without complying with initial training and qualification requirements of subparts N and O of part 121 of this chapter. The crewmember must comply with the applicable recurrent training requirements of part 121 of this chapter.
