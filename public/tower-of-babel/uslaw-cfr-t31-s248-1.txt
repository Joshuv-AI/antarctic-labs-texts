Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 248 (Issue Of Substitutes Of Lost, Stolen, Destroyed, Mutilated And Defaced Checks Of The United States Drawn On Accounts Maintained In Depositary Banks In Foreign Countries Or United States Territories Or Possessions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part governs the issuance of substitutes for checks of the United States drawn on United States dollar or foreign currency accounts, maintained with designated depositaries in foreign countries or territories or possessions of the United States. Checks of the United States drawn on such depositaries are hereafter referred to as “depositary checks.”
