22 C.F.R. § 128.16 — Extension of time.
Citation: us:cfr:22:128.16
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-128.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 128 (Administrative Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrative Law Judge, for good cause shown, may extend the time within which to prepare and submit an answer to a charging letter or to perform any other act required by this part.
