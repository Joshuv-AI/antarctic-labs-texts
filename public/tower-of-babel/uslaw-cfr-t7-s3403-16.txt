7 C.F.R. § 3403.16 — Other considerations.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3403.16
Creator: National Institute Of Food And Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3403.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3403 (Small Business Innovation Research Grants Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department may, with respect to any research project grant, impose additional conditions prior to or at the time of any award when, in the Department's judgment, such conditions are necessary to assure or protect advancement of the approved project, the interests of the public, or the conservation of grant funds.
