40 C.F.R. § 86.007-17 — -17 Onboard diagnostics for engines used in applications less than or equal to 14,000 pounds GVWR.
Citation: us:cfr:40:86.007-17
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-86.007-17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 86 (Control Of Emissions From New And In-Use Highway Vehicles And Engines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Heavy-duty engines intended to be installed in heavy duty vehicles at or below 14,000 pounds GVWR that are subject to standards under this subpart must meet onboard diagnostic requirements as specified in § 86.1806.
