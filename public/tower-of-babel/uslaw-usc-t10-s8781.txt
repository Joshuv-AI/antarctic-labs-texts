10 U.S.C. § 8781 — Marine Corps post laundries: disposition of receipts
Citation: us:usc:10:8781
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8781
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 877 (Quarters, Utilities, And Services). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Money received for laundry work performed by Marine Corps post laundries shall be used to pay the cost of maintenance and operation of those laundries. Any amount remaining at the end of the fiscal year after the cost has been so paid shall be deposited in the Treasury to the credit of the appropriation from which the cost of operating the laundries is paid.
(b)
The receipts and expenditures of Marine Corps post laundries shall be accounted for as public funds.
