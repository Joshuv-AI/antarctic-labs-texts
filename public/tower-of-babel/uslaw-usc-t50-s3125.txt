50 U.S.C. § 3125 — Providing information to Congress
Citation: us:usc:50:3125
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section3125
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 44 (National Security); Subchapter IV (Protection Of Certain National Security Information). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subchapter may be construed as authority to withhold information from the Congress or from a committee of either House of Congress.
