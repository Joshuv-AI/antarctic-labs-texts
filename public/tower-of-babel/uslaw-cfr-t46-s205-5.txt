46 C.F.R. § 205.5 — Contracts containing disputes article.
Citation: us:cfr:46:205.5
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-205.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 205 (Audit Appeals; Policy And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a contract contains a disputes article, the disputes article will govern the bases for negotiating disputes regarding audit findings, interpretations, or decisions made by MARAD and any appeals.
