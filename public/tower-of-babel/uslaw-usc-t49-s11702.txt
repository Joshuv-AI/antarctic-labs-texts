49 U.S.C. § 11702 — Enforcement by the Board
Citation: us:usc:49:11702
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section11702
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Rail); Chapter 117 (Enforcement: Investigations, Rights, And Remedies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Board may bring a civil action—
(1)
to enjoin a rail carrier from violating sections 10901 through 10906 of this title, or a regulation prescribed or order or certificate issued under any of those sections;
(2)
to enforce subchapter II of chapter 113 of this title and to compel compliance with an order of the Board under that subchapter; and
(3)
to enforce an order of the Board, except a civil action to enforce an order for the payment of money, when it is violated by a rail carrier providing transportation subject to the jurisdiction of the Board under this part.
