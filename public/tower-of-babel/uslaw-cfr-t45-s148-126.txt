45 C.F.R. § 148.126 — Determination of an eligible individual.
Citation: us:cfr:45:148.126
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-148.126
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 148 (Requirements For The Individual Health Insurance Market). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules for guaranteeing the availability of individual health insurance coverage to certain eligible individuals with prior group coverage have been superseded by the requirements of § 147.104 of this subchapter, which set forth Federal requirements for guaranteed availability of coverage in the group and individual markets.
