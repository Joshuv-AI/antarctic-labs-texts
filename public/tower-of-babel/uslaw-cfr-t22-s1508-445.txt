22 C.F.R. § 1508.445 — What action may I take if a primary tier participant knowingly does business with an excluded or disqualified person?
Citation: us:cfr:22:1508.445
Creator: African Development Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1508.445
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1508 (Governmentwide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a participant knowingly does business with an excluded or disqualified person, you as an agency official may refer the matter for suspension and debarment consideration. You may also disallow costs, annul or terminate the transaction, issue a stop work order, or take any other appropriate remedy.
