Error in Figure

In the original publication [1], there were two mistakes in Figure 2C as published. After the dot plot data used in Figures 2C and 5B were re-examined, the dot plot results used in Figure 5B for trametinib (T) and trametinib+capivasertib (T+C)-treated+Dox BAKP cells were determined to have been inadvertently duplicated and used for the Figure 2C dot plot for T and T+C-treated-Dox BAKP cells. Figure 5B is correct; however, the duplicated dot plot data in Figure 2C need to be replaced with the correct dot plot data for T and T+C-treated-Dox BAKP cells. The corrected Figure 2 appears below.

The authors state that the scientific conclusions of this paper are unaffected. This correction was approved by the Academic Editor. The original publication has also been updated.
