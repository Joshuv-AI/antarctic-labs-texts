48 C.F.R. § 12.201 — 12.201 General.
Citation: us:cfr:48:12.201
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-12.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 12 (Acquisition Of Commercial Products And Commercial Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart identifies special requirements for the acquisition of commercial products and commercial services intended to more closely resemble those customarily used in the commercial marketplace, as well as other considerations necessary for proper planning, solicitation, evaluation, and award of contracts for commercial products and commercial services.
