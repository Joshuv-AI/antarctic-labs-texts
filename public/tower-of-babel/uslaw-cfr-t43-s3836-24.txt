43 C.F.R. § 3836.24 — If BLM approves my petition, what else must I do to obtain a deferment of assessment work?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3836.24
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3836.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3836 (Annual Assessment Work Requirements For Mining Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must record a copy of BLM's decision regarding your petition in the local recording office.
