7 C.F.R. § 989.31 — Failure to nominate.
Citation: us:cfr:7:989.31
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-989.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 989 (Raisins Produced From Grapes Grown In California). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the event nomination for a member or alternate member position on the Committee is not certified pursuant to and within the time specified in § 989.29, the Secretary may select an eligible person to fill such position without regard to nomination.
