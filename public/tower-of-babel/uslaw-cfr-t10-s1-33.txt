10 C.F.R. § 1.33 — Office of Enforcement.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1.33
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1 (Statement Of Organization And General Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Office of Enforcement—
(a) Develops policies and programs for enforcement of NRC requirements;
(b) Manages major enforcement action;
(c) Assesses the effectiveness and uniformity of Regional enforcement actions; and
(d) Manages the NRC allegation program.
