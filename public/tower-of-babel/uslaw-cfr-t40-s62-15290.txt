40 C.F.R. § 62.15290 — Where must I keep my records and for how long?
Citation: us:cfr:40:62.15290
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-62.15290
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 62 (Approval And Promulgation Of State Plans For Designated Facilities And Pollutants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Keep all records onsite in paper copy or electronic format unless the Administrator approves another format.
(b) Keep all records on each municipal waste combustion unit for at least 5 years.
(c) Make all records available for submittal to the Administrator, or for onsite review by an inspector.
