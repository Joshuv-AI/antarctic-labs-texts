25 C.F.R. § 301.5 — Applique elements in design.

Creator: Indian Arts And Crafts Board, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 301 (Navajo, Pueblo, And Hopi Silver And Turquoise Products; Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All such parts of the ornament are to be hand-made. If wire is used, it is to be hand-made with no tool other than a hand-made draw plate. These requirements apply to the boxes for stone used in the design.
