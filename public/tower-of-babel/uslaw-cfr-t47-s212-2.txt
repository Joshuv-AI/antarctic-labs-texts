47 C.F.R. § 212.2 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:47:212.2
Creator: Office Of Science And Technology Policy And National Security Council
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-212.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 212 (Procedures For Obtaining International Telecommunication Service For Use During A Wartime Emergency). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The procedures in this part provide guidance for the submission of emergency requirements for telecommunication channels from the United States to overseas or foreign points. Guidance on this subject was previously contained in Annex 2 of DMO 3000.1 and Mobilization Plan IX-3. Mobilization Plan IX-3 has been canceled.
