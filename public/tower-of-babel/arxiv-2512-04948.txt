Imperial-TP–2025-AT-02 On world-volume supersymmetry of supermembrane action in static gauge
Arkady A. Tseytlin and Zihan Wang

Abstract
We review and elaborate on the issue of 3d world volume supersymmetry that appears as a residual part of global target space supersymmetry in the BST supermembrane action. While there is no direct “spinning membrane” analog of the world-volume supersymmetric spinning string action that could be obtained by coupling D D copies of 3d scalar multiplet to 3d supergravity, we discuss how one may construct an 𝒩 = 1 {\cal N}=1 3d supersymmetric analog of the derivative expansion of the bosonic membrane action in static gauge. We compare the resulting 𝒩 = 1 {\cal N}=1 supersymmetric action for eight 3d scalar multiplets to the 𝒩 = 8 {\cal N}=8 3d supersymmetric action describing the D = 11 D=11 supermembrane in the static gauge. The two actions are not equivalent which is related to the fact that the full 𝒩 = 8 {\cal N}=8 supersymmetry of the static-gauge D = 11 D=11 supermembrane action can be realised only if the fermions are described by an S ​ O ​ ( 8 ) SO(8) spinor rather than vector. The two actions are still directly related in special dimensions D = 4 D=4 and 5. We also compute the one-loop world-volume scattering amplitudes for the two theories finding that they indeed agree for D = 4 , 5 D=4,5 but disagree for D = 11 D=11 .
