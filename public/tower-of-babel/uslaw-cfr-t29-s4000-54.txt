29 C.F.R. § 4000.54 — May I dispose of original paper records if I keep electronic copies?
Citation: us:cfr:29:4000.54
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4000.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4000 (Filing, Issuance, Computation Of Time, And Record Retention). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may dispose of original paper records any time after they are transferred to an electronic recordkeeping system that complies with the requirements of this subpart, except such original records may not be discarded if the electronic record would not constitute a duplicate or substitute record under the terms of the plan and applicable federal or state law.
