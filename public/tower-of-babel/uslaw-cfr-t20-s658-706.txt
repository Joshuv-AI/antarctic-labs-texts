20 C.F.R. § 658.706 — Notice of decertification.
Citation: us:cfr:20:658.706
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-658.706
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 658 (Administrative Provisions Governing The Wagner-Peyser Act Employment Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Secretary decides to decertify a SWA, they must send a Notice of Decertification to the SWA stating the reasons for this action and providing a 10-business-day period during which the SWA may request an administrative hearing in writing to the Secretary. The document must be published promptly in the Federal Register.
