Crossing the phantom divide in scalar-tensor and vector-tensor theories Preprint: WUCG-25-09
Shinji Tsujikawa

Abstract
DESI observations of baryon acoustic oscillations (BAOs), combined with cosmic microwave background (CMB) and type-Ia supernova (SN Ia) data, suggest that the dark energy equation of state w DE w_{\rm DE} crosses the phantom divide from w DE < − 1 w_{\rm DE}<-1 to w DE > − 1 w_{\rm DE}>-1 at low redshifts. In shift-symmetric Horndeski and generalized Proca theories with luminal gravitational-wave speed and no direct couplings to dark matter, we show that such a phantom-divide crossing is generically difficult without introducing theoretical pathologies. In Horndeski theories, however, breaking the shift symmetry allows this transition. We present an explicit model with broken shift symmetry, in which the scalar field has a potential in addition to a Galileon self-interaction and a higher-order derivative term. This model realizes the desired phantom-divide crossing at low redshifts while avoiding ghosts and Laplacian instabilities.



I
Introduction

Recent DESI observations of BAOs challenge
a cosmological constant and support dynamical dark energy (DE) models,
in which the DE equation of state
w_{\rm DE}
evolves with time
Adame et al. 2025
;
Calderon et al. 2024
.
When the DESI DR2 data
Abdul Karim et al. 2025
are combined with
CMB observations
Aghanim et al. 2020
,
there is a
3.1\sigma
preference for dynamical DE based on
the Chevallier-Polarski-Linder (CPL) parametrization of

w_{\rm DE}

Chevallier and Polarski 2001
;
Linder 2003
.
This trend persists when SN Ia data are included,
showing a preference for dynamical DE with a significance between

2.8\sigma
and
4.2\sigma
, depending on the dataset
combination
Lodha et al. 2025
.

In particular, current observations favor models in which the DE equation of state crosses the phantom divide from
w_{\rm DE}<-1
to
w_{\rm DE}>-1
, occurring at a critical redshift
0<z_{c}<1

Calderon et al. 2024
;
Lodha et al. 2025
. Such a crossing may be realized by introducing a ghost scalar field with a negative kinetic term
Caldwell 2002
, in addition to a quintessence field with a positive kinetic term
X
. However, it typically proceeds from
w_{\rm DE}>-1
to
w_{\rm DE}<-1

Feng et al. 2005
;
Guo et al. 2005
. Moreover, the presence of a ghost
field renders the vacuum unstable due to its unboundedly negative energy density
Carroll et al. 2003
;
Cline et al. 2004
.

One way to achieve
w_{\rm DE}<-1
without introducing ghosts is to extend scalar-field theories to include field derivative self-interactions or nonminimal couplings to gravity. For example, in covariant Galileon models
Deffayet et al. 2009
, there exists a tracker solution along which
w_{\rm DE}
evolves from
-2
during the matter era to the asymptotic de-Sitter value
-1

De Felice and Tsujikawa 2010
. In this case, it is possible to avoid both ghost and Laplacian instabilities, but
w_{\rm DE}
does not enter the region
w_{\rm DE}>-1
at low redshifts. Moreover, the tracker solution for covariant Galileons is ruled out by joint analyses of CMB, SN Ia, and BAO data, mostly due to the large deviation of
w_{\rm DE}
from
-1
from the matter era to today
Nesseris et al. 2010
;
Neveu et al. 2017
;
Peirone et al. 2018
.

If the quadratic kinetic term
X^{2}
is included in the Lagrangian of covariant Galileons, known as the Galileon Ghost Condensate (GGC)
Peirone et al. 2019
, the approach to the tracker is hindered by this additional contribution. As a result, the deviation of
w_{\rm DE}
from
-1
tends to be smaller compared to that in covariant Galileons. A statistical analysis using CMB, SN Ia, and BAO data in 2019 showed that the GGC model is favored over the cosmological constant
Peirone et al. 2019
. However,
as long as linear stability conditions
hold in the GGC model,
w_{\rm DE}
does not cross the phantom divide at low redshifts.

The GGC model belongs to a subclass of shift-symmetric (SS) Horndeski theories
Horndeski 1974
, in which the Lagrangian is invariant under a constant shift of the scalar field,
\phi\to\phi+c
. Such SS scalar-tensor theories are related to vector-tensor theories with broken
U(1)
gauge symmetry, known as generalized Proca (GP) theories
Heisenberg 2014
;
Tasinato 2014
;
Allys et al. 2016
;
Beltran Jimenez and Heisenberg 2016
.
In the limit where the vector field
A_{\mu}
is replaced by the derivative of a scalar,

A_{\mu}\to\partial_{\mu}\phi
, the Lagrangian of GP theories reduces to that of SS Horndeski theories. The DE dynamics in GP theories was studied in Ref.
De Felice et al. 2016
, showing that the evolution of
w_{\rm DE}
mimics that of the tracker solution for covariant Galileons. This implies that
w_{\rm DE}
remains in the
regime
w_{\rm DE}<-1
from the matter era
up to the present.

In this paper, we first show that crossing the phantom divide from

w_{\rm DE}<-1
to
w_{\rm DE}>-1

without theoretical pathologies is generally challenging in both SS Horndeski and GP theories with luminal
gravitational-wave (GW) speed.
This includes covariant Galileons, the GGC model, and DE models in GP theories.
To demonstrate such a property, we employ the effective field theory (EFT)
of DE, which unifies Horndeski and GP theories
Aoki et al. 2022
;
Aoki et al. 2025
.
This challenge can be overcome by breaking the shift symmetry in scalar-tensor theories.

One such approach is to introduce a nonminimal coupling
F(\phi)R
with the Ricci
scalar
R

Boisseau et al. 2000
;
Perivolaropoulos 2005
;
Amendola and Tsujikawa 2008
;
Tsujikawa et al. 2008
;
Motohashi et al. 2010
,
which includes
f(R)
models of
late-time cosmic acceleration
Hu and Sawicki 2007
;
Starobinsky 2007
;
Appleby and Battye 2007
;
Tsujikawa 2008
.
In these theories, the nonminimal coupling mediates fifth forces between the scalar field and matter. For consistency with local gravity tests and structure formation, the deviation of
w_{\rm DE}
from
-1
in
f(R)
models is so tiny that they are effectively indistinguishable from the cosmological
constant
Hu and Sawicki 2007
;
Brax et al. 2008
.
In the presence of the nonminimal coupling
F(\phi)R
,
even if the scalar-matter coupling is suppressed by a screening mechanism,
the cosmological evolution of the DE scalar
\phi
induces a time variation of
the gravitational coupling
G
in overdense
regions
Babichev et al. 2011
;
Kimura et al. 2012a
.
In nonminimally coupled theories,
the stringent Lunar Laser Ranging bounds on
the time variation of
G

Hofmann and Müller 2018
suppress deviations of
w_{\rm DE}
from
-1
at low redshifts
Tsujikawa 2019
.

Here, we extend the GGC model by introducing a scalar potential
V(\phi)

without a nonminimal coupling.
The potential breaks the shift symmetry and allows the phantom-divide crossing,
leading to an appreciable deviation of
w_{\rm DE}
from
-1
.
The new model, defined by an explicit Lagrangian, can realize
the evolution of
w_{\rm DE}
at low redshifts required by DESI observations.

II
SS Horndeski and GP theories

We first consider SS Horndeski and GP theories with luminal GW speed,
consistent with the GW170817
observation
Abbott et al. 2017
.
The dynamics of the background and perturbations can be treated in a unified manner using the EFT of DE
Aoki et al. 2022
;
Aoki et al. 2025
(see also

Gubitosi et al. 2013
;
Bloomfield et al. 2013
;
Gleyzes et al. 2013
;
Raveri et al. 2014
;
Lagos et al. 2016
).
On the spatially flat, isotropic cosmological background described by the line element

{\rm d}s^{2}=-\bar{N}^{2}(t){\rm d}t^{2}+a^{2}(t)\,\delta_{ij}{\rm d}x^{i}{\rm d}x^{j}
,
we include matter fields with energy density
\rho_{M}
and pressure
p_{M}
.
We consider a vector-field configuration
of the form
A_{\mu}=[A_{0}(t),{\bm{0}}]
,
where the temporal component
A_{0}
depends
on time
t

By choosing the unitary gauge and
introducing a gauge coupling constant
g_{M}

a preferred timelike vector field is given by

orthogonal to constant-
t

hypersurfaces. In GP theories we have
g_{M}\neq 0
, in which case the symmetry-breaking pattern differs from that in Horndeski theories (
g_{M}=0
).

The EFT action describing the background and linear perturbations in SS Horndeski and GP theories is given by

{\cal S}=\int{\rm d}^{4}x\sqrt{-g}\,{\cal L}+{\cal S}_{M}

Aoki et al. 2022
,
where
{\cal S}_{M}
is the action of matter
minimally coupled to gravity, and

\displaystyle{\cal L}=\frac{M_{*}^{2}}{2}f\left[{}^{(3)}R+K_{\mu\nu}K^{\mu\nu}-K^{2}\right]-\Lambda-\tilde{c}\,\tilde{g}^{00}-d\,K\,,

\displaystyle~~~~~+\frac{M_{2}^{4}}{2}\left(\frac{\delta\tilde{g}^{00}}{\tilde{g}^{00}_{\rm BG}}\right)^{2}+\frac{\bar{M}_{1}^{3}}{2}\frac{\delta\tilde{g}^{00}}{\tilde{g}^{00}_{\rm BG}}\delta K+\frac{\gamma_{1}}{2}F_{\mu}F^{\mu}\,,

(1)

where
M_{*}
is a constant, while
f
,
\Lambda
,
\tilde{c}
,
d
,

M_{2}^{4}
,
\bar{M}_{1}^{3}
, and
\gamma_{1}
are time-dependent functions.
Here,
{}^{(3)}R
is the three-dimensional Ricci scalar in the
Arnowitt-Deser-Misner decomposition of spacetime
Arnowitt et al. 1959
,

K
is the trace of
the extrinsic curvature
K_{\mu\nu}
,
and
\tilde{g}^{00}=(1+g_{M}A_{0})^{2}g^{00}
with background value

\tilde{g}_{\rm BG}^{00}
and perturbation
\delta\tilde{g}^{00}
,
and
F_{\mu}=2n^{\nu}\nabla_{[\mu}A_{\nu]}
.
The background value of
g^{00}
is
g_{\rm BG}^{00}=-\bar{N}^{-2}
.
Unlike Ref.
Aoki et al. 2025
, we do not incorporate a direct coupling
between DE and dark matter.

Varying the EFT action with respect to the background lapse
\bar{N}
and the scale factor
a
, it follows that

\displaystyle 3M_{*}^{2}fH^{2}=\rho_{\rm DE}+\rho_{M}\,,

(2)

\displaystyle M_{*}^{2}\left(2f\dot{H}+2\dot{f}H+3fH^{2}\right)=-p_{\rm DE}-p_{M}\,,

(3)

where a dot denotes the derivative with respect to

\tilde{t}=\int\bar{N}\,{\rm d}t
,

H=({\rm d}a/{\rm d}\tilde{t})/a
, and

\displaystyle\rho_{\rm DE}

\displaystyle\equiv

\displaystyle\Lambda+\tilde{c}\bar{N}^{-2}(1+g_{M}A_{0})^{2}\,,

(4)

\displaystyle p_{\rm DE}

\displaystyle\equiv

\displaystyle-\Lambda+\tilde{c}\bar{N}^{-2}(1+g_{M}A_{0})^{2}+\dot{d}\,.

(5)

In SS Horndeski and GP theories, the EFT functions are subject to several consistency conditions
Aoki et al. 2022
, i.e.,

\displaystyle\dot{f}=0\,,

(6)

\displaystyle\dot{\Lambda}+3H\dot{d}+\dot{\tilde{c}}\,\tilde{g}^{00}_{\rm BG}=0\,,

(7)

\displaystyle\dot{d}=\frac{\bar{M}_{1}^{3}[3\bar{M}_{1}^{3}\dot{H}-2\dot{\tilde{c}}\,(1+g_{M}A_{0})^{2}\bar{N}^{-2}]}{4M_{2}^{4}}\,.

(8)

From Eq. (
6

M_{*}^{2}f\equiv M_{\rm Pl}^{2}={\rm constant}\,,

(9)

which shows the absence of a nonminimal coupling.
In GP theories, variation of
{\cal S}
with respect to
A_{0}
yields

\tilde{c}\,(1+g_{M}A_{0})=0
.
The EFT setup based on a preferred vector field
requires
1+g_{M}A_{0}\neq 0

\tilde{c}=0\,,\qquad({\rm GP~theories})\,.

(10)

Since
g_{M}A_{0}=0

2
)
and (
3

7

(a^{3}\tilde{c}/\bar{N})^{\cdot}=0

(11)

with constant
\tilde{c}_{0}
.
Note that
\tilde{c}=0

found in Ref.
De Felice and Tsujikawa 2010
.
Since
\tilde{c}
decreases to 0 in an

Taking the sum of Eqs. (
4
) and (
5
) and
using Eq. (
8

\rho_{\rm DE}+p_{\rm DE}=12M_{\rm Pl}^{2}\frac{\alpha_{B}^{2}}{\alpha_{K}}\dot{H}+\frac{2}{\bar{N}^{2}}\left(\tilde{c}+\frac{2\dot{\tilde{c}}\,\alpha_{B}}{H\alpha_{K}}\right)(1+g_{M}A_{0})^{2},

In GP theories, the last term in Eq. (
12

A consistent background solution corresponds to a DE equation of state
w_{\rm DE}\equiv p_{\rm DE}/\rho_{\rm DE}<-1

\rho_{\rm DE}>0
,
\dot{H}<0
,
and
\alpha_{K}>0

The ghost-free condition for scalar perturbations in the small-scale

Q_{s}=\alpha_{K}+6\alpha_{B}^{2}>0

;
Aoki et al. 2025

Therefore, as long as

.
To realize a phantom-divide crossing from
w_{\rm DE}<-1
to
w_{\rm DE}>-1

\rho_{\rm DE}>0
and
\dot{H}<0
, the parameter

must cross zero.
However, this would cause

\rho_{\rm DE}+p_{\rm DE}

If

\rho_{\rm DE}+p_{\rm DE}

\dot{H}
.
In this case, however,

These arguments illustrate the difficulty of crossing

In SS Horndeski theories,
\tilde{c}

decreases to 0 according to Eq. (
11

so the late-time cosmological evolution

To realize the behavior

The ghost-free condition in Horndeski theories

Aoki et al. 2022
;
Aoki et al. 2025

where

Using the dictionary between EFT functions and Horndeski couplings

;
Aoki et al. 2025

\alpha_{K}
and
\alpha_{B}

with integer

.
Since
\Omega_{\tilde{c}}
also vanishes at

, the crossing

implies

Thus, in both GP and SS Horndeski theories, realizing the

suggested by DESI data is difficult without introducing theoretical pathologies.

III
Models with the phantom divide crossing

As discussed in Sec.
II
, realizing the phantom-divide crossing
requires breaking the shift symmetry,
which can be achieved by introducing a scalar potential
V(\phi)
. We propose a model
with the four-dimensional Lagrangian

{\cal L}=\frac{M_{\rm Pl}^{2}}{2}R+a_{1}X+a_{2}X^{2}+3a_{3}X\square\phi-V(\phi)\,,

(14)

with
X=-(1/2)\partial_{\mu}\phi\partial^{\mu}\phi
, where

a_{1}
,
a_{2}
, and
a_{3}
are constants.
The SS GGC model
Peirone et al. 2019

corresponds to the limit
V(\phi)\to 0
.
The Lagrangian (
14
) involves no nonminimal coupling,
so
\dot{f}=0
in the EFT language.
Since the shift symmetry is broken,
the properties (
11
) and (
12
) no longer hold.

To analyze the background dynamics,
we introduce the dimensionless variables

\displaystyle x_{1}\equiv\frac{\dot{\phi}}{\sqrt{6}M_{\rm Pl}H}\,,\qquad x_{2}\equiv\frac{a_{2}\dot{\phi}^{4}}{4M_{\rm Pl}^{2}H^{2}}\,,

\displaystyle x_{3}\equiv-\frac{3a_{3}\dot{\phi}^{3}}{M_{\rm Pl}^{2}H}\,,\qquad x_{4}\equiv\frac{V(\phi)}{3M_{\rm Pl}^{2}H^{2}}\,.

(15)

In the matter sector, we include nonrelativistic matter (energy density
\rho_{m}
,
negligible pressure) and radiation (energy density
\rho_{r}
, pressure
p_{r}=\rho_{r}/3
).
Then, the background equations of motion are of the
forms (
2
) and (
3
), with
M_{*}^{2}f=M_{\rm Pl}^{2}
,

\rho_{M}=\rho_{m}+\rho_{r}
,
p_{M}=\rho_{r}/3
, and

\displaystyle\rho_{\rm DE}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 3M_{\rm Pl}^{2}H^{2}\left(a_{1}x_{1}^{2}+x_{2}+x_{3}+x_{4}\right)\,,

(16)

\displaystyle p_{\rm DE}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle M_{\rm Pl}^{2}H^{2}\left(3a_{1}x_{1}^{2}+x_{2}-x_{3}\epsilon_{\phi}-3x_{4}\right)\,,

(17)

where
\epsilon_{\phi}\equiv\ddot{\phi}/(H\dot{\phi})
.
Then, the DE equation of state is given by

w_{\rm DE}=\frac{p_{\rm DE}}{\rho_{\rm DE}}=\frac{3a_{1}x_{1}^{2}+x_{2}-x_{3}\epsilon_{\phi}-3x_{4}}{3(a_{1}x_{1}^{2}+x_{2}+x_{3}+x_{4})}\,.

(18)

The dimensionless variables defined
in Eq. (
15
) and the radiation
density parameter
\Omega_{r}=\rho_{r}/(3M_{\rm Pl}^{2}H^{2})
satisfy

\displaystyle x_{1}^{\prime}=x_{1}\left(\epsilon_{\phi}-h\right)\,,\qquad x_{2}^{\prime}=2x_{2}\left(2\epsilon_{\phi}-h\right)\,,

\displaystyle x_{3}^{\prime}=x_{3}\left(3\epsilon_{\phi}-h\right)\,,\quad~\,x_{4}^{\prime}=-x_{4}(\sqrt{6}\lambda x_{1}+2h)\,,

\displaystyle\Omega_{r}^{\prime}=-2\Omega_{r}(2+h)\,,

where
h\equiv\dot{H}/H^{2}
,

\lambda\equiv-(M_{\rm Pl}/V)({\rm d}V/{\rm d}\phi)
, and a prime represents the derivative with respect to
\ln a
.
Differentiating Eq. (
2
) with respect to
t
and combining it with Eq. (
3
) yields expressions
that determine
\epsilon_{\phi}
and

.
From Eq. (
2

(20)

Since the Lagrangian (
14
)
belongs to a subclass of
Horndeski theories with luminal GW speed

the second-order action of tensor perturbations is identical to

The ghost-free condition for the

The scalar perturbation is free from Laplacian instabilities if

In what follows, we focus on the case of constant
\lambda
, i.e., the exponential potential
Ratra and Peebles 1988
;
Copeland et al. 1998

V(\phi)=V_{0}e^{-\lambda\phi/M_{\rm Pl}}\,,

Provided that the inequality

IV
Conclusions

We have shown that breaking the shift symmetry in Horndeski theories is crucial for realizing a crossing of
w_{\rm DE}=-1
at low redshifts.
If future observations, including those from the Euclid satellite
Amendola et al. 2018
, were to firmly confirm the phantom-divide crossing, both SS Horndeski and GP theories could be ruled out as viable DE models.

We proposed an explicit model for the phantom-divide crossing, described by the
Lagrangian (
14
), in which the potential
V(\phi)
is incorporated into the GGC model to break the shift symmetry.
As seen in case (a) of Fig.
1
, even the model with
a_{2}=0
allows a crossing of
w_{\rm DE}=-1
, albeit with a moderate deviation of
w_{\rm DE}
from
-1
.
While we have adopted the exponential potential (
23
), one may also consider a linear potential,
V(\phi)=m^{3}\phi
, for
a_{2}=0
, which preserves Galilean symmetry in the
flat-spacetime limit
Nicolis et al. 2009
.
In cases (b) and (c) of Fig.
1
, corresponding to models with
a_{2}\neq 0
, the crossing of
w_{\rm DE}=-1
can occur in the redshift range

0<z_{c}<1
, with larger deviations of
w_{\rm DE}
from
-1
than in case (a).
We also showed that the models in cases (a)-(c) exhibit suppressed cosmic growth rates at low redshifts compared to the tracker solution of covariant Galileons, while allowing the possibility of positive ISW-galaxy
cross-correlations.

Since our model has neither nonminimal couplings nor direct couplings of DE to baryons,
we do not need to worry about fifth-force propagation in overdense regions.
Unlike nonminimally coupled DE models,
such as those in
f(R)
gravity
Hu and Sawicki 2007
;
Brax et al. 2008
,
this framework permits larger deviations of

w_{\rm DE}
from
-1
both before and after the phantom-divide crossing.
It is therefore of interest to constrain the model using DESI data in combination with other observations.

In our model, the DE equation of state approaches
a positive value
w_{\rm DE}=(3-\Omega_{r})/12
in the asymptotic past,
similar to covariant Galileons.
This high-redshift evolution can be tested with CMB constraints on
the sound horizon at recombination.
The Galileon Lagrangian can be generalized
to the form
X^{p}\square\phi
to accommodate tracker solutions with

-2<w_{\rm DE}<-1
during the matter era
De Felice and Tsujikawa 2012
.
By including the potential
V(\phi)
in these extended Galileon DE models,
it should be possible to realize the
phantom-divide crossing at late times with a negative asymptotic value of
w_{\rm DE}

in the past
(e.g., in the range
-1.5<w_{\rm DE}<-1
).
Such extensions deserve further study, along with their observational constraints.
