49 C.F.R. § 194.3 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:49:194.3
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-194.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 194 (Response Plans For Onshore Oil Pipelines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to an operator of an onshore oil pipeline that, because of its location, could reasonably be expected to cause substantial harm, or significant and substantial harm to the environment by discharging oil into or on any navigable waters of the United States or adjoining shorelines.
