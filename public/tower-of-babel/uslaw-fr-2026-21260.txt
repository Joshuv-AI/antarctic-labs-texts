Television Broadcasting Services Des Moines, Iowa

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document proposes to amend the Table of TV Allotments 
(Table) of the Federal Communications Commission's (Commission) rules 
in response to a petition for rulemaking filed by Iowa Public 
Broadcasting Board (Licensee), the licensee of noncommercial 
educational PBS member television station KDIN-TV (KDIN-TV), Des 
Moines, Iowa (Des Moines). The Licensee holds a construction permit 
(CP) to construct a facility on UHF channel *34 at Des Moines, and now 
requests to substitute VHF channel *11 for UHF channel *34 in the 
Table, with the technical parameters as set forth in KDIN-TV's current 
license. The Media Bureau previously granted a petition for rulemaking 
submitted by the Licensee to substitute UHF channel *34 in place of VHF 
channel *11 at Des Moines. The Licensee states that it is now unable to 
complete construction of the channel *34 facility within the time 
specified on the construction permit, and therefore

[[Page 66070]]

requests amendment of the Table to allow it to continue to operate on 
VHF channel *11 at its currently licensed technical parameters to 
provide uninterrupted service to viewers. Given that the Licensee 
proposes to utilize its currently licensed parameters, we believe VHF 
channel *11 can be substituted for UHF channel *34 at Des Moines as 
proposed, in compliance with the principal community coverage 
requirements. In addition, we find that this channel change meets the 
technical requirements as well as the Commission's interference 
protection requirements.
