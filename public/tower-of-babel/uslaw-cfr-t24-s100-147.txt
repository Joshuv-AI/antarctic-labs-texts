24 C.F.R. § 100.147 — Adjudication.
Citation: us:cfr:24:100.147
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Equal Opportunity, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-100.147
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 100 (Discriminatory Conduct Under The Fair Housing Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An aggrieved person, complainant, department or agency that challenges a privilege asserted under § 100.144 may seek a determination of the existence and application of that privilege in:
(a) A court of competent jurisdiction; or
(b) An administrative law proceeding with appropriate jurisdiction.
