20 C.F.R. § 336.16 — Notice to employee.
Citation: us:cfr:20:336.16
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-336.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 336 (Duration Of Normal And Extended Benefits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon determining that an employee is eligible for a period of extended unemployment or sickness benefits, the Board will notify the employee of the beginning and ending dates of such extended benefit period.
