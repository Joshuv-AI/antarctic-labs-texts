7 C.F.R. § 22.309 — Seeking Federal review.
Citation: us:cfr:7:22.309
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-22.309
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 22 (Rural Development Coordination). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

States may, if they elect, submit multicounty development plans and proposals to the Federal Regional Councils and to the Rural Development Service, USDA, for review and comment. Such review will neither obligate the Federal government with respect to such programs nor require States to conform with suggestions supplied by the USDA or the Federal Regional Council.
