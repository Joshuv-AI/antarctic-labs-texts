7 C.F.R. § 1423.12 — Application, inspection, and annual agreement fees.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1423.12
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1423.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1423 (Commodity Credit Corporation Approved Warehouses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each warehouse operator not licensed under USWA shall pay to CCC a fee or fees, including an application fee, inspection fee, and an annual agreement fee for each warehouse approved by CCC or for which approval is sought. The terms and conditions of such fees will be set forth in the applicable agreement.
