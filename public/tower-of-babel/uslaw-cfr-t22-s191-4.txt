22 C.F.R. § 191.4 — Notification of eligible persons.
Citation: us:cfr:22:191.4
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-191.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 191 (Hostage Relief Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Assistant Secretary of State for Administration shall be responsible for notifying each individual determined to be eligible for benefits under the Act or, if that person is not available, a representative or Family Member of the hostage.
