15 U.S.C. § 3211 — Relationship to other authority
Citation: us:usc:15:3211
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section3211
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 59 (Retail Policies For Natural Gas Utilities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to limit or affect any authority of the Secretary or the Commission under any other provision of law.
