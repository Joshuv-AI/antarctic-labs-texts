24 C.F.R. § 891.620 — Overcrowded and underoccupied units.
Citation: us:cfr:24:891.620
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary For Housing-Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development (Section 8 Housing Assistance Programs, Section 202 Direct Loan Program, Section 202 Supportive Housing For The Elderly Program And Section 811 Supportive Housing For Persons With Disabilities Program)
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 891 (Supportive Housing For The Elderly And Persons With Disabilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Borrower determines that because of change in family size, an assisted unit is smaller than appropriate for the eligible family to which it is leased, or that the assisted unit is larger than appropriate, housing assistance payments or project assistance payments (as applicable) with respect to the unit will not be reduced or terminated until the eligible family has been relocated to an appropriate alternate unit. If possible, the Borrower will, as promptly as possible, offer the family an appropriate alternate unit. The Borrower may receive vacancy payments for the vacated unit if the Borrower complies with the requirements of § 891.650.
