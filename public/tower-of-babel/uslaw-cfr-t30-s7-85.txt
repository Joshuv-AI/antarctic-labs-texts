30 C.F.R. § 7.85 — Critical characteristics.
Citation: us:cfr:30:7.85
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-7.85
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 7 (Testing By Applicant Or Third Party). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following critical characteristics shall be inspected or tested on each diesel engine to which an approval marking is affixed—
(a) Fuel rate is set properly; and
(b) Fuel injection pump adjustment is sealed, if applicable.
