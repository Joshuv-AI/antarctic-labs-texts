30 U.S.C. § 1027 — Land subject to prohibition on leasing
Citation: us:usc:30:1027
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section1027
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 23 (Geothermal Resources). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall not issue any lease under this chapter on those lands subject to the prohibition provided under section 226–3 of this title.
