23 C.F.R. § 1.5 — Information furnished by State highway departments.
Citation: us:cfr:23:1.5
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-1.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 1 (General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At the request of the Administrator the State highway department shall furnish to him such information as the Administrator shall deem desirable in administering the Federal-aid highway program.
