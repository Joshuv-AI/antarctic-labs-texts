39 C.F.R. § 3010.152 — Notices initiating dockets for consideration of negotiated service agreements.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3010.152
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3010.152
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3010 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Office of the General Counsel shall issue a notice to initiate a docket for each request that proposes the addition or removal of a negotiated service agreement from the market dominant or the competitive product list, or the modification of an existing product currently appearing on the market dominant or the competitive product list. Multiple requests may be combined into a single notice.
(b) The document shall specify:
(1) The docket number associated with each Postal Service request;
(2) The title of each Postal Service request;
(3) The request's acceptance date;
(4) The legal authority cited by the Postal Service for each request;
(5) The appointment of an officer of the Commission to represent the interests of the general public in the proceeding, unless the proceeding is not a public proceeding;
(6) The comment deadline pertaining to each request, unless the proceeding is not a public proceeding.
(c) The document shall be published in the Federal Register.
