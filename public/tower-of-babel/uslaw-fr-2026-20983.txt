SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Disaster Declaration #21946 and #21947; GUAM Disaster Number GU-20002]


Presidential Declaration of a Major Disaster for the Territory of 
GUAM

AGENCY: U.S. Small Business Administration.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This is notice of the Presidential declaration of a major 
disaster for the Territory of Guam (FEMA-4946-DR), dated October 7, 
2026.
    Incident: Super Typhoon Bavi.

DATES: Issued on October 7, 2026.
    Incident Period: July 4, 2026 through July 12, 2026.
    Physical Loan Application Deadline Date: December 7, 2026.
    Economic Injury (EIDL) Loan Application Deadline Date: July 7, 
2027.

ADDRESSES: Visit the MySBA Loan Portal at https://lending.sba.gov to 
apply for a disaster assistance loan.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Milton Murphy, Office of Disaster 
Recovery, U.S. Small Business Administration, 409 3rd Street SW, Suite 
6050, Washington, DC 20416, (202) 205-6734.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Notice is hereby given as a result of the 
President's major disaster declaration on October 7, 2026, applications 
for disaster loans may be submitted online using the MySBA Loan Portal 
https://lending.sba.gov or in person at other locally announced 
locations. For further assistance please contact the SBA disaster 
assistance customer service center by email at 
[email protected] or by

[[Page 65288]]

phone at 1-800-659-2955. If you are deaf, hard of hearing or have a 
speech disability, please dial 7-1-1 to access telecommunications relay 
services.
