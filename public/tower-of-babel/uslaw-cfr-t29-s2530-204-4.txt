29 C.F.R. § 2530.204-4 — -4 Deferral of benefit accrual.
Citation: us:cfr:29:2530.204-4
Creator: Employee Benefits Security Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-2530.204-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 2530 (Rules And Regulations For Minimum Standards For Employee Pension Benefit Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of section 204(b)(1)(E) of the Act and section 411(b)(1)(E) of the Code (which permit deferral of benefit accrual until an employee has 2 continuous years of service), an employee shall be credited with a year of service for each computation period in which he or she completes 1,000 hours of service. The computation period shall be the eligibility computation period designated in accordance with § 2530.202-2.
