28 C.F.R. § 55.9 — Coverage of political units within a county.
Citation: us:cfr:28:55.9
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-55.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 55 (Implementation Of The Provisions Of The Voting Rights Act Regarding Language Minority Groups). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where a political subdivision (e.g., a county) is determined to be subject to section 4(f)(4) or section 203(c), all political units that hold elections within that political subdivision (e.g., cities, school districts) are subject to the same requirements as the political subdivision.
