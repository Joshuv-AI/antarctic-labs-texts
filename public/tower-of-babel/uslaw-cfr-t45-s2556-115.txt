45 C.F.R. § 2556.115 — Is a VISTA sponsor required to provide a cash or in-kind match?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2556.115
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2556.115
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2556 (Volunteers In Service To America). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A sponsor is not required to provide a cash match for any of the assistance listed in § 2556.110.
(b) A sponsor must provide supervision, workspace, service-related transportation, and any other materials necessary to operate and complete the VISTA project and support the VISTA.
