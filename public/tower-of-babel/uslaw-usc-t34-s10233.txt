34 U.S.C. § 10233 — Prohibition on land acquisition
Citation: us:usc:34:10233
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section10233
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 101 (Justice System Improvement); Subchapter VII (Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No funds under this chapter shall be used for land acquisition.
