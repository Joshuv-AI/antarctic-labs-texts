43 U.S.C. § 422k — Supplement to Federal reclamation laws; short title
Citation: us:usc:43:422k
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section422k
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 12 (Reclamation And Irrigation Of Lands By Federal Government); Subchapter IV (Construction Of Small Projects). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This subchapter shall be a supplement to the Federal reclamation laws and may be cited as the Small Reclamation Projects Act of 1956.
