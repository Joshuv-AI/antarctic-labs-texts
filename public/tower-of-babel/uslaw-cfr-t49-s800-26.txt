49 C.F.R. § 800.26 — Delegation to the Chief, Public Inquiries Branch.
Citation: us:cfr:49:800.26
Creator: National Transportation Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-800.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 800 (Administrative Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board delegates to the Chief, Public Inquiries Branch, the authority to determine, initially, the withholding of a Board record from inspection or copying, pursuant to part 801 of this chapter.
