50 C.F.R. § 665.421 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:50:665.421
Creator: Fishery Conservation And Management, National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-665.421
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 665 (Fisheries In The Western Pacific). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in §§ 665.420 through 665.439, Mariana coral reef ecosystem component species (Mariana coral reef ECS) are those species identified in the Marianas Archipelago FEP and are not defined as MUS or another ECS in this subpart.
