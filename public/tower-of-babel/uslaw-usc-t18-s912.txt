18 U.S.C. § 912 — Officer or employee of the United States
Citation: us:usc:18:912
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section912
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 43 (False Personation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever falsely assumes or pretends to be an officer or employee acting under the authority of the United States or any department, agency or officer thereof, and acts as such, or in such pretended character demands or obtains any money, paper, document, or thing of value, shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than three years, or both.
