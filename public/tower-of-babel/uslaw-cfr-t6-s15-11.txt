6 C.F.R. § 15.11 — Notice.
Citation: us:cfr:6:15.11
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security, Office Of The Secretary
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-6/section-15.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 6 (Domestic Security), Part 15 (Enforcement Of Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Disability In Programs Or Activities Conducted By The Department Of Homeland Security). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department shall make available to all Department employees and interested persons information regarding the provisions of this part and its applicability to the programs or activities conducted by the Department, and make such information available to them in such a manner as is necessary to apprise them of the protections against discrimination assured them by section 504 and this part.
