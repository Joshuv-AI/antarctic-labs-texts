41 C.F.R. § 128-1.5002-7 — -1.5002-7 Property management officer (PMO).
Citation: us:cfr:41:128-1.5002-7
Creator: Chapter 128—Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-128-1.5002-7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 128-1 (1—Introduction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An individual responsible for the overall administration, coordination, and control of the personal property management program of a bureau. The designation as PMO may or may not correspond to the individual's official job title.
