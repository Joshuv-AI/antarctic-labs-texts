15 C.F.R. § 2.6 — Payment of claims.
Citation: us:cfr:15:2.6
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-2.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 2 (Procedures For Handling And Settlement Of Claims Under The Federal Tort Claims Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When an award is made, the file on the case shall be transmitted to the appropriate fiscal office for payment by the Department or for transmittal for payment as prescribed by § 14.10 of the Regulations (28 CFR Part 14). Prior to payment appropriate releases shall be obtained, as provided in said section.
