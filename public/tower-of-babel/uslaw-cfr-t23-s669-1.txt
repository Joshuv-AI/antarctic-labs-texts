23 C.F.R. § 669.1 — Scope and purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:23:669.1
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-669.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 669 (Enforcement Of Heavy Vehicle Use Tax). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To prescribe requirements for certification by the states that evidence of proof of payment is obtained either before vehicles subject to the Federal heavy vehicle use tax are lawfully registered or within 4 months after being lawfully registered if a suspension registration system is implemented.
