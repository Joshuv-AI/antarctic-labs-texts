25 C.F.R. § 273.129 — May the BIE Director reimburse a public school district for educating non-resident Indian students?
Citation: us:cfr:25:273.129
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-273.129
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 273 (Education Contracts Under Johnson-O'Malley Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The BIE Director may consider a contract proposal to reimburse a public school district for the full per capita costs of educating Indian students who meet all of the following:
(a) Are members of recognized Indian Tribes;
(b) Do not normally reside in the State in which the school district is located; and
(c) Are residing in Federal boarding facilities for the purpose of attending public schools within the school district.
