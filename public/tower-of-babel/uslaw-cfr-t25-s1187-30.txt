25 C.F.R. § 1187.30 — How will OIED disburse the grant funds to awardees?
Citation: us:cfr:25:1187.30
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary, Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-1187.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 1187 (Indian Business Incubators Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

OIED will disburse grant funds awarded to eligible applicants in annual installments except that, OIED may make disbursements more frequently, on request by the applicant, as long as disbursements are not made more frequently than quarterly.
