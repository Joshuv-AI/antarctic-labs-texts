28 U.S.C. § 175 — Official duty station; residence
Citation: us:usc:28:175
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section175
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part I (Organization Of Courts); Chapter 7 (United States Court Of Federal Claims). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The official duty station of each judge of the United States Court of Federal Claims is the District of Columbia.
(b)
After appointment and while in active service, each judge shall reside within fifty miles of the District of Columbia.
(c)
Retired judges of the Court of Federal Claims are not subject to restrictions as to residence. The place where a retired judge maintains the actual abode in which such judge customarily lives shall be deemed to be the judge’s official duty station for the purposes of section 456 of this title.
