21 C.F.R. § 111.520 — When may a returned dietary supplement be salvaged?
Citation: us:cfr:21:111.520
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-111.520
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 111 (Current Good Manufacturing Practice In Manufacturing, Packaging, Labeling, Or Holding Operations For Dietary Supplements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may salvage a returned dietary supplement only if quality control personnel conduct a material review and make a disposition decision to allow the salvage.
