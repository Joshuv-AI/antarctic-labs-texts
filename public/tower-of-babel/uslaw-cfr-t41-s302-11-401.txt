41 C.F.R. § 302-11.401 — -11.401 Authorizing an extension of time.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-11.401
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-11.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-11 (11—Allowances For Expenses Incurred In Connection With Residence Transactions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When authorizing an extension of time limitation, agencies must determine that the:
(a) Employee has extenuating circumstances which have prevented them from completing the sale and purchase or lease termination transactions in the initial authorized time frame of one year; and
(b) The employee's residence transactions are reasonably related to the transfer of the official station.
