43 U.S.C. § 617s — Recognition of rights of Mexico to Colorado River waters
Citation: us:usc:43:617s
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section617s
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 12A (Boulder Canyon Project); Subchapter I (Boulder Canyon Project Act). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subchapter shall be construed as a denial or recognition of any rights, if any, in Mexico to the use of the waters of the Colorado River system.
