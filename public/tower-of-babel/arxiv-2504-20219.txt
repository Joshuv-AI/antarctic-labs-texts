Abstract




The new combinatorial identities of symmetric functions

In this paper, we provide some novel
binomial convolution related to symmetric functions, as well as convolution sums without the binomial symbol. Moreover we give some new convolution sums of Bernoulli, Euler, and Genocchi numbers and polynomials with symmetric functions
, by making use of the
elementary methods including exponential generating functions. From these
convolutions we deduce several new combinatorial identities for bivariate polynomials, and
we establish several new identities related Bernoulli, Euler and Genocchi numbers and polynomials with certain bivariate polynomials such as bivariate Fibonacci, bivariate Lucas, bivariate balancing and bivariate balancing-Lucas polynomials.

1.
Introduction and preliminaries

Symmetric functions are fundamental in several areas of mathematics, such as combinatorics, algebra, and representation theory. The concept of symmetry is essential for analysis and plays a key role in uncovering new insights and relationships.
A symmetric function is a formal power series in the variables

\{x_{1},x_{2},...,x_{n}\}

that does not change when the variables are rearranged in any order. This means the function remains unchanged under any permutation of its variables.
For instance,

f\{x_{1},x_{2},...,x_{n}\}
is a symmetric function if for every permutation
\alpha
of the set
\{x_{1},x_{2},...,x_{n}\}
, the following relationship is holds:

\sigma f(x_{1},x_{2},...x_{n})=f(x_{\sigma(1)},x_{\sigma(2)},...x_{\sigma(n)})=f(x_{1},x_{2},...x_{n}).

New theorems

Theorem 1
.

Given an alphabet
A=\{x_{1},x_{2}\}
, then for any positive integer
n
, we
have

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}S_{n-k-1}(x_{1}+x_{2})S_{k-1}(x_{1}+x_{2})=\frac{1}{(x_{1}-x_{2})^{2}}\left(2^{n}\phi_{n}(x_{1}+x_{2})-2(x_{1}+x_{2})^{n}\right).

(2.1)

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\phi_{n-k}(x_{1}+x_{2})\phi_{k}(x_{1}+x_{2})=2^{n}\phi_{n}(x_{1}+x_{2})+2(x_{1}+x_{2})^{n}.

(2.2)

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}S_{n-k-1}(x_{1}+x_{2})\phi_{k}(x_{1}+x_{2})=2^{n}S_{n-1}(x_{1}+x_{2}).

(2.3)

Proof.

We square both sides of the exponential generating function associated with the symmetric function
S_{k-1}(x_{1}+x_{2})
, we obtain

\left(\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}S_{n-1}(x_{1}+x_{2})\frac{z^{n}}{n!}\right)\left(\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}S_{k-1}(x_{1}+x_{2})\frac{z^{n}}{n!}\right)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}S_{n-k-1}(x_{1}+x_{2})S_{k-1}(x_{1}+x_{2})\frac{z^{n}}{n!},

and

\displaystyle\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}-x_{2}}(\exp(x_{1}z)-\exp(x_{2}z))\right)^{2}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{(x_{1}-x_{2})^{2}}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(2^{n}(x_{1}^{n}+x_{2}^{n})-2(x_{1}+x_{2})^{n}\right)\frac{z^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{(x_{1}-x_{2})^{2}}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(2^{n}\phi_{n}(x_{1}+x_{2})-2(x_{1}+x_{2})^{n}\right)\frac{z^{n}}{n!}.

Comparing of the coefficients of
\frac{z^{n}}{n!}
, we obtain

\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}S_{n-k-1}(x_{1}+x_{2})S_{k-1}(x_{1}+x_{2})=\frac{1}{(x_{1}+x_{2})^{2}}\left(2^{n}\phi_{n}(x_{1}+x_{2})-2(x_{1}+x_{2})^{n}\right).

Similarly, we square both sides of the exponential generating function associated with the symmetric function
\phi_{n}(x_{1}+x_{2})
we obtain the equation (
2.2
). As for the relationship it is the result of the product of the exponential generating function of symmetric function
S_{n-1}(x_{1}+x_{2})
and the exponential generating function of symmetric function
\phi_{n}(x_{1}+x_{2})
.

∎

Theorem 2
.

Given an alphabet
A=\{x_{1},x_{2}\}
, then for any positive integer
n
, we
have

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{n}\phi_{k}(x_{1}+x_{2})\phi_{n-k}(x_{1}+x_{2})=(n+1)\phi_{n}(x_{1}+x_{2})+2S_{n}(x_{1}+x_{2}).

(2.4)

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{n}S_{k-1}(x_{1}+x_{2})S_{n-k-1}(x_{1}+x_{2})=\frac{1}{(x_{1}-x_{2})^{2}}\left((n+1)\phi_{n}(x_{1}+x_{2})-2S_{n}(x_{1}+x_{2})\right).

(2.5)

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{n}\phi_{k}(x_{1}+x_{2})S_{n-k-1}(x_{1}+x_{2})=(n+1)S_{n-1}(x_{1}+x_{2}).

(2.6)

Proof.

We prove only the first relation; the proof for the other relations follows in the same manner using the definition of the two symmetric functions
S_{n-1}(x_{1}+x_{2})
and
\phi_{n}(x_{1}+x_{2})
.

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{n}\phi_{k}(x_{1}+x_{2})\phi_{n-k}(x_{1}+x_{2})

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{n}(x_{1}^{k}+x_{2}^{k})(x_{1}^{n-k}+x_{2}^{n-k})

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{n}(x_{1}^{n}+x_{1}^{k}x_{2}^{n-k}+x_{2}^{k}x_{1}^{n-k}+x_{2}^{n})

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{n}(x_{1}^{n}+x_{2}^{n})+x_{2}^{n}\sum_{k=0}^{n}\left(\frac{x_{1}}{x_{2}}\right)^{k}+x_{1}^{n}\sum_{k=0}^{n}\left(\frac{x_{2}}{x_{1}}\right)^{k}

\displaystyle=(n+1)(x_{1}^{n}+x_{2}^{n})+x_{2}^{n}\frac{\left(\frac{x_{1}}{x_{2}}\right)^{n+1}-1}{\frac{x_{1}}{x_{2}}-1}+x_{1}^{n}\frac{\left(\frac{x_{2}}{x_{1}}\right)^{n+1}-1}{\frac{x_{2}}{x_{1}}-1}

\displaystyle=(n+1)(x_{1}^{n}+x_{2}^{n})+2\frac{x_{1}^{n+1}+x_{2}^{n+1}}{x_{1}-x_{2}}

\displaystyle=(n+1)\phi_{n}(x_{1}+x_{2})+2S_{n}(x_{1}+x_{2}).

The proof is now complete.
∎
