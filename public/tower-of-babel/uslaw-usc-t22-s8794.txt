22 U.S.C. § 8794 — Waiver
Citation: us:usc:22:8794
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section8794
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 94 (Iran Threat Reduction And Syria Human Rights); Subchapter VII (Sanctions With Respect To Human Rights Abuses In Syria). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may waive the requirement to include a person on a list required by section 8791, 8792, or 8793 of this title or to impose sanctions pursuant to any such section if the President—
(1)
determines that such a waiver is in the national security interests of the United States; and
(2)
submits to the appropriate congressional committees a report on the reasons for that determination.
