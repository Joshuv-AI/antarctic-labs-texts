16 U.S.C. § 821 — State laws and water rights unaffected
Citation: us:usc:16:821
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section821
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 12 (Federal Regulation And Development Of Power); Subchapter I (Regulation Of The Development Of Water Power And Resources). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing contained in this chapter shall be construed as affecting or intending to affect or in any way to interfere with the laws of the respective States relating to the control, appropriation, use, or distribution of water used in irrigation or for municipal or other uses, or any vested right acquired therein.
