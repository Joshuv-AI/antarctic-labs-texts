21 C.F.R. § 173.210 — Acetone.
Citation: us:cfr:21:173.210
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-173.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 173 (Secondary Direct Food Additives Permitted In Food For Human Consumption). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A tolerance of 30 parts per million is established for acetone in spice oleoresins when present therein as a residue from the extraction of spice.
