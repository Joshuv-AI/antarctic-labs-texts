16 U.S.C. § 6882 — Data standardization
Citation: us:usc:16:6882
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section6882
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 87C (Modernizing Access To Public Oceans). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 31 months after September 25, 2026, the Secretary, in consultation with relevant stakeholders, including State and local governments, Indian Tribes, and Native Hawaiian organizations, shall develop and adopt standards with respect to the coordination and dissemination of geospatial data relating to fishing restrictions, use of the exclusive economic zone by recreational vessels, and access to Federal waters by such vessels, including such data made available under section 6883 of this title.
