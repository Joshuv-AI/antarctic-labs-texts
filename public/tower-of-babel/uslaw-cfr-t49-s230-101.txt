49 C.F.R. § 230.101 — Steam locomotive driving journal boxes.
Citation: us:cfr:49:230.101
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-230.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 230 (Steam Locomotive Inspection And Maintenance Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Driving journal boxes. Driving journal boxes shall be maintained in a safe and suitable condition for service. Not more than one shim may be used between the box and bearing.
(b) Broken bearings. Broken bearings shall be renewed.
(c) Loose bearings. Loose bearings shall be repaired or renewed.
