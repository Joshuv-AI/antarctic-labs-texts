16 C.F.R. § 1608.4 — Guaranties furnished by nonresidents of the U.S. no bar to prosecution.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1608.4
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1608.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1608 (General Rules And Regulations Under The Flammable Fabrics Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A guaranty furnished under section 8 of the act by a person who is not a resident of the United States may not be relied upon as a bar to prosecution under section 7 of the act for a violation of section 3 of the act.
