25 C.F.R. § 273.128 — How are contracts prioritized?
Citation: us:cfr:25:273.128
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-273.128
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 273 (Education Contracts Under Johnson-O'Malley Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Priority will be given to contracts:
(a) Which would serve Indian students on or near reservations; and
(b) Where a majority of the Indian students will be members of the Tribe(s) of those reservations.
