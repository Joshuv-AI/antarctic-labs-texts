24 C.F.R. § 81.66 — Submission of reports.
Citation: us:cfr:24:81.66
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-81.66
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 81 (The Secretary Of Hud'S Regulation Of The Federal National Mortgage Association (Fannie Mae) And The Federal Home Loan Mortgage Corporation (Freddie Mac)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each GSE shall submit all hard copy reports or other written information required under this subpart to the Secretary and the Director, Office of Government-Sponsored Enterprises. Each GSE shall submit computerized data required under this subpart to the Director, Financial Institutions Regulations, Office of Policy Development and Research. The address for both of these offices is Department of Housing and Urban Development, 451 7th Street, SW. Washington, DC 20410.
