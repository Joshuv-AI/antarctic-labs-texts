41 C.F.R. § 102-42.105 — -42.105 Destruction.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-42.105
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-42.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-42 (42—Utilization, Donation, And Disposal Of Foreign Gifts And Decorations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Foreign gifts or decorations that are not sold under this section may be destroyed and disposed of as scrap or for their material content, in accordance with part 102-38 of this subchapter.
