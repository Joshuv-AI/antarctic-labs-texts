49 U.S.C. § 41707 — Incorporating contract terms into written instrument
Citation: us:usc:49:41707
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section41707
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Air Commerce And Safety); Subpart ii (Economic Regulation); Chapter 417 (Operations Of Carriers); Subchapter I (Requirements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To the extent the Secretary of Transportation prescribes by regulation, an air carrier may incorporate by reference in a ticket or written instrument any term of the contract for providing interstate air transportation.
