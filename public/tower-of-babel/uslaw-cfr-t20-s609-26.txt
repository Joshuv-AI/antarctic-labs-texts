20 C.F.R. § 609.26 — Liaison with Department.
Citation: us:cfr:20:609.26
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-609.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 609 (Unemployment Compensation For Federal Civilian Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To facilitate the Department's administration of the UCFE Program, each Federal agency shall designate one or more of its officials to be the liaison with the Department. Each Federal agency will inform the Department of its designation(s) and of any change in a designation.
