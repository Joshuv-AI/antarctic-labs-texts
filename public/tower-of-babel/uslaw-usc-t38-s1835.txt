38 U.S.C. § 1835 — Advisory council
Citation: us:usc:38:1835
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section1835
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 18 (Benefits For Children Of Vietnam Veterans And Certain Other Veterans); Subchapter IV (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In General.—
The Secretary shall establish an advisory council on health care and benefits for covered children.
(b) Membership.—
The advisory council established under subsection (a) shall be composed of Federal employees.
(c) Duties.—
The advisory council established under subsection (a) shall solicit feedback from covered children and covered veterans on the health care and benefits provided under this chapter and communicate such feedback to the Secretary.
