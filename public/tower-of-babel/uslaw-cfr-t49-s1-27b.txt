49 C.F.R. § 1.27b — b Delegations to the Assistant General Counsel for General Law.

Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1 (Organization And Delegation Of Powers And Duties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Assistant General Counsel for General Law is delegated authority to serve as the Department's Alternate Agency Ethics Official in accordance with 5 CFR 2638.202.
