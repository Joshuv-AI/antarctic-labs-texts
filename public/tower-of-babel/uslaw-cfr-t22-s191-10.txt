22 C.F.R. § 191.10 — Eligibility for benefits.
Citation: us:cfr:22:191.10
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-191.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 191 (Hostage Relief Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person designated as a hostage under subpart A of this subchapter, other than a member of the Armed Forces covered by the provisions of the Soldiers' and Sailors' Civil Relief Act of 1940, shall be eligible for benefits under this part.
