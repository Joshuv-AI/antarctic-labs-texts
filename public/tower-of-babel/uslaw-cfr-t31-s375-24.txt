31 C.F.R. § 375.24 — Does the Treasury Department prorate offers at the highest accepted price for a particular security?
Citation: us:cfr:31:375.24
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-375.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 375 (Marketable Treasury Securities Redemption Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Offers at the highest accepted price for a particular security may be accepted on a prorated basis.
