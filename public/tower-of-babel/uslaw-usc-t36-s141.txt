36 U.S.C. § 141 — Thomas Jefferson’s birthday
Citation: us:usc:36:141
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section141
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part A (Observances And Ceremonies); Chapter 1 (Patriotic And National Observances). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President shall issue each year a proclamation—
(1)
calling on officials of the United States Government to display the flag of the United States on all Government buildings on April 13; and
(2)
inviting the people of the United States to observe April 13 in schools and churches, or other suitable places, with appropriate ceremonies in commemoration of Thomas Jefferson’s birthday.
