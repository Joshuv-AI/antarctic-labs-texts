42 U.S.C. § 10803 — Allotments
Citation: us:usc:42:10803
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section10803
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 114 (Protection And Advocacy For Individuals With Mental Illness); Subchapter I (Protection And Advocacy Systems); Part A (Establishment Of Systems). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall make allotments under this subchapter to eligible systems to establish and administer systems—
(1)
which meet the requirements of section 10805 of this title; and
(2)
which are designed to—
(A)
protect and advocate the rights of individuals with mental illness; and
(B)
investigate incidents of abuse and neglect of individuals with mental illness if the incidents are reported to the system or if there is probable cause to believe that the incidents occurred.
