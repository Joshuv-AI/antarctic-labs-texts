Correction to: Emergence and evolution of canonical microRNAs: A case study in Arabidopsis halleri and A. lyrata
Published: 2026

This is a correction to: Ng, P.Q. Emergence and evolution of canonical microRNAs: A case study in Arabidopsis halleri and A. lyrata, The Plant Cell, Volume 37, Issue 7, July 2025, koaf159, https://doi.org/10.1093/plcell/koaf159

The original article included the following misleading or inaccurate statements:

A line in paragraph 5 of the article incorrectly stated that A. thaliana is the common ancestor of A. halleri and A. lyrata. Rather, A. lyrata and A. halleri are close living relatives of A. thaliana. A. lyrata and A. thaliana diverged from a common ancestor about 5 MYA, and A. halleri and A. lyrata diverged from each other about 1 MYA.
