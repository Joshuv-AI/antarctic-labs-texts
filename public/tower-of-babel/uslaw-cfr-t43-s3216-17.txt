43 C.F.R. § 3216.17 — Where must I file estate transfers, corporate mergers, and name changes?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3216.17
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3216.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3200 (Geothermal Resource Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If you have posted a bond for any Federal lease, you must file estate transfers, corporate mergers, and name changes in the BLM State Office that maintains your bond.
(b) If you have not posted a bond, you must file estate transfers, corporate mergers, and name changes in the State Office having jurisdiction over the lease.
