Applications of the factorization theorem of conformal blocks to vertex operator algebras
Xu Gao, Jianqi Liu

Abstract
. We apply the factorization and vector bundle propositionerty of the sheaves of conformal blocks on M ¯ g , n \overline{{M}}_{g,n} . defined by vertex operator algebras (VOAs) and give geometric proofs of essential results in the representation theory of strongly rational VOAs. We first prove the associativity of the fusion tensor product by investigating four-point conformal blocks on genus zero curves. Then we give a formula to calculate the ranks of VOA-conformal block bundles. Finally, by investigating one-point conformal blocks on genus-one curves, we prove the modular invariance of intertwining operators for VOAs, which is a generalization of Zhu’s modular invariance for module vertex operators of strongly rational VOAs, and is a specialization of Huang’s recent modular invariance of logarithmic intertwining operators for C 2 C_{2} -cofinite VOAs.

Introduction

The notion of conformal blocks on stable curves defined by modules over a vertex operator algebra (VOA)
[

,

]
is a generalization of the WZNW-conformal blocks on stable curves defined by modules over affine Lie algebras
[

,

,

. The recent advances in the theory of VOA-conformal blocks shed new light on the representation theory of VOAs. A couple of fundamental theorems in the representation theory of VOAs, like the associativity of fusion tensor product and the modular invariance of intertwining operators, which were previously proven using analytic and algebraic methods in

,

,

,

,

,

In Moore-Seiberg’s formulation of two-dimensional conformal field theory (CFT)
[
MS89
]
, the associativity of fusion rules and the modular invariance of chiral vertex operators (intertwining operators) are key properties of the rational conformal field theory (RCFT). They were stated as part of the axioms of RCFT in the physics literature without a proof. Since the chiral algebra in a CFT is essentially the universal enveloping algebra of a VOA, it is widely believed that the axioms of strongly rational VOAs could give rise to the full Moore-Seiberg datum for RCFT. However, the axiom of VOA given by Borcherds
[
Bor86
]
and Frenkel-Lepowsky-Meurman
[
FLM88
]
are almost purely algebraic, motivated by the study of moonshine conjecture. It is a highly non-trivial problem to prove these axiomatic assumptions of RCFT using the VOA language. Recently, there has been some significant development in the theory of conformal blocks defined by representation of VOAs
[
DGT21
,
DG23
,
DGT24
,
DGK25
]
. For strongly rational VOAs, the factorization property and vector bundle property of the sheaves of VOA-conformal block on
\overline{{M}}_{g,n}
., which were formerly conjectured in
[
Zhu94
,
FBZ04
]
, has now been proven in
[
DGT24
]
. With these new theorems, we can provide new geometric proofs of part of the Moore-Seiberg’s axioms for RCFT from VOAs, namely, the associativity of fusion rings and modular invariance of intertwining operators for strongly rational VOAs.

Huang and Lepowsky explicitly constructed the tensor products of modules over a VOA
V

[

In the seminal paper

3.
The fusion ring of a vertex operator algebra

Let
{W}
be a set of representatives of isomorphism classes of simple
V
-module.

Definition 3.1
.

The
fusion ring

\mathfrak{F}(V)
of
V
(with respect to the basis
{W}
) is the vector space spanned by
{W}
equipped with the multiplication

W^{1}\cdot W^{2}:=\sum_{W^{3}\in W}\mathsf{N}{\textstyle\binom{W^{3}}{W^{1}\;W^{2}}}W^{3},

for all
W^{1},W^{2}\in{W}
.

3.a.
The ring structure of the fusion ring

Proposition 3.2
.

The multiplication on
\mathfrak{F}(V)
has an identity.

Proof.

For simplicity, assume
V\in W
.
It suffices to show

(3.3)

\mathsf{N}{\textstyle\binom{W^{3}}{W^{1}\;V}}=\begin{cases}1&if $W^{1}\cong W^{3}$,\\
0&otherwise.\end{cases}

By
Theorem 2.15

(Propagation of Vacua)
.
,
[W^{1}\otimes V\otimes(W^{3})^{\prime}]_{\mathbb{P}^{1},(0,1,\infty)}\cong[W^{1}\otimes(W^{3})^{\prime}]_{\mathbb{P}^{1},(0,\infty)}
.
On the other hand, the dual space of
[W^{1}\otimes(W^{3})^{\prime}]_{\mathbb{P}^{1},(0,\infty)}
is precisely
\mathrm{Hom}_{V}(W^{1},W^{3})
.
Then the statement follows.
∎

Proposition 3.4
.

The multiplication on
\mathfrak{F}(V)
is commutative.

Proof.

Need to show

(3.5)

\mathsf{N}{\textstyle\binom{W^{3}}{W^{1}\;W^{2}}}=\mathsf{N}{\textstyle\binom{W^{3}}{W^{2}\;W^{1}}}.

Since
0
and
1
are symmetric on
\mathbb{P}^{1}
(or, there is an isomorphism of
\mathbb{P}^{1}
interchange
0
and
1
, or there is an isomorphism from
(\mathbb{P}^{1},(0,1,\infty))
to
(\mathbb{P}^{1},(1,0,\infty))
), we must have
[W^{1}\otimes W^{2}\otimes(W^{3})^{\prime}]_{\mathbb{P}^{1},(0,1,\infty)}\cong[W^{2}\otimes W^{1}\otimes(W^{3})^{\prime}]_{\mathbb{P}^{1},(0,1,\infty)}
.
∎

Theorem 3.6
.

The multiplication on
\mathfrak{F}(V)
is associative.

Proof.

We need to show

(3.7)

\sum_{W\in W}\mathsf{N}{\textstyle\binom{W}{W^{1}\;W^{2}}}\mathsf{N}{\textstyle\binom{W^{4}}{W\;W^{3}}}=\sum_{W\in W}\mathsf{N}{\textstyle\binom{W}{W^{2}\;W^{3}}}\mathsf{N}{\textstyle\binom{W^{4}}{W^{1}\;W}}.

Consider the following factorization:

X_{0}
\mathsf{p}_{1}
\mathsf{p}_{2}
\mathsf{p}_{3}
\mathsf{p}_{4}
X_{1}
\mathsf{p}_{1}
\mathsf{p}_{2}
\mathsf{p}_{3}
\mathsf{p}_{4}
X_{1}^{+}
X_{1}^{-}
\mathsf{p}_{1}
\mathsf{p}_{2}
\mathsf{q}_{+}
\mathsf{p}_{3}
\mathsf{p}_{4}
\mathsf{q}_{-}

Then, by
Theorem 2.18

(VB Corollary)
.
and
Theorem 2.16

(Factorization Theorem)
.
, we have

\displaystyle\dim[W^{1}\otimes W^{2}\otimes W^{3}\otimes(W^{4})^{\prime}]_{(X_{0},\mathsf{p}_{1},\mathsf{p}_{2},\mathsf{p}_{3},\mathsf{p}_{4})}

\displaystyle=\sum_{W\in W}\dim[W^{1}\otimes W^{2}\otimes W^{\prime}]_{(X_{1}^{+},\mathsf{p}_{1},\mathsf{p}_{2},\mathsf{q}_{+})}\cdot\dim[W\otimes W^{3}\otimes(W^{4})^{\prime}]_{(X_{1}^{-},\mathsf{q}_{-},\mathsf{p}_{3},\mathsf{p}_{4})}

\displaystyle=\sum_{W\in W}\mathsf{N}{\textstyle\binom{W}{W^{1}\;W^{2}}}\mathsf{N}{\textstyle\binom{W^{4}}{W\;W^{3}}}.

On the other hand, consider the following factorization:

X_{0}
\mathsf{p}_{1}
\mathsf{p}_{2}
\mathsf{p}_{3}
\mathsf{p}_{4}

\mathsf{p}_{1}

\displaystyle=\sum_{W\in W}\dim[W^{2}\otimes W^{3}\otimes W^{\prime}]_{(X_{1}^{+},\mathsf{p}_{2},\mathsf{p}_{3},\mathsf{q}_{+})}\cdot\dim[W^{1}\otimes W\otimes(W^{4})^{\prime}]_{(X_{1}^{-},\mathsf{q}_{1},\mathsf{p}_{-},\mathsf{p}_{4})}

Coordinate change

Elliptic functions

Recall the following power series in Section 3 of

(4.3)

Lemma 4.4
.

The limit function of

satisfies

(4.5)

\displaystyle P_{1}(e^{2\pi iz},q)

\displaystyle=-\wp_{1}(z,\tau)+G_{2}(\tau)-\pi i,

(4.6)

\displaystyle P_{2}(e^{2\pi iz},q)

\displaystyle=\wp_{2}(z,\tau)+G_{2}(\tau),

(4.7)

\wp

4.b.
One-point conformal blocks on torus

Let

, we denote the elliptic curve
\mathbb{C}/(\mathbb Z+\mathbb Z\tau)

\Gamma(\mathcal{O}_{E_{\tau}},E_{\tau}\delimiter 69657359\mathfrak{q})=\mathrm{span}\{1,\wp_{k}(z,\tau):k\geq 1\}.

(4.9)

The space of coinvariant associated to

\displaystyle\langle\varphi(\tau)|(a\otimes 1).v\rangle

(4.12)

(5.1)

\mathrm{tr}|_{M}o_{I}(v)q^{L(0)-h_{M}}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\mathrm{tr}|_{M(n)}o_{I}(v)q^{n}.

Note that
I(v,w)=\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}v(n)w^{-n-1-h_{W}}.
Denote

(5.2)

\displaystyle F((v,w),q):

\displaystyle=w^{\deg v+h_{W}}\mathrm{tr}|_{M}I(v,w)q^{L(0)-h_{M}},

\displaystyle F((a,z)(v,w),q):

\displaystyle=z^{\mathrm{wt}a}w^{\deg v+h_{W}}\mathrm{tr}|_{M}Y(a,z)I(v,w)q^{L(0)-h_{M}},

which are formal power series in
q
with coefficients in
\mathbb{C}[z^{\pm 1},w^{\pm 1},(z-w)^{\pm 1}]
.
It follows from the bracket relation
[L(0),Y(a,z)]=(z\frac{d}{dz}+\mathrm{wt}a)Y(a,z)
that

(5.3)

\displaystyle Y(a,z)q^{L(0)}=q^{-\mathrm{wt}a}q^{L(0)}Y(a,zq^{-1}),

(5.4)

\displaystyle I(v,w)q^{L(0)}=q^{-(\deg v+h_{W})}q^{L(0)}I(v,wq^{-1}).

By taking residue in (
5.3
), we have

(5.5)

a(\mathrm{wt}a-1+k)q^{L(0)}=q^{L(0)}a(\mathrm{wt}a-1+k)q^{k},\quad a\in V,\ k\in\mathbb Z.

Lemma 5.6
.

For any
a\in V
and
v\in W
, we have

(5.7)

\displaystyle\sum_{i\geq 0}\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\left(\binom{\mathrm{wt}a-1+k}{i}\frac{q^{k}}{1-q^{k}}\left(\frac{w}{x}\right)^{k}+\binom{\mathrm{wt}a-1-k}{i}\frac{q^{-k}}{1-q^{-k}}\left(\frac{w}{x}\right)^{-k}\right)a(i)v

\displaystyle=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}P_{m+1}(x,q)a[m]v.

5.b.
Recursive formulas for the trace functions

We have the following recursive formulas about the trace functions (
5.2
), generalizing Propositions 4.3.2, 4.3.3 in
[
Zhu96
]
.

Proposition 5.8
.

For
a\in V
and
v\in W
, we have

(5.9)

\displaystyle F((v,w)(a,x),q)

\displaystyle=w^{\deg v+h_{W}}\mathrm{tr}|_{M}a(\mathrm{wt}a-1)I(v,w)q^{L(0)-h_{M}}-2\pi iF((a[0]v,w),q)

\displaystyle+\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}P_{m+1}\left(\frac{w}{x}q,q\right)F((a[m]v,w),q)

Proof.

First, we note that
a[0]=(2\pi i)^{-1}\operatorname{Res}_{z}\left(Y(a,z)(1+z)^{\mathrm{wt}a-1}\right)=(2\pi i)^{-1}\sum_{j\geq 0}\binom{\mathrm{wt}a-1}{j}a(j)
, and

I(v,w)a(\mathrm{wt}a-1)=a(\mathrm{wt}a-1)I(v,w)+\sum_{j\geq 0}\binom{\mathrm{wt}a-1}{j}I(a(j)v,w)w^{\mathrm{wt}a-1-j}.

Then by (
5.2
) we have

\displaystyle F((v,w)(a,x),q)

\displaystyle=x^{\mathrm{wt}a}w^{\deg v+h_{W}}\mathrm{tr}|_{M}I(v,w)\sum_{k\in\mathbb Z}a(\mathrm{wt}a-1+k)x^{-\mathrm{wt}a-k}q^{L(0)-h_{M}}

\displaystyle=w^{\deg v+h_{W}}\mathrm{tr}|_{M}I(v,w)a(\mathrm{wt}a-1)q^{L(0)-h_{M}}

\displaystyle\ +\sum_{k\neq 0}x^{-k}w^{\deg v+h_{W}}\mathrm{tr}|_{M}I(v,w)a(\mathrm{wt}a-1+k)q^{L(0)-h_{M}}

\displaystyle=w^{\deg v+h_{W}}\mathrm{tr}|_{M}a(\mathrm{wt}a-1)I(v,w)q^{L(0)-h_{M}}-2\pi iF((a[0]v,w),q)

\displaystyle\ +\sum_{k\neq 0}x^{-k}w^{\deg v+h_{W}}\mathrm{tr}|_{M}I(v,w)a(\mathrm{wt}a-1+k)q^{L(0)-h_{M}}.

For
k\neq 0

\displaystyle\mathrm{tr}|_{M}I(v,w)a(\mathrm{wt}a-1+k)q^{L(0)-h_{M}}

\displaystyle=\mathrm{tr}|_{M}I(v,w)q^{L(0)-h_{M}}a(\mathrm{wt}a-1+k)q^{k}

\displaystyle=\mathrm{tr}|_{M}a(\mathrm{wt}a-1+k)I(v,w)q^{L(0)-h_{M}}q^{k}

\displaystyle=\mathrm{tr}|_{M}I(v,w)a(\mathrm{wt}a-1+k)q^{L(0)-h_{M}}q^{k}+\sum_{i\geq 0}\binom{\mathrm{wt}a-1+k}{i}\mathrm{tr}|_{M}I(a(i)v,w)w^{\mathrm{wt}a-1-i+k}q^{L(0)-h_{M}}q^{k}.

It follows that

\displaystyle\sum_{k\neq 0}x^{-k}w^{\deg v+h_{W}}\mathrm{tr}|_{M}I(v,w)a(\mathrm{wt}a-1+k)q^{L(0)-h_{M}}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k\neq 0}x^{-k}w^{\deg v+h_{W}}\sum_{i\geq 0}\binom{\mathrm{wt}a-1+k}{i}\frac{q^{k}}{1-q^{k}}\mathrm{tr}|_{M}I(a(i)v,w)w^{\mathrm{wt}a-1+k-i}q^{L(0)-h_{M}}

\displaystyle=\sum_{i\geq 0}\left(\sum_{k\geq 1}\binom{\mathrm{wt}a-1+k}{i}\frac{q^{k}}{1-q^{k}}\left(\frac{w}{x}\right)^{k}+\sum_{k\geq 1}\binom{\mathrm{wt}a-1-k}{i}\frac{q^{-k}}{1-q^{-k}}\left(\frac{w}{x}\right)^{-k}\right)F((a(i)v,w),q)

where the last equality follows from Lemma (
5.6
