10 C.F.R. § 835.1003 — Workplace controls.
Citation: us:cfr:10:835.1003
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-835.1003
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 835 (Occupational Radiation Protection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

During routine operations, the combination of engineered and administrative controls shall provide that:
(a) The anticipated occupational dose to general employees shall not exceed the limits established at § 835.202; and
(b) The ALARA process is utilized for personnel exposures to ionizing radiation.
