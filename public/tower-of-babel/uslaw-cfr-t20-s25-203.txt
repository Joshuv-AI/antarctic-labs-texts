20 C.F.R. § 25.203 — How is the Special Schedule applied to non-resident aliens in the Territory of Guam?
Citation: us:cfr:20:25.203
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-25.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 25 (Compensation For Disability And Death Of Noncitizen Federal Employees Outside The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The special schedule of compensation established by subpart B of this part shall apply to an injury or death occurring on or after August 29, 2011 in the Territory of Guam to non-resident alien employees recruited in foreign countries for employment by the military departments in the Territory of Guam. This schedule shall not apply to any employee who becomes a bona fide permanent resident as such claims will be decided in accordance with § 25.5.
