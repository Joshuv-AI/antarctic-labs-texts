40 C.F.R. § 51.1301 — Applicability of this part.
Citation: us:cfr:40:51.1301
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-51.1301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 51 (Requirements For Preparation, Adoption, And Submittal Of Implementation Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions in subparts A through Y and AA of this part apply to areas for purposes of the 2015 ozone NAAQS to the extent they are not inconsistent with the provisions of this subpart.
