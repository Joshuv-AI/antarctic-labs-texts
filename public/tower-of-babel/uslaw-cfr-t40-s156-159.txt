40 C.F.R. § 156.159 — Compliance date.
Citation: us:cfr:40:156.159
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-156.159
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 156 (Labeling Requirements For Pesticides And Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any pesticide product released for shipment by a registrant after August 16, 2011 must bear a label that complies with §§ 156.10(d)(7), 156.10(f), 156.10(i)(2)(ix), 156.140, 156.144, 156.146 and 156.156.
