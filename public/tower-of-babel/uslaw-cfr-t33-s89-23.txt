33 C.F.R. § 89.23 — Definitions.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 89 (Inland Navigation Rules: Implementing Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in this subpart:
Inland Rules refers to the Inland Navigation Rules contained in the Inland Navigational Rules Act of 1980 (Pub. L. 96-591, 33 U.S.C. 2001 et. seq.) and the technical annexes established under that Act.
