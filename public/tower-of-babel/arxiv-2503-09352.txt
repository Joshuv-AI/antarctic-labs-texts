de Rham cohomology of Lubin-Tate spaces and Drinfeld spaces
Benchao Su

Abstract
Let d ≥ 1 d\geq 1 be an integer. We use the methods [ Pan22a ] , [ Pan22b ] introduced by Lue Pan to prove that the compactly supported cohomology of Lubin-Tate towers and Drinfeld towers are isomorphic, as GL d + 1 ⁡ ( L ) × D L , 1 d + 1 × \GL_{d+1}(L)\times D_{L,\frac{1}{d+1}}^{\times} -modules.



0.1
Introduction

Let
L
be a finite extension of
\mathbb{Q}_{p}
, and let
d\geq 1
be an integer. Let
G=\GL_{d+1}(L)
, and let
\check{G}=D_{L,\frac{1}{d+1}}^{\times}
be the group of invertible elements in a division algebra over
L
with invariant
\frac{1}{d+1}
. Let
C
be the
p
-adic completion of an algebraic closure of
L
. The foundational works
[
LT66
,
Dri76
,
RZ96
]
introduced two important towers of rigid analytic spaces
\{\mathcal{M}_{\text{LT},n}\}_{n}
,
\{\mathcal{M}_{\text{Dr},n}\}_{n}
over
C
, namely the Lubin-Tate towers and the Drinfeld towers. They carry natural
G\times\check{G}
-actions and the action of the Weil group of
L
. The non-abelian Lubin-Tate theory
[
Car90
,
HT01
,
Dat07
]
shows that the
\ell
-adic cohomology of these towers encodes the local Langlands correspondence and the local Jacquet-Langlands correspondence. In the
p
-adic case, several recent works
[
CDN20
,
Jun22a
,
Jun22b
]
have shown that in certain cases the compactly supported de Rham cohomology of these towers also encodes the local Langlands correspondence and the local Jacquet-Langlands correspondence. The main theorem of this note is as follows:

Theorem 0.1.1
.

For any
i\geq 0
, there exists a
C
-linear
G\times\check{G}
-equivariant isomorphism of the compactly supported de Rham cohomology groups

\displaystyle H^{i}_{\dR,c}(\mathcal{M}_{\text{LT},\infty})\cong H^{i}_{\dR,c}(\mathcal{M}_{\text{Dr},\infty}).

In the case where the dimension
d=1
, this was proved in
[
CDN20
, Théorème 4.1]
. In general, this was proved in
[
DC24
, Theorem 5.2.2]
and by Bosco-Dospinescu-Niziol (flip-flopping). We provide a relatively elementary and explicit approach to these results.

First, from
[
SW13
]
we know that by taking the limit over the levels of these two towers, we obtain two perfectoid spaces
\mathcal{M}_{\text{LT},\infty}
,
\mathcal{M}_{\text{Dr},\infty}
over
C
, which are
G\times\check{G}
-equivariantly isomorphic:

\displaystyle\mathcal{M}_{\text{LT},\infty}\cong\mathcal{M}_{\text{Dr},\infty}.

Let
\mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{M}_{\text{LT},\infty}}
be the completed structure sheaf on
\mathcal{M}_{\text{LT},\infty}
, and let
\mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{M}_{\text{LT},\infty}}^{\text{sm}}\subset\mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{M}_{\text{LT},\infty}}
be the subsheaf consisting of sections which are fixed by a sufficiently small open compact subgroup of
G
. Then we can associate a de Rham complex

\displaystyle\DR_{\text{LT}}:=[\mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{M}_{\text{LT},\infty}}^{\text{sm}}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle d}}{{\to}}\Omega_{\mathcal{M}_{\text{LT},\infty}}^{1,\text{sm}}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle d}}{{\to}}\cdots\stackrel{{\scriptstyle d}}{{\to}}\Omega_{\mathcal{M}_{\text{LT},\infty}}^{d,\text{sm}}]

given by taking differentials along the Lubin-Tate tower of finite levels, where
\Omega_{\mathcal{M}_{\text{LT},\infty}}^{k,\text{sm}}:=\varinjlim_{n}\pi_{n}^{-1}\Omega_{\mathcal{M}_{\text{LT},n}}^{k}
with
\pi_{n}:\mathcal{M}_{\text{LT},\infty}\to\mathcal{M}_{\text{LT},n}
for
k=1,...,d
. The compactly supported de Rham cohomology groups
H^{i}_{\dR,c}(\mathcal{M}_{\text{LT},\infty})
are defined (in a suitable sense) as the compactly supported cohomology groups of the complex
\DR_{\text{LT}}
. Similarly, on the Drinfeld side we can define the de Rham complex
\DR_{\text{Dr}}
and the compactly supported de Rham cohomology groups
H^{i}_{\dR,c}(\mathcal{M}_{\text{Dr},\infty})
for the Drinfeld tower. The isomorphism of Theorem
0.1.1
then can be given by the isomorphism of the de Rham complexes
\DR_{\text{LT}}
and
\DR_{\text{Dr}}
.

However, it is difficult to directly deduce the isomorphism of de Rham cohomology groups from the isomorphism on the completed structure sheaves
\mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{M}_{\text{LT},\infty}}\cong\mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{M}_{\text{Dr},\infty}}
, since the naive definition of de Rham cohomology for perfectoid spaces is problematic. It turns out that on a smaller subsheaf of
\mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{M}_{\text{LT},\infty}}
containing
\mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{M}_{\text{LT},\infty}}^{\text{sm}}
(which we will define later), we can lift the differential operators on
\mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{M}_{\text{LT},\infty}}^{\text{sm}}
to this slightly larger sheaf. This allows us to use the isomorphism
\mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{M}_{\text{LT},\infty}}\cong\mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{M}_{\text{Dr},\infty}}
and directly compare these two de Rham complexes.

Motivated by the works
[
Pan22a
]
,
[
Pan22b
]
,
[
Cam22
]
,
[
Cam24
]
, we are led to study the subsheaf consisting of locally
L
-analytic sections (for the
G
-action)
\mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{M}_{\text{LT},\infty}}^{L\text{-}\text{la}}\subset\mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{M}_{\text{LT},\infty}}
. By geometric Sen theory
[
Cam22
]
, there exists an action of the Lie algebroid
\pi^{-1}\mathfrak{m}^{0}
on
\mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{M}_{\text{LT},\infty}}^{L\text{-}\text{la}}
, where
\pi:\mathcal{M}_{\text{LT},\infty}\to\mathscr{F}\ell
is the Hodge-Tate period map
[
SW13
]
, and
\mathfrak{m}^{0}\cong\mathfrak{p}^{0}/\mathfrak{n}^{0}
with
\mathfrak{p}^{0}
being the universal parabolic subalgebra on
\mathscr{F}\ell
and
\mathfrak{n}^{0}
being the universal nilpotent algebra on
\mathscr{F}\ell
. Let
\mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{M}_{\text{LT},\infty}}^{L\text{-}\text{la},\mathfrak{m}^{0}=0}\subset\mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{M}_{\text{LT},\infty}}^{L\text{-}\text{la}}
be the subsheaf consisting of sections killed by the
\pi^{-1}\mathfrak{m}^{0}
-action. Then we study the local structure of
\mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{M}_{\text{LT},\infty}}^{L\text{-}\text{la},\mathfrak{m}^{0}=0}
in detail. Moreover, although the sheaf we consider on the Drinfeld side
\mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{M}_{\text{Dr},\infty}}^{\text{sm}}
consisting of sections that are smooth under the
\check{G}
-action, these sections are locally
L
-analytic under the
G
-action. Then one can show that the natural map

\displaystyle\mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{M}_{\text{LT},\infty}}^{\text{sm}}\ox_{C}\mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{M}_{\text{Dr},\infty}}^{\text{sm}}\to\mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{M}_{\text{LT},\infty}}^{L\text{-}\text{la},\mathfrak{m}^{0}=0}

has dense image (Corollary
0.4.5
). We also define two differential operators on
\mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{M}_{\text{LT},\infty}}^{L\text{-}\text{la},\mathfrak{m}^{0}=0}
in the same spirit as in
[
Pan22b
]
, which is essentially given by taking differentials along the Lubin-Tate spaces of finite level or the Drinfeld spaces of finite level. In order to compute the cohomology of these differential operators, we do the same construction on the Drinfeld side. In particular, there is also a subsheaf
\mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{M}_{\text{Dr},\infty}}^{L\text{-}\text{la},\check{\mathfrak{m}}^{0}=0}
on
\mathcal{M}_{\text{Dr},\infty}
consisting of locally
L
-analytic sections for the
\check{G}
-action, and killed by the horizontal action of
\check{\pi}^{-1}\check{\mathfrak{m}}^{0}
with
\check{\pi}:\mathcal{M}_{\text{Dr},\infty}\to\check{\mathscr{F}\ell}
the Hodge-Tate period map on the Drinfeld side. We check directly that under the isomorphism
\mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{M}_{\text{LT},\infty}}\cong\mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{M}_{\text{Dr},\infty}}
, there is a
G\times\check{G}
-equivariant isomorphism (Theorem
0.4.4
)

\displaystyle\mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{M}_{\text{LT},\infty}}^{L\text{-}\text{la},\mathfrak{m}^{0}=0}\cong\mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{M}_{\text{Dr},\infty}}^{L\text{-}\text{la},\check{\mathfrak{m}}^{0}=0}.

We also identify the differential operators on these sheaves appropriately (Theorem
0.4.9
). This allows us to compute the cohomology of these differential operators. From this computation, we can deduce our main result.
