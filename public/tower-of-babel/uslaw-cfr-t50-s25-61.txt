50 C.F.R. § 25.61 — General provisions.
Citation: us:cfr:50:25.61
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-25.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 25 (Administrative Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Public use facilities may be operated by concessionaires or cooperators under appropriate contact or legal agreement on national wildlife refuges where there is a demonstrated justified need for services or facilities including, but not limited to, boat rentals, swimming facilities, conducted tours of special natural attractions, shelters, tables, trailer lots, food, lodging, and related service.
