28 C.F.R. § 69.410 — Enforcement.
Citation: us:cfr:28:69.410
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-69.410
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 69 (New Restrictions On Lobbying). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The head of each agency shall take such actions as are necessary to ensure that the provisions herein are vigorously implemented and enforced in that agency.
