40 C.F.R. § 60.1025 — Do subpart E new source performance standards also apply to my municipal waste combustion unit?
Citation: us:cfr:40:60.1025
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.1025
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 60 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If this subpart AAAA applies to your municipal waste combustion unit, then subpart E of this part does not apply to your municipal waste combustion unit.
