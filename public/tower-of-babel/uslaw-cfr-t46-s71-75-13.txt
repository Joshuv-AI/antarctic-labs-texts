46 C.F.R. § 71.75-13 — -13 Safety Management Certificate.
Citation: us:cfr:46:71.75-13
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-71.75-13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 71 (Inspection And Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All vessels to which 33 CFR part 96 applies on an international voyage must have a valid Safety Management Certificate and a copy of their company's valid Document of Compliance certificate on board.
