41 C.F.R. § 301-10.122 — -10.122 Compensation for denied seat.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-10.122
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-10.122
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-10 (10—Transportation Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When performing official travel and a carrier denies a confirmed reserved seat, employees must provide any liquidated damages payment to their agency in accordance with their agency's procedures.
