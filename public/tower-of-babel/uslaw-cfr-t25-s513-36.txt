25 C.F.R. § 513.36 — Under what conditions will the Commission refund amounts collected by salary offset?
Citation: us:cfr:25:513.36
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-513.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 513 (Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) As the creditor agency, the Commission will promptly refund any amount deducted under the authority of 5 U.S.C. 5514, when:
(1) The Commission determines that the debt is not owed; or
(2) An administrative or judicial order directs the Commission to make a refund.
(b) Unless required or permitted by law or contract, refunds under this section will not bear interest.
