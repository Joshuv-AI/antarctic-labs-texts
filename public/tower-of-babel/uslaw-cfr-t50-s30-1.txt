50 C.F.R. § 30.1 — Surplus range animals.
Citation: us:cfr:50:30.1
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-30.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 30 (Range And Feral Animal Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Range animals on fenced wildlife refuge areas, including buffalo and longhorn cattle, determined to be surplus to the needs of the conservation program may be planned and scheduled for disposal.
