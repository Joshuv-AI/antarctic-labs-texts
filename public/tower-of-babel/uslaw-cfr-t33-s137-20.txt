33 C.F.R. § 137.20 — May industry standards be used to comply with this regulation?
Citation: us:cfr:33:137.20
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-137.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 137 (Oil Spill Liability: Standards For Conducting All Appropriate Inquiries Under The Innocent Land-Owner Defense). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The industry standards in ASTM E 1527-05, (Referenced in § 137.15) may be used to comply with the requirements set forth in §§ 137.45 through 137.85 of this part. Use of ASTM E 1527-05 for this purpose is optional and not mandatory.
