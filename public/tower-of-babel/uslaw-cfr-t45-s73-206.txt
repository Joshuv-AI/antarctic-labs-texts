45 C.F.R. § 73.206 — Other prohibitions.
Citation: us:cfr:45:73.206
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-73.206
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 73 (Standards Of Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees must avoid any action, irrespective of whether it is specifically prohibited by this part, that might result in or create the appearance of:
(a) Impeding Government efficiency or economy; or
(b) Making a Government decision outside of official channels.
