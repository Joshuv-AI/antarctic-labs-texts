22 U.S.C. § 6745 — Recordkeeping violations
Citation: us:usc:22:6745
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section6745
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 75 (Chemical Weapons Convention Implementation); Subchapter III (Reports). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It shall be unlawful for any person willfully to fail or refuse—
(1)
to establish or maintain any record required by this chapter or any regulation prescribed under this chapter;
(2)
to submit any report, notice, or other information to the United States Government in accordance with this chapter or any regulation prescribed under this chapter; or
(3)
to permit access to or copying of any record that is exempt from disclosure under this chapter or any regulation prescribed under this chapter.
