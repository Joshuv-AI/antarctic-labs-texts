19 U.S.C. § 4436 — Application to new and existing preclearance operations
Citation: us:usc:19:4436
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section4436
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 28 (Trade Facilitation And Trade Enforcement); Subchapter VII (Preclearance Operations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except for sections 814(d) [19 U.S.C. 4433(d)], 815, 817 [19 U.S.C. 4435], and 818, this subchapter shall only apply to the establishment of preclearance operations in a foreign country in which no preclearance operations have been established as of February 24, 2016.
