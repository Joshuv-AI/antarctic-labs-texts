35 U.S.C. § 131 — Examination of application
Citation: us:usc:35:131
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title35-section131
Context: United States Code, Title 35 (Patents); Part II (Patentability Of Inventions And Grant Of Patents); Chapter 12 (Examination Of Application). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director shall cause an examination to be made of the application and the alleged new invention; and if on such examination it appears that the applicant is entitled to a patent under the law, the Director shall issue a patent therefor.
