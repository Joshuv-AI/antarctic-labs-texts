16 U.S.C. § 838a — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:16:838a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section838a
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 12G (Pacific Northwest Federal Transmission System). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this chapter—
(a)
The term “Administrator” means the Administrator, Bonneville Power Administration.
(b)
The term “electric power” means electric peaking capacity or electric energy, or both.
(c)
The term “major transmission facilities” means transmission facilities intended to be used to provide services not previously provided by the Bonneville Power Administration with its own facilities.
