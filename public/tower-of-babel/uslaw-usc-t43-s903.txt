43 U.S.C. § 903 — Relief of settlers on lands granted in aid of wagon roads
Citation: us:usc:43:903
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section903
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 21 (Grants In Aid Of Railroads And Wagon Roads). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provision of section 888 of this title and all statutes amendatory thereof or supplementary thereto, including sections 894 to 899 of this title, as modified or supplemented by sections 900 to 902 of this title, shall apply to grants of land in aid of the construction of wagon roads.
