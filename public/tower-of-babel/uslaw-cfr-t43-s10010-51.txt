43 C.F.R. § 10010.51 — Limitations on actions.
Citation: us:cfr:43:10010.51
Creator: Utah Reclamation Mitigation And Conservation Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-10010.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 10010 (Policies And Procedures For Implementing The National Environmental Policy Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Executive Director will notify the Chairman of the Commission and the Office of the Solicitor of any situations where Commission or applicant action would, if taken prior to completion of a NEPA proceeding, potentially have an adverse environmental impact or limit the choice of reasonable alternatives.
