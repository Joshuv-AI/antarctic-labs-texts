54 U.S.C. § 100702 — Research mandate
Citation: us:usc:54:100702
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section100702
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 1007 (Resource Management); Subchapter I (System Resource Inventory And Management). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall ensure that management of System units is enhanced by the availability and utilization of a broad program of the highest quality science and information.
