14 C.F.R. § 107.53 — Automatic Dependent Surveillance-Broadcast (ADS-B) Out prohibition.
Citation: us:cfr:14:107.53
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-107.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 107 (Small Unmanned Aircraft Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless otherwise authorized by the Administrator, no person may operate a small unmanned aircraft system under this part with ADS-B Out equipment in transmit mode.
