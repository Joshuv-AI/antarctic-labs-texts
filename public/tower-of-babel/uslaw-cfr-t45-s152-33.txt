45 C.F.R. § 152.33 — Initial allocation of funds.
Citation: us:cfr:45:152.33
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-152.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 152 (Pre-Existing Condition Insurance Plan Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

HHS will establish an initial ceiling for the amount of the $5 billion in Federal funds allocated for PCIPs in each State using a methodology consistent with that used to established allocations under the Children's Health Insurance Program, as set forth under 42 CFR part 457, subpart F, Payment to States.
