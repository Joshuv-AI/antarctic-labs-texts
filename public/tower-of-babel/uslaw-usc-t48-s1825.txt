48 U.S.C. § 1825 — Effective date
Citation: us:usc:48:1825
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1825
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 17 (Northern Mariana Islands); Subchapter II (Judicial Matters). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This subchapter shall come into force upon its approval or at the time proclaimed by the President for the Constitution of the Northern Mariana Islands to become effective, whichever is the later date.
