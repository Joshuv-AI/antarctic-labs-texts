18 C.F.R. § 50.9 — Notice of application.
Citation: us:cfr:18:50.9
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-50.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 50 (Applications For Permits To Site Interstate Electric Transmission Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Notice of each application filed, except when rejected in accordance with § 50.8, will be issued and subsequently published in the Federal Register.
(b) The notice will establish prompt and binding intermediate milestones and ultimate deadlines for the review of, and Federal authorization decisions relating to, the proposed facilities.
