9 C.F.R. § 202.5 — Hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:9:202.5
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Fair Trade Practices Program), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-202.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 202 (Rules Of Practice Governing Proceedings Under The Packers And Stockyards Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The hearing will be oral unless all parties waive oral hearing. It will be written if not oral. Notice of the date, time and place of oral hearing, or of the date and place for filing of written submissions in a written hearing, will be served on the Administrator and the respondent, and on such other persons as have requested in writing to be heard.
