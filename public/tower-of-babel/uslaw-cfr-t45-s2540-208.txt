45 C.F.R. § 2540.208 — Under what circumstances may participants be engaged?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2540.208
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2540.208
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2540 (General Administrative Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A State may not engage a participant to serve in any program that receives Corporation assistance unless and until amounts have been appropriated under section 501 of the Act (42 U.S.C. 12681) for the provision of AmeriCorps educational awards and for the payment of other necessary expenses and costs associated with such participant.
