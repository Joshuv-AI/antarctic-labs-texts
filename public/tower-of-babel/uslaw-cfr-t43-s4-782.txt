43 C.F.R. § 4.782 — What happens if a petition for reconsideration and a notice of appeal are timely filed?
Citation: us:cfr:43:4.782
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4.782
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4 (Department Of The Interior Hearings And Appeals Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a petition for reconsideration is timely filed with the presiding officer and a notice of appeal is timely filed with the Board, the Board will dismiss the appeal without prejudice and the presiding officer will issue an order upon reconsideration.
