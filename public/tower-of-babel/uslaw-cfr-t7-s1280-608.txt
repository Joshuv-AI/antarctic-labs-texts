7 C.F.R. § 1280.608 — Farm Service Agency State Committee.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1280.608
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Miscellaneous Commodities), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1280.608
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1280 (Lamb Promotion, Research, And Information Order). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Farm Service Agency State Committee, also referred to as “FSA State Committee,” means the group of persons within a State who are appointed by the Secretary to act as the Farm Service Agency State Committee.
