42 U.S.C. § 16042 — Department of Homeland Security consultation
Citation: us:usc:42:16042
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section16042
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 149 (National Energy Policy And Programs); Subchapter VI (Nuclear Matters); Part C (Nuclear Security). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Before issuing a license for a utilization facility, the Nuclear Regulatory Commission shall consult with the Department of Homeland Security concerning the potential vulnerabilities of the location of the proposed facility to terrorist attack.
