43 C.F.R. § 4710.3-2 — -2 Wild horse and burro ranges.
Citation: us:cfr:43:4710.3-2
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4710.3-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4700 (Protection, Management, And Control Of Wild Free-Roaming Horses And Burros). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Herd management areas may also be designated as wild horse or burro ranges to be managed principally, but not necessarily exclusively, for wild horse or burro herds.
