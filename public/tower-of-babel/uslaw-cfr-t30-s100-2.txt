30 C.F.R. § 100.2 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:30:100.2
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-100.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 100 (Criteria And Procedures For Proposed Assessment Of Civil Penalties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The criteria and procedures in this part are applicable to all proposed assessments of civil penalties for violations of the Mine Act and the standards and regulations promulgated pursuant to the Mine Act, as amended. MSHA shall review each citation and order and shall make proposed assessments of civil penalties.
