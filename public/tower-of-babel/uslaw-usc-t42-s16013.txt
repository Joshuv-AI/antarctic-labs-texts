42 U.S.C. § 16013 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:42:16013
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section16013
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 149 (National Energy Policy And Programs); Subchapter VI (Nuclear Matters); Part A (General Nuclear Matters). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as are necessary to carry out this subtitle and the amendments made by this subtitle.
