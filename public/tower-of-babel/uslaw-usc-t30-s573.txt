30 U.S.C. § 573 — Statement by Commonwealth for Secretary
Citation: us:usc:30:573
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section573
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 14 (Anthracite Mine Drainage And Flood Control). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commonwealth shall furnish to the Secretary of the Interior a statement with respect to the project showing work done, the status of the project, expenditures and amounts obligated, at such times and in such detail as the Secretary of the Interior shall require for the purposes of this chapter.
