19 C.F.R. § 10.731 — Textile and apparel goods classifiable as goods put up in sets.
Citation: us:cfr:19:10.731
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-10.731
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 10 (Articles Conditionally Free, Subject To A Reduced Rate, Etc.). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notwithstanding the specific rules set forth in General Note 28(n), HTSUS, textile or apparel goods classifiable as goods put up in sets for retail sale as provided for in General Rule of Interpretation 3, HTSUS, will not be considered to be originating goods unless each of the goods in the set is an originating good or the total value of the non-originating goods in the set does not exceed 10 percent of the value of the set.
