7 C.F.R. § 1005.62 — Announcement of uniform prices.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1005.62
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Milk), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1005.62
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1005 (Milk In The Appalachian Marketing Area). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

On or before the 11th day after the end of the month, the market administrator shall announce the uniform prices for the month computed pursuant to § 1005.61.
