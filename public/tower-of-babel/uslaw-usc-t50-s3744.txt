50 U.S.C. § 3744 — Metrics for Department of Defense Cooperative Threat Reduction Program
Citation: us:usc:50:3744
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section3744
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 48 (Department Of Defense Cooperative Threat Reduction); Subchapter III (Recurring Certifications And Reports). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Defense shall implement metrics to measure the impact and effectiveness of activities of the Program to address threats arising from the proliferation of chemical, nuclear, and biological weapons and weapons-related materials, technologies, and expertise.
