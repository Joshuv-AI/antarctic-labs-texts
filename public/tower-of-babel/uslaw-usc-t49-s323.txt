49 U.S.C. § 323 — Personnel
Citation: us:usc:49:323
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section323
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Chapter 3 (General Duties And Powers); Subchapter II (Administrative). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Secretary of Transportation may appoint and fix the pay of officers and employees of the Department of Transportation and may prescribe their duties and powers.
(b)
The Secretary may procure services under section 3109 of title 5. However, an individual may be paid not more than $100 a day for services.
