31 C.F.R. § 342.8 — Governing regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:31:342.8
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-342.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 342 (Offering Of United States Savings Notes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Savings notes are subject to the regulations of the Department of the Treasury, now or hereafter prescribed, governing United States Savings Bonds, contained in 31 CFR part 315, also published as Department of the Treasury Circular No. 530, current revision, except as otherwise specifically provided herein.
