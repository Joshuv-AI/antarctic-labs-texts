9 C.F.R. § 307.3 — Inspectors to furnish and maintain implements in a sanitary condition.
Citation: us:cfr:9:307.3
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-307.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 307 (Facilities For Inspection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Inspectors shall furnish their own work clothing and implements, such as flashlights and triers, for conducting inspection and shall maintain their implements in sanitary condition as prescribed by § 416.3(a) of this chapter.
