5 C.F.R. § 532.223 — Establishments included in regular nonappropriated fund surveys.
Citation: us:cfr:5:532.223
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-532.223
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 532 (Prevailing Rate Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) All establishments having 20 or more employees in the prescribed industries within a survey area must be included in the survey universe. Establishments in NAICS codes 4571, 71391, and 71395 must be included in the survey universe if they have eight or more employees.
(b) Establishment selection procedures are the same as those prescribed for appropriated fund surveys in paragraphs (b) and (c) of § 532.213 of this subpart.
