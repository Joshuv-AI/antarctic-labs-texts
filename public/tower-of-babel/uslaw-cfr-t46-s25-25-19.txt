46 C.F.R. § 25.25-19 — -19 Visual distress signals.
Citation: us:cfr:46:25.25-19
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-25.25-19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 25 (Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each uninspected passenger vessel must meet the visual distress signal requirements of 33 CFR part 175 applicable to the vessel.
