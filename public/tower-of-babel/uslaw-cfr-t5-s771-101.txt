5 C.F.R. § 771.101 — Continuation of Grievance Systems.
Citation: us:cfr:5:771.101
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-771.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 771 (Agency Administrative Grievance System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each administrative grievance system in operation as of October 11, 1995, that has been established under former regulations under this part must remain in effect until the system is either modified by the agency or replaced with another dispute resolution process.
