5 C.F.R. § 831.205 — CSRS coverage determinations to be approved by OPM.
Citation: us:cfr:5:831.205
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-831.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 831 (Retirement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an agency determines that an employee is CSRS-covered, the agency must submit its determination to OPM for written approval. This requirement does not apply if the employee has been employed in Federal service with CSRS coverage within the preceding 365 days.
