42 U.S.C. § 3058cc — Administration
Citation: us:usc:42:3058cc
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section3058cc
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 35 (Programs For Older Americans); Subchapter XI (Allotments For Vulnerable Elder Rights Protection Activities); Part C (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A State agency may carry out vulnerable elder rights protection activities either directly or through contracts or agreements with public or nonprofit private agencies or organizations, such as—
(1)
other State agencies;
(2)
area agencies on aging;
(3)
county governments;
(4)
institutions of higher education;
(5)
Indian tribes; or
(6)
nonprofit service providers or volunteer organizations.
