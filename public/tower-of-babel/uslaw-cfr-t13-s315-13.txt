13 C.F.R. § 315.13 — Persons engaged by Firms to expedite petitions and Adjustment Proposals.
Citation: us:cfr:13:315.13
Creator: Economic Development Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-315.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 315 (Trade Adjustment Assistance For Firms). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

EDA will provide no Adjustment Assistance to any Firm unless the owners, partners, members, directors, or officers thereof certify in writing to EDA:
(a) The names of any attorneys, agents, and other Persons engaged by or on behalf of the Firm for the purpose of expediting petitions for such Adjustment Assistance or Adjustment Proposals; and
(b) The fees paid or to be paid to any such Person.
