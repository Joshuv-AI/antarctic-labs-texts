16 U.S.C. § 2463 — Prohibition of Antarctic mineral resource activities
Citation: us:usc:16:2463
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section2463
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 44B (Antarctic Mineral Resources Protection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is unlawful for any person to engage in, finance, or otherwise knowingly provide assistance to any Antarctic mineral resource activity.
