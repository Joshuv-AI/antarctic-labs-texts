45 C.F.R. § 660.13 — May the Director waive any provision of these regulations?
Citation: us:cfr:45:660.13
Creator: National Science Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-660.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 660 (Intergovernmental Review Of The National Science Foundation Programs And Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In an emergency, the Director may waive any provision of these regulations.
