7 C.F.R. § 987.161 — Handler carryover.
Citation: us:cfr:7:987.161
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-987.161
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 987 (Domestic Dates Produced Or Packed In Riverside County, California). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each handler shall file with the Committee, a report of his carryover of dates as of March 1 and October 1 and, when volume regulation is established, as of January 1. This report shall be on CDAC Form No. 5 and shall show, by variety, at least:
(a) The quantity of DAC dates held within and outside the area,
(b) The quantity of FP dates held within the area,
(c) The quantity of export dates, and
(d) The quantity of dates held graded but not certified, and as field-run, segregated as to outlet category.
