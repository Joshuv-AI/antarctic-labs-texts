40 C.F.R. § 98.502 — GHGs to report.
Citation: us:cfr:40:98.502
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-98.502
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 98 (Mandatory Greenhouse Gas Reporting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must report:
(a) Process CO2 emissions from each calcium carbide process unit or furnace used for the production of calcium carbide.
(b) CO2, CH4, and N2O emissions from each stationary combustion unit following the requirements of subpart C of this part. You must report these emissions under subpart C of this part by following the requirements of subpart C.
