35 U.S.C. § 375 — Patent issued on international application: Effect
Citation: us:usc:35:375
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title35-section375
Context: United States Code, Title 35 (Patents); Part IV (Patent Cooperation Treaty); Chapter 37 (National Stage). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
A patent may be issued by the Director based on an international application designating the United States, in accordance with the provisions of this title. Such patent shall have the force and effect of a patent issued on a national application filed under the provisions of chapter 11.
(b)
Where due to an incorrect translation the scope of a patent granted on an international application designating the United States, which was not originally filed in the English language, exceeds the scope of the international application in its original language, a court of competent jurisdiction may retroactively limit the scope of the patent, by declaring it unenforceable to the extent that it exceeds the scope of the international application in its original language.
