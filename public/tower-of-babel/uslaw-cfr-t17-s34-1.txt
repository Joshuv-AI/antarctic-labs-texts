17 C.F.R. § 34.1 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:17:34.1
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-34.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 34 (Regulation Of Hybrid Instruments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this part shall apply to any hybrid instrument which may be subject to the Act, and which has been entered into on or after October 23, 1974.
