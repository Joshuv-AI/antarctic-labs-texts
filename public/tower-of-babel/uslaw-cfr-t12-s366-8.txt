12 C.F.R. § 366.8 — Who can grant a waiver of a prohibition or conflict of interest?
Citation: us:cfr:12:366.8
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-366.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 366 (Minimum Standards Of Integrity And Fitness For An Fdic Contractor). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The FDIC's Board of Directors delegates to the Chairman, or his designee, authority to issue waivers and implement procedures for part 366.
