7 C.F.R. § 1427.1084 — Examination of warehouses.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1427.1084
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1427.1084
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1427 (Cotton). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise provided in this subpart, a warehouse must be examined by a person designated by CCC before it may be approved by CCC for the storage and handling of the commodity and periodically thereafter to determine its compliance with CCC's standards and requirements.
