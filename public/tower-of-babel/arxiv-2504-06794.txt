A new model for all C C -sequences are trivial
Assaf Rinot, Zhixing You, Jiachen Yuan

Abstract
. We construct a model in which all C C -sequences are trivial, yet there exists a κ \kappa -Souslin tree with full vanishing levels. This answers a question from [ RYY23 ] , and provides an optimal combination of compactness and incompactness. It is obtained by incorporating a so-called mutually exclusive ascent path to Kunen’s original forcing construction.

Main Theorem
