16 U.S.C. § 410ff–6 — Entrance or admission fees prohibited
Citation: us:usc:16:410ff–6
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section410ff–6
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LIX–D (Channel Islands National Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, no fees shall be charged for entrance or admission to the park.
