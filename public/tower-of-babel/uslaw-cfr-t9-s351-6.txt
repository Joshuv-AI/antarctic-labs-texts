9 C.F.R. § 351.6 — Official number.
Citation: us:cfr:9:351.6
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-351.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 351 (Certification Of Technical Animal Fats For Export). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator will assign a certified technical animal fat plant number to each plant granted service. Such number shall be preceded by the letter “C” and be used to identify all certified technical animal fat prepared or stored by the plant.
