42 U.S.C. § 5416 — Consumer’s manual; contents
Citation: us:usc:42:5416
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section5416
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 70 (Manufactured Home Construction And Safety Standards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall develop guidelines for a consumer’s manual to be provided to manufactured home purchasers by the manufacturer. These manuals should identify and explain the purchasers’ responsibilities for operation, maintenance, and repair of their manufactured homes.
