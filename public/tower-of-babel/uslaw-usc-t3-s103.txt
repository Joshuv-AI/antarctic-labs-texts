3 U.S.C. § 103 — Traveling expenses
Citation: us:usc:3:103
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title3-section103
Context: United States Code, Title 3 (The President); Chapter 2 (Office And Compensation Of President). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There may be expended for or on account of the traveling expenses of the President of the United States such sum as Congress may from time to time appropriate, not exceeding $100,000 per annum, such sum when appropriated to be expended in the discretion of the President and accounted for on his certificate solely.
