7 U.S.C. § 3197 — Availability of appropriated funds
Citation: us:usc:7:3197
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section3197
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 64 (Agricultural Research, Extension, And Teaching); Subchapter V (Animal Health And Disease Research). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Funds available to carry out sections 3195(a) and 3196 of this title shall be paid to each State or eligible institution at such times and in such amounts as shall be determined by the Secretary. Funds shall remain available for payment of unliquidated obligations for one additional fiscal year following the year of appropriation.
