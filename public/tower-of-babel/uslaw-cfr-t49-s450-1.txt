49 C.F.R. § 450.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:49:450.1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-450.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 450 (General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subchapter establishes requirements and procedures for safety approval and periodic examination of cargo containers used in international transport, as defined in the International Safe Container Act.
