29 C.F.R. § 1919.34 — Proof tests after repairs or alterations.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1919.34
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1919.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1919 (Gear Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When proof loads are applied after repairs or alterations, all parts of the assembled gear shall be examined as required in §§ 1919.30, 1919.31(c), or 1919.32(c), whichever is applicable.
