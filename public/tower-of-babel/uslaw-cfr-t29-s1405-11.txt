29 C.F.R. § 1405.11 — Effect on employee benefits.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1405.11
Creator: Federal Mediation And Conciliation Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1405.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1405 (Part-Time Employment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Career part-time employees are entitled to coverage under the Federal Employees Group Life Insurance and Federal Employees Health Benefits Programs. The Government contribution for health insurance of eligible part-time employees will be prorated on the basis of the fraction of a full-time schedule worked.
