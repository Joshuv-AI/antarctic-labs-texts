43 U.S.C. § 2506 — Limitations of suits to annul patents
Citation: us:usc:43:2506
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section2506
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 43 (Suspended Entries And Claims; Patents). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Suits by the United States to vacate and annul any patent shall only be brought within six years after the date of the issuance of such patents.
