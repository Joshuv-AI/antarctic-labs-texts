15 U.S.C. § 1225 — State laws as affected
Citation: us:usc:15:1225
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1225
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 27 (Automobile Dealer Suits Against Manufacturers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter shall not invalidate any provision of the laws of any State except insofar as there is a direct conflict between an express provision of this chapter and an express provision of State law which can not 1 be reconciled.
