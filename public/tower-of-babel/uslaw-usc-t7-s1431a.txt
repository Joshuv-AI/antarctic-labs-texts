7 U.S.C. § 1431a — Cotton donations to educational institutions
Citation: us:usc:7:1431a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1431a
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 35A (Price Support Of Agricultural Commodities); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Commodity Credit Corporation is authorized, on such terms as the Secretary of Agriculture may approve, to donate cotton acquired through its price support operations to educational institutions for use in the training of students in the processing and manufacture of cotton into textiles.
