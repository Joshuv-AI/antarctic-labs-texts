T c ¯ ​ s ¯ ​ 1 0 T_{\bar{c}\bar{s}1}^{0} production in the B + B^{+} decays processes
Zhuo Yu 1, Qi Wu 2, Zi-Li Yue 1, Dian-Yong Chen 1,3

Abstract
In the present work, we studied the T c ¯ ​ s ¯ ​ 1 0 T_{\bar{c}\bar{s}1}^{0} state production through the meson loop mechanism in the B + B^{+} meson decays, where T c ¯ ​ s ¯ ​ 1 0 T_{\bar{c}\bar{s}1}^{0} is considered as a D ¯ ​ K ∗ \bar{D}K^{*} molecular state with J P = 1 + J^{P}=1^{+} . By employing the effective Lagrangian approach, we estimated the branching ratio of the B + → D + ​ T c ¯ ​ s ¯ ​ 1 0 B^{+}\to D^{+}T_{\bar{c}\bar{s}1}^{0} and B + → D ∗ ⁣ + ​ T c ¯ ​ s ¯ ​ 1 0 B^{+}\to D^{*+}T_{\bar{c}\bar{s}1}^{0} processes and found them to be on the order of 10 − 5 ∼ 10 − 4 10^{-5}\sim 10^{-4} . The fit fraction of T c ¯ ​ s ¯ ​ 1 0 T_{\bar{c}\bar{s}1}^{0} in different processes was also estimated. We propose to search for T c ¯ ​ s ¯ ​ 1 0 T_{\bar{c}\bar{s}1}^{0} in the B + → D ∗ ⁣ + ​ D ¯ ∗ ⁣ − ​ K + B^{+}\to D^{*+}\bar{D}^{*-}K^{+} process, which should be accessible to the Belle II and LHCb Collaborations.

\displaystyle\qquad\Big[ig_{T_{\bar{c}\bar{s}1}DK^{*}}g_{\phi\theta}\epsilon^{\theta}(p_{2})\Big]\Big[\frac{-g^{\mu\alpha}+q_{1}^{\mu}q_{1}^{\alpha}/m_{q_{1}}^{2}}{q_{1}^{2}-m_{q_{1}}^{2}}\Big]\Big[\frac{1}{q_{2}^{2}-m_{q_{2}}^{2}}\Big]

\displaystyle\qquad\Big[\frac{-g^{\beta\phi}+q_{3}^{\beta}q_{3}^{\phi}/m_{q_{3}}^{2}}{q_{3}^{2}-m_{q_{3}}^{2}}\Big]\mathcal{F}^{2}(q_{3}^{2},m_{q_{3}}^{2}),

\displaystyle\mathcal{M}_{D_{s1}^{+}\bar{D}^{0}K^{*0}}=i^{3}\int\frac{d^{4}q_{3}}{(2\pi)^{4}}\Big[\mathcal{A}_{\mu}^{B^{+}\rightarrow D_{s1}^{\prime+}\bar{D}^{0}}(q_{1},q_{2})\Big]\Big[ig_{\mathcal{D}_{1}\mathcal{D}Vg_{\alpha\beta}}\Big]

\displaystyle\qquad\Big[ig_{T_{\bar{c}\bar{s}1}DK^{*}}g_{\phi\theta}\epsilon^{\theta}(p_{2})\Big]\Big[\frac{-g^{\mu\alpha}+q_{1}^{\mu}q_{1}^{\alpha}/m_{q_{1}}^{2}}{q_{1}^{2}-m_{q_{1}}^{2}}\Big]\Big[\frac{1}{q_{2}^{2}-m_{q_{2}}^{2}}\Big]

\displaystyle\qquad\Big[\frac{-g^{\beta\phi}+q_{3}^{\beta}q_{3}^{\phi}/m_{q_{3}}^{2}}{q_{3}^{2}-m_{q_{3}}^{2}}\Big]\mathcal{F}^{2}(q_{3}^{2},m_{q_{3}}^{2}).

(10)

In the above amplitudes, a form factor
\mathcal{F}(q_{3}^{2},m_{q_{3}}^{2})
in the monopole form is introduced to compensate for the off-shell effect and avoid ultraviolet divergence in loop integrals. Its concrete form is,

\mathcal{F}\left(q_{3}^{2},m_{q_{3}}^{2}\right)=\frac{m_{q_{3}}^{2}-\Lambda^{2}}{q_{3}^{2}-\Lambda^{2}},

(11)

where the parameter
\Lambda
can be reparameterized as
{\Lambda}=m_{q_{3}}+\alpha{\rm\Lambda_{QCD}}
with
\Lambda_{\rm QCD}
= 220 MeV, and
\alpha
to be the model parameter, which should be of order unity
Cheng:2004ru
.

Then, the amplitude of
B^{+}\to D^{+}T_{\bar{c}\bar{s}1}^{0}
reads,

\displaystyle\mathcal{M}_{B^{+}\to D^{+}T_{\bar{c}\bar{s}1}^{0}}=\mathcal{M}_{D_{s}^{+}}+\mathcal{M}_{D_{s}^{*+}}+\mathcal{M}_{D_{s1}^{\prime+}}+\mathcal{M}_{D_{s1}^{+}},

(12)

and the partial width of
B^{+}\to D^{+}T_{\bar{c}\bar{s}1}^{0}
can be estimated by,

\displaystyle\Gamma_{B^{+}\to D^{+}T_{\bar{c}\bar{s}1}^{0}}=\frac{1}{8\pi}\frac{|\vec{p}\,|}{m_{B^{+}}^{2}}\overline{\left|\mathcal{M}_{B^{+}\to D^{+}T_{\bar{c}\bar{s}1}^{0}}\right|^{2}},

(13)

where
\vec{p}
is the momentum of final states in the rest frame of

B^{+}
, and the overline above the amplitude indicates the sum over the spins of final states.

IV
SUMMARY

In the molecular state scenario, the states near the
D^{(*)}K^{(*)}/\bar{D}^{(*)}K^{(*)}
threshold have attracted much attention. Predictions of molecular states near the
DK,D^{*}K
and
D^{*}K^{*}
thresholds have been experimentally observed, while the
DK^{*}
molecular state have also been predicted but has not been observed yet. In the present work, we assumed
T_{\bar{c}\bar{s}1}^{0}
as a
DK^{*}
molecular state with a binding energy
E_{b}=10
MeV, and investigated its production in the
B^{+}
decay process through the meson loop mechanism. The branching ratio of
B^{+}\to T_{\bar{c}\bar{s}1}^{0}D^{(*)+}
was estimated to be
(1.29_{-0.63}^{+0.73})\times 10^{-4}
, which are significantly larger than that of
B^{+}\to D^{+}T_{\bar{c}\bar{s}1}^{*}(2900)^{0}
by about an order of magnitude.

Besides
B^{+}\to T_{\bar{c}\bar{s}1}^{0}D^{+}
, we also considered the case with a charmed meson
D^{*+}
in final state, and the branching ratio of
B^{+}\to T_{\bar{c}\bar{s}1}^{0}D^{*+}
is
(3.73_{-1.84}^{+2.43})\times 10^{-4}
. Using the measured branching ratio of
B^{+}\to D^{(*)+}D^{*-}K^{+}
and
B^{+}\to D^{(*)+}\bar{D}^{0}K^{0}
from the PDG, we estimated the fit fraction of
T_{\bar{c}\bar{s}1}^{0}
in
B^{+}\to D^{+}D^{*-}K^{+}
to be
3.54_{-1.39}^{+2.16}\%
in the
\bar{D}K^{*}
molecular scenario. The fit fraction of
T_{\bar{c}\bar{s}1}^{0}
in
B^{+}\to D^{*+}D^{*-}K^{+}
is estimated to be
4.60_{-1.61}^{+2.16}
, which is comparable to that in the
B^{+}\to D^{+}D^{*-}K^{+}
process. Thus, we suggest searching for
T_{\bar{c}\bar{s}1}^{0}
in
B^{+}\to D^{*+}D^{*-}K^{+}
.
