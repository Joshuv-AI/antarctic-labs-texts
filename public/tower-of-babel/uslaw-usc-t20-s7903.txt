20 U.S.C. § 7903 — Privacy of assessment results
Citation: us:usc:20:7903
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section7903
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 70 (Strengthening And Improvement Of Elementary And Secondary Schools); Subchapter VIII (General Provisions); Part F (Uniform Provisions); Subpart 2 (Other Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any results from an individual assessment referred to in this chapter of a student that become part of the education records of the student shall have the protections provided in section 1232g of this title.
