35 U.S.C. § 153 — How issued
Citation: us:usc:35:153
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title35-section153
Context: United States Code, Title 35 (Patents); Part II (Patentability Of Inventions And Grant Of Patents); Chapter 14 (Issue Of Patent). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Patents shall be issued in the name of the United States of America, under the seal of the Patent and Trademark Office, and shall be signed by the Director or have his signature placed thereon and shall be recorded in the Patent and Trademark Office.
