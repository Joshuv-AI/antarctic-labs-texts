48 C.F.R. § 528.202 — 528.202 Acceptability of corporate sureties.
Citation: us:cfr:48:528.202
Creator: Chapter 5—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-528.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 528 (Bonds And Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Corporate surety bonds must be manually signed by the Attorney-in-Fact or officer of the surety company and the corporate seal affixed. The contracting officer may waive failure of the surety to affix the corporate seal as a minor informality. (See B-184120, July 2, 1975, 75-2 CPD 9.)
