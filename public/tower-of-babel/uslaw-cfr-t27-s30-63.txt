27 C.F.R. § 30.63 — Table 3, for determining the number of proof gallons from the weight and proof of spirituous liquor.
Citation: us:cfr:27:30.63
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-30.63
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 30 (Gauging Manual). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the weight or proof of a quantity of distilled spirits is not found in Table 2, the proof gallons may be ascertained from Table 3. The wine gallons (at 60 degrees Fahrenheit) may be ascertained by dividing the proof gallons by the proof.
