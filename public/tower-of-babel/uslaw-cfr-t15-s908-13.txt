15 C.F.R. § 908.13 — Address of letters.
Citation: us:cfr:15:908.13
Creator: National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-908.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 908 (Maintaining Records And Submitting Reports On Weather Modification Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Letters and other communications intended for the Administrator, in connection with weather modification reporting or activities, shall be addressed to: The Administrator, National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration, Environmental Modification Office, Rockville, Md. 20852.
