9 C.F.R. § 392.2 — Definition of petition.
Citation: us:cfr:9:392.2
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-392.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 392 (Petitions For Rulemaking). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of this part, a “petition” is a written request to issue, amend, or repeal a regulation administered by FSIS. A request to issue, amend, or repeal a document that interprets a regulation administered by FSIS may also be submitted by petition.
