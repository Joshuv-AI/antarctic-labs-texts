46 C.F.R. § 199.240 — Muster stations.
Citation: us:cfr:46:199.240
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-199.240
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 199 (Lifesaving Systems For Certain Inspected Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each passenger vessel must, in addition to meeting the requirements of § 199.110, have muster stations that—
(a) Are near the embarkation stations, unless a muster station is also an embarkation station;
(b) Permit ready access to the embarkation station, unless a muster station is also an embarkation station; and
(c) Have sufficient room to marshal and instruct passengers and special personnel.
