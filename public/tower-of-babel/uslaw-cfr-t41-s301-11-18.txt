41 C.F.R. § 301-11.18 — -11.18 Reimbursement for advance room deposit.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-11.18
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-11.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-11 (11—Subsistence Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agency may reimburse an advance room deposit required by a lodging facility to secure a room reservation before scheduled official travel. If the employee fails to perform the scheduled travel for reasons unacceptable to the agency and forfeits the deposit, the employee is indebted to the Government and must repay the amount as prescribed by the agency.
