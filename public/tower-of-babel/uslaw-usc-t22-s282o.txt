22 U.S.C. § 282o — Selective capital increase and amendment of the Articles of Agreement
Citation: us:usc:22:282o
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section282o
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter XI (International Finance Corporation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Vote authorized
The United States Governor of the Corporation is authorized to vote in favor of a resolution to increase the capital stock of the Corporation by $130,000,000.
(b) Amendment of the Articles of Agreement
The United States Governor of the Corporation is authorized to agree to and accept an amendment to Article IV, Section 3(a) of the Articles of Agreement of the Corporation that achieves an increase in basic votes to 5.55 percent of total votes.
