5 C.F.R. § 894.509 — What are the QLEs that are consistent with increasing my type of enrollment?

Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 894 (Federal Employees Dental And Vision Insurance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Marriage; except for a TEI who is an unremarried survivor, widow or widower; TEI former spouse; and TEI child(ren);
(b) Acquiring an eligible child or TEI child; or
(c) Loss of other dental or vision coverage by an eligible family member or TEI family member.
