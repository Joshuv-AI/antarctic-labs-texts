12 C.F.R. § 1080.3 — Policy as to private controversies.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1080.3
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1080.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1080 (Rules Relating To Investigations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Bureau shall act only in the public interest and will not initiate an investigation or take other enforcement action when the alleged violation is merely a matter of private controversy and does not tend to affect adversely the public interest.
