42 U.S.C. § 4956 — Participation of program beneficiaries
Citation: us:usc:42:4956
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section4956
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 66 (Domestic Volunteer Services); Subchapter I (National Volunteer Antipoverty Programs); Part A (Volunteers In Service To America). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To the maximum extent practicable, the people of the communities to be served by volunteers under this subchapter shall participate in planning, developing, and implementing programs thereunder, and the Director, after consultation with sponsoring agencies (including volunteers assigned to them) and the people served by such agencies, shall establish in regulations, a continuing mechanism for the meaningful participation of such program beneficiaries.
