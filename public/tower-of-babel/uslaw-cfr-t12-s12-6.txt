12 C.F.R. § 12.6 — Fees.
Citation: us:cfr:12:12.6
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-12.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 12 (Recordkeeping And Confirmation Requirements For Securities Transactions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A national bank may charge a reasonable fee for providing notification pursuant to § 12.5(b), (c), and (e). A national bank may not charge a fee for providing notification pursuant to § 12.4 or § 12.5 (a) and (d).
