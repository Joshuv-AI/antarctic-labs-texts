36 C.F.R. § 13.1326 — Snowmachines.
Citation: us:cfr:36:13.1326
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-13.1326
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 13 (National Park System Units In Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The use of snowmachines is prohibited within the EGDA, except—
(a) On Exit Glacier Road;
(b) In parking areas;
(c) On a designated route through the Exit Glacier Campground to Exit Creek;
(d) Within Exit Creek; and
(e) For NPS administrative activities.
