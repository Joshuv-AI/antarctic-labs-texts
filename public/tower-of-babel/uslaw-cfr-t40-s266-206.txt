40 C.F.R. § 266.206 — Standards applicable to the treatment and disposal of waste military munitions.
Citation: us:cfr:40:266.206
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-266.206
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 266 (Standards For The Management Of Specific Hazardous Wastes And Specific Types Of Hazardous Waste Management Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The treatment and disposal of hazardous waste military munitions are subject to the applicable permitting, procedural, and technical standards in 40 CFR Parts 260 through 270.
