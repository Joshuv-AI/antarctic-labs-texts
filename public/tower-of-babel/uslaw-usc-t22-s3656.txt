22 U.S.C. § 3656 — Uniform application of standards and rates
Citation: us:usc:22:3656
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section3656
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 51 (Panama Canal); Subchapter I (Administration And Regulations); Part 2 (Employees); Subpart ii (Wage And Employment Practices). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The standards established pursuant to section 3653 of this title and the rates of basic pay established pursuant to section 3642 of this title shall be applied without regard to whether the employee or individual concerned is a citizen of the United States or a citizen of the Republic of Panama.
