29 C.F.R. § 1401.30 — Other rights and services.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1401.30
Creator: Federal Mediation And Conciliation Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1401.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1401 (Public Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this subpart shall be construed to entitle any person, as of right, to any service or to the disclosure of any record to which such person is not entitled under the FOIA.
