14 C.F.R. § 1261.508 — Offset against a judgment.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1261.508
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1261.508
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1261 (Processing Of Monetary Claims (General)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Collection by offset against a judgment obtained by a debtor against the United States shall be accomplished in accordance with 31 U.S.C. 3728.
