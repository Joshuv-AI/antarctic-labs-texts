49 C.F.R. § 1185.3 — Procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1185.3
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1185.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1185 (Interlocking Officers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The original application or petition shall be signed by the individual applicant or petitioner and shall be verified under oath. Petitions and applications should comply with the STB's general rules of practice set forth at 49 CFR part 1104. Applications or petitions may be made by persons on their own behalf.
