48 C.F.R. § 414.407-3 — 414.407-3 Other mistakes disclosed before award.
Citation: us:cfr:48:414.407-3
Creator: Chapter 4—Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-414.407-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 414 (Sealed Bidding). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The authority to make the determinations under FAR 14.407-3(a), (b), and (d) is delegated, without power of redelegation, to the HCA. The authority to make the determination under FAR 14.407-3(c) is delegated to the contracting officer. Each determination pursuant to FAR 14.407- 3 shall have the concurrence of the Office of the General Counsel (OGC).
