22 U.S.C. § 2680a — Compensation for disability or death
Citation: us:usc:22:2680a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2680a
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 38 (Department Of State). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Section 1651 of title 42 shall not apply with respect to such contracts as the Secretary of State may determine which are contracts with persons employed to perform work for the Department of State or the Foreign Service on an intermittent basis for not more than 90 days in a calendar year.
