47 C.F.R. § 76.1601 — Deletion or repositioning of broadcast signals.
Citation: us:cfr:47:76.1601
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-76.1601
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 76 (Multichannel Video And Cable Television Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A cable operator shall provide written notice to any broadcast television station at least 30 days prior to either deleting from carriage or repositioning that station.
