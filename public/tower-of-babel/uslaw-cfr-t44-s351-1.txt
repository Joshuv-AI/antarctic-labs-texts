44 C.F.R. § 351.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:44:351.1
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-351.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 351 (Radiological Emergency Planning And Preparedness). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets out Federal agency roles and assigns tasks regarding Federal assistance to State and local governments in their radiological emergency planning and preparedness activities. Assignments in this part are applicable to radiological accidents at fixed nuclear facilities and transportation accidents involving radioactive materials.
