45 C.F.R. § 98.17 — Period covered by Plan.
Citation: us:cfr:45:98.17
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-98.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 98 (Child Care And Development Fund). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) For States, Territories, and Indian Tribes the Plan shall cover a period of three years.
(b) The Lead Agency shall submit a new Plan prior to the expiration of the time period specified in paragraph (a) of this section, at such time as required by the Secretary in written instructions.
