30 U.S.C. § 25 — Affidavit of citizenship
Citation: us:usc:30:25
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section25
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 2 (Mineral Lands And Regulations In General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Applicants for mineral patents, if residing beyond the limits of the district wherein the claim is situated, may make any oath or affidavit required for proof of citizenship before the clerk of any court of record or before any notary public of any State or Territory.
