43 C.F.R. § 3203.18 — What happens to parcels that receive no bids at a competitive lease sale?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3203.18
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3203.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3200 (Geothermal Resource Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Lands offered at a competitive lease sale that receive no bids will be available for leasing in accordance with subpart 3204.
