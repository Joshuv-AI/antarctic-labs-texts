30 U.S.C. § 1473 — Revenue and customs or tariff treatment of deep seabed mining unaffected
Citation: us:usc:30:1473
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section1473
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 26 (Deep Seabed Hard Mineral Resources); Subchapter III (Enforcement And Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as otherwise provided in sections 4495 to 4498 1 of title 26, nothing in this chapter shall affect the application of title 26. Nothing in this chapter shall affect the application of the customs or tariff laws of the United States.
