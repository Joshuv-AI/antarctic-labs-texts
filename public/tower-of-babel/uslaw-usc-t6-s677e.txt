6 U.S.C. § 677e — Rule of construction
Citation: us:usc:6:677e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 1 (Homeland Security Organization); Subchapter XVIII (Cybersecurity And Infrastructure Security Agency); Part C (Declaration Of A Significant Incident). Nothing in this part shall be construed to impair or limit the ability of the Director to carry out the authorized activities of the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency.
