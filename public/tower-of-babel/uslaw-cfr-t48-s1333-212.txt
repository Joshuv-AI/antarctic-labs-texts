48 C.F.R. § 1333.212 — 1333.212 Contracting officer's duties upon appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1333.212
Creator: Chapter 13—Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1333.212
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1333 (Protests, Disputes, And Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Only Procurement Counsel will communicate with the Civilian Board of Contract Appeals or the Department of Justice regarding appeals of contracting officer decisions. Procurement Counsel shall be responsible for preparation and submission of all filings with the Board.
