49 U.S.C. § 41301 — Requirement for a permit
Citation: us:usc:49:41301
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section41301
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Air Commerce And Safety); Subpart ii (Economic Regulation); Chapter 413 (Foreign Air Transportation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A foreign air carrier may provide foreign air transportation only if the foreign air carrier holds a permit issued under this chapter authorizing the foreign air transportation.
