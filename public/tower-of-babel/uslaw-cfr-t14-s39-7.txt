14 C.F.R. § 39.7 — What is the legal effect of failing to comply with an airworthiness directive?
Citation: us:cfr:14:39.7
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-39.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 39 (Airworthiness Directives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Anyone who operates a product that does not meet the requirements of an applicable airworthiness directive is in violation of this section.
