28 C.F.R. § 83.625 — Criminal drug statute.
Citation: us:cfr:28:83.625
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-83.625
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 83 (Government-Wide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Grants)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Criminal drug statute means a Federal or non-Federal criminal statute involving the manufacture, distribution, dispensing, use, or possession of any controlled substance.
