4 C.F.R. § 25.1 — Applicability and governing laws.

Creator: Government Accountability Office
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-4/section-25.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 4 (Accounts), Part 25 (Conduct In The Government Accountability Office Building And On Its Grounds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These rules and regulations, and the laws of the United States and the District of Columbia, apply to the Government Accountability Office (GAO) Building and its grounds, 441 G Street NW., in the District of Columbia, and to all persons while in the building or while entering or leaving it.
