46 C.F.R. § 148.50 — Cargoes subject to this subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:46:148.50
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-148.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 148 (Carriage Of Bulk Solid Materials That Require Special Handling). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this subpart apply to each bulk shipment of—
(a) A material listed in Table 148.10 of this part; and
(b) Any solid material shipped under the terms of a Coast Guard special permit.
