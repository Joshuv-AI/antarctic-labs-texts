6 C.F.R. § 126.9 — Scope of review.
Citation: us:cfr:6:126.9
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security, Office Of The Secretary
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-6/section-126.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 6 (Domestic Security), Part 126 (Transportation Security Oversight Board Review Panel Process And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A TSOB Review Panel reviews an ALJ's decision to address whether the decision is supported by substantial evidence in the record before the TSOB Review Panel.
(b) A TSOB Review Panel will not consider the constitutionality of any statute, regulation, Executive order, or order issued by the TSA.
