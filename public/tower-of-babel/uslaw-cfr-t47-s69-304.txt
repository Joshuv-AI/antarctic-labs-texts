47 C.F.R. § 69.304 — Subscriber line cable and wire facilities.
Citation: us:cfr:47:69.304
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-69.304
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 69 (Access Charges). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Investment in local exchange subscriber lines shall be assigned to the Common Line element.
(b) Investment in interstate and foreign private lines and interstate WATS access lines shall be assigned to the Special access element.
