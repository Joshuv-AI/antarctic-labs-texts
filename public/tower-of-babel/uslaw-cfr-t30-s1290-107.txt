30 C.F.R. § 1290.107 — Where are the rules concerning the effect of the Department not issuing a decision in my appeal within the statutory time frame?
Citation: us:cfr:30:1290.107
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1290.107
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1290 (Appeal Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If your appeal involves monetary or nonmonetary obligations under Federal oil and gas leases, the rules concerning the effect of the Department not issuing a final decision in your appeal within the 33-month period prescribed under 30 U.S.C. 1724(h) are located in 43 CFR part 4, subpart J.
