25 C.F.R. § 214.28 — Location of sites for mines and buildings.
Citation: us:cfr:25:214.28
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-214.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 214 (Leasing Of Osage Reservation Lands, Oklahoma, For Mining, Except Oil And Gas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In event of disagreement between two or more mineral lessees regarding sites for the location of wells, mines, buildings, plants, etc., the same shall be determined by the superintendent after investigation and after due consideration of prior right of any lessee by reason of date of approval of lease.
