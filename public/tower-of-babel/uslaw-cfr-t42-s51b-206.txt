42 C.F.R. § 51b.206 — b.206 How can grant funds be used?
Citation: us:cfr:42:51b.206
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-51b.206
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 51b (PART 51b—PROJECT GRANTS FOR PREVENTIVE HEALTH SERVICES). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Grant funds awarded under this subpart may be used to purchase supplies, materials, and equipment for childhood immunization programs. Grant funds also may be used to pay for salaries or wages and related expenses for personnel directly involved in the planning, organization, promotion, epidemiology, surveillance, and other program activities.
