7 C.F.R. § 4284.1053 — Award approval.
Citation: us:cfr:7:4284.1053
Creator: Rural Business-Cooperative Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-4284.1053
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 4284 (Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Agency will approve an award once the Applicant has met all the conditions of the award. The approval will be conveyed through the execution of Form RD 4280-2, which is the Financial Assistance Agreement, and provides all terms of the award. Once the award has been approved, the Recipient may begin work on the project and incur costs.
