9 C.F.R. § 590.590 — Use of irradiated shell eggs to produce egg products.
Citation: us:cfr:9:590.590
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-590.590
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 590 (Inspection Of Eggs And Egg Products (Egg Products Inspection Act)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Irradiated shell eggs used to produce pasteurized egg products must be used in conjunction with heat or another lethality treatment sufficient to produce egg products that are edible without additional preparation to achieve food safety. Unless otherwise approved by FDA, the irradiation treatment of the shell eggs must precede the heat or other lethality treatment applied to the egg products.
