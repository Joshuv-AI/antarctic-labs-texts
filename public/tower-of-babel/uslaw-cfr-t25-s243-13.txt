25 C.F.R. § 243.13 — Who may appeal an action under this part?
Citation: us:cfr:25:243.13
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-243.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 243 (Reindeer In Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any interested party adversely affected by a decision under this part has the right of appeal as provided in 25 CFR part 2 and 43 CFR part 4, subpart D.
