9 C.F.R. § 555.2 — Fish product transported within the United States as part of export movement.
Citation: us:cfr:9:555.2
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-555.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 555 (Transportation Of Fish Products In Commerce). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When any shipment of any fish product is offered to any carrier for transportation within the United States as a part of an export movement, the same certificate shall be required as if the shipment were destined to a point within the United States.
