25 C.F.R. § 37.130 — Who establishes boundaries for Off-Reservation Boarding Schools?
Citation: us:cfr:25:37.130
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-37.130
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 37 (Geographic Boundaries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary or the Secretary's designee, in consultation with the affected Tribes, establishes the boundaries for off-reservation boarding schools (ORBS).
