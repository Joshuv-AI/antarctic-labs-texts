49 U.S.C. § 48104 — Operations and maintenance
Citation: us:usc:49:48104
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section48104
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part C (Financing); Chapter 481 (Airport And Airway Trust Fund Authorizations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The balance of the money available in the Airport and Airway Trust Fund established under section 9502 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (26 U.S.C. 9502) may be appropriated to the Secretary of Transportation out of the Fund for—
(1)
direct costs the Secretary incurs to flight check, operate, and maintain air navigation facilities referred to in section 44502(a)(1)(A) of this title safely and efficiently; and
(2)
the costs of services provided under international agreements related to the joint financing of air navigation services assessed against the United States Government.
