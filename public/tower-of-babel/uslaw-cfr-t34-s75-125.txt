34 C.F.R. § 75.125 — Submit a separate application to each program.
Citation: us:cfr:34:75.125
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-75.125
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 75 (Direct Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An applicant shall submit a separate application to each program under which it wants a grant.
