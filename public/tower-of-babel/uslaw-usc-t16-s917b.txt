16 U.S.C. § 917b — Cooperation of other Federal agencies
Citation: us:usc:16:917b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section917b
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 14A (Whale Conservation And Protection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All Federal agencies shall cooperate, to the fullest extent possible, with the Secretary of Commerce in preparing the study and recommendations required by section 917a of this title.
