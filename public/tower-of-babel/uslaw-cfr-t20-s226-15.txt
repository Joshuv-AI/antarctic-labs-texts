20 C.F.R. § 226.15 — Deductions from employee regular annuity rate.
Citation: us:cfr:20:226.15
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-226.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 226 (Computing Employee, Spouse, And Divorced Spouse Annuities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The employee annuity as computed under this subpart may be reduced by premiums required for supplemental medicare coverage, income tax withholding, recovery of debts due the Federal government, garnishment pursuant to part 350 of the chapter and property awards as provided for in part 295 of this chapter.
