34 C.F.R. § 647.2 — Who is eligible for a grant?
Citation: us:cfr:34:647.2
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-647.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 647 (Ronald E. Mcnair Postbaccalaureate Achievement Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Institutions of higher education and combinations of those institutions are eligible for grants to carry out McNair projects.
