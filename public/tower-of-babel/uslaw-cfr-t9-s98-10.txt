9 C.F.R. § 98.10 — Other importations.
Citation: us:cfr:9:98.10
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-98.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 98 (Importation Of Certain Animal Embryos And Animal Semen). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notwithstanding other provisions in this part, the Administrator may in specific cases allow the importation and entry into the United States of embryos other than as provided for in this part under such conditions as the Administrator may prescribe to prevent the introduction into the United States of infectious animal diseases.
