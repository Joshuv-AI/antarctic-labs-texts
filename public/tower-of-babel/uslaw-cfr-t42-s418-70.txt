42 C.F.R. § 418.70 — Condition of participation: Furnishing of non-core services.
Citation: us:cfr:42:418.70
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-418.70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 418 (Hospice Care). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A hospice must ensure that the services described in § 418.72 through § 418.78 are provided directly by the hospice or under arrangements made by the hospice as specified in § 418.100. These services must be provided in a manner consistent with current standards of practice.
