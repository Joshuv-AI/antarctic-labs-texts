19 U.S.C. § 2517 — Availability of information to Members of Congress designated as official advisers
Citation: us:usc:19:2517
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section2517
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 13 (Trade Agreements Act Of 1979); Subchapter I (Government Procurement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States Trade Representative shall make available to the Members of Congress designated as official advisers pursuant to section 2211 of this title information compiled by the Committee on Government Procurement under article XIX(5) of the Agreement.
