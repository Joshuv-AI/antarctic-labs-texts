10 U.S.C. § 2868 — Utility services: furnishing for certain buildings
Citation: us:usc:10:2868
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2868
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Property); Chapter 169 (Military Construction And Military Family Housing); Subchapter III (Administration Of Military Construction And Military Family Housing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Appropriations for the Department of Defense may be used for utility services for buildings constructed at private cost, as authorized by law.
