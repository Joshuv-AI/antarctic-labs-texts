32 C.F.R. § 553.37 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:32:553.37
Creator: Department Of The Army
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-553.37
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 553 (Army Cemeteries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart specifies the eligibility for interment and inurnment in the twenty-five Army Post Cemeteries, the West Point Post Cemetery, NY and the U.S. Disciplinary Barracks Cemetery at Fort Leavenworth, KS.
