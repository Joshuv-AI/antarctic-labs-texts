28 C.F.R. § 76.28 — Location of hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:28:76.28
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-76.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 76 (Rules Of Procedure For Assessment Of Civil Penalties For Possession Of Certain Controlled Substances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The hearing shall be held in the judicial district of the United States Attorney's Office having jurisdiction over the matter.
