39 C.F.R. § 955.19 — (Rule 19) Posthearing briefs.
Citation: us:cfr:39:955.19
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-955.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 955 (Rules Of Practice Before The Postal Service Board Of Contract Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Posthearing briefs may be submitted as ordered by the Board at the end of the hearing. Ordinarily, briefs will be filed simultaneously on a date and by a method established by the Board. The Board may also allow reply briefs.
