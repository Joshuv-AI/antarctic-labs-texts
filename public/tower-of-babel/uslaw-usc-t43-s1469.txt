43 U.S.C. § 1469 — Employment and compensation of personnel to perform work occasioned by emergencies
Citation: us:usc:43:1469
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section1469
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 31 (Department Of The Interior). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, persons may be employed or otherwise contracted with by the Secretary of the Interior to perform work occasioned by emergencies such as fire, flood, storm, or any other unavoidable cause and may be compensated at regular rates of pay without regard to Sundays, Federal holidays, and the regular workweek.
