42 C.F.R. § 408.52 — Change from direct remittance to deduction.
Citation: us:cfr:42:408.52
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-408.52
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 408 (Premiums For Supplementary Medical Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a direct remittance enrollee becomes entitled to monthly benefits—
(a) The SMI premiums are deducted from those benefits; and
(b) The enrollee is notified of the deduction and of any adjustment of the initial benefit check that is required to collect overdue premiums or refund premiums paid in advance.
