17 C.F.R. § 274.301 — Notification of claim of exemption pursuant to Rule 6e-2 or Rule 6e-3(T) under the Investment Company Act.
Citation: us:cfr:17:274.301
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 274 (Forms Prescribed Under The Investment Company Act Of 1940). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This form shall be filed with the Commission as required by § 270.6e-2 or § 270.6e-3(T) of this chapter by each insurance company with respect to each separate account for which exemption is claimed pursuant to § 270.6e-2 or § 270.6e-3(T).
