36 C.F.R. § 1206.34 — What type of proposal is ineligible for a records grant?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1206.34
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1206.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1206 (National Historical Publications And Records Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to other programmatic limitations established by the Commission as found in the grant opportunity announcements, NHPRC does not support proposals:
(a) For building projects;
(b) To purchase manuscripts or historical records;
(c) For projects involving substantial work with artifacts, library materials, or works of art; or
(d) For exhibits or celebrations, reenactments, and other observations of historical events.
