40 C.F.R. § 60.1125 — What must I include in my siting analysis?
Citation: us:cfr:40:60.1125
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.1125
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 60 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Include an analysis of how your municipal waste combustion unit affects four areas:
(1) Ambient air quality.
(2) Visibility.
(3) Soils.
(4) Vegetation.
(b) Include an analysis of alternatives for controlling air pollution that minimize potential risks to the public health and the environment.
