Abstract

Following Bakry Émery

The comparison theory and the structure theory for the Bakry-Émery Ricci tensor lower bound with bounded

2.1.
f
-Laplacian,
N
-Bakry Émery Ricci Tensor and splitting theorem

The
f
-Laplacian
\Delta_{f}
is the differential operator defined by

\Delta_{f}u:=e^{f}\operatorname{div}(e^{-f}\nabla u)=\Delta u-\langle\nabla u,\nabla f\rangle

It is clear that
\Delta_{f}
is a self-adjoint operator with respect to the weighted measure
e^{-f}\mathrm{dVol_{g}}
. Assume
u,v\in C^{2,\alpha}_{0}(M)
, then we have

\int_{M}u(\Delta_{f}v)\,e^{-f}\mathrm{dVol_{g}}=-\int_{M}\langle\nabla u,\nabla v\rangle\,e^{-f}\mathrm{dVol_{g}}=\int_{M}v(\Delta_{f}u)\,e^{-f}\mathrm{dVol_{g}}

Lichnerowicz
[
Lic70
]
and Bakry-Émery
[
BÉ06
]
introduced a natural generalization of the Ricci tensor

\operatorname{Ric}_{f}:=\operatorname{Ric}+\mathrm{Hess}(f)

More generally, the
N
-Bakry Émery Ricci tensor is defined by

(2.1)

\operatorname{Ric}^{N}_{f}:=\operatorname{Ric}+\mathrm{Hess}(f)-\dfrac{1}{N}df\otimes df

By the definition of the
f
-Laplacian and the classical Bochner formula on Riemannian manifolds, one immediately obtains a Bochner formula for weighted manifolds and the relationship to the
N
-Bakry-Émery Ricci tensor.

(2.2)

\dfrac{1}{2}\Delta_{f}|\nabla u|^{2}=|\mathrm{Hess}(u)|^{2}+\langle\nabla u,\nabla\Delta_{f}u\rangle+\operatorname{Ric}_{f}(\nabla u,\nabla u)\geq\dfrac{(\Delta_{f}u)^{2}}{N+n}+\langle\nabla u,\nabla\Delta_{f}u\rangle+\operatorname{Ric}^{N}_{f}(\nabla u,\nabla u)

We recall the classical splitting theorem for the
N
-Bakry Émery Ricci tensor proved by Fang,Li and Zhang
[
FLZ07
, Theorem 1.3]

Theorem 2.1
.

Let
(M,g)
be a complete connected Riemannian

-dimensional manifold and

For more comparison geometry results on the Bakry-Émery Ricci tensor, we refer to Wei-Wylie’s paper
