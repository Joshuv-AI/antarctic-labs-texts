Doubly heavy hadron production in ultraperipheral collisions
Shi-Yuan Li

Abstract
The inclusive production of pseudoscalar heavy quarkoniua ( η c , η b , B c \eta_{c},\,\eta_{b},\,B_{c} ), double heavy baryon Ξ Q ​ Q ′ \Xi_{QQ^{\prime}} ( Q ( ′ ) = c , b Q^{(\prime)}=c,\,b quarks) and tetraquark T Q ​ Q T_{QQ} in heavy ion ultraperipheral collisions (UPCs) are studied. Numerical results indicate that the experimental investigation for η c , Ξ c ​ c \eta_{c},\,\Xi_{cc} , and T c ​ c T_{cc} are feasible at the coming HL-LHC and future FCC. The heavy ion UPCs open another door of the study on the production of these doubly heavy hadrons.

1
Why ultraperipheral collisions?

In heavy ion collisions, due to the large proton charge number
Z
, the highly relativistic ions become a strong source of electromagnetic radiation, which can be considered as fluxes of quasi-real photons in the equivalent photon approximation
[
1
,
2
]
.
Thus, the heavy ion collisions can be used to study photon-photon, photon-proton, and photon-Pb interactions in the ultraperipheral collisions (UPCs)
[
3
,
4
,
5
]
.
In this article, we focus on the photon-photon fusion (photon-initiated) mechanism in heavy ion UPCs.
In comparison with the central heavy ion collision, the quasi-real photon interaction in UPCs has low event multiplicity because the ion impact parameter is larger than twice the ion radius and the ions are kept unbroken, where there is no noisy hadron background.
In comparison with the photon-photon fusion in proton-proton collision, the photon density is in particular enhanced by the squared ion charge
Z^{2}
for each ion, leading to an overall
Z^{4}

enhancement in the production rate.
In comparison with the photon-photon interaction at an electron-positron collider, one could expect that the de-excitation photons with energies upto 80 GeV and 600 GeV
[
6
]
can be emitted in Pb-Pb UPCs at the high luminosity Large Hadron Collider (HL-LHC)
[
7
,
8
,
9
]
and the Future Circular Collider (FCC)
[
10
,
11
]
, respectively.
Therefore, the photon-initiated UPC processes should be another good laboratory to study the production of heavy hadrons in the electromagnetic environment.

2
Why doubly heavy hadrons?

Doubly heavy hadrons (quarkonia
\mathcal{H}(Q\bar{Q})
, baryons
\Xi_{QQ^{\prime}}
and tetraquarks
T_{QQ^{\prime}}
with
Q^{(\prime)}=c,\,b
quarks) are excellent subjects to study both perturbative QCD and nonperturbative QCD.
The heavy quarkonium is a bound state which consists of a heavy quark and a heavy antiquark.
The production of heavy quarkonium involves the perturbative production of the heavy quarks pair
Q\bar{Q}
and its nonperturbative hadronization into the heavy quarkonium
\mathcal{H}(Q\bar{Q})
.
The nonrelativistic QCD (NRQCD) factorization formalism
[
12
,
13
]
are developed to study the quarkonium production and decay, where the perturbative and nonperturbative effects are factored out.
The creation of the binding
Q\bar{Q}
intermediate state with definite
J^{PC}
and color configuration can be calculated perturbatively under the double series of the strong coupling
\alpha_{s}
and the relative velocity
v
between the heavy quark
Q
and antiquark
\bar{Q}
.
While the nonperturbative hadronization of the intermediate states into the physical heavy quarkonium
\mathcal{H}(Q\bar{Q})
is described by the long distance matrix elements (LDMEs).
For doubly heavy baryons
\Xi_{QQ^{\prime}}
and tetraquarks
T_{QQ^{\prime}}
, the doubly heavy components within those hadrons indicates typically nonrelativistic feature, hence they tend to stay close and form heavy-heavy diquark
(QQ^{\prime})
.
In this way, the production of doubly heavy baryons and tetraquarks can also be described by NRQCD, which factorizes the production into two steps.
The first step is to produce doubly heavy diquark with given spin-color structure, which is perturbatively calculatable.
The second step is reserved for the hadronization of the diquark though nonperturbative QCD mechanism, which is encoded into LDMEs and fragmentation functions.

Heavy quarkonium production in UPCs

In this section, the inclusive production of pseudoscalar heavy quarkoniua (
\eta_{c},~\eta_{b}
and
B_{c}
) via photon-photon fusion in UPCs are calculated to QCD next-to-leading order within NRQCD framework
[
14
]
.

In the equivalent photon approximation (EPA) formulism

[
1
,
2
]
, the cross section for the production of final state
X
via photon-photon fusion in UPCs can be factorized into a convolution of the equivalent photon spectra and the cross section for the subprocess of
X
production,

\sigma(\mathrm{A}\mathrm{B}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\gamma\gamma}}{{\longrightarrow}}\mathrm{A}\mathrm{B}X)=\int\frac{dx_{1}}{x_{1}}\frac{dx_{2}}{x_{2}}f(x_{1})f(x_{2})\times\mathrm{d}\hat{\sigma}({\gamma\gamma\rightarrow X}).

(1)

Here
x_{i}=E_{i}/E_{\mathrm{beam}}
is the ratio of the photon energy
E_{i}
to the beam energy
E_{\mathrm{beam}}
, the photon spectrum
f(x)
produced by an ion of charge
Z
reads
[
15
,
6
]

f(x)=\frac{2\alpha Z^{2}}{\pi}\left[\chi K_{0}(\chi)K_{1}(\chi)-\left(1-\gamma_{\mathrm{L}}^{-2}\right)\frac{\chi^{2}}{2}\left(K_{1}^{2}(\chi)-K_{0}^{2}(\chi)\right)\right],

(2)

where the variable
x
is absorbed into
\chi\equiv xm_{N}b_{\mathrm{min}}
, in which
m_{N}
is the nucleon mass and the minimum of impact parameter
b_{\mathrm{min}}
will be set as the nuclear radius.
And
K_{0},~K_{1}
are the modified Bessel functions of the second kind of zero and first order, respectively.
\gamma_{L}=E_{\mathrm{beam}}/m_{N}
is the Lorentz factor.
For the subprocess of inclusive production of pseudoscalar heavy quarkoniua via photon-photon fusion,
\gamma+\gamma\to\mathcal{H}(Q_{1}\bar{Q}_{2})+Q_{2}+\bar{Q}_{1}
, where
\mathcal{H}(Q_{1}\bar{Q}_{2})=\eta_{c}
,
\eta_{b}
or
B_{c}
and
Q_{1,2}
are the heavy charm or bottom quarks accordingly, we have made the calculation up to the next-to-leading order in QCD which has been discussed in details in Ref.
[
16
,
14
]
.

Doubly heavy baryon and tetraquark production in UPCs

We discuss the doubly heavy baryon
\Xi_{QQ^{\prime}}
and tetraquark
T_{QQ}
production through photon-photon fusion and photon-gluon interaction (photoproduction) in UPCs at the LHC and FCC in this section
[
17
]
.
We adopt a phenomenological diquark fragmentation model to describe the production of baryon
\Xi_{QQ^{\prime}}
and cmpact tetraquark
T_{QQ}
.
This diquark fragmentation contribution becomes dominant only in the large transverse momentum
p_{t}
region.
In contrast, there are contributions from other production mechanisms,
i.e.
all the quark constitutes are produced at short-distance first then followed by the hadronization. Such contributions would dominate in the relatively small
p_{t}
region, which are neglected here.

In the phenomenological diquark fragmentation model, the production of baryon
\Xi_{QQ^{\prime}}
and cmpact tetraquark
T_{QQ}
has been factored out into two steps: the perturbative production of the
(QQ^{\prime})
-diquark because of the nonrelativistic nature of doubly heavy diquark, and the nonperturbative hadronization of colored
(QQ^{\prime})
-diquark into baryon
\Xi_{QQ^{\prime}}
or cmpact tetraquark
T_{QQ}
.
The creation of
(QQ^{\prime})
-diquark can be related to the production of quark-antiquark pair
(Q\bar{Q}^{\prime})
by reversing one of the Dirac fermion chain and introducing the color structures of diquarks
[
18
,
17
]
.
The hadronization probabilities of
(QQ^{\prime})
-diquark into baryon
\Xi_{QQ^{\prime}}
or cmpact tetraquark
T_{QQ}
can be determined by fitting the experimental results, the potential models, and the heavy diquark-antiquark symmetry
[
18
,
17
]
.
This phenomenological diquark fragmentation model has been widely employed to study the doubly heavy baryons
[
19
,
20
,
21
,
22
,
23
,
24
,
17
]
and
T_{cc}

[

,

Summary

We investigate the inclusive production of pseudoscalar heavy quarkonia via photon-photon fusion in ultraperipheral collisions (UPCs) within the framework of NRQCD.
Additionally, we study the inclusive production of doubly heavy baryons and tetraquarks via both photon-photon fusion and photoproduction in UPCs, employing a phenomenological diquark fragmentation model.
It is found that the
\eta_{c}
and
\Xi_{cc}^{++}
could potentially be observed via photon-photon fusion in UPCs at the HL-LHC and at future FCC, respectively.
For photoproduction in UPCs, the production yield of
\Xi_{cc}^{++}
is higher than that from photon-photon fusion.
Furthermore, investigations into the
T_{cc}
via photoproduction in UPCs could be conducted at the HL-LHC and the future FCC.
Overall, at the upcoming HL-LHC and the future FCC, heavy-ion UPCs offer an additional avenue for studying the production of doubly heavy hadrons.
