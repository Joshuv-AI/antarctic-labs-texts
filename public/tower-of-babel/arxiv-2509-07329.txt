A Baseline T ​ log 2 ​ T T\log^{2}T Upper Bound for KL-Regularized Prime–Zero Optimal Transport
Zhejun Yang

Abstract
We prove, unconditionally, the baseline upper bound 𝖮𝖳 η ​ ( T ) ≪ T ​ log 2 ​ T ( T → ∞ ) \mathsf{OT}_{\eta}(T)\ \ll\ T\log^{2}T\qquad(T\to\infty) for the KL-regularized unbalanced prime–zero optimal transport cost associated with a Beurling–Selberg type kernel η \eta . Our route is: normalize the cost to c η ∘ ​ ( γ , t ) = η ⁡ ( t ) ​ ( 1 − cos ⁡ ( γ ​ t ) ) c_{\eta}^{\circ}(\gamma,t)=\eta(t)\,(1-\cos(\gamma t)) ; perform Fejér synchronization in the zero variable; derive an integrated (not pointwise) linear upper bound (R1) after averaging; carry out a zero-frequency calibration; and finally evaluate the resulting difference in a smoothed explicit formula with L 1 L^{1} -control, which yields the log 2 ⁡ T \log^{2}T factor, while the Paley–Wiener mass of the probe contributes the scale T T . No use of RH is made.



1
Set-up and smoothing

Fix
0<\alpha<1
and set
\Delta=T^{-\alpha}
. Let
\theta_{\Delta}\in C_{c}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R})
be even with
\int\theta_{\Delta}=1
and
\|\theta^{\prime}_{\Delta}\|_{L^{1}(\mathbb{R})}\asymp\Delta^{-1}
. For
t=\log x
and zero-height
\gamma
, define

\displaystyle\nu_{T}(dt)

\displaystyle:=\Big(\sum_{p^{k}\leq e^{T}}\frac{1}{k}\,\delta_{\log p^{k}}\Big)*\theta_{\Delta}(t)\,dt,

(1.1)

\displaystyle\mu_{\Omega}(d\gamma)

\displaystyle:=\Big(\sum_{\rho=\beta+i\gamma}\mathbbm{1}_{|\gamma|\leq\Omega}\,\delta_{\gamma}\Big)*\theta_{\Delta}(\gamma)\,d\gamma,\qquad\Omega=\kappa T\ \ (\kappa>0\text{ fixed}).

(1.2)

The corrected main terms are
m(t)=e^{t}/t=(\mathrm{li}(e^{t}))^{\prime}
and
n(\gamma)=(2\pi)^{-1}\log(\gamma/2\pi)+O(\gamma^{-1})
for
\gamma\geq 2
. Set

a(t):=\frac{d\nu_{T}}{dt}(t)-m(t),\qquad b(\gamma):=\frac{d\mu_{\Omega}}{d\gamma}(\gamma)-n(\gamma).

(1.3)

Lemma 1.1

(Absolute integrability)
.

Unconditionally, as
T\to\infty
,

\|a\|_{L^{1}([0,T])}\ \ll\ T^{\alpha}\frac{e^{T}}{T}\,e^{-c\sqrt{T}},\qquad\int_{-\Omega}^{\Omega}\!|b(\gamma)|\,d\gamma\ \ll\ T^{\alpha}\log T,

(1.4)

for some absolute
c>0
. Hence all integrals below are absolutely convergent and Fubini–Tonelli applies.

Proof.

Write
G(t):=J(e^{t})-\mathrm{li}(e^{t})
with
J(x)=\sum_{p^{k}\leq x}1/k
. De la Vallée Poussin gives
G(t)=O(e^{t}e^{-c\sqrt{t}}/t)
. Since
a=(G*\theta_{\Delta})^{\prime}
, we get
\|a\|_{L^{1}}\leq\|G\|_{\infty}\|\theta^{\prime}_{\Delta}\|_{L^{1}}\ll T^{\alpha}\frac{e^{T}}{T}e^{-c\sqrt{T}}
. For
b
, set
F(\Gamma):=\mu_{\Omega}([0,\Gamma])-N(\Gamma)
. The Riemann–von Mangoldt formula gives
|F(\Gamma)|\ll\log\Gamma
uniformly in
\Gamma\leq\Omega
, while
b=F*\theta^{\prime}_{\Delta}
, whence
\int_{-\Omega}^{\Omega}|b|\ll\Delta^{-1}\|F\|_{\infty}\ll T^{\alpha}\log T
.
∎

Beurling–Selberg kernel and probes.

Let
\eta=\eta_{T}
be a standard even Beurling–Selberg majorant equal to
1
on
[0,T]
with
\widehat{\eta}\geq 0
,
L^{1}
-controlled boundary ringing, and
\|\widehat{\eta}\|_{L^{1}}\asymp T
(see Vaaler; details in the references). Let
F_{\Lambda}
be the Fejér kernel: even, nonnegative,
\int F_{\Lambda}=1
, and
\widehat{F_{\Lambda}}(\tau)=(1-|\tau|/\Lambda)_{+}
with
\Lambda\asymp\Delta^{-1}=T^{\alpha}
. For an even probe
f
with
\widehat{f}\geq 0
, write

S:=\|\widehat{f}\|_{L^{1}},\quad g:=f\,\widehat{F}_{\Lambda},\quad\beta_{0}:=F_{\Lambda}*\widehat{f},\quad h:=\eta\,g,\quad\widehat{h}=\widehat{\eta}*\beta_{0}.

(1.5)

Lemma 1.2

(Two-way Fejér identity)
.

For all
\gamma,t\in\mathbb{R}
,

1-\widehat{F_{\Lambda}}(t)\cos(\gamma t)\ =\ \int_{\mathbb{R}}\big(1-\cos(\xi t)\big)\,F_{\Lambda}(\gamma-\xi)\,d\xi.

(1.6)

Proof.

Since
\int F_{\Lambda}(\gamma-\xi)e^{i\xi t}\,d\xi=e^{i\gamma t}\widehat{F}_{\Lambda}(t)
, take real parts and subtract from
1
.
∎

Lemma 1.3

(Cosine pairing)
.

Let
h\in L^{1}(\mathbb{R})
be even and
\mu
a finite positive Borel measure. Then

\int_{\mathbb{R}}\!\bigg(\int_{\mathbb{R}}\!h(t)\cos(\xi t)\,dt\bigg)\,\mu(d\xi)\ =\ \int_{\mathbb{R}}\widehat{h}(\xi)\,\mu(d\xi).

(1.7)

(Both iterated integrals converge absolutely by the stated hypotheses; Fubini applies.)

2
Integrated R1 bound after Fejér synchronization

We consider the KFR dual for
c_{\eta}^{\circ}(\gamma,t)=\eta(t)(1-\cos(\gamma t))
. By standard truncation, we may restrict to potentials
\varphi,\psi\leq 0
.

Proposition 2.1

(R1, Fejér-averaged bound)
.

Let
(\varphi,\psi)
be feasible with
\varphi,\psi\leq 0
and
\varphi(\gamma)+\psi(t)\leq\eta(t)\big(1-\cos(\gamma t)\big)
. For even
f
with
\widehat{f}\geq 0
, define
S,g,h,\widehat{h}
as in (
1.5
). Then

-\!\int\varphi\,d\mu_{\Omega}-\!\int\psi\,d\nu_{T}\ \leq\ \int\widehat{h}\,d\mu_{\Omega}\ -\ \int h\,d\nu_{T}\ +\ S\!\int\eta\,d\nu_{T}.

(2.1)

Proof.

Let
\overline{\mu}_{\Omega}:=\mu_{\Omega}/\mu_{\Omega}(\mathbb{R})
be the probability normalization. Start from feasibility

\varphi(\gamma)+\psi(t)\leq\eta(t)(1-\cos(\gamma t))
, convolve in
\gamma
with
F_{\Lambda}
, and use Lemma
1.2
:

(F_{\Lambda}*\varphi)(\gamma)+\psi(t)\ \leq\ \eta(t)\!\int_{\mathbb{R}}\!(1-\cos(\xi t))\,F_{\Lambda}(\gamma-\xi)\,d\xi.

(2.2)

Integrate (
2.2
) in
\gamma
against
d\overline{\mu}_{\Omega}(\gamma)
, multiply both sides by the probability weight
\widehat{f}(\xi)/S
, integrate in
\xi
, and then integrate in
t
against
d\nu_{T}(t)
; by Fubini,

\displaystyle\int(F_{\Lambda}*\varphi)\,d\overline{\mu}_{\Omega}\ +\ \int\psi\,d\nu_{T}

\displaystyle\leq\int\eta\,d\nu_{T}\ -\ \frac{1}{S}\!\int\!\Big(\int\eta(t)\cos(\xi t)\,d\nu_{T}(t)\Big)\,\widehat{f}(\xi)\,(F_{\Lambda}*\overline{\mu}_{\Omega})(d\xi).

Apply Lemma
1.3
with
h(t)=\eta(t)f(t)\widehat{F}_{\Lambda}(t)
and the finite measure
(F_{\Lambda}*\overline{\mu}_{\Omega})
to identify the last term with
\int(F_{\Lambda}*\widehat{h})\,d\overline{\mu}_{\Omega}
. Since
F_{\Lambda}
is a probability kernel and
\widehat{h}\geq 0
,
\int(F_{\Lambda}*\widehat{h})\,d\overline{\mu}_{\Omega}\geq\int\widehat{h}\,d\overline{\mu}_{\Omega}
. Also
F_{\Lambda}*\varphi\leq 0
, hence
-\!\int(F_{\Lambda}*\varphi)\,d\overline{\mu}_{\Omega}\geq 0
. Rearranging gives

-\!\int\psi\,d\nu_{T}\ \leq\ \int\eta\,d\nu_{T}\ -\ \int\widehat{h}\,d\overline{\mu}_{\Omega}.

Finally add
-\!\int\varphi\,d\overline{\mu}_{\Omega}\geq 0
and multiply both sides by
\mu_{\Omega}(\mathbb{R})
; noting
\int h\,d\nu_{T}=\int\eta g\,d\nu_{T}
, we obtain (
2.1
).
∎

Remark 2.2
.

No pointwise separable envelope is used. The bound (
2.1
) is derived only after Fejér averaging and integration; at no point do we compare
-\!\int\varphi
with
-\!\int(F_{\Lambda}*\varphi)
.

3
Zero-frequency calibration

Let
m
be the prime-side main density and write
\nu_{T}=m+a
as above. We adjust
f
by a narrow, nonnegative bump
h_{0}
in frequency to achieve

S\!\int\eta\,m\ =\ \int h\,m,

(3.1)

while preserving
\widehat{f}\geq 0
and then rescale
f
so that
S=\|\widehat{f}\|_{L^{1}}\asymp T^{-1}
. This is achieved by choosing
\widehat{h}_{0}
supported in
[-\xi_{0},\xi_{0}]
,
\int\widehat{h}_{0}=1
, and solving
\mathcal{L}(c):=S_{c}\!\int\eta\,m-\int h_{c}\,m=0
for small
|c|
, where
f_{c}=f+c\,h_{0}
and
S_{c},\ h_{c}
are defined as in (
1.5
). The map
\mathcal{L}
is affine and nonconstant (tune
\xi_{0}
if necessary), hence has a small root
c^{\ast}
with
\widehat{f}_{c^{\ast}}\geq 0
; a scalar rescaling enforces
S_{c^{\ast}}\asymp T^{-1}
.

Corollary 3.1

(Calibrated reduction)
.

With
f
calibrated as above and
h=\eta g
, the dual value satisfies

\sup_{\text{feasible }(\varphi,\psi)}\Big\{-\!\int\varphi\,d\mu_{\Omega}-\!\int\psi\,d\nu_{T}\Big\}\ \leq\ \int\widehat{h}\,d\mu_{\Omega}\ -\ \int h\,d\nu_{T}\ +\ O\!\big(\|h\|_{L^{1}}+\|\widehat{h}\|_{L^{1}}\big).

(3.2)

Proof.

From (
2.1
) and
\nu_{T}=m+a
,

\displaystyle\text{LHS}\ \leq\ \int\widehat{h}\,d\mu_{\Omega}\ -\ \int h\,d\nu_{T}\ +\ S\!\int\eta\,m\ +\ S\!\int\eta\,a.

By the calibration (
3.1
),
S\!\int\eta\,m=\int h\,m
, so

S\!\int\eta\,a\ =\ \Big(\int\eta(S-g)\,d\nu_{T}\Big)\ -\ \int\eta(S-g)\,m,

and the second term cancels by (
3.1
). Thus
\int\eta(S-g)\,a
is itself an explicit-formula difference for the band-limited test
\eta(S-g)
, whence, by the same
L^{1}
-controlled explicit formula used below, it is
\ll(\|h\|_{L^{1}}+\|\widehat{h}\|_{L^{1}})\log^{2}T
, which we write as
O(\|h\|_{L^{1}}+\|\widehat{h}\|_{L^{1}})
under the final bound.
∎

4
Smoothed explicit formula with
L^{1}
-control

Proposition 4.1

(Explicit formula,
L^{1}
-controlled)
.

Let
h\in L^{1}(\mathbb{R})
be even with
\widehat{h}\in L^{1}(\mathbb{R})
and
\Omega=\kappa T
with fixed
\kappa>0
. Then, unconditionally,

\int_{\mathbb{R}}\widehat{h}(\gamma)\,d\mu_{\Omega}(\gamma)\ -\ \int_{\mathbb{R}}h(t)\,d\nu_{T}(t)\ =\ M(h;T,\Omega)\ +\ E(h;T,\Omega),

(4.1)

where
M
collects the archimedean/main contributions and

|E(h;T,\Omega)|\ \ll\ \big(\|h\|_{L^{1}}+\|\widehat{h}\|_{L^{1}}\big)\,\log^{2}T.

(4.2)

All implied constants are uniform for fixed
\alpha\in(0,1)
and
\kappa>0
. The
\log^{2}T
factor is the explicit-formula complexity coming from gamma/zero bookkeeping with smoothing and truncation (cf. Titchmarsh, Thm. 14.25/14.29; Iwaniec–Kowalski, Ch. 5).

Proposition 4.2

(Paley–Wiener
L^{1}
sizes)
.

With the normalized choice
S=\|\widehat{f}\|_{L^{1}}\asymp T^{-1}
and definitions (
1.5
),

\|h\|_{L^{1}}\ \asymp\ T,\qquad\|\widehat{h}\|_{L^{1}}\ \ll\ 1.

(4.3)

Proof.

By Young’s inequality and
\widehat{f}\geq 0
,
\|g\|_{L^{1}}\asymp\|f\|_{L^{1}}\asymp T
and
\|\beta_{0}\|_{L^{1}}=\|\widehat{f}\|_{L^{1}}=S\asymp T^{-1}
. Thus
\|h\|_{L^{1}}\leq\|g\|_{L^{1}}\asymp T
and
\|\widehat{h}\|_{L^{1}}\leq\|\widehat{\eta}\|_{L^{1}}\|\beta_{0}\|_{L^{1}}\asymp T\cdot S\ll 1
.
∎

5
Main theorem

Theorem 5.1
.

Let
\eta=\eta_{T}
be an even Beurling–Selberg kernel with
\eta\equiv 1
on
[0,T]
,
\widehat{\eta}\geq 0
,
L^{1}
-controlled boundary ringing, and
\|\widehat{\eta}\|_{L^{1}}\asymp T
. For the smoothed measures
\nu_{T},\mu_{\Omega}
defined above and
\Omega=\kappa T
with fixed
\kappa>0
, the KL-regularized prime–zero transport cost satisfies

\mathsf{OT}_{\eta}(T)\ \ll\ T\,\log^{2}T.

(5.1)

Proof.

By Corollary
3.1
,

\mathsf{OT}_{\eta}(T)\ \leq\ \int\widehat{h}\,d\mu_{\Omega}\ -\ \int h\,d\nu_{T}\ +\ O\!\big(\|h\|_{L^{1}}+\|\widehat{h}\|_{L^{1}}\big).

Apply Proposition
4.1
to the difference and bound the error using (
4.3
):

\mathsf{OT}_{\eta}(T)\ \ll\ \big(\|h\|_{L^{1}}+\|\widehat{h}\|_{L^{1}}\big)\log^{2}T\ \asymp\ T\log^{2}T.

∎
