45 C.F.R. § 641.12 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:45:641.12
Creator: National Science Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-641.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 641 (Environmental Assessment Procedures For Proposed National Science Foundation Actions In Antarctica). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirements set forth in this part apply to all proposed projects, programs and actions authorized or approved by, or subject to the control and responsibility of NSF that may have an impact on the Antarctic environment.
