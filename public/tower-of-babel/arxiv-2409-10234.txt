Compressions of selfadjoint and maximal dissipative extensions of non-densely defined symmetric operators
Yu.M. Arlinskiĭ

Abstract
. Selfadjoint and maximal dissipative extensions of a non-densely defined symmetric operator S S in an infinite-dimensional separable Hilbert space are considered and their compressions on the subspace dom ¯ ​ S {\rm\overline{dom}\,}S are studied. The main focus is on the case codim ​ dom ¯ ​ S = ∞ {\rm codim\,}{\rm\overline{dom}\,}S=\infty . New properties of the characteristic functions of non-densely defined symmetric operators are established.

2.
Symmetric and maximal dissipative operators

2.1.
Symmetric operators

Recall that a complex number
\lambda
is a point of regular type of the linear operator
T

[
1
]
if there exists a positive number
k(\lambda)
such that

||(T-\lambda I)f||\geq k(\lambda)||f||\;\forall f\in{\rm dom\,}T.

If
T
is a closed linear operator and
\ker(T-\lambda I)=\{0\}
, then the linear manifold
{\rm ran\,}(T-\lambda I)
is a subspace in
{\mathfrak{H}}
if and only if the number
\lambda
is a point of regular type of
T
. We denote by
\widehat{\rho}(T)
the set of all points of regular type (the field of regularity) of the operator
T
.

Recall that a linear operator
S
in a Hilbert space
{\mathcal{H}}
is called symmetric (or Hermitian) if
{\rm Im\,}(Sf,f)=0
for all
f\in{\rm dom\,}S
. If
{\rm dom\,}S
is dense, then
S
is symmetric iff
S\subseteq S^{*}
.

Let
S
be a closed symmetric operator in
{\mathcal{H}}
. Then the set
{\mathbb{C}}\setminus{\mathbb{R}}\subseteq\widehat{\rho}(S)
. Set

{\mathfrak{M}}_{\lambda}:={\rm ran\,}(S-\lambda I),\;{\mathfrak{N}}_{\lambda}:={\mathfrak{M}}_{\bar{\lambda}}^{\perp}.

The numbers
n_{\pm}={\rm dim\,}{\mathfrak{N}}_{\lambda},\;\lambda\in{\mathbb{C}}_{\pm}

are called the deficiency indices (defect numbers) of
S

[
1
,
23
]
.

If
S
is densely defined, then due to J. von Neumann’s results
[
1
]

(1)

the domain of the adjoint operator
S^{*}
for each
\lambda\in{\mathbb{C}}\setminus{\mathbb{R}}
admits the direct decomposition

{\rm dom\,}S^{*}={\rm dom\,}S\dot{+}{\mathfrak{N}}_{\lambda}\dot{+}{\mathfrak{N}}_{\bar{\lambda}};

(2)

S
admits selfadjoint extensions in
{\mathcal{H}}
if and only if the deficiency indices of
S
are equal; moreover, fix
\lambda\in{\mathbb{C}}\setminus{\mathbb{R}}
, then the formulas in
(
1.1
)
give a one-to-one
correspondence between all unitary operators

, acting from
{\mathfrak{N}}_{\lambda}
onto

and all selfadjoint extensions of

.

If

It is proved in (

The adjoint of

The domain

Moreover,

Similarly

(2) (see

(3) Suppose

-2{\rm Re\,}(Y^{*}h,D_{Y}(I-Y)^{-1}D_{Y^{*}}h)=||Y^{*}h||^{2}+\left\|(I-Y)^{-1}D_{Y^{*}}h\right\|^{2}\\[5.69054pt]
-\left\|{Y}(I-Y)^{-1}D_{Y^{*}}h\right\|^{2}-2{\rm Re\,}(D_{Y^{*}}h,Y(I-Y)^{-1}D_{Y^{*}}h)\\[5.69054pt]
=||h||^{2}-||D_{Y^{*}}h||^{2}-\left\|{Y}(I-Y)^{-1}D_{Y^{*}}h\right\|^{2}-2{\rm Re\,}(D_{Y^{*}}h,Y(I-Y)^{-1}D_{Y^{*}}h)\\[5.69054pt]
+\left\|(I-Y)^{-1}D_{Y^{*}}h\right\|^{2}=||h||^{2}-||D_{Y^{*}}h+{Y}(I-Y)^{-1}D_{Y^{*}}h||^{2}+\left\|(I-Y)^{-1}D_{Y^{*}}h\right\|^{2}\\[5.69054pt]

+\left\|(I-Y)^{-1}D_{Y^{*}}h\right\|^{2}=||h||^{2}-\left\|(I-Y)^{-1}D_{Y^{*}}h\right\|^{2}+\left\|(I-Y)^{-1}D_{Y^{*}}h\right\|^{2}=||h||^{2}.\end{array}

(5), (6), (7). Since equalities in (
