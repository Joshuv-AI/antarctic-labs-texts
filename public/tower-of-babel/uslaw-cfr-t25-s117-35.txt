25 C.F.R. § 117.35 — Appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:25:117.35
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-117.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 117 (Deposit And Expenditure Of Individual Funds Of Members Of The Osage Tribe Of Indians Who Do Not Have Certificates Of Competency). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any decision by the superintendent may be appealed to the area director, any decision by the area director may be appealed to the Commissioner, and any decision by the Commissioner may be appealed to the Secretary.
