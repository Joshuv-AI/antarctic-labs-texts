10 U.S.C. § 8329 — Officers not to be retired for misconduct
Citation: us:usc:10:8329
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8329
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 841 (Voluntary Retirement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No officer of the Navy or the Marine Corps may be retired because of misconduct for which trial by court-martial would be appropriate.
