31 C.F.R. § 339.5 — Governing regulations.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 339 (Exchange Offering Of United States Savings Bonds, Series H). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All Series H bonds issued under this circular are subject to the regulations, now or hereafter prescribed, contained in Department Circular No. 530, current revision (part 315 of this chapter).
