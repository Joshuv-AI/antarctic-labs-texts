2 U.S.C. § 2147 — Plant material exchanges
Citation: us:usc:2:2147
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section2147
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 30 (Operation And Maintenance Of Capitol Complex); Subchapter VI (Botanic Garden And National Garden). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On and after July 8, 1935, plant material exchanges may be made with botanic gardens, institutions, municipal parks, and gardens.
