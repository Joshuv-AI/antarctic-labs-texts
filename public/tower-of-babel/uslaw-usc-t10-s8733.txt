10 U.S.C. § 8733 — Disposition of royalties
Citation: us:usc:10:8733
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8733
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 869 (Naval Petroleum Reserves). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Any oil, gas, gasoline or other substance accruing to the United States as royalty from any lease under this chapter shall be delivered to the United States, or shall be paid for in money, as the Secretary elects.
(b)
All money accruing to the United States from lands in the naval petroleum reserves shall be covered into the Treasury.
