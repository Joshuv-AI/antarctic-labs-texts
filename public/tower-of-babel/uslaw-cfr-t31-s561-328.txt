31 C.F.R. § 561.328 — Reduce significantly, significantly reduced, and significant reduction.
Citation: us:cfr:31:561.328
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-561.328
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 561 (Iranian Financial Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The terms reduce significantly, significantly reduced, and significant reduction, used with respect to purchases from Iran of petroleum and petroleum products, include a reduction in such purchases in terms of price or volume toward a complete cessation of such purchases.
