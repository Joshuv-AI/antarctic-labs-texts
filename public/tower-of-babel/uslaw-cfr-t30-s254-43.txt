30 C.F.R. § 254.43 — Maintenance and periodic inspection of response equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:30:254.43
Creator: Bureau Of Safety And Environmental Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-254.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 254 (Oil-Spill Response Requirements For Facilities Located Seaward Of The Coast Line). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You must ensure that the response equipment listed in your OSRP is inspected at least monthly and is maintained, as necessary, to ensure optimal performance.
(b) You must ensure that records of the inspections and the maintenance activities are kept for at least 2 years and are made available to any authorized BSEE representative upon request.
