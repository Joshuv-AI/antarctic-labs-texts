24 C.F.R. § 984.204 — On-site facilities.
Citation: us:cfr:24:984.204
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-984.204
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 984 (Section 8 And Public Housing Family Self-Sufficiency Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each PHA or owner may, subject to the approval of HUD, make available and utilize common areas or unoccupied dwelling units in properties owned by the entity to provide or coordinate supportive services under any FSS program.
