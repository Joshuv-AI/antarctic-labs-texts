39 C.F.R. § 912.12 — Review by legal officers.
Citation: us:cfr:39:912.12
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-912.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 912 (Procedures To Adjudicate Claims For Personal Injury Or Property Damage Arising Out Of The Operation Of The U.S. Postal Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The authority of the Postal Service to adjust, determine, compromise, and settle a claim under the provisions of the Federal Tort Claims Act shall, if the amount of a proposed compromise, settlement, or award exceeds $5,000, be exercised only after review by a legal officer of the Postal Service.
