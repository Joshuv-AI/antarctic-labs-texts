47 C.F.R. § 87.71 — Frequency measurements.
Citation: us:cfr:47:87.71
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-87.71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 87 (Aviation Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A licensed operator must measure the operating frequencies of all land-based transmitters at the following times:
(a) When the transmitter is originally installed;
(b) When any change or adjustment is made in the transmitter which may affect an operating frequency; or
(c) When an operating frequency has shifted beyond tolerance.
