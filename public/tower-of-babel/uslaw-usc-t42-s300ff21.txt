42 U.S.C. § 300ff–21 — Grants
Citation: us:usc:42:300ff–21
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section300ff–21
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter XXIV (Hiv Health Care Services Program); Part B (Care Grant Program); Subpart i (General Grant Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall, subject to the availability of appropriations, make grants to States to enable such States to improve the quality, availability and organization of health care and support services for individuals and families with HIV/AIDS. The authority of the Secretary to provide grants under this section is subject to section 300ff–34(e)(2) 1 of this title (relating to the decrease in perinatal transmission of HIV/AIDS).
