30 C.F.R. § 903.785 — Requirements for permits for special categories of mining.
Citation: us:cfr:30:903.785
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-903.785
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 903 (Arizona). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Part 785 of this chapter, Requirements for permits for Special Categories of Mining, applies to any person who submits an application for a permit to conduct certain categories of surface coal mining and reclamation operations as specified therein.
