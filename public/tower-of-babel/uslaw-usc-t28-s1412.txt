28 U.S.C. § 1412 — Change of venue
Citation: us:usc:28:1412
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1412
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part IV (Jurisdiction And Venue); Chapter 87 (District Courts; Venue). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A district court may transfer a case or proceeding under title 11 to a district court for another district, in the interest of justice or for the convenience of the parties.
