41 C.F.R. § 301-31.3 — -31.3 Lodging location determination.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-31.3
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-31.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-31 (31—Threatened Law Enforcement/Investigative Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agency designates the area where the employee and/or immediate family should obtain lodging, which may be within the official station or at an alternate location. The employee and immediate family may occupy lodging at different locations if authorized by the agency.
