54 U.S.C. § 100804 — Improved use of partners and volunteers in interpretation and education
Citation: us:usc:54:100804
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section100804
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 1008 (Education And Interpretation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may—
(1)
coordinate with park partners and volunteers in the delivery of quality programs and services to supplement those provided by the Service as part of a park’s Long Range Interpretive Plan;
(2)
support interpretive partners by providing opportunities to participate in interpretive training; and
(3)
collaborate with other Federal and non-Federal public or private agencies, organizations, or institutions for the purposes of developing, promoting, and making available educational opportunities related to resources of the System and programs.
