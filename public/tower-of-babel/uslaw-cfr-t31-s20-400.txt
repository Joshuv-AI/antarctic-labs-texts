31 C.F.R. § 20.400 — What are my responsibilities as a(n) Department of the Treasury awarding official?
Citation: us:cfr:31:20.400
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-20.400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 20 (Governmentwide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As a(n) Department of the Treasury awarding official, you must obtain each recipient's agreement, as a condition of the award, to comply with the requirements in—
(a) Subpart B of this part, if the recipient is not an individual; or
(b) Subpart C of this part, if the recipient is an individual.
