46 U.S.C. § 53908 — Additional insurance privately obtained
Citation: us:usc:46:53908
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section53908
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part C (Financial Assistance Programs); Chapter 539 (War Risk Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

With the approval of the Secretary of Transportation, a person having an insurable interest in a vessel may obtain insurance on the vessel with other underwriting agents in addition to the insurance with the Secretary. The Secretary is not entitled to the benefit of the additional insurance.
