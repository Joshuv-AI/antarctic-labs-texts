24 C.F.R. § 241.1035 — Charges by lender.
Citation: us:cfr:24:241.1035
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-241.1035
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 241 (Supplementary Financing For Insured Project Mortgages). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The lender may collect from the borrower the amount of the fees provided for by subpart E of this part.
(b) The lender may also collect from the borrower an initial service charge, as reimbursement for the cost of closing the transaction, in an amount not to exceed 2 percent of the original principal amount of the loan.
(c) Any charges to be collected by the lender in addition to those prescribed in paragraphs (a) and (b) of this section, shall be subject to the prior approval of the Commissioner.
