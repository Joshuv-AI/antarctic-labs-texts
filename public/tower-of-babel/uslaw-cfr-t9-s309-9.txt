9 C.F.R. § 309.9 — Swine erysipelas.
Citation: us:cfr:9:309.9
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-309.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 309 (Ante-Mortem Inspection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All hogs plainly showing on ante-mortem inspection that they are affected with acute swine erysipelas shall be identified as U.S. Condemned and disposed of in accordance with § 309.13.
