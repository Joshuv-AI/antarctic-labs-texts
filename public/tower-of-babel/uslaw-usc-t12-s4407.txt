12 U.S.C. § 4407 — National emergencies
Citation: us:usc:12:4407
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section4407
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 45 (Payment System Risk Reduction); Subchapter I (Bilateral And Clearing Organization Netting). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of this chapter may not be construed to limit the authority of the President under the Trading With the Enemy Act (50 U.S.C. App. 1 et seq.) [now 50 U.S.C. 4301 et seq.] or the International Emergency Economic Powers Act (50 U.S.C. 1701 et seq.).
