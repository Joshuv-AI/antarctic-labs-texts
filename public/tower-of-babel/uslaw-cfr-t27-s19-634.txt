27 C.F.R. § 19.634 — Computer-generated reports and transaction forms.
Citation: us:cfr:27:19.634
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-19.634
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 19 (Distilled Spirits Plants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

TTB will accept computer-generated reports of operations and transaction forms made using a computer printer on plain white paper without preapproval from TTB if they conform to the following standards:
(a) The computer-generated report or form must approximate the physical layout of the corresponding TTB report or form, although the typeface may vary;
(b) The text of the computer-generated report or form including each line entry, must exactly match the official TTB report or form; and
(c) Each penalty of perjury statement specified for the TTB report or form must be reproduced in its entirety.
