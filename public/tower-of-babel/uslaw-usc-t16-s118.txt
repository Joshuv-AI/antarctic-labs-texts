16 U.S.C. § 118 — Appropriations; availability for operation of Aileen Nusbaum Hospital
Citation: us:usc:16:118
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section118
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter XII (Mesa Verde National Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Appropriations made for Mesa Verde National Park shall be available for the operation of the Aileen Nusbaum Hospital and the furnishing of the necessary service in connection therewith at rates to be fixed by the Secretary of the Interior.
