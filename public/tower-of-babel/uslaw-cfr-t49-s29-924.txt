49 C.F.R. § 29.924 — May a Tribe appeal the Department's decision to terminate a compact or funding agreement?
Citation: us:cfr:49:29.924
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-29.924
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 29 (Tribal Transportation Self-Governance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Tribe may appeal the Department's decision to terminate a compact or funding agreement to the Department's Office of Hearings.
