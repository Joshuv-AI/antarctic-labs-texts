13 C.F.R. § 400.202 — Loan amount.
Citation: us:cfr:13:400.202
Creator: Emergency Steel Guarantee Loan Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-400.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 400 (Emergency Steel Guarantee Loan Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The aggregate amount of loan principal guaranteed under this Program to a single Qualified Steel Company may not exceed $ 250 million.
(b) Of the aggregate amount of loans authorized to be guaranteed and outstanding at any one time, not more than $30 million shall be loans to iron ore companies.
