33 U.S.C. § 1474 — Federal intervention actions
Citation: us:usc:33:1474
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section1474
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 28 (Pollution Casualties On The High Seas: United States Intervention). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Upon a determination under section 1472 of this title of a grave and imminent danger to the coastline or related interests of the United States, the Secretary may—
(1)
coordinate and direct all public and private efforts directed at the removal or elimination of the threatened pollution damage;
(2)
directly or indirectly undertake the whole or any part of any salvage or other action he could require or direct under subsection (1) of this section; and
(3)
remove, and, if necessary, destroy the ship and cargo which is the source of the danger.
