19 C.F.R. § 112.26 — Duration of license.
Citation: us:cfr:19:112.26
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-112.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 112 (Carriers, Cartmen, And Lightermen). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A license issued in accordance with this subpart shall remain in force and effect until the license is suspended or revoked pursuant to § 112.30 or until the required bond is terminated pursuant to § 113.27 of this chapter.
