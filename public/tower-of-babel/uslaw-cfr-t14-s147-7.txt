14 C.F.R. § 147.7 — Duration of certificates.
Citation: us:cfr:14:147.7
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-147.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 147 (Aviation Maintenance Technician Schools). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An aviation maintenance technician school certificate or rating issued under this part is effective from the date of issue until the certificate or rating is surrendered, suspended, or revoked.
