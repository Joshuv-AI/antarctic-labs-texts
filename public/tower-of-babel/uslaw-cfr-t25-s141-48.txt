25 C.F.R. § 141.48 — Translation of disclosure statements.
Citation: us:cfr:25:141.48
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-141.48
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 141 (Business Practices On The Navajo, Hopi And Zuni Reservations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Disclosure required by §§ 141.46 and 141.47 shall be made in writing regardless of the customer's ability to speak, read, or write the English language. Disclosure to non-English speaking persons shall be translated orally into the appropriate language.
