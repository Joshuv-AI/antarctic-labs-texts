Moduli of continuity for the local times of rebirthed Markov processes
P.J. Fitzsimmons Michael B. Marcus Jay Rosen

Abstract
Let S S a be locally compact space with a countable base. Let 𝒴 {\cal Y} be a transient symmetric Borel right process with state space S S and continuous strictly positive p p –potential densities u p ​ ( x , y ) u^{p}(x,y) . Local and uniform moduli of continuity are obtained for the local times of both fully and partially rebirthed versions of 𝒴 {\cal Y} . A fully rebirthed version of 𝒴 {\cal Y} is an extension of 𝒴 {\cal Y} so that instead of terminating at the end of its lifetime it is immediately “reborn” with a probability measure μ \mu , on S S . I.e., the process goes to the set B ⊂ S B\subset S with probability μ ⁡ ( B ) \mu(B) , after which it continues to evolve the way 𝒴 {\cal Y} did, being reborn with probability μ \mu each time it dies. This rebirthed version of 𝒴 {\cal Y} is a recurrent Borel right process with state space S S and p p -potential densities of form, u p ​ ( x , y ) + h ⁡ ( x , y ) , x , y ∈ S , p > 0 , u^{p}(x,y)+h(x,y),\qquad x,y\in S,\,\,p>0, where h ⁡ ( x , y ) h(x,y) is not symmetric. The local times of the rebirthed process are given in terms of the local times of 𝒴 {\cal Y} and isomorphism theorems in the spirit of Dynkin, Eisenbam and Kaspi are obtained that relate these local times to generalized chi–square processes formed by Gaussian processes with covariances u q ​ ( x , y ) u^{q}(x,y) for different values of q q . These isomorphisms allow one to obtain exact local and uniform moduli of continuity for the local times of the rebirthed process. Several explicit examples are given in which 𝒴 {\cal Y} is either a modified Lévy process or a diffusion. Analogous results are obtained for partially rebirthed versions of 𝒴 {\cal Y} . This is obtained by starting 𝒴 {\cal Y} in S S and when it dies returning it to S S with a sub-probability measure Ξ \Xi . (With probability 1 − | Ξ | 1-|\Xi| it is sent to a disjoint state space S ′ S^{\prime} , where it remains.)

In this paper we obtain exact local and uniform moduli of continuity for the local times of fully and partially

Let

6

In

from exact local moduli of continuity of associated permanental process.
Getting the exact local moduli of continuity for the associated permanental process is difficult and the methods developed do not extend to finding uniform moduli of continuity of permanental processes.

Here, using a completely different approach, we use properties of Gaussian processes to obtain exact local and uniform moduli of continuity for the local times of many fully rebirthed Markov processes
\widetilde{Z}
.

By (

\displaystyle A^{y}_{t}\circ\widetilde{\theta}_{s}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum^{N_{t}-1}_{i=1}L^{y}_{\zeta(\omega_{i})}(\omega_{i})\circ\widetilde{\theta}_{s}+L^{y}_{t-\zeta_{N_{t}-1}}(\omega_{N_{t}})\circ\widetilde{\theta}_{s}

3.1

Let

U_{i}=\{U_{i}(s,t),s,t\in[0,1]\},

\sigma_{i}^{2}(s,t)=E(\eta_{i}(s)-\eta_{i}(t))^{2},\qquad i=0,\ldots k.

(3.2)

6
Partially rebirthed Markov processes

A partially rebirthed Markov process is obtained by starting a transient Markov process with state space
S
and when it dies returning it to
S
with a sub-probability measure
\Xi
. (With probability
1-|\Xi|
it is sent to a disjoint state space
S^{\prime}
, where it remains.)
The potential density of such a process on
S
has the simple form,

\widetilde{u}^{0}(x,y)=u^{0}(x,y)+f(y),\hskip 14.45377ptx,y\in S,

(6.1)

where
u^{0}(x,y)
is the potential density of the original transient process and

f(y)=\int_{S}u^{0}\left(x,y\right)\,d\nu(x),

(6.2)

and
\nu(x)=\Xi(x)/(1-|\Xi(S)|)
.

Let
S
a be locally compact space with a countable base.
Let
{\cal Y}=(\Omega,{\cal F}_{t},{\cal Y}_{t},\newline
\theta_{t},P^{x})
be a transient symmetric Borel right process with state space
S
and continuous strictly positive
p-
potential densities

u^{p}=\{u^{p}(x,y),x,y\in S\},\quad p\geq 0,

(6.3)

with respect to some
\sigma
–finite positive measure
m
on
S
. Let
\zeta=\inf\{t\,|\,{\cal Y}_{t}=\Delta\}
, where
\Delta
is the cemetery state for
{\cal Y}
and assume that
\zeta<\infty
almost surely.

Let
S^{\prime}
a be locally compact space with a countable base, which is disjoint from S.
Let
{\cal Y}^{\prime}=(\Omega^{\prime},{\cal F}^{\prime}_{t},{\cal Y}^{\prime}_{t},\theta^{\prime}_{t},P^{{}^{\prime}x})
be a transient symmetric Borel right process with state space
S^{\prime}

p-
potential densities

(6.4)

with respect to some
\sigma
–finite positive measure
m^{\prime}
on

\widetilde{X}\!=\!(\widetilde{\Omega},\widetilde{\cal F}_{t},\widetilde{X}_{t},\widetilde{\theta}_{t},\widetilde{P}^{x})
