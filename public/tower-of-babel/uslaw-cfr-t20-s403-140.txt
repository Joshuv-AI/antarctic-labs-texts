20 C.F.R. § 403.140 — If the Commissioner authorizes testimony, what will be the scope and form of that testimony?
Citation: us:cfr:20:403.140
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-403.140
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 403 (Testimony By Employees And The Production Of Records And Information In Legal Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The employee's testimony must be limited to matters that were specifically approved. We will provide testimony in the form that is least burdensome to SSA unless you provide sufficient information in your application for SSA to justify a different form. For example, we will provide an affidavit or declaration rather than a deposition and a deposition rather than trial testimony.
