10 U.S.C. § 6118 — Form of certifications regarding the safety or reliability of the nuclear weapons stockpile
Citation: us:usc:10:6118
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section6118
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part VI (Elements Of Department Of Defense And Other Matters); Subpart B (Atomic Energy Defense); Chapter 602 (Nuclear Weapons Stockpile Matters); Subchapter I (Stockpile Stewardship And Weapons Production). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any certification submitted to the President by the Secretary of Defense or the Secretary of Energy regarding confidence in the safety or reliability of a nuclear weapon type in the United States nuclear weapons stockpile shall be submitted in classified form only.
