21 C.F.R. § 82.50 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:21:82.50
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-82.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 82 (Listing Of Certified Provisionally Listed Colors And Specifications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A batch of a straight color listed in this subpart may be certified, in accordance with the provisions of the regulations in this part, for use in food, drugs, and cosmetics, if such batch conforms to the requirements of § 82.5 and to the specifications in this subpart set forth for such color.
