12 U.S.C. § 5324 — Registration of nonbank financial companies supervised by the Board of Governors
Citation: us:usc:12:5324
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section5324
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 53 (Wall Street Reform And Consumer Protection); Subchapter I (Financial Stability); Part A (Financial Stability Oversight Council). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 180 days after the date of a final Council determination under section 5323 of this title that a nonbank financial company is to be supervised by the Board of Governors, such company shall register with the Board of Governors, on forms prescribed by the Board of Governors, which shall include such information as the Board of Governors, in consultation with the Council, may deem necessary or appropriate to carry out this subchapter.
