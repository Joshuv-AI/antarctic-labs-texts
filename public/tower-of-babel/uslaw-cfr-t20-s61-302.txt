20 C.F.R. § 61.302 — Time limitations for filing a claim for detention benefits.
Citation: us:cfr:20:61.302
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-61.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 61 (Claims For Compensation Under The War Hazards Compensation Act, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The time limitation provisions found in the Federal Employees' Compensation Act, as amended (5 U.S.C. 8101 et seq.) apply to the filing of claims for detention benefits. The Office may waive the time limitations if it finds that circumstances beyond the claimant's control prevented the filing of a timely claim.
