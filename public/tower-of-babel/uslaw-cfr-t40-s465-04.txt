40 C.F.R. § 465.04 — Compliance date for PSES.
Citation: us:cfr:40:465.04
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-465.04
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 465 (Coil Coating Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) For subparts A, B, and C the compliance date for Pretreatment Standards for Existing Source (PSES) is December 1, 1985.
(b) For subpart D, the compliance date for Pretreatment Standards for Existing Sources will be as soon as possible, but in no case later than November 17, 1986.
