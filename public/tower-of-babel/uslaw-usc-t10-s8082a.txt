10 U.S.C. § 8082a — Deputy Chief of Chaplains
Citation: us:usc:10:8082a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8082a
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization); Chapter 809 (Bureaus; Office Of The Judge Advocate General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Navy may detail as the Deputy Chief of Chaplains an officer of the Chaplain Corps in the grade of commander or above who is on active duty and who has served on active duty in the Chaplain Corps for at least eight years.
