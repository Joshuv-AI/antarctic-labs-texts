25 U.S.C. § 43 — Persons paid for other services not paid for interpreting
Citation: us:usc:25:43
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section43
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 2 (Officers Of Indian Affairs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No person employed by the United States and paid for any other service shall be paid for interpreting.
