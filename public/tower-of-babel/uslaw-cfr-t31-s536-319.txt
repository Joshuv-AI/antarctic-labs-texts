31 C.F.R. § 536.319 — Narcotic drug; controlled substance; listed chemical.
Citation: us:cfr:31:536.319
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-536.319
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 536 (Narcotics Trafficking Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The terms narcotic drug, controlled substance, and listed chemical have the meanings given those terms in section 102 of the Controlled Substances Act (21 U.S.C. 802).
