Correction: Pan-cancer analysis of UGGT1 in human tumors and experimental validation in breast cancer
Authors: Xuan Chu, Ligai Zhang, Shiqing Xiang, Yuting Huang
Published: 2025

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-12619-x, published online 24 July 2025

The original version of this Article contained errors in Figure 3.

Due to an error during figure assembly, in Figure 3A, the ‘UVM Overall Survival’ graph was a duplication of the ‘LIHC Overall Survival’ graph.

In addition, in Figure 3B, the label of the ‘LUSC Disease free Survival’ graph was incorrectly given as ‘LUAD Disease free Survival’.

The original Figure 3 and accompanying legend appears below.Fig. 3The prognostic potential of UGGT1 in tumor prognosis. (A) Connection between UGGT1 expression level and overall survival (OS) of tumor patient was evaluated by GEPIA2. (B) Association between UGGT1 expression and disease-free survival (DFS) was estimated by GEPIA2.

The original Article has been corrected.
