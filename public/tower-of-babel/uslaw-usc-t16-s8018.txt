16 U.S.C. § 8018 — Capacity building in foreign fisheries
Citation: us:usc:16:8018
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section8018
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 99 (Maritime Security And Fisheries Enforcement); Subchapter I (Programs To Combat Iuu Fishing And Increase Maritime Security). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary, in consultation with the heads of other Federal agencies, as appropriate, shall develop and carry out with partner governments and civil society—
(1)
multi-year international environmental cooperation agreements and projects; and
(2)
multi-year capacity-building projects for implementing measures to address illegal, unreported, or unregulated fishing, fraud, forced labor, bycatch, and other conservation measures.
