38 U.S.C. § 7732 — Functions
Citation: us:usc:38:7732
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section7732
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part V (Boards, Administrations, And Services); Chapter 77 (Veterans Benefits Administration); Subchapter II (Quality Assurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Under Secretary for Benefits, acting through the quality assurance entities established under section 7731(a), shall on an ongoing basis perform and oversee quality reviews of the functions of each of the principal organizational elements of the Veterans Benefits Administration.
