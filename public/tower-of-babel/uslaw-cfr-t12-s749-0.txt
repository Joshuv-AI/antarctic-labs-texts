12 C.F.R. § 749.0 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:12:749.0
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-749.0
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 749 (Vital Records Preservation Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) This part describes the obligations of a federally insured credit union to maintain a vital records preservation program to identify, store, and reconstruct vital records in the event such records are destroyed.
(b) This part does not supersede records preservation requirements that may apply to a credit union pursuant to other law or regulation.
