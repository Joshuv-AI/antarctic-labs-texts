34 C.F.R. § 75.615 — Build America, Buy America Act.
Citation: us:cfr:34:75.615
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-75.615
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 75 (Direct Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A grantee must comply with the requirements of the Build America, Buy America Act, Pub. L. 117-58, § 70901 through 70927 and implementing regulations, as applicable.
