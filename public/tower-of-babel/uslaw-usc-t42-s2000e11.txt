42 U.S.C. § 2000e–11 — Veterans’ special rights or preference
Citation: us:usc:42:2000e–11
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2000e–11
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 21 (Civil Rights); Subchapter VI (Equal Employment Opportunities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing contained in this subchapter shall be construed to repeal or modify any Federal, State, territorial, or local law creating special rights or preference for veterans.
