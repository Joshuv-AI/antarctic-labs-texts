9 C.F.R. § 151.10 — Recognition of additional breeds and books of record.
Citation: us:cfr:9:151.10
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-151.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 151 (Recognition Of Breeds And Books Of Record Of Purebred Animals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before a breed or a book of record shall be added to those listed in this part, the custodian of the book of record involved shall submit to APHIS a complete copy of the book of record, consisting of any published printed volumes and any microfilm records issued by the registry association up to date of application, together with a copy of all rules and forms in force on said date affecting the registration of animals in said book.
