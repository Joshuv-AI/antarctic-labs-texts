26 C.F.R. § 403.61 — Sale on open, competitive bids.
Citation: us:cfr:26:403.61
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-403.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 403 (Disposition Of Seized Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If forfeited property is to be sold at public auction to the highest bidder on open, competitive bids, the notice of sale shall so specify, and state the date, hour, and place of such sale.
