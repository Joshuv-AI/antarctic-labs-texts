7 C.F.R. § 1401.6 — Assignments.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1401.6
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1401.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1401 (Commodity Certificates, In Kind Payments, And Other Forms Of Payment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter, a payment made under this part may not be the subject of an assignment, except as determined and announced by CCC.
