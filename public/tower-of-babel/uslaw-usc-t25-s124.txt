25 U.S.C. § 124 — Expenditures from tribal funds of Five Civilized Tribes without specific appropriations
Citation: us:usc:25:124
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section124
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 4 (Performance By United States Of Obligations To Indians); Subchapter II (Disbursement Of Moneys And Supplies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No money shall be expended from tribal funds belonging to the Five Civilized Tribes without specific appropriation by Congress.
