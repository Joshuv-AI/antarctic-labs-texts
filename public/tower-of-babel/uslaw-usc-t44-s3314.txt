44 U.S.C. § 3314 — Procedures for disposal of records exclusive
Citation: us:usc:44:3314
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section3314
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 33 (Disposal Of Records). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The procedures prescribed by this chapter are exclusive, and records of the United States Government may not be alienated or destroyed except under this chapter.
