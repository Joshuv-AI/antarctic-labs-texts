24 C.F.R. § 570.464 — Project closeout.
Citation: us:cfr:24:570.464
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Community Planning And Development, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-570.464
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 570 (Community Development Block Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

HUD will advise the recipient to initiate closeout procedures when HUD determines, in consultation with the recipient, that there are not impediments to closeout. Closeout shall be carried out in accordance with § 570.509 and applicable HUD guidelines.
