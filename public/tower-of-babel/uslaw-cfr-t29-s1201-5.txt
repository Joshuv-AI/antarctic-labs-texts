29 C.F.R. § 1201.5 — Exceptions.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1201.5
Creator: National Mediation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1201.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1201 (Definitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term “employee” shall not include any individual while such individual is engaged in the physical operations consisting of the mining of coal, the preparation of coal, the handling (other than movement by rail with standard locomotives) of coal not beyond the mine tipple, or the loading of coal at the tipple.
