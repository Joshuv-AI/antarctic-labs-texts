10 U.S.C. § 1172 — Enlisted members: during war or emergency; discharge
Citation: us:usc:10:1172
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1172
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 59 (Separation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A person enlisted under section 518 of this title may be discharged at any time by the President, or otherwise according to law.
