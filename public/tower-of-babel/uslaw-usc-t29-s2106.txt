29 U.S.C. § 2106 — Procedures encouraged where not required
Citation: us:usc:29:2106
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section2106
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 23 (Worker Adjustment And Retraining Notification). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the sense of Congress that an employer who is not required to comply with the notice requirements of section 2102 of this title should, to the extent possible, provide notice to its employees about a proposal to close a plant or permanently reduce its workforce.
