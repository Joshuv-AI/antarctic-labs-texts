12 C.F.R. § 1215.18 — Inspector General.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1215.18
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1215.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1215 (Production Of Fhfa Records, Information, And Employee Testimony In Third-Party Legal Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notwithstanding the general prohibition of disclosure of records and information, to the minimum extent required by the Inspector General Act, Public Law 9-452 (1978), FHFA's Office of Inspector General is permitted under this section to disclose records and information and permit FHFA-OIG employee testimony without Director approval.
