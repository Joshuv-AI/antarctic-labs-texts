Retraction Note: Machine learning-driven evaluation of mechanical and microstructural properties of agro-waste-derived geopolymer concrete
Authors: Noureddine Elboughdiri, Karim Kriaa, George Uwadiegwu Alaneme, Mutiu Shola Bakare, Obinna Onyebuchi Barah, Chemseddine Maatki
Published: 2026

Retraction of: Scientific Reports https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-50251-5, published online 26 May 2026

The Editors have retracted this Article.

After publication, concerns were raised regarding the authorship of this Article. The Editors requested clarification and supporting evidence from the Authors, including the data underlying the paper. The Authors were unable to provide the requested data due to previously undisclosed data-sharing constraints, and their explanation regarding authorship were also not satisfactory. The Editors have therefore lost confidence in this Article.

None of the Authors agree to this retraction.
