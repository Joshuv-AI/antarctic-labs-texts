19 C.F.R. § 19.10 — Examination packages.
Citation: us:cfr:19:19.10
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-19.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 19 (Customs Warehouses, Container Stations And Control Of Merchandise Therein). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Merchandise sent from a bonded warehouse to the appraiser's stores for examination shall be returned by the port director to the warehouse for delivery unless the warehouse proprietor endorses the duty-paid permit to authorize delivery to another person.
