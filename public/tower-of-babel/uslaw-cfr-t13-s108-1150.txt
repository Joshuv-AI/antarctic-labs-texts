13 C.F.R. § 108.1150 — Maximum amount of Leverage for a NMVC Company.
Citation: us:cfr:13:108.1150
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-108.1150
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 108 (New Markets Venture Capital (“Nmvc”) Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The face amount of a NMVC Company's outstanding Debentures may not exceed 150 percent of its Leverageable Capital.
