46 C.F.R. § 8.310 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:46:8.310
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-8.310
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 8 (Vessel Inspection Alternatives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to:
(a) Recognized classification societies; and
(b) All U.S. flag vessels that are certificated for international voyages and are classed by a recognized classification society that is authorized by the Coast Guard to issue the applicable international certificate as specified in this subpart.
