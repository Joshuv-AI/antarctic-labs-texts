7 C.F.R. § 1219.19 — Person.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1219 (Hass Avocado Promotion, Research, And Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).
