41 C.F.R. § 301-11.14 — -11.14 Agency reimbursement for return home or to the official station during TDY.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-11.14
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-11.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-11 (11—Subsistence Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agency may authorize per diem or actual expense and round-trip transportation expenses for periodic return travel to the employee's home or official station under the following circumstances:
(a) The agency requires the employee to return to their official station to perform official business;
(b) The agency will realize substantial cost savings by the employee's return home; or
(c) Periodic return travel home is justified as part of an extended TDY assignment.
