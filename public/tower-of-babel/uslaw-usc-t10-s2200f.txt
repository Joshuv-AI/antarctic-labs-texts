10 U.S.C. § 2200f — Inapplicability to Coast Guard
Citation: us:usc:10:2200f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2200f
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training And Education); Chapter 112 (Cyber Scholarship Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter does not apply to the Coast Guard when it is not operating as a service in the Navy.
