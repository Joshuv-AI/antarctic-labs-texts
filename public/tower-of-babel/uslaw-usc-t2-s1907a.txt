2 U.S.C. § 1907a — Authority to transfer amounts between salaries and general expenses
Citation: us:usc:2:1907a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1907a
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 29 (Capitol Police); Subchapter I (Organization And Administration); Part A (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

During fiscal year 2014 and any succeeding fiscal year, the Capitol Police may transfer amounts appropriated for the fiscal year between the category for salaries and the category for general expenses, upon the approval of the Committees on Appropriations of the House of Representatives and Senate.
