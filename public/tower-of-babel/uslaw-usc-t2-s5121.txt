2 U.S.C. § 5121 — Expense allowance of Speaker of House of Representatives
Citation: us:usc:2:5121
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section5121
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 51 (House Of Representatives Leadership); Subchapter II (The Speaker). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There shall be paid to the Speaker of the House of Representatives in equal monthly installments an expense allowance of $10,000 per annum to assist in defraying expenses relating to or resulting from the discharge of his official duties, for which no accounting, other than for income tax purposes, shall be made by him.
