17 C.F.R. § 240.3a12-1 — a12-1 Exemption of certain mortgages and interests in mortgages.
Citation: us:cfr:17:240.3a12-1
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-240.3a12-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 240 (General Rules And Regulations, Securities Exchange Act Of 1934). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Mortgages, as defined in section 302(d) of the Emergency Home Finance Act of 1970, which are or have been sold by the Federal Home Loan Mortgage Corporation are hereby exempted from the operation of such provisions of the Act as by their terms do not apply to an “exempted security” or to “exempted securities”.
