36 C.F.R. § 910.37 — Fire and life safety.
Citation: us:cfr:36:910.37
Creator: Pennsylvania Avenue Development Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-910.37
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 910 (General Guidelines And Uniform Standards For Urban Planning And Design Of Development Within The Pennsylvania Avenue Development Area). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As a complementary action to satisfying required District of Columbia codes related to fire safety, it is highly recommended that all new development be guided by standards of the NFPA Codes for fire and life safety and that all buildings be equipped with an approved sprinkler system.
