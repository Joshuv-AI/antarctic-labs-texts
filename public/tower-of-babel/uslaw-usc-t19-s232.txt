19 U.S.C. § 232 — “Port” defined
Citation: us:usc:19:232
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section232
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 3 (The Tariff And Related Provisions); Part 1 (Definitions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The word “port”, as used in title 34 of the Revised Statutes, may include any place from which merchandise can be shipped for importation, or at which merchandise can be imported.
