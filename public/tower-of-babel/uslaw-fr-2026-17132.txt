Draft NUREG: Training Review Criteria and Procedures

AGENCY: Nuclear Regulatory Commission.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On July 22, 2026, the U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) 
solicited comments on draft NUREG-1220, Revision 2, ``Training Review 
Criteria and Procedures.'' The public comment period was originally 
scheduled to close on August 21, 2026.

[[Page 54408]]

The NRC has decided to extend the public comment to September 21, 2026, 
to allow more time for members of the public to prepare and submit 
their comments.

filed no later than September 21, 2026. Comments received after this 
date will be considered if it is practical to do so, but the Commission 
is able to ensure consideration only for comments received on or before

II. Discussion

    On July 22, 2026, the NRC published a notice in the Federal 
Register (91 FR 46175) which provided members of the public an 
opportunity to submit comments on the draft NUREG-1220, Revision 2, 
``Training Review Criteria and Procedures''. This NUREG provides 
guidance to the NRC staff for reviewing training programs at nuclear 
power plants to verify that they are developed and maintained using a 
Systems Approach to Training (SAT). It describes the review criteria 
and evaluation methods used by the NRC staff for both initial training 
program reviews and ongoing training program inspections, and it 
summarizes the five SAT elements consistent with the framework outlined 
in ANSI/ANS-3.1-2014, as endorsed by Regulatory Guide 1.8, 
``Qualification and Training of Personnel for Nuclear Power Plants.''
    The public comment period was originally scheduled to close August 
21, 2026. The NRC is extending the public comment period on this 
document until September 21, 2026, to ensure meaningful public 
participation and uphold the agency's commitment to openness and 
transparency. Given overlapping and complex rulemakings currently open 
for comment, additional time prevents stakeholder fatigue, enables 
thorough technical and policy reviews, and allows for coordinated 
responses across related dockets. This extension supports high-quality, 
complete feedback and facilitates robust engagement from all interested 
parties, including public groups, industry experts, and tribal nations.
    Comments should be filed no later than September 21, 2026.
