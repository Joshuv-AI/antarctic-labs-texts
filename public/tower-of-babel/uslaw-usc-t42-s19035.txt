42 U.S.C. § 19035 — Research awards
Citation: us:usc:42:19035
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section19035
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 163 (Research And Development, Competition, And Innovation); Subchapter III (National Science Foundation For The Future); Part D (Nsf Research Security). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director shall continue to make awards, on a competitive basis, to institutions of higher education or non-profit organizations (or consortia of such institutions or organizations) to support research on the conduct of research and the research environment, including research on research misconduct or breaches of research integrity and detrimental research practices.
