43 C.F.R. § 3735.2 — No limitation of rights where claimant in diligent prosecution of work when future withdrawals made.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3735.2
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3735.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3730 (Public Law 359; Mining In Powersite Withdrawals: General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Under section 5 of the Act the rights to a location made prior to any future withdrawal or reservation for power development or one on which the locator was diligently working to make a discovery of valuable minerals are not limited or restricted.
