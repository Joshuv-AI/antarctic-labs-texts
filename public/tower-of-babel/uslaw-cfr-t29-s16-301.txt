29 C.F.R. § 16.301 — Filing and service of documents.
Citation: us:cfr:29:16.301
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-16.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 16 (Equal Access To Justice Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any application for an award or other pleading or document related to an application shall be filed with the adjudicative officer and served on all parties to the proceeding in the same manner as other pleadings in the proceeding.
