19 C.F.R. § 141.12 — Right to make entry of importations by other than common carrier.
Citation: us:cfr:19:141.12
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-141.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 141 (Entry Of Merchandise). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When merchandise is not imported by a common carrier, possession of the merchandise at the time of arrival in the United States shall be deemed sufficient evidence of the right to make entry.
