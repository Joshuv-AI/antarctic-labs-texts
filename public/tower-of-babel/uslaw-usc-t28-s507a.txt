28 U.S.C. § 507A — Assistant Attorney General for National Security
Citation: us:usc:28:507A
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section507A
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part II (Department Of Justice); Chapter 31 (The Attorney General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Of the Assistant Attorneys General appointed under section 506, one shall serve, upon the designation of the President, as the Assistant Attorney General for National Security.
(b)
The Assistant Attorney General for National Security shall—
(1)
serve as the head of the National Security Division of the Department of Justice under section 509A of this title;
(2)
serve as primary liaison to the Director of National Intelligence for the Department of Justice; and
(3)
perform such other duties as the Attorney General may prescribe.
