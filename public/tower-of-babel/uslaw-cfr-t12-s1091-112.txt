12 C.F.R. § 1091.112 — No limitation on relief sought in civil action or administrative adjudication.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1091.112
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1091.112
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1091 (Procedures For Supervisory Designation Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this part shall be construed to limit the relief the Bureau may seek in any civil action or administrative adjudication, including but not limited to, seeking an order to have a person deemed subject to the Bureau's supervisory authority under 12 U.S.C. 5514, including for the reasons set forth in 12 U.S.C. 5514(a)(1)(C).
