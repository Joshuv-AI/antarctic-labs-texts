31 C.F.R. § 306.21 — Reissue of nontransferable securities.
Citation: us:cfr:31:306.21
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-306.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 306 (General Regulations Governing U.S. Securities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Treasury Bonds, Investment Series B—1975-80, may be reissued only in the names of:
(a) Lawful successors in title,
(b) The legal representatives or distributees of a deceased owner's estate, or the distributees of a trust estate, and
(c) State supervisory authorities in pursuance of any pledge required of the owner under State law, or upon termination of the pledge in the names of the pledgors or their successors.
