16 U.S.C. § 4266 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:4266
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section4266
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 62A (Asian Elephant Conservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
There is authorized to be appropriated to the Fund $5,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2025 through 2029 to carry out this chapter, which may remain available until expended.
(b) Administrative expenses
Of amounts available each fiscal year to carry out this chapter, the Secretary may expend not more than 3 percent or $100,000, whichever is greater, to pay the administrative expenses necessary to carry out this chapter.
