10 C.F.R. § 76.23 — Specific exemptions.
Citation: us:cfr:10:76.23
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-76.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 76 (Certification Of Gaseous Diffusion Plants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission may, upon its own initiative or upon application of the Corporation, grant such exemptions from the requirements of the certification regulations as it determines are authorized by law and will not endanger life, or property, or the common defense and security, and are otherwise in the public interest.
