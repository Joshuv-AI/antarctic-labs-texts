31 C.F.R. § 539.406 — Importation into and release from a bonded warehouse or foreign trade zone.
Citation: us:cfr:31:539.406
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-539.406
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 539 (Weapons Of Mass Destruction Trade Control Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The prohibitions in §§ 539.201 and 539.202 apply to importation into a bonded warehouse or a foreign trade zone of the United States.
