20 C.F.R. § 61.307 — Transportation of recovered bodies of missing persons.
Citation: us:cfr:20:61.307
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-61.307
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 61 (Claims For Compensation Under The War Hazards Compensation Act, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an employee dies while in detention and the body is later recovered, the Office may provide the cost of transporting the body to the home of the deceased or to any place designated by the employee's next of kin, near relative, or legal representative.
