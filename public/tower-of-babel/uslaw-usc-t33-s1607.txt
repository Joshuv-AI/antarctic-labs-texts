33 U.S.C. § 1607 — Implementation by rules and regulations; authority to promulgate
Citation: us:usc:33:1607
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section1607
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 30 (International Regulations For Preventing Collisions At Sea). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the department in which the Coast Guard is operating is authorized to promulgate such reasonable rules and regulations as are necessary to implement the provisions of this chapter and the International Regulations proclaimed hereunder.
