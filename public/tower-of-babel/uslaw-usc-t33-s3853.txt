33 U.S.C. § 3853 — Liability in rem
Citation: us:usc:33:3853
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section3853
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 51 (Clean Hulls); Subchapter IV (Action On Violation, Penalties, And Referrals). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A vessel operated in violation of the Convention, this chapter, or any regulation prescribed under this chapter, is liable in rem for any fine imposed under title 18 or civil penalty assessed pursuant to section 3852 of this title, and may be proceeded against in the United States district court of any district in which the vessel may be found.
