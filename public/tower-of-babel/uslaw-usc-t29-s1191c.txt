29 U.S.C. § 1191c — Regulations
Citation: us:usc:29:1191c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section1191c
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 18 (Employee Retirement Income Security Program); Subchapter I (Protection Of Employee Benefit Rights); Part 7 (Group Health Plan Requirements); Subpart C (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary, consistent with section 104 of the Health Care Portability and Accountability Act of 1996, may promulgate such regulations as may be necessary or appropriate to carry out the provisions of this part. The Secretary may promulgate any interim final rules as the Secretary determines are appropriate to carry out this part.
