32 C.F.R. § 746.13 — Transfer of custody of Government inventions.
Citation: us:cfr:32:746.13
Creator: Department Of The Navy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-746.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 746 (Licensing Of Government Inventions In The Custody Of The Department Of The Navy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Chief of Naval Research may enter into an agreement to transfer custody of a Government invention to another government agency for purposes of administration, including the granting of licenses pursuant to this part.
