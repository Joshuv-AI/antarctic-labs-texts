28 U.S.C. § 1731 — Handwriting
Citation: us:usc:28:1731
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1731
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part V (Procedure); Chapter 115 (Evidence; Documentary). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The admitted or proved handwriting of any person shall be admissible, for purposes of comparison, to determine genuineness of other handwriting attributed to such person.
