2 C.F.R. § 1130.600 — Purpose of PROP Article VI.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1130.600
Creator: Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1130.600
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1130 (Property Administration: General Award Terms And Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

PROP Article VI sets forth the rights and responsibilities of recipients and the Federal Government with respect to intangible property. It thereby implements OMB guidance in 2 CFR 200.315.
