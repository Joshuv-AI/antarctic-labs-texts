24 C.F.R. § 943.144 — What financial impact do operations of a subsidiary, affiliate, or joint venture have on a PHA?
Citation: us:cfr:24:943.144
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-943.144
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 943 (Public Housing Agency Consortia And Joint Ventures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Income generated by subsidiaries, affiliates, or joint ventures formed under the authority of this subpart is to be used for low-income housing or to benefit the residents assisted by the PHA. This income will not cause a decrease in funding provided under the public housing program, except as otherwise provided under the Operating Fund and Capital Fund formulas.
