43 C.F.R. § 402.11 — Termination or cancellation.
Citation: us:cfr:43:402.11
Creator: Bureau Of Reclamation, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-402.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 402 (Sale Of Lands In Federal Reclamation Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Immediately upon the termination or cancellation of any contract for nonpayment or other appropriate reason the Commissioner shall notify the proper office of the Bureau of Land Management in order that the tract books located there may reflect the termination or cancellation of the contract.
