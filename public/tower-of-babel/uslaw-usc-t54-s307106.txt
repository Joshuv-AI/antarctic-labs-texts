54 U.S.C. § 307106 — Authorization for expenditure of appropriated funds
Citation: us:usc:54:307106
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section307106
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3071 (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Where appropriate, each Federal agency may expend funds appropriated for its authorized programs for the purposes of activities carried out pursuant to this division, except to the extent that appropriations legislation expressly provides otherwise.
