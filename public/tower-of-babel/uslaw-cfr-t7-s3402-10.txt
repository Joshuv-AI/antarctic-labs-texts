7 C.F.R. § 3402.10 — Application package.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3402.10
Creator: National Institute Of Food And Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3402.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3402 (Food And Agricultural Sciences National Needs Graduate And Postgraduate Fellowship Grants Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applications will be available at http://www.grants.gov and through the NIFA Web site. An application package will be made available to any potential grant applicant upon request. This package will include all necessary forms and instructions to apply for a grant under this program.
