6 U.S.C. § 764 — National exercise simulation center
Citation: us:usc:6:764
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section764
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 2 (National Emergency Management); Subchapter II (Comprehensive Preparedness System); Part B (Additional Preparedness). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President shall establish a national exercise simulation center that—
(1)
uses a mix of live, virtual, and constructive simulations to—
(A)
prepare elected officials, emergency managers, emergency response providers, and emergency support providers at all levels of government to operate cohesively;
(B)
provide a learning environment for the homeland security personnel of all Federal agencies;
(C)
assist in the development of operational procedures and exercises, particularly those based on catastrophic incidents; and
(D)
allow incident commanders to exercise decisionmaking in a simulated environment; and
(2)
uses modeling and simulation for training, exercises, and command and control functions at the operational level.
