44 C.F.R. § 61.16 — Probation additional premium.
Citation: us:cfr:44:61.16
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-61.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 61 (Insurance Coverage And Rates). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The additional premium charged pursuant to § 59.24(b) on each policy sold or renewed within a community placed on probation prior to October 1, 1992, is $25.00. Where the community was placed on probation on or after October 1, 1992, the additional premium charge is $50.00.”
