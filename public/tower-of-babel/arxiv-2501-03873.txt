Functors from the infinitary model theory of modules and the Auslander-Gruson-Jensen 2-functor
Samuel Dean

Abstract
We define the notion of a λ \lambda -definable category, a generalisation of the notion of definable category from the model theory of modules. Let 𝒞 \mathcal{C} be a λ \lambda -accessible additive category. We characterise the additive functors 𝒞 → Ab \mathcal{C}\to\operatorname{Ab} which preserve λ \lambda -directed colimits and products, by showing that they are the finitely presented functors determined by a morphism between λ \lambda -presented objects (the same result appears, for the case λ = ω \lambda=\omega , in [ 12 ] , but we give a proof for any infinite regular cardinal λ \lambda ). We remark that [ 13 ] shows that every λ \lambda -definable subcategory of 𝒞 \mathcal{C} is the class of zeroes of some set of such functors, thus obtaining a λ \lambda -ary generalisation of the finitary ( λ = ω \lambda=\omega ) result from the finitary model theory of modules. We show that, to analyse the λ \lambda -ary model theory of a locally λ \lambda -presentable additive category 𝒞 \mathcal{C} , it is sufficient to consider finitary pp formulas in the language of right Pres λ ⁡ ( 𝒞 ) \operatorname{Pres}_{\lambda}(\mathcal{C}) -modules, where Pres λ ⁡ ( 𝒞 ) \operatorname{Pres}_{\lambda}(\mathcal{C}) is the category of λ \lambda -presented objects of 𝒞 \mathcal{C} , with the caveat that these pp formulas are interpreted among right Pres λ ⁡ ( 𝒞 ) \operatorname{Pres}_{\lambda}(\mathcal{C}) -modules which preserve λ \lambda -small products. In particular, for an additive category ℛ \mathcal{R} with λ \lambda -small products (e.g. ℛ = Pres λ ⁡ ( 𝒞 ) op \mathcal{R}=\operatorname{Pres}_{\lambda}(\mathcal{C})^{\operatorname{op}} for 𝒞 \mathcal{C} a λ \lambda -presented additive category), the λ \lambda -accessible functors 𝒩 → Ab \mathcal{N}\to\operatorname{Ab} which preserve products are precisely the finitely accessible functors ℛ ​ Mod → Ab \mathcal{R}\operatorname{Mod}\to\operatorname{Ab} which preserve products, restricted to 𝒩 \mathcal{N} , where 𝒩 ⊆ ℛ ​ Mod \mathcal{N}\subseteq\mathcal{R}\operatorname{Mod} is the category of left ℛ \mathcal{R} -modules which preserve λ \lambda -small products.
