34 C.F.R. § 30.30 — What procedures apply when the Secretary requests another agency to offset a debt owed under a program or activity of the Department?
Citation: us:cfr:34:30.30
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-30.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 30 (Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Secretary may request another Federal agency to offset a debt owed under a program or activity of the Department if the Secretary certifies in writing to the other Federal agency:
(1) That the debtor owes a debt to the United States;
(2) The amount of the debt; and
(3) That the Secretary has complied with 4 CFR 102.3.
(b) Before providing the certification required under paragraph (a) of this section, the Secretary complies with the procedures in §§ 30.20-30.27.
