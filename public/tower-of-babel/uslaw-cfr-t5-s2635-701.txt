5 C.F.R. § 2635.701 — Overview.
Citation: us:cfr:5:2635.701
Creator: Office Of Government Ethics
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-2635.701
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 2635 (Standards Of Ethical Conduct For Employees Of The Executive Branch). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart contains provisions relating to the proper use of official time and authority, and of information and resources to which employees have access because of their Federal employment. This subpart sets forth standards relating to:
(a) Use of public office for private gain;
(b) Use of nonpublic information;
(c) Use of Government property; and
(d) Use of official time.
