25 C.F.R. § 23.134 — Who has access to reports and records during a proceeding?
Citation: us:cfr:25:23.134
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-23.134
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 23 (Indian Child Welfare Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each party to an emergency proceeding or a foster-care-placement or termination-of-parental-rights proceeding under State law involving an Indian child has a right to timely examine all reports and other documents filed or lodged with the court upon which any decision with respect to such action may be based.
