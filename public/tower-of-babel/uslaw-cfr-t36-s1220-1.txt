36 C.F.R. § 1220.1 — What is the scope of Subchapter B?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1220.1
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1220.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1220 (Federal Records; General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subchapter B specifies policies for Federal agencies' records management programs relating to proper records creation and maintenance, adequate documentation, and records disposition.
