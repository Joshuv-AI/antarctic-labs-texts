49 C.F.R. § 215.125 — Defective uncoupling device.
Citation: us:cfr:49:215.125
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-215.125
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 215 (Railroad Freight Car Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A railroad may not place or continue in service a car, if the car has an uncoupling device without sufficient vertical and lateral clearance to prevent—
(a) Fouling on curves; or
(b) Unintentional uncouplings.
