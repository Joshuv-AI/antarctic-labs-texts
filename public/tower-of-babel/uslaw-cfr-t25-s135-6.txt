25 C.F.R. § 135.6 — Refusal of water delivery.
Citation: us:cfr:25:135.6
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-135.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 135 (Construction Assessments, Crow Indian Irrigation Project). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The right is reserved to the United States to refuse the delivery of water to each of the said Irrigation Districts in the event of default in the payment of assessments, including penalties on account of delinquencies.
