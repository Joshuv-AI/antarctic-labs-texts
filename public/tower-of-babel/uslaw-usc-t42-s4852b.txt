42 U.S.C. § 4852b — National consultation on lead-based paint hazard reduction
Citation: us:usc:42:4852b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section4852b
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 63A (Residential Lead-Based Paint Hazard Reduction); Subchapter I (Lead-Based Paint Hazard Reduction). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In carrying out this chapter, the Secretary shall consult on an ongoing basis with the Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency, the Director of the Centers for Disease Control, other Federal agencies concerned with lead poisoning prevention, and the task force established pursuant to section 4852a of this title.
