16 U.S.C. § 6857 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:6857
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section6857
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 87A (Modernizing Access To Public Land). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated—
(1)
to the Secretary of the Interior to carry out this chapter—
(A)
$2,500,000 for fiscal year 2022; and
(B)
$5,500,000 for each of fiscal years 2023 through 2025;
(2)
to the Secretary of Agriculture to carry out this chapter—
(A)
$2,500,000 for fiscal year 2022; and
(B)
$5,500,000 for each of fiscal years 2023 through 2025; and
(3)
to the Secretary of the Army to carry out this chapter—
(A)
$1,500,000 for fiscal year 2022; and
(B)
$2,500,000 for each of fiscal years 2023 through 2025.
