19 U.S.C. § 4375 — Trade negotiating objectives
Citation: us:usc:19:4375
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section4375
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 28 (Trade Facilitation And Trade Enforcement); Subchapter IV (Prevention Of Evasion Of Antidumping And Countervailing Duty Orders); Part I (Actions Relating To Enforcement Of Trade Remedy Laws). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The principal negotiating objectives of the United States shall include obtaining the objectives of the bilateral agreements described under section 4374(a) of this title for any trade agreements under negotiation as of February 24, 2016, or future trade agreement negotiations.
