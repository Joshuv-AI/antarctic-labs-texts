20 C.F.R. § 725.211 — Time of determination of relationship and dependency of spouse or child for purposes of augmentation of benefits.
Citation: us:cfr:20:725.211
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-725.211
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 725 (Claims For Benefits Under Part C Of Title Iv Of The Federal Mine Safety And Health Act, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

With respect to the spouse or child of a miner entitled to benefits, and with respect to the child of a surviving spouse entitled to benefits, the determination as to whether an individual purporting to be a spouse or child is related to or dependent upon such miner or surviving spouse shall be based on the facts and circumstances present in each case, at the appropriate time.
