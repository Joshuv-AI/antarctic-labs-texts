33 U.S.C. § 568 — Limitation on power of committee of Congress to consider projects
Citation: us:usc:33:568
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section568
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 12 (River And Harbor Improvements Generally); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No project shall be considered by any committee of Congress with a view to its adoption, except with a view to a survey, if five years have elapsed since a report upon a survey of such project has been submitted to Congress pursuant to law.
