5 U.S.C. § 2906 — Oath; custody
Citation: us:usc:5:2906
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section2906
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart A (General Provisions); Chapter 29 (Commissions, Oaths, Records, And Reports); Subchapter I (Commissions, Oaths, And Records). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The oath of office taken by an individual under section 3331 of this title shall be delivered by him to, and preserved by, the House of Congress, agency, or court to which the office pertains.
