47 U.S.C. § 1751 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:47:1751
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title47-section1751
Context: United States Code, Title 47 (Telecommunications); Chapter 16 (Broadband Access); Subchapter IV (Broadband Affordability). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this subchapter—
(1)
the term “broadband internet access service” has the meaning given the term in section 8.1(b) of title 47, Code of Federal Regulations, or any successor regulation; and
(2)
the term “Commission” means the Federal Communications Commission.
