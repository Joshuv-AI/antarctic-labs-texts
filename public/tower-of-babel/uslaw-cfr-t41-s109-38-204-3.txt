41 C.F.R. § 109-38.204-3 — -38.204-3 Requests for exempted motor vehicles in the District of Columbia.
Citation: us:cfr:41:109-38.204-3
Creator: Chapter 109—Department Of Energy Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-109-38.204-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 109-38 (38—Motor Equipment Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director, Office of Administrative Services is designated to approve requests for regular District of Columbia license tags, and furnishes annually the name and specimen signature of each representative authorized to approve such requests to the District of Columbia Department of Transportation.
