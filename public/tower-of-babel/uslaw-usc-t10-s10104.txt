10 U.S.C. § 10104 — Army Reserve: composition
Citation: us:usc:10:10104
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section10104
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And Administration); Chapter 1003 (Reserve Components Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Army Reserve includes all Reserves of the Army who are not members of the Army National Guard of the United States.
