32 C.F.R. § 241.10 — Small business consideration.
Citation: us:cfr:32:241.10
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-241.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 241 (Pilot Program For Temporary Exchange Of Information Technology Personnel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The DoD CIO on behalf of the Secretary of Defense shall:
(a) Ensure that, of the assignments made each year, at least 20 percent are from small business concerns (as defined by 5 U.S.C. 3703(e)(2)(A)).
(b) Take into consideration the questions of how assignments might be used to help meet the needs of the DoD with respect to the training of employees in IT.
