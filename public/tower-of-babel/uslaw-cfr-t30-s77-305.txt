30 C.F.R. § 77.305 — Access to drying chambers, hot gas inlet chambers and ductwork; installation and maintenance.
Citation: us:cfr:30:77.305
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-77.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 77 (Mandatory Safety Standards, Surface Coal Mines And Surface Work Areas Of Underground Coal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Drying chambers, hot gas inlet chambers and all ductwork in which coal dust may accumulate shall be equipped with tight sealing access doors which shall remain latched during dryer operation to prevent the emission of coal dust and the loss of fluidizing air.
