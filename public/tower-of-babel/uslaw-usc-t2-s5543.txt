2 U.S.C. § 5543 — Rebates under Government Travel Charge Card Program
Citation: us:usc:2:5543
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section5543
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 55 (House Of Representatives Officers And Administration); Subchapter III (Chief Administrative Officer). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Effective with respect to fiscal years beginning with fiscal year 1995, amounts received by the Chief Administrative Officer of the House of Representatives from the Administrator of General Services for rebates under the Government Travel Charge Card Program shall be deposited in the Treasury as miscellaneous receipts.
