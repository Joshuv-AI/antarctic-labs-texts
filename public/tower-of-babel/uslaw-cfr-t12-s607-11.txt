12 C.F.R. § 607.11 — Report of assessments and expenses.
Citation: us:cfr:12:607.11
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-607.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 607 (Assessment And Apportionment Of Administrative Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

By January 15 of each calendar year, the FCA shall provide each assessed System institution with a report of assessments and expenses for the preceding fiscal year showing total assessments and other income received as applied to expenses incurred by major budget category and amounts set aside for a necessary reserve.
