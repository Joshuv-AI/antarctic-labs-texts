10 C.F.R. § 436.19 — Life cycle costs.
Citation: us:cfr:10:436.19
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-436.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 436 (Federal Energy Management And Planning Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Life cycle costs are the sum of the present values of—
(a) Investment costs, less salvage values at the end of the study period;
(b) Non-fuel operation and maintenance costs:
(c) Replacement costs less salvage costs of replaced building systems; and
(d) Energy and/or water costs.
