5 C.F.R. § 2606.105 — Rules for individuals seeking to ascertain if they are the subject of a record.
Citation: us:cfr:5:2606.105
Creator: Office Of Government Ethics
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-2606.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 2606 (Privacy Act Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An individual seeking to ascertain if any OGE system of records contains a record pertaining to him must follow the access procedures set forth at § 2606.201(a) and (b).
