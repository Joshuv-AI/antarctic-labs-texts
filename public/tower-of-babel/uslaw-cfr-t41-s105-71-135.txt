41 C.F.R. § 105-71.135 — -71.135 Subawards to debarred and suspended parties.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-71.135
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-71.135
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-71 (71—Uniform Administrative Requirements For Grants And Cooperative Agreements With State And Local Governments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Grantees and subgrantees must not make any award or permit any award (subgrant or contract) at any tier to any party which is debarred or suspended or is otherwise excluded from or ineligible for participation in Federal assistance programs under Executive Order 12549, “Debarment and Suspension”.
