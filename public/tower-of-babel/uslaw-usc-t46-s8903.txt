46 U.S.C. § 8903 — Self-propelled, uninspected passenger vessels
Citation: us:usc:46:8903
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section8903
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part F (Manning Of Vessels); Chapter 89 (Small Vessel Manning). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A self-propelled, uninspected passenger vessel shall be operated by an individual licensed by the Secretary to operate that type of vessel, under prescribed regulations.
