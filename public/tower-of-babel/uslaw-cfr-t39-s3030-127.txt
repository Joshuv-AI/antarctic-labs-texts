39 C.F.R. § 3030.127 — Maximum rate adjustment authority.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3030.127
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3030.127
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3030 (Regulation Of Rates For Market Dominant Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The maximum rate adjustment authority available to the Postal Service for each class of market dominant mail is limited to the sum of the percentage points developed in subparts C through E and G through H of this part.
(b) For any product where the attributable cost for that product exceeded the revenue from that product as determined by the Commission, rates may not be reduced.
