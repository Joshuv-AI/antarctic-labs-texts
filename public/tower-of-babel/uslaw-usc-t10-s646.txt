10 U.S.C. § 646 — Consideration of performance as a member of the Joint Staff
Citation: us:usc:10:646
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section646
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 36 (Promotion, Separation, And Involuntary Retirement Of Officers On The Active-Duty List); Subchapter V (Additional Provisions Relating To Promotion, Separation, And Retirement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Defense, in consultation with the Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff, shall ensure that officer personnel policies of the Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, and Space Force concerning promotion, retention, and assignment give appropriate consideration to the performance of an officer as a member of the Joint Staff.
