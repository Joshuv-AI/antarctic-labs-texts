On asymptotics of ring Q Q -homeomorphisms with respect to p p -modulus near the origin
Ruslan Salimov and Bogdan Klishchuk

Abstract
We consider the class of ring Q Q -homeomorphisms with respect to p p -modulus in ℝ n \mathbb{R}^{n} with p > n p>n , and obtain lower bounds for limsups of the distance distortions under such mappings. These estimates can be treated as Hölder’s continuity of the inverses near the origin. The sharpness is illustrated by example.

Introduction

In this paper, we study the distortion of distances under ring
Q
-homeomorphisms
with respect to
p
-modulus in
\mathbb{R}^{n}
with
p>n
. For such

Let us recall some definitions, see
Vai
. Let
\Gamma
be a family of curves

\gamma

\mathbb{R}^{n}

\rho:{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\to[0,\infty]

\int\limits_{\gamma}\rho(x)\,ds\ \geqslant\ 1

for all locally rectifiable

. Let

.

The quantity

M_{p}(\Gamma)\ =\ \inf_{\rho\in\mathrm{adm}\,\Gamma}\int\limits_{{\mathbb{R}^{n}}}\rho^{p}(x)\,dm(x)\,

is called
p
–modulus
of the family
\Gamma
. Here
dm(x)
corresponds to the Lebesgue
measure in
\mathbb{R}^{n}
.

Let
Q:D\to[0,\infty]
be a measurable function. Recall that a homeomorphism

f:D\to\mathbb{R}^{n}
is said to be a
Q
-homeomorphism with respect to

p
-modulus
if

\mathrm{M}_{p}(f\Gamma)\leqslant\int\limits_{D}Q(x)\,\rho^{p}(x)\ dm(x)

for every family
\Gamma
of
paths in
D
and every admissible function
\rho
for
\Gamma
.

For arbitrary sets

and
G

\mathbb{R}^{n}
we denote by

a set of all continuous curves

\gamma:[a,b]\rightarrow\mathbb{R}^{n}
, that connect
E
and
F
in
G
,
i.e. , such that
\gamma(a)\in E
,
\gamma(b)\in F
and
\gamma(t)\in G

for
a<t<b
.

Let
D
be a domain in
\mathbb{R}^{n}
,

,
x_{0}\in D
and

d_{0}={\rm dist}(x_{0},\partial D)
. Set

\mathbb{A}(x_{0},r_{1},r_{2})=\{x\in\mathbb{R}^{n}:r_{1}<|x-x_{0}|<r_{2}\}\,,

B(x_{0},r)=\{x\in\mathbb{R}^{n}:|x-x_{0}|<r\}\,,

S_{i}=S(x_{0},r_{i})=\{x\in\mathbb{R}^{n}:\,|x-x_{0}|=r_{i}\}\,,\quad i=1,\,2\,.

The following concept generalizes and localizes the concept of a
Q
-homeomorphism
with respect to
p
-modulus.

Let a function
Q:D\rightarrow[0,\infty]
be Lebesgue measurable.
We say that a homeomorphism
f:D\rightarrow\mathbb{R}^{n}
is a
ring

-homeomorphism with respect to
p

relation

M_{p}(\Delta(fS_{1},fS_{2},fD))\ \leqslant\ \int\limits_{{\mathbb{A}}}Q(x)\,\eta^{p}(|x-x_{0}|)\,dm(x)\,

holds for any ring
\mathbb{A}=\mathbb{A}(x_{0},r_{1},r_{2})
,

0<r_{1}<r_{2}<d_{0}

, and for any measurable function

\eta:(r_{1},r_{2})\rightarrow[0,\infty]
such that

\int\limits_{r_{1}}^{r_{2}}\eta(r)\,dr=1\,.

The theory of

-homeomorphisms for

Denote by

q_{x_{0}}(r)=\frac{1}{\omega_{n-1}\,r^{n-1}}\int\limits_{S(x_{0},r)}Q(x)\,d\mathcal{A}

Proposition 1

Let
D
be a domain in
\mathbb{R}^{n}
,

n\geqslant 2
,
x_{0}\in D

be a Lebesgue measurable

Theorem 1.1

Let

2
Main results

In the following theorem, we provide a lower bound for upper limits
of distance ratios under ring
Q
-homeomorphisms with respect to
p
-modulus
with
p>n
.

Theorem 2.1

Let
f:\mathbb{B}^{n}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}^{n}
be a ring

Q
-homeomorphism with respect to
p
-modulus at a point
x_{0}=0

with
p>n
and
f(0)=0
. Assume that the function
Q
satisfies the condition

q_{x_{0}}(t)\leqslant q_{0}\,t^{-\alpha},\,q_{0}\in(0,\infty)\,,\,\alpha\in[0,\infty),

for a.e.
t\in(0,\varepsilon_{0})
,
\varepsilon_{0}\in(0,1)
. Then

\limsup\limits_{x\rightarrow 0}\frac{|f(x)|}{|x|^{\frac{\alpha+p-n}{p-n}}}\geqslant\,\left(\frac{p-n}{\alpha+p-n}\right)^{\frac{p-1}{p-n}}\,q_{0}^{\frac{1}{n-p}}\,.

(1)

Proof

We denote by
L_{f}(r)=\max\limits_{|x|=r}|f(x)|
. Since
f(0)=0
,
\Omega_{n}\,L^{n}_{f}(r)\geqslant m(fB(0,r))
.
Hence,

L_{f}(r)\geqslant\left(\frac{m(fB(0,r))}{\Omega_{n}}\right)^{\frac{1}{n}}\,.

Then, by Theorem 1, we have

L_{f}(r)\geqslant\left(\frac{m(fB(0,r))}{\Omega_{n}}\right)^{\frac{1}{n}}\geqslant\left(\frac{p-n}{\alpha+p-n}\right)^{\frac{p-1}{p-n}}q_{0}^{\frac{1}{n-p}}\,r^{\frac{\alpha+p-n}{p-n}}\,

for any
r\in[0,\varepsilon_{0})
.

Thus, we obtain

\limsup\limits_{x\rightarrow 0}\frac{|f(x)|}{|x|^{\frac{\alpha+p-n}{p-n}}}=\limsup\limits_{r\rightarrow 0}\frac{L_{f}(r)}{r^{\frac{\alpha+p-n}{p-n}}}\geqslant\left(\frac{p-n}{\alpha+p-n}\right)^{\frac{p-1}{p-n}}q_{0}^{\frac{1}{n-p}}\,.

This completes the proof of Theorem 2.

Assuming a stronger growth condition on the majorant
Q
we deduce

Corollary 1

Let
f:\mathbb{B}^{n}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}^{n}
be a ring

Q
-homeomorphism with respect to
p
-modulus at a point
x_{0}=0

with
p>n
and
f(0)=0
. Assume that the function
Q
satisfies the condition

Q(x)\leqslant K\,|x|^{-\alpha},\,K\in(0,\infty)\,,\,\alpha\in[0,\infty),

for a.e.
x\in B(0,\varepsilon_{0})
,
\varepsilon_{0}\in(0,1)
. Then

\limsup\limits_{x\rightarrow 0}\frac{|f(x)|}{|x|^{\frac{\alpha+p-n}{p-n}}}\geqslant\,\left(\frac{p-n}{\alpha+p-n}\right)^{\frac{p-1}{p-n}}\,K^{\frac{1}{n-p}}\,.

Letting
\alpha=0
in Theorem 2, we obtain the following statement.

Corollary 2

Let
f:\mathbb{B}^{n}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}^{n}
be a ring

Q
-homeomorphism with respect to
p
-modulus at a point
x_{0}=0

with
p>n
and
f(0)=0
. Assume that the function
Q
satisfies the condition

q_{x_{0}}(t)\leqslant q_{0},\,\,q_{0}\in(0,\infty),

for a.e.
t\in(0,\varepsilon_{0})
,
\varepsilon_{0}\in(0,1)
. Then

\limsup\limits_{x\rightarrow 0}\frac{|f(x)|}{|x|}\geqslant\,q_{0}^{\frac{1}{n-p}}\,.

Similarly to Corollary 1, we get

Corollary 3

Let
f:\mathbb{B}^{n}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}^{n}
be a ring

Q
-homeomorphism with respect to
p
-modulus at a point
x_{0}=0

with
p>n
and
f(0)=0
. If the function
Q(x)\leqslant K
,
for a.e.
x\in\mathbb{B}^{n}
,
K\in(0,\infty)
, then

\limsup\limits_{x\rightarrow 0}\frac{|f(x)|}{|x|}\geqslant\,K^{\frac{1}{n-p}}\,.

Now we verify the sharpness of the bound in our main result.

Example.
Let
p>n
and
q_{0}>0
. Define
f_{0}:\mathbb{B}^{n}\to\mathbb{R}^{n}
by

f_{0}(x)=\begin{cases}q_{0}^{\frac{1}{n-p}}\left(\frac{p-n}{\alpha+p-n}\right)^{\frac{p-1}{p-n}}\,|x|^{\frac{\alpha+p-n}{p-n}}\,\frac{x}{|x|}\,,&x\neq 0\\
0,&x=0\,.\end{cases}\,

It can be easily seen that the estimate (
1
) is sharp, and it
becomes an equality under
f_{0}
.

Let us show that
f_{0}
is a ring
Q
-homeomorphism with
respect to
p
-modulus with
Q(x)=q_{0}\,|x|^{-\alpha}
at
x_{0}=0
with
p>n
. Clearly,

q_{x_{0}}(t)=q_{0}\,t^{-\alpha}
. Consider a ring
\mathbb{A}(0,r_{1},r_{2})
,
0<r_{1}<r_{2}<1
. Note that the mapping

f_{0}
maps the ring
\mathbb{A}(0,r_{1},r_{2})
onto the ring

\widetilde{\mathbb{A}}(0,\widetilde{r}_{1},\widetilde{r}_{2})
,
where

\widetilde{r}_{i}=q_{0}^{\frac{1}{n-p}}\,\left(\frac{p-n}{\alpha+p-n}\right)^{\frac{p-1}{p-n}}\,r_{i}^{\frac{\alpha+p-n}{p-n}}\,,\quad i=1,\,2.

Denote by
\Gamma
a set of all curves that join the spheres

S_{1}=S(0,r_{1})
and
S_{2}=S(0,r_{2})
in the ring
\mathbb{A}=\mathbb{A}(0,r_{1},r_{2})
. Then one can calculate the
p
-modulus of the family of curves

f_{0}\Gamma
in an implicit form:

M_{p}(f_{0}\Gamma)=\omega_{n-1}\,\left(\frac{p-n}{p-1}\right)^{p-1}\,\left(\widetilde{r}_{2}^{\frac{p-n}{p-1}}-\widetilde{r}_{1}^{\frac{p-n}{p-1}}\right)^{1-p}\,

(see, e.g., (2) in
Ge
).

Substituting the values
\widetilde{r}_{1}

and
\widetilde{r}_{2}
, defined above, in the previous equality one gets

M_{p}(f_{0}\Gamma)=\omega_{n-1}\,q_{0}\,\left(\frac{\alpha+p-n}{p-1}\right)^{p-1}\,\left(r_{2}^{\frac{\alpha+p-n}{p-1}}-r_{1}^{\frac{\alpha+p-n}{p-1}}\right)^{1-p}\,.

Note that the last equality can be written as

M_{p}(f_{0}\Gamma)=\frac{\omega_{n-1}}{\left(\int\limits_{r_{1}}^{r_{2}}\frac{dt}{t^{\frac{n-1}{p-1}}\,q_{x_{0}}^{\frac{1}{p-1}}(t)}\right)^{p-1}}\,,

where
q_{x_{0}}(t)=q_{0}\,t^{-\alpha}
and
x_{0}=0
.

Hence, by Proposition 1, the homeomorphism
f_{0}
is a ring

Q
-homeomorphism with respect to
p
-modulus for
p>n
with

Q(x)=q_{0}\,|x|^{-\alpha}
at origin.
