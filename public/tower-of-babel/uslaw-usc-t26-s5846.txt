26 U.S.C. § 5846 — Other laws applicable
Citation: us:usc:26:5846
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section5846
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 53 (Machine Guns, Destructive Devices, And Certain Other Firearms); Subchapter B (General Provisions And Exemptions); Part I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All provisions of law relating to special taxes imposed by chapter 51 and to engraving, issuance, sale, accountability, cancellation, and distribution of stamps for tax payment shall, insofar as not inconsistent with the provisions of this chapter, be applicable with respect to the taxes imposed by sections 5801, 5811, and 5821.
