31 C.F.R. § 50.14 — Separate line item.
Citation: us:cfr:31:50.14
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-50.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 50 (Terrorism Risk Insurance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An insurer is deemed to be in compliance with the requirement of providing disclosure on a “separate line item in the policy” under § 50.10(b) if the insurer makes the disclosure:
(a) On the declarations page of the policy;
(b) Elsewhere within the policy itself; or
(c) In any rider or endorsement, or other document that is made a part of the policy.
