38 U.S.C. § 1814 — Vocational training
Citation: us:usc:38:1814
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section1814
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 18 (Benefits For Children Of Vietnam Veterans And Certain Other Veterans); Subchapter II (Children Of Women Vietnam Veterans Born With Certain Birth Defects). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Authority.—
The Secretary may provide a program of vocational training to an eligible child if the Secretary determines that the achievement of a vocational goal by the child is reasonably feasible.
(b) Applicable Provisions.—
Subsections (b) through (e) of section 1804 of this title shall apply with respect to any program of vocational training provided under subsection (a).
