25 C.F.R. § 256.21 — Who does the work?
Citation: us:cfr:25:256.21
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-256.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 256 (Housing Improvement Program (Hip)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Your house will be renovated or replaced by either:
(a) A licensed and bonded independent contractor or construction company; or
(b) A tribe that operates the HIP under an Indian Self-Determination and Education Assistance Act agreement.
