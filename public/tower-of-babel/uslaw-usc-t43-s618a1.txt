43 U.S.C. § 618a–1 — Availability of Colorado River Development Fund for investigation and construction purposes
Citation: us:usc:43:618a–1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section618a–1
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 12A (Boulder Canyon Project); Subchapter II (Boulder Canyon Project Adjustment Act). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The availability of appropriations from the Colorado River Development Fund for the investigation and construction of projects in any of the States of the Colorado River Basin shall not be held to forbid the expenditure of other funds for those purposes in any of those States where such funds are otherwise available therefor.
