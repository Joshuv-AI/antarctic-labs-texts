42 U.S.C. § 15963 — Report
Citation: us:usc:42:15963
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section15963
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 149 (National Energy Policy And Programs); Subchapter IV (Coal); Part A (Clean Coal Power Initiative). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 1 year after August 8, 2005, and once every 2 years thereafter through 2014, the Secretary, in consultation with other appropriate Federal agencies, shall submit to Congress a report describing—
(1)
the technical milestones set forth in section 15962 of this title and how those milestones ensure progress toward meeting the requirements of subsections (b)(1)(B) and (b)(2) of section 15962 of this title; and
(2)
the status of projects funded under this part.
