5 C.F.R. § 591.218 — How does OPM compute price indexes?
Citation: us:cfr:5:591.218
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-591.218
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 591 (Allowances And Differentials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except for shelter and energy utilities, OPM averages by item the prices collected in each survey area. For the Washington, DC, area, OPM computes a simple average for each item based on the average prices from each DC survey area. On an item-by-item basis, OPM divides the COLA survey area average price by the DC average price and produces a price index.
