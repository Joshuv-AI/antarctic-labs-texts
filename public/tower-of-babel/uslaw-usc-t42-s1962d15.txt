42 U.S.C. § 1962d–15 — Protection of United States from liability for damages; exception of damages due to fault or negligence of United States
Citation: us:usc:42:1962d–15
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1962d–15
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 19B (Water Resources Planning); Subchapter IV (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The requirement in any water resources development project under the jurisdiction of the Secretary of the Army, that non-Federal interests hold and save the United States free from damages due to the construction, operation, and maintenance of the project, does not include damages due to the fault or negligence of the United States or its contractors.
