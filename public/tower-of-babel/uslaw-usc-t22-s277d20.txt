22 U.S.C. § 277d–20 — Limitation on application for reimbursement or compensation
Citation: us:usc:22:277d–20
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section277d–20
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter IV (International Boundary And Water Commission). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Application for reimbursement or compensation under section 277d–19 of this title shall be submitted to the Commissioner within either one year from the date of acquisition or the date of vacating the premises by the applicant, whichever date is later. Applications not submitted within said period shall be forever barred.
