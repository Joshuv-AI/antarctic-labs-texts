5 C.F.R. § 919.210 — Which nonprocurement transactions are covered transactions?
Citation: us:cfr:5:919.210
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-919.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 919 (Governmentwide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All nonprocurement transactions, as defined in § 919.970, are covered transactions unless listed in § 919.215. (See appendix to this part.)
