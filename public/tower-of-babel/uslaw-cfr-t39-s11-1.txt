39 C.F.R. § 11.1 — Establishment.
Citation: us:cfr:39:11.1
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-11.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 11 (Advisory Boards (Article Xi)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board of Governors may create such advisory boards as it may deem appropriate and may appoint persons to serve thereon or may delegate such latter authority to the Postmaster General.
