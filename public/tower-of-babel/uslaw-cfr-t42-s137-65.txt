42 C.F.R. § 137.65 — May a Self-Governance Tribe receive statutorily mandated grant funding in an annual lump sum advance payment?
Citation: us:cfr:42:137.65
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-137.65
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 137 (Tribal Self-Governance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, grant funds shall be added to the funding agreement as an annual lump sum advance payment after the grant is awarded.
