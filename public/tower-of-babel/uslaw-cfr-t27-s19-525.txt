27 C.F.R. § 19.525 — Reclosing.
Citation: us:cfr:27:19.525
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-19.525
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 19 (Distilled Spirits Plants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A proprietor may reclose bottles of distilled spirits filled on bonded premises as provided in subpart N of this part. A proprietor may also reclose bottles of distilled spirits to which closures or other devices have been affixed as provided in § 19.498.
