10 C.F.R. § 709.23 — Obtaining consent to a polygraph examination.
Citation: us:cfr:10:709.23
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-709.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 709 (Counterintelligence Evaluation Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

DOE may not administer a polygraph examination unless DOE:
(a) Notifies the covered person of the polygraph examination in writing in accordance with § 709.21 of this part; and
(b) Obtains written consent from the covered person prior to the polygraph examination.
