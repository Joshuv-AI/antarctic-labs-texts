32 C.F.R. § 1615.3 — Registration procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1615.3
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1615.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1615 (Administration Of Registration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Persons required by selective service law and the Proclamation of the President to register shall be registered in accord with procedures prescribed by the Director of Selective Service.
