33 C.F.R. § 401.54 — Interference with navigation aids.
Citation: us:cfr:33:401.54
Creator: Great Lakes St. Lawrence Seaway Development Corporation, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-401.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 401 (Seaway Regulations And Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Aids to navigation shall not be interfered with or used as moorings.
(b) No person shall, unless authorized by the Corporation or the Manager, set out buoys or navigation markers on the Seaway.
