7 C.F.R. § 2100.041 — Accreditation of third-party verifiers.
Citation: us:cfr:7:2100.041
Creator: Office Of Energy And Environmental Policy, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-2100.041
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 2100 (Technical Guidelines For The Production Of Regenerative Agricultural Biofuel Feedstocks). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Third-party verifiers that conduct audits in accordance with this part must:
(a) Be accredited to ISO 14065: General principles and requirements for bodies validating and verifying environmental information by a member of the Global Accreditation Cooperation Incorporated (Global ACI);
(b) Be unrelated to the entity seeking verification. To avoid any real or potential conflict of interest, a third-party verifier must not have a relationship with the entity based on one or more of the following factors (this is a non-exclusive list): familial or personal relationship, or shared ownership, governance, management, personnel, resources, finances, contracts, or marketing (including branding); and
(c) Assign at least one verifier to the verification team who is an Agricultural Expert.
