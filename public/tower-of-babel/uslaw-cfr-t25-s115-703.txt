25 C.F.R. § 115.703 — May we accept for deposit into a trust account money not specified in § 115.702?
Citation: us:cfr:25:115.703
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-115.703
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 115 (Trust Funds For Tribes And Individual Indians). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No, we will not accept funds from sources that are not identified in the table in § 115.702 for deposit into a trust account.
