22 U.S.C. § 6712 — No abridgement of constitutional rights
Citation: us:usc:22:6712
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section6712
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 75 (Chemical Weapons Convention Implementation); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No person may be required, as a condition for entering into a contract with the United States or as a condition for receiving any benefit from the United States, to waive any right under the Constitution for any purpose related to this chapter or the Convention.
