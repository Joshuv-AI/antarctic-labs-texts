21 C.F.R. § 660.32 — Collection of source material.

Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-660.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 660 (Additional Standards For Diagnostic Substances For Laboratory Tests). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Blood for Reagent Red Blood Cells from donors of peripheral blood shall be collected as prescribed under § 640.4 of this chapter, except that paragraphs (c), (d), (g), and (h) of § 640.4 shall not apply.
