6 U.S.C. § 541 — Definitions

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 1 (Homeland Security Organization); Subchapter XII (Transition); Part A (Reorganization Plan). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of this subchapter:
(1)
The term “agency” includes any entity, organizational unit, program, or function.
(2)
The term “transition period” means the 12-month period beginning on the effective date of this chapter.
