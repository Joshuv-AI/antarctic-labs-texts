34 U.S.C. § 12181 — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:34:12181
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section12181
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 121 (Violent Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Subchapter II (Crime Prevention); Part E (National Community Economic Partnership); Subpart 1 (Community Economic Partnership Investment Funds). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the purpose of this subpart to increase private investment in distressed local communities and to build and expand the capacity of local institutions to better serve the economic needs of local residents through the provision of financial and technical assistance to community development corporations.
