42 C.F.R. § 447.202 — Audits.
Citation: us:cfr:42:447.202
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-447.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 447 (Payments For Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Medicaid agency must assure appropriate audit of records if payment is based on costs of services or on a fee plus cost of materials.
