39 U.S.C. § 3681 — Reimbursement
Citation: us:usc:39:3681
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title39-section3681
Context: United States Code, Title 39 (Postal Service); Part IV (Mail Matter); Chapter 36 (Postal Rates, Classes, And Services); Subchapter VI (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No mailer may be reimbursed for any amount paid under any rate or fee which, after such payment, is determined to have been unlawful after proceedings in accordance with the provisions of sections 3662 through 3664 of this title, or is superseded by a lower rate or fee established under subchapter II of this chapter.
