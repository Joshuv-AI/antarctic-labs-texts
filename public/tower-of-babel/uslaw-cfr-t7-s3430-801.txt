7 C.F.R. § 3430.801 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3430.801
Creator: National Institute Of Food And Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3430.801
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3430 (Competitive And Noncompetitive Non-Formula Federal Assistance Programs—General Award Administrative Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this program is to make competitive grants to Non Land Grant Colleges of Agriculture (NLGCA) Institutions to assist the NLGCA Institutions in maintaining and expanding the capacity to conduct education, research, and outreach activities relating to agriculture, renewable resources, and other similar disciplines.
