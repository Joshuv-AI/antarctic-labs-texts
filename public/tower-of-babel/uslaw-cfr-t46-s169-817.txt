46 C.F.R. § 169.817 — Master to instruct ship's company.
Citation: us:cfr:46:169.817
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-169.817
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 169 (Sailing School Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The master shall conduct drills and give instructions as necessary to insure that all hands are familiar with their duties as specified in the station bill.
