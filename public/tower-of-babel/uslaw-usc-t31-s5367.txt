31 U.S.C. § 5367 — Circumventions prohibited
Citation: us:usc:31:5367
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section5367
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 53 (Monetary Transactions); Subchapter IV (Prohibition On Funding Of Unlawful Internet Gambling). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding section 5362(2), a financial transaction provider, or any interactive computer service or telecommunications service, may be liable under this subchapter if such person has actual knowledge and control of bets and wagers, and—
(1)
operates, manages, supervises, or directs an Internet website at which unlawful bets or wagers may be placed, received, or otherwise made, or at which unlawful bets or wagers are offered to be placed, received, or otherwise made; or
(2)
owns or controls, or is owned or controlled by, any person who operates, manages, supervises, or directs an Internet website at which unlawful bets or wagers may be placed, received, or otherwise made, or at which unlawful bets or wagers are offered to be placed, received, or otherwise made.
