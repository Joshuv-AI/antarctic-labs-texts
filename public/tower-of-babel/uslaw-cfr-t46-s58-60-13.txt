46 C.F.R. § 58.60-13 — -13 Inspection.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 58 (Main And Auxiliary Machinery And Related Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An industrial system is accepted by the Coast Guard if the inspector finds—
(a) The system meets this subpart;
(b) There are guards, shields, insulation or similar devices for protection of personnel; and
(c) The system is not manifestly unsafe.
