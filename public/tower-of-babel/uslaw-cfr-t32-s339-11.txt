32 C.F.R. § 339.11 — Rescinded guidance.
Citation: us:cfr:32:339.11
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-339.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 339 (Dod Guidance Documents). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) All effective guidance documents must appear on the central website. If the guidance document does not appear on the central website, the guidance is rescinded and without effect.
(b) No component may cite, use, or rely on guidance documents that are rescinded, except to establish historical facts.
