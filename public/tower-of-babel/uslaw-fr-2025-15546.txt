COMMISSION ON CIVIL RIGHTS


Notice of Public Meeting of the Louisiana Advisory Committee; 
Cancellation

AGENCY: Commission on Civil Rights.

ACTION: Notice; cancellation of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission on Civil Rights published a notice in the 
Federal Register concerning a meeting of the Louisiana Advisory 
Committee. The meeting, scheduled for Thursday, August 21, 2025, at 
10:00 a.m. CST, has been cancelled. The notice is in the Federal 
Register on Monday, August 4, 2025, in FR Document Number 2025-14714 on 
page 36419.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Corrine Sanders, Support Services 
Specialist, at [email protected] or (202) 768-5474.
