7 C.F.R. § 631.23 — State conservationist's authority.
Citation: us:cfr:7:631.23
Creator: Natural Resources Conservation Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-631.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 631 (Great Plains Conservation Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The state conservationist may take the initiative to revise or require revision of any determination made by the contracting officer or the district conservationist in connection with the program, except that the state conservationist may not revise any executed contract other than as may specifically be authorized herein.
