44 U.S.C. § 1316 — Fish and Wildlife Service: report of the Director
Citation: us:usc:44:1316
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section1316
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 13 (Particular Reports And Documents). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In addition to the usual number of the report of the Director of the Fish and Wildlife Service, eight thousand copies shall be printed: two thousand for the Senate, four thousand for the House of Representatives, and two thousand for distribution by the Service.
