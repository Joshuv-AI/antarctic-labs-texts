43 C.F.R. § 5420.0-6 — -6 Policy.

Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 5420 (Preparation For Sale). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All timber or other vegetative resources to be sold, except materials that qualify under § 5402.0-6(e) of this chapter, will be appraised to estimate fair market value. Measurement shall be by tree cruise, log scale, weight, or such other form of measurement as may be determined to be in the public interest.
