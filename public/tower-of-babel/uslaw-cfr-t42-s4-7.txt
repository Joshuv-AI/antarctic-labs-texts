42 C.F.R. § 4.7 — Fees.
Citation: us:cfr:42:4.7
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-4.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 4 (National Library Of Medicine). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director may charge reasonable fees for any service provided by the Library under this part, in accordance with a schedule available at the Library upon request, which are designed to recover all or a portion of the cost to the Library of providing the service.
