36 C.F.R. § 902.72 — Time limitations on filing an appeal.
Citation: us:cfr:36:902.72
Creator: Pennsylvania Avenue Development Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-902.72
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 902 (Freedom Of Information Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An appeal must be submitted in writing within thirty days from the date of receipt of the initial written denial and must contain the information requested in § 902.71.
