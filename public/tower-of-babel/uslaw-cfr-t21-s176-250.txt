21 C.F.R. § 176.250 — Poly-1,4,7,10,13-pentaaza-15-hydroxyhexadecane.
Citation: us:cfr:21:176.250
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-176.250
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 176 (Indirect Food Additives: Paper And Paperboard Components). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Poly-1,4,7,10,13-pentaaza-15-hydrox-yhexadecane may be safely used as a retention aid employed prior to the sheet-forming operation in the manufacture of paper and paperboard intended for use in contact with food in an amount not to exceed that necessary to accomplish the intended physical or technical effect and not to exceed 6 pounds per ton of finished paper or paperboard.
