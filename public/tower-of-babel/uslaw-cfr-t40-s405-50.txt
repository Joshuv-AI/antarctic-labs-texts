40 C.F.R. § 405.50 — Applicability; description of the cottage cheese and cultured cream cheese subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:405.50
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-405.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 405 (Dairy Products Processing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges resulting from the manufacture of cottage cheese and cultured cream cheese.
