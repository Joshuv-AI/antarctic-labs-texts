21 C.F.R. § 868.5720 — Bronchial tube.
Citation: us:cfr:21:868.5720
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-868.5720
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 868 (Anesthesiology Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. A bronchial tube is a device used to differentially intubate a patient's bronchus (one of the two main branches of the trachea leading directly to the lung) in order to isolate a portion of lung distal to the tube.
(b) Classification. Class II (performance standards).
