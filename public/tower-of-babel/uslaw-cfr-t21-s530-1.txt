21 C.F.R. § 530.1 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:21:530.1
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-530.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 530 (Extralabel Drug Use In Animals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to the extralabel use in an animal of any approved new animal drug or approved new human drug by or on the lawful order of a licensed veterinarian within the context of a valid veterinary-client-patient relationship.
