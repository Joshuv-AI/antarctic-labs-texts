23 C.F.R. § 661.51 — Can TTFBP funds be used for the approach roadway to a bridge?
Citation: us:cfr:23:661.51
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-661.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 661 (Tribal Transportation Facility Bridge Program (Ttfbp)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, costs associated with approach roadway work, as defined in § 661.5 are eligible. Long approach fills, causeways, connecting roadways, interchanges, ramps, and other extensive earth structures, when constructed beyond an attainable touchdown point, are not eligible uses of TTFBP funds.
