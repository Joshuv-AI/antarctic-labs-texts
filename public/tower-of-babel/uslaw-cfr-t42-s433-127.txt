42 C.F.R. § 433.127 — Termination of FFP for failure to provide access to claims processing and information retrieval systems.
Citation: us:cfr:42:433.127
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-433.127
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 433 (State Fiscal Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

CMS will terminate FFP at any time if the Medicaid agency fails to provide State and Federal representatives with full access to the system, including on-site inspection. CMS may request such access at any time to determine whether the conditions in this subpart are being met.
