16 C.F.R. § 23.26 — Misrepresentation as to varietal name.
Citation: us:cfr:16:23.26
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-23.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 23 (Guides For The Jewelry, Precious Metals, And Pewter Industries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) It is unfair or deceptive to mark or describe an industry product with the incorrect varietal name.
(b) The following are examples of markings or descriptions that may be misleading:
(1) Use of the term “yellow emerald” to describe golden beryl or heliodor.
(2) Use of the term “green amethyst” to describe prasiolite.
