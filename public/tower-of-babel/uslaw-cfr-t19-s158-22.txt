19 C.F.R. § 158.22 — Not applicable when allowances made under other provisions.
Citation: us:cfr:19:158.22
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-158.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 158 (Relief From Duties On Merchandise Lost, Damaged, Abandoned, Or Exported). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The procedures in this subpart do not apply in cases where allowances in duties are made under subpart A or subpart B of this part, or § 18.6 of this chapter.
