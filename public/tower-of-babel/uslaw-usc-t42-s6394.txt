42 U.S.C. § 6394 — Prohibited acts
Citation: us:usc:42:6394
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section6394
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 77 (Energy Conservation); Subchapter IV (General Provisions); Part B (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It shall be unlawful for any person—
(1)
to violate any provision of title I or title II of this Act or this title 1 (other than any provision of such titles which amend 2 another law),
(2)
to violate any rule, regulation, or order issued pursuant to any such provision or any provision of section 383 of this Act [42 U.S.C. 6363]; or
(3)
to fail to comply with any provision prescribed in, or pursuant to, an energy conservation contingency plan which is in effect.
