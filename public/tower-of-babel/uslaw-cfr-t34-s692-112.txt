34 C.F.R. § 692.112 — May a State use the funds it receives from the GAP Program to pay administrative costs?
Citation: us:cfr:34:692.112
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-692.112
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 692 (Leveraging Educational Assistance Partnership Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A State that receives an allotment under this subpart may reserve not more than two percent of the funds made available annually for State administrative functions required for administering the partnership and other program activities.
(b) A State must use not less than ninety-eight (98) percent of an allotment under this subpart to make LEAP Grants under GAP.
