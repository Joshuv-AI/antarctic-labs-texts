41 U.S.C. § 2305 — Authority of Director of Office of Management and Budget not affected
Citation: us:usc:41:2305
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title41-section2305
Context: United States Code, Title 41 (Public Contracts); Chapter 23 (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This division does not limit the authorities and responsibilities of the Director of the Office of Management and Budget in effect on December 1, 1983.
