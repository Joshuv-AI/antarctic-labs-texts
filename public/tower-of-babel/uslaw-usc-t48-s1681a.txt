48 U.S.C. § 1681a — Appointment of High Commissioner
Citation: us:usc:48:1681a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1681a
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 14 (Trust Territory Of The Pacific Islands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any appointment made on or after May 10, 1967, to the office of the High Commissioner of the Trust Territory of the Pacific Islands shall be made by the President by and with the advice and consent of the Senate.
