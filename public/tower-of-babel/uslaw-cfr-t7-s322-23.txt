7 C.F.R. § 322.23 — Documentation.
Citation: us:cfr:7:322.23
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-322.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 322 (Bees, Beekeeping Byproducts, And Beekeeping Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each shipment of restricted organisms transiting the United States must be accompanied by a document issued by the appropriate regulatory authority of the national government of the region of origin stating that the shipment has been inspected and determined to meet the packaging requirements in § 322.24.
