25 C.F.R. § 141.24 — Attendance at semi-annual meetings.
Citation: us:cfr:25:141.24
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-141.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 141 (Business Practices On The Navajo, Hopi And Zuni Reservations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon the request of a tribal official designated by the governing body, each licensee shall attend a semi-annual public meeting of a tribal governing body to respond to customer inquiries.
