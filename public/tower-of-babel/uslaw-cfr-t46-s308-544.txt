46 C.F.R. § 308.544 — Facultative binder, Form MA-315.
Citation: us:cfr:46:308.544
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-308.544
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 308 (War Risk Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The standard form of War Risk Facultative Cargo Binder, which may be obtained from MARAD's underwriting agent of MARAD, shall be completed by the applicant and submitted, in duplicate, to an Underwriting Agent before the insurance can be bound.
