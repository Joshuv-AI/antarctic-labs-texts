10 C.F.R. § 51.105a — a Public hearings in proceedings for issuance of manufacturing licenses.
Citation: us:cfr:10:51.105a
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-51.105a
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 51 (Environmental Protection Regulations For Domestic Licensing And Related Regulatory Functions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to complying with applicable requirements of § 51.31(c), in a proceeding for the issuance of a manufacturing license, the presiding officer will determine whether, in accordance with the regulations in this subpart, the manufacturing license should be issued as proposed by the NRC's Director, Office of Nuclear Reactor Regulation.
