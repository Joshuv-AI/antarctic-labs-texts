15 C.F.R. § 998.41 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:15:998.41
Creator: National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-998.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 998 (Marine And Aviation Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to NOAA Corps officers, personnel boards convened by the Director of Office of Marine and Aviation Operations (OMAO) and the NOAA Corps (Director), and the Inspector General of the Department of Commerce.
