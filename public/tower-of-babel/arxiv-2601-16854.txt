Stochastic Analysis of Fifth-Order KdV Soliton in Damping Regime and Reduction to Painlevé Second Equation
Irfan Mahmood, Adeena Iqbal, Sohail Mumtaz, [1ex] Center for High Energy Physics, University of the PunjabQuaid-e-Azam Campus, Lahore 54590, Pakistan

Abstract
This work presents a stochastic analysis of fifth-order KdV soliton momentum distribution in a damping regime. An explicit representation of the soliton momentum associated with amplitude variation is derived in terms of a random time function in the presence of dissipation. Statistical interpretations of soliton propagation modes, amplitude fluctuations, and amplification are analyzed within a δ \delta -correlated Gaussian random framework. Graphical results obtained using Python illustrate the physical insight into amplitude fluctuation and energy flow. Finally, under a dominant approximation, the nonlinear momentum evolution equation is shown to reduce to the Painlevé second equation, a well-known integrable model appearing in diverse physical systems.

Conclusion

This finding on discrete analog will be towards more generalization of stochastic analysis of multi-solitons on lattice and reducible to continuum region for multi-dimensional cases.
