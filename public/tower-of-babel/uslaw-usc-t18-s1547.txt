18 U.S.C. § 1547 — Alternative imprisonment maximum for certain offenses
Citation: us:usc:18:1547
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1547
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 75 (Passports And Visas). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of this title, the maximum term of imprisonment that may be imposed for an offense under this chapter (other than an offense under section 1545)—
(1)
if committed to facilitate a drug trafficking crime (as defined in 929(a)) is 15 years; and
(2)
if committed to facilitate an act of international terrorism (as defined in section 2331) is 20 years.
