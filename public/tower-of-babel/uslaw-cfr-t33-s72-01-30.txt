33 C.F.R. § 72.01-30 — -30 Temporary deficiencies.
Citation: us:cfr:33:72.01-30
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-72.01-30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 72 (Marine Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Temporary deficiencies in aids to navigation are not published in Notices to Mariners when it is known that the defects will be corrected promptly.
