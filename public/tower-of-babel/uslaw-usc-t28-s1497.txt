28 U.S.C. § 1497 — Oyster growers’ damages from dredging operations
Citation: us:usc:28:1497
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1497
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part IV (Jurisdiction And Venue); Chapter 91 (United States Court Of Federal Claims). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States Court of Federal Claims shall have jurisdiction to render judgment upon any claim for damages to oyster growers on private or leased lands or bottoms arising from dredging operations or use of other machinery and equipment in making river and harbor improvements authorized by Act of Congress.
