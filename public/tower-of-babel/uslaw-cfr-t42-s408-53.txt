42 C.F.R. § 408.53 — Change from partial direct remittance to full deduction.
Citation: us:cfr:42:408.53
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-408.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 408 (Premiums For Supplementary Medical Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a benefit that was less than the premium (and therefore required direct remittance of the difference) is increased to an amount equal to, or greater than, the premium—
(a) The full premium is paid from the benefit; and
(b) Any amounts the enrollee had paid toward premiums not yet due are refunded.
