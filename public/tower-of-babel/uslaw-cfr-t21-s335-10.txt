21 C.F.R. § 335.10 — Antidiarrheal active ingredients.
Citation: us:cfr:21:335.10
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-335.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 335 (Antidiarrheal Drug Products For Over-The-Counter Human Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The active ingredient of the product consists of any one of the following when used within the dosage limits established for each ingredient in § 335.50(d):
(a) Bismuth subsalicylate.
(b) Kaolin.
