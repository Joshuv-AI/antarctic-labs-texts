7 U.S.C. § 2009i — Interstate collaboration
Citation: us:usc:7:2009i
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2009i
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 50 (Agricultural Credit); Subchapter V (Rural Community Advancement Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall permit the establishment of voluntary pooling arrangements among States, and regional fund-sharing agreements, to carry out projects receiving assistance under this subchapter.
