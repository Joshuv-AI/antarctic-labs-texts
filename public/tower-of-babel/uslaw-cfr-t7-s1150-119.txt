7 C.F.R. § 1150.119 — Cooperative association.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1150.119
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Milk), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1150.119
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1150 (Dairy Promotion Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Cooperative association means any cooperative marketing association of producers which is organized under the provisions of the Act of Congress of February 18, 1922, as amended, known as the “Capper-Volstead Act”.
