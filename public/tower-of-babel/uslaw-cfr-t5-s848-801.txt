5 C.F.R. § 848.801 — Reemployment of an individual who has separated from phased employment and who dies before submitting an application for a composite retirement annuity.
Citation: us:cfr:5:848.801
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-848.801
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 848 (Phased Retirement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A phased retiree who has been separated from employment for more than 3 days and who has entered full retirement status, but who has not submitted an application for composite retirement annuity, is deemed to be an annuitant receiving annuity from the Civil Service Retirement and Disability Fund during any period of employment in an appointive or elective position in the Federal Government.
