Introduction

Let
k\geq 3
and
0<\theta\leq 1
be fixed. Following the breakthrough of Green and Tao
[
3

The key input is a uniform prime number theorem (PNT) in short intervals

\sum_{x<n\leq x+x^{\theta}}\Lambda(n)=x^{\theta}(1+o(1))

holding for all

X\to\infty
.
This uniform statement follows from the recent long slender zero–density bounds for

4

With (

–trick and apply the relative Szemerédi theorem
[
1
,
3
]
to a short–interval majorant to deduce our main counting result.

Theorem 1.1

(Uniform many
k
–APs in short intervals)
.

Fix

,

, and

The
W
–trick, dense model, and pseudorandom majorant

Let
w:=\tfrac{1}{2}\log\log X
,
W:=\prod_{p\leq w}p
, and for each reduced residue
b\bmod W
set

\tilde{\Lambda}_{x,b}(t):=\frac{\varphi(W)}{W}\,\Lambda\bigl(W(n_{b}+t-1)+b\bigr),\qquad 1\leq t\leq N:=\Big\lfloor\frac{H}{W}\Big\rfloor,

where
n_{b}
aligns the progression
Wn+b
with the window
[x,x+H]
.
Summing in
b
and using the uniform short–interval PNT and the fact that
W
–divisible prime powers contribute

, there exists a reduced
b=b(x)
with

(3.1)

\mathbb{E}_{t\leq N}\tilde{\Lambda}_{x,b}(t)\ \geq\ c_{0}>0.

Define the (shifted) Selberg/GPY majorant

\nu_{x,b}(t):=\frac{\varphi(W)}{W}\cdot\frac{\Lambda_{R}\bigl(W(n_{b}+t-1)+b\bigr)^{2}}{\log R},\qquad\Lambda_{R}(m):=\sum_{d\mid m,\,d\leq R}\mu(d)\log\frac{R}{d},

with
R:=N^{\eta}
for a small fixed

.

Lemma 3.1

(Pseudorandomness)
.

For each fixed
k
there exists
\eta_{0}=\eta_{0}(k)>0
such that if
0<\eta\leq\eta_{0}
then
\nu_{x,b}
satisfies the linear–forms and correlation conditions of complexity
k-2
with
o(1)
errors, uniformly in the alignment parameters
x
,
b
, and

.

Sketch.

Expand moments of
\nu_{x,b}
and average over rectangular boxes in the
(n,r)
plane. The resulting sums over divisors are controlled by local congruence densities with least common multiple
\ll R^{C_{k}}=N^{o(1)}
, so the main terms factor and the error terms are

. Uniformity in the constant terms (shifts) is standard; see
[

and the streamlined proof in
[
1
]
.

4.
Relative Szemerédi and the count of
k
–APs

We now give the full proof of Theorem
1.1
.

Proof of Theorem
1.1
.

Fix
k\geq 3
and
\theta>17/30
. Let
X
be large and
x\in[X,2X]
. Set

H:=\lfloor x^{\theta}\rfloor,\qquad w:=\tfrac{1}{2}\log\log X,\qquad W:=\prod_{p\leq w}p,

so that
W=(\log X)^{1/2+o(1)}
. Put
N:=\lfloor H/W\rfloor\asymp X^{\theta}/(\log X)^{1/2+o(1)}
. Choose a small fixed
\eta=\eta(k,\theta)>0
and set
R:=N^{\eta}=X^{\eta\theta+o(1)}
.

Write
\psi(t):=\sum_{n\leq t}\Lambda(n)
and
\psi(t;q,a):=\sum_{n\leq t\atop n\equiv a\ (\mathrm{mod}\ q)}\Lambda(n)
.

Uniform short–interval PNT (Guth–Maynard).

For
\theta>17/30
one has uniformly for all
x\in[X,2X]
,

(4.1)

\sum_{x<n\leq x+H}\!\Lambda(n)=\psi(x+H)-\psi(x)=H\,(1+o(1)).

Selecting a residue class modulo
W
and aligning the window.

We have

\displaystyle\sum_{b\in(\mathbb{Z}/W\mathbb{Z})^{\times}}\big(\psi(x+H;W,b)-\psi(x;W,b)\big)

\displaystyle=\sum_{x<n\leq x+H\atop(n,W)=1}\!\Lambda(n)

\displaystyle=\big(\psi(x+H)-\psi(x)\big)\ -\ \sum_{x<n\leq x+H\atop(n,W)>1}\!\Lambda(n).

If
(n,W)>1
and
\Lambda(n)>0
, then
n=p^{m}
with
p\mid W
and
m\geq 2
(the case
m=1
is impossible for large
X
since
p\leq w\ll X<x
). Hence

\sum_{x<n\leq x+H\atop(n,W)>1}\!\Lambda(n)\ \leq\ \sum_{x<p^{m}\leq x+H\atop p\leq w,\,m\geq 2}\!\log p\ \ll\ (\log w)\cdot\frac{H}{x^{1/2}}\ =\ o(H)

uniformly for
x\in[X,2X]
. Using (
4.1
),

\sum_{b\in(\mathbb{Z}/W\mathbb{Z})^{\times}}\big(\psi(x+H;W,b)-\psi(x;W,b)\big)=H\,(1+o(1)).

By pigeonhole, there exists
b=b(x)\in(\mathbb{Z}/W\mathbb{Z})^{\times}
such that

(4.2)

\psi(x+H;W,b)-\psi(x;W,b)\ \geq\ \frac{H}{\varphi(W)}\,(1+o(1))\qquad\text{uniformly in }x.

Fix such a
b
and set

m_{0}:=\big\lfloor\tfrac{x-b}{W}\big\rfloor+1,

so that
Wm_{0}+b\in(x,x+W]
and, since
N=\lfloor H/W\rfloor
, we have

x<W(m_{0}+n-1)+b\leq x+H\qquad(1\leq n\leq N).

Weights and density.

Define for
1\leq n\leq N
the aligned weights

f_{x}(n):=\frac{\varphi(W)}{W}\cdot\frac{\Lambda(W(m_{0}+n-1)+b)}{\log R},

\nu_{x}(n):=c_{0}\,\frac{\varphi(W)}{W\,\log R}\Bigg(\sum_{d\mid(W(m_{0}+n-1)+b)\atop d\leq R}\!\mu(d)\log\frac{R}{d}\Bigg)^{\!2},

with
c_{0}>0
chosen so that
\mathbb{E}_{n\leq N}\,\nu_{x}(n)=1+o(1)
. Since
(b,W)=1
, every divisor
d\mid(W(m_{0}+n-1)+b)
satisfies
(d,W)=1
, and the standard Selberg–sieve comparison gives
0\leq f_{x}\ll\nu_{x}
uniformly.

Define the density

\delta_{x}:=\mathbb{E}_{n\leq N}f_{x}(n)=\frac{1}{N}\,\frac{\varphi(W)}{W\log R}\sum_{n=1}^{N}\Lambda\big(W(m_{0}+n-1)+b\big).

Because
(x,x+H]
contains either
N
or
N+1
terms of the progression
\{Wm+b\}
and we retained the first
N
of them, we have

\sum_{n=1}^{N}\Lambda\big(W(m_{0}+n-1)+b\big)\geq\psi(x+H;W,b)-\psi(x;W,b)-O(\log X).

Using (
4.2
) and
N\asymp H/W
gives

\displaystyle\delta_{x}

\displaystyle\geq\frac{\varphi(W)}{W\log R}\cdot\frac{1}{N}\Big(\frac{H}{\varphi(W)}(1+o(1))-O(\log X)\Big)

\displaystyle=\frac{H}{WN\log R}\,(1+o(1))\quad\text{since }\frac{\log X}{N}=o\!\left(\frac{H}{W}\right)

(4.3)

\displaystyle\geq\frac{1+o(1)}{\log R},

uniformly in
x
(using
WN\leq H<WN+W
).

Pseudorandomness of
\nu_{x}
.

Fix
t\ll_{k}1
and consider any system of affine–linear forms

L_{i}(n,r)=W\big(m_{0}+n+j_{i}r-1\big)+b\qquad(j_{i}\in\{0,1,\dots,k-1\}).

Expanding products of the inner divisor sums in
\nu_{x}
reduces moments of
\nu_{x}
to averages of the shape

\frac{1}{\#\mathcal{B}}\sum_{(n,r)\in\mathcal{B}}\prod_{i=1}^{t}\Big(\sum_{d_{i}\leq R\atop d_{i}\mid L_{i}(n,r)}\mu(d_{i})\log\tfrac{R}{d_{i}}\Big),

where
\mathcal{B}
is a rectangular box of dimensions
\asymp N\times N
(e.g.
1\leq r\leq N/(3k)
and
1\leq n\leq N-(k-1)r
). For fixed
\mathbf{d}=(d_{1},\dots,d_{t})
with
(d_{i},W)=1
, the inner average equals

\frac{\alpha_{m_{0}}(\mathbf{d})}{\operatorname{lcm}(d_{1},\dots,d_{t})}+O\!\left(\frac{\operatorname{lcm}(d_{1},\dots,d_{t})}{N}\right),

with
0\leq\alpha_{m_{0}}(\mathbf{d})\ll 1
depending only on the residues of
m_{0}
and
\{j_{i}\}
modulo
d_{i}
. Since
\operatorname{lcm}(d_{1},\dots,d_{t})\leq R^{C_{k}}
for some
C_{k}\ll_{k}1
, choosing
\eta>0
sufficiently small (depending on
k
) ensures
R^{C_{k}}=N^{o(1)}
. Summing over
\mathbf{d}
with weights
\prod_{i}\mu(d_{i})\log(R/d_{i})
yields

\mathbb{E}_{(n,r)\in\mathcal{B}}\,\prod_{i=1}^{t}\nu_{x}\big(n+j_{i}r\big)=1+o(1),\qquad\mathbb{E}_{n\leq N}\,\nu_{x}(n)=1+o(1),

uniformly in
x
,
W
,
b
, and the shift
m_{0}
. Thus
\nu_{x}
is a pseudorandom majorant of the required complexity uniformly for all
x\in[X,2X]
; compare
[
3
, §9]
and
[
1
]
.

Relative Szemerédi and a weighted count.

Applying the relative Szemerédi theorem (for the
k
–AP hypergraph system) to
f_{x}\leq\nu_{x}
on
[N]
and using (
4.3
), we obtain

(4.4)

\sum_{1\leq r\leq N/(3k)}\ \sum_{1\leq n\leq N-(k-1)r}\prod_{j=0}^{k-1}f_{x}(n+jr)\ \geq\ c_{k}\,\delta_{x}^{k}\,N^{2}+o\big(N^{2}\delta_{x}^{k}\big)\ \geq\ c^{\prime}_{k}\,\frac{N^{2}}{(\log R)^{k}}+o\!\left(\frac{N^{2}}{(\log R)^{k}}\right),

for some
c_{k},c^{\prime}_{k}>0
depending only on
k
, uniformly in
x
.

Conversion to an unweighted count of prime progressions.

Let

S_{x}:=\sum_{1\leq r\leq N/(3k)}\ \sum_{1\leq n\leq N-(k-1)r}\prod_{j=0}^{k-1}f_{x}(n+jr).

Since
f_{x}\geq 0
and
\Lambda(m)\leq\log m\leq\log(3X)
whenever
m\in(x,x+H]
, for each contributing pair
(n,r)
(i.e. all
W(m_{0}+n+jr-1)+b
are prime powers) we have

(4.5)

\prod_{j=0}^{k-1}f_{x}(n+jr)\ \leq\ \Bigg(\frac{\varphi(W)}{W\,\log R}\cdot\log(3X)\Bigg)^{\!k}.

Let
\mathcal{T}_{x}
be the set of pairs
(n,r)
for which all
W(m_{0}+n+jr-1)+b
are prime powers, and let
\mathcal{M}_{x}\subset\mathcal{T}_{x}
be those for which they are all primes. Then (
4.5
) gives

(4.6)

S_{x}\leq\Bigg(\frac{\varphi(W)}{W\,\log R}\cdot\log(3X)\Bigg)^{\!k}\#\mathcal{T}_{x}.

Write
\mathcal{T}_{x}=\mathcal{M}_{x}\sqcup\mathcal{E}_{x}
, where
\mathcal{E}_{x}
consists of those
(n,r)
with at least one prime power of exponent
\geq 2
. The number of prime powers
q=p^{m}\in(x,x+H]
with
m\geq 2
is
\ll H/x^{1/2}
. For each such
q
and each fixed
j\in\{0,\dots,k-1\}
there are
\ll N
admissible pairs
(n,r)
with
W(m_{0}+n+jr-1)+b=q
(indeed
r
ranges over
\ll N
values and then
n
is determined, with at most
O(1)
boundary losses). Hence

(4.7)

\#\mathcal{E}_{x}\ \ll_{k}\ N\cdot\frac{H}{x^{1/2}}.

Combining (
4.6
) and (
4.7
), and recalling
\#\mathcal{M}_{x}
is precisely the number of
k
–APs of primes of the form
\{W(m_{0}+n+jr-1)+b\}_{j=0}^{k-1}\subset(x,x+H]
with
r\leq N/(3k)
, we obtain

(4.8)

\#\mathcal{M}_{x}\ \geq\ \frac{S_{x}}{\big(\tfrac{\varphi(W)}{W\,\log R}\log(3X)\big)^{k}}\ -\ C_{k}\,N\frac{H}{x^{1/2}}.

By (
4.4
),
S_{x}\geq c^{\prime}_{k}\,N^{2}/(\log R)^{k}+o(N^{2}/(\log R)^{k})
. Inserting this in (
4.8
) and using
\log(3X)\asymp\log X
yields

\#\mathcal{M}_{x}\ \geq\ c^{\prime\prime}_{k}\,\frac{N^{2}}{\big((\varphi(W)/W)^{k}(\log X)^{k}\big)}\ +\ o\!\left(\frac{N^{2}}{\big((\varphi(W)/W)^{k}(\log X)^{k}\big)}\right)\ -\ C_{k}\,N\frac{H}{x^{1/2}}.

Since
N\asymp H/W
,
x\asymp X
, and
W=(\log X)^{1/2+o(1)}
, we have

N\frac{H}{x^{1/2}}\ \ll\ \frac{X^{2\theta-1/2}}{(\log X)^{1/2+o(1)}}\ =\ o\!\left(\frac{N^{2}}{\big((\varphi(W)/W)^{k}(\log X)^{k}\big)}\right)

because
\theta>1/2
and
(\varphi(W)/W)^{k}\leq 1
. Thus

\#\mathcal{M}_{x}\ \geq\ c_{k,\theta}\,\frac{N^{2}}{\big((\varphi(W)/W)^{k}(\log X)^{k}\big)}\ \geq\ c_{k,\theta}\,\frac{N^{2}}{\big((\varphi(W)/W)^{k}(\log R)^{k}\big)},

using
\log R\asymp_{\theta}\log X
for the last inequality (absorbing the constant into
c_{k,\theta}
). Finally, with
W=\prod_{p\leq\tfrac{1}{2}\log\log X}p
we have
W=(\log X)^{1/2+o(1)}
,
\varphi(W)/W=(\log\log\log X)^{-1+o(1)}
, and
N\asymp X^{\theta}/W
, so

\frac{N^{2}}{\big((\varphi(W)/W)^{k}(\log R)^{k}\big)}\asymp\frac{X^{2\theta}}{(\log X)^{k+1+o(1)}},

uniformly for all
x\in[X,2X]
. This gives the claimed uniform lower bound for the number of
k
–term arithmetic progressions of primes in
[x,x+H]
, completing the proof.
∎

5.
Almost–all
x
version

Proof of Theorem
1.2
.

Fix
k\geq 3
and
\theta\in(17/30,1)
, and set
H(y):=y^{\theta}
. For
X
large and
x\in[X,2X]
abbreviate
H:=H(x)
. We shall show that for all but
\ll X^{1-\delta+o(1)}
such
x
the interval
[x,x+H]
contains
\gg_{k,\theta}N^{2}/((\varphi(W)/W)^{k}(\log R)^{k})
distinct
k
–term APs of primes; in particular, it contains one.

Exceptional set for the short-interval PNT.

Let

E_{\theta}(X):=\bigl\{x\in[X,2X]:\,\psi(x+H)-\psi(x)\neq H(1+o(1))\bigr\}.

By the zero-density seed with exponent
A=30/13
one has

|E_{\theta}(X)|\ll X^{\mu(\theta)+o(1)},\qquad\mu(\theta)\leq\inf_{\sigma\in[1/2,1)}\min\Bigl((1-\theta)(1-\sigma)A+2\sigma-1,\ (1-\theta)(1-\sigma)A+4\sigma-3\Bigr).

Choosing
\sigma=3/4
gives
\mu(\theta)\leq\tfrac{1}{2}+\tfrac{A}{4}(1-\theta)<\tfrac{3}{4}
for
\theta>17/30
. Set
\delta:=1-\mu(\theta)>0
. Thus for all but
\ll X^{1-\delta+o(1)}
values of
x\in[X,2X]
we have

(5.1)

\sum_{n\in[x,x+H]}\!\Lambda(n)=H(1+o(1)).

Fix such a good
x
and write
I:=I(x;H)=[x,x+H]
.

W
–trick and dense model on a short interval (with reindexing).

Let
w:=\tfrac{1}{2}\log\log X
and
W:=\prod_{p\leq w}p
, so
\log W\sim w
and hence
W=(\log X)^{1/2+o(1)}
while
\varphi(W)/W\asymp 1/\log w
. For any reduced residue
b\bmod W
, the set

\mathcal{N}_{x,b}:=\{n\in\mathbb{N}:\ x\leq Wn+b\leq x+H\}

is a contiguous block of indices. Set
N:=\lfloor H/W\rfloor
(so
N\asymp H/W=X^{\theta+o(1)}\to\infty
). For each such
b
, let
n_{b}:=\min\{n:\ Wn+b\geq x\}
. Reindex the block
\mathcal{N}_{x,b}
onto
[N]:=\{1,\dots,N\}
by
t\mapsto n_{b}+t-1
, and define the
W
–tricked (normalized) von Mangoldt weight on
[N]
by

(5.2)

\tilde{\Lambda}_{x,b}(t):=\frac{\varphi(W)}{W}\,\Lambda\bigl(W(n_{b}+t-1)+b\bigr)\qquad(1\leq t\leq N).

Note that
W(n_{b}+t-1)+b\in I
for every
1\leq t\leq N
because
W(n_{b}+N-1)+b\leq(x+W-1)+WN-W\leq x+H-1
.

Summing (
5.2
) over reduced
b\bmod W
, we cover all
m\in I
with
(m,W)=1
, except that for those
b
with
|\mathcal{N}_{x,b}|=N+1
we omit the last element of the block. Hence

(5.3)

\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}(b,W)=1\end{subarray}}\ \sum_{t\leq N}\tilde{\Lambda}_{x,b}(t)\ \geq\ \frac{\varphi(W)}{W}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in I\\
(m,W)=1\end{subarray}}\!\Lambda(m)\ -\ O\!\left(\frac{\varphi(W)^{2}}{W}\log X\right).

Because
\Lambda
is supported on prime powers and
(m,W)>1
forces the base prime
\leq w
, the number of such
m\in I
is
\ll H^{1/2}
. Using (
5.1
) we get

\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}(b,W)=1\end{subarray}}\ \sum_{t\leq N}\tilde{\Lambda}_{x,b}(t)\ \geq\ \Big(1+o(1)\Big)\,H\cdot\frac{\varphi(W)}{W},

since the errors
\ll(\varphi(W)/W)H^{1/2}\log X+\varphi(W)^{2}\log X/W
are
o\big(H\varphi(W)/W\big)
. By pigeonhole there exists
b=b(x)
with
\gcd(b,W)=1
such that

(5.4)

\sum_{t\leq N}\tilde{\Lambda}_{x,b}(t)\ \geq\ \Big(1-o(1)\Big)\,\frac{H}{W}.

Dividing (
5.4
) by
N\asymp H/W
yields

(5.5)

\mathbb{E}_{t\leq N}\tilde{\Lambda}_{x,b}(t)\geq 1-o(1).

To excise prime powers, set

Since the number of prime powers in

Combining with (

\mathbb{E}_{t\leq N}\tilde{\Lambda}^{\mathrm{prime}}_{x,b}(t)\geq c_{0}>0\qquad(X\ \text{large}).

Shifted pseudorandom majorant, admissible truncation, and relative Szemerédi.

Let

. By Green–Tao (Ann. of Math. 167 (2008), §§6–10; in particular §9 and Theorem 3.18), there exists

errors, uniformly in the constant terms of the forms. Fix any

Write the Selberg/GPY truncated divisor sum

\Lambda_{R}(m):=\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}d\mid m\\
d\leq R\end{subarray}}\mu(d)\,\log\frac{R}{d}.

For our reindexed block, define the shifted Green–Tao majorant on
[N]
by

\nu_{x,b}(t):=\frac{\varphi(W)}{W}\,\frac{\Lambda_{R}\bigl(W(n_{b}+t-1)+b\bigr)^{2}}{\log R}\qquad(1\leq t\leq N).

This is the standard GT majorant applied to the integers

and in the translation

Since

so every prime

\Lambda_{R}(m)=\log R\quad\text{and}\quad\nu_{x,b}(t)=\frac{\varphi(W)}{W}\,\log R\qquad\text{whenever }m=W(n_{b}+t-1)+b\in\mathbb{P}.

Define the truncated prime weight

Since

Moreover, using (

for

Appendix A
A short–interval BDH mean square (uniform in
x
)

We record the following standard large–sieve consequence; its proof follows the classical Barban–Davenport–Halberstam route.

Lemma A.1
.

Fix
\theta\in(0,1)
and
A>0
. There exists
B=B(\theta,A)>0
such that for all sufficiently large
X
and all
x\in[X,2X]
, with
H:=\lfloor x^{\theta}\rfloor
,

\sum_{q\leq X^{1/2}(\log X)^{-B}}\ \sum_{a\,(\mathrm{mod}\ q)}\Big|\theta(x+H;q,a)-\theta(x;q,a)-\tfrac{H}{\varphi(q)}\Big|^{2}\ \ll_{A}\ H\,X\,(\log X)^{1-A},

uniformly in
x
.

Proof.

Fix
\theta\in(0,1)
and
A>0
. For
x\in[X,2X]
set

H:=H(x):=\lfloor x^{\theta}\rfloor,\qquad Q:=X^{1/2}(\log X)^{-B},

with
B=B(\theta,A)>0
to be chosen later. For
(a,q)=1
write

\theta(y;q,a):=\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}p\leq y\\
p\equiv a\,(\bmod q)\end{subarray}}\!\log p,\qquad\psi(y;q,a):=\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}n\leq y\\
n\equiv a\,(\bmod q)\end{subarray}}\!\Lambda(n).

Denote

\mathcal{S}(x):=\sum_{q\leq Q}\ \sum_{a\,(\bmod q)}\Big|\theta(x+H;q,a)-\theta(x;q,a)-\tfrac{H}{\varphi(q)}\Big|^{2}.

We split
\mathcal{S}(x)
into coprime and non-coprime residue classes:

\mathcal{S}(x)=\mathcal{S}^{\!*}(x)+\mathcal{S}^{(0)}(x),\qquad\mathcal{S}^{\!*}(x):=\sum_{q\leq Q}\ \sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}a\,(\bmod q)\\
(a,q)=1\end{subarray}}\Big|\theta(x+H;q,a)-\theta(x;q,a)-\tfrac{H}{\varphi(q)}\Big|^{2}.

For the non-coprime classes, if
(a,q)>1
and
p\equiv a\pmod{q}
is prime then
p\mid q
. Since
q\leq Q\leq X^{1/2}(\log X)^{-B}<x\leq x+H
, there is no such
p\in[x,x+H]
, hence

\theta(x+H;q,a)-\theta(x;q,a)=0.

Therefore

\mathcal{S}^{(0)}(x)=\sum_{q\leq Q}\Big(q-\varphi(q)\Big)\Big(\tfrac{H}{\varphi(q)}\Big)^{2}\leq H^{2}\sum_{q\leq Q}\frac{q}{\varphi(q)^{2}}\ll H^{2}(\log Q)(\log\log Q)^{2}\ll H^{2}(\log X)(\log\log X)^{2}.

Since
H/X=X^{\theta-1}\to 0
, for large
X
this implies

\mathcal{S}^{(0)}(x)\leq\tfrac{1}{4}\,HX(\log X)^{1-A}.

It remains to bound
\mathcal{S}^{\!*}(x)
. For
(a,q)=1
define

\displaystyle\Psi_{q,a}(x)

\displaystyle:=\sum_{x<n\leq x+H}\!\Lambda(n)\,1_{n\equiv a\,(\bmod q)}-\frac{H}{\varphi(q)},

\displaystyle\mathcal{P}_{q,a}(x)

\displaystyle:=\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}x<p^{k}\leq x+H\\
k\geq 2\\
p^{k}\equiv a\,(\bmod q)\end{subarray}}\!\log p.

Since
\psi=\theta+
(higher prime powers), for each
(a,q)=1
we have the exact identity

\theta(x+H;q,a)-\theta(x;q,a)-\frac{H}{\varphi(q)}=\Psi_{q,a}(x)-\mathcal{P}_{q,a}(x).

Hence, by
|u-v|^{2}\leq 2(|u|^{2}+|v|^{2})
,

\mathcal{S}^{\!*}(x)\leq 2\,\mathcal{S}_{\psi}(x)+2\,\mathcal{S}_{\rm pp}^{\!*}(x),

where

\displaystyle\mathcal{S}_{\psi}(x)

\displaystyle:=\sum_{q\leq Q}\ \sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}a\,(\bmod q)\\
(a,q)=1\end{subarray}}|\Psi_{q,a}(x)|^{2},

\displaystyle\mathcal{S}_{\rm pp}^{\!*}(x)

\displaystyle:=\sum_{q\leq Q}\ \sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}a\,(\bmod q)\\
(a,q)=1\end{subarray}}|\mathcal{P}_{q,a}(x)|^{2}.

We now bound
\mathcal{S}_{\psi}
and
\mathcal{S}_{\rm pp}^{\!*}
.

1) Bounding
\mathcal{S}_{\psi}(x)
.

Using orthogonality on
(\mathbb{Z}/q\mathbb{Z})^{\times}
,

\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}a\,(\bmod q)\\
(a,q)=1\end{subarray}}|\Psi_{q,a}(x)|^{2}=\frac{1}{\varphi(q)}\sum_{\chi\,(\bmod q)}\Big|\sum_{x<n\leq x+H}\!\Lambda(n)\chi(n)-H\,1_{\chi=\chi_{0}}\Big|^{2}.

Split the character sum into non-principal and principal characters.

(a) Non-principal characters.

Put
a_{n}:=\Lambda(n)\,1_{(x,\,x+H]}(n)
and
N:=H
. Then

\mathcal{S}_{\psi,\mathrm{npr}}(x)\leq\sum_{q\leq Q}\frac{1}{\varphi(q)}\sum_{\chi\,(\bmod q)}\Big|\sum_{n}a_{n}\chi(n)\Big|^{2}.

We invoke the multiplicative large sieve in its standard primitive, weighted form together with the conductor-lifting to all characters (Montgomery–Vaughan, MNT I, Thm. 7.12): for any complex sequence
(a_{n})
supported on an interval of length
N
,

\sum_{q\leq Q}\frac{1}{\varphi(q)}\sum_{\chi\,(\bmod q)}\Big|\sum_{n}a_{n}\chi(n)\Big|^{2}\ \ll\ (Q^{2}+N)\,(\log Q)\,\sum_{n}|a_{n}|^{2}.

Using
\sum_{x<n\leq x+H}\!\Lambda(n)^{2}\ll H\log X
uniformly in
x
, we obtain

\mathcal{S}_{\psi,\mathrm{npr}}(x)\ \ll\ (Q^{2}+H)\,H\,(\log X)(\log Q).

Choosing
B=B(\theta,A)
sufficiently large so that
Q^{2}H(\log X)(\log Q)\leq\tfrac{1}{16}HX(\log X)^{1-A}
, and noting that
H^{2}(\log X)(\log Q)\leq\tfrac{1}{16}HX(\log X)^{1-A}
for large
X
(since
H/X\to 0
), we deduce

(A.1)

\mathcal{S}_{\psi,\mathrm{npr}}(x)\leq\tfrac{1}{8}\,HX(\log X)^{1-A}.

(b) Principal characters.

For
\chi_{0}\,(\bmod q)
,

\displaystyle\sum_{x<n\leq x+H}\!\Lambda(n)\chi_{0}(n)-H

\displaystyle=\sum_{x<n\leq x+H}\!\Lambda(n)1_{(n,q)=1}-H

\displaystyle=\underbrace{(\psi(x+H)-\psi(x)-H)}_{=:\,\Delta_{\psi}(x)}\ -\ \sum_{p\mid q}A_{p}(x),

where
A_{p}(x):=\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}x<p^{k}\leq x+H\\
k\geq 2\end{subarray}}\!\log p\geq 0
. Hence, by
|u-v|^{2}\leq 2(|u|^{2}+|v|^{2})
and
\Sigma(Q):=\sum_{q\leq Q}\varphi(q)^{-1}\ll\log Q
,

\displaystyle\sum_{q\leq Q}\frac{1}{\varphi(q)}\Big|\sum_{x<n\leq x+H}\!\Lambda(n)\chi_{0}(n)-H\Big|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq 2\Sigma(Q)|\Delta_{\psi}(x)|^{2}\ +\ 2\sum_{q\leq Q}\frac{1}{\varphi(q)}\Big|\sum_{p\mid q}A_{p}(x)\Big|^{2}.

For the first term,
|\Delta_{\psi}(x)|\leq\sum_{x<n\leq x+H}\!\Lambda(n)+H\ll H\log X
gives

2\Sigma(Q)|\Delta_{\psi}(x)|^{2}\ll H^{2}(\log X)^{2}\log Q\leq\tfrac{1}{8}\,HX(\log X)^{1-A}

for all sufficiently large
X
. For the second term, since
A_{p}(x)\geq 0
we have uniformly in
q
,

\Big|\sum_{p\mid q}A_{p}(x)\Big|\leq\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}x<p^{k}\leq x+H\\
k\geq 2\end{subarray}}\!\log p\,=:R(x).

Estimating prime powers in short intervals: for
k=2
,
\sum_{x<p^{2}\leq x+H}\!\log p\ \ll\ \big(\tfrac{H}{\sqrt{x}}+1\big)\log X
, and for
k\geq 3

(x+H)^{1/k}-x^{1/k}\ll Hx^{1/k-1}
, the same bound holds. Hence
R(x)\ll\big(\tfrac{H}{\sqrt{x}}+1\big)\log X
, and

\sum_{q\leq Q}\frac{1}{\varphi(q)}\Big|\sum_{p\mid q}A_{p}(x)\Big|^{2}\ \ll\ \log Q\,\Big(\tfrac{H}{\sqrt{x}}+1\Big)^{2}(\log X)^{2}\ =\ o\!\big(HX(\log X)^{1-A}\big),

uniformly for
x\in[X,2X]
. Consequently,

(A.2)

\mathcal{S}_{\psi,\mathrm{pr}}(x)\leq\tfrac{1}{8}\,HX(\log X)^{1-A}.

Combining (
A.1
) and (
A.2
) gives

(A.3)

\mathcal{S}_{\psi}(x)\leq\tfrac{1}{4}\,HX(\log X)^{1-A}.

2) Bounding the prime-power term
\mathcal{S}_{\rm pp}^{\!*}(x)
.

Enlarging to all residue classes can only increase the sum, hence

\displaystyle\mathcal{S}_{\rm pp}^{\!*}(x)

\displaystyle\leq\sum_{q\leq Q}\sum_{a\,(\bmod q)}\Big|\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}x<p^{k}\leq x+H\\
k\geq 2\\
p^{k}\equiv a\,(\bmod q)\end{subarray}}\!\log p\Big|^{2}

\displaystyle=\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}x<p^{k}\leq x+H\\
k\geq 2\end{subarray}}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}x<p^{\ell}\leq x+H\\
\ell\geq 2\end{subarray}}(\log p)(\log p^{\prime})\sum_{q\leq Q}1_{p^{k}\equiv p^{\ell}\,(\bmod q)}.

Splitting the diagonal and off-diagonal pairs
p^{k}=p^{\ell}
,
p^{k}\neq p^{\ell}
, and writing
\tau_{Q}(h):=|\{q\leq Q:q\mid h\}|
, we have

\mathcal{S}_{\rm pp}^{\!*}(x)\leq Q\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}x<p^{k}\leq x+H\\
k\geq 2\end{subarray}}(\log p)^{2}\ +\ \sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}x<p^{k},\,p^{\ell}\leq x+H\\
k,\ell\geq 2\\
p^{k}\neq p^{\ell}\end{subarray}}(\log p)(\log p^{\prime})\,\tau_{Q}(|p^{k}-p^{\ell}|).

For the diagonal, using
(x+H)^{1/k}-x^{1/k}\ll Hx^{1/k-1}
and summing over
k\geq 2
,

\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}x<p^{k}\leq x+H\\
k\geq 2\end{subarray}}(\log p)^{2}\ \ll\ \Big(\tfrac{H}{\sqrt{x}}+1\Big)(\log X)^{2}.

Thus the diagonal contribution is
\ll Q\big(\tfrac{H}{\sqrt{X}}+1\big)(\log X)^{2}
. For the off-diagonal,
\tau_{Q}(h)\leq d(h)\ll h^{o(1)}\ll X^{o(1)}
and

\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}x<p^{k}\leq x+H\\
k\geq 2\end{subarray}}\!\log p\ \ll\ \Big(\tfrac{H}{\sqrt{x}}+1\Big)\log X,

so the off-diagonal is
\ll X^{o(1)}\big(\tfrac{H^{2}}{X}+1\big)(\log X)^{2}
. Therefore, for large
X
,

(A.4)

\mathcal{S}_{\rm pp}^{\!*}(x)\ll Q\frac{H}{\sqrt{X}}(\log X)^{2}+Q(\log X)^{2}+X^{o(1)}\frac{H^{2}}{X}(\log X)^{2}\leq\tfrac{1}{4}\,HX(\log X)^{1-A}.

(Indeed, the three terms are respectively
\ll H(\log X)^{2-B}
,
\ll\sqrt{X}(\log X)^{2-B}
, and
\ll H^{2}X^{-1+o(1)}(\log X)^{2}
, each
o\big(HX(\log X)^{1-A}\big)
as
X\to\infty
.)

3) Conclusion.

From
\mathcal{S}^{\!*}(x)\leq 2\mathcal{S}_{\psi}(x)+2\mathcal{S}_{\rm pp}^{\!*}(x)
together with (
A.3
) and (
A.4
), and adding the non-coprime contribution, we obtain for all sufficiently large
X
(once
B=B(\theta,A)
is fixed) and all
x\in[X,2X]
,

\sum_{q\leq X^{1/2}(\log X)^{-B}}\ \sum_{a\,(\bmod q)}\Big|\theta(x+H(x);q,a)-\theta(x;q,a)-\tfrac{H(x)}{\varphi(q)}\Big|^{2}\ \ll_{A}\ H(x)X(\log X)^{1-A}.

This completes the proof.
∎

Appendix B: No uniform-in-
Q
lower bound at the conjectural variance size

We note a simple observation ruling out a uniform (in
Q
) lower bound at the conjectural BDH variance size in short intervals.

Proposition B.1
.

Fix
\theta\in(0,1)
and for
x\in[X,2X]
set
H(x)=\lfloor x^{\theta}\rfloor
. There does not exist
B_{1}=B_{1}(\theta)>0
such that, for all sufficiently large
X
, all
x\in[X,2X]
, and all
Q\leq Q(X,B_{1}):=X^{1/2}(\log X)^{-B_{1}}
,

\sum_{q\leq Q}\ \sum_{a\ (\mathrm{mod}\ q)}\Big|\,\theta(x+H(x);q,a)-\theta(x;q,a)-\tfrac{H(x)}{\varphi(q)}\,\Big|^{2}\ \gg_{\theta}\ H(x)\,X\,\log\!\Big(\tfrac{X}{H(x)}\Big).

In particular, a uniform-in-
Q
lower bound of the conjectured variance size cannot hold.

Proof.

Fix
\theta\in(0,1)
and set
H(x)=\lfloor x^{\theta}\rfloor
. Put

E(x;q,a):=\theta(x+H(x);q,a)-\theta(x;q,a)-\frac{H(x)}{\varphi(q)},\qquad S(x;Q):=\sum_{q\leq Q}\ \sum_{a\ (\mathrm{mod}\ q)}|E(x;q,a)|^{2},

and
Q(X,B):=X^{1/2}(\log X)^{-B}
.

By Lemma
A.1
(taking
A=1
), there exists
B_{0}=B_{0}(\theta)>0
such that, for all sufficiently large
X
and all
x\in[X,2X]
,

S\big(x;Q(X,B_{0})\big)\ \ll_{\theta}\ H(x)\,X.

Since
S(x;Q)
is nondecreasing in
Q
, for every
Q\leq Q(X,B_{0})
we also have

S(x;Q)\ \leq\ S\big(x;Q(X,B_{0})\big)\ \ll_{\theta}\ H(x)\,X.

Moreover, because
x\in[X,2X]
and
H(x)=\lfloor x^{\theta}\rfloor
with
\theta\in(0,1)
,

\log\!\Big(\tfrac{X}{H(x)}\Big)=\log(X/x^{\theta})+O(1)\asymp\ \log X.

Assume for contradiction that there exists
B_{1}=B_{1}(\theta)>0
such that, for all sufficiently large
X
, all
x\in[X,2X]
, and all
Q\leq Q(X,B_{1})
,

S(x;Q)\ \gg_{\theta}\ H(x)\,X\,\log\!\Big(\tfrac{X}{H(x)}\Big).

Fix such an
X
and
x
, and set
Q_{*}:=\min\{Q(X,B_{0}),\,Q(X,B_{1})\}
. Then
Q_{*}\leq Q(X,B_{1})
, so by the assumed uniform lower bound,

S(x;Q_{*})\ \gg_{\theta}\ H(x)\,X\,\log\!\Big(\tfrac{X}{H(x)}\Big),

whereas
Q_{*}\leq Q(X,B_{0})
, so by the variance bound and monotonicity,

S(x;Q_{*})\ \ll_{\theta}\ H(x)\,X.

Using
\log(X/H(x))\asymp\log X
and dividing the two bounds yields

\frac{S(x;Q_{*})}{H(x)\,X\,\log\!\big(\tfrac{X}{H(x)}\big)}\ \ll_{\theta}\ (\log X)^{-1}\ \to\ 0\qquad(X\to\infty),

which contradicts the asserted lower bound. Therefore no such
B_{1}
exists.
∎

Appendix C: A simple Chebotarev obstruction below
\theta=\tfrac{1}{2}

The following elementary lower bound (by the “empty class” trick) shows that average-in-
q
error terms of size
H/(\log X)^{A+1}
cannot hold for almost all
x
when
\theta<\tfrac{1}{2}
.

Proposition B.2
.

Fix a finite Galois extension
L/\mathbb{Q}
with Galois group
G
and a conjugacy class
\mathcal{C}\subset G
, and let
\delta_{\mathcal{C}}>0
be its Chebotarev density. For any
\theta\in(0,1/2)
there exists a constant
c_{\theta,\mathcal{C}}>0
such that for every
B\geq 0
and all sufficiently large
X
, uniformly for all
x\in[X,2X]
, with
H(x):=\lfloor x^{\theta}\rfloor
and
Q:=X^{1/2}(\log X)^{-B}
, one has

\sum_{q\leq Q}\max_{(a,q)=1}\Big|\#\{x<p\leq x+H(x):\ p\equiv a\ (\mathrm{mod}\ q),\ \mathrm{Frob}_{p}\in\mathcal{C}\}\ -\ \frac{\delta_{\mathcal{C}}}{\varphi(q)}\cdot\frac{H(x)}{\log X}\Big|\ \geq\ c_{\theta,\mathcal{C}}\,\frac{H(x)}{\log X}.

In particular, no bound of size
H(x)/(\log X)^{A+1}
can hold for almost all
x
when
\theta\in(0,1/2)
.

Proof.

Fix
\theta\in(0,1/2)
and
B\geq 0
, and set
H:=\lfloor x^{\theta}\rfloor
and
Q:=X^{1/2}(\log X)^{-B}
. Let

S(x;Q,H):=\sum_{q\leq Q}\max_{(a,q)=1}\Big|\#\{x<p\leq x+H:p\equiv a\ (\mathrm{mod}\ q),\ \mathrm{Frob}_{p}\in\mathcal{C}\}-\frac{\delta_{\mathcal{C}}}{\varphi(q)}\cdot\frac{H}{\log X}\Big|.

We will show that for all sufficiently large
X
(so that
H+1\leq Q
, which holds since
\theta<1/2
), uniformly for
x\in[X,2X]
,

S(x;Q,H)\ \geq\ c_{\theta,\mathcal{C}}\,\frac{H}{\log X}

with, say,
c_{\theta,\mathcal{C}}:=\tfrac{\delta_{\mathcal{C}}}{4}\log\tfrac{1}{2\theta}
. Suppose, for contradiction, that there exist arbitrarily large
X
and some
x\in[X,2X]
with

S(x;Q,H)\ <\ c_{\theta,\mathcal{C}}\,\frac{H}{\log X}.

Let
M:=\#\{x<p\leq x+H:\mathrm{Frob}_{p}\in\mathcal{C}\}
; then
0\leq M\leq H
. For any prime modulus
q
with
H+1<q\leq Q
, we have
q\leq Q\leq X^{1/2}<p
for all primes
p\in(x,x+H]
, so
(p,q)=1
and each such
p
lies in some reduced residue class modulo
q
. Among the
\varphi(q)=q-1
reduced classes, at most
M\leq H<q-1
are occupied by these primes, so there exists a reduced class
a_{q}\pmod{q}
containing none of them. Hence

\#\{x<p\leq x+H:p\equiv a_{q}\ (\mathrm{mod}\ q),\ \mathrm{Frob}_{p}\in\mathcal{C}\}=0,

and therefore

\max_{(a,q)=1}\Big|\#\{x<p\leq x+H:p\equiv a\ (\mathrm{mod}\ q),\ \mathrm{Frob}_{p}\in\mathcal{C}\}-\frac{\delta_{\mathcal{C}}}{\varphi(q)}\cdot\frac{H}{\log X}\Big|\ \geq\ \frac{\delta_{\mathcal{C}}}{\varphi(q)}\cdot\frac{H}{\log X}.

Summing this over primes
q
with
H+1<q\leq Q
and using
1/(q-1)\geq 1/(2q)
for
q\geq 3
, we obtain

S(x;Q,H)\ \geq\ \frac{\delta_{\mathcal{C}}H}{2\log X}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}H+1<q\leq Q\\
q\ \text{prime}\end{subarray}}\frac{1}{q}.

By Mertens’ theorem for primes, uniformly for
x\in[X,2X]
,

\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}H+1<q\leq Q\\
q\ \text{prime}\end{subarray}}\frac{1}{q}=\log\log Q-\log\log(H+1)+o(1)=\log\Big(\frac{1}{2\theta}\Big)+o(1),\quad X\to\infty.

Hence, for all sufficiently large
X
,

S(x;Q,H)\ \geq\ \frac{\delta_{\mathcal{C}}H}{2\log X}\cdot\frac{1}{2}\log\Big(\frac{1}{2\theta}\Big)=c_{\theta,\mathcal{C}}\,\frac{H}{\log X},

contradicting the assumption. Therefore the stated lower bound holds uniformly for all
x\in[X,2X]
once
X
is large enough.
∎
