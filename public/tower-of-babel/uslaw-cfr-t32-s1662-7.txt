32 C.F.R. § 1662.7 — Where to submit a FOIA request.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1662.7
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1662.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1662 (Freedom Of Information Act (Foia) Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Submission of requests. Requesters must submit FOIA requests in writing to the Agency through one of the following options:
(a) Online FOIA portal: link available from the Agency's www.sss.gov/foia website.
(b) Email: FOIA@sss.gov.
(c) Mail: SSS, ATTN: Freedom of Information Act Officer, 1501 Wilson Boulevard, Suite 700, Arlington, VA 22209.
