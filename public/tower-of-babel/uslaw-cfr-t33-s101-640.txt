33 C.F.R. § 101.640 — Records and documentation.
Citation: us:cfr:33:101.640
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-101.640
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 101 (Maritime Security: General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All records, reports, and other documents mentioned in this subpart must be created and maintained in accordance with 33 CFR 104.235 for U.S.-flagged vessels, 105.225 for facilities, and 106.230 for OCS facilities. At a minimum, the records must be created for the following activities: training, drills, exercises, cybersecurity threats, reportable cyber incidents, and audits of the Cybersecurity Plan.
