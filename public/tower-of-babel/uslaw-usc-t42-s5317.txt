42 U.S.C. § 5317 — Liquidation of superseded or inactive programs
Citation: us:usc:42:5317
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section5317
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 69 (Community Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to transfer the assets and liabilities of any program which is superseded or inactive by reason of this chapter to the revolving fund for liquidating programs established pursuant to title II of the Independent Offices Appropriation Act, 1955 (Public Law 83–428; 68 Stat. 272, 295) [12 U.S.C. 1701g–5].
