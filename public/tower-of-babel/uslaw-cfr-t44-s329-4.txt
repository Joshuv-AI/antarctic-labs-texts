44 C.F.R. § 329.4 — Implementation.
Citation: us:cfr:44:329.4
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-329.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 329 (Use Of Priorities And Allocation Authority For Federal Supply Classification (Fsc) Common Use Items (Dmo-12)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Departments and agencies involved with this program shall issue implementing instructions and directives no later than 30 work days from the effective date of this order. Copies of such instructions, directives, and related documents shall be furnished to the General Services Administration (AP) on a routine basis as issued.
