21 C.F.R. § 573.1020 — Yellow prussiate of soda.
Citation: us:cfr:21:573.1020
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-573.1020
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 573 (Food Additives Permitted In Feed And Drinking Water Of Animals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yellow prussiate of soda (sodium ferrocyanide decahydrate: Na4Fe(Cn)6°-10H2O) may be safely used as an anticaking agent in salt for animal consumption at a level not to exceed 13 parts per million. The additive contains a minimum of 99.0 percent by weight of sodium ferrocyanide decahydrate.
