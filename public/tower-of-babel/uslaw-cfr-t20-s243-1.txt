20 C.F.R. § 243.1 — Prohibition against garnishment.
Citation: us:cfr:20:243.1
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-243.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 243 (Transfer, Assignment, Or Waiver Of Payments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as hereinafter provided in this part, no benefits paid under the Railroad Retirement Act are assignable or subject to any tax or to garnishment, attachment, or other legal process (including any order issued by any court in connection with a bankruptcy proceeding), nor shall any payment be anticipated.
