40 C.F.R. § 180.1089 — Poly-N-acetyl-D-glucosamine; exemption from the requirement of tolerance.
Citation: us:cfr:40:180.1089
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-180.1089
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 180 (Tolerances And Exemptions For Pesticide Chemical Residues In Food). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An exemption from the requirement of a tolerance is established for residues of the biochemical nematicide poly-N-acetyl-D-glucosamine on a variety of agricultural crops.
