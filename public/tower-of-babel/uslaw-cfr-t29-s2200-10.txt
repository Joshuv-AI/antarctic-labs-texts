29 C.F.R. § 2200.10 — Severance.
Citation: us:cfr:29:2200.10
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Review Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-2200.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 2200 (Rules Of Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon its own motion, or upon motion of any party or intervenor conforming to § 2200.40, where a showing of good cause has been made by the party or intervenor, the Commission or the Judge may order any proceeding severed with respect to some or all claims or parties.
