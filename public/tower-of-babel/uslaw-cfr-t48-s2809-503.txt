48 C.F.R. § 2809.503 — 2809.503 Waiver.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2809.503
Creator: Chapter 28—Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2809.503
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2809 (Contractor Qualifications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The HCA is the agency head for the purpose of waiving any general rule or procedure prescribed in FAR subpart 9.5. As prescribed in FAR 9.503, the authority delegated to the HCA to waive any general rule or procedure prescribed in FAR subpart 9.5 may not be delegated below the level of the HCA.
