46 C.F.R. § 58.01-50 — -50 Machinery space, noise.
Citation: us:cfr:46:58.01-50
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-58.01-50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 58 (Main And Auxiliary Machinery And Related Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each machinery space must be designed to minimize the exposure of personnel to noise in accordance with IMO Resolution MSC.337(91) (incorporated by reference, see § 58.03-1).
