33 C.F.R. § 162.150 — Maumee Bay and River, Ohio.
Citation: us:cfr:33:162.150
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-162.150
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 162 (Inland Waterways Navigation Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In Maumee Bay (lakeward of Maumee River Lighted Buoy 49(L/L No. 770)), no vessel greater than 100 feet in length may exceed 12 miles per hour.
(b) In Maumee River (inward of Maumee River Lighted Buoy 49(L/L No. 770)):
(1) No vessel greater than 40 feet in length may exceed 6 miles per hour.
(2) No vessel greater than 100 feet in length (including tug and tow combinations) may overtake another vessel.
