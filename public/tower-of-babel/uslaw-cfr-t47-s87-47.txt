47 C.F.R. § 87.47 — Application for a portable aircraft station license.
Citation: us:cfr:47:87.47
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-87.47
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 87 (Aviation Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person may apply for a portable aircraft radio station license if the need exists to operate the same station on more than one U.S. aircraft.
