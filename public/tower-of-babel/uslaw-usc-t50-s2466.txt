50 U.S.C. § 2466 — Use of funds for the purchase of options to purchase real property
Citation: us:usc:50:2466
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section2466
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 41 (National Nuclear Security Administration); Subchapter V (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Authority
Subject to the limitation in subsection (b), funds authorized to be appropriated for the Administration for the purchase of real property may be expended to purchase options for the purchase of real property.
(b) Limitation on price of options
The price of any option purchased pursuant to subsection (a) may not exceed the minor construction threshold (as defined in section 2741 of this title).
(c) Notice
Not later than 14 days after the date an option is purchased pursuant to subsection (a), the Administrator shall submit to the congressional defense committees—
(1)
a notification of such purchase; and
(2)
a summary of the rationale for such purchase.
