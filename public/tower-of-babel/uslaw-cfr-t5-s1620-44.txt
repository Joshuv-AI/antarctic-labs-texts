5 C.F.R. § 1620.44 — Restoring forfeited agency automatic (1%) contributions.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1620.44
Creator: Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1620.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1620 (Expanded And Continuing Eligibility). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an employee's agency automatic (1%) contributions were forfeited because the employee was not vested when he or she separated to perform military service, the employee must notify the employing agency that a forfeiture occurred. The employing agency will follow the procedure described in § 1620.46(e) to have those funds restored.
