16 U.S.C. § 917c — Negotiations with Mexico and Canada
Citation: us:usc:16:917c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section917c
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 14A (Whale Conservation And Protection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Commerce, through the Secretary of State, shall immediately initiate negotiations for the purpose of developing appropriate bilateral agreements with Mexico and Canada for the protection and conservation of whales.
