25 C.F.R. § 18.109 — How will a tribe be notified of the disapproval of a tribal probate code?
Citation: us:cfr:25:18.109
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-18.109
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 18 (Tribal Probate Codes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If we disapprove a tribal probate code, we must provide the tribe with a written notification of the disapproval that includes an explanation of the reasons for the disapproval.
