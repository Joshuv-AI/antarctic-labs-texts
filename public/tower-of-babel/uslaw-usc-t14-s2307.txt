14 U.S.C. § 2307 — Retirement of enlisted members: increase in retired pay
Citation: us:usc:14:2307
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2307
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 23 (Personnel; Enlisted). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An enlisted member voluntarily or involuntarily retired after twenty years of service who was cited for extraordinary heroism in the line of duty shall be entitled to an increase in retired pay. The retired pay shall be increased by 10 percent of—
(1)
the active-duty pay and permanent additions thereto of the grade or rating with which retired when the member’s retired pay is computed under section 2504(a) of this title; or
(2)
the member’s retired pay base under section 1407 of title 10, when a member’s retired pay is computed under section 2504(b) of this title.
