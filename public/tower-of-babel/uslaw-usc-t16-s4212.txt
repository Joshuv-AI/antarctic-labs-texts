16 U.S.C. § 4212 — Acceptance and use of donations
Citation: us:usc:16:4212
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section4212
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 62 (African Elephant Conservation); Subchapter I (African Elephant Conservation Assistance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may accept and use donations of funds to provide assistance under this subchapter. Amounts received by the Secretary in the form of such donations shall be transferred by the Secretary to the Secretary of the Treasury for deposit into the Fund.
