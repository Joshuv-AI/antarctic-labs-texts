6 C.F.R. § 25.3 — Delegation.
Citation: us:cfr:6:25.3
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security, Office Of The Secretary
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-6/section-25.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 6 (Domestic Security), Part 25 (Regulations To Support Anti-Terrorism By Fostering Effective Technologies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All of the Secretary's responsibilities, powers, and functions under the SAFETY Act, except the authority to declare that an act is an Act of Terrorism for purposes of section 865(2) of the SAFETY Act, may be exercised by the Under Secretary for Science and Technology of the Department of Homeland Security or the Under Secretary's designees.
