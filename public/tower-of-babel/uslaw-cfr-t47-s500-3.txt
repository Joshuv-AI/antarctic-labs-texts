47 C.F.R. § 500.3 — NTIA duty to review FirstNet proposed fees.
Citation: us:cfr:47:500.3
Creator: The First Responder Network Authority
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-500.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 500 (Review And Approval Of Fees Proposed By The First Responder Network Authority (Firstnet)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As required under 47 U.S.C. 1428(c), NTIA shall exclusively review fees, which must be proposed by FirstNet in writing, through NTIA's review and approval process conducted on an annual basis.
