31 C.F.R. § 306.16 — Exchanges of registered securities.
Citation: us:cfr:31:306.16
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-306.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 306 (General Regulations Governing U.S. Securities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No assignments will be required for:
(a) Authorized denominational exchanges of registered securities for like securities in the same names and forms of registration and
(b) Redemption-exchanges, or prefundings, or advance refundings in the same names and forms as appear in the registration or assignments of the securities surrendered.
