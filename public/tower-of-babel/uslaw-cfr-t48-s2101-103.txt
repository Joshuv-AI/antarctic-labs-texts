48 C.F.R. § 2101.103 — 2101.103 Applicability.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2101 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The FAR is generally applicable to contracts negotiated in the FEGLI Program pursuant to 5 U.S.C. chapter 87. The LIFAR implements and supplements the FAR where necessary to identify basic and significant acquisition policies unique to the FEGLI Program.
