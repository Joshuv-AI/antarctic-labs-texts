10 C.F.R. § 602.1 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:10:602.1
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-602.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 602 (Epidemiology And Other Health Studies Financial Assistance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth the policies and procedures applicable to the award and administration of grants and cooperative agreements by DOE (through the Office of Environment, Health Safety and Security or any office to which its functions are subsequently redelegated) for health related research, education/training, conferences, communication, and related activities.
