36 C.F.R. § 3.3 — Am I required to obtain a permit to operate a vessel in a park area?
Citation: us:cfr:36:3.3
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-3.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 3 (Boating And Water Use Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Generally, you are not required to obtain a permit to operate a vessel in a park area. However, in certain circumstances, taking into consideration public safety, protection of park resources, and weather and park management objectives, the superintendent may require a permit for use of a vessel within a park area, under §§ 1.5 and 1.7, and will issue permits consistent with § 1.6 of this chapter.
