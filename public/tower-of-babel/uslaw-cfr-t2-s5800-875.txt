2 C.F.R. § 5800.875 — May I ask the debarring official to reconsider a decision to debar me?
Citation: us:cfr:2:5800.875
Creator: Election Assistance Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-5800.875
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 5800 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. Within 30 days of receiving a final notice of debarment, you may make a written request for the debarring official to reconsider your debarment pursuant to § 5800.880. The disposition of your request for reconsideration; or the result of your appeal; shall be considered a final agency action.
