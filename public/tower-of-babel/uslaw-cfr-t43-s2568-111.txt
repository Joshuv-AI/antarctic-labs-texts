43 C.F.R. § 2568.111 — What if BLM decides that I qualify for land that is in the category of Federal land that BLM cannot convey?
Citation: us:cfr:43:2568.111
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2568.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2560 (Alaska Occupancy And Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

BLM will notify you in writing that you are eligible to choose an alternative allotment from lands described in section 2568.110.
