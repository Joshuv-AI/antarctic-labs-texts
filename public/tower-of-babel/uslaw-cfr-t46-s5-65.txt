46 C.F.R. § 5.65 — Commandant's decisions in appeal or review cases.
Citation: us:cfr:46:5.65
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-5.65
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 5 (Marine Investigation Regulations—Personnel Action). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The decisions of the Commandant in cases of appeal or review of decisions of Administrative Law Judges are officially noticed and the principles and policies enunciated therein are binding upon all Administrative Law Judges, unless they are modified or rejected by competent authority.
