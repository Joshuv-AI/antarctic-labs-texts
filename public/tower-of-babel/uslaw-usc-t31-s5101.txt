31 U.S.C. § 5101 — Decimal system
Citation: us:usc:31:5101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section5101
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 51 (Coins And Currency); Subchapter I (Monetary System). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

United States money is expressed in dollars, dimes or tenths, cents or hundreths,1 and mills or thousandths. A dime is a tenth of a dollar, a cent is a hundredth of a dollar, and a mill is a thousandth of a dollar.
