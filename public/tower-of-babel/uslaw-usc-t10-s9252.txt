10 U.S.C. § 9252 — Service credit: regular enlisted members; service as an officer to be counted as enlisted service
Citation: us:usc:10:9252
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9252
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 933 (Miscellaneous Rights And Benefits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An enlisted member of the Regular Air Force or the Space Force is entitled to count active service as an officer in the Air Force, in the Space Force, and in the Army, as enlisted service for all purposes.
