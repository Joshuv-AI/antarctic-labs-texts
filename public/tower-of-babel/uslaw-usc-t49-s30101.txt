49 U.S.C. § 30101 — Purpose and policy
Citation: us:usc:49:30101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section30101
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (General); Chapter 301 (Motor Vehicle Safety); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of this chapter is to reduce traffic accidents and deaths and injuries resulting from traffic accidents. Therefore it is necessary—
(1)
to prescribe motor vehicle safety standards for motor vehicles and motor vehicle equipment in interstate commerce; and
(2)
to carry out needed safety research and development.
