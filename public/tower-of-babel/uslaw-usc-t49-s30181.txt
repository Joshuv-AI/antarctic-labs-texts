49 U.S.C. § 30181 — Policy
Citation: us:usc:49:30181
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section30181
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (General); Chapter 301 (Motor Vehicle Safety); Subchapter V (Motor Vehicle Safety Research And Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Transportation shall conduct research, development, and testing on any area or aspect of motor vehicle safety necessary to carry out this chapter.
