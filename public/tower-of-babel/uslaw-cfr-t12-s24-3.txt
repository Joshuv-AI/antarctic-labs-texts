12 C.F.R. § 24.3 — Public welfare investments.
Citation: us:cfr:12:24.3
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-24.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 24 (Community And Economic Development Entities, Community Development Projects, And Other Public Welfare Investments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A national bank or national bank subsidiary may make an investment directly or indirectly under this part if the investment primarily benefits low- and moderate income individuals, low- and moderate income areas, or other areas targeted by a governmental entity for redevelopment, or the investment would receive consideration under § 25.23 of appendix G to 12 CFR part 25 as a “qualified investment.”
