10 C.F.R. § 733.2 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:10:733.2
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-733.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 733 (Allegations Of Research Misconduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to allegations of research misconduct with regard to scientific research conducted under a Department of Energy contract or an agreement.
