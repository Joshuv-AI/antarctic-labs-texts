12 U.S.C. § 4405 — Preemption
Citation: us:usc:12:4405
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section4405
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 45 (Payment System Risk Reduction); Subchapter I (Bilateral And Clearing Organization Netting). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No stay, injunction, avoidance, moratorium, or similar proceeding or order, whether issued or granted by a court, administrative agency, or otherwise, shall limit or delay application of otherwise enforceable netting contracts in accordance with sections 4403 and 4404 of this title.
