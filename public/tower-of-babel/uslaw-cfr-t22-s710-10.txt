22 C.F.R. § 710.10 — Findings.
Citation: us:cfr:22:710.10
Creator: Us International Development Finance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-710.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 710 (Administrative Enforcement Procedures Of Post-Employment Restrictions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The examiner shall make a determination exclusively on matters of record in the proceeding and shall set forth in the written decision all findings of fact and conclusions of law relevant to the matters in issue.
