2 U.S.C. § 6594 — Compensation of Deputy Sergeant at Arms and Doorkeeper of Senate
Citation: us:usc:2:6594
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section6594
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 65 (Senate Officers And Administration); Subchapter III (Sergeant At Arms And Doorkeeper Of Senate); Part A (Organization And Personnel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Effective August 1, 1979, the Sergeant at Arms and Doorkeeper may fix the compensation of the Deputy Sergeant at Arms and Doorkeeper at an annual rate not to exceed the maximum annual rate of compensation of the Assistant Secretary of the Senate.
