25 C.F.R. § 150.301 — How does the LTRO certify copies of title documents?
Citation: us:cfr:25:150.301
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-150.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 150 (Record Of Title To Indian Land). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Certifying Officer certifies copies of title documents in the record of title by affixing an official seal to the copy of the title document. The official seal attests that the certified copy is a true and correct copy of the recorded title document.
