11 C.F.R. § 103.4 — Vice Presidential candidate campaign depositories.
Citation: us:cfr:11:103.4
Creator: Federal Election Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-103.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 103 (CAMPAIGN DEPOSITORIES (52 U.S.C. 30102(h))). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any campaign depository designated by the principal campaign committee of a political party's candidate for President shall be the campaign depository for that political party's candidate for the office of Vice President.
