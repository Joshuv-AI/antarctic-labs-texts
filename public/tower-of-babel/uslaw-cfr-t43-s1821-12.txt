43 C.F.R. § 1821.12 — Are these the only regulations that will apply to my application or other required document?
Citation: us:cfr:43:1821.12
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-1821.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 1820 (Application Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. These general regulations are supplemented by specific program regulations. You should consult the regulations applying to the specific program.
