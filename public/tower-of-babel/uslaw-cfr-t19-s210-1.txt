19 C.F.R. § 210.1 — Applicability of part.
Citation: us:cfr:19:210.1
Creator: United States International Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-210.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 210 (Adjudication And Enforcement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules in this part apply to investigations under section 337 of the Tariff Act of 1930 and related proceedings. These rules are authorized by sections 333, 335, or 337 of the Tariff Act of 1930 (19 U.S.C. §§ 1333, 1335, and 1337) and sections 2 and 1342(d)(1)(B) of the Omnibus Trade and Competitiveness Act of 1988, Pub. L. No. 100-418, 102 Stat. 1107 (1988).
