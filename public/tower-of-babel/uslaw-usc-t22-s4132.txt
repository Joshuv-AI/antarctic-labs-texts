22 U.S.C. § 4132 — Grievances concerning former members or their survivors
Citation: us:usc:22:4132
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section4132
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 52 (Foreign Service); Subchapter XI (Grievances). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Within the time limitations of section 4134 of this title, a former member of the Service or the surviving spouse (or, if none, another member of the family) of a deceased member or former member of the Service may file a grievance under this subchapter only with respect to allegations described in section 4131(a)(1)(G) of this title.
