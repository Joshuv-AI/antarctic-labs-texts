47 C.F.R. § 32.2006 — Nonoperating plant.
Citation: us:cfr:47:32.2006
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-32.2006
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 32 (Uniform System Of Accounts For Telecommunications Companies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) This account shall include the company's investment in regulated property which is not includable in the plant accounts as operating telecommunications plant. It shall include the company's investment in telecommunications property held for sale. (Note also Account 1406, Nonregulated Investments.)
(b) Subsidiary records shall be maintained to show the character of the amounts carried in this account.
