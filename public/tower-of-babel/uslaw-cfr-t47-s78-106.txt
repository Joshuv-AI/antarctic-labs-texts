47 C.F.R. § 78.106 — Interference to geostationary-satellites.
Citation: us:cfr:47:78.106
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-78.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 78 (Cable Television Relay Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applicants and licensees must comply with § 101.145 of this chapter to minimize the potential of interference to geostationary-satellites.
