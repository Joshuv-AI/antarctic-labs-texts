28 C.F.R. § 90.67 — Review of applications.
Citation: us:cfr:28:90.67
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-90.67
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 90 (Violence Against Women). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of 42 U.S.C. 3796 et seq. and this subpart provide the basis for review and approval or disapproval of applications and amendments in whole or in part.
