7 C.F.R. § 764.153 — Limitations.
Citation: us:cfr:7:764.153
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-764.153
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 764 (Direct Loan Making). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The applicant must:
(a) Comply with the general limitations established at § 764.102;
(b) Have dwellings and other buildings necessary for the planned operation of the farm available for use after the loan is made.
