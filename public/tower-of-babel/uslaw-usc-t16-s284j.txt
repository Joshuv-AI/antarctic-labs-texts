16 U.S.C. § 284j — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:16:284j
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section284j
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter XXXV (Wolf Trap National Park For The Performing Arts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this subchapter, the term—
(1)
“Secretary” means the Secretary of the Interior.
(2)
“Park” means the Wolf Trap National Park for the Performing Arts established under this subchapter, including the Center.
(3)
“Center” means the Filene Center in the Park. Such term includes all real property and fixtures which are within or directly related to the Filene Center.
(4)
“Foundation” means the Wolf Trap Foundation for the Performing Arts organized pursuant to the District of Columbia Nonprofit Organization Act.
