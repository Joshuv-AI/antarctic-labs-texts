RETRACTION: R. Salatti Ferrari, D. Pase da Rosa, L. F. Forgiarini, S. Bona, A. S. Dias, and N. P. Marroni. “Oxidative Stress and Pulmonary Changes in Experimental Liver Cirrhosis,” Oxidative Medicine and Cellular Longevity 2012, no. 1 (2012): https://doi.org/10.1155/2012/486190.

The above article, published online on 17 December 2012 in Wiley Online Library (wileyonlinelibrary.com), has been retracted by agreement between the journal Editor‐in‐Chief, Jeannette Vasquez‐Vivar and John Wiley & Sons Ltd. The retraction has been agreed after an investigation into figure concerns raised by Hydrophis melanocephalus on PubPeer [1]. Specifically, duplicate bands and splicing were detected in Figure 9.

The authors agree with this decision.
