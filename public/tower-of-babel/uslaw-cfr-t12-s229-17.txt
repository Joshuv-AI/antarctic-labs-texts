12 C.F.R. § 229.17 — Initial disclosures.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 229 (Availability Of Funds And Collection Of Checks (Regulation Cc)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before opening a new account, a bank shall provide a potential customer with the applicable specific availability policy disclosure described in § 229.16.
