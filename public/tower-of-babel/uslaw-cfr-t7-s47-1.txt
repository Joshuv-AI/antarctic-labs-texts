7 C.F.R. § 47.1 — Meaning of words.
Citation: us:cfr:7:47.1
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-47.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 47 (Administrative Procedures Under The Perishable Agricultural Commodities Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Words in the regulations in this part in the singular form shall be deemed to import the plural, and vice versa, as the case may demand.
