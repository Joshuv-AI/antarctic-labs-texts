19 C.F.R. § 115.55 — Termination of approval.
Citation: us:cfr:19:115.55
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-115.55
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 115 (Cargo Container And Road Vehicle Certification Pursuant To International Customs Conventions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Approval of a road vehicle terminates:
(a) Upon expiration of the certificate of approval; or
(b) Upon a change in the road vehicle by a major repair or alteration of any of the essential features required in § 115.51. Repairs by replacement in kind do not constitute a change of the essential features.
