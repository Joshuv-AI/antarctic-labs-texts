24 C.F.R. § 10.16 — Adoption of a final rule.
Citation: us:cfr:24:10.16
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-10.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 10 (Rulemaking: Policy And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All timely comments are considered in taking final action on a proposed rule. Each preamble to a final rule will contain a short analysis and evaluation of the relevant significant issues set forth in the comments submitted, and a clear concise statement of the basis and purpose of the rule.
