52 U.S.C. § 20106 — Relationship to Voting Rights Act of 1965
Citation: us:usc:52:20106
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title52-section20106
Context: United States Code, Title 52 (Voting And Elections); Chapter 201 (Voting Accessibility For The Elderly And Handicapped). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter shall not be construed to impair any right guaranteed by the Voting Rights Act of 1965 (42 U.S.C. 1973 et seq.) [now 52 U.S.C. 10301 et seq.].
