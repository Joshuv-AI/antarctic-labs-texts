43 C.F.R. § 3216.18 — How do I describe the lands in my lease transfer?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3216.18
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3216.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3200 (Geothermal Resource Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If you are transferring an interest in your entire lease, you do not need to give BLM a legal description of the land.
(b) If you are transferring an interest in a portion of your lease, describe the lands that are transferred in the same way they are described in the lease.
