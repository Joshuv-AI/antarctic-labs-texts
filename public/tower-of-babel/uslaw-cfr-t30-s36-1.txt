30 C.F.R. § 36.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:30:36.1
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-36.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 36 (Approval Requirements For Permissible Mobile Diesel-Powered Transportation Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part set forth the requirements for mobile diesel-powered transportation equipment to procure their approval and certification as permissible; procedures for applying for such certification; and fees.
