16 U.S.C. § 3923 — Restriction on use of eminent domain in acquisitions
Citation: us:usc:16:3923
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section3923
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 59 (Wetlands Resources); Subchapter III (State And Federal Wetlands Acquisition). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The powers of condemnation or eminent domain shall not be used in the acquisition of wetlands under any provision of this chapter where such wetlands have been constructed for the purpose of farming or ranching, or result from conservation activities associated with farming or ranching.
