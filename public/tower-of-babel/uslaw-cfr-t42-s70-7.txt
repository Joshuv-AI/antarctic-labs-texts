42 C.F.R. § 70.7 — Responsibility with respect to minors, wards, and patients.
Citation: us:cfr:42:70.7
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-70.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 70 (Interstate Quarantine). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A parent, guardian, physician, nurse, or other such person shall not transport, or procure or furnish transportation for any minor child or ward, patient or other such person who is in the communicable period of a communicable disease, except in accordance with provisions of this part.
