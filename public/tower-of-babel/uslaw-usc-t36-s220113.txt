36 U.S.C. § 220113 — Annual report
Citation: us:usc:36:220113
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section220113
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 2201 (United Service Organizations, Incorporated). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation shall make public an annual report on its activities for the prior calendar year.
