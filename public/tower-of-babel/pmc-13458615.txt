Research on attention mechanism and loss function based on YOLOv5 to improve the detection method of underground personnel wearing safety helmets
Authors: Gengmu Qin, Jingtao Pan, Dan Zhao, Chuanxu Zhang
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
Detecting whether underground workers are wearing safety helmets has long been an essential yet challenging task in industrial safety monitoring. Due to dim lighting, occlusion, and complex backgrounds in mining environments, existing object detection models often fail to achieve satisfactory performance. To address these issues, this study proposes an improved helmet detection approach based on YOLOv5. The Squeeze-and-Excitation Network (SENet) is enhanced with a multi-scale average pooling mechanism and the smoother Swish activation function, forming a lightweight attention module named SENet-tn. In addition, a novel loss function, INWDLoss, is introduced by integrating the advantages of the Normalized Wasserstein Distance (NWDLoss) and IoULoss to improve robustness in small-target detection and high-overlap scenarios. Experiments conducted on the self-built Mine Wearing Helmet Dataset (MWHD) demonstrate that the proposed YOLOv5s-tn model increases the mean Average Precision (mAP) by 2.91% compared to the baseline YOLOv5s. These results confirm that the enhanced attention mechanism and loss function effectively improve detection accuracy and reliability for underground helmet-wearing scenarios, providing technical support for intelligent safety supervision in mines.

Introduction

In the field of computer vision, traditional object detection algorithms mainly include Viola Jones Detectors1, HOGDetector2, and Deformable Part-based Model (DPM)3 that use statistical learning methods to identify objects. Figure 2 shows the flow chart of the traditional object detection algorithm, which plays a key role in the development and progress of early object detection technology.

According to the characteristics and application purposes of data in mining production scenarios, scholars have proposed various object detection methods to meet the actual needs of mining production. Zheng Junhui et al.4 employed the YOLOv3 framework, incorporated the Gc Net attention module, established a reverse feature fusion pathway, and developed a three - scale prediction module. These modifications were designed to identify hazards with different dimensions and configurations, thus enhancing the robustness of the method under complex circumstances. In 2013, Jiping S et al.5 used the Mallat algorithm based on two-dimensional wavelet transform to preprocess the face images of miners in mines. The security monitoring system for small target detection designed by Chen Yulian6 organically integrates early warning functions with linkage systems, achieving favorable operational outcomes and effectively preventing the occurrence of hazards during work processes. Zhikang Chi et al.7. proposed a lightweight sonar image target detection and recognition method based on frequency domain design, which improves detection efficiency.

Although the above methods improve the stability of detection to different degrees, the accuracy is affected to varying degrees due to the complex underground conditions and dust, light and other factors, so this paper mainly proposes an efficient detection algorithm from the aspects of improving the yolov5 attention mechanism and loss function.

Results

Research on helmet wearing detection algorithm based on improved SE-Net and loss function

In order to solve the above problem of helmet detection in underground mines, this paper improves on the basis of YOLOv5s, improves the original SENet attention mechanism, and adds multi-pixel average pooling operation to extract more detailed information. Combined with the advantages of NWDLoss in the extraction of small targets, a new loss function INWDLoss is established to improve the detection accuracy of targets.

Improved SENet attention mechanism

Attention mechanism is a technology to enhance the performance of neural network, it is proposed by imitating the process of people’s attention to things, so it can be well understood that the attention mechanism in deep learning can flexibly grasp the correlation between global information and local information, and its goal is to make important regions so that the model can identify, and at the same time, more weight values can be assigned to the target area, further highlight the useful target features, and depress and ignore irrelevant features.

SENet attention mechanism (Squeeze-and-Excit-ation Networks) is a convolutional neural network architecture based on attention mechanism proposed in 2017. One of the most striking aspects of the SENet attention mechanism is that it is easy to implement in the existing network structure, and the computational complexity brought by the improved performance is only slightly increased. The idea of SENet is to adaptively readjust the response of convolutional feature channels, mainly Squeeze and Excition.

Improved loss function

The loss function plays a crucial role in machine learning and deep learning. The loss function is mostly used to measure the difference between the predicted value of the model and the real label, which is one of the key indicators of model optimization, but different tasks need to choose different loss functions according to different situations. The loss function of the YOLOv5s version used in this paper is similar to the loss function design of YOLOv5, which mainly consists of three functions: classification loss, localization loss, also known as regression box loss function and confidence loss. Compared with the confidence loss function and the classification loss function, the design of the regression box loss function has an important impact on the positioning ability and object detection accuracy of the model. Therefore, when modifying the loss function, the adjustment and optimization of the loss function of the regression box are usually considered. The main loss functions in this part are IoULoss, GIoULoss, DIoULoss8, CIoULoss, and the Normalized Wasserstein Distance (NWD) loss function improved in this paper9.

As mentioned above, the principle of the IoU loss function is to calculate the ratio of the intersection and union area of two bounding boxes. The GIoU loss function is the loss function used by YOLOv5s, which is the same as the IoU loss function when the two feature boxes overlap, and GIoU is extended on its basis, taking into account the positioning bias between the boundary and the boundary, and adds a boundary condition on the basis of IoU, which is the intersection of the real box A and the prediction box B, as shown in Fig. 7. The safety helmets worn by workers in the mine belong to the small and medium-sized targets in the target characteristics, and the loss functions such as IoU and GIoU are sensitive to the position deviation of the small target, and the position deviation of the detection of the small target will significantly reduce the loss function IoU9, so IoU is not a good measure for the small target. Therefore, this paper uses NWDLoss to replace GIoULoss, and the NWD loss function has great advantages in object detection compared with the original loss function, in which its scale will not change, and it has a good smoothness for the position deviation of IoU, and has the ability to measure the similarity of the bounding box without overlapping and containing each other. Experiments and analysis of results

Experimental environment

Based on the YOLOv5s model algorithm, this paper uses the self-built dataset MWHD (Mine wearing safety helmets dataset), and the input resolution is 640 × 640 in all experiments. In this paper, the initial learning rate is set to 0.01 and the final learning rate is set to 0.2. The network was optimized using the SGD optimizer with a momentum decay of 0.9 and a weight decay of 5 × 10 − 4 and a passage time of 300 rounds.

Conclusion

In view of the problems of dim environment, many occlusions and small detection targets in the detection of safety helmets in mines, this paper improves the detection performance of the model algorithm by improving YOLOv5s, improves the SENet to enrich the extracted target information, enhances the extraction ability of target information, and avoids the omission of target information. At the same time, considering the problem of small detection targets, combined with the advantages of NWDLoss to better adapt to the unbalanced data distribution when dealing with small target detection, the advantages of paying attention to the relationship between small targets and the advantages of IoULoss paying attention to overlapping bounding boxes, a new loss function INWDLoss is proposed, which can better meet the requirements of safety helmets as small target detection in mines. Experimental results show that the improved model algorithm can effectively improve the detection accuracy of the model algorithm while sacrificing part of the model size and operation time on the self-built dataset MWHD.
