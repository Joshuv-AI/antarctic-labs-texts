29 C.F.R. § 570.12 — Revoked certificates of age.
Citation: us:cfr:29:570.12
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-570.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 570 (Child Labor Regulations, Orders And Statements Of Interpretation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A certificate which has been revoked as proof of age under the Act shall be of no force and effect under the Act after notice of such revocation.
