29 C.F.R. § 2.41 — Severability.
Citation: us:cfr:29:2.41
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-2.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 2 (General Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Should a court of competent jurisdiction hold any provision(s) of this subpart to be invalid, such action will not affect any other provision of this subpart.
