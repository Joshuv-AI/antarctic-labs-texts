28 C.F.R. § 44.305 — Regional offices.
Citation: us:cfr:28:44.305
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-44.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 44 (Unfair Immigration-Related Employment Practices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Special Counsel, in accordance with regulations of the Attorney General, shall establish such regional offices as may be necessary to carry out the Special Counsel's duties.
