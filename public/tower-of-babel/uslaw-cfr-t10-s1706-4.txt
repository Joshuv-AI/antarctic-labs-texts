10 C.F.R. § 1706.4 — Head of the contracting activity.

Creator: Defense Nuclear Facilities Safety Board
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1706 (Organizational And Consultant Conflicts Of Interests). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The head of the contracting activity for the Board shall be the General Manager.
