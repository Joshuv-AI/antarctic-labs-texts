5 C.F.R. § 838.121 — OPM's responsibilities.
Citation: us:cfr:5:838.121
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-838.121
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 838 (Court Orders Affecting Retirement Benefits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

OPM is responsible for authorizing payments in accordance with clear, specific and express provisions of court orders acceptable for processing.
