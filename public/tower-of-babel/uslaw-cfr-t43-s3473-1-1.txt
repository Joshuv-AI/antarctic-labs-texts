43 C.F.R. § 3473.1-1 — -1 Form of remittance.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3473.1-1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3473.1-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3470 (Coal Management Provisions And Limitations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All remittances shall be by U.S. currency, postal money order or negotiable instrument payable in U.S. currency and shall be made payable to the Department of the Interior—Bureau of Land Management or the Department of the Interior—Minerals Management Service, as appropriate. In the case of payments made to the Service, such payments may also be made by electronic funds transfer.
