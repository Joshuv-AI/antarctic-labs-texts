10 C.F.R. § 15.27 — Contact with debtor's employing agency.
Citation: us:cfr:10:15.27
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-15.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 15 (Debt Collection Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the debtor is employed by the Federal government or is a member of the military establishment or the Coast Guard, collection by offset must be accomplished in accordance with 5 U.S.C. 5514 and the provisions of 10 CFR part 16.
