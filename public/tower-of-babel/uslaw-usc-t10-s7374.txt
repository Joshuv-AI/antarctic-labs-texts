10 U.S.C. § 7374 — Expert accountant for Inspector General
Citation: us:usc:10:7374
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7374
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 747 (Civilian Employees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Army shall appoint an expert accountant to perform duties under the Inspector General.
