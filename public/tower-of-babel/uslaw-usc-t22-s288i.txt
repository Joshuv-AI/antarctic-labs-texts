22 U.S.C. § 288i — Liaison Office of the People’s Republic of China; extension of privileges and immunities to members
Citation: us:usc:22:288i
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section288i
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter XVIII (Privileges And Immunities Of International Organizations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Under such terms and conditions as he shall determine and consonant with the purposes of this section, the President is authorized to extend to the Liaison Office of the People’s Republic of China in Washington and to the members thereof the same privileges and immunities subject to corresponding conditions and obligations as are enjoyed by diplomatic missions accredited to the United States and by members thereof.
