41 C.F.R. § 101-29.209 — -29.209 Purchase description.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-29.209
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-29.209
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-29 (29—Federal Product Descriptions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A purchase description is any informal product description prepared for one-time use only or for small purchases when issuance of a formal product description is not cost effective.
