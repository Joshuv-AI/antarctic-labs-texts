14 C.F.R. § 323.17 — Delays in discontinuing service.
Citation: us:cfr:14:323.17
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-323.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 323 (Terminations, Suspensions, And Reductions Of Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If transportation that is the subject of a notice under this part is not discontinued within 90 days of the intended date stated in the notice, a new notice must be filed before the service may be discontinued. However, if DOT requires the carrier to provide service beyond the stated date, the carrier need not file a new notice if it discontinues the service within 90 days after DOT permits it to do so.
