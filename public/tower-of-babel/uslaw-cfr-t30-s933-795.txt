30 C.F.R. § 933.795 — Small operator assistance.
Citation: us:cfr:30:933.795
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-933.795
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 933 (North Carolina). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Part 795 of this chapter, Small Operator Assistance, shall apply to any person making application for assistance under the small operator assistance program.
