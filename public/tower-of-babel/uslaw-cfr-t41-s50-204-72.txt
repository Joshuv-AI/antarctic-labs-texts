41 C.F.R. § 50-204.72 — -204.72 Safe practices for welding and cutting on containers which have held combustibles.
Citation: us:cfr:41:50-204.72
Creator: Chapter 50—Public Contracts, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-50-204.72
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 50-204 (204—Safety And Health Standards For Federal Supply Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Welding or cutting, or both, on containers which have held flammable or combustible solids, liquids, or gases, or have contained substances which may produce flammable vapors or gases will not be attempted until the containers have been thoroughly cleaned, purged, or inerted in strict accordance with the rules and procedures embodied in American Welding Society Pamphlet A-6.0-65, edition of 1965.
