19 U.S.C. § 1356l — “Coffee” defined
Citation: us:usc:19:1356l
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section1356l
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 4 (Tariff Act Of 1930); Part III (Promotion Of Foreign Trade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this section and section 1356k of this title, the term “coffee” means coffee as defined in article 3 of the International Coffee Agreement, 1983.
