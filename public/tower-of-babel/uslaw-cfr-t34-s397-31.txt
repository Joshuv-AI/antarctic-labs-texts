34 C.F.R. § 397.31 — What are the contracting limitations on educational agencies under this part?
Citation: us:cfr:34:397.31
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-397.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 397 (Limitations On Use Of Subminimum Wage). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Neither a local educational agency, as defined in § 397.5(b)(1), nor a State educational agency, as defined in § 397.5(b)(2), may enter into a contract or other arrangement with an entity, as defined in § 397.5(d), for the purpose of operating a program for a youth under which work is compensated at a subminimum wage.
