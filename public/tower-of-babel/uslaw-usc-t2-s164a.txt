2 U.S.C. § 164a — Official distribution of State legislation index and digest
Citation: us:usc:2:164a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section164a
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 5 (Library Of Congress). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Librarian of Congress is directed to have the indexes and digests authorized by section 164 of this title printed and bound for official distribution only.
