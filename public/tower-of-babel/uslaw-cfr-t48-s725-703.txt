48 C.F.R. § 725.703 — 725.703 Source and Nationality requirements—Contract clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:725.703
Creator: Chapter 7—Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-725.703
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 725 (Foreign Acquisition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Insert the clause at 752.225-70, Source and Nationality Requirements in all USAID program-funded solicitations and contracts for goods or services, unless source and nationality requirements do not apply as set forth at 22 CFR 228.02.
