43 U.S.C. § 2243 — Temperature control at Shasta Dam, Central Valley Project
Citation: us:usc:43:2243
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section2243
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 40 (Reclamation States Emergency Drought Relief); Subchapter III (General And Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to complete the design and specifications for construction of a device to control the temperature of water releases from Shasta Dam, Central Valley Project, California, and to construct facilities needed to attach such device to the dam. There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out the authority of this section not more than $12,000,000.
