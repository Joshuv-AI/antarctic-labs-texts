54 U.S.C. § 304111 — Reimbursements from State and local agencies
Citation: us:usc:54:304111
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section304111
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3041 (Advisory Council On Historic Preservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Subject to applicable conflict of interest laws, the Council may receive reimbursements from State and local agencies and others pursuant to agreements executed in furtherance of this division.
