24 C.F.R. § 964.117 — Resident council partnerships.
Citation: us:cfr:24:964.117
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-964.117
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 964 (Tenant Participation And Tenant Opportunities In Public Housing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A resident council may form partnerships with outside organizations, provided that such relationships are complementary to the resident council in its duty to represent the residents, and provided that such outside organizations do not become the governing entity of the resident council.
