39 C.F.R. § 955.23 — (Rule 23) Decisions.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 955 (Rules Of Practice Before The Postal Service Board Of Contract Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board's decisions will be in writing and sent to both parties electronically or by United States mail. Except as required by law, all final orders and decisions are available for public inspection at the Board's office. They are also accessible on the Board's official website and through commercial publishers. The Board's decisions will be made solely on the evidentiary record as described in Rule 13.
