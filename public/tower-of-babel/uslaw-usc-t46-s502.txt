46 U.S.C. § 502 — Cargo exempt from forfeiture
Citation: us:usc:46:502
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section502
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 5 (Other General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Cargo on a vessel is exempt from forfeiture under this title if—
(1)
the cargo is owned in good faith by a person not the owner, master, or crewmember of the vessel; and
(2)
the customs duties on the cargo have been paid or secured for payment as provided by law.
