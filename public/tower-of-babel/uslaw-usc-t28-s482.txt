28 U.S.C. § 482 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:28:482
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section482
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part I (Organization Of Courts); Chapter 23 (Civil Justice Expense And Delay Reduction Plans). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this chapter, the term “judicial officer” means a United States district court judge or a United States magistrate judge.
