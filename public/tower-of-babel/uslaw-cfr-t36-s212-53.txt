36 C.F.R. § 212.53 — Coordination with Federal, State, county, and other local governmental entities and tribal governments.
Citation: us:cfr:36:212.53
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-212.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 212 (Travel Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The responsible official shall coordinate with appropriate Federal, State, county, and other local governmental entities and tribal governments when designating National Forest System roads, National Forest System trails, and areas on National Forest System lands pursuant to this subpart.
