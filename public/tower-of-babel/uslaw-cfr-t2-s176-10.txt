2 C.F.R. § 176.10 — Purpose of this part.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 176 (Award Terms For Assistance Agreements That Include Funds Under The American Recovery And Reinvestment Act Of 2009, Public Law 111-5). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part establishes Federal Governmentwide award terms for financial assistance awards, namely, grants, cooperative agreements, and loans, to implement the cross-cutting requirements of the American Recovery and Reinvestment Act of 2009, Public Law 111-5 (Recovery Act). These requirements are cross-cutting in that they apply to more than one agency's awards.
