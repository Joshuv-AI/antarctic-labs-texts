15 C.F.R. § 930.110 — Objectives.
Citation: us:cfr:15:930.110
Creator: National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-930.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 930 (Federal Consistency With Approved Coastal Management Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this subpart is to describe mediation procedures which Federal and State agencies may use to attempt to resolve serious disagreements which arise during the administration of approved management programs.
