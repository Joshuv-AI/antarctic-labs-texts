22 U.S.C. § 2775 — Foreign military sales to less developed countries
Citation: us:usc:22:2775
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2775
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 39 (Arms Export Control); Subchapter III (Military Export Controls). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
When the President finds that any economically less developed country is diverting development assistance furnished pursuant to the Foreign Assistance Act of 1961, as amended [22 U.S.C. 2151 et seq.], or sales under the Food for Peace Act, as amended [7 U.S.C. 1691 et seq.], to military expenditures, or is diverting its own resources to unnecessary military expenditures, to a degree which materially interferes with its development, such country shall be immediately ineligible for further sales and guarantees under sections 2761, 2762, 2763, and 2764 of this title, until the President is assured that such diversion will no longer take place.
