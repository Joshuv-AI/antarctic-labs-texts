47 C.F.R. § 26.109 — Assignment and transfer.
Citation: us:cfr:47:26.109
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-26.109
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 26 (Space Launch Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Licensees may assign or transfer their non-exclusive nationwide licenses upon application to and prior approval from the Commission, and any stations registered under those licenses will remain associated with those licenses unless otherwise agreed upon by the parties to the assignment or transfer and approved by the Commission.
