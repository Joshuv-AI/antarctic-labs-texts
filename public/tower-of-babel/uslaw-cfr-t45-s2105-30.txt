45 C.F.R. § 2105.30 — How will the Agency consider the submitter's objections?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2105.30
Creator: Commission Of Fine Arts
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2105.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2105 (Requirements For Compliance With The Freedom Of Information Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Agency must carefully consider a submitter's objections and specific grounds for nondisclosure in deciding whether to disclose the requested information.
(b) The Agency, not the submitter, is responsible for deciding whether the information will be released or withheld.
