45 C.F.R. § 506.11 — “Prisoner of war” defined.
Citation: us:cfr:45:506.11
Creator: Foreign Claims Settlement Commission Of The United States, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-506.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 506 (Eligibility Requirements For Compensation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Prisoner of war means any regularly appointed, enrolled, enlisted or inducted member of the Armed Forces of the United States who was held by any force hostile to the United States for any period of time during the Vietnam conflict.
