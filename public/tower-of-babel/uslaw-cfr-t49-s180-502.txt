49 C.F.R. § 180.502 — Alternative qualification and maintenance.
Citation: us:cfr:49:180.502
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-180.502
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 180 (Continuing Qualification And Maintenance Of Packagings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-28 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notwithstanding the applicability of § 180.501 for the qualification and maintenance of multi-unit tank car tanks, and subject to conditions and limitations set forth in § 173.252 of this subchapter, the NBIC (IBR, see § 171.7 of this subchapter), must be used for the continuing qualification, maintenance, and periodic testing (i.e., continued service) of Class DOT-106A and 110AW multi-unit tank car tanks constructed to ASME Code Section XII in accordance with § 179.302 of this subchapter.
