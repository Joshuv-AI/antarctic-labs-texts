31 C.F.R. § 539.201 — Prohibited importation of goods, technology, or services.
Citation: us:cfr:31:539.201
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-539.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 539 (Weapons Of Mass Destruction Trade Control Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise authorized, the importation into the United States, on or after the effective date, directly or indirectly, of any goods, technology, or services produced or provided by a designated foreign person, other than information or informational materials, is prohibited.
