18 U.S.C. § 1701 — Obstruction of mails generally
Citation: us:usc:18:1701
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1701
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 83 (Postal Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever knowingly and willfully obstructs or retards the passage of the mail, or any carrier or conveyance carrying the mail, shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than six months, or both.
