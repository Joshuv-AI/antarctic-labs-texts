42 U.S.C. § 12004 — Energy efficiency authorizations
Citation: us:usc:42:12004
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section12004
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 125 (Renewable Energy And Energy Efficiency Technology Competitiveness). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary for the following energy efficiency research, development, and demonstration programs: transportation, industrial, buildings and community systems, multi-sector, and policy and management—
(1)
not to exceed $201,100,000 for fiscal year 1991, of which—
(A)
not to exceed $68,300,000 shall be available for the transportation program; and
(B)
not to exceed $53,500,000 shall be available for the industrial program; and
(2)
not to exceed $210,600,000 for fiscal year 1992, of which—
(A)
not to exceed $71,000,000 shall be available for the transportation program; and
(B)
not to exceed $54,700,000 shall be available for the industrial program.
