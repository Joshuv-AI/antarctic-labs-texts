14 U.S.C. § 3714 — Enlistment of members engaged in schooling
Citation: us:usc:14:3714
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section3714
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 37 (Coast Guard Reserve); Subchapter I (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The initial period of active duty for training required by section 12103(d) of title 10, may be divided into two successive annual periods of not less than six weeks each, to permit the enlistment of a Reserve member without interrupting any full-time schooling in which the member is engaged.
