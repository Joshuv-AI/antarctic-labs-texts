29 C.F.R. § 2204.403 — Reply.
Citation: us:cfr:29:2204.403
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Review Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-2204.403
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 2204 (Implementation Of The Equal Access To Justice Act In Proceedings Before The Occupational Safety And Health Review Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within 15 days after service of an answer, the applicant may file a reply. If the reply is based on any alleged facts not already in the record of the proceeding, the applicant shall include with the reply either supporting affidavits or a request for further proceedings under § 2204.405.
