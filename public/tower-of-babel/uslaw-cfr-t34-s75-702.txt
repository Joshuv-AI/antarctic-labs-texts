34 C.F.R. § 75.702 — Fiscal control and fund accounting procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:34:75.702
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-75.702
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 75 (Direct Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A grantee shall use fiscal control and fund accounting procedures that ensure proper disbursement of, and accounting for, Federal funds as required in 2 CFR part 200, subpart D—Post Federal Award Requirements.
