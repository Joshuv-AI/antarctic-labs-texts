43 C.F.R. § 3133.2-1 — -1 Minimum royalties.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3133.2-1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3133.2-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3130 (Oil And Gas Leasing: National Petroleum Reserve, Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For leases which provide for minimum royalty payments, each lessee shall pay the minimum royalty specified in the lease at the end of each lease year beginning with the first lease year following a discovery on the lease.
