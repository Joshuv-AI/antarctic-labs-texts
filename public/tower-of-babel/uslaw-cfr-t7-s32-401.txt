7 C.F.R. § 32.401 — Cost of samples for grease mohair grades.
Citation: us:cfr:7:32.401
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-32.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 32 (Purchase Of Grease Mohair And Mohair Top Samples). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Complete set. $22 each, delivered to any destination with the United States and $25 each, delivered to any destination outside the United States.
(b) Individual sample. $2.50 each, delivered to any destination within the United States, and $3 each, delivered to any destination outside the United States.
