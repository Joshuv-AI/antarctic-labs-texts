44 C.F.R. § 321.8 — Reports.
Citation: us:cfr:44:321.8
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-321.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 321 (Maintenance Of The Mobilization Base (Department Of Defense, Department Of Energy, Maritime Administration)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department of Defense, Department of Energy, and Maritime Administration shall furnish the Administrator of the Federal Emergency Management Agency with reports on items and facilities for programs under § 321.2 (a) and (b) of this part, and with such other periodic and special reports as he may require affecting the maintenance of the mobilization base.
