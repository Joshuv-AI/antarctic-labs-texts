25 C.F.R. § 163.24 — Duration of timber contracts.
Citation: us:cfr:25:163.24
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-163.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 163 (General Forestry Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After the effective date of a forest product contract, unless otherwise authorized by the Secretary, the maximum period which shall be allowed for harvesting the estimated volume of timber purchased, shall be five years.
