15 U.S.C. § 312 — Employees
Citation: us:usc:15:312
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section312
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 9 (National Weather Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The National Weather Service shall consist of such civilian employees as Congress may annually provide for and as may be necessary to properly perform the duties devolving on said Service by law.
