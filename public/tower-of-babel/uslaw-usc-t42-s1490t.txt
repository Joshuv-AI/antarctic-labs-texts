42 U.S.C. § 1490t — Indian tribes
Citation: us:usc:42:1490t
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1490t
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 8A (Slum Clearance, Urban Renewal, And Farm Housing); Subchapter III (Farm Housing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Title VI of the Civil Rights Act of 1964 (42 U.S.C. 2000d et seq.) and title VIII of the Civil Rights Act of 1968 (42 U.S.C. 3601 et seq.) shall not apply to actions by federally recognized Indian tribes (including instrumentalities of such Indian tribes) under this Act.
