10 U.S.C. § 506 — Regular components: extension of enlistments during war
Citation: us:usc:10:506
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section506
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 31 (Enlistments). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An enlistment in the Regular Army, Regular Navy, Regular Air Force, Regular Marine Corps, Space Force, or Regular Coast Guard in effect at the beginning of a war, or entered into during a war, unless sooner terminated by the President, continues in effect until six months after the termination of that war.
