7 C.F.R. § 51.1542 — U.S. Commercial.
Citation: us:cfr:7:51.1542
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-51.1542
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 51 (Fresh Fruits, Vegetables, And Other Products (Inspection, Certification, And Standards)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

“U.S. Commercial” consists of potatoes which meet the requirements of U.S. No. 1 grade except for the following:
(a) Free from serious damage caused by:
(1) Dirt or other foreign matter;
(2) Russet scab; and,
(3) Rhizoctonia.
(b) Increased tolerances for defects specified in § 51.1546.
