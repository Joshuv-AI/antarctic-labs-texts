36 C.F.R. § 1235.18 — How do agencies transfer records to the National Archives of the United States?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1235.18
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1235.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1235 (Transfer Of Records To The National Archives Of The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies transfer records by submitting a signed SF 258, Agreement to Transfer Records to the National Archives of the United States. Each SF 258 must correlate to a specific records series or other aggregation of records, as identified in an item on the SF 115 or cited on the SF 258.
