5 C.F.R. § 315.725 — Disqualifications.
Citation: us:cfr:5:315.725
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-315.725
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 315 (Career And Career-Conditional Employment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any law, executive order, or civil service rule or regulation which would disqualify an applicant for appointment shall also disqualify an employee for conversion of his employment to career or career-conditional employment under this subpart.
