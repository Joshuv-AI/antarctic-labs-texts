On the second partial Global Euler-Poincaré characteristics for Galois cohomology
Yufan Luo

Abstract
. Let K K be a number field, let S S be a finite set of primes of K K containing all archimedean primes, and let G K , S G_{K,S} denote the Galois group of the maximal extension of K K unramified outside S S . In this paper, we study the second partial Euler–Poincaré characteristic χ 2 ​ ( G K , S , M ) \chi_{2}(G_{K,S},M) for a finite G K , S G_{K,S} -module M M , without imposing the condition that the order of M M is an S S -unit. By adjoining a further finite set of primes of K K , which can be chosen to be disjoint from any prescribed set of primes of density zero, we obtain an explicit formula for χ 2 ​ ( G K , S , M ) \chi_{2}(G_{K,S},M) . As an application, we investigate the presentation of the Galois group G K , S G_{K,S} . Furthermore, we construct counterexamples to the dimension conjecture for Galois deformation rings over all number fields.

\chi_{2}(G_{K,S},M):=\dfrac{[H^{0}(G_{K,S},M)]\times[H^{2}(G_{K,S},M)]}{[H^{1}(G_{K,S},M)]},

If

Our first observation is that if

Remark 1.3

(1)

(2)

(3)

When

This theorem provides the optimal upper bound for the general formula of the second partial Euler–Poincaré characteristic
\chi_{2}(G_{K,S},M)

The proof strategy of the theorem involves a comparison between the étale cohomology groups and the Galois cohomology groups, along with an exact computation of the étale cohomology groups. In addition, the proof of the second part of the theorem relies on results due to Schmidt, Labute, among others.

The presentation of the group

Theorem 1.4

Let

(1)

(1.4)

1.3.
Mazur’s dimensional conjecture

In this subsection, we present applications of our main theorem to Galois deformation theory. Let
p
be a prime,
K
a number field and
S
a finite set of primes of
K
. Let

be a finite field of characteristic

. In
[

]

Conjecture 1.5

(Mazur’s dimension conjecture)
.

If

contains

and

of

\text{Krulldim}(R_{\overline{\rho}}/(p))=h^{1}(G_{K,S},\text{ad})-h^{2}(G_{K,S},\text{ad}),

where

\text{ad}:=\text{ad}(\overline{\rho})
is the adjoint representation of

.

In
[
4
, Conjecture]
and
[
5
, Lecture 4]
, Gouvêa proposed a generalization of Conjecture

to more general settings.

Conjecture 1.6
.

Conjecture
1.5

By

\text{Krulldim}(R_{\overline{\rho}}/(p))\geq h^{1}(G_{K,S},\text{ad})-h^{2}(G_{K,S},\text{ad}),

(1.5)

\text{Krulldim}(R_{S}/(p))\leq h^{1}(G_{K,S},\text{ad})-h^{2}(G_{K,S},\text{ad}).

Theorem 1.7

Let

.

Assume the unramified Fontaine-Mazur conjecture
