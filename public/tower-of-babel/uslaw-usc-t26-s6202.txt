26 U.S.C. § 6202 — Establishment by regulations of mode or time of assessment
Citation: us:usc:26:6202
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section6202
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 63 (Assessment); Subchapter A (In General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If the mode or time for the assessment of any internal revenue tax (including interest, additional amounts, additions to the tax, and assessable penalties) is not otherwise provided for, the Secretary may establish the same by regulations.
