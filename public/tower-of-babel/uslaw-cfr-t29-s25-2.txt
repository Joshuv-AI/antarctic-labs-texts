29 C.F.R. § 25.2 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:29:25.2
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-25.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 25 (Rules For The Nomination Of Arbitrators Under Section 11 Of Executive Order 10988). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When used in these procedures:
(a) Order means Executive Order No. 10988;
(b) Agency, employee organization, and employee have the same meaning as in the Order;
(c) Recognition means recognition which is or may be accorded to an employee organization pursuant to the provisions of the Order;
(d) Secretary means the Secretary of Labor.
