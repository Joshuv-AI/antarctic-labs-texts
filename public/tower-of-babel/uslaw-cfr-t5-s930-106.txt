5 C.F.R. § 930.106 — Details in the competitive service.
Citation: us:cfr:5:930.106
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-930.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 930 (Programs For Specific Positions And Examinations (Miscellaneous)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An agency may detail an employee to an operator position in the competitive service for 30 days or less when the employee possesses a State license. For details exceeding 30 days, the employee must meet all the requirements of § 930.105 and any applicable OPM and agency regulations governing such details.
