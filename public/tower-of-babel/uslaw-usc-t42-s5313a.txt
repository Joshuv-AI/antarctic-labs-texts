42 U.S.C. § 5313a — Duplication of benefits
Citation: us:usc:42:5313a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section5313a
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 69 (Community Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall establish procedures to prevent recipients from receiving any duplication of benefits and report annually to the Committees on Appropriations with regard to all steps taken to prevent fraud and abuse of funds made available under this heading including duplication of benefits.
