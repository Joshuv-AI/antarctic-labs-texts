10 U.S.C. § 8869 — Security for costs
Citation: us:usc:10:8869
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8869
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 883 (Prize). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The court may require any party to give security for costs at any stage of the cause and upon filing an appeal.
