45 C.F.R. § 99.2 — Presiding officer.

Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-99.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 99 (Procedure For Hearings For The Child Care And Development Fund). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) (1) The presiding officer at a hearing shall be the Assistant Secretary or the Assistant Secretary's designee.
(2) The designation of the presiding officer shall be in writing. A copy of the designation shall be served on all parties.
(b) The presiding officer, for all hearings, shall be bound by all applicable laws and regulations.
