7 C.F.R. § 1146.208 — Confidentiality.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1146.208
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Milk), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1146.208
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1146 (Milk Donation Reimbursement Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

AMS will collect only that information deemed necessary to administer the Milk Donation Reimbursement Program and will use the information only for that purpose. AMS will keep all proprietary business information collected under the program confidential.
