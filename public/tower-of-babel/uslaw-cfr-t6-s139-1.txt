6 C.F.R. § 139.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:6:139.1
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security, Office Of The Secretary
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-6/section-139.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 6 (Domestic Security), Part 139 (Conduct On Federal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part provide for the protection and administration of the buildings, grounds, and property or portions thereof that are owned, occupied, or secured by the Federal Government (including any agency, instrumentality, or wholly owned or mixed-ownership corporation thereof) and the persons on the property.
