25 C.F.R. § 20.333 — How do I apply for Adult Care Assistance?
Citation: us:cfr:25:20.333
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-20.333
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 20 (Financial Assistance And Social Services Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To apply for adult care assistance, you or someone acting on your behalf must submit an application form to the social services worker.
