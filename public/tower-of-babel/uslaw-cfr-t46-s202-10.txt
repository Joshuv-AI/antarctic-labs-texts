46 C.F.R. § 202.10 — Petitions for reconsideration.
Citation: us:cfr:46:202.10
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-202.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 202 (Procedures Relating To Review By Secretary Of Transportation Of Actions By Maritime Subsidy Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Petitions for reconsideration of decisions by the Secretary in any case taken under review will be considered, upon a showing of good cause, if filed within ten (10) days of service of the Secretary's decision.
