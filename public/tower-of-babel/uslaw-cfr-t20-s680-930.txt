20 C.F.R. § 680.930 — What are needs-related payments?
Citation: us:cfr:20:680.930
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-680.930
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 680 (Adult And Dislocated Worker Activities Under Title I Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Needs-related payments provide financial assistance to participants for the purpose of enabling them to participate in training and are a supportive service authorized by WIOA sec. 134(d)(3). Unlike other supportive services, in order to qualify for needs-related payments a participant must be enrolled in training.
