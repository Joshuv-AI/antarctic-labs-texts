29 C.F.R. § 71.15 — Training.
Citation: us:cfr:29:71.15
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-71.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 71 (Protection Of Individual Privacy And Access To Records Under The Privacy Act Of 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All DOL systems managers, disclosure officers, and employees with responsibilities under the Privacy Act shall periodically attend training offered by the Department on the Privacy Act.
