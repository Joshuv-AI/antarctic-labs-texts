37 C.F.R. § 501.5 — Agency liaison officer.
Citation: us:cfr:37:501.5
Creator: National Institute Of Standards And Technology, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-501.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 501 (Uniform Patent Policy For Rights In Inventions Made By Government Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each Government agency shall designate a liaison officer to represent the agency before the Secretary; Provided, however, that the Departments of the Army, the Navy, and the Air Force may each designate a liaison officer.
