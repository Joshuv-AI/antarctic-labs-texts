33 C.F.R. § 80.753 — St. Petersburg, FL to the Anclote, FL.
Citation: us:cfr:33:80.753
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-80.753
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 80 (Colregs Demarcation Lines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A line drawn across Blind Pass, from the seaward extremity of the Long Key jetty to the seaward extremity of the Treasure Island jetty.
(b) Lines formed by the centerline of the highway bridges over Johns and Clearwater Passes.
(c) A line drawn across Dunedin and Hurricane Passes parallel with the general trend of the seaward, highwater shoreline.
(d) A line drawn from the northernmost extremity of Honeymoon Island to Anclote Anchorage South Entrance Light 3; thence to Anclote Key position latitude 28°10.0′ N longitude 082°50.6′ W; thence a straight line to position latitude 28°11.11′ N, longitude 082°47.91′ W.
