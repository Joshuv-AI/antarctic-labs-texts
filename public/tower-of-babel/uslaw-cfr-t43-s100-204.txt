43 C.F.R. § 100.204 — When must the tribe or individual Indian submit a package for Secretarial verification of appraiser qualifications?
Citation: us:cfr:43:100.204
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-100.204
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 100 (Waiving Departmental Review Of Appraisals And Valuations Of Indian Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The tribe or individual Indian must submit the package of appraiser qualifications to the Secretary with the appraisal or valuation.
