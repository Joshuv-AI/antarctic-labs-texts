14 C.F.R. § 296.6 — Public disclosure of cargo liability limits and insurance.
Citation: us:cfr:14:296.6
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-296.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 296 (Indirect Air Transportation Of Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every indirect cargo air carrier shall give notice in writing to the shipper, when any shipment is accepted, of the existence or absence of cargo liability accident insurance, and of the limits on the extent of its liability, if any. The notice shall be clear and conspicuously included on or attached to all of its rate sheets and airwaybills.
