33 C.F.R. § 80.760 — Horseshoe Point, FL to Rock Island, FL.
Citation: us:cfr:33:80.760
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-80.760
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 80 (Colregs Demarcation Lines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except inside lines specifically described provided in this section, the 72 COLREGS shall apply on the bays, bayous, creeks, marinas, and rivers from Horseshoe Point to the Rock Islands.
(b) A north-south line drawn through Steinhatchee River Light 21.
(c) A line drawn from Fenholloway River Approach Light FR east across the entrance to Fenholloway River.
