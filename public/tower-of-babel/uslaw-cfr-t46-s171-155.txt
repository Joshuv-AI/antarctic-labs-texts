46 C.F.R. § 171.155 — Drainage of an open boat.
Citation: us:cfr:46:171.155
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-171.155
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 171 (Special Rules Pertaining To Vessels Carrying Passengers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The deck within the hull of an open boat must drain to the bilge. Overboard drainage of the deck is not permitted.
