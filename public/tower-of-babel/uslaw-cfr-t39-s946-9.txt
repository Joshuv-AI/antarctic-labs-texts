39 C.F.R. § 946.9 — Reconsideration of claims.
Citation: us:cfr:39:946.9
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-946.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 946 (Rules Of Procedure Relating To The Disposition Of Stolen Mail Matter And Property Acquired By The Postal Inspection Service For Use As Evidence). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A written request for reconsideration of denied claims may be submitted within 10 days of the postmarked date of the mailing denying the claim. Such requests must be addressed to the Chief Postal Inspector or delegate and must be based on evidence recently developed or not previously presented.
