48 C.F.R. § 22.2108 — 22.2108 Multiemployer plans or other funds, plans, or programs.
Citation: us:cfr:48:22.2108
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-22.2108
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 22 (Application Of Labor Laws To Government Acquisitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contractors may fulfill their obligations under E.O. 13706 and 29 CFR part 13 jointly with other contractors through a multiemployer plan, or may fulfill their obligations through an individual fund, plan, or program (see 29 CFR 13.8).
