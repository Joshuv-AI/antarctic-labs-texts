14 C.F.R. § 258.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:14:258.1
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-258.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 258 (Disclosure Of Change-Of-Gauge Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to ensure that consumers are adequately informed before they book air transportation or embark on travel involving change-of-gauge services that these services require a change of aircraft en route.
