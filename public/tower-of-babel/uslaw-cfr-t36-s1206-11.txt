36 C.F.R. § 1206.11 — How may an applicant apply for an NHPRC grant?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1206.11
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1206.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1206 (National Historical Publications And Records Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applicants may apply for a grant using Grants.gov or by using other electronic or paper forms and documents, according to the instructions in each announcement.
