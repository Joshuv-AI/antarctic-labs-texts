FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Notice of Agreements Filed

    The Commission hereby gives notice of filing of the following 
agreements under the Shipping Act of 1984. Interested parties may 
submit comments, relevant information, or documents regarding the 
agreement to the Secretary by email at [email protected], or by mail, 
Federal Maritime Commission, 800 North Capitol Street, Washington, DC 
20573. Comments will be most helpful to the Commission if received 
within 12 days of the date this notice appears in the Federal Register, 
and the Commission requests that comments be submitted within 7 days on 
agreements that request expedited review. Copies of agreements are 
available through the Commission's website (www.fmc.gov) or by 
contacting the Office of General Counsel at (202)-523-5740 or 
[email protected].
    Agreement No.: 010761-002.
    Agreement Name: Somers Isles Shipping Agreement.
    Parties: Bermuda Container Line Ltd.; Bermuda International 
Shipping Limited; and Somers Isles Shipping Limited.
    Filing Party: Bryant Gardner, Winston & Strawn LLP.
    Synopsis: The Amendment updates corporate address of Somers Isles 
Shipping Limited and removes the authority set forth in Article 5(b)(6) 
to use common marshalling, terminal and other cargo-handling 
facilities, and other transportation facilities, which facilities may 
be owned, leased or licensed from the other parties or from third 
persons.
    Proposed Effective Date: 8/12/2026.
    Location: https://www2.fmc.gov/eAgreementsSP/Public/AgreementHistory/1180.
    Agreement No.: 201353-003.
    Agreement Name: THE Alliance/Evergreen Vessel Sharing Agreement.

[[Page 53415]]

    Parties: HMM Company Limited; Evergreen Marine Corporation (Taiwan) 
Ltd.; Ocean Network Express Pte. Ltd.; and Yang Ming Joint Service 
Agreement, FMC No. 201392.
    Filing Party: Joshua Stein, Cozen O'Connor.
    Synopsis: The Amendment updates the name of the Agreement to 
Premier Alliance/Evergreen Vessel Sharing Agreement and deletes Hapag-
Lloyd AG as a party to the Agreement.
    Proposed Effective Date: 8/13/2026.
    Location: https://www2.fmc.gov/eAgreementsSP/Public/AgreementHistory/39502.
