33 C.F.R. § 104.150 — Right to appeal.
Citation: us:cfr:33:104.150
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-104.150
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 104 (Maritime Security: Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person directly affected by a decision or action taken under this part, by or on behalf of the Coast Guard, may appeal as described in § 101.420 of this subchapter.
