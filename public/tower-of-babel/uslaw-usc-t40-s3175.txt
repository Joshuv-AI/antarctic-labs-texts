40 U.S.C. § 3175 — Acceptance of gifts of property
Citation: us:usc:40:3175
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section3175
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part A (General); Chapter 31 (General); Subchapter VI (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator of General Services, and the United States Postal Service where that office is concerned, may accept on behalf of the Federal Government unconditional gifts of property in aid of any project or function within their respective jurisdictions.
