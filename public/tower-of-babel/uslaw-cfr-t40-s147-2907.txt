40 C.F.R. § 147.2907 — Confidentiality of information.
Citation: us:cfr:40:147.2907
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-147.2907
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 147 (State, Tribal, And Epa-Administered Underground Injection Control Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The following information cannot be claimed confidential by the submitter:
(1) Name and address of permit applicant or permittee.
(2) Information concerning the existence, absence or level of contaminants in drinking water.
(b) Other information claimed as confidential will be processed in accordance with 40 CFR part 2.
