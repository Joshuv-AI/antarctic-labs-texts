15 U.S.C. § 9012 — Emergency rulemaking authority
Citation: us:usc:15:9012
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section9012
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 116 (Coronavirus Economic Stabilization (Cares Act)); Subchapter I (Keeping American Workers Paid And Employed). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 15 days after March 27, 2020, the Administrator shall issue regulations to carry out this title 1 and the amendments made by this title 1 without regard to the notice requirements under section 553(b) of title 5.
