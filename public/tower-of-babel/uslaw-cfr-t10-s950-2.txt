10 C.F.R. § 950.2 — Scope and applicability.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 950 (Standby Support For Certain Nuclear Plant Delays). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth the policies and procedures for the award and administration of Standby Support Contracts between the Department and sponsors of new advanced nuclear facilities.
