Tetrahedron Conjecture in the ℓ 2 \ell_{2} -norm
Levente Bodnár Wanfang Chen Jinghua Deng Jianfeng Hou Xizhi Liu Jialei Song Jiabao Yang Yixiao Zhang

Abstract
The famous Tetrahedron Conjecture of Turán from the 1940s asserts that the number of edges in an n n -vertex 3 3 -graph without the tetrahedron, the complete 3 3 -graph on four vertices, cannot exceed that of the balanced complete cyclic 3 3 -partite 3 3 -graph, whose edges are of types V 1 ​ V 2 ​ V 3 V_{1}V_{2}V_{3} , V 1 ​ V 1 ​ V 2 V_{1}V_{1}V_{2} , V 2 ​ V 2 ​ V 3 V_{2}V_{2}V_{3} , and V 3 ​ V 3 ​ V 1 V_{3}V_{3}V_{1} . A recent surprising result of Balogh–Clemen–Lidický [J. Lond. Math. Soc. (2) 106 (2022)] shows that this conjecture is asymptotically true in the ℓ 2 \ell_{2} -norm, where the number of edges is replaced by the sum of squared codegrees. They further conjectured that, in this ℓ 2 \ell_{2} -norm setting, the 3 3 -partite construction is uniquely extremal for large n n . We confirm this conjecture. Two key ingredients in our proofs include establishing a Mantel theorem for vertex-colored graphs that forbid certain types of triangles, and introducing a novel procedure integrated into Simonovits’ stability method, which essentially reduces the task to verifying that the ℓ 2 \ell_{2} -norm of certain near-extremal constructions increases under suitable local modifications. The strategy in the latter may be of independent interest and potentially applicable to other extremal problems.

Given an
r
-graph
\mathcal{H}
on a vertex set
V
, the
codegree
of an
(r-1)
-subset
S\subseteq V
in
\mathcal{H}

containing

An simple double-counting argument shows that
\sum_{e\in\binom{V}{r-1}}d_{\mathcal{H}}(e)=r|\mathcal{H}|
.
Balogh–Clemen–Lidický viewed the summation on the left side of the equality as the
\ell_{1}
-norm of
\mathcal{H}
, and they further introduced the
\ell_{2}
-norm of

\displaystyle\lVert\mathcal{H}\rVert_{2}\coloneqq\sum_{e\in\binom{V}{r-1}}d_{\mathcal{H}}^{2}(e).

The Turán number of
\mathcal{F}
in the
\ell_{2}
-norm is then defined as

\displaystyle\mathrm{ex}_{\ell_{2}}(n,\mathcal{F})\coloneqq\max\left\{\lVert\mathcal{H}\rVert_{2}\colon\text{$v(\mathcal{H})=n$ and $\mathcal{H}$ is $\mathcal{F}$-free}\right\}.

The Turán density of
\mathcal{F}
in the
\ell_{2}
-norm is given by

\displaystyle\pi_{\ell_{2}}(\mathcal{F})\coloneqq\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{\mathrm{ex}_{\ell_{2}}(n,\mathcal{F})}{\binom{n}{r-1}(n-r+1)^{2}}.

Turán problems under the

-norm were systematically studied by Balogh–Clemen–Lidický in
[

]
, and later extended to the more general

-norms as well as to degenerate hypergraphs in
[

GLMP25
]

The construction

shows that

Using Razborov’s flag algebra framework, Balogh–Clemen–Lidický

]
proved, with computer assistance, that

and thus established the following theorem.

Theorem 1.1

(
[

, Theorem 1.2]
)

We have

They further conjectured (see

-norm, in contrast to the

-norm setting, where exponentially many extremal constructions may exist. Our main result confirms this conjecture.

Theorem 1.2
.

There exists

Suppose that
\mathcal{H}

In Section

-norm (Theorem

) and the

Although the

In Section

2
Mantel’s theorem in vertex-colored graphs

In this section, we consider a variation of Mantel’s theorem in vertex-colored graphs.

Let
G
be a graph on vertex set
V
, and let
V\coloneqq V_{1}\cup V_{2}\cup V_{3}
be a partition of
V
(equivalently, a
3
-coloring of the vertices of
G
).
For a triple of vertices
\{u,v,w\}\subseteq V
, we say that
G[\{u,v,w\}]
is
a triangle of type
V_{i}V_{j}V_{k}
for some
(i,j,k)\in[3]^{3}
if
\{u,v,w\}
induces a copy of
K_{3}
in
G
and
(u,v,w)\in V_{i}\times V_{j}\times V_{k}
.
Denote by
\rho_{3}(G)
the density of triangles of types
V_{1}V_{2}V_{3}
,
V_{1}V_{1}V_{2}
,
V_{2}V_{2}V_{3}
, and
V_{3}V_{3}V_{1}
in
G
, that is, the probability that a uniformly chosen
3
-subset of
V
induces a triangle of one of these types. We say that
G
is
cyclically triangle-free
if
\rho_{3}(G)=0
.

For a vertex set
S\subseteq V
, we use
G[S]
to denote the
induced subgraph
of
G
on
S
.
For pairwise disjoint sets
S_{1},\ldots,S_{k}\subseteq V
, we use
G[S_{1},\ldots,S_{k}]
to denote the
induced
k
-partite subgraph
of
G
with parts
S_{1},\ldots,S_{k}
.

Let
\Lambda[V_{1},V_{2},V_{3}]
denote the graph on vertex set
V
whose edge set is

\displaystyle\left\{e\in\tbinom{V}{2}\colon(|e\cap V_{1}|,|e\cap V_{2}|,|e\cap V_{3}|)\in\{(1,1,0),(0,1,1),(0,0,2)\}\right\}.

Observe that
\Lambda[V_{1},V_{2},V_{3}]
is cyclically triangle-free.
Suppose that
|V_{1}|=|V_{2}|=|V_{3}|=n
. Then straightforward calculations show that

\displaystyle|\Lambda[V_{1},V_{2},V_{3}]|

\displaystyle=2n^{2}+\binom{n}{2}=\frac{5}{2}n^{2}-\frac{n}{2},

\displaystyle\lVert\Lambda[V_{1},V_{2},V_{3}]\rVert_{2}

\displaystyle=n\cdot n^{2}+n\cdot(2n)^{2}+n\cdot(2n-1)^{2}=9n^{3}-4n^{2}+n.

Throughout this section, we assume that
G
is a graph with vertex set
V
, and that
V=V_{1}\cup V_{2}\cup V_{3}
is a fixed partition. We further assume that all indices are taken modulo
3
.

The main results of this section are as follows.

Theorem 2.1
.

For every
\varepsilon>0
, there exist
\delta_{\ref{THM:3-colored-Mantel-L1-norm}}=\delta_{\ref{THM:3-colored-Mantel-L1-norm}}(\varepsilon)>0
and
N_{\ref{THM:3-colored-Mantel-L1-norm}}=N_{\ref{THM:3-colored-Mantel-L1-norm}}(\varepsilon)
such that the following holds for

.
Suppose that

\rho_{3}(G)\leq\delta_{\ref{THM:3-colored-Mantel-L1-norm}}

Then

\displaystyle|G|\leq\frac{5}{2}n^{2}+\varepsilon n^{2}.

We will also use the following stability theorem.

2.3
Proof of Theorem

In this section, we present the proof of Theorem
2.3
, derived by computer using the flag algebra method of Razborov
[
Raz07
]
, which is also described in e.g.
[
Raz10
,
BT11
,
dCSdOFS16
,
GGH+22
]
.
Since this method is well-known by now, we will be very brief. In particular, we omit many definitions, referring
the reader to
[
Raz07
,
Raz10
]
for any missing notions. Roughly speaking, a
flag algebra proof using
0
-flags on
m
vertices
of an upper bound
u\in\mathbb{R}
on the given objective function
f
consists of an identity

\displaystyle u-f(\mathcal{H})=\mathrm{SOS}+\sum_{F\in\mathcal{F}_{m}^{0}}c_{F}\cdot p(F,\mathcal{H})+o(1),

which is asymptotically true for any admissible
\mathcal{H}
with
|V(\mathcal{H})|\to\infty
, where the
\mathrm{SOS}
-term can be represented as a sum of squares (as described e.g. in
[
Raz10
, Section 3]
), each coefficient
c_{F}\in\mathbb{R}
is non-negative, and
\mathcal{F}_{m}^{0}
consists of isomorphism types of
0
-flags
(unlabeled
3
-graphs) with
m
vertices. If
f(\mathcal{H})
can be represented as a linear combination of the densities of members of
\mathcal{F}_{m}^{0}
in
\mathcal{H}
then finding the smallest possible
u
amounts to solving a semi-definite program (SDP) with
|\mathcal{F}_{m}^{0}|
linear constraints (so we write the size of
\mathcal{F}_{m}^{0}
in each case to give the reader some idea of the size of the programs that we had to solve).

We formed the corresponding SDPs and then analyzed the solutions returned by computer, using a modified version of the SageMath package, which can be found in the GitHub repository
https://github.com/bodnalev/sage
.
The script together with the certificate can be found at
https://github.com/xliu2022/xliu2022.github.io/tree/main/FlagAlgebra_Certificate/ThreeColored-Mantel-L2norm
.

.

\displaystyle\frac{x_{i}x_{i+1}\left(2\left(x_{i}+x_{i+2}\right)^{2}+x_{i}x_{i+1}\right)}{2}n^{4}

Phase Two

Let

Claim 3.16

4
Concluding remarks

Let
\mathbb{S}_{2}
denote the unique
3
-graph on
4
vertices with
2
edges.
Let
\mathrm{N}(\mathbb{S}_{2},\mathcal{H})
denote the number of copies of
\mathbb{S}_{2}
in a
3
-graph
\mathcal{H}
.
By definition, for every
3
-graph
\mathcal{H}
,

\displaystyle\lVert\mathcal{H}\rVert_{2}=\sum_{e\in\binom{V(\mathcal{H})}{2}}d_{\mathcal{H}}^{2}(e)=\sum_{e\in\binom{V(\mathcal{H})}{2}}\left(2\tbinom{d_{\mathcal{H}}(e)}{2}+d_{\mathcal{H}}(e)\right)=2\mathrm{N}(\mathbb{S}_{2},\mathcal{H})+3|\mathcal{H}|.

Thus, Theorem
1.2
implies that, for large
n
, the maximum of
\mathbb{N}(\mathbb{S}_{2},\mathcal{H})+\frac{3}{2}|\mathcal{H}|
over all
n
-vertex
K_{4}^{3}
-free
3
-graphs
\mathcal{H}
is uniquely attained by
\mathbb{C}_{n}
.
A slight modification of our proof shows that
3/2
can be replaced by any constant.

Theorem 4.1
.

For every real number
\alpha
, there exists
N_{0}=N_{0}(\alpha)
such that the following holds for every
n\geq N_{0}
.
Suppose that
\mathcal{H}
is an
n
-vertex
K_{4}^{3}
-free
3
-graph.
Then

\displaystyle\mathbb{N}(\mathbb{S}_{2},\mathcal{H})+\alpha|\mathcal{H}|\leq\mathbb{N}(\mathbb{S}_{2},\mathbb{C}_{n})+\alpha|\mathbb{C}_{n}|,

with equality holds iff
\mathcal{H}\cong\mathbb{C}_{n}
.

In particular, the case
\alpha=0
establishes exact bound for the generalized Turán number
\mathrm{ex}(n,\mathbb{S}_{2},K_{4}^{3})
for large
n
.

Given two disjoint sets
V_{1}
and
V_{2}
, let
\mathbb{B}[V_{1},V_{2}]
denote the
3
-graph on
V_{1}\cup V_{2}
consisting of all triples of types
V_{1}V_{1}V_{2}
and
V_{2}V_{2}V_{1}
(see Figure
1
), that is,

\displaystyle\mathbb{B}[V_{1},V_{2}]\coloneqq\left\{e\colon\big(|e\cap V_{1}|,|e\cap V_{2}|\big)\in\{(2,1),(1,2)\}\right\}.

When
V_{1}\cup V_{2}=[n]
is a balanced partition of
[n]
, we write
\mathbb{B}_{n}
for
\mathbb{B}[V_{1},V_{2}]
.

The construction
\mathbb{B}_{n}
does not contain the complete
3
-graph on five vertices
K_{5}^{3}
.
It follows that
\pi_{\ell_{2}}(K_{5}^{3})\geq\frac{5}{8}
.
Using computer-assisted flag algebra computations, Balogh–Clemen–Lidický
[
BCL22b
]
proved that
\pi_{\ell_{2}}(K_{5}^{3})\leq\frac{5}{8}
, and thus,
\pi_{\ell_{2}}(K_{5}^{3})=\frac{5}{8}
.
They further conjectured (see
[
BCL22b
, Conjecture 1.4]
) that for large
n
, the construction
\mathbb{B}_{n}
is the unique extremal construction for the Turán problem of

in the
\ell_{2}
-norm.
We will confirm this conjecture along with a special case of a related conjecture of Frankl–Gryaznov–Talebanfard
[
FGT22
]
in a forthcoming work, after establishing a Turán theorem in vertex-colored graphs.
