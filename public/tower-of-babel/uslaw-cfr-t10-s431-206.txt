10 C.F.R. § 431.206 — Energy conservation standards and their effective dates.
Citation: us:cfr:10:431.206
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-431.206
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 431 (Energy Efficiency Program For Certain Commercial And Industrial Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An illuminated exit sign manufactured on or after January 1, 2006, shall have an input power demand of 5 watts or less per face.
