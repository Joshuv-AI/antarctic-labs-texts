50 C.F.R. § 12.1 — What is the purpose of the regulations in this part?
Citation: us:cfr:50:12.1
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-12.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 12 (Seizure And Forfeiture Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These regulations provide procedures that govern the seizure and administrative forfeiture or abandonment of property, as well as the disposal of such property, and the recovery of costs associated with handling and storage of seized property under various laws enforced by the Service.
