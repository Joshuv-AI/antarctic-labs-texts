5 C.F.R. § 294.101 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:5:294.101
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-294.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 294 (Availability Of Official Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart contains the regulations of the Office of Personnel Management (OPM) implementing the Freedom of Information Act (FOIA), 5 U.S.C. 552. Except as provided by § 294.105, OPM will use the provisions of this subpart to process all requests for records.
