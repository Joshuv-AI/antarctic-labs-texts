22 C.F.R. § 1414.7 — Solicitation of prohibited communications.
Citation: us:cfr:22:1414.7
Creator: Foreign Service Labor Relations Board; Federal Labor Relations Authority; General Counsel Of The Federal Labor Relations Authority; And The Foreign Service Impasse Disputes Panel
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1414.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1414 (Ex Parte Communications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person shall knowingly and willfully solicit the making of an unauthorized ex parte communication by any other person.
