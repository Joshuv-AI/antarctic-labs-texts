5 U.S.C. § 418 — Rule of construction of special provisions
Citation: us:usc:5:418
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section418
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part I (The Agencies Generally); Chapter 4 (Inspectors General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The special provisions under section 408, 409, 410, 411, 412, 413, 414, 416, or 421 of this title relate only to the establishment named in such section and no inference shall be drawn from the presence or absence of a provision in any such section with respect to an establishment not named in such section or with respect to a designated Federal entity as defined under section 415(a) of this title.
