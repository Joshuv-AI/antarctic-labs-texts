20 C.F.R. § 404.1323 — Post-World War II service excluded.
Citation: us:cfr:20:404.1323
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-404.1323
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 404 (Federal Old-Age, Survivors And Disability Insurance (1950- )). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Your service was not in the active service of the United States during the post-World War II period if, for example, you were in the—
(a) Coast Guard Auxiliary;
(b) Coast Guard Reserve (Temporary) unless you served on active full-time service with military pay and allowances;
(c) Civil Air Patrol; or
(d) Civilian Auxiliary to the Military Police.
