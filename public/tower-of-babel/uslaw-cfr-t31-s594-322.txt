31 C.F.R. § 594.322 — Arms or related materiel.
Citation: us:cfr:31:594.322
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-594.322
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 594 (Global Terrorism Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term arms or related materiel means:
(a) Nuclear, biological, chemical, or radiological weapons or materials or components of such weapons;
(b) Ballistic or cruise missile weapons or materials or components of such weapons; or
(c) Destabilizing numbers and types of advanced conventional weapons.
