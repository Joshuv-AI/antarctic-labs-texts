17 C.F.R. § 403.3 — Use of customers' free credit balances.
Citation: us:cfr:17:403.3
Creator: Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-403.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 403 (Protection Of Customer Securities And Balances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every registered government securities broker or dealer shall comply with the requirement of § 240.15c3-2 of this title concerning the use of customer free credit balances. For purposes of this section, all references to “broker or dealer” in § 240.15c3-2 shall include government securities brokers and dealers.
