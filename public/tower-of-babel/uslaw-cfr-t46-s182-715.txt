46 C.F.R. § 182.715 — Piping subject to more than 1,034 kPa (150 psig) in non-vital systems.
Citation: us:cfr:46:182.715
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-182.715
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 182 (Machinery Installation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Piping subject to more than 1,034 kPa (150 psig) in a non-vital system must be designed, fabricated, and inspected in accordance with the principles of ANSI B 31.1, or other industry standard acceptable to the Commandant.
