22 U.S.C. § 3354 — Use of Special Defense Acquisition Fund
Citation: us:usc:22:3354
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section3354
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 48A (Taiwan Enhanced Resilience); Subchapter I (Implementation Of An Enhanced Defense Partnership Between The United States And Taiwan). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Defense, in consultation with the Secretary of State, shall seek to utilize the Special Defense Acquisition Fund established under chapter 5 of the Arms Export Control Act (22 U.S.C. 2795 et seq.) to expedite the procurement and delivery of defense articles and defense services for the purpose of assisting and supporting the armed forces of Taiwan.
