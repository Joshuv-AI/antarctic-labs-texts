43 C.F.R. § 2742.1 — Lands subject to disposition.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2742.1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2742.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2740 (Recreation And Public Purposes Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Omitted lands and unsurveyed islands may be conveyed to States and their local political subdivisions under the provisions of section 211 of the Federal Land Policy and Management Act (43 U.S.C. 1721).
