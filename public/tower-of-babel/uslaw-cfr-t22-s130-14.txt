22 C.F.R. § 130.14 — Recordkeeping.
Citation: us:cfr:22:130.14
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-130.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 130 (Political Contributions, Fees And Commissions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each applicant, supplier and vendor must maintain a record of any information it was required to furnish or obtain under this part and all records upon which its reports are based for a period of not less than five years following the date of the report to which they pertain.
