36 U.S.C. § 146 — National Atomic Veterans Day
Citation: us:usc:36:146
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section146
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part A (Observances And Ceremonies); Chapter 1 (Patriotic And National Observances). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President shall issue each year a proclamation calling on the people of the United States to—
(1)
observe Atomic Veterans Day with appropriate ceremonies and activities; and
(2)
remember and honor the atomic veterans of the United States whose brave service and sacrifice played an important role in the defense of the Nation.
