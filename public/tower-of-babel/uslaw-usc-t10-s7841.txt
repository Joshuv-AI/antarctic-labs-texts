10 U.S.C. § 7841 — Payment of small amounts to public creditors
Citation: us:usc:10:7841
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7841
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Procurement); Chapter 783 (Accountability And Responsibility). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When authorized by the Secretary of the Army, a disbursing official of Army subsistence funds may keep a limited amount of those funds in the personal possession and at the risk of the disbursing official to pay small amounts to public creditors.
