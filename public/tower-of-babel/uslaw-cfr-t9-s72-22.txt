9 C.F.R. § 72.22 — Cars, vehicles, and premises; cleaning and treatment after containing infested or exposed animals.
Citation: us:cfr:9:72.22
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-72.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 72 (Bovine Babesiosis). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Cars and other vehicles, and yards, pens, chutes, or other premises or facilities, which have contained interstate shipments of animals infested with or exposed to ticks, shall be cleaned and treated within 72 hours of use and prior to further use in the required concentration with a permitted dip listed in § 72.13 under supervision of a State or Federal inspector or an accredited veterinarian.
