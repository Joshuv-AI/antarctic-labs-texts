45 C.F.R. § 172.602 — Basis for appeal by QHIN or Applicant QHIN.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 172 (Trusted Exchange Framework And Common Agreement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An Applicant QHIN or QHIN may appeal the following decisions to ASTP/ONC or a hearing officer, as appropriate:
(1) Applicant QHIN. An Applicant QHIN may appeal a denial of its QHIN application.
(2) QHIN. A QHIN may appeal:
(i) A decision to suspend the QHIN or to instruct the QHIN to suspend its Participant or Subparticipant.
(ii) A decision to terminate the QHIN's Common Agreement.
