49 C.F.R. § 262.17 — Combining grant awards.
Citation: us:cfr:49:262.17
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-262.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 262 (Implementation Of Program For Capital Grants For Rail Line Relocation And Improvement Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Two or more States, but not political subdivisions of States, may, pursuant to an agreement entered into by the States, combine any part of the amounts provided through grants for a project under this section provided:
(1) The project will benefit each of the States entering into the agreement; and
(2) The agreement is not a violation of the law of any such State.
