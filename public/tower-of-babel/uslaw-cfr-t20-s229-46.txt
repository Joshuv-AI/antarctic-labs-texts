20 C.F.R. § 229.46 — Spouse or divorced spouse benefit.
Citation: us:cfr:20:229.46
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-229.46
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 229 (Social Security Overall Minimum Guarantee). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a spouse or divorced spouse is included in the computation of the overall minimum, a benefit of 50 percent times the Overall Minimum PIA is computed. In the case of a spouse, the benefit may be adjusted for the family maximum, age, or other benefits. In the case of a divorced spouse, the benefit may be adjusted only for age or other benefits.
