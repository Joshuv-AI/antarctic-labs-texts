Revised note on surface-link of trivial components
Akio Kawauchi

Abstract
In a previous note, it is claimed that every surface-link consisting of trivial components and having at most one non-sphere component is a ribbon surface-link, but it was false. In this revised note, this claim is replaced by the claim that a surface-link L L with trivial components is a ribbon surface-link if and only if the surface-link obtained from L L by every fusion is a ribbon surface-link if and only if the surface-link obtained from L L by any one fusion is a ribbon surface-link. For any closed oriented disconnected surface 𝐅 {\mathbf{F}} containing at least two non-sphere components, there is a pair of a ribbon 𝐅 {\mathbf{F}} -link L L consisting of trivial components and a non-ribbon 𝐅 {\mathbf{F}} -link L ′ L^{\prime} consisting of trivial components such that the fundamental groups of L L and L ′ L^{\prime} are the same group up to meridian-preserving isomorphisms and the pair of the 𝐅 ′ {\mathbf{F}}^{\prime} -links K K and K ′ K^{\prime} obtained from L L and L ′ L^{\prime} by every corresponding fusion is a pair of a ribbon surface-link and a non-ribbon surface-link such that the fundamental groups of K K and K ′ K^{\prime} are the same group up to meridian-preserving isomorphisms. Keywords : Ribbon, Non-ribbon, Surface-link, Fusion. Mathematics Subject Classification 2010 : Primary 57Q45; Secondary 57N13

Theorem 1.
The following statements (1)-(3) on a surface-link

(2) The surface-link obtained from

(3) The surface-link obtained from

Proof of Theorem 1.

Since a surface-link obtained from

This completes the proof of Theorem 1.
