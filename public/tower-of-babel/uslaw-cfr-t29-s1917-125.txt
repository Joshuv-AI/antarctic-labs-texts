29 C.F.R. § 1917.125 — Guarding temporary hazards.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1917.125
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1917.125
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1917 (Marine Terminals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Ditches, pits, excavations and surfaces in poor repair shall be guarded by readily visible barricades, rails or other equally effective means.
