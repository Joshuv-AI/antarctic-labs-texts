41 C.F.R. § 105-9.109 — -9.109 Affirmative action by recipient.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-9.109
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-9.109
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-9 (9—Discrimination Prohibited On The Basis Of Age). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Even in the absence of a finding of age discrimination, a recipient may take affirmative action to overcome the effects resulting in limited participation in the recipient's program or activity.
