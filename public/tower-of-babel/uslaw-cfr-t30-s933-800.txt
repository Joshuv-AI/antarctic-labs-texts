30 C.F.R. § 933.800 — General requirements for bonding of surface coal mining and reclamation operations.
Citation: us:cfr:30:933.800
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-933.800
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 933 (North Carolina). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Part 800 of this chapter, General Requirements for Bonding of Surface Coal Mining and Reclamation Operations Under Regulatory Programs, shall apply to all surface coal mining and reclamation operations.
