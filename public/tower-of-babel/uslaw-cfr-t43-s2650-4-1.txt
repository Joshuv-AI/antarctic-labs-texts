43 C.F.R. § 2650.4-1 — -1 Existing rights and contracts.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2650.4-1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2650.4-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2650 (Alaska Native Selections). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any conveyance issued for surface and subsurface rights under this act will be subject to any lease, contract, permit, right-of-way, or easement and the rights of the lessee, contractee, permittee, or grantee to the complete enjoyment of all rights, privileges, and benefits thereby granted him.
