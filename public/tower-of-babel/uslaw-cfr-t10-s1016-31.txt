10 C.F.R. § 1016.31 — Inspections.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1016.31
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1016.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1016 (Safeguarding Of Restricted Data By Access Permittees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The DOE shall make such inspections and surveys of the premises, activities, records, and procedures of any person subject to the regulations in this part as DOE deems necessary to effectuate the purposes of the Act, Executive Order 13526, and DOE orders and procedures.
