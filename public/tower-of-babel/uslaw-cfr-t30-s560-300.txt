30 C.F.R. § 560.300 — Operating allowances.
Citation: us:cfr:30:560.300
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-560.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 560 (Outer Continental Shelf Oil And Gas Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notwithstanding any other provision in the regulations in this part, BOEM may issue a lease containing an operating allowance when so specified in the final notice of sale and the lease. The allowance amount or formula will be specified in the final notice of sale and in the lease.
