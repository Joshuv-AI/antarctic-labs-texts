47 C.F.R. § 101.1513 — License term.
Citation: us:cfr:47:101.1513
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-101.1513
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 101 (Fixed Microwave Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The license term is ten years, beginning on the date of the initial authorization (nationwide license) grant. Registering links will not change the overall renewal period of the license.
