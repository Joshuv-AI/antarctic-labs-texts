DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Foreign-Trade Zones Board

[B-23-2025]


Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) 196; Authorization of Production 
Activity; RECARO Aircraft Seating Americas, LLC; (Aircraft Seats); Fort 
Worth, Texas

    On April 7, 2025, RECARO Aircraft Seating Americas, LLC submitted a 
notification of proposed production activity to the FTZ Board for its 
facilities within FTZ 196, in Fort Worth, Texas.
    The notification was processed in accordance with the regulations 
of the FTZ Board (15 CFR part 400), including notice in the Federal 
Register inviting public comment (90 FR 15434, April 11, 2025). On 
March 5, 2026, the applicant was notified of the FTZ Board's decision 
that no further review of the activity is warranted at this time. The 
production activity described in the notification was authorized, 
subject to the FTZ Act and the FTZ Board's regulations, including 
section 400.14.

    Dated: March 5, 2026.
Elizabeth Whiteman,
Executive Secretary.
