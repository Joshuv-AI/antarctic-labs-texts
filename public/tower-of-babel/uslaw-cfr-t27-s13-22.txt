27 C.F.R. § 13.22 — Withdrawal of applications.
Citation: us:cfr:27:13.22
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-13.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 13 (Labeling Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person who has filed an application for a certificate of label approval, certificate of exemption from label approval, or distinctive liquor bottle approval, may withdraw such application at any time before TTB takes action on the application.
