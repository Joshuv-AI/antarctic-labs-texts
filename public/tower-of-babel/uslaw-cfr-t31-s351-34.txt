31 C.F.R. § 351.34 — What are the maturity periods of Series EE bonds with issue dates of May 1, 2005, or thereafter?
Citation: us:cfr:31:351.34
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-351.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 351 (Offering Of United States Savings Bonds, Series Ee). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Original maturity. Bonds reach original maturity at 20 years after the issue date.
(b) Final maturity. Bonds reach final maturity at 30 years after the issue date. Bonds cease to earn interest at final maturity.
