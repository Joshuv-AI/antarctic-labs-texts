25 C.F.R. § 11.108 — How are tribal ordinances affected by this part?
Citation: us:cfr:25:11.108
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-11.108
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 11 (Courts Of Indian Offenses And Law And Order Code). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The governing body of each tribe occupying the Indian country over which a Court of Indian Offenses has jurisdiction may enact ordinances which, when approved by the Assistant Secretary—Indian Affairs or his or her designee:
(a) Are enforceable in the Court of Indian Offenses having jurisdiction over the Indian country occupied by that tribe; and
(b) Supersede any conflicting regulation in this part.
