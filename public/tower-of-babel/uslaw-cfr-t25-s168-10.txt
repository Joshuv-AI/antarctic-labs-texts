25 C.F.R. § 168.10 — Conservation and land use provisions.
Citation: us:cfr:25:168.10
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-168.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 168 (Grazing Regulations For The Hopi Partitioned Lands Area). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Grazing operations shall be conducted in accordance with recognized principles of good range management. Conservation management plans necessary to accomplish this will be made a part of the grazing permit by stipulation.
