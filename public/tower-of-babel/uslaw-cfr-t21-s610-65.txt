21 C.F.R. § 610.65 — Products for export.
Citation: us:cfr:21:610.65
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-610.65
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 610 (General Biological Products Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Labels on packages or containers of products for export may be adapted to meet specific requirements of the regulations of the country to which the product is to be exported provided that in all such cases the minimum label requirements prescribed in § 610.60 are observed.
