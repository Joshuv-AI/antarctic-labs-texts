Improving learning outcomes through simulation-based teaching in Ayurveda education
Authors: Gaurav Sawarkar
Published: 2024

Dear Editor,

Simulation-based teaching is becoming increasingly vital in Ayurveda education, particularly as the healthcare landscape evolves. This method offers a safe, interactive environment where students can practice and hone clinical skills without risk to patients, which is essential for building competence in Ayurvedic practices.[1] It enables exposure to various clinical situations, including rare and complex cases, enhancing students’ knowledge and decision-making skills for handling diverse patient scenarios confidently.[2]

Active learning is a cornerstone of simulation-based education, involving students in immersive experiences that allow for the application of knowledge, teamwork, and instant feedback. This approach helps deepen their understanding of Ayurvedic principles and practices.[3] Moreover, it aligns with evidence-based practice by integrating modern technologies and methods with traditional teachings, thus preparing graduates to meet contemporary healthcare demands effectively.[45]

In light of these benefits, it is crucial for educational institutions and policymakers to prioritize and expand the use of simulation-based methods in Ayurveda training. Such strategic investments will cultivate a new generation of practitioners skilled in clinical applications and equipped to address the challenges of modern healthcare environments.

Simulation-based teaching promotes health, focusing on preventative care and holistic health management. By simulating diverse clinical scenarios, it enables students to practice and master a range of Ayurvedic treatments and diagnostic skills safely. This proactive learning approach empowers students with the knowledge and skills to effectively manage health, emphasizing preventive measures and promoting long-term wellness in patient care.

Novelty of the study

This study introduces simulation-based teaching as an essential innovation in Ayurveda education. It provides a risk-free, interactive learning environment where students can refine their clinical skills and decision-making through exposure to a wide array of clinical scenarios, including rare and complex conditions. This method enhances traditional Ayurvedic training with modern educational technologies, preparing students to meet the evolving demands of healthcare effectively.
