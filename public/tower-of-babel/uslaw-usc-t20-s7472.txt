20 U.S.C. § 7472 — Peer review
Citation: us:usc:20:7472
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section7472
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 70 (Strengthening And Improvement Of Elementary And Secondary Schools); Subchapter VI (Indian, Native Hawaiian, And Alaska Native Education); Part A (Indian Education); Subpart 4 (Federal Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may use a peer review process to review applications submitted to the Secretary under subpart 2 or subpart 3 of this part.
