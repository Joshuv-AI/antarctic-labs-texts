36 C.F.R. § 272.2 — Use of official campaign materials.
Citation: us:cfr:36:272.2
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-272.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 272 (Use Of “Woodsy Owl” Symbol). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Official materials produced for the Woodsy Owl campaign may be used without express approval from the Chief of the Forest Service where such use is solely for the purpose of increasing public knowledge about wise use of the environment and programs which foster maintenance and improvement of environmental quality.
