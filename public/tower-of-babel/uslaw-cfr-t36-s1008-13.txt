36 C.F.R. § 1008.13 — Requests for access to records.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1008.13
Creator: Presidio Trust
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1008.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1008 (Requests Under The Privacy Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Privacy Act permits individuals, upon request, to gain access to their records or to any information pertaining to them which is contained in a system and to review the records and have a copy made of all or any portion thereof in a form comprehensive to them. 5 U.S.C. 552a(d)(1). A request for access shall be submitted in accordance with the procedures in this part.
