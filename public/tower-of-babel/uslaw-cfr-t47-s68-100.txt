47 C.F.R. § 68.100 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:47:68.100
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-68.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 68 (Connection Of Terminal Equipment To The Telephone Network). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In accordance with the rules and regulations in this part, terminal equipment may be directly connected to the public switched telephone network, including private line services provided over wireline facilities that are owned by providers of wireline telecommunications.
