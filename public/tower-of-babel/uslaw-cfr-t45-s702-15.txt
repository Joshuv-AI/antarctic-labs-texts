45 C.F.R. § 702.15 — Witness fees.
Citation: us:cfr:45:702.15
Creator: Commission On Civil Rights
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-702.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 702 (Rules On Hearings, Reports, And Meetings Of The Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A witness attending any session of the Commission shall be paid the same fees and mileage that are paid witnesses in the courts of the United States. Mileage payments must be tendered at the witness' request upon service of a subpoena issued on behalf of the Commission or any subcommittee thereof.
