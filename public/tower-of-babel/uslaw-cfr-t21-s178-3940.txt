21 C.F.R. § 178.3940 — Tetraethylene glycol di-(2-ethylhexoate).
Citation: us:cfr:21:178.3940
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-178.3940
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 178 (Indirect Food Additives: Adjuvants, Production Aids, And Sanitizers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Tetraethylene glycol di-(2-ethylhexoate) containing not more than 22 parts per million ethylene and/or diethylene glycols may be used at a level not to exceed 0.7 percent by weight of twine as a finish on twine to be used for tying meat provided the twine fibers are produced from nylon resins complying with § 177.1500 of this chapter.
