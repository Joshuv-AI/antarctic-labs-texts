33 C.F.R. § 149.110 — What are the requirements for pipeline end manifold shutoff valves?
Citation: us:cfr:33:149.110
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-149.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 149 (Deepwater Ports: Design, Construction, And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each pipeline end manifold must have a shutoff valve capable of operating both manually and from the pumping platform complex.
