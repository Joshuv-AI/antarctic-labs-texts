10 C.F.R. § 15.11 — Form of payment.
Citation: us:cfr:10:15.11
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-15.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 15 (Debt Collection Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These procedures are directed primarily to the recovery of money on behalf of the Government. The NRC may demand:
(a) The return of specific property; or
(b) The performance of specific services.
