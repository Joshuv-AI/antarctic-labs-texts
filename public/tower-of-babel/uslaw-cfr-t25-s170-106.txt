25 C.F.R. § 170.106 — Should planning organizations and local governments consult with Tribes when planning for transportation projects?
Citation: us:cfr:25:170.106
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-170.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 170 (Tribal Transportation Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. When planning for transportation projects, planning organizations and local governments should consult with Tribes in the area where the project is located.
