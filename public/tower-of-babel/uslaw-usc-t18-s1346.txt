18 U.S.C. § 1346 — Definition of “scheme or artifice to defraud”
Citation: us:usc:18:1346
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1346
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 63 (Mail Fraud And Other Fraud Offenses). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purposes of this chapter, the term “scheme or artifice to defraud” includes a scheme or artifice to deprive another of the intangible right of honest services.
