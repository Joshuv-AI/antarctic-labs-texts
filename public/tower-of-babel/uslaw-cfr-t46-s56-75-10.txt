46 C.F.R. § 56.75-10 — -10 Joint clearance.
Citation: us:cfr:46:56.75-10
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-56.75-10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 56 (Piping Systems And Appurtenances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The clearance between surfaces to be joined must be no larger than is necessary to allow complete capillary distribution of the brazing alloy or solder.
