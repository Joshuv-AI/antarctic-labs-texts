46 C.F.R. § 119.400 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:46:119.400
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-119.400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 119 (Machinery Installation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) This subpart applies to all propulsion and auxiliary machinery installations of the internal combustion piston type.
(b) Where no specific fuel designation exists, the requirements of this subpart are applicable to all types of fuels and machinery.
