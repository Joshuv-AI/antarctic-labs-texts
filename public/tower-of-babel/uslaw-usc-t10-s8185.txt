10 U.S.C. § 8185 — Nautical Schools: detail of naval officers as superintendents or instructors
Citation: us:usc:10:8185
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8185
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 823 (Special Assignments And Details). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may detail officers of the Navy as superintendents or instructors of institutions receiving benefits under chapter 515 of title 46 when in his opinion it can be done without detriment to the naval service. Officers so detailed shall be recalled from an institution if it is discontinued or if the good of the naval service requires.
