45 C.F.R. § 1703.207 — Change in meeting plans after public announcement.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1703.207
Creator: National Commission On Libraries And Information Science
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1703.207
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1703 (Government In The Sunshine Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Following public announcement of a meeting, the time or place of a meeting may be changed only if the change is announced publicly at the earliest practicable time.
