7 U.S.C. § 584 — Exemptions
Citation: us:usc:7:584
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section584
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 25 (Export Standards For Apples). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
Apples in less than carload lots as defined by the Secretary may, in his discretion, be shipped to any foreign country without complying with the provisions of this chapter.
(b) Bulk containers
Apples may be shipped to Canada in bulk containers without complying with the provisions of this chapter.
