30 C.F.R. § 285.638 — What must I do upon completion of my commercial operations as approved in my COP or FERC license?
Citation: us:cfr:30:285.638
Creator: Bureau Of Safety And Environmental Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-285.638
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 285 (Renewable Energy And Alternate Uses Of Existing Facilities On The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Upon completion of your approved activities under your COP, you must decommission your project as set forth in subpart I of this part. You must submit your decommissioning application as provided in §§ 285.905 and 285.906.
(b) Upon completion of your approved activities under your FERC license, the terms of your FERC license will govern your decommissioning activities.
