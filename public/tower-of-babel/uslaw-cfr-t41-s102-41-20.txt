41 C.F.R. § 102-41.20 — -41.20 Sale proceeds of forfeited personal property.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-41.20
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-41.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-41 (41—Disposition Of Seized, Forfeited, Voluntarily Abandoned, And Unclaimed Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must deposit the sales proceeds in the U.S. Treasury as miscellaneous receipts, unless otherwise directed by court decree or specifically authorized by statute.
