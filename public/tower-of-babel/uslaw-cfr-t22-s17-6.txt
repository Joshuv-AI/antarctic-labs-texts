22 C.F.R. § 17.6 — Ordinary and necessary living expenses.
Citation: us:cfr:22:17.6
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-17.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 17 (Overpayments From The Foreign Service Retirement And Disability Fund Under The Foreign Service Retirement And Disability System (Fsrds) And The Foreign Service Pension System (Fsps)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An individual's ordinary and necessary living expenses include rent, mortgage payments, utilities, maintenance, food, clothing, insurance (life, health and accident), taxes, installment payments, medical expenses, support expenses when the individual is legally responsible, and other miscellaneous expenses which the individual can establish as being ordinary and necessary.
