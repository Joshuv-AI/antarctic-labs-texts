34 U.S.C. § 12133 — “Indian tribe” defined
Citation: us:usc:34:12133
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section12133
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 121 (Violent Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Subchapter II (Crime Prevention); Part A (Ounce Of Prevention Council). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this part, “Indian tribe” means a tribe, band, pueblo, nation, or other organized group or community of Indians, including an Alaska Native village (as defined in or established under the Alaska Native Claims Settlement Act (43 U.S.C. 1601 et seq.),1 that is recognized as eligible for the special programs and services provided by the United States to Indians because of their status as Indians.
