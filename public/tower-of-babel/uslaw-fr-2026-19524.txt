DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Foreign-Trade Zone 38; Application for Subzone; Schaeffler Group 
USA Inc.; Fort Mill, South Carolina

    An application has been submitted to the Foreign-Trade Zones Board 
by the South Carolina State Ports Authority, grantee of FTZ 38, 
requesting subzone status for the facility of Schaeffler Group USA 
Inc., located in Fort Mill, South Carolina. The application was 
submitted pursuant to the provisions of the Foreign-Trade Zones Act, as 
amended (19 U.S.C. 81a-81u), and the regulations of the FTZ Board (15 
CFR part 400). It was formally docketed on September 14, 2026.
    The proposed subzone (15.291 acres) is located at 194 Springhill 
Farm Road, Fort Mill, South Carolina. No authorization for production 
activity has been requested at this time. A notification of proposed 
production activity has been submitted and will be published separately 
for public comment.
    In accordance with the FTZ Board's regulations, Juanita Chen of the 
FTZ Staff is designated examiner to review the application and make 
recommendations to the FTZ Board.
    Public comment is invited from interested parties. Submissions 
shall be addressed to the FTZ Board's Executive Secretary and sent to: 
[email protected]. The closing period for their receipt is November 3, 
2026. Rebuttal comments in response to material submitted during the 
foregoing period may be submitted through November 18, 2026.
    A copy of the application will be available for public inspection 
in the ``Online FTZ Information Section'' section of the FTZ Board's 
website, which is accessible via www.trade.gov/ftz.
    For further information, contact Juanita Chen at 
[email protected].

    Dated: September 21, 2026.
Colin Agostisi,
Executive Secretary.
