45 C.F.R. § 1150.25 — Will the collection of a claim by salary offset act as a waiver of my rights to dispute the claimed debt?
Citation: us:cfr:45:1150.25
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1150.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1150 (Collection Of Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Your involuntary payment of all or any portion of a debt under this subpart will not be construed as a waiver of any rights that you may have under 5 U.S.C. 5514 or other provisions of a law or written contract, unless there are statutory or contractual provisions to the contrary.
