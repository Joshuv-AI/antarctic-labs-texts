42 U.S.C. § 6396 — State laws or programs
Citation: us:usc:42:6396
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section6396
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 77 (Energy Conservation); Subchapter IV (General Provisions); Part B (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No State law or State program in effect on December 22, 1975, or which may become effective thereafter, shall be superseded by any provision of title I or II of this Act (other than any provision of such title which amends another law) or any rule, regulation, or order thereunder, except insofar as such State law or State program is in conflict with such provision, rule, regulation, or order.
