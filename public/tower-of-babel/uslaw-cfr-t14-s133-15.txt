14 C.F.R. § 133.15 — Application for certificate issuance or renewal.
Citation: us:cfr:14:133.15
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-133.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 133 (Rotorcraft External-Load Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Application for an original certificate or renewal of a certificate issued under this part is made on a form, and in a manner, prescribed by the Administrator. The form may be obtained from a Flight Standards office. The completed application is sent to the responsible Flight Standards office for the area in which the applicant's home base of operation is located.
