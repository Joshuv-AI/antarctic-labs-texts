46 U.S.C. § 9305 — Agreements with Canada
Citation: us:usc:46:9305
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section9305
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part F (Manning Of Vessels); Chapter 93 (Great Lakes Pilotage). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To provide for a coordinated system of pilotage service on the Great Lakes, the Secretary, subject to the concurrence of the Secretary of State, may make agreements with the appropriate agency of Canada to—
(1)
fix the number of pilots to be registered in each country;
(2)
provide for participation on an equitable basis;
(3)
prescribe joint or identical rates and charges;
(4)
coordinate pool operations; and
(5)
establish conditions for services by registered pilots.
