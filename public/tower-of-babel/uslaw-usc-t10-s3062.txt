10 U.S.C. § 3062 — Regulations
Citation: us:usc:10:3062
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section3062
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part V (Acquisition); Subpart A (General); Chapter 203 (General Matters). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Defense is required by section 2202 of this title to prescribe regulations governing the performance within the Department of Defense of the procurement functions, and related functions, of the Department of Defense.
