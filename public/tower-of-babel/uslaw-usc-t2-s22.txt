2 U.S.C. § 22 — Oath of President of Senate
Citation: us:usc:2:22
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section22
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 2 (Organization Of Congress). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When a President of the Senate has not taken the oath of office, it shall be administered to him by any Member of the Senate.
