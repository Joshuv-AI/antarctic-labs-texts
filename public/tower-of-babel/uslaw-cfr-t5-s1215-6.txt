5 C.F.R. § 1215.6 — Written decision.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1215.6
Creator: Merit Systems Protection Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1215.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1215 (Debt Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The hearing official shall issue a written opinion no later than 60 days after the hearing.
(b) The written opinion will include: A statement of the facts presented to demonstrate the nature and origin of the alleged debt; the hearing official's analysis, findings and conclusions; the amount and validity of the debt, and the repayment schedule.
