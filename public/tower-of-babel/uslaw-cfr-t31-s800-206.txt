31 C.F.R. § 800.206 — Completion date.
Citation: us:cfr:31:800.206
Creator: Office Of Investment Security, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-800.206
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 800 (Regulations Pertaining To Certain Investments In The United States By Foreign Persons). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term completion date means, with respect to a transaction, the earliest date upon which any ownership interest, including a contingent equity interest, is conveyed, assigned, delivered, or otherwise transferred to a person, or a change in rights that could result in a covered control transaction or covered investment occurs.
