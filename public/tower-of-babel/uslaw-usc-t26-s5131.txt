26 U.S.C. § 5131 — Packaging distilled spirits for industrial uses
Citation: us:usc:26:5131
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section5131
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 51 (Distilled Spirits, Wines, And Beer); Subchapter A (Gallonage And Occupational Taxes); Part II (Miscellaneous Provisions); Subpart D (Other Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) General
The Secretary may, at his discretion and under such regulations as he may prescribe, authorize a dealer (as defined in section 5121(c)) engaging in the business of supplying distilled spirits for industrial uses to package distilled spirits, on which the tax has been paid or determined, for such uses in containers of a capacity in excess of 1 wine gallon and not more than 5 wine gallons.
(b) Cross reference
For provisions relating to containers of distilled spirits, see section 5206.
