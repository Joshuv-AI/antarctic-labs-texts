7 C.F.R. § 1421.409 — Monitoring AGI.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1421.409
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1421.409
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1421 (Grains And Similarly Handled Commodities—Marketing Assistance Loans And Loan Deficiency Payments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

DMAs are required to monitor their producers' AGIs and may not permit repayments with a market loan gain on peanut MALs or process peanut LDPs for those producers with annual AGI over the allowable limit as specified in part 1400 of this chapter.
