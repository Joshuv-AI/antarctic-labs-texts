46 C.F.R. § 143.270 — Piping systems and tanks.
Citation: us:cfr:46:143.270
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-143.270
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 143 (Machinery And Electrical Systems And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Piping and tanks exposed to the outside of the hull must be made of metal and maintained in a leak free condition.
