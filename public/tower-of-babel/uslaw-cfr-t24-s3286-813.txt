24 C.F.R. § 3286.813 — Inclusion in state plan.
Citation: us:cfr:24:3286.813
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-3286.813
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 3286 (Manufactured Home Installation Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a state installation program is included in a state plan approved in accordance with § 3282.302 of this chapter, the state installation program is subject to all of the requirements for such a state plan, including annual review by HUD.
