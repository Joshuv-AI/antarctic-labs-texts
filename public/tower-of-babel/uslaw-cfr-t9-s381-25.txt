9 C.F.R. § 381.25 — Official establishment numbers.
Citation: us:cfr:9:381.25
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-381.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 381 (Poultry Products Inspection Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An official establishment number shall be assigned to each establishment granted inspection service. Such number shall be used to identify all containers of inspected poultry products prepared in the establishment. An establishment shall not have more than one establishment number.
