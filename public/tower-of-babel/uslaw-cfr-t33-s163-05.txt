33 C.F.R. § 163.05 — Tows of seagoing barges within inland waters.
Citation: us:cfr:33:163.05
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-163.05
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 163 (Towing Of Barges). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The tows of seagoing barges when navigating the inland waters of the United States shall be limited in length to five vessels, including the towing vessel or vessels.
