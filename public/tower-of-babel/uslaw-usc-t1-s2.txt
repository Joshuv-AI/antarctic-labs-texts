1 U.S.C. § 2 — “County” as including “parish”, and so forth
Citation: us:usc:1:2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title1-section2
Context: United States Code, Title 1 (General Provisions); Chapter 1 (Rules Of Construction). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The word “county” includes a parish, or any other equivalent subdivision of a State or Territory of the United States.
