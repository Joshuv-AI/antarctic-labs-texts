16 C.F.R. § 1014.11 — Penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1014.11
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1014.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1014 (Policies And Procedures Implementing The Privacy Act Of 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person who makes a false statement in connection with any request for a record, or an amendment thereto, under this part, is subject to the penalties prescribed in 18 U.S.C. 494, 495, and 1001; and 5 U.S.C. 552a(i)(3).
