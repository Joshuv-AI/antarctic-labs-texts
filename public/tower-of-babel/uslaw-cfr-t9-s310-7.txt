9 C.F.R. § 310.7 — Removal of spermatic cords, pizzles and preputial diverticuli.
Citation: us:cfr:9:310.7
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-310.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 310 (Post-Mortem Inspection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Spermatic cords and pizzles shall be removed from all carcasses. Preputial diverticuli shall be removed from hog carcasses.
