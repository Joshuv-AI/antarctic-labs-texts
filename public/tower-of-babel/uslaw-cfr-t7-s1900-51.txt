7 C.F.R. § 1900.51 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1900.51
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Rural Business-Cooperative Service, And Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1900.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1900 (General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Act means the Federal Crop Insurance Reform and Department of Agriculture Reorganization Act of 1994, Public Law No. 103-354 (7 U.S.C. 6991 et seq.).
Agency means the Rural Utilities Service (RUS), the Rural Housing Service (RHS), and the Rural Business-Cooperative Development Service (RBS), or their successor agencies.
Refer to 7 CFR 11.1 for other definitions applicable to appeals of adverse decisions covered by this subpart.
