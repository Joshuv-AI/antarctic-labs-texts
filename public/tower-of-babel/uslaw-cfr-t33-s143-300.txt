33 C.F.R. § 143.300 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:33:143.300
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 143 (Design And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to all vessels engaged in OCS activities except mobile offshore drilling units.
