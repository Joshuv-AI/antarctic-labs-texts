42 U.S.C. § 300dd–31 — Grants for anonymous testing
Citation: us:usc:42:300dd–31
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section300dd–31
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter XXII (Health Services With Respect To Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome); Part C (Other Health Services). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may make grants to the States for the purpose of providing opportunities for individuals—
(1)
to undergo counseling and testing with respect to the etiologic agent for acquired immune deficiency syndrome without being required to provide any information relating to the identity of the individuals; and
(2)
to undergo such counseling and testing through the use of a pseudonym.
