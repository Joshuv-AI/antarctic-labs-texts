40 C.F.R. § 1048.330 — May I sell engines from an engine family with a suspended certificate of conformity?
Citation: us:cfr:40:1048.330
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1048.330
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1048 (Control Of Emissions From New, Large Nonroad Spark-Ignition Engines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may sell engines that you produce after we suspend the engine family's certificate of conformity under § 1048.315 only if one of the following occurs:
(a) You test each engine you produce and show it complies with emission standards that apply.
(b) We conditionally reinstate the certificate for the engine family. We may do so if you agree to recall all the affected engines and remedy any noncompliance at no expense to the owner if later testing shows that the engine family still does not comply.
