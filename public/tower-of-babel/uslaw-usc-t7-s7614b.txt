7 U.S.C. § 7614b — Review of plan of work requirements
Citation: us:usc:7:7614b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section7614b
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 103 (Agricultural Research, Extension, And Education Reform); Subchapter I (Priorities, Scope, Review, And Coordination Of Agricultural Research, Extension, And Education). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Review
The Secretary shall work with university partners in extension and research to review and identify measures to streamline the submission, reporting under, and implementation of plan of work requirements, including those requirements under—
(1)
sections 3221(d) and 3222(c) of this title;
(2)
section 361g of this title; and
(3)
section 344 of this title.
(b) Consultation
In carrying out the review and formulating and compiling the recommendations, the Secretary shall consult with the land-grant institutions.
