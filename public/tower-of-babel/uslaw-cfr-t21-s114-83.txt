21 C.F.R. § 114.83 — Establishing scheduled processes.
Citation: us:cfr:21:114.83
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-114.83
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 114 (Acidified Foods). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The scheduled process shall be established by a qualified person who has expert knowledge acquired through appropriate training and experience in the acidification and processing of acidified foods.
