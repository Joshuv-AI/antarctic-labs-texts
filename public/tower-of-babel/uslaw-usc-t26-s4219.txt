26 U.S.C. § 4219 — Application of tax in case of sales by other than manufacturer or importer
Citation: us:usc:26:4219
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section4219
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 32 (Manufacturers Excise Taxes); Subchapter F (Special Provisions Applicable To Manufacturers Tax). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In case any person acquires from the manufacturer, producer, or importer of an article, by operation of law or as a result of any transaction not taxable under this chapter, the right to sell such article, the sale of such article by such person shall be taxable under this chapter as if made by the manufacturer, producer, or importer, and such person shall be liable for the tax.
