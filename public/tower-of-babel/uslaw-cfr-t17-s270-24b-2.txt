17 C.F.R. § 270.24b-2 — b-2 Filing copies of sales literature.
Citation: us:cfr:17:270.24b-2
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-270.24b-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 270 (Rules And Regulations, Investment Company Act Of 1940). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Copies of material filed with the Commission for the sole purpose of complying with section 24(b) of the Act (15 U.S.C. 80a-24(b)) either shall be accompanied by a letter of transmittal which makes appropriate references to said section or shall make such appropriate reference on the face of the material.
