29 C.F.R. § 20.57 — Accrual of interest.
Citation: us:cfr:29:20.57
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-20.57
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 20 (Federal Claims Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Interest shall accrue from the date on which notice of the debt and the interest requirements is first mailed or hand-delivered to the debtor, using the most current address that is available to the agency.
