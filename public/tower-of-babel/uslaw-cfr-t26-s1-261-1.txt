26 C.F.R. § 1.261-1 — -1 General rule for disallowance of deductions.
Citation: us:cfr:26:1.261-1
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.261-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 1 (Income Taxes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In computing taxable income, no deduction shall be allowed, except as otherwise expressly provided in Chapter 1 of the Code, in respect of any of the items specified in Part IX (section 262 and following), Subchapter B, Chapter 1 of the Code, and the regulations thereunder.
