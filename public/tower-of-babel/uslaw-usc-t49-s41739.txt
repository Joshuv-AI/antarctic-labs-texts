49 U.S.C. § 41739 — Air carrier obligations
Citation: us:usc:49:41739
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section41739
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Air Commerce And Safety); Subpart ii (Economic Regulation); Chapter 417 (Operations Of Carriers); Subchapter II (Small Community Air Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If at least 2 air carriers make an agreement to operate under or use a single carrier designator code to provide air transportation, the carrier whose code is being used shares responsibility with the other carriers for the quality of transportation provided the public under the code by the other carriers.
