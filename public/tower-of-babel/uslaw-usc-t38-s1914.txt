38 U.S.C. § 1914 — Statutory total disabilities
Citation: us:usc:38:1914
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section1914
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 19 (Insurance); Subchapter I (National Service Life Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Without prejudice to any other cause of disability, the permanent loss of the use of both feet, of both hands, or of both eyes, or of one foot and one hand, or of one foot and one eye, or of one hand and one eye, or the total loss of hearing of both ears, or the organic loss of speech, shall be deemed total disability for insurance purposes.
