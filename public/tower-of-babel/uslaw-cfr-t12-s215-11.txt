12 C.F.R. § 215.11 — Civil penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:12:215.11
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-215.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 215 (Loans To Executive Officers, Directors, And Principal Shareholders Of Member Banks (Regulation O)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any member bank, or any officer, director, employee, agent, or other person participating in the conduct of the affairs of the bank, that violates any provision of this part (other than § 215.9) is subject to civil penalties as specified in section 29 of the Federal Reserve Act (12 U.S.C. 504).
