7 U.S.C. § 1929–1 — Level of loan programs under Agricultural Credit Insurance Fund
Citation: us:usc:7:1929–1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1929–1
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 50 (Agricultural Credit); Subchapter I (Real Estate Loans). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On and after October 28, 1991, no funds in this Act or any other Act shall be available to carry out loan programs under the Agricultural Credit Insurance Fund at levels other than those provided for in advance in appropriations Acts.
