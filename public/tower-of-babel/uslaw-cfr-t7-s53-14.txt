7 C.F.R. § 53.14 — Financial interest of official grader.
Citation: us:cfr:7:53.14
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-53.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 53 (Livestock (Grading, Certification, And Standards)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No official grader shall grade or determine compliance of any livestock in which he or any of his relatives by blood or marriage is directly or indirectly financially interested.
