7 C.F.R. § 97.155 — Signatures.
Citation: us:cfr:7:97.155
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-97.155
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 97 (Plant Variety And Protection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every document filed by an attorney or agent representing an applicant or party to a proceeding in the Office shall bear the signature of such attorney or agent, except documents which are required to be signed by the applicant or party.
