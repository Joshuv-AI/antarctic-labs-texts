43 C.F.R. § 51.15 — Rural determination process.
Citation: us:cfr:43:51.15
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-51.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 51 (Subsistence Management Regulations For Public Lands In Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Board determines which areas or communities in Alaska are nonrural. Current determinations are listed at § 51.23.
(b) All other communities and areas are, therefore, rural.
