12 C.F.R. § 223.13 — What safety and soundness requirement applies to covered transactions?
Citation: us:cfr:12:223.13
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-223.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 223 (Transactions Between Member Banks And Their Affiliates (Regulation W)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A member bank may not engage in any covered transaction, including any transaction exempt under this regulation, unless the transaction is on terms and conditions that are consistent with safe and sound banking practices.
