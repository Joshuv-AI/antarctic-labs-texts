48 C.F.R. § 233.215 — 233.215 Contract clauses.
Citation: us:cfr:48:233.215
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-233.215
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 233 (Protests, Disputes, And Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Use Alternate I of the clause at FAR 52.233-1, Disputes, when—
(1) The acquisition is for—
(i) Aircraft
(ii) Spacecraft and launch vehicles
(iii) Naval vessels
(iv) Missile systems
(v) Tracked combat vehicles
(vi) Related electronic systems;
(2) The contracting officer determines that continued performance is—
(i) Vital to the national security, or
(ii) Vital to the public health and welfare; or
(3) The head of the contracting activity determines that continued performance is necessary pending resolution of any claim that might arise under or be related to the contract.
