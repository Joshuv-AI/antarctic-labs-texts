28 C.F.R. § 541.29 — Staff verification of need for protection.
Citation: us:cfr:28:541.29
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-541.29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 541 (Inmate Discipline And Special Housing Units). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a staff investigation verifies your need for placement in the SHU as a protection case, you may remain in the SHU or be transferred to another institution where your status as a protection case may not be necessary, at the Warden's discretion.
