23 C.F.R. § 190.3 — Agreement to control advertising.
Citation: us:cfr:23:190.3
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-190.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 190 (Incentive Payments For Controlling Outdoor Advertising On The Interstate System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To qualify for the bonus payment, a State must have entered into an agreement with the Secretary to control outdoor advertising. It must fulfill, and must continue to fulfill its obligations under such agreement consistent with 23 CFR 750.101.
