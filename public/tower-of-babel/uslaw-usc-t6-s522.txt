6 U.S.C. § 522 — Statutory construction
Citation: us:usc:6:522
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section522
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 1 (Homeland Security Organization); Subchapter XI (Department Of Justice Divisions); Part A (Executive Office For Immigration Review). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter, any amendment made by this chapter, or in section 1103 of title 8, shall be construed to limit judicial deference to regulations, adjudications, interpretations, orders, decisions, judgments, or any other actions of the Secretary of Homeland Security or the Attorney General.
