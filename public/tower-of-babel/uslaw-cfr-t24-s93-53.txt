24 C.F.R. § 93.53 — Federal Register notice of formula allocations.
Citation: us:cfr:24:93.53
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-93.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 93 (Housing Trust Fund). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Not later than 60 calendar days after the date that HUD determines the formula amounts under this subpart, HUD will publish a notice in the Federal Register announcing the availability of the allocations to States.
