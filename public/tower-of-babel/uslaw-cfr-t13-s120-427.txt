13 C.F.R. § 120.427 — Will SBA approve a securitization application from a capital impaired Securitizer?
Citation: us:cfr:13:120.427
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-120.427
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 120 (Business Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a securitizer does not maintain the level of capital required by this subpart, SBA will not approve a securitization application from that securitizer.
