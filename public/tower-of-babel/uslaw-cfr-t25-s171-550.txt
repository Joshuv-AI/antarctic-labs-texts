25 C.F.R. § 171.550 — Can I arrange a Payment Plan if I cannot pay the full amount due?
Citation: us:cfr:25:171.550
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-171.550
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 171 (Irrigation Operation And Maintenance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We may approve a Payment Plan if:
(a) You are a landowner and your land is not leased;
(b) You certify that you are financially unable to make a lump sum payment;
(c) You provide additional information we request, which may include information identified in 31 CFR 901.8, “Collection in installments”; and
(d) You sign our Payment Plan containing terms and conditions we specify.
