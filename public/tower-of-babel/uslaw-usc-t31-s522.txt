31 U.S.C. § 522 — Necessary expenditures
Citation: us:usc:31:522
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section522
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 5 (Office Of Management And Budget); Subchapter II (Administrative). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of the Office of Management and Budget may make necessary expenditures for the Office under regulations prescribed by the President.
