44 C.F.R. § 304.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:44:304.1
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-304.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 304 (Consolidated Grants To Insular Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of the regulations in this part is to prescribe the basis under which the Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA) contributes Federal funds to an insular area through a consolidated grant.
