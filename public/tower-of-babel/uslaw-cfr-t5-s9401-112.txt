5 C.F.R. § 9401.112 — Prohibited purchase of assets.
Citation: us:cfr:5:9401.112
Creator: Bureau Of Consumer Financial Protection
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-9401.112
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 9401 (Supplemental Standards Of Ethical Conduct For Employees Of The Bureau Of Consumer Financial Protection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee, or an employee's spouse or minor child, shall not purchase, directly or indirectly, any real or personal property from an entity supervised by the Bureau, unless it is sold at public auction or by other means which assures that the selling price reflects the asset's fair market value.
