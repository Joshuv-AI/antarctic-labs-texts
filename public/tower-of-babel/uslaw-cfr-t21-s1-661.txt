21 C.F.R. § 1.661 — What is the duration of accreditation by a recognized accreditation body?
Citation: us:cfr:21:1.661
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1.661
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1 (General Enforcement Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A recognized accreditation body may grant accreditation to a third-party certification body under this subpart for a period not to exceed 4 years.
