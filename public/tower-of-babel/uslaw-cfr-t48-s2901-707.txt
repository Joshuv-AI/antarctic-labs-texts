48 C.F.R. § 2901.707 — 2901.707 Signatory authority.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2901.707
Creator: Chapter 29—Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2901.707
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2901 (Department Of Labor Acquisition Regulation System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as shown in the applicable FAR or DOLAR, or where prohibited by statute, the authority to sign or delegate signatory authority for the various determinations and findings (D&Fs) resides with the SPE, or their designee.
