46 C.F.R. § 506.3 — Civil monetary penalty inflation adjustment.
Citation: us:cfr:46:506.3
Creator: Federal Maritime Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-506.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 506 (Civil Monetary Penalty Inflation Adjustment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission shall, not later than August 1, 2016, and at least every year thereafter—
(a) By regulation adjust each civil monetary penalty provided by law within the jurisdiction of the Commission by the inflation adjustment described in § 506.4; and
(b) Publish each such regulation in the Federal Register.
