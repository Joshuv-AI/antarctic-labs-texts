40 C.F.R. § 256.40 — Requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:40:256.40
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-256.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 256 (Guidelines For Development And Implementation Of State Solid Waste Management Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In order to comply with section 4003(6), the State plan shall provide for adequate resource conservation, recovery, storage, treatment and disposal facilities and practices necessary to use or dispose of solid and hazardous waste in an environmentally sound manner.
