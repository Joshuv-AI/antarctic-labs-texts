The Editor-in-Chief has retracted this article. After publication, concerns were raised regarding the figures presented in this work, specifically:

Figure 6a and b appear to partially overlap with each other.

Figure 6a and b appear to partially overlap with each other.

The data in question represent different experimental conditions. The authors were unable to provide a satisfactory explanation or supporting data to address these concerns. The Editor-in-Chief therefore no longer has confidence in the reliability of the research presented in this article.

Mohammad M. Aboulwafa has stated on behalf of all authors that they disagree with this retraction.
