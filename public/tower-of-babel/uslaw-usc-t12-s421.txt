12 U.S.C. § 421 — Examination of plates and dies
Citation: us:usc:12:421
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section421
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 3 (Federal Reserve System); Subchapter XII (Federal Reserve Notes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Treasury may examine the plates, dies, bed pieces, and other material used in the printing of Federal Reserve notes and issue regulations relating to such examinations.
