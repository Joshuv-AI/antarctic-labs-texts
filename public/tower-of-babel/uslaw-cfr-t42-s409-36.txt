42 C.F.R. § 409.36 — Effect of discharge from posthospital SNF care.
Citation: us:cfr:42:409.36
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-409.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 409 (Hospital Insurance Benefits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a beneficiary is discharged from a facility after receiving posthospital SNF care, he or she is not entitled to additional services of this kind in the same benefit period unless—
(a) He or she is readmitted to the same or another facility within 30 calendar days following the day of discharge (or, before December 5, 1980, within 14 calendar days after discharge); or
(b) He or she is again hospitalized for at least 3 consecutive calendar days.
