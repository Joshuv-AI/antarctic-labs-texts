11 U.S.C. § 1185 — Removal of debtor in possession
Citation: us:usc:11:1185
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section1185
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 11 (Reorganization); Subchapter V (Small Business Debtor Reorganization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In General.—
On request of a party in interest, and after notice and a hearing, the court shall order that the debtor shall not be a debtor in possession for cause, including fraud, dishonesty, incompetence, or gross mismanagement of the affairs of the debtor, either before or after the date of commencement of the case, or for failure to perform the obligations of the debtor under a plan confirmed under this subchapter.
(b) Reinstatement.—
On request of a party in interest, and after notice and a hearing, the court may reinstate the debtor in possession.
