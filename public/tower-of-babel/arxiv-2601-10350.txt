Nonsingular Cosmologies in Presence of String Cloud
Karma P. Sherpa, Rishi Pokhrel, Tanay K. Dey

Abstract
In the braneworld scenario, we introduce a uniformly distributed cloud of infinitely long strings in the five dimensional AdS bulk spacetime. The end points of the strings are attached to the brane and becomes the source of the four dimensional matter on the brane, while the body of the strings hang onto the radial direction of the bulk and act as the gluonic field on the brane. The presence of matter in the brane induces a nonsingular cosmological evolution for the scale factor of the brane world under certain conditions of mass and cosmological parameters. However, the nonsingular nature is unstable since the bounce occurs inside the Cauchy horizon. Further, we consider the shellworld or the dark bubble scenario for the same bulk spacetime. It shows stable nonsingular cosmological nature of the bubble universe under certain conditions on the bulk and bubble parameters.

Introduction

The standard cosmological model based on general relativity successfully explains a wide range of cosmological observations yet faces significant challenges. One of the most profound issues is the presence of an initial singularity. At the point of singularity, the energy density and the spacetime curvature diverges, leading to an inconsistency in the classical description of general relativity. This suggests the necessity of alternative approaches, either by modifying the gravitational theory itself or by altering the fundamental nature of spacetime.

Following the Anti de Sitter (AdS)/ Conformal Field Theory (CFT) correspondence
[
23
]
and the holographic principle
[
34

[

(3+1)
-dimensional hypersurface which is embeded in a
(4+1)
-dimensional AdS bulk spacetime. The hypersurface divides the AdS geometry in two identical regions on either side of the brane which imposes a
\mathbb{Z}_{2}
symmetry. There are two possible approaches to study the cosmological evolution of the brane universe. In the first approach called the Binetruy-Deffayet-Langlos (BDL) approach, the brane is considered to be fixed at a certain position in the bulk and the bulk metric is made time dependent
[
18
,
14
,
6

. In the second approach, the bulk metric is kept static and the brane is allowed to move in the radial direction of the bulk
[
11
,
19
,
27
]
. The motion of the brane in the bulk results in a time dependent induced metric on the brane which can be interpreted as the cosmological evolution of the brane universe. In
[
5
]
, a charge parameter is considered in the bulk AdS-Schwarzschild spacetime and cosmology on the boundary of the spacetime is studied.

A singularity free solution for the charged AdS-Schwarzschild spacetime is obtained in
[
25

, where the scale factor of the brane smoothly evolves from contraction to expansion. The black hole charge, interpreted as stiff matter on the brane, contributes a negative energy density, which provides nonsingular or bouncing cosmological scenarios. Such bouncing brane universe was further studied through the BDL approach along with the moving domain wall approach in
[

[

[

In the present work, we consider the braneworld model in the presence of string cloud in the spacetime. The cloud of strings are uniformly distributed and are extended in the radial direction of the bulk with their endpoints attached to the brane
[
10
,
9
]
. These endpoints represents massive particles in four dimensions effectively leading to a matter contribution in the brane universe. Braneworld cosmology with cloud of strings in the bulk has been considered in
[

20

We start with a brief review on bulk spacetime and the corresponding black hole solution. The black hole mass parameter can take negative value above a certain value of string density and black hole possess an inner horizon called Cauchy horizon inside the event horizon. We analysed the cosmological evolution of the brane universe by obtaining and solving the corresponding Friedmann equation for the brane. The Friedmann equation admits an exact solution for a critical brane. In case of positive or zero mass parameter, the universe shows the singular nature. In case of non-critical brane, the exact solution can not be derived. A qualitative study of the Friedmann equation can be carried out to investigate the behavior of the scale factor of the brane by interpreting the evolution of the brane universe as the motion of a classical particle in an effective potential. For a closed brane universe with positive brane cosmological constant and positive or zero mass parameter, the analysis shows that the brane universe exists in two possible regions below a certain value of string density. In one region, a nonsingular bouncing universe driven by the spatial curvature of the brane universe appears. In the other region, we obtain crunching universe from an expanding mode and finally goes to a singularity. As the value of string density increases, the two regions merge at a critical value of string density above which we find only an continuously expanding universe or collapsing universe to a singularity. For the other types of non-critical brane universe, a singular nature is observed. In the case of a critical brane with negative mass parameter, closed brane universe appears as a bouncing and cyclic nature while the open and flat brane universes are found to be eternally expanding nonsingular universes. This bounce is induced by the negative dark radiation term. For a non-critical closed brane with negative mass parameter and positive brane cosmological constant, the brane universe exists in two possible regions below a certain value of string density. In one region, a nonsingular bouncing and cyclic universe and in the other region, we obtain a nonsingular gradually expanding universe. As the value of string density increases, the two regions merge at a critical value of string density above which we find an eternally expanding nonsingular universe. For open and flat non-critical branes with a positive brane cosmological constant we find expanding nonsingular universe. For non-critical brane with negative mass parameter and negative brane cosmological constant, we find nonsingular bouncing and cyclic brane universes for all closed, open and flat geometries. However, in this case the bounce occurs inside the Cauchy horizon of the spacetime which leads to an instability of the universe as it encounters a curvature singularity at the turning point.

In consequence, we further consider the shellworld or the dark bubble scenario in presence of cloud of strings in the same bulk spacetime. The shellworld scenario is developed in
[
1
,
2
,
4
,
3
]
. It possesses a similar setup as the braneworld scenario except the
{\mathbb{Z}}_{2}
symmetry of the braneworld scenario. The AdS vacuum decays through bubble nucleation which leads to the spherical brane (bubble) joining the two different AdS vacua in such a way that it lacks
{\mathbb{Z}}_{2}
symmetry and has an inside and outside regions. Unlike the braneworld scenario, here the mass parameter doesn’t need to be negative to obtain a nonsingular brane universe. In presence of string density and for negative or zero brane cosmological constant, we find a nonsingular bouncing and cyclic universe while for positive brane cosmological constant, we find a nonsingular eternally expanding universe. Here, we find that the bubble universe has a stable bouncing and cyclic nature for certain conditions of the bulk parameters. For the absence of string density shellworld universe shows a stable nonsingular behaviour for certain conditions of mass and cosmological parameters.

2
The background

We consider a five dimensional AdS-Schwarzschild bulk in the presence of uniformly distributed cloud of infinitely long strings. The cloud of strings are extended in the radial direction of the bulk with their endpoints attached on the four dimensional brane placed perpendicular to the radial coordinate of the bulk. The brane divides the AdS spacetime in two different regions. The end points represent massive particles in the four dimensional theory living on the brane. The action for this configuration can be written as,

I=-\frac{1}{2\kappa^{2}}\int\limits_{M}d^{5}x\sqrt{-g}\left(R_{5}-2\Lambda_{5}\right)-\frac{I_{M}}{\kappa^{2}}+\frac{1}{\kappa^{2}}\int\limits_{\partial M}d^{4}x\sqrt{-\gamma}(K+\sigma).

(1)

Here,
R_{5}
represents the Ricci scalar of the five dimensional spacetime,
g_{\mu\nu}
is the five dimensional bulk metric,
\kappa^{2}=8\pi G_{5}
is the bulk gravitational constant,
l
is the curvature radius of the AdS spacetime and is related to the bulk cosmological constant as
\Lambda_{5}=-\frac{6}{l^{2}}
. The metric
\gamma_{cd}
is the induced metric on the brane with tension
\sigma
and
K
is the trace of the extrinsic curvature
K_{cd}
on the brane. The contribution of the cloud of strings is expressed as,

I_{M}=\frac{1}{2}\sum_{i}\mathcal{T}_{i}\int d^{2}\xi\sqrt{-h}h^{\alpha\beta}\partial_{\alpha}X^{\mu}\partial_{\beta}X^{\nu}g_{\mu\nu}.

(2)

Here,
h^{\alpha\beta}
denotes the induced metric on the string world-sheet parameterized by
\xi
, with the
i^{th}
string having tension
\mathcal{T}_{i}
. Variation of the action (
1
) with respect to the bulk metric
g_{\mu\nu}
give us the bulk equation of motion as,

R_{\mu\nu}-\frac{1}{2}Rg_{\mu\nu}+\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}=T_{\mu\nu},

(3)

where,
T_{\mu\nu}
represents the stress energy tensor of the string cloud and is given by,

T^{\mu\nu}=-\sum_{i}\mathcal{T}_{i}\int d^{2}\xi\frac{1}{\sqrt{\left|g_{\mu\nu}\right|}}\sqrt{\left|h_{\alpha\beta}\right|}h^{\alpha\beta}\partial_{\alpha}X^{\mu}\partial_{\beta}X^{\nu}\delta_{i}^{5}(x-X).

(4)

By considering the strings to have uniform tension
\mathcal{T}
, the string cloud density can be expressed as
[
10
]
,

b(x)=\mathcal{T}\sum_{i}\delta^{3}_{i}(x-X_{i}).

(5)

Under the static gauge
t=\xi^{0}\;{\rm and}\;r=\xi^{1}
, the non vanishing components of
T^{\mu\nu}
can be found as,

T^{tt}=-\frac{bg^{tt}}{r^{3}},\;T^{rr}=-\frac{bg^{rr}}{r^{3}}\;{\rm and}\;T^{t}_{t}=T^{r}_{r}=-\frac{b}{r^{3}},

(6)

where,
b
stands for average density of the tension of the strings and can be expressed as,

b=\frac{1}{\omega_{3}}\int b(x)d^{3}x={\mathcal{T}\over\omega_{3}}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\int\delta_{i}^{3}(x-X_{i})d^{3}x={\mathcal{T}\over\omega_{3}}\sum_{i=1}^{N}1={{\mathcal{T}}N\over\omega_{3}}.

(7)

Here,
\omega_{3}
is the volume of a 3-dimensional space,
N
represents the number of strings and
b
is considered to be positive. The static and symmetric spacetime metric solution of the equation (
3
) can be written by,

ds^{2}_{5}=-f(r)dt^{2}+\frac{dr^{2}}{f(r)}+r^{2}\tilde{\gamma}_{ij}dx^{i}dx^{j},

(8)

with
f(r)
given by,

f(r)=k+\frac{r^{2}}{l^{2}}-\frac{m}{r^{2}}-\frac{2b}{3r}.

(9)

Here,
k=0,-1,+1
represents flat, open, or closed geometries respectively and
\tilde{\gamma}_{ij}
is the metric for the constant curvature manifold
M^{3}
with the volume
\omega_{3}=d^{3}x\sqrt{\gamma}
. The constant
m
is the mass parameter related to the ADM mass
M
of the black hole as,

M=\frac{3\omega_{3}m}{2\kappa^{2}}.

(10)

The metric (
8
) admits a black hole solution having a curvature singularity at
r=0
, with the horizon determined by the condition
f(r)=0
. So, the horizon radius
r_{h}
satisfies the relation,

\displaystyle k+\frac{r_{h}^{2}}{l^{2}}-\frac{m}{r_{h}^{2}}-\frac{2b}{3r_{h}}

\displaystyle=0.

(11)

\displaystyle{\rm or,}\quad\frac{r_{h}^{4}}{l^{2}}+kr_{h}^{2}-\frac{2br_{h}}{3}-m

\displaystyle=0.

(12)

Since, string density is positive, Descarte’s sign rule indicates that the number of horizon depends only on the nature of mass parameter
m
. As long as the mass parameter is greater or equal to zero, there will be only one horizon called event horizon, otherwise there will be maximum two horizons. The outer horizon corresponds to the event horizon and the inner horizon is called Cauchy horizon.

The mass parameter
m
of the black hole in terms of the horizon radius can be written as,

m=kr_{h}^{2}+\frac{r_{h}^{4}}{l^{2}}-\frac{2br_{h}}{3}.

(13)

From equation (

) and figure (

k=+1
. However, even with a negative mass parameter, the entropy of the black hole still maintains one fourth of the area law,

S=\frac{r_{h}^{3}\omega_{3}}{4G_{5}}.

(14)

The temperature (

) of the black hole is expressed as,

T=\frac{1}{4\pi}\left.\frac{df(r)}{dr}\right|_{r=r_{h}}=\frac{12r_{h}^{10}+6kl^{2}r_{h}^{8}-2l^{2}br_{h}^{7}}{12\pi l^{2}r_{h}^{9}}.

(15)

With this brief discussion on five dimensional background in the next section, we initiate to study the cosmological evolution of the brane world volume.

3
Cosmological evolution

In order to study the cosmological evolution, we construct a time dependent induced brane metric from
5
-dimensional bulk metric (
8
). With this aim the bulk time
t
is parameterized by proper time
\tau
of the braneworld volume as
t=t(\tau)
which introduces a time-dependent radial coordinate
r=a(\tau)
in the bulk. For this specific parametrization, we get a 4-dimensional FRW metric on the brane of the form,

ds_{4}^{2}=-d\tau^{2}+a^{2}(\tau)\tilde{\gamma}_{ij}dx^{i}dx^{j},

(16)

for the constraint,

-f(a)\left(\frac{dt}{d\tau}\right)^{2}+\frac{1}{f(a)}\left(\frac{da}{d\tau}\right)^{2}=-1.

(17)

Therefore,
a(\tau)
becomes the scale factor as well as position of the brane universe in the radial direction of the bulk. The velocity components of the brane are
v^{t}=\frac{dt}{d\tau}=\frac{\sqrt{f(a)+\dot{a}^{2}}}{f(a)}
and
v^{r}=\dot{a}
. The corresponding unit normal vectors are
n^{t}=\dot{a}/f(a)\;{\rm and}\;n^{r}=\sqrt{{f(a)+\dot{a}^{2}}}
. Here, dot (
\dot{}
) denotes differentiation with respect to time
\tau
. The components of the extrinsic curvature of the brane can then be calculated from the relation,

K_{cd}=\frac{1}{2}n^{\mu}\partial_{\mu}\gamma_{cd}.

(18)

The spatial components of the extrinsic curvature are found to be,

K_{ij}=\frac{\sqrt{f(a)+\dot{a}^{2}}}{a}\gamma_{ij},

(19)

where,
i,j
are the indices of the spatial coordinates on the boundary.

3.1
The braneworld scenario

In the Randall-Sundrum braneworld scenario, the brane is considered to be a hypersurface
\partial M
that seperates the bulk spacetime
M
into two distinct regions. The Israel junction condition
[
17
]
defines the discontinuity of the extrinsic curvature across the brane as,

[K_{cd}]\equiv K^{+}_{cd}-K^{-}_{cd},

(20)

where,
K^{+}_{cd}
and
K^{-}_{cd}
are the extrinsic curvatures on the two sides of the brane. The junction condition relates the discontinuity of the extrinsic curvature to the energy momentum tensor
S_{cd}
on the brane as,

[K_{cd}]=-\kappa^{2}\left(S_{cd}-\frac{1}{3}S\gamma_{cd}\right).

(21)

If the two regions on either side of the brane are identical, a
{\mathbb{Z}}_{2}
symmetry is imposed which leads to
K^{+}_{cd}=-K^{-}_{cd}
and
[K_{cd}]=2K^{+}_{cd}
. Considering the brane energy momentum tensor contains only the brane tension
S_{cd}=-\sigma\gamma_{cd}
, the junction condition reduces to,

K^{+}_{cd}=-\frac{\kappa^{2}}{6}\sigma\gamma_{cd}.

(22)

Using the value of spatial components of (
19
) in (
22
), we get,

\frac{\sqrt{f(a)+\dot{a}^{2}}}{a}=-\frac{\kappa^{2}}{6}\sigma.

(23)

Substituting the value of
f(a)
from equation (
9
) in the above equation, the first Friedmann equation can be written in the following form,

H^{2}=\frac{\dot{a}^{2}}{a^{2}}=-\frac{k}{a^{2}}+\frac{m}{a^{4}}+\frac{2b}{3a^{3}}+\frac{\Lambda_{4}}{3},

(24)

where,
H
represents the Hubble factor and
\Lambda_{4}=\frac{\kappa^{4}\sigma^{2}}{12}-\frac{3}{l^{2}}
is the cosmological constant on the brane. By tuning the brane tension and AdS curvature radius, the value of the brane cosmological constant can be set as positive, negative or zero accordingly. Here, the effective Friedmann equation involves a mass parameter term
m
that gives a dark radiation contribution and a string density term
b
that gives a matter contribution.

Differentiating the Friedmann equation with respect to
\tau
, we obtain the second Friedmann equation as,

\frac{\ddot{a}}{a}=-\frac{m}{a^{4}}-\frac{b}{3a^{3}}+\frac{\Lambda_{4}}{3}.

(25)

24

3.2
The shellworld scenario

The shellworld or the dark bubble scenario lacks
\mathbb{Z}_{2}
symmetry with an outer false AdS vacuum and an inner true AdS vacuum separated by a spherical brane (bubble). The presence of mass and cloud of strings in the bulk results in the metric of the inside and outside regions to be,

ds^{2}_{\pm}=-f_{\pm}(r)dt^{2}+\frac{1}{f_{\pm}(r)}dr^{2}+r^{2}\tilde{\gamma}_{ij}dx^{i}dx^{j},

(39)

where,
+
and
-

f_{\pm}(r)
is given by the same metric function as in equation (
9

k=1

m_{\pm}

b_{\pm}

l_{\pm}
for the two regions. Here,
l_{+}

l_{-}

K^{\pm}_{ij}=\frac{\sqrt{f_{\pm}(a)+\dot{a}^{2}}}{a}\gamma_{ij}.

(40)

The junction condition (
21

\frac{\sqrt{f_{-}(a)+\dot{a}^{2}}}{a}-\frac{\sqrt{f_{+}(a)+\dot{a}^{2}}}{a}=\frac{\kappa^{2}}{3}\sigma.

(41)

4
Summary

In this work, we have studied the cosmological evolution of the four dimensional brane universe. The brane is placed perpendicular to the radial direction of the AdS bulk spacetime in presence of string cloud and the brane divides the bulk in two different regions. The string cloud is uniformly distributed and stretches along the radial direction of the bulk, with its endpoints attached to the four-dimensional brane.
The attached end points of the strings represent massive particles or matter on the four dimensional brane. The cloud of strings deform the AdS bulk spacetime and allows the mass parameter of the bulk to take negative values at smaller size of the bulk spacetime on increasing values of the string density. We have derived the effective Friedmann equations on the brane in which the mass parameter term gives a dark radiation contribution and the string density term gives a matter contribution.

In the braneworld framework where the two bulk regions on either side of the brane are identical and related by a
\mathbb{Z}_{2}
symmetry, we have investigated the cosmological evolution of the brane universe using the Friedmann equations for both critical and non-critical branes. We derive exact analytical solutions of the Friedmann equation for the critical brane. In the case of a non-critical brane, we have performed a qualitative analysis of the nature of cosmological evolution by treating the evolution equation of the brane universe as the dynamics of a classical particle moving in an effective potential.

The critical brane universe with a positive or zero mass parameter shows the brane universe to begin from a nonsingular value of scale factor and collapse back to singularity for the closed geometry while for open and flat geometries, the brane universe expands exponentially and quadratically respectively from a singularity.
For a non-critical brane with a positive or zero mass parameter, we find that there are two possible regions for the universe to exist below a certain critical value of string density for a closed universe with a positive brane cosmological constant. In one region the universe crunching up to a certain minimum volume and then bounces back for the expansion eternally. Whereas in the other region the universe crunches from an expanding mode to a singularity. As the string density increases the two regions come closer and finally merge at the critical value of string density. Above the critical value, the universe does not have any turning point and expands or crunches continuously. For a negative brane cosmological constant, the universe quickly turn back from expanding mode to contracting mode and finally collapses to the singularity. For an open and flat universe with a positive brane cosmological constant, the universe eternally expands without a bouncing nature while for a negative brane cosmological constant, the universe goes from an expanding nature to a contracting nature and finally collapses to the singularity .

In the case of a negative mass parameter, we find that the brane universe has a bouncing and cyclic nonsingular nature for a closed critical brane universe. For an open and flat critical brane universe, the scale factor undergoes a nonsingular exponential and quadratic expansion respectively. For a closed universe with a positive brane cosmological constant, again there are two possible regions for the universe to exist below a certain critical value of string density. One region provides a bouncing nature and the other one behaves as cyclic nature of universe. Above the critical value of string density, two regions merge and only one region of universe exists where the universe begins from a nonsingular value of scale factor and expands eternally. For open and flat universe with a positive brane cosmological constant, the universe gradually expands from a nonsingular value of scale factor. For a negative brane cosmological constant, the universe has a nonsingular bouncing and cyclic nature for all three geometries of the brane. To verify the stability of the bouncing nature, we plotted the metric function
f(a)
and effective potential
U(a)
as a function of the scale factor. We found that the bulk has two horizons and the brane bounces inside the inner horizon of the black hole. This leads to an instability as the brane encounters a curvature singularity at the turning point.

Therefore, we further studied the bouncing scenario in the shellworld model. The shellworld or the dark bubble scenario lacks
\mathbb{Z}_{2}
symmetry with two distinct AdS vacua representing an inside and outside region. We have derived the Friedmann equations on the shellworld in the presence of cloud of strings for zero or positive mass parameter and negative mass parameter separately. We then obtain an exact solution of the Friedmann equation for the critical shellworld universe. The solution shows a nonsingular bouncing and cyclic nature of the shellworld universe under certain conditions on the mass and cosmological parameters and string densities. We also perform a qualitative analysis of the nature of cosmological evolution of the shellworld universe in the presence of a non-zero brane cosmological constant. Again for certain conditions, the shellworld universe shows a nonsingular bouncing nature for a positive brane cosmological constant and a bouncing as well as cyclic nature for a negative brane cosmological constant. The nonsingular nature is independent of the sign of the mass parameter.

Finally, we verify the stability of the bounce by plotting the metric function
f_{\pm}(a)
and effective potential
U(a)
as a function of the scale factor. We find that under certain values of the parameters, the shellworld bounces outside the Cauchy horizon of the bulk avoiding the instability.

We also verify that even for the absence of string density, the shellworld universe shows a stable nonsingular nature for certain conditions of mass and cosmological parameter of the bubble space time.

Therefore, we conclude that the bouncing mechanism in this model provides an effective approach to avoid the instabilities due to the lack of the Cauchy horizon.
