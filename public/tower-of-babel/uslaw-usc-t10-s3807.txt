10 U.S.C. § 3807 — Vesting of title in the United States
Citation: us:usc:10:3807
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section3807
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part V (Acquisition); Subpart D (General Contracting Provisions); Chapter 277 (Contract Financing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If a contract paid by a method authorized under section 3801(a) of this title provides for title to property to vest in the United States, the title to the property shall vest in accordance with the terms of the contract, regardless of any security interest in the property that is asserted before or after the contract is entered into.
