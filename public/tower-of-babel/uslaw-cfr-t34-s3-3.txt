34 C.F.R. § 3.3 — Authority to affix seal.
Citation: us:cfr:34:3.3
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-3.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 3 (Official Seal). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary and the Secretary's designees are authorized to affix the Official Seal, replicas, reproductions, and embossing seals to appropriate documents, certifications, and other material for all purposes as authorized by this section.
