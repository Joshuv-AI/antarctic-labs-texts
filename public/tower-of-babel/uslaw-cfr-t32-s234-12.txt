32 C.F.R. § 234.12 — Restriction on animals.
Citation: us:cfr:32:234.12
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-234.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 234 (Conduct On The Pentagon Reservation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Animals, except guide dogs for persons with disabilities, shall not be brought upon the Pentagon Reservation for other than official purposes.
