15 U.S.C. § 689f — Fees
Citation: us:usc:15:689f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section689f
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 14B (Small Business Investment Program); Subchapter III (Investment Division Programs); Part B (New Markets Venture Capital Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as provided in section 689e(d) of this title, the Administrator may charge such fees as it deems appropriate with respect to any guarantee or grant issued under this part.
