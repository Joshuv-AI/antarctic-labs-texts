Thermal Performance of ternary hybrid nanofluid with activation energy and surface tension gradient using HCM and YOM models
Authors: Mouna Ettoumi, Imen Safra, Aydin Secer, Farkhod Rakhmonov, Saira Kanwal
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
This study inspects the Darcy Forchheimer flow of MHD trihybrid nanofluid with heat generation is briefly examined using the expanded model, also known as the YOM (Yamada-Ota model) and HCM (Hamilton Crosser model). The momentum equation includes the Darcy Forchheimer permeable medium. The importance of viscous dissipation and Joule heating are examined. Accurate prediction of thermal processes is a major concern for the community of energy researchers as well as for those conducting research on heat transport. Using a basic mathematical model as a sample example, the current work aims to examine the properties of heat transmission when LTNECs (local thermal non-equilibrium conditions) are not present. Different thermal gradients are present in the fluid and solid states under the theory of LTNE. The system of ODEs is numerically solved using the MATLAB solver bvp4c by applying the appropriate similarity modifications, which are derived from the constitutive PDEs. The results show that as compared to the HCM model, the YOM has much better heat transmission capacities. Furthermore, the findings show that the liquid phase’s thermal field decreases more sharply with a rise in the inter-phase heat transportation parameter, whereas the solid phase’s temperature profile decreases less sharply. Additionally, when the Marangoni convection factor increases, the rate of heat exchange between the fluid and solid phases increases as well.

Introduction

Marangoni convection is a particular type of liquid flow that originates from surface tension gradients in liquids. Since the ST (surface tension) of a fluid is thermal sensitive, thermal changes at the liquid’s surface will cause the surface tension to fluctuate as well. As a result, there will be a differential in surface tension between the liquid’s hot and cold portions, which may cause fluid flow. Maranna et al. [1] scrutinize the outcome of radiation on MC (Marangoni convective) flow of THNF (trihybrid nanofluid) in a permeable media with mass transmission. In complicated fluid systems such as Marangoni convection and trihybrid nanofluids with activation energy local thermal non-equilibrium, the YOM and HCM are crucial for understanding mass and heat transfer phenomena. Various technological and engineering domains make extensive use of these models. For instance, they help in the enhancement of heating operations in the materials processing productions, like as metal casting and semiconductor fabrication, by accurately anticipating temperature distributions and fluid behaviors. Additionally, these models are helpful in environmental engineering for analyzing the dispersion of pollutants and heat transfer in artificial and naturally occurring systems, like air pollution control devices and water treatment plants. Additionally, they shed light on the flow of mass and heat within biological tissues and fluids, which is helpful for biomedical engineering projects that include the development of thermal therapies for cancer treatment and drug delivery systems.

Conclusions

The activation energy and heat production effects on the Darcy Forchheimer flow of a trihybrid nanofluid are examined in this work utilizing the YOM and the HCM, while accounting for LTNECs and Marangoni convection. Because the two thermal conductivity models, YOM and HCM, have different tendencies for heat transmission, a comparison analysis has been done to evaluate how well they function. The thorough examination carried out in this work has produced a number of important discoveries that encapsulate the main conclusions and revelations obtained from the theoretical and numerical analysis.

The primary conclusions of the present investigation are that a rise in Darcy Forchheimer number and Darcy factor values causes a decline in flow distribution.Heat transportation in the liquid state increases as the magnetic factor rises, but the flow field decreases.When the inter-phase transmission of heat factor upsurges, the solid state’s rate of heat transmission rises while the fluid phase’s rate decreases.The thermal and solutal field for both phases behaves in the opposite manner, yet the flow profile, rate of heat transfers of the both states all increase with the Marangoni convection factor. Therefore, when surface tension and bulk magnetism among molecules on the surface improve, temperatures decrease.Elevating the activation energy parameter values accelerates the solutal dispersion and mass transmission rate.The heat transfer findings obtained from the YOM are more effective than those obtained from the HCM trihybrid nanofluid model.
