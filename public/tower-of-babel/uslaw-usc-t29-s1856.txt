29 U.S.C. § 1856 — Waiver of rights
Citation: us:usc:29:1856
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section1856
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 20 (Migrant And Seasonal Agricultural Worker Protection); Subchapter V (General Provisions); Part A (Enforcement Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Agreements by employees purporting to waive or to modify their rights under this chapter shall be void as contrary to public policy, except that a waiver or modification of rights in favor of the Secretary shall be valid for purposes of enforcement of this chapter.
