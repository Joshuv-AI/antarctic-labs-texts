7 C.F.R. § 632.23 — Access to land unit and records.
Citation: us:cfr:7:632.23
Creator: Natural Resources Conservation Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-632.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 632 (Rural Abandoned Mine Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any authorized NRCS employee or agent is to have the right of access to land under application or contract and the right to examine any program records to ascertain the accuracy of any representations made in the application or contract. This includes the right to furnish technical assistance and to inspect work done under the contract.
