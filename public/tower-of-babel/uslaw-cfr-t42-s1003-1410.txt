42 C.F.R. § 1003.1410 — Amount of penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:42:1003.1410
Creator: Office Of Inspector General-Health Care, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-1003.1410
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 1003 (Civil Money Penalties, Assessments And Exclusions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The OIG may impose a penalty of not more than $1,000,000 per violation.
(a) For this subpart, violation means a practice, as defined in 45 CFR 171.102, that constitutes information blocking, as set forth in 45 CFR part 171.
