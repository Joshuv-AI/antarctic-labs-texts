20 C.F.R. § 202.11 — Termination of employer status.
Citation: us:cfr:20:202.11
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-202.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 202 (Employers Under The Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The employer status of any company or person shall terminate whenever such company or person loses any of the characteristics essential to the existence of an employer status.
