46 C.F.R. § 107.415 — Safety Management Certificate.
Citation: us:cfr:46:107.415
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-107.415
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 107 (Inspection And Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) All self-propelled mobile offshore drilling units of 500 gross tons or over to which 33 CFR part 96 applies, on an international voyage must have a valid Safety Management Certificate and a copy of their company's valid Document of Compliance certificate on board.
(b) A Safety Management Certificate is issued for a period of not more than 60 months.
