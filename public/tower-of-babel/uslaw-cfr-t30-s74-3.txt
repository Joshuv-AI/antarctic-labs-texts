30 C.F.R. § 74.3 — Sampler unit.
Citation: us:cfr:30:74.3
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-74.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 74 (Coal Mine Dust Sampling Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A CMDPSU shall consist of:
(a) A pump unit,
(b) A sampling head assembly, and
(c) If rechargeable batteries are used in the pump unit, a battery charger.
