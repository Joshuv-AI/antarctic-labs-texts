10 C.F.R. § 712.2 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:10:712.2
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-712.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 712 (Human Reliability Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The HRP applies to all applicants for, or current employees of DOE or NNSA or a DOE or NNSA contractor or subcontractor in a position defined or designated under § 712.10 of this subpart as an HRP position.
