39 C.F.R. § 230.30 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:39:230.30
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-230.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 230 (Office Of Inspector General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes procedures governing the disposition of any property (real, personal, tangible, or intangible) obtained by the United States Postal Service Office of Inspector General (Office of Inspector General) for possible use as evidence after the need to retain such property no longer exists.
