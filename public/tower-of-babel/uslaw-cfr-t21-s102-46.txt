21 C.F.R. § 102.46 — Pacific whiting.
Citation: us:cfr:21:102.46
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-102.46
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 102 (Common Or Usual Name For Nonstandardized Foods). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

“Pacific whiting” or “North Pacific whiting” is the common or usual name of the food fish Merluccius productus.
