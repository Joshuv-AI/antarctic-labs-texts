14 C.F.R. § 1204.1400 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1204.1400
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1204.1400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1204 (Administrative Authority And Policy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart establishes the responsibility and sets forth the conditions and procedures for the use of NASA airfield facilities by aircraft not operated for the benefit of the Federal Government.
