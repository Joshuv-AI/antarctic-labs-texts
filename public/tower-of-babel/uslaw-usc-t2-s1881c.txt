2 U.S.C. § 1881c — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:2:1881c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1881c
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 28 (Architect Of The Capitol); Subchapter V (Capitol-Flown Flags For Families Of Fallen Heroes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated for each of fiscal years 2017 through 2028 such sums as may be necessary to carry out this subchapter, to be derived from amounts appropriated in each such fiscal year for the operation of the Architect of the Capitol, except that the aggregate amount appropriated to carry out this subchapter for all such fiscal years may not exceed $40,000.
