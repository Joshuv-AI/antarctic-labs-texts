7 C.F.R. § 1212.85 — Personal liability.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1212.85
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Miscellaneous Commodities), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1212.85
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1212 (Honey Packers And Importers Research, Promotion, Consumer Education And Industry Information Order). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No member, alternate member, or employee of the Board may be held personally responsible, either individually or jointly with others, in any way whatsoever to any person for errors in judgment, mistakes, or other acts, either of commission or omission, as a member, alternate member, or employee, except for acts of dishonesty or willful misconduct.
