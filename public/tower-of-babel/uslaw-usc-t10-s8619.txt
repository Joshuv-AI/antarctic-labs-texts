10 U.S.C. § 8619 — Leases of waterfront property from States or municipalities
Citation: us:usc:10:8619
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8619
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 861 (Secretary Of The Navy: Miscellaneous Powers And Duties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In leasing waterfront property from a State or municipality, the Secretary of the Navy may provide in the lease, where it is required by state law or municipal charter, that, as part or all of the consideration, any improvements placed upon the property by the United States become the property of the lessor when the lease, including any renewal, ends.
