15 U.S.C. § 1516a — Statistics relating to social, health, and economic conditions of Americans of Spanish origin or descent
Citation: us:usc:15:1516a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1516a
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 40 (Department Of Commerce). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Department of Commerce, the Department of Labor, the Department of Health and Human Services, and the Department of Agriculture shall each collect, and publish regularly, statistics which indicate the social, health, and economic condition of Americans of Spanish origin or descent.
