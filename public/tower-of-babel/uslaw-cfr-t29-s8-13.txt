29 C.F.R. § 8.13 — Right to counsel.
Citation: us:cfr:29:8.13
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-8.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 8 (Practice Before The Administrative Review Board With Regard To Federal Service Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each interested party shall have the right to appear in person or by counsel or other representative in any proceeding before the Board.
