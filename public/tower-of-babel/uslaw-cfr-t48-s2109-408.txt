48 C.F.R. § 2109.408 — 2109.408 Certification regarding debarment, suspension, proposed debarment and other responsibility matters.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2109 (Contractor Qualifications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

FAR subpart 9.4 is implemented by changing the FAR offeror's certification at FAR 52.209-5 (which is part of a solicitation) to a pre-contract certificate and a contract clause. These provisions reflect the FEGLI Program's statutory exemption from competitive bidding (5 U.S.C. 8709), which obviates the issuance of solicitations.
