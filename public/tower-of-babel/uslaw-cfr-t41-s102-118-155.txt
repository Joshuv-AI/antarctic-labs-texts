41 C.F.R. § 102-118.155 — -118.155 Records.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-118.155
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-118.155
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-118 (118—Transportation Payment And Audit). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies must properly maintain and store transportation records, including paid transportation bills, in accordance with the General Records Schedule 1.1 et seq. (36 CFR part 1220).
