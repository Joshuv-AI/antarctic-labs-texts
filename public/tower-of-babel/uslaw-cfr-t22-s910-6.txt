22 C.F.R. § 910.6 — Pending grievances.
Citation: us:cfr:22:910.6
Creator: Foreign Service Grievance Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-910.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 910 (Miscellaneous). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any grievance pending before the Board prior to February 15, 1981 shall be resolved under the provisions of the Foreign Service Act of 1946 as amended, and the regulations promulgated thereunder.
