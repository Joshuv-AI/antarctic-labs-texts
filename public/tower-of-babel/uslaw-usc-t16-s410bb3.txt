16 U.S.C. § 410bb–3 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:410bb–3
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section410bb–3
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LIX (Klondike Gold Rush National Historical Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are hereby authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary to carry out the purposes of this subchapter, but not more than $2,655,000 for the acquisition of lands and interests in lands, and not more than $5,885,000 for development.
