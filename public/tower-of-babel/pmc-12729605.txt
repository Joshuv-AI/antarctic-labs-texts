Correction: Aydin and Diken (2025). Examining Problem Behaviors and Social Skills in Preschoolers Exhibiting Early Signs of Learning Disabilities. Behavioral Sciences, 15(8), 1087
Authors: Yalcin Aydin, Ibrahim Halil Diken
Published: 2025

An error was identified in the original publication (Aydin & Diken, 2025) concerning the formatting of the references. While adapting the citations to match the journal’s style, an artificial intelligence tool was employed that, beyond reformatting, unintentionally altered some details. These included the replacement of journal titles, changes to volume and issue numbers, and in certain cases, the creation of references that did not exist. These issues went unnoticed at the time of submission.

As part of the correction, the entire reference list was thoroughly reviewed, all errors were amended, and DOIs were added where applicable to ensure the list’s accuracy and completeness. Additionally, the text was corrected to reflect the details in the reference list. This was carried out in addition to wording revisions.
