Stochastic motions of the two-dimensional many-body delta-Bose gas, III: Path integrals Support from an NSERC Discovery grant is gratefully acknowledged.
Yu-Ting Chen

Abstract
This paper is the third in a series devoted to constructing stochastic motions for the two-dimensional N N -body delta-Bose gas for all integers N ≥ 3 N\geq 3 and establishing the associated Feynman–Kac-type formulas. The main results here prove the Feynman–Kac-type formulas by using the stochastic many- δ \delta motions from [ 7 ] as the underlying diffusions. The associated multiplicative functionals show a new form and are derived from the analytic solutions of the two-dimensional N N -body delta-Bose gas obtained in [ 4 ] . For completeness, the main theorem includes the formula for N = 2 N=2 , which is a minor modification of the Feynman–Kac-type formula proven in [ 5 ] for the relative motions.



Keywords:
Delta-Bose gas; Schrödinger operators; diagrammatic expansions; path integrals; Bessel processes.

\displaystyle\mathcal{E}_{N}\,{\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\rm def}}{{=}}}\,\{\mathbf{j}=(j\prime,j)\in\mathbb{N}^{2};1\leq j<j\prime\leq N\}.

The main properties of this stochastic many-

The primary applications of the above stochastic many-

N

\delta

\boldsymbol{\delta}

6

\delta

Main results of this paper

The main results of this paper are summarized as the following theorem. It gives Feynman–Kac-type formulas
for the analytic solutions
Q^{{\boldsymbol{\beta}};N}_{0;t}f(z_{0})
in (
1.4

N=2
, the formula is a minor modification of the one proven in
[
5
]
for the relative motions.

Theorem 1.1 (Feynman–Kac-type formulas).

Let
N\geq 2
be an integer,
{\boldsymbol{\beta}}\in(0,\infty)^{\mathcal{E}_{N}}
, and
\boldsymbol{w}\in(0,\infty)^{\mathcal{E}_{N}}
.
For any
z_{0}\in{\mathbb{C}}^{\it N}
with

\displaystyle Q^{{\boldsymbol{\beta}};N}_{0;t}f(z_{0})={\mathbb{E}}^{{\boldsymbol{\beta}},\boldsymbol{w}}_{z_{0}}\Biggl[\frac{{\mathrm{e}}^{{A}^{{\boldsymbol{\beta}},\boldsymbol{w}}_{0}(t)}\sum_{\mathbf{j}\in\mathcal{E}_{N}}w_{\mathbf{j}}K_{0}(\sqrt{2\beta_{\mathbf{j}}}|Z^{\mathbf{j}}_{0}|)}{\sum_{\mathbf{j}\in\mathcal{E}_{N}}w_{\mathbf{j}}K_{0}(\sqrt{2\beta_{\mathbf{j}}}|Z^{\mathbf{j}}_{t}|)}f(\mathscr{Z}_{t});t<T_{\partial}\Biggr],\;\;\forall\;f\in\mathscr{B}_{b,+}({\mathbb{C}}^{\it N}),

(1.7)

where
K_{\nu}(\cdot)
is the Macdonald function of index
\nu
,

\displaystyle\begin{split}{A}^{{\boldsymbol{\beta}},\boldsymbol{w}}_{0}(t)&\,{\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\rm def}}{{=}}}\,\sum_{\mathbf{i},\mathbf{j}\in\mathcal{E}_{N},\mathbf{i}\neq\mathbf{j}}2\left(\frac{w_{\mathbf{j}}}{w_{\mathbf{i}}}\right)\int_{0}^{t}K_{0}(\sqrt{2\beta_{\mathbf{j}}}|Z^{\mathbf{j}}_{s}|){\mathrm{d}}L^{\mathbf{i}}_{s}+\int_{0}^{t}\frac{\sum_{\mathbf{j}\in\mathcal{E}_{N}}\beta_{\mathbf{j}}w_{\mathbf{j}}K_{0}(\sqrt{2\beta_{\mathbf{j}}}|Z^{\mathbf{j}}_{s}|)}{\sum_{\mathbf{j}\in\mathcal{E}_{N}}w_{\mathbf{j}}K_{0}(\sqrt{2\beta_{\mathbf{j}}}|Z^{\mathbf{j}}_{s}|)}{\mathrm{d}}s,\end{split}

(1.8)

and
\{L^{\mathbf{i}}_{t}\}
is the local time of the

\{Z^{\mathbf{i}}_{t}\,{\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\rm def}}{{=}}}\,(Z^{i\prime}_{t}-Z^{i}_{t})/{\sqrt{2}}\}

Note that in the formula of (
1.7

\boldsymbol{w}
in the multiplicative functional and the stochastic many-
\delta

2.4

1.1

2.5

Theorem
1.1
for

is established in this paper in the finer form of Theorem
