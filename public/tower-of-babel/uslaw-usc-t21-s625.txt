21 U.S.C. § 625 — Inapplicability of certain requirements to catfish
Citation: us:usc:21:625
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section625
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 12 (Meat Inspection); Subchapter I (Inspection Requirements; Adulteration And Misbranding). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter, the requirements of sections 603, 604, 605, 610(b), and 623 of this title shall not apply to any fish described in section 601(w)(2) of this title.
