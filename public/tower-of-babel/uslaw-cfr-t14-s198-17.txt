14 C.F.R. § 198.17 — Ground support and other coverage.
Citation: us:cfr:14:198.17
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-198.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 198 (Aviation Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An aircraft operator may apply for insurance to cover any risks arising from the provision of goods or services directly supporting the operation of an aircraft that meets the requirements of § 198.3(b).
