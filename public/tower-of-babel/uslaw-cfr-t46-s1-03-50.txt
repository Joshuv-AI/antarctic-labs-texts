46 C.F.R. § 1.03-50 — -50 Appeals from decisions or actions of the Great Lakes Pilotage Division (CG-WWM-2).
Citation: us:cfr:46:1.03-50
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-1.03-50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 1 (Organization, General Course And Methods Governing Marine Safety Functions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person directly affected by a decision or action of the Great Lakes Pilotage Division (CG-WWM-2), may make a formal appeal of that decision or action to Commandant (CG-5P), in accordance with the procedures contained in § 1.03-15 of this subpart.
