25 U.S.C. § 41a — Indian inspectors
Citation: us:usc:25:41a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section41a
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 2 (Officers Of Indian Affairs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Indian inspectors shall on and after March 4, 1909 be termed inspectors, and shall be included in the classified service.
