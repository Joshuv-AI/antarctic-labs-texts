5 U.S.C. § 5706b — Interview expenses
Citation: us:usc:5:5706b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section5706b
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart D (Pay And Allowances); Chapter 57 (Travel, Transportation, And Subsistence); Subchapter I (Travel And Subsistence Expenses; Mileage Allowances). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An individual being considered for employment by an agency may be paid travel or transportation expenses under this subchapter for travel to and from pre-employment interviews determined necessary by the agency.
