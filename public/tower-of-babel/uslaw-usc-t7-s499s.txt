7 U.S.C. § 499s — Depositing appropriations in fund
Citation: us:usc:7:499s
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section499s
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 20A (Perishable Agricultural Commodities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any unexpended balances of appropriations for the current fiscal year, and any subsequent appropriations, made to carry out the Acts referred to in section 499c(b) of this title, may be deposited in the Perishable Agricultural Commodities Act fund.
