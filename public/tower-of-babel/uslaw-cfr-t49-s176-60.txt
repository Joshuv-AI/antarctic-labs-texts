49 C.F.R. § 176.60 — “No Smoking” signs.
Citation: us:cfr:49:176.60
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-176.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 176 (Carriage By Vessel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When smoking is prohibited during the loading, stowing, storing, transportation, or unloading of hazardous materials by this part, the carrier and the master of the vessel are jointly responsible for posting “NO SMOKING” signs in conspicuous locations.
