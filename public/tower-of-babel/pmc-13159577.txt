Response to letter regarding “relationship between menopausal hormone therapy and incidence risk of breast cancer: systematic review and meta-analysis”
Authors: Qing Wu, Zhijun Dai
Published: 2026

Dear Editor,

We thank Pr. Anne Gompel for her comments on our article [1] and thank the Editor for the opportunity to clarify our study selection and inclusion criteria.

First, regarding the exclusion of studies by Vinogradova et al. and Fournier et al.

Our criteria required that for observational studies the control group be defined as ‘never-users’ of menopausal hormone therapy (MHT). For estrogen-only therapy (ET) we specified systemic estrogen administration and excluded vaginal estrogen formulations. Vinogradova et al. (2020) [2] included topical oestrogen preparations (vaginal pessaries or cream) as part of HRT exposure. Their effect estimates for ‘any HRT use’ or ‘estrogen-only use’ do not separate systemic estrogen use from topical preparations. We could not extract data matching our definition of systemic et al.one, so this study was not eligible for quantitative synthesis. We retained it in the reference list for context.

For the estrogen-progestin therapy (EPT) subgroup analysis, our protocol required a single estimate comparing all EPT users (any progestogen type) with never‑users. Fournier et al [3] reported separate hazard ratios for estrogen + progesterone, estrogen + dydrogesterone, and estrogen + other progestagens, but we could not extract a combined EPT estimate. Fournier et al. [4] examined the risk after discontinuing MHT, and this study design does not meet our inclusion criteria.

Second, the WHI program includes two distinct trials (ET in hysterectomized women, EPT in non‑hysterectomized women) with non‑overlapping populations. We included separate reports for different exposure categories. Sensitivity analyses (Figure 7) confirmed that removing any single WHI report did not alter the pooled estimates (all ORs within the 95% CI of the main result). Hence no bias was introduced.

Sincerely,

Zhijun Dai, MD, PhD
