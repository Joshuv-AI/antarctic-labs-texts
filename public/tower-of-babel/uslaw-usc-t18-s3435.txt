18 U.S.C. § 3435 — Receiver of stolen property triable before or after principal
Citation: us:usc:18:3435
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section3435
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part II (Criminal Procedure); Chapter 221 (Arraignment, Pleas And Trial). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A person charged with receiving or concealing stolen property may be tried either before or after the trial of the principal offender.
