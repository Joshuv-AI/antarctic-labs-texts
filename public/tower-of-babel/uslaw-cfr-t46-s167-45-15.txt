46 C.F.R. § 167.45-15 — -15 Capacity of pipes and hose.
Citation: us:cfr:46:167.45-15
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-167.45-15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 167 (Public Nautical School Ships). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The capacity of the pipes and hose leading from the pumps shall in no case be less than that of the discharge opening of the pump: Provided, however, That the pipe and hose shall in no instance be less than 1 1/2 inches in internal diameter.
