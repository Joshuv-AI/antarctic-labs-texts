42 C.F.R. § 52i.8 — i.8 When and for what purposes may a grantee spend the endowment fund corpus?
Citation: us:cfr:42:52i.8
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-52i.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 52i (PART 52i—JOHN LEWIS NIMHD RESEARCH ENDOWMENT PROGRAM). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A grantee may not withdraw or spend any part of the endowment fund corpus for a total of 20 years from the end of the project period.
(b) At the end of the 20-year period, during which the endowment corpus must be maintained, the grantee institution is encouraged to preserve the endowment fund corpus but may use the endowment fund corpus for any purpose that expands or develops the institution's minority health and/or health disparities research and/or training capacity.
