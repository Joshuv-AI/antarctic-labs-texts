10 U.S.C. § 8664 — Suspension of construction in case of treaty
Citation: us:usc:10:8664
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8664
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 863 (Naval Vessels). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In case of a treaty for the limitation of naval armament to which the United States is a signatory, the President may suspend so much of the authorized naval construction as is necessary to bring the naval vessels of the United States within the limitations agreed upon. Such a suspension does not apply to vessels under construction at the time the suspension is made.
