2 U.S.C. § 2134 — Art exhibits
Citation: us:usc:2:2134
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section2134
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 30 (Operation And Maintenance Of Capitol Complex); Subchapter V (Historical Preservation And Fine Arts); Part D (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No work of art or manufacture other than the property of the United States shall be exhibited in the National Statuary Hall, the Rotunda, Emancipation Hall of the Capitol Visitor Center, or the corridors of the Capitol.
