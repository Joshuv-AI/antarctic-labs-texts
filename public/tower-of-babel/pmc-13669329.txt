Author Correction: A microbial platform for selective biosynthesis of monoterpene esters
Authors: Dianqi Yang, Hong Liang, Xuxu Li, Bowen Wang, Chenyu Zhang, Mengyao Yuan, Kang Zhou, Zhiyong Cui, Panhao Niu, Yi-Lei Zhao, Yuan Liu, Zuobing Xiao, Lianzhong Ai, Yongjin J. Zhou, Baoguo Sun, Xiaoqiang Ma
Published: 2026

Correction to: Nature Communications https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-71681-9, published online 9 April 2026

In the version of this article initially published, there were some terminology and labeling errors. In two section titles in the Results, “monoterpenyl ester” now replaces “monoterpenoate ester”; in several instances in the Results “benzyl alcohol with benzoyl-CoA” now replaces the original “benzoyl-CoA with benzoyl-CoA”; “geranyl-CoA” has been revised to read “geranoyl-CoA” and “perillyl-CoA” to “perilloyl-CoA” in Fig. 3, the legends for Figs. 1 and 3, Supplementary Fig. 37 and associated Source Data file labels; in the Methods, “perillyl generate” has been revised to read “(S)-(-)-perillyl geranate” and “geranyl generate” to “geranyl geranate”; and the scope description for the reported chemical-space coverage has been corrected in the Discussion section, revising “61.2% of the theoretical chemical structure space of MEs” to read “61.2% of the predefined combinatorial ME space evaluated in this study.” The changes to Fig. 3, the text, Supplementary Information and Source Data have been made in the HTML and PDF versions of the file.
