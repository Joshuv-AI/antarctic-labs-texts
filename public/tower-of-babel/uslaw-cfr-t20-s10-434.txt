20 C.F.R. § 10.434 — If OWCP finds that the recipient of an overpayment was not at fault, what criteria are used to decide whether to waive recovery of it?
Citation: us:cfr:20:10.434
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-10.434
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 10 (Claims For Compensation Under The Federal Employees' Compensation Act, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If OWCP finds that the recipient of an overpayment was not at fault, repayment will still be required unless:
(a) Adjustment or recovery of the overpayment would defeat the purpose of the FECA (see § 10.436), or
(b) Adjustment or recovery of the overpayment would be against equity and good conscience (see § 10.437).
