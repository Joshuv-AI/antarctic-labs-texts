23 C.F.R. § 635.405 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:23:635.405
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-635.405
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 635 (Construction And Maintenance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirements and procedures prescribed in this subpart apply to all contracts relating to Federal-aid highway projects.
