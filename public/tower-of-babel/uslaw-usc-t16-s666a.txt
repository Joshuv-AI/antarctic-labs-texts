16 U.S.C. § 666a — Penalties
Citation: us:usc:16:666a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section666a
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 5A (Protection And Conservation Of Wildlife); Subchapter I (Game, Fur-Bearing Animals, And Fish). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person who shall violate any rule or regulation promulgated in accordance with this Act shall be guilty of a misdemeanor and upon conviction thereof shall be fined not more than $500 or imprisoned for not more than one year, or both.
