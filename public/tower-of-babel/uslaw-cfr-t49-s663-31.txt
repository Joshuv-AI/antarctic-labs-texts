49 C.F.R. § 663.31 — Post-delivery audit requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:49:663.31
Creator: Federal Transit Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-663.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 663 (Pre-Award And Post-Delivery Audits Of Rolling Stock Purchases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A recipient purchasing revenue service rolling stock with FTA funds must ensure that a post-delivery audit under this part is complete before title to the rolling stock is transferred to the recipient.
