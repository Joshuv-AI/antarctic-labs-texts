46 C.F.R. § 154.1740 — Vinyl chloride: Inhibiting and inerting.
Citation: us:cfr:46:154.1740
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-154.1740
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 154 (Safety Standards For Self-Propelled Vessels Carrying Bulk Liquefied Gases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a vessel is carrying vinyl chloride, the master shall ensure that:
(a) Section 154.1818 is met; or
(b) Section 154.1710 is met, and the oxygen content of inert gas is less than 0.1% by volume.
