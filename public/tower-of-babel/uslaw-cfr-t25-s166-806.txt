25 C.F.R. § 166.806 — What actions does the BIA take against trespassers?
Citation: us:cfr:25:166.806
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-166.806
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 166 (Grazing Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the trespasser fails to take the corrective action specified by us, we may take one or more of the following actions, as appropriate:
(a) Seize, impound, sell or dispose of unauthorized livestock or other property involved in the trespass. We may keep such property we seize for use as evidence.
(b) Assess penalties, damages, and costs, under § 166.812 of this subpart.
