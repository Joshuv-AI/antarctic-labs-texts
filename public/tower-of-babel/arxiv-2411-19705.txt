On zeros of discrete paraorthogonal polynomials on the unit circle
G. Gordillo-Núñez, A. Suzuki

Abstract
. In this note we investigate, as a natural continuation of [K. Castillo, Constr. Approx., 55 (2022) 605-627], the behaviour of the zeros of discrete paraorthogonal polynomials on the unit circle with respect to a real parameter.

2.
Main results

We begin this section with the following result.

Theorem 2.1
.

Let

\mathrm{d}\mu(\theta;t)=\sum_{j=0}^{N}\gamma_{j}(t)\,\delta(\theta-\omega_{j}(t))

be a finite nonnegative measure on the unit circle parametrized by
z=e^{i\theta}
and depending on a parameter
t
varying in a real open interval containing
t_{0}
. Suppose that
\gamma_{j}(t)
and
\omega_{j}(t)
are differentiable functions of
t
. Let
P(\cdot;t)
be a nonconstant monic POPUC of degree
n
associated with
\mathrm{d}\mu(\theta;t)
. Assume that
P(\zeta_{0};t_{0})=0
. Then there exist
\epsilon>0
and
\delta>0
, and a unique function,
\zeta:I_{\epsilon}(t_{0})\to C_{\delta}(\zeta_{0})
, differentiable on
I_{\epsilon}(t_{0})
, with
\zeta(t_{0})=\zeta_{0}
, and such that
P(\zeta(t);t)=0
for each
t\in I_{\epsilon}(t_{0})
. Assume that
P(e^{i\theta_{0}};t)=0
for each
t\in I_{\epsilon}(t_{0})
, with
\zeta_{0}\neq e^{i\theta_{0}}
. Denote the zeros of
P(\cdot;t)
by
\zeta_{k}(t)=e^{i\varphi_{k}(t)}

(k=1,\dots,n)
and define

(5)

\displaystyle s(\theta;t)

\displaystyle=\dfrac{\sin{\left(\dfrac{\varphi(t)-\theta_{0}}{2}\right)}}{2\sin{\left(\dfrac{\varphi(t)-\theta}{2}\right)}\sin{\left(\dfrac{\theta_{0}-\theta}{2}\right)}},

(6)

\displaystyle S(\theta;t)

\displaystyle=\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{n}\;\dfrac{1}{1+\delta_{k,n-1}+\delta_{k,n}}\,\cot{\left(\dfrac{\varphi_{k}(t)-\theta}{2}\right)},

where we have set
\theta_{0}=\varphi_{n-1}(t)
and
\varphi(t)=\varphi_{n}(t)
. Then
\zeta(t)=e^{i\varphi(t)}
moves strictly counterclockwise along
\mathbb{S}^{1}
as
t
increases on
I_{\epsilon}(t_{0})
, provided that

\displaystyle W_{j}(t)=s(\omega_{j}(t);t)\,\dfrac{\mathrm{d}\gamma_{j}}{\mathrm{d}t}(t)-\gamma_{j}(t)s(\omega_{j}(t);t)S(\omega_{j}(t);t)\dfrac{\mathrm{d}\omega_{j}}{\mathrm{d}t}(t)

is nonnegative for all
j
and nonzero for at least one value of
j
, and
\omega_{j}(t)\neq\varphi_{k}(t)
for all
j,k
. Furthermore, if
W_{j}(t)=0
for all
t\in I_{\epsilon}(t_{0})
and all
j
, then
\zeta(t)
does not move as
t
increases on
I_{\epsilon}(t_{0})
.

Proof.

The first part of the theorem follows from the proof of
[
2
, Proposition 3.1]
and the details are left to the reader.

Assume
P
has fixed degree
n\geq 2
and write
P_{n}
instead of
P
. Set
\xi=e^{i\theta_{0}}
. Following the proof of
[
2
, Theorem 3.1]
and using
[
2
, (15)]
, we obtain, after elementary computations,

(7)

\displaystyle\dfrac{\mathrm{d}\varphi}{\mathrm{d}t}(t)

\displaystyle=\displaystyle\frac{\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{N}\gamma_{j}(t)\dfrac{ie^{i\omega_{j}(t)}(\xi-\zeta(t))P_{n}(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)};t)}{(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}-\xi)(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}-\zeta(t))}\left.\overline{\dfrac{\partial P_{n}}{\partial t}(e^{i\theta};t)}\;\right|_{\theta=\omega_{j}(t)}}{\displaystyle\int\left|\dfrac{P_{n}(e^{i\theta};t)}{e^{i\theta}-\zeta(t)}\right|^{2}\mathrm{d}\mu(\theta;t)},

with the denominator on the right-hand side being positive.
Let
\tau\in I_{\epsilon}(t_{0})
. Since

\dfrac{zP_{n}(z;\tau)}{(z-\xi)(z-\zeta(\tau))}

is a nonzero polynomial of degree

(8)

\displaystyle\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{N}\gamma_{j}(t)\dfrac{e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}P_{n}(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)};\tau)}{(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}-\xi)(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}-\zeta(\tau))}\overline{P_{n}(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)};t)}=0.

Now, taking partial derivative of (
8
) with respect to
t
yields

(9)

\displaystyle\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{N}\gamma_{j}(t)\dfrac{e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}P_{n}(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)};\tau)}{(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}-\xi)(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}-\zeta(\tau))}\overline{\dfrac{\mathrm{d}P_{n}}{\mathrm{d}t}(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)};t)}

\displaystyle=-\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{N}\left(\dfrac{\mathrm{d}\gamma_{j}}{\mathrm{d}t}(t)\dfrac{e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}P_{n}(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)};\tau)}{(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}-\xi)(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}-\zeta(\tau))}\overline{P_{n}(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)};t)}\right.

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\qquad\left.+\gamma_{j}(t)\dfrac{\partial}{\partial t}\left(\dfrac{e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}P_{n}(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)};\tau)}{(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}-\xi)(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}-\zeta(\tau))}\right)\overline{P_{n}(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)};t)}\right).

In addition, note that

\displaystyle\dfrac{e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}P_{n}(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)};\tau)}{(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}-\xi)(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}-\zeta(\tau))}=e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}\displaystyle\prod_{k=1}^{n-2}(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}-\zeta_{k}(\tau)).

Hence,

(10)

\displaystyle\dfrac{\partial}{\partial t}\left(\dfrac{e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}P_{n}(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)};\tau)}{(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}-\xi)(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}-\zeta(\tau))}\right)

\displaystyle=i\dfrac{\mathrm{d}\omega_{j}}{\mathrm{d}t}(t)\dfrac{e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}P_{n}(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)};\tau)}{(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}-\xi)(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}-\zeta(\tau))}\left(1+\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{n-2}\dfrac{e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}}{e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}-\zeta_{k}(\tau)}\right).

Furthermore,

(11)

\displaystyle\dfrac{\mathrm{d}P_{n}}{\mathrm{d}t}(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)};t)

\displaystyle=\left.\dfrac{\partial P_{n}}{\partial t}(e^{i\theta};t)\right|_{\theta=\omega_{j}(t)}+i\dfrac{\mathrm{d}\omega_{j}}{\mathrm{d}t}(t)\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{n}\dfrac{e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}P_{n}(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)};t)}{e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}-\zeta_{k}(t)}.

Substituting (
10
) and (
11
) into (
9
), we can assert that

(12)

\displaystyle-\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{N}\gamma_{j}(t)\dfrac{e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}P_{n}(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)};\tau)}{(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}-\xi)(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}-\zeta(\tau))}\left.\overline{\dfrac{\partial P_{n}}{\partial t}(e^{i\theta};t)}\right|_{\theta=\omega_{j}(t)}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{N}\dfrac{e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}P_{n}(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)};\tau)\overline{P_{n}(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)};t)}}{(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}-\xi)(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}-\zeta(\tau))}

\displaystyle\quad\!\times\!\left(\!\dfrac{\mathrm{d}\gamma_{j}}{\mathrm{d}t}(t)\!+\!i\gamma_{j}(t)\dfrac{\mathrm{d}\omega_{j}}{\mathrm{d}t}(t)\!\left(1\!-\!\displaystyle\sum^{n}_{k=1}\dfrac{e^{-i\omega_{j}(t)}}{e^{-i\omega_{j}(t)}-\overline{\zeta_{k}(t)}}\!+\!\displaystyle\sum^{n-2}_{k=1}\dfrac{e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}}{e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}-\zeta_{k}(t)}\right)\!\!\right).

Multiplying (
12
) by
i(\xi-\zeta(\tau))
and letting
\tau\rightarrow t
yields

(13)

\displaystyle\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{N}\gamma_{j}(t)\dfrac{ie^{i\omega_{j}(t)}(\xi-\zeta(t))P_{n}(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)};t)}{(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}-\xi)(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}-\zeta(t))}\left.\overline{\dfrac{\partial P_{n}}{\partial t}(e^{i\theta};t)}\right|_{\theta=\omega_{j}(t)}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{N}\dfrac{ie^{i\omega_{j}(t)}(\zeta(t)-\xi)\left|P_{n}(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)};t)\right|^{2}}{(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}-\xi)(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}-\zeta(t))}

\displaystyle\quad\!\times\!\left(\!\dfrac{\mathrm{d}\gamma_{j}}{\mathrm{d}t}(t)\!+\!i\gamma_{j}(t)\dfrac{\mathrm{d}\omega_{j}}{\mathrm{d}t}(t)\!\left(1\!-\!\displaystyle\sum^{n}_{k=1}\dfrac{e^{-i\omega_{j}(t)}}{e^{-i\omega_{j}(t)}-\overline{\zeta_{k}(t)}}\!+\!\displaystyle\sum^{n-2}_{k=1}\dfrac{e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}}{e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}-\zeta_{k}(t)}\right)\!\right).

It is straightforward to check that

(14)

\displaystyle s(\theta;t)

\displaystyle=\dfrac{i(\zeta(t)-\xi)e^{i\theta}}{(e^{i\theta}-\xi)(e^{i\theta}-\zeta(t))},

(15)

\displaystyle S(\theta;t)

\displaystyle=-i\left(1-\displaystyle\sum^{n}_{k=1}\dfrac{e^{-i\theta}}{e^{-i\theta}-\overline{\zeta_{k}(t)}}+\displaystyle\sum^{n-2}_{k=1}\dfrac{e^{i\theta}}{e^{i\theta}-\zeta_{k}(t)}\right).

Hence, combining (
7
) and (

),

(16)

\displaystyle\dfrac{\mathrm{d}\varphi}{\mathrm{d}t}(t)\displaystyle\int\left|\dfrac{P_{n}(e^{i\theta};t)}{e^{i\theta}-\zeta(t)}\right|^{2}\mathrm{d}\mu(\theta;t)

\displaystyle=\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{N}W_{j}(t)\left|P_{n}(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)};t)\right|^{2},

and the second part of the theorem follows. The last part of the theorem follows directly from (
16
).

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{N}\dfrac{ie^{i\omega_{j}(t)}(\zeta(t)-\overline{\zeta(t)})\left|P_{n}(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)};t)\right|^{2}}{(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}-\overline{\zeta(t)})(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}-\zeta(t))}

\displaystyle\quad\times\!\left(\!\dfrac{\mathrm{d}\gamma_{j}}{\mathrm{d}t}(t)\!+\!i\gamma_{j}(t)\dfrac{\mathrm{d}\omega_{j}}{\mathrm{d}t}(t)\!\left(1\!-\!\displaystyle\sum^{n}_{k=1}\dfrac{e^{-i\omega_{j}(t)}}{e^{-i\omega_{j}(t)}-\overline{\zeta_{k}(t)}}\!+\!\displaystyle\sum^{n-2}_{k=1}\dfrac{e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}}{e^{i\omega_{j}(t)}-\zeta_{k}(t)}\right)\!\right).

Again, following (
14
) and (
15
), it is straightforward to check that

\displaystyle 2\Im{(\zeta(t))}

\displaystyle=i(\overline{\zeta(t)}-\zeta(t)),

\displaystyle\widetilde{s}(\theta;t)

\displaystyle=-\dfrac{e^{i\theta}}{(e^{i\theta}-\overline{\zeta(t)})(e^{i\theta}-\zeta(t))},

\displaystyle\widetilde{S}(\theta;t)

\displaystyle=-i\left(1-\displaystyle\sum^{n}_{k=1}\dfrac{e^{-i\theta}}{e^{-i\theta}-\overline{\zeta_{k}(t)}}+\displaystyle\sum^{n-2}_{k=1}\dfrac{e^{i\theta}}{e^{i\theta}-\zeta_{k}(t)}\right).

Hence, combining (

) and (

), we deduce

\displaystyle C(t)\dfrac{\mathrm{d}\varphi}{\mathrm{d}t}(t)

\displaystyle=2\Im{(\zeta(t))}\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{N}\widetilde{W}_{j}(t)\left|P_{n}(e^{i\omega_{j}(t)};t)\right|^{2},

and the result follows as in the end of the proof of Theorem
2.1
.
∎
