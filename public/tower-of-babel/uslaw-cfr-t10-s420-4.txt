10 C.F.R. § 420.4 — Technical assistance.
Citation: us:cfr:10:420.4
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-420.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 420 (State Energy Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At the request of the Governor of any State to DOE and subject to the availability of personnel and funds, DOE will provide information and technical assistance to the State in connection with effectuating the purposes of this part.
