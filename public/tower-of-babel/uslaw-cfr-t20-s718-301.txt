20 C.F.R. § 718.301 — Establishing length of employment as a miner.
Citation: us:cfr:20:718.301
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-718.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 718 (Standards For Determining Coal Miners' Total Disability Or Death Due To Pneumoconiosis). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The presumptions set forth in §§ 718.302 and 718.305 apply only if a miner worked in one or more coal mines for the number of years required to invoke the presumption. The length of the miner's coal mine work history must be computed as provided by 20 CFR 725.101(a)(32).
