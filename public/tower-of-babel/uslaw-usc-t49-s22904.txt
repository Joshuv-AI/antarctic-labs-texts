49 U.S.C. § 22904 — Use of capital grants to finance first-dollar liability of grant project
Citation: us:usc:49:22904
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section22904
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part B (Assistance); Chapter 229 (Rail Improvement Grants). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding the requirements of section 22902 of this chapter, the Secretary of Transportation may approve the use of a capital assistance grant under this chapter to fund self-insured retention of risk for the first tier of liability insurance coverage for rail passenger service associated with the grant, but the coverage may not exceed $20,000,000 per occurrence or $20,000,000 in aggregate per year.
