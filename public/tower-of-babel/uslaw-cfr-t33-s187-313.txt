33 C.F.R. § 187.313 — Effect of certificate of title.
Citation: us:cfr:33:187.313
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-187.313
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 187 (Vessel Identification System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A certificate of title is prima facie evidence of the accuracy of the information in the record that constitutes the certificate.
