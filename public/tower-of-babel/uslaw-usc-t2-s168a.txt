2 U.S.C. § 168a — Printing of Constitution Annotated as Senate documents
Citation: us:usc:2:168a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section168a
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 5 (Library Of Congress). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All hardbound revised editions and all cumulative pocket-part supplements shall be printed as Senate documents.
