42 U.S.C. § 1395b — Option to individuals to obtain other health insurance protection
Citation: us:usc:42:1395b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1395b
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 7 (Social Security); Subchapter XVIII (Health Insurance For Aged And Disabled). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing contained in this subchapter shall be construed to preclude any State from providing, or any individual from purchasing or otherwise securing, protection against the cost of any health services.
