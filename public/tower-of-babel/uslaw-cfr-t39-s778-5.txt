39 C.F.R. § 778.5 — What is the Postal Service's obligation with respect to federal interagency coordination?
Citation: us:cfr:39:778.5
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-778.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 778 (Intergovernmental Review Of Postal Service Facility Actions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Postal Service, to the extent practicable, consults with and seeks advice from other federal departments and agencies substantially affected by Postal Service facility project actions covered under these regulations.
