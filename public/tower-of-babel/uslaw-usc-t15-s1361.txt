15 U.S.C. § 1361 — Reference services to assist designated agencies to obtain information outside State
Citation: us:usc:15:1361
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1361
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 37 (State Technical Services). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized and directed to aid designated agencies in carrying out their technical services programs by providing reference services which a designated agency may use to obtain scientific, technical, and engineering information from sources outside the State or States which it serves, for the purposes of this chapter.
