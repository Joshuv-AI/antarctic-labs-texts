7 U.S.C. § 2009bb–11 — Annual report
Citation: us:usc:7:2009bb–11
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2009bb–11
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 50 (Agricultural Credit); Subchapter VII (Northern Great Plains Regional Authority). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 180 days after the end of each fiscal year, the Authority shall submit to the President and to Congress a report describing the activities carried out under this subchapter.
