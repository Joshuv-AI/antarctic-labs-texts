49 C.F.R. § 380.103 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:49:380.103
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-380.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 380 (Special Training Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules in this part apply to all operators of LCVs in interstate commerce, employers of such persons, and LCV driver-instructors.
