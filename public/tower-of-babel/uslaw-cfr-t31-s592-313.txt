31 C.F.R. § 592.313 — Voided certificate.
Citation: us:cfr:31:592.313
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-592.313
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 592 (Rough Diamonds Control Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term voided certificate means a Kimberley Process Certificate intended to be used for the exportation of rough diamonds from the United States that has been cancelled for reasons such as loss or error.
