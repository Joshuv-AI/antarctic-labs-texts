43 C.F.R. § 2932.32 — When must I pay the fees?
Citation: us:cfr:43:2932.32
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2932.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2930 (Permits For Recreation On Public Lands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must pay the required fees before BLM will authorize your use and by the deadline or deadlines that BLM will establish in each case. We may allow you to make periodic payments for commercial use. We will not process or continue processing your application until you have paid the required fees or installments.
