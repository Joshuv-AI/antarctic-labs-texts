48 C.F.R. § 6104.407 — 6104.407 Reconsideration of Board decision [Rule 407].
Citation: us:cfr:48:6104.407
Creator: Chapter 61—Civilian Board Of Contract Appeals, General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-6104.407
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 6104 (Travel And Relocation Expenses Cases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The claimant or the agency may request reconsideration. A request must be received by the Board within 30 calendar days after the date the decision was issued. The request should state the reasons why the Board should consider the request. Mere disagreement with a decision or re-argument of points already made is not a sufficient ground for reconsideration.
