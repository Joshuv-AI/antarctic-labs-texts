30 C.F.R. § 745.14 — Amendments.
Citation: us:cfr:30:745.14
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-745.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 745 (State-Federal Cooperative Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A cooperative agreement which has been approved pursuant to § 745.11 may be amended by mutual agreement of the Secretary and the Governor of a State. Amendments shall be adopted by Federal rulemaking, in accordance with § 745.11.
