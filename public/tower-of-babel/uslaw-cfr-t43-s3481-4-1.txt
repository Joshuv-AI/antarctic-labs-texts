43 C.F.R. § 3481.4-1 — -1 Can I temporarily interrupt coal severance and still be qualified as producing?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3481.4-1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3481.4-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3480 (Coal Exploration And Mining Operations Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, a temporary interruption in coal severance allows you (the lessee/operator) to halt the extraction of coal for a limited period of time without jeopardizing your qualifications under section (2)(a)(2)(A) of MLA to receive additional leases. During the period of a temporary interruption in coal severance, BLM still considers you lease or LMU to be producing so as not to preclude you from receiving a new or transferred lease.
