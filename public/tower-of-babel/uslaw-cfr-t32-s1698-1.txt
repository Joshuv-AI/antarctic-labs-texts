32 C.F.R. § 1698.1 — Purpose.

Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1698.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1698 (Advisory Opinions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this part prescribe the procedures for requesting and processing requests for advisory opinions relative to a named individual's liability for registration under the Military Selective Service Act (MSSA), 50 U.S.C. App. 451 et seq.
