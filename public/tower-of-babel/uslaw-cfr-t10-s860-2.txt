10 C.F.R. § 860.2 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:10:860.2
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-860.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 860 (Trespassing On Department Of Energy Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part apply to all facilities, installations and real property subject to the jurisdiction or administration of the Department of Energy or in its custody which have been posted with a notice of the prohibitions and penalties set forth in this part.
