21 C.F.R. § 520.301 — Caramiphen ethanedisulfonate and ammonium chloride tablets.
Citation: us:cfr:21:520.301
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-520.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 520 (Oral Dosage Form New Animal Drugs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Specifications. Each tablet contains 10 milligrams of 5st caramiphen ethanedisulfonate and 80 milligrams of ammonium chloride.
(b) Sponsor. See No. 054771 in § 510.600(c) of this chapter.
(c) Conditions of use in dogs—(1) Amount. One tablet per 15 to 30 pounds of body weight every 4 to 6 hours.
(2) Indications for use. For relief of cough.
