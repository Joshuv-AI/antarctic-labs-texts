21 U.S.C. § 144 — Unlawful receiving of imported milk or cream
Citation: us:usc:21:144
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section144
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 4 (Animals, Meats, And Meat And Dairy Products); Subchapter IV (Importation Of Milk And Cream). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It shall be unlawful for any person in the United States to receive milk or cream imported into the United States unless the importation is in accordance with the provisions of this subchapter.
