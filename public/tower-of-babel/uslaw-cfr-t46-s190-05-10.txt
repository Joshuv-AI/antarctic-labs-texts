46 C.F.R. § 190.05-10 — -10 Chemical storeroom and lamp room construction.
Citation: us:cfr:46:190.05-10
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-190.05-10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 190 (Construction And Arrangement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Chemical storerooms, lamp, paint, and oil lockers and similar compartments shall be constructed of steel or shall be wholly lined with metal.
