20 U.S.C. § 7945 — Preemption and election of State nonapplicability
Citation: us:usc:20:7945
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section7945
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 70 (Strengthening And Improvement Of Elementary And Secondary Schools); Subchapter VIII (General Provisions); Part F (Uniform Provisions); Subpart 3 (Teacher Liability Protection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Preemption
This subpart preempts the laws of any State to the extent that such laws are inconsistent with this subpart, except that this subpart shall not preempt any State law that provides additional protection from liability relating to teachers.
(b) Election of State regarding nonapplicability
This subpart shall not apply to any civil action in a State court against a teacher with respect to claims arising within that State if such State enacts a statute in accordance with State requirements for enacting legislation—
(1)
citing the authority of this subsection;
(2)
declaring the election of such State that this subpart shall not apply, as of a date certain, to such civil action in the State; and
(3)
containing no other provisions.
