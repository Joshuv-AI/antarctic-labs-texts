32 C.F.R. § 845.11 — Correspondence.
Citation: us:cfr:32:845.11
Creator: Department Of The Air Force
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-845.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 845 (Counsel Fees And Other Expenses In Foreign Tribunals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Judge advocates who advise officers responsible under § 845.3 are authorized to correspond directly with each other and with the Judge Advocate General of the service concerned for advice with regard to payment of counsel fees and other expenses.
