10 C.F.R. § 19.31 — Application for exemptions.
Citation: us:cfr:10:19.31
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-19.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 19 (Notices, Instructions And Reports To Workers: Inspection And Investigations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission may, upon application by any interested person or upon its own initiative, grant such exemptions from the requirements of the regulations in this part as it determines are authorized by law, will not result in undue hazard to life and property.
