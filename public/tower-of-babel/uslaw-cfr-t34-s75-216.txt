34 C.F.R. § 75.216 — Applications that the Secretary may choose not to evaluate for funding.
Citation: us:cfr:34:75.216
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-75.216
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 75 (Direct Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary may choose not to evaluate an application if—
(a) The applicant does not comply with all of the procedural rules that govern the submission of the application; or
(b) The application does not contain the information required under the program.
