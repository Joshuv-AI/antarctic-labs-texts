48 C.F.R. § 903.603 — 903.603 Responsibilities of the contracting officer.
Citation: us:cfr:48:903.603
Creator: Chapter 9—Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-903.603
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 903 (Improper Business Practices And Personal Conflicts Of Interest). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) When the needs of the Government cannot be reasonably supplied by sources other than employees of the Government or sources which are substantially owned or controlled by Government employees, the contracting officer, in accordance with 48 CFR 3.602, may submit, through the HCA, a request to the Senior Procurement Executive, with appropriate justification, for approval of an exception to the prohibitions contained in 48 CFR 3.601.
