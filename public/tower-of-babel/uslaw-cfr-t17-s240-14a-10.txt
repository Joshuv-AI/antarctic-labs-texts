17 C.F.R. § 240.14a-10 — a-10 Prohibition of certain solicitations.
Citation: us:cfr:17:240.14a-10
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-240.14a-10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 240 (General Rules And Regulations, Securities Exchange Act Of 1934). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person making a solicitation which is subject to §§ 240.14a-1 to 240.14a-10 shall solicit:
(a) Any undated or postdated proxy; or
(b) Any proxy which provides that it shall be deemed to be dated as of any date subsequent to the date on which it is signed by the security holder.
