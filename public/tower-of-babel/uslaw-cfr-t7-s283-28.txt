7 C.F.R. § 283.28 — Discovery.
Citation: us:cfr:7:283.28
Creator: Food And Nutrition Administration, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-283.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 283 (Appeals Of Quality Control (“Qc”) Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon motion and as ordered by the ALJ, written interrogatories, written requests for admissions and written requests for the production of documents, may be served by any party to the appeal upon any other party and used in accordance with § 283.12(b).
