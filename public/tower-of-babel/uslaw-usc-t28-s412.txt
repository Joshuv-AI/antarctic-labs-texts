28 U.S.C. § 412 — Sale of Supreme Court reports
Citation: us:usc:28:412
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section412
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part I (Organization Of Courts); Chapter 19 (Distribution Of Reports And Digests). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of the Government Publishing Office, or other printer designated by the Supreme Court of the United States shall print such additional bound volumes and preliminary prints of such reports as may be required for sale to the public. Such additional copies shall be sold by the Superintendent of Documents, as provided by law.
