44 C.F.R. § 60.21 — Purpose of this subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:44:60.21
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-60.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 60 (Criteria For Land Management And Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this subpart is to encourage the formation and adoption of overall comprehensive management plans for flood-prone, mudslide (i.e., mudflow)-prone and flood-related erosion-prone areas. While adoption by a community of the standards in this subpart is not mandatory, the community shall completely evaluate these standards.
