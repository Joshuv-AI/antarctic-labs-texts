12 C.F.R. § 34.6 — Applicability of state law to Federal savings associations and subsidiaries.
Citation: us:cfr:12:34.6
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-34.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 34 (Real Estate Lending And Appraisals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In accordance with section 1046 of the Dodd-Frank Wall Street Reform and Consumer Protection Act (12 U.S.C. 25b), Federal savings associations and their subsidiaries shall be subject to the same laws and legal standards, including regulations of the OCC, as are applicable to national banks and their subsidiaries, regarding the preemption of state law.
