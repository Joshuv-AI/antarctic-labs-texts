49 C.F.R. § 176.112 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:49:176.112
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-176.112
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 176 (Carriage By Vessel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of §§ 176.116(e), 176,118, and 176.120 of this subpart do not apply to Division 1.4 (explosive) materials, compatibility group S. Such materials may be stowed together with all other Class 1 (explosive) materials except those of compatibility group A or L. They must be segregated from other hazardous materials in accordance with table 176.83(b) of this part.
