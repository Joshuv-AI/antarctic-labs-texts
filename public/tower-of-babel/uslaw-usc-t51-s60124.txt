51 U.S.C. § 60124 — Regulatory authority of Secretary
Citation: us:usc:51:60124
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section60124
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 601 (Land Remote Sensing Policy); Subchapter III (Licensing Of Private Remote Sensing Space Systems). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may issue regulations to carry out this subchapter. Such regulations shall be promulgated only after public notice and comment in accordance with the provisions of section 553 of title 5.
