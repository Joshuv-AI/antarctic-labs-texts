44 U.S.C. § 1906 — Land-grant colleges constituted depositories
Citation: us:usc:44:1906
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section1906
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 19 (Depository Library Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Land-grant colleges are constituted depositories to receive Government publications subject to the depository laws.
