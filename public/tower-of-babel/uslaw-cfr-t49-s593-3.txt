49 C.F.R. § 593.3 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:49:593.3
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-593.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 593 (Determinations That A Vehicle Not Originally Manufactured To Conform To The Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standards Is Eligible For Importation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to a motor vehicle that was not originally manufactured and certified by its original manufacturer to conform with all applicable Federal motor vehicle safety standards and that is offered for importation into the United States.
