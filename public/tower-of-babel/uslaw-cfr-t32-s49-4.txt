32 C.F.R. § 49.4 — Consultation with the Department of State.
Citation: us:cfr:32:49.4
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-49.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 49 (Implementation Of The Havana Act Of 2021). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a covered employee or covered dependent seeks payment for an incident that occurred overseas under Secretary of State security responsibility, the Department will coordinate with the Department of State as appropriate in evaluating whether the incident is an “other incident” for purposes of establishing a qualifying injury or should be so designated.
