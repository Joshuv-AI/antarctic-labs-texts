31 C.F.R. § 592.314 — Tamper-resistant container.
Citation: us:cfr:31:592.314
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-592.314
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 592 (Rough Diamonds Control Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term tamper-resistant container means packaging having an indicator or barrier to entry that could reasonably be expected to provide visible evidence that tampering had occurred. Standard mailing and express consignment packaging, or such packaging that simply contains a resealable plastic bag, is not considered to be a tamper-resistant container.
