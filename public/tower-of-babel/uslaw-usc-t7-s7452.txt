7 U.S.C. § 7452 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:7:7452
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section7452
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 101 (Agricultural Promotion); Subchapter III (Canola And Rapeseed). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
There are authorized to be appropriated for each fiscal year such sums as are necessary to carry out this subchapter.
(b) Administrative expenses
Funds appropriated under subsection (a) shall not be available for payment of the expenses or expenditures of the Board in administering a provision of an order issued under this subchapter.
