22 C.F.R. § 228.23 — Other delivery services.
Citation: us:cfr:22:228.23
Creator: Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-228.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 228 (Rules For Procurement Of Commodities And Services Financed By Usaid). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No source or nationality rules apply to other delivery services, such as export packing, loading, commodity inspection services, and services of a freight forwarder. Such services are eligible when provided as part of a commodity procurement financed by USAID.
