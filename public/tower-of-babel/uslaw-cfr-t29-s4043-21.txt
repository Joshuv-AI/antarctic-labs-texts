29 C.F.R. § 4043.21 — Tax disqualification and Title I noncompliance.
Citation: us:cfr:29:4043.21
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4043.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4043 (Reportable Events And Certain Other Notification Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Reportable event. A reportable event occurs when the Secretary of the Treasury issues notice that a plan has ceased to be a plan described in section 4021(a)(2) of ERISA, or when the Secretary of Labor determines that a plan is not in compliance with title I of ERISA.
(b) Waiver. Notice is waived for this event.
