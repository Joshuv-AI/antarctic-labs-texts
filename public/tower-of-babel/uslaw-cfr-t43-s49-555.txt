43 C.F.R. § 49.555 — What procedures govern an appeal of an administrative law judge's decision?
Citation: us:cfr:43:49.555
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-49.555
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 49 (Paleontological Resources Preservation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Upon receipt of a notice of appeal filed under § 49.550(a), the OHA Director will appoint an Ad Hoc Board of Appeals to consider the appeal and issue a decision thereon.
(b) To the extent not inconsistent with the provisions of this subpart, the rules in 43 CFR part 4, subparts A, B, and G, will apply to the appeal proceedings under § 49.550.
