12 C.F.R. § 714.1 — What does this part cover?

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 714 (Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part covers the standards and requirements that you, a federal credit union, must follow when engaged in the leasing of personal property.
