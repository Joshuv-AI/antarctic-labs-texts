12 C.F.R. § 340.6 — What is the effect of this part on transactions that were entered into before its effective date?
Citation: us:cfr:12:340.6
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-340.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 340 (Restrictions On Sale Of Assets Of A Failed Institution By The Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part does not affect the enforceability of a contract of sale and/or agreement for seller financing in effect prior to July 1, 2000.
