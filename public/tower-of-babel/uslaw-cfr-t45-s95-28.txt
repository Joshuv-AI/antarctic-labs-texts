45 C.F.R. § 95.28 — What a waiver request for good cause must include.
Citation: us:cfr:45:95.28
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-95.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 95 (General Administration—Grant Programs (Public Assistance, Medical Assistance And State Children'S Health Insurance Programs)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The State's request for waiver must include a specific explanation, justification or documentation of why the claim is or will be late. This request must establish that the lateness in filing the claim is for good cause as defined in § 95.22 and not due to neglect or administrative inadequacy. If the claim has not been filed, the State must also tell us when the claim will be filed.
