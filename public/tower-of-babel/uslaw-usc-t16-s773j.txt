16 U.S.C. § 773j — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:773j
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section773j
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 10 (Northern Pacific Halibut Fishing); Subchapter IV (Northern Pacific Halibut Act Of 1982). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is hereby authorized to be appropriated for fiscal year 1983 and beyond, such sums as may be necessary for carrying out the Convention and this subchapter, including—
(a)
necessary travel expenses of the United States Commissioners or alternate Commissioners; and
(b)
the United States share of the joint expenses of the Commission: Provided, That the Commissioners shall not, with respect to commitments concerning the United States share of the joint expenses of the Commission, be subject to section 262(b) 1 of title 22 insofar as it limits the authority of United States representatives to international organizations with respect to such commitments.
