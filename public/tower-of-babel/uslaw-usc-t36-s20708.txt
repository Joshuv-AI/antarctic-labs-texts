36 U.S.C. § 20708 — Duty to maintain tax-exempt status
Citation: us:usc:36:20708
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section20708
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 207 (American Council Of Learned Societies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation shall maintain its status as an organization exempt from taxation under the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (26 U.S.C. 1 et seq.). If the corporation does not maintain that status, the charter granted by this chapter expires.
