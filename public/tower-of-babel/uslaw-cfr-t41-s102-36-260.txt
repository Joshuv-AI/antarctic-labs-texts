41 C.F.R. § 102-36.260 — -36.260 Excess medical shelf-life items held for national emergency purposes.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-36.260
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-36.260
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-36 (36—Disposition Of Excess Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the remaining shelf-life of any medical materials or supplies held for national emergency purposes is of too short a period to justify their continued retention, you should report such property excess for possible transfer and disposal. You must make such excess determinations at such time as to ensure that sufficient time remains to permit their use before their shelf-life expires and the items are unfit for human use.
