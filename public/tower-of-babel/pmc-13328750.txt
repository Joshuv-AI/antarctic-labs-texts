TrustFed-RHIO: an optimization-driven differential privacy federated learning framework for secure and explainable IIoT attack detection
Authors: Linda Joseph, B Prabha, M Sambath
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
AbstractA rapid proliferation of industrial internet of things (IIoT) systems has increased the vulnerability of interconnected devices for sophisticated cyberattacks, which necessitates intelligent and privacy-preserving solution for security. This paper presents TrustFed-RHIO, a novel hybrid model which integrates rock hyrax intelligence optimization (RHIO) for optimal selection of feature with a trustworthy differential privacy-enhanced federated learning (TrustFed) scheme for the collaborative detection of attack. The algorithm of RHIO mimics collective intelligence of rock hyrax colonies for identifying most discriminative features, thus reducing dimensionality and enhancing efficiency of classifier. The proposed TrustFed-RHIO scheme ensures, secure, distributed learning over multiple IIoT nodes on embedding differential privacy mechanisms thus mitigating the data leakage risks and adversarial inference. A suggested scheme is thus empowered with explainable artificial intelligence (XAI) scheme termed SHAP and LIME for enhancing interpretability and trust in model predictions. Experimental estimation on benchmark IIoT dataset shows that TrustFed-RHIO attains superior performance on detection accuracy, robustness against adversarial attacks, and privacy preservation on comparing existing schemes. At last, this framework supports secure storage of cloud on detection outcomes, thus enabling scalable deployment in the real-world IIoT framework. The performance evaluation is carried on benchmark dataset CCIoT2024-DIAD and performance is estimated for various metrics like latency, accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, specificity, training time, and so on. Overall analysis shows that the proposed model is effective in detecting IIoT attacks.Keywords: Industrial internet of things, Rock hyrax intelligence optimization, Trustworthy differential privacy-enhanced federated learning (TrustFed), Explainable artificial intelligence (XAI), Attack detection, Cloud storageSubject terms: Engineering, Mathematics and computing

Introduction

An Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) becomes a cornerstone of modern manufacturing and smart infrastructure systems. On interconnecting varied devices, sensors, and control systems over an internet, IIoT enables predictive analytics, real-time monitoring, and autonomous decision making1. Despite these advantages, the explosion of interconnected nodes & heterogenous communication protocols simultaneously increased the attack surface, thus rendering IIoT systems that are vulnerable highly to sophisticated cyber threats2. Malicious entities mostly exploit insecure channel communication, weak authentication, or firmware vulnerabilities thus leading to leakage of data, disruption in service, and even physical damage to industrial assets. Therefore, attaining secure, explainable, and privacy-preserving IIoT attack detection becomes an indispensable need for reliability3–5.

Traditional centralized intrusion detection system (IDS) is inadequate most often for IIoT environments because of their dependency on central repository, that introduces privacy concerns and scalability bottlenecks6–9. However, this massive and heterogenous nature of IIoT data calls for intelligent and distributed learning mechanism competent of adopting to the evolving attack patterns on safeguarding sensitive data10,11. In this regard, Federated Learning (FL) becomes a promising paradigm enabling collaborative training model over multiple IIoT nodes without any direct data sharing. Moreover, vanilla FL remains susceptible to inference attacks and data leakage still, thereby necessitating Differential Privacy (DP) integration models for strengthening confidentiality and ensures aggregation of secure model12.

At this context, the proposed model presents a trustworthy differential privacy-enhanced FL (TrustFed) model, that ensures decentralized and secure attack detection over distributed IIoT nodes13,14. For improving classification efficiency further, this model integrates a novel Rock Hyrax Intelligence Optimization (RHIO) model for effective and optimal selection of features15. From the inspiration of collective behavior of rock hyrax colonies, RHIO identifies most discriminative features effectively, thus reducing computational overhead and dimensionality. An integration of RHIO with TrustFed outcomes in a hybrid model termed TrustFed-RHIO, employed for enhancing robustness, scalability, and detection accuracy in IIoT environments.

In addition, to foster interpretability and transparency in model decisions, suggested model integrates Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) scheme, in specific LIME and SHAP for offering clear insights to feature contribution & attack classification results. These interpretability mechanisms are thus crucial to increase trust of user and support decision-making in critical industrial systems.

Extensive experiments were conducted on benchmark IIoT datasets to validate proposed model efficiency (TrustFed-RHIO). The outcome demonstrates superior detection accuracy, decreased false alarm rates, with improved resilience over adversarial and privacy attack on comparing conventional machine learning and federated schemes16. An explainability inclusion further aids in understanding the behavior of model and enhancing operational transparency.

On the whole, the proposed framework signifies a significant advancement towards interpretable, secure, and privacy-preserving IIoT attack detection, which supports trustworthy and scalable deployment in real-world industrial environments.

Problem statement

An increasing connectivity of IIoT devices has amplified the vulnerabilities for cyberattacks and privacy breaches. Traditional centralized and machine-learning based intrusion detection system mostly fails in ensuring scalability, data confidentiality, and interpretability in distributed IIoT networks17. Federated learning provides decentralized alternatives but stay prone to leakage of data and model inference threats. Hence, a secure, privacy-preserving, and explainable attack detection model is needed highly.

Motivation

Recent IIoT network demands intelligent security solutions that are competent of learning in a collaborative manner without sharing raw data. High-dimensional IIoT dataset moreover needs optimal selection of features for enhancing detection accuracy and efficiency. In addition, explainability is needed for industrial trust and compliance. These challenges thus motivate the integration of intelligent optimization, explainable AI, and differential privacy with unified detection framework.

Related works

In recent years, research in IIoT security focus on employing intelligent and privacy preserving intrusion detection model. Several studies explore machine learning, deep learning, and federated learning models for enhancing accuracy of attack detection thereby safeguarding sensitive data. Moreover, most traditional models either compromise privacy of data, interpretability lack or fails in attaining effective feature optimization in a large-scale IIoT environments.

A federated learning model was suggested in18 which collaboratively trains on edge on preserving privacy. To its core, server-side rule which weights updates of client with their SHAP dependent feature stability and differential privacy budgets. HADA noisy down weights or lower privacy updates speeds up convergence & hardening global model over poisoning. The outcome demonstrates that HADA-FL offers privacy-preserving, scalable, and attack-resilient system of intrusion detection suitable for the deployment of real-world heterogenous environment.

A Bidirectional Long-short term memory (Bi-LSTM) scheme was suggested in the work19 along with self-adaptive mechanism. It is a new feature of this IDS model, designed for IIoT environments specifically. This model adjusts their focus dynamically for prioritizing crucial elements in dataset, thus allocating high computational resource for segments of data more likely to comprise anomalies and patterns indicative of security threats.

An Artificial intelligence (AI)-driven scheme that utilizes optimized and explainable deep-ensemble learning along with pruning for DDoS detection was suggested in20. The model employs attention aided ensemble DL for DDoS, that integrates CNN and LSTM networks. For addressing resource constraints of edge devices in CIoT model, system employs differential evolution (DE) aided pruning and quantization models, thus optimizing effective model for effectual deployment on edge nodes thereby preserving higher performance.

In the work21, multi-class classification scheme was developed for handling vulnerabilities and security attacks as Intrusion detection system (IDS). A suggested scheme will be built with several ML schemes like random forest, decision tree, gradient boosting, and multinominal Naïve Bayes classifier. A gradient boosting model on comparing with other scheme offers better accuracy and classification probability which was employed in Local interpretable model agnostic explainer (LIME) development. A random forest scheme offers better accuracy and was employed for global surrogacy with SHAP explainer.

A model presented in22 introduces Explainable AI based IDS (EAI-IDS) aiming at strengthening security of IoT aided autonomous vehicle communication. The suggested model incorporates LSTM scheme for capturing temporal dependencies within sensor data thereby employing Random Forest approach for feature level anomaly detection. In addition, SHAP enables the interpretability on highlighting features that influence decision of anomy detection mostly.

A hybrid ML framework was presented in23 that integrates deep feature extraction over CNN with strong classification ability of XGBoost. This model removes the manual feature engineering dependency thereby enhancing accuracy of detection on learning directly from raw packet level network traffic. For ensuring interpretability that is critical is critical in application of security sensitive framework, XAI model will be integrated.

In the work24, XAI model was implemented for generating actionable insights which bridges the gap among technical output of intrusion detection & operational decision making. For enhancing the scalability and preserving confidentiality of data, FL was integrated thus enabling distributed and secure deployment over varied IoT environments.

In25, a multistage optimization scheme was presented which intends at enhancing accuracy of intrusion detection. The initial stage uses a hybrid ACO-GA/PSO model for selection of features so as to retain most significant attributes for effectual classification. In next step, LSTM were fine-tuned using BAT model and whale optimization strategy thereby optimizing hyperparameters for maximum performance. At last, GAN model was used for estimating the robustness of model against unseen data, followed by retraining optimized LSTM using samples generated.

The author in the work26, proposed an explainable & resilient ML aided web attack detection system for secured IIoT application, using TON-IoT and NF-ToN-IoT V2 datasets that represent IIoT network traffic effectively. On leveraging transparency of XAI, study employes feature selection, offering global interpretability and explaining feature contributions to the model decision.

Likewise, the work27 addressed IIoT security challenges on employing FL dependent IDS which prioritizes privacy of data. A suggested Explainable DFL model employs DNN for identifying anomalies in IIoT traffic. Moreover, integration with XAI enhances interpretability, offering clear insights into IDS’s decision making model.

Research gap identified

Despite notable development on employing machine learning, deep learning, and federated learning for IIoT intrusion detection, traditional models still face some key limitations. Most of them either aims at accuracy or privacy but fails in balancing them both together while ensuring scalability and interpretability28,29. Moreover, the system struggles with heterogenous, huge-scale IIoT environments and evolving attack patterns. Hence, there is a necessitate for explainable, unified, and privacy preserving intrusion detection model competent of adaptive and real-time learning, trustworthy operations in a dynamic IIoT network.

Trustworthy DP-enhanced federated learning (TrustFed) for attack detection

In IIoT framework, improper resource allocation might inadvertently expose the system for potential security threats, enhancing sensitive data leakage likelihood. Hence, it becomes crucial for integrating the mechanism of attack detection for safeguarding the network against such vulnerabilities. For attaining this, TrustFed model is used. Traditional FL model primarily focusses on maintaining balanced datasets over users, each participant holds the approximate that has equal data amount. Moreover, in practical scenarios, this assumption is unrealistic. Th suggested scheme addresses this limitation on assigning distinct privacy and model parameters for each user depending on volume and distribution of its local data. In this framework, DP is employed at user level for ensuring that the model’s shared parameters do not reveal sensitive information. At the time of training, each local user scheme incorporates noise perturbation (attained from specified probability distribution), and modified parameters will be transmitted to central server. This mechanism enhances resistance to inference attacks significantly on comparing earlier FL based scheme. XAI using SHAP & LIME for generating explanations

The suggested framework integrates XAI model for enhancing interpretability and transparency of DL scheme. XAI model inclusion enables explanation of complex decision-making process inherent in deep neural architectures. Among the various interpretability models, this model uses SHAP and LIME for offering global and local interpretative perspectives of model’s prediction.

A SHAP model, grounded in cooperative game theory provides global quantification of feature significance on computing contribution of each feature to overall output model. This in turn evaluates how every feature influences prediction on assigning additive significant values, signified as SHAP value. The use of SHAP value is advantageous specifically, since they offer concise and interpretable insights to model behavior. Through the plot of SHAP visualization, it is competent to determine how each feature contributes negatively or positively to the specified predictions. The summary plot defines global feature importance, whereas dependence plot highlights interaction among correlated features. However, force plot represents in what way each feature drives given prediction outcomes. Collectively, this interpretative visualization improves model debugging, validation, and refinement thus promoting trust in automated decision systems particularly in high stakes domains like clinical or security analytics.

In parallel, LIME is used for complementing SHAP on focusing local interpretability that is understanding rationale behind individual predictions. LIME approximates complex behavior, non-linear “black box” scheme on generating interpretable, locally faithful surrogate scheme around the specified prediction instance. It is attained by slightly perturbing input data and observing output variations thus revealing the way that every feature influence local decision boundary. Conclusion

In this work, a novel TrustFed-RHIO model was introduced for enhancing the privacy and security of IIoT environments. On combining RHIO approach for feature selection with Trustworthy DP-Fed Learning mechanism, this scheme attains both efficiency and privacy preserving collaborative learning. This combination of SHAP and LIME thus enhances interpretability and transparency of attack prediction, thereby promoting trust in automated decision making. An experimental analysis performed on CCIoT2024-DIAD dataset illustrates that TrustFed-RHIO outperforms traditional model significantly in terms of robustness, detection accuracy, and privacy protection on maintaining low latency and training time. Overall, the proposed methodology offers an explainable and scalable security solution for real-world IIoT networks, thus contributing to the expansion of trustworthy and resilient industrial systems.
