44 C.F.R. § 6.9 — Inconsistent issuances of FEMA and/or its predecessor agencies superseded.
Citation: us:cfr:44:6.9
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-6.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 6 (Implementation Of The Privacy Act Of 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any policies and procedures in any issuances of FEMA or any of its predecessor agencies which are inconsistent with the policies and procedures in this part are superseded to the extent of that inconsistency.
