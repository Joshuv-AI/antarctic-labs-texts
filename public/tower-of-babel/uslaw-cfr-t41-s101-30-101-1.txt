41 C.F.R. § 101-30.101-1 — -30.101-1 Civil agency item.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-30.101-1
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-30.101-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-30 (30—Federal Catalog System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Civil agency item means an item of supply in the supply system of one or more civilian agencies, which is repetitively procured, stocked, or otherwise managed (includes direct delivery requirements as well as items stocked for issue).
