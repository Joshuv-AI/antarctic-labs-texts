21 U.S.C. § 2110 — Rule of construction
Citation: us:usc:21:2110
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section2110
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 26 (Food Safety). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter (or an amendment made by this chapter) shall be construed to affect—
(1)
the regulation of dietary supplements under the Dietary Supplement Health and Education Act of 1994 (Public Law 103–417); or
(2)
the adverse event reporting system for dietary supplements created under the Dietary Supplement and Nonprescription Drug Consumer Protection Act (Public Law 109–462).
