29 C.F.R. § 38.97 — Notification of finding of noncompliance.
Citation: us:cfr:29:38.97
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-38.97
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 38 (Implementation Of The Nondiscrimination And Equal Opportunity Provisions Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where a compliance review or complaint investigation results in a finding of noncompliance, the Director must notify:
(a) The grant applicant or recipient;
(b) The grantmaking agency; and
(c) The Assistant Attorney General.
