42 U.S.C. § 19054 — Climate change research
Citation: us:usc:42:19054
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section19054
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 163 (Research And Development, Competition, And Innovation); Subchapter III (National Science Foundation For The Future); Part E (Fundamental Research). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director shall make awards, on a competitive basis, to institutions of higher education or non-profit organizations (or consortia of such institutions or organizations) to support research to improve our understanding of the climate system and related human and environmental systems.
