41 C.F.R. § 102-71.60 — -71.60 Occupant agencies are required to cooperate with the Designated Official in the implementation of the emergency plans and the staffing of the emergency organization.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-71.60
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-71.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-71 (71—Real Property Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All occupant agencies of a facility must fully cooperate with the Designated Official in the implementation of the emergency plans and the staffing of the emergency organization.
