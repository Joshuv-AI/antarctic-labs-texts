13 C.F.R. § 120.313 — DAL-2 use of proceeds and other program conditions.
Citation: us:cfr:13:120.313
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-120.313
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 120 (Business Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The DAL-2 loan proceeds may be used for any 7(a) loan purposes.
(b) An applicant may use DAL-2 loan proceeds to acquire an eligible small business without complying with the change of ownership conditions in § 120.202.
(c) A DAL-2 applicant must submit evidence from a physician, psychiatrist, or other qualified professional as to the permanent nature of the disability and the limitation it places on the applicant.
