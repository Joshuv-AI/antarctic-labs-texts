20 C.F.R. § 229.68 — Reduction of DIB O/M.
Citation: us:cfr:20:229.68
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-229.68
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 229 (Social Security Overall Minimum Guarantee). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A reduction for entitlement to worker's compensation or a public disability benefit is applied after the DIB O/M is reduced for age and the family maximum. The spouse and child O/M benefits are first reduced proportionately. The employee O/M benefit is decreased by any remaining reduction amount.
