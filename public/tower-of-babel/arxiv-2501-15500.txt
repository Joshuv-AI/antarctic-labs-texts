Abstract
We examine a contribution of axion-like particles (ALPs) to a top pair production via the collision of Compton backscattered photons at the CLIC operating in a γ ​ γ \gamma\gamma mode. The exclusion bounds on the ALP-top quark coupling depending on the ALP mass m a m_{a} are given. The mass range 10 GeV – 10 TeV is considered. We have obtained that for m a = 10 m_{a}=10 GeV the ALP-top quark couplings as small as 0.17 0.17 TeV -1 and 0.11 0.11 TeV -1 can be probed at the CLIC with the energy of 1.5 TeV and 3 TeV, respectively.

In many BSM theories, such as composite Higgs scenarios, new
top-philic vector resonances are predicted. These resonances
couple more strongly to the top quark than to the light quarks, such
that all the other couplings except for the one with top quarks can
be neglected. A typical approach to search for such particles is to

searches for resonances decaying to top quarks have typically
focused on production through light quarks. This production mode
requires a sizeable coupling between the new resonances and the
lighter quarks. If the coupling to the lighter quarks is however
suppressed, the resonance would mainly be produced through a pair of
top quarks. In this case, the resonance is produced in association

Different models of top-philic resonances have been studied in

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\,\partial_{\mu}a\,\partial^{\,\mu}\!a-\frac{1}{2}m_{a}^{2}a^{2}+g_{a\gamma\gamma}aF_{\mu\nu}\tilde{F}^{\mu\nu}+g_{a\gamma Z}aF_{\mu\nu}\tilde{Z}^{\mu\nu}

\displaystyle+g_{aZZ}aZ_{\mu\nu}\tilde{Z}^{\mu\nu}+g_{aWW}aW^{+}_{\mu\nu}\tilde{W}^{-\mu\nu}.

\displaystyle g_{a\gamma\gamma}

\displaystyle=\frac{e^{2}}{f_{a}}\,[C_{WW}+C_{BB}]\;,

\displaystyle g_{a\gamma Z}

\displaystyle=\frac{2e^{2}}{f_{a}s_{w}c_{w}}\,[c_{w}^{2}C_{WW}-s_{w}^{2}C_{BB}]\;,

\displaystyle g_{aZZ}

\displaystyle=\frac{e^{2}}{f_{a}s_{w}^{2}c_{w}^{2}}\,[c_{w}^{4}C_{WW}+s_{w}^{4}C_{BB}]\;,

\displaystyle g_{aWW}

\displaystyle=\frac{e^{2}}{f_{a}s_{w}^{2}}\,C_{WW}\;,

(3)

\displaystyle=g_{a\gamma\gamma}+\frac{c_{w}^{2}-s_{w}^{2}}{2s_{w}c_{w}}g_{a\gamma Z}\;,

\displaystyle g_{aWW}

\displaystyle=s_{w}^{2}g_{a\gamma\gamma}+\frac{s_{w}c_{w}}{2}g_{a\gamma Z}\;.

(4)

The interaction of the ALP with fermions is described by the
Lagrangian

\mathcal{L}^{a}_{\mathrm{f}}=\frac{1}{2f_{a}}\,\partial_{\mu}a\sum_{f}c_{af\bar{f}}\bar{f}\gamma_{\mu}\gamma_{5}f\;.

(5)

By using equations of motion, this Lagrangian can be rewritten as

\mathcal{L}^{a}_{\mathrm{f}}=-i\sum_{f}\frac{m_{f}c_{af\bar{f}}}{f_{a}}\,a\bar{f}\gamma_{5}f\;,

(6)

where
m_{f}
is a mass of the fermion
f

\mathcal{L}^{a}_{\mathrm{f}}=-i\frac{m_{t}c_{at\bar{t}}}{f_{a}}\,a\bar{t}\gamma_{5}t\;.

(7)

Correspondingly, the decay rate of a massive ALP into a top pair is
equal to

\Gamma(a\rightarrow t\bar{t})=\frac{3m_{a}m_{t}^{2}}{8\pi f_{a}^{2}}\,|c_{at\bar{t}}|^{2}\left(1-\frac{4m_{t}^{2}}{m_{a}^{2}}\right)^{\!\!1/2}.

In
[
53

ATLAS search looks like (here and in what follows
c_{t}\equiv c_{at\bar{t}}
)

\left|\frac{c_{t}}{f_{a}}\right|<1.81\mathrm{\ TeV}^{-1}.

(34)

The ALP-mediated di-boson production via top coupling results in the
bound

\left|\frac{c_{t}}{f_{a}}\right|<44.4\mathrm{\ TeV}^{-1}.

(35)

p_{t}

top quark was also used in
[
53
]
to get the limit

\left|\frac{c_{t}}{f_{a}}\right|<5.9\mathrm{\ TeV}^{-1}.

(36)

From lower-energy precision NA62 and Babar measurements of rare Kaon

5
Conclusions

In the present paper, we have studied the contribution of the ALPs
to the top pair production at the CLIC via the scattering of the
Compton backscattered photons. The collision energies of 1.5 TeV and
3 TeV are considered. We have presented both differential and total
cross sections depending on the invariant mass of the top quarks
pair. The bounds on the ALP-top quark coupling
c_{at\bar{t}}/f_{a}

are obtained as functions of the ALP mass
m_{a}
in the mass range 10
GeV – 10 TeV. We have found that these constraints become less
stringent with an increase of
m_{a}
. The best bounds on

c_{at\bar{t}}/f_{a}
for
m_{a}=10
GeV are equal to
0.17

TeV
-1
and
0.11
TeV
-1
for the CLIC energy of 1.5 TeV and
3 TeV, respectively. These bounds are compatible with constraints on

c_{at\bar{t}}/f_{a}
obtained for the HL-LHC with 3 ab
-1
. We can
conclude that the CLIC operating in the
\gamma\gamma
mode has a
great physical potential in searching for the ALP-top quarks
coupling.
