7 U.S.C. § 390c — Applicability of chapter 10 of title 5
Citation: us:usc:7:390c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section390c
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 14 (Agricultural Experiment Stations); Subchapter III (Research Facilities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Chapter 10 of title 5 and title XVIII of the Food and Agriculture Act of 1977 (7 U.S.C. 2281 et seq.) shall not apply to a panel or board created solely for the purpose of reviewing applications or proposals submitted under this subchapter.
