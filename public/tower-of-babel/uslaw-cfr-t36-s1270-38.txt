36 C.F.R. § 1270.38 — Public access to Presidential records.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1270.38
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1270.38
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1270 (Presidential Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Public access to Presidential records generally begins five years after the President leaves office, and is administered through the Freedom of Information Act (5 U.S.C. 552), as modified by the Presidential Records Act (44 U.S.C. 2204(c)).
