45 C.F.R. § 2523.100 — Are participants in programs operated by Federal agencies Federal employees?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2523.100
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2523.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2523 (Agreements With Other Federal Agencies For The Provision Of Americorps Program Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. Participants in these programs have the same employee status as participants in other approved AmeriCorps programs, and are not considered Federal employees, except for the purposes of the Family and Medical Leave Act as specified in § 2540.220(b) of this chapter.
