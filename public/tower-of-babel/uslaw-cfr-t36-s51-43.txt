36 C.F.R. § 51.43 — Who will make the determination that a concession contract is an outfitter and guide contract?
Citation: us:cfr:36:51.43
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-51.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 51 (Concession Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Only a Deputy Director or an Associate Director will make the determination that a concession contract is or is not an outfitter and guide contract.
