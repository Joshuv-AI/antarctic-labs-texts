24 C.F.R. § 1000.126 — May a recipient charge flat or income-adjusted rents?
Citation: us:cfr:24:1000.126
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-1000.126
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 1000 (Native American Housing Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, providing the rental or homebuyer payment of the low-income family does not exceed 30 percent of the family's adjusted income.
