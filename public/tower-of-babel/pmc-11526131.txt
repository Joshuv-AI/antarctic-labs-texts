Adaptive fuzzy neural super-twisting control of micro gyroscope sensor
Authors: Jiapeng Xie, Juntao Fei
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
AbstractTo maintain the vibrations of the gyroscope proof mass, an adaptive super-twisting sliding mode control (STSMC) of a micro gyroscope based on a two-loop recursive fuzzy neural network (TLRFNN) is designed. In order to estimate the unknown parameters of the nonlinear system online, an adaptive technology is adopted based on the parameter linearization of the nonlinear model. In response to the problem of system chattering caused by ordinary sliding mode control, the STSMC with the advantages of high order sliding mode and conventional sliding mode is introduced. Aiming at the problem of uncertainty in system parameters, a two-loop recursive fuzzy neural network approximation with the competence over the storage of a priori information is utilized. All adaptive laws are obtained under the Lyapunov stability framework which contributes to the stability of the system. The simulation research shows the system exhibits good tracking performance and maintains a small tracking error under the control of the suggested control system. The effect is measured by calculating the root mean square error (RMSE) parameter of the tracking error and the proposed control system achieves a tracking effect of and in the x and y directions, respectively.Keywords: Micro gyroscope, Two-loop recursive fuzzy neural network, Super-twisting sliding mode control, Adaptive controlSubject terms: Electrical and electronic engineering, Mechanical engineering

Introduction

Micro gyroscopes have always played a key role in the measurement of angular velocity in military, aerospace and other fields. The measurement principle of its angular velocity is calculated by the measured velocity of the mass block and Coriolis acceleration. Defects in the manufacturing process are unavoidable, resulting in micro gyroscopes that are not fully symmetrical in their physical structure. In the actual physical model, there is an orthogonal coupling between the drive shaft and the sensing shaft. Research on improving the performance of micro gyroscopes has also received great attention. The goal of the study is to make trajectory of the gyroscope proof mass follow that of the reference model1–3.

In4, a serial-parallel estimation model (SPEM)-based sliding mode control method is designed. Using the errors, which are generated in the process tracking and filtering modeling and obtained by a SPEM algorithm, the parameter vector and the update rule of the radial basis function neural network (RBFNN) weight are constructed. In5, A composite control consisting of fuzzy control and PID realizes the intelligent regulation of PID parameters by utilizing a fuzzy regulator based on error-related information. In6, the control accuracy is enhanced by discussing the different orders of fractional-order sliding mode control (FOSMC) to find a suitable order. It has also been demonstrated that FOSMC outperforms normal sliding mode control (SMC) and integer-order SMC. In3, the approximator with minimal parameters is developed to focus on problems such as overshoot in the transient estimation process for other approximator. In7, the input vector of the approximator introduced to estimate unknown nonlinear terms is expanded by substituting the original input into the Chebyshev formula and the network structure is endowed with the ability to self-construct. In8, considering that speed information is not available in reality, the state observer was chosen as a solution, and for the time-varying disturbances, a disturbance observer is utilized.

Sliding mode control has been valued for its strong robustness and other advantages9,10, but chattering has also become its unavoidable shortcoming, and the high-frequency vibration caused by the SMC method in practical applications can easily cause damage to the device. Higher-order SMC becomes an effective technique, in which super-twisting sliding mode control (STSMC) is widely used because it does not require derivation of sliding mode variables11–13. In14, STSMC is designed for acquiring robust property and tracking performance. To further improve the performance of STSMC, the non-singular terminal SMC is taken into account in the design, in15. In16, for enhancing the robustness of the direct-current microgrid, a STSMC is utilized to engage in the reaching law. In17, the combination of fractional sliding mode and super-twisting achieves both the flexibility and smoothness of the control law. In18, STSMC is considered as an alternative method to avoid wear and tear on machinery arising from SMC chatter. In19, a control system adopting super-twisting control presents better tracking accuracy and robustness. Similarly, for the trajectory tracking problem, STSMC is applied to the control system20–23.

Since the system parameters are difficult to obtain, it is necessary to estimate the parameters. In addition to the above-mentioned methods of approximating the overall unknown model using neural networks, efficient acquisition of dynamic parameters can be achieved by using observers to estimate auxiliary states introduced into the model24,25. However, for the estimation of a model with many unknown parameters, this method will cause huge computational work. In addition, adaptive control has also been applied to online estimation of nonlinear system parameters, which can effectively deal with dynamic parameter problems26. For the unknown parameters, the adaptive laws for parameter identification are designed27,28. In29 and 30, since the dynamic parameters are unknown, adaptive control technology is used for compensation.

For the lumped uncertainty of system parameters, it is usually assumed to be bounded and compensated by other control methods. Since the neural network has good approximation ability for nonlinear functions, it can be used to approximate the lumped uncertainty of parameters31–38. In31, aiming at the uncertainty of nonlinear system, the method using neural network approximation is adopted. For each subsystem, a Gaussian RBFNN is used to estimate the uncertainty in32. In33, RBFNN was used to estimate the uncertainty of lumped parameters online in an induction motor model. RBFNN is also used for the approximation in34 for the lumped uncertainty of the nonlinear unmanned aerial vehicle model. In35, a double-loop fuzzy neural network (DLFNN) is shown to have a better approximation ability in estimating an unknown model.

In many studies, a variety of neural networks with good approximation accuracy have been used for model-unknown problems, and their structures are generally complex. However, this also leads to a large increase in computational effort, and the same approximation progress may not be achieved for simple neural networks. For parameter uncertainty, many studies have dealt with it in an indirect way. One approach is to use neural networks to directly approximate unknown models under the influence of parameter uncertainty, which may reduce the approximation accuracy. One approach is to treat it as a disturbance and use the robustness of SMC to achieve the anti-disturbance of the system, which may make it necessary to set a large value for the switching gain of SMC. But this in turn will cause the chattering to become larger. For example, in39 and 40, a RBFNN and a DLFNN are introduced to approximate the unknown model terms, respectively. Furthermore, the robust performance of the super-twisted sliding mode control becomes crucial for coping with the effects of parameter uncertainty terms and disturbances. This phenomenon is amply demonstrated in their proofs of the theory.

In this paper, a STSMC based on adaptive TLRFNN is designed for micro gyroscopes, which can realize tracking control for unknown nonlinear systems and estimation on system parameters. The main contributions of this paper can be summarized as follows:

For the unknown micro-gyroscope dynamic model, the adaptive technology is applied to estimate the unknown parameters. Since the dynamic model is a nonlinear function, in order to facilitate the use of adaptive control technology, the parametric linear system model is first reconstructed. Then, the system parameters are approximated according to the new system model. The adaptive laws of the system parameters are designed in the stability framework to achieve the estimation of the real system parameters while ensuring the system stability.

Aiming at the uncertainty of system parameters, a neural network approximator called a two-loop recursive fuzzy neural network (TLRFNN) is employed, which optimizes and supplements the estimation of unknown model by adaptive control. The adaptive rate of TLRFNN is designed to satisfy the Lyapunov stability theory. The approximator cooperates with adaptive control to enhance the approximation capability and further increase the control accuracy effectively.

Conclusion

This paper proposes an adaptive control based on STSMC for maintaining the proof mass vibration. To decrease chattering, a STSMC is introduced in this control scheme. A TLRFNN, designed to compensate for parameter uncertainty terms, has the feedback structure that achieves full utilization of the information. Meanwhile, in order to optimize the parameter approximation result and control performance, the learning criteria of adaptive controller parameters and neural network parameters are studied. The stability of the control system is guaranteed by Lyapunov stability theory, and the simulation results present satisfactory tracking and convergence performance. In addition, compared with SMC-RBFNN method, the accuracy of the STSMC-TLRFNN is reflected by the fact that the tracking error of the STSMC-TLRFNN can be stabilized within a small range. However, there are some limitations of the control methods that need to be considered in future studies. The use of more complex neural networks may increase computational costs. In addition, the parameter estimation rate gain and neural network parameter learning rate selected through experimental experience can achieve the control goals but may not be the optimal parameters.
