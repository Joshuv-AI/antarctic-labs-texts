10 C.F.R. § 170.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:10:170.1

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 170 (Fees For Facilities, Materials, Import And Export Licenses, And Other Regulatory Services Under The Atomic Energy Act Of 1954, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part set out fees charged for licensing services, inspection services, and special projects rendered by the Nuclear Regulatory Commission as authorized under title V of the Independent Offices Appropriation Act, 1952 (31 U.S.C. 9701(a)).
