29 C.F.R. § 30.16 — Reinstatement of program registration.
Citation: us:cfr:29:30.16
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-30.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 30 (Equal Employment Opportunity In Apprenticeship). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An apprenticeship program that has been deregistered pursuant to this part may be reinstated by the Registration Agency upon presentation of adequate evidence that the apprenticeship program is operating in accordance with this part.
