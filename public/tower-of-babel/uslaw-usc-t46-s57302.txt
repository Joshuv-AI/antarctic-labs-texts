46 U.S.C. § 57302 — Authority to acquire vessels
Citation: us:usc:46:57302
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section57302
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part F (Government-Owned Merchant Vessels); Chapter 573 (Vessel Trade-In Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To promote the construction of new, safe, and efficient vessels to carry the domestic and foreign waterborne commerce of the United States, the Secretary of Transportation may acquire an obsolete vessel in exchange for an allowance of credit toward the cost of construction or purchase of a new vessel as provided in this chapter.
