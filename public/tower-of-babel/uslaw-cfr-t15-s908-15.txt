15 C.F.R. § 908.15 — Times for taking action; expiration on Saturday, Sunday, or holiday.
Citation: us:cfr:15:908.15
Creator: National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-908.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 908 (Maintaining Records And Submitting Reports On Weather Modification Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever periods of time are specified in these rules in days, calendar days are intended. When the day, or the last day, fixed under these rules for taking any action falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or on a Federal holiday, the action may be taken on the next succeeding day which is not a Saturday, Sunday, or Federal holiday.
