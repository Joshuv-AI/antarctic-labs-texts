22 C.F.R. § 196.4 — Administering office.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 196 (Thomas R. Pickering Foreign Affairs/Graduate Foreign Affairs Fellowship Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department of State's Bureau of Human Resources, Office of Recruitment is responsible for administering the Thomas R. Pickering Foreign Affairs/Graduate Foreign Affairs Fellowship Program and grants to post-secondary institutions and may be contacted for more detailed information.
