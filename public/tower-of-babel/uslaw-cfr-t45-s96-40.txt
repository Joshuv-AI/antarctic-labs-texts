45 C.F.R. § 96.40 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:45:96.40
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-96.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 96 (Block Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to the community services, alcohol and drug abuse and mental health services, preventive health and health services, primary care, and low-income home energy assistance block grants.
