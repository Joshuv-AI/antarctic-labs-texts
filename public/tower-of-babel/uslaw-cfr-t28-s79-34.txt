28 C.F.R. § 79.34 — Proof of medical condition.
Citation: us:cfr:28:79.34
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-79.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 79 (Claims Under The Radiation Exposure Compensation Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Proof of medical condition under this subpart will be made in the same manner and according to the same procedures and limitations as are set forth in § 79.16 and § 79.26.
