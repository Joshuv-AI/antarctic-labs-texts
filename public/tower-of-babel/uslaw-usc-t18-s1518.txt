18 U.S.C. § 1518 — Obstruction of criminal investigations of health care offenses
Citation: us:usc:18:1518
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1518
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 73 (Obstruction Of Justice). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Whoever willfully prevents, obstructs, misleads, delays or attempts to prevent, obstruct, mislead, or delay the communication of information or records relating to a violation of a Federal health care offense to a criminal investigator shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than 5 years, or both.
(b)
As used in this section the term “criminal investigator” means any individual duly authorized by a department, agency, or armed force of the United States to conduct or engage in investigations for prosecutions for violations of health care offenses.
