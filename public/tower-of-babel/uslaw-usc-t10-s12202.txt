10 U.S.C. § 12202 — Commissioned officer grades
Citation: us:usc:10:12202
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section12202
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel Generally); Chapter 1205 (Appointment Of Reserve Officers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except for commissioned warrant officers, the reserve commissioned officer grades in each armed force are those authorized for regular commissioned officers of that armed force.
