41 C.F.R. § 102-37.410 — -37.410 Public airport donation authority.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-37.410
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-37.410
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-37 (37—Donation Of Surplus Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 47151 of title 49, U.S. Code, authorizes executive agencies to give priority consideration to requests from a public airport as defined in 49 U.S.C. 47102 for the donation of surplus property if the Department of Transportation (DOT) considers the property appropriate for airport purposes and GSA approves the donation.
