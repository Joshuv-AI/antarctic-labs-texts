20 C.F.R. § 718.302 — Relationship of pneumoconiosis to coal mine employment.
Citation: us:cfr:20:718.302
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-718.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 718 (Standards For Determining Coal Miners' Total Disability Or Death Due To Pneumoconiosis). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a miner who is suffering or suffered from pneumoconiosis was employed for ten years or more in one or more coal mines, there shall be a rebuttable presumption that the pneumoconiosis arose out of such employment. (See § 718.203.)
