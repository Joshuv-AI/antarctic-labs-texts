31 C.F.R. § 0.202 — Conformance with policy and subordination to authority.
Citation: us:cfr:31:0.202
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-0.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 0 (Department Of The Treasury Employee Rules Of Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Employees are required to comply with the lawful directives of their supervisor and other management officials.
(b) Employees shall be familiar and comply with regulations and published instructions that relate to their official duties and responsibilities.
