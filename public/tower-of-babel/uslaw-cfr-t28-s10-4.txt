28 C.F.R. § 10.4 — Date of filing.
Citation: us:cfr:28:10.4
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-10.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 10 (Registration Of Certain Organizations Carrying On Activities Within The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The date on which a registration statement properly executed is accepted by the Attorney General for filing shall be considered the date of the filing of such registration statement pursuant to the said act. All statements must be filed not later than thirty days after January 15, 1941.
