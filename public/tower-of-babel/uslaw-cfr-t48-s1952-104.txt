48 C.F.R. § 1952.104 — 1952.104 Procedures for modifying and completing provisions and clauses.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1952.104
Creator: Chapter 19—Broadcasting Board Of Governors
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1952.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1952 (Solicitation Provisions And Contract Clauses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

IAAR provisions and clauses shall not be modified (see FAR 1952.101(a) unless authorized by the Director, Office of Contracts, and when so authorized, contracting officers must comply with the procedures in FAR 1952.104.
