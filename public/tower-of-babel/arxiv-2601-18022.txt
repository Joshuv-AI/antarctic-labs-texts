A relative Langlands dual realization of T ∗ ​ ( G / K ) T^{*}(G/K) and derived Satake
Tsao-Hsien Chen

Abstract
. We show that the cotangent bundle T ∗ ​ ( G / K ) T^{*}(G/K) of a quasi-split symmetric space G / K G/K is isomorphic to the dual variety of the loop symmetric space for the Langlands dual group, providing instances of the relative Langlands duality for non-split groups. Then we establish a Langlands dual description of equivariant coherent sheaves on T ∗ ​ ( G / K ) T^{*}(G/K) in terms of constructible sheaves on the loop symmetric spaces, generalizing the derived Satake equivalence for reductive groups to quasi-split symmetric spaces. To this end, we prove the derived Satake equivalence for the twisted affine Grassmannians, study ring objects arising from loop symmetric spaces, and explore the formality and fully-faithfulness properties of ! ! -pure objects. We deduce a version of Bezrukavnikov equivalence for quasi-split symmetric spaces and make connections to the geometric Langlands on the twistor ℙ 1 \mathbb{P}^{1} .

2.1.
Symmetric spaces

Let
G
be a complex adjoint semisimple group.
We fix a pinning
(G,B,T,x)
where
(B,T)
is a Borel pair
and
x=\sum_{\alpha\in\Delta}x_{\alpha}\in\operatorname{Lie}B
,
here
\Delta
is the set of simple roots and

is a basis for the weight space
(\operatorname{Lie}G)_{\alpha}
.

Let
\operatorname{Aut}(G,B,T,x)\subset\operatorname{Aut}(G)
be the subgroup of pinned automorphisms. Since

is adjoint there is an isomorphism between
\operatorname{Aut}(G,B,T,x)
with the automorphism group of the Dynkin diagram of
G
.
Let
\operatorname{Chev}\in\operatorname{Aut}(G,B,T,x)
be the Chevalley involution, that is, the unique involution
such that
\operatorname{Chev}(t)=w_{0}(t^{-1})

for any
t\in T_{0}
, here
w_{0}
is the longest element in the Weyl group
{\mathrm{W}}_{G}=N_{G}(T)/T
.
We have
\operatorname{Chev}=\operatorname{id}
if and only if

G
is not of type
A_{n},D_{2n+1},E_{6}
.
According to
[
AV
, Lemma 5.3 and 5.4]
, we can find a
lifting
n_{0}\in N_{G}(T)
of
w_{0}

such that
n_{0}^{2}=1

and
\sigma(n_{0})=n_{0}
for all
\sigma\in\operatorname{Aut}(G,B,T,x)
.
In particular, the composition

\operatorname{Ad}_{n_{0}}\circ\operatorname{Chev}
is an involution satisfying

\operatorname{Ad}_{n_{0}}\circ\operatorname{Chev}(t)=t^{-1}

for all
t\in T_{0}
, that is, a split involution.

Let
\theta_{0}\in\operatorname{Aut}(G,B,T,x)

be a pinned involution.
Then the composition

(2.1)

\theta=\operatorname{Ad}_{n_{0}}\circ\operatorname{Chev}\circ\theta_{0}

defines a quasi-split involution. Indeed we have

\theta(B)\cap B=n_{0}Bn_{0}^{-1}\cap B=T
.
We will write

H=G^{\theta_{0}}

and
K=G^{\theta}

for the corresponding symmetric subgroups.

Example 2.1
.

(1) If
\theta_{0}=\operatorname{id}
, we have
H=G

and
(G,K)
is a split symmetric pair.

(2) If
\theta_{0}\neq\operatorname{id}
, then
G
must be of type

A_{n},D_{n},E_{6}
and
(G,K)
is a quasi-split but not split
symmetric pair. Here is a list of the triple

(G,K,H)
:

(PGL_{2n+1},P(GL_{n+1}\times GL_{n}),SO_{2n+1}),(PGL_{2n},P(GL_{n}\times GL_{n})),PSp_{2n}),(PSO_{2n},PGL_{n},SO_{2n-1}),

(E_{6}^{ad},SL_{6}\times SL_{2}/\operatorname{center},F_{4}).

Although
K
might be disconnected,
from the list above, we see that

H=G^{\theta_{0}}

and
T_{H}=T^{\theta_{0}}
are connected
[
Z
, Lemma 4.6]
.
Since

(T,B)
is a preserved by
\theta_{0}
,
it restricts to a Borel pair

(T_{H}=T\cap H,B_{H}=B\cap H)
of
H
.
We have

\theta\circ\theta_{0}=\theta_{0}\circ\theta

and
\theta
restricts to a split involution on
H
with
symmetric subgroup

and
split maximal torus

.
Indeed,
we have
\theta(t)=\operatorname{Ad}_{n_{0}}\circ\operatorname{Chev}(a)=a^{-1}

for

.

We denote by

and
Y=H/M
the symmetric spaces associated to the
symmetric pairs
(G,K)
and
(H,M)
.
