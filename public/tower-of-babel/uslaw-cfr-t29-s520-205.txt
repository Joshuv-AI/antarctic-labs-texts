29 C.F.R. § 520.205 — How do these rules affect other Federal, state and local laws and collective bargaining agreements?
Citation: us:cfr:29:520.205
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-520.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 520 (Employment Under Special Certificate Of Messengers, Learners (Including Student-Learners), And Apprentices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No provision of this part, or of any special minimum wage certificate issued thereunder, shall excuse noncompliance with any other Federal or state law or municipal ordinance or collective bargaining agreement establishing higher standards.
