16 C.F.R. § 304.4 — Application of other law or regulation.
Citation: us:cfr:16:304.4
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-304.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 304 (Rules And Regulations Under The Hobby Protection Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of these regulations are in addition to, and not in substitution for or limitation of, the provisions of any other law or regulation of the United States (including the existing statutes and regulations prohibiting the reproduction of genuine currency) or of the law or regulation of any State.
