5 C.F.R. § 734.104 — Restriction of political activity.
Citation: us:cfr:5:734.104
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-734.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 734 (Political Activities Of Federal Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No further proscriptions or restrictions may be imposed upon employees covered under this regulation except:
(a) Employees who are appointed by the President by and with the advice and consent of the Senate;
(b) Employees who are appointed by the President;
(c) Non-career senior executive service members;
(d) Schedule C employees, 5 CFR 213.3301, 213.3302; and
(e) Any other employees who serve at the pleasure of the President.
