43 C.F.R. § 3137.20 — Is there a standard unit agreement form?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3137.20
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3137.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3130 (Oil And Gas Leasing: National Petroleum Reserve, Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

There is no standard unit agreement form. BLM will accept any unit agreement format if it protects the public interest and includes the mandatory terms required in § 3137.21 of this subpart.
