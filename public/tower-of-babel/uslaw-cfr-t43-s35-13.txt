43 C.F.R. § 35.13 — Parties to the hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:43:35.13
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-35.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 35 (Administrative Remedies For Fraudulent Claims And Statements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The parties to the hearing shall be the defendant and the Department of the Interior.
(b) Pursuant to 31 U.S.C. 3730(c)(5), a private plaintiff under the False Claims Act may participate in these proceedings to the extent authorized by the provisions of that Act.
