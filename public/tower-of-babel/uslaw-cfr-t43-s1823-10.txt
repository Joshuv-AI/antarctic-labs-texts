43 C.F.R. § 1823.10 — How may I make my payments to BLM?
Citation: us:cfr:43:1823.10
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-1823.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 1820 (Application Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless specific regulations provide otherwise, you may pay by:
(a) United States currency; or
(b) Checks, money orders, or bank drafts made payable to the Bureau of Land Management; or
(c) Visa or Master Card credit charge, except as specified by pertinent regulation(s).
