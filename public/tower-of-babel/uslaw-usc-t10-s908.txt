10 U.S.C. § 908 — Art. 108. Military property of United States—Loss, damage, destruction, or wrongful disposition
Citation: us:usc:10:908
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section908
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 47 (Uniform Code Of Military Justice); Subchapter X (Punitive Articles). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person subject to this chapter who, without proper authority—
(1)
sells or otherwise disposes of;
(2)
willfully or through neglect damages, destroys, or loses; or
(3)
willfully or through neglect suffers to be lost, damaged, destroyed, sold, or wrongfully disposed of;
any military property of the United States, shall be punished as a court-martial may direct.
