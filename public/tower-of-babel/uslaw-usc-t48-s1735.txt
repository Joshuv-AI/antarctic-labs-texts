48 U.S.C. § 1735 — Operation of Office; compensation, allowances, and benefits; privileges and immunities
Citation: us:usc:48:1735
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1735
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 16 (Delegates To Congress); Subchapter II (American Samoa). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Until the Rules of the House of Representatives are amended to provide otherwise, the Delegate from American Samoa shall receive the same compensation, allowances, and benefits as a Member of the House of Representatives, and shall be entitled to whatever privileges and immunities that are, or hereinafter may be, granted to the nonvoting Delegate from the Territory of Guam.
