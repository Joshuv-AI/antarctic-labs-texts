29 C.F.R. § 2700.35 — Time to file.
Citation: us:cfr:29:2700.35
Creator: Federal Mine Safety And Health Review Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-2700.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 2700 (Procedural Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A complaint for compensation under section 111 of the Act, 30 U.S.C. 821, shall be filed within 90 days after the beginning of the period during which the complainants are idled or would have been idled by the order that gives rise to the claim.
