49 C.F.R. § 1544.301 — Contingency plan.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1544.301
Creator: Transportation Security Administration, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1544.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1544 (Aircraft Operator Security: Air Carriers And Commercial Operators). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each aircraft operator must adopt a contingency plan and must:
(a) Implement its contingency plan when directed by TSA.
(b) Ensure that all information contained in the plan is updated annually and that appropriate persons are notified of any changes.
(c) Participate in an airport-sponsored exercise of the airport contingency plan or its equivalent, as provided in its security program.
