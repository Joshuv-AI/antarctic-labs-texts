5 C.F.R. § 894.202 — I am an enrollee; if I enroll for self plus one, may I decide which family member to cover?
Citation: us:cfr:5:894.202
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-894.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 894 (Federal Employees Dental And Vision Insurance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, if you enroll for self plus one, you must state at the time you enroll which eligible family member you want to cover under your enrollment.
