Prediction of solar irradiance using convolutional neural network and attention mechanism-based long short-term memory network based on similar day analysis and an attention mechanism
Authors: Xinxing Hou, Chao Ju, Bo Wang
Published: 2023

ABSTRACT
AbstractAs one of the future's most promising clean energy sources, solar energy is the key to developing renewable energy. The randomness of solar irradiance can affect the efficiency of photovoltaic power generation, which makes photovoltaic power generation planning extremely difficult. The main goal of this study is to accurately predict solar irradiance and establish a prediction model with meteorological characteristics to improve prediction accuracy. This paper proposes a convolutional neural network (CNN) and attention mechanism-based long short-term memory network (A-LSTM) to predict solar irradiance the next day. In addition, the prediction accuracy is further improved by combining similar day analyses. A similar day prediction model is constructed by selecting solar energy data from Andhra Pradesh, India. The experimental results show that the method proposed in this paper can predict solar irradiance more accurately, providing a new idea for photovoltaic power generation planning.Keywords: Solar irradiance prediction, Convolutional neural network, Long short-term memory, Similar day analysis

Introduction

In recent years, with the energy crisis and environmental pollution becoming increasingly severe, the demand for clean energy in the world is growing daily [1,2]. Solar energy, as one of the cleaner sources of renewable energy, can save a lot of greenhouse gas emissions [3,4]. At the same time, the use of solar energy can bring significant economic benefits [5]. As a result, it has received a great deal of attention. However, the problem of stochastic and intermittent nature of solar energy brings difficulties in the stable operation and planning and management of power grids, which affects the economic benefits of solar energy [[6], [7], [8]]. Accurately forecasting solar irradiance is of paramount importance in the efficient management of energy resources. It empowers grid operators and energy providers to proactively predict variations in solar power generation, facilitating a more seamless integration of renewable energy sources into the electrical grid. Given this, the current study is very relevant [9]. At present, forecasting methods can be divided into two categories: direct and indirect. The direct method uses historical power data as the input variable to predict the output power [10,11]. Nejati and Amjady proposed a direct prediction of solar power based on feature clustering and hybrid classification regression [12]. Kumar et al. directly predicted solar power using multilinear convolutional neural networks and multipoint time series methods to obtain high prediction accuracy [13]. The indirect method estimates the output power of the photovoltaic system according to the expected value of solar irradiance in the photovoltaic system [14,15]. Haoran Wen et al. used a regional solar forecasting method using generative adversarial networks and solar irradiance maps to indirectly predict solar power [16]. Li et al. proposed a solar irradiance prediction method based on CEE-WGAN-LSTM model to improve solar power prediction by increasing solar irradiance [17].

Solar irradiance prediction is mainly divided into data processing [18] and algorithm model prediction [19]. Data processing is primarily used to process abnormal features in metadata to improve data availability [20,21]. In addition, some feature engineering methods will be used to make data features more prominent. Hai Lan and other scholars developed an efficient solar irradiance prediction method using Fourier transform to extract the frequency features of the solar irradiance time series, and then principal component analysis (PCA) to identify the key frequency features to construct an efficient solar irradiance prediction method [22]. Irani Majumder et al. combines variational mode decomposition and a limit-learning machine. It decomposes the data into various modes using the variational mode decomposition method to predict non-stationary time series data accurately [23]. Presently, domestic and foreign scholars use different clustering algorithms to focus on the data in all its detail and improve the learning effect of prediction models on data and the trends therein [24,25]. Some scholars applied clustering and mixing strategies to improve the accuracy of solar irradiance prediction and proved the results through three methods [26,27]. However, they all ignored the fact that the solar irradiance data are strongly affected by meteorological factors. The relationship between meteorological factors and solar irradiance data needs to be fully considered and utilized to realize more accurate predictions [28,29].

The algorithm model can be divided into traditional prediction methods [30] and mixed model prediction methods [31,32]. The commonly used traditional prediction methods mainly include the statistical method [33,34], the physical method [35], and the artificial intelligence (AI) method [36,37]. Statistical methods establish a functional mapping between historical data and output, such as the time series method [38,39], regression analysis method [40,41], etc. Statistical models also depend on historical data, and it is necessary to exclude ill-conditioned data points that are not conducive to these models. Physical methods do not rely on much historical information, but instead study the equipment characteristics of data detection, and establish corresponding mathematical models for prediction. Meteorological and geological parameters used in physical and mathematical models are usually obtained through numerical weather forecasting or ground-based measurement equipment [42]. Therefore, this method requires very accurate measuring equipment. The AI method benefits from the rapid improvements in computing ability and can obtain better prediction accuracy by learning the mapping relationship between input and output using the AI algorithms [43,44]. The first two methods are simple in model training but highly dependent on data and poor in anti-interference ability. AI method has the capability of autonomous learning, can mine data rules, and provides better predictions. AI methods have been widely used in various predictions and related fields. The use of AI algorithms to solve the problem of solar irradiance prediction has also been widely recognized. Alberto Torres-Barr’ an et al. studied a bat algorithm combined with a support vector machine model to predict solar irradiance and obtained higher training accuracy and shorter training time [45]. Elsewhere, M. L. López et al. reviewed various regression tree algorithm models to predict solar irradiance and wind energy and confirmed that gradient integration-based methods could work effectively on prediction problems [46].

The hybrid model prediction method is a method that combines multiple algorithms to solve various problems in the prediction process by using the functions of different algorithms [47,48]. M. H. Ahmadi et al. proposed an artificial neural network (ANN) and empirical model, constructed multiple groups of comparison models for verification, and utilized numerous measurement indicators to verify that the proposed model is superior to the comparison model from various perspectives [49]. Pratima Kumari et al. proposed an artificial neural network model based on particle swarm optimization and genetic optimization to predict solar energy [50]. As an extension of machine learning, deep learning has received extensive attention from researchers. Its concept comes from the study of artificial neural networks [51]. Elsewhere, Ying Wang et al. proposed a method based on an enhanced forest and depth neural network to predict solar irradiance, and the model showed superior prediction performance in many experiments [52]. Fei Wang et al. proposed a new strategy based on short-term memory neural networks and Gaussian process regression to obtain reliable point prediction [53]. The performance of this model is proved to be better than that of a single LSTM model. Other workers also verified the excellent performance of the LSTM algorithm, indicating that the LSTM model has great application value in the field of solar irradiance prediction [54,55]. CNN is used to extract spatial features from a two-dimensional matrix and then uses LSTM to predict target data [56]. CNN and MLP have been used to predict target data at the same time; the results show that the CNN model is more robust, the prediction curve is smoother, and the non-linear piecewise fitting is better [57].

LSTM is one of the most important deep learning techniques, which has been widely used in related work. However, as a single deep learning model, LSTM can only extract long-term dependent features [58,59]. Our research proposes a new, improved CNN-A-LSTM depth fusion model that integrates similar day analysis and attention mechanisms to improve prediction accuracy further. The time and frequency domain characteristics of the data are extracted through CNN, and then LSTM is used to provide an accurate prediction.

The structure of this paper is as follows: Section 2 describes the introduction of the new solar irradiance prediction model used in this paper and the method of statistical analysis; Section 3 is results and discussion; Section 4 summarizes the work.

Meanwhile, LSTM reads the previous learning memory of the model through the memory and combines it with the current GHI data to complete the solar irradiance prediction. This memory mode connects the whole prediction process and correlates with each other. LSTM selectively remembers unusual events in long-term memory and gradually forgets those ordinary events. In other words, it stores the essential states of solar irradiance in memory and ignores the unimportant conditions. Similar day analysis

In this paper, similar days are defined as historical days with the same weather type as those days to be predicted. The data set of similar days can effectively reflect the power generation trend in cloudy and rainy weather and other partially cloudy sky conditions. Under partially cloudy sky conditions, solar irradiance is affected by weather factors and suffers inevitable fluctuations. Therefore, similar daily data sets are significant in predicting solar irradiance under partially cloudy sky weather. In this study, similar days are divided into clear sky weather (sunny days) and partially cloudy sky weather (cloudy days, rainy days). Fig. 9 displays an example of the GHI curve under clear sky and partially cloudy sky weather conditions. As shown in Fig. 9(a), Under clear sky weather conditions, GHI is typically distributed. As shown in Fig. 9(b–d), Under partially cloudy sky weather conditions, GHI has intense volatility, and it is difficult to mine the data rules. Therefore, the data under different conditions can be predicted by dividing them into similar days.

Conclusion

A new solar irradiance prediction method based on improved CNN-A-LSTM and attention mechanism is proposed to improve the accuracy of solar irradiance prediction and achieve the goal of improving power system optimal dispatching and management. The proposed model is based on the spatial characteristics of CNN-extracted meteorological parameters. A new structure based on the CNN-LSTM model is developed and applied to the prediction of solar irradiance. The attention mechanism is integrated into the model to achieve accurate prediction under different conditions to enhance the generalization and prediction abilities. In addition, a similar day model is established for targeted prediction research under different weather conditions. The performance of the proposed CNN-A-LSTM model is evaluated. The proposed model is compared with GBT, SVM, ANN, DNN, LSTM, CNN-DNN, and CNN-LSTM models. The prediction results show that for clear sky weather conditions, the proposed nRMSE and nMAE reach 0.054 and 0.049, respectively; R2 also reaches 96.57 %. Compared with other models, the error is significantly reduced, and predictions with higher accuracy can be obtained. Under partially cloudy sky weather conditions, the performance indices of the proposed model are lower than those under clear sky conditions. However, under the same conditions, there is still a significant improvement. Therefore, the model proposed in this paper has proved to be a favorable choice for predicting solar irradiance and can maintain a relatively high prediction accuracy under partially cloudy sky weather conditions. Experiments are also conducted using multiple datasets to demonstrate the good robustness and generalization of the proposed model. However, in the study of this paper, the tilt angle and azimuth angle of PV panels affect the conversion of solar radiation absorption thereby affecting the economic efficiency of PV power generation [62]. In future research, solar irradiance will be combined with the conversion rate of photovoltaic power generation to promote the further development of the solar energy business.
