17 C.F.R. § 232.14 — Paper filings not accepted without exemption.
Citation: us:cfr:17:232.14
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-232.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 232 (Regulation S-T—General Rules And Regulations For Electronic Filings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission will not accept in paper format any filing required to be submitted electronically under Rules 100 and 101 of Regulation S-T (§§ 232.100 and 232.101 respectively), unless the filing satisfies the requirements for a temporary or continuing hardship exemption under Rule 201 or 202 of Regulation S-T (§§ 232.201 or 232.202 respectively).
