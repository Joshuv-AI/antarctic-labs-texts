31 C.F.R. § 10.23 — Prompt disposition of pending matters.
Citation: us:cfr:31:10.23
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-10.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 10 (Practice Before The Internal Revenue Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A practitioner may not unreasonably delay the prompt disposition of any matter before the Internal Revenue Service.
