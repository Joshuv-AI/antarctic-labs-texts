47 C.F.R. § 14.33 — Informal or formal complaints.
Citation: us:cfr:47:14.33
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-14.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 14 (Access To Advanced Communications Services And Equipment By People With Disabilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Complaints against manufacturers or service providers, as defined under this subpart, for alleged violations of this subpart may be either informal or formal.
