48 C.F.R. § 2433.106 — 2433.106 Solicitation provision.

Year: 2026

The Contracting Officer shall insert the provision at 2452.233-70, Review of Contracting Officer Protest Decisions, in all solicitations for contracts expected to exceed the simplified acquisition threshold.
