38 U.S.C. § 3540 — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:38:3540
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section3540
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part III (Readjustment And Related Benefits); Chapter 35 (Survivors’ And Dependents’ Educational Assistance); Subchapter V (Special Restorative Training). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of special restorative training is to overcome, or lessen, the effects of a manifest physical or mental disability which would handicap an eligible person (other than a person made eligible under subparagraph (C) of such section 1 by reason of a spouse being listed in one of the categories referred to in that subparagraph) in the pursuit of a program of education.
