29 C.F.R. § 500.158 — Functions delegatable.
Citation: us:cfr:29:500.158
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-500.158
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 500 (Migrant And Seasonal Agricultural Worker Protection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary may delegate to the State such functions as he deems useful including the
(a) Receipt, handling and processing of applications for certificates of registration;
(b) Issuance of certificates of registration;
(c) Conduct of various investigations; and
(d) Enforcement of the Act.
