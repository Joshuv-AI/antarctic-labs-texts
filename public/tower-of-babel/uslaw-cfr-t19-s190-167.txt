19 C.F.R. § 190.167 — Liquidation.
Citation: us:cfr:19:190.167

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 190 (Modernized Drawback). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No deduction of 1 percent of the internal revenue taxes paid or determined will be made in allowing entries under section 5062(c), Internal Revenue Code, as amended (26 U.S.C. 5062(c)).
