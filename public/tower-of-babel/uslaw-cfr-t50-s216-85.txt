50 C.F.R. § 216.85 — Walrus and Otter Islands.
Citation: us:cfr:50:216.85
Creator: National Marine Fisheries Service, National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-216.85
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 216 (Regulations Governing The Taking And Importing Of Marine Mammals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

By Executive Order 1044, dated February 27, 1909, Walrus and Otter Islands were set aside as bird reservations. All persons are prohibited to land on these islands except those authorized by the appropriate representative of the National Marine Fisheries Service.
