36 U.S.C. § 151708 — Service of process
Citation: us:usc:36:151708
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section151708
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1517 (National Film Preservation Foundation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation shall have a designated agent to receive service of process for the corporation. Notice to or service on the agent, or mailed to the business address of the agent, is notice to or service on the corporation.
