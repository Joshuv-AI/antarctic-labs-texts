16 U.S.C. § 668d — Availability of appropriations for Migratory Bird Treaty Act
Citation: us:usc:16:668d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section668d
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 5A (Protection And Conservation Of Wildlife); Subchapter II (Protection Of Bald And Golden Eagles). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Moneys now or hereafter available to the Secretary of the Interior for the administration and enforcement of the Migratory Bird Treaty Act of July 3, 1918 [16 U.S.C. 703 et seq.], shall be equally available for the administration and enforcement of this subchapter.
