40 C.F.R. § 62.15230 — What types of stack tests must I conduct?
Citation: us:cfr:40:62.15230
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-62.15230
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 62 (Approval And Promulgation Of State Plans For Designated Facilities And Pollutants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Conduct initial and annual stack tests to measure the emission levels of dioxins/furans, cadmium, lead, mercury, particulate matter, opacity, hydrogen chloride, and fugitive ash.
