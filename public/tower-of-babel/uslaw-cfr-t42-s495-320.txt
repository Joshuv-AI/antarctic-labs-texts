42 C.F.R. § 495.320 — FFP for payments to Medicaid providers.
Citation: us:cfr:42:495.320
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-495.320
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 495 (Standards For The Electronic Health Record Technology Incentive Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subject to the requirements outlined in this subpart, FFP is available at 100 percent of State expenditures for payments to Medicaid eligible providers to encourage the adoption and meaningful use of certified EHR technology.
