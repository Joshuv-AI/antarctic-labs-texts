40 C.F.R. § 420.28 — Pretreatment standards compliance dates.
Citation: us:cfr:40:420.28
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-420.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 420 (Iron And Steel Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Compliance with the pretreatment standards for 2,3,7,8-TCDF for existing sources set forth in § 420.25(a) is required not later than October 17, 2005 whether or not the pretreatment authority issues or amends a pretreatment permit requiring such compliance.
