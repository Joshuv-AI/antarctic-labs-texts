46 C.F.R. § 159.003-7 — -7 Multiple approval numbers.
Citation: us:cfr:46:159.003-7
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-159.003-7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 159 (Approval Of Equipment And Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A product will not be issued a Coast Guard approval number by the Coast Guard if it already holds a Coast Guard approval number issued by a foreign authority under a Mutual Recognition Agreement.
