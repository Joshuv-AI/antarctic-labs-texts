32 C.F.R. § 1903.16 — Restriction on animals.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1903.16
Creator: Central Intelligence Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1903.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1903 (Conduct On Agency Installations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Animals, except for those animals used for the assistance of persons with disabilities, or animals under the charge and control of the Central Intelligence Agency, shall not be brought onto an Agency installation for other than official purposes.
