15 U.S.C. § 1223 — Limitations
Citation: us:usc:15:1223
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1223
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 27 (Automobile Dealer Suits Against Manufacturers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any action brought pursuant to this chapter shall be forever barred unless commenced within three years after the cause of action shall have accrued.
