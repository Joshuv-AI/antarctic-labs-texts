20 C.F.R. § 650.5 — Annual appeals performance plan.
Citation: us:cfr:20:650.5
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-650.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 650 (Standard For Appeals Promptness—Unemployment Compensation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No later than December 15 of each year, each State shall submit an appeals performance plan showing how it will operate during the following calendar year so as to achieve or maintain the issuance of at least 60 percent of all first level benefit appeals decisions within 30 days of the date of appeal, and 80 percent within 45 days.
