9 C.F.R. § 590.406 — Disposition of official certificates.
Citation: us:cfr:9:590.406
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-590.406
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 590 (Inspection Of Eggs And Egg Products (Egg Products Inspection Act)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The original and up to two copies of each official certificate shall be issued to the applicant or person designated by him. Other copies shall be filed and retained in accordance with the disposition schedule for inspection program records.
