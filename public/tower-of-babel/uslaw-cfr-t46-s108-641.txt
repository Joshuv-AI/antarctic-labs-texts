46 C.F.R. § 108.641 — Instructions for changing steering gear.
Citation: us:cfr:46:108.641
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-108.641
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 108 (Design And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Instructions stating, in order, the different steps to be taken for changing to emergency and secondary steering gear must be posted in the steering gear room and at each secondary steering station in 1.3 centimeters ( 1/2 inch) letters and numerals of contrasting color to the background.
