44 C.F.R. § 208.4 — Purpose for System.
Citation: us:cfr:44:208.4
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-208.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 208 (National Urban Search And Rescue Response System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is DHS policy to develop and provide a national system of standardized US&R resources to respond to Emergencies and Major Disasters that are beyond the capabilities of affected State and Local Governments.
