48 C.F.R. § 3001.301-72 — 3001.301-72 HSAC or HSAR Notice numbering.
Citation: us:cfr:48:3001.301-72
Creator: Chapter 30—Department Of Homeland Security, Homeland Security Acquisition Regulation (Hsar)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-3001.301-72
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 3001 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

HSACs and HSAR Notices will be numbered consecutively on a fiscal year basis beginning with number “01” prefixed by the last two digits of the fiscal year (e.g., HSAR Notices 03-01 and 03-02 indicate the first two HSAR Notices issued in fiscal year 2003).
