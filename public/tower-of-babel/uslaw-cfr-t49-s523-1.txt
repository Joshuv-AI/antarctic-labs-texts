49 C.F.R. § 523.1 — Scope.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 523 (Vehicle Classification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part establishes categories of vehicles that are subject to title V of the Motor Vehicle Information and Cost Savings Act, 15 U.S.C. 2001 et seq.
