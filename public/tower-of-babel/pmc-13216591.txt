Correction: Biological activity, UHPLC-MS phytochemical profiling, and computational studies of the leaf extract of Acridocarpus socotranus
Authors: Wafa M Al-Madhagi, Nasser A Awadh Ali, Mohamed A Al-Fatimi, Sirajudheen Anwar, Nafees Ahemad, Iman A Mansi, Nizar M Mhaidat, Rowaida N Al-Badani, Ibrahim Almarabi, Amani Naouar, Qosay Al-balas, Abdulwali Al-Khulaidi
Published: 2026

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-29208-7, published online 04 December 2025

The original version of this Article contained an error in the name of author Sirajudheen Anwar, which was incorrectly given as Siraj Aldeen Anwar.

Also, affiliation 4 was incorrectly given as “Department of Pharmaceutical Chemistry, College of Pharmacy, University of Hail, 81442 Hail, KSA, Saudi Arabia,” and it now reads “Department of Pharmacology and Toxicology, College of Pharmacy, University of Hail, 81442 Hail, Saudi Arabia.”

The original Article has been corrected.
