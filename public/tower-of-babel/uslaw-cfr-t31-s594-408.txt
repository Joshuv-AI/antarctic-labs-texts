31 C.F.R. § 594.408 — Payments from blocked accounts to satisfy obligations prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:31:594.408
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-594.408
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 594 (Global Terrorism Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pursuant to §§ 594.201 and 594.204, no debits may be made to a blocked account to pay obligations to U.S. persons or other persons, except as authorized pursuant to this part.
