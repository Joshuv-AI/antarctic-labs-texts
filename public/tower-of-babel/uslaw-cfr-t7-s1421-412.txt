7 C.F.R. § 1421.412 — Powers of attorney.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1421.412
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1421.412
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1421 (Grains And Similarly Handled Commodities—Marketing Assistance Loans And Loan Deficiency Payments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

DMAs may hold a power of attorney from a producer allowing the DMA to sign MAL and LDP documents for the producer, but DMAs may obtain and hold such powers only in accordance with the requirements of CCC governing such powers.
