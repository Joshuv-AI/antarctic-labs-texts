18 U.S.C. § 2390 — Enlistment to serve against United States
Citation: us:usc:18:2390
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section2390
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 115 (Treason, Sedition, And Subversive Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever enlists or is engaged within the United States or in any place subject to the jurisdiction thereof, with intent to serve in armed hostility against the United States, shall be fined under this title 1 or imprisoned not more than three years, or both.
