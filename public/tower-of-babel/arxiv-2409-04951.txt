Hidden charm 𝒫 c ​ s ​ ( 4338 ) 0 {\cal P}_{cs}(4338)^{0} production in baryonic B − → J / ψ ​ Λ ​ p ¯ B^{-}\to J/\psi\Lambda\bar{p} decay
Yu-Kuo Hsiao, Shu-Ting Cai, Yan-Li Wang

Abstract
We investigate the resonant baryonic B B decay B − → 𝒫 c ​ s 0 ​ p ¯ , 𝒫 c ​ s 0 → J / ψ ​ Λ B^{-}\to{\cal P}_{cs}^{0}\bar{p},{\cal P}_{cs}^{0}\to J/\psi\Lambda , where 𝒫 c ​ s 0 ≡ 𝒫 c ​ s ​ ( 4338 ) 0 {\cal P}_{cs}^{0}\equiv{\cal P}_{cs}(4338)^{0} is identified as a hidden charm pentaquark candidate with strangeness. By interpreting 𝒫 c ​ s 0 {\cal P}_{cs}^{0} as the Ξ c ​ D ¯ \Xi_{c}\bar{D} molecule that strongly decays into J / ψ ​ Λ J/\psi\Lambda and η c ​ Λ \eta_{c}\Lambda , we discover a dominant triangle rescattering effect for B − → 𝒫 c ​ s 0 ​ p ¯ B^{-}\to{\cal P}_{cs}^{0}\bar{p} , initiated by B − → J / ψ ​ K − B^{-}\to J/\psi K^{-} . Through the exchange of a Λ ¯ \bar{\Lambda} anti-baryon, J / ψ J/\psi and K − K^{-} undergo rescattering, transforming into 𝒫 c ​ s 0 {\cal P}_{cs}^{0} and p ¯ \bar{p} , respectively. Based on this rescattering mechanism, we calculate ℬ ( B − → 𝒫 c ​ s 0 p ¯ , 𝒫 c ​ s 0 → J / ψ Λ ) = ( 1.4 − 1.1 + 2.1 ) × 10 − 6 {\cal B}(B^{-}\to{\cal P}_{cs}^{0}\bar{p},{\cal P}_{cs}^{0}\to J/\psi\Lambda)=(1.4^{+2.1}_{-1.1})\times 10^{-6} , which is consistent with the measured data.

Discussions and Conclusion

The resonant

relies on the final state interaction. In our study,
we consider potential triangle rescattering processes,

the coupling constants from
{\cal P}_{cs}^{0}
strong decays.
Information from Refs.

, and
Wang:2024rsm

listed in Table

the uncertainties from the coupling constants and the cut-off parameter, respectively.
Additionally, it is found that some of these results are sensitive to

of
\Lambda_{\rm cut}

In S1 (Ortega)

is studied to consist of 45% of

in the constituent quark model.
Using

MeV

significantly decaying into
\Lambda_{c}^{+}\bar{D}_{s}^{-}

We adopt the decay widths in
