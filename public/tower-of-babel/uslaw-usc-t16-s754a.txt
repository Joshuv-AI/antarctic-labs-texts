16 U.S.C. § 754a — Appropriations for United States Fish and Wildlife Service; purchases from
Citation: us:usc:16:754a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section754a
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 9 (Fish And Wildlife Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior may purchase, to the extent of not to exceed $5,000, from the appropriations for the United States Fish and Wildlife Service, clothing and small stores for the crews of vessels, to be sold to the employees of said service and the appropriations reimbursed.
