Push-forward of smooth measures and strong Thom stratifications
Avraham Aizenbud, Nir Avni, and Shahar Carmeli

Abstract
. We study the collection of measures obtained via push-forward along a map between smooth varieties over p-adic fields. We investigate when the stalks of this collection are finite-dimensional. We provide an algebro-geometric criterion ensuring this property. This criterion is formulated in terms of a canonical subvariety of the cotangent bundle of the source of the map.

1.3.2.
A

The existence of a Thom stratification can be thought of as an infinitesimal version of the existence of a group acting on

(preserving
\phi

The Artin Approximation Theorem allows us replace the analytic flow of vector fields by an algebraic approximation of the formal flow, hence producing a Nisnevich neighborhood over which

\phi
factors as a submersion followed by a map from a lower dimensional space.
Using this machinery, we prove a local structure theorem for Thom stratifications:

(2)

Proof.

First, observe that

We shall describe how the operations

and

•

For

, by the compatibility of the relative spectrum construction with base change we have

, regarded as a subvariety of

For

and
