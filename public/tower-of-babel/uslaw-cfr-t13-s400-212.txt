13 C.F.R. § 400.212 — Guarantee.
Citation: us:cfr:13:400.212
Creator: Emergency Steel Guarantee Loan Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-400.212
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 400 (Emergency Steel Guarantee Loan Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board shall adopt a form of Guarantee to be used by the Board under the Program, and shall publish the Guarantee on its website. Modifications to the provisions of the form of Guarantee must be approved and adopted by the Board.
