5 C.F.R. § 1655.10 — Loan request process.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1655.10
Creator: Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1655.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1655 (Loan Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Any participant may apply for a loan by submitting a completed TSP loan request in the form and manner prescribed by the TSP record keeper.
(b) If a participant has a uniformed services account and a civilian account, a separate loan request must be made for each account.
