41 C.F.R. § 105-72.600 — -72.600 Purpose of reports and records.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-72 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Sections 105-72.601 through 105-72.603 set forth the procedures for monitoring and reporting on the recipient's financial and program performance and the necessary standard reporting forms. They also set forth record retention requirements.
