28 C.F.R. § 513.68 — Time limits for responses to Freedom of Information Act requests.
Citation: us:cfr:28:513.68
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-513.68
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 513 (Access To Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Consistent with sound administrative practice and the provisions of 28 CFR 16.1, the Bureau strives to comply with the time limits set forth in the Freedom of Information Act.
