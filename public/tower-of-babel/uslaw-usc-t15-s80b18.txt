15 U.S.C. § 80b–18 — Hiring and leasing authority of Commission
Citation: us:usc:15:80b–18
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section80b–18
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 2D (Investment Companies And Advisers); Subchapter II (Investment Advisers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of section 78d(b) of this title shall be applicable with respect to the power of the Commission—
(1)
to appoint and fix the compensation of such other employees as may be necessary for carrying out its functions under this subchapter, and
(2)
to lease and allocate such real property as may be necessary for carrying out its functions under this subchapter.
