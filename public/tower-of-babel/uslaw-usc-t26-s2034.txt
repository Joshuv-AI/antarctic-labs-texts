26 U.S.C. § 2034 — Dower or curtesy interests
Citation: us:usc:26:2034
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section2034
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 11 (Estate Tax); Subchapter A (Estates Of Citizens Or Residents); Part III (Gross Estate). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The value of the gross estate shall include the value of all property to the extent of any interest therein of the surviving spouse, existing at the time of the decedent’s death as dower or curtesy, or by virtue of a statute creating an estate in lieu of dower or curtesy.
