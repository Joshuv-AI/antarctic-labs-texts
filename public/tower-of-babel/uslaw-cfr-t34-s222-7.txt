34 C.F.R. § 222.7 — What information may a local educational agency submit after the application deadline?
Citation: us:cfr:34:222.7
Creator: Office Of Elementary And Secondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-222.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 222 (Impact Aid Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) General. Except as indicated in paragraph (b) of this section, the Secretary does not consider information submitted by an applicant after the deadlines prescribed in this subpart for submission of applications and amendments to applications.
(b) Information solicited by the Secretary. The Secretary may solicit from an applicant at any time additional information to process an application.
