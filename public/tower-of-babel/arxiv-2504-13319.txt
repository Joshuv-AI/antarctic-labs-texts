Let us consider a supersymmetric Landau problem,

\displaystyle H=\frac{1}{2m}\bigg(P^{2}_{i}+C_{\alpha\beta}P_{\alpha}P_{\beta}\bigg),

where

-1&0\end{array}\right),

the covariant momenta are given by:

P_{i}=-i\big(\partial_{i}+i\big(\tilde{B}/2\big)\varepsilon_{ij}x_{j}\big)\quad\mbox{and}\quad P_{\alpha}=i\big(\partial_{\alpha}-\big(\tilde{B}/2\big)\varepsilon^{2}_{\alpha\beta}\theta_{\beta}\big),

where

\tilde{B}
is a constant magnetic

x_{i}
and

are the bosonic and fermionic coordinates,
respectively.

\displaystyle\,z

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2\,l_{\tilde{B}}}\big(x+iy\big),\quad z^{\star}=\frac{1}{2\,l_{\tilde{B}}}\big(x-iy\big)

\displaystyle\,\partial

\displaystyle=l_{\tilde{B}}\big(\partial_{x}-i\partial_{y}\big),\quad\partial^{\star}=l_{\tilde{B}}\big(\partial_{x}+i\partial_{y}\big),

(4.4)

\displaystyle\,\theta

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}l_{\tilde{B}}}\,\theta_{1},\quad\theta^{\star}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}l_{\tilde{B}}}\,\theta_{2}

\displaystyle=\sqrt{2}l_{\tilde{B}}\,\partial_{\theta_{1}},\quad\partial_{\theta}=\sqrt{2}l_{\tilde{B}}\,\partial_{\theta_{2}},

(4.5)

where

In the symmetric gauge, we may define the bosonic

\displaystyle a=-\frac{i}{\sqrt{2}}\big(z+\partial^{\star}\big),\quad a^{\dagger}=\frac{i}{\sqrt{2}}\big(z^{\star}-\partial\big),

\displaystyle b=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\big(z^{\star}+\partial\big),\quad b^{\dagger}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\big(z-\partial^{\star}\big),

(4.7)

\displaystyle\alpha=-\frac{i}{\sqrt{2}}\big(\theta-\partial^{\star}_{\theta}\big),\quad\alpha^{\dagger}=-\frac{i}{\sqrt{2}}\big(\theta^{\star}-\partial_{\theta}\big),

(4.8)

\displaystyle\beta=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\big(\theta^{\star}+\partial_{\theta}\big),\quad\beta^{\dagger}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\big(\theta+\partial^{\star}_{\theta}\big).

They satisfy
[a,\,a^{\dagger}]=[b,\,b^{\dagger}]=1

\{\alpha,\,\alpha^{\dagger}\}=\{\beta,\,\beta^{\dagger}\}=1.
Other (anti) commutators are zeros.

In terms of the creation and annihilation operators,

\displaystyle H=\frac{\tilde{B}}{m}\big(a^{\dagger}a+\alpha^{\dagger}\alpha\big).

\displaystyle=(\sigma_{2})_{ij}x_{i}\partial_{j}+\frac{1}{2}(\sigma_{3})_{\alpha\beta}\theta_{\alpha}\partial_{\beta}
