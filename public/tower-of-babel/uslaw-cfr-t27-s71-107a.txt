27 C.F.R. § 71.107a — a Appropriate TTB officer's decision.
Citation: us:cfr:27:71.107a
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-71.107a
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 71 (Rules Of Practice In Permit Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) When the appropriate TTB officer issues an initial decision in accordance with § 71.79, the decision shall become a part of the record. The decision shall consist of
(1) A brief statement of the issues involved in the proceedings;
(2) The appropriate TTB officer's findings and conclusions, as well as the reasons therefor; and
(3) The appropriate TTB officer's determination on the record.
