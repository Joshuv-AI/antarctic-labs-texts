46 U.S.C. § 8906 — Penalty
Citation: us:usc:46:8906
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section8906
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part F (Manning Of Vessels); Chapter 89 (Small Vessel Manning). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An owner, charterer, managing operator, agent, master, or individual in charge of a vessel operated in violation of this chapter or a regulation prescribed under this chapter is liable to the United States Government for a civil penalty of not more than $25,000. The vessel also is liable in rem for the penalty.
