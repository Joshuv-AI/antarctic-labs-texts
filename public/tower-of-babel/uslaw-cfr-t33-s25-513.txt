33 C.F.R. § 25.513 — Amount claimed.
Citation: us:cfr:33:25.513
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-25.513
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 25 (Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The claimant shall state the amount claimed in the currency of the country where the incident occurred or where the claimant resided at the time of the incident.
