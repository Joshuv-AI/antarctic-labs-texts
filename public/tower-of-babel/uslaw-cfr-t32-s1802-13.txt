32 C.F.R. § 1802.13 — Identification of material at issue.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1802.13
Creator: National Counterintelligence Center
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1802.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1802 (Challenges To Classification Of Documents By Authorized Holders Pursuant To Section 1.9 Of Executive Order 12958). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Authorized holders shall append the documents at issue and clearly mark those portions subject to the challenge. If information not in documentary form is in issue, the challenge shall state so clearly and present or otherwise refer with specificity to that information in the body of the challenge.
