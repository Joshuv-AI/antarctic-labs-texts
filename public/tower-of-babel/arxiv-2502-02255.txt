Introduction

1.1.
Background and motivation

The uncertainty principle, which states that a non-zero function and its Fourier transform cannot be simultaneously sharply localized, is ubiquitous in harmonic analysis. The Fourier transform is given by

\hat{f}(\xi):=(2\pi)^{-\frac{n}{2}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}f(x)e^{-{\rm i}x\cdot\xi}\,{\rm d}x.

In
[
20

(
1.1)

with

\alpha\beta>\Big|\cos{\Big(\frac{p\pi}{2}\Big)}\Big|^{\frac{1}{p}},

(
1.2)

then
