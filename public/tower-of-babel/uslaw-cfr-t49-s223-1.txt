49 C.F.R. § 223.1 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:49:223.1
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-223.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 223 (Safety Glazing Standards—Locomotives, Passenger Cars And Cabooses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part provides minimum requirements for glazing materials in order to protect railroad employees and railroad passengers from injury as a result of objects striking the windows of locomotives, caboose and passenger cars.
