22 U.S.C. § 3661 — Panama Canal Board of Appeals; duties
Citation: us:usc:22:3661
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section3661
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 51 (Panama Canal); Subchapter I (Administration And Regulations); Part 2 (Employees); Subpart ii (Wage And Employment Practices). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Subject to the provisions of this part, the Commission shall prescribe regulations establishing a Panama Canal Board of Appeals. The regulations shall provide for the number of members of the Board and their appointment, compensation, and terms of office, the selection of a Chairman of the Board, the appointment and compensation of the Board’s employees, and other appropriate matters relating to the Board.
(b)
The Board shall review and determine the appeals of employees in accordance with section 3662 of this title. The decisions of the Board shall conform to the provisions of this subpart.
