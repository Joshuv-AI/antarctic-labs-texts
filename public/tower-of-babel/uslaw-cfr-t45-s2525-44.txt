45 C.F.R. § 2525.44 — How will AmeriCorps notify the Eligible Individual or Designated Recipient of its decision on the extension request?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2525.44
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2525.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2525 (National Service Trust). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

AmeriCorps will notify the Eligible Individual or Designated Recipient in writing if the request for an extension has been granted or denied. The notification will advise the requester of the process for appealing the denial if the requester has a good-faith basis to believe their request was erroneously denied.
