25 C.F.R. § 531.2 — Prohibited provisions.
Citation: us:cfr:25:531.2
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-531.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 531 (Content Of Management Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A management contract shall not transfer or, in any other manner, convey any interest in land or other real property, unless specific statutory authority exists and unless clearly specified in writing in the contract.
