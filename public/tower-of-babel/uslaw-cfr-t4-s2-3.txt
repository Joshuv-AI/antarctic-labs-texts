4 C.F.R. § 2.3 — GAO Personnel Appeals Board.
Citation: us:cfr:4:2.3
Creator: Government Accountability Office
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-4/section-2.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 4 (Accounts), Part 2 (Purpose And General Provision). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Government Accountability Office Personnel Appeals Board is established by 31 U.S.C. 751. This board will promulgate regulations providing for employee appeals and establishing its operating procedures.
