12 C.F.R. § 367.18 — Abrogation of contracts.
Citation: us:cfr:12:367.18
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-367.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 367 (Suspension And Exclusion Of Contractor And Termination Of Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The FDIC may, in its discretion, rescind or terminate any contract in existence at the time a contractor is suspended or excluded.
(b) Any contract not rescinded or terminated shall continue in force in accordance with the terms thereof.
(c) The right to rescind or terminate a contract in existence is cumulative and in addition to any other remedies or rights the FDIC may have under the terms of the contract, at law, or otherwise.
