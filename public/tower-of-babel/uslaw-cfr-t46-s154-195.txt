46 C.F.R. § 154.195 — Aluminum cargo tank: Steel enclosure.
Citation: us:cfr:46:154.195
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-154.195
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 154 (Safety Standards For Self-Propelled Vessels Carrying Bulk Liquefied Gases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An aluminum cargo tank and its dome must be enclosed by the vessel's hull structure or a separate steel cover.
(b) The steel cover for the aluminum cargo tank must meet the steel structural standards of the American Bureau of Shipping published in “Rules for Building and Classing Steel Vessels”, 1981.
(c) The steel cover for the aluminum tank dome must be:
(1) At least 3.2 mm ( 1/8 in.) thick;
(2) Separated from the tank dome, except at the support points; and
(3) Thermally isolated from the dome.
