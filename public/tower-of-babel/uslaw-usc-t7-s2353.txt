7 U.S.C. § 2353 — Testimony in Plant Variety Protection Office cases
Citation: us:usc:7:2353
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2353
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 57 (Plant Variety Protection); Subchapter I (Plant Variety Protection Office); Part B (Legal Provisions As To The Plant Variety Protection Office). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may establish regulations for taking affidavits, depositions, and other evidence required in cases before the Plant Variety Protection Office. Any officer authorized by law to take depositions to be used in the courts of the United States, or of the State where the officer resides, may take such affidavits and depositions, and swear the witnesses. If any person acts as a hearing officer by authority of the Secretary, the person shall have like power.
