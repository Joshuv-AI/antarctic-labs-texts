5 U.S.C. § 7501 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:5:7501
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section7501
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart F (Labor-Management And Employee Relations); Chapter 75 (Adverse Actions); Subchapter I (Suspension For 14 Days Or Less). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of this subchapter—
(1)
“employee” means an individual in the competitive service who is not serving a probationary or trial period under an initial appointment or who has completed 1 year of current continuous employment in the same or similar positions under other than a temporary appointment limited to 1 year or less; and
(2)
“suspension” means the placing of an employee, for disciplinary reasons, in a temporary status without duties and pay.
