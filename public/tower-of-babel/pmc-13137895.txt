Response to “Comment on ‘Prevalence and risk factors of paediatric seizures in COVID-19 infection: A study from Thailand’”
Authors: Vitchayaporn Emarach Saengow
Published: 2026

Dear Editor,

Thank you for your kind regards.

1. The prevalence of COVID-19 and seizures in this study is clearly shown in Fig. 3. We calculated it based on the total number of pediatric patients diagnosed with COVID-19, not on the 40/963 cases.

2. Neurodevelopmental outcomes were assessed using the Denver II, which is an appropriate tool for limited-resource settings; we used it together with clinical evaluation.
