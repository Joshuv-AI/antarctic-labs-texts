21 C.F.R. § 864.8175 — Calibrator for platelet counting.

Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 864 (Hematology And Pathology Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. A calibrator for platelet counting is a device that resembles platelets in plasma or whole blood and that is used to set a platelet counting instrument. It is a suspension of particles or cells whose size, shape concentration, and other characteristics have been precisely and accurately determined.
(b) Classification. Class II (special controls). The device is exempt from the premarket notification procedures in subpart E of part 807 of this chapter subject to the limitations in § 864.9.
