46 C.F.R. § 154.1750 — Butadiene or vinyl chloride: Refrigeration system.
Citation: us:cfr:46:154.1750
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-154.1750
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 154 (Safety Standards For Self-Propelled Vessels Carrying Bulk Liquefied Gases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A refrigeration system for butadiene or vinyl chloride must not use vapor compression unless it:
(a) Avoids any stagnation points where uninhibited liquid can accumulate; or
(b) Has inhibited liquid from the cargo tank added to the vapor upstream of the condenser.
