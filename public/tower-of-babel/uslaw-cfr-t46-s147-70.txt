46 C.F.R. § 147.70 — Acetylene.
Citation: us:cfr:46:147.70
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-147.70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 147 (Hazardous Ships' Stores). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Seventeen cubic meters (600 standard cubic feet) or less of acetylene may be stowed on or below decks on any vessel.
(b) More than 17 m 3 (600 standard cubic feet) of acetylene may be on board a vessel engaged in industrial operations, if it is stowed on deck.
