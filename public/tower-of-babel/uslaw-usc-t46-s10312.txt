46 U.S.C. § 10312 — Settlements on discharge
Citation: us:usc:46:10312
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section10312
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part G (Merchant Seamen Protection And Relief); Chapter 103 (Foreign And Intercoastal Voyages). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When discharge and settlement are completed, the master, individual in charge, or owner and each seaman shall sign the agreement required by section 10302 of this title.
