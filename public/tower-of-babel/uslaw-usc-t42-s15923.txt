42 U.S.C. § 15923 — Methodology
Citation: us:usc:42:15923
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section15923
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 149 (National Energy Policy And Programs); Subchapter III (Oil And Gas); Part B (Access To Federal Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior shall use the same assessment methodology across all geological provinces, areas, and regions in preparing and issuing national geological assessments to ensure accurate comparisons of geological resources.
