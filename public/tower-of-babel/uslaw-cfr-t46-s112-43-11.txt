46 C.F.R. § 112.43-11 — -11 Illumination for launching operations.
Citation: us:cfr:46:112.43-11
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-112.43-11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 112 (Emergency Lighting And Power Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Branch circuits supplying power to lights for survival craft launching operations must supply no other equipment and meet § 111.75-16 of this chapter.
