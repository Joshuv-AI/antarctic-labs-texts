2 U.S.C. § 5329 — Vouchers
Citation: us:usc:2:5329
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section5329
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 53 (House Of Representatives Members); Subchapter II (Employees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Payments under sections 5327 to 5329 of this title shall be made on vouchers approved by the Committee on House Oversight of the House of Representatives and signed by the chairman of such committee.
