17 C.F.R. § 21.04 — Special calls for information on customer accounts or related cleared positions.
Citation: us:cfr:17:21.04
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-21.04
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 21 (Special Calls). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon special call by the Commission, each futures commission merchant, clearing member or foreign broker shall provide information to the Commission concerning customer accounts or related positions cleared on a derivatives clearing organization in the format and manner and within the time provided by the Commission in the special call.
