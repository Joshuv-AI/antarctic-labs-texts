42 U.S.C. § 5903b — Environmental and safety research, development, and demonstration program
Citation: us:usc:42:5903b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section5903b
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 74 (Nonnuclear Energy Research And Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall conduct an environmental and safety research, development, and demonstration program related to fossil fuels.
