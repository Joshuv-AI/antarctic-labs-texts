Infinite-dimensional pre-Lie bialgebras via affinization of pre-Novikov bialgebras
Yue Li, Yanyong Hong

Abstract
. In this paper, we show that there is a pre-Lie algebra structure on the tensor product of a pre-Novikov algebra and a right Novikov dialgebra and the tensor product of a pre-Novikov algebra and a special right Novikov algebra on the vector space of Laurent polynomials being a pre-Lie algebra characterizes the pre-Novikov algebra. The latter is called the affinization of a pre-Novikov algebra. Moreover, we extend this construction of pre-Lie algebras and the affinization of pre-Novikov algebras to the context of bialgebras. We show that there is a completed pre-Lie bialgebra structure on the tensor product of a pre-Novikov bialgebra and a quadratic ℤ {\mathbb{Z}} -graded right Novikov algebra. Moreover, a pre-Novikov bialgebra can be characterized by the fact that its affinization by a quadratic ℤ {\mathbb{Z}} -graded right Novikov algebra on the vector space of Laurent polynomials gives an infinite-dimensional completed pre-Lie bialgebras. Note that the reason why we choose a quadratic right Novikov algebra instead of a right Novikov dialgebra with a special bilinear form is also given. Furthermore, we construct symmetric completed solutions of the S S -equation in the induced pre-Lie algebra by symmetric solutions of the pre-Novikov Yang-Baxter equation in a pre-Novikov algebra.

Introduction

Pre-Lie algebras (or left-symmetric algebras) are a class of Lie-admissible algebras whose commutators are Lie algebras, which
play important roles in many fields in mathematics and mathematical
physics such as convex homogenous cones
[
22
]
, affine manifolds and affine structures on Lie groups
[
14
,
19
]
, deformation of associative algebras
[
8
]
and so on.
Novikov algebras are pre-Lie algebras whose right multiplications are commutative. Novikov algebras appeared in the study of Hamiltonian
operators in the formal variational calculus
[
6
,
7
]
and Poisson brackets of hydrodynamic type
[
4
]
. Moreover, by
[
23
]
, Novikov algebras correspond to a class of Lie conformal algebras which describe the singular part of operator product expansion of chiral fields in conformal field
theory
[
12
]
.

The definition of pre-Novikov algebras was introduced in
[
10
]
in the study of Novikov bialgebras. It was shown in
[
10
]
that a pre-Novikov algebra naturally provides a skew-symmetric solution of the Novikov Yang-Baxter equation in the associated Novikov algebra and thus a Novikov bialgebra.
Moreover, there are close relationships between pre-Novikov algebras and other algebra structures. For example, pre-Novikov algebras are a class of
L
-dendriform algebras
[
3
]
, correspond to a class of left-symmetric conformal algebras
[
11
,
25
]
, and are closely related to Zinbiel algebras with derivations (see
[
10
,
13
]
).

It was shown in
[
4
]
that there is a natural Lie algebra structure on the tensor product of a Novikov algebra and the vector space
{\bf k}[t,t^{-1}]
, which is called the affinization of a Novikov algebra, and this affinization also characterizes the Novikov algebra. It can be stated in detail as follows.

Theorem 1.1
.

[
4
]

Let
A
be a vector space with a binary operation
\circ
. Define a binary operation on
A[t,t^{-1}]:=A\otimes{\bf k}[t,t^{-1}]
by

(1)

\displaystyle[at^{i},bt^{j}]=i(a\circ b)t^{i+j-1}-j(b\circ a)t^{i+j-1},\;\;\;a,b\in A,i,j\in\mathbb{Z},\vskip-2.84544pt

where
at^{i}:=a\otimes t^{i}
. Then
(A[t,t^{-1}],[\cdot,\cdot])

is a Lie algebra if and only if
(A,\circ)
is a Novikov algebra.

Note that
t^{i}\diamond t^{j}=it^{i+j-1}
for all
i
,
j\in{\mathbb{Z}}
defines a right Novikov algebra structure on
{\bf k}[t,t^{-1}]
. Such construction of Lie algebras can be interpretated from the view of operads. Let
\mathcal{P}
be a binary quadratic operad and
\mathcal{P}^{\textup{!}}
be its operadic Koszul dual. By
[
9
, Corollary 2.29]
(see also
[
18
, Proposition 7.6.5]
), the tensor product of a
\mathcal{P}
-algebra with a
\mathcal{P}^{\textup{!}}
-algebra is naturally endowed with a Lie algebra structure, giving a potentially very general procedure of constructing Lie algebras.
Note that, by the result in
[
5
]
, the Koszul dual of the operad of Novikov algebras is the operad of right Novikov algebras. Therefore, Novikov algebra affinization has
the operadic interpretation. It should be pointed out that the fact that the Lie bracket defined by Eq. (
1
) defining a Lie algebra characterizes a Novikov algebra is not trivial, since it has no good operadic interpretation, as far as we know.
The construction of Lie algebras from Novikov algebras and right Novikov algebras and the affinization of Novikov algebras were lifted to the context of bialgebras in
[
10
]
. It was shown that there is a natural completed Lie bialgebra structure on the tensor product of a Novikov bialgebra and a quadratic
{\mathbb{Z}}
-graded right Novikov algebra. Moreover, when the quadratic
{\mathbb{Z}}
-graded right Novikov algebra is a specific one defined on the vector space
{\bf k}[t,t^{-1}]
, the definition of Novikov bialgebras can be derived from the definition of completed Lie bialgebras
[
10
]
. Note that the operads of perm algebras and pre-Lie algebras are the Koszul dual each other and hence there is also a Lie algebra structure on the tensor product of a perm algebra and a pre-Lie algebra. The affinizations of perm algebras and pre-Lie algebras were introduced in
[
17
]
. Such construction and affinizations were also extended to the context of bialgebras in
[
17
]
.

Note that there is a Novikov algebra associated to a pre-Novikov algebra and the commutator of a pre-Lie algebra is a Lie algebra. Therefore, there should exist a natural construction of pre-Lie algebras from pre-Novikov algebras and right Novikov algebras. Note that a right Novikov algebra is a special right Novikov dialgebra
[
20
,
24
]
. In fact, we show that there is a natural pre-Lie algebra structure on the tensor product of a pre-Novikov algebra and a right Novikov dialgebra. In particular, when the right Novikov dialgebra is taken as the one on
{\bf k}[t,t^{-1}]
given in Theorem
1.1
, we present an affinization of pre-Novikov algebras, i.e. the tensor product of a pre-Novikov algebra and
{\bf k}[t,t^{-1}]
with a special binary operation being a pre-Lie algebra characterizes the pre-Novikov algebra (see Theorems
2.8
and
2.10
). Motivated by the results in
[
10
,
17
]
, the goal of this paper is to lift the construction of pre-Lie algebras from pre-Novikov algebras and right Novikov dialgebras and the affinization of pre-Novikov algebras to the level of bialgebras.

Note that the theory of pre-Novikov bialgebras was developed in
[
16
]
. Based on the the results in
[
10
,
17
]
, we need to construct a pre-Lie bialgebra from a finite-dimensional pre-Novikov bialgebra by a right Novikov dialgebra equipped with a special nondegenerate bilinear form. In the finite-dimensional case, a pre-Lie bialgebra is equivalent to a para-K
\ddot{\rm{a}}
hler Lie algebra which gives a quadratic pre-Lie algebra
[
2
]
, and a pre-Novikov bialgebra is equivalent to a double construction of quasi-Frobenius Novikov algebras which gives a quadratic pre-Novikov algebra
[
16
]
. Therefore, in the finite-dimensional case, we need to consider whether the tensor product of a double construction of quasi-Frobenius Novikov algebras
and a right Novikov dialgebra with a special nondegenerate bilinear form can be endowed with a natural para-K
\ddot{\texttt{a}}
hler Lie algebra structure. Note that a para-K
\ddot{\rm{a}}
hler Lie algebra gives a quadratic pre-Lie algebra and a double construction of quasi-Frobenius Novikov algebras gives a quadratic pre-Novikov algebra.
We should consider whether there is a natural quadratic pre-Lie algebra structure on the tensor product of a quadratic pre-Novikov algebra and a right Novikov dialgebra with a special nondegenerate bilinear form. We show that if the product of the bilinear form on the pre-Novikov algebra and the bilinear form on the right Novikov dialgebra defines a
quadratic pre-Lie algebra structure on the tensor product, then the right Novikov dialgebra with a special nondegenerate bilinear form should be a quadratic right Novikov algebra.
Note that this situation is totally different from those in
[
10
,
17
]
. Based on this observation, we focus on constructing a completed pre-Lie bialgebra from a finite-dimensional pre-Novikov bialgebra and a quadratic
{\mathbb{Z}}
-graded right Novikov algebra.

This paper is organized as follows. In Section 2, we recall the definitions of pre-Novikov (co)algebras, right Novikov algebras and right Novikov dialgebras and introduce the definitions of completed pre-Lie coalgebras and completed right Novikov co-dialgebras. We show that there is a pre-Lie algebra structure on the tensor product of a pre-Novikov algebra and a
{\mathbb{Z}}
-graded right Novikov dialgebra.
Moreover, if we take the right Novikov dialgebra as a special right Novikov algebra on the vector space of Laurent polynomials, the tensor product of a pre-Novikov algebra and this Novikov algebra being a pre-Lie algebra characterizes this pre-Novikov algebra, which is called the affinization of pre-Novikov algebras. Dually, we show that there is a completed pre-Lie coalgebra structure on the tensor product of a pre-Novikov coalgebra and a completed right Novikov co-dialgebra, and we also give an affinization of pre-Novikov coalgebras.

In Section 3, we first provide the reason why we choose a quadratic right Novikov algebra instead of a right Novikov dialgebra with a special bilinear form in the finite dimensional case. We show that there exists a completed pre-Lie bialgebra structure on the tensor product of a finite-dimensional pre-Novikov bialgebra and a quadratic
{\mathbb{Z}}
-graded right Novikov algebra. In the special case when the quadratic
{\mathbb{Z}}
-graded right Novikov algebra is a special one on the vector space of Laurent polynomials, we give a characterization of the pre-Novikov bialgebra by the affinization.

In Section 4, we recall the definitions of pre-Novikov Yang-Baxter equation in a pre-Novikov algebra and
S
-equation in a pre-Lie algebra. We construct symmetric completed solutions of the
S
-equation in the corresponding pre-Lie algebra from symmetric solutions of the pre-Novikov Yang-Baxter equation in a pre-Novikov algebra. Conversely, through a special quadratic
{\mathbb{Z}}
-graded right Novikov algebra, we can obtain symmetric solutions of the pre-Novikov Yang-Baxter equation in the corresponding pre-Novikov algebra from some symmetric completed solutions of the
S
-equation in the pre-Lie algebra.

Notations.

Throughout this paper, we fix a base field
{\bf k}
of characteristic
0
. All vector spaces and algebras are over
\bf k
. The identity map is denoted by
{\rm id}
and the set of integers is denoted by
{\mathbb{Z}}
. In this paper,
(A,\vartriangleleft,\vartriangleright)
is assumed to be a finite-dimensional pre-Novikov algebra. Additionally, for a
{\mathbb{Z}}
-graded vector space
B=\oplus_{i\in{\mathbb{Z}}}B_{i}
, we assume that each homogeneous component
B_{i}
is finite-dimensional.
Let
A
be a vector space with a binary operation
\circ
. Define linear maps

L_{\circ},R_{\circ}:A\rightarrow{\rm End}_{\bf k}(A)
by

\displaystyle L_{\circ}(a)b:=a\circ b,\;\;R_{\circ}(a)b:=b\circ a,\;\;\;a,b\in A.

Let

\tau:A\otimes A\rightarrow A\otimes A,\quad a\otimes b\mapsto b\otimes a,\;\;\;\;a,b\in A,

be the flip operator.

2.
Pre-Lie (co)algebras from pre-Novikov (co)algebras and right-Novikov (co)dialgebras

In this section, we show that there is a pre-Lie algebra structure on the tensor product of a pre-Novikov algebra and a right Novikov dialgebra. Moreover, if we take the right Novikov dialgebra as a special right Novikov algebra on the vector space of Laurent polynomials, the tensor product of a pre-Novikov algebra and this right Novikov algebra being a pre-Lie algebra characterizes this pre-Novikov algebra, which is called an affinization of pre-Novikov algebras. Dually, we show that there is a completed pre-Lie coalgebra structure on the tensor product of a pre-Novikov coalgebra and a completed right Novikov co-dialgebra and also give an affinization of pre-Novikov coalgebras.

2.1.
Pre-Lie algebras from pre-Novikov algebras and right-Novikov dialgebras

Recall that a
pre-Lie algebra
is a vector space
A
with a binary operation
\circ:A\otimes A\rightarrow A
satisfying

(2)

\displaystyle(a\circ b)\circ c-a\circ(b\circ c)

\displaystyle=(b\circ a)\circ c-b\circ(a\circ c),\quad a,b,c\in A.

Denote it by
(A,\circ)
. If the binary operation
\circ
also satisfies

(3)

\displaystyle(a\circ b)\circ c=(a\circ c)\circ b,\quad a,b,c\in A,

then we call
(A,\circ)
a
Novikov algebra
.

Let
(A,\circ)
be a pre-Lie algebra. Then
A
is a Lie algebra under the bracket operation
[a,b]=a\circ b-b\circ a
for all
a
,
b\in A
, which is denoted by
(\mathcal{G}(A),[\cdot,\cdot])
.
(\mathcal{G}(A),[\cdot,\cdot])
is called the
sub-adjacent Lie algebra
of
(A,\circ)
. We also call
(A,\circ)
the
compatible pre-Lie algebra structure
on the Lie algebra
(\mathcal{G}(A),[\cdot,\cdot])
.

Definition 2.1
.

[
10
]

A
pre-Novikov algebra
is a vector space
A
with binary operations
\vartriangleleft,\vartriangleright:A\otimes A\rightarrow A
satisfying

(4)

\displaystyle a\vartriangleright(b\vartriangleright c)

\displaystyle=(a\circ b)\vartriangleright c+b\vartriangleright(a\vartriangleright c)-(b\circ a)\vartriangleright c,

(5)

\displaystyle a\vartriangleright(b\vartriangleleft c)

\displaystyle=(a\vartriangleright b)\vartriangleleft c+b\vartriangleleft(a\circ c)-(b\vartriangleleft a)\vartriangleleft c,

(6)

\displaystyle(a\circ b)\vartriangleright c

\displaystyle=(a\vartriangleright c)\vartriangleleft b,

(7)

\displaystyle(a\vartriangleleft b)\vartriangleleft c

\displaystyle=(a\vartriangleleft c)\vartriangleleft b,\quad a,b,c\in A,

where
a\circ b:=a\vartriangleleft b+a\vartriangleright b
. Denote it by
(A,\vartriangleleft,\vartriangleright)
.

Proposition 2.2
.

[
10
, Proposition 3.33]

Let
(A,\vartriangleleft,\vartriangleright)
be a pre-Novikov algebra. The binary operation

(8)

\displaystyle a\circ b:=a\vartriangleleft b+a\vartriangleright b,\quad a,b\in A,

defines a Novikov algebra
(A,\circ)
, which is called the
associated Novikov algebra
of
(A,\vartriangleleft,\vartriangleright)
.

Next, we recall the definitions of right Novikov dialgebras and right Novikov algebras.

Definition 2.3
.

[
15
,
24
]

A
right Novikov dialgebra

(B,\dashv,\vdash)
is a vector space
B
equipped with two binary operations
\dashv
,
\vdash:B\otimes B\longrightarrow B
satisfying the following compatibility conditions

(9)

\displaystyle x\vdash(y\vdash z)=y\vdash(x\vdash z),\;\;x\vdash(y\dashv z)=y\dashv(x\dashv z),

(10)

\displaystyle(x\vdash y-x\dashv y)\vdash z=0,\;\;x\dashv(y\vdash z-y\dashv z)=0,

(11)

\displaystyle x\vdash(y\vdash z-z\dashv y)=(x\vdash y)\vdash z-(x\vdash z)\dashv y,

(12)

\displaystyle x\dashv(y\vdash z-z\dashv y)=(x\dashv y)\dashv z-(x\dashv z)\dashv y,\quad x,y,z\in B.

Definition 2.4
.

A
right Novikov algebra

(B,\diamond)
is a vector space
B
equipped with a binary operation
\diamond:B\otimes B\longrightarrow B
satisfying the following compatibility conditions

(13)

\displaystyle(x\diamond y)\diamond z-x\diamond(y\diamond z)=(x\diamond z)\diamond y-x\diamond(z\diamond y),

(14)

\displaystyle x\diamond(y\diamond z)=y\diamond(x\diamond z),\qquad x,y,z\in B.

Remark 2.5
.

If
(B,\dashv,\vdash)
is a right Novikov dialgebra where
\dashv=\vdash
, then
(B,\diamond:=\dashv=\vdash)
is a right Novikov algebra. Therefore, right Novikov algebras are special right Novikov dialgebras.

Definition 2.6
.

[
10
]

A
{\mathbb{Z}}
-graded right Novikov algebra
is a right Novikov algebra
(B,\diamond)
with a linear decomposition

B=\oplus_{i\in{\mathbb{Z}}}B_{i}
such that
B_{i}\diamond B_{j}\subset B_{i+j}
for all
i,j\in{\mathbb{Z}}
.

A
{\mathbb{Z}}
-graded pre-Lie algebra
is a pre-Lie algebra
(L,\circ)
with a linear decomposition
L=\oplus_{i\in{\mathbb{Z}}}L_{i}
such that
L_{i}\circ L_{j}\subset L_{i+j}
for all
i,j\in{\mathbb{Z}}

Example 2.7
.

[

Let
B={\bf k}[t,t^{-1}]
be the vector space of Laurent polynomials with a binary operation

Theorem 2.10
.

Let
A
be a vector space and
\vartriangleleft

2.2.
Completed pre-Lie coalgebras from pre-Novikov coalgebras and completed right-Novikov co-dialgebras

We first recall the definition of pre-Novikov coalgebras.

Definition 2.11
.

[
16
]

A
pre-Novikov coalgebra
is a vector space
A
with linear maps
\alpha,\beta:A\rightarrow A\otimes A
satisfying

(16)

\displaystyle(\alpha\otimes{\rm id})\alpha(a)+(\tau\otimes{\rm id})({\rm id}\otimes\alpha)\beta(a)-({\rm id}\otimes(\alpha+\beta))\alpha(a)-(\tau\otimes{\rm id})(\beta\otimes{\rm id})\alpha(a)=0,

(17)

\displaystyle({\rm id}\otimes\beta)\beta(a)+(\tau\otimes{\rm id})((\alpha+\beta)\otimes{\rm id})\beta(a)-((\alpha+\beta)\otimes{\rm id})\beta(a)-(\tau\otimes{\rm id})({\rm id}\otimes\beta)\beta(a)=0,

(18)

\displaystyle({\rm id}\otimes\tau)(\beta\otimes{\rm id})\alpha(a)-((\alpha+\beta)\otimes{\rm id})\beta(a)=0,

(19)

\displaystyle({\rm id}\otimes\tau)(\alpha\otimes{\rm id})\alpha(a)-(\alpha\otimes{\rm id})\alpha(a)=0,\quad a\in A.

Denote it by
(A,\alpha,\beta)
.

Let
V
and
W
be vector spaces. Suppose that
\phi:V\rightarrow W
is a linear map. Then there is an induced dual linear map
\phi^{*}:W^{*}\rightarrow V^{*}
defined by

(20)

\displaystyle\langle\phi^{*}(f),v\rangle=\langle f,\phi(v)\rangle,\quad v\in V,f\in W^{*}.

It is easy to see that

(A,\alpha,\beta)
is a pre-Novikov coalgebra if and only if
(A^{*},\alpha^{*},\beta^{*})
is a pre-Novikov algebra, if
A
is finite-dimensional.

By
[
10
, Lemma 2.10]
, we need to recall the notion of completed tensor products.

Let
C=\oplus_{i\in{\mathbb{Z}}}C_{i}
and
D=\oplus_{j\in{\mathbb{Z}}}D_{j}
be
{\mathbb{Z}}
-graded vector spaces. We call the
completed tensor product
of
C
and
D
to be the vector space

C\,\widehat{\otimes}\,D\coloneqq\prod_{i,j\in{\mathbb{Z}}}C_{i}\otimes D_{j}.

If
C
and
D
are finite-dimensional, then
C\,\widehat{\otimes}\,D
is just the usual tensor product
C\otimes D
.

In general, an element in
C\,\widehat{\otimes}\,D
is an infinite sum
\sum_{i,j\in{\mathbb{Z}}}\mathfrak{p}_{ij}
with
\mathfrak{p}_{ij}\in C_{i}\otimes D_{j}
. So
\mathfrak{p}_{ij}=\sum_{\alpha}c_{i\alpha}\otimes d_{j\alpha}
for pure tensors
c_{i\alpha}\otimes d_{j\alpha}\in C_{i}\otimes D_{j}
with
\alpha
in a finite index set.
Thus a general term of
C\,\widehat{\otimes}\,D
is a possibly infinite sum

(21)

\sum_{i,j,\alpha}c_{i\alpha}\otimes d_{j\alpha},

where
i,j\in{\mathbb{Z}}
and
\alpha
is in a finite index set (which might depend on
i,j
).

With these notations, for linear maps
f:C\to C^{\prime}
and
g:D\to D^{\prime}
, define

f\,\widehat{\otimes}\,g:C\,\widehat{\otimes}\,D\to C^{\prime}\,\widehat{\otimes}\,D^{\prime},\quad\sum_{i,j,\alpha}c_{i\alpha}\otimes d_{j\alpha}\mapsto\sum_{i,j,\alpha}f(c_{i\alpha})\otimes g(d_{j\alpha}).\vskip-5.69046pt

Also the twist map
\tau
has its completion

\widehat{\tau}:C\,\widehat{\otimes}\,C\to C\,\widehat{\otimes}\,C,\quad\sum_{i,j,\alpha}c_{i\alpha}\otimes d_{j\alpha}\mapsto\sum_{i,j,\alpha}d_{j\alpha}\otimes c_{i\alpha}.

Finally, we define a completed coproduct to be a linear map

\Delta:C\to C\,\widehat{\otimes}\,C,\quad\Delta(a):=\sum_{i,j,\alpha}a_{1i\alpha}\otimes a_{2j\alpha}.

Then we have the well-defined map

(\Delta\,\widehat{\otimes}\,{\rm id})\Delta(a)=(\Delta\,\widehat{\otimes}\,{\rm id})\Big(\sum_{i,j,\alpha}a_{1i\alpha}\otimes a_{2j\alpha}\Big):=\sum_{i,j,\alpha}\Delta(a_{1i\alpha})\otimes a_{2j\alpha}\in C\,\widehat{\otimes}\,C\,\widehat{\otimes}\,C.

Definition 2.12
.

A
completed right Novikov co-dialgebra
is a triple
(B,\Delta_{\alpha},\Delta_{\beta})
, where
B
is a vector space and

\Delta_{\alpha},\Delta_{\beta}:B\rightarrow B\,\widehat{\otimes}\,B
are linear maps satisfying for all
x\in B

(22)

\displaystyle({\rm id}\,\widehat{\otimes}\,\Delta_{\beta})\Delta_{\beta}(x)=(\tau\,\widehat{\otimes}\,{\rm id})({\rm id}\,\widehat{\otimes}\,\Delta_{\beta})\Delta_{\beta}(x),({\rm id}\,\widehat{\otimes}\,\Delta_{\alpha})\Delta_{\beta}(x)=(\tau\,\widehat{\otimes}\,{\rm id})({\rm id}\,\widehat{\otimes}\,\Delta_{\alpha})\Delta_{\alpha}(x),

(23)

\displaystyle(\Delta_{\beta}\,\widehat{\otimes}\,{\rm id})\Delta_{\beta}(x)=(\Delta_{\alpha}\,\widehat{\otimes}\,{\rm id})\Delta_{\beta}(x),({\rm id}\,\widehat{\otimes}\,\Delta_{\beta})\Delta_{\alpha}(x)=({\rm id}\,\widehat{\otimes}\,\Delta_{\alpha})\Delta_{\alpha}(x),

(24)

\displaystyle({\rm id}\,\widehat{\otimes}\,\Delta_{\beta})\Delta_{\beta}(x)-({\rm id}\,\widehat{\otimes}\,\tau)({\rm id}\,\widehat{\otimes}\,\Delta_{\alpha}(b))\Delta_{\beta}(x)=(\Delta_{\beta}\,\widehat{\otimes}\,{\rm id})\Delta_{\beta}(x)-({\rm id}\,\widehat{\otimes}\,\tau)(\Delta_{\beta}\,\widehat{\otimes}\,{\rm id})\Delta_{\alpha}(x),

(25)

\displaystyle({\rm id}\,\widehat{\otimes}\,\Delta_{\beta})\Delta_{\alpha}(x)-({\rm id}\,\widehat{\otimes}\,\tau)({\rm id}\,\widehat{\otimes}\,\Delta_{\alpha}(b))\Delta_{\alpha}(x)=(\Delta_{\alpha}\,\widehat{\otimes}\,{\rm id})\Delta_{\alpha}(x)-({\rm id}\,\widehat{\otimes}\,\tau)(\Delta_{\alpha}\,\widehat{\otimes}\,{\rm id})\Delta_{\alpha}(x).

Remark 2.13

If

is finite-dimensional, then
(B,\Delta_{\alpha},\Delta_{\beta})
is called a
right Novikov co-dialgebra
.

Definition 2.14

(a)

[

(b)

A
completed pre-Lie coalgebra

is a pair
(L,\delta)
, where

{\mathbb{Z}}
-graded vector space and

Remark 2.15

3.
Completed pre-Lie bialgebras from pre-Novikov bialgebras and quadratic right Novikov algebras

In this section, we first give the reason why we choose a quadratic right Novikov algebra instead of a right Novikov dialgebra with a special bilinear form in the finite dimensional case. Then we give a construction of compelted pre-Lie bialgebras from pre-Novikov bialgebras and quadratic
{\mathbb{Z}}

Remark 3.16
.

By Proposition
3.14
, since

\omega(c\circ b,a)

\omega(a\circ c,b)

\omega(c\circ b,a)

\omega(a\circ c,b)

\displaystyle-(y,x\vdash z)-(y,z\dashv x)+(y,z\vdash x)+(y,x\dashv z)=0,

\displaystyle-(y,z\dashv x)+(y,z\vdash x)=0,

\displaystyle(x\vdash y,z)-(y,x\vdash z)=0,

\displaystyle(x\vdash y,z)+(y\dashv x,z)+(y,z\dashv x)=0.

By the nondegenerate property of

is a quadratic right Novikov algebra.

On the other hand, let

be a quadratic right Novikov algebra. It is straightforward to verify that there is a para-K
\ddot{\texttt{a}}
hler Lie algebra

Hence, we only consider to construct pre-Lie bialgebras by pre-Novikov bialgebras and quadratic right Novikov algebras.

3.2.

Based on the discussion in Section

First, we recall some basic facts about quadratic
\mathbb{Z}
-graded right Novikov algebras.

Definition 3.17
.

[
10
]

Let
(B=\oplus_{i\in{\mathbb{Z}}}B_{i},\diamond)
be a
{\mathbb{Z}}
-graded right Novikov algebra. A bilinear form

(\cdot,\cdot)
on

if there exists some

such that

\displaystyle(B_{i},B_{j})=0,\;\;\;\text{for any $i$, $j\in\mathbb{Z}$ satisfying $i+j+m\neq 0$.}

A
quadratic
{\mathbb{Z}}
-graded right Novikov algebra
, denoted by
(B=\oplus_{i\in{\mathbb{Z}}}B_{i},\diamond,(\cdot,\cdot))
, is a
{\mathbb{Z}}
-graded right Novikov algebra
(B,\diamond)
together
with a symmetric nondegenerate graded bilinear form

(\cdot,\cdot)
satisfying Eq. (
49
). In particular, when
B=B_{0}
, then it is just a quadratic right Novikov algebra.

For a quadratic
{\mathbb{Z}}
-graded right Novikov algebra
(B=\oplus_{i\in{\mathbb{Z}}}B_{i},\diamond,(\cdot,\cdot))
, the nondegenerate symmetric bilinear form
(\cdot,\cdot)
induces multilinear forms
(\cdot,\cdot)_{k},k\geq 2
, by

(50)

(\cdot,\cdot)_{k}:(\underbrace{B\,\widehat{\otimes}\,\cdots\,\widehat{\otimes}\,B}_{k\text{-fold}})\otimes(\underbrace{B\otimes\cdots\otimes B}_{k\text{-fold}})\to{\bf k},\Big(\hskip-8.5359pt\sum_{i_{1},\cdots,i_{k},\alpha}a_{1i_{1}\alpha}\otimes\cdots\otimes a_{ki_{k}\alpha},b_{1}\otimes\cdots\otimes b_{k}\Big)_{k}\coloneqq\hskip-11.38092pt\sum_{i_{1},\cdots,i_{k},\alpha}\prod_{\ell=1}^{k}(a_{\ell i_{\ell}\alpha},b_{\ell})

with the notation in Eq. (
21

.
Further the forms are
left nondegenerate
in the sense that if

\Big(\sum_{i_{1},\cdots,i_{k},\alpha}a_{1i_{1}\alpha}\otimes\cdots\otimes a_{ki_{k}\alpha},b_{1}\otimes\cdots\otimes b_{k}\Big)_{k}=\Big(\sum_{j_{1},\cdots,j_{k},\beta}b_{1j_{1}\beta}\otimes\cdots\otimes b_{kj_{k}\beta},b_{1}\otimes\cdots\otimes b_{k}\Big)_{k}

for all homogeneous elements

, then

\sum_{i_{1},\cdots,i_{k},\alpha}a_{1i_{1}\alpha}\otimes\cdots\otimes a_{ki_{k}\alpha}=\sum_{j_{1},\cdots,j_{k},\beta}b_{1j_{1}\beta}\otimes\cdots\otimes b_{kj_{k}\beta}.

[

[

This completes the proof.
∎
