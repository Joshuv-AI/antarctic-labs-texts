DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service


Kootenai National Forest; Montana; Kootenai National Forest Over-
Snow Motorized Use Travel Plan; Withdrawal

AGENCY: Forest Service, Agriculture.

ACTION: Notice; Withdrawal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Kootenai National Forest is withdrawing its notice of 
intent to prepare an environmental impact statement (EIS) for the 
Kootenai National Forest Over-Snow Motorized Use Plan.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Questions concerning this project 
should be directed to NEPA Coordinator Moira McKelvey, 406-293-6211, 
[email protected].
    Individuals who use telecommunication devices for the hearing-
impaired may call 711 to reach the Telecommunications Relay Service, 24 
hours a day, every day of the year, including holidays.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: A Notice of Intent was published in the 
Federal Register on April 26, 2024, 89 FR 32394. The Kootenai National 
Forest's decision to withdraw the Notice of Intent is based on the 
length of time since the Notice of Intent was published. The project 
will be reinitiated at a later date.

Lisa Northrop,
Associate Deputy Chief, National Forest System.
