47 U.S.C. § 304 — Waiver by license of claims to particular frequency or of electromagnetic spectrum
Citation: us:usc:47:304
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title47-section304
Context: United States Code, Title 47 (Telecommunications); Chapter 5 (Wire Or Radio Communication); Subchapter III (Special Provisions Relating To Radio); Part I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No station license shall be granted by the Commission until the applicant therefor shall have waived any claim to the use of any particular frequency or of the electromagnetic spectrum as against the regulatory power of the United States because of the previous use of the same, whether by license or otherwise.
