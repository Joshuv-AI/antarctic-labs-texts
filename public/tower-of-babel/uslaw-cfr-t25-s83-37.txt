25 C.F.R. § 83.37 — What procedure follows the end of the comment period on a negative proposed finding?
Citation: us:cfr:25:83.37
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-83.37
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 83 (Procedures For Federal Acknowledgment Of Indian Tribes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If OFA has received comments on the negative proposed finding, then the petitioner will have 60 days to submit a written response, with citations to and explanations of supporting evidence, and the supporting evidence cited and explained in the response. The Department will not consider additional comments or evidence on the proposed finding submitted by individuals or entities during this response period.
