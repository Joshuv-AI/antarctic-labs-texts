20 C.F.R. § 687.160 — What is the timeframe for the Department to issue decisions on National Dislocated Worker Grant applications?
Citation: us:cfr:20:687.160
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-687.160
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 687 (National Dislocated Worker Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department will issue a final decision on a DWG application within 45 calendar days of receipt of an application that meets the requirements of this part. Applicants are encouraged to review their DWG application submissions carefully and consult with the appropriate Employment and Training Administration Regional Office to ensure their applications meet the requirements established in this part and those that may be set forth in guidance.
