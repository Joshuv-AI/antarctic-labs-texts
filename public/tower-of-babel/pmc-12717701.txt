Correction: Selenium mitigates vanadium toxicity through enhanced nutrition, photosynthesis, and antioxidant defense in rice (Oryza sativa L.) seedlings
Authors: Arwa Abdulkreem AL-Huqail, Suliman Mohammed Suliman Alghanem, Haifa Abdulaziz Sakit Alhaithloul, Zahid Khorshid Abbas, Siham M AL-Balawi, Doaa Bahaa Eldin Darwish, Baber Ali, Tabarak Malik, Sadia Javed
Published: 2025

Correction: BMC Plant Biol 24, 1071 (2024)

https://doi.org/10.1186/s12870-024-05790-2

Following publication of the original article [1], minor errors were identified in the Conclusions section. The updated conclusion is given below and the changes have been highlighted in bold typeface.

Current Conclusion: This research conclusively demonstrates the detrimental effects of V stress on tomato seedlings and the potent protective role of Se in mitigating these harmful impacts. Vanadium toxicity severely effects plant growth, and mineral homeostasis, leading to an overproduction of ROS. Conversely, Se application effectively circumvented V-induced phytotoxicity, resulting in substantial improvements in Se effectively regulated the uptake and distribution of essential minerals, ensuring balanced nutrient availability for optimal plant growth. Se protected as a potent antioxidant, significantly reducing ROS levels and protecting plant cells from oxidative damage. Furthermore, Se treatment significantly reduced V uptake in tomato seedlings, further minimizing the potential for V-induced damage. This study contributes valuable insights into the protective role of Se against V stress in tomato seedlings.

Corrected Conclusion: This research conclusively demonstrates the detrimental effects of V stress on rice seedlings and the potent protective role of Se in mitigating these harmful impacts. Vanadium toxicity severely effects plant growth, and mineral homeostasis, leading to an overproduction of ROS. Conversely, Se application effectively circumvented V-induced phytotoxicity, resulting in substantial improvements in Se effectively regulated the uptake and distribution of essential minerals, ensuring balanced nutrient availability for optimal plant growth. Se protected as a potent antioxidant, significantly reducing ROS levels and protecting plant cells from oxidative damage. Furthermore, Se treatment significantly reduced V uptake in rice seedlings, further minimizing the potential for V-induced damage. This study contributes valuable insights into the protective role of Se against V stress in rice seedlings.

The corrections do not compromise the validity of the conclusions and the overall content of the article. The author group has been updated above and the original article [1] has been corrected.
