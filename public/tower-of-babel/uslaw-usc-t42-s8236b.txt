42 U.S.C. § 8236b — Report
Citation: us:usc:42:8236b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section8236b
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 91 (National Energy Conservation Policy); Subchapter II (Residential Energy Conservation); Part D (Residential Energy Efficiency Rating Guidelines). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 3 years after October 24, 1992, the Secretary shall transmit to the President and the Congress a final report containing—
(1)
a description of actions taken by the Secretary and other Federal agencies to implement this part;
(2)
a description of the action taken by States, local governments, and other organizations to implement the voluntary guidelines issued under section 8236 of this title and any problems encountered in implementing such guidelines; and
(3)
recommendations on the feasibility of requiring, as a prerequisite to receiving federally assisted, guaranteed, or insured mortgages, the achievement of a minimum energy efficiency rating.
