40 C.F.R. § 13.27 — Joint and several liability.
Citation: us:cfr:40:13.27
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-13.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 13 (Claims Collection Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When two or more debtors are jointly and severally liable, collection action will not be withheld against one debtor until the other or others pay their proportionate share. The amount of a compromise with one debtor is not precedent in determining compromises from other debtors who have been determined to be jointly and severally liable on the claim.
