Correction to: The utility of endoscopic ultrasound-guided fine-needle aspiration and robotic surgery for submucosal-type metastatic rectal cancer with persistent descending mesocolon
Published: 2026

This is a correction to: Kurodo Koshino, Kimitaka Tani, Shimpei Ogawa, Fumi Maeda, Yuka Kaneko, Ryo Futatsuki, Yoshiko Bamba, Shunichi Ito, Shinsuke Maeda, Shigeki Yamaguchi, The utility of endoscopic ultrasound-guided fine-needle aspiration and robotic surgery for submucosal-type metastatic rectal cancer with persistent descending mesocolon, Journal of Surgical Case Reports, Volume 2026, Issue 6, June 2026, rjag512, https://doi.org/10.1093/jscr/rjag512

In the originally published version of this manuscript the authors missed to acknowledge the contributions of Ayumi Ito, Institute of Gastroenterology, Department of Internal Medicine, Tokyo Women’s Medical University.

The authors regret this oversight. The Acknowledgements section should read as follows:

Acknowledgements

The authors would like to express their gratitude to Dr. Ayumi Ito and the internal medicine staff who performed the endoscopy in this case at Tokyo Women’s Medical University, Institute of Gastroenterology, Department of Internal Medicine.

This error has been corrected.
