14 C.F.R. § 1259.404 — Suspension or termination of designation.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1259.404
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1259.404
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1259 (National Space Grant College And Fellowship Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator or designee, Director, NASA Space Grant Program, may, for cause, after an opportunity for a hearing before a Federal administrative judge appointed by the Deputy Administrator, suspend or terminate the Space Grant designation of any institution or consortium.
