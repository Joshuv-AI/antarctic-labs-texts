46 U.S.C. § 30910 — Exoneration and limitation
Citation: us:usc:46:30910
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section30910
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 309 (Suits In Admiralty Against The United States). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States is entitled to the exemptions from and limitations of liability provided by law to an owner, charterer, operator, or agent of a vessel.
