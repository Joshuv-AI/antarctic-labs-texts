15 U.S.C. § 671 — Establishment; Associate Administrator; appointment and compensation
Citation: us:usc:15:671
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section671
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 14B (Small Business Investment Program); Subchapter II (Small Business Investment Division Of Small Business Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is hereby established in the Small Business Administration a division to be known as the Small Business Investment Division. The Division shall be headed by an Associate Administrator who shall be appointed by the Administrator, and shall receive compensation at the rate provided by law for other Associate Administrators of the Small Business Administration.
