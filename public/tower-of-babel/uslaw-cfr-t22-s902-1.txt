22 C.F.R. § 902.1 — Chairperson and deputy chairperson.
Citation: us:cfr:22:902.1
Creator: Foreign Service Grievance Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-902.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 902 (Organization). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The chairperson presides over meetings of the Board. The chairperson shall select one of the Board members as deputy. In the absence of the chairperson, the deputy chairperson, or in his or her absence, another member designated by the chairperson, may act for him or her.
