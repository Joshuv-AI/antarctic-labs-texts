25 C.F.R. § 41.41 — Can the Tribal college or university receive technical assistance?
Citation: us:cfr:25:41.41
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-41.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 41 (Grants To Tribal Colleges And Universities And Diné College). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If a Tribal college or university sends the BIE a written request for technical assistance, BIE will respond within 30 days.
(b) The BIE will provide technical assistance either directly or through annual contract to a national Indian organization that the Tribal college or university designates.
(c) Technical assistance may include consulting services for developing programs, plans, and eligibility studies and accounting, and other services or technical advice.
