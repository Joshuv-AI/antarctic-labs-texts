5 C.F.R. § 470.205 — Initiation of research programs.
Citation: us:cfr:5:470.205
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-470.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 470 (Personnel Management Research Programs And Demonstrations Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

OPM will announce opportunities for research contracts by issuing Requests for Proposals (RFP's) in accordance with Federal procurement regulations. Unsolicited proposals may be accepted; however the relevance of the proposed research to OPM research needs will determine the acceptability of the proposal.
