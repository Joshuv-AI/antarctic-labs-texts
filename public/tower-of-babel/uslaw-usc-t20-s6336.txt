20 U.S.C. § 6336 — Adequacy of funding to local educational agencies in fiscal years after fiscal year 2001
Citation: us:usc:20:6336
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section6336
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 70 (Strengthening And Improvement Of Elementary And Secondary Schools); Subchapter I (Improving The Academic Achievement Of The Disadvantaged); Part A (Improving Basic Programs Operated By Local Educational Agencies); Subpart 2 (Allocations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Pursuant to section 6332 of this title, the total amount allocated in any fiscal year after fiscal year 2001 for programs and activities under this part shall not exceed the amount allocated in fiscal year 2001 for such programs and activities unless the amount available for targeted grants to local educational agencies under section 6335 of this title in the applicable fiscal year meets the requirements of section 6332(a) of this title.
