50 U.S.C. § 1514 — “United States” defined
Citation: us:usc:50:1514
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section1514
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 32 (Chemical And Biological Warfare Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Unless otherwise indicated, as used in this section 1 [50 U.S.C. 1512, 1513–1515, 1517] the term “United States” means the several States the District of Columbia, and the territories and possessions of the United States.
