31 C.F.R. § 409.1 — Standard.
Citation: us:cfr:31:409.1
Creator: Secret Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-409.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 409 (Standard And Procedures Utilized In Issuing A Security Clearance In Connection With An Application For A Press Pass To The White House). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In granting or denying a request for a security clearance made in response to an application for a White House press pass, officials of the Secret Service will be guided solely by the principle of whether the applicant presents a potential source of physical danger to the President and/or the family of the President so serious as to justify his or her exclusion from White House press privileges.
