Retraction Note: A multilayer deep autoencoder approach for cross layer IoT attack detection using deep learning algorithms
Authors: K. Saranya, A. Valarmathi
Published: 2026

Retraction of: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-93473-9, published online 25 March 2025

The Editors have retracted this Article.

After publication, concerns were raised regarding the use of nonstandard vocabulary throughout the manuscript. Further investigation identified internal inconsistencies in the reporting of the underlying data, methods, and results that prevent independent verification of the reported findings. These issues undermine confidence in the results and conclusions. Authors were unable to answer these concerns satisfactorily. The Editors therefore have lost confidence in the reliability of the content presented in this Article.

K. Saranya and A. Valarmathi disagree with this retraction.
