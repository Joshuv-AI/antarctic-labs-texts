16 U.S.C. § 482l — Perfection of mining claims within forest
Citation: us:usc:16:482l
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section482l
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 2 (National Forests); Subchapter I (Establishment And Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Valid mining claims within the said lands, existing on June 10, 1949, and thereafter maintained in compliance with the law under which they were initiated and the laws of the State of New Mexico, may be perfected under sections 482j to 482l of this title, or under the laws under which they were initiated, as the claimant may desire.
