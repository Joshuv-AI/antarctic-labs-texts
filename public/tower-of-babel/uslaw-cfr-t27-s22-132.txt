27 C.F.R. § 22.132 — Deposit in storage tanks.
Citation: us:cfr:27:22.132
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-22.132
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 22 (Distribution And Use Of Tax-Free Alcohol). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Recovered alcohol shall be accumulated and kept in separate storage tanks conforming to § 22.93. Recovered alcohol shall be measured before being redistilled or reused.
(b) Recovered alcohol may be removed from storage tanks for packaging and shipment to a distilled spirits plant for redistillation.
