41 C.F.R. § 105-64.103 — -64.103 What is GSA's policy on collecting and using information in a system of records?
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-64.103
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-64.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-64 (64—Gsa Privacy Act Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

System managers must collect information that is used to determine your rights, benefits, or privileges under GSA programs directly from you whenever practical, and use the information only for the intended purpose(s).
