14 C.F.R. § 1310.3 — Authority of the Board.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1310.3
Creator: Air Transportation System Stabilization
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1310.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1310 (Air Carrier Guarantee Loan Program Administrative Regulations And Amendment Or Waiver Of A Term Or Condition Of Guaranteed Loan). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pursuant to the provisions of the Act, the Board is authorized to guarantee loans provided to airlines by eligible lenders in accordance with the procedures, rules, and regulations established by the Board, to make the determinations authorized by the Act, and to take such other actions as necessary to carry out its functions specified in the Act.
