25 U.S.C. § 36 — Special agents and other officers to administer oaths
Citation: us:usc:25:36
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section36
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 2 (Officers Of Indian Affairs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each special agent, supervisor of schools, or other official charged with the investigation of Indian agencies and schools, in the pursuit of his official duties shall have power to administer oaths and to examine on oath all officers and persons employed in the Indian Service, and all such other persons as may be deemed necessary and proper.
