40 C.F.R. § 84.18 — Authorization of production for export allowances.
Citation: us:cfr:40:84.18
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-84.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 84 (Phasedown Of Hydrofluorocarbons). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) EPA will allocate 3,000.0 MTEVe of production for export allowances to Iofina Chemical by October 1 of the calendar year prior to the year in which the allowances may be used for calendar years 2026, 2027, 2028, 2029, and 2030.
(b) Production for export allowances cannot be transferred.
(c) Any regulated substances produced with production for export allowances must be exported in the same calendar year it was produced.
