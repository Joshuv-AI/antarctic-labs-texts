12 C.F.R. § 1805.810 — Limitation on liability.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1805.810
Creator: Community Development Financial Institutions Fund, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1805.810
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1805 (Community Development Financial Institutions Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The liability of the CDFI Fund and the United States Government arising out of any assistance to a CDFI in accordance with this part shall be limited to the amount of the investment in the CDFI. The CDFI Fund shall be exempt from any assessments and other liabilities that may be imposed on controlling or principal shareholders by any Federal law or the law of any State. Nothing in this section shall affect the application of any Federal tax law.
