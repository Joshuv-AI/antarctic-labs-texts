42 U.S.C. § 12163 — Conformance of accessibility standards
Citation: us:usc:42:12163
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section12163
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 126 (Equal Opportunity For Individuals With Disabilities); Subchapter II (Public Services); Part B (Actions Applicable To Public Transportation Provided By Public Entities Considered Discriminatory); Subpart ii (Public Transportation By Intercity And Commuter Rail). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Accessibility standards included in regulations issued under this subpart shall be consistent with the minimum guidelines issued by the Architectural and Transportation Barriers Compliance Board under section 12204(a) of this title.
