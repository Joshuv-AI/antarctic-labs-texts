25 C.F.R. § 15.101 — When should I notify the agency of the death of a person owning trust or restricted property?
Citation: us:cfr:25:15.101
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-15.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 15 (Probate Of Indian Estates, Except For Members Of The Osage Nation And The Five Civilized Tribes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

There is no deadline for notifying us of a death.
(a) Notify us as provided in § 15.103 to assure timely distribution of the estate.
(b) If we find out about the death of a person owning trust or restricted property we may initiate the process to collect the necessary documentation.
