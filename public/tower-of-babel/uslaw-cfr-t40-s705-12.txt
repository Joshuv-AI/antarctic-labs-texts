40 C.F.R. § 705.12 — Activities for which reporting is not required.
Citation: us:cfr:40:705.12
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-705.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 705 (Reporting And Recordkeeping Requirements For Certain Per- And Polyfluoroalkyl Substances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Reporting under this part is not required for the import of municipal solid waste streams for the purpose of disposal or destruction of the waste. Additionally, reporting is not required for a Federal agency which imports PFAS when it is not for any immediate or eventual commercial advantage.
