43 C.F.R. § 2543.3 — Purchase price required.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2543.3
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2543.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2540 (Color-Of-Title And Omitted Lands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If upon consideration of the application it shall be determined that the applicant is entitled to purchase the lands applied for, the applicant will be notified by registered mail that he must within 30 days from service of notice deposit the appraised price, or thereafter, and without further notice, forfeit all rights under his application.
