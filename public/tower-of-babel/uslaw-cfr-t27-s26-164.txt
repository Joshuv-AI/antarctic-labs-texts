27 C.F.R. § 26.164 — Proprietors of taxpaid premises.
Citation: us:cfr:27:26.164
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-26.164
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 26 (Liquors And Articles From Puerto Rico And The Virgin Islands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Transactions involving the bringing of liquors into the United States from Puerto Rico by proprietors of distilled spirits plants in the United States qualified under the provisions of this chapter shall be recorded and reported in accordance with the regulations governing the operations of such premises in the United States.
