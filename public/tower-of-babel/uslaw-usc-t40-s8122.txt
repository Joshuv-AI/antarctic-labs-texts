40 U.S.C. § 8122 — Jurisdiction over portion of Constitution Avenue
Citation: us:usc:40:8122
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section8122
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part D (Public Buildings, Grounds, And Parks In The District Of Columbia); Chapter 81 (Administrative); Subchapter II (Jurisdiction). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of the National Park Service has jurisdiction over that part of Constitution Avenue west of Virginia Avenue that was under the control of the Commissioners of the District of Columbia prior to May 27, 1908.
