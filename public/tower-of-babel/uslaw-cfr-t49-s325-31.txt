49 C.F.R. § 325.31 — Scope of the rules in this subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:49:325.31
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-325.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 325 (Compliance With Interstate Motor Carrier Noise Emission Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules in this subpart specify conditions and procedures for measurement of the sound level generated by a motor vehicle engaged in a highway operation for the purpose of ascertaining whether the motor vehicle conforms to the Standards for Highway Operations set forth in 40 CFR 202.20.
