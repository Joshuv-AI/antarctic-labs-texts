20 C.F.R. § 217.31 — Applicant's right to appeal denial.
Citation: us:cfr:20:217.31
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-217.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 217 (Application For Annuity Or Lump Sum). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each applicant is given the right to appeal the denial of his or her application if he or she does not agree with the Board's decision. The appeals process is explained in part 260 of this chapter.
