2 C.F.R. § 1800.210 — Pre-award costs.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1800.210
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1800.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1800 (Uniform Administrative Requirements, Cost Principles, And Audit Requirements For Federal Awards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As authorized by 2 CFR 200.308, Revision of budget and program plans, NASA waives the approval requirement for pre-award costs of 90 days or less. For more information on NASA's pre-award cost policy, see NASA's policy documents at https://www.nasa.gov/general/grants-policy-and-compliance-team/.
