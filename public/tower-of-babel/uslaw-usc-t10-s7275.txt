10 U.S.C. § 7275 — Medal of honor; distinguished-service cross; distinguished-service medal: delegation of power to award
Citation: us:usc:10:7275
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7275
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 737 (Decorations And Awards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may delegate his authority to award the medal of honor, distinguished-service cross, and distinguished-service medal, to a commanding general of a separate army or higher unit in the field.
