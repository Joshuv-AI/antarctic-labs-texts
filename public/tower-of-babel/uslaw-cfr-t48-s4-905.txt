48 C.F.R. § 4.905 — 4.905 Solicitation provision.
Citation: us:cfr:48:4.905
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-4.905
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 4 (Administrative And Information Matters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall insert the provision at 52.204-3, Taxpayer Identification, in solicitations that—
(a) Do not include the provision at 52.204-7, System for Award Management; and
(b) Are not conducted under the procedures of part 12.
