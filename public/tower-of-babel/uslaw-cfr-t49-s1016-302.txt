49 C.F.R. § 1016.302 — Filing and service of documents.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1016.302
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1016.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1016 (Special Procedures Governing The Recovery Of Expenses By Parties To Board Adjudicatory Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any application for an award or other pleading or document related to an application shall be filed and served on all parties to the proceeding in the same manner as other pleadings in the proceeding, except as provided in § 1016.202(b) for confidential financial information.
