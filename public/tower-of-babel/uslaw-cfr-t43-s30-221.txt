43 C.F.R. § 30.221 — May the judge limit the time, place, and scope of discovery?
Citation: us:cfr:43:30.221
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-30.221
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 30 (Indian Probate Hearings Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. The judge may limit the time, place, and scope of discovery either:
(a) On timely motion by any interested party, if that party also gives notice to all interested parties and shows good cause; or
(b) When the judge determines that limits are necessary to prevent delay of the proceeding or prevent undue hardship to a party or witness.
