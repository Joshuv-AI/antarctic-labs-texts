29 C.F.R. § 2580.412-7 — -7 Statutory provision—scope of the bond.
Citation: us:cfr:29:2580.412-7
Creator: Employee Benefits Security Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-2580.412-7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 2580 (Temporary Bonding Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The statute requires that the bond shall provide protection to the plan against loss by reason of acts of fraud or dishonesty on the part of a plan administrator, officer, or employee, directly or through connivance with others.
