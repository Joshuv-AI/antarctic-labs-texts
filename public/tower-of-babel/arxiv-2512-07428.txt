Single-Q and Double-Q magnetic orders: A Theoretical Analysis of Inelastic Neutron Scattering in a Centrosymmetric Structure.
Artem O. Nosenko, Dmitri V. Efremov

Abstract
Recent discoveries of multi- Q magnetic structures in centrosymmetric compounds have stimulated growing interest in their microscopic origin and observable properties. Here, we calculate the dynamical magnetic structure factor for a double- Q magnetic structure and compare it with that of a single- Q configuration. We show that double- Q structures exhibit characteristic features in their dynamical structure factor, enabling their unambiguous identification in inelastic neutron scattering experiments.

VII
Discussions and Conclusion

When comparing the low-energy part of the neutron-scattering cross section with the experimental results of Andryushin et al. (measured in the energy window up to 4 meV), we find good agreement. In addition, incorporating magnon damping is likely to further improve the quantitative agreement between theory and experiment (see Supplemental Material). However, the main spectral features of interest—most notably the roton-like minimum in the magnon dispersion—occur at higher energies, outside the reported range. We therefore expect that extending the measurements to higher energies would allow a more direct test of our predictions.

Summarising, in this study, we have examined the dynamical structure
factor associated with a set of magnetic configurations that can
emerge in a two-dimensional square lattice system. By constructing
the phase diagram as a function of anisotropy parameters, we have
identified the regions in parameter space where these distinct
magnetic structures are stabilized. Furthermore, we have analyzed the
corresponding dynamical structure factors, providing theoretical
predictions that can be tested in future inelastic neutron scattering
experiments.

When the paper was submitted, we learned that similar
results were obtained by M. Mostovoy and his co-authors.
