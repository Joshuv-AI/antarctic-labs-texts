22 C.F.R. § 1203.735-104 — -104 Applicability to detailed employees.
Citation: us:cfr:22:1203.735-104
Creator: United States International Development Cooperation Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1203.735-104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1203 (Employee Responsibilities And Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All the regulations of subparts A, B, and D of this part are applicable to an employee of another U.S. Government agency who may be serving on detail or assignment, formally or informally, on a reimbursable or nonreimbursable basis through a Participating Agency Service Agreement or otherwise, with an agency named in § 1203.735-102(a). However, disciplinary action shall be taken against such an employee only by the employing agency.
