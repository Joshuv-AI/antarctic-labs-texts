12 C.F.R. § 1263.32 — Official membership insignia.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1263.32
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1263.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1263 (Members Of The Banks). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Members may display the approved insignia of membership on their documents, advertising and quarters, and likewise use the words “Member Federal Home Loan Bank System.”
