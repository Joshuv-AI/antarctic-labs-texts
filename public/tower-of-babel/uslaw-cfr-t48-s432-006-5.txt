48 C.F.R. § 432.006-5 — 432.006-5 Reporting.
Citation: us:cfr:48:432.006-5
Creator: Chapter 4—Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-432.006-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 432 (Contract Financing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The annual report required by FAR 32.006-5 is to be prepared by the SPE and submitted to the Secretary within 90 calendar days after the end of the fiscal year. When signed by the Secretary, the report is to be maintained by the SPE.
