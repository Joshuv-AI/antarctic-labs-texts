31 C.F.R. § 351.6 — When may I redeem my Series EE savings bond?
Citation: us:cfr:31:351.6
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-351.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 351 (Offering Of United States Savings Bonds, Series Ee). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Bonds with issue dates on or before January 1, 2003. You may redeem your Series EE savings bond at any time beginning six months after its issue date.
(b) Bonds with issue dates on or after February 1, 2003. You may redeem your Series EE savings bond at any time beginning twelve months after its issue date.
