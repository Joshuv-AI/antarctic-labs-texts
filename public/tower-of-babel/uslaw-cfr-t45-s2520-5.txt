45 C.F.R. § 2520.5 — What definitions apply to this part?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2520.5
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2520.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2520 (General Provisions: Americorps Subtitle C Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

AmeriCorps means the Corporation for National and Community Service, established pursuant to section 191 of the National and Community Service Act of 1990, as amended, 42 U.S.C. 12651, which operates as AmeriCorps.
You. For this part, you refers to the grantee or an organization operating an AmeriCorps program.
