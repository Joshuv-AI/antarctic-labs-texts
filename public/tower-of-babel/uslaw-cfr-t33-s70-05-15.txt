33 C.F.R. § 70.05-15 — -15 Liability for damages.
Citation: us:cfr:33:70.05-15
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-70.05-15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 70 (Interference With Or Damage To Aids To Navigation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any boat, vessel, scow, raft or other craft used or employed in violating any of the provisions of § 70.05-1 shall be liable for the pecuniary penalties specified in § 70.05-5, and in addition thereto for the amount of damage done by said boat, vessel, scow, raft or other craft, which may be proceeded against summarily by way of libel in any district court of the United States having jurisdiction thereof.
