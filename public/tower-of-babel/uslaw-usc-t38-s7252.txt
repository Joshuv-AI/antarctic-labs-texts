38 U.S.C. § 7252 — Jurisdiction; finality of decisions
Citation: us:usc:38:7252
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section7252
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part V (Boards, Administrations, And Services); Chapter 72 (United States Court Of Appeals For Veterans Claims); Subchapter I (Organization And Jurisdiction). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Court of Appeals for Veterans Claims shall have exclusive jurisdiction to review decisions of the Board of Veterans’ Appeals. The Secretary may not seek review of any such decision. The Court shall have power to affirm, modify, or reverse a decision of the Board or to remand the matter, as appropriate.
(b)
Review in the Court shall be on the record of proceedings before the Secretary and the Board. The extent of the review shall be limited to the scope provided in section 7261 of this title. The Court may not review the schedule of ratings for disabilities adopted under section 1155 of this title or any action of the Secretary in adopting or revising that schedule.
(c)
Decisions by the Court are subject to review as provided in section 7292 of this title.
