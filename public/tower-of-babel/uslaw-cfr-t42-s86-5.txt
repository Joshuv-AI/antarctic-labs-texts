42 C.F.R. § 86.5 — Grant appeals procedure.
Citation: us:cfr:42:86.5
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-86.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 86 (Grants For Education Programs In Occupational Safety And Health). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The informal Public Health Service procedure for resolution of post-award grant disputes set forth in subpart D of part 50 of this title and the Department post-award grant appeals procedure in 45 CFR part 16 are applicable to any award made pursuant to this part.
