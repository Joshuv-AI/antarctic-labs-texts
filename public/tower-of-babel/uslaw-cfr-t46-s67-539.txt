46 C.F.R. § 67.539 — Copies of instruments and documents.
Citation: us:cfr:46:67.539
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-67.539
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 67 (Documentation Of Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The fee charged for furnishing a copy of any instrument or document is calculated in the same manner as described in 49 CFR 7.95.
