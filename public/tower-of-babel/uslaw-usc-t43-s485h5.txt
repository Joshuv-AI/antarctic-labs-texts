43 U.S.C. § 485h–5 — Supplement to Federal reclamation laws
Citation: us:usc:43:485h–5
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section485h–5
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 12 (Reclamation And Irrigation Of Lands By Federal Government); Subchapter X (Payment Of Construction Charges). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Sections 485h–1 to 485h–5 of this title shall be a supplement to the Federal reclamation laws (Act of June 17, 1902, 32 Stat. 388, and Acts amendatory thereof or supplementary thereto).
