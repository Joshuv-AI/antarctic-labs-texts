45 C.F.R. § 2525.440 — Is there a limit on the number of recipients an individual may designate to receive a transferred award?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2525.440
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2525.440
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2525 (National Service Trust). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An Eligible Individual may transfer all or part of a non-expired education award to no more than two recipients.
(b) If a Designated Recipient rejects, in whole or in part, a transferred education award, or a transfer was revoked in accordance with § 2525.480, the education award can be transferred to another Designated Recipient, so long as the education award has not yet expired.
