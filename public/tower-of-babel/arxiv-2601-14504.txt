On refined nonvanishing conjectures by Kurihara and Kolyvagin
Francesc Castella, Takamichi Sano

Abstract
. In this paper, we extend the results of [ 5 ] on refined conjectures by Kurihara and Kolyvagin, allowing primes of any reduction type in the case of Kurihara’s conjectures, and inert primes in the underlying imaginary quadratic field in the case of Kolyvagin’s. The key innovation is a new approach to the computation of the p p -divisibility index of certain special elements in Galois cohomology (the bottom class of a Λ \Lambda -adic Euler system twisted by a character sufficiently close to the trivial character) based on a reformulation of the Iwasawa Main Conjectures in terms of determinants of Selmer complexes.

For every

\bm{\kappa}^{\rm Kato}
or
\bm{\kappa}_{\Lambda}^{\rm Kato}(\alpha)

Proposition 2.1.5
.

Suppose

is a prime satisfying (

\mathscr{M}_{\infty}(\bm{\kappa})=\mathscr{M}_{\infty}^{(m)}(\bm{\kappa})

The inequality

\mathscr{M}_{\infty}(\bm{\kappa})\leq\mathscr{M}_{\infty}^{(m)}(\bm{\kappa})

is immediate from (

). Let

be such that

\mathscr{M}_{\infty}(\bm{\kappa})=\mathscr{M}_{r}:=\min\{{\rm ind}_{p}(\kappa_{n})\;\colon\;n\in\mathcal{N}\;\textrm{and}\;\nu(n)=r\},

and take

with
\nu(n)=r
and
{\rm ind}_{p}(\kappa_{n})=\mathscr{M}_{r}
. If
\mathscr{M}_{r}=\infty
there is nothing to show, so in the following we assume
\mathscr{M}_{r}<\infty
. Put

for the ease of notation, and note that necessarily

Suppose there exists a prime factor

to simplify notation. Letting

where

. By

, there exists

with

, the class
\kappa_{n\ell^{\prime}}^{(\mu+1)}\in\mathrm{H}^{1}_{\mathcal{F}(n\ell^{\prime})}(\mathbb{Q},T^{(\mu+1)})
