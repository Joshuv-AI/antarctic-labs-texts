41 C.F.R. § 105-50.001-4 — -50.001-4 Special-purpose unit of local government.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-50.001-4
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-50.001-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-50 (50—Provision Of Special Or Technical Services To State And Local Units Of Government). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Special-purpose unit of local government means any special district, public-purpose corporation, or other strictly limited-purpose political subdivision of a State, but shall not include a school district.
