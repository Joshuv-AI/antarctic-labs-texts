7 U.S.C. § 2104 — Finding and issuance of orders
Citation: us:usc:7:2104
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2104
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 53 (Cotton Research And Promotion). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

After notice and opportunity for hearing as provided in section 2103 of this title, the Secretary shall issue an order if he finds, and sets forth in such order, upon the evidence introduced at such hearing, that the issuance of such order and all the terms and conditions thereof will tend to effectuate the declared policy of this chapter.
