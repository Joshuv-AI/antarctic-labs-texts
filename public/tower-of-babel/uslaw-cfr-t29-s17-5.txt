29 C.F.R. § 17.5 — What is the Secretary's obligation with respect to Federal interagency coordination?
Citation: us:cfr:29:17.5
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-17.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 17 (Intergovernmental Review Of Department Of Labor Programs And Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary, to the extent practicable, consults with and seeks advice from all other substantially affected Federal departments and agencies in an effort to assure full coordination between such agencies and the Department regarding programs and activities covered under these regulations.
