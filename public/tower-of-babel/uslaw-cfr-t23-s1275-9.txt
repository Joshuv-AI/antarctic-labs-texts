23 C.F.R. § 1275.9 — States' responsibilities regarding compliance.
Citation: us:cfr:23:1275.9
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration And Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-1275.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 1275 (Repeat Intoxicated Driver Laws). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) States are responsible for ensuring compliance with 23 U.S.C. 164 and this part.
(b) A State that has been determined to be in compliance with the requirements of 23 U.S.C. 164 and this part must promptly notify the appropriate NHTSA Regional Administrator in writing of any change or change in enforcement of the State's repeat intoxicated driver law, identifying the specific change(s).
