46 C.F.R. § 530.2 — Scope and applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:46:530.2
Creator: Federal Maritime Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-530.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 530 (Service Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An individual ocean common carrier or an agreement between or among ocean common carriers may enter into a service contract with one or more shippers subject to the requirements of the Act.
