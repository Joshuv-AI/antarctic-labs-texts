10 U.S.C. § 3307 — Antitrust violations
Citation: us:usc:10:3307
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section3307
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part V (Acquisition); Subpart C (Contracting Methods And Contract Types); Chapter 241 (Awarding Of Contracts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If the head of an agency considers that a bid or proposal evidences a violation of the antitrust laws, he shall refer the bid or proposal to the Attorney General for appropriate action.
