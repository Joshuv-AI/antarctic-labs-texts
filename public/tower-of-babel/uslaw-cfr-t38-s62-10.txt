38 C.F.R. § 62.10 — Supportive services grants—general.
Citation: us:cfr:38:62.10
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-62.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 62 (Supportive Services For Veteran Families Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) VA provides supportive services grants to eligible entities as described in this part.
(b) Grantees must use at least 90 percent of supportive services grant funds to provide and coordinate the provision of supportive services to very low-income veteran families who are occupying permanent housing.
(c) Grantees may use up to 10 percent of supportive services grant funds for administrative costs identified in § 62.70.
