14 C.F.R. § 1214.610 — Violations.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1214.610
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1214.610
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1214 (Space Flight). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any items carried in violation of the requirements of this subpart shall become property of the U.S. Government, subject to applicable Federal laws and regulations, and the violator may be subject to disciplinary action, including being permanently prohibited from use of, or if an individual, from flying aboard a NASA mission.
