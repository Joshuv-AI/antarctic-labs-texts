7 C.F.R. § 3560.703 — Identification of unauthorized assistance.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3560.703
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3560.703
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3560 (Direct Multi-Family Housing Loans And Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Agency will use all available means to identify unauthorized assistance, including Agency monitoring activities, OIG reports, GAO reports, and reports from any source, if the information provided can be substantiated by the Agency.
(b) Borrowers have the primary responsibility for identifying repayment of unauthorized assistance received by tenants.
