10 U.S.C. § 1170 — Regular enlisted members: minority discharge
Citation: us:usc:10:1170
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1170
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 59 (Separation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Upon application by the parents or guardian of a regular enlisted member of an armed force to the Secretary concerned within 90 days after the member’s enlistment, the member shall be discharged for his own convenience, with the pay and form of discharge certificate to which his service entitles him, if—
(1)
there is evidence satisfactory to the Secretary concerned that the member is under eighteen years of age; and
(2)
the member enlisted without the written consent of his parent or guardian.
