12 U.S.C. § 2285a — Acquisition of obligations involving loan guarantees for New York City
Citation: us:usc:12:2285a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section2285a
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 24 (Federal Financing Bank). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in any provision of law shall be construed to authorize the Federal Financing Bank to acquire any obligation the payment of interest or principal of which has at any time been guaranteed in whole or in part under title I of the New York City Loan Guarantee Act of 1978.
