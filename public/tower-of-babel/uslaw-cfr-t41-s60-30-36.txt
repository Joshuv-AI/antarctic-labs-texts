41 C.F.R. § 60-30.36 — -30.36 Exceptions to recommendations.
Citation: us:cfr:41:60-30.36
Creator: Chapter 60—Office Of Federal Contract Compliance Programs, Equal Employment Opportunity, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-60-30.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 60-30 (30—Rules Of Practice For Administrative Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within 10 days after receipt of the recommended findings, conclusions and decision, any party may submit exceptions to said recommendations. Exceptions may be responded to by other parties within 7 days after receipt by said parties of the exceptions. All exceptions and responses shall be filed with the Administrative Review Board, United States Department of Labor. Briefs or exceptions and responses shall be served simultaneously on all parties to the proceeding.
