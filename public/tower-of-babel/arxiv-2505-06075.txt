Sinh Deformed Nakajima Operators
Boan Zhao, Paul Luis Roehl, Chunhao Li

Abstract
We prove a novel action of the (three-dimensional) Heisenberg algebra on the equivariant K-theory of the Hilbert scheme of points on ℂ 2 \mathbb{C}^{2} . These operators are defined via pushforwards and pullbacks via the Nakajima correspondences NakajimaMoreLectures while tensoring the square roots of the canonical line bundles of the correspondences. We show, using supersymmetric localisation in 6d ( 1 , 1 ) (1,1) Super Yang-Mills compactified on a circle, that these operators correspond to instanton line operators wrapping the extra circle.
