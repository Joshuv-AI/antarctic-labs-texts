46 U.S.C. § 12152 — Denial or revocation of endorsement for non-payment of civil penalty
Citation: us:usc:46:12152
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section12152
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part H (Identification Of Vessels); Chapter 121 (Documentation Of Vessels); Subchapter IV (Penalties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If the owner of a vessel fails to pay a civil penalty imposed by the Secretary, the Secretary may deny the issuance or renewal of an endorsement, or revoke the endorsement, on a certificate of documentation issued for the vessel under this chapter.
