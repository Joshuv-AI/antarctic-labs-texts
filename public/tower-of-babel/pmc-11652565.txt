Correction: Vorasidenib: First Approval
Authors: Yvette N. Lamb
Published: 2024

Correction: Drugs (2024) 84:1325–1331 10.1007/s40265-024-02097-2

The article Vorasidenib: First Approval, written by Yvette N. Lamb, was originally published Online First without Open Access. After publication in volume 84, issue 10, pages 1325–1331, Servier Affaires Medicales requested that the article be Open Choice to make the article an Open Access publication. Post-publication open access was funded by Servier Affaires Medicales.
