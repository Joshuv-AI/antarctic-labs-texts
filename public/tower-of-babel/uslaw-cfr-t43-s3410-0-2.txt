43 C.F.R. § 3410.0-2 — -2 Objective.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3410.0-2
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3410.0-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3410 (Exploration Licenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The objective of this subpart is to allow private parties singularly or jointly to explore coal deposits to obtain geological, environmental, and other pertinent data concerning the coal deposits.
