40 C.F.R. § 60.4770 — Does this subpart apply to my sewage sludge incineration unit?
Citation: us:cfr:40:60.4770
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.4770
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 60 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, your SSI unit is an affected source if it meets all the criteria specified in paragraphs (a) through (c) of this section.
(a) Your SSI unit is a SSI unit for which construction commenced after October 14, 2010 or for which modification commenced after September 21, 2011.
(b) Your SSI unit is a SSI unit as defined in § 60.4930.
(c) Your SSI unit is not exempt under § 60.4780.
