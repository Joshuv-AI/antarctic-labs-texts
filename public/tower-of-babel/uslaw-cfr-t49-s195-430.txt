49 C.F.R. § 195.430 — Firefighting equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:49:195.430
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-195.430
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 195 (Transportation Of Hazardous Liquids By Pipeline). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each operator shall maintain adequate firefighting equipment at each pump station and breakout tank area. The equipment must be—
(a) In proper operating condition at all times;
(b) Plainly marked so that its identity as firefighting equipment is clear; and
(c) Located so that it is easily accessible during a fire.
