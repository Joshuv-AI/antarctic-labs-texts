2 U.S.C. § 281 — Establishment

Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 9 (Office Of Legislative Counsel); Subchapter II (House Of Representatives); Part I (Purpose, Policy, And Function). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is established in the House of Representatives an office to be known as the Office of the Legislative Counsel, referred to hereinafter in this subchapter as the “Office”.
