40 C.F.R. § 227.9 — Limitations on quantities of waste materials.
Citation: us:cfr:40:227.9
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-227.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 227 (Criteria For The Evaluation Of Permit Applications For Ocean Dumping Of Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Substances which may damage the ocean environment due to the quantities in which they are dumped, or which may seriously reduce amenities, may be dumped only when the quantities to be dumped at a single time and place are controlled to prevent long-term damage to the environment or to amenities.
