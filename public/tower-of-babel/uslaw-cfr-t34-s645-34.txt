34 C.F.R. § 645.34 — How long is a project period?
Citation: us:cfr:34:645.34
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-645.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 645 (Upward Bound Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A project period under the Upward Bound program is five years.
