12 C.F.R. § 268.708 — Program accessibility: New construction and alterations.
Citation: us:cfr:12:268.708
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-268.708
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 268 (Rules Regarding Equal Opportunity). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each building or part of a building that is constructed or altered by, on behalf of, or for the use of the Board shall be designed, constructed, or altered so as to be readily accessible to and usable by individuals with a disability.
