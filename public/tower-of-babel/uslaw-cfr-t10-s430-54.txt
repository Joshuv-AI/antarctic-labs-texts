10 C.F.R. § 430.54 — Referral to the Attorney General.
Citation: us:cfr:10:430.54
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-430.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 430 (Energy Conservation Program For Consumer Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notice of the application for exemption under this subpart shall be transmitted to the Attorney General by the Secretary and shall contain (a) a statement of the facts and of the reasons for the exemption, and (b) copies of all documents submitted.
