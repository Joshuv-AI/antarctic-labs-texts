45 C.F.R. § 155.1405 — Enrollee satisfaction survey system.
Citation: us:cfr:45:155.1405
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-155.1405
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 155 (Exchange Establishment Standards And Other Related Standards Under The Affordable Care Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Exchange must prominently display results from the Enrollee Satisfaction Survey for each QHP on its website, in accordance with § 155.205(b)(1)(iv), in a form and manner specified by HHS.
