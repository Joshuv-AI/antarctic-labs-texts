    Abstract: Respondents to these forms supply the USGS with domestic 
production and consumption data for 13 ores, concentrates, metals, and 
ferroalloys, some of which are considered strategic and critical, to 
assist in determining Defense National Stockpile Center goals. These 
data and derived information will be published as chapters in USGS 
Minerals Yearbooks, monthly USGS Mineral Industry Surveys, annual USGS 
Mineral Commodity Summaries, and special publications for use by 
Government agencies, Congressional offices, industry education 
programs, research organizations, financial institutions, consulting 
firms, industry, academia, and the general public.
    The USGS Mineral Resources Program is submitting this Federal 
Register Notice as a revision to add a new `Iron and Steel Scrap and 
Pig Iron' monthly canvass (USGS Form 9-4148-M) to this ICR and to 
revise the `Iron and Steel Scrap and Pig Iron' monthly/annual canvass 
(USGS Form 9-4116-MA) including its conversion to an annual canvass 
only (USGS Form 9-4116-A). We estimate that the current 30 minutes 
average estimated burden time for the monthly/annual canvass will 
decrease to 20 minutes once converted to an annual canvass only. We 
also estimate an average estimated burden time of 10 minutes for the 
new `Iron and Steel Scrap and Pig Iron' monthly canvass. These 
adjustments are designed to boost response rates and maintain essential 
statistical insights for industry analyses. The annual canvass, in 
particular, will focus on eliminating redundant data points, allowing 
for more efficient responses. Both canvasses aim to capture the 
``missing middle'' of scrap consumption--materials sent to 
manufacturers with scrap and waste materials returned via contract--
whereas the previous canvass only identified post-consumer materials 
and home scrap. The updated form and new form also improve data 
collection, expand coverage of consumption and production, and support 
more advanced analytical capabilities.
    Title of Collection: Ferrous Metals Surveys.
    OMB Control Number: 1028-0068.
    Form Number: Various (16 USGS forms).
    Type of Review: Revision of a currently approved collection.
    Respondents/Affected Public: Businesses or other for-profit 
institutions; U.S. nonfuel minerals producers and consumers of ferrous 
and related metals.
    Total Estimated Number of Annual Respondents: 980.
    Total Estimated Number of Annual Responses: 2,718.
    Estimated Completion Time per Response: For each form, we will 
include an average burden time ranging from 10 minutes to 1 hour.
    Total Estimated Number of Annual Burden Hours: 899.
    Respondent's Obligation: Voluntary.
    Frequency of Collection: Monthly or annually.
    Total Estimated Annual Non-hour Burden Cost: There are no ``non-
hour cost'' burdens associated with this ICR.
    An agency may not conduct or sponsor, nor is a person required to

valid OMB control number.
