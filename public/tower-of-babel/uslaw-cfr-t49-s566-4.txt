49 C.F.R. § 566.4 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:49:566.4
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-566.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 566 (Manufacturer Identification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All terms defined in the Act and the rules and standards issued under its authority are used as defined therein. Specifically, incomplete vehicle, intermediate manufacturer, and final-stage manufacturer are used as defined in Part 568—Vehicles Manufactured in Two or More Stages.
