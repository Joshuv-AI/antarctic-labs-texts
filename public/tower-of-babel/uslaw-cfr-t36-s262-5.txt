36 C.F.R. § 262.5 — Disposal of purchased property.
Citation: us:cfr:36:262.5
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-262.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 262 (Law Enforcement Support Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All evidence purchased under the authority of this subpart shall be maintained in accordance with all laws, regulations, and rules applicable to the care, custody, and control of evidence. Evidence purchased under this subpart shall be disposed of in accordance with laws, regulation, rules, and Forest Service policy applicable to the disposal of evidence.
