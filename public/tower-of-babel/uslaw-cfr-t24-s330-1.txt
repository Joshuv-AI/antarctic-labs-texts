24 C.F.R. § 330.1 — Scope of part.
Citation: us:cfr:24:330.1
Creator: Government National Mortgage Association, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-330.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 330 (Guaranty Of Multiclass Securities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part is limited to multiclass securities. It does not purport to set forth all the procedures and requirements that apply to the issuance and guaranty of such securities. All such transactions are governed by the specific terms and provisions of the contracts entered into by the parties and by the GNMA Multiclass Securities Guide (Multiclass Guide).
