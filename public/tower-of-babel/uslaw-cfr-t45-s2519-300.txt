45 C.F.R. § 2519.300 — Who may participate in a Higher Education program?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2519.300
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2519.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2519 (Higher Education Innovative Programs For Community Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Students, faculty, administration and staff of an institution, as well as residents of the community may participate. For the purpose of this part, the term “student” means an individual who is enrolled in an institution of higher education on a full-time or part-time basis.
