42 C.F.R. § 137.450 — Does the Equal Access to Justice Act (EAJA) apply to appeals under this subpart?
Citation: us:cfr:42:137.450
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-137.450
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 137 (Tribal Self-Governance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, EAJA claims against the Department will be heard pursuant to 25 CFR 900.177.
