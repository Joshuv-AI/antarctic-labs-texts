20 U.S.C. § 80r–9 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:20:80r–9
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section80r–9
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 3 (Smithsonian Institution, National Museums And Art Galleries); Subchapter XIV (National Museum Of African American History And Culture). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
There are authorized to be appropriated to the Smithsonian Institution to carry out this subchapter, other than sections 80r–5(b) and 80r–6 of this title—
(1)
$17,000,000 for fiscal year 2004; and
(2)
such sums as are necessary for each fiscal year thereafter.
(b) Availability
Amounts made available under subsection (a) shall remain available until expended.
(c) Use of funds for fundraising
Amounts appropriated pursuant to the authorization under this section may be used to conduct fundraising in support of the Museum from private sources.
