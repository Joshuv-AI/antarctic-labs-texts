42 U.S.C. § 263 — Preparation of biological products by Service
Citation: us:usc:42:263
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section263
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter II (General Powers And Duties); Part F (Licensing Of Biological Products And Clinical Laboratories); Subpart 1 (Biological Products). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Service may prepare for its own use any product described in section 262 of this title and any product necessary to carrying out any of the purposes of section 241 of this title.
(b)
The Service may prepare any product described in section 262 of this title for the use of other Federal departments or agencies, and public or private agencies and individuals engaged in work in the field of medicine when such product is not available from establishments licensed under such section.
