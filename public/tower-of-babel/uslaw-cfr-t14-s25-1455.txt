14 C.F.R. § 25.1455 — Draining of fluids subject to freezing.
Citation: us:cfr:14:25.1455
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-25.1455
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 25 (Airworthiness Standards: Transport Category Airplanes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If fluids subject to freezing may be drained overboard in flight or during ground operation, the drains must be designed and located to prevent the formation of hazardous quantities of ice on the airplane as a result of the drainage.
