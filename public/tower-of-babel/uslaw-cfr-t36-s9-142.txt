36 C.F.R. § 9.142 — Will the NPS adjust my financial assurance?
Citation: us:cfr:36:9.142
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-9.142
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 9 (Minerals Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Regional Director may require, or you may request, an adjustment to the financial assurance amount because of any circumstance that increases or decreases the estimated costs established under § 9.141.
