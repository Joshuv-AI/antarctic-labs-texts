14 C.F.R. § 1245.200 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1245.200
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1245.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1245 (Patents And Other Intellectual Property Rights). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this subpart is to set forth policies and procedures for the filing and disposition of claims of infringement of privately owned rights in patented inventions or copyrighted works asserted against NASA.
