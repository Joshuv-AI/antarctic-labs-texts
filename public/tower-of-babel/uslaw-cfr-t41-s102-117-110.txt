41 C.F.R. § 102-117.110 — -117.110 Corrective options.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-117.110
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-117.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-117 (117—Transportation Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Decisions regarding temporary nonuse, suspension, or debarment are made by the agency, following procedures outlined in the FAR in title 48 of the Code of Federal Regulations and other applicable regulations. Agencies must maintain records of these actions and notify relevant parties as required.
