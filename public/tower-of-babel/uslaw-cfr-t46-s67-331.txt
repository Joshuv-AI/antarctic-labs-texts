46 C.F.R. § 67.331 — Improper markings.
Citation: us:cfr:46:67.331
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-67.331
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 67 (Documentation Of Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The owner of a documented vessel shall not permit the operation of that vessel unless it is marked in accordance with subpart I of this part.
