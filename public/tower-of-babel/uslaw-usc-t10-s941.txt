10 U.S.C. § 941 — Art. 141. Status
Citation: us:usc:10:941
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section941
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 47 (Uniform Code Of Military Justice); Subchapter XII (United States Court Of Appeals For The Armed Forces). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is a court of record known as the United States Court of Appeals for the Armed Forces. The court is established under article I of the Constitution. The court is located for administrative purposes only in the Department of Defense.
