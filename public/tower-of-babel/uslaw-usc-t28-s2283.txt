28 U.S.C. § 2283 — Stay of State court proceedings
Citation: us:usc:28:2283
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section2283
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part VI (Particular Proceedings); Chapter 155 (Injunctions; Three-Judge Courts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A court of the United States may not grant an injunction to stay proceedings in a State court except as expressly authorized by Act of Congress, or where necessary in aid of its jurisdiction, or to protect or effectuate its judgments.
