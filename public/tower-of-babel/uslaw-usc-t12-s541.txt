12 U.S.C. § 541 — Tax on circulating notes generally
Citation: us:usc:12:541
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section541
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 4 (Taxation); Subchapter II (National Bank Circulation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In lieu of all existing taxes, every association shall pay to the Treasurer of the United States, in the months of January and July, a duty of one-half of 1 per centum each half year upon the average amount of its notes in circulation.
