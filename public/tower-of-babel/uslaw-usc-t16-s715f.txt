16 U.S.C. § 715f — Consent of State to conveyance in fee
Citation: us:usc:16:715f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section715f
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 7 (Protection Of Migratory Game And Insectivorous Birds); Subchapter III (Migratory Bird Conservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No deed or instrument of conveyance in fee shall be accepted by the Secretary of the Interior under this subchapter unless the State in which the area lies shall have consented by law to the acquisition by the United States of lands in that State.
