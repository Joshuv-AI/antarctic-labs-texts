22 C.F.R. § 911.6 — Finality of choice.
Citation: us:cfr:22:911.6
Creator: Foreign Service Grievance Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-911.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 911 (Implementation Disputes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An alleged violation of an institutional right as reflected in a collective bargaining agreement may be filed under these procedures or as an unfair labor practice, but not both.
