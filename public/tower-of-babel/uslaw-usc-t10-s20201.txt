10 U.S.C. § 20201 — Original appointments: how made
Citation: us:usc:10:20201
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Space Force); Chapter 2005 (Officers); Subchapter I (Original Appointments). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In General.—
The provisions of section 531 of this title shall apply to original appointments of commissioned officers in the Space Force.
(b) Grade Upon Appointment.—
(1)
The grade of a person receiving an appointment under this section who at the time of appointment is credited with service under section 20203 of this title shall be determined under regulations prescribed by the Secretary of the Defense based upon the amount of service credited.
(2)
The grade of a person receiving an appointment under this section who at the time of the appointment is a commissioned officer of a reserve component shall be determined under section 20203(e) of this title.
