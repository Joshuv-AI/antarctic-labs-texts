48 C.F.R. § 6104.404 — 6104.404 Reply to agency response [Rule 404].
Citation: us:cfr:48:6104.404
Creator: Chapter 61—Civilian Board Of Contract Appeals, General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-6104.404
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 6104 (Travel And Relocation Expenses Cases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A claimant may file a reply to the agency response within 30 calendar days after receiving the response. If the claim has been forwarded by the agency, the claimant shall have 30 calendar days from the time the claim is docketed by the Board to reply. To expedite proceedings, if the claimant does not wish to reply, the claimant should so notify the Board and the agency.
