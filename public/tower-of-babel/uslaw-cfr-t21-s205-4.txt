21 C.F.R. § 205.4 — Wholesale drug distributor licensing requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:21:205.4
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-205.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 205 (Guidelines For State Licensing Of Wholesale Prescription Drug Distributors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every wholesale distributor in a State who engages in wholesale distributions of prescription drugs in interstate commerce must be licensed by the State licensing authority in accordance with this part before engaging in wholesale distributions of prescription drugs in interstate commerce.
