38 U.S.C. § 7807 — Audit of accounts
Citation: us:usc:38:7807
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section7807
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part V (Boards, Administrations, And Services); Chapter 78 (Veterans’ Canteen Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Service shall maintain a set of accounts which shall be audited by the Comptroller General in accordance with the provisions of chapter 35 of title 31.
