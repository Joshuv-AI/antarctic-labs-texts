40 C.F.R. § 203.3 — Test procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:40:203.3
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-203.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 203 (Low-Noise-Emission Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The applicant shall test or cause his product to be tested in accordance with procedures contained in the regulations issued pursuant to section 6 of the act unless otherwise specified.
(b) The Administrator may conduct whatever investigation is necessary, including actual inspection of the product at a place designated by him.
