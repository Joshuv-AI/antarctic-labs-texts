42 U.S.C. § 5408 — Cooperation by Secretary with public and private agencies
Citation: us:usc:42:5408
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section5408
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 70 (Manufactured Home Construction And Safety Standards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to advise, assist, and cooperate with other Federal agencies and with State and other interested public and private agencies, in the planning and development of—
(1)
manufactured home construction and safety standards;
(2)
methods for inspecting and testing to determine compliance with manufactured home standards; and
(3)
model guidance to support the submission of the certification required under section 5403(i) of this title.
