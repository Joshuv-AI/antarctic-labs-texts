39 C.F.R. § 3013.21 — Authority to order depositions and responses to written interrogatories.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3013.21
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3013.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3013 (Procedures For Compelling Production Of Information By The Postal Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Chairman, any designated Commissioner, or any administrative law judge may order the taking of depositions and responses to written interrogatories by a covered person with respect to any proceeding conducted under title 39 of the United States Code or to obtain information to be used to prepare a report under that title.
