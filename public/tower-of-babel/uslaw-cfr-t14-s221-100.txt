14 C.F.R. § 221.100 — Public notice of tariff information.
Citation: us:cfr:14:221.100
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-221.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 221 (Tariffs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Carriers must make tariff information available to the general public, and in so doing must comply with either:
(a) Sections 221.101, 221.102, 221.103, 221.104, 221.105, and 221.106, or
(b) Sections 221.105, 221.106 and 221.107 of this subpart.
