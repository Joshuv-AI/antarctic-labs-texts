26 U.S.C. § 997 — Special subchapter C rules
Citation: us:usc:26:997
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section997
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 1 (Normal Taxes And Surtaxes); Subchapter N (Tax Based On Income From Sources Within Or Without The United States); Part IV (Domestic International Sales Corporations); Subpart B (Treatment Of Distributions To Shareholders). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of applying the provisions of subchapter C of chapter 1, any distribution in property to a corporation by a DISC or former DISC which is made out of previously taxed income or accumulated DISC income shall—
(1)
be treated as a distribution in the same amount as if such distribution of property were made to an individual, and
(2)
have a basis, in the hands of the recipient corporation, equal to the amount determined under paragraph (1).
