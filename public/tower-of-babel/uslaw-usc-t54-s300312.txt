54 U.S.C. § 300312 — National Trust
Citation: us:usc:54:300312
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section300312
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3003 (Definitions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this division, the term “National Trust” means the National Trust for Historic Preservation in the United States established under section 312102 of this title.
