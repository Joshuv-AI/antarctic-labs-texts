19 C.F.R. § 351.603 — Complaint of price-undercutting by subsidized imports.
Citation: us:cfr:19:351.603
Creator: International Trade Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-351.603
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 351 (Antidumping And Countervailing Duties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon receipt of a complaint filed with the Secretary of Agriculture under section 702(b) of the Trade Agreements Act concerning price-undercutting by subsidized imports, the Secretary will promptly determine, under section 702(a)(3) of the Trade Agreements Act of 1979, whether or not the alleged subsidies are included in or should be added to the latest annual list or quarterly update.
