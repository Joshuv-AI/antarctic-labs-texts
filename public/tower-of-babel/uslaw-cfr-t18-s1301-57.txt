18 C.F.R. § 1301.57 — Waiver.
Citation: us:cfr:18:1301.57
Creator: Tennessee Valley Authority
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-1301.57
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 1301 (Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The General Counsel may grant a waiver of any procedure described by this part where a waiver is considered necessary to promote a significant interest of TVA or the United States, or for other good cause.
