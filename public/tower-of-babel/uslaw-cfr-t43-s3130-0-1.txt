43 C.F.R. § 3130.0-1 — -1 Purpose.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3130 (Oil And Gas Leasing: National Petroleum Reserve, Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These regulations establish the procedures under which the Secretary of the Interior will exercise the authority granted to administer a competitive leasing program for oil and gas within the National Petroleum Reserve—Alaska.
