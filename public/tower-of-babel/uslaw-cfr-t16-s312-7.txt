16 C.F.R. § 312.7 — Prohibition against conditioning a child's participation on collection of personal information.
Citation: us:cfr:16:312.7
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-312.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 312 (Children'S Online Privacy Protection Rule (Coppa Rule)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An operator is prohibited from conditioning a child's participation in a game, the offering of a prize, or another activity on the child's disclosing more personal information than is reasonably necessary to participate in such activity.
