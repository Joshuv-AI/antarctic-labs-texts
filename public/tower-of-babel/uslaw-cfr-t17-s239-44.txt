17 C.F.R. § 239.44 — Form SF-1, registration statement under the Securities Act of 1933 for offerings of asset-backed securities.
Citation: us:cfr:17:239.44
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-239.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 239 (Forms Prescribed Under The Securities Act Of 1933). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This Form shall be used for registration under the Securities Act of 1933 of all offerings of asset-backed securities, as defined in 17 CFR 229.1101(c).
