17 C.F.R. § 269.1 — Form T-1, for statement of eligibility and qualification for corporate trustees.
Citation: us:cfr:17:269.1
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-269.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 269 (Forms Prescribed Under The Trust Indenture Act Of 1939). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This form shall be filed pursuant to Rule 5a-1(a) (§ 260.5a-1(a) of this chapter) for statements of eligibility and qualification of corporations designated to act as trustees under thrust indentures to be qualified pursuant to section 305 or 307 of the Trust Indenture Act of 1939.
