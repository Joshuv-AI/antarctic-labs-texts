33 C.F.R. § 115.30 — Transfer of permits.
Citation: us:cfr:33:115.30
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-115.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 115 (Bridge Locations And Clearances; Administrative Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Permits express merely the assent of the Federal Government so far as concerns the public rights of navigation. Although issued to a specific party, the assent is not limited to execution of the work by that party and may be availed of by the assignees or purchasers of the property affected, provided the terms of the instrument are strictly complied with.
