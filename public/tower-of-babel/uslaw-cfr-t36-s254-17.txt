36 C.F.R. § 254.17 — Information requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:36:254.17
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-254.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 254 (Landownership Adjustments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirements governing the preparation of an agreement to initiate in § 254.4 of this subpart and an exchange agreement in § 254.14 of this subpart constitute information requirements as defined by the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1980 (44 U.S.C. 3507) and have been approved for use pursuant to 5 CFR part 1320 and assigned OMB Control Number 0596-0105.
