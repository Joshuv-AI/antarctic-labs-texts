5 C.F.R. § 850.104 — Implementing directives.
Citation: us:cfr:5:850.104
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-850.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 850 (Electronic Retirement Processing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director must prescribe, in the form he or she deems appropriate, such detailed procedures as the Director determines to be necessary to carry out the purpose of this part.
