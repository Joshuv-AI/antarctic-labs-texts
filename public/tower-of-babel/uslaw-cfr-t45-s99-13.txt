45 C.F.R. § 99.13 — Place.
Citation: us:cfr:45:99.13
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-99.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 99 (Procedure For Hearings For The Child Care And Development Fund). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The hearing shall be held in the city in which the regional office of the Department responsible for oversight of the Lead Agency is located or in such other place as the Assistant Secretary determines, considering both the circumstances of the case and the convenience and necessity of the parties or their representatives.
