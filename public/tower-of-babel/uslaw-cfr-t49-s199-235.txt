49 C.F.R. § 199.235 — Required evaluation and testing.
Citation: us:cfr:49:199.235
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-199.235
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 199 (Drug And Alcohol Testing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No operator shall permit a covered employee who has engaged in conduct prohibited by §§ 199.215 through 199.223 to perform covered functions unless the employee has met the requirements of § 199.243.
