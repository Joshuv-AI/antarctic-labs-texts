16 U.S.C. § 1108 — Insurance
Citation: us:usc:16:1108
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section1108
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 22 (International Parks). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commission shall maintain insurance in reasonable amounts, including, but not limited to, liability and property insurance. Such insurance may not cover the Commissioners or employees of the Commission except when sued by name for acts done in the scope of their employment.
