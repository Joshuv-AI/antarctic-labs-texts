45 C.F.R. § 2553.108 — What happens if a sponsor fails to meet the target performance measures included in the approved grant application?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2553.108
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2553.108
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2553 (The Retired And Senior Volunteer Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a sponsor fails to meet a target performance measure established in the approved grant application, AmeriCorps may take one or more of the following actions:
(a) Reduce the amount, suspend, or deny refunding of the grant, in accordance with the provisions of § 2553.31;
(b) Terminate the grant, in accordance with 45 CFR part 1206.
