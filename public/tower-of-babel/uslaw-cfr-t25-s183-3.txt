25 C.F.R. § 183.3 — Does the American Indian Trust Fund Management Reform Act of 1994 apply to this part?
Citation: us:cfr:25:183.3
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-183.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 183 (Use And Distribution Of The San Carlos Apache Tribe Development Trust Fund And San Carlos Apache Tribe Lease Fund). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. We will manage and make distributions from the Trust Fund in accordance with the American Indian Trust Funds Management Act of 1994 (Management Act), except where the Management Act conflicts with the Settlement Act or this part. If there is a conflict, we will follow the provisions of the Settlement Act or this part.
