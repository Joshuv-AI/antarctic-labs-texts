44 C.F.R. § 6.84 — Form of payment.
Citation: us:cfr:44:6.84
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-6.84
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 6 (Implementation Of The Privacy Act Of 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Payment shall be by check or money order payable to The Federal Emergency Management Agency and shall be addressed to the system manager.
