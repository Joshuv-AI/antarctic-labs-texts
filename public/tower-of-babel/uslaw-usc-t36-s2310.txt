36 U.S.C. § 2310 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:36:2310
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section2310
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (United States Government Organizations Involved With Observances And Ceremonies); Chapter 23 (United States Holocaust Memorial Museum). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To carry out the purposes of this chapter, there are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary. Notwithstanding any other provision of law, none of the funds authorized to carry out this chapter may be made available for construction. Authority to enter into contracts and to make payments under this chapter, using funds authorized to be appropriated under this chapter, shall be effective only to the extent, and in such amounts, as provided in advance in appropriations Acts.
