21 C.F.R. § 868.5330 — Breathing gas mixer.
Citation: us:cfr:21:868.5330
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-868.5330
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 868 (Anesthesiology Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. A breathing gas mixer is a device intended for use in conjunction with a respiratory support apparatus to control the mixing of gases that are to be breathed by a patient.
(b) Classification. Class II (performance standards).
