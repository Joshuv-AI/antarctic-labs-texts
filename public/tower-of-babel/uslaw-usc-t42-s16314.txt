42 U.S.C. § 16314 — Hydrogen

Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 149 (National Energy Policy And Programs); Subchapter IX (Research And Development); Part G (Science). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The Secretary shall conduct a program of fundamental research and development in support of programs authorized under subchapter VIII.
(b) Methods
The program shall include support for methods of generating hydrogen without the use of natural gas.
