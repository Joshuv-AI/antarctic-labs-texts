25 C.F.R. § 83.44 — Is the Assistant Secretary's final determination final for the Department?
Citation: us:cfr:25:83.44
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-83.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 83 (Procedures For Federal Acknowledgment Of Indian Tribes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. The AS-IA's final determination is final for the Department and is a final agency action under the Administrative Procedure Act (5 U.S.C. 704).
