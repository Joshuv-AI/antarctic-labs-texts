DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS


Advisory Committee on the Readjustment of Veterans, Notice of 
Meeting Cancellation

    The Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) gives notice under the 
Federal Advisory Committee Act, 5 U.S.C. Ch. 10, that the Advisory 
Committee on the Readjustment of Veterans, previously scheduled to be 
held on October 21 and October 22, 2025, has been cancelled due to a 
lapse in appropriations.
    For more information, please contact Mr. Joshua Mathis, Designated 
Federal Officer, at 313-310-3891 or via email at [email protected].

    Dated: October 15, 2025.
Taylor N. Mattson,
Alternate Federal Register Liaison Officer, Department of Veterans 
Affairs.
