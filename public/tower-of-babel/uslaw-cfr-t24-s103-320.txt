24 C.F.R. § 103.320 — Provisions sought for the public interest.
Citation: us:cfr:24:103.320
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Equal Opportunity, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-103.320
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 103 (Fair Housing—Complaint Processing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following are types of provisions may be sought for the vindication of the public interest:
(a) Elimination of discriminatory housing practices.
(b) Prevention of future discriminatory housing practices.
(c) Remedial affirmative activities to overcome discriminatory housing practices.
(d) Reporting requirements.
(e) Monitoring and enforcement activities.
