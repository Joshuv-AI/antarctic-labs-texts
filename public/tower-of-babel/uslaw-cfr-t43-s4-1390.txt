43 C.F.R. § 4.1390 — Scope.

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4.1390
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4 (Department Of The Interior Hearings And Appeals Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Sections 4.1391 through 4.1394 set forth the procedures for obtaining review of an OSM determination under 30 CFR 761.16 that a person does or does not have valid existing rights.
