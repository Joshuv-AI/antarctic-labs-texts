22 U.S.C. § 282n — Authority to agree to amendments to Articles of Agreement
Citation: us:usc:22:282n
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section282n
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter XI (International Finance Corporation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States Governor of the Corporation is authorized to agree to amendments to the Articles of Agreement of the Corporation that would—
(1)
amend Article II, Section 2(c)(ii), to increase the vote by which the Board of Governors of the Corporation may increase the capital stock of the Corporation from a three-fourths majority to a four-fifths majority; and
(2)
amend Article VII(a) to increase the vote by which the Board of Governors of the Corporation may amend the Articles of Agreement of the Corporation from a four-fifths majority to an eighty-five percent majority.
