15 U.S.C. § 1196 — Penalties
Citation: us:usc:15:1196
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1196
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 25 (Flammable Fabrics). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Violation of section 1192 or 1197(b) of this title, or failure to comply with section 1202(c) of this title, is punishable by—
(1)
imprisonment for not more than 5 years for a knowing and willful violation of that section;
(2)
a fine determined under section 3571 of title 18; or
(3)
both.
