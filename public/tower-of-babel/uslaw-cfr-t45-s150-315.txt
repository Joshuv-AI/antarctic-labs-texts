45 C.F.R. § 150.315 — Amount of penalty—General.
Citation: us:cfr:45:150.315
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-150.315
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 150 (Cms Enforcement In Group And Individual Insurance Markets). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A civil money penalty for each violation of 42 U.S.C. 300gg et seq. may not exceed $100 as adjusted annually under 45 CFR part 102 for each day, for each responsible entity, for each individual affected by the violation. Penalties imposed under this part are in addition to any other penalties prescribed or allowed by law.
