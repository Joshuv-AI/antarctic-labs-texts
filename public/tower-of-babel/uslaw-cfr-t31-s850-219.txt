31 C.F.R. § 850.219 — Parent.
Citation: us:cfr:31:850.219
Creator: Office Of Investment Security, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-850.219
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 850 (Provisions Pertaining To U.S. Investments In Certain National Security Technologies And Products In Countries Of Concern). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term parent means, with respect to an entity:
(a) A person who or which directly or indirectly holds more than 50 percent of:
(1) The outstanding voting interest in the entity; or
(2) The voting power of the board of the entity;
(b) The general partner, managing member, or equivalent of the entity; or
(c) The investment adviser to any entity that is a pooled investment fund, with “investment adviser” as defined in the Investment Advisers Act of 1940 (15 U.S.C. 80b-2(a)(11)).
