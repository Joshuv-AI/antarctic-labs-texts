Kundan Suxena, Indibar Debnath, Om Prakash

Abstract
For a prime p p , let F q F_{q} be the finite field of order q = p d q=p^{d} . This paper presents the study on skew generalized quasi-cyclic (SGQC) codes of length n n over the non-chain ring F q + v ​ F q F_{q}+vF_{q} where v 2 = v v^{2}=v and θ t \theta_{t} is the Galois automorphism. Here, first, we prove the dual of an SGQC code of length n n is also an SGQC code of the same length and derive a necessary and sufficient condition for the existence of a self-dual SGQC code. Then, we discuss the 1 1 -generator polynomial and the ρ \rho -generator polynomial for skew generalized quasi-cyclic codes. Further, we determine the dimension and BCH type bound for the 1-generator skew generalized quasi-cyclic codes. As a by-product, with the help of MAGMA software, we provide a few examples of SGQC codes and obtain some 2 2 -generator SGQC codes of index 2 2 . Keywords. Skew cyclic codes , Skew generalized quasi-cyclic codes, Gray map , Generator polynomial , Idempotent generator. 2010 AMS Classification. 94B05, 94B15, 94B60.

Suppose
\mathsf{C}
is a skew generalized quasi-cyclic code of block length
(t_{1},t_{2},\dots,t_{l})
and length
N
with index
l
. Let
a(x)=(a_{1}(x)+va^{\prime}_{1}(x),\dots,a_{l}(x)+va^{\prime}_{l}(x))

\displaystyle K_{i}=\begin{Bmatrix}p_{i}(x):\text{a codeword}~~a(x)=(a_{1}(x)+va{\prime}_{1}(x),a_{2}(x)+va{\prime}_{2}(x),\dots,p_{i}(x),0,0,\dots,0)\in\mathsf{C},\\
\text{where}~p_{i}(x)\in\dfrac{S[x;\theta_{t}]}{\langle x^{t_{i}}-1\rangle}~\text{and}~a_{i+1}(x)+va{\prime}_{i+1}(x)=a_{i+2}(x)+va{\prime}_{i+2}(x)=\dots=0\end{Bmatrix},

i.e.,

\displaystyle K_{1}

\displaystyle=\{p_{1}(x):\text{ a codeword}~a(x)=(p_{1}(x),0,0,\dots,0)\in\mathsf{C}\},

\displaystyle K_{2}

\displaystyle=\{p_{2}(x):\text{ a codeword}~~a(x)=(a_{1}(x)+va^{\prime}_{1}(x),p_{2}(x),0,0,\dots,0)\in\mathsf{C}\}~\text{and}

\displaystyle K_{l}

\displaystyle=\{p_{l}(x):\text{a codeword}~~a(x)=(a_{1}(x)+va^{\prime}_{1}(x),a_{2}(x)+va^{\prime}_{2}(x),\dots,p_{l}(x))\in\mathsf{C}\}.

As
(0,0,\dots,0)\in\mathsf{C},~K_{i}
is a non-empty set
\text{ for all }i=1,2,\dots,l
.

Lemma 5.27
.

The above set
K_{i}
is a left submodule of
\dfrac{S[x;\theta_{t}]}{\langle x^{t_{i}}-1\rangle}

\text{ for all }i=1,2,\dots,l
.

Proof 5.28
.

Suppose
p_{i}(x),q_{i}(x)\in K_{i}
and
s(x)\in S[x;\theta_{t}]
, then there are

\displaystyle a(x)

\displaystyle=(a_{1}(x)+va^{\prime}_{1}(x),a_{2}(x)+va^{\prime}_{2}(x),\dots,p_{i}(x),0,\dots,0)~\text{and}

\displaystyle~b(x)

\displaystyle=(b_{1}(x)+vb^{\prime}_{1}(x),b_{2}(x)+vb^{\prime}_{2}(x),\dots,q_{i}(x),0,\dots,0)\in\mathsf{C}.

As
\mathsf{C}
is a left submodule of
S_{t_{1}}\times S_{t_{2}}\times\dots\times S_{t_{l}}
,
a(x)+b(x)=(a_{1}(x)+va^{\prime}_{1}(x)+(b_{1}(x)+vb^{\prime}_{1}((x)),a_{2}(x)+va^{\prime}_{2}(x)+(b_{2}(x)+vb^{\prime}_{2}((x),\dots,p_{i}(x)+q_{i}(x),0,0,\dots,0)
, and
s(x)a(x)=(s(x)(a_{1}(x)+va^{\prime}_{1}(x)),s(x)(a_{2}(x)+va^{\prime}_{2}(x)),\dots,s(x)p_{i}(x),0,0,\dots,0)
are codewords in
\mathsf{C}
. Thus,
p_{i}(x)+q_{i}(x)
and
s(x)p_{i}(x)
are elements in
K_{i}
, which implies that
K_{i}
is a left submodule of
\dfrac{S[x;\theta_{t}]}{\langle x^{t_{i}}-1\rangle}
. ∎

Note:
From Corollary
2.7
, each

is principally generated, i.e.,

where

is a right divisor of

.

Let
\mathsf{C}
be an SGQC code of block length

and length
N
with index
l

\displaystyle L

\displaystyle=\begin{Bmatrix}(h_{(}x),h_{2}(x),\dots,h_{l-1}(x):~\text{a codeword}~(h_{1}(x),h_{2}(x),\dots,h_{l-1}(x),h_{l}(x))\in\mathsf{C}\end{Bmatrix}\text{and}~

\displaystyle M

\displaystyle=\begin{Bmatrix}(h_{2}(x),\dots,h_{l}(x):~\text{a codeword}~(h_{1}(x),h_{2}(x),\dots,h_{l-1}(x),h_{l}(x))\in\mathsf{C},\\
~\text{where}~h_{i}(x)=h_{i}(x)+vh^{\prime}_{i}(x)\end{Bmatrix}

Proof 5.30

The proof is almost similar to the proof of Lemma

Theorem 5.31
.

Let
\mathsf{C}

\displaystyle\mathsf{C}=

\displaystyle\left\langle\begin{matrix}((1-v)f_{1}(x)+vf^{\prime}_{1}(x),0,\dots,0),(p_{21}(x),(1-v)f_{2}(x)+vf^{\prime}_{2}(x),0,\dots,0),(p_{31}(x),p_{32}(x),\\

Proof 5.32
.

We will prove it inductively. Suppose the index

i<l

\mathsf{C}
be an SGQC code of length
N
with index
l
and
a(x)=(a_{1}(x)+va^{\prime}_{1}(x),a_{2}(x)+va^{\prime}_{2}(x),\dots,a_{l}(x)+va^{\prime}_{l}(x))\in\mathsf{C}

. From the definition of set

, there is a codeword
(p_{l1}(x),p_{l2}(x),\dots,p_{l(l-1)}(x),f_{t_{l}}(x))\in\mathsf{C}
. Thus,

\displaystyle a(x)

\displaystyle=\Bigl(a_{1}(x)+va^{\prime}_{1}(x),a_{2}(x)+va^{\prime}_{2}(x),\dots,q_{t_{l}}(x)f_{t_{l}}(x)\Bigl)

Since

\displaystyle\begin{pmatrix}q_{t_{l}}(x)(p_{l1}(x),p_{l2}(x),\dots,p_{l(l-1)}(x),f_{t_{l}}(x))\end{pmatrix}\in\mathsf{C},and

\displaystyle\begin{pmatrix}a_{1}(x)+va^{\prime}_{1}(x)-q_{t_{l}}(x)p_{l1}(x),a_{2}(x)+va^{\prime}_{2}(x)-q_{t_{l}}(x)p_{l2}(x),\dots,\\

By Lemma
5.29

(a_{1}(x)+va^{\prime}_{1}(x)-q_{t_{l}}(x)p_{l1}(x),a_{2}(x)+va^{\prime}_{2}(x)-q_{t_{l}}(x)p_{l2}(x)
,
\dots,a_{l-1}(x)+va^{\prime}_{l-1}(x)-q_{t_{l}}(x)p_{l(l-1)}(x))\in L

is a left-submodule of

and by the inductive hypothesis, we have

\displaystyle L=\left\langle\begin{matrix}((1-v)f_{1}(x)+vf^{\prime}_{1}(x),0,\dots,0),(p_{21}(x),(1-v)f_{2}(x)+vf^{\prime}_{2}(x),0,\dots,0),(p_{31}(x),p_{32}(x),\\

where

is a right divisor of
x^{t_{i}}-1
,
\text{ for all }i=1,2,\dots,l-1

(a_{1}(x)+va^{\prime}_{1}(x)-q_{t_{l}}(x)p_{l1}(x),a_{2}(x)+va^{\prime}_{2}(x)-q_{t_{l}}(x)p_{l2}(x),\dots,a_{l-1}(x)+va^{\prime}_{l-1}(x)-q_{t_{l}}(x)p_{l(l-1)}(x))

,

\displaystyle a(x)

\displaystyle=(a_{1}(x)+va^{\prime}_{1}(x),a_{2}(x)+va^{\prime}_{2}(x),\dots,q_{t_{l}}(x)f_{t_{l}}(x))

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\begin{pmatrix}(q_{t_{l}}(x)(p_{l1}(x),p_{l2}(x),\dots,p_{l(l-1)}(x),f_{t_{l}}(x))+(a_{1}(x)+va^{\prime}_{1}(x)-q_{t_{l}}(x)p_{l1}(x),\\

+\dots+c_{l-1}(x)(p_{(l-1)1}(x),p_{(l-1)2}(x),\dots,p_{(l-1)(l-2)}(x),f_{t_{l-1}}(x))\end{pmatrix}.

Therefore,

\displaystyle\mathsf{C}

\displaystyle=\left\langle\begin{matrix}((1-v)f_{1}(x)+vf^{\prime}_{1}(x),0,\dots,0),(p_{21}(x),(1-v)f_{2}(x)+vf^{\prime}_{2}(x),0,\dots,0),(p_{31}(x),p_{32}(x),\\

\text{ for all }i=1,2,\dots,l

.

Let
\mathsf{C}
be an SGQC code of block length

6
Conclusion

This work studies the structure of skew generalized quasi-cyclic codes over
S
without any restriction on length. It derives
1
-generator and multi-generator polynomial codes along with their corresponding dimensions. Furthermore, it examines the
1
-generator idempotent polynomial over
F_{q}+vF_{q}
and
S
, provides examples, and derives linear codes. Moreover, the study establishes a lower bound on the minimum Hamming distance for the
1
-generator skew generalized quasi-cyclic codes.

In the future, one may determine the minimum distances and generator polynomials of
\mathsf{C}^{\perp}
in terms of the generator polynomial of
\mathsf{C}
for the
\rho
-generator polynomial codes. Furthermore, the construction of quantum codes based on these codes is a promising area of investigation.
