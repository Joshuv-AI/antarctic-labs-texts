45 C.F.R. § 2500.3 — Purpose and mission.
Citation: us:cfr:45:2500.3
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2500.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2500 (Americorps Statement Of Organization, Agency Operating Name, And Logos). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

AmeriCorps' purpose is to administer the programs established under the national service laws. AmeriCorps' mission is to improve lives, strengthen communities, and foster civic engagement through service and volunteering.
