54 U.S.C. § 100725 — Donations
Citation: us:usc:54:100725
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section100725
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 1007 (Resource Management); Subchapter II (System Unit Resource Protection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may accept donations of money or services for expenditure or employment to meet expected, immediate, or ongoing response costs. The donations may be expended or employed at any time after their acceptance, without further Congressional action.
