2 U.S.C. § 23 — Presiding officer of Senate may administer oaths
Citation: us:usc:2:23
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section23
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 2 (Organization Of Congress). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The presiding officer, for the time being, of the Senate of the United States, shall have power to administer all oaths and affirmations that are or may be required by the Constitution, or by law, to be taken by any Senator, officer of the Senate, witness, or other person, in respect to any matter within the jurisdiction of the Senate.
