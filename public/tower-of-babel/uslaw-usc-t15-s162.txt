15 U.S.C. § 162 — Creation of China corporations restricted
Citation: us:usc:15:162
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section162
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 4 (China Trade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No corporation for the purpose of engaging in business within China shall be created under any law of the United States other than this chapter.
