20 C.F.R. § 401.195 — Situations not specified in this part.
Citation: us:cfr:20:401.195
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-401.195
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 401 (Privacy And Disclosure Of Official Records And Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If no other provision in this part specifically allows SSA to disclose information, the Commissioner or designee may disclose this information if not prohibited by Federal law. For example, the Commissioner or designee may disclose information necessary to respond to life threatening situations.
