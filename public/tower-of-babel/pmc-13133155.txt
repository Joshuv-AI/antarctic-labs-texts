Correction: Luteoloside alleviates bleomycin-induced pulmonary fibrosis in mice via SIRT1-mediated protective effect against alveolar epithelial cell senescence
Authors: Lilan Deng, Biqiang Sun, Jiangnan Xia, Wei Zhuo, Meixiang Jiang, Sheng Yin, Feng Yang
Published: 2026

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-21752-6, published online 29 October 2025

The original version of this Article contained errors in Figure 3. Specifically Figure 3A where the representative image for the BLM+30 mg/kg group was inadvertently selected from a preliminary optimization batch with inconsistent acquisition settings (e.g., exposure time and gain). This did not accurately reflect the standardized experimental conditions of the final validated dataset. The original Figure 3 appears below.

The original Article has been corrected.Fig. 3Luteoloside inhibits bleomycin-induced pulmonary senescence in mice. (A) Immunofluorescence staining to assess AECs senescence in mouse lung tissue, blue represents DAPI, green represents SPC, red represents P16, scale bar represents 100 μm. (B–D) Western blotting analysis of protein levels of P53 and P21 in mouse lung tissue homogenates. Data represents standard deviation, with each experiment independently repeated at least three times. (*p < 0.05, **p < 0.01, and ***p < 0.001) (n = 6).
