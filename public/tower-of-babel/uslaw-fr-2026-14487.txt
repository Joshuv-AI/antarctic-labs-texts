Northern Illinois Hydropower, LLC; Notice of Availability of 
Environmental Assessment

    The EA contains Commission staff's analysis of the potential 
environmental effects of the proposed amendment, alternatives to the 
proposed action, and concludes that the proposed amendment of the 
Dresden Island Lock and Dam Project would not constitute a major 
federal action that would significantly affect the quality of the human 
environment.
