49 U.S.C. § 24901 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:49:24901
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section24901
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part C (Passenger Transportation); Chapter 249 (Northeast Corridor Improvement Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter—
(1)
“final system plan” means the final system plan (including additions) adopted by the United States Railway Association under the Regional Rail Reorganization Act of 1973 (45 U.S.C. 701 et seq.).
(2)
“rail carrier” means an express carrier and a rail carrier as defined in section 10102 of this title, including Amtrak.
