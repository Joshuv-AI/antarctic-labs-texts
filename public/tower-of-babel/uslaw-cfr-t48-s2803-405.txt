48 C.F.R. § 2803.405 — 2803.405 Misrepresentations or violations of the Covenant Against Contingent Fees.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2803.405
Creator: Chapter 28—Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2803.405
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2803 (Improper Business Practices And Personal Conflicts Of Interest). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees who suspect, or have evidence of, violations by a contractor of the Covenant Against Contingent Fees, see FAR subpart 3.4, must report the matter to the contracting officer or appropriate higher authority, in accordance with agency procedures. Employees who suspect or have evidence of fraudulent or criminal activities must report the matter to the SPE and the OIG.
