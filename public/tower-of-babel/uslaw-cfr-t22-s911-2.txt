22 C.F.R. § 911.2 — Filing complaint.
Citation: us:cfr:22:911.2
Creator: Foreign Service Grievance Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-911.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 911 (Implementation Disputes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the dispute is not satisfactorily resolved at the agency level, the moving party may file a complaint within 45 calendar days from the date of the response (or in any case must file within 90 days of filing the implementation dispute) with the Board in writing and with specificity as to the nature of the violation.
