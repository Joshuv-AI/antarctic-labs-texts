10 C.F.R. § 709.25 — Limits on use of polygraph examination results that reflect “Significant Response” or “No Opinion”.
Citation: us:cfr:10:709.25
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-709.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 709 (Counterintelligence Evaluation Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

DOE or its contractors may not:
(a) Take an adverse personnel action against a covered person or make an adverse access recommendation solely on the basis of a polygraph examination result of “significant response” or “no opinion”; or
(b) Use a polygraph examination that reflects “significant response” or “no opinion” as a substitute for any other required investigation.
