17 C.F.R. § 10.25 — Form of pleadings.
Citation: us:cfr:17:10.25
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-10.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 10 (Rules Of Practice). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All averments of claim and defense shall be made in consecutively numbered paragraphs. The contents of each paragraph shall be limited as far as practicable to a single set of circumstances.
