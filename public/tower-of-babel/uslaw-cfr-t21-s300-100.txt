21 C.F.R. § 300.100 — Chlorofluorocarbon propellants.
Citation: us:cfr:21:300.100
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-300.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 300 (General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The use of chlorofluorocarbons in human drugs as propellants in self-pressurized containers is generally prohibited except as provided by § 2.125 of this chapter.
