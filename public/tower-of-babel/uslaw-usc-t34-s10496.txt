34 U.S.C. § 10496 — Discretionary research grants
Citation: us:usc:34:10496
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section10496
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 101 (Justice System Improvement); Subchapter XXII (Support For Law Enforcement Officers And Families). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General may reserve 10 percent of funds to award research grants to a State or local law enforcement agency or organization to study issues of importance in the law enforcement field as determined by the Attorney General.
