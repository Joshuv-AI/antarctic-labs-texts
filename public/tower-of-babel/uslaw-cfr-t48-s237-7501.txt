48 C.F.R. § 237.7501 — 237.7501 Definition.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 237 (Service Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Facilities project, as used in this subpart, means a Government project to provide, modernize, or replace real property for use by a contractor in performing a Government contract or subcontract.
