AI-powered epidemic control: Deepseek’s role in global health resilience
Authors: Hong Jiang, Meixian Wu, Juan Yu, Ying Xiao, Chengliang Yin
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
DeepSeek, an AI-driven platform, revolutionizes epidemic management through early warning systems, trend prediction, treatment optimization, and public consultation. By integrating multi-source real-time data (epidemiological, social media, mobility) with deep learning models (LSTM, Transformers) and explainable decision frameworks,, it enhances public health responsiveness. Challenges include data privacy and model accuracy, but its potential to fortify global health resilience is transformative.

Introduction

Epidemic outbreaks have long posed critical challenges to global public health. The recent advancements in artificial intelligence (AI), particularly in deep learning, have introduced transformative tools for pandemic management. However, traditional approaches—like manual contact tracing and static SEIR models—faced critical delays during COVID-19, with hotspot identification averaging 21 days (WHO, 2023). Among these innovations, DeepSeek—a high-efficiency intelligent platform—has emerged as a pivotal solution. By integrating real-time mobility, climatic, and social data, DeepSeek reduces response latency to < 72 h while offering open-source, interpretable analytics—bridging gaps in timeliness and accessibility for resource-limited settings. By enabling early warning systems, precise trend forecasting, treatment optimization, and public consultation services, DeepSeek significantly enhances the responsiveness of public health systems. This article examines DeepSeek’s technical foundations, applications in epidemic control, and future potential, offering insights into its role in safeguarding global health security (Fig. 1).

Overview of deepseek technology

DeepSeek is an integrated AI platform leveraging deep learning to analyze multidimensional datasets, including epidemiological records, social media trends, meteorological updates, and population mobility patterns. Its architecture is built on advanced neural networks to identify hidden data correlations within complex data, thereby driving actionable insights through a structured four-layer framework:

Data Acquisition Layer: Gathers real-time information from diverse sources via APIs, web scraping, and IoT devices, ensuring a comprehensive and up-to-date dataset.Preprocessing Layer: Cleans, standardizes, and extracts features from raw data to enhance quality. This step is crucial for preparing the data for subsequent analysis.Model Training Layer: Utilizes algorithms such as Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM), Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), and Transformer models for predictive analytics. These algorithms are specifically chosen for their ability to handle complex temporal and spatial data, making them ideal for epidemic forecasting and trend analysis.Decision Support Layer: The insights generated by the model training layer are translated into interpretable reports and visualizations, designed to support policymakers and public health officials in making informed decisions.

Applications in epidemic prevention and control

Early warning systems

Early warning systems

Early warning systems

DeepSeek’s integration of global surveillance data with environmental variables, enabling it to forecast outbreak hotspots and timelines. By correlating historical trends with climatic shifts, the platform can issue alerts weeks in advance, empowering governments to mobilize preemptive measures [2, 3]. Additionally, DeepSeek’s analysis of social media sentiment detects emerging public concerns, guiding targeted communication strategies [4]. This combination of data-driven insights and real-time monitoring positions DeepSeek as a critical tool for effective epidemic response and public health management.

Epidemic trend prediction

Traditional models often struggle with the dynamic and unpredictable nature of disease spread. DeepSeek overcomes these limitations by integrating outbreak trend forecasting, disease spread mapping, and variables such as viral mutations and vaccination rates. These advanced techniques predictions enable efficient resource allocation and informed policy decisions, mitigating socio-economic disruptions. This capability highlights the platform’s role in enhancing the resilience of public health systems and minimizing the broader impacts of disease outbreaks.

3.Treatment optimization

3.Treatment optimization

Treatment optimization

DeepSeek supports personalized medicine by analyzing clinical data, genetic profiles, and drug interactions. For example, in the case of rare genetic disorders where standard treatments are ineffective, DeepSeek can analyze the patient’s genetic makeup and cross-reference it with global drug databases to identify off-label or experimental therapies targeting specific mutations. In cases lacking established therapies, DeepSeek identifies potential treatments from existing databases, offering novel avenues for patient care. As Mikhail stated that DeepSeek-R1 achieved 82% accuracy in ophthalmology cases, with 15-fold lower cost than OpenAI’s o1 model. It showed the the effectiveness and cost advantages of DeepSeek-R1 in specialized medical fields (e.g., ophthalmology, pediatrics) [5].

4.Public consultation services

4.Public consultation services

Public consultation services

During crises, DeepSeek’s natural language processing (NLP) capabilities provide real-time answers to public inquiries on prevention, mental health, and vaccinations. Tailored recommendations based on user behavior empower individuals to adopt protective measures. By addressing public concerns promptly and accurately, DeepSeek fosters trust and facilitates effective public engagement in epidemic control efforts. DeepSeek’s public consultation module processes multilingual queries through language-agnostic embeddings and specialized submodels (e.g., Chinese/English MoE experts) trained on localized medical corpora like WHO guidelines, enabling accurate intent recognition across 48 + languages. To mitigate misinformation risks, it employs a dual safety protocol: (1) Real-time claim screening against verified databases (e.g., WHO Mythbusters) using semantic similarity thresholds; (2) Evidence-grounded response generation constrained to peer-reviewed sources, with uncertainty disclaimers for low-confidence outputs.

Challenges and future directions

Despite its significant potential in epidemic prevention and control, DeepSeek confronts several notable challenges, including the need for stringent data privacy measures to protect sensitive health information, continuous model updates to maintain accuracy amid evolving datasets, and effective interdisciplinary collaboration between epidemiologists, data scientists, and policymakers to ensure seamless deployment. Looking ahead, advancements in explainable AI are poised to enhance transparency in model outputs, fostering public trust and acceptance, while the establishment of robust ethical frameworks will be essential to help ensure fairness and mitigate bias in algorithmic applications [6]. Furthermore, deeper integration with healthcare systems, particularly in telemedicine and AI-assisted diagnostics, could expand DeepSeek’s impact, positioning it as a more comprehensive and versatile tool in global health management. Public health officials report 40% faster outbreak response using DeepSeek, clinicians value its 87% diagnostic accuracy in pediatrics but emphasize human oversight, and public surveys show 78% trust when sources are cited. For privacy, on-premise deployment and federated learning ensure GDPR/HIPAA compliance, while algorithmic audits mitigate social media bias risks [7].

Conclusion

DeepSeek exemplifies the transformative potential of AI in public health, offering scalable and innovative solutions for epidemic management. Unlike proprietary systems constrained by centralized infrastructures, DeepSeek’s open-source architecture enables transparent, adaptable deployment across diverse healthcare settings. Its emphasis on explainable reasoning and local customization provides a critical alternative to “black-box” tools that limit clinical trust and institutional autonomy. However, its development must prioritize human-centric values to avoid exacerbating existing healthcare disparities. By embracing ongoing technological innovation and ethical stewardship, DeepSeek can fortify global health resilience, underscoring technology’s role in protecting human well-being. The future of epidemic management lies in the harmonious convergence of advanced technology and human values, and DeepSeek stands as a testament to this vision.
