45 C.F.R. § 1156.18 — Prohibition against intimidation or retaliation.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1156.18
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1156.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1156 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Age). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A recipient may not engage in acts of intimidation or retaliation against any person who:
(a) Attempts to assert a right protected by the Act; or
(b) Cooperates in any mediation, investigation, hearing, or other part of the Endowment's investigation, conciliation and enforcement process.
