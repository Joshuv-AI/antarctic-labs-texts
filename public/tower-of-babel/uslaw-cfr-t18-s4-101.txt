18 C.F.R. § 4.101 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:18:4.101
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-4.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 4 (Licenses, Permits, Exemptions, And Determination Of Project Costs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart provides procedures for exemption on a case-specific basis from all or part of Part I of the Federal Power Act (Act), including licensing, for small hydroelectric power projects as defined in § 4.30(b)(31).
