43 C.F.R. § 3835.91 — What if I fail to file annual FLPMA documents?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3835.91
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3835.91
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3835 (Waivers From Annual Maintenance Fees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you fail to file an annual FLPMA document by December 30, as required in § 3835.31(d), you forfeit the affected mining claims or sites.
