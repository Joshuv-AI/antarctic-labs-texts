40 C.F.R. § 60.720a — a Applicability and designation of affected facility.
Citation: us:cfr:40:60.720a
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.720a
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 60 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The provisions of this subpart apply to each spray booth in which plastic parts for use in the manufacture of business machines receive prime coats, color coats, texture coats, or touch-up coats.
(b) This subpart applies to any affected facility for which construction, modification, or reconstruction begins after June 21, 2022.
