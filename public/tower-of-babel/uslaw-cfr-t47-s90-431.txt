47 C.F.R. § 90.431 — Unattended operation.
Citation: us:cfr:47:90.431
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-90.431
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 90 (Private Land Mobile Radio Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person is required to be in attendance at a station when transmitting during normal rendition of service and when either:
(a) Transmitting for telemetering purposes; or,
(b) Retransmitting by self-actuating means a radio signal received from another radio station or stations.
