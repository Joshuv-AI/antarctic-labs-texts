29 C.F.R. § 1601.8 — Where to make a charge.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1601.8
Creator: Equal Employment Opportunity Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1601.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1601 (Procedural Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A charge may be made using the EEOC's designated digital systems, in person, by facsimile, or by mail to any EEOC office or to any designated representative of the Commission. The addresses of the EEOC's offices appear at www.eeoc.gov.
