43 U.S.C. § 14 — Returns relative to lands
Citation: us:usc:43:14
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section14
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 1 (Bureau Of Land Management). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All returns relative to the public lands shall be made to the Secretary of the Interior or such officer as he may designate.
