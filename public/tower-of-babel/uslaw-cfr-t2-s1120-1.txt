2 C.F.R. § 1120.1 — Purpose of this part.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1120.1
Creator: Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1120.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1120 (Award Format For Dod Grants And Cooperative Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part of the DoD Grant and Agreement Regulations (DoDGARs) establishes a standard award format for DoD Components' grants and cooperative agreements. It thereby makes the content easier for a recipient to locate in different DoD Components' awards.
