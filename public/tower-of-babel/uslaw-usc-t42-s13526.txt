42 U.S.C. § 13526 — Uncosted obligations
Citation: us:usc:42:13526
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section13526
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 134 (Energy Policy); Subchapter XI (Policy And Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Report
Along with the submission of each of the President’s annual budget requests to Congress, the Secretary shall submit to Congress a report which—
(1)
identifies the amount of Department of Energy funds that were, as of the end of the previous fiscal year—
(A)
committed uncosted obligations; and
(B)
uncommitted uncosted obligations;
(2)
specifically describes the purposes for which all such funds are intended; and
(3)
explains the effect that information contained in the report has had on the annual budget request for the Department of Energy being simultaneously submitted.
(b) Definitions
Within 90 days after October 24, 1992, the Secretary shall submit a report to the Congress containing definitions of the terms “uncosted obligation”, “committed uncosted obligation”, and “uncommitted uncosted obligation” for purposes of reports to be submitted under subsection (a).
