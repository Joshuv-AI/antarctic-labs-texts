47 C.F.R. § 90.138 — Applications for itinerant frequencies.
Citation: us:cfr:47:90.138
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-90.138
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 90 (Private Land Mobile Radio Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An application for authority to conduct an itinerant operation in the Industrial/Business Pool must be restricted to use of itinerant frequencies or other frequencies not designated for permanent use and need not be accompanied by evidence of frequency coordination. Users should be aware that no interference protection is provided from other itinerant operations.
