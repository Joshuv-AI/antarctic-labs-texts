Black River Limited Partnership Notice of Availability of 
Environmental Assessment

    The EA contains Commission staff's analysis of the potential 
environmental effects of the proposed amendment, alternatives to the 
proposed action, and concludes that the proposed amendment, would not 
constitute a major federal action that would affect the quality of the 
human environment.
