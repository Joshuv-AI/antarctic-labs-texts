21 C.F.R. § 507.125 — Determining appropriate supplier verification activities (including determining the frequency of conducting the activity).
Citation: us:cfr:21:507.125
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-507.125
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 507 (Current Good Manufacturing Practice, Hazard Analysis, And Risk-Based Preventive Controls For Food For Animals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).
