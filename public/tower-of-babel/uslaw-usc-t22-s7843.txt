22 U.S.C. § 7843 — Facilitating submission of applications for admission as a refugee
Citation: us:usc:22:7843
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section7843
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 85 (North Korean Human Rights); Subchapter III (Protecting North Korean Refugees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of State shall undertake to facilitate the submission of applications under section 1157 of title 8 by citizens of North Korea seeking protection as refugees (as defined in section 1101(a)(42) of title 8).
