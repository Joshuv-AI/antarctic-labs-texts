42 U.S.C. § 3172 — Termination or modification of economic development districts
Citation: us:usc:42:3172
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section3172
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 38 (Public Works And Economic Development); Subchapter IV (Economic Development Districts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall, by regulation, promulgate standards for the termination or modification of the designation of economic development districts.
