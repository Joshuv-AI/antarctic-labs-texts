5 C.F.R. § 214.403 — Change of position type.
Citation: us:cfr:5:214.403
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-214.403
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 214 (Senior Executive Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An agency may not change the designation of an established position from career reserved to general, or from general to career reserved, without the prior approval of the Office of Personnel Management.
