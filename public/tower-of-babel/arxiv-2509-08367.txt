\alpha(G)=\alpha(G^{\prime})+1
.

It follows
f_{G^{\prime}}(x)-f_{G}(x)\geq c_{1}-\frac{4}{7}
and

\alpha(G)=\alpha(G^{\prime})+1\geq\sum\limits_{v\in V(G^{\prime})}f_{G^{\prime}}(v)-(2c_{1}-\frac{8}{7})+1

\geq\sum\limits_{v\in V(G)}f_{G}(v)-(2c_{1}-\frac{8}{7})-c_{1}-\frac{3}{7}+c_{1}-\frac{4}{7}+1=\sum\limits_{v\in V(G)}f_{G}(v)-(2c_{1}-\frac{8}{7})
.

Case 2.2
:
w\in V_{3}(G)
.

Let
N_{G}(w)=\{u,x_{1},x_{2}\}
and
G^{\prime}
be obtained from
G
by removing
\{u,w\}
and, if
x_{1}x_{2}\notin E(G)
, by adding the edge
x_{1}x_{2}
. It follows
|V(G^{\prime})|\geq 4
and that
G^{\prime}
is connected.

Note that a maximum independent set of
G^{\prime}
is an independent set of
G-\{u,w\}
, hence,
\alpha(G)\geq\alpha(G^{\prime})+1
.

It follows
f_{G^{\prime}}(x_{1})-f_{G}(x_{1})\geq 0
and
f_{G^{\prime}}(x_{2})-f_{G}(x_{2})\geq 0
and, by induction,

\alpha(G)\geq\alpha(G^{\prime})+1\geq\sum\limits_{v\in V(G^{\prime})}f_{G^{\prime}}(v)-(2c_{1}-\frac{8}{7})+1

\geq\sum\limits_{v\in V(G)}f_{G}(v)-(2c_{1}-\frac{8}{7})-c_{1}-(1-c_{1})+1=\sum\limits_{v\in V(G)}f_{G}(v)-(2c_{1}-\frac{8}{7})
.
∎
