45 C.F.R. § 144.214 — Notifications of noncompliance with reporting requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:45:144.214
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-144.214
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 144 (Requirements Relating To Health Insurance Coverage). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an insurer of a qualified long-term care insurance policy does not submit the required reports by the due dates specified in this subpart, the Secretary notifies the appropriate State insurance commissioner within 45 days after the deadline for submission of the information and data specified in § 144.208.
