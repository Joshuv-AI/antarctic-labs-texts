51 U.S.C. § 70101 — Recovery of fair value of placing Department of Defense payloads in orbit with space launch system
Citation: us:usc:51:70101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section70101
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 701 (Use Of Space Launch System Or Alternatives). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, or any interagency agreement, the Administrator shall charge such prices as are necessary to recover the fair value of placing Department of Defense payloads into orbit by means of the space launch system.
