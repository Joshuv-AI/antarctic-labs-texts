40 C.F.R. § 311.2 — Definition of employee.
Citation: us:cfr:40:311.2
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-311.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 311 (Worker Protection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employee in § 311.1 is defined as a compensated or non-compensated worker who is controlled directly by a State or local government, as contrasted to an independent contractor.
