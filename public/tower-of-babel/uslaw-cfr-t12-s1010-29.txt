12 C.F.R. § 1010.29 — Use of property report—misstatements, omissions, or representation of Bureau approval prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1010.29
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1010.29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1010 (Land Registration (Regulation J)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in these regulations shall be construed to authorize or approve the use of a property report containing any untrue statement of a material fact or omitting to state a material fact required to be stated therein. Nor shall anything in these regulations be construed to authorize or permit any representation that the Property Report is prepared or approved by the Director, ILSRP or the Bureau of Consumer Financial Protection.
