46 C.F.R. § 4.07-15 — -15 Recommendations, action on.
Citation: us:cfr:46:4.07-15
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-4.07-15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 4 (Marine Casualties And Investigations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where the recommendations of an investigating officer are such that their accomplishment is within the authority of the District Commander or any of the personnel under his command, immediate steps shall be taken to put them into effect and his forwarding endorsement shall so indicate.
