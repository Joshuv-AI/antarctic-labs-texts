46 U.S.C. § 12120 — Liquified gas tankers
Citation: us:usc:46:12120
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section12120
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part H (Identification Of Vessels); Chapter 121 (Documentation Of Vessels); Subchapter II (Endorsements And Special Documentation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any agreement with the United States Government, the Secretary may issue a certificate of documentation with a coastwise endorsement for a vessel to transport liquified natural gas or liquified petroleum gas to Puerto Rico from other ports in the United States, if the vessel—
(1)
is a foreign built vessel that was built before October 19, 1996; or
(2)
was documented under this chapter before that date, even if the vessel is placed under a foreign registry and subsequently redocumented under this chapter for operation under this section.
