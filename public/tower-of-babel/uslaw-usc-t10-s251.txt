10 U.S.C. § 251 — Federal aid for State governments
Citation: us:usc:10:251
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section251
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And General Military Powers); Chapter 13 (Insurrection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whenever there is an insurrection in any State against its government, the President may, upon the request of its legislature or of its governor if the legislature cannot be convened, call into Federal service such of the militia of the other States, in the number requested by that State, and use such of the armed forces, as he considers necessary to suppress the insurrection.
