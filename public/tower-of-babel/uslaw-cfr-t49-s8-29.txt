49 C.F.R. § 8.29 — Access by historical researchers and former Presidential appointees.
Citation: us:cfr:49:8.29
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-8.29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 8 (Classified Information: Classification/Declassification/Access). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Access to classified information may be granted to historical researchers and former Presidents and Vice-Presidents and their appointees as outlined in Executive Order 13526 or its successor order. The general guidelines for access to classified information are contained in Executive Order 12968.
