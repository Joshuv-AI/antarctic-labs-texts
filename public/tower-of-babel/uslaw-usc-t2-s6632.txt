2 U.S.C. § 6632 — Disposal of used or surplus automobiles and trucks by Sergeant at Arms and Doorkeeper of Senate; procedure; deposit of receipts
Citation: us:usc:2:6632
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section6632
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 65 (Senate Officers And Administration); Subchapter III (Sergeant At Arms And Doorkeeper Of Senate); Part B (General Powers And Duties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On and after October 1, 1982, the Sergeant at Arms and Doorkeeper of the Senate is authorized to dispose of used or surplus automobiles and trucks by trade-in or by sale through the General Services Administration. Receipts from the sale of such automobiles and trucks shall be deposited in the United States Treasury for credit to the appropriation for “Automobiles and Maintenance” under the heading “Contingent Expenses of the Senate”.
