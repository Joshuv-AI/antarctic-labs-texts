21 C.F.R. § 822.27 — If I go out of business, what must I do?
Citation: us:cfr:21:822.27
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-822.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 822 (Postmarket Surveillance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must notify us within 30 days of the date of your decision to close your business. You should provide the expected date of closure and discuss your plans to complete or terminate postmarket surveillance of your device. You must also identify who will retain the records related to the surveillance (described in subpart G of this part) and where the records will be kept.
