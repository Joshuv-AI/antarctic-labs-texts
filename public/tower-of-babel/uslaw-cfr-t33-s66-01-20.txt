33 C.F.R. § 66.01-20 — -20 Inspection.
Citation: us:cfr:33:66.01-20
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-66.01-20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 66 (Private Aids To Navigation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All classes of private aids to navigation shall be maintained in proper operating condition. They are subject to inspection by the Coast Guard at any time and without prior notice.
