12 C.F.R. § 345.45 — Publication of planned examination schedule.
Citation: us:cfr:12:345.45
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-345.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 345 (Community Reinvestment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The FDIC publishes on its public website, at least 30 days in advance of the beginning of each calendar quarter, a list of banks scheduled for CRA examinations for the next two quarters.
