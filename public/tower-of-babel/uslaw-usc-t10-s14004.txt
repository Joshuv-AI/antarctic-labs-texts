10 U.S.C. § 14004 — Reserve active-status lists: eligibility for Reserve promotion
Citation: us:usc:10:14004
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section14004
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Promotion And Retention Of Officers On The Reserve Active-Status List); Chapter 1401 (Applicability And Reserve Active-Status Lists). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as otherwise provided by law, an officer must be on a reserve active-status list to be eligible under chapter 1405 of this title for consideration for selection for promotion or for promotion.
