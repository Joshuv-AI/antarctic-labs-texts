19 C.F.R. § 356.6 — Receipt of notice of a class or kind of merchandise determination by the Government of a FTA country.
Citation: us:cfr:19:356.6
Creator: International Trade Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-356.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 356 (Procedures And Rules For Article 10.12 Of The United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where the Department has made a class or kind of merchandise determination, notice of such determination shall be deemed received by the Government of a FTA country:
(a) On the date of publication in the official publication of the determination; or
(b) If the determination was not published in the official publication, on the date on which the Department conveys a copy of the determination to the electronic mail address provided by the Embassy of the FTA country during its normal business hours.
