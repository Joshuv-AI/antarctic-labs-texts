43 C.F.R. § 428.10 — Districts' responsibilities concerning certain formerly excess land.
Citation: us:cfr:43:428.10
Creator: Bureau Of Reclamation, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-428.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 428 (Information Requirements For Certain Farm Operations In Excess Of 960 Acres And The Eligibility Of Certain Formerly Excess Land). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Districts must not make irrigation water available to formerly excess land that meets the criteria under § 428.9(a), unless an exception provided in § 428.9(b) applies.
