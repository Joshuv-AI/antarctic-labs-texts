7 C.F.R. § 1221.114 — Operating reserve.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1221.114
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Miscellaneous Commodities), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1221.114
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1221 (Sorghum Promotion, Research, And Information Order). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board may establish an operating monetary reserve and may carry over to subsequent fiscal period excess funds in a reserve so established, provided that funds in the reserve shall not exceed one fiscal period's anticipated expenses.
