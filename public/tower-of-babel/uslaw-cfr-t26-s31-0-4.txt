26 C.F.R. § 31.0-4 — -4 Extent to which the regulations in this part supersede prior regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:26:31.0-4
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-31.0-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 31 (Employment Taxes And Collection Of Income Tax At Source). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part, with respect to the subject matter within the scope thereof, supersede 25 CFR (1939) Parts 403, 406, 408, and 411 (Regulations 107, 120, 128, and 114, respectively). The Regulation on Monthly Returns and Payment of Employment Taxes (23 FR 5006) are also superseded.
