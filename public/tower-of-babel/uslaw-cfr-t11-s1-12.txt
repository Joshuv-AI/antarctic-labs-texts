11 C.F.R. § 1.12 — Penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:11:1.12
Creator: Federal Election Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-1.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 1 (Privacy Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person who makes a false statement in connection with any request for a record, or an amendment or correction thereto, under this part, is subject to the penalties prescribed in 18 U.S.C. 494 and 495.
