10 U.S.C. § 16134 — Termination of assistance
Citation: us:usc:10:16134
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section16134
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Training For Reserve Components And Educational Assistance Programs); Chapter 1606 (Educational Assistance For Members Of The Selected Reserve). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Educational assistance may not be provided under this chapter—
(1)
to a member receiving financial assistance under section 2107 of this title as a member of the Senior Reserve Officers’ Training Corps program; or
(2)
to a member who fails to participate satisfactorily in required training as a member of the Selected Reserve.
