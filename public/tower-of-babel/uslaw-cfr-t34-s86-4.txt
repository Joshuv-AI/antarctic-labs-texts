34 C.F.R. § 86.4 — What are the procedures for submitting a drug prevention program certification?
Citation: us:cfr:34:86.4
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-86.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 86 (Drug And Alcohol Abuse Prevention). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An IHE shall submit to the Secretary the drug prevention program certification required by § 86.3(b).
