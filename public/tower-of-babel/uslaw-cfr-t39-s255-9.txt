39 C.F.R. § 255.9 — Other postal regulations; authority of postal managers and employees.
Citation: us:cfr:39:255.9
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-255.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 255 (Access Of Persons With Disabilities To Postal Service Programs, Activities, Facilities, And Electronic And Information Technology). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part supplements all other postal regulations. Nothing in this part is intended either to repeal, modify, or amend any other postal regulation, to authorize any postal manager or employee to violate or exceed any regulatory limit, or to confer any budgetary authority on any postal official or employee outside normal budgetary procedures.
