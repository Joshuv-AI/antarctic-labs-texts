49 C.F.R. § 29.415 — Does the Prompt Payment Act apply to funds included in a funding agreement?
Citation: us:cfr:49:29.415
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-29.415
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 29 (Tribal Transportation Self-Governance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Prompt Payment Act, 31 U.S.C. 3901 et seq., applies to the transfer of funds under the Program.
