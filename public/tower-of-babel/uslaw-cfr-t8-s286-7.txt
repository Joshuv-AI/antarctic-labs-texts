8 C.F.R. § 286.7 — Penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:8:286.7
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-286.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 286 (Immigration User Fee). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Failure of any air or sea carrier to comply with the provisions of section 286 of the Act and this part shall subject it to one or more of the following:
(a) Termination of existing agreements under the provisions of section 238 of the Act; and
(b) Suspension of enroute inspections or preinspections.
