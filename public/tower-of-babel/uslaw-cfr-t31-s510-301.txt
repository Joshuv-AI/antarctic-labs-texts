31 C.F.R. § 510.301 — Arms or related materiel.
Citation: us:cfr:31:510.301
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-510.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 510 (North Korea Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term arms or related materiel means arms or related materiel of all types, including any battle tanks, armored combat vehicles, large caliber artillery systems, combat aircraft, attack helicopters, warships, missiles or missile systems, or related materiel including spare parts.
