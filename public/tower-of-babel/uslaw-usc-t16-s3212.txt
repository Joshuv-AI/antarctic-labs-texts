16 U.S.C. § 3212 — Terror Lake Hydroelectric Project in Kodiak National Wildlife Refuge
Citation: us:usc:16:3212
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section3212
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 51 (Alaska National Interest Lands Conservation); Subchapter VI (Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this Act or the National Wildlife Refuge System Administration Act of 1966 (16 U.S.C. 668dd) shall be construed as necessarily prohibiting or mandating the construction of the Terror Lake Hydroelectric Project within the Kodiak National Wildlife Refuge. The permissibility of such development shall be determined by the Secretary on a case-by-case basis under existing law.
