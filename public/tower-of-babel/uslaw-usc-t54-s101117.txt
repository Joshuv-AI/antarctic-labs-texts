54 U.S.C. § 101117 — Authority of Board
Citation: us:usc:54:101117
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section101117
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 1011 (Donations); Subchapter II (National Park Foundation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In carrying out this chapter, the Board may—
(1)
adopt bylaws and regulations necessary for the administration of its functions; and
(2)
contract for any necessary services.
