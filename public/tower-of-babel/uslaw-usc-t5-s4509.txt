5 U.S.C. § 4509 — Prohibition of cash award to Executive Schedule officers
Citation: us:usc:5:4509
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section4509
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart C (Employee Performance); Chapter 45 (Incentive Awards); Subchapter I (Awards For Superior Accomplishments). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No officer may receive a cash award under the provisions of this subchapter, if such officer—
(1)
serves in—
(A)
an Executive Schedule position under subchapter II of chapter 53; or
(B)
a position for which the compensation is set in statute by reference to a section or level under subchapter II of chapter 53; and
(2)
was appointed to such position by the President, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate.
