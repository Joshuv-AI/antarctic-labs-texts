40 C.F.R. § 141.511 — What is required of new finished water reservoirs?
Citation: us:cfr:40:141.511
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-141.511
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 141 (National Primary Drinking Water Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If your system begins construction of a finished water reservoir on or after March 15, 2002 the reservoir must be covered. Finished water reservoirs for which your system began construction prior to March 15, 2002 are not subject to this requirement.
