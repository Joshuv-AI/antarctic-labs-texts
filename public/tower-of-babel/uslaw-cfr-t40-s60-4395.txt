40 C.F.R. § 60.4395 — When must I submit my reports?
Citation: us:cfr:40:60.4395
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.4395
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 60 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All reports required under § 60.7(c) must be electronically submitted via CEDRI by the 30th day following the end of each 6-month period.
