40 C.F.R. § 1066.925 — Enclosure calculations for evaporative and refueling emissions.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1066.925
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1066.925
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1066 (Vehicle-Testing Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Calculate emissions for evaporative emissions as described in 40 CFR 86.143-96. Calculate emissions for refueling emissions as described in 40 CFR 86.143-96 and 86.156-98.
