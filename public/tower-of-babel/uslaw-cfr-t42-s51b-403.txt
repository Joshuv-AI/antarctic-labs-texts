42 C.F.R. § 51b.403 — b.403 Who is eligible for a grant under this subpart?
Citation: us:cfr:42:51b.403
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-51b.403
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 51b (PART 51b—PROJECT GRANTS FOR PREVENTIVE HEALTH SERVICES). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An applicant must be a State agency or a political subdivision of a State which has legal responsibility for disease control under the laws of the State.
