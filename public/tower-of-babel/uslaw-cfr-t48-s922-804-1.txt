48 C.F.R. § 922.804-1 — 922.804-1 Nonconstruction.
Citation: us:cfr:48:922.804-1
Creator: Chapter 9—Department Of Energy
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 922 (Application Of Labor Laws To Government Acquisition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the event a prospective contractor or subcontractor is entering into its first contract containing the Equal Opportunity clause, the contracting officer shall determine that the prospective contractor understands and appears able to conform to the requirements of the EEO clause.
