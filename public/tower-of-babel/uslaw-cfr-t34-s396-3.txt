34 C.F.R. § 396.3 — What regulations apply?
Citation: us:cfr:34:396.3
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-396.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 396 (Training Of Interpreters For Individuals Who Are Deaf Or Hard Of Hearing And Individuals Who Are Deaf-Blind). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following regulations apply to the Training of Interpreters for Individuals Who Are Deaf or Hard of Hearing and Individuals Who Are Deaf-Blind program:
(a) 34 CFR part 385 (Rehabilitation Training), sections—
(1) 385.3(a) and (d);
(2) 385.40 through 385.46; and
(b) The regulations under this part 396.
