27 C.F.R. § 46.16 — Penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:27:46.16
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-46.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 46 (Miscellaneous Regulations Relating To Tobacco Products And Cigarette Papers And Tubes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is an offense punishable by fine and imprisonment for anyone to make or cause to be made any false or fraudulent claim upon the United States, or to make any false or fraudulent statements, or representations, in support of any claim, or to falsely or fraudulently execute any documents required by the provisions of the internal revenue laws, or any regulations made in pursuance thereof.
