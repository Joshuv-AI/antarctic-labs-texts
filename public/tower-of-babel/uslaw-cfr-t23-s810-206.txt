23 C.F.R. § 810.206 — Review by the State Highway Agency.
Citation: us:cfr:23:810.206
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-810.206
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 810 (Mass Transit And Special Use Highway Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The State highway agency, after reviewing the application, may request the Federal Highway Administrator to authorize the State to make available to the publicly-owned mass transit authority the land needed for the proposed facility. A request shall be accompanied by evidence that utilization of the land for the proposed purposes will not impair future highway improvements or the safety of highway users.
