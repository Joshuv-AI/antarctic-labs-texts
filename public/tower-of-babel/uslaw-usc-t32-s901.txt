32 U.S.C. § 901 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:32:901
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title32-section901
Context: United States Code, Title 32 (National Guard); Chapter 9 (Homeland Defense Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter:
(1)
The term “homeland defense activity” means an activity undertaken for the military protection of the territory or domestic population of the United States, or of infrastructure or other assets of the United States determined by the Secretary of Defense as being critical to national security, from a threat or aggression against the United States.
(2)
The term “State” means each of the several States, the District of Columbia, the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico, or a territory or possession of the United States.
