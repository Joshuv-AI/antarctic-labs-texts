49 C.F.R. § 541.2 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:49:541.2
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-541.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 541 (Federal Motor Vehicle Theft Prevention Standard). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this standard is to reduce the incidence of motor vehicle thefts by facilitating the tracing and recovery of parts from stolen vehicles.
