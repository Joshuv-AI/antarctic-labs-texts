10 U.S.C. § 9434 — Command and supervision
Citation: us:usc:10:9434
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9434
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training); Chapter 953 (United States Air Force Academy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The immediate government of the Academy is under the Superintendent, who is also the commanding officer of the Academy and of the military post.
(b)
The permanent professors and the director of admissions exercise command only in the academic department of the Academy.
