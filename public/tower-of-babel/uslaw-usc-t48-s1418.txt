48 U.S.C. § 1418 — Employment of land and naval forces in protection of rights
Citation: us:usc:48:1418
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1418
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 8 (Guano Islands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President is authorized, at his discretion, to employ the land and naval forces of the United States to protect the rights of the discoverer or of his widow, heir, executor, administrator, or assigns.
