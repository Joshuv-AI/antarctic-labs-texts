45 C.F.R. § 1803.1 — Purpose.

Creator: Harry S. Truman Scholarship Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1803.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1803 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part effectuates section 119 of the Rehabilitation, Comprehensive Services, and Developmental Disabilities Amendments of 1978, which amended section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973 to prohibit discrimination on the basis of handicap in programs or activities conducted by executive agencies.
