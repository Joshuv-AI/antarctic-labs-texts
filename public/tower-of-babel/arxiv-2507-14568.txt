Extremal Degree Irregularity Bounds for Albertson and Sigma Indices in Trees and Bipartite Graphs


Abstract
In this paper, study of extreme values of bounds in topological indices plays an important role in determining the impact of these indices on trees and bipartite graphs. For any integers α , p \alpha,p where 1 ≤ p ≤ α ≤ Δ − 3 1\leq p\leq\alpha\leq\Delta-3 , the upper bound of the minimum value of Albertson index irr min \operatorname{irr}_{\min} satisfy: irr min ⩾ Δ 2 ​ ( Δ − 1 ) ​ 2 α ( Δ − p ) 2 . \operatorname{irr}_{\min}\geqslant\Delta^{2}(\Delta-1)\frac{2^{\alpha}}{(\Delta-p)^{2}}. Studies the maximum and minimum values of the Albertson index in bipartite graphs based on the sizes of their bipartition sets. Leveraging a known corollary, explicit formulas and bounds are derived that characterize the irregularity measure under various vertex size conditions. Upper bounds for both the Albertson and Sigma indices are established using graph parameters such as degrees and edge counts. Theoretical results are supported by examples and inequalities involving degree sequences, enhancing understanding of extremal irregularity behavior in bipartite graphs.

~\sigma(\mathscr{G})\leqslant\Delta^{3}(\Delta-1)-2(n_{1}n_{2})^{2}+\frac{4n_{2}^{2}}{n_{1}n_{2}+3\delta^{2}}+2n_{1}^{3}.

(55)

Therefore, from (

) and (

), we conclude that (

) is valid.

Case 2:

If
\mathscr{G}
is not a complete bipartite graph. In this case, the Albertson index satisfies the bound

\operatorname{irr}(\mathscr{G})=n_{1}n_{2}|n_{1}-n_{2}|.

Then, we have

~\operatorname{irr}(\mathscr{G})\leqslant\Delta^{2}(\Delta-1)+\delta(\delta-1)^{2}+\frac{\Delta^{2}(\Delta-1)+4n_{2}}{n_{1}n_{2}+5\delta}.

By considering

\Delta^{2}(\Delta-1)>2n_{1}^{2}>\delta(\delta-1)^{2}.

Thus, according to (

) is valid.

For the Sigma index, we find that when

but when

~\sigma(\mathscr{G})\leqslant\Delta^{3}(\Delta-1)+\delta(\delta-1)^{3}+\frac{\Delta^{3}(\Delta-1)+4n_{2}^{2}}{n_{1}n_{2}+3\delta^{2}}.

(57)

. This completes the proof.

5
Conclusion

Through this paper, we presented a study of bounds on the topological indices which we elected to the Sigma Index and the Albertson Index. For any tree
T
of order
n
, the Albertson index and the Sigma index play significant roles in understanding structural properties of trees and bipartite graphs. The
effect of the extreme values
of the Albertson and Sigma indices reveals important information about the bounds on irregularity and structural variance in both trees and bipartite graphs, illustrating how these indices govern graph properties under extremal conditions. The
maximum and minimum values
of the bounds for each index have been characterized, providing precise limitations on these indices, which correspond to specific graph structures such as regular graphs and
(\Delta,\delta)
-semiregular bipartite graphs.

These extreme values are instrumental in
studying bounds on bipartite graphs
, enabling the identification of extremal graphs which achieve these bounds and guiding further combinatorial investigations and applications in network analysis. This framework not only advances the theoretical understanding of these indices but also supports diverse applications involving the measurement of irregularity and connectivity in bipartite graphs and trees.
