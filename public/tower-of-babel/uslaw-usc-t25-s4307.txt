25 U.S.C. § 4307 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:25:4307
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section4307
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 44 (Native American Business Development, Trade Promotion, And Tourism). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as are necessary to carry out this chapter, to remain available until expended.
