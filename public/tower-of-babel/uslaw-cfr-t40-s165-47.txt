40 C.F.R. § 165.47 — What information must I report about my refillable containers?
Citation: us:cfr:40:165.47
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-165.47
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 165 (Pesticide Management And Disposal). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You are not required to report to EPA with information about your refillable containers under the regulations in this subpart. You should refer to the reporting standards in part 159 of this chapter to determine if information on container failures or other incidents involving pesticide containers must be reported to EPA under FIFRA section 6(a)(2) (7 U.S.C. 136d(a)(2)).
