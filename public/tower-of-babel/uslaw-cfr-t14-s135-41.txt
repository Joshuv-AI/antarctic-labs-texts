14 C.F.R. § 135.41 — Carriage of narcotic drugs, marihuana, and depressant or stimulant drugs or substances.
Citation: us:cfr:14:135.41
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-135.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 135 (Operating Requirements: Commuter And On Demand Operations And Rules Governing Persons On Board Such Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the holder of a certificate operating under this part allows any aircraft owned or leased by that holder to be engaged in any operation that the certificate holder knows to be in violation of § 91.19(a) of this chapter, that operation is a basis for suspending or revoking the certificate.
