41 C.F.R. § 50-203.16 — -203.16 Industry panel meetings.
Citation: us:cfr:41:50-203.16
Creator: Chapter 50—Public Contracts, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-50-203.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 50-203 (203—Rules Of Practice). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary of Labor may, within his discretion, invite representatives of employers and employees in an industry to meet as an informal panel group to discuss with representatives of the Department of Labor the various questions relating to the issuance of a wage determination for the industry.
