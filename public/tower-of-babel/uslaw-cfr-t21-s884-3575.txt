21 C.F.R. § 884.3575 — Vaginal pessary.
Citation: us:cfr:21:884.3575
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-884.3575
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 884 (Obstetrical And Gynecological Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. A vaginal pessary is a removable structure placed in the vagina to support the pelvic organs and is used to treat conditions such as uterine prolapse (falling down of uterus), uterine retroposition (backward displacement), or gynecologic hernia.
(b) Classification. Class II (performance standards).
