41 C.F.R. § 105-64.111 — -64.111 What is GSA's policy on directives that may conflict with this part?
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-64.111
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-64.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-64 (64—Gsa Privacy Act Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These rules take precedence over any GSA directive that may conflict with the requirements stated here. GSA officials will ensure that no such conflict exists in new or existing directives.
