30 C.F.R. § 43.8 — Informal review upon issuance of a notice of negative finding.
Citation: us:cfr:30:43.8
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-43.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 43 (Procedures For Processing Hazardous Conditions Complaints). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person to whom a notice of negative finding has been issued pursuant to § 43.6 of this part may request informal review of such finding in accordance with the provisions of § 43.7(b)-(d) of this subpart.
