42 C.F.R. § 62.76 — How will amounts of money due under the option under section 204(c)(1) of Public Law 100-177 be required to be repaid?
Citation: us:cfr:42:62.76
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-62.76
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 62 (National Health Service Corps Scholarship And Loan Repayment Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Program participants who elect to serve under section 204(c)(1) of Pub. L. 100-177 will be required to pay the full scholarship amount at least 60 days prior to the service start date specified in the documentation submitted to the Secretary.
