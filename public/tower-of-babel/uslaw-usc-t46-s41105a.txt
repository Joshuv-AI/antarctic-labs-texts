46 U.S.C. § 41105A — Authority
Citation: us:usc:46:41105A
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section41105A
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part A (Ocean Shipping); Chapter 411 (Prohibitions And Penalties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in section 41105, as amended by the Federal Maritime Commission Authorization Act of 2017, shall be construed to limit the authority of the Department of Justice regarding antitrust matters.
