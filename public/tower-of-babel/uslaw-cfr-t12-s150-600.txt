12 C.F.R. § 150.600 — How may funds be invested when I act in an exempt fiduciary capacity?
Citation: us:cfr:12:150.600
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-150.600
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 150 (Fiduciary Powers Of Federal Savings Associations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you act in an exempt fiduciary capacity under § 150.580, the funds of the fiduciary account may be invested only in the following:
(a) Your accounts, deposits, obligations, or securities.
(b) Other assets as the customer may direct, provided you do not exercise any investment discretion and do not directly or indirectly provide any investment advice for the fiduciary account.
