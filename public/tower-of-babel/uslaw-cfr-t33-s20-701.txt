33 C.F.R. § 20.701 — Standard of proof.
Citation: us:cfr:33:20.701
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-20.701
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 20 (Rules Of Practice, Procedure, And Evidence For Formal Administrative Proceedings Of The Coast Guard). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The party that bears the burden of proof shall prove his or her case or affirmative defense by a preponderance of the evidence.
