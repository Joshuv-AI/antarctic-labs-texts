48 C.F.R. § 370.400 — 370.400 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:48:370.400
Creator: Chapter 3—Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-370.400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 370 (Special Programs Affecting Acquisition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to all research, research training, biological testing, housing and maintenance, and other activities involving live vertebrate animals conducted under contract. Additional information can be found in Public Health Service (PHS) Policy on Humane Care and Use of Laboratory Animals http://grants.nih.gov/grants/olaw/references/phspolicylabanimals.pdf.
