40 U.S.C. § 9302 — Consent of Theodore Roosevelt Association required for development
Citation: us:usc:40:9302
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section9302
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part D (Public Buildings, Grounds, And Parks In The District Of Columbia); Chapter 93 (Theodore Roosevelt Island). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) General Plan for Development.—
The Theodore Roosevelt Association must approve every general plan for the development of Theodore Roosevelt Island.
(b) Development Inconsistent With Plan.—
As long as the Association remains in existence, development inconsistent with the general plan may not be carried out without the Association’s consent.
