31 C.F.R. § 363.53 — What is the minimum amount of book-entry savings bonds that I may purchase in any transaction?
Citation: us:cfr:31:363.53
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-363.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 363 (Regulations Governing Securities Held In Treasurydirect). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each bond purchase must be in a minimum amount of $25, with additional one-cent increments above that amount, in any one transaction. For example, a purchase may be $25.00, $25.01, $25.02, or $25.03, and so forth.
