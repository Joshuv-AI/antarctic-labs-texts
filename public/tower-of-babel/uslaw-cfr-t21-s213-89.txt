21 C.F.R. § 213.89 — Rejected components, incoming designated medical gases, and medical gas containers and closures.
Citation: us:cfr:21:213.89
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-213.89
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 213 (Current Good Manufacturing Practice For Medical Gases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Rejected components, incoming designated medical gases, and medical gas containers and closures shall be identified and controlled under a quarantine system designed to prevent their use in manufacturing or processing operations for which they are unsuitable and shall be documented and assessed.
