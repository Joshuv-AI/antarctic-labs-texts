30 C.F.R. § 1290.100 — What is the purpose of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:30:1290.100
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1290.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1290 (Appeal Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part tells you how to appeal Office of Natural Resources Revenue (ONRR) or delegated State orders concerning reporting to the Minerals Revenue Management (MRM) and the payment of royalties and other payments due under leases subject to this part.
