15 U.S.C. § 6902 — Establishment of program
Citation: us:usc:15:6902
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section6902
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 95 (Microenterprise Technical Assistance And Capacity Building Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator shall establish a microenterprise technical assistance and capacity building grant program to provide assistance from the Administration in the form of grants to qualified organizations in accordance with this chapter.
