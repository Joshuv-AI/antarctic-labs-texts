34 C.F.R. § 75.682 — Treatment of animals.
Citation: us:cfr:34:75.682
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-75.682
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 75 (Direct Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a grantee uses an animal in a project, the grantee must provide the animal with proper care and humane treatment in accordance with the Animal Welfare Act.
