10 C.F.R. § 430.57 — Duration of temporary exemption.
Citation: us:cfr:10:430.57
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-430.57
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 430 (Energy Conservation Program For Consumer Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A temporary exemption terminates according to its terms but not later than twenty-four months after the affective date of the rule for which the exemption is allowed.
