45 U.S.C. § 161 — Effect of partial invalidity of chapter
Citation: us:usc:45:161
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2024
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title45-section161
Context: United States Code, Title 45 (Railroads); Chapter 8 (Railway Labor); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this chapter, or the application thereof to any person or circumstance, is held invalid, the remainder of the chapter, and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances, shall not be affected thereby.
