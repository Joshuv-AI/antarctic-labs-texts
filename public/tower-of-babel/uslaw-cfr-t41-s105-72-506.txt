41 C.F.R. § 105-72.506 — -72.506 Procurement records.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-72.506
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-72.506
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-72 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Procurement records and files for purchases in excess of the small purchase threshold shall include the following at a minimum:
(a) Basis for contractor selection,
(b) Justification for lack of competition when competitive bids or offers are not obtained, and
(c) Basis for award cost or price.
