43 C.F.R. § 423.43 — Alcoholic beverages.
Citation: us:cfr:43:423.43
Creator: Bureau Of Reclamation, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-423.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 423 (Public Conduct On Bureau Of Reclamation Facilities, Lands, And Waterbodies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must not possess or consume alcoholic beverages in violation of Federal, State, or local law, or the rules of a special use area established by an authorized official under subpart E of this part 423.
