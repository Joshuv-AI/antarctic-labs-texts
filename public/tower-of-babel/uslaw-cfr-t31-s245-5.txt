31 C.F.R. § 245.5 — Recertification of payment.
Citation: us:cfr:31:245.5
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-245.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 245 (Claims On Account Of Treasury Checks). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon receipt of a claim concerning the nonreceipt, loss, destruction, mutilation or defacement of a check, or the cancellation of a check pursuant to § 240.4 of this chapter, the certifying agency may certify a new payment.
