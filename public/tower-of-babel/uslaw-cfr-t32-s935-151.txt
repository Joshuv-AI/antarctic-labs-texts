32 C.F.R. § 935.151 — Island permit for boat and vehicle.
Citation: us:cfr:32:935.151
Creator: Department Of The Air Force
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-935.151
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 935 (Wake Island Code). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) No person may use a privately owned motor vehicle or boat on Wake Island unless he has an island permit for it.
(b) The operator of a motor vehicle shall display its registration number on the vehicle in a place and manner prescribed by the Commander.
