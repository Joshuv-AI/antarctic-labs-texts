2 C.F.R. § 2205.332 — Fixed amount subawards.
Citation: us:cfr:2:2205.332
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-2205.332
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 2205 (Implementation Of And Exemptions To 2 Cfr). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Fixed amount subawards may be made in the manner and in amounts determined under the national service laws, as authorized by the Corporation, without respect to the Simplified Acquisition Threshold.
