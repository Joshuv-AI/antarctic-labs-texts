17 C.F.R. § 204.50 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:17:204.50
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-204.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 204 (Rules Relating To Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart establishes procedures for the Commission's referral of past-due legally enforceable debts to the Department of the Treasury's Financial Management Service (FMS) for offset against the income tax refunds of the debtor.
