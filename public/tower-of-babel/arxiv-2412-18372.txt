The injective hull of ideals of weighted holomorphic mappings
A. Jiménez-Vargas, María Isabel Ramírez

Abstract
. We study the injectivity of normed ideals of weighted holomorphic mappings. To be more precise, the concept of injective hull of normed weighted holomorphic ideals is introduced and characterized in terms of a domination property. The injective hulls of those ideals – generated by the procedures of composition and dual – are described and these descriptions are applied to some examples of such ideals. A characterization of the closed injective hull of an operator ideal in terms of an Ehrling-type inequality – due to Jarchow and Pelczyński– is established for weighted holomorphic mappings.

Given an open subset
U

About the theory of weighted holomorphic mappings, the interested reader can consult the papers

By definition, the injective hull of a normed weighted holomorphic ideal
[\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}},\|\cdot\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}]
is the smallest injective normed weighted holomorphic ideal containing
\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}

1.1

In Subsection

[
7

8
]

Using the linearization of weighted holomorphic mappings, we describe in Subsection

the injective hull of composition ideals of weighted holomorphic mappings and apply this description to establish the injectivity of the normed weighted holomorphic ideals generated by composition with some distinguished classes of bounded linear operators such as finite-rank, compact, weakly compact, separable, Rosenthal and Asplund operators.

In Subsection
1.4

\mathcal{I}
is introduced and showed that it coincides with the weighted holomorphic ideal generated by composition with the dual operator ideal

1.1.
The injective hull of ideals of weighted holomorphic mappings

In the light of Definition 2.4 in
[
9
]
, a normed (Banach) ideal of weighted holomorphic mappings – or, in short, a normed (Banach) weighted holomorphic ideal – is an assignment
[\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}]
which associates every pair
(U,F)
, – where
E
is a complex Banach space,
U
is an open subset of
E
and
F
is a complex Banach space– to both a set
\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}(U,F)\subseteq\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,F)
and a function
\|\cdot\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}\colon\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}(U,F)\to\mathbb{R}
satisfying

(P1)

(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}(U,F),\|\cdot\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}})
is a normed (Banach) space with
\|f\|_{v}\leq\|f\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}
for
f\in\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}(U,F)
,

(P2)

Given
h\in\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U)
and
y\in F
, the map
h\cdot y\colon x\in U\mapsto h(x)y\in F
is in
\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}(U,F)
with
\|h\cdot y\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}=\|h\|_{v}||y||
,

(P3)

The ideal property: if
V
is an open subset of
E
such that
V\subseteq U
,
h\in\mathcal{H}(V,U)
with
c_{v}(h):=\sup_{x\in V}(v(x)/v(h(x)))<\infty
,
f\in\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}(U,F)
and
T\in\mathcal{L}(F,G)
where
G
is a complex Banach space, then
T\circ f\circ h\in\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}(V,G)
with
\|T\circ f\circ h\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}\leq\left\|T\right\|\|f\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}c_{v}(h)
.

According to Sections 4.6 and 8.4 in
[
22
]
, an operator ideal
\mathcal{I}
is said to be injective if for each Banach space
G
and each isometric linear embedding
\iota\colon F\to G
, an operator
T\in\mathcal{L}(E,F)
belongs to
\mathcal{I}(E,F)
whenever
\iota\circ T\in\mathcal{I}(E,G)
. A normed operator ideal
[\mathcal{I},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{I}}]
is called injective if, in addition,
\left\|T\right\|_{\mathcal{I}}=\left\|\iota\circ T\right\|_{\mathcal{I}}
.

The adaptation of this notion to the weighted holomorphic setting could be as follows.

A normed weighted holomorphic ideal
[\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}]
is called:

(I)

injective if for any map
f\in\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,F)
, any complex Banach space
G
and any into linear isometry
\iota\colon F\to G
, one has
f\in\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}(U,F)
with
\left\|f\right\|_{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}=\left\|\iota\circ f\right\|_{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}
whenever
\iota\circ f\in\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}(U,G)
.

Given normed weighted holomorphic ideals
[\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}]
and
[\mathcal{J}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{J}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}]
, the relation

[\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}]\leq[\mathcal{J}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{J}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}]

means that for any complex Banach space
E
, any open set
U\subseteq E
and any complex Banach space
F
, one has
\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}(U,F)\subseteq\mathcal{J}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}(U,F)
with
\left\|f\right\|_{\mathcal{J}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}\leq\left\|f\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}
for
f\in\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}(U,F)
.

Motivated by the linear and polynomial versions (see
[
16
, Proposition 19.2.2]
and
[
8
, Proposition 2.3]
), we next address the existence of the injective hull of a normed weighted holomorphic ideal.

Recall that the unique smallest injective operator ideal
\mathcal{I}^{inj}
that contains an operator ideal
\mathcal{I}
is called the injective hull of
\mathcal{I}
and described as the set

\mathcal{I}^{inj}(E,F)=\left\{T\in\mathcal{L}(E,F)\colon\iota_{F}\circ T\in\mathcal{I}(E,\ell_{\infty}(B_{Y^{*}})\right\},

where
\iota_{F}\colon F\to\ell_{\infty}(B_{F^{*}})
is the canonical isometric linear embedding defined by

\left\langle\iota_{F}(y),y^{*}\right\rangle=y^{*}(y)\qquad(y^{*}\in B_{F^{*}},\;y\in F).

Taking
\left\|T\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{inj}}=\left\|\iota_{F}\circ T\right\|_{\mathcal{I}}
for
T\in\mathcal{I}^{inj}(E,F)
,
[\mathcal{I}^{inj},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{inj}}]
is a normed (Banach) operator ideal whenever
[\mathcal{I},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{I}}]
is so.

We now present the closely related concept in the setting of weighted holomorphic maps.

Proposition 1.1
.

Let
[\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}},\|\cdot\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}]
be a normed (Banach) weighted holomorphic ideal. Then there exists a unique smallest normed (Banach) injective weighted holomorphic ideal
[(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}}]
such that

[\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}]\leq[(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}}].

In fact, for any complex Banach space
F
, we have

(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}(U,F)=\left\{f\in\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,F)\colon\iota_{F}\circ f\in\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}(U,\ell_{\infty}(B_{F^{*}})\right\}

where
\iota_{F}\colon F\to\ell_{\infty}(B_{F^{*}})
is the canonical isometric linear embedding, and

\left\|f\right\|_{(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}}=\left\|\iota_{F}\circ f\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}\qquad(f\in(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}(U,F)).

The normed (Banach) ideal
[(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}}]
) is called the injective hull of
[\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}},\|\cdot\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}]
.

Proof.

Defining the set
(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}(U,F)
and the function
\left\|\cdot\right\|_{(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}}\colon(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}(U,F)\to\mathbb{R}^{+}_{0}
as above, we first show that
[(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}}]
is an injective normed (Banach) weighted holomorphic ideal.

(P1) Given
f\in(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}(U,F)
, for all
x\in U
, we have

v(x)\left\|f(x)\right\|=v(x)\left\|\iota_{F}(f(x))\right\|\leq\left\|\iota_{F}\circ f\right\|_{v}\leq\left\|\iota_{F}\circ f\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}=\left\|f\right\|_{(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}},

and thus
\left\|f\right\|_{v}\leq\left\|f\right\|_{(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}}
. Hence
f=0
whenever
\left\|f\right\|_{(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}}=0
. It is readily to prove that
(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}(U,F)
is a linear subspace of
\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,F)
on which
\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}
is absolutely homogeneous and satisfies the triangle inequality.

(P2) Given
h\in\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U)
and
y\in F
, we have
\iota_{F}\circ(h\cdot y)=h\cdot\iota_{F}(y)\in\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}(U,\ell_{\infty}(B_{F^{*}}))
and therefore
h\cdot y\in(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}(U,F)
with

\left\|h\cdot y\right\|_{(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}}=\left\|\iota_{F}\circ(h\cdot y)\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}=\left\|h\cdot\iota_{F}(y)\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}=\left\|h\right\|_{v}\left\|\iota_{F}(y)\right\|=\left\|h\right\|_{v}\left\|y\right\|
.

(P3) Let
V\subseteq E
be an open set such that
V\subseteq U
,
h\in\mathcal{H}(V,U)
with
c_{v}(h):=\sup_{x\in V}(v(x)/v(h(x)))<\infty
,
f\in\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}(U,F)
and
T\in\mathcal{L}(F,G)
where
G
is a complex Banach space. Clearly,
\iota_{F}\circ f\in\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}(U,\ell_{\infty}(B_{F^{*}}))
. Since
\iota_{F}
is an into linear isometry, there exists
S\in\mathcal{L}(\ell_{\infty}(B_{F^{*}}),\ell_{\infty}(B_{G^{*}}))
such that
S\circ\iota_{F}=\iota_{G}\circ T
and
\left\|S\right\|=\left\|\iota_{F}\circ T\right\|
by the metric extension property of
\ell_{\infty}(B_{F^{*}})
(see, for example,
[
22
, Proposition C.3.2.1]
).
From
\iota_{G}\circ(T\circ f\circ h)=S\circ(\iota_{F}\circ f)\circ h\in\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}(U,\ell_{\infty}(B_{G^{*}}))
, we infer that
T\circ f\circ h\in(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}(U,G)
with

\displaystyle\left\|T\circ f\circ h\right\|_{(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}}

\displaystyle=\left\|\iota_{G}\circ T\circ f\circ h\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}=\left\|S\circ\iota_{F}\circ f\circ h\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}

\displaystyle\leq\left\|S\right\|\left\|\iota_{F}\circ f\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}=\left\|\iota_{G}\circ T\right\|\left\|f\right\|_{(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}}\leq\left\|T\right\|\left\|f\right\|_{(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}}

(I) Let
f\in\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,F)
so that
\iota\circ f\in(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}(U,G)
for any into linear isometry
\iota\colon F\to G
. The metric extension property of
\ell_{\infty}(B_{F^{*}})
provides a
P\in\mathcal{L}(\ell_{\infty}(B_{G^{*}}),\ell_{\infty}(B_{F^{*}}))
so that
P\circ\iota_{G}\circ\iota=\iota_{F}
and
\left\|P\right\|=\left\|\iota_{F}\right\|=1
.
The conditions
\iota_{G}\circ\iota\circ f\in\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}(U,\ell_{\infty}(B_{G^{*}}))
and
\iota_{F}\circ f=P\circ\iota_{G}\circ\iota\circ f
imply
\iota_{F}\circ f\in\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}(U,\ell_{\infty}(B_{F^{*}}))
, and so
f\in(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}(U,F)
with

\left\|f\right\|_{(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}}=\left\|\iota_{F}\circ f\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}=\left\|P\circ\iota_{G}\circ\iota\circ f\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}\leq\left\|P\right\|\left\|\iota_{G}\circ\iota\circ f\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}}=\left\|\iota\circ f\right\|_{(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}}\leq\left\|f\right\|_{(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}}.

On a hand, the ideal property of
[\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}]
yields
[\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}]\leq[(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}}]
. On the other hand, suppose
[\mathcal{J}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{J}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}]
is an injective normed weighted holomorphic ideal so that
[\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}]\leq[\mathcal{J}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{J}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}]
. If
f\in(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}(U,F)
, one has that
\iota_{F}\circ f\in\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}(U,\ell_{\infty}(B_{F^{*}}))\subseteq\mathcal{J}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}(U,\ell_{\infty}(B_{F^{*}}))
, hence
f\in\mathcal{J}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}(U,F)
with
\left\|f\right\|_{\mathcal{J}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}=\left\|\iota_{F}\circ f\right\|_{\mathcal{J}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}
by the injectivity of
\mathcal{J}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}
, and so
\left\|f\right\|_{\mathcal{J}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}=\left\|\iota_{F}\circ f\right\|_{\mathcal{J}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}\leq\left\|\iota_{F}\circ f\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}=\left\|f\right\|_{(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}}
.

The uniqueness of
[(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}}]
follows easily and this completes the proof.
∎

Based on the linear and polynomial variants in
[
16
, Proposition 19.2.2]
and
[
8
, Corollary 2.4]
, respectively, the injectivity of a normed weighted holomorphic ideal is characterized by the coincidence with its injective hull.

Corollary 1.2
.

Let
[\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}},\|\cdot\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}]
be a normed weighted holomorphic ideal. The following are equivalent:

(i)

[\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}},\|\cdot\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}]
is injective.

(ii)

[\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}},\|\cdot\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}]=[(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}}]
.

\hfill\qed

Influenced by the hull procedure for the family of normed operator ideals – introduced by Pietsch in
[
22
, Section 8.1]
–, we obtain that the correspondence
\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}\mapsto(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}
is a hull procedure in the weighted holomorphic setting.

Proposition 1.3
.

If
[\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}},\|\cdot\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}]
and
[\mathcal{J}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}},\|\cdot\|_{\mathcal{J}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}]
are normed (Banach) weighted holomorphic ideals, then:

(i)

[(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj},\|\cdot\|_{(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}}]
is a normed (Banach) weighted holomorphic ideal,

(ii)

[(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj},\|\cdot\|_{(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}}]\leq[(\mathcal{J}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj},\|\cdot\|_{(\mathcal{J}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}}]
whenever
[\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}},\|\cdot\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}]\leq[\mathcal{J}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}},\|\cdot\|_{\mathcal{J}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}]
,

(iii)

[((\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj})^{inj},\|\cdot\|_{((\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj})^{inj}}]=[(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj},\|\cdot\|_{(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}}]
,

(iv)

[\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}},\|\cdot\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}]\leq[(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj},\|\cdot\|_{(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}}]
.

Proof.

(i)
and
(iv)
are deduced from Proposition
1.1
,
(iii)
from Corollary
1.2
, and
(ii)
follows as in the last part of the proof of Proposition
1.1
.
∎

1.2.
The domination property

The injective hull of a normed weighted holomorphic ideal can be characterized by the following domination property. This result is based on both the linear and polynomial versions stated respectively in
[
7
, Lemma 3.1]
and
[
8
, Theorem 3.4]
.

Theorem 1.4
.

Let
[\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}]
be a normed weighted holomorphic ideal, let
F
be a complex Banach space and let
f\in\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,F)
. The following assertions are equivalent:

(i)

f
belongs to
(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}(U,F)
.

(ii)

There exists a complex normed space
G
and a mapping
g\in\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}(U,G)
such that

\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}v(x_{i})f(x_{i})\right\|\leq\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}v(x_{i})g(x_{i})\right\|

for all
n\in\mathbb{N}
,
\lambda_{1},\ldots,\lambda_{n}\in\mathbb{C}
and
x_{1},\ldots,x_{n}\in U
.

In this case,
\left\|f\right\|_{(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}}=\inf\left\{\left\|g\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}\right\}
, where the infimum is taken over all spaces
G
and all mappings
g\in\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}(U,G)
as in
(ii)
, and this infimum is attained.

Proof.

(i)\Rightarrow(ii)
: Suppose that
f\in(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}(U,F)
. Take
G=\ell_{\infty}(B_{F^{*}})
and
g=\iota_{F}\circ f
. Clearly,
g\in\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}(U,G)
with
\left\|g\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}=\left\|\iota_{F}\circ f\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}=\left\|f\right\|_{(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}}
. Set
n\in\mathbb{N}
,
\lambda_{1},\ldots,\lambda_{n}\in\mathbb{C}
and
x_{1},\ldots,x_{n}\in U
.
We can take some
y^{*}\in B_{E^{*}}
so that
\left|y^{*}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}v(x_{i})f(x_{i})\right)\right|=\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}v(x_{i})f(x_{i})\right\|
, and thus

\displaystyle\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}v(x_{i})f(x_{i})\right\|

\displaystyle=\left|\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}v(x_{i})y^{*}(f(x_{i}))\right|=\left|\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}v(x_{i})\left\langle\iota_{F}(f(x_{i})),y^{*}\right\rangle\right|

\displaystyle=\left|\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}v(x_{i})\left\langle g(x_{i}),y^{*}\right\rangle\right|\leq\sup_{x^{*}\in B_{E^{*}}}\left|\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}v(x_{i})\left\langle g(x_{i}),x^{*}\right\rangle\right|

\displaystyle=\sup_{x^{*}\in B_{E^{*}}}\left|\left\langle\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}v(x_{i})g(x_{i}),x^{*}\right\rangle\right|=\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}v(x_{i})g(x_{i})\right\|

(ii)\Rightarrow(i)
: Let
G
and
g
be as in
(ii)
. Take
G_{0}=\mathrm{lin}(g(U))\subseteq G
and
T_{0}\colon G_{0}\to F
given by

T_{0}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}v(x_{i})g(x_{i})\right)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}v(x_{i})f(x_{i})

for all
n\in\mathbb{N}
,
\lambda_{1},\ldots,\lambda_{n}\in\mathbb{C}
and
x_{1},\ldots,x_{n}\in U
. Note that
T_{0}
is well defined since

\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}v(x_{i})g(x_{i})=\sum_{j=1}^{m}\alpha_{j}v(x_{j})g(x_{j})

\displaystyle\Rightarrow\left|\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}v(x_{i})g(x_{i})-\sum_{j=1}^{m}\alpha_{j}v(x_{j})g(x_{j})\right|=0

\displaystyle\Rightarrow\left|\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}v(x_{i})f(x_{i})-\sum_{j=1}^{m}\alpha_{j}v(x_{j})f(x_{j})\right|=0

\displaystyle\Rightarrow\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}v(x_{i})f(x_{i})=\sum_{j=1}^{m}\alpha_{j}v(x_{j})f(x_{j}),

by using the inequality in
(ii)
. The linearity of
T_{0}
is clear, and since

\left\|T_{0}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}v(x_{i})g(x_{i})\right)\right\|=\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}v(x_{i})f(x_{i})\right\|\leq\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}v(x_{i})g(x_{i})\right\|

for all
n\in\mathbb{N}
,
\lambda_{1},\ldots,\lambda_{n}\in\mathbb{C}
and
x_{1},\ldots,x_{n}\in U
, we deduce that
T_{0}
is continuous with
\left\|T_{0}\right\|\leq 1
. There exists a unique operator
T\in\mathcal{L}(\overline{G_{0}},F)
such that
T|_{G_{0}}=T_{0}
and
\left\|T\right\|=\left\|T_{0}\right\|
. If
\iota\colon\overline{G_{0}}\to G
is the inclusion operator, the metric extension property of
\ell_{\infty}(B_{G^{*}})
yields an operator
S\in\mathcal{L}(G,\ell_{\infty}(B_{F^{*}}))
so that
\iota_{F}\circ T=S\circ\iota
and
\left\|S\right\|=\left\|\iota_{F}\circ T\right\|
. Since
(T\circ g)(x)=T(g(x))=T_{0}(g(x))=f(x)
for all
x\in U
, we have
T\circ g=f
, and thus
\iota_{F}\circ f=\iota_{F}\circ T\circ g=S\circ\iota\circ g=S\circ g
.
Since
g\in\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}(U,G)
, the ideal property of
\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}
shows that
\iota_{F}\circ f\in\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}(U,\ell_{\infty}(B_{F^{*}}))
, that is,
f\in(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}(U,F)
with
\left\|f\right\|_{(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}}=\left\|\iota_{F}\circ f\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}\leq\left\|S\right\|\left\|g\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}\leq\left\|g\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}
. Passing to the infimum over all
G^{\prime}
s and
g^{\prime}
s as in
(ii)
, we conclude that
\left\|f\right\|_{(\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}}\leq\inf\{\left\|g\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}\}
.
∎

The combination of Corollary
1.2
and Theorem
1.4
immediately provides the next characterization of the injectivity of a normed weighted holomorphic ideal, that can be compared with its linear version
[
7
, Lemma 3.1]
and its polynomial version
[

, Theorem 3.4]
.

Corollary 1.5
.

Let
[\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}]
be a normed weighted holomorphic ideal. Then
[\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}]
is injective if, and only if, given complex Banach spaces
F,G
and mappings
f\in\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,F)
,
g\in\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}(U,G)
such that

\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}v(x_{i})f(x_{i})\right\|\leq\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}v(x_{i})g(x_{i})\right\|

for all

,
\lambda_{1},\ldots,\lambda_{n}\in\mathbb{C}
and

, then
f\in\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}(U,F)
and
\left\|f\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}=\inf\left\{\left\|g\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}\right\}
, where the infimum is taken over all complex Banach spaces
G
and all such mappings
g
.
\hfill\Box

1.3.
The injective hull of composition ideals of weighted holomorphic mappings

According to
[
9
, Definition 2.5]
, given a normed operator ideal
[\mathcal{I},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{I}}]
, a map
f\in\mathcal{H}(U,F)
belongs to the composition ideal
\mathcal{I}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,F)
if there exist a complex Banach space
G
, an operator
T\in\mathcal{I}(G,F)
and a map
g\in\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,G)
such that
f=T\circ g
. For any
f\in\mathcal{I}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,F)
, define

\left\|f\right\|_{\mathcal{I}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}=\inf\left\{\left\|T\right\|_{\mathcal{I}}\left\|g\right\|_{v}\right\},

where the infimum is extended over all such factorizations of
f
. By
[
9
, Proposition 2.6]
,
[\mathcal{I}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{I}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}]
is a normed weighted holomorphic ideal.

We now describe the injective hull of this ideal
[\mathcal{I}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{I}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}]
. Our approach requires some preliminaries about the linearization of weighted holomorphic maps.

Following
[
2
,
15
]
,
\mathcal{G}_{v}^{\infty}(U)
is the space of all linear functionals on
\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U)
whose restriction to
B_{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U)}
is continuous for the compact-open topology. The following result collects the properties of
\mathcal{G}_{v}^{\infty}(U)
that we will need later.

Theorem 1.6
.

[
2
,
6
,
15
,
20
]

Let
U
be an open set of a complex Banach space
E
and
v
be a weight on
U
.

(i)

\mathcal{G}_{v}^{\infty}(U)
is a closed subspace of
\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U)^{*}
, and the evaluation mapping
J_{v}\colon\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U)\to\mathcal{G}_{v}^{\infty}(U)^{*}
, given by
J_{v}(f)(\phi)=\phi(f)
for
\phi\in\mathcal{G}_{v}^{\infty}(U)
and
f\in\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U)
, is an isometric isomorphism.

(ii)

For each
x\in U
, the evaluation functional
\delta_{x}\colon\mathcal{H}^{\infty}_{v}(U)\to\mathbb{C}
, defined by
\delta_{x}(f)=f(x)
for
f\in\mathcal{H}^{\infty}_{v}(U)
, is in
\mathcal{G}^{\infty}_{v}(U)
.

(iii)

The mapping
\Delta_{v}\colon U\to\mathcal{G}_{v}^{\infty}(U)
given by
\Delta_{v}(x)=\delta_{x}
is in
\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,\mathcal{G}_{v}^{\infty}(U))
with
\left\|\Delta_{v}\right\|_{v}\leq 1
.

(iv)

B_{\mathcal{G}^{\infty}_{v}(U)}=\overline{\mathrm{aco}}(\mathrm{At}_{\mathcal{G}^{\infty}_{v}(U)})\subseteq\mathcal{H}^{\infty}_{v}(U)^{*}
and
\mathcal{G}_{v}^{\infty}(U)=\overline{\mathrm{lin}}(\mathrm{At}_{\mathcal{G}^{\infty}_{v}(U)})\subseteq\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U)^{*}
, where
\mathrm{At}_{\mathcal{G}^{\infty}_{v}(U)}=\{v(x)\delta_{x}\colon x\in U\}
.

(v)

For each
\phi\in\mathrm{lin}(\mathrm{At}_{\mathcal{G}^{\infty}_{v}(U)})
, we have

\left\|\phi\right\|=\inf\left\{\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left|\lambda_{i}\right|\colon\phi=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}v(x_{i})\delta_{x_{i}}\right\}.

(vi)

For every complex Banach space
F
and every mapping
f\in\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,F)
, there exists a unique operator
T_{f}\in\mathcal{L}(\mathcal{G}_{v}^{\infty}(U),F)
such that
T_{f}\circ\Delta_{v}=f
. Furthermore,
\left\|T_{f}\right\|=\left\|f\right\|_{v}
.

(vii)

For each
f\in\mathcal{H}^{\infty}_{v}(U,F)
, the mapping
f^{t}\colon F^{*}\to\mathcal{H}^{\infty}_{v}(U)
, defined by
f^{t}(y^{*})=y^{*}\circ f
for all
y^{*}\in F^{*}
, is in
\mathcal{L}(F^{*},\mathcal{H}^{\infty}_{v}(U))
with
||f^{t}||=\left\|f\right\|_{v}
and
f^{t}=J_{v}^{-1}\circ(T_{f})^{*}
, where
(T_{f})^{*}\colon F^{*}\to\mathcal{G}^{\infty}_{v}(U)^{*}
is the adjoint operator of
T_{f}
.

\hfill\qed

For
v=1_{U}
where
1_{U}(x)=1
for all
x\in U
, it is usual to write
\mathcal{H}^{\infty}(U,F)
(the Banach space of all bounded holomorphic mappings from
U
into
F
, under the supremum norm) instead of
\mathcal{H}^{\infty}_{v}(U,F)
,
\mathcal{H}^{\infty}(U)
rather than
\mathcal{H}^{\infty}(U,\mathbb{C})
and, following Mujica’s notation in
[
20
]
,
\mathcal{G}^{\infty}(U)
instead of
\mathcal{G}^{\infty}_{v}(U)
.

Proposition 1.7
.

Let
[\mathcal{I},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{I}}]
be an operator ideal. Then

[(\mathcal{I}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty})^{inj},\|\cdot\|_{(\mathcal{I}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty})^{inj}}]=[\mathcal{I}^{inj}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty},\|\cdot\|_{\mathcal{I}^{inj}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}].

In particular, the weighted holomorphic ideal
[\mathcal{I}^{inj}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty},\|\cdot\|_{\mathcal{I}^{inj}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}]
is injective.

Proof.

Let
F
be a complex Banach space and
f\in(\mathcal{I}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty})^{inj}(U,F)
. Hence
\iota_{F}\circ f\in\mathcal{I}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,\ell_{\infty}(B_{F^{*}}))
, and so
\iota_{F}\circ f=T\circ g
for some complex Banach space
G
, an operator
T\in\mathcal{I}(G,\ell_{\infty}(B_{F^{*}}))
and a map
g\in\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,G)
. By Theorem
1.6
, we can find two operators
T_{f}\in\mathcal{L}(\mathcal{G}^{\infty}_{v}(U),F)
and
T_{g}\in\mathcal{L}(\mathcal{G}^{\infty}_{v}(U),G)
with
\|T_{f}\|=\|f\|_{v}
and
\|T_{g}\|=\|g\|
such that
T_{f}\circ\Delta_{v}=f
and
T_{g}\circ\Delta_{v}=g
. Since
\mathcal{G}^{\infty}_{v}(U)=\overline{\mathrm{lin}}(\Delta_{v}(U))\subseteq\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U)^{*}
and

\iota_{F}\circ T_{f}\circ\Delta_{v}=\iota_{F}\circ f=T\circ g=T\circ T_{g}\circ\Delta_{v},

it follows that
\iota_{F}\circ T_{f}=T\circ T_{g}
, and thus
\iota_{F}\circ T_{f}\in\mathcal{I}(\mathcal{G}^{\infty}_{v}(U),\ell_{\infty}(B_{F^{*}}))
, that is,
T_{f}\in\mathcal{I}^{inj}(\mathcal{G}^{\infty}_{v}(U),F)
. Hence
f=T_{f}\circ\Delta_{v}\in\mathcal{I}^{inj}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,F)
. Moreover,

\left\|f\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{inj}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}\leq\left\|T_{f}\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{inj}}\|\Delta_{v}\|\leq\left\|T_{f}\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{inj}}=\left\|\iota_{F}\circ T_{f}\right\|_{\mathcal{I}}\leq\left\|T\right\|_{\mathcal{I}}\left\|T_{g}\right\|=\left\|T\right\|_{\mathcal{I}}\|g\|_{v},

and passing to the infimum over all the factorizations of
\iota_{F}\circ f
yields
\left\|f\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{inj}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}\leq\left\|\iota_{F}\circ f\right\|_{\mathcal{I}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}=\left\|f\right\|_{(\mathcal{I}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty})^{inj}}
.

Conversely, let
f\in\mathcal{I}^{inj}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,F)
. Hence
f=T\circ g
for some complex Banach space
G
,
T\in\mathcal{I}^{inj}(G,F)
and
g\in\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,G)
. Therefore
\iota_{F}\circ f=(\iota_{F}\circ T)\circ g\in\mathcal{I}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,\ell_{\infty}(B_{F^{*}})
, and thus
f\in(\mathcal{I}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty})^{inj}(U,F)
with

\left\|f\right\|_{(\mathcal{I}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty})^{inj}}=\left\|\iota_{F}\circ f\right\|_{\mathcal{I}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}=\left\|\iota_{F}\circ T\circ g\right\|_{\mathcal{I}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}\leq\left\|\iota_{F}\circ T\right\|_{\mathcal{I}}\|g\|_{v}=\left\|T\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{inj}}\|g\|_{v}.

Taking the infimum over all the factorizations of
f
, we conclude that
\left\|f\right\|_{(\mathcal{I}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty})^{inj}}\leq\left\|f\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{inj}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}
.
∎

From Proposition
1.7
and Corollary
1.2
, we deduce the following.

Corollary 1.8
.

Let
[\mathcal{I},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{I}}]
be an injective normed operator ideal. Then
[\mathcal{I}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{I}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}]
is an injective weighted holomorphic ideal.
∎

For
\mathcal{I}=\mathcal{F},\overline{\mathcal{F}},\mathcal{K},\mathcal{W},\mathcal{S},\mathcal{R},\mathcal{AS}
and Banach spaces
E,F
, we will denote by
\mathcal{I}(E,F)
the linear space of all finite-rank (approximable, compact, weakly compact, separable, Rosenthal, Asplund) bounded linear operators from
E
to
F
, respectively. The components
\mathcal{I}(E,F)
, equipped with the operator canonical norm

, generate a normed operator ideal (see
[

).

For a map
f\in\mathcal{H}(U,F)
, the v-range of

is the set

(vf)(U)=\left\{v(x)f(x)\colon x\in U\right\}\subseteq F.

Note that

belongs to

if and only if

is a norm-bounded subset of

. This motivates the following concepts.

Definition 1.9
.

Let
U
be an open set of a complex Banach space

, let

be a weight on

be a complex Banach space.

.

is a finite dimensional subspace of

.

For
\mathcal{I}=\mathcal{F},\overline{\mathcal{F}},\mathcal{K},\mathcal{W},\mathcal{S},\mathcal{R},\mathcal{AS}
,
\mathcal{H}_{v\mathcal{I}}^{\infty}(U,F)
stand for the linear space of all finite v-rank (resp., v-approximable, v-compact, v-weakly compact, v-separable, v-Rosenthal, v-Asplund) weighted holomorphic mappings from
U
into
F
.

The same proofs of Theorem 2.9 and Corollary 2.10 in
[

]
yield the following two results.

Theorem 1.10
.

Let
f\in\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,F)
and
\mathcal{I}=\mathcal{F},\overline{\mathcal{F}},\mathcal{K},\mathcal{W},\mathcal{S},\mathcal{R},\mathcal{AS}
. For the normed operator ideal
[\mathcal{I},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{I}}]
, the following are equivalent:

(i)

(ii)

Corollary 1.11
.

[\mathcal{H}_{v\mathcal{I}}^{\infty},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{v}]=[\mathcal{I}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{I}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}]

1.4.
The injective hull of dual ideals of weighted holomorphic mappings

Following
[
22
, Section 4.4]
, given a normed operator ideal
[\mathcal{I},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{I}}]
, the components

\mathcal{I}^{\mathrm{dual}}(E,F):=\left\{T\in\mathcal{L}(E,F)\colon T^{*}\in\mathcal{I}(F^{*},E^{*})\right\}

for any normed spaces
E
and
F
, endowed with the norm

\left\|T\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathrm{dual}}}=\left\|T^{*}\right\|_{\mathcal{I}}\qquad(T\in\mathcal{I}^{\mathrm{dual}}(E,F)),

define a normed operator ideal,
[\mathcal{I}^{\mathrm{dual}},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{d}}]
, called dual ideal of
\mathcal{I}
. Moreover,
[\mathcal{I},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{I}}]
is said to be symmetric and completely symmetric if
[\mathcal{I},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{I}}]\leq[\mathcal{I}^{\mathrm{dual}},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathrm{dual}}}]
and
[\mathcal{I},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{I}}]=[\mathcal{I}^{\mathrm{dual}},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathrm{dual}}}]
, respectively.

Based on the notion of transpose of a weighted holomorphic map (see Theorem
1.6
), we introduce the concept of dual weighted holomorphic ideal of an operator ideal
\mathcal{I}
.

Definition 1.14
.

Let
\mathcal{I}
be an operator ideal. For any open subset
U
of a complex Banach space
E
, any weight
v
on
U
and any complex Banach space
F
, we define

\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}\text{-}\mathrm{dual}}(U,F)=\{f\in\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,F)\colon f^{t}\in\mathcal{I}(F^{*},\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U))\}

If
[\mathcal{I},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{I}}]
is a normed operator ideal, we set

\|f\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}\text{-}\mathrm{dual}}}=\|f^{t}\|_{\mathcal{I}}\qquad(f\in\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}\text{-}\mathrm{dual}}(U,F)).

We now show that
[\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}\text{-}\mathrm{dual}},\|\cdot\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}\text{-}\mathrm{dual}}}]
is in fact a normed weighted holomorphic ideal.

Theorem 1.15
.

Let
\mathcal{I}
be an operator ideal. The following statements about a mapping
f\in\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,F)
are equivalent:

(i)

f
belongs to
\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}\text{-}\mathrm{dual}}(U,F)
.

(ii)

f
belongs to
\mathcal{I}^{\mathrm{dual}}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,F)
.

If in addition
(\mathcal{I},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{I}})
is a normed operator ideal, then

\left\|f\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}\text{-}\mathrm{dual}}}=\left\|f\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\text{dual}}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}\qquad(f\in\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}\text{-}\mathrm{dual}}(U,F)).

Proof.

(i)\Rightarrow(ii)
: Let
f\in\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}\text{-}\mathrm{dual}}(U,F)
. Then
f^{t}\in\mathcal{I}(F^{*},\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U))
. By Theorem
1.6
, we can take
T_{f}\in\mathcal{L}(\mathcal{G}_{v}^{\infty}(U),F)
such that
T_{f}\circ\Delta_{v}=f
and also
(T_{f})^{*}=J_{v}\circ f^{t}
. Hence
(T_{f})^{*}\in\mathcal{I}(F^{*},\mathcal{G}_{v}^{\infty}(U)^{*})
and therefore
T_{f}\in\mathcal{I}^{\mathrm{dual}}(\mathcal{G}_{v}^{\infty},F)
. Thus, by
[
9
, Theorem 2.7]
we have
f\in\mathcal{I}^{\mathrm{dual}}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,F)
with
\|f\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathrm{dual}}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}=\|T_{f}\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathrm{dual}}}
. Further,

\|f\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathrm{dual}}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}=\|T_{f}\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathrm{dual}}}=\|(T_{f})^{*}\|_{\mathcal{I}}=\|J_{v}\circ f^{t}\|_{\mathcal{I}}\leq\|J_{v}\|\|f^{t}\|_{\mathcal{I}}=\|f\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}\text{-}\mathrm{dual}}}.

(ii)\Rightarrow(i)
: Let
f\in\mathcal{I}^{\mathrm{dual}}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,F)
. Then there are a complex Banach space
G
, a map
g\in\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,G)
and an operator
T\in\mathcal{I}^{\text{dual}}(G,F)
such that
f=T\circ g
. Given
y^{*}\in F^{*}
, we have

f^{t}(y^{*})=(T\circ g)^{t}(y^{*})=y^{*}\circ(T\circ g)=(y^{*}\circ T)\circ g=T^{*}(y^{*})\circ g=g^{t}(T^{*}(y^{*}))=(g^{t}\circ T^{*})(y^{*}),

and thus
f^{t}=g^{t}\circ T^{*}
. Since
T^{*}\in\mathcal{I}(F^{*},G^{*})
and
g^{t}\in\mathcal{L}(G^{*},\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U))
, we obtain that
f^{t}\in\mathcal{I}(F^{*},\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U))
. Hence
f\in\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}\text{-}\mathrm{dual}}(U,F)
and since

\left\|f\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}\text{-}\mathrm{dual}}}=\left\|f^{t}\right\|_{\mathcal{I}}=\left\|g^{t}\circ T^{*}\right\|_{\mathcal{I}}\leq\left\|g^{t}\right\|\left\|T^{*}\right\|_{\mathcal{I}}=\|g\|_{v}\left\|T\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathrm{dual}}},

and taking the infimum over all representations
T\circ g
of
f
, we conclude that
\left\|f\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}\text{-}\mathrm{dual}}}\leq\left\|f\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathrm{dual}}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}
.
∎

Theorem
1.15
enables us to include the following description of the dual weighted holomorphic ideal of a completely symmetric normed operator ideal.

Corollary 1.16
.

[\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}\text{-}\mathrm{dual}},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}\text{-}\mathrm{dual}}}]=[\mathcal{I}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{I}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}]
whenever
[\mathcal{I},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{I}}]
is a completely symmetric normed operator ideal.
\hfill\square

The operator ideal

\hfill\square

On the injectivity property, we now can give the following.

If

Applying Theorem

1.5.
The closed injective hull of ideals of weighted holomorphic mappings

According to
[
22
, Section 4.2.1]
, given an operator ideal
\mathcal{I}
and Banach spaces
E,F
, an operator
T\in\mathcal{L}(E,F)
is in the closure of
\mathcal{I}(E,F)
in
(\mathcal{L}(E,F),\left\|\cdot\right\|)
, denoted by
\overline{\mathcal{I}}(E,F)
, if there exists a sequence
(T_{n})
in
\mathcal{I}(E,F)
such that
\lim_{n\to\infty}\left\|T_{n}-T\right\|=0
. In this way, the components
\overline{\mathcal{I}}(E,F)
define an operator ideal
\overline{\mathcal{I}}
.

This concept motivates the following in the setting of weighted holomorphic maps.

Definition 1.20
.

Let
U
be an open set of a complex Banach space
E
, let
v
be a weight on
U
and let
F
be a complex Banach space. Given a weighted holomorphic ideal
\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}
, a map
f\in\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,F)
is said to belong to the closure of
\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}(U,F)
in
(\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,F),\|\cdot\|_{v})
, and it is denoted by
f\in\overline{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}(U,F)
, if there exists a sequence
(f_{n})
in
\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}(U,F)
such that
\lim_{n\to\infty}\|f_{n}-f\|_{v}=0
.

It is easy to prove the following result.

Proposition 1.21
.

Let
\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}
be a weighted holomorphic ideal. Then
\overline{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}
is a weighted holomorphic ideal containing
\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}
, and it is called the closure of
\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}
.

We say that
\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}
is closed if
\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}=\overline{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}
, and we call closed injective hull of
\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}
to the injective hull of the ideal
\overline{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}
and it is denoted by
(\overline{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}})^{inj}
.
\hfill\qed

The closed injective hull of a weighted holomorphic ideal of composition type admits the following description.

Proposition 1.22
.

Let
[\mathcal{I},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{I}}]
be an operator ideal. Then

[(\overline{\mathcal{I}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj},\|\cdot\|_{(\overline{\mathcal{I}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}}]=[(\overline{\mathcal{I}})^{inj}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty},\|\cdot\|_{(\overline{\mathcal{I}})^{inj}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}].

In particular, the weighted holomorphic ideal
[(\overline{\mathcal{I}})^{inj}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty},\|\cdot\|_{(\overline{\mathcal{I}})^{inj}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}]
is injective.

Proof.

We claim that
\overline{\mathcal{I}}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,F)=\overline{\mathcal{I}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}(U,F)
.
Indeed, note first that
\overline{\mathcal{I}}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,F)
is closed: let
f\in\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,F)
and assume that
(f_{n})_{n\in\mathbb{N}}
is a sequence in
\overline{\mathcal{I}}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,F)
such that
\|f_{n}-f\|_{v}\to 0
as
n\to\infty
; since
T_{f_{n}}\in\overline{\mathcal{I}}(\mathcal{G}_{v}^{\infty}(U),F)
by Theorem
1.10
and
\left\|T_{f_{n}}-T_{f}\right\|=\|f_{n}-f\|_{v}
for all
n\in\mathbb{N}
by Theorem
1.6
, we have that
T_{f}\in\overline{\mathcal{I}}(\mathcal{G}_{v}^{\infty}(U),F)
, and thus
f\in\overline{\mathcal{I}}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,F)
again by Theorem
1.10
.

Now, from
\mathcal{I}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,F)\subseteq\overline{\mathcal{I}}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,F)
, we infer that
\overline{\mathcal{I}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}(U,F)\subseteq\overline{\mathcal{I}}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,F)
. For the converse, take
f\in\overline{\mathcal{I}}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,F)
; hence
T=T\circ g
for some complex Banach space
G
,
T\in\overline{\mathcal{I}}(G,F)
and
g\in\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,G)
; thus we can find a sequence
(T_{n})_{n\in\mathbb{N}}
in
\mathcal{I}(G,F)
such that
\left\|T_{n}-T\right\|\to 0
as
n\to\infty
, and since
\|T_{n}\circ g-T\circ g\|_{v}=\|(T_{n}-T)\circ g\|_{v}\leq\left\|T_{n}-T\right\|\|g\|_{v}
for all
n\in\mathbb{N}
, we deduce that
f\in\overline{\mathcal{I}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}(U,F)
, and this proves our claim.

Now, using Proposition
1.7
, we conclude that

[(\overline{\mathcal{I}})^{inj}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty},\|\cdot\|_{(\overline{\mathcal{I}})^{inj}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}]=[(\overline{\mathcal{I}}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty})^{inj},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{(\overline{\mathcal{I}}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty})^{inj}}]=[(\overline{\mathcal{I}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj},\|\cdot\|_{(\overline{\mathcal{I}\circ\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}})^{inj}}].

∎

In terms of an Ehrling-type inequality

Theorem 1.23

For a weighted holomorphic ideal
\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}

(ii)

for all
n\in\mathbb{N}
,
\lambda_{1},\ldots,\lambda_{n}\in\mathbb{C}
and
x_{1},\ldots,x_{n}\in U

Proof.

(i)\Rightarrow(ii)

f\in(\overline{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}})^{inj}(U,F)
and
\varepsilon>0

\iota_{F}\circ f\in\overline{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}(U,\ell_{\infty}(B_{F^{*}}))

\displaystyle\leq\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}v(x_{i})g_{\varepsilon}(x_{i})\right\|+\varepsilon\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left|\lambda_{i}\right|

\displaystyle=\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}v(x_{i})T_{g_{\varepsilon}}(\delta_{x_{i}})\right\|+\varepsilon\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left|\lambda_{i}\right|

\displaystyle=\left\|T_{g_{\varepsilon}}(\phi)\right\|+\varepsilon\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left|\lambda_{i}\right|,

and taking the infimum over all the representations of

for all

In the case that the weighted holomorphic ideal
\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}

Corollary 1.24
.

Let
[\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}},\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}]
be a Banach weighted holomorphic ideal and let
f\in\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}(U,F)
. The following are equivalent:

(i)

f
belongs to
(\overline{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}})^{inj}(U,F)
.

(ii)

There exists a complex Banach space
G
, a mapping
g\in\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}(U,G)
and a function
N\colon\mathbb{R}^{+}\to\mathbb{R}^{+}
such that

\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}v(x_{i})f(x_{i})\right\|\leq N(\varepsilon)\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}v(x_{i})g(x_{i})\right\|+\varepsilon\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left|\lambda_{i}\right|

for all
n\in\mathbb{N}
,
\lambda_{1},\ldots,\lambda_{n}\in\mathbb{C}
,
x_{1},\ldots,x_{n}\in U
, and
\varepsilon>0
.

Proof.

In view of Theorem
1.23
, all we need to show is
(i)\Rightarrow(ii)
. Let
f\in(\overline{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}})^{inj}(U,F)
. By Theorem
1.23
, for each
m\in\mathbb{N}
, there are a complex Banach space
G_{m}
and a map
g_{m}\in\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}(U,G_{m})

\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}v(x_{i})f(x_{i})\right\|\leq\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}v(x_{i})g_{m}(x_{i})\right\|+\frac{1}{2^{m}}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left|\lambda_{i}\right|

for all

\lambda_{1},\ldots,\lambda_{n}\in\mathbb{C}

\sum_{k=1}^{m}\frac{\left\|I_{k}\circ g_{k}\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}}{2^{k}\left\|g_{k}\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}}\leq\sum_{k=1}^{m}\frac{\left\|I_{k}\right\|\left\|g_{k}\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}}{2^{k}\left\|g_{k}\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}}\leq\sum_{k=1}^{m}\frac{1}{2^{k}}\leq 1,

\displaystyle\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}v(x_{i})f(x_{i})\right\|

\displaystyle\leq 2^{m}\left\|g_{m}\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{n}\frac{\lambda_{i}v(x_{i})}{2^{m}\left\|g_{m}\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}}g_{m}(x_{i})\right\|+\frac{1}{2^{m}}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left|\lambda_{i}\right|

\displaystyle\leq 2^{m}\left\|g_{m}\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{n}\frac{\lambda_{i}v(x_{i})}{2^{m}\left\|g_{m}\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}}g_{m}(x_{i})\right\|+\frac{1}{2^{m}}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left|\lambda_{i}\right|

\displaystyle=2^{m}\left\|g_{m}\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}v(x_{i})g(x_{i})\right\|+\frac{1}{2^{m}}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left|\lambda_{i}\right|

for all
n\in\mathbb{N}
,
\lambda_{1},\ldots,\lambda_{n}\in\mathbb{C}
,
x_{1},\ldots,x_{n}\in U
and
m\in\mathbb{N}
. Finally, this inequality yields the inequality in the statement defining
N\colon\mathbb{R}^{+}\to\mathbb{R}^{+}
by

N(\varepsilon)=\left\{\begin{array}[]{lll}2\left\|g_{1}\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}&\text{if}&\varepsilon>1,\\
&&\\
2^{m}\left\|g_{m}\right\|_{\mathcal{I}^{\mathcal{H}_{v}^{\infty}}}&\text{if}&2^{-m}<\varepsilon\leq 2^{-m+1},\;m\in\mathbb{N}.\end{array}\right.

∎
