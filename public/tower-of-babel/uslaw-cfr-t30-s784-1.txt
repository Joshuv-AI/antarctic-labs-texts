30 C.F.R. § 784.1 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:30:784.1
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-784.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 784 (Underground Mining Permit Applications—Minimum Requirements For Reclamation And Operation Plan). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part provides the minimum requirements for the Secretary's approval of regulatory program provisions for the mining operations and reclamation plans portions of applications for permits for underground mining activities, except to the extent that different requirements for those plans are established under 30 CFR part 785.
