41 C.F.R. § 102-118.20 — -118.20 Ordering transportation.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-118 (118—Transportation Payment And Audit). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Ordering methods are prescribed in 41 CFR part 102-117 for freight, household goods, and small parcels. Ordering transportation for travel is prescribed in the Federal Travel Regulation in 41 CFR subtitle F.
