39 C.F.R. § 3001.115 — Additional limitation on acceptance of anything of value.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3001.115
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3001.115
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3001 (Employee Standards Of Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Regardless of 5 CFR 2635.203(b)(7), a Commission employee may not accept a gift from the Postal Service, unless another exception or exclusion to 5 CFR 2635.203 applies or a waiver is granted by the DAEO.
