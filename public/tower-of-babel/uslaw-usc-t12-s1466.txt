12 U.S.C. § 1466 — Applicability
Citation: us:usc:12:1466
Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 12 (Savings Associations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of this chapter shall apply to the United States and to Puerto Rico, Guam, and the Virgin Islands.
