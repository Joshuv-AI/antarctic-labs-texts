42 C.F.R. § 457.530 — General cost-sharing protection for lower income children.
Citation: us:cfr:42:457.530
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-457.530
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 457 (Allotments And Grants To States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The State may vary premiums, deductibles, coinsurance, copayments or any other cost sharing based on household income only in a manner that does not favor children from families with higher income over children from families with lower income.
