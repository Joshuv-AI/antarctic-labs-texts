22 U.S.C. § 1642k — Time limitation on completion of affairs of Commission
Citation: us:usc:22:1642k
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1642k
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 21 (Settlement Of International Claims); Subchapter IV (Claims Against Czechoslovakia). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commission shall complete its affairs in connection with the settlement of claims pursuant to this subchapter not later than three years following the final date for the filing of claims as provided in section 1642j of this title or following the enactment of legislation making appropriations to the Commission for payment of administrative expenses incurred in carrying out its functions under this subchapter, whichever date is later.
