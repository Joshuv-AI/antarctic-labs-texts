36 C.F.R. § 223.46 — Adjustment of contract termination date.
Citation: us:cfr:36:223.46
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-223.46
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 223 (Sale And Disposal Of National Forest System Timber). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Timber sale contracts may provide for adjustment of the termination date to provide additional time to compensate for delays in road construction and timber removal due to those causes beyond the purchaser's control, which may include but are not limited to acts of God, acts of the public enemy, acts of the Government, labor disputes, fires, insurrections or floods.
