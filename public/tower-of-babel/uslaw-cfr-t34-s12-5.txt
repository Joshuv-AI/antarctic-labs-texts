34 C.F.R. § 12.5 — Who may apply for surplus Federal real property?
Citation: us:cfr:34:12.5
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-12.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 12 (Disposal And Utilization Of Surplus Federal Real Property For Educational Purposes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following entities may apply for surplus Federal real property:
(a) A State.
(b) A political subdivision or instrumentality of a State.
(c) A tax-supported institution.
(d) A nonprofit institution.
(e) Any combination of these entities.
