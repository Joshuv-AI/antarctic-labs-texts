24 C.F.R. § 180.450 — Resolution of charge or notice of proposed adverse action.
Citation: us:cfr:24:180.450
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Equal Opportunity, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-180.450
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 180 (Consolidated Hud Hearing Procedures For Civil Rights Matters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At any time before a final decision is issued, the parties may submit to the ALJ an agreement resolving the charge or notice of proposed adverse action. A charge under the Fair Housing Act can only be resolved with the agreement of the aggrieved person on whose behalf the charge was issued. If the agreement is in the public interest, the ALJ shall accept it by issuing an initial decision and consent order based on the agreement.
