17 C.F.R. § 156.4 — Disclosure of Broker Association Membership.
Citation: us:cfr:17:156.4
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-156.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 156 (Broker Associations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each contract market shall make available to the public generally and upon request a list of all registered broker associations which identifies for each such association the name of each person who is a member or otherwise has a direct beneficial interest in the association. This list shall be updated at least semi-annually.
