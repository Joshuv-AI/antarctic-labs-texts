22 C.F.R. § 212.17 — Rights and services qualified by the FOIA statute.
Citation: us:cfr:22:212.17
Creator: Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-212.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 212 (Public Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this subpart shall be construed to entitle any person, as a right, to any service or to the disclosure of any record to which such person is not entitled under the FOIA.
