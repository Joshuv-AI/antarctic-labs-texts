20 U.S.C. § 2398 — Limitation on Federal regulations
Citation: us:usc:20:2398
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section2398
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 44 (Career And Technical Education); Subchapter II (General Provisions); Part A (Federal Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may issue regulations under this chapter only to the extent necessary to administer and ensure compliance with the specific requirements of this chapter.
