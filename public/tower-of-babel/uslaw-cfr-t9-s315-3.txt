9 C.F.R. § 315.3 — Disposal of products passed for cooking if not handled according to this part.
Citation: us:cfr:9:315.3
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-315.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 315 (Rendering Or Other Disposal Of Carcasses And Parts Passed For Cooking). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Products passed for cooking if not handled and processed in accordance with the provisions of this part, shall be disposed of in accordance with § 314.1 or § 314.3 of this subchapter.
