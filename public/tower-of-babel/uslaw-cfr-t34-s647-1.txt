34 C.F.R. § 647.1 — What is the Ronald E. McNair Postbaccalaureate Achievement Program?
Citation: us:cfr:34:647.1
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-647.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 647 (Ronald E. Mcnair Postbaccalaureate Achievement Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Ronald E. McNair Postbaccalaureate Achievement Program—referred to in these regulations as the McNair program—awards grants to institutions of higher education for projects designed to provide disadvantaged college students with effective preparation for doctoral study.
