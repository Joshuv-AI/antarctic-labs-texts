DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice: 12892]


Determination Pursuant to Section 451 of the Foreign Assistance 
Act of 1961

    Pursuant to section 451 of the Foreign Assistance Act of 1961 (the 
``Act'') (22 U.S.C. 2261) and section 1-100(a)(1) of Executive Order 
12163, I hereby authorize, notwithstanding any other provision of law, 
the use of up to $109,000,000 made available to carry out provisions of 
the Act (other than the provisions of chapter 1 of part I of the Act) 
to provide assistance authorized by part I of the Act to support the 
Armenia-Azerbaijan peace deal.
    This Determination and the accompanying Memorandum of Justification 
shall be promptly reported to the Congress. This Determination shall be 
published in the Federal Register.

    Dated: September 12, 2025.
Marco Rubio,
Secretary of State.
