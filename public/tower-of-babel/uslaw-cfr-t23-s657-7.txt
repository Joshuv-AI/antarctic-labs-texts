23 C.F.R. § 657.7 — Objective.
Citation: us:cfr:23:657.7
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-657.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 657 (Certification Of Size And Weight Enforcement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The objective of this regulation is the development and operation by each State of an enforcement process which identifies vehicles of excessive size and weight and provides a systematic approach to eliminate violations and thus improve conditions.
