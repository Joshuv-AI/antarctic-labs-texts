46 C.F.R. § 249.3 — Amounts of insurance.
Citation: us:cfr:46:249.3
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-249.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 249 (Approval Of Underwriters For Marine Hull Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

MARAD will inform the owner of each vessel that is subsidized or covered by vessel obligation guarantees, prior to initial placement and at least annually thereafter, of the minimum amount of insurance required to be placed on the vessel.
