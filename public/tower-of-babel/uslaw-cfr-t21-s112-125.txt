21 C.F.R. § 112.125 — What requirements apply to equipment that is subject to this subpart used in the transport of covered produce?
Citation: us:cfr:21:112.125
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-112.125
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 112 (Standards For The Growing, Harvesting, Packing, And Holding Of Produce For Human Consumption). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Equipment that is subject to this subpart that you use to transport covered produce must be:
(a) Adequately clean before use in transporting covered produce; and
(b) Adequate for use in transporting covered produce.
