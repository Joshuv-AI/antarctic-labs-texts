32 C.F.R. § 37.855 — How should I handle protected data?
Citation: us:cfr:32:37.855
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-37.855
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 37 (Technology Investment Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Prior to releasing or disclosing data marked with a restrictive legend (as described in § 37.850) to third parties, you should require those parties to agree in writing that they will:
(a) Use the data only for governmental purposes; and
(b) Not release or disclose the data without the permission of the licensor (i.e., the recipient).
