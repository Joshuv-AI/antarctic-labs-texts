49 U.S.C. § 60502 — Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
Citation: us:usc:49:60502
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section60502
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Chapter 605 (Interstate Commerce Regulation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission has the duties and powers related to the establishment of a rate or charge for the transportation of oil by pipeline or the valuation of that pipeline that were vested on October 1, 1977, in the Interstate Commerce Commission or an officer or component of the Interstate Commerce Commission.
