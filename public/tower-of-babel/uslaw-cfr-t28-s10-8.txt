28 C.F.R. § 10.8 — Information to be kept current.
Citation: us:cfr:28:10.8
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-10.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 10 (Registration Of Certain Organizations Carrying On Activities Within The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A supplemental statement must be filed with the Attorney General within thirty days after the expiration of each period of six months succeeding the original filing of a registration statement. Each supplemental statement must contain information and documents as may be necessary to make information and documents previously filed accurate and current with respect to the preceding six months' period.
