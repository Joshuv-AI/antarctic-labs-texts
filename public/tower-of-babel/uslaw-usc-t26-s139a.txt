26 U.S.C. § 139A — Federal subsidies for prescription drug plans
Citation: us:usc:26:139A
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section139A
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 1 (Normal Taxes And Surtaxes); Subchapter B (Computation Of Taxable Income); Part III (Items Specifically Excluded From Gross Income). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Gross income shall not include any special subsidy payment received under section 1860D–22 of the Social Security Act.
