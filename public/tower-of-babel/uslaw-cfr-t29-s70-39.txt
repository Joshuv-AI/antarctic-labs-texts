29 C.F.R. § 70.39 — Statutes specifically providing for setting of fees.
Citation: us:cfr:29:70.39
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-70.39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 70 (Production Or Disclosure Of Information Or Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart will not apply to fees charged under any statute, other than the FOIA, that specifically requires an agency to set and collect fees for particular types of records.
