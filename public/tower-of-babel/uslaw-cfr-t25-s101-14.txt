25 C.F.R. § 101.14 — Maturity.
Citation: us:cfr:25:101.14
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-101.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 101 (Loans To Indians From The Revolving Loan Fund). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The maturity of any United States direct loan shall not exceed thirty years. Loans made will be scheduled for repayment at the earliest possible date consistent with the purpose of the loan and the repayment capacity of the borrower.
