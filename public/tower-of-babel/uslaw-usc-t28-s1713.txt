28 U.S.C. § 1713 — Protection against loss by class members
Citation: us:usc:28:1713
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1713
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part V (Procedure); Chapter 114 (Class Actions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The court may approve a proposed settlement under which any class member is obligated to pay sums to class counsel that would result in a net loss to the class member only if the court makes a written finding that nonmonetary benefits to the class member substantially outweigh the monetary loss.
