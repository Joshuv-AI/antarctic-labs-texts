2 U.S.C. § 6134 — Compensation and appointment of employees by Senate Majority and Minority Whips
Citation: us:usc:2:6134
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section6134
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 61 (Senate Leadership); Subchapter III (Majority And Minority Leaders And Whips). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Effective April 1, 1977, the Majority Whip and the Minority Whip are each authorized to appoint and fix the compensation of such employees as they deem appropriate: Provided, That the gross compensation paid to such employees shall not exceed $111,100 each fiscal year for each Whip.
