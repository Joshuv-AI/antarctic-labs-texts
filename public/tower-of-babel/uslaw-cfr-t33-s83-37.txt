33 C.F.R. § 83.37 — Distress signals (Rule 37).
Citation: us:cfr:33:83.37
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-83.37
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 83 (Navigation Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a vessel is in distress and requires assistance she shall use or exhibit the signals described in Annex IV to these Rules (33 CFR part 87).
