36 C.F.R. § 13.1240 — Unattended property.
Citation: us:cfr:36:13.1240
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-13.1240
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 13 (National Park System Units In Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Leaving property, other than motorboats and planes, unattended for any length of time within the BCDA is prohibited, except at the Brooks Lodge Porch, Brooks Campground, or designated equipment caches as posted at the Brooks Camp Visitor Center.
