42 C.F.R. § 512.470 — Waivers.
Citation: us:cfr:42:512.470
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-512.470
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 512 (Standard Provisions For Mandatory Innovation Center Models And Specific Provisions For Certain Models). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

CMS waives the requirements of sections 1881(b), 1833(a), 1833(b), and 1851(i)(2) of the Act, and 42 CFR 422.322(c) only to the extent necessary to make the payments under the IOTA Model described in this subpart.
