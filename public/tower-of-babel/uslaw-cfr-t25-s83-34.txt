25 C.F.R. § 83.34 — What notice of the proposed finding will OFA provide?
Citation: us:cfr:25:83.34
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-83.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 83 (Procedures For Federal Acknowledgment Of Indian Tribes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to publishing notice of the proposed finding in the Federal Register, OFA will:
(a) Provide copies of the proposed finding and any supporting reports to the petitioner and those listed in § 83.22(d); and
(b) Publish the proposed finding and reports on the OFA Web site.
