22 C.F.R. § 146.410 — Comparable facilities.
Citation: us:cfr:22:146.410
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-146.410
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 146 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Sex In Education Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A recipient may provide separate toilet, locker room, and shower facilities on the basis of sex, but such facilities provided for students of one sex shall be comparable to such facilities provided for students of the other sex.
