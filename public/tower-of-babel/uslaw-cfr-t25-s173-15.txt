25 C.F.R. § 173.15 — Permittee subject to State law.
Citation: us:cfr:25:173.15
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-173.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 173 (Concessions, Permits And Leases On Lands Withdrawn Or Acquired In Connection With Indian Irrigation Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The holder of any permit issued under this part shall be subject to and abide by the laws and regulations of the United States and State laws if applicable to the conduct of the particular business or activity conducted by the permittee. Violations of this section shall render the permit void but shall not release the permittee from any obligations arising thereunder.
