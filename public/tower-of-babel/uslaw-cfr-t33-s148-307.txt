33 C.F.R. § 148.307 — Who may consult with the Commandant (CG-5P) and MARAD on developing the proposed conditions of a license?
Citation: us:cfr:33:148.307
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-148.307
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 148 (Deepwater Ports: General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Federal agencies, the Adjacent Coastal States, and the owner of the deepwater port may consult with the Commandant (CG-5P) and MARAD on the conditions of the license being developed under 33 U.S.C. 1503(e).
