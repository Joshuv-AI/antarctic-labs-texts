33 C.F.R. § 154.730 — Persons in charge: Evidence of designation.
Citation: us:cfr:33:154.730
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-154.730
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 154 (Facilities Transferring Oil Or Hazardous Material In Bulk). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each person in charge shall carry evidence of his designation as a person in charge when he is engaged in transfer operations unless such evidence is immediately available at the facility.
