10 C.F.R. § 140.8 — Specific exemptions.
Citation: us:cfr:10:140.8
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-140.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 140 (Financial Protection Requirements And Indemnity Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission may, upon application of any interested person or upon its own initiative, grant such exemptions from the requirements of the regulations in this part as it determines are authorized by law and are otherwise in the public interest.
