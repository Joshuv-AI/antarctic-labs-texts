18 C.F.R. § 1b.15 — b.15 Non-compliance with compulsory processes.
Citation: us:cfr:18:1b.15
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-1b.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 1b (PART 1b—RULES RELATING TO INVESTIGATIONS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In cases of failure to comply with Commission compulsory processes, appropriate action may be initiated by the Commission or the Attorney General, including but not limited to actions for enforcement or the imposition of penalties.
