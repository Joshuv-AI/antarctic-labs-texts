43 U.S.C. § 15 — Issuance and signing of patents by Secretary of the Interior; delegation of authority; notice
Citation: us:usc:43:15
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section15
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 1 (Bureau Of Land Management). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All patents for public lands shall be issued and signed by the Secretary of the Interior in the name of the United States: Provided, That the Secretary may delegate his authority under this section to officers or employees of the Department of the Interior, but notice of any such delegation shall be given by publication in the Federal Register.
