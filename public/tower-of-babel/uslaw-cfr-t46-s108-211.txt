46 C.F.R. § 108.211 — Miscellaneous accommodation spaces.
Citation: us:cfr:46:108.211
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-108.211
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 108 (Design And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each unit must have enough facilities for personnel to wash their own clothes, including at least one tub or sink that has hot and cold running water.
(b) Each unit must have enough equipment or space for the personnel to dry their own clothes.
(c) Each unit must have an accommodation space that can be used for recreation.
