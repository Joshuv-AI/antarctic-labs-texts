7 C.F.R. § 1150.109 — Qualified program.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1150.109
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Milk), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1150.109
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1150 (Dairy Promotion Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Qualified program means any dairy product promotion, research or nutrition education program which is certified as a qualified program pursuant to § 1150.153.
