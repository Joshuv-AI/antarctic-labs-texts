7 C.F.R. § 984.348 — Payment of assessments.
Citation: us:cfr:7:984.348
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-984.348
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 984 (Walnuts Grown In California). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each handler shall pay assessments on walnut receipts reported by the handler pursuant to § 984.473(a) in three installments, invoiced by the Board, on January 31, April 30, and July 31 of each marketing year.
(b) Each handler shall pay assessments on walnut receipts reported by the handler pursuant to § 984.473(b), as requested by the Board, on demand.
