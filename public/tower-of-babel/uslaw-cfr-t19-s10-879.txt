19 C.F.R. § 10.879 — Indirect materials.
Citation: us:cfr:19:10.879
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-10.879
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 10 (Articles Conditionally Free, Subject To A Reduced Rate, Etc.). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Indirect materials are to be disregarded in determining whether a good qualifies as an originating good under § 10.873 of this subpart and General Note 31, HTSUS, except that the cost of such indirect materials may be included in meeting the value-content requirement specified in § 10.873(b) of this subpart.
