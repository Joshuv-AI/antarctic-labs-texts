40 C.F.R. § 1090.165 — Retailers and WPCs.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1090.165
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1090.165
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1090 (Regulation Of Fuels, Fuel Additives, And Regulated Blendstocks). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A retailer or WPC must comply with the requirements of this section.
(a) Gasoline and diesel fuel standards. A retailer or WPC must comply with the applicable requirements of subparts C and D of this part.
(b) Labeling. A retailer or WPC that dispenses fuels requiring a label under this part must display fuel labels under subpart P of this part.
(c) Fuels produced through fuel dispensers. A retailer or WPC that produces gasoline (e.g., E15) through a fuel dispenser with anything other than PCG and DFE is also a blending manufacturer and must comply with the applicable requirements in § 1090.105.
