29 C.F.R. § 500.266 — Responsibility of the Office of Administrative Law Judges.
Citation: us:cfr:29:500.266
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-500.266
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 500 (Migrant And Seasonal Agricultural Worker Protection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon receipt of the Administrative Review Board's (Board) Notice of Intent to Modify or Vacate a Decision and Order of an Administrative Law Judge, the Chief Administrative Law Judge shall, within fifteen (15) days, index, certify, and forward a copy of the complete hearing record to the Board.
