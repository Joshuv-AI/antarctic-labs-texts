45 C.F.R. § 1321.105 — Modification during major disaster declaration or public health emergency.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1321.105
Creator: Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1321.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1321 (Grants To State And Community Programs On Aging). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Assistant Secretary for Aging retains the right to modify the requirements described in these regulations pursuant to a major disaster declaration or public health emergency.
