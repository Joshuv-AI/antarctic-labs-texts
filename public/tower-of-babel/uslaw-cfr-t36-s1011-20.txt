36 C.F.R. § 1011.20 — Will the Presidio Trust's failure to comply with these regulations be a defense to a debt?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1011.20
Creator: Presidio Trust
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1011.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1011 (Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No, the failure of the Presidio Trust to comply with any standard in the FCCS, these regulations or such other procedures of the Presidio Trust will not be available to any debtor as a defense.
