10 U.S.C. § 711 — Senior members of Military Staff Committee of United Nations: appointment
Citation: us:usc:10:711
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section711
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 41 (Special Appointments, Assignments, Details, And Duties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, may appoint an officer of the Army, an officer of the Navy or the Marine Corps, and an officer of the Air Force or the Space Force, as senior members of the Military Staff Committee of the United Nations.
