34 C.F.R. § 81.10 — Ex parte communications.
Citation: us:cfr:34:81.10
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-81.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 81 (General Education Provisions Act—Enforcement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A party to, or other participant in, a case may not communicate with an ALJ on any fact in issue in the case or on any matter relevant to the merits of the case unless the parties are given notice and an opportunity to participate.
