10 C.F.R. § 35.2433 — Records of decay of strontium-90 sources for ophthalmic treatments.
Citation: us:cfr:10:35.2433
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-35.2433
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 35 (Medical Use Of Byproduct Material). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A licensee shall maintain a record of the activity of a strontium-90 source required by § 35.433 for the life of the source.
(b) The record must include—
(1) The date and initial activity of the source as determined under § 35.432; and
(2) For each decay calculation, the date and the source activity as determined under § 35.433.
