50 C.F.R. § 25.42 — Permits required to be exhibited on request.
Citation: us:cfr:50:25.42
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-25.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 25 (Administrative Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person on a national wildlife refuge shall upon request by any authorized official exhibit the required Federal or State permit or license authorizing their presence and activity on the area and shall furnish such other information for identification purposes as may be requested.
