46 C.F.R. § 169.746 — Fuel shutoff valves.
Citation: us:cfr:46:169.746
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-169.746
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 169 (Sailing School Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each remote fuel shutoff station must be marked in at least 1-inch letters indicating purpose of the valves and direction of operation.
