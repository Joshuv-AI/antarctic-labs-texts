10 U.S.C. § 9655 — Arms and ammunition: agencies and departments of United States
Citation: us:usc:10:9655
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9655
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Procurement); Chapter 971 (Issue Of Serviceable Material Other Than To Armed Forces). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Whenever required for the protection of public money and property, the Secretary of the Air Force may lend arms and their accouterments, and issue ammunition, to a department or independent agency of the United States, upon request of its head. Property lent or issued under this subsection may be delivered to an officer of the department or agency designated by the head thereof, and that officer shall account for the property to the Secretary of the Air Force. Property lent or issued under this subsection and not properly expended shall be returned when it is no longer needed.
(b)
The department or agency to which property is lent or issued under subsection (a) shall transfer funds to the credit of the Department of the Air Force to cover the costs of—
(1)
ammunition issued;
(2)
replacing arms and accouterments that have been lost or destroyed or cannot be repaired;
(3)
repairing arms and accouterments returned to the Department of the Air Force; and
(4)
making and receiving shipments by the Department of the Air Force.
