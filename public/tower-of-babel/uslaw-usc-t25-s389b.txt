25 U.S.C. § 389b — Elimination to permanently nonirrigable lands
Citation: us:usc:25:389b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section389b
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 11 (Irrigation Of Allotted Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Where the Secretary finds that any such lands are permanently nonirrigable he may, with the consent of the landowner, eliminate such lands from the project.
