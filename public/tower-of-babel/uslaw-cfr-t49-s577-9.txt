49 C.F.R. § 577.9 — Conformity to statutory requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:49:577.9
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-577.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 577 (Defect And Noncompliance Notification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A notification that does not conform to the requirements of this part is a violation of the Act.
