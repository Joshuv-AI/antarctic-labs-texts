41 U.S.C. § 3509 — Notification of violations of Federal criminal law or overpayments
Citation: us:usc:41:3509
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title41-section3509
Context: United States Code, Title 41 (Public Contracts); Chapter 35 (Truthful Cost Or Pricing Data). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Definition.—
In this section, the term “covered contract” means any contract in an amount greater than $5,000,000 and more than 120 days in duration.
(b) Federal Acquisition Regulation.—
The Federal Acquisition Regulation shall include, pursuant to FAR Case 2007–006 (as published at 72 Fed. Reg. 64019, November 14, 2007) or any follow-on FAR case, provisions that require timely notification by Federal contractors of violations of Federal criminal law or overpayments in connection with the award or performance of covered contracts or subcontracts, including those performed outside the United States and those for commercial products or commercial services.
