18 C.F.R. § 4.400 — Applicability and purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:18:4.400
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-4.400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 4 (Licenses, Permits, Exemptions, And Determination Of Project Costs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part implements section 30(a) of the Federal Power Act, as amended, and provides procedures for obtaining a determination from the Commission that the facility to be constructed is a qualifying conduit hydropower facility, as defined in § 4.30(b)(26), and thus, is not required to be licensed under Part I of the FPA.
