32 C.F.R. § 188.4 — Policy.
Citation: us:cfr:32:188.4
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-188.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 188 (Dod Environmental Laboratory Accreditation Program (Elap)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is DoD policy, in accordance with DoD Instruction 4715.15, to implement the DoD ELAP for the collection of definitive data in support of the Defense Environmental Restoration Program (DERP) at all DoD operations, activities, and installations, including government-owned, contractor-operated facilities and formerly used defense sites.
