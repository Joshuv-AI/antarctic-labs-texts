10 C.F.R. § 35.2404 — Records of surveys after source implant and removal.
Citation: us:cfr:10:35.2404
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-35.2404
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 35 (Medical Use Of Byproduct Material). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A licensee shall maintain a record of the surveys required by §§ 35.404 and 35.604 for 3 years. Each record must include the date and results of the survey, the survey instrument used, and the name of the individual who made the survey.
