51 U.S.C. § 31501 — Orbital debris
Citation: us:usc:51:31501
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section31501
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 315 (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator, in conjunction with the heads of other Federal agencies, shall take steps to develop or acquire technologies that will enable the Administration to decrease the risks associated with orbital debris.
