18 C.F.R. § 342.1 — General rule.
Citation: us:cfr:18:342.1
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-342.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 342 (Oil Pipeline Rate Methodologies And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each carrier subject to the jurisdiction of the Commission under the Interstate Commerce Act:
(a) Must establish its initial rates subject to such Act pursuant to § 342.2; and
(b) Must make any change in existing rates pursuant to § 342.3 or § 342.4, whichever is applicable, unless directed otherwise by the Commission.
