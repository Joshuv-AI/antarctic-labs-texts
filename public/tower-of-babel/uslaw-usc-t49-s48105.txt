49 U.S.C. § 48105 — Weather reporting services
Citation: us:usc:49:48105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section48105
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part C (Financing); Chapter 481 (Airport And Airway Trust Fund Authorizations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To sustain the aviation weather reporting programs of the Federal Aviation Administration, the Secretary of Transportation may expend from amounts available under section 48104 of this title not more than the following amounts:
(1)
for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1993, $35,596,000.
(2)
for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1994, $37,800,000.
(3)
for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1995, $39,000,000.
(4)
$39,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2019 through 2023.
(5)
$60,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2024 through 2028.
