30 C.F.R. § 707.11 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:30:707.11
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-707.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 707 (Exemption For Coal Extraction Incident To Government-Financed Highway Or Other Construction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Coal extraction which is an incidental part of government-financed construction is exempt from the Act and this chapter.
(b) Any person who conducts or intends to conduct coal extraction which does not satisfy paragraph (a) of this section shall not proceed until a permit has been obtained from the regulatory authority under a State, Federal or Federal lands program.
