22 U.S.C. § 6084 — Limitation of actions
Citation: us:usc:22:6084
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section6084
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 69A (Cuban Liberty And Democratic Solidarity (Libertad)); Subchapter III (Protection Of Property Rights Of United States Nationals). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An action under section 6082 of this title may not be brought more than 2 years after the trafficking giving rise to the action has ceased to occur.
