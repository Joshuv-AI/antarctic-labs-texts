14 C.F.R. § 11.91 — How does FAA inform me of its decision on my petition for exemption?
Citation: us:cfr:14:11.91
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-11.91
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 11 (General Rulemaking Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The FAA will notify you in writing about its decision on your petition. A copy of this decision is also placed in the public docket. We will include the docket number associated with your petition in our letter to you.
