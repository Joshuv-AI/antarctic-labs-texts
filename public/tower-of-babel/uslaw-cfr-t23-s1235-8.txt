23 C.F.R. § 1235.8 — Reciprocity.
Citation: us:cfr:23:1235.8
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration And Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-1235.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 1235 (Uniform System For Parking For Persons With Disabilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The State system shall recognize removable windshield placards, temporary removable windshield placards and special license plates which have been issued by issuing authorities of other States and countries, for the purpose of identifying vehicles permitted to utilize parking spaces reserved for persons with disabilities which limit or impair the ability to walk.
