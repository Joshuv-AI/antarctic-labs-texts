10 U.S.C. § 887a — Art. 87a. Resistance, flight, breach of arrest, and escape
Citation: us:usc:10:887a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section887a
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 47 (Uniform Code Of Military Justice); Subchapter X (Punitive Articles). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person subject to this chapter who—
(1)
resists apprehension;
(2)
flees from apprehension;
(3)
breaks arrest; or
(4)
escapes from custody or confinement;
shall be punished as a court-martial may direct.
