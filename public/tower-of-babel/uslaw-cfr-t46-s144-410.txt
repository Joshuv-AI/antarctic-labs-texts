46 C.F.R. § 144.410 — Separation of machinery and fuel tank spaces from accommodation spaces.
Citation: us:cfr:46:144.410
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-144.410
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 144 (Construction And Arrangement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Machinery and fuel tank spaces must be separated from accommodation spaces by bulkheads. Doors may be installed provided they are the self-closing type.
