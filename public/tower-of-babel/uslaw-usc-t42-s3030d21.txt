42 U.S.C. § 3030d–21 — Purposes

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 35 (Programs For Older Americans); Subchapter III (Grants For State And Community Programs On Aging); Part C (Nutrition Services). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purposes of this part are—
(1)
to reduce hunger, food insecurity, and malnutrition;
(2)
to promote socialization of older individuals; and
(3)
to promote the health and well-being of older individuals by assisting such individuals to gain access to nutrition and other disease prevention and health promotion services to delay the onset of adverse health conditions resulting from poor nutritional health or sedentary behavior.
