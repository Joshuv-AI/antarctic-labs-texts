43 C.F.R. § 420.2 — General closure.
Citation: us:cfr:43:420.2
Creator: Bureau Of Reclamation, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-420.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 420 (Off-Road Vehicle Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Reclamation lands are closed to off- road vehicle use, except for an area or trail specifically opened to use of off- road vehicles in accordance with § 420.21.
