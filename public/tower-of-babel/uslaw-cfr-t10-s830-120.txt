10 C.F.R. § 830.120 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:10:830.120
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-830.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 830 (Nuclear Safety Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart establishes quality assurance requirements for contractors conducting activities, including providing items or services that affect, or may affect, nuclear safety of DOE nuclear facilities.
