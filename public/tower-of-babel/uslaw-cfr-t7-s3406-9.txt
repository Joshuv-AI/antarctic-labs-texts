7 C.F.R. § 3406.9 — Complementary project proposals.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3406.9
Creator: National Institute Of Food And Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3406.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3406 (1890 Institution Capacity Building Grants Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Institutions may submit proposals that are complementary in nature as defined in § 3406.2. Such complementary project proposals may be submitted by the same or by different eligible institutions.
