30 C.F.R. § 60.10 — Permissible exposure limit (PEL).
Citation: us:cfr:30:60.10
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-60.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 60 (Respirable Crystalline Silica). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The mine operator shall ensure that no miner is exposed to an airborne concentration of respirable crystalline silica in excess of 50 µg/m 3 for a full-shift exposure, calculated as an 8-hour TWA.
