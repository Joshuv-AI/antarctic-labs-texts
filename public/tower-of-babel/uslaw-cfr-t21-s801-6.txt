21 C.F.R. § 801.6 — Medical devices; misleading statements.
Citation: us:cfr:21:801.6
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-801.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 801 (Labeling). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Among representations in the labeling of a device which render such device misbranded is a false or misleading representation with respect to another device or a drug or food or cosmetic.
