18 C.F.R. § 1318.202 — Public notification.
Citation: us:cfr:18:1318.202
Creator: Tennessee Valley Authority
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-1318.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 1318 (Implementation Of The National Environmental Policy Act Of 1969). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

TVA may, in its sole discretion, provide public notification on any action for which a categorical exclusion is used if TVA determines that the public may have relevant and important information relating to the proposal that will assist TVA in its decisionmaking.
