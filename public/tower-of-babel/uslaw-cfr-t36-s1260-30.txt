36 C.F.R. § 1260.30 — What is the NDC?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1260.30
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1260.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1260 (Declassification Of National Security Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The National Declassification Center (NDC) is established within NARA to streamline declassification processes, facilitate quality-assurance measures, and implement standardized training for declassification of records determined to have permanent historical value.
