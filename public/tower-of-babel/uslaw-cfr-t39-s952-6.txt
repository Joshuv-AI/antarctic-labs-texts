39 C.F.R. § 952.6 — Interim impounding.
Citation: us:cfr:39:952.6
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-952.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 952 (False Representation And Lottery Orders). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In preparation for or during the pendency of a proceeding initiated under 39 U.S.C. 3005, mail addressed to Respondent may be impounded upon obtaining an appropriate order from a United States District Court, as provided in 39 U.S.C. 3007.
