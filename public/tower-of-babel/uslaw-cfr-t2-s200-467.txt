2 C.F.R. § 200.467 — Selling and marketing costs.
Citation: us:cfr:2:200.467
Creator: Office Of Management And Budget Guidance
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-200.467
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 200 (Uniform Administrative Requirements, Cost Principles, And Audit Requirements For Federal Awards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Costs of selling and marketing any products or services of the recipient or subrecipient are unallowable unless they are allowed under § 200.421 and are necessary to meet the requirements of the Federal award.
