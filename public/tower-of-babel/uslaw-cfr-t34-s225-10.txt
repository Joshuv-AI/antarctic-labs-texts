34 C.F.R. § 225.10 — How does the Secretary evaluate an application?
Citation: us:cfr:34:225.10
Creator: Office Of Elementary And Secondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-225.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 225 (Credit Enhancement For Charter School Facilities Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Secretary evaluates an application on the basis of the criteria in § 225.11.
(b) The Secretary awards up to 100 points for these criteria.
(c) The maximum possible score for each criterion is indicated in parentheses.
