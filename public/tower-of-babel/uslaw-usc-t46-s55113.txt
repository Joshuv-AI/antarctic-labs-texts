46 U.S.C. § 55113 — Use of foreign documented oil spill response vessels
Citation: us:usc:46:55113
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section55113
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part D (Promotional Programs); Chapter 551 (Coastwise Trade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, an oil spill response vessel documented under the laws of a foreign country may operate in waters of the United States on an emergency and temporary basis, for the purpose of recovering, transporting, and unloading in a United States port oil discharged as a result of an oil spill in or near those waters, if—
(1)
an adequate number and type of oil spill response vessels documented under the laws of the United States cannot be engaged to recover oil from an oil spill in or near those waters in a timely manner, as determined by the Federal On-Scene Coordinator for a discharge or threat of a discharge of oil; and
(2)
the foreign country has by its laws accorded to vessels of the United States the same privileges accorded to vessels of the foreign country under this section.
