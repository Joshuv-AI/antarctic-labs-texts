7 U.S.C. § 594 — Exemption of minimum quantities
Citation: us:usc:7:594
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section594
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 25A (Export Standards For Grapes And Plums). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may, by regulation, exempt from compliance with the provisions of this chapter (1) any variety or varieties of grapes and plums, and (2) the shipment of such minimum quantities of grapes and plums to any foreign country as he may prescribe.
