Bağcı − - Hoggan Complete and Orthonormal Sets of ETOs. Results for He − - like atoms.
A. Bağcı, Philip E. Hoggan

Abstract
The Hartree − - Fock − - Rothaan equations are solved for He − - like ions using the iterative self − - consistent method. Bağcı − - Hoggan complete and orthonormal sets of exponential − - type orbitals are employed as the basis. These orbitals satisfy the orthonormality relationship for quantum numbers with fractional order. They are solution of Schrödinger − - like differential equation derived by the authors. In a recent study conducted for the calculation of the hydrogen atom energy levels, it has been demonstrated that the fractional formalism of the principal and the angular momentum quantum numbers converges to the 1 ​ s 1s level of the ground state energy of hydrogen atom, obtained from the solution of the standard Schrödinger equation. This study examines the effect of fractional values of the quantum numbers for two-electron systems, which is the simplest system with electron correlation effects. Keywords BH − - ETOs, Hartree − - Fock equations, He − - like atoms

Note that test calculations conducted for one

The radial components of the NSTOs naturally emerge as basis functions in the variational solution of the relativistic Dirac equation. The solution of the Dirac equation for hydrogen
