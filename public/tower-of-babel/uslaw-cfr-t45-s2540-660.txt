45 C.F.R. § 2540.660 — If the final decision determines that I received a financial benefit improperly, will I be required to repay that benefit?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2540.660
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2540.660
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2540 (General Administrative Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If it is determined that you received a financial benefit improperly, you may be required to reimburse the program for that benefit.
