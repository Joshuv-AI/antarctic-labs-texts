43 C.F.R. § 8340.0-1 — -1 Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:43:8340.0-1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-8340.0-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 8340 (Off-Road Vehicles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to establish criteria for designating public lands as open, limited or closed to the use of off-road vehicles and for establishing controls governing the use and operation of off-road vehicles in such areas.
