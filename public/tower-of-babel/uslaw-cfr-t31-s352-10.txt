31 C.F.R. § 352.10 — Taxation.
Citation: us:cfr:31:352.10
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-352.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 352 (Offering Of United States Savings Bonds, Series Hh). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The interest paid on Series HH bonds is subject to all taxes imposed under the Internal Revenue Code of 1954, as amended. The bonds are subject to estate, inheritance, gift, or other excise taxes, whether Federal or State, but are exempt from all taxation now or hereafter imposed on the principal or interest by any State or any local taxing authority.
