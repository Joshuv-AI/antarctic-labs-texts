43 C.F.R. § 3216.22 — Does BLM approve all transfer requests?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3216.22
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3216.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3200 (Geothermal Resource Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

BLM will not approve a transfer if:
(a) The lease account is not in good standing;
(b) The transferee does not qualify to hold a lease under this part; or
(c) An adequate bond has not been provided.
