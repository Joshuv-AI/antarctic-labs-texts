43 C.F.R. § 418.23 — Diversion of Rock Dam Ditch water.
Citation: us:cfr:43:418.23
Creator: Bureau Of Reclamation, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-418.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 418 (Operating Criteria And Procedures For The Newlands Reclamation Project, Nevada). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Project water may be diverted directly to Rock Dam Ditch from the Truckee Canal only when diversions cannot be made from the outlet works of Lahontan Reservoir. Such diversions will require the prior written approval of the Bureau and be used in calculating Project diversions.
