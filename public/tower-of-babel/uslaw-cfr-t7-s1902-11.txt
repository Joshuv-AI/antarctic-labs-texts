7 C.F.R. § 1902.11 — Servicing Office records.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1902.11
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Rural Business-Cooperative Service, And Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1902.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1902 (Supervised Bank Accounts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A record of funds deposited in a supervised bank account will be maintained on Form RD 402-2 in accordance with the Forms Manual Insert. The record of funds provided for operating purposes by another creditor or grantor will be on a separate Form RD 402-2 so that they can be clearly identified.
