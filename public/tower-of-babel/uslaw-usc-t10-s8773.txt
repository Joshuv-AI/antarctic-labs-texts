10 U.S.C. § 8773 — Quarters: temporary; transient members
Citation: us:usc:10:8773
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8773
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 877 (Quarters, Utilities, And Services). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Temporary quarters may be furnished on a rental basis to transient members of the naval service with their dependents, for periods not exceeding 60 days, without loss of entitlement to basic allowance for housing under section 403 of title 37.
