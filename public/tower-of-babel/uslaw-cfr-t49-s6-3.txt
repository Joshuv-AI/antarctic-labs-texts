49 C.F.R. § 6.3 — Applicability.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 6 (Implementation Of Equal Access To Justice Act In Agency Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 6.9(a) applies to any adversary adjudication pending before the Department on or after October 1, 1981. In addition, applicants for awards must also meet the standards of § 6.9(b) for any adversary adjudication commenced on or after March 29, 1996.
