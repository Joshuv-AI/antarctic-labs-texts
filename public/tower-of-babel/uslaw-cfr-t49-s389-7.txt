49 C.F.R. § 389.7 — Records.
Citation: us:cfr:49:389.7
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-389.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 389 (Rulemaking Procedures—Federal Motor Carrier Safety Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Records of the Administrator relating to rulemaking proceedings are available for inspection as provided in section 552(b) of title 5 of the United States Code and part 7 of the regulations of the Secretary of Transportation (part 7 of this title; 32 FR 9284 et seq.).
