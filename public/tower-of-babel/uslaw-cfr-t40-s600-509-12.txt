40 C.F.R. § 600.509-12 — -12 Voluntary submission of additional data.
Citation: us:cfr:40:600.509-12
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-600.509-12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 600 (Fuel Economy And Greenhouse Gas Exhaust Emissions Of Motor Vehicles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The manufacturer may optionally submit data in addition to the data required by the Administrator.
(b) Additional fuel economy data may be submitted by the manufacturer for any vehicle configuration which is to be tested as required in § 600.507 or for which fuel economy data were previously submitted under paragraph (c) of this section.
(c) Within a base level, additional fuel economy data may be submitted by the manufacturer for any vehicle configuration which is not required to be tested by § 600.507.
