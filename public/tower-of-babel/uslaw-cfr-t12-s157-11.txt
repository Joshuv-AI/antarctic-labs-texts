12 C.F.R. § 157.11 — To what extent does Federal law preempt deposit-related state laws?
Citation: us:cfr:12:157.11
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-157.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 157 (Deposits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

State law applies to the deposit activities of Federal savings associations and their subsidiaries to the same extent and in the same manner that those laws apply to national banks and their subsidiaries.
