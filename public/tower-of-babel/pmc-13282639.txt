Authors: Juergen Siepmann, Axel Zeitler, Abdul Basit
Published: 2026

This special issue is dedicated to the European INTERREG project “Site-Specific Drug Delivery”: A consortium of 10 academic and industrial partners in the “2 Seas Region” (Northern France, Southern UK, Northern Belgium and a part of The Netherlands) worked together to develop innovative local controlled drug delivery systems to improve therapeutic efficacies and reduce undesired side. The project was funded by the Interreg 2 Seas programme and the European Regional Development Fund (Site Drug 2S07–033).

The large majority of drug products available on the market deliver the drug in a non-targeted manner to the human body: The active agent is circulated via the blood throughout the entire organism. Parts of it reach the target site. However, most of the administered drug dose is distributed within the rest of the body. Importantly, this drug is not only “lost” for the treatment, it is often also harmful for the patient, causing potentially severe side effects. The latter have 3 major consequences, they: 1) constitute a health burden for the patient; 2) can limit the drug dose that can be given to the patient, leading to nonoptimal therapeutic effects; and 3) cause considerable costs for the health care systems of our societies.

A collection of articles is provided, describing innovative approaches to treat diseases & disorders of the colon and of the cochlear. Furthermore, cutting-edge experimental techniques are reported, allowing to better understand fundamental aspects of site-specific drug delivery.

We would like to cordially thank all the authors of the articles of this special issue for their time and efforts as well as the reviewers for their highly valuable help. Please enjoy reading, we hope you will be able to benefit from the contributions.
