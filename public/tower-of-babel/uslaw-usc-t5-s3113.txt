5 U.S.C. § 3113 — Restriction on reemployment after conviction of certain crimes
Citation: us:usc:5:3113
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section3113
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart B (Employment And Retention); Chapter 31 (Authority For Employment); Subchapter I (Employment Authorities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An employee shall be separated from service and barred from reemployment in the Federal service, if—
(1)
the employee is convicted of a violation of section 201(b) of title 18; and
(2)
such violation related to conduct prohibited under section 1010(a) of the Controlled Substances Import and Export Act (21 U.S.C. 960(a)).
