14 C.F.R. § 21.120 — Responsibility of supplemental type certificate holders to provide written permission for alterations.
Citation: us:cfr:14:21.120
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-21.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 21 (Certification Procedures For Products And Articles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A supplemental type certificate holder who allows a person to use the supplemental type certificate to alter an aircraft, aircraft engine, or propeller must provide that person with written permission acceptable to the FAA.
