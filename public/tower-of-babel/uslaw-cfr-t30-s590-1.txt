30 C.F.R. § 590.1 — What is the purpose of this subpart?
Citation: us:cfr:30:590.1
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-590.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 590 (Appeal Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this subpart is to explain the procedures for appeals of Bureau of Ocean Energy Management (BOEM) decisions and orders.
