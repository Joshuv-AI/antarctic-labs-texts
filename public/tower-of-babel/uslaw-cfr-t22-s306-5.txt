22 C.F.R. § 306.5 — Representation.
Citation: us:cfr:22:306.5
Creator: Peace Corps
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-306.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 306 (Volunteer Discrimination Complaint Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any aggrieved party may be assisted in all stages of these procedures under this Part by an attorney or non-staff representative of his or her own choosing at his or her own expense. An aggrieved party must immediately inform the agency if representation is retained.
