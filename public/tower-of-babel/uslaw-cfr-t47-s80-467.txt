47 C.F.R. § 80.467 — Duplication of VHF service.
Citation: us:cfr:47:80.467
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-80.467
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 80 (Stations In The Maritime Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No duplication of service areas as determined by subpart P of this part will be permitted by public coast stations operating on the same VHF public correspondence channel. Within the service area of a station, the ratio of desired to undesired co-channel signal strengths on public correspondence channels must be at least 12dB.
