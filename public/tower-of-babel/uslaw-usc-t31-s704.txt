31 U.S.C. § 704 — Relationship to other laws
Citation: us:usc:31:704
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section704
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 7 (Government Accountability Office); Subchapter I (Definitions And General Organization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
To the extent applicable, all laws generally related to administering an agency apply to the Comptroller General.
(b)
A copy of a record and a transcript from a record or proceeding of the Comptroller General, that the Comptroller General or Deputy Comptroller General certifies under seal, shall be admitted as evidence with the same effect as a copy or transcript referred to in section 1733 of title 28.
