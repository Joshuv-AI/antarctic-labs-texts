33 U.S.C. § 2351a — Operation and maintenance of existing infrastructure
Citation: us:usc:33:2351a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section2351a
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 36 (Water Resources Development); Subchapter V (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall improve the reliability, and operation and maintenance of, existing infrastructure of the Corps of Engineers, and, as necessary, improve its resilience to cyber-related threats.
