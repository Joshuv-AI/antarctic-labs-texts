Nonexistence of global weak solutions of Klein-Gordon equations with gauge variant semilinear terms in Friedmann-Lemaître-Robertson-Walker spacetimes
Makoto NAKAMURA, Takuma YOSHIZUMI

Abstract
Nonexistence of global weak solutions of Klein-Gordon equations with gauge variant semilinear terms are considered in Friedmann-Lemaître-Robertson-Walker spacetimes. Effects of spatial expansion or contraction on the solutions are studied through the scale-function and the curved mass.

Introduction

We show the nonexistence of global weak solutions
of Klein-Gordon equations with gauge-variant semilinear terms in Friedmann-Lemaître-Robertson-Walker spacetimes
(FLRW spacetimes for short).
FLRW spacetimes are solutions of the Einstein equations
with the cosmological constant under the cosmological principle.
They describe the spatial expansion or contraction,
and yield some important models of the universe.
Let
n\geq 1
be the spatial dimension,

a(\cdot)>0
be a scale-function defined on an interval
[0,T_{0})
for some
0<T_{0}\leq\infty
,
and let
c>0
be the speed of light.
The metrics
\{g_{\alpha\beta}\}
of FLRW spacetimes are expressed by

\sum_{0\leq\alpha,\beta\leq n}g_{\alpha\beta}dx^{\alpha}dx^{\beta}:=-c^{2}(dt)^{2}+a^{2}(t)\sum_{j=1}^{n}(dx^{j})^{2}
,
where we have put the spatial curvature as zero,
and
x^{0}=t
is the time-variable
(see e.g.,

[
3
,
4
]
).
When
a
is a positive constant,
the spacetime
reduces to the Minkowski spacetime.

We denote the first and second derivatives of one variable function
a
by
\dot{a}
and
\ddot{a}
.
The Klein-Gordon equation generated by the above metric

(g_{\alpha\beta})_{0\leq\alpha,\beta\leq n}
is given by

(\sqrt{|g|})^{-1}\partial_{\alpha}\left(\sqrt{|g|}g^{\alpha\beta}\partial_{\beta}v\right)=m^{2}v+f(v)

for the determinant
g:=\mbox{det}\left(g_{\alpha\beta}\right)

and the inverse matrix
\left(g^{\alpha\beta}\right)
,
i.e.,

\frac{1}{c^{2}}\partial_{t}^{2}v+\frac{n\dot{a}}{c^{2}a}\partial_{t}v-\frac{1}{a^{2}}\Delta v+m^{2}v+f(v)=0,

(1.1)

where

m
denotes the mass,

\Delta:=\sum_{j=1}^{n}\partial_{j}^{2}
denotes the Laplacian,
and
f
denotes a force term.
This equation is rewritten as

c^{-2}\partial_{t}^{2}u-a^{-2}\Delta u+M^{2}u+a^{n/2}f(a^{-n/2}u)=0

(1.2)

by the transformation
u:=va^{n/2}
,
where the function
M
is called curved mass defined by

M^{2}=m^{2}-\frac{n(n-2)}{4c^{2}}\left(\frac{\dot{a}}{a}\right)^{2}-\frac{n}{2c^{2}}\cdot\frac{\ddot{a}}{a},\ \ \ \ M:=\sqrt{M^{2}}.

(1.3)

In this paper, we consider the Cauchy problem in the gauge-variant case
f(v)=\lambda|v|^{p}
in
(
1.2
), namely,

\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}(c^{-2}\partial_{t}^{2}-a^{-2}(t)\Delta+M^{2}(t))u(t,x)-\lambda a^{-n(p-1)/2}(t)|u|^{p}(t,x)=0,\\
u(0,\cdot)=u_{0}(\cdot),\ \ \partial_{t}u(0,\cdot)=u_{1}(\cdot)\end{array}\right.

(1.4)

for
(t,x)\in[0,T)\times{\mathbb{R}}^{n}
and
0<T\leq T_{0}
,
where

u_{0}
and
u_{1}
are given initial data.
We expect some dissipative effects when
\dot{a}>0
in (
1.1
)
from the term
n\dot{a}\partial_{t}v/c^{2}a
.
Actually, the spatial expansion yields dissipative effects in energy estimates for the Klein-Gordon equation
(see

[
10
,
14
]
).
The Cauchy problem of (
1.4
) including gauge invariant semilinear term is widely studied especially on local and global solutions, and their asymptotic behaviors
(see

[
2
,
5
,
7
,
11
,
14
,
19
]
for closely related results).

Blowing-up solutions for (
1.4
) have been considered in

[
11
, Proposition 1]
and

[
18
, Theorems 1.1 and 1.2]

in the de Sitter spacetime.
The aim of this paper is to extend these results into general FLRW spacetimes including concrete examples of scale functions of Big-Rip and Big-Crunch.
We consider the real and purely imaginary mass
m\in{\mathbb{R}}\cup i{\mathbb{R}}

since
m\in i{\mathbb{R}}
plays an important role when we consider the breakdown of symmetry of potential well, or in
\phi^{4}
-theory in physics
(see
[
11
]
).
We refer to
[
17
]
and the references therein
on blowing-up solutions for small initial data in FLRW spacetimes
for gauge-variant semilinear terms and vanishing mass
m=0
, i.e., wave equation,
under some conditions on
p
concerned with the Strauss conjecture
(see also
[
6
]
in the Einstein-de Sitter spacetime).
Our results are for relatively large data and arbitrary
m\in{\mathbb{R}}\cup i{\mathbb{R}}
and
1<p<\infty
.

The case of the gauge invariant semilinear term of the form

f(u)=\lambda|v|^{p-1}v
in (
1.2
)
has been considered by McCollum, Mwamba and Oliver in

[
9
, Theorem 1]
for
\dot{a}>0
,
and in
[
14
, Theorem 1.4]
for
\dot{a}\leq 0
by different techniques from this paper to show the blowing-up of the norm
\|u\|_{2}
,
while the nonexistence of global weak solutions
is considered through the blowing-up of
\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}u(t,x)dx
in this paper.

Definition 1.1

(Weak global solution)
.

Let
a\in C([0,\infty),{\mathbb{R}})
,
M\in C([0,\infty),{\mathbb{C}})

and
h\in L^{1}_{\mbox{\rm loc}}([0,\infty)\times{\mathbb{R}^{n}},{\mathbb{C}})
.
We say that
u\in L^{1}_{\mbox{\rm loc}}([0,\infty)\times{\mathbb{R}^{n}})
is a weak global solution of the Cauchy problem

\begin{cases}c^{-2}\partial_{t}^{2}u-a^{-2}\Delta u+M^{2}u+h=0,\\
u(0,\cdot)=u_{0}(\cdot),\ \ \partial_{t}u(0,\cdot)=u_{1}(\cdot)\end{cases}

if
u
satisfies the integral equation

c^{-2}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}u_{0}(x)\partial_{t}\psi(0,x)dx-c^{-2}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}u_{1}(x)\psi(0,x)dx\\
+\int_{0}^{\infty}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}u(t,x)\left\{c^{-2}\partial_{t}^{2}\psi(t,x)-a^{-2}(t)\Delta\psi(t,x)+M^{2}(t)\psi(t,x)\right\}dxdt\\
+\int_{0}^{\infty}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}h(t,x)\psi(t,x)dxdt=0

(1.5)

for any
\psi\in C_{0}^{2}([0,\infty)\times{\mathbb{R}^{n}})
.

Throughout the paper, we assume that the solution
u
of (
1.4
) satisfies the finite speed of propagation.
Namely, the support of
u
is inside of the characteristic curve such that

\{(t,x);0\leq t<T,\ |x|\leq r(t)\}

if the initial data with their supports satisfying

\mbox{\rm supp}\ u_{0}\,\cup\,\mbox{\rm supp}\ u_{1}\subset\{x\in{\mathbb{R}^{n}};\,|x|\leq r_{0}\}
,
where

r(t):=r_{0}+\int_{0}^{t}\frac{c}{a(s)}ds

(1.6)

(see
[
13
, Appendix]
for details).

Theorem 1.2

(Nonexistence of global weak solutions)
.

Let
n\geq 1
,
\lambda>0
,
1<p<\infty
and
r_{0}>0
.
Let

u_{0}\in L^{1}({\mathbb{R}^{n}}),\ \ u_{1}\in L^{1}({\mathbb{R}^{n}}),\ \ \mbox{\rm supp}\ u_{0}\cup\mbox{\rm supp}\ u_{1}\subset\{x\in{\mathbb{R}^{n}};\,|x|\leq r_{0}\}.

(1.7)

Put

w_{0}:=\operatorname{Re}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}u_{0}(x)dx,\ \ w_{1}:=\operatorname{Re}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}u_{1}(x)dx.

(1.8)

Let
a\in C([0,\infty),(0,\infty))
,
M\in C([0,\infty),i{\mathbb{R}})
,
and let
N
be a number with

\inf_{0<t<\infty}M^{2}(t)>-\infty,\ \ 0\leq N<\infty,\ \ N^{2}+\inf_{0<t<\infty}M^{2}(t)\geq 0.

(1.9)

Let
0<\theta<1
.
Put

b:=\frac{\lambda}{\omega_{n}^{p-1}\left(ar^{2}\right)^{n(p-1)/2}},\ \ S:=\sup_{0<t<\infty}\left\{e^{-cNt}\left(\frac{N^{2}+M^{2}(t)}{(1-\theta)b(t)}\right)^{1/(p-1)}\right\},

(1.10)

where
\omega_{n}
denotes the volume of unit ball in
{\mathbb{R}^{n}}
,
and assume

S<\infty,\ \ w_{0}>S,\ \ w_{1}\geq cNw_{0},

(1.11)

\lim_{R\to\infty}R^{-2}\left\{\int_{R/2}^{R}\left(\min\{R,r(t)\}\right)^{n}a^{n/2}(t)dt\right\}^{1/p^{\prime}}=0,

(1.12)

\lim_{R\to\infty}R^{-2}\left\{\int_{0}^{R}\int_{\begin{subarray}{c}R/2<|x|<R\\
|x|<r(t)\end{subarray}}dx\,a^{n/2-2p^{\prime}}(t)dt\right\}^{1/p^{\prime}}=0,

\int_{0}^{\infty}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|M^{2}(t)u(t,x)|dxdt<\infty.

u
of (
1.4
