36 C.F.R. § 51.49 — May the Director grant a right of preference except in accordance with this part?
Citation: us:cfr:36:51.49
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-51.49
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 51 (Concession Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director may not grant a concessioner or any other person a right of preference or any other form of entitlement of any nature to a new concession contract, except in accordance with this part or in accordance with 36 CFR part 13.
