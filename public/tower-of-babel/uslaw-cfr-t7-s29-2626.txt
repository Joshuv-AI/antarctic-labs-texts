7 C.F.R. § 29.2626 — Rule 10.
Citation: us:cfr:7:29.2626
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-29.2626
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 29 (Tobacco Inspection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any special factor approved by the Director of the Tobacco Division, Agricultural Marketing Service, may be used to show a peculiar side or characteristic of the tobacco which tends to modify the grade.
