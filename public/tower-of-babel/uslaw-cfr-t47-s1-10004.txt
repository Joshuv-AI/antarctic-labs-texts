47 C.F.R. § 1.10004 — What am I allowed to do if I am approved?
Citation: us:cfr:47:1.10004
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-1.10004
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 1 (Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you are approved and receive a license or authorization, you must operate in accordance with, and not beyond, your terms of approval.
