31 C.F.R. § 15.737-18 — -18 Representation.
Citation: us:cfr:31:15.737-18
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-15.737-18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 15 (Post Employment Conflict Of Interest). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A respondent or proposed respondent may appear in person or he/she may be represented by counsel or other representative. The Director may be represented by an attorney or other employee of the Department.
