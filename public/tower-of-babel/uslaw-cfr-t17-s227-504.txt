17 C.F.R. § 227.504 — Definition of “qualified purchaser”.
Citation: us:cfr:17:227.504
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-227.504
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 227 (Regulation Crowdfunding, General Rules And Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of section 18(b)(3) of the Securities Act [15 U.S.C. 77r(b)(3)], a “qualified purchaser” means any person to whom securities are offered or sold pursuant to an offering under §§ 227.100 through 227.504 (Regulation Crowdfunding).
