33 C.F.R. § 76.01-5 — -5 Sale of condemned equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:33:76.01-5
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-76.01-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 76 (Sale And Transfer Of Aids To Navigation Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When any condemned supplies, materials, or equipment cannot be profitably used in work of the Coast Guard, they will be disposed of under appropriate regulations of the General Services Administration. Applications for purchase of such materials may be submitted to the Commandant who will process them for further action under the applicable regulations.
