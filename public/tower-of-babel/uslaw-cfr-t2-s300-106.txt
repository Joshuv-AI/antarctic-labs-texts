2 C.F.R. § 300.106 — Adoption of 2 CFR part 200.
Citation: us:cfr:2:300.106
Creator: Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-300.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 300 (Uniform Administrative Requirements, Cost Principles, And Audit Requirements For Federal Awards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department of Health and Human Services adopts the Office of Management and Budget (OMB) guidance in 2 CFR part 200, with the additions included in this part and part 376 of this chapter. Thus, this part gives regulatory effect to the OMB guidance and supplements the guidance as needed for the Department.
