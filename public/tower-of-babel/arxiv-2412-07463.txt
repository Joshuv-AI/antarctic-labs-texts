Abstract
In our previous paper [Fei Hou, Fei Tao, Huicheng Yin, Global existence and scattering of small data smooth solutions to a class of quasilinear wave systems on ℝ 2 × 𝕋 \mathbb{R}^{2}\times\mathbb{T} , Preprint (2024), arXiv:2405.03242], for the Q 0 Q_{0} -type quadratic nonlinearities, we have shown the global well-posedness and scattering properties of small data smooth solutions to the quasilinear wave systems on ℝ 2 × 𝕋 \mathbb{R}^{2}\times\mathbb{T} . In this paper, we start to solve the global existence problem for the remaining Q α ​ β Q_{\alpha\beta} -type nonlinearities. By combining these results, we have established the global well-posedness of small solutions on ℝ 2 × 𝕋 \mathbb{R}^{2}\times\mathbb{T} for the general 3-D quadratically quasilinear wave systems when the related 2-D null conditions are fulfilled. Keywords. Global existence, scattering, quasilinear wave system, Q α ​ β Q_{\alpha\beta} -type nonlinearity, space-time decay estimate, ghost weight 2020 Mathematical Subject Classification. 35L05, 35L15, 35L70

For the

\mathbb{R}^{2}\times\mathbb{T}

[

We now deal with the remaining
Q_{\alpha\beta}
-type nonlinearities, namely,
consider the following 3-D quasilinear wave systems on
\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{2}\times\mathop{\mathbb{T}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits

\left\{\begin{aligned} &\Box u^{i}=G^{i}(\partial u,\partial^{2}u):=\sum_{j,k=1}^{m}\sum_{\alpha,\beta=0}^{2}\Big(\sum_{\gamma=0}^{3}C^{\alpha\beta\gamma}_{ijk}Q_{\alpha\beta}(\partial_{\gamma}u^{j},u^{k})+C^{\alpha\beta}_{ijk}Q_{\alpha\beta}(u^{j},u^{k})\Big)\\
&\hskip 99.58464pt+\sum_{j,k,l=1}^{m}\sum_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma=0}^{3}Q^{\alpha\beta\gamma}_{ijkl}\partial_{\alpha}u^{j}\partial_{\beta}u^{k}\partial_{\gamma}u^{l},\quad i=1,\cdots,m,\\
&(u,\partial_{t}u)(0,x,y)=(u_{(0)},u_{(1)})(x,y),\end{aligned}\right.

(1.1)

where
u=(u^{1},...,u^{m})
,
(t,x,y)\in[0,+\infty)\times\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{2}\times\mathop{\mathbb{T}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits
,

,
\partial=(\partial_{0},\partial_{1},\partial_{2},\partial_{3})=(\partial_{t},\partial_{x_{1}},\partial_{x_{2}},

\partial_{y})

\displaystyle\Box:=\Box_{t,x,y}=\partial_{t}^{2}-\Delta_{x}-\partial_{y}^{2}

\displaystyle Q_{\alpha\beta}(f,g)=\partial_{\alpha}f\partial_{\beta}g-\partial_{\beta}f\partial_{\alpha}g

C^{\alpha\beta\gamma}_{ijk}

2\pi

In addition, the right hand side of the first line in (

\begin{split}\sum_{\alpha,\beta=0}^{2}\sum_{\gamma=0}^{3}C^{\alpha\beta\gamma}_{ijk}Q_{\alpha\beta}(\partial_{\gamma}u^{j},u^{k})=\sum_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma=0}^{3}Q_{ijk}^{\alpha\beta\gamma}\partial^{2}_{\alpha\beta}u^{j}\partial_{\gamma}u^{k},\end{split}

(1.2)

and the following symmetric conditions are imposed for

Q_{ijk}^{\alpha\beta\gamma}=Q_{ijk}^{\beta\alpha\gamma}=Q_{jik}^{\alpha\beta\gamma}.

It is easy to check that for

\sum_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma=0}^{2}Q_{ijk}^{\alpha\beta\gamma}\xi_{\alpha}\xi_{\beta}\xi_{\gamma}\equiv 0,

On the other hand, for the 2-D nonlinear scalar wave equations, both the first and second null conditions
are sufficient and necessary in order to guarantee the global existence of small data smooth solutions

[
1

2
]

Therefore, for the cubic nonlinearity in (

For

2.1
Some basic lemmas

At first, we present some properties associated with the projection operators
P_{=0}
and
P_{\neq 0}
,
which will be frequently utilized later.

Lemma 2.1
.

For any real valued functions
f(t,x,y)
and
g(t,x,y)
on
\mathbb{R}^{2}\times\mathbb{T}
, it holds that

\begin{split}&\int_{\mathop{\mathbb{T}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits}P_{=0}fP_{\neq 0}gdy=0,\quad\|f\|^{2}_{L_{y}^{2}}=\|P_{=0}f\|^{2}_{L_{y}^{2}}+\|P_{\neq 0}f\|^{2}_{L_{y}^{2}},\\
&P_{=0}Zf=ZP_{=0}f,\quad P_{\neq 0}Zf=ZP_{\neq 0}f,\\
&P_{=0}\partial_{y}f=\partial_{y}P_{=0}f=0,\quad(\Gamma f)_{n}=\Gamma(f_{n}),\\
&P_{=0}(fg)=P_{=0}fP_{=0}g+P_{=0}(P_{\neq 0}fP_{\neq 0}g),\\
&P_{\neq 0}(fg)=P_{\neq 0}(P_{=0}fP_{\neq 0}g)+P_{\neq 0}(P_{\neq 0}fP_{=0}g)+P_{\neq 0}(P_{\neq 0}fP_{\neq 0}g),\\
&|P_{=0}f|\lesssim\|f\|_{L_{y}^{\infty}},\quad|P_{\neq 0}f|\lesssim\|f\|_{L_{y}^{\infty}}.\end{split}

(2.1)

Proof.

Since these properties can be directly verified, we omit the details here.
∎

The following lemma indicates that the nonlinearities of
Q_{\alpha\beta}
-type remain invariant
under the action of the vector fields
Z^{\prime}s
.

Lemma 2.2
.

For any real valued functions
f(t,x,y)
and
g(t,x,y)
on
\mathbb{R}^{2}\times\mathbb{T}
, it holds

ZQ_{\alpha\beta}(f,g)=Q_{\alpha\beta}(Zf,g)+Q_{\alpha\beta}(f,Zg)+\sum_{\mu,\nu=0}^{2}a^{Z,\mu\nu}_{\alpha\beta}Q_{\mu\nu}(f,g),

(2.2)

where
a^{Z,\mu\nu}_{\alpha\beta}\in\{0,\pm 1\}
.
In addition, one has

[Z,\partial_{y}]=0,\qquad[Z,\partial_{\alpha}]=\sum_{\beta=0}^{2}a^{Z,\beta}_{\alpha}\partial_{\beta}\quad\text{for $\alpha=0,1,2$},

(2.3)

where
a^{Z,\beta}_{\alpha}\in\{0,\pm 1\}
.

Proof.

It is easy to check that for
\alpha=0,1,2
,

\begin{split}[L_{i},\partial_{\alpha}]&=-\delta_{i\alpha}\partial_{0}-\delta_{0\alpha}\partial_{i},\quad i=1,2,\\
[\Omega_{12},\partial_{\alpha}]&=-\delta_{1\alpha}\partial_{2}+\delta_{2\alpha}\partial_{1},\end{split}

(2.4)

where
\delta_{\alpha\beta}(\alpha,\beta\in\{0,1,2\})
is the Kronecker symbol. By (
2.4
), we have

\begin{split}L_{i}Q_{\alpha\beta}(f,g)&=Q_{\alpha\beta}(L_{i}f,g)+Q_{\alpha\beta}(f,L_{i}g)-\delta_{i\alpha}Q_{0\beta}(f,g)-\delta_{0\alpha}Q_{i\beta}(f,g)\\
&\quad-\delta_{i\beta}Q_{\alpha 0}(f,g)-\delta_{0\beta}Q_{\alpha i}(f,g),\quad i=1,2,\\
\Omega_{12}Q_{\alpha\beta}(f,g)&=Q_{\alpha\beta}(\Omega_{12}f,g)+Q_{\alpha\beta}(f,\Omega_{12}g)+\delta_{2\alpha}Q_{1\beta}(f,g)-\delta_{1\alpha}Q_{2\beta}(f,g)\\
&\quad+\delta_{2\beta}Q_{\alpha 1}(f,g)-\delta_{1\beta}Q_{\alpha 2}(f,g).\end{split}

\begin{split}|Q_{\alpha\beta}(\partial_{\gamma}Z^{b}u^{j},Z^{c}u^{k})|&\lesssim|\bar{\partial}\partial Z^{b}u||\partial Z^{c}u|+|\partial^{2}Z^{b}u||\bar{\partial}Z^{c}u|\\
&\lesssim|\bar{\partial}\partial Z^{b}u||\partial Z^{c}u|+|P_{\neq 0}\partial^{2}Z^{b}u||\bar{\partial}Z^{c}u|+|P_{=0}\partial^{2}Z^{b}u||\bar{\partial}Z^{c}u|\\
|Q_{\alpha\beta}(Z^{b}u^{j},Z^{c}u^{k})|&\lesssim|\bar{\partial}Z^{b}u||\partial Z^{c}u|+|P_{\neq 0}\partial Z^{b}u||\bar{\partial}Z^{c}u|+|P_{=0}\partial Z^{b}u||\bar{\partial}Z^{c}u|.\end{split}

(4.10)

Therefore, by using (
4.10
) with (

), (

), (

) and Young’s inequality again, we can get that

\begin{split}&\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}|b|+|c|\leq|a|\leq 2N,\\
|b|\leq N\end{subarray}}\sum_{\alpha,\beta=0}^{2}\int_{0}^{t^{\prime}}\iint_{\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{2}\times\mathop{\mathbb{T}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits}|\partial_{t}Z^{a}u^{i}|\Big\{\sum_{\gamma=0}^{3}|Q_{\alpha\beta}(\partial_{\gamma}Z^{b}u^{j},Z^{c}u^{k})|+|Q_{\alpha\beta}(Z^{b}u^{j},Z^{c}u^{k})|\Big\}dxdydt\\
&\lesssim\varepsilon_{1}\sum_{|c|\leq 2N}\int_{0}^{t^{\prime}}\iint_{\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{2}\times\mathop{\mathbb{T}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits}\frac{|\bar{\partial}Z^{c}u|^{2}}{\langle{t-|x|}\rangle^{1.1}}dxdydt+\varepsilon_{1}\int_{0}^{t^{\prime}}\langle{t}\rangle^{-1}E^{2}_{2N}[u](t)dt.\end{split}

(4.11)

If

|c|\leq N-1
.
Instead of (

\begin{split}|Q_{\alpha\beta}(\partial_{\gamma}Z^{b}u^{j},Z^{c}u^{k})|&\lesssim|\partial^{2}Z^{b}u||\bar{\partial}Z^{c}u|+|\bar{\partial}\partial Z^{b}u||P_{\neq 0}\partial Z^{c}u|+|\bar{\partial}\partial Z^{b}u||P_{=0}\partial Z^{c}u|,\\
|Q_{\alpha\beta}(Z^{b}u^{j},Z^{c}u^{k})|&\lesssim|\partial Z^{b}u||\bar{\partial}Z^{c}u|+|\bar{\partial}Z^{b}u||P_{\neq 0}\partial Z^{c}u|+|\bar{\partial}Z^{b}u||P_{=0}\partial Z^{c}u|.\end{split}

Analogously,

\begin{split}&\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}|b|+|c|\leq|a|\leq 2N,\\
N+1\leq|b|\leq 2N-1\end{subarray}}\sum_{\alpha,\beta=0}^{2}\sum_{\gamma=0}^{3}\int_{0}^{t^{\prime}}\iint_{\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{2}\times\mathop{\mathbb{T}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits}|\partial_{t}Z^{a}u^{i}Q_{\alpha\beta}(\partial_{\gamma}Z^{b}u^{j},Z^{c}u^{k})|\\
&\quad+\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}|b|+|c|\leq|a|\leq 2N,\\
|b|\geq N+1\end{subarray}}\sum_{\alpha,\beta=0}^{2}\int_{0}^{t^{\prime}}\iint_{\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{2}\times\mathop{\mathbb{T}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits}|\partial_{t}Z^{a}u^{i}Q_{\alpha\beta}(Z^{b}u^{j},Z^{c}u^{k})|dxdydt\\
&\lesssim\varepsilon_{1}\sum_{|c|\leq 2N}\int_{0}^{t^{\prime}}\iint_{\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{2}\times\mathop{\mathbb{T}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits}\frac{|\bar{\partial}Z^{c}u|^{2}}{\langle{t-|x|}\rangle^{1.1}}dxdydt+\varepsilon_{1}\int_{0}^{t^{\prime}}\langle{t}\rangle^{-1}E^{2}_{2N}[u](t)dt.\end{split}

\begin{split}&\sum_{|a|\leq N+3}|P_{=0}Z^{a}u(t,x,y)|\leq\frac{1}{2}\varepsilon_{1}\langle{t+|x|}\rangle^{-1/2}\langle{t-|x|}\rangle^{-0.4},\\
&\sum_{|a|\leq N+2}|P_{=0}\partial Z^{a}u(t,x,y)|\leq\frac{1}{2}\varepsilon_{1}\langle{x}\rangle^{-1/2}\langle{t-|x|}\rangle^{-1.4},\\
&\sum_{|a|\leq 2N-16}|P_{\neq 0}Z^{a}u(t,x,y)|\leq\frac{1}{2}\varepsilon_{1}\langle{t+|x|}\rangle^{-1}.\end{split}

This, together with the local existence of classical solution to (

admits a unique global solution

Finally, we prove (

Denote

and

Then

This, together with Duhamel’s principle, leads to

\mathcal{U}(t)=e^{it\Lambda}\mathcal{U}(0)+\int_{0}^{t}e^{i(t-s)\Lambda}\Box u(s)ds.

(5.1)

Set

\begin{split}&\mathcal{U}_{(0)}^{\infty}:=\mathcal{U}(0)+\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-is\Lambda}\Box u(s)ds,\\
&u^{\infty}_{(0)}:=\Lambda^{-1}\mathrm{Im}~\mathcal{U}_{(0)}^{\infty},\quad u^{\infty}_{(1)}:=\mathrm{Re}~\mathcal{U}_{(0)}^{\infty}.\end{split}

(5.2)

By (

), the first and fourth lines of (

)-(

that for

\begin{split}|\Box Z^{\leq\emph{l}}u|&\lesssim\sum_{|b|+|c|\leq\emph{l}}\langle{t+|x|}\rangle^{-1}(|\partial^{\leq 1}\partial Z^{b}u||ZZ^{c}u|+|Z\partial^{\leq 1}Z^{b}u||\partial Z^{c}u|)\\
&+\sum_{|b|+|c|+|d|\leq\emph{l}}|P_{=0}\bar{\partial}Z^{b}u||P_{=0}\partial Z^{c}u||P_{=0}\partial Z^{d}u|+\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}|b|+|c|+|d|\leq\emph{l},\\

On the other hand, by the standard energy estimates, (

\begin{split}\|\partial Z^{\leq N+2}u(t)\|^{2}_{L^{2}_{x,y}}&\lesssim\|\partial Z^{\leq N+2}u(0)\|^{2}_{L^{2}_{x,y}}+\int_{0}^{t}\|\partial_{t}Z^{\leq N+2}u(s)\|_{L^{2}_{x,y}}\|\Box Z^{\leq N+2}u(s)\|_{L^{2}_{x,y}}ds\\
&\lesssim\varepsilon\|\partial Z^{\leq N+2}u(0)\|_{L^{2}_{x,y}}+\varepsilon^{2}\int_{0}^{t}(1+s)^{-3/2+\varepsilon_{2}}ds\sup_{s\in[0,t]}\|\partial Z^{\leq N+2}u(s)\|_{L^{2}_{x,y}}\\
