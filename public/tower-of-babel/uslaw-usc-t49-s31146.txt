49 U.S.C. § 31146 — Relationship to other laws
Citation: us:usc:49:31146
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part B (Commercial); Chapter 311 (Commercial Motor Vehicle Safety); Subchapter III (Safety Regulation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as provided in section 31136(b) of this title, this subchapter and the regulations prescribed under this subchapter do not affect chapter 51 of this title or a regulation prescribed under chapter 51.
