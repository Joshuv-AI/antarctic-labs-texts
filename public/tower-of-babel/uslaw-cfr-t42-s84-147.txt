42 C.F.R. § 84.147 — Type B supplied-air respirator; minimum requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:42:84.147
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-84.147
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 84 (Approval Of Respiratory Protective Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No Type B supplied-air respirator shall be approved for use with a blower or with connection to an air supply device at positive pressures.
