48 C.F.R. § 2828.203 — 2828.203 Acceptability of individual sureties.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2828.203
Creator: Chapter 28—Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2828.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2828 (Bonds And Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All assets pledged by individual sureties must be eligible obligations as defined in 31 CFR part 225, “Acceptable Collateral for Pledging to Federal Agencies.” This collateral will be placed in the custody of the U.S. Treasury, with a Federal Reserve Bank acting as the depositary until the completion of the obligation.
