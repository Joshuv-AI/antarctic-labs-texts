49 C.F.R. § 29.927 — What notice and service must the Department and the Tribe provide?
Citation: us:cfr:49:29.927
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-29.927
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 29 (Tribal Transportation Self-Governance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Department and the Tribe must file each document with U.S. Department of Transportation, Docket Operations, M-30, West Building Ground Floor, Room W12-140, 1200 New Jersey Avenue SE, Washington, DC 20590.
(b) The Department and the Tribe must serve copies of each document with:
(1) The Self-Governance Official; and
(2) The authorized Tribal representative.
