17 C.F.R. § 270.30a-4 — a-4 Annual report for wholly-owned registered management investment company subsidiary of registered management investment company.
Citation: us:cfr:17:270.30a-4
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-270.30a-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 270 (Rules And Regulations, Investment Company Act Of 1940). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notwithstanding the provisions of § 270.30a-1, a registered management investment company that is a wholly-owned subsidiary of a registered management investment company need not file an annual report on Form N-CEN if financial information with respect to that subsidiary is reported in the parent's annual report on Form N-CEN.
