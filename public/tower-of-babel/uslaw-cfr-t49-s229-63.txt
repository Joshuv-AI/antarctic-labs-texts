49 C.F.R. § 229.63 — Lateral motion.
Citation: us:cfr:49:229.63
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-229.63
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 229 (Railroad Locomotive Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except as provided in paragraph (b), the total uncontrolled lateral motion between the hubs of the wheels and boxes, between boxes and pedestals or both, on any pair of wheels may not exceed 1 inch on non-powered axles and friction bearing powered axles, or 3/4 inch on all other powered axles.
(b) The total uncontrolled lateral motion may not exceed 1 1/4 inches on the center axle of three-axle trucks.
