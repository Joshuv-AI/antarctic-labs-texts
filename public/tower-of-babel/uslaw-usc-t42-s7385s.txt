42 U.S.C. § 7385s — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:42:7385s
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section7385s
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 84 (Department Of Energy); Subchapter XVI (Energy Employees Occupational Illness Compensation Program); Part E (Contractor Employee Compensation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this part:
(1)
The term “covered DOE contractor employee” means any Department of Energy contractor employee determined under section 7385s–4 of this title to have contracted a covered illness through exposure at a Department of Energy facility.
(2)
The term “covered illness” means an illness or death resulting from exposure to a toxic substance.
(3)
The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of Labor.
