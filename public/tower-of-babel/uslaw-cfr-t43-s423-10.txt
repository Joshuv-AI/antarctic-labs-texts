43 C.F.R. § 423.10 — What areas are open to public use?
Citation: us:cfr:43:423.10
Creator: Bureau Of Reclamation, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-423.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 423 (Public Conduct On Bureau Of Reclamation Facilities, Lands, And Waterbodies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All Reclamation facilities, lands, and waterbodies are open to lawful use by the public unless they are closed to public use under this subpart B of this part 423, or as provided by 43 CFR part 420, Off-Road Vehicle Use.
