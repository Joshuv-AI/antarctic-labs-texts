41 C.F.R. § 102-2.75 — -2.75 What timeframes apply to deviations?
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-2.75
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-2.75
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-2 (2—Federal Management Regulation System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Timeframes vary based on the nature of the deviation. However, deviations cannot be open-ended. When consulting with GSA about using an individual or class deviation, you must set a timeframe for the deviation's duration.
