10 C.F.R. § 1708.113 — Right to submit statements.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1708.113
Creator: Defense Nuclear Facilities Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1708.113
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1708 (Procedures For Safety Investigations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At any time during the course of an investigation, any person may submit documents, statements of facts, or memoranda of law for the purpose of explanation or further development of the facts and circumstances relevant to the safety matter under investigation.
