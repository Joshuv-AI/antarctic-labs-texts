30 C.F.R. § 820.11 — Performance standards: Anthracite mines in Pennsylvania.
Citation: us:cfr:30:820.11
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-820.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 820 (Special Permanent Program Performance Standards—Anthracite Mines In Pennsylvania). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Anthracite mines in Pennsylvania, as specified in section 529 of the Act, shall comply with its approved State program, including Commonwealth of Pennsylvania statutes and regulations, and revisions thereto that are approved by OSM pursuant to part 732 of this chapter.
