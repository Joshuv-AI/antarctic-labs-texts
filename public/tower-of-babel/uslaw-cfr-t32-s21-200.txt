32 C.F.R. § 21.200 — What is the Defense Grant and Agreement Regulatory System (DGARS)?
Citation: us:cfr:32:21.200
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-21.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 21 (DoD GRANTS AND AGREEMENTS—GENERAL MATTERS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Defense Grant and Agreement Regulatory System (DGARS) is the system of regulatory policies and procedures for the award and administration of DoD Components' assistance and other nonprocurement awards. DoD Directive 3210.6 1 established the DGARS.
