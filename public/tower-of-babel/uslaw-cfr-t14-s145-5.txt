14 C.F.R. § 145.5 — Certificate and operations specifications requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:14:145.5
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-145.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 145 (Repair Stations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) No person may operate as a certificated repair station without, or in violation of, a repair station certificate, ratings, or operations specifications issued under this part.
(b) The certificate and operations specifications issued to a certificated repair station must be available on the premises for inspection by the public and the FAA.
