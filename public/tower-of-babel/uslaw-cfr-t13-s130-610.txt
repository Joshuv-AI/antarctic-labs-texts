13 C.F.R. § 130.610 — General terms.
Citation: us:cfr:13:130.610
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-130.610
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 130 (Small Business Development Centers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon approval of the initial or renewal application, SBA will enter into a cooperative agreement with the recipient organization, setting forth the programmatic and fiscal responsibilities of the recipient organization and SBA, the scope of the project to be funded, and the budget of the program year covered by the cooperative agreement.
