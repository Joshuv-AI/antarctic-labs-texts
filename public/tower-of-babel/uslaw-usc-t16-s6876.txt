16 U.S.C. § 6876 — Effect
Citation: us:usc:16:6876
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section6876
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 87B (Modernizing Access To Public Waters). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter—
(1)
modifies or alters the definition of the term “navigable waters” under Federal law;
(2)
affects the jurisdiction or authority of State or Federal agencies to regulate navigable waters;
(3)
modifies or alters the authority or jurisdiction of Federal or State agencies to manage fisheries; or
(4)
authorizes or is intended to result in a change in the accessibility of waters open to hunting, fishing, or other forms of outdoor recreation as of December 26, 2025.
