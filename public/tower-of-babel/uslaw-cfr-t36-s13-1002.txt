36 C.F.R. § 13.1002 — Subsistence resident zone.
Citation: us:cfr:36:13.1002
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-13.1002
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 13 (National Park System Units In Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following communities and areas are included within the resident zone for Gates of the Arctic National Park: Alatna, Allakaket, Ambler, Anaktuvuk Pass, Bettles/Evansville, Hughes, Kobuk, Nuiqsut, Shungnak, and Wiseman.
