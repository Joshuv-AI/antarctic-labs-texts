16 U.S.C. § 939c — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:939c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section939c
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 15A (Great Lakes Fisheries). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is hereby authorized to be appropriated from time to time such sums as may be necessary for carrying out the purposes and provisions of the Convention and this chapter.
