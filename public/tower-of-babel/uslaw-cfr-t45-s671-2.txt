45 C.F.R. § 671.2 — Scope.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 671 (Waste Regulation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These regulations in part 671 apply to any U.S. citizen's use or release of a banned substance, designated pollutant or waste in Antarctica.
