12 C.F.R. § 313.143 — Cancellation of deduction.
Citation: us:cfr:12:313.143
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-313.143
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 313 (Procedures For Collection Of Corporate Debt, Criminal Restitution Debt, And Civil Money Penalty Debt). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the FDIC collects part or all of the debt by other means before deductions are made or completed pursuant to § 313.140, the FDIC shall act promptly to modify or terminate its request for such offset.
