48 C.F.R. § 17.107 — 17.107 Options.
Citation: us:cfr:48:17.107
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-17.107
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 17 (Special Contracting Methods). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Benefits may accrue by including options in a multiyear contract. In that event, contracting officers must follow the requirements of subpart 17.2. Options should not include charges for plant and equipment already amortized, or other nonrecurring charges which were included in the basic contract.
