LJUNGGREN, GUSTAF HÅKAN JORDAN (1823–1905),
Swedish man of letters, was born at Lund on the 6th of March
1823. He was educated at Lund university, where he was
professor of German (1850–1859), of aesthetics (1859–1889) and
rector (1875–1885). He had been a member of the Swedish
Academy for twenty years at the time of his death in September
1905. His most important work, Svenska vitterhetens häfder efter
Gustav III.’s död (5 vols., Lund., 1873–1895), is a comprehensive
study of Swedish literature in the 19th century. His other
works include: Framställning af de förnämste estetiska systemerna
(an exposition of the principal system of aesthetics; 2 vols.,
1856–1860); Svenska dramat intill slutet af 17 århundradet
(a history of the Swedish drama down to the end of the 17th
century, Lund, 1864); an edition (1864) of the Epistlar of Bellman
and Fredman, and a history of the Swedish Academy in the
year of its centenary (1886).

His scattered writings were collected as Smärre Skrifter (3 vols.,
1872–1881).
