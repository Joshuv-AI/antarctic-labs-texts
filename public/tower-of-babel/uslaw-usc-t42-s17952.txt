42 U.S.C. § 17952 — Regulatory references
Citation: us:usc:42:17952
Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 156 (Health Information Technology); Subchapter III (Privacy); Part B (Relationship To Other Laws; Regulatory References; Effective Date; Reports). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each reference in this subchapter to a provision of the Code of Federal Regulations refers to such provision as in effect on February 17, 2009 (or to the most recent update of such provision).
