49 C.F.R. § 234.229 — Shunting sensitivity.
Citation: us:cfr:49:234.229
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-234.229
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 234 (Grade Crossing Safety). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each highway-rail grade crossing train detection circuit shall detect the application of a shunt of 0.06 ohm resistance when the shunt is connected across the track rails of any part of the circuit.
