43 C.F.R. § 2911.2-4 — -4 Execution of lease.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2911.2-4
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2911.2-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2910 (Leases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon receipt of the payments required by § 2911.2-2(b) of this title and not less than 45 days following the publications required by § 2911.2-4 of this title, the authorized officer shall make a decision on the application and, if the application is approved, issue the lease.
