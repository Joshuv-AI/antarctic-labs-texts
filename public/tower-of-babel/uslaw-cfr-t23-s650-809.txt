23 C.F.R. § 650.809 — Movable span bridges.
Citation: us:cfr:23:650.809
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-650.809
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 650 (Bridges, Structures, And Hydraulics). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A fixed bridge shall be selected wherever practicable. If there are social, economic, environmental or engineering reasons which favor the selection of a movable bridge, a cost benefit analysis to support the need for the movable bridge shall he prepared as a part of the preliminary plans.
