45 C.F.R. § 158.342 — Invitation for public comments.
Citation: us:cfr:45:158.342
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-158.342
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 158 (Issuer Use Of Premium Revenue: Reporting And Rebate Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary will invite public comment regarding a State's request for an adjustment to the MLR standard. All public comments must be submitted in writing within 10 days of the posting of the request, and must be submitted in the manner prescribed by the Secretary. The Secretary will consider timely public comments in assessing a State's request for an adjustment to the MLR standard.
