7 C.F.R. § 772.1 — Policy.

Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-772.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 772 (Servicing Minor Program Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Purpose. This part contains the Agency's policies and procedures for servicing Minor Program loans which include: Grazing Association loans, Irrigation and Drainage Association loans, and Non-Farm Enterprise and Recreation loans to individuals.
(b) Appeals. The regulations at 7 CFR parts 11 and 780 apply to decisions made under this part.
