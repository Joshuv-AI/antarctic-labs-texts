50 C.F.R. § 14.250 — What is the purpose of the regulations in this subpart?
Citation: us:cfr:50:14.250
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-14.250
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 14 (Importation, Exportation, And Transportation Of Wildlife). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this subpart implement the Big Cat Public Safety Act (BCPSA), 136 Stat. 2336, which amended the Captive Wildlife Safety Act (CWSA), 117 Stat. 2871, which amended the Lacey Act Amendments of 1981, 16 U.S.C. 3371-3378.
