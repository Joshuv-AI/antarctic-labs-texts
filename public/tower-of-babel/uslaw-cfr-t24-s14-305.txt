24 C.F.R. § 14.305 — Filing and service of documents.
Citation: us:cfr:24:14.305
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-14.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 14 (Implementation Of The Equal Access To Justice Act In Administrative Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any application for an award or other pleading or document related to an application shall be filed and served on all parties to the proceeding in the same manner as other pleadings in the proceeding, except as provided in § 14.205(c) for confidential financial information.
