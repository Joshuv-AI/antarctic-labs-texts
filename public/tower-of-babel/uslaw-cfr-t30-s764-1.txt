30 C.F.R. § 764.1 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:30:764.1
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-764.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 764 (State Processes For Designating Areas Unsuitable For Surface Coal Mining Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part establishes minimum procedures and standards to be included in each approved State program for designating non-Federal and non-Indian lands in a State as unsuitable for all or certain types of surface coal mining operations and for terminating designations.
