39 C.F.R. § 3005.106 — Fees.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3005.106
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3005.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3005 (Privacy Act Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The first copy of any record furnished under the Privacy Act of 1974 will be provided without charge. Additional copies will be charged at the cost of reproduction.
