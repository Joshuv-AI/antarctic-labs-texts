40 C.F.R. § 176.15 — Effect of a tolerance.
Citation: us:cfr:40:176.15
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-176.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 176 (Time-Limited Tolerances For Emergency Exemptions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The establishment of a tolerance under this part does not alter the requirement that any applicant comply with procedures established in part 166 of this chapter for emergency exemptions of FIFRA.
