24 C.F.R. § 570.800 — Urban renewal regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:24:570.800
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Community Planning And Development, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-570.800
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 570 (Community Development Block Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations governing urban renewal projects and neighborhood development programs in subpart N of this part, that were effective immediately before April 19, 1996, will continue to govern the rights and obligations of recipients and HUD with respect to such projects and programs.
