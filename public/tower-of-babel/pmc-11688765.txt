PREDICTING MORTALITY IN ADULTS USING TRANSFORMERS: INSIGHTS FROM THE HEALTH AND RETIREMENT STUDY
Authors: Jordan Weiss, Alaleh Azhir, Nilam Ram, David Rehkopf
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
AbstractWe introduce a natural language processing (NLP)-based prediction model to predict next-wave mortality in respondents from the US-based Health and Retirement Study (HRS). Akin to how NLP models can be used to predict future words in a sentence, we use a transformer architecture to build embeddings of a respondent’s demographic, social, behavioral, and health histories which we then use to predict their probability of death at subsequent waves. Our model surpasses performance metrics established by conventional statistical models (average precision score = 0.900 versus 0.395), and provides new avenues for personalized healthcare in an observational setting.
