10 U.S.C. § 7777 — Permits: military reservations; landing ferries, erecting bridges, driving livestock
Citation: us:usc:10:7777
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7777
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Procurement); Chapter 779 (Real Property). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whenever the Secretary of the Army considers that it can be done without injury to the reservation or inconvenience to the military forces stationed there, he may permit—
(1)
the landing of ferries at a military reservation;
(2)
the erection of bridges on a military reservation; and
(3)
the driving of livestock across a military reservation.
