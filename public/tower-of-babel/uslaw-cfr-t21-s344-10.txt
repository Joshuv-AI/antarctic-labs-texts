21 C.F.R. § 344.10 — Earwax removal aid active ingredient.
Citation: us:cfr:21:344.10
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-344.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 344 (Topical Otic Drug Products For Over-The-Counter Human Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The active ingredient of the product consists of carbamide peroxide 6.5 percent formulated in an anhydrous glycerin vehicle.
