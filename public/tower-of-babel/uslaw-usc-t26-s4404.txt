26 U.S.C. § 4404 — Territorial extent
Citation: us:usc:26:4404
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section4404
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 35 (Taxes On Wagering); Subchapter A (Tax On Wagers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The tax imposed by this subchapter shall apply only to wagers
(1)
accepted in the United States, or
(2)
placed by a person who is in the United States
(A)
with a person who is a citizen or resident of the United States, or
(B)
in a wagering pool or lottery conducted by a person who is a citizen or resident of the United States.
