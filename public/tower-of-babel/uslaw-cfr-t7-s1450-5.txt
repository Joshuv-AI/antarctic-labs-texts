7 C.F.R. § 1450.5 — Performance based on advice or action of USDA.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1450.5
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1450.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1450 (Biomass Crop Assistance Program (Bcap)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The provisions of § 718.303 of this title relating to performance based on the action or advice of an authorized representative of USDA apply to this part, and may be considered as a basis to provide relief to persons subject to sanctions under this part to the extent that relief is otherwise permitted by this part.
