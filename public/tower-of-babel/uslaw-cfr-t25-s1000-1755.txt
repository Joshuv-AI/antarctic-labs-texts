25 C.F.R. § 1000.1755 — What must a Tribe/Consortium do when a program is reassumed?
Citation: us:cfr:25:1000.1755
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary, Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-1000.1755
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 1000 (Annual Funding Agreements Under The Tribal Self-Government Act Amendments To The Indian Self-Determination And Education Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

On the effective date of reassumption, the Tribe/Consortium must, at the request of the Secretary, deliver all property and equipment, and title thereto:
(a) That the Tribe/Consortium received for the program under the funding agreement; and
(b) That has a per item value in excess of $5,000, or as otherwise provided in the funding agreement.
