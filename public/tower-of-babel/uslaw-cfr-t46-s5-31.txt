46 C.F.R. § 5.31 — Incompetence.
Citation: us:cfr:46:5.31
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-5.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 5 (Marine Investigation Regulations—Personnel Action). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Incompetence is the inability on the part of a person to perform required duties, whether due to professional deficiencies, physical disability, mental incapacity, or any combination thereof.
