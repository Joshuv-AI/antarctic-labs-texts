49 C.F.R. § 1120.1 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1120.1
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1120.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1120 (Use Of 1977-1978 Study Of Motor Carrier Platform Handling Factors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this part apply only to Class I and II motor common carriers of general freight subject to accounting instruction number 27 of the Board's Uniform System of Accounts (49 CFR Part 1207).
