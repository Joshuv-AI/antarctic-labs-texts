45 C.F.R. § 674.4 — Restrictions on collection of meteorites in Antarctica.
Citation: us:cfr:45:674.4
Creator: National Science Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-674.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 674 (Antarctic Meteorites). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person may collect meteorites in Antarctica for other than scientific research purposes.
