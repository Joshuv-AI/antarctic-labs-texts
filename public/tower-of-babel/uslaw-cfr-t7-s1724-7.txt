7 C.F.R. § 1724.7 — Debarment and suspension.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1724.7
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1724.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1724 (Electric Engineering, Architectural Services And Design Policies And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Borrowers shall comply with the requirements on debarment and suspension in connection with procurement activities set forth in 2 CFR part 180, as adopted by USDA through 2 CFR part 417, particularly with respect to lower tier transactions, e.g., procurement contracts for goods or services.
