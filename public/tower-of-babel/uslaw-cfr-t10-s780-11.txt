10 C.F.R. § 780.11 — Records of the Board.
Citation: us:cfr:10:780.11
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-780.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 780 (Patent Compensation Board Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The records of the Board in cases filed before it, including the pleadings, the transcript, and the final decision, shall be open to public inspection, except to the extent that such records or portions thereof are withheld from disclosure by the Board pursuant to 10 CFR part 1004.
