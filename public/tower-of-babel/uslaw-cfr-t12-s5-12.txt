12 C.F.R. § 5.12 — Computation of time.
Citation: us:cfr:12:5.12
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-5.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 5 (Rules, Policies, And Procedures For Corporate Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In computing the period of days, the OCC does not include the day of the act or event (e.g., the date a filing is received by the OCC) from which the period begins to run. When the last day of a time period is a Saturday, Sunday, or Federal holiday, the time period runs until the end of the next day that is not a Saturday, Sunday or Federal holiday.
