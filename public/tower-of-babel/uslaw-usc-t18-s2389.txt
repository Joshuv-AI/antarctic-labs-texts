18 U.S.C. § 2389 — Recruiting for service against United States
Citation: us:usc:18:2389
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section2389
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 115 (Treason, Sedition, And Subversive Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever recruits soldiers or sailors within the United States, or in any place subject to the jurisdiction thereof, to engage in armed hostility against the same; or
Whoever opens within the United States, or in any place subject to the jurisdiction thereof, a recruiting station for the enlistment of such soldiers or sailors to serve in any manner in armed hostility against the United States—
Shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than five years, or both.
