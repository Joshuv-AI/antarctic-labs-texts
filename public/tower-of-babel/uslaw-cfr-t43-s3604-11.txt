43 C.F.R. § 3604.11 — How do I apply for a free use permit?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3604.11
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3604.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3600 (Mineral Materials Disposal). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you wish to apply for free use of mineral materials, you may file a letter of request or a BLM standard application form approved by the Office of Management and Budget.
