24 C.F.R. § 883.606 — Administration fee.
Citation: us:cfr:24:883.606
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary For Housing-Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development (Section 8 Housing Assistance Programs, Section 202 Direct Loan Program, Section 202 Supportive Housing For The Elderly Program And Section 811 Supportive Housing For Persons With Disabilities Program)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-883.606
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 883 (Section 8 Housing Assistance Payments Program—State Housing Agencies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The State Agency is responsible for administration of the Contract subject to periodic review and audit by HUD.
(b) The Agency is entitled to a reasonable fee, determined by HUD, for administering a Contract on newly constructed or substantially rehabilitated units provided there is no override on the permanent loan granted by the Agency to the owner for a project containing assisted units.
