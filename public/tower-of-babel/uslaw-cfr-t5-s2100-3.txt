5 C.F.R. § 2100.3 — Procedure for requesting information.
Citation: us:cfr:5:2100.3
Creator: Armed Forces Retirement Home
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-2100.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 2100 (Armed Forces Retirement Home Privacy Act Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Individuals shall submit written inquiries regarding all AFRH records to the appropriate facility at the following addresses: Associate Director, Resource Management, U.S. Soldiers' and Airmen's Home, 3700 N. Capitol Street, NW., Washington, DC 20317-0002; or, Administrative Services, U.S. Naval Home, 1800 Beach Drive, Gulfport, Mississippi 39507-1597. All personal (walk-in) requests will require some form of common identification.
