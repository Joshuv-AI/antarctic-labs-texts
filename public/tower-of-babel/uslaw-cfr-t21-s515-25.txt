21 C.F.R. § 515.25 — Revocation of order refusing to approve a medicated feed mill license application or suspending or revoking a license.
Citation: us:cfr:21:515.25
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-515.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 515 (Medicated Feed Mill License). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commissioner of Food and Drugs (the Commissioner), upon his/her own initiative or upon request of an applicant stating reasonable grounds therefor and if the Commissioner finds that the facts so require, may issue an order approving a medicated feed mill license application that previously has had its approval refused, suspended, or revoked.
