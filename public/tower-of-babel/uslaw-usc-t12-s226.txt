12 U.S.C. § 226 — “Federal Reserve Act”
Citation: us:usc:12:226
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section226
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 3 (Federal Reserve System); Subchapter I (Definitions, Organization, And General Provisions Affecting System). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The short title of the Act of December 23, 1913, ch. 6, 38 Stat. 251, shall be the “Federal Reserve Act.”
