15 U.S.C. § 1842 — Authority for loan guarantees; terms and conditions
Citation: us:usc:15:1842
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1842
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 45 (Emergency Loan Guarantees To Business Enterprises). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Board, on such terms and conditions as it deems appropriate, may guarantee, or make commitments to guarantee, lenders against loss of principal or interest on loans that meet the requirements of this chapter.
