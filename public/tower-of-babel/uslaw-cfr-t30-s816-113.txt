30 C.F.R. § 816.113 — Revegetation: Timing.
Citation: us:cfr:30:816.113
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-816.113
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 816 (Permanent Program Performance Standards—Surface Mining Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Disturbed areas shall be planted during the first normal period for favorable planting conditions after replacement of the plant-growth medium. The normal period for favorable planting is that planting time generally accepted locally for the type of plant materials selected.
