Proof.
Let

-subgroups of

Q_{1}\bigcup Q_{2}\bigcup\cdots\bigcup\\
Q_{t}\subseteq F_{|Q|}(G)

. It is clear that
|Q_{1}\bigcup Q_{2}\bigcup\cdots\bigcup Q_{t}|\\
=|Q_{1}\bigcup(Q_{2}\setminus Q_{1})\bigcup(Q_{3}\setminus(Q_{1}\bigcup Q_{2}))\bigcup\cdots\bigcup(Q_{t}\setminus(Q_{1}\bigcup Q_{2}\bigcup\cdots\bigcup Q_{t-1}))|=|Q_{1}|+|Q_{2}\setminus Q_{1}|+|Q_{3}\setminus(Q_{1}\bigcup Q_{2})|+\cdots+|Q_{t}\setminus(Q_{1}\bigcup Q_{2}\bigcup\cdots\bigcup Q_{t-1})|

Note that for every

|Q_{j}\setminus(Q_{1}\bigcup Q_{2}\bigcup\cdots\bigcup Q_{j-1})|=|Q_{j}\setminus(Q_{j}\bigcap(Q_{1}\bigcup\\
Q_{2}\bigcup\cdots\bigcup Q_{j-1}))|=|Q_{j}\setminus((Q_{j}\bigcap Q_{1})\bigcup(Q_{j}\bigcap Q_{2})\bigcup\cdots\bigcup(Q_{j}\bigcap Q_{j-1}))|

is a cyclic group of prime-power order,
