7 U.S.C. § 4803 — Pork and pork product orders
Citation: us:usc:7:4803
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section4803
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 79 (Pork Promotion, Research, And Consumer Information). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
To carry out this chapter, the Secretary shall, in accordance with this chapter, issue and, from time to time, amend orders applicable to persons engaged in—
(1)
the production and sale of porcine animals, pork, and pork products in the United States; and
(2)
the importation of porcine animals, pork, or pork products into the United States.
(b)
The Secretary may issue such regulations as are necessary to carry out this chapter.
