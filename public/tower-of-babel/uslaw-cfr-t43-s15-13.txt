43 C.F.R. § 15.13 — Report of accidents.
Citation: us:cfr:43:15.13
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-15.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 15 (Key Largo Coral Reef Preserve). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Accidents involving injury to life or property shall be reported as soon as possible by the person or persons involved to the officer in charge of the Preserve.
