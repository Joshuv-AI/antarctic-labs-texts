21 C.F.R. § 12.83 — Advisory opinions.
Citation: us:cfr:21:12.83
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-12.83
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 12 (Formal Evidentiary Public Hearing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before or during a hearing, a person may, under § 10.85, request the Commissioner for an advisory opinion on whether any regulation or order under consideration in the proceeding applies to a specific situation.
