48 C.F.R. § 1552.332-39 — 1552.332-39 Unenforceability of unauthorized obligations (far deviation).
Citation: us:cfr:48:1552.332-39
Creator: Chapter 15—Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1552.332-39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1552 (Solicitation Provisions And Contract Clauses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As prescribed in 1513.507(b) and 1532.1070, use clause 1552.332-39 (FAR DEVIATION) instead of the nondeviated version for purchase orders, modifications and contracts that include commercial supplier agreements.
