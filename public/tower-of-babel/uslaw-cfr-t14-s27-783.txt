14 C.F.R. § 27.783 — Doors.
Citation: us:cfr:14:27.783
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-27.783
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 27 (Airworthiness Standards: Normal Category Rotorcraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each closed cabin must have at least one adequate and easily accessible external door.
(b) Each external door must be located where persons using it will not be endangered by the rotors, propellers, engine intakes, and exhausts when appropriate operating procedures are used. If opening procedures are required, they must be marked inside, on or adjacent to the door opening device.
