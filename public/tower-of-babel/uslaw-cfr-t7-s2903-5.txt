7 C.F.R. § 2903.5 — Matching requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:7:2903.5
Creator: Office Of Energy Policy And New Uses, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-2903.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 2903 (Biodiesel Fuel Education Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

There are no matching funds requirements for the Biodiesel Fuel Education Program and matching resources will not be factored into the review process as evaluation criteria.
