40 C.F.R. § 97.82 — NOX authorized account representative.
Citation: us:cfr:40:97.82
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-97.82
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 97 (Federal Nox Budget Trading Program, Cair Nox And So2 Trading Programs, Csapr Nox And So2 Trading Programs, And Texas So2 Trading Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A unit for which an application for a NOX Budget opt-in permit is submitted, or a NOX Budget opt-in unit, located at the same source as one or more NOX Budget units, shall have the same NOX authorized account representative as such NOX Budget units.
