50 U.S.C. § 3933 — Fines and penalties under contracts
Citation: us:usc:50:3933
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section3933
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 50 (Servicemembers Civil Relief); Subchapter II (General Relief). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Prohibition of penalties
When an action for compliance with the terms of a contract is stayed pursuant to this chapter, a penalty shall not accrue for failure to comply with the terms of the contract during the period of the stay.
(b) Reduction or waiver of fines or penalties
If a servicemember fails to perform an obligation arising under a contract and a penalty is incurred arising from that nonperformance, a court may reduce or waive the fine or penalty if—
(1)
the servicemember was in military service at the time the fine or penalty was incurred; and
(2)
the ability of the servicemember to perform the obligation was materially affected by such military service.
