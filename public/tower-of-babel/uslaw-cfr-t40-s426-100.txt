40 C.F.R. § 426.100 — Applicability; description of the glass tubing (Danner) manufacturing subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:426.100
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-426.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 426 (Glass Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges resulting from the process by which raw materials are melted in a furnace and glass tubing mechanically drawn from the furnace horizontally by means of the Danner process, which requires the intermittent quenching of cullet.
