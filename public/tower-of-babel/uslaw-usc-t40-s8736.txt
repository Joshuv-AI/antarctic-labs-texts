40 U.S.C. § 8736 — Execution of deeds
Citation: us:usc:40:8736
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section8736
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part D (Public Buildings, Grounds, And Parks In The District Of Columbia); Chapter 87 (Physical Development Of National Capital Region); Subchapter IV (Acquiring And Disposing Of Land). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Mayor of the District of Columbia may execute deeds of conveyance for real estate sold under this subchapter. The deeds shall contain a full description of the land sold as required by law.
