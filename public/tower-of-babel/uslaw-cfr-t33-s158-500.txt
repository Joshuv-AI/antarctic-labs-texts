33 C.F.R. § 158.500 — Draining cargo area and piping systems.
Citation: us:cfr:33:158.500
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-158.500
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 158 (Reception Facilities For Oil, Noxious Liquid Substances, And Garbage). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The person in charge shall ensure that each cargo hose and each piping system containing NLS received from each oceangoing ship carrying NLS cargo is not drained back into the ship.
