21 C.F.R. § 601.8 — Publication of revocation.
Citation: us:cfr:21:601.8
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-601.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 601 (Licensing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commissioner, following revocation of a biologics license under 21 CFR 601.5(b), will publish a notice in the Federal Register with a statement of the specific grounds for the revocation.
