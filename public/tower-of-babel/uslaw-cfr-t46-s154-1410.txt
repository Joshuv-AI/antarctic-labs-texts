46 C.F.R. § 154.1410 — Decontamination shower.
Citation: us:cfr:46:154.1410
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-154.1410
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 154 (Safety Standards For Self-Propelled Vessels Carrying Bulk Liquefied Gases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When Table 4 references this section, a vessel carrying the listed cargo must have a decontamination shower and an eye wash that:
(a) Are on the weatherdeck; and
(b) Have their location marked EMERGENCY SHOWER in letters:
(1) 7.6 cm (3 in.) high; and
(2) 5.1 cm (2 in.) wide.
