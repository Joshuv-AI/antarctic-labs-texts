42 U.S.C. § 4628 — State acting as agent for Federal program
Citation: us:usc:42:4628
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section4628
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 61 (Uniform Relocation Assistance And Real Property Acquisition Policies For Federal And Federally Assisted Programs); Subchapter II (Uniform Relocation Assistance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whenever real property is acquired by a State agency at the request of a Federal agency for a Federal program or project, such acquisition shall, for the purposes of this chapter, be deemed an acquisition by the Federal agency having authority over such program or project.
