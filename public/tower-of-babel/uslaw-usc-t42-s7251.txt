42 U.S.C. § 7251 — General authority
Citation: us:usc:42:7251
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section7251
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 84 (Department Of Energy); Subchapter VI (Administrative Provisions); Part C (General Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To the extent necessary or appropriate to perform any function transferred by this chapter, the Secretary or any officer or employee of the Department may exercise, in carrying out the function so transferred, any authority or part thereof available by law, including appropriation Acts, to the official or agency from which such function was transferred.
