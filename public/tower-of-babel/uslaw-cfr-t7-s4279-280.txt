7 C.F.R. § 4279.280 — Changes in Borrowers.
Citation: us:cfr:7:4279.280
Creator: Rural Business-Cooperative Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-4279.280
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 4279 (Guaranteed Loanmaking). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any changes in Borrower ownership or organization prior to the issuance of the Loan Note Guarantee must meet the eligibility requirements of the Program and be approved by the Agency.
