29 C.F.R. § 100.619 — When a debt may be compromised.
Citation: us:cfr:29:100.619
Creator: National Labor Relations Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-100.619
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 100 (Administrative Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The NLRB may compromise a debt not in excess of the monetary limitation in accordance with 31 CFR part 902 if it has not been referred to the Department of Justice for litigation.
