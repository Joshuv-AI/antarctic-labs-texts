28 U.S.C. § 1695 — Stockholder’s derivative action
Citation: us:usc:28:1695
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1695
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part V (Procedure); Chapter 113 (Process). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Process in a stockholder’s action in behalf of his corporation may be served upon such corporation in any district where it is organized or licensed to do business or is doing business.
