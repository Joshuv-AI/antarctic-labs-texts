20 C.F.R. § 219.41 — Evidence of relationship of grandchild or stepgrandchild.
Citation: us:cfr:20:219.41
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-219.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 219 (Evidence Required For Payment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the child is the grandchild or stepgrandchild of the employee, the Board will require the kind of evidence described in §§ 219.36-219.38 that shows that child's relationship to his or her parents and his or her parents' relationship to the employee.
