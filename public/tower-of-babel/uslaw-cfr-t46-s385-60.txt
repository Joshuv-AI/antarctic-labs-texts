46 C.F.R. § 385.60 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:46:385.60
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-385.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 385 (Research And Development Grant And Cooperative Agreements Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Sections 385.61 through 385.62 describe the form and content of the two parts which comprise a grant agreement or a cooperative agreement which will be executed by MarAd and a recipient of financial assistance.
