46 C.F.R. § 173.059 — Penetrations and openings in watertight bulkheads.
Citation: us:cfr:46:173.059
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-173.059
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 173 (Special Rules Pertaining To Vessel Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Penetrations and openings in watertight bulkheads must comply with the requirements in subpart E of part 171 of this subchapter or §§ 179.320, 179.330, and 179.340 in subchapter T of this chapter.
