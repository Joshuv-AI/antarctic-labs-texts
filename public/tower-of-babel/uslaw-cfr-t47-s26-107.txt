47 C.F.R. § 26.107 — Restrictions on the operation of stations.
Citation: us:cfr:47:26.107
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-26.107
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 26 (Space Launch Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Stations in the Space Launch Services may operate in a particular frequency band only if they have been registered pursuant to this subpart and cleared to operate in that frequency band by the space launch frequency coordinator using the frequency coordination process set forth in subpart C of this part.
