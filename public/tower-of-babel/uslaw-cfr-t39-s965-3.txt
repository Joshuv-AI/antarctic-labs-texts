39 C.F.R. § 965.3 — Notice to parties.
Citation: us:cfr:39:965.3
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-965.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 965 (Mail Disputes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon receipt of a mail dispute case, the Recorder, Office of the Judicial Officer, United States Postal Service, 2101 Wilson Boulevard, Suite 600, Arlington, VA 22201-3078, will send a notice of docketing and submission due date to the parties together with a copy of these rules.
