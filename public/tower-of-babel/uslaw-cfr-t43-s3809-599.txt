43 C.F.R. § 3809.599 — What if the amount forfeited exceeds the cost of reclamation?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3809.599
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3809.599
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3800 (Mining Claims Under The General Mining Laws). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the amount of financial guarantee forfeited is more than the amount necessary to complete reclamation, BLM will return the unused funds within a reasonable amount of time to the party from whom they were collected.
