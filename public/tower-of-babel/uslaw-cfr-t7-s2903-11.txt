7 C.F.R. § 2903.11 — Acknowledgment of applications.
Citation: us:cfr:7:2903.11
Creator: Office Of Energy Policy And New Uses, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-2903.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 2903 (Biodiesel Fuel Education Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The receipt of all applications will be acknowledged. Applicants who do not receive an acknowledgment within 60 days of the submission deadline should contact the program contact indicated on the program announcement. Once the application has been assigned a proposal number, that number should be cited on all future correspondence.
