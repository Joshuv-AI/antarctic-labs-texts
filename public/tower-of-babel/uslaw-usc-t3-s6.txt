3 U.S.C. § 6 — Duties of Archivist
Citation: us:usc:3:6
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title3-section6
Context: United States Code, Title 3 (The President); Chapter 1 (Presidential Elections And Vacancies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The certificates of ascertainment of appointment of electors received by the Archivist of the United States under section 5 shall—
(1)
be preserved for one year;
(2)
be a part of the public records of such office; and
(3)
be open to public inspection.
