48 C.F.R. § 1331.205-70 — 1331.205-70 Duplication of effort.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1331.205-70
Creator: Chapter 13—Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1331.205-70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1331 (Contract Cost Principles And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department will not pay any costs for work that is duplicative of costs charged against any other contract, subcontract or Government source. Insert clause 1352.231-71, Duplication of Effort, in all cost-reimbursement, time and materials, and labor hour solicitations and contracts when applicable.
