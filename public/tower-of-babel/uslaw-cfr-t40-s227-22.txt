40 C.F.R. § 227.22 — Assessment of impact.
Citation: us:cfr:40:227.22
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-227.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 227 (Criteria For The Evaluation Of Permit Applications For Ocean Dumping Of Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The assessment of impact on other uses of the ocean will consider both temporary and long-range effects within the state of the art, but particular emphasis will be placed on any irreversible or irretrievable commitment of resources that would result from the proposed dumping.
