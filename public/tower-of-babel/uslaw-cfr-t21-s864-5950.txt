21 C.F.R. § 864.5950 — Blood volume measuring device.
Citation: us:cfr:21:864.5950
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-864.5950
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 864 (Hematology And Pathology Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. A blood volume measuring device is a manual, semiautomated, or automated system that is used to calculate the red cell mass, plasma volume, and total blood volume.
(b) Classification. Class II (performance standards).
