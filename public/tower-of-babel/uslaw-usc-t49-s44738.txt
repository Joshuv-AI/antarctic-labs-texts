49 U.S.C. § 44738 — Training on human trafficking for certain staff
Citation: us:usc:49:44738
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section44738
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Air Commerce And Safety); Subpart iii (Safety); Chapter 447 (Safety Regulation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In addition to other training requirements, each air carrier shall provide training to ticket counter agents, gate agents, and other air carrier workers whose jobs require regular interaction with passengers on recognizing and responding to potential human trafficking victims.
