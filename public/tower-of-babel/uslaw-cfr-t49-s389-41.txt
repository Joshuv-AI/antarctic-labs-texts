49 C.F.R. § 389.41 — Severability.
Citation: us:cfr:49:389.41
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-389.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 389 (Rulemaking Procedures—Federal Motor Carrier Safety Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If any provision of a rule issued under this part is held invalid, the remaining provisions are not affected unless specifically stated otherwise in the rule.
