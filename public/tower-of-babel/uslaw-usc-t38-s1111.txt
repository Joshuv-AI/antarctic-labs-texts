38 U.S.C. § 1111 — Presumption of sound condition
Citation: us:usc:38:1111
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section1111
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 11 (Compensation For Service-Connected Disability Or Death); Subchapter II (Wartime Disability Compensation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purposes of section 1110 of this title, every veteran shall be taken to have been in sound condition when examined, accepted, and enrolled for service, except as to defects, infirmities, or disorders noted at the time of the examination, acceptance, and enrollment, or where clear and unmistakable evidence demonstrates that the injury or disease existed before acceptance and enrollment and was not aggravated by such service.
