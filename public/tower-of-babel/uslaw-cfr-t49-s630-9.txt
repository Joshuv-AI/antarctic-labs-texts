49 C.F.R. § 630.9 — Notice of FTA action.
Citation: us:cfr:49:630.9
Creator: Federal Transit Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-630.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 630 (National Transit Database). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before taking final action under §§ 630.5 or 630.8, FTA will transmit a written request to the reporting entity to provide the necessary information within a specified reasonable period of time. FTA will advise the reporting entity of its final decision.
