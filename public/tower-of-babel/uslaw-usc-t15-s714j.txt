15 U.S.C. § 714j — Utilization of associations and trade facilities
Citation: us:usc:15:714j
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section714j
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 15 (Economic Recovery); Subchapter II (Commodity Credit Corporation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Corporation may, in the conduct of its business, utilize on a contract or fee basis, committees or associations of producers, producer-owned and producer-controlled cooperative associations, and trade facilities.
