26 U.S.C. § 5849 — Citation of chapter
Citation: us:usc:26:5849
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section5849
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 53 (Machine Guns, Destructive Devices, And Certain Other Firearms); Subchapter B (General Provisions And Exemptions); Part I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter may be cited as the “National Firearms Act” and any reference in any other provision of law to the “National Firearms Act” shall be held to refer to the provisions of this chapter.
