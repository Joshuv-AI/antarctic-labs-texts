45 C.F.R. § 1605.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1605.1
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1605.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1605 (Appeals On Behalf Of Clients). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part is intended to promote efficient and effective use of Corporation funds. It does not apply to any case or matter in which assistance is not being rendered with funds provided under the Act.
