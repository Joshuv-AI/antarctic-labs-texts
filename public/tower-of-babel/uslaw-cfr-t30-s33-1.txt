30 C.F.R. § 33.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:30:33.1
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-33.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 33 (Dust Collectors For Use In Connection With Rock Drilling In Coal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part set forth the requirements for dust collectors used in connection with rock drilling in coal mines to procure their certification as permissible for use in coal mines; procedures for applying for such certification; and fees.
