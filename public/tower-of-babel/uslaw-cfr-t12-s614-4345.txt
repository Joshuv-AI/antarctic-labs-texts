12 C.F.R. § 614.4345 — Guaranty agreements.
Citation: us:cfr:12:614.4345
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-614.4345
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 614 (Loan Policies And Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Guaranty agreements under which a percentage of the risk associated with specific loans is assumed may be entered into by or among System banks and associations.
