14 C.F.R. § 27.687 — Spring devices.
Citation: us:cfr:14:27.687
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-27.687
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 27 (Airworthiness Standards: Normal Category Rotorcraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each control system spring device whose failure could cause flutter or other unsafe characteristics must be reliable.
(b) Compliance with paragraph (a) of this section must be shown by tests simulating service conditions.
