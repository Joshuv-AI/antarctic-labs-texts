42 U.S.C. § 280l–2 — Prioritization of evaluation by Secretary
Citation: us:usc:42:280l–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section280l–2
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter II (General Powers And Duties); Part U (Employer-Based Wellness Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall evaluate, in accordance with this part, all programs funded through the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention before conducting such an evaluation of privately funded programs unless an entity with a privately funded wellness program requests such an evaluation.
