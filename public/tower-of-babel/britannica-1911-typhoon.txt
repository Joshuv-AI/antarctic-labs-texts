TYPHOON (probably from the Arabic and Hindustani tufān,
a tempest, which is perhaps derived from Typhon, q.v.: the Chinese t'ai fung, strong wind, is not used in application to typhoons), the name given to a heavy cyclonic storm in the seas fringing the eastern coast of Asia from Japan to the
Philippine Islands. Typhoons generally occur in a series
during the August, September and October, the
season when the belt of equatorial calms in the Pacific Ocean
reaches its northerly extension.
