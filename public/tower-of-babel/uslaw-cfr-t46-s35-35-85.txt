46 C.F.R. § 35.35-85 — -85 Air compressors—TB/ALL.
Citation: us:cfr:46:35.35-85
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-35.35-85
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 35 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person may operate, install, or reinstall an air compressor in a cargo area described in § 32.35-15 of this subchapter.
