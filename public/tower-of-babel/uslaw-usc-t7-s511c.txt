7 U.S.C. § 511c — Demonstration of official standards; samples; cost
Citation: us:usc:7:511c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section511c
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 21A (Tobacco Inspection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to demonstrate the official standards; to prepare and distribute, upon request, samples, illustrations, or sets thereof; and to make reasonable charges therefore: Provided, That in no event shall charges be in excess of the cost of said samples, illustrations, and services so rendered.
