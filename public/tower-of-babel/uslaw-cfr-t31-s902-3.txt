31 C.F.R. § 902.3 — Enforcement policy.
Citation: us:cfr:31:902.3
Creator: Federal Claims Collection Standards (Department Of The Treasury—Department Of Justice)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-902.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 902 (Standards For The Compromise Of Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pursuant to this part, agencies may compromise statutory penalties, forfeitures, or claims established as an aid to enforcement and to compel compliance, if the agency's enforcement policy in terms of deterrence and securing compliance, present and future, will be adequately served by the agency's acceptance of the sum to be agreed upon.
