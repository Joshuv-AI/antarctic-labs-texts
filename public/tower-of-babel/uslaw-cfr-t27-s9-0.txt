27 C.F.R. § 9.0 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:27:9.0
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-9.0
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 9 (American Viticultural Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part relate to American viticultural areas created under the authority of the Federal Alcohol Administration Act and referred to in § 4.25(e) of this chapter.
