33 C.F.R. § 144.01-1 — -1 Life floats.
Citation: us:cfr:33:144.01-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-144.01-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 144 (Lifesaving Appliances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each manned platform shall be provided with at least two approved life floats. The life floats shall have sufficient capacity to accommodate all persons present at any one time.
