45 C.F.R. § 2556.7 — Are waivers of the regulations in this part allowed?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2556.7
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2556.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2556 (Volunteers In Service To America). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon a determination of good cause, the Chief Executive Officer of AmeriCorps may, subject to statutory limitations, waive any provisions of this part.
