38 C.F.R. § 80.9 — Withdrawal of grant application.
Citation: us:cfr:38:80.9
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-80.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 80 (Veteran And Spouse Transitional Assistance Grant Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applicants may withdraw a VSTAGP application submitted through Grants.gov by writing to the specified VA point of contact. An applicant must provide a rationale for the withdrawal request as specified in the NOFO.
