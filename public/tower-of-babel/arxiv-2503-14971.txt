Band gaps and phonons of quasi-bulk rocksalt ScN
Jona Grümbel, Yuichi Oshima, Adam Dubroka, Manfred Ramsteiner, Rüdiger Goldhahn, Martin Feneberg

Abstract
ScN is an emerging transition metal nitride with unique physical properties arising from the d d -electrons of Sc. In this work we present the results of optical characterization techniques spectroscopic ellipsometry, Raman spectroscopy, and photoluminescence measurements of a 40 µ ​ m 40\text{\,}\mathrm{\SIUnitSymbolMicro m} thick, fully relaxed, and only weakly n-type doped ( n = 1.2 × 10 18 cm − 3 n=$1.2\text{\times}{10}^{18}\text{\,}{\mathrm{cm}}^{-3}$ ) ScN film deposited by halide vapor phase epitaxy (HVPE) on r-sapphire substrate. Spectroscopic ellipsometry yields an indirect bandgap of 1.1 eV 1.1\text{\,}\mathrm{eV} while the lowest direct interband transition is observed at E g , opt = 2.16 eV E_{\mathrm{g,opt}}=$2.16\text{\,}\mathrm{eV}$ in the dielectric function. A broad luminescence feature at 2.15 eV 2.15\text{\,}\mathrm{eV} is observed, matching this transition. We derive an estimate for the exciton binding energy ( E bX ≈ 14 meV E_{\mathrm{bX}}\approx$14\text{\,}\mathrm{meV}$ ) as well as the Born effective charges Z Sc ∗ = − Z N ∗ = 3.78 Z^{*}_{\mathrm{Sc}}=-Z^{*}_{\mathrm{N}}=3.78 . In the infrared spectral region we observe a strong phonon and a weak plasmon absorption. We precisely determine the transverse optical phonon eigenfrequency ( ω TO = 340.7 cm − 1 \omega_{\text{TO}}=$340.7\text{\,}{\mathrm{cm}}^{-1}$ ), the high frequency dielectric constant ( ε ∞ = 8.3 \varepsilon_{\infty}=8.3 ) and the static dielectric constant ( ε stat = 29.5 \varepsilon_{\text{stat}}=29.5 ). Raman measurements using various excitation energies show resonant multi-phonon scattering up to 6LO ( 6 ​ t ​ h 6th order overtone for longitudinal optical (LO) phonons) for excitation above the optical band gap ( E Laser > E g , opt E_{\text{Laser}}>E_{\mathrm{g},\text{opt}} ), where the allowed 2 2 LO scattering is the dominant scattering mechanism for all excitation energies . Their characteristic parameters determined from Lorentzian line shape fitting yield ω LO = 681 cm − 1 \omega_{\text{LO}}=$681\text{\,}{\mathrm{cm}}^{-1}$ and an increased broadening and reduced asymmetry for higher LO scattering order n n .

V
Conclusions

In summary, we investigated a HVPE grown quasi-bulk rs-ScN single crystal and derived fundamental material parameters which are listed in Tab.
2
(see supplement for error analysis
[
47
]
).
UV-SE
precisely yields an absorption edge of
E_{g}=$2.16\text{\,}\mathrm{eV}$
from an Elliott model fit as well as
\varepsilon_{\infty}=8.3
from Shokhovets model fit. Additionally, an estimate of the exciton binding energy of
E_{\mathrm{bX}}=$14\text{\,}\mathrm{meV}$
was extracted.
IR-SE
and IR reflectivity showed a strong phonon and a weak free carrier (plasmon) absorption. Drude-Lorentz fitting yields
\omega_{\text{TO}}=$340.7\text{\,}{\mathrm{cm}}^{-1}$
,
\gamma_{\text{TO}}=$3.7\text{\,}{\mathrm{cm}}^{-1}$
, and
\varepsilon_{\text{stat}}=29.5
.

Checking the Lyddane-Sachs-Teller (LST) relation yields
\frac{\omega_{LO}^{2}}{\omega_{TO}^{2}}\frac{\varepsilon_{\infty}}{\varepsilon_{\text{stat}}}=1.14\pm 0.06
, which is significantly off the expected value of 1. Deriving the LO frequency from LST yields
\omega_{LO}^{(\text{LST})}=$640\pm\pm 20\text{\,}{\mathrm{cm}}^{-1}$
, which is much closer to theoretically calculated values than our Raman result. Possible reasons for this deviation are the large LO broadening, which is neglected in the LST relation, or the coupling of the LO phonon mode with free charge carriers. Born effective charges are calculated as
Z^{*}_{\text{Sc}}=3.81
and
Z^{*(\text{LST})}_{\text{Sc}}=3.5
using
\omega_{\text{LO}}
and
\omega_{LO}^{(\text{LST})}
, respectively.

The free carrier absorption could not be described adequately by the Drude model. From Raman scattering experiments at different excitation wavelengths we observed efficient multi-phonon scattering up to 4LO and 7TO, and even 6LO and 9TO from temperature dependent combined Raman/PL spectroscopy. The
n
LO and
n
TO positions and broadening parameters are determined from Lorentzian line shape fits, which describe the spectra adequately for
E_{\text{Laser}}=$2.62\text{\,}\mathrm{eV}$
. The LO broadening parameter increases with higher scattering order
n
and the determined first order LO-frequency is
\omega_{\text{LO}}=$684.5\text{\,}{\mathrm{cm}}^{-1}$
in good agreement with previous experiments
[
50
,
29
]
. In a combined Raman and PL measurement we observed a broad luminescence signal at
\approx$2.15\text{\,}\mathrm{eV}$
which perfectly matches the
UV-SE
determined direct bandgap of
2.16\text{\,}\mathrm{eV}
. From this we assume a possible local valence band maximum at the
X
-point in agreement with earlier ARPES measurements
[
3
]
.
