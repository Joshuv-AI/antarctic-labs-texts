30 C.F.R. § 28.3 — Installation, use, and maintenance of approved fuses.
Citation: us:cfr:30:28.3
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-28.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 28 (Fuses For Use With Direct Current In Providing Short-Circuit Protection For Trailing Cables In Coal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Approved fuses shall be installed and maintained in accordance with the specifications prescribed by the manufacturer of the fuses, and shall be selected and used in accordance with the standards prescribed for short-circuit protective devices for trailing cables in Parts 75 and 77, Subchapter O of this chapter.
