19 C.F.R. § 207.26 — Statements by nonparties.
Citation: us:cfr:19:207.26
Creator: United States International Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-207.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 207 (Investigations Of Whether Injury To Domestic Industries Results From Imports Sold At Less Than Fair Value Or From Subsidized Exports To The United States). Any person other than a party may submit a brief written statement of information pertinent to the investigation within the time specified for the filing of posthearing briefs.
