46 C.F.R. § 67.237 — Requirements for assignments of mortgages.
Citation: us:cfr:46:67.237
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-67.237
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 67 (Documentation Of Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An assignment of mortgage presented for filing and recording must:
(a) Be signed by or on behalf of each assignor; and
(b) Recite the following:
(1) The name and address of each assignor and the interest in the mortgage held by the assignor(s); and
(2) The name and address of each assignee and the interest in the mortgage granted to the assignee(s).
