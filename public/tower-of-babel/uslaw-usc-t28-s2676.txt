28 U.S.C. § 2676 — Judgment as bar
Citation: us:usc:28:2676
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section2676
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part VI (Particular Proceedings); Chapter 171 (Tort Claims Procedure). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The judgment in an action under section 1346(b) of this title shall constitute a complete bar to any action by the claimant, by reason of the same subject matter, against the employee of the government whose act or omission gave rise to the claim.
