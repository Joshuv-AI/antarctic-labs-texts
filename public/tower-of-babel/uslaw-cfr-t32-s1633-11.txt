32 C.F.R. § 1633.11 — Assignment of registrant to a local board.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1633.11
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1633.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1633 (Administration Of Classification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A registrant is assigned to the local board that has jurisdiction over his permanent address that he last furnished the Selective Service System prior to the issuance of his induction order.
(b) The Director may change a registrant's assignment when he deems it necessary to assure the fair and equitable administration of the Selective Service Law.
