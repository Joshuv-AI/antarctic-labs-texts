50 C.F.R. § 270.12 — Notice of Council meetings.
Citation: us:cfr:50:270.12
Creator: National Marine Fisheries Service, National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-270.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 270 (Species-Specific Seafood Marketing Councils). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Council will give NMFS the same notice of its meetings as it gives to its members. NMFS will have the right to participate in all Council meetings.
