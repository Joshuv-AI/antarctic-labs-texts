29 C.F.R. § 417.25 — Certification of results of vote.
Citation: us:cfr:29:417.25
Creator: Office Of Labor-Management Standards, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-417.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 417 (Procedure For Removal Of Local Labor Organization Officers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon receipt of the report of the Director's Representative on the hearing and vote on removal, the Director shall certify the results of the vote to the court as required by section 402(c) of the Act.
