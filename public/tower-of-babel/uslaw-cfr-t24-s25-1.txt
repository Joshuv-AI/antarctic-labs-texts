24 C.F.R. § 25.1 — Scope of rules in this part.
Citation: us:cfr:24:25.1
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-25.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 25 (Mortgagee Review Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules in this part are applicable to the operation of the Mortgagee Review Board and to proceedings arising from administrative actions of the Mortgagee Review Board.
