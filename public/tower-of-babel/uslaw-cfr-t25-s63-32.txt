25 C.F.R. § 63.32 — Under what authority are Indian child protection and family violence prevention program funds awarded?
Citation: us:cfr:25:63.32
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-63.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 63 (Indian Child Protection And Family Violence Prevention). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary is authorized to enter into contracts with Indian tribes, tribal organizations, or tribal consortia pursuant to the Indian Self-Determination and Education Assistance Act, as amended, 25 U.S.C. 450 et seq., for the development and establishment of Indian child protection and family violence prevention programs. This includes compacting with tribes under the Self-Governance program procedures.
