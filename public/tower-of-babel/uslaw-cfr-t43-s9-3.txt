43 C.F.R. § 9.3 — What programs and activities of the Department are subject to these regulations?
Citation: us:cfr:43:9.3
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-9.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 9 (Intergovernmental Review Of Department Of The Interior Programs And Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Secretary publishes in the Federal Register a list of the Department's programs and activities that are subject to these regulations and a list of programs and activities that have existing consultation processes.
(b) With respect to programs and activities that a state chooses to cover, and that have existing consultation processes, the state must agree to adopt those existing processes.
