42 C.F.R. § 407.4 — Basic requirements for entitlement.

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-407.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 407 (Supplementary Medical Insurance (Smi) Enrollment And Entitlement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An individual must meet the following requirements to be entitled to SMI:
(1) Eligibility. The individual must meet the eligibility requirements specified in § 407.10(a).
(2) Enrollment. The individual must enroll for SMI, or must be enrolled by a State under a buy-in agreement as specified in § 407.40.
(b) SMI pays only for covered expenses incurred during an individual's period of entitlement.
