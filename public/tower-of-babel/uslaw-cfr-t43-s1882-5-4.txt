43 C.F.R. § 1882.5-4 — -4 Loan repayment.
Citation: us:cfr:43:1882.5-4
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-1882.5-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 1880 (Financial Assistance, Local Governments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Loan repayment shall be by withholding mineral revenues payable to the qualified State for itself or its political subdivisions under the Act until the full amount of the loan and interest have been recovered.
