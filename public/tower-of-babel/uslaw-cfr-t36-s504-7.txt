36 C.F.R. § 504.7 — Intoxicating beverages and narcotics.
Citation: us:cfr:36:504.7
Creator: Smithsonian Institution
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-504.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 504 (Rules And Regulations Governing Smithsonian Institution Buildings And Grounds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Entering the premises or the operating of a motor vehicle thereon by a person under the influence of any intoxicating beverage or narcotic drug or the use of such drug in or on the premises is prohibited. Consumption of intoxicating beverages on the premises is prohibited unless officially authorized.
