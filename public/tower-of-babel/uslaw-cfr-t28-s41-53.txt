28 C.F.R. § 41.53 — Reasonable accommodation.
Citation: us:cfr:28:41.53
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-41.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 41 (Implementation Of Executive Order 12250, Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap In Federally Assisted Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A recipient shall make reasonable accommodation to the known physical or mental limitations of an otherwise qualified handicapped applicant or employee unless the recipient can demonstrate that the accommodation would impose an undue hardship on the operation of its program.
