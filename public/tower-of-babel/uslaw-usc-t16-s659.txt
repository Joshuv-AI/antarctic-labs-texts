16 U.S.C. § 659 — Sea lions; acts prohibiting killing repealed
Citation: us:usc:16:659
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section659
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 5 (Protection Of Fur Seals And Other Fur-Bearing Animals). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All Acts and parts of Acts making it unlawful to kill sea lions, as game animals or otherwise, in the waters of the Territory of Alaska are repealed.
