6 U.S.C. § 543 — Review of congressional committee structures
Citation: us:usc:6:543
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section543
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 1 (Homeland Security Organization); Subchapter XII (Transition); Part A (Reorganization Plan). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the sense of Congress that each House of Congress should review its committee structure in light of the reorganization of responsibilities within the executive branch by the establishment of the Department.
