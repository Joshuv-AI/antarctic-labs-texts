36 C.F.R. § 293.12 — Access to surrounded State and private lands.
Citation: us:cfr:36:293.12
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-293.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 293 (Wilderness—Primitive Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

States or persons, and their successors in interest, who own land completely surrounded by National Forest Wilderness shall be given access as provided in subpart D of part 251 of this chapter.
