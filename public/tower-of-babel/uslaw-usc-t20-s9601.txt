20 U.S.C. § 9601 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:20:9601
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 76 (Education Research, Statistics, Evaluation, Information, And Dissemination); Subchapter II (Educational Technical Assistance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this subchapter:
(1) In general
The terms “local educational agency” and “State educational agency” have the meanings given those terms in section 7801 of this title.
(2) Secretary
The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of Education.
