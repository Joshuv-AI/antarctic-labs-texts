14 C.F.R. § 1259.300 — Description.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1259.300
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1259.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1259 (National Space Grant College And Fellowship Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

National needs awards may be awarded by the Administrator or designee, Director, NASA Space Grant Program, to meet such needs or problems relating to aerospace identified by the Space Grant Review Panel, by NASA officials, or by any person. NASA may fund such awards in an amount not to exceed 100 percent of the total cost of the program or project.
