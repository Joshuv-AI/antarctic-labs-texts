42 C.F.R. § 55a.105 — a.105 How must grantees carry out their projects?
Citation: us:cfr:42:55a.105
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-55a.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 55a (PART 55a—PROGRAM GRANTS FOR BLACK LUNG CLINICS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Grantees must carry out their projects in accordance with their applications and the provisions of this part.
