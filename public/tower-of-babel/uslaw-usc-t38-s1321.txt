38 U.S.C. § 1321 — Certifications with respect to pay grade
Citation: us:usc:38:1321
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section1321
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 13 (Dependency And Indemnity Compensation For Service-Connected Deaths); Subchapter III (Certifications). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary concerned shall, at the request of the Secretary, certify to the Secretary the pay grade of deceased persons with respect to whose deaths applications for benefits are filed under this chapter. The certification of the Secretary concerned shall be binding upon the Secretary.
