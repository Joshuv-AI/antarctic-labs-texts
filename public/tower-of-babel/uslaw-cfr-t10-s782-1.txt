10 C.F.R. § 782.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:10:782.1
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-782.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 782 (Claims For Patent And Copyright Infringement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this regulation is to set forth policies and procedures for the filing and disposition of claims asserted against the Department of Energy of infringement of privately owned rights in patented inventions or copyrighted works.
