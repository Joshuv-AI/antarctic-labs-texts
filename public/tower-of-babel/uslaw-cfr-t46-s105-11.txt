46 C.F.R. § 105.11 — Prohibitions.
Citation: us:cfr:46:105.11
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-105.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 105 (Commercial Fishing Vessels Dispensing Petroleum Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each vessel to which this part applies is prohibited from transporting Grade A flammable liquids in bulk, or carrying bulk flammable or combustible liquids in portable or temporarily installed dispensing tanks or containers that are either below deck or in closed compartments on or above deck.
