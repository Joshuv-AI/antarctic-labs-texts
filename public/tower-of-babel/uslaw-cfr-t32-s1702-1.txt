32 C.F.R. § 1702.1 — Scope and purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1702.1
Creator: Office Of The Director Of National Intelligence
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1702.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1702 (Procedures Governing The Acceptance Of Service Of Process). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth the ODNI policy concerning service of process upon the ODNI and ODNI employees in their official, individual or combined official and individual capacities. This part is intended to ensure the orderly execution of ODNI affairs and is not intended to impede the legal process.
