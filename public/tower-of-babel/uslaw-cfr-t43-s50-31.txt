43 C.F.R. § 50.31 — What opportunity will the requester have to respond to comments?
Citation: us:cfr:43:50.31
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-50.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 50 (Procedures For Reestablishing A Formal Government-To-Government Relationship With The Native Hawaiian Community). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Following the Web site posting described in § 50.30(b), the requester will have 60 days to respond to any comment or evidence that was timely submitted to the Department in accordance with § 50.30(a)(1) and (a)(2)(iv).
