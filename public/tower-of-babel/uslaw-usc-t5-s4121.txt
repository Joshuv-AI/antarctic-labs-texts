5 U.S.C. § 4121 — Specific training programs
Citation: us:usc:5:4121
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section4121
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart C (Employee Performance); Chapter 41 (Training). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In consultation with the Office of Personnel Management, the head of each agency shall establish—
(1)
a comprehensive management succession program to provide training to employees to develop managers for the agency; and
(2)
a program to provide training to managers on actions, options, and strategies a manager may use in—
(A)
relating to employees with unacceptable performance;
(B)
mentoring employees and improving employee performance and productivity; and
(C)
conducting employee performance appraisals.
