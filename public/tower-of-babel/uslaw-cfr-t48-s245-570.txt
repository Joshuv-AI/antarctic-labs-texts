48 C.F.R. § 245.570 — 245.570 Storage at the Government's expense.
Citation: us:cfr:48:245.570
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-245.570
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 245 (Government Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All storage contracts or agreements shall be separately priced and shall include all costs associated with the storage.
