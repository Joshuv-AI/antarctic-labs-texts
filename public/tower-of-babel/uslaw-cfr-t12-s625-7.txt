12 C.F.R. § 625.7 — Awards against other agencies.
Citation: us:cfr:12:625.7
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-625.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 625 (Application For Award Of Fees And Other Expenses Under The Equal Access To Justice Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an applicant is entitled to an award because it prevails over another agency of the United States that participates in or in any way is a part of an adversary adjudication before the FCA and that agency's position is not substantially justified, the award or an appropriate portion of the award shall be made against that agency.
