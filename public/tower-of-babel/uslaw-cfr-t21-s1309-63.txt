21 C.F.R. § 1309.63 — Transfer of registration.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1309.63
Creator: Drug Enforcement Administration, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1309.63
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1309 (Registration Of Manufacturers, Distributors, Importers And Exporters Of List I Chemicals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No registration or any authority conferred thereby shall be assigned or otherwise transferred except upon such conditions as the Administrator may specifically designate and then only pursuant to his written consent.
