10 U.S.C. § 7437 — Chaplain
Citation: us:usc:10:7437
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7437
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training); Chapter 753 (United States Military Academy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There shall be a chaplain at the Academy, who must be a clergyman, appointed by the President for a term of four years. The chaplain is entitled to a monthly housing allowance in the same amount as the basic allowance for housing allowed to a lieutenant colonel, and to fuel and light for quarters in kind. The chaplain may be reappointed.
