46 U.S.C. § 12137 — Recording of vessels built in the United States
Citation: us:usc:46:12137
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section12137
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part H (Identification Of Vessels); Chapter 121 (Documentation Of Vessels); Subchapter III (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may provide for recording and certifying information about vessels built in the United States that the Secretary considers to be in the public interest.
