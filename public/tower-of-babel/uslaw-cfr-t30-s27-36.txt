30 C.F.R. § 27.36 — Test for adequacy of electrical insulation and clearances.
Citation: us:cfr:30:27.36
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-27.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 27 (Methane-Monitoring Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

MSHA shall examine, and test in a manner it deems suitable, electrical insulation and clearances between electrical conductors to determine adequacy for the intended service.
