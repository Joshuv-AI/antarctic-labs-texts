18 U.S.C. § 502 — Postage and revenue stamps of foreign governments
Citation: us:usc:18:502
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section502
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 25 (Counterfeiting And Forgery). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever forges, or counterfeits, or knowingly utters or uses any forged or counterfeit postage stamp or revenue stamp of any foreign government, shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than five years, or both.
