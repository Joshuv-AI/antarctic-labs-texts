25 C.F.R. § 543.1 — What does this part cover?

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 543 (Minimum Internal Control Standards For Class Ii Gaming). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part establishes the minimum internal control standards for the conduct of Class II games on Indian lands as defined in 25 U.S.C. 2701 et seq.
