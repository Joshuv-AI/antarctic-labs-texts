40 C.F.R. § 27.45 — Deposit in Treasury of United States.
Citation: us:cfr:40:27.45
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-27.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 27 (Program Fraud Civil Remedies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All amounts collected pursuant to this part shall be deposited as miscellaneous receipts in the Treasury of the United States, except as provided in 31 U.S.C. 3806(g).
