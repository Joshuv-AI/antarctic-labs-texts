14 U.S.C. § 549 — Lighthouse and other sites; necessity and sufficiency of cession by State of jurisdiction
Citation: us:usc:14:549
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section549
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 5 (Functions And Powers); Subchapter III (Aids To Navigation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
No lighthouse, beacon, public pier, or landmark, shall be built or erected on any site until cession of jurisdiction over the same has been made to the United States.
(b)
For the purposes of subsection (a), a cession by a State of jurisdiction over a place selected as the site of a lighthouse, or other structure or work referred to in subsection (a), shall be deemed sufficient if the cession contains a reservation that process issued under authority of such State may continue to be served within such place.
(c)
If no reservation of service described in subsection (b) is contained in a cession, all process may be served and executed within the place ceded, in the same manner as if no cession had been made.
