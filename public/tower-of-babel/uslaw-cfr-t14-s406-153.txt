14 C.F.R. § 406.153 — Burden of proof.
Citation: us:cfr:14:406.153
Creator: Commercial Space Transportation, Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-406.153
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 406 (Investigations, Enforcement, And Administrative Review). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except in the case of an affirmative defense, in a civil penalty adjudication the burden of proof is on the complainant.
(b) Except as otherwise provided by statute or rule, the proponent of a motion, request, or order has the burden of proof.
(c) A party who has asserted an affirmative defense has the burden of proving the affirmative defense.
