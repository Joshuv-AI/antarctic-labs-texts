49 C.F.R. § 196.111 — What if a pipeline operator fails to respond to a locate request or fails to accurately locate and mark its pipeline?
Citation: us:cfr:49:196.111
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-196.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 196 (Protection Of Underground Pipelines From Excavation Activity). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

PHMSA may enforce existing requirements applicable to pipeline operators, including those specified in 49 CFR 192.614 and 195.442 and 49 U.S.C. 60114 if a pipeline operator fails to properly respond to a locate request or fails to accurately locate and mark its pipeline. The limitation in 49 U.S.C. 60114(f) does not apply to enforcement taken against pipeline operators and excavators working for pipeline operators.
