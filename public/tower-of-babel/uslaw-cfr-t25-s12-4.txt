25 C.F.R. § 12.4 — Who supervises the Bureau of Indian Affairs uniformed police, detention, and conservation enforcement functions?
Citation: us:cfr:25:12.4
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-12.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 12 (Indian Country Law Enforcement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agency superintendent is directly responsible for the operation and management of BIA uniformed police operations, detention facilities, and conservation enforcement operations at any agency having these programs. The agency superintendent must also ensure technical support is provided to any agency contracting the law enforcement and/or detention program.
