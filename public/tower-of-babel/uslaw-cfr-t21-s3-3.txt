21 C.F.R. § 3.3 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:21:3.3
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-3.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 3 (Product Jurisdiction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This section applies to:
(a) Any combination product, or
(b) Any product where the agency component with primary jurisdiction is unclear or in dispute.
