4 C.F.R. § 25.16 — Penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:4:25.16
Creator: Government Accountability Office
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-4/section-25.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 4 (Accounts), Part 25 (Conduct In The Government Accountability Office Building And On Its Grounds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whoever shall be found guilty of violating any rule or regulation governing the GAO Building is subject to a fine of not more than $500, or imprisonment for not more than 6 months, or both. Nothing in these rules and regulations shall be construed to abrogate any other Federal laws applicable to the GAO Building.
