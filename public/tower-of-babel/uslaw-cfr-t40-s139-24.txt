40 C.F.R. § 139.24 — Motor gasoline and compensating systems.
Citation: us:cfr:40:139.24
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-139.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 139 (Discharges Incidental To The Normal Operation Of Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The requirements in paragraph (b) of this section apply to the discharge of motor gasoline and compensating ambient water added to keep gasoline tanks full to prevent potentially explosive gasoline vapors from forming.
(b) Additional standards applicable to discharges from motor gasoline and compensating systems when a vessel is operating in federally-protected waters are contained in § 139.40(j).
