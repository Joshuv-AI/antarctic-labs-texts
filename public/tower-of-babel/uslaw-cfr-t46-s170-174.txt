46 C.F.R. § 170.174 — Specific applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:46:170.174
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-170.174
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 170 (Stability Requirements For All Inspected Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to each vessel for which the lightweight displacement and centers of gravity must be determined in order to do the calculations required in this subchapter.
