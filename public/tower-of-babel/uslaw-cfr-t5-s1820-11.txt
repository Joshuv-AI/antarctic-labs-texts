5 C.F.R. § 1820.11 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1820.11
Creator: Office Of Special Counsel
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1820.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1820 (Freedom Of Information Act Requests; Production Of Records Or Testimony). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to demands and requests from non-OSC litigants for testimony from current and former OSC employees, contractors, advisors, and consultants relating to official OSC information and/or for production of official OSC records or information in legal proceedings in which OSC is not a party.
