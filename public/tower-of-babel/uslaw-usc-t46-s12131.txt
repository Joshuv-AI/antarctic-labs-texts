46 U.S.C. § 12131 — Command of documented vessels
Citation: us:usc:46:12131
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section12131
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part H (Identification Of Vessels); Chapter 121 (Documentation Of Vessels); Subchapter III (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In General.—
Except as provided in subsection (b), a documented vessel may be placed under the command only of a citizen or noncitizen national (as such term is described in section 308 of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1408)) of the United States.
(b) Exceptions.—
Subsection (a) does not apply to—
(1)
a vessel with only a recreational endorsement; or
(2)
an unmanned barge operating outside of the territorial waters of the United States.
