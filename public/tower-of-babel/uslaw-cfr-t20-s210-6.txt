20 C.F.R. § 210.6 — Service credited for creditable military service.
Citation: us:cfr:20:210.6
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-210.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 210 (Creditable Railroad Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any calendar month in which an employee performed creditable military service, as defined in part 212 of this chapter, shall be counted as a month of service and shall be included in the employee's years of service, as provided for in § 210.5, provided that the employee has not previously been credited with reported or deemed service for an employer for the same month(s).
