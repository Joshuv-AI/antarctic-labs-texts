26 C.F.R. § 2.1-15 — -15 Time for making deposits.
Citation: us:cfr:26:2.1-15
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-2.1-15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 2 (Maritime Construction Reserve Fund). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Proceeds of sale or indemnification. Deposits of amounts representing proceeds of the sale or indemnification for loss of a vessel or share therein must be made within 60 days after receipt by the taxpayer.
(b) Earnings and receipts. Earnings and receipts for the taxable year may be deposited at any time. (See § 2.1-14.)
