40 C.F.R. § 170.102 — Applicability of this subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:40:170.102
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-170.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 170 (Worker Protection Standard). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided by §§ 170.103 and 170.104, this subpart applies when any pesticide product is used on an agricultural establishment in the production of agricultural plants.
