14 C.F.R. § 420.3 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:420.3
Creator: Commercial Space Transportation, Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-420.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 420 (License To Operate A Launch Site). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to any person seeking a license to operate a launch site or to a person licensed under this part. A person operating a site that only supports amateur rocket activities as defined in 14 CFR 1.1, does not need a license under this part to operate the site.
