46 C.F.R. § 14.305 — Entries in continuous discharge book.
Citation: us:cfr:46:14.305
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-14.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 14 (Shipment And Discharge Of Merchant Mariners). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the merchant mariner holds a continuous discharge book, the master or individual in charge of the vessel must make the proper entries in it.
