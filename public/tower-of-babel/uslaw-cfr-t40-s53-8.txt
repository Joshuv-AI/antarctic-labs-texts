40 C.F.R. § 53.8 — Designation of reference and equivalent methods.
Citation: us:cfr:40:53.8
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-53.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 53 (Ambient Air Monitoring Reference And Equivalent Methods). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A candidate method determined by the Administrator to satisfy the applicable requirements of this part shall be designated as an FRM or FEM (as applicable) by and upon publication of the designation in the Federal Register. Applicants shall not publicly announce, market, or sell the candidate sampler and analyzer as an approved FRM or FEM (as applicable) until the designation is published in the Federal Register.
(b) Upon designation, a notice indicating that the method has been designated as a FRM or FEM shall be sent to the applicant.
(c) The Administrator will maintain a current list of methods designated as FRM or FEM in accordance with this part and will send a copy of the list to any person or group upon request. A copy of the list will be available via the Internet and may be available from other sources.
