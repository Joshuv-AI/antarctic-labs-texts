5 C.F.R. § 300.702 — Coverage.
Citation: us:cfr:5:300.702
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-300.702
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 300 (Employment (General)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Appointments in the competitive service, the excepted service, the Senior Executive Service, or any other civil service personnel management system in an executive agency are covered by these regulations.
