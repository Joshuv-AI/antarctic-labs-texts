22 U.S.C. § 3392 — Rule of construction regarding the use of military force
Citation: us:usc:22:3392
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section3392
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 48A (Taiwan Enhanced Resilience); Subchapter V (Rules Of Construction). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter may be construed as authorizing the use of military force or the introduction of United States forces into hostilities.
