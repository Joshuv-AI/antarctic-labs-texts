32 U.S.C. § 706 — Return of arms and equipment upon relief from Federal service
Citation: us:usc:32:706
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title32-section706
Context: United States Code, Title 32 (National Guard); Chapter 7 (Service, Supply, And Procurement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

So far as practicable, whenever units, organizations, or members of the National Guard are returned to their National Guard status under section 325(b) 1 of this title, arms and equipment that the Secretary concerned determines are sufficient to accomplish their peacetime mission shall be returned with them.
