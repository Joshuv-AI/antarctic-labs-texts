\displaystyle\zeta(s)=\chi(s)\zeta(1-s)\quad\text{where}\quad\chi(s):=\pi^{s-1/2}\frac{\Gamma((1-s)/2)}{\Gamma(s/2)}.

\displaystyle N(T)=\frac{T}{2\pi}\bigg(\log\frac{T}{2\pi}-1\bigg)+\frac{7}{8}+O(\log T)

[

\displaystyle\kappa=\mathop{\lim\inf}\limits_{T\to\infty}\frac{{{N_{0}}(T)}}{{N(T)}}.

The history behind the value of

[

consider a weaker form of the Riemann hypothesis.

of

in
