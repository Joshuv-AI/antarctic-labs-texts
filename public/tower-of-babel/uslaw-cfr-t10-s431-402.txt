10 C.F.R. § 431.402 — Preemption of State regulations for commercial HVAC & WH products.
Citation: us:cfr:10:431.402
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-431.402
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 431 (Energy Efficiency Program For Certain Commercial And Industrial Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Beginning on the effective date of such standard, an energy conservation standard set forth in this part for a commercial HVAC & WH product supersedes any State or local regulation concerning the energy efficiency or energy use of that product, except as provided for in Section 345(b)(2)(B)-(D) of the Act.
