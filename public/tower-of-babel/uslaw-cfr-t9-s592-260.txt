9 C.F.R. § 592.260 — Reuse of containers bearing official identification prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:9:592.260
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-592.260
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 592 (Voluntary Inspection Of Egg Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The reuse, by any person, of containers bearing official identification is prohibited unless such identification is applicable in all respects to product being packed therein. In such instances, the container and label may be used provided the packaging is accomplished under the supervision of inspection program personnel or program employee, and the container is in clean, sound condition and lined with a suitable inner liner.
