15 C.F.R. § 791.5 — Effect on other laws.
Citation: us:cfr:15:791.5
Creator: Bureau Of Industry And Security, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-791.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 791 (Securing The Information And Communications Technology And Services Supply Chain). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this part shall be construed as altering or affecting any other authority, process, regulation, investigation, enforcement measure, or review provided by or established under any other provision of Federal law, including prohibitions under the National Defense Authorization Act of 2019, the Federal Acquisition Regulations, or IEEPA, or any other authority of the President or the Congress under the Constitution of the United States.
