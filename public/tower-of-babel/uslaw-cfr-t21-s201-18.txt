21 C.F.R. § 201.18 — Drugs; significance of control numbers.
Citation: us:cfr:21:201.18
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-201.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 201 (Labeling). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The lot number on the label of a drug should be capable of yielding the complete manufacturing history of the package. An incorrect lot number may be regarded as causing the article to be misbranded.
