46 C.F.R. § 109.419 — Report of unsafe machinery.
Citation: us:cfr:46:109.419
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-109.419
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 109 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a boiler, unfired pressure vessel, or other machinery on a unit is unsafe to operate, the master or person in charge shall report the existence of the unsafe condition to the Officer in Charge, Marine Inspection.
