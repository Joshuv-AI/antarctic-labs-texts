14 U.S.C. § 2306 — Voluntary retirement after twenty years’ service
Citation: us:usc:14:2306
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2306
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 23 (Personnel; Enlisted). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any enlisted member who has completed twenty years’ service may, upon his own application, in the discretion of the Commandant, be retired from active service.
