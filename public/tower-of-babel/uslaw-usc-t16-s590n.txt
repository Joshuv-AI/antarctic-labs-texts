16 U.S.C. § 590n — Payments reviewable only by Secretary
Citation: us:usc:16:590n
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section590n
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 3B (Soil Conservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The facts constituting the bases for any payment or grant or the amount thereof authorized to be made under section 590g of this title, when officially determined in conformity with rules or regulations prescribed by the Secretary of Agriculture, shall be reviewable only by the Secretary of Agriculture.
