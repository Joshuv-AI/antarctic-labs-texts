20 C.F.R. § 234.60 — Escheat.
Citation: us:cfr:20:234.60
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-234.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 234 (Lump-Sum Payments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any payment under this part which would be payable to any state, political subdivision of a state, the U.S. government or a foreign government because of the lack of a legal heir, shall remain in the Railroad Retirement Account.
