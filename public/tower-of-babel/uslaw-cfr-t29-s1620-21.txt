29 C.F.R. § 1620.21 — Head of household.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1620.21
Creator: Equal Employment Opportunity Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1620.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1620 (The Equal Pay Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Since a “head of household” or “head of family” status bears no relationship to the requirements of the job or to the individual's performance on the job, such a claimed defense to an alleged EPA violation will be closely scrutinized as stated in § 1620.11(c).
