10 U.S.C. § 20109 — Policy for order to active duty based upon determination by Congress
Citation: us:usc:10:20109
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section20109
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Space Force); Chapter 2003 (Status And Participation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whenever Congress determines that more units and organizations capable of conducting space operations are needed for the national security than are available among those units comprised of members of the Space Force serving on active duty, members of the Space Force not serving on active duty shall be ordered to active duty and retained as long as so needed.
