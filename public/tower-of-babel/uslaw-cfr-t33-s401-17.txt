33 C.F.R. § 401.17 — Pitch indicators and alarms.
Citation: us:cfr:33:401.17
Creator: Great Lakes St. Lawrence Seaway Development Corporation, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-401.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 401 (Seaway Regulations And Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every vessel of 1600 gross registered tons or integrated tug and barge or articulated tug and barge unit of combined 1600 gross registered tons or more equipped with a variable pitch propeller shall be equipped with—
(a) A pitch indicator in the wheelhouse and the engine room; and
(b) Visible and audible pitch alarms, with a time delay of not greater than 8 seconds, in the wheelhouse and engine room to indicate wrong way pitch.
