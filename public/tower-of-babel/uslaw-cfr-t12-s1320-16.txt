12 C.F.R. § 1320.16 — Extension of time periods.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1320.16
Creator: Financial Stability Oversight Council
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1320.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1320 (Designation Of Financial Market Utilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Council may extend any time period established in § 1320.12, § 1320.14, or § 1320.15 as the Council determines to be necessary or appropriate.
