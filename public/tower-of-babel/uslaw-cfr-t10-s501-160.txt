10 C.F.R. § 501.160 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:10:501.160
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-501.160
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 501 (Administrative Procedures And Sanctions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart provides the procedures by which OFE may initiate enforcement proceedings on its own behalf and by which complaints concerning a violation of the Act or any rule or order thereunder may be filed.
