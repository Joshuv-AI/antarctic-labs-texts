49 U.S.C. § 6108 — Relationship to State laws
Citation: us:usc:49:6108
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section6108
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Chapter 61 (One-Call Notification Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter preempts State law or shall impose a new requirement on any State or mandate revisions to a one-call system.
