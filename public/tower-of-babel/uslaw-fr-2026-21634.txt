General Applicability Public Interest Waiver to Indian Tribes 
Proposed Extension of Manufactured Products Waiver

AGENCY: Office of the Secretary, Interior.

ACTION: Notice of proposed extension of waiver; request for comments.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of the Interior (DOI); Department of 
Agriculture (USDA); Department of Education (ED); Department of Energy 
(DOE); Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD); Environmental 
Protection Agency (EPA); Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA); 
and Department of Commerce (DOC); (hereafter ``the Agencies''), are 
proposing to issue an extension of the manufactured products element of 
the general applicability joint public interest waiver to Indian Tribes 
(issued on January 10, 2025).

DATES: Written comments must be submitted online or by mail no later

INFORMATION.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: This proposed extension of waiver would 
continue to waive the manufactured products requirement of section 
70914 of the Build America, Buy America Act (BABA) included in the 
Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act (Pub. L. 117-58) (Buy America 
Preference) for Federal Financial Assistance agreements awarded to 
Indian Tribes through December 31, 2026. Recipients will be permitted 
to purchase non-compliant manufactured products, regardless of the 
Federal financial assistance amount. For Federal financial assistance 
issued prior to the effective date of the final waiver, the waiver 
would apply to funds not yet expended. The Waiver Type is Public 
Interest, the Waiver Level is General Applicability, All Regions. The 
waiver is applicable to Federal financial assistance obligated on or 
after the effective date of the final waiver, and before its 
expiration. This extension would expire on December 31, 2026.
    The proposed waiver will be posted on each agency's public facing 
web page, and a notice of the proposed waiver will be posted to the 
Made in America website to satisfy the requirement to publish any BABA 
Project Waiver and provide the public with thirty (30) days to submit 
comments. The Agencies seek public and industry comments from all 
interested parties, particularly regarding common, critical 
manufactured products in infrastructure projects; the availability of 
BABA-compliant vendors, including those that are Native enterprises; 
current and anticipated availability of domestic products; and current 
and future states of domestic production and supply chains. Relevant 
information and comments will help the Agencies understand the facts 
and impacts surrounding this waiver request.

Justification for Waiver Extension

    Compliance with the BABA manufactured products requirements 
continues to pose implementation challenges in Tribal communities. This 
extension of the manufactured products portion of the waiver is 
intended to allow infrastructure projects in Tribal communities to 
proceed while allowing Agencies time to examine further modifications 
to the waiver consistent with the President's mandate for strengthening 
Made in America implementation. This extension is also intended to 
allow agencies additional time to move toward efforts to bring Tribal 
recipients into fuller alignment with BABA requirements, including 
through strengthening and coordinating Agency guidance to tribes and 
refining compliance support, training, and technical assistance.
