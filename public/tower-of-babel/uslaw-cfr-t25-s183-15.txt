25 C.F.R. § 183.15 — Must the Tribe submit any reports?
Citation: us:cfr:25:183.15
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-183.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 183 (Use And Distribution Of The San Carlos Apache Tribe Development Trust Fund And San Carlos Apache Tribe Lease Fund). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. The Tribe must submit the following reports after receiving funds under this part:
(a) An Annual Report, that must be submitted no later than December 31 of each year; and
(b) A Financial Audit, that must be submitted no later than March 1 of each year.
