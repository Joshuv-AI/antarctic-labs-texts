7 C.F.R. § 766.55 — Eligibility determination.
Citation: us:cfr:7:766.55
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-766.55
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 766 (Direct Loan Servicing—Special). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within 30 days of a complete DSA application, the Agency will determine if the borrower meets the eligibility requirements for DSA.
