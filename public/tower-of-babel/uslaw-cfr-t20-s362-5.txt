20 C.F.R. § 362.5 — Time limits for filing a claim.
Citation: us:cfr:20:362.5
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-362.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 362 (Employees' Personal Property Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A claim under this part may be considered only if:
(a) The damage or loss occurred after August 31, 1964; and
(b) The claim is filed in writing within two years after the damage, loss or theft occurred or became known to the employee.
