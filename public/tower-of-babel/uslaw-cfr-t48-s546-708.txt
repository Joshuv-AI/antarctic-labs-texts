48 C.F.R. § 546.708 — 546.708 Warranties of data.

Creator: Chapter 5—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-546.708
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 546 (Quality Assurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The contracting officer shall use warranties of data only when both of the following conditions are applicable:
(1) Use of a warranty is in the Government's interest and is documented; and
(2) The contracting director concurs with the decision.
(b) The contracting officer shall consult with the technical or specification manager responsible for developing any warranties of data.
