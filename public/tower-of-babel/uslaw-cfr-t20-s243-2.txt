20 C.F.R. § 243.2 — Legal process for the enforcement of child support and alimony obligations.
Citation: us:cfr:20:243.2
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-243.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 243 (Transfer, Assignment, Or Waiver Of Payments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Benefits paid by the Board are subject to legal process brought for the enforcement of legal obligations to provide child support or to make alimony payments, as provided in part 350 of this chapter.
