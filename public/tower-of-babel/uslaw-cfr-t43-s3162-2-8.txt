43 C.F.R. § 3162.2-8 — -8 Does my responsibility for drainage protection end when I assign or transfer my lease interest?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3162.2-8
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3162.2-8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3160 (Onshore Oil And Gas Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you assign your record title interest in a lease or transfer your operating rights, you are not liable for drainage that occurs after the date we approve the assignment or transfer. However, you remain responsible for the payment of compensatory royalties for any drainage that occurred when you held the lease interest.
