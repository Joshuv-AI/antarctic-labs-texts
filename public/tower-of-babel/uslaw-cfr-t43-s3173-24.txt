43 C.F.R. § 3173.24 — Effective date of an off-lease measurement approval.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3173.24
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3173.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3170 (Onshore Oil And Gas Production). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the BLM approves off-lease measurement, the approval is effective on the date that the approval is issued, unless the approval specifies a different effective date.
