25 C.F.R. § 150.302 — What reports does the LTRO provide?
Citation: us:cfr:25:150.302
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-150.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 150 (Record Of Title To Indian Land). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The LTRO provides the following types of reports for Indian land to those persons or entities authorized to receive such information:
(a) Certified reports, including a Title Status Report, Land Status Map, and, as part of the probate record, the Probate Inventory Report; and
(b) Uncertified reports or other reports based upon the information in the record of title.
