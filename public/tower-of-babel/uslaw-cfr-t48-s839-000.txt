48 C.F.R. § 839.000 — 839.000 Scope of part.
Citation: us:cfr:48:839.000
Creator: Chapter 8—Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-839.000
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 839 (Acquisition Of Information Technology). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes acquisition policies and procedures for use in acquiring VA information technology and information technology-related contracts (see 802.101) and applies to both VA-procured information technology systems as well as interagency acquisitions defined in FAR part 17 and part 817.
