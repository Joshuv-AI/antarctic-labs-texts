Retraction Note: Artificial intelligence-augmented smart grid architecture for cyber intrusion detection and mitigation in electric vehicle charging infrastructure
Authors: Ankita Sharma, Shalli Rani, Mohammad Shabaz
Published: 2026

Retraction of: 10.1038/s41598-025-04984-4, published online 01 July 2025

The Editors have retracted this Article.

Following publication, concerns were raised about the methods, results, and the dataset underlying the study, which is no longer available at the stated source. Additional concerns were noted regarding the use of non-standard vocabulary used in the paper. The Editors requested explanations, the data and the code underlying the paper. Although the Authors did deposit the code, their responses to other concerns were not satisfactory, and they did not provide the data. The Editors have therefore lost confidence in the results of this Article.

Mohammad Shabaz did not explicitly that whether they agree or disagree with the retraction. Ankita Sharma and Shalli Rani did not respond to the Editors’ correspondence about this retraction.
