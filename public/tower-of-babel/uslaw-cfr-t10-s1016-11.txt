10 C.F.R. § 1016.11 — Cancellation of requests for security facility approval.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1016.11
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1016.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1016 (Safeguarding Of Restricted Data By Access Permittees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a request for security facility approval is to be withdrawn or cancelled, the cognizant DOE Office will be notified by the requester immediately by telephone and confirmed in writing so that processing of this approval may be terminated.
