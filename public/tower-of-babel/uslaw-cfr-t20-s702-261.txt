20 C.F.R. § 702.261 — Claimant's contest of actions taken by employer or carrier with respect to the claim.
Citation: us:cfr:20:702.261
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-702.261
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 702 (Administration And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where the claimant contests an action by the employer or carrier reducing, suspending, or terminating benefits, including medical care, he should immediately notify the office of the district director who is administering the claim and set forth the facts pertinent to his complaint.
