33 C.F.R. § 2.34 — Waters subject to tidal influence; waters subject to the ebb and flow of the tide; mean high water.
Citation: us:cfr:33:2.34
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-2.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 2 (Jurisdiction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Waters subject to tidal influence and waters subject to the ebb and flow of the tide are waters below mean high water. These terms do not include waters above mean high water caused by flood flows, storms, high winds, seismic waves, or other non-lunar phenomena.
(b) Mean high water is the average of the height of the diurnal high water at a particular location measured over a lunar cycle of 19 years.
