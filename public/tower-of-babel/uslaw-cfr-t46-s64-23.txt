46 C.F.R. § 64.23 — Gasket and lining.
Citation: us:cfr:46:64.23
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-64.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 64 (Marine Portable Tanks And Cargo Handling Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each gasket and lining must be made of material that is—
(a) Chemically compatible with the product for which the tank is approved; and
(b) Resistant to deterioration from the product for which the tank is approved.
