28 C.F.R. § 32.35 — Disqualification.
Citation: us:cfr:28:32.35
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-32.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 32 (Public Safety Officers' Death, Disability, And Educational Assistance Benefit Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No claim shall be approved if the claimant is—
(a) In default on any student loan obtained under 20 U.S.C. 1091 (higher education assistance), unless, for good cause shown, the Director grants a waiver; or
(b) Subject to a denial of federal benefits under 21 U.S.C. 862 (drug traffickers and possessors).
