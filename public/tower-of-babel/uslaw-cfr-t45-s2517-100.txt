45 C.F.R. § 2517.100 — Who may apply for a direct grant from the Corporation?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2517.100
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2517.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2517 (Community-Based Service-Learning Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The following entities may apply for a direct grant from the Corporation: (1) A State Commission established under part 2550 of this chapter.
(2) A grantmaking entity as defined in § 2510.20 of this chapter.
(3) A qualified organization as defined in § 2515.20 of this chapter.
(b) The types of grants for which each entity is eligible are described in § 2517.200.
