36 U.S.C. § 22711 — Liability for acts of officials, representatives, and agents
Citation: us:usc:36:22711
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section22711
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 227 (Amvets (American Veterans)). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation is liable for the acts of its officials, representatives, and agents acting within the scope of their authority.
