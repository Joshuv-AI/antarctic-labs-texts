32 C.F.R. § 1602.8 — Classifying authority.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1602.8
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1602.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1602 (Definitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term classifying authority refers to any official or board who is authorized in § 1633.1 to classify a registrant.
