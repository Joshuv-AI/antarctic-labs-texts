27 C.F.R. § 5.194 — Adoption of predecessor's formulas.
Citation: us:cfr:27:5.194
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-5.194
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 5 (Labeling And Advertising Of Distilled Spirits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A successor to a person listed in § 5.191 may adopt a predecessor's approved formulas by filing an application with the appropriate TTB officer. The application must include a list of the formulas for adoption and must identify each formula by formula number, name of product, and date of approval. The application must clearly show that the predecessor has authorized the use of the previously approved formulas by the successor.
