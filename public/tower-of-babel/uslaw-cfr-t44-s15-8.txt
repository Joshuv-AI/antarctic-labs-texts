44 C.F.R. § 15.8 — Gambling.
Citation: us:cfr:44:15.8
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-15.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 15 (Conduct At The Mt. Weather Emergency Assistance Center And At The National Emergency Training Center). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We prohibit participating in games for money or other personal property, including the operation of gambling devices, the conduct of a lottery or pool, or the sale or purchase of numbers tickets at both facilities.
