39 C.F.R. § 601.103 — Applicability and coverage.
Citation: us:cfr:39:601.103
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-601.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 601 (Purchasing Of Property And Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations contained in this part apply to all Postal Service acquisition of property (except real property) and services.
