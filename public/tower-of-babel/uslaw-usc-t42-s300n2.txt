42 U.S.C. § 300n–2 — Requirement of submission of application
Citation: us:usc:42:300n–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section300n–2
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter XIII (Preventive Health Measures With Respect To Breast And Cervical Cancers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may not make a grant under section 300k of this title unless an application for the grant is submitted to the Secretary, the application contains the description of intended uses required in section 300n–1 of this title, and the application is in such form, is made in such manner, and contains such agreements, assurances, and information as the Secretary determines to be necessary to carry out this subchapter.
