22 U.S.C. § 283z–4 — Amendments to Articles of Agreement in resolution on Merger of Interregional and Ordinary Capital Resources
Citation: us:usc:22:283z–4
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section283z–4
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter XII (Inter-American Development Bank). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States Governor of the Inter-American Development Bank is hereby authorized to agree to and to accept the amendments to the Articles of Agreement in the proposed resolution entitled “Merger of Inter-regional and Ordinary Capital Resources”.
