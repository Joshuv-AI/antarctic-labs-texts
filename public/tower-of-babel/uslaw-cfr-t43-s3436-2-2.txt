43 C.F.R. § 3436.2-2 — -2 Federal coal deposits subject to disposal by exchange.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3436.2-2
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3436.2-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3430 (Noncompetitive Leases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The coal deposits offered in exchange by the Secretary shall be determined to be acceptable for further consideration for coal leasing pursuant to § 3420.1 of this title and shall be in the same State as the coal deposit offered in exchange by the proponent.
