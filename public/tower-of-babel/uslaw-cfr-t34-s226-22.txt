34 C.F.R. § 226.22 — May grantees use grant funds for administrative costs?
Citation: us:cfr:34:226.22
Creator: Office Of Elementary And Secondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-226.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 226 (State Charter School Facilities Incentive Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

State grantees may use up to five percent of their grant award for administrative expenses that include: indirect costs, evaluation, technical assistance, dissemination, personnel costs, and any other costs involved in administering the State's per-pupil facilities aid program.
