26 U.S.C. § 6873 — Unpaid claims
Citation: us:usc:26:6873
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section6873
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 70 (Jeopardy, Receiverships, Etc.); Subchapter B (Receiverships, Etc.). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) General rule
Any portion of a claim for taxes allowed in a receivership proceeding which is unpaid shall be paid by the taxpayer upon notice and demand from the Secretary after the termination of such proceeding.
(b) Cross references
(1)
For suspension of running of period of limitations on collection, see section 6503(b).
(2)
For extension of time for payment, see section 6161(c).
