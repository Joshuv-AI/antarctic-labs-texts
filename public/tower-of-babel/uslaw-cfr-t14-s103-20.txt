14 C.F.R. § 103.20 — Flight restrictions in the proximity of certain areas designated by notice to airmen.
Citation: us:cfr:14:103.20
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-103.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 103 (Ultralight Vehicles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person may operate an ultralight vehicle in areas designated in a Notice to Airmen under § 91.137, § 91.138, § 91.141, § 91.143 or § 91.145 of this chapter, unless authorized by:
(a) Air Traffic Control (ATC); or
(b) A Flight Standards Certificate of Waiver or Authorization issued for the demonstration or event.
