16 C.F.R. § 317.4 — Preemption.
Citation: us:cfr:16:317.4
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-317.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 317 (Prohibition Of Energy Market Manipulation Rule). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Federal Trade Commission does not intend, through the promulgation of this Rule, to preempt the laws of any state or local government, except to the extent that any such law conflicts with this Rule. A law is not in conflict with this Rule if it affords equal or greater protection from the prohibited practices set forth in § 317.3.
