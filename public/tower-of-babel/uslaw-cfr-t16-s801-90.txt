16 C.F.R. § 801.90 — Transactions or devices for avoidance.
Citation: us:cfr:16:801.90
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-801.90
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 801 (Coverage Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any transaction(s) or other device(s) entered into or employed for the purpose of avoiding the obligation to comply with the requirements of the act shall be disregarded, and the obligation to comply shall be determined by applying the act and these rules to the substance of the transaction.
