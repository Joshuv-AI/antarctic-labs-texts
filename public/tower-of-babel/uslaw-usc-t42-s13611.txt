42 U.S.C. § 13611 — Authority
Citation: us:usc:42:13611
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section13611
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 135 (Residency And Service Requirements In Federally Assisted Housing); Subchapter II (Authority To Provide Preferences For Elderly Residents And Units For Disabled Residents In Certain Section 8 Assisted Housing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, an owner of a covered section 8 housing project (as such term is defined in section 13619 of this title) designed primarily for occupancy by elderly families may, in selecting tenants for units in the project that become available for occupancy, give preference to elderly families who have applied for occupancy in the housing, subject to the requirements of this subchapter.
