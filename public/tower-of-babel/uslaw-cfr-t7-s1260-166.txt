7 C.F.R. § 1260.166 — Officers of the Committee.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1260.166
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Miscellaneous Commodities), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1260.166
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1260 (Beef Promotion And Research). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following persons shall serve as officers of the Committee:
(a) The chairperson of the Board shall be chairperson of the Committee.
(b) The chairperson of the Federation shall be vice-chairperson of the Committee.
(c) The treasurer of the Board shall be treasurer of the Committee.
(d) The Committee shall elect or appoint such other officers as it may deem necessary.
