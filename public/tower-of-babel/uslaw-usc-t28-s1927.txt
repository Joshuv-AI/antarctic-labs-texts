28 U.S.C. § 1927 — Counsel’s liability for excessive costs
Citation: us:usc:28:1927
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1927
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part V (Procedure); Chapter 123 (Fees And Costs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any attorney or other person admitted to conduct cases in any court of the United States or any Territory thereof who so multiplies the proceedings in any case unreasonably and vexatiously may be required by the court to satisfy personally the excess costs, expenses, and attorneys’ fees reasonably incurred because of such conduct.
