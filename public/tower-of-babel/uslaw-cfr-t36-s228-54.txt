36 C.F.R. § 228.54 — Single entry sales or permits.
Citation: us:cfr:36:228.54
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-228.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 228 (Minerals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purchaser or permittee is required to reclaim a single entry source in accordance with an approved operating plan which describes operating procedures and reclamation measures, unless the requirement is waived by the authorized officer.
