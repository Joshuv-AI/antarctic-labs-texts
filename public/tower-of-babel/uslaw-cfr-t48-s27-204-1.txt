48 C.F.R. § 27.204-1 — 27.204-1 Use of patented technology under the United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement.
Citation: us:cfr:48:27.204-1
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-27.204-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 27 (Patents, Data, And Copyrights). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When questions arise with regard to use of patented technology under the United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement, the contracting officer should consult with legal counsel. Note that Article 20.6(a) of the Agreement discusses public health and pharmaceuticals.
