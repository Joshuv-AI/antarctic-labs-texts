41 C.F.R. § 105-50.202-4 — -50.202-4 Technical assistance incident to Federal surplus personal property.

Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-50 (50—Provision Of Special Or Technical Services To State And Local Units Of Government). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Technical assistance will be provided in the screening and selection of surplus personal property under existing laws, provided such aid primarily strengthens the ability of the recipient in developing its own capacity to prepare proposals.
