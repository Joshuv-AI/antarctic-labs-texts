22 U.S.C. § 4610 — Dissolution or liquidation
Citation: us:usc:22:4610
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section4610
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 56 (United States Institute Of Peace). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Upon dissolution or final liquidation of the Institute or of any legal entity created pursuant to this chapter, all income and assets of the Institute or other legal entity shall revert to the United States Treasury.
