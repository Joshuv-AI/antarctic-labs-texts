16 U.S.C. § 757d — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:757d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section757d
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 9A (Preservation Of Fishery Resources). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out the purposes of sections 757a to 757f of this title not to exceed $4,500,000 for each of fiscal years 2007 through 2012.
