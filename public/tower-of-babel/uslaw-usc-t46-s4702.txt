46 U.S.C. § 4702 — Abandonment of barge prohibited
Citation: us:usc:46:4702
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section4702
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part B (Inspection And Regulation Of Vessels); Chapter 47 (Abandonment Of Vessels); Subchapter I (Barges). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An owner or operator of a barge may not abandon it on the navigable waters of the United States. A barge is deemed not to be abandoned if—
(1)
it is located at a Federally- or State-approved mooring area;
(2)
it is on private property with the permission of the owner of the property; or
(3)
the owner or operator notifies the Secretary that the barge is not abandoned and the location of the barge.
