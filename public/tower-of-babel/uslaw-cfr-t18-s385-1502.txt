18 C.F.R. § 385.1502 — Persons subject to civil penalties (Rule 1502).
Citation: us:cfr:18:385.1502
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-385.1502
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 385 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Any licensee or permittee under the Federal Power Act, or exemptee from any requirement of Part I of the Federal Power Act, may be subject to civil penalties; and
(b) Any person who must have a license under, or exemption from, the Federal Power Act, but does not, may be subject to civil penalties.
