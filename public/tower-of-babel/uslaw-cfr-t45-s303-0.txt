45 C.F.R. § 303.0 — Scope and applicability of this part.
Citation: us:cfr:45:303.0
Creator: Office Of Child Support Services, Administration Of Families And Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-303.0
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 303 (Standards For Program Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes:
(a) The minimum organizational and staffing requirements the State IV-D agency must meet in carrying out the IV-D program, and
(b) The standards for program operation which the IV-D agency must meet.
