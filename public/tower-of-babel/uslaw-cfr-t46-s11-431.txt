46 C.F.R. § 11.431 — Tonnage requirements for Great Lakes and inland endorsements for vessels of 1,600 GRT or more.
Citation: us:cfr:46:11.431
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-11.431
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 11 (Requirements For Officer Endorsements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) All required experience for Great Lakes and inland unlimited endorsements must be obtained on vessels of 100 GRT or more. At least one-half of the required experience must be obtained on vessels of 1,600 GRT or more.
(b) Tonnage limitations may be imposed on these endorsements in accordance with § 11.402(b) and (c) of this subpart.
