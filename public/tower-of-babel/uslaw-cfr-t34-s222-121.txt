34 C.F.R. § 222.121 — How does the affected Indian tribe or tribes request that payments to a local educational agency not be withheld?
Citation: us:cfr:34:222.121
Creator: Office Of Elementary And Secondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-222.121
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 222 (Impact Aid Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The affected Indian tribe or tribes may submit to the Assistant Secretary a formal request not to withhold payments from an LEA.
(b) The formal request must be in writing and signed by the tribal chairman or authorized designee.
