10 U.S.C. § 948c — Persons subject to military commissions
Citation: us:usc:10:948c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section948c
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 47A (Military Commissions); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any alien unprivileged enemy belligerent is subject to trial by military commission as set forth in this chapter.
