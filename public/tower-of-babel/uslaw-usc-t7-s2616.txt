7 U.S.C. § 2616 — Regulations
Citation: us:usc:7:2616
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2616
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 58 (Potato Research And Promotion). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to make such regulations with the force and effect of law, as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this chapter and the powers vested in him by this chapter.
