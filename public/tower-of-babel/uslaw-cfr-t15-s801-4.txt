15 C.F.R. § 801.4 — Recordkeeping requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:15:801.4
Creator: Bureau Of Economic Analysis, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-801.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 801 (Survey Of International Trade In Services Between U.S. And Foreign Persons And Surveys Of Direct Investment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In accordance with section 3104(b)(1) of title 22 of the United States Code, persons subject to the jurisdiction of the United States shall maintain any information essential for carrying out the surveys and studies provided for by the Act.
