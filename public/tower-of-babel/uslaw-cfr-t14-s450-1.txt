14 C.F.R. § 450.1 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:450.1

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-450.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 450 (Launch And Reentry License Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes requirements for obtaining and maintaining a license to launch, reenter, or both launch and reenter, a launch or reentry vehicle.
