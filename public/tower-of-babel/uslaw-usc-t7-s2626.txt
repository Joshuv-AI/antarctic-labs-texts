7 U.S.C. § 2626 — Separability
Citation: us:usc:7:2626
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2626
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 58 (Potato Research And Promotion). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this chapter or the application thereof to any person or circumstances is held invalid, the validity of the remainder of this chapter and of the application of such provision to other persons and circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
