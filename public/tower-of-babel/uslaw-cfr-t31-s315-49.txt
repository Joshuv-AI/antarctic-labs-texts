31 C.F.R. § 315.49 — Correction of errors.
Citation: us:cfr:31:315.49
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-315.49
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 315 (Regulations Governing U.S. Savings Bonds, Series A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, J, And K, And U.S. Savings Notes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A bond may be reissued to correct an error in registration upon appropriate request, supported by satisfactory proof of the error.
