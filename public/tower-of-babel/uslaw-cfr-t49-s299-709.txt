49 C.F.R. § 299.709 — Quality control program.
Citation: us:cfr:49:299.709
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-299.709
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 299 (Texas Central Railroad High-Speed Rail Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The railroad shall establish an inspection, testing, and maintenance quality control program enforced by the railroad or its contractor(s) to reasonably ensure that inspections, testing, and maintenance are performed in accordance with inspection, testing, and maintenance program established under this subpart.
