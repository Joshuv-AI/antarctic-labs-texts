46 C.F.R. § 95.16-15 — -15 Extinguishing agent: Quantity.
Citation: us:cfr:46:95.16-15
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-95.16-15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 95 (Fire Protection Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A separate supply need not be provided for each space protected, but the total available supply must be at least sufficient for the space requiring the greatest amount.
