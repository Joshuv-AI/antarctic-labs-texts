16 C.F.R. § 1512.8 — Requirements for drive chain.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1512.8
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1512.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1512 (Requirements For Bicycles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The drive chain shall operate over the sprockets without catching or binding. The tensile stength of the drive chain shall be no less than 8010 N (1,800 lbf) or 6230 N (1,400 lbf) for sidewalk bicycles.
