15 U.S.C. § 13c — Exemption of non-profit institutions from price discrimination provisions
Citation: us:usc:15:13c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section13c
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 1 (Monopolies And Combinations In Restraint Of Trade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in the Act approved June 19, 1936, known as the Robinson-Patman Antidiscrimination Act, shall apply to purchases of their supplies for their own use by schools, colleges, universities, public libraries, churches, hospitals, and charitable institutions not operated for profit.
