25 C.F.R. § 169.9 — What laws apply to rights-of-way approved under this part?
Citation: us:cfr:25:169.9
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-169.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 169 (Rights-Of-Way Over Indian Land). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to the regulations in this part, rights-of-way approved under this part:
(a) Are subject to all applicable Federal laws;
(b) Are subject to tribal law; except to the extent that those tribal laws are inconsistent with applicable Federal law; and
(c) Are generally not subject to State law or the law of a political subdivision thereof.
