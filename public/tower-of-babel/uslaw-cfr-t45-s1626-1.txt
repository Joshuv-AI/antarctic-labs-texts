45 C.F.R. § 1626.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1626.1
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1626.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1626 (Restrictions On Legal Assistance To Aliens). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part is designed to ensure that recipients provide legal assistance only to citizens of the United States and eligible aliens. It is also designed to assist recipients in determining the eligibility and immigration status of persons who seek legal assistance.
