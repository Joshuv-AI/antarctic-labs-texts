13 C.F.R. § 108.650 — Requirement to report portfolio valuations to SBA.
Citation: us:cfr:13:108.650
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-108.650
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 108 (New Markets Venture Capital (“Nmvc”) Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must determine the value of your Loans and Investments in accordance with § 108.503. You must report such valuations to SBA within 90 days of the end of the fiscal year in the case of annual valuations, and within 30 days following the close of other reporting periods. You must report material adverse changes in valuations at least quarterly, within thirty days following the close of the quarter.
