33 U.S.C. § 1418 — Regulations
Citation: us:usc:33:1418
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section1418
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 27 (Ocean Dumping); Subchapter I (Regulation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In carrying out the responsibilities and authority conferred by this subchapter, the Administrator, the Secretary, and the Secretary of the department in which the Coast Guard is operating are authorized to issue such regulations as they may deem appropriate.
