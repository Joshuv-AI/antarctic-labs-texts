16 U.S.C. § 194 — Lands held in private, municipal, or State ownership not affected
Citation: us:usc:16:194
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section194
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter XXI (Rocky Mountain National Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No lands located within the park boundaries held in private, municipal, or State ownership on January 26, 1915, shall be affected by or subject to the provisions of sections 191, 193, 195, and 195a of this title.
