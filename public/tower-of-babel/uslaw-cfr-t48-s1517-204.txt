48 C.F.R. § 1517.204 — 1517.204 Contracts.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1517.204
Creator: Chapter 15—Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1517.204
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1517 (Special Contracting Methods). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The SCM may approve a contract with a base contract period and option periods which total in excess of five (5) years, unless otherwise prohibited by statute.
