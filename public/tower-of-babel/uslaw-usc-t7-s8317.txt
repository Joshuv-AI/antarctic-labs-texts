7 U.S.C. § 8317 — Effect on regulations
Citation: us:usc:7:8317
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section8317
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 109 (Animal Health Protection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A regulation issued under a provision of law repealed by subsection (a) shall remain in effect until the Secretary issues a regulation under section 8303(b) or 8315 of this title that supersedes the earlier regulation.
