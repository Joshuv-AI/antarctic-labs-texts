9 C.F.R. § 311.20 — Sexual odor of swine.
Citation: us:cfr:9:311.20
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-311.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 311 (Disposal Of Diseased Or Otherwise Adulterated Carcasses And Parts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Carcasses of swine which give off a pronounced sexual odor shall be condemned.
(b) The meat of swine carcasses which give off a sexual odor less than pronounced may be passed for use in comminuted cooked meat food product or for rendering. Otherwise it shall be condemned.
