30 C.F.R. § 553.704 — How will BOEM publish the offshore facility limit of liability adjustment?
Citation: us:cfr:30:553.704
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-553.704
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 553 (Oil Spill Financial Responsibility For Offshore Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

BOEM will publish the inflation-adjusted limit of liability, and any statutory amendments to that limit of liability in the Federal Register, as amendments to § 553.702. Updates to the limit of liability under this section are effective on the 90th day after publication in the Federal Register of the amendments to § 553.702, unless otherwise specified by statute (in the event of a statutory amendment to the limit of liability), or in the Federal Register rule amending § 553.702.
