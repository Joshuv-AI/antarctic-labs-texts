42 U.S.C. § 3020e — Nutrition education
Citation: us:usc:42:3020e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section3020e
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 35 (Programs For Older Americans); Subchapter II (Administration On Aging). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Assistant Secretary, in consultation with the Secretary of Agriculture, shall conduct outreach and provide technical assistance to agencies and organizations that serve older individuals to assist such agencies and organizations to carry out integrated health promotion and disease prevention programs that—
(1)
are designed for older individuals; and
(2)
include—
(A)
nutrition education;
(B)
physical activity; and
(C)
other activities to modify behavior and to improve health literacy, including providing information on optimal nutrient intake, through nutrition education and nutrition assessment and counseling, in accordance with section 3030g–21(2)(J) of this title.
