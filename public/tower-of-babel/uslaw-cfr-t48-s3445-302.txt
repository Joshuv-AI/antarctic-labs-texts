48 C.F.R. § 3445.302 — 3445.302 Contracts with foreign governments or international organizations.
Citation: us:cfr:48:3445.302
Creator: Chapter 34—Department Of Education Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-3445.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 3445 (Government Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Requests by, or for the benefit of, foreign governments or international organizations to use ED production and research property must be approved by the HCA. The HCA must determine the amount of cost to be recovered or rental charged, if any, based on the facts and circumstances of each case.
