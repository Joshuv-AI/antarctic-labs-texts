10 U.S.C. § 9023a — Legislative Liaison of the Space Force

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization); Chapter 903 (Department Of The Air Force). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Establishment.—
There is a Legislative Liaison of the Space Force.
(b) Functions.—
The Legislative Liaison shall perform legislative affairs functions under the direction of the Chief of Space Operations.
