2 U.S.C. § 6112 — Compensation of Deputy President pro tempore of Senate
Citation: us:usc:2:6112
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section6112
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 61 (Senate Leadership); Subchapter II (Vice President And President Pro Tempore). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Effective January 5, 1977, the compensation of a Deputy President pro tempore of the Senate shall be at a rate equal to the rate of annual compensation of the President pro tempore and the Majority and Minority Leaders of the Senate.
