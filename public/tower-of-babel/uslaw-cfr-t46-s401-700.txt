46 C.F.R. § 401.700 — Operating requirements for United States Registered Pilots.
Citation: us:cfr:46:401.700
Creator: Coast Guard (Great Lakes Pilotage), Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-401.700
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 401 (Great Lakes Pilotage Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each United States Registered Pilot must—
(a) Provide pilotage service when dispatched by their pilotage pool; and
(b) Comply with the dispatching orders of the Director under § 401.720 (b).
