46 C.F.R. § 173.051 — Public nautical school ships.
Citation: us:cfr:46:173.051
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-173.051
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 173 (Special Rules Pertaining To Vessel Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each public nautical school ship must comply with—
(a) Section 171.070(a) of this subchapter as a passenger vessel carrying 400 or less passengers;
(b) Section 171.070(e) of this subchapter;
(c) Section 171.072 of this subchapter; and
(d) Section 171.073 of this subchapter.
