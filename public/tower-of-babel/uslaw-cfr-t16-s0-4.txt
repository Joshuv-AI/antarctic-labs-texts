16 C.F.R. § 0.4 — Laws administered.

Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 0 (Organization). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission exercises enforcement and administrative authority under the Federal Trade Commission Act (15 U.S.C. 41-58), Clayton Act (15 U.S.C. 12-27), and more than 70 other Federal statutes, which are listed at https://www.ftc.gov/enforcement/statutes.
