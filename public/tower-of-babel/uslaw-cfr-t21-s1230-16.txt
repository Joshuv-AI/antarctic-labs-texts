21 C.F.R. § 1230.16 — Exemption from labeling directions for treatment.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1230.16
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1230.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1230 (Regulations Under The Federal Caustic Poison Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Manufacturers and wholesalers only, at the time of shipment or delivery for shipment, are exempted from placing directions for treatment on the label or sticker of any container for other than household use, but in any event the information required by section 2(b) (1), (2), and (3) of the act (44 Stat. 1407; 15 U.S.C. 402) and the regulations in this part shall be given.
