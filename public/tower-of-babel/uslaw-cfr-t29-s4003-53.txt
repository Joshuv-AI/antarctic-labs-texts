29 C.F.R. § 4003.53 — Where to file.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4003 (Rules For Administrative Review Of Agency Decisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An appeal or a request for an extension of time to appeal must be submitted to the Appeals Board. See § 4000.4 of this chapter for additional information on where to file.
