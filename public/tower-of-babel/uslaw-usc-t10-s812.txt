10 U.S.C. § 812 — Art. 12. Prohibition of confinement of members of the armed forces with enemy prisoners and certain others
Citation: us:usc:10:812
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section812
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 47 (Uniform Code Of Military Justice); Subchapter II (Apprehension And Restraint). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No member of the armed forces may be placed in confinement in immediate association with—
(1)
enemy prisoners; or
(2)
other individuals—
(A)
who are detained under the law of war and are foreign nationals; and
(B)
who are not members of the armed forces.
