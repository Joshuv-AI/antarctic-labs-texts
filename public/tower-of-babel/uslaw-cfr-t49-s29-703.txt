49 C.F.R. § 29.703 — What happens if the Department of the Interior determines that it does not provide the transportation services the Tribe intends to retrocede?
Citation: us:cfr:49:29.703
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-29.703
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 29 (Tribal Transportation Self-Governance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Department of the Interior determines that it does not provide the transportation services the Tribe intends to retrocede, the Tribe may withdraw its notice to retrocede or return all remaining funds, less closeout costs, associated with the retroceded PSFA, and the Department will distribute those funds in accordance with applicable law.
