46 C.F.R. § 64.47 — Type of relief devices.
Citation: us:cfr:46:64.47
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-64.47
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 64 (Marine Portable Tanks And Cargo Handling Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An MPT with an internal capacity of more than 550 U.S. gallons must have one or more spring loaded relief valves. In addition, a rupture disc may be attached.
(b) An MPT with an internal capacity of 550 U.S. gallons or less must have a rupture disc or a spring loaded relief valve.
