48 C.F.R. § 225.7012-2 — 225.7012-2 Waiver.
Citation: us:cfr:48:225.7012-2
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-225.7012-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 225 (Foreign Acquisition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary of Defense may waive this restriction, on a case-by-case basis, after certifying to the Armed Services and Appropriations Committees of Congress that—
(a) Adequate U.S. supplies are not available to meet requirements on a timely basis; and
(b) The acquisition must be made in order to acquire capability for national security purposes.
