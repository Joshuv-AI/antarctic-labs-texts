Motivic p p -adic periods of 1 1 -motives
Felix Sefzig

Abstract
We give an explicit construction of the p p -adic de Rham comparison isomorphism for 1 1 -motives. In particular, we prove that our construction recovers the classical de Rham comparison isomorphism and is functorial with respect to morphisms of rigid 1 1 -motives. In the second part, we construct a new ring of motivic p p -adic periods using the formalism of rigid analytic motives. Furthermore, we explain the relation between these motivic periods and the periods of the classical p p -adic de Rham comparison isomorphism. Finally, we apply our construction to explicitly compute the period pairing for several examples of 1 1 -motives and curves.
