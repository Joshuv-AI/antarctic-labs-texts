On the Asymptotics of Orthogonal Polynomials on Multiple Intervals with Non-Analytic Weights
Thomas TROGDON

Abstract
We consider the asymptotics of orthogonal polynomials for measures that are differentiable, but not necessarily analytic, multiplicative perturbations of Jacobi-like measures supported on disjoint intervals. We analyze the Fokas–Its–Kitaev Riemann–Hilbert problem using the Deift–Zhou method of nonlinear steepest descent and its ∂ ¯ \overline{\partial} extension due to Miller and McLaughlin. Our results extend that of Yattselev in the case of Chebyshev-like measures with error bounds that give similar rates while allowing less regular perturbations. For the general Jacobi-like case, we present, what appears to be the first result for asymptotics when the perturbation of the measure is only assumed to be differentiable with bounded second derivative.

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{\bigl((x-a_{j})^{2}+y^{2}\bigr)^{1/4}}.

And we find that

\displaystyle\left|\Re\frac{\sqrt{y}}{\sqrt{x+\mathrm{i}y-b_{j}}\sqrt{x+\mathrm{i}y-a_{j}}}\right|

\displaystyle\leq\min\left\{\frac{1}{\bigl((x-a_{j})^{2}+y^{2}\bigr)^{1/4}},\frac{1}{\bigl((x-b_{j})^{2}+y^{2}\bigr)^{1/4}}\right\}

\displaystyle c^{\prime}\Im\frac{1}{\sqrt{x+\mathrm{i}y-b_{j}}\sqrt{x+\mathrm{i}y-a_{j}}}+C^{\prime\prime}\sqrt{y}\geq\Im\mathfrak{g}^{\prime}(x+\mathrm{i}y){\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}y}

\displaystyle\phantom{c^{\prime}\Im\frac{1}{\sqrt{x+\mathrm{i}y-b_{j}}\sqrt{x+\mathrm{i}y-a_{j}}}+C^{\prime\prime}\sqrt{y}}{}\geq c\Im\frac{1}{\sqrt{x+\mathrm{i}y-b_{j}}\sqrt{x+\mathrm{i}y-a_{j}}}-C^{\prime\prime}\sqrt{y},

\displaystyle c^{\prime}\log|{\phi}(M(z;a_{j},b_{j}))|+\frac{2C^{\prime\prime}}{3}y^{3/2}\geq\int_{0}^{y}\Im\mathfrak{g}^{\prime}(x+\mathrm{i}y)\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}y

\displaystyle\phantom{c^{\prime}\log|{\phi}(M(z;a_{j},b_{j}))|+\frac{2C^{\prime\prime}}{3}y^{3/2}}{}\geq c\log|{\phi}(M(z;a_{j},b_{j}))|-\frac{2C^{\prime\prime}}{3}y^{3/2}.

We then claim for

and

, that there exists

such that

\displaystyle I(x,y)=\Im\frac{1}{\sqrt{x+\mathrm{i}y-b_{j}}\sqrt{x+\mathrm{i}y-a_{j}}}\geq{c^{\prime\prime}}.

Indeed, if this were not the case, then there would exist a convergent sequence

such that

. And we must have that

or

And therefore

\displaystyle\left(c^{\prime}+\sqrt{y}\frac{2C^{\prime\prime}}{3{c^{\prime\prime}}}\right)\log|{\phi}(M(z;a_{j},b_{j}))|

\displaystyle\geq\int_{0}^{y}\Im\mathfrak{g}^{\prime}(x+\mathrm{i}y){\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}y}

from which the claim follows. Similar considerations work for

. Continuity of the ratio

near
a_{j}
,
b_{j}
allows the desired bound to hold in neighborhoods of

,
