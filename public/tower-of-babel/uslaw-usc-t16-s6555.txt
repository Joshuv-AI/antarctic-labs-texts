16 U.S.C. § 6555 — Relation to other laws
Citation: us:usc:16:6555
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 84 (Healthy Forest Restoration); Subchapter IV (Insect Infestations And Related Diseases). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The authority provided to each Secretary under this subchapter is supplemental to, and not in lieu of, any authority provided to the Secretaries under any other law.
