43 C.F.R. § 3838.1 — What are SRHA lands?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3838.1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3838.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3838 (Special Procedures For Locating And Recording Mining Claims And Tunnel Sites On Stockraising Homestead Act (Srha) Lands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

SRHA lands are lands that were—
(a) Patented under the Stockraising Homestead Act of 1916, as amended (30 U.S.C. 54 and 43 U.S.C. 299); or
(b) Originally entered under the Homestead Act of 1862, as amended, and patented under the SRHA after December 29, 1916.
