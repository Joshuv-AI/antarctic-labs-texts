50 U.S.C. § 3999 — Regulations
Citation: us:usc:50:3999
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section3999
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 50 (Servicemembers Civil Relief); Subchapter V (Taxes And Public Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior may issue regulations necessary to carry out this subchapter (other than sections 3991, 4000, and 4001 of this title).
