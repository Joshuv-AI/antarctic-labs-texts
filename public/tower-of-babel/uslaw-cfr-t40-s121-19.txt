40 C.F.R. § 121.19 — Severability.
Citation: us:cfr:40:121.19
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-121.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 121 (State Certification Of Activities Requiring A Federal License Or Permit). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this part are separate and severable from one another. If any provision is stayed or determined to be invalid, the remaining provisions shall continue in effect.
