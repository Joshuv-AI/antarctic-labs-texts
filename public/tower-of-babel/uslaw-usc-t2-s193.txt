2 U.S.C. § 193 — Privilege of witnesses
Citation: us:usc:2:193
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section193
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 6 (Congressional And Committee Procedure; Investigations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No witness is privileged to refuse to testify to any fact, or to produce any paper, respecting which he shall be examined by either House of Congress, or by any joint committee established by a joint or concurrent resolution of the two Houses of Congress, or by any committee of either House, upon the ground that his testimony to such fact or his production of such paper may tend to disgrace him or otherwise render him infamous.
