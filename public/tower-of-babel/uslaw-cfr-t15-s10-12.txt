15 C.F.R. § 10.12 — Editorial changes.
Citation: us:cfr:15:10.12
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-10.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 10 (Procedures For The Development Of Voluntary Product Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department may, without prior notice, make such editorial or other minor changes as it deems necessary to reduce ambiguity or to improve clarity in any proposed, recommended, or published standard, or revision or amendment thereof.
