33 C.F.R. § 150.825 — Reporting a diving-related casualty.
Citation: us:cfr:33:150.825
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-150.825
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 150 (Deepwater Ports: Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Deaths and injuries related to diving within the safety zone of a deepwater port must be reported according to 46 CFR 197.484 and 197.486, rather than to §§ 150.815 and 150.820 of this part.
