10 U.S.C. § 7551 — Definitions

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Procurement); Chapter 764 (Armaments Industrial Base). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter:
(1)
The term “ARMS Initiative” means the Armament Retooling and Manufacturing Support Initiative authorized by this chapter.
(2)
The term “eligible facility” means a Government-owned, contractor-operated ammunition manufacturing facility, or a Government-owned, contractor-operated depot for the storage, maintenance, renovation, or demilitarization of ammunition, of the Department of the Army that is in an active, inactive, layaway, or caretaker status.
(3)
The term “property manager” includes any person or entity managing an eligible facility made available under the ARMS Initiative through a property management contract.
(4)
The term “property management contract” includes facility use contracts, site management contracts, leases, and other agreements entered into under the authority of this chapter.
(5)
The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of the Army.
