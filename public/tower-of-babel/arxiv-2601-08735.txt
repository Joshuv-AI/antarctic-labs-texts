By matching the non-extensive β \beta -function to one-loop QCD results, a fundamental scaling of the thermofractal index q q is derived as a function of the number of flavours N f N_{f} . A unified master equation for the Polyakov loop ⟨ L ⟩ \langle L\rangle is presented, governed by the thermofractal index q q and a single variance parameter σ 2 ​ ( T ) \sigma^{2}(T) that scales as T 1 / ( q − 1 ) T^{1/(q-1)} . The observed phase dynamics are shown to be asymptotic limits of this unified density: a “soft” algebraic growth ⟨ L ⟩ ∝ T 11 \langle L\rangle\propto T^{11} in the 1D string-like confined regime for N f = 0 N_{f}=0 , and a rapid 1 − ⟨ L ⟩ ∝ T − 21 1-\langle L\rangle\propto T^{-21} suppression in the 3D deconfined volume for N f = 3 N_{f}=3 .
