2.4.

Recall that two algebras

.

(

]

3.2.

We firstly recall the definition of (twisted) fractionally Calabi-Yau algebras. Then we give a construction of

-Calabi-Yau algebra for some integer

. We show with an example that not all such algebras can be constructed in this way.

Let

be an algebra automorphism. It induces an autoequivalence

\phi^{\ast}=-\otimes^{\mathbb{L}}_{A}{{}_{\phi}A}:\mathcal{D}^{b}(A)\rightarrow\mathcal{D}^{b}(A)

where

is the

-module

.

Definition 3.5
.

[
HI11a
, Definition 0.3]

We say that

is twisted fractionally Calabi-Yau (or twisted
\frac{m}{\ell}
-CY) if there exists an isomorphism

of functors for some integers
\ell\neq 0
and

and
\phi
an algebra automorphism of

.
When
\phi=id
, we say that

is fractionally Calabi-Yau (or
\frac{m}{\ell}
-CY).

Theorem 3.6
.

Let
A
be a finite dimensional algebra such that
{\rm{gldim}}A<\infty
and

\frac{d}{a+1}
-CY.
Suppose
