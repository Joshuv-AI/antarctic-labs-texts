10 U.S.C. § 8112 — Marine Corps: grades above chief warrant officer, W–5
Citation: us:usc:10:8112
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8112
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 812 (Grade And Rank Of Officers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The commissioned grades in the Marine Corps above the grade of chief warrant officer, W–5, are:
(1)
General.
(2)
Lieutenant general.
(3)
Major general.
(4)
Brigadier general.
(5)
Colonel.
(6)
Lieutenant colonel.
(7)
Major.
(8)
Captain.
(9)
First lieutenant.
(10)
Second lieutenant.
