34 C.F.R. § 81.17 — Privileges.
Citation: us:cfr:34:81.17
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-81.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 81 (General Education Provisions Act—Enforcement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The privilege of a person or governmental organization not to produce documents or provide information in a proceeding under this part is governed by the principles of common law as interpreted by the courts of the United States.
