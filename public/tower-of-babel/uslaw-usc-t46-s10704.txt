46 U.S.C. § 10704 — Duties of consular officers
Citation: us:usc:46:10704
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section10704
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part G (Merchant Seamen Protection And Relief); Chapter 107 (Effects Of Deceased Seamen). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When a seaman dies outside the United States leaving money or property not on board a vessel, the consular officer nearest the place at which the money and property is located shall claim and take charge of it.
