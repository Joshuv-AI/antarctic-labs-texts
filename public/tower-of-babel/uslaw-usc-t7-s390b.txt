7 U.S.C. § 390b — Competitive grant program
Citation: us:usc:7:390b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section390b
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 14 (Agricultural Experiment Stations); Subchapter III (Research Facilities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall establish a program to make competitive grants to assist in the construction, alteration, acquisition, modernization, renovation, or remodeling of agricultural research facilities.
