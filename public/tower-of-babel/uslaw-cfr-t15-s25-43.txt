15 C.F.R. § 25.43 — Collection of civil penalties and assessments.
Citation: us:cfr:15:25.43
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-25.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 25 (Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Sections 3806 and 3808(b) of title 31, United States Code, authorize actions for collection of civil penalties and assessments imposed under this part and specify the procedures for such actions.
