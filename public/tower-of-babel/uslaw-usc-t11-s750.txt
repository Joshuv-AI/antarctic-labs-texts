11 U.S.C. § 750 — Distribution of securities
Citation: us:usc:11:750
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 7 (Liquidation); Subchapter III (Stockbroker Liquidation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The trustee may not distribute a security except under section 751 of this title.
