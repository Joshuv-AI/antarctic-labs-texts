23 C.F.R. § 1235.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:23:1235.1
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration And Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-1235.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 1235 (Uniform System For Parking For Persons With Disabilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to provide guidelines to States for the establishment of a uniform system for handicapped parking for persons with disabilities to enhance access and the safety of persons with disabilities which limit or impair the ability to walk.
