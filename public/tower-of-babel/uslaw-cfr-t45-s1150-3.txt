45 C.F.R. § 1150.3 — What other regulations also apply to the Endowment's debt collection efforts?
Citation: us:cfr:45:1150.3
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1150.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1150 (Collection Of Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All provisions of the Federal Claims Collection Standards also apply to the regulations in this part. This part supplements the Federal Claims Collection Standards by prescribing procedures and directives necessary and appropriate for operations of the Endowment.
