5 C.F.R. § 1215.3 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1215.3
Creator: Merit Systems Protection Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1215.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1215 (Debt Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) These regulations are to be followed when:
(1) The MSPB is owed a debt by an individual currently employed by another Federal agency;
(2) The MSPB is owed a debt by an individual who is a current employee of the MSPB; or
(3) The MSPB employs an individual who owes a debt to another Federal agency.
