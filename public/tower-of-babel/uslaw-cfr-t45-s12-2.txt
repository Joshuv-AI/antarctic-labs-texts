45 C.F.R. § 12.2 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:45:12.2
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-12.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 12 (Disposal And Utilization Of Surplus Real Property For Public Health Purposes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part is applicable to surplus real property located within any State which is appropriate for assignment to, or which has been assigned to, the Department for transfer for public health purposes, as provided for in section 203(k) of the Act.
