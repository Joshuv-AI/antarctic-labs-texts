10 U.S.C. § 9222 — Rank: commissioned officers serving under temporary appointments
Citation: us:usc:10:9222
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9222
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 925 (Rank And Command). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may, in accordance with the needs of the Air Force or the Space Force, adjust dates of rank of commissioned officers of the Air Force or the Space Force serving in temporary grades.
