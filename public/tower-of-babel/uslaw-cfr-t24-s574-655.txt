24 C.F.R. § 574.655 — Wage rates.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 574 (Housing Opportunities For Persons With Aids). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of the Davis-Bacon Act (40 U.S.C. 276a-276a-5) do not apply to this program, except where funds received under this part are combined with funds from other Federal programs that are subject to the Act.
