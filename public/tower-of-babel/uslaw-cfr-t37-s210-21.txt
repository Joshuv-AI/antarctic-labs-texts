37 C.F.R. § 210.21 — General.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 210 (Compulsory License For Making And Distributing Physical And Digital Phonorecords Of Nondramatic Musical Works). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes rules for the compulsory blanket license to make and distribute digital phonorecord deliveries of nondramatic musical works pursuant to 17 U.S.C. 115(d), including rules for digital music providers, significant nonblanket licensees, the mechanical licensing collective, and the digital licensee coordinator.
