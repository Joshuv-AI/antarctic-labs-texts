42 C.F.R. § 493.1355 — Condition: Laboratories performing PPM procedures; laboratory director.
Citation: us:cfr:42:493.1355
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-493.1355
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 493 (Laboratory Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The laboratory must have a director who meets the qualification requirements of § 493.1357 and provides overall management and direction in accordance with § 493.1359.
