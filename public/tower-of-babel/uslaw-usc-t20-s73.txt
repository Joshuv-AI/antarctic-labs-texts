20 U.S.C. § 73 — Acceptance of gift from A. W. Mellon
Citation: us:usc:20:73
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section73
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 3 (Smithsonian Institution, National Museums And Art Galleries); Subchapter II (National Gallery Of Art). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Upon completion of the National Gallery of Art, the board shall accept for the Smithsonian Institution as a gift from the donor a collection of works of art which shall be housed and exhibited in the National Gallery of Art.
