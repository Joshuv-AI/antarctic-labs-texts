49 C.F.R. § 1113.19 — Pleadings: part of the record.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1113.19
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1113.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1113 (Oral Hearing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Matters of fact that are verified and filed prior to oral hearing and that are not specifically denied constitute evidence and are part of the record. A witness, who would present such evidence, must be made available for cross-examination if a request is reasonably made. This rule does not apply to protests against tariffs or schedules.
