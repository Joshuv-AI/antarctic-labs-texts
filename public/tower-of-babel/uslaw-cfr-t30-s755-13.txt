30 C.F.R. § 755.13 — Authority reserved by the Secretary.
Citation: us:cfr:30:755.13
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-755.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 755 (Tribal-Federal Intergovernmental Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary shall not delegate to any Indian tribe, nor shall any Tribal-Federal Intergovernmental Agreement be construed to delegate to any tribe, the nondelegable authority exercised by or reserved to the Secretary on Indian lands.
