9 C.F.R. § 156.4 — Application for service.
Citation: us:cfr:9:156.4
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-156.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 156 (Voluntary Inspection And Certification Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person who is eligible to receive service under this part may apply therefor to the Administrator, upon an application form which will be furnished by the Administrator upon request. The application form shall require the applicant to state, among other things, the forms of certificates desired.
