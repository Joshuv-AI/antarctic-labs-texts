25 C.F.R. § 115.428 — Will you automatically receive all of your trust funds when you reach the age of 18?
Citation: us:cfr:25:115.428
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-115.428
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 115 (Trust Funds For Tribes And Individual Indians). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No, we will not automatically send your trust funds to you when you reach the age of 18.
