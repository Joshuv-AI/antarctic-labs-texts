43 C.F.R. § 3137.134 — What happens to committed leases if the unit terminates?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3137.134
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3137.134
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3130 (Oil And Gas Leasing: National Petroleum Reserve, Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If the unit terminates, all committed NPR-A leases return to individual lease status and are subject to their original provisions.
(b) An NPR-A lease that has completed its primary term on or before the date the unit terminates will expire unless it is granted an extension or renewal under § 3135.1-5 or § 3135.1-6.
