46 C.F.R. § 112.35-1 — -1 General.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 112 (Emergency Lighting And Power Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart contains requirements applicable to emergency power installations having a manually controlled storage battery, diesel engine, or gas turbine driven generator as the sole emergency power source.
