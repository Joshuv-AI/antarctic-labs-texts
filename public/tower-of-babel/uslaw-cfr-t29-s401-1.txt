29 C.F.R. § 401.1 — Commerce.
Citation: us:cfr:29:401.1
Creator: Office Of Labor-Management Standards, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-401.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 401 (Meaning Of Terms Used In This Subchapter). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Commerce means trade, traffic, commerce, transportation, transmission, or communication among the several States or between any State and any place outside thereof.
