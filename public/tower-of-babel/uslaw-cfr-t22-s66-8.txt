22 C.F.R. § 66.8 — Reports.
Citation: us:cfr:22:66.8
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-66.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 66 (Availability Of The Records Of The National Endowment For Democracy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

On or before March 1 of each calendar year, NED shall submit a reporting covering the preceding calendar year to the Speaker of the House of Representatives and the President of the Senate for referral to the appropriate committees of the Congress. The report shall include those items specified at 5 U.S.C. 552(d).
