49 C.F.R. § 229.89 — Jumpers; cable connections.
Citation: us:cfr:49:229.89
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-229.89
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 229 (Railroad Locomotive Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Jumpers and cable connections between locomotives shall be so located and guarded to provide sufficient vertical clearance. They may not hang with one end free.
(b) Cable and jumper connections between locomotive may not have any of the following conditions:
(1) Broken or badly chafed insulation.
(2) Broken plugs, receptacles or terminals.
(3) Broken or protruding strands of wire.
