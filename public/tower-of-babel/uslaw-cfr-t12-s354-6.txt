12 C.F.R. § 354.6 — Reservation of authority.
Citation: us:cfr:12:354.6
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-354.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 354 (Industrial Banks). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this part limits the authority of the FDIC under any other provision of law or regulation to take supervisory or enforcement actions, including actions to address unsafe or unsound practices or conditions, or violations of law.
