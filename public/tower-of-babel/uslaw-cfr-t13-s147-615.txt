13 C.F.R. § 147.615 — Conviction.
Citation: us:cfr:13:147.615
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-147.615
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 147 (Governmentwide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Conviction means a finding of guilt (including a plea of nolo contendere) or imposition of sentence, or both, by any judicial body charged with the responsibility to determine violations of the Federal or State criminal drug statutes.
