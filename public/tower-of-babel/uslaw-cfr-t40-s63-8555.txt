40 C.F.R. § 63.8555 — What emission limitations and work practice standards must I meet?
Citation: us:cfr:40:63.8555
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-63.8555
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 63 (National Emission Standards For Hazardous Air Pollutants For Source Categories). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You must meet each emission limit in Table 1 to this subpart that applies to you.
(b) You must meet each operating limit in Table 2 to this subpart that applies to you.
(c) You must meet each work practice standard in Table 3 to this subpart that applies to you.
