22 C.F.R. § 9a.8 — a.8 Physical protection.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 9a (PART 9a—SECURITY INFORMATION REGULATIONS APPLICABLE TO CERTAIN INTERNATIONAL ENERGY PROGRAMS; RELATED MATERIAL). Except as provided in § 9a.7, the physical protection of information or material classified under this regulation shall be governed by the appropriate provisions of 22 CFR 9.45 through 9.49.
