8 C.F.R. § 241.23 — Cost of maintenance not assessed.
Citation: us:cfr:8:241.23
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-241.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 241 (Apprehension And Detention Of Aliens Ordered Removed). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A claim pursuant to section 237(a)(1) of the Act shall be established to the satisfaction of the district director in charge of the port of arrival, from whose adverse decision no appeal shall lie. The district director shall afford the line a reasonable time within which to submit affidavits and briefs to support its claim.
