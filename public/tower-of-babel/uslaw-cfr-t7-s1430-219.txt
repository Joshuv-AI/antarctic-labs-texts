7 C.F.R. § 1430.219 — Appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1430.219
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1430.219
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1430 (Dairy Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any producer who is dissatisfied with a determination made pursuant to this subpart may request reconsideration or appeal of such determination under part 11 or 780 of this title.
