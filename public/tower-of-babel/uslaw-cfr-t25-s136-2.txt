25 C.F.R. § 136.2 — Construction costs.
Citation: us:cfr:25:136.2
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-136.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 136 (Fort Hall Indian Irrigation Project, Idaho). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The program of rehabilitation has now been completed at a cost of $419,186.52. This amount, chargeable on an equal per acre basis against 60,000 acres, amounts to a rate of $6.986 per acre, which rate is hereby determined to be the per acre cost to be repaid to the United States under the 1936 contracts.
