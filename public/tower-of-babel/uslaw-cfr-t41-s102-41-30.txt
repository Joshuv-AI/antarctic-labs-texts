41 C.F.R. § 102-41.30 — -41.30 Transfer reimbursement.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-41.30
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-41.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-41 (41—Disposition Of Seized, Forfeited, Voluntarily Abandoned, And Unclaimed Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All transfers of voluntarily abandoned personal property are without reimbursement. You may charge the recipient agency costs you incurred in storing, packing, loading, preparing for shipment, and transporting the property.
