31 C.F.R. § 1023.600 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:31:1023.600
Creator: Financial Crimes Enforcement Network, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-1023.600
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 1023 (Rules For Brokers Or Dealers In Securities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Brokers or dealers in securities are subject to the special standards of diligence; prohibitions; and special measures requirements set forth and cross referenced in this subpart. Brokers or dealers in securities should also refer to subpart F of part 1010 of this chapter for special standards of diligence; prohibitions; and special measures contained in that subpart which apply to brokers or dealers in securities.
