2 U.S.C. § 638 — House committee action on all appropriation bills to be completed by June 10
Citation: us:usc:2:638
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section638
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 17A (Congressional Budget And Fiscal Operations); Subchapter I (Congressional Budget Process). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On or before June 10 of each year, the Committee on Appropriations of the House of Representatives shall report annual appropriation bills providing new budget authority under the jurisdiction of all of its subcommittees for the fiscal year which begins on October 1 of that year.
