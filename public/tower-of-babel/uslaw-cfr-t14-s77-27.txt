14 C.F.R. § 77.27 — Initiation of studies.
Citation: us:cfr:14:77.27
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-77.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 77 (Safe, Efficient Use, And Preservation Of The Navigable Airspace). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The FAA will conduct an aeronautical study when:
(a) Requested by the sponsor of any proposed construction or alteration for which a notice is submitted; or
(b) The FAA determines a study is necessary.
