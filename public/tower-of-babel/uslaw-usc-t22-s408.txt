22 U.S.C. § 408 — Use of land and naval forces to prevent exportation
Citation: us:usc:22:408
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section408
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 9 (Foreign Wars, War Materials, And Neutrality); Subchapter I (War Materials). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may employ such part of the land or naval forces of the United States as he may deem necessary to carry out the purposes of sections 401 to 408 of this title.
