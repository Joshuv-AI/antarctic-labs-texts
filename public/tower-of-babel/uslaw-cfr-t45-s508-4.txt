45 C.F.R. § 508.4 — Failure to file request for hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:45:508.4
Creator: Foreign Claims Settlement Commission Of The United States, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-508.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 508 (Hearings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The failure to file a request for a hearing within the period specified in § 509.2 of this chapter will be deemed to constitute a waiver of right to a hearing and the decision of the Commission will constitute a full and final disposition of the case.
