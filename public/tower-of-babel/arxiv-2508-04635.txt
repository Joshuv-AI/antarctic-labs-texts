Variational free complement method with Gaussian complements
Cong Wang

Abstract
The complement functions in the free complement (FC) method are constructed by decontracting the Gaussian expansions of the Slater functions formed by the initial wavefunction and the g g functions. The helium ground state is used to demonstrate the accuracy.
