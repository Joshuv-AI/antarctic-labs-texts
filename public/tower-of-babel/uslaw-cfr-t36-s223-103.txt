36 C.F.R. § 223.103 — Award of small business set-aside sales.
Citation: us:cfr:36:223.103
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-223.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 223 (Sale And Disposal Of National Forest System Timber). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If timber is advertised as set aside for competitive bidding by small business concerns, award will be made to the highest bidder who qualifies as a small business concern and who has not been determined by the Small Business Administration to be ineligible for preferential award of set-aside sales. If there are no qualified small business bidders any readvertisement shall be without restriction on the size of bidders.
