43 C.F.R. § 30.180 — May I give up an inherited interest in trust or restricted property or trust personalty?
Citation: us:cfr:43:30.180
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-30.180
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 30 (Indian Probate Hearings Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may renounce an inherited or devised interest in trust or restricted property, including a life estate, or in trust personalty if:
(a) You are 18 years or older and not under a legal disability; or
(b) You are an entity.
