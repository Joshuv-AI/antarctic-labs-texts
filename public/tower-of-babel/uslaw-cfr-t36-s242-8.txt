36 C.F.R. § 242.8 — Penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:36:242.8
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-242.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 242 (Subsistence Management Regulations For Public Lands In Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you are convicted of violating any provision of 43 CFR part 51 or this part, you may be punished by a fine or by imprisonment in accordance with the penalty provisions applicable to the public land where the violation occurred.
