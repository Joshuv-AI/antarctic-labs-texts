25 C.F.R. § 2.802 — What must I do if I want to challenge the accuracy of activity within a Statement of Performance?
Citation: us:cfr:25:2.802
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-2.802
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 2 (Appeals From Administrative Decisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you want to challenge the accuracy of activity within a Statement of Performance, you must submit an Objection to the Statement of Performance within 60 calendar days of the statement date.
