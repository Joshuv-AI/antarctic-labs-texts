50 C.F.R. § 402.48 — Conference on proposed species or proposed critical habitat.
Citation: us:cfr:50:402.48
Creator: Joint Regulations (United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior And National Marine Fisheries Service, National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce); Endangered Species Committee Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-402.48
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 402 (Interagency Cooperation—Endangered Species Act Of 1973, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

EPA may employ the procedures described in § 402.10 to confer on any species proposed for listing or any habitat proposed for designation as critical habitat. For the purposes of § 402.10(d), the procedures in § 402.46 are a permissible form of formal consultation.
