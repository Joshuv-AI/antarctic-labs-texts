16 U.S.C. § 3773 — Partners for Fish and Wildlife Program
Citation: us:usc:16:3773
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section3773
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 57B (Partners For Fish And Wildlife). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall carry out the Partners for Fish and Wildlife Program within the United States Fish and Wildlife Service to provide—
(1)
technical and financial assistance to private landowners for the conduct of voluntary projects to benefit Federal trust species by promoting habitat improvement, habitat restoration, habitat enhancement, and habitat establishment; and
(2)
technical assistance to other public and private entities regarding fish and wildlife habitat restoration on private land.
