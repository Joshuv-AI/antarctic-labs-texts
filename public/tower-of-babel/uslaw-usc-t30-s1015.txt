30 U.S.C. § 1015 — Requirement for lessees
Citation: us:usc:30:1015
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section1015
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 23 (Geothermal Resources). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Leases under this chapter may be issued only to citizens of the United States, associations of such citizens, corporations organized under the laws of the United States or of any State or the District of Columbia, or governmental units, including, without limitation, municipalities.
