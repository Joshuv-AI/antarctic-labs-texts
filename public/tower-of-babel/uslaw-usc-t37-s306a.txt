37 U.S.C. § 306a — Special pay: members assigned to international military headquarters
Citation: us:usc:37:306a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title37-section306a
Context: United States Code, Title 37 (Pay And Allowances Of The Uniformed Services); Chapter 5 (Special And Incentive Pays); Subchapter I (Existing Special Pay, Incentive Pay, And Bonus Authorities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not more than nine members of the armed forces, including members detailed to international military headquarters, may be paid pay and allowances at rates referred to in section 625(d) of the Foreign Assistance Act of 1961 (22 U.S.C. 2385(d)).
