16 C.F.R. § 1211.3 — Units of measurement.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1211.3
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1211.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1211 (Safety Standard For Automatic Residential Garage Door Operators). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a value for measurement is followed by a value in other units, in parentheses, the second value may be only approximate. The first stated value is the requirement.
