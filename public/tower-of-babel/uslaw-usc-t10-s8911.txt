10 U.S.C. § 8911 — Custody of departmental records and property
Citation: us:usc:10:8911
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8911
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 889 (Accountability And Responsibility). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Navy has custody and charge of all books, records, papers, furniture, fixtures, and other property under the lawful control of the executive part of the Department of the Navy.
