23 C.F.R. § 470.115 — Approval authority.
Citation: us:cfr:23:470.115
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-470.115
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 470 (Highway Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Federal Highway Administrator will approve Federal-aid highway system actions involving the designation, or revision, of routes on the Interstate System, including route numbers, future Interstate routes, and routes on the National Highway System.
(b) The Federal Highway Administrator will approve functional classification actions.
