47 C.F.R. § 25.206 — Station identification.
Citation: us:cfr:47:25.206
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-25.206
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 25 (Satellite Communications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirement to transmit station identification is waived for all radio stations licensed under this part with the exception of earth stations subject to the requirements of § 25.281.
