25 C.F.R. § 40.4 — Security.
Citation: us:cfr:25:40.4
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-40.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 40 (Administration Of Educational Loans, Grants And Other Assistance For Higher Education). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a borrower or cosigner has security to offer for an educational loan it must be given in an amount adequate to protect the loan.
