40 C.F.R. § 51.105 — Approval of plans.
Citation: us:cfr:40:51.105
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-51.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 51 (Requirements For Preparation, Adoption, And Submittal Of Implementation Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Revisions of a plan, or any portion thereof, will not be considered part of an applicable plan until such revisions have been approved by the Administrator in accordance with this part.
