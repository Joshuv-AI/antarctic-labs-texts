Decomposition of Cliques into k k -Star-Forests
Jiaxi Nie, Yibo Ren, Hehui Wu

Abstract
A k k -star-forest is a forest with at most k k connected components where each component is a star. Let F k ​ ( n ) F_{k}(n) be the minimum integer such that the complete graph on n n vertices can be decomposed into F k ​ ( n ) F_{k}(n) k k -star-forests. Pach, Saghafian and Schnider showed that F 2 ​ ( n ) = ⌈ 3 ​ n / 4 ⌉ F_{2}(n)=\lceil 3n/4\rceil . In this paper, we show that F 3 ​ ( n ) = 5 ​ n / 9 F_{3}(n)=5n/9 when n n is a multiple of 27. Further, for k ≥ 4 k\geq 4 , we show that F k ​ ( n ) = n / 2 + 2 F_{k}(n)=n/2+2 when n > 2 ​ k n>2k and n ≡ 4 ( mod 12 ) n\equiv 4\pmod{12} . Our results disprove a conjecture of Pach, Saghafian and Schnider.

Introduction

It is a classic theme in combinatorics to determine the minimum number of subgraphs of a certain type that a graph
G
can be decomposed into. Here a decomposition of a graph
G
is a family of subgraphs that partitions
E(G)
. For example, the seminal result of Graham and Pollak
[
3
]
shows that

, the complete graph on
n

n-1
complete bipartite graphs (on the other hand, it is easy to see that
K_{n}
can be decomposed into
n-1
stars). Other known results under this theme have also been obtained by Vizing
[
7
]
for matchings, by Lovász
[
4
]
for paths and cycles, and by Nash-Williams
[
5
]
for forests.

A

is a forest whose components are all stars. Akiyama and Kano
[
1
]
proved that

cannot be decomposed into less than
\lceil n/2\rceil+1
star-forests and this bound is tight.

For integer

, a
k
-star-forest
is a star-forest with at most
k
connected components. Recently, Pach, Saghafian and Schnider
[
6
]
studied the decomposition of cliques into
k
-star-forests. Let

be the minimum integer such that the complete graph on
n
vertices can be decomposed into
F_{k}(n)

k
-star-forests. Pach, Saghafian and Schinider proved the following theorem.

Theorem 1.1

(
[
6
]
, Theorem 4)
.

For
n\geq 3
,
F_{2}(n)=\lceil 3n/4\rceil
.

We use the notation

to denote the star with center

and leaves

. The construction for

can be described as follows. For simplicity, we will only describe it for even integer

Theorem 1.2

(
[

]

Let

into

Note that for 2-star-forests, one can decompose the edges of the form

Theorem 1.4
.

such that centers of

, and

\begin{cases}Q_{ib}Q_{ub^{\prime}},~~\forall b^{\prime}\in\{1,2,\dots,t\},~\text{if }P_{i}P_{u}\in S_{j},\\
