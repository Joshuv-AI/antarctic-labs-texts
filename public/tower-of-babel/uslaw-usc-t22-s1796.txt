22 U.S.C. § 1796 — Participation in Joint Commission on Rural Reconstruction in China
Citation: us:usc:22:1796
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1796
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 24 (Mutual Security Program); Subchapter I (General And Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President is authorized to continue to participate in the Joint Commission on Rural Reconstruction in China and to appoint citizens of the United States to the Commission.
