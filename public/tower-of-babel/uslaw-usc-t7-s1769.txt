7 U.S.C. § 1769 — “Agricultural commodity” defined
Citation: us:usc:7:1769
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1769
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 43 (Foreign Market Development); Subchapter III (Representation Allowances, Regulations, General Provisions, And Authorization For Appropriations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The term “agricultural commodity” as used in this chapter includes any agricultural commodity or product thereof.
