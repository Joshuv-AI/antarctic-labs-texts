10 U.S.C. § 884 — Art. 84. Breach of medical quarantine
Citation: us:usc:10:884
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section884
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 47 (Uniform Code Of Military Justice); Subchapter X (Punitive Articles). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person subject to this chapter—
(1)
who is ordered into medical quarantine by a person authorized to issue such order; and
(2)
who, with knowledge of the quarantine and the limits of the quarantine, goes beyond those limits before being released from the quarantine by proper authority;
shall be punished as a court-martial may direct.
