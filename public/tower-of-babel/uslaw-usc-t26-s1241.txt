26 U.S.C. § 1241 — Cancellation of lease or distributor’s agreement
Citation: us:usc:26:1241
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section1241
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 1 (Normal Taxes And Surtaxes); Subchapter P (Capital Gains And Losses); Part IV (Special Rules For Determining Capital Gains And Losses). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Amounts received by a lessee for the cancellation of a lease, or by a distributor of goods for the cancellation of a distributor’s agreement (if the distributor has a substantial capital investment in the distributorship), shall be considered as amounts received in exchange for such lease or agreement.
