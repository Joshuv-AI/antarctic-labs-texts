20 U.S.C. § 113 — Teachers’ certificates dependent on passing examination on effect of alcoholic drinks and narcotics
Citation: us:usc:20:113
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section113
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 7 (Instruction As To Nature And Effect Of Alcoholic Drinks And Narcotics). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No certificate shall be granted to any person to teach in the public schools of the District of Columbia or Territories who has not passed a satisfactory examination in physiology and hygiene, with special reference to the nature and the effects of alcoholic drinks and other narcotics upon the human system.
