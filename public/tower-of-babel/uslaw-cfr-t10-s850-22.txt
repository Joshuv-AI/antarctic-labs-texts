10 C.F.R. § 850.22 — Permissible exposure limit.
Citation: us:cfr:10:850.22
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-850.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 850 (Chronic Beryllium Disease Prevention Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The responsible employer must assure that no worker is exposed to an airborne concentration of beryllium greater than the permissible exposure limit established in 29 CFR 1910.1000, as measured in the worker's breathing zone by personal monitoring, or a more stringent TWA PEL that may be promulgated by the Occupational Safety and Health Administration as a health standard.
