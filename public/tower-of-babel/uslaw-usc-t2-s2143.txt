2 U.S.C. § 2143 — Utilization of personnel by Architect of the Capitol for maintenance and operation of Botanic Garden
Citation: us:usc:2:2143
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section2143
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 30 (Operation And Maintenance Of Capitol Complex); Subchapter VI (Botanic Garden And National Garden). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On and after December 27, 1974, with the approval of the Joint Committee on the Library, the Architect of the Capitol may utilize personnel paid from appropriations under his control for performance of administrative and clerical duties in connection with the maintenance and operation of the United States Botanic Garden, to such extent as he may deem feasible.
