30 C.F.R. § 778.18 — Insurance.
Citation: us:cfr:30:778.18
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-778.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 778 (Permit Applications—Minimum Requirements For Legal, Financial, Compliance, And Related Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An application shall contain either a certificate of liability insurance or evidence of self-insurance in compliance with § 800.60 of this chapter.
