45 U.S.C. § 231s — Separability
Citation: us:usc:45:231s
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2024
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title45-section231s
Context: United States Code, Title 45 (Railroads); Chapter 9 (Retirement Of Railroad Employees); Subchapter IV (Railroad Retirement Act Of 1974). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this subchapter, or the application thereof to any person or circumstance, should be held invalid, the remainder of such subchapter, or the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances, shall not be affected thereby.
