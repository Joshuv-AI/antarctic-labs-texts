7 C.F.R. § 621.21 — Who may obtain assistance.
Citation: us:cfr:7:621.21
Creator: Natural Resources Conservation Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-621.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 621 (River Basin Investigations And Surveys). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Assistance is available to conservation districts, communities, county governments, regional planning boards, other planning groups, and State and Federal agencies.
