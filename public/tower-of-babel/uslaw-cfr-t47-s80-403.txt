47 C.F.R. § 80.403 — Availability of documents.
Citation: us:cfr:47:80.403
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-80.403
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 80 (Stations In The Maritime Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Station documents must be readily available to the licensed operator(s) on duty during the hours of service of the station and to authorized Commission employees upon request.
