2 U.S.C. § 1962 — Detail of police
Citation: us:usc:2:1962
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1962
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 29 (Capitol Police); Subchapter II (Powers And Duties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Capitol Police Board is authorized to detail police from the House Office, Senate Office, and Capitol Buildings for police duty on the Capitol Grounds and on the Library of Congress Grounds.
