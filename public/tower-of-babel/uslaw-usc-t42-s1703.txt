42 U.S.C. § 1703 — “Contractor with the United States” defined
Citation: us:usc:42:1703
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1703
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 12 (Compensation For Injury, Death, Or Detention Of Employees Of Contractors With United States Outside United States); Subchapter I (Compensation, Reimbursement, Etc., By Secretary Of Labor). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this subchapter, the term “contractor with the United States” includes any subcontractor or subordinate subcontractor with respect to the contract of such contractor.
