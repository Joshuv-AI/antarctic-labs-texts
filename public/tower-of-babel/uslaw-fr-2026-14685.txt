Order Adopting Categorical Exclusions From Tennessee Valley 
Authority Under the National Environmental Policy Act Notice of 
Adoption of Categorical Exclusions Under the National Environmental 
Policy Act

    On July 16, 2026, the Commission issued an order in Docket No. 
CX26-2-000, pursuant to section 109 of the National Environmental 
Policy Act (NEPA), 42 U.S.C. 4336c, adopting two categorical exclusions 
from the Tennessee Valley Authority (TVA) relating the development of 
recreation sites and public use areas. Order Adopting Categorical 
Exclusions from TVA under NEPA, 196 FERC ] 61,037 (2026). The adopted 
categorical exclusions will be used for actions concerning water power 
projects.
    The adoption becomes effective 31 days after the issuance date of 
the Commission's order, i.e., on August 17, 2026.
