33 C.F.R. § 149.675 — What are the requirements for the public address system?
Citation: us:cfr:33:149.675
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-149.675
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 149 (Deepwater Ports: Design, Construction, And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) For a manned deepwater port, each pumping platform complex must have a public address system operable from two locations on the complex.
(b) For an unmanned deepwater port, the vessel master must provide a working public address system on a vessel while it is moored or otherwise connected to the deepwater port.
