16 C.F.R. § 1027.11 — Non-waiver of rights.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1027.11
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1027.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1027 (Salary Offset). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee's involuntary payment of all or any part of a debt collected under these regulations will not be construed as a waiver of any rights that the employee may have under 5 U.S.C. 5514 or any other provision of law.
