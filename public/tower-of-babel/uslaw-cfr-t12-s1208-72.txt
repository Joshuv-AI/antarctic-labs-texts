12 C.F.R. § 1208.72 — Failure to appear.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1208.72
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1208.72
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1208 (Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the absence of good cause shown, a debtor who fails to appear at a scheduled hearing will be deemed as not having timely filed a request for a hearing.
