49 C.F.R. § 561.11 — Record retention.
Citation: us:cfr:49:561.11
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-561.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 561 (Documentation For Electric-Powered Vehicles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each applicable manufacturer shall maintain the information specified in §§ 561.7 through 561.10 for the documentation requirements for a period of five years from the date of manufacture.
