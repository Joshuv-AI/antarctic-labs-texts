31 C.F.R. § 375.11 — Who may participate in a redemption operation?
Citation: us:cfr:31:375.11
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-375.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 375 (Marketable Treasury Securities Redemption Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Submitters. To be a submitter, you must be:
(1) An institution that the Federal Reserve Bank of New York has designated as a primary dealer; or
(2) An entity approved by the Treasury Department to participate directly in redemption operations.
(b) Others. A person or entity other than a submitter may participate only if it arranges to have an offer or offers submitted on its behalf by a submitter.
