20 C.F.R. § 217.5 — When an application is a claim for an annuity or lump sum.
Citation: us:cfr:20:217.5
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-217.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 217 (Application For Annuity Or Lump Sum). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An application is a claim for an annuity or lump sum if it meets all of the following conditions:
(a) It is on an application form completed and filed with the Board as described in § 217.6;
(b) It is signed by the claimant or by someone described in § 217.17 who can sign the application for the claimant;
(c) It is filed with the Board on or before the date of death of the claimant. (See § 217.10 for limited exceptions.)
