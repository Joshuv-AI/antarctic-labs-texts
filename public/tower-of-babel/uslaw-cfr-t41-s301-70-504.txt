41 C.F.R. § 301-70.504 — -70.504 Reimbursement if an employee discontinues a TDY assignment because of a personal emergency situation.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-70.504
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-70.504
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-70 (70—Internal Policy And Procedure Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agency, with the approval of an appropriate agency official, may authorize reimbursement of appropriate transportation and per diem expenses while en route for return travel from the point of interruption to the official station.
