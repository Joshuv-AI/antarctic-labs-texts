9 C.F.R. § 96.4 — Uncertified animal casings; disposition.
Citation: us:cfr:9:96.4
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-96.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 96 (Restriction Of Importations Of Foreign Animal Casings Offered For Entry Into The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Animal casings which are unaccompanied by the required certificate, those shipped in sheepskins or other skins as containers, and those found upon inspection to be unclean or unsound when offered for importation into the United States shall be kept in customs custody until exported or destroyed, or until disinfected and denaturated as prescribed by the Administrator, Animal and Plant Health Inspection Service (APHIS) in §§ 96.5 through 96.16.
