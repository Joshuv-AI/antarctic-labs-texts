22 C.F.R. § 909.6 — Summaries of Board decisions.
Citation: us:cfr:22:909.6
Creator: Foreign Service Grievance Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-909.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 909 (Decisionmaking). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board may, from time to time, issue such summaries and expurgated versions of its decisions as it may consider necessary to permit the Agencies, the exclusive representative organization(s), and the members of the Service to become aware of the general nature of the cases it has received and their manner of disposition, without invading the privacy of the grievants.
