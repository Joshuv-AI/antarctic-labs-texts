43 C.F.R. § 3514.12 — What additional information should I include in a request for partial relinquishment?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3514.12
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3514.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3500 (Leasing Of Solid Minerals Other Than Coal And Oil Shale). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any partial relinquishment must also clearly describe the lands you are relinquishing and give the exact area involved.
