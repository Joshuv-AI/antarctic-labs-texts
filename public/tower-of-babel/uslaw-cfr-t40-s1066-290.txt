40 C.F.R. § 1066.290 — Verification of speed accuracy for the driver's aid.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1066.290
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1066.290
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1066 (Vehicle-Testing Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Use good engineering judgment to provide a driver's aid that facilitates compliance with the requirements of § 1066.425. Verify the speed accuracy of the driver's aid as described in § 1066.235.
