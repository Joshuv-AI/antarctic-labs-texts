29 C.F.R. § 2400.3 — Delegation of authority.
Citation: us:cfr:29:2400.3
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Review Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-2400.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 2400 (Regulations Implementing The Privacy Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Chairman shall designate an OSHRC employee as the Privacy Officer and shall delegate to the Privacy Officer the authority to ensure agency-wide compliance with this part. As necessary, the Privacy Officer shall coordinate this delegated responsibility with the Senior Agency Official for Privacy.
