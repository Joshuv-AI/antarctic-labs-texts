15 U.S.C. § 3418 — Applicability of other Federal statutory provisions relating to information-gathering
Citation: us:usc:15:3418
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section3418
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 60 (Natural Gas Policy); Subchapter V (Administration, Enforcement, And Review). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In order to obtain information for the purpose of carrying out its functions under this chapter, the Commission shall have the same authority as is vested in the Secretary under 7151(a) of title 42 with respect to the exercise of authority under section 796(b) of this title and section 772(b), (c), and (d) of this title.
