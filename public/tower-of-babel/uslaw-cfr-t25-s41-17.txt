25 C.F.R. § 41.17 — What is the role of the Secretary of Education?
Citation: us:cfr:25:41.17
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-41.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 41 (Grants To Tribal Colleges And Universities And Diné College). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Secretary may enter into an agreement with the Secretary of Education to obtain assistance to:
(1) Develop plans, procedures, and criteria for eligibility studies required under this subpart; and
(2) Conduct such studies.
(b) BIE must consult with the Secretary of Education to determine the reasonable number of students required to support a Tribal college or university.
