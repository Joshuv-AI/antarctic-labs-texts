42 C.F.R. § 402.13 — Penalty, assessment, and exclusion not exclusive.
Citation: us:cfr:42:402.13
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-402.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 402 (Civil Money Penalties, Assessments, And Exclusions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Penalties, assessments, and exclusions imposed under this part are in addition to any other penalties prescribed by law.
