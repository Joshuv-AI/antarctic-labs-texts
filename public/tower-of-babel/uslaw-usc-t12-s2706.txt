12 U.S.C. § 2706 — Emergency Homeowners’ Relief Fund
Citation: us:usc:12:2706
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section2706
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 28 (Emergency Mortgage Relief). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To carry out the purposes of this chapter, the Secretary is authorized to establish in the Treasury of the United States an Emergency Homeowners’ Relief Fund (hereinafter in this chapter referred to as the “fund”) which shall be available to the Secretary without fiscal year limitation—
(1)
for making payments in connection with defaulted loans or advances of credit insured under section 2704 of this title;
(2)
for making emergency mortgage relief payments under section 2705 of this title;
(3)
to pay such administrative expenses (or portion of such expenses) of carrying out the provisions of this chapter as the Secretary may deem necessary.
