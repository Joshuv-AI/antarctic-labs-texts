28 U.S.C. § 1693 — Place of arrest in civil action
Citation: us:usc:28:1693
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1693
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part V (Procedure); Chapter 113 (Process). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as otherwise provided by Act of Congress, no person shall be arrested in one district for trial in another in any civil action in a district court.
