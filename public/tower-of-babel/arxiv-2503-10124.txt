Spivey’s type recurrence relation for Lah-Bell polynomials
Taekyun Kim, Dae San Kim

Abstract
. The aim of this paper is to derive Spivey’s type recurrence relations for the Lah-Bell polynomials and the r r -Lah-Bell polynomials by utilizing operators X X and D D satisfying the commutation relation D ​ X − X ​ D = 1 DX-XD=1 . Here X X is the ‘multiplication by x x ’ operator and D D is the differentiation operator D = d d ​ x D=\frac{d}{dx} . In addition, we obtain Spivey’s type recurrence relation for the λ \lambda analogue of r r -Lah-Bell polynomials by some other method without using the operators X X and D D .

1.
Introduction

The Stirling number of the second kind
{n\brace k}
counts the number of partitions of the set
[n]=\{1,2,\dots,n\}
into
k
nonempty disjoint subsets (see [18,21,24,26]). The Bell number
\phi_{n}
enumerates the number of all partitions of the set
[n]
into nonempty disjoint subsets and hence we have
\phi_{n}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n\brace k}
. The Bell polynomial
\phi_{n}(x)
is the natural polynomial extension of the Bell number
\phi_{n}
(see [9,10,22,32]), defined by
\phi_{n}(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n\brace k}x^{k}
and also given by Dobinski’s formula
\phi_{n}(x)=e^{-x}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{k^{n}}{k!}x^{k}
.

The Lah number
L(n,k)
is the number of partitions of the set
[n]
into
k
nonempty disjoint linear ordered subsets (see [6,14,27,30,34,37]). The Lah-Bell number
LB_{n}
is the number of all partitions of the set
[n]
into nonempty disjoint linearly ordered subsets and hence we have
LB_{n}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}L(n,k)
(see [1,5,6,12-15,27,29]). The Lah-Bell polynomial
LB_{n}(x)
is the natural polynomial extension of the Lah-Bell number
LB_{n}
, defined by
LB_{n}(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}L(n,k)x^{k}
.

Let
r
be a nonnegative integer. The
r
-Lah number
L^{r}(n,k)
is the number of partitions of the set
[n]
into
k
nonempty disjoint linearly ordered subsets in such a way that the number
1,2,3,\dots,r
are in distinct nonempty disjoint linearly ordered subsets (see [12,30,34]). The
r
-Lah-Bell number
LB_{n}^{(r)}
is the number of all partitions of the set
[n]
into nonempty disjoint linearly ordered subsets in such a way that the number
1,2,3,\dots,r
are in distinct nonempty disjoint linearly ordered subsets and hence we have
LB_{n}^{(r)}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}L^{r}(n,k)
. The
r
-Lah-Bell polynomial
LB_{n}^{(r)}(x)
is the natural polynomial extension of the
r
-Lah-Bell number
LB_{n}^{(r)}
, defined by
LB_{n}^{(r)}(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}L^{r}(n,k)x^{k}
.

The Spivey showed by combinatorial method the following recurrence relation for the Bell numbers which is given by

\phi_{n+m}=\sum_{j=0}^{m}\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}{m\brace j}j^{n-k}\phi_{k},\quad(n,m\geq 0),\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [35]).

Subsequent to Spivey’s work, a lot of researchers have investigated and expanded upon his formula for the Bell numbers (see [20]).

Let
X
be the ‘multiplication by
x
’ operator, and let
D=\frac{d}{dx}
. Then
X
and
D
satisfy the commutation relation
DX-XD=1
(see [3,4,11,16,17,23,25,31]). In this paper, we show Spivey’s type recurrence relations for the Lah-Bell polynomials
LB_{n}(x)
(see [12,13,15,28,29]) and the
r
-Lah-Bell polynomials
LB_{n}^{(r)}(x)
by using the operators
X
and
D
. Indeed, for the
r
-Lah-Bell polynomials we show

\mathrm{LB}_{n+m}^{(r)}(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{m}\sum_{l=0}^{n}\binom{n}{l}L^{r}(m,k)\langle m+k\rangle_{n-l}x^{k}\mathrm{LB}_{l}^{(r)}(x).

Futhermore, we consider the
\lambda
-analogues of
r
-Lah numbers and
r
-Lah-Bell polynomials and show by utilizing some other method without using the operators
X
and
D
the following Spivey’s type recurrence relation for the
\lambda
-analogue of
r
-Lah-Bell polynomials

\mathrm{LB}_{n+m,\lambda}^{(r)}(x)=\sum_{j=0}^{m}\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}L_{\lambda}^{r}(m,j)\langle m+j\rangle_{n-k}x^{j}\lambda^{n-k}\mathrm{LB}_{k,\lambda}^{(r)}(x).

Along the way, we derive the generating function and an explicit expression for the
r
-Lah numbers. We obtain the generating function and Dobinski-like formula for the
r
-Lah-Bell polynomials. We deduce the generating function and an explicit expression for the
\lambda
-analogue of
r
-Lah numbers. We get the generating function and Dobinski-like formula for the
\lambda
-analogue of the
r
-Lah-Bell polynomials. We let the reader refer to [1,2,5,7,8,19,33,36] as general references. For the rest of this section, we recall what are necessary throughout this paper.

Let
(x)_{n}
be the falling factorial sequence with

(x)_{0}=1,\ (x)_{n}=x(x-1)(x-2)\cdots(x-n+1),\ (n\geq 1),

and let
\langle x\rangle_{n}
be the rising factorial sequence with

\langle x\rangle_{0}=1,\quad\langle x\rangle_{n}=x(x+1)(x+2)\cdots(x+n-1),\ (n\geq 1).

For
n,k\geq 0
, the Lah numbers are given by

(1)

\langle x\rangle_{n}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}L(n,k)(x)_{k},\quad(n\geq 0),\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [12,13]).

From (
1
), we note that

(2)

\displaystyle\Big(\frac{1}{1-t}\Big)^{x}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\binom{x+n-1}{n}t^{n}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\langle x\rangle_{n}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{k=0}^{n}L(n,k)(x)_{k}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}L(n,k)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}(x)_{k}.

On the other other hand, by binomial expansion, we get

(3)

\Big(\frac{1}{1-t}\Big)^{x}=\Big(1+\frac{t}{1-t}\Big)^{x}=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k!}\Big(\frac{t}{1-t}\Big)^{k}(x)_{k}.

By (
2
) and (
3
), we get

(4)

\frac{1}{k!}\Big(\frac{t}{1-t}\Big)^{k}=\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}L(n,k)\frac{t^{n}}{n!},\quad(k\geq 0),\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [12,13]).

From (
4
), we have

(5)

L(n,k)=\frac{n!}{k!}\binom{n-1}{k-1},\quad(n,k\geq 0),\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [12,13,27,29]).

The Lah-Bell polynomials are defined by

(6)

\mathrm{LB}_{n}(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}L(n,k)x^{k},\quad(n\geq 0),\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [13]).

By (
4
) and (
6
), we get

(7)

e^{x(\frac{1}{1-t}-1)}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\mathrm{LB}_{n}(x)\frac{t^{n}}{n!},\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [13]).

From (
7
), we have the Dobinski-like formula for the Lah-Bell polynomials

(8)

\mathrm{LB}_{n}(x)=e^{-x}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{\langle k\rangle_{n}}{k!}x^{k},\quad(n\geq 0).

Now, we define the operators
X
and
D
by

(9)

Xf(x)=xf(x),\quad Df(x)=\frac{df(x)}{dx},\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [3,4,9,10,11,17]),

satisfying the commutation relation
DX-XD=1
.

From (
9
), we note that

(10)

\displaystyle DX^{k}-X^{k}D

\displaystyle=\big(DX^{k-1}-X^{k-1}D\big)X+X^{k-1}\big(DX-XD\big)

\displaystyle=\big(DX-XD\big)X^{k-1}+(k-1)X^{k-1}=kX^{k-1},\quad(k\in\mathbb{N}).

Thus, by (
10
), we get

(11)

\big(XD\big)X^{k}=X^{k}\big(XD+k\big),\quad(k\in\mathbb{N}),\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [3,4,11,16,17,23,25,31]).

2.
Spivey’s type recurrence relations for Lah-Bell and
r
-Lah-Bell polynomials

For
f(x)=x^{m},\ (m\geq 0)
, we have

(12)

\displaystyle\langle XD\rangle_{n}f(x)

\displaystyle=\big(XD\big)\big(XD+1\big)\big(XD+2\big)\cdots\big(XD+n-1\big)x^{m}

\displaystyle=m(m+1)(m+2)\cdots(m+n-1\big)x^{m}

\displaystyle=\langle m\rangle_{n}x^{m}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}L(n,k)(m)_{k}x^{m}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{n}L(n,k)X^{k}D^{k}x^{m}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}L(n,k)X^{k}D^{k}f(x).

Thus, by (
12
), we get

(13)

\langle XD\rangle_{n}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}L(n,k)X^{k}D^{k},\quad(n\geq 0).

From (
13
), we note that

(14)

\displaystyle\frac{1}{e^{x}}\langle XD\rangle_{n}e^{x}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{e^{x}}\sum_{k=0}^{n}L(n,k)X^{k}D^{k}e^{x}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{e^{x}}\sum_{k=0}^{n}L(n,k)x^{k}e^{x}=\mathrm{LB}_{n}(x),\quad(n\geq 0).

Therefore, by (
14
), we obtain the following theorem.

Theorem 2.1
.

For
n\geq 0
, we have

\frac{1}{e^{x}}\langle XD\rangle_{n}e^{x}=\mathrm{LB}_{n}(x).

Now, we observe from (
2
) that

(15)

\displaystyle\Big(\frac{1}{1-t}\Big)^{x+y}

\displaystyle=\Big(\frac{1}{1-t}\Big)^{x}\Big(\frac{1}{1-t}\Big)^{y}=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\langle x\rangle_{k}\frac{t^{k}}{k!}\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}\langle y\rangle_{l}\frac{t^{l}}{l!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\langle x\rangle_{k}\langle y\rangle_{n-l}\frac{t^{n}}{n!},

and that

(16)

\displaystyle\Big(\frac{1}{1-t}\Big)^{x+y}

\displaystyle=\Big(\frac{1}{1-t}\Big)^{x}\Big(\frac{1}{1-t}\Big)^{y}=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\binom{x+k-1}{k}t^{k}\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}\binom{y+l-1}{l}t^{l}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{x+k-1}{k}\binom{y+n-k-1}{n-k}t^{n}.

From (
15
) and (
16
), we get

(17)

\displaystyle\langle x+y\rangle_{n}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\langle x\rangle_{k}\langle y\rangle_{n-k},

\displaystyle\binom{x+y+n-1}{n}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{x+k-1}{k}\binom{y+n-k-1}{n-k},\quad(n\geq 0).

For
m,n\geq 0
, we have

(18)

\displaystyle\langle XD\rangle_{n+m}

\displaystyle=XD\big(XD+1\big)\cdots\big(XD+m-1\big)\cdots\big(XD+m+(n-1)\big)

\displaystyle=\langle XD\rangle_{m}\langle XD+m\rangle_{n}=\langle XD+m\rangle_{n}\langle XD\rangle_{m}

\displaystyle=\langle XD+m\rangle_{n}\sum_{j=0}^{m}L(m,j)X^{j}D^{j}=\sum_{j=0}^{m}L(m,j)\langle XD+m\rangle_{n}X^{j}D^{j}.

From (
11
) and (
17
), we note that

(19)

\displaystyle\langle XD+m\rangle_{n}X^{j}

\displaystyle=\big(XD+m\big)\big(XD+m+1\big)\cdots\big(XD+m+n-1)X^{j}

\displaystyle=\big(XD+m\big)\cdots\big(XD+m+n-2\big)X^{j}\big(XD+m+n-1+j\big)

\displaystyle=\cdots

\displaystyle=X^{j}\big(XD+m+j\big)\big(XD+m+j+1\big)\cdots\big(XD+m+j+n-1\big)

\displaystyle=X^{j}\langle XD+m+j\rangle_{n}=X^{j}\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\langle XD\rangle_{k}\langle m+j\rangle_{n-k}.

By (
18
) and (
19
), we get

(20)

\langle XD\rangle_{n+m}=\sum_{j=0}^{m}\sum_{k=0}^{n}L(m,j)\binom{n}{k}\langle m+j\rangle_{n-k}X^{j}\langle XD\rangle_{k}D^{j}.

For
m,n\geq 0
, by (
14
) and (
20
), we have

(21)

\displaystyle\mathrm{LB}_{n+m}(x)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{e^{x}}\langle XD\rangle_{n+m}e^{x}=\frac{1}{e^{x}}\langle XD+m\rangle_{n}\langle XD\rangle_{m}e^{x}

\displaystyle=\sum_{j=0}^{m}\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}L(m,j)\langle m+j\rangle_{n-k}\frac{1}{e^{x}}X^{j}\langle XD\rangle_{k}D^{j}e^{x}

\displaystyle=\sum_{j=0}^{m}\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}L(m,j)\langle m+j\rangle_{n-k}\frac{1}{e^{x}}x^{j}\langle XD\rangle_{k}e^{x}

\displaystyle=\sum_{j=0}^{m}\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}L(m,j)\langle m+j\rangle_{n-k}x^{j}\mathrm{LB}_{k}(x).

Therefore, by (
21
), we obtain the following Spivey’s type formula for the Lah-Bell polynomials.

Theorem 2.2
.

For
m,n\geq 0
, we have

\mathrm{LB}_{n+m}(x)=\sum_{j=0}^{m}\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}L(m,j)\langle m+j\rangle_{n-k}x^{j}\mathrm{LB}_{k}(x).

In particular, letting
x=1
gives

\mathrm{LB}_{n+m}=\sum_{j=0}^{m}\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}L(m,j)\langle m+j\rangle_{n-k}\mathrm{LB}_{k}.

For any integer
r\geq 0
, we consider the
r
-Lah numbers defined by

(22)

\langle x+r\rangle_{n}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}L^{r}(n,k)(x)_{k},\quad(n\geq 0).

Thus, by (
2
), (
3
) and (
22
), we get

(23)

\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k!}\Big(\frac{1}{1-t}\Big)^{r}\Big(\frac{t}{1-t}\Big)^{k}(x)_{k}=\Big(\frac{1}{1-t}\Big)^{x+r}=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}L^{r}(n,k)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}(x)_{k}.

From (
23
), we note that

(24)

\frac{1}{k!}\Big(\frac{1}{1-t}\Big)^{r}\Big(\frac{1}{1-t}-1\Big)^{k}=\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}L^{r}(n,k)\frac{t^{n}}{n!},\quad(k\geq 0).

By (
24
), we easily get

(25)

\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}L^{r}(n,k)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}=\frac{t^{k}}{k!}\Big(\frac{1}{1-t}\Big)^{k+r}=\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}\frac{n!}{k!}\binom{n+r-1}{k+r-1}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

By comparing the coefficients on both sides of (
25
), we get

(26)

L^{r}(n,k)=\frac{n!}{k!}\binom{n+r-1}{k+r-1},\quad(n,k,r\geq 0).

In view of (
6
), we define
r
-Lah-Bell polynomials

(27)

\mathrm{LB}_{n}^{(r)}(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}L^{r}(n,k)x^{k},\quad(n\geq 0).

From (
24
) and (
27
), we note that

(28)

\Big(\frac{1}{1-t}\Big)^{r}e^{x\big(\frac{1}{1-t}-1\big)}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\mathrm{LB}_{n}^{(r)}(x)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

Thus, by (
28
), we get

(29)

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\mathrm{LB}_{n}^{(r)}(x)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{e^{x}}e^{\frac{x}{1-t}}\Big(\frac{1}{1-t}\Big)^{r}=\frac{1}{e^{x}}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}x^{k}\frac{1}{k!}\Big(\frac{1}{1-t}\Big)^{k+r}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{e^{x}}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{\langle k+r\rangle_{n}}{k!}x^{k}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

By comparing the coefficients on both sides of (
29
), we get the Dobinski-like formula for the
r
-Lah-Bell polynomials

(30)

\mathrm{LB}_{n}^{(r)}(x)=e^{-x}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{\langle k+r\rangle_{n}}{k!}x^{k},\quad(n\geq 0).

For
f(x)=x^{m},\ (m\geq 0)
, we have

(31)

\displaystyle\langle XD+r\rangle_{n}f(x)

\displaystyle=\langle XD+r\rangle_{n}x^{m}

\displaystyle=\big(XD+r\big)\big(XD+r+1\big)\cdots\big(XD+r+n-1\big)x^{m}

\displaystyle=\langle m+r\rangle_{n}x^{m}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}L^{r}(n,k)(m)_{k}x^{m}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{n}L^{r}(n,k)X^{k}D^{k}x^{m}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}L^{r}(n,k)X^{k}D^{k}f(x).

Thus, by (
31
), we get

(32)

\langle XD+r\rangle_{n}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}L^{r}(n,k)X^{k}D^{k},\quad(n,r\geq 0).

From (
32
), we note that

(33)

\displaystyle\frac{1}{e^{x}}\langle XD+r\rangle_{n}e^{x}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{e^{x}}\sum_{k=0}^{n}L^{r}(n,k)X^{k}D^{k}e^{x}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{e^{x}}\sum_{k=0}^{n}L^{r}(n,k)x^{k}e^{x}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{n}L^{r}(n,k)x^{k}=\mathrm{LB}_{n}^{(r)}(x),\quad(n\geq 0).

Therefore, by (
33
), we obtain the following theorem.

Theorem 2.3
.

For
n\geq 0
, we have

(34)

\frac{1}{e^{x}}\langle XD+r\rangle_{n}e^{x}=\mathrm{LB}_{n}^{(r)}(x).

For
m,n\geq 0
, we observe that

(35)

\langle XD+r\rangle_{n+m}=\langle XD+r\rangle_{m}\langle XD+r+m\rangle_{n}=\langle XD+r+m\rangle_{n}\langle XD+r\rangle_{m}.

For
k\in\mathbb{N}
, by (
11
), we get

(36)

\langle XD+r+m\rangle_{n}X^{k}=X^{k}\langle XD+r+m+k\rangle_{n}=X^{k}\sum_{l=0}^{n}\binom{n}{l}\langle XD+r\rangle_{l}\langle m+k\rangle_{n-l}.

Therefore, by (
36
), we obtain the following theorem.

Theorem 2.4
.

For
k\geq 1
, we have

(37)

\langle XD+r+m\rangle_{n}X^{k}=X^{k}\sum_{l=0}^{n}\binom{n}{l}\langle XD+r\rangle_{l}\langle m+k\rangle_{n-l},

where
m,n
are nonnegative integers.

By (
32
), (
35
) and (
37
), we get

(38)

\displaystyle\langle XD+r\rangle_{n+m}

\displaystyle=\langle XD+r+m\rangle_{n}\langle XD+r\rangle_{m}

\displaystyle=\langle XD+r+m\rangle_{n}\sum_{k=0}^{m}L^{r}(m,k)X^{k}D^{k}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{m}L^{r}(m,k)\langle XD+r+m\rangle_{n}X^{k}D^{k}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{m}\sum_{l=0}^{n}\binom{n}{l}L^{r}(m,k)\langle m+k\rangle_{n-l}X^{k}\langle XD+r\rangle_{l}D^{k}.

Therefore, by (
38
), we obtain the following theorem.

Theorem 2.5
.

For
m,n\geq 0
, we have

(39)

\langle XD+r\rangle_{n+m}=\sum_{k=0}^{m}\sum_{l=0}^{n}\binom{n}{l}L^{r}(m,k)\langle m+k\rangle_{n-l}X^{k}\langle XD+r\rangle_{l}D^{k}.

For
m,n\geq 0
, by (
34
) and (
39
), we get

(40)

\displaystyle\mathrm{LB}_{n+m}^{(r)}(x)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{e^{x}}\langle XD+r\rangle_{n+m}e^{x}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{e^{x}}\sum_{k=0}^{m}\sum_{l=0}^{n}\binom{n}{l}L^{r}(m,k)\langle m+k\rangle_{n-l}X^{k}\langle XD+r\rangle_{l}D^{k}e^{x}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{m}\sum_{l=0}^{n}\binom{n}{l}L^{r}(n,k)\langle m+k\rangle_{n-l}x^{k}\frac{1}{e^{x}}\langle XD+r\rangle_{l}e^{x}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{m}\sum_{l=0}^{n}\binom{n}{l}L^{r}(m,k)\langle m+k\rangle_{n-l}x^{k}\mathrm{LB}_{l}^{(r)}(x).

Therefore, by (
40
), we obtain the following Spivey’s type formula for the
r
-Lah-Bell polynomials.

Theorem 2.6
.

For
m,n\geq 0
, we have

\mathrm{LB}_{n+m}^{(r)}(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{m}\sum_{l=0}^{n}\binom{n}{l}L^{r}(m,k)\langle m+k\rangle_{n-l}x^{k}\mathrm{LB}_{l}^{(r)}(x).

In particular, letting
x=1
yields

\mathrm{LB}_{n+m}^{(r)}=\sum_{k=0}^{m}\sum_{l=0}^{n}\binom{n}{l}L^{r}(m,k)\langle m+k\rangle_{n-l}\mathrm{LB}_{l}^{(r)}.

3.
Spivey’s type recurrence relation for
\lambda
-analogue of
r
-Lah-Bell polynomials

Let
\lambda
be any nonzero real number. Then the degenerate falling factorial sequence
(x)_{n,\lambda}
, and the degenerate rising factorial sequence
\langle x\rangle_{n,\lambda}
are respectively defined by

(41)

(x)_{0,\lambda}=1,\quad(x)_{n,\lambda}=x(x-\lambda)(x-2\lambda)\cdots\big(x-(n-1)\lambda\big),\ (n\geq 1),

and

\langle x\rangle_{0,\lambda}=1,\ \langle x\rangle_{n,\lambda}=x(x+\lambda)(x+2\lambda)\cdots\big(x+(n-1)\lambda\big),\ (n\geq 1),\ (\mathrm{see}\ [12,16,17,23]).

The degenerate exponentials are given by

(42)

e_{\lambda}^{x}(t)=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}(x)_{k,\lambda}\frac{t^{k}}{k!},\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [16,17,23,23,27]).

In view of (
22
), we consider the
\lambda
-analogue of
r
-Lah numbers defined by

(43)

\langle x+r\rangle_{n,\lambda}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}L_{\lambda}^{r}(n,k)(x)_{k,\lambda},\quad(n,r\geq 0).

From (
43
), we note that

(44)

\displaystyle\Big(\frac{1}{1-\lambda t}\Big)^{\frac{x+r}{\lambda}}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\langle x+r\rangle_{n,\lambda}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{k=0}^{n}L_{\lambda}^{r}(n,k)(x)_{k,\lambda}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}L_{\lambda}^{r}(n,k)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}(x)_{k,\lambda}.

On the other hand,
\big(\frac{1}{1-\lambda t}\big)^{\frac{n+r}{\lambda}}
is given by

(45)

\displaystyle\Big(\frac{1}{1-\lambda t}\Big)^{\frac{x+r}{\lambda}}

\displaystyle=\Big(\frac{1}{1-\lambda t}\Big)^{\frac{r}{\lambda}}\Big(\frac{1}{1-\lambda t}\Big)^{\frac{x}{\lambda}}=\Big(\frac{1}{1-\lambda t}\Big)^{\frac{r}{\lambda}}\Big(\lambda\frac{t}{1-\lambda t}+1\Big)^{\frac{x}{\lambda}}

\displaystyle=\Big(\frac{1}{1-\lambda t}\Big)^{\frac{r}{\lambda}}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k!}\Big(\frac{t}{1-\lambda t}\Big)^{k}(x)_{k,\lambda}.

By (
44
) and (
45
), we obtain the following theorem.

Theorem 3.1
.

For
k\geq 0
, we have

(46)

\frac{1}{k!}\Big(\frac{t}{1-\lambda t}\Big)^{k}\Big(\frac{1}{1-\lambda t}\Big)^{\frac{r}{\lambda}}=\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}L_{\lambda}^{r}(n,k)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

From (
46
), we have

(47)

\displaystyle\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}L_{\lambda}^{r}(n,k)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=\frac{t^{k}}{k!}\Big(\frac{1}{1-\lambda t}\Big)^{\frac{r+\lambda k}{\lambda}}=\frac{t^{k}}{k!}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\binom{\frac{r+\lambda k}{\lambda}+n-1}{n}\lambda^{n}t^{n}

\displaystyle=\frac{t^{k}}{k!}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{\big(r+\lambda(k+n-1)\big)_{n,\lambda}}{n!}t^{n}=\frac{t^{k}}{k!}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\binom{r+\lambda(k+n-1)}{n}_{\lambda}t^{n}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}\frac{n!}{k!}\binom{r+\lambda(n-1)}{n-k}_{\lambda}\frac{t^{n}}{n!},

where
\binom{x}{n}_{\lambda}
are the degenerate binomial coefficients given by

\binom{x}{n}_{\lambda}=\frac{(x)_{n,\lambda}}{n!},\ (n\geq 1),\quad\binom{x}{0}_{\lambda}=1.

Therefore, by (
47
), we obtain the following theorem.

Theorem 3.2
.

For
n,k\geq 0
, we have

L^{r}_{\lambda}(n,k)=\frac{n!}{k!}\binom{r+\lambda(n-1)}{n-k}_{\lambda},

where
\binom{x}{k}_{\lambda}
are the degenerate binomial coefficients.

In view of (
27
), we define the
\lambda
-analogue of
r
-Lah-Bell polynomials given by

(48)

\mathrm{LB}_{n,\lambda}^{(r)}(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}L_{\lambda}^{r}(n,k)x^{k},\quad(n\geq 0).

By (
46
) and (
48
), we get

(49)

e^{\frac{x}{\lambda}\big(\frac{1}{1-\lambda t}-1\big)}\Big(\frac{1}{1-\lambda t}\Big)^{\frac{r}{\lambda}}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\mathrm{LB}_{n,\lambda}^{(r)}(x)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

Therefore, by (
49
), we obtain the following Dobinski-like theorem.

Theorem 3.3
.

For
n\geq 0
, we have

\mathrm{LB}_{n,\lambda}^{(r)}(x)=e^{-\frac{x}{\lambda}}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{\langle\lambda k+r\rangle_{n,\lambda}}{k!}\Big(\frac{x}{\lambda}\Big)^{k}.

Now, we observe from (
49
) that

(50)

\displaystyle\sum_{m,n=0}^{\infty}\mathrm{LB}_{n+m,\lambda}^{(r)}(t)\frac{x^{n}}{n!}\frac{y^{m}}{m!}=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\frac{y^{m}}{m!}D_{x}^{m}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\mathrm{LB}_{n,\lambda}^{(r)}(t)\frac{x^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{\mathrm{LB}_{n,\lambda}^{(r)}(t)}{n!}\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\frac{y^{m}}{m!}D_{x}^{m}x^{n}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{\mathrm{LB}_{n,\lambda}^{(r)}(t)}{n!}\sum_{m=0}^{n}\binom{n}{m}y^{m}x^{n-m}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{\mathrm{LB}_{n,\lambda}^{(r)}(t)}{n!}(x+y)^{n}=\bigg(\frac{1}{1-\lambda(x+y)}\bigg)^{\frac{r}{\lambda}}e^{\frac{t}{\lambda}\big(\frac{1}{1-\lambda(x+y)}-1\big)},

where
D_{x}=\frac{d}{dx}
.

We note from (
46
) and (
49
) that

(51)

\displaystyle\Big(\frac{1}{1-\lambda(x+y)}\Big)^{\frac{r}{\lambda}}e^{\frac{t}{\lambda}\big(\frac{1}{1-\lambda(x+y)}-1\big)}=\bigg(\cfrac{1}{(1-\lambda x)\big(1-\frac{\lambda y}{1-\lambda x}\big)}\bigg)^{\frac{r}{\lambda}}e^{\frac{t}{\lambda}\big(\frac{1}{(1-\lambda x)(1-\frac{\lambda y}{1-\lambda x})}-1\big)}

\displaystyle=\Big(\frac{1}{1-\lambda x}\Big)^{\frac{r}{\lambda}}e^{\frac{t}{\lambda}\big(\frac{1}{1-\lambda x}-1\big)}e^{\frac{t}{\lambda}\big(\frac{1}{1-\lambda x}\big)\big(\frac{1}{1-\frac{\lambda y}{1-\lambda x}}-1\big)}\bigg(\cfrac{1}{1-\frac{\lambda y}{1-\lambda x}}\bigg)^{\frac{r}{\lambda}}

\displaystyle=\Big(\frac{1}{1-\lambda x}\Big)^{\frac{r}{\lambda}}e^{\frac{t}{\lambda}\big(\frac{1}{1-\lambda x}-1\big)}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\Big(\frac{t}{\lambda}\Big)^{j}\Big(\frac{1}{1-\lambda x}\Big)^{j}\frac{1}{j!}\bigg(\cfrac{1}{1-\frac{\lambda y}{1-\lambda x}}-1\bigg)^{j}\bigg(\cfrac{1}{1-\frac{\lambda y}{1-\lambda x}}\bigg)^{\frac{r}{\lambda}}

\displaystyle=\Big(\frac{1}{1-\lambda x}\Big)^{\frac{r}{\lambda}}e^{\frac{t}{\lambda}\big(\frac{1}{1-\lambda x}-1\big)}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}t^{j}\sum_{m=j}^{\infty}L_{\lambda}^{r}(m,j)\frac{y^{m}}{m!}\Big(\frac{1}{1-\lambda x}\Big)^{m+j}

\displaystyle=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\sum_{j=0}^{m}t^{j}L_{\lambda}^{r}(m,j)\frac{y^{m}}{m!}\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}\langle m+j\rangle_{l}\lambda^{l}\frac{x^{l}}{l!}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\mathrm{LB}_{k,\lambda}^{(r)}(t)\frac{x^{k}}{k!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{m,n=0}^{\infty}\bigg(\sum_{j=0}^{m}\sum_{k=0}^{n}L_{\lambda}^{r}(m,j)\binom{n}{k}t^{j}\mathrm{LB}_{k,\lambda}^{(r)}(t)\langle m+j\rangle_{n-k}\lambda^{n-k}\bigg)\frac{x^{n}}{n!}\frac{y^{m}}{m!}.

Therefore, by (
50
) and (
51
), we obtain the following Spivey’s type formula for
\lambda
-analogue of
r
-Lah-Bell numbers.

Theorem 3.4
.

For
m,n\geq 0
, we have

\mathrm{LB}_{n+m,\lambda}^{(r)}(t)=\sum_{j=0}^{m}\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}L_{\lambda}^{r}(m,j)\langle m+j\rangle_{n-k}t^{j}\lambda^{n-k}\mathrm{LB}_{k,\lambda}^{(r)}(t).

In particular, letting
t=1
yields

\mathrm{LB}_{n+m,\lambda}^{(r)}=\sum_{j=0}^{m}\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}L_{\lambda}^{r}(m,j)\langle m+j\rangle_{n-k}\lambda^{n-k}\mathrm{LB}_{k,\lambda}^{(r)}.

4.
Conclusion

In this paper, we showed Spivey’s type recurrence relations for the Lah-Bell polynomials and the
r
-Lah-Bell polynomials by using the ‘multiplication by
x
’ operator
X
and the differentiation operator
D=\frac{d}{dx}
, which satisfy the commutation relation
DX-XD=1
. In addition, we obtained Spivey’s type recurrence relation for the
\lambda
-analogue of
r
-Lah-Bell polynomials without using the operators
X
and
D
.

Along the way, we derived the generating functions and explicit expressions for the
r
-Lah numbers and the
\lambda
-analogue of
r
-Lah numbers. For the latter, we obtained

\displaystyle\frac{1}{k!}\Big(\frac{t}{1-\lambda t}\Big)^{k}\Big(\frac{1}{1-\lambda t}\Big)^{\frac{r}{\lambda}}=\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}L_{\lambda}^{r}(n,k)\frac{t^{n}}{n!},

\displaystyle L^{r}_{\lambda}(n,k)=\frac{n!}{k!}\binom{r+\lambda(n-1)}{n-k}_{\lambda}.

In addition, we deduced the generating functions and Dobinski-like formulas for
r
-Lah-Bell polynomials and
\lambda
-analogue of
r
-Lah-Bell polynomials. For the latter, we got

\displaystyle e^{\frac{x}{\lambda}\big(\frac{1}{1-\lambda t}-1\big)}\Big(\frac{1}{1-\lambda t}\Big)^{\frac{r}{\lambda}}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\mathrm{LB}_{n,\lambda}^{(r)}(x)\frac{t^{n}}{n!},

\displaystyle\mathrm{LB}_{n,\lambda}^{(r)}(x)=e^{-\frac{x}{\lambda}}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{\langle\lambda k+r\rangle_{n,\lambda}}{k!}\Big(\frac{x}{\lambda}\Big)^{k}.
