46 C.F.R. § 80.40 — Civil penalty.
Citation: us:cfr:46:80.40
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-80.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 80 (Disclosure Of Safety Standards And Country Of Registry). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For each violation of the regulations in this part, the owner, operator, agent, or other person involved is subject to the penalties prescribed in 46 U.S.C. 3504.
