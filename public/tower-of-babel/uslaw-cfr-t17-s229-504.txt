17 C.F.R. § 229.504 — (Item 504) Use of proceeds.
Citation: us:cfr:17:229.504
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-229.504
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 229 (Standard Instructions For Filing Forms Under Securities Act Of 1933, Securities Exchange Act Of 1934 And Energy Policy And Conservation Act Of 1975—Regulation S-K). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

State the principal purposes for which the net proceeds to the registrant from the securities to be offered are intended to be used and the approximate amount intended to be used for each such purpose. Where registrant has no current specific plan for the proceeds, or a significant portion thereof, the registrant shall so state and discuss the principal reasons for the offering.
