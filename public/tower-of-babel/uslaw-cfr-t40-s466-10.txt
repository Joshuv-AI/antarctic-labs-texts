40 C.F.R. § 466.10 — Applicability; description of the steel basis material.
Citation: us:cfr:40:466.10
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-466.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 466 (Porcelain Enameling Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to discharges to waters of the United States, and introduction of pollutants into publicly owned treatment works from porcelain enameling on steel basis materials.
