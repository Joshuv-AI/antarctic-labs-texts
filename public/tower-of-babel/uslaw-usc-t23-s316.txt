23 U.S.C. § 316 — Consent by United States to conveyance of property
Citation: us:usc:23:316
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title23-section316
Context: United States Code, Title 23 (Highways); Chapter 3 (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purposes of this title the consent of the United States is given to any railroad or canal company to convey to the State transportation department of any State, or its nominee, any part of its right-of-way or other property in that State acquired by grant from the United States.
