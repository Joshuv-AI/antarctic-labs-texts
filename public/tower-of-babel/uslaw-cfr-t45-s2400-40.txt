45 C.F.R. § 2400.40 — Institutions of graduate study.
Citation: us:cfr:45:2400.40
Creator: James Madison Memorial Fellowship Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2400.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2400 (Fellowship Program Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Fellowship recipients may attend any accredited university in the United States with a master's degree program offering courses or training that emphasize the origins, principles, and development of the Constitution of the United States and its comparison with the constitutions and history of other forms of government.
