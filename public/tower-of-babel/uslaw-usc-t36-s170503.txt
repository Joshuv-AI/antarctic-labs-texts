36 U.S.C. § 170503 — Purposes
Citation: us:usc:36:170503
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section170503
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1705 (Polish Legion Of American Veterans, U.S.A.). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purposes of the corporation are as provided in its articles of incorporation. The corporation shall function as a veterans’ and patriotic organization as authorized by the laws of each State in which it is incorporated.
