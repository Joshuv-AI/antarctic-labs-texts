28 U.S.C. § 335 — Judicial Conference of the Court of International Trade
Citation: us:usc:28:335
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section335
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part I (Organization Of Courts); Chapter 15 (Conferences And Councils Of Judges). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The chief judge of the Court of International Trade is authorized to summon annually the judges of such court to a judicial conference, at a time and place that such chief judge designates, for the purpose of considering the business of such court and improvements in the administration of justice in such court.
(b)
The Court of International Trade shall provide by its rules for representation and active participation at such conference by members of the bar.
