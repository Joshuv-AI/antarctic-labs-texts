7 C.F.R. § 75.46 — When appeal refused or withdrawn.
Citation: us:cfr:7:75.46
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-75.46
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 75 (Provisions For Inspection And Certification Of Quality Of Agricultural And Vegetable Seeds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When an appeal is refused in accordance with § 75.29 or withdrawn in accordance with § 75.28, the applicant will be required to pay for the time used by the appeal inspector and other expenses incurred in connection with such appeal prior to its denial, dismissal, or withdrawal.
