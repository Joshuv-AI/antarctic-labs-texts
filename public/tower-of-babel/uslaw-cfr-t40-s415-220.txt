40 C.F.R. § 415.220 — Applicability; description of the titanium dioxide production subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:415.220
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-415.220
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 415 (Inorganic Chemicals Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to discharges to waters of the United States and introduction of pollutants into publicly owned treatment works resulting from the production of titanium dioxide by the sulfate process, the chloride process, and the simultaneous beneficiation-chlorination (chloride-ilmenite) process.
