43 U.S.C. § 1907 — National Grasslands; exemptions
Citation: us:usc:43:1907
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 37 (Public Rangelands Improvement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All National Grasslands are exempted from the provisions of this chapter.
