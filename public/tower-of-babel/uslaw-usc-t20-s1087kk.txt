20 U.S.C. § 1087kk — Amount of need
Citation: us:usc:20:1087kk
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1087kk
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 28 (Higher Education Resources And Student Assistance); Subchapter IV (Student Assistance); Part F (Need Analysis). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as otherwise provided therein, for award year 2024–2025 and each subsequent award year, the amount of need of any student for financial assistance under this subchapter (except subpart 1 or 2 of part A) is equal to—
(1)
the cost of attendance of such student, minus
(2)
the student aid index (as defined in section 1087mm of this title) for such student, minus
(3)
other financial assistance not received under this subchapter (as defined in section 1087vv(i) of this title).
