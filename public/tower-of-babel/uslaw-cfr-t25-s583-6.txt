25 C.F.R. § 583.6 — When will the Commission issue its final decision?
Citation: us:cfr:25:583.6
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-583.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 583 (Appeals From Approvals Or Disapprovals Of Management Contracts Or Amendments To Management Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Commission shall issue its final decision within 90 days after service of the appeal brief or within 90 days after the conclusion of briefing by the parties, whichever is later.
(b) The Commission shall notify the tribe and management contractor of its final decision and the reason(s) supporting it.
