40 C.F.R. § 60.5690 — Does this subpart apply to my municipal waste combustor?
Citation: us:cfr:40:60.5690
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.5690
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 60 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, if your municipal waste combustor meets both of the following criteria:
(a) Your municipal waste combustor is a new municipal waste combustor.
(b) Your municipal waste combustor has the capacity to combust greater than 250 tons per day of municipal solid waste.
