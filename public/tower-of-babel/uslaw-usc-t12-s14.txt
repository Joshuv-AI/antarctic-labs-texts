12 U.S.C. § 14 — Report of Comptroller
Citation: us:usc:12:14
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section14
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 1 (The Comptroller Of The Currency). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Comptroller of the Currency shall make an annual report to Congress. The report required under this section shall include the report required under section 57a(f)(7) 1 of title 15.
