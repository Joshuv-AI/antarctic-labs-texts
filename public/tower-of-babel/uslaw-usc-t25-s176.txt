25 U.S.C. § 176 — Survey of reservations
Citation: us:usc:25:176
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section176
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 5 (Protection Of Indians). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whenever it becomes necessary to survey any Indian or other reservations, or any lands, the same shall be surveyed under the direction and control of the Bureau of Land Management, and as nearly as may be in conformity to the rules and regulations under which other public lands are surveyed.
