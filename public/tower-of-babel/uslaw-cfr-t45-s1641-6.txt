45 C.F.R. § 1641.6 — Procedures for debarment.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1641.6
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1641.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1641 (Debarment, Suspension And Removal Of Recipient Auditors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before debarring an IPA, the OIG shall provide the IPA with a hearing in accordance with the procedures set out in §§ 1641.7 through 1641.9. Such hearing shall be held entirely by written submissions, except:
(a) Additional proceedings shall be held under § 1641.10 if the debarring official finds there is a genuine dispute of material fact; and/or
(b) A meeting may be held under § 1641.9(c).
