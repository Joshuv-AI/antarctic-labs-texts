28 C.F.R. § 548.11 — Definition.
Citation: us:cfr:28:548.11
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-548.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 548 (Religious Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of this subpart, the term “religious activity” includes religious diets, services, ceremonies, and meetings.
