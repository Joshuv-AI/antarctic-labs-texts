34 C.F.R. § 76.235 — The notification of grant award.
Citation: us:cfr:34:76.235
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-76.235
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 76 (State-Administered Formula Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) To make a grant to a State, the Secretary issues and sends to the State a notification of grant award.
(b) The notification of grant award tells the amount of the grant and provides other information about the grant.
