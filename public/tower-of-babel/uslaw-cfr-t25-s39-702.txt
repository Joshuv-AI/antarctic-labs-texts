25 C.F.R. § 39.702 — Can a school receive funds to transport residential students using commercial transportation?
Citation: us:cfr:25:39.702
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-39.702
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 39 (The Indian School Equalization Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A school transporting students by commercial bus, train, airplane, or other commercial modes of transportation will be funded at the cost of the commercial ticket for:
(a) The trip from home to school in the Fall;
(b) The round-trip return home at Christmas; and
(c) The return trip home at the end of the school year.
