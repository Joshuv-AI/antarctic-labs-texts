49 C.F.R. § 1241.15 — Railroad classification survey form.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1241.15
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1241.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1241 (Annual, Special, Or Periodic Reports—Carriers Subject To Part I Of The Interstate Commerce Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Commencing with the year ending December 31, 1982, and thereafter, until further order, all railroad companies not required to file an Annual Report (Form R-1) shall compute their adjusted revenues using the railroad revenue deflator formula. If there is a change in a carrier's classification the survey form shall be filed with the Office of Economics, Surface Transportation Board, Washington, DC, on or before March 31.
