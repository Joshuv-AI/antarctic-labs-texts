23 C.F.R. § 661.43 — Can other sources of funds be used to finance a queued project in advance of receipt of TTFBP funds?
Citation: us:cfr:23:661.43
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-661.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 661 (Tribal Transportation Facility Bridge Program (Ttfbp)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. A Tribe can use other sources of funds, including TTP construction funds, on a project that has been approved for funding and placed on the queue and then be reimbursed when TTFBP funds become available. If TTP construction funds are used for this purpose, the funds must be identified on an FHWA approved TTP TIP prior to their expenditure.
