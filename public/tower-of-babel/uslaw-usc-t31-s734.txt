31 U.S.C. § 734 — Assignments and details to Congress
Citation: us:usc:31:734
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section734
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 7 (Government Accountability Office); Subchapter III (Personnel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Comptroller General may assign or detail an officer or employee of the Government Accountability Office to full-time continuous duty with a committee of Congress for not more than one year.
