40 C.F.R. § 240.208-2 — -2 Recommended procedures: Design.
Citation: us:cfr:40:240.208-2
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-240.208-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 240 (Guidelines For The Thermal Processing Of Solid Wastes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Thermal processing facilities should be so designed as to allow for removal from the site of residue or other solids in a manner that protects the environment.
