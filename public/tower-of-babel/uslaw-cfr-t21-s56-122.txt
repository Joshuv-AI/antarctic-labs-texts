21 C.F.R. § 56.122 — Public disclosure of information regarding revocation.
Citation: us:cfr:21:56.122
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-56.122
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 56 (Institutional Review Boards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A determination that the Food and Drug Administration has disqualified an institution and the administrative record regarding that determination are disclosable to the public under part 20.
