35 U.S.C. § 298 — Advice of counsel
Citation: us:usc:35:298
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title35-section298
Context: United States Code, Title 35 (Patents); Part III (Patents And Protection Of Patent Rights); Chapter 29 (Remedies For Infringement Of Patent, And Other Actions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The failure of an infringer to obtain the advice of counsel with respect to any allegedly infringed patent, or the failure of the infringer to present such advice to the court or jury, may not be used to prove that the accused infringer willfully infringed the patent or that the infringer intended to induce infringement of the patent.
