31 C.F.R. § 23.47 — Hearings, decisions, post-termination proceedings.
Citation: us:cfr:31:23.47
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-23.47
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 23 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Age In Programs And Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance From The Department Of The Treasury). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Treasury procedural provisions for hearings, decisions, and post-termination proceedings applicable to Title VI of the Civil Rights Act of 1964 and its implementing regulations within Title 31 of the CFR shall apply to Treasury enforcement of these regulations.
