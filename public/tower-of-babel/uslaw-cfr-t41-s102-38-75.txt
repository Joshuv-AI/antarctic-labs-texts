41 C.F.R. § 102-38.75 — -38.75 Binding terms and conditions.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-38.75
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-38.75
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-38 (38—Sale Of Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Terms and conditions in the offer to sell are normally incorporated into the sales contract and are binding once a bid is accepted.
