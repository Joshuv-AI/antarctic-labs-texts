49 U.S.C. § 11328 — Restrictions on officers and directors
Citation: us:usc:49:11328
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section11328
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Rail); Chapter 113 (Finance); Subchapter II (Combinations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
A person may hold the position of officer or director of more than one rail carrier only when authorized by the Board. The Board may authorize a person to hold the position of officer or director of more than one of those carriers when public or private interests will not be adversely affected.
(b)
This section shall not apply to an individual holding the position of officer or director only of Class III rail carriers.
