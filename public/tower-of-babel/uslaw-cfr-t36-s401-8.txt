36 C.F.R. § 401.8 — Requirement for Commission approval.
Citation: us:cfr:36:401.8
Creator: American Battle Monuments Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-401.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 401 (Monuments And Memorials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No administrative agency of the United States Government may give assistance to build a memorial unless the plan for the memorial has been approved by the Commission. In deciding whether to approve a memorial request the Commission will apply the criteria set forth in § 401.9.
