20 U.S.C. § 7922 — Rule of construction regarding travel to and from school
Citation: us:usc:20:7922
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section7922
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 70 (Strengthening And Improvement Of Elementary And Secondary Schools); Subchapter VIII (General Provisions); Part F (Uniform Provisions); Subpart 2 (Other Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
Subject to subsection (b), nothing in this chapter shall authorize the Secretary to, or shall be construed to—
(1)
prohibit a child from traveling to and from school on foot or by car, bus, or bike when the parents of the child have given permission; or
(2)
expose parents to civil or criminal charges for allowing their child to responsibly and safely travel to and from school by a means the parents believe is age appropriate.
(b) No preemption of State or local laws
Notwithstanding subsection (a), nothing in this section shall be construed to preempt State or local laws.
