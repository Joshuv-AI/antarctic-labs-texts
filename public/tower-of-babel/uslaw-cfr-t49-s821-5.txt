49 C.F.R. § 821.5 — Procedural rules.
Citation: us:cfr:49:821.5
Creator: National Transportation Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-821.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 821 (Rules Of Practice In Air Safety Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In proceedings under subparts C, D, F, and I, for situations not covered by a specific Board rule, the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure will be followed to the extent practicable.
