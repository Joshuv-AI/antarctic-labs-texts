16 C.F.R. § 1116.10 — Restrictions on use of reports.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1116.10
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1116.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1116 (Reports Submitted Pursuant To Section 37 Of The Consumer Product Safety Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No member of the Commission, no officer or employee of the Commission, and no officer or employee of the Department of Justice may use information provided to the Commission under section 37 for any purpose other than to carry out the responsibilities of the Commission.
