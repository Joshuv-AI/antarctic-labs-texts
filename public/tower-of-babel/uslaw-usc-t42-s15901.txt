42 U.S.C. § 15901 — Definition of Secretary

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 149 (National Energy Policy And Programs); Subchapter III (Oil And Gas); Part A (Production Incentives). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this part, the term “Secretary” means the Secretary of the Interior.
