28 C.F.R. § 522.14 — Inmates serving civil contempt commitments.
Citation: us:cfr:28:522.14
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-522.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 522 (Admission To Institution). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We treat inmates serving civil contempt commitments in Bureau institutions the same as pretrial inmates. If an inmate is serving a civil contempt commitment and a concurrent criminal sentence, we treat the inmate the same as a person serving a criminal sentence.
