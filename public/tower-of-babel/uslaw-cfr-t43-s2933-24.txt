43 C.F.R. § 2933.24 — When can I get a refund of Recreation Use Permit fees?
Citation: us:cfr:43:2933.24
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2933.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2930 (Permits For Recreation On Public Lands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If we close the fee site for administrative or emergency reasons, we will refund the unused portion of your permit fee upon request.
