36 C.F.R. § 213.4 — Prior rules and regulations superseded.
Citation: us:cfr:36:213.4
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-213.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 213 (Administration Of Lands Under Title Iii Of The Bankhead-Jones Farm Tenant Act By The Forest Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in § 213.3, the rules and regulations heretofore issued for the land utilization projects are hereby superseded as to all such projects administered by the Forest Service, but not as to such project lands administered by other agencies.
