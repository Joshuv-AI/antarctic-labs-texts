14 C.F.R. § 161.207 — Comment by interested parties.
Citation: us:cfr:14:161.207
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-161.207
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 161 (Notice And Approval Of Airport Noise And Access Restrictions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each airport operator shall establish a public docket or similar method for receiving and considering comments, and shall make comments available for inspection by interested parties upon request. Comments must be retained as long as the restriction is in effect.
