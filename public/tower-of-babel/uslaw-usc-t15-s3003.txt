15 U.S.C. § 3003 — Acceptance of interstate off-track wager
Citation: us:usc:15:3003
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section3003
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 57 (Interstate Horseracing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No person may accept an interstate off-track wager except as provided in this chapter.
