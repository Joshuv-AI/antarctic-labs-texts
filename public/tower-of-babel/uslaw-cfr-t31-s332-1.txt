31 C.F.R. § 332.1 — Offering of bonds.
Citation: us:cfr:31:332.1
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-332.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 332 (Offering Of United States Savings Bonds, Series H). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary of the Treasury offered for sale to the people of the United States, Unites States Savings Bonds of Series H, hereinafter generally referred to as “Series H bonds” or “bonds”. This offer was terminated on December 31, 1979.
