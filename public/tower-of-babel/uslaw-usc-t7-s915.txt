7 U.S.C. § 915 — Purchase of financial and credit reports
Citation: us:usc:7:915
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section915
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 31 (Rural Electrification And Telephone Service); Subchapter I (Rural Electrification). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Agriculture is authorized to purchase such financial and credit reports as may be necessary to carry out the Secretary’s authorized work: Provided, That purchases under this authority shall not be made unless provision is made therefor in the applicable appropriation and the cost thereof is not in excess of limitations prescribed therein.
