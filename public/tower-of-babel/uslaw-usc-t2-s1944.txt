2 U.S.C. § 1944 — Wearing uniform on duty
Citation: us:usc:2:1944
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1944
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 29 (Capitol Police); Subchapter I (Organization And Administration); Part C (Uniform And Arms). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The officers, privates, and watchmen of the Capitol police shall, when on duty, wear the regulation uniform.
