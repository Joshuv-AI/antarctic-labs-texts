16 C.F.R. § 1310.1 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1310.1
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1310.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1310 (Ban Of Inclined Sleepers For Infants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this rule is to prohibit the sale, offer for sale, manufacture for sale, distribution in commerce, or importation into the United States, of any inclined sleepers for infants, as defined in part 1310.2 and as set forth in the Safe Sleep for Babies Act of 2021 (15. U.S.C. 2057d).
