18 C.F.R. § 1311.5 — What is TVA's obligation with respect to federal interagency coordination?
Citation: us:cfr:18:1311.5
Creator: Tennessee Valley Authority
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-1311.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 1311 (Intergovernmental Review Of Tennessee Valley Authority Federal Financial Assistance And Direct Federal Development Programs And Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

TVA, to the extent practicable, consults with and seeks advice from all other substantially affected federal departments and agencies in an effort to assure full coordination between such agencies and TVA regarding programs and activities covered under these regulations.
