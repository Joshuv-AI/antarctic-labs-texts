7 C.F.R. § 1150.154 — Influencing governmental action.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1150.154
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Milk), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1150.154
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1150 (Dairy Promotion Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No funds collected by the Board under this subpart shall in any manner be used for the purpose of influencing governmental policy or action, except to recommend to the Secretary amendments to this subpart.
