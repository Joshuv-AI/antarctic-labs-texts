25 C.F.R. § 226.4 — Form of payment.
Citation: us:cfr:25:226.4
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-226.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 226 (Leasing Of Osage Reservation Lands For Oil And Gas Mining). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Sums due under a lease contract and/or the regulations in this part shall be paid by cash or check made payable to the Bureau of Indian Affairs and delivered to the Osage Agency, Pawhuska, Oklahoma 74056. Such sums shall be a prior lien on all equipment and unsold oil on the leased premises.
