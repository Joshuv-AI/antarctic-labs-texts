11 U.S.C. § 1108 — Authorization to operate business
Citation: us:usc:11:1108
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section1108
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 11 (Reorganization); Subchapter I (Officers And Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Unless the court, on request of a party in interest and after notice and a hearing, orders otherwise, the trustee may operate the debtor’s business.
