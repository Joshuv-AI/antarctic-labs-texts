21 C.F.R. § 205.1 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:21:205.1
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-205.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 205 (Guidelines For State Licensing Of Wholesale Prescription Drug Distributors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to any person, partnership, corporation, or business firm in a State engaging in the wholesale distribution of human prescription drugs in interstate commerce.
