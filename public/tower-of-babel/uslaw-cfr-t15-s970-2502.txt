15 C.F.R. § 970.2502 — Post voyage report.
Citation: us:cfr:15:970.2502
Creator: National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-970.2502
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 970 (Deep Seabed Mining Regulations For Exploration Licenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within 30 days of the conclusion of each exploration voyage, the United States citizen engaging in the voyage shall submit to NOAA a report containing any environmental data or information obtained during that voyage.
