20 C.F.R. § 258.5 — Exhibits.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 258 (Hearings Before The Board Or Designated Examiners). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Copies of all exhibits admitted in evidence at any hearing held pursuant to the provisions of this part shall be furnished by the party offering the same to all other parties participating in the proceedings.
