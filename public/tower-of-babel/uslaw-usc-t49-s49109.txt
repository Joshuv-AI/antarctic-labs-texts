49 U.S.C. § 49109 — Nonstop flights
Citation: us:usc:49:49109
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section49109
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part D (Public Airports); Chapter 491 (Metropolitan Washington Airports). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An air carrier may not operate an aircraft nonstop in air transportation between Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport and another airport that is more than 1,250 statute miles away from Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport.
