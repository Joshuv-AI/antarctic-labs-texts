18 U.S.C. § 1837 — Applicability to conduct outside the United States
Citation: us:usc:18:1837
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1837
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 90 (Protection Of Trade Secrets). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter also applies to conduct occurring outside the United States if—
(1)
the offender is a natural person who is a citizen or permanent resident alien of the United States, or an organization organized under the laws of the United States or a State or political subdivision thereof; or
(2)
an act in furtherance of the offense was committed in the United States.
