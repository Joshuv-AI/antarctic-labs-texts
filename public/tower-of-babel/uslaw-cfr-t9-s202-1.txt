9 C.F.R. § 202.1 — Applicability of other rules.
Citation: us:cfr:9:202.1
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Fair Trade Practices Program), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-202.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 202 (Rules Of Practice Governing Proceedings Under The Packers And Stockyards Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Rules of Practice Governing Formal Adjudicatory Proceedings Instituted by the Secretary Under Various Statutes, 7 CFR part 1, subpart H, are applicable to all rate proceedings under Sections 304, 305, 306, 307 and 310 of the Packers and Stockyards Act, 1921, as amended, 7 U.S.C. 205, 206, 207, 208 and 211, except insofar as those Rules are in conflict with any provision herein.
