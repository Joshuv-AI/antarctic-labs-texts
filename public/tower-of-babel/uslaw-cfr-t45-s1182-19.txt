45 C.F.R. § 1182.19 — Restrictions that exist regarding the release of mailing lists.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1182.19
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1182.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1182 (Implementation Of The Privacy Act Of 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Institute may not sell or rent an individual's name and address unless such action specifically is authorized by law. This section shall not be construed to require the withholding of names and addresses otherwise permitted to be made public.
