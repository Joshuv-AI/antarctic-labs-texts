7 C.F.R. § 1146.202 — Rollover of fiscal year funds.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1146.202
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Milk), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1146.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1146 (Milk Donation Reimbursement Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If reimbursement monies remain after all fiscal year reimbursement claims have been approved and distributed, the remaining monies will be available to fund reimbursement claims in subsequent fiscal years.
