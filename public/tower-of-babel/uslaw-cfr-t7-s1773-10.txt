7 C.F.R. § 1773.10 — Access to audit documentation.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1773.10
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1773.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1773 (Policy On Audits Of Rus Awardees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pursuant to the terms of this part and the audit engagement letter, the auditor must make all audit documentation available to RUS, or its designated representative, upon request and must permit RUS, or its designated representative, to photocopy or image all audit documentation.
