15 C.F.R. § 231.109 — Members of the affiliated group.
Citation: us:cfr:15:231.109
Creator: National Institute Of Standards And Technology, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-231.109
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 231 (Clawbacks Of Chips Funding). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Members of the affiliated group includes any entity that is a member of the covered entity's “affiliated group,” as that term is defined under 26 U.S.C. 1504(a), without regard to 26 U.S.C. 1504(b)(3).
