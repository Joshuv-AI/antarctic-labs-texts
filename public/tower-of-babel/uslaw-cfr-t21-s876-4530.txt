21 C.F.R. § 876.4530 — Gastroenterology-urology fiberoptic retractor.
Citation: us:cfr:21:876.4530
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-876.4530
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 876 (Gastroenterology-Urology Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. A gastroenterology-urology fiberoptic retractor is a device that consists of a mechanical retractor with a fiberoptic light system that is used to illuminate deep surgical sites.
(b) Classification. Class I (general controls). The device is exempt from the premarket notification procedures in subpart E of part 807 of this chapter subject to the limitations in § 876.9.
