34 C.F.R. § 664.11 — What is a short-term seminar project?
Citation: us:cfr:34:664.11
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-664.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 664 (Fulbright-Hays Group Projects Abroad Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A short-term seminar project is—
(a) Designed to help integrate international studies into an institution's or school system's general curriculum; and
(b) Normally four to six weeks in length and focuses on a particular aspect of area study, such as, for example, the culture of the area or a portion of the culture.
