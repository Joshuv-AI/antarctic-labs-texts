48 C.F.R. § 949.101 — 949.101 Authorities and responsibilities.
Citation: us:cfr:48:949.101
Creator: Chapter 9—Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-949.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 949 (Termination Of Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Senior Procurement Executive shall be notified prior to taking any action to terminate (a) contracts for the operation of Government-owned facilities, (b) any prime contract or subcontract in excess of $10 million, and (c) any contract the termination of which is likely to provoke unusual interest.
