48 C.F.R. § 246.504 — 246.504 Certificate of conformance.
Citation: us:cfr:48:246.504
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-246.504
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 246 (Quality Assurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before authorizing a certificate of conformance for aviation or ship critical safety items, obtain the concurrence of the head of the design control activity (see 209.270).
