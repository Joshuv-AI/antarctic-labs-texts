25 C.F.R. § 20.401 — What is included under Services to Children, Elderly, and Families?
Citation: us:cfr:25:20.401
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-20.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 20 (Financial Assistance And Social Services Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Services to Children, Elderly, and Families include, but are not limited to, the following:
(a) Assistance in solving problems related to family functioning and interpersonal relationships;
(b) Referral to the appropriate resource for problems related to illness, physical or mental handicaps, drug abuse, alcoholism, and violation of the law; and
(c) Protective services.
In addition, economic opportunity and money management may also be provided.
