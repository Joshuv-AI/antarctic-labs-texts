10 U.S.C. § 8292 — Navy cross
Citation: us:usc:10:8292
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8292
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 837 (Decorations And Awards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may award a Navy cross of appropriate design, with ribbons and appurtenances, to a person who, while serving in any capacity with the Navy or Marine Corps, distinguishes himself by extraordinary heroism not justifying the award of a medal of honor—
(1)
while engaged in an action against an enemy of the United States;
(2)
while engaged in military operations involving conflict with an opposing foreign force; or
(3)
while serving with friendly foreign forces engaged in an armed conflict against an opposing armed force in which the United States is not a belligerent party.
