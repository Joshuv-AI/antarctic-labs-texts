50 C.F.R. § 80.38 — May a State fish and wildlife agency certify a license when an entity other than the agency offers a discount on a license or offers a free license?
Citation: us:cfr:50:80.38
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-80.38
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 80 (Administrative Requirements, Pittman-Robertson Wildlife Restoration And Dingell-Johnson Sport Fish Restoration Acts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A State fish and wildlife agency may certify a license when an entity other than the agency offers a license that costs less than the regulated price only if:
(a) The license is issued to the individual according to the requirements at § 80.33;
(b) The amount received by the agency meets all other requirements in this subpart; and
(c) The license meets any other conditions required by the agency.
