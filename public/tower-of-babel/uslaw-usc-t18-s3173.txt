18 U.S.C. § 3173 — Sixth amendment rights
Citation: us:usc:18:3173
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section3173
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part II (Criminal Procedure); Chapter 208 (Speedy Trial). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No provision of this chapter shall be interpreted as a bar to any claim of denial of speedy trial as required by amendment VI of the Constitution.
