Del Pezzo surfaces with four log terminal singularities
Grigory Belousov, DongSeon Hwang

Abstract
. We classify del Pezzo surfaces of Picard number one with four log terminal singular points.



Dedicated to Yurii (Gennadievich) Prokhorov

on the occasion of his 60th birthday.

1.
Introduction

A
log del Pezzo surface
is a projective surface X with only
log terminal singularities such that the anti-canonical divisor
-K_{X}
is ample. For surfaces, log terminal singularities are exactly quotient singularities (
[
11
, Corollary 1.9]
), which are completely classified (
[
4
]
).

Log del Pezzo surfaces naturally appear in the log minimal model program
(see, e.g.,
[
13
]
). The most interesting class of log del Pezzo surfaces
is the class of such surfaces of Picard number one. The systematic study of log del Pezzo surfaces of Picard number one was initiated by Miyanishi and Zhang (
[
22
]
). Following their approach Kojima classified such surfaces with one singular point (
[
12
]
). See also
[
23
]
and
[
6
]
for further results following this approach.

Log del Pezzo surfaces of Picard number one are classified (
[
16
]
) following the approach of
[
14
]
in terms of blow-ups of rational surfaces. See
[
19
]
and
[
20
]
for a recent different approach for the classification. Log del Pezzo surfaces of Picard number one have at most
4
singular points by (
[
2
]
or
[
3
]
).

In this paper we classify log del Pezzo surfaces of Picard number one with
4
singular points, using
\mathbb{P}^{1}
-fibration structures and the minimal model program, based on Miyanishi and Zhang’s approach. In particular, we can list the types of singular points of such surfaces.

2.
Preliminaries

2.1.
Some useful theorems in algebraic geometry

Theorem 2.1

(Hurwitz, see, e.g.,
[
7
]
, Corollary 2.4, Ch. 4)
.

Let
\phi\colon X\rightarrow Y
be a finite morphism of curves. Then

2g(X)-2=n(2g(Y)-2)+\deg R,

where
n=\deg\phi
,
g(X),g(Y)
are genuses of curves,
R
is ramification divisor.

Theorem 2.2

(Hodge, see, e.g.,
[
7
]
, Theorem 1.9, Remark 1.9.1, Ch. 5)
.

The intersection form on a surface
X
has the signature
(1,\rho(X)-1)
, where
\rho(X)
is the Picard number of
X
.

Theorem 2.3

(see
[
14
, Corollary 9.2]
)
.

Let
X
be a rational surface with log terminal
singularities. If
\rho(X)=1
, then

\sum_{P\in X}\frac{m_{P}-1}{m_{P}}\leq 3,

[formula]

where

m_{P}
is the order of the local fundamental group
\pi_{1}(U_{P}-\{P\})
, where

U_{P}
is a sufficiently small neighborhood of
P
.

2.2.

has exactly

.

(
[
8

)

Let

\sum_{P\in X}\frac{m_{P}-1}{m_{P}}<3,
