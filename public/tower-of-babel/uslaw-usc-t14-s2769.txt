14 U.S.C. § 2769 — Remission of indebtedness
Citation: us:usc:14:2769
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2769
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 27 (Pay, Allowances, Awards, And Other Rights And Benefits); Subchapter III (Payments). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may have remitted or cancelled any part of an individual’s indebtedness to the United States or any instrumentality of the United States if—
(1)
the indebtedness was incurred while the individual served as a member of the Coast Guard, whether as a regular or a reserve in active status; and
(2)
the Secretary determines that remitting or cancelling the indebtedness is in the best interest of the United States.
