10 U.S.C. § 2131 — Reference to chapter 1606
Citation: us:usc:10:2131
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2131
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training And Education); Chapter 106 (Educational Assistance For Members Of The Selected Reserve). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Provisions of law relating to educational assistance for members of the Selected Reserve under the Montgomery GI Bill program are set forth in chapter 1606 of this title (beginning with section 16131).
