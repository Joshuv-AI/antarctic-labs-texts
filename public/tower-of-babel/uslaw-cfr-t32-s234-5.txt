32 C.F.R. § 234.5 — Compliance with official signs.
Citation: us:cfr:32:234.5
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-234.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 234 (Conduct On The Pentagon Reservation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Persons on the Pentagon Reservation shall at all times comply with official signs of a prohibitory, regulatory, or directory nature.
