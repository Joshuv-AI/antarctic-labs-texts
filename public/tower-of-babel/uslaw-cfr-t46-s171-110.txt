46 C.F.R. § 171.110 — Specific applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:46:171.110
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-171.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 171 (Special Rules Pertaining To Vessels Carrying Passengers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Sections 171.111, 171.112, and 171.113 apply to each vessel of 100 gross tons or more.
(b) Section 171.114 applies to each vessel under 100 gross tons.
