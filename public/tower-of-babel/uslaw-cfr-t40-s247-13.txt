40 C.F.R. § 247.13 — Transportation products.
Citation: us:cfr:40:247.13
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-247.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 247 (Comprehensive Procurement Guideline For Products Containing Recovered Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Traffic barricades and traffic cones used in controlling or restricting vehicular traffic.
(b) Parking stops made from concrete or containing recovered plastic or rubber.
(c) Channelizers containing recovered plastic or rubber.
(d) Delineators containing recovered plastic, rubber, or steel.
(e) Flexible delineators containing recovered plastic.
