2 C.F.R. § 180.300 — What must I do before I enter into a covered transaction with another person at the next lower tier?
Citation: us:cfr:2:180.300
Creator: Office Of Management And Budget Government-Wide Guidance For Federal Financial Assistance
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-180.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 180 (Omb Guidelines To Agencies On Government-Wide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When you enter into a covered transaction with another person at the next lower tier, you must verify that the person with whom you intend to do business is not excluded or disqualified. You do this by:
(a) Checking SAM.gov Exclusions; or
(b) Collecting a certification from that person; or
(c) Adding a clause or condition to the covered transaction with that person.
