28 C.F.R. § 80.11 — Effect of FCPA Opinion.
Citation: us:cfr:28:80.11
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-80.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 80 (Foreign Corrupt Practices Act Opinion Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as specified in § 80.10, an FCPA Opinion will not bind or obligate any agency other than the Department of Justice. It will not affect the requesting issuer's or domestic concern's obligations to any other agency, or under any statutory or regulatory provision other than those specifically cited in the particular FCPA Opinion.
