5 C.F.R. § 630.203 — Pay periods other than biweekly.
Citation: us:cfr:5:630.203
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-630.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 630 (Absence And Leave). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee paid on other than a biweekly pay period basis earns leave on a pro rata basis for a full pay period.
