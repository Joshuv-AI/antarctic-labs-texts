28 C.F.R. § 700.19 — Preservation of records.
Citation: us:cfr:28:700.19
Creator: Office Of Independent Counsel
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-700.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 700 (Production Or Disclosure Of Material Or Information Of The Office Of Independent Counsel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Office shall preserve all correspondence relating to the requests it receives under this subpart, and all records processed pursuant to such requests, until such time as the destruction of such correspondence and records is authorized pursuant to title 44 of the U.S. Code. Under no circumstances shall records be destroyed while they are the subject of a pending request for access, appeal, or lawsuit under the Act.
