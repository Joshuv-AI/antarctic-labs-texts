7 U.S.C. § 4305 — Finding and issuance of orders
Citation: us:usc:7:4305
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section4305
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 74 (Floral Research And Consumer Information). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

After notice and opportunity for hearing as provided in section 4304 of this title, the Secretary shall issue an order if the Secretary finds, and sets forth in such order, upon the evidence introduced at such hearing, that the issuance of such order and all the terms and conditions thereof will tend to effectuate the declared policy of this chapter.
