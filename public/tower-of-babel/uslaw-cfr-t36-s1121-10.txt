36 C.F.R. § 1121.10 — Disclosure of record to a person other than the individual to whom the record pertains.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1121.10
Creator: Architectural And Transportation Barriers Compliance Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1121.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1121 (Privacy Act Implementation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board will not disclose a record to any individual or agency other than the individual to whom the record pertains, except to an authorized representative, unless the disclosure has been listed as a “routine use” in the Board's notices of its systems of records, or falls within one of the special disclosure situations listed in the Privacy Act of 1974 (5 U.S.C. 552a(b)).
