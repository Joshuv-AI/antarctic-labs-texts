41 C.F.R. § 102-118.230 — -118.230 TSP challenges a statement of difference.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-118.230
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-118.230
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-118 (118—Transportation Payment And Audit). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A TSP may appeal an agency's denial of its challenge to the statement of difference. However, the appeal must be handled at a higher level within the agency.
