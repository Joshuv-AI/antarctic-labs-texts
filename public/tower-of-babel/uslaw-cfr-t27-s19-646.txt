27 C.F.R. § 19.646 — Construction and equipment requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:27:19.646
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-19.646
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 19 (Distilled Spirits Plants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The proprietor of a vinegar plant must construct and equip the plant to ensure that:
(a) The distilled spirits vapors that are separated by the vaporizing process from the mash are condensed only by introducing them into the water or other liquid used in making the vinegar; and
(b) The distilled spirits produced are accurately accounted for and are secure from unlawful removal from the premises or from unauthorized use.
