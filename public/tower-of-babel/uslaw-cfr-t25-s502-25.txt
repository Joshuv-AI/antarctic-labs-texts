25 C.F.R. § 502.25 — Gaming Enterprise.
Citation: us:cfr:25:502.25
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-502.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 502 (Definitions Of This Chapter). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Gaming Enterprise means the entities through which Tribe conducts, regulates, and secures gaming on Indian lands within such tribe's jurisdiction pursuant to the Indian Gaming Regulatory Act.
