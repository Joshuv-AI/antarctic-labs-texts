12 C.F.R. § 608.813 — Contracting for collection services.
Citation: us:cfr:12:608.813
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-608.813
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 608 (Collection Of Claims Owed The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Chairman, or designee of the Chairman, may contract for collection services in accordance with 31 U.S.C. 3718 and 4 CFR 102.6 to recover debts.
