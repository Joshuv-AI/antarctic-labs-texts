29 C.F.R. § 18.84 — Official notice.
Citation: us:cfr:29:18.84
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-18.84
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 18 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure For Administrative Hearings Before The Office Of Administrative Law Judges). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

On motion of a party or on the judge's own, official notice may be taken of any adjudicative fact or other matter subject to judicial notice. The parties must be given an adequate opportunity to show the contrary of the matter noticed.
