42 U.S.C. § 7385s–14 — Payment of compensation and benefits from compensation fund
Citation: us:usc:42:7385s–14
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section7385s–14
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 84 (Department Of Energy); Subchapter XVI (Energy Employees Occupational Illness Compensation Program); Part E (Contractor Employee Compensation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The compensation and benefits provided under this subchapter, when authorized or approved by the President, shall be paid from the compensation fund established under section 7384e of this title.
