41 C.F.R. § 50-205.4 — -205.4 Plan of cooperation.
Citation: us:cfr:41:50-205.4
Creator: Chapter 50—Public Contracts, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-50-205.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 50-205 (205—Enforcement Of Safety And Health Standards By State Officers And Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each agreement under this part shall incorporate a plan of cooperation between the Department of Labor and the State agency. The plan shall include the operative details of the cooperation contemplated in the making of safety and health inspections. The plan shall include a statement of the location of the State offices designated to make inspections and those of the Department of Labor designated to cooperate with such State offices.
