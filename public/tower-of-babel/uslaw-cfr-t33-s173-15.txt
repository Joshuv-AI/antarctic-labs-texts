33 C.F.R. § 173.15 — Vessel number required.
Citation: us:cfr:33:173.15
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-173.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 173 (Vessel Numbering And Casualty And Accident Reporting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except as provided in § 173.17, no person may use a vessel to which this part applies unless:
(1) It has a number issued on a certificate of number by the issuing authority in the State of principal operation; and
(2) The number is displayed as described in § 173.27.
(b) This section does not apply to a vessel for which a valid temporary certificate has been issued to its owner by the issuing authority in the State of principal operation.
