10 U.S.C. § 15107 — Retirement: retirement for years of service; selective early retirement
Citation: us:usc:10:15107
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section15107
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Promotion And Retention Of Officers On The Reserve Active-Status List); Chapter 1413 (Alternative Promotion Authority For Officers In Designated Competitive Categories). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Retirement for Years of Service.—
Sections 14507 through 14515 of this title shall apply to the retirement of officers in competitive categories of officers designated for purposes of this chapter.
(b) Selective Early Retirement.—
Section 14101(b) of this title shall apply to the retirement of officers described in subsection (a).
