7 C.F.R. § 4280.47 — Non-selection of applications.
Citation: us:cfr:7:4280.47
Creator: Rural Business-Cooperative Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-4280.47
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 4280 (Loans And Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Provided the application requirements have not changed, an application not selected will be reconsidered in 3 subsequent funding competitions for a total of four funding competitions. If an application is withdrawn, it can be resubmitted and will be evaluated as a new application.
