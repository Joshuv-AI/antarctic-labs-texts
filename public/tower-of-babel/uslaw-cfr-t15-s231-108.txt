15 C.F.R. § 231.108 — Material expansion.
Citation: us:cfr:15:231.108
Creator: National Institute Of Standards And Technology, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-231.108
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 231 (Clawbacks Of Chips Funding). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Material expansion means:
(1) with respect to an existing facility, the increase of the semiconductor manufacturing capacity of that facility by more than five percent of the capacity memorialized in the required agreement due to the addition of a cleanroom, production line or other physical space, or a series of such additions; or
(2) any construction of a new facility for semiconductor manufacturing.
