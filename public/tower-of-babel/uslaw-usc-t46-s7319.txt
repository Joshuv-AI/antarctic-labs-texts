46 U.S.C. § 7319 — Records of merchant mariners’ documents
Citation: us:usc:46:7319
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section7319
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part E (Merchant Seamen Licenses, Certificates, And Documents); Chapter 73 (Merchant Mariners’ Documents). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall maintain records on each merchant mariner’s document issued, including the name and address of the individual to whom issued and the next of kin of the individual.
