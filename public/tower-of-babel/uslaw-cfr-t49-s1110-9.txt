49 C.F.R. § 1110.9 — Petition for waiver.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1110.9
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1110.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1110 (Procedures Governing Informal Rulemaking Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person may petition the Board for a permanent or temporary waiver of any rule. Petitions should be filed with the Chief of Case Administration, Office of Chief Counsel, Surface Transportation Board, Washington, DC 20423-0001, and should identify the rule involved.
