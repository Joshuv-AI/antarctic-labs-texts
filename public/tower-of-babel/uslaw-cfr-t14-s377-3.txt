14 C.F.R. § 377.3 — Authorizations not covered by 5 U.S.C. 558(c).
Citation: us:cfr:14:377.3
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-377.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 377 (Continuance Of Expired Authorizations By Operation Of Law Pending Final Determination Of Applications For Renewal Thereof). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department hereby determines that the following authorizations are not licenses “with reference to an activity of a continuing nature” within the meaning of 5 U.S.C. 558(c):
(a) Authorizations granted for a specified period of 180 days or less; and
(b) Authorizations, other than those granted under 49 U.S.C. 41102, that by their terms are subject to termination at an uncertain date upon the happening of an event, including fulfillment of a condition subsequent or occurrence of a contingency.
