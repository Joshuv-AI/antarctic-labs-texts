29 U.S.C. § 415 — Information to members of provisions of chapter
Citation: us:usc:29:415
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section415
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 11 (Labor-Management Reporting And Disclosure Procedure); Subchapter II (Bill Of Rights Of Members Of Labor Organizations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Every labor organization shall inform its members concerning the provisions of this chapter.
