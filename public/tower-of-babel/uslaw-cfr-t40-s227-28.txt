40 C.F.R. § 227.28 — Release zone.
Citation: us:cfr:40:227.28
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-227.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 227 (Criteria For The Evaluation Of Permit Applications For Ocean Dumping Of Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The release zone is the area swept out by the locus of points constantly 100 meters from the perimeter of the conveyance engaged in dumping activities, beginning at the first moment in which dumping is scheduled to occur and ending at the last moment in which dumping is scheduled to occur. No release zone shall exceed the total surface area of the dumpsite.
