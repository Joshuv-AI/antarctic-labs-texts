16 U.S.C. § 4223 — Prohibited acts
Citation: us:usc:16:4223
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section4223
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 62 (African Elephant Conservation); Subchapter II (Moratoria And Prohibited Acts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as provided in section 4222(e) of this title, it is unlawful for any person—
(1)
to import raw ivory from any country other than an ivory producing country;
(2)
to export raw ivory from the United States;
(3)
to import raw or worked ivory that was exported from an ivory producing country in violation of that country’s laws or of the CITES Ivory Control System;
(4)
to import worked ivory, other than personal effects, from any country unless that country has certified that such ivory was derived from legal sources; or
(5)
to import raw or worked ivory from a country for which a moratorium is in effect under section 4222 of this title.
