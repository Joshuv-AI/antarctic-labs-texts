48 C.F.R. § 1425.701 — 1425.701 Restriction on acquisition of supplies or services from prohibited sources.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1425.701
Creator: Chapter 14—Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1425.701
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1425 (Foreign Acquisition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The AS/PMB is authorized to request permission from the Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) for DOI use of supplies and services from the sources described in FAR 25.701. Requests shall be prepared by the CO and submitted through the HCA to the Director, PAM.
