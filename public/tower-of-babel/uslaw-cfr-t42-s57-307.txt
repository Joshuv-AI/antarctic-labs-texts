42 C.F.R. § 57.307 — Maximum amount of nursing student loans.
Citation: us:cfr:42:57.307
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-57.307
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 57 (Grants For Construction Of Teaching Facilities, Educational Improvements, Scholarships And Student Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The total of the nursing student loans made from the fund to any student for an academic year may not exceed $2,500, except that for each of the final 2 academic years of the program, the total must not exceed $4,000. The maximum amount loaned during a 12-month period to any student enrolled in a school which provides a course of study longer than the 9-month academic year may be proportionately increased. The total of all nursing student loans to any student must not exceed $13,000.
