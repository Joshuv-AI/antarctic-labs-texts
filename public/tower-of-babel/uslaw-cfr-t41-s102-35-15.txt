41 C.F.R. § 102-35.15 — -35.15 Deviations.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-35.15
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-35.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-35 (35—Disposition Of Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All provisions in parts 102-36 through 102-42 of this subchapter are subject to deviation with adequate justification except for those mandated by statute, as described in §§ 102-39.20(a)(2) and 102-39.30 of this subchapter. See §§ 102-2.60 through 102-2.110 of this chapter to request a deviation.
