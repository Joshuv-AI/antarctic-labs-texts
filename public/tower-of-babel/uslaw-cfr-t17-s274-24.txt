17 C.F.R. § 274.24 — Form 24F-2, annual filing of securities sold pursuant to registration of certain investment company securities and registered non-variable annuities.
Citation: us:cfr:17:274.24
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-274.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 274 (Forms Prescribed Under The Investment Company Act Of 1940). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Form 24F-2 shall be used as the annual report filed by face amount certificate companies, open-end management companies, unit investment trusts, and registered non-variable annuities pursuant to §§ 230.456, 230.457, or 270.24f-2 of this chapter for reporting securities sold during the fiscal year.
