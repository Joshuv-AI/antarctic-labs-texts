10 C.F.R. § 1048.2 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1048.2
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1048.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1048 (Trespassing On Strategic Petroleum Reserve Facilities And Other Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part apply to entry into or upon all SPR storage or related facilities, and real property subject to DOE jurisdiction or administration, or in its custody under part B, title I of the EPCA, which have been posted with a notice of the prohibitions and penalties contained in this part.
