24 C.F.R. § 573.4 — Loan term.
Citation: us:cfr:24:573.4
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Community Planning And Development, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-573.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 573 (Loan Guarantee Recovery Fund). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term of the loan to be guaranteed by HUD under this part may not exceed 20 years.
