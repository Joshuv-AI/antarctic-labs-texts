Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1101 (Jewish War Veterans Of The United States Of America, Incorporated). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation has only the powers provided in its bylaws and articles of incorporation filed in each State in which it is incorporated.
