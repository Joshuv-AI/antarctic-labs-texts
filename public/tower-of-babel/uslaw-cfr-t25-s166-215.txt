25 C.F.R. § 166.215 — How can I find Indian land available for grazing?
Citation: us:cfr:25:166.215
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-166.215
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 166 (Grazing Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may contact a local BIA office or tribal office to determine what Indian land may be available for grazing permits.
