29 C.F.R. § 515.6 — Audits.
Citation: us:cfr:29:515.6
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-515.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 515 (Utilization Of State Agencies For Investigations And Inspections). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The accounting records and the supporting data pertaining to expenditures for investigations and inspections under the Acts shall be subject to audit by the Division and the Secretary of Labor, annually, or so often as the Administrator and the Secretary of Labor, may require.
