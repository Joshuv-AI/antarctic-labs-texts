46 C.F.R. § 164.201-1 — -1 Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:46:164.201-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-164.201-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 164 (Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes requirements for approval of fire-resisting materials for high-speed craft as required by the International Code of Safety for High Speed Craft (HSC Code).
