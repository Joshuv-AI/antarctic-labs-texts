47 C.F.R. § 101.215 — Posting of station authorization and transmitter identification cards, plates, or signs.
Citation: us:cfr:47:101.215
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-101.215
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 101 (Fixed Microwave Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each licensee shall post at the station the name, address and telephone number of the custodian of the station license or other authorization if such license or authorization is not maintained at the station.
(b) The requirements in paragraph (a) of this section do not apply to remote stations using frequencies listed in § 101.147(b).
