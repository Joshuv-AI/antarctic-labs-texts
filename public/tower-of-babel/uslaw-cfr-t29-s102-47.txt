29 C.F.R. § 102.47 — Filing of motion after transfer of case to Board.
Citation: us:cfr:29:102.47
Creator: National Labor Relations Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-102.47
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 102 (Rules And Regulations, Series 8). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All motions filed after the case has been transferred to the Board pursuant to § 102.45 must be filed with the Board in Washington, DC, and served upon the other parties. Such motions must be printed or otherwise legibly duplicated.
