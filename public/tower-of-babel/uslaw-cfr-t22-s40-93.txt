22 C.F.R. § 40.93 — Aliens unlawfully present after previous immigration violation.
Citation: us:cfr:22:40.93
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-40.93
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 40 (Regulations Pertaining To Both Nonimmigrants And Immigrants Under The Immigration And Nationality Act, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An alien described in INA 212(a)(9)(C)(i) is permanently ineligible for a visa unless the Secretary of Homeland Security consents to the alien's application for readmission not less than 10 years following the alien's last departure from the United States. Such application for readmission shall be made prior to the alien's reembarkation at a place outside the United States.
