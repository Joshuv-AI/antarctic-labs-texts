48 C.F.R. § 3046.791-3 — 3046.791-3 Warranties on Government-furnished property (USCG).
Citation: us:cfr:48:3046.791-3
Creator: Chapter 30—Department Of Homeland Security, Homeland Security Acquisition Regulation (Hsar)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-3046.791-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 3046 (Quality Assurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A contractor for a major systems acquisition shall not be required to provide the warranties specified in (HSAR) 48 CFR 3046.790-1 on any property furnished to that contractor by the Government except for:
(a) Defects in installation; and
(b) Installation or modification in such a manner that invalidates a warranty provided by the manufacturer of the property.
