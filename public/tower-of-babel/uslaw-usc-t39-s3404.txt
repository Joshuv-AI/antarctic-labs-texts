39 U.S.C. § 3404 — Unsealed letters sent by blind or physically handicapped persons
Citation: us:usc:39:3404
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title39-section3404
Context: United States Code, Title 39 (Postal Service); Part IV (Mail Matter); Chapter 34 (Armed Forces And Free Postage). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Unsealed letters sent by a blind person or a person having a physical impairment, as described in section 3403(a)(1) of this title, in raised characters or sightsaving type, or in the form of sound recordings, may be mailed free of postage.
