49 C.F.R. § 800.6 — Availability of information and materials.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 800 (Administrative Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Part 801 of this chapter provides detailed information concerning the availability of Board documents and records. That part also provides a fee schedule and information concerning inspection and copying.
