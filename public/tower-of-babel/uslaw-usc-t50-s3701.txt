50 U.S.C. § 3701 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:50:3701
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section3701
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 48 (Department Of Defense Cooperative Threat Reduction). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter:
(1)
The term “congressional defense committees” has the meaning given that term in section 101(a)(16) of title 10.
(2)
The term “Cooperative Threat Reduction funds” means funds appropriated pursuant to an authorization of appropriations for the Program, or otherwise made available to the Program.
(3)
The term “Program” means the Cooperative Threat Reduction Program of the Department of Defense established under section 3711 of this title.
