41 C.F.R. § 302-17.64 — -17.64 Failure to provide required tax information to the agency.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-17.64
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-17.64
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-17 (17—Taxes On Relocation Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The WTA is an advance on an employee's income tax expenses, thus if an employee does not provide the required tax information and file the RITA claim in a timely manner, the agency will require the employee to repay the entire amount of the withholding and WTA (if any) that the agency has paid.
