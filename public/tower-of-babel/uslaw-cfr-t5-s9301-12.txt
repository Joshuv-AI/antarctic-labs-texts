5 C.F.R. § 9301.12 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:5:9301.12
Creator: Special Inspector General For Afghanistan Reconstruction
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-9301.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 9301 (Disclosure Of Records And Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this subpart is to provide certain safeguards for an individual against the invasion of his or her personal privacy by SIGAR. This subpart is promulgated pursuant to the requirements applicable to all federal agencies contained in 5 U.S.C. 552a(f).
