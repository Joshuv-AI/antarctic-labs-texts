46 U.S.C. § 53515 — Records and reports
Citation: us:usc:46:53515
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section53515
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part C (Financial Assistance Programs); Chapter 535 (Capital Construction Funds). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A person maintaining a fund under this chapter shall keep records and make reports as required by the Secretary or the Secretary of the Treasury.
