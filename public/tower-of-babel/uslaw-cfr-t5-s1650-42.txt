5 C.F.R. § 1650.42 — How to obtain a financial hardship withdrawal.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1650.42
Creator: Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1650.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1650 (Methods Of Withdrawing Funds From The Thrift Savings Plan). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) To request a financial hardship withdrawal, a participant must initiate a request in the form and manner prescribed by the TSP record keeper.
(b) There is no limit on the number of financial hardship withdrawals a participant can make; however, the TSP record keeper will not accept a financial hardship withdrawal request for a period of six months after a financial hardship disbursement is made.
