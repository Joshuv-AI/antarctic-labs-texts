48 C.F.R. § 9903.302-3 — 9903.302-3 Illustrations of changes which meet the definition of “change to a cost accounting practice.”

Creator: Chapter 99—Cost Accounting Standards Board, Office Of Federal Procurement Policy, Office Of Management And Budget
Year: 2026

(a) The method or technique used for measuring costs has been changed.
(b) The method or technique used for assignment of cost to cost accounting periods has been changed.
(c) The method or technique used for allocating costs has been changed.
