20 U.S.C. § 75a — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:20:75a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section75a
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 3 (Smithsonian Institution, National Museums And Art Galleries); Subchapter III (National Portrait Gallery). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purposes of this subchapter—
(a)
The term “Board” means the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution.
(b)
The term “Commission” means the National Portrait Gallery Commission as provided for in this subchapter.
(c)
The term “Gallery” means the National Portrait Gallery established by this subchapter.
(d)
The term “gift” includes a gift, bequest, or devise, whether outright or in trust, and any legal instrument by which the gift is effected.
(e)
The term “portraiture” includes portraits and reproductions thereof made by any means or process, whether invented or developed heretofore or hereafter.
