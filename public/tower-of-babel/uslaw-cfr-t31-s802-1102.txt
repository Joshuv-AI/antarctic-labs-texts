31 C.F.R. § 802.1102 — Timing of payment.
Citation: us:cfr:31:802.1102
Creator: Office Of Investment Security, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-802.1102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 802 (Regulations Pertaining To Certain Transactions By Foreign Persons Involving Real Estate In The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subject to §§ 802.1106 through 802.1108, the Staff Chairperson shall not accept a formal written notice under § 802.503(a) until payment of any fee required under this subpart is received by the Department of the Treasury in the manner specified on the Committee's section of the Department of the Treasury website.
