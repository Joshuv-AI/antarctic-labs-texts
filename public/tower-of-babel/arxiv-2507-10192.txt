Abstract
. We construct a map of operads from an E 2 E_{2} -operad to the condensation of the operad for multiplicative hyperoperads. We deduce from it the existence of an E 2 E_{2} -action on the homotopy limit of the underlying functor of a multiplicative hyperoperad. This result is the higher dimensional analogue of a result due to Batanin and Berger implying Deligne’s conjecture.



1.
Introduction

In order to define weak
n
-categories, Baez and Dolan
[
1
]
introduced a notion of
plus construction
. This construction creates a new (symmetric coloured) operad
P^{+}
from an operad

. In
[
5
]
, we studied the geometric aspects of the iterations of this construction. More specifically, we focused on the operad
I^{+++}
obtained by iterating this construction three times, starting from the initial one-coloured operad
I
. We called

the algebras for this operad. The explicit definition of a (multiplicative) hyperoperad will be given in Subsection

and the description of the operad
\mathcal{H}
for multiplicative hyperoperads will be given in Subsection
3.1
. We proved
[
5
, Corollary 6.13]
that for a multiplicative hyperoperad
\mathcal{A}
satisfying some reduceness conditions, there is a triple delooping

\Omega^{3}\mathrm{Map}_{\mathrm{HOp}}(\zeta,u^{*}(\mathcal{A}))\sim\operatornamewithlimits{holim}_{\Omega_{p}}\mathcal{A}^{\bullet},

where
\Omega
is the loop space functor,
\mathrm{Map}_{\mathrm{HOp}}(-,-)
is the homotopy mapping space in the category of hyperoperads,
\zeta
is the terminal hyperoperad,

is the obvious forgetful functor and
\mathcal{A}^{\bullet}
is the underlying functor of
\mathcal{A}
(see Subsection

). This result is the higher dimensional analogue of the double delooping proved independently by Turchin
[
11
]
and Dwyer and Hess
[
6
]
.

For
m\geq 0
,
m
-fold loop spaces are related to
E_{m}
-operads through May’s recognition principle
[
8
]
. Therefore, a natural question to ask is whether there is in general an
E_{3}
-action on the homotopy limit of the underlying functor of a multiplicative hyperoperad. This is what we investigate in this paper. We will follow the techniques developed by Batanin and Berger in
[
2
]
. In this paper, the authors introduced the notion of
condensation
of a (symmetric) coloured operad to a one-coloured operad. They also introduced a coloured operad
\mathcal{L}
called
lattice path operad
together with a filtration of it. They computed the condensation of the
m
-th stage filtration
\mathcal{L}_{m}
and deduced from it the existence of an
E_{m}
-action on the totalization of the underlying cosimplicial object of an algebra over
\mathcal{L}_{m}
. Similarly, we would like to compute the condensation of the operad for multiplicative hyperoperads. Our first conjecture was that the resulting one-coloured operad is an
E_{3}
-operad. However, this does not seem to be the case. Still, we are able to construct a map from an
E_{2}
-operad to this resulting one-coloured operad.

Here is a more detailed description of the contents of this paper. In Section
2
, we will recall the notions of a coloured operad and its condensation. We will also recall the definition of the complete graph operad and multiplicative hyperoperads. In Section
3
, we will prove our main result which is Theorem
3.5
. We will start by giving the explicit description of the operad
\mathcal{H}
for multiplicative hyperoperads. As in
[
2
]
, the crucial ingredient is the construction of a complexity map
c
. In our case, it is a map from
\mathcal{H}
to the third stage filtration
\mathcal{K}_{3}
of the complete graph operad. This map

allows us to define another coloured operad
\hat{\mathcal{H}}
, and the main argument in the proof of Theorem
3.5
is that the condensation of
\hat{\mathcal{H}}
yields an
E_{2}
-operad. We will also rely on Lemma

For
k\geq 0
and a
k+1
-tuple
(T_{1},\ldots,T_{k};T)
of planar trees,
\mathcal{H}(T_{1},\ldots,T_{k};T)
is the set of (isomorphism classes of) circled planar trees, where the circles can be either black or white, such that

•

•

3.2.
The complexity map

Let us assume that

For

Lemma 3.4

Let

We proceed by induction on

contains only one element which is given by the planar tree

be the complete graph obtained by deleting the vertex

. There is a functor

which deletes the white circle

induces a weak equivalence between nerves. According to Quillen’s Theorem A, it suffices to show that for all

is a reflective subcategory of

Proof.

The proof is inspired from the proof of

be the map of operads which sends

be the coloured operad whose set of colours is the set of planar trees. For

where the right map is a weak equivalence . First, there is an obvious projection map

Remark 3.6
.

One could be tempted to define

as the coloured operad whose operations are pairs operations

such that

. Then one could try to construct a weak equivalence of operads
