2 C.F.R. § 5801.10 — Adoption of 2 CFR Part 200.
Citation: us:cfr:2:5801.10
Creator: Election Assistance Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-5801.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 5801 (Uniform Administrative Requirements, Cost Principles, And Audit Requirements For Federal Awards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The U.S. Election Assistance Commission adopts the Office of Management and Budget (OMB) Guidance in 2 CFR part 200. Thus, this part gives regulatory effect to the OMB guidance.
