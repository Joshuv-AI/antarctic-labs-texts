27 C.F.R. § 27.206 — Bottles not constituting approved containers.
Citation: us:cfr:27:27.206
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-27.206
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 27 (Importation Of Distilled Spirits, Wines, And Beer). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The appropriate TTB officer is authorized to disapprove any bottle, including a bottle of less than 200 ml. capacity, for use as a liquor bottle which he determines to be deceptive. Disapproved bottles may not be imported into the United States.
