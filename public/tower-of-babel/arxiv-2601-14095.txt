BASIS NUMBER AND PATHWIDTH
Babak Miraftab, Pat Morin, and Yelena Yuditsky

Abstract
We prove two results relating the basis number of a graph G G to path decompositions of G G . Our first result shows that the basis number of a graph is at most four times its pathwidth. Our second result shows that, if a graph G G has a path decomposition with adhesions of size at most k k in which the graph induced by each bag has basis number at most b b , then G G has basis number at most b + O ⁡ ( k ​ log 2 ​ k ) b+O(k\log^{2}k) . The first result, combined with recent work of \NoHyper 3 shows that the basis number of a graph is bounded by a polynomial function of its treewidth. The second result (also combined with the work of \NoHyper 3 ) shows that every K t K_{t} -minor-free graph has a basis number bounded by a polynomial function of t t .
