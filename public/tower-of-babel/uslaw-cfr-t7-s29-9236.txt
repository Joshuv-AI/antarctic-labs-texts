7 C.F.R. § 29.9236 — Form of application.
Citation: us:cfr:7:29.9236
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-29.9236
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 29 (Tobacco Inspection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Application for inspection and certification of class or type of tobacco shall include the following information;
(a) The date of the application;
(b) The producer's certification of the type of the tobacco, the crop year when produced, and where produced;
(c) The name and post office address of the applicant;
(d) The financial interest of the applicant in the tobacco;
(e) The exact nature of the service desired;
(f) A statement that the tobacco is (1) in commerce, as defined in the act, or (2) entering such commerce; and
(g) Such other necessary information as the Director may require.
