10 U.S.C. § 8669 — Contracts: applicability of chapter 65 of title 41
Citation: us:usc:10:8669
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8669
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 863 (Naval Vessels). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each contract for the construction, alteration, furnishing, or equipping of a naval vessel is subject to chapter 65 of title 41 unless the President determines that this requirement is not in the interest of national defense.
