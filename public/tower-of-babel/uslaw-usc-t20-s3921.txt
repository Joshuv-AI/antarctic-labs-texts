20 U.S.C. § 3921 — Prohibition against the Federal control of education
Citation: us:usc:20:3921
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section3921
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 52 (Education For Economic Security); Subchapter I (National Science Foundation Science And Engineering Education). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of section 1232a 1 of this title, relating to prohibition against Federal control of education, shall apply to each program and award authorized by this subchapter.
