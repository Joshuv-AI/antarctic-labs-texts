4 C.F.R. § 82.2 — Fees and charges.

Creator: Government Accountability Office
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 4 (Accounts), Part 82 (Furnishing Records Of The Government Accountability Office In Judicial Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of § 81.7 of this chapter are applicable to this part; however, where the charging of fees is appropriate, they need not be collected in advance.
