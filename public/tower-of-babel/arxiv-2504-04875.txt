found that the quotient class of

This paper is organized as follows. In Section

2.
Milnor–Novikov pairing, Toda bracket and composition commutative diagram

The Milnor–Novikov pairing is both a classical method to construct homotopy spheres and a method to construct non-trivial elements in
\pi_{i}\mathrm{Diff}_{\partial}(D^{n})
. In this section, we introduce the definition of the Milnor–Novikov pairing from two points of view. From these viewpoints, Antonelli, Burghelea and Kahn’s theorem (Theorem
2.1
) relates this paring to the Gromoll filtration groups.

On the other hand, the Frank–Kosinski–Smith theorem (Theorem
2.2
) provides a way to connect certain Milnor–Novikov parings to Toda brackets, some
of which yield homotopy spheres that map non-trivially to the cokernel of the
J
-homomorphism.

2.4.
Morlet equivalences and composition diagrams

Let
\mathrm{PL}_{n}
be the simplicial group of piecewise linear homeomorphisms of
\mathbb{R}^{n}
fixing the origin,
and let
B\mathrm{PL}_{n}
be its classifying space which classifies piecewise linear
\mathbb{R}^{n}
-bundles. Denote
\mathrm{PL}=\lim\limits_{n\to\infty}\mathrm{PL}_{n}
and
B\mathrm{PL}=\lim\limits_{n\to\infty}B\mathrm{PL}_{n}
.

We also write
\mathrm{PL}_{n}/O_{n}
for the homotopy fiber of the natural map
BO(n)\longrightarrow B\mathrm{PL}_{n}
and write
\mathrm{PL}/O
for the homotopy fiber of
BO\longrightarrow B\mathrm{PL}
. The Morlet equivalence
\mathrm{Diff}_{\partial}(D^{n})\simeq\Omega^{n+1}\mathrm{PL}_{n}/O_{n}
(
[
10
, Theorem 4.4 (a)]
) induces an isomorphism

M_{n}\colon\pi_{i}\mathrm{Diff}_{\partial}(D^{n})\longrightarrow\pi_{n+i+1}\mathrm{PL}_{n}/O_{n}.

According to
[
10
,
\lx@sectionsign
7]
, for any pair
(k,t)
with
t\leq k-2
, precomposition defines a group homomorphism

\tilde{\psi}^{X}_{k,t}\colon\pi_{k}(X)\otimes\pi_{t}^{s}\longrightarrow\pi_{k+t}(X)

for any pointed topological space
X
. We define

\psi^{n}_{i;t}\colon\pi_{i}\mathrm{Diff}_{\partial}(D^{n})\otimes\pi^{s}_{t}\longrightarrow\pi_{i+t}\mathrm{Diff}_{\partial}(D^{n})

to be the composition

\begin{CD}\pi_{i}\mathrm{Diff}_{\partial}(D^{n})\otimes\pi^{s}_{t}@>{M_{n}\otimes id}>{}>\pi_{n+i+1}\mathrm{PL}_{n}/O_{n}\otimes\pi^{s}_{t}@>{\tilde{\psi}^{\mathrm{PL}_{n}/O_{n}}_{n+i+1,t}}>{}>\pi_{n+i+1+t}\mathrm{PL}_{n}/O_{n}@>{M_{n}^{-1}}>{}>\pi_{i+t}\mathrm{Diff}_{\partial}(D^{n}).\end{CD}

By
[
10
, Theorem 7.1]
, when
t\leq n+i-1
, the following commutative diagram holds

\begin{CD}\pi_{i}\mathrm{Diff}_{\partial}(D^{n})\otimes\pi^{s}_{t}@>{\psi^{n}_{i;t}}>{}>\pi_{i+t}\mathrm{Diff}_{\partial}(D^{n})\\
@V{M_{n}}V{}V@V{M_{n}}V{}V\\
\pi_{n+i+1}\mathrm{PL}_{n}/O_{n}\otimes\pi^{s}_{t}@>{\tilde{\psi}^{\mathrm{PL}_{n}/O_{n}}_{n+i+1,t}}>{}>\pi_{n+i+1+t}\mathrm{PL}_{n}/O_{n}\\
@V{S_{*}}V{}V@V{S_{*}}V{}V\\
\Theta_{n+i+1}\otimes\pi^{s}_{t}@>{\cong}>{}>\pi_{n+i+1}\mathrm{PL}/O\otimes\pi^{s}_{t}@>{\tilde{\psi}^{\mathrm{PL}/O}_{n+i+1,t}}>{}>\pi_{n+i+1+t}\mathrm{PL}/O\\
@V{}V{}V@V{}V{}V@V{}V{}V\\
\mathrm{Coker}J_{n+i+1}\otimes\pi^{s}_{t}@>{}>{}>\pi_{n+i+1}\mathrm{G}/O\otimes\pi^{s}_{t}@>{\tilde{\psi}^{\mathrm{G}/O}_{n+i+1,t}}>{}>\pi_{n+i+1+t}\mathrm{G}/O,\\
\end{CD}

where
S_{*}
denotes the homomorphism induced by the natural inclusion, and
G=\lim\limits_{n\to\infty}G_{n}
denotes the homotopy direct limit of the spaces
G_{n}
of homotopy equivalences of
S^{n}
.

Meanwhile, according to
[
14
, Lemma 2.5]
and
[
12
, Page 1082]
, the Gromoll homomorphism
\lambda^{n+i}_{i,i}
and the Morlet equivalence
M_{n}
commute as follows

\begin{CD}\pi_{i}\mathrm{Diff}_{\partial}(D^{n})@>{M_{n}}>{}>\pi_{n+i+1}\mathrm{PL}_{n}/O_{n}\\
@V{\lambda^{n+i}_{i,i}}V{}V@V{S_{*}}V{}V\\
\pi_{0}\mathrm{Diff}_{\partial}(D^{n+i})@>{M_{n+i}}>{}>\pi_{n+i+1}\mathrm{PL}_{n+i}/O_{n+i}.\end{CD}

Combining these results, we obtain

Proposition 2.4
.

When
t\leq n+i-1
, the following commutative diagram holds

\begin{CD}\pi_{i}\mathrm{Diff}_{\partial}(D^{n})\otimes\pi^{s}_{t}@>{\psi^{n}_{i;t}}>{}>\pi_{i+t}\mathrm{Diff}_{\partial}(D^{n})\\
@V{\lambda^{n+i}_{i,i}\otimes id}V{}V@V{\lambda^{n+i+t}_{i+t,i+t}}V{}V\\
\pi_{0}\mathrm{Diff}_{\partial}(D^{n+i})\otimes\pi^{s}_{t}@>{\hat{\psi}^{n+i}_{0;t}}>{}>\pi_{0}\mathrm{Diff}_{\partial}(D^{n+i+t})\\
@V{P\otimes id}V{}V@V{P}V{}V\\
\mathrm{Coker}J_{n+i+1}\otimes\pi^{s}_{t}@>{precomposition}>{}>\mathrm{Coker}J_{n+i+t+1},\end{CD}

where
\hat{\psi}^{n+i}_{0;t}
denotes the composition of
\lambda^{n+i+t}_{t,t}
and
\psi^{n+i}_{0;t}
.

Proof.

We only mention that
\mathrm{Im}J_{n+i+1}\circ\pi^{s}_{t}\subset\mathrm{Im}J_{n+i+1+t}
when
t\leq n+i-1
.
∎

Corollary 2.5
.

For the Gromoll filtration groups, one has the following commutative diagram

\begin{CD}\Gamma_{i+1}^{n+i+1}\otimes\pi^{s}_{t}@>{\hat{\psi}^{n+i}_{0;t}}>{}>\Gamma_{i+t+1}^{n+i+t+1}\\

The Gromoll filtration groups

are images of

and

to the images of

∎
