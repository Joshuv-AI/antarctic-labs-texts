7 C.F.R. § 400.11 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:7:400.11
Creator: Federal Crop Insurance Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-400.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 400 (General Administrative Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this part are applicable to each crop for which Federal crop insurance coverage is available and for which the producer elects such coverage.
