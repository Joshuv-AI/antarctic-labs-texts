33 U.S.C. § 524 — Applicability of administrative procedure provisions
Citation: us:usc:33:524
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section524
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 11 (Bridges Over Navigable Waters); Subchapter II (Alteration Of Bridges). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the administration of this Act, hearings and other procedures shall be exempted from the provisions of subchapter II of chapter 5, and chapter 7, of title 5, except as to the requirements of section 552 of title 5.
