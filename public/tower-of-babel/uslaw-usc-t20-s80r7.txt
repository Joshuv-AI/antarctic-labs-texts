20 U.S.C. § 80r–7 — Congressional Budget Act compliance
Citation: us:usc:20:80r–7
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section80r–7
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 3 (Smithsonian Institution, National Museums And Art Galleries); Subchapter XIV (National Museum Of African American History And Culture). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Authority under this subchapter to enter into contracts or to make payments shall be effective in any fiscal year only to the extent provided in advance in an appropriations Act, except as provided under section 80r–9(b) of this title.
