44 U.S.C. § 306 — Director of the Government Publishing Office: employment of skilled workmen; trial of skill
Citation: us:usc:44:306
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section306
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 3 (Government Publishing Office). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of the Government Publishing Office shall employ workmen who are thoroughly skilled in their respective branches of industry, as shown by trial of their skill under the direction of the Director.
