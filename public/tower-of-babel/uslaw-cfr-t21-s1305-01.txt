21 C.F.R. § 1305.01 — Scope of part 1305.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1305.01
Creator: Drug Enforcement Administration, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1305.01
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1305 (Orders For Schedule I And Ii Controlled Substances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Procedures governing the issuance, use, and preservation of orders for Schedule I and II controlled substances are set forth generally by section 308 of the Act (21 U.S.C. 828) and specifically by the sections of this part.
