21 C.F.R. § 864.9125 — Vacuum-assisted blood collection system.
Citation: us:cfr:21:864.9125
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-864.9125
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 864 (Hematology And Pathology Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. A vacuum-assisted blood collection system is a device intended for medical purposes that uses a vacuum to draw blood for subsequent reinfusion.
(b) Classification. Class I (general controls). The manual device is exempt from the premarket notification procedures in subpart E of part 807 of this chapter subject to § 864.9.
