5 C.F.R. § 1620.31 — Definition.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1620.31
Creator: Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1620.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1620 (Expanded And Continuing Eligibility). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in this subpart, move means moving from a position covered by CSRS or FERS to a NAF instrumentality of the DOD or Coast Guard, or vice versa, without a break in service of more than one year.
