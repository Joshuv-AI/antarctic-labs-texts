12 C.F.R. § 1081.121 — Cooperation with other agencies.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1081.121
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1081.121
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1081 (Rules Of Practice For Adjudication Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is the policy of the Bureau to cooperate with other governmental agencies to avoid unnecessary overlap or duplication of regulatory functions.
