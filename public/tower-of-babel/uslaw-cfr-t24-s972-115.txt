24 C.F.R. § 972.115 — Relationship between required conversions and HOPE VI developments.
Citation: us:cfr:24:972.115
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-972.115
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 972 (Conversion Of Public Housing To Tenant-Based Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

HUD actions to approve or deny proposed HOPE VI revitalization plans must be consistent with the requirements of this subpart. Developments with HOPE VI revitalization grants, but without approved HOPE VI revitalization plans, are fully subject to required conversion standards under this subpart.
