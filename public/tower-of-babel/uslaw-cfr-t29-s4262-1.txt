29 C.F.R. § 4262.1 — Purpose.

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4262.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4262 (Special Financial Assistance By Pbgc). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to prescribe rules governing applications for special financial assistance under section 4262 of ERISA and related requirements.
