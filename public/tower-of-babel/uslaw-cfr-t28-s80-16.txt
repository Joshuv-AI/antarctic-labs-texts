28 C.F.R. § 80.16 — Additional requests.
Citation: us:cfr:28:80.16
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-80.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 80 (Foreign Corrupt Practices Act Opinion Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Additional requests for FCPA Opinions may be filed with the Attorney General under the foregoing procedure regarding other prospective conduct that is beyond the scope of conduct specified in previous requests.
