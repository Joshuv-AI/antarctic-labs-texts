7 C.F.R. § 52.3759 — Determining the rating for the factors which are scored.
Citation: us:cfr:7:52.3759
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-52.3759
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 52 (Processed Fruits And Vegetables, Processed Products Thereof, And Certain Other Processed Food Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The essential variations within each factor which is scored are so described that the value may be determined for each factor and expressed numerically. The numerical range within each factor which is scored is inclusive (for example “27 to 30 points” means 27, 28, 29, and 30 points).
