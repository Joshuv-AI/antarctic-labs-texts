49 C.F.R. § 396.5 — Lubrication.
Citation: us:cfr:49:396.5
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-396.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 396 (Inspection, Repair, And Maintenance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every motor carrier shall ensure that each motor vehicle subject to its control is—
(a) Properly lubricated; and
(b) Free of oil and grease leaks.
