18 U.S.C. § 1464 — Broadcasting obscene language
Citation: us:usc:18:1464
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1464
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 71 (Obscenity). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever utters any obscene, indecent, or profane language by means of radio communication shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than two years, or both.
