20 U.S.C. § 1221e–3 — General authority of Secretary
Citation: us:usc:20:1221e–3
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1221e–3
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 31 (General Provisions Concerning Education); Subchapter I (Functions Of Department Of Education). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary, in order to carry out functions otherwise vested in the Secretary by law or by delegation of authority pursuant to law, and subject to limitations as may be otherwise imposed by law, is authorized to make, promulgate, issue, rescind, and amend rules and regulations governing the manner of operation of, and governing the applicable programs administered by, the Department.
