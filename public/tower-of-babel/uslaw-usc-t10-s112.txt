10 U.S.C. § 112 — Department of Defense: seal
Citation: us:usc:10:112
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section112
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And General Military Powers); Chapter 2 (Department Of Defense). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Defense shall have a seal for the Department of Defense. The design of the seal is subject to approval by the President. Judicial notice shall be taken of the seal.
