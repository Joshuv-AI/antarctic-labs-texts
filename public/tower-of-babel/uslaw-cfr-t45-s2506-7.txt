45 C.F.R. § 2506.7 — Why does the Corporation have to collect debts?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2506.7
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2506.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2506 (Collection Of Debts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Federal agencies are required to try to collect claims or debts of the Federal Government for money, funds, or property arising out of the agency's activities.
