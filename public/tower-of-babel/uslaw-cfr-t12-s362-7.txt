12 C.F.R. § 362.7 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:12:362.7
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-362.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 362 (Activities Of Insured State Banks And Insured Savings Associations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the purposes of this subpart, the following definitions apply:
(a) Affiliate has the same meaning contained in section 3 of the Federal Deposit Insurance Act (12 U.S.C. 1813).
(b) Activity, company, control, equity security, insured state nonmember bank, security and subsidiary have the same meaning as provided in subpart A of this part.
