50 C.F.R. § 221.34 — What is the law governing ex parte communications?
Citation: us:cfr:50:221.34
Creator: National Marine Fisheries Service, National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-221.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 221 (Conditions And Prescriptions In Ferc Hydropower Licenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Ex parte communications with the ALJ or his or her staff are prohibited in accordance with 5 U.S.C. 554(d).
(b) This section does not prohibit ex parte inquiries concerning case status or procedural requirements, unless the inquiry involves an area of controversy in the hearing process.
