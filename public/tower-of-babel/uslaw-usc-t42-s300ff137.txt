42 U.S.C. § 300ff–137 — Limitation with respect to duties of medical facilities
Citation: us:usc:42:300ff–137
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section300ff–137
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter XXIV (Hiv Health Care Services Program); Part G (Notification Of Possible Exposure To Infectious Diseases). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The duties established in this part for a medical facility—
(1)
shall apply only to medical information possessed by the facility during the period in which the facility is treating the victim for conditions arising from the emergency, or during the 60-day period beginning on the date on which the victim is transported by emergency response employees to the facility, whichever period expires first; and
(2)
shall not apply to any extent after the expiration of the 30-day period beginning on the expiration of the applicable period referred to in paragraph (1), except that such duties shall apply with respect to any request under section 300ff–133(c) of this title received by a medical facility before the expiration of such 30-day period.
