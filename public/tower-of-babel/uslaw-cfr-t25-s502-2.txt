25 C.F.R. § 502.2 — Class I gaming.
Citation: us:cfr:25:502.2
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-502.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 502 (Definitions Of This Chapter). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Class I gaming means:
(a) Social games played solely for prizes of minimal value; or
(b) Traditional forms of Indian gaming when played by individuals in connection with tribal ceremonies or celebrations.
