32 C.F.R. § 553.35 — Media.
Citation: us:cfr:32:553.35
Creator: Department Of The Army
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-553.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 553 (Army Cemeteries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All officials and staff of the media are subject to the Visitors Rules enumerated in § 553.33 and shall comply with the Department of the Army's media policy.
