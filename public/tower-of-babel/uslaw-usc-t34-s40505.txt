34 U.S.C. § 40505 — Reporting on National Missing and Unidentified Persons System (NamUs) Program
Citation: us:usc:34:40505
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section40505
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 405 (Reporting Of Unidentified And Missing Persons). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 18 months after December 31, 2020, and every year thereafter, the Attorney General shall submit a report to the appropriate committees of Congress regarding—
(1)
the number of unidentified person cases processed;
(2)
CODIS associations and identifications;
(3)
the number of anthropology cases processed;
(4)
the number of suspected border crossing cases and associations made;
(5)
the number of trials supported with expert testimony;
(6)
the number of students trained and professions of those students; and
(7)
the turnaround time and backlog.
