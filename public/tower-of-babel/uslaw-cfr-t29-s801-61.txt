29 C.F.R. § 801.61 — Designation of record.
Citation: us:cfr:29:801.61
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-801.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 801 (Application Of The Employee Polygraph Protection Act Of 1988). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each administrative proceeding instituted under the Act and this part shall be identified of record by a number preceded by the year and the letters “EPPA”.
(b) The number, letter, and designation assigned to each such proceeding shall be clearly displayed on each pleading, motion, brief, or other formal document filed and docketed of record.
