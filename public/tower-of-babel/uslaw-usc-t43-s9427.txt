43 U.S.C. § 942–7 — Military park, Indian or other reservation
Citation: us:usc:43:942–7
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section942–7
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 22 (Rights-Of-Way And Other Easements In Public Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Sections 687a, 687a–2 to 687a–5,1 and 942–1 to 942–9 of this title and sections 607a and 615a of title 16 shall not apply to any lands within the limits of any military park, Indian, or other reservation unless such right of way shall be provided for by Act of Congress.
