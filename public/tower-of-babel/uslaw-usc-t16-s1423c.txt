16 U.S.C. § 1423c — Cooperative management agreement; authority to delegate enforcement authority
Citation: us:usc:16:1423c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section1423c
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 31 (Marine Mammal Protection); Subchapter VI (Polar Bears). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The Secretary, acting through the Director of the United States Fish and Wildlife Service, may share authority under this subchapter for the management of the taking of polar bears for subsistence purposes with the Alaska Nanuuq Commission if such commission is eligible under subsection (b).
(b) Delegation
To be eligible for the management authority described in subsection (a), the Alaska Nanuuq Commission shall—
(1)
enter into a cooperative agreement with the Secretary under section 1388 of this title for the conservation of polar bears;
(2)
meaningfully monitor compliance with this subchapter and the Agreement by Alaska Natives; and
(3)
administer its co-management program for polar bears in accordance with—
(A)
this subchapter; and
(B)
the Agreement.
