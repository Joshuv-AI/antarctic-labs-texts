33 C.F.R. § 164.42 — Rate of turn indicator.
Citation: us:cfr:33:164.42
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-164.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 164 (Navigation Safety Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each vessel of 100,000 gross tons or more constructed on or after September 1, 1984 shall be fitted with a rate of turn indicator.
