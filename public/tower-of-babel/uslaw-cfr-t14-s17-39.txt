14 C.F.R. § 17.39 — Confidentiality of ADR.
Citation: us:cfr:14:17.39
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-17.39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 17 (Procedures For Protests And Contract Disputes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The provisions of the Administrative Dispute Resolution Act of 1996, 5 U.S.C. 571, et seq., shall apply to ODRA ADR proceedings.
(b) The ODRA looks to the principles of Rule 408 of the Federal Rules of Evidence in deciding admissibility issues related to ADR communications.
(c) ADR communications are not part of the administrative record unless otherwise agreed by the parties.
