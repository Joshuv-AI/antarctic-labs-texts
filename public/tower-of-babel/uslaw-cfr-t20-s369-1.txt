20 C.F.R. § 369.1 — Unofficial use of the seal of the Railroad Retirement Board.
Citation: us:cfr:20:369.1
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-369.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 369 (Use Of The Seal Of The Railroad Retirement Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Use of the seal of the Railroad Retirement Board for non-Agency business is prohibited unless permission for use of the seal has been obtained in accordance with this part.
