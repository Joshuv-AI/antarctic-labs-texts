7 U.S.C. § 7755 — Protection for mail handlers
Citation: us:usc:7:7755
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section7755
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 104 (Plant Protection); Subchapter III (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter shall not apply to any employee of the United States in the performance of the duties of the employee in handling the mail.
