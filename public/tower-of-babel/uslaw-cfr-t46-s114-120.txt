46 C.F.R. § 114.120 — Vessels on an international voyage.
Citation: us:cfr:46:114.120
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-114.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 114 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A mechanically propelled vessel that carries more than 12 passengers on an international voyage must comply with the applicable requirements of SOLAS as well as this subchapter.
