7 C.F.R. § 763.4 — Authorized land contract purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:7:763.4
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-763.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 763 (Land Contract Guarantee Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Agency will only guarantee the Contract installments, real estate taxes and insurance; or outstanding principal balance for an eligible seller of a family farm, through a land contract sale to an eligible beginning or socially disadvantaged farmer.
