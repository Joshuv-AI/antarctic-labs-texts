40 C.F.R. § 170.202 — Applicability of this subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:40:170.202
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-170.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 170 (Worker Protection Standard). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided by §§ 170.203 and 170.204, this subpart applies when any pesticide is handled for use on an agricultural establishment.
