8 C.F.R. § 1003.22 — Interpreters.
Citation: us:cfr:8:1003.22
Creator: Executive Office For Immigration Review, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-1003.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 1003 (Executive Office For Immigration Review). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person acting as an interpreter in a hearing shall swear or affirm to interpret and translate accurately, unless the interpreter is an employee of the United States Government, in which event no such oath or affirmation shall be required.
