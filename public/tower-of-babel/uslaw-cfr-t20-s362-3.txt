20 C.F.R. § 362.3 — Who may file a claim.
Citation: us:cfr:20:362.3
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-362.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 362 (Employees' Personal Property Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A claim may be filed by an employee, by his spouse in his name as authorized agent, or by any other authorized agent or legal representative of the employee. If the employee is dead, his (a) spouse, (b) child, (c) father or mother, or both, or (d) brother or sister, or both, may file the claim and be entitled to payment in that order of priority.
