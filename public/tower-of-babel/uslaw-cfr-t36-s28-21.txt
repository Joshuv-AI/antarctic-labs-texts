36 C.F.R. § 28.21 — Suspension of condemnation authority in the communities.
Citation: us:cfr:36:28.21
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-28.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 28 (Fire Island National Seashore: Zoning Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary has the authority to acquire land by condemnation. Upon Secretarial approval of local ordinances, Secretarial authority to acquire by condemnation private property within the communities and “improved property” in the Seashore District that conforms to the federal standards and the provisions of the Act or is not likely to cause significant harm to the natural resources of the Seashore is suspended, except as provided for in § 28.22.
