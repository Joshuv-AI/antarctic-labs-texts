46 C.F.R. § 106.125 — Penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:46:106.125
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-106.125
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 106 (Requirements For Nonqualified Vessels That Perform Certain Aquaculture Support Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person who violates any requirement prescribed by the regulations in this part is subject to penalty under 46 U.S.C. 12151.
