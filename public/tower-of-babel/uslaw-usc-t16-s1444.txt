16 U.S.C. § 1444 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:1444
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section1444
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 32 (Marine Sanctuaries). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary—
(1)
to carry out this chapter—
(A)
$32,000,000 for fiscal year 2001;
(B)
$34,000,000 for fiscal year 2002;
(C)
$36,000,000 for fiscal year 2003;
(D)
$38,000,000 for fiscal year 2004;
(E)
$40,000,000 for fiscal year 2005; and
(2)
for construction projects at national marine sanctuaries, $6,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2001, 2002, 2003, 2004, and 2005.
