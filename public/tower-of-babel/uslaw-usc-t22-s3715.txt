22 U.S.C. § 3715 — Establishment of Compensation Fund
Citation: us:usc:22:3715
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section3715
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 51 (Panama Canal); Subchapter I (Administration And Regulations); Part 3 (Funds And Accounts); Subpart i (Funds). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is established in the Treasury of the United States the Panama Canal Commission Compensation Fund (hereafter in this Act referred to as the “Fund”).
