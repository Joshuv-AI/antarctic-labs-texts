54 U.S.C. § 312103 — Principal office
Citation: us:usc:54:312103
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section312103
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3121 (National Trust For Historic Preservation In The United States). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The National Trust shall have its principal office in the District of Columbia and shall be deemed, for purposes of venue in civil actions, to be a resident of the District of Columbia. The National Trust may establish offices in other places as it may consider necessary or appropriate in the conduct of its business.
