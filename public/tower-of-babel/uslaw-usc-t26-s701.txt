26 U.S.C. § 701 — Partners, not partnership, subject to tax
Citation: us:usc:26:701
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section701
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 1 (Normal Taxes And Surtaxes); Subchapter K (Partners And Partnerships); Part I (Determination Of Tax Liability). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A partnership as such shall not be subject to the income tax imposed by this chapter. Persons carrying on business as partners shall be liable for income tax only in their separate or individual capacities.
