6 C.F.R. § 115.167 — Agency protection against retaliation.
Citation: us:cfr:6:115.167
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security, Office Of The Secretary
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-6/section-115.167
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 6 (Domestic Security), Part 115 (Sexual Abuse And Assault Prevention Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agency employees shall not retaliate against any person, including a detainee, who reports, complains about, or participates in an investigation into an allegation of sexual abuse, or for participating in sexual activity as a result of force, coercion, threats, or fear of force.
