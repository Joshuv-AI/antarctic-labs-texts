41 C.F.R. § 105-68.210 — -68.210 Which nonprocurement transactions are covered transactions?
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-68.210
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-68.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-68 (68—Governmentwide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All nonprocurement transactions, as defined in § 105-68.970, are covered transactions unless listed in § 105-68.215. (See appendix to this part.)
