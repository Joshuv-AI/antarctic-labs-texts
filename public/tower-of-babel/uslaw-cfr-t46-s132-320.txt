46 C.F.R. § 132.320 — Helicopter-landing decks.
Citation: us:cfr:46:132.320
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-132.320
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 132 (Fire-Protection Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each vessel with a helicopter-landing deck must meet the fire fighting requirements of part 108 of this chapter.
