2 U.S.C. § 2184 — Purchase of furniture or carpets for House or Senate
Citation: us:usc:2:2184
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section2184
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 30 (Operation And Maintenance Of Capitol Complex); Subchapter VIII (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No furniture or carpets for either House shall be purchased without the written order of the chairman of the Committee on Rules and Administration, for the Senate, or without the written order of the chairman of the Committee on House Oversight of the House of Representatives, for the House of Representatives.
