52 U.S.C. § 20309 — Effect on certain other laws
Citation: us:usc:52:20309
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title52-section20309
Context: United States Code, Title 52 (Voting And Elections); Chapter 203 (Registration And Voting By Absent Uniformed Services Voters And Overseas Voters In Elections For Federal Office). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The exercise of any right under this chapter shall not affect, for purposes of any Federal, State, or local tax, the residence or domicile of a person exercising such right.
