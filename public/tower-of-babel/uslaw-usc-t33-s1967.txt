33 U.S.C. § 1967 — United States release from liability
Citation: us:usc:33:1967
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section1967
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 33A (Marine Debris Research, Prevention, And Reduction); Subchapter II (Marine Debris Foundation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States shall not be liable for any debts, defaults, acts, or omissions of the Foundation nor shall the full faith and credit of the United States extend to any obligation of the Foundation.
