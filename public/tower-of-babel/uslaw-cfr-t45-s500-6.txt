45 C.F.R. § 500.6 — Disqualification of former employees.
Citation: us:cfr:45:500.6
Creator: Foreign Claims Settlement Commission Of The United States, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-500.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 500 (Appearance And Practice). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of 18 U.S.C. 207 shall govern the post-employment appearance of former Commission members, officers, and employees, including special Government employees, in the capacity of agent, attorney or representative on behalf of claimants before the Commission.
