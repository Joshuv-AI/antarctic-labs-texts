45 C.F.R. § 265.10 — When are the annual reports due?
Citation: us:cfr:45:265.10
Creator: Office Of Family Assistance (Assistance Programs), Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-265.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 265 (Data Collection And Reporting Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The annual reports required by § 265.9 are due 45 days after the end of the fiscal year.
