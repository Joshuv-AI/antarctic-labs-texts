41 C.F.R. § 128-1.5002-6 — -1.5002-6 Personal property management.
Citation: us:cfr:41:128-1.5002-6
Creator: Chapter 128—Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-128-1.5002-6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 128-1 (1—Introduction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A system for controlling the acquisition, receipt, storage issue, utilization, maintenance, protection, accountability, and disposal of personal property to best satisfy the program needs of the Department.
