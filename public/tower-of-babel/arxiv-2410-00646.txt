Asymptotic moment of fourth power twisted Kloosterman sum
Neelam Saikia

Abstract
. In this paper, we study the asymptotic behavior of the fourth power moment of twisted Kloosterman sums. We express this moment in terms of weighted sums involving Hurwitz class numbers. To derive the asymptotic formula, we employ the theory of harmonic Maass forms and mock modular forms. As a consequence, we also obtain asymptotic average values for certain families of hypergeometric functions over large finite fields.

6.
Hypergeometric functions

In this section, we delve into related topics involving
p
-adic hypergeometric functions over finite fields and present two main results. To state our results precisely, we now fix some notation and recall some basic definitions.
Let
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
denote the ring of
p
-adic integers and
\mathbb{Q}_{p}
denote the field of
p
-adic numbers.
For a positive integer
n,

the
p
-adic gamma function
\Gamma_{p}(n)
is defined as

\displaystyle\Gamma_{p}(n):=(-1)^{n}\prod\limits_{0<j<n,p\nmid j}j.

It can be extended to all

by setting
\Gamma_{p}(0):=1
and for
x\neq 0

\displaystyle\Gamma_{p}(x):=\lim_{x_{n}\rightarrow x}\Gamma_{p}(x_{n}),

where
(x_{n})
is a sequence of positive integers
p
-adically approaching to
x.

For further details, see
[
28
]
.
Let
\omega
be the Teichmüller character of
\mathbb{F}_{p}
with
\omega(a)\equiv a\pmod{p}.
For
x\in\mathbb{Q},

\lfloor x\rfloor
denotes the greatest integer less than or equal to
x
and
\langle x\rangle
denotes the fractional part of
x
, satisfying
0\leq\langle x\rangle<1
. Using these notation
p
-adic hypergeometric function over finite field is given as follows:

Definition 6.1
.

[
32
, Definition 5.1]

Let
p
be an odd prime and
t\in\mathbb{F}_{p}
.
For a positive integer
n
and
1\leq k\leq n
, let
a_{k}
,
b_{k}

\in\mathbb{Q}\cap\mathbb{Z}_{p}

Then

\displaystyle{{}_{n}\mathbb{G}_{n}}\left(\begin{array}[]{cccc}a_{1},&a_{2},&\ldots,&a_{n}\\
b_{1},&b_{2},&\ldots,&b_{n}\end{array}\mid t\right)_{p}:=\frac{-1}{p-1}\sum_{a=0}^{p-2}(-1)^{an}~~\overline{\omega}^{a}(t)

\displaystyle\times\prod\limits_{k=1}^{n}(-p)^{-\lfloor\langle a_{k}\rangle-\frac{a}{p-1}\rfloor-\lfloor\langle-b_{k}\rangle+\frac{a}{p-1}\rfloor}\frac{\Gamma_{p}(\langle a_{k}-\frac{a}{p-1}\rangle)}{\Gamma_{p}(\langle a_{k}\rangle)}\frac{\Gamma_{p}(\langle-b_{k}+\frac{a}{p-1}\rangle)}{\Gamma_{p}(\langle-b_{k}\rangle)}.

In our purpose, for
\lambda\in\mathbb{F}_{p},
we study two families of these functions, namely,

{{}_{3}G_{3}}(\lambda)_{p}:={{}_{3}\mathbb{G}_{3}}\left(\begin{matrix}\frac{5}{6}&\frac{1}{12},&\frac{7}{12}\vskip 2.84526pt\\
\frac{1}{3}&\frac{1}{3}&\frac{1}{3}\end{matrix}\mid\frac{\lambda-1}{\lambda}\right)_{p}

\frac{1}{3}&\frac{1}{3}&\frac{1}{3}&\frac{2}{3}&\frac{2}{3}&\frac{2}{3}&0&0&0\end{matrix}\mid\lambda\right)_{p}.

For brevity, we use these two notation in the rest of the paper. In the next two theorems, we compute the asymptotic averages of these two families of hypergeometric functions twisted by a character.

Theorem 6.2
.

Let
p\equiv 1\pmod{3}
be a prime and

be a character of order 6. Then as

\sum\limits_{\lambda\in\mathbb{F}_{p}}\psi_{6}\left(\lambda(1-\lambda)^{2}\right)\cdot{{}_{3}G_{3}}(\lambda)_{p}=o(1).

.

Let

be an odd prime. Then as

To proceed further, we first recall some important theorems, lemmas and definitions that are very useful to prove the results. For multiplicative characters
\chi
and
\psi
of

\mathbb{F}_{p}
(with the convention that
\chi(0)=0
for multiplicative characters), Jacobi sum

is defined by

\displaystyle J(\chi,\psi):=\sum_{y\in\mathbb{F}_{p}}\chi(y)\psi(1-y).

Moreover, the normalized Jacobi sum also known as a binomial coefficient

{\chi\choose\psi}:=\frac{\psi(-1)}{p}J(\chi,\overline{\psi}).

Using these binomial coefficients, Greene
[
19
]

{{}_{2}F_{1}}(\lambda)_{p}:={{}_{2}F_{1}}\left(\begin{matrix}\phi&\phi\\
~&\varepsilon\end{matrix}\mid\lambda\right)_{p}:=\frac{p}{p-1}\sum\limits_{\chi\in\widehat{\mathbb{F}_{p}^{\times}}}{\phi\chi\choose\chi}{\phi\chi\choose\chi}\chi(\lambda).

[
16

17

19
]

-hypergeometric functions. More precisely, for

(6.3)

\displaystyle{{}_{2}F_{1}}(\lambda)_{p}=\frac{-\phi(-1)}{p}\cdot a_{p}(\lambda).

In
[

[
