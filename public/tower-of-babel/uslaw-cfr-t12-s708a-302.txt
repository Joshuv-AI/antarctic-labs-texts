12 C.F.R. § 708a.302 — a.302 Authority to merge.
Citation: us:cfr:12:708a.302
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-708a.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 708a (PART 708a—BANK CONVERSIONS AND MERGERS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A credit union, with the approval of its members, may merge into a bank only with the prior approval of NCUA, the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation, and the regulator of the bank. If the credit union is State chartered, it also needs the prior approval of its State regulator.
