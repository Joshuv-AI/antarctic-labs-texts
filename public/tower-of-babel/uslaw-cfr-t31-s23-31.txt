31 C.F.R. § 23.31 — General responsibilities.
Citation: us:cfr:31:23.31
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-23.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 23 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Age In Programs And Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance From The Department Of The Treasury). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each Treasury recipient has primary responsibility to ensure that its programs and activities are in compliance with the Act and these regulations, and shall take steps to eliminate violations of the Act. A recipient also has responsibility to maintain records, provide information, and afford Treasury access to its records to the extent Treasury finds necessary to determine whether the recipient is in compliance with the Act and these regulations.
