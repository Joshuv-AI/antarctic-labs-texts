DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Office of the Secretary


Charter Renewals of Department of Defense Federal Advisory 
Committees--United States Military Academy Board of Visitors, United 
States Naval Academy Board of Visitors, and Board of Visitors of the 
United States Air Force Academy

AGENCY: Department of Defense (DoD).

ACTION: Charter renewal of Federal Advisory Committees.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The DoD (referred to herein as ``the Department,'' 
``Department of War,'' or ``DoW'') is publishing this notice to 
announce that it is renewing the charters of the United States Military 
Academy Board of Visitors, United States Naval Academy Board of 
Visitors, and Board of Visitors of the United States Air Force Academy, 
hereinafter referred to as the ``Military Service Academy Boards of 
Visitors,'' as non-discretionary Federal advisory committees.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Jim Freeman, Advisory Committee 
Management Officer for the DoW, 703-692-5952, 
[email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The charters for the Military Service 
Academy Boards of Visitors are being renewed in accordance with chapter 
10 of title 5, United States Code (U.S.C.) (commonly known as ``the 
Federal Advisory Committee Act'' or ``FACA'') and 41 Code of Federal 
Regulations (CFR) part 102-3.50(a). The charters and contact 
information for the Military Service Academy Boards of Visitors' 
Designated Federal Officers (DFOs) can be found at https://www.facadatabase.gov/FACA/apex/FACAPublicAgencyNavigation.
    The mission/scope for the Military Service Academy Boards of 
Visitors along with their membership requirements are described in 10 
U.S.C. 7455, 8468, and 9455. Members of the Military Service Academy 
Boards of Visitors who are not full-time or permanent part-time Federal 
civilian officers or employees, or active-duty members of the Uniformed 
Services, shall be appointed as experts or consultants, pursuant to 5 
U.S.C. 3109, to serve as special government employee members. Members 
of the Military Service Academy Boards of Visitors members who are 
full-time or permanent part-time Federal civilian officers or 
employees, or active-duty members of the Uniformed Services, shall be 
designated pursuant to 41 CFR part 102-3.130(a) to serve as regular 
government employee members.
    The public or interested organizations may submit written 
statements about the missions and functions of the Military Service 
Academy Boards of Visitors. Written statements shall be submitted to 
the respective DFO and may be submitted at any time or in response to 
the stated agenda of a planned meeting of a Military Service Academy 
Boards of Visitors. The respective DFO will ensure that the written 
statements are provided to their respective membership for their 
consideration.

    Dated: October 14, 2026.
