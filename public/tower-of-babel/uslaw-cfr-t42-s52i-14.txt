42 C.F.R. § 52i.14 — i.14 Additional conditions.
Citation: us:cfr:42:52i.14
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-52i.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 52i (PART 52i—JOHN LEWIS NIMHD RESEARCH ENDOWMENT PROGRAM). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director may, with respect to any grant award, impose additional conditions prior to, or at the time of, any award when in the Director's judgment the conditions are necessary to ensure the carrying out of the purposes of the award, the interests of the public health, or the conservation of grant funds.
