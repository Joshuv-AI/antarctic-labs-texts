Correction for Tse et al., Large-scale combination screens reveal small-molecule sensitization of antibiotic-resistant gram-negative ESKAPE pathogens
Published: 2025

Correction for “Large-scale combination screens reveal small-molecule sensitization of antibiotic-resistant gram-negative ESKAPE pathogens,” by Megan W. Tse, Meilin Zhu, Benjamin Peters, Efrat Hamami, Julie Chen, Kathleen P. Davis, Samuel Nitz, Juliane Weller, Thulasi Warrier, Diana K. Hunt, Yoelkys Morales, Tomohiko Kawate, Jeffrey L. Gaulin, Jon H. Come, Juan Hernandez-Bird, Wenwen Huo, Isabelle Neisewander, Xiao Yu, Jose A. Aceves-Salvador, Laura L. Kiessling, Deborah T. Hung, Joan Mecsas, Bree B. Aldridge, Ralph R. Isberg, and Paul C. Blainey, which published March 24, 2025; 10.1073/pnas.2402017122 (Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. U.S.A. 122, e2402017122).

The authors note that the following statement appeared incorrectly within the Acknowledgments, “This work was completed as part of the Center for Innovation to Transform Antibiotic Discovery at the Broad Institute and Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) with support from NIH National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases Grant U19AI42780, as well as funds from the Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard, the MIT Deshpande Center Innovation Award, the Merkin Institute for Transformative Technologies in Healthcare, the Koch Institute for Integrative Cancer Research at MIT, and the Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency.”

This statement should instead appear as, “This work was completed as part of the Center for Innovation to Transform Antibiotic Discovery at the Broad Institute and Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) with support from NIH National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases Grant U19AI42780, as well as funds from the Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard, the MIT Deshpande Center Innovation Award, the Merkin Institute for Transformative Technologies in Healthcare, the Koch Institute for Integrative Cancer Research at MIT, and the Advanced Research Projects Agency for Health (ARPA-H) award 1AY2AX000005.” The online version has been corrected.
