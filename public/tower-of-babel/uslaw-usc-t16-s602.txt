16 U.S.C. § 602 — Seizure of timber cut
Citation: us:usc:16:602
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section602
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 4 (Protection Of Timber, And Depredations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any timber cut on the public lands shall be exported from the Territories of the United States, it shall be liable to seizure by United States authority wherever found.
