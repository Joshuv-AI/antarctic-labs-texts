Vertex-distinguishing edge coloring of graphs
Yuping Gao, Songling Shan, Guanghui Wang, Yiming Zhou

Abstract
Let k ≥ 1 k\geq 1 be an integer and let G G be a nonempty simple graph. An edge- k k -coloring φ \varphi of G G is an assignment of colors from { 1 , … , k } \{1,\ldots,k\} to the edges of G G such that no two adjacent edges receive the same color. For a vertex v ∈ V ⁡ ( G ) v\in V(G) , we write φ ⁡ ( v ) \varphi(v) for the set of colors assigned to the edges incident with v v . The coloring φ \varphi is called vertex-distinguishing if φ ⁡ ( u ) ≠ φ ⁡ ( v ) \varphi(u)\neq\varphi(v) for every pair of distinct vertices u , v ∈ V ⁡ ( G ) u,v\in V(G) . A vertex-distinguishing edge- k k -coloring exists if and only if G G has at most one isolated vertex and no isolated edge. The least integer k k for which such a coloring exists is called the vertex-distinguishing chromatic index of G G , denoted χ v ​ d ′ ​ ( G ) \chi^{\prime}_{vd}(G) . In 1997, Burris and Schelp conjectured that for every graph G G with at most one isolated vertex and no isolated edge, k ⁡ ( G ) ≤ χ v ​ d ′ ​ ( G ) ≤ k ⁡ ( G ) + 1 k(G)\;\leq\;\chi^{\prime}_{vd}(G)\;\leq\;k(G)+1 , where k ⁡ ( G ) k(G) is the natural lower bound required for a vertex-distinguishing coloring in G G . In 2004, Balister, Kostochka, Li, and Schelp verified the conjecture for graphs G G satisfying Δ ⁡ ( G ) ≥ 2 ​ | V ⁡ ( G ) | + 4 \Delta(G)\geq\sqrt{2|V(G)|}+4 and δ ⁡ ( G ) ≥ 5 \delta(G)\geq 5 . For graphs that do not satisfy these conditions, the best known general upper bound on χ v ​ d ′ ​ ( G ) \chi^{\prime}_{vd}(G) remains | V ⁡ ( G ) | + 1 |V(G)|+1 , established in 1999 by Bazgan, Harkat-Benhamdine, Li, and Woźniak. In this paper, we prove that χ v ​ d ′ ​ ( G ) ≤ ⌊ 5.5 ​ k ​ ( G ) + 6.5 ⌋ \chi^{\prime}_{vd}(G)\leq\lfloor 5.5k(G)+6.5\rfloor , which represents a substantial improvement over the bound | V ⁡ ( G ) | + 1 |V(G)|+1 whenever k ⁡ ( G ) = o ⁡ ( | V ⁡ ( G ) | ) k(G)=o(|V(G)|) . We further show that χ v ​ d ′ ​ ( G ) ≤ k ⁡ ( G ) + 3 \chi^{\prime}_{vd}(G)\leq k(G)+3 , for all d d -regular graphs G G with d ≥ log 2 ⁡ | V ⁡ ( G ) | ≥ 8 d\geq\log_{2}|V(G)|\geq 8 . Let

.
By the result of Balister, Kostochka, Li, and Schelp in 2004

As

.
By Lemma

,

has a linear forest

that covers all the vertices of

. Let

. Then

contains at most
\frac{2}{3}n

\frac{3}{5}n

. Since

,
we know that
\delta(H)=d-2\geq 6
.

As
k(G)\geq d+2\geq\Delta(H)+3
, it follows that

by Vizing’s Theorem on the chromatic index of a graph.
Thus
H
has an optimal edge-

-coloring

.
We first show that
\varphi
is semi-vd on

.
Suppose that there exists a color-set
S\subseteq[1,k(G)]
with
|S|=s
and
n_{\varphi}^{S}\geq 3
.
Since

and
k(G)\leq 2d
,
{k(G)\choose d}>n

{k(G)\choose d-1}=\frac{d}{k(G)-d+1}{k(G)\choose d}\geq\frac{d}{2d-d+1}n\geq\frac{2}{3}n

{k(G)\choose d-2}=\frac{d(d-1)}{(k(G)-d+1)(k(G)-d+2)}{k(G)\choose d}\geq\frac{d(d-1)}{(d+1)(d+2)}n\geq\frac{3}{5}n.

Thus there exists another set
