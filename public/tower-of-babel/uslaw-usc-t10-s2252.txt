10 U.S.C. § 2252 — Rewards: missing property
Citation: us:usc:10:2252
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2252
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Property); Chapter 134 (Miscellaneous Administrative Provisions); Subchapter II (Miscellaneous Administrative Authority). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Defense and the Secretary of each military department may pay a reward of not more than $500 in any case for information leading to the discovery of missing property under the jurisdiction of that Secretary or leading to the recovery of such property.
