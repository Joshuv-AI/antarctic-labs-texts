48 C.F.R. § 3009.507-2 — 3009.507-2 Contract clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:3009.507-2
Creator: Chapter 30—Department Of Homeland Security, Homeland Security Acquisition Regulation (Hsar)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-3009.507-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 3009 (Contractor Qualifications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall insert a clause substantially the same as the clause at (HSAR) 48 CFR 3052.209-73, Limitation of Future Contracting, in solicitations and contracts when a potential organizational conflict of interest exists and mitigation is not feasible.
