5 C.F.R. § 1820.15 — Service of requests or demands.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1820.15
Creator: Office Of Special Counsel
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1820.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1820 (Freedom Of Information Act Requests; Production Of Records Or Testimony). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Requests or demands for official records or information or testimony under this subpart must be served by mail to the U.S. Office of Special Counsel, Office of General Counsel, 1730 M Street NW, Suite 218, Washington, DC 20036-4505; or by email to ogc@osc.gov. The subject line should read “Touhy Request.”
