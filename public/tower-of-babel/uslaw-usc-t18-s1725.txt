18 U.S.C. § 1725 — Postage unpaid on deposited mail matter
Citation: us:usc:18:1725
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1725
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 83 (Postal Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever knowingly and willfully deposits any mailable matter such as statements of accounts, circulars, sale bills, or other like matter, on which no postage has been paid, in any letter box established, approved, or accepted by the Postal Service for the receipt or delivery of mail matter on any mail route with intent to avoid payment of lawful postage thereon, shall for each such offense be fined under this title.
