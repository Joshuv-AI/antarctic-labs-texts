41 C.F.R. § 105-54.203 — -54.203 Advisory committee charters.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-54.203
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-54.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-54 (54—Advisory Committee Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No advisory committee may operate, meet, or take any action until the Administrator approves its charter and the Committee Management Officer sends a copy of it to the standing committees of the Senate and the House of Representatives having legislative jurisdiction over GSA.
