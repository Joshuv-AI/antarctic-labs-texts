40 C.F.R. § 60.6345 — When must I submit the notifications of achievement of increments of progress?
Citation: us:cfr:40:60.6345
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.6345
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 60 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notifications of the achievement of increments of progress must be postmarked no later than 10 days after the compliance date for the increment.
