28 C.F.R. § 301.316 — Subsequent incarceration of compensation recipient.
Citation: us:cfr:28:301.316
Creator: Federal Prison Industries, Inc., Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-301.316
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 301 (Inmate Accident Compensation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a claimant, who has been awarded compensation on a monthly basis, is or becomes incarcerated at any federal, state, or local correctional facility, monthly compensation payments payable to the claimant shall ordinarily be suspended until such time as the claimant is released from the correctional facility.
