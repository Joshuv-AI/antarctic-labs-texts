10 C.F.R. § 1017.24 — Storage.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1017.24
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1017.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1017 (Identification And Protection Of Unclassified Controlled Nuclear Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A document or material marked as containing UCNI must be stored to preclude unauthorized disclosure. When not in use, documents or material containing UCNI must be stored in locked receptacles (e.g., file cabinet, desk drawer), or if in secured areas or facilities, in a manner that would prevent inadvertent access by an unauthorized individual.
