15 C.F.R. § 1110.104 — Revocation of certification.
Citation: us:cfr:15:1110.104
Creator: National Technical Information Service, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-1110.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 1110 (Certification Program For Access To The Death Master File). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

False certification as to any element of § 1110.102(a)(1) through (4) shall be grounds for revocation of certification, in addition to any other penalties at law. A Person properly certified who thereafter becomes aware that the Person no longer satisfies one or more elements of § 1110.102(a) shall promptly inform NTIS thereof in writing.
