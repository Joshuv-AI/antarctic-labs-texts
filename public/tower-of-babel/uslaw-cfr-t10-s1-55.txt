10 C.F.R. § 1.55 — Establishment of official NRC flag.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1.55
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1.55
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1 (Statement Of Organization And General Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The official flag is based on the design of the NRC seal. It is 50 inches by 66 inches in size with a 38-inch diameter seal incorporated in the center of a dark blue field with a gold fringe.
