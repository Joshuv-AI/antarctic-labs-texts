46 C.F.R. § 76.01-1 — -1 General; preemptive effect.
Citation: us:cfr:46:76.01-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-76.01-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 76 (Fire Protection Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The provisions of this part shall apply to all vessels except as specifically noted in this part.
(b) The regulations in this part have preemptive effect over State or local regulations in the same field.
