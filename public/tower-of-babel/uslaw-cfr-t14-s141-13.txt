14 C.F.R. § 141.13 — Application for issuance, amendment, or renewal.
Citation: us:cfr:14:141.13
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-141.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 141 (Pilot Schools). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Application for an original certificate and rating, an additional rating, or the renewal of a certificate under this part must be made on a form and in a manner prescribed by the Administrator.
(b) Application for the issuance or amendment of a certificate or rating must be accompanied by two copies of each proposed training course curriculum for which approval is sought.
