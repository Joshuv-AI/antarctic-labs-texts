32 C.F.R. § 1645.5 — Impartiality.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1645.5
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1645.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1645 (Classification Of Ministers Of Religion). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Boards may not give preferential treatment to one religion or sect over another and no preferential treatment will be given a duly ordained minister over a regular minister.
