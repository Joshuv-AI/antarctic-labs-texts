34 U.S.C. § 12551 — Purposes

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 121 (Violent Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Subchapter VII (Police Corps And Law Enforcement Officers Training And Education); Part A (Police Corps). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purposes of this part are to—
(1)
address violent crime by increasing the number of police with advanced education and training on community patrol; and
(2)
provide educational assistance to law enforcement personnel and to students who possess a sincere interest in public service in the form of law enforcement.
