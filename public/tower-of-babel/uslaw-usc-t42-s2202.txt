42 U.S.C. § 2202 — Contracts
Citation: us:usc:42:2202
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2202
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 23 (Development And Control Of Atomic Energy); Subchapter XIII (General Authority Of Commission). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may, in advance, exempt any specific action of the Commission in a particular matter from the provisions of law relating to contracts whenever he determines that such action is essential in the interest of the common defense and security.
