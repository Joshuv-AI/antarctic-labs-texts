41 U.S.C. § 1102 — Administrator

Context: United States Code, Title 41 (Public Contracts); Chapter 11 (Establishment Of Office And Authority And Functions Of Administrator); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Head of Office.—
The head of the Office of Federal Procurement Policy is the Administrator for Federal Procurement Policy.
(b) Appointment.—
The Administrator is appointed by the President, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate.
