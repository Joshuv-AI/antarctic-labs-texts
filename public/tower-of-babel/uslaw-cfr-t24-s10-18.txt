24 C.F.R. § 10.18 — Petitions for reconsideration.
Citation: us:cfr:24:10.18
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-10.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 10 (Rulemaking: Policy And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Petitions for reconsideration of a final rule will not be considered. Such petitions, if filed, will be treated as petitions for rulemaking in accordance with § 10.20.
