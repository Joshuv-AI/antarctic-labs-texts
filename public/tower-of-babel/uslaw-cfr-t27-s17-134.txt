27 C.F.R. § 17.134 — Determination of unfitness for beverage purposes.
Citation: us:cfr:27:17.134
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-17.134
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 17 (Drawback On Taxpaid Distilled Spirits Used In Manufacturing Nonbeverage Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The appropriate TTB officer has responsibility for determining whether products are fit or unfit for beverage purposes within the meaning of 26 U.S.C. 5111. This determination may be based either on the content and description of the ingredients as shown on TTB Form 5154.1, or on organoleptic examination. In such examination, samples of products may be diluted with water to an alcoholic concentration of 15% and tasted. Sale or use for beverage purposes is indicative of fitness for beverage use.
