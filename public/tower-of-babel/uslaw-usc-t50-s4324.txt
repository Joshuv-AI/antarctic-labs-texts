50 U.S.C. § 4324 — Return by Custodian, to United States, of payments under licenses, assignments or sales of patents
Citation: us:usc:50:4324
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section4324
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 53 (Trading With The Enemy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Alien Property Custodian is authorized and directed to return to the United States any consideration paid to him by the United States under any license, assignment, or sale by the Alien Property Custodian to the United States of any patent (or any right therein or claim thereto, and including an application therefor and any patent issued pursuant to any such application).
