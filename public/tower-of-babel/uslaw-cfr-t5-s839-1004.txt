5 C.F.R. § 839.1004 — Are lost earnings payable if I separated or if the employee died?
Citation: us:cfr:5:839.1004
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-839.1004
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 839 (Correction Of Retirement Coverage Errors Under The Federal Erroneous Retirement Coverage Corrections Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Yes. If the TSP account is not withdrawn, the lost earnings are paid to the account.
(b) If there is no TSP account at the time the lost earnings are payable, you or your survivors will receive the payment directly.
