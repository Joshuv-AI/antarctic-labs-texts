10 C.F.R. § 590.502 — Application is not a stay.
Citation: us:cfr:10:590.502
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-590.502
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 590 (Administrative Procedures With Respect To The Import And Export Of Natural Gas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The filing of an application for rehearing does not operate as a stay of the Assistant Secretary's order, unless specifically ordered by the Assistant Secretary.
