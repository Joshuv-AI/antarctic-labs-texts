22 U.S.C. § 8810 — Applicability to certain natural gas projects
Citation: us:usc:22:8810
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section8810
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 95 (Iran Freedom And Counterproliferation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subtitle or the amendments made by this subtitle shall apply with respect to any activity relating to a project described in subsection (a) of section 8783 of this title to which the exception under that section applies at the time of the activity.
