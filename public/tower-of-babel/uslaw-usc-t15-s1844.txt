15 U.S.C. § 1844 — Security for loan guarantees
Citation: us:usc:15:1844
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1844
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 45 (Emergency Loan Guarantees To Business Enterprises). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In negotiating a loan guarantee under this chapter, the Board shall make every effort to arrange that the payment of the principal of and interest on any plan guaranteed shall be secured by sufficient property of the enterprise to collateralize fully the amount of the loan guarantee.
