27 C.F.R. § 72.38 — Return of defective petition.
Citation: us:cfr:27:72.38
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-72.38
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 72 (Disposition Of Seized Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the petition is defective in some correctable respect, the original of the petition will be returned by letter to the petitioner for his submission of a corrected petition, in triplicate, within a reasonable time.
