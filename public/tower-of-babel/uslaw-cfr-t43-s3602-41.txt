43 C.F.R. § 3602.41 — When will BLM sell mineral materials on a competitive basis?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3602.41
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3602.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3600 (Mineral Materials Disposal). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except for sales from community pits and common use areas under subpart 3603 of this part, and noncompetitive sales under § 3602.30 et seq., BLM will make sales only after inviting competitive bids through publication and posting under § 3602.42.
