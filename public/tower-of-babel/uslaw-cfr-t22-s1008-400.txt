22 C.F.R. § 1008.400 — What are my responsibilities as a(n) Inter-American Foundation awarding official?
Citation: us:cfr:22:1008.400
Creator: Inter-American Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1008.400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1008 (Governmentwide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As a(n) Inter-American Foundation awarding official, you must obtain each recipient's agreement, as a condition of the award, to comply with the requirements in—
(a) Subpart B of this part, if the recipient is not an individual; or
(b) Subpart C of this part, if the recipient is an individual.
