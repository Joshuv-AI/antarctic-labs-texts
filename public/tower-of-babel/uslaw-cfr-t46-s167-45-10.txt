46 C.F.R. § 167.45-10 — -10 Couplings on fire hose.
Citation: us:cfr:46:167.45-10
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-167.45-10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 167 (Public Nautical School Ships). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The couplings on fire hose shall be of brass, copper, or composition material. All hydrants shall be provided with suitable spanners.
