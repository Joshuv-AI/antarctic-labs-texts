26 U.S.C. § 4471 — Imposition of tax
Citation: us:usc:26:4471
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section4471
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 36 (Certain Other Excise Taxes); Subchapter B (Transportation By Water). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
There is hereby imposed a tax of $3 per passenger on a covered voyage.
(b) By whom paid
The tax imposed by this section shall be paid by the person providing the covered voyage.
(c) Time of imposition
The tax imposed by this section shall be imposed only once for each passenger on a covered voyage, either at the time of first embarkation or disembarkation in the United States.
