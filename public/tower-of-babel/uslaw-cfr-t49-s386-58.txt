49 C.F.R. § 386.58 — Burden of proof.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 386 (Rules Of Practice For Fmcsa Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Enforcement cases. The burden of proof shall be on the Administration in enforcement cases.
(b) Conflict of medical opinion. The burden of proof in cases arising under § 391.47 of this chapter shall be on the party petitioning for review under § 386.13(a).
