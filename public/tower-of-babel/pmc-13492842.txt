Introduction

Telemedicine is the digital revolution emerging as a transformative force in sustainable global era platform in recent years. It is one of the AI tool technologies in the new trend healthcare systems. Telemedicine is an AI tool in the field of healthcare. Telemedicine platforms cover various things like remote consultations and monitoring, personalized healthcare services and video consulting in remote or rural areas. In healthcare, the advantages of telemedicine services are more accessible, efficient, and more centered care. These platforms are used to deliver clinical care services at distances. It ensures that a person receives healthcare when needed, especially for those with limited access to care. Use telemedicine and telecommunication technology to provide an exchange of medical information, despite a person and their doctor not being in the same place (room). Telemedicine can be as simple as text messaging medical care to as advanced as remotely controlled surgery. Telemedicine (TM) includes the use of Communication networks and digital solutions in providing health services. Collaboration between the healthcare providers and patients across spatial boundaries (Overcoming barriers such as geographical limitations, limited medical infrastructure, and high patient volumes, while also offering timely care to patients from the comfort of their homes) for activities linked to health promotion, prevention, and diagnosis and timely referral management and follow-up of patients. Telemedicine involves optimizing resource usage and contributing to environmental sustainability. Further AI integration in telemedicine allows for predictive analytics, and automated administrative tasks, enhancing operational efficiency and reducing the burden on healthcare professionals. A major aspect of telemedicine adoption is understanding patient trust, satisfaction, and engagement. These factors involved and influenced the willingness of individuals to embrace digital healthcare platforms as reliable, effective, and alternatives to traditional healthcare services. With the presence of telemedicine platforms such as Teladoc, Doctors on Demand, MDLive, Health Tap, and Babylon Health, understanding user sentiments and perceptions becomes more essential to improving the service design, addressing the user concerns, and enhancing the overall healthcare experience.

Materials and Methods

Study design and settings

The study adopts a descriptive research design utilizing secondary data to analyze the patient sentiments towards AI-powered telemedicine platforms. The study focuses on trust, satisfaction, engagement, and sustainable healthcare delivery. Data were collected from available online platforms, specifically X (formerly Twitter), YouTube, and official Telemedicine service portals (Teladoc, Doctors on Demand, MD Live, Health Tap, and Babylon Health). These healthcare settings of the research are entirely virtual, reflecting digital healthcare environments and patients’ interaction on online platforms.

Study participation and sampling

The study did not involve direct human participation. Instead, “participants” are represented by online users who posted publicly available comments and reviews related to the selected telemedicine platforms. A total of 2000 user comments were collected between January 2025 and March 2025.

Inclusion

Include English-language comments discussing the telemedicine platform features, functionality, and user experience, trust, or satisfaction.

Exclusion

Duplicate comments, spam, irrelevant posts, and telemedicine service related to unrelated content, and the data set was stratified to ensure diversity across multiple telemedicine platforms and perspectives from global users.

Result

The sentiment analysis of AI-powered telemedicine platforms revealed that 54.9% of users expressed positive experiences, while 45.1% reported negative feedback. Teladoc and Health Tap emerged as the most positively received platforms, indicating strong user satisfaction and service reliability. Conversely, MD Live Health had the highest negative sentiment, suggesting potential concerns related to service quality, accessibility, or technical issues. Key drivers of positive sentiment included ease of access, reliability, and AI-driven efficiencies, while negative feedback centered around response time, user support, and privacy concerns. The findings highlight not only the growing acceptance of telemedicine but also emphasize areas such as sustainable healthcare delivery, trust, satisfaction, and engagement. Addressing the user concerns through improved AI integration, faster response times, and enhanced security measures can further AI-driven adoption. Overall, AI-powered telemedicine continues to reshape healthcare, but continuous improvements are essential for sustainable healthcare delivery to optimize patient experiences.

Discussion

The findings of the study revealed that 54.9% Positive sentiment towards AI-powered telemedicine platforms, indicating growing patient trust and satisfaction. Platforms like Teladoc, Health Tap reviewed the highest positive feedback, with Wang et al. and Xu et al., who emphasized the importance of service quality and real-time access in digital healthcare. However, the 45.1% negative sentiment particularly for MD Live Health it reflects concerns about delayed responses, technical issues, and user experience, consistent with Lee and Jung. Trust in AI platforms is influenced by data privacy, platform transparency, and alignment with physician input, as supported by Kumar et al. and yan et al. Additionally, telemedicine supports sustainable healthcare by reducing travel and environmental burden (Smith et al.). While sentiment is generally favorable, addressing data security, inclusivity, and human AI balance is essential to drive long-term adoption and ensure equitable access.

Limitations and recommendation: Recommendation

The study has several limitations. Firstly, the data were collected from secondary sources such as social media platforms, which may not fully represent the broader patient population. Secondly, the sentimental analysis using Excel-based formulas may lack accuracy and in-depth offered by advanced tools. Third, those comments analyzed were limited to the English language, excluding valuable insights from the non-English speaking users. Lastly, the study focused only on five telemedicine platforms, which may not capture the full range of patient experience and patient satisfaction across other available services.

Recommendations

AI-powered telemedicine platforms should prioritize, enhancing the user trust through stronger data privacy safeguards, transparent service policies, and improved response times. Investing in a user-friendly interface and a personalized AI-driven support system can boost satisfaction and engagement. Continuous monitoring of patient feedback via sentiment analysis will help identify gaps and guide sustainable healthcare delivery improvements.

Conclusion

In conclusion, AI-powered telemedicine platforms represent a potential advancement in modern healthcare, offering solutions that enhance accessibility, patient engagement, and system efficiency. However, realizing their full potential demands the establishment of comprehensive health policy benchmarks. These benchmarks must guide the ethical deployment of AI, ensure data privacy, promote equitable access, and standardize technology integration across healthcare systems. Policy frameworks should support AI literacy programs for healthcare providers and patients, incentivize innovation, and enforce interoperability standards with electronic health records (EHRs) and wearable technologies.

Furthermore, benchmarks must address environmental and infrastructural sustainability by recognizing telemedicine’s role in reducing physical hospital visits and associated carbon emissions. Governments and their regulatory bodies must develop adaptive, transparent, and inclusive AI governance structures to ensure public trust and equitable service delivery, especially in underserved regions. Finally, AI-driven telemedicine in national health strategies, with measurable benchmarks and continuous evaluation, is essential for shaping a resilient, efficient, and ready-to-future healthcare system.

Suggestions and future scope

For future research, exploring the impact of AI on diagnostic accuracy, real-time patient monitoring, and personalized treatment recommendations can offer deeper insights. Additionally, integrating advanced AI models, such as generative AI and deep learning, could enhance chatbot interactions and virtual consultations. Strengthening cybersecurity measures and improving the adaptability of AI to diverse patient needs will be crucial for wider adoption. As AI in telemedicine continues to evolve, continuous innovation and regulatory frameworks will play a vital role in ensuring ethical, efficient, and patient-centric healthcare solutions. Furthermore, future studies should examine how AI-driven telemedicine platforms can contribute to sustainable healthcare delivery, particularly in reducing the environmental footprint of traditional healthcare infrastructure. As AI in telemedicine continues to evolve, continuous innovation, and regulatory frameworks and focus on ethical practices will play a pivotal role in ensuring that healthcare delivery remains efficient, equitable, and environmentally sustainable.
