29 U.S.C. § 3226a — Re-enrollment in alternative school by high-school dropout
Citation: us:usc:29:3226a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section3226a
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 32 (Workforce Innovation And Opportunity); Subchapter I (Workforce Development Activities); Part D (National Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For program year 2010 and each program year thereafter, the YouthBuild program may serve an individual who has dropped out of high school and re-enrolled in an alternative school, if that re-enrollment is part of a sequential service strategy.
