6 U.S.C. § 721 — Evacuation preparedness technical assistance
Citation: us:usc:6:721
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section721
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 2 (National Emergency Management); Subchapter I (Personnel Provisions); Part B (Emergency Management Capabilities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator, in coordination with the heads of other appropriate Federal agencies, shall provide evacuation preparedness technical assistance to State, local, and tribal governments, including the preparation of hurricane evacuation studies and technical assistance in developing evacuation plans, assessing storm surge estimates, evacuation zones, evacuation clearance times, transportation capacity, and shelter capacity.
