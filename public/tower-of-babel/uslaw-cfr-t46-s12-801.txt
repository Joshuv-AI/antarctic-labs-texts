46 C.F.R. § 12.801 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:46:12.801
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-12.801
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 12 (Requirements For Rating Endorsements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules in this subpart implement 46 U.S.C. 8103(k) by establishing requirements for the issuance of MMCs, valid only for service in the steward's department of U.S. flag large passenger vessels, to non-resident aliens.
