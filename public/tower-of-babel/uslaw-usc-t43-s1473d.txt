43 U.S.C. § 1473d — Insurance costs covering vehicles, aircraft, and boats operated by Department of the Interior in Canada and Mexico
Citation: us:usc:43:1473d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section1473d
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 31 (Department Of The Interior). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provisions of law, in fiscal year 1992 and thereafter, appropriations in this title 1 shall be available to provide insurance on official motor vehicles, aircraft, and boats operated by the Department of the Interior in Canada and Mexico.
