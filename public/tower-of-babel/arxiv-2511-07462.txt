Integral Formulas for the Noncentral Tanny-Dowling Polynomials


Abstract
In this paper, we established some integral formulas for and involving the noncentral Tanny-Dowling polynomials. These formulas are shown to be generalizations of some known results on the classical geometric polynomials. Keywords: Geometric polynomial, exponential polynomial, noncentral Tanny-Dowling polynomial, noncentral Dowling polynomial 2020 MSC: 11B83, 11B73

Introduction

Let
\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{n}{k}
denote the Stirling numbers of the second kind
[
11
]
. In the classical distribution problems,
\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{n}{k}
count the number of ways to distribute
n
distinct objects into
k
identical boxes such that no box is empty
[
3
, pg. 47]
. These numbers also appear as coefficients in the expansion of

x^{n}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{n}{k}(x)_{k},

(1)

where
(x)_{k}=x(x-1)(x-2)\cdots(x-k+1)

[
4
]
. In relation to this, it is easy to show that the Stirling numbers of the second kind multiplied by the appropriate factorial
k!\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{n}{k}
counts the number of ways to distribute
n
distinct objects to
k
distinct boxes such that no box is empty. On the other hand, the geometric polynomials (also known as Fubini Polynomials)
[
12
]
, denoted by
w_{n}(x)
, are defined to be the
n
th degree polynomial whose coefficients are
k!\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{n}{k}
. That is,

w_{n}(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}k!\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{n}{k}x^{k}.

(2)

These polynomials are known to satisfy the exponential generating function given by
[
1
, Eq. 3.14]

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}w_{n}(x)\frac{z^{n}}{n!}=\frac{1}{1-x(e^{z}-1)}.

(3)

The case when
x=1
given by

w_{n}:=w_{n}(1)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}k!\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{n}{k}{\color[rgb]{1,0,0}x^{k}}

(4)

is called geometric numbers (of Fubini numbers).
These count all the possible set partitions of an
n
element set such that the order of the blocks matters
. The exponential generating function of
w_{n}
can be easily by setting
x=1
in (
3
). That is,

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}w_{n}(1)\frac{z^{n}}{n!}:=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}w_{n}\frac{z^{n}}{n!}=\frac{1}{2-e^{z}}.

(5)

The study of geometric polynomials became a thrend for some mathematicians even up to this date. For instance, Kellner
[
8
]
established a number of identities involving the polynomials
w_{n}(x)
. Among these identities is the integral identity over the interval
[-1,0]
given by

\int_{-1}^{0}w_{n}(x)dx=B_{n}.

(6)

Here,
B_{n}
denotes the
n^{th}
Bernoulli number defined by the exponential generating function

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}B_{n}\frac{x^{k}}{n!}=\frac{x}{e^{x}-1}.

(7)

The proof of (
6
) uses Worpitzky’s identity
[
13
, pg. 215 (36)]
given by

B_{n}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\sum_{j=0}^{k}(-1)^{j}\binom{k}{j}\frac{j^{n}}{k+1}

(8)

and its equivalent form

B_{n}=\sum_{k=1}^{n}{(-1)}^{k}\frac{k!}{k+1}\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{n}{k}.

(9)

Boyadzhiev
[
1
]
established transformation formulas for the geometric polynomials. In his paper, given the exponential polynomials (Bell polynomials)
\phi_{n}(x)
defined by

\phi_{n}(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{n}{k}x^{k},

(10)

Boyadzhiev
[
1
]
expressed the polynomials
w_{n}(x)
in terms of the exponential polynomials, viz.

w_{n}(x)=\int_{0}^{\infty}\phi_{n}(x\lambda)e^{-\lambda}d\lambda.

(11)

This was used to derive more properties for
w_{n}(x)
including the exponential generating function
[
1
, Eq. (3.13)]

\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-\lambda(1-x(e^{x}-1))}d\lambda=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}w_{n}(x)\frac{z^{n}}{n!}.

(12)

Other notable works are due to Kargın
[
6
]
, Dil and Kurt
[
5
]
, Boyadzhiev and Dil
[
2
]
, Kargın and Çekim
[
7
]
, and the references therein.

In 2016, Mangontarum et al.
[
10
]
introduced the noncentral Tanny-Dowling polynomials
\widetilde{F}_{m,a}(n;x)
defined by

\widetilde{F}_{m,a}(n;x)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}k!\widetilde{W}_{m,a}(n,k)x^{k}

(13)

and satisfying the exponential generating function

\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}\widetilde{F}_{m,a}(n;x)\frac{z^{n}}{n!}=\frac{me^{-az}}{m-x(e^{mz}-1)},

(14)

where the numbers
\widetilde{W}_{m,a}(n,k)
are the noncentral Whitney numbers of the second kind, a generalization of
\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{n}{k}
. Further, in a recent paper by Mangontarum and Madid
[
9
]
, a number of identities for
\widetilde{F}_{m,a}(n;x)
are established. Such identities are shown to generalizes some known results on the geometric polynomials, including the ones in the paper of Kargın
[
6
]
.

In this paper, we will establish integral formulas for and involving the noncentral Tanny-Dowling polynomials. In particulay, we will derive a generalization of Kellner’s
[
8
]
integral formula relating the noncentral Tanny-Dowling polynomials with the Bernoulli polynomials, obtain generalizations of the Worpitzky’s
[
13
]
explicit formulas in terms of noncentral Whitney numbers of the second kind, and derive a generalizations of Boyadzhiev’s
[
1
]
identities for the noncentral Tanny-Dowling polynomials.

2
Results and Discussions

Theorem 1
.

For any real numbers
a
and positive integer
m
, the following integral formula over the interval
[-1,0]
holds:

\int_{-1}^{0}\widetilde{F}_{m,a}(n;mx)dx=m^{n}B_{n}\left(\frac{-a}{m}\right)

(15)

Proof.

Note that from (
14
), we have

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\int_{-1}^{0}\widetilde{F}_{m,a}(n;mx)\frac{z^{n}}{n!}dx=\int_{-1}^{0}\frac{e^{-az}}{1-xe^{mz}+x}dx.

(16)

Evaluating the integral and by (
7
), we get

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\int_{-1}^{0}\widetilde{F}_{m,a}(n;mx)\frac{z^{n}}{n!}dx

\displaystyle=\frac{-e^{-az}}{e^{mz}-1}\ln\left|1-xe^{mz}+x\right|\biggl|^{0}_{1}

\displaystyle=\frac{-e^{-az}}{e^{mz}-1}\left(-\ln\left|e^{mz}\right|\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{mze^{-az}}{e^{mz}-1}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}m^{n}B_{n}\left(\frac{-a}{m}\right)\frac{z^{n}}{n!}.

Comparing the coefficients of
\frac{z^{n}}{n!}
completes the proof.
∎

Remark 2
.

Since
\widetilde{F}_{1,0}(n;x)=w_{n}(x)
, then by setting
m=1
and
a=0
in Theorem
1
, we get the integral

\int_{-1}^{0}\widetilde{F}_{1,0}(n;x)dx=B_{n}(0)

which is Kellner’s
[
8
]
identity in (
6
).

Now, observe that from (
13
),

\displaystyle m^{n}B_{n}\left(\frac{-a}{m}\right)

\displaystyle=\int_{-1}^{0}\left(\sum_{k=0}^{n}m^{k}k!\widetilde{W}_{m,a}(n,k)x^{k}\right)dx

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{n}m^{k}k!\widetilde{W}_{m,a}(n,k)\int_{-1}^{0}x^{k}dx

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{n}m^{k}k!\widetilde{W}_{m,a}(n,k)\frac{(-1)^{k}}{k+1}.

Thus, we have the following corollary:

Corollary 3
.

The
n^{\rm th}
Bernoulli polynomial
B_{n}\left(\frac{-a}{m}\right)
satisfies the following explicit formula:

B_{n}\left(\frac{-a}{m}\right)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}k!\widetilde{W}_{m,a}(n,k)\frac{(-1)^{k}}{m^{n-k}(k+1)}.

(17)

Remark 4
.

Since
\widetilde{W}_{1,0}(n,k)=\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{n}{k}
, then when
m=1
and
a=0
in Corollary
3
, we recover the Bernoulli formula in (
9
). Moreover, using the explicit formula of
\widetilde{W}_{m,a}(n,k)

[
10
, (38)]
given by

\widetilde{W}_{m,a}(n,k)=\frac{1}{m^{k}k!}\sum_{j=0}^{k}\binom{k}{j}(-1)^{k-j}(mj-a)^{n},

equation (
17
) can be written as

B_{n}\left(\frac{-a}{m}\right)=\sum_{j=0}^{n}\sum_{j=0}^{k}\binom{k}{j}(mj-a)^{n}\frac{(-1)^{j}}{m^{n}(k+1)}.

(18)

This is a generalization of Worpitzky’s
[
13
]
identity in (
8
).

Before proceeding, note that by induction on
k
, it is easy to show that

\int_{0}^{\infty}x^{k}e^{-x}dx=k!.

(19)

Also, we recall the noncentral Dowling polynomials
[
10
, Eq. (89)]
defined by

\widetilde{D}_{m,a}(n;x)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\widetilde{W}_{m,a}(n,k)x^{k}

which satisfies the exponential generating function
[
10
, Eq. (91)]

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\widetilde{D}_{m,a}(n;x)\frac{z^{n}}{n!}=e^{-az+(emz-a)(x/m)}.

(20)

The next theorem expresses the noncentral Tanny-Dowling polynomias in terms of an integral of the noncentral Dowling polynomials.

Theorem 5
.

The noncentral Tanny-Dowling polynomials satisfy the following relation:

\widetilde{F}_{m,a}(n;x)=\int_{0}^{\infty}\widetilde{D}_{m,a}(n;x\lambda)e^{-\lambda}\ d\lambda.

(21)

Proof.

By definition,

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\infty}\widetilde{D}_{m,a}(n;x\lambda)e^{-\lambda}d\lambda

\displaystyle=\int_{0}^{\infty}\left[\sum_{k=0}^{n}\widetilde{W}_{m,a}(n,k)x^{k}\lambda^{k}\right]e^{-\lambda}d\lambda

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\widetilde{W}_{m,a}(n,k)x^{k}\int_{0}^{\infty}\lambda^{k}e^{-\lambda}d\lambda.

Using (
19
) and then (
13
) yield

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\infty}\widetilde{D}_{m,a}(n;x\lambda)e^{-\lambda}d\lambda

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{n}k!\widetilde{W}_{m,a}(n,k)x^{k}

\displaystyle=\widetilde{F}_{m,a}(n;x)

which is the desired result.
∎

Remark 6
.

Since
\widetilde{D}_{1,0}(n;x\lambda)=\phi_{n}(x\lambda)
, then when
m=1
and
a=0
, the following relation

\int_{0}^{\infty}\widetilde{D}_{1,0}(n;x\lambda)e^{-\lambda}\ d\lambda=\widetilde{F}_{1,0}(n;x)

(22)

is precisely Boyadzhiev’s
[
1
]
formula in (
11
).

The next theorem presents another form of exponential generating function for the polynomials
\widetilde{F}_{m,a}(n;x)
.

Theorem 7
.

The exponential generating function of the noncentral Tanny-Dowling polynomial satisfies the following integral formula:

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\widetilde{F}_{m,a}(n;x)\frac{z^{n}}{n!}=\int_{0}^{\infty}exp\left[-az-\lambda(1-\frac{x}{m}(e^{mz}-1))\right]d\lambda.

(23)

Proof.

Multiplying both sides of (
21
) by
\frac{z^{n}}{n!}
and summing over
n
gives

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\widetilde{F}_{m,a}(n;x)\frac{z^{n}}{n!}=\int_{0}^{\infty}\left(e^{-\lambda}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\widetilde{D}_{m,a}(n;x\lambda)\frac{z^{n}}{n!}\right)d\lambda.

By apply (
20
) in the right-hand side,

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\widetilde{F}_{m,a}(n;x)\frac{z^{n}}{n!}=\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-az-\lambda(1-\frac{x}{m}(e^{mz}-1))}d\lambda.

∎

Remark 8
.

When
m=1
and
a=0
, we obtain

\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-(0)z-\lambda(1-\frac{x}{1}(e^{(1)z}-1)}d\lambda=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\widetilde{F}_{1,0}(n;x)\frac{z^{n}}{n!},

(24)

an equivalent representation of Boyadzhiev’s
[
1
]
exponential generating function in (
12
).
