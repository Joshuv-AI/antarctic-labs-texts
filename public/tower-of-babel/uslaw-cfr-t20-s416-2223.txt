20 C.F.R. § 416.2223 — Other Federal laws and regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:20:416.2223
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-416.2223
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 416 (Supplemental Security Income For The Aged, Blind, And Disabled). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each State VR agency shall comply with the provisions of other Federal laws and regulations that directly affect its responsibilities in carrying out the vocational rehabilitation function.
