7 C.F.R. § 1463.114 — Appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1463.114
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1463.114
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1463 (2005-2014 Tobacco Transition Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person may obtain reconsideration and review of any adverse determination made under this subpart in accordance with the appeal regulations found at parts 11 and 780 of this title.
