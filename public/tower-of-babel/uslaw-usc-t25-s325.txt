25 U.S.C. § 325 — Payment and disposition of compensation
Citation: us:usc:25:325
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section325
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 8 (Rights-Of-Way Through Indian Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No grant of a right-of-way shall be made without the payment of such compensation as the Secretary of the Interior shall determine to be just. The compensation received on behalf of the Indian owners shall be disposed of under rules and regulations to be prescribed by the Secretary of the Interior.
