18 U.S.C. § 3673 — Definitions for sentencing provisions
Citation: us:usc:18:3673
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section3673
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part II (Criminal Procedure); Chapter 232 (Miscellaneous Sentencing Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in chapters 227 and 229—
(1)
the term “found guilty” includes acceptance by a court of a plea of guilty or nolo contendere;
(2)
the term “commission of an offense” includes the attempted commission of an offense, the consummation of an offense, and any immediate flight after the commission of an offense; and
(3)
the term “law enforcement officer” means a public servant authorized by law or by a government agency to engage in or supervise the prevention, detection, investigation, or prosecution of an offense.
