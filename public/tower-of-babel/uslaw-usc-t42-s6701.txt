42 U.S.C. § 6701 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:42:6701
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section6701
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 80 (Public Works Employment); Subchapter I (Local Public Works). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this subchapter, the term—
(1)
“Secretary” means the Secretary of Commerce, acting through the Economic Development Administration.
(2)
“State” includes the several States, the District of Columbia, the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico, the Virgin Islands, Guam, American Samoa, and the Trust Territory of the Pacific Islands.
(3)
“local government” means any city, county, town, parish, or other political subdivision of a State, and any Indian tribe.
(4)
“public works project” includes a project for the transportation and provision of water to a drought-stricken area.
