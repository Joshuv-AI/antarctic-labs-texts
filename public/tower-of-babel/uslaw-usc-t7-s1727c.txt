7 U.S.C. § 1727c — Direct uses or sales of commodities
Citation: us:usc:7:1727c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1727c
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 41 (Food For Peace); Subchapter III–A (Food For Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Agricultural commodities provided to a least developed country under this section—
(1)
may be used in such country for—
(A)
direct feeding programs, including programs that include activities that deal directly with the special health needs of children and mothers consistent with section 2151b(c)(2) of title 22, relating to the Child Survival Fund; or
(B)
the development of emergency food reserves; or
(2)
may be sold in such country by the government of the country or the Administrator (or their designees) as provided in the agreement, and the proceeds of such sale used in accordance with this subchapter.
