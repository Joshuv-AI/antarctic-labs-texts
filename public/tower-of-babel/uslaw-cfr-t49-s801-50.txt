49 C.F.R. § 801.50 — Exemptions from disclosure.
Citation: us:cfr:49:801.50
Creator: National Transportation Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-801.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 801 (Public Availability Of Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Title 5 U.S.C. 552(a) and (b) exempt certain records from public disclosure. Examples of records given in this subpart included within a particular statutory exemption are not necessarily illustrative of all types of records covered by the applicable exemption.
