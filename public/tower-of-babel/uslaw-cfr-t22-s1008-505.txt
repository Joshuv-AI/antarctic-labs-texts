22 C.F.R. § 1008.505 — How are violations of this part determined for recipients who are individuals?
Citation: us:cfr:22:1008.505
Creator: Inter-American Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1008.505
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1008 (Governmentwide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An individual recipient is in violation of the requirements of this part if the Inter-American Foundation President or designee determines, in writing, that—
(a) The recipient has violated the requirements of subpart C of this part; or
(b) The recipient is convicted of a criminal drug offense resulting from a violation occurring during the conduct of any award activity.
