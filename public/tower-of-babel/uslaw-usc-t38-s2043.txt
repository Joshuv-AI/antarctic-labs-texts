38 U.S.C. § 2043 — Domiciliary care programs

Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 20 (Benefits For Homeless Veterans); Subchapter V (Housing Assistance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Authority.—
The Secretary may establish up to 10 programs under section 1710(b) of this title (in addition to any program that is established as of the date of the enactment of this section) to provide domiciliary services under such section to homeless veterans.
(b) Enhancement of Capacity of Domiciliary Care Programs for Female Veterans.—
The Secretary shall take appropriate actions to ensure that the domiciliary care programs of the Department are adequate, with respect to capacity and with respect to safety, to meet the needs of veterans who are women.
