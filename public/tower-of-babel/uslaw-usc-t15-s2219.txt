15 U.S.C. § 2219 — Assistance to Consumer Product Safety Commission
Citation: us:usc:15:2219
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section2219
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 49 (Fire Prevention And Control). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Upon request, the Administrator shall assist the Consumer Product Safety Commission in the development of fire safety standards or codes for consumer products, as defined in the Consumer Product Safety Act (15 U.S.C. 2051 et seq.).
