Author Correction: CCL3 predicts exceptional response to TGFβ inhibition in basal-like pancreatic cancer enriched in LIF-producing macrophages
Authors: Silvia Pietrobono, Monica Bertolini, Veronica De Vita, Fabio Sabbadini, Federica Fazzini, Cristina Frusteri, Enza Scarlato, Domenico Mangiameli, Alberto Quinzii, Simona Casalino, Camilla Zecchetto, Valeria Merz, Davide Melisi
Published: 2026

Correction to: npj Precision Oncology 10.1038/s41698-024-00742-3, published online 30 October 2024

A reader informed us of inconsistencies in the publication. Source data provided for Figures 2C, 2D, and 2E were incorrect. In the Figure 3C legend, reference to a related publication was missing. In Figure 7G, DT4313(Ccl3): gemcitabine treatment Vimentin staining panels were incorrect due to a copy-and-paste error. In Supplementary Figures 4B and 4C, DT4313 and PAN610 scratch assays were incorrect. The original article has been corrected.
