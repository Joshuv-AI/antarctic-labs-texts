18 U.S.C. § 1385 — Use of Army, Navy, Marine Corps, Air Force, and Space Force as posse comitatus
Citation: us:usc:18:1385
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1385
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 67 (Military And Navy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever, except in cases and under circumstances expressly authorized by the Constitution or Act of Congress, willfully uses any part of the Army, the Navy, the Marine Corps, the Air Force, or the Space Force as a posse comitatus or otherwise to execute the laws shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than two years, or both.
