22 U.S.C. § 277d–27 — Execution of agreements
Citation: us:usc:22:277d–27
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section277d–27
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter IV (International Boundary And Water Commission). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States Commissioner, International Boundary and Water Commission, United States and Mexico, is authorized to carry out those measures agreed upon for execution by the United States in the agreements concluded pursuant to section 277d–26 of this title.
