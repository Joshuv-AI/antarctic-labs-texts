16 U.S.C. § 460yyy — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:16:460yyy
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section460yyy
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter CXXXVI (Fort Stanton-Snowy River Cave National Conservation Area). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this subchapter:
(1) Conservation Area
The term “Conservation Area” means the Fort Stanton-Snowy River Cave National Conservation Area established by section 460yyy–1(a) of this title.
(2) Management plan
The term “management plan” means the management plan developed for the Conservation Area under section 460yyy–2(c) of this title.
(3) Secretary
The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of the Interior, acting through the Director of the Bureau of Land Management.
