43 U.S.C. § 770 — Rectangular mode of survey; departure from
Citation: us:usc:43:770
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section770
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 18 (Survey Of Public Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior may, by regulation, provide that departures may be made from the system of rectangular surveys whenever it is not feasible or economical to extend the rectangular surveys in the regular manner or whenever such departure would promote the beneficial use of lands.
