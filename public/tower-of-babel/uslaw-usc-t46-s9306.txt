46 U.S.C. § 9306 — State regulation prohibited
Citation: us:usc:46:9306
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section9306
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part F (Manning Of Vessels); Chapter 93 (Great Lakes Pilotage). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A State or political subdivision of a State may not regulate or impose any requirement on pilotage on the Great Lakes.
