20 C.F.R. § 688.200 — How are YouthBuild grants funded and administered?
Citation: us:cfr:20:688.200
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-688.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 688 (Provisions Governing The Youthbuild Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary uses funds authorized for appropriation under WIOA sec. 171(i) to administer YouthBuild as a national program under title I, subtitle D of WIOA. YouthBuild grants are awarded to eligible entities, as defined in § 688.120, through the competitive selection process described in § 688.210.
