29 C.F.R. § 38.37 — Notice requirement for service providers.
Citation: us:cfr:29:38.37
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-38.37
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 38 (Implementation Of The Nondiscrimination And Equal Opportunity Provisions Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Governor or the LWDA grant recipient, as determined by the Governor and as provided in that State's Nondiscrimination Plan, will be responsible for meeting the notice requirement provided in §§ 38.34 and 38.35 with respect to a State's service providers.
