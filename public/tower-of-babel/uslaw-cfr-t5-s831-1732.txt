5 C.F.R. § 831.1732 — Commencing date of composite retirement annuity.
Citation: us:cfr:5:831.1732
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-831.1732
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 831 (Retirement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The commencing date of the composite retirement annuity of a phased retiree who enters full retirement status is the day after separation.
(b) A phased retirement annuity terminates upon separation from service.
