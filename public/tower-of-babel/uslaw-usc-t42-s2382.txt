42 U.S.C. § 2382 — Transfer of municipal installations
Citation: us:usc:42:2382
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2382
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 24 (Disposal Of Atomic Energy Communities); Subchapter VII (Municipalities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commission is authorized to transfer to one or more of the entities specified in this subchapter such municipal installations as in the judgment of the Commission, will be appropriate to enable the transferees to meet the needs of the residents of the community for adequate school, hospital, and other municipal services.
