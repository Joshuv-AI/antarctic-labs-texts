5 C.F.R. § 842.601 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:5:842.601
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-842.601
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 842 (Federal Employees Retirement System—Basic Annuity). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart explains the survivor annuity elections available under FERS for retirees, and retiring employees and Members, and the actions that they must take to provide these survivor annuities.
