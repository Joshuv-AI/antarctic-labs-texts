16 U.S.C. § 342 — Administration, protection, and promotion
Citation: us:usc:16:342
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section342
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter XXXVII (Acadia National Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The administration, protection, and promotion of Acadia National Park shall be exercised under the direction of the Secretary of the Interior by the National Park Service, subject to the provisions of the Act of August twenty-fifth, nineteen hundred and sixteen, entitled “An Act to establish a National Park Service, and for other purposes,” 1 and Acts additional thereto or amendatory thereof.
