40 C.F.R. § 35.312 — Basis for allotment.
Citation: us:cfr:40:35.312
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-35.312
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 35 (State And Local Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

EPA will allot and award Toxic Substances Control Act Compliance Monitoring grant funds to States based on national program guidance.
