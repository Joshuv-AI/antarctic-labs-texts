40 C.F.R. § 255.25 — Public participation.
Citation: us:cfr:40:255.25
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-255.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 255 (Identification Of Regions And Agencies For Solid Waste Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Public participation in the process of identifying regions and agencies should be provided for, encouraged, and assisted by the State and local officials.
