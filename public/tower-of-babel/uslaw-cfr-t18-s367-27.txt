18 C.F.R. § 367.27 — Billing procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:18:367.27
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-367.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 367 (Uniform System Of Accounts For Centralized Service Companies Subject To The Provisions Of The Public Utility Holding Company Act Of 2005, Federal Power Act And Natural Gas Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Charges for services to associate public-utility companies must be made monthly with sufficient information and in sufficient detail to permit such company, where applicable, to identify and classify the charge in terms of the system of accounts prescribed by the regulatory authorities to which it is subject. The information provided to associate public-utility companies must provide a summary of the accounts by service provided and showing the charges, classified as direct cost, indirect cost, and compensation for use of capital.
