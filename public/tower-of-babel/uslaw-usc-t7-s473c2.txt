7 U.S.C. § 473c–2 — Penalties for offenses relating to sampling of cotton
Citation: us:usc:7:473c–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section473c–2
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 19 (Cotton Statistics And Estimates). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person violating any provision of section 473c–1 of this title shall be guilty of a misdemeanor and upon conviction thereof shall be fined not more than $1,000, or imprisoned not more than one year, or both.
