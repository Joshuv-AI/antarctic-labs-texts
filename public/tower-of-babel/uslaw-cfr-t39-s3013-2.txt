39 C.F.R. § 3013.2 — Terms defined for purposes of this part.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3013.2
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3013.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3013 (Procedures For Compelling Production Of Information By The Postal Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Administrative law judge means an administrative law judge appointed by the Commission under 5 U.S.C. 3105.
(b) Chairman means the Chairman of the Commission.
(c) Covered person means an officer, employee, agent, or contractor of the Postal Service.
(d) Designated Commissioner means any Commissioner who has been designated by the Chairman to act under this part.
