42 U.S.C. § 8235h — Revoking approval of plans and terminating financial assistance
Citation: us:usc:42:8235h
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section8235h
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 91 (National Energy Conservation Policy); Subchapter II (Residential Energy Conservation); Part C (Residential Energy Efficiency Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall revoke the approval of any plan under section 8235a(a) of this title for the establishment of a prototype residential energy efficiency program, and shall terminate the provision of financial assistance under section 8235a(c) of this title to carry out such plan, if the Secretary determines, in consultation with the Federal Trade Commission and after notice and the opportunity for a hearing, that carrying out such plan—
(1)
causes unfair methods of competition;
(2)
has a substantial adverse effect on competition in the portion of the service area of the public utility designated by the contract entered into under the plan; or
(3)
provides a supplier or contractor of energy conservation measures with an unreasonably large share of the contracts for the supply or installation of such measures under such plan in the service area of the public utility designated by the contract entered into under such plan.
