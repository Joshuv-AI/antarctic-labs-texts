23 C.F.R. § 650.501 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:23:650.501
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-650.501
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 650 (Bridges, Structures, And Hydraulics). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart sets the national minimum standards for the proper safety inspection and evaluation of all highway tunnels in accordance with 23 U.S.C. 144(h) and the requirements for preparing and maintaining an inventory in accordance with 23 U.S.C. 144(b).
