41 C.F.R. § 102-39.30 — -39.30 Accounting requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-39.30
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-39.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-39 (39—Replacement Of Personal Property Pursuant To The Exchange/Sale Authority). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Exchange allowances or proceeds of sale under this part will be available during the fiscal year in which the property was exchanged or sold and for one fiscal year thereafter for the purchase of replacement property. Any proceeds of sale not applied to replacement purchases during this time must be deposited in the United States Treasury as miscellaneous receipts. Deviations will not be granted for this section.
