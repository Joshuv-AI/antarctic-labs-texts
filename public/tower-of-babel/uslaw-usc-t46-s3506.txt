46 U.S.C. § 3506 — Copies of laws
Citation: us:usc:46:3506
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section3506
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part B (Inspection And Regulation Of Vessels); Chapter 35 (Carriage Of Passengers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A master of a passenger vessel shall keep on board a copy of this subtitle, to be provided by the Secretary at reasonable cost. If the master fails to do so, the master is liable to the United States Government for a civil penalty of $200.
