12 C.F.R. § 1237.6 — Authority to enforce contracts.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1237.6
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1237.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1237 (Conservatorship And Receivership). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The conservator or receiver may enforce any contract entered into by the regulated entity pursuant to the provisions and subject to the restrictions of section 1367(d)(13) of the Safety and Soundness Act.
