39 C.F.R. § 962.17 — (Rule 17) Ex parte communications.
Citation: us:cfr:39:962.17
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-962.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 962 (Administrative False Claims Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Communications between a Presiding Officer and a party may not be made on any matter in issue unless on notice and opportunity for all parties to participate. This prohibition does not apply to procedural matters. A memorandum of any communication between the Presiding Officer and a party will be transmitted by the Presiding Officer to all parties.
