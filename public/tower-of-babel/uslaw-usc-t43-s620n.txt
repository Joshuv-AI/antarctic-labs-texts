43 U.S.C. § 620n — Water quality study and reports
Citation: us:usc:43:620n
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section620n
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 12B (Colorado River Storage Project). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior is directed to continue studies and to make a report to the Congress and to the States of the Colorado River Basin on the quality of water of the Colorado River.
