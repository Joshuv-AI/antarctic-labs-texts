15 U.S.C. § 9594 — Review and report by Comptroller General
Citation: us:usc:15:9594
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section9594
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 120 (Minority Business Development); Subchapter VII (Administrative Powers Of The Agency; Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 4 years after November 5, 2021, the Comptroller General of the United States shall—
(1)
conduct a thorough review of the programs carried out under this chapter; and
(2)
submit to Congress a detailed report of the findings of the Comptroller General of the United States under the review carried out under paragraph (1), which shall include—
(A)
an evaluation of the effectiveness of the programs in achieving the purposes of this chapter;
(B)
a description of any failure by any recipient of assistance under this chapter to comply with the requirements under this chapter; and
(C)
recommendations for any legislative or administrative action that should be taken to improve the achievement of the purposes of this chapter.
