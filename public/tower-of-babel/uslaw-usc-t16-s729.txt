16 U.S.C. § 729 — Price per acre
Citation: us:usc:16:729
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section729
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 8 (Upper Mississippi River National Wildlife And Fish Refuge). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior shall not pay for any land or land and water a price which shall exceed an average cost of $10 per acre: Provided, That this provision shall not apply to any land or land and water prior to May 12, 1928, acquired or contracted for under the provisions of this chapter.
