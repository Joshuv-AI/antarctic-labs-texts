2 C.F.R. § 1108.15 — Acquisition.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1108.15
Creator: Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1108.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1108 (Definitions Of Terms Used In Subchapters A Through F Of This Chapter). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Acquisition means the process of acquiring as described in:
(a) Paragraph (a) of § 1108.10 when used in connection with DoD Component actions at the prime tier.
(b) Paragraph (b) of § 1108.10 when used in connection with recipient or subrecipient actions at a lower tier under a DoD Component's award.
