40 C.F.R. § 450.11 — General definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:40:450.11
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-450.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 450 (Construction And Development Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) New source. New source means any source, whose discharges are defined in 40 CFR 122.26(b)(14)(x) and (b)(15), that commences construction activity after the effective date of this rule.
(b) Infeasible. Infeasible means not technologically possible, or not economically practicable and achievable in light of best industry practices.
