ALPs coupled to gluons in the GeV mass range - data-driven and consistent
Maksym Ovchynnikov, Andrii Zaporozhchenko

Abstract
We present a new, fully consistent framework for describing GeV-scale axion-like particles (ALPs) coupled to gluons. Unlike previous approaches, which either rely on pure chiral perturbation theory or introduce scheme-dependent parameters, our treatment simultaneously (i) enforces invariance under unphysical chiral rotations used to remove the ALP-gluon coupling, and (ii) incorporates heavier resonances relevant at the GeV scale. This construction enables robust matching between exclusive and perturbative descriptions of ALP decays, and reveals that commonly used descriptions can underestimate or overestimate ALP decay widths and production rates by orders of magnitude. We further show that the production of ALPs at proton accelerator experiments can incur large theoretical uncertainties, significantly impacting ALP event yields at past and future Lifetime Frontier experiments.

Phenomenology of ALPs.
If the ALP mass is in the GeV range, the description of the hadronic interactions in terms of quarks and gluons breaks down; instead, one needs to know how the ALPs couple to the operators with various bound states, such as mesons and baryons. The generic algorithm to study these interactions is the following
Bauer:2020jbp
;
Aloni:2018vki
. The first step is to make a matching between the ALP interactions with quarks and gluons and a modified Chiral Perturbation Theory (ChPT) describing the interaction of ALPs with pseudoscalar mesons
P=\pi^{0},\eta,\eta^{\prime},\dots

U(3)

q\to\exp\left[-i\gamma_{5}c_{G}\kappa_{q}\frac{a}{f_{a}}\right]q,

(2)

where
\kappa_{q}
is a matrix satisfying the condition
\text{Tr}[\kappa_{q}]=1
, chosen to be diagonal. This rotation converts the gluon coupling into the derivative coupling to the quark axial-vector current (and also modifies the quark mass term), which can be translated to ChPT.

Our approach.
The method we develop basically merges the central dedicated ideas of Refs.

We consider the following production mechanisms of the ALPs within the GeV mass range: 2- and 3-body decays of light mesons –
\eta,\eta^{\prime},K_{S},K^{+},\rho^{0},\omega

Conclusions.
To summarize, we have systematically explored the phenomenology of the ALPs coupled to gluons in the GeV mass range relevant to the accelerator experiments, exploring both the production and decay modes. Our study has two main outcomes. For the first time, we have developed a description of the ALP decay modes that is both free from unphysical chiral rotation and includes effective interactions with scalar, vector, and tensor resonances. It allows us to perform meaningful matching between the perturbative and exclusive descriptions of the ALP decays. Second, we have highlighted significant uncertainties in the ALP production. They spoil the status of its parameter space, where constraints and sensitivities of various proton beam experiments are obtained under the assumption that the uncertainties are invisible in the logarithmic scale. Our findings are also available in the form of publicly available
Mathematica
notebook. They will be implemented in the event rate sampler
SensCalc

Ovchynnikov:2023cry
, allowing the community to recast the parameter space probed by past and future experiments.

.4
Cross-checks of our approach

We validate our approach in various independent ways:

1.

Compare symbolic expressions for various quantities in various limits with the literature. For instance, assuming the
\pi^{0}
-a mixing only, we compare the mass matrix (
25
), the ALP-photon coupling, and the ALP decay width into three pions with the works
Bauer:2017ris
;
Bauer:2020jbp
. We also compare the expressions for the contribution of the pure ChPT to the matrix elements of ALP decays to Ref.
Aloni:2018vki
and mostly find agreement (except for the
\kappa_{q}
-dependent piece, which is absent for the
a\to\eta\pi\pi
matrix element in
Aloni:2018vki
).

2.

Ensure
\kappa_{q}
independence of various vertices and matrix elements. When computing different quantities, we insert an explicit form of the mixing angles and calculate the
\kappa_{q}
-dependent pieces. They always vanish.

3.

Compare the widths of the Standard Model processes with their measured values. We consider the processes
\eta\to\pi^{+}\pi^{0}\gamma
,
\eta^{\prime}\to\eta 2\pi
,
\eta^{\prime}\to 4\pi
,
\pi^{0}/\eta/\eta^{\prime}\to 2\gamma
. We find the agreement within 10%, which is well enough for our purposes.

4.

Reproduce the results of Ref.
DallaValleGarcia:2023xhh
, which studied the ALPs universally coupled to fermions. Although no chiral rotation is needed in that case, we have modified the sector of the interactions with the scalar mesons, so it was crucial to ensure that it does not break the description.
