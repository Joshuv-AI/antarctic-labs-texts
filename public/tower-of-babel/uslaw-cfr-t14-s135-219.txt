14 C.F.R. § 135.219 — IFR: Destination airport weather minimums.
Citation: us:cfr:14:135.219
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-135.219
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 135 (Operating Requirements: Commuter And On Demand Operations And Rules Governing Persons On Board Such Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person may take off an aircraft under IFR or begin an IFR or over-the-top operation unless the latest weather reports or forecasts, or any combination of them, indicate that weather conditions at the estimated time of arrival at the next airport of intended landing will be at or above authorized IFR landing minimums.
