40 C.F.R. § 262.44 — Recordkeeping for small quantity generators.
Citation: us:cfr:40:262.44
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-262.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 262 (Standards Applicable To Generators Of Hazardous Waste). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A small quantity generator is subject only to the following independent requirements in this subpart:
(a) Section 262.40(a), (c), and (d), recordkeeping;
(b) Section 262.42(b), exception reporting; and
(c) Section 262.43, additional reporting.
