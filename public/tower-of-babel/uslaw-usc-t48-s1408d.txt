48 U.S.C. § 1408d — Ratification of prior acts
Citation: us:usc:48:1408d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1408d
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 7 (Virgin Islands); Subchapter IV (Public Housing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All legislation heretofore enacted by the legislative assembly of the Virgin Islands dealing with any part of the subject matter of this Act and not inconsistent herewith is ratified and confirmed.
