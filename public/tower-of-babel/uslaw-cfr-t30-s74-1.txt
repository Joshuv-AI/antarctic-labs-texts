30 C.F.R. § 74.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:30:74.1
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-74.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 74 (Coal Mine Dust Sampling Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part set forth the requirements for approval of coal mine dust sampling devices for determining the concentrations of respirable dust in coal mine atmospheres; procedures for applying for such approval; test procedures; and labeling.
