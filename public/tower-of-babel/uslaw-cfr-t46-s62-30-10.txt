46 C.F.R. § 62.30-10 — -10 Testing.
Citation: us:cfr:46:62.30-10
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-62.30-10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 62 (Vital System Automation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Automated vital systems must be tested in accordance with subpart 61.40 of this subchapter.
(b) On-line built-in test equipment must not lock out or override safety trip control systems. This equipment must indicate when it is active.
