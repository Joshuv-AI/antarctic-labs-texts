33 U.S.C. § 3610 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:33:3610
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section3610
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 49 (Integrated Coastal And Ocean Observation System). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary of Commerce to support the integrated oceans observations under this chapter—
(1)
$48,000,000 for fiscal year 2021;
(2)
$50,000,000 for fiscal year 2022;
(3)
$52,000,000 for fiscal year 2023;
(4)
$54,000,000 for fiscal year 2024; and
(5)
$56,000,000 for fiscal year 2025.
