37 U.S.C. § 504 — Cadets and midshipmen: chapter does not apply to
Citation: us:usc:37:504
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title37-section504
Context: United States Code, Title 37 (Pay And Allowances Of The Uniformed Services); Chapter 9 (Leave). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter does not apply to cadets at the United States Military Academy, the United States Air Force Academy, the Coast Guard Academy, midshipmen at the United States Naval Academy, or cadets or midshipmen serving elsewhere in the armed forces.
