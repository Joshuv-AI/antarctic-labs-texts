Correction: For a clinical application of optical triangulation to assess respiratory rate using an RGB camera and a line laser
Authors: Yoosoo Jeong, Chanho Song, Seungmin Lee, Jaebum Son
Published: 2024

Correction: BMC Med Imaging 24, 274 (2024)

10.1186/s12880-024-01448-5

Following publication of the original article [1], the authors reported an error in the Acknowledgements section. The supporting institution was incorrectly given as ‘UNI-CORE(University & National Institute COllaboration for REgional Innovation)’ and should have been the ‘Commercialization Promotion Agency for R&D Outcomes(COMPA)’ with grant number: RS-2023-00304776.

The final amended Acknowledgements section of the article [1] is as follows.

Acknowledgements

This work was supported by the Commercialization Promotion Agency for R&D Outcomes(COMPA) grant funded by the Korean Government (Ministry of Science and ICT) (RS-2023-00304776). This work was supported by the Korea Medical Device Development Fund grant funded by the Korea government (the Ministry of Science and ICT, the Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy, the Ministry of Health & Welfare, and the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety) (Project Number: 1711174476, RS-2022-00141072).

The original article [1] has been corrected.
