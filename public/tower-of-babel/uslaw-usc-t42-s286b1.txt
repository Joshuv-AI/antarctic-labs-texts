42 U.S.C. § 286b–1 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:42:286b–1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section286b–1
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter III (National Research Institutes); Part D (National Library Of Medicine); Subpart 2 (Financial Assistance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this subpart—
(1)
the term “medical library” means a library related to the sciences related to health; and
(2)
the term “sciences related to health” includes medicine, osteopathy, dentistry, and public health, and fundamental and applied sciences when related thereto.
