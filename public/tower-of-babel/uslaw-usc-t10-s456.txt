10 U.S.C. § 456 — Civil actions barred
Citation: us:usc:10:456
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section456
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And General Military Powers); Chapter 22 (National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency); Subchapter II (Maps, Charts, And Geomatics Products). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No civil action may be brought against the United States on the basis of the content of geospatial information prepared or disseminated by the National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency.
