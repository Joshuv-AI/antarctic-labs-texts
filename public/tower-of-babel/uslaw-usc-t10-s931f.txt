10 U.S.C. § 931f — Art. 131f. Noncompliance with procedural rules
Citation: us:usc:10:931f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section931f
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 47 (Uniform Code Of Military Justice); Subchapter X (Punitive Articles). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person subject to this chapter who—
(1)
is responsible for unnecessary delay in the disposition of any case of a person accused of an offense under this chapter; or
(2)
knowingly and intentionally fails to enforce or comply with any provision of this chapter regulating the proceedings before, during, or after trial of an accused;
shall be punished as a court-martial may direct.
