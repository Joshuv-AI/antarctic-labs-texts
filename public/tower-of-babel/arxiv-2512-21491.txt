Generators of a Bosonic VOA and
Connections to a Boundary VOA

Abstract
We show how to identify generators of bosonic VOAs associated with T [ n − 1 , 1 ] [ 1 n ] ​ ( S ​ U ​ ( n ) ) T_{[n-1,1]}^{[1^{n}]}(SU(n)) and T [ n − 1 , 1 2 ] [ 2 , 1 n − 1 ] ​ ( S ​ U ​ ( n + 1 ) ) T_{[n-1,1^{2}]}^{[2,1^{n-1}]}(SU(n+1)) , and conjecture that the algebraic structure of these VOAs can be constructed by these generators. We also find out that the boundary VOA associated with T [ n − 1 , 1 ] [ 1 n ] ​ ( S ​ U ​ ( n ) ) T_{[n-1,1]}^{[1^{n}]}(SU(n)) naturally includes the bosonic VOA.

\displaystyle\sum_{s=p}^{q}(-1)^{p+q+s}\binom{r}{s}\binom{s-1}{p-1}\binom{r-s-1}{r-q-1}

\displaystyle=(-1)^{q}\binom{r-1}{q}q\binom{q-1}{p-1}\sum_{s=0}^{q-p}\frac{(-1)^{s}}{s+p}\binom{q-p}{s}

\displaystyle\quad+(-1)^{p}\binom{r-1}{p-1}(r-p)\binom{r-p-1}{r-q-1}\sum_{s=0}^{q-p}\frac{(-1)^{s}}{s+r-q}\binom{q-p}{s}

\displaystyle=(-1)^{p}\binom{r-1}{p-1}+(-1)^{q}\binom{r-1}{q}~.

Lemma 7.5
.

For three positive integer

,

and

satisfying

\displaystyle\sum_{s=p}^{q}(-1)^{p+q+s}\binom{r}{s}\binom{s-1}{p-1}\binom{r-s-1}{r-q}=\delta_{q=r}(-1)^{p}\binom{r-1}{p-1}-(-1)^{p}\binom{r-1}{p-1}+(-1)^{q}\binom{r-1}{q-1}~.

(7.15)

Proof.

For

\displaystyle\sum_{s=p}^{q}(-1)^{p+q+s}\binom{r}{s}\binom{s-1}{p-1}\binom{r-s-1}{r-q}

\displaystyle=\delta_{p=r}(-1)^{p}~.

\displaystyle\quad\sum_{s=p}^{q}(-1)^{p+q+s}\binom{r}{s}\binom{s-1}{p-1}\binom{r-s-1}{r-q}

\displaystyle=\delta_{q=r}(-1)^{p}\binom{r-1}{p-1}-(-1)^{p}\binom{r-1}{p-1}+(-1)^{q}\binom{r-1}{q-1}~.
