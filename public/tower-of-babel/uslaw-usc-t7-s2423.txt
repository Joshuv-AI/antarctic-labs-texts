7 U.S.C. § 2423 — Joint breeders
Citation: us:usc:7:2423
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2423
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 57 (Plant Variety Protection); Subchapter II (Protectability Of Plant Varieties And Certificates Of Protection); Part E (Applications; Form; Who May File; Relating Back; Confidentiality). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
When two or more persons are the breeders, one person (or the successor of the person) may apply, naming the others.
(b)
The Secretary, after such notice as the Secretary may prescribe, may issue a certificate of plant variety protection to the applicant and such of the other breeders (or their successors in interest) as may have subsequently joined in the application.
