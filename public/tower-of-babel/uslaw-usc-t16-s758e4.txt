16 U.S.C. § 758e–4 — “Central, Western, and South Pacific Ocean area” defined
Citation: us:usc:16:758e–4
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section758e–4
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 9A (Preservation Of Fishery Resources). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in sections 758e to 758e–5 of this title, the term “Central, Western, and South Pacific Ocean” means that area of the Pacific Ocean between latitudes 30 degrees north to 30 degrees south and from longitudes 120 degrees east to 130 degrees west.
