10 U.S.C. § 7318 — Thirty years or more: regular commissioned officers
Citation: us:usc:10:7318
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7318
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 741 (Retirement For Length Of Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A regular commissioned officer of the Army who has at least 30 years of service computed under section 7326 of this title may be retired upon his request, in the discretion of the President.
