50 C.F.R. § 36.36 — Sled dogs and household pets.
Citation: us:cfr:50:36.36
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-36.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 36 (Alaska National Wildlife Refuges). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The general trespass provisions of 50 CFR 26.21 shall not apply to household pets and sled, work, or pack dogs under the direct control of their owners or handlers, but such activities may be prohibited or otherwise restricted pursuant to the provisions of § 36.42.
