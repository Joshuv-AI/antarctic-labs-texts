45 C.F.R. § 307.20 — Submittal of advance planning documents for computerized support enforcement systems.
Citation: us:cfr:45:307.20
Creator: Office Of Child Support Services, Administration Of Families And Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-307.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 307 (Computerized Support Enforcement Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The State IV-D agency must submit an APD for a computerized support enforcement system, approved and signed by the State IV-D Director and the appropriate State official, in accordance with the submission process prescribed in 45 CFR part 95, subpart F.
