43 C.F.R. § 8340.0-7 — -7 Penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:43:8340.0-7
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-8340.0-7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 8340 (Off-Road Vehicles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person who violates or fails to comply with the regulations of subparts 8341 and 8343 is subject to arrest, conviction, and punishment pursuant to appropriate laws and regulations. Such punishment may be a fine of not more than $1,000 or imprisonment for not longer than 12 months, or both.
