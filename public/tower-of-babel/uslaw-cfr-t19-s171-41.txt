19 C.F.R. § 171.41 — Application of provisions for petitions for relief.
Citation: us:cfr:19:171.41
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-171.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 171 (Fines, Penalties, And Forfeitures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The general provisions of subpart A of this part on filing and content of petitions for relief apply to petitions for restoration of proceeds of sale except insofar as modified by this subpart.
