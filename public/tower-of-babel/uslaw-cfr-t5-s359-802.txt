5 C.F.R. § 359.802 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:5:359.802
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-359.802
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 359 (Removal From The Senior Executive Service; Guaranteed Placement In Other Personnel Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the purpose of this subpart, furlough means the placing of an appointee in a temporary status without duties and pay because of lack of work or funds or other nondisciplinary reasons; except it does not refer to an emergency shutdown furlough caused by a lapse in congressional appropriations where the ultimate duration of the furlough is not known by the agency at the outset of the furlough.
