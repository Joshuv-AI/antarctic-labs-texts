48 C.F.R. § 1301.105-2 — 1301.105-2 Arrangement of regulations.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1301 (Department Of Commerce Acquisition Regulations System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) General. The CAR is divided into the same parts, subparts, sections, and subsections as the FAR.
(b) Numbering. If the DOC does not have supplemental regulations there will be no corresponding coverage in the CAR, and there will be gaps in the CAR numbering system.
