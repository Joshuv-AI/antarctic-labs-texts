1. Prognostic Value of Retinoblastoma in Small Intestinal Adenocarcinoma: A Multicenter Retrospective Study.

Jun SY, et al.

The study explored RB protein expression in 229 surgically resected small intestinal adenocarcinomas (SIACs). High RB expression (RBHigh) was observed in 33% of SIACs and was associated with favorable clinicopathologic characteristics and better overall survival. RBHigh was an independent prognostic predictor, regardless of microsatellite instability (MSI) status, particularly in lower-stage SIACs. RB expression may aid in selecting chemotherapy for patients.

2. Role of Genetic Testing in Diagnosis and Prognosis Prediction in Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy in Korea.

Hong GR, et al.

The study investigated genetic variants in 1,554 Korean patients with suspected hypertrophic cardiomyopathy (HCM). Genetic testing revealed 43.5% had disease-causing mutations. Combining genetic tests, imaging, and clinical data, 90.9% had HCM, and 5.5% had Fabry disease. Genotype-positive HCM patients experienced more adverse outcomes. Genetic testing aids HCM diagnosis and predicts clinical outcomes.

3. Cesarean Delivery Upon Request in Pregnancies Following Vaginal Delivery: A Nationwide Study.

Cho GJ, et al.

The study examined why mothers choose cesarean delivery (CD) after a previous vaginal delivery (VD). It included women with VD in their first pregnancy and analyzed factors influencing CD requests in their second pregnancy. Factors like age, pregnancy interval, hypertension, gestational diabetes, and traumatic delivery events increased the likelihood of choosing CD. Understanding these factors can help make informed decisions about delivery methods and potentially reduce CD rates.

4. Gender Difference of Moderated Mediating Effect of Grit Between Occupational Stress and Suicidal Ideation in Korean Workers.

Cho SJ, et al.

The study examined gender differences in occupational stress, depressive symptoms, and suicidal ideation among Korean employees, considering the moderating effect of grit. It involved 11,369 participants from private companies and local government organizations. Depressive symptoms mediated the relationship between occupational stress and suicidal ideation, with grit moderating this relationship in men but not in women. The findings highlight the need for gender-specific approaches to prevent suicidal ideation and emphasize the protective role of grit, especially in male employees.

5. Concise Recommendations for EDitorials: Enhancing Narratives and Composition Effectively (CREDENCE).

Benlidayi IC, et al.

This review briefly describes the principles and tips for editorial writing.
