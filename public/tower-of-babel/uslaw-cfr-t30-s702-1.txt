30 C.F.R. § 702.1 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:30:702.1
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-702.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 702 (Exemption For Coal Extraction Incidental To The Extraction Of Other Minerals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part implements the exemption contained in section 701(28) of the Act concerning the extraction of coal incidental to the extraction of other minerals where coal does not exceed 16 2/3 percent of the total tonnage of coal and other minerals removed for purposes of commercial use or sale.
