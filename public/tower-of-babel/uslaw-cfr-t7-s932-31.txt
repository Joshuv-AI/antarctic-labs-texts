7 C.F.R. § 932.31 — Failure to nominate.
Citation: us:cfr:7:932.31
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-932.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 932 (Olives Grown In California). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If nominations for any position on the committee are not received by the Secretary by May 1 of the year in which nominations are to be made, the Secretary may select an eligible individual without regard to nomination.
