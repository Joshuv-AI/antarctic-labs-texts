48 C.F.R. § 841.501-70 — 841.501-70 Disputes—Utility contracts.

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 852.241-70, Disputes—Utility Contracts, in solicitations and contracts for utility services subject to the jurisdiction and regulation of a utility rate commission.
