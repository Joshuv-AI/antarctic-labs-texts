Consistency formula is strictly stronger in PA than PA -consistency
Sergei Artemov

Abstract
In this note, we show that, despite the wide-spread assumption, the consistency formula for Peano Arithmetic PA , 𝖢𝗈𝗇 𝖯𝖠 {\sf Con}_{\sf PA} , ∀ x \forall x “ x x is not a code of a derivation of ( 0 = 1 ) (0=1) ,” is not equivalent in PA to the consistency of PA . Specifically, we demonstrate that “ PA is consistent” is provably in PA equivalent to the series 𝖢𝗈𝗇 𝖯𝖠 S {\sf Con}^{S}_{\sf PA} of arithmetical sentences “ n n is not a code of a derivation of ( 0 = 1 ) \ (0=1) ” for n = 0 , 1 , 2 , … n=0,1,2,\ldots . Since 𝖢𝗈𝗇 𝖯𝖠 {\sf Con}_{\sf PA} is strictly stronger in PA than 𝖢𝗈𝗇 𝖯𝖠 S {\sf Con}^{S}_{\sf PA} , the unprovability of 𝖢𝗈𝗇 𝖯𝖠 {\sf Con}_{\sf PA} in PA does not yield the unprovability of PA -consistency. In [ 1 ] , the PA -consistency in the form 𝖢𝗈𝗇 𝖯𝖠 S {\sf Con}^{S}_{\sf PA} has been proven in PA .



1
Arithmetic: contentual vs. formal

We consider some fragment of contentual mathematics which, within this paper, we call
Ar
(for “Arithmetic”) which contains the set of natural numbers
\omega

0,1,2,\ldots,n,\ldots

and includes all primitive recursive operations on
\omega
.

We also consider the Peano Arithmetic
PA
, which is a formal first-order theory with the symbols and defining identities for all primitive recursive functions, one sort of variables to symbolically represent unspecified “natural numbers.” Along with logical axioms,
PA
has the Induction Principle represented by an infinite series of instances of induction for each arithmetical formula.
PA
has a unique standard model which is the structure with the domain
\omega
in the language of
PA
satisfying all postulates of
PA
.

By Gödel’s Incompleteness Theorem, there are models of
PA
which are not isomorphic to the standard model. Simply speaking, there are (continuum many) countable nonstandard models of
PA
each of which, in addition to
\omega
, has so-called nonstandard numbers.

The domain of formal quantifiers
\forall x
and
\exists x
in
PA
is not specified and depends on a model. In nonstandard models,
\forall x
and
\exists x

do not range over
0,1,2,\ldots,n,\ldots
.

2
Contentual property vs. its formalization

Consider a contentual property
P
of natural numbers expressible in
Ar
. Suppose that
P
can be formalized as a formula
P_{f}
in the language of
PA
by the following step-by-step formalization process: recursive operations on natural numbers in
Ar
are directly represented by
PA
-terms, propositional logic operations are straightforward, informal quantifiers “for all natural numbers
n
” and “exists a natural number
n
” are formalized as
\forall x
and
\exists x
. We refer to
P_{f}
as

the direct formalization of
P
.

2.1
PA
consistency vs. the consistency formula
{\sf Con}_{\sf PA}

The consistency of a formal theory means that all its formal derivations are free of contradictions. For the case of
PA
,

for any derivation
D
,
D
is not a proof of
(0\!=\!1)
.

(1)

Derivations are finite syntactic objects, and their Gödel codes are all standard natural numbers.
Such numbers can be identified with numerals, terms
\overline{0},\overline{1},\overline{2},\ldots,\overline{n}\ldots
in the language of
PA
.
We do not distinguish a syntactic object
X
and its Gödel number
\ulcorner{X}\urcorner
, a natural number
n
, and the corresponding numeral
\overline{n}
when safe.
Let also
\bot
denote the arithmetic formula
(0\!=\!1)
.

Let
x\!:\!y
denote the standard primitive recursive proof predicate in
PA
,

“x is code of a proof of a formula having code y.”

(2)

The proof predicate is represented by a quantifier-free
PA
-formula which we also call
x\!:\!y
.

Since the proof predicate is a direct copy of the definition of a formal proof, for each derivation
D
and formula
\varphi
, the equivalence of “
D
is a proof of
\varphi
” and
\ulcorner{D}\urcorner\!:\!\ulcorner{\varphi}\urcorner
is a straightforward combinatorial observation about Gödel coding and it is uniformly provable in
PA
.

Following Gödel, we reformulate
PA
consistency (
1
) in an equivalent form in the contentual arithmetic
Ar
:

for any natural number
n
,
\neg n\!\!:\!\!\bot
.

(3)

This Gödelized definition (
3
) of
PA
-consistency is not yet in the language of
PA
since it contains an informal quantifier “
for any natural number
n
” not expressible in
PA
.

The direct formalization of (
3
) is the consistency formula
{\sf Con}_{\sf PA}
, i.e.,

\forall x(\neg x\!\!:\!\!\bot).

(4)

It would be a mistake to assume
a priori
that the consistency formula
{\sf Con}_{\sf PA}
(
4
) adequately represents

PA
-consistency property (
3
) in
PA
because of different quantifications in (
3
) and (
4
).

The direct mathematical comparison of (
3
) and (
4
) is not possible since (
3
) is a well-defined mathematical property whereas (
4
) is not; the formal quantifier “
\forall x
” in
{\sf Con}_{\sf PA}
is not specified and its meaning depends on a choice of an arithmetical model. Comparing (
3
) and (
4
) formally within
PA
is also not possible since (
3
) is not in the language of
PA
.
So, the choice of
{\sf Con}_{\sf PA}
as representing
“
PA
is consistent”
in
PA
has not had a mathematical justification.

As it follows from the results of the next Section
2.2
, such a choice cannot be justified since, in
PA
, consistency formula
{\sf Con}_{\sf PA}
is strictly stronger than
PA
-consistency.

2.2
PA
-consistency is equivalent in
PA
to the consistency scheme

As we have already mentioned, the Gödelized definition (
3
) of
PA
-consistency contains an informal quantifier “
for any natural number
n
” not expressible in
PA
. However, we can emulate quantifiers over standard numbers by sets of formulas in
PA
.

Consistency scheme
is the set
{\sf Con}^{S}_{\sf PA}
of
PA
-formulas

\{\neg 0\!\!:\!\!\bot,\neg 1\!\!:\!\!\bot,\neg 2\!\!:\!\!\bot,\neg 3\!\!:\!\!\bot\ldots\}.

(5)

Sets (sequences, schemes, etc.) of formulas are common objects in metamathematics of first-order theories. In particular, the induction principle is expressed in
PA
by the Induction Scheme, which is a primitive recursive set of
PA
-formulas.
A set
X
of
PA
–formulas holds under a given interpretation if each formula in
X
holds.

Proposition 1

PA

Proof.

(\Rightarrow)
. Suppose

{\sf Con}^{S}_{\sf PA}

(\Leftarrow)
. Suppose
{\sf Con}^{S}_{\sf PA}

\neg n\!\!:\!\!\bot

\Box

Proposition 2

The proof of Proposition
1
is formalizable in

Proof.
This is a Gödelian formalization under which syntactic objects, such as numerals, terms, formulas, are represented by their Gödel numbers, operations and relations on these objects by primitive recursive terms and predicates. Furthermore, after Gödel, we use the
PA

Given an arithmetic formula
\varphi(x)

{\sf Con}^{S}_{\sf PA}

Consider a function which is, in a sense, the converse of

N(y)
be a natural arithmetical term for a primitive recursive function

N(k)=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}n,&\mbox{\it if $k=\ulcorner{\neg n\!\!:\!\!\bot}\urcorner$}\\
0,&\mbox{\it otherwise}.\end{array}\right.

By the construction of
N(y)

{\sf PA}\vdash\ \forall x\ (N[(\neg x\!\!:\!\!\bot)^{\bullet}]=x).

(7)

Consider a formula

.

{\sf PA}\vdash\ S(y)\ \leftrightarrow\ \exists x(y=(\neg x\!\!:\!\!\bot)^{\bullet}).

(8)

Indeed, argue in

Now we are back to proving Proposition

To convert Gödel numbers

(9)

The direct formalization of

The direct formalization of

(\Rightarrow)

\mbox{\it Tr}_{1}((\neg x\!\!:\!\!\bot)^{\bullet})

3
On Hilbert’s consistency program

Since
{\sf Con}^{S}_{\sf PA}
is a mathematically justified way to represent the consistency of
PA
in
PA
, and
{\sf Con}_{\sf PA}
is strictly stronger in
PA
than
{\sf Con}^{S}_{\sf PA}
(an easy observation, cf.
[
1
]
), Gödel’s theorem stating the unprovability of
{\sf Con}_{\sf PA}
does not answer the question of provability of
PA
-consistency in
PA
. The selector proof of the
PA
-consistency in the form
{\sf Con}^{S}_{\sf PA}
in
[
1
]
provides a mathematically justified (affirmative) answer to the question of provability of
PA
-consistency in
PA
.

For many decades, Gödel’s Second Incompleteness Theorem, yielding the unprovability of
{\sf Con}_{\sf PA}
in
PA
, has been regarded as mathematical evidence of the impossibility of Hilbert’s consistency program (cf., for example,
[
3
,
5
]
). The aforementioned analysis of differences between the consistency formula
{\sf Con}_{\sf PA}
and
PA
-consistency suggests that such cancelling of Hilbert’s consistency program has been premature.

For the recent progress in Hilbert’s program related to treating consistency as a serial property, including the disproof of the unprovability of consistency paradigm, cf.
[
1
,
2
]
.
