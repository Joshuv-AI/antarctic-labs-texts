9 C.F.R. § 82.20 — General restrictions.
Citation: us:cfr:9:82.20
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-82.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 82 (Newcastle Disease And Chlamydiosis). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following items may not be moved interstate:
(a) Live poultry infected with chlamydiosis;
(b) Dead poultry that were infected with chlamydiosis when they died, and parts of dead poultry that were infected with chlamydiosis when they died; and
(c) Offal from poultry infected with chlamydiosis.
