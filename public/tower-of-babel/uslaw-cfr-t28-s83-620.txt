28 C.F.R. § 83.620 — Cooperative agreement.
Citation: us:cfr:28:83.620
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-83.620
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 83 (Government-Wide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Grants)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Cooperative agreement means an award of financial assistance that, consistent with 31 U.S.C. 6305, is used to enter into the same kind of relationship as a grant (see definition of grant in § 83.650), except that substantial involvement is expected between the Federal agency and the recipient when carrying out the activity contemplated by the award. The term does not include cooperative research and development agreements as defined in 15 U.S.C. 3710a.
