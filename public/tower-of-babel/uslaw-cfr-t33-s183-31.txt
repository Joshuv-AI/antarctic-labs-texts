33 C.F.R. § 183.31 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:33:183.31
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-183.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 183 (Boats And Associated Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to monohull boats less than 20 feet in length except sailboats, canoes, kayaks, and inflatable boats.
