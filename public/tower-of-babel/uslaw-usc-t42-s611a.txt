42 U.S.C. § 611a — State required to provide certain information
Citation: us:usc:42:611a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section611a
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 7 (Social Security); Subchapter IV (Grants To States For Aid And Services To Needy Families With Children And For Child-Welfare Services); Part A (Block Grants To States For Temporary Assistance For Needy Families). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each State to which a grant is made under section 603 of this title shall, at least 4 times annually and upon request of the Immigration and Naturalization Service, furnish the Immigration and Naturalization Service with the name and address of, and other identifying information on, any individual who the State knows is unlawfully in the United States.
