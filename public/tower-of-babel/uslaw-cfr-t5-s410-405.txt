5 C.F.R. § 410.405 — Protection of Government interest.
Citation: us:cfr:5:410.405
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-410.405
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 410 (Training). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The head of an agency shall establish such procedures as he or she considers necessary to protect the Government's interest when employees fail to complete, or to successfully complete, training for which the agency pays the expenses.
