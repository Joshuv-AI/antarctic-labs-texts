42 C.F.R. § 512.652 — Treatment of incentive programs or add-on payments under existing Medicare payment systems.
Citation: us:cfr:42:512.652
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-512.652
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 512 (Standard Provisions For Mandatory Innovation Center Models And Specific Provisions For Certain Models). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The CJR-X Model does not replace any existing Medicare incentive programs or add-on payments. The CJR-X payments are independent of, and do not affect, any incentive programs or add-on payments under existing Medicare payment systems.
