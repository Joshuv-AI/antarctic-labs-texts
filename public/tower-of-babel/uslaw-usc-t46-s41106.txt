46 U.S.C. § 41106 — Marine terminal operators
Citation: us:usc:46:41106
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section41106
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part A (Ocean Shipping); Chapter 411 (Prohibitions And Penalties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A marine terminal operator may not—
(1)
agree with another marine terminal operator or with a common carrier to boycott, or unreasonably discriminate in the provision of terminal services to, a common carrier or ocean tramp;
(2)
give any undue or unreasonable preference or advantage or impose any undue or unreasonable prejudice or disadvantage with respect to any person; or
(3)
unreasonably refuse to deal or negotiate.
