Topological quantum electrodynamics in synthetic non-Abelian gauge fields Preprint: APS/123-QED
Qinan Huang, Bengy T. T. Wong, Zehai Pang, Xudong Zhang, Zeling Chen, Yi Yang

Abstract
Quantum electrodynamics (QED), a cornerstone framework that describes light-matter interactions rooted in Abelian symmetries, renders the harnessing of synthetic non-Abelian gauge fields as a fundamental yet uncharted frontier. Here, we develop a general theory of light-matter interaction of quantum emitters embedded in non-Abelian photonic lattices. Based on analytical solutions to the non-Abelian Landau dressed states beyond the continuum limit, we reveal chiral photon emission and vortices with emergent nonreciprocity enabled by selective coupling between emitters and spin-momentum-locked bands. When coexisting with Abelian and non-Abelian magnetic fields, emitters hybridize with Landau dressed orbits to form spin-polarized, squeezed Landau polaritons that carry quantized angular momenta, with Rabi frequencies tunable via Landau levels and pseudospin interactions. Multi-emitter dynamics further exhibit collective phenomena governed by real-space staggered phases induced by nonsymmorphic crystalline symmetry. These results bridge non-Abelian physics with quantum optics, and establish non-Abelian gauge fields as a versatile tool for synthesizing topological quantum optical states, angular momentum transfer, and controlling photon-mediated correlations in QED systems, relevant for applications in quantum simulations and chiral quantum optical networks.

When we turn on the Abelian field with
\mathcal{B}\neq 0

Next, we turn to the dynamics and interactions of multi-emitter collective systems. Again, we work on pure non-Abelian fields

The interaction among multiple emitters is mediated by photon exchanges, and, therefore, can be strongly shaped by the crystalline symmetry of the photonic bath.
We identify a nonsymmorphic symmetry
C(\mathbf{k})=\left[\cos\left(\theta(\mathbf{k})\right),-\mathrm{i}\sin\left(\theta(\mathbf{k})\right);\mathrm{i}\sin\left(\theta(\mathbf{k})\right),\cos\left(\theta(\mathbf{k})\right)\right]

Furthermore, the phase between the photon pseudospin components transforms as
\theta(\mathbf{k}+\boldsymbol{\pi})=\theta(\mathbf{k})+\boldsymbol{\pi}

\mathbf{k}\to\mathbf{k}+\boldsymbol{\pi}

Specifically at the vanishing detuning
\Delta=0

\omega_{\pm}(\mathbf{k})=\omega_{\mp}(\mathbf{k}+\boldsymbol{\pi})=0

VI
Summary

Our work establishes non-Abelian gauge fields as a transformative paradigm in quantum electrodynamics, revealing their unique capacity to engineer light-matter interactions with topological and symmetry-protected features.
By coupling quantum emitters to photonic bath threaded by non-Abelian flux, we uncovered chiral photon emission with spin-momentum-locked vortices, squeezing and tuning of spin-polarized Landau polaritons, and symmetry-enhanced quantum control of collective emitter dynamics.
The proposed effects could be realized in state-of-the-art platforms, such as semiconductor quantum dots or solid-state defects coupled to the degeneracy of transverse electric and transverse magnetic bands of exciton polaritons subject to external magnetic fields
Gianfrate et al. 2020
and superconducting qubits embedded in topological microwave waveguide networks
Mezzacapo et al. 2015
;
Kim et al. 2021
.
Potential applications of our work include emitter-mediated integrated tunable nonreciprocity, deterministic selection and transfer of quantized angular momenta between light and matter, and quantum control of emitter properties via crystalline symmetries of the bath.
