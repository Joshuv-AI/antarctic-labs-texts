40 C.F.R. § 790.97 — Hearing procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:40:790.97
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-790.97
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 790 (Procedures Governing Testing Consent Agreements And Test Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Hearing requests must be submitted using the method specified in § 790.5(b). Such requests must include the applicant's basis for appealing EPA's decision.
(b) If more than one applicant has requested a hearing on similar grounds, all of those appeals will be considered at the same hearing unless confidentiality claims preclude a joint hearing.
(c) EPA will notify each applicant of EPA's decision within 60 days after the hearing.
