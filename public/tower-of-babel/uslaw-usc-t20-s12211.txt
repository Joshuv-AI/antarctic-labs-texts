20 U.S.C. § 1221–1 — National policy with respect to equal educational opportunity
Citation: us:usc:20:1221–1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1221–1
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 31 (General Provisions Concerning Education). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Recognizing that the Nation’s economic, political, and social security require a well-educated citizenry, the Congress (1) reaffirms, as a matter of high priority, the Nation’s goal of equal educational opportunity, and (2) declares it to be the policy of the United States of America that every citizen is entitled to an education to meet his or her full potential without financial barriers.
