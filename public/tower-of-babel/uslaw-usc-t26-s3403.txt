26 U.S.C. § 3403 — Liability for tax
Citation: us:usc:26:3403
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section3403
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 24 (Collection Of Income Tax At Source On Wages). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The employer shall be liable for the payment of the tax required to be deducted and withheld under this chapter, and shall not be liable to any person for the amount of any such payment.
