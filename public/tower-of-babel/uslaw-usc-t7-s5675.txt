7 U.S.C. § 5675 — Technical assistance in trade negotiations
Citation: us:usc:7:5675
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section5675
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 87 (Export Promotion); Subchapter IV (General Provisions); Part B (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall provide technical services to the United States Trade Representative on matters pertaining to agricultural trade and with respect to international negotiations on issues related to agricultural trade.
