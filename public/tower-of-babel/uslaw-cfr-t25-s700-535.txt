25 C.F.R. § 700.535 — Nepotism.
Citation: us:cfr:25:700.535
Creator: The Office Of Navajo And Hopi Indian Relocation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-700.535
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 700 (Commission Operations And Relocation Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee may not appoint or advocate the appointment to any position under his or her control, any individual who is a relative of the employee. No employee shall supervise a member of his or her own family except in emergency situations.
