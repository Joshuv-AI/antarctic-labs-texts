14 C.F.R. § 91.857 — Stage 2 operations outside of the 48 contiguous United States.
Citation: us:cfr:14:91.857
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-91.857
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 91 (General Operating And Flight Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An operator of a Stage 2 airplane that is operating only between points outside the contiguous United States on or after November 5, 1990, must include in its operations specifications a statement that such airplane may not be used to provide air transportation to or from any airport in the contiguous United States.
