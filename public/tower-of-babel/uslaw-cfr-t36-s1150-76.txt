36 C.F.R. § 1150.76 — Exceptions.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1150.76
Creator: Architectural And Transportation Barriers Compliance Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1150.76
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1150 (Practice And Procedures For Compliance Hearings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Exceptions to rulings of the judge are unnecessary. It is sufficient that a party at the time the ruling of the judge is sought, makes known the action which he/she desires the judge to take, or his/her objection to an action taken, and his/her grounds for it.
