28 C.F.R. § 76.14 — Separation of functions.
Citation: us:cfr:28:76.14
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-76.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 76 (Rules Of Procedure For Assessment Of Civil Penalties For Possession Of Certain Controlled Substances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee or an agent of the Department who is or was engaged in investigative or prosecutive functions for or on behalf of the United States in a case may not participate in the decision of that case.
