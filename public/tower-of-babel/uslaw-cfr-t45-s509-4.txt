45 C.F.R. § 509.4 — Acknowledgment and numbering.
Citation: us:cfr:45:509.4
Creator: Foreign Claims Settlement Commission Of The United States, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-509.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 509 (Filing Of Claims And Procedures Therefor). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission will acknowledge the receipt of a claim in writing and will notify the claimant of the claim number assigned to it, which number must be used on all further correspondence and papers filed with regard to the claim.
