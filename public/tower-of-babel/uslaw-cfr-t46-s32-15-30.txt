46 C.F.R. § 32.15-30 — -30 Radar—T/OC.
Citation: us:cfr:46:32.15-30
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-32.15-30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 32 (Special Equipment, Machinery, And Hull Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All tankships of 1,600 gross tons and over in ocean or coastwise service must be fitted with a marine radar system for surface navigation. Facilities for plotting radar readings must be provided on the bridge.
