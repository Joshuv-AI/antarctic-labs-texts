48 C.F.R. § 1842.7202 — 1842.7202 Contract clause.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1842 (Contract Administration And Audit Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 1852.242-73, NASA Contractor Financial Management Reporting, in solicitations and contracts when any of the NASA Form 533 series of reports is required from the contractor.
