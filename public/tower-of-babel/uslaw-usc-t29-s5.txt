29 U.S.C. § 5 — Bulletin as to labor conditions
Citation: us:usc:29:5
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section5
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 1 (Labor Statistics); Subchapter I (Bureau Of Labor Statistics). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commissioner of Labor Statistics is authorized to prepare and publish a bulletin of the Bureau of Labor Statistics, as to the condition of labor in this and other countries, condensations of State and foreign labor reports, facts as to conditions of employment, and such other facts as may be deemed of value to the industrial interests of the country.
