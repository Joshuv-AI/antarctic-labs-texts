Change of weights operations for triangulated ( φ , Γ ) (\varphi,\Gamma) -modules
Zichuan Wang

Abstract
. Zhixiang Wu has shown the existence of “change of weights” operation on ( φ , Γ ) (\varphi,\Gamma) -modules in families, [ 28 , Prop. 3.16] . We interpret it in the trianguline case as pullbacks with a discussion on related stacks. Finally, we prove that it intertwines well with translation functors via a 1-1 correspondence defined by Yiwen Ding [ 13 ] in the non-critical crystabelline case.

&1\end{pmatrix}

(\varphi,\Gamma_{K})
-modules
\gr_{m}(\Fil^{\bullet}(D))\cong{\mathcal{R}}_{K,A}(\delta_{m})\hat{\otimes}_{A}{\mathcal{L}}_{m}
for some line bundles
{\mathcal{L}}_{m}
over
A
.
There is an finite admissible cover
\{\Sp(A_{l})\}_{l=1}^{r}
of
\Sp(A)
trivializing them.
Since

commutes with flat base change by
[

, Theorem 4.4.3(2)]
, passing to
\Sp(A_{l})
we may assume that the line bundles
{\mathcal{L}}_{m}
are all trivial.

After the reductions above, we show that

p_{i,\sigma}:\Ext^{1}(D^{i},D_{n-i})\cong H^{1}({D^{i}}^{\vee}\otimes D_{n-i})\to\Ext^{1}(t_{\sigma}D^{i},D_{n-i})\cong H^{1}(t_{\sigma}^{-1}({D^{i}}^{\vee}\otimes D_{n-i}))

is bijective, where we have by assumption that

D_{n-i}=({\mathcal{R}}_{K,A}(\delta_{1})-\dots-{\mathcal{R}}_{K,A}(\delta_{n-i}))\quad\text{ and }\quad D^{i}=({\mathcal{R}}_{K,A}(\delta_{n-i+1})-\dots-{\mathcal{R}}_{K,A}(\delta_{n})).

Thus, putting
M:={D^{i}}^{\vee}\otimes D_{n-i}
, we see that
M
is trianguline over
{\mathcal{R}}_{K,A}
with parameters

\left\{\delta_{j}\delta_{k}^{-1}\middle|1\leq j\leq n-i\text{ and }n-i+1\leq k\leq n\right\}.

Note that
\mathrm{wt}_{\sigma}(\delta_{j}\delta_{k}^{-1})\neq 1
for these parameters by the assumption.
By the short exact sequence

0\to M\to t_{\sigma}^{-1}M\to t_{\sigma}^{-1}M/M\to 0

it is enough to establish the vanishing of
H^{i}(t_{\sigma}^{-1}M/M)
as coherent sheaves for
i=0,1,2
.

This follows from a dévissage argument on the above triangulation on
M
, using the proof of Lemma
2.9
(i).
Indeed, we know that
M=({\mathcal{R}}_{K,A}(\eta_{1})-\dots-{\mathcal{R}}_{K,A}(\eta_{m}))
of parameters
\eta_{s}
whose weights are not
1
at all
x\in\Sp(A)
, so that

t_{\sigma}^{-1}M/M\cong M/t_{\sigma}=({\mathcal{R}}_{K,A}(\eta_{1})/t_{\sigma}-\dots-{\mathcal{R}}_{K,A}(\eta_{m})/t_{\sigma})

We induct on the rank of
M
to show the vanishing of cohomologies of
M/t_{\sigma}
.
The base case where
m=1
was shown in the proof of Lemma
2.9
(i).
For

, we have

\dots\to H^{i}({\mathcal{R}}_{K,A}(\eta_{1})/t_{\sigma})\to H^{i}(M/t_{\sigma})\to H^{i}((M/{\mathcal{R}}_{K,A}(\eta_{1}))/t_{\sigma})\to\dots

from which we know

by inductive hypothesis.
∎

Proposition 3.4
.

For trianguline
(\varphi,\Gamma_{K})
-module
(D,\Fil^{\bullet}(D))
of parameters
\delta=(\delta_{i})_{1\leq i\leq n}\in T^{n}_{\circ}(E)
over
{\mathcal{R}}_{K,E}

(a).

\Fil^{n-i}(D)

(b).

Then, for each

\Fil^{j}(D)
is the unique saturated rank

(\varphi,\Gamma_{K})
-submodule of

.

Proof.

Note that similar to Remark
2.11
, (a) and (b) imply that
(D,\Fil^{\bullet}(D))
is non-split.

We proceed by induction.
Let
D^{\prime}
be a saturated (
\varphi,\Gamma_{K}
)-submodule of
D
.
We claim that
D^{\prime}
contains
\Fil^{1}(D)
.
Indeed, let
m
be the largest integer such that
\Fil^{m-1}(D)\cap D^{\prime}=0
.
Then

D^{\prime}\cap\Fil^{m}(D)
is an
{\mathcal{R}}_{K,E}
-submodule of
D^{\prime}
stable under the
(\varphi,\Gamma_{K})
-action such that the quotient
D^{\prime}/(D^{\prime}\cap\Fil^{m}(D))
is torsion-free as it injects into

.
As

is a product of Bézout domains on which

acts transitively, it follows from the torsion-freeness that

is free over
{\mathcal{R}}_{K,E}
(cf.
[
2
, §2.6]
) and hence is a
(\varphi,\Gamma_{K})
-module.

Now if
m>1
, then
D^{\prime}\cap\Fil^{m}(D)
is a nonzero
(\varphi,\Gamma_{K})
-submodule of
\Fil^{m}(D)
injecting to the quotient
\Fil^{i}(D)/\Fil^{m-1}(D)\cong{\mathcal{R}}_{K,E}(\delta_{m})
, which is of the form

for some
{\bf k}\in{\mathbb{N}}^{[K:{\mathbb{Q}}_{p}]}
by Lemma
2.7
(iii).
Then,
\Fil^{m}(D)
contains the split
(\varphi,\Gamma_{K})
-submodule

\Fil^{m-1}(D)\oplus(D^{\prime}\cap\Fil^{m}(D))\cong\Fil^{m-1}(D)\oplus t^{\bf k}{\mathcal{R}}(\delta_{m})

which is the image of
\Fil^{m}(D)
under the pullback map

p_{\bf k}:\Ext^{1}(\delta_{m},\Fil^{m-1}(D))\to\Ext^{1}(t^{\bf k}\delta_{m},\Fil^{m-1}(D))

which is bijective by Theorem

, contrary to our assumptions (a) and (b).
Hence,

.
Since

is nonzero and saturated in

, it has to be

In particular,
\Fil^{1}(D)
is the unique saturated rank
1

(\varphi,\Gamma_{K})
-submodule of
D
.
If
n-i>1
, we pass to the quotient by
\Fil^{1}(D)
and claim that every saturated
(\varphi,\Gamma_{K})
-submodule of

contains
\Fil^{2}(D)/\Fil^{1}(D)
.
By (a) and (b),

with its induced filtration is non-split and satisfies the analogous (a) and (b), so the argument above works.
We can iterate this argument until

, hence the conclusion.
∎

Remark 3.6

One cannot expect uniqueness result for non-split
