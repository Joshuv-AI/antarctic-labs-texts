7 C.F.R. § 1924.49 — State supplements.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1924.49
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Rural Business-Cooperative Service, And Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1924.49
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1924 (Construction And Repair). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

State Supplements or policies will not be issued or adopted to either supplement or set requirements different from those of this subpart, unless specifically authorized in this subpart, without prior written approval of the National Office.
