9 C.F.R. § 325.6 — Shipment of paunches between official establishments under official seal; certificate.
Citation: us:cfr:9:325.6
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-325.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 325 (Transportation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Cattle and sheep paunches which have been made clean and from which the mucous membrane has not been removed may be transported from one official establishment to another official establishment for further processing, only under an official seal of the Department as prescribed in § 312.5(a) of this subchapter.
