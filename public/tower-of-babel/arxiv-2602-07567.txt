A Weighted Regularity Criterion for Suitable Weak Solutions of Incompressible Non-Newtonian Fluids
Jae-Myoung Kim

Abstract
It establishes a regularity criterion for non-Newtonian fluids in ℝ 3 \mathbb{R}^{3} in terms of the weighted gradient of the velocity field, based on the Caffarelli–Kohn–Nirenberg inequality.

Introduction

In this paper, we study the non-Newtonian fluid of shear-thinning type:

\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\displaystyle u_{t}-\nabla\cdot S+(u\cdot\nabla)u+\nabla\pi=0,\\
\\
\displaystyle\text{div}\ u=0,\end{array}\right.\quad\mbox{ in }\,\,Q_{T}:={\mathbb{R}}^{3}\times(0,\,T),

(1.1)

where
u:{\mathbb{R}}^{3}\times(0,\,T)\rightarrow{\mathbb{R}}^{3}
is the flow velocity
vector and
\pi:{\mathbb{R}}^{3}\times(0,\,T)\rightarrow{\mathbb{R}}
is the pressure.
Also,
S=(S_{ij})_{i,j=1,2,3}
is the stress tensor depending on the
deformation tensor
Du=(\nabla u+\nabla u^{T})/2
. We consider
the initial value problem of (
1.1
), which requires initial
conditions

u(x,0)=u_{0}(x).

(1.2)

We assume that the initial data
u_{0}(x)\in L^{2}(\Omega)
hold the
incompressibility, i.e.
\text{div}\ u_{0}(x)=0
. To motivate the
conditions on the stress tensor
S
is called ”power law fluids”. This paper deals with the following forms

S(Du)=(\mu_{0}+\mu_{1}|Du|^{q-2})Du,\quad 1<q<+\infty.

(1.3)

where
\mu_{0}>0
and
\mu_{1}>0
are constants (see e.g.
[
8
]
,

[
3
]
).

We review the known results regarding existence of solutions related to our result.
For
\mu_{0}\geq 0
and
\mu_{1}>0
, the existence of weak
solutions for
\frac{3n+2}{n+2}\leq q
was firstly established in
[
8
]
, and later,
in
[
6
]
, the range of
q
was extended up to
\frac{2n}{n+2}<q
.
If
\mu_{0}>0
and
\frac{n+2}{2}\leq q
, it was shown that the weak solution is
unique (see e.g.
[
10
]
and compare to
[
9
]
).
Málek et.al, in
[
10
]
proved that the unique strong
solution exists globally in time for
q\geq\frac{3n+2}{n+2}
in
the case of periodic domains of
{\mathbb{R}}^{n}
,
n\geq 3
(see
[
11
]
for the case of the whole space).

We report the known results related to the regularity criteria of a weak solution in Navier-stokes equations of non-Newontian type. For a thinning fluid, that is,
\frac{8}{5}<p<2
, Bae et al.
[
2
]
obtained the
regularity criterion of Serrin type for Ostwald-de Waele flows (i.e. a class of shear thinning fluids):

\left\|u\right\|_{L^{\alpha,\beta}_{x,t}(Q_{T})}:=\left\|\left\|u\right\|_{L^{\alpha}({\mathbb{R}}^{3})}\right\|_{L^{\beta}(0,T)}<\infty,\quad\frac{3}{\alpha}+\frac{5p-6}{2\beta}\leq\frac{5p-8}{2},\quad\alpha>\frac{6}{5p-8}.

For a point
z=(x,t)\in{\mathbb{R}}^{3}\times(0,T)
, we denote

B_{\rho}(x)=\{y\in{\mathbb{R}}^{3}:\left|y-x\right|<\rho\}
,

Q_{\rho}(z)=B_{\rho}(x)\times(t-\rho^{2},t),\quad\rho<\sqrt{t}.

On the other hand, the authors
[
7
]
showed that the global-in-time existence of a solution to the problem (
1.1
) considered such that
u\in C^{1,\alpha}({\mathbb{T}}^{2}\times(0,T))
,
0<\alpha<1
, provided
q>\frac{4}{3}
and some conditions on the function the external force
f
are fulfilled.

In the present paper, we give regularity conditions of the weighted type
for a weak solution to the 3D the non-Newtonian fluid of shear-thinning type which is extensions of
results in
[
13
]
. For a proof, we use the energy estimate based on the
Caffarelli-Kohn-Nirenberg and Stein’s inequalities. After
then, using
\epsilon
-regularity criteria for the suitable weak solution, we
show a weak solution is semi-regular.

Next, we are to state the second part of our main result.

Theorem 1.1

Let
q>2
. Assume that
u_{0}\in H^{1}({\mathbb{R}}^{3})
with
\nabla\cdot u_{0}=0
satisfies

\sup_{x_{0}\in{\mathbb{R}}^{3}}\|x-x_{0}|^{-\frac{1}{2}}u_{0}\|_{L^{2}}<\infty,

and
if a weak solution

u
satisfies

\sup_{x_{0}\in{\mathbb{R}}^{3}}\||x-x_{0}|^{\beta}\nabla u\|_{L_{x,t}^{r,s}}<\infty,\quad\sup_{x_{0}\in R^{3}}\int_{0}^{T}\|x-x_{0}|^{-\frac{4}{3}}|\nabla u(\cdot,\tau)|^{q-1}\|^{\frac{3}{2}}_{L^{\frac{3}{2}}}\,d\tau<\infty,

with

\frac{3}{r}+\frac{5q-6}{2s}=\frac{5q-8}{2}+1-\beta,\quad 1<s<\infty,

\frac{3}{\frac{5q-8}{2}+1-\beta}<r<\infty,\quad-1\leq\beta<\frac{5q-6}{2}.

Then the weak solution
u
is semi-regular up to time
T>0
.

(3)

(Generalized energy inequality)
For each real-valued function
