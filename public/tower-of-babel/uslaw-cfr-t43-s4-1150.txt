43 C.F.R. § 4.1150 — Who may file.
Citation: us:cfr:43:4.1150
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4.1150
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4 (Department Of The Interior Hearings And Appeals Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person charged with a civil penalty may file a petition for review of a proposed assessment of that penalty with DCHD, 801 North Quincy Street, Arlington, Va. 22203.
