21 C.F.R. § 330.3 — Imprinting of solid oral dosage form drug products.
Citation: us:cfr:21:330.3
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-330.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 330 (Over-The-Counter (Otc) Human Drugs Which Are Generally Recognized As Safe And Effective And Not Misbranded). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A requirement to imprint an identification code on solid oral dosage form drug products is set forth under part 206 of this chapter.
