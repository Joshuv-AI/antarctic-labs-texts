38 U.S.C. § 3536 — Specialized vocational training courses
Citation: us:usc:38:3536
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section3536
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part III (Readjustment And Related Benefits); Chapter 35 (Survivors’ And Dependents’ Educational Assistance); Subchapter IV (Payments To Eligible Persons). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may approve a specialized course of vocational training leading to a predetermined vocational objective for the enrollment of an eligible person under this subchapter if the Secretary finds that such course, either alone or when combined with other courses, constitutes a program of education which is suitable for that person and is required because of a mental or physical handicap.
