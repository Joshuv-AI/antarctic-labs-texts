34 C.F.R. § 642.12 — What activities may a project conduct?
Citation: us:cfr:34:642.12
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-642.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 642 (Training Program For Federal Trio Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Training program project may include on-site training, on-line training, conferences, internships, seminars, workshops, and the publication of manuals designed to improve the operations of Federal TRIO program projects.
