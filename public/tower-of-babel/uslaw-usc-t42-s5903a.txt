42 U.S.C. § 5903a — Nonduplication of programs, projects, and research facilities
Citation: us:usc:42:5903a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section5903a
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 74 (Nonnuclear Energy Research And Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall coordinate nonnuclear programs of the Department of Energy with the heads of relevant Federal agencies in order to minimize unnecessary duplication of programs, projects, and research facilities.
