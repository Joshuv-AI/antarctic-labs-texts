The G-signature theorem on Witt spaces
Markus Banagl, Eric Leichtnam, Paolo Piazza

Abstract
. Let G G be a compact Lie group and let X X be an oriented Witt G-pseudomanifold. Using intersection cohomology it is possible to define Sign ⁡ ( G , X ) ∈ R ⁡ ( G ) {\rm Sign}(G,X)\in R(G) , the G G -signature of X X . Let g ∈ G g\in G . Assuming that the inclusion of the fixed point set associated to g g is normally non-singular, we prove a formula for Sign ⁡ ( g , X ) {\rm Sign}(g,X) , the G G -signature of X X computed at g g , thus extending to Witt G G -pseudomanifolds the fundamental result proved by Atiyah, Segal and Singer on smooth compact G G -manifolds. Along the way, we give a detailed study of the fixed point set of a Thom-Mather G G -space X X and our main result in this direction is a sufficient condition ensuring that the fixed point set X G X^{G} is included in X X in a normally non-singular manner. This latter result provides many examples where our formula applies.

Introduction

Let

Using Poincaré duality it is possible to define out of the cohomology of

and the G-signature of

{\rm Sign}(G,X):=[H^{+}]-[H^{-}]\in R(G)\,.

Let us now fix a G-invariant riemannian metric on

We can then define the signature operator

Making crucial use of the Hodge theorem it is possible to prove that

{\rm Sign}(G,X)={\rm ind}_{G}(D^{{\rm sign},+}):=[{\rm Ker}(D^{{\rm sign},+})]-[{\rm Ker}(D^{{\rm sign},-})]\in R(G)\,.

For

{\rm Sign}(g,X):={\rm tr}(g_{*}|_{H^{+}})-{\rm tr}(g_{*}|_{H^{-}})\,.

This is of course also equal to the equivariant index of the signature operator computed at

{\rm ind}_{G}(D^{{\rm sign},+},g):={\rm tr}(g_{*}|_{{\rm Ker}(D^{{\rm sign},+})})-{\rm tr}(g_{*}|_{{\rm Ker}(D^{{\rm sign},})})

The proof of the Atiyah-Segal-Singer

•

Recall that a pseudomanifold is called a
Witt pseudomanifold
, if the (lower, and hence upper)
middle perversity intersection chain sheaf complex is Verdier self-dual.
Note that Witt pseudomanifolds are orientable.
Equivalently, we require that the pseudomanifold is orientable and the links of odd-codimensional strata of any topological
stratification have vanishing (lower) middle-perversity intersection
homology in their middle degree. Such spaces have been introduced by

[

]
; see also Goresky-MacPherson
[
GM83

For example, any pure-dimensional complex algebraic variety is a Witt pseudomanifold.

Let now
X^{2\ell}
be a smoothly stratified Witt
G

Using Poincaré duality in intersection cohomology
one can define the

-signature

and thanks to the Hodge theorem on Witt spaces, due to Cheeger

Wedge metrics are iteratively locally on the regular part modelled on
metrics of the form

where

Wedge metrics are also known as

The main goal of this article is to prove a formula for
{\rm Sign}(g,X)
,

Our formula applies to the case in which

is equivariantly strongly normally non-singularly included in

that the fixed point set
X^{g}
admits an equivariant normal vector bundle which is, in addition,

Under this crucial assumption we express

associated normal bundle (the same characteristic classes as in Atiyah-Segal-Singer).
The proof must of course proceed differently with respect to Atiyah-Segal-Singer, as on Witt spaces
we have neither the topological G-index, as a homomorphism

,
nor its equality with an analytic index. Instead we follow an alternative route to the original result,

Precisely for these reasons it can be extended to the Witt case, assuming that

in Rosenberg’s argument.

Theorem 1.1

Let

Assume that the inclusion

For instance, as we point out in Proposition

We can also bring in, as in

{\rm Sign}\,(g,X)\,=\,\sum_{F\in\mathcal{C}}\langle C(E_{F}(-1))\prod_{j=1}^{k}(-1)^{s_{j}}(i\tan(\theta_{j}/2))^{-s_{j}}\mathcal{M}^{\theta_{j}}(E_{F}(e^{i\theta_{j}}))\;;\;\mathcal{L}_{*}(F)\,\rangle

G-actions and fixed point sets

We begin this Section by giving the notion of Thom-Mather

-stratified space, with

Let
(X,\mathcal{S},\mathcal{T})

Definition 2.1
.

Let

(ii)

The following result is due to Pflaum and Wilkin, see
[

Theorem 2.2

Let

-manifold, where

This implies that

An inclusion

5.
The equivariant signature

Let

be a compact Lie group and

Recall that by definition this means that

To define a

Let

Let

Suppose that
