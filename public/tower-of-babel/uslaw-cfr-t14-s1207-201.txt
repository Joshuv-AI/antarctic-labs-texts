14 C.F.R. § 1207.201 — Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1207.201
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1207.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1207 (Standards Of Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart provides guidance to former NASA government employees who are subject to the restrictions of Title V of the Ethics of Government Act of 1978, as amended, and who want to communicate scientific or technical information to NASA.
