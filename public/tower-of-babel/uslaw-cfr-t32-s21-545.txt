32 C.F.R. § 21.545 — Must DoD Components report every obligation to the DAADS?
Citation: us:cfr:32:21.545
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-21.545
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 21 (DoD GRANTS AND AGREEMENTS—GENERAL MATTERS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, DoD Components' central points must collect and report the data required by the DD Form 2566 for each individual action that involves the obligation or deobligation of Federal funds for an award that is subject to 31 U.S.C. chapter 61.
