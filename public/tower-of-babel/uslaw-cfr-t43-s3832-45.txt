43 C.F.R. § 3832.45 — How do I obtain any minerals that I discover within my tunnel site?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3832.45
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3832.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3832 (Locating Mining Claims Or Sites). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Even if you have located the tunnel site, you must separately locate a lode claim to acquire the possessory right to a blind vein, lode, or ledge you have discovered within the boundaries of the tunnel site sidelines.
(b) The date of location of your lode claim is retroactive to the date of location of your tunnel site.
