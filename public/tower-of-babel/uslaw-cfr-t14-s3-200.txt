14 C.F.R. § 3.200 — Effect of Transportation Security Administration notification on a certificate or any part of a certificate held by an individual.
Citation: us:cfr:14:3.200
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-3.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 3 (General Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the Transportation Security Administration (TSA) notifies the FAA that an individual holding a certificate or part of a certificate issued by the FAA poses, or is suspected of posing, a risk of air piracy or terrorism or a threat to airline or passenger safety, the FAA will issue an order amending, modifying, suspending, or revoking any certificate or part of a certificate issued by the FAA.
