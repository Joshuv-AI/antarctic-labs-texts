31 U.S.C. § 303 — Bureau of Engraving and Printing
Citation: us:usc:31:303
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section303
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 3 (Department Of The Treasury); Subchapter I (Organization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Bureau of Engraving and Printing is a bureau in the Department of the Treasury.
(b)
The head of the Bureau is the Director of the Bureau of Engraving and Printing appointed by the Secretary of the Treasury. The Director—
(1)
shall carry out duties and powers prescribed by the Secretary; and
(2)
reports directly to the Secretary.
