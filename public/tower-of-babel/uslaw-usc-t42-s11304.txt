42 U.S.C. § 11304 — Evaluation by Comptroller General
Citation: us:usc:42:11304
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section11304
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 119 (Homeless Assistance); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Comptroller General of the United States may evaluate the disbursement and use of the amounts made available by appropriation Acts under the authorizations in subchapters III and IV.
