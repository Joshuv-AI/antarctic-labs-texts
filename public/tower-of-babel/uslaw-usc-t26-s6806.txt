26 U.S.C. § 6806 — Occupational tax stamps
Citation: us:usc:26:6806
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section6806
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 69 (General Provisions Relating To Stamps). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Every person engaged in any business, avocation, or employment, who is thereby made liable to a special tax (other than a special tax under subchapter B of chapter 35, under subchapter B of chapter 36, or under subtitle E) shall place and keep conspicuously in his establishment or place of business all stamps denoting payment of such special tax.
