25 C.F.R. § 700.197 — Basic eligibility requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:25:700.197
Creator: The Office Of Navajo And Hopi Indian Relocation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-700.197
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 700 (Commission Operations And Relocation Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A certified eligible head of household is eligible for reimbursement of expenses that are incidental to the purchase of a replacement dwelling, as provided in § 700.199 hereof.
