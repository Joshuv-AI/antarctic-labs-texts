33 U.S.C. § 677 — Limitation as to quantity of debris washed away
Citation: us:usc:33:677
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section677
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 14 (California Debris Commission). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

At no time shall any more debris be permitted to be washed away from any hydraulic mine or mines situated on the tributaries of said rivers and the respective branches of each, worked under the provisions of this chapter, than can be impounded within the restraining works erected.
