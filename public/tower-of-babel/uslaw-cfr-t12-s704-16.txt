12 C.F.R. § 704.16 — Contracts/written agreements.
Citation: us:cfr:12:704.16
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-704.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 704 (Corporate Credit Unions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Services, facilities, personnel, or equipment shared with any party shall be supported by a written contract, with the duties and responsibilities of each party specified and the allocation of service fee/expenses fully supported and documented.
