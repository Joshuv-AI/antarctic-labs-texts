48 C.F.R. § 1533.203 — 1533.203 Applicability.

Creator: Chapter 15—Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1533 (Protests, Disputes And Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Civilian Board of Contract Appeals (CBCA) will hear appeals from final decisions of EPA Contracting Officers issued pursuant to the Contracts Disputes Act. The rules and regulations of the CBCA appear in 48 CFR chapter 61.
