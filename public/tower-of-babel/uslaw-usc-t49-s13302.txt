49 U.S.C. § 13302 — Intervention
Citation: us:usc:49:13302
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section13302
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part B (Motor Carriers, Water Carriers, Brokers, And Freight Forwarders); Chapter 133 (Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Under regulations of the Secretary, reasonable notice of, and an opportunity to intervene and participate in, a proceeding under this part related to transportation subject to jurisdiction under subchapter I of chapter 135 shall be given to interested persons.
