Overview of This Information Collection

    Abstract: Title XI of the Organized Crime Control Act of 1970 (the 
Act) and its implementing regulations at 27 CFR part 555 require that 
persons who want to engage in the business of manufacturing, importing, 
or dealing in explosives, or using them, must file an application with 
ATF for an explosives license/permit. This requirement also mandates 
that the applicant include specific information about responsible 
persons (RPs) and other employees who are authorized to handle 
explosives in the course of their duties (employee possessors, EPs). 
Further, 18 U.S.C. 843(h) requires ATF to conduct background checks on 
the applicant and any RPs and EPs. These requirements help ensure 
compliance with the provisions of 18 U.S.C. 842(i), which prohibits 
certain persons from possessing explosive materials. This information 
collection covers the requirement to submit information on EPs. All 
persons holding ATF explosives licenses/permits must report to ATF 
identifying information about their EPs and any subsequent changes to 
their EPs. ATF's regulations establish that applicants must use ATF 
Form 5400.28, Explosives Employee Possessor Questionnaire (EPQ) for 
this purpose. Doing so helps ensure that respondents provide all the 
necessary information to conduct the EP background checks and ensure 
they are not prohibited from operations involving explosives.
    Type of information collection: revising a previously approved 
collection.
    Title of the form/collection: Explosives Employee Possessor 
Questionnaire (EPQ).
    Agency form number, if any, and the applicable component of the 
Department sponsoring the collection: Form number: ATF Form 5400.28. 
Component: Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms, and Explosives.
    Affected public who will be asked or required to respond, as well 
as the obligation to respond: Affected public: business or other for-
profit, individuals or households. Obligation to respond: required to 
obtain a benefit.
    Estimate of the total number of respondents and the amount of time 
estimated for an average respondent to respond: There are an estimated 
80,661 respondents associated with this collection per year, and it 
will take each respondent an average of approximately 20 minutes (0.33 
hours) per response to complete their responses.
    Estimate of the total annual burden (in hours) associated with the 
collection: The estimated annual public burden associated with this 
collection is 26,860 hours, which is equal to 80,661 (total 
respondents) * 1 (# of responses per respondent) * 0.33 hours.
    Estimate of the total annual other cost burden associated with the 
collection, if applicable: 0; there are no costs.

Revisions to This Information Collection

    As part of this ICR renewal, ATF is making a minor change to the 
title of the IC to align with the way ATF and industry have been 
referring to it, from ``List of Responsible Persons'' to ``Explosives 
Responsible Person Questionnaire (RPQ).'' ATF has also updated the 
digitally fillable and signable form to include an email address and 
instructions for submitting electronically, and made minor plain 
writing edits throughout. Finally, the number of respondents has 
decreased during the past three years, from 83,125 in 2023 to 80,661 
now, a decrease of 2,464 respondents. The decrease in respondents 
resulted in a corresponding decrease in the total annual burden hours, 
from 27,708 to 26,860, a decrease of 848 hours.
