49 U.S.C. § 13504 — Exempt motor carrier transportation entirely in one State
Citation: us:usc:49:13504
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section13504
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part B (Motor Carriers, Water Carriers, Brokers, And Freight Forwarders); Chapter 135 (Jurisdiction); Subchapter I (Motor Carrier Transportation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Neither the Secretary nor the Board has jurisdiction under this subchapter over transportation, except transportation of household goods, by a motor carrier operating solely within the State of Hawaii. The State of Hawaii may regulate transportation exempt from jurisdiction under this section and, to the extent provided by a motor carrier operating solely within the State of Hawaii, transportation exempt under section 13503 of this title.
