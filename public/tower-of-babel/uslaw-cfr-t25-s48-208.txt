25 C.F.R. § 48.208 — How does a Bureau-operated school process donated funds?
Citation: us:cfr:25:48.208
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-48.208
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 48 (Leases Of Land Or Facilities Of Bureau-Operated Schools And Fundraising Activities At Bureau-Operated Schools). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Bureau will deposit all funds received as donations into the designated Treasury account. Once the Bureau deposits the funds, the Bureau will work with the Bureau-operated school to make the funds available for school purposes.
