48 C.F.R. § 6103.306 — 6103.306 Decisions [Rule 306].
Citation: us:cfr:48:6103.306
Creator: Chapter 61—Civilian Board Of Contract Appeals, General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-6103.306
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 6103 (Transportation Rate Cases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The judge will issue a written decision based upon the record, which includes submissions by the claimant, the Audit Division, and the agency, and information provided during conferences. The Board will provide the claimant, the Audit Division, and the agency a copy of the decision. In addition, all Board decisions are posted weekly on the Board's website.
