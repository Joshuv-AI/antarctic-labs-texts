20 C.F.R. § 404.270 — Cost-of-living increases.
Citation: us:cfr:20:404.270
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-404.270
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 404 (Federal Old-Age, Survivors And Disability Insurance (1950- )). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Your primary insurance amount may be automatically increased each December so it keeps up with rises in the cost of living. These automatic increases also apply to other benefit amounts, as described in § 404.271.
