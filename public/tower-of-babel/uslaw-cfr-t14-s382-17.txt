14 C.F.R. § 382.17 — May carriers limit the number of passengers with a disability on a flight?
Citation: us:cfr:14:382.17
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-382.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 382 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Disability In Air Travel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As a carrier, you must not limit the number of passengers with a disability who travel on a flight. (See also § 382.27(c)(6) of this part.)
