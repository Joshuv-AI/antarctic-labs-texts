40 U.S.C. § 6302 — Public use of grounds
Citation: us:usc:40:6302
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section6302
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part C (Federal Building Complexes); Chapter 63 (Smithsonian Institution, National Gallery Of Art, And John F. Kennedy Center For The Performing Arts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Public travel in, and occupancy of, the grounds specified under section 6301 of this title are restricted to the sidewalks and other paved surfaces, except in the National Zoological Park.
