35 U.S.C. § 212 — Disposition of rights in educational awards
Citation: us:usc:35:212
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title35-section212
Context: United States Code, Title 35 (Patents); Part II (Patentability Of Inventions And Grant Of Patents); Chapter 18 (Patent Rights In Inventions Made With Federal Assistance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No scholarship, fellowship, training grant, or other funding agreement made by a Federal agency primarily to an awardee for educational purposes will contain any provision giving the Federal agency any rights to inventions made by the awardee.
