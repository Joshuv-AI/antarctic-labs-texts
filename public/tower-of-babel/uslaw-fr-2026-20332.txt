NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION


Proposal Review Committee Name Changes

AGENCY: National Science Foundation.

ACTION: Notice: committee management notice of amendment.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice announces amendments by the National Science 
Foundation (NSF) of the names for eight of its proposal review 
committees (listing follows) due to a recent reorganization at NSF.

DATES: NSF approves the amending of these committees on 8/31/2026. 
Applicable date for amendment is October 1, 2026. For more information, 
please contact Crystal Robinson, NSF, at (703) 292-8687.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Crystal Robinson, Committee Management 
Officer, NSF, at (703) 292-8687, or by mail to National Science 
Foundation, Randolph Building, 401 Dulany Street, Alexandria, VA 22314.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The NSF management officials having 
responsibility for the advisory committees listed below have determined 
that amending these committees is necessary and in the public interest 
in connection with the performance of duties imposed upon the Director, 
National Science Foundation (NSF), by 42 U.S.C. 1861 et seq. This 
determination follows consultation with the Committee Management 
Secretariat, General Services Administration.
    Committees:

--#57 Proposal Review Panel for STEM Workforce and Talent Development; 
formerly the Proposal Review Panel for Graduate Education
--#59 Proposal Review Panel for STEM Research and Innovation; formerly 
the Proposal Review Panel for Research on Learning in Formal and 
Informal Settings
--#1185 Proposal Review Panel for Computer and Information Science and 
Engineering; formerly the Proposal Review Panel for Cyberinfrastructure
--#1208 Proposal Review Panel for Mathematical and Physical Sciences; 
formerly the Proposal Review Panel for Physics
--#34558 Proposal Review Panel for Engineering; formerly the Proposal 
Review Panel for Emerging Frontiers and Multidisciplinary Activities
--#44011 Proposal Review Panel for Biological Sciences; formerly the 
Proposal Review Panel for Emerging Frontiers in Biological Sciences
--#84683 Proposal Review Panel for Startups and Small Businesses; 
formerly the Proposal Review Panel for Translational Impacts
--#84685 Proposal Review Panel for Technology, Innovation, and 
Workforce Building Programs; formerly the Proposal Review Panel for 
Innovation and Technology Ecosystems

    Dated: October 1, 2026.
Crystal Robinson,
Committee Management Officer, National Science Foundation.
