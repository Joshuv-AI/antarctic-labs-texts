2 U.S.C. § 1976 — Acceptance of donations of animals
Citation: us:usc:2:1976
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1976
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 29 (Capitol Police); Subchapter II (Powers And Duties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The Capitol Police may accept the donation of animals to be used in the canine units of the Capitol Police.
(b) Effective date
This section shall apply with respect to fiscal year 2005 and each fiscal year thereafter.
