27 C.F.R. § 20.245 — Discontinuance of use.
Citation: us:cfr:27:20.245
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-20.245
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 20 (Distribution And Use Of Denatured Alcohol And Rum). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a Government agency, holding a permit issued under this subpart, no longer intends to procure and use specially denatured spirits, the permit shall be returned to the appropriate TTB officer for cancellation. All photocopies of the permit furnished to vendors shall be returned to the agency for destruction.
