42 U.S.C. § 7301 — Environmental impact statements
Citation: us:usc:42:7301
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section7301
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 84 (Department Of Energy); Subchapter VII (Transitional, Savings, And Conforming Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The transfer of functions under subchapters III and IV of this chapter shall not affect the validity of any draft environmental impact statement published before October 1, 1977.
