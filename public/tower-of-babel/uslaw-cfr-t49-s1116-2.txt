49 C.F.R. § 1116.2 — Manner of presentation.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1116.2
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1116.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1116 (Oral Argument Before The Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Proponents of a rule or order will be heard first, and opponents will be heard second. One counsel only will usually be heard for each of the opposing interests, unless additional presentations are specifically authorized.
