7 C.F.R. § 1738.155 — Advance of funds.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1738.155
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1738.155
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1738 (Rural Broadband Loans, Loan/Grant Combinations, And Loan Guarantees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

RUS loan and grant advances are made at the request of the Awardee according to the procedures stipulated in the Award documents. For loan and loan/grant combination Awards, all non-RUS funds must be expended first, followed by loan funds and then grant funds, except for RUS approved development costs. Grant funds for eligible development costs, if any, will be used only on the first advance request.
