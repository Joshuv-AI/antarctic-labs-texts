30 C.F.R. § 885.19 — What happens to unused funds from my grant?
Citation: us:cfr:30:885.19
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-885.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 885 (Grants For Certified States And Indian Tribes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All program grant funds are available until expended. If there are any unexpended funds after your grant is completed, we deobligate the funds when we close your grant. We make these unused funds available for re-award to the same certified State or Indian tribe to which they were originally distributed. You may apply for unused funds whenever you choose to request them either in a new grant award or as an amendment to an existing open grant.
