22 C.F.R. § 3a.6 — a.6 Notification of approval.
Citation: us:cfr:22:3a.6
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-3a.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 3a (PART 3a—ACCEPTANCE OF EMPLOYMENT FROM FOREIGN GOVERNMENTS BY MEMBERS OF THE UNIFORMED SERVICES). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director, Bureau of Politico-Military Affairs, will notify the Secretary concerned when an applicant's proposed foreign government employment is approved. Notification of approval to the applicant will be made by the Secretary concerned or his designee.
