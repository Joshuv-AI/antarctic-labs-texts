16 C.F.R. § 1512.1 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1512.1
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1512.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1512 (Requirements For Bicycles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth the requirements for a bicycle as defined in § 1512.2(a) (except a bicycle that is a “track bicycle” or a “one-of-a-kind bicycle” as defined in § 1512.2 (d) and (e)) which is not a banned article under § 1500.18(a)(12) of this chapter.
