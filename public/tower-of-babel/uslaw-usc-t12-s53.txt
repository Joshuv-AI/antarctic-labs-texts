12 U.S.C. § 53 — When capital stock paid in
Citation: us:usc:12:53
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section53
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 2 (National Banks); Subchapter II (Capital, Stock, And Stockholders). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All of the capital stock of every national banking association shall be paid in before it shall be authorized to commence business.
