29 C.F.R. § 458.53 — Filing of complaints.
Citation: us:cfr:29:458.53
Creator: Office Of Labor-Management Standards, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-458.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 458 (Standards Of Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A complaint alleging violations of this part may be filed with any district office, or any other office of the Office of Labor-Management Standards.
