15 U.S.C. § 13b — Cooperative association; return of net earnings or surplus
Citation: us:usc:15:13b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section13b
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 1 (Monopolies And Combinations In Restraint Of Trade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this Act shall prevent a cooperative association from returning to its members, producers, or consumers the whole, or any part of, the net earnings or surplus resulting from its trading operations, in proportion to their purchases or sales from, to, or through the association.
