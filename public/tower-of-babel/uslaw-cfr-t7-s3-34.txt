7 C.F.R. § 3.34 — Fees.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3.34
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3 (Debt Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Federal agencies operating Treasury-designated debt collection centers are authorized to charge a fee for services rendered regarding referred or transferred debts. The fee may be paid out of amounts collected and may be added to the debt as an administrative cost.
