38 U.S.C. § 313 — Availability of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:38:313
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section313
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part I (General Provisions); Chapter 3 (Department Of Veterans Affairs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Funds appropriated to the Department may remain available until expended.
(b)
Funds appropriated to the Department may not be used for a settlement of more than $1,000,000 on a construction contract unless—
(1)
the settlement is audited by an entity outside the Department for reasonableness and appropriateness of expenditures; and
(2)
the settlement is provided for specifically in an appropriation law.
