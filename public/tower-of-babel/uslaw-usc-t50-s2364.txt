50 U.S.C. § 2364 — Purchase of low-enriched uranium derived from Russian highly enriched uranium
Citation: us:usc:50:2364
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section2364
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 40 (Defense Against Weapons Of Mass Destruction); Subchapter V (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Sense of Congress
It is the sense of Congress that the allies of the United States and other nations should participate in efforts to ensure that stockpiles of weapons-grade nuclear material are reduced.
(b) Actions by Secretary of State
Congress urges the Secretary of State to encourage, in consultation with the Secretary of Energy, other countries to purchase low-enriched uranium that is derived from highly enriched uranium extracted from Russian nuclear weapons.
