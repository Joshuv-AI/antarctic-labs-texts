30 C.F.R. § 707.4 — Responsibility.
Citation: us:cfr:30:707.4
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-707.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 707 (Exemption For Coal Extraction Incident To Government-Financed Highway Or Other Construction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The regulatory authority is responsible for enforcing the requirements of this part.
(b) Any person conducting coal extraction as an incidental part of government-financed construction is responsible for possessing, on the site of the extraction operation, the documentation required by 30 CFR 707.12.
