15 C.F.R. § 960.17 — Investigations and enforcement.
Citation: us:cfr:15:960.17
Creator: National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-960.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 960 (Licensing Of Private Remote Sensing Space Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Secretary may investigate, provide penalties for noncompliance, and prevent future noncompliance, by using the authorities specified at 51 U.S.C. 60123(a).
(b) When the Secretary undertakes administrative enforcement proceedings as authorized by 51 U.S.C. 60123(a)(3) and (4), the parties will follow the procedures provided at 15 CFR part 904.
