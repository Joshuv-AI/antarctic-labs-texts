28 C.F.R. § 12.3 — Prior registration with the Foreign Agents Registration Unit.
Citation: us:cfr:28:12.3
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-12.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 12 (Registration Of Certain Persons Having Knowledge Of Foreign Espionage, Counterespionage, Or Sabotage Matters Under The Act Of August 1, 1956). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person who has filed a registration statement under the terms of the Foreign Agents Registration Act of 1938, as amended by section 20(a) of the Internal Security Act of 1950, shall be required to file a registration statement under the act, unless otherwise determined by the Chief, Registration Unit.
