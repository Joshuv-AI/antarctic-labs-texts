19 C.F.R. § 176.2 — Service of notice of appeal.
Citation: us:cfr:19:176.2
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-176.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 176 (Proceedings In The Court Of International Trade). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the United States is an appellee in an appeal taken to the Court of Appeals for the Federal Circuit, a copy of the notice of appeal shall be served upon the Assistant Chief Counsel for Court of International Trade Litigation.
