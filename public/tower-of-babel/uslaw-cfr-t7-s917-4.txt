7 C.F.R. § 917.4 — Fruit.

Fruit means the edible product of the following kinds of trees:
(a) All varieties of pears except Beurre Hardy, Beurre D'Anjou, Bosc, Winter Nelis, Doyenne du Comice, Beurre Easter, and Beurre Clairgeau.
(b) [Reserved]
