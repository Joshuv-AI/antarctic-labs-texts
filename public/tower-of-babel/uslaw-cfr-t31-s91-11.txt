31 C.F.R. § 91.11 — Dogs and other animals.
Citation: us:cfr:31:91.11
Creator: Monetary Offices, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-91.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 91 (Regulations Governing Conduct In Or On The Bureau Of The Mint Buildings And Grounds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Dogs and other animals, except seeing-eye dogs, shall not be brought upon the property for other than official purposes.
