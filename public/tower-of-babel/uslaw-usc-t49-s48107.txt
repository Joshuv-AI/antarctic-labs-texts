49 U.S.C. § 48107 — Civil aviation security research and development
Citation: us:usc:49:48107
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section48107
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part C (Financing); Chapter 481 (Airport And Airway Trust Fund Authorizations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

After the review under section 44912(b) of this title is completed, necessary amounts may be appropriated to the Secretary of Transportation out of the Airport and Airway Trust Fund established under section 9502 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (26 U.S.C. 9502) to make grants under section 44912(a)(4)(A).
