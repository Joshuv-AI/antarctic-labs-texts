46 C.F.R. § 161.013-11 — -11 Prototype test.
Citation: us:cfr:46:161.013-11
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-161.013-11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 161 (Electrical Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each manufacturer must test a prototype light identical to the lights to be certified prior to the labeling required by § 161.013-13.
(b) If the prototype light fails to meet any of the general performance requirements of § 161.013-3 the lights must not be certified under this subpart.
(c) Each manufacturer must:
(1) Forward the test results within 30 days to the Commandant (CG-ENG) according to § 159.005-1 of this subchapter; and
(2) Retain records of the test results for at least 5 years, or as long as the light is manufactured and certified, whichever is longer.
