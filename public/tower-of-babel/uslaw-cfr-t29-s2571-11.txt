29 C.F.R. § 2571.11 — Scope of review by the Secretary.
Citation: us:cfr:29:2571.11
Creator: Employee Benefits Security Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-2571.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 2571 (Procedural Regulations For Administration And Enforcement Under The Employee Retirement Income Security Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The review of the Secretary shall be based on the record established before the administrative law judge. There shall be no opportunity for oral argument.
