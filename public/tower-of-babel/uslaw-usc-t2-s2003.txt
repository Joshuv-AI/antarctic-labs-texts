2 U.S.C. § 2003 — Speaker as member of House Office Building commission
Citation: us:usc:2:2003
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section2003
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 30 (Operation And Maintenance Of Capitol Complex); Subchapter I (House Of Representatives). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Speaker shall continue a member of the commission in control of said building until his successor as Speaker is elected or his term as a Representative in Congress shall have expired.
