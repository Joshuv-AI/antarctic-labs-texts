46 U.S.C. § 3101 — Authority to suspend inspection
Citation: us:usc:46:3101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section3101
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part B (Inspection And Regulation Of Vessels); Chapter 31 (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When the President decides that the needs of foreign commerce require, the President may suspend a provision of this part for a foreign-built vessel registered as a vessel of the United States on conditions the President may specify.
