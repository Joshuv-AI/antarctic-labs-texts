7 C.F.R. § 60.133 — Wild fish and shellfish.
Citation: us:cfr:7:60.133
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-60.133
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 60 (Country Of Origin Labeling For Fish And Shellfish). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Wild fish and shellfish means naturally-born or hatchery-originated fish or shellfish released in the wild, and caught, taken, or harvested from non-controlled waters or beds; and fillets, steaks, nuggets, and any other flesh from a wild fish or shellfish.
