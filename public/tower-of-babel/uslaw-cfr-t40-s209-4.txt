40 C.F.R. § 209.4 — Issuance of complaint.
Citation: us:cfr:40:209.4
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-209.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 209 (Rules Of Practice Governing Proceedings Under The Noise Control Act Of 1972). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the complainant has reason to believe that a person has violated any provision of the Act or the regulations, he or she may institute a proceeding for the issuance of a remedial order by issuing a complaint.
