21 C.F.R. § 571.115 — Application of the cancer clause of section 409 of the act.
Citation: us:cfr:21:571.115
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-571.115
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 571 (Food Additive Petitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Food additives intended for use as an ingredient in food for animals that are raised for food production and that have the potential to contaminate human food with residues whose consumption could present a risk of cancer to people must satisfy the requirements of subpart E of part 500 of this chapter.
