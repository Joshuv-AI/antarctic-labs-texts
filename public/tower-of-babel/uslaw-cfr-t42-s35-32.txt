42 C.F.R. § 35.32 — Board of appraisers.
Citation: us:cfr:42:35.32
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-35.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 35 (Hospital And Station Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The officer in charge shall appoint, from the personnel of the station or hospital, a board of three persons to serve at his pleasure. The board shall provide for the sale of articles having commercial value and shall keep appropriate records of such articles and their disposition.
