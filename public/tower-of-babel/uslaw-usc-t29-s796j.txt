29 U.S.C. § 796j — “Older individual who is blind” defined
Citation: us:usc:29:796j
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section796j
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 16 (Vocational Rehabilitation And Other Rehabilitation Services); Subchapter VII (Independent Living Services And Centers For Independent Living); Part B (Independent Living Services For Older Individuals Who Are Blind). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of this part, the term “older individual who is blind” means an individual age 55 or older whose significant visual impairment makes competitive employment extremely difficult to attain but for whom independent living goals are feasible.
