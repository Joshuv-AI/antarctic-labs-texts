16 U.S.C. § 428m — Authorization of appropriation
Citation: us:usc:16:428m
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section428m
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LX (National Military Parks). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is authorized to be appropriated the sum of not to exceed $454,000 for the purpose of acquiring lands, interests in lands, and improvements thereon as may be necessary for carrying out sections 428k to 428o of this title.
