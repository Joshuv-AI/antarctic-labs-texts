5 C.F.R. § 334.108 — Reports required.
Citation: us:cfr:5:334.108
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-334.108
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 334 (Temporary Assignments Under The Intergovernmental Personnel Act (Ipa)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Federal agency which assigns an employee to or receives an employee from a State, local, Indian tribal government, institution of higher education, or other eligible organization in accordance with this part must submit to OPM such reports as OPM may request.
