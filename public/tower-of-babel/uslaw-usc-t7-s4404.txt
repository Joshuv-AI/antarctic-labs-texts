7 U.S.C. § 4404 — Duties of Secretary of State
Citation: us:usc:7:4404
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section4404
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 75 (International Carriage Of Perishable Foodstuffs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of State, with the concurrence of the Secretary of Agriculture, may take such action as may be considered appropriate to assert and protect the rights of the United States under the agreement.
