Retraction Note: Reactive oxygen species-producing genes regulate mosquito midgut bacteria colonization, transcriptomic changes and cell repair
Authors: Baolin Song, Jehan Zeb
Published: 2026

Retraction to: Communications Biology https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-025-09024-5, published online 27 February 2026

The Editors have retracted this article.

Following publication, the journal was notified that not all listed authors were aware of the submission of this article and did not provide permission to be listed as an author. The affected author has been removed from the authorship list. Additionally, the remaining authors did not have permission to publish the data included in this article. The content of this article has therefore been removed.

The remaining authors have not responded to any correspondence from the editor about this retraction.
