A vision for the future: ASPC's new educational offerings for 2025
Authors: Michael D Shapiro
Published: 2024

Dear ASPC Members and Colleagues,

It is both a privilege and an honor to address you for the second time as President of the American Society for Preventive Cardiology. Serving in this role is truly one of the greatest responsibilities of my career, and it is inspiring to work alongside our exceptional board of directors, committees, work groups, and the passionate members who make up our Society. Together, we are committed to promote the prevention of cardiovascular disease, improve patient outcomes, and support the professional growth of our community. This quarter, I am thrilled to announce two new ASPC initiatives aimed at enriching our educational offerings and expanding opportunities for growth and learning within preventive cardiology.

Introducing the ASPC CardioCore Webinar Series

One of our goals this year is to enhance access to foundational knowledge in preventive cardiology for trainees and clinicians alike. With that, we are proud to introduce the ASPC CardioCore Webinar Series—a program designed to provide accessible, high-quality lectures on core topics in preventive cardiology. This series, called ASPC CardioCore: Foundational Lectures in Cardiovascular Prevention, aims to reach trainees who may not have access to dedicated preventive cardiology programs at their institutions. However, all ASPC members and interested clinicians are invited to attend.

Each month, we will present a new lecture, spotlighting important clinical concepts and emerging research in preventive cardiology. We are honored to have Dr. Khurram Nasir launch the series with a lecture on “The Role of CAC Testing in Preventive Cardiology.” This session will consist of a 30–40 min lecture, followed by a live 20-minute Q&A, encouraging dialogue and deeper understanding. Over the next two years, we aim to cover all the essential topics in clinical preventive cardiology, featuring some of the most prominent experts in the field.

By offering this series, we hope to bridge the gap in training opportunities, extend our reach to a broader audience, and foster an interactive learning environment. The ASPC CardioCore Series exemplifies our commitment to supporting educational growth and empowering clinicians at all stages of their careers with the knowledge to provide top-tier preventive care.

Launching the ASPC Training Academy

In addition to the monthly webinars, I am excited to announce the launch of the ASPC Training Academy—a program that will serve as an intensive, in-person educational experience held one day before our annual ASPC Congress on Cardiovascular Disease Prevention. The Training Academy will offer an immersive, full-day curriculum focused on the most relevant topics in preventive cardiology. This program is tailored specifically for residents and fellows interested in cardiovascular disease prevention and will feature an interactive format that includes lectures, small group sessions, and discussions on high-impact topics.

Our goal with the Training Academy is to provide an unparalleled educational experience for trainees who aspire to make preventive cardiology a core component of their future practice. Beyond the academic component, the academy will provide myriad opportunities to network with fellow trainees and seasoned leaders in the field of preventive cardiology and within the ASPC. We believe that fostering these relationships is crucial to developing a robust community of professionals dedicated to advancing cardiovascular prevention.

We will be opening applications for the Training Academy in the coming weeks. We are planning to keep the size of the program relatively small to ensure a personalized experience, allowing ample time for participants to engage directly with faculty and peers. For those selected, this will be a unique opportunity to gain hands-on experience, build lasting connections, and strengthen their knowledge base in preventive cardiology.

A year of innovation and growth

These two initiatives reflect our ongoing dedication to supporting and advancing preventive cardiology, not only within the ASPC but also in the broader medical community. By cultivating innovative educational programs, we aim to extend ASPC's impact, supporting the next generation of preventive cardiologists and reinforcing the value of preventive strategies in clinical practice. As we roll out these new offerings, I encourage you to share them within your networks. Word of mouth and social media engagement are invaluable in helping us reach clinicians and trainees who can benefit from these resources.

Looking ahead

This is an exciting time for the ASPC, and I look forward to what we will accomplish together in the months to come. Our members, board, and committees are actively exploring new projects to keep ASPC at the forefront of cardiovascular prevention. Additionally, our work this year will continue to emphasize collaboration, advocacy, and research that drives forward our understanding of cardiovascular risk and the best approaches for its prevention. Your ideas and input are always welcome, and I invite all of you to engage with ASPC leadership as we continue to build our collective vision for the future of preventive cardiology.

In closing, I would like to express my gratitude to all of you for your continued commitment to the mission of ASPC. I am humbled by the opportunity to lead such a dedicated community and excited to continue this journey together. Please stay tuned for updates on the ASPC CardioCore Series, the Training Academy, and other initiatives we have in store. Together, we are shaping the future of preventive cardiology.

Warmest regards,

Michael Shapiro, DO, FASPC

President, ASPC
