25 U.S.C. § 1658 — Limitation on contract authority
Citation: us:usc:25:1658
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section1658
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 18 (Indian Health Care); Subchapter IV (Health Services For Urban Indians). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The authority of the Secretary to enter into contracts under this subchapter shall be to the extent, and in an amount, provided for in appropriation Acts.
