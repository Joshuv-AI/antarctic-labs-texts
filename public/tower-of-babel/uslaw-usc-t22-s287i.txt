22 U.S.C. § 287i — Deduction of principal and interest from annual payment of assessed share of United States of budget
Citation: us:usc:22:287i
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section287i
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter XVI (United Nations Organization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There shall be deducted from the annual payment of the assessed share of the United States of the budget of the United Nations an amount equal to the corresponding annual installment of principal and interest due to the United States on account of the loan made pursuant to section 287g of this title.
