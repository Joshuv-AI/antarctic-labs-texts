23 C.F.R. § 620.102 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:23:620.102
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-620.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 620 (Engineering). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirements of this section apply to all projects on which Federal-aid highway funds are to be expended and to both civil and military airports.
