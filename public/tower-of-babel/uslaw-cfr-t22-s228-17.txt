22 C.F.R. § 228.17 — Special procurement rules for construction and engineering services.
Citation: us:cfr:22:228.17
Creator: Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-228.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 228 (Rules For Procurement Of Commodities And Services Financed By Usaid). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Advanced developing countries, as defined in § 228.01, which USAID has determined to have attained a competitive capability in international markets for construction services or engineering services are not eligible to furnish USAID-financed construction and engineering services unless approved to do so under a waiver to Code 935 under subpart D of this part.
