On KV-Poisson Structure and related invariants.
Prosper Rosaire Mama Assandje, Herguey Mopeng, Joseph Dongho

Abstract
We propose an deepened analysis of KV-Poisson structures of on I ​ R 2 . \mathrm{I\!R}^{2}. We present their classification their properties an their possible applications in different domains. We prove that these structure give rise to a new Cohomological invariant. We explicitly compute the Cohomological groups of some of these structures.

Introduction

The characterization of the KV-Poisson structures of
\mathrm{I\!R}^{2}
is a recent research topic in mathematics that is attracting
increasing interest. KV-Poisson structures is Poisson structures associated to a KV-algebra structures.
They appear in many mathematical areas, in particular in symplectic
geometry, field theory and mathematical physics. The relations
between the KV-Cohomology of the Koszul-Vinberg algebras and some
properties of various geometric objects have been studied in

Boyom 2005
. More precisely,
Boyom 2005
proved that the scalar
KV-Cohomology of the algebras of real Koszul-Vinbergs or holomorphic
is closely related to the real Poisson manifolds. M.N. Boyom has
also underlined the close relations between the Nijenhuis’s works

Koszul 1985
and the KV-Cohomology. In
Nguiffo Boyom et al. 2012
, M.N. Boyom
studied KV cohomology, which is a form of cohomology associated with
certain geometric structures, particularly in the context of the
flat affine manifold. M.N. Boyom investigated how the KV structures
apply to the flat affine manifold. He studied the topological and
geometric properties of these manifolds, as well as the applications
of KV cohomology to the study of their structures.
In
Radko and Shlyakhtenko 2006
, N.P.Landsman had explored the more advanced Poisson structures,
including the structures of Kozul-Vinberg-Poisson, which are important generalizations
of the standard Poisson structure. He also approached the applications of Poisson structures
in mathematical physics, emphasizing their role in classical mechanics, quantum field theory,
sympletic geometry and other related areas. In the recent works of
Herguey and Dongho 2023
a complete
classification of KV-structures on the real affine plane is proposed. The authors there also
determine all the KV-hessian. Having on the affine plan an algebraic structure, knowing
the usefulness of the Poisson structures as well in the classical mechanics as in the quantum
mechanics, we wonder if there exist KV-structures that are
Poisson structures on the real affine plan? What are therefore the possible
different classifications of the structures of KV-Poisson of
\mathrm{I\!R}^{2}
?
How can we characterize these structures in terms
of their algebraic and geometric properties? In this
paper we give some answers to these questions by determining
all KV-structures that are Poisson structures; then we propose
a deeper study of the different KV-Poisson structures on the
vectorial space
\mathrm{I\!R}^{2}
. This article begins in
section
1
, with an introduction, in section
2
, the
preliminaries on the basic concepts of KV algebras and Poisson
algebras, It also presents the different classes of KV-Poisson
structures, with emphasis on their geometric
and algebraic properties. In section
3
, we present the characterization of the KV-Poisson structures of
\mathrm{I\!R}^{2}
;
in section
4
,the complex of KV-Poisson Cohomology; in the
ends in section
5
, the conclusion.

Proposition 1

\displaystyle-[\mu(u,\mu(v,w))+\mu(v,\mu(w,u))+\mu(w,\mu(u,v))]-\mu(w,\mu(u,v))=0
