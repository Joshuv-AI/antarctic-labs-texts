15 U.S.C. § 6313 — Relationship with State law
Citation: us:usc:15:6313
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section6313
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 89 (Professional Boxing Safety). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall prohibit a State from adopting or enforcing supplemental or more stringent laws or regulations not inconsistent with this chapter, or criminal, civil, or administrative fines for violations of such laws or regulations.
