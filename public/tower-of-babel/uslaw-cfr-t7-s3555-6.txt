7 C.F.R. § 3555.6 — State and local law.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3555.6
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3555.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3555 (Guaranteed Rural Housing Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Lenders will comply with applicable State and local laws and regulations, including the laws of American Indian tribes. Supplemental guidance will be issued in the case of any conflict with or significant differences from provisions of this part.
