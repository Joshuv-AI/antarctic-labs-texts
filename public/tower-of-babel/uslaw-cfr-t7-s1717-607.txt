7 C.F.R. § 1717.607 — Contract bidding requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1717.607
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1717.607
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1717 (Post-Loan Policies And Procedures Common To Insured And Guaranteed Electric Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Borrowers must follow RUS requirements regarding bidding for contracts for construction, materials, and equipment only if financing of the construction or procurement will be provided by RUS. These requirements are set forth in 7 CFR part 1726.
