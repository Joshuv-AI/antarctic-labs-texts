5 C.F.R. § 319.302 — Individual qualifications.
Citation: us:cfr:5:319.302
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-319.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 319 (Employment In Senior-Level And Scientific And Professional Positions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agency heads are delegated authority to approve the qualifications of individuals appointed to SL and ST positions. The agency head must determine that the individual meets the qualifications standards for the position to which appointed.
