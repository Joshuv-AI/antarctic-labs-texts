29 C.F.R. § 2704.305 — Settlement.
Citation: us:cfr:29:2704.305
Creator: Federal Mine Safety And Health Review Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-2704.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 2704 (Implementation Of The Equal Access To Justice Act In Commission Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the event that counsel for the Secretary and an applicant agree to settle an EAJA claim after an application has been filed with the Commission, the applicant shall timely notify the Commission of the settlement and request dismissal of the application.
