46 C.F.R. § 72.05-45 — -45 Paint.
Citation: us:cfr:46:72.05-45
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-72.05-45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 72 (Construction And Arrangement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An excessive number of coats of paint will be discouraged unless noncombustible paint is used.
(b) Nitrocellulose or other highly flammable or noxious fume-producing paints or lacquers shall not be used.
