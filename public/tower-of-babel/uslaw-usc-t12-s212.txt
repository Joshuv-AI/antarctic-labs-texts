12 U.S.C. § 212 — Right to amend; separability
Citation: us:usc:12:212
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section212
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 2 (National Banks); Subchapter XIV (Bank Conservation Act). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is expressly reserved. If any provision of this Act, or the application there of to any person or circumstances, is held invalid, the remainder of the Act, and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances, shall not be affected thereby.
