22 U.S.C. § 3985 — Temporary details
Citation: us:usc:22:3985
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section3985
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 52 (Foreign Service); Subchapter V (Classification Of Positions And Assignments). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A period of duty of not more than six months in duration by a member of the Service shall be considered a temporary detail and shall not be considered an assignment within the meaning of this subchapter.
