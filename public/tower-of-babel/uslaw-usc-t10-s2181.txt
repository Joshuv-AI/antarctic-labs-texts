10 U.S.C. § 2181 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:10:2181
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2181
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training And Education); Chapter 110 (Educational Assistance For Members Held As Captives And Their Dependents). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter:
(1)
The terms “captive status” and “former captive” have the meanings given those terms in section 559 of title 37.
(2)
The term “dependent” has the meaning given that term in section 551 of that title.
