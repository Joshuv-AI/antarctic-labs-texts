30 C.F.R. § 14.1 — Purpose, effective date for approval holders.
Citation: us:cfr:30:14.1
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-14.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 14 (Requirements For The Approval Of Flame-Resistant Conveyor Belts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This Part establishes the flame resistance requirements for MSHA approval of conveyor belts for use in underground coal mines. Applications for approval or extensions of approval submitted after December 31, 2008, must meet the requirements of this Part.
