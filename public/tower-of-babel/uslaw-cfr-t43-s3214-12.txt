43 C.F.R. § 3214.12 — What activities must my bond cover?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3214.12
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3214.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3200 (Geothermal Resource Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Your bond must cover:
(a) Any activities related to exploration, drilling, utilization, or associated operations on a Federal lease;
(b) Reclamation of the surface and other resources;
(c) Rental and royalty payments; and
(d) Compliance with the requirements of § 3200.4.
