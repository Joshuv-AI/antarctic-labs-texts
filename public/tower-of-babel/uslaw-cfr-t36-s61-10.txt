36 C.F.R. § 61.10 — Waiver.
Citation: us:cfr:36:61.10
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-61.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 61 (Procedures For State, Tribal, And Local Government Historic Preservation Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary may waive any of the requirements of the rules in this part that are not mandated by statute or by other applicable regulations if the Secretary finds, in writing, that the historic preservation program would benefit from such waiver and the waiver would not compromise the purposes, conditions, and requirements of the National Historic Preservation Act of 1966, as amended.
