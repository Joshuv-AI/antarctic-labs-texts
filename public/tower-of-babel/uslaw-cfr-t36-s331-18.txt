36 C.F.R. § 331.18 — Restrictions.
Citation: us:cfr:36:331.18
Creator: Corps Of Engineers, Department Of The Army
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-331.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 331 (Regulations Governing The Protection, Use And Management Of The Falls Of The Ohio National Wildlife Conservation Area, Kentucky And Indiana). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The District Engineer may establish and post a schedule of visiting hours and/or restrictions on the public use of a portion or portions of the WCA. The District Engineer may close or restrict the use of the WCA or portion of the WCA when necessitated by reason of public health, public safety, security, maintenance, or other reasons in the public interest. Entering or using the project in a manner which is contrary to the schedule of visiting hours, closure or restrictions is prohibited.
