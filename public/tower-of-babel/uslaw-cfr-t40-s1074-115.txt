40 C.F.R. § 1074.115 — Relationship of federal and state standards.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1074.115
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1074.115
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1074 (Preemption Of State Standards And Procedures For Waiver Of Federal Preemption For Nonroad Engines And Nonroad Vehicles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If state standards apply to a new nonroad engine or vehicle pursuant to authorization granted under section 209 of the Act (42 U.S.C. 7543), compliance with such state standards will be treated as compliance with the otherwise applicable standards of this chapter for engines or vehicles introduced into commerce in that state.
