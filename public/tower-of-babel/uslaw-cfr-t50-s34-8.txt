50 C.F.R. § 34.8 — Appropriations authorized.
Citation: us:cfr:50:34.8
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-34.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 34 (Refuge Revenue Sharing With Counties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Act authorizes appropriations to the fund for any fiscal year when the aggregate amount of payments required to be made exceeds the net receipts in the fund.
