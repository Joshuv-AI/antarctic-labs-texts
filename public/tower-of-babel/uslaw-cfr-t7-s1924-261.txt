7 C.F.R. § 1924.261 — Handling complaints involving dwellings covered by an independent or insured home warranty plan.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1924.261
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Rural Business-Cooperative Service, And Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1924.261
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1924 (Construction And Repair). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Borrowers with complaints about dwellings covered by an independent or insured home warranty plan will be instructed to first contact the warranty company and follow the complaint resolution process for that company, with the assistance of Rural Development, if needed. If the complaint is not resolved in this manner, it will be handled under § 1924.259 of this subpart.
