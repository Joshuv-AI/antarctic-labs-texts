49 C.F.R. § 272.3 — Application.
Citation: us:cfr:49:272.3
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-272.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 272 (Critical Incident Stress Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to each
(a) Class I railroad, including the National Railroad Passenger Corporation;
(b) Intercity passenger railroad; or
(c) Commuter railroad.
