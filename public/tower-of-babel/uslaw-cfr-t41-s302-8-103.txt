41 C.F.R. § 302-8.103 — -8.103 Changes to the type of storage.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-8.103
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-8.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-8 (8—Allowances For Extended Storage Of Household Goods (Hhg)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees may change from temporary to extended storage or from storage at personal expense to extended storage at Government expense, if authorized by their agency.
