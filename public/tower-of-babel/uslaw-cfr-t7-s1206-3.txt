7 C.F.R. § 1206.3 — Conflict of interest.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1206.3
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Miscellaneous Commodities), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1206.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1206 (Mango Promotion, Research, And Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Conflict of interest means a situation in which a member or employee of the Board has a direct or indirect financial interest in a person who performs a service for, or enters into a contract with, the Board for anything of economic value.
