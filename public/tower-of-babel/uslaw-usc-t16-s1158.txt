16 U.S.C. § 1158 — Acceptance or rejection by Secretaries of State and Commerce of Commission recommendations
Citation: us:usc:16:1158
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section1158
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 24 (Conservation And Protection Of North Pacific Fur Seals); Subchapter I (Fur Seal Management). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of State, with the concurrence of the Secretary, is authorized to accept or reject, on behalf of the United States, recommendations made by the Commission pursuant to article V of the Convention.
