21 C.F.R. § 178.3450 — Esters of stearic and palmitic acids.
Citation: us:cfr:21:178.3450
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-178.3450
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 178 (Indirect Food Additives: Adjuvants, Production Aids, And Sanitizers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The ester stearyl palmitate or palmityl stearate or mixtures thereof may be safely used as adjuvants in food-packaging materials when used in accordance with the following prescribed conditions:
(a) They are used or intended for use as plasticizers or lubricants in polystyrene intended for use in contact with food.
(b) They are added to the formulated polymer prior to extrusion.
(c) The quantity used shall not exceed that required to accomplish the intended technical effect.
