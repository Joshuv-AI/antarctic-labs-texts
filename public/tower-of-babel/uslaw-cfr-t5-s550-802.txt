5 C.F.R. § 550.802 — Coverage.
Citation: us:cfr:5:550.802
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-550.802
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 550 (Pay Administration (General)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except as provided in paragraph (b) of this section, this subpart applies to employees, as defined in § 550.803 of this subpart.
(b) This subpart does not apply to—
(1) Employees of the government of the District of Columbia; and
(2) Employees of the Tennessee Valley Authority.
