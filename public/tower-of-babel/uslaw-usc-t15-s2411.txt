15 U.S.C. § 2411 — Establishment
Citation: us:usc:15:2411
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section2411
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 51 (National Productivity And Quality Of Working Life); Subchapter II (National Center For Productivity And Quality Of Working Life). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is hereby established as an independent establishment of the executive branch of the Government the National Center for Productivity and Quality of Working Life.
