12 C.F.R. § 229.41 — Relation to State law.
Citation: us:cfr:12:229.41
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-229.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 229 (Availability Of Funds And Collection Of Checks (Regulation Cc)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart supersede any inconsistent provisions of the U.C.C. as adopted in any state, or of any other state law, but only to the extent of the inconsistency.
