10 U.S.C. § 7001 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:10:7001
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7001
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization); Chapter 701 (Definitions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this title, the term “Army” means the Army or Armies referred to in the Constitution of the United States, less that part established by law as the Air Force.
