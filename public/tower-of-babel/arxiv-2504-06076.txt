K 4 − K_{4}^{-} -free triple systems without large stars in the complement
Dhruv Mubayi, Nicholas Spanier

Abstract
The n n -star S n S_{n} is the n n -vertex triple system with ( n − 1 2 ) {n-1\choose 2} edges all of which contain a fixed vertex, and K 4 − K_{4}^{-} is the unique triple system with four vertices and three edges. We prove that the Ramsey number r ⁡ ( K 4 − , S n ) r(K_{4}^{-},S_{n}) has order of magnitude n 2 / log ⁡ n n^{2}/\log n . This confirms a conjecture of Conlon, Fox, He, Suk, Verstraëte and the first author. It also generalizes the well-known bound of Kim for the graph Ramsey number r ⁡ ( 3 , n ) r(3,n) , as the link of any vertex in a K 4 − K_{4}^{-} -free triple system is a triangle-free graph. Our method builds on the approach of Guo and Warnke who adapted Kim’s lower bound for r ⁡ ( 3 , n ) r(3,n) to the pseudorandom setting.

Introduction

A

is fixed and

, we often refer to this class of parameters as off-diagonal Ramsey numbers.

The off-diagonal graph Ramsey numbers

have been intensively studied for many decades.
Erdős conjectured that for fixed

, we have

and this was reiterated recently by the first author and Verstraëte
[
12
]
. Only the first two cases,

have been proved. The order of magnitude of

is

, where the upper bound was obtained by Ajtai-Komlós-Szemerédi
[
1
]
and the lower bound was proved by Kim
[
7
]
and later by Bohman
[
3
]
using a different method. More recently, Mattheus and Verstraëte proved that
r(4,n)=n^{3-o(1)}
with the order of magnitude still undetermined. In this paper we consider an analogous parameter to

in the hypergraph setting.

It is well-known that for each

, the hypergraph Ramsey number

for some positive constant

. On the other hand, there is a well-known large class

of 3-graphs

is polynomial in

. Recently, it was conjectured
[
4
]
that

is at most polynomial in
n
if and only if

. On the other hand, it is not clear whether

implies that
r(G,n)
is exponential in
n
. In fact, the following result from
[
5
]
showed an intermediate growth rate for the following related problem.
The link graph of a vertex

in a 3-graph

is the graph whose vertex set is

and edge set is
\{yz:vyz\in E(H)\}
. Write

for the

-vertex star: this is the 3-graph with vertex set

is the 3-graph for which there is a vertex whose link graph is isomorphic to

. The 3-graph

is a natural way to generalize

to hypergraphs. It is proved in

]
that there are positive constants

such that

(1)

This shows that

. Therefore, one needs to consider sparser 3-graphs

. The smallest such nontrivial 3-graph

The link graph

-free 3-graph

In the other direction, it was proved in

]
using the local lemma that

r(K_{4}^{-},S_{n})=\Omega(n^{2}/\log^{2}n)
.
This led the authors of
[

to conjecture that

r(K_{4}^{-},S_{n})=\Theta\left(\frac{n^{2}}{\log n}\right).

(2)

A similar state of affairs existed for the graph case

prior to Kim’s improved lower bound. Note, however, that (

as it posits the existence of a triple system such that

There are two general approaches to obtain graphs or hypergraphs that have good Ramsey properties of the type sought in Theorems

New ideas are required to implement the additional technical steps needed to control their deviation probability from their mean (see, for example, the event

We end by remarking that we were not able to prove Theorem

The following theorem is a well known version of the standard Chernoff bound.

Theorem 6

(

14
]
)

Let

\mathbb{P}(X\geq\lambda+t)\leq\exp\left(-\frac{t^{2}}{2\lambda}\right)

\mathbb{P}(X\leq\lambda-t)\leq\exp\left(-\frac{t^{2}}{2(\lambda+t)}\right)

The next theorem of Mcdiarmid
[

]
, and Warnke
[

]
bounds a function of

Theorem 7

(
[

,
11
,

]
)

Let
(\xi_{\alpha})_{\alpha\in J}
be a finite set of independent

(c_{\alpha})_{\alpha\in J}
so for all

\mathbb{P}(X\geq\mathbb{E}(X)+t)\leq\exp\left(-\frac{t^{2}}{2\lambda}\right).

(30)

Further, if

and if we drop the decreasing assumption on

\mathbb{P}(X\leq\mathbb{E}(X)-t)\leq\exp\left(-\frac{t^{2}}{2(\lambda+Ct)}\right)

(31)

Theorem 8

[
15
]
)

be a family of independent variables with values in

\mathbb{P}(Z_{C}\geq\mu+t)\leq\exp\left(-\frac{t^{2}}{2C(\mu+t)}\right)

Lemma 13

|U|\geq|\cup_{i\in J}U_{i}|\geq\sum_{i\in J}\left(|U_{i}|-\sum_{j\in J:j\neq i}|U_{i}\cap U_{j}|\right)\geq\sum_{i\in J}\frac{|U_{i}|}{2}\geq|J|\frac{z}{2}=\left(\lfloor\frac{2|U|}{z}\rfloor+1\right)\frac{z}{2}>|U|

which is a contradiction. Hence
|I|\leq\frac{2|U|}{z}
