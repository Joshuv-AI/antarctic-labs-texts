33 U.S.C. § 701f–2 — Funds for specific and authorized projects merged with and accounted for under regular annual appropriation
Citation: us:usc:33:701f–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section701f–2
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 15 (Flood Control). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Funds on and after May 17, 1950, appropriated for a specific and heretofore authorized project for a river, harbor, or flood-control works shall be merged with and be accounted for under the regular annual appropriation title applicable to such item.
