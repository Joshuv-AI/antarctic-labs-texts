The Brauer group of tori
Julian Demeio

Abstract
We show that the map Br ⁡ T → ( Br ⁡ T k ¯ ) Γ k \Br T\rightarrow(\Br T_{\overline{k}})^{\Gamma_{k}} is surjective for a torus T T defined over a field k k of characteristic 0 0 when k k is a local or global field or T T is quasi-trivial.



1
Introduction

The
Brauer group
of a scheme
X
is the second étale cohomology group
H^{2}_{\acute{e}t}(X,\mathbb{G}_{m})
. When
X
is a variety over a perfect field
k
, we have a natural map

\Br X\rightarrow(\Br X_{\overline{k}})^{\Gamma_{k}}

from Brauer classes of
X
to Galois-invariant Brauer classes of
X_{\overline{k}}
. Its kernel
\Br_{1}X
is the
algebraic Brauer group
of
X
, and its image
\Br_{tr}X
is the
transcendental Brauer group
of
X
.

In this note, we are interested in the Brauer group of a torus
T
over a field
k
of characteristic
0
. The algebraic Brauer group of
T
is well understood (see e.g. Section 9.1 of
[
5
]
), so we focus our attention on the transcendental Brauer group. It is known that the exponent of the quotient
(\Br X_{\overline{k}})^{\Gamma_{k}}/\Br_{tr}X
divides
2
, see e.g. the proof of
[
5
, Proposition 9.1.3]
in Colliot-Thélène and Skorobogatov’s book.
Our main theorem is:

Theorem 1.1
.

Let
k
be a field of characteristic
0
, and
T
be a
k
-torus. The identity
\Br_{tr}X=(\Br X_{\overline{k}})^{\Gamma_{k}}
holds if either:

(i)

the torus
T/k
is quasi-trivial;

(ii)

k
is a local or global field.

In case (i), we prove in fact a more precise result (see Theorem
3.1
), that provides an explicit basis of the finite abelian group
\Br T/\Br_{1}T
. The exact expression for the elements of this basis was inspired by some Hilbert symbols appearing in the work
[
14
]
(see also
[
10
, Theorem IX.9.3.2]
) of Shafarevich.
Case (ii) is proven by analyzing the exact sequence

0\rightarrow\Br T/\Br_{1}T\rightarrow(\Br T_{\overline{k}})^{\Gamma_{k}}\xrightarrow{d^{0,2}_{3}}H^{3}(k,\overline{k}[T]^{*}),

(1.1)

where
d^{0,2}_{3}
is the third-page Hochschild-Serre spectral sequence differential. Since the cohomological dimension of non-archimedean local fields is
2
, and when
k
is a number field
H^{3}(k,\overline{k}[T]^{*})=\bigoplus_{\begin{subarray}{c}v\in M_{K}\\
v\text{ real}\end{subarray}}H^{3}(k_{v},\overline{k}_{v}[T]^{*})
by the Poitou-Tate theorems, the only interesting case is that of
k=\mathbb{R}
. In this case, we use the theory of characteristic classes for the
Hochschild-Serre spectral sequence of group extensions developed by Charlap and Vasquez
[
3
]
[
4
]
(and a subsequent result by Sah
[
11
]
) to show that the differential
d^{0,2}_{3}
is trivial.

Remark
.

We ignore whether the injection
\Br T/\Br_{1}T\rightarrow(\Br T_{\overline{k}})^{\Gamma_{k}}
is an isomorphism for a general torus
T
defined over an arbitrary field
k
of characteristic
0
, with a slight suspicion that this should not be the case as there seems to be no transparent reason for the differential
d^{2,0}_{3}
to be trivial (our proof of its triviality in the real case heavily relies on the cyclicity of the absolute Galois group of
\mathbb{R}
).

Structure of the paper.
In Section
2
we fix some notation. In Section
3
we state and prove Theorem
3.1
. In Section
4
we state and prove Theorem
4.1
(i.e. Theorem
1.1
for real tori), and prove Theorem
1.1
. In the appendix, we show a compatibility result between different Hochschild-Serre spectral sequences (this is used in the proof of Theorem
4.1
).

Transcendental Brauer groups of quasi-trivial tori

Let

For

\Gamma_{E_{ij}}<\Gamma_{k}

ii.

\Gamma_{E^{\prime}_{ij}}<\Gamma_{k}

Note that

iv.

\sigma_{ij}
be the generator of the group

v.

n^{\prime}_{ij}\coloneqq\gcd(n_{ij},1+\chi(\sigma_{ij}))

We let

\Gamma_{k}
-action on the unordered pairs
\{y_{i},y_{j}\},1\leq i<j\leq r

be a pair of indices such that

be the collection of pairs
\{i(o),j(o)\}_{o\in O}

We fix an inverse system of roots of unity
(\zeta_{n})_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\subset\bar{k}^{*},\zeta_{m}^{m/n}=\zeta_{n}

.

Theorem 3.1
.

The following elements of
\Br k(Q)
lie in
\Br Q\subset\Br k(Q)
:

(I)

\cores_{E_{ij}/k}(y_{i},y_{j})_{n_{ij}}
for every pair
i<j
,

(II)

\cores_{E_{ij}/k}(y_{i},y_{j}-y_{i})_{n^{\prime}_{ij}}
for every pair
i<j
such that
E_{ij}/E^{\prime}_{ij}
is quadratic.

Moreover, the images of (I) and (II) under the natural map

\Br Q/\Br_{1}Q\rightarrow(\Br Q_{\overline{k}})^{\Gamma_{k}}

generate
(\Br Q_{\overline{k}})^{\Gamma_{k}}
and they form a basis of this abelian group when we restrict only to pairs
(i,j)
with
\{i,j\}\in R
.

Here the symbols
(-,-)_{m}\coloneqq(-,-)_{m,\zeta_{m}}
denote the Hilbert symbols

(E_{ij}(Q)^{*}/m)^{\otimes 2}\rightarrow(\Br E_{ij}(Q))_{m}(1)=(\Br E_{ij}(Q))_{m}

where
m=n_{ij}
or
n^{\prime}_{ij}
, and the symbols
\cores_{E_{ij}/k}
denote the corestriction maps
\Br E_{ij}(Q)\rightarrow\Br k(Q)
along the finite field extensions
E_{ij}(Q)/k(Q)
.

Remark 3.2
.

Since
y_{i}
and
y_{j}
define invertible functions on the scheme
Q\times_{k}E_{ij}
, that
\cores_{E_{ij}/k}(y_{i},y_{j})_{n_{ij}}
lies in
\Br Q
follows immediately from general corestriction formalism for schemes (see
[
5
, Sec. 3.8]
), so the only non-trivial part of the first statement of Theorem

is for elements (II).

Transcendental Brauer groups of real tori

Theorem 4.1
.

Let
T
be an algebraic torus over the real field
\mathbb{R}
. Then the natural map

\Br T\rightarrow(\Br T_{\mathbb{C}})^{\Gal(\mathbb{C}/\mathbb{R})}

is surjective.

To prove this, we use the theory of Charlap–Vasquez
[
3
]
[
4
]
. Let
G
be a semi-direct product

, where
N
is a free finitely generated abelian group, and
\pi

and relate them to the Hochschild-Serre differentials (see

We only need a relatively simple case of their theory (Theorem

]
[

]

Since

-free, the universal coefficient theorem gives an identification of

and thus of groups

(4.1)

(Charlap-Vasquez)

There exists a class

{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces{H^{2}(N,M_{univ})^{\pi}}}

{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces{H^{2}(\pi,H^{1}(N,M))}}
