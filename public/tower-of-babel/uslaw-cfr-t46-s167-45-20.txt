46 C.F.R. § 167.45-20 — -20 Examination and testing of pumps and fire-extinguishing equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:46:167.45-20
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-167.45-20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 167 (Public Nautical School Ships). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The inspectors will examine all pumps, hose, and other fire apparatus and will see that the hose is subjected to a pressure of 100 pounds to the square inch at each annual inspection and that the hose couplings are securely fastened.
