45 C.F.R. § 1800.9 — Disclosure of record to a person other than the individual to whom the record pertains.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1800.9
Creator: Harry S. Truman Scholarship Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1800.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1800 (Privacy Act Of 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Foundation will not disclose a record to any individual other than to the individual to whom the record pertains without receiving the prior written consent of the individual to whom the record pertains, unless the disclosure has been listed as a “routine use” in the Foundation's notices of its systems of records.
