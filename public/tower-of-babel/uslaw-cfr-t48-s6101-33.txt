48 C.F.R. § 6101.33 — 6101.33 Remand from appellate Court [Rule 33].
Citation: us:cfr:48:6101.33
Creator: Chapter 61—Civilian Board Of Contract Appeals, General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-6101.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 6101 (Contract Disputes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a Court remands a case to the Board for further proceedings, each party shall, within 30 days of receipt of the appellate mandate, recommend procedures to comply with the remand order. The Board will then issue an order on further proceedings.
