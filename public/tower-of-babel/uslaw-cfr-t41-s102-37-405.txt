41 C.F.R. § 102-37.405 — -37.405 SEA priority for DoD property.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-37.405
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-37.405
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-37 (37—Donation Of Surplus Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

SEAs have a priority over other SASP donees for DoD property, but only if DoD requests GSA to allocate surplus DoD property through a SASP for donation to a specific SEA. DoD must clearly identify the items and justify the request.
