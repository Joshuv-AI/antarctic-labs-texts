10 C.F.R. § 36.35 — Source rack protection.
Citation: us:cfr:10:36.35
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-36.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 36 (Licenses And Radiation Safety Requirements For Irradiators). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the product to be irradiated moves on a product conveyor system, the source rack and the mechanism that moves the rack must be protected by a barrier or guides to prevent products and product carriers from hitting or touching the rack or mechanism.
