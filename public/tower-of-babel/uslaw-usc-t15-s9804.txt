15 U.S.C. § 9804 — Travel and tourism strategy
Citation: us:usc:15:9804
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section9804
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 122 (Travel And Tourism). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not less frequently than once every 10 years, the Secretary of Commerce, in consultation with the United States Travel and Tourism Advisory Board, the Tourism Policy Council, the Secretary of State, and the Secretary of Homeland Security, shall develop and submit to Congress a 10-year travel and tourism strategy, which shall include—
(1)
the establishment of goals with respect to the number of annual international visitors to the United States and the annual amount of travel and tourism commerce in the United States during such 10-year period;
(2)
the resources needed to achieve the goals established pursuant to paragraph (1); and
(3)
recommendations for statutory or regulatory changes that would be necessary to achieve such goals.
