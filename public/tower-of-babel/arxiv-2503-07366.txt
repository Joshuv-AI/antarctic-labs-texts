Abstract
. We present a class of abelian groups that exhibit a high degree of freeness while possessing no non-trivial homomorphisms to a canonical free object. Unlike prior investigations, which primarily focused on torsion-free groups, our work broadens the scope to include groups with torsion. Our main focus is on reduced separable p p -groups, for which we formulate and prove the Trivial Duality Conjecture . Key tools in our analysis include the multi black-box method and the application of specific homological properties of relative trees. These techniques allow us to navigate the intricate interplay between freeness and duality in a broader class of abelian groups.
