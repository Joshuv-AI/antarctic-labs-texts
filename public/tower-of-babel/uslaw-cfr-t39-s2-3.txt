39 C.F.R. § 2.3 — Offices.
Citation: us:cfr:39:2.3
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-2.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 2 (General And Technical Provisions (Article Ii)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The principal office of the Postal Service is located in Washington, DC, with such regional and other offices and places of business as the Postmaster General establishes from time to time, or the business of the Postal Service requires.
