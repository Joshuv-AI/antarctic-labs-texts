43 C.F.R. § 3585.2-1 — -1 Leasable minerals.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3585.2-1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3585.2-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3580 (Special Leasing Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Leasing of deposits of leasable minerals shall be governed by the applicable regulations in parts 3500, 3510, 3520, 3530, 3540 and 3550 of this title.
