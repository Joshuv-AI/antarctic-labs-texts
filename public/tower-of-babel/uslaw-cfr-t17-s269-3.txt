17 C.F.R. § 269.3 — Form T-3, for application for qualification of trust indentures.
Citation: us:cfr:17:269.3
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-269.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 269 (Forms Prescribed Under The Trust Indenture Act Of 1939). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This form shall be filed pursuant to Rule 7a-1 (§ 260.7a-1 of this chapter) for applications for qualification of indentures pursuant to section 307(a) of the Trust Indenture Act of 1939, but only when securities to be issued thereunder are not required to be registered under the Securities Act of 1933 (15 U.S.C. 77a et seq.).
