26 U.S.C. § 5673 — Forfeiture for flagrant and willful removal of beer without taxpayment
Citation: us:usc:26:5673
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section5673
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 51 (Distilled Spirits, Wines, And Beer); Subchapter J (Penalties, Seizures, And Forfeitures Relating To Liquors); Part III (Penalty, Seizure, And Forfeiture Provisions Applicable To Beer And Brewing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For flagrant and willful removal of taxable beer for consumption or sale, with intent to defraud the United States of the tax thereon, all the right, title, and interest of each person who knowingly has suffered or permitted such removal, or has connived at the same, in the lands and buildings constituting the brewery shall be forfeited by a proceeding in rem in the District Court of the United States having jurisdiction thereof.
