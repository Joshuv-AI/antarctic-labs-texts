10 C.F.R. § 54.11 — Public inspection of applications.
Citation: us:cfr:10:54.11
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-54.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 54 (Requirements For Renewal Of Operating Licenses For Nuclear Power Plants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applications and documents submitted to the Commission in connection with renewal applications may be made available for public inspection in accordance with the provisions of the regulations contained in 10 CFR part 2.
