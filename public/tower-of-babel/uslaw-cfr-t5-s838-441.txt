5 C.F.R. § 838.441 — Computing lengths of service.
Citation: us:cfr:5:838.441
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-838.441
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 838 (Court Orders Affecting Retirement Benefits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The smallest unit of time that OPM will calculate in computing a formula in a court order is a month, even where the court order directs OPM to make a more precise calculation.
(b) If the court order states a formula using a specified simple or decimal fraction other than twelfth parts of a year, OPM will use the specified number to perform simple mathematical computations.
