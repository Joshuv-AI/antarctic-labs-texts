5 C.F.R. § 531.506 — Effective date of a quality step increase.
Citation: us:cfr:5:531.506
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-531.506
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 531 (Pay Under The General Schedule). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The quality step increase should be made effective as soon as practicable after it is approved.
