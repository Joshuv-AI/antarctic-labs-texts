41 C.F.R. § 115-1.103-50 — -1.103-50 Temporary-type changes to EPPMR.
Citation: us:cfr:41:115-1.103-50
Creator: Chapter 115—Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-115-1.103-50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 115-1 (1—Introduction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where required, temporary changes will be published as EPPMR-Temporary Regulations. Temporary Regulations will be cross-referenced to related EPPMR subparts and will indicate dates for compliance with, and cancellation of each issuance.
