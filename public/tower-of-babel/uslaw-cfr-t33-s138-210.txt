33 C.F.R. § 138.210 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:33:138.210
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-138.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 138 (Evidence Of Financial Responsibility For Water Pollution (Vessels) And Opa 90 Limits Of Liability (Vessels, Deepwater Ports And Onshore Facilities)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to you if you are a responsible party for a vessel, a deepwater port, or an onshore facility (including, but not limited to, motor vehicles, rolling stock and onshore pipelines), unless your liability is unlimited under OPA 90 (33 U.S.C. 2704(c)).
