11 C.F.R. § 300.62 — Non-Federal elections (52 U.S.C. 30125(e)(1)(B)).
Citation: us:cfr:11:300.62
Creator: Federal Election Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-300.62
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 300 (Non-Federal Funds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person described in 11 CFR 300.60 may solicit, receive, direct, transfer, spend, or disburse funds in connection with any non-Federal election, only in amounts and from sources that are consistent with State law, and that do not exceed the Act's contribution limits or come from prohibited sources under the Act.
