18 C.F.R. § 343.1 — Definitions.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 343 (Procedural Rules Applicable To Oil Pipeline Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of this part, the following definitions apply:
(a) Complaint means a filing challenging an existing rate or practice under section 13(1) of the Interstate Commerce Act.
(b) Protest means a filing, under section 15(7) of the Interstate Commerce Act, challenging a tariff publication.
