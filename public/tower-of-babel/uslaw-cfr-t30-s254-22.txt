30 C.F.R. § 254.22 — What information must I include in the “Introduction and OSRP contents” section?
Citation: us:cfr:30:254.22
Creator: Bureau Of Safety And Environmental Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-254.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 254 (Oil-Spill Response Requirements For Facilities Located Seaward Of The Coast Line). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The “Introduction and OSRP contents” section must provide:
(a) Identification of the facility the OSRP covers, including its location and type;
(b) A table of contents;
(c) A record of changes made to the OSRP; and
(d) A cross-reference table, if needed, because you are using an alternate format for your OSRP.
