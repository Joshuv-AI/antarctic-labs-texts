38 U.S.C. § 2001 — Purpose

Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 20 (Benefits For Homeless Veterans); Subchapter I (Purpose; Definitions; Administrative Matters). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of this chapter is to provide for the special needs of homeless veterans.
