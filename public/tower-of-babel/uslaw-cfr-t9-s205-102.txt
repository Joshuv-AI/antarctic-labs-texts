9 C.F.R. § 205.102 — Name of person subjecting a farm product to a security interest, on EFS and master list—format.
Citation: us:cfr:9:205.102
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Fair Trade Practices Program), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-205.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 205 (Clear Title—Protection For Purchasers Of Farm Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

On an EFS, and on a master list, the name of the person subjecting a farm product to a security interest must appear as follows:
(a) In the case of a natural person, the surname (last name or family name) must appear first;
(b) In the case of a corporation or other entity not a natural person, the name must appear beginning with the first word or character not an article or punctuation mark.
