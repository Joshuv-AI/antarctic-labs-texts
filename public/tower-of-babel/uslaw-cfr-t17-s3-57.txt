17 C.F.R. § 3.57 — Proceedings under section 8a(2)(E) of the Act.
Citation: us:cfr:17:3.57
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-3.57
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 3 (Registration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission will not initiate a proceeding under section 8a(2)(E) of the Act, if respondeat superior is the sole basis upon which the registrant may be found subject to a statutory disqualification.
