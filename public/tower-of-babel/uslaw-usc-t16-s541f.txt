16 U.S.C. § 541f — Withdrawal from location, entry and patent under mining laws; withdrawal from disposition under mineral leasing laws
Citation: us:usc:16:541f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section541f
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 2 (National Forests); Subchapter II (Scenic Areas). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The lands within the Area, subject to valid existing rights, are hereby withdrawn from location, entry, and patent under the United States mining laws and from disposition under all laws pertaining to mineral leasing and all amendments thereto.
