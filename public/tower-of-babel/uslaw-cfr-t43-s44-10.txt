43 C.F.R. § 44.10 — What is the purpose of this subpart?
Citation: us:cfr:43:44.10
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-44.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 44 (Financial Assistance, Local Governments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart sets forth procedures the Department of the Interior uses in disbursing Federal payments in lieu of taxes to local governments for entitlement lands within their boundaries.
