5 U.S.C. § 5507 — Officer affidavit; condition to pay
Citation: us:usc:5:5507
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section5507
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart D (Pay And Allowances); Chapter 55 (Pay Administration); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An officer required by section 3332 of this title to file an affidavit may not be paid until the affidavit has been filed.
