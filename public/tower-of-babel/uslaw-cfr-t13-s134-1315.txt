13 C.F.R. § 134.1315 — What are the effects of the Judge's decision on the procurement at issue?
Citation: us:cfr:13:134.1315
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-134.1315
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 134 (Rules Of Procedure Governing Cases Before The Office Of Hearings And Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Judge's decision is the final agency decision and becomes effective upon issuance. For the effects of the decision on the procurement at issue, see § 126.803(e) of this chapter.
