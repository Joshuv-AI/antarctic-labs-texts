19 U.S.C. § 1353 — Indebtedness of foreign countries, effect on
Citation: us:usc:19:1353
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section1353
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 4 (Tariff Act Of 1930); Part III (Promotion Of Foreign Trade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this part shall be construed to give any authority to cancel or reduce, in any manner, any of the indebtedness of any foreign country to the United States.
