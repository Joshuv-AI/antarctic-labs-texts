45 C.F.R. § 98.34 — Parental rights and responsibilities.
Citation: us:cfr:45:98.34
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-98.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 98 (Child Care And Development Fund). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing under this part shall be construed or applied in any manner to infringe on or usurp the moral and legal rights and responsibilities of parents or legal guardians.
