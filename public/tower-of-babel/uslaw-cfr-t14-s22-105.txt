14 C.F.R. § 22.105 — Control and maneuverability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:22.105
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-22.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 22 (Design, Production, And Airworthiness Requirements For Non-Type Certificated Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A light-sport category aircraft must—
(a) Be consistently and predictably controllable and maneuverable at all loading conditions during all phases of flight; and,
(b) Not have a tendency to inadvertently depart controlled flight or require exceptional piloting skill, alertness, or strength.
