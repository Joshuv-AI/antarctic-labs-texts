32 C.F.R. § 150.13 — Notice of appearance of counsel.
Citation: us:cfr:32:150.13
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-150.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 150 (Courts Of Criminal Appeals Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Military and civilian appellate counsel shall file a written notice of appearance with the Court. The filing of any pleading relative to a case which contains the signature of counsel constitutes notice of appearance of such counsel.
