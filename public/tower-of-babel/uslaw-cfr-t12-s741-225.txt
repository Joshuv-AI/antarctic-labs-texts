12 C.F.R. § 741.225 — Loan participations.
Citation: us:cfr:12:741.225
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-741.225
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 741 (Requirements For Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any credit union that is insured pursuant to Title II of the Act must adhere to the requirements stated in § 701.22 of this chapter, except that federally insured state-chartered credit unions are exempt from the requirement in § 701.22(b)(4).
