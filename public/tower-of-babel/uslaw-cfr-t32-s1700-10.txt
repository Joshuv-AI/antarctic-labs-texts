32 C.F.R. § 1700.10 — Other rights and services.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1700.10
Creator: Office Of The Director Of National Intelligence
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1700.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1700 (Procedures For Disclosure Of Records Pursuant To The Freedom Of Information Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this part shall be construed so as to enlarge, diminish, modify, or alter the powers or authority of the ODNI; or construed to entitle any person, as of right, to any service or to the disclosure of any record to which such person is not entitled under the FOIA.
