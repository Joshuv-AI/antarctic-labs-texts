20 C.F.R. § 234.17 — When an equitably entitled person's estate is payable.
Citation: us:cfr:20:234.17
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-234.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 234 (Lump-Sum Payments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When an equitably entitled person dies before negotiating the LSDP check, that person's share is payable to his or her estate.
