31 C.F.R. § 800.218 — Excepted foreign state.
Citation: us:cfr:31:800.218
Creator: Office Of Investment Security, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-800.218
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 800 (Regulations Pertaining To Certain Investments In The United States By Foreign Persons). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term excepted foreign state means, until February 13, 2023, a foreign state that meets the criteria in paragraph (a) of this section, and, beginning on February 13, 2023, a foreign state that meets both the criteria in paragraphs (a) and (b) of this section:
(a) Is identified by the Committee as an eligible foreign state, and
(b) Is a foreign state for which the Committee has made a determination under § 800.1001(a).
