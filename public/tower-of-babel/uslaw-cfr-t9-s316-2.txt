9 C.F.R. § 316.2 — Approval required for official marks.
Citation: us:cfr:9:316.2
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-316.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 316 (Marking Products And Their Containers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No device containing any official mark shall be made or caused to be made for use on any product until it has been approved by the Administrator as provided for in part 317 of this subchapter.
