7 C.F.R. § 4290.140 — Approval of initial Management Expenses.
Citation: us:cfr:7:4290.140
Creator: Rural Business-Cooperative Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-4290.140
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 4290 (Rural Business Investment Company (“Rbic”) Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A RBIC must have its Management Expenses approved by the Agency at the time it is licensed. (See § 4290.520 for the definition of Management Expenses.)
