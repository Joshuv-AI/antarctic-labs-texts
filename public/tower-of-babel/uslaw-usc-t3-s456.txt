3 U.S.C. § 456 — Confidentiality
Citation: us:usc:3:456
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title3-section456
Context: United States Code, Title 3 (The President); Chapter 5 (Extension Of Certain Rights And Protections To Presidential Offices); Subchapter III (Administrative And Judicial Dispute Resolution Procedures). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Counseling.—
All counseling under section 452 shall be strictly confidential, except that, with the consent of the covered employee, the employing office may be notified.
(b) Mediation.—
All mediation under section 452 shall be strictly confidential.
