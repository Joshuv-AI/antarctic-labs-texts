42 U.S.C. § 9858p — Severability
Citation: us:usc:42:9858p
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section9858p
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 105 (Community Services Programs); Subchapter II–B (Child Care And Development Block Grant). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this subchapter or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the invalidity shall not affect other provisions of applications of this subchapter which can be given effect without regard to the invalid provision or application, and to this end the provisions of this subchapter shall be severable.
