30 C.F.R. § 560.220 — How does royalty suspension apply to leases issued in a sale held after November 2000?
Citation: us:cfr:30:560.220
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-560.220
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 560 (Outer Continental Shelf Oil And Gas Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We may issue leases with suspension of royalties for a period, volume or value of production, as authorized in section 303 of the Act. For purposes of this section and §§ 560.221 through 560.224:
(a) Any volumes of production that are not normally royalty-bearing under the lease or the regulations (e.g., fuel gas) do not count against royalty suspension volumes; and
(b) Production includes volumes allocated to a lease under an approved unit agreement.
