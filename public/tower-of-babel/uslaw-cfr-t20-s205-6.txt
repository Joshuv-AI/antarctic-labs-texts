20 C.F.R. § 205.6 — Service of an employee representative.
Citation: us:cfr:20:205.6
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-205.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 205 (Employee Representative). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Service rendered as an employee representative is creditable in the same manner and to the same extent as though the organization by which the employee representative was employed were an employer under the Railroad Retirement Act. (Creditable railroad service is discussed under part 210 of the Board's regulations.)
