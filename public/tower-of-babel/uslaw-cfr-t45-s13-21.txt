45 C.F.R. § 13.21 — Filing and service of pleadings.
Citation: us:cfr:45:13.21
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-13.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 13 (Implementation Of The Equal Access To Justice Act In Agency Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All pleadings, including applications for an award of fees, answers, comments, and other pleadings related to the applications, shall be filed in the same manner as other pleadings in the proceeding and served on all other parties and participants, except as provided in § 13.11(b) of this part concerning confidential financial information.
