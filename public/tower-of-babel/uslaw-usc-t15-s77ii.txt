15 U.S.C. § 77ii — Subscriptions accepted by Corporation as loans; repayment
Citation: us:usc:15:77ii
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section77ii
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 2A (Securities And Trust Indentures); Subchapter II (Foreign Securities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Corporation may receive subscriptions from any person, foundation with a public purpose, or agency of the United States Government, and such subscriptions may, in the discretion of the board of directors, be treated as loans repayable when and as the board of directors shall determine.
