21 C.F.R. § 862.3120 — Arsenic test system.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 862 (Clinical Chemistry And Clinical Toxicology Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. An arsenic test system is a device intended to measure arsenic, a poisonous heavy metal, in urine, vomitus, stomach contents, nails, hair, and blood. Measurements obtained by this device are used in the diagnosis and treatment of arsenic poisoning.
(b) Classification. Class I.
