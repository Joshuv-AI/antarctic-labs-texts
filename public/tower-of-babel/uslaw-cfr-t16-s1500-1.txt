16 C.F.R. § 1500.1 — Scope of subchapter.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1500.1
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1500.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1500 (Hazardous Substances And Articles: Administration And Enforcement Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Set forth in this subchapter C are the regulations of the Consumer Product Safety Commission issued pursuant to and for the implementation of the Federal Hazardous Substances Act as amended (see § 1500.3(a)(1)).
