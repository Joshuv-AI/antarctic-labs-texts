7 C.F.R. § 33.7 — Less than carload lot.
Citation: us:cfr:7:33.7
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-33.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 33 (Requirements Under The Export Apple Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Less than carload lot means a quantity of apples in packages not exceeding 20,000 pounds gross weight or 400 standard boxes or equivalent.
