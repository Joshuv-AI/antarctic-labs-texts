39 U.S.C. § 208 — Reservation of powers
Citation: us:usc:39:208
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title39-section208
Context: United States Code, Title 39 (Postal Service); Part I (General); Chapter 2 (Organization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Congress reserves the power to alter, amend, or repeal any or all of the sections of this title, but no such alteration, amendment, or repeal shall impair the obligation of any contract made by the Postal Service under any power conferred by this title.
