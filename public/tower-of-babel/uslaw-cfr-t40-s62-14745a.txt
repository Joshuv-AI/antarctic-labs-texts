40 C.F.R. § 62.14745a — a What must I do if I close my air curtain incinerator and then restart it?
Citation: us:cfr:40:62.14745a
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-62.14745a
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 62 (Approval And Promulgation Of State Plans For Designated Facilities And Pollutants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If you close your incinerator but will reopen it prior to the final compliance date in this subpart, you must comply with the final standards in this subpart on January 10, 2025.
(b) If you close your incinerator but will restart it after January 10, 2025, you must complete emission control retrofits and meet the emission limitations under this subpart on the date your incinerator restarts operation.
