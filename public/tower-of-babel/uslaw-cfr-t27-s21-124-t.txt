27 C.F.R. § 21.124-T — -T Raffinate.
Citation: us:cfr:27:21.124-T
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-21.124-T
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 21 (Formulas For Denatured Alcohol And Rum). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) API Gravity at 60 °F. 30 to 85.
(b) Reid Vapor Pressure (PSI). 5 to 11.
(c) Octane (R+M/2). 66 to 70.
(d) Distillation ( °F):
(i) 10 percent. 120 to 150.
(ii) 50 percent. 144 to 180.
(iii) 90 percent. 168 to 200.
(iv) End point distillation. 216 to 285.
