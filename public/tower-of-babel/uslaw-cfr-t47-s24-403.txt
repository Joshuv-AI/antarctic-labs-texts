47 C.F.R. § 24.403 — Authorization required.
Citation: us:cfr:47:24.403
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-24.403
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 24 (Personal Communications Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person shall use or operate any device for the transmission of energy or communications by radio in the services authorized by this part except as provided in this part.
