18 C.F.R. § 1b.5 — b.5 Formal investigations.
Citation: us:cfr:18:1b.5
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-1b.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 1b (PART 1b—RULES RELATING TO INVESTIGATIONS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission may, in its discretion, initiate a formal investigation by issuing an Order of Investigation. Orders of Investigation will outline the basis for the investigation, the matters to be investigated, the officer(s) designated to conduct the investigation and their authority. The director of the office responsible for the investigation may add or delete Investigating Officers in the Order of Investigation.
