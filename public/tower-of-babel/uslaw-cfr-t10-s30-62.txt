10 C.F.R. § 30.62 — Right to cause the withholding or recall of byproduct material.
Citation: us:cfr:10:30.62
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-30.62
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 30 (Rules Of General Applicability To Domestic Licensing Of Byproduct Material). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission may cause the withholding or recall of byproduct material from any licensee who is not equipped to observe or fails to observe such safety standards to protect health as may be established by the Commission, or who uses such materials in violation of law or regulation of the Commission, or in a manner other than as disclosed in the application therefor or approved by the Commission.
