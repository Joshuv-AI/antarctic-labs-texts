24 C.F.R. § 990.220 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:24:990.220
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-990.220
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 990 (The Public Housing Operating Fund Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This policy is aimed at assisting all PHAs in transitioning to the new funding levels as determined by the formula set forth in this rule. PHAs will be subject to a transition funding policy that will either increase or reduce their total operating subsidy for a given year.
