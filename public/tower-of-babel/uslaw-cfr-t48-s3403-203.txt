48 C.F.R. § 3403.203 — 3403.203 Reporting suspected violations of the Gratuities clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:3403.203
Creator: Chapter 34—Department Of Education Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-3403.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 3403 (Improper Business Practices And Personal Conflicts Of Interest). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Suspected violations of the Gratuities clause at FAR 52.203-3 must be reported to the HCA in writing detailing the circumstances.
(b) The HCA evaluates the report with the assistance of the Designated Agency Ethics Officer. If the HCA determines that a violation may have occurred, the HCA refers the report to the SPE for disposition.
