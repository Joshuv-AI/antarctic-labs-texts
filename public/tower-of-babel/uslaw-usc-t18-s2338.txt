18 U.S.C. § 2338 — Exclusive Federal jurisdiction
Citation: us:usc:18:2338
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section2338
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 113B (Terrorism). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The district courts of the United States shall have exclusive jurisdiction over an action brought under this chapter.
