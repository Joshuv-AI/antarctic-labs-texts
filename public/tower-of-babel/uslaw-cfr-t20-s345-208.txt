20 C.F.R. § 345.208 — System records.
Citation: us:cfr:20:345.208
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-345.208
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 345 (Employers' Contributions And Contribution Reports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Effective January 1, 1990, the Board will establish and maintain records necessary to determine pooled charges, pooled credits, and unallocated charges for the experience rating system and will publish a notice with respect thereto no later than October 15 of each year. See § 345.302 of this part for the definition of these terms.
