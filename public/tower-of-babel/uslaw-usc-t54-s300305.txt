54 U.S.C. § 300305 — Historic conservation district
Citation: us:usc:54:300305
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section300305
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3003 (Definitions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this division, the term “historic conservation district” means an area that contains—
(1)
historic property;
(2)
buildings having similar or related architectural characteristics;
(3)
cultural cohesiveness; or
(4)
any combination of features described in paragraphs (1) to (3).
