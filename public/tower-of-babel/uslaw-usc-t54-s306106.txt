54 U.S.C. § 306106 — Review of plans of transferees of surplus federally owned historic property
Citation: us:usc:54:306106
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section306106
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3061 (Program Responsibilities And Authorities); Subchapter I (In General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall review and approve the plans of transferees of surplus federally owned historic property not later than 90 days after receipt of the plans to ensure that the prehistorical, historical, architectural, or culturally significant values will be preserved or enhanced.
