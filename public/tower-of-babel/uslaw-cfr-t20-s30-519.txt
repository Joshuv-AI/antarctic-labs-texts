20 C.F.R. § 30.519 — How does OWCP communicate its final decision concerning recovery of an overpayment?
Citation: us:cfr:20:30.519
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-30.519
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 30 (Claims For Compensation Under The Energy Employees Occupational Illness Compensation Program Act Of 2000, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) After considering any written documentation or argument submitted to OWCP within the 30-day period set out in § 30.512(d), OWCP will issue a final decision on the overpayment. OWCP will send a copy of the final decision to the individual from whom recovery is sought and his or her representative, if any.
(b) The provisions of subpart D of this part do not apply to any decision regarding the recovery of an overpayment.
