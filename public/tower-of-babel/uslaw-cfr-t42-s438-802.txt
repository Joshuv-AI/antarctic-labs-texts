42 C.F.R. § 438.802 — Basic requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:42:438.802
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-438.802
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 438 (Managed Care). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

FFP is available in expenditures for payments under an MCO contract only for the periods during which the contract—
(a) Meets the requirements of this part; and
(b) Is in effect.
