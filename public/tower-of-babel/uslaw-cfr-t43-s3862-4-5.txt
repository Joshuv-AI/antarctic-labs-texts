43 C.F.R. § 3862.4-5 — -5 Proof by applicant of publication and posting.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3862.4-5
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3862.4-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3860 (Mineral Patent Applications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After the 60-day period of newspaper publication has expired, the claimant will furnish from the office of publication a sworn statement that the notice was published for the statutory period, giving the first and last day of such publication, and his own statement showing that the plat and notice aforesaid remained conspicuously posted upon the claim sought to be patented during said 60-day publication, giving the dates.
