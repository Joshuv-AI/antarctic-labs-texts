40 C.F.R. § 420.134 — New source performance standards (NSPS).
Citation: us:cfr:40:420.134
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-420.134
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 420 (Iron And Steel Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

New sources subject to this subpart must achieve the following new source performance standards (NSPS), as applicable.
(a) Direct-reduced iron.
(b) Forging operations.
(c) Briquetting. There shall be no discharge of process wastewater pollutants to waters of the U.S.
