12 C.F.R. § 619.9250 — Participation certificates.
Citation: us:cfr:12:619.9250
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-619.9250
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 619 (Definitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Evidence of investment in a bank or association to which all the rights and obligations of stock attach with the exception of the right to vote in the affairs of the institution.
