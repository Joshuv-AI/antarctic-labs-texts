45 C.F.R. § 164.302 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:45:164.302
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-164.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 164 (Security And Privacy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A covered entity or business associate must comply with the applicable standards, implementation specifications, and requirements of this subpart with respect to electronic protected health information of a covered entity.
