20 C.F.R. § 230.5 — Exception concerning service to a local lodge or division.
Citation: us:cfr:20:230.5
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-230.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 230 (Months Annuities Not Payable By Reason Of Work). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In determining whether an annuity is subject to the provisions of this part the Board shall disregard any compensated service rendered after December 31, 1936, to a local lodge or division of a railway-labor-organization employer if the compensation for such service is required to be disregarded under the provisions of § 222.3(f) of this chapter.
