15 U.S.C. § 687m — Periodic issuance of guarantees and trust certificates
Citation: us:usc:15:687m
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section687m
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 14B (Small Business Investment Program); Subchapter III (Investment Division Programs); Part A (Small Business Investment Companies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administration shall issue guarantees under section 683 of this title and trust certificates under section 687l of this title at periodic intervals of not less than every 12 months and shall do so at such shorter intervals as its 1 deems appropriate, taking into consideration the amount and number of such guarantees or trust certificates.
