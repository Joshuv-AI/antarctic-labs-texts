43 C.F.R. § 3509.15 — Do I have to pay for a future interest lease?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3509.15
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3509.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3500 (Leasing Of Solid Minerals Other Than Coal And Oil Shale). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must pay fair market value for the mineral deposit when title vests in the United States. You also will be required to pay royalty on your production.
