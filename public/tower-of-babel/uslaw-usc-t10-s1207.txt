10 U.S.C. § 1207 — Disability from intentional misconduct or willful neglect: separation
Citation: us:usc:10:1207
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1207
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 61 (Retirement Or Separation For Physical Disability). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each member of the armed forces who incurs a physical disability that, in the determination of the Secretary concerned, makes him unfit to perform the duties of his office, grade, rank, or rating, and that resulted from his intentional misconduct or willful neglect or was incurred during a period of unauthorized absence, shall be separated from his armed force without entitlement to any benefits under this chapter.
