12 C.F.R. § 19.4 — Authority of the Comptroller.
Citation: us:cfr:12:19.4
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-19.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 19 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Comptroller may, at any time during the pendency of a proceeding, perform, direct the performance of, or waive performance of, any act which could be done or ordered by the ALJ.
