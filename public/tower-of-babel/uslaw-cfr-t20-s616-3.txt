20 C.F.R. § 616.3 — Interstate cooperation.
Citation: us:cfr:20:616.3
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-616.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 616 (Interstate Arrangement For Combining Employment And Wages). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each State agency will cooperate with every other State agency by implementing such rules, regulations, and procedures as may be prescribed for the operation of this arrangement. Each State agency shall identify the paying and the transferring State with respect to Combined-Wage Claims filed in its State.
