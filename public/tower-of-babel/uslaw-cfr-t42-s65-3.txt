42 C.F.R. § 65.3 — Who is eligible to apply for a grant?
Citation: us:cfr:42:65.3
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-65.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 65 (National Institute Of Environmental Health Sciences Hazardous Waste Worker Training). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Public and private nonprofit entities providing worker health and safety education and training may apply for grants under these regulations. Applicants for a grant may use services, as appropriate, of other public or private organizations necessary to develop, administer, or evaluate proposed worker training programs so long as the majority of the work is done by the applicant.
