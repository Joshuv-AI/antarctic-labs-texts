48 C.F.R. § 1436.602-2 — 1436.602-2 Evaluation boards.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1436.602-2
Creator: Chapter 14—Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1436.602-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1436 (Construction And Architect-Engineer Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

HCAs shall establish procedures for providing permanent or ad hoc architect-engineer evaluation boards. Bureau procedures shall provide for the appointment of private practitioners of architecture, engineering, or related professions when such action is determined by the HCA to be essential to meet the Government's minimum needs.
