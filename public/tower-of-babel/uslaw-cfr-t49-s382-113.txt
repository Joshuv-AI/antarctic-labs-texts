49 C.F.R. § 382.113 — Requirement for notice.
Citation: us:cfr:49:382.113
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-382.113
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 382 (Controlled Substances And Alcohol Use And Testing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before performing each alcohol or controlled substances test under this part, each employer shall notify a driver that the alcohol or controlled substances test is required by this part. No employer shall falsely represent that a test is administered under this part.
