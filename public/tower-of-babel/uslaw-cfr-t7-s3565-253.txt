7 C.F.R. § 3565.253 — Form of ownership.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3565.253
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3565.253
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3565 (Guaranteed Rural Rental Housing Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The property must be owned in fee simple or be subject to a ground lease or other legal right in land acceptable to the Agency.
