14 C.F.R. § 262.3 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:262.3
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-262.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 262 (Travel Credits Or Vouchers Due To A Serious Communicable Disease). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to all covered carriers that are the merchant of record for a covered flight or the operating carrier of a covered flight when a ticket agent is the merchant of record.
