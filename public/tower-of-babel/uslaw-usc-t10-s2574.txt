10 U.S.C. § 2574 — Armament: sale of individual pieces
Citation: us:usc:10:2574
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2574
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Property); Chapter 153 (Exchange Of Material And Disposal Of Obsolete, Surplus, Or Unclaimed Property). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A piece of armament that can be advantageously replaced, and that is not needed for its historical value, may be sold by the military department having jurisdiction over it for not less than cost, if the Secretary concerned considers that there are adequate sentimental reasons for the sale.
