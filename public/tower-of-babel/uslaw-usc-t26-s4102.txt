26 U.S.C. § 4102 — Inspection of records by local officers
Citation: us:usc:26:4102
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section4102
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 32 (Manufacturers Excise Taxes); Subchapter A (Automotive And Related Items); Part III (Petroleum Products); Subpart B (Special Provisions Applicable To Fuels Tax). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Under regulations prescribed by the Secretary, records required to be kept with respect to taxes under this part shall be open to inspection by such officers of a State, or a political subdivision of any such State, as shall be charged with the enforcement or collection of any tax on any taxable fuel (as defined in section 4083).
