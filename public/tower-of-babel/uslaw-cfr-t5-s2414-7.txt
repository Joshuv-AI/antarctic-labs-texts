5 C.F.R. § 2414.7 — Solicitation of prohibited communications.
Citation: us:cfr:5:2414.7
Creator: Federal Labor Relations Authority, General Counsel Of The Federal Labor Relations Authority And Federal Service Impasses Panel
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-2414.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 2414 (Ex Parte Communications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person shall knowingly and willfully solicit the making of an unauthorized ex parte communication by any other person.
