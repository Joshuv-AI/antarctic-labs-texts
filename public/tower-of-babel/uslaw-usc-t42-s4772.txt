42 U.S.C. § 4772 — Effective date of grant provisions
Citation: us:usc:42:4772
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section4772
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 62 (Intergovernmental Personnel Program); Subchapter IV (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Grant provisions of this chapter shall become effective one hundred and eighty days following January 5, 1971.
