36 C.F.R. § 1275.30 — Access by designees or assignees of former President Nixon.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1275.30
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1275.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1275 (Preservation And Protection Of And Access To The Presidential Historical Materials Of The Nixon Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In accordance with subpart B of this part, former President Richard M. Nixon's designated or assigned agent(s) at all times have access to Presidential historical materials in the custody and control of the Archivist.
