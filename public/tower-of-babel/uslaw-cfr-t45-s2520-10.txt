45 C.F.R. § 2520.10 — What is the purpose of the AmeriCorps subtitle C program described in parts 2520 through 2524 of this chapter?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2520.10
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2520.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2520 (General Provisions: Americorps Subtitle C Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of the AmeriCorps subtitle C program is to provide financial assistance under subtitle C of the National and Community Service Act to support AmeriCorps programs that address educational, public safety, human, or environmental needs through national and community service, and to provide AmeriCorps education awards to participants in such programs.
