Bipartite Turán problem on graph gluing
Zichao Dong, Jun Gao, Hong Liu

Abstract
For graphs H 1 H_{1} and H 2 H_{2} , if we glue them by identifying a given pair of vertices u ∈ V ⁡ ( H 1 ) u\in V(H_{1}) and v ∈ V ⁡ ( H 2 ) v\in V(H_{2}) , what is the extremal number of the resulting graph H 1 u ⊙ H 2 v H_{1}^{u}\odot H_{2}^{v} ? Introduction

The extremal number of a graph
H
, denoted by
\mathrm{ex}(n,H)
, is the maximum number of edges in an
n
-vertex
H
-free graph. Determining this function is one of the most important topics in extremal graph theory, originating from the classical work of Mantel
[
16
]
and Turán
[
18
]
. For any graph
H
and
n\to\infty
, the celebrated Erdős–Stone–Simonovits theorem
[
6
,
8
]
states that

\mathrm{ex}(n,H)=\Bigl(1-\frac{1}{\chi(H)}+o(1)\Bigr)\cdot\frac{n^{2}}{2},

where
\chi(H)
is the chromatic number of
H
. This result asymptotically resolves the problem for every non-bipartite
H
. However, for a bipartite
H
, it only shows
\mathrm{ex}(n,H)=o(n^{2})
. Complete comprehension of the behavior of
\mathrm{ex}(n,H)
for bipartite
H
remains elusive. The order of magnitude is known only for a handful of bipartite graphs. For more details, we refer the readers to the Füredi–Simonovits survey
[
10
]
.

As every graph can be built from smaller ones, a natural approach is to study how graph operations could affect the extremal function for bipartite graphs. In this paper, we investigate the following simple gluing operation: given bipartite
H_{1},H_{2}
and vertices
u\in V(H_{1}),\,v\in V(H_{2})
, denote by
H_{1}^{u}\odot H_{2}^{v}
the graph built from gluing
H_{1}
and
H_{2}
by identifying
u
and
v
. Throughout this paper, the big-oh, little-oh, and big-theta notations always hide the
n\to\infty
limit process. When a gluing
H_{1}^{u}\odot H_{2}^{v}
is considered, we implicitly assume that the vertex sets
V(H_{1}),V(H_{2})
are disjoint. We propose the following conjecture.

Conjecture 1.1
.

If
H_{1},H_{2}
are bipartite graphs and
u\in V(H_{1}),\,v\in V(H_{2})
, then

\mathrm{ex}(n,H_{1}^{u}\odot H_{2}^{v})=\Theta\bigl(\mathrm{ex}(n,H_{1})+\mathrm{ex}(n,H_{2})\bigr).

The following special case of
1.1
when
H_{1},H_{2}
are isomorphic is particularly interesting, as we shall see that it is closely related to the famous Zarankiewicz problem.

Conjecture 1.2
.

If
H_{1},H_{2}
are two copies of a bipartite graph
H
and
u\in V(H_{1}),\,v\in V(H_{2})
, then

\mathrm{ex}(n,H_{1}^{u}\odot H_{2}^{v})=\Theta\bigl(\mathrm{ex}(n,H)\bigr).

To explain the connection to the Zarankiewicz problem, define
\mathrm{ex}(n,m,H)
as the maximum number of edges of a subgraph of the complete bipartite graph
K_{n,m}
that does not contain
H
as a subgraph. The Zarankiewicz problem is an asymmetric version: given a bipartition
(L,R)
of a bipartite graph
H
, its Zarankiewicz number, denoted by
Z(n,m,H[L,R])
, is the maximum number of edges of a subgraph of
K_{n,m}
that does not contain
H
as a subgraph with
L
in the part of size
n
and
R
in the part of size
m
. When
n=m
, we write
Z(n,n,H)\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\mbox{\tiny def}}}{{=}}Z(n,n,H[L,R])=Z(n,n,H[R,L])
for ease of notations.

It follows directly from the definitions that
Z(n,n,H)\geq\mathrm{ex}(n,n,H)
and
\mathrm{ex}(2n,H)\geq\mathrm{ex}(n,n,H)
. Since every graph contains a balanced bipartite subgraph with at least half of its edges, we have that
\mathrm{ex}(n,n,H)\geq\frac{1}{2}\mathrm{ex}(2n,H)
. For the relationship between
\mathrm{ex}(n,n,H)
and
Z(n,n,H)
, Erdős and Simonovits
[
17
]
made the following conjecture (see
[
10
, Conjecture 2.12]
).

Conjecture 1.3

(
[
17
]
)
.

If
H
is a bipartite graph, then

Z(n,n,H)=\Theta\bigl(\mathrm{ex}(n,n,H)\bigr).

Let
H_{1}
and
H_{2}
be two copies of a bipartite graph
H
with bipartition
(A,B)
. For any
u\in A
and
v\in B
, we have
Z(n,n,H)\leq\mathrm{ex}(n,n,H_{1}^{u}\odot H_{2}^{v})
. So,
1.2
implies
1.3
.

Our first result shows that these three conjectures are in fact equivalent to each other.

Theorem 1.4
.

1.1
,
1.2
, and
1.3
are all equivalent.

Considering
1.2
, our next result states that if the two vertices we merge come from the same part of the graph, then the conjecture holds.

Theorem 1.5
.

Let
H_{1}
and
H_{2}
be two copies of a bipartite graph
H
with bipartition
(A,B)
. If vertices
u\in V(H_{1}),\,v\in V(H_{2})
satisfy that

are both from
A

\mathrm{ex}(n,H_{1}^{u}\odot H_{2}^{v})=\Theta\bigl(\mathrm{ex}(n,H)\bigr).

Proposition 2.3

(
[
9

)

Let

and

\mathbb{P}\bigl[|X-\mu|\geq\delta\mu\bigr]\leq 2e^{-\delta^{2}\mu/3}

Every

contains a subgraph
H

.

For any

, there exists some sufficiently large
