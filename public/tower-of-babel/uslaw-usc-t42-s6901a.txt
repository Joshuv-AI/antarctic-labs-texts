42 U.S.C. § 6901a — Congressional findings: used oil recycling
Citation: us:usc:42:6901a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section6901a
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 82 (Solid Waste Disposal); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Congress finds and declares that—
(1)
used oil is a valuable source of increasingly scarce energy and materials;
(2)
technology exists to re-refine, reprocess, reclaim, and otherwise recycle used oil;
(3)
used oil constitutes a threat to public health and the environment when reused or disposed of improperly; and
that, therefore, it is in the national interest to recycle used oil in a manner which does not constitute a threat to public health and the environment and which conserves energy and materials.
