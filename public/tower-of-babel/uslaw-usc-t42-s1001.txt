42 U.S.C. § 1001 — Basic entitlement to benefits
Citation: us:usc:42:1001
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1001
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 7 (Social Security); Subchapter VIII (Special Benefits For Certain World War Ii Veterans). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Every individual who is a qualified individual under section 1002 of this title shall, in accordance with and subject to the provisions of this subchapter, be entitled to a monthly benefit paid by the Commissioner of Social Security for each month after September 2000 (or such earlier month, if the Commissioner determines is administratively feasible) the individual resides outside the United States.
