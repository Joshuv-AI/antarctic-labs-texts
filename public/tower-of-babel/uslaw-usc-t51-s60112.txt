51 U.S.C. § 60112 — Transfer of Landsat 6 program responsibilities
Citation: us:usc:51:60112
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section60112
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 601 (Land Remote Sensing Policy); Subchapter II (Landsat). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The responsibilities of the Secretary with respect to Landsat 6 shall be transferred to the Landsat Program Management, as agreed to between the Secretary and the Landsat Program Management, pursuant to section 60111 of this title.
