20 C.F.R. § 418.3655 — How will we notify you of our decision after our review?
Citation: us:cfr:20:418.3655
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-418.3655
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 418 (Medicare Subsidies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We will mail a written notice of our decision on the issue(s) you appealed to you at your last known address. Generally, we will not send a notice if your subsidy stops because of your death. The written notice that we send will tell you:
(a) What our decision is;
(b) The reasons for our decision;
(c) The effect of our decision; and
(d) Your right to judicial review of the decision.
