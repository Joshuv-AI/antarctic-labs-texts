31 U.S.C. § 1102 — Fiscal year
Citation: us:usc:31:1102
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section1102
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 11 (The Budget And Fiscal, Budget, And Program Information). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The fiscal year of the Treasury begins on October 1 of each year and ends on September 30 of the following year. Accounts of receipts and expenditures required under law to be published each year shall be published for the fiscal year.
