41 C.F.R. § 105-53.136 — -53.136 Office of Congressional Affairs.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-53 (53—Statement Of Organization And Functions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Office of Congressional Affairs, headed by the Associate Administrator for Congressional Affairs, is responsible for directing and coordinating the legislative and congressional activities of GSA.
