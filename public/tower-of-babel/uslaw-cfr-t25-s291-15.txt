25 C.F.R. § 291.15 — How long do Class III gaming procedures remain in effect?
Citation: us:cfr:25:291.15
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-291.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 291 (Class Iii Gaming Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Class III gaming procedures remain in effect for the duration specified in the procedures or until amended pursuant to § 291.14.
