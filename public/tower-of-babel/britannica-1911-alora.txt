ÁLORA, a town of southern Spain in the province of Málaga;
17 m. W.N.W. of Málaga, on the right bank of the river Guadalhorce,
and on the Cordova-Málaga railway. Pop. (1900) 10,525.
Álora, which is an ancient and picturesque town, with several
Moorish ruins, occupies an outlying hill of the Sierra de Tolox,
and overlooks a fertile valley where maize, sugar-cane and date-palms
are cultivated. There are hot sulphurous springs in the
town, which has also a fine climate; and many of the wealthy
families from Málaga reside here in summer. Brandy distilling
is, after agriculture, the chief local industry.
