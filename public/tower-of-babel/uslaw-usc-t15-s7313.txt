15 U.S.C. § 7313 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:15:7313
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 99 (National Construction Safety Team). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The National Institute of Standards and Technology is authorized to use funds otherwise authorized by law to carry out this chapter.
