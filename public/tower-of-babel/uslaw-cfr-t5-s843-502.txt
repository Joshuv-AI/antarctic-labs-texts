5 C.F.R. § 843.502 — Eligibility.
Citation: us:cfr:5:843.502
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-843.502
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 843 (Federal Employees Retirement System—Death Benefits And Employee Refunds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An insurable interest beneficiary is eligible for an annuity under this subpart upon the death of a retiree if the retiree had elected (under § 842.606 of this chapter) to receive an insurable interest rate with the insurable interest beneficiary as his or her survivor.
