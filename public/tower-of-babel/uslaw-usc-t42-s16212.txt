42 U.S.C. § 16212 — High power density industry program
Citation: us:usc:42:16212
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section16212
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 149 (National Energy Policy And Programs); Subchapter IX (Research And Development); Part B (Distributed Energy And Electric Energy Systems). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The Secretary shall establish a comprehensive research, development, demonstration, and commercial application to improve the energy efficiency of high power density facilities, including data centers, server farms, and telecommunications facilities.
(b) Technologies
The program shall consider technologies that provide significant improvement in thermal controls, metering, load management, peak load reduction, or the efficient cooling of electronics.
