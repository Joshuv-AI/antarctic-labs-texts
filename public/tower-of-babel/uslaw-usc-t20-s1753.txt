20 U.S.C. § 1753 — Uniform rules of evidence requirement
Citation: us:usc:20:1753
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1753
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 39 (Equal Educational Opportunities And Transportation Of Students); Subchapter II (Assignment And Transportation Of Students). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The rules of evidence required to prove that State or local authorities are practicing racial discrimination in assigning students to public schools shall be uniform throughout the United States.
