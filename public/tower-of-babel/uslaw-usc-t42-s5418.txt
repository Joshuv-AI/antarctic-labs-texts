42 U.S.C. § 5418 — Use of services, research and testing facilities of public agencies and independent laboratories
Citation: us:usc:42:5418
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section5418
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 70 (Manufactured Home Construction And Safety Standards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary, in exercising the authority under this chapter, shall utilize the services, research and testing facilities of public agencies and independent testing laboratories to the maximum extent practicable in order to avoid duplication.
