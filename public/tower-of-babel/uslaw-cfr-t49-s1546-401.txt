49 C.F.R. § 1546.401 — Applicability of this subpart.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1546 (Foreign Air Carrier Security). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies when the aircraft operator is conducting inspections as provided in § 1546.207.
