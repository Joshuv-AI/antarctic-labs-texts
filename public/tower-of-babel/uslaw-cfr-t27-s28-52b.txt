27 C.F.R. § 28.52b — b Execution of powers of attorney.
Citation: us:cfr:27:28.52b
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-28.52b
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 28 (Exportation Of Alcohol). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The power of attorney shall be prepared on a form provided by the surety company and executed under the corporate seal of the company. If the power of attorney submitted is other than a manually signed original, it shall be accompanied by certification of its validity.
