46 U.S.C. § 53304 — Vessel ownership
Citation: us:usc:46:53304
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section53304
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part C (Financial Assistance Programs); Chapter 533 (Construction Reserve Funds). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter, a vessel is deemed to be constructed or acquired by a taxpayer if constructed or acquired by a corporation when the taxpayer owns at least 95 percent of each class of stock of the corporation.
