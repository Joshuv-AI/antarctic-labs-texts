49 C.F.R. § 1103.11 — Standards of ethical conduct in courts of the United States to be observed.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1103.11
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1103.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1103 (Practitioners). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These canons further the purpose of the Board's Rules of Practice which direct all persons appearing in proceedings before it to conform, as nearly as possible, to the standards of ethical conduct required of practice before the courts of the United States. Such standards are taken as the basis for these specifications and are modified as the nature of the practice before the Board requires.
