22 C.F.R. § 34.1 — Purpose.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 34 (Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These regulations prescribe the procedures to be used by the United States Department of State (STATE) in the collection of debts owed to STATE and to the United States.
