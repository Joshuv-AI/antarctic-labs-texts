16 U.S.C. § 698t — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:698t
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 6 (Game And Bird Preserves; Protection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as are necessary to carry out sections 698q to 698t of this title.
