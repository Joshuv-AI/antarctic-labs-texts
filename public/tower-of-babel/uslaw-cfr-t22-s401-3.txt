22 C.F.R. § 401.3 — Permanent offices.
Citation: us:cfr:22:401.3
Creator: International Joint Commission, United States And Canada
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-401.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 401 (Rules Of Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The permanent offices of the Commission shall be at Washington, in the District of Columbia, and at Ottawa, in the Province of Ontario, and, subject to the directions of the respective chairmen acting for their respective sections, the secretaries of the United States and Canadian sections of the Commission shall have full charge and control of said offices, respectively.
