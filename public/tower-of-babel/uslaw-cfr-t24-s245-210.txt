24 C.F.R. § 245.210 — Availability of information.
Citation: us:cfr:24:245.210
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-245.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 245 (Tenant Participation In Multifamily Housing Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A mortgagor subject to the requirements of this subpart shall make available to tenants any information concerning rent subsidies or other public assistance that is prepared and distributed by HUD to the project for the purpose of distribution to tenants.
