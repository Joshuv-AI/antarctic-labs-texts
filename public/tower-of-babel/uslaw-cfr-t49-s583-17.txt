49 C.F.R. § 583.17 — Reporting.
Citation: us:cfr:49:583.17
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-583.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 583 (Automobile Parts Content Labeling). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For each model year, manufacturers shall submit to the Administrator 3 copies of the information required by § 583.5(a) to be placed on a label for each carline. The information for each carline shall be submitted not later than the date the first vehicle of the carline is offered for sale to the ultimate purchaser.
