7 C.F.R. § 4288.103 — Review or appeal rights.
Citation: us:cfr:7:4288.103
Creator: Rural Business-Cooperative Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-4288.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 4288 (Payment Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person may seek a review of an Agency decision or appeal to the National Appeals Division in accordance with 7 CFR part 11 of this title.
