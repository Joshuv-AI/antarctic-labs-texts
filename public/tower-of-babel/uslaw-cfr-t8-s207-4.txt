8 C.F.R. § 207.4 — Approved application.
Citation: us:cfr:8:207.4
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-207.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 207 (Admission Of Refugees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Approval of a refugee application by USCIS outside the United States authorizes CBP to admit the applicant conditionally as a refugee upon arrival at the port within four months of the date the refugee application was approved. There is no appeal from a denial of refugee status under this chapter.
