9 C.F.R. § 95.31 — Animal manure.
Citation: us:cfr:9:95.31
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-95.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 95 (Sanitary Control Of Animal Byproducts (Except Casings), And Hay And Straw, Offered For Entry Into The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Manure of horses, cattle, sheep, other ruminants, and swine shall not be imported except upon permission from the Deputy Administrator, Veterinary Services. Importations permitted shall be subject to such restrictions as he may deem necessary in each instance: Provided, however, That manure produced by animals while in transit to the United States shall be subject only to the requirements of the Department regulations governing the importation of domestic livestock and other animals.
