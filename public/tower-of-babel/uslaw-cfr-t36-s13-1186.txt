36 C.F.R. § 13.1186 — What are the emission standards for vessels?
Citation: us:cfr:36:13.1186
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-13.1186
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 13 (National Park System Units In Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The State of Alaska statutes and regulations applicable to marine vessel emission standards are adopted as a part of these regulations.
(b) Violating a State of Alaska statute or regulation applicable to marine vessel visible emission standards is prohibited.
