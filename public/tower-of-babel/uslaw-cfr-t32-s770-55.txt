32 C.F.R. § 770.55 — Responsibility.
Citation: us:cfr:32:770.55
Creator: Department Of The Navy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-770.55
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 770 (Rules Limiting Public Access To Particular Installations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The responsibility for proper identification and control of personnel and vehicle movement on the Portsmouth Naval Shipyard is vested with the Shipyard Security Manager (Code 1700).
