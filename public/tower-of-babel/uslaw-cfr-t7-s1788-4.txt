7 C.F.R. § 1788.4 — Disclosure of irregularities and illegal acts.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1788.4
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1788.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1788 (Rus Fidelity And Insurance Requirements For Electric And Telecommunications Borrowers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Borrowers must immediately report, in writing, all irregularities and all indications or instances of illegal acts in its operations, whether material or not, to RUS and the Office of the Inspector General (OIG). See 7 CFR 1773.9(c)(3) for OIG addresses. The reporting requirements for borrowers are the same as those for CPA's set forth in § 1773.9
(b) Borrowers are required to make full disclosure to the bonding company of the dishonest or fraudulent acts.
