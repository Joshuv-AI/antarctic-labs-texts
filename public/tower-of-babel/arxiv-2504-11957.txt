Unconditional robustness of multipartite entanglement of superposition
Hui-Hui Qin 1, Shao-Shuai Zhao 2, Shao-Ming Fei 3

Abstract
We study the robustness of genuine multipartite entanglement and inseparability of multipartite pure states under superposition with product pure states. We introduce the concept of the maximal and the minimal Schmidt ranks for multipartite states. From the minimal Schmidt rank of the first order we present criterion of verifying unconditional robustness of genuine multipartite entanglement of multipartite pure states under superposition with product pure states. By the maximal Schmidt rank of the first order we verify the unconditional robustness of multipartite inseparability under superposition with product pure states. The number of product states superposed to a given entangled state which result in a separable state is investigated in detail. Furthermore, the minimal Schmidt ranks of the second order are also introduced to identify the unconditional robustness of an entangled state for tripartite inseparability.



I
1. Introduction

Quantum entanglement, especially genuine multipartite entanglement (GME)
[
1
]
plays an important role in quantum information processing
[
2
]
and quantum physics
[
3
]
, with significant applications in fields such as quantum teleportation
[
4
]
, quantum dense coding
[
5
]
and quantum key distribution
[
6
]
. Entanglement is basically due to the superposition principle in quantum physics
[
7
]
. However, superposition of an entangled pure state and product pure states may not always lead to entanglement. Here, the Schmidt rank of bipartite pure states
[
8
,
7
]
plays an important role both in the entanglement clarification of quantum states
[
9
,
10
,
11
]
and in the purification of quantum systems
[
12
]
. Halder and Sen showed in
[
13
]
that any superposition of a bipartite entangled pure state and a product pure state produces only an entangled state if the initial entangled state has Schmidt rank no less than two, independent of the superposition coefficients and the choice of product states. The original entangled state is then called unconditionally robust in entanglement under superpositions, or unconditional inseparability under superpositions.

Since there are no longer universal Schmidt decompositions for multipartite quantum systems, the problem of unconditional robustness or unconditional inseparability under superpositions becomes more complicated. The superposition of a multipartite entangled state and a product state may result in both entanglement including GME and full separability. For example, a superposition of the maximally genuinely multipartite entangled state
|\psi_{+}\rangle=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(|000\rangle+|111\rangle)
and
|000\rangle
can be fully separable
\sqrt{2}|\psi_{+}\rangle-|000\rangle=|111\rangle
. Another superposition of
|\psi_{+}\rangle
and
|011\rangle
can be genuinely multipartite entangled
a|\psi_{+}\rangle+b|011\rangle
.

In this study, we first introduce the concepts of the minimal and the maximal Schmidt rank of the first order for multipartite pure states. Based on these Schmidt ranks we obtain two criteria that identify whether a genuinely multipartite entangled state is unconditionally robust in GME and in inseparability, respectively in the second section. Also in the second section the corresponding Schmidt rank of the second order is also defined, which clarifies whether a superposition of a genuinely multipartite entangled pure state and a set of fully product states is triple separable. A series of detailed examples are given to illustrate all these conclusions. In the third section we consider the minimal number of superposed product states for vanishing the entanglement of a given bipartite pure state. One step further we also consider the number of superposed fully product states for vanishing the genuinely multipartite entanglement of a given multipartite pure state. In the last section some possible generalizations which might be considered are put forward.

II
2. Unconditionally robust in GME and inseparability under superposition

Let
\mathcal{H}_{A_{i}}
be the Hilbert space associated with the
i
th subsystem
A_{i}~(i=1,2,\ldots,n)
of the multipartite system
A=A_{1}A_{2}\ldots A_{n}
. We denote
\mathcal{G}_{2}(A)
the set of all bi-partitions of
A
. We then define the minimal (maximal) Schmidt rank of the first order of an n-partite pure state
|\psi\rangle\in\otimes^{n}_{i=1}\mathcal{H}_{A_{i}}
as

\displaystyle r^{1}_{{\rm min}}\hat{=}\min_{X|\bar{X}\in\mathcal{G}_{2}(A)}Rank[\rho_{X}],

(1)

\displaystyle r^{1}_{{\rm max}}\hat{=}\max_{X|\bar{X}\in\mathcal{G}_{2}(A)}Rank[\rho_{X}],

where
{\rm Rank}[\rho_{X}]
is the rank of the reduced density matrix
\rho_{X}=Tr_{X}|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|
of
|\psi\rangle
.

According to the above definition we have the following conclusion,

Lemma 1
.

An
n
-partite pure state
|\psi\rangle\in\otimes^{n}_{i=1}\mathcal{H}_{A_{i}}
is genuinely multipartite entangled if and only if
r^{1}_{{\rm min}}\geq 2
, and entangled if and only if
r^{1}_{{\rm max}}\geq 2
.

Proof.

It is obvious that
|\psi\rangle\in\otimes^{n}_{i=1}\mathcal{H}_{A_{i}}
is genuinely multipartite entangled if and only if there exists no bi-partition
X|\bar{X}\in\mathcal{G}_{2}(A)
satisfying that
|\psi\rangle=|\psi_{X}\rangle\otimes|\psi_{\bar{X}}\rangle
. That is to say
{\rm Rank}\Big[Tr_{X}|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|\Big]\geq 2
for arbitrary bi-partition
X|\bar{X}\in\mathcal{G}_{2}(A)
. Equivalently,
r^{1}_{{\rm min}}\geq 2
, while
|\psi\rangle
is entangled if and only if there exists at least one bi-partition
Y|\bar{Y}\in\mathcal{G}_{2}(A)
such that
{\rm Rank}[\rho_{Y}]\geq 2
, i.e.,
r^{1}_{{\rm max}}\geq{\rm Rank}[\rho_{Y}]\geq 2
.

Unconditional inseparability of superposition is defined in
[
13
]
. A bipartite entangled pure state
|\psi\rangle
is unconditionally robust in inseparability under superposition, if for arbitrary
p\in(0,1)
and arbitrary product state
|p\rangle=|\alpha\rangle\otimes|\beta\rangle
such that the superposed state
\sqrt{p}|\psi\rangle+\sqrt{1-p}|p\rangle
cannot be separable. For multipartite entangled pure states, inspired by
[
13
]
we first give the following definition. If it is impossible to obtain a bi-separable state by superposing a genuinely multipartite entangled pure state
|\psi\rangle
and any product pure states, we call
|\psi\rangle

unconditionally robust in GME
under superposition.
If
|\psi\rangle
is entangled, but not necessarily genuinely multipartite entangled, we say that
|\psi\rangle
is
unconditionally robust in inseparability
under superposition if no fully separable states can be obtained by superposing
|\psi\rangle
with any product states nontrivially. Obviously, if a multipartite pure state
|\psi\rangle
is unconditionally robust in GME under superposition, then it must be unconditionally robust in inseparability under superposition. According to our definition of Schmidt ranks of first order, we have

Theorem 1
.

Any nontrivial superposition of a
n
-partite genuinely multipartite entangled pure state
|\psi\rangle
with
r^{1}_{{\rm min}}\geq 3
and arbitrary product pure states is still genuinely multipartite entangled; namely, arbitrary genuinely multipartite entangled pure state with
r^{1}_{{\rm min}}\geq 3
is unconditionally robust in GME under superposition. More precisely, any nontrivial superposition of
|\psi\rangle
and
r^{1}_{min}-2
product pure states gives rise to a genuinely multipartite entangled state.

Proof.

Assume that under the bi-partition
Y|\bar{Y}
of
|\psi\rangle
one gets the minimal Schmidt rank
r^{1}_{{\rm min}}
of the first order, i.e.,
{\rm Rank}[\rho_{Y}]={\rm Rank}[Tr_{Y}|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|]=r^{1}_{{\rm min}}
. Then
|\psi\rangle
can be decomposed into Schmidt form,

|\psi\rangle=\sum^{r^{1}_{{\rm min}}}_{i=1}a_{i}|i_{Y}\rangle|i_{\bar{Y}}\rangle,

where
\{|i_{Y}\rangle\}
and
\{|i_{\bar{Y}}\rangle\}
are respectively orthonormal sets in subspaces
Y
and
\bar{Y}
,
\sum_{i}|a_{i}|^{2}=1
. Consider the superposition of
|\psi\rangle
and an arbitrary product pure state,
|p\rangle=\otimes^{n}_{i=1}|\alpha_{i}\rangle=\left(\underset{|\alpha_{i}\rangle\in Y}{\otimes}|\alpha_{i}\rangle\right)\otimes\left(\underset{|\alpha_{k}\rangle\in\bar{Y}}{\otimes}|\alpha_{k}\rangle\right)\hat{=}|\alpha_{Y}\rangle\otimes|\alpha_{\bar{Y}}\rangle
. According to Theorem 1 in
[
13
]
any superposed state
|\psi^{\prime}\rangle=\lambda|\psi\rangle+\mu|p\rangle=\lambda|\psi\rangle+\mu|\alpha_{Y}\rangle\otimes|\alpha_{\bar{Y}}\rangle
with nontrivial superposition coefficient
\lambda\neq 0
satisfies that
{\rm Rank}[Tr_{Y}|\psi^{\prime}\rangle\langle\psi^{\prime}|]\geq r^{1}_{{\rm min}}-1\geq 2
. Then for arbitrary bi-partition
X|\bar{X}\in\mathcal{G}_{2}
,
|\psi\rangle
can be decomposed into another Schmidt form
|\psi\rangle=\sum^{r}_{k=1}b_{i}|k_{X}\rangle|k_{\bar{X}}\rangle
, where
r={\rm Rank}[Tr_{X}|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|]\geq r^{1}_{{\rm min}}
is the Schmidt rank in this bi-partition. Therefore, the superposed state
|\psi^{\prime}\rangle
satisfies that
{\rm Rank}[Tr_{X}|\psi^{\prime}\rangle\langle\psi^{\prime}|]\geq r-1\geq r^{1}_{{\rm min}}-1\geq 2
. This means that
|\psi^{\prime}\rangle
is genuinely multipartite entangled, and
|\psi\rangle
is unconditionally robust in GME under superposition.

According to the generalization of Theorem 1 in
[
13
]
, by superposing
|\psi\rangle
and
r^{1}_{{\rm min}}-2
fully product pure states
\{|p_{i}\rangle\}^{r^{1}_{{\rm min}}-2}_{i=1}
we finally get a state
|\tilde{\psi}\rangle=\lambda|\psi\rangle+\sum^{r^{1}_{{\rm min}}-2}_{i=1}\mu_{i}|p_{i}\rangle
with nontrivial superposition coefficient
\lambda\neq 0
satisfying

{\rm Rank}[Tr_{X}|\tilde{\psi}\rangle\langle\tilde{\psi}|]\geq r^{1}_{{\rm min}}-(r^{1}_{{\rm min}}-2)=2

for arbitrary bi-partition
X|\bar{X}\in\mathcal{G}_{2}
. It implies that
|\tilde{\psi}\rangle
is still a genuinely multipartite entangled state.

Theorem 1
characterizes a class of multipartite pure states that is unconditionally robust in GME under superposition. In addition to the unconditionally robust in GME under superposition, we have following conclusion for the unconditionally robust in inseparability under superposition.

Theorem 2
.

Any nontrivial superposition of an entangled pure state (may not be genuinely multipartite entangled)
|\psi\rangle
with
r^{1}_{{\rm max}}\geq 3
and
r^{1}_{{\rm max}}-2
product pure states gives rise to an entangled state.

Proof.

The proof is similar to the proof of
Theorem 1
. Assume that under the bi-partition
Y|\bar{Y}
the state
|\psi\rangle
attains the Schmidt Rank
r^{1}_{{\rm max}}
, i.e.,
|\psi\rangle
can be decomposed into the form

|\psi\rangle=\sum^{r^{1}_{{\rm max}}}_{i=1}c_{i}|i_{Y}\rangle|i_{\bar{Y}}\rangle,

where
\sum^{r^{1}_{{\rm max}}}_{i=1}|c_{i}|^{2}=1
. According to the generalization of Theorem 1 in
[
13
]
any nontrivial superposition of
|\psi\rangle
and a set of fully product states
\{|p_{i}\rangle=|\alpha^{i}_{1}\rangle\otimes|\alpha^{i}_{2}\rangle\otimes\cdots\otimes|\alpha^{i}_{n}\rangle=|\alpha^{i}_{Y}\rangle\otimes|\alpha^{i}_{\bar{Y}}\rangle|\}^{r^{1}_{{\rm max}}-2}_{i=1}
still gives rise to an entangled state under this bi-partition.

The theorems above can be also understood as follows. For a genuinely multipartite entangled pure state
|\psi\rangle
with
r^{1}_{{\rm max}}\geq r^{1}_{{\rm min}}\geq 3
, one cannot obtain any bi-separable state by superposing
|\psi\rangle
with
r^{1}_{{\rm min}}-2
product pure states according to
Theorem 1
.
Theorem 2
tells us that no fully separable state can be obtained by superposing
|\psi\rangle
with
r^{1}_{{\rm max}}-2
product pure states. Next, we introduce the minimal Schmidt rank of second order for further investigations.

For an
n
-partite pure state
|\psi\rangle\in\otimes^{n}_{i=1}\mathcal{H}_{A_{i}}
, under given bi-partition
X_{1}|X_{2}\in\mathcal{G}_{2}(A)
there exists an unique
r(X_{1}|X_{2})={\rm Rank}[Tr_{X_{1}}|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|]
such that

|\psi\rangle=\sum^{r(X_{1}|X_{2})}_{i_{1}=1}a_{i_{1}}|\psi_{i_{1}}(X_{1})\rangle|\phi_{i_{1}}(X_{2})\rangle

for some orthonormal states
\{|\psi_{i_{1}}(X_{1})\rangle\}
and
\{|\phi_{i_{1}}(X_{2})\rangle\}
in subspaces
X_{1}
and
X_{2}
, respectively. If
X_{1}
contains two subspaces
X_{11}
and
X_{12}
, one can sequentially partition
X_{1}
as
X_{11}|X_{12}
. Then there exists a unique Schmidt rank for every
|\psi_{i_{1}}(X_{1})\rangle
such that

|\psi_{i_{1}}(X_{1})\rangle=\sum^{r(X_{11}|X_{12};X_{1}|X_{2},i_{1})}_{i_{2}=1}a_{i_{2}i_{1}}|\psi_{i_{2}i_{1}}(X_{11})\rangle|\phi_{i_{2}i_{1}}(X_{12})\rangle,

where

r(X_{11}|X_{12};X_{1}|X_{2},i_{1})={\rm Rank}[Tr_{X_{11}}|\psi_{i_{1}}(X_{1})\rangle\langle\psi_{i_{1}}(X_{1})|]

is the Schmidt rank of
|\psi_{i_{1}}(X_{1})\rangle
. For the sake of convenience we define

r(X_{11}|X_{12};X_{1}|X_{2},i_{1})=\left.\Big\{\begin{aligned} &{\rm Rank}[Tr_{X_{11}}|\psi_{i_{1}}(X_{1})\rangle\langle\psi_{i_{1}}(X_{1})|],~~~{\rm if}~X_{11}|X_{12}~{\rm is}~{\rm an}~{\rm available}~{\rm bi-partition}~{\rm of}~\mathcal{G}_{2}(X_{1});\\
&1,\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad~{\rm if}~X_{1}~{\rm is}~{\rm single}~{\rm subspace}.\end{aligned}\right.

We then define the minimal Schmidt rank of the second order of
|\psi\rangle
by

r^{2}_{{\rm min}}=\min_{X_{1}|X_{2}\in\mathcal{G}_{2}(A)}\sum^{r(X_{1}|X_{2})}_{i_{1}=1}\min_{X_{11}|X_{12}\in\mathcal{G}_{2}(X_{1})}r(X_{11}|X_{12};X_{1}|X_{2},i_{1}).

(2)

Based on the Schmidt rank of the second order we have the following results about triple separable states.

Theorem 3
.

Any nontrivial superposition of an
n
-partite pure state
|\psi\rangle
with
r^{2}_{{\rm min}}\geq 3
and any
r^{2}_{{\rm min}}-2
product pure states cannot be a triple separable state.

Proof.

According to the definition of
r^{2}_{{\rm min}}
, it is obvious that
r^{2}_{{\rm min}}\geq r^{1}_{{\rm min}}
. The state
|\psi\rangle
is genuinely multipartite entangled when
r^{1}_{{\rm min}}\geq 2
. The Schmidt rank of second order takes over the minimal values of Schmidt rank of all kinds of bi-partitions. After superposing
r^{2}_{{\rm min}}-2
product pure states with
|\psi\rangle
, the resulted state can not be triple separable, as there exists at least one
|\psi_{i_{1}}(X_{1})\rangle
which is genuinely multipartite entangled in subsystem
X_{1}
. Then the total state can not be triple separable.

Example 1.
Let us consider the generalized
n
-partite GHZ state,
|\psi\rangle=\frac{1}{\sqrt{d}}\sum^{d-1}_{i=0}|ii\cdots i\rangle\in\otimes^{n}_{i=1}\mathcal{H}_{A_{i}}
. We have that
r(X_{1}|X_{2})=d
for all bi-partitions in
\mathcal{G}_{2}(A)
, and
r(X_{11}|X_{12};X_{1}|X_{2},i_{1})=1
for arbitrary
i_{1}
and bipartition in
\mathcal{G}_{2}(X_{1})
. Hence,
r^{2}_{{\rm min}}=r^{1}_{{\rm min}}=d
. Therefore, it is impossible to obtain a bi-separable state by superposing
|\psi\rangle
with
d-2
product pure states. Of course it is impossible to obtain a triple separable state either.

Note that in Theorems 1 and 2, the entangled state
|\psi\rangle
and the arbitrary product states
|p\rangle
are not necessary to be orthogonal each other. One may also consider the orthogonal superposition of
|\psi\rangle
and product pure states in the complementary space of
|\psi\rangle
. Let us consider the following examples in Hilbert space
\mathbb{C}^{2}\otimes\mathbb{C}^{2}\otimes\mathbb{C}^{2}
.

Example 2.
We consider the genuinely multipartite entangled state
|\psi_{1}\rangle=a_{0}|000\rangle+a_{1}|111\rangle
with
a^{2}_{0}+a^{2}_{1}=1
. It is directly verified that
|\psi_{1}\rangle
is unconditionally robust in GME under superposition, with respect to any product pure states
|p\rangle=|\alpha\rangle|\beta\rangle|\gamma\rangle
which is orthogonal to
|\psi_{1}\rangle
, since any nontrivial superposed state
\cos\alpha|\psi\rangle+\sin\alpha|p\rangle

(\cos\alpha\sin\alpha\neq 0)
has all reduced density matrices of rank
2
.

Example 3.
Consider
|\psi_{2}\rangle=\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}(|000\rangle+|100\rangle+|111\rangle)
, which is a GMEd with
{\rm Rank}[\rho_{i}]=2
for all the reduced single qubit states
(i=1,2,3)
.
|\psi_{2}\rangle
is not unconditionally robust in GME under superposition. This can be seen from that the superposed state
\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}|\psi_{2}\rangle+\frac{1}{2}|001\rangle=|+\rangle\otimes|\psi_{+}\rangle
is bi-separable, where
|+\rangle=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(|0\rangle+|1\rangle)
and
|\psi_{+}\rangle=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(|00\rangle+|11\rangle)
.

Example 4.
Consider state
|\psi_{3}\rangle=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{4}|001\rangle+\frac{\sqrt{5}}{4}|01\rangle|+\rangle+\frac{3}{4}|1\rangle|b\rangle|+\rangle
, where
|b\rangle=\frac{2}{3}|0\rangle+\frac{\sqrt{5}}{3}|1\rangle
and
|+\rangle
is defined in Example 3.
|\psi_{3}\rangle
is genuinely multipartite entangled with
{\rm Rank}[\rho_{i}]=2

(i=1,2,3)
. It can be verified that the superposition of
|\psi_{3}\rangle
and the orthogonal product state
|p\rangle=|000\rangle
gives a separable state,
\frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}|\psi_{3}\rangle+\frac{1}{3}|p\rangle=|+\rangle|b\rangle|+\rangle
.

The examples above show that superpositions of a genuinely multipartite entangled pure state and an orthogonal product pure state result in different types of entanglement or full separability, depending both on the superposition coefficients and the choices of the product pure states.
If more product states are superposed to a genuinely multipartite entangled state, a separable state can be obtained eventually. We investigate the minimal number of product states needed to be superposed so as to obtain a separable state in next section.

III
3. Minimal number of superposed product states for separability

It is impossible to vanish the entanglement of a bipartite state
|\psi\rangle
with Schmidt rank
r
by superposing
r-2
product states
[
13
]
. Obviously, superposing
r-1
product states is enough to make
|\psi\rangle
separable in general. However, if the superposed product states are required to be orthogonal, the things are different.

Lemma 2
.

For a bipartite pure state
|\psi\rangle
with Schmidt rank
r\geq 3
, there exits a set of
r
mutually orthogonal product states
\mathbb{S}=\{|p_{i}\rangle\}^{i=r}_{i=1}
such that the superposition of
|\psi\rangle
and the product states gives a separable state.

Proof.

|\psi\rangle
has a Schmidt form,

|\psi\rangle=\sum^{r}_{i=1}a_{i}|i\rangle|i\rangle
with
\sum^{r}_{i=1}a^{2}_{i}=1
. Set

|p_{i}\rangle=|i\rangle\otimes\frac{1}{\sqrt{r-1}}\sum_{j\neq i}|j\rangle.

Then
\mathbb{S}
is a set of mutually orthogonal product states,
\langle p_{i}|p_{j}\rangle=\delta_{ij}
. Moreover,
\langle\psi|p_{i}\rangle=0,~\forall i=1,2,\ldots,r
. Taking
\lambda=\frac{1}{\sqrt{r}}
and
\mu_{i}=\sqrt{\frac{r-1}{r}}a_{i}

(i=1,2,\ldots,r)
, we have the following separable superposed
state,

\lambda|\psi\rangle+\sum^{r}_{i=1}\mu_{i}|p_{i}\rangle=\left(\sum^{r}_{i=1}a_{i}|i\rangle\right)\otimes\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{r}}\sum^{r}_{i=1}|i\rangle\right).

Then it is an interesting issue to assure whether it is possible to obtain a separable state by superposing
r-1
pairwise orthogonal product states with a given entangled state of Schmidt rank
r
. The answer depends on the detailed entangled state.

Theorem 4
.

For a bipartite state with Schmidt form
|\psi\rangle=\sum^{r}_{i=1}a_{i}|ii\rangle
, it is possible to obtain a separable state by superposing
|\psi\rangle
with
r-1
pairwise orthogonal product pure states only when not all
|a_{i}|\equiv 1/\sqrt{r}
for
i=1,2,\cdots,r
.

Proof.

We first prove the case of
r=2
, i.e.,
|\psi\rangle=a_{0}|00\rangle+a_{1}|11\rangle
. Note that the orthogonality between
|\psi\rangle
and any separable state
|p_{1}\rangle=|\alpha_{1}\rangle|\beta_{1}\rangle=(\cos\alpha_{1}|0\rangle+e^{i\theta}\sin\alpha_{1}|1\rangle)\otimes(\cos\beta_{1}|0\rangle+e^{i\delta}\sin\beta_{1}|1\rangle)
requires that

a_{0}\cos\alpha_{1}\cos\beta_{1}+a_{1}\sin\alpha_{1}\sin\beta_{1}=0.

(3)

For some
p\in(0,1)
the superposed state
\sqrt{p}|\psi_{1}\rangle+\sqrt{1-p}|p_{1}\rangle
is separable only if

pa_{0}a_{1}+\sqrt{p(1-p)}\Big[a_{0}\sin\alpha_{1}\sin\beta_{1}+a_{1}\cos\alpha_{1}\cos\beta_{1}\Big]=0.

(4)

(
3
) and (
4
) hold simultaneously only if
|a_{0}|\neq|a_{1}|
. Taking into account the normalization
a^{2}_{0}+a^{2}_{1}=1
, we have that the solutions of (
3
) and (
4
) exists for
p\in(0,1)
only if
|a_{0}|\neq|a_{1}|\neq 1/\sqrt{2}
.

Next we prove the general case by induction. Without loss of generality, we assume that
|a_{0}|\neq|a_{1}|
. There exist
p\in(0,1)
and
\alpha_{1},\beta_{1}
such that
\sqrt{p}\frac{1}{\sqrt{a^{2}_{0}+a^{2}_{1}}}(a_{0}|00\rangle+a_{1}|11\rangle)+\sqrt{1-p}(\cos\alpha_{1}|0\rangle+\sin\alpha_{1}|1\rangle)\otimes(\cos\beta_{1}|0\rangle+\sin\beta_{1}|1\rangle)=(x_{0}|0\rangle+x_{1}|1\rangle)\otimes(y_{0}|0\rangle+y_{1}|1\rangle)=|\phi_{1}\rangle
. Therefore, the entanglement in the term
|00\rangle+|11\rangle
is vanished. In this way, we can then vanish the entanglement given by
|\phi_{1}\rangle
and the next term
a_{2}|22\rangle
in
|\psi\rangle
. By iterating
r-1
times we obtain a separable state at last.

Example 5.

Consider
|\psi\rangle=\frac{3}{\sqrt{149}}|00\rangle+\frac{6}{\sqrt{149}}|11\rangle+\frac{2\sqrt{26}}{\sqrt{149}}|22\rangle
with Schmidt rank 3. Set
|p_{1}\rangle=\frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}(-|0\rangle+2|1\rangle)\otimes\frac{1}{\sqrt{17}}(4|0\rangle+|1\rangle)
. The entanglement given by the terms
|00\rangle
and
|11\rangle
is vanished by superposing
|p_{1}\rangle
,

\displaystyle\frac{3}{\sqrt{26}}

\displaystyle(\frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}|00\rangle+\frac{2}{\sqrt{5}}|11\rangle)+\sqrt{\frac{17}{26}}|p_{1}\rangle

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\sqrt{65}}(-|0\rangle+8|1\rangle)\otimes\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(|0\rangle+|1\rangle)=|\alpha_{1}\rangle\otimes|+\rangle,

where
|\alpha_{1}\rangle=\frac{1}{\sqrt{65}}(-|0\rangle+8|1\rangle)
and
|+\rangle=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(|0\rangle+|1\rangle)
, which are both orthogonal both to
|2\rangle
. We then take
|p_{2}\rangle=\frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}(|\alpha_{1}\rangle+2|2\rangle)\otimes\frac{1}{\sqrt{21}}(4|+\rangle-\sqrt{5}|2\rangle)
. The superposition between
|\alpha_{1}\rangle\otimes|+\rangle
and
|p_{2}\rangle
gives again a separable state,

\displaystyle\sqrt{\frac{3}{178}}\Big[\frac{\sqrt{5}}{3}|\alpha_{1}\rangle|+\rangle+\frac{2}{3}|22\rangle\Big]+\sqrt{\frac{175}{178}}|p_{2}\rangle

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\sqrt{89}}(5|\alpha_{1}\rangle+8|2\rangle)\otimes\frac{1}{\sqrt{6}}(\sqrt{5}|+\rangle-|2\rangle).

Finally we have

\displaystyle\lambda|\psi\rangle+\mu_{1}|p_{1}\rangle+\mu_{2}|p_{2}\rangle

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\sqrt{89}}(5|\alpha_{1}\rangle+8|2\rangle)\otimes\frac{1}{\sqrt{6}}(\sqrt{5}|+\rangle-|2\rangle),

where
\lambda=\sqrt{\frac{149}{178\times 78}}
,
\mu_{1}=\sqrt{\frac{85}{178\times 78}}
and

.

For tripartite systems, we consider the generalized GHZ states.

Theorem 5
.

The entanglement of the genuinely multipartite entangled state
|\psi\rangle=\sum^{r}_{i=1}a_{i}|iii\rangle

(r\geq 3)
can be vanished by superposing
2r-1
product pure states which are orthogonal to
|\psi\rangle
(but not orthogonal mutually) when not all
|a_{i}|
equal to
1/\sqrt{r}
.

Proof.

Making use of the method in proving Lemma 2, we take

|p_{i}\rangle=|ii\rangle\otimes\frac{1}{\sqrt{r-1}}\sum_{j\neq i}|j\rangle
which are orthogonal to
|\psi\rangle
. Set
\lambda^{\prime}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{r}}
and
\mu^{\prime}_{i}=\sqrt{\frac{r-1}{r}}a_{i}
. Then the superposition of the state
|\psi\rangle
and
|p_{i}\rangle
leads to a bi-separable state,

\lambda^{\prime}|\psi\rangle+\sum^{r}_{i=1}\mu^{\prime}_{i}|p_{i}\rangle=\left(\sum^{r}_{i=1}a_{i}|ii\rangle\right)\otimes\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{r}}\sum^{r}_{i=1}|i\rangle\right).

4. Conclusions

The superposition of pure states is the hypostatic cause of quantum entanglement. A suitable superposition of an entangled state with certain product states may give rise to a separable state. The multipartite entanglement is more complicated than bipartite cases. We have studied the robustness of genuine multipartite entanglement and inseparability of multipartite pure states under superposition with product pure states, by introducing the concepts of maximal and minimal Schmidt ranks for multipartite states. Based on the minimal and maximal Schmidt ranks of the first order, as well as the minimal Schmidt ranks of the second order, we have presented the number of (either arbitrary or orthogonal) product states superposed to a given (genuine) multipartite entangled state, so that the whole superposed state keeps (genuine) multipartite entangled.
