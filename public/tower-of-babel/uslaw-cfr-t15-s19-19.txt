15 C.F.R. § 19.19 — Will Commerce entities issue a refund if money is erroneously collected on a Commerce debt?
Citation: us:cfr:15:19.19
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-19.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 19 (Commerce Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Commerce entities shall promptly refund to a debtor any amount collected on a Commerce debt when the Commerce debt is waived or otherwise found not to be owed to the United States, or as otherwise required by law. Refunds under this part shall not bear interest unless required by law.
