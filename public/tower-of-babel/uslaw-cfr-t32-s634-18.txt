32 C.F.R. § 634.18 — Reinstatement of driving privileges.
Citation: us:cfr:32:634.18
Creator: Department Of The Army
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-634.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 634 (Motor Vehicle Traffic Supervision). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Reinstatement of driving privileges shall be automatic, provided all revocations applicable have expired, proper proof of completion of remedial driving course and/or substance abuse counseling has been provided, and reinstatement requirements of individual's home state and/or state the individual may have been suspended in, have been met.
