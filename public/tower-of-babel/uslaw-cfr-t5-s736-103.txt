5 C.F.R. § 736.103 — Protecting the identity of a source.
Citation: us:cfr:5:736.103
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-736.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 736 (Personnel Investigations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a source is granted a promise that the source's identity will be kept confidential, the investigative agency and all other agencies that receive information obtained under the promise are required to take all reasonable precautions to protect the source's identity. Each agency will prepare for its investigators and agents implementing instructions consistent with this part.
