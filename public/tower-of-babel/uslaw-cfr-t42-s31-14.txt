42 C.F.R. § 31.14 — Application for treatment; active duty personnel.
Citation: us:cfr:42:31.14
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-31.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 31 (Medical Care For Certain Personnel Of The Coast Guard, National Ocean Survey, Public Health Service, And Former Lighthouse Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An applicant for medical relief who is on active duty shall furnish a certificate identifying him. Such certificate shall be signed by an officer or other appropriate supervisory official of the Coast Guard. In an emergency, the officer in charge of a medical relief station, or a designated physician or designated dentist, may accept other evidence of status satisfactory to him.
