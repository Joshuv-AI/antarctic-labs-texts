25 C.F.R. § 171.420 — Can I dispose of sewage, trash, or other refuse on a BIA irrigation project?
Citation: us:cfr:25:171.420
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-171.420
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 171 (Irrigation Operation And Maintenance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. Sewage, trash, or other refuse are considered obstructions and must be removed in accordance with § 171.415.
