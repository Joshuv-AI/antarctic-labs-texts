20 C.F.R. § 652.211 — What are State planning requirements under the Wagner-Peyser Act?
Citation: us:cfr:20:652.211
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-652.211
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 652 (Establishment And Functioning Of State Employment Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The ES is a core program identified in WIOA and must be included as part of each State's Unified or Combined State Plans. See §§ 676.105 through 676.125 of this chapter for planning requirements for the core programs.
