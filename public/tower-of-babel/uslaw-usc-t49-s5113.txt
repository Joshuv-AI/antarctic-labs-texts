49 U.S.C. § 5113 — Unsatisfactory safety rating

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Chapter 51 (Transportation Of Hazardous Material). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A violation of section 31144(c)(3) shall be considered a violation of this chapter, and shall be subject to the penalties in sections 5123 and 5124.
