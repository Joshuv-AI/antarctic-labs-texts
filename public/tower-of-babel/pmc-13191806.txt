Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
Cyclodehydrogenation Contrary to long‐held expectations, tetraphenyl‐substituted tetraazapyrene undergoes selective pyrrole cyclisation followed by C–C coupling on Au(111), forming a fused tetraazadiindenopyrene and phenanthrene cores with embedded pyridinic and graphitic nitrogen. Combined experimental and theoretical evidence supports the C–N coupling over the C–C coupling during cyclodehydrogenation.
