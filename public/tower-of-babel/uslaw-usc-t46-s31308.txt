46 U.S.C. § 31308 — Secretary of Commerce or Transportation as mortgagee
Citation: us:usc:46:31308
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section31308
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 313 (Commercial Instruments And Maritime Liens); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Commerce or Transportation, as a mortgagee under this chapter, may foreclose on a lien arising from a right established under a mortgage under chapter 537 of this title, subject to section 362(b) of title 11.
