Strain tuning of the nonlinear anomalous Hall effect in MoS 2 \textrm{MoS}_{2} monolayer
Yuebei Xiong, Zhirui Gong, Hao Jin

Abstract
Due to the time reversal symmetry, the linear anomalous Hall effect (AHE) usually vanishes in MoS 2 \textrm{MoS}_{2} monolayer. In contrast, the nonlinear AHE plays an essential role in such system when the uniaxial strain breaks the C 3 ​ v C_{3v} symmetry and eventually results in the nonzero Berry curvature dipole (BCD). We find that not only the magnitude of the AHE but also the nonlinear Hall angle can be tuned by the strain. Especially the nonlinear Hall angle exhibits a deep relationship which is analogy to the birefraction phenomenon in optics. It actually results from the pseudotensor nature of the BCD moment. Besides the ordinary positive and negative crystals in optics, there are two more birefraction-like cases corresponding to an imaginary refraction index ratio in monolayer MoS 2 \textrm{MoS}_{2} . Our findings shed lights on the strain controlled electronic devices based on the two-dimensional (2D) materials with BCD.

the uniaxial strain, the nonlinear AHE can be achieved in such system.
We find that not only the magnitude of the nonlinear AHE

Method

The band structure of
\textrm{MoS}_{2}
was calculated using first-principles simulations within density functional theory (DFT). The projected augmented wave pseudopotentials method was used as implemented in the Vienna Ab
initio
Simulation Package (VASP). The exchange correlation energy was calculated using the generalized gradient approximation (GGA) of the Perdew-Burke-Ernzerhof form, and the plane wave cutoff energy was set to 500 eV. For the calculation of band structure, a centered 21 x 21 x 1 kpoint mesh was used. The vacuum space along the z direction was set to be
>
20 Å. The plane lattice constant and atomic coordinates were fully relaxed until the energy and force converged to
10^{-8}
and
10^{-2}
eV/Å, respectively.
