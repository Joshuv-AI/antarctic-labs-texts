42 U.S.C. § 4026 — Expiration of program
Citation: us:usc:42:4026
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section4026
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 50 (National Flood Insurance); Subchapter I (The National Flood Insurance Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No new contract for flood insurance under this chapter shall be entered into after September 30, 2026.
