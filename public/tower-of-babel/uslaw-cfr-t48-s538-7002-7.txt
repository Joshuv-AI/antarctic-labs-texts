48 C.F.R. § 538.7002-7 — 538.7002-7 Native American Housing Assistance and Self Determination Act (NAHASDA).
Citation: us:cfr:48:538.7002-7
Creator: Chapter 5—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-538.7002-7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 538 (Federal Supply Schedule Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

25 U.S.C 4111(j) allows Indian Tribes, as defined in 25 U.S.C. 4103, and tribally designated housing entities, as defined in 25 U.S.C. 4103, that have an active NAHASDA contract, grant, or cooperative agreement to purchase supplies and services for the purposes of carrying out the NAHASDA contract, grant, or cooperative agreement.
