40 C.F.R. § 60.5725b — b In lieu of a State plan submittal, are there other acceptable option(s) for a State to meet its CAA section 111(d) obligations?
Citation: us:cfr:40:60.5725b
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.5725b
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 60 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A State may meet its CAA section 111(d) obligations only by submitting a State plan or a negative declaration letter (if applicable).
