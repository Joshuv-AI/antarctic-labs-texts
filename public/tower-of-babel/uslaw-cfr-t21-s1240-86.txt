21 C.F.R. § 1240.86 — Protection of pier water system.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1240.86
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1240.86
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1240 (Control Of Communicable Diseases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No vessel engaged in interstate traffic shall make a connection between its nonpotable water system and any pier potable water system unless provisions are made to prevent backflow from the vessel to the pier.
