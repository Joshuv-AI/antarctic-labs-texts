14 C.F.R. § 13.216 — Waivers.
Citation: us:cfr:14:13.216
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-13.216
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 13 (Investigative And Enforcement Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Waivers of any rights provided by statute or regulation must be in writing or by stipulation made at a hearing and entered into the record. The parties must set forth the precise terms of the waiver and any conditions.
