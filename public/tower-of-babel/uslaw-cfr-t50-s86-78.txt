50 C.F.R. § 86.78 — How must I treat income earned after the period of performance?
Citation: us:cfr:50:86.78
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-86.78
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 86 (Boating Infrastructure Grant Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You are not accountable to us for income earned by you or a subgrantee after the period of performance as a result of the grant except as required at §§ 86.90 and 86.91.
