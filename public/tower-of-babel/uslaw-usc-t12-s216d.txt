12 U.S.C. § 216d — Severability
Citation: us:usc:12:216d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section216d
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 2 (National Banks); Subchapter XVII (Disposition Of Unclaimed Property Recovered From Closed National Banks). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this subchapter or the application of such provision to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of this subchapter and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
