49 U.S.C. § 47526 — Limitations for noncomplying airport noise and access restrictions
Citation: us:usc:49:47526
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section47526
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part B (Airport Development And Noise); Chapter 475 (Noise); Subchapter II (National Aviation Noise Policy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Unless the Secretary of Transportation is satisfied that an airport is not imposing an airport noise or access restriction not in compliance with this subchapter, the airport may not—
(1)
receive money under subchapter I of chapter 471 of this title; or
(2)
impose a passenger facility charge under section 40117 of this title.
