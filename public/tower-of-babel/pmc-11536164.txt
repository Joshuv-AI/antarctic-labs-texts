Correction for Galli et al., Toll-like receptor 3 (TLR3) activation induces microRNA-dependent reexpression of functional RARβ and tumor regression
Published: 2024

Cell Biology Correction for “Toll-like receptor 3 (TLR3) activation induces microRNA-dependent reexpression of functional RARβ and tumor regression,” by Roberta Galli, Alessio Paone, Muller Fabbri, Nicola Zanesi, Federica Calore, Luciano Cascione, Mario Acunzo, Antonella Stoppacciaro, Andrea Tubaro, Francesca Lovat, Pierluigi Gasparini, Paolo Fadda, Hansjuerg Alder, Stefano Volinia, Antonio Filippini, Elio Ziparo, Anna Riccioli, and Carlo M. Croce, which was first published May 28, 2013; 10.1073/pnas.1304610110 (Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. U.S.A. 110, 9812–9817).

The authors note that “the panel for PARP Precursor in Fig. 6D, which was labeled as representing the MDA-MB231 cell line, was inadvertently cropped from the image related to the DU145 cell line during figure preparation. The image has now been replaced with the correct, original data from the MDA-MB231 cell line. The scientific results remain unchanged. We apologize to readers for the original mistake.”

Apoptotic effect on DU145, TRAMP, and MDA-MB-231 cells after stimulation of RARβ induced by Poly(I:C). DU145, TRAMP, or MDA-MB-231 cells were pretreated with 25 μg/mL Poly(I:C) for 48 h, then with 10 μM 9-cis-RA for 72 h. Cells untreated or stimulated with 9-cis-RA alone were used as a control. The cells then were detached and subjected to propidium iodide PI staining. M1 is the percentage of apoptotic cells (A–C). (D) Western blotting of PARP precursor and PARP cleaved forms of DU145, TRAMP, and MDA-MB-231 cells treated with 25 μg/mL Poly(I:C) for 48 h or with 10 μM 9-cis-RA for 72 h alone or pretreated with 25 μg/mL Poly(I:C) for 48 h and subsequently with 10 μM 9-cis-RA for 72 h. Untreated cells were used as a control. β-Actin was used as loading control. Data represent typical experiments that were repeated three times with similar results. *P < 0.05; **P < 0.01. a.u., arbitrary units.
