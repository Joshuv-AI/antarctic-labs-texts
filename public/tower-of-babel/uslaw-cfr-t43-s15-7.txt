43 C.F.R. § 15.7 — Fishing.
Citation: us:cfr:43:15.7
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-15.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 15 (Key Largo Coral Reef Preserve). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Spear fishing within the boundaries or confines of this Preserve is prohibited.
(b) The use of poisons, electric charges, or other such methods is prohibited.
