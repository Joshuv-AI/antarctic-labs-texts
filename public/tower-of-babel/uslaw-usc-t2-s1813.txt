2 U.S.C. § 1813 — Exterior of Capitol
Citation: us:usc:2:1813
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1813
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 28 (Architect Of The Capitol); Subchapter II (General Powers And Duties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On and after July 7, 1884, it shall be the duty of the Architect to clean and keep in proper order the exterior of the Capitol.
