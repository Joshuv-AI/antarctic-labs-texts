F-Term Hybrid Inflation and SUSY Breaking


Abstract
We consider F-term hybrid inflation and supersymmetry breaking in the context of a model which largely respects a global U ⁡ ( 1 ) U(1) R R symmetry. The Kähler potential parameterizes the Kähler manifold with an enhanced U ​ ( 1 ) R × ( S ​ U ​ ( 1 , 1 ) / U ⁡ ( 1 ) ) U(1)_{R}\times(SU(1,1)/U(1)) symmetry, where the scalar curvature of the second factor is determined by the achievement of a supersymmetry-breaking de Sitter vacuum without ugly tuning. The magnitude of the emergent soft tadpole term for the inflaton can be adjusted in the range ( 1.2 − 460 ) ​ TeV (1.2-460)~{\mbox{\rm TeV}} – increasing with the dimensionality of the representation of the waterfall fields – so that the inflationary observables are in agreement with the observational requirements. The mass scale of the supersymmetric partners turns out to lie in the region ( 0.09 − 253 ) ​ PeV (0.09-253)~{\mbox{\rm PeV}} which is compatible with high-scale supersymmetry and the results of LHC on the Higgs boson mass. The μ \mu parameter can be generated by conveniently applying the Giudice-Masiero mechanism and assures the out-of-equilibrium decay of the R R saxion at a low reheat temperature T rh ≤ 163 ​ GeV T_{\rm rh}\leq 163~{\mbox{\rm GeV}} .

Motivation

The
Supersymmetric
(
SUSY
) hybrid inflation

[
1

based on F terms, called for short henceforth

F-term hybrid inflation
(
FHI

well-motivated, flexible and easily embedded in Particle models
inflationary model for a number of reasons. Most notably:

•

It is based on a renormalizable superpotential uniquely
determined by a gauge
\mathbb{G}
and a global
U(1)~R
symmetries;

•

It does not require fine tuned parameters and
transplanckian inflaton values;

•

It can be naturally followed by a
Grand Unified
Theory
(
GUT
) phase transition which may lead to the
production of cosmological defects, if these are predicted by the
symmetry-breaking scheme.

However, the original version of FHI
[
1
]
, which employs
only
radiative corrections
(
RCs
) into the
inflationary potential is considered as strongly disfavored by the

Planck

[
2

This conclusion can be evaded, if we take in to account soft
SUSY-breaking terms
[
3
,
4
,
5
]
and

Supergravity
(
SUGRA
) corrections
[
6

(

)

sector
(

A possible combination of the aforementioned sectors is presented
in Sec.
2
following Ref.
[
7
]
– see also Ref.
[
8
]
.
Then, in Sec.
3

4

Dark Energy
(

) problem,
the immunity of

(

)
from the notorious moduli problem and the

(

2.3
Kähler Potential

The Kähler potential respects the adopted
\mathbb{G}
symmetry in Table
1
. It
has the following contributions

K=K_{\rm I}+K_{\rm H}+K_{\mu}+|Y_{\alpha}|^{2},

(5)

which can be specified as follows:

(a)

K_{\rm I}
depends on the fields
involved in FHI – cf. Eq. (
3a
). We adopt the simplest possible
choice
[
4
,
6
]
that has the form

K_{\rm I}=|S|^{2}+|\Phi|^{2}+|\bar{\Phi}|^{2}.\\

(6a)

Higher order terms of the form
|S|^{2\nu_{S}}/m_{\rm P}^{2\nu_{S}-2}
with

\nu_{S}>1
can not be excluded by the imposed symmetries but may
become harmless if
S\ll m_{\rm P}
and assume low enough coefficients.

(b)

K_{\rm H}
is devoted to HS. We adopt
the form introduced in Ref.
[
9
]
where

K_{\rm H}=Nm_{\rm P}^{2}\ln\left(1+\frac{|Z|^{2}-k^{2}Z_{-}^{4}/m_{\rm P}^{2}}{Nm_{\rm P}^{2}}\right)~~\mbox{with}~~Z_{\pm}=Z\pm Z^{*}.

(6b)

Here,
k>0
mildly violates
R
symmetry endowing
R
axion with
phenomenologically acceptable mass. The selected
K_{\rm H}
is
largely respects the
R
symmetry, which is a crucial ingredient
for FHI, and it ensures – as we see in Sec.
4.1
– a dS vacuum
of the whole field system with tunable cosmological constant for

N=\frac{4\nu^{2}}{3-4\nu}~~\mbox{with}~~\frac{3}{4}<\nu<\frac{3}{2}~~\mbox{for}~~N<0.

(7)

Our favored
\nu
range finally is
3/4<\nu<1
. Since
N<0
,

K_{\rm H}
parameterizes the
SU(1,1)/U(1)
hyperbolic Kähler manifold for

k\sim 0
.

(c)

K_{\mu}
includes higher order terms which
generate the needed mixing term between
{H_{u}}
and
{H_{d}}
in the
lagrangian of MSSM
[
9
]
and has the form

K_{\mu}=\lambda_{\mu}\left({{Z^{*}}^{2\nu}}/{m_{\rm P}^{2\nu}}\right)H_{\rm B}+\ {\rm h.c.},

(8)

where the dimensionless constant
\lambda_{\mu}
is taken real for
simplicity.

The total
K
in Eq. (
5
) enjoys an enhanced
symmetry for the
\bar{Y}_{A},S
and
Z
fields with
\bar{Y}_{A}=Y_{\alpha},\bar{\Phi},\Phi,S
. Namely,

\prod_{A}U(1)_{\bar{Y}_{A}}\times\left(SU(1,1)/U(1)\right)_{Z},

(9)

where the indices indicate the moduli which parameterize the
corresponding manifolds. Thanks to this symmetry, mixing terms of
the form
S^{\tilde{\nu}_{s}}Z^{*\tilde{\nu}_{z}}
can be ignored
although they may be allowed by the
R
symmetry for

\tilde{\nu}_{z}=\nu\tilde{\nu}_{s}
. Most notably,
U(1)_{S}
protects

K_{\rm I}
from
S
depended terms which violates the
R

symmetry, thereby, spoiling the inflationary set-up.

\sigma

4.2
Reheating Stage

After the termination of FHI, the IS and

is comparable

\rho_{z\rm It}

\rho_{z\rm I}\sim m_{z}^{2}\langle{z}\rangle^{2}~~\mbox{with}~~\langle{z}\rangle\sim m_{\rm P}~~\mbox{and}~~\rho_{z\rm It}=3m_{\rm P}^{2}H_{z\rm I}^{2}\simeq 3m_{\rm P}^{2}m_{z}^{2}.

Due to the weakness of the gravitational interactions which govern
the
z
decay, the reheating temperature

T_{\rm rh}=\left({72/5\pi^{2}g_{\rm rh*}}\right)^{1/4}{\Gamma}_{{\delta}z}^{1/2}m_{\rm P}^{1/2},~~\mbox{with}~~g_{\rm rh*}\simeq 10.75-100

(30)

the effective number of the relativistic degrees of freedom, is
rather low – in accordance with our expectations related to the
cosmic moduli problem
[
12
]
. Indeed, the total decay width
of the (canonically normalized) sgoldstino

{\Gamma}_{{\delta}z}\simeq{\Gamma}_{{\theta}}+{\Gamma}_{\tilde{h}},

(31)

where the individual decay widths are found to be

{\Gamma}_{{\theta}}\simeq\frac{\lambda_{\theta}^{2}m_{z}^{3}}{32\pi m_{\rm P}^{2}}\sqrt{1-\frac{4m_{\theta}^{2}}{m_{3/2}^{2}}}~~\mbox{and}~~{\Gamma}_{\tilde{h}}=\frac{2^{4\nu-1}}{3^{2\nu-1}}\lambda_{\mu}^{2}\frac{\omega^{2}}{4\pi}\frac{m_{z}^{3}}{m_{\rm P}^{2}}\nu^{4\nu},

(32a)

with
\lambda_{\theta}={\langle{z}\rangle}/{N}m_{\rm P}=({4\nu-3})/{\sqrt{6}\nu}
. From
the expressions above we readily recognize that
{\Gamma}_{{\delta}z}
is roughly
proportional to
m_{z}^{3}/m_{\rm P}^{2}
as expected for any typical modulus

[
12
]
. Selecting

\widehat{\delta z}
into
\widetilde{G}

\widetilde{G}

We analyzed the implementation of FHI together with the SUSY
breaking within various GUTs in Eqs. (

the super- and Kähler potentials in Eqs. (

worth-mentioning achievements:

•

Observationally acceptable FHI adjusting the tadpole
parameter,
{\rm a}_{S}

-breaking scale

•

A prediction of the SUSY-mass scale,

•

Generation of the

•

Compatibility of

•

An explanation of NG15 via the decay of metastable
B-L
CSs
if

.

A complete cosmological picture of our framework could be achieved
if the issues of the correct baryon asymmetry and the
cold-dark-matter abundance of the universe are also addressed.
