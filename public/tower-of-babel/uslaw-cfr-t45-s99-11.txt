45 C.F.R. § 99.11 — Notice of hearing or opportunity for hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:45:99.11
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-99.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 99 (Procedure For Hearings For The Child Care And Development Fund). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Proceedings commence when the Assistant Secretary mails a notice of hearing or opportunity for hearing to the Lead Agency. The notice shall state the time and place for the hearing, and the issues which will be considered. A copy of the notice shall be published in the Federal Register.
