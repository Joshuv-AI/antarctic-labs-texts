17 C.F.R. § 249.25 — Form 25, for notification of removal from listing and/or registration.
Citation: us:cfr:17:249.25
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-249.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 249 (Forms, Securities Exchange Act Of 1934). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This form shall be used by registered national securities exchanges and issuers for notification of removal of a class of securities from listing on a national securities exchange and/or withdrawal of registration under section 12(b) of the Act (15 U.S.C. 78l(b)).
