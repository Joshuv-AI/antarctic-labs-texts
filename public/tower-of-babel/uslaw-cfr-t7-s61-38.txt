7 C.F.R. § 61.38 — Suspended license to be returned to Division.
Citation: us:cfr:7:61.38
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-61.38
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 61 (Cottonseed Sold Or Offered For Sale For Crushing Purposes (Inspection, Sampling And Certification)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In case a license issued to a sampler is suspended or revoked such license shall be returned to the Division. At the expiration of any period of suspension of such license, unless in the meantime it be revoked, the dates of beginning and termination of such suspension shall be endorsed thereon, it shall be returned to the person to whom it was originally issued, and its shall be posted as prescribed in § 61.31.
