40 C.F.R. § 85.2303 — Duration of model year.
Citation: us:cfr:40:85.2303
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-85.2303
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 85 (Control Of Air Pollution From Mobile Sources). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A specific model year must always include January 1 of the calendar year for which it is designated and may not include a January 1 of any other calendar year. Thus, the maximum duration of a model year is one calendar year plus 364 days.
