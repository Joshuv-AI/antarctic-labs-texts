31 C.F.R. § 535.901 — Dollar accounts at banks abroad.
Citation: us:cfr:31:535.901
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-535.901
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 535 (Iranian Assets Control Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any domestic bank is hereby authorized to effect withdrawals or other transfers from any account held in the name of a non-Iranian bank located in a foreign country, provided such non-Iranian foreign bank is not a person subject to the jurisdiction of the United States.
