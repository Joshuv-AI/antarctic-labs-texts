7 C.F.R. § 1752.26 — No rights to special servicing actions.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1752.26
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1752.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1752 (Servicing Of Telecommunications Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this part should be assumed guaranteed as a right to the Borrower for any of the special servicing actions noted in this part.
