30 C.F.R. § 285.811 — Am I required to obtain a certification of my SMS?
Citation: us:cfr:30:285.811
Creator: Bureau Of Safety And Environmental Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-285.811
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 285 (Renewable Energy And Alternate Uses Of Existing Facilities On The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You are not required to obtain a certification that your SMS meets acceptable health and safety standards (e.g., ANSI/ASSP Z10.0, API RP 75, ISO 45001) from a recognized accreditation organization. However, BSEE will consider such certification in determining the frequency and scope of SMS-related inspections that it conducts under this subpart, as well as the scope and nature of its oversight over any audit-induced corrective actions.
