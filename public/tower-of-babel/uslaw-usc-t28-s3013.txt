28 U.S.C. § 3013 — Modification or protective order; supervision of enforcement
Citation: us:usc:28:3013
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section3013
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part VI (Particular Proceedings); Chapter 176 (Federal Debt Collection Procedure); Subchapter A (Definitions And General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The court may at any time on its own initiative or the motion of any interested person, and after such notice as it may require, make an order denying, limiting, conditioning, regulating, extending, or modifying the use of any enforcement procedure under this chapter.
