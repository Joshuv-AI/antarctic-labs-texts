40 C.F.R. § 1700.15 — Catapult water brake tank & post-launch retraction exhaust.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1700.15
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency And Department Of Defense; Uniform National Discharge Standards For Vessels Of The Armed Forces
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1700.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1700 (Uniform National Discharge Standards For Vessels Of The Armed Forces). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Discharges of catapult water brake tank effluent are prohibited.
(b) The number of post-launch retractions must be limited to the minimum number required to test and validate the system and conduct qualification and operational training.
