10 U.S.C. § 1043 — Service credit: service in the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration or the Public Health Service
Citation: us:usc:10:1043
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1043
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 53 (Miscellaneous Rights And Benefits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Active commissioned service in the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration or the Public Health Service shall be credited as active commissioned service in the armed forces for purposes of determining the retirement eligibility and computing the retired pay of a member of the armed forces.
