18 C.F.R. § 3b.5 — b.5 Legal guardians.
Citation: us:cfr:18:3b.5
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-3b.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 3b (PART 3b—COLLECTION, MAINTENANCE, USE, AND DISSEMINATION OF RECORDS OF IDENTIFIABLE PERSONAL INFORMATION). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the purposes of this part, the parent of any minor, or the legal guardian of any individual who has been declared to be incompetent due to physical or mental incapacity or age by a court of competent jurisdiction, may act on behalf of the individual.
