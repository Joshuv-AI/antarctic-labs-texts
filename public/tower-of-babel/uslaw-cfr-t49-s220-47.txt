49 C.F.R. § 220.47 — Emergency radio transmissions.
Citation: us:cfr:49:220.47
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-220.47
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 220 (Railroad Communications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An initial emergency radio transmission shall be preceded by the word “emergency,” repeated three times. An emergency transmission shall have priority over all other transmissions and the frequency or channel shall be kept clear of non-emergency traffic for the duration of the emergency communication.
