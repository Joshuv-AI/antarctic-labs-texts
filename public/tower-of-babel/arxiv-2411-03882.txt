Ricci-flat manifolds, parallel spinors and the Rosenberg index
Thomas Tony

Abstract
Any closed connected Riemannian spin manifold of non-zero A ^ \hat{A} -genus or non-zero Hitchin invariant with non-negative scalar curvature admits a parallel spinor, in particular is Ricci-flat. In this note, we generalize this result to closed connected spin manifolds of non-vanishing Rosenberg index. This provides a criterion for the existence of a parallel spinor on a finite covering and yields that any closed connected Ricci-flat spin manifold of dimension ≥ 2 \geq 2 with non-vanishing Rosenberg index has special holonomy.

Introduction and main results

Let
\left(M,g\right)
be a connected Riemannian manifold of dimension
n
whose universal cover
\tilde{M}
admits a spin structure. We denote by
\tilde{g}
the pullback metric of
g
along the covering map
\tilde{M}\to M
.
If
\normal(\tilde{M},\tilde{g}\normal)
carries a parallel spinor,
g
is Ricci-flat, i.e. its Ricci tensor vanishes identically. The converse of this statement holds for closed connected Riemannian spin manifolds with non-zero
\hat{A}
-genus or more general non-zero Hitchin invariant
[
Hit74
]
. This is a direct consequence of the Schrödinger-Lichnerowicz formula and the non-triviality of the kernel of the spin Dirac operator. It is an open question whether the converse of this statement holds in general.

Question 1
.

If
g
is Ricci-flat, does
\normal(\tilde{M},\tilde{g}\normal)
admit a parallel spinor?

In this note, we give a positive answer to
1
for closed connected spin manifolds with non-vanishing Rosenberg index (
A
). Moreover, we show that in dimension
\geq 2
these manifolds have special holonomy for any Ricci-flat metric (
B
).

The Rosenberg index of a closed spin manifold is a class in the Real K-theory of the maximal group
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}
-algebra of the fundamental group of the manifold. It is defined as the higher index of the
\mathrm{\mathbb{C}l}_{n}
-linear spin Dirac operator twisted by the Mishchenko bundle (see
section

2
). In general, the Rosenberg index does not vanish whenever the
\hat{A}
-genus or the Hitchin invariant is non-zero, but also for many other classes of manifolds, as for example (area-)enlargeable manifolds
[
HS06
,
HS07
]
, and aspherical manifolds whose fundamental group satisfies the Novikov conjecture
[
Ros83
]
.

Theorem A
.

Let
\left(M,g\right)
be a closed connected Riemannian spin manifold with non-vanishing Rosenberg index. Then the following statements are equivalent:

(1)

The Riemannian manifold
\left(M,g\right)
is Ricci-flat.

(2)

The universal cover
\tilde{M}
, equipped with the pullback metric, admits a parallel spinor.

(3)

There exists a finite Riemannian covering carrying a non-trivial parallel spinor with respect to the pullback spin structure.

(4)

The metric
g
has non-negative scalar curvature.

The main part of the proof of
A
is to show the implication
(4)
\Rightarrow
(3)
. A non-zero classical index as the
\hat{A}
-genus or the Hitchin invariant gives rise to a non-trivial harmonic spinor, hence the implication
(4)
\Rightarrow
(3)
in
A
holds in this case by the Schrödinger-Lichnerowicz formula. In general, a non-vanishing higher index as the Rosenberg index does not give rise to a non-trivial harmonic spinor. But non-vanishing of the Rosenberg index is still an obstruction — so far the best obstruction based on index theory of Dirac operators — to the existence of a positive scalar curvature metric on a closed connected spin manifold. A classical rigidity statement by Bourguignon states that any closed Riemannian manifold that does not admit a positive scalar curvature metric is Ricci-flat if the scalar curvature is non-negative
[
KW75
]
. A new proof in the case of a non-vanishing Rosenberg index using purely spinorial techniques is given in
[
Ton25
, Section 3 and Section 4]
. Since Ricci-flat manifolds have a very special fundamental group by the
structure theorem for Ricci-flat manifolds
(see
3
), it is possible to detect the non-vanishing Rosenberg index by the spin Dirac operator twisted by some finite dimensional Hermitian vector bundle (
4
). This is the main ingredient for the proof of
A
. This detection principle we are using here is due to
[
SW21
]
, while an alternative approach is given in
[
RWY13
, Section 3]
. Here
[
RWY13
]
study the class of groups for which an associated non-vanishing higher index can be detected by a finite representation.

By Berger’s holonomy list
[
Ber55
]
, the reduced holonomy group of any connected
irreducible
Ricci-flat manifold of dimension
n
is given by
\SO\left(n\right)
in the generic case, or one of the following subgroups
[
Bes87
]
:

\SU\left(\left.n\middle/2\right.\right)\>\text{({locally Calabi-Yau})},\quad\Sp\left(\left.n\middle/4\right.\right)\>\text{({locally hyper-K\"{a}hler})},\quad G_{2}\quad\text{or}\quad\Spin\left(7\right).

Here irreducible means that the representation of the reduced holonomy group on the general
linear group of the tangent space is irreducible. By the de Rham splitting theorem
[
Rha52
]
,
any complete simply-connected Riemannian manifold is isometric to a Riemannian product of
complete non-flat irreducible manifolds and an Euclidean space. There exist examples of closed locally Calabi-Yau, locally hyper-Kähler,
G_{2}
and
\Spin\left(7\right)
manifolds but there is no connected irreducible Ricci-flat manifold with generic reduced holonomy known.

Question 2
.

Does any closed connected Ricci-flat manifold of dimension
n\geq 2
have special holonomy, i.e. its reduced holonomy group is a proper subgroup of
\SO\left(n\right)
?

For reducible manifolds and flat manifolds the answer to
2
is obviously yes. Since any Ricci-flat manifold of dimension
\leq 3
is already flat, this includes all manifolds of dimension 2 and 3. In dimension
\geq 4
,
2
is still open. It is related to spin geometry as follows. Any complete simply-connected irreducible non-flat Riemannian spin manifolds
\normal(\tilde{M},\tilde{g}\normal)
admits a parallel spinor if and only if it has special holonomy
[
Hit74
,
Wan89
]
. We obtain by
A
the following corollary, which answers
2
for spin manifolds of non-vanishing Rosenberg index.

Corollary B
.

Any closed connected Ricci-flat spin manifold of dimension
\geq 2
with non-vanishing Rosenberg index has special holonomy.

The Rosenberg index

In this section, we give a brief introduction to higher index theory with the goal to define the Rosenberg index

Ros83
]

-linear) spinor bundle,

for some backgrounds concerning Hilbert

Let
\left(M,g\right)
be a closed connected Riemannian spin manifold of dimension
n

\Spin\left(M,g\right)
, which is a lift of the
\SO\left(n\right)
-principle bundle of oriented orthonormal frames along the double covering
\Spin\left(n\right)\to\SO\left(n\right)

\displaystyle\mathcal{S}\coloneqq\Spin\left(M,g\right)\times_{\rho}\Delta,

\displaystyle\rho\colon\Spin\left(n\right)\to\Aut\left(\Delta\right)

complex spin representation

(2.1)

\displaystyle\mathfrak{S}\coloneqq\Spin\left(M,g\right)\times_{\text{cl}}\mathrm{\mathbb{C}l}_{n},

\displaystyle\text{cl}\colon\Spin\left(n\right)\to\Aut\left(\mathrm{\mathbb{C}l}_{n}\right)

left multiplication

(2.2)

They are called the
spinor bundle
and the
\mathrm{\mathbb{C}l}_{n}
-
linear spinor bundle
, respectively. Here we denote by
\mathrm{\mathbb{C}l}_{n}
the complexification of the Clifford algebra of
\mathbb{R}^{n}
which carries the structure of a graded Real
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}
-algebra and a graded Real Hilbert
\mathrm{\mathbb{C}l}_{n}
-module. The spinor bundle
\mathcal{S}
equipped with its natural metric, connection and Clifford multiplication has the structure of a Dirac bundle. The
\mathrm{\mathbb{C}l}_{n}
-linear spinor bundle
\mathfrak{S}
, equipped with the grading, Real structure and Hilbert module structure obtained fiber-wise from
\mathrm{\mathbb{C}l}_{n}
, the induced
\mathrm{\mathbb{C}l}_{n}
-linear connection, and the Clifford multiplication, carries the structure of a graded Real
\mathrm{\mathbb{C}l}_{n}
-linear Dirac bundle. Note that
\mathfrak{S}
is isomorphic as a Dirac bundle to a direct sum of copies of the spinor bundle
\mathcal{S}
.

We denote by
\pi
the fundamental group of the manifold
M
and by
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}\pi
the maximal group
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}
-algebra of
\pi
. The
Mishchenko bundle

\mathcal{L}
is the associated bundle of the universal cover of
M
by the representation

given by left multiplication
[
MF80
]
. It carries a natural flat connection, a
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}\pi
-valued metric and a Real structure. Tensoring the
\mathrm{\mathbb{C}l}_{n}
-linear spinor bundle by the Mishchenko bundle yields a graded Real
\mathrm{\mathbb{C}l}_{n}\otimes\mathrm{C}^{\ast}\pi
-linear Dirac bundle. The induced
\mathrm{\mathbb{C}l}_{n}\otimes\mathrm{C}^{\ast}\pi
-linear Dirac operator
\mathfrak{D}_{\mathcal{L}}
extends to a self-adjoint and regular unbounded operator

\mathfrak{D}_{\mathcal{L}}\colon\Hblanc^{1}\left(M,\mathfrak{S}\otimes\mathcal{L}\right)\xrightarrow{\mathmakebox[2em]{}}\Ltwoblanc\left(M,\mathfrak{S}\otimes\mathcal{L}\right).

(2.3)

Here the Sobolev spaces
\Hblanc^{1}\left(M,\mathfrak{S}\otimes\mathcal{L}\right)
and
\Ltwoblanc\left(M,\mathfrak{S}\otimes\mathcal{L}\right)
are countably generated Hilbert
\mathrm{\mathbb{C}l}_{n}\otimes\mathrm{C}^{\ast}\pi
-modules which are similarly defined as the corresponding Sobolev spaces in the classical case. Since the manifold
M
is compact, the generalized Rellich theorem
[
Ebe16
, Theorem 2.33]
yields that the functional calculus for unbounded regular and self-adjoint operators on Hilbert
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}
-modules restricts to

\Phi_{\mathfrak{D}_{\mathcal{L}}}\colon\Czblanc\left(\mathbb{R}\right)\xrightarrow{\mathmakebox[2em]{}}\Kom_{\mathrm{\mathbb{C}l}_{n}\otimes\mathrm{C}^{\ast}\pi}\left(\Ltwoblanc\left(M,\mathfrak{S}\otimes\mathcal{L}\right)\right)

(2.4)

satisfying all properties stated in
[
Ebe16
, Theorem 1.19]
. Here

denotes the compact operators on a Hilbert
\mathcal{A}
-module

\mathrm{C}^{\ast}
-algebra
\mathcal{A}
. The map
\Phi_{\mathfrak{D}_{\mathcal{L}}}
defines a class in the spectral picture of the

-th Real K-theory group of
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}\pi

[
Roe17
, Lecture 13]
. It is called the

of the manifold

.

Proof of the main theorem

In this section, we give a proof of
A
. It is based on the structure theorem for Ricci-flat manifolds
[
FW75
, Theorem 4.5]
which is a direct consequence of the Cheeger–Gromoll splitting theorem
[

]

\mathrm{C}^{\ast}\pi
-modules by

]
.

Theorem 3

(Structure theorem for Ricci-flat manifolds)
.

Any closed connected Ricci-flat manifold
M
admits a finite normal Riemannian covering
N\times T^{q}\to M
consisting of a closed simply-connected Ricci-flat manifold
N
and a flat torus
T^{q}
. In particular, the fundamental group of
M
contains the subgroup
\mathbb{Z}^{q}
with finite index.

Proposition 4

(Proposition 3.6 in
[
SW21
]
)
.

Let
\pi
be a group containing the subgroup
\mathbb{Z}^{q}
with index
d
, and let
\mathcal{B}
be a possibly unbounded self-adjoint and regular operator on a countably generated Hilbert
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}\pi
-module. Suppose

is compact in the sense of Hilbert
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}\pi
-modules for the bounded transform
T\coloneqq\mathcal{B}\left(\mathcal{B}^{2}+1\right)^{-1/2}
. If 0 is in the spectrum of
\mathcal{B}

\rho\colon\pi\to U\left(d\right)
such that 0 is in the spectrum of
\mathcal{B}\otimes_{\rho}\id
.

The non-vanishing Rosenberg index gives rise to a family of almost

\big\|\bar{u}_{\epsilon}-\big\langle u_{\epsilon}\left(p\right)\mkern 2.0mu,u_{\epsilon}\left(p\right)\big\rangle\big\|_{\mathrm{\mathbb{C}l}_{n}\otimes\mathrm{C}^{\ast}\pi}\lesssim\epsilon^{r},\quad\text{with}\quad\bar{u}_{\epsilon}\coloneqq\frac{1}{\mathrm{vol}\left(M\right)}\int_{M}\big\langle u_{\epsilon}\left(q\right)\mkern 2.0mu,u_{\epsilon}\left(q\right)\big\rangle\mkern 4.0mu\mathrm{d}\mkern-0.7muq,
