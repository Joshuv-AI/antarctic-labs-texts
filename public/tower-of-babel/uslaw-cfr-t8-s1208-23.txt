8 C.F.R. § 1208.23 — Restoration of status.
Citation: us:cfr:8:1208.23
Creator: Executive Office For Immigration Review, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-1208.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 1208 (Procedures For Asylum And Withholding Of Removal). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An alien who was maintaining his or her nonimmigrant status at the time of filing an asylum application and has such application denied may continue in or be restored to that status, if it has not expired.
