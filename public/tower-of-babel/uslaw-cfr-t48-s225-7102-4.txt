48 C.F.R. § 225.7102-4 — 225.7102-4 Contract clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:225.7102-4
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-225.7102-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 225 (Foreign Acquisition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Use the clause at 252.225-7025, Restriction on Acquisition of Forgings, in solicitations and contracts, unless—
(a) The supplies being acquired do not contain any of the items listed in 225.7102-1; or
(b) An exception in 225.7102-2 applies. If an exception applies to only a portion of the acquisition, specify the excepted portion in the solicitation and contract.
