51 U.S.C. § 31502 — Maintenance of facilities
Citation: us:usc:51:31502
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section31502
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 315 (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In order to sustain healthy Centers that are capable of carrying out the Administration’s missions, the Administrator shall ensure that adequate maintenance and upgrading of those Center facilities is performed on a regular basis.
