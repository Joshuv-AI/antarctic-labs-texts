50 C.F.R. § 260.25 — Licensed samplers.
Citation: us:cfr:50:260.25
Creator: National Marine Fisheries Service, National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-260.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 260 (Inspection And Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Any person deemed to have the necessary qualifications may be approved as a licensed sampler.
(b) Licensed samplers are authorized to draw samples, to confirm the identity of the lot, and assess the condition of containers in the lot.
(c) Licensed samplers are not authorized to inspect fish or fishery products or other marine ingredients.
