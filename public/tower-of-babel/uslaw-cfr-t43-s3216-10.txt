43 C.F.R. § 3216.10 — What types of lease interests may I transfer?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3216.10
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3216.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3200 (Geothermal Resource Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may transfer record title or operating rights, but you need BLM approval before your transfer is effective (see § 3216.21).
