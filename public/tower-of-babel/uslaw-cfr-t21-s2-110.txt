21 C.F.R. § 2.110 — Definition of ammonia under Federal Caustic Poison Act.
Citation: us:cfr:21:2.110
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-2.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 2 (General Administrative Rulings And Decisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the purpose of determining whether an article containing ammonia is subject to the Federal Caustic Poison Act, the ammonia content is to be calculated as NH3.
