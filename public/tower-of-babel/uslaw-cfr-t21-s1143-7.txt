21 C.F.R. § 1143.7 — Language requirements for required warning statements.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1143.7
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1143.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1143 (Minimum Required Warning Statements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The text in each warning statement required in § 1143.3 or § 1143.5 must be in the English language, except as follows:
(a) In the case of an advertisement that appears in a non-English medium, the text in the required warning statement must appear in the predominant language of the medium whether or not the advertisement is in English, and;
(b) In the case of an advertisement that appears in an English language medium but that is not in English, the text in the required warning statement must appear in the same language as that principally used in the advertisement.
