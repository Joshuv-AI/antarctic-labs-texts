22 U.S.C. § 286v — Membership for Taiwan in Fund
Citation: us:usc:22:286v
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section286v
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter XV (International Monetary Fund And Bank For Reconstruction And Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the sense of the Congress that it is the policy of the United States that Taiwan (before January 1, 1979, known as the Republic of China) shall be granted appropriate membership in the Fund and that the United States Executive Director of the Fund shall so notify the Fund.
