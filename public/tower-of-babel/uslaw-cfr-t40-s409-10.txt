40 C.F.R. § 409.10 — Applicability; description of the beet sugar processing subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:409.10
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-409.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 409 (Sugar Processing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges resulting from any operation attendant to the processing of sugar beets for the production of sugar.
