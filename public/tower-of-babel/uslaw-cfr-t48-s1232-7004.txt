48 C.F.R. § 1232.7004 — 1232.7004 Waivers.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1232.7004
Creator: Chapter 12—Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1232.7004
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1232 (Contract Financing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a vendor is unable to utilize DOT's DELPHI electronic invoicing system, DOT may consider waivers on a case-by-case basis. Vendors should contact their COR for procedures, or access the DELPHI website at http://www.dot.gov/cfo/delphi-einvoicing-system.html.
