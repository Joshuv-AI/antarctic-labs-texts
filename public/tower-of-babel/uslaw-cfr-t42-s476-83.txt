42 C.F.R. § 476.83 — Initial denial determinations.
Citation: us:cfr:42:476.83
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-476.83
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 476 (Quality Improvement Organization Review). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A determination by a QIO that the health care services furnished or proposed to be furnished to a patient are not medically necessary, are not reasonable, or are not at the appropriate level of care, is an initial denial determination and is appealable under part 473 of this chapter.
