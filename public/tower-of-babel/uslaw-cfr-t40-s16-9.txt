40 C.F.R. § 16.9 — Fees.
Citation: us:cfr:40:16.9
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-16.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 16 (Implementation Of Privacy Act Of 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No fees will be charged for providing the first copy of a record or any portion of a record to an individual to whom the record pertains. The fee schedule for reproducing other records is the same as that set forth in 40 CFR 21.07.
