16 U.S.C. § 835i — Contracts with State of Washington for maintenance and operation of fish hatcheries
Citation: us:usc:16:835i
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section835i
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 12D (Columbia Basin Project). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In connection with fish hatcheries built or to be built as a part of the fish-protection program required on the Columbia Basin Dam project, the Secretary of the Interior is authorized to contract with the State of Washington for the maintenance and operation of any of them at the expense of said State.
