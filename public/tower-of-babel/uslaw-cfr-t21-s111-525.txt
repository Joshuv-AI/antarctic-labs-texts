21 C.F.R. § 111.525 — What requirements apply to a returned dietary supplement that quality control personnel approve for reprocessing?
Citation: us:cfr:21:111.525
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-111.525
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 111 (Current Good Manufacturing Practice In Manufacturing, Packaging, Labeling, Or Holding Operations For Dietary Supplements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You must ensure that any returned dietary supplements that are reprocessed meet all product specifications established in accordance with § 111.70(e); and
(b) Quality control personnel must approve or reject the release for distribution of any returned dietary supplement that is reprocessed.
