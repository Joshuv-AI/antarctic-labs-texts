10 C.F.R. § 63.201 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:10:63.201
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-63.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 63 (Disposal Of High-Level Radioactive Wastes In A Geologic Repository At Yucca Mountain, Nevada). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart covers the storage of radioactive material by DOE in the Yucca Mountain repository and on the Yucca Mountain site. For the purposes of demonstrating compliance with this subpart, to the extent there may be any conflict with the requirements specified in this subpart and the requirements contained in subparts A-J of this regulation, including definitions, the requirements in this subpart shall take precedence.
