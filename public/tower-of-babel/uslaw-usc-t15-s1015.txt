15 U.S.C. § 1015 — “State” defined
Citation: us:usc:15:1015
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1015
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 20 (Regulation Of Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this chapter, the term “State” includes the several States, Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, Guam, and the District of Columbia.
