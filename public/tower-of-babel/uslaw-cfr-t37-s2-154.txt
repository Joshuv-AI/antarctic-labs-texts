37 C.F.R. § 2.154 — Publication in Official Gazette.
Citation: us:cfr:37:2.154
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-2.154
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 2 (Rules Of Practice In Trademark Cases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A notice of the claim of benefits under the Act of 1946 and a reproduction of the mark will then be published in the Official Gazette as soon as practicable. The published mark will retain its original registration number.
