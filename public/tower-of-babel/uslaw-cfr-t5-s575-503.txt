5 C.F.R. § 575.503 — Who may approve the payment of an extended assignment incentive?
Citation: us:cfr:5:575.503
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-575.503
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 575 (Recruitment, Relocation, And Retention Incentives; Supervisory Differentials; And Extended Assignment Incentives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An authorized agency official must review and approve the offer of an extended assignment incentive for an employee, including the amount of such incentive. The authorized agency official must be at a higher level than the official who made the initial decision to offer an extended assignment incentive, unless there is no official at a higher level in the agency.
