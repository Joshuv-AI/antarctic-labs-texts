41 C.F.R. § 303-70.402 — -70.402 Transportation of deceased employee's POV.
Citation: us:cfr:41:303-70.402
Creator: Chapter 303—Payment Of Expenses Connected With The Death Of Certain Employees
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-303-70.402
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 303-70 (70—Agency Requirements For Payment Of Expenses Connected With The Death Of Certain Employees And Immediate Family Members). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies must pay costs associated with returning the POV from the TDY location to the employee's permanent official station, but only if the agency had authorized the use of the employee's POV at the TDY location as more advantageous to the Government than other means of transportation.
