42 U.S.C. § 4101e — Exemption from fees for certain map change requests
Citation: us:usc:42:4101e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section4101e
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 50 (National Flood Insurance); Subchapter III (Coordination Of Flood Insurance With Land-Management Programs In Flood-Prone Areas). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, a requester shall be exempt from submitting a review or processing fee for a request for a flood insurance rate map change based on a habitat restoration project that is funded in whole or in part with Federal or State funds, including dam removal, culvert redesign or installation, or the installation of fish passage.
