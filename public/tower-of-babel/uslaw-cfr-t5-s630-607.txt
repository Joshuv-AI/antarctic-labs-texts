5 C.F.R. § 630.607 — Transfer and recredit of home leave.
Citation: us:cfr:5:630.607
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-630.607
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 630 (Absence And Leave). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee is entitled to have his home leave account transferred or recredited to his account when he moves between agencies or is reemployed without a break in service of more than 90 days.
