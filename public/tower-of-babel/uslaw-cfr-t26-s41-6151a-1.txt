26 C.F.R. § 41.6151(a)-1 — (a)-1 Time and place for paying tax.
Citation: us:cfr:26:41.6151(a)-1
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-41.6151(a)-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 41 (Excise Tax On Use Of Certain Highway Motor Vehicles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In general. The tax must be paid at the time prescribed in § 41.6071(a)-1 for filing the return and at the place prescribed in § 41.6091-1 for filing the return.
(b) Effective/applicability date. This section applies on and after July 1, 2015. For rules applicable before that date, see 26 CFR 41.6151(a)-1 and 41.6151(a)-1T (revised as of April 1, 2014).
