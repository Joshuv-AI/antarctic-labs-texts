46 C.F.R. § 111.05-31 — -31 Grounding conductors for systems.
Citation: us:cfr:46:111.05-31
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-111.05-31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 111 (Electric Systems—General Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A conductor for grounding a direct-current system must be the larger of:
(1) The largest conductor supplying the system; or
(2) No. 8 AWG (8.4mm 2).
(b) A conductor for grounding the neutral of an alternating-current system must meet Table 111.05-31(b).
