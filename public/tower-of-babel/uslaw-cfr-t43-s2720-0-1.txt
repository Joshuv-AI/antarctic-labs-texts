43 C.F.R. § 2720.0-1 — -1 Purpose.

Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2720.0-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2720 (Conveyance Of Federally-Owned Mineral Interests). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of these regulations is to establish procedures under section 209 of the Federal Land Policy and Management Act of 1976, 43 U.S.C. 1719, for conveyance of mineral interests owned by the United States where the surface is or will be in non-Federal ownership.
