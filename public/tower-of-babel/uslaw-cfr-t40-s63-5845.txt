40 C.F.R. § 63.5845 — When must I conduct subsequent performance tests?
Citation: us:cfr:40:63.5845
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-63.5845
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 63 (National Emission Standards For Hazardous Air Pollutants For Source Categories). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must conduct a performance test every 5 years following the initial performance test for any standard you meet with an add-on control device.
