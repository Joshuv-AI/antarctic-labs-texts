39 C.F.R. § 964.18 — Compromise and informal disposition.
Citation: us:cfr:39:964.18
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-964.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 964 (Withheld Mail). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in these rules precludes the compromise, settlement, and informal disposition of proceedings initiated under these rules at any time prior to the issuance of the final agency decision.
