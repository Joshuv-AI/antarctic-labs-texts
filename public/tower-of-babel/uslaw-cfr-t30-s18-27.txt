30 C.F.R. § 18.27 — Gaskets.
Citation: us:cfr:30:18.27
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-18.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 18 (Electric Motor-Driven Mine Equipment And Accessories). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A gasket(s) shall not be used between any two surfaces forming a flame-arresting path except as follows:
(a) A gasket of lead, elastomer, or equivalent will be acceptable provided the gasket does not interfere with an acceptable metal-to-metal joint.
(b) A lead gasket(s) or equivalent will be acceptable between glass and a hard metal to form all or a part of a flame-arresting path.
