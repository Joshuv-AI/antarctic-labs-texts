36 C.F.R. § 272.3 — Public service use.
Citation: us:cfr:36:272.3
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-272.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 272 (Use Of “Woodsy Owl” Symbol). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Chief of the Forest Service may authorize the use of Woodsy Owl for noncommercial educational purposes, without charge, when such use is essentially as a public service and will, in his judgment, contribute to public information and education concerning wise use of the environment and programs which foster maintenance and improvement of environmental quality.
