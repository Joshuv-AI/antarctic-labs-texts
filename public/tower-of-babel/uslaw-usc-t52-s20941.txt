52 U.S.C. § 20941 — Establishment
Citation: us:usc:52:20941
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title52-section20941
Context: United States Code, Title 52 (Voting And Elections); Chapter 209 (Election Administration Improvement); Subchapter II (Commission); Part A (Establishment And General Organization); Subpart 2 (Election Assistance Commission Standards Board And Board Of Advisors). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are hereby established the Election Assistance Commission Standards Board (hereafter in this subchapter referred to as the “Standards Board”) and the Election Assistance Commission Board of Advisors (hereafter in this subchapter referred to as the “Board of Advisors”).
