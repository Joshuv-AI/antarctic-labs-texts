42 C.F.R. § 137.275 — May Self-Governance Tribes include IHS construction programs in a construction project agreement or in a funding agreement?
Citation: us:cfr:42:137.275
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-137.275
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 137 (Tribal Self-Governance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, Self-Governance Tribes may choose to assume construction programs in a construction project agreement, in a funding agreement, or in a combination of the two. These programs may include the following:
(a) Maintenance and improvement program;
(b) Construction program functions; and
(c) Planning services and construction management services.
