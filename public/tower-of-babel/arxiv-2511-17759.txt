TTP25-045, P3H-25-095 Decay matrix of 𝑩 B - 𝑩 ¯ \bar{B} mixing: Mixing of dimension-seven operators into dimension-six operators under renormalization
Artyom Hovhannisyan a and, Ulrich Nierste b

Abstract
The precise measurement of the width difference Δ ​ Γ s \Delta\Gamma_{s} among the mass eigenstates of the B s B_{s} - B ¯ s \bar{B}_{s} system requires the calculation of the corresponding decay matrix to order α s / m b \alpha_{s}/m_{b} . QCD corrections to power-suppressed terms in the Heavy Quark Expansion involve the renormalization of dimension-7 four-quark operators for which no general methodology is available yet. In the MS ¯ \overline{\rm MS} scheme one-loop corrections to matrix elements of dimension-7 operators violate the power counting, but we find the responsible terms to be infrared-finite and show that they can be absorbed into finite counterterms proportional to dimension-6 operators. We calculate all these counterterms and subsequently verify the consistency of our results with hadronic matrix elements calculated in the limit of a large number N c N_{c} of colours. The condition of correct power counting implies constraints on the possible definitions of evanescent operators.

4
Matrix elements for
\mathbf{N_{c}\to\infty}

In the limit
N_{c}\to\infty
one can calculate the hadronic matrix
elements exactly, because they factorize into the product of two current
operators which can be expressed in term of the
B_{s}
meson decay
constant
f_{B_{s}}
. In this section we do this calculation for the

\overline{\rm MS}
-renormalized one-loop matrix elements of the studied
dimension-7 operators. These calculations provide sophisticated checks
of the conceptual and calculational correctness of our results and
shed light on the issue of allowed choices for the parameters

\widetilde{a}_{2}
and
\widetilde{b}_{2,3}
in the definitions of the
evanescent operators in Eq. (
3.7
). The latter feature has to do
with the fact that the large-
N_{c}
calculation of the matrix elements
of the colour-flipped operators
\widetilde{R}_{2,3}
involves a
four-dimensional Fierz transform.

In Ref.
Beneke:1998sy
the limit
N_{c}\to\infty
has lead to a
non-trivial result of the calculation of
\delta R_{0}
: In the

\overline{\rm MS}
scheme the factorized matrix element

\langle\bar{B}_{s}|R_{0}^{\overline{\rm MS}}|B_{s}\rangle^{\rm fac}
is proportional to

(\bar{m}_{b}+\bar{m}_{s})^{2}-M_{B_{s}}^{2}
with the
\overline{\rm MS}
quark masses

\bar{m}_{b,s}
. This factor does does not scale like
m_{b}\Lambda_{\textit{\scriptsize{QCD}}}
as
required for the proper power counting, one needs instead a (properly
renormalon-subtracted) pole mass
m_{b}^{\rm pole}
. The contribution from

\delta R_{0}
was found to fix this problem by providing the
\alpha_{s}

correction which changes
\bar{m}_{b}^{2}-M_{B_{s}}^{2}
to
m_{b}^{\rm pole\,2}-M_{B_{s}}^{2}
. So the large-
N_{c}
check illustrates that

\langle\bar{B}_{s}|R_{0}^{\overline{\rm MS}}|B_{s}\rangle
does not scale like

\Lambda_{\textit{\scriptsize{QCD}}}/m_{b}
, while

\langle\bar{B}_{s}|R_{0}^{\rm ren}|B_{s}\rangle=\langle\bar{B}_{s}|R_{0}^{\overline{\rm MS}}+\delta R_{0}|B_{s}\rangle
does.

To calculate the factorized matrix elements one uses

\displaystyle\langle 0|\bar{b}\gamma_{\mu}\gamma_{5}s(x)|B_{s}\rangle=if_{B_{s}}P_{\mu}e^{-iPx},\qquad\qquad~\,\langle\bar{B}_{s}|\bar{b}\gamma_{\mu}\gamma_{5}s(x)|0\rangle=-if_{B_{s}}P_{\mu}e^{iPx},

(4.1)

\displaystyle\langle 0|\bar{b}\gamma_{5}s(x)|B_{s}\rangle=-if_{B_{s}}\frac{M_{B_{s}}^{2}}{m_{b}+m_{s}}e^{-iPx},\qquad\langle\bar{B}_{s}|\bar{b}\gamma_{5}s(x)|0\rangle=-if_{B_{s}}\frac{M_{B_{s}}^{2}}{m_{b}+m_{s}}e^{iPx},

(4.2)

where
|B_{s}\rangle=|B_{s}(P)\rangle
,
|\bar{B}_{s}\rangle=|\bar{B}_{s}(P)\rangle

and the results in Eq. (
4.2
) are obtained by applying

\partial^{\mu}
to Eq. (
4.1
) and using

\partial^{\mu}\bar{b}\gamma_{\mu}\gamma_{5}s=\bar{b}(\overleftarrow{D}^{\mu}+D^{\mu})\gamma_{\mu}\gamma_{5}s
. With
these results one finds the factorized matrix elements

Beneke:1996gn
;
Lenz:2006hd

\displaystyle\langle\bar{B}_{s}|Q|B_{s}\rangle^{\mathrm{fac}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle f_{B_{s}}^{2}M_{B_{s}}^{2}\left(2+\frac{2}{N_{c}}\right),\quad\langle\bar{B}_{s}|\widetilde{Q}_{S}^{\overline{\rm MS}}(\mu)|B_{s}\rangle^{\mathrm{fac}}\;=\;f_{B_{s}}^{2}M_{B_{s}}^{2}\frac{M_{B_{s}}^{2}}{(\overline{m}_{b}+\overline{m}_{s})^{2}}\left(1-\frac{2}{N_{c}}\right).\quad

(4.3)

4.1
Operator
R_{2}

In the large-
N_{c}
limit we obtain from Eq. (
3.9
) with Eq. (
4.3
):

\displaystyle\langle\bar{B}_{s}|R_{2}^{\overline{\rm MS}}|B_{s}\rangle^{\mathrm{large}\,N_{c}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle f_{B_{s}}^{2}M_{B_{s}}^{2}\frac{\alpha_{s}\,N_{c}}{4\pi}\cdot\left[4\log\frac{m_{b}}{\mu}-4\right]\,+\,\mathcal{O}(\alpha_{s}^{2},1/m_{b}).

(4.4)

The large-
N_{c}
matrix element of
m_{b}^{2}R_{2}
reads

\displaystyle m_{b}^{2}\langle\bar{B}_{s}|R_{2}|B_{s}\rangle^{\mathrm{large}\,N_{c}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\langle\bar{B}_{s}|\bar{b}\gamma_{\mu}\gamma_{5}s|0\rangle\langle 0|\bar{b}\overleftarrow{D}_{\rho}\gamma_{\mu}\gamma_{5}D^{\rho}s|B_{s}\rangle+\langle\bar{B}_{s}|\bar{b}\overleftarrow{D}_{\rho}\gamma_{\mu}\gamma_{5}D^{\rho}s|0\rangle\langle 0|\bar{b}\gamma_{\mu}\gamma_{5}s|B_{s}\rangle.\quad

(4.5)

In order to calculate the current matrix element with derivatives, one
applies
\partial_{\rho}\partial^{\rho}
to Eq. (
4.1
) giving the
desired term twice and terms with two covariant derivatives acting on either

\bar{b}
or
s
:

\displaystyle 2\langle 0|\bar{b}\overleftarrow{D}_{\rho}\gamma_{\mu}\gamma_{5}D^{\rho}s|{B}_{s}\rangle+\langle 0|\bar{b}\overleftarrow{D}_{\rho}\overleftarrow{D}^{\rho}\gamma_{\mu}\gamma_{5}s|{B}_{s}\rangle+\langle 0|\bar{b}\gamma_{\mu}\gamma_{5}D_{\rho}D^{\rho}s|{B}_{s}\rangle=-if_{B_{s}}P_{\mu}M_{B_{s}}^{2}e^{-iPx}.

(4.6)

For the latter one needs

\displaystyle D^{2}=\not{\!\!D}\not{\!\!D}+\frac{g_{s}}{2}\sigma_{{\rho}{\nu}}F^{\rho\nu,a}T^{a},

(4.7)

with the gluon field strength tensor
F^{\rho\nu,a}
in order to apply

i\bar{b}\overleftarrow{\not{\!\!D}}=-m_{b}\bar{b}
and

i\!\!\not{\!\!\!D}s=m_{s}s
, and
\sigma_{\rho\nu}=i(\gamma_{\rho}\gamma_{\nu}-\gamma_{\nu}\gamma_{\rho})/2
.
Using this relation we can write

\displaystyle\langle 0|\bar{b}\gamma_{\mu}\gamma_{5}D_{\rho}D^{\rho}s|{B}_{s}\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-m_{s}^{2}\langle 0|\bar{b}\gamma_{\mu}\gamma_{5}s|{B}_{s}\rangle+\frac{1}{2}\langle 0|\bar{b}\gamma_{\mu}\sigma_{\rho\nu}g_{s}F^{\rho\nu,a}T^{a}\gamma_{5}s|{B}_{s}\rangle,

(4.8)

\displaystyle\langle 0|\bar{b}\overleftarrow{D}_{\rho}\overleftarrow{D}^{\rho}\gamma_{\mu}\gamma_{5}s|{B}_{s}\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-m_{b}^{2}\langle 0|\bar{b}\gamma_{\mu}\gamma_{5}s|{B}_{s}\rangle+\frac{1}{2}\langle 0|\bar{b}\sigma_{\rho\nu}\gamma_{\mu}g_{s}F^{\rho\nu,a}T^{a}\gamma_{5}s|{B}_{s}\rangle.

(4.9)

Combining these results one arrives at

\displaystyle\langle 0|\bar{b}\overleftarrow{D}_{\rho}\gamma_{\mu}\gamma_{5}D^{\rho}s|{B}_{s}\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{i}{2}f_{B_{s}}P_{\mu}e^{-iPx}\left(m_{b}^{2}+m_{s}^{2}-M_{B_{s}}^{2}\right)-\frac{1}{2}\langle 0|S_{\mu}|{B}_{s}\rangle.

(4.10)

where we have introduced the operator

\displaystyle S_{\mu}

\displaystyle\equiv

\displaystyle\frac{g_{s}}{2}\,\bar{b}F^{\rho\nu,a}T^{a}\{\gamma_{\mu},\sigma_{\rho\nu}\}\gamma_{5}s.

(4.11)

In Eq. (
4.10
) one can drop the term
m_{s}^{2}
because it is of higher
order in our
1/m_{b}
expansion. Eq. (
4.10
) holds in any
renormalization scheme. The terms with
\langle 0|S_{\mu}|{B}_{s}\rangle
are formally of higher order in
1/m_{b}
, but in the
\overline{\rm MS}
scheme the power counting is not manifest. Just as in the case
of
R_{2},\ldots\widetilde{R}_{3}
in Sec.
3
the matrix elements of
the current operator
S_{\mu}^{\overline{\rm MS}}
contains unsuppressed
pieces, this time
\propto\alpha_{s}\langle 0|\bar{b}\gamma_{\mu}\gamma_{5}s(x)|B_{s}\rangle
. Again, these terms result from hard loop
momenta, are IR finite and perturbatively calculable. Putting everything
together yields

\displaystyle m_{b}^{2}\langle\bar{B}_{s}|{R}_{2}^{\overline{\rm MS}}|B_{s}\rangle^{\mathrm{large}\,N_{c}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-f_{B_{s}}^{2}M_{B_{s}}^{2}\left(M_{B_{s}}^{2}-\bar{m}_{b}^{2}-\bar{m}_{s}^{2}\right)-\frac{1}{2}\langle\bar{B}_{s}|\bar{b}\gamma_{\mu}\gamma_{5}s|0\rangle\langle 0|S_{\mu}^{\overline{\rm MS}}|{B}_{s}\rangle

(4.12)

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{2}\langle\bar{B}_{s}|S_{\mu}^{\overline{\rm MS}}|0\rangle\langle 0|\bar{b}\gamma_{\mu}\gamma_{5}s|B_{s}\rangle.

Dropping the
S_{\mu}
terms, which are NLO in
\alpha_{s}
, reproduces the
result of the LO calculation in Ref.
Beneke:1996gn
.

It remains to calculate the matrix element of
S_{\mu}
. The operator

S_{\mu}
defined in Eq. (
4.11
) has the following Feynman rule

\displaystyle-ig_{s}k^{\rho}\{\gamma_{\mu},\sigma_{\rho\nu}\}T^{a}_{ij}\gamma_{5},

(4.13)

where

k
is the incoming momentum of the gluon. A second coupling involves
two gluons and does not contribute at order
\alpha_{s}
. Using this
Feynman rule we find the one-loop matrix element
S_{\mu}
as

\displaystyle\langle 0|S_{\mu}^{\overline{\rm MS}}|{B}_{s}\rangle^{(1)}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle m_{b}^{2}\,\frac{\alpha_{s}C_{F}}{4\pi}\,4\log\frac{m_{b}}{\mu}\,if_{B_{s}}P_{\mu}e^{-iPx}+\mathcal{O}(\alpha_{s}^{2},m_{b}).

(4.14)

This result solely stems from hard loop momenta, is insensitive to the
external states and could therefore calculated in perturbative QCD with external quark
states. At the calculated order the renormalization scheme for
m_{b}
in
Eq. (
4.14
) is not defined, this will matter only in an NNLO calculation.

In calculation of Eq.(
4.14
) it was necessary to calculate
\langle 0|\bar{b}\gamma_{5}D_{\rho}s|{B}_{s}\rangle
. We use
D_{\rho}=\{\gamma_{\rho},\not{\!\!D}\}/2
:

\displaystyle\langle 0|\bar{b}\gamma_{5}D_{\rho}s|{B}_{s}\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\langle 0|\bar{b}\gamma_{5}\not{\!\!D}\gamma_{\rho}s|{B}_{s}\rangle-\frac{1}{2}im_{s}\langle 0|\bar{b}\gamma_{5}\gamma_{\rho}s|{B}_{s}\rangle

(4.15)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\partial_{\mu}\langle 0|\bar{b}\gamma_{5}\gamma_{\mu}\gamma_{\rho}s|{B}_{s}\rangle-\frac{1}{2}\langle 0|\bar{b}\gamma_{5}\overleftarrow{\not{\!\!D}}\gamma_{\rho}s|{B}_{s}\rangle+\frac{1}{2}im_{s}\langle 0|\bar{b}\gamma_{\rho}\gamma_{5}s|{B}_{s}\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\partial_{\mu}\langle 0|\bar{b}\gamma_{5}\gamma_{\mu}\gamma_{\rho}s|{B}_{s}\rangle-\frac{1}{2}i(m_{b}-m_{s})\langle 0|\bar{b}\gamma_{\rho}\gamma_{5}s|{B}_{s}\rangle.

Using
\gamma_{\mu}\gamma_{\rho}=g_{\mu\rho}-i\sigma_{\mu\rho}

and
\langle 0|\bar{b}\gamma_{5}\sigma_{\mu\rho}s|{B}_{s}\rangle=0
one arrives at

\displaystyle\langle 0|\bar{b}\gamma_{5}D_{\rho}s|{B}_{s}\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{2}f_{B_{s}}P_{\rho}\frac{M_{B_{s}}^{2}}{m_{b}+m_{s}}e^{-iPx}+\frac{1}{2}(m_{b}-m_{s})P_{\rho}f_{B_{s}}e^{-iPx}

(4.16)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{2}f_{B_{s}}P_{\rho}\frac{M_{B_{s}}^{2}-m_{b}^{2}-m_{s}^{2}}{m_{b}+m_{s}}e^{-iPx}.

To specify to the
\overline{\rm MS}
scheme we finally need

\displaystyle\bar{m}_{b}(\mu)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle m_{b}^{\rm pole}\left[1+\frac{\alpha_{s}(\mu)}{4\pi}C_{F}\left(-4+6\log\frac{m_{b}}{\mu}\right)\right]

(4.17)

which implies

\displaystyle\frac{M_{B_{s}}^{2}}{\bar{m}_{b}^{2}}-1

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left[\frac{M_{B_{s}}^{2}}{m_{b}^{\rm pole\,2}}-1\right]\,+\,\frac{M_{B_{s}}^{2}}{m_{b}^{2}}\frac{\alpha_{s}(\mu)}{4\pi}2C_{F}\left(4-6\log\frac{m_{b}}{\mu}\right)+\mathcal{O}(\alpha_{s}^{2})

(4.18)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\alpha_{s}(\mu)}{4\pi}2C_{F}\left(4-6\log\frac{m_{b}}{\mu}\right)+\mathcal{O}(\alpha_{s}^{2},1/m_{b}).

Using this in Eq. (
4.16
) we obtain the final result for the current
matrix element

\displaystyle\langle 0|\left[\bar{b}\gamma_{5}D_{\rho}s\right]^{\overline{\rm MS}}|{B}_{s}\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-f_{B_{s}}m_{b}P_{\rho}e^{-iPx}\,\frac{\alpha_{s}(\mu)}{4\pi}C_{F}\left(4-6\log\frac{m_{b}}{\mu}\right)+\mathcal{O}(\alpha_{s}^{2},1/m_{b}).

(4.19)

Here the factor in brackets is specific to the
\overline{\rm MS}
scheme,
while the the scheme of
m_{b}
is still arbitrary, because a scheme
transformation will affect the result at order
\alpha_{s}^{2}
.
m_{s}
has
been neglected, since terms of order
m_{s}/m_{b}
are power-suppressed.

Recalling
C_{F}\simeq N_{c}/2
and plugging Eqs. (
4.14
) and (
4.19
) into
Eq.(
4.12
) we find

\displaystyle\langle\bar{B}_{s}|{R}_{2}^{\overline{\rm MS}}|B_{s}\rangle^{\mathrm{large}\,N_{c}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle f_{B_{s}}^{2}M_{B_{s}}^{2}\,\frac{\alpha_{s}N_{c}}{4\pi}\left(4\log\frac{m_{b}}{\mu}-4\right)+\mathcal{O}(\alpha_{s}^{2},1/m_{b}).

(4.20)

Comparing this result with Eq. (
4.4
) we see that the two
results are in agreement with each other. Thus the properly
renormalized matrix elements satisfy

\langle\bar{B}_{s}|{R}_{2}^{\rm ren}|B_{s}\rangle^{\mathrm{large}\,N_{c}}=\mathcal{O}(1/m_{b})
at NLO, because the
\alpha_{s}
term in Eq. (
4.20
) is canceled by

\delta R_{2}
.

From Eq. (

\displaystyle\langle\bar{B}_{s}|R_{3}^{\overline{\rm MS}}|B_{s}\rangle^{\mathrm{large}\,N_{c}}

The large-

\displaystyle m_{b}^{2}\langle{R}_{3}\rangle^{\mathrm{large}\,N_{c}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\langle\bar{B}_{s}|\bar{b}\gamma_{5}s|0\rangle\langle 0|\bar{b}\overleftarrow{D}_{\rho}\gamma_{5}D^{\rho}s|{B}_{s}\rangle+\langle\bar{B}_{s}|\bar{b}\overleftarrow{D}_{\rho}\gamma_{5}D^{\rho}s|0\rangle\langle 0|\bar{b}\gamma_{5}s|B_{s}\rangle.

(4.22)

Repeating the steps shown in the previous section associated operator

\displaystyle m_{b}^{2}\langle\bar{B}_{s}|{R}_{3}^{\overline{\rm MS}}|B_{s}\rangle^{\mathrm{large}\,N_{c}}

\displaystyle\phantom{+}f_{B_{s}}^{2}\frac{M_{B_{s}}^{2}}{(\bar{m}_{b}+\bar{m}_{s})^{2}}\left(M_{B_{s}}^{2}-\bar{m}_{b}^{2}-\bar{m}_{s}^{2}\right)

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{2}\langle\bar{B}_{s}|\bar{b}\gamma_{5}s|0\rangle\langle 0|G_{S}^{\overline{\rm MS}}|{B}_{s}\rangle-\frac{1}{2}\langle\bar{B}_{s}|G_{S}^{\overline{\rm MS}}|0\rangle\langle 0|\bar{b}\gamma_{5}s|B_{s}\rangle,

where we have introduced the operator

\displaystyle G_{S}=g_{s}\,\bar{b}_{i}F^{\rho\nu,a}T^{a}_{ij}\sigma_{\rho\nu}\gamma_{5}s_{j},

(4.24)

with the following Feynman rule

\displaystyle-2ig_{s}k^{\rho}\sigma_{\rho\nu}T^{a}_{ij}\gamma_{5},

(4.25)

where

\displaystyle\langle 0|G_{S}^{\overline{\rm MS}}|{B}_{s}\rangle^{(1)}=-m_{b}^{2}\frac{\alpha_{s}C_{F}}{4\pi}\cdot\left[-2+12\log\frac{m_{b}}{\mu}\right]if_{B_{s}}\frac{M_{B_{s}}^{2}}{m_{b}+m_{s}}e^{-iPx}+\mathcal{O}(\alpha_{s}^{2},m_{b}).

(4.26)

Also here the renormalization scheme for

will only be fixed if one extends the calculation to NNLO.

4.18
) and (

) into Eq. (
4.23

\displaystyle\langle\bar{B}_{s}|{R}_{3}|B_{s}\rangle^{\mathrm{large}\,N_{c}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-f_{B_{s}}^{2}M_{B_{s}}^{2}\frac{\alpha_{s}N_{c}}{4\pi}\left(6\log\frac{m_{b}}{\mu}-4\right)

\displaystyle+f_{B}^{2}M_{B_{s}}^{2}\frac{\alpha_{s}N_{c}}{4\pi}\left(6\log\frac{m_{b}}{\mu}-1\right)+\mathcal{O}(\alpha_{s}^{2},1/m_{b})

\displaystyle\phantom{+}3f_{B_{s}}^{2}M_{B_{s}}^{2}\frac{\alpha_{s}N_{c}}{4\pi}+\mathcal{O}(\alpha_{s}^{2},1/m_{b}).

Comparing this result with (

finite counterterms to the dimension-7 operators restoring the power
counting must be defined.

In this work we have done a first step in this direction
and calculated those parts of the
\overline{\rm MS}
-renormalized matrix
elements of the dimension-7 operators
R_{2,3}
and

\widetilde{R}_{2,3}
at order
\alpha_{s}
, which are associated with the
finite renormalization of these operators by terms proportional to
dimension-6 operators. We have found that the loop contributions
to
\langle R_{2,3}\rangle
and

\langle\widetilde{R}_{2,3}\rangle
scaling like
m_{b}^{0}
are indeed
infrared-finite, so that they can be cancelled by counterterms
proportional to the dimension-6 operators
Q
and
\widetilde{Q}_{S}

to restore the correct power counting,

\langle R_{2,3}^{\rm ren}\rangle,\langle\widetilde{R}_{2,3}^{\rm ren}\rangle=\mathcal{O}(1/m_{b})
. However,
we have also
found that these counterterms depend on the definitions of
evanescent operators and have shown that in the case of the
colour-flipped operators
\widetilde{R}_{2,3}
the
\epsilon
terms
of these operators must be chosen to obey Fierz symmetry. This
feature has been observed from the hadronic matrix elements
calculated in the large-
N_{c}
limit, which turned out to be very
subtle, with power-unsuppressed contributions from hard loop
corrections to operators involving the gluon field strength tensor.
The large-
N_{c}
criterion does not apply to the colour-straight
operators
R_{2,3}
and it is still an open question, whether
arbitrary choices of the
\epsilon
terms in the evanescent operators
are permissible or whether other conditions will fix them, for
example

\langle R_{2}\rangle=-\langle\widetilde{R}_{2}\rangle+{\cal O}(1/m_{b}^{2})
, which automatically holds at tree-level

Beneke:1996gn
but needs a finite counterterm at NLO.
Since
Z_{1Q}=Z_{1\widetilde{Q}_{S}}=0
and
Z_{0Q}
,
Z_{0\widetilde{Q}_{S}}
could be extracted from Ref.
Beneke:1998sy
, the
calculations presented in this paper complete the set of
renormalisation constants related to the mixing of
R_{j}
,
\widetilde{R}_{j}

into dimension-6 operators.

In conclusion, we have made a first step towards the calculation of the

B
-
\bar{B}
decay matrix at order
\alpha_{s}/m_{b}
,
i.e.
to
extend the 29-year-old LO result of Ref.
Beneke:1996gn
to NLO, by
calculating the counterterms listed in Tab.
1
.
As a by-product, we have calculated matrix elements of several

\bar{b}
-
s
current operators. Our results for the hard,
infrared-finite loop contributions are needed to restore the correct power
counting in any application involving these currents.
