25 U.S.C. § 1523 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:25:1523
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section1523
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 17 (Financing Economic Development Of Indians And Indian Organizations); Subchapter IV (Indian Business Grants). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated not to exceed the sum of $10,000,000 per year for fiscal year 1986 and each fiscal year thereafter for the purposes of this subchapter.
