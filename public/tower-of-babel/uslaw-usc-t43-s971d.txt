43 U.S.C. § 971d — Effect on valid existing Alaskan rights
Citation: us:usc:43:971d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section971d
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 22 (Rights-Of-Way And Other Easements In Public Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in sections 971a to 971e of this title shall affect adversely any valid existing rights.
