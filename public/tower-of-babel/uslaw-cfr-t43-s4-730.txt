43 C.F.R. § 4.730 — How does the Project Director commence the determination process?
Citation: us:cfr:43:4.730
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4.730
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4 (Department Of The Interior Hearings And Appeals Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless an heirship determination which is recognized by the Act already exists, the Project Director will commence the process of determining the heirs of a decedent by filing with the presiding officer the evidence described in § 4.731.
