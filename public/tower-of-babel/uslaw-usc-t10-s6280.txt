10 U.S.C. § 6280 — Availability of funds
Citation: us:usc:10:6280
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section6280
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part VI (Elements Of Department Of Defense And Other Matters); Subpart B (Atomic Energy Defense); Chapter 607 (Budget And Financial Management Matters); Subchapter I (Recurring National Security Authorization Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In General.—
Except as provided in subsection (b), amounts appropriated pursuant to a DOE national security authorization for operation and maintenance or for plant projects may, when so specified in an appropriations Act, remain available until expended.
(b) Exception for Program Direction Funds.—
Amounts appropriated for program direction pursuant to a DOE national security authorization for a fiscal year shall remain available to be obligated only until the end of that fiscal year.
