22 U.S.C. § 282h — Loans to or from International Bank for Reconstruction and Development; amendment to Articles of Agreement
Citation: us:usc:22:282h
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section282h
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter XI (International Finance Corporation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States Governor of the Corporation is authorized to agree to the amendments of the articles of agreement of the Corporation to remove the prohibition therein contained against the Corporation lending to or borrowing from the International Bank for Reconstruction and Development, and to place limitations on such borrowing.
