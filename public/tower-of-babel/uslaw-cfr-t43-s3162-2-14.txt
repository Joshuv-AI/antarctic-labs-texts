43 C.F.R. § 3162.2-14 — -14 May I appeal BLM's decision to require drainage protective measures?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3162.2-14
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3162.2-14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3160 (Onshore Oil And Gas Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may appeal any BLM decision requiring you take drainage protective measures. You may request BLM State Director review under 43 CFR 3165.3 and/or appeal to the Interior Board of Land Appeals under 43 CFR part 4 and subpart 1840.
