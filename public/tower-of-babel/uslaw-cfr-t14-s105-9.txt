14 C.F.R. § 105.9 — Inspections.
Citation: us:cfr:14:105.9
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-105.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 105 (Parachute Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator may inspect any parachute operation to which this part applies (including inspections at the site where the parachute operation is being conducted) to determine compliance with the regulations of this part.
