49 C.F.R. § 229.103 — Safe working pressure; factor of safety.
Citation: us:cfr:49:229.103
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-229.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 229 (Railroad Locomotive Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The safe working pressure for each steam generator shall be fixed by the chief mechanical officer of the carrier. The minimum factor of safety shall be four. The fixed safe working pressure shall be indicated on FRA Form F 6180-49A.
