34 C.F.R. § 299.22 — Filing requirements.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 299 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Any written submission under § 299.21 must be filed by hand delivery, mail, or email.
(b) The filing date for a written submission is the date on which the document is—
(1) Hand delivered;
(2) Mailed; or
(3) Emailed.
