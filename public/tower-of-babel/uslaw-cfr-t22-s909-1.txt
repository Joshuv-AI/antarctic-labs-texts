22 C.F.R. § 909.1 — Basis.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 909 (Decisionmaking). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Decisions of the Board shall be based upon the record of proceedings, shall be in writing, shall include findings of fact, and shall include a statement of the reasons for the decision.
