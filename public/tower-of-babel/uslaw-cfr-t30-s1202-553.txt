30 C.F.R. § 1202.553 — How do I value my production if I take more than my entitled share?
Citation: us:cfr:30:1202.553
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1202.553
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1202 (Royalties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you take more than your entitled share of production from a lease in an AFA for any month, you must determine the weighted-average value of all of the production that you take using the procedures in 30 CFR part 1206, and use that value for your entitled share of production.
