29 C.F.R. § 101.31 — Initiation of proceedings to hear and determine jurisdictional disputes under section 10(k).
Citation: us:cfr:29:101.31
Creator: National Labor Relations Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-101.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 101 (Statements Of Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The investigation of a jurisdictional dispute under section 10(k) is initiated by the filing of a charge, as described in § 101.2, by any person alleging a violation of paragraph (4)(D) of section 8(b). As soon as possible after a charge has been filed, the Regional Director serves on the parties a copy of the charge together with a notice of the filing of such charge.
