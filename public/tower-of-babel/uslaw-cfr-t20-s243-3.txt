20 C.F.R. § 243.3 — Payments pursuant to court decree or court-approved property settlement.
Citation: us:cfr:20:243.3
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-243.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 243 (Transfer, Assignment, Or Waiver Of Payments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Certain annuity components are subject to division pursuant to a court decree or to a court-approved property settlement incident to any such decree, as provided in part 295 of this chapter.
