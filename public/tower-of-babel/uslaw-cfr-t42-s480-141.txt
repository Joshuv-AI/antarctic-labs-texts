42 C.F.R. § 480.141 — Disclosure of QIO interpretations on the quality of health care.
Citation: us:cfr:42:480.141
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-480.141
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 480 (Acquisition, Protection, And Disclosure Of Quality Improvement Organization Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subject to the procedures for disclosure and notice of disclosure specified in §§ 480.104 and 480.105, a QIO may disclose to the public QIO interpretations and generalizations on the quality of health care that identify a particular institution.
