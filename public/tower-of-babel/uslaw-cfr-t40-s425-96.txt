40 C.F.R. § 425.96 — Pretreatment standards for new sources (PSNS).
Citation: us:cfr:40:425.96
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-425.96
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 425 (Leather Tanning And Finishing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in 40 CFR 403.7, any new source subject to this subpart that introduces process wastewater pollutants into a publicly owned treatment works must comply with 40 CFR part 403, and achieve the pretreatment standards contained in § 425.95.
