25 U.S.C. § 1543 — Funds limitation for private contracts
Citation: us:usc:25:1543
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section1543
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 17 (Financing Economic Development Of Indians And Indian Organizations); Subchapter V (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of entering into contracts pursuant to section 1542 of this title in fiscal year 1985, the Secretary is authorized to use not to exceed 6 percent of any funds appropriated for any fiscal year pursuant to section 1512 of this title. For fiscal year 1986 and for each fiscal year thereafter, there are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this subchapter.
