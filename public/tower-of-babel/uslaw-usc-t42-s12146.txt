42 U.S.C. § 12146 — New facilities
Citation: us:usc:42:12146
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section12146
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 126 (Equal Opportunity For Individuals With Disabilities); Subchapter II (Public Services); Part B (Actions Applicable To Public Transportation Provided By Public Entities Considered Discriminatory); Subpart i (Public Transportation Other Than By Aircraft Or Certain Rail Operations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of section 12132 of this title and section 794 of title 29, it shall be considered discrimination for a public entity to construct a new facility to be used in the provision of designated public transportation services unless such facility is readily accessible to and usable by individuals with disabilities, including individuals who use wheelchairs.
