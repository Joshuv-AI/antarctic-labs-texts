14 C.F.R. § 401.3 — The Associate Administrator for Commercial Space Transportation.
Citation: us:cfr:14:401.3
Creator: Commercial Space Transportation, Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-401.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 401 (Organization And Definitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Office is headed by an Associate Administrator to exercise the Secretary's authority to license or permit and otherwise regulate commercial space transportation and to discharge the Secretary's responsibility to encourage, facilitate, and promote commercial space transportation by the United States private sector.
