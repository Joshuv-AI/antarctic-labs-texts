27 C.F.R. § 22.174 — Receipt of shipment.
Citation: us:cfr:27:22.174
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-22.174
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 22 (Distribution And Use Of Tax-Free Alcohol). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

On receipt of a shipment of tax-free spirits, a representative of the Government agency shall inspect the shipment for any loss or deficiency. In the case of loss or deficiency, the agency shall annotate the receiving document and forward a copy to the appropriate TTB officer.
