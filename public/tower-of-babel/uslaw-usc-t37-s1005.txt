37 U.S.C. § 1005 — Army, Air Force, and Space Force: prompt payments required
Citation: us:usc:37:1005
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title37-section1005
Context: United States Code, Title 37 (Pay And Allowances Of The Uniformed Services); Chapter 19 (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Members of the Army, the Air Force, and the Space Force shall be paid at such times that arrears will at no time be more than two months, unless circumstances make further arrears unavoidable.
