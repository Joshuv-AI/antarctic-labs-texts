33 C.F.R. § 146.105 — General alarm system.
Citation: us:cfr:33:146.105
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-146.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 146 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each manned facility must have a general alarm system. When operated, this system shall be audible in all parts of the structure on which provided.
