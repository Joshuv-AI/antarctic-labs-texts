7 C.F.R. § 1.111 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1.111
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1 (Administrative Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of this subpart the terms individual, maintain, record, system of records, statistical record, and routine use shall have the meanings set forth in 5 U.S.C. 552a(a). The term agency shall mean an agency of USDA, unless otherwise indicated.
