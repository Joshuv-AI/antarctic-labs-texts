29 C.F.R. § 1614.309 — Enforcement of Special Panel decision.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1614.309
Creator: Equal Employment Opportunity Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1614.309
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1614 (Federal Sector Equal Employment Opportunity). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board shall, upon receipt of the decision of the Special Panel, order the agency concerned to take any action appropriate to carry out the decision of the Panel. The Board's regulations regarding enforcement of a final order of the Board shall apply. These regulations are set out at 5 CFR part 1201, subpart E.
