36 C.F.R. § 21.11 — Redemption of bath tickets.
Citation: us:cfr:36:21.11
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-21.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 21 (Hot Springs National Park; Bathhouse Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unused tickets may be redeemed by the purchaser within one year from the date of purchase, according to the redemption scale approved by the Superintendent.
