49 C.F.R. § 1037.2 — Cars.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1037.2
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1037.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1037 (Bulk Grain And Grain Products—Loss And Damage Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A car is not in suitable condition for the transportation of bulk grain and grain products when it is defective. The rules prescribed in this part 1037 apply on shipments transported solely in railroad-owned and railroad-leased cars.
