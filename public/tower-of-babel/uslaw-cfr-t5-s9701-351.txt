5 C.F.R. § 9701.351 — Setting an employee's starting pay.
Citation: us:cfr:5:9701.351
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security Human Resources Management System (Department Of Homeland Security—Office Of Personnel Management)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-9701.351
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 9701 (Department Of Homeland Security Human Resources Management System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

DHS will, after coordination with OPM, issue implementing directives regarding the starting rate of pay for an employee, including—
(a) An individual who is newly appointed or reappointed to the Federal service;
(b) An employee transferring to DHS from another Federal agency; and
(c) A DHS employee who moves from a noncovered position to a position already covered by this subpart.
