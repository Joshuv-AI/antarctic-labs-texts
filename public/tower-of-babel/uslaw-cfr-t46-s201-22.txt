46 C.F.R. § 201.22 — Firms and corporations.
Citation: us:cfr:46:201.22
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-201.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 201 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as regards law firms, practice before the Administration by firms or corporations on behalf of others shall not be permitted.
