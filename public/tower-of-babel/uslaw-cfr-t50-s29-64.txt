50 C.F.R. § 29.64 — What other provisions apply to my operations?
Citation: us:cfr:50:29.64
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-29.64
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 29 (Land Use Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Your pre-existing operations are also subject to the following regulations in this part 29:
(a) § 29.120(b), (d), (f), and (g) and § 29.121(a) and (c)-(f);
(b) § 29.170(a);
(c) §§ 29.180 and 29.181;
(d) § 29.190; and
(e) § 29.200.
