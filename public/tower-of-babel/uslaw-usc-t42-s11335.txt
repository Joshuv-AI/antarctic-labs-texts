42 U.S.C. § 11335 — Annual report
Citation: us:usc:42:11335
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section11335
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 119 (Homeless Assistance); Subchapter III (Federal Emergency Management Food And Shelter Program); Part A (Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The National Board shall transmit to the Congress an annual report covering each year in which it conducts activities with funds made available under this subchapter.
