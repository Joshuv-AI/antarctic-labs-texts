22 C.F.R. § 62.61 — Revocation.
Citation: us:cfr:22:62.61
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-62.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 62 (Exchange Visitor Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department may terminate a sponsor's program designation by revocation for cause as specified in § 62.50. Such sponsor may not apply for a new designation for five (5) years following the effective date of the revocation.
