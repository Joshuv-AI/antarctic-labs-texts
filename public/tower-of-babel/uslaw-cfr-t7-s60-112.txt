7 C.F.R. § 60.112 — Ingredient.
Citation: us:cfr:7:60.112
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-60.112
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 60 (Country Of Origin Labeling For Fish And Shellfish). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Ingredient means a component either in part or in full, of a finished retail food product.
