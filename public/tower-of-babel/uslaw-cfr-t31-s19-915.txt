31 C.F.R. § 19.915 — Agent or representative.
Citation: us:cfr:31:19.915
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-19.915
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 19 (Governmentwide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agent or representative means any person who acts on behalf of, or who is authorized to commit, a participant in a covered transaction.
