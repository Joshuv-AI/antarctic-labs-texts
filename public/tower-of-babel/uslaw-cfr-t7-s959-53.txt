7 C.F.R. § 959.53 — Handling for special purposes.
Citation: us:cfr:7:959.53
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-959.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 959 (Onions Grown In South Texas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Regulations in effect pursuant to §§ 959.42, 959.52, or 959.60 may be modified, suspended, or terminated to facilitate handling of onions for:
(a) Relief or charity;
(b) Experimental purposes;
(c) Export; and
(d) Other purposes which may be recommended by the committee and approved by the Secretary.
