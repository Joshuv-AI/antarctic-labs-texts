29 C.F.R. § 1902.15 — Certification of the record of a hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1902.15
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1902.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1902 (State Plans For The Development And Enforcement Of State Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon completion of any formal or informal hearing, the transcript thereof, together with written submissions, exhibits filed during the hearing, and any post-hearing presentations shall be certified by the officer presiding at the hearing to the Assistant Secretary.
