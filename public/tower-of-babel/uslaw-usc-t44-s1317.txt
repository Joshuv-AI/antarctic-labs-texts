44 U.S.C. § 1317 — Foreign Relations
Citation: us:usc:44:1317
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section1317
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 13 (Particular Reports And Documents). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In addition to the usual number of Foreign Relations, three thousand copies of each shall be printed: one thousand for the Senate and two thousand for the House of Representatives.
