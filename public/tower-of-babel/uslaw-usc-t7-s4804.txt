7 U.S.C. § 4804 — Notice and hearing
Citation: us:usc:7:4804
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section4804
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 79 (Pork Promotion, Research, And Consumer Information). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

During the period beginning on January 1, 1986, and ending 30 days after receipt of a proposal for an initial order submitted by any person affected by this chapter, the Secretary shall—
(1)
publish such proposed order; and
(2)
give due notice of and opportunity for public comment on such proposed order.
