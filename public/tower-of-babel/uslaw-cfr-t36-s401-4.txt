36 C.F.R. § 401.4 — Responsibility.
Citation: us:cfr:36:401.4
Creator: American Battle Monuments Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-401.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 401 (Monuments And Memorials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission is responsible for building and maintaining appropriate memorials commemorating the service of American Armed Forces at any place outside the United States where Armed Forces have served since April 6, 1917.
