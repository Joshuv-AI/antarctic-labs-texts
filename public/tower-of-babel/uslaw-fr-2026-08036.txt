Certain Cold-Rolled Steel Flat Products From the Republic of 
Korea: Preliminary Results and Rescission, in Part, of Countervailing 
Duty Administrative Review; 2023; Correction

AGENCY: Enforcement and Compliance, International Trade Administration, 
Department of Commerce.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The U.S. Department of Commerce (Commerce) published notice in 
the Federal Register of March 5, 2026, in which Commerce announced the 
preliminary results of the 2023 administrative review of the 
countervailing duty (CVD) order on certain cold-rolled steel flat 
products (CRS) from the Republic of Korea (Korea). This notice corrects 
the name of one of the companies for which this review was rescinded.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Christopher Doyle, AD/CVD Operations, 
Office IX, Enforcement and Compliance, International Trade 
Administration, U.S. Department of Commerce, 1401 Constitution Avenue 
NW, Washington, DC 20230; telephone: (202) 482-5882.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Background

    On March 5, 2026, Commerce published in the Federal Register the 
Preliminary Results of the 2023 CVD administrative review of CRS from 
Korea.\1\ In the Preliminary Results Federal Register notice, we 
incorrectly listed the name of POSCO C&C Co., Ltd.,'' a company for 
which we rescinded the review, as ``POSCO C&C., Ltd.''
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ See Certain Cold-Rolled Steel Flat Products From Republic of 
Korea: Preliminary Results and Rescission, in Part, of 
Countervailing Duty Administrative Review; 2023, 91 FR 10795 (March 
5, 2026) (Preliminary Results).
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

Correction

    In the Federal Register of March 5, 2026, in FR Doc 2026-04328, on 
page 10797, in the second column, correct the name ``POSCO C&C., Ltd.'' 
to be ``POSCO C&C Co., Ltd.''

Notification to Interested Parties

    This correction of preliminary results is issued and published in 
accordance with sections 751(a)(1) and 777(i)(1) of the Tariff Act of 
1930, as amended, and 19 CFR 351.221(b)(4).
