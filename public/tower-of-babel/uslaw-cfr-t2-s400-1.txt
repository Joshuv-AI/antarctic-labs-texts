2 C.F.R. § 400.1 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:2:400.1
Creator: Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-400.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 400 (Uniform Administrative Requirements, Cost Principles, And Audit Requirements For Federal Awards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part adopts the OMB guidance in subparts A through F of 2 CFR part 200, as supplemented by this chapter, as USDA policies and procedures for uniform administrative requirements, cost principles, and audit requirements for Federal awards. It thereby gives regulatory effect for the USDA to the OMB guidance, as supplemented by this chapter.
