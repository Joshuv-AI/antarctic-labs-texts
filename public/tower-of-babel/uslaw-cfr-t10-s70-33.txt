10 C.F.R. § 70.33 — Applications for renewal of licenses.
Citation: us:cfr:10:70.33
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-70.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 70 (Domestic Licensing Of Special Nuclear Material). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applications for renewal of a license should be filed in accordance with §§ 70.21 and 70.22. Information contained in previous applications, statements or reports filed with the Commission under the license may be incorporated by reference, provided that such references are clear and specific.
