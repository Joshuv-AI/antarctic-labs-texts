7 C.F.R. § 766.57 — Borrower acceptance of Disaster Set-Aside.
Citation: us:cfr:7:766.57
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-766.57
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 766 (Direct Loan Servicing—Special). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The borrower must execute the appropriate Agency documents within 45 days after the borrower receives notification of Agency approval of DSA.
