20 C.F.R. § 355.8 — Service of complaint.

Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-355.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 355 (Regulations Under The Administrative False Claims Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Service of a complaint must be made by certified or registered mail or by delivery in any manner authorized by Rule 4(d) of the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure.
(b) Proof of service, stating the name and address of the person on whom the complaint was served, and the manner and date of service, may be made by—
(1) Affidavit of the individual making service;
(2) An acknowledged U.S. Postal Service return receipt card; or
(3) Written acknowledgment of the respondent.
