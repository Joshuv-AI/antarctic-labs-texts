5 C.F.R. § 1600.18 — Separate service member and civilian contributions.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1600.18
Creator: Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1600.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1600 (Employee Contribution Elections, Investment Elections, And Automatic Enrollment Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The TSP record keeper maintains uniformed services accounts separately from civilian accounts. Therefore, a participant who has made contributions as a uniformed service member and as a civilian employee will have two TSP accounts: A uniformed services account and a civilian account.
