10 U.S.C. § 12404 — Army and Air National Guard of the United States: mobilization; maintenance of organization
Citation: us:usc:10:12404
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section12404
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel Generally); Chapter 1211 (National Guard Members In Federal Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

During an initial mobilization, the organization of a unit of the Army National Guard of the United States or of the Air National Guard of the United States ordered into active Federal service shall, so far as practicable, be maintained as it existed on the date of the order to duty.
