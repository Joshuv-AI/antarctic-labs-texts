23 C.F.R. § 636.505 — Must a contracting agency establish a competitive range if it intends to have discussions with offerors?
Citation: us:cfr:23:636.505
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-636.505
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 636 (Design-Build Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, if discussions are held, they must be conducted with all offerors in the competitive range. If you wish to hold discussions and do not formally establish a competitive range, then you must hold discussions with all responsive offerors.
