16 U.S.C. § 521g — Road rights-of-way subject to State or local law
Citation: us:usc:16:521g
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section521g
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 2 (National Forests); Subchapter I (Establishment And Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Conveyance of any road rights-of-way under sections 521c to 521i of this title shall not be construed as permitting any designation, maintenance, or use of such rights-of-way for road or other purposes except to the extent permitted by State or local law and under conditions imposed by such law.
