33 C.F.R. § 183.554 — Fittings, joints, and connections.
Citation: us:cfr:33:183.554
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-183.554
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 183 (Boats And Associated Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each fuel system fitting, joint, and connection must be arranged so that it can be reached for inspection, removal, or maintenance without removal of permanent boat structure.
