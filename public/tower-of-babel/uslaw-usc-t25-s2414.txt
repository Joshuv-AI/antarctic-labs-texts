25 U.S.C. § 2414 — Congressional intent in construction of laws
Citation: us:usc:25:2414
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section2414
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 26 (Indian Alcohol And Substance Abuse Prevention And Treatment); Subchapter II (Coordination Of Resources And Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the intent of Congress that—
(1)
specific Federal laws, and administrative regulations promulgated thereunder, establishing programs of the Bureau of Indian Affairs, the Indian Health Service, and other Federal agencies, and
(2)
general Federal laws, including laws limiting augmentation of Federal appropriations or encouraging joint or cooperative funding,
shall be liberally construed and administered to achieve the purposes of this chapter.
