14 C.F.R. § 302.217 — Exceptions to administrative law judge's initial or recommended decision.
Citation: us:cfr:14:302.217
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-302.217
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 302 (Rules Of Practice In Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Within seven (7) days after service of any initial or recommended decision of an administrative law judge, any party may file exceptions to the decision with the DOT decisionmaker.
(b) If timely and adequate exceptions are filed, review of the initial or recommended decision is automatic.
(c) In all other respects, the provisions of § 302.34 shall apply.
