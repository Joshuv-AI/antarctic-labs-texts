10 U.S.C. § 8374 — Treatment of fractions of years of service in computing retired pay and separation pay
Citation: us:usc:10:8374
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8374
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 843 (Involuntary Retirement, Separation, And Furlough). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In determining the total number of years of service to be used as a multiplier in computing retired pay and separation pay on discharge under this chapter, each full month of service that is in addition to the number of full years of service creditable to a member is counted as one-twelfth of a year and any remaining fractional part of a month is disregarded.
