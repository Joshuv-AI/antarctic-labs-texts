21 C.F.R. § 884.1300 — Uterotubal carbon dioxide insufflator and accessories.
Citation: us:cfr:21:884.1300

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-884.1300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 884 (Obstetrical And Gynecological Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. A uterotubal carbon dioxide insufflator and accessories is a device used to test the patency (lack of obstruction) of the fallopian tubes by pressurizing the uterus and fallopian tubes and filling them with carbon dioxide gas.
(b) Classification. Class II (performance standards).
