42 C.F.R. § 64a.101 — a.101 Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:42:64a.101
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-64a.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 64a (PART 64a—OBLIGATED SERVICE FOR MENTAL HEALTH TRAINEESHIPS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part establishes requirements to implement the service payback obligation of individuals who receive clinical traineeships in pyschology, psychiatry, social work, or nursing (that are not of limited duration or experimental nature) under section 303 of the Public Health Service Act.
