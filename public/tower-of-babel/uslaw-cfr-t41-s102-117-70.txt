41 C.F.R. § 102-117.70 — -117.70 Property description.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-117.70
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-117.70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-117 (117—Transportation Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Describe property in enough detail for the TSP to determine the type of equipment or any special precautions necessary to move the shipment including, the cargo's declared value, weight, volume, measurements, routing, hazardous cargo, or special handling designations.
