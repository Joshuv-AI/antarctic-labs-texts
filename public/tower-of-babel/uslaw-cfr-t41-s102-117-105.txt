41 C.F.R. § 102-117.105 — -117.105 TSP performance expectations.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-117.105
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-117.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-117 (117—Transportation Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies are required to ensure that TSPs deliver consistent, satisfactory service that meets their transportation needs. At a minimum, monitor—
(a) On-time delivery rates;
(b) Accuracy of billing, including overcharges or undercharges;
(c) Frequency of claims filed;
(d) Shipment rejection rates; and
(e) Responsiveness to shipment tracing requests.
