15 U.S.C. § 715 — Purpose of chapter
Citation: us:usc:15:715
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section715
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 15A (Interstate Transportation Of Petroleum Products). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is declared to be the policy of Congress to protect interstate and foreign commerce from the diversion and obstruction of, and the burden and harmful effect upon, such commerce caused by contraband oil as herein defined, and to encourage the conservation of deposits of crude oil situated within the United States.
