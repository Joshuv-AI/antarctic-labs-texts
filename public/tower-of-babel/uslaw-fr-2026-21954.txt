SUMMARY: The Food and Drug Administration (FDA or Agency) is announcing 
the availability of a draft document titled ``Individualized Human 
Genome Editing Products: Considerations for BLA Submission and 
Postapproval Management.'' The draft guidance document provides 
recommendations for how an individualized human genome editing (GE) 
product that includes multiple product variants can be submitted in a 
single Biologics License Application (BLA). When finalized, this 
guidance will describe the FDA's current thinking on individualized GE 
products with respect to how to specify the extent of GE component 
variation in a BLA, how to add new product variants to the BLA after 
licensure, and related topics.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Background

    FDA is announcing the availability of a draft document titled 
``Individualized Human Genome Editing Products: Considerations for BLA 
Submission and Postapproval Management.'' The draft guidance document, 
intended for sponsors and applicants developing individualized GE 
products, provides recommendations for how an individualized GE product 
that includes multiple product variants can be submitted in a single 
BLA under section 351(a) of the Public Health Service Act (PHS Act) (42 
U.S.C. 262(a)). When finalized, this guidance will describe FDA's 
current thinking regarding how to specify the extent of GE component 
variation in a BLA and how to add new product variants to the BLA after 
licensure. This guidance also addresses related topics, including 
potency and safety evaluation of product variants, manufacturing 
process validation, labeling and indication statements, and drug naming 
and identification. This guidance is intended to complement existing 
FDA guidances on GE products addressing investigational new drug (IND)-
stage chemistry, manufacturing, and controls (CMC); nonclinical and 
clinical trial design; evidentiary standards for approval of 
individualized therapies; and leveraging of CMC, nonclinical, and 
clinical knowledge across GE programs 1 2 3 with additional 
advice and considerations on how to prepare and submit BLAs and BLA 
supplements for individualized GE products.

    This draft guidance is being issued consistent with FDA's good 
guidance practices regulation (21 CFR 10.115). The draft guidance, when 
finalized, will represent the current thinking of FDA on 
``Individualized Human Genome Editing Products: Considerations for BLA 
Submission and Postapproval Management.'' It does not establish any 
rights for any person and is not binding on FDA or the public. You can 
use an alternative approach if it satisfies the requirements of the 
applicable statutes and regulations.
    As we develop final guidance on this topic, FDA will consider 
comments on costs or cost savings the guidance may generate, relevant 
for Executive Order 14192.

Act of 1995 (PRA) (44 U.S.C. 3501-3521). The collections of information 
in 21 CFR parts 210 and 211 pertaining to manufacturing, processing, 
packing, and holding of finished pharmaceuticals and biological 
products for human use (CGMP regulations) have been approved under OMB 
control number 0910-0139. The collections of information in 21 CFR
