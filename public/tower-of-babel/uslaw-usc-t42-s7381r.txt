42 U.S.C. § 7381r — Mentoring program
Citation: us:usc:42:7381r
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section7381r
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 84 (Department Of Energy); Subchapter XIII (Department Of Energy Science Education Programs); Part B (Science, Engineering, And Mathematics Education Programs); Subpart 6 (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
As part of the programs established under subparts 3 and 4, the Director shall establish a program to recruit and provide mentors for women and underrepresented minorities who are interested in careers in science, engineering, and mathematics.
(b) Pairing
The program shall pair mentors with women and minorities who are in programs of study at specialty schools for science and mathematics, Centers of Excellence, and summer institutes established under subparts 3 and 4, respectively.
(c) Program evaluation
The Secretary shall annually—
(1)
use metrics to evaluate the success of the programs established under subsection (a); and
(2)
submit to Congress a report that describes the results of each evaluation.
