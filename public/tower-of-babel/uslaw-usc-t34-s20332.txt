34 U.S.C. § 20332 — Grants for juvenile and family court personnel
Citation: us:usc:34:20332
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section20332
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 203 (Victims Of Child Abuse); Subchapter III (Child Abuse Training Programs For Judicial Personnel And Practitioners). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In order to improve the judicial system’s handling of child abuse and neglect cases, the Administrator of the Office of Juvenile Justice and Delinquency Prevention shall make grants for the purpose of providing—
(1)
technical assistance and training to judicial personnel and attorneys, particularly personnel and practitioners in juvenile and family courts; and
(2)
administrative reform in juvenile and family courts.
