39 C.F.R. § 3050.12 — Obsolescence of special studies relied on to produce the Postal Service's annual periodic reports to the Commission.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3050.12
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3050.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3050 (Periodic Reporting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Postal Service shall provide a list of special studies whose results are used to produce the estimates in its annual periodic reports to the Commission. It shall indicate the date the study was completed and whether the study reflects current operating conditions and procedures. The Postal Service shall update the list annually.
