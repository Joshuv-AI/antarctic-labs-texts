30 U.S.C. § 644 — Advice and assistance by Government departments and agencies; expenditure of funds
Citation: us:usc:30:644
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section644
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 17 (Exploration Program For Discovery Of Minerals). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Departments and agencies of the Government are authorized to advise and assist the Secretary of the Interior, upon his request, in carrying out the provisions of this chapter and may expend their funds for such purposes, with or without reimbursement, in accordance with such agreements as may be necessary.
