25 C.F.R. § 140.25 — Trade in antiquities prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:25:140.25
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-140.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 140 (Licensed Indian Traders). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Traders shall not deal in objects of antiquity removed from any historic or prehistoric ruin or monument on land owned or controlled by the United States.
