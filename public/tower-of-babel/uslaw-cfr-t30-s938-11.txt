30 C.F.R. § 938.11 — Conditions of State regulatory program approval.
Citation: us:cfr:30:938.11
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-938.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 938 (Pennsylvania). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The approval of the Pennsylvania state program is subject to the Commonwealth revising its program to correct the deficiencies listed in this section. The program revisions may be made, as appropriate, to the statutes, the regulations, the program narrative, or the Attorney General's opinion. This section indicates, for the general guidance of the Commonwealth, the component of the program to which the Secretary recommends the change be made.
