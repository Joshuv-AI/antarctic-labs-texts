43 C.F.R. § 3203.12 — What fees must I pay to nominate lands?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3203.12
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3203.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3200 (Geothermal Resource Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Submit with your nomination a filing fee for nominations of lands as found in the fee schedule in § 3000.12 of this chapter.
