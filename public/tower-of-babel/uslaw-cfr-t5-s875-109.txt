5 C.F.R. § 875.109 — Which board of contract appeals has jurisdiction for resolving contract disputes between OPM and the Carrier?
Citation: us:cfr:5:875.109
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-875.109
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 875 (Federal Long Term Care Insurance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of applying chapter 71 of title 41 to disputes arising between OPM and the Carrier, the Civilian Board of Contract Appeals has jurisdiction to decide an appeal relative to such a dispute.
