25 C.F.R. § 23.116 — What happens after a petition for transfer is made?
Citation: us:cfr:25:23.116
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-23.116
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 23 (Indian Child Welfare Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon receipt of a transfer petition, the State court must ensure that the Tribal court is promptly notified in writing of the transfer petition. This notification may request a timely response regarding whether the Tribal court wishes to decline the transfer.
