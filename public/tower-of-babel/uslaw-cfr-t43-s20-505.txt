43 C.F.R. § 20.505 — Habitual use of intoxicants.
Citation: us:cfr:43:20.505
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-20.505
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 20 (Employee Responsibilities And Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee who habitually uses intoxicants to excess may be subject to removal (5 U.S.C. 7352).
