31 C.F.R. § 501.712 — Acknowledgment of hearing request.
Citation: us:cfr:31:501.712
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-501.712
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 501 (Reporting, Procedures And Penalties Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No later than 60 days after service of any hearing request, the Director shall acknowledge receipt and inform a respondent, in writing, whether an Order Instituting Proceedings shall be issued.
