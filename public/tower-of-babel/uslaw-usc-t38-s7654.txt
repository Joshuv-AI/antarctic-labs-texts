38 U.S.C. § 7654 — Obligated service
Citation: us:usc:38:7654
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section7654
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part V (Boards, Administrations, And Services); Chapter 76 (Health Professionals Educational Assistance Program); Subchapter V (Stipend Program For Members Of The Selected Reserve). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A person receiving assistance under this subchapter shall provide service in the full-time clinical practice of the person’s profession as a full-time employee of the Department for the period of obligated service provided in the agreement of such person entered into under section 7603 of this title.
