16 U.S.C. § 470mm — Surveying of lands; reporting of violations
Citation: us:usc:16:470mm
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section470mm
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1B (Archaeological Resources Protection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretaries of the Interior, Agriculture, and Defense and the Chairman of the Board of the Tennessee Valley Authority shall—
(a)
develop plans for surveying lands under their control to determine the nature and extent of archeological resources on those lands;
(b)
prepare a schedule for surveying lands that are likely to contain the most scientifically valuable archeological resources; and
(c)
develop documents for the reporting of suspected violations of this chapter and establish when and how those documents are to be completed by officers, employees, and agents of their respective agencies.
