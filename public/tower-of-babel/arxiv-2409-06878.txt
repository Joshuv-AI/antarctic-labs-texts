Deformed homogeneous polynomials and the deformed q q -exponential operator
Ronald Orozco López

Abstract
In this paper, we introduce the deformed homogeneous polynomials R n ​ ( x , y ; u | q ) \mathrm{R}_{n}(x,y;u|q) . These polynomials generalize some classical polynomials: the Rogers-Szegö polynomials h n ​ ( x | q ) \mathrm{h}_{n}(x|q) , the generalized Rogers-Szegö polynomials r n ​ ( x , y ) \mathrm{r}_{n}(x,y) , the Stieltjes-Wigert polynomials S n ​ ( x , q ) \mathrm{S}_{n}(x;q) , among others. Basic properties of the polynomial R n \mathrm{R}_{n} are given, along with recurrence relations, its q q -difference equation, and representations. Generating functions for the polynomials R n ​ ( x , y ; u | q ) \mathrm{R}_{n}(x,y;u|q) are given. These functions include generalizations of the Mehler and Rogers formulas. In addition, generalizations of the q q -binomial formula and the Heine transformation formula are obtained. These results are obtained via the u u -deformed q q -exponential operator E ⁡ ( y ​ D q | u ) \mathrm{E}(yD_{q}|u) , defined here. From this operator, we obtain for free the operators T ⁡ ( y ​ D q ) \opT(yD_{q}) the Chen, R ⁡ ( y ​ D q ) \mathrm{R}(yD_{q}) of Saad, ℰ ⁡ ( y ​ D q ) \mathcal{E}(yD_{q}) of Exton, and ℛ ⁡ ( y ​ D q ) \mathcal{R}(yD_{q}) of Rogers-Ramanujan when u = 1 , q , q , q 2 u=1,q,\sqrt{q},q^{2} , respectively. We introduce the deformed basic hypergeometric series Φ s r {}_{r}\Phi_{s} , a generalization of the classical basic hypergeometric series. New transformation formulas for basic hypergeometric series are obtained.

Introduction

We begin with some notation and terminology for basic hypergeometric series
[
1
]
. The
q
-shifted factorial is defined by

\displaystyle(a;q)_{n}

\displaystyle=\begin{cases}1&\text{ if }n=0;\\
\prod_{k=0}^{n-1}(1-q^{k}a),&\text{ if }n\neq 0,\\
\end{cases}\hskip 28.45274ptq\in\mathbb{C},

\displaystyle(a;q)_{\infty}

\displaystyle=\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}(a;q)_{n}=\prod_{k=0}^{\infty}(1-aq^{k}),\hskip 28.45274pt|q|<1.

The multiple
q
-shifted factorials are defined by

\displaystyle(a_{1},a_{2},\ldots,a_{m};q)_{n}

\displaystyle=(a_{1};q)_{n}(a_{2};q)_{n}\cdots(a_{m};q)_{n},\hskip 8.5359ptq\in\mathbb{C},

\displaystyle(a_{1},a_{2},\ldots,a_{m};q)_{\infty}

\displaystyle=(a_{1};q)_{\infty}(a_{2};q)_{\infty}\cdots(a_{m};q)_{\infty},\hskip 8.5359pt|q|<1.

Some useful identities for
q
-shifted factorial:

\displaystyle(a;q)_{n}

\displaystyle=\frac{(a;q)_{\infty}}{(aq^{n};q)_{\infty}},\ aq^{n}\neq q^{-k},\ k=1,2,3,\ldots,

(1)

\displaystyle(a;q)_{n+k}

\displaystyle=(a;q)_{n}(aq^{n};q)_{k},

(2)

\displaystyle(a;q)_{2n}

\displaystyle=(a;q^{2})_{n}(aq;q^{2})_{n},

(3)

\displaystyle(a^{2};q^{2})_{n}

\displaystyle=(a;q)_{n}(-a;q)_{n}.

(4)

In our work, we will use the identities for binomial coefficients:

\displaystyle\binom{n+k}{2}

\displaystyle=\binom{n}{2}+\binom{k}{2}+nk,

\displaystyle\binom{n-k}{2}

\displaystyle=\binom{n}{2}+\binom{k}{2}+k(1-n).

The
q
-binomial coefficient is defined by

\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}=\frac{(q;q)_{n}}{(q;q)_{k}(q;q)_{n-k}}.

The
q
-binomial coefficient satisfies

\displaystyle\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n+1}{k}_{q}

\displaystyle=\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}+q^{n+1-k}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k-1}_{q}=q^{k}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}+\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k-1}_{q},

(5)

\displaystyle\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}

\displaystyle=\frac{(q^{-n};q)_{k}}{(q;q)_{k}}(-q^{n})^{k}q^{-\binom{k}{2}}.

The
{}_{r}\phi_{s}
basic hypergeometric series is defined by

{}_{r}\phi_{s}\left(\begin{array}[]{c}a_{1},a_{2},\ldots,a_{r}\\
b_{1},b_{2},\ldots,b_{s}\end{array};q,z\right)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(a_{1},a_{2},\ldots,a_{r};q)_{n}}{(q;q)_{n}(b_{1},b_{2},\ldots,b_{s};q)_{n}}\Big[(-1)^{n}q^{\binom{n}{2}}\Big]^{1+s-r}z^{n}.

In this paper, we will frequently use the
q
-binomial theorem:

{}_{1}\phi_{0}\left(\begin{array}[]{c}a\\
-\end{array};q,-z\right)=\frac{(az;q)_{\infty}}{(z;q)_{\infty}}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(a;q)_{n}}{(q;q)_{n}}z^{n}.

(6)

The

-exponential

\ope_{q}(z)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{z^{n}}{(q;q)_{n}}={}_{1}\phi_{0}\left(\begin{array}[]{c}0\\

For all

Some deformed

\displaystyle\ope_{q}(z,1)

\displaystyle=e_{q}(z)=\frac{1}{(z;q)_{\infty}},\ |z|<1,

and the Exton polynomials

\displaystyle\opE_{n}(x,y;q)

\displaystyle=\opR_{n}(x,y;1,\sqrt{q}|q)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}\sqrt{q}^{\binom{k}{2}}x^{n-k}y^{k}

(45)

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{\sqrt{q}}\frac{(-\sqrt{q};\sqrt{q})_{n}}{(-\sqrt{q};\sqrt{q})_{k}(-\sqrt{q};\sqrt{q})_{n-k}}\sqrt{q}^{\binom{n}{2}}x^{n-k}y^{k}.

(46)

As we will see below, the Stieltjes-Wigert and Exton polynomials are related to the Rogers-Ramanujan and Exton
q
-exponential functions, respectively. As

\opR_{n}(x,y;u,v|q)=u^{\binom{n}{2}}\opR_{n}(x,u^{1-n}y;1,uv|q),

(47)

then we will deal with
u
-deformed homogeneous polynomials

\opR_{n}(x,y;u|q)\equiv\opR_{n}(x,y;1,u|q).

(48)

4.2
Recurrence relations

Theorem 4.2
.

The polynomials
\opR_{n}(x,y;u|q)
satisfy the following recursion relations

\displaystyle\opR_{n+1}(x,y;u|q)

\displaystyle=x\opR_{n}(x,qy;u|q)+y\opR_{n}(x,uy;u|q),

(49)

\displaystyle\opR_{n+1}(x,y;u|q)

\displaystyle=x\opR_{n}(x,y;u|q)+y\opR_{n}(qx,uy;u|q).

(50)

Proof 4.3
.

By Eq. (
48
),

\displaystyle x\opR_{n}(x,qy;u|q)+y\opR_{n}(x,uy;u|q)

\displaystyle\hskip 28.45274pt=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}q^{k}u^{\binom{k}{2}}x^{n+1-k}y^{k}+\sum_{k=0}^{n}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}u^{\binom{k+1}{2}}x^{n-k}y^{k+1}

\displaystyle\hskip 28.45274pt=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}q^{k}u^{\binom{k}{2}}x^{n+1-k}y^{k}+\sum_{k=1}^{n+1}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k-1}_{q}u^{\binom{k}{2}}x^{n+1-k}y^{k}

\displaystyle\hskip 28.45274pt=x^{n+1}+u^{\binom{n+1}{2}}y^{n+1}+\sum_{k=1}^{n}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}q^{k}u^{\binom{k}{2}}x^{n+1-k}y^{k}+\sum_{k=1}^{n}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k-1}_{q}u^{\binom{k}{2}}x^{n+1-k}y^{k}.

Now, by using Eq. (
5
) we have,

\displaystyle x\opR_{n}(x,qy;u|q)+y\opR_{n}(x,uy;u|q)

\displaystyle\hskip 28.45274pt=x^{n+1}+u^{\binom{n+1}{2}}y^{n+1}+\sum_{k=1}^{n}\bigg\{\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}q^{k}+\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k-1}_{q}\bigg\}u^{\binom{k}{2}}x^{n+1-k}y^{k}

\displaystyle\hskip 28.45274pt=x^{n+1}+u^{\binom{n+1}{2}}y^{n+1}+\sum_{k=1}^{n}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n+1}{k}_{q}u^{\binom{k}{2}}x^{n+1-k}y^{k}

\displaystyle\hskip 28.45274pt=\sum_{k=0}^{n+1}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n+1}{k}_{q}u^{\binom{k}{2}}x^{n+1-k}y^{k}

\displaystyle\hskip 28.45274pt=\opR_{n+1}(x,y;u|q).

The proof is reached.

By iterating the above theorem, we have the following result.

Theorem 4.4
.

For
m\geq 0
,

\opR_{n+m}(x,y;u|q)=\sum_{k=0}^{m}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{m}{k}_{q}u^{\binom{k}{2}}x^{m-k}y^{k}\opR_{n}(x,q^{m-k}u^{k}y;u|q).

(51)

4.3
Deformed q-exponential function as limit of deformed homogeneous polynomials

Theorem 4.5
.

If
0<|q|<1
, then

\displaystyle\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\opR_{n}(1,x;u|q)

\displaystyle=\ope_{q}(x,u).

(52)

Proof 4.6
.

As

\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}=\frac{1}{(q;q)_{k}},

then

\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\opR_{n}(1,x;u|q)=\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\sum_{k=0}^{n}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}u^{\binom{k}{2}}x^{k}=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}u^{\binom{k}{2}}\frac{x^{k}}{(q;q)_{k}}=\ope_{q}(x,u).

4.5
The q-difference equation

Theorem 4.9
.

The polynomials
y(x)=\opR_{n}(1,x;u|q)
are solutions of the proportional functional difference equation

(D_{q}y)(u^{-1}x)+q^{n-1}x(D_{q}y)(q^{-1}x)=(1-q^{n})y(x),

(54)

with initial value

.

Proof 4.10
.

As

then the left side of Eq. (
54
) is equal to

\displaystyle(D_{q}y)(u^{-1}x)+q^{n-1}x(D_{q}y)(q^{-1}x)

\displaystyle\hskip 28.45274pt=(1-q^{n})\opR_{n-1}(1,x;u|q)+q^{n-1}(1-q^{n})x\opR_{n-1}(1,uq^{-1}x;u|q)

\displaystyle\hskip 28.45274pt=(1-q^{n})\bigg(\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n-1}{k}_{q}u^{\binom{k}{2}}x^{k}+\sum_{k=1}^{n}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n-1}{k-1}_{q}u^{\binom{k}{2}}q^{n-k}x^{k}\bigg)

\displaystyle\hskip 28.45274pt=(1-q^{n})\sum_{k=0}^{n}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}u^{\binom{k}{2}}x^{k}=(1-q^{n})\opR_{n}(1,x;u|q).

Eq. (
54

f(u^{-1}x)-f(qu^{-1}x)+q^{n}u^{-1}xf(q^{-1}x)-u^{-1}xf(x)=0.

(55)
