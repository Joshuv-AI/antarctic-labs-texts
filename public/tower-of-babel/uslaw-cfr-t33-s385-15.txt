33 C.F.R. § 385.15 — Consistency with requirements of the State of Florida.
Citation: us:cfr:33:385.15
Creator: Corps Of Engineers, Department Of The Army, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-385.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 385 (Programmatic Regulations For The Comprehensive Everglades Restoration Plan). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The State of Florida has established procedures, requirements, and approvals that are needed before the State or the South Florida Water Management District can participate as the non-Federal sponsor for projects of the Plan. Project Implementation Reports shall include such information and analyses, consistent with this part, as are necessary to facilitate review and approval of projects by the South Florida Water Management District and the State pursuant to the requirements of Florida law.
