10 C.F.R. § 60.7 — License not required for certain preliminary activities.
Citation: us:cfr:10:60.7
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-60.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 60 (Disposal Of High-Level Radioactive Wastes In Geologic Repositories). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirement for a license set forth in § 60.3(a) of this part is not applicable to the extent that DOE receives and possesses source, special nuclear, and byproduct material at a geologic repository:
(a) For purposes of site characterization; or
(b) For use, during site characterization or construction, as components of radiographic, radiation monitoring, or similar equipment or instrumentation.
