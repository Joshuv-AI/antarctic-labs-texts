24 C.F.R. § 310.1 — Bylaws of the Association.
Citation: us:cfr:24:310.1
Creator: Government National Mortgage Association, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-310.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 310 (Bylaws Of The Government National Mortgage Association). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The bylaws of the Association shall be duly adopted by the Secretary of Housing and Urban Development pursuant to section 308 of the National Housing Act (12 U.S.C. 1723) and shall govern the performance of the powers and duties granted to or imposed upon the Association by law.
