50 U.S.C. § 1548 — Separability
Citation: us:usc:50:1548
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section1548
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 33 (War Powers Resolution). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this chapter or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of the chapter and the application of such provision to any other person or circumstance shall not be affected thereby.
