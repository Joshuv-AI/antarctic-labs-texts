21 C.F.R. § 1302.06 — Sealing of controlled substances.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1302.06
Creator: Drug Enforcement Administration, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1302.06
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1302 (Labeling And Packaging Requirements For Controlled Substances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

On each bottle, multiple dose vial, or other commercial container of any controlled substance, there shall be securely affixed to the stopper, cap, lid, covering, or wrapper or such container a seal to disclose upon inspection any tampering or opening of the container.
