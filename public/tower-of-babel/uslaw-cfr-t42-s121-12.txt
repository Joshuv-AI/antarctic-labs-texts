42 C.F.R. § 121.12 — Advisory Committee on Organ Transplantation.
Citation: us:cfr:42:121.12
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-121.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 121 (Organ Procurement And Transplantation Network). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary will establish, consistent with the Federal Advisory Committee Act, the Advisory Committee on Organ Transplantation. The Secretary may seek the comments of the Advisory Committee on proposed OPTN policies and such other matters as the Secretary determines.
