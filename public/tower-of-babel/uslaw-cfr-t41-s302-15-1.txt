41 C.F.R. § 302-15.1 — -15.1 Purpose of property management services.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-15.1
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-15.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-15 (15—Allowance For Property Management Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

“Property management services” are programs provided by private companies for a fee, which help an employee to manage a residence at the old official station as a rental property.
