10 C.F.R. § 1040.89-1 — -1 General responsibilities.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1040.89-1
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1040.89-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1040 (Nondiscrimination In Federally Assisted Programs Or Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each DOE recipient has primary responsibility to ensure that its programs or activities are in compliance with the Act and these regulations. A recipient also has responsibility to maintain records, provide information, and afford access to its records to DOE, to the extent required to determine whether it is in compliance with the Act and these regulations.
