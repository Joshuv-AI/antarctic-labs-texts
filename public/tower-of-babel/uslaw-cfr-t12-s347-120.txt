12 C.F.R. § 347.120 — Computation of investment amounts.
Citation: us:cfr:12:347.120
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-347.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 347 (International Banking). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In computing the amount that may be invested in any foreign organization under §§ 347.117 through 347.119, any investments held by an affiliate of a bank must be included.
