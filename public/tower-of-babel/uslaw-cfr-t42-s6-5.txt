42 C.F.R. § 6.5 — Deeming process for eligible entities.
Citation: us:cfr:42:6.5
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-6.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 6 (Federal Tort Claims Act Coverage Of Certain Grantees And Individuals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Eligible entities will be covered by this part only on and after the effective date of a determination by the Secretary that they meet the requirements of section 224(h) of the Act. In making such determination, the Secretary will receive such assurances and conduct such investigations as he or she deems necessary.
