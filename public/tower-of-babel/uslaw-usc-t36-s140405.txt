Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1404 (Military Officers Association Of America). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation has only those powers provided in its bylaws and articles of incorporation filed in each State in which it is incorporated.
