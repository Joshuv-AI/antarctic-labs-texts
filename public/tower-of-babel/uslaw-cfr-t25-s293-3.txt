25 C.F.R. § 293.3 — What authority does the Secretary have to approve or disapprove compacts and amendments?
Citation: us:cfr:25:293.3
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-293.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 293 (Class Iii Tribal-State Gaming Compacts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary has the authority to approve a compact or amendment “entered into” by a Tribe and a State under IGRA. See § 293.15 for the Secretary's authority to disapprove compacts or amendments.
