46 C.F.R. § 385.1 — Scope.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 385 (Research And Development Grant And Cooperative Agreements Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth information about the Maritime Administration (MarAd) assistance regulations: Their purpose, authority, applicability, issuance, arrangement, implementation, and exception procedure; definitions of terms; and general MarAd assistance policies.
