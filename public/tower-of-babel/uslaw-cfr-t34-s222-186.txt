34 C.F.R. § 222.186 — What additional information must be included in a modernization grant application?
Citation: us:cfr:34:222.186
Creator: Office Of Elementary And Secondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-222.186
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 222 (Impact Aid Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to the information specified in § 222.184, an application for a modernization grant must contain a description of—
(a) The need for modernization; and
(b) How the applicant will use funds received under this program to address the need referenced in paragraph (a) of this section.
