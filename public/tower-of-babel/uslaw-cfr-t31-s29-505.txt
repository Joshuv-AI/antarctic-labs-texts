31 C.F.R. § 29.505 — Compromise of debts; termination and suspension of collection actions.
Citation: us:cfr:31:29.505
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-29.505
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 29 (Federal Benefit Payments Under Certain District Of Columbia Retirement Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The procedures for compromise of a claim for an overpayment or the termination or suspension of a collection action seeking to recover an overpayment, other than waiver of an overpayment under §§ 29.521 through 29.526, are controlled exclusively by the FCCS and 31 CFR part 5.
