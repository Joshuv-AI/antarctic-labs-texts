5 C.F.R. § 1820.13 — General prohibition.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1820.13
Creator: Office Of Special Counsel
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1820.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1820 (Freedom Of Information Act Requests; Production Of Records Or Testimony). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No OSC employee may testify or produce official records or information in response to a demand or request without the General Counsel's prior written approval.
