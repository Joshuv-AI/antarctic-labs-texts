Consistent 𝒩 = 4 \mathcal{N}=4 , D = 4 D=4 truncation of type IIB supergravity on S 1 × S 5 \textrm{S}^{1}\times\textrm{S}^{5}
Adolfo Guarino, Colin Sterckx, Mario Trigiante

Abstract
Fetching techniques from Generalised Geometry and Exceptional Field Theory, we develop a new method to identify consistent subsectors of four-dimensional gauged maximal supergravities that possess a (locally) geometric embedding in type IIB or 11D supergravity. We show that a subsector that is invariant under a structure group G S ⊂ E 7 ​ ( 7 ) \textrm{G}_{\textrm{S}}\subset\textrm{E}_{7(7)} can define a consistent truncation, even when G S \textrm{G}_{\textrm{S}} is not part of the symmetry of the gauged maximal supergravity. As an illustration of the method, type IIB supergravity on S 1 × S 5 \textrm{S}^{1}\times\textrm{S}^{5} is shown to admit a consistent truncation to pure 𝒩 = 4 \mathcal{N}=4 , D = 4 D=4 gauged supergravity. Explicit uplift formulae are presented which provide a type IIB alternative to the M-theory embedding constructed by Cvetic, Lu and Pope 25 25 years ago.
