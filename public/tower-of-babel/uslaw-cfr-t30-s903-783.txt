30 C.F.R. § 903.783 — Underground mining permit applications—Minimum requirements for information on environmental resources.

Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-903.783
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 903 (Arizona). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Part 783 of this chapter, Underground Mining Permit Applications—Minimum Requirements for Information on Environmental Resources, applies to any person who submits an application to conduct underground coal mining operations.
(b) Each permit application must include a map that delineates existing vegetative types and a description of the plant communities within the proposed permit area and within any proposed reference area.
