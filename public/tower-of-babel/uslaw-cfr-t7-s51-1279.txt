7 C.F.R. § 51.1279 — Seriously misshapen.
Citation: us:cfr:7:51.1279
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-51.1279
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 51 (Fresh Fruits, Vegetables, And Other Products (Inspection, Certification, And Standards)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Seriously misshapen means that the pear is excessively flattened or elongated for the variety, or is constricted or deformed so it will not cut three fairly uniform good quarters, or is so badly misshapen that the appearance is seriously affected.
