31 C.F.R. § 516.307 — Government of Cuba.
Citation: us:cfr:31:516.307
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-516.307
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 516 (Cuba Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term Government of Cuba means the Government of Cuba, any political subdivision, agency, or instrumentality thereof, including the Central Bank of Cuba, and any person owned, controlled, or acting for or on behalf of, the Government of Cuba.
