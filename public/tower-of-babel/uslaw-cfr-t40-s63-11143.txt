40 C.F.R. § 63.11143 — What General Provisions apply to this subpart?
Citation: us:cfr:40:63.11143
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-63.11143
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 63 (National Emission Standards For Hazardous Air Pollutants For Source Categories). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) All the provisions in part 61, subpart A of this chapter apply to this subpart.
(b) The provisions in subpart A of this part, applicable to this subpart are specified in paragraphs (b)(1) and (2) of this section.
(1) § 63.1(a)(1) through (10).
(2) § 63.1(b) except paragraph (b)(3), §§ 63.1(c) and 63.1(e).
(c) Section 63.11885 specifies which parts of the General Provisions in subpart A of this part apply to you.
