14 C.F.R. § 161.109 — Notice of termination of restriction pursuant to an agreement.
Citation: us:cfr:14:161.109
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-161.109
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 161 (Notice And Approval Of Airport Noise And Access Restrictions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An airport operator must notify the FAA within 10 days of the date of termination of a restriction pursuant to an agreement under this subpart.
