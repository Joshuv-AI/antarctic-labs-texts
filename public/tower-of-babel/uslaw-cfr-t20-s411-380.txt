20 C.F.R. § 411.380 — What does a State VR agency do if the State VR agency wants to determine whether a person seeking services has a ticket?
Citation: us:cfr:20:411.380
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-411.380
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 411 (The Ticket To Work And Self-Sufficiency Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A State VR agency can contact the Program Manager (PM) to determine if a person seeking VR services has a ticket and, if so, whether the ticket may be assigned to the State VR agency (see § 411.140) or reassigned to the State VR agency (see § 411.150). (See § 411.115(k) for a definition of the PM.)
