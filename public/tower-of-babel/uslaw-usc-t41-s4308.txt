41 U.S.C. § 4308 — Penalties for submission of cost known to be unallowable
Citation: us:usc:41:4308
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title41-section4308
Context: United States Code, Title 41 (Public Contracts); Chapter 43 (Allowable Costs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The submission to an executive agency of a proposal for settlement of costs for any period after those costs have been accrued that includes a cost that is expressly specified by statute or regulation as being unallowable, with the knowledge that the cost is unallowable, is subject to section 287 of title 18 and section 3729 of title 31.
