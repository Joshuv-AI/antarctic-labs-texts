29 C.F.R. § 1926.1418 — Authority to stop operation.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1926.1418
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1926.1418
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1926 (Safety And Health Regulations For Construction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever there is a concern as to safety, the operator must have the authority to stop and refuse to handle loads until a qualified person has determined that safety has been assured.
