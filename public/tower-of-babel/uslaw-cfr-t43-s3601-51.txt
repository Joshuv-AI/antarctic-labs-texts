43 C.F.R. § 3601.51 — How will BLM inspect my operation?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3601.51
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3601.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3600 (Mineral Materials Disposal). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must allow BLM access at any reasonable time:
(a) To inspect or investigate the mine condition;
(b) To conduct surveys;
(c) To estimate the volume, types, and composition of commodities that you mine or remove;
(d) To examine weight tickets, truck logs, and other records that BLM finds necessary to verify production; or
(e) To determine whether you comply with contract, permit, statutory, or regulatory requirements.
