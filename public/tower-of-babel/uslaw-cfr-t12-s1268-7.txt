12 C.F.R. § 1268.7 — Reporting requirements for acquired member assets.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1268.7
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1268.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1268 (Acquired Member Assets). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each Bank shall report information related to AMA in accordance with the instructions provided in the Data Reporting Manual issued by FHFA, as amended from time to time.
