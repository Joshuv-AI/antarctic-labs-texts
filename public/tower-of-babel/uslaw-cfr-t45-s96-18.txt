45 C.F.R. § 96.18 — Participation by faith-based organizations.
Citation: us:cfr:45:96.18
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-96.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 96 (Block Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The funds provided under this part shall be administered in compliance with the standards set forth in part 87 (Equal Treatment for Faith-based Organizations) of this chapter.
