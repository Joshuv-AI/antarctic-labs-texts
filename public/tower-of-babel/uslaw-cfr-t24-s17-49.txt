24 C.F.R. § 17.49 — Attorney's fees.
Citation: us:cfr:24:17.49
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-17.49
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 17 (Administrative Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No more than 10 per centum of the amount paid in settlement of each individual claim submitted and settled under this subpart shall be paid or delivered to or received by any agent or attorney on account of services rendered in connection with that claim.
