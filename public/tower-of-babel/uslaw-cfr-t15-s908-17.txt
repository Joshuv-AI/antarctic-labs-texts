15 C.F.R. § 908.17 — Suspension or waiver of rules.
Citation: us:cfr:15:908.17
Creator: National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-908.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 908 (Maintaining Records And Submitting Reports On Weather Modification Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In an extraordinary situation, any requirement of these rules may be suspended or waived by the Administrator on request of the interested party, to the extent such waiver is consistent with the provisions of Public Law 92-205 and subject to such other requirements as may be imposed.
