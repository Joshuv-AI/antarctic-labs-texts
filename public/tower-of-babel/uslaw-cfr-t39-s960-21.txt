39 C.F.R. § 960.21 — Judicial review.
Citation: us:cfr:39:960.21
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-960.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 960 (Equal Access To Justice Act In Postal Service Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A party other than the Postal Service may, within 30 days after a determination on the award is made, appeal the determination to the court of the United States having jurisdiction to review the merits of the underlying decision of the agency adversary adjudication in accordance with 5 U.S.C. 504(c)(2).
