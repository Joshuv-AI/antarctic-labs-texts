15 U.S.C. § 8711 — Amendments to orders
Citation: us:usc:15:8711
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section8711
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 113 (Concrete Masonry Products Research, Education, And Promotion). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of this chapter applicable to the order shall be applicable to any amendment to the order, except that section 8707 of this title shall not apply to an amendment.
