22 U.S.C. § 9635 — Coordination with other development agencies
Citation: us:usc:22:9635
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section9635
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 103 (Better Utilization Of Investments Leading To Development); Subchapter III (Administrative And General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the sense of Congress that the Corporation should use relevant data of the Department of State, the Millennium Challenge Corporation, the United States Agency for International Development, and other departments and agencies that have development functions to better inform the decisions of the Corporation with respect to providing support under subchapter II of this chapter.
