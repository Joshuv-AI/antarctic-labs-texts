23 C.F.R. § 669.13 — Effect of failure to certify or to adequately obtain proof-of-payment.
Citation: us:cfr:23:669.13
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-669.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 669 (Enforcement Of Heavy Vehicle Use Tax). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a State fails to certify as required by this regulation or if the Secretary of Transportation determines that a State is not adequately obtaining proof-of-payment of the heavy vehicle use tax as a condition of registration notwithstanding the State's certification, Federal-aid highway funds apportioned to the State under 23 U.S.C. 104(b)(1) for the next fiscal year shall be reduced in an amount up to 8 percent as determined by the Secretary.
