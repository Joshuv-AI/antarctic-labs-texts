14 C.F.R. § 300.9 — Disqualification of partners of DOT employees.
Citation: us:cfr:14:300.9
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-300.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 300 (Rules Of Conduct In Dot Proceedings Under This Chapter). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No partner of a DOT employee shall act as agent or attorney for anyone other than the United States in any DOT proceeding or matter in which such employee participates or has participated personally and substantially through decision, approval, disapproval, recommendation, rendering advice, investigation, or otherwise, or which is the subject of his or her official responsibility.
