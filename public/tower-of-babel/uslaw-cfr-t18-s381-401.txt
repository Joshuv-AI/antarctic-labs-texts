18 C.F.R. § 381.401 — Review of jurisdictional agency determinations.
Citation: us:cfr:18:381.401
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-381.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 381 (Fees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The fee established for review of a jurisdictional agency determination is $115. The fee must be submitted in accordance with subpart A of this part and § 270.301(c) of this chapter.
