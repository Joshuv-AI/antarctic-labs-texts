Fisheries of the Exclusive Economic Zone Off Alaska; Full 
Coverage Trawl Electronic Monitoring Fee Notice

AGENCY: National Marine Fisheries Service (NMFS), National Oceanic and 
Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), Commerce.

ACTION: Notification of fee percentage for vessels participating in the 
full coverage category of the Trawl Electronic Monitoring Program.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: NMFS publishes this notice to announce the 2025 fee percentage 
of 0.12 percent applicable to participants in the full coverage 
category of the Trawl Electronic Monitoring Program (Trawl EM Program). 
NMFS used this fee percentage to calculate fee liabilities for the 2025 
fishing season. Annually, NMFS issues invoices to vessel owners subject 
to the fee. Fees for the 2025 fishing season are due to NMFS on or 
before May 31, 2026.

DATES: The fee percentage is valid on May 20, 2026.

ADDRESSES: Additional information about the Trawl EM Program is 
available on NMFS Alaska Region's website at https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/alaska/resources-fishing/electronic-Monitoring-alaska.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: For general questions about this fee, 
contact Amy Hadfield at (907) 586-7376. For questions about the fee 
billing process, contact Tristan Mandeville at (907) 586-7231.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Background

    On July 29, 2024, NMFS published a final rule to implement the 
Trawl EM Program for pelagic trawl pollock catcher vessels and tender 
vessels delivering to shoreside processors and stationary floating 
processors in the Bering Sea, Aleutian Islands, and Gulf of Alaska (89 
FR 60796). Under the Trawl EM Program regulations at 50 CFR 679.51(g), 
a vessel owner or operator of a catcher vessel or tender vessel can 
request to be placed in the Trawl EM Program each year and, therein, to 
use an EM system in place of an observer in most cases. Placement in 
the Trawl EM Program reduces overall monitoring costs for fishery 
participants and increases data quality necessary for fishery 
management. A vessel placed in the Trawl EM Program remains in the

[[Page 29464]]

Trawl EM Program for that calendar year. NMFS can choose to disallow 
participation in the Trawl EM Program for reasons such as compliance 
issues or non-payment of fees.
    Vessels in the Trawl EM Program are either in the partial coverage 
category or the full coverage category based on their assigned level of 
observer coverage. Only participants in the full coverage category are 
subject to the trawl EM fee announced in this notice. Participants in 
the partial coverage category pay their fee through the North Pacific 
Observer Program fee as calculated from the standard prices published 
in the Federal Register on December 30, 2025 (90 FR 61117).
    Pursuant to section 313 of the Magnuson-Stevens Fishery 
Conservation and Management Act, NMFS established a process for 
calculating and administering the trawl EM fee for the full coverage 
category as specified at Sec.  679.56. Activities directly related to 
NMFS's administration of the trawl EM full coverage category are funded 
by the trawl EM fee. These activities include EM data collection, EM 
data review, vessel monitoring plan (VMP) approval, and trawl EM 
category data. The fee liability is based on the ex-vessel value of 
pollock harvested by vessels in the full coverage trawl EM category. 
This notice announces the fee percentage in the Federal Register. The 
Regional Administrator sends invoices to vessel owners and each vessel 
owner is responsible for the payment of the trawl EM fee no later than 
May 31 of the fishing year following the year in which pollock landings 
occurred. The total dollar amount of the fee due is determined by 
multiplying the trawl EM fee percentage by the annual ex-vessel value 
of pelagic trawl pollock landings under the full coverage category of 
the Trawl EM Program as described in this notice.

Standard Price

    For purposes of calculating the trawl EM fee percentage, NMFS uses 
a standard ex-vessel price (standard price) for pollock. A standard 
price is determined under Sec.  679.66(b) using information on landings 
purchased (volume) and ex-vessel value paid (value). NMFS annually 
receives information used to calculate the pollock standard price in 
the existing First Wholesale Volume and Value Report, which is 
submitted in early November of each year. NMFS published the standard 
price of $0.15 per pound for pollock for 2025 in the Federal Register 
on December 5, 2025 (90 FR 56134).

Fee Percentage

    Annually, NMFS calculates the total costs directly related to the 
EM data collection, EM data review, VMP approval, and trawl EM category 
data maintenance (direct program costs). For 2025, direct program costs 
were tracked from January 1, 2025 through December 31, 2025. A more 
detailed explanation will be provided in the North Pacific Observer 
Program Annual report, which will be published in June of 2026.
    NMFS calculates the applicable fee percentage according to the 
factors described at Sec.  679.56(c)(2). NMFS determined the fee 
percentage which applies to full coverage trawl EM landings made in 
2025 by dividing the direct program costs by the value of the catch 
subject to the trawl EM fee.
    Under the fee percentage formula described generally above, the 
percentage of direct program costs to fishery value for the 2025 
calendar year is 0.12 percent. For 2025, NMFS applied the calculated 
trawl EM fee percentage to all pollock landings in the full coverage 
trawl EM category made between January 1 and December 31 to calculate 
the trawl EM fee liability for each vessel owner in the full coverage 
trawl EM category. A Trawl EM Program vessel owner's 2025 fee payment 
must be submitted to NMFS on or before May 31, 2026. Payment must be 
made in accordance with the payment methods set forth at Sec.  
679.56(a)(4)(ii).
