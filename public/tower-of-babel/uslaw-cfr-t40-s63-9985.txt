40 C.F.R. § 63.9985 — What is a new EGU?
Citation: us:cfr:40:63.9985
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-63.9985
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 63 (National Emission Standards For Hazardous Air Pollutants For Source Categories). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A new EGU is an EGU that meets any of the criteria specified in paragraph (a)(1) through (a)(2) of this section.
(1) An EGU that commenced construction after May 3, 2011.
(2) An EGU that commenced reconstruction after May 3, 2011.
