7 U.S.C. § 3506 — Public inspection
Citation: us:usc:7:3506
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section3506
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 66 (Agricultural Foreign Investment Disclosure). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any report submitted to the Secretary under section 3501 of this title shall be available for public inspection at the Department of Agriculture located in the District of Columbia not later than 10 days after the date on which such report is received by the Secretary.
