7 C.F.R. § 501.6 — Gambling.
Citation: us:cfr:7:501.6
Creator: Agricultural Research Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-501.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 501 (Conduct On U.S. Meat Animal Research Center, Clay Center, Nebraska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Participating in games for money or other personal property, or the operation of gambling devices, the conduct of a lottery or pool, or the selling or purchasing of numbers tickets, in or on Research Center property, is prohibited.
