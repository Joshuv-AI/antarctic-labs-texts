10 C.F.R. § 602.13 — Limitation of DOE liability.
Citation: us:cfr:10:602.13
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-602.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 602 (Epidemiology And Other Health Studies Financial Assistance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Awards made under this part are subject to the requirement that the maximum DOE obligation to the recipient is the amount shown in the Notice of Financial Assistance Award as the amount of DOE funds obligated. DOE shall not be obligated to make any additional, supplemental, continuation, renewal, or other award for the same or any other purpose.
