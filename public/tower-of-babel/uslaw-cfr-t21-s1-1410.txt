21 C.F.R. § 1.1410 — When will FDA consider whether to waive a requirement of this subpart?
Citation: us:cfr:21:1.1410
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1.1410
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1 (General Enforcement Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

FDA will consider whether to waive a requirement of this subpart on our own initiative or in response to the following:
(a) A written request for a waiver for an individual entity; or
(b) A citizen petition requesting a waiver for a type of entity submitted under § 10.30 of this chapter by any person subject to the requirements of this subpart.
