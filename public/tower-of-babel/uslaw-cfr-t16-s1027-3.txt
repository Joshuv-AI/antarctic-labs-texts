16 C.F.R. § 1027.3 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1027.3
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1027.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1027 (Salary Offset). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) These regulations are to be followed when:
(1) The Commission is owed a debt by an individual who is a current employee of the CPSC; or
(2) The Commission is owed a debt by an individual currently employed by another Federal agency; or
(3) The Commission employs an individual who owes a debt to another federal agency.
