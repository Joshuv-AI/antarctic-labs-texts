43 U.S.C. § 2430 — Full faith and credit
Citation: us:usc:43:2430
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section2430
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 42 (Rural Water Supply); Subchapter II (Twenty-First Century Water Works). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The full faith and credit of the United States is pledged to the payment of all guarantees issued under this section with respect to principal and interest.
