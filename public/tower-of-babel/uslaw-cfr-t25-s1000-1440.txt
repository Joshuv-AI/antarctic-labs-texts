25 C.F.R. § 1000.1440 — What constitutes a “significant change” in the original scope of work?
Citation: us:cfr:25:1000.1440
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary, Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-1000.1440
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 1000 (Annual Funding Agreements Under The Tribal Self-Government Act Amendments To The Indian Self-Determination And Education Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A significant change in the original scope of work is:
(a) A change that would result in a cost that exceeds the total of the Federal project funds available and the Tribe's/Consortium's contingency funds; or
(b) A material departure from the original scope of work, including substantial departure from timelines negotiated in the construction project agreement.
