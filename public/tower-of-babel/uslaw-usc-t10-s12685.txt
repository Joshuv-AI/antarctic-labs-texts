10 U.S.C. § 12685 — Reserves separated for cause: character of discharge
Citation: us:usc:10:12685
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section12685
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel Generally); Chapter 1221 (Separation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A member of a reserve component who is separated for cause, except under section 12684 of this title, is entitled to a discharge under honorable conditions unless—
(1)
the member is discharged under conditions other than honorable under an approved sentence of a court-martial or under the approved findings of a board of officers convened by an authority designated by the Secretary concerned; or
(2)
the member consents to a discharge under conditions other than honorable with a waiver of proceedings of a court-martial or a board.
