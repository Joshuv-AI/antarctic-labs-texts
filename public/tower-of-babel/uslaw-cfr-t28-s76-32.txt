28 C.F.R. § 76.32 — Hearing room conduct.
Citation: us:cfr:28:76.32
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-76.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 76 (Rules Of Procedure For Assessment Of Civil Penalties For Possession Of Certain Controlled Substances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Proceedings shall be conducted in an orderly manner. The consumption of food or beverage, smoking, or rearranging of courtroom furniture, unless specifically authorized by the Judge, is prohibited.
