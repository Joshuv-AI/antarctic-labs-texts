21 U.S.C. § 970 — Criminal forfeitures
Citation: us:usc:21:970
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section970
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 13 (Drug Abuse Prevention And Control); Subchapter II (Import And Export). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Section 853 of this title, relating to criminal forfeitures, shall apply in every respect to a violation of this subchapter punishable by imprisonment for more than one year.
