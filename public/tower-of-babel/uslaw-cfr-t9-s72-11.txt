9 C.F.R. § 72.11 — Quarantined area; cattle considered infested; requirements for placing in noninfectious pens or premises.
Citation: us:cfr:9:72.11
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-72.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 72 (Bovine Babesiosis). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Cattle of the quarantined area shall be considered infested and shall not be placed in noninfectious pens or premises until after the final inspection or dipping.
