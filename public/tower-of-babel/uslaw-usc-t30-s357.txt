30 U.S.C. § 357 — State or local government rights; taxation
Citation: us:usc:30:357
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section357
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 7 (Lease Of Mineral Deposits Within Acquired Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing contained in this chapter shall be construed to affect the rights of the State or other local authorities to exercise any right which they may have with respect to properties covered by leases issued under this chapter, including the right to levy and collect taxes upon improvements, output of mines, or other rights, property, or assets of any lessee of the United States.
