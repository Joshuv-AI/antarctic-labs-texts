10 U.S.C. § 1123 — Right to wear badges of military societies
Citation: us:usc:10:1123
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1123
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 57 (Decorations And Awards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
A member of the Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, or Space Force who is a member of a military society originally composed of men who served in an armed force of the United States during the Revolutionary War, the War of 1812, the Mexican War, the Civil War, the Spanish-American War, the Philippine Insurrection, or the Chinese Relief Expedition of 1900 may wear, on occasions of ceremony, the distinctive badges adopted by that society.
(b)
A member of the Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, or Space Force who is a member of the Army and Navy Union of the United States may wear, on public occasions of ceremony, the distinctive badges adopted by that society.
