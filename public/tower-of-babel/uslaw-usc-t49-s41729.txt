49 U.S.C. § 41729 — COVID–19 vaccination status
Citation: us:usc:49:41729
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section41729
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Air Commerce And Safety); Subpart ii (Economic Regulation); Chapter 417 (Operations Of Carriers); Subchapter I (Requirements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In General.—
An air carrier (as such term is defined in section 40102) may not deny service to any individual solely based on the vaccination status of the individual with respect to COVID–19.
(b) Rule of Construction.—
Nothing in this section shall be construed to apply to the regulation of intrastate travel, transportation, or movement, including the intrastate transportation of passengers.
