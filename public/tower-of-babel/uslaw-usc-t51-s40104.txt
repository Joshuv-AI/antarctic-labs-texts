51 U.S.C. § 40104 — Cooperation among Mission Directorates
Citation: us:usc:51:40104
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section40104
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 401 (Aeronautics); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Research and development activities performed by the Aeronautics Research Mission Directorate with the primary objective of assisting in the development of a flight project in another Mission Directorate shall be funded by the Mission Directorate seeking assistance.
