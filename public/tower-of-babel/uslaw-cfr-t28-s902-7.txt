28 C.F.R. § 902.7 — Court action.
Citation: us:cfr:28:902.7
Creator: National Crime Prevention And Privacy Compact Council
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-902.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 902 (Dispute Adjudication Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pursuant to Section (c) of Article XI of the Compact, a decision by the Attorney General on an appeal under § 902.6 may be appealed by filing a suit seeking to have the decision reversed in the appropriate district court of the United States.
