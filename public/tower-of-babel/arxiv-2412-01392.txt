Quasilifting of hulls and depth of tensor product of modules
Sutapa Dey, Amit Tripathi

Abstract
. We use a quasilift type construction to obtain some bounds on the depth of the tensor product of certain modules over a local 𝒯 ​ ℰ \mathcal{TE} ring. We recover a result of Celikbas, Sadeghi, and Takahashi for local complete intersection rings. Some general conditions for lifting hulls and approximations are also studied.

13D07, 13C14, 13C15

The aim of this paper is to study the connection between bounds on the depth of tensor products of modules and the vanishing of Tor modules. This area traces its origin to the seminal work of Auslander
[
2
]
, who established that for an unramified regular local ring
R
, if
M\otimes_{R}N
is torsion-free for some non-zero finitely generated modules
M
and
N,
then
M
and
N
are torsion-free,
Tor_{i}^{R}(M,N)=0
for all
i\geq 1,
and further, the depth formula holds i.e.
depth(M\otimes_{R}N)=depth(M)+depth(N)-depth(R).
Later, Lichtenbaum
[
27
]
generalized this result to the case of all regular local rings.

These results were further extended to the case of abstract hypersurface rings by Huneke and Wiegand
[
22
, 2.7]
(see also
[
23
]
). They proved that if
M\otimes_{R}N
is reflexive, and either of these modules has a constant rank, then
Tor_{i}^{R}(M,N)=0
for
i\geq 1
and at least one of the
M
and
N
is reflexive (see
[
14
, 1.3]
for a proof of the last claim). In particular, the depth formula holds. These investigations were continued by Huneke, Jorgensen, and Wiegand
[
20
]
, who used quasilifts to prove certain Tor-vanishing results for codimension
2
and
3
cases under additional assumptions.

Without any assumption on the modules or the ring, even if

is totally reflexive, it is not enough to force Tor-independence; see
[
12
, 3.14]
for a counterexample over a codimension

local complete intersection. In the same paper, Celikbas obtains some Tor vanishing criteria
[
12
, 1.1, 1.2]
.

Recall that a local complete intersection ring is a local ring

whose

Proposition 7
.

Let
R
be a local ring and let
M
and
N
be finitely generated
R
-modules. Assume the following:

(i)

depth(N)\geq CI\mbox{-}dim\ M.

(ii)

for some positive integer

and for each
i\in\{r,\cdots,CI\mbox{-}dim\ M\},
either
depth(Tor_{i}^{R}(M,N))=0
or
Tor_{i}^{R}(M,N)=0.

Then
Tor_{i}^{R}(M,N)=0
for
i\gg 0
if and only if
Tor_{i}^{R}(M,N)=0
for
i\geq r.

Proof.

It is sufficient to prove the forward direction. So we assume that
Tor_{i}^{R}(M,N)=0
for
i\gg 0.
Take a projective hull of
M
(see
1.3
(a)
)

\displaystyle 0\rightarrow M\rightarrow X\rightarrow G\rightarrow 0.

(3.1)

Here
CI\mbox{-}dim\ G=0
and
pd\ X=CI\mbox{-}dim\ M.
We tensor (
3.1
) with
N,
to obtain
Tor_{i}^{R}(G,N)=0
for
i\gg 0,
which by
1.3
(i)
gives
Tor_{i}^{R}(G,N)=0
for
i\geq 1.
This means that
depth(Tor_{i}^{R}(X,N))\in\{0,\infty\}
for
i=r,\cdots,pd\ X.
Set

and
s=pd\ X.
Then
pd\ Y=s-r+1
and by assumption, for each

depth(Tor_{i}^{R}(Y,N))=0
or

Let

be a free resolution of

Consider the complex

For

(a)

(b)

By acyclicity lemma

the complex

Equivalently,
Tor_{i}^{R}(M,N)\cong Tor_{i}^{R}(X,N)=0

∎
