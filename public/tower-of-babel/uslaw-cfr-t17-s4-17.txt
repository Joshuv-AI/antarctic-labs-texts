17 C.F.R. § 4.17 — Severability.
Citation: us:cfr:17:4.17
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-4.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 4 (Commodity Pool Operators And Commodity Trading Advisors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If any provision of this part, or the application thereof to any person or circumstances, is held invalid, such invalidity shall not affect other provisions or application of such provision to other persons or circumstances which can be given effect without the invalid provision or application.
