10 U.S.C. § 9495 — Funds appropriated for the Civil Air Patrol
Citation: us:usc:10:9495
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9495
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training); Chapter 959 (Civil Air Patrol). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Funds appropriated for the Civil Air Patrol shall be available only for the exclusive use of the Civil Air Patrol.
