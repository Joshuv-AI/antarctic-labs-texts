49 C.F.R. § 23.71 — Does a recipient have to change existing concession agreements?
Citation: us:cfr:49:23.71
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-23.71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 23 (Participation Of Disadvantaged Business Enterprise In Airport Concessions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When an extension or option to renew such an agreement is exercised, or when a material amendment is made, you must assess potential for ACDBE participation and may, if permitted by the agreement, use any means authorized by this part to obtain a modified amount of ACDBE participation in the renewed or amended agreement.
