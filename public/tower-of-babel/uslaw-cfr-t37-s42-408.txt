37 C.F.R. § 42.408 — Institution of derivation proceeding.
Citation: us:cfr:37:42.408
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-42.408
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 42 (Trial Practice Before The Patent Trial And Appeal Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An administrative patent judge institutes, and may as necessary reinstitute, the derivation proceeding on behalf of the Director.
(b) Additional derivation proceeding. The petitioner may suggest the addition of a patent or application to the derivation proceeding. The suggestion should make the showings required under § 42.405 and explain why the suggestion could not have been made in the original petition.
