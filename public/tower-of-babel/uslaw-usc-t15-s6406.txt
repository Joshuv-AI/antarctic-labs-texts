15 U.S.C. § 6406 — Compliance
Citation: us:usc:15:6406
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section6406
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 90 (Propane Education And Research). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Council may bring suit in Federal court to compel compliance with an assessment levied by the Council under this chapter. A successful action for compliance under this section may also require payment by the defendant of the costs incurred by the Council in bringing such action.
