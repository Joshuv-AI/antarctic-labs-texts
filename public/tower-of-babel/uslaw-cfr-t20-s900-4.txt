20 C.F.R. § 900.4 — Meetings.
Citation: us:cfr:20:900.4
Creator: Joint Board For The Enrollment Of Actuaries
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-900.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 900 (Statement Of Organization). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Joint Board meets on the call of the Chairman at such times as are necessary in order to consider matters requiring action. Minutes are kept of each meeting by the Secretary.
