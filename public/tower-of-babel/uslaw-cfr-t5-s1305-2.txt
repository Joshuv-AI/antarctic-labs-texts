5 C.F.R. § 1305.2 — Production prohibited unless approved.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1305.2
Creator: Office Of Management And Budget
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1305.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1305 (Release Of Official Information, And Testimony By Omb Personnel As Witnesses, In Litigation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No employee or former employee of OMB shall, in response to a demand of a court or other authority, produce any material contained in the files of OMB, disclose any information relating to materials contained in the files of OMB, or disclose any information or produce any material acquired as part of the performance of the person's official duties, or because of the person's official status, without the prior approval of the General Counsel.
