20 C.F.R. § 362.8 — Investigation of a claim.
Citation: us:cfr:20:362.8
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-362.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 362 (Employees' Personal Property Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Deputy General Counsel or his designee may investigate the circumstances surrounding the theft, loss or damage of an employee's property. In order to complete such investigation, the Deputy General Counsel or his designee may request the submission of information in addition to that described in § 362.7 of this part.
