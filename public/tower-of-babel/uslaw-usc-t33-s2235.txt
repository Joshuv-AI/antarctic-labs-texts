33 U.S.C. § 2235 — Construction in usable increments
Citation: us:usc:33:2235
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section2235
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 36 (Water Resources Development); Subchapter II (Harbor Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any navigation project for a harbor or inland harbor authorized by this subchapter or any other provision of law enacted before, on, or after November 17, 1986, may be constructed in usable increments.
