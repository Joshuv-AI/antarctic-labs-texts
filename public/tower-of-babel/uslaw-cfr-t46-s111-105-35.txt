46 C.F.R. § 111.105-35 — -35 Vessels carrying coal.
Citation: us:cfr:46:111.105-35
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-111.105-35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 111 (Electric Systems—General Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The following are Class II, Division 1, (Zone 20) locations on a vessel that carries coal:
(1) The interior of each coal bin and hold.
(2) Each compartment that has a coal transfer point where coal is transferred, dropped, or dumped.
(3) Each open area within 3 meters (10 ft) of a coal transfer point where coal is dropped or dumped.
(b) Each space that has a coal conveyer on a vessel that carries coal is a Class II, Division 2, (Zone 11 or Y) space.
(c) A space that has a coal conveyer on a vessel that carries coal must have electrical equipment approved for Class II, Division 2, (Zone 22) hazardous locations, except watertight general emergency alarm signals.
