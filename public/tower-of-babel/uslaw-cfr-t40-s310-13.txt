40 C.F.R. § 310.13 — Do I need to notify anyone while the response is underway?
Citation: us:cfr:40:310.13
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-310.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 310 (Reimbursement To Local Governments For Emergency Response To Hazardous Substance Releases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. You should notify EPA, the National Response Center, or use another established response communication channel, but it is not a requirement for reimbursement. Telephone numbers for EPA regional offices and the NRC are in appendix II to this part.
