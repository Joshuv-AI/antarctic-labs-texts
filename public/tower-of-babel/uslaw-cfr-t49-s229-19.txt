49 C.F.R. § 229.19 — Prior waivers.
Citation: us:cfr:49:229.19
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-229.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 229 (Railroad Locomotive Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Waivers from any requirement of this part, issued prior to June 8, 2012, shall terminate on the date specified in the letter granting the waiver. If no date is specified, then the waiver shall automatically terminate on June 8, 2017.
