2 C.F.R. § 1134.500 — Purpose of REP Article V.

Creator: Department Of Defense

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1134 (Financial, Programmatic, And Property Reporting: General Award Terms And Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

REP Article V of the general terms and conditions specifies requirements for recipients to provide any type of report not addressed in REP Articles I-IV.
