48 U.S.C. § 1954 — Public auditor and special prosecutor
Citation: us:usc:48:1954
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1954
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 18 (Micronesia, Marshall Islands, And Palau); Subchapter II (Palau); Part B (Implementation Of Compact). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
1 Upon request of the Government of Palau the President shall provide, on a nonreimbursable basis, appropriate technical assistance to the public auditor or special prosecutor. The assistance provided pursuant to this subsection for the first five years after the effective date of the Compact shall, upon the request of the Government of Palau, and to the extent personnel are available, include (but not be limited to) the full time services of—
(1)
an auditor or accountant, as determined by the public auditor, for the office of public auditor; and
(2)
an attorney or investigator, as determined by the special prosecutor, for the office of special prosecutor.
