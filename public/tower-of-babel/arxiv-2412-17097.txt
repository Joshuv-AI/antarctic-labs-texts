On the extension of one-parameter operator semigroups to completions of Archimedean vector lattices


Abstract
Extensions of one-parameter operator semigroups on Archimedean vector lattices to their order/ru-completions are studied. Existence and uniqueness of the extension to the ru-completion is established in the class of positive semigroups. An extension theorem for positive ru-continuos semigroups on vector lattices with property (R) is proved. This theorem allows one to abandon the requirement of ru-completeness in various results concerning positive ru-continuous semigroups.

constructing a necessary in the Cauchy problem operator

-) semigroups on Banach spaces
is a well investigated area of functional analysis. In the setting of locally convex spaces, the theory of semigroups is also rather developed (it goes back to
works of I. Miyadera, H. Komatsu, K. Singbal-Vedak, T. Komura
[
1
,
2

,

The recent progress in this direction is achieved in works of M. Kandić, M. Kaplin, M. Kramar Fijavž, and J. Glück

5

6

It these works, the relatively uniform topology is considered, that need not to be locally convex (for instance on

In particular, in
[
6
]
a generator (constructed by use of the ru-convergence)
of a semigroup on a relatively uniformly complete vector lattice is investigated. Y. A. Dabboorasad, M. A. A. Marabeh, and the author
in Theorem 5 of
[

established that the ru-convergence in an Archimedean vector lattice is described by a linear topology
if and only if the vector lattice has a strong order unit. Therefore, in the study of a semigroup

It is of a considerable interest to build a generator of the extension of a semigroup which is defined on a dense, in an appropriate sense, sublattice.
In the case of norm convergence, the extension preserving strong continuity is trivial for semigroups which are bounded in the operator norm
in a neighborhood of zero.
In the present paper we consider extendability of a semigroup on an Archimedean vector lattice
to its order/ru- completion and study when such an extension preserves continuity at zero
with respect to the order/ru- convergence.

2
On the extension of a semigroup to the order completion

In order to extend a semigroup
(T_{s})_{s\geq 0}
on
X
to a semigroup
(T_{s}^{\delta})_{s\geq 0}
on
X^{\delta}
,
it is necessary to extend every
T_{s}
to an operator
T_{s}^{\delta}
on
X^{\delta}
. Due to the well known Veksler result
[
14
]
,
it suffices to assume that the operators
T_{s}
are regular and order continuous.

The regularity condition on operators
T_{s}
is not too restrictive, because the order continuity of

T_{s}:X\to X
is equivalent to the order continuity of
T_{s}:X\to X^{\delta}

(as
X
is a regular sublattice of
X^{\delta}
).

Since
X^{\delta}
is Dedekind complete, the operator
T_{s}:X\to X^{\delta}
is regular if and only if

T_{s}:X\to X^{\delta}
is order bounded, which is in turn equivalent to the order boundedness of
T_{s}:X\to X
.

Furthermore, even the order boundedness of
T_{s}:X\to X
can be easily dropped due to the
Abramovich – Sirotkin theorem on automatic order boundedness of order continuous operators
[
15
, Theorem 2.1]
.
Note that the extensions
T_{s}^{\delta}
are unique and order continuous on
X^{\delta}
.
Summarizing, we obtain.

Theorem 1
.

Every semigroup
(T_{s})_{s\geq 0}
of order continuous operators on
X
possesses a unique extension
to the semigroup
\big(T_{s}^{\delta}\big)_{s\geq 0}
of order continuous operators on
X^{\delta}
.

It seems to be important to find supplementary conditions under which
this extension preserves order continuity/ru-continuity of the semigroup
(T_{s})_{s\geq 0}
, namely the following properties.

Definition 1
.

A semigroup
(T_{s})_{s\geq 0}
has the property

oc

if, for every
x\in X
and
s\geq 0
, we have
T_{s+h}x\convo T_{s}x
as
h\to 0
;

oc
0

if, for every
x\in X
we have
T_{h}x\convo x
as
h\downarrow 0
;

ruc

if, for every
x\in X
and
s\geq 0
we have
T_{s+h}x\convr T_{s}x
as
h\to 0
;

ruc
0

if, for every
x\in X
we have
T_{h}x\convr x
as
h\downarrow 0
.

It can be easily seen

\text{\rm\bf ruc}\Longrightarrow\text{\rm\bf ruc}_{0};\ \ \ \text{\rm\bf oc}\Longrightarrow\text{\rm\bf oc}_{0};\ \ \ \text{\rm\bf ruc}\Longrightarrow\text{\rm\bf oc};\ \ \ \text{\rm\bf ruc}_{0}\Longrightarrow\text{\rm\bf oc}_{0}.

We shall return to question on reverse of the first implication in the last section.
