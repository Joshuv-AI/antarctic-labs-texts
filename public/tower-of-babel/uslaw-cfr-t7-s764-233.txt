7 C.F.R. § 764.233 — Limitations.
Citation: us:cfr:7:764.233
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-764.233
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 764 (Direct Loan Making). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The applicant must comply with the general limitations specified in § 764.102 except § 764.102(f), which does not apply to applicants for the CL Program.
(b) The applicant must agree to repay any duplicative financial benefits or assistance to CL.
