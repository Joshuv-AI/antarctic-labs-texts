29 C.F.R. § 20.60 — Application of partial payments to amounts owed.
Citation: us:cfr:29:20.60
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-20.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 20 (Federal Claims Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a debt is paid in partial or installment payments, amounts received by the responsible agency should be applied first to outstanding penalty and administrative cost charges, second to accrued interest, and third to outstanding principal.
