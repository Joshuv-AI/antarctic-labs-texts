14 C.F.R. § 402.1 — Applicability and definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:14:402.1
Creator: Commercial Space Transportation, Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-402.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 402 (General Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) This part applies to any person subject to the requirements in subchapter C of this chapter.
(b) For purposes of this part, “document in any format” includes documents (electronic or physical), and also other tangible items, such as data plates or marked parts.
