45 C.F.R. § 2556.200 — Who may serve as a VISTA?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2556.200
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2556.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2556 (Volunteers In Service To America). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An individual may serve as a VISTA if all the following requirements are met as of the date the individual takes the oath or affirmation, as appropriate, to enter VISTA service:
(a) The individual is at least eighteen years of age. There is no upper age limit.
(b) The individual is a United States citizen or national, or is legally residing within a State.
