43 C.F.R. § 2544.3 — Notice to deposit purchase price.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2544.3
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2544.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2540 (Color-Of-Title And Omitted Lands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If, upon consideration of the application, it shall be determined that the applicant is entitled to purchase the lands applied for, the applicant will be notified, by registered mail, that he must within 6 months from receipt of notice deposit the appraised price of the land or else forfeit all his rights under his application.
