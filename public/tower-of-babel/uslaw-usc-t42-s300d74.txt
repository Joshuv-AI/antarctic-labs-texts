42 U.S.C. § 300d–74 — Rule of construction
Citation: us:usc:42:300d–74
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section300d–74
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter X (Trauma Care); Part G (Poison Control). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this part may be construed to ease any restriction in Federal law applicable to the amount or percentage of funds appropriated to carry out this part that may be used to prepare or submit a report.
