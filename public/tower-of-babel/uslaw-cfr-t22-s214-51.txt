22 C.F.R. § 214.51 — Administrative review of denial for public access to records.
Citation: us:cfr:22:214.51
Creator: Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-214.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 214 (Advisory Committee Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person whose request for access to an advisory committee document is denied may seek administrative review in accordance with § 212.36(c) of A.I.D. Regulation 12, 22 CFR 212.36(c).
