25 C.F.R. § 37.124 — How are boundaries established for a new school or dorm?
Citation: us:cfr:25:37.124
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-37.124
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 37 (Geographic Boundaries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Geographic attendance boundaries for a new day school, on-reservation boarding school, or peripheral dorm must be established by either:
(a) The tribe; or
(b) If the tribe chooses not to establish boundaries, the Secretary.
