36 C.F.R. § 520.15 — Weapons and explosives.
Citation: us:cfr:36:520.15
Creator: Smithsonian Institution
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-520.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 520 (Rules And Regulations Governing The Buildings And Grounds Of The National Zoological Park Of The Smithsonian Institution). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person while on the premises shall carry firearms, other dangerous or deadly weapons, or explosives, either openly or concealed, except for official purposes, nor shall any person discharge or set off any firework or explosive of any nature on the premises.
