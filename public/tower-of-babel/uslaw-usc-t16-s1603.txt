16 U.S.C. § 1603 — National Forest System resource inventories; development, maintenance, and updating by Secretary as part of Assessment
Citation: us:usc:16:1603
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section1603
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 36 (Forest And Rangeland Renewable Resources Planning); Subchapter I (Planning). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As a part of the Assessment, the Secretary shall develop and maintain on a continuing basis a comprehensive and appropriately detailed inventory of all National Forest System lands and renewable resources. This inventory shall be kept current so as to reflect changes in conditions and identify new and emerging resources and values.
