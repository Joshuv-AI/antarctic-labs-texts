1 C.F.R. § 15.10 — Information on drafting and publication.
Citation: us:cfr:1:15.10
Creator: Administrative Committee Of The Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-1/section-15.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 1 (General Provisions), Part 15 (Services To Federal Agencies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-23 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director of the Federal Register may prepare, and distribute to agencies, information and instructions designed to promote effective compliance with the purposes of chapter 15 of title 44, United States Code, sections 552-553 of title 5, United States Code, related statutes, and this chapter. The Director may also develop and conduct programs of technical instruction.
