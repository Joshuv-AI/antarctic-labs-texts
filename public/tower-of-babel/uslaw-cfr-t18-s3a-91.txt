18 C.F.R. § 3a.91 — a.91 Data index system.
Citation: us:cfr:18:3a.91
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-3a.91
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 3a (PART 3a—NATIONAL SECURITY INFORMATION). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A data index system shall be established for Top Secret, Secret, and Confidential information in selected categories prescribed by the Interagency Classification Review Committee, in accordance with section VII of the National Security Council Directive Governing the Classification, Downgrading, Declassification, and Safeguarding of National Security Information, May 17, 1972.
