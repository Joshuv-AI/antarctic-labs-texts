43 U.S.C. § 316a — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:43:316a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section316a
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 8A (Grazing Lands); Subchapter II (Alaska). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this subchapter—
(1)
The term “person” means individual, partnership, corporation, or association.
(2)
The term “district” means any grazing district established under the provisions of section 316b of this title.
(3)
The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of the Interior.
(4)
The term “lessee” means the holder of any lease.
