10 C.F.R. § 140.19 — Failure by licensees to maintain financial protection.
Citation: us:cfr:10:140.19
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-140.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 140 (Financial Protection Requirements And Indemnity Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In any case where the Commission finds that the financial protection maintained by a licensee is not adequate to meet the requirements of this part, the Commission may suspend or revoke the license or may issue such order with respect to licensed activities as the Commission determines to be appropriate or necessary in order to carry out the provisions of this part and of section 170 of the Act.
