41 C.F.R. § 109-1.5148 — -1.5148 Personal property management reports.
Citation: us:cfr:41:109-1.5148
Creator: Chapter 109—Department Of Energy Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-109-1.5148
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 109-1 (1—Introduction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Annual personal property reports as required by 41 CFR 102 35.25 and internal DOE personal property reports must be submitted to the Office of Management at a date determined by the Property Executive.
