31 C.F.R. § 29.503 — Prohibition against collection of debts.
Citation: us:cfr:31:29.503
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-29.503
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 29 (Federal Benefit Payments Under Certain District Of Columbia Retirement Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Debts may be collected from Federal Benefit Payments only to the extent expressly authorized by Federal debt collection statutes and any other applicable Federal law.
(b) When collection of a debt from Federal Benefit Payments is authorized under paragraph (a) of this section, the collection will be made in accordance with this subpart and other applicable federal law.
