10 U.S.C. § 877 — Art. 77. Principals
Citation: us:usc:10:877
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section877
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 47 (Uniform Code Of Military Justice); Subchapter X (Punitive Articles). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person punishable under this chapter who—
(1)
commits an offense punishable by this chapter, or aids, abets, counsels, commands, or procures its commission; or
(2)
causes an act to be done which if directly performed by him would be punishable by this chapter;
is a principal.
