22 C.F.R. § 710.5 — Failure to request hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:22:710.5
Creator: Us International Development Finance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-710.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 710 (Administrative Enforcement Procedures Of Post-Employment Restrictions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The President of DFC may take appropriate action referred to in § 710.13 in the case of any former DFC employee who has failed to make a written request to DFC for a hearing within 30 days after receiving adequate notice.
