46 U.S.C. § 42105 — Disclosure to public
Citation: us:usc:46:42105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section42105
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part B (Actions To Address Foreign Practices); Chapter 421 (Regulations Affecting Shipping In Foreign Trade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the Federal Maritime Commission may refuse to disclose to the public a response or other information submitted to it under this chapter.
