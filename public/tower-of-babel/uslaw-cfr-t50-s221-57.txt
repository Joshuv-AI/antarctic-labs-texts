50 C.F.R. § 221.57 — Who has the burden of persuasion, and what standard of proof applies?
Citation: us:cfr:50:221.57
Creator: National Marine Fisheries Service, National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-221.57
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 221 (Conditions And Prescriptions In Ferc Hydropower Licenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Any party who has filed a request for a hearing has the burden of persuasion with respect to the issues of material fact raised by that party.
(b) The standard of proof is a preponderance of the evidence.
