Dear Dr. Sarita Anil Shinde

We thank you for your precise comments.

In the present study, the subjects are healthy young adults.

Therefore, we did not know in advance whether the subjects in this study had GSD disorders. We will take this into consideration when we continue our research in the future. Thank you very much.

We agree with you on the issue of the metabolic response of the organism when fructose is administered alone. We feel that we must alert the public people and consumers.

On the other hand, the public is sensitive to the word fructose, and many criticize sucrose and isomaltulose because they contain fructose. Since it is difficult to prepare a diet completely devoid of sugar, and some people prefer to eliminate it completely, we engaged in this study because we felt it was necessary to communicate the biological response well.

Also, as you pointed out, judging solely on the biological response up to 4 hours is not sufficient. Therefore, we would like to continue working on this as an ongoing research issue.

We would like to address this point in our next research.

I would like to express my sincere appreciation for your remarks.

Best regards,
