46 C.F.R. § 141.320 — Inflatable survival craft placards.
Citation: us:cfr:46:141.320
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-141.320
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 141 (Lifesaving). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every towing vessel equipped with an inflatable survival craft must have, in conspicuous places near each inflatable survival craft, approved placards or other posted instructions for launching and inflating inflatable survival craft.
