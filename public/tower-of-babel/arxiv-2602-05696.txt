Weak and strong averaging principle for 2D Boussinesq equations with non-Lipschitz Poisson jump noise
Yangyang Shi, Dong Su, Hui Liu

Abstract
In this paper, we study the averaging principle for 2D Boussinesq equations with non-Lipschitz Poisson jump noise. Precisely, we will first explore the well-posedness, regularity estimates and tightness of the vorticity variable. Then, we prove the ergodicity of the temperature variable. Next, we prove that the vorticity variable converge to the solution of the averaged equation in probability and 2 ​ p 2p th-mean, under different conditions, as time scale parameter ε \varepsilon goes to zero. Finally, we present a specific case study and conduct numerical simulations to substantiate the main conclusions of this paper.



Keywords:
Boussinesq equations, averaging principle, Poisson jump noise, non-Lipschitz.

Introduction

In this paper, we refer to the Boussinesq equations on two dimensional torus
\mathbb{T}^{2}=[0,2\pi]\times[0,2\pi]
by

\left\{\begin{aligned} &\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}+(u\cdot\triangledown)u=\triangle u-\triangledown p+\theta e_{2},\\
&\frac{\partial\theta}{\partial t}+(u\cdot\triangledown)\theta=\triangle\theta,\\
&\triangledown\cdot u=0,\\
&u(0,x,y)=u_{0}(x,y),\ \theta(0,x,y)=\theta_{0}(x,y),\end{aligned}\right.

(1.1)

with periodic boundary value condition in space. Where
u(t,x,y)=(u_{1},u_{2})
represents the velocity vector,
p=p(t,x,y)
is the scalar pressure, variable
\theta(t,x,y)
represents the scalar temperature and vector
e_{2}=(0,1)
.

Since the thermal diffusivity determines the rate at which heat is transferred within a fluid, particularly when the Prandtl number (Pr) or Lewis number (Le) of the fluid is significantly less than 1, the thermal diffusivity is substantially greater than both the momentum diffusivity and solute diffusivity
[
1

Applying a curl operator on the first equation of (
1.1
), we shall consider the following two-scale stochastic Boussinesq equations driven by Poisson jump noise

\left\{\begin{aligned} &\frac{\partial j^{\varepsilon}}{\partial t}+(u^{\varepsilon}\cdot\triangledown)j^{\varepsilon}=\triangle j^{\varepsilon}+f(j^{\varepsilon},\theta^{\varepsilon})+\partial_{x}\theta^{\varepsilon}+\int_{\left|z\right|_{H}<1}\sigma_{1}(j^{\varepsilon},\theta^{\varepsilon},z)\dot{\tilde{\eta}}_{1}(t,dz),\\
&\frac{\partial\theta^{\varepsilon}}{\partial t}+\frac{1}{\varepsilon}(u^{\varepsilon}\cdot\triangledown)\theta^{\varepsilon}=\frac{1}{\varepsilon}\triangle\theta^{\varepsilon}+\int_{\left|z\right|_{H}<1}\sigma_{2}(j^{\varepsilon},\theta^{\varepsilon},z)\dot{\tilde{\eta}}_{2}^{\varepsilon}(t,dz),\\
&j^{\varepsilon}(0)=j_{0},\ \theta^{\varepsilon}(0)=\theta_{0},\end{aligned}\right.

(1.2)

where
j^{\varepsilon}:=\triangledown^{\perp}\cdot u^{\varepsilon}=\partial_{x}u^{\varepsilon}_{2}-\partial_{y}u^{\varepsilon}_{1}
is the vorticity, small parameter
0<\varepsilon\ll 1
describing the ratio of the time scale between
j^{\varepsilon}
and
\theta^{\varepsilon}
. The compensated Poisson random measures
\tilde{\eta}_{1}(t,z)=\eta_{1}(t,z)-\nu_{1}(z)\cdot t
and
\tilde{\eta}^{\varepsilon}_{2}(t,z)=\eta_{2}(\frac{1}{\varepsilon}t,z)-\frac{1}{\varepsilon}\nu_{2}(z)\cdot t
on a measurable space
(H,\mathscr{B}(H))
, and intensity measures
\nu_{i}(i=1,2)
defined over the filtered probability space
(\Omega,\mathscr{F},\left\{\mathscr{F}_{t}\right\}_{t\geq 0},\mathbb{P})
.

We give the assumptions for the coefficients in the equation (

Assumption 1
.

f:H\times H\rightarrow H

Assumption 2
.

\sigma_{1}:H\times H\times H\rightarrow H
, for some

, and for any

\displaystyle\int_{\left|z\right|_{H}<1}\left|\sigma_{1}(u_{1},v_{1},z)-\sigma_{1}(u_{2},v_{2},z)\right|^{2p}_{H}\nu_{1}(dz)\leq\kappa\left(\left|u_{1}-u_{2}\right|^{2p}_{H}+\left|v_{1}-v_{2}\right|^{2p}_{H}\right),

\displaystyle\int_{\left|z\right|_{H}<1}\left|\sigma_{1}(u,v,z)\right|^{2p}_{H}\nu_{1}(dz)\leq C_{1}\left(1+\left|u\right|^{2p}_{H}+\left|v\right|^{2p}_{H}\right).

Assumption 3
.

\sigma_{2}:H\times H\times H\rightarrow H
, for some
p\geq 1
, for any

\displaystyle C_{T}\left(1+\left|j_{0}\right|^{2p}_{H}+\left|\theta_{0}\right|^{2p}_{H}\right)+C_{p}\int_{0}^{T}\mathbb{E}\left|j^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2p}_{H}ds+\frac{C_{p}}{\varepsilon}\int_{0}^{T}\int_{0}^{s}e^{-\frac{\lambda_{p,\gamma}}{\varepsilon}(s-\tau)}\mathbb{E}\left|j^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2p}_{H}d\tau ds

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle C_{T}\left(1+\left|j_{0}\right|^{2p}_{H}+\left|\theta_{0}\right|^{2p}_{H}\right)+C_{p}\int_{0}^{T}\mathbb{E}\left|j^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2p}_{H}ds+\frac{C_{p}}{\varepsilon}\int_{0}^{T}\int_{\tau}^{T}e^{-\frac{\lambda_{p,\gamma}}{\varepsilon}(s-\tau)}ds\mathbb{E}\left|j^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2p}_{H}d\tau

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{T}\left(1+\left|j_{0}\right|^{2p}_{H}+\left|\theta_{0}\right|^{2p}_{H}\right)+C_{p}\int_{0}^{T}\mathbb{E}\sup_{r\in[0,s]}\left|j^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2p}_{H}ds.

Using Gronwall’s inequality, we obtain that

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\sup_{t\in[0,T]}\left|j^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2p}_{H}+C_{p}\mathbb{E}\int_{0}^{T}\left|j^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2(p-1)}_{H}\left|\triangledown j^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2}_{H}ds\leq C_{T}\left(1+\left|j_{0}\right|^{2p}_{H}+\left|\theta_{0}\right|^{2p}_{H}\right).

And so

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\left|\theta^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2p}_{H}+\frac{2p\gamma}{\varepsilon}\mathbb{E}\int_{0}^{t}\left|\theta^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2(p-1)}_{H}\left|\triangledown\theta^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2}_{H}ds\leq C_{T}\left(1+\left|j_{0}\right|^{2p}_{H}+\left|\theta_{0}\right|^{2p}_{H}\right).

∎

Remark 3.1
.

In the previous theorem, we have only proved uniform bounds with respect to
\varepsilon
for
\sup_{t\in[0,T]}\mathbb{E}\left|\theta^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2p}
and not for
\mathbb{E}\sup_{t\in[0,T]}\left|\theta^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2p}
. In fact, we can only able to prove that

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\sup_{t\in[0,T]}\left|\theta^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2p}\leqslant C_{T}\left(1+\left|j_{0}\right|^{2p}_{H}+\left|\theta_{0}\right|^{2p}_{H}+\varepsilon^{-1}\right).

(3.5)

Next, we consider the regularity estimates of the vorticity variable
j^{\varepsilon}
. We declare the following theorem:

Theorem 3.3
.

For any
T>0
,
M>0
and
\left(j_{0},\theta_{0}\right)\in H^{1}\times H
, we have

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\sup_{t\in[0,\tau_{M}\wedge T]}\left|j^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2}_{H^{1}}+\mathbb{E}\int_{0}^{\tau_{M}\wedge T}\left|j^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2}_{H^{2}}ds\leqslant C_{T,M}\left(1+\left|j_{0}\right|^{2}_{H^{1}}+\left|\theta_{0}\right|^{2}_{H}\right),

(3.6)

where stopping time
\tau_{M}:=\inf\left\{t\geq 0;\left|j^{\varepsilon}\right|_{H}>M\right\}
.

Proof.

We take the inner product of the first equation in (
1.2
) with
-\triangle j^{\varepsilon}
, yielding

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\frac{d}{dt}\left|\triangledown j^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2}_{H}+\left|\triangle j^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2}_{H}=

\displaystyle\left((u^{\varepsilon}\cdot\triangledown)j^{\varepsilon},\triangle j^{\varepsilon}\right)+\left(f(j^{\varepsilon},\theta^{\varepsilon}),-\triangle j^{\varepsilon}\right)+\left(\partial_{x}\theta^{\varepsilon},-\triangle j^{\varepsilon}\right)

\displaystyle+\int_{\left|z\right|_{H}<1}\left(-\triangle j^{\varepsilon},\sigma_{1}(j^{\varepsilon},\theta^{\varepsilon},z)\dot{\tilde{\eta}}_{1}(t,dz)\right).

Note that

\displaystyle\left((u^{\varepsilon}\cdot\triangledown)j^{\varepsilon},\triangle j^{\varepsilon}\right)=-\left(\triangledown(u^{\varepsilon}\cdot\triangledown)j^{\varepsilon},\triangledown j^{\varepsilon}\right)=-\left((\triangledown u^{\varepsilon}\cdot\triangledown)j^{\varepsilon},\triangledown j^{\varepsilon}\right),

by Ladyzhenskaya’s inequality, we have

\displaystyle\left|\left((u^{\varepsilon}\cdot\triangledown)j^{\varepsilon},\triangle j^{\varepsilon}\right)\right|\leq\left|\triangledown u^{\varepsilon}\right|_{H}\left|\triangledown j^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2}_{L^{4}}\leq\left|j^{\varepsilon}\right|_{H}\left|\triangledown j^{\varepsilon}\right|_{H}\left|\triangle j^{\varepsilon}\right|_{H}\leq C_{\gamma}\left|j^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2}_{H}\left|\triangledown j^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2}_{H}+\gamma\left|\triangle j^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2}_{H}.

Hence

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\frac{d}{dt}\left|\triangledown j^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2}_{H}+\left|\triangle j^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2}_{H}\leq

\displaystyle C_{\gamma}\left|j^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2}_{H}\left|\triangledown j^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2}_{H}+C_{\gamma}\left(1+\left|j^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2}_{H}+\left|\theta^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2}_{H^{1}}\right)+4\gamma\left|\triangle j^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2}_{H}

\displaystyle+C_{\gamma}\int_{\left|z\right|_{H}<1}\left|\sigma_{1}(j^{\varepsilon},\theta^{\varepsilon},z)\right|^{2}_{H}\dot{\tilde{\eta}}_{1}(t,dz).

Choose
\gamma
small enough, and by (
3.1
), (
3.2
), we have

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\sup_{t\in[0,\tau_{M}\wedge T]}\left|\triangledown j^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2}_{H}+\mathbb{E}\int_{0}^{\tau_{M}\wedge T}\left|\triangle j^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2}_{H}ds

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\left|\triangledown j_{0}\right|^{2}_{H}+C_{M}\mathbb{E}\int_{0}^{\tau_{M}\wedge T}\left|\triangledown j^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2}_{H}ds+C\mathbb{E}\int_{0}^{\tau_{M}\wedge T}\left(1+\left|j^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2}_{H}+\left|\theta^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2}_{H^{1}}\right)ds

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{T}\left(1+\left|j_{0}\right|^{2}_{H^{1}}+\left|\theta_{0}\right|^{2}_{H}\right)+C_{M}\mathbb{E}\int_{0}^{\tau_{M}\wedge T}\left|\triangledown j^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2}_{H}ds.

The Gronwall’s inequality yields that

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\sup_{t\in[0,\tau_{M}\wedge T]}\left|\triangledown j^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2}_{H}+\mathbb{E}\int_{0}^{\tau_{M}\wedge T}\left|\triangle j^{\varepsilon}\right|^{2}_{H}ds\leq C_{T,M}\left(1+\left|j_{0}\right|^{2}_{H^{1}}+\left|\theta_{0}\right|^{2}_{H}\right).

∎

In the next, we will show the tightness of the vorticity variable
j^{\varepsilon}
. For this we need the following Lemma:

Lemma 3.1
.

For any
0\leq t-\delta<t\leq\tau_{M}\wedge T
and
\left(j_{0},\theta_{0}\right)\in H^{1}\times H
, we have

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\left|j^{\varepsilon}_{t}-j^{\varepsilon}_{t-\delta}\right|^{2}_{H}\leqslant C_{T,M}\delta^{\frac{1}{2}}.

(3.7)

In addition, for any
(j_{0},\theta_{0})\in H\times H
, and for some
p\geq 1
, we have

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\int_{0}^{\tau_{M}\wedge T}\left|j^{\varepsilon}_{t}-j^{\varepsilon}_{t-\delta}\right|^{2p}_{H}dt\leqslant C_{T,M}\delta^{\frac{1}{2}}.

(3.8)

Proof.

For any
0\leq t-\delta<t\leq\tau_{M}\wedge T
, we have

\displaystyle j^{\varepsilon}_{t}-j^{\varepsilon}_{t-\delta}=

\displaystyle-\int_{t-\delta}^{t}\left(u^{\varepsilon}_{s}\cdot\triangledown\right)j^{\varepsilon}_{s}ds+\int_{t-\delta}^{t}\left(\triangle j^{\varepsilon}_{s}+f(j^{\varepsilon}_{s},\theta^{\varepsilon}_{s})+\partial_{x}\theta^{\varepsilon}_{s}\right)ds

\displaystyle+\int_{t-\delta}^{t}\int_{\left|z\right|_{H}<1}\sigma_{1}(j^{\varepsilon}_{s},\theta^{\varepsilon}_{s},z)\tilde{\eta}_{1}(ds,dz).

By Itô’s formula, we get

\displaystyle\left|j^{\varepsilon}_{t}-j^{\varepsilon}_{t-\delta}\right|^{2}_{H}=

\displaystyle-2\int_{t-\delta}^{t}\left(\left(u^{\varepsilon}_{s}\cdot\triangledown\right)j^{\varepsilon}_{s},j^{\varepsilon}_{s}-j^{\varepsilon}_{t-\delta}\right)ds+2\int_{t-\delta}^{t}\left\langle\triangle j^{\varepsilon}_{s},j^{\varepsilon}_{s}-j^{\varepsilon}_{t-\delta}\right\rangle ds

\displaystyle+2\int_{t-\delta}^{t}\left(f(j^{\varepsilon}_{s},\theta^{\varepsilon}_{s}),j^{\varepsilon}_{s}-j^{\varepsilon}_{t-\delta}\right)ds+2\int_{t-\delta}^{t}\left(\partial_{x}\theta^{\varepsilon}_{s},j^{\varepsilon}_{s}-j^{\varepsilon}_{t-\delta}\right)ds

\displaystyle+2\int_{t-\delta}^{t}\int_{\left|z\right|_{H}<1}\left(\sigma_{1}(j^{\varepsilon}_{s},\theta^{\varepsilon}_{s},z),j^{\varepsilon}_{s}-j^{\varepsilon}_{t-\delta}\right)\tilde{\eta}_{1}(ds,dz)

\displaystyle+\int_{t-\delta}^{t}\int_{\left|z\right|_{H}<1}\left|\sigma_{1}(j^{\varepsilon}_{s},\theta^{\varepsilon}_{s},z)\right|^{2}_{H}\eta_{1}(ds,dz).

(3.9)

Hence

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\left|j^{\varepsilon}_{t}-j^{\varepsilon}_{t-\delta}\right|^{2}_{H}+2\mathbb{E}\int_{t-\delta}^{t}\left|\triangledown j^{\varepsilon}_{s}\right|^{2}_{H}ds

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left\{2\mathbb{E}\int_{t-\delta}^{t}\left(\left(u^{\varepsilon}_{s}\cdot\triangledown\right)j^{\varepsilon}_{s},j^{\varepsilon}_{t-\delta}\right)ds\right\}_{I_{1}}+\left\{2\mathbb{E}\int_{t-\delta}^{t}\left(\triangledown j^{\varepsilon}_{s},\triangledown j^{\varepsilon}_{t-\delta}\right)ds\right\}_{I_{2}}

\displaystyle+\left\{2\mathbb{E}\int_{t-\delta}^{t}\left(f(j^{\varepsilon}_{s},\theta^{\varepsilon}_{s}),j^{\varepsilon}_{s}-j^{\varepsilon}_{t-\delta}\right)ds\right\}_{I_{3}}-\left\{2\mathbb{E}\int_{t-\delta}^{t}\left(\theta^{\varepsilon}_{s},\partial_{x}j^{\varepsilon}_{s}-\partial_{x}j^{\varepsilon}_{t-\delta}\right)ds\right\}_{I_{4}}

\displaystyle+\left\{\mathbb{E}\int_{t-\delta}^{t}\int_{\left|z\right|_{H}<1}\left|\sigma_{1}(j^{\varepsilon}_{s},\theta^{\varepsilon}_{s},z)\right|^{2}_{H}\eta_{1}(ds,dz)\right\}_{I_{5}}.

For
I_{1}
, according to the following useful inequality(cf.
[
43
]
)

\displaystyle\left|\left((u\cdot\triangledown)v,w\right)\right|\leq C\left|u\right|_{H^{s_{1}}}\left|v\right|_{H^{1+s_{2}}}\left|w\right|_{H^{s_{3}}},

(3.10)

where

\displaystyle\left|I_{1}\right|\leq

\displaystyle C\mathbb{E}\int_{t-\delta}^{t}\left|u^{\varepsilon}_{s}\right|_{H^{1}}\left|j^{\varepsilon}_{s}\right|_{H^{1}}\left|j^{\varepsilon}_{t-\delta}\right|_{H^{1}}ds\leq C\mathbb{E}\int_{t-\delta}^{t}\left|j^{\varepsilon}_{s}\right|_{H}\left|j^{\varepsilon}_{s}\right|_{H^{1}}\left|j^{\varepsilon}_{t-\delta}\right|_{H^{1}}ds

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{M}\left(\mathbb{E}\left(\int_{t-\delta}^{t}\left|j^{\varepsilon}_{s}\right|_{H^{1}}ds\right)^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}\cdot\left(\mathbb{E}\left|j^{\varepsilon}_{t-\delta}\right|^{2}_{H^{1}}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{T,M}\delta^{\frac{1}{2}}.

For

\displaystyle+C_{p,T}\mathbb{E}\left(\sup_{t\in[\delta,\tau_{M}\wedge T]}\left|j^{\varepsilon}_{t-\delta}\right|^{2p}_{H}\int_{\delta}^{\tau_{M}\wedge T}\int_{t-\delta}^{t}\int_{\left|z\right|_{H}<1}\left|j^{\varepsilon}_{t-\delta}\right|^{2p-2}_{H}\left|\sigma_{1}(j^{\varepsilon}_{s},\theta^{\varepsilon}_{s},z)\right|^{2}_{H}\eta_{1}(ds,dz)dt\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{p,T}\left(\mathbb{E}\sup_{s\in[0,\tau_{M}\wedge T]}\left|j^{\varepsilon}_{s}\right|^{2p}_{H}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}\left(\mathbb{E}\int_{0}^{\tau_{M}\wedge T}\int_{s}^{s+\delta}\int_{\left|z\right|_{H}<1}\left|j^{\varepsilon}_{s}\right|^{2p-2}_{H}\left|\sigma_{1}(j^{\varepsilon}_{s},\theta^{\varepsilon}_{s},z)\right|^{2}_{H}\eta_{1}(dt,dz)ds\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle+C_{p,T}\left(\mathbb{E}\sup_{t\in[\delta,\tau_{M}\wedge T]}\left|j^{\varepsilon}_{t-\delta}\right|^{2p}_{H}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}\left(\mathbb{E}\int_{\delta}^{\tau_{M}\wedge T}\int_{t-\delta}^{t}\int_{\left|z\right|_{H}<1}\left|j^{\varepsilon}_{t-\delta}\right|^{2p-2}_{H}\left|\sigma_{1}(j^{\varepsilon}_{s},\theta^{\varepsilon}_{s},z)\right|^{2}_{H}\eta_{1}(ds,dz)dt\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{p,T}\left(\mathbb{E}\int_{0}^{\tau_{M}\wedge T}\int_{s}^{s+\delta}\left(1+\left|j^{\varepsilon}_{s}\right|^{2p}_{H}+\left|\theta^{\varepsilon}_{s}\right|^{2p}_{H}\right)dtds\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle+C_{p,T}\left(\mathbb{E}\int_{\delta}^{\tau_{M}\wedge T}\int_{t-\delta}^{t}\left(1+\left|j^{\varepsilon}_{t-\delta}\right|^{2p}_{H}+\left|j^{\varepsilon}_{s}\right|^{2p}_{H}+\left|\theta^{\varepsilon}_{s}\right|^{2p}_{H}\right)dsdt\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{p,T}\delta^{\frac{1}{2}}.

Hence, (
3.8
) hold.
∎

4
Ergodicity of the frozen equation

In this section, we focus on the frozen equation for the second equation of (

). For any initial value
\theta\in H
and fixed

, we consider the following equation:

\left\{\begin{aligned} &\frac{\partial\tilde{\theta}_{t}}{\partial t}+(u\cdot\triangledown)\tilde{\theta}_{t}=\triangle\tilde{\theta}_{t}+\int_{\left|z\right|_{H}<1}\sigma_{2}(j,\tilde{\theta}_{t},z)\dot{\tilde{\eta}}_{2}(t,dz),\\
&\tilde{\theta}_{0}=\theta,\end{aligned}\right.

(4.1)

where

, compensated Poisson random measure
\tilde{\eta}_{2}(t,x)=\eta_{2}(t,x)-\nu_{2}(x)\cdot t
with intensity measure
\nu_{2}
.

It is easy to known that Eq. (
4.1
) has a unique solution
\tilde{\theta}_{t}^{u,\theta}
, and it is easy to check that
\theta_{t}=\tilde{\theta}_{\frac{t}{\varepsilon}}^{u^{\varepsilon},\theta_{0}}
, which is the solution for the second equation of (
1.2
), if we take a time-scale transformation
t\rightarrow\varepsilon t
and

. Let
\left\{P_{t}^{u}\right\}_{t\geqslant 0}
be the Markov transition semigroup of
\tilde{\theta}_{t}^{u,\theta}
, i.e. for any
\varphi\in\mathcal{B}_{b}(H)
,

\displaystyle P_{t}^{u}\varphi(\theta):=\mathbb{E}\varphi(\tilde{\theta}_{t}^{u,\theta}),\quad\theta\in H,\quad t\geqslant 0.

For Eq.(
4.1
), we declare the following lemma:

Lemma 4.1
.

For any
(\theta,u)\in H\times H^{1}
, and for some
p\geq 1
, we have

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{t}\right|^{2p}_{H}+\mathbb{E}\int_{0}^{t}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{s}\right|^{2p-2}_{H}\left|\triangledown\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{s}\right|^{2}_{H}ds\leqslant\left|\theta\right|^{2p}_{H}e^{-\lambda_{p,\gamma}t}+C_{p}\left(1+\left|j\right|^{2p}_{H}\right),

(4.2)

where
\lambda_{p,\gamma}>0
has already appeared in (
3
).
In addition, for any
\theta_{1},\theta_{2}\in H
,

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{1}}_{t}-\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{2}}_{t}\right|^{2p}_{H}\leqslant\left|\theta_{1}-\theta_{2}\right|^{2p}_{H}e^{-\lambda_{p}t}.

(4.3)

Furthermore, for any
u_{1},u_{2}\in H^{1}
,

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{t}-\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{t}\right|^{2p}_{H}\leq

\displaystyle C\int_{0}^{t}e^{-\lambda_{p,\gamma}(t-s)}\mathbb{E}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{s}-\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{s}\right|^{2p-2}_{H}\left|\triangledown\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{s}\right|^{2}_{H}ds\cdot\left|j_{1}-j_{2}\right|^{2}_{H}

\displaystyle+C\kappa\left(\left|j_{1}-j_{2}\right|^{2p}_{H}\right).

(4.4)

Proof.

By Itô’s formula, we have

\displaystyle\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{t}\right|^{2p}_{H}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left|\theta\right|^{2p}_{H}+2p\int_{0}^{t}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{s}\right|^{2(p-1)}_{H}\left\langle\triangle\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{s},\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{s}\right\rangle ds

\displaystyle+2p\int_{0}^{t}\int_{\left|z\right|_{H}<1}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{s}\right|^{2(p-1)}_{H}\left(\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{s},\sigma_{2}(j,\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{s},z)\right)\tilde{\eta}_{2}(ds,dz)

\displaystyle+\int_{0}^{t}\int_{\left|z\right|_{H}<1}\left[\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{s}+\sigma_{2}(j,\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{s},z)\right|^{2p}_{H}-\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{s}\right|^{2p}_{H}-2p\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{s}\right|^{2(p-1)}_{H}\left(\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{s},\sigma_{2}(j,\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{s},z)\right)\right]\eta_{2}(ds,dz).

Thereby, we have

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{t}\right|^{2p}_{H}=

\displaystyle\left|\theta\right|^{2p}_{H}+2p\mathbb{E}\int_{0}^{t}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{s}\right|^{2(p-1)}_{H}\left\langle\triangle\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{s},\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{s}\right\rangle ds+\mathbb{E}\int_{0}^{t}\int_{\left|z\right|_{H}<1}\left[\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{s}+\sigma_{2}(j,\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{s},z)\right|^{2p}_{H}\right.

\displaystyle\left.-\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{s}\right|^{2p}_{H}-2p\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{s}\right|^{2(p-1)}_{H}\left(\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{s},\sigma_{2}(j,\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{s},z)\right)\right]\nu_{2}(dz)ds.

And so

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dt}\mathbb{E}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{t}\right|^{2p}_{H}+2p\gamma\mathbb{E}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{t}\right|^{2p-2}_{H}\left|\triangledown\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{t}\right|^{2}_{H}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle-2p(1-\gamma)\lambda\mathbb{E}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{t}\right|^{2p}_{H}+M_{p}\mathbb{E}\int_{\left|z\right|_{H}<1}\left(\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{t}\right|^{2(p-1)}_{H}\left|\sigma_{2}(j,\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{t},z)\right|^{2}_{H}+\left|\sigma_{2}(j,\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{t},z)\right|^{2p}_{H}\right)\nu_{2}(dz)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle-2p(1-\gamma)\lambda\mathbb{E}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{t}\right|^{2p}_{H}+M_{p}\mathbb{E}\int_{\left|z\right|_{H}<1}\left(\frac{p-1}{p}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{t}\right|^{2p}_{H}+\frac{1}{p}\left|\sigma_{2}(j,\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{t},z)\right|^{2p}_{H}\right)\nu_{2}(dz)

\displaystyle+M_{p}L_{\sigma_{2}}\mathbb{E}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{t}\right|^{2p}_{H}+M_{p}C_{1}\left(1+\left|j\right|^{2p}_{H}\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle-\lambda_{p,\gamma}\mathbb{E}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{t}\right|^{2p}_{H}+M_{p}C_{1}\left(1+\left|j\right|^{2p}_{H}\right).

Using Gronwall’s inequality, we have

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{t}\right|^{2p}_{H}+\mathbb{E}\int_{0}^{t}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{s}\right|^{2p-2}_{H}\left|\triangledown\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta}_{s}\right|^{2}_{H}ds\leqslant\left|\theta\right|^{2p}_{H}e^{-\lambda_{p,\gamma}t}+M_{p}C_{1}\left(1+\left|j\right|^{2p}_{H}\right).

Furthermore, for any
\theta_{1},\theta_{2}\in H
, we have

\displaystyle\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{1}}_{t}-\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{2}}_{t}\right|^{2p}_{H}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left|\theta_{1}-\theta_{2}\right|^{2p}_{H}+2p\int_{0}^{t}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{1}}_{s}-\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{2}}_{s}\right|^{2p-2}_{H}\left\langle\triangle\left(\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{1}}_{s}-\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{2}}_{s}\right),\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{1}}_{s}-\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{2}}_{s}\right\rangle ds

\displaystyle+2p\int_{0}^{t}\int_{\left|z\right|_{H}<1}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{1}}_{s}-\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{2}}_{s}\right|^{2p-2}_{H}\left(\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{1}}_{s}-\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{2}}_{s},\sigma_{2}(j,\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{1}}_{s},z)-\sigma_{2}(j,\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{2}}_{s},z)\right)\tilde{\eta}_{2}(ds,dz)

\displaystyle+\int_{0}^{t}\int_{\left|z\right|_{H}<1}\left[\left|\left(\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{1}}_{s}-\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{2}}_{s}\right)+\left(\sigma_{2}(j,\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{1}}_{s},z)-\sigma_{2}(j,\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{2}}_{s},z)\right)\right|^{2p}_{H}-\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{1}}_{s}-\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{2}}_{s}\right|^{2p}_{H}\right.

\displaystyle\left.-2p\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{1}}_{s}-\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{2}}_{s}\right|^{2p-2}_{H}\left(\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{1}}_{s}-\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{2}}_{s},\sigma_{2}(j,\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{1}}_{s},z)-\sigma_{2}(j,\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{2}}_{s},z)\right)\right]\eta_{2}(ds,dz).

According to Assumptions
3
,
4
and (

), we have

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dt}\mathbb{E}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{1}}_{t}-\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{2}}_{t}\right|^{2p}_{H}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle-2p\lambda\mathbb{E}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{1}}_{t}-\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{2}}_{t}\right|^{2p}_{H}+M_{p}\mathbb{E}\int_{\left|z\right|_{H}<1}\left(\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{1}}_{t}-\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{2}}_{t}\right|^{2p-2}_{H}\left|\sigma_{2}(j,\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{1}}_{t},z)-\sigma_{2}(j,\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{2}}_{t},z)\right|^{2}_{H}\right.

\displaystyle\left.+\left|\sigma_{2}(j,\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{1}}_{t},z)-\sigma_{2}(j,\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{2}}_{t},z)\right|^{2p}_{H}\right)\nu_{2}(dz)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle-\left(2p\lambda-\frac{M_{p}(p-1)}{p}\right)\mathbb{E}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{1}}_{t}-\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{2}}_{t}\right|^{2p}_{H}+\frac{M_{p}(p+1)}{p}\mathbb{E}\int_{\left|z\right|_{H}<1}\left|\sigma_{2}(j,\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{1}}_{t},z)-\sigma_{2}(j,\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{2}}_{t},z)\right|^{2p}_{H}\nu_{2}(dz)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle-\lambda_{p}\mathbb{E}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{1}}_{t}-\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{2}}_{t}\right|^{2p}_{H}.

Gronwall’s inequality implies that

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{1}}_{t}-\tilde{\theta}^{u,\theta_{2}}_{t}\right|^{2p}_{H}\leqslant\left|\theta_{1}-\theta_{2}\right|^{2p}_{H}e^{-\lambda_{p}t}.

Similarly, for any
u_{1},u_{2}\in H^{2}
, we have

\displaystyle\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{t}-\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{t}\right|^{2p}_{H}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-2p\int_{0}^{t}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{s}-\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{s}\right|^{2p-2}_{H}\left((u_{1}\cdot\triangledown)\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{s}-(u_{2}\cdot\triangledown)\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{s},\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{s}-\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{s}\right)ds

\displaystyle+2p\int_{0}^{t}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{s}-\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{s}\right|^{2p-2}_{H}\left\langle\triangle\left(\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{s}-\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{s}\right),\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{s}-\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{s}\right\rangle ds

\displaystyle+2p\int_{0}^{t}\int_{\left|z\right|_{H}<1}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{s}-\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{s}\right|^{2p-2}_{H}\left(\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{s}-\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{s},\sigma_{2}(j_{1},\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{s},z)-\sigma_{2}(j_{2},\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{s},z)\right)\tilde{\eta}_{2}(ds,dz)

\displaystyle+\int_{0}^{t}\int_{\left|z\right|_{H}<1}\left[\left|\left(\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{s}-\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{s}\right)+\left(\sigma_{2}(j_{1},\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{s},z)-\sigma_{2}(j_{2},\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{s},z)\right)\right|^{2p}_{H}-\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{s}-\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{s}\right|^{2p}_{H}\right.

\displaystyle\left.-2p\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{s}-\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{s}\right|^{2p-2}_{H}\left(\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{s}-\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{s},\sigma_{2}(j_{1},\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{s},z)-\sigma_{2}(j_{2},\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{s},z)\right)\right]\eta_{2}(ds,dz).

By (
3.10

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dt}\mathbb{E}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{t}-\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{t}\right|^{2p}_{H}+2p\gamma\mathbb{E}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{t}-\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{t}\right|^{2p-2}_{H}\left|\triangledown\left(\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{t}-\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{t}\right)\right|^{2}_{H}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle-2p(1-\gamma)\lambda\mathbb{E}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{t}-\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{t}\right|^{2p}_{H}+2p\mathbb{E}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{t}-\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{t}\right|^{2p-2}_{H}\left(\left((u_{1}-u_{2})\cdot\triangledown\right)\left(\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{t}-\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{t}\right),\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{t}\right)

\displaystyle+M_{p}\mathbb{E}\int_{\left|z\right|_{H}<1}\left(\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{t}-\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{t}\right|^{2p-2}_{H}\left|\sigma_{2}(j_{1},\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{t},z)-\sigma_{2}(j_{2},\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{t},z)\right|^{2}_{H}\right.

\displaystyle\left.+\left|\sigma_{2}(j_{1},\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{t},z)-\sigma_{2}(j_{2},\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{t},z)\right|^{2p}_{H}\right)\nu_{2}(dz)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle-\left(2p(1-\gamma)-\frac{M(p-1)}{p}\right)\lambda\mathbb{E}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{t}-\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{t}\right|^{2p}_{H}+2p\mathbb{E}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{t}-\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{t}\right|^{2p-2}_{H}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{t}-\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{t}\right|_{H^{1}}

\displaystyle\cdot\left|\triangledown\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{t}\right|_{H}\left|u_{1}-u_{2}\right|_{H^{1}}+\frac{M_{p}(p+1)}{p}\mathbb{E}\int_{\left|z\right|_{H}<1}\left|\sigma_{2}(j_{1},\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{t},z)-\sigma_{2}(j_{1},\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta_{2}}_{t},z)\right|^{2p}_{H}\nu_{2}(dz).

Using Young’s inequality, and for
\gamma
small enough, we have

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dt}\mathbb{E}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{t}-\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{t}\right|^{2p}_{H}\leq

\displaystyle-\left(2p(1-\gamma)-\frac{M(p-1)}{p}-\frac{M_{p}(p+1)L_{\sigma_{2}}}{p}\right)\lambda\mathbb{E}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{t}-\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{t}\right|^{2p}_{H}

\displaystyle+C_{p,\gamma}2p\mathbb{E}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{t}-\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{t}\right|^{2p-2}_{H}\left|\triangledown\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{t}\right|^{2}_{H}\left|u_{1}-u_{2}\right|^{2}_{H^{1}}+\kappa\left(\left|j_{1}-j_{2}\right|^{2p}_{H}\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle-\lambda_{p,\gamma}\mathbb{E}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{t}-\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{t}\right|^{2p}_{H}+C_{p,\gamma}\mathbb{E}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{t}-\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{t}\right|^{2p-2}_{H}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{t}\right|^{2}_{H^{1}}\left|j_{1}-j_{2}\right|^{2}_{H}

\displaystyle+C\kappa\left(\left|j_{1}-j_{2}\right|^{2p}_{H}\right).

By Gronwall’s inequality and (
4.2
), we have

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{t}-\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{t}\right|^{2p}_{H}\leq

\displaystyle C\int_{0}^{t}e^{-\lambda_{p,\gamma}(t-s)}\mathbb{E}\left|\tilde{\theta}^{u_{1},\theta}_{s}-\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{s}\right|^{2p-2}_{H}\left|\triangledown\tilde{\theta}^{u_{2},\theta}_{s}\right|^{2}_{H}ds\cdot\left|j_{1}-j_{2}\right|^{2}_{H}

\displaystyle+C\kappa\left(\left|j_{1}-j_{2}\right|^{2p}_{H}\right).

∎

Next, we will introduce the ergodicity of
\left\{P_{t}^{u}\right\}_{t\geqslant 0}
. We have reached the following conclusion:

Theorem 4.1
.

For any
u\in H^{1}
,
\left\{P_{t}^{u}\right\}_{t\geqslant 0}
has a unique ergodic invariant measure
\pi^{u}(\cdot)
. Moreover,

\displaystyle\int_{H}{\left|\theta\right|^{2p}_{H}\pi^{u}(d\theta)}\leqslant C\left(1+\left|j\right|^{2p}_{H}\right).

(4.5)

Proof.

According to the classical Bogoliubov-Krylov argument, (
4.2
) imples the existence of invariant measure. Moreover, let
\pi^{u}(\cdot)
be an invariant measure of
\left\{P_{t}^{u}\right\}_{t\geqslant 0}
, we have

\displaystyle\int_{H}{\left|\theta\right|^{2p}_{H}\pi^{u}(d\theta)}

\displaystyle=\int_{H}{\mathbb{E}\left|\tilde{\theta}_{t}^{u,\theta}\right|^{2p}_{H}\pi^{u}(d\theta)}

\displaystyle\leq\int_{H}{\left|\theta\right|^{2p}_{H}e^{-\lambda_{p,\gamma}t}\pi^{u}(d\theta)}+C_{p}\left(1+\left|j\right|^{2p}_{H}\right).

Choose
t=-\frac{\ln\frac{1}{2}}{\lambda_{p,\gamma}}
, and (
4.5
) hold.

For the uniqueness of invariant measure, as shown in
[
44
]
, we only need to prove that for any Lipschitz-continuous function
\varphi:H\rightarrow\mathbb{R}
and any invariant measure
\pi^{u}(\cdot)
, the following formula holds:

\displaystyle\left|P_{t}^{u}\varphi(\theta_{1})-\int_{H}{\varphi(\theta_{2})\pi^{u}(d\theta_{2})}\right|\leqslant CL_{\varphi}\left(1+\left|\theta\right|_{H}+\left|j\right|_{H}\right)e^{-\frac{\lambda_{p}}{p}t},

(4.6)

where
L_{\varphi}=\mathop{\sup}\limits_{\theta_{1},\theta_{2}\in H}\frac{\left|\varphi(\theta_{1})-\varphi(\theta_{2})\right|}{\left|\theta_{1}-\theta_{2}\right|_{H}}
.

To this end, by (
4.3
) and (
4.5
), we have

\displaystyle\left|P_{t}^{u}\varphi(\theta_{1})-\int_{H}{\varphi(\theta_{2})\pi^{u}(d\theta_{2})}\right|

\displaystyle=\left|\int_{H}{P_{t}^{u}\varphi(\theta_{1})-P_{t}^{u}\varphi(\theta_{2})\pi^{u}(d\theta_{2})}\right|

\displaystyle\leq\int_{H}{\mathbb{E}\left|\varphi(\tilde{\theta}_{t}^{u,\theta_{1}})-\varphi(\tilde{\theta}_{t}^{u,\theta_{2}})\right|\pi^{u}(d\theta_{2})}

\displaystyle\leq L_{\varphi}\int_{H}{\mathbb{E}\left|\tilde{\theta}_{t}^{u,\theta_{1}}-\tilde{\theta}_{t}^{u,\theta_{2}}\right|_{H}\pi^{u}(d\theta_{2})}

\displaystyle\leq L_{\varphi}e^{-\frac{\lambda_{p}}{p}t}\int_{H}{\left|\theta_{1}-\theta_{2}\right|_{H}\pi^{u}(d\theta_{2})}

\displaystyle\leq CL_{\varphi}\left(1+\left|\theta\right|_{H}+\left|j\right|_{H}\right)e^{-\frac{\lambda_{p}}{p}t}.

∎

5

)

In this section, we introduce the averaged equation for Eq.(
1.2
) and prove the first main result of this paper. Namely, for any
T>0
and
\left(j_{0},\theta_{0}\right)\in H\times H
, the slow variables
j^{\varepsilon}
converges to the solution
\bar{j}
of the following averaged equations:

\left\{\begin{aligned} &\frac{\partial\bar{j}}{\partial t}+\left(\bar{u}\cdot\triangledown\right)\bar{j}=\triangle\bar{j}+\bar{f}(\bar{j},\bar{u})+\partial_{x}g(\bar{u})+\int_{\left|z\right|_{H}<1}\bar{\sigma}_{1}(\bar{j},\bar{u},z)\dot{\tilde{\eta}}_{1}(t,dz),\\
&\bar{j}(0)=j_{0},\end{aligned}\right.

(5.1)

where
\bar{j}:=\triangledown^{\perp}\cdot\bar{u}
,
g:H^{1}\rightarrow H
defined by

\displaystyle g(u):=\int_{H}{\hat{\theta}\pi^{u}d(\hat{\theta})},

and

\displaystyle\bar{f}(j,u):=\int_{H}f(j,\hat{\theta})\pi^{u}(d\hat{\theta}),\quad\bar{\sigma}_{1}(j,u,z):=\int_{H}\sigma(j,\hat{\theta},z)\pi^{u}(d\hat{\theta}).

To establish the well-posedness of (
5.1
), we require the non-Lipschitz continuity of
g
,
\bar{f}
and
\bar{\sigma}_{2}
. To this end, we introduce the following lemma:

Lemma 5.1
.

Let
a>0
and
f(t)\geq 0
, if
F(t):=\int_{0}^{t}f(s)ds
is bounded with respect to
t
, we have

\displaystyle\lim_{t\rightarrow+\infty}\int_{0}^{t}e^{-a(t-s)}f(s)ds=0.

The proof of this lemma is given in the Appendix.

Next, we derive the non-Lipschitz continuity of the coefficients in Eq.(
5.1
). We assert the following property:

Proposition 5.1
.

Under Assumptions
1
-
4
, for any
u_{1},u_{2}\in H^{1}
and for some

, we have

Proof of Lemma
5.1

Note that
F
is increasing and bounded with respect to
t
, we have

\displaystyle\lim_{t\rightarrow+\infty}F(t)=\sup_{t\geq 0}F(t):=M.

Therefore, for any
\varepsilon>0
, there exists some
\delta>0
, such that

\displaystyle e^{-a\delta}M<\frac{\varepsilon}{2}.

Let

\displaystyle I(t):=\int_{0}^{t}e^{-a(t-s)}f(s)ds.

For sufficiently large
t
, we have

\displaystyle I(t)=\left\{\int_{0}^{t-\delta}e^{-a(t-s)}f(s)ds\right\}_{I_{1}(t)}+\left\{\int_{t-\delta}^{t}e^{-a(t-s)}f(s)ds\right\}_{I_{2}(t)}.

For
I_{1}(t)
, due to
\delta\leq t-s\leq t
, we have

\displaystyle I_{1}(t)\leq e^{-a\delta}\int_{0}^{t-\delta}f(s)ds\leq e^{-a\delta}M<\frac{\varepsilon}{2}.

For
I_{2}(t)
, we have

\displaystyle I_{2}(t)\leq\int_{t-\delta}^{t}f(s)ds=F(t)-F(t-\delta)<\frac{\varepsilon}{2},

for sufficiently large
t
. Hence, Lemma
5.1
hold.
