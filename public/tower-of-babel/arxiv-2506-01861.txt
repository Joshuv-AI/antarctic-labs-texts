Proximity-Induced Rashba Spin-Orbit Interaction in BaMnO 3 | {}_{\text{3}}| KTaO 3 {}_{\text{3}} Heterostructure for Antiferromagnetic Spintronics
Vivek Kumar, Nirmal Ganguli

Abstract
We investigate the emergence of Rashba-like spin-orbit interaction in the antiferromagnetic BaMnO 3 | {}_{3}| KTaO 3 oxide heterostructure using density functional theory (DFT). The analysis of our charge transfer model based on electrostatic potential reveals a type-I band alignment and establishes the conditions under which the interface becomes conducting. Our calculations uncover pronounced linear Rashba splitting ( α ( 1 ) \alpha_{(1)} = 0.114 eVÅ) in Mn 3 d d bands near the Fermi level, induced by proximity to Ta 5 d d orbitals of the KTO substrate. The heterostructure exhibits inversion asymmetry due to polar discontinuities at its p p -type and n n -type interfaces. Using three-dimensional band dispersions and projected spin textures along with isoenergetic contours obtained from DFT calculations, we confirm the linear Rashba nature of the spin-orbit coupling. Employing Monte Carlo simulations based on DFT-derived magnetic exchange interactions, we estimate a Néel temperature of approximately ∼ 54 ​ K \sim 54\mathrm{~K} . Our results demonstrate a viable route to engineering strong spin-orbit coupling in centrosymmetric antiferromagnets for spintronics applications.
