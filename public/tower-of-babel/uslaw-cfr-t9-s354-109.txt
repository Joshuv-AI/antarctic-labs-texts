9 C.F.R. § 354.109 — Fees or charges for inspection service performed under cooperative agreement.
Citation: us:cfr:9:354.109
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-354.109
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 354 (Voluntary Inspection Of Rabbits And Edible Products Thereof). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Fees or charges to be made to an applicant for any inspection service which differ from those listed in §§ 354.100 through 354.107 shall be provided for by a cooperative agreement.
