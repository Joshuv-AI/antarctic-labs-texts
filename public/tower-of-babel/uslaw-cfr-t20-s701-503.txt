20 C.F.R. § 701.503 — Did the American Recovery and Reinvestment Act of 2009 amend the recreational vessel exclusion?
Citation: us:cfr:20:701.503
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-701.503
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 701 (General; Administering Agency; Definitions And Use Of Terms). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. The amended exclusion was effective February 17, 2009, the effective date of the American Recovery and Reinvestment Act of 2009.
