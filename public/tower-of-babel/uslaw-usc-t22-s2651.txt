22 U.S.C. § 2651 — Establishment of Department
Citation: us:usc:22:2651
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2651
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 38 (Department Of State). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There shall be at the seat of government an executive department to be known as the “Department of State”, and a Secretary of State, who shall be the head thereof.
