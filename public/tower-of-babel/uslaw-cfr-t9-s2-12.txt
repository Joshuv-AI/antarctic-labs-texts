9 C.F.R. § 2.12 — Termination of a license.
Citation: us:cfr:9:2.12
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-2.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 2 (Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A license may be terminated at any time for any reason that a license application may be denied pursuant to § 2.11 after a hearing in accordance with the applicable rules of practice in 7 CFR part 1.
