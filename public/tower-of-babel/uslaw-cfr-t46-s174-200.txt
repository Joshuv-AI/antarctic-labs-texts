46 C.F.R. § 174.200 — Damaged stability in machinery spaces for all OSVs.
Citation: us:cfr:46:174.200
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-174.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 174 (Special Rules Pertaining To Specific Vessel Types). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each OSV must be shown by design calculations to comply, under each afloat condition of loading and operation, with § 174.207 of this subpart in case of damage between any two watertight bulkheads in each machinery space.
