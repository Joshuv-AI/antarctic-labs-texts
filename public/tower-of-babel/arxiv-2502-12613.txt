Two-body strong decays of the hidden-charm tetraquark molecular states via the QCD sum rules


Abstract
In this work, we extend our previous work on the D ∗ ​ D ¯ ∗ D^{*}\bar{D}^{*} molecular states with the J P ​ C = 0 + ⁣ + J^{PC}=0^{++} , 1 + ⁣ − 1^{+-} and 2 + ⁣ + 2^{++} to investigate their two-body strong decays via the QCD sum rules based on rigorous quark-hadron duality.
