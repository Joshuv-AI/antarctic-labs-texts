25 C.F.R. § 41.9 — What is the purpose of this subpart?
Citation: us:cfr:25:41.9
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-41.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 41 (Grants To Tribal Colleges And Universities And Diné College). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes procedures for providing financial and technical assistance under the TCCUA for the operation and improvement of Tribal colleges and universities and advancement of educational opportunities for Indian students. This subpart does not apply to Diné College.
