12 U.S.C. § 3903 — Strengthened supervision of international lending
Citation: us:usc:12:3903
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section3903
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 40 (International Lending Supervision). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Each appropriate Federal banking agency shall evaluate banking institution foreign country exposure and transfer risk for use in banking institution examination and supervision.
(b)
Each such agency shall establish examination and supervisory procedures to assure that factors such as foreign country exposure and transfer risk are taken into account in evaluating the adequacy of the capital of banking institutions.
