Horton-Strahler numbers for binary butterfly trees: exact analysis
Helmut Prodinger

Abstract
. Peca suggested in a recent paper on the arxiv to consider binary butterfly trees and their Horton-Strahler numbers. The trees are obtained by glueing two binary trees together in a special way; the results are again binary trees but with a different probability distribution. A thorough combinatorial analysis is provided and leads asymptotically to the same results as for classical binary trees.

2.
Horton-Strahler numbers resulting from glueing together two binary trees

Recall that

\displaystyle S_{p}(z)=zS_{p-1}^{2}+zS_{p}(B-S_{p-1})+z(B-S_{p-1})S_{p},\quad p\geq 1.

Using a similar reasoning, distinguishing whether the first right edge is marked (first line) or is not marked (second line), we find

\displaystyle T_{p}(z)

\displaystyle=zS_{p-1}^{2}+zS_{p}(B-S_{p-1})+z(B-S_{p-1})S_{p}

\displaystyle+zS_{p-1}T_{p-1}+zS_{p}(A-T_{p-1})+z(B-S_{p-1})T_{p},

or

\displaystyle T_{p}(z)

\displaystyle=S_{p}+zS_{p-1}T_{p-1}+zS_{p}(A-T_{p-1})+z(B-S_{p-1})T_{p},\quad p\geq 2,\text{ and }T_{1}=A.

Further

\displaystyle T_{p}(z)\big(1-z(B-S_{p})\big)

\displaystyle=S_{p}(1+zA)+zT_{p-1}(S_{p-1}-S_{p}).

A direct computation yields

\displaystyle 1-z(B-S_{p})

\displaystyle=\frac{1-u^{2^{p}+1}}{(1+u)(1-u^{2^{p}})},\quad 1+zA=\frac{1+u+u^{2}}{1+u}.

Further

\displaystyle S_{p-1}-S_{p}=R_{p-1}=\frac{1-u^{2}}{u}\frac{u^{2^{p-1}}}{1-u^{2^{p}}}.

Some rearrangements lead to

\displaystyle T_{p}(z)(1-u^{2^{p-1}+1})(1+u^{2^{p-1}})

\displaystyle=\frac{1-u^{2}}{u}u^{2^{p}}(1+u+u^{2})+T_{p-1}(1-u)u^{2^{p-1}};

this first order recursion will be solved by introduction of summation factors and eventually by summation. This will be done in a few steps;

\displaystyle\frac{T_{p}(z)}{u^{2^{p}}}(1-u^{2^{p-1}+1})(1+u^{2^{p-1}})

\displaystyle=\frac{1-u^{2}}{u}(1+u+u^{2})+\frac{T_{p-1}(z)}{u^{2^{p-1}}}(1-u);

\displaystyle\frac{T_{p}(z)}{u^{2^{p}}}(1-u^{2^{p-1}+1})\prod_{j=0}^{p-1}(1+u^{2^{j}})

\displaystyle=\frac{1-u^{2}}{u}(1+u+u^{2})\prod_{j=0}^{p-2}(1+u^{2^{j}})

\displaystyle+\frac{T_{p-1}(z)}{u^{2^{p-1}}}(1-u)\prod_{j=0}^{p-2}(1+u^{2^{j}}).

Evaluating the products yields

\displaystyle\frac{T_{p}(z)(1-u^{2^{p-1}+1})}{u^{2^{p}}}(1-u^{2^{p}})

\displaystyle=\frac{1-u^{2}}{u}(1+u+u^{2})(1-u^{2^{p-1}})

\displaystyle+\frac{T_{p-1}(z)(1-u^{2^{p-1}})}{u^{2^{p-1}}}(1-u)

and

\displaystyle\frac{T_{p}(z)(1-u^{2^{p}})}{u^{2^{p}}(1-u)^{p}}(1-u^{2^{p-1}+1})

\displaystyle=\frac{1-u^{2}}{u(1-u)^{p}}(1+u+u^{2})(1-u^{2^{p-1}})

\displaystyle+\frac{T_{p-1}(z)(1-u^{2^{p-1}})}{u^{2^{p-1}}(1-u)^{p-1}}

Introducing a further product,

\displaystyle\frac{T_{p}(z)(1-u^{2^{p}})}{u^{2^{p}}(1-u)^{p}}\prod_{j=0}^{p-1}(1-u^{2^{j}+1})

\displaystyle=\frac{1+u}{u(1-u)^{p-1}}(1+u+u^{2})(1-u^{2^{p-1}})\prod_{j=0}^{p-2}(1-u^{2^{j}+1})

\displaystyle+\frac{T_{p-1}(z)(1-u^{2^{p-1}})}{u^{2^{p-1}}(1-u)^{p-1}}\prod_{j=0}^{p-2}(1-u^{2^{j}+1}).

An abbreviation is useful:

\displaystyle\omega_{p}:=\frac{T_{p}(z)(1-u^{2^{p}})}{u^{2^{p}}(1-u)^{p}}\prod_{j=0}^{p-1}(1-u^{2^{j}+1}),

then we get a form that can be summed:

\displaystyle\omega_{p}=\omega_{p-1}+\frac{1+u}{u(1-u)^{p-1}}(1+u+u^{2})(1-u^{2^{p-1}})\prod_{j=0}^{p-2}(1-u^{2^{j}+1})

and so

\displaystyle\omega_{p}=\omega_{1}+\sum_{h=1}^{p-1}\frac{1+u}{u(1-u)^{h}}(1+u+u^{2})(1-u^{2^{h}})\prod_{j=0}^{h-1}(1-u^{2^{j}+1}).

Coming back to the original quantities
T_{p}(z)
,

\displaystyle T_{p}(z)

\displaystyle=\omega_{p}\frac{u^{2^{p}}(1-u)^{p}}{(1-u^{2^{p}})}\bigg/\prod_{j=0}^{p-1}(1-u^{2^{j}+1})

\displaystyle=\bigg[(1+u)^{2}+(1+u+u^{2})\sum_{h=1}^{p-1}\frac{1-u^{2^{h}}}{1-u}\prod_{j=0}^{h-1}\frac{1-u^{2^{j}+1}}{1-u}\bigg]

\displaystyle\qquad\times\frac{1-u^{2}}{u}\frac{u^{2^{p}}}{1-u^{2^{p}}}\prod_{j=0}^{p-1}\frac{1-u}{1-u^{2^{j}+1}}.

Note that
\dfrac{1-u^{2}}{u}\dfrac{u^{2^{p}}}{1-u^{2^{p}}}=S_{p}(z)
.

Theorem 1
.

The generating function
T_{p}(z)
of trees in
\mathscr{A}
with Horton-Strahler number
\geq p
is for
p\geq 0

given by

\displaystyle T_{p}(z)=S_{p}(z)\bigg[(1+u)^{2}+(1+u+u^{2})\sum_{h=1}^{p-1}\frac{1-u^{2^{h}}}{1-u}\prod_{j=0}^{h-1}\frac{1-u^{2^{j}+1}}{1-u}\bigg]\prod_{j=0}^{p-1}\frac{1-u}{1-u^{2^{j}+1}}.

Note that this formula has been tested. Since the generating function is fully explicit one has (in principle) access to the coefficients, i. e., to the numbers of binary butterfly trees of a given number of nodes and a given Horton-Strahler number.

3.
The average value of Horton-Strahler numbers in marked binary trees

By general principles, the generating function

\displaystyle\sum_{p\geq 1}T_{p}(z)

is, apart from normalization, the generating function of the averages; note that it was

\displaystyle\sum_{p\geq 1}S_{p}(z)

in the classical case, and the latter series is well-understood.

To understand the strategy, we need to expand the generating function around
u=1
; in some instances it helps to
set
u=e^{-t}
and expand around
t=0
. Consider

\displaystyle\bigg[(1+u)^{2}+(1+u+u^{2})\sum_{h=1}^{p-1}\frac{1-u^{2^{h}}}{1-u}\prod_{j=0}^{h-1}\frac{1-u^{2^{j}+1}}{1-u}\bigg]\prod_{j=0}^{p-1}\frac{1-u}{1-u^{2^{j}+1}};

(1)

the term
\dfrac{1-u^{2}}{u}\displaystyle{\sum\limits_{p\geq 1}\frac{u^{2^{p}}}{1-u^{2^{p}}}}
will be brought in later.

The ugly term (
1
) is actually simpler to handle, since we are only interested in the leading term, which is a constant (in the expansion around
t=0
). For the following we might replace a factor
\frac{1-u^{d}}{1-u}
by
d
, and instead of (
1
)
consider

\displaystyle\lambda_{p}:=\bigg[4+3\sum_{h=1}^{p-1}2^{h}\prod_{j=0}^{h-1}(2^{j}+1)\bigg]\prod_{j=0}^{p-1}\frac{1}{2^{j}+1}.

(2)

The sequence
\lambda_{p}
converges to 3 exponentially fast.
We consider now

\displaystyle\sum_{p\geq 1}\frac{u^{2^{p}}}{1-u^{2^{p}}}\lambda_{p}=\sum_{p,k\geq 1}\lambda_{p}e^{-t{k2^{p}}}.

The Mellin transformation will be applied, as in many related projects:

\displaystyle\mathscr{M}\sum_{p,k\geq 1}\lambda_{p}e^{-tk2^{p}}=\zeta(s)\mathscr{M}\sum_{p\geq 1}\lambda_{p}e^{-t2^{p}}=\zeta(s)\Gamma(s)\underbrace{\sum_{p\geq 1}\lambda_{p}2^{-ps}}_{\Lambda(s):=}.

Now

\displaystyle\Lambda(s)=\sum_{p\geq 1}\lambda_{p}2^{-ps}=\frac{3}{2^{s}-1}+\sum_{p\geq 1}\mathscr{O}(2^{-p})2^{-ps}.

The remainder has shifted singularities, and thus the dominant ones are at
\Re s=0
. The general plan is to look at the
inverse Mellin transform
\zeta(s)\Gamma(s)\Lambda(s)t^{-s}
and at its residues. Since the dominant ones are
at
\Re s=0
, we can concentrate on
3\zeta(s)\Gamma(s)\frac{t^{-s}}{2^{s}-1}
, which is just 3 times the relevant quantity for classical binary trees.

For the final averages of the Horton-Strahler numbers, we have to divide by

\displaystyle[z^{n}]A(z)=\frac{3(2n)!}{(n-1)!(n+2)!}=\frac{3(2n)!}{n!(n+1)!}\Big(1+\mathscr{O}\Big(\frac{1}{n}\Big)\Big),

so that within the accuracy the average Horton-Strahler numbers are computed, the asymptotic formula is the same:

Theorem 2
.

The average value of the Horton-Strahler numbers (register function) of all butterfly trees in the sense of Peca of size
n

is given by the asymptotic formula (with
\chi_{k}=\frac{2k\pi i}{\log 2}
)

\displaystyle\log_{4}n

\displaystyle-\frac{\gamma}{2\log 2}-\frac{1}{\log 2}+\frac{1}{2}+\log_{2}\pi+\frac{1}{\log 2}\sum_{k\neq 0}\zeta(\chi_{k})\Gamma\big(\frac{\chi_{k}}{2}\big)(\chi_{k}-1)n^{\chi_{k}/2}

\displaystyle=\log_{4}n-\frac{\gamma}{2\log 2}-\frac{1}{\log 2}+\frac{1}{2}+\log_{2}\pi+\psi(\log_{4}n),

with a tiny periodic function
\psi(x)
of period 1.

These oscillations are usually bounded by
10^{-5}
, say. See
[
2
]
for some explicit error bounds in the classical case.
The remainder term in the asymptotic formula is of the form
\mathscr{O}((\log^{\ast}\!n)/n)
and has never been computed explicitly neither in the classical case nor for the butterfly trees because of the complexity of the computations.
