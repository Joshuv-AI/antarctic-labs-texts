10 U.S.C. § 7283 — Civil War battle streamers
Citation: us:usc:10:7283
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7283
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 737 (Decorations And Awards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If, under regulations prescribed by the Secretary of the Army, it is determined that a regiment or other unit of the Army is entitled to that honor, the regiment or unit may carry any appropriate Civil War battle streamer with its colors or standards.
