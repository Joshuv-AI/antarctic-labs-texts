50 C.F.R. § 11.32 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:50:11.32
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-11.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 11 (Civil Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to make the inflation adjustment, described in and required by the Inflation Adjustment Act, of each civil monetary penalty provided by law within the jurisdiction of the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service.
