12 U.S.C. § 5621 — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:12:5621
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section5621
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 53 (Wall Street Reform And Consumer Protection); Subchapter VII (Improving Access To Mainstream Financial Institutions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of this subchapter is to encourage initiatives for financial products and services that are appropriate and accessible for millions of Americans who are not fully incorporated into the financial mainstream.
