32 C.F.R. § 219.122 — Use of Federal funds.
Citation: us:cfr:32:219.122
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-219.122
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 219 (Protection Of Human Subjects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Federal funds administered by a Federal department or agency may not be expended for research involving human subjects unless the requirements of this policy have been satisfied.
