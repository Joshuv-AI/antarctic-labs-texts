9 C.F.R. § 381.166 — Breaded products.
Citation: us:cfr:9:381.166
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-381.166
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 381 (Poultry Products Inspection Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

“Breaded” is a term applicable to any poultry product which is coated with breading or a batter and breading in an amount not to exceed 30 percent of the weight of the finished breaded product.
