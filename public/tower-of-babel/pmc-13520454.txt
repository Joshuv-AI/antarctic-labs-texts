Correction to “Quantifying Formation Permeability Effects on High-Temperature Aquifer Thermal Energy Storage”
Authors: Zhenjie Sun, Xiaoguang Wang, Di Deng, Yanyong Wang, Yun Huang, Chuanyin Jiang, Yinhui Zuo, Wei Jiang, Lijun Liu
Published: 2026

This correction addresses a typesetting error in the Acknowledgment section of the published paper. The error is purely textual and formatting in nature and does not alter the scientific findings, data, conclusions, or interpretations of the original work in any way.

During production, two independent funding bodies were incorrectly merged, and three grant numbers from the National Natural Science Foundation of China were omitted. This correction restores the full and accurate funding acknowledgment as submitted in the final accepted manuscript.
