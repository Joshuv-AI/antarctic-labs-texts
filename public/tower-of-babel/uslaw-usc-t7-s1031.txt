7 U.S.C. § 1031 — Conveyance of mineral rights with land
Citation: us:usc:7:1031
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1031
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 33 (Farm Tenancy); Subchapter IV (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any conveyance of real estate by the Government or any Government agency under this Act shall include all mineral rights.
