43 C.F.R. § 3732.1 — Act ineffective as to other withdrawals.

Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3730 (Public Law 359; Mining In Powersite Withdrawals: General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Act in section 2(c) provides as follows:
(b) If the power site lands are also affected by any other type of withdrawal which prevents mining location in whole or in part, the provisions of the Act apply only to the extent that the lands are otherwise open to location.
