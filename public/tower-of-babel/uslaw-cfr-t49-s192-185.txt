49 C.F.R. § 192.185 — Vaults: Accessibility.
Citation: us:cfr:49:192.185
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-192.185
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 192 (Transportation Of Natural And Other Gas By Pipeline: Minimum Federal Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each vault must be located in an accessible location and, so far as practical, away from:
(a) Street intersections or points where traffic is heavy or dense;
(b) Points of minimum elevation, catch basins, or places where the access cover will be in the course of surface waters; and
(c) Water, electric, steam, or other facilities.
