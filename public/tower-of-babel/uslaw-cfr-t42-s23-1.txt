42 C.F.R. § 23.1 — To what entities does this regulation apply?
Citation: us:cfr:42:23.1
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-23.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 23 (National Health Service Corps). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This regulation applies to the assignment of National Health Service Corps personnel under section 333 et seq. of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 254f) to provide health services in or to health manpower shortage areas as designated under section 332 of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 254e).
