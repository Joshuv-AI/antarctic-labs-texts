42 C.F.R. § 137.248 — What effect will a retrocession have on a retroceding Self-Governance Tribe's rights to contract or compact under the Act?
Citation: us:cfr:42:137.248
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-137.248
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 137 (Tribal Self-Governance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A retrocession request shall not negatively affect:
(a) Any other contract or compact to which the retroceding Self-Governance Tribe is a party;
(b) Any other contracts or compacts the retroceding Self-Governance Tribe may request; and
(c) Any future request by such Self-Governance Tribe or an Indian Tribe to compact or contract for the same program.
