49 C.F.R. § 1115.4 — Petitions to reopen administratively final actions.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1115.4
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1115.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1115 (Appellate Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person at any time may file a petition to reopen any administratively final action of the Board pursuant to the requirements of § 1115.3 (c) and (d) of this part. A petition to reopen must state in detail the respects in which the proceeding involves material error, new evidence, or substantially changed circumstances and must include a request that the Board make such a determination.
