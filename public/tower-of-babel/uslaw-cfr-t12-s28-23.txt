12 C.F.R. § 28.23 — Procedures for closing of some of a foreign bank's Federal branches and/or agencies.
Citation: us:cfr:12:28.23
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-28.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 28 (International Banking Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In cases where § 28.22 does not apply, and a foreign bank is closing one or more, but not all, of its Federal branches and/or agencies, it shall follow the procedures set forth in 12 U.S.C. 1831r-1(a) and (b) (branch closings).
