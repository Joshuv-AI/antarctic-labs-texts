2 C.F.R. § 2600.101 — Indirect costs exception to 2 CFR 200.414.
Citation: us:cfr:2:2600.101
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-2600.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 2600 (Uniform Administrative Requirements, Cost Principles, And Audit Requirements For Federal Awards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As approved by the Archivist of the United States, the National Archives does not permit grant recipients to use allocated funds from NARA or NHPRC for indirect costs. Grant recipients may use cost sharing to cover indirect costs instead. NARA's policies on indirect costs are located at http://www.archives.gov/nhprc, and are included in grant opportunity announcements.
