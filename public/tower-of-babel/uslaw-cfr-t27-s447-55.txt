27 C.F.R. § 447.55 — Departments of State and Defense consulted.
Citation: us:cfr:27:447.55
Creator: Bureau Of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms, And Explosives, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-447.55
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 447 (Importation Of Arms, Ammunition And Implements Of War). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The administration of the provisions of this part will be subject to the guidance of the Secretaries of State and Defense on matters affecting world peace and the external security and foreign policy of the United States.
