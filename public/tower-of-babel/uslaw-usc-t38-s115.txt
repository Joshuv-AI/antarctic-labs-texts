38 U.S.C. § 115 — Acquisition of real property
Citation: us:usc:38:115
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section115
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part I (General Provisions); Chapter 1 (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purposes of sections 314, 315, 316, and 2406 of this title and subchapter I of chapter 81 of this title, the Secretary may acquire and use real property—
(1)
before title to the property is approved under section 3111 of title 40; and
(2)
even though the property will be held in other than a fee simple interest in a case in which the Secretary determines that the interest to be acquired is sufficient for the purposes of the intended use.
