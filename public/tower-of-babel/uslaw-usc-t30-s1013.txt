30 U.S.C. § 1013 — Surface land use
Citation: us:usc:30:1013
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section1013
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 23 (Geothermal Resources). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Subject to the other provisions of this chapter, a lessee shall be entitled to use so much of the surface of the land covered by his geothermal lease as may be found by the Secretary to be necessary for the production, utilization, and conservation of geothermal resources.
