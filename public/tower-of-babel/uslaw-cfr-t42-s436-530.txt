42 C.F.R. § 436.530 — Definition of blindness.
Citation: us:cfr:42:436.530
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-436.530
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 436 (Eligibility In Guam, Puerto Rico, And The Virgin Islands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Definition. The agency must use the definition of blindness that is used in the State plan for AB or AABD.
(b) State plan requirement. The State plan must contain the definition of blindness, expressed in ophthalmic measurements.
