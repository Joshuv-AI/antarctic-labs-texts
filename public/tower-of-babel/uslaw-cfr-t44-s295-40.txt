44 C.F.R. § 295.40 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:44:295.40
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-295.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 295 (Cerro Grande Fire Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart describes a Claimant's right to bring an Administrative Appeal in response to the Authorized Official's Determination. It also describes the Claimant's right to pursue arbitration or seek judicial review following an Administrative Appeal.
