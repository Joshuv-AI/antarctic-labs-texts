31 C.F.R. § 553.301 — Arms and related materiel.
Citation: us:cfr:31:553.301
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-553.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 553 (Central African Republic Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term arms and related materiel means arms or related materiel of all types, including military aircraft and equipment, transferred in contravention of the United Nations arms embargo on the Central African Republic.
