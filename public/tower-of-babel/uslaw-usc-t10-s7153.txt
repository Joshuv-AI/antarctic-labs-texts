10 U.S.C. § 7153 — Commissioned officers: appointment without specification of branch; transfer between branches
Citation: us:usc:10:7153
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7153
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 715 (Appointments In The Regular Army). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Appointments in commissioned grades in the Regular Army shall be made without specification of branch except in each of the special branches and as professors or director of admissions of the United States Military Academy.
(b)
Commissioned officers appointed in the Regular Army without specification of branch shall be assigned, and may be transferred and reassigned, by the Secretary of the Army to branches other than the special branches, according to their professional qualifications and the needs of the Army.
