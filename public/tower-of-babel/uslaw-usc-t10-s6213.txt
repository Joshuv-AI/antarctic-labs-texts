10 U.S.C. § 6213 — Continuation of processing, treatment, and disposal of legacy nuclear materials
Citation: us:usc:10:6213
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section6213
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part VI (Elements Of Department Of Defense And Other Matters); Subpart B (Atomic Energy Defense); Chapter 604 (Defense Environmental Cleanup Matters); Subchapter IV (Savannah River Site, South Carolina). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Energy shall continue operations and maintain a high state of readiness at the H–canyon facility at the Savannah River Site, Aiken, South Carolina, and shall provide technical staff necessary to operate and so maintain such facility.
