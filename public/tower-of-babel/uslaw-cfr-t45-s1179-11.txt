45 C.F.R. § 1179.11 — Non-waiver of rights.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1179.11
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1179.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1179 (Salary Offset). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee's involuntary payment of all or any part of a debt collected under these regulations will not be construed as a waiver of any rights that employee may have under 5 U.S.C. 5514 or any other provision of contract law unless there are statutes or contract(s) to the contrary.
