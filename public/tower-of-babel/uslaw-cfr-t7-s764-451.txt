7 C.F.R. § 764.451 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:7:764.451
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-764.451
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 764 (Direct Loan Making). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of borrower training is to help an applicant develop and improve skills necessary to:
(a) Successfully operate a farm;
(b) Build equity in the operation; and
(c) Become financially successful and prepared to graduate from Agency financing to commercial sources of credit.
