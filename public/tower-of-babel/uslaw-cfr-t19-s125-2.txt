19 C.F.R. § 125.2 — Supervision of cartage and lighterage.
Citation: us:cfr:19:125.2
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-125.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 125 (Cartage And Lighterage Of Merchandise). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All licensed vehicles or lighters shall be subject to the control and direction of the officer having charge of the merchandise being carried.
