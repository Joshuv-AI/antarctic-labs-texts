45 C.F.R. § 1629.7 — Can LSC funds be used to cover bonding costs?
Citation: us:cfr:45:1629.7
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1629.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1629 (Bonding Requirements For Recipients). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Costs of bonding required by this part are allowable if expended consistent with 45 CFR part 1630. Costs of bonding such as rates, deductibles, single loss retention, and premiums, are allowable as an indirect cost if such bonding is in accordance with sound business practice and is reasonable.
