22 U.S.C. § 285n — Authorization of appropriations to provide United States Special Resources
Citation: us:usc:22:285n
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section285n
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter XIV (Asian Development Bank). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of providing United States Special Resources to the Bank there is hereby authorized to be appropriated $100,000,000, all of which shall remain available until expended.
