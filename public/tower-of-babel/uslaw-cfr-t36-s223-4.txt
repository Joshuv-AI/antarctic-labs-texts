36 C.F.R. § 223.4 — Exchange of trees or portions of trees.
Citation: us:cfr:36:223.4
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-223.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 223 (Sale And Disposal Of National Forest System Timber). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Trees or portions of trees may be exchanged for land under laws authorizing the exchange of National Forest timber. Cutting of exchange timber must comply with the purposes cited in § 223.1.
