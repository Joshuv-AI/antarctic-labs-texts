31 C.F.R. § 352.11 — Reservation as to issue of bonds.
Citation: us:cfr:31:352.11
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-352.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 352 (Offering Of United States Savings Bonds, Series Hh). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commissioner of the Fiscal Service, as delegate of the Secretary of the Treasury, reserved the right to reject any application for Series HH bonds, in whole or in part, and to refuse to issue or permit to be issued any bonds in any case or class of cases, if the action was deemed to be in the public interest. The Commissioner's action in such respect was final.
