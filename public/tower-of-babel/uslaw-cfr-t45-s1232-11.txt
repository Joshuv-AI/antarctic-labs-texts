45 C.F.R. § 1232.11 — Employment and volunteer selection criteria.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1232.11
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1232.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1232 (Nondiscrimination On Basis Of Handicap In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A recipient may not use employment tests or criteria that discriminate against handicapped persons and shall ensure that employment tests are adapted for use by persons who have handicaps that impair sensory, manual, or speaking skills.
