14 C.F.R. § 298.37 — Prohibition of services not covered by insurance.
Citation: us:cfr:14:298.37
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-298.37
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 298 (Exemptions For Air Taxi And Commuter Air Carrier Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An air taxi operator or commuter air carrier shall not operate in air transportation or provide or offer to provide air transportation unless there is in effect liability insurance which covers such transportation and which is evidenced by a current certificate of insurance on file with the Department as required by part 205 of this chapter.
