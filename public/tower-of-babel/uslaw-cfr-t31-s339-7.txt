31 C.F.R. § 339.7 — Preservation of rights.
Citation: us:cfr:31:339.7
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-339.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 339 (Exchange Offering Of United States Savings Bonds, Series H). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of Treasury Department Circulars Nos. 530, 653, and 905, as currently revised, are hereby modified and amended to the extent that they are not in accordance with this circular. However, nothing contained herein shall limit or restrict rights which owners of Series H bonds received in earlier exchanges have heretofore acquired.
