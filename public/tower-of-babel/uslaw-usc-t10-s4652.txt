10 U.S.C. § 4652 — Prohibition on use of funds for documenting economic or employment impact of certain acquisition programs
Citation: us:usc:10:4652
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section4652
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part V (Acquisition); Subpart H (Contract Management); Chapter 363 (Prohibition And Penalties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No funds appropriated by the Congress may be obligated or expended to assist any contractor of the Department of Defense in preparing any material, report, lists, or analysis with respect to the actual or projected economic or employment impact in a particular State or congressional district of an acquisition program for which all research, development, testing, and evaluation has not been completed.
