30 U.S.C. § 1716 — Liabilities and bonding
Citation: us:usc:30:1716
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section1716
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 29 (Oil And Gas Royalty Management); Subchapter I (Federal Royalty Management And Enforcement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A person (including any agent or employee of the United States and any independent contractor) authorized to collect, receive, account for, or otherwise handle any moneys payable to, or received by, the Department of the Interior which are derived from the sale, lease, or other disposal of any oil or gas shall be—
(1)
liable to the United States for any losses caused by any intentional or reckless action or inaction of such individual with respect to such moneys; and
(2)
in the case of an independent contractor, required as the Secretary deems necessary to maintain a bond commensurate with the amount of money for which such individual could be liable to the United States.
