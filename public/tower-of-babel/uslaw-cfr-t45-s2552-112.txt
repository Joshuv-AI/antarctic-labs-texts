45 C.F.R. § 2552.112 — What are the resources and benefits to which a non-AmeriCorps funded project is entitled?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2552.112
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2552.112
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2552 (Foster Grandparent Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Notice of Grant Award entitles the sponsor of a non-AmeriCorps funded project to:
(a) All technical assistance and materials provided to AmeriCorps funded Foster Grandparent projects; and
(b) The application of the provisions of 42 U.S.C. 5044 and 5058.
