16 U.S.C. § 580p–1 — Property of the United States
Citation: us:usc:16:580p–1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section580p–1
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 3 (Forests; Forest Service; Reforestation; Management); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The following are hereby declared the property of the United States:
(1)
The name and character “Smokey Bear”.
(2)
The name and character “Woodsy Owl” and the associated slogan, “Give a Hoot, Don’t Pollute”.
