Abstract
We observe the radiative decay D s ​ 0 ∗ ​ ( 2317 ) + → D s ∗ ⁣ + ​ γ D^{*}_{s0}(2317)^{+}\to D_{s}^{*+}\gamma for the first time, with a significance exceeding 10 10 standard deviations. The signal is found in the continuum e + ​ e − → c ​ c ¯ e^{+}e^{-}\to c\bar{c} process with the combined data samples of 980.4 fb − 1 \rm fb^{-1} and 427.9 fb − 1 \rm fb^{-1} collected by the Belle and Belle II detectors operating at the KEKB and SuperKEKB asymmetric-energy e + ​ e − e^{+}e^{-} colliders, respectively. The branching fraction ratio ℬ ⁡ ( D s ​ 0 ∗ ​ ( 2317 ) + → D s ∗ ⁣ + ​ γ ) / ℬ ⁡ ( D s ​ 0 ∗ ​ ( 2317 ) + → D s + ​ π 0 ) {\cal B}(D^{*}_{s0}(2317)^{+}\to D_{s}^{*+}\gamma)/{\cal B}(D^{*}_{s0}(2317)^{+}\to D_{s}^{+}\pi^{0}) is measured to be [ 7.14 ± 0.70 ( stat . ) ± 0.23 ( syst . ) ] % [7.14\pm 0.70({\rm stat.})\pm 0.23({\rm syst.})]\% . This result provides significant new experimental input for the determination of the quark structure of the D s ​ 0 ∗ ​ ( 2317 ) + D^{*}_{s0}(2317)^{+} , which remains unknown.

To characterize possible systematic effects in the
D^{*}_{s0}(2317)^{+}\to D_{s}^{+}\pi^{0}
reference channel, (a) the order of the background polynomials and the fit range are varied, (b) the widths of the triple-Gaussian functions are increased by 1
\sigma

A series of pseudo-experiments is conducted to estimate the systematic uncertainty contribution to
{\cal R}
from the
D^{*}_{s0}(2317)^{+}\to D_{s}^{+}\pi^{0}
channel. In each trial, we randomly fluctuate the
D^{*}_{s0}(2317)^{+}\to D_{s}^{+}\pi^{0}
yields for both Belle and Belle II by sampling from Gaussian distributions. Each Gaussian distribution is constructed with its mean value set to the corresponding nominal
D^{*}_{s0}(2317)^{+}\to D_{s}^{+}\pi^{0}

N^{\rm fit}_{\rm exp}(D_{s}^{+}\pi^{0})

A simultaneous fit similar to the nominal fit to the data described above is then performed to the
M(D_{s}^{*+}\gamma)

D^{*}_{s0}(2317)^{+}\to D_{s}^{+}\pi^{0}

{\cal R}
from
D^{*}_{s0}(2317)^{+}\to D_{s}^{+}\pi^{0}
decay.

{\cal R}

\sigma

2\sigma

To estimate the uncertainty due to
x_{p}

M(D_{s}^{*+}\gamma)

\cal{R}

The systematic uncertainty on detection efficiencies due to the limited size of the signal MC sample is estimated by
\sqrt{(1-\varepsilon)\varepsilon/N}

\varepsilon

1\sigma

M(D_{s}^{*+}\gamma)
from data, the change of the fitted
\cal{R}

\rm fb^{-1}
and 428
\rm fb^{-1}
collected by the Belle and Belle II detectors operating at the KEKB and SuperKEKB asymmetric-energy
e^{+}e^{-}

in the continuum
e^{+}e^{-}

\to

[7.14\pm 0.70({\rm stat.})\pm 0.23({\rm syst.})]\%

;
Fu:2021wde
