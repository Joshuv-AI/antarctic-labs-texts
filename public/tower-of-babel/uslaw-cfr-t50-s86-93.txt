50 C.F.R. § 86.93 — May I prohibit overnight use by eligible vessels at a BIG-funded facility?
Citation: us:cfr:50:86.93
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-86.93
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 86 (Boating Infrastructure Grant Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may prohibit overnight use at a BIG-funded facility if you state in the approved grant application that the facility is only for day use. If after we award the grant you wish to change to day use only, you must follow the requirements at subpart H of this part.
