5 C.F.R. § 550.808 — Prohibition against setting aside proper promotions.
Citation: us:cfr:5:550.808
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-550.808
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 550 (Pay Administration (General)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in section 5596 of title 5, United States Code, or this subpart shall be construed as authorizing the setting aside of an otherwise proper promotion by a selecting official from a group of properly ranked and certified candidates.
