42 C.F.R. § 71.43 — Exemption for mails.
Citation: us:cfr:42:71.43
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-71.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 71 (Foreign Quarantine). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except to the extent that mail contains any article or thing subject to restrictions under subpart F of this part, nothing in the regulations in this part shall render liable to detention, disinfection, or destruction any mail conveyed under the authority of the postal administration of the United States or of any other Government.
