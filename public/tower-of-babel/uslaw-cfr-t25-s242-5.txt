25 C.F.R. § 242.5 — Disposition of unmarketable fish.
Citation: us:cfr:25:242.5
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-242.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 242 (Commercial Fishing On Red Lake Indian Reservation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All unmarketable live fish taken under authority of these regulations must be returned to the water, and all unmarketable dead fish taken must be buried by the person taking the same.
