49 C.F.R. § 37.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:49:37.1
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-37.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 37 (Transportation Services For Individuals With Disabilities (Ada)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to implement the transportation and related provisions of titles II and III of the Americans with Disabilities Act of 1990.
