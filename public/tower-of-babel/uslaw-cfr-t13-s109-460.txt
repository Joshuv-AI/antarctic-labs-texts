13 C.F.R. § 109.460 — Prohibition on sales of ILP Intermediary Loans to Eligible Small Business Concerns.
Citation: us:cfr:13:109.460
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-109.460
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 109 (Intermediary Lending Pilot Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An ILP Intermediary may not sell all or any portion of a loan made to an Eligible Small Business Concern without prior written consent from the AA/CA or designee.
