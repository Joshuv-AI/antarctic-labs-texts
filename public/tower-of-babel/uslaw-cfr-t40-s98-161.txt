40 C.F.R. § 98.161 — Reporting threshold.
Citation: us:cfr:40:98.161
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-98.161
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 98 (Mandatory Greenhouse Gas Reporting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must report GHG emissions under this subpart if your facility contains a hydrogen production process and the facility meets the requirements of either § 98.2(a)(1) or (a)(2).
