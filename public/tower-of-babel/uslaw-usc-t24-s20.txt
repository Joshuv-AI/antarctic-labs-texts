24 U.S.C. § 20 — Discipline of patients at Army and Navy Hospital
Citation: us:usc:24:20
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title24-section20
Context: United States Code, Title 24 (Hospitals And Asylums); Chapter 1 (Navy Hospitals, Army And Navy Hospital, And Hospital Relief For Seamen And Others). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All persons admitted to treatment in the Army and Navy General Hospital at Hot Springs, Arkansas, shall, while patients in said hospital, be subject to the rules and articles for the government of the armies of the United States.
