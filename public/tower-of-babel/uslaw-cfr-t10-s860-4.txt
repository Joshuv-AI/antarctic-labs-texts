10 C.F.R. § 860.4 — Unauthorized introduction of weapons or dangerous materials.
Citation: us:cfr:10:860.4
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-860.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 860 (Trespassing On Department Of Energy Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unauthorized carrying, transporting, or otherwise introducing or causing to be introduced any dangerous weapon, explosive, or other dangerous instrument or material likely to produce substantial injury or damage to persons or property, into or upon any facility, installation or real property subject to this part, is prohibited.
