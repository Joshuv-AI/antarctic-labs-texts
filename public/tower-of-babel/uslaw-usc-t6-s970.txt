6 U.S.C. § 970 — Noncontainerized cargo
Citation: us:usc:6:970
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section970
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 3 (Security And Accountability For Every Port); Subchapter II (Security Of The International Supply Chain); Part B (Customs–Trade Partnership Against Terrorism). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary, acting through the Commissioner, shall consider the potential for participation in C–TPAT by importers of noncontainerized cargoes that otherwise meet the requirements under this part.
