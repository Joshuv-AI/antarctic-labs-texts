19 C.F.R. § 122.185 — Report of loss or theft of Customs access seal.
Citation: us:cfr:19:122.185
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-122.185
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 122 (Air Commerce Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The loss or theft of an approved Customs access seal must be promptly reported in writing by the employee to the port director. The Customs access seal may be replaced, as provided in § 122.182(f).
