30 C.F.R. § 882.1 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:30:882.1
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-882.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 882 (Reclamation On Private Land). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part authorizes reclamation on private land and establishes procedures for recovery of the cost of reclamation activities conducted on privately owned land by the OSM, State, or Indian tribe.
