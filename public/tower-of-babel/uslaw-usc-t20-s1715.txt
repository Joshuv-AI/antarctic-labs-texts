20 U.S.C. § 1715 — District lines
Citation: us:usc:20:1715
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1715
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 39 (Equal Educational Opportunities And Transportation Of Students); Subchapter I (Equal Educational Opportunities); Part 4 (Remedies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the formulation of remedies under section 1712 or 1713 of this title the lines drawn by a State, subdividing its territory into separate school districts, shall not be ignored or altered except where it is established that the lines were drawn for the purpose, and had the effect, of segregating children among public schools on the basis of race, color, sex, or national origin.
