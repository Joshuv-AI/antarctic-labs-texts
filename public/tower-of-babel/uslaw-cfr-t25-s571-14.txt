25 C.F.R. § 571.14 — Relationship of financial statements to fee assessment reports.
Citation: us:cfr:25:571.14
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-571.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 571 (Monitoring And Investigations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A tribe shall reconcile its Commission fee assessment reports, submitted under 25 CFR part 514, with its audited or reviewed financial statements for each location and make available such reconciliation upon request by the Commission's authorized representative.
