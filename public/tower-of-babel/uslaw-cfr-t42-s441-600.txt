42 C.F.R. § 441.600 — Basis and purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:42:441.600
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-441.600
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 441 (Services: Requirements And Limits Applicable To Specific Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart implements sections 1902(a)(62) and 1928 of the Act by requiring states to provide for a program for the purchase and distribution of pediatric vaccines to program-registered providers for the immunization of vaccine-eligible children.
