10 U.S.C. § 10209 — Regular and reserve components: discrimination prohibited
Citation: us:usc:10:10209
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section10209
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And Administration); Chapter 1007 (Administration Of Reserve Components). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Laws applying to both Regulars and Reserves shall be administered without discrimination—
(1)
among Regulars;
(2)
among Reserves; and
(3)
between Regulars and Reserves.
