2 U.S.C. § 2009 — Assignment of rooms to Commissioner from Puerto Rico
Citation: us:usc:2:2009
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section2009
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 30 (Operation And Maintenance Of Capitol Complex); Subchapter I (House Of Representatives). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the matter of the assignment of rooms under sections 2004 to 2011 of this title, Delegates in Congress and the Commissioner from Puerto Rico shall be treated the same as Representatives.
