42 C.F.R. § 435.640 — Protected Medicaid eligibility for individuals eligible in December 1973.
Citation: us:cfr:42:435.640
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-435.640
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 435 (Eligibility In The States, District Of Columbia, The Northern Mariana Islands, And American Samoa). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In determining whether individuals continue to meet the income requirements used in December 1973, for purposes of determining eligibility under §§ 435.131, 435.132, and 435.133, the agency must deduct increased OASDI payments to the same extent that these deductions were in effect in December 1973. These deductions are required by section 306 of the Social Security Amendments of 1972 (Pub. L. 92-603) and section 1007 of Pub. L. 91-172 (enacted Dec. 30, 1969), modified by section 304 of Pub. L. 92-603.
