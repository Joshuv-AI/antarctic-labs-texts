20 U.S.C. § 7355a — Supplement, not supplant
Citation: us:usc:20:7355a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section7355a
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 70 (Strengthening And Improvement Of Elementary And Secondary Schools); Subchapter V (Flexibility And Accountability); Part B (Rural Education Initiative); Subpart 3 (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Funds made available under subpart 1 or subpart 2 of this part shall be used to supplement, and not supplant, any other Federal, State, or local education funds.
