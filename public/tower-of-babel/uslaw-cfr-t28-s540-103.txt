28 C.F.R. § 540.103 — Inmate telephone calls to attorneys.
Citation: us:cfr:28:540.103
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-540.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 540 (Contact With Persons In The Community). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Warden may not apply frequency limitations on inmate telephone calls to attorneys when the inmate demonstrates that communication with attorneys by correspondence, visiting, or normal telephone use is not adequate.
