Weak dual symmetries of two color QCD phase diagram
K. G. Klimenko, R. N. Zhokhov

Abstract
The phase diagram of two color QCD under influence of baryon density ( μ B \mu_{B} ), isospin ( μ I \mu_{I} ), chiral ( μ 5 \mu_{5} and ν 5 \nu_{5} ) imbalances is investigated within the framework NJL model approach. This letter establishes the existence of weak dualities in two color quark matter—symmetries revealed when full thermodynamic potential is projected or restricted to specific condensation channels. These dualities provide a unified understanding of two interesting phenomena in the phase diagram: universal catalysis and chameleon effect of chiral chemical potential.

,

and

\displaystyle{\cal D}_{1}:~\mu\longleftrightarrow\nu,~~~\pi_{1}\longleftrightarrow|\Delta|,~~~~~~{\cal D}_{2}:~\mu\longleftrightarrow\nu_{5},~~M\longleftrightarrow|\Delta|,~~~~~~{\cal D}_{3}:~\nu\longleftrightarrow\nu_{5},~~M\longleftrightarrow\pi_{1}.

IV
Weak dualities

Now let us recall that in the three color case it was demonstrated that there exist so called weak dualities
weakdual
. These dualities refer to symmetries in the phase diagram that become apparent when the TDP is projected onto the axes corresponding to individual condensates. So in order to investigate weak dualities one need to consider the projections of the TDP on various axes.

In effective NJL model (
1
), when the diquark condensate is absent (
\Delta=0
), the TDP depends on the quantity
P_{+}(\eta)P_{-}(\eta)
(we denote
\eta=p_{0}+\mu
), where

\displaystyle P_{\pm}(\eta)

\displaystyle\equiv

\displaystyle\pi_{1}^{4}-2\pi_{1}^{2}\big[(p_{0}+\mu)^{2}-(|\vec{p}|\pm\mu_{5})^{2}-M^{2}+\nu_{5}^{2}-\nu^{2}\big]

(2)

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle\big[M^{2}+(|\vec{p}|\pm\mu_{5}+\nu_{5})^{2}-(p_{0}+\mu\pm\nu)^{2}\big]\big[M^{2}+(|\vec{p}|\pm\mu_{5}-\nu_{5})^{2}-(p_{0}+\mu\mp\nu)^{2}\big].

Additionally restricting to the case of a vanishing pion condensate (
\pi=0
), one can obtain that the TDP depends on

\displaystyle P_{M}(\mu,\,\nu,\,\nu_{5},\,\mu_{5})=\ln P_{+}(\eta)P_{-}(\eta)\Big|_{\Delta=0,\,\pi=0}=\ln\Big\{\big[M^{2}+(|\vec{p}|+\mu_{5}+\nu_{5})^{2}-(p_{0}+\mu+\nu)^{2}\big]\times

\displaystyle\times\big[M^{2}+(|\vec{p}|+\mu_{5}-\nu_{5})^{2}-(p_{0}+\mu-\nu)^{2}\big]\big[M^{2}+(|\vec{p}|-\mu_{5}+\nu_{5})^{2}-(p_{0}+\mu-\nu)^{2}\big]\times

\displaystyle\times\big[M^{2}+(|\vec{p}|-\mu_{5}-\nu_{5})^{2}-(p_{0}+\mu+\nu)^{2}\big]\Big\}.

One can see that the quantity

exhibits the following property
P_{M}(\mu,\,\nu,\,\nu_{5},\,\mu_{5})=P_{M}(\nu,\,\mu,\,\nu_{5},\,\mu_{5})

(or one could argue just by dual property

So projection of the TDP on
M

P_{M}
should possess this property, i. e it is invariant with respect to the transformation

\displaystyle\mu\longleftrightarrow\nu.

(4)

Moreover, it is possible to note that the quantity
P_{M}
satisfies:
P_{M}(\mu,\,\nu,\,\nu_{5},\,\mu_{5})=P_{M}(\mu,\,\nu,\,\mu_{5},\,\nu_{5})

And hence the projection of the TDP on
M

\displaystyle{\cal D_{M}}:~~~\mu_{5}\longleftrightarrow\nu_{5}.

(5)

The invariance
5
is called weak dual symmetry of the phase diagram. It represents a symmetry that is not present in the full TDP but emerges in the case when it is enough to consider the projection onto a specific order parameter. We will discuss the implications and usage in the next section.

We now consider the phase structure under the condition of restored chiral symmetry (chiral condensate is zero,
M=0
) and in the absence of diquark condensation (
\Delta=0
). This scenario is physically relevant, for example, for systems with high isospin density, where pion condensation is expected to be the dominant phenomenon. In this case, the TDP depends on the quantity

\displaystyle P_{\pi}(\mu,\,\nu,\,\nu_{5},\,\mu_{5})=\big[\pi_{1}^{2}+(|\vec{p}|+\mu_{5}+\nu)^{2}-(\eta+\nu_{5})^{2}\big]\big[\pi_{1}^{2}+(|\vec{p}|+\mu_{5}-\nu)^{2}-(\eta-\nu_{5})^{2}\big]

\displaystyle\big[\pi_{1}^{2}+(|\vec{p}|-\mu_{5}+\nu)^{2}-(\eta-\nu_{5})^{2}\big]\big[\pi_{1}^{2}+(|\vec{p}|-\mu_{5}-\nu)^{2}-(\eta+\nu_{5})^{2}\big].

(6)

A direct analysis of this expression reveals a symmetry under the interchange of the chemical potentials
\nu
and
\mu_{5}

Consequently, the projection of the TDP onto the pion condensate axis,
\Omega(\pi|\mu,\nu,\nu_{5},\mu_{5})

So in situations if chiral symmetry breaking phenomenon and color superconductivity phenomena can be omitted and only pion condensation matters the TDP satisfies the dual symmetry

\displaystyle{\cal D}_{\pi}:~~~~\mu_{5}\longleftrightarrow\nu.

(7)

This establishes a weak dual symmetry in the sector dominated by pion condensation.

Finally, we consider quark matter with diquark condensation, i. e. under the condition of restored chiral symmetry (
M=0
) and in the absence of pion condensation (
\pi_{1}=0

\displaystyle P_{\Delta}(\mu,\,\nu,\,\nu_{5},\,\mu_{5})=\ln P_{+}^{{\small\cal\Delta}}(\eta)P_{-}^{\small\cal\Delta}(\eta)\Big|_{M=0,\,\pi=0}=\ln\Big\{\big[\Delta^{2}+(|\vec{p}|+\mu_{5}+\mu)^{2}-(p_{0}+\nu_{5}+\nu)^{2}\big]\times

V

There have been inferred several unique inherent property of chiral chemical potential
\mu_{5}

V.1

VI
Conclusion

In this letter, it was found that the phase diagram of two color QCD, described by an effective NJL model, possesses weak dual symmetries. The weak dualities provide a unified explanation for two remarkable features of phase structure of two color quark matter.

Universal catalysis
. Chiral chemical potential
\mu_{5}
acts as a universal catalyzer enhancing with identical functional dependence the formation of the chiral, pion and the diquark condensates.

Chameleon property of chiral imbalance
. The chemical potential
\mu_{5}
in different physical contexts can exactly mimic the effects of other chemical potentials.
