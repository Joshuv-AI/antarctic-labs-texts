50 U.S.C. § 4111 — Payments to certain members of religious orders
Citation: us:usc:50:4111
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section4111
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 51 (War Claims); Subchapter I (Title I Of War Claims Act Of 1948). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In any case in which any money is payable as a result of the enactment of this subchapter to any person who is prevented from accepting such money by the rules, regulations, or customs of the church or the religious order or organization of which he is a member, such money shall be paid, upon the request of such person, to such church or to such religious order or organization.
