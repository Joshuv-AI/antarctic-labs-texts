Neth Heart J 2024

In the article titled “Combined strategy of upfront CTCA and optimal treatment for stable chest pain: rationale and design of the CLEAR-CAD trial”, there was an error in table 2 in the Ranked secondary endpoints section. The list of ranked secondary endpoints should have also included ‘Myocardial Infarction’ as the last and separate endpoint.

This erratum corrects the ‘Ranked secondary endpoints’ section in accordance with the original study protocol. The original article has been corrected.
