45 C.F.R. § 1160.7 — Certificate of national interest.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1160.7
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1160.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1160 (Indemnities Under The Arts And Artifacts Indemnity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After preliminary review applications for international exhibitions with eligible items under § 1160.4 will be submitted to the Secretary of State or his designee for determination of national interest and issuance of a Certificate of National Interest.
