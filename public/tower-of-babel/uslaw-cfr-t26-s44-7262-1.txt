26 C.F.R. § 44.7262-1 — -1 Failure to pay special tax.
Citation: us:cfr:26:44.7262-1
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-44.7262-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 44 (Taxes On Wagering; Effective January 1, 1955). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person liable for the special tax who does any act which makes him liable for such tax, without having paid the tax, is, besides being liable for the tax, subject to a fine of not less than $1,000 and not more than $5,000.
