12 U.S.C. § 1445 — Succession of Federal Home Loan Banks
Citation: us:usc:12:1445
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section1445
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 11 (Federal Home Loan Banks). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each Federal Home Loan Bank shall have succession until dissolved by the Director under this chapter or by further act of Congress.
