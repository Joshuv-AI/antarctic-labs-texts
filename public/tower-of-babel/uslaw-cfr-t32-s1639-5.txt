32 C.F.R. § 1639.5 — Impartiality.

Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1639.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1639 (Classification Of Registrants Preparing For The Ministry). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Boards may not give precedence to any religious organization or school over another, and all are to be given equal consideration.
