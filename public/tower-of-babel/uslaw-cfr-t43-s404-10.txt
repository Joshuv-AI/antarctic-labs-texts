43 C.F.R. § 404.10 — Are there certain types of infrastructure and facilities that may not be included in a rural water supply project?
Citation: us:cfr:43:404.10
Creator: Bureau Of Reclamation, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-404.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 404 (Reclamation Rural Water Supply Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. A rural water supply project may not include:
(a) Any infrastructure or facilities that would deliver water for commercial irrigation; and
(b) The construction of major impoundment structures.
