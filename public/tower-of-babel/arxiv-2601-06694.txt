A Z 3 Z_{3} -symmetric Quantum Chromodynamics
Richard Kerner

Abstract
We propose a description of colour triplets of quarks by entangled Z 3 Z_{3} -graded Lee-Wick type fields, one with real mass and the two remaining ones with mutually conjugate complex masses. This is obtained by attributing colour degrees of freedom to six Pauli spinors, three endowed with colours and three with anti-colours, which are united into one 12 12 -component generalized "coloured Dirac spinor". The so entangled triplet of quark fields satisfies a generalized Dirac equation, with generalized γ \gamma -matrices acting on 12 12 -component coloured spinors. The sixth-order dispersion relations lead to solutions suitably vanishing in asymptotic region, exhibiting the well established confinement property of coloured quarks’ degrees of freedom. We show how one can construct certain cubic combinations of those solutions in a way that cancels the damping factors, producing freely propagating functions.

To obtain a general solution of the colour Dirac equation we should use its Fourier transformed version,

\left(\Gamma^{\mu}\,p_{\mu}-m\;{\mbox{l\hskip-5.50003pt1}}_{12}\right)\;{\hat{\Psi}}(p)=0.

V
Conclusion

In this short overview we tried to convey two points of a more systematic and ambitious program aiming at the construction of enlarged
version of the current QCD entangling the color
SU(3)
symmetry with the Lorentz group. The resulting “colour Dirac equation” can be diagonalzed
only after a sixth-order iteration, leading to propagators having six poles instead of two usual simple poles of the Klein-Gordon equation.
The Lorentz invariance is achieved through a
Z_{3}
-covering of the Lorentz group, as discussed in
RKJL2021
.
Due to the appearance of complex poles in the Fourier transform of propagators, corresponding to complex mass shells, the solutions are damped
and cannot propagate freely on distances greater than a few wavelenghts.

However, we are able to show that certain ternary products of such solutions represent free propagating states, which can be interpreted
as some form of algebraic confinement: single quarks cannot propagate, whereas some tensor products of three quark states can.

Another new result is an explicit calculus in the coordinate space of propagators for massless quarks. It turns out that the Green function
contains a formfactor proportional to the usually admitted quark potential
V(r)\simeq\alpha r^{-1}+\beta r
, which appears naturally
with no need of postulating its form
ab initio
.

We are still far from a genuine field theory, requiring second quantization and operator-valued fields. The
Z_{3}
symmetry should be also employed
in generalized statistical properties, involving not only commutators and anti-commutators, but also ternary relations, as suggested in our previous
articles (see
RKOS
,
RKOS2013
). A generalized Pauli exclusion principle based on such ternary constitutive relations can be found in
Kerner2017
.

The lagrangian variational principle yielding the colour Dirac equation was introduced in (
Kerner2022
), along with the minimal interaction with

SU(3)\times SU(2)\times U(1)
gauge fields. We hope to present more novel results soon (R. Kerner and J. Lukierski, work in progress).
