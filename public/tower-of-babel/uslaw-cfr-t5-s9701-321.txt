5 C.F.R. § 9701.321 — Structure of bands.
Citation: us:cfr:5:9701.321
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security Human Resources Management System (Department Of Homeland Security—Office Of Personnel Management)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-9701.321
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 9701 (Department Of Homeland Security Human Resources Management System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) DHS may, after coordination with OPM, establish ranges of basic pay for bands, with minimum and maximum rates set and adjusted as provided in § 9701.322. Rates must be expressed as annual rates.
(b) For each band within an occupational cluster, DHS will establish a common rate range that applies in all locations.
