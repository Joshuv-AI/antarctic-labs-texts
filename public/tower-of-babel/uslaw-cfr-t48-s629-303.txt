48 C.F.R. § 629.303 — 629.303 Application of State and local taxes to Government contractors and subcontractors.
Citation: us:cfr:48:629.303
Creator: Chapter 6—Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-629.303
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 629 (Taxes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The authority to make the determination prescribed in FAR 29.303(a) is delegated, without power of redelegation, to the head of the contracting activity (see 601.603-70). The Office of the Legal Adviser is the agency-designated counsel for the purposes of FAR 29.303(c).
