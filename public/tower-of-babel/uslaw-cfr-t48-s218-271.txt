48 C.F.R. § 218.271 — 218.271 Use of electronic business tools.
Citation: us:cfr:48:218.271
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-218.271
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 218 (Emergency Acquisitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When supporting a contingency operation or humanitarian or peacekeeping operation, follow the procedures at PGI 218.271 concerning the use of electronic business tools.
