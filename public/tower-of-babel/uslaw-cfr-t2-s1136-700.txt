2 C.F.R. § 1136.700 — Purpose of OAR Article VII.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1136.700
Creator: Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1136.700
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1136 (Other Administrative Requirements: General Award Terms And Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

OAR Article VII addresses post-closeout funding adjustments and recipients' continuing responsibilities after award closeout. It thereby implements OMB guidance in 2 CFR 200.344.
