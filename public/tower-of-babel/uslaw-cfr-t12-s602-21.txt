12 C.F.R. § 602.21 — Production of FCA documents.
Citation: us:cfr:12:602.21
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-602.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 602 (Releasing Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An FCA employee may produce documents only as the Chairman allows.
(b) Before we will release any documents, the requesting party must get an acceptable protective order from the court before which the action is pending that will preserve the confidentiality of the documents to be released.
(c) On request, we may provide certified or authenticated copies of documents.
