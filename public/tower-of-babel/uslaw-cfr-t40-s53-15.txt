40 C.F.R. § 53.15 — Trade secrets and confidential or privileged information.
Citation: us:cfr:40:53.15
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-53.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 53 (Ambient Air Monitoring Reference And Equivalent Methods). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any information submitted under this part that is claimed to be a trade secret or confidential or privileged information shall be marked or otherwise clearly identified as such in the submittal. Information so identified will be treated in accordance with part 2 of this chapter (concerning public information).
