30 C.F.R. § 872.34 — Are there any restrictions on how you may use certified in lieu funds?
Citation: us:cfr:30:872.34
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-872.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 872 (Moneys Available To Eligible States And Indian Tribes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

There are no limitations or restrictions on the use of certified in lieu funds in the Surface Mining Control and Reclamation Act Amendments of 2006 which were enacted as Division C, Title II, Subtitle A of P.L. 109-432.
