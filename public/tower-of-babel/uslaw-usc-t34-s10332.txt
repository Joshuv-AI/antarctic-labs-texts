34 U.S.C. § 10332 — Description of grant program
Citation: us:usc:34:10332
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section10332
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 101 (Justice System Improvement); Subchapter XIII (Grants For Closed-Circuit Televising Of Testimony Of Children Who Are Victims Of Abuse). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director is authorized to make grants to provide equipment and personnel training for the closed-circuit televising and video taping of the testimony of children in criminal proceedings for the violation of laws relating to the abuse of children.
