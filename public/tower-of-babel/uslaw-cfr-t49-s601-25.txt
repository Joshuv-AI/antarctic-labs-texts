49 C.F.R. § 601.25 — Participation by interested persons.
Citation: us:cfr:49:601.25
Creator: Federal Transit Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-601.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 601 (Organization, Functions, And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Any interested person may participate in rulemaking proceedings by submitting comments in writing containing information, views, or arguments.
(b) In his/her discretion, the Administrator may invite any interested person to participate in the rulemaking procedures described in § 601.29.
