34 C.F.R. § 76.761 — Federal funds may pay 100 percent of cost.
Citation: us:cfr:34:76.761
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-76.761
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 76 (State-Administered Formula Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A State or a subgrantee may use program funds to pay up to 100 percent of the cost of a project if:
(a) The State or subgrantee is not required to match the funds; and
(b) The project can be assisted under applicable statutes and regulations.
