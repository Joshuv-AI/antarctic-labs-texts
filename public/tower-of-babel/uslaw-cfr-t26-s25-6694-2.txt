26 C.F.R. § 25.6694-2 — -2 Penalties for understatement due to an unreasonable position.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 25 (Gift Tax; Gifts Made After December 31, 1954). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In general. A person who is a tax return preparer of any return or claim for refund of gift tax under chapter 12 of subtitle B of the Internal Revenue Code (Code) shall be subject to penalties under section 6694(a) of the Code in the manner stated in § 1.6694-2 of this chapter.
(b) Effective/applicability date. This section is applicable to returns and claims for refund filed, and advice provided, after December 31, 2008.
