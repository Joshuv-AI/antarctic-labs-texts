37 C.F.R. § 385.30 — Scope.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 385 (Rates And Terms For Use Of Nondramatic Musical Works In The Making And Distributing Of Physical And Digital Phonorecords). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart establishes rates and terms of royalty payments for Promotional Offerings, Free Trial Offerings, and certain Purchased Content Locker Services provided by subscription and nonsubscription digital music Service Providers in accordance with the provisions of 17 U.S.C. 115.
