7 U.S.C. § 8719 — Period of effectiveness
Citation: us:usc:7:8719
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section8719
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 113 (Agricultural Commodity Support Programs); Subchapter I (Direct Payments And Counter-Cyclical Payments). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This subchapter shall be effective beginning with the 2008 crop year of each covered commodity through the 2012 crop year.
