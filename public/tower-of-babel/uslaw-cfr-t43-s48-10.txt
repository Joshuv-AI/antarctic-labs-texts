43 C.F.R. § 48.10 — What is the Secretary's role in reviewing proposed amendments to the HHCA?
Citation: us:cfr:43:48.10
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-48.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 48 (Amendments To The Hawaiian Homes Commission Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Secretary must review proposed amendments to the Hawaiian Homes Commission Act (HHCA) by the State of Hawai'i to determine whether the proposed amendment requires approval of Congress.
(b) The Secretary will notify the Chairman and Congress of this determination, and if approval is required, submit to Congress the documents required by § 48.35(b).
