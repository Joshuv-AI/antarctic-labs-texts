On commutative invariants for modules over crossed products of minimax nilpotent linear groups
Anatolii V. Tushev

Abstract
Let N N be a minimax nilpotent torsion-free normal subgroup of a soluble group G G of finite rank, R R be a finitely generated commutative domain and R ∗ N R*N be a crossed product of R R and N N . In the paper we construct a correspondence between an R ∗ N R*N -module W W and a finite set M M of equivalent classes of prime ideals minimal over A ​ n ​ n k ​ A ​ ( W / W ​ I ) Ann_{kA}(W/WI) , where k ​ A kA is a group algebra of an abelian minimax group A A and I I is an appropriative G G -invariant ideal of R ​ G RG . It is shown that if W ​ g ≅ W Wg\cong W for all g ∈ g g\in g then the action of the group G G by conjugations on N N can be extended to an action of the group G G on the set M M . The results allow us to apply methods of commutative algebra to the study of W W .
