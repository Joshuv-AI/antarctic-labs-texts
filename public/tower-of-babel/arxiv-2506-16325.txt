Images of toric variety and amplified endomorphism of weak Fano threefolds
Supravat Sarkar

Abstract
. We show that some important classes of weak Fano 3 3 -folds of Picard rank 2 2 do not satisfy Bott vanishing. Using this we show that any smooth projective 3 3 -fold X X of Picard rank 2 2 with − K X -K_{X} nef which is the image of a projective toric variety is toric. This proves a special case of a conjecture by Ochetta-Wisniewski, extending a corresponding previous work for Fano 3 3 -folds. We also show that a weak Fano 3 3 -fold of Picard rank 2 2 having an int-amplified endomorphism is toric. This proves a special case of a conjecture by Fakhrudding, Meng, Zhang and Zhong, extending corresponding previous work for Fano 3 3 -folds.



Keywords
: Toric image, int-amplified endomorphism, Bott vanishing
