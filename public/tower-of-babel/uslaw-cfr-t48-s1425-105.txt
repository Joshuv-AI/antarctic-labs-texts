48 C.F.R. § 1425.105 — 1425.105 Determining reasonableness of cost.

Creator: Chapter 14—Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1425 (Foreign Acquisition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In unusual circumstances, the Director, PAM may authorize the use of evaluation differentials other than those prescribed in FAR 25.105 for a particular acquisition.
(b) Requests for use of other evaluation differentials shall be submitted by the HCA to the Director, PAM for further action.
