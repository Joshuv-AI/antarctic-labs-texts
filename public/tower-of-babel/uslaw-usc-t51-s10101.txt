51 U.S.C. § 10101 — Definitions

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 101 (Definitions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this title:
(1) Administration.—
The term “Administration” means the National Aeronautics and Space Administration.
(2) Administrator.—
The term “Administrator” means the Administrator of the National Aeronautics and Space Administration.
