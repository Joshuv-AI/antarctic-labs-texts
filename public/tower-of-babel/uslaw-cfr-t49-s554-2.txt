49 C.F.R. § 554.2 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:49:554.2
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-554.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 554 (Standards Enforcement And Defects Investigation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to inform interested persons of the procedures followed by the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration in order more fairly and effectively to implement 49 U.S.C. Chapter 301.
