22 U.S.C. § 8811 — Rule of construction
Citation: us:usc:22:8811
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section8811
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 95 (Iran Freedom And Counterproliferation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subtitle or the amendments made by this subtitle shall be construed to limit sanctions imposed with respect to Iran under any other provision of law or to limit the authority of the President to impose additional sanctions with respect to Iran.
