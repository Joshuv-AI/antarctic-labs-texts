22 U.S.C. § 3315 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:22:3315
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section3315
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 48 (Taiwan Relations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In addition to funds otherwise available to carry out the provisions of this chapter, there are authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary of State for the fiscal year 1980 such funds as may be necessary to carry out such provisions. Such funds are authorized to remain available until expended.
