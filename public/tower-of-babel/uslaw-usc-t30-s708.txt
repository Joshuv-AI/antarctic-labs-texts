30 U.S.C. § 708 — Assignments; succession
Citation: us:usc:30:708
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section708
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 20 (Conveyances To Occupants Of Unpatented Mining Claims). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Rights and privileges to qualify as an applicant under this chapter shall not be assignable, but may pass through devise or descent.
