42 U.S.C. § 242q–5 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:42:242q–5
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section242q–5
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter II (General Powers And Duties); Part A (Research And Investigations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of carrying out sections 242q to 242q–5 of this title, there are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary for each of the fiscal years 1991 through 1993.
