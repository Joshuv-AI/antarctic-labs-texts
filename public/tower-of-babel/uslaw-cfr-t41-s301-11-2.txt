41 C.F.R. § 301-11.2 — -11.2 Agency requirement to pay subsistence expenses.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-11.2
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-11.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-11 (11—Subsistence Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agency must pay subsistence expenses (either a per diem allowance or actual expense) unless:
(a) The travel is to a training event under the Government Employees Training Act (5 U.S.C. 4101-4121), and the employee agrees not to be paid subsistence expenses; or
(b) The travel is for a pre-employment interview, and the interviewing agency does not authorize subsistence expense payment.
