46 C.F.R. § 315.11 — Duties of agents.
Citation: us:cfr:46:315.11
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-315.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 315 (Agency Agreements And Appointment Of Agents). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agent must perform all duties prescribed in the service agreement or ship manager contract, as such agreement is applicable, and must follow directions, orders, or regulations issued by MARAD.
