20 C.F.R. § 1010.320 — Will recipients be required to collect information and report on priority of service?
Citation: us:cfr:20:1010.320
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary For Veterans' Employment And Training Service, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-1010.320
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 1010 (Application Of Priority Of Service For Covered Persons). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. Every recipient of funds for qualified job training programs must collect such information, maintain such records, and submit reports containing such information and in such formats as the Secretary may require related to the provision of priority of service.
