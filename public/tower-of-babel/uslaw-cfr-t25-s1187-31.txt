25 C.F.R. § 1187.31 — May OIED award a grant that is duplicative of Federal funding from another source?
Citation: us:cfr:25:1187.31
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary, Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-1187.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 1187 (Indian Business Incubators Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

OIED may not award a grant under the IBIP that is duplicative of existing Federal funding from another source. Duplicative funding means any funding from other Federal grants that would overlap with the IBIP grant for the same activities described in the applicant's IBIP proposal.
