47 C.F.R. § 27.1217 — Competitive bidding procedures for the Broadband Radio Service and the Educational Broadband Service.
Citation: us:cfr:47:27.1217
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-27.1217
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 27 (Miscellaneous Wireless Communications Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Mutually exclusive initial applications for BRS and EBS licenses are subject to competitive bidding. For BRS auctions, the designated entity provisions of § 27.1218 apply. For EBS auctions, the designated entity provisions of § 27.1219 apply. The general competitive bidding procedures set forth in part 1, subpart Q, of this chapter apply unless otherwise provided in this subpart.
