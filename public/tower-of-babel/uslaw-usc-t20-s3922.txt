20 U.S.C. § 3922 — Participation of teachers from private schools
Citation: us:usc:20:3922
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section3922
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 52 (Education For Economic Security); Subchapter I (National Science Foundation Science And Engineering Education). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Foundation shall, after consultation with appropriate private school representatives, make provision for the benefit of teachers in private elementary and secondary schools in the programs authorized by this subchapter, in order to assure equitable participation of such teachers.
