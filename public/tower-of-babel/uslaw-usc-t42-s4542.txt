42 U.S.C. § 4542 — Congressional declaration for utilization of programs under other Federal laws in fields of health and social services
Citation: us:usc:42:4542
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section4542
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 60 (Comprehensive Alcohol Abuse And Alcoholism Prevention, Treatment, And Rehabilitation Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Congress declares that, in addition to the programs under the Comprehensive Alcohol Abuse and Alcoholism Prevention, Treatment, and Rehabilitation Act of 1970 [42 U.S.C. 4541 et seq.], programs under other Federal laws which provide Federal or federally assisted research, prevention, treatment, or rehabilitation in the fields of health and social services should be appropriately utilized to help eradicate alcohol abuse and alcoholism as a major problem.
