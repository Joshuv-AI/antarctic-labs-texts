12 C.F.R. § 1235.1 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1235.1
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1235.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1235 (Record Retention For Regulated Entities And Office Of Finance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to set forth minimum requirements for a record retention program for each regulated entity and the Office of Finance. The requirements are intended to further prudent management as well as to ensure that complete and accurate records of each regulated entity and the Office of Finance are readily accessible to FHFA.
