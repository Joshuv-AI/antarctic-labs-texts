14 C.F.R. § 136.73 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:14:136.73
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-136.73
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 136 (Commercial Air Tours And National Parks Air Tour Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the purposes of this subpart:
Air tour means any sightseeing flight conducted under visual flight rules in an airplane, powered-lift, or rotorcraft for compensation or hire.
Air tour operator means any person who conducts an air tour.
