In the Discussion section, the citation in the sentence beginning “Byrsell et al. first supported this time window…” should be corrected from [20] to [25].

In addition, the citation in the sentence beginning “Dispatchers frequently encounter various barriers to early OHCA recognition…” should be corrected from [18,24–26] to [18,24,26].

The citation [16] referring to the “two-question protocol” in the Methods section remains unchanged, as Reference 16 now correctly corresponds to Lerner et al.

Further errors were identified in the Abstract and Fig. 2. In the Results section of the Abstract, the 95% confidence interval for the adjusted odds ratio for ROSC in the overall cohort was incorrectly reported as “adjusted odds ratio [aOR] per 10-s increase, 1.01; 95% confidence interval [CI], 1.00–1.02”.

The correct value is “adjusted odds ratio [aOR] per 10-s increase, 1.01; 95% confidence interval [CI], 1.01–1.02”, consistent with the Results section and Table 2.

In Fig. 2, the P value for non-linearity was incorrectly presented as 0.16. The correct value is 0.19, as correctly reported in the Results section. The original statistical analysis was reconfirmed and verified that 0.19 is the correct value. Fig. 2 has therefore been corrected accordingly.

These corrections do not affect the statistical analyses, interpretation, or conclusions of the study.

The original article has been updated.
