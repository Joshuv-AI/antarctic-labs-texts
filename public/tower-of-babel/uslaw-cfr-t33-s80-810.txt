33 C.F.R. § 80.810 — Cape San Blas, FL to Perdido Bay, FL.
Citation: us:cfr:33:80.810
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-80.810
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 80 (Colregs Demarcation Lines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A line drawn from St. Joseph Bay Entrance Range A Rear Light through St. Joseph Bay Entrance Range B Front Light to St. Joseph Point.
(b) A line drawn across the mouth of Salt Creek as an extension of the general trend of the shoreline to continue across the inlet to St. Andrews sound in the middle of Crooked Island.
(c) A line drawn between the seaward end of the St. Andrews Bay Entrance Jetties.
(d) A line drawn between the seaward end of the Choctawatchee Bay Entrance Jetties.
(e) An east-west line drawn from Fort McRee Leading Light across the Pensacola Bay Entrance along latitude 30°19.5′ N.
(f) A line drawn between the seaward end of the Perdido Pass Jetties.
