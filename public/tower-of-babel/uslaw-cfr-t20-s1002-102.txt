20 C.F.R. § 1002.102 — Does the five-year service limit include periods of service that the employee performed before USERRA was enacted?
Citation: us:cfr:20:1002.102
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary For Veterans' Employment And Training Service, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-1002.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 1002 (Regulations Under The Uniformed Services Employment And Reemployment Rights Act Of 1994). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It depends. USERRA provides reemployment rights to which an employee may become entitled beginning on or after December 12, 1994, but any uniformed service performed before December 12, 1994, that was counted against the service limitations of the previous law (the Veterans Reemployment Rights Act), also counts against USERRA's five-year limit.
