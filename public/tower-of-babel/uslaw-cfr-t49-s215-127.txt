49 C.F.R. § 215.127 — Defective draft arrangement.
Citation: us:cfr:49:215.127
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-215.127
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 215 (Railroad Freight Car Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A railroad may not place or continue in service a car, if—
(a) The car has a draft gear that is inoperative;
(b) The car has a broken yoke;
(c) A vertical coupler pin retainer plate—
(1) Is missing (except by design); or
(2) Has a missing fastener;
(d) The car has a draft key, or draft key retainer, that is—
(1) Inoperative; or
(2) Missing; or
(e) The car has a missing or broken follower plate.
