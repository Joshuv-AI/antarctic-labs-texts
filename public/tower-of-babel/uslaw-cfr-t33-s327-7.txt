33 C.F.R. § 327.7 — Representation.
Citation: us:cfr:33:327.7
Creator: Corps Of Engineers, Department Of The Army, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-327.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 327 (Public Hearings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At the public hearing, any person may appear on his own behalf, or may be represented by counsel, or by other representatives.
