36 U.S.C. § 220106 — Exclusive right to name, seals, emblems, and badges
Citation: us:usc:36:220106
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section220106
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 2201 (United Service Organizations, Incorporated). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation and its councils, organizations, chapters, and affiliates have the exclusive right to use the names “United Service Organizations, Incorporated” and “USO” and seals, emblems, and badges the corporation adopts.
