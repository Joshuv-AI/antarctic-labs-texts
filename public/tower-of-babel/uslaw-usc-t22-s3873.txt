22 U.S.C. § 3873 — Exemption from Metric Conversion Act of 1975
Citation: us:usc:22:3873
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section3873
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 51 (Panama Canal); Subchapter III (General Provisions); Part 2 (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commission is exempt from the provisions of the Metric Conversion Act of 1975 (15 U.S.C. 205a et seq.).
