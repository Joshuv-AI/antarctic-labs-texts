42 U.S.C. § 14321 — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:42:14321
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section14321
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 137 (Management Of Rechargeable Batteries And Batteries Containing Mercury); Subchapter II (Recycling Of Rechargeable Batteries). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of this subchapter is to facilitate the efficient recycling or proper disposal of used nickel-cadmium rechargeable batteries, used small sealed lead-acid rechargeable batteries, other regulated batteries, and such rechargeable batteries in used consumer products, by—
(1)
providing for uniform labeling requirements and streamlined regulatory requirements for regulated battery collection programs; and
(2)
encouraging voluntary industry programs by eliminating barriers to funding the collection and recycling or proper disposal of used rechargeable batteries.
