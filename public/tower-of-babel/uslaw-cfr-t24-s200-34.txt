24 C.F.R. § 200.34 — Property and mortgage assessment.
Citation: us:cfr:24:200.34
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-200.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 200 (Introduction To Fha Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirements set forth in 24 CFR part 200, subpart E, regarding the mortgagor's responsibility for making those investigations, analysis and inspections it deems necessary for protecting its interests in the property apply to these programs.
