7 C.F.R. § 987.38 — Handlers of record.
Citation: us:cfr:7:987.38
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-987.38
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 987 (Domestic Dates Produced Or Packed In Riverside County, California). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each crop year but no later than October 10 for continuing handlers and prior to handling dates in the case of new handlers, any person desiring to handle dates shall submit a report to the Committee on a form prescribed by it containing the following information with respect to all dates which such person expects to handle:
(a) The name and address of each producer;
(b) The location of each date garden; and
(c) The acreage and estimated current season's production thereon.
