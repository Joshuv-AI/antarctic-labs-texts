25 U.S.C. § 1463 — Repayment of loan; financing from other sources
Citation: us:usc:25:1463
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section1463
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 17 (Financing Economic Development Of Indians And Indian Organizations); Subchapter I (Indian Revolving Loan Fund). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Loans may be made only when, in the judgment of the Secretary, there is a reasonable prospect of repayment, and only to applicants who in the opinion of the Secretary are unable to obtain financing from other sources on reasonable terms and conditions.
