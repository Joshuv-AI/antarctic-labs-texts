2 C.F.R. § 1128.325 — Fee or profit.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1128.325
Creator: Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1128.325
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1128 (Recipient Financial And Program Management: General Award Terms And Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Requirement. A DoD Component's general terms and conditions must specify that recipients may neither receive fee or profit nor pay fee or profit to subrecipients.
(b) Award terms and conditions. A DoD Component must use the wording appendix C to this part provides for Section D of FMS Article III to specify the limitation on payment of fee or profit.
