[A(\omega^{\prime})]^{-1}A(\omega)

m_{\omega}

m_{\omega}=m_{\omega^{\prime}},\quad\text{whenever}\quad\omega_{-1}=\omega_{-1}^{\prime}\quad\text{and}\quad\omega_{0}=\omega^{\prime}_{0}.

A(\omega)m_{\omega}=m_{T\omega}

implies

m_{T\omega^{\prime}}=m_{T\omega}
whenever
\omega_{-1}=\omega^{\prime}_{-1}
and
\omega_{0}=\omega^{\prime}_{0}
.

This property can be formulated in terms of the cylinder sets, of the form

[n;j_{0},j_{1}]=\{\omega\in\Omega:\quad\omega_{n+i}=j_{i},\quad 0\leq i\leq 1\}

with
n\in{\mathbb{Z}}
and
j_{0},j_{1}\in{\mathcal{A}}

j_{0},j_{1}
of symbols in
\mathcal{A}
, the function
\omega\to m_{\omega}

Let us now give a condition that makes the latter property impossible.
First, define
{\mathcal{W}}_{2}
as the set of words
(j,j^{\prime})\in{\mathcal{A}}\times{\mathcal{A}}
of length two that are allowed in
\Omega
:

{\mathcal{W}}_{2}=\{(j,j^{\prime})\in{\mathcal{A}}\times{\mathcal{A}}:\exists\omega\in\Omega\,\,\text{such that}\,\,\omega_{-1}=j,\,\omega_{0}=j^{\prime}\}.

(j_{-2},j_{-1})\in{\mathcal{W}}_{2}

\tilde{\omega}\in[-1;j_{-1}j_{0}]\cap[-2;j_{-2},j_{-1}]

and the property that the function

is constant on each of these cylinders.

depends only on

m_{\omega}=m_{\omega^{\prime}}\qquad\text{whenever}\quad\omega_{-1}=\omega^{\prime}_{-1}.

{\mathcal{A}}
