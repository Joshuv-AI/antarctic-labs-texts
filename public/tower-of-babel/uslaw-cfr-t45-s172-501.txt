45 C.F.R. § 172.501 — QHIN self-termination.
Citation: us:cfr:45:172.501
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-172.501
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 172 (Trusted Exchange Framework And Common Agreement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A QHIN may terminate its own Designation at any time without cause by providing ninety (90) calendar days prior written notice.
