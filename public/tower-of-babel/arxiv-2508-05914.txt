Detecting Traces of Light-Quark Yukawa Couplings to the Higgs Boson in Fragmentation Products Preprint: Nikhef 2025-010
Johannes K. L. Michel

Abstract
We point out that Yukawa interactions of light quarks with the Higgs boson are imprinted as unique azimuthal modulations in the density of fragmentation hadrons relative to the Higgs p T p_{T} . We introduce Yukawa Fragmentation Asymmetries (YFAs), interference observables that are linearly proportional to real (Standard Model) or CP-odd Yukawa couplings, respectively. The chiral suppression is lifted nonperturbatively by chiral-odd multi-hadron fragmentation functions. As a case study, we consider V ​ H VH production with a tagged target fragmentation hadron at the HL-LHC. We estimate that the YFAs from this single example process are already competitive with current projected HL-LHC sensitivities for second-generation couplings, and outperform them by a factor of 2-3 for first-generation couplings. Our results point to deep synergies between precision studies of confinement and the in-depth exploration of the Higgs sector.

We are interested in constraining the parameters

III
Key properties

Eqs. (
3
) and (
4
), apart from their great experimental simplicity,
have several theoretical properties
Michel 2025

that make them particularly powerful probes of light-quark Yukawa couplings:

(A)

Forming an asymmetry linear in
p_{T}^{h}

at the nonperturbative scale, which is an intrinsically chiral-odd, fermionic phenomenon,

uniquely
picks out a single chiral-odd contribution at the hard scale
to all orders in perturbation theory and at leading power in
\Lambda_{\mathrm{QCD}}/Q

The only such term in the SM is Eq. (
2

(B)

The quark and antiquark terms feature a relative sign.
This property is phenomenologically critical,
since it means that sea (anti)quark contributions can cancel,
leaving behind the valence quark contributions of interest.
It is likewise stable to all orders,
and can be understood by noting that the SM Lagrangian (approximately)
preserves CP, while the
\sin\varphi
modulation is P odd.

(C)

Forming an asymmetry
\mathrm{\tilde{Y}FA}_{h,V}

with respect to
p_{h}^{x}
instead,
one arrives at a signal prediction of the same form as Eq. (
4

and with identical coefficient

,
but with the
y_{q}
replaced by
{\tilde{y}}_{q}
.
Therefore all of our sensitivity estimates for
y_{q}/y_{q}^{\mathrm{SM}}

in this letter
immediately carry over
to possible CP-odd
{\tilde{y}}_{q}
in units of
y_{q}^{\mathrm{SM}}
.

(D)

While the BMFrF is an
a priori
unknown nonperturbative matrix element,
it can readily be extracted using azimuthal correlations
between
two
target fragmentation hadrons,
one in each beam direction, with chiral-even hard probes.
These include high-statistics SM baseline processes like Drell-Yan production.
In light of the present work,
we strongly encourage our experimental colleagues
to initiate differential measurements
of such azimuthal hadron-hadron correlations.
Pending clarification of its universality
Collins 1998
,
the BMFrF may also be extracted in a clean fashion
at the future EIC
Abdul Khalek et al. 2022

VI
Conclusions

We have introduced Yukawa Fragmentation Asymmetries (YFAs),
a new class of observables that combine linear sensitivity,
theoretical control, and experimental simplicity
into a uniquely powerful probe of light-quark Yukawa couplings to the Higgs boson.
The observable pulls out all the stops of the Standard Model
to approach these challenging measurement targets,
drawing on confinement, chiral symmetry breaking,
the valence/sea structure of hadrons, and weak parity violation.
As part of recent renewed interest in interdisciplinary applications
of multi-hadron fragmentation functions

VI.2
Estimated degrees of transverse polarization

For the light/light DOPs, we have two doubly-favored flavor combinations

P_{d\pi^{+}}=P_{u\pi^{-}}=P_{\mathrm{fav}^{2}}

where the “quark hole” has the quantum numbers of the pion valence antiquark
and the pion valence quark is already present in the proton.
For these cases we take
P_{\mathrm{fav}^{2}}=1
.
We stress that even for an estimate of
P_{qh}=1
,
the ratio
h^{\perp}_{qh}/f_{qh}\sim P_{qh}/n_{h}\approx 0.1

is still far from its positivity bound
\leq 1
,
and well below
\lesssim f_{\pi}/m_{p}
.
We further have two singly-favored cases
P_{{\bar{u}}\pi^{+}}=P_{{\bar{d}}\pi^{-}}=P_{\mathrm{fav}}=0.5
,
where only the “quark hole” quantum numbers agree,
but an antiquark is required from the remnant.
Other combinations of
u
and
d
(anti)quarks with
\pi^{\pm}
are disfavored,
P_{\mathrm{dis}}=0.1
.

For heavier quarks
Q=s,c,b
, it is useful to separately consider the total (symmetrized)
DOP
\bar{P}_{Qh}\equiv(P_{Qh}+P_{{\bar{Q}}h})/2
and the sea-quark asymmetry

\Delta P_{Qh}\equiv(P_{Qh}-P_{{\bar{Q}}h})/2
.
For the strange/pion DOP we take
\bar{P}_{sh}=P_{\mathrm{dis}}(1\pm 3)

and, to be conservative, the extreme case
\Delta P_{sh}=(0\pm 1)\bar{P}_{sh}
.
To estimate the heavy/pion DOPs with
Q=c,b
, we work to leading power in
\Lambda_{\mathrm{QCD}}\ll m_{Q}

and find, using techniques from Ref.
von Kuk et al. 2023
,

\displaystyle\bar{P}_{Q\pi^{\pm}}=\frac{\Lambda^{(3)}_{g\pi^{\pm}}}{m_{Q}}(1\pm 3)\,,\qquad\Delta P_{Q\pi^{\pm}}=\frac{\alpha_{s}(m_{Q})}{\pi}\frac{\Lambda^{(3)}_{g\pi^{\pm}}}{m_{Q}}(0\pm 5)\,,

(S4)

where
\Lambda^{(3)}_{g\pi^{\pm}}=0.3\,\mathrm{GeV}
is a twist-3 gluon/pion FrF.
We note that while the matching onto this twist-3 FrF
will involve an additional power of
\alpha_{s}(m_{Q})/\pi
,
the latter drops out since the DOPs are defined relative to
f_{Q}\sim(\alpha_{s}/\pi)\,f_{g}
.
The estimate for the sea asymmetry follows by noting
that perturbatively, heavy quarks and antiquarks
only become distinguished by higher-order color structures like
d_{abc}\,d_{abc}
.

For the case of reconstructed kaons and
D
mesons,
we simply set the first-generation DOPs to zero,

P_{qH}=P_{{\bar{q}}H}=0
,
where
H=K^{\pm},D^{\pm}
and
q=u,d
.
The strange/kaon DOPs are

P_{sK^{+}}=P_{\mathrm{fav}^{2}}
,

P_{{\bar{s}}K^{-}}=P_{\mathrm{fav}}
,
and
P_{sK^{-}}=P_{{\bar{s}}K^{+}}=P_{\mathrm{dis}}
.
For the heavy/kaon DOPs and sea asymmetries involving
Q=c,b

we again use Eq. (
S4
) with
\Lambda^{(3)}_{gK^{\pm}}=0.3\,\mathrm{GeV}
as its central value.
For signal charm mesons
D^{+}
, estimates for all BMFrFs
are essentially perturbative
von Kuk et al. 2023
.
At leading power in
\Lambda_{\mathrm{QCD}}/m_{c}

they only involve the twist-2 gluon PDF
f_{g}\sim f_{u,d}

and an
\mathcal{O}(1)

\chi_{D^{\pm}}
encoding the probability for a free
c

into the experimentally selected set of
D

\displaystyle P_{{\bar{c}}D^{+}}

\displaystyle=P_{cD^{-}}=\frac{\alpha_{s}(m_{c})}{\pi}\,,\qquad P_{cD^{+}}

\displaystyle=P_{{\bar{c}}D^{-}}=P_{sD^{+}}=P_{sD^{-}}=\frac{\alpha_{s}^{2}(m_{c})}{\pi^{2}}\,,

\displaystyle\bar{P}_{bD^{\pm}}

\displaystyle=\frac{\alpha_{s}^{2}(m_{b})}{\pi^{2}}(1\pm 3)\,,\qquad\Delta P_{bD^{\pm}}

\displaystyle=\frac{\alpha_{s}^{3}(m_{b})}{\pi^{3}}(0\pm 5)\,.

(S5)

Here the leading contribution to the valence BMFrF starts at

\alpha_{s}/\pi
