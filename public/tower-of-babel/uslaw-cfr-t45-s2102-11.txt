45 C.F.R. § 2102.11 — Scope and content of submissions for proposed medals, insignia, coins, seals, and the like.
Citation: us:cfr:45:2102.11
Creator: Commission Of Fine Arts
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2102.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2102 (Meetings And Procedures Of The Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each submission of the design for a proposed item which is within the Commission's purview under § 2101.1 (d) should identify the sponsoring government unit and disclose the uses and purpose of the item, the size and forms in which it will be produced, and the materials and finishes to be used, including colors if any, along with a sketch, model, or prototype.
