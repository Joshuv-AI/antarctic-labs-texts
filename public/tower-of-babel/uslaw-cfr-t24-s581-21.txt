24 C.F.R. § 581.21 — Compliance inspections and reports.
Citation: us:cfr:24:581.21
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Community Planning And Development, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-581.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 581 (Use Of Federal Real Property To Assist The Homeless). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Transferees are required to allow HHS to conduct compliance inspections and to submit such compliance reports and actions as are deemed necessary by HHS. At a minimum, the transferee will be required to submit an annual utilization report regarding the operation and maintenance of the property, including current images of the entire property and such information as HHS shall require.
