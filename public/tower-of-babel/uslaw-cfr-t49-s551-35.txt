49 C.F.R. § 551.35 — Subscription of communications.
Citation: us:cfr:49:551.35
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-551.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 551 (Procedural Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each communication shall be signed in ink and shall disclose the full legal name and the address of the person signing it and, if he is an agent, of his principal.
