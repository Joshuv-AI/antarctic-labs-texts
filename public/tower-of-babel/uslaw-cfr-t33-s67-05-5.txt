33 C.F.R. § 67.05-5 — -5 Multiple obstruction lights.
Citation: us:cfr:33:67.05-5
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-67.05-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 67 (Aids To Navigation On Artificial Islands And Fixed Structures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When more than one obstruction light is required by this part to mark a structure, all such lights shall be operated to flash in unison.
