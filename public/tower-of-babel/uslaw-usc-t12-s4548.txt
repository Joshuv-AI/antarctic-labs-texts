12 U.S.C. § 4548 — Regulations for use of credit scores
Citation: us:usc:12:4548
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section4548
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 46 (Government Sponsored Enterprises); Subchapter I (Supervision And Regulation Of Enterprises); Part B (Additional Authorities Of The Director); Subpart 1 (General Authority). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director shall—
(1)
by regulation, establish standards and criteria for any process used by an enterprise to validate and approve credit scoring models pursuant to section 1717(b)(7) of this title and section 1454(d) of this title; and
(2)
ensure that any credit scoring model that is validated and approved by an enterprise under section 1717(b)(7) of this title or section 1454(d) of this title meets the requirements of clauses (i), (ii), and (iii) of section 1717(b)(7)(C) of this title and subparagraphs (A), (B), and (C) of section 1454(d) of this title, respectively.
