27 C.F.R. § 555.43 — Permit fees.
Citation: us:cfr:27:555.43
Creator: Bureau Of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms, And Explosives, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-555.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 555 (Commerce In Explosives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each applicant must pay a fee for obtaining a permit as follows:
(1) User—$100 for a three-year period.
(2) User-limited (nonrenewable)—$75.
(3) Limited—$25 for a one-year period.
(b)(1) Each applicant for renewal of a user permit must pay a fee of $50 for a three-year period.
(2) Each applicant for renewal of a limited permit must pay a fee of $12 for a one-year period.
