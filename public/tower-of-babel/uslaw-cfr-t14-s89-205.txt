14 C.F.R. § 89.205 — Eligibility.
Citation: us:cfr:14:89.205
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-89.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 89 (Remote Identification Of Unmanned Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Only the following persons are eligible to apply for the establishment of an FAA-recognized identification area under this subpart:
(a) A community-based organization recognized by the Administrator.
(b) An educational institution, including primary and secondary educational institutions, trade schools, colleges, and universities.
