42 U.S.C. § 1437bbb–9 — Termination and evaluation
Citation: us:usc:42:1437bbb–9
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1437bbb–9
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 8 (Low-Income Housing); Subchapter II–B (Home Rule Flexible Grant Demonstration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Termination
The demonstration program under this subchapter shall terminate not less than 2 and not more than 5 years after the date on which the demonstration program is commenced.
(b) Evaluation
Not later than 6 months after the termination of the demonstration program under this subchapter, the Secretary shall submit to the Congress a final report, which shall include—
(1)
an evaluation 1 the effectiveness of the activities carried out under the demonstration program; and
(2)
any findings and recommendations of the Secretary for any appropriate legislative action.
