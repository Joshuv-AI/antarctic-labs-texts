34 U.S.C. § 60909 — Multiple agencies
Citation: us:usc:34:60909
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section60909
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 609 (Homicide Victims’ Families’ Rights). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the case that more than one agency conducted the initial investigation of a cold case murder, each agency shall coordinate their case file review or full reinvestigation such that there is only one joint case file review or full reinvestigation occurring at a time in compliance with section 60901(f) or 60903(d) of this title, as applicable.
