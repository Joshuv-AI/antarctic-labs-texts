10 U.S.C. § 20101 — Members in Space Force active status: amount of annual training or active duty service required
Citation: us:usc:10:20101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section20101
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Space Force); Chapter 2003 (Status And Participation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as specifically provided in regulations prescribed by the Secretary of Defense, a member of the Space Force in a space force active status who is not serving on sustained duty shall be required to—
(1)
participate in at least 48 scheduled drills or training periods during each year and serve on active duty for not less than 14 days (exclusive of travel time) during each year; or
(2)
serve on active duty for training for not more than 30 days during each year.
