2 C.F.R. § 183.25 — Responsibilities of recipients.
Citation: us:cfr:2:183.25
Creator: Office Of Management And Budget Government-Wide Guidance For Federal Financial Assistance
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-183.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 183 (Never Contract With The Enemy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Recipients of covered grants or cooperative agreements must fulfill the requirements outlined in the award term provided in Appendix A to this part.
(b) Recipients must also flow down the provisions in award terms covered in Appendix A to this part to all contracts and subawards under the award.
