43 C.F.R. § 3173.19 — Effective date of a commingling and allocation approval.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3173.19
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3173.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3170 (Onshore Oil And Gas Production). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If the BLM approves a CAA, the effective date of the CAA is the first day of the month following first production through the FMPs for the CAA.
(b) If the BLM approves a modification, the effective date is the first day of the month following approval of the modification.
(c) A CAA does not modify any of the terms of the leases, units, or CAs covered by the CAA.
