On string theory on deformed BTZ and T ​ T ¯ + J ​ T ¯ + T ​ J ¯ T\bar{T}+J\bar{T}+T\bar{J}


Abstract
Aspects of superstring theory on black-strings backgrounds, corresponding to deformed BTZ black holes, formed near k k N ​ S ​ 5 NS5 branes by p p fundamental strings, and single-trace T ​ T ¯ + J ​ T ¯ + T ​ J ¯ T\bar{T}+J\bar{T}+T\bar{J} holography, are presented. It is shown that for a particular asymptotic value of the B B -field, the excitation energy of a long string plus its contribution to the energy of the black hole is that in T ​ T ¯ + J ​ T ¯ + T ​ J ¯ T\bar{T}+J\bar{T}+T\bar{J} deformed C ​ F ​ T 2 CFT_{2} with c = 6 ​ k c=6k . The excitation energy of a winding w > 1 w>1 long string plus its contribution to the background, a w / p w/p fraction of the black-hole energy, evolves according to that in a Z w Z_{w} twisted sector of a p p -fold symmetric product of the latter.
