49 C.F.R. § 299.1 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:49:299.1
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-299.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 299 (Texas Central Railroad High-Speed Rail Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes minimum Federal safety standards for the high-speed transportation system described in detail in § 299.13, known as Texas Central Railroad, LLC and hereinafter referred to as the “railroad.” The purpose of this part is to prevent accidents, casualties, and property damage which could result from operation of this system.
