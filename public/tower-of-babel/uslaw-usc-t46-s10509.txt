46 U.S.C. § 10509 — Penalty for failing to begin voyage
Citation: us:usc:46:10509
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section10509
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part G (Merchant Seamen Protection And Relief); Chapter 105 (Coastwise Voyages). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
A seaman who fails to be on board at the time contained in the agreement required by section 10502 of this title, without having given 24 hours’ notice of inability to do so, shall forfeit, for each hour’s lateness, one-half of one day’s pay to be deducted from the seaman’s wages if the lateness is recorded in the official logbook on the date of the violation.
(b)
A seaman who does not report at all or subsequently deserts forfeits all wages.
(c)
This section does not apply to a fishing or whaling vessel or a yacht.
