9 C.F.R. § 416.11 — General rules.
Citation: us:cfr:9:416.11
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-416.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 416 (Sanitation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each official establishment shall develop, implement, and maintain written standard operating procedures for sanitation (Sanitation SOP's) in accordance with the requirements of this part.
