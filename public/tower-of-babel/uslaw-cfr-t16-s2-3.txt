16 C.F.R. § 2.3 — Policy as to private controversies.
Citation: us:cfr:16:2.3
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-2.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 2 (Nonadjudicative Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission acts only in the public interest and does not initiate an investigation or take other action when the alleged violation of law is merely a matter of private controversy and does not tend adversely to affect the public.
