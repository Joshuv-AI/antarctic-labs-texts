40 C.F.R. § 16.2 — Definitions.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 16 (Implementation Of Privacy Act Of 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in this part:
(a) The terms individual, maintain, record, and system of records have the same meanings as specified in 5 U.S.C. 552a.
(b) EPA means the Environmental Protection Agency.
(c) Working days means calendar days excluding Saturdays, Sundays, and Federal holidays.
