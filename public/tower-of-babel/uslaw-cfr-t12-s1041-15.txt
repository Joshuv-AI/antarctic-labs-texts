12 C.F.R. § 1041.15 — Effective and compliance dates.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1041.15
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1041.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1041 (Payday, Vehicle Title, And Certain High-Cost Installment Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Effective date. The effective date of this part is January 16, 2018.
(b) April 16, 2018 application deadline. The deadline to submit an application for preliminary approval for registration pursuant to § 1041.11(c)(1) is April 16, 2018.
(c) August 19, 2019 compliance date. The compliance date for §§ 1041.2, 1041.3, 1041.7 through 1041.9, 1041.12(a), (b) introductory text and (b)(4) and (5), and 1041.13 is August 19, 2019.
