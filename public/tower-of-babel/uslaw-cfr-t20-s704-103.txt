20 C.F.R. § 704.103 — Removal of certain minimums when computing or paying compensation.
Citation: us:cfr:20:704.103
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-704.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 704 (Special Provisions For Lhwca Extensions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The minimum limitation on weekly compensation for disability established by section 6 of the LHWCA, 33 U.S.C. 906, and the minimum limit on the average weekly wages on which death benefits are to be computed under section 9 of the LHWCA, 33 U.S.C. 909, shall not apply in computing compensation and death benefits under this Act; section 2(a), 42 U.S.C. 1652(a).
