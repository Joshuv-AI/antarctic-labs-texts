48 C.F.R. § 3001.403 — 3001.403 Individual deviations.
Citation: us:cfr:48:3001.403
Creator: Chapter 30—Department Of Homeland Security, Homeland Security Acquisition Regulation (Hsar)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-3001.403
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 3001 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless precluded by law, executive order, or other regulation, the HCA is authorized to approve individual deviation (except with respect to (FAR) 48 CFR 30.201-3, 30.201-4; the requirements of the Cost Accounting Standards board rules and regulations at 48 CFR chapter 99 (FAR appendix); and part 50). Submit requests per (HSAR) 48 CFR 3001.7000, including complete documentation of the justification for the deviations (See HSAM 3001.403).
