14 C.F.R. § 1221.202 — Description of the medal.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1221 (The Nasa Seal And Other Devices, And The Congressional Space Medal Of Honor). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The description of the Congressional Space Medal of Honor, which was designed by the Institute of Heraldry, U.S. Army, is set forth in appendix A to this subpart. Each person awarded the Medal also shall receive a citation describing the basis for the award.
