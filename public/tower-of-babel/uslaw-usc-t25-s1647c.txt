25 U.S.C. § 1647c — General exceptions

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 18 (Indian Health Care); Subchapter III–A (Access To Health Services). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The requirements of this subchapter shall not apply to any excepted benefits described in paragraph (1)(A) or (3) of section 300gg–91(c) of title 42.
