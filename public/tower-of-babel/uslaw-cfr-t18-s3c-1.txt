18 C.F.R. § 3c.1 — c.1 Cross-reference to employee ethical conduct standards and financial disclosure regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:18:3c.1
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-3c.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 3c (PART 3c—STANDARDS OF CONDUCT). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (Commission) are subject to the executive branch-wide financial disclosure regulations at 5 CFR part 2634, the Standards of Ethical Conduct for Employees of the Executive Branch at 5 CFR part 2635, the Commission regulations at 5 CFR part 3401 which supplement the Standards of Ethical Conduct, and the executive branch-wide employee responsibilities and conduct regulation at 5 CFR part 735.
