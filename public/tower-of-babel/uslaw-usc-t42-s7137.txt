42 U.S.C. § 7137 — Functions of Comptroller General
Citation: us:usc:42:7137
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section7137
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 84 (Department Of Energy); Subchapter II (Establishment Of Department). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The functions of the Comptroller General of the United States under section 771 of title 15 shall apply with respect to the monitoring and evaluation of all functions and activities of the Department under this chapter or any other Act administered by the Department.
