13 C.F.R. § 128.301 — Where must an application be filed?
Citation: us:cfr:13:128.301
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-128.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 128 (Veteran Small Business Certification Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An application for certification as a VOSB or SDVOSB must be electronically filed according to the instructions on SBA's website at www.sba.gov. The qualifying veteran must take responsibility for the accuracy of all information submitted on behalf of the applicant.
