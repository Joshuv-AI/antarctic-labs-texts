5 U.S.C. § 8322 — Effect on other statutes
Citation: us:usc:5:8322
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section8322
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart G (Insurance And Annuities); Chapter 83 (Retirement); Subchapter II (Forfeiture Of Annuities And Retired Pay). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This subchapter does not restrict authority under a statute, other than this subchapter, to deny or withhold benefits authorized by statute.
