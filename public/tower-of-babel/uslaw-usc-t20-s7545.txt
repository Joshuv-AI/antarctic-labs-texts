20 U.S.C. § 7545 — Administrative provisions
Citation: us:usc:20:7545
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section7545
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 70 (Strengthening And Improvement Of Elementary And Secondary Schools); Subchapter VI (Indian, Native Hawaiian, And Alaska Native Education); Part C (Alaska Native Education). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not more than 5 percent of funds provided to an award recipient under this part for any fiscal year may be used for administrative purposes.
