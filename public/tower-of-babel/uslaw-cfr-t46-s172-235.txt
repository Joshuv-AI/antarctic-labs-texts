46 C.F.R. § 172.235 — Extent of damage.
Citation: us:cfr:46:172.235
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-172.235
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 172 (Special Rules Pertaining To Bulk Cargoes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the purpose of the calculations required in § 172.225—
(a) Design calculations must include both side and bottom damage, applied separately; and
(b) Damage must consist of the penetrations having the dimensions given in Table 172.235 except that, if the most disabling penetrations would be less than the penetrations described in this paragraph, the smaller penetration must be assumed.
