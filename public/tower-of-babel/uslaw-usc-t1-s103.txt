1 U.S.C. § 103 — Enacting or resolving words after first section
Citation: us:usc:1:103
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title1-section103
Context: United States Code, Title 1 (General Provisions); Chapter 2 (Acts And Resolutions; Formalities Of Enactment; Repeals; Sealing Of Instruments). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No enacting or resolving words shall be used in any section of an Act or resolution of Congress except in the first.
