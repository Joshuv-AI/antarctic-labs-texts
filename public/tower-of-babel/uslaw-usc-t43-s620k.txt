43 U.S.C. § 620k — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:43:620k
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section620k
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 12B (Colorado River Storage Project). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated, out of any moneys in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, such sums as may be required to carry out the purposes of this chapter, but not to exceed $760,000,000.
