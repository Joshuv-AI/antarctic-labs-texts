48 U.S.C. § 910b — Ratification of prior acts
Citation: us:usc:48:910b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section910b
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 4 (Puerto Rico); Subchapter VI (Slum Clearance And Urban Redevelopment Projects). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All legislation heretofore enacted by the Legislature of the Territory of Puerto Rico dealing with the subject matter of this Act and not inconsistent herewith is ratified and confirmed.
