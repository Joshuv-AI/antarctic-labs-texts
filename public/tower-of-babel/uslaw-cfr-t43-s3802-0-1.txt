43 C.F.R. § 3802.0-1 — -1 Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3802.0-1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3802.0-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3800 (Mining Claims Under The General Mining Laws). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this subpart is to establish procedures to prevent impairment of the suitability of lands under wilderness review for inclusion in the wilderness system and to prevent unnecessary or undue degradation by activities authorized by the U.S. Mining Laws and provide for environmental protection of the public lands and resources.
