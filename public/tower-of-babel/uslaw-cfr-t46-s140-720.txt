46 C.F.R. § 140.720 — Navigation lights, shapes, and sound signals.
Citation: us:cfr:46:140.720
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-140.720
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 140 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each towing vessel must be equipped with navigation lights, shapes, and sound signals in accordance with the International Regulations for Prevention of Collisions at Sea (COLREGS) or 33 CFR part 84 as appropriate to its area of operation.
