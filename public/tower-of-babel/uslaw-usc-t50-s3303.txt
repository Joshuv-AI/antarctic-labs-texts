50 U.S.C. § 3303 — Construction of intelligence community facilities; Presidential authorization
Citation: us:usc:50:3303
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section3303
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 45 (Miscellaneous Intelligence Community Authorities); Subchapter I (Budget And Oversight). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
No project for the construction of any facility, or improvement to any facility, having an estimated Federal cost in excess of $300,000, may be undertaken in any fiscal year unless specifically identified as a separate item in the President’s annual fiscal year budget request or otherwise specifically authorized and appropriated if such facility or improvement would be used primarily by personnel of the intelligence community.
(b)
As used in this section, the term “intelligence community” has the same meaning given that term in section 3003(4) of this title.
