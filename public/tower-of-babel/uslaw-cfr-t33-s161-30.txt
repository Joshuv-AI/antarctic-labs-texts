33 C.F.R. § 161.30 — Vessel Traffic Service Louisville.
Citation: us:cfr:33:161.30
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-161.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 161 (Vessel Traffic Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The VTS area consists of the navigable waters of the Ohio River between McAlpine Locks (Mile 606.8) and Twelve Mile Island (Mile 593), only when the McAlpine upper pool gauge is at 13.0 feet or above.
