48 C.F.R. § 750.7102 — 750.7102 General policy.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 750 (Extraordinary Contractual Actions And The Safety Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Extra-contractual claims arising from foreign assistance contracts will be processed similarly to claims for extraordinary relief under FAR part 50, and in consideration of the circumstances and authorities granted by the Foreign Assistance Act.
