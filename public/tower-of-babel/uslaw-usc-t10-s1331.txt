10 U.S.C. § 1331 — Reference to chapter 1223
Citation: us:usc:10:1331
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 67 (Retired Pay For Non-Regular Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Provisions of law relating to retired pay for nonregular service are set forth in chapter 1223 of this title (beginning with section 12731).
