48 C.F.R. § 752.245-70 — 752.245-70 Government property—USAID reporting requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:48:752.245-70
Creator: Chapter 7—Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-752.245-70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 752 (Solicitation Provisions And Contract Clauses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

USAID contracts, except those for commercial items, must contain the following preface and reporting requirement as additions to the appropriate Government Property clause prescribed by (48 CFR) FAR 45.107, per a GAO audit recommendation.
Preface: To be inserted preceding the text of the FAR clause.
