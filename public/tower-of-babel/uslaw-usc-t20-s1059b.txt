20 U.S.C. § 1059b — Goals for financial management and academic program
Citation: us:usc:20:1059b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1059b
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 28 (Higher Education Resources And Student Assistance); Subchapter III (Institutional Aid); Part A (Strengthening Institutions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Goals
Any application for a grant under this part shall describe measurable goals for the institution’s financial management and academic programs, and include a plan of how the applicant intends to achieve those goals.
(b) Continuation requirements
Any continuation application shall demonstrate the progress made toward achievement of the goals described pursuant to subsection (a).
