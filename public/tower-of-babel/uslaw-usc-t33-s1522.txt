33 U.S.C. § 1522 — Limitations on export provisions of section 185(u) of title 30 unaffected
Citation: us:usc:33:1522
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section1522
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 29 (Deepwater Ports). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to amend, restrict, or otherwise limit the application of section 185(u) of title 30.
