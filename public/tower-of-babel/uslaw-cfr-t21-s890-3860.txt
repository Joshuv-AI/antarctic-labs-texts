21 C.F.R. § 890.3860 — Powered wheelchair.
Citation: us:cfr:21:890.3860
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-890.3860
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 890 (Physical Medicine Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. A powered wheelchair is a battery-operated device with wheels that is intended for medical purposes to provide mobility to persons restricted to a sitting position.
(b) Classification. Class II (performance standards).
