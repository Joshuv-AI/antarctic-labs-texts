30 C.F.R. § 921.955 — Certification of blasters.
Citation: us:cfr:30:921.955
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-921.955
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 921 (Massachusetts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Parts 955 of this chapter, Certification of Blasters in Federal Program States and on Indian Lands, shall apply to the training, examination and certification of blasters for surface coal mining and reclamation operations.
