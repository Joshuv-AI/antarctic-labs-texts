5 C.F.R. § 715.201 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:5:715.201
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-715.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 715 (Nondisciplinary Separations, Demotions, And Furloughs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to separation actions requested by employees in the executive departments and independent establishments of the Federal Government, including Government-owned or controlled corporations, and in those portions of the legislative and judicial branches of the Federal Government and the government of the District of Columbia having positions in the competitive service.
