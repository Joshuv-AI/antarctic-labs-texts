30 C.F.R. § 77.800 — High-voltage circuits; circuit breakers.
Citation: us:cfr:30:77.800
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-77.800
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 77 (Mandatory Safety Standards, Surface Coal Mines And Surface Work Areas Of Underground Coal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

High-voltage circuits supplying power to portable or mobile equipment shall be protected by suitable circuit breakers of adequate interrupting capacity which are properly tested and maintained and equipped with devices to provide protection against under voltage, grounded phase, short circuit and overcurrent. High-voltage circuits supplying power to stationary equipment shall be protected against overloads by either a circuit breaker or fuses of the correct type and capacity.
