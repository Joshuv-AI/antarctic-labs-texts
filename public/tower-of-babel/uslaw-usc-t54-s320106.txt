54 U.S.C. § 320106 — Limitation on obligation or expenditure of appropriated amounts
Citation: us:usc:54:320106
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section320106
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3201 (Policy And Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, no funds appropriated or otherwise made available to the Secretary to carry out subsection (f) or (g) of section 320102 of this title may be obligated or expended—
(1)
unless the appropriation of the funds has been specifically authorized by law enacted on or after October 30, 1992; or
(2)
in excess of the amount prescribed by law enacted on or after October 30, 1992.
