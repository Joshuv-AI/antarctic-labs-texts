33 C.F.R. § 154.750 — Compliance with operations manual.
Citation: us:cfr:33:154.750
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-154.750
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 154 (Facilities Transferring Oil Or Hazardous Material In Bulk). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The facility operator shall require facility personnel to use the procedures in the operations manual prescribed by § 154.300 for operations under this part.
