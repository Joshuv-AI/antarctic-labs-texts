30 C.F.R. § 41.13 — Failure to notify.
Citation: us:cfr:30:41.13
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-41.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 41 (Notification Of Legal Identity). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Failure of the operator to notify the Mine Safety and Health Administration, in writing, of the legal identity of the operator or any changes thereof within the time required under this part will be considered to be a violation of section 109(d) of the Act and shall be subject to penalties as provided in section 110 of the Act.
