48 C.F.R. § 1313.302-3 — 1313.302-3 Obtaining contractor acceptance and modifying purchase orders.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1313.302-3
Creator: Chapter 13—Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1313.302-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1313 (Simplified Acquisition Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A contractor's written acceptance of a purchase order modification is required, unless the contracting officer determines otherwise.
