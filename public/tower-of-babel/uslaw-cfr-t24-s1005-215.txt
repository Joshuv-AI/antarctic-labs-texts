24 C.F.R. § 1005.215 — Direct Guarantee Lender annual reporting requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:24:1005.215
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-1005.215
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 1005 (Loan Guarantees For Indian Housing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Direct Guarantee Lenders must submit an annual report on Loan performance, including reporting on all its Sponsored Entities, where applicable, along with any other required reporting under § 1005.903 and other such reports as prescribed by Section 184 Program Guidance.
