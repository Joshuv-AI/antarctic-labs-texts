48 C.F.R. § 1236.570 — 1236.570 Special precautions for work at operating airports.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1236 (Construction And Architect-Engineer Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where any acquisition will require work at an operating airport, insert the clause at 1252.236-70, Special Precautions for Work at Operating Airports, in solicitations and contracts.
