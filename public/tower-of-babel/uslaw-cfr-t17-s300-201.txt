17 C.F.R. § 300.201 — Accounts introduced by same or different broker or dealer.
Citation: us:cfr:17:300.201
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-300.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 300 (Rules Of The Securities Investor Protection Corporation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All accounts of a person which are introduced by the same broker or dealer shall be combined and protected as the single account of a separate customer, unless such accounts are maintained in different capacities as specified in §§ 300.100 through 300.105; accounts introduced by different brokers or dealers shall be protected separately.
