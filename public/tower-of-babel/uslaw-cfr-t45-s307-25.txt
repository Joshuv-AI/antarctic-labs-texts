45 C.F.R. § 307.25 — Review and certification of computerized support enforcement systems.
Citation: us:cfr:45:307.25
Creator: Office Of Child Support Services, Administration Of Families And Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-307.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 307 (Computerized Support Enforcement Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Office will review, assess and inspect the planning, design, development, installation, enhancement and operation of computerized support enforcement systems developed under § 307.10, or § 307.11 to determine the extent to which such systems:
(a) Meet the requirements found in § 307.15; and
(b) Can be certified as meeting the requirements described in § 307.10 and in the OCSE guideline entitled “Automated Systems for Child Support Enforcement: A Guide for States”.
