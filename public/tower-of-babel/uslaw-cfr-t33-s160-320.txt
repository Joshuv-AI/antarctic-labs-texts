33 C.F.R. § 160.320 — Sanctions and vessel control.

Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-160.320
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 160 (Ports And Waterways Safety—General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Failure to comply with this subpart will subject the crewmember and operator to a civil penalty under 46 U.S.C. 70119 and the vessel to control under 46 U.S.C. 70002.
