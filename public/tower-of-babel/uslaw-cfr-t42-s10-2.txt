42 C.F.R. § 10.2 — Summary of 340B Drug Pricing Program.
Citation: us:cfr:42:10.2
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-10.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 10 (340B Drug Pricing Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 340B of the PHSA instructs the Secretary of Health and Human Services to enter into agreements with manufacturers of covered outpatient drugs under which the amount to be paid to manufacturers by certain statutorily-defined covered entities does not exceed the 340B ceiling price.
