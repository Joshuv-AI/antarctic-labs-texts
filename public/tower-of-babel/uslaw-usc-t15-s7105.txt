15 U.S.C. § 7105 — Establishment of the National Women’s Business Council
Citation: us:usc:15:7105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section7105
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 97 (Women’S Business Enterprise Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is established a council to be known as the National Women’s Business Council, which shall serve as an independent source of advice and policy recommendations to the Interagency Committee, to the Administrator through the Assistant Administrator of the Office of Women’s Business Ownership, to the Congress, and to the President.
