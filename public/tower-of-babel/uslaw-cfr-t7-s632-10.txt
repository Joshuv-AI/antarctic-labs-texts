7 C.F.R. § 632.10 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:7:632.10
Creator: Natural Resources Conservation Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-632.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 632 (Rural Abandoned Mine Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This program applies to any county or other designated area within a State that had abandoned or inadequately reclaimed coal-mined lands within its borders before August 3, 1977.
