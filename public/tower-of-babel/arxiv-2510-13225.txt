Grothendieck groups and completions of Gorenstein local rings
Tony J. Puthenpurakal

Abstract
. Let ( A , 𝔪 ) (A,\mathfrak{m}) be an excellent Gorenstein local ring of dimension d ≥ 2 d\geq 2 which is an isolated singularity. Let A ^ \widehat{A} denote the completion of A A . If G ⁡ ( A ) G(A) is the Grothendieck group of A A then by G ​ ( A ) ℚ G(A)_{\mathbb{Q}} we denote G ⁡ ( A ) ⊗ ℤ ℚ G(A)\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}}\mathbb{Q} . We prove that the natural map G ​ ( A ) ℚ → G ​ ( A ^ ) ℚ G(A)_{\mathbb{Q}}\rightarrow G(\widehat{A})_{\mathbb{Q}} is an isomorphism if and only if for any maximal Cohen-Macaulay(= MCM) A ^ \widehat{A} -module M M there exists an MCM A A -module N N and integers r ≥ 1 r\geq 1 and s ≥ 0 s\geq 0 (depending on M M ) such that M r ⊕ A ^ s ≅ N ^ M^{r}\oplus\widehat{A}^{s}\cong\widehat{N} . An essential ingredient is the classification of ℚ \mathbb{Q} -subspaces of G ​ ( 𝒞 ) ℚ G(\mathcal{C})_{\mathbb{Q}} (here 𝒞 \mathcal{C} is a skelletaly small triangulated category) in terms of certain dense subcategories of 𝒞 \mathcal{C} . We also give criterion for a Henselian Gorenstein ring B B (not an isolated singularity) such that the natural map G ​ ( B ) ℚ → G ​ ( B ^ ) ℚ G(B)_{\mathbb{Q}}\rightarrow G(\widehat{B})_{\mathbb{Q}} is an isomorphism ( when dim B = 2 , 3 \dim B=2,3 ). We give many examples where our result holds.

The Grothendieck group
