10 U.S.C. § 6143 — Modernization and consolidation of tritium recycling facilities
Citation: us:usc:10:6143
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section6143
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part VI (Elements Of Department Of Defense And Other Matters); Subpart B (Atomic Energy Defense); Chapter 602 (Nuclear Weapons Stockpile Matters); Subchapter II (Tritium). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Energy shall carry out activities at the Savannah River Site, South Carolina, to—
(1)
modernize and consolidate the facilities for recycling tritium from weapons; and
(2)
provide a modern tritium extraction facility so as to ensure that such facilities have a capacity to recycle tritium from weapons that is adequate to meet the requirements for tritium for weapons specified in the Nuclear Weapons Stockpile Memorandum.
