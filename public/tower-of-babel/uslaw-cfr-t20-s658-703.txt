20 C.F.R. § 658.703 — Emergency corrective action.
Citation: us:cfr:20:658.703
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-658.703
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 658 (Administrative Provisions Governing The Wagner-Peyser Act Employment Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In critical situations as determined by the Regional Administrator, where it is necessary to protect the integrity of the funds, or ensure the proper operation of the program, the Regional Administrator may impose immediate corrective action. Where immediate corrective action is imposed, the Regional Administrator must notify the SWA of the reason for imposing the emergency corrective action prior to providing the SWA an opportunity to comment.
