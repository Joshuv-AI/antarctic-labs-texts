32 C.F.R. § 1698.8 — Effect of advisory opinion.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1698.8
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1698.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1698 (Advisory Opinions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Selective Service System will not take action with respect to any person concerning whom the Director has issued an advisory opinion insonsistent with that advisory opinion.
