1 U.S.C. § 213 — Appropriation for preparing and editing supplements
Citation: us:usc:1:213
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title1-section213
Context: United States Code, Title 1 (General Provisions); Chapter 3 (Code Of Laws Of United States And Supplements; District Of Columbia Code And Supplements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For preparation and editing an annual appropriation of $6,500 is authorized to carry out the purposes of sections 202 and 203 of this title.
