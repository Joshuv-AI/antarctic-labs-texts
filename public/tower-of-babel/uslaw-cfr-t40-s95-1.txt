40 C.F.R. § 95.1 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:40:95.1
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-95.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 95 (Mandatory Patent Licenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) As used in this part, all terms not defined in this section shall have the meaning given them by the Act.
(b) Act means the Clean Air Act, as amended (42 U.S.C. §§ 7401-7671).
(c) Agency means the Environmental Protection Agency.
(d) Administrator means the Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency.
