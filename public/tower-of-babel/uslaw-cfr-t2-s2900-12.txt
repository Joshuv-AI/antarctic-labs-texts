2 C.F.R. § 2900.12 — Revision of budget and program plans approval from Grant Officers.
Citation: us:cfr:2:2900.12
Creator: Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-2900.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 2900 (Uniform Administrative Requirements, Cost Principles, And Audit Requirements For Federal Awards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the DOL, unless otherwise noted in the grant agreement or cooperative agreement, prior written approval for revision of budget and program plans must come from the Grant Officer (see 2 CFR 200.308(d)).
