7 C.F.R. § 1774.100 — OMB Control Number.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1774.100
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1774.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1774 (Special Evaluation Assistance For Rural Communities And Households Program (Search)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The information collection requirements in this part will not be effective until approved by the Office of Management and Budget (OMB), subject to the submission of a paperwork package to OMB and assigned an OMB Control Number.
