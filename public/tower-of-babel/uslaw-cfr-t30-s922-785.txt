30 C.F.R. § 922.785 — Requirements for permits for special categories of mining.
Citation: us:cfr:30:922.785
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-922.785
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 922 (Michigan). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Part 785 of this chapter, Requirements for Permits for Special Categories of Mining, shall apply to each person who makes application for a permit to conduct certain categories of surface coal mining and reclamation operations as specified therein.
