20 C.F.R. § 686.600 — Are students provided with government-paid transportation to and from Job Corps centers?
Citation: us:cfr:20:686.600
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-686.600
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 686 (The Job Corps Under Title I Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, Job Corps provides for the transportation of students between their homes and centers as described in policies and procedures issued by the Secretary.
