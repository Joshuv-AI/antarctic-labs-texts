48 C.F.R. § 2005.502 — 2005.502 Authority.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2005.502
Creator: Chapter 20—Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2005.502
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2005 (Publicizing Contract Actions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before placing paid advertisements in newspapers and trade journals to publicize contract actions, written authority must be obtained from the Director, Division of Contracts and Property Management, for Headquarters activities, or the Director, Division of Resource Management and Administration, within each regional office for a regional procurement.
