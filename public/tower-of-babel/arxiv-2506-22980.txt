Abelianized Descent Obstruction for 0-Cycles Thanks: MSC 2020 : 14G12 11G35 14G05 14C25
Hui Zhang

Abstract
. Classical descent theory of Colliot-Thélène and Sansuc for rational points tells that, over a smooth variety X X , the algebraic Brauer–Manin subset equals the descent obstruction subset defined by a universal torsor. Moreover, Harari shows that the Brauer–Manin subset equals the descent obstruction subset defined by torsors under connected linear groups. By using the abelian cohomology theory by Borovoi, we define abelianized descent obstructions by torsors under connected linear groups. As an analogy, we show the equality between the Brauer–Manin obstruction and the abelianized descent obstruction for 0-cycles. We also show that the abelianized descent obstruction is the closure of the descent obstruction defined by Balestrieri and Berg when X X is projective and Br ​ ( X ) / Br 0 ​ ( X ) \mathrm{Br}(X)/\mathrm{Br}_{0}(X) is finite.

2.3.

By spectral sequence

(2) The

The following theorem gives the relation between

and
\mathrm{Z}_{0,\mathbf{A}}(X)^{f}_{\mathrm{BB}}
.

Theorem 5.5
.

Let
X
be a smooth, proper and geometrically integral variety over a number field
k
,
G
a connected linear
k
-group and
f:Y\rightarrow X
a torsor under
G
.

(1) We have that

\mathrm{Z}_{0,\mathbf{A}}(X)_{\mathrm{BB}}^{f}\subseteq\mathrm{Z}_{0,\mathbf{A}}(X)_{\mathrm{ab}}^{f}.

(2) Assume that
X
is projective and
\mathrm{Z}_{0,\mathbf{A}}(X)^{f}_{\mathrm{ab}}
is open, then we have that

\mathrm{Z}_{0,\mathbf{A}}(X)_{\mathrm{ab}}^{f}=\overline{\mathrm{Z}_{0,\mathbf{A}}(X)_{\mathrm{BB}}^{f}}.

Proof.

Claim (1). We only need to claim that any adelic 0-cycle
(x_{v})_{v\in\Omega_{k}}
,
f
-descent by an adelic 0-cycle, lies in
\mathrm{Z}_{0,\mathbf{A}}(X)_{\mathrm{ab}}^{f}
. Assume that
(x_{v})_{v\in\Omega_{k}}
has form
\pi_{K/k}(f_{K,*}^{\sigma}((y_{w})_{w\in\Omega_{K}}))
for a finite extension
K/k
and an element
\sigma\in\mathrm{H}^{1}(K,G)
. Take
\alpha\in\mathrm{Br}_{f}^{\prime}\subseteq\mathrm{Br}(X)
, then one has

\langle\alpha,(x_{v})_{v\in\Omega_{k}}\rangle_{\mathrm{BM}}{{}_{,k}}=\langle\beta,(y_{w})_{w\in\Omega_{K}}\rangle_{\mathrm{BM}}{{}_{,K}}

where
\beta
is the image of
\alpha
along
\mathrm{Br}(X)\rightarrow\mathrm{Br}(X_{K})\xrightarrow{f_{K}^{\sigma,\ast}}\mathrm{Br}(Y_{K}^{\sigma})
by functoriality of Brauer–Manin pairing. Since the restriction
\alpha_{K}\in\mathrm{Br}(X_{K})
lies in
\mathrm{Br}_{f_{K}}^{\prime}
, the image
\beta
lies in
\mathrm{Br}_{0}(Y_{K}^{\sigma})
by Lemma
4.5
, hence,
\langle\beta,(y_{w})_{w\in\Omega_{K}}\rangle_{\mathrm{BM}}{{}_{,K}}=0
. Now,
(x_{v})_{v\in\Omega_{k}}
is orthogonal to
\mathrm{Br}_{f}^{\prime}
and lies in
\mathrm{Z}_{0,\mathbf{A}}(X)^{f}_{\mathrm{ab}}
by Theorem
4.3
.

Claim (2). Since
\mathrm{Z}_{0,\mathbf{A}}(X)^{f}_{\mathrm{ab}}
is open, we have that
\mathrm{Z}_{0,\mathbf{A}}(X)^{f}_{\mathrm{ab}}=\overline{\Phi_{\mathbf{A}}(X)\cap\mathrm{Z}_{0,\mathbf{A}}(X)^{f}_{\mathrm{ab}}}
by Lemma
5.3
. Hence, we only need claim that

\Phi_{\mathbf{A}}(X)\cap\mathrm{Z}_{0,\mathbf{A}}(X)^{f}_{\mathrm{ab}}\subseteq\mathrm{Z}_{0,\mathbf{A}}(X)_{\mathrm{BB}}^{f}.

Take an adelic 0-cycle
(x_{v})_{v\in\Omega_{k}}\in\Phi_{\mathbf{A}}(X)\cap\mathrm{Z}_{0,\mathbf{A}}(X)_{\mathrm{ab}}^{f}
. Since any global 0-cycle lies both in
\mathrm{Z}_{0,\mathbf{A}}(X)_{\mathrm{ab}}^{f}
and
\mathrm{Z}_{0,\mathbf{A}}(X)_{\mathrm{BB}}^{f}
, we may assume
(x_{v})_{v\in\Omega_{k}}=\pi_{K/k}((Q_{w})_{w\in\Omega_{K}})
for an adelic point
(Q_{w})_{w\in\Omega_{K}}
on
X_{K}
for some finite extension
K
of
k
.

Since the exact sequence (
11
) gives rise to a commutative diagram

\textstyle{\mathrm{Pic}(G)\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces}
\scriptstyle{\Delta_{Y/X}}
\textstyle{\mathrm{Br}(X)\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces}
\scriptstyle{f^{*}}
\textstyle{\mathrm{Br}(Y)\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces}
\textstyle{\mathrm{Pic}(G)\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces}
\scriptstyle{\Delta_{Y_{K}/X_{K}}}
\textstyle{\mathrm{Br}(X_{K})\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces}
\scriptstyle{f^{*}_{K}}
\textstyle{\mathrm{Br}(Y_{K})}

with exact rows, the restriction homomorphism
\mathrm{Br}_{f}^{\prime}\twoheadrightarrow\mathrm{Br}_{f_{K}}^{\prime}
is surjective. Therefore, the functoriality of the Brauer–Manin obstruction shows that
(Q_{w})_{w\in\Omega_{K}}
is orthogonal to
\mathrm{Br}_{f_{K}}^{\prime}
, hence,
(Q_{w})_{w\in\Omega_{K}}
lies in
\mathrm{Z}_{0,\mathbf{A}}(X_{K})_{\mathrm{ab}}^{f_{K}}
. By Lemma
4.2
,
(Q_{w})_{w\in\Omega_{K}}
also lies in
X_{K}(\mathbf{A})^{f_{K}}
, hence, by
f
-descent decomposition (
4
) for adelic points,
(Q_{w})_{w\in\Omega_{K}}
has the form
f_{K,\ast}^{\sigma}((Q^{\prime}_{w})_{w\in\Omega_{K}})
for an adelic point
(Q^{\prime}_{w})_{w\in\Omega_{K}}
on
Y_{K}^{\sigma}
with suitable
\sigma\in\mathrm{H}^{1}(K,G)
. Therefore,
(x_{v})_{v\in\Omega_{k}}
lies in
\mathrm{Z}_{0,\mathbf{A}}(X)_{\mathrm{BB}}^{f}
. This completes the proof.
∎

Denote
\mathrm{Z}_{0,\mathbf{A}}(X)^{\mathrm{conn}}_{\mathrm{BB}}=\bigcap\limits_{f:Y\xrightarrow{G}X}\mathrm{Z}_{0,\mathbf{A}}(X)^{f}_{\mathrm{BB}}
, where the intersection is taken over all
X
-torsors
f:Y\xrightarrow{G}X
under all connected linear groups
G
over
k
. We also has a similar relation between

and

Theorem 5.6
.

Let
X
be a smooth, projective and geometrically integral variety over a number field
k
. Assume that
\mathrm{Z}_{0,\mathbf{A}}(X)^{\mathrm{Br}}
is open, then

\mathrm{Z}_{0,\mathbf{A}}(X)^{\mathrm{Br}}=\overline{\mathrm{Z}_{0,\mathbf{A}}(X)_{\mathrm{BB}}^{\mathrm{conn}}}

Proof.

Due to the same reason as in the proof of Theorem
5.5
, we only need to notice that

\Phi_{\mathbf{A}}(X)\cap\mathrm{Z}_{0,\mathbf{A}}(X)^{\mathrm{Br}}=\bigcap\limits_{f:Y\xrightarrow{G}X}\Big(\Phi_{\mathbf{A}}(X)\cap\mathrm{Z}_{0,\mathbf{A}}(X)^{f}_{\mathrm{ab}}\Big)\subseteq\bigcap\limits_{f:Y\xrightarrow{G}X}\mathrm{Z}_{0,\mathbf{A}}(X)_{\mathrm{BB}}^{f}=\mathrm{Z}_{0,\mathbf{A}}(X)_{\mathrm{BB}}^{\mathrm{conn}}

where the intersection is taken over all
X
-torsors
f:Y\xrightarrow{G}X
under all connected linear groups
G
over
k
and
\mathrm{Z}_{0,\mathbf{A}}(X)^{f}_{\mathrm{ab}}
is automatically open (it contains the open subgroup
\mathrm{Z}_{0,\mathbf{A}}(X)^{\mathrm{Br}}
).
∎
