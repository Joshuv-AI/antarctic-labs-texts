43 C.F.R. § 3175.116 — Composite sampling methods.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3175.116
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3175.116
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3170 (Onshore Oil And Gas Production). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Composite samplers must be flow-proportional.
(b) Samples must be collected using a positive-displacement pump.
(c) Sample cylinders must be sized to ensure the cylinder capacity is not exceeded within the normal collection frequency.
