2 C.F.R. § 2000.330 — What method must be used to pass requirements down to participants at lower tiers?
Citation: us:cfr:2:2000.330
Creator: United States Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-2000.330
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 2000 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A participant in a covered transaction must include a term or condition in any lower-tier covered transaction to require the participant of that transaction to—
(a) Comply with subpart C of the OMB guidance in 2 CFR part 180; and
(b) Include a similar term or condition in any covered transaction into which it enters at the next lower tier.
