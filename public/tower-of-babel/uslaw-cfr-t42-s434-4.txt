42 C.F.R. § 434.4 — State plan requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:42:434.4
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-434.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 434 (Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the State plan provides for contracts of the types covered by this part, the plan must also provide for meeting the applicable requirements of this part.
