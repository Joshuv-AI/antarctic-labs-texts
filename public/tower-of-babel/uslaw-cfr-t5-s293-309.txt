5 C.F.R. § 293.309 — Reconstruction of lost OPFs.
Citation: us:cfr:5:293.309
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-293.309
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 293 (Personnel Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies will take necessary precautions to safeguard all OPFs. In the event of a lost or destroyed OPF, the current (or last, in the case of a former Federal employee) employing agency shall take the necessary action to reconstruct the essential portions of the OPF as specified in the Guide to Personnel Recordkeeping or other Office instructions.
