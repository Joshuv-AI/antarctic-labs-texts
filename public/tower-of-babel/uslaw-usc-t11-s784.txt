11 U.S.C. § 784 — Right to be heard
Citation: us:usc:11:784
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section784
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 7 (Liquidation); Subchapter V (Clearing Bank Liquidation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Board or a Federal reserve bank (in the case of a clearing bank that is a member of that bank) may raise and may appear and be heard on any issue in a case under this subchapter.
