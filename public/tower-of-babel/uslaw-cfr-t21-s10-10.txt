21 C.F.R. § 10.10 — Summaries of administrative practices and procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:21:10.10
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-10.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 10 (Administrative Practices And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To encourage public participation in all agency activities, the Commissioner will prepare for public distribution summaries of FDA administrative practices and procedures in readily understandable terms.
