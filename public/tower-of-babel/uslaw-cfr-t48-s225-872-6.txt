48 C.F.R. § 225.872-6 — 225.872-6 Request for audit services.
Citation: us:cfr:48:225.872-6
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-225.872-6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 225 (Foreign Acquisition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Handle requests for audit services in France, Germany, the Netherlands, or the United Kingdom in accordance with PGI 215.404-2(c), but follow the additional procedures at PGI 225.872-6.
