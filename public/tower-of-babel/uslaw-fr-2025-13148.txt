DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice: 12713]


Updating Cuba Prohibited Accommodations List

SUMMARY: The Department of State is adding 11 properties to its Cuba 
Prohibited Accommodations List (CPA List). The Cuban Assets Control 
Regulations generally prohibit persons subject to U.S. jurisdiction 
from lodging, paying for lodging, or making any reservation for, or on 
behalf of, a third party to lodge at any property in Cuba on the CPA 
List.

DATES: Effective July 14, 2025.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Robert Haas, Office of the Coordinator 
for Cuban Affairs, tel.: 771-204-7384, [email protected], 
Department of State, Washington, DC 20520.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Background

    On September 24, 2020, the Department of the Treasury amended its 
Cuba sanctions with a regulatory amendment to the CACR, adding Sec.  
515.10 to generally prohibit persons subject to U.S. jurisdiction from 
lodging, paying for lodging, or making any reservation for, or on 
behalf of, a third party to lodge at any property in Cuba on the CPA 
List. The State Department, on behalf of the Secretary of State, is now 
updating the CPA List, as published below and available on the State 
Department's website: https://www.state.gov/cuba-sanctions/cuba-prohibited-accommodations-list/.
    This update includes eleven additional accommodations, one address 
correction, four name refinements, and one merging of duplicative 
entries. The State Department continues to update the Cuba Prohibited 
Accommodations List periodically.

Electronic Availability

    This document and additional information concerning the CPA List 
are available from the Department of State's website: (https://www.state.gov/cuba-sanctions/cuba-prohibited-accommodations-list/).

prohibited official of the Government of Cuba, as defined in 31 CFR

prohibited official of the Government of Cuba, or a close relative of a 
prohibited member of the Cuban Communist Party. For information

please see 31 CFR 515.210. All properties were listed effective 
September 28, 2020, unless otherwise indicated.
