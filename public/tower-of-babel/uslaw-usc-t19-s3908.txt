19 U.S.C. § 3908 — Technical assistance
Citation: us:usc:19:3908
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section3908
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 25 (Clean Diamond Trade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may direct the appropriate agencies of the United States Government to make available technical assistance to countries seeking to implement the Kimberley Process Certification Scheme.
