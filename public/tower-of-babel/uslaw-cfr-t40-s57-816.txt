40 C.F.R. § 57.816 — Effect of negative recommendation.
Citation: us:cfr:40:57.816
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-57.816
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 57 (Primary Nonferrous Smelter Orders). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No waiver of the interim requirement for the use of constant controls shall be granted by the Administrator or a State unless the Administrator or a State first takes into account the Administrator's report, findings, and recommendations as to whether the use of constant controls would be so costly as to necessitate permanent or prolonged temporary cessations of operation of the smelter.
