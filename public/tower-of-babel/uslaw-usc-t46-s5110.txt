46 U.S.C. § 5110 — Submersible vessels
Citation: us:usc:46:5110
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section5110
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part C (Load Lines Of Vessels); Chapter 51 (Load Lines). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding sections 5103–5105 of this title, the Secretary may prescribe regulations for submersible vessels to provide a minimum level of safety. In developing the regulations, the Secretary shall consider factors relevant to submersible vessels, including the structure, stability, and watertight integrity of those vessels.
