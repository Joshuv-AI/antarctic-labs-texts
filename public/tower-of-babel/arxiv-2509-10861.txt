An Improvement of 2-Distance Chromatic Number of Planar Graphs with Maximum Degree at Least 6
Sara Al Hajjar

Abstract
A 2-distance k k -coloring of a graph is a proper coloring of the vertices using k k colors such that any two vertices at distance two or less get different colors. The 2-distance chromatic number of G, denoted as χ 2 ​ ( G ) \chi_{2}(G) , is the minimum integer k k such that G G has a 2 distance k k -coloring. In [ 8 ] , Jan van den Heuvel and Sean Mcguinness proved that χ 2 ​ ( G ) ≤ 2 ​ Δ + 25 \chi_{2}(G)\leq 2\Delta+25 for planar garphs without adding any restriction to Δ \Delta . Later, Zhu and Bu [ 10 ] proved that χ 2 ​ ( G ) ≤ 5 ​ Δ − 7 \chi_{2}(G)\leq 5\Delta-7 for Δ ≥ 6 \Delta\geq 6 improving the bound of χ 2 ​ ( G ) \chi_{2}(G) for 6 ≤ Δ ≤ 10 6\leq\Delta\leq 10 . We prove that χ 2 ​ ( G ) ≤ 3 ​ Δ + 2 \chi_{2}(G)\leq 3\Delta+2 for a planar graph G G with a maximum degree Δ \Delta at least 6 improving the bound of χ 2 ​ ( G ) \chi_{2}(G) for 6 ≤ Δ ≤ 22 6\leq\Delta\leq 22 .

Let

\sum_{v\in V(G)}(d(v)-4)+\sum_{f\in F(G)}(d(f)-4)=-8
