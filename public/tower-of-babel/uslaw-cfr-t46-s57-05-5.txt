46 C.F.R. § 57.05-5 — -5 Low temperature application.
Citation: us:cfr:46:57.05-5
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-57.05-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 57 (Welding And Brazing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For low temperature application, each welder must demonstrate their ability to weld satisfactorily in accordance with procedures qualified in accordance with § 57.03-1(b). Manual welding must be qualified in the position prescribed by the procedure.
