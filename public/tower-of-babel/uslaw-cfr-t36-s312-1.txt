36 C.F.R. § 312.1 — Areas covered.
Citation: us:cfr:36:312.1
Creator: Corps Of Engineers, Department Of The Army
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-312.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 312 (Prohibition Of Discriminatory Practices In Water Resource Development Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulation covered in this part shall be applicable to all water resource project lands under the supervision of the Secretary of the Army not covered in parts 311 and 326, of this title.
