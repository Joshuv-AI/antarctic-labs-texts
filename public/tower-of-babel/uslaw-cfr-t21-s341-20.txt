21 C.F.R. § 341.20 — Nasal decongestant active ingredients.
Citation: us:cfr:21:341.20
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-341.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 341 (Cold, Cough, Allergy, Bronchodilator, And Antiasthmatic Drug Products For Over-The-Counter Human Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The active ingredient of the product consists of any of the following when used within the dosage limits and in the dosage forms established for each ingredient:
(a) Oral nasal decongestants. (1) Phenylephrine hydrochloride.
(2) Pseudoephedrine hydrochloride.
(3) Pseudoephedrine sulfate.
(4) Phenylephrine bitartrate in an effervescent dosage form.
(b) Topical nasal decongestants. (1) Levmetamfetamine.
(2) Ephedrine.
(3) Ephedrine hydrochloride.
(4) Ephedrine sulfate.
(5) [Reserved]
(6) Naphazoline hydrochloride.
(7) Oxymetazoline hydrochloride.
(8) Phenylephrine hydrochloride.
(9) Propylhexedrine.
(10) Xylometazoline hydrochloride.
