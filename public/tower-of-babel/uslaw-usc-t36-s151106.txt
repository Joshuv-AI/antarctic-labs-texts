36 U.S.C. § 151106 — Tax exemption

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section151106
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1511 (National Education Association Of The United States). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Real Property.—
Real property of the corporation is exempt from taxation if it is—
(1)
located in the District of Columbia;
(2)
used for the purposes provided in section 151102 of this title; and
(3)
not used to produce income.
(b) Personal Property.—
Personal property of the corporation is exempt from taxation if it is used for the purposes provided in section 151102 of this title or to produce income to be used for those purposes.
(c) Annual Report.—
The corporation shall submit annually to the Secretary of Education a written report stating in detail for the prior year—
(1)
the real and personal property held by the corporation;
(2)
the income from the property; and
(3)
the expenditure or other use or disposition of the property and income from the property.
