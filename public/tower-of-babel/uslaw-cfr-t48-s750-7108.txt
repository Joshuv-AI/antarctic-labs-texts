48 C.F.R. § 750.7108 — 750.7108 Contractual requirements.

Creator: Chapter 7—Agency For International Development
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 750 (Extraordinary Contractual Actions And The Safety Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every contract modified pursuant to this subpart shall contain:
(a) A citation of the Act and Executive Order 11223;
(b) A brief statement of the circumstances justifying the action; and
(c) With respect to cases falling within section 4 of Executive Order 11223, a statement that the action is necessary to protect the foreign policy interests of the United States.
