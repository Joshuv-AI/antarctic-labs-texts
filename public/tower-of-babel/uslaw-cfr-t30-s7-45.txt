30 C.F.R. § 7.45 — Critical characteristics
Citation: us:cfr:30:7.45
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-7.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 7 (Testing By Applicant Or Third Party). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following critical characteristics shall be inspected or tested on each battery assembly to which an approval marking is affixed:
(a) Thickness of covers and boxes.
(b) Application and resistance of insulating material.
(c) Size and location of ventilation openings.
(d) Method of cell terminations.
(e) Strain relief devices for cables leaving boxes.
(f) Type, location, and physical protection of cables.
