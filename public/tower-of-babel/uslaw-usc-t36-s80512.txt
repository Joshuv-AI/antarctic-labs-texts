36 U.S.C. § 80512 — Annual report
Citation: us:usc:36:80512
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section80512
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 805 (Gold Star Wives Of America). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation shall submit an annual report to Congress on the activities of the corporation during the prior calendar year. The report may not be printed as a public document.
