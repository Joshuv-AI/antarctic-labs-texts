43 C.F.R. § 3872.3 — Presumption as to land returned as mineral.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3872.3
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3872.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3870 (Adverse Claims, Protests And Conflicts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Public land returned upon the survey records as mineral shall be withheld from entry as agricultural land until the presumption arising from such a return shall be overcome.
