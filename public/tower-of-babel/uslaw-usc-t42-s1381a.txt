42 U.S.C. § 1381a — Basic entitlement to benefits
Citation: us:usc:42:1381a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1381a
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 7 (Social Security); Subchapter XVI (Supplemental Security Income For Aged, Blind, And Disabled). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Every aged, blind, or disabled individual who is determined under part A to be eligible on the basis of his income and resources shall, in accordance with and subject to the provisions of this subchapter, be paid benefits by the Commissioner of Social Security.
