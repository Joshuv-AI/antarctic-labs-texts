41 C.F.R. § 303-70.4 — -70.4 Limitation on duplicate death-related expense payments.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 303-70 (70—Agency Requirements For Payment Of Expenses Connected With The Death Of Certain Employees And Immediate Family Members). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies will not pay death-related expenses under this chapter when the same expenses are payable under other United States laws. Specifically, when an employee dies from injuries sustained while performing official duty, death-related expenses are payable under the Federal Employees' Compensation Act (FECA), 5 U.S.C. 8134.
