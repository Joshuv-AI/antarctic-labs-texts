46 U.S.C. § 60107 — Payment of fees on departing vessel
Citation: us:usc:46:60107
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section60107
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 601 (Arrival And Departure Requirements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A departing vessel may be cleared only when all legal fees that have accrued on the vessel are paid and proof of payment is presented to the individual granting the clearance.
