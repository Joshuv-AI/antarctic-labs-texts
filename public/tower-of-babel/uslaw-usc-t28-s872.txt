28 U.S.C. § 872 — Criers, bailiffs, and messengers
Citation: us:usc:28:872
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section872
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part III (Court Officers And Employees); Chapter 55 (Court Of International Trade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Court of International Trade may appoint such criers as it may require for said court, which criers shall also perform the duties of bailiffs and messengers and such other duties as the court directs and shall be subject to removal by the court.
