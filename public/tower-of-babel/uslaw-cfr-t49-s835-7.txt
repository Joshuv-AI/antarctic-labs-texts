49 C.F.R. § 835.7 — Testimony of former Board employees.
Citation: us:cfr:49:835.7
Creator: National Transportation Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-835.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 835 (Testimony Of Board Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is not necessary to request Board approval for testimony of a former Board employee, nor is testimony limited to depositions. However, the scope of permissible testimony continues to be constrained by all the limitations set forth in § 835.3 and § 835.4.
