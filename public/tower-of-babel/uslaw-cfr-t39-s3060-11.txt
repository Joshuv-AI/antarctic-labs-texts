39 C.F.R. § 3060.11 — Valuation of assets.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3060.11
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3060.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3060 (Accounting Practices And Tax Rules For The Theoretical Competitive Products Enterprise). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the purposes of 39 U.S.C. 2011, the total assets of the Postal Service theoretical competitive products enterprise are the greater of:
(a) The percentage of total Postal Service revenues and receipts from competitive products times the total net assets of the Postal Service, or
(b) The net assets related to the provision of competitive products as determined pursuant to § 3060.12.
