24 C.F.R. § 965.504 — Period for which allowances are established.
Citation: us:cfr:24:965.504
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-965.504
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 965 (Pha-Owned Or Leased Projects—General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) PHA-furnished utilities. Allowances will normally be established on a quarterly basis; however, residents may be surcharged on a monthly basis. The allowances established may provide for seasonal variations.
(b) Resident-purchased utilities. Monthly allowances shall be established. The allowances established may provide for seasonal variations.
