42 C.F.R. § 422.57 — Limited enrollment under MA RFB plans.
Citation: us:cfr:42:422.57
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-422.57
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 422 (Medicare Advantage Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An RFB society that offers an MA RFB plan may offer that plan only to members of the church, or convention or group of churches with which the society is affiliated.
