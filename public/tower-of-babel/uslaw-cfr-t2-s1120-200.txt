2 C.F.R. § 1120.200 — Purpose and inclusion of award-specific terms and conditions.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1120.200
Creator: Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1120.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1120 (Award Format For Dod Grants And Cooperative Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A DoD Component must include with each award, for transmission to the recipient, any terms and conditions needed to communicate requirements specific to the individual award as distinct from the more broadly applicable requirements in the general terms and conditions. For a modification to an award, only changes to previously transmitted terms and conditions must be included.
