30 C.F.R. § 44.8 — Ex parte communication.
Citation: us:cfr:30:44.8
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-44.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 44 (Rules Of Practice For Petitions For Modification Of Mandatory Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

There shall be no ex parte communication with respect to the merits of any case not concluded between the Assistant Secretary or the administrative law judge, including any employee or agent of the Assistant Secretary or of the administrative law judge, and any of the parties, intervenors, representatives, or other interested parties.
