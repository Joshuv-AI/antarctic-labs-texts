20 U.S.C. § 1161l–2 — Preparation for future disasters plan by the Secretary
Citation: us:usc:20:1161l–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1161l–2
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 28 (Higher Education Resources And Student Assistance); Subchapter IX (Additional Programs); Part L (Student Safety And Campus Emergency Management). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall continue to coordinate with the Secretary of Homeland Security and other appropriate agencies to develop and maintain procedures to address the preparedness, response, and recovery needs of institutions of higher education in the event of a natural or manmade disaster with respect to which the President has declared a major disaster or emergency (as such terms are defined in section 1161l–3 of this title).
