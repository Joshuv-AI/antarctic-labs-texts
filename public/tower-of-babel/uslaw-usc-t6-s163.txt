6 U.S.C. § 163 — Definition of law enforcement technology
Citation: us:usc:6:163
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section163
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 1 (Homeland Security Organization); Subchapter II (Information Analysis); Part C (Office Of Science And Technology). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purposes of this subchapter, the term “law enforcement technology” includes investigative and forensic technologies, corrections technologies, and technologies that support the judicial process.
