24 C.F.R. § 27.35 — Foreclosure costs.
Citation: us:cfr:24:27.35
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-27.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 27 (Nonjudicial Foreclosure Of Multifamily And Single Family Mortgages). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pursuant to section 369C(5) of the Act (12 U.S.C. 3711(5)), a commission to the foreclosure commissioner for the conduct of the foreclosure will be paid in an amount to be determined by the General Counsel. A commission may be allowed to the commissioner notwithstanding termination of the sale or appointment of a substitute commissioner before the sale takes place.
