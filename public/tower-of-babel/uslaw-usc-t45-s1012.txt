45 U.S.C. § 1012 — Publications and reports
Citation: us:usc:45:1012
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2024
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title45-section1012
Context: United States Code, Title 45 (Railroads); Chapter 19 (Rock Island Railroad Employee Assistance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Within 45 days after May 30, 1980, the Board shall publish, and make available for distribution by the Rock Island Railroad to all eligible employees, a document which describes in detail the rights of such employees under sections 1005, 1006,1 1007, and 1014 of this title.
