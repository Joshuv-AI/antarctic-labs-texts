48 C.F.R. § 270.000 — 270.000 Scope of part.
Citation: us:cfr:48:270.000
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-270.000
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 270 (Defense Contracting Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part has been created to facilitate promulgation of additional DFARS coverage of defense-specific contracting programs that do not properly fall under DFARS subchapter D, Socioeconomic Programs, and neither implement nor supplement existing FAR part 19 or parts 22 through 25.
