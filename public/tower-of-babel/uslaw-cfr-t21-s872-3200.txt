21 C.F.R. § 872.3200 — Resin tooth bonding agent.
Citation: us:cfr:21:872.3200
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-872.3200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 872 (Dental Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. A resin tooth bonding agent is a device material, such as methylmethacrylate, intended to be painted on the interior of a prepared cavity of a tooth to improve retention of a restoration, such as a filling.
(b) Classification. Class II.
