25 U.S.C. § 402 — Leases of surplus lands
Citation: us:usc:25:402
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section402
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 12 (Lease, Sale, Or Surrender Of Allotted Or Unallotted Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The surplus lands of any tribe may be leased for farming purposes by the council of such tribe under the same rules and regulations and for the same term of years as was on August 15, 1894, allowed in the case of leases for grazing purposes.
