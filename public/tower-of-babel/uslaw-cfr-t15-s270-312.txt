15 C.F.R. § 270.312 — Voluntary submission of evidence.
Citation: us:cfr:15:270.312
Creator: National Institute Of Standards And Technology, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-270.312
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 270 (National Construction Safety Teams). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After the Director establishes and deploys a Team, members of the public are encouraged to voluntarily submit to the Team non-privileged evidence that is relevant to the subject matter of the pending investigation.
