15 C.F.R. § 998.23 — Service obligations for original appointments.
Citation: us:cfr:15:998.23
Creator: National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-998.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 998 (Marine And Aviation Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Officer candidates accepting an original appointment in the NOAA Corps upon graduation from the NOAA Corps Basic Officer Training Class as described in subpart B of this part shall incur an ADSO of 4 years.
