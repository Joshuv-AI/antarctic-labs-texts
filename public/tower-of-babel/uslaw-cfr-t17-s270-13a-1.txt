17 C.F.R. § 270.13a-1 — a-1 Exemption for change of status by temporarily diversified company.
Citation: us:cfr:17:270.13a-1
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-270.13a-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 270 (Rules And Regulations, Investment Company Act Of 1940). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A change of its subclassification by a registered management company from that of a diversified company to that of a nondiversified company shall be exempt from the provisions of section 13(a)(1) of the Act (54 Stat. 811; 15 U.S.C. 80a-13), if such change occurs under the following circumstances:
(a) Such company was a nondiversified company at the time of its registration pursuant to section 8(a) (54 Stat. 803; 15 U.S.C. 80a-8), or thereafter legally became a nondiversified company.
(b) After its registration and within 3 years prior to such change, such company became a diversified company.
(c) At the time such company became a diversified company, its registration statement filed pursuant to section 8(b) (54 Stat. 803; 15 U.S.C. 80a-8), as supplemented and modified by any amendments and reports theretofore filed, did not stated that the registrant proposed to become a diversified company.
