31 C.F.R. § 357.42 — Liability of Department and Federal Reserve Banks.
Citation: us:cfr:31:357.42
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-357.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 357 (Regulations Governing Book-Entry Treasury Bonds, Notes And Bills Held In Treasury/Reserve Automated Debt Entry System (Trades) And Legacy Treasury Direct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department and the Federal Reserve Banks may rely on the information provided in a tender, transaction request form, or Transfer Message, and are not required to verify the information. The Department and the Federal Reserve Banks shall not be liable for any action taken in accordance with the information set out in a tender, transaction request form, or Transfer Message, or evidence submitted in support thereof.
