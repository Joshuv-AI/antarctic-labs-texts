10 C.F.R. § 605.16 — Indirect cost limitations.
Citation: us:cfr:10:605.16
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-605.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 605 (The Office Of Science Financial Assistance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Awards issued under this part for conferences and scientific/technical meetings will not include payment for indirect costs.
