24 C.F.R. § 574.440 — Confidentiality.
Citation: us:cfr:24:574.440
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Community Planning And Development, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-574.440
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 574 (Housing Opportunities For Persons With Aids). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The grantee shall agree, and shall ensure that each project sponsor agrees, to ensure the confidentiality of the name of any individual assisted under this part and any other information regarding individuals receiving assistance.
