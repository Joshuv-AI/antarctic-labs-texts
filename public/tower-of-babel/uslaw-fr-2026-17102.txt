DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project No. 7404-011]


Glencoe Mill, LLC; Notice of Availability of Environmental 
Assessment

    In accordance with the National Environmental Policy Act of 1969 
and the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission's (Commission or FERC)

[[Page 54322]]

regulations, 18 CFR part 380, Commission staff reviewed the Glencoe 
Mill, LLC's application to surrender the exemption for the Glencoe Mill 
Hydroelectric Project No. 7404 and have prepared an Environmental 
Assessment (EA) for the project.\1\ The exemptee is proposing to 
surrender the project's exemption from licensing but does not propose 
any additional activities at the project. The project is located on the 
Haw River in Alamance County, North Carolina. The project does not 
occupy federal lands.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ The unique identification number for documents relating to 
this environmental review is EAXX-019-20-000-1775046641.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    The EA contains Commission staff's analysis of the potential 
environmental effects of the proposed surrender, alternatives to the 
proposed action, and concludes that the proposed surrender of exemption 
for the Glencoe Mill Hydroelectric Project would not constitute a major 
federal action that would significantly affect the quality of the human 
environment.
