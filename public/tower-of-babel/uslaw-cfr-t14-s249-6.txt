14 C.F.R. § 249.6 — Destruction of records.
Citation: us:cfr:14:249.6
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-249.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 249 (Preservation Of Air Carrier Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Upon the expiration of the period of preservation prescribed in this part, records may be destroyed at the option of the carrier.
(b) Unless otherwise specified, duplicate copies of records may be destroyed at any time if they contain no significant information not shown on the originals.
