Weakly Compatible Mappings and Common Fixed Points Under Generalized Contractive Conditions
Alemayehu Negash, Meaza Bogale

Abstract
This paper establishes new common fixed point theorems for weakly compatible mappings in metric spaces, relaxing traditional requirements such as continuity, compatibility, and reciprocal continuity. We present a unified framework for three self-mappings T T , f f , and g g with a contractive condition involving a control function ψ \psi , along with corollaries extending results to pairs of mappings and upper semi-continuous control functions. Further generalizations include iterated mappings and sequences of mappings. Rigorous examples demonstrate the necessity of hypotheses and show our results strictly generalize theorems by Al-Thagafi et. al. [ 1 ] , Babu et. al. [ 4 ] , Jungck [ 7 , 8 ] , Singh [ 16 , 17 ] , Som [ 19 ] , Song [ 20 ] and Zhang et. al. [ 21 ] . Key advancements include eliminating completeness assumptions on the entire space and relaxing mapping compatibility conditions. Keywords: Common fixed point, metric space, compatible maps, weakly compatible maps, reciprocal continuity. AMS(2000) Mathematics Subject Classification: 47H10, 54H25.

in

We write

(3.3)

In the sequence

for some

, then

\displaystyle=d(Tx_{2n+1},Tx_{2n})

\displaystyle\leq\psi\Big(\max\big\{d(fx_{2n+1},gx_{2n}),d(Tx_{2n+1},fx_{2n+1}),

\displaystyle\quad d(Tx_{2n},gx_{2n}),\tfrac{1}{2}[d(Tx_{2n+1},gx_{2n})+d(Tx_{2n},fx_{2n+1})]\big\}\Big)

\displaystyle d(Tx_{2n},gx_{2n})

is the unique common fixed point of
T^{2}
and
f
. We have

F(T^{m})\cap F(f)=\left\{\begin{array}[]{cc}\{2\}&\text{if }m\text{ is even}\\
\\
\emptyset&\text{if }m\text{ is odd.}\end{array}\right.

Hence
F(T^{m})\cap F(f)\neq\emptyset
for all
m
even, and
Tf(2)=T(2)=\frac{1}{2}
while
fT(2)=f\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)=2
, so that
T
and
f
do not commute on
F(T^{m})\cap F(f)
for any
m
even; consequently,
T
and
f
have no common fixed points.

We now extend Corollary (
3.4
) to a sequence of selfmaps.

Theorem 4.5
.

Let
\{T_{n}\}_{n=1}^{\infty}
and
f
be selfmaps of a subset
K
of a metric space
(X,d)
satisfying the condition

T_{1}(K)\subset f(K).

(4.6)

Assume that

d(T_{1}x,T_{j}y)\leq\varphi\left(\max\left\{\begin{aligned} &d(fx,fy),d(T_{1}x,fx),d(T_{j}y,fy),\\
&\tfrac{1}{2}[d(T_{1}x,fy)+d(T_{j}y,fx)]\end{aligned}\right\}\right)

(4.7)

for all
x,y
in
K
and for all
j
, where
\varphi:\mathbb{R}_{+}\to\mathbb{R}_{+}
is monotonically increasing and continuous on
\mathbb{R}_{+}
and
0\leq\varphi(t)<t
for
t>0
. Further assume that either
T_{1}(K)
or
f(K)
is complete and the pair of mappings
(T_{1},f)
is weakly compatible. Then
\{T_{n}\}_{n=1}^{\infty}
and
f
have a unique common fixed point in
K
.

Proof
.

By Corollary (
3.4
),
T_{1}
and
f
have a unique fixed point
z
in
K
. Thus,

T_{1}z=fz=z.

(4.8)

Now, let
j\in\mathbb{N}
with
j\neq 1
. Then from the inequality (
4.7
) and using (
4.8
), we get

\displaystyle d(T_{1}z,T_{j}z)

\displaystyle\leq\varphi\left(\max\left\{\begin{aligned} &d(fz,fz),d(T_{1}z,fz),d(T_{j}z,fz),\\
&\tfrac{1}{2}[d(T_{1}z,fz)+d(T_{j}z,fz)]\end{aligned}\right\}\right)

\displaystyle=\varphi\left(\max\left\{d(z,z),d(z,z),d(T_{j}z,z),\tfrac{1}{2}[d(z,z)+d(T_{j}z,z)]\right\}\right)

\displaystyle=\varphi(d(T_{j}z,z))<d(T_{j}z,z),

a contradiction. Hence
T_{j}z=z
.

The uniqueness of
z
follows from inequality (
4.7
). Therefore,
z
is the unique common fixed point of the sequence of selfmaps
\{T_{n}\}_{n=1}^{\infty}
and
f
.
∎

Remark 4.6
.

Theorem (

) follows as a corollary to Theorem (

) in the sense that inequality (
1.5
) implies inequality (
4.7

Example 4.7
.

Let

\frac{4}{3}-x&\frac{2}{3}\leq x<1\end{cases}
