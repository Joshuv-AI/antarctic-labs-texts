7 C.F.R. § 989.35 — Powers.
Citation: us:cfr:7:989.35
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-989.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 989 (Raisins Produced From Grapes Grown In California). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Committee shall have the following powers:
(a) To administer the terms and provisions of this part;
(b) To make rules and regulations to effectuate the terms and provisions of this part;
(c) To recommend to the Secretary amendments to this part; and
(d) To receive, investigate, and report to the Secretary complaints of violations of this part.
