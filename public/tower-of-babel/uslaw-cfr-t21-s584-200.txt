21 C.F.R. § 584.200 — Ethyl alcohol containing ethyl acetate.
Citation: us:cfr:21:584.200
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-584.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 584 (Food Substances Affirmed As Generally Recognized As Safe In Feed And Drinking Water Of Animals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The feed additive ethyl alcohol containing ethyl acetate meets the requirement of 27 CFR 21.62, being not less than 92.5 percent ethyl alcohol, each 100 gallons having had added the equivalent of 4.25 gallons of 100 percent ethyl acetate. It is used in accordance with good feeding practices in ruminant feed supplements as a source of added energy.
