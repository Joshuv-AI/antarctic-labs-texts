36 U.S.C. § 130109 — Records and inspection
Citation: us:usc:36:130109
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section130109
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1301 (Ladies Of The Grand Army Of The Republic). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Records.—
The corporation shall keep—
(1)
correct and complete records of account; and
(2)
minutes of the proceedings of its national conventions and council of administration.
(b) Inspection.—
A member, or an agent or attorney of the member, may inspect the records of the corporation for any proper purpose, at any reasonable time.
