29 C.F.R. § 23.70 — Waiver of rights.
Citation: us:cfr:29:23.70
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-23.70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 23 (Increasing The Minimum Wage For Federal Contractors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Workers cannot waive, nor may contractors induce workers to waive, their rights under Executive Order 14026 or this part.
