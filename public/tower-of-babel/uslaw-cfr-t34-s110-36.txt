34 C.F.R. § 110.36 — Hearings, decisions, and post-termination proceedings.

Creator: Office For Civil Rights, Department Of Education
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 110 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Age In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The following ED procedural provisions applicable to Title VI of the Civil Rights Act of 1964 also apply to ED's enforcement of these regulations: 34 CFR 100.9 and 100.10 and 34 CFR part 101.
(b) Action taken under section 305 of the Act is subject to judicial review as provided by section 306 of the Act.
