43 C.F.R. § 30.419 — What happens after the successful bidder submits payment?
Citation: us:cfr:43:30.419
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-30.419
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 30 (Indian Probate Hearings Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the judge is notified by BIA that BIA has received payment, the judge will issue an order:
(a) Approving the sale and stating that title must transfer as of the date the order becomes final; and
(b) For the sale of an interest subject to a life estate, directing allocation of the proceeds of the sale and accrued income among the holder of the life estate and the holders of any remainder interests using 25 CFR part 179.
