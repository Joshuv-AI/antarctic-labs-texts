25 C.F.R. § 115.412 — Will child support payments be accepted for deposit into a minor's supervised account?
Citation: us:cfr:25:115.412
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-115.412
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 115 (Trust Funds For Tribes And Individual Indians). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary will not accept child support payments for deposit into a minor's supervised account.
