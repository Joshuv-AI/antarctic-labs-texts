33 U.S.C. § 2238d — Maintenance of harbors of refuge
Citation: us:usc:33:2238d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section2238d
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 36 (Water Resources Development); Subchapter II (Harbor Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to maintain federally authorized harbors of refuge to restore and maintain the authorized dimensions of the harbors.
