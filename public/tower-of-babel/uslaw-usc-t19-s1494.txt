19 U.S.C. § 1494 — Expense of weighing and measuring
Citation: us:usc:19:1494
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section1494
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 4 (Tariff Act Of 1930); Part III (Ascertainment, Collection, And Recovery Of Duties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In all cases in which the invoice or entry does not state the weight, quantity, or measure of the merchandise, the expense of ascertaining the same shall be collected from the importer of record before its release from customs custody.
