12 C.F.R. § 1002.113 — Severability.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1002.113
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1002.113
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1002 (Equal Credit Opportunity Act (Regulation B)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If any provision of this subpart, or any application of a provision, is stayed or determined to be invalid, the remaining provisions or applications are severable and shall continue in effect.
