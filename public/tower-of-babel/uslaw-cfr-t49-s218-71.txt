49 C.F.R. § 218.71 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:49:218.71
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-218.71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 218 (Railroad Operating Practices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes minimum requirements governing protection of camp cars that house railroad employees. The rule does not apply to such cars while they are in a train.
