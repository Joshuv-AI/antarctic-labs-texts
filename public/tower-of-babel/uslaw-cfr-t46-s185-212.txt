46 C.F.R. § 185.212 — Mandatory chemical testing following serious marine incidents.
Citation: us:cfr:46:185.212
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-185.212
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 185 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A marine employer whose vessel is involved in a casualty or incident that is, or is likely to become, a serious marine incident as defined in § 4.03-2 of subchapter A of this chapter shall comply with the requirements of § 4.06 in subchapter A of this chapter.
