20 U.S.C. § 3981 — Statement of purpose
Citation: us:usc:20:3981
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section3981
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 52 (Education For Economic Security); Subchapter III (Partnerships In Education For Mathematics, Science, And Engineering); Part A (Higher Education Partnerships). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the purpose of this part to supplement State and local resources to—
(1)
improve the quality of instruction in the fields of mathematics, science, and engineering in the State;
(2)
furnish additional resources and support for research, student scholarships, and faculty exchange programs in the fields of mathematics, science, and engineering; and
(3)
encourage partnerships in education between the business community, institutions of higher education, and elementary and secondary schools in the community.
