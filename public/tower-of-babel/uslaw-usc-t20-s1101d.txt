20 U.S.C. § 1101d — Special rule
Citation: us:usc:20:1101d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1101d
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 28 (Higher Education Resources And Student Assistance); Subchapter V (Developing Institutions); Part A (Hispanic-Serving Institutions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No Hispanic-serving institution that is eligible for and receives funds under this subchapter may receive funds under part A or B of subchapter III during the period for which funds under this subchapter are awarded.
