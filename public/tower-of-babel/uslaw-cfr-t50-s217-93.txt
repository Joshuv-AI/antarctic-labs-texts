50 C.F.R. § 217.93 — Prohibitions.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 217 (Regulations Governing The Take Of Marine Mammals Incidental To Specified Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except for the taking permitted in § 217.90 and authorized by the LOA issued under § 216.106 of this chapter, it is unlawful for any person to do any of the following in connection with the specified activities:
(1) Violate or fail to comply with the terms, conditions, and requirements of this subpart or the LOA issued under this subpart;
(2) Take any marine mammal not specified in § 217.90;
(3) Take any marine mammal specified in the LOA in any manner other than as specified in the LOA;
(4) Take any marine mammal specified in § 217.90 after NMFS determines such taking results in more than a negligible impact on the species or stock of such marine mammal; or
(5) Take any marine mammal specified in § 217.90 after NMFS determines such taking results in an unmitigable adverse impact on the species or stock of such marine mammal for taking for subsistence uses.
