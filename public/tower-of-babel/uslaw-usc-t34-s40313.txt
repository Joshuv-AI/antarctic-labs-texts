34 U.S.C. § 40313 — Enactment and consent of the United States
Citation: us:usc:34:40313
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section40313
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 403 (Criminal Justice Identification, Information, And Communication); Subchapter II (Exchange Of Criminal History Records For Noncriminal Justice Purposes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The National Crime Prevention and Privacy Compact, as set forth in section 40316 of this title, is enacted into law and entered into by the Federal Government. The consent of Congress is given to States to enter into the Compact.
