6 U.S.C. § 181 — Under Secretary for Science and Technology
Citation: us:usc:6:181
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section181
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 1 (Homeland Security Organization); Subchapter III (Science And Technology In Support Of Homeland Security). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There shall be in the Department a Directorate of Science and Technology headed by an Under Secretary for Science and Technology.
