46 C.F.R. § 45.103 — Structural stress and stability.
Citation: us:cfr:46:45.103
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-45.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 45 (Great Lakes Load Lines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The nature and stowage of the cargo, ballast, and other variable weights must be such as to make the vessel stable and avoid unacceptable structural stress.
(b) The vessel must meet all applicable stability and subdivision requirements of this chapter.
