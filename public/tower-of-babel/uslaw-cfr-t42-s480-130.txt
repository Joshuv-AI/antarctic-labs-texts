42 C.F.R. § 480.130 — Disclosure to the Department.
Citation: us:cfr:42:480.130
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-480.130
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 480 (Acquisition, Protection, And Disclosure Of Quality Improvement Organization Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as limited by § 480.139(a) and § 480.140 of this subpart, QIOs must disclose to the Department all information requested by the Department in the manner and form requested. The information can include confidential and non-confidential information and requests can include those made by any component of the Department, such as CMS.
