46 C.F.R. § 32.56-25 — -25 Category A machinery spaces: Windows and port lights—T/ALL.
Citation: us:cfr:46:32.56-25
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-32.56-25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 32 (Special Equipment, Machinery, And Hull Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except as provided in paragraph (b) of this section and subpart 111.105, of this chapter, boundaries of category A machinery spaces and boundaries of cargo pumprooms must not be pierced for windows or portlights.
(b) Skylights that can be closed from outside the spaces they serve may be fitted in boundaries of category A machinery spaces.
