25 C.F.R. § 161.717 — What happens if BIA does not collect enough money to satisfy the penalty?
Citation: us:cfr:25:161.717
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-161.717
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 161 (Navajo Partitioned Lands Grazing Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

BIA will send written notice to the trespasser demanding immediate settlement and advising the trespasser that unless settlement is received within 5 business days from the date of receipt, BIA will forward the case for appropriate legal action. BIA may send a copy of the notice to the Navajo Nation, permittee, and any known lien holders.
