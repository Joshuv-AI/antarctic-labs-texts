CONFERENCE SECTION Nonfactorizable charming loops in exclusive FCNC B B decays
D.I. Melikhov

Abstract
We compare (i) nonfactorizable charm-quark loops in exclusive FCNC B-decays and (ii) three-particle contributions to the amplitude of semileptonic B-decay. Both amplitudes are given in the heavy-quark limit, m b → ∞ m_{b}\to\infty , by a convolution of a hard kernel and a three-particle wave function of the B B -meson, ⟨ 0 | q ¯ ​ ( x ) ​ G μ ​ ν ​ ( z ) ​ b ​ ( 0 ) | B ⁡ ( p ) ⟩ \langle 0|\bar{q}(x)G_{\mu\nu}(z)b(0)|B(p)\rangle . An essential difference between the two amplitudes is that the amplitude of semileptonic B B -decay involves this 3-particle wave function in a collinear light-cone configuration , whereas the amplitude of nonfactorizable charm in FCNC B B -decays involves this 3-particle wave function in a double collinear light-cone configuration .

It is convenient to use light-cone coordinates: For any 4-vectors

We shall consider the case

(+)
-axis, and

\displaystyle p=(q_{+},q^{\prime}_{-},0),\quad q=(q_{+},0,0),\quad q^{\prime}=(0,q_{-},0),\quad q_{+}\propto M_{B},\quad q^{\prime}_{-}\propto M_{B}.

(8)

2.1
The
x
-vertex

We introduce
\kappa=k+q
, the momentum carried by the constituent field
\phi(x)
:

\displaystyle\int\frac{dxd\kappa}{\mu^{2}-(\kappa-q)^{2}}e^{i\kappa x}\langle 0|\phi(x)...|B(p)\rangle.

(9)

Due to properties of the
B
-meson wave function, the vector
\kappa
is soft,

\kappa_{\mu}\sim O(\Lambda_{\rm QCD})
,
reflecting the fact that the heavy
b
quark carries almost the full momentum of the heavy
B
-meson, whereas the light degrees of freedom carry its small fraction
O(\Lambda_{\rm QCD}/M_{B})
.

The component
q_{+}\sim M_{B}
is large and the propagator is highly virtual,

\displaystyle\mu^{2}-2\kappa_{-}(\kappa_{+}-q_{+})+\kappa_{\perp}^{2}\sim\Lambda_{\rm QCD}M_{B}.

(10)

Let us try to expand the field operator
\phi(x)
near
x=0
. The expansion in powers of
x_{-}
and
x_{\perp}
leads
to a well-behaved Taylor series for the amplitude because

\displaystyle x_{-}e^{i\kappa_{+}x_{-}}\frac{1}{\mu^{2}-2\kappa_{-}(\kappa_{+}-q_{+})+\kappa_{\perp}^{2}}\to e^{i\kappa_{+}x_{-}}\frac{\kappa_{-}}{(\mu^{2}-2\kappa_{-}(\kappa_{+}-q_{+})+\kappa_{\perp}^{2})^{2}}.

(11)

Since
\kappa_{-}=O(\Lambda_{\rm QCD})
and the virtuality of the propagator is
O(\Lambda_{\rm QCD}M_{B})
,
any term
(x_{-})^{n}
is suppressed by a factor
(1/M_{B})^{n}
compared to the term
(x_{-})^{0}
.
The same property holds for
(x_{\perp})^{n}
.

However, for powers of the variable
x_{+}
the situation is different:

\displaystyle x_{+}e^{i\kappa_{-}x_{+}}\frac{1}{\mu^{2}-2\kappa_{-}(\kappa_{+}-q_{+})+\kappa_{\perp}^{2}}\to e^{i\kappa_{-}x_{+}}\frac{q_{+}}{(\mu^{2}-2\kappa_{-}(\kappa_{+}-q_{+})+\kappa_{\perp}^{2})^{2}}.

(12)

q_{+}\sim M_{B}
, all powers of
x_{+}^{n}
have the same order of magnitude
O(1)
:
the Taylor expansion of
\phi(x_{+})
near
x_{+}=0
does not lead to hierarchy
in the corresponding expansion of the
B
-decay amplitude.
We need to keep the full
x_{+}
-dependence of the operator
\phi(x_{+})
on the light cone (
x^{2}=0

So, the leading term of the expansion of the
B
-decay amplitude related to the
x
-vertex
corresponds to the expansion near
x_{-}=0,x_{\perp}=0
and has the form

\displaystyle\int\frac{dx_{+}dx_{-}dx_{\perp}}{(2\pi)^{4}}\frac{d\kappa_{+}d\kappa_{-}d\kappa_{\perp}}{\mu^{2}-2(\kappa_{+}-q_{+})k_{-}+k_{\perp}^{2}}e^{i\kappa_{+}x_{-}+i\kappa_{-}x_{+}-ik_{\perp}x_{\perp}}\langle 0|...\phi(x_{+})...|B(p)\rangle,

(13)

The integrals over
x_{-}
and

Integrating these

\displaystyle\int\frac{dx_{+}d\kappa_{-}}{2\pi}\frac{e^{i\kappa_{-}x_{+}}}{2q_{+}\kappa_{-}-i0}\langle 0|...\phi(x_{+})...|B(p)\rangle.

5
Summary

We studied a
B
-decay amplitude of a generic form factor topology (an amplitude given by diagrams in which the heavy
field hits an intermediate point of the line along which fast light degrees of freedom propagate) and obtained the following results:

\bullet
The 3-particle contribution to the amplitudes describing nonfactorizable charming loops in FCNC
B
decays,
A_{\rm NFcc}
,
may be calculated in the
B
-meson rest frame specified by the conditions:

\displaystyle q_{\mu}\propto a_{\mu},\quad q^{\prime}_{\mu}\propto a^{\prime}_{\mu},\quad a^{2}=0,\quad a^{\prime 2}=0,\quad aa^{\prime}=2,

(24)

as the convolution of (i) the hard kernel (hard propagators) and (ii)
the three-particle quark-antiquark-gluon wave function of the
B
-meson in a
double collinear LC configuration

\displaystyle\langle 0|\bar{q}(\tau a_{\mu})b(0)G_{\mu\nu}(\tau^{\prime}a^{\prime}_{\mu})|B(p)\rangle.

(25)

Eq. (
16
) gives the corresponding convolution formula; corrections to this formula are suppressed by powers of
m_{b}
.
The application of double-collinear 3-particle wave functions to FCNC
B
-decays in QCD were presented in
wang2022
;
wang2023
;
bbm2023
;
bbm2024
.

\bullet
The 3-particle contributions to the amplitude of SL
B
-decays,
A_{\rm SL}
, may be calculated in the reference frame
(
24
) as the convolution of (i) the hard kernel and (ii) the three-particle quark-antiquark-gluon wave function of the

\displaystyle\langle 0|\bar{q}(\tau a^{\prime}_{\mu})b(0)G_{\mu\nu}(v\tau a^{\prime}_{\mu})|B(p)\rangle,\quad 0<v<1.

Corrections to the convolution formula are suppressed by powers of
