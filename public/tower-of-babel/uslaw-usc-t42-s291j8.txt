42 U.S.C. § 291j–8 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:42:291j–8
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section291j–8
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter IV (Construction And Modernization Of Hospitals And Other Medical Facilities); Part C (Construction Or Modernization Of Emergency Rooms). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In order to assist in the provision of adequate emergency room service in various communities of the Nation for treatment of accident victims and handling of other medical emergencies through special project grants for the construction or modernization of emergency rooms of general hospitals, there are authorized to be appropriated $20,000,000 each for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1971, and the next two fiscal years.
