49 C.F.R. § 551.47 — Who may serve as an agent for a foreign manufacturer?
Citation: us:cfr:49:551.47
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-551.47
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 551 (Procedural Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Only an individual, a domestic firm or a domestic corporation that is a permanent resident of the United States may serve as an agent under this subpart.
