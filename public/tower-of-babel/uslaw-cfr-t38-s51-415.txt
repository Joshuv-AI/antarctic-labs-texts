38 C.F.R. § 51.415 — Restraints, abuse, and staff treatment of participants.
Citation: us:cfr:38:51.415
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-51.415
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 51 (Per Diem For Nursing Home, Domiciliary, Or Adult Day Health Care Of Veterans In State Homes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The State home must meet the requirements regarding the use of restraints, abuse, and other matters concerning staff treatment of participants set forth in § 51.90. For purposes of this section, the references in the cited section to resident refer to a participant in this section.
