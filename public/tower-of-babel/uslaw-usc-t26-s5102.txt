26 U.S.C. § 5102 — Definition of manufacturer of stills
Citation: us:usc:26:5102
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section5102
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 51 (Distilled Spirits, Wines, And Beer); Subchapter A (Gallonage And Occupational Taxes); Part II (Miscellaneous Provisions); Subpart A (Manufacturers Of Stills). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person who manufactures any still or condenser to be used in distilling shall be deemed a manufacturer of stills.
