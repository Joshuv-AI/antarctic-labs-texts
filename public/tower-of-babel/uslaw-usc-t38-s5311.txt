38 U.S.C. § 5311 — Prohibition of certain benefit payments
Citation: us:usc:38:5311
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section5311
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part IV (General Administrative Provisions); Chapter 53 (Special Provisions Relating To Benefits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There shall be no payment of dependency and indemnity compensation, death compensation, or death pension which, because of a widow’s relationship with another man before enactment of Public Law 87–674, would not have been payable by the Veterans’ Administration under the standard for determining remarriage applied by that agency before said enactment.
