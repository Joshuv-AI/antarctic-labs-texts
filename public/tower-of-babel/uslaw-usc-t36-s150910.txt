36 U.S.C. § 150910 — Service of process
Citation: us:usc:36:150910
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section150910
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1509 (National Council On Radiation Protection And Measurements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation shall have a designated agent in the District of Columbia to receive service of process for the corporation. Notice to or service on the agent, or mailed to the business address of the agent, is notice to or service on the corporation.
