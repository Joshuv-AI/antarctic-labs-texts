10 C.F.R. § 140.9 — Modification of indemnity agreements.
Citation: us:cfr:10:140.9
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-140.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 140 (Financial Protection Requirements And Indemnity Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission will publish in the Federal Register a notice of its intent to enter into an indemnity agreement, or agreement amending an indemnity agreement, which contains provisions different from the form of the applicable indemnity agreement set forth in the appendices to this part, as such appendices may be amended from time to time.
