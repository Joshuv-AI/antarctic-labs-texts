42 U.S.C. § 300a–5 — Voluntary participation by individuals; participation not prerequisite for eligibility or receipt of other services and information
Citation: us:usc:42:300a–5
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section300a–5
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter VIII (Population Research And Voluntary Family Planning Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The acceptance by any individual of family planning services or family planning or population growth information (including educational materials) provided through financial assistance under this subchapter (whether by grant or contract) shall be voluntary and shall not be a prerequisite to eligibility for or receipt of any other service or assistance from, or to participation in, any other program of the entity or individual that provided such service or information.
