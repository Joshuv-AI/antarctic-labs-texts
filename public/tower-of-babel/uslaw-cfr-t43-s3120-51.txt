43 C.F.R. § 3120.51 — Competitive auction.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3120.51
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3120.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3120 (Competitive Leases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Parcels will be offered by competitive auction.
(b) A winning bid will be the highest bid by a responsible and qualified bidder, equal to or exceeding the national minimum acceptable bid. The decision of the auctioneer will be final.
