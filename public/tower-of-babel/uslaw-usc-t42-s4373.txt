42 U.S.C. § 4373 — Referral of Environmental Quality Reports to standing committees having jurisdiction
Citation: us:usc:42:4373
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section4373
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 56 (Environmental Quality Improvement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each Environmental Quality Report required by Public Law 91–190 shall, upon transmittal to Congress, be referred to each standing committee having jurisdiction over any part of the subject matter of the Report.
