49 C.F.R. § 1244.7 — Special studies.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1244.7
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1244.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1244 (Waybill Analysis Of Transportation Of Property—Railroads). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Although routine submission of hard copy waybills is not required, the Board may order railroads to submit hard copies of the underlying waybills for special studies.
(b) The Board may order the subject railroads to supply additional data for submitted waybill copies or records for special studies.
