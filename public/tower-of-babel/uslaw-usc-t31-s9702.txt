31 U.S.C. § 9702 — Investment of trust funds
Citation: us:usc:31:9702
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section9702
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 97 (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as required by a treaty of the United States, amounts held in trust by the United States Government (including annual interest earned on the amounts)—
(1)
shall be invested in Government obligations; and
(2)
shall earn interest at an annual rate of at least 5 percent.
