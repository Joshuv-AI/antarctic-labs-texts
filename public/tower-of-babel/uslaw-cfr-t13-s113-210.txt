13 C.F.R. § 113.210 — Military and merchant marine educational institutions.
Citation: us:cfr:13:113.210
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-113.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 113 (Nondiscrimination In Financial Assistance Programs Of Sba—Effectuation Of Policies Of Federal Government And Sba Administrator). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These Title IX regulations do not apply to an educational institution whose primary purpose is the training of individuals for a military service of the United States or for the merchant marine.
