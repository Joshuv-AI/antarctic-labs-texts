14 U.S.C. § 2124 — Promotions; pay and allowances
Citation: us:usc:14:2124
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2124
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 21 (Personnel; Officers); Subchapter I (Appointment And Promotion). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An officer who is promoted under section 2121 of this title shall be entitled to the pay and allowances of the grade to which promoted from his date of rank in such grade.
