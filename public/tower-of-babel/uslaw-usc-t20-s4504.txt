20 U.S.C. § 4504 — Period for award
Citation: us:usc:20:4504
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section4504
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 57 (James Madison Memorial Fellowship Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Junior fellowships shall be granted for such periods as the Foundation may prescribe but not to exceed two academic years. Senior fellowship shall be granted for such periods as the Foundation may prescribe, but not to exceed five calendar years.
