7 C.F.R. § 57.220 — Information and assistance to be furnished to inspectors.
Citation: us:cfr:7:57.220
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-57.220
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 57 (Inspection Of Eggs (Egg Products Inspection Act)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When surveillance inspection service is performed at any plant, the plant operator shall furnish the inspector such information and assistance as may be required for the performance of inspection functions, preparing certificates, reports, and for other official duties.
