10 C.F.R. § 503.5 — Contents of petition.
Citation: us:cfr:10:503.5
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-503.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 503 (New Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before OFE will accept a petition for either a temporary or permanent exemption under this part, the petition must include all of the evidence and information required in this part and part 501 of this chapter.
