34 C.F.R. § 686.36 — Fiscal control and fund accounting procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:34:686.36
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-686.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 686 (Teacher Education Assistance For College And Higher Education (Teach) Grant Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An institution must follow the provisions for maintaining general fiscal records in this section and in 34 CFR 668.24(b).
(b) An institution must maintain funds received under this section in accordance with the requirements in 34 CFR 668.164.
