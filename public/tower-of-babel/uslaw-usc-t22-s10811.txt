22 U.S.C. § 10811 — Prohibition on importing, and sanctions with respect to, uranium from the Russian Federation
Citation: us:usc:22:10811
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section10811
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 115 (Sanctions With Respect To The Russian Federation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Implementation of prohibition on uranium imports from the Russian Federation
Upon the date of September 18, 2026, the President shall take all necessary steps to implement the requirements of section 2297h–10a(d) of title 42 regarding the importation of uranium from the Russian Federation, including the importation of any uranium from Rosatom State Atomic Energy Corporation or any subsidiary or successor entity.
(b) Sanctions
Beginning on the date described in section 2297h–10a(d)(2)(C) of title 42, and every 180 days thereafter, the President shall impose sanctions described in section 10802(e) of this title with respect to any leaders, officials, senior executive officers, or members of the board of directors of, or principal shareholders with a controlling or majority interest in, Rosatom State Atomic Energy Corporation or any subsidiary or successor entity.
