8 C.F.R. § 1337.9 — Effective date of naturalization.
Citation: us:cfr:8:1337.9
Creator: Executive Office For Immigration Review, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-1337.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 1337 (Oath Of Allegiance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An applicant for naturalization shall be deemed a citizen of the United States as of the date on which the applicant takes the prescribed oath of allegiance, administered either by the Service or an Immigration Judge in an administrative ceremony or in a ceremony conducted by an appropriate court under § 1337.8 of 8 CFR chapter I.
