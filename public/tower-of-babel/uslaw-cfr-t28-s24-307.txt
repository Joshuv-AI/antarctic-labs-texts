28 C.F.R. § 24.307 — Department review.
Citation: us:cfr:28:24.307
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-24.307
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 24 (Implementation Of The Equal Access To Justice Act In Department Of Justice Administrative Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The decision of the adjudicative officer will be reviewed to the extent permitted by law by the Department in accordance with the Department's procedures for the type of proceeding involved. The Department will issue the final decision on the application.
