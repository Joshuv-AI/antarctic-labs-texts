5 C.F.R. § 734.413 — Employees of the Federal Election Commission; prohibitions.
Citation: us:cfr:5:734.413
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-734.413
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 734 (Political Activities Of Federal Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An employee of the Federal Election Commission may not request or receive from, or give to, an employee, a Member of Congress, or an officer of a uniformed service a political contribution.
(b) This section does not cover employee of the Federal Election Commission who are appointed by the President by and with the advice and consent of the Senate.
