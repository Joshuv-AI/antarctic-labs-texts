42 U.S.C. § 7385f — Claims not assignable or transferable; choice of remedies
Citation: us:usc:42:7385f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section7385f
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 84 (Department Of Energy); Subchapter XVI (Energy Employees Occupational Illness Compensation Program); Part C (Treatment, Coordination, And Forfeiture Of Compensation And Benefits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Claims not assignable or transferable
No claim cognizable under this subchapter shall be assignable or transferable.
(b) Choice of remedies
No individual may receive more than one payment of compensation under part B.
