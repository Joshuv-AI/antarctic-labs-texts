42 U.S.C. § 6372i — Records
Citation: us:usc:42:6372i
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section6372i
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 77 (Energy Conservation); Subchapter III (Improving Energy Efficiency); Part F (Energy Conservation Program For Buildings Owned By Units Of Local Government And Public Care Institutions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each recipient of assistance under this part shall keep such records, provide such reports, and furnish such access to books and records as the Secretary may by rule prescribe.
