10 C.F.R. § 960.3-1-4-3 — -1-4-3 Site recommendation for characterization.

Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-960.3-1-4-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 960 (General Guidelines For The Preliminary Screening Of Potential Sites For A Nuclear Waste Repository). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The evidence required to support the recommendation of a site as a candidate site for characterization shall consist of the evaluations and data contained or referenced in the environmental assessment for such site, unless the Secretary certifies that such information, in the absence of additional preliminary borings or excavations, will not be adequate to satisfy applicable requirements of the Act.
