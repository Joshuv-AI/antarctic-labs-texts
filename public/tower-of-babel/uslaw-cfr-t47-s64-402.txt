47 C.F.R. § 64.402 — Policies and procedures for the provision of Wireless Priority Service by wireless service providers.
Citation: us:cfr:47:64.402
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-64.402
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 64 (Miscellaneous Rules Relating To Common Carriers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Wireless service providers that elect to provide Wireless Priority Service to National Security and Emergency Preparedness personnel shall provide Wireless Priority Service in accordance with the policies and procedures set forth in appendix B to this part.
