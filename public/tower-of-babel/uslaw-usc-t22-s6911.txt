22 U.S.C. § 6911 — Establishment of Congressional-Executive Commission on the People’s Republic of China
Citation: us:usc:22:6911
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section6911
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 77 (United States-China Relations); Subchapter II (Congressional-Executive Commission On The People’S Republic Of China). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is established a Congressional-Executive Commission on the People’s Republic of China (in this subchapter referred to as the “Commission”).
