42 C.F.R. § 433.114 — Procedures for obtaining initial approval; notice of decision.
Citation: us:cfr:42:433.114
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-433.114
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 433 (State Fiscal Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) To obtain initial approval, the Medicaid agency must inform CMS in writing that the system meets the conditions specified in § 433.116(c) through (i).
(b) If CMS disapproves the system, the notice will include all of the following information:
(1) The findings of fact upon which the determination was made.
(2) The procedures for appeal of the determination in the context of a reconsideration of the resulting disallowance to the Departmental Appeals Board.
