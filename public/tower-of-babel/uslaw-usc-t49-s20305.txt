49 U.S.C. § 20305 — Inspection of mail cars
Citation: us:usc:49:20305
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section20305
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Safety); Chapter 203 (Safety Appliances). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Transportation shall inspect the construction, adaptability, design, and condition of mail cars used on railroads in the United States. The Secretary shall make a report on the inspection and submit a copy of the report to the United States Postal Service.
