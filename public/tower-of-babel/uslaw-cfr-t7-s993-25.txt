7 C.F.R. § 993.25 — Term of office.
Citation: us:cfr:7:993.25
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-993.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 993 (Dried Prunes Produced In California). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term of office of members, and their respective alternates, shall be two years, ending on May 31 of even numbered years, and any later date which may be necessary for the selection and qualification of their respective successors.
