40 C.F.R. § 451.10 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:40:451.10
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-451.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 451 (Concentrated Aquatic Animal Production Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to the discharge of pollutants from a concentrated aquatic animal production facility that produces 100,000 pounds or more per year of aquatic animals in a flow-through or recirculating system.
