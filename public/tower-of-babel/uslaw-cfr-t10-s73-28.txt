10 C.F.R. § 73.28 — Security background checks for secure transfer of nuclear materials.
Citation: us:cfr:10:73.28
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-73.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 73 (Physical Protection Of Plants And Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Licensees are excepted from the security background check provisions in Section 170I of the AEA if they have not received Orders from the Nuclear Regulatory Commission containing requirements for background checks for trustworthiness and reliability that include fingerprinting and criminal history record checks as a prerequisite for unescorted access to radioactive materials.
