47 U.S.C. § 1512 — Relationship to Middle Class Tax Relief and Job Creation Act of 2012
Citation: us:usc:47:1512
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title47-section1512
Context: United States Code, Title 47 (Telecommunications); Chapter 14 (Making Opportunities For Broadband Investment And Limiting Excessive And Needless Obstacles To Wireless). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to limit, restrict, or circumvent in any way the implementation of the nationwide public safety broadband network defined in section 6001 of title VI of the Middle Class Tax Relief and Job Creation Act of 2012 (47 U.S.C. 1401) or any rules implementing that network under title VI of that Act (47 U.S.C. 1401 et seq.).
