24 C.F.R. § 146.1 — Purpose of the Age Discrimination Act of 1975.
Citation: us:cfr:24:146.1
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Equal Opportunity, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-146.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 146 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Age In Hud Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Age Discrimination Act of 1975 (the Act) prohibits discrimination on the basis of age in programs or activities receiving Federal financial assistance. The Act, however, permits federally assisted programs and activities and recipients of Federal funds to continue to use certain age distinctions and factors other than age which meet the requirements of the Act and this part.
