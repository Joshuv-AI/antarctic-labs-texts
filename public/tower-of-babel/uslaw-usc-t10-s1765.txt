10 U.S.C. § 1765 — Competency development
Citation: us:usc:10:1765
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1765
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 87 (Defense Acquisition Workforce); Subchapter V (General Management Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For each acquisition workforce career field, the Secretary of Defense shall—
(1)
establish, for the civilian personnel in that career field, defined proficiency standards and technical and nontechnical competencies which shall be used in personnel qualification assessments; and
(2)
assign resources to accomplish such technical and nontechnical competencies.
