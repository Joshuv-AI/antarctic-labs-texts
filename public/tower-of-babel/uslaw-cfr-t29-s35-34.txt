29 C.F.R. § 35.34 — Effect of agreements on enforcement effort.
Citation: us:cfr:29:35.34
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-35.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 35 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Age In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance From The Department Of Labor). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An agreement reached pursuant to either § 35.32(c) or § 35.33(a) shall have no effect on the operation of any other enforcement effort of DOL, such as compliance reviews and investigations of other complaints, including those against the recipient.
