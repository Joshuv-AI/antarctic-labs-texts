12 C.F.R. § 229.42 — Exclusions.
Citation: us:cfr:12:229.42
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-229.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 229 (Availability Of Funds And Collection Of Checks (Regulation Cc)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The expeditious return (§§ 229.31(b) and 229.32(b)), notice of nonpayment (§ 229.31(c)), and same-day settlement (§ 229.36(d)) requirements of this subpart do not apply to a check drawn upon the United States Treasury, to a U.S. Postal Service money order, or to a check drawn on a state or a unit of general local government that is not payable through or at a bank.
