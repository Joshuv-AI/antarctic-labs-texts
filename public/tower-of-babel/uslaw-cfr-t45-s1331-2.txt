45 C.F.R. § 1331.2 — Eligibility for grants.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1331.2
Creator: Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1331.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1331 (State Health Insurance Assistance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To be eligible for a grant under this subpart, the State must have an approved Medicare supplemental regulatory program under section 1882 of the Act and submit a timely application to ACL that meets the requirements of—
(a) Section 4360 of Public Law 101-508 (42 U.S.C. 1395b-4);
(b) This subpart; and
(c) The applicable solicitation for grant applications issued by ACL.
