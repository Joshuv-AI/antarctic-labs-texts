7 C.F.R. § 987.81 — Agents.
Citation: us:cfr:7:987.81
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-987.81
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 987 (Domestic Dates Produced Or Packed In Riverside County, California). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary may, by a designation in writing, name any person, including any officer or employee of the United States Government, or name any bureau or division of the United States Department of Agriculture, to act as his agent or representative in connection with any of the provisions of this part.
