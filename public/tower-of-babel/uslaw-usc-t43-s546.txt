43 U.S.C. § 546 — Jurisdiction of district court for enforcement of this subchapter
Citation: us:usc:43:546
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section546
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 12 (Reclamation And Irrigation Of Lands By Federal Government); Subchapter XIV (Patents And Final Water-Right Certificates). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Jurisdiction of suits by the United States for the enforcement of the provisions of this subchapter is conferred on the United States district courts of the districts in which the lands are situated.
