20 U.S.C. § 130aa–5 — Authorization of appropriations

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section130aa–5
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 8 (Howard University); Subchapter II (Endowment). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.
