29 U.S.C. § 53 — “Person” or “persons” defined
Citation: us:usc:29:53
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section53
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 5 (Labor Disputes; Mediation And Injunctive Relief). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The word “person” or “persons” wherever used in section 52 of this title shall be deemed to include corporations and associations existing under or authorized by the laws of either the United States, the laws of any of the Territories, the laws of any State, or the laws of any foreign country.
