7 C.F.R. § 1400.211 — Persons and legal entities not considered to be actively engaged in farming.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1400.211
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1400.211
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1400 (Payment Limitation And Payment Eligibility). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person or legal entity that does not satisfy all of the applicable provisions of §§ 1400.201 through 1400.210 and a landowner who rents land to a farming operation for cash or a crop share guaranteed as to the amount of the commodity will not be considered to be actively engaged in farming with respect to the farming operation.
