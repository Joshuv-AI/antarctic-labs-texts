46 C.F.R. § 130.310 — Radar.
Citation: us:cfr:46:130.310
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-130.310
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 130 (Vessel Control, And Miscellaneous Equipment And Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each vessel of 100 or more gross tons must be fitted with a general marine radar in the pilothouse.
