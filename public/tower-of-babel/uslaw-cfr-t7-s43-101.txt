7 C.F.R. § 43.101 — Meaning of words.
Citation: us:cfr:7:43.101
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-43.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 43 (Standards For Sampling Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Words used in this subpart in the singular form shall be considered to impart the plural, or vice versa, as the case may demand.
