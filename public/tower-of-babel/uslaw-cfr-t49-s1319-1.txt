49 C.F.R. § 1319.1 — Exemption of freight forwarders in the noncontiguous domestic trade from tariff filing requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1319.1
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1319.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1319 (Exemptions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Freight forwarders subject to the Board's jurisdiction under 49 U.S.C. 13531 are exempted from the tariff filing requirements of 49 U.S.C. 13702.
