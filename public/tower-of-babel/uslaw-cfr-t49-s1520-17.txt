49 C.F.R. § 1520.17 — Consequences of unauthorized disclosure of SSI.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1520.17
Creator: Transportation Security Administration, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1520.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1520 (Protection Of Sensitive Security Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Violation of this part is grounds for a civil penalty and other enforcement or corrective action by DHS, and appropriate personnel actions for Federal employees. Corrective action may include issuance of an order requiring retrieval of SSI to remedy unauthorized disclosure or an order to cease future unauthorized disclosure.
