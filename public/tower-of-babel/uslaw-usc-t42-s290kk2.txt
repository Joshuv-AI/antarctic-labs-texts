42 U.S.C. § 290kk–2 — Limitations on use of funds for certain purposes
Citation: us:usc:42:290kk–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section290kk–2
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter III–A (Substance Abuse And Mental Health Services Administration); Part J (Services Provided Through Religious Organizations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No funds provided under a designated program shall be expended for sectarian worship, instruction, or proselytization.
