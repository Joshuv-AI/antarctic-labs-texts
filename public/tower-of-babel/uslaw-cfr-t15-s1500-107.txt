15 C.F.R. § 1500.107 — Confidential information.
Citation: us:cfr:15:1500.107
Creator: Office Of The Under-Secretary For Economic Affairs, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-1500.107
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 1500 (Concrete Masonry Research, Education, And Promotion). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The ballots and other information or reports that reveal, or tend to reveal, the vote of any person covered under the order and the voter list shall be strictly confidential and shall not be disclosed.
