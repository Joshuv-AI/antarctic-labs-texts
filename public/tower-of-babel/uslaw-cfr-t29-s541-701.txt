29 C.F.R. § 541.701 — Customarily and regularly.
Citation: us:cfr:29:541.701
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-541.701
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 541 (Defining And Delimiting The Exemptions For Executive, Administrative, Professional, Computer And Outside Sales Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The phrase “customarily and regularly” means a frequency that must be greater than occasional but which, of course, may be less than constant. Tasks or work performed “customarily and regularly” includes work normally and recurrently performed every workweek; it does not include isolated or one-time tasks.
