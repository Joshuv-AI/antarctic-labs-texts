22 U.S.C. § 2372a — Renewal, reissuance, etc., of export licenses to or for Argentina
Citation: us:usc:22:2372a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2372a
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 32 (Foreign Assistance); Subchapter III (General And Administrative Provisions); Part I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any export license referred to in section 2372 1 of this title which is issued initially on or before September 30, 1978 may from time to time thereafter be renewed, reissued or modified (or in the event of lapse of such license, replacement licenses may be issued), provided that any such renewal, reissuance or modification (or any such replacement license) does not change significantly any such license as initially issued.
