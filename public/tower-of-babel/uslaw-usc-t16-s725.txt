16 U.S.C. § 725 — Regulations, etc., by Secretary of the Interior
Citation: us:usc:16:725
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section725
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 8 (Upper Mississippi River National Wildlife And Fish Refuge). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except where it is specifically provided otherwise, the Secretary of the Interior shall prescribe such regulations, exercise such functions, and perform such duties as may be necessary to carry out the purposes of this chapter.
