34 C.F.R. § 12.9 — What warranties does the Secretary give?
Citation: us:cfr:34:12.9
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-12.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 12 (Disposal And Utilization Of Surplus Federal Real Property For Educational Purposes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary transfers or leases surplus Federal real property on an “as is, where is,” basis without warranty of any kind.
