43 C.F.R. § 2541.1 — Who may apply.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2541.1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2541.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2540 (Color-Of-Title And Omitted Lands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any individual, group, or corporation authorized to hold title to land in the State and who believes he has a valid claim under color of title may make application.
