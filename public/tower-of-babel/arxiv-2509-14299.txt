4 4 -manifolds and non-existence of open book
Shital Lawande, Kuldeep Saha

Abstract
. Following a recent work of Kastenholz [ 6 ] , we show existence of infinitely many parallelizable closed oriented 4 4 -manifolds, none of which admit an open book decomposition. This implies there is no analogue of the Giroux correspondence for Engel manifolds. In particular, not every Engel manifold has a supporting open book, in the sense of Colin, Presas and Vogel [ 1 ] .



1.
Introduction

An open book decomposition of a manifold
M^{m}
is a pair
(V^{m-1},h)
, such that
M^{m}
is diffeomorphic to the quotient space
\mathcal{MT}(V^{m-1},h)\cup_{id}\partial V^{m-1}\times D^{2}
. Here,
V^{m-1}
, called
page
, is a manifold with boundary. The map
h
, called
monodromy
, is a diffeomorphism of
V^{m-1}
that restricts to identity near the boundary
\partial V
, and
\mathcal{MT}(V^{m-1},h)
denotes the mapping torus of
h
. It is well known that every closed, orientable, odd dimensional manifold admits open book decomposition. For closed orientable manifolds of even dimension greater than
4
, Quinn’s signature invariant is the only obstruction to existence of an open book decomposition
[
7
]
. For a long time it was not known whether the vanishing of signature is a sufficient condition in dimension
4
. A recent work of Kastenholz
[
6
]
, has proved that vanishing of signature is not enough for existence of an open book decomposition.

Theorem 1.1

(Kastenholz
[
6
]
)
.

A
4
-manifold admitting open book decomposition must have zero simplicial volume.

The product of two surfaces of genus greater than equal to two has signature zero, but has nonzero simplicial volume. The notion of
simplicial volume
was introduced by Gromov
[
4
]
. For a topological space
X
, let
c=\sum_{i}r_{i}\sigma_{i}
(
\sigma_{i}
s singular simplices) be a real
k
-chain in
C_{k}(X;\mathbb{R})
. The
simplicial
l^{1}
-norm
of
c
is defined to be
\left\lVert c\right\rVert=\sum_{i}|r_{i}|
. The
Gromov norm
of a homology class
\alpha\in H_{k}(X;\mathbb{R})
is then defined as
\left\lVert\alpha\right\rVert=\inf_{z}\left\lVert z\right\rVert
, where
z
runs over all singular cycles representing
\alpha
. For a closed orientable manifold
V
its
simplicial volume

\left\lVert V\right\rVert
is defined as the Gromov norm
of its fundamental class. It is known that a hyperbolic manifold has non-zero simplicial volume (see sections
0.2
and
0.3
in
[
4
]
). Since,
\Sigma_{g}\times\Sigma_{h}
is hyperbolic for
h\geq 2
and
g\geq 2
, by Theorem
1.1
it can not admit an open book decomposition, despite having signature zero.

As an application of Theorem
1.1
, we prove the following.

Theorem 1.2
.

There exist infinitely many parallelizable closed orinted
4
-manifolds, none of which admit open book decomposition.

The Thurston–Winkelenkemper
[
9
]
construction associates a contact structure to an open book with symplectic page and a symplectomorphism as monodromy. The Giroux correspondence
[
3
]
says that the converse is also true. In particular, every contact structure is supported by an open book. Colin, Presas and Vogel
[
1
]
investigated an analogue of the Giroux correspondence for Engel manifolds. Vogel
[
10
]
has shown that every parallelizable
4
-manifold admits an Engel structure and vice versa. Colin, Presas and Vogel
[
1
]
gave an analogue of the Thurston–Winkelenkemper construction for Engel manifolds by associating an Engel structure to an open book with page a contact
3
-manifold with tori boundary components and monodromy which preserves a framing on the page. They asked the following.

Question 1.3
.

(Question
5.6
in
[
1
]
)
Is every Engel structure homotopic to an Engel structure carried by an
open book ?

Theorem
1.2
answers Question
1.3
in negative.

Corollary 1.4
.

There exist infinitely many Engel manifolds that admit no supporting open book.

2.
Proof of Theorem
1.2

Lemma 2.1
.

Let
M^{4}
be a stably parallelizable closed orientable
4
-manifold such that
\chi(M)
is even and
\left\lVert M\right\rVert>0
. Then, there exists a closed orientable
4
-manifold
X
such that
X\#M
is parallelizable and
\left\lVert X\#M\right\rVert>0
.

Proof.

The obstruction to parallelizability of a stably parallelizable closed
4
-manifold is its Euler characteristic. Let
\chi(M)=2k
. When
k=0
, we take
X
to be empty set and the result follows. For
k\geq 1
, we take
X=\#^{k}\mathbb{S}^{1}\times\mathbb{S}^{3}
. Since
\chi(\mathbb{S}^{1}\times\mathbb{S}^{3}\#M)=\chi(M)-2
, we get
\chi(X\#M)=0
. For
k\leq-1
, we take
X=\#^{|k|}\mathbb{S}^{2}\times\mathbb{S}^{2}
. Since
\chi(\mathbb{S}^{2}\times\mathbb{S}^{2}\#M)=\chi(M)+2
, we again have
\chi(X\#M)=0
. Both
\mathbb{S}^{1}\times\mathbb{S}^{3}
and
\mathbb{S}^{2}\times\mathbb{S}^{2}
are stably parallelizable, as they smoothly embed in
\mathbb{R}^{5}
. Since stable parallelizability is preserved under connected sum (see proposition
1.3
in
[
5
]
),
X\#M
is also stably parallelizable with
\chi(X\#M)=0
. Thus,
X\#M
is parallelizable. By Gromov (section
3.5
in
[
4
]
),
\left\lVert X\#M\right\rVert=\left\lVert X\right\rVert+\left\lVert M\right\rVert
. Now, both
\mathbb{S}^{1}\times\mathbb{S}^{3}
and
\mathbb{S}^{2}\times\mathbb{S}^{2}
admits open book decompositions with page
\mathbb{S}^{1}\times\mathbb{D}^{2}
and
\mathbb{S}^{2}\times[0,1]
, respectively, with the identity map as monodromies. Taking boundary connected sum of their pages induces open book structures on their connected sums. Therefore, by Theorem
1.1
,
\left\lVert X\right\rVert=0
. Hence,
\left\lVert X\#M\right\rVert>0
.
∎

The proof of Theorem
1.2
now follows from the fact that
\{\Sigma_{g}\times\Sigma_{h}\}_{g\geq 2,h\geq 2}
gives an infinite family of stably parallelizable closed oriented
4
-manifolds with non-zero simplicial volume and with distinct Euler characteristics of the form
4(g-1)(h-1)
. Among the corresponding parallelizable manifolds
X_{g,h}\#\Sigma_{g}\times\Sigma_{h}
, as constructed in Lemma
2.1
, infinitely many can be distinguished by the number of free generators in their fundamental groups.

3.
Open books and a conjecture of Gromov

Kastenholz’s work connects the existence question for
4
-dimensional open books to the following conjecture of Gromov (conjecture
2
in
[
2
]
).

Conjecture 3.1
.

(Gromov)
If
M
is aspherical and
\left\lVert M\right\rVert=0
, then
\chi(M)=0
.

If Conjecture
3.1
is true in dimension
4
, then along with Theorem
1.1
, it implies that an aspherical closed oriented
4
-manifold
V
can admit open book decomposition only if
\chi(V)=0
. For example, Ratcliffe and Tscantz
[
8
]
have constructed an inifinite family of aspherical closed
4
-manifolds which are integral homology
4
-spheres. A positive answer to Conjecture
3.1
will imply that none of these aspherical manifolds can admit open book decomposition. On the other hand, existence of open book on a
4
-dimensional aspherical manifold with non-zero Euler characteristic will provide a counter example to Conjecture
3.1
in dimension
4
.
