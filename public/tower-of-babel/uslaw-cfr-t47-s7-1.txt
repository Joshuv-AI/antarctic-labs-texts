47 C.F.R. § 7.1 — Who must comply with these rules?
Citation: us:cfr:47:7.1
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-7.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 7 (Access To Voicemail And Interactive Menu Services And Equipment By People With Disabilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules in this part apply to:
(a) Any provider of voicemail or interactive menu service;
(b) Any manufacturer of telecommunications equipment or customer premises equipment which performs a voicemail or interactive menu function.
