21 C.F.R. § 146.137 — Frozen orange juice.
Citation: us:cfr:21:146.137
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-146.137
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 146 (Canned Fruit Juices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Frozen orange juice is orange juice as defined in § 146.135, except that it is frozen.
(b) The name of the food is “Frozen orange juice”. Such name may be preceded on the label by the varietal name of the oranges used, and if the oranges grew in a single State, the name of such State may be included in the name, as for example, “California Valencia frozen orange juice”.
