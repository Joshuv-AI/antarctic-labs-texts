Derived Equivalences of Generalized Grassmannian Flops: D 4 D_{4} and G 2 † G_{2}^{\dagger} Cases
Ying Xie

Abstract
. We prove that the generalized Grassmannian flops of both D 4 D_{4} and G 2 † G_{2}^{\dagger} type induce derived equivalences, which provide new evidence for the DK conjecture by Bondal-Orlov and Kawamta. The proof is based on Kuznetsov’s mutation technique, which takes a sequence of mutations of exceptional objects.



1.
Introduction

1.1.
Background

Flops represent a class of birational transformations that play a crucial role in the minimal model program (MMP), which occurs when two birational varieties have isomorphic canonical bundles. Derived categories of coherent sheaves encode rich geometric information and allow for subtle comparisons between birational models, which has led to the formulation of DK conjectures about the behavior of derived categories under flops by Bondal-Orlov and Kawamata independently:

Conjecture 1.1

(DK conjecture
[
BO02
,
Kaw02
]
)

such that

and

are linearly equivalent (i.e. a K-equivalence). Then

and
X_{2}
are derived equivalent.

DK conjecture holds for typical examples of K-equivalences such as standard flops and Mukai flops, as well as some sporadic flops like

flops (
[

]
,

]

]

]
