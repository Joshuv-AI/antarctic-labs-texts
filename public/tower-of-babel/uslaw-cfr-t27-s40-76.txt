27 C.F.R. § 40.76 — Retention of permit and supporting documents.
Citation: us:cfr:27:40.76
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-40.76
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 40 (Manufacture Of Tobacco Products, Cigarette Papers And Tubes, And Processed Tobacco). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The manufacturer shall retain his permit, together with the copy of the application and supporting documents returned to him with the permit, at the same place where the records required by this part are kept and they shall be made available for inspection by any appropriate TTB officer upon his request.
