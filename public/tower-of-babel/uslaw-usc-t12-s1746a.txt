12 U.S.C. § 1746a — Termination of commitment authority under this subchapter
Citation: us:usc:12:1746a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section1746a
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 13 (National Housing); Subchapter VI (War Housing Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of this subchapter, no mortgage or loan shall be insured under any section of this subchapter after August 2, 1954 except pursuant to a commitment to insure issued on or before such date.
