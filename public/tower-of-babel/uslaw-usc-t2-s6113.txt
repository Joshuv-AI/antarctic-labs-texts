2 U.S.C. § 6113 — Appointment and compensation of employees by President pro tempore of Senate
Citation: us:usc:2:6113
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section6113
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 61 (Senate Leadership); Subchapter II (Vice President And President Pro Tempore). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Effective October 1, 1979, the President pro tempore is authorized to appoint and fix the compensation of such employees as he deems appropriate: Provided, That the gross compensation paid to such employees shall not exceed $123,000 each fiscal year.
