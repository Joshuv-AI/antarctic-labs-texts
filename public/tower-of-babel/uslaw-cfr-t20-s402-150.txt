20 C.F.R. § 402.150 — The FOIA Exemptions 8 and 9: Records on financial institutions; records on wells.
Citation: us:cfr:20:402.150
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-402.150
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 402 (Availability Of Information And Records To The Public). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Exemption 8 permits us to withhold records about regulation or supervision of financial institutions. Exemption 9 permits the withholding of geological and geophysical information and data, including maps, concerning wells.
