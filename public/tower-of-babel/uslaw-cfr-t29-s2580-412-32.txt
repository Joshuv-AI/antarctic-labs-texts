29 C.F.R. § 2580.412-32 — -32 Conditions of exemption.
Citation: us:cfr:29:2580.412-32
Creator: Employee Benefits Security Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-2580.412-32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 2580 (Temporary Bonding Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This exemption applies only to those insurance carriers, service or other similar organizations providing or underwriting welfare or pension plan benefits in accordance with State law.
