7 U.S.C. § 1944 — Soil conservation district loans; limitation; purchase of conservation equipment
Citation: us:usc:7:1944
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1944
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 50 (Agricultural Credit); Subchapter II (Operating Loans). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Loans aggregating not more than $500,000 in any one year may also be made to soil conservation districts which cannot obtain necessary credit elsewhere upon reasonable terms and conditions for the purchase of equipment customarily used for soil conservation purposes.
