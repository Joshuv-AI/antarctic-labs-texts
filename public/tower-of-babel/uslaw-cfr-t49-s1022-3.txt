49 C.F.R. § 1022.3 — Civil monetary penalty inflation adjustment.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1022.3
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1022.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1022 (Civil Monetary Penalty Inflation Adjustment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board shall, immediately, and at least every year thereafter—
(a) By regulation adjust each civil monetary penalty provided by law within the jurisdiction of the Board by the inflation adjustment described in § 1022.4; and
(b) Publish each such adjustment in the Federal Register.
