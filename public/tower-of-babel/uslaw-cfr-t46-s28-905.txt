46 C.F.R. § 28.905 — Repairs and alterations.
Citation: us:cfr:46:28.905
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-28.905
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 28 (Requirements For Commercial Fishing Industry Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No repairs or alterations affecting the safety of the vessel with regard to the hull, machinery, or equipment, shall be made without the notification of a Coast Guard Representative.
