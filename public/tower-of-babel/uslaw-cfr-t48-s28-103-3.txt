48 C.F.R. § 28.103-3 — 28.103-3 Payment bonds.
Citation: us:cfr:48:28.103-3
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-28.103-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 28 (Bonds And Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A payment bond is required only when a performance bond is required, and if the use of payment bond is in the Government's interest.
(b) When a contract price is increased, the Government may require additional bond protection in an amount adequate to protect suppliers of labor and material.
