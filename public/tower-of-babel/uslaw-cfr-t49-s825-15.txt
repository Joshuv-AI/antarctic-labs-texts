49 C.F.R. § 825.15 — Issues on appeal.
Citation: us:cfr:49:825.15
Creator: National Transportation Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-825.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 825 (Rules Of Procedure For Merchant Marine Appeals From Decisions Of The Commandant, U.S. Coast Guard). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The only issues that may be considered on appeal are:
(a) A finding of a material fact is erroneous;
(b) A necessary legal conclusion is without governing precedent or is a departure from or contrary to law or precedent;
(c) A substantial and important question of law, policy, or discretion is involved; or
(d) A prejudicial procedural error has occurred.
