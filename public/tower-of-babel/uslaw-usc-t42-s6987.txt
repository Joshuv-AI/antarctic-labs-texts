42 U.S.C. § 6987 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:42:6987
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section6987
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 82 (Solid Waste Disposal); Subchapter VIII (Research, Development, Demonstration, And Information). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated not to exceed $35,000,000 for the fiscal year 1978 to carry out the purposes of this subchapter (except for section 6982 of this title).
