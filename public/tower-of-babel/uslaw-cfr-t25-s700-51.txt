25 C.F.R. § 700.51 — Custodial parent.
Citation: us:cfr:25:700.51
Creator: The Office Of Navajo And Hopi Indian Relocation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-700.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 700 (Commission Operations And Relocation Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A custodial parent is a person who has the immediate personal care, charge, and control of a minor child who resides in his/her household, or a person who fills the parental role but who is not necessarily blood-related.
