The corresponding author stated that the panel similarities were the result of figure preparation errors and stated that the original data underlying the published results are no longer available due to the time elapsed since the experiments were conducted. They offered to repeat the experiments, but PLOS does not consider that repeat experiment data are sufficient to resolve the concerns in the absence of the original data.

In light of these concerns, which call into question the integrity and reliability of the published results, the PLOS One Editors retract this article.

All authors responded but expressed neither agreement nor disagreement with the editorial decision.

In addition to the above concerns, PLOS is aware of additional concerns involving similarities between the right Fig 2D HSF1 panel in [1] and the right Fig 5D RNA polymerase II panel in [3], but these concerns were not investigated further.
