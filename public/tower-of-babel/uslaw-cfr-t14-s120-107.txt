14 C.F.R. § 120.107 — Substances for which testing must be conducted.
Citation: us:cfr:14:120.107
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-120.107
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 120 (Drug And Alcohol Testing Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each employer shall test each employee who performs a safety-sensitive function for evidence of a prohibited drug during each test required by § 120.109.
