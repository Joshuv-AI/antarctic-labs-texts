50 C.F.R. § 402.04 — Counterpart regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:50:402.04
Creator: Joint Regulations (United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior And National Marine Fisheries Service, National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce); Endangered Species Committee Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-402.04
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 402 (Interagency Cooperation—Endangered Species Act Of 1973, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The consultation procedures set forth in this part may be superseded for a particular Federal agency by joint counterpart regulations among that agency, the Fish and Wildlife Service, and the National Marine Fisheries Service. Such counterpart regulations shall be published in the Federal Register in proposed form and shall be subject to public comment for at least 60 days before final rules are published.
