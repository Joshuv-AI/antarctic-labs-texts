42 U.S.C. § 1489 — Transfer of excess funds out of Rural Housing Insurance Fund
Citation: us:usc:42:1489
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1489
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 8A (Slum Clearance, Urban Renewal, And Farm Housing); Subchapter III (Farm Housing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any sums in the Rural Housing Insurance Fund which the Secretary determines are in excess of amounts needed to meet the obligations and carry out the purposes of such Fund shall be returned to miscellaneous receipts of the Treasury.
