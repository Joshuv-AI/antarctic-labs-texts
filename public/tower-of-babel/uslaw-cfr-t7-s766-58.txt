7 C.F.R. § 766.58 — Installment to be set aside.
Citation: us:cfr:7:766.58
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-766.58
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 766 (Direct Loan Servicing—Special). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Agency will set-aside the first installment due immediately after the disaster occurred.
(b) If the borrower has already paid the installment due immediately after the disaster occurred, the Agency will set-aside the next annual installment.
