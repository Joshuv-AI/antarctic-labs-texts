5 C.F.R. § 582.201 — Agent to receive process.
Citation: us:cfr:5:582.201
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-582.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 582 (Commercial Garnishment Of Federal Employees' Pay). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except as provided in appendix A to this part, appendix A to 5 CFR part 581 lists agents designated to accept service of process under part 581 and this part.
(b) United States Attorneys are not considered appropriate agents to accept service of process.
