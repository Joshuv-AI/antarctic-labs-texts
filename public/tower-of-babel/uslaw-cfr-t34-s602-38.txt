34 C.F.R. § 602.38 — Contesting the Secretary's final decision to deny, limit, suspend, or terminate an agency's recognition.
Citation: us:cfr:34:602.38
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-602.38
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 602 (The Secretary'S Recognition Of Accrediting Agencies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An agency may contest the Secretary's decision under this part in the Federal courts as a final decision in accordance with applicable Federal law. Unless otherwise directed by the court, a decision of the Secretary to deny, limit, suspend, or terminate the agency's recognition is not stayed during an appeal in the Federal courts.
