10 C.F.R. § 32.201 — Serialization of nationally tracked sources.
Citation: us:cfr:10:32.201
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-32.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 32 (Specific Domestic Licenses To Manufacture Or Transfer Certain Items Containing Byproduct Material). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each licensee who manufactures a nationally tracked source after February 6, 2007 shall assign a unique serial number to each nationally tracked source. Serial numbers must be composed only of alpha-numeric characters.
