9 U.S.C. § 304 — Recognition and enforcement of foreign arbitral decisions and awards; reciprocity
Citation: us:usc:9:304
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title9-section304
Context: United States Code, Title 9 (Arbitration); Chapter 3 (Inter-American Convention On International Commercial Arbitration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Arbitral decisions or awards made in the territory of a foreign State shall, on the basis of reciprocity, be recognized and enforced under this chapter only if that State has ratified or acceded to the Inter-American Convention.
