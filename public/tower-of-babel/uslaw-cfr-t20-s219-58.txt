20 C.F.R. § 219.58 — When evidence regarding payment of burial expenses is required.
Citation: us:cfr:20:219.58
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-219.58
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 219 (Evidence Required For Payment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a person applies for the lump-sum death payment because he or she is responsible for paying the funeral home or burial expenses of the employee or because he or she has paid some or all of these expenses, the Board will require evidence of such payment.
