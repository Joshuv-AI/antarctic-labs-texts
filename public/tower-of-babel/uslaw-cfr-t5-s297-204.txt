5 C.F.R. § 297.204 — Access by the representative of the data subject.
Citation: us:cfr:5:297.204
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-297.204
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 297 (Privacy Procedures For Personnel Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A record may be disclosed to a representative of the individual to whom the record pertains after the system manager receives written authorization from the individual who is the subject of the record.
