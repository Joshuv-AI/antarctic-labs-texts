43 C.F.R. § 49.505 — When and how does the Federal land manager serve a notice of violation?
Citation: us:cfr:43:49.505
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-49.505
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 49 (Paleontological Resources Preservation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the Federal land manager believes that a person has committed a violation of this part, he or she may serve a notice of violation in person, by certified mail, return receipt requested, or other verifiable delivery method upon the person.
