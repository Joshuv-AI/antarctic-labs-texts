45 C.F.R. § 264.84 — What will we do if a Territory fails to meet the Matching Grant funding requirements at § 264.80?
Citation: us:cfr:45:264.84
Creator: Office Of Family Assistance (Assistance Programs), Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-264.84
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 264 (Other Accountability Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a Territory does not meet the requirements at either or both of § 264.80(a)(2) and (a)(3), we will disallow all Matching Grant funds received for the fiscal year.
