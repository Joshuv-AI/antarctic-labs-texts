33 U.S.C. § 1476 — Emergencies
Citation: us:usc:33:1476
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section1476
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 28 (Pollution Casualties On The High Seas: United States Intervention). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In cases of extreme urgency requiring measures to be taken immediately, the Secretary may take those measures rendered necessary by the urgency of the situation without the prior consultation or notification as required by section 1475 of this title or without the continuation of consultations already begun.
