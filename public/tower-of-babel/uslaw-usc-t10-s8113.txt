10 U.S.C. § 8113 — Navy and Marine Corps: warrant officer grades
Citation: us:usc:10:8113
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8113
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 812 (Grade And Rank Of Officers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The warrant officer grades in the Navy and the Marine Corps are:
(1)
Chief warrant officer, W–5.
(2)
Chief warrant officer, W–4.
(3)
Chief warrant officer, W–3.
(4)
Chief warrant officer, W–2.
(5)
Warrant officer, W–1.
