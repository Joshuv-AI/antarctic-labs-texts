29 C.F.R. § 810.530 — Verification of labor value content compliance for producers subject to alternative staging regime.
Citation: us:cfr:29:810.530
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-810.530
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 810 (High-Wage Components Of The Labor Value Content Requirements Under The United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement Implementation Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The verification procedures outlined in this subpart apply to producers whether or not they are subject to the alternative staging regime, as outlined in Articles 7 and 8 of the Automotive Appendix.
