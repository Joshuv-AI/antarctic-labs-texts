24 C.F.R. § 3800.60 — Settlements.
Citation: us:cfr:24:3800.60
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-3800.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 3800 (Investigations In Consumer Regulatory Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) At any time during an investigation, the Department and the parties subject to an investigation may conduct settlement negotiations.
(b) When the Secretary or Secretary's designee deems it appropriate, the Department may enter into a settlement agreement.
