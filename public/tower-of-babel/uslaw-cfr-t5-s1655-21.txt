5 C.F.R. § 1655.21 — Loan fee.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1655.21
Creator: Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1655.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1655 (Loan Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The TSP will charge a participant a $50.00 loan fee when it disburses a general purpose loan and a $100.00 loan fee when it disburses a residential loan and will deduct the applicable fee from the proceeds of the loan.
