34 U.S.C. § 50106 — Prohibition of land acquisition
Citation: us:usc:34:50106
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section50106
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 501 (Emergency Federal Law Enforcement Assistance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No funds provided under this chapter shall be used for land acquisition.
