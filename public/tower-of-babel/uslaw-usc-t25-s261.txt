25 U.S.C. § 261 — Power to appoint traders with Indians
Citation: us:usc:25:261
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section261
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 6 (Government Of Indian Country And Reservations); Subchapter III (Traders With Indians). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commissioner of Indian Affairs shall have the sole power and authority to appoint traders to the Indian tribes and to make such rules and regulations as he may deem just and proper specifying the kind and quantity of goods and the prices at which such goods shall be sold to the Indians.
