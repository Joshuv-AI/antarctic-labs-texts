13 C.F.R. § 123.302 — What is the interest rate on an economic injury disaster loan?
Citation: us:cfr:13:123.302
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-123.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 123 (Disaster Loan Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Your economic injury loan will have an interest rate of 4 percent per annum or less.
