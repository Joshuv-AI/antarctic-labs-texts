Hopfological invariants for tame subextensions
Mariko Ohara

Abstract
. Let H H be a finite dimensional Hopf algebra over a field K K . In this paper, we study when an H H -extension becomes a tame H H -extension by calculating Hopfological homology and Hopf-cyclic homology. In the (derived) category of ℋ \mathcal{H} -comodules for a Hopf algebra ℋ \mathcal{H} studied by Farinati [ 3 ] , we take Hopf subalgebra H < ℋ H<\mathcal{H} and a certain order 𝒜 ⊂ H \mathcal{A}\subset H . We see the behavior of Hopfological homology for a tame 𝒜 \mathcal{A} -subextension S / R S/R in terms of the surjectivity of trace map and of cyclic modules, which induce Hopf-cyclic homology, for Hopf-Galois extensions with H H in terms of relative Hopf modules.
