48 U.S.C. § 1469b — Auditing of transactions of territorial and local governments
Citation: us:usc:48:1469b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1469b
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 10 (Territorial Provisions Of A General Nature). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

That all financial transactions of the territorial and local governments herein provided for, including such transactions of all agencies or instrumentalities established or used by such governments, may be audited by the Government Accountability Office, at its discretion, in accordance with chapter 35 of title 31.
