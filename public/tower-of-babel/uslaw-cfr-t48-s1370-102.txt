48 C.F.R. § 1370.102 — 1370.102 Pre-bid/pre-proposal conference and site visit.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1370.102
Creator: Chapter 13—Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1370.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1370 (Universal Solicitation Provisions And Contract Clauses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Insert provision 1352.270-71, Pre-Bid/Pre-Proposal Conference and Site Visit, in solicitations where a pre-proposal conference will be held. The provision is optional for construction and may be modified as necessary. The contracting officer shall include or delete the paragraph regarding site visits.
