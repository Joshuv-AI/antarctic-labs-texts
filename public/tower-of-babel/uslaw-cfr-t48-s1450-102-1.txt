48 C.F.R. § 1450.102-1 — 1450.102-1 Delegation of authority.

Creator: Chapter 14—Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1450 (Extraordinary Contractual Actions And The Safety Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The AS/PMB shall approve all actions under FAR Part 50, except for actions in excess of $55,000, actions which increase the contract price without consideration, and indemnification actions, which shall be approved by the Secretary.
