22 U.S.C. § 1447 — Books and materials
Citation: us:usc:22:1447
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1447
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 18 (United States Information And Educational Exchange Programs); Subchapter II (Interchange Of Persons, Knowledge, And Skills Between United States And Foreign Countries). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to provide for interchanges between the United States and other countries of books and periodicals, including government publications, for the translation of such writings, and for the preparation, distribution, and interchange of other educational materials.
