41 C.F.R. § 105-68.700 — -68.700 When may the suspending official issue a suspension?
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-68.700
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-68.700
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-68 (68—Governmentwide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Suspension is a serious action. Using the procedures of this subpart and subpart F of this part, the suspending official may impose suspension only when that official determines that—
(a) There exists an indictment for, or other adequate evidence to suspect, an offense listed under § 105-68.800(a), or
(b) There exists adequate evidence to suspect any other cause for debarment listed under § 105-68.800(b) through (d); and
(c) Immediate action is necessary to protect the public interest.
