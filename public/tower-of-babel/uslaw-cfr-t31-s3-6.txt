31 C.F.R. § 3.6 — Final denial of a claim.
Citation: us:cfr:31:3.6
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-3.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 3 (Claims Regulations And Indemnification Of Department Of Treasury Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The final denial of an administrative claim shall conform with the requirements of 28 CFR 14.9 and shall be signed by the head of the bureau or office, or his designee.
