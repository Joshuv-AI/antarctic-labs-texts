18 U.S.C. § 599 — Promise of appointment by candidate
Citation: us:usc:18:599
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section599
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 29 (Elections And Political Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever, being a candidate, directly or indirectly promises or pledges the appointment, or the use of his influence or support for the appointment of any person to any public or private position or employment, for the purpose of procuring support in his candidacy shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than one year, or both; and if the violation was willful, shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than two years, or both.
