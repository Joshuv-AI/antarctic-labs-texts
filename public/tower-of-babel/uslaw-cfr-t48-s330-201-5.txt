48 C.F.R. § 330.201-5 — 330.201-5 Waiver.
Citation: us:cfr:48:330.201-5
Creator: Chapter 3—Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-330.201-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 330 (Cost Accounting Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Senior Procurement Executive (SPE) shall exercise the waiver authority under Federal Acquisition Regulation 30.201-5(a)(2). Operating Divisions and Staff Divisions shall forward waiver requests to the SPE.
