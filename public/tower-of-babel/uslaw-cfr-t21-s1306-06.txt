21 C.F.R. § 1306.06 — Persons entitled to fill prescriptions.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1306.06
Creator: Drug Enforcement Administration, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1306.06
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1306 (Prescriptions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A prescription for a controlled substance may only be filled by a pharmacist, acting in the usual course of his professional practice and either registered individually or employed in a registered pharmacy, a registered central fill pharmacy, or registered institutional practitioner.
