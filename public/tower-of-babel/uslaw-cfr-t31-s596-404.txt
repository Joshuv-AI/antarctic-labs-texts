31 C.F.R. § 596.404 — Financial transactions transferred through a bank of a Terrorism List Government.
Citation: us:cfr:31:596.404
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-596.404
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 596 (Terrorism List Governments Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the purposes of this part only, a financial transaction not originated by a Terrorism List Government, but transferred to the United States through a bank owned or controlled by a Terrorism List Government, shall not be deemed a financial transaction with the government of a country supporting international terrorism pursuant to § 596.201.
