42 C.F.R. § 51b.201 — b.201 To which programs does this subpart apply?
Citation: us:cfr:42:51b.201
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-51b.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 51b (PART 51b—PROJECT GRANTS FOR PREVENTIVE HEALTH SERVICES). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this subpart apply to the award of grants under section 317 of the Act for programs to immunize children against vaccine preventable diseases.
