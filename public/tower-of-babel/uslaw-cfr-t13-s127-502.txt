13 C.F.R. § 127.502 — How will SBA identify and provide notice of the designated industries?
Citation: us:cfr:13:127.502
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-127.502
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 127 (Women-Owned Small Business Federal Contract Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

SBA will post on its Internet Web site at http://www.sba.gov a list of NAICS Industry Subsector industries it designates under § 127.501. The list of designated industries also may be obtained from the local SBA district office and may be posted on the General Services Administration Internet Web site.
