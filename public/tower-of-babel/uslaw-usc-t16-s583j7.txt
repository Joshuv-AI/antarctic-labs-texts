16 U.S.C. § 583j–7 — Activities of Foundation and United States Forest Service
Citation: us:usc:16:583j–7
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section583j–7
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 3 (Forests; Forest Service; Reforestation; Management); Subchapter V (Forest Foundation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The activities of the Foundation authorized under the provisions of this Act shall be supplemental to and shall not preempt any authority or responsibility of the United States Forest Service under any other provision of law.
