15 U.S.C. § 715m — Cooperation between Secretary of the Interior and Federal and State authorities
Citation: us:usc:15:715m
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section715m
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 15A (Interstate Transportation Of Petroleum Products). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior, in carrying out the Act of February 22, 1935, as amended (15 U.S.C., ch. 15A), is authorized to cooperate with Federal and State authorities.
