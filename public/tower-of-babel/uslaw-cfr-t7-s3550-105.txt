7 C.F.R. § 3550.105 — Site requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3550.105
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3550.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3550 (Direct Single Family Housing Loans And Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Rural areas. Loans may be made only in rural areas designated by RHS. If an area designation is changed to nonrural an existing RHS borrower may receive 504 assistance.
(b) Not subdividable. The site must not be large enough to subdivide into more than one site under existing local zoning ordinances.
