17 C.F.R. § 203.8 — Service of subpoenas.
Citation: us:cfr:17:203.8
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-203.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 203 (Rules Relating To Investigations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Service of subpoenas issued in formal investigative proceedings shall be effected in the manner prescribed by Rule 232(c) of the Commission's Rules of Practice, § 201.232(c) of this chapter.
