36 U.S.C. § 21905 — Restrictions

Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 219 (The American National Theater And Academy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Profit and Stock.—
The corporation shall be nonprofit and may not issue stock.
(b) Political Activities.—
The corporation shall be nonpolitical and nonsectarian, and may not promote the candidacy of an individual seeking public office.
(c) Honorary Members.—
The corporation may not have honorary members.
