41 C.F.R. § 102-38.5 — -38.5 Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-38.5
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-38.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-38 (38—Sale Of Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part covers the policies governing the sale of surplus and exchange/sale personal property. Unless otherwise indicated, use of pronouns “we,” “you,” and their variants throughout this part refer to the executive agency responsible for the sale of the property.
