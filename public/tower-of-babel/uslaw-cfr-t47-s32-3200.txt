47 C.F.R. § 32.3200 — Accumulated depreciation—held for future telecommunications use.
Citation: us:cfr:47:32.3200
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-32.3200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 32 (Uniform System Of Accounts For Telecommunications Companies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) This account shall include the accumulated depreciation associated with the investment contained in Account 2002, Property Held for Future Telecommunications Use.
(b) This account shall be credited with amounts concurrently charged to Account 6562, Depreciation expense—property held for future telecommunications use.
