48 C.F.R. § 450.102-1 — 450.102-1 Delegation of authority.
Citation: us:cfr:48:450.102-1
Creator: Chapter 4—Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-450.102-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 450 (Extraordinary Contractual Actions And The Safety Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Assistant Secretary for Administration is authorized to approve all actions under FAR part 50 except indemnification actions listed in FAR 50.102-1(d), which must be approved by the Secretary, without power of redelegation.
