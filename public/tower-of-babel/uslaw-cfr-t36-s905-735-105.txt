36 C.F.R. § 905.735-105 — -105 Designation of counselor.
Citation: us:cfr:36:905.735-105
Creator: Pennsylvania Avenue Development Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-905.735-105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 905 (Standards Of Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In accordance with 5 CFR 735.105(a), the General Counsel of the Corporation is designated to be Ethics Counselor and shall serve as the Corporation's liaison with the Civil Service Commission for matters covered in this part.
