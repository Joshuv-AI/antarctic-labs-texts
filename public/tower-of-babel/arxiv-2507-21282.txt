Tightness of Chekanov’s bound on displacement energy for some Lagrangian knots
David Keren Yaar

Abstract
By a classical theorem of Chekanov, the displacement energy, e e , of a Lagrangian submanifold is bounded from below by the minimal area of pseudo-holomorphic disks with boundary on the Lagrangian, ℏ \hbar . We compute e e and ℏ \hbar for displaceable Chekanov tori in ℂ ​ P n \mathbb{C}P^{n} , and for an infinite family of exotic tori in ℂ 3 \mathbb{C}^{3} constructed by Brendel. In these families, e = ℏ e=\hbar . We compare continuity properties of e e and ℏ \hbar on the space of Lagrangians. This provides an example (suggested by Fukaya, Oh, Ohta, and Ono) where e > ℏ e>\hbar . Our calculations have further applications such as a new proof, inspired by work of Auroux, that Brendel’s family of exotic tori consists of infinitely many distinct Lagrangians.
