33 C.F.R. § 127.403 — Inspections.
Citation: us:cfr:33:127.403
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-127.403
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 127 (Waterfront Facilities Handling Liquefied Natural Gas And Liquefied Hazardous Gas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The operator must conduct a visual inspection for defects of each pressure-relief device not capable of being tested, at least once each calendar year, with intervals between inspections not exceeding 15 months, and make all repairs in accordance with § 127.405.
