34 C.F.R. § 690.82 — Maintenance and retention of records.
Citation: us:cfr:34:690.82
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-690.82
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 690 (Federal Pell Grant Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An institution shall follow the record retention and examination provisions in this part and in 34 CFR 668.24.
(b) For any disputed expenditures in any award year for which the institution cannot provide records, the Secretary determines the final authorized level of expenditures.
