25 C.F.R. § 18.304 — What happens if the Department approves the single heir rule?
Citation: us:cfr:25:18.304
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-18.304
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 18 (Tribal Probate Codes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If we approve the single heir rule, we will notify the tribe of the approval and forward a copy of the single heir rule to OHA.
