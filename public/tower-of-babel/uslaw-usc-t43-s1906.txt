43 U.S.C. § 1906 — Authority for cooperative agreements and payments effective as provided in appropriations
Citation: us:usc:43:1906
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section1906
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 37 (Public Rangelands Improvement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter, authority to enter into cooperative agreements and to make payments under this chapter shall be effective only to the extent or in such amounts as are provided in advance in appropriation Acts.
