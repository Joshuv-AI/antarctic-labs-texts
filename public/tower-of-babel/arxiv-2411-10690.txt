Neutron skin thickness for 208 Pb from total cross sections of neutron scattering at 14.137 MeV and neutron skin thickness for 48 Ca, O, N, C isotopes from reaction and interaction cross sections
Shingo Tagami, Takayuki Myo, Masanobu Yahiro

Abstract
Background Foster et al. measured total neutron cross sections σ T \sigma_{\rm T} of n+ 208 Pb scattering at E lab = 14.137 E_{\rm lab}=14.137 MeV, where E lab E_{\rm lab} is an incident energy per nucleon. Carlson et al. measured reaction cross sections σ R \sigma_{\rm R} for p p + 48 Ca scattering in E lab = 23 ​ – ​ 48 E_{\rm lab}=23\text{--}48 MeV. Tanaka et al. measured interaction cross sections σ I \sigma_{\rm I} for 42 ​ – ​ 51 {}^{42\text{--}51} Ca + 12 C scattering at 280 MeV/nucleon. Ozawa et al. accumulated the measured values of σ I \sigma_{\rm I} and the extracted matter radii r m r_{\rm m} from 4 He to 32 Mg. Bagchi et al. measured the charge-changing (CC) cross sections and determined proton radii r p ​ ( CC ) r_{\rm p}({\rm CC}) for 14 , 15 , 17 ​ – ​ 22 {}^{14,15,17\text{--}22} N from the CC cross sections, and found a signal of 22 N being a halo nucleus. Kanungo et al. measured the CC cross sections and extracted r p ​ ( CC ) r_{\rm p}({\rm CC}) for 12 ​ – ​ 19 {}^{12\text{--}19} C. Kaur et al. measured the CC cross sections and determined r p ​ ( CC ) r_{\rm p}({\rm CC}) for 16 , 18 ​ – ​ 24 {}^{16,18\text{--}24} O. Purposes Our 1st aim is to extract neutron tkin thickness r skin 208 r_{\rm skin}^{208} from the the σ T \sigma_{\rm T} of n+ 208 Pb scattering at E lab = 14.137 E_{\rm lab}=14.137 MeV. Our 2nd aim is to determine r skin 48 ​ ( skin ) r_{\rm skin}^{48}({\rm skin}) from σ R \sigma_{\rm R} on p+ 48 Ca scattering in E lab = 23 ​ – ​ 48 E_{\rm lab}=23\text{--}48 MeV. Our 3rd aim is to find light stable nuclei having nuclei having large r skin r_{\rm skin} . Methods We use the Kyushu g g -matrix folding model for lower E lab E_{\rm lab} and the folding model based on the Love-Franey t t -matrix for higher E lab E_{\rm lab} . We tested the Kyushu (chira)l g g -matrix folding model for reaction cross sections σ R \sigma_{\rm R} on p+ 208 Pb scattering in 20 ​ ~ < ​ E lab ​ ~ < ​ 180 20~\,\pic@makebox@{\makebox}{}(1.0,1.0){$\stackrel{{\scriptstyle<}}{{\widetilde{}}}$}\,~E_{\rm lab}~\,\pic@makebox@{\makebox}{}(1.0,1.0){$\stackrel{{\scriptstyle<}}{{\widetilde{}}}$}\,~180 MeV and found that our folding model is reliable. As for 12 C+ 12 C scattering, we tested the Kyushu g g -matrix folding model and found that the folding model is reliable for σ R \sigma_{\rm R} in 30 ​ ~ < ​ E lab ​ ~ < ​ 100 30~\,\pic@makebox@{\makebox}{}(1.0,1.0){$\stackrel{{\scriptstyle<}}{{\widetilde{}}}$}\,~E_{\rm lab}~\,\pic@makebox@{\makebox}{}(1.0,1.0){$\stackrel{{\scriptstyle<}}{{\widetilde{}}}$}\,~100 MeV/u and 250 ​ ~ < ​ E lab ​ ~ < ​ 400 250~\,\pic@makebox@{\makebox}{}(1.0,1.0){$\stackrel{{\scriptstyle<}}{{\widetilde{}}}$}\,~E_{\rm lab}~\,\pic@makebox@{\makebox}{}(1.0,1.0){$\stackrel{{\scriptstyle<}}{{\widetilde{}}}$}\,~400 MeV/u. Results We extract r skin 208 = 0.309 ± 0.057 r_{\rm skin}^{208}=0.309\pm 0.057 fm from the σ T \sigma_{\rm T} . As for 48 Ca, we determine r skin 48 ​ ( skin ) = 0.163 ± 0.037 ​ fm r_{\rm skin}^{48}({\rm skin})=0.163\pm 0.037~{\rm fm} from the σ R \sigma_{\rm R} on p+ 48 Ca scattering, using the Kyushu g g -matrix folding model with the D1M-GHFB+AMP proton and neutron densities, where D1M-GHFB+AMP is Gogny-D1M HFB (D1M-GHFB) with angular momentum projection (AMP). We show that D1M-GHFB+AMP is better than D1S-GHFB+AMP for the matter radius and the binding energy. Our skin value is consistent with r skin 48 ​ ( CREX ) r_{\rm skin}^{48}({\rm CREX}) . As for C, N, O isotopes, we find that r skin = 0.267 ± 0.056 r_{\rm skin}=0.267\pm 0.056 fm for 14 N and r skin = 0.197 ± 0.067 r_{\rm skin}=0.197\pm 0.067 fm for 17 O. Conclusion The value r skin 208 = 0.309 ± 0.057 r_{\rm skin}^{208}=0.309\pm 0.057 fm agrees with r skin 208 ​ ( PREX2 ) r_{\rm skin}^{208}({\rm PREX2}) .
