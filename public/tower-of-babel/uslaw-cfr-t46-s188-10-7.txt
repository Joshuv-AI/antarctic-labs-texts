46 C.F.R. § 188.10-7 — -7 Chemical stores.

Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 188 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This term means those chemicals intended for use in the performance of the vessel's scientific activities and is further defined in § 194.05-3.
