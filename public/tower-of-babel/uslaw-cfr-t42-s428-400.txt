42 C.F.R. § 428.400 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:42:428.400
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-428.400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 428 (Medicare Part D Drug Inflation Rebate Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the purposes of this subpart, date of receipt is the calendar day following the day in which a report of a rebate amount (as set forth in §§ 428.401(b), (c), and (d) and 428.402(b) and (c)) is made available to the manufacturer of a Part D rebatable drug by CMS.
