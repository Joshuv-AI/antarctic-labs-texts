12 C.F.R. § 796.6 — What other definitions and rules of construction apply for purposes of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:12:796.6
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-796.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 796 (Post-Employment Restrictions For Certain Ncua Examiners). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of this part, a person shall be deemed to act as a “consultant” for a federally insured credit union or other company only if the person works directly on matters for, or on behalf of, such credit union.
