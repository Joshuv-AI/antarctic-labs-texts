15 U.S.C. § 1339 — Injunction proceedings
Citation: us:usc:15:1339
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1339
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 36 (Cigarette Labeling And Advertising). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The several district courts of the United States are invested with jurisdiction, for cause shown, to prevent and restrain violations of this chapter upon the application of the Attorney General of the United States acting through the several United States attorneys in their several districts.
