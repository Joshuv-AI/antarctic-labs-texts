43 U.S.C. § 58 — Transcripts from records of Louisiana
Citation: us:usc:43:58
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section58
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 3 (Surveys). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any copy of a plat of survey, or transcript from the records of the office of the former surveyor general of Louisiana, duly certified, shall be admitted as evidence in all the courts of the United States and the Territories thereof.
