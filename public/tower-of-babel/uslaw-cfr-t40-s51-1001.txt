40 C.F.R. § 51.1001 — Applicability of part 51.
Citation: us:cfr:40:51.1001
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-51.1001
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 51 (Requirements For Preparation, Adoption, And Submittal Of Implementation Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions in subparts A through X of this part apply to areas for purposes of the PM2.5 NAAQS to the extent they are not inconsistent with the provisions of this subpart.
