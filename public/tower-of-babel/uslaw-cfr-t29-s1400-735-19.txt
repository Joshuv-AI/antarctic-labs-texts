29 C.F.R. § 1400.735-19 — -19 Influencing Members of Congress.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1400.735-19
Creator: Federal Mediation And Conciliation Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1400.735-19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1400 (Standards Of Conduct, Responsibilities, And Discipline). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No money appropriated to the Service shall be used by any employee of the Service to pay for any personal service, printed or written matter, or other devices intended to influence any Member of Congress regarding any legislation or appropriation before the Congress.
