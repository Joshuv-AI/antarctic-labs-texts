7 U.S.C. § 3175b — Expansion of effective food, nutrition, and consumer education services
Citation: us:usc:7:3175b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section3175b
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 64 (Agricultural Research, Extension, And Teaching); Subchapter IV (National Food And Human Nutrition Research And Extension Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of the program provided for under sections 3175a through 3175e of this title is to expand effective food, nutrition, and consumer education services to the greatest practicable number of low-income individuals, including those participating in or eligible to participate in the programs under the Food Stamp Act of 1977 [7 U.S.C. 2011 et seq.], to assist them to—
(1)
increase their ability to manage their food budgets, including food stamps and other food assistance;
(2)
increase their ability to buy food that satisfies nutritional needs and promotes good health; and
(3)
improve their food preparation, storage, safety, preservation, and sanitation practices.
