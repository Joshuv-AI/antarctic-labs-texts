25 C.F.R. § 36.94 — What must a homeliving handbook contain?
Citation: us:cfr:25:36.94
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-36.94
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 36 (Minimum Academic Standards For The Basic Education Of Indian Children And National Criteria For Dormitory Situations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A homeliving handbook must contain all of the following, and may include additional information:
(a) Mission/Vision Statement;
(b) Discipline Policy;
(c) Parent/Student Rights and Responsibilities;
(d) Confidentiality;
(e) Sexual Harassment Policy;
(f) Violence/Bullying Policy;
(g) Homeliving Policies and Procedures;
(h) Services Available;
(i) Personnel and Position Listing;
(j) Emergency Procedures and Contact Numbers;
(k) Bank Procedures;
(l) Transportation Policy;
(m) Check-Out Procedures;
(n) Dress Code;
(o) Drug/Alcohol Policy;
(p) Computer Usage Policy;
(q) Medication Administration Policy and Procedure; and
(r) Isolation/Separation Policy.
