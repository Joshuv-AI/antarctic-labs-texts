43 U.S.C. § 479 — Shutting off water for nonpayment of construction charge
Citation: us:usc:43:479
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section479
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 12 (Reclamation And Irrigation Of Lands By Federal Government); Subchapter IX (Construction Charges). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No water shall be delivered to the lands of any water-right applicant or entryman who shall be in arrears for more than one calendar year for the payment of any annual construction charge and penalties.
