41 C.F.R. § 60-30.26 — -30.26 Record for recommended decision.
Citation: us:cfr:41:60-30.26
Creator: Chapter 60—Office Of Federal Contract Compliance Programs, Equal Employment Opportunity, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-60-30.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 60-30 (30—Rules Of Practice For Administrative Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The transcript of testimony, exhibits, and all papers, documents, and requests filed in the proceedings, including briefs, but excepting the correspondence section of the docket, shall constitute the record for decision.
