29 C.F.R. § 2701.5 — Petition for review.
Citation: us:cfr:29:2701.5
Creator: Federal Mine Safety And Health Review Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-2701.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 2701 (Government In The Sunshine Act Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person may petition the Commission to review any action he alleges to be in violation of this part or 5 U.S.C. 552b that was taken by any employee or member of the Commission. The petition shall be in writing and shall be filed with the General Counsel within 30 days of the alleged violation. The Commission shall consider and rule upon the petition with expedition.
