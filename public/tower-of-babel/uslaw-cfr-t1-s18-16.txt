1 C.F.R. § 18.16 — Reinstatement of expired regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:1:18.16
Creator: Administrative Committee Of The Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-1/section-18.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 1 (General Provisions), Part 18 (Preparation And Transmittal Of Documents Generally). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-23 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies may reinstate regulations removed from the Code of Federal Regulations data base which have expired by their own terms only by republishing the regulations in full text in the Federal Register.
