45 C.F.R. § 2.6 — Certification and authentication of records.
Citation: us:cfr:45:2.6
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2 (Testimony By Employees And Production Of Documents In Proceedings Where The United States Is Not A Party). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon request, DHHS agencies will certify, pursuant to 42 U.S.C. 3505, the authenticity of copies of records that are to be disclosed. Fees for copying and certification are set forth in 45 CFR 5.43.
