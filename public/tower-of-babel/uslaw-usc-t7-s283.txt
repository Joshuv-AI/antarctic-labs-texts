7 U.S.C. § 283 — Propagation of stock and release of germ plasm
Citation: us:usc:7:283
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section283
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 11 (Honeybees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Agriculture may propagate bee-breeding stock and may release bee germ plasm to the public.
