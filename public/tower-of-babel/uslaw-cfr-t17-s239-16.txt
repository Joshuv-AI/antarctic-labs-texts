17 C.F.R. § 239.16 — Form S-6, for unit investment trusts registered on Form N-8B-2.
Citation: us:cfr:17:239.16
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-239.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 239 (Forms Prescribed Under The Securities Act Of 1933). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This form may be used for registration under the Securities Act of 1933 of securities of any unit investment trust registered under the Investment Company Act of 1940 on Form N-8B-2 (§ 274.12 of this chapter).
