Single-cell RNA sequencing reveals immune cell alterations in patients with allergic rhinitis treated with Peiyuan Tong-qiao decoction
Authors: Jingbo Li, Junjie Wang, Yinian Lu, Yan Wang, Yang Cao, Yuanyuan Li, Yinglin Cui
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
Allergic rhinitis (AR) is a chronic inflammatory disease with complex causes. Traditional Chinese medicine (TCM) shows promise for treating AR. This study aimed to explore the mechanisms behind TCM's effectiveness. In this study, we collected peripheral blood mononuclear cells (PBMCs) from AR patients before and after TCM treatment. Single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq) was performed to identify cell types and differentially expressed genes (DEGs) and analyzed immune cell pathways to understand how TCM treats AR. Cell-cell communication and regulatory networks were also investigated. Accordingly, 11 major cell types were annotated. The strength of the outgoing interaction between CD14+ monocytes and Central Memory CD4+ T cells and Transitional B cells decreased and in-creased, respectively. Transcription factors (TFs), VDR, and RUNX3 regulated the balance of Th1/Th2 cells to improve AR symptoms. This study confirms the therapeutic effect of Peiyuan Tongqiao Decoction on AR and provides insights into its mechanism of action.

Introduction

Allergic rhinitis (AR) is a common chronic inflammatory disorder worldwide that is characterized by pale, paroxysmal, or repeated sneezing; stuffed-up nose; nasal itching; runny nose; headache; ear pain; retracted tympanic membrane; and conjunctival congestion [1,2]. The International Study of Asthma and Allergy in Childhood Phase III research from 236 areas across 98 countries found that the prevalence of AR increased from 8.5 % in people aged 6–7 to 14.6 % in people aged 13–14 [3]. However, this condition is often ignored, understood simplistically, diagnosed ineffectively, and treated incorrectly. Ineffective management of allergic rhinitis symptoms can lead to sleep disturbances, secondary daytime fatigue, cognitive impairment, poor concentration, activity limitations (study and career), and decreased long-term productivity [4]. More importantly, it is clinically relevant because it is associated with several complications (acute and chronic sinusitis, otitis media/otitis media with effusion, nasal polyps, and orthodental problems) [2].

In addition to genetic factors, risk factors linked to AR include allergens such as plant pollen, molds, house dust mites, animal proteins, and other non-IgE chemical substances. It is currently understood that AR result from the nasal mucosa's immunoglobulin E (IgE)-mediated immune response to airborne allergens, which releases biochemical products into the nasal tissue. When a patient is first exposed to an allergen without exhibiting clinical symptoms, the immune system undergoes a sensitization phase that initiates an allergic immunological response. At this stage, the allergen is taken up by dendritic cells in the nasal mucosa, which then triggers B cell activation and IgE class switching. After that, the subsequent allergen (high-affinity IgE receptor) binds to IgE (Cε3 domain) on effector cells in the nasal mucosa contributes to mast cell activation, degranulation, and the release of allergic mediators (such as histamine and sulfidopeptide leukotrienes), which further causes AR symptoms [5,6].

Owing to the treatment of both symptoms and root causes, traditional Chinese medicine (TCM) for allergic rhinitis has been progressively researched and utilized in China. TCM decoctions composed of Chinese herbal formulas and their detailed therapeutic effects have been reported, including Ximin Jian (Ephedra, Cinnamon Twig, White Peony Root), Qi-tonifying (Astragalus, Codonopsis, Dried Ginger), Yifei Tiaoxue Decoction (Astragalus, Atractylodes, Saposhnikovia), and Qingre Tongyang Decoction (Pueraria Root, Scutellaria, Mulberry Leaf) [7]. Compared with Flixonase nasal spray and oral application of loratadine tablets, Ximin not only enhanced the body's immune system but also alleviated nasal and systemic symptoms of allergic rhinitis, including lung and kidney colds. In addition, some potential molecular biological mechanisms of AR have been explored and demonstrated at the theoretical level, such as decreased IgE levels, structural changes in IgE, blockade of antigen binding, pro-inflammatory substance generation, eosinophil cell apoptosis, inhibition of Th2 polarization, Th1/Th2 cell balance, mast cell or Th-cell proliferation and differentiation, cell membrane stabilization, and nuclear transcription factors. However, the inflammatory induction mechanism of AR, extent of harm to the body, and mental health are complex and have not yet been fully elucidated. The interaction networks of TCM components for the treatment of AR, such as targets and pathways, must be studied and clarified more systematically.

To improve understanding of pathological mechanisms and drug action mechanisms, multi-omics techniques (genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, metabolomics, epigenomics, and other emerging omics) are essential for locating various targets of serious health problems [8,9]. Single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq) is a practical tool for single-cell research because of its technical characteristics and high accuracy. It can be applied for novel cell type identification, cellular property characterization, genome annotation, transcriptome detection, and proteome analysis, as well as for determining the cell lineage differentiation track. Recently, it has been widely applied for expounding AR pathogenesis, efficacy, biological pathways, and interaction networks of drug treatments targeting AR. For example, CASP3 was identified by scRNA-seq as a critical DEG and a novel therapeutic target of AR in the progression of the TNF signaling pathway, which contributes to immune upregulation [10].

The aim of this study was to elucidate the biological activity of Peiyuan Tongqiao Decoction, which can act on different AR processes through cell abundance, DEGs, pathways, and transcription factors (TFs). This will confirm the effect of the Peiyuan Tongqiao Decoction in the treatment of AR at a theoretical level, provide a basis for the treatment of AR, and help to find a more effective treatment for this disease.

Materials and methods

Study design and sample collection

This study enrolled two adults who visited the Henan Provincial Hospital of Traditional Chinese Medicine for the treatment of allergic rhinitis from 2022 to 2023. The participants were male (H1) and female (H2). Both subjects met the diagnostic criteria for allergic rhinitis, exhibited seasonal patterns of disease onset, and had no other nasal or pharyngeal pathologies, or chronic or organic diseases.

The subjects were treated with Peiyuan Tongqiao Decoction, composed of the following ingredients: 10 g ginseng, 15 g Bran-fried Yam, 12 g Prepared Rehmannia, 9 g Salt-fried Eucommia, 12 g Angelica Sinensis, 12 g Wine-processed Cornus, 12 g Wolfberry, 6 g Honey-fried Licorice, 9 g Magnolia Flower, 9 g Fried Cocklebur, and 10 g Angelica Dahurica. This decoction was uniformly prepared and decocted by the pharmacy of the Henan Provincial Hospital of Traditional Chinese Medicine and administered once daily in two divided doses for a treatment course of 14 days. Blood samples were collected from the outpatient department before and after 14 days of treatment, followed by PBMC isolation and cryopreservation for subsequent analysis. All the samples were collected during the non-pollen dispersal period.

The symptoms before and after treatment were assessed and quantified using the Total Nasal Symptom Score (TNSS), nasal resistance testing, and TCM syndrome scoring based on the “National Standard Clinical Terminology of Traditional Chinese Medical Diagnosis and Treatment Syndromes (GB/T 16751.2-1997) Guiding Principles of Clinical Research on New Drugs of Chinese Medicines (Zheng, 2002). During the study, potential adverse reactions in the participants, including headache, nasal pain, nasal bleeding, and gastrointestinal reactions, were closely monitored and recorded. Concurrently, safety indicators, such as complete blood count, liver function, kidney function, and electrocardiograms were tested before and after treatment to ensure the safety of the study and the health of the participants.

Isolation and preparation of PBMC

Blood samples were collected from two recruited volunteers via venipuncture and drawn into 4.5 mL tubes containing lithium heparin (Becton Dickinson, Franklin Lakes, NJ, USA). Peripheral PBMCs were isolated and prepared as previously described [11]. Briefly, PBMCs were separated from other blood cells by density gradient centrifugation using Histopaque (Sigma Aldrich, St. Louis, MO, USA) and cryopreserved for further analysis.

Single-cell RNA sequencing

The prepared PBMCs were thawed and used to construct single-cell RNA-sequencing (scRNA-seq) libraries. Upon thawing, the cell suspension was immediately transferred to centrifuge tubes containing RPMI-1640 + 10 % FBS, followed by low-speed centrifugation (300–400g, 5 min) to remove the freezing medium and dead cells. The supernatant was discarded, the cell pellet was resuspended in fresh medium, and the washing steps 2–3 times. Cell counting and viability assessments were performed using trypan blue staining and an automated cell counter, respectively, ensuring that the viability of all PBMC samples exceeded 80 %. The cell suspension was filtered through a 40 μm cell strainer. The cell concentration was adjusted to a range of 600–900 cells/μL. The cell suspension was then loaded into the 10 K Genomics-Perseus™ controller (10 K Genomics, Shanghai, China) to generate "oil-in-water" microdroplets for cDNA synthesis, amplification, and sequencing library preparation [12]. Sequencing was performed on the Illumina NovaSeq 6000 platform in the PE150 mode, generating 90G of sequencing data per sample.

Raw data processing and quality control

Single-cell expression data were processed and aligned to the GRCh38 reference genome using CellCosmo software (v1.0.9, https://github.com/10KGenomics/CellCOSMO). Gene expression matrices of the samples were subjected to quality control using the Seurat package (v4.3.0) in R (v4.2) [13], applying the following criteria: mitochondrial gene percentage <20 %, percentage hb <5 %, and gene count greater than 200. Doublets were filtered using the Scrublet package [14]. Gene expression matrices were normalized and calculated for the top 3000 variable genes using the NormalizeData and FindVariableFeatures functions in the Seurat package [15]. The integration of gene expression matrices was performed using the FindIntegrationAnchors and IntegrateData functions to correct for batch effects. To remove potential batch effects across samples, the Harmony algorithm (v1.2.0) was further applied, resulting in well-aligned cell clusters as visualized in Fig. S1 [16]. The integrated data were scaled using default parameters of the ScaleData function. Clustering was performed using 30 principal components in the principal component analysis (PCA) via the FindNeighbors and FindClusters functions, followed by visualization using uniform manifold approximation and projection (UMAP) [17].

Cell type annotation and differential expression analysis

Cell type annotation was performed based on typical cell type marker genes from the CellMarker database to identify major cell groups and subtypes [18,19]. Differential expression analysis between groups was conducted using the FindMarkers function in Seurat, defining differentially expressed genes according to an adjusted p-value <0.05, and an absolute value of average log fold change (avg_logFC) > 0.1. To visualize differentially expressed genes, GO [20] and KEGG [21] pathway enrichment analyses were conducted for each cell subtype using the ClusterProfiler package (v4.8.3) [22].

Cell-cell communication analysis

Intercellular communication was inferred and analyzed using Cellchat (http://github.com/sqjin/CellChat) based on the scRNA-seq data [23]. Communication between various cell types was visualized using CellChat.

Gene regulatory network analysis

We used the R package SCENIC (https://github.com/aertslab/SCENIC, version 1.1.1–10, RcisTarget 1.6.0, and AUCell 1.8.0) for transcription factor enrichment analysis [24]. The GENIE3 method (for individual samples) [25,26] and GRNBoost (for combined samples) [27] were used to identify potential transcription factor (TF) targets. Additionally, AUCell was used to evaluate the activity of each regulon in each cell. AUCell was used to calculate the area under the curve (AUC) and integrate the expression ranks of all genes within the regulon.

Data and materials availability

The raw and analyzed data used in this study are available under the accession number GSE273975 in the Gene Expression Omnibus (www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo).

Analysis of public databases for regulon expression

To further validate the differential expression of the regulons, data available from public allergic rhinitis datasets (GSE140454) were analyzed. The limma package was used to identify DEGs between the treatment group (treated with ovalbumin) and the control group [28]; genes with |log2FC| > log2(1.5) and p-value <0.05 were defined as significantly differentially expressed between the groups. The DEGs were subjected to GO and KEGG pathway enrichment analyses using the ClusterProfiler package [22].

RT-qPCR analysis

Total RNA was reverse transcribed into cDNA using the HiScript III RT SuperMix for qPCR (+gDNA wiper) kit (Vazyme, Nanjing, China) according to the manufacturer's instructions. Quantitative PCR was then performed in triplicate with the ChamQ Universal SYBR qPCR Master Mix (Vazyme, Nanjing, China) on a LightCycler q2000 Real-Time PCR System (Longgene Scientific, Hangzhou, China). The relative expression levels of target genes were calculated using the 2–ΔΔCt method, with β-actin used as an internal reference control. The sequences of the gene-specific primers used are listed in Table S1.

Statistical analyses

Student's t-test was used for intergroup comparisons of cell proportions and mRNA expression level detected by RT-qPCR assays, and P < 0.05 was considered as statistically significant.

Results

Single-cell transcriptomic profiling of PBMC and cell type identification

Four PBMC samples (PBMCs) from two patients with allergic rhinitis before and after treatment with the Peiyuan Tongqiao formula were obtained, and single-cell sequencing was conducted. Subject 1 (H1) was 43 years old with a BMI of 20.5, and a medical history of 15 years. Subject 2 (H2) was 35 years old with a BMI of 19.2 and a medical history of 18 years. After two weeks of treatment, both subjects exhibited significant improvements in nasal symptoms, particularly nasal itching and rhinorrhea, with both symptoms reduced to below 1 on the symptom scale. The alleviation of nasal congestion was relatively limited, and no notable changes were observed in the nasal resistance values before and after the treatment. Furthermore, the overall TCM syndrome scores of both patients showed marked post-treatment improvements, indicating a substantial reduction in various symptoms. Throughout the study period, no severe adverse drug reactions were reported by any subject. Additionally, safety indicators such as routine blood tests, liver function, renal function, and electrocardiograms remained within normal ranges, demonstrating the safety and feasibility of the treatment regimen. Key immunological markers in both patients were measured and showed a decrease in the levels of IL-4 and IgE and increase trend in the levels of IL-2, TGF-β1, and IFN-α following treatment (Table S2), indicating a rescovery of immune balance in allergic rhinitis.

After preprocessing and quality assessment of the single-cell sequencing data, 20,421 cells were used for downstream analysis (Table S3). The samples were divided into two groups: the "pre-treatment" group, which included two samples from both patients prior to treatment (H1-Btx and H2-Btx), and the "post-treatment" group, which included two samples (H1-Atx and H2-Atx) from after treatment (Fig. 1A). Differential expression analysis was conducted for each cell subtype between the pre- and post-treatment groups, followed by functional enrichment analysis of the differentially expressed genes (Fig. 3). The results indicated that the strength of the outgoing interaction between these T cell subtypes and Transitional B cells significantly increased after treatment (Fig. 4A). Simultaneously, the number of outgoing interactions of most cellular subtypes with Exhausted CD8+ T cells decreased, whereas the outgoing interaction strength increased after treatment (Fig. 4B). SCENIC analysis was conducted to investigate differences in the transcription factors (TFs) activity between the pre- and post-treatment cohorts. The results revealed that monocytes exhibited relatively higher activity of specific transcription factors, such as VDR, ETV6, CEBPB, TCF7L2, ETS2, MAFB, BACH1, KLF4, ATF5, CEBPA, CEBPD, ARID3A, RXRA, and FOS (Fig. 5A). Central memory CD4+ T, naïve CD4+ T, and naïve CD8+ T cells showed relatively higher activity of the transcription factors ETS1, ZEB1, FOXO1, LEF1, and TCF7 (Fig. 5A). Effector Memory CD8+ T and NK cells exhibited relatively high activity of the transcription factors XBP1, FOSL2, RUNX3, EOMES, TBX21, BHLHE40, and ZNF595 (Fig. 5A). Additionally, memory and naïve B cells demonstrated relatively higher activity of the transcription factors IRF8, EBF1, and SPIB (Fig. 5A). We found increased activities of TFs such as ZNF735, ELF3, TP73, FOSL, and POU5F1, and decreased activities of TFs such as ETS1, IRF2, IKZF2, ELK3, and IKZF1 after treatment (Fig. 5B). Discussion

Particularly, the levels of Th2 cells and their secretory factor IL-4 in healthy individuals are lower than those in patients with AR [29]. However, TCM and its bioactive compounds carried potential therapeutic benefits which were validated by in vivo and in vitro experiments. Both ginseng and Bran-fried Yam targeted NF-κB pathway for the therapy of AR through down-regulating the expression levels of IL-4 [30,31]. Ginsenosides (e.g., Rg1, Rb1, Rg3) and PNS-R1 derived from ginseng serve as the main active ingredients and are involved in suppressing Th2 cytokine activation (e.g., IL-4, IL-5) [[32], [33], [34], [35]]. Chemical selenylation of yam polysaccharides using the NaSeO3–HNO3 system increases the activity of natural yam polysaccharide extracts, enabling them to modulate the CD4+/CD8+ ratio in T lymphocytes and reduce the secretion of IL-4 [30]. In the field of treating AR with traditional TCM, we believe that the reasonable and scientific combination of different TCM herbs can achieve more effective therapeutic effects targeting inflammatory cytokines and even the immune system, and even further safeguard human health by enhancing the biological functions of TCM herbs (e.g., chemical sialylation).

As reported by Zou et al., normal individuals exhibit significantly lower percentages of classical monocytes and monocytes compared with patients with AR [36]; however, the related mechanisms remain incompletely explored. In this study, we investigated the therapeutic effects and mechanisms of action of Peiyuan Tongqiao Decoction using scRNA-seq of peripheral blood PBMC samples from AR patients. We systematically revealed a potential association between immune cell phenotypes and AR progression. Accordingly, 11 major cell types were annotated, and a reduction in the proportion of CD14+ Monocytes was identified as a key feature involved in AR development. TMC may promote immune cell responses by regulating the in vivo microecology [37,38]. A previous report showed that CD14+ Monocytes were recruited to the nasal mucosa and further engaged in the generation of proinflammatory chemokines within hours of allergen invasion of an in vivo model [39]. We hypothesized that a decrease in the abundance of CD14+ monocytes would alleviate AR symptoms by lowering the levels of inflammatory markers.

Subsequently, DEGs were identified, and related pathways in different cells were analyzed. Compared to the pre-treatment group, the downregulated genes in CD4+ Tregs, Central Memory CD4+ T cells, Effector CD8+ T cells, and Effector Memory CD8+ T cells in the post-treatment group were enriched in lymphocyte and mononuclear cell differentiation. Generally, lymphocyte differentiation focuses on the evolution of the unique features and functions of a relatively unspecialized precursor cell. For example, CD4+ Tregs, characterized by the expression of the transcription factor forkhead box protein P3 (FOXP3), are generated in the thymus as a functionally mature T cell subset and in the periphery of naïve T cells, whose abundance and functions are related to the maintenance of immunological tolerance to self-antigens and the suppression of heightened immune responses destructive to the tissue during asthma inflammation [40,41]. In addition, the downregulated genes in Central Memory CD4+ T cells and Effector Memory CD8+ T cells of the post-treatment group were mainly enriched in the antigen receptor-mediated signaling pathway, which plays a critical role in T cell differentiation, activation, proliferation, and memory [42]. These processes are induced by cytokines, costimulatory molecules, chemokines, integrins, and their metabolites. This pathway contributes to the delicate balance of sufficient T-cell activation to respond to self-antigens or non-pathogenic foreign antigens, prevent or kill infectious pathogens, and fight cancer.

Cell-cell communication analysis was also performed to reveal and demonstrate the communication pathways that enable tissues to function systematically to improve AR symptoms or cure AR disorders. Significantly weakened communication between CD14+ monocytes and central memory CD4+ T cells was demonstrated after Peiyuan Tongqiao decoction treatment. It is known that when T cells recognize antigenic peptide-major histocompatibility complex on monocytes through T-cell receptor, a "immune synapse" was formed which is maintained by intercellular cell adhesion molecule 1 and leukocyte-function-associated antigen 1 [43,44]. This process is linked to T cell signal transduction and rapid polarization for the high sensitivity and selectivity of T cell activation or responses [45]. Activated CD14+ monocytes secrete high concentrations of IL-6 and TNF-α via the NF-κB/AP-1 pathway. They can bypass T cell-independent responses, directly inducing T2 transitional B cells to rapidly differentiate into short-lived plasma cells, which then produce large amounts of IgM antibodies within 1–3 days post-infection [46,47]. On the other hand, activate CD4+ T cells differentiate into follicular helper T cells (Tfh) via antigen presentation. And the PI3K-AKT signaling pathway in B cells is activated when CD40L attaches to CD40 on the surface of transitional B cells. As a result, T2 transitional B cells undergo differentiation into memory B cells or long-lived plasma cells (LLPCs) and release IgG [48].

Finally, we screened and identified TFs related to patient rehabilitation to highlight the underlying mechanisms of action of Peiyuan Tongqiao Decoction. Monocytes exhibit relatively higher VDR activity. 1,25(OH)2D3, VDR, and the retinoid X receptor form a complex that then binds to vitamin D to initiate biological processes via gene transcription and signal transduction pathways [49,50]. ApaI and TaqbI in the VDR gene (chromosome 12q13.1) may affect VDR expression levels in specific cell types in many tissues and carry a risk of mite-sensitized persistent allergic rhinitis in Han Chinese individuals [51]. Although it is unclear how VDR polymorphisms affect autoimmunity, the activation of VDR regulates immune responses and maintains the stability of cell function by controlling the Th1/Th2 balance and lowering Th2-related cytokine levels [[52], [53], [54], [55]]. Additionally, Effector Memory CD8+ T and NK cells show relatively high RUNX3 activity, which were associated with a significant decrease in the levels of IL-4 and IgE in patients receiving the targeted therapy (Table S2). RUNX3 enhanced IFN-γ activity and significantly decreased IL-4 level through cooperation with T-bet and plays a critical role in the T-cell differentiation, further regulating the balance of Th1/Th2. Usually, unnecessary accumulation of Th2 cytokines, accompanied by Th1 underexpression, results in Th2 polarization, M1 macrophages polarization [56], IgE production by acidophilic granulocytes, and mucus secretion, which serve as pathogenic factors in AR. This study proposes a hypothesis that Peiyuan Tongqiao Decoction treatment could exhibit a positive influence on the Th1/Th2 balance and M2 macrophages polarization by enhancing the activity of the specific TFs, VDR and RUNX3.

In contrast, the majority of ETS1 target genes experienced downregulation, which was further validated by qPCR, despite the fact that central memory CD4+, naïve CD4+, and naïve CD8+ T cells displayed comparatively higher activity in ETS1. Bruhn et al. previously reported that ETS1 knockdown in CD4+ cells significantly decreased IL-5 and IL-13 protein levels as well as Th2-related cytokine levels. Further functional pathway analysis showed that ETS1 contributes to the role of cytokines in mediating the communication between immune cells, T cell receptor signaling, and airway inflammation in asthma [57]. Moreover, Effector Memory CD8+ T and NK cells exhibit relatively higher XBP1 activity. Previous studies have indicated that XBP1 increases Th2 cytokine levels in an mTOR- and MAPK-dependent manner, and promotes the induction of Th2 polarization in the mouse airway tract [58]. These results suggest that further studies should focus on treatment strategies that specifically target Th2 polarization.

Although sample size in this study was insufficient, the hypothetical arguments obtained from investigating the mechanism of action of TMC against AR using scRNA-seq hold significant reference value—they provide a preliminary framework for further validation with larger cohorts and in vivo experiments. It was reported that Fried Cocklebur and Angelica Dahurica shared many targets. For example, RTK is associated with the MAPK and PI3K-Akt signaling pathways, while JNK and P38MAPK are associated with the MAPK and TNF signaling pathways [59]. In the future, we need to focus more on exploring network pharmacology of Peiyuan Tongqiao Decoction by screening the TCMSP or BATMAN-TCM databases to further reveal the functions of TFs in related biological pathways.

In conclusion, we preliminarily demonstrated that Peiyuan Tongqiao Decoction has great application prospects for improving the symptoms of AR and even curing AR. Both lymphocyte and mononuclear cell differentiation are important pathways when the specific TFs VDR and RUNX3 regulate the balance of Th1/Th2.
