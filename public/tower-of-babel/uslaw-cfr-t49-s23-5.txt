49 C.F.R. § 23.5 — To whom does this part apply?
Citation: us:cfr:49:23.5
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-23.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 23 (Participation Of Disadvantaged Business Enterprise In Airport Concessions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you are a recipient that has received a grant for airport development at any time after January 1988 that was authorized under Title 49 of the United States Code, this part applies to you.
