24 C.F.R. § 232.570 — Endorsement of credit instrument.
Citation: us:cfr:24:232.570
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-232.570
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 232 (Mortgage Insurance For Nursing Homes, Intermediate Care Facilities, Board And Care Homes, And Assisted Living Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commissioner shall indicate his insurance of the loan by endorsing the credit instrument and identifying the section of the Act and regulations under which the loan is insured and the date of insurance, subject to the presentation and approval by him of the following:
(a) Certification of full disbursement of loan proceeds as provided for in § 232.530.
(b) Certification of costs as required by § 232.610.
(c) Certification that fire safety equipment was installed as required by § 232.500(c).
