15 C.F.R. § 921.82 — Amendments to financial assistance awards.
Citation: us:cfr:15:921.82
Creator: National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-921.82
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 921 (National Estuarine Research Reserve System Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Actions requiring an amendment to the financial assistance award, such as a request for additional Federal funds, revisions of the approved project budget or original scope of work, or extension of the performance period must be submitted to NOAA on Standard Form 424 and approved in writing.
