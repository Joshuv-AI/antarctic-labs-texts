48 C.F.R. § 1552.217-73 — 1552.217-73 Option for increased quantity—cost-type contract.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1552.217-73
Creator: Chapter 15—Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1552.217-73
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1552 (Solicitation Provisions And Contract Clauses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As prescribed in 1517.208(d), insert this contract clause in cost-reimbursement type term form contracts when applicable. If only one option period is used, enter “NA” in the proper places of the clause. If more than two option periods apply, modify the clause accordingly.
