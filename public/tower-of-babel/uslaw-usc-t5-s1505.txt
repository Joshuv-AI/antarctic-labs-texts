5 U.S.C. § 1505 — Hearings; adjudications; notice of determinations
Citation: us:usc:5:1505
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section1505
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part II (Civil Service Functions And Responsibilities); Chapter 15 (Political Activity Of Certain State And Local Employees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Either the State or local officer or employee or the State or local agency employing him, or both, are entitled to appear with counsel at the hearing under section 1504 of this title, and be heard. After this hearing, the Merit Systems Protection Board shall—
(1)
determine whether a violation of section 1502 of this title has occurred;
(2)
determine whether the violation warrants the removal of the officer or employee from his office or employment; and
(3)
notify the officer or employee and the agency of the determination by registered or certified mail.
