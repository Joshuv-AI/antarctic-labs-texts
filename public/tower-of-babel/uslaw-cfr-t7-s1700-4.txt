7 C.F.R. § 1700.4 — Public comments on proposed rules.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1700.4
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1700.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1700 (General Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

RUS requires that all persons submitting comments to a proposed rule or other document published by the agency in the Federal Register, submit comments as specified in the published notice. Copies of comments submitted are available to the public in conformance with 7 CFR part 1.
