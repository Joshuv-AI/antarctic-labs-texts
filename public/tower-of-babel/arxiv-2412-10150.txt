The bremsstrahlung-like production of the massive spin-2 dark matter mediator
I. V. Voronchikhin, D. V. Kirpichnikov

Abstract
The link between Standard Model (SM) particles and dark matter (DM) can be introduced via spin-2 massive mediator, G , that couples to photon and charged leptons. Moreover, in a mediator mass range from sub-MeV to sub-GeV, fixed-target facilities such as NA64e, LDMX, NA64 μ \mu , M 3 , and E137, can potentially probe such particle of the hidden sector via the signatures that are described by the bremsstrahlung-like process involving tensor mediator. We compare numerically the Weizsaker-Williams (WW) approximation and the exact tree-level (ETL) approach for the bremsstrahlung-like mediator production cross section by choosing various parameters of the fixed-target experiments. In addition, we derive novel constraints on spin-2 DM mediator parameter space from the data of the E137 fixed-target experiment. In particular, we demonstrate that the E137 experiment has been ruled out the the couplings of the spin-2 mediator at the level of 8 × 10 − 8 ​ GeV − 1 ≲ c e ​ e G ≲ 10 − 5 ​ GeV − 1 8\times 10^{-8}\penalty\ \mbox{GeV}^{-1}\penalty\ \lesssim\penalty\ c^{\text{G}}_{ee}\penalty\ \lesssim\penalty\ 10^{-5}\penalty\ \mbox{GeV}^{-1} for the typical masses in the range 100 ​ MeV ≲ m G ≲ 800 ​ MeV 100\penalty\ \mbox{MeV}\penalty\ \lesssim\penalty\ m_{\text{G}}\penalty\ \lesssim 800\penalty\ \mbox{MeV} , that corresponds to the statistics of 1.87 × 10 20 1.87\times 10^{20} electrons accumulated on target. The latter implies its universal coupling to photons and leptons, c e ​ e G = c γ ​ γ G c^{\rm G}_{ee}=c^{\rm G}_{\gamma\gamma} .

Bremsstrahlung-like production of the tensor mediator

The radiation of a dark matter mediator by a lepton scattering off a heavy nucleus can be
represented as a
2\penalty\ \to\penalty\ 3
process:

l^{\pm}(p)\penalty\ +\penalty\ N(P_{i})\penalty\ \rightarrow\penalty\ l^{\pm}(p^{\prime})\penalty\ +\penalty\ N(P_{f})\penalty\ +\penalty\ \text{G}(k),

where
p\penalty\ =\penalty\ (E_{l},\bm{p})
,

P_{i}\penalty\ =\penalty\ (M,0)
and
P_{f}\penalty\ =\penalty\ (P^{0}_{f},\bm{P}_{f})
are
4-momenta
of the initial and final nucleus, respectively,

t\penalty\ \equiv\penalty\ -q^{2}\penalty\ =\penalty\ -(P_{i}\penalty\ -\penalty\ P_{f})^{2}

The Mandelstam-like variables for the process (
10

\tilde{t}=(p-p^{\prime})^{2}-m_{\rm G}^{2}.

VI
Conclusion

In the present paper, we have discussed in detail the
calculation of the DM mediator production cross sections in the
case of ETL and WW approaches for fixed-target experiments, such as NA64e, LDMX, NA64
\mu
,
and M
3
. Tensor massive particle
G
is chosen as hidden-sector mediator, that is produced in
the bremsstrahlung-like reaction
lN\penalty\ \to\penalty\ lN{\text{G}}
followed by its invisible decay into DM pair,

{\text{G}}\penalty\ \to\penalty\ {\text{DM}}\penalty\ +\penalty\ {\text{DM}}

m_{\text{G}}\penalty\ \gtrsim\penalty\ 200\penalty\ \mbox{MeV}
the
agreement between ETL and WW methods can be at the level of
\lesssim\mathcal{O}(1)\penalty\ \%
for all
fixed-target experiments of interest.

In addition, we have studied the properties of the spin-2 particle production
in the bremsstrahlung-like reaction
lN\penalty\ \to\penalty\ lN{\text{G}}
and its visible decays,

{\text{G}}\penalty\ \to\penalty\ \gamma\gamma
and
{\text{G}}\penalty\ \to\penalty\ e^{+}e^{-}

We have calculated the expected sensitivity to spin-2 DM mediator of the LDMX

and
have shown that the regarding parameter space was ruled out by mono-photon BaBar
