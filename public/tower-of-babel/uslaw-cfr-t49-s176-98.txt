49 C.F.R. § 176.98 — Stowage of hazardous materials on board barges.
Citation: us:cfr:49:176.98
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-176.98
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 176 (Carriage By Vessel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A material for which “on deck” stowage only is required by column (10) of the Hazardous Materials Table (§ 172.101 of this subchapter) may be stowed “under deck” on unmanned barges.
