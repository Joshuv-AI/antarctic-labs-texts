30 U.S.C. § 1313 — Limitation on payments
Citation: us:usc:30:1313
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section1313
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 25 (Surface Mining Control And Reclamation); Subchapter VIII (University Coal Research Laboratories). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
No institutions of higher education may receive more than $4,000,000 for the construction of its coal research laboratory, including initially installed fixed equipment, nor may it receive more than $1,500,000 for initially installed movable equipment, nor may it receive more than $500,000 for new program startup expenses.
(b)
No institution of higher education may receive more than $1,500,000 per year from the Federal Government for operating expenses.
