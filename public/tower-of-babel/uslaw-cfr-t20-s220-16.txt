20 C.F.R. § 220.16 — Responsibility to notify the Board of events which affect disability.
Citation: us:cfr:20:220.16
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-220.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 220 (Determining Disability). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the annuitant is entitled to a disability annuity because he or she is disabled for work in his or her regular occupation, the annuitant should promptly tell the Board if—
(a) His or her impairment(s) improves;
(b) He or she returns to any type of work;
(c) He or she increases the amount of work; or
(d) His or her earnings increase.
