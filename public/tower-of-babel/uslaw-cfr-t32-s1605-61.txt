32 C.F.R. § 1605.61 — Staff of area offices for selective service.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1605.61
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1605.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1605 (Selective Service System Organization). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subject to applicable law and within the limits of available funds, the staff of each area office shall consist of as many compensated employees, either military or civilian, as shall be authorized by the Director of Selective Service.
