29 U.S.C. § 156 — Rules and regulations
Citation: us:usc:29:156
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section156
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 7 (Labor-Management Relations); Subchapter II (National Labor Relations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Board shall have authority from time to time to make, amend, and rescind, in the manner prescribed by subchapter II of chapter 5 of title 5, such rules and regulations as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this subchapter.
