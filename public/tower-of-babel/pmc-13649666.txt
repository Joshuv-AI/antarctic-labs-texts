Author Correction: Arf1 is involved in Neisseria meningitidis-induced cortical branched F-actin network reorganization
Authors: Margot Sahnine, Morgane Crochet, Stéphane Tachon, Sylvie Goussard, Esthel Pénard, Cyril Scandola, Magalie Duchateau, Quentin Giai Gianetto, Audrey Salles, Gaël Moneron, Léa Swistak, Arthur Charles-Orszag, Adeline Mallet, Mariette Matondo, Jean-Yves Tinevez, Daria Bonazzi, Anna Sartori-Rupp, Guillaume Duménil, Dorian Obino
Published: 2026

Correction to: EMBO Reports (2026) 27:4544–4582. https://doi.org/10.1038/s44319-026-00843-z | Published online 25 June 2026

A citation is removed.

A citation is added.

Citation removed

In the Results section “Cdc42 regulates Arp2/3 activity through N-WASP in endothelial protrusions induced by N. meningitidis”, the sentence:

“Strikingly, while previous reports have suggested the involvement of the small GTPase Cdc42, but not that of Rac1, in regulating F-actin reorganization in endothelial cells upon meningococcal infection (Coureuil et al, 2009; Eugene et al, 2002), others have rather shown a role for Rac1 (Coureuil et al, 2010; Lambotin et al, 2005)”.

Is corrected to: (changes in bold)

“Strikingly, while previous reports have suggested the involvement of the small GTPase Cdc42, but not that of Rac1, in regulating F-actin reorganization in endothelial cells upon meningococcal infection (Coureuil et al, 2009; Eugene et al, 2002), others have rather shown a role for Rac1 (Lambotin et al, 2005)”.

The citation of Coureuil et al, 2010 is removed, as this reference does not relate to the role of Rac1 in this context.

Citation added

In the Results section “F-actin reorganization in N. meningitidis-induced endothelial tubular protrusions relies on Arp2/3 activity”, the sentence:

“Equivalent results were obtained when assessing the local enrichment of endogenous Arp3 (Fig. EV2A-C) or that of the accessory subunit ArpC5-GFP transiently expressed in endothelial cells by live and fixed imaging experiments (Fig. 3D-G and Movie EV5)”.

Is corrected to: (changes in bold)

“Equivalent results were obtained when assessing the local enrichment of endogenous Arp3 (Fig. EV2A-C) or that of the accessory subunit ArpC5-GFP transiently expressed in endothelial cells by live and fixed imaging experiments (Fig. 3D-G and Movie EV5), as previously reported for this subunit (Coureuil et al, 2009)”.

The citation of Coureuil et al, 2009 was inadvertently omitted and is now added.

Both references remain cited elsewhere in the article; the reference list is unchanged. These changes do not affect the results or conclusions of the study.
