34 C.F.R. § 300.327 — Educational placements.
Citation: us:cfr:34:300.327
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-300.327
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 300 (Assistance To States For The Education Of Children With Disabilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Consistent with § 300.501(c), each public agency must ensure that the parents of each child with a disability are members of any group that makes decisions on the educational placement of their child.
