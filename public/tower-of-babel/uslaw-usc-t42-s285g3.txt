42 U.S.C. § 285g–3 — Associate Director for Prevention; appointment; function
Citation: us:usc:42:285g–3
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section285g–3
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter III (National Research Institutes); Part C (Specific Provisions Respecting National Research Institutes); Subpart 7 (Eunice Kennedy Shriver National Institute Of Child Health And Human Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There shall be in the Institute an Associate Director for Prevention to coordinate and promote the programs in the Institute concerning the prevention of health problems of mothers and children. The Associate Director shall be appointed by the Director of the Institute from individuals who because of their professional training or experience are experts in public health or preventive medicine.
