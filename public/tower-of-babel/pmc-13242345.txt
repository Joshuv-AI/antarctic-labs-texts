Significance of oscillatory TiO2-water nanofluid and heat transfer performance over vertical magnetized flat plate using artificial neural network
Authors: Zia Ullah, Md Mahbub Alam, Wubale Demis Alamirew, Mohamed Ahmed Said, M D Alsulami, Y M Mahrous, Hela Ahmad Gnaba, Fayaz Hussain
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
AbstractThe oscillatory motion of titanium dioxide (TiO2) nanoparticles in water-based fluid for heat and mass transfer performance over vertical plate has been investigated. The novelty of this work evaluates the steady and periodical behavior of heat and mass transfer using TiO2-water nanofluid, magnetic field and mixed convection. The artificial neural network on dissipative Maxwell TiO2-water nanofluid is applied to predict the accuracy and convergence of velocity profile under defined boundary conditions. The oscillatory flow pattern is applied to develop the fluctuating layers in heating frequency and mass transmission. This model converts the mathematical equations into steady, real and imaginary forms using stokes and primitive transformations. Several authors solved the fluid models by converting into ordinary-differential form but this model presents direct unsteady partial differential equations. Implicit finite-difference approach is used to convert the mathematical model into algebraic system in the presence of Gaussian elimination simulation. It is noticed that the rate of velocity amplitude increases as radiation, Maxwell index, and heat dissipation increase. The lowest training mean square error (1.77 × 10⁻10), close validation, testing errors, small gradient (9.82 × 10⁻8) at fewer epochs (636) are observed for magnetic number Mf = 3.0. The steady behavior of heat and mass rate increases as Schmidt number and thermal radiation are enhanced. The amplitude of oscillatory heat and mass transfer intensifies as magnetic number and Richardson number are increased. The increasing skin friction rate is observed at each value of Maxwell parameter and Eckert number.Keywords: TiO2 nanoparticles, Oscillatory Maxwell nanofluid, Viscous dissipation, Artificial neural network, Radiating heat oscillation, Vertical flat plate

Introduction

The influence of titanium dioxide TiO2 nanoparticles and viscous dissipation on Maxwell water fluid through vertical plate heat exchanger is very prominent in advanced thermal control systems. The impact of heat radiation, oscillatory motion and mixed convection forces is very significant for enhanced heat transfer. Maxwell dissipative TiO2-water nanofluid with suspended nanoparticles offers remarkable cooling ability thermal systems due to their enhanced thermal conductivity and heat transfer properties. The main goal of developing nanofluids is to distribute and suspend nanomaterial in basic liquids uniformly and steadily to achieve the greatest thermal conductivity of nanomaterial. The present work is very effective engineering applications such as fabrication of materials, energy efficiency, petroleum identification, fuel-cell development and oil filtration procedures, movement of liquids in porous media, glass-based materials, rubber industries, plastic sheets, and freezing metallic plates. Abbas et al. [1] investigated the physical quantities of Nusselt number and temperature variations of MHD nanofluid motion over a porous surface with heat source effects. The importance of several pertinent parameters on the graphical pattern of velocity fields and temperature variations of Jeffery MHD nanofluid flow with heat source effects was discussed by Sharma and Gupta [2]. Narender et al. [3] applied several values of Biot number and magnetic parameter to capture the physical and thermal properties of Maxwell nanofluid along stretchable structure. Hady et al. [4] used gyro-tactic microorganisms in bio-convective nanofluid boundary layers and heat transport flow rate with MHD and viscous dissipation effects. The significance of viscous dissipation and fluid-particle suspension in heat transfer performance over stretching structure was studied by Krishnamurthy et al. [5]. Pandey et al. [6] examined the impact of nonlinear thermal radiation and mixed convection on heat transfer of non-Newtonian fluid flow along stretching surface. The significance of heat source/sink, viscous dissipation and magnetic field on unsteady nanofluid flow and heat transfer with chemical reaction effects was discussed in [7].

Magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) is very important mechanism which relates connection between electromagnetic flux and energy conducting liquid. The TiO2-water nanofluids under the impact of magnetic forces are extremely beneficial in multiple cooling operations and heat reduction in thermal systems. The magnetic nanofluid has various applications such as damping oscillation, regulating high-temperature plasma, electrical measurement devices, pumping technology, MHD generators, magnetic coating for corrosion prevention and liquid cooling networks for thermonuclear fusion power reactors. Mishra and Kumar [8] studied the theoretical computations of Nusselt number, slip velocity variation, and temperature slip distributions using MHD nanofluid with several thermal variables. Ali et al. [9] predicted the thermal transport performance of slender moving needle using ethylene glycol fluid containing TiO2 nanoparticles. Thumma and Mishra [10] investigated the numeric measurements of Nusselt number and velocity variations over stretching structure with and without slip conditions. Ibrahim and Negera [11] depicted the thermal and fluid properties of MHD nanofluid motion along expanding cylinder with chemical reaction, radiation and viscous dissipation impacts. The implication of viscous dissipation on Nusselt number and temperature variations was calculated graphically and numerically in [12, 13]. The contribution of chemical reaction, MHD and viscous dissipation for the improvement of heat and mass diffusion features in saturated porous medium was explained in [14].

The thermal transmission of nanofluid over heated surfaces, radiation and viscous dissipation are important properties for heat transfer enhancement. The viscous dissipation is the frequency per mass unit at which movement energy in a viscous liquid is transformed into thermal energy. In energy productions, the flow through flat-plate heat exchanger is very useful in food industries, condenser systems, heat recovery and marine engine cooling. Nanofluid is very important in electrical products, automobiles, spaceships, missile technology, medical research, and solar-powered heating programs. Alrihieli et al. [15] examined the squeezing MHD flow analysis and heat transport of Maxwell nanofluid containing magnetic and viscous dissipation properties. Narender et al. [16] investigated the influence of slip velocity, dissipation and magnetization in physical quantities of Nusselt and temperature quantities numerically. Gurejala et al. [17] performed a statistical analysis of Maxwell MHD fluid to enhance the heat and mass transmission over linear surface. Hsiao [18] studied the graphical pattern of thermal energy and mass flow distribution using micropolar nanofluid with different thermal variables. The magneto-hydrodynamic boundary layers and heat transport using different thermal properties were solved in [19, 20]. In literature [21, 22], the impact of titanium dioxide TiO2 nanoparticles in water-based fluid was used to display the behavior of heat transmission in heat exchanger tube and bi-directional sheet. Kathyayani and Gowd [23] explored the flow dynamics and thermal properties of Maxwell fluid containing thermal properties and couple stress effects. The importance of artificial neural networks and effects of titanium dioxide TiO2 nanoparticles in water-based fluid for transfer assessment was depicted by references [24] and [25]. Masood et al. [26] predicted the numerical measurements of friction rate and heat transport of MHD nanofluid in bio-medical fields. Aziz and Shams [27] calculated the theoretical and numerical values of heating efficiency using heat generation, radiation, slip and thermal variable conditions. The investigation of heating performance, friction rate and mass diffusion features of oscillating plate with viscous dissipation and chemical reaction was investigated in reference [28].

The novelty of current analysis presents the steady and oscillatory performance of skin friction, heat transfer and mass transfer over vertical plate using unsteady flow of TiO2-water nanofluid. The time-dependent partial differential equations are converted into steady, real and imaginary parts using oscillating Stokes condition. The real and imaginary parts are used to compute the fluctuating layers in heat and mass transfer. For maximum thermal efficiency and cooling performance of nanoparticles, the magnetic effects are applied in Maxwell nanofluid. Theme of this inspection explores the periodicity and fluctuations in thermal boundary layers of Maxwell TiO2-water nanofluid. For smooth convergence and accuracy of results, the steady, real and imaginary parts are converted into primitive form using primitive transformations. Several authors solved the fluid models by converting into ordinary-differential form without time-dependent results but this model investigates direct partial differentiation equations. Implicit technique of finite-difference approach is used to convert the convenient model into algebraic system with the help of Gaussian elimination simulation. The steady velocity quantity, steady temperature function and steady fluid concentration are inspected using steady model. The real and imaginary models are used to display oscillatory friction rate, oscillatory heating rate and oscillatory mass transmission.

Using stream functions and similarity variables, the existing studies predominantly simplify transient TiO2-water nanofluid models into ordinary differential form. Only steady form of ordinary differential models limits the accurate representation of oscillatory and fluctuating thermal behaviors. In current study, the artificial neural network (ANN) is used as a surrogate regression tool to approximate the numerical computed results of velocity field. The ANN is trained entirely on computed solutions, rather than as an independent validation framework. The dataset used for training consists of a total of 1000 samples generated from the numerical simulation. These data were randomly divided into three subsets: 70% (700 samples) for training, 15% (150 samples) for validation, and 15% (150 samples) for testing. The ANN architecture comprises one input neuron, single hidden layer containing 10 neurons, and one output neuron. The network is trained using the Levenberg–Marquardt back-propagation algorithm. Conclusions

Intelligence computing of fluctuating heat transfer along flat-plate using radiating flow of dissipative Maxwell Tio2-water nanofluid using artificial neural network is important significance of this work. Periodic heat radiations, nonlinear magnetic waves, thermal buoyancy and energy dissipation influence on the periodicity, fluctuation, amplitude and oscillatory frequency of heat transfer in Tio2-water nanofluid is discussed. The oscillatory motion of TiO2-water nanofluid is applied over vertical flat plate. The governing model is converted into steady, real and imaginary form. To maximize the thermal efficiency of plate, the TiO2-water nanofluid is used in oscillatory motion. The dimensionless variables are utilized to produce the physical characters to present the physical sequence of steady fluid velocity, steady temperature variation, and steady fluid concentration quantity. The accuracy of velocity field for magnetic parameter was examined through ANN Levenberg–Marquardt back propagation technique. Later, the steady results are consumed to draw the oscillatory heat amplitude, oscillatory friction rate and oscillatory mass transportation of TiO2 nanoparticles. These results are obtained through FORTRAN programming tool using stokes variables, primitive transformation and implicit finite-difference scheme under Gaussian eliminating approach. The validation of numerical outputs is calculated to ensure the accuracy of results with the previous literature. The important results are explained below.

The viscous dissipation and fluid friction examine the conversion of kinetic energy into thermal energy. An increase in Eckert number improves fluid velocity and temperature which is important for understanding energy losses and designing of thermal systems.
