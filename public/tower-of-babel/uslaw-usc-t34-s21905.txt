34 U.S.C. § 21905 — Voluntary participation
Citation: us:usc:34:21905
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section21905
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 219 (Ashanti Alert Communications Network). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The minimum standards established under section 21904(a) of this title, and any other guidelines and programs established under section 21903 of this title, shall be adoptable on a voluntary basis only.
