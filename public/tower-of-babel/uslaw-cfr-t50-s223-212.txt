50 C.F.R. § 223.212 — Southern DPS of spotted seal.
Citation: us:cfr:50:223.212
Creator: National Marine Fisheries Service, National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-223.212
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 223 (Threatened Marine And Anadromous Species). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The prohibitions of section 9(a)(1) of the ESA (16 U.S.C. 1538(a)(1)) relating to endangered species shall apply to the Southern Distinct Population Segment of spotted seal listed in § 223.102.
