19 C.F.R. § 208.18 — Notification of other agencies.
Citation: us:cfr:19:208.18
Creator: United States International Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-208.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 208 (Investigations Of United States-Mexico Cross-Border Long-Haul Trucking Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For each investigation subject to the provisions of this part 208, the Commission will transmit copies of the petition, request, or resolution to the Trade Representative and the Secretary of Transportation, along with a copy of the notice of investigation.
