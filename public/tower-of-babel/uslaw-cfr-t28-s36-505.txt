28 C.F.R. § 36.505 — Attorneys fees.
Citation: us:cfr:28:36.505
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-36.505
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 36 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Disability By Public Accommodations And In Commercial Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In any action or administrative proceeding commenced pursuant to the Act or this part, the court or agency, in its discretion, may allow the prevailing party, other than the United States, a reasonable attorney's fee, including litigation expenses, and costs, and the United States shall be liable for the foregoing the same as a private individual.
