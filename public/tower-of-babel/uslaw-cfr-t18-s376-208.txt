18 C.F.R. § 376.208 — Effect upon existing Commission requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:18:376.208
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-376.208
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 376 (Organization, Mission, And Functions; Operations During Emergency Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All outstanding Commission orders, rules and regulations shall remain in force and effect during the continuance of emergency conditions, except to the extent modified in accordance with authority exercised under this subpart.
