33 C.F.R. § 401.21 — Requirements for U.S. waters of the St. Lawrence Seaway.
Citation: us:cfr:33:401.21
Creator: Great Lakes St. Lawrence Seaway Development Corporation, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-401.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 401 (Seaway Regulations And Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to the requirements set forth elsewhere in these Regulations, vessels transiting the U.S. waters of the St. Lawrence Seaway are subject to the requirements set out in Schedule I.
