42 C.F.R. § 426.130 — Party submissions.
Citation: us:cfr:42:426.130
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-426.130
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 426 (Review Of National Coverage Determinations And Local Coverage Determinations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any party submitting material, except for material for which a privilege is asserted, or proprietary data, to the ALJ or the Board after that party's initial challenge must serve the material on all other parties at the same time.
