10 U.S.C. § 2633 — Stevedoring and terminal services: vessels carrying cargo or passengers sponsored by military department
Citation: us:usc:10:2633
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2633
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Property); Chapter 157 (Transportation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Notwithstanding section 1301(a) of title 31, the Secretary of a military department may, under such regulations as he may prescribe, furnish stevedoring and terminal services and facilities to vessels carrying cargo, or passengers, or both, sponsored by his department.
(b)
The furnishing of services and facilities under this section shall be at fair and reasonable rates.
(c)
The proceeds from furnishing services and facilities under this section shall be paid to the credit of the appropriation or fund out of which the services or facilities were supplied.
