48 C.F.R. § 6105.505 — 6105.505 Decisions [Rule 505].

Creator: Chapter 61—Civilian Board Of Contract Appeals, General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-6105.505
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 6105 (Decisions Authorized Under 31 U.S.C. 3529). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The judge will issue a written decision based upon the record, which includes submissions by the agency and the affected employee, and information provided during conferences. The Board will provide a copy of the decision to the agency and affected employee. In addition, all Board decisions are posted weekly on the Board's website. Published decisions will identify only first names of claimants and the first initial of their surnames.
