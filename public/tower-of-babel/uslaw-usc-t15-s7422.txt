15 U.S.C. § 7422 — No regulatory authority
Citation: us:usc:15:7422
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section7422
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 100A (Cybersecurity Enhancement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to confer any regulatory authority on any Federal, State, tribal, or local department or agency.
