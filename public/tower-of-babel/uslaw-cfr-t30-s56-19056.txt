30 C.F.R. § 56.19056 — Availability of hoist operator for automatic hoists.
Citation: us:cfr:30:56.19056
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-56.19056
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 56 (Safety And Health Standards—Surface Metal And Nonmetal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When automatic hoisting is used, a competent operator of the hoist shall be readily available at or near the hoisting device while any person is underground.
