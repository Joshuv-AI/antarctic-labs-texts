40 C.F.R. § 40.160-2 — -2 Financial status report.

Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-40.160-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 40 (Research And Demonstration Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A financial status report must be prepared and submitted within 120 days after completion of the budget and project periods in accordance with 2 CFR 200.328 and 2 CFR 200.344
