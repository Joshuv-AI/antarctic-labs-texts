14 U.S.C. § 2127 — Recall to active duty during war or national emergency
Citation: us:usc:14:2127
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2127
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 21 (Personnel; Officers); Subchapter I (Appointment And Promotion). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In time of war or national emergency, the Secretary may order any regular officer on the retired list to active duty.
