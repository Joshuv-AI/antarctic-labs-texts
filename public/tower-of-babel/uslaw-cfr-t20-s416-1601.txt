20 C.F.R. § 416.1601 — Definitions and terms used in this subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:20:416.1601
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-416.1601
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 416 (Supplemental Security Income For The Aged, Blind, And Disabled). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We or Us means the Social Security Administration.
You or Your means the person who applies for or receives SSI benefits or the person for whom an application is filed.
