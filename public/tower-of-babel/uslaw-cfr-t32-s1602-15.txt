32 C.F.R. § 1602.15 — Local board of jurisdiction.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1602.15
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1602.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1602 (Definitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The local board of jurisdiction is the local board to which a registrant is assigned and which has authority, in accord with the provisions of this chapter, to determine his claim or to issue to him an order. His local board and registrant's local board refer to the local board of jurisdiction.
