18 U.S.C. § 1967 — Evidence
Citation: us:usc:18:1967
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1967
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 96 (Racketeer Influenced And Corrupt Organizations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In any proceeding ancillary to or in any civil action instituted by the United States under this chapter the proceedings may be open or closed to the public at the discretion of the court after consideration of the rights of affected persons.
