31 C.F.R. § 902.6 — Consideration of tax consequences to the Government.
Citation: us:cfr:31:902.6
Creator: Federal Claims Collection Standards (Department Of The Treasury—Department Of Justice)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-902.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 902 (Standards For The Compromise Of Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In negotiating a compromise, agencies should consider the tax consequences to the Government. In particular, agencies should consider requiring a waiver of tax-loss-carry-forward and tax-loss-carry-back rights of the debtor. For information on discharge of indebtedness reporting requirements see § 903.5 of this chapter.
