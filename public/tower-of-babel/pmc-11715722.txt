Care negotiation between people living alone with dementia in the United States and non‐kin caregivers
Authors: Jane Lowers, Colby Smith, Kaitlyn Brus, Molly Perkins, Kenneth Hepburn
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractBackgroundOne in five people with cognitive impairment or dementia lacks family caregivers. People aging alone receive care from non‐family sources, including friends and neighbors, but the dynamics of these relationships are unexplored.MethodThematic analysis of qualitative interviews with 15 people with cognitive impairment or early dementia without close family and 14 non‐family caregiversResultWe identified five themes that shape non‐family dementia caregiving dynamics: 1) establishing a relationship of trust, 2) personal motivations for seeking/providing care, 3) negotiating caregiving etiquette, 4) establishing caregiving boundaries, 5) the role of distant family.ConclusionDementia caregiving by non‐family involves ongoing interpersonal negotiation of trust, roles, and boundaries.
