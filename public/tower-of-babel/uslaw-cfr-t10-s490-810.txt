10 C.F.R. § 490.810 — Record retention.
Citation: us:cfr:10:490.810
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-490.810
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 490 (Alternative Fuel Transportation Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A State or covered person that receives a waiver under this subpart must retain documentation pertaining to its waiver application and alternative compliance, including petroleum fuel reduction by its fleet, for a period of three years following the model year for which the waiver is granted.
