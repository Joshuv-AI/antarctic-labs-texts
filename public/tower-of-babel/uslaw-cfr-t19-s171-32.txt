19 C.F.R. § 171.32 — Acceptance of offers in compromise.
Citation: us:cfr:19:171.32
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-171.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 171 (Fines, Penalties, And Forfeitures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An offer in compromise will be considered accepted only when the offeror is so notified in writing. As a condition to accepting an offer in compromise, the offeror may be required to enter into any collateral agreement or to post any security which is deemed necessary for the protection of the interest of the United States.
