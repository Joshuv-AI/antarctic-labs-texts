5 C.F.R. § 316.703 — Effect on tenure of position change of status quo employees.
Citation: us:cfr:5:316.703
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-316.703
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 316 (Temporary And Term Employment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A status quo employee who is promoted, demoted, or reassigned becomes:
(1) An indefinite employee when the position change occurs while he is not serving overseas; or
(2) An overseas limited employee when the position change occurs while he is serving overseas.
(b) An employee referred to in paragraph (a) of this section who is changed back to his status quo position becomes a status quo employee.
