40 C.F.R. § 60.1905 — Can reporting dates be changed?
Citation: us:cfr:40:60.1905
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.1905
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 60 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If the Administrator agrees, you may change the semiannual or annual reporting dates.
(b) See § 60.19(c) for procedures to seek approval to change your reporting date.
