39 C.F.R. § 3041.510 — Renewals of competitive negotiated service agreements.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3041.510
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3041.510
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3041 (Competitive Negotiated Service Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A renewal of a negotiated service agreement is deemed a new negotiated service agreement.
(b) A renewal of a competitive negotiated service agreement will be added to the competitive product list pursuant to the applicable rules for adding a new negotiated service agreement to the competitive product list.
