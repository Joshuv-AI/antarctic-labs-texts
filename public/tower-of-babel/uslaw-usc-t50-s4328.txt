50 U.S.C. § 4328 — “Member of the former ruling family” defined
Citation: us:usc:50:4328
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section4328
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 53 (Trading With The Enemy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this chapter, the term “member of the former ruling family” means (1) any person who was at any time between April 6, 1917, and July 2, 1921, the German Emperor or the ruler of any constituent kingdom of the German Empire, or (2) the wife or any child of such person.
