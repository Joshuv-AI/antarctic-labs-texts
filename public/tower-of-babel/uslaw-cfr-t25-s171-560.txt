25 C.F.R. § 171.560 — What if I fail to make payments as specified in my Payment Plan?
Citation: us:cfr:25:171.560
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-171.560
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 171 (Irrigation Operation And Maintenance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) We will discontinue irrigation service until your bill is paid in full;
(b) You will be in default, you will be assessed an administrative fee, and your debt will be immediately forwarded to the United States Treasury in accordance with the Debt Collection Improvement Act of 1996 (Pub. L. 104-134).
(c) You will be ineligible for Payment Plans for the next 6 years.
