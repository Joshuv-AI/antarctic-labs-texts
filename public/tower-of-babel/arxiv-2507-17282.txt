Long time existence for a Boussinesq-like system with strong topography variations
Qi Li, Jean-Claude Saut, Li Xu

Abstract




Abstract
.
This paper investigates the long-time existence of solutions for a Boussinesq-like system modeling surface water waves in shallow water with
strong bottom topography variations
(i.e.
b=O(1)
where
b
measures the bathymetry). The system is derived under the
long-wavelength, small-amplitude
regime, with
h=1-b
represents the mean water depth.
The strong bottom topography may exhibit
slow (
\nabla h=O(\varepsilon)
) or fast (
\nabla h=O(1)
) oscillations
, where the small parameter
\varepsilon
measures the comparable long wave and weak nonlinearity effects. We establish the long time existence results in two typical regimes:

1).

Slow oscillation (
\nabla h=O(\varepsilon)
):
For both
1D
and
2D
cases, we prove the local existence on time scale
O(1/\varepsilon)
under some restrictions on the parameters ensuring the symmetry of the main dispersive terms. To overcome key challenges arising from
h
-dependent coefficients and the incomplete control of
\nabla\bm{V}
in
2D
, we design adapted energy functionals and exploit the cancellation for lower-order linear terms in
2D
.

2).

Fast oscillation (
\nabla h=O(1)
):
In
1D
, we extend the natural
O(1)
existence time to
O(1/\varepsilon)
for a special
case maintaining the symmetric principal dispersive structure. The proof uses a strategy that first establishes time regularity before recovering spatial derivatives, supported by carefully designed energy estimates and cancellation of lower-order terms.

These results provide the first rigorous long-time existence theory for Boussinesq-like system with strong topography variations in both
1D
and
2D
,
extending significantly previous work limited to fully symmetric cases.

Keywords:
Long time existence; Boussinesq-like system; uneven bottom

1.3.
Difficulties, parameter selection and strategies

The analysis of long-time existence for the Boussinesq-like system (
1.2
) presents two principal challenges originating from significant bottom topography variations (e.g.,
h(x)=1-b(x)=O(1)
):

(i)

Dependence on bottom variations:

Large bottom variation introduce explicit
h
-dependence in both the linear and dispersive terms of (
1.2
), fundamentally distinguishing it from flat-bottom Boussinesq systems where these terms have constant coefficients.

(ii)

Structural implications:
This structural difference requires careful parameter selection to preserve essential mathematical properties-particularly symmetry and cancellation mechanisms-which are crucial for ensuring long-time existence of solutions.

\bullet

Parameter selection

The well-posedness analysis is intrinsically linked to the selection of parameters. Based on the linearized analysis of the flat-bottom case, the dispersion parameters set
(a_{1},b_{1},c_{1},d_{1})
must satisfy the constraints (
1.7
). To eliminate the coupling effect of depth function
h
on dispersive terms, we adopt a symmetrized dispersive principal part by setting
b_{1}=c_{1}
. The determination of other parameters depends on both the system structure and key technical challenges:

(1). Energy functionals.
In view of system (
1.2
), we design the associated energy functional via
h
-dependent operators
{\mathcal{P}}_{h}^{1}
and
{\mathcal{P}}_{h}^{2}
as follows

(1.17)

\displaystyle\bigl((1-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}{\mathcal{P}}_{h}^{1})\bm{V}\,\big|\,\bm{V}\bigr)_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle=\|\bm{V}\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}a_{1}\|h\nabla\cdot\bm{V}\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}a_{2}\bigl(\nabla h\cdot\bm{V}\,\big|\,h\nabla\cdot\bm{V}\bigr)_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle\sim\|\bm{V}\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\varepsilon a_{1}\|h\nabla\cdot\bm{V}\|_{L^{2}}^{2}

\displaystyle\bigl((1-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}{\mathcal{P}}_{h}^{2})\eta\,\big|\,\eta\bigr)_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle=\|\eta\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}d_{1}\|h\nabla\eta\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}d_{2}\bigl(\nabla h\eta\,\big|\,h\nabla\eta\bigr)_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle\sim\|\eta\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\varepsilon d_{1}\|h\nabla\eta\|_{L^{2}}^{2},

provided that
0<h_{0}\leq h\leq 2
and

\displaystyle\begin{aligned} &a_{1}\geq 0,\,a_{2}=0,\,\,\text{or}\,\,a_{1}>0,\,a_{2}\neq 0,\,\,\|\nabla h\|_{L^{\infty}}\ll 1,\\
\text{and }\,\,&d_{1}\geq 0,\,d_{2}=0,\,\,\text{or}\,\,d_{1}>0,\,d_{2}\neq 0,\,\,\|\nabla h\|_{L^{\infty}}\ll 1.\end{aligned}

The higher-order energy functionals for
(\bm{V},\eta)
are designed in a similar way. In this paper, we impose

.

(1.18)

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\frac{d}{dt}\bigl((1-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}{\mathcal{P}}_{h}^{1})\bm{V}\,\big|\,\bm{V}\bigr)_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle=\bigl((1-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}{\mathcal{P}}_{h}^{1})\bm{V}_{t}\,\big|\,\bm{V}\bigr)_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}a_{2}\bigl[\bigl(\nabla h\cdot\bm{V}\,\big|\,h\nabla\cdot\bm{V}_{t}\bigr)_{L^{2}}-\bigl(\nabla h\cdot\bm{V}_{t}\,\big|\,h\nabla\cdot\bm{V}\bigr)_{L^{2}}\bigr]

The lack of full information on
\nabla\cdot\bm{V}
means that the cross term with
a_{2}
in (
1.18
) cannot be controlled unless
a_{2}=0
. Similarly, we have to take
b_{3}=-c_{3}
for
n=2
. Thus, for
n=2
, we impose

\displaystyle a_{2}=0,\quad b_{3}=-c_{3}.

(3). Difficulty in higher-order energy estimates.
The first-order energy estimate for
\bm{V}
leads to

\displaystyle\begin{aligned} \frac{1}{2}\frac{d}{dt}\bigl((1-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}{\mathcal{P}}_{h}^{1})\partial_{j}\bm{V}\,\big|\,\partial_{j}\bm{V}\bigr)_{L^{2}}&=\bigl(\partial_{j}[(1-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}{\mathcal{P}}_{h}^{1})\bm{V}_{t}]\,\big|\,\partial_{j}\bm{V}\bigr)_{L^{2}}\\
&\quad-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}a_{1}\bigl(h\partial_{j}h\nabla\cdot\bm{V}_{t}\,\big|\,\nabla\cdot\partial_{j}\bm{V}\bigr)_{L^{2}}+\cdots.\end{aligned}

When
a_{1}>0
, the spatial derivative
\partial_{j}
acts on
{\mathcal{P}}_{h}^{1}
(and consequently on
h
), producing the cross term
-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}a_{1}\bigl(h\partial_{j}h\nabla\cdot\bm{V}_{t}\,\big|\,\nabla\cdot\partial_{j}\bm{V}\bigr)_{L^{2}}
. Estimates on this term suggest an existence time scale of
O(\frac{1}{|\nabla h|})
. Accordingly, we analyze (
1.2
) in two regimes:

\displaystyle\textit{slow bottom oscillation:}\,\nabla h=O(\varepsilon)\quad\text{and}\quad\textit{fast bottom oscillation:}\,\nabla h=O(1).

The slow oscillations, the standard energy method on
H^{s}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{n})
yields the desired
O(\frac{1}{\varepsilon})
existence time under appropriate parameter choices. For fast oscillations, the expected existence time scale is
O(1)
. Achieving the long time scale
O(\frac{1}{\varepsilon})
requires a refined approach: we first improve time regularity (since
\partial_{t}
commutes with time-independent
h
) and then recover spatial regularity from the equations.

The fully symmetric case

(1.19)

\displaystyle a_{1}\geq 0,d_{1}\geq 0,a_{2}=d_{2}=0;\quad b_{1}=c_{1},\,b_{2}=-c_{2},\,b_{3}=-c_{3},\,b_{4}=c_{4},

would naturally ensure long-time existence for solutions of system (
1.2
). However, the constraint (
1.19
) cannot be satisfied for any
\{\lambda_{1},\lambda_{2},\mu,\theta\}
. Therefore, we consider systems where only the principal parts maintain symmetry, while developing careful estimates to control the resulting non-symmetric lower-order terms.

Based on this analysis, we select parameters for
slow
and
fast bottom oscillations
as specified in (
1.8
) and (
1.9
).

\bullet

Strategies of proofs

(1).
For Theorem
1.2
concerning the slow bottom oscillation, we employ standard energy methods for symmetric quasi-linear systems in
H^{s}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{n})
. For
n=2
, cancellations between lower-order linear terms is crucial.

(2).
For Theorem
1.4
concerning the fast bottom oscillation (
n=1
), the
h
-dependent coefficients in the linear part of system (
1.2
) (with
\partial_{x}h=O(1)
) require a two-stage regularity analysis. Since the spatial derivatives in
x
for the solution
(V,\eta)
present substantial analytical difficulties, we first derive
a priori
energy estimates for higher-order temporal regularity in
t
for
(V,\eta)
,(specially,
(\partial_{t}^{k}V,\,\partial_{x}^{k}\eta)\,
for
k=0,1,2
), by carefully utilizing both the structural properties of the principal linear operator and the cancellation mechanisms in lower-order terms. The established temporal regularity of
(V,\eta)
then enable us to recover the corresponding spatial regularity through the careful analysis of the coupled equations in system (
1.2
).

2.1.
Notations

The notation
f\lesssim g
means that there exists an universal constant
C>0
such that
f\leq Cg
. While the notation
g\gtrsim f
means
f\lesssim g
and
f\sim g
represents both
f\lesssim g
and

.
The constant
C>0
denotes a universal constant which may changes from line to line.

If
A,B
are two operators,
[A,B]=AB-BA
denotes their commutator.

The Fourier transform of a tempered distribution

\displaystyle\widehat{f}(\xi)={\mathcal{F}}(f)(\xi)\mathrel{\mathop{\kern 0.0pt=}\limits^{\hbox{\footnotesize def}}}\int_{\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{n}}e^{-ix\cdot\xi}f(x)dx.

.
And the Fourier multiplier

is defined by

\displaystyle\Lambda^{s}f(x)\mathrel{\mathop{\kern 0.0pt=}\limits^{\hbox{\footnotesize def}}}{\mathcal{F}}^{-1}\bigl[(1+|\xi|^{2})^{\frac{s}{2}}\widehat{f}(\xi)\bigr],\quad\forall\,f\in{\mathcal{S}}^{\prime}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{n}).

The notation
\|\cdot\|_{L^{p}}
stands for the
L^{p}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{n})

. For any

and

,
W^{s,p}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{n})
denote the classical
L^{p}
based non-homogeneous Sobolev spaces with the norm
\|\cdot\|_{W^{s,p}}
. We also use the notation
H^{s}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{n})=W^{s,2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{n})
for the sake of convenience.

The
L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{n})
-inner product of
f
and
g
is defined by

(f\,|\,g)_{L^{2}}\mathrel{\mathop{\kern 0.0pt=}\limits^{\hbox{\footnotesize def}}}\int_{\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{n}}f\bar{g}dx.

And the
H^{s}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{n})
-inner product of
f
and

is denoted by

\displaystyle(f\,|\,g)_{H^{s}}\mathrel{\mathop{\kern 0.0pt=}\limits^{\hbox{\footnotesize def}}}(\Lambda^{s}f\,|\,\Lambda^{s}g)_{L^{2}}.

For the function

2.2.
Technical lemmas

In this subsection, we present several crucial technical lemmas.
Firstly, we recall the classical tame product estimate:
if
s\geq 0
,

(2.1)

\|f\cdot g\|_{H^{s}}\lesssim\|f\|_{H^{s}}\|g\|_{L^{\infty}}+\|f\|_{L^{\infty}}\|g\|_{H^{s}},\quad\forall\,f,g\in H^{s}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{n})\cap L^{\infty}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{n}).

Wee also restate the lemma concerning the classical commutator estimates, as detailed in
[
18
]
, Theorems 3 and 6:

Lemma 2.1
.

Let
s\geq 0
,
t_{0}>\frac{n}{2}
and
-t_{0}<r\leq t_{0}+1
, then for any
f\in H^{t_{0}+1}\cap H^{s+r}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{n})
and
u\in H^{s+r-1}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{n})
, there holds

(2.2)

\|[\Lambda^{s},f]u\|_{H^{r}}\lesssim\begin{cases}\|\nabla f\|_{H^{t_{0}}}\|u\|_{H^{s+r-1}},\quad&s+r-1\leq t_{0}\\
\|\nabla f\|_{H^{s+r-1}}\|u\|_{H^{s+r-1}},&s+r-1>t_{0}\end{cases}.

Furthermore, we need more refined product estimates that are stated in the following lemma.

Lemma 2.2
.

Let
s\geq 0
,
t_{0}>\frac{n}{2}
, and
h-1\in H^{t_{0}+1}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{n})\cap H^{s}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{n})
be a real value function satisfying
h\in[0,2]
. Then there holds

(2.3)

\displaystyle\|hu\|_{H^{s}}\lesssim(\|\nabla h\|_{H^{t_{0}}}+\|\nabla h\|_{H^{s-1}})\|u\|_{H^{s-1}}+\|h\|_{L^{\infty}}\|u\|_{H^{s}},\quad\forall u\in H^{s}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{n}).

Proof.

Since

\displaystyle\Lambda^{s}(hu)=[\Lambda^{s},h-1]u+h\Lambda^{s}u,

we obtain (
2.3
) by using Lemma
2.1
for
r=0
.
∎

Lemma 2.3
.

Let
s>\frac{n}{2}+1
,
n=1,2
,
[s]
be the integer part of
s
and
h
be a real valued function taking values on
[h_{0},2]
for some
0<h_{0}<2
.

(1). If
F\in C^{1}([h_{0},2])
and
h-1\in H^{s}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{n})
, there holds

(2.4)

\|F(h)-F(1)\|_{H^{s}}\leq C_{\|F^{\prime}\|_{L^{\infty}},\|h-1\|_{L^{\infty}}}\|h-1\|_{H^{s}}.

(2). If
F\in C^{[s]+1}([h_{0},2])
and
h-1\in H^{s+1}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{n})
, there holds

(2.5)

\|\nabla(F\circ h)\|_{H^{[s]}}\leq C_{\|F\|_{W^{[s]+1,\infty}},\|\nabla h\|_{H^{s}}}\|\nabla h\|_{H^{s}}.

(3). If
r\in[0,2]
,
F\in C^{[s+r]+1}([h_{0},2])
and
h-1\in H^{s+r+1}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{n})

(2.6)

\displaystyle\|[\Lambda^{s},F\circ h]u\|_{H^{r}}\leq C_{\|F\|_{W^{[s+r]+1,\infty}},\|\nabla h\|_{H^{s+r}}}\|\nabla h\|_{H^{s+r}}\|u\|_{H^{s+r-1}},\quad\forall u\in H^{s+r-1}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{n}).

Proof.

(1). The composition estimate (
2.4
) is a consequence of Theorem 2.61 in
[
4
]
.

(2). For (
2.5
), we recall the Faà di Bruno’s formula on

as follows: for
\alpha\in({\mathop{\mathbb{Z}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits}_{\geq 0})^{n}

\displaystyle\partial^{\alpha}(F\circ h)=\sum_{1\leq j\leq|\alpha|}\sum_{\beta_{1}+\cdots+\beta_{j}=\alpha}C_{j,\beta_{1},\cdots,\beta_{j}}F^{(j)}(h)\cdot\prod_{l=1}^{j}\partial^{\beta_{l}}h,

where
|\beta_{l}|\geq 1\,(l=1,\cdots,j)
,
F^{(j)}(h)\mathrel{\mathop{\kern 0.0pt=}\limits^{\hbox{\footnotesize def}}}\frac{d^{j}F}{dh^{j}}(h)
, and the coefficients
C_{j,\beta_{1},\cdots,\beta_{j}}
are positive integers.

Consequently, for any multi-index
\alpha
such that
1\leq|\alpha|\leq[s]+1
, we obtain by using Hölder inequality and Sobolev’s embedding theorem that

\displaystyle\|\partial^{\alpha}(F\circ h)\|_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle\lesssim\|F\|_{W^{{[s]+1},\infty}}\sum_{1\leq j\leq|\alpha|}\sum_{\beta_{1}+\cdots+\beta_{j}=\alpha}\prod_{l=1}^{j}\|\partial^{\beta_{l}}h\|_{L^{\frac{2|\alpha|}{|\beta_{l}|}}}

\displaystyle\lesssim\|F\|_{W^{{[s]+1},\infty}}\sum_{1\leq j\leq|\alpha|}\sum_{\beta_{1}+\cdots+\beta_{j}=\alpha}\prod_{l=1}^{j}\|\nabla h\|_{H^{(\frac{1}{2}-\frac{|\beta_{l}|}{2|\alpha|})n+|\beta_{l}|-1}}.

Since

\displaystyle(\frac{1}{2}-\frac{|\beta_{l}|}{2|\alpha|})n+|\beta_{l}|-1=\bigl(1-\frac{n}{2|\alpha|}\bigr)|\beta_{l}|+\frac{n}{2}-1\leq[s]\leq s,\quad\forall l=1,\cdots,j,

we get

\displaystyle\|\partial^{\alpha}(F\circ h)\|_{L^{2}}\lesssim\|F\|_{W^{{[s]+1},\infty}}\sum_{1\leq j\leq|\alpha|}\|\nabla h\|_{H^{s}}^{j},

which implies

\displaystyle\|\nabla(F\circ h)\|_{H^{[s]}}\lesssim\sum_{1\leq|\alpha|\leq[s]+1}\|\partial^{\alpha}(F\circ h)\|_{L^{2}}\lesssim\sum_{j=1}^{[s]+1}\|\nabla h\|_{H^{s}}^{j}\|F\|_{W^{{[s]+1},\infty}}.

This gives rise to (
2.5
).

(3). Thanks to (
2.2

\displaystyle\begin{aligned} \|[\Lambda^{s},F\circ h]u\|_{H^{r}}&\lesssim\bigl(\|\nabla(F\circ h)\|_{H^{t_{0}}}+\|\nabla(F\circ h)\|_{H^{s+r-1}}\bigr)\|u\|_{H^{s+r-1}}\lesssim\|\nabla(F\circ h)\|_{H^{[s+r]}}\|u\|_{H^{s+r-1}},\end{aligned}

where

if

∎

+b_{3}\nabla h\nabla\cdot(h\sqrt{h}\nabla\eta)+b_{4}\sqrt{h}\nabla h(\nabla h\cdot\nabla\eta)\Bigr]=0,\\
(1-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\mathcal{P}_{h}^{2})\partial_{t}\eta+\nabla\cdot(\sqrt{h}\bm{V})+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\Bigl[f_{h}(\bm{V},\eta)+\nabla\cdot\bigl(b_{1}h^{2}\nabla\nabla\cdot(\sqrt{h}\bm{V})+c_{2}h\nabla h\nabla\cdot(\sqrt{h}\bm{V})\quad\quad\\
-b_{3}h\sqrt{h}\nabla(\nabla h\cdot\bm{V})+c_{4}\sqrt{h}\nabla h(\nabla h\cdot\bm{V})\bigr)\Bigr]=0,\end{aligned}\right.

where
\bm{F}_{h}(\bm{V},\eta)
and
f_{h}(\bm{V},\eta)
are defined in (
1.4
).

The
a priori
energy estimates for system (
1.2
) under restriction (
1.8
) is stated in the following proposition. For
n=2
, the system (
1.2
) reduces to (
3.2
).

Proposition 3.1
.

Let
{\color[rgb]{1,0,0}s>\frac{n}{2}+2}
,
n=1,2
,
T_{0}>0
,
\varepsilon\in(0,1)
,

\{a_{j},d_{j}\}_{j=1,2}
and
\{b_{j},c_{j}\}_{i=1,2,3,4}
satisfy the conditions outlined in (
1.8
). Assume that
h
satisfies

(3.3)

\|\nabla h\|_{H^{s+2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{n})}\leq C\varepsilon\leq c_{0}\leq 1,\quad 2\geq h(x)\geq h_{0}>0,\quad\text{for some}\,h_{0}>0,

where
C>0
is a universal constant and
c_{0}>0
is the constant stated in Lemma
2.4
. Then for any sufficiently smooth solutions
(\bm{V},\eta)
of (
1.2
)-(
1.10
) over
[0,\frac{T_{0}}{\varepsilon}]
, there exist constants
C_{1}>1
and
C_{2}>1
such that

(3.4)

{\mathcal{E}}_{s}(t)\leq C_{1}{\mathcal{E}}_{s}(0)+C_{2}\varepsilon t\max_{\tau\in[0,t]}{\mathcal{E}}_{s}(\tau)\bigl(1+\varepsilon{\mathcal{E}}_{s}(\tau)^{\frac{1}{2}}\bigr),\quad\forall\,t\in[0,\frac{T_{0}}{\varepsilon}],

where

{\mathcal{E}}_{s}(t)\mathrel{\mathop{\kern 0.0pt=}\limits^{\hbox{\footnotesize def}}}\|\bm{V}(t,\cdot)\|_{{\bf X}_{\varepsilon}^{s}}^{2}+\|\eta(t,\cdot)\|_{X_{\varepsilon}^{s}}^{2}.

Proof.

We only sketch the proof of case
n=2
. The proof is divided into several steps.

Step 1. Energy estimates.
Firstly, we define the energy functional associated with (
3.2
) as follows:

(3.5)

E_{s}(t)\mathrel{\mathop{\kern 0.0pt=}\limits^{\hbox{\footnotesize def}}}\bigl((1-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}{\mathcal{P}}_{h}^{1})\bm{V}\,|\,\bm{V}\bigr)_{H^{s}}+\bigl((1-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}{\mathcal{P}}_{h}^{2})\eta\,|\,\eta\bigr)_{H^{s}}.

Thanks to Lemma
2.4
, there holds

(3.6)

\displaystyle E_{s}(t)\sim\|\bm{V}\|_{{\bf X}_{\varepsilon}^{s}}^{2}+\|\eta\|_{X_{\varepsilon}^{s}}^{2}={\mathcal{E}}_{s}(t).

For system (
3.2
), a direct calculation yields to

(3.7)

\frac{1}{2}\frac{d}{dt}E_{s}(t)=\mathcal{S}+\mathcal{T}+\mathcal{N},

where

\displaystyle\begin{aligned} \mathcal{S}&\mathrel{\mathop{\kern 0.0pt=}\limits^{\hbox{\footnotesize def}}}-\left\{\bigl(\sqrt{h}\nabla\eta\,|\,\bm{V}\bigr)_{H^{s}}+\left(\nabla\cdot(\sqrt{h}\bm{V})\,|\,\eta\right)_{H^{s}}\right\}\\
&\,\,\quad-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}b_{1}\left\{\bigl(\sqrt{h}\nabla\nabla\cdot(h^{2}\nabla\eta)\,|\,\bm{V}\bigr)_{H^{s}}+\bigl(\nabla\cdot(h^{2}\nabla\nabla\cdot(\sqrt{h}\bm{V}))\,|\,\eta\bigr)_{H^{s}}\right\}\\
&\,\,\quad-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}b_{3}\left\{\bigl(\nabla h\nabla\cdot(h\sqrt{h}\nabla\eta)\,|\,\bm{V}\bigr)_{H^{s}}-\bigl(\nabla\cdot(h\sqrt{h}\nabla(\nabla h\cdot\bm{V}))\,|\,\eta\bigr)_{H^{s}}\right\}\\
&\mathrel{\mathop{\kern 0.0pt=}\limits^{\hbox{\footnotesize def}}}\mathcal{S}_{1}-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}b_{1}\mathcal{S}_{2}-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}b_{3}\mathcal{S}_{3},\\
\mathcal{T}&\mathrel{\mathop{\kern 0.0pt=}\limits^{\hbox{\footnotesize def}}}-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\left({\bm{F}}_{h}(\bm{V},\eta)\,|\,\bm{V}\right)_{H^{s}}-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\left(f_{h}(\bm{V},\eta)\,|\,\eta\right)_{H^{s}},\\
\mathcal{N}&\mathrel{\mathop{\kern 0.0pt=}\limits^{\hbox{\footnotesize def}}}\frac{\varepsilon}{4}\Bigl\{\left(\mathcal{P}_{h}^{1}\bm{V}_{t}\,|\,\bm{V}\right)_{H^{s}}-\left(\mathcal{P}_{h}^{1}\bm{V}\,|\,\bm{V}_{t}\right)_{H^{s}}+\left(\mathcal{P}_{h}^{2}\eta_{t}\,|\,\eta\right)_{H^{s}}-\left(\mathcal{P}_{h}^{2}\eta\,|\,\eta_{t}\right)_{H^{s}}\Bigr\}\\
&\quad\,\,-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\Bigl\{b_{2}\bigl(\sqrt{h}\nabla(h\nabla h\cdot\nabla\eta)\,|\,\bm{V}\bigr)_{H^{s}}-c_{2}\bigl(h\nabla h\nabla\cdot(\sqrt{h}\bm{V})\,|\,\nabla\eta\bigr)_{H^{s}}\\
&\quad\,\,+b_{4}\bigl(\sqrt{h}\nabla h(\nabla h\cdot\nabla\eta)\,|\,\bm{V}\bigr)_{H^{s}}-c_{4}\bigl(\sqrt{h}\nabla h(\nabla h\cdot\bm{V})\,|\,\nabla\eta\bigr)_{H^{s}}\Bigr\}\\
&\mathrel{\mathop{\kern 0.0pt=}\limits^{\hbox{\footnotesize def}}}\frac{\varepsilon}{4}\mathcal{N}_{1}-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\mathcal{N}_{2}.\end{aligned}

Step 2. Estimate of
\mathcal{S}
.
We estimate
\mathcal{S}_{j}\,(j=1,2,3)
one by one.

Step 2.1. Estimate of
\mathcal{S}_{1}
.
Due to the definition of
\mathcal{S}_{1}
, we have

\displaystyle\mathcal{S}_{1}

\displaystyle=-\left(\Lambda^{s}(\sqrt{h}\nabla\eta)\,|\,\Lambda^{s}\bm{V}\right)_{L^{2}}+\left(\Lambda^{s}(\sqrt{h}\bm{V})\,|\,\Lambda^{s}\nabla\eta\right)_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle=-\left([\Lambda^{s},\sqrt{h}]\nabla\eta\,|\,\Lambda^{s}\bm{V}\right)_{L^{2}}-\left(\Lambda^{s}\eta\,|\,\nabla([\Lambda^{s},\sqrt{h}]\bm{V})\right)_{L^{2}}.

Using the assumption
\|\nabla h\|_{H^{s+2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{n})}\leq C\varepsilon\leq c_{0}\leq 1
, (
2.2
) and (
2.6
) with
F(x)=\sqrt{x}
, we obtain

(3.8)

\displaystyle|\mathcal{S}_{1}|

\displaystyle\lesssim\|\nabla h\|_{H^{s}}\|\nabla\eta\|_{H^{s-1}}\|\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}}+\|\nabla h\|_{H^{s+1}}\|\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}}\|\eta\|_{H^{s}}

\displaystyle\lesssim\|\nabla h\|_{H^{s+1}}\|\eta\|_{H^{s}}\|\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}}\lesssim\varepsilon\|\eta\|_{H^{s}}\|\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}}\lesssim\varepsilon{\mathcal{E}}_{s}(t).

Step 2.2. Estimate of
\mathcal{S}_{2}
.
For
\mathcal{S}_{2}
, we rewrite it as

\displaystyle\begin{aligned} \mathcal{S}_{2}&=\Bigl(\Lambda^{s}\bigl(\sqrt{h}\nabla\nabla\cdot(h^{2}\nabla\eta)\bigr)\,\Big|\,\Lambda^{s}\bm{V}\Bigr)_{L^{2}}-\Bigl(\Lambda^{s}\bigl(h^{2}\nabla\nabla\cdot(\sqrt{h}\bm{V})\bigr)\,\Big|\,\Lambda^{s}\nabla\eta\Bigr)_{L^{2}}\\
&=\bigl([\Lambda^{s},\sqrt{h}]\nabla\nabla\cdot(h^{2}\nabla\eta)\,\big|\,\Lambda^{s}\bm{V}\bigr)_{L^{2}}-\bigl([\Lambda^{s},h^{2}]\nabla\nabla\cdot(\sqrt{h}\bm{V})\,|\,\Lambda^{s}\nabla\eta\bigr)_{L^{2}}\\
&\quad\,-\bigl(\Lambda^{s}(h^{2}\nabla\eta)\,|\,\nabla\nabla\cdot([\Lambda^{s},\sqrt{h}]\bm{V})\bigr)_{L^{2}}+\bigl(\Lambda^{s}\nabla\nabla\cdot(\sqrt{h}\bm{V})\,|\,[\Lambda^{s},h^{2}]\nabla\eta\bigr)_{L^{2}}\\
&\mathrel{\mathop{\kern 0.0pt=}\limits^{\hbox{\footnotesize def}}}\sum_{j=1}^{4}{\mathcal{S}}_{2j}.\end{aligned}

For
{\mathcal{S}}_{21}
, using integration by parts, we have

\displaystyle\mathcal{S}_{21}

\displaystyle=-\bigl([\Lambda^{s},\nabla(\sqrt{h})]\nabla\cdot(h^{2}\nabla\eta)\,|\,\Lambda^{s}\bm{V}\bigr)_{L^{2}}-\bigl([\Lambda^{s},\sqrt{h}]\nabla\cdot(h^{2}\nabla\eta)\,|\,\Lambda^{s}\nabla\cdot\bm{V}\bigr)_{L^{2}}

which along with (
2.2
) implies

\displaystyle\begin{aligned} |\mathcal{S}_{21}|&\lesssim\|\nabla^{2}(\sqrt{h})\|_{H^{s-1}}\|\nabla\cdot(h^{2}\nabla\eta)\|_{H^{s-1}}\|\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}}+\|\nabla\sqrt{h}\|_{H^{s-1}}\|\nabla\cdot(h^{2}\nabla\eta)\|_{H^{s-1}}\|\nabla\cdot\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}}\\
&\lesssim\|\nabla\sqrt{h}\|_{H^{s}}\|h^{2}\nabla\eta\|_{H^{s}}(\|\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}}+\|\nabla\cdot\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}}).\end{aligned}

By virtue of (
2.5
) with
F(r)=\sqrt{r}
and (
2.7
) with
F(r)=r^{2}
, we get by using the assumption
\|\nabla h\|_{H^{s+2}}\leq C\varepsilon\leq c_{0}\leq 1
that

(3.9)

\|\nabla\sqrt{h}\|_{H^{s+1}}\leq\|\nabla\sqrt{h}\|_{H^{[s]+2}}\lesssim\|\nabla h\|_{H^{s+2}}\lesssim\varepsilon,\quad\|h^{2}\nabla\eta\|_{H^{s}}\lesssim\|\nabla\eta\|_{H^{s}}.

Then we obtain

(3.10)

|\mathcal{S}_{21}|\lesssim\varepsilon\|\nabla\eta\|_{H^{s}}(\|\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}}+\|\nabla\cdot\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}})\lesssim\|\eta\|_{X^{s}_{\varepsilon}}\|\bm{V}\|_{\textbf{X}^{s}_{\varepsilon}}.

For
\mathcal{S}_{23}
, we rewrite as

\displaystyle\mathcal{S}_{23}=-\bigl(\Lambda^{s}(h^{2}\nabla\eta)\,|\,\nabla([\Lambda^{s},\sqrt{h}]\nabla\cdot\bm{V})\bigr)_{L^{2}}-\bigl(\Lambda^{s}(h^{2}\nabla\eta)\,|\,\nabla([\Lambda^{s},\nabla\sqrt{h}]\cdot\bm{V})\bigr)_{L^{2}},

which together with (
2.2
), (
2.6
) and the assumption
\|\nabla h\|_{H^{s+2}}\leq C\varepsilon\leq c_{0}\leq 1
gives rise to

\displaystyle|{\mathcal{S}}_{23}|\lesssim\bigl(\|\nabla h\|_{H^{s+1}}\|\nabla\cdot\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}}+\|\nabla^{2}\sqrt{h}\|_{H^{s}}\|\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}}\bigr)\|h^{2}\nabla\eta\|_{H^{s}}.

Using (
3.9
), we obtain

(3.11)

|\mathcal{S}_{23}|\lesssim\varepsilon\|\nabla\eta\|_{H^{s}}(\|\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}}+\|\nabla\cdot\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}})\lesssim\|\eta\|_{X^{s}_{\varepsilon}}\|\bm{V}\|_{\textbf{X}^{s}_{\varepsilon}}.

For
\mathcal{S}_{22}
and
\mathcal{S}_{24}
,
due to (
2.6
) and the assumption
\|\nabla h\|_{H^{s+2}}\leq C\varepsilon\leq c_{0}\leq 1
, we get

\displaystyle|\mathcal{S}_{22}|+|\mathcal{S}_{24}|\lesssim\|\nabla h\|_{H^{s+1}}\|\nabla\cdot(\sqrt{h}\bm{V})\|_{H^{s}}\|\nabla\eta\|_{H^{s}}\lesssim\varepsilon\bigl(\|\nabla\sqrt{h}\|_{H^{s}}\|\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}}+\|\sqrt{h}\nabla\cdot\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}}\bigr)\|\nabla\eta\|_{H^{s}},

which along with (
3.9
) and (
2.4
) yields to

(3.12)

|\mathcal{S}_{22}|+|\mathcal{S}_{24}|\lesssim\varepsilon\|\nabla\eta\|_{H^{s}}(\|\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}}+\|\nabla\cdot\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}})\lesssim\|\eta\|_{X^{s}_{\varepsilon}}\|\bm{V}\|_{\bm{X}^{s}_{\varepsilon}}.

Thanks to (
3.10
),(
3.11
) and (
3.12
), we deduce

(3.13)

|\mathcal{S}_{2}|\lesssim\|\eta\|_{X^{s}_{\varepsilon}}\|\bm{V}\|_{\bm{X}^{s}_{\varepsilon}}\lesssim{\mathcal{E}}_{s}(t).

Step 2.3. Estimate of
\mathcal{S}_{3}
.
Using the expression of
\mathcal{S}_{3}
, we have

\displaystyle\mathcal{S}_{3}

\displaystyle=\bigl\{\bigl([\Lambda^{s},\nabla h]\nabla\cdot(h\sqrt{h}\nabla\eta)\,|\,\Lambda^{s}\bm{V}\bigr)_{L^{2}}+\bigl([\Lambda^{s},h\sqrt{h}]\nabla(\nabla h\cdot\bm{V})\,|\,\Lambda^{s}\nabla\eta)\bigr)_{L^{2}}\bigr\}

\displaystyle\qquad-\bigl\{\bigl(\Lambda^{s}(h\sqrt{h}\nabla\eta)\,|\,\nabla(\nabla h\cdot\Lambda^{s}\bm{V})\bigr)_{L^{2}}-\bigl(\Lambda^{s}\nabla(\nabla h\cdot\bm{V})\,|\,h\sqrt{h}\Lambda^{s}\nabla\eta\bigr)_{L^{2}}\bigr\}\mathrel{\mathop{\kern 0.0pt=}\limits^{\hbox{\footnotesize def}}}\mathcal{S}_{31}+\mathcal{S}_{32}.

For
{\mathcal{S}}_{31}
, by virtue of (
2.2
) and (
2.6
), we have

\displaystyle|\mathcal{S}_{31}|\lesssim\|\nabla^{2}h\|_{H^{s-1}}\|\nabla(h\sqrt{h}\nabla\eta)\|_{H^{s-1}}\|\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}}+\|\nabla h\|_{H^{s}}\|\nabla(\nabla h\cdot\bm{V})\|_{H^{s-1}}\|\nabla\eta\|_{H^{s}}

which along with (
2.7
) and the assumption
\|\nabla h\|_{H^{s+2}}\leq C\varepsilon\leq c_{0}\leq 1
yields to

(3.14)

|\mathcal{S}_{31}|\lesssim\|\nabla h\|_{H^{s}}\|\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}}\|\nabla\eta\|_{H^{s}}\lesssim\varepsilon\|\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}}\|\nabla\eta\|_{H^{s}}\lesssim\|\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}}\|\eta\|_{X^{s}_{\varepsilon}}.

For
\mathcal{S}_{32}
, we rewrite it as

\displaystyle\begin{aligned} \mathcal{S}_{32}&=-\bigl([\Lambda^{s},h\sqrt{h}]\nabla\eta\,|\,\nabla(\nabla h\cdot\Lambda^{s}\bm{V})\bigr)_{L^{2}}+\bigl(\nabla([\Lambda^{s},\nabla h]\cdot\bm{V})\,|\,h\sqrt{h}\Lambda^{s}\nabla\eta\bigr)_{L^{2}}\\
&=\bigl(\nabla\cdot([\Lambda^{s},h\sqrt{h}]\nabla\eta)\,|\,\nabla h\cdot\Lambda^{s}\bm{V}\bigr)_{L^{2}}+\bigl(\nabla([\Lambda^{s},\nabla h]\cdot\bm{V})\,|\,h\sqrt{h}\Lambda^{s}\nabla\eta\bigr)_{L^{2}}.\end{aligned}

Thanks to (
2.2
), (
2.6
),(
2.7
) and the assumption (
3.3
), we get

\displaystyle\begin{aligned} |\mathcal{S}_{32}|&\lesssim\|\nabla h\|_{H^{s+1}}\|\nabla\eta\|_{H^{s}}\|\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}}+\|\nabla h\|_{H^{s+1}}\|\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}}\|\Lambda^{s}\nabla\eta\|_{L^{2}}\\
&\lesssim\varepsilon\|\eta\|_{H^{s+1}}\|\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}}\lesssim\|\eta\|_{X^{s}_{\varepsilon}}\|\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}},\end{aligned}

which along with (
3.14
) implies

(3.15)

|S_{3}|\lesssim\|\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}}\|\eta\|_{X^{s}_{\varepsilon}}\lesssim{\mathcal{E}}_{s}(t).

Step 2.4. Estimate of
{\mathcal{S}}
.
Due to (
3.8
),(
3.13
) and (
3.15
), we obtain

(3.16)

\displaystyle|\mathcal{S}|\leq|\mathcal{S}_{1}|+\varepsilon|\mathcal{S}_{2}|+\varepsilon|\mathcal{S}_{3}|\lesssim\varepsilon{\mathcal{E}}_{s}(t).

Remark 3.1
.

We remark that each term in
{\mathcal{S}}_{32}
cannot be controlled by
{\mathcal{E}}_{s}(t)
due to the absence of information regarding
\sqrt{\varepsilon}\|\nabla\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}}
in
{\mathcal{E}}_{s}(t)
. Nevertheless, there exists a cancellation between the two terms in
{\mathcal{S}}_{32}
. This is why we require
b_{3}=-c_{3}
in the two dimensional case.

Step 3. Estimate of
\mathcal{T}
.

By the expressions of
\bm{F}_{h}(\bm{V},\eta)
and
f_{h}(\bm{V},\eta)
, we rewrite
\mathcal{T}
as

\displaystyle\mathcal{T}=

\displaystyle-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\left\{\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{h}}\eta\nabla\eta\,\Big|\,\bm{V}\right)_{H^{s}}+\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{h}}\nabla\cdot(\eta\bm{V})\,\Big|\,\eta\right)_{H^{s}}\right\}

\displaystyle-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{h}}(\frac{1}{2}\nabla|\bm{V}|^{2}+\bm{V}\cdot\nabla\bm{V}+\bm{V}\nabla\cdot\bm{V})\,\Big|\,\bm{V}\right)_{H^{s}}

\displaystyle-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\left\{\left(\frac{1}{h^{\frac{3}{2}}}(\frac{1}{2}(\nabla h\cdot\bm{V})\bm{V}-|\bm{V}|^{2}\nabla h)\,\Big|\,\bm{V}\right)_{H^{s}}-\left(\frac{\eta}{2h^{\frac{3}{2}}}\nabla h\cdot\bm{V}\,\Big|\,\eta\right)_{H^{s}}\right\}

\displaystyle\mathrel{\mathop{\kern 0.0pt=}\limits^{\hbox{\footnotesize def}}}

\displaystyle-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}{\mathcal{T}}_{1}-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}{\mathcal{T}}_{2}-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}{\mathcal{T}}_{3}

Step 3.1. Estimate of
\mathcal{T}_{1}
.
A direct calculation leads to

\displaystyle\mathcal{T}_{1}

\displaystyle=\left(\Lambda^{s}(\frac{\eta}{\sqrt{h}}\nabla\eta)\,\Big|\,\Lambda^{s}\bm{V}\right)_{L^{2}}+\left(\Lambda^{s}(\frac{\eta}{\sqrt{h}}\nabla\cdot\bm{V})\,\Big|\,\Lambda^{s}\eta\right)_{L^{2}}+\left(\Lambda^{s}(\frac{\bm{V}}{\sqrt{h}}\cdot\nabla\eta)\,\Big|\,\Lambda^{s}\eta\right)_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle=\left\{\left([\Lambda^{s},\frac{\eta}{\sqrt{h}}]\nabla\eta\,\Big|\,\Lambda^{s}\bm{V}\right)_{L^{2}}+\left([\Lambda^{s},\frac{\eta}{\sqrt{h}}]\nabla\cdot\bm{V}\,\Big|\,\Lambda^{s}\eta\right)_{L^{2}}+\left([\Lambda^{s},\frac{\bm{V}}{\sqrt{h}}]\cdot\nabla\eta\,\Big|\,\Lambda^{s}\eta\right)_{L^{2}}\right\}

\displaystyle\qquad+\left\{-\left(\nabla(\frac{\eta}{\sqrt{h}})\Lambda^{s}\eta\,\Big|\,\Lambda^{s}\bm{V}\right)_{L^{2}}+\left(\frac{\bm{V}}{\sqrt{h}}\cdot\nabla\Lambda^{s}\eta\,\Big|\,\Lambda^{s}\eta\right)_{L^{2}}\right\}

\displaystyle\mathrel{\mathop{\kern 0.0pt=}\limits^{\hbox{\footnotesize def}}}{\mathcal{T}}_{11}+{\mathcal{T}}_{12}.

For
{\mathcal{T}}_{11}
, applying (
2.2
) and then using (
2.7
) and the assumption (
3.3
), we have

(3.17)

|{\mathcal{T}}_{11}|\lesssim\Big\|\nabla(\frac{\eta}{\sqrt{h}})\Big\|_{H^{s-1}}\|\eta\|_{H^{s}}\|\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}}+\Big\|\nabla(\frac{\bm{V}}{\sqrt{h}})\Big\|_{H^{s-1}}\|\eta\|_{H^{s}}^{2}\lesssim\|\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}}\|\eta\|_{H^{s}}^{2}.

While for last term of
{\mathcal{T}}_{12}
, integration by parts yields to

\displaystyle\left(\frac{\bm{V}}{\sqrt{h}}\cdot\nabla\Lambda^{s}\eta\,\Big|\,\Lambda^{s}\eta\right)_{L^{2}}=-\frac{1}{2}\left(\nabla\cdot\frac{\bm{V}}{\sqrt{h}}\Lambda^{s}\eta\,\Big|\,\Lambda^{s}\eta\right)_{L^{2}}.

Then using the Sobolev’s embedding
H^{s-1}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{n})\hookrightarrow L^{\infty}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{n})
for
s>\frac{n}{2}+1
, (
2.7
) and the assumption (
3.3
), we obtain

\displaystyle|\mathcal{T}_{12}|\lesssim\Big\|\nabla(\frac{\eta}{\sqrt{h}})\Big\|_{H^{s-1}}\|\eta\|_{H^{s}}\|\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}}+\Big\|\nabla\cdot(\frac{\bm{V}}{\sqrt{h}})\Big\|_{H^{s-1}}\|\eta\|_{H^{s}}^{2}\lesssim\|\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}}\|\eta\|_{H^{s}}^{2},

which along with (
3.17
) leads to

(3.18)

|\mathcal{T}_{1}|\lesssim\|\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}}\|\eta\|_{H^{s}}^{2}\lesssim{\mathcal{E}}_{s}(t)^{\frac{3}{2}}.

Step 3.2. Estimate of
\mathcal{T}_{2}
.
For the first term of
{\mathcal{T}}_{2}
, denoting by
{\bm{V}}=(V_{1},V_{2})^{T}
, we have

\displaystyle\begin{aligned} &\quad\left(\frac{1}{2\sqrt{h}}\nabla|\bm{V}|^{2}\,\Big|\,\bm{V}\right)_{H^{s}}=\sum_{j,k=1}^{2}\left(\Lambda^{s}(\frac{1}{\sqrt{h}}V_{j}\partial_{k}V_{j})\,\Big|\,\Lambda^{s}V_{k}\right)_{L^{2}}\\
&=\sum_{j,k=1}^{2}\Bigl\{\left([\Lambda^{s},\frac{V_{j}}{\sqrt{h}}]\partial_{k}V_{j})\,\Big|\,\Lambda^{s}V_{k}\right)_{L^{2}}+\left(\partial_{k}\Lambda^{s}V_{j}\,\Big|\,\frac{V_{j}}{\sqrt{h}}\Lambda^{s}V_{k}\right)_{L^{2}}\Bigr\}\\
&=\sum_{j,k=1}^{2}\Bigl\{\left([\Lambda^{s},\frac{V_{j}}{\sqrt{h}}]\partial_{k}V_{j})\,\Big|\,\Lambda^{s}V_{k}\right)_{L^{2}}-\left(\Lambda^{s}V_{j}\,\Big|\,\partial_{k}\bigl(\frac{V_{j}}{\sqrt{h}}\bigr)\Lambda^{s}V_{k}\right)_{L^{2}}\Bigr\}-\left(\Lambda^{s}\bm{V}\,\Big|\,\frac{\bm{V}}{\sqrt{h}}\Lambda^{s}\nabla\cdot\bm{V}\right)_{L^{2}}\\
&\mathrel{\mathop{\kern 0.0pt=}\limits^{\hbox{\footnotesize def}}}{\mathcal{T}}_{21}-\left(\Lambda^{s}\bm{V}\,\Big|\,\frac{\bm{V}}{\sqrt{h}}\Lambda^{s}\nabla\cdot\bm{V}\right)_{L^{2}}.\end{aligned}

Then we get

\displaystyle\mathcal{T}_{2}

\displaystyle={\mathcal{T}}_{21}-\left(\Lambda^{s}\bm{V}\,\Big|\,\frac{\bm{V}}{\sqrt{h}}\Lambda^{s}\nabla\cdot\bm{V}\right)_{L^{2}}+\left(\Lambda^{s}\bigl(\frac{\bm{V}}{\sqrt{h}}\nabla\cdot\bm{V}\bigr)\,\Big|\,\Lambda^{s}\bm{V}\bigr)\right)_{L^{2}}+\left(\Lambda^{s}\bigl(\frac{\bm{V}}{\sqrt{h}}\cdot\nabla\bm{V}\bigr)\,\Big|\,\Lambda^{s}\bm{V}\bigr)\right)_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle={\mathcal{T}}_{21}+\left([\Lambda^{s},\frac{\bm{V}}{\sqrt{h}}]\nabla\cdot\bm{V}\,\Big|\,\Lambda^{s}\bm{V}\bigr)\right)_{L^{2}}+\left([\Lambda^{s},\frac{\bm{V}}{\sqrt{h}}]\cdot\nabla\bm{V}\,\Big|\,\Lambda^{s}\bm{V}\bigr)\right)_{L^{2}}-\frac{1}{2}\left(\nabla\cdot\bigl(\frac{\bm{V}}{\sqrt{h}}\bigr)\Lambda^{s}\bm{V}\,\Big|\,\Lambda^{s}\bm{V}\bigr)\right)_{L^{2}}

Similar derivation as (
3.18
) yields to

(3.19)

|\mathcal{T}_{2}|\lesssim\Big\|\nabla(\frac{\bm{V}}{\sqrt{h}})\Big\|_{H^{s-1}}\|\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}}^{2}\lesssim\|\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}}^{3}\lesssim{\mathcal{E}}_{s}(t)^{\frac{3}{2}}.

Step 3.3. Estimate of
\mathcal{T}_{3}
.
The classical tame estimate and (
2.7
) give rise to

(3.20)

\displaystyle|\mathcal{T}_{3}|\lesssim\Big\|\frac{\nabla h}{h^{\frac{3}{2}}}\Big\|_{H^{s}}\|\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}}\bigl(\|\bm{V}\|_{H^{s}}^{2}+\|\eta\|_{H^{s}}^{2}\bigr)\lesssim{\mathcal{E}}_{s}(t)^{\frac{3}{2}}.

Step 3.4. Estimate of
{\mathcal{T}}
.
Due to (
3.18
)-(
3.20
), we get

(3.21)

\displaystyle|\mathcal{T}|\lesssim\varepsilon{\mathcal{E}}_{s}(t)^{\frac{3}{2}}.

Step 4. Estimate of
\mathcal{N}
.
We estimate
\mathcal{N}_{j}\,(j=1,2)
one by one.

Step 4.1. Estimate of
\mathcal{N}_{1}
.
Recall the definitions of operators
\mathcal{P}_{h}^{1}
and
\mathcal{P}_{h}^{2}
in (
3.1
), it follows that

\displaystyle\mathcal{N}_{1}

\displaystyle=a_{1}\bigl\{(\nabla(h^{2}\nabla\cdot\bm{V}_{t})\,|\,\bm{V})_{H^{s}}-(\nabla(h^{2}\nabla\cdot\bm{V})\,|\,\bm{V}_{t})_{H^{s}}\bigr\}

\displaystyle\quad+d_{1}\bigl\{(\nabla\cdot(h^{2}\nabla\eta_{t})\,|\,\eta)_{H^{s}}-(\nabla\cdot(h^{2}\nabla\eta)\,|\,\eta_{t})_{H^{s}}\bigr\}

\displaystyle\quad+d_{2}\bigl\{(\nabla\cdot(h\nabla h\,\eta_{t})\,|\,\eta)_{H^{s}}-(\nabla\cdot(h\nabla h\,\eta)\,|\,\eta_{t})_{H^{s}}\bigr\}

\displaystyle\mathrel{\mathop{\kern 0.0pt=}\limits^{\hbox{\footnotesize def}}}a_{1}\mathcal{N}_{11}+d_{1}\mathcal{N}_{12}+d_{2}\mathcal{N}_{13}.

For term
\mathcal{N}_{11}
, we have

\displaystyle\mathcal{N}_{11}

\displaystyle=-\bigl(\Lambda^{s}(h^{2}\nabla\cdot\bm{V}_{t})\,|\,\Lambda^{s}\nabla\cdot\bm{V})_{L^{2}}+\bigl(\Lambda^{s}(h^{2}\nabla\cdot\bm{V})\,\big|\,\Lambda^{s}\nabla\cdot\bm{V}_{t}\bigr)_{L^{2}}

which together with (

\displaystyle|\mathcal{N}_{1}|\lesssim(\|\bm{V}_{t}(t,\cdot)\|_{{\bf X}_{\varepsilon}^{s-1}}+\|\eta_{t}(t,\cdot)\|_{X_{\varepsilon}^{s-1}}){\mathcal{E}}_{s}(t)^{\frac{1}{2}}.

The goal of this section is to prove Theorem

) (

) with a fast oscillating bottom, under the condition specified in (

The

4.1.
Reduction of the system

When
n=1
and the parameters
\{a_{i},d_{i}\}_{i=1,2},\{b_{i},c_{i}\}_{1\leq i\leq 4}
fulfill (
1.9
), i.e.,

\displaystyle a_{1}>0,\,a_{2}=0,\,d_{1}=d_{2}=0,\,b_{1}=c_{1}<0,\,b_{2}+c_{2}+b_{3}+c_{3}=0,\,(b_{4},d_{4})\in\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{2},

there hold

\displaystyle{\mathcal{P}}_{h}^{1}=a_{1}\partial_{x}(h^{2}\partial_{x}\quad),\quad\mathcal{P}_{h}^{2}=0.

By rewriting
F_{h}(V,\eta)
and
f_{h}(V,\eta)
into

\displaystyle F_{h}(V,\eta)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{h}}\eta\partial_{x}\eta+\frac{3}{\sqrt{h}}V\partial_{x}V-\frac{\partial_{x}h}{2h\sqrt{h}}|V|^{2},\quad f_{h}(V,\eta)=\partial_{x}\bigl(\frac{\eta V}{\sqrt{h}}\bigr),

the system (
1.2
) is reduced to

(4.1)

\left\{\begin{aligned} &(1-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}{\mathcal{P}}_{h}^{1})\partial_{t}V+\sqrt{h}\partial_{x}\eta+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}B_{h}(D)\eta+\frac{\varepsilon}{2\sqrt{h}}\eta\partial_{x}\eta+\frac{3\varepsilon}{2\sqrt{h}}V\partial_{x}V=\frac{\varepsilon\partial_{x}h}{4h^{\frac{3}{2}}}|V|^{2},\\
&\partial_{t}\eta+\partial_{x}(\sqrt{h}V)+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}C_{h}(D)V+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\partial_{x}\bigl(\frac{\eta V}{\sqrt{h}}\bigr)=0,\end{aligned}\right.

where the operators
B_{h}(D),C_{h}(D)
are defined by

\displaystyle\begin{aligned} &B_{h}(D)f\mathrel{\mathop{\kern 0.0pt=}\limits^{\hbox{\footnotesize def}}}b_{1}\sqrt{h}\partial_{x}^{2}(h^{2}\partial_{x}f)+(b_{2}+b_{3})h^{\frac{3}{2}}\partial_{x}h\partial_{x}^{2}f+r_{1}(h)\partial_{x}f,\\
&C_{h}(D)g\mathrel{\mathop{\kern 0.0pt=}\limits^{\hbox{\footnotesize def}}}c_{1}\partial_{x}\big(h^{2}\partial_{x}^{2}(\sqrt{h}g)\big)+(c_{2}+c_{3})\partial_{x}^{2}\bigl(h^{\frac{3}{2}}\partial_{x}hg\bigr)-\partial_{x}(r_{2}(h)g),\end{aligned}

and

(4.2)

\displaystyle r_{1}(h)\mathrel{\mathop{\kern 0.0pt=}\limits^{\hbox{\footnotesize def}}}(b_{2}+\frac{3}{2}b_{3}+b_{4})\sqrt{h}(\partial_{x}h)^{2}+b_{2}h^{\frac{3}{2}}\partial_{x}^{2}h,

\displaystyle r_{2}(h)\mathrel{\mathop{\kern 0.0pt=}\limits^{\hbox{\footnotesize def}}}(c_{2}+\frac{3}{2}c_{3}-c_{4})\sqrt{h}(\partial_{x}h)^{2}+c_{2}h^{\frac{3}{2}}\partial_{x}^{2}h.

Notice that
b_{1}=c_{1}
and
b_{2}+b_{3}+c_{2}+c_{3}=0
. Then we obtain

(4.3)

\displaystyle B_{h}(D)f=b_{1}\sqrt{h}\partial_{x}^{2}(h^{2}\partial_{x}f)+(b_{2}+b_{3})h^{\frac{3}{2}}\partial_{x}h\partial_{x}^{2}f+r_{1}(h)\partial_{x}f,

\displaystyle C_{h}(D)g=b_{1}\partial_{x}\big(h^{2}\partial_{x}^{2}(\sqrt{h}g)\big)-(b_{2}+b_{3})\partial_{x}^{2}\bigl(h^{\frac{3}{2}}\partial_{x}hg\bigr)-\partial_{x}(r_{2}(h)g).

Moreover, there holds

(4.4)

\bigl(B_{h}(D)f\,|\,g\bigr)_{L^{2}}+\bigl(C_{h}(D)g\,|\,f\bigr)_{L^{2}}=\bigl(r(h)\partial_{x}f\,|\,g\bigr)_{L^{2}},\quad\forall\,f,g\in{\mathcal{S}}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits),

where

(4.5)

r(h)=r_{1}(h)+r_{2}(h)=(\frac{1}{2}b_{3}+\frac{1}{2}c_{3}+b_{4}-c_{4})\sqrt{h}(\partial_{x}h)^{2}+(b_{2}+c_{2})h^{\frac{3}{2}}\partial_{x}^{2}h.

Remark 4.1
.

(1). Formula (
4.4
) reveals that the principal parts of the operators
B_{h}(D)
and
C_{h}(D)
possess antisymmetric properties, leading to cancellation in (
4.4
). Despite this favorable cancellation, (
4.4
) still induces a loss of derivatives in the highest-order energy estimates (e.g.,estimates for

). We will study this issue more closely during the derivation of the highest-order energy estimates.

(2). The terms
\frac{\varepsilon}{2}B_{h}(D)\eta
and
\frac{\varepsilon}{2}C_{h}(D)\eta
in (

and

behave like

Before presenting the lemma, we state a useful interpolation inequality as follows:

(4.6)

\varepsilon^{\frac{j}{2}}\|\partial_{x}^{j}f\|_{L^{2}}\lesssim\|f\|_{L^{2}}^{1-\frac{j}{k}}\cdot\bigl(\varepsilon^{\frac{k}{2}}\|\partial_{x}^{k}f\|_{L^{2}}\bigr)^{\frac{j}{k}}\lesssim\|f\|_{X^{0}_{\varepsilon^{k}}},\quad 0<j<k,\,j,k\in{\mathop{\mathbb{Z}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits}_{\geq 1}.

Lemma 4.2
.

Let
b_{1}<0
,
h-1\in H^{5}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits)
satisfy

(4.7)

2\geq h(x)\geq h_{0}>0,\quad\|\partial_{x}h\|_{H^{4}}\leq 1,

for some

. There exists
\varepsilon_{1}\in(0,1)
such that for all
\varepsilon\in(0,\varepsilon_{1})
, there hold for any

,

(4.8)

\displaystyle(1).\quad\bigl((\sqrt{h}\partial_{x}+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}B_{h}(D))f\,|\,\partial_{x}f\bigr)_{L^{2}}\sim\|f_{x}\|_{X_{\varepsilon}^{0}}^{2},

(4.9)

\displaystyle(2).\quad\bigl\|(\sqrt{h}\partial_{x}+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}B_{h}(D))f\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\sim\|f_{x}\|_{X_{\varepsilon^{2}}^{0}}^{2},

(4.10)

\displaystyle(3).\quad\|f_{x}\|_{X^{0}_{\varepsilon^{2}}}^{2}-\|f\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\lesssim\bigl\|\bigl(\partial_{x}(\sqrt{h}\cdot)+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}C_{h}(D)\bigr)f\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\lesssim\|f_{x}\|_{X^{0}_{\varepsilon^{2}}}^{2}+\|f\|_{L^{2}}^{2},

(4.11)

\displaystyle(4).\quad\|f_{x}\|_{X_{\varepsilon}^{0}}^{2}-\|f\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\lesssim\bigl((\partial_{x}(\sqrt{h}\cdot)+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}C_{h}(D))f\,|\,\partial_{x}f\bigr)_{L^{2}}\|f_{x}\|_{X_{\varepsilon}^{0}}^{2}+\|f\|_{L^{2}}^{2}.

Proof.

We proceed to verify the equivalences one by one.

(1). Proof of (
4.8
).
Using expression of
B_{h}(D)
in (
4.3
), we have

\displaystyle\begin{aligned} \bigl((\sqrt{h}\partial_{x}&+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}B_{h}(D))f\,|\,\partial_{x}f\bigr)_{L^{2}}=\bigl(\sqrt{h}\partial_{x}f\,|\,\partial_{x}f\bigr)_{L^{2}}-\frac{b_{1}\varepsilon}{2}\bigl(\partial_{x}(h^{2}\partial_{x}f)\,|\,\partial_{x}(\sqrt{h}\partial_{x}f)\bigr)_{L^{2}}\\
&\quad+\frac{(b_{2}+b_{3})\varepsilon}{2}\bigl(h^{\frac{3}{2}}\partial_{x}h\partial_{x}^{2}f\,|\,\partial_{x}f\bigr)_{L^{2}}+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\bigl(r_{1}(h)\partial_{x}f\,|\,\partial_{x}f\bigr)_{L^{2}}\\
&=\bigl(\sqrt{h}\partial_{x}f\,|\,\partial_{x}f\bigr)_{L^{2}}-\frac{b_{1}\varepsilon}{2}\bigl(h^{\frac{5}{2}}\partial_{x}^{2}f\,|\,\partial_{x}^{2}f)\bigr)_{L^{2}}+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}I_{1},\end{aligned}

where

\displaystyle I_{1}\mathrel{\mathop{\kern 0.0pt=}\limits^{\hbox{\footnotesize def}}}(b_{2}+b_{3}-\frac{5}{2}b_{1})\bigl(h^{\frac{3}{2}}\partial_{x}h\partial_{x}^{2}f\,|\,\partial_{x}f\bigr)_{L^{2}}+\bigl([r_{1}(h)-b_{1}h^{\frac{1}{2}}(\partial_{x}h)^{2}]\partial_{x}f\,|\,\partial_{x}f\bigr)_{L^{2}}.

For
I_{1}
, we get

\displaystyle I_{1}=\bigl(\{-\frac{1}{2}(b_{2}+b_{3}-\frac{5}{2}b_{1})\partial_{x}(h^{\frac{3}{2}}\partial_{x}h)+r_{1}(h)-b_{1}h^{\frac{1}{2}}|\partial_{x}h|^{2}\}\partial_{x}f\,|\,\partial_{x}f\bigr)_{L^{2}},

which along with the expression of
r_{1}(h)
in (
4.2
) and the condition (
4.7
) yields to

\displaystyle\begin{aligned} |I_{1}|&\lesssim\bigl(\|h^{\frac{3}{2}}\partial_{x}^{2}h\|_{L^{\infty}}+\|h^{\frac{1}{2}}(\partial_{x}h)^{2}\|_{L^{\infty}}\bigr)\cdot\|f_{x}\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\\
&\lesssim\bigl(\|\partial_{x}^{2}h\|_{H^{1}}+\|\partial_{x}h\|_{H^{1}}^{2}\bigr)\cdot\|f_{x}\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\lesssim\|f_{x}\|_{L^{2}}^{2}.\end{aligned}

Using the condition
0<h_{0}\leq h\leq 2
and
b_{1}<0
lead to

\displaystyle\bigl(\sqrt{h}\partial_{x}f\,|\,\partial_{x}f\bigr)_{L^{2}}-\frac{b_{1}\varepsilon}{2}\bigl(h^{\frac{5}{2}}\partial_{x}^{2}f\,|\,\partial_{x}^{2}f)\bigr)_{L^{2}}\sim\|f_{x}\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\varepsilon\|\partial_{x}f_{x}\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\sim\|f_{x}\|_{X^{0}_{\varepsilon}}^{2},

Consequently, for sufficiently small
\varepsilon\in(0,1)
, there holds

\displaystyle\bigl((\sqrt{h}\partial_{x}+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}B_{h}(D))f\,|\,\partial_{x}f\bigr)_{L^{2}}\sim\|f_{x}\|_{X_{\varepsilon}^{0}}^{2}.

This is exactly (
4.8

(2). Proof of (
4.9
).
Thanks to the expression of

(4.12)

where

\displaystyle L_{h,1}(f)=\frac{\varepsilon}{2}(b_{2}+b_{3}+4b_{1})h^{\frac{3}{2}}\partial_{x}h\partial_{x}^{2}f+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\bigl(2b_{1}h^{\frac{1}{2}}\partial_{x}(h\partial_{x}h)+r_{1}(h)\bigr)\partial_{x}f.

Then we obtain

\displaystyle\|(\sqrt{h}\partial_{x}+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}B_{h}(D))f\|_{L^{2}}^{2}=\|\sqrt{h}\partial_{x}f+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}b_{1}h^{\frac{5}{2}}\partial_{x}^{3}f\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\|L_{h,1}(f)\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+2\bigl(\sqrt{h}\partial_{x}f+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}b_{1}h^{\frac{5}{2}}\partial_{x}^{3}f\,\big|\,L_{h,1}(f)\bigr),

\displaystyle 2|\bigl(\sqrt{h}\partial_{x}f+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}b_{1}h^{\frac{5}{2}}\partial_{x}^{3}f\,\big|\,L_{h,1}(f)\bigr)|\leq\frac{1}{2}\|\sqrt{h}\partial_{x}f+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}b_{1}h^{\frac{5}{2}}\partial_{x}^{3}f\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+2\|L_{h,1}(f)\|_{L^{2}}^{2},

which imply that

(4.13)

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\|\sqrt{h}\partial_{x}f+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}b_{1}h^{\frac{5}{2}}\partial_{x}^{3}f\|_{L^{2}}^{2}-\|L_{h,1}(f)\|_{L^{2}}^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\|(\sqrt{h}\partial_{x}+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}B_{h}(D))f\|_{L^{2}}^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{3}{2}\|\sqrt{h}\partial_{x}f+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}b_{1}h^{\frac{5}{2}}\partial_{x}^{3}f\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+3\|L_{h,1}(f)\|_{L^{2}}^{2}.

By virtue of

\displaystyle\begin{aligned} \|L_{h,1}(f)\|_{L^{2}}&\lesssim\varepsilon\|h^{\frac{3}{2}}\partial_{x}h\|_{L^{\infty}}\|\partial_{x}^{2}f\|_{L^{2}}+\varepsilon\bigl(\|h^{\frac{3}{2}}\partial_{x}^{2}h\|_{L^{\infty}}+\|h^{\frac{1}{2}}(\partial_{x}h)^{2}\|_{L^{\infty}}\bigr)\|\partial_{x}f\|_{L^{2}}\\

\displaystyle\|L_{h,1}(f)\|_{L^{2}}\lesssim\varepsilon\|f_{x}\|_{L^{2}}+\varepsilon^{\frac{1}{2}}\cdot\varepsilon^{\frac{1}{2}}\|\partial_{x}f_{x}\|_{L^{2}}.

Using interpolation inequality (
4.6

(4.14)

\displaystyle\bigl(h^{3}\partial_{x}f\,\big|\,\partial_{x}^{3}f\bigr)_{L^{2}}=-\bigl(h^{3}\partial_{x}^{2}f\,\big|\,\partial_{x}^{2}f\bigr)_{L^{2}}-3\bigl(h^{2}\partial_{x}h\partial_{x}f\,\big|\,\partial_{x}^{2}f\bigr)_{L^{2}},

which imply that

\displaystyle\begin{aligned} \|\sqrt{h}\partial_{x}f+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}b_{1}h^{\frac{5}{2}}\partial_{x}^{3}f\|_{L^{2}}^{2}&=\|\sqrt{h}\partial_{x}f\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\frac{\varepsilon^{2}}{4}|b_{1}|^{2}\|h^{\frac{5}{2}}\partial_{x}^{3}f\|_{L^{2}}^{2}-\varepsilon b_{1}\|h^{\frac{3}{2}}\partial_{x}^{2}f\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\\

Using the condition (
4.7

\displaystyle\varepsilon\bigl|\bigl(h^{2}\partial_{x}h\partial_{x}f\,\big|\,\partial_{x}^{2}f\bigr)_{L^{2}}\bigr|\lesssim\varepsilon\|\partial_{x}h\|_{H^{1}}\|\partial_{x}f\|_{L^{2}}\|\partial_{x}^{2}f\|_{L^{2}}\lesssim\varepsilon\|f_{x}\|_{L^{2}}\|\partial_{x}f_{x}\|_{L^{2}}\lesssim\varepsilon^{\frac{1}{2}}\|f_{x}\|_{X^{0}_{\varepsilon}}^{2}.

Since

\displaystyle\begin{aligned} &\|\sqrt{h}\partial_{x}f\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\frac{\varepsilon^{2}}{4}|b_{1}|^{2}\|h^{\frac{5}{2}}\partial_{x}^{3}f\|_{L^{2}}^{2}-\varepsilon b_{1}\|h^{\frac{3}{2}}\partial_{x}^{2}f\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\\
\sim&\|f_{x}\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\varepsilon^{2}\|\partial_{x}^{2}f_{x}\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\varepsilon\|\partial_{x}f_{x}\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\sim\|f_{x}\|_{X^{0}_{\varepsilon^{2}}}^{2},\end{aligned}

where we used (
4.6

\|\sqrt{h}\partial_{x}f+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}b_{1}h^{\frac{5}{2}}\partial_{x}^{3}f\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\sim\|f_{x}\|_{X^{0}_{\varepsilon^{2}}}^{2}.

Thanks to (
4.14

4.15

\displaystyle\|(\sqrt{h}\partial_{x}+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}B_{h}(D))f\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\sim\|f_{x}\|_{X^{0}_{\varepsilon^{2}}}^{2},

4.6
) implies

\displaystyle\begin{aligned} \|\eta_{t}|_{t=0}\|_{X^{1}_{\varepsilon}}&\lesssim\|\eta_{t}|_{t=0}\|_{H^{1}}+\varepsilon^{\frac{1}{2}}\|\partial_{x}^{2}\eta_{t}|_{t=0}\|_{L^{2}}\\
&\lesssim\|V_{0}\|_{X^{2}_{\varepsilon^{3}}}+\varepsilon\|\eta_{0}\|_{X^{2}_{\varepsilon}}\|V_{0}\|_{X^{2}_{\varepsilon}}^{2}\lesssim\|V_{0}\|_{X^{2}_{\varepsilon^{3}}}.\end{aligned}

Then we obtain

(4.76)

\|\eta_{t}|_{t=0}\|_{X^{1}_{\varepsilon}}^{2}\lesssim{\mathcal{E}}_{0}.

Step 3.3. Construction for
V_{tt}|_{t=0}\in X^{0}_{\varepsilon}
.
Using the first equation of (
4.1
) leads to

\displaystyle\begin{aligned} (1-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}{\mathcal{P}}_{h}^{1})V_{tt}|_{t=0}&=\Bigl(-\sqrt{h}\partial_{x}\eta_{t}-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}B_{h}(D)\eta_{t}-\frac{\varepsilon}{2\sqrt{h}}\partial_{x}(\eta\eta_{t})\\
&\quad-\frac{3\varepsilon}{2\sqrt{h}}\partial_{x}(VV_{t})+\frac{\varepsilon\partial_{x}h}{2h^{\frac{3}{2}}}VV_{t}\Bigr)\Big|_{t=0},\end{aligned}

which along with the condition (
4.42
) yields to

(4.77)

\displaystyle\bigl((1-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}{\mathcal{P}}_{h}^{1})V_{tt}\bigl|_{t=0}\,\big|\,V_{tt}\bigl|_{t=0}\bigr)_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle\lesssim\Bigl(\|\eta_{t}|_{t=0}\|_{H^{1}}+\varepsilon\|\eta_{0}\|_{H^{1}}\|\eta_{t}|_{t=0}\|_{H^{1}}+\varepsilon\|V_{0}\|_{H^{1}}\|V_{t}|_{t=0}\|_{H^{1}}\Bigr)

\displaystyle\times\|V_{tt}|_{t=0}\|_{L^{2}}+\varepsilon\bigl|\bigl(B_{h}(D)\eta_{t}\,\big|\,V_{tt}\bigr)_{L^{2}}\big|_{t=0}\bigr|.

Since
{\mathcal{P}}_{h}^{1}=a_{1}\partial_{x}(h^{2}\partial_{x}\quad)
,
a_{1}>0
and
0<h_{0}\leq h\leq 2
, there holds

\displaystyle\bigl((1-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}{\mathcal{P}}_{h}^{1})f\,\big|\,f\bigr)_{L^{2}}=\|f\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\frac{a_{1}\varepsilon}{2}\|h\partial_{x}f\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\sim\|f\|_{X^{0}_{\varepsilon}}^{2}.

Thanks to the ansatz
{\mathcal{E}}_{0}=1
, (
4.75
) and (
4.76
), we deduce from (
4.77
) that

(4.78)

\|V_{tt}|_{t=0}\|_{X^{0}_{\varepsilon}}^{2}\lesssim{\mathcal{E}}_{0}^{\frac{1}{2}}\cdot\|V_{tt}|_{t=0}\|_{L^{2}}+\varepsilon\bigl|\bigl(B_{h}(D)\eta_{t}\,\big|\,V_{tt}\bigr)_{L^{2}}\big|_{t=0}\bigr|.

By virtue of the expression of
B_{h}(D)
in (
4.3
), we have

\displaystyle\bigl(B_{h}(D)\eta_{t}\,\big|\,V_{tt}\bigr)_{L^{2}}=-b_{1}\bigl(\partial_{x}(h^{2}\partial_{x}\eta_{t})\,\big|\,\partial_{x}(\sqrt{h}V_{tt})\bigr)_{L^{2}}+\bigl((b_{2}+b_{3})h^{\frac{3}{2}}\partial_{x}h\partial_{x}^{2}\eta_{t}+r_{1}(h)\partial_{x}\eta_{t}\,\big|\,V_{tt}\bigr)_{L^{2}},

which along with the expression of
r_{1}(h)
and (
4.42
) yields to

\displaystyle\varepsilon\big|\bigl(B_{h}(D)\eta_{t}\,\big|\,V_{tt}\bigr)_{L^{2}}\big|_{t=0}\big|\lesssim\varepsilon\|\eta_{t}|_{t=0}\|_{H^{2}}\|V_{tt}|_{t=0}\|_{H^{1}}\lesssim\|\eta_{t}|_{t=0}\|_{X^{1}_{\varepsilon}}\|V_{tt}|_{t=0}\|_{X^{0}_{\varepsilon}}.

Consequently, using (
4.76
), we deduce from (
4.78
) that

\displaystyle\|V_{tt}|_{t=0}\|_{X^{0}_{\varepsilon}}^{2}\lesssim{\mathcal{E}}_{0}^{\frac{1}{2}}\cdot\|V_{tt}|_{t=0}\|_{X^{0}_{\varepsilon}},

which gives rise to

(4.79)

\|V_{tt}|_{t=0}\|_{X^{0}_{\varepsilon}}^{2}\lesssim{\mathcal{E}}_{0}.

Step 3.4. Construction for
\eta_{tt}|_{t=0}\in L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits)
.
Due to the second equation of (
4.1
), we have

\displaystyle\eta_{tt}|_{t=0}=\Bigl(-\partial_{x}(\sqrt{h}V_{t})-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}C_{h}(D)V_{t}-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\partial_{x}\bigl(\frac{\eta_{t}V+\eta V_{t}}{\sqrt{h}}\bigr)\Bigr)\Big|_{t=0},

which together with the expression of
C_{h}(D)
in (
4.3
), ansatz (
4.42
) and
{\mathcal{E}}_{0}=1
shows that

\displaystyle\begin{aligned} \|\eta_{tt}|_{t=0}\|_{L^{2}}&\lesssim\|V_{t}|_{t=0}\|_{H^{1}}+\varepsilon\|V_{t}|_{t=0}\|_{H^{3}}+\varepsilon\|\eta_{t}|_{t=0}\|_{H^{1}}\|V_{0}\|_{H^{1}}+\varepsilon\|\eta_{0}\|_{H^{1}}\|V_{t}|_{t=0}\|_{H^{1}}\\
&\lesssim\|V_{t}|_{t=0}\|_{X^{1}_{\varepsilon^{2}}}+\varepsilon\|\eta_{t}|_{t=0}\|_{H^{1}}.\end{aligned}

Using (
4.75
) and (
4.76
), we get

(4.80)

\|\eta_{tt}|_{t=0}\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\lesssim{\mathcal{E}}_{0}.

Step 3.5. Initial energy functional.
Combining (
4.75
), (
4.76
), (
4.79
) and (
4.80
), we obtain

(4.81)

{\mathcal{E}}(0)\lesssim{\mathcal{E}}_{0}.

Step 4. Final energy estimates and the closure of the continuity argument.
Going back to the
a priori
energy estimate (
4.40
), using (
4.66
) (i.e.,
E(t)+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\widetilde{E}_{2}(t)\sim{\mathcal{E}}(t)\sim E(t)
), there exists sufficiently small
\varepsilon_{0}\in(0,\min\{\varepsilon_{1},C_{0}^{-1}\}]
such that for any
\varepsilon\in(0,\varepsilon_{0})
,

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dt}\bigl(E(t)+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\widetilde{E}_{2}(t)\bigr)\lesssim\varepsilon\cdot E(t)+\varepsilon\bigl(E(t)\bigr)^{\frac{3}{2}},\quad\forall\,0<t\leq T_{0}/\varepsilon.

Using (
4.66
) again, we get

\displaystyle E(t)\lesssim E(0)+\varepsilon t\cdot\max_{0\leq\tau\leq t}E(\tau)+\varepsilon t\cdot\bigl(\max_{0\leq\tau\leq t}E(\tau)\big)^{\frac{3}{2}},\quad\forall\,0<t\leq T_{0}/\varepsilon.

Consequently, by virtue of (
4.45
), (
4.81
) and
{\mathcal{E}}(0)\sim E(0)
, there exist constants
C_{1},C_{2}>1
independent of
T_{0},C_{0},\varepsilon
, such that

(4.82)

{\mathcal{E}}(0)\leq C_{1}{\mathcal{E}}_{0},

and

(4.83)

\max_{0\leq\tau\leq t}{\mathcal{E}}(\tau)\leq 2C_{1}{\mathcal{E}}_{0}+C_{2}\varepsilon t\cdot\bigl(1+\bigl(\max_{0\leq\tau\leq t}{\mathcal{E}}(\tau)\big)^{\frac{1}{2}}\bigr)\cdot\max_{0\leq\tau\leq t}{\mathcal{E}}(\tau),\quad\forall\,0<t\leq T_{0}/\varepsilon.

Here (
4.82
) guarantees that (
4.1
)-(
1.10
) admits solutions on a short time interval
[0,T]
, during which the ansatz (
4.43
) still holds.

Taking
C_{0}=C_{1}
, using the ansatz (
4.43
), we derive from (
4.83
) that

\displaystyle\max_{0\leq\tau\leq t}{\mathcal{E}}(\tau)\leq 2C_{1}{\mathcal{E}}_{0}+C_{2}\varepsilon t\cdot\bigl(1+\sqrt{8C_{1}}\bigr)\cdot\max_{0\leq\tau\leq t}{\mathcal{E}}(\tau),\quad\forall\,0<t\leq T_{0}/\varepsilon.

Taking
T_{0}=\frac{1}{2C_{2}(1+\sqrt{8C_{1}})}
, we obtain

(4.84)

\max_{0\leq\tau\leq T_{0}/\varepsilon}{\mathcal{E}}(\tau)\leq 4C_{1}{\mathcal{E}}_{0}=4C_{0}{\mathcal{E}}_{0}.

This is the desired energy estimate (
1.16
).
It also improves the ansatz (
4.43
) so that the continuity argument is closed. This concludes the proof of Theorem
1.4
.
