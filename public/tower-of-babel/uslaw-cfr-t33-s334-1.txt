33 C.F.R. § 334.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:33:334.1
Creator: Corps Of Engineers, Department Of The Army, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-334.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 334 (Danger Zone And Restricted Area Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to:
(a) Prescribe procedures for establishing, amending and disestablishing danger zones and restricted areas;
(b) List the specific danger zones and restricted areas and their boundaries; and
(c) Prescribe specific requirements, access limitations and controlled activities within the danger zones and restricted areas.
