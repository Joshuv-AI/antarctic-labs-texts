5.4-O. The document contained typographical errors.

Commission, 100 F Street NE, Washington, DC 20549, (202) 551-5400.

22395, at 89 FR 57230 on page 57235, in the third column, under the 
heading ``Paper Comments'' correct the reference to ``SR-NYSEAMER-2025-
64'' to read ``SR-NYSEArca-2025-79;'' and on page 57236, in the first 
column, on the fourth line, correct the reference to ``SR-NYSEAMER-
2025-64'' to read ``SR-NYSEArca-2025-79.''
