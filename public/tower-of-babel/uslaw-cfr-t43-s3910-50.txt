43 C.F.R. § 3910.50 — Surface use.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3910.50
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3910.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3910 (Oil Shale Exploration Licenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Operations conducted under an exploration license must:
(a) Not unreasonably interfere with or endanger any other lawful activity on the same lands;
(b) Not damage any improvements on the lands; and
(c) Comply with all applicable Federal, state, and local laws and regulations.
