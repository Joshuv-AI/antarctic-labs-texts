42 U.S.C. § 1962d–6 — Feasibility studies; acceleration; advancement of costs by non-Federal sources
Citation: us:usc:42:1962d–6
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1962d–6
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 19B (Water Resources Planning); Subchapter IV (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may accelerate feasibility studies authorized by law when and to the extent that the costs of such studies shall have been advanced by non-Federal sources.
