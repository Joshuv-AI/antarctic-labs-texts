19 U.S.C. § 288 — Documented vessels
Citation: us:usc:19:288
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section288
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 3 (The Tariff And Related Provisions); Part 2 (Report, Entry, And Unlading Of Vessels And Vehicles). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Documented vessels with a registry endorsement, engaged in foreign trade on the Great Lakes or their tributary or connecting waters in trade with Canada, shall not thereby become liable to the payment of entry and clearance fees.
