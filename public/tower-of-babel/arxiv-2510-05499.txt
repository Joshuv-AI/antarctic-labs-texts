Generalized ( C , λ ) (C,\lambda) -structure for nonlinear diffeomorphisms of Banach spaces.
Sergey Tikhomirov

Abstract
We introduce a notion of generalized ( C , λ ) (C,\lambda) -structure for nonlinear diffeomorphisms in Banach spaces. It combines generalized hyperbolicity for linear operators in Banach spaces with ( C , λ ) (C,\lambda) -structure for diffeomorphisms on compact manifolds. The main differencies are that we allow the hyperbolic splitting to be discontinuous and in invariance condition assume only inclusions instead of equalities for both stable and unstable subspace, which allows us to cover Morse-Smale systems and generalized hyperbolicity. We suggest that the generalized ( C , λ ) (C,\lambda) -structure for infinite-dimensional dynamics plays a role similar to “Axiom A and strong transversality condition” for dynamics on compact manifolds. For diffeomorphisms in reflexive Banach space we showed that generalized ( C , λ ) (C,\lambda) -structure implies Lipschitz (periodic) shadowing and is robust under C 1 C^{1} -small perturbations. Assuming that generalized ( C , λ ) (C,\lambda) -structure is continuous for diffeomorphisms on an arbitrary Banach space we obtain a weak form of structural stability: the diffeomorphism is semiconjugated from both sides with any C 1 C^{1} -small perturbation.

As before, let

Definition 14

(Exponential dichotomy)
.

We say that the sequence
A

admits an exponential dichotomy
on
I
, where
I
is
\mathbb{Z}^{+}
,
\mathbb{Z}^{-}
or
\mathbb{Z}
if there exist
constants
C>0
,
\lambda\in(0,1)
and a family of bounded projections

P_{k},Q_{k}:B\to B
(
k\in\mathbb{Z}
), satisfying the following properties for
E^{s}_{k}:=P_{k}(\mathbb{B})
,
E^{u}_{k}:=Q_{k}(\mathbb{B})
.

(ED1)

Splitting.

P_{k}+Q_{k}=Id
,

\|P_{k}\|,\|Q_{k}\|\leq C
, hence

E^{s}_{k}\oplus E^{u}_{k}=\mathbb{B}
.

(ED2)

Invariance.
The splitting is carried exactly
onto itself:

A_{k}E^{s}_{k}=E^{s}_{k+1},\qquad A_{k}^{-1}E^{u}_{k}=E^{u}_{k-1},\qquad k\in I.

Equivalently
A_{k}P_{k}=P_{k+1}A_{k}
and
A_{k}Q_{k}=Q_{k+1}A_{k}
.

(ED3)

Exponential estimates.
For
n\geq 0
the following estimates hold

\displaystyle\|\Phi_{A}(k+n,k)v^{s}\|\leq C\lambda^{n}\|v^{s}\|,

\displaystyle v^{s}\in E^{s}_{k},\;[k,k+n]\subset I,

\displaystyle\|\Phi_{A}(k-n,k)v^{u}\|\leq C\lambda^{n}\|v^{u}\|,

\displaystyle v^{u}\in E^{u}_{k},\;[k-n,k]\subset I.

We abbreviate this by saying that
A
has the
(C,\lambda)
–exponential
dichotomy
.

Definition 15

(Transversality condition)
.

Assume that the restrictions of
A
to the future and to the past,

{\cal A}^{+}:=\{A_{k}\}_{k\geq 0},{\cal A}^{-}:=\{A_{k}\}_{k\leq 0},

each admit an exponential dichotomy with splittings

E^{s,+}_{k}\oplus E^{u,+}_{k}=\mathbb{B}
and

E^{s,-}_{k}\oplus E^{u,-}_{k}=\mathbb{B}
, respectively.
We say that
A
satisfies the

if

E^{s,+}_{0}\ +E^{u,-}_{0}=\mathbb{B},

i.e. the stable subspace determined by the future
is

to the unstable subspace determined by the past at time

\varphi_{t_{1}}\in t_{1},\quad\mathcal{F}_{t_{2}}\subset\mathcal{F}_{t_{1}},\quad t_{2}>t_{1}>0.

These inclusions are well-aligned with condition (CL2). We do not believe that non-reversibility of a semigroup would have a strong effect on the theorems about shadowing property.
Those observation makes us optimistic in ideas of generalizing notion of generalized
(C,\lambda)
-structure for this case. At the same time we do not know which effect would have that the generator of a semigroup generally is only densely-defined and is not bounded.

Periodic sequence of operators.
In the proof of robustness of generalized
(C,\lambda)
-structure (Theorem
5
) we used periodic shadowing property (compare with
[
126
,
66
]
). At the same time its usage was relatively mild. One can try to use another argument. Let
f
be a diffeomorphism satisfying generalized
(C,\lambda)
-structure and
g
be a diffeomorphism
C^{1}
-close to it. Consider
\{y_{k}\}
periodic trajectory of
g
. By the (not periodic) shadowing Theorem
2
it can be shadowed by not-necessarily periodic exact trajectory
\{x_{k}\}
of
f
. Denote

B_{k}=Dg(y_{k}),\quad A_{k}=Df(x_{k}),\quad k\in\mathbb{Z}.

Note that
B_{k}
is periodic, and
A_{k}
is not necessarily periodic. Similarly to section
6.3
the sequence
\{B_{k}\}
satisfies
(C,\lambda)
-structure, however we do not know if this
(C,\lambda)
-structure is periodic. We do not know if it possible that periodic sequence of operators admits
(C,\lambda)
-structure and does not admit periodic
(C,\lambda)
-structure. Below we formulate the simplest version of this question as a conjecture.

Conjecture 1
.

Let
T:\mathbb{B}\to\mathbb{B}
be a bounded linear isomorphism. Assume that
\mathcal{T}=\{T_{k}=T\}
constant sequence of operators satisfies generalized
(C,\lambda)
-structure (Definition
16
) then
T
is generalized hyperbolic (Definition
1
).

If this conjecture is not correct than
T
would satisfy Lipschitz shadowing property not being generalized hyperbolic. For the discussion if for linear operators Lipschitz shadowing implies generalized hyperbolicity see
[
15
,
28
]
.

Converse implication.
Note that for diffeomorphisms on finite-dimensional compact manifolds the Lipschitz shadowing property implies
(C,\lambda)
-structure
[
109
]
. It would be interesting to understand if a similar statement is correct for infinite-dimensional spaces. We expect that similarly to
[
109
,
129
]
one can prove that Lipschitz shadowing implies bounded solution property for differentials trajectories with uniform constant
L
. The main difficulty is to prove that bounded solution property implies generalized
(C,\lambda)
-structure.

We believe that it might be useful additionally assume that
\mathbb{B}
is a Hilbert space. Let
A_{k}:\mathbb{B}\to\mathbb{B}
be a sequence of linear isomorphisms satisfying bounded solution property with constant
L
. Consider sequence of subspaces
E_{k}\subset\mathbb{B}
and operators
B_{k}:E_{k}\to E_{k+1}
(similarly to Gram-Schmidt process) defined as

E_{k+1}=\left(A_{k}(E_{k}^{\perp})\right)^{\perp},\quad B_{k}=P_{E_{k+1}}A_{k}.

Then
\{B_{k}\}
satisfy bounded solution property with constant
L
as well. In the work of the author
[
129
, Lemma 4.2]
it was shown that for the case
dimE_{k}=1
bounded solution implies generalized
(C,\lambda)
-structure with
C=C(L)
,
\lambda=\lambda(L)
. In the proof for general case
[
129
, Theorem 2.3]
the constants
C
and
\lambda
change with increasing of the dimensions. However we believe that the argument can be improved and applied for infinite-dimensional Hilbert space.

In contrast to finite-dimensional case it is not clear if bounded solution property and strong bounded solution property are in fact equivalent. This question is related to Michael and Bartle–Graves continuous selections
[
9
,
89
,
90
]
, for discussion on possibility of Lipschitz selection see
[
22
,
88
]
.
