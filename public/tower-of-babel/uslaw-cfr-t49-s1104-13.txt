49 C.F.R. § 1104.13 — Replies and motions.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1104.13
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1104.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1104 (Filing With The Board-Copies-Verification-Service-Pleadings, Generally). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Time. A party may file a reply or motion addressed to any pleading within 20 days after the pleading is filed with the Board, unless otherwise provided.
(b) Number of copies. The original of a reply or motion should be accompanied by the same number of copies required to be filed with the pleading to which the reply or motion is addressed.
(c) Reply to a Reply. A reply to a reply is not permitted.
