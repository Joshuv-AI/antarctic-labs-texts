24 C.F.R. § 219.1 — Program operations.
Citation: us:cfr:24:219.1
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-219.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 219 (Flexible Subsidy Program For Troubled Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Effective May 1, 1996, the Flexible Subsidy Program for Troubled Projects will be governed and operate under the statutory provisions codified at 12 U.S.C. 1715z-1a, under the administrative policies and procedures contained in any applicable HUD Handbooks, and other administrative bulletins and notices as the Department may issue from time to time.
