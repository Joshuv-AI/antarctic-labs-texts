2 U.S.C. § 1963 — Protection of grounds
Citation: us:usc:2:1963
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1963
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 29 (Capitol Police); Subchapter II (Powers And Duties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It shall be the duty of the Capitol police on and after April 29, 1876, to prevent any portion of the Capitol Grounds and terraces from being used as playgrounds or otherwise, so far as may be necessary to protect the public property, turf and grass from destruction or injury.
