16 U.S.C. § 656 — Agents; administering oaths and taking testimony
Citation: us:usc:16:656
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section656
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 5 (Protection Of Fur Seals And Other Fur-Bearing Animals). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The agents are empowered to administer oaths in all cases relating to the service of the United States, and to take testimony in Alaska for the use of the Government in any matter concerning the public revenue.
