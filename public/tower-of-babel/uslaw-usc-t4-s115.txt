4 U.S.C. § 115 — Limitation on State authority to tax compensation paid to individuals performing services at Fort Campbell, Kentucky
Citation: us:usc:4:115
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title4-section115
Context: United States Code, Title 4 (Flag And Seal, Seat Of Government, And The States); Chapter 4 (The States). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Pay and compensation paid to an individual for personal services at Fort Campbell, Kentucky, shall be subject to taxation by the State or any political subdivision thereof of which such employee is a resident.
