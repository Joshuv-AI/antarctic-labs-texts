24 C.F.R. § 570.706 — Federal guarantee; subrogation.
Citation: us:cfr:24:570.706
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Community Planning And Development, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-570.706
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 570 (Community Development Block Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 108(f) of the Act provides for the incontestability of guarantees by HUD under subpart M of this part in the hands of a holder of such guaranteed obligations. If HUD pays a claim under a guarantee made under section 108 of the Act, HUD shall be fully subrogated for all the rights of the holder of the guaranteed debt obligation with respect to such obligation.
