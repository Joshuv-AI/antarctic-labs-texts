40 U.S.C. § 304 — Federal information centers
Citation: us:usc:40:304
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section304
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Chapter 3 (Organization Of General Services Administration); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator of General Services may establish within the General Services Administration a nationwide network of federal information centers for the purpose of providing the public with information about the programs and procedures of the Federal Government and for other appropriate and related purposes.
