49 C.F.R. § 1152.15 — Reservation of jurisdiction.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1152.15
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1152.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1152 (Abandonment And Discontinuance Of Rail Lines And Rail Transportation Under 49 U.S.C. 10903). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

49 U.S.C. 10903(c)(1) authorizes the Board, at its discretion, to provide for designation of lines as “potentially subject to abandonment” under standards which vary by region of the United States, by railroad, or by group of railroads. The Board expressly reserves the right to adopt such varying standards in the future.
