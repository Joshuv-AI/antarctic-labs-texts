34 C.F.R. § 686.10 — Application.
Citation: us:cfr:34:686.10
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-686.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 686 (Teacher Education Assistance For College And Higher Education (Teach) Grant Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To receive a grant under this part, a student must—
(a) Complete and submit the Free application for Federal student aid (FAFSA) in accordance with the instructions in the FAFSA;
(b) Complete and sign an agreement to serve or repay in accordance with § 686.12; and
(c) Provide any additional information requested by the Secretary and the institution.
