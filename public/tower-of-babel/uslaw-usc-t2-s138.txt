2 U.S.C. § 138 — Law library; hours kept open
Citation: us:usc:2:138
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section138
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 5 (Library Of Congress). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The law library shall be kept open every day so long as either House of Congress is in session.
