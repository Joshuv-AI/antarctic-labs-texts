8 C.F.R. § 251.6 — Exemptions for private vessels and aircraft.
Citation: us:cfr:8:251.6
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-251.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 251 (Arrival And Departure Manifests And Lists: Supporting Documents). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this part relating to the presentation of arrival and departure manifests do not apply to a private vessel or private aircraft not engaged directly or indirectly in the carrying of persons or cargo for hire.
