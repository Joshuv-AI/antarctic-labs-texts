2 C.F.R. § 2000.220 — What contracts and subcontracts, in addition to those listed in 2 CFR 180.220, are covered transactions?

Creator: United States Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-2000.220
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 2000 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The NRC nonprocurement suspension and debarment requirements apply only to first-tier procurement contracts under a covered nonprocurement transaction.
