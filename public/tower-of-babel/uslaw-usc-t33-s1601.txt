33 U.S.C. § 1601 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:33:1601
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section1601
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 30 (International Regulations For Preventing Collisions At Sea). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purposes of this chapter—
(1)
“vessel” means every description of watercraft, including nondisplacement craft and seaplanes, used or capable of being used as a means of transportation on water; and
(2)
“high seas” means all parts of the sea that are not included in the territorial sea or in the internal waters of any nation.
