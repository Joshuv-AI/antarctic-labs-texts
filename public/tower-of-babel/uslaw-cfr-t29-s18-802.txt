29 C.F.R. § 18.802 — Hearsay rule.
Citation: us:cfr:29:18.802
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-18.802
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 18 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure For Administrative Hearings Before The Office Of Administrative Law Judges). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Hearsay is not admissible except as provided by these rules, or by rules or regulations of the administrative agency prescribed pursuant to statutory authority, or pursuant to executive order, or by Act of Congress.
