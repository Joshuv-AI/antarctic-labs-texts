Safety Zone; Luminsea Miami Shootout and Races, Miami Marine 
Stadium Harbor, Miami FL

AGENCY: Coast Guard, Department of Homeland Security.

ACTION: Temporary final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Coast Guard is establishing a temporary safety zone for 
navigable waters in Miami Marine Stadium Harbor. The safety zone is 
needed to protect personnel, vessels, and the marine environment from 
potential hazards associated with powerboat racing. Entry of vessels or 
persons into this zone is prohibited unless specifically authorized by 
the Captain of the Port, Sector Miami, or their designated 
representative.

DATES: This rule is effective from October 22, 2026, through October 
25, 2026.

II. Background and Authority

    The Coast Guard received notification that an organization will be 
sponsoring a set of powerboat races in the Miami Marine Stadium Habor, 
Miami, FL on October 22, 2026, through October 25, 2026, from 10:00 
a.m. through 11:59 p.m. daily. The race will include approximately 60 
participants and 50 spectator craft. The COTP has determined that 
potential hazards associated with powerboat racing include the 
possibility of vessel collisions, capsizing, participant

[[Page 64117]]

ejections into the water and interactions with spectator craft or 
transiting vessels. The purpose of this rulemaking is to protect event 
participants, non-participants, and transiting vessels before, during, 
and after the scheduled event. Therefore, the COTP is issuing this rule 
under the authority in 46 U.S.C. 70034, which is needed to protect 
personnel, vessels, and the marine environment.
    Because of these potential hazards, the Coast Guard is issuing this 
rule without prior notice and comment. As is authorized by 5 U.S.C. 
553(b)(B), the Coast Guard finds that good cause exists for not 
publishing a notice of proposed rulemaking (NPRM) with respect to this 
rule because it is impracticable. The Coast Guard was notified of this 
event too late in the planning process and we establish this safety 
zone by October 22, 2026, to protect personnel, vessels, and the marine 
environment. Therefore, we do not have enough time to solicit and 
respond to comments.
    For the same reason, the Coast Guard finds that under 5 U.S.C. 
553(d)(3), good cause exists for making this rule effective less than 
30 days after publication in the Federal Register.

III. Discussion of the Rule

    This rule establishes a safety zone from October 22, 2026 through 
October 25, 2026, each day from 10:00 a.m. until 11:59 p.m. The safety 
zone will cover all navigable waters in the Miami Marine Stadium Harbor 
within the following points: 25.751362, -80.173021, 25.748879, -
80.176015, thence to 25.739098, -80.165124, 25.741536, -80.162165, and 
along the shoreline back to the beginning point. Vessels and persons 
will not be allowed to enter the zone during this time, unless 
authorized by the Captain of the Port.

Sec.  165.T07-1194   Safety Zone; Luminsea Miami Shootout and Races, 
Miami Marine Stadium Harbor, FL.

    (a) Location. The following area is a safety zone: All waters of 
Miami Marine Stadium Harbor, from surface to bottom, encompassed by a 
line connecting the following points beginning at 25.751362, -
80.173021, 25.748879, -80.176015, thence to 25.739098, -80.165124, 
25.741536, -80.162165, and along the shoreline back to the beginning 
point. These coordinates are based on the World Geodetic System (WGS 
84).
    (b) Definitions. As used in this section, designated representative 
means a Coast Guard Patrol Commander, including a Coast Guard coxswain, 
petty officer, or other officer operating a Coast Guard vessel and a 
Federal, State, and local officer designated by or assisting the 
Captain of the Port Miami (COTP) in the enforcement of the safety zone.
    (c) Regulations. (1) Under the general safety zone regulations in 
subpart C of this part, you may not enter the safety zone described in 
paragraph (a) of this section unless authorized by the COTP or the 
COTP's designated representative.
    (2) To seek permission to enter, contact the COTP or the COTP's 
representative on VHF-FM channel 16 or by telephone at (833) 642-7271. 
Those in the safety zone must comply with all lawful orders or 
directions

[[Page 64118]]

given to them by the COTP or the COTP's designated representative.
    (d) Enforcement period. This section will be enforced from 10:00 
a.m. to 11:59 p.m. each day from October 22, 2026, through October 25, 
2026.
