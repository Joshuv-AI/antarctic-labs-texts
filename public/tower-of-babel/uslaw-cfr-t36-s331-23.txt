36 C.F.R. § 331.23 — Control of animals.
Citation: us:cfr:36:331.23
Creator: Corps Of Engineers, Department Of The Army
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-331.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 331 (Regulations Governing The Protection, Use And Management Of The Falls Of The Ohio National Wildlife Conservation Area, Kentucky And Indiana). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) No person shall bring or allow horses, cattle, or other livestock in the WCA.
(b) No person shall bring dogs, cats, or other pets into the WCA unless penned, caged, or on a leash under 6 feet in length, or otherwise under physical restraint at all times. Unclaimed or unattended animals are subject to immediate impoundment and removal in accordance with State and local laws.
