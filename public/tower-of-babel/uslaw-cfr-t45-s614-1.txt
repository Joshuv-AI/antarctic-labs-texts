45 C.F.R. § 614.1 — General rule.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 614 (Government In The Sunshine Act Regulations Of The National Science Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise provided in these regulations, every portion of every meeting of the National Science Board will be open to public observation.
