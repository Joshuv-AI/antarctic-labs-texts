9 C.F.R. § 431.12 — Recall procedure.
Citation: us:cfr:9:431.12
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-431.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 431 (Thermally Processed, Commercially Sterile Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Establishments must prepare and maintain a current procedure for the recall of all canned product covered by this subpart. Upon request, the recall procedure must be made available to Program employees for review.
