12 C.F.R. § 263.451 — Definitions.

Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-263.451
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 263 (Rules Of Practice For Hearings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in this subpart:
(a) Formal investigative proceeding means an investigation conducted pursuant to an order of investigation as provided in § 263.452(a).
(b) Designated representative means the person or persons empowered by the Board or by the General Counsel or his or her designees in accordance with 12 CFR 265.6 to conduct a formal investigative proceeding.
