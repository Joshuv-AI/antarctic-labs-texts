20 C.F.R. § 688.340 — What timeframes apply to participation?
Citation: us:cfr:20:688.340
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-688.340
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 688 (Provisions Governing The Youthbuild Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An eligible individual selected for participation in the program must be offered full-time participation in the program for not less than 6 months and not more than 24 months.
