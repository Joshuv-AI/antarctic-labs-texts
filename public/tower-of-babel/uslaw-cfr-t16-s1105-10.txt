16 C.F.R. § 1105.10 — Reasonable costs.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1105.10
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1105.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1105 (Contributions To Costs Of Participants In Development Of Consumer Product Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission shall compensate participants only for costs that it determines are reasonable. As guidelines in these determinations, the Commission shall consider market rates and rates normally paid by the Commission for comparable goods and services, as appropriate.
