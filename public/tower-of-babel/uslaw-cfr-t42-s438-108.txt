42 C.F.R. § 438.108 — Cost sharing.
Citation: us:cfr:42:438.108
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-438.108
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 438 (Managed Care). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contract must provide that any cost sharing imposed on Medicaid enrollees is in accordance with §§ 447.50 through 447.82 of this chapter.
