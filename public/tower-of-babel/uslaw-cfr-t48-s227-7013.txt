48 C.F.R. § 227.7013 — 227.7013 Recordation.
Citation: us:cfr:48:227.7013
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-227.7013
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 227 (Patents, Data, And Copyrights). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Executive Order No. 9424 of 18 February 1944 requires all executive Departments and agencies of the Government to forward through appropriate channels to the Commissioner of Patents and Trademarks, for recording, all Government interests in patents or applications for patents.
