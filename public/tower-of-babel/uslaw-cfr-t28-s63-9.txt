28 C.F.R. § 63.9 — Exception.
Citation: us:cfr:28:63.9
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-63.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 63 (Floodplain Management And Wetland Protection Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in these guidelines shall apply to assistance provided for emergency work essential to save lives and protect property and public health and safety performed pursuant to sections 305 and 306 of the Disaster Relief Act of 1974 (88 Stat. 148, 42 U.S.C. 5145 and 5146).
