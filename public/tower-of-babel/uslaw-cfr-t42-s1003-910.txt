42 C.F.R. § 1003.910 — Amount of penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:42:1003.910
Creator: Office Of Inspector General-Health Care, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-1003.910
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 1003 (Civil Money Penalties, Assessments And Exclusions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For each individual violation of section 351A(b) or (c) of the Public Health Service Act or 42 CFR part 73, the OIG may impose a penalty of not more than $250,000 in the case of an individual, and not more than $500,000 in the case of any other person.
