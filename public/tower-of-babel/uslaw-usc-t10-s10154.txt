10 U.S.C. § 10154 — Retired Reserve
Citation: us:usc:10:10154
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section10154
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And Administration); Chapter 1005 (Elements Of Reserve Components). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Retired Reserve consists of the following Reserves:
(1)
Reserves who are or have been retired under section 7311, 8323, or 9311 of this title or under section 291 1 of title 14.
(2)
Reserves who have been transferred to the Retired Reserve, retain their status as Reserves, and are otherwise qualified.
