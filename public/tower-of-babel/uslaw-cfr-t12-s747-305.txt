12 C.F.R. § 747.305 — Effectiveness of suspension or removal until completion of hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:12:747.305
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-747.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 747 (Administrative Actions, Adjudicative Hearings, Rules Of Practice And Procedure, And Investigations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any notice of suspension or prohibition issued under § 747.303 and any order of removal or prohibition issued under § 747.304 will be effective upon service on the concerned party and will remain effective and outstanding until the completion of any hearing or appeal authorized under section 206(i) of the Act (12 U.S.C. 1786(i)) and this subpart, unless such notice of suspension or order of removal is terminated by the NCUA Board.
