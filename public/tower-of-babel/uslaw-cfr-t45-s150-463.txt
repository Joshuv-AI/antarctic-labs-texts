45 C.F.R. § 150.463 — Final order not subject to review.
Citation: us:cfr:45:150.463
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-150.463
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 150 (Cms Enforcement In Group And Individual Insurance Markets). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In an action brought under § 150.461, the validity and appropriateness of the final order described in § 150.459 is not subject to review.
