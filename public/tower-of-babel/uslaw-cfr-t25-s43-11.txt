25 C.F.R. § 43.11 — Informal proceedings.
Citation: us:cfr:25:43.11
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-43.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 43 (Maintenance And Control Of Student Records In Bureau Schools). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Educational institutions may attempt to resolve differences with the parent of a student or the eligible student regarding the content of the student's records through informal meetings and discussions with the parent or eligible student.
