46 C.F.R. § 169.664 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:46:169.664
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-169.664
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 169 (Sailing School Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirements in this subpart apply to electrical installations operating at potentials of less than 50 volts on vessels of less than 100 gross tons.
