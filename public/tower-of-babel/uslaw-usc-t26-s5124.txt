26 U.S.C. § 5124 — Registration by dealers
Citation: us:usc:26:5124
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section5124
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 51 (Distilled Spirits, Wines, And Beer); Subchapter A (Gallonage And Occupational Taxes); Part II (Miscellaneous Provisions); Subpart C (Recordkeeping And Registration By Dealers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Every dealer who is subject to the recordkeeping requirements under section 5121 or 5122 shall register with the Secretary such dealer’s name or style, place of residence, trade or business, and the place where such trade or business is to be carried on. In the case of a firm or company, the names of the several persons constituting the same, and the places of residence, shall be so registered.
