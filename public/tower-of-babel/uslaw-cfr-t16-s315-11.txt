16 C.F.R. § 315.11 — Effect on state and local laws.
Citation: us:cfr:16:315.11
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-315.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 315 (Contact Lens Rule). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) State and local laws and regulations that establish a prescription expiration date of less than one year or that restrict prescription release or require active verification are preempted.
(b) Any other State or local laws or regulations that are inconsistent with the Act or this part are preempted to the extent of the inconsistency.
