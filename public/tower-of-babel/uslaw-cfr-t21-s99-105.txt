21 C.F.R. § 99.105 — Recipients of information.
Citation: us:cfr:21:99.105
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-99.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 99 (Dissemination Of Information On Unapproved/New Uses For Marketed Drugs, Biologics, And Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A manufacturer disseminating information on a new use under this part may only disseminate that information to a health care practitioner, a pharmacy benefit manager, a health insurance issuer, a group health plan, or a Federal or State Government agency.
