28 C.F.R. § 2.42 — Probation officer's reports to Commission.
Citation: us:cfr:28:2.42
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-2.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 2 (Parole, Release, Supervision And Recommitment Of Prisoners, Youth Offenders, And Juvenile Delinquents). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A supervision report shall be submitted by the responsible probation officer to the Commission for each parolee after the completion of 24 months of continuous supervision and annually thereafter. The probation officer shall submit such additional reports as the Commission may direct.
