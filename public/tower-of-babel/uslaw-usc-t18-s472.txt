18 U.S.C. § 472 — Uttering counterfeit obligations or securities
Citation: us:usc:18:472
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section472
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 25 (Counterfeiting And Forgery). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever, with intent to defraud, passes, utters, publishes, or sells, or attempts to pass, utter, publish, or sell, or with like intent brings into the United States or keeps in possession or conceals any falsely made, forged, counterfeited, or altered obligation or other security of the United States, shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than 20 years, or both.
