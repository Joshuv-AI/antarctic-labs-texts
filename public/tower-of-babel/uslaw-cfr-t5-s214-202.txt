5 C.F.R. § 214.202 — Authority to make determinations.
Citation: us:cfr:5:214.202
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-214.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 214 (Senior Executive Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each agency is responsible for determining, in accordance with Office of Personnel Management guidelines, which of its positions should be included in the Senior Executive Service.
(b) Agency determinations may be reviewed by the Office of Personnel Management to ensure adherence with law and regulation.
