2 U.S.C. § 1721 — Establishment
Citation: us:usc:2:1721
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1721
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 27 (Sound Recording Preservation By The Library Of Congress); Subchapter III (National Recording Preservation Board). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Librarian shall establish in the Library of Congress a National Recording Preservation Board whose members shall be selected in accordance with the procedures described in section 1722 of this title.
