25 C.F.R. § 18.203 — Which probate code amendments require approval?
Citation: us:cfr:25:18.203
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-18.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 18 (Tribal Probate Codes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Only those tribal probate code amendments regarding the descent and distribution of trust and restricted lands require the Department's approval.
