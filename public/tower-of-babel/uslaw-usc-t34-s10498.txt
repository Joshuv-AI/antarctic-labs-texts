34 U.S.C. § 10498 — Definitions

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section10498
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 101 (Justice System Improvement); Subchapter XXII (Support For Law Enforcement Officers And Families). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of this subchapter—
(1)
the term “family-friendly policy” means a policy to promote or improve the morale and well being of law enforcement personnel and their families; and
(2)
the term “law enforcement personnel” means individuals employed by Federal, State, and local law enforcement agencies.
