28 U.S.C. § 1609 — Immunity from attachment and execution of property of a foreign state
Citation: us:usc:28:1609
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1609
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part IV (Jurisdiction And Venue); Chapter 97 (Jurisdictional Immunities Of Foreign States). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Subject to existing international agreements to which the United States is a party at the time of enactment of this Act the property in the United States of a foreign state shall be immune from attachment 1 arrest 1 and execution except as provided in sections 1610 and 1611 of this chapter.
