36 C.F.R. § 52.40 — Does this part affect concession contracts under part 51 of this chapter?
Citation: us:cfr:36:52.40
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-52.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 52 (Visitor Experience Improvements Authority Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No, nothing in this part modifies the terms or conditions of any existing concession contract or the ability of the Director to enter into concession contracts under part 51 of this chapter. The 1998 Act (as that term is defined in part 51 of this chapter) remains in effect.
