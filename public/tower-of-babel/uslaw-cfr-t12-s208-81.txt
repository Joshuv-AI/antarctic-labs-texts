12 C.F.R. § 208.81 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:12:208.81
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-208.81
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 208 (Membership Of State Banking Institutions In The Federal Reserve System (Regulation H)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart establishes consumer protections in connection with retail sales practices, solicitations, advertising, or offers of any insurance product or annuity to a consumer by:
(a) Any state member bank; or
(b) Any other person that is engaged in such activities at an office of the bank or on behalf of the bank.
