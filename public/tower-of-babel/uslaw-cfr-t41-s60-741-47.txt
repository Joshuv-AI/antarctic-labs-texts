41 C.F.R. § 60-741.47 — -741.47 Sheltered workshops.
Citation: us:cfr:41:60-741.47
Creator: Chapter 60—Office Of Federal Contract Compliance Programs, Equal Employment Opportunity, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 60-741 (741—Affirmative Action And Nondiscrimination Obligations Of Federal Contractors And Subcontractors Regarding Individuals With Disabilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contracts with sheltered workshops do not constitute affirmative action in lieu of employment and advancement of qualified individuals with disabilities in the contractor's own work force. Contracts with sheltered workshops may be included within an affirmative action program if the sheltered workshop trains employees for the contractor and the contractor is obligated to hire trainees at full compensation when such trainees become “qualified individuals with disabilities.”
