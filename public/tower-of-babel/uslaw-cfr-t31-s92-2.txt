31 C.F.R. § 92.2 — Sale of “list” medals.
Citation: us:cfr:31:92.2
Creator: Monetary Offices, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-92.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 92 (United States Mint Operations And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Medals on the regular Mint list, when available, are sold to the public at a charge sufficient to cover their cost, and to include mailing cost when mailed. Copies of the list of medals available for sale and their selling prices may be obtained from the Director of the Mint, Washington, DC.
