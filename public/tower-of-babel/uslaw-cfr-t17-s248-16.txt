17 C.F.R. § 248.16 — Protection of Fair Credit Reporting Act.
Citation: us:cfr:17:248.16
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-248.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 248 (Regulations S-P, S-Am, And S-Id). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this subpart shall be construed to modify, limit, or supersede the operation of the Fair Credit Reporting Act (15 U.S.C. 1681 et seq.), and no inference shall be drawn on the basis of the provisions of this subpart regarding whether information is transaction or experience information under section 603 of that Act.
