9 C.F.R. § 112.10 — Special packaging and labeling.
Citation: us:cfr:9:112.10
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-112.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 112 (Packaging And Labeling). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A biological product, which requires special packaging and/or labeling not provided for in this part, shall be packaged and/or labeled in accordance with requirements written into the approved outline for such product.
