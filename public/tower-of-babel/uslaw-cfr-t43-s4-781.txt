43 C.F.R. § 4.781 — Which presiding officer decisions or orders may be appealed and who may appeal them?
Citation: us:cfr:43:4.781
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4.781
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4 (Department Of The Interior Hearings And Appeals Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any of the following decisions or orders of the presiding officer may be appealed to the Board by the Project Director and by any party in interest who is adversely affected by that decision or order:
(a) A final decision;
(b) An order upon reconsideration issued under § 4.763(a);
(c) An order upon reopening issued under § 4.774(a) or § 4.775(c); or
(d) An order upon remand issued under § 4.790(b).
