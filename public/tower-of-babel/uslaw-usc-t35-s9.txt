35 U.S.C. § 9 — Certified copies of records
Citation: us:usc:35:9
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title35-section9
Context: United States Code, Title 35 (Patents); Part I (United States Patent And Trademark Office); Chapter 1 (Establishment, Officers And Employees, Functions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director may furnish certified copies of specifications and drawings of patents issued by the Patent and Trademark Office, and of other records available either to the public or to the person applying therefor.
