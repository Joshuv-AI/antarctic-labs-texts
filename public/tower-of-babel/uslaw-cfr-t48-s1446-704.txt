48 C.F.R. § 1446.704 — 1446.704 Authority for use of warranties.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1446 (Quality Assurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The CCO is authorized to make the written determination to use a warranty in an acquisition.
