21 U.S.C. § 469 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:21:469
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section469
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 10 (Poultry And Poultry Products Inspection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is authorized to be appropriated such sums as are necessary to carry out the provisions of this chapter.
