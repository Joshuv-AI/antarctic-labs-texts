31 U.S.C. § 305 — Federal Financing Bank
Citation: us:usc:31:305
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section305
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 3 (Department Of The Treasury); Subchapter I (Organization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Federal Financing Bank, established under section 4 of the Federal Financing Bank Act of 1973 (12 U.S.C. 2283), is subject to the direction and supervision of the Secretary of the Treasury.
