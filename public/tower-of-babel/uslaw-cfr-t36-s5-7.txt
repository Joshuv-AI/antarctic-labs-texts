36 C.F.R. § 5.7 — Construction of buildings or other facilities.
Citation: us:cfr:36:5.7
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-5.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 5 (Commercial And Private Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Constructing or attempting to construct a building, or other structure, boat dock, road, trail, path, or other way, telephone line, telegraph line, power line, or any other private or public utility, upon across, over, through, or under any park areas, except in accordance with the provisions of a valid permit, contract, or other written agreement with the United States, is prohibited.
