39 U.S.C. § 5604 — Fines on ocean carriers
Citation: us:usc:39:5604
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title39-section5604
Context: United States Code, Title 39 (Postal Service); Part V (Transportation Of Mail); Chapter 56 (Transportation Of Mail By Vessel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Postal Service may impose or remit fines on carriers transporting mail by vessel on routes extending beyond the borders of the United States for—
(1)
unreasonable or unnecessary delay to the mails; and
(2)
other delinquencies in the transportation of mail.
