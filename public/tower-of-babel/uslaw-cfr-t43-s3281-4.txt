43 C.F.R. § 3281.4 — What are the size and shape requirements for a unit area?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3281.4
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3281.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3280 (Geothermal Resources Unit Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

There are no specific size or shape requirements for a unit area, except that it must meet the requirements of § 3281.3. The size of the unit area may affect the minimum initial unit obligation requirements (see § 3281.15(b)).
