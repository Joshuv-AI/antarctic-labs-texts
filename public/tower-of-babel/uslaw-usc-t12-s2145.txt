12 U.S.C. § 2145 — Exchange of ownership interests
Citation: us:usc:12:2145
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section2145
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 23 (Farm Credit System); Subchapter III (Banks For Cooperatives); Part B (National Banks For Cooperatives). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On the establishment of the consolidated bank, ownership interests of the stockholders and subscribers to the guaranty funds of the constituent district banks for cooperatives (including stock, participation certificates, and allocated equities) shall be exchanged for like ownership interests in the consolidated bank on a book value basis.
