28 U.S.C. § 608 — Seal
Citation: us:usc:28:608
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section608
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part III (Court Officers And Employees); Chapter 41 (Administrative Office Of United States Courts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director shall use a seal approved by the Supreme Court. Judicial notice shall be taken of such seal.
