21 C.F.R. § 117.93 — Warehousing and distribution.
Citation: us:cfr:21:117.93
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-117.93
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 117 (Current Good Manufacturing Practice, Hazard Analysis, And Risk-Based Preventive Controls For Human Food). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Storage and transportation of food must be under conditions that will protect against allergen cross-contact and against biological, chemical (including radiological), and physical contamination of food, as well as against deterioration of the food and the container.
