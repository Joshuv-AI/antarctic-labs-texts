22 C.F.R. § 507.3 — Requirement for open meetings.
Citation: us:cfr:22:507.3
Creator: United States Agency For Global Media
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-507.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 507 (Rules For Implementing Open Meetings Under The Sunshine Act For The Broadcasting Board Of Governors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Members shall not jointly conduct or dispose of agency business other than in accordance with this part. Except as provided in § 507.4 every portion of every meeting of the agency shall be open to public observation.
