33 C.F.R. § 70.05-20 — -20 Report required.
Citation: us:cfr:33:70.05-20
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-70.05-20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 70 (Interference With Or Damage To Aids To Navigation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever any vessel collides with an aid to navigation established and maintained by the United States or any private aid to navigation established or maintained in accordance with part 64, 66, 67, or 118 of this chapter, or is connected with any such collision, it shall be the duty of the person in charge of such vessel to report the accident to the nearest Officer in Charge, Marine Inspection, in accordance with 46 CFR part 4.
