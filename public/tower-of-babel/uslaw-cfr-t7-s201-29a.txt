7 C.F.R. § 201.29a — a Germination of vegetable seed in containers of more than 1 pound.
Citation: us:cfr:7:201.29a
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-201.29a
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 201 (Federal Seed Act Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each variety of vegetable seeds in containers of more than 1 pound shall be labeled to show the percentage of germination and the percentage of hard seed (if any).
