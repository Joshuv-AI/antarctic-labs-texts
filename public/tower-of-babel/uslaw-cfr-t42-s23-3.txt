42 C.F.R. § 23.3 — What entities are eligible to apply for assignment?
Citation: us:cfr:42:23.3
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-23.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 23 (National Health Service Corps). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any public or nonprofit private entity which is located in a health manpower shortage area, or has a demonstrated interest in the shortage area, may apply for the assignment of National Health Service Corps personnel.
