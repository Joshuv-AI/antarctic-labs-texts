36 U.S.C. § 120110 — Liability for acts of officers and agents
Citation: us:usc:36:120110
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section120110
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1201 (Korean War Veterans Association, Incorporated). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation is liable for any act of any officer or agent of the corporation acting within the scope of the authority of the corporation.
