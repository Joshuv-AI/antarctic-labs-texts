9 C.F.R. § 548.9 — Accreditation of non-Federal chemistry laboratories.
Citation: us:cfr:9:548.9
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-548.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 548 (Preparation Of Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A non-Federal analytical laboratory that has met the requirements for accreditation specified in 9 CFR part 439 and hence, at an establishment's discretion, may be used in lieu of an FSIS laboratory for analyzing official regulatory samples. Payment for the analysis of regulatory samples is to be made by the establishment using the accredited laboratory.
