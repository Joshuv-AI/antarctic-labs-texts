43 C.F.R. § 3800.6 — Am I required to pay any fees to use the surface of public lands for mining purposes?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3800.6
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3800.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3800 (Mining Claims Under The General Mining Laws). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must pay all processing fees, location fees, and maintenance fees specified in 43 CFR parts 3800 and 3830. Other than the processing, location and maintenance fees, you are not required to pay any other fees to the BLM to use the surface of public lands for mining purposes.
