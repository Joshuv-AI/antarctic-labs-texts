15 U.S.C. § 266 — Applicability of administrative procedure provisions
Citation: us:usc:15:266
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section266
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 6 (Weights And Measures And Standard Time); Subchapter IX (Standard Time). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Subchapter II of chapter 5, and chapter 7, of title 5 shall apply to all proceedings under this Act, sections 261 to 264 of this title, and section 265 of this title.
