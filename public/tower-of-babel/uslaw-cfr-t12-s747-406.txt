12 C.F.R. § 747.406 — Cancellation of charter.
Citation: us:cfr:12:747.406
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-747.406
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 747 (Administrative Actions, Adjudicative Hearings, Rules Of Practice And Procedure, And Investigations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon the completion of the liquidation and certification by the agent for the liquidating agent that the distribution of the assets of the Federal credit union has been completed, the NCUA Board shall cancel the charter of the Federal credit union concerned.
