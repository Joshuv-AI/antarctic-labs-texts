38 U.S.C. § 3023 — Payment of supplemental educational assistance under this subchapter
Citation: us:usc:38:3023
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section3023
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part III (Readjustment And Related Benefits); Chapter 30 (All-Volunteer Force Educational Assistance Program); Subchapter III (Supplemental Educational Assistance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall increase the monthly basic educational assistance allowance paid to an individual who is entitled to supplemental educational assistance under this subchapter by the monthly amount of the supplemental educational assistance to which the individual is entitled.
