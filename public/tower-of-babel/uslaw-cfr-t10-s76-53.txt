10 C.F.R. § 76.53 — Consultation with Environmental Protection Agency.
Citation: us:cfr:10:76.53
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-76.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 76 (Certification Of Gaseous Diffusion Plants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In reviewing an application for a certificate, including the provisions of any compliance plan, the Director shall consult with the Environmental Protection Agency and solicit the Environmental Protection Agency's written comments on the application.
