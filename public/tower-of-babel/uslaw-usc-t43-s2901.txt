43 U.S.C. § 2901 — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:43:2901
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section2901
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 47 (Reclamation Title Transfer). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of this chapter is to facilitate the transfer of title to Reclamation project facilities to qualifying entities on the completion of repayment of capital costs.
