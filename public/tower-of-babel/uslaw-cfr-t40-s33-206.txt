40 C.F.R. § 33.206 — Is there a list of certified MBEs and WBEs?
Citation: us:cfr:40:33.206
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-33.206
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 33 (Participation By Disadvantaged Business Enterprises In United States Environmental Protection Agency Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

EPA OSDBU will maintain a list of certified MBEs and WBEs on EPA OSDBU's Home Page on the Internet. Any interested person may also obtain a copy of the list from EPA OSDBU.
