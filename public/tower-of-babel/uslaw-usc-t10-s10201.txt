10 U.S.C. § 10201 — Assistant Secretary of Defense for Manpower and Reserve Affairs
Citation: us:usc:10:10201
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section10201
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And Administration); Chapter 1007 (Administration Of Reserve Components). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As provided in section 138(b)(2) 1 of this title, the official in the Department of Defense with responsibility for overall supervision of reserve affairs of the Department of Defense is the Assistant Secretary of Defense for Manpower and Reserve Affairs.
