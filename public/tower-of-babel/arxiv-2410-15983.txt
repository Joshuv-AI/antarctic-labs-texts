A critical drift-diffusion equation: connections to the diffusion on SL ​ ( 2 ) \textbf{SL}(2)
Peter S. Morfe, Felix Otto, Christian Wagner

Abstract
. In this note, we connect two seemingly unrelated objects: On the one hand is a two-dimensional drift-diffusion process X X with divergence-free and time-independent drift b b . The drift is given by a stationary Gaussian ensemble, and we focus on the critical case where a small-scale cut-off is necessary for well-posedness and the large-scale cancellations lead to a borderline super-diffusive behavior. On the other hand is the natural diffusion F F on the Lie group SL ​ ( 2 ) \textbf{SL}(2) of matrices of determinant one. As a consequence of this connection, the strongly non-Gaussian character of F F transmits to how X X depends on its starting point.
