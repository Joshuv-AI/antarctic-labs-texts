46 C.F.R. § 197.452 — Oxygen cleaning.
Citation: us:cfr:46:197.452
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-197.452
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 197 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The diving supervisor shall ensure that equipment used with oxygen or oxygen mixtures greater than 40 percent by volume is cleaned of flammable materials—
(a) Before being placed into service; and
(b) After any repair, alteration, modification, or suspected contamination.
