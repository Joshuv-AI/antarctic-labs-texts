7 C.F.R. § 58.250 — Dry whole milk.
Citation: us:cfr:7:58.250
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-58.250
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 58 (Grading And Inspection, General Specifications For Approved Plants And Standards For Grades Of Dairy Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Dry whole milk in commercial bulk containers which bears an official identification shall meet the requirements for the U.S. Standards for Grades of Dry Whole Milk. Quality requirements for dry whole milk in consumer packages shall be for U.S. Extra Grade and shall be gas packed with an oxygen content of not more than 2.0 percent.
