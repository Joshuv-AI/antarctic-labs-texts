49 C.F.R. § 1016.304 — Reply.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1016.304
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1016.304
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1016 (Special Procedures Governing The Recovery Of Expenses By Parties To Board Adjudicatory Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within 15 days after service of an answer, the applicant may file a reply. If the reply is based on any alleged facts not already in the record of the proceeding, the applicant shall include with the reply either supporting affidavits or a request for further proceedings under § 1016.307.
