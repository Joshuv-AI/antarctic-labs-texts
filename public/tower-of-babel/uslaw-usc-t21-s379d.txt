21 U.S.C. § 379d — Automation of Food and Drug Administration
Citation: us:usc:21:379d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section379d
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 9 (Federal Food, Drug, And Cosmetic Act); Subchapter VII (General Authority); Part A (General Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The Secretary, acting through the Commissioner of Food and Drugs, shall automate appropriate activities of the Food and Drug Administration to ensure timely review of activities regulated under this chapter.
(b) Authorization of appropriations
There are authorized to be appropriated each fiscal year such sums as are necessary to carry out this section.
