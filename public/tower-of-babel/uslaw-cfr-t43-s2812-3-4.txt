43 C.F.R. § 2812.3-4 — -4 Where no road use agreement is required.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2812.3-4
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2812.3-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2810 (Tramroads And Logging Roads). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where, in the judgment of the authorized officer, it is consistent with the policy set forth in subpart 2811 he may issue a permit without requesting the applicant to grant any rights to the United States under this paragraph.
