33 C.F.R. § 6.01-4 — -4 Waterfront facility.
Citation: us:cfr:33:6.01-4
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-6.01-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 6 (Protection And Security Of Vessels, Harbors, And Waterfront Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Waterfront facility. “Waterfront facility,” as used in this part, means all piers, wharves, docks, or similar structures to which vessels may be secured and naval yards, stations, and installations, including ranges; areas of land, water, or land and water under and in immediate proximity to them; buildings on them or contiguous to them and equipment and materials on or in them.
