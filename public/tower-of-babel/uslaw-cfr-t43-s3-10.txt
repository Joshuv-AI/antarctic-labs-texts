43 C.F.R. § 3.10 — Reports.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3.10
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3 (Preservation Of American Antiquities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At the close of each season's field work the permitee shall report in duplicate to the Smithsonian Institution, in such form as its secretary may prescribe, and shall prepare in duplicate a catalogue of the collections and of the photographs made during the season, indicating therein such material, if any, as may be available for exchange.
