49 C.F.R. § 1018.64 — Transfer of a claim.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1018.64
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1018.64
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1018 (Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board may refer a claim to GAO when there is doubt as to whether or not a collection action should be suspended or terminated.
