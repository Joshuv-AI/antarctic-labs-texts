45 C.F.R. § 12a.14 — a.14 No right of administrative review for agency decisions.
Citation: us:cfr:45:12a.14
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-12a.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 12a (PART 12a—USE OF FEDERAL REAL PROPERTY TO ASSIST THE HOMELESS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

There is no right to administrative review within HHS, including requests for reconsideration or appeal, of agency decisions on applications and other discretionary decisions.
