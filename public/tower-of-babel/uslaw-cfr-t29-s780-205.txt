29 C.F.R. § 780.205 — Nursery activities generally.
Citation: us:cfr:29:780.205
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-780.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 780 (Exemptions Applicable To Agriculture, Processing Of Agricultural Commodities, And Related Subjects Under The Fair Labor Standards Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The employees of a nursery who are engaged in the following activities are employed in “agriculture”:
(a) Sowing seeds and otherwise propagating fruit, nut, shade, vegetable, and ornamental plants or trees (but not Christmas trees), and shrubs, vines, and flowers;
(b) Handling such plants from propagating frames to the field;
(c) Planting, cultivating, watering, spraying, fertilizing, pruning, bracing, and feeding the growing crop.
