Establishment of Class E Airspace; Ottawa, IL; Correction

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This action corrects a final rule published by the FAA in the 
Federal Register on September 22, 2026, establishing Class E airspace 
at OSF St Elizabeth Medical Center Heliport, Ottawa, IL. This action 
corrects a typographical error in the airspace legal description.

DATES: The effective date of the final rule published in the Federal 
Register on September 22, 2026 remains 0901 UTC, December 24, 2026. The 
Director of the Federal Register approves this incorporation by 
reference action under 1 CFR part 51, subject to the annual revision of 
FAA Order JO 7400.11 and publication of conforming amendments.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

History

    The FAA published a final rule for Docket No. FAA-2026-8548 in the 
Federal Register (91 FR 59987; September 22, 2026), establishing Class 
E airspace at OSF St Elizabeth Medical Center Heliport, Ottawa, IL. 
After publication of the final rule, an FAA database review noted that 
the incorrect heliport (OSF St Francis Medical Center Heliport) was 
used in the legal description. This action replaces the incorrect 
heliport with the correct heliport, OSF St Elizabeth Medical Center 
Heliport. This correction represents a ministerial change. It does not 
change the airspace dimensions or operating requirements. Accordingly, 
the FAA finds good cause that recirculating the NPRM for notice and 
comment is unnecessary.

Correction of Final Rule

    Accordingly, pursuant to the authority delegated to me, the final 
rule for Docket No. FAA-2026-8548, as published in the Federal Register 
on September 22, 2026 (91 FR 59987; FR Doc. 2026-19391), is corrected 
as follows:
    On page 59988, in the third column, amend Paragraph 6005 ``Class E 
Airspace Areas Extending Upward From 700 Feet or More Above the Surface 
of the Earth.'' by revising the legal description for ``AGL IL E5 
Ottawa, IL'' to read as follows:

AGL IL E5 Ottawa, IL [Establish]

OSF St Elizabeth Medical Center Heliport, IL
    (Lat. 41[deg]21'30'' N, long 88[deg]49'30'' W)

    That airspace extending upward from 700 feet above the surface 
within a 6.8-mile radius of the OSF St Elizabeth Medical Center 
Heliport.
