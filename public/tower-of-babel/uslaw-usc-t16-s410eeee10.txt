16 U.S.C. § 410eeee–10 — Class I or class II redesignation for clean air purposes
Citation: us:usc:16:410eeee–10
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section410eeee–10
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LIX–CCC (New River Gorge National Park And Preserve). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purposes of part C of the Clean Air Act [42 U.S.C. 7470 et seq.], the State may redesignate the national river only as class I or class II.
