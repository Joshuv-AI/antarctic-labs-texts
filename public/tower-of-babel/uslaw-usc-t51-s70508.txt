51 U.S.C. § 70508 — Robotic or human servicing of spacecraft
Citation: us:usc:51:70508
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section70508
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 705 (Exploration Initiatives). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator shall take all necessary steps to ensure that provision is made in the design and construction of all future observatory-class scientific spacecraft intended to be deployed in Earth orbit or at a Lagrangian point in space for robotic or human servicing and repair to the extent practicable and appropriate.
