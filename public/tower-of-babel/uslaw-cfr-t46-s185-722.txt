46 C.F.R. § 185.722 — Monthly inspections.
Citation: us:cfr:46:185.722
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-185.722
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 185 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each survival craft, rescue boat, and launching appliance on a vessel must be inspected monthly, using the manufacturer's instructions to make sure it is complete and in good order.
