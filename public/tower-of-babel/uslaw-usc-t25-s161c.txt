25 U.S.C. § 161c — Surplus above requirements of fund; transfer to surplus fund of Treasury; retransfer
Citation: us:usc:25:161c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section161c
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 4 (Performance By United States Of Obligations To Indians); Subchapter III (Deposit, Care, And Investment Of Indian Moneys). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The amount held in any tribal fund account which, in the judgment of the Secretary of the Interior, is not required for the purpose for which the fund was created, shall be covered into the surplus fund of the Treasury; and so much thereof as is found to be necessary for such purpose may at any time thereafter be restored to the account on books of the Treasury without appropriation by Congress.
