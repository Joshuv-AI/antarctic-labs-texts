A Hamiltonian driven Geometric Construction of Neural Networks on the Lognormal Statistical Manifold Journal: Nuclear Physics B
Prosper Rosaire Mama Assandje, Teumsa Aboubakar, Dongho Joseph, Takemi Nakamura

Abstract
Bridging information geometry with machine learning, this paper presents a method for constructing neural networks intrinsically on statistical manifolds. We demonstrate this approach by formulating a neural network architecture directly on the lognormal statistical manifold. The construction is driven by the Hamiltonian system that is equivalent to the gradient flow on this manifold. First, we define the network’s input values using the coordinate system of this Hamiltonian dynamics, naturally embedded in the Poincar e ´ \acute{e} disk. The core of our contribution lies in the derivation of the network’s components from geometric principles: the rotation component of the synaptic weight matrix is determined by the Lie group action of S ​ U ​ ( 1 , 1 ) SU(1,1) on the disk, while the activation function emerges from the symplectic structure of the system. We subsequently obtain the complete weight matrix, including its translation vector, and the resulting output values. This work shows that the lognormal manifold can be seamlessly viewed as a neural manifold, with its geometric properties dictating a unique and interpretable neural network structure. The proposed method offers a new paradigm for building learning systems grounded in the differential geometry of their underlying parameter spaces.
