2 C.F.R. § 2205.100 — Adoption of 2 CFR Part 200.
Citation: us:cfr:2:2205.100
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-2205.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 2205 (Implementation Of And Exemptions To 2 Cfr). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Under the authority listed above, the Corporation for National and Community Service adopts the Office of Management and Budget's (OMB) Guidance in 2 CFR part 200, except as specified in this part. Thus, this part gives regulatory effect to the OMB guidance and supplements the guidance for recipients of awards from the Corporation.
