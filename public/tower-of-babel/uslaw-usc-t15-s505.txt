15 U.S.C. § 505 — Report to Congress
Citation: us:usc:15:505
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section505
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 13 (Textile Foundation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation shall, on or before the 1st day of December in each year, transmit to Congress and to the President a report of its proceedings and activities for the preceding calendar year, including a detailed statement of its receipts and expenditures. Such reports shall not be printed as public documents.
