25 C.F.R. § 2.607 — What happens if no official responds to my requests under this subpart?
Citation: us:cfr:25:2.607
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-2.607
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 2 (Appeals From Administrative Decisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you exhaust all the provisions of this subpart and the Department has still not taken action on your request, the Department's inaction may be subject to judicial review pursuant to 5 U.S.C. 706(1).
