20 U.S.C. § 3474 — Rules and regulations
Citation: us:usc:20:3474
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 48 (Department Of Education); Subchapter IV (Administrative Provisions); Part B (General Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to prescribe such rules and regulations as the Secretary determines necessary or appropriate to administer and manage the functions of the Secretary or the Department.
