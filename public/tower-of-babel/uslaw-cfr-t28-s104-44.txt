28 C.F.R. § 104.44 — Determination of presumed noneconomic losses for death for claims on behalf of decedents.
Citation: us:cfr:28:104.44
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-104.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 104 (September 11Th Victim Compensation Fund). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The presumed non-economic losses for an eligible death shall be $250,000 plus an additional $100,000 for the spouse and each dependent of the deceased victim. Such presumed losses include a noneconomic component of replacement services loss.
