10 U.S.C. § 1048 — Gratuity payment to persons discharged for fraudulent enlistment
Citation: us:usc:10:1048
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1048
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 53 (Miscellaneous Rights And Benefits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary concerned may pay a gratuity of not to exceed $25 to a person discharged for fraudulent enlistment.
