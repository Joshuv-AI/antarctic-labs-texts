7 C.F.R. § 3405.5 — Matching funds.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3405.5
Creator: National Institute Of Food And Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3405.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3405 (Higher Education Challenge Grants Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each application must provide for matching support from a non-Federal source. NIFA will cite in the program announcement the required percentage of institutional cost sharing.
