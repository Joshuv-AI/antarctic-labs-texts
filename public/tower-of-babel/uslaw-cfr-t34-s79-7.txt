34 C.F.R. § 79.7 — How does the Secretary communicate with State and local officials concerning the Department's programs and activities?
Citation: us:cfr:34:79.7
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-79.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 79 (Intergovernmental Review Of Department Of Education Programs And Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) [Reserved]
(b)(1) The Secretary provides notice to directly affected State, areawide, regional, and local entities in a State of proposed federal financial assistance if:
(i) The State has not adopted a process under the Order; or
(ii) The assistance involves a program or activity not selected for the State process.
(2) This notice may be made by publication in the Federal Register or other means which the Secretary determine appropriate.
