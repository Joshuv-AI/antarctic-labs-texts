31 C.F.R. § 597.309 — Foreign terrorist organization.
Citation: us:cfr:31:597.309
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-597.309
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 597 (Foreign Terrorist Organizations Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term foreign terrorist organization means an organization designated or redesignated as a foreign terrorist organization, or with respect to which the Secretary of State has notified Congress of the intention to designate as a foreign terrorist organization, under 8 U.S.C. 1189(a).
