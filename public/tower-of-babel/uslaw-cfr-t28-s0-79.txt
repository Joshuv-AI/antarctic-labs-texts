28 C.F.R. § 0.79 — Redelegation of authority.
Citation: us:cfr:28:0.79
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-0.79
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 0 (Organization Of The Department Of Justice). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Assistant Attorney General for Administration is authorized to redelegate to any Department official any of the power or authority vested in him by this subpart O. Existing redelegations by the Assistant Attorney General for Administration shall continue in force and effect until modified or revoked.
