20 U.S.C. § 75e — Powers of Board
Citation: us:usc:20:75e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section75e
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 3 (Smithsonian Institution, National Museums And Art Galleries); Subchapter III (National Portrait Gallery). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of carrying out any function authorized by section 75b of this title, the Board may—
(1)
purchase, accept, borrow, or otherwise acquire portraiture, statuary, and other items for preservation, exhibition, or study. The Board may acquire any such item on the basis of its general historical interest, its artistic merit, or the historical significance of the individual to which it relates, or any combination of any such factors. The Board may acquire period furniture and other items to enhance its displays of portraiture and statuary.
(2)
preserve or restore any item acquired pursuant to paragraph (1).
(3)
display, loan, store, or otherwise hold any such item.
(4)
sell, exchange, donate, return, or otherwise dispose of any such item.
