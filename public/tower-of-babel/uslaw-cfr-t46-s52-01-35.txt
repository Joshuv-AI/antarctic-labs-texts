46 C.F.R. § 52.01-35 — -35 Auxiliary, donkey, fired thermal fluid heater, and heating boilers.
Citation: us:cfr:46:52.01-35
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-52.01-35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 52 (Power Boilers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) To determine the appropriate part of the regulations where requirements for miscellaneous boiler types, such as donkey, fired thermal fluid heater, heating boiler, etc., may be found, refer to table 1 to § 54.01-5 of this subchapter.
(b) Fired vessels in which steam is generated at pressures exceeding 103 kPa gage (15 psig) must meet the requirements of this part.
