29 C.F.R. § 2200.39 — Statement of position.
Citation: us:cfr:29:2200.39
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Review Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-2200.39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 2200 (Rules Of Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At any time prior to the commencement of the hearing before the Judge, any person entitled to appear as a party, or any person who has been granted leave to intervene, may file a statement of position with respect to any or all issues to be heard. The Judge may order the filing of a statement of position.
