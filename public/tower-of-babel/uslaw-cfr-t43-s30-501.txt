43 C.F.R. § 30.501 — When is joint tenancy presumed?
Citation: us:cfr:43:30.501
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-30.501
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 30 (Indian Probate Hearings Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A judge will presume that a testator intended to devise interests in joint tenancy when:
(a) A testator devises trust or restricted interests in the same parcel of land to more than one person; and
(b) The will does not contain clear and express language stating that the devisees receive the interests as tenants in common.
