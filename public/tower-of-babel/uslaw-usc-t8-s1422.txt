8 U.S.C. § 1422 — Eligibility for naturalization
Citation: us:usc:8:1422
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title8-section1422
Context: United States Code, Title 8 (Aliens And Nationality); Chapter 12 (Immigration And Nationality); Subchapter III (Nationality And Naturalization); Part II (Nationality Through Naturalization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The right of a person to become a naturalized citizen of the United States shall not be denied or abridged because of race or sex or because such person is married.
