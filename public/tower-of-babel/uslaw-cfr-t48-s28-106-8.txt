48 C.F.R. § 28.106-8 — 28.106-8 Payment to subcontractors or suppliers.
Citation: us:cfr:48:28.106-8
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-28.106-8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 28 (Bonds And Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer will only authorize payment to subcontractors or suppliers from an ILC (or any other cash equivalent security) upon a judicial determination of the rights of the parties, a signed notarized statement by the contractor that the payment is due and owed, or a signed agreement between the parties as to amount due and owed.
