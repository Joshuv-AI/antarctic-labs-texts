17 C.F.R. § 11.5 — Transcripts.
Citation: us:cfr:17:11.5
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-11.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 11 (Rules Relating To Investigations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Transcripts of testimony taken in the course of an investigative proceeding shall be recorded solely by an official reporter or other person or by other means authorized by the Commission or by a member of the Commission or its staff conducting the investigation for the Commission.
