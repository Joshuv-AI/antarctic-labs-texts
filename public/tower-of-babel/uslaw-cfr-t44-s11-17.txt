44 C.F.R. § 11.17 — Referral to Department of Justice.
Citation: us:cfr:44:11.17
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-11.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 11 (Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When Department of Justice approval or consultation is required under § 11.16, the referral or request shall be transmitted to the Department of Justice by the Chief Counsel or his or her designee.
