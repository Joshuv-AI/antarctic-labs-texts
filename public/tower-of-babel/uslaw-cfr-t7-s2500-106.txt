7 C.F.R. § 2500.106 — Funding restrictions.
Citation: us:cfr:7:2500.106
Creator: Office Of Advocacy And Outreach, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-2500.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 2500 (Oao Federal Financial Assistance Programs—General Award Administrative Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Funds made available under this subpart shall not be used for the construction of a new building or facility or the acquisition, expansion, remodeling, or alteration of an existing facility (including site grading and improvement, and architect fees).
