8 C.F.R. § 318.1 — Warrant of arrest.
Citation: us:cfr:8:318.1
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-318.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 318 (Pending Removal Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the purposes of section 318 of the Act, a notice to appear issued under 8 CFR part 239 (including a charging document issued to commence proceedings under sections 236 or 242 of the Act prior to April 1, 1997) shall be regarded as a warrant of arrest.
