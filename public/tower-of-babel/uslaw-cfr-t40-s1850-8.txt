40 C.F.R. § 1850.8 — Authority to determine.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1850.8
Creator: Gulf Coast Ecosystem Restoration Council
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1850.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1850 (Availability Of Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Records Management Officer or Council Executive Director, when receiving a request pursuant to these regulations, shall grant or deny such request. That decision shall be final, subject only to administrative appeal as provided in § 1850.7. The Council General Counsel shall deny or grant an administrative appeal requested under § 1850.7.
