29 C.F.R. § 1926.23 — First aid and medical attention.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1926.23
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1926.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1926 (Safety And Health Regulations For Construction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

First aid services and provisions for medical care shall be made available by the employer for every employee covered by these regulations. Regulations prescribing specific requirements for first aid, medical attention, and emergency facilities are contained in subpart D of this part.
