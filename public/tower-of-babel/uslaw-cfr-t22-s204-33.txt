22 C.F.R. § 204.33 — A.I.D. approval of acceleration of notes.
Citation: us:cfr:22:204.33
Creator: Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-204.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 204 (Housing Guaranty Standard Terms And Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Without the prior approval of A.I.D., the Lender or any Assignee shall not accelerate any Eligible Notes held by it on account of the happening of an Event of Default other than failure to make a payment when due on the note.
