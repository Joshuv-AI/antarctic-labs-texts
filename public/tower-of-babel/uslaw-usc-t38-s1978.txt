38 U.S.C. § 1978 — Reinstatement
Citation: us:usc:38:1978
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section1978
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 19 (Insurance); Subchapter III (Servicemembers’ Group Life Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Reinstatement of insurance coverage granted under this subchapter but lapsed for nonpayment of premiums shall be under terms and conditions prescribed by the Secretary.
