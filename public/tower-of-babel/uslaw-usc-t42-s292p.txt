42 U.S.C. § 292p — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:42:292p
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section292p
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter V (Health Professions Education); Part A (Student Loans); Subpart i (Insured Health Education Assistance Loans To Graduate Students). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
For fiscal year 1993 and subsequent fiscal years, there are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary for the adequacy of the student loan insurance account under this subpart and for the purpose of administering this subpart.
(b) Availability of sums
Sums appropriated under subsection (a) shall remain available until expended.
