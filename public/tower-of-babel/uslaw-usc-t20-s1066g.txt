20 U.S.C. § 1066g — Minority business enterprise utilization
Citation: us:usc:20:1066g
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1066g
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 28 (Higher Education Resources And Student Assistance); Subchapter III (Institutional Aid); Part D (Historically Black College And University Capital Financing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the performance of and with respect to the Secretary’s effectuation of his responsibilities under section 1066d(1) of this title and to the maximum extent feasible in the implementation of the purposes of this part, minority business persons, including bond underwriters and credit enhancers, bond counsel, marketers, accountants, advisors, construction contractors, and managers should be utilized.
