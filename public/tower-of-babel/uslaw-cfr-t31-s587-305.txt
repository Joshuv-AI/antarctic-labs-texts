31 C.F.R. § 587.305 — Government of the Russian Federation.
Citation: us:cfr:31:587.305
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-587.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 587 (Russian Harmful Foreign Activities Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term Government of the Russian Federation means the Government of the Russian Federation, any political subdivision, agency, or instrumentality thereof, including the Central Bank of the Russian Federation, and any person owned, controlled, or directed by, or acting for or on behalf of, the Government of the Russian Federation.
