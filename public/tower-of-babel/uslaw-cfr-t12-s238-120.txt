12 C.F.R. § 238.120 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:12:238.120
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-238.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 238 (Savings And Loan Holding Companies (Regulation Ll)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to covered savings and loan holding companies with average total consolidated assets of $100 billion or more.
