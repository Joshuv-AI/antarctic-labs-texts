7 C.F.R. § 63.7 — Fund.
Citation: us:cfr:7:63.7
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-63.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 63 (National Sheep Industry Improvement Center). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Fund means the NSIIC Revolving Fund established in the United States Department of the Treasury that is available to the NSIIC without fiscal year limitation, to carry out the programs and activities authorized under the Act.
