40 U.S.C. § 608 — Scrip, tokens, tickets
Citation: us:usc:40:608
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section608
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Chapter 5 (Property Management); Subchapter VI (Motor Vehicle Pools And Transportation Systems). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator of General Services, in the operation of motor vehicle pools or systems under this subchapter, may provide for the sale and use of scrip, tokens, tickets, and similar devices to collect payment.
