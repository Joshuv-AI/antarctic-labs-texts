Definition 2.1

Let

(i)

\displaystyle\frac{tP_{s-1}(t)}{(1-t)^{s}}=\sum_{d=1}^{\infty}d^{s-1}t^{d}.

(2.2)

(iii)

(3.1)

\displaystyle D_{x}=x\frac{\rm d}{{\rm d}x}.

(3.2)

The following lemma regarding the sums of powers should be well-known
to the experts in analytic number theory. However, the author is unable
to find a reference. For completeness, we present its proof here.

Lemma 4.1
.

Fix integers
n\geq 0
,
r\geq 1
and
t_{1},\ldots,t_{r}\geq 1
. Then,

(i)

\displaystyle{\sum_{d_{1},\ldots,d_{r}\geq 1,\,d_{1}+\ldots+d_{r}\leq n}\prod_{i=1}^{r}d_{i}^{t_{i}}}

is a polynomial in
\mathbb{Q}[n]\cdot n
of degree
\sum_{i=1}^{r}t_{i}+r
.

(ii)

\displaystyle{\sum_{d_{1},\ldots,d_{r}\geq 1,\,d_{1}+\ldots+d_{r}=n}\prod_{i=1}^{r}d_{i}^{t_{i}}}

is a polynomial in
\mathbb{Q}[n]\cdot n
of degree
\sum_{i=1}^{r}t_{i}+r-1
.

Proof.

(i) We use induction on
r
. Denote

\displaystyle{\sum_{d_{1},\ldots,d_{r}\geq 1,\,d_{1}+\ldots+d_{r}\leq n}\prod_{i=1}^{r}d_{i}^{t_{i}}}
by
A_{r}
. Then

A_{r}=\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}d_{1},\ldots,d_{r}\geq 0,\,d_{1}+\ldots+d_{r}\leq n\end{subarray}}\prod_{i=1}^{r}d_{i}^{t_{i}}

since
t_{1},\ldots,t_{r}\geq 1
. When
r=1
, the lemma is
the classical Faulhaber’s formula. Assume our lemma holds for
r-1
.
We have

A_{r}=\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}0\leq d_{r}\leq n\end{subarray}}d_{r}^{t_{r}}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}d_{1},\ldots,d_{r-1}\geq 0,\,d_{1}+\ldots+d_{r-1}\leq n-d_{r}\end{subarray}}\prod_{i=1}^{r-1}d_{i}^{t_{i}}.

By induction,
\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}d_{1},\ldots,d_{r-1}\geq 0,\,d_{1}+\ldots+d_{r-1}\leq n-d_{r}\end{subarray}}\prod_{i=1}^{r-1}d_{i}^{t_{i}}

is a polynomial in

\mathbb{Q}[(n-d_{r})]\cdot(n-d_{r})

of degree
\sum_{i=1}^{r-1}(t_{i}+1)
.
So
A_{r}
is a
\mathbb{Q}
-linear combination of expressions of the form

\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}0\leq d_{r}\leq n\end{subarray}}d_{r}^{t_{r}}(n^{a}d_{r}^{b})

with
a,b\geq 0
and
a+b\leq\sum_{i=1}^{r-1}(t_{i}+1)
.
By Faulhaber’s formula,

\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}0\leq d_{r}\leq n\end{subarray}}d_{r}^{t_{r}}(n^{a}d_{r}^{b})=n^{a}\cdot\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}0\leq d_{r}\leq n\end{subarray}}d_{r}^{t_{r}+b}

is a polynomial in
\mathbb{Q}[n]\cdot n^{a+1}
of degree
a+(t_{r}+b+1)\leq\sum_{i=1}^{r}(t_{i}+1)
. It follows that
A_{r}
is a polynomial in

\mathbb{Q}[n]\cdot n
of degree
\sum_{i=1}^{r}t_{i}+r
.

(ii) Denote
\displaystyle{\sum_{d_{1},\ldots,d_{r}\geq 1,\,d_{1}+\ldots+d_{r}=n}\prod_{i=1}^{r}d_{i}^{t_{i}}}
by
B_{r}
.
Then,

\displaystyle B_{r}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{d_{1},\ldots,d_{r}\geq 0,\,d_{1}+\ldots+d_{r}=n}\prod_{i=1}^{r}d_{i}^{t_{i}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{d_{1},\ldots,d_{r-1}\geq 0,\,d_{1}+\ldots+d_{r-1}\leq n}\prod_{i=1}^{r-1}d_{i}^{t_{i}}\cdot(n-d_{1}-\ldots-d_{r-1})^{t_{r}}.

Thus,
B_{r}
is a
\mathbb{Q}
-linear combination of expressions of the form

n^{a}\cdot\sum_{d_{1},\ldots,d_{r-1}\geq 0,\,d_{1}+\ldots+d_{r-1}\leq n}\prod_{i=1}^{r-1}d_{i}^{\tilde{t}_{i}}

where
a\geq 0
,
\tilde{t}_{i}\geq 1
and
a+\sum_{i=1}^{r-1}\tilde{t}_{i}=\sum_{i=1}^{r}t_{i}
. By (i),
B_{r}
is a polynomial in

\mathbb{Q}[n]\cdot n
of degree
a+\sum_{i=1}^{r-1}\tilde{t}_{i}+r-1=\sum_{i=1}^{r}t_{i}+r-1
.
∎

\prod_{1\leq i<j\leq N}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}n_{1}^{i,j},\ldots,n_{r^{i,j}}^{i,j}\geq 0,d_{1}^{i,j},\ldots,d_{r^{i,j}}^{i,j}\geq 1\\
n_{r^{i,j}+1}^{i,j},\ldots,n_{r^{i,j}+s^{i,j}}^{i,j}\geq 1,d_{r^{i,j}+1}^{i,j},\ldots,d_{r^{i,j}+s^{i,j}}^{i,j}\geq 1\end{subarray}}\prod_{k=1}^{r^{i,j}+s^{i,j}}(n_{k}^{i,j})^{u_{k}^{i,j}}(d_{k}^{i,j})^{t_{k}^{i,j}}\cdot q^{\sum_{k=1}^{r^{i,j}+s^{i,j}}n_{k}^{i,j}d_{k}^{i,j}}

\cdot\prod_{1\leq i\leq N}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}n_{1}^{(i)},\ldots,n_{r_{i}}^{(i)}\geq 0,d_{1}^{(i)},\ldots,d_{r_{i}}^{(i)}\geq 1\\
n_{r_{i}+1}^{(i)},\ldots,n_{r_{i}+s_{i}}^{(i)}\geq 1,d_{r_{i}+1}^{(i)},\ldots,d_{r_{i}+s_{i}}^{(i)}\geq 1\end{subarray}}\prod_{k=1}^{r_{i}+s_{i}}(n_{k}^{(i)})^{u_{k}^{(i)}}(d_{k}^{(i)})^{t_{k}^{(i)}}\cdot q^{\sum_{k=1}^{r_{i}+s_{i}}n_{k}^{(i)}d_{k}^{(i)}}

together with the condition that for every
1\leq i\leq N
,

\displaystyle\sum_{1\leq j<i}\bigg(\sum_{k=1}^{r^{j,i}}d_{k}^{j,i}-\sum_{k=1}^{s^{j,i}}d_{r^{j,i}+k}^{j,i}\bigg)+\bigg(\sum_{k=1}^{r_{i}}d_{k}^{(i)}-\sum_{k=1}^{s_{i}}d_{r_{i}+k}^{(i)}\bigg)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{N\geq j>i}\bigg(\sum_{k=1}^{r^{i,j}}d_{k}^{i,j}-\sum_{k=1}^{s^{i,j}}d_{r^{i,j}+k}^{i,j}\bigg),

Then,
\widetilde{V}^{(N)}\in\mathcal{BD}
with weight at most

\sum_{1\leq i<j\leq N}\sum_{k=1}^{r^{i,j}+s^{i,j}}(u_{k}^{i,j}+t_{k}^{i,j}+1)+\sum_{1\leq i\leq N}\sum_{k=1}^{r_{i}+s_{i}}(u_{k}^{(i)}+t_{k}^{(i)}+1).

Moreover, if
t_{k}^{i,j},t_{k}^{(i^{\prime})}\geq 1
for all
k
,
i<j
and
i^{\prime}
,
then
\widetilde{V}^{(N)}\in{\rm q}{\mathcal{BD}}
.

Proof.

Follows immediately from Lemma
5.1
and
Lemma
5.2
.
∎

The following result is our Theorem
1.3
.

Theorem 5.4
.

Let notations be from Definition
1.2
. Then,

(i)

for
m_{\ell}^{i,j},n_{\ell}^{i,j},m_{i},n_{i}\geq 0
, the coefficient of

\prod_{1\leq i<j\leq N,1\leq\ell\leq 2}(z_{\ell}^{i,j})^{m_{\ell}^{i,j}}(v_{\ell}^{i,j})^{n_{\ell}^{i,j}}\cdot\prod_{1\leq i\leq N}z_{i}^{m_{i}}v_{i}^{n_{i}}

in

{\rm Coeff}_{y_{1}^{0}\cdots y_{N}^{0}}(\mathfrak{P}_{N}^{a,b})

is contained in
\mathcal{BD}[a,b]
with weight at most

\displaystyle\sum_{1\leq i<j\leq N,1\leq\ell\leq 2}(m_{\ell}^{i,j}+n_{\ell}^{i,j})+\sum_{1\leq i\leq N}(m_{i}+n_{i})

(5.11)

and with degree in (
a
and
b
) at most

\sum_{1\leq i\leq N}(m_{i}+n_{i})
.

(ii)

for
a=b=0
and
m_{\ell}^{i,j},n_{\ell}^{i,j}\geq 0
, the coefficient of

\prod_{1\leq i<j\leq N,1\leq\ell\leq 2}(z_{\ell}^{i,j})^{m_{\ell}^{i,j}}(v_{\ell}^{i,j})^{n_{\ell}^{i,j}}

in

{\rm Coeff}_{y_{1}^{0}\cdots y_{N}^{0}}(\mathfrak{P}_{N}^{0,0})

is contained in
{\rm q}{\mathcal{BD}}
with weight at most

\displaystyle\sum_{1\leq i<j\leq N,1\leq\ell\leq 2}(m_{\ell}^{i,j}+n_{\ell}^{i,j}).

(5.12)

Proof.

(i) By (
4.24
), the coefficient of

\prod_{1\leq\ell\leq 2}(z_{\ell}^{i,j})^{m_{\ell}^{i,j}}(v_{\ell}^{i,j})^{n_{\ell}^{i,j}}
in

\frac{(x_{1}^{i,j}x_{2}^{i,j}y_{i,j})_{\infty}(y_{i,j})_{\infty}}{(x_{1}^{i,j}y_{i,j})_{\infty}(x_{2}^{i,j}y_{i,j})_{\infty}}\frac{(u_{1}^{i,j}u_{2}^{i,j}y_{i,j}^{-1}q)_{\infty}(y_{i,j}^{-1}q)_{\infty}}{(u_{1}^{i,j}y_{i,j}^{-1}q)_{\infty}(u_{2}^{i,j}y_{i,j}^{-1}q)_{\infty}}

is a finite
\mathbb{Q}
-linear combination of expressions of the form

\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}n_{1},\ldots,n_{r}\geq 0,d_{1},\ldots,d_{r}\geq 1\\
n_{r+1},\ldots,n_{r+s}\geq 1,d_{r+1},\ldots,d_{r+s}\geq 1\end{subarray}}\prod_{k=1}^{r+s}d_{k}^{t_{k}}\cdot q^{\sum_{k=1}^{r+s}n_{k}d_{k}}y_{i,j}^{\sum_{k=1}^{r}d_{k}-\sum_{k=1}^{s}d_{r+k}}

where
t_{k}\geq 1
for every
k
and
\sum_{k=1}^{r+s}(t_{k}+1)=\sum_{1\leq\ell\leq 2}(m_{\ell}^{i,j}+n_{\ell}^{i,j})
.
So the coefficient of
\prod_{1\leq i<j\leq N,1\leq\ell\leq 2}(z_{\ell}^{i,j})^{m_{\ell}^{i,j}}(v_{\ell}^{i,j})^{n_{\ell}^{i,j}}
in

\displaystyle\prod_{1\leq i<j\leq N}\frac{(x_{1}^{i,j}x_{2}^{i,j}y_{i,j})_{\infty}(y_{i,j})_{\infty}}{(x_{1}^{i,j}y_{i,j})_{\infty}(x_{2}^{i,j}y_{i,j})_{\infty}}\frac{(u_{1}^{i,j}u_{2}^{i,j}y_{i,j}^{-1}q)_{\infty}(y_{i,j}^{-1}q)_{\infty}}{(u_{1}^{i,j}y_{i,j}^{-1}q)_{\infty}(u_{2}^{i,j}y_{i,j}^{-1}q)_{\infty}}

(5.13)

is a finite
\mathbb{Q}
-linear combination of expressions of the form

\prod_{1\leq i<j\leq N}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}n_{1}^{i,j},\ldots,n_{r^{i,j}}^{i,j}\geq 0,d_{1}^{i,j},\ldots,d_{r^{i,j}}^{i,j}\geq 1\\
n_{r^{i,j}+1}^{i,j},\ldots,n_{r^{i,j}+s^{i,j}}^{i,j}\geq 1,d_{r^{i,j}+1}^{i,j},\ldots,d_{r^{i,j}+s^{i,j}}^{i,j}\geq 1\end{subarray}}\prod_{k=1}^{r^{i,j}+s^{i,j}}(d_{k}^{i,j})^{t_{k}^{i,j}}

\displaystyle\cdot q^{\sum_{k=1}^{r^{i,j}+s^{i,j}}n_{k}^{i,j}d_{k}^{i,j}}y_{i,j}^{\sum_{k=1}^{r^{i,j}}d_{k}^{i,j}-\sum_{k=1}^{s^{i,j}}d_{r+k}^{i,j}}

(5.14)

where
t_{k}^{i,j}\geq 1
for all
k
and
1\leq i<j\leq N
, and

\displaystyle\sum_{1\leq i<j\leq N}\sum_{k=1}^{r^{i,j}+s^{i,j}}(t_{k}^{i,j}+1)=\sum_{1\leq i<j\leq N,1\leq\ell\leq 2}(m_{\ell}^{i,j}+n_{\ell}^{i,j}).

(5.15)

Next, regard
((x_{i}y_{i})_{\infty}(y_{i})_{\infty}^{-1})^{a}

as an element in
\mathbb{Q}[[z_{i},q,y_{i},a]]
. Put

f(z_{i})=a\ln\big((x_{i}y_{i})_{\infty}(y_{i})_{\infty}^{-1}\big).

Then,
f(0)=0
. For
m\geq 0
, let
a_{m}={\rm Coeff}_{z_{i}^{m}}((x_{i}y_{i})_{\infty}(y_{i})_{\infty}^{-1})^{a}\in\mathbb{C}[[q,y_{i},a]]
.
Then,

\displaystyle a_{m}=\sum_{k_{1}+2k_{2}+3k_{3}+\cdots=m}\frac{1}{k_{1}!k_{2}!k_{3}!\cdots}\prod_{t\geq 1}\left(\frac{D_{x_{i}}^{t}f}{t!}\Big|_{x_{i}=1}\right)^{k_{t}}

(5.16)

by (
3.4
).
Since
f(z_{i})=a\ln\big((y_{i})_{\infty}^{-1}\big)+a\sum_{n\geq 0}\ln(1-x_{i}y_{i}q^{n})
, we have

D_{x_{i}}f=-a\sum_{n\geq 0}\frac{x_{i}y_{i}q^{n}}{1-x_{i}y_{i}q^{n}}=-a\sum_{n\geq 0}\sum_{d\geq 1}(x_{i}y_{i}q^{n})^{d}.

So for
t\geq 1
, we get

D_{x_{i}}^{t}f|_{x_{i}=1}=-a\sum_{n\geq 0}\sum_{d\geq 1}d^{t-1}(y_{i}q^{n})^{d}.

By (
5.16
), for
m\geq 1
,
a_{m}
is
a finite
\mathbb{Q}
-linear combination of expressions of the form:

\displaystyle a^{r}\prod_{j=1}^{r}\left(\sum_{n_{j}\geq 0}\sum_{d_{j}\geq 1}d_{j}^{t_{j}-1}y^{d_{j}}q^{n_{j}d_{j}}\right)

(5.17)

where
t_{j}\geq 1
and
r\leq\sum_{j=1}^{r}t_{j}=m
.
Note that since
a_{0}=1
,
we have

\displaystyle\bigg(\frac{(x_{i}y_{i})_{\infty}}{(y_{i})_{\infty}}\bigg)^{a}=1+\sum_{m\geq 1}a_{m}z_{i}^{m}\in 1+\mathbb{Q}[[q,y_{i},a,z_{i}]]\cdot z_{i}.

(5.18)

Similarly, for
n\geq 1
, the coefficient of
v_{i}^{n}
in

\bigg(\frac{(u_{i}y_{i}^{-1}q)_{\infty}}{(y_{i}^{-1}q)_{\infty}}\bigg)^{b}\in 1+\mathbb{Q}[[q,y_{i}^{-1},b,v_{i}]]\cdot v_{i}

is a finite
\mathbb{Q}
-linear combination of expressions of the form:

\displaystyle b^{s}\prod_{j=1}^{s}\left(\sum_{n_{j}\geq 0}\sum_{d_{j}\geq 1}d_{j}^{t_{j}-1}(y^{-1})^{d_{j}}q^{n_{j}d_{j}}\right)

(5.19)

where
t_{j}\geq 1
and
s\leq\sum_{j=1}^{s}t_{j}=n
. For simplicity, put

{\rm z^{m}v^{n}}=\prod_{1\leq i<j\leq N,1\leq\ell\leq 2}(z_{\ell}^{i,j})^{m_{\ell}^{i,j}}(v_{\ell}^{i,j})^{n_{\ell}^{i,j}}\cdot\prod_{1\leq i\leq N}z_{i}^{m_{i}}v_{i}^{n_{i}}.

By (
5.14
), (
5.17
) and (
5.19
),

{\rm Coeff}_{{\rm z^{m}v^{n}}}\mathfrak{P}_{N}^{a,b}

is a finite
\mathbb{Q}
-linear combination of expressions of the form

\prod_{1\leq i<j\leq N}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}n_{1}^{i,j},\ldots,n_{r^{i,j}}^{i,j}\geq 0,d_{1}^{i,j},\ldots,d_{r^{i,j}}^{i,j}\geq 1\\
n_{r^{i,j}+1}^{i,j},\ldots,n_{r^{i,j}+s^{i,j}}^{i,j}\geq 1\\
d_{r^{i,j}+1}^{i,j},\ldots,d_{r^{i,j}+s^{i,j}}^{i,j}\geq 1\end{subarray}}\prod_{k=1}^{r^{i,j}+s^{i,j}}(d_{k}^{i,j})^{t_{k}^{i,j}}\cdot q^{\sum_{k=1}^{r^{i,j}+s^{i,j}}n_{k}^{i,j}d_{k}^{i,j}}y_{i,j}^{\sum_{k=1}^{r^{i,j}}d_{k}^{i,j}-\sum_{k=1}^{s^{i,j}}d_{r^{i,j}+k}^{i,j}}

\cdot a^{r}b^{s}\prod_{1\leq i\leq N}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}n_{1}^{(i)},\ldots,n_{r_{i}}^{(i)}\geq 0,d_{1}^{(i)},\ldots,d_{r_{i}}^{(i)}\geq 1\\
n_{r_{i}+1}^{(i)},\ldots,n_{r_{i}+s_{i}}^{(i)}\geq 1\\
d_{r_{i}+1}^{(i)},\ldots,d_{r_{i}+s_{i}}^{(i)}\geq 1\end{subarray}}\prod_{k=1}^{r_{i}+s_{i}}(d_{k}^{(i)})^{t_{k}^{(i)}}\cdot q^{\sum_{k=1}^{r_{i}+s_{i}}n_{k}^{(i)}d_{k}^{(i)}}y_{i}^{\sum_{k=1}^{r_{i}}d_{k}^{(i)}-\sum_{k=1}^{s_{i}}d_{r_{i}+k}^{(i)}}

where
t_{k}^{i,j}\geq 1
for all
k
and
1\leq i<j\leq N
,

t_{k}^{(i)}\geq 0
for all

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle r+s\leq\sum_{1\leq i\leq N}\sum_{k=1}^{r_{i}+s_{i}}(t_{k}^{(i)}+1)\leq\sum_{1\leq i\leq N}(m_{i}+n_{i}).

(5.21)

It follows that the coefficient of
{\rm z^{m}v^{n}}
in

is a finite
\mathbb{Q}
-linear combination of expressions of the form

\cdot\prod_{1\leq i\leq N}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}n_{1}^{(i)},\ldots,n_{r_{i}}^{(i)}\geq 0,d_{1}^{(i)},\ldots,d_{r_{i}}^{(i)}\geq 1\\

\displaystyle\sum_{1\leq j<i}\bigg(\sum_{k=1}^{r^{j,i}}d_{k}^{j,i}-\sum_{k=1}^{s^{j,i}}d_{r^{j,i}+k}^{j,i}\bigg)+\bigg(\sum_{k=1}^{r_{i}}d_{k}^{(i)}-\sum_{k=1}^{s_{i}}d_{r_{i}+k}^{(i)}\bigg)

(ii) Let
