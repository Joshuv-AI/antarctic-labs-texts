34 C.F.R. § 21.22 — Applicants representing others.
Citation: us:cfr:34:21.22
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-21.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 21 (Equal Access To Justice). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an applicant is a party in an adversary adjudication primarily on behalf of one or more persons or entities that are ineligible under this part, then the applicant is not eligible for an award.
