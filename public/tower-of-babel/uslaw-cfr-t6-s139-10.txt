6 C.F.R. § 139.10 — Assessments for protective services.
Citation: us:cfr:6:139.10
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security, Office Of The Secretary
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-6/section-139.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 6 (Domestic Security), Part 139 (Conduct On Federal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary of Homeland Security is authorized to charge Federal agencies under the Secretary's protection responsibility for security services provided by the Federal Protective Service.
