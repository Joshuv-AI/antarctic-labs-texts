32 C.F.R. § 1618.1 — Abandonment of rights or privileges.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1618.1
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1618.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1618 (Notice To Registrants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a registrant fails to claim and exercise any right or privilege within the required time, he shall be deemed to have abandoned the right or privilege unless the Director of Selective Service, for good cause, waives the time limit.
