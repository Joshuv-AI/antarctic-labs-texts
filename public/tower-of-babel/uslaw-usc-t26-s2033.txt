26 U.S.C. § 2033 — Property in which the decedent had an interest
Citation: us:usc:26:2033
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section2033
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 11 (Estate Tax); Subchapter A (Estates Of Citizens Or Residents); Part III (Gross Estate). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The value of the gross estate shall include the value of all property to the extent of the interest therein of the decedent at the time of his death.
