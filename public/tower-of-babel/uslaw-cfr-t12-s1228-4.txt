12 C.F.R. § 1228.4 — State restrictions unaffected.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1228.4
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1228.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1228 (Restrictions On The Acquisition Of, Or Taking Security Interests In, Mortgages On Properties Encumbered By Certain Private Transfer Fee Covenants And Related Securities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part does not affect state restrictions or requirements with respect to private transfer fee covenants, such as with respect to validity, enforceability, disclosures, or duration.
