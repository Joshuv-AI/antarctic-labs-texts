32 C.F.R. § 1630.47 — Class 4-W: Registrant who has completed alternative service in lieu of induction.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1630.47
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1630.47
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1630 (Classification Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In Class 4-W shall be placed any registrant who subsequent to being ordered to perform alternative service in lieu of induction has been released from such service after satisfactorily performing the work for a period of 24 months, or has been granted an early release by the Director of Selective Service after completing at least 6 months of satisfactory service.
