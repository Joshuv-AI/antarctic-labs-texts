10 C.F.R. § 40.45 — Commission action on applications to renew or amend.
Citation: us:cfr:10:40.45
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-40.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 40 (Domestic Licensing Of Source Material). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In considering an application by a licensee to renew or amend his license the Commission will apply the applicable criteria set forth in § 40.32.
