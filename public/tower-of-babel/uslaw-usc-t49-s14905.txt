49 U.S.C. § 14905 — Penalties for violations of rules relating to loading and unloading motor vehicles
Citation: us:usc:49:14905
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section14905
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part B (Motor Carriers, Water Carriers, Brokers, And Freight Forwarders); Chapter 149 (Civil And Criminal Penalties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Civil Penalties.—
Whoever knowingly authorizes, consents to, or permits a violation of subsection (a) or (b) of section 14103 or who knowingly violates subsection (a) of such section is liable to the United States for a civil penalty of not more than $10,000 for each violation.
(b) Criminal Penalties.—
Whoever knowingly violates section 14103(b) of this title shall be fined under title 18 or imprisoned not more than 2 years, or both.
