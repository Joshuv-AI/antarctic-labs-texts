46 U.S.C. § 3103 — Use of reports, documents, and records
Citation: us:usc:46:3103
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section3103
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part B (Inspection And Regulation Of Vessels); Chapter 31 (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may rely, as evidence of compliance with this subtitle, on—
(1)
reports, documents, and records of other persons who have been determined by the Secretary to be reliable; and
(2)
other methods the Secretary has determined to be reliable.
