34 C.F.R. § 299.15 — How does an organization or individual file a complaint?
Citation: us:cfr:34:299.15
Creator: Office Of Elementary And Secondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-299.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 299 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An organization or individual may file a written signed complaint with an SEA. The complaint must be in writing and signed by the complainant, and include—
(a) A statement that the SEA or an agency or consortium of agencies has violated a requirement of a Federal statute or regulation that applies to an applicable program; and
(b) The facts on which the statement is based and the specific requirement allegedly violated.
