10 C.F.R. § 580.01 — Purpose.

Creator: Department Of Energy

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 580 (Curtailment Priorities For Essential Agricultural Uses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to implement the authority granted to the Secretary of Energy in section 401 of the Natural Gas Policy Act of 1978, Public Law 95-621, 92 Stat. 3394-3395 (1978).
