16 U.S.C. § 542a — Congressional findings
Citation: us:usc:16:542a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section542a
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 2 (National Forests); Subchapter II (Scenic Areas). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Congress finds that the high altitude and freedom from air pollution and night luminosity caused by human activity, make the research site uniquely suited to the conduct of research probes into thunder clouds and for other atmospheric and astronomical research purposes.
