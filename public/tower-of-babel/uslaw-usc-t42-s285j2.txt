42 U.S.C. § 285j–2 — Bioengineering research
Citation: us:usc:42:285j–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section285j–2
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter III (National Research Institutes); Part C (Specific Provisions Respecting National Research Institutes); Subpart 10 (National Institute Of Neurological Disorders And Stroke). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of the Institute shall make grants or enter into contracts for research on the means to overcome paralysis of the extremities through electrical stimulation and the use of computers.
