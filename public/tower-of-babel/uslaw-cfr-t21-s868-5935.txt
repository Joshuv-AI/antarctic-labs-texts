21 C.F.R. § 868.5935 — External negative pressure ventilator.
Citation: us:cfr:21:868.5935
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-868.5935
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 868 (Anesthesiology Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. An external negative pressure ventilator (e.g., iron lung, cuirass) is a device chamber that is intended to support a patient's ventilation by alternately applying and releasing external negative pressure over the diaphragm and upper trunk of the patient.
(b) Classification. Class II (performance standards).
