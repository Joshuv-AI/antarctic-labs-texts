46 C.F.R. § 327.3 — Required claims submission.
Citation: us:cfr:46:327.3
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-327.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 327 (Seamen'S Claims; Administrative Action And Litigation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All claims specified in 50 U.S.C.A. 4701 (a) must be submitted for administrative consideration, as provided in §§ 327.4 and 327.5, prior to institution of court action thereon.
