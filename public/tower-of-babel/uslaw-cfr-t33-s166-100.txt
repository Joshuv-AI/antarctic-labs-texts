33 C.F.R. § 166.100 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:33:166.100
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-166.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 166 (Shipping Safety Fairways). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of these regulations is to establish and designate shipping safety fairways and fairway anchorages to provide unobstructed approaches for vessels using U.S. ports.
