42 C.F.R. § 35.41 — Inventory.
Citation: us:cfr:42:35.41
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-35.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 35 (Hospital And Station Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Promptly after the death of a patient in a station or hospital of the Service, an inventory of his money and effects left therein shall be made by two or more officers or employees of the Service designated for such purpose by the officer in charge.
