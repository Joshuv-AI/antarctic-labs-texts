14 C.F.R. § 158.21 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:14:158.21
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-158.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 158 (Passenger Facility Charges (Pfc'S)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart specifies the consultation and application requirements under which a public agency may obtain approval to impose a PFC and use PFC revenue on a project. This subpart also establishes the procedure for the Administrator's review and approval of applications and amendments and establishes requirements for use of excess PFC revenue.
