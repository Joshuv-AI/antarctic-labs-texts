7 U.S.C. § 1639a — Applicability

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1639a
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 38 (Distribution And Marketing Of Agricultural Products); Subchapter V (National Bioengineered Food Disclosure Standard). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
This subchapter shall apply to any claim in a disclosure that a food bears that indicates that the food is a bioengineered food.
(b) Application of definition
The definition of the term “bioengineering” under section 1639 of this title shall not affect any other definition, program, rule, or regulation of the Federal Government.
(c) Application to foods
This subchapter shall apply only to a food subject to—
(1)
the labeling requirements under the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (21 U.S.C. 301 et seq.); or
(2)
the labeling requirements under the Federal Meat Inspection Act (21 U.S.C. 601 et seq.), the Poultry Products Inspection Act (21 U.S.C. 451 et seq.), or the Egg Products Inspection Act (21 U.S.C. 1031 et seq.) only if—
(A)
the most predominant ingredient of the food would independently be subject to the labeling requirements under the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (21 U.S.C. 301 et seq.); or
(B)
(i)
the most predominant ingredient of the food is broth, stock, water, or a similar solution; and
(ii)
the second-most predominant ingredient of the food would independently be subject to the labeling requirements under the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (21 U.S.C. 301 et seq.).
