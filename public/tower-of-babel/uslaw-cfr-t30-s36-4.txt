30 C.F.R. § 36.4 — Mobile diesel-powered transportation equipment for which certificates of approval may be granted.
Citation: us:cfr:30:36.4
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-36.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 36 (Approval Requirements For Permissible Mobile Diesel-Powered Transportation Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Certificates of approval will be granted for completely assembled mobile diesel-powered transportation equipment only. Subassemblies or components may be granted letters of certification in accordance with § 36.5.
