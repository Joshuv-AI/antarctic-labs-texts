34 C.F.R. § 663.5 — What is the duration of a fellowship?
Citation: us:cfr:34:663.5
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-663.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 663 (Fulbright-Hays Faculty Research Abroad Fellowship Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A fellowship is for a period of not fewer than three nor more than twelve months.
(b) A fellowship may not be renewed.
