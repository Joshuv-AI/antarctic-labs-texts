48 C.F.R. § 9903.202-2 — 9903.202-2 Impracticality of submission.
Citation: us:cfr:48:9903.202-2
Creator: Chapter 99—Cost Accounting Standards Board, Office Of Federal Procurement Policy, Office Of Management And Budget
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-9903.202-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 9903 (Contract Coverage). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agency head may determine that it is impractical to secure the Disclosure Statement, although submission is required, and authorize contract award without obtaining the Statement. He shall, within 30 days of having done so, submit a report to the Cost Accounting Standards Board setting forth all material facts. This authority may not be delegated.
