38 C.F.R. § 26.84 — Non-compliance by applicants.
Citation: us:cfr:38:26.84
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-26.84
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 26 (National Environmental Policy Act Implementing Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

VA will promptly notify the applicant that VA requires appropriate action to comply with NEPA when VA becomes aware that an applicant is about to take action that would have an adverse environmental effect or limit the choice of reasonable alternatives prior to the issuance of a CATEX, FONSI, or ROD.
