42 U.S.C. § 11272 — Dissemination
Citation: us:usc:42:11272
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section11272
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 118 (Alzheimer’S Disease And Related Dementias Research); Subchapter IV (Research Relating To Services For Individuals With Alzheimer’S Disease And Related Dementias And Their Families); Part 3 (Responsibilities Of The Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator of the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services shall disseminate the results of research conducted under this part to appropriate professional entities and to the public.
