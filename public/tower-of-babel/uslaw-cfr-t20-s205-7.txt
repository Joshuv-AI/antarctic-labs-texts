20 C.F.R. § 205.7 — Termination of employee representative status.
Citation: us:cfr:20:205.7
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-205.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 205 (Employee Representative). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The employee representative status of any individual shall terminate whenever the individual or the organization by whom he or she is employed loses any of the characteristics essential to the existence of employee representative status.
