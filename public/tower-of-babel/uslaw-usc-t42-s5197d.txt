42 U.S.C. § 5197d — Applicability of subchapter
Citation: us:usc:42:5197d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section5197d
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 68 (Disaster Relief); Subchapter IV–B (Emergency Preparedness); Part B (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of this subchapter shall be applicable to the United States, its States, Territories and possessions, and the District of Columbia, and their political subdivisions.
