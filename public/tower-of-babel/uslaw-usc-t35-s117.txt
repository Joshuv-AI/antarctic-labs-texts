35 U.S.C. § 117 — Death or incapacity of inventor
Citation: us:usc:35:117
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title35-section117
Context: United States Code, Title 35 (Patents); Part II (Patentability Of Inventions And Grant Of Patents); Chapter 11 (Application For Patent). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Legal representatives of deceased inventors and of those under legal incapacity may make application for patent upon compliance with the requirements and on the same terms and conditions applicable to the inventor.
