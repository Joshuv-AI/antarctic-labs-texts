50 C.F.R. § 219.64 — Prohibitions.
Citation: us:cfr:50:219.64
Creator: National Marine Fisheries Service, National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-219.64
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 219 (Regulations Governing The Taking And Importing Of Marine Mammals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except for the takings described in §§ 219.61 and authorized by a LOA issued under 216.106 of this chapter and this subpart, it shall be unlawful for any person to do any of the following in connection with the activities described in § 219.61:
(a) Violate, or fail to comply with, the terms, conditions, and requirements of this subpart or a LOA issued under § 216.106 of this chapter and this subpart;
(b) Take any marine mammal species or stock not specified in such LOA;
(c) Take any marine mammal in any manner other than as specified in the LOA;
(d) Take a marine mammal specified in such LOA after NMFS determines such taking results in more than a negligible impact on the species or stocks of such marine mammal; or
(e) Take a marine mammal specified in such LOA after NMFS determines such taking results in an unmitigable adverse impact on the species or stock of such marine mammal for taking for subsistence uses.
