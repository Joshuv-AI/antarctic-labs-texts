18 U.S.C. § 5042 — Revocation of probation
Citation: us:usc:18:5042
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section5042
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part IV (Correction Of Youthful Offenders); Chapter 403 (Juvenile Delinquency). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any juvenile probationer shall be accorded notice and a hearing with counsel before his probation can be revoked.
