28 C.F.R. § 31.203 — Open meetings and public access to records.
Citation: us:cfr:28:31.203
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-31.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 31 (Ojjdp Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The State advisory group established pursuant to section 223(a)(3) will follow applicable State open meeting and public access laws and regulations in the conduct of meetings and the maintenance of records relating to their functions.
