7 C.F.R. § 1466.43 — Incentive contract period.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1466.43
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1466.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1466 (Environmental Quality Incentives Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) NRCS shall apply science-based criteria to determine an appropriate contract period to achieve desired conservation benefits.
(b) The period determined as appropriate under paragraph (a) of this section shall not be less than 5 years nor exceed 10 years.
