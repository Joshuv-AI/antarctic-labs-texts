46 C.F.R. § 386.3 — Preservation of property.
Citation: us:cfr:46:386.3
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-386.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 386 (Regulations Governing Public Buildings And Grounds At The United States Merchant Marine Academy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Prohibited actions against property on the Academy grounds are improper disposal of rubbish; theft of or damage to property; throwing articles from an Academy building; and climbing on statues, fountains or any part of a building.
