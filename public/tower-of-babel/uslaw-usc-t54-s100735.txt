54 U.S.C. § 100735 — Civil actions for just compensation by mining claim holders
Citation: us:usc:54:100735
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section100735
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 1007 (Resource Management); Subchapter III (Mining Activity Within System Units). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The holder of any patented or unpatented mining claim subject to this subchapter that believes the holder has suffered a loss by operation of this subchapter, or by orders or regulations issued pursuant to this subchapter, may bring a civil action in United States district court to recover just compensation, which shall be awarded if the court finds that the loss constitutes a taking of property compensable under the Constitution.
