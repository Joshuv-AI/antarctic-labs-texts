26 U.S.C. § 6407 — Date of allowance of refund or credit
Citation: us:usc:26:6407
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section6407
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 65 (Abatements, Credits, And Refunds); Subchapter A (Procedure In General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The date on which the Secretary first authorizes the scheduling of an overassessment in respect of any internal revenue tax shall be considered as the date of allowance of refund or credit in respect of such tax.
