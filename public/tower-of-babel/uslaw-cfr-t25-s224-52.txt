25 C.F.R. § 224.52 — What may a Tribe include in a TERA?
Citation: us:cfr:25:224.52
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-224.52
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 224 (Tribal Energy Resource Agreements Under The Indian Tribal Energy Development And Self Determination Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A TERA under this part:
(a) May include development of all or part of a Tribe's energy resources;
(b) Must specify the type of energy resource included;
(c) May include assumption by the Tribe of certain activities normally carried out by the Department, except for inherently Federal functions; and
(d) Must specify the services or resources related to the specific activity related to energy resource development that the Tribe proposes to assume from the Department.
