22 U.S.C. § 1473 — Use of existing Government property and facilities
Citation: us:usc:22:1473
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1473
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 18 (United States Information And Educational Exchange Programs); Subchapter VII (Administrative Procedure). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In carrying on activities under this chapter which require the utilization of Government property and facilities, maximum use shall be made of existing Government property and facilities.
