7 C.F.R. § 985.21 — Eligibility.
Citation: us:cfr:7:985.21
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-985.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 985 (Marketing Order Regulating The Handling Of Spearmint Oil Produced In The Far West). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each member and alternate member of the Committee shall be, at the time of selection and during the term of office, a producer, or an officer or employee of a producer, in the district for which selected: Provided, That these requirements should not apply to the public member and alternate member.
