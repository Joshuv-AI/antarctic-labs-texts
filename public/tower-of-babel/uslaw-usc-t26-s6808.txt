26 U.S.C. § 6808 — Special provisions relating to stamps
Citation: us:usc:26:6808
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section6808
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 69 (General Provisions Relating To Stamps). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For special provisions on stamps relating to—
(1)
Distilled spirits and fermented liquors, see chapter 51.
(2)
Machine guns and short-barrelled firearms, see chapter 53.
(3)
Tobacco, snuff, cigars and cigarettes, see chapter 52.
