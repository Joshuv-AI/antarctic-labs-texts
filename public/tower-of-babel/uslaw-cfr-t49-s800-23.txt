49 C.F.R. § 800.23 — Delegation to the administrative law judges, Office of Administrative Law Judges.
Citation: us:cfr:49:800.23
Creator: National Transportation Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-800.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 800 (Administrative Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board delegates to the administrative law judges the authority generally detailed in its procedural regulations at Part 821 of this chapter.
