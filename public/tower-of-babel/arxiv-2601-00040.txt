The paper is structured as follows: In Section 1 we recall the basic definitions and properties of Hom-dendriform and Hom-diassociative algebras. In Section 2 we introduce the notion of Hom-quadri-dendriform algebras and define relative averaging operators on Hom-dendriform algebras. 2.
Hom-dendriform algebra and Hom-diassociative algebra

In this section we introduce the notions of Hom-dendriform algebras and Hom-diassociative algebras

2.1.
Hom-dendriform algebras

Dendriform algebras were introduced by Loday in
[
18
]
. Dendriform algebras are algebras with two operations which dichotomize the notion of associative algebra. We
generalize now this notion by twisting the identities by a linear map.

Definition 2.1
.

A Hom-dendriform algebra is a quadruple
(D,\ \prec,\ \succ,\ \alpha)
where
D
is a
\mathbb{K}
-vector space the operations
\prec,\ \succ:\ D\times D\to D
are bilinear maps and
\alpha:\ D\to D
is a linear map (called the structure map) such that for all
x,\ y,z\in D
the following identities hold:

\displaystyle\alpha(x)\prec(y\prec z+y\succ z)

\displaystyle=(x\prec y)\prec\alpha(z)

(1)

\displaystyle\alpha(x)\succ(y\prec z)

\displaystyle=(x\succ y)\prec\alpha(z)

(2)

\displaystyle\alpha(x)\succ(y\succ z)

\displaystyle=(x\prec y+x\succ y)\succ\alpha(z).

(3)

(D,\ \prec,\ \succ,\ \alpha)
is said to be a multiplicative Hom-algebra if
\forall x,\ y\in D
we have

\alpha(x\prec y)=\alpha(x)\prec\alpha(y)\quad\text{and}\quad\alpha(x\succ y)=\alpha(x)\succ\alpha(y).

Definition 2.2
.

Given two Hom-dendriform algebras
(D,\ \prec,\ \succ,\ \alpha)
and
(D^{\prime},\ \prec^{\prime},\ \succ^{\prime},\ \alpha^{\prime})
. A Hom-dendriform homomorphism is a linear map
T:\ D\to D^{\prime}
that satisfies the following compatibility conditions

T(x\prec y)=T(x)\prec^{\prime}T(y),\quad T(x\succ y)=T(x)\succ^{\prime}T(y)\quad\ and\ T\circ\alpha=\alpha^{\prime}\circ T

for all
x,\ y\in D
.

In particular if
T
is bijective then
(D,\ \prec,\ \succ,\ \alpha)
and
(D^{\prime},\ \prec^{\prime},\ \succ^{\prime},\ \alpha^{\prime})
are said to be isomorphic.

Example 2.3
.

Let
(D,\ \prec,\ \succ,\ \alpha)
be a Hom-dendriform algebra with a basis
\left\{e_{1},\ e_{2},\ e_{3}\right\}
whose non-zero product are given by

D(\eta)=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}e_{1}\prec e_{2}=\eta e_{3}&e_{2}\succ e_{1}=e_{3}\\
e_{2}\prec e_{2}=-\frac{1}{2}e_{3}&e_{2}\succ e_{2}=e_{1}+\frac{1}{4}e_{3}\\
e_{3}\prec e_{2}=e_{3}&e_{3}\succ e_{2}=\eta e_{1}+e_{3}\end{array}\right.,\quad\alpha=\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0\\
0&0&0\\
0&0&b\end{pmatrix},\quad b,\ \eta\in\mathbb{R}.

Definition 2.4
.

Let
(D,\ \prec,\ \succ,\ \alpha)
be a Hom-dendriform algebra. A representation of
D
is given
by a vector space
M
together with four bilinear maps

\prec_{l},\ \succ_{l}:\ D\times M\to M,\quad\prec_{r},\ \succ_{r}:\ M\times D\to M

representing the left and right actions. These maps must satisfy the following nine compatibility conditions:
\forall\ x,\ y\in D,\ m\in M
we have

(I) Left compatibility conditions

\displaystyle(x\prec y)\prec_{l}\beta(m)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\prec_{l}(y\prec_{l}m+y\succ_{l}m),

\displaystyle(x\succ y)\prec_{l}\beta(m)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\succ_{l}(y\prec_{l}m),

\displaystyle\alpha(x)\succ_{l}(y\succ_{l}m)

\displaystyle=(x\prec y+x\succ y)\succ_{l}\beta(m).

(II) Right compatibility conditions

\displaystyle\beta(m)\prec_{r}(x\prec y+x\succ y)

\displaystyle=(m\prec_{r}x)\prec_{r}\alpha(y),

\displaystyle\beta(m)\succ_{r}(x\prec y)

\displaystyle=(m\succ_{r}x)\prec_{r}\alpha(y),

\displaystyle(m\prec_{r}x+m\succ_{r}x)\succ_{r}\alpha(y)

\displaystyle=\beta(m)\succ_{r}(x\succ y).

(III) Mixed compatibility conditions

\displaystyle(x\prec_{l}m)\prec_{r}\alpha(y)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\prec_{l}(m\prec_{r}y+m\succ_{r}y),

\displaystyle(x\succ_{l}m)\prec_{r}\alpha(y)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\succ_{l}(m\prec_{r}y),

\displaystyle(x\prec_{l}m+x\succ_{l}m)\succ_{r}\alpha(y)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\succ_{l}(m\succ_{r}y).

Any Hom-dendriform algebra
(D,\ \prec,\ \succ,\ \alpha)
is a representation of itself with

\prec=\prec_{l}=\prec_{r}\ and\ \succ=\succ_{l}=\succ_{r}.

This representation is known as the adjoint representation.

3.
Hom-quadri-dendriform algebra

In this section we introduce the notions of Hom-quadri-dendriform algebras generalizing the classical quadri-dendriform algebras to Hom-algebras setting. Next we introduce relative averaging
operators on Hom-dendriform algebras. We show that a relative averaging operator induces a
Hom-quadri-dendriform algebra. Finally we show that any Hom-quadri-dendriform algebra can be
embedded into an averaging Hom-dendriform algebra.

Definition 3.1
.

A Hom-quadri-dendriform algebra is a tuple

(D,\ \prec_{\vdash},\ \prec_{\dashv},\ \succ_{\vdash},\ \succ_{\dashv},\ \alpha)
where
D
is a vector space the operations
\prec_{\vdash},\ \prec_{\dashv},\ \succ_{\vdash},\ \ \succ_{\dashv}:\ D\times D\to D
are bilinear and
\alpha:\ D\to D
is a linear map
such that for all
x,\ y,\ z\in D
the following identities hold

\displaystyle(x\prec_{\vdash}y)\prec_{\vdash}\alpha(z)

\displaystyle=(x\prec_{\dashv}y)\prec_{\vdash}\alpha(z)=\alpha(x)\prec_{\vdash}(y\prec_{\vdash}z+y\succ_{\vdash}z)

(12)

\displaystyle(x\succ_{\vdash}y)\prec_{\vdash}\alpha(z)

\displaystyle=(x\succ_{\dashv}y)\prec_{\vdash}\alpha(z)=\alpha(x)\succ_{\vdash}(y\prec_{\vdash}z)

(13)

\displaystyle\alpha(x)\succ_{\vdash}(y\succ_{\vdash}z)

\displaystyle=(x\prec_{\vdash}y+x\succ_{\vdash}y)\succ_{\vdash}\alpha(z)=(x\prec_{\dashv}y+x\succ_{\dashv}y)\succ_{\vdash}\alpha(z)

(14)

\displaystyle\alpha(x)\succ_{\vdash}(y\succ_{\vdash}z)

\displaystyle=(x\prec_{\dashv}y+x\succ_{\vdash}y)\succ_{\vdash}\alpha(z)=(x\prec_{\vdash}y+x\succ_{\dashv}y)\succ_{\vdash}\alpha(z)

(15)

\displaystyle(x\prec_{\vdash}y)\prec_{\dashv}\alpha(z)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\prec_{\vdash}(y\prec_{\dashv}z+y\succ_{\dashv}z)

(16)

\displaystyle(x\succ_{\vdash}y)\prec_{\dashv}\alpha(z)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\succ_{\vdash}(y\prec_{\dashv}z)

(17)

\displaystyle\alpha(x)\succ_{\vdash}(y\succ_{\dashv}z)

\displaystyle=(x\prec_{\vdash}y+x\succ_{\vdash}y)\succ_{\dashv}\alpha(z)

(18)

\displaystyle(x\prec_{\dashv}y)\prec_{\dashv}\alpha(z)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\prec_{\dashv}(y\prec_{\vdash}z+y\succ_{\vdash}z)=\alpha(x)\prec_{\dashv}(y\prec_{\dashv}z+y\succ_{\dashv}z)

(19)

\displaystyle(x\prec_{\dashv}y)\prec_{\dashv}\alpha(z)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\prec_{\dashv}(y\prec_{\vdash}z+y\succ_{\dashv}z)=\alpha(x)\prec_{\dashv}(y\prec_{\dashv}z+y\succ_{\vdash}z)

(20)

\displaystyle(x\succ_{\dashv}y)\prec_{\dashv}\alpha(z)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\succ_{\dashv}(y\prec_{\vdash}z)=\alpha(x)\succ_{\dashv}(y\prec_{\dashv}z)

(21)

\displaystyle\alpha(x)\succ_{\dashv}(y\succ_{\vdash}z)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\succ_{\dashv}(y\succ_{\dashv}z)=(x\prec_{\dashv}y+x\succ_{\dashv}y)\succ_{\dashv}\alpha(z)

(22)

(D,\ \prec_{\vdash},\ \prec_{\dashv},\ \succ_{\vdash},\ \succ_{\dashv},\ \alpha)
is said to be a multiplicative Hom-algebra if
\forall x,\ y,\ \in D
we have

\displaystyle\alpha(x\prec_{\vdash}y)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\prec_{\vdash}^{\prime}\alpha(y),\quad\alpha(x\prec_{\dashv}y)=\alpha(x)\prec_{\dashv}^{\prime}\alpha(y),

\displaystyle\alpha(x\succ_{\vdash}y)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\succ_{\vdash}^{\prime}\alpha(y),\quad\alpha(x\succ_{\dashv}y)=\alpha(x)\succ_{\dashv}^{\prime}\alpha(y).

.

Given two Hom-quadri-dendriform algebras

\displaystyle T(x\prec_{\vdash}y)

\displaystyle=T(x)\succ_{\vdash}^{\prime}T(y),\quad T(x\succ_{\dashv}y)=T(x)\succ_{\dashv}^{\prime}T(y),

\displaystyle-(x\prec_{l}v+u\prec_{r}y+u\prec^{\prime}v)\succ^{\prime}\alpha^{\prime}(w)-(x\succ y)\succ_{l}\alpha^{\prime}(w)-(x\succ_{l}v+u\succ_{r}y+u\succ^{\prime}v)\succ_{r}\alpha(z)

\displaystyle-(x\succ_{l}v+u\succ_{r}y+u\succ^{\prime}v)\succ^{\prime}\alpha^{\prime}(w))

\displaystyle=(0,\ \alpha(x)\succ_{l}(y\succ_{l}w+v\succ_{r}z+v\succ^{\prime}w)+\alpha^{\prime}(u)\succ_{r}(y\succ z)+\alpha^{\prime}(u)\succ^{\prime}(y\succ_{l}w+v\succ_{r}z)

\displaystyle-(x\prec y)\succ_{l}\alpha^{\prime}(w)-(x\prec_{l}v+u\prec_{r}y+u\prec^{\prime}v)\succ_{r}\alpha(z)-(x\prec_{l}v+u\prec_{r}y)\succ^{{}^{\prime}}\alpha^{\prime}(w)

Then
(D\oplus D^{\prime},\ \succ_{\bowtie},\ \prec_{\bowtie},\ \alpha\otimes\alpha^{\prime})
is a Hom-dendriform algebra if and only if

is a representation of

and Eqs.(

Definition 4.3
.

A linear map
T:\ D^{\prime}\to D
is called a homomorphic relative averaging operator on the Hom-dendriform algebra
(D,\ \prec,\ \succ,\ \alpha)
with respect to an action
(D^{\prime},\ \prec^{\prime},\ \succ^{\prime},\ \prec_{l},\ \succ_{l},\ \prec_{r},\ \succ_{r},\ \alpha^{\prime})
if
T
qualifies both as a relative averaging operator and a Hom-dendriform homomorphism.

A homomorphic relative averaging operator on a Hom-dendriform algebra is also called a relative averaging operator of weight 1 on a Hom-dendriform algebra. In particular a homomorphic relative averaging operator on a Hom-dendriform algebra
(D,\ \prec,\ \succ,\ \alpha)
with respect to the adjoint representation is called a homomorphic averaging operator.

Definition 4.4
.

A Hom-six-dendriform algebra is a tuple
(D,\prec_{\perp},\succ_{\perp},\prec_{\vdash},\succ_{\vdash},\prec_{\dashv},\succ_{\dashv},\alpha)

consisting of a Hom-dendriform algebra
(D,\prec_{\perp},\succ_{\perp},\alpha)
and a Hom-quadri-dendriform algebra
(D,\prec_{\vdash},\succ_{\vdash},\prec_{\dashv},\succ_{\dashv},\alpha)

such that for all
x,\ y,\ z\in D

\displaystyle(x\prec_{\vdash}y)\prec_{\perp}\alpha(z)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\prec_{\vdash}(y\prec_{\perp}z+y\succ_{\perp}z)

(33)

\displaystyle(x\succ_{\vdash}y)\prec_{\perp}\alpha(z)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\succ_{\vdash}(y\prec_{\perp}z)

(34)

\displaystyle\alpha(x)\succ_{\vdash}(y\succ_{\perp}z)

\displaystyle=(x\prec_{\vdash}y+x\succ_{\vdash}y)\succ_{\perp}\alpha(z)

(35)

\displaystyle(x\prec_{\dashv}y)\prec_{\perp}\alpha(z)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\prec_{\perp}(y\prec_{\vdash}z+y\succ_{\vdash}z)

(36)

\displaystyle(x\succ_{\dashv}y)\prec_{\perp}\alpha(z)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\succ_{\perp}(y\prec_{\vdash}z)

(37)

\displaystyle\alpha(x)\succ_{\perp}(y\succ_{\vdash}z)

\displaystyle=(x\prec_{\dashv}y+x\succ_{\dashv}y)\succ_{\perp}\alpha(z)

(38)

\displaystyle(x\prec_{\perp}y)\prec_{\dashv}\alpha(z)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\prec_{\perp}(y\prec_{\dashv}z+y\succ_{\dashv}z)

(39)

\displaystyle(x\succ_{\perp}y)\prec_{\dashv}\alpha(z)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\succ_{\perp}(y\prec_{\dashv}z)

(40)

\displaystyle\alpha(x)\succ_{\perp}(y\succ_{\dashv}z)

\displaystyle=(x\prec_{\perp}y+x\succ_{\perp}y)\succ_{\dashv}\alpha(z)

(41)

\displaystyle(x\prec_{\perp}y)\prec_{\vdash}\alpha(z)

\displaystyle=(x\prec_{\vdash}y)\prec_{\vdash}\alpha(z)=(x\prec_{\dashv}y)\prec_{\vdash}\alpha(z)

(42)

\displaystyle(x\succ_{\perp}y)\prec_{\vdash}\alpha(z)

\displaystyle=(x\succ_{\vdash}y)\prec_{\vdash}\alpha(z)=(x\succ_{\dashv}y)\prec_{\vdash}\alpha(z)

(43)

\displaystyle(x\prec_{\perp}y)\succ_{\vdash}\alpha(z)

\displaystyle=(x\prec_{\vdash}y)\succ_{\vdash}\alpha(z)=(x\prec_{\dashv}y)\succ_{\vdash}\alpha(z)

(44)

\displaystyle(x\succ_{\perp}y)\succ_{\vdash}\alpha(z)

\displaystyle=(x\succ_{\vdash}y)\succ_{\vdash}\alpha(z)=(x\succ_{\dashv}y)\succ_{\vdash}\alpha(z)

(45)

\displaystyle\alpha(x)\prec_{\dashv}(y\prec_{\perp}z)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\prec_{\dashv}(y\prec_{\vdash}z)=\alpha(x)\prec_{\dashv}(y\prec_{\dashv}z)

(46)

\displaystyle\alpha(x)\succ_{\dashv}(y\prec_{\perp}z)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\prec_{\dashv}(y\prec_{\vdash}z)=\alpha(x)\prec_{\dashv}(y\prec_{\dashv}z)

(47)

\displaystyle\alpha(x)\succ_{\dashv}(y\succ_{\perp}z)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\succ_{\dashv}(y\succ_{\vdash}z)=\alpha(x)\succ_{\dashv}(y\succ_{\dashv}z)

(48)

\displaystyle\alpha(x)\prec_{\dashv}(y\succ_{\perp}z)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\prec_{\dashv}(y\succ_{\vdash}z)=\alpha(x)\prec_{\dashv}(y\succ_{\dashv}z).

(49)

(D,\ \prec_{\perp},\ \succ_{\perp},\ \prec_{\vdash},\ \succ_{\vdash},\ \prec_{\dashv},\ \succ_{\dashv},\ \alpha)
is said to be a multiplicative Hom-algebra if:

\displaystyle\alpha(x\prec_{\perp}y)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\prec_{\perp}\alpha(y),\quad\alpha(x\succ_{\perp}y)=\alpha(x)\succ_{\perp}\alpha(y),

\displaystyle\alpha(x\prec_{\vdash}y)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\prec_{\vdash}\alpha(y),\quad\alpha(x\succ_{\vdash}y)=\alpha(x)\succ_{\vdash}\alpha(y),

\displaystyle\alpha(x\prec_{\dashv}y)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\prec_{\dashv}\alpha(y),\quad\alpha(x\succ_{\dashv}y)=\alpha(x)\succ_{\dashv}\alpha(y).

If

\prec_{\perp}=\prec_{\vdash}=\prec_{\dashv}\ and\ \succ_{\perp}=\succ_{\vdash}=\succ_{\dashv}

then we simply obtain a Hom-dendriform algebra.

If

\prec_{\perp}=\prec_{\vdash}=\prec_{\dashv}\ and\ \succ_{\perp}=\succ_{\vdash}=\succ_{\dashv}

then we simply obtain a Hom-dendriform algebra.

Definition 4.5
.

Given two Hom-six-dendriform algebras
(D,\ \prec_{\perp},\ \succ_{\perp},\ \prec_{\vdash},\ \succ_{\vdash},\ \prec_{\dashv},\ \succ_{\dashv},\ \alpha)
and
(D^{\prime},\ \prec_{\perp}^{\prime},\ \succ_{\perp}^{\prime},\ \prec_{\vdash}^{\prime},\ \succ_{\vdash}^{\prime},\ \prec_{\dashv}^{\prime},\ \succ_{\dashv}^{\prime},\alpha^{\prime})
a Hom-six-dendriform homomorphism is a linear map
T:\ D\to D^{\prime}
that satisfies the following compatibility conditions:

\displaystyle T(x\prec_{\perp}y)

\displaystyle=T(x)\prec_{\perp}^{\prime}T(y),\quad T(x\succ_{\perp}y)=T(x)\succ_{\perp}^{\prime}T(y),

\displaystyle T(x\prec_{\vdash}y)

\displaystyle=T(x)\prec_{\vdash}^{\prime}T(y),\quad T(x\succ_{\vdash}y)=T(x)\succ_{\vdash}^{\prime}T(y),

\displaystyle T(x\prec_{\dashv}y)

\displaystyle=T(x)\prec_{\dashv}^{\prime}T(y),\quad T(x\succ_{\dashv}y)=T(x)\succ_{\dashv}^{\prime}T(y).

In particular if
T
is bijective then
(D,\ \prec_{\perp},\ \succ_{\perp},\ \prec_{\vdash},\ \succ_{\vdash},\ \prec_{\dashv},\ \succ_{\dashv},\ \alpha)
and

(D^{\prime},\ \prec_{\perp}^{\prime},\ \succ_{\perp}^{\prime},\ \prec_{\vdash}^{\prime},\ \succ_{\vdash}^{\prime},\ \prec_{\dashv}^{\prime},\ \succ_{\dashv}^{\prime},\ \alpha^{\prime})
are said to be isomorphic.

Definition 4.6
.

A Hom-triassociative algebra is a vector space
D
equipped with three binary operations

\vdash,\ \dashv,\ \perp:\ D\otimes D\to D
a linear map
\alpha:\ D\to D
such that
(D,\ \vdash,\ \dashv,\ \alpha)
constitutes a Hom-diassociative algebra
(D,\ \perp,\ \alpha)
forms an Hom-associative algebra and for all
x,\ y,\ z\in D
the following equations hold:

\displaystyle(x\dashv y)\dashv\alpha(z)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\dashv(y\perp z)

(50)

\displaystyle(x\vdash y)\perp\alpha(z)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\vdash(y\perp z)

(51)

\displaystyle(x\perp y)\dashv\alpha(z)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\perp(y\dashv z)

(52)

\displaystyle(x\perp y)\vdash\alpha(z)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\vdash(y\vdash z)

(53)

\displaystyle(x\dashv y)\perp\alpha(z)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\perp(y\vdash z).

(54)

(D,\ \vdash,\ \dashv,\ \perp,\ \alpha)
is said to be a multiplicative Hom-algebra if
\forall\ x,\ y\in D
:

\alpha(x\vdash y)=\alpha(x)\vdash\alpha(y),\quad\alpha(x\dashv y)=\alpha(x)\dashv\alpha(y)\ and\ \alpha(x\perp y)=\alpha(x)\perp\alpha(y).

Proposition 4.7
.

Let
(D,\ \prec_{\perp},\ \succ_{\perp},\ \prec_{\vdash},\ \prec_{\dashv},\ \succ_{\vdash},\ \succ_{\dashv},\ \alpha)
be a Hom-six-dendriform algebra.

Then
(D,\ \perp,\ \vdash,\ \dashv,\ \alpha)
is a Hom-triassociative algebra where the operations are defined by:

\displaystyle x\perp y

\displaystyle=x\prec_{\perp}y+x\succ_{\perp}y,

\displaystyle x\vdash y

\displaystyle=x\prec_{\vdash}y+x\succ_{\vdash}y,

\displaystyle x\dashv y

\displaystyle=x\prec_{\dashv}y+x\succ_{\dashv}y,

for all
x,\ y\in D
.

Proof.

It follows from Proposition
3.3
that
(D,\ \vdash,\ \dashv,\ \alpha)
is a Hom-di-associative algebra. The fact
that
(D,\ \prec_{\perp},\ \succ_{\perp},\ \alpha)
is a Hom-dendriform algebra means that
(D,\ \perp,\ \alpha)
is a Hom-associative algebra. Next for any
x,\ y,\ z\in D

by Eqs.(
12
)-(
16
) and (
17
)-(
22
) we have

\displaystyle(x\dashv y)\dashv\alpha(z)

\displaystyle=(x\prec_{\dashv}y+x\succ_{\dashv}y)\prec_{\dashv}\alpha(z)+(x\prec_{\dashv}y+x\succ_{\dashv}y)\succ_{\dashv}\alpha(z)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\prec_{\dashv}(y\prec_{\vdash}z+y\succ_{\vdash}z)+\alpha(x)\succ_{\dashv}(y\prec_{\dashv}z)+\alpha(x)\succ_{\dashv}(y\succ_{\vdash}z)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\prec_{\dashv}(y\prec_{\perp}z+y\succ_{\perp}z)+\alpha(x)\succ_{\dashv}(y\prec_{\perp}z+y\succ_{\perp}z)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\dashv(y\perp z)

\displaystyle(x\perp y)\dashv\alpha(z)

\displaystyle=(x\prec_{\perp}y+x\succ_{\perp}y)\prec_{\dashv}\alpha(z)+(x\prec_{\perp}y+x\succ_{\perp}y)\succ_{\dashv}\alpha(z)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\prec_{\perp}(y\prec_{\dashv}z+y\succ_{\dashv}z)+\alpha(x)\succ_{\perp}(y\prec_{\dashv}z+y\succ_{\dashv}z)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\perp(y\dashv z)

\displaystyle(x\dashv y)\perp\alpha(z)

\displaystyle=(x\prec_{\dashv}y+x\succ_{\dashv}y)\prec_{\perp}\alpha(z)+(x\prec_{\dashv}y+x\succ_{\dashv}y)\succ_{\perp}\alpha(z)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\prec_{\perp}(y\prec_{\vdash}z+y\succ_{\vdash}z)+\alpha(x)\succ_{\perp}(y\prec_{\vdash}z+y\succ_{\vdash}z)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\perp(y\vdash z)

\displaystyle(x\vdash y)\vdash\alpha(z)

\displaystyle=(x\prec_{\vdash}y+x\succ_{\vdash}y)\prec_{\vdash}\alpha(z)+(x\prec_{\vdash}y+x\succ_{\vdash}y)\succ_{\vdash}\alpha(z)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\prec_{\vdash}(y\prec_{\vdash}z+y\succ_{\vdash}z)+\alpha(x)\succ_{\vdash}(y\prec_{\vdash}z+y\succ_{\vdash}z)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\vdash(y\vdash z)

\displaystyle(x\perp y)\vdash\alpha(z)

\displaystyle=(x\prec_{\perp}y+x\succ_{\perp}y)\prec_{\vdash}\alpha(z)+(x\prec_{\perp}y+x\succ_{\perp}y)\succ_{\vdash}\alpha(z)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\prec_{\vdash}(y\prec_{\vdash}z+y\succ_{\vdash}z)+\alpha(x)\succ_{\vdash}(y\prec_{\vdash}z+y\succ_{\vdash}z)

\displaystyle=\alpha(x)\vdash(y\vdash z)

This completes the proof.
∎

Theorem 4.8
.

Let
T:\ D^{\prime}\to D
be a homomorphic relative averaging operator on the Hom-dendriform algebra
(D,\ \prec,\ \succ,\alpha)
with respect to an action
(D^{\prime},\ \prec^{\prime},\ \succ^{\prime},\ \prec_{\ell},\ \succ_{\ell},\ \prec_{r},\ \succ_{r},\ \alpha^{\prime})
. Then

(D^{\prime},\ \prec_{\perp},\ \succ_{\perp},\ \prec^{T}_{\vdash},\ \prec^{T}_{\dashv},\ \succ^{T}_{\vdash},\ \succ^{T}_{\dashv},\ \alpha^{\prime})
is a Hom-six-dendriform algebra, where

\displaystyle u\prec_{\perp}v

\displaystyle=u\prec^{\prime}v,\quad u\succ_{\perp}v=u\succ^{\prime}v,

\displaystyle u\prec^{T}_{\vdash}v

\displaystyle=Tu\prec_{l}v,\quad u\prec^{T}_{\dashv}v=u\prec_{r}Tv,

\displaystyle u\succ^{T}_{\vdash}v

\displaystyle=Tu\succ_{l}v,\quad u\succ^{T}_{\dashv}v=u\succ_{r}Tv

Proof.

We have already
(D^{\prime},\ \prec_{\perp},\ \succ_{\perp},\ \alpha^{\prime})
is a Hom-dendriform algebra. Moreover it following from Proposition
3.11
that
(D^{\prime},\ \prec^{T}_{\vdash},\ \prec^{T}_{\dashv},\ \succ^{T}_{\vdash},\ \succ^{T}_{\dashv},\ \alpha^{\prime})

is a Hom-quadri-dendriform algebra. Now for any
u,\ v,\ w\in D^{\prime}
according to Eqs.(
24
)-(
32
), we have

\displaystyle(u\prec^{T}_{\vdash}v)\prec_{\perp}\alpha^{\prime}(w)

\displaystyle=(Tu\prec_{\ell}v)\prec^{\prime}\alpha^{\prime}(w)=\alpha(Tu)\prec_{\ell}(v\prec_{\perp}w+v\succ_{\perp}w)

\displaystyle=T\alpha^{\prime}(u)\prec_{\ell}(v\prec_{\perp}w+v\succ_{\perp}w)=\alpha^{\prime}(u)\prec^{T}_{\vdash}(v\prec_{\perp}w+v\succ_{\perp}w)

\displaystyle(u\succ^{T}_{\vdash}v)\prec_{\perp}\alpha^{\prime}(w)

\displaystyle=(Tu\succ_{\ell}v)\prec^{\prime}\alpha^{\prime}(w)=\alpha(Tu)\succ_{\ell}(v\prec^{\prime}w)

\displaystyle=T\alpha^{\prime}(u)\succ_{\ell}(v\prec^{\prime}w)=\alpha^{\prime}(u)\succ^{T}_{\vdash}(v\prec^{\prime}w)=\alpha^{\prime}(u)\succ^{T}_{\vdash}(v\succ_{\perp}w)

\displaystyle\alpha^{\prime}(u)\succ^{T}_{\vdash}(v\succ_{\perp}w)

\displaystyle=T\alpha^{\prime}(u)\succ_{\ell}(v\prec^{\prime}w)=(Tu\prec_{\ell}v+Tu\succ_{\ell}v)\succ^{\prime}\alpha^{\prime}(w)

\displaystyle(u\prec^{T}_{\dashv}v)\prec_{\perp}\alpha^{\prime}(w)

\displaystyle=(u\prec_{r}Tv)\prec^{\prime}\alpha^{\prime}(w)=\alpha^{\prime}(u)\prec^{\prime}(Tv\prec_{\ell}w+Tv\succ_{\ell}w)

\displaystyle=\alpha^{\prime}(u)\prec_{\perp}(v\prec^{T}_{\vdash}w+v\succ^{T}_{\vdash}w)

\displaystyle(u\succ^{T}_{\dashv}v)\prec_{\perp}\alpha^{\prime}(w)

\displaystyle=(u\succ_{r}Tv)\prec^{\prime}\alpha^{\prime}(w)=\alpha^{\prime}(u)\succ^{\prime}(Tv\prec_{\ell}w)=\alpha^{\prime}(u)\succ_{\perp}(v\prec^{T}_{\vdash}w)

\displaystyle\alpha^{\prime}(u)\succ_{\perp}(v\succ^{T}_{\vdash}w)

\displaystyle=\alpha^{\prime}(u)\succ^{\prime}(Tv\succ_{\ell}w)=(u\prec_{r}Tv+u\succ_{r}Tv)\succ^{\prime}\alpha^{\prime}(w)

\displaystyle=(u\prec^{T}_{\vdash}v+u\succ^{T}_{\vdash}v)\succ_{\perp}\alpha^{\prime}(w)

\displaystyle(u\prec_{\perp}v)\prec^{T}_{\vdash}\alpha^{\prime}(w)

\displaystyle=(u\prec^{\prime}v)\prec_{r}T\alpha^{\prime}(w)=(u\prec^{\prime}v)\prec_{r}\alpha(Tw)

\displaystyle=\alpha^{\prime}(u)\prec^{\prime}(v\prec_{r}Tw+v\succ_{r}Tw)=\alpha^{\prime}(u)\prec_{\perp}(v\prec^{T}_{\vdash}w+v\succ^{T}_{\vdash}w)

\displaystyle(u\succ_{\perp}v)\prec^{T}_{\vdash}\alpha^{\prime}(w)

\displaystyle=(u\succ^{\prime}v)\prec_{r}T\alpha^{\prime}(w)=(u\succ^{\prime}v)\prec_{r}\alpha(Tw)

\displaystyle=\alpha^{\prime}(u)\succ^{\prime}(v\prec_{r}Tw)=\alpha^{\prime}(u)\succ_{\perp}(v\prec^{T}_{\vdash}w)

Similarly it can be proved that other equations

Corollary 4.9
.

Let
(D,\ \prec,\ \succ,\ \alpha)
be a Hom-dendriform algebra and
T:\ D\to D
be a homomorphic averaging operator. Then
(D^{\prime},\ \prec_{\perp},\ \succ_{\perp},\ \prec^{T}_{\vdash},\ \prec^{T}_{\dashv},\ \succ^{T}_{\vdash},\ \succ^{T}_{\dashv},\ \alpha)
is a Hom-six-dendriform algebra where

\prec_{\perp}=\prec,\quad\succ_{\perp}=\succ,\quad x\prec^{T}_{\vdash}y=Tx\prec y,\quad x\prec^{T}_{\dashv}y=x\prec Ty,\quad x\succ^{T}_{\vdash}y=Tx\succ y,\quad x\succ^{T}_{\dashv}y=x\succ Ty

for all
x,\ y\in D.

Theorem 4.10
.

Every Hom-six-dendriform algebra can induce a homomorphic relative averaging operator on a Hom-dendriform algebra with respect to a action.

Proof.

Let
(D,\ \prec_{\perp},\ \succ_{\perp},\ \prec_{\vdash},\ \prec_{\dashv},\ \succ_{\vdash},\ \succ_{\dashv},\ \alpha)
be a Hom-six-dendriform algebra. The vector subspace
I_{D}
generated by

\left\{x\prec_{\dashv}y-x\prec_{\vdash}y,\quad x\succ_{\dashv}y-x\succ_{\vdash}y\;\middle|\;x,\ y\in D\right\}

is an ideal of
D
. The quotient algebra

(D/I_{D},\ \prec,\ \succ,\ \overline{\alpha})

is a Hom-dendriform algebra denoted by
D_{Dend}
where

\overline{\alpha}(\overline{x})=\overline{\alpha(x)},\quad\overline{x}\prec\overline{y}=\overline{x\prec_{\vdash}y}=\overline{x\prec_{\dashv}y},\quad\overline{x}\succ\overline{y}=\overline{x\succ_{\vdash}y}=\overline{x\succ_{\dashv}y}

for all
\overline{x},\ \overline{y}\in D/I_{D}.

On the other hand
(D,\ \prec_{\perp},\ \succ_{\perp},\ \alpha)
is a Hom-dendriform algebra denoted by
D_{\perp}
.

D_{\perp}
is a
D_{Dend}
-module defined by the actions:

\displaystyle\overline{x}\prec_{l}\overline{y}

\displaystyle=x\prec_{\vdash}y,\quad\overline{x}\succ_{l}y=x\succ_{\vdash}y,

\displaystyle y\prec_{r}\overline{x}

\displaystyle=y\prec_{\dashv}x,\quad y\succ_{r}\overline{x}=y\succ_{\dashv}x.

for all
x,\ y\in D_{\perp}
. Moreover the quotient map
T:\ D_{\perp}\to D_{Dend}\quad x\mapsto\overline{x}
is a homomorphic relative averaging operator.
∎
