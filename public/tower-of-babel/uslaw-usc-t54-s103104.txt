54 U.S.C. § 103104 — Recovery of costs associated with special use permits
Citation: us:usc:54:103104
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section103104
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 1031 (Appropriations And Accounting). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the Service may recover all costs of providing necessary services associated with special use permits. The reimbursements shall be credited to the appropriation current at that time.
