12 U.S.C. § 1735f–1 — Waiver of deduction on assignment of property to Secretary in lieu of foreclosure
Citation: us:usc:12:1735f–1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section1735f–1
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 13 (National Housing); Subchapter V (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter, from and after November 3, 1966, the Secretary, under such terms and conditions as he may approve, may waive all or a part of the 1 per centum deduction otherwise made from insurance benefits with respect to multifamily housing or land development mortgages assigned to him, where the assignment is made at his request in lieu of foreclosure of the mortgage.
