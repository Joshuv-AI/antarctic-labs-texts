41 C.F.R. § 105-74.660 — -74.660 Recipient.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-74.660
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-74.660
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-74 (74—Governmentwide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Recipient means any individual, corporation, partnership, association, unit of government (except a Federal agency) or legal entity, however organized, that receives an award directly from a Federal agency.
