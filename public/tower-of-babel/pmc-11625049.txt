Author Correction: Infrared laser–induced gene expression in single cells characterized by quantitative imaging in Physcomitrium patens
Authors: Takumi Tomoi, Yuka Yoshida, Suguru Ohe, Yukiko Kabeya, Mitsuyasu Hasebe, Tomohiro Morohoshi, Takashi Murata, Joe Sakamoto, Yosuke Tamada, Yasuhiro Kamei
Published: 2024

Correction to: Communications Biology 10.1038/s42003-024-07141-1, published online 06 November 2024

The original version of the Article contained errors in the text and figure captions.

In the original version of the Article, the figure caption in Figures 2 and 3 incorrectly stated “Ratio of chloronemal cells…” which has now been corrected to “Relative distribution of chloronemal cells…”.

In the first paragraph under the section “Quantitative analysis of gene induction in single cells,” the original version of the Article stated “p2 values were higher at lower IR laser power output for both irradiation durations (Fig. 5b), indicating that gentle heating induces NmYG expression and, thus, NmYG accumulation more rapidly.” This has now been corrected to “Quantitative analysis of gene induction in single cells,” the original version of the Article stated “p2 values were higher at lower IR laser power output for both irradiation durations (Fig. 5b), indicating that gentle heating induces NmYG expression, and thus NmYG accumulation more rapidly.”

Lastly, the figure caption in Figure 5 incorrectly contained the statement “Increasingly darker red lines reflect progressively stronger (a), swifter (b), or later (c) induction of NmYG.” which belongs in the Supplementary Fig. caption. The updated Article has removed this statement.

This has now been corrected in the PDF and HTML versions of the Article.
