Abstract
. The study of sums of finite sets of integers has mostly concentrated on sets with small sumsets (Freiman’s theorem and related work) and on sets with large sumsets (Sidon sets and B h B_{h} -sets). This paper considers the sets ℛ 𝐙 ​ ( h , k ) \mathcal{R}_{\mathbf{Z}}(h,k) and ℛ 𝐙 n ​ ( h , k ) \mathcal{R}_{\mathbf{Z}^{n}}(h,k) of all sizes of h h -fold sums of sets of k k integers or of k k lattice points, and the geometric and computational complexity of the sets ℛ 𝐙 ​ ( h , k ) \mathcal{R}_{\mathbf{Z}}(h,k) and ℛ 𝐙 n ​ ( h , k ) \mathcal{R}_{\mathbf{Z}^{n}}(h,k) .

By abuse of notation, we write

Theorem 1

Let

Let

be a positive integer.
If there exist an integer

(3)

(6)

\displaystyle\geq a_{j_{1}+1}-a_{j_{1}}

\displaystyle(h-

Let
X
and

be additive abelian groups. Let
A
be a finite subset
of

.
The function

is a

if

is a bijection and, for all
a_{1},\ldots,a_{h},a^{\prime}_{1},\ldots,a^{\prime}_{h}\in A
, we have

a_{1}+\cdots+a_{h}=a^{\prime}_{1}+\cdots+a^{\prime}_{h}

if and only if

f(a_{1})+\cdots+f(a_{h})=f(a^{\prime}_{1})+\cdots+f(a^{\prime}_{h}).

If

is a Freiman isomorphism of order

,
then
|hA|=|hB|
.
For applications of Freiman isomorphisms, see
[
7
]
.

The
\ell^{\infty}
-norm of a vector
\mathbf{x}=(x_{1},\ldots,x_{n})\in\mathbf{R}^{n}
is

\|\mathbf{x}\|_{\infty}=\max(|x_{j}|:j\in[1,n]\}.

The proof of the following result is a standard application of

Theorem 5
.

For all positive integers
h
,
k
, and

\mathcal{R}_{\mathbf{Z}^{n}}(h,k)=\mathcal{R}_{\mathbf{Z}}(h,k).

Proof.

The group
\mathbf{Z}^{n}
contains subgroups isomorphic to
\mathbf{Z}
,
and so
\mathcal{R}_{\mathbf{Z}}(h,k)\subseteq\mathcal{R}_{\mathbf{Z}^{n}}(h,k)
.
To prove that
\mathcal{R}_{\mathbf{Z}^{n}}(h,k)\subseteq\mathcal{R}_{\mathbf{Z}}(h,k)

it suffices to show that, for every positive integer
h
and every finite

A
of lattice points, there is a finite set
B
of integers that is
Freiman isomorphic of order
h
to
A
.

Let
A\subseteq\mathbf{Z}^{n}
with
|A|=k
.
By translation, we can assume that
A\subseteq\mathbf{N}_{0}^{n}
.
Choose a positive integer

g>n\max\{\|\mathbf{a}\|_{\infty}:\mathbf{a}\in A\}.

Define
f:\mathbf{Z}^{n}\rightarrow\mathbf{Z}
by

f(a_{1},\ldots,a_{n})=\sum_{j=1}^{n}a_{j}g^{j-1}

and let

B=f(A)\subseteq\mathbf{N}_{0}.

Let
\mathbf{a}_{i}=(a_{i,1},\ldots,a_{i,n})\in A
and

\mathbf{a}^{\prime}_{i}=(a^{\prime}_{i,1},\ldots,a^{\prime}_{i,n})\in A

for all
i\in[1,h]
.
We have

\sum_{i=1}^{h}\mathbf{a}_{i}=\sum_{i=1}^{h}(a_{i,1},\ldots,a_{i,n})=\left(\sum_{i=1}^{h}a_{i,1},\ldots,\sum_{i=1}^{h}a_{i,n}\right)

and

\sum_{i=1}^{h}\mathbf{a}^{\prime}_{i}=\sum_{i=1}^{h}(a^{\prime}_{i,1},\ldots,a^{\prime}_{i,n})=\left(\sum_{i=1}^{h}a^{\prime}_{i,1},\ldots,\sum_{i=1}^{h}a^{\prime}_{i,n}\right).

Moreover,

f(\mathbf{a}_{i})=\sum_{j=1}^{n}a_{i,j}g^{j-1}\qqand f(\mathbf{a}^{\prime}_{i})=\sum_{j=1}^{n}a^{\prime}_{i,j}g^{j-1}.

We have

\sum_{i=1}^{h}f(\mathbf{a}_{i})=\sum_{i=1}^{h}\sum_{j=1}^{n}a_{i,j}g^{j-1}=\sum_{j=1}^{n}\sum_{i=1}^{h}a_{i,j}g^{j-1}

and
