12 U.S.C. § 3416 — Jurisdiction
Citation: us:usc:12:3416
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section3416
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 35 (Right To Financial Privacy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An action to enforce any provision of this chapter may be brought in any appropriate United States district court without regard to the amount in controversy within three years from the date on which the violation occurs or the date of discovery of such violation, whichever is later.
