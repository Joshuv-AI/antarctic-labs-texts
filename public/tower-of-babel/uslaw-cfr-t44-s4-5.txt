44 C.F.R. § 4.5 — What is the Administrator's obligation with respect to Federal interagency coordination?
Citation: us:cfr:44:4.5
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-4.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 4 (Intergovernmental Review Of Federal Emergency Management Agency (Fema) Programs And Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator, to the extent practicable, consults with and seeks advice from all other substantially affected Federal departments and agencies in an effort to assure full coordination between such agencies and FEMA regarding programs and activities covered under these regulations.
