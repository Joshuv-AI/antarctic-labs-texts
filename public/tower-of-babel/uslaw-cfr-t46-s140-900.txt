46 C.F.R. § 140.900 — Marine casualty reporting.
Citation: us:cfr:46:140.900
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-140.900
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 140 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each towing vessel must comply with the requirements of part 4 of this chapter for reporting marine casualties and retaining voyage records.
