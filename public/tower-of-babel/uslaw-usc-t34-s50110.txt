34 U.S.C. § 50110 — Limitation on civil justice matters
Citation: us:usc:34:50110
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section50110
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 501 (Emergency Federal Law Enforcement Assistance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Federal law enforcement assistance provided under this chapter may not be used with respect to civil justice matters except to the extent that such civil justice matters bear directly and substantially upon criminal justice matters or are inextricably intertwined with criminal justice matters.
