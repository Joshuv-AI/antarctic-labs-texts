32 C.F.R. § 239.14 — On-site inspections.
Citation: us:cfr:32:239.14
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-239.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 239 (Homeowners Assistance Program—Application Processing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The HQUSACE and its major subordinate commands may conduct periodic on-site inspections of district offices and monitor program execution through HAPMIS and CEFMS reports.
