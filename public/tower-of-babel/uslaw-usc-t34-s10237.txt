34 U.S.C. § 10237 — Limitation on civil justice matters
Citation: us:usc:34:10237
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section10237
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 101 (Justice System Improvement); Subchapter VII (Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Authority of any entity established under this chapter shall extend to civil justice matters only to the extent that such civil justice matters bear directly and substantially upon criminal justice matters or are inextricably intertwined with criminal justice matters.
