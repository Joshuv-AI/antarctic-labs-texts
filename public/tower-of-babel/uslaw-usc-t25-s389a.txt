25 U.S.C. § 389a — Declaring lands to be temporarily nonirrigable
Citation: us:usc:25:389a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section389a
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 11 (Irrigation Of Allotted Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Where the Secretary finds that any such lands cannot be cultivated profitably due to a present lack of water supply, proper drainage facilities, or need of additional construction work, he shall declare such lands temporarily nonirrigable for periods not to exceed five years and no charges shall be assessed against such lands during such periods.
