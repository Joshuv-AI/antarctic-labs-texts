15 U.S.C. § 1651 — Procedure for timely settlement of estates of decedent obligors
Citation: us:usc:15:1651
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1651
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 41 (Consumer Credit Protection); Subchapter I (Consumer Credit Cost Disclosure); Part B (Credit Transactions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Bureau, in consultation with the Bureau 1 and each other agency referred to in section 1607(a) of this title, shall prescribe regulations to require any creditor, with respect to any credit card account under an open end consumer credit plan, to establish procedures to ensure that any administrator of an estate of any deceased obligor with respect to such account can resolve outstanding credit balances in a timely manner.
