41 C.F.R. § 301-31.1 — -31.1 Purpose of subsistence and transportation expenses for threatened law enforcement/investigative employees.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-31.1
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-31.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-31 (31—Threatened Law Enforcement/Investigative Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To protect law enforcement/investigative employees and their “immediate family” (as defined in § 300-1.1 of this subtitle) when their lives are placed in jeopardy as a result of the employee's assigned duties. The agency may, on a case-by-case basis, expand the definition of “immediate family” to include other members of the employee's and/or the employee's spouse's or domestic partner's extended family.
