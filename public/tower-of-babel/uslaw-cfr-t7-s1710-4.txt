7 C.F.R. § 1710.4 — Exception authority.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1710.4
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1710.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1710 (General And Pre-Loan Policies And Procedures Common To Electric Loans And Guarantees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Consistent with the RE Act and other applicable laws, the Administrator may waive or reduce any requirement imposed by this part or other RUS regulations on an electric borrower, or a lender whose loan is guaranteed by RUS, if the Administrator determines that imposition of the requirement would adversely affect the Government's financial interest.
