43 C.F.R. § 2884.27 — What additional requirements are necessary for grants for pipelines 24 or more inches in diameter?
Citation: us:cfr:43:2884.27
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2884.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2880 (Rights-Of-Way Under The Mineral Leasing Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an application is for a grant for a pipeline 24 inches or more in diameter, the BLM will not issue or renew the grant until after the BLM notifies the appropriate committees of Congress in accordance with 30 U.S.C. 185(w).
