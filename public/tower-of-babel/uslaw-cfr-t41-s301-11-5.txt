41 C.F.R. § 301-11.5 — -11.5 Entitlement period for subsistence expenses.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-11.5
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-11.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-11 (11—Subsistence Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The period for subsistence expense entitlement starts on the day the employee departs their residence, office, or other authorized point and ends on the day they return to their residence, office, or other authorized point.
