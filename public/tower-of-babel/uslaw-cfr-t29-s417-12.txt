29 C.F.R. § 417.12 — Proposed findings and conclusions.
Citation: us:cfr:29:417.12
Creator: Office Of Labor-Management Standards, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-417.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 417 (Procedure For Removal Of Local Labor Organization Officers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within 10 days following the close of hearings, interested persons may submit proposed findings and conclusions to the Administrative Law Judge, together with supporting reasons therefor, which shall become a part of the record.
