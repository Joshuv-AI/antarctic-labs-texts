4 C.F.R. § 25.11 — Photographs for news, advertising, or commercial purposes.
Citation: us:cfr:4:25.11
Creator: Government Accountability Office
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-4/section-25.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 4 (Accounts), Part 25 (Conduct In The Government Accountability Office Building And On Its Grounds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Photographs may be taken in the GAO Building only with the approval or authorization of the Comptroller General or his designee.
