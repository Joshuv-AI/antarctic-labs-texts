20 C.F.R. § 686.945 — What are the procedures for management of student records?
Citation: us:cfr:20:686.945
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-686.945
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 686 (The Job Corps Under Title I Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary issues guidelines for a system for maintaining records for each student during enrollment and for disposition of such records after separation.
