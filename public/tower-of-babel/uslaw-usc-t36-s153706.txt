36 U.S.C. § 153706 — Exclusive right to name, seals, emblems, and badges
Citation: us:usc:36:153706
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section153706
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1537 (National Woman’S Relief Corps, Auxiliary To The Grand Army Of The Republic). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation and its subordinate corps have the exclusive right to use the name “National Woman’s Relief Corps, Auxiliary to the Grand Army of the Republic”. The corporation has the exclusive right to use and to allow others to use seals, emblems, and badges the corporation adopts.
