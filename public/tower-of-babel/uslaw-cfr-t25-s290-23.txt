25 C.F.R. § 290.23 — How does the Indian tribe resolve disputes arising from per capita payments to individual members or identified groups of members?
Citation: us:cfr:25:290.23
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-290.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 290 (Tribal Revenue Allocation Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must utilize or establish a tribal court system, forum or administrative process for resolving disputes arising from the allocation of net gaming revenue and the distribution of per capita payments.
