36 U.S.C. § 30905 — Exclusive right to emblems, badges, marks, and words
Citation: us:usc:36:30905
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section30905
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 309 (Boy Scouts Of America). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation has the exclusive right to use emblems, badges, descriptive or designating marks, and words or phrases the corporation adopts. This section does not affect any vested rights.
