31 C.F.R. § 1060.800 — Penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:31:1060.800
Creator: Financial Crimes Enforcement Network, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-1060.800
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 1060 (Provisions Relating To The Comprehensive Iran Sanctions, Accountability, And Divestment Act Of 2010). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person violating any requirement under this part is subject to the penalties provided for in sections 5321(a) and 5322 of title 31, United States Code, in the same manner and to the same extent as such penalties would apply to any person that is otherwise subject to such section 5321(a) or 5322.
