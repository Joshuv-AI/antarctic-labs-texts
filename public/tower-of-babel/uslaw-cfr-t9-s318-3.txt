9 C.F.R. § 318.3 — Designation of places of receipt of products and other articles for reinspection.
Citation: us:cfr:9:318.3
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-318.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 318 (Entry Into Official Establishments; Reinspection And Preparation Of Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every official establishment shall designate, with the approval of the circuit supervisor, a dock or place at which products and other articles subject to reinspection under § 318.2 shall be received, and such products and articles shall be received only at such dock or place.
