47 U.S.C. § 1433 — Provision of technical assistance
Citation: us:usc:47:1433
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title47-section1433
Context: United States Code, Title 47 (Telecommunications); Chapter 13 (Public Safety Communications And Electromagnetic Spectrum Auctions); Subchapter II (Governance Of Public Safety Spectrum). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commission may provide technical assistance to the First Responder Network Authority and may take any action necessary to assist the First Responder Network Authority in effectuating its duties and responsibilities under this subchapter.
