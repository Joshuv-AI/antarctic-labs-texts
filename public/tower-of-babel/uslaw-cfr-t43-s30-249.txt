43 C.F.R. § 30.249 — What happens when the judge issues an order on reopening?
Citation: us:cfr:43:30.249
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-30.249
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 30 (Indian Probate Hearings Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Copies of the judge's order on reopening must be mailed to the petitioner, the affected agencies, and all interested parties.
(b) The judge must submit the record made on a reopening petition to the designated LTRO.
(c) The order on reopening will become final on the expiration of the 30 days allowed for the filing of a notice of appeal, as provided in this part.
