28 C.F.R. § 5.208 — Disclosure of foreign principals.
Citation: us:cfr:28:5.208
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-5.208
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 5 (Administration And Enforcement Of Foreign Agents Registration Act Of 1938, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A registrant who represents more than one foreign principal is required to list in the statements he files under the Act only those foreign principals for whom he is not entitled to claim exemption under section 3 of the Act.
