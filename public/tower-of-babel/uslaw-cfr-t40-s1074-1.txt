40 C.F.R. § 1074.1 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1074.1
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1074.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1074 (Preemption Of State Standards And Procedures For Waiver Of Federal Preemption For Nonroad Engines And Nonroad Vehicles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirements of this part apply with respect to state and local standards and other requirements relating to the control of emissions from nonroad engines and nonroad vehicles.
