42 C.F.R. § 435.930 — Furnishing Medicaid.
Citation: us:cfr:42:435.930
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-435.930
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 435 (Eligibility In The States, District Of Columbia, The Northern Mariana Islands, And American Samoa). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agency must—
(a) Furnish Medicaid promptly to beneficiaries without any delay caused by the agency's administrative procedures;
(b) Continue to furnish Medicaid regularly to all eligible individuals until they are found to be ineligible; and
(c) Make arrangements to assist applicants and beneficiaries to get emergency medical care whenever needed, 24 hours a day and 7 days a week.
