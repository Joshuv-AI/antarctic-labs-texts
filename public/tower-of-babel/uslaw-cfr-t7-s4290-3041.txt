7 C.F.R. § 4290.3041 — Events of default and the Agency's remedies for RBIC's noncompliance with terms of licensure.
Citation: us:cfr:7:4290.3041
Creator: Rural Business-Cooperative Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-4290.3041
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 4290 (Rural Business Investment Company (“Rbic”) Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to complying with the provisions of § 4290.1810, a RBIC's failure to comply with the terms of this part may result in the Agency revoking the Non-leveraged RBIC's license issued under this part.
