20 C.F.R. § 416.1493 — Effect of revised determination or decision.
Citation: us:cfr:20:416.1493
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-416.1493
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 416 (Supplemental Security Income For The Aged, Blind, And Disabled). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A revised determination or decision is binding unless—
(a) You or a party to the revised determination file a written request for a reconsideration or a hearing;
(b) You or another party to the revised decision file, as appropriate, a request for review by the Appeals Council or a hearing;
(c) The Appeals Council reviews the revised decision; or
(d) The revised determination or decision is further revised.
