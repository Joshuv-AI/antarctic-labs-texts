33 C.F.R. § 101.655 — Cybersecurity compliance dates.
Citation: us:cfr:33:101.655
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-101.655
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 101 (Maritime Security: General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All Cybersecurity Plans mentioned in this subpart must be submitted to the Coast Guard for review and approval no later than July 16, 2027, according to 33 CFR 104.410 for U.S.-flagged vessels, 33 CFR 105.410 for facilities, or 33 CFR 106.410 for OCS facilities.
