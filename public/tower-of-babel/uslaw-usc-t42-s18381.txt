42 U.S.C. § 18381 — Technology development
Citation: us:usc:42:18381
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section18381
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 159 (Space Exploration, Technology, And Science); Subchapter VII (Space Science). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator shall ensure that the Science Mission Directorate maintains a long term technology development program for space and Earth science. This effort should be coordinated with an overall Agency technology investment approach, as authorized in section 905 of this Act.
