32 C.F.R. § 1698.7 — Reconsideration of advisory opinions.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1698.7
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1698.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1698 (Advisory Opinions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever the Director has reason to believe that there is substantial error in the information on which an advisory opinion is based, he may reconsider it and issue an appropriate revised opinion.
