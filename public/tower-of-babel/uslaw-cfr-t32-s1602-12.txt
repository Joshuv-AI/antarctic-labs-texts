32 C.F.R. § 1602.12 — Governor.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1602.12
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1602.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1602 (Definitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The word Governor includes, where applicable, the Governor of each of the States of the United States, the Mayor of the District of Columbia, the Governor of Puerto Rico, the Governor of the Virgin Islands, and the Governor of Guam.
