10 U.S.C. § 255 — Guam and Virgin Islands included as “State”
Citation: us:usc:10:255
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section255
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And General Military Powers); Chapter 13 (Insurrection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of this chapter, the term “State” includes Guam and the Virgin Islands.
