10 C.F.R. § 950.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:10:950.1
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-950.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 950 (Standby Support For Certain Nuclear Plant Delays). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to facilitate the construction and full power operation of new advanced nuclear facilities by providing risk insurance for certain delays attributed to the Nuclear Regulatory Commission regulatory process or to litigation.
