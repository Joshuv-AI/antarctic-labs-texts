7 C.F.R. § 1786.210 — Approvals.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1786.210
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1786.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1786 (Prepayment Of Rus Guaranteed And Insured Loans To Electric And Telephone Borrowers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The borrower shall be responsible for obtaining all approvals necessary to consummate the transaction as required by the refinancing note, including such approvals as may be required by regulatory bodies and other lenders.
