34 C.F.R. § 646.5 — How long is a project period?
Citation: us:cfr:34:646.5
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-646.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 646 (Student Support Services Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A project period under the Student Support Services program is five years.
