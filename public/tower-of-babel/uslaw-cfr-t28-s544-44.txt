28 C.F.R. § 544.44 — Disciplinary action.
Citation: us:cfr:28:544.44
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-544.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 544 (Education). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As with any other mandatory programs, such as work assignments, staff may take disciplinary action against an inmate when that inmate refuses to enroll and participate in, or to meet the minimum requirements of the mandatory ESL program.
