48 C.F.R. § 19.301-3 — 19.301-3 Rerepresentation by a contractor that represented itself as other than a small business concern.
Citation: us:cfr:48:19.301-3
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-19.301-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 19 (Small Business Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A contractor that represented itself as other than small before contract award may, but is not required to, rerepresent its size status when—
(a) The conditions in 19.301-2(b) apply; and
(b) The contractor qualifies as a small business concern under the applicable size standard in effect at the time of its rerepresentation.
