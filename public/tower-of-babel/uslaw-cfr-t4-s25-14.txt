4 C.F.R. § 25.14 — Weapons and explosives.
Citation: us:cfr:4:25.14
Creator: Government Accountability Office
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-4/section-25.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 4 (Accounts), Part 25 (Conduct In The Government Accountability Office Building And On Its Grounds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person while entering or in the GAO Building or on its grounds shall carry or possess firearms, other dangerous or deadly weapons, explosives or items intended to be used to fabricate an explosive or incendiary device, either openly or concealed, except for official purposes.
