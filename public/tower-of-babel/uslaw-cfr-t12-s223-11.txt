12 C.F.R. § 223.11 — What is the maximum amount of covered transactions that a member bank may enter into with any single affiliate?
Citation: us:cfr:12:223.11
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-223.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 223 (Transactions Between Member Banks And Their Affiliates (Regulation W)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A member bank may not engage in a covered transaction with an affiliate (other than a financial subsidiary of the member bank) if the aggregate amount of the member bank's covered transactions with such affiliate would exceed 10 percent of the capital stock and surplus of the member bank.
