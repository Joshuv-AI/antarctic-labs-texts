Correction: Journal of Nanobiotechnology (2025) 23:404

After careful rechecking of all figure raw data post-publication, the authors identified a layout error in Fig. 3E of this work: the subpanel of the C.+ZnO group at 12 was misplaced during the final figure assembly process, which does not match the correct original experimental confocal microscopy image we intended to present. This layout mistake is purely a graphic typesetting error in figure composition, it does not alter the core experimental conclusions, quantitative statistics, or any other data presented in this manuscript. For completeness and transparency, the old incorrect and correct versions of Fig. 3 are displayed below.
