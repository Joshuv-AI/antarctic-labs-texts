34 C.F.R. § 33.41 — Stay pending appeal.
Citation: us:cfr:34:33.41
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-33.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 33 (Program Fraud Civil Remedies Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An initial decision is stayed automatically pending disposition of a motion for reconsideration or of an appeal to the Department head.
(b) No administrative stay is available following a final decision of the Department head.
