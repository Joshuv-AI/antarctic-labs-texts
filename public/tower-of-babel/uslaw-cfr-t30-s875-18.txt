30 C.F.R. § 875.18 — Lien requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:30:875.18
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-875.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 875 (Certification And Noncoal Reclamation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The lien requirements found in part 882—Reclamation on Private Land shall apply to a State's or Indian tribe's noncoal reclamation program under section 411 of the Act, except that for purposes of this section, references made to coal shall not apply. In lieu of the term coal, the word noncoal should be used.
