50 C.F.R. § 13.48 — Compliance with conditions of permit.
Citation: us:cfr:50:13.48
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-13.48
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 13 (General Permit Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person holding a permit under subchapter B and any person acting under authority of such permit must comply with all conditions of the permit and with all appllicable laws and regulations governing the permitted activity.
