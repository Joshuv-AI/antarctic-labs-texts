48 C.F.R. § 326.605-1 — 326.605-1 Subcontracting limitations.
Citation: us:cfr:48:326.605-1
Creator: Chapter 3—Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-326.605-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 326 (Other Socioeconomic Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The CO shall insert FAR clause at 52.219-14, Limitations on Subcontracting, in solicitations and contracts for supplies, services, and construction, if any portion of the requirement is to be set-aside for ISBEEs and IEEs.
(b) The CO must also insert the clause 352.226-6, Indian Economic Enterprise Subcontracting Limitations, in all awards to ISBEEs and IEEs pursuant this part.
