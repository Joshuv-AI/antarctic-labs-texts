5 C.F.R. § 894.511 — What are the QLEs that are consistent with decreasing my type of enrollment?
Citation: us:cfr:5:894.511
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-894.511
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 894 (Federal Employees Dental And Vision Insurance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Loss of an eligible family member or TEI family member due to:
(1) Divorce;
(2) Death; or
(3) Loss of eligibility of a previously enrolled child or TEI child.
(b) You are an enrollee and your spouse deploys to active military service.
