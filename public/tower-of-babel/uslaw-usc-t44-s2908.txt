44 U.S.C. § 2908 — Regulations
Citation: us:usc:44:2908
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section2908
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 29 (Records Management By The Archivist Of The United States And By The Administrator Of General Services). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Subject to applicable law, the Archivist shall promulgate regulations governing the transfer of records from the custody of one executive agency to that of another.
