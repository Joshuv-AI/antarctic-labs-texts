48 C.F.R. § 53.101 — 53.101 Requirements for use of forms.
Citation: us:cfr:48:53.101
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-53.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 53 (Forms). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirements for use of the forms prescribed or referenced in this part are contained in parts 1 through 52, where the subject matter applicable to each form is addressed. The specific location of each requirement is identified in subpart 53.2.
