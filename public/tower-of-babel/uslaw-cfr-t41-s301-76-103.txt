41 C.F.R. § 301-76.103 — -76.103 Maximum deduction limit.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-76 (76—Collection Of Undisputed Delinquent Amounts Owed To The Contractor Issuing The Individually Billed Travel Charge Card). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As set forth in the Travel and Transportation Reform Act of 1998 (5 U.S.C. 5701 note) the maximum amount an agency may deduct from the employee's disposable pay is 15 percent per pay period, unless the employee consents in writing to deduction of a greater percentage.
