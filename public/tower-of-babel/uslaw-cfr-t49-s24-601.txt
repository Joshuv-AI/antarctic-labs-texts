49 C.F.R. § 24.601 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:49:24.601
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-24.601
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 24 (Uniform Relocation Assistance And Real Property Acquisition For Federal And Federally Assisted Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart permits a State agency to fulfill its responsibilities under the Uniform Act by certifying that it shall operate in accordance with State laws and regulations which shall accomplish the purpose and effect of the Uniform Act, in lieu of providing the assurances required by § 24.4.
