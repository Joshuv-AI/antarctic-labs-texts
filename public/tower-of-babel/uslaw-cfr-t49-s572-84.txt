49 C.F.R. § 572.84 — Thorax.
Citation: us:cfr:49:572.84
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-572.84
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 572 (Anthropomorphic Test Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The thorax consists of the part of the torso shown in assembly drawing LP 1049/A and conforms to each of the applicable drawings listed under LP 1049/0 through 54.
