2 U.S.C. § 1612 — Sense of Senate that lobbying expenses should remain nondeductible
Citation: us:usc:2:1612
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1612
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 26 (Disclosure Of Lobbying Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Findings
The Senate finds that ordinary Americans generally are not allowed to deduct the costs of communicating with their elected representatives.
(b) Sense of Senate
It is the sense of the Senate that lobbying expenses should not be tax deductible.
