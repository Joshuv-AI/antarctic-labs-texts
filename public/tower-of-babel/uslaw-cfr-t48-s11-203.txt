48 C.F.R. § 11.203 — 11.203 Customer satisfaction.
Citation: us:cfr:48:11.203
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-11.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 11 (Describing Agency Needs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Acquisition organizations shall communicate with customers to determine how well the requirements document reflects the customer's needs and to obtain suggestions for corrective actions. Whenever practicable, the agency may provide affected industry an opportunity to comment on the requirements documents.
