Gauge-independent treatment of electroweak phase transition
Jie Liu, Renhui Qin, Ligong Bian

Abstract
We provide the first certificate of the gauge-independent bubble nucleation at the electroweak phase transition with the standard model effective field theory. Taking advantage of the thermal effective field theory framework, with the power counting λ ∼ g 3 \lambda\sim g^{3} , we rigorously demonstrate the gauge independence of the bubble nucleation rate up to two-loop order. Furthermore, we analyze the influence of relevant phase transition parameters on the gauge parameter and investigate its implications for gravitational waves generated by the electroweak phase transitions.

Physically, the bubble nucleation rate describes a fundamental physical process and should not depend on an artificial gauge choice

Fortunately, the Nielsen identity provides the chance to obtain gauge-independent quantities

V
Conclusion

In this paper, we study the gauge dependence of the three-dimensional thermal SM effective field theory with the Nielsen identity. Our result is valid by ignoring the higher order of
\xi

\sqrt{\xi}-
terms. We split the effective potential as LO and NLO parts, the LO is gauge-independent while NLO include the
\sqrt{\xi}
terms. The Nielsen identity shows that the result of method
\mathcal{B}_{0,1}
is gauge-independent, and our result shows that the effect of the gauge parameter could be ignored by using this method.

For the PT parameters, we found the gauge-independent method including,
\mathcal{B}_{0,1}
, gives lower
T_{n}
and larger
\alpha
