46 C.F.R. § 64.65 — Vacuum relief device.
Citation: us:cfr:46:64.65
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-64.65
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 64 (Marine Portable Tanks And Cargo Handling Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each MPT that is designed for an external pressure of less than 7.5 psig must have a vacuum relief device.
(b) A vacuum relief device for an MPT must—
(1) Open at an external pressure of not less than 3 psig; and
(2) Have an opening with a cross-section of 0.44 square inch or more.
