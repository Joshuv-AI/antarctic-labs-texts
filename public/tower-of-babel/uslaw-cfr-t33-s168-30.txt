33 C.F.R. § 168.30 — Applicable cargoes.

Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-168.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 168 (Escort Requirements For Certain Tankers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirements of this part apply to any petroleum oil listed in 46 CFR Table 30.25-1 as a pollution category I cargo.
