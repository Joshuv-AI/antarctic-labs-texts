40 C.F.R. § 705.10 — Persons who must report.
Citation: us:cfr:40:705.10
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-705.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 705 (Reporting And Recordkeeping Requirements For Certain Per- And Polyfluoroalkyl Substances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Persons who have manufactured for commercial purposes a chemical substance identified in § 705.5 at any period from January 1, 2011, through the end of the last calendar year prior to November 13, 2023, except as described in § 705.12, is subject to the requirements of this part.
