10 U.S.C. § 7415 — The Judge Advocate General’s School: master of laws in military law
Citation: us:usc:10:7415
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7415
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training); Chapter 751 (Training Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Under regulations prescribed by the Secretary of the Army, the Commandant of the Judge Advocate General’s School of the Army may, upon recommendation by the faculty of such school, confer the degree of master of laws (LL.M.) in military law upon graduates of the school who have fulfilled the requirements for that degree.
