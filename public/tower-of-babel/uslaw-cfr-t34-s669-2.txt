34 C.F.R. § 669.2 — Who is eligible to receive assistance under this program?
Citation: us:cfr:34:669.2
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-669.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 669 (Language Resource Centers Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An institution of higher education or a combination of institutions of higher education is eligible to receive an award under this part.
