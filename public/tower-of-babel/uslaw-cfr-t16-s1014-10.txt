16 C.F.R. § 1014.10 — Fees.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1014.10
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1014.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1014 (Policies And Procedures Implementing The Privacy Act Of 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission shall not charge an individual for the costs of making a search for a record, the costs of reviewing or copying a record, or the cost of correcting or amending a record.
