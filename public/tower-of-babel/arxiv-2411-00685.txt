Improved non-Abelian tensor multiplet action


Abstract
Construction of the superfield action of the N = ( 1 , 0 ) N=(1,0) , d = 6 d=6 non-Abelian tensor multiplet based on the non-Abelian tensor hierarchies is considered. It is shown that while straightforward non-Abelian generalization of the Pasti-Sorokin-Tonin action is not a workable solution, a suitable truncation of the PST action can be still be modified to include non-Abelian tensor field. In the modified action, the self-dual equation of motion of the tensor field is induced by a composite Lagrange multiplier, which is not a component of a standard dynamical tensor multiplet. As a result, the constraint that enforces the gauge group to be non-compact, as in the usual tensor hierarchies, can be avoided.

Scalar kinetic term contains fields of both multiplets linearly, and, therefore, possesses indefinite metric.

A possibility to avoid presence of two multiplets and resulting indefiniteness of the metric is the use of Past-Sorokin-Tonin mechanism of generating self-dual equation of motion for the tensor field, which was done in

In this article, we discuss the possibility of construction of the non-Abelian tensor multiplet action with positive-definite metric in the scalar sector. Firstly, we consider the bosonic tensor hierarchy
[
14

15
]

Secondly, we discuss simple non-Abelian deformation of the PST action to show that it does not possess the required symmetries and does not generate a useful equation of motion without an additional 3-form present. Thirdly, we consider an alternative approach based on the truncation of the PST action that is equivalent to the standard Lagrange multiplier method in the purely bosonic case but allows for different supersymmetrization. Finally, we present a manifestly supersymmetric harmonic superspace action and show that it possesses the required bosonic limit without putting unappropriate constraints on the gauge group and briefly discuss its properties.

Throughout this article, we use vector and spinor notations interchangeably, with the details given in the Appendix A. Standard and capital letters, Greek or Latin, are reserved for space-time and harmonic indices, while bold ones are used for internal symmetries.

2.2
Pasti-Sorokin-Tonin mechanism

Observing the structure of the action (
2.8
), one can link the problem of indefiniteness of the metric in the scalar sector to the presence of the Lagrange multiplier that induces the self-dual equation of motion of the tensor field. As we already concluded, it is not actually needed from the gauge transformation point of view, and a possible solution to the problem of indefinite metric is to induce the tensor equation of motion in a different way.

The obvious alternative is the Pasti-Sorokin-Tonin mechanism
[
19
]
. The corresponding action involves, in addition to the antisymmetric tensor, one scalar field:

\displaystyle S_{PST}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int d^{6}x\Big(\frac{1}{6}F_{MNP}F^{MNP}-\frac{1}{2\partial^{K}z\partial_{K}z}{\cal F}_{ABC}{\cal F}^{ABD}\partial^{C}z\partial_{D}z\Big),

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle F_{ABC}-\frac{1}{6}\epsilon_{ABCMNP}F^{MNP},\;\;F_{ABC}=\partial_{A}B_{BC}-\partial_{B}B_{AC}+\partial_{C}B_{AB}.

(2.12)

This action possesses three gauge symmetries:

\displaystyle 1)\;\;\delta_{f}B_{MN}=\partial_{M}f_{N}-\partial_{N}f_{M},\;\;\delta_{f}z=0,

\displaystyle 2)\;\;\delta_{\lambda}z=\lambda,\;\;\delta_{\lambda}B_{MN}=\frac{\lambda}{\partial^{S}z\,\partial_{S}z}{\cal F}_{MNP}\partial^{P}z,

\displaystyle 3)\;\;\delta_{a}B_{MN}=\partial_{[M}z\;a_{N]},\;\;\delta_{a}z=0.

\displaystyle\epsilon^{ABMNPQ}{\cal F}_{MNP}\partial_{Q}z=0\;\Rightarrow\;{\cal F}_{MNP}=\partial_{[P}zK_{MN]}\;\Rightarrow

\displaystyle\big(\partial^{P}z\,\partial_{P}z\big)\,K_{MN}=\partial_{M}z\,\big(\partial^{P}z\,K_{NP}\big)-\partial_{N}z\,\big(\partial^{P}z\,K_{MP}\big).

(2.22)

If

{\cal F}_{MNP}=0
. Moreover, using the same reasoning one can show that any self-dual tensor

can be presented as

C_{MNP}=3\partial_{[M}z\,{\widetilde{R}}_{NP]}+\frac{1}{2}\epsilon_{MNPIJK}\partial^{K}z\,{\widetilde{R}}^{IJ}.

(2.23)

Indeed, multiplying (
2.23
) by
\partial^{P}z
, one finds

{\widetilde{R}}_{MN}=-\frac{C_{MNP}\,\partial^{P}z}{\partial^{S}z\partial_{S}z}+\mbox{irrelevant terms}\;\sim\;\partial z.

(2.24)

Then substitution
{\widetilde{R}}_{MN}
(
2.24
) into (
2.23
) with use of relation
\epsilon_{[ABCMNP}\partial_{Q]}z=0
results in an identity.

Formula (
2.23
) implies that the action (
2.21
) is equivalent to the standard one that induces self-dual equation of motion for
F_{MNP}
with a Lagrange multiplier. It is different, however, from supersymmetric point of view, as it becomes possible to introduce a composite Lagrange multiplier without adding a new complete dynamical tensor multiplet. Therefore, appearance of the scalar component partner to the Lagrange multiplier, the key reason why indefinite metric appears in the tensor hierarchy actions, can be avoided.

Our approach to construct the non-Abelian tensor multiplet action can be, therefore, summarized in the following way. The starting point is the manifestly supersymmetric harmonic superspace action of the Abelian tensor multiplet constructed in
[
21
]
. We perform truncation of this action analogous to one that was done to the action (
2.20
) by removing one of the terms and add minimal interaction to the Yang-Mills multiplet. Then we add nonlinear conditions that allow us to remove excessive auxiliary components from the resulting action and show that the bosonic limit of the resulting action appears to be non-Abelian generalization of (
2.21
). Analyzing the result, we discuss the possible constraints on the gauge group, demanding that the Lagrange multiplier has to be non-dynamical. Finally, we comment on the possibility of adding a non-minimal interaction.

4
Conclusion

In this article we constructed the harmonic superfield action with
N=(1,0)
,
d=6
supersymmetry that involves non-Abelian extension of the tensor multiplet and possesses positive-definite metric in the scalar sector. Starting from the tensor hierarchy
[
13
,
14
]
, we showed that possible non-trivial gauge symmetries of the tensor field include gauge rotations, and, if underlying group is not semi-simple, also shifts by the strength tensor of the Yang-Mills field while analogs of shift symmetries of the Abelian tensor are completely compensated by the Stückelberg fields. These transformations were realized in terms of harmonic superfields that define tensor multiplet off-shell.
The action we proposed was inspired by the Pasti-Sorokin-Tonin polynomial
[
20
]
and superfield
[
21
]
actions. While the standard PST mechanism does not function properly for the non-Abelian tensor fields, truncation of the corresponding action still induces the self-duality equation in the same way as the Lagrange multiplier. The difference from supersymmetric point of view is that in this case Lagrange multiplier is composite and can be introduced in such a way that does not involve extra dynamical scalar, allowing to keep the metric in the scalar sector definite. Analogous truncation of the action
[
21
]
, together with introduction of additional constraints to remove the auxiliary fields, forms the basis of our non-Abelian system. Minimal interaction with the Yang-Mills multiplet then can be introduced by promoting all the derivatives to the covariant ones using standard connection superfields, though modification of one of the tensor multiplet field strengths by terms explicitly involving prepotential is required to keep the superfield Lagrangian analytic. The component action was found in the bosonic limit. It involves the standard kinetic term for the scalar field and the Lagrangian multiplier for the self-duality equation of the tensor, as well as interaction of these fields with the Yang-Mills strength tensor. Conditions that make the Lagrangian multiplier non-dynamical were discussed, and two cases were found when this happens without algebraically constraining other involved fields. Study of possible modifications of the constructed Lagrangian showed that only semi-simple gauge groups are allowed.

Whole our construction was made possible by the use of harmonic superfield method that allowed us to deal with the multiplets and gauge symmetries completely off-shell and to avoid making any hypotheses about the on-shell transformation laws of physical fields, as is required in the component constructions. It is, obviously, desirable to compare our results to the standard supersymmetric tensor hierarchy ones
[
14
,
15
]
in this context. However, current superfield construction involves a lot of nonlinear constraints on the auxiliary fields that have simple solutions only in the bosonic limit, and we prefer not to discuss the fermionic structures in this article.

The most important technique we use in this article is the harmonic superspace

N=(1,0)

u^{+i}
,
u^{-j}

i,j=1,2

u^{+i}u^{-j}\epsilon_{ij}=1,\;\;\epsilon_{ij}=-\epsilon_{ji},\;\;\epsilon_{12}=1

(5.83)

and parameterize unit 2-sphere. Derivatives with respect to them are defined as

\partial^{++}=u^{+i}\frac{\partial}{\partial u^{-i}},\;\;\partial^{--}=u^{-i}\frac{\partial}{\partial u^{+i}},\;\;\partial_{0}=u^{+i}\frac{\partial}{\partial u^{+i}}-u^{-i}\frac{\partial}{\partial u^{-i}}

(5.84)

to preserve the relation (
5.83
).

In the harmonic superspace, the so called analytic basis can be chosen

x^{[\alpha\beta]},\;\;\theta^{+\alpha},\;\;\theta^{-\alpha},\;\;u^{+i},u^{-j},

(5.85)

in which one of the odd derivatives becomes simpler:

\displaystyle D^{+}_{\alpha}=\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta^{-\alpha}},\;\;D^{-}_{\alpha}=-\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta^{+\alpha}}-2{\rm i}\theta^{-\beta}\partial_{\alpha\beta},\;\;D_{0}=\partial_{0}+\theta^{+\gamma}\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta^{+\gamma}}-\theta^{-\gamma}\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta^{-\gamma}},

\displaystyle D^{++}=\partial^{++}+{\rm i}\theta^{+\alpha}\theta^{+\beta}\partial_{\alpha\beta}+\theta^{+\gamma}\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta^{-\gamma}},\;\;D^{--}=\partial^{--}+{\rm i}\theta^{-\alpha}\theta^{-\beta}\partial_{\alpha\beta}+\theta^{-\gamma}\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta^{+\gamma}}.

(5.86)

The derivatives obey the (anti)commutation relations

\displaystyle\big\{D^{+}_{\alpha},D^{-}_{\beta}\big\}=2{\rm i}\partial_{\alpha\beta},\;\;\big\{D^{+}_{\alpha},D^{+}_{\beta}\big\}=0,\;\;\big\{D^{-}_{\alpha},D^{-}_{\beta}\big\}=0,

\displaystyle\big[D^{++},D^{--}\big]=D_{0},\;\;\big[D_{0},D^{++}\big]=2D^{++},\;\;\big[D_{0},D^{--}\big]=-2D^{--},

\displaystyle\big[D^{++},D^{+}_{\alpha}\big]=0,\;\;\big[D^{--},D^{+}_{\alpha}\big]=D^{-}_{\alpha},\;\;\big[D_{0},D^{+}_{\alpha}\big]=D^{+}_{\alpha},

\displaystyle\big[D^{++},D^{-}_{\alpha}\big]=D^{+}_{\alpha},\;\;\big[D^{--},D^{-}_{\alpha}\big]=0,\;\;\big[D_{0},D^{-}_{\alpha}\big]=-D^{-}_{\alpha}.

(5.87)

Simple structure of
D^{+}_{\alpha}
allows to consider the analytic harmonic superspace
x^{[\alpha\beta]},\;\;\theta^{+\alpha},\;\;u^{+i},\;\;u^{-j}
with twice less Grassmann coordinates.

Superfields defined on the harmonic superspace have to possess definite charge:
D_{0}f^{q}=qf^{q}

,

(5.88)

For
f^{q}
with negative charge, roles of
u^{+i}
and
u^{-i}

Integration over harmonic variables is defined by the rules

\int du\,1=1,\;\;\int duu^{+}_{(i_{1}}\ldots u^{+}_{i_{n}}u^{-}_{j_{1}}\ldots u^{-}_{j_{m})}=0,\;\;m\;\mbox{or}\;n\neq 0.

\int du\partial^{++}f^{--}(u)=\int du\partial^{--}f^{++}(u)=0

\int d^{8}\theta\,\theta^{-4}\theta^{+4}=1,\;\;\int d^{4}\theta^{(-)}\theta^{+4}=1.
