2 C.F.R. § 1138.1105 — Content of SUB Article XI.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1138.1105
Creator: Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1138.1105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1138 (Requirements Related To Subawards: General Award Terms And Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Requirement. A DoD Component's general terms and conditions must address requirements that recipients must include in subawards to entities that may make lower-tier subawards.
(b) Award terms and conditions. A DoD Component's general terms and conditions must use the wording appendix K to this part provides for SUB Article XI.
