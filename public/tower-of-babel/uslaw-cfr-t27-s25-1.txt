27 C.F.R. § 25.1 — Production and removal of beer.
Citation: us:cfr:27:25.1
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-25.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 25 (Beer). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part relate to beer and cereal beverages and cover the location, construction, equipment, operations and qualifications of breweries and pilot brewing plants.
