10 U.S.C. § 1584 — Employment of non-citizens
Citation: us:usc:10:1584
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1584
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 81 (Civilian Employees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Laws prohibiting the employment of, or payment of pay or expenses to, a person who is not a citizen of the United States do not apply to personnel of the Department of Defense.
