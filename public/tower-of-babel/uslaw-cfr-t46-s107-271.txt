46 C.F.R. § 107.271 — Inspection: Alterations.
Citation: us:cfr:46:107.271
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-107.271
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 107 (Inspection And Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After plans are approved for alterations affecting the safety of the unit the Coast Guard conducts inspections of the affected—
(a) Hull;
(b) Machinery; or
(c) Equipment.
