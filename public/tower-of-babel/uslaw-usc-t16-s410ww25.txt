16 U.S.C. § 410ww–25 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:410ww–25
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section410ww–25
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LIX–U (Dayton Aviation Heritage National Historical Park); Part B (Dayton Aviation Heritage Commission). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated annually to the Commission to carry out its duties under this subchapter $350,000, except that the Federal contribution to the Commission shall not exceed 50 percent of the annual costs to the Commission in carrying out those duties.
