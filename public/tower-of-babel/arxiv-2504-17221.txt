Formal Manifold Structures on Positive Characteristic Varieties
Runjie Hu, Siqing Zhang

Abstract
. In his 1970 ICM report [ 36 ] , Sullivan proposes the program of l l -adic formalization of the concept of manifolds. In this program, he claims that smooth positive characteristic varieties should carry l l -adic formal manifold structures. He also claims the existence of an abelianized Galois symmetry on l l -adic formal manifold structures. This paper carries out this program, establishes the claims for certain varieties, and relates the abelianized Galois symmetry on l l -adic formal manifold structures to the Galois symmetry of varieties. Meanwhile, we prove that a simply-connected variety is l l -adic homotopic equivalent to a simply-connected finite CW complex if and only if the l l -profinite completion of its étale homotopy type admits an l l -local lifting.

4.
l
-adic Formal Manifold Structures and Galois Symmetry

In this section, we study various Galois symmetries on
l
-adic formal manifolds.

4.1.
l
-adic Normal Structure Set

Recall that the structure set
\mathbf{S}^{TOP}(M)
of a simply-connected closed topological manifold
M
consists of equivalence classes of homotopy equivalences
N\rightarrow M
with
N
a closed manifold, where
N\rightarrow M
is equivalent to
N^{\prime}\rightarrow M
if there exists a homeomorphism
N\rightarrow N^{\prime}
such that the following diagram commutes up to homotopy.

{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces N}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces M}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces N^{\prime}}

It is technically difficult to directly
l
-adic complete this structure set. Instead, recall the surgery exact sequence of based sets (
[
31
]
*p. 82, Theorem 3):

0\rightarrow\mathbf{S}^{TOP}(M)\rightarrow[M,G/TOP]\rightarrow P_{m},

where
G/TOP
is the fiber of
BTOP\rightarrow BG
and
P_{m}
is the simply-connected surgery obstruction group. Our idea is to take the
l
-adic completion of “normal structure set”
[M,G/TOP]
.

The local homotopy type of the surgery space
G/TOP
are known (see
[
21
]
*p. 329, 15.3).

Fact 4.1
.

(1)

(G/TOP)_{(2)}\simeq\prod_{k>0}(K(\mathbb{Z}_{(2)},4k)\times K(\mathbb{Z}/2,4k-2))
;

(2)

If
l
is odd, then
(G/TOP)_{(l)}\simeq BO_{(l)}
.

This motivates the following definitions.

Definition 4.2
.

Let
Z
be a simply-connected, mod-
l
Poincaré finite CW complex of formal dimension
m\geq 5
, with an
l
-adic formal manifold structure.

(1)

if
l=2
, a
2
-adic normal homotopy manifold structure
over
Z
is a pair
(L,K)
of positive graded classes
L\in H^{4*}(Z;\widehat{\mathbb{Z}}_{2})
and
K\in H^{4*-2}(Z;\mathbb{Z}/2)
.

(2)

If
l
is odd, an
l
-adic normal homotopy manifold structure
on
Z
is an element
\phi\in 1+\widetilde{KO_{l}^{\wedge}}(Z)
. The abelian group
1+\widetilde{KO_{l}^{\wedge}}(Z)
is defined by the classifying space
BO^{\wedge}_{l}\times\{1\}\subset BO^{\wedge}_{l}\times\mathbb{Z}
.

Remark 4.3
.

Note that the
H
-space structure on
BO^{\wedge}_{l}\times\{1\}
is induced by tensor product of vector bundles.

Definition 4.4
.

The
l
-adic normal structure set

\mathbf{S}(Z)^{\wedge}_{N,l}
of
Z
is the set of all
l
-adic normal homotopy manifold structures over
Z
.

Definition 4.5
.

A map
g:Z_{l}\rightarrow Z^{\prime}_{l}
of
l
-completions of simply-connected, mod-
l
Poincaré finite CW complexes is said to have
degree one
if
g_{*}\circ(f_{l})_{*}[Z]_{\mathbb{Z}/l}=(f^{\prime}_{l})_{*}[Z^{\prime}]_{\mathbb{Z}/l}
, where
f_{l}:Z\rightarrow Z_{l}
and
f^{\prime}_{l}:Z^{\prime}\rightarrow Z^{\prime}_{l}
are
l
-completions.

The following result implies that our definition of
l
-adic normal structure set contains the examples we want, i.e., an
l
-adic homotopy equivalence of
l
-adic formal manifolds is an
l
-adic normal homotopy manifold structure.

Proposition 4.6
.

Let
Z
and
Z^{\prime}
be simply-connected mod-

Poincaré finite CW complexes of formal dimension

with
l
-adic formal manifold structures. If

is a degree

homotopy equivalence, then

gives rise to an

-adic normal homotopy manifold structure on

.
More explicitly,

(1)

is odd, then

, where
\phi
is the

-adic normal homotopy manifold structure on

induced by

and
\Delta_{Z},\Delta_{Z^{\prime}}
correspond to the

-adic formal manifold structures of

respectively;

(2)

, where

is part of the
2
-adic normal homotopy manifold structure on

induced by
g
and
L_{Z},L_{Z^{\prime}}
correspond to part of the
2
-adic formal manifold structures of
Z,Z^{\prime}
respectively.

Let

and

be the

-adic formal manifold structures on

are two

-adic

-bundle liftings of the

of

. This corresponds to a map

. Then the rest follows from the surgery theory (see
[

]
*p. 218 and
[
29
]
*p. 543, the paragraph above Notes) and Fact

.

Let

4.2.
Galois Symmetry

Recall that for each prime

\widehat{\mathbb{Z}}^{\times}_{l}
on the
l
-complete complex
K
-theory and
l
-complete real
K
-theory induced by the Adams operations
\psi^{\sigma_{l}}

\sigma_{l}\in\widehat{\mathbb{Z}}^{\times}_{l}
(see
[

]
*p. 156).
The following fact follows from Sullivan’s proof of the Adams conjecture in
[

]
(or see
[

]
*p. 106, 5.13):

Fact 4.8
.

The map
BU^{\wedge}_{l}\xrightarrow{\psi^{\sigma_{l}}-1}BU^{\wedge}_{l}
canonically factors through
(G/U)^{\wedge}_{l}
. The same is true for
BO
and
G/O
.

When
l=2
, there is a composition of maps
f_{\sigma_{2}}:BU^{\wedge}_{2}\rightarrow(G/U)^{\wedge}_{2}\rightarrow(G/TOP)^{\wedge}_{2}
. Recall that there are characteristic classes
K^{q}\in H^{4*+2}((G/TOP)^{\wedge}_{2};\mathbb{Z}/2)
for the Kervaire surgery obstructions (see
[
31
]
*p. 88, Corollary 1). Let
\widetilde{K}^{\sigma_{2}}=f^{*}_{\sigma_{2}}K^{q}
.

Since
\widetilde{K}^{\sigma_{2}}
is a combination of Stiefel-Whitney classes, this combination defines a characteristic class
K^{\sigma_{2}}
in
H^{4*+2}(BSG_{2};\mathbb{Z}/2)
.

Lemma 4.9
.

Let
\sigma_{2},\tau_{2}\in\widehat{\mathbb{Z}}^{\times}_{2}
,
K^{\sigma_{2}\tau_{2}}=K^{\tau_{2}}+K^{\sigma_{2}}
.

Proof.

Since
\psi^{\sigma_{2}\tau_{2}}-1=\psi^{\sigma_{2}}(\psi^{\tau_{2}}-1)+(\psi^{\sigma_{2}}-1)
,
\widetilde{K}^{\sigma_{2}\tau_{2}}=(\psi^{\sigma_{2}})^{*}\widetilde{K}^{\tau_{2}}+\widetilde{K}^{\sigma_{2}}
. Furthermore,
(\psi^{\sigma_{2}})^{*}=\psi^{\sigma_{2}}_{H}
. So
(\psi^{\sigma_{2}})^{*}
is the identity for the
\mathbb{Z}/2
-coefficient.
∎

Definition 4.10
.

Define
K^{\sigma_{2}}_{Z}=(\nu_{Z})^{*}K^{\sigma_{2}}\in H^{4*+2}(Z;\mathbb{Z}/2)
, where
\nu_{Z}:Z\rightarrow BG_{2}
is the stable
2
-adic Spivak normal spherical fibration of
Z
.

Sullivan’s proof of Adams conjecture motivates the construction of an abelianized Galois action of
\widehat{\mathbb{Z}}^{\times}_{l}
on an
l
-adic normal structure set
\mathbf{S}(Z)^{\wedge}_{N,l}
.

Definition 4.11
.

Let
\sigma_{l}\in\widehat{\mathbb{Z}}^{\times}_{l}
. The

\widehat{\mathbb{Z}}^{\times}_{l}
on
\mathbf{S}(Z)^{\wedge}_{N,l}
is defined as follows:

(1)

if
l
is odd,
\sigma_{l}\cdot\phi=\psi^{\sigma_{l}}\phi\cdot\frac{\psi^{\sigma_{l}}\Delta_{Z}}{\Delta_{Z}}
, where

is an
l
-adic normal homotopy manifold structure on

;

(2)

if

,

\sigma_{2}\cdot K=K+K^{\sigma_{2}}_{Z}
, where

is an
l
-adic normal homotopy manifold structure on

and
\psi^{\sigma_{2}}_{H}
is the cohomological Adams operation on

Remark 4.12
.

Notice that the coefficient of

at degree

is
\sigma_{2}^{2n}-1
, which is divisible by
