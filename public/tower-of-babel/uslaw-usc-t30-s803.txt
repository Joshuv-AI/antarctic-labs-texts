30 U.S.C. § 803 — Mines subject to coverage
Citation: us:usc:30:803
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section803
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 22 (Mine Safety And Health). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each coal or other mine, the products of which enter commerce, or the operations or products of which affect commerce, and each operator of such mine, and every miner in such mine shall be subject to the provisions of this chapter.
