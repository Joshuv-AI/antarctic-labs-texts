42 C.F.R. § 410.14 — Special requirements for services furnished outside the United States.
Citation: us:cfr:42:410.14
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-410.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 410 (Supplementary Medical Insurance (Smi) Benefits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Medicare part B pays for physicians' services and ambulance services furnished outside the United States if the services meet the applicable conditions of § 410.12 and are furnished in connection with covered inpatient hospital services that meet the specific requirements and conditions set forth in subpart H of part 424 of this chapter.
