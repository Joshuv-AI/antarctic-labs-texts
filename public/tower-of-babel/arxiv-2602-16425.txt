Dynamical generation of fermion mass in a scalar-fermion theory with λ ​ ϕ 4 \lambda\phi^{4} interaction
Somnath Majumder, Krishnendu Mukherjee

Abstract
The effective potential for a scalar theory with λ ​ ϕ 4 \lambda\phi^{4} interaction, coupled to a massless fermion through Yukawa interaction is calculated by summing over infinite number of two particle irreducible (2PI) diagrams of two different types and a 2PI diagram of a third type using Cornwall, Jackiw and Tomboulis (CJT) method. There is an inversion symmetry present in the effective potential under ϕ → − ϕ \phi\rightarrow-\phi . At large coupling it exhibits minima below the zero potential line and on either side of the maximum at ϕ = 0 \phi=0 . The fermion acquires a mass at any non-trivial, positive minimum breaking the inversion symmetry of the vacuum.

Introduction

It is always interesting to study the behaviour of quantum field theory
at large coupling which is commonly known as non-pertubative regime
of the theory. It is believed that the theory in this regime may behave
non-trivially which is completely absent in the perturbative
expansion in powers of coupling constant to a finite order.
Attempts were made in this direction to understand the behaviour of
quantum field theories in the non-perturbative regime
[
1
,
2
]
. The estimates using large order perturbation series
were also made for this purpose
[
3
]
.
There was an investigation to produce symmetry breaking
generated by radiative corrections in gauge theories using
perturbative framework of field theory
[
4
]
.
There is a method of composite operators developed by Cornwall, Jackiw
and Tomboulis (CJT)
[
6
]
to address
the non-perturbative issues in quantum field theories. This method has
been employed to study non-perturbative effects on the phase
transitions in scalar field theories
[
7
,
8
]
.
This has also been used to study kaon condensation in colour-flavour
locked phase
[
9
,
10

and also in the linear
sigma model at finite density and temperature
[

.

In this paper we take a scalar field theory with
\lambda\phi^{4}
interactions

[
12
,
13
,
14
]
and the scalar is coupled
to a massless fermion field through Yukawa interaction.
We have proceeded to compute the effective potential using the
formalism developed by Cornwall-Jackiw-Tomboulis (CJT)
[
6
]

to understand its nature at large coupling.
We have identified three different types of
2
-particle irreducible (2PI)
diagrams each of the first two types denoting infinite number of members
and a third type denoting a single member contributing to
the effective action. We have taken into account of the contributions of three
different types of diagrams and obtained the effective potential.
Since the closed form
expression of the effective potential has been obtained after
summing up of the infinite set of 2PI diagrams, the result is
inherently non-perturbative in nature.

We observed that the effective potential exhibits maxima and minima
above and below the zero potential line respectively
at non-zero
\phi
on either side of the minimum at
\phi=0

when the coupling constant is below certain characteristic value

\lambda_{1}
.
As the coupling constant increases beyond
\lambda_{1}

the maxima disappear and
\phi=0
becomes the maximum of the potential.
With the increase of
\lambda
beyond
\lambda_{1}
, the minima shift
towards
\phi=0
until it attains the value
\lambda_{2}
beyond which
the minima start shifting away from
\phi=0
. Since, the potential
becomes unstable at
\phi=0
beyond
\lambda=\lambda_{1}
, the
system may settle down at any one of the minima for
\lambda\geq\lambda_{1}
.
The fermion coupled to scalar acquires a mass at the positive minimum, which
breaks the inversion symmetry of the vacuum.

Discussions

We have obtained effective potentials for a scalar theory with

\lambda\phi^{4}
interaction, coupled to a massless fermion through Yukawa
interaction.
We have evaluated the sum of infinite number of 2PI diagrams of types
a
,

b
and a single diagram of type
c
contributing to the
effective potential using CJT method.
It is obtained as functional of the correlation
function
G
for constant
\phi
. The requirement that
the effective potential remains stationary under the variation of

G
gives an equation which is required to be solved to obtain
the effective potential as a function of
\phi
.
We solve the equation by the method of iteration to obtain
G

retaining terms to order
\phi^{2}/M^{2}(\phi)
or

k^{2}/M^{2}(\phi)
, where
k
is the external momentum of a Feynman Diagram.
It is observed that poles of
G
become imaginary for very large
coupling (
\hat{\lambda}>9.6
), which are indications of either
the invalidity of the result of iteration to first order only or, the
inadequate inclusion of more diagrams of different types, where
each of them may contain infinite number of members.

It is clear that owing to non-perturbative corrections to the effective
potential, it develops maxima of small height and minima of very large depth
above and below the zero potential line respectively on either side
of
\phi=0
. As the coupling increases the positions of the maxima
shift towards
\phi=0
and disappear for
\hat{\lambda}\geq 0.28
.
However, the minima shift towards
\phi=0
with
\hat{\lambda}

until it reaches the value
1.0
and beyond this value the minima
shift away from the point
\phi=0
with increase of
\hat{\lambda}
.
It is to note that the maxima of the potential disappear for

\hat{\lambda}\geq 0.28
. It indicates that the potential
shows an instability around the classical minimum at
\phi=0

in the region
\hat{\lambda}\geq 0.28
and thereby, achieving
a stable configurations around any one of the non-trivial
minima of the potential. The behaviour of the potential shows the
presence of symmetry under the transformation
\phi\rightarrow-\phi
.
Fermion receives mass in a non-trivial minima at positive
\phi
,
breaking the inversion symmetry of the vacuum.
