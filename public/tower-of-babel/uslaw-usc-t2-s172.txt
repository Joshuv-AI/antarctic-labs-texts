2 U.S.C. § 172 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:2:172
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section172
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 5 (Library Of Congress). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in sections 171 to 175 of this title—
(1)
the term Center means the Center for the Book;
(2)
the term Librarian means the Librarian of Congress.
