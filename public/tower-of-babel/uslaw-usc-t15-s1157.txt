15 U.S.C. § 1157 — Relation to other provisions
Citation: us:usc:15:1157
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1157
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 23 (Dissemination Of Technical, Scientific And Engineering Information). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to repeal or amend any other legislation pertaining to the Department of Commerce or its component offices or bureaus.
