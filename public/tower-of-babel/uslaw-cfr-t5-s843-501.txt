5 C.F.R. § 843.501 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:5:843.501
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-843.501
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 843 (Federal Employees Retirement System—Death Benefits And Employee Refunds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart explains the benefit payable under FERS to an insurable interest beneficiary based on the death of a retiree who elected to take an annuity reduction to provide such benefits.
