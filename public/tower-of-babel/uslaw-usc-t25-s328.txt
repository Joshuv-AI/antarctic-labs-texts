25 U.S.C. § 328 — Rules and regulations

Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 8 (Rights-Of-Way Through Indian Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior is authorized to prescribe any necessary regulations for the purpose of administering the provisions of sections 323 to 328 of this title.
