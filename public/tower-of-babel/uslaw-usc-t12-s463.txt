12 U.S.C. § 463 — Limitation on amount of balance with any depository institution without access to Federal Reserve advances
Citation: us:usc:12:463
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section463
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 3 (Federal Reserve System); Subchapter XIV (Bank Reserves). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No member bank shall keep on deposit with any depository institution which is not authorized to have access to Federal Reserve advances under section 347b 1 of this title a sum in excess of 10 per centum of its own paid-up capital and surplus.
