17 U.S.C. § 1324 — Power of court over registration
Citation: us:usc:17:1324
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title17-section1324
Context: United States Code, Title 17 (Copyrights); Chapter 13 (Protection Of Original Designs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In any action involving the protection of a design under this chapter, the court, when appropriate, may order registration of a design under this chapter or the cancellation of such a registration. Any such order shall be certified by the court to the Administrator, who shall make an appropriate entry upon the record.
