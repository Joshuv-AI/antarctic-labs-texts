5 C.F.R. § 359.805 — Appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:5:359.805
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-359.805
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 359 (Removal From The Senior Executive Service; Guaranteed Placement In Other Personnel Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A career appointee who has been furloughed and who believes this subpart or the agency's procedures have not been correctly applied may appeal to the Merit Systems Protection Board under provisions of the Board's regulations.
