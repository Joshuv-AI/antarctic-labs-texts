47 C.F.R. § 73.6002 — Licensing requirements.

Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-73.6002
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 73 (Radio Broadcast Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A Class A television broadcast license will only be issued to a qualified low power television licensee that:
(1) Filed a Statement of Eligibility for Class A Low Power Television Station Status on or before January 28, 2000, which was granted by the Commission; and
(2) Files an acceptable application for a Class A Television license (FCC Form 2100, Schedule F).
