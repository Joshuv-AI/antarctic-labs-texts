42 U.S.C. § 6323a — Matching State contributions
Citation: us:usc:42:6323a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section6323a
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 77 (Energy Conservation); Subchapter III (Improving Energy Efficiency); Part B (State Energy Conservation Plans). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the base State Energy Conservation Program (part D of the Energy Policy and Conservation Act, sections 361 through 366 [42 U.S.C. 6321–6326]), each State will hereafter match in cash or in kind not less than 20 percent of the Federal contribution.
