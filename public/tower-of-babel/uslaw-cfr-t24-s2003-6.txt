24 C.F.R. § 2003.6 — Disclosure of a record to a person other than the individual to whom it pertains.
Citation: us:cfr:24:2003.6
Creator: Office Of Inspector General, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-2003.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 2003 (Implementation Of The Privacy Act Of 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The OIG may disclose an individual's record to a person other than the individual to whom the record pertains in the following instances:
(1) Upon written request by the individual, including authorization under 24 CFR 16.5(e);
(2) With the prior written consent of the individual;
(3) To a parent or legal guardian of the individual under 5 U.S.C. 552a(h); or
(4) When permitted by the provisions of 5 U.S.C. 552a(b) (1) through (12).
