5 C.F.R. § 4.3 — Prohibition against securing withdrawal from competition.
Citation: us:cfr:5:4.3
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-4.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 4 (Prohibited Practices (Rule Iv)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person shall influence another person to withdraw from competition for any position in the competitive service for the purpose of either improving or injuring the prospects of any applicant for appointment.
