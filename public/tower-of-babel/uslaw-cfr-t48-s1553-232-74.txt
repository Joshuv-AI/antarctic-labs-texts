48 C.F.R. § 1553.232-74 — 1553.232-74 EPA Form 1900-10, Contractor's Cumulative Claim and Reconciliation.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1553.232-74
Creator: Chapter 15—Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1553.232-74
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1553 (Forms). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As prescribed in 1532.170(a), the EPA Form 1900-10 shall be used for an accounting of the cumulative charges and costs for cost-reimbursement contracts from the inception of the contract to completion. It shall be submitted by the Contractor along with the completion voucher.
