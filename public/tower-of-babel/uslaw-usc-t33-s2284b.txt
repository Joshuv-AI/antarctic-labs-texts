33 U.S.C. § 2284b — Scenic and aesthetic considerations
Citation: us:usc:33:2284b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section2284b
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 36 (Water Resources Development); Subchapter V (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In conducting studies of potential water resources projects, the Secretary shall consider measures to preserve and enhance scenic and aesthetic qualities in the vicinity of such projects.
