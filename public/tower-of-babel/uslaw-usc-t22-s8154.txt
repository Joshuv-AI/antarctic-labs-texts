22 U.S.C. § 8154 — Rule of construction
Citation: us:usc:22:8154
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section8154
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 88 (Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty—United States Additional Protocol Implementation); Subchapter V (Environmental Sampling). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this subchapter, the term “necessary to increase the capability of the IAEA to detect undeclared nuclear activities in the territory of a non-nuclear-weapon State Party” shall not be construed to encompass proposed uses of environmental sampling that might assist the IAEA in detecting undeclared nuclear activities in the territory of a non-nuclear-weapon State Party by—
(1)
setting a good example of cooperation in the conduct of such sampling; or
(2)
facilitating the formation of a political consensus or political support for such sampling in the territory of a non-nuclear-weapon State Party.
