11 U.S.C. § 1505 — Authorization to act in a foreign country
Citation: us:usc:11:1505
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section1505
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 15 (Ancillary And Other Cross-Border Cases); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A trustee or another entity (including an examiner) may be authorized by the court to act in a foreign country on behalf of an estate created under section 541. An entity authorized to act under this section may act in any way permitted by the applicable foreign law.
