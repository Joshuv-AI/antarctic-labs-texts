15 U.S.C. § 762 — Establishment
Citation: us:usc:15:762
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section762
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 16B (Federal Energy Administration); Subchapter I (Federal Energy Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is hereby established an independent agency in the executive branch to be known as the Federal Energy Administration (hereinafter in this chapter referred to as the “Administration”).
