41 C.F.R. § 102-38.130 — -38.130 Applicability of this part to SASPs when conducting sales.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-38.130
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-38.130
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-38 (38—Sale Of Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

SASPs must follow the regulations in this part when conducting sales of Government personal property in their custody on behalf of GSA.
