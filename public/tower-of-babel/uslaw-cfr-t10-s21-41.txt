10 C.F.R. § 21.41 — Inspections.
Citation: us:cfr:10:21.41
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-21.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 21 (Reporting Of Defects And Noncompliance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each individual, corporation, partnership, dedicating entity, or other entity subject to the regulations in this part shall permit the Commission to inspect records, premises, activities, and basic components as necessary to accomplish the purposes of this part.
