22 C.F.R. § 92.42 — Certification of copies of foreign records relating to land titles.
Citation: us:cfr:22:92.42
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-92.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 92 (Notarial And Related Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In certifying documents of the kind described in title 28, section 1742, of the United States Code, diplomatic and consular officers of the United States will conform to the Federal procedures for authenticating foreign public documents (§ 92.39), unless otherwise instructed in a specific case.
