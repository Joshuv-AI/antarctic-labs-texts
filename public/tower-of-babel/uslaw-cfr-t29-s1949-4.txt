29 C.F.R. § 1949.4 — Procedure for payment.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1949.4
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1949.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1949 (Office Of Training And Education, Occupational Safety And Health Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Applications for Institute courses shall be submitted to the Institute Registrar's office in accordance with instructions issued by the Institute.
(b) Private sector personnel shall, upon notification of their acceptance by the Institute, submit a check payable to “U.S. Department of Labor” in the amount indicated by the course announcement prior to the commencement of the course.
