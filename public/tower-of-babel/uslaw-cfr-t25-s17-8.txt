25 C.F.R. § 17.8 — Supplemental hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:25:17.8
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-17.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 17 (Action On Wills Of Osage Indians). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When it appears that a supplemental hearing is necessary to secure material evidence, such a hearing may be conducted after notice has been given to those persons on whom notice of the original hearing was served and to such other persons as the testimony taken at the original hearing indicates may have a possible interest in the estate.
