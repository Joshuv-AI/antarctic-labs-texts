42 C.F.R. § 63a.5 — a.5 How to apply for a training grant.
Citation: us:cfr:42:63a.5
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-63a.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 63a (PART 63a—NATIONAL INSTITUTES OF HEALTH TRAINING GRANTS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any agency, institution, or entity interested in applying for a grant under this part must submit an application at the time and in the form and manner that the Secretary may require.
