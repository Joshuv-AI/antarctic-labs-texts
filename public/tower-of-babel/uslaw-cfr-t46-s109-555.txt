46 C.F.R. § 109.555 — Propulsion boilers.
Citation: us:cfr:46:109.555
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-109.555
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 109 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The master or person in charge and the engineer in charge shall ensure that—
(a) Steam pressure does not exceed that allowed by the certificate of inspection; and
(b) The safety valves, once set, are not tampered with or made inoperative.
