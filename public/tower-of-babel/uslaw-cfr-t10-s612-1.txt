10 C.F.R. § 612.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:10:612.1
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-612.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 612 (Recapture Of Civil Nuclear Credits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part implements section 40323(g)(2) of the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act (Pub. L. 117-58), codified at 42 U.S.C. 18753(g)(2), to set forth the procedure to recapture credits awarded pursuant to the civil nuclear credit program.
