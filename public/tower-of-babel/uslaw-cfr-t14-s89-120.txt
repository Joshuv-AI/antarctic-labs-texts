14 C.F.R. § 89.120 — Operations for aeronautical research or to show compliance with regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:14:89.120
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-89.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 89 (Remote Identification Of Unmanned Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator may authorize operations without remote identification where the operation is solely for the purpose of aeronautical research or to show compliance with regulations.
