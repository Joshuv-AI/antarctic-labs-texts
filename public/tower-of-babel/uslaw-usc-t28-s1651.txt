28 U.S.C. § 1651 — Writs
Citation: us:usc:28:1651
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1651
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part V (Procedure); Chapter 111 (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Supreme Court and all courts established by Act of Congress may issue all writs necessary or appropriate in aid of their respective jurisdictions and agreeable to the usages and principles of law.
(b)
An alternative writ or rule nisi may be issued by a justice or judge of a court which has jurisdiction.
