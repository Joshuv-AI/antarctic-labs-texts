12 C.F.R. § 702.209 — Reserves.
Citation: us:cfr:12:702.209
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-702.209
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 702 (Capital Adequacy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each new credit union shall establish and maintain such reserves as may be required by the FCUA, by state law, by regulation, or in special cases by the NCUA Board or appropriate state official.
