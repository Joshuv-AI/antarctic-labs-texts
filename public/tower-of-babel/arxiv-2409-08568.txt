Predictions for the rare kaon decays K S , L → π 0 ​ ℓ + ​ ℓ − K_{S,L}\to\pi^{0}\ell^{+}\ell^{-} from QCD in the limit of a large number of colours


Abstract
The long-distance and non-local parts of the form factors describing the single-photon mediated K S , L → π 0 ​ γ ∗ → π 0 ​ ℓ + ​ ℓ − K_{S,L}\to\pi^{0}\gamma^{*}\to\pi^{0}\ell^{+}\ell^{-} ( ℓ = e , μ \ell=e,\mu ) transitions in the standard model are addressed in QCD in the limit where the number N c N_{c} of colours becomes infinite. It is shown that this provides a suitable theoretical framework to study these decay modes and that it allows to predict the decay rates for K S → π 0 ​ ℓ + ​ ℓ − K_{S}\to\pi^{0}\ell^{+}\ell^{-} . It also unambiguously predicts that the interference between the direct and indirect CP-violating contributions to the decay rate for K L → π 0 ​ ℓ + ​ ℓ − K_{L}\to\pi^{0}\ell^{+}\ell^{-} is constructive.

It remains to discuss the functions

and
{\tilde{\cal P}}(q^{2},(q-k)^{2})

the factors of the kaon poles replaced by

the chiral limit,

must vanish linearly with vanishing quark masses. Indeed, estimates based on QCD sum rules

[

[

]
, and an even smaller value for the second radial excitation.
In addition, the factor
M_{K^{\prime}}^{2}
in the residue of the pole in cancelled
by the denominator when one eventually takes

\sim-F_{K^{\prime}}

, this indicates that the
contributions of
