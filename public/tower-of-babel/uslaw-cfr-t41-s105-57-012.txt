41 C.F.R. § 105-57.012 — -57.012 Actions prohibited by the employer.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-57.012
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-57.012
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-57 (57—Administration Wage Garnishment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employer may not discharge, refuse to employ, or take disciplinary action against the debtor due to the issuance of a withholding order under this part. See 31 U.S.C. 3720D(e).
