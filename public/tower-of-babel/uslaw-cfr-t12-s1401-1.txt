12 C.F.R. § 1401.1 — Cross-references to employee ethical conduct standards and financial disclosure regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1401.1
Creator: Farm Credit System Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1401.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1401 (Employee Responsibilities And Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Board members, officers, and other employees of the Farm Credit System Insurance Corporation are subject to the Standards of Ethical Conduct for Employees of the Executive Branch at 5 CFR part 2635, the Farm Credit System Insurance Corporation regulation at 5 CFR part 4001, which supplements the Executive Branch-wide Standards, and the executive branch-wide financial disclosure regulations at 5 CFR part 2634.
