42 U.S.C. § 1474a — Security for direct or insured rural housing loans to farmer applicants
Citation: us:usc:42:1474a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1474a
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 8A (Slum Clearance, Urban Renewal, And Farm Housing); Subchapter III (Farm Housing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On and after August 8, 1968, farmer applicants for direct or insured rural housing loans shall be required to provide only such collateral security as is required of owners of nonfarm tracts.
