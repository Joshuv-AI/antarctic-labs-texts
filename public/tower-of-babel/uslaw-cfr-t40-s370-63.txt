40 C.F.R. § 370.63 — What responsibilities do the SERC and LEPC have to make requested information available?
Citation: us:cfr:40:370.63
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-370.63
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 370 (Hazardous Chemical Reporting: Community Right-To-Know). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Under this subpart, the SERC or LEPC must make the following information (except for confidential location information discussed in § 370.64(b)) available if a person requests it:
(a) All information obtained from an owner or operator in response to a request under this subpart.
(b) Any requested Tier II information or SDS otherwise in possession of the SERC or the LEPC.
