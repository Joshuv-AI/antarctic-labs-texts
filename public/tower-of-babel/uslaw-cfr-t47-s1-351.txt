47 C.F.R. § 1.351 — Rules of evidence.
Citation: us:cfr:47:1.351
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-1.351
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 1 (Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In hearings subject to this subpart B, any oral or documentary evidence may be adduced, but the presiding officer shall exclude irrelevant, immaterial, or unduly repetitious evidence.
