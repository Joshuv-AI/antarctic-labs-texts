37 C.F.R. § 352.3 — Final determinations.
Citation: us:cfr:37:352.3
Creator: Copyright Royalty Board, Library Of Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-352.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 352 (Determinations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless a motion for a rehearing is timely filed within 15 days, the determination by the Copyright Royalty Judges pursuant to 17 U.S.C. 803(c) in a proceeding is final when it is issued.
