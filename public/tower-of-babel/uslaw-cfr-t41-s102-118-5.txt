41 C.F.R. § 102-118.5 — -118.5 Objective.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-118 (118—Transportation Payment And Audit). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to interpret statutes and other policies that assure that payment and payment mechanisms for agency transportation services are uniform and appropriate. This part communicates the policies clearly to agencies and transportation service providers (TSPs). (See § 102-118.15 for the definition of TSP.)
