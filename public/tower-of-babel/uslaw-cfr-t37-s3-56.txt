37 C.F.R. § 3.56 — Conditional assignments.
Citation: us:cfr:37:3.56
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-3.56
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 3 (Assignment, Recording And Rights Of Assignee). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Assignments which are made conditional on the performance of certain acts or events, such as the payment of money or other condition subsequent, if recorded in the Office, are regarded as absolute assignments for Office purposes until cancelled with the written consent of all parties or by the decree of a court of competent jurisdiction. The Office does not determine whether such conditions have been fulfilled.
