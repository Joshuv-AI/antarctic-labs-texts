7 C.F.R. § 1924.274 — Final inspection.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1924.274
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Rural Business-Cooperative Service, And Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1924.274
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1924 (Construction And Repair). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except for emergency repairs, all repair work must be performed in accordance with subpart A of this part. In all cases, Rural Development will make a final inspection of the repair work performed before final payment is made for the work.
