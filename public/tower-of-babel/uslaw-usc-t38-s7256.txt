38 U.S.C. § 7256 — Times and places of sessions
Citation: us:usc:38:7256
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section7256
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part V (Boards, Administrations, And Services); Chapter 72 (United States Court Of Appeals For Veterans Claims); Subchapter I (Organization And Jurisdiction). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The times and places of sessions of the Court of Appeals for Veterans Claims shall be prescribed by the chief judge.
