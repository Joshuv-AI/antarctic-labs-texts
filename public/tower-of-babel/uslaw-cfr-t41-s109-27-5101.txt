41 C.F.R. § 109-27.5101 — -27.5101 Definition.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 109-27 (27—Inventory Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Precious metals means uncommon and highly valuable metals characterized by their superior resistance to corrosion and oxidation. Included are gold, silver, and the platinum group metals—platinum, palladium, rhodium, iridium, ruthenium and osmium.
