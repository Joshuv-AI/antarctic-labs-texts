48 C.F.R. § 1505.000 — 1505.000 Scope of part.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1505.000
Creator: Chapter 15—Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1505.000
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1505 (Publicizing Contract Actions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part provides instructions on publicizing contract opportunities and response time, instructions on information to include in the synopses of proposed contracts, instructions on publicizing orders under GSA schedule contracts, policy references relative to release of information, and procedures for obtaining information on previous Government contracts.
