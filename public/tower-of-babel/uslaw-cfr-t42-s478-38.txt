42 C.F.R. § 478.38 — Effect of a reconsidered determination.
Citation: us:cfr:42:478.38
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-478.38
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 478 (Reconsiderations And Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A QIO reconsidered determination is binding upon all parties to the reconsideration unless—
(a) A hearing is requested in accordance with § 478.40 and a final decision rendered; or
(b) The reconsidered determination is later reopened and revised in accordance with § 478.48.
