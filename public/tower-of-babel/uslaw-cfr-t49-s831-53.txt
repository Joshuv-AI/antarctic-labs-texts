49 C.F.R. § 831.53 — Authority of Director, Office of Marine Safety.
Citation: us:cfr:49:831.53
Creator: National Transportation Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-831.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 831 (Investigation Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director, Office of Marine Safety, subject to the provisions of § 831.52 of this part and part 800 of this chapter, may order an investigation into any major marine casualty or marine casualty.
