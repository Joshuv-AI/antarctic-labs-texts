16 U.S.C. § 773b — Acceptance or rejection of Commission recommendations
Citation: us:usc:16:773b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section773b
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 10 (Northern Pacific Halibut Fishing); Subchapter IV (Northern Pacific Halibut Act Of 1982). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of State, with the concurrence of the Secretary, may accept or reject, on behalf of the United States, recommendations made by the Commission in accordance with article III of the Convention and paragraphs 14 and 15 of the annex to the Convention.
