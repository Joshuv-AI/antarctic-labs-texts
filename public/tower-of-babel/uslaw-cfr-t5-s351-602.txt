5 C.F.R. § 351.602 — Prohibitions.
Citation: us:cfr:5:351.602
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-351.602
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 351 (Reduction In Force). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An agency may not release a competing employee from a competitive level while retaining in that level an employee with:
(a) A specifically limited temporary or term promotion; or
(b) A written decision under part 432 or 752 of this chapter of removal or demotion from the competitive level.
