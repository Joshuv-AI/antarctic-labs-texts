10 U.S.C. § 12737 — Limitation on active duty
Citation: us:usc:10:12737
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section12737
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel Generally); Chapter 1223 (Retired Pay For Non-Regular Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A member of the armed forces may not be ordered to active duty solely for the purpose of qualifying the member for retired pay under this chapter.
