33 U.S.C. § 3505 — Effect on other laws
Citation: us:usc:33:3505
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section3505
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 48 (Ocean And Coastal Mapping Integration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to supersede or alter the existing authorities of any Federal agency with respect to ocean and coastal mapping.
