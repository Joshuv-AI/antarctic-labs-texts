Autism Longitudinal Data Project and Linking the Boston Birth 
Cohort and Pregnancy to Early Life Longitudinal Data System and

AGENCY: Health Resources and Services Administration (HRSA), Department 
of Health and Human Services.

ACTION: Notice of supplemental funding to Johns Hopkins University 
(JHU), a current HRSA awardee for the Autism Longitudinal Data Project.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: HRSA administers this grant using funding made available by 
the Health and Human Services Assistant Secretary for Planning and 
Evaluation (ASPE) from the Patient Centered Outcomes Research Trust 
Fund (PCORTF) and awarded for this purpose to JHU. ASPE/PCORTF supports 
this multi-agency data linkage project to build further data capacity. 
Supplemental funding was previously awarded in fiscal year (FY) 2024 
and FY25 to initiate the data linkage and user testing process. This 
award is a continuation of this work and the final year for this ASPE/
PCORTF award. If JHU does not receive this award, it will not have the 
funds needed to execute the final phase of the data linkage project.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Evva Assing-Murray, Senior Health

    Justification: There is a lack of longitudinal data from birth 
cohorts in the United States to assess maternal and infant health and 
inter-generational health from a life course perspective. Through this 
supplement, the data linkages will generate rich datasets with broad 
array of data on biological, socio-economic, home and neighborhood 
environment, behavioral risk and protective factors, health services 
and expenses across the developmental and life stages. JHU is uniquely 
qualified to conduct this work because it has:
     JHU is currently in the 4th year of its second 5-year 
award from HRSA and has consistently met its goals and objectives.
     Led and managed the Boston Birth Cohort since its 
inception in 1998. As such, is experienced in the complexities of data 
sharing across institutions and technical aspects of linking data.
     Access to these restricted datasets. The Boston Birth 
Cohort is a restricted use data resource that contains personally 
identifiable, confidential data, the disclosure of which is protected 
by law. JHU and its clinical site, Boston Medical Center, maintain 
these data and are the only entities that currently have access to the 
datasets required for this activity.
    This data linkage project will run from July 2024 to July 2027. 
This is a continuation of an ongoing activity that was initially funded 
in FY24 by ASPE/PCORTF to the same intended recipient. Not making the 
award would preclude the successful implementation of the data linkage 
project by JHU.
