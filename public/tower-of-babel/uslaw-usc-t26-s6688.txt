26 U.S.C. § 6688 — Assessable penalties with respect to information required to be furnished under section 7654
Citation: us:usc:26:6688
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section6688
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 68 (Additions To The Tax, Additional Amounts, And Assessable Penalties); Subchapter B (Assessable Penalties); Part I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In addition to any criminal penalty provided by law, any person described in section 7654(a) who is required under section 937(c) or by regulations prescribed under section 7654 to furnish information and who fails to comply with such requirement at the time prescribed by such regulations unless it is shown that such failure is due to reasonable cause and not to willful neglect, shall pay (upon notice and demand by the Secretary and in the same manner as tax) a penalty of $1,000 for each such failure.
