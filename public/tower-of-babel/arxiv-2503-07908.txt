1.
Introduction

In this paper, we study the existence of solutions to stochastic incompressible non-Newtonian fluid models governed by the system:

\begin{split}du&=(\divg(\mdmathbb{F}(\mathbf{D}u)\mathbf{D}u-u\otimes u)-\nabla p)dt+gdB_{t},\quad\divg u=0,\quad u|_{t=0}=u_{0}\end{split}

(1.1)

for
(t,x)\in(0,\infty)\times{\mdmathbb R}^{n}
,
n\geq 2
with the given initial condition
u_{0}
and random noise
g(t,x)dB_{t}
, where
B_{t}
denotes Brownian motion.
Here, the diffusion term
\divg(\mdmathbb{F}(\mathbf{D}u)\mathbf{D}u)
models the non-Newtonian stress tensor.
The symmetric gradient
\mathbf{D}u
is defined as

\mathbf{D}u=\frac{1}{2}(\nabla u+(\nabla u)^{T}),

where
(\nabla u)^{T}
is the transpose of the gradient matrix
\nabla u
.
When
\mdmathbb{F}(\mathbf{D}u)=\mdmathbb{I}
( the
n\times n
identity matrix), the system (
1.1
) reduces to the stochastic Navier-Stokes equations, which describe standard Newtonian fluids.

Common choices for the stress tensor
\mdmathbb{F}(\mathbf{D}u)
include:

\begin{split}\mdmathbb{F}_{1}(\mathbf{D}u)&=(\mu_{0}+\mu_{1}|\mathbf{D}u|)^{d-2}\mdmathbb{I},\\
\mdmathbb{F}_{2}(\mathbf{D}u)&=(\mu_{0}+\mu_{1}|\mathbf{D}u|^{2})^{\frac{d-2}{2}}\mdmathbb{I},\\
\mdmathbb{F}_{3}(\mathbf{D}u)&=(\mu_{0}+\mu_{1}|\mathbf{D}u|^{d-2})\mdmathbb{I},\end{split}

(1.2)

where
\mu_{0}
and
\mu_{1}
are positive constants.
The norm of a matrix
{\mdmathbb A}=(a_{ij})_{1\leq ij\leq n}
is given by
|{\mdmathbb A}|=\big(\sum_{1\leq i,j\leq n}|a_{ij}|^{2}\big)^{\frac{1}{2}}
.
The parameter
d
characterizes the fluid’s behavior.
Shear-thinning fluids correspond to
1<d<2
and shear-thickening fluids correspond to
2<d<\infty
.

We review prior results on the existence of solutions relevant to our study.
The deterministic incompressible non-Newtonian fluid equations have been extensively studied by many mathematicians.

Many prominent mathematicians, including Nečas, have made significant contributions to this topic (see

He established the local existence of weak solutions to (

Kang et al.

Motivated by these results, we aim to establish the existence of solutions to stochastic incompressible non-Newtonian fluid models in
{\mdmathbb R}^{n}
.
We emphasize that our solutions are constructed in standard Besov spaces.

To proceed, we assume that
