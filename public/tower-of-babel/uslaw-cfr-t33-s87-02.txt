33 C.F.R. § 87.02 — Exclusive use.
Citation: us:cfr:33:87.02
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-87.02
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 87 (Annex Iv: Distress Signals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The use or exhibition of any of the foregoing signals except for the purpose of indicating distress and need of assistance and the use of other signals which may be confused with any of the above signals is prohibited.
