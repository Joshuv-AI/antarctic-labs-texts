49 C.F.R. § 71.14 — Chamorro Zone.
Citation: us:cfr:49:71.14
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-71.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 71 (Standard Time Zone Boundaries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The ninth zone, the Chamorro standard time zone, includes the Island of Guam and the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands.
