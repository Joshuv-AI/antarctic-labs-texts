10 U.S.C. § 8661 — Classification
Citation: us:usc:10:8661
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8661
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 863 (Naval Vessels). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may establish, and from time to time modify, as the needs of the service require, a classification of naval vessels.
