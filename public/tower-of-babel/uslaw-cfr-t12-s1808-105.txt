12 C.F.R. § 1808.105 — Relationship to other CDFI Fund programs.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1808.105
Creator: Community Development Financial Institutions Fund, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1808.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1808 (Community Development Financial Institutions Bond Guarantee Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Award funds received under any other CDFI Fund program cannot be used by any participant, including Qualified Issuers, Eligible CDFIs and Secondary Borrowers, to pay principal, interest, fees, administrative costs, or issuance costs (including Bond Issuance Fees) related to the CDFI Bond Guarantee Program, or to fund the Risk-Share Pool.
