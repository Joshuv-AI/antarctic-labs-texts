45 C.F.R. § 1608.2 — Definition.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1608.2
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1608.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1608 (Prohibited Political Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Legal assistance activities, as used in this part, means any activity.
(a) Carried out during an employee's working hours;
(b) Using resources provided by the Corporation or by a recipient; or
(c) That, in fact, provides legal advice, or representation to an eligible client.
