10 U.S.C. § 8118 — Rank of line and staff corps officers of the Navy and officers of the Marine Corps
Citation: us:usc:10:8118
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8118
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 812 (Grade And Rank Of Officers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except for an officer entitled to a rank higher than his grade, line and staff corps officers of the Navy serving in the same grade and officers of the Marine Corps serving in the corresponding grade rank among themselves according to their respective dates of rank in grade whether or not they are on an active-duty list.
