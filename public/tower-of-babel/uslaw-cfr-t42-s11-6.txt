42 C.F.R. § 11.6 — What are the requirements for the submission of truthful information?
Citation: us:cfr:42:11.6
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-11.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 11 (Clinical Trials Registration And Results Information Submission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The clinical trial information submitted by a responsible party under this part shall not be false or misleading in any particular. A responsible party who submits false and/or misleading information is subject to civil monetary penalties and/or other civil or criminal remedies available under U.S. law.
