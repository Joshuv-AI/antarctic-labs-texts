30 U.S.C. § 1757 — Severability
Citation: us:usc:30:1757
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section1757
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 29 (Oil And Gas Royalty Management); Subchapter III (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this chapter or the applicability thereof to any person or circumstances is held invalid, the remainder of this chapter and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
