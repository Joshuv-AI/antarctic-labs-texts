46 U.S.C. § 12303 — Exemption from numbering requirements
Citation: us:usc:46:12303
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section12303
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part H (Identification Of Vessels); Chapter 123 (Numbering Undocumented Vessels). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
When the Secretary is the authority issuing a number under this chapter, the Secretary may exempt a vessel or class of vessels from the numbering requirements of this chapter under conditions the Secretary may prescribe.
(b)
When a State is the issuing authority, it may exempt from the numbering requirements of this chapter a vessel or class of vessels exempted under subsection (a) of this section or otherwise as permitted by the Secretary.
