29 C.F.R. § 1204.2 — Arbitrary changing of contracts.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1204.2
Creator: National Mediation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1204.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1204 (Labor Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No carrier, its officers, or agents shall change the rates of pay, rules, or working conditions of its employees, as a class as embodied in agreements except in the manner prescribed in such agreements or in section 6 of the Railway Labor Act.
