28 C.F.R. § 24.301 — Filing and service of documents.
Citation: us:cfr:28:24.301
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-24.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 24 (Implementation Of The Equal Access To Justice Act In Department Of Justice Administrative Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An application for an award and any other pleading or document related to the application shall be filed and served on all parties to the proceeding in the same manner as other pleadings in the proceeding.
