20 C.F.R. § 416.1701 — Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:20:416.1701
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-416.1701
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 416 (Supplemental Security Income For The Aged, Blind, And Disabled). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart describes whom we refer to agencies for vocational rehabilitation services. The purpose of these services is to restore your ability to work.
