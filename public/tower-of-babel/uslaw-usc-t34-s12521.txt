34 U.S.C. § 12521 — Increased penalties for drug-dealing in “drug-free” zones
Citation: us:usc:34:12521
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section12521
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 121 (Violent Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Subchapter IV (Drug Control). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Pursuant to its authority under section 994 of title 28, the United States Sentencing Commission shall amend its sentencing guidelines to provide an appropriate enhancement for a defendant convicted of violating section 860 of title 21.
