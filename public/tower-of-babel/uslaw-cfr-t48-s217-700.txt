48 C.F.R. § 217.700 — 217.700 Scope of subpart.

Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-217.700
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 217 (Special Contracting Methods). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart—
(a) Implements section 854 of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2005 (Pub. L. 108-375), section 801 of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2008 (Pub. L. 110-181), and section 806 of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2010 (Pub. L. 111-84); and
(b) Prescribes policy for the acquisition of supplies and services through the use of contracts or orders issued by non-DoD agencies.
