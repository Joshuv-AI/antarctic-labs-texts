18 C.F.R. § 153.17 — Effectiveness of Presidential Permit.
Citation: us:cfr:18:153.17
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-153.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 153 (Applications For Authorization To Construct, Operate, Or Modify Facilities Used For The Export Or Import Of Natural Gas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Presidential Permit, once issued by the Commission, shall not be effective until it has been accepted by the highest authority of the Permittee, as indicated by Permittee's execution of a Testimony of Acceptance, and a certified copy of the accepted Presidential Permit and the executed Testimony of Acceptance has been filed with the Commission.
