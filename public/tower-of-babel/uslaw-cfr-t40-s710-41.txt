40 C.F.R. § 710.41 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:40:710.41
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-710.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 710 (Compilation Of The Tsca Chemical Substance Inventory). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to the substantiation and review of claims of confidentiality asserted in Notices of Activity Form A to protect the specific chemical identities of chemical substances.
