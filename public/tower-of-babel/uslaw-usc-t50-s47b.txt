50 U.S.C. § 47b — Determination by Attorney General of entitlement and amount of reward; consultation; Presidential approval
Citation: us:usc:50:47b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section47b
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 4C (Atomic Weapons And Special Nuclear Materials Information Rewards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General shall determine whether a person furnishing information to the United States is entitled to a reward and the amount to be paid pursuant to section 47a of this title. Before making a reward under this section the Attorney General shall advise and consult with the Atomic Energy Commission. A reward of $50,000 or more may not be made without the approval of the President.
