31 C.F.R. § 360.61 — Payment after death.
Citation: us:cfr:31:360.61
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-360.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 360 (Regulations Governing Definitive United States Savings Bonds, Series I). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After the death of the ward, and at any time prior to the representative's discharge, the representative of the estate will be entitled to obtain payment of a bond to which the ward was solely entitled.
