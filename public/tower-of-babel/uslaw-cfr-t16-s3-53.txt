16 C.F.R. § 3.53 — Review of recommended decision in absence of exceptions.
Citation: us:cfr:16:3.53
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-3.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 3 (Rules Of Practice For Adjudicative Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If no party files exceptions to the recommended decision of the Administrative Law Judge under § 3.52(a)(1) or § 3.52(b)(1), the Commission will enter an order placing the case on its own docket for review. The Commission's order will set forth the scope of such review and the issues which will be considered and will make provision for the filing of briefs if deemed appropriate by the Commission.
