42 U.S.C. § 300a–6 — Prohibition against funding programs using abortion as family planning method
Citation: us:usc:42:300a–6
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section300a–6
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter VIII (Population Research And Voluntary Family Planning Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

None of the funds appropriated under this subchapter shall be used in programs where abortion is a method of family planning.
