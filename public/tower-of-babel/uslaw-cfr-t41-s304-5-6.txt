41 C.F.R. § 304-5.6 — -5.6 Multiple non-Federal source payments.
Citation: us:cfr:41:304-5.6
Creator: Chapter 304—Payment Of Travel Expenses From A Non-Federal Source
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-304-5.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 304-5 (5—Agency Responsibilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies may accept payment from more than one non-Federal source for a single trip, as long as the total of such payments do not exceed the total cost of the trip.
