42 C.F.R. § 84.196 — Harnesses; installation and construction; minimum requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:42:84.196
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-84.196
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 84 (Approval Of Respiratory Protective Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each respirator shall, where necessary, be equipped with a suitable harness designed and constructed to hold the components of the respirator in position against the wearer's body.
(b) Harnesses shall be designed and constructed to permit easy removal and replacement of respirator parts and, where applicable, provide for holding a full facepiece in the ready position when not in use.
