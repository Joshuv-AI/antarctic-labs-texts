30 C.F.R. § 800.12 — Form of the performance bond.
Citation: us:cfr:30:800.12
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-800.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 800 (Bond And Insurance Requirements For Surface Coal Mining And Reclamation Operations Under Regulatory Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulatory authority shall prescribe the form of the performance bond. The regulatory authority may allow for:
(a) A surety bond;
(b) A collateral bond;
(c) A self-bond; or
(d) A combination of any of these bonding methods.
