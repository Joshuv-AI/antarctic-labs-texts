41 C.F.R. § 101-27.500 — -27.500 Scope and applicability of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-27.500
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-27.500
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-27 (27—Inventory Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart sets forth policy and procedures for the return to GSA for credit of items which are in long supply or for which no current or future requirements are anticipated. The provisions of this subpart 101-27.5 are applicable to all executive agencies. Federal agencies other than executive agencies may participate in this program and are encouraged to do so.
