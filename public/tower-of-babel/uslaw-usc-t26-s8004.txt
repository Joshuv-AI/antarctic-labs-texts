26 U.S.C. § 8004 — Appointment and compensation of staff
Citation: us:usc:26:8004
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section8004
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 91 (Organization And Membership Of The Joint Committee). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as otherwise provided by law, the Joint Committee shall have power to appoint and fix the compensation of the Chief of Staff of the Joint Committee and such experts and clerical, stenographic, and other assistants as it deems advisable.
