7 C.F.R. § 1709.8 — Electronic submission.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1709.8
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1709.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1709 (Assistance To High Energy Cost Communities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applicants may submit applications and reports electronically if so provided in the applicable grant announcement and grant agreements or if other regulations provide for electronic submission. Any electronic submissions must be in the form prescribed in the applicable grant announcement, grant agreement, or regulation.
