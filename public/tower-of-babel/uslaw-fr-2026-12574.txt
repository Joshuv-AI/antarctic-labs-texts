DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Foreign-Trade Zones Board

[Order No. 2182]


Denial of Production Authority; Foreign-Trade Zone 3; Phillips 66 
Company; (Renewable Fuels and By-Products); Rodeo, California; 
Correction

SUMMARY: The Federal Register notice published on June 17, 2026, 
regarding Board Order 2182 not to approve the application submitted to 
the Foreign-Trade Zones (FTZ) Board by the grantee of FTZ 3, is being 
corrected.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Juanita Chen at 
[email protected]; telephone 202-482-1378.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: 

Correction

    In the Federal Register of June 17, 2026, in FR Doc. 2026-12216, on 
page 36569, the second paragraph, which states ``WHEREAS, the San 
Francisco Port Commission, grantee of FTZ 3, has requested production 
authority on behalf of Phillips 66 Company, within Subzone 3D in Rodeo, 
California (B-43-2024, docketed August 8, 2024)'' is corrected to read 
as ``WHEREAS, the San Francisco Port Commission, grantee of FTZ 3, has 
requested production authority on behalf of Phillips 66 Company, within 
Subzone 3E in Rodeo, California (B-43-2024, docketed August 8, 2024)''

    Dated: June 18, 2026.
Juanita Chen,
Acting Executive Secretary.
