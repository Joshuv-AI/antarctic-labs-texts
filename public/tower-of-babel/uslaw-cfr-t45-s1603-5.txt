45 C.F.R. § 1603.5 — How will LSC respond to a request for expert testimony from an employee?
Citation: us:cfr:45:1603.5
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1603.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1603 (Testimony By Employees And Production Of Documents In Proceedings Where The United States Is Not A Party). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No employee shall serve as an expert witness in any proceeding described in § 1603.1(c) of this part or before a court or agency of the United States unless the General Counsel or the OIG Legal Counsel authorizes the employee's participation.
