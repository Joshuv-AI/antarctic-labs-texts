34 C.F.R. § 75.617 — Access for individuals with disabilities.
Citation: us:cfr:34:75.617
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-75.617
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 75 (Direct Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A grantee must comply with the following Federal regulations on access by individuals with disabilities that apply to the construction of facilities:
(a) For residential facilities: 24 CFR part 40.
(b) For non-residential facilities: 41 CFR 102-76.60 to 102-76.95.
