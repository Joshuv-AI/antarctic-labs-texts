22 C.F.R. § 134.2 — When the Act applies.
Citation: us:cfr:22:134.2
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-134.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 134 (Equal Access To Justice Act; Implementation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Act applies to any adversary adjudication pending before the Department of State at any time between October 1, 1981 and September 30, 1984. This includes proceedings begun before October 1, 1981 if final agency action has not been taken before that date, and proceedings pending on September 30, 1984, regardless of when they were initiated or when final agency action occurs.
