40 C.F.R. § 180.1342 — d-Limonene; exemption from the requirement of a tolerance.
Citation: us:cfr:40:180.1342
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-180.1342
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 180 (Tolerances And Exemptions For Pesticide Chemical Residues In Food). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An exemption from the requirement of a tolerance is established for residues of d-limonene, (4R)-1-methyl-4-(1-methylethenyl)cyclohexene, in or on all food commodities when applied as an herbicide in accordance with good agricultural practices.
(b) An exemption from the requirement of a tolerance is established for residues of d-limonene, (4R)-1-methyl-4-(1-methylethenyl)cyclohexene, in or on all food commodities when applied as an insecticide in kitchens and pantries.
