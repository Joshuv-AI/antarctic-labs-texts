49 C.F.R. § 89.11 — Standards for exercise of delegated authority.
Citation: us:cfr:49:89.11
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-89.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 89 (Implementation Of The Federal Claims Collection Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The authority delegated under § 89.5 shall be exercised in accordance with the standards for the collection and compromise of claims and for the suspension and termination of action to collect claims promulgated by the United States General Accounting Office and the United States Department of Justice, and published at 4 CFR chapter II, as those standards may be amended.
