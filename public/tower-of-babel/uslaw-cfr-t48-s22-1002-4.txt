48 C.F.R. § 22.1002-4 — 22.1002-4 Application of the Fair Labor Standards Act minimum wage.
Citation: us:cfr:48:22.1002-4
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-22.1002-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 22 (Application Of Labor Laws To Government Acquisitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No contractor or subcontractor holding a service contract for any dollar amount shall pay any of its employees working on the contract less than the minimum wage specified in section 6(a)(1) of the Fair Labor Standards Act (29 U.S.C. 206).
