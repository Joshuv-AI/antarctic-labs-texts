20 C.F.R. § 903.7 — Guardianship.
Citation: us:cfr:20:903.7
Creator: Joint Board For The Enrollment Of Actuaries
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-903.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 903 (Access To Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The guardian of a person judicially determined to be incompetent shall, in addition to establishing the identity of the person he represents, establish his own guardianship by furnishing a copy of a court order establishing the guardianship and may thereafter act on behalf of such individual. (See 5 U.S.C. 552a(h)).
