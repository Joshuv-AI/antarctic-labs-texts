25 C.F.R. § 115.814 — If a tribe withdraws money from its trust account for a particular purpose or project, may the tribe redeposit any money that was not used for its intended purpose?
Citation: us:cfr:25:115.814
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-115.814
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 115 (Trust Funds For Tribes And Individual Indians). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A tribe may redeposit funds not used for a particular purpose or project if:
(a) The funds were withdrawn in accordance with:
(1) The terms of Trust Reform Act;
(2) The terms of the legislative settlement; or
(3) The terms of a judgment use and distribution plan; and
(b) The tribe can provide documentation showing the source of the funds to be redeposited.
