25 C.F.R. § 48.206 — What approvals are necessary to accept a donation under this subpart?
Citation: us:cfr:25:48.206
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-48.206
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 48 (Leases Of Land Or Facilities Of Bureau-Operated Schools And Fundraising Activities At Bureau-Operated Schools). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Prior to accepting a donation valued at $5,000 or more under this subpart, the Director's designee must approve the acceptance and certify that it complies with this subpart, including the considerations of § 48.205, Departmental policy, and any applicable statute or regulation.
