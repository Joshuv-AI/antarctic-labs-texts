50 C.F.R. § 14.253 — What are the restrictions contained in the regulations in this subpart?
Citation: us:cfr:50:14.253
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-14.253
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 14 (Importation, Exportation, And Transportation Of Wildlife). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in § 14.257, it is unlawful for any person to:
(a) Import, export, transport, sell, receive, acquire, or purchase, in interstate or foreign commerce, or in a manner substantially affecting interstate or foreign commerce, any live prohibited wildlife species;
(b) Breed any live prohibited wildlife species;
(c) Possess any live prohibited wildlife species; or
(d) Attempt to commit any act described in paragraphs (a) through (c) of this section.
