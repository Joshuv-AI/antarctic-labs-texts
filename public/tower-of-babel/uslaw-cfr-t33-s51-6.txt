33 C.F.R. § 51.6 — Propriety standard of review.
Citation: us:cfr:33:51.6
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-51.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 51 (Coast Guard Discharge Review Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A discharge is deemed to be proper except that:
(a) A discharge may be improper if an error of fact, law, procedure, or discretion was associated with the discharge at the time of issuance which prejudiced the rights of the applicant.
(b) A discharge may be improper if there has been a change in policy by the Coast Guard made expressly retroactive to the type of discharge under consideration.
