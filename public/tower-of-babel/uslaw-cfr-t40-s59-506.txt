40 C.F.R. § 59.506 — How do I demonstrate compliance if I manufacture multi-component kits?
Citation: us:cfr:40:59.506
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-59.506
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 59 (National Volatile Organic Compound Emission Standards For Consumer And Commercial Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If you manufacture multi-component kits as defined in § 59.503, then the Kit PWR must not exceed the Total Reactivity Limit.
(b) You must calculate the Kit PWR and the Total Reactivity Limit as follows:
(1) KIT PWR = (PWR(1) × W1) + (PWR(2) × W2) + . ... + (PWR(n) × Wn)
(2) Total Reactivity Limit = (RL1 × W1) + (RL2 × W2) + ... + (RLn × Wn).
(3) Kit PWR ≤Total Reactivity Limit.
