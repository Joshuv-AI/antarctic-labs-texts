22 C.F.R. § 120.61 — Person.
Citation: us:cfr:22:120.61
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-120.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 120 (Purpose And Definitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Person means a natural person as well as a corporation, business association, partnership, society, trust, or any other entity, organization or group, including governmental entities. If a provision in this subchapter does not refer exclusively to a foreign person or U.S. person, then it refers to both.
