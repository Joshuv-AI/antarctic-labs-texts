13 C.F.R. § 147.400 — What are my responsibilities as a(n) SBA awarding official?
Citation: us:cfr:13:147.400
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-147.400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 147 (Governmentwide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As a(n) SBA awarding official, you must obtain each recipient's agreement, as a condition of the award, to comply with the requirements in—
(a) Subpart B of this part, if the recipient is not an individual; or
(b) Subpart C of this part, if the recipient is an individual.
