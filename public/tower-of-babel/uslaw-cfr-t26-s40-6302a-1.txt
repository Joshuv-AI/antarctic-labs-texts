26 C.F.R. § 40.6302(a)-1 — (a)-1 Voluntary payments of excise taxes by electronic funds transfer.
Citation: us:cfr:26:40.6302(a)-1
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-40.6302(a)-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 40 (Excise Tax Procedural Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person may voluntarily remit by electronic funds transfer any payment of tax to which this part 40 applies. Such payment must be made in accordance with procedures prescribed by the Commissioner.
