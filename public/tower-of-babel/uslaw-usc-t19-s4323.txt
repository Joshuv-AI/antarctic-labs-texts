19 U.S.C. § 4323 — Appropriate congressional committees defined
Citation: us:usc:19:4323
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section4323
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 28 (Trade Facilitation And Trade Enforcement); Subchapter I (Trade Facilitation And Trade Enforcement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this subchapter, the term “appropriate congressional committees” means—
(1)
the Committee on Finance and the Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs of the Senate; and
(2)
the Committee on Ways and Means and the Committee on Homeland Security of the House of Representatives.
