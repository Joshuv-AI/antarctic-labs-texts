24 C.F.R. § 982.454 — Termination of HAP contract: Insufficient funding.
Citation: us:cfr:24:982.454
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-982.454
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 982 (Section 8 Tenant-Based Assistance: Housing Choice Voucher Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The PHA may terminate the HAP contract if the PHA determines, in accordance with HUD requirements, that funding under the consolidated ACC is insufficient to support continued assistance for families in the program.
