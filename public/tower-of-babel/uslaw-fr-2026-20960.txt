DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Health Resources and Services Administration


Health Center Program Performance Period Extensions; Correction

AGENCY: Health Resources and Services Administration (HRSA), Department 
of Health and Human Services (HHS).

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: HRSA published a document in the Federal Register on September 
24, 2026, concerning performance period extensions for health centers 
scheduled to compete for a January, February, or March FY 2027 Service 
Area Competition. The document did not include one service area that 
was not eligible for a performance period extension at the time of 
publication but has since become eligible.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Patricia Burbano, Lead Public Health 
Analyst, Office of Program and Policy Development, Bureau of Primary 
Care, Health Resources and Services Administration, at 
[email protected] and 301-443-7238.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Correction

60626), page 60632, column titled ``Grant No.'' add ``H80CS06645'' to 
the last row; column titled ``Budget period start'' add ``JAN;'' column 
titled ``Name'' add ``Sayre Health Center;'' column titled ``City'' add 
``Philadelphia;'' column titled ``State'' add ``PA;'' and column
