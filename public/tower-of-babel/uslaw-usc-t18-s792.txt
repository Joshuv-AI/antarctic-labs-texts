18 U.S.C. § 792 — Harboring or concealing persons
Citation: us:usc:18:792
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section792
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 37 (Espionage And Censorship). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever harbors or conceals any person who he knows, or has reasonable grounds to believe or suspect, has committed, or is about to commit, an offense under sections 793 or 794 of this title, shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than ten years, or both.
