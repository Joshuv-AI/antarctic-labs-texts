15 U.S.C. § 2105 — Application of other laws
Citation: us:usc:15:2105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section2105
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 48 (Hobby Protection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of this chapter are in addition to, and not in substitution for or limitation of, the provisions of any other law of the United States or of the law of any State.
