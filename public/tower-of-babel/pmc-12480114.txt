Trends in intake of sodium, 2012–2021
Published: 2023

In 2021, the sodium intake among the Korean population aged ≥1 year was 3,038 mg, showing a decrease over the past 10 years (Figure 1). Sodium intake was higher in men than that in women, and highest in 30s and 40s (Figure 2). Mean sodium intakes among all age groups excluding those aged 1–5 years were higher than the goal intake of sodium (2,000 mg).
