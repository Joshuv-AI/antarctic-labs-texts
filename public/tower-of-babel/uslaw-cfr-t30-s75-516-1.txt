30 C.F.R. § 75.516-1 — -1 Installed insulators.
Citation: us:cfr:30:75.516-1
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-75.516-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 75 (Mandatory Safety Standards—Underground Coal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Well-insulated insulators is interpreted to mean well-installed insulators. Insulated J-hooks may be used to suspend insulated power cables for temporary installation not exceeding 6 months and for permanent installation of control cables such as may be used along belt conveyors.
