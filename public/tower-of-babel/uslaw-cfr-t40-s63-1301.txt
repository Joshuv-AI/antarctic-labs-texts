40 C.F.R. § 63.1301 — Standards for rebond foam production.
Citation: us:cfr:40:63.1301
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-63.1301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 63 (National Emission Standards For Hazardous Air Pollutants For Source Categories). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each owner or operator of a new or existing rebond foam affected source shall comply with the provisions in paragraphs (a) and (b) of this section.
(a) A HAP or HAP-based material shall not be used as an equipment cleaner at a rebond foam source.
(b) A HAP-based mold release agent shall not be used in a rebond foam source.
