43 U.S.C. § 50b — Recording of obligations against accounts receivable and crediting of amounts received; work involving cooperation with State, Territory, etc.
Citation: us:usc:43:50b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section50b
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 2 (United States Geological Survey). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Before, on, and after October 18, 1986, in carrying out work involving cooperation with any State, Territory, possession, or political subdivision thereof, the United States Geological Survey may, notwithstanding any other provision of law, record obligations against accounts receivable from any such entities and shall credit amounts received from such entities to this appropriation.
