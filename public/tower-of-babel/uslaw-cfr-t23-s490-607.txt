23 C.F.R. § 490.607 — National performance management measures to assess freight movement on the Interstate System.
Citation: us:cfr:23:490.607
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-490.607
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 490 (National Performance Management Measures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The performance measure to assess freight movement on the Interstate System is the: Truck Travel Time Reliability (TTTR) Index (referred to as the Freight Reliability measure).
