38 U.S.C. § 8166 — Construction standards
Citation: us:usc:38:8166
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section8166
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part VI (Acquisition And Disposition Of Property); Chapter 81 (Acquisition And Operation Of Hospital And Domiciliary Facilities; Procurement And Supply; Enhanced-Use Leases Of Real Property); Subchapter V (Enhanced-Use Leases Of Real Property). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The construction, alteration, repair, remodeling, or improvement of a property that is the subject of an enhanced-use lease shall be carried out so as to comply with all applicable provisions of Federal, State, and local law relating to land use, building standards, permits, and inspections.
