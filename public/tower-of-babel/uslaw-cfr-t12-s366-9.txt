12 C.F.R. § 366.9 — What other requirements could prevent me from performing contractual services for the FDIC?
Citation: us:cfr:12:366.9
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-366.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 366 (Minimum Standards Of Integrity And Fitness For An Fdic Contractor). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must avoid a conflict of interest, be ethically responsible, and maintain confidential information as described in §§ 366.10 through 366.13. You must also provide us with the information we require in § 366.14. Failure to meet these requirements may prevent you from contracting with us.
