Retraction for Khan et al., Macrophage TBK1 signaling drives the development and outgrowth of breast cancer brain metastasis
Published: 2026

Retraction for “Macrophage TBK1 signaling drives the development and outgrowth of breast cancer brain metastasis,” by Fatima Khan, Yang Liu, Donovan Whitfield, Lizhi Pang, Heba Ali, Yuyun Huang, Fei Zhou, Robert S. Hagan, Katie Frenis, R. Grant Rowe, and Peiwen Chen; which published August 20, 2025; 10.1073/pnas.2420793122 (Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. U.S.A. 122, e2420793122).

The undersigned authors note, “We are retracting this paper because potential duplications of bioluminescence mouse images have been identified in Figs. 4–6, raising concerns regarding the integrity of the associated data. The undersigned authors regret these errors.” The journal was unable to reach Yang Liu, Donovan Whitfield, Heba Ali, Yuyun Huang, Fei Zhou, and Katie Frenis.

—Fatima Khan, Lizhi Pang, Robert S. Hagan, R. Grant Rowe, and Peiwen Chen
