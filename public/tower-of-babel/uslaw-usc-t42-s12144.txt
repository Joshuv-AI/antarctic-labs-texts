42 U.S.C. § 12144 — Public entity operating a demand responsive system
Citation: us:usc:42:12144
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section12144
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 126 (Equal Opportunity For Individuals With Disabilities); Subchapter II (Public Services); Part B (Actions Applicable To Public Transportation Provided By Public Entities Considered Discriminatory); Subpart i (Public Transportation Other Than By Aircraft Or Certain Rail Operations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If a public entity operates a demand responsive system, it shall be considered discrimination, for purposes of section 12132 of this title and section 794 of title 29, for such entity to purchase or lease a new vehicle for use on such system, for which a solicitation is made after the 30th day following July 26, 1990, that is not readily accessible to and usable by individuals with disabilities, including individuals who use wheelchairs, unless such system, when viewed in its entirety, provides a level of service to such individuals equivalent to the level of service such system provides to individuals without disabilities.
