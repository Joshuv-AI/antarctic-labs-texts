16 U.S.C. § 758b — Cooperation with agencies, organizations, and others
Citation: us:usc:16:758b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section758b
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 9A (Preservation Of Fishery Resources). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In carrying out the purposes and objectives of sections 758 and 758a of this title, the Secretary of Commerce may cooperate with appropriate agencies of the State and island governments, and with such educational, industrial, or other organizations, enterprises, and individuals as may be expedient.
