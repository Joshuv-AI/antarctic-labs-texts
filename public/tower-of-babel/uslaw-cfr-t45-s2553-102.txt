45 C.F.R. § 2553.102 — What performance measurement information must be part of an application for funding under RSVP?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2553.102
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2553.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2553 (The Retired And Senior Volunteer Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An application to AmeriCorps for funding under RSVP must contain:
(a) In a year one renewal application:
(1) Performance measures.
(2) Estimated performance data for the project years covered by the application.
(b) In a year two or three continuation application:
(1) Performance measures.
(2) Estimated performance data for the project years covered by the application.
(3) Actual performance data, where available, for the preceding completed project year.
