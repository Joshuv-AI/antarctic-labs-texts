34 C.F.R. § 76.51 — A State distributes funds by formula or competition.
Citation: us:cfr:34:76.51
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-76.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 76 (State-Administered Formula Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If applicable statutes and regulations authorize a State to make subgrants, the statute:
(a) Requires the State to use a formula to distribute funds;
(b) Gives the State discretion to select subgrantees through a competition among the applicants or through some other procedure; or
(c) Allows some combination of these procedures.
