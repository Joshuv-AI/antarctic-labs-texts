The dynamically generated N ⁡ ( 1535 ) N(1535) state in the Λ c + → p ​ K ¯ 0 ​ π 0 \Lambda_{c}^{+}\to p\bar{K}^{0}\pi^{0} decay
Ying Li, En Wang, Li-Sheng Geng, Ju-Jun Xie

Abstract
We present a theoretical analysis of the process Λ c + → p ​ K ¯ 0 ​ π 0 \Lambda_{c}^{+}\to p\bar{K}^{0}\pi^{0} within the chiral unitary approach, with particular emphasis on the dynamically generated N ⁡ ( 1535 ) N(1535) resonance. In addition to N ⁡ ( 1535 ) N(1535) , our model incorporates contributions from other intermediate resonances including N ⁡ ( 1650 ) N(1650) , K ∗ ​ ( 892 ) K^{*}(892) , K 0 ∗ ​ ( 1430 ) K_{0}^{*}(1430) , N ⁡ ( 1440 ) N(1440) , and Σ ⁡ ( 1750 ) \Sigma(1750) . The calculated invariant mass distributions and Dalitz plot are in good agreement with the recent Belle measurements. Our analysis highlights the crucial role of N ⁡ ( 1535 ) N(1535) state in this decay channel and supports its interpretation as a dynamically generated state arising from coupled-channel meson-baryon interactions.

We begin by discussing the primary vertex of the
\Lambda_{c}^{+}\to p\bar{K}^{0}\pi^{0}

Miyahara:2015cja
;
Xie:2017erh
;

uud

\Lambda_{c}^{+}
wave function of Refs.

;
Roberts:2007ni
, the weak decay and subsequent hadronization can be expressed as,

\displaystyle\Lambda_{c}^{+}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}c\left(ud-du\right)\chi_{MA}

\displaystyle\Rightarrow\bar{K}^{0}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}u\left(\bar{u}u+\bar{d}d+\bar{s}s\right)\left(ud-du\right)\chi_{MA},

(1)

where
\chi_{MA}
denotes the mixed antisymmetric spin wave function of the
(ud-du)
diquark. Using the pseudoscalar meson matrix
P
with the
\eta
,
\eta^{\prime}
mixing following Ref.
Bramon:1992kr
,

\displaystyle P=\left(\begin{matrix}\frac{{\eta}}{\sqrt{3}}+\frac{{\pi}^{0}}{\sqrt{2}}+\frac{{\eta}^{\prime}}{\sqrt{6}}&\pi^{+}&K^{+}\\
\pi^{-}&\frac{{\eta}}{\sqrt{3}}-\frac{{\pi}^{0}}{\sqrt{2}}+\frac{{\eta}^{\prime}}{\sqrt{6}}&K^{0}\\
K^{-}&\bar{K}^{0}&\sqrt{\frac{2}{3}}{\eta}^{\prime}-\frac{{\eta}}{\sqrt{3}}\end{matrix}\right),

(2)

the hadronization process in Eq. (
1
) can be further expressed as,

\displaystyle\Lambda_{c}^{+}

\displaystyle\Rightarrow\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\bar{K}^{0}\displaystyle\sum_{i}P_{1i}q_{i}\left(ud-du\right)\chi_{MA}

\displaystyle\Rightarrow\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\bar{K}^{0}\Bigg\{\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\eta u(ud-du)+\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\pi^{0}u(ud-du)

\displaystyle+\pi^{+}d(ud-du)+K^{+}s(ud-du)\Bigg\}\chi_{MA}

\displaystyle\Rightarrow\bar{K}^{0}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\Bigg\{\pi^{+}n+\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\pi^{0}p+\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\eta p-\frac{2}{\sqrt{6}}K^{+}\Lambda\Bigg\}.

(3)

The baryon octet wave function is given by,

\displaystyle\psi=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(\phi_{MS}\chi_{MS}+\phi_{MA}\chi_{MA}),

(4)

where
\phi_{MS}\chi_{MS}
and
\phi_{MA}\chi_{MA}
denote the mixed-symmetric and mixed-antisymmetry flavor-spin wave functions. The wave function

is taken from Table III of Ref.
Miyahara:2016yyh
. Using the isospin triplet

(p,n)

3

\displaystyle\Lambda_{c}^{+}\Rightarrow\bar{K}^{0}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\Bigg\{-\frac{\sqrt{6}}{2}\pi N+\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\eta N-\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}K\Lambda\Bigg\},

II.1
Amplitude for Dynamically Generated
N(1535)

The dynamical generation mechanism of the
N(1535)
resonance is shown in Fig.
2
, with the corresponding decay amplitude given by,

\displaystyle\mathcal{T}^{N(1535)}=

\displaystyle V_{N(1535)}\bigg[h_{\pi N}+h_{\pi N}G_{\pi N}(M_{\mathrm{inv}})t_{\pi N\to\pi N}(M_{\mathrm{inv}})

\displaystyle+h_{\eta N}G_{\eta N}(M_{\mathrm{inv}})t_{\eta N\to\pi N}(M_{\mathrm{inv}})

\displaystyle+h_{K\Lambda}G_{K\Lambda}(M_{\mathrm{inv}})t_{K\Lambda\to\pi N}(M_{\mathrm{inv}})\bigg],

(6)

where
M_{\mathrm{inv}}
is the
\pi^{0}p
invariant mass,
V_{N(1535)}
parameterizes the production strength of
N(1535)
, and the coefficients
h_{MB}
(
h_{\pi N}=-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}
,
h_{\eta N}=\frac{\sqrt{6}}{6}
, and
h_{K\Lambda}=-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}
) from Eq. (
5
) originate from the flavor structure.

In Eq. (
6
),
G_{MB}
is the meson-baryon loop function:

G_{MB}(s)=i\int\frac{d^{4}q}{(2\pi)^{4}}\frac{2M_{i}}{(p-q)^{2}-M_{i}^{2}+i\epsilon}\frac{1}{q^{2}-m_{i}^{2}+i\epsilon},

(7)

which is evaluated using cut-off regularization with
q_{\mathrm{max}}=1150\mathrm{MeV}
, as taken from Ref.
Li:2024rqb
. Noting that the threshold of the
\pi N
channel lies far from the pole position of
N(1535)
, we adopt the following form, as in Ref.
Li:2025msk
;
Oset:2024fbk
;
Dai:2020yfu
.

G_{\pi N}(s)=0-i\frac{2M}{8\pi\sqrt{s}}\times\frac{\lambda\left({\rm Re}(s),m^{2},M^{2}\right)}{2\sqrt{s}},

(8)

where
\lambda(x,y,z)=x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}-2xy-2xz-2yz
.

The transition amplitudes
t_{MB\to\pi N}
for the coupled channels
\pi N
,
\eta N
,
K\Lambda
, and
K\Sigma
are obtained by solving the Bethe-Salpeter equation,

T=[1-VG]^{-1}V,

(9)

with
V_{ij}
the interaction kernel obtained from the leading-order chiral Lagrangian,

\displaystyle V_{ij}=

\displaystyle-C_{ij}\frac{1}{4f_{i}f_{j}}(2\sqrt{s}-M_{i}-M_{j})\times

\displaystyle\sqrt{\frac{E_{i}+M_{i}}{2M_{i}}}\sqrt{\frac{E_{j}+M_{j}}{2M_{j}}};

(10)

\displaystyle f_{\pi}=93~\mathrm{MeV}

\displaystyle,\qquad f_{K}=1.2f_{\pi},\qquad f_{\eta}=1.3f_{\pi},

where
M_{i}/E_{i}
and
M_{j}/E_{j}
are the masses and energies of the baryon in the meson-baryon center of mass frame, respectively. The coefficients
C_{ij}
, obtained from the chiral Lagrangians and reflecting
\mathrm{SU(3)}
flavor symmetry, are given in Table I of Ref.
Garzon:2014ida
.

\displaystyle\tilde{P}^{0}_{\Lambda_{c}^{+}}

\displaystyle=\sqrt{M_{\Lambda_{c}^{+}}^{2}+\tilde{P}_{\Lambda_{c}^{+}}^{\,2}},

\displaystyle=\frac{\lambda^{1/2}\left(M_{\bar{K}^{0}\pi^{0}}^{2},m_{\pi^{0}}^{2},m_{\bar{K}^{0}}^{2}\right)}{2M_{\bar{K}^{0}\pi^{0}}},

II.3
Invariant Mass Distributions

With the above formalism, one can write down the total amplitude for the process
\Lambda_{c}^{+}\to p\bar{K}^{0}\pi^{0}
as

\displaystyle\mathcal{T}^{\mathrm{ModelA}}=~

\displaystyle\mathcal{T}^{N(1535)}+\mathcal{T}^{N(1650)}e^{i\phi_{1}}

\displaystyle+\mathcal{T}^{K^{*}(892)}e^{i\phi_{2}}+\mathcal{T}^{K_{0}^{*}(1430)}e^{i\phi_{3}}.

(22)

Using the standard form for the three-body decay width from RPP, we have

\displaystyle\frac{d^{2}\Gamma}{dM^{2}_{\pi^{0}p}{dM^{2}_{\bar{K}^{0}p}}}=\frac{4M_{\Lambda_{c}^{+}}M_{p}}{{(2\pi)}^{3}{32M_{\Lambda_{c}^{+}}}^{3}}|\mathcal{T}^{\mathrm{ModelA}}|^{2},

(23)

\displaystyle\frac{d^{2}\Gamma}{dM^{2}_{\bar{K}^{0}\pi^{0}}{dM^{2}_{\pi^{0}p}}}=\frac{4M_{\Lambda_{c}^{+}}M_{p}}{{(2\pi)}^{3}{32M_{\Lambda_{c}^{+}}}^{3}}|\mathcal{T}^{\mathrm{ModelA}}|^{2}.

(24)

The invariant mass distributions
d\Gamma/dM_{\pi^{0}p}
,
d\Gamma/dM_{p\bar{K}^{0}}
, and
d\Gamma/dM_{\bar{K}^{0}\pi^{0}}
are obtained by integrating over the other invariant mass variable within the kinematic limits.

IV
Summary

Recently, the Belle Collaboration has measured the branching fraction ratio of the
\Lambda_{c}^{+}\to pK_{S}^{0}\pi^{0}
decay relative to the
\Lambda_{c}^{+}\to pK^{-}\pi^{+}
reaction and reported the final-state invariant mass distributions, in which distinct signals corresponding to the
N(1535)
,
N(1650)
,
K^{*}(892)
, and
K_{0}^{*}(1430)
resonances can be clearly identified.

We have performed a detailed theoretical analysis of
\Lambda_{c}^{+}\to p\bar{K}^{0}\pi^{0}
decay within the chiral unitary approach. Our dynamically generated
N(1535)
resonance successfully reproduces the peak structure in the
M_{p\pi^{0}}
distribution observed by Belle. The inclusion of
N(1650)
,
K^{*}(892)
, and
K_{0}^{*}(1430)
provides a reasonable description of other features in the invariant mass spectra.

The extended Model B, incorporating the
N(1440)
and
\Sigma(1750)
contributions, significantly improves the agreement with the experimental data, particularly in the
1750
MeV region of
M_{p\bar{K}^{0}}
and the
1000\sim 1200
MeV region of
M_{\bar{K}^{0}\pi^{0}}
. This demonstrates the importance of these additional resonances for a complete description of the decay dynamics.

Our analysis is consistent with the interpretation of
N(1535)
as a dynamically generated state from coupled-channel meson-baryon interactions. The successful description of Belle data within this framework provides support for the molecular nature of
N(1535)
.

Future partial-wave analyses of
\Lambda_{c}^{+}\to pK_{S}^{0}\pi^{0}
by experimental collaborations will further elucidate contributions from intermediate resonances including
K^{*}
,
\Lambda^{*}
,
\Sigma^{*}
, and
\Delta^{*}
states. Such analyses, combined with improved theoretical models, will enhance our understanding of non-factorizable processes in charmed baryon decays and provide stringent tests of isospin symmetry.
