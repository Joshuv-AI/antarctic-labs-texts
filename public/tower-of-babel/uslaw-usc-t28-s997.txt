28 U.S.C. § 997 — Annual report
Citation: us:usc:28:997
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section997
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part III (Court Officers And Employees); Chapter 58 (United States Sentencing Commission). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commission shall report annually to the Judicial Conference of the United States, the Congress, and the President of the United States on the activities of the Commission.
