7 C.F.R. § 3402.24 — Evaluation of program.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3402.24
Creator: National Institute Of Food And Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3402.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3402 (Food And Agricultural Sciences National Needs Graduate And Postgraduate Fellowship Grants Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Grantees should be aware that HEP may, as a part of its own program evaluation activities, carry out in-depth evaluations of assisted activities through independent third parties. Thus, grantees should be prepared to cooperate with evaluators retained by HEP to analyze both the institutional context and the impact of any supported project.
