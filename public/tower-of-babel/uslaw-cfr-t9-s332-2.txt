9 C.F.R. § 332.2 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:9:332.2
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-332.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 332 (Selected Establishments; Cooperative Program For Interstate Shipment Of Carcasses, Parts Of Carcasses, Meat, And Meat Food Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes the conditions under which States that administer cooperative State meat inspection programs and establishments that operate under such programs may participate in a cooperative interstate shipment program.
