10 C.F.R. § 20.1801 — Security of stored material.
Citation: us:cfr:10:20.1801
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-20.1801
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 20 (Standards For Protection Against Radiation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The licensee shall secure from unauthorized removal or access licensed materials that are stored in controlled or unrestricted areas.
