Retraction Note: Toxicity of herbal extracts used in ethno-veterinary medicine and green-encapsulated ZnO nanoparticles against Aedes aegypti and microbial pathogens
Authors: Balan Banumathi, Baskaralingam Vaseeharan, Ramachandran Ishwarya, Marimuthu Govindarajan, Naiyf S Alharbi, Shine Kadaikunnan, Jamal M Khaled, Giovanni Benelli
Published: 2026

Retraction Note: Parasitol Res (2017) 116:1637-1651

https://doi.org/10.1007/s00436-017-5438-6

The Editor-in-Chief has retracted this article. A number of image integrity issues were raised. Specifically:

Figure 5 (L. leschenaultiana leaf extract - Pr.vulgaris), Fig. 5( Lle-ZnO nanoparticles - Pr.vulgaris) and Fig. 5 ( zinc acetate - Pr.vulgaris are highly similar

Figure 5 (L. leschenaultiana leaf extract - V. parahaemolyticus), Fig. 5 (Lle-ZnO nanoparticles - V. parahaemolyticus) and Fig. 5 (Lle-ZnO nanoparticles - Ps. aeruginosa) are highly similar.

Figure 5 (L. leschenaultiana leaf extract - V. parahaemolyticus), Fig. 5 (Lle-ZnO nanoparticles - V. parahaemolyticus) and Fig. 5 (Lle-ZnO nanoparticles - Ps. aeruginosa) are highly similar.

Figure 5 ( Lle-ZnO nanoparticles - S. sonnei) and Fig. 5(L. leschenaultiana leaf extract - S. sonnei) are similar

Figure 5 ( Lle-ZnO nanoparticles - S. sonnei) and Fig. 5(L. leschenaultiana leaf extract - S. sonnei) are similar

Figure 6 (Ps. aeruginosa - control) and Fig. 6 (V. parahaemolyticus - control) are highly similar

Figure 6 (Ps. aeruginosa - control) and Fig. 6 (V. parahaemolyticus - control) are highly similar

The Editor-in-Chief has therefore lost confidence in this data and conclusions.

Dr. Baskaralingam Vaseeharan disagree with this retraction and none of the remaining authors have responded to the correspondence from the Publisher about this retraction.
