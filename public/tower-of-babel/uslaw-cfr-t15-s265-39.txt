15 C.F.R. § 265.39 — Weapons and explosives.
Citation: us:cfr:15:265.39
Creator: National Institute Of Standards And Technology, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-265.39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 265 (Regulations Governing Traffic And Conduct On The Grounds Of The National Institute Of Standards & Technology, Gaithersburg, Maryland, And Boulder And Fort Collins, Colorado). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except in connection with the conduct of official business on the site, no person other than uniformed guards specifically authorized, or other Federal, State, or local law enforcement officials so authorized, shall carry, transport, or otherwise possess on the site, firearms whether loaded or not, other dangerous or deadly weapons or materials, or explosives, either openly or concealed, without the written permission of the Director or his designee.
