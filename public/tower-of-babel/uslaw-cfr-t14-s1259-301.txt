14 C.F.R. § 1259.301 — Identification of national needs.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1259.301
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1259.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1259 (National Space Grant College And Fellowship Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

National needs shall be identified by the Administrator who shall consider specific national needs and problems relating to space proposed by the Space Grant Review Panel, any NASA official or any person.
