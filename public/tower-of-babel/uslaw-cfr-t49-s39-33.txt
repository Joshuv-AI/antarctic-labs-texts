49 C.F.R. § 39.33 — May PVOs require a passenger with a disability to provide a medical certificate?
Citation: us:cfr:49:39.33
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-39.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 39 (Transportation For Individuals With Disabilities: Passenger Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in § 39.31, you must not require a passenger with a disability to have a medical certificate as a condition for being provided transportation on your vessel.
