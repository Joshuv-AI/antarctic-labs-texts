10 U.S.C. § 7682 — Obsolete or excess material: sale to National Council of Boy Scouts of America
Citation: us:usc:10:7682
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7682
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Procurement); Chapter 773 (Disposal Of Obsolete Or Surplus Material). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Subject to regulations under section 121 of title 40, the Secretary of the Army, under such conditions as he may prescribe, may sell obsolete or excess material to the National Council of the Boy Scouts of America. Sales under this section shall be at fair value to the Department of the Army, including packing, handling, and transportation.
