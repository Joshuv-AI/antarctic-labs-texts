Effective Upper Bound Estimates for | ζ ′ ​ ( 1 / 2 + i ​ t ) | |\zeta^{\prime}(1/2+it)| via Exponential Sums
Ting, Liu, Jinjin, Ma, BinJie, Chang, Xinhua Xiong

Abstract
. In this paper, we use methods of exponential sums to derive a formula for estimating effective upper bounds of | ζ ′ ​ ( 1 / 2 + i ​ t ) | |\zeta^{\prime}(1/2+it)| . Different effective upper bounds can be obtained by choosing different parameters.



1.
Introduction

The Riemann zeta function is defined by

\zeta(s)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}n^{-s}\quad(\sigma>1),\quad s=\sigma+it.

Using the Euler–Maclaurin summation formula, we have

(1)

\displaystyle\zeta(s)

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=1}^{N-1}n^{-s}+\frac{N^{1-s}}{s-1}+\frac{1}{2}N^{-s}+\frac{B_{2}}{2}sN^{-s-1}+\cdots

\displaystyle+\frac{B_{2v}}{(2v)!}s(s+1)\cdots(s+2v-2)N^{-s-2v+1}+R_{2v},

where
B_{m}
are Bernoulli polynomials,
N
is a positive integer,
v\geq 0
is a natural number, and

R_{2v}=-\frac{s(s+1)\cdots(s+2v-1)}{(2v)!}\int_{N}^{\infty}\bar{B}_{2v}(x)x^{-s-2v}dx.

The integral for
R_{2v}
converges, and (
1
) holds in the half-plane
\text{Re}(s+2v+1)>1
.

When
v=0
, the formula simplifies to

\zeta(s)=\frac{s}{s-1}-s\int_{1}^{\infty}\frac{u-[u]}{u^{s+1}}du,

and

\zeta(s)=\frac{1}{s}+\frac{1}{2}-s\int_{1}^{\infty}\frac{u-[u]-\frac{1}{2}}{u^{s+1}}du.

In 1985, Titchmarsh
[
5
]
proved

\zeta^{\prime}(\sigma+it)\leq\exp\left(\frac{C\log t}{\log\log t}\right),\quad\sigma\geq 1/2,\quad t\geq 10.

For effective upper bounds of the zeta function and its derivatives, see the references.

In this paper, using exponential sum methods, we obtain effective estimates for
|\zeta^{\prime}(1/2+it)|
:

Theorem 1
.

If
t\geq e^{2}
, then

|\zeta^{\prime}(1/2+it)|\leq 2t^{1/2}\log t-4t^{1/2}+8.047\log t+6.399.

Theorem 2
.

If
t\geq e^{6}
, then

|\zeta^{\prime}(1/2+it)|\leq Q_{1}t^{1/6}(\log t)^{2}+Q_{2}t^{1/6}\log t+Q_{3}t^{1/6}+Q_{4}(\log t)^{2}+Q_{5}\log t+Q_{6},

where
Q_{1}(\tau,q,t_{2}),Q_{2}(k,\tau,q,t_{2}),Q_{3}(k,\tau,q,t_{2}),Q_{4}(\tau,t_{2}),Q_{5}(k,\tau,q,t_{2}),Q_{6}(k,\tau,q,t_{1},t_{2})
are constants, and
k,\tau,q,t_{1},t_{2}
are parameters to be determined.

To estimate
|\zeta^{\prime}(1/2+it)|
, we use the representation

\zeta^{\prime}(s)=-\sum_{n=1}^{N}\frac{\log n}{n^{s}}-\int_{N}^{\infty}\frac{u-[u]}{u^{s+1}}du+s\int_{N}^{\infty}\frac{u-[u]}{u^{s+1}}\log u\,du-\frac{1}{(s-1)^{2}N^{s-1}}-\frac{\log N}{(s-1)N^{s-1}},

and divide the exponential sum part into
\sum_{n\leq t^{1/3}}\frac{\log n}{n^{s}}
,
\sum_{t^{1/3}<n\leq t^{2/3}}\frac{\log n}{n^{s}}
,
\sum_{t^{2/3}<n\leq t}\frac{\log n}{n^{s}}
,
\sum_{t<n\leq N}\frac{\log n}{n^{s}}
. In Section 2, we prove some lemmas and estimate
\sum_{t<n\leq N}\frac{\log n}{n^{s}}
. In Section 3, we directly integrate to obtain an effective upper bound for
|\zeta^{\prime}(1/2+it)|
. In Section 4, using exponential sum methods, we estimate
\sum_{t^{1/3}<n\leq t^{2/3}}\frac{\log n}{n^{s}}
and
\sum_{t^{2/3}<n\leq t}\frac{\log n}{n^{s}}
, and combine the results to get the final effective upper bound for
|\zeta^{\prime}(1/2+it)|
.

Lemma 5
.

If
\sigma=1/2

|E(s)|\leq|A|+|B|+|C|+|D|\leq\frac{2}{\sqrt{t^{2}-1}}+\sqrt{\frac{4t^{2}+1}{t^{2}-1}}(2\log t+2)+\frac{1}{t}+2\log t.

\sigma>0

\displaystyle|E(s)|

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{\sigma N^{\sigma}}+\sqrt{(t/\sigma)^{2}+1}\frac{(\sigma\log N+1)}{\sigma N^{\sigma}}+\frac{N^{1-\sigma}}{t^{2}}+\frac{N^{1-\sigma}\log N}{t}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{2}{\sqrt{t^{2}-1}}+\sqrt{\frac{4t^{2}+1}{t^{2}-1}}(2\log t+2)+\frac{1}{t}+2\log t.

This completes the proof of Lemma
5

\displaystyle=I_{1}+I_{2}+I_{3}+I_{4}+I_{5},

\displaystyle:=\int_{t}^{t^{2}}\frac{\log x}{x^{s}}dx,

\displaystyle I_{2}

\displaystyle:=\frac{((t))\log t}{t^{s}},

\displaystyle I_{3}

\displaystyle:=-\frac{((t^{2}))\log t^{2}}{t^{2s}},

\displaystyle I_{4}

,

5.
Conclusion

Since Titchmarsh’s 1985 result

\zeta^{\prime}(\sigma+it)\leq\exp\left(\frac{C\log t}{\log\log t}\right),\quad\sigma\geq 1/2,\quad t\geq 10,

the study of upper bounds for the derivative of the zeta function has seen limited progress. However, recently there have been many estimates for the absolute value of the zeta function on the 1-line and 1/2-line, see references
[
2
,
9
,
12
,
15
,
19
]
. In this paper, using exponential sum methods, we systematically derive a formula for effective estimates of
|\zeta^{\prime}(1/2+it)|
, further refining Titchmarsh’s result. Our results may not be optimal, and seeking the best possible bounds and applying the method to higher derivatives of the zeta function are worthwhile directions for future research.
