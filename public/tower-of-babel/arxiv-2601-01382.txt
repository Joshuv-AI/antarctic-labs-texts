Abstract
Motivated by the observations of T c ​ s ¯ ​ 0 ​ ( 2900 ) 0 T_{c\bar{s}0}(2900)^{0} and T c ​ s ¯ ​ 0 ​ ( 2900 ) + ⁣ + T_{c\bar{s}0}(2900)^{++} , we propose to search for T c ​ s ¯ ​ 0 ​ ( 2900 ) 0 {T_{c\bar{s}0}(2900)}^{0} in the cleaner process B − → K − ​ D 0 ​ K 0 B^{-}\to K^{-}D^{0}K^{0} . In the D ∗ ​ K ∗ D^{*}K^{*} molecular picture, our estimates suggest that T c ​ s ¯ ​ 0 ​ ( 2900 ) 0 T_{c\bar{s}0}(2900)^{0} should contribute significantly to the D 0 ​ K 0 D^{0}K^{0} invariant mass distribution in B − → K − ​ D 0 ​ K 0 B^{-}\to K^{-}D^{0}K^{0} , as reported by the Belle II Collaboration. The corresponding fit fraction is estimated to be ( 9.72 ± 3.92 ) % (9.72\pm 3.92)\% . Further precise measurements of this process at Belle II and LHCb could help clarify the nature of T c ​ s ¯ ​ 0 ​ ( 2900 ) T_{c\bar{s}0}(2900) .

Summary
.—In 2022, the LHCb Collaboration reported the first doubly charged tetraquark candidates,
T_{c\bar{s}0}(2900)^{0}
and
T_{c\bar{s}0}(2900)^{++}
, in the
D_{s}^{+}\pi^{-}
and
D_{s}^{+}\pi^{+}
mass spectra. Their compatible resonance parameters suggest they belong to an isospin triplet. The distinctive features of these states—fully open-flavor quark content and a mass near the
D^{*}K^{*}
threshold—have spurred extensive theoretical interest, with interpretations ranging from compact tetraquarks and
D^{*}K^{*}
molecules to threshold effects.

To elucidate the nature of
T_{c\bar{s}0}(2900)
, we have proposed searching for its neutral partner in the decay
B^{-}\to K^{-}D^{0}K^{0}
. Within the
D^{*}K^{*}
molecular picture, our analysis shows that
{T_{c\bar{s}0}(2900)}^{0}
is essential for describing the
D^{0}K^{0}
invariant mass distribution measured by Belle II. By including contributions from
\rho(770)^{-}
,
\rho(1450)^{-}
,
a_{0}(980)^{-}
, and
{T_{c\bar{s}0}(2900)}^{0}
, we achieve a consistent description of the
K^{-}K^{0}
,
D^{0}K^{0}
, and
D^{0}K^{-}
spectra, with a fit fraction for
{T_{c\bar{s}0}(2900)}^{0}
of
(9.72\pm 3.92)\%
. Further precision measurements of this channel at Belle II and LHCb will help clarify the internal structure of the
{T_{c\bar{s}0}(2900)}
states.
