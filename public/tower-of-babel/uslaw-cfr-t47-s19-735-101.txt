47 C.F.R. § 19.735-101 — -101 Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:47:19.735-101
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-19.735-101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 19 (Employee Responsibilities And Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part prescribe procedures and standards of conduct that are appropriate to the particular functions and activities of the Commission, and are issued by the Commission under authority independent of the uniform Standards of Ethical Conduct for Employees of the Executive Branch at 5 CFR part 2635 or otherwise in accordance with 5 CFR 2635.105(c).
