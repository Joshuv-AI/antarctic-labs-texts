33 C.F.R. § 334.990 — Long Beach Harbor, Calif.; naval restricted area.
Citation: us:cfr:33:334.990
Creator: Corps Of Engineers, Department Of The Army, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-334.990
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 334 (Danger Zone And Restricted Area Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The area. All the waters between the Navy mole and Terminal Island to the westward of longitude 118°13′10″.
(b) The regulations. (1) The area is reserved exclusively for use by naval vessels. Permission for any person or vessel to enter the area must be obtained from the enforcing agency.
(2) The regulations in this section shall be enforced by the Commander, U.S. Naval Base Los Angeles, Long Beach, California, and such agencies as he may designate.
