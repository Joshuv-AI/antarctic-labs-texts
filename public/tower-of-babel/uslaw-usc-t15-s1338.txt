15 U.S.C. § 1338 — Criminal penalty
Citation: us:usc:15:1338
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1338
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 36 (Cigarette Labeling And Advertising). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person who violates the provisions of this chapter shall be guilty of a misdemeanor and shall on conviction thereof be subject to a fine of not more than $10,000.
