42 U.S.C. § 5811 — Establishment of Energy Research and Development Administration
Citation: us:usc:42:5811
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section5811
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 73 (Development Of Energy Sources); Subchapter I (Energy Research And Development Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is hereby established an independent executive agency to be known as the Energy Research and Development Administration (hereinafter in this chapter referred to as the “Administration”).
