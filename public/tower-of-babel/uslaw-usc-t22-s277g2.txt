22 U.S.C. § 277g–2 — Consultation with Administrator of Environmental Protection Agency and other authorities
Citation: us:usc:22:277g–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section277g–2
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter IV (International Boundary And Water Commission). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of State shall consult with the Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency and other concerned Federal, State, and local government officials in implementing sections 277g to 277g–3 of this title.
