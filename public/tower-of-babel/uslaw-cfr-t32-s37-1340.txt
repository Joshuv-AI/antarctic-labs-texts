32 C.F.R. § 37.1340 — Program official.
Citation: us:cfr:32:37.1340
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-37.1340
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 37 (Technology Investment Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Federal Government program manager, scientific officer, or other individual who is responsible for managing the technical program being carried out through the use of a TIA.
