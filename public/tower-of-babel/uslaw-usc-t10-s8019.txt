10 U.S.C. § 8019 — General Counsel
Citation: us:usc:10:8019
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8019
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization); Chapter 803 (Department Of The Navy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
There is a General Counsel of the Department of the Navy, appointed from civilian life by the President, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate.
(b)
The General Counsel shall perform such functions as the Secretary of the Navy may prescribe.
