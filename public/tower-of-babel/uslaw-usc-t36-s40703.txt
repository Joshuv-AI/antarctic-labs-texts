36 U.S.C. § 40703 — Powers
Citation: us:usc:36:40703
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section40703
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 407 (Corporation For The Promotion Of Rifle Practice And Firearms Safety); Subchapter I (Corporation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation may—
(1)
adopt, use, and alter a corporate seal, which shall be judicially noticed;
(2)
make contracts;
(3)
acquire, own, lease, encumber, and transfer property as necessary or convenient to carry out the activities of the corporation;
(4)
incur and pay obligations;
(5)
charge fees to cover the corporation’s costs in carrying out the Civilian Marksmanship Program; and
(6)
do any other act necessary and proper to carry out the activities of the corporation.
