46 C.F.R. § 80.20 — Exception to requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:46:80.20
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-80.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 80 (Disclosure Of Safety Standards And Country Of Registry). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) This part does not apply to vessels that comply with the safety standards set forth in the International Convention for Safety of Life at Sea, 1974.
(b) If the exception in paragraph (a) of this section applies, the country of registry must appear in printed advertising or promotional literature as described in § 80.30(a), in a type no smaller than six points, American point system.
