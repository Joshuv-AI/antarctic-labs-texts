Amortized Stabilizer Rényi Entropy of Quantum Dynamics
Chengkai Zhu, Yu-Ao Chen, Zanqiu Shen, Zhiping Liu, Zhan Yu, Xin Wang

Abstract
Unraveling the secrets of how much nonstabilizerness a quantum dynamic can generate is crucial for harnessing the power of magic states, the essential resources for achieving quantum advantage and realizing fault-tolerant quantum computation. In this work, we introduce the amortized α \alpha -stabilizer Rényi entropy, a magic monotone for unitary operations that quantifies the nonstabilizerness generation capability of quantum dynamics. Amortization is key in quantifying the magic of quantum dynamics, as we reveal that nonstabilizerness generation can be enhanced by prior nonstabilizerness in input states when considering the α \alpha -stabilizer Rényi entropy, while this is not the case for robustness of magic or stabilizer extent. We demonstrate the versatility of the amortized α \alpha -stabilizer Rényi entropy in investigating the nonstabilizerness resources of quantum dynamics of computational and fundamental interest. In particular, we establish improved lower bounds on the T T -count of quantum Fourier transforms and the quantum evolutions of one-dimensional Heisenberg Hamiltonians, showcasing the power of this tool in studying quantum advantages and the corresponding cost in fault-tolerant quantum computation.

Amortized magic.
—
Throughout the paper, we consider multi-qubit quantum systems and denote by
{\cal H}_{n}
the
2^{n}
dimensional Hilbert space. The
n
-qubit Pauli group is denoted as
\hat{{\cal P}}_{n}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\{i^{k}\sigma_{h_{1}}\otimes\sigma_{h_{2}}\otimes\cdots\otimes\sigma_{h_{n}}|~k,h_{j}=0,1,2,3\}
where
\sigma_{0}=\mathbbm{1}_{2},~\sigma_{1}=\sigma^{x},~\sigma_{2}=\sigma^{y},~\sigma_{3}=\sigma^{z}
with identity matrix
\mathbbm{1}_{2}
and Pauli matrices
\sigma^{x},\sigma^{y},\sigma^{z}
. The
n
-qubit Pauli group modulo phases is denoted as
{\cal P}_{n}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\hat{{\cal P}}_{n}/\langle\pm i\mathbbm{1}_{2^{n}}\rangle
. Let
\mathrm{Stab}_{n}
be the set of
n
-qubit stabilizer states which is the convex hull of pure stabilizer states. For an
n
-qubit pure quantum state
|\psi\rangle\in{\cal H}_{n}
, the
\alpha
-stabilizer Rényi entropy is defined as
[
22
]

M_{\alpha}(|\psi\rangle)\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\frac{1}{1-\alpha}\log_{2}\sum_{P\in{\cal P}_{n}}\Xi_{P}^{\alpha}(|\psi\rangle)-n,

(1)

where
\Xi_{P}(|\psi\rangle)\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=|\langle\psi|P|\psi\rangle|^{2}/2^{n}
and
P\in{\cal P}_{n}
is an
n
-bit Pauli string. Notice that
\Xi_{P}(|\psi\rangle)\geq 0,\sum_{P\in{\cal P}_{n}}\Xi_{P}(|\psi\rangle)=\langle\psi|\psi\rangle^{2}=1
, and
\{\Xi_{P}(|\psi\rangle)\}
is a probability distribution. The
\alpha
-stabilizer Rényi entropy is shown to be a magic quantifier of quantum states that satisfies the faithfulness, the invariance under Clifford operations, and additivity under the tensor product of quantum states
[
22
]
. Recent studies have shown that
M_{\alpha}(\cdot)
serves as a nonstabilizerness monotone for pure states when
\alpha\geq 2
under general stabilizer protocols, while the monotonicity property fails for
0\leq\alpha<2

[
52
,
19
]
.

We begin by reporting an intriguing phenomenon: the initial nonstabilizerness in the input state can boost the SRE generation of a unitary operation. Consider the single-qubit unitary operator
\sqrt{T}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\mathrm{diag}(1,e^{i\pi/8})
. By exhausting all single-qubit pure stabilizer states, we obtain

\max_{|\phi\rangle\in\mathrm{Stab}_{1}}M_{2}(\sqrt{T}|\phi\rangle)=M_{2}(\sqrt{T}|+\rangle)=3-\log_{2}7,

(2)

where
|+\rangle\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=(|0\rangle+|1\rangle)/\sqrt{2}
. Furthermore, consider a magic state
|\psi\rangle=(|0\rangle+e^{i\pi/10}|1\rangle)/\sqrt{2}
. By direct calculation, we have

\displaystyle M_{2}(|\psi\rangle)=3-\log_{2}\left[7+\cos\left(\frac{2\pi}{5}\right)\right],

(3)

\displaystyle M_{2}(\sqrt{T}|\psi\rangle)=3-\log_{2}\left[7+\cos\left(\frac{9\pi}{10}\right)\right].

It follows that
M_{2}(\sqrt{T}|\psi\rangle)-M_{2}(|\psi\rangle)>3-\log_{2}7=M_{2}(\sqrt{T}|+\rangle)
. This demonstrates that the initial nonstabilizerness can indeed boost the 2-stabilizer Rényi entropy generation of a
\sqrt{T}
gate. Interestingly, a similar property has been observed in the context of entanglement: when the initial two-qubit state has prior entanglement, the entangling capacity of a unitary operation can be enhanced
[
47
]
. This property, also known as
resource dependence
, has been shown to be related to the chosen resource measure
[
40
]
. Our findings indicate that the
2
-stabilizer Rényi entropy exhibits a nonstabilizerness dependence, i.e., prior nonstabilizerness can boost the nonstabilizerness generation of a unitary operation.

Motivated by this observation, we proceed to investigate the maximum amount of nonstabilizerness that a unitary operation can generate in general. To this end, we introduce the amortized magic of a multi-qubit unitary with respect to a magic measure of states
{{\mathbb{M}}}(\cdot)
that satisfies two axioms: i) faithfulness, i.e.,
{{\mathbb{M}}}(|\psi\rangle)\geq 0
for all
|\psi\rangle\in{\cal H}_{n}
and
{{\mathbb{M}}}(|\psi\rangle)=0
for all
|\psi\rangle\in\mathrm{Stab}_{n}
. ii) monotonicity, i.e.,
{{\mathbb{M}}}(U|\psi\rangle)\leq{{\mathbb{M}}}(|\psi\rangle)
for all
|\psi\rangle\in{\cal H}_{n}
where
U
is any Clifford operation.

Definition 1

(Amortized magic)

Let
{{\mathbb{M}}}(\cdot)
be a magic measure of multi-qubit quantum states. The amortized magic of an
n
-qubit unitary
U
with respect to
{{\mathbb{M}}}(\cdot)
is defined as

{{\mathbb{M}}}^{{\cal A}}(U)\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\sup_{m\in{{\mathbb{N}}}^{+}}\max_{|\phi\rangle\in{\cal H}_{n+m}}\Big[{{\mathbb{M}}}\left(\left(U\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m}}\right)|\phi\rangle\right)-{{\mathbb{M}}}\left(|\phi\rangle\right)\Big],

where the maximization is with respect to all pure states in Hilbert space
{\cal H}_{n+m}
. The strict amortized magic of
U
is defined as

\widetilde{{{\mathbb{M}}}}^{{\cal A}}(U)\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\max_{|\phi\rangle\in\mathrm{Stab}_{n+m}}{{\mathbb{M}}}\left(\left(U\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m}}\right)|\phi\rangle\right),

where the input state
|\phi\rangle
ranges over all pure stabilizer states in Hilbert space
{\cal H}_{n+m}
.

Although a similar definition of amortized magic has been introduced for general quantum channels
[
36
]
, the crucial case of multi-qubit unitary operations has not been explored. The amortized magic of an
n
-qubit unitary arguably captures the most general scenario of nonstabilizerness generation through quantum evolution by optimizing all possible numbers of ancillary qubits and input states. It exhibits desirable properties from the perspective of dynamical quantum resource theory
[
53
,
54
]
as follows.

1.

Faithfulness:
{{\mathbb{M}}}^{{\cal A}}(U)=0
if and only if
U
is a Clifford gate, and
{{\mathbb{M}}}^{{\cal A}}(U)>0
otherwise.

2.

Subadditivity under composition: for any two unitaries
U
and
V
,
{{\mathbb{M}}}^{{\cal A}}(UV)\leq{{\mathbb{M}}}^{{\cal A}}(U)+{{\mathbb{M}}}^{{\cal A}}(V)
.

3.

Subadditivity under tensor product: for any two unitaries
U
and
V
,
{{\mathbb{M}}}^{{\cal A}}(U\otimes V)\leq{{\mathbb{M}}}^{{\cal A}}(U)+{{\mathbb{M}}}^{{\cal A}}(V)
.

The proofs of these properties can be found in the Appendix. It is worth noting that these properties imply that
{{\mathbb{M}}}^{{\cal A}}(C_{1}UC_{2})\leq{{\mathbb{M}}}^{{\cal A}}(U)
, and
{{\mathbb{M}}}^{{\cal A}}(C\otimes U)\leq{{\mathbb{M}}}^{{\cal A}}(U)
where
C_{1},C_{2},C
are arbitrary Clifford operations. It follows that
{{\mathbb{M}}}^{{\cal A}}(\cdot)
is a nonstabilizerness monotone of unitary operations, i.e., non-increasing under left and right composition with free operations as well as tensoring. Remarkably, it is easy to see that such monotonicity of
{{\mathbb{M}}}^{{\cal A}}(\cdot)
holds as long as
{{\mathbb{M}}}(\cdot)
itself satisfies faithfulness and monotonicity under Clifford operations.

{\cal H}_{n+m}

Concluding remarks.—

In this work, we have introduced the amortized
\alpha
-stabilizer Rényi entropy of a unitary operation, which captures the maximum nonstabilizerness generation and exhibits desirable properties as a magic monotone. Notably, prior nonstabilizerness in the input state can boost the nonstabilizerness generation. As an application, we have derived a lower bound on the
T
-count of unitaries based on the amortized 2-stabilizer Rényi entropy, which enjoys several advantages over previous methods. Our work adds a valuable tool to the magic resource theory and it remains an insightful direction to explore the properties of amortized
\alpha
-stabilizer Rényi entropy. For instance, it is unknown whether the equality
M_{\alpha}^{{\cal A}}(U^{\dagger})=M_{\alpha}^{{\cal A}}(U)
holds for an arbitrary unitary operation
U
. If the equality does not hold, it would imply an intriguing phenomenon: the nonstabilizerness generation and destruction capacities of a unitary operation are unequal, similar to the case for entanglement, where the entangling and disentangling powers of a unitary are unequal
[
60
]
.

Appendix A: Properties of the amortized magic

Lemma S1

(Faithfulness)

Let
{{\mathbb{M}}}(\cdot)
be a magic measure of quantum states.
{{\mathbb{M}}}^{{\cal A}}(U)=0
if and only if
U
is a Clifford gate.

Proof.

\Leftarrow
: if
U
is a Clifford gate, by the monotonicity of
{{\mathbb{M}}}(\cdot)
, it follows that
{{\mathbb{M}}}\left((U\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m}})|\phi\rangle\right)-{{\mathbb{M}}}\left(|\phi\rangle\right)\leq 0
for any state
|\phi\rangle
which yields
{{\mathbb{M}}}^{{\cal A}}(U)\leq 0
. As
{{\mathbb{M}}}^{{\cal A}}(U)\geq{{\mathbb{M}}}((U\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m}})|0\rangle)-{{\mathbb{M}}}(|0\rangle)=0
, we conclude
{{\mathbb{M}}}^{{\cal A}}(U)=0
.

\Rightarrow
: if
{{\mathbb{M}}}^{{\cal A}}(U)=0
, it follows that for any state
|\phi\rangle
,
{{\mathbb{M}}}\left((U\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m}})|\phi\rangle\right)-{{\mathbb{M}}}\left(|\phi\rangle\right)\leq 0
. For any
|\phi\rangle\in\mathrm{Stab}_{n}
, by the faithfulness of
{{\mathbb{M}}}(\cdot)
, we have
{{\mathbb{M}}}(|\phi\rangle)=0
and
{{\mathbb{M}}}\left((U\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m}})|\phi\rangle\right)=0
. It follows that
(U\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m}})|\phi\rangle
is a stabilizer state. Thus,
U
is a Clifford gate.

Lemma S2

(Subadditivity under composition)

Let
{{\mathbb{M}}}(\cdot)
be a magic measure of quantum states. For any two unitaries
U
and
V
,

{{\mathbb{M}}}^{{\cal A}}(UV)\leq{{\mathbb{M}}}^{{\cal A}}(U)+{{\mathbb{M}}}^{{\cal A}}(V).

(S1)

Proof.

Consider that

\displaystyle{{\mathbb{M}}}^{{\cal A}}(UV)

\displaystyle=\sup_{m\in{{\mathbb{N}}}^{+}}\max_{|\psi\rangle}\Big[{{\mathbb{M}}}[(UV\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m}})|\psi\rangle]-{{\mathbb{M}}}(|\psi\rangle)\Big]

(S2)

\displaystyle=\sup_{m\in{{\mathbb{N}}}^{+}}\max_{|\psi\rangle}\Big[{{\mathbb{M}}}[(UV\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m}})|\psi\rangle]-{{\mathbb{M}}}((V\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m}})|\psi\rangle)+{{\mathbb{M}}}[(V\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m}})|\psi\rangle]-{{\mathbb{M}}}(|\psi\rangle)\Big]

\displaystyle=\sup_{m\in{{\mathbb{N}}}^{+}}\max_{|\phi\rangle=(V\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{R})|\psi\rangle,|\psi\rangle}\Big[{{\mathbb{M}}}[(U\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m}})|\phi\rangle]-{{\mathbb{M}}}(|\phi\rangle)+{{\mathbb{M}}}[(V\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m}})|\psi\rangle]-{{\mathbb{M}}}(|\psi\rangle)\Big]

\displaystyle\leq\sup_{m\in{{\mathbb{N}}}^{+}}\max_{|\phi\rangle,|\psi\rangle}\Big[{{\mathbb{M}}}[(U\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m}})|\phi\rangle]-{{\mathbb{M}}}(|\phi\rangle)+{{\mathbb{M}}}[(V\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m}})|\psi\rangle]-{{\mathbb{M}}}(|\psi\rangle)\Big]

\displaystyle\leq\sup_{m\in{{\mathbb{N}}}^{+}}\max_{|\phi\rangle}\Big[{{\mathbb{M}}}[(U\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m}})|\phi\rangle]-{{\mathbb{M}}}(|\phi\rangle)\Big]+\sup_{m\in{{\mathbb{N}}}^{+}}\max_{|\psi\rangle}{{\mathbb{M}}}\Big[(V\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m}})|\psi\rangle]-{{\mathbb{M}}}(|\psi\rangle)\Big]

\displaystyle={{\mathbb{M}}}^{{\cal A}}(U)+{{\mathbb{M}}}^{{\cal A}}(V).

Hence, we complete the proof.

Lemma S3

(Invariance under Clifford)

Let
{{\mathbb{M}}}(\cdot)
be a magic measure of quantum states, and let
C_{1}
be a Clifford operator. Then for any unitary
U
,

{{\mathbb{M}}}^{{\cal A}}(C_{1}U)={{\mathbb{M}}}^{{\cal A}}(U).

(S3)

Proof.

By the subadditivity under composition in Lemma
S2
, we have
{{\mathbb{M}}}^{{\cal A}}(C_{1}U)\leq 0+{{\mathbb{M}}}^{{\cal A}}(U)={{\mathbb{M}}}^{{\cal A}}(U)
. Consider that

\displaystyle{{\mathbb{M}}}^{{\cal A}}(C_{1}U)

\displaystyle=\sup_{m\in{{\mathbb{N}}}^{+}}\max_{|\psi\rangle}\Big[{{\mathbb{M}}}[(C_{1}U\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m}})|\psi\rangle]-{{\mathbb{M}}}(|\psi\rangle)\Big]

(S4)

\displaystyle\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\mathclap{\scriptsize\mbox{(i)}}}}{{\geq}}\sup_{m\in{{\mathbb{N}}}^{+}}\max_{|\psi\rangle}\Big[{{\mathbb{M}}}[(C_{1}^{\dagger}C_{1}U\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m}})|\psi\rangle]-{{\mathbb{M}}}(|\psi\rangle)\Big]

\displaystyle=\sup_{m\in{{\mathbb{N}}}^{+}}\max_{|\psi\rangle}\Big[{{\mathbb{M}}}[(U\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m}})|\psi\rangle]-{{\mathbb{M}}}(|\psi\rangle)\Big]

\displaystyle={{\mathbb{M}}}^{{\cal A}}(U),

where in (i) we used the fact that
C_{1}^{{\dagger}}\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m}}
is also a Clifford operator and
{{\mathbb{M}}}[(C_{1}^{\dagger}C_{1}U\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m}})|\psi\rangle]\leq{{\mathbb{M}}}[(C_{1}U\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m}})|\psi\rangle]
by the monotonicity of
{{\mathbb{M}}}(\cdot)
. Hence, we conclude that
{{\mathbb{M}}}^{{\cal A}}(C_{1}U)={{\mathbb{M}}}^{{\cal A}}(U)
.

Appendix B: Amortized log robustness of magic and amortized stabilizer extent

Definition S1

(Log RoM
[
37
]
)

The log robustness of magic of an
n
-qubit quantum state
\rho
is defined as

\log_{2}{\cal R}(\rho)=\log_{2}\min_{\vec{q}}\Big\{\|\vec{q}\|_{1}\ \mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}\ \sum_{i}q_{i}|s_{i}\rangle\!\langle s_{i}|=\rho\ ,|s_{i}\rangle\!\langle s_{i}|\in\mathrm{Stab}_{n}\Big\},

(S9)

where
\|\vec{q}\|_{1}=\sum_{i}|q_{i}|
denotes the
\ell_{1}
-norm, and the minimization ranges over possible decompositions of all pure stabilizer states.

Definition S2

(Amortized log RoM)

The amortized log robustness of magic of an
n
-qubit unitary
U
is defined as

{\cal R}_{\log}^{{\cal A}}(U)\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\sup_{m\in{{\mathbb{N}}}^{+}}\max_{|\phi\rangle\in{\cal H}_{n+m}}\log_{2}{\cal R}\left[(U\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m}})|\phi\rangle\right]-\log_{2}{\cal R}(|\phi\rangle),

(S10)

where the maximization is with respect to all pure states in Hilbert space
{\cal H}_{n+m}
. The strict amortized log robustness of magic of
U
is defined as

\widetilde{{\cal R}}_{\log}^{{\cal A}}(U)\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\sup_{m\in{{\mathbb{N}}}^{+}}\max_{|\phi\rangle\in\mathrm{Stab}_{n+m}}\log_{2}{\cal R}[(U\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m}})|\phi\rangle].

(S11)

Proposition S6

For any
n
-qubit unitary
U
,
{\cal R}_{\log}^{{\cal A}}(U)=\widetilde{{\cal R}}_{\log}^{{\cal A}}(U)
.

Proof.

From the definitions, we directly have
{\cal R}_{\log}^{{\cal A}}(U)\geq\widetilde{{\cal R}}_{\log}^{{\cal A}}(U)
. Followed from the result in Ref.
[
37
]
that for any
n
-qubit given quantum channel
{\cal E}
,

\frac{{\cal R}[({\cal E}\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m}})\rho]}{{\cal R}(\rho)}\leq\max_{|\psi\rangle\in\mathrm{Stab}_{n+m}}{\cal R}[({\cal E}\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m}})|\psi\rangle\!\langle\psi|]=\max_{|\psi\rangle\in\mathrm{Stab}_{2n}}{\cal R}[({\cal E}\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{n}})|\psi\rangle\!\langle\psi|],\ \forall\rho,

(S12)

where
m\geq n
. When considering an
n
-qubit unitary
U
, we suppose that
{\cal R}_{\log}^{{\cal A}}(U)
is achieved by an
m^{*}
-qubit ancillary system and an
(n+m^{*})
-qubit input state
|\phi^{*}\rangle
. We have
\log_{2}{\cal R}\left[(U\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m^{*}}})|\phi^{*}\rangle\right]-\log_{2}{\cal R}(|\phi^{*}\rangle)={\cal R}_{\log}^{{\cal A}}(U)\leq\widetilde{{\cal R}}_{\log}^{{\cal A}}(U)
. Thus, we complete the proof.

Definition S3

(Stabilizer extent
[
21
]
)

For any
n
-qubit pure state
|\psi\rangle
, the stabilizer extent
\xi(|\psi\rangle)
is defined as the minimum of
\|\vec{c}\|^{2}_{1}
over all stabilizer decompositions
|\psi\rangle=\sum_{\alpha=1}^{k}c_{\alpha}|\phi_{\alpha}\rangle

, where
|\phi_{\alpha}\rangle
are normalized pure stabilizer states.

Definition S4

(Amortized log stabilizer extent)

The amortized log stabilizer extent of an
n
-qubit unitary
U
is defined as

\xi_{\log}^{{\cal A}}(U)\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\sup_{m\in{{\mathbb{N}}}^{+}}\max_{|\phi\rangle\in{\cal H}_{n+m}}\log_{2}\xi\left[(U\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m}})|\phi\rangle\right]-\log_{2}\xi(|\phi\rangle),

(S13)

where the maximization is with respect to all pure states in Hilbert space
{\cal H}_{n+m}
. The strict amortized log stabilizer extent of
U
is defined as

\widetilde{\xi}_{\log}^{{\cal A}}(U)\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\sup_{m\in{{\mathbb{N}}}^{+}}\max_{|\phi\rangle\in\mathrm{Stab}_{n+m}}\log_{2}\xi[(U\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m}})|\phi\rangle].

(S14)

Analogous to Lemma
S5
for the restricted amortized
\alpha
-stabilizer Rényi entropy, we show that for the strict amortized log stabilizer extent, it suffices to tensor the input unitary with an identity operation of the same dimension, i.e.,
\widetilde{\xi}_{\log}^{{\cal A}}(U)=\max_{|\phi\rangle\in\mathrm{Stab}_{2n}}\log_{2}\xi[(U\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{n}})|\phi\rangle]
. This also follows from the subadditivity property and invariance under Clifford operations of the log stabilizer extent.

Proposition S7

For any
n
-qubit unitary
U
,
\xi_{\log}^{{\cal A}}(U)=\widetilde{\xi}_{\log}^{{\cal A}}(U)
.

Proof.

From the definitions, we directly have
\xi_{\log}^{{\cal A}}(U)\geq\widetilde{\xi}_{\log}^{{\cal A}}(U)
. It suffices to show that
\xi_{\log}^{{\cal A}}(U)\leq\widetilde{\xi}_{\log}^{{\cal A}}(U)
. For any fixed
(n+m)
-qubit state
|\psi\rangle
, suppose the optimal stabilizer state decomposition regarding to stabilizer extent is
|\psi\rangle=\sum_{j}c_{j}|\phi_{j}\rangle
, where
\log_{2}\xi(|\psi\rangle)=\log_{2}(\sum_{j}|c_{j}|)
. It follows that

(U\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m}})|\psi\rangle=\sum_{j}c_{j}(U\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m}})|\phi_{j}\rangle=\sum_{i,j}c_{j}\beta_{i,j}|\phi_{i,j}\rangle,

(S15)

where
\xi[(U\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m}})|\phi_{j}\rangle]=\sum_{i}|\beta_{i,j}|
.
Note that Eq. (
S15
) is a feasible decomposition of
(U\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m}})|\psi\rangle
but not optimal. It follows that

\displaystyle\xi[(U\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m}})|\psi\rangle]

\displaystyle\leq\sum_{i,j}|c_{j}\beta_{i,j}|

(S16a)

\displaystyle=\sum_{j}|c_{j}|\sum_{i}|\beta_{i,j}|

(S16b)

\displaystyle=\sum_{j}|c_{j}|\xi[(U\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m}})|\phi_{j}\rangle]

(S16c)

\displaystyle\leq\xi(|\psi\rangle)\xi[(U\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{n}})|\phi^{*}\rangle],

(S16d)

where
|\phi^{*}\rangle
is the optimal
2n
-qubit pure stabilizer state enabling
\widetilde{\xi}_{\log}^{{\cal A}}(U)=\log_{2}\xi[(U\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{n}})|\phi^{*}\rangle]
.
Thus, after taking the logarithm on both sides of Eq. (
S16d
), it follows that for any
(n+m)
-qubit pure state
|\psi\rangle
,

\log_{2}\xi[(U\otimes\mathbbm{1}_{2^{m}})|\psi\rangle]-\log_{2}\xi(|\psi\rangle)\leq\widetilde{\xi}_{\log}^{{\cal A}}(U),

(S17)

which yields
\xi_{\log}^{{\cal A}}(U)\leq\widetilde{\xi}_{\log}^{{\cal A}}(U)
and completes the proof.
