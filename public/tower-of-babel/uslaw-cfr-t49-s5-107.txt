49 C.F.R. § 5.107 — Coordination with the Office of Inspector General on criminal matters.
Citation: us:cfr:49:5.107
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-5.107
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 5 (Administrative Rulemaking, Guidance, And Enforcement Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All Department employees must comply with the operative DOT Order(s) addressing referrals of potential criminal matters to the Office of Inspector General (OIG), consistent with the respective roles of the OIG and DOT OAs and components of OST in criminal investigations and the OIG's investigative procedures under the Inspector General Act of 1978, as amended.
