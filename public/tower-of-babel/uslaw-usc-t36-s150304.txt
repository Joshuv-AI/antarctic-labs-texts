36 U.S.C. § 150304 — Annual meeting
Citation: us:usc:36:150304
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section150304
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1503 (National Academy Of Sciences). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation shall hold an annual meeting at a place designated by the corporation.
