32 C.F.R. § 770.41 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:32:770.41
Creator: Department Of The Navy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-770.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 770 (Rules Limiting Public Access To Particular Installations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this subpart is to promulgate regulations and procedures governing entry upon Naval Submarine Base New London, and to prevent the interruption of the stated functions and operations of Naval Submarine Base New London, by the presence of any unauthorized person within the boundaries of Naval Submarine Base New London.
