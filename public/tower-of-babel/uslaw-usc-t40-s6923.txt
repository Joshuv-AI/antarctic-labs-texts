40 U.S.C. § 6923 — Compensation and expenses
Citation: us:usc:40:6923
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section6923
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part C (Federal Building Complexes); Chapter 69 (Union Station Redevelopment); Subchapter II (National Visitor Facilities Advisory Commission). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Members of the National Visitor Facilities Advisory Commission who are not officers or employees of the Federal Government or the government of the District of Columbia are entitled to receive compensation under section 3109 of title 5 and expenses under section 5703 of title 5.
