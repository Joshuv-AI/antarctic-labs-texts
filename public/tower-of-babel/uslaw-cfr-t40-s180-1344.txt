40 C.F.R. § 180.1344 — Cyclaniliprole; exemption from the requirement of a tolerance.
Citation: us:cfr:40:180.1344
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-180.1344
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 180 (Tolerances And Exemptions For Pesticide Chemical Residues In Food). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An exemption from the requirement of a tolerance is established for indirect and inadvertent residues of the insecticide cyclaniliprole, including its metabolites and degradates, in or on all raw agricultural commodities not listed in paragraph (a) of § 180.694, when residues are present therein as a result of subsequent uptake by crops rotated into fields where the crops in § 180.694 (a) were treated with cyclaniliprole.
