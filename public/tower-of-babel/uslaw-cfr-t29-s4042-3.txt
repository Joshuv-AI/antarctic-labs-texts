29 C.F.R. § 4042.3 — Issuance rules.
Citation: us:cfr:29:4042.3
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4042.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4042 (Single-Employer Plan Termination Initiated By Pbgc). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

PBGC applies the rules in subpart B of part 4000 of this chapter to determine permissible methods of issuance under this part. PBGC applies the rules in subpart C of part 4000 of this chapter to determine the date that an issuance under this part was provided.
