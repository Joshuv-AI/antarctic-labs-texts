31 C.F.R. § 208.6 — Availability of Treasury-sponsored accounts.
Citation: us:cfr:31:208.6
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-208.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 208 (Management Of Federal Agency Disbursements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An individual who receives a Federal payment shall be eligible to open a Treasury-sponsored account under terms and conditions established by Treasury.
