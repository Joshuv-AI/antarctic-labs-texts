31 U.S.C. § 103 — United States
Citation: us:usc:31:103
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section103
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 1 (Definitions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this title, “United States”, when used in a geographic sense, means the States of the United States and the District of Columbia.
