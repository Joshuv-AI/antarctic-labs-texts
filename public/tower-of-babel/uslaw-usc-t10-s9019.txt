10 U.S.C. § 9019 — General Counsel
Citation: us:usc:10:9019
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9019
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization); Chapter 903 (Department Of The Air Force). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
There is a General Counsel of the Department of the Air Force, appointed from civilian life by the President, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate.
(b)
The General Counsel shall perform such functions as the Secretary of the Air Force may prescribe.
