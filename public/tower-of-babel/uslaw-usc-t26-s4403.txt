26 U.S.C. § 4403 — Record requirements
Citation: us:usc:26:4403
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section4403
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 35 (Taxes On Wagering); Subchapter A (Tax On Wagers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each person liable for tax under this subchapter shall keep a daily record showing the gross amount of all wagers on which he is so liable, in addition to all other records required pursuant to section 6001(a).
