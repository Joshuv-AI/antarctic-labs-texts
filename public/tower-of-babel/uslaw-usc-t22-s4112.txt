22 U.S.C. § 4112 — Employees represented
Citation: us:usc:22:4112
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section4112
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 52 (Foreign Service); Subchapter X (Labor-Management Relations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The employees of the Department shall constitute a single and separate worldwide bargaining unit, from which there shall be excluded—
(1)
employees engaged in personnel work in other than a purely clerical capacity; and
(2)
employees engaged in criminal or national security investigations or who audit the work of individuals to insure that their functions are discharged honestly and with integrity.
