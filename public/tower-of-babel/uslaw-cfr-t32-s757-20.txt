32 C.F.R. § 757.20 — Receipt and release.
Citation: us:cfr:32:757.20
Creator: Department Of The Navy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-757.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 757 (Affirmative Claims Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The JAG designee will execute and deliver appropriate releases to third parties who have made full or agreed upon compromised payments. A copy of the release will be kept in the claims file.
