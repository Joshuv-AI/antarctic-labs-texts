36 U.S.C. § 150405 — Officers
Citation: us:usc:36:150405
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section150405
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1504 (National American Indian Veterans, Incorporated). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Subject to section 150406, the officers of the corporation, and the election of such officers, shall be as provided in the constitution and bylaws of the corporation and in conformity with the laws of the jurisdiction under which the corporation is incorporated.
