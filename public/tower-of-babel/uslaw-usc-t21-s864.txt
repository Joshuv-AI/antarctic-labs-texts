21 U.S.C. § 864 — Anhydrous ammonia
Citation: us:usc:21:864
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section864
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 13 (Drug Abuse Prevention And Control); Subchapter I (Control And Enforcement); Part D (Offenses And Penalties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
It is unlawful for any person—
(1)
to steal anhydrous ammonia, or
(2)
to transport stolen anhydrous ammonia across State lines,
knowing, intending, or having reasonable cause to believe that such anhydrous ammonia will be used to manufacture a controlled substance in violation of this part.
(b)
Any person who violates subsection (a) shall be imprisoned or fined, or both, in accordance with section 843(d) of this title as if such violation were a violation of a provision of section 843 of this title.
