Correction to: Nature Medicine https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-026-04578-1, published online 10 August 2026.

In the version of the article initially published, the y-axis label in Fig. 3c, g and the right panel of Fig. 5c was “log10(Padj)” but should have been “–log10(Padj)”. This correction has been made to the HTML and PDF versions of the article.
