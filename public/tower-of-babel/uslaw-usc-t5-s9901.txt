5 U.S.C. § 9901 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:5:9901
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section9901
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart I (Miscellaneous); Chapter 99 (Department Of Defense Personnel Authorities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of this chapter—
(1)
the term “Director” means the Director of the Office of Personnel Management; and
(2)
the term “Secretary” means the Secretary of Defense.
