24 C.F.R. § 1000.103 — How may IHBG funds be used for tenant-based or project-based rental assistance?
Citation: us:cfr:24:1000.103
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-1000.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 1000 (Native American Housing Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) IHBG funds may be used for project-based or tenant-based rental assistance.
(b) IHBG funds may be used for project-based or tenant-based rental assistance that is provided in a manner consistent with section 8 of the United States Housing Act of 1937 (42 U.S.C. 1437f).
(c) IHBG funds used for project-based or tenant-based rental assistance must comply with the requirements of NAHASDA and this part.
