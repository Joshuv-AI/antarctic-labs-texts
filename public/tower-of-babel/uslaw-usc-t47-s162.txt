47 U.S.C. § 162 — Additional research authorities of the FCC
Citation: us:usc:47:162
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title47-section162
Context: United States Code, Title 47 (Telecommunications); Chapter 5 (Wire Or Radio Communication); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In order to carry out the purposes of this chapter, the Commission may—
(1)
undertake research and development work in connection with any matter in relation to which the Commission has jurisdiction; and
(2)
promote the carrying out of such research and development by others, or otherwise to arrange for such research and development to be carried out by others.
