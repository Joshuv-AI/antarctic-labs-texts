49 C.F.R. § 262.7 — Eligibility.
Citation: us:cfr:49:262.7
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-262.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 262 (Implementation Of Program For Capital Grants For Rail Line Relocation And Improvement Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A State is eligible for a grant from FRA under this section for any construction project for the improvement of the route or structure of a rail line that either:
(1) Is carried out for the purpose of mitigating the adverse effects of rail traffic on safety, motor vehicle traffic flow, community quality of life, or economic development; or
(2) Involves a lateral or vertical relocation of any portion of the rail line.
(b) Only costs associated with construction as defined in § 262.3 will be considered allowable costs.
