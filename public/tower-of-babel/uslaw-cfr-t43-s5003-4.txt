43 C.F.R. § 5003.4 — Definitions: General.
Citation: us:cfr:43:5003.4
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-5003.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 5000 (Administration Of Forest Management Decisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Forest management activity generally means activities with a silvicultural or forest protection objective including associated actions needed to carry out the silvicultural or forest protection objective, such as construction and maintenance of roads and improvements.
