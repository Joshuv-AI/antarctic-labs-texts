42 C.F.R. § 411.30 — Effect of primary payment on benefit utilization and deductibles.
Citation: us:cfr:42:411.30
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-411.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 411 (Exclusions From Medicare And Limitations On Medicare Payment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Benefit utilization. Inpatient psychiatric hospital and SNF care that is paid for by a primary payer is not counted against the number of inpatient care days available to the beneficiary under Medicare Part A.
(b) Deductibles. Expenses for Medicare covered services that are paid for by primary payers are credited toward the Medicare Part A and Part B deductibles.
