29 C.F.R. § 779.260 — Trade-in allowances.
Citation: us:cfr:29:779.260
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-779.260
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 779 (The Fair Labor Standards Act As Applied To Retailers Of Goods Or Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where merchandise is taken in trade when a sale is made, the annual gross volume of sales or business will include the gross amount of the sale before deduction of the allowance on such trade-in merchandise. This is so even though an overallowance or excessive value is allowed on the trade-in merchandise. In turn, when the trade-in merchandise is sold the amount of the sale will be included in the annual gross volume.
