49 C.F.R. § 555.14 — Processing of petitions.
Citation: us:cfr:49:555.14
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-555.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 555 (Temporary Exemption From Motor Vehicle Safety And Bumper Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator shall notify the petitioner whether the petition is complete within 30 days of receipt. The Administrator shall attempt to approve or deny any complete petition submitted under this subpart within 120 days after the agency acknowledges that the application is complete. Upon good cause shown, the Administrator may review a petition on an expedited basis.
