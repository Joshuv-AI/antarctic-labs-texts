5 C.F.R. § 2428.1 — Scope.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 2428 (Enforcement Of Assistant Secretary Standards Of Conduct Decisions And Orders). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth procedures under which the Authority, pursuant to 5 U.S.C. 7105(a)(2)(I), will enforce decisions and orders of the Assistant Secretary in standards of conduct matters arising under 5 U.S.C. 7120.
