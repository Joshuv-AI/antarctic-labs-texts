42 C.F.R. § 93.238 — Retaliation.
Citation: us:cfr:42:93.238
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-93.238
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 93 (Public Health Service Policies On Research Misconduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Retaliation means an adverse action taken against a complainant, witness, or committee member by an institution or one of its members in response to:
(a) A good faith allegation of research misconduct; or
(b) Good faith cooperation with a research misconduct proceeding.
