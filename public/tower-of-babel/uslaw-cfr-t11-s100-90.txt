11 C.F.R. § 100.90 — Ballot access fees.
Citation: us:cfr:11:100.90
Creator: Federal Election Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-100.90
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 100 (Scope And Definitions (52 U.S.C. 30101)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Payments made to any party committee by a candidate or the authorized committee of a candidate as a condition of ballot access are not contributions.
