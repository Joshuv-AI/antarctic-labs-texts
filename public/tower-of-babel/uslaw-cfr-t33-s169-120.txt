33 C.F.R. § 169.120 — When is the southeastern reporting system in effect?
Citation: us:cfr:33:169.120
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-169.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 169 (Ship Reporting Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The mandatory ship reporting system in the southeastern United States operates during the period beginning on November 15 each year through April 16 of the following year.
