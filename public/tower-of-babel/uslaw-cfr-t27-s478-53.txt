27 C.F.R. § 478.53 — Change in trade name.
Citation: us:cfr:27:478.53
Creator: Bureau Of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms, And Explosives, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-478.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 478 (Commerce In Firearms And Ammunition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A licensee continuing to conduct business at the location shown on his license is not required to obtain a new license by reason of a mere change in trade name under which he conducts his business: Provided, That such licensee furnishes his license for endorsement of such change to the Chief, Federal Firearms Licensing Center within 30 days from the date the licensee begins his business under the new trade name.
