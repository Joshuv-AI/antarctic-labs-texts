38 U.S.C. § 3481 — Educational assistance allowance

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part III (Readjustment And Related Benefits); Chapter 34 (Veterans’ Educational Assistance); Subchapter IV (Payments To Eligible Veterans; Veteran-Student Services). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) General.—
The Secretary shall, in accordance with the applicable provisions of this section and chapter 36 of this title, pay to each eligible veteran who is pursuing a program of education under this chapter an educational assistance allowance to meet, in part, the expenses of the veteran’s subsistence, tuition, fees, supplies, books, equipment, and other educational costs.
(b) Institutional Training.—
The educational assistance allowance of an eligible veteran pursuing a program of education, other than a program exclusively by correspondence, at an educational institution shall be paid as provided in chapter 36 of this title.
