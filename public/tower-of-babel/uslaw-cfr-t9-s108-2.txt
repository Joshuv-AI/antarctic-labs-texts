9 C.F.R. § 108.2 — Plot plans, blueprints, and legends required.
Citation: us:cfr:9:108.2
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-108.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 108 (Facility Requirements For Licensed Establishments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each applicant for an establishment license shall prepare a plot plan showing all buildings for each particular land area, blueprints for each building used in the preparation of biological products and legends containing a brief description of all activities in each room or area.
