42 U.S.C. § 10306 — Administrative costs
Citation: us:usc:42:10306
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section10306
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 109 (Water Resources Research). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

From the sums appropriated pursuant to this chapter, not more than 7.5 per centum shall be utilized for administrative costs.
