10 C.F.R. § 871.4 — Limitation on redelegation of authority.
Citation: us:cfr:10:871.4
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-871.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 871 (Air Transportation Of Plutonium). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The authority delegated in this part may not be redelegated without the prior approval of the Administrator of the National Nuclear Security Administration.
