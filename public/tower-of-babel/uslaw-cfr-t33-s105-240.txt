33 C.F.R. § 105.240 — Procedures for interfacing with vessels.
Citation: us:cfr:33:105.240
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-105.240
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 105 (Maritime Security: Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The facility owner or operator must ensure that there are measures for interfacing with vessels at all MARSEC Levels.
