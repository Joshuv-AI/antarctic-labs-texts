29 C.F.R. § 1960.73 — Federal agency injury and illness recordkeeping forms.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1960.73
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1960.73
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1960 (Basic Program Elements For Federal Employee Occupational Safety And Health Programs And Related Matters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) When filling out the OSHA Form 300 or equivalent, each agency must enter the employee's OPM job series number and job title in Column (c).
(b) When recording the injuries and illnesses of uncompensated volunteers, each agency must enter a “V” before the OPM job series number in Column (c) of the OSH Form 300 log or equivalent.
(c) Each agency must calculate the total number of hours worked by uncompensated volunteers.
