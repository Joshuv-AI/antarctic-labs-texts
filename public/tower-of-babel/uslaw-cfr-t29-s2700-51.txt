29 C.F.R. § 2700.51 — Hearing dates and sites.
Citation: us:cfr:29:2700.51
Creator: Federal Mine Safety And Health Review Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-2700.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 2700 (Procedural Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All cases will be assigned a hearing date and site by order of the ALJ. In fixing the time and place of the hearing, the ALJ shall give due regard to the convenience and necessity of the parties or their representatives and witnesses, the availability of suitable hearing facilities, and other relevant factors.
