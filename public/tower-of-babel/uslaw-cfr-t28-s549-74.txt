28 C.F.R. § 549.74 — Inmates without funds.
Citation: us:cfr:28:549.74
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-549.74
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 549 (Medical Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You will not be charged a health care service fee if you are considered indigent and unable to pay the health care service fee. The Warden may establish procedures to prevent abuse of this provision.
