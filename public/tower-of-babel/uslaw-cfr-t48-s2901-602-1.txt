48 C.F.R. § 2901.602-1 — 2901.602-1 Authority.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2901.602-1
Creator: Chapter 29—Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2901.602-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2901 (Department Of Labor Acquisition Regulation System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Only DOL contracting officers have the authority to enter into, administer, or terminate contracts and to make related determinations and findings. DOL contracting officers may bind DOL to obligations under contracts only to the extent of the authority delegated to them.
