49 C.F.R. § 523.9 — Truck tractors.
Citation: us:cfr:49:523.9
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-523.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 523 (Vehicle Classification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Truck tractors for the purpose of this part are considered as any truck tractor as defined in 49 CFR part 571 having a GVWR above 26,000 pounds.
