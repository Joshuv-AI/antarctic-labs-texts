7 U.S.C. § 1424 — Utilization of services and facilities of Commodity Credit Corporation
Citation: us:usc:7:1424
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1424
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 35A (Price Support Of Agricultural Commodities); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary, in carrying out programs under section 612c of this title and section 1755 of title 42, may utilize the services and facilities of the Commodity Credit Corporation (including but not limited to procurement by contract), and make advance payments to it.
