3 C.F.R. § 101.1 — Executive Office of the President.
Citation: us:cfr:3:101.1
Creator: Executive Office Of The President
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-3/section-101.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 3 (The President), Part 101 (Public Information Provisions Of The Administrative Procedures Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Until further regulations are promulgated, the remainder of the entities within the Executive Office of the President, to the extent that 5 U.S.C. 552 is applicable, shall follow the procedures set forth in the regulations applicable to the Office of Management and Budget (5 CFR Ch. III). Requests for information from these other entities should be submitted directly to such entity.
