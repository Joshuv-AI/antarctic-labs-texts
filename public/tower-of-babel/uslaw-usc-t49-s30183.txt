49 U.S.C. § 30183 — Prohibition on certain disclosures
Citation: us:usc:49:30183
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section30183
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (General); Chapter 301 (Motor Vehicle Safety); Subchapter V (Motor Vehicle Safety Research And Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any report of the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, or of any officer, employee, or contractor of the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, relating to any highway traffic accident or the investigation of such accident conducted pursuant to this chapter or section 403 of title 23, may be made available to the public only in a manner that does not identify individuals.
