50 C.F.R. § 300.120 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:50:300.120
Creator: International Fishing And Related Activities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-300.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 300 (International Fisheries Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart implements fishery conservation and management measures as provided in fishery agreements pursuant to the Treaty Between the Government of the United States of America and the Government of the Republic of Colombia Concerning the Status of Quita Sueno, Roncador and Serrana (TIAS 10120) (Treaty).
