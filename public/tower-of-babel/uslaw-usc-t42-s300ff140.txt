42 U.S.C. § 300ff–140 — Applicability of part
Citation: us:usc:42:300ff–140
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section300ff–140
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter XXIV (Hiv Health Care Services Program); Part G (Notification Of Possible Exposure To Infectious Diseases). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This part shall not apply in a State if the chief executive officer of the State certifies to the Secretary that the law of the State is substantially consistent with this part.
