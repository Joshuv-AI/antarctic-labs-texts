46 C.F.R. § 401.500 — Penalties for violations.
Citation: us:cfr:46:401.500
Creator: Coast Guard (Great Lakes Pilotage), Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-401.500
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 401 (Great Lakes Pilotage Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person, including a Pilot, Master, owner, or agent, who violates any provision of this part may be liable to the United States for a civil penalty as set forth in 46 U.S.C. 9308.
