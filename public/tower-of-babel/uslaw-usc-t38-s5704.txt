38 U.S.C. § 5704 — Transcript of trial records
Citation: us:usc:38:5704
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section5704
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part IV (General Administrative Provisions); Chapter 57 (Records And Investigations); Subchapter I (Records). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may purchase transcripts of the record, including all evidence, of trial of litigated cases.
