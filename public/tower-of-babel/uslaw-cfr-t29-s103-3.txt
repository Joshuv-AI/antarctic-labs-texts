29 C.F.R. § 103.3 — Horseracing and dogracing industries.
Citation: us:cfr:29:103.3
Creator: National Labor Relations Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-103.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 103 (Other Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board will not assert its jurisdiction in any proceeding under sections 8, 9, and 10 of the Act involving the horseracing and dogracing industries.
