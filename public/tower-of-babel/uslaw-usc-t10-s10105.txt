10 U.S.C. § 10105 — Army National Guard of the United States: composition
Citation: us:usc:10:10105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section10105
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And Administration); Chapter 1003 (Reserve Components Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Army National Guard of the United States is the reserve component of the Army that consists of—
(1)
federally recognized units and organizations of the Army National Guard; and
(2)
members of the Army National Guard who are also Reserves of the Army.
