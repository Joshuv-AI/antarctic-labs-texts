46 C.F.R. § 119.405 — Fuel restrictions.
Citation: us:cfr:46:119.405
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-119.405
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 119 (Machinery Installation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The use of a fuel, other than diesel fuel, as an alternative fuel for an internal combustion engine, except gasoline when used as a fuel for outboard motors as allowed by § 119.458 of this part, will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis by the Commandant.
