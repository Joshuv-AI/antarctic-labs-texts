22 U.S.C. § 2796c — Applicability of other statutory provisions
Citation: us:usc:22:2796c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2796c
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 39 (Arms Export Control); Subchapter VI (Leases Of Defense Articles And Loan Authority For Cooperative Research And Development Purposes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any reference to sales of defense articles under this chapter in any provision of law restricting the countries or organizations to which such sales may be made shall be deemed to include a reference to leases of defense articles under this subchapter.
