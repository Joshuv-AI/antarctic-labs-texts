16 C.F.R. § 460.15 — How installers must handle fact sheets.
Citation: us:cfr:16:460.15
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-460.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 460 (Labeling And Advertising Of Home Insulation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you are an installer, you must have fact sheets for the insulation products you sell. Before customers agree to buy insulation from you, you must show them the fact sheet(s) for the type(s) of insulation they want. You can decide how to do this. For example, you can give each customer a copy of the fact sheet(s). You can keep the fact sheets in a binder, and show customers the binder before they agree to buy.
