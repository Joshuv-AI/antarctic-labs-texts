34 C.F.R. § 694.11 — What is the maximum indirect cost rate for an agency of a State or local government?
Citation: us:cfr:34:694.11
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-694.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 694 (Gaining Early Awareness And Readiness For Undergraduate Programs (Gear Up)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notwithstanding 34 CFR 75.560-75.562 and 2 CFR part 200, subpart E—Cost Principles, the maximum indirect cost rate that an agency of a State or local government receiving funds under GEAR UP may use to charge indirect costs to these funds is the lesser of—
(a) The rate established by the negotiated indirect cost agreement; or
(b) Eight percent of a modified total direct cost base.
