Groupoids, equivalence bibundles and bimodules for noncommutative solenoids
Paulo Carrillo Rouse, Laurent Guillaume

Abstract
Let p \displaystyle p be a prime number and 𝒮 p \displaystyle\mathscr{S}_{p} the p \displaystyle p -solenoid. For α ∈ ℝ × ℚ p \displaystyle\alpha\in\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{Q}_{p} we consider in this paper a naturally associated action groupoid S α := ℤ ⁡ [ 1 / p ] ⋉ α 𝒮 p ⇉ 𝒮 p \displaystyle S_{\alpha}:={\mathbb{Z}}[1/p]\ltimes_{\alpha}\mathscr{S}_{p}\rightrightarrows\mathscr{S}_{p} whose C ∗ − \displaystyle C^{*}- algebra is a model for the noncommutative solenoid 𝒜 α 𝒮 \displaystyle{\mathcal{A}}_{\alpha}^{\mathscr{S}} studied by Latremolière and Packer. Following the geometric ideas of Connes and Rieffel to describe the Morita equivalences of noncommutative torus using the Kronecker foliation on the torus, we give an explicit description of the geometric/topologic equivalence bibundle for groupoids S α \displaystyle S_{\alpha} and S β \displaystyle S_{\beta} whenever α , β ∈ ℝ × ℚ p \displaystyle\alpha,\beta\in{\mathbb{R}}\times{\mathbb{Q}}_{p} are in the same orbit of the G ​ L 2 ​ ( ℤ ⁡ [ 1 / p ] ) \displaystyle GL_{2}({\mathbb{Z}}[1/p]) action by linear fractional transformations. As a corollary, for α , β ∈ ℝ × ℚ p \displaystyle\alpha,\beta\in{\mathbb{R}}\times{\mathbb{Q}}_{p} as above we get an explicit description of the imprimitivity bimodules for the associated noncommutative solenoids.



Introduction

One of the first and most studied examples of noncommutative spaces in noncommuative geometry is the so called noncommutative torus
\displaystyle A_{\theta}
for non rational
\displaystyle\theta\in\mathbb{R}
(the rational case can be as well defined but it is less interesting as a ”noncommutative” space). The
\displaystyle C^{*}
-algebra
\displaystyle A_{\theta}
admits many different descriptions (up to isomorphism and up to Morita as we will recall in the text). For example it can be realized as the
\displaystyle C^{*}-
algebra of the transformation groupoid associated to the action of
\displaystyle{\mathbb{Z}}
on
\displaystyle S^{1}
by rotations by
\displaystyle\theta
, we denote by
\displaystyle\mathbb{T}_{\theta}:={\mathbb{Z}}\ltimes_{\theta}S^{1}\rightrightarrows S^{1}
this Lie groupoid.

One of main notions of equivalence for
\displaystyle C^{*}
-algebras in noncommutative geometry is the one introduced by Rieffel and called strong Morita equivalence. In
[
10
]
, Rieffel showed that
\displaystyle A_{\theta}
and
\displaystyle A_{\theta^{\prime}}
are strongly Morita equivalent if and only if
\displaystyle\theta
and
\displaystyle\theta^{\prime}
are in the same orbit of the
\displaystyle GL_{2}({\mathbb{Z}})
action on irrational numbers by linear fractional transformations. Later on, with Connes (see Connes book
[
1
]
section
\displaystyle II.8.\beta
), they gave an even more geometric description of these equivalences for noncommutative torus by exploring the restriction to complete transversals on the holonomy groupoid associated to the so called Kronecker foliation of the torus by irrational slopes
\displaystyle\theta
. This geometric approach allows them in particular to give a precise description of the Morita equivalence bimodules.

For groupoids (Lie or locally compact) there is a topological/geometrical notion of Morita equivalence given by the so called equivalence bibundles. This very studied equivalence in geometry/topology allows to properly formalize the study of geometrical/topological stacks. Now, given two Morita equivalent groupoids it is known that the corresponding
\displaystyle C^{*}
-algebras are strongly Morita equivalent (Renault’s equivalence theorem, see for instance 2.70 in
[
11
]
). In fact, whenever an appropriate/explicit description of an equivalence bibundle is given there is a very natural construction of the associated Morita equivalence bimodule.

For
\displaystyle\theta,\theta^{\prime}
irrationals in the same
\displaystyle GL_{2}({\mathbb{Z}})
-orbit, the Connes-Rieffel approach gives a good glimpse of the explicit description of an equivalence bibundle. By explicit we mean a precise bibundle space, explicit left and right moment maps and explicit left and right groupoid actions. The motivation for the content of the present article was to give an explicit description for the equivalence bibundles for
\displaystyle\mathbb{T}_{\theta}
and
\displaystyle\mathbb{T}_{\theta^{\prime}}
for
\displaystyle\theta,\theta^{\prime}
irrationals in the same
\displaystyle GL_{2}({\mathbb{Z}})
-orbit. For noncommutative torus, this expected folklore result was for us the starting point to go beyond on the exploration for the case of noncommutative solenoids. Let us explain with more detail the content of this work.

Let
\displaystyle p
be a prime number and
\displaystyle\mathscr{S}_{p}
the associated solenoid. Given
\displaystyle\alpha\in{\mathbb{R}}\times{\mathbb{Q}}_{p}
there is a transformation groupoid

(0.1)

whose

Our main theorem (statement

-equivalence where
