46 C.F.R. § 147A.9 — A.9 Persons in charge of fumigation and the vessel; designation.
Citation: us:cfr:46:147A.9
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-147A.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 147A (Part 147A—Interim Regulations For Shipboard Fumigation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The person, including any individual, firm, association, partnership, or corporation, that is conducting a fumigation operation shall designate a person in charge of fumigation for each operation.
(b) The operator of each vessel shall designate a person in charge of the vessel for each fumigation operation.
