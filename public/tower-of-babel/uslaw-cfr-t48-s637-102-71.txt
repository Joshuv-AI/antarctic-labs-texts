48 C.F.R. § 637.102-71 — 637.102-71 Safety considerations.
Citation: us:cfr:48:637.102-71
Creator: Chapter 6—Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-637.102-71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 637 (Service Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When contracting for services to be performed overseas, always consider 636.513(b) and FAR 36.513(b), and consult with technical representatives to determine whether special precautions are appropriate, such as when the services are for building operations, building maintenance or infrastructure repairs.
