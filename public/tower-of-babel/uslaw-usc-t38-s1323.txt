38 U.S.C. § 1323 — Certifications with respect to circumstances of death
Citation: us:usc:38:1323
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section1323
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 13 (Dependency And Indemnity Compensation For Service-Connected Deaths); Subchapter III (Certifications). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whenever the Secretary determines on the basis of a claim for benefits filed with the Secretary that a death occurred under the circumstances referred to in section 1476(a) of title 10, the Secretary shall certify that fact to the Secretary concerned. In all other cases, the Secretary shall make the determination referred to in such section at the request of the Secretary concerned.
