20 U.S.C. § 80k — Donation and transfer of lands and improvements, works of art, and other assets and property of Museum of African Art to Smithsonian Institution
Citation: us:usc:20:80k
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section80k
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 3 (Smithsonian Institution, National Museums And Art Galleries); Subchapter XII (Museum Of African Art). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution (hereinafter in this subchapter referred to as the “Board”) is authorized to accept a deed or other instrument donating and transferring to the Smithsonian Institution, the land and improvements thereto, collections of works of art, and all other assets and property of the Museum of African Art.
