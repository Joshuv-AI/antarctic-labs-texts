43 C.F.R. § 35.43 — Collection of civil penalties and assessments.
Citation: us:cfr:43:35.43
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-35.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 35 (Administrative Remedies For Fraudulent Claims And Statements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Sections 3806 and 3808(b) of title 31, U.S. Code, authorize actions for collection of civil penalties and assessments imposed under this part and specify the procedures for such actions.
