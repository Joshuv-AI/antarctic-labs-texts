22 U.S.C. § 9762 — Prohibition on construction of provisions of this chapter as an authorization for the use of military force
Citation: us:usc:22:9762
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section9762
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 104 (Venezuela Assistance); Subchapter VII (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter may be construed as an authorization for the use of military force.
