12 C.F.R. § 619.9200 — Loss-sharing agreements.
Citation: us:cfr:12:619.9200
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-619.9200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 619 (Definitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A contractual arrangement under which the parties agree to share losses associated with loans or otherwise, as may be provided for in the agreement.
