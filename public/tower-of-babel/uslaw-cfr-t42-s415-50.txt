42 C.F.R. § 415.50 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:42:415.50
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-415.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 415 (Services Furnished By Physicians In Providers, Supervising Physicians In Teaching Settings, And Residents In Certain Settings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart sets forth rules for payment by fiscal intermediaries to providers for services furnished by physicians. Payment for covered services is made either under the prospective payment system (PPS) to PPS-participating providers in accordance with part 412 of this chapter or under the reasonable cost method to non-PPS participating providers in accordance with part 413 of this chapter.
