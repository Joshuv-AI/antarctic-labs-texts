42 C.F.R. § 87.5 — What information must be included in the grant application?
Citation: us:cfr:42:87.5
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-87.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 87 (National Institute For Occupational Safety And Health Research And Demonstration Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The application must contain a complete description of the objective of the project and the plan for carrying out the research or demonstration, the name and qualifications of the principal investigator or project director and principal staff members, the total resources and facilities that will be available, and a justification of the amount of grant funds requested.
