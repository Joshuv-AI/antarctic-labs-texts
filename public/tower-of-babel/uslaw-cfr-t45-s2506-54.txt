45 C.F.R. § 2506.54 — Can a judgment I have obtained against the United States be used to satisfy a debt that I owe to the Corporation?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2506.54
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2506.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2506 (Collection Of Debts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. Collection by offset against a judgment obtained by a debtor against the United States will be accomplished in accordance with 31 U.S.C. 3728 and 31 U.S.C. 3716.
