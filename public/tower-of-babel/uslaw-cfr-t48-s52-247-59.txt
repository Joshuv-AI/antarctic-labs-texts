48 C.F.R. § 52.247-59 — 52.247-59 F.o.b. Origin—Carload and Truckload Shipments.
Citation: us:cfr:48:52.247-59

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-52.247-59
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 52 (Solicitation Provisions And Contract Clauses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As prescribed in 47.305-16(a), insert the following clause in solicitations and contracts when it is contemplated that they may result in f.o.b. origin contracts with shipments in carloads or truckloads. This will facilitate realistic freight cost evaluations of offers and ensure that contractors produce economical shipments of agreed size.
