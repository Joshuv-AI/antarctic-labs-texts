5 C.F.R. § 339.101 — Coverage.
Citation: us:cfr:5:339.101
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-339.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 339 (Medical Qualification Determinations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to—
(a) Applicants for and employees in competitive service positions; and
(b) Applicants for and employees in positions excepted from the competitive service when medical issues arise in connection with an OPM regulation that governs a particular personnel action, such as removal of a preference eligible employee in the excepted service under part 752.
