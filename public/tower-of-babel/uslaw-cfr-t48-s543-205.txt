48 C.F.R. § 543.205 — 543.205 Contract clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:543.205
Creator: Chapter 5—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-543.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 543 (Contract Modifications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Insert the clause at 552.243-71, Equitable Adjustments, in solicitations and contracts that include any of the following FAR clauses: 52.243-4, 52.243-5, or 52.236-2.
