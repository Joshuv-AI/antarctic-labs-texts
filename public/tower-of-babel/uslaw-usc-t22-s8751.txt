22 U.S.C. § 8751 — Codification of sanctions with respect to grave human rights abuses by the Governments of Iran and Syria using information technology
Citation: us:usc:22:8751
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section8751
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 94 (Iran Threat Reduction And Syria Human Rights); Subchapter IV (Measures To Promote Human Rights). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

United States sanctions with respect to Iran and Syria provided for in Executive Order No. 13606 (77 Fed. Reg. 24571), as in effect on the day before August 10, 2012, shall remain in effect—
(1)
with respect to Iran, until the date that is 30 days after the date on which the President submits to Congress the certification described in section 8551(a) of this title; and
(2)
with respect to Syria, until the date on which the provisions of and sanctions imposed pursuant to subchapter VII terminate pursuant to section 8795 of this title.
