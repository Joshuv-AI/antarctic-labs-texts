Notice of Joint Meeting of the Federal Energy Regulatory 
Commission and the Nuclear Regulatory Commission

    The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (FERC) and the Nuclear 
Regulatory Commission (NRC) will hold a joint open meeting on Thursday, 
October 22, 2026, from 1:00 p.m. to 3:30 p.m. Eastern Standard Time. 
The meeting will be held in-person at the U.S. Nuclear Regulatory 
Commission's headquarters, One White Flint North, 11555 Rockville Pike, 
Rockville, MD 20852-2738 in the Commissioners Hearing Room. 
Commissioners from both agencies are expected to participate.
    The format for the joint meeting will consist of discussions 
between the two sets of Commissioners following presentations by their 
respective staffs. In addition, a representative of the North American 
Electric Reliability Corporation (NERC) will attend and participate in 
this meeting.
    The meeting is not for the purpose of discussing the merits of any 
specific proceedings before FERC, and any such discussions will be 
curtailed. Proceedings before FERC raising possibly relevant issues 
include, but are not limited to:

is not required and there is no fee for attendance. Information on this

will also be transcribed. Transcripts will be available on NRC's 
website at: https://www.nrc.gov/documents-reports/document-collections/commission-documents/commission-meetings-agendas-slides-transcripts-meeting-srms-and-full-written-explanation-for-closed-meetings/2026transcribe.
