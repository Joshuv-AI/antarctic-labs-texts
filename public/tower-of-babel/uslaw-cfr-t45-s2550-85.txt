45 C.F.R. § 2550.85 — How will the State Plan be assessed?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2550.85
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2550.85
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2550 (Requirements And General Provisions For State Commissions And Alternative Administrative Entities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Corporation will assess the quality of your State Plan as evidenced by:
(a) The development and quality of realistic goals and objectives for moving service ahead in the State;
(b) The extent to which proposed strategies can reasonably be expected to accomplish stated goals; and
(c) The extent of input in the development of the State plan from a broad cross-section of individuals and organizations as required by § 2550.80(a)(1).
