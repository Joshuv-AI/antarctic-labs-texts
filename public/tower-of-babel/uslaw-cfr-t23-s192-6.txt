23 C.F.R. § 192.6 — Period of availability of withheld funds.

Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 192 (Drug Offender'S Driver'S License Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Funds withheld under § 192.4 from apportionment to any State will not be available for apportionment to the State and shall lapse immediately.
