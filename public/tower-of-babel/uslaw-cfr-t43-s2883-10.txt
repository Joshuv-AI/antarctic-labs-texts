43 C.F.R. § 2883.10 — Who may hold a grant or TUP?
Citation: us:cfr:43:2883.10
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2883.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2880 (Rights-Of-Way Under The Mineral Leasing Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To hold a grant or TUP under these regulations, you must be:
(a)(1) A United States citizen, an association of such citizens, or a corporation, partnership, association, or similar business entity organized under the laws of the United States, or of any state therein; or
(2) A state or local government; and
(b) Financially and technically able to construct, operate, maintain, and terminate the proposed facilities.
