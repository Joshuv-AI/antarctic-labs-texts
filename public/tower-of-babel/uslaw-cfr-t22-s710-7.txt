22 C.F.R. § 710.7 — Time, date and place of hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:22:710.7
Creator: Us International Development Finance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-710.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 710 (Administrative Enforcement Procedures Of Post-Employment Restrictions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The examiner shall establish a reasonable time, date and place to conduct the hearing. In establishing a date, the examiner shall give due regard to the former employee's need for:
(a) Adequate time to prepare a defense properly; and
(b) An expeditious resolution of allegations that may be damaging to the individual's reputation.
