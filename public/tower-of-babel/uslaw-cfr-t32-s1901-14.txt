32 C.F.R. § 1901.14 — Fees.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1901.14
Creator: Central Intelligence Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1901.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1901 (Public Rights Under The Privacy Act Of 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No fees will be charged for any action under the authority of the Privacy Act, 5 U.S.C. 552a, irrespective of the fact that a request is or may be processed under the authority of both the Privacy Act and the Freedom of Information Act.
