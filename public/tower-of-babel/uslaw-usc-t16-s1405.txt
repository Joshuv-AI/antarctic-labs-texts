16 U.S.C. § 1405 — Coordination with other Federal agencies
Citation: us:usc:16:1405
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section1405
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 31 (Marine Mammal Protection); Subchapter III (Marine Mammal Commission). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commission shall have access to all studies and data compiled by Federal agencies regarding marine mammals. With the consent of the appropriate Secretary or Agency head, the Commission may also utilize the facilities or services of any Federal agency and shall take every feasible step to avoid duplication of research and to carry out the purposes of this chapter.
