25 C.F.R. § 900.74 — How may an Indian tribe or tribal organization propose a lease to be compensated for the use of facilities?
Citation: us:cfr:25:900.74
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior, And Indian Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-900.74
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 900 (Contracts Under The Indian Self-Determination And Education Assistance Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

There are three options available:
(a) The lease may be based on fair market rental.
(b) The lease may be based on a combination of fair market rental and paragraphs (a) through (h) of § 900.70, provided that no element of expense is duplicated in fair market rental.
(c) The lease may be based on paragraphs (a) through (h) of § 900.70 only.
