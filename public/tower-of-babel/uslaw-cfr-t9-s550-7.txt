9 C.F.R. § 550.7 — Reports by consignees of allegedly adulterated or misbranded products; sale or transportation as violations.
Citation: us:cfr:9:550.7
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-550.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 550 (Records Required To Be Kept). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirements in 9 CFR 320.7 for reports by consignees of allegedly adulterated or misbranded products apply with respect to fish and fish products as they do with respect to products of other species amenable to the Act.
