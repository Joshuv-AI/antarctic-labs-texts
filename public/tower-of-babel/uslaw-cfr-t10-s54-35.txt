10 C.F.R. § 54.35 — Requirements during term of renewed license.
Citation: us:cfr:10:54.35
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-54.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 54 (Requirements For Renewal Of Operating Licenses For Nuclear Power Plants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

During the term of a renewed license, licensees shall be subject to and shall continue to comply with all Commission regulations contained in 10 CFR parts 2, 19, 20, 21, 26, 30, 40, 50, 51, 52, 54, 55, 70, 72, 73, and 100, and the appendices to these parts that are applicable to holders of operating licenses or combined licenses, respectively.
