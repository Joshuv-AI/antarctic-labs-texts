29 C.F.R. § 4905.6 — Penalty.
Citation: us:cfr:29:4905.6
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4905.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4905 (Appearances In Certain Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A PBGC employee who testifies or produces documents or other material in violation of a provision of this part of the regulations shall be subject to disciplinary action.
