7 C.F.R. § 1784.29 — Grant servicing and accountability.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1784.29
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1784.29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1784 (Rural Alaskan Village Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Grants will be serviced in accordance with 7 CFR part 1782.
(b) RUS reserves the right to request and review project files from grantees at any time.
(c) If at any time an application is determined ineligible, 7 CFR part 11 will be followed.
