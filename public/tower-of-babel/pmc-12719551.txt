Report on the 10th Asia–Oceania Mass Spectrometry Conference (AOMSC2025)
Authors: Yasushi Ishihama
Published: 2025

As Chair of the 10th Asia–Oceania Mass Spectrometry Conference (AOMSC2025) organizing committee, I extend my greetings.

First and foremost, I would like to express my heartfelt gratitude to the approximately 700 participants who joined us at AOMSC2025. The conference was held from June 22 to 25, 2025, at the ANA InterContinental Ishigaki Resort, facing the beautiful beaches of Ishigaki Island, located just north of the Tropic of Cancer, under the slogan “From Bench to Beach.” Registrations came from 23 countries, primarily across the Asia–Oceania region. Altogether, 505 presentations were delivered and discussed, including 4 special lectures, 3 MSSJ award lectures, 15 keynote lectures, 34 invited lectures, 64 oral presentations, 364 poster presentations, 9 corporate posters, 10 luncheon seminar lectures and 2 tea-time session lectures. Furthermore, the Young Scientists Forum was held on June 21 and the morning of June 22, preceding the main conference. This journal features contributions from a diverse range of individuals, including plenary, keynote, and invited speakers, as well as young researchers such as the Outstanding Presentation Award winners. These articles surely reflect the passionate discussions that took place both indoors and outdoors during the conference, made all the more vibrant by the breathtaking beachfront location. Please enjoy this journal while feeling the refreshing breeze blowing in from the Asia–Oceania region.

The next AOMSC will be held in Taiwan in 2027. I sincerely look forward to welcoming you all back.
