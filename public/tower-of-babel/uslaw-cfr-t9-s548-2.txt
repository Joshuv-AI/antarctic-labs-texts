9 C.F.R. § 548.2 — Requirements concerning ingredients and other articles used in the preparation of fish products.
Citation: us:cfr:9:548.2
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-548.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 548 (Preparation Of Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All ingredients and other articles used in the preparation of any fish product must be clean, sound, healthful, wholesome, and otherwise such as will not result in the product's being adulterated.
