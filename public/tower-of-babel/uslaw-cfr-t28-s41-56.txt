28 C.F.R. § 41.56 — General requirement concerning program accessibility.
Citation: us:cfr:28:41.56
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-41.56
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 41 (Implementation Of Executive Order 12250, Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap In Federally Assisted Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No qualified handicapped person shall, because a recipient's facilities are inaccessible to or unusable by handicapped persons, be denied the benefits of, be excluded from participation in, or otherwise be subjected to discrimination under any program or activity that receives or benefits from federal financial assistance.
