C 1 C^{1} -Diffeomorphism Class of some Circle Maps with a Flat Interval
Bertuel TANGUE NDAWA, Carlos OGOUYANDJOU

Abstract




Abstract

We study a certain class circle maps which are constant on one interval (called flat piece), and such that the degrees of the singularities at the boundary of the flat piece are different.
In this paper, we show that if the topological conjugacy between two maps of my class is a bi-Lipschitz homeomorphism, then it is a
C^{1}
diffeomorphism; that is, the bi-Lipschitz homeomorphism class and
C^{1}
diffeomorphism class of a map in our class are equivalent.

Key words
: Circle map, Flat interval (piece), Critical exponent, Geometry, Renormalization, Bi-Lipschitz,
C^{1}
-Diffeomorphism.

Introduction

A dynamical system (or a dynamic) is a triplet
(E,S_{G},A)
where
S_{G}
is a semi-group, and
A:S_{G}\times E\longrightarrow E
is an action of the semi-group
S_{G}
on the set

defines an action of the set
{\mathbb{N}}
of non negative integer number on
E
. It is known that, a approximate solution of the stability of the solar system can be given by a circle map (dynamic)
f:S^{1}\longleftarrow S^{1}
with a flat piece. Also, some of these maps appear naturally in the study of Cherry flows on the two dimensions torus (see
[
11

[

), non-invertible (non-injective) circle continuous maps (see
[

), and of the dependence of the rotation interval on the parameter value for one-parameter families of circle continuous maps (see
[
17

). In this work, we are interested in a certain class of weakly order preserving
C^{3}
circle map with a flat piece.
We write
S^{1}=\mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z}
for the circle, and
\pi:\mathbb{R}\longrightarrow S^{1}
is the natural projection. As a consequence, each circle map
f
in our class lifts as a unique (up to integer translation) real continuous map
F
. The rotation number
\rho(f)
of
f
is

\rho(f):=\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\dfrac{F^{n}(x)-x}{n}(mod\>1).

This concept was first introduced by J. H. Poincaré, see
[
16
]
.
The dynamic defined by a such map

is more interesting when

[
5

q_{0}=1,\;q_{1}=a_{0},\mbox{ and }q_{n+1}=a_{n}q_{n}+q_{n-1}\mbox{ when }n\geq 2

with

\rho(f)=[a_{0}a_{1}\cdots]:=\dfrac{1}{a_{0}+\dfrac{1}{a_{1}+\dfrac{1}{\ddots}}},\;a_{i}\in\mathbb{N}^{*},\;i\in\mathbb{N}.

The rotation number
\rho(f)
is said to be Fibonacci or golden mean if

for every

. In this work, we are interested in Fibonacci circle maps; which are circle maps with Fibonacci rotation number. In addition to this assumption, we also assume that the critical exponents (the degrees of the singularities at the boundary of the flat interval) are different, and that the schwarzian derivative

S(f)=\dfrac{f^{{}^{\prime\prime\prime}}}{f^{\prime}}-\dfrac{3}{2}\left(\dfrac{f^{{}^{\prime\prime}}}{f^{\prime}}\right)^{2}
