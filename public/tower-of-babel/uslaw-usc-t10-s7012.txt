10 U.S.C. § 7012 — Department of the Army: seal
Citation: us:usc:10:7012
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7012
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization); Chapter 703 (Department Of The Army). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Army shall have a seal for the Department of the Army. The design of the seal must be approved by the President. Judicial notice shall be taken of the seal.
