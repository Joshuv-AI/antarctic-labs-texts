18 C.F.R. § 50.10 — Interventions.
Citation: us:cfr:18:50.10
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-50.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 50 (Applications For Permits To Site Interstate Electric Transmission Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notices of applications, as provided by § 50.9, will fix the time within which any person desiring to participate in the proceeding may file a petition to intervene, and within which any interested regulatory agency, as provided by § 385.214 of this chapter, desiring to intervene may file its notice of intervention.
