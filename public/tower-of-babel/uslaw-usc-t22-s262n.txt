22 U.S.C. § 262n — Congressional findings and policies respecting agricultural and commodity production
Citation: us:usc:22:262n
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section262n
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Congress hereby finds the following:
(1)
The financing of certain programs and projects by multilateral development banks has been of great concern insofar as the programs and projects have been detrimental to the interests of American farmers and the agribusiness sector.
(2)
An increase in rural income in developing countries will generally result in an increase in exports of United States agricultural and food products.
