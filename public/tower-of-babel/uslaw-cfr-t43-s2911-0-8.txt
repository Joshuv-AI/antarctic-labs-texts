43 C.F.R. § 2911.0-8 — -8 Lands available for leasing.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2911.0-8
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2911.0-8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2910 (Leases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any contiguous unreserved and unappropriated public lands, surveyed or unsurveyed, not exceeding 2,560 acres in area, may be leased under the provisions of the Act, subject to valid existing rights under the public land laws.
