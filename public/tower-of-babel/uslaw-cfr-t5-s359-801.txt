5 C.F.R. § 359.801 — Agency authority.
Citation: us:cfr:5:359.801
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-359.801
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 359 (Removal From The Senior Executive Service; Guaranteed Placement In Other Personnel Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart sets the conditions under which an agency may furlough career appointees in the Senior Executive Service. The furlough of a noncareer, limited term, or limited emergency appointee is not subject to this subpart. The furlough of a reemployed annuitant holding a career appointment also is not subject to the subpart.
