29 C.F.R. § 1918.36 — Weather deck rails.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1918.36
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1918.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1918 (Safety And Health Regulations For Longshoring). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Removable weather deck rails shall be kept in place except when cargo operations require them to be removed, in which case they shall be replaced as soon as such cargo operations are completed.
