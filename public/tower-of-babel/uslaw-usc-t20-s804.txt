20 U.S.C. § 804 — Limitation on grants to any one State
Citation: us:usc:20:804
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section804
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 23 (Training And Fellowship Programs For Community Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not more than 10 per centum of the total amount appropriated for the purposes of this chapter may be used for making grants to any one State.
