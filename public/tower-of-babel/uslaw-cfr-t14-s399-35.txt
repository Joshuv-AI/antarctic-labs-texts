14 C.F.R. § 399.35 — Special tariff permission.
Citation: us:cfr:14:399.35
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-399.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 399 (Statements Of General Policy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary of Transportation may approve, under such terms as the Secretary may require, a carrier's application for Special Tariff Permission to file a tariff for foreign air transportation required under part 293 of this chapter on less than the notice required by 49 U.S.C. 41504(b).
