26 C.F.R. § 18.0 — Effective date of temporary regulations under the Subchapter S Revision Act of 1982.
Citation: us:cfr:26:18.0
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-18.0
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 18 (Temporary Income Tax Regulations Under The Subchapter S Revision Act Of 1982). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The temporary regulations provided under § 18.1377-1, 18.1379-1, and 18.1379-2 are effective with respect to taxable years beginning after 1982, and the temporary regulations provided under § 18.1378-1 are effective with respect to elections made after October 19, 1982.
