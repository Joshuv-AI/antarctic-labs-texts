The measured value of Δ m s = [ 144 201.9 ± 44.2 ( stat . ) ± 29.9 ( syst . ) ± 15.0 ( PDG ) ] \Delta m_{s}=[144\,201.9\pm 44.2({\rm stat.})\pm 29.9({\rm syst.})\pm 15.0({\rm PDG})] keV/ c 2 c^{2} is about seven times more precise than the current Particle Data Group average, where the last uncertainty is from the Particle Data Group average of the D ∗ ⁣ + − D + D^{*+}-D^{+} mass difference.
