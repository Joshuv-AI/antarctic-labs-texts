46 U.S.C. § 31324 — Retention and examination of mortgages of vessels covered by preferred mortgages
Citation: us:usc:46:31324
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section31324
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 313 (Commercial Instruments And Maritime Liens); Subchapter II (Commercial Instruments). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
On request, the owner, master, or individual in charge of a vessel covered by a preferred mortgage shall permit a person to examine the mortgage if the person has business with the vessel that may give rise to a maritime lien or the sale, conveyance, mortgage, or assignment of a mortgage of the vessel.
(b)
A mortgagor of a preferred mortgage covering a self-propelled vessel shall use diligence in keeping a certified copy of the mortgage on the vessel.
