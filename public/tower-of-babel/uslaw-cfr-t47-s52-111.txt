47 C.F.R. § 52.111 — Toll free number assignment.
Citation: us:cfr:47:52.111
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-52.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 52 (Numbering). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Toll free telephone numbers must be made available to Responsible Organizations and subscribers on an equitable basis. The Commission will assign toll free numbers by competitive bidding, on a first-come, first-served basis, by an alternative assignment methodology, or by a combination of the foregoing options.
