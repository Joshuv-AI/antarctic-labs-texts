Fixed points theorems for b b -enriched multivalued nonexpansive mappings and *- b b -enriched nonexpansive mappings
Ioan Trifoi

Abstract
The main purpose of this paper is to extend some fixed point results for single valued b b -enriched nonexpansive mappings to the case of multivalued mappings. To this end, we introduce *- b b -enriched nonexpansive mappings, as a generalization of *-nonexpansive mappings [ 10 ] for which we establish an existence theorem in Hilbert space. We proved weak and strong convergence results of Krasnoselskii iteration process for b b -enriched multivalued nonexpasive mappings and *- b b -enriched nonexpansive mappings.
