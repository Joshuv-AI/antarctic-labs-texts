12 C.F.R. § 251.5 — No evasion.
Citation: us:cfr:12:251.5
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-251.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 251 (Concentration Limit (Regulation Xx)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A financial company may not organize or operate its business or structure any acquisition of or merger or consolidation with another company in such a manner that results in evasion of the concentration limit established by section 14 of the Bank Holding Company Act or this part.
