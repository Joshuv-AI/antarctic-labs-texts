30 C.F.R. § 71.100 — Respirable dust standard.
Citation: us:cfr:30:71.100
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-71.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 71 (Mandatory Health Standards—Surface Coal Mines And Surface Work Areas Of Underground Coal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each operator shall continuously maintain the average concentration of respirable dust in the mine atmosphere during each shift to which each miner in the active workings of each mine is exposed, as measured with an approved sampling device and expressed in terms of an equivalent concentration, at or below:
(a) 2.0 milligrams of respirable dust per cubic meter of air (mg/m 3).
(b) 1.5 mg/m 3 as of August 1, 2016.
