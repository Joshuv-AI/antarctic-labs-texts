31 C.F.R. § 235.5 — Reclamation amounts.
Citation: us:cfr:31:235.5
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-235.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 235 (Issuance Of Settlement Checks For Forged Checks Drawn On Designated Depositaries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Amounts received by way of reclamation on forged checks shall be deposited to the credit of the Check Forgery Insurance Fund or to the appropriate foreign currency fund or other account charged for the settlement payment.
