32 C.F.R. § 720.24 — Interviews and depositions in connection with civil litigation in matters pertaining to official duties.
Citation: us:cfr:32:720.24
Creator: Department Of The Navy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-720.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 720 (Delivery Of Personnel; Service Of Process And Subpoenas; Production Of Official Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Requests to interview, depose, or call as witnesses, current or former members or civilian employees of the Department of the Navy, regarding information obtained in the course of their official duties, including expert testimony related thereto, shall be processed in accordance with 32 CFR part 725.
