14 C.F.R. § 187.7 — Copies; seal.
Citation: us:cfr:14:187.7
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-187.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 187 (Fees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The fees for furnishing photostatic or similar copies of documents and for affixation of the seal for a certification or validation are the same as those provided in subpart H of 49 CFR part 7.
