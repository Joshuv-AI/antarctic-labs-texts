38 C.F.R. § 17.245 — Covered facilities.
Citation: us:cfr:38:17.245
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-17.245
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 17 (Medical). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A covered facility is any of the following:
(a) A VA health care facility;
(b) A health care facility operated by an Indian tribe or tribal organization, as those terms are defined in 25 U.S.C. 5304 and at 25 CFR 273.106;
(c) A health care facility operated by the Indian Health Service;
(d) A federally-qualified health center as defined in 42 U.S.C. 1396d(l)(2)(B);
(e) A health care facility operated by the Department of Defense; or
(f) Other health care facilities deemed appropriate by VA.
