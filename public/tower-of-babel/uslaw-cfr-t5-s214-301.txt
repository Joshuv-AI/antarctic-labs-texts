5 C.F.R. § 214.301 — Exclusions.
Citation: us:cfr:5:214.301
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-214.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 214 (Senior Executive Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If not excluded from the Senior Executive Service by section 3132(a) (1) or (2) of title 5, United States Code, an agency, or unit thereof, may be excluded only under the provisions of section 3132 (c) through (f) of title 5.
