43 U.S.C. § 2502 — Adjudications as to suspended entries; approval
Citation: us:usc:43:2502
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section2502
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 43 (Suspended Entries And Claims; Patents). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Every such adjudication shall be approved by the Secretary of the Interior and shall operate only to divest the United States of the title to the land embraced thereby, without prejudice to the rights of conflicting claimants.
