43 C.F.R. § 3175.41 — Flange-tapped orifice plates.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3175.41
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3175.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3170 (Onshore Oil And Gas Production). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Flange-tapped orifice plates that are constructed, installed, operated, and maintained in accordance with the standards in § 3175.80 are approved for use.
