2 U.S.C. § 1438 — Severability
Citation: us:usc:2:1438
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1438
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 24 (Congressional Accountability); Subchapter V (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this chapter or the application of such provision to any person or circumstance is held to be invalid, the remainder of this chapter and the application of the provisions of the remainder to any person or circumstance shall not be affected thereby.
