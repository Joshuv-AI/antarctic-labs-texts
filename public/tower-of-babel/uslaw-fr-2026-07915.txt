DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Combined Notice of Filings

    Take notice that the Commission has received the following Natural 
Gas Pipeline Rate and Refund Report filings and Accounting Request 
filings:

Filings Instituting Proceedings

    Docket Numbers: AC26-55-000.
    Applicants: Eastern Gas Transmission and Storage, Inc.
    Description: Eastern Gas Transmission and Storage, Inc. submits

projects at Boom, Bridgeport, and Sweeney compressor stations.
    Filed Date: 4/16/26.
    Accession Number: 20260416-5077.
    Comment Date: 5 p.m. ET 5/7/26.

    Docket Numbers: RP26-764-000.
    Applicants: Gillis Hub Pipeline, LLC.
    Description: Sec.  4(d) Rate Filing: Filing of Negotiated Rate, 
Conforming IW Agreement 4.17.2026 to be effective 4/20/2026.

    Accession Number: 20260417-5151.
    Comment Date: 5 p.m. ET 4/29/26.

    Applicants: Centra Pipelines Minnesota Inc.
    Description: Sec.  4(d) Rate Filing: Updated Index of Shippers

    Filed Date: 4/17/26.
    Accession Number: 20260417-5153.
    Comment Date: 5 p.m. ET 4/29/26.

    Any person desiring to intervene, to protest, or to answer a 
complaint in any of the above proceedings must file in accordance with 
Rules 211, 214, or 206 of the Commission's Regulations (18 CFR 385.211, 
385.214, or 385.206) on or before 5:00 p.m. Eastern time on the 
specified comment date. Protests may be considered, but intervention is 
necessary to become a party to the proceeding.
