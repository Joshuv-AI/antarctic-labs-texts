5 C.F.R. § 352.901 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:5:352.901
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-352.901
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 352 (Reemployment Rights). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart implements section 1203 of the Panama Canal Act of 1979, which provides for the detail or transfer of Federal employees to the Panama Canal Commission with reemployment rights in the former agency.
