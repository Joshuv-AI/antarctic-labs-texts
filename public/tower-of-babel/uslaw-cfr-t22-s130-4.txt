22 C.F.R. § 130.4 — Defense articles and defense services.
Citation: us:cfr:22:130.4
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-130.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 130 (Political Contributions, Fees And Commissions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Defense articles and defense services have the meaning given those terms in paragraphs (3), (4) and (7) of section 47 of the Arms Export Control Act (22 U.S.C. 2794 (3), (4), and (7)). When used with reference to commercial sales, the definitions in §§ 120.31 and 120.32 of this subchapter apply.
