21 C.F.R. § 316.40 — Treatment use of a designated orphan drug.
Citation: us:cfr:21:316.40
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-316.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 316 (Orphan Drugs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Prospective investigators seeking to obtain treatment use of designated orphan drugs may do so as provided in subpart I of this chapter.
