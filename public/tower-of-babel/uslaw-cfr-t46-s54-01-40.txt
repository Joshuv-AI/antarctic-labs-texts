46 C.F.R. § 54.01-40 — -40 External pressure (modifies UG-28).
Citation: us:cfr:46:54.01-40
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-54.01-40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 54 (Pressure Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The exemption from external pressure consideration provided by the note under UG-28(f) does not apply.
(b) Vessels which may at times be subjected to partial vacuum due to nature of the contents, temperature, unloading operations, or other facet of employment must either have vacuum breaker protection or be designed for not less than one-half atmosphere of external pressure.
