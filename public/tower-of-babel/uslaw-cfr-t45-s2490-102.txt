45 C.F.R. § 2490.102 — Application.
Citation: us:cfr:45:2490.102
Creator: James Madison Memorial Fellowship Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2490.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2490 (Enforcement Of Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap In Programs Or Activities Conducted By The James Madison Memorial Fellowship Foundation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part (§§ 2490.101-2490.170) applies to all programs or activities conducted by the agency, except for programs or activities conducted outside the United States that do not involve individuals with handicaps in the United States.
