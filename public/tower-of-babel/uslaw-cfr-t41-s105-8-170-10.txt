41 C.F.R. § 105-8.170-10 — -8.170-10 Acceptance of appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-8.170-10
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-8.170-10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-8 (8—Enforcement Of Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap In Programs Or Activities Conducted By General Services Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Special Counsel shall accept and process any timely appeal. A party may appeal to the Deputy Administrator from a decision of the Special Counsel that an appeal is untimely. This appeal shall be filed within 15 days of receipt of the decision from the Special Counsel.
