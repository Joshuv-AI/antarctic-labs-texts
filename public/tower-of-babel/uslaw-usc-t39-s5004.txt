39 U.S.C. § 5004 — Discontinuance of service on post roads
Citation: us:usc:39:5004
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title39-section5004
Context: United States Code, Title 39 (Postal Service); Part V (Transportation Of Mail); Chapter 50 (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Postal Service may discontinue service on a post road or part thereof when, in its opinion, the public interest so requires.
