47 C.F.R. § 80.881 — Equipment requirements for ship stations.
Citation: us:cfr:47:80.881
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-80.881
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 80 (Stations In The Maritime Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Vessels subject to subpart R of this part must be equipped as follows:
(a) A category 1, 406.0-406.1 MHz EPIRB meeting the requirements of § 80.1061;
(b) A NAVTEX receiver meeting the requirements of § 80.1101(c)(1);
(c) A Search and Rescue Transponder meeting the requirements of § 80.1101(c)(6); and
(d) A two-way VHF radiotelephone meeting the requirements of § 80.1101(c)(7).
