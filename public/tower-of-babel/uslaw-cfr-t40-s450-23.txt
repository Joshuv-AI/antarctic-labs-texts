40 C.F.R. § 450.23 — Effluent limitations reflecting the best conventional pollutant control technology (BCT).
Citation: us:cfr:40:450.23
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-450.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 450 (Construction And Development Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in 40 CFR 125.30 through 125.32, any point source subject to this subpart must achieve, at a minimum, the following effluent limitations representing the degree of effluent reduction attainable by application of the best conventional pollutant control technology (BCT). The effluent limitations are described at § 450.21.
