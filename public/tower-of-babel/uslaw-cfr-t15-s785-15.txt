15 C.F.R. § 785.15 — Post-hearing submissions.
Citation: us:cfr:15:785.15
Creator: Bureau Of Industry And Security, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-785.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 785 (Enforcement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All parties shall have the opportunity to file post-hearing submissions that may include findings of fact and conclusions of law, supporting evidence and legal arguments, exceptions to the ALJ's rulings or to the admissibility of evidence, and orders and settlements.
