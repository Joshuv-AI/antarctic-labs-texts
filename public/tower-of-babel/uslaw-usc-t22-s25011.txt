22 U.S.C. § 2501–1 — Independent agency status of Peace Corps
Citation: us:usc:22:2501–1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2501–1
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 34 (The Peace Corps). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Effective on December 29, 1981, the Peace Corps shall be an independent agency within the executive branch and shall not be an agency within the ACTION Agency, the successor to the ACTION Agency, or any other department or agency of the United States.
