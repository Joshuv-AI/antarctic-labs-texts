21 C.F.R. § 181.33 — Sodium nitrate and potassium nitrate.
Citation: us:cfr:21:181.33
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-181.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 181 (Prior-Sanctioned Food Ingredients). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Sodium nitrate and potassium nitrate are subject to prior sanctions issued by the U.S. Department of Agriculture for use as sources of nitrite, with or without sodium or potassium nitrite, in the production of cured red meat products and cured poultry products.
