30 C.F.R. § 250.827 — Surface-controlled SSSVs—subsea trees.
Citation: us:cfr:30:250.827
Creator: Bureau Of Safety And Environmental Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-250.827
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 250 (Oil And Gas And Sulphur Operations In The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must equip all tubing installations open to a hydrocarbon-bearing zone that is capable of natural flow with a surface-controlled SSSV, except as specified in §§ 250.829 and 250.830. The surface controls must be located on the host facility.
