32 U.S.C. § 905 — Funding assistance
Citation: us:usc:32:905
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title32-section905
Context: United States Code, Title 32 (National Guard); Chapter 9 (Homeland Defense Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the case of any homeland defense activity for which the Secretary of Defense determines under section 902 of this title that participation of units or members of the National Guard of a State is necessary and appropriate, the Secretary may provide funds to that State in an amount that the Secretary determines is appropriate for the following costs of the participation in that activity from funds available to the Department for related purposes:
(1)
The pay, allowances, clothing, subsistence, gratuities, travel, and related expenses of personnel of the National Guard of that State.
(2)
The operation and maintenance of the equipment and facilities of the National Guard of that State.
(3)
The procurement of services and equipment, and the leasing of equipment, for the National Guard of that State.
