34 C.F.R. § 396.30 — How does the Secretary evaluate an application?
Citation: us:cfr:34:396.30
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-396.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 396 (Training Of Interpreters For Individuals Who Are Deaf Or Hard Of Hearing And Individuals Who Are Deaf-Blind). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Secretary evaluates applications under the procedures in 34 CFR part 75.
(b) The Secretary evaluates each application using selection criteria in § 396.31.
(c) In addition to the selection criteria described in paragraph (b) of this section, the Secretary evaluates each application using—
(1) Selection criteria in 34 CFR 75.210;
(2) Selection criteria established under 34 CFR 75.209; or
(3) A combination of selection criteria established under 34 CFR 75.209 and selection criteria in 34 CFR 75.210.
