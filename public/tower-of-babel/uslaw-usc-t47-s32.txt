47 U.S.C. § 32 — Application
Citation: us:usc:47:32
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title47-section32
Context: United States Code, Title 47 (Telecommunications); Chapter 2 (Submarine Cables). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of this chapter shall be held to apply only to cables to which the convention for the time being applies.
