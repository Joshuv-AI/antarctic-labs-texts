12 U.S.C. § 5402 — Definition
Citation: us:usc:12:5402
Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 53 (Wall Street Reform And Consumer Protection); Subchapter III (Transfer Of Powers To The Comptroller Of The Currency, The Corporation, And The Board Of Governors). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this title,1 the term “transferred employee” means, as the context requires, an employee transferred to the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency or the Corporation under section 5432 of this title.
