7 C.F.R. § 1717.613 — RUS approval of data processing and system control equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1717.613
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1717.613
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1717 (Post-Loan Policies And Procedures Common To Insured And Guaranteed Electric Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a borrower's mortgage or loan contract requires the borrower to obtain approval from RUS before purchasing data processing equipment or system control equipment, such approval is hereby granted if the equipment will not be financed by RUS.
