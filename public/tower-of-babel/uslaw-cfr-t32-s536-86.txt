32 C.F.R. § 536.86 — Claims not payable under the Federal Tort Claims Act.
Citation: us:cfr:32:536.86
Creator: Department Of The Army
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-536.86
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 536 (Claims Against The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A claim is not payable if it is identified as an exclusion in DA Pam 27-162, paragraphs 2-36 through 2-43.
