47 C.F.R. § 64.1514 — Generation of signalling tones.
Citation: us:cfr:47:64.1514
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-64.1514
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 64 (Miscellaneous Rules Relating To Common Carriers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No common carrier shall assign a telephone number for any pay-per-call service that employs broadcast advertising which generates the audible tones necessary to complete a call to a pay-per-call service.
