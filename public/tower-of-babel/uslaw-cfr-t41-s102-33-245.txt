41 C.F.R. § 102-33.245 — -33.245 Timeline for FAIRS reports.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-33.245
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-33.245
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-33 (33—Management Of Government Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Report any changes in your Federal aircraft inventory within 14 calendar days.
(b) Report cost and utilization data to FAIRS at the end of each fiscal quarter. You may submit data more frequently. Additionally, a quarter's data may be reported in the following quarter if necessary.
