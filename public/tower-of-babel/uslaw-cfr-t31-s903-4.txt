31 C.F.R. § 903.4 — Exception to termination.
Citation: us:cfr:31:903.4
Creator: Federal Claims Collection Standards (Department Of The Treasury—Department Of Justice)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-903.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 903 (Standards For Suspending Or Terminating Collection Activity). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a significant enforcement policy is involved, or recovery of a judgment is a prerequisite to the imposition of administrative sanctions, agencies may refer debts for litigation even though termination of collection activity may otherwise be appropriate.
