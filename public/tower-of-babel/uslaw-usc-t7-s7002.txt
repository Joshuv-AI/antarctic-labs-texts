7 U.S.C. § 7002 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:7:7002
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section7002
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 98 (Department Of Agriculture Reorganization); Subchapter VIII (National Appeals Division). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary to carry out the activities of the Division.
