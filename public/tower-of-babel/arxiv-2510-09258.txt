Introduction

In this paper, we address the non-existence of global solutions for the Grushin heat equation with mixed nonlinear memory and reaction terms:

(1.1)

\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\displaystyle{u_{t}-\Delta_{\mathcal{G}}u=k_{1}\int_{0}^{t}(t-s)^{-\gamma}\lvert u\rvert^{p_{1}-1}u(s)\,\mathrm{d}s}+k_{2}|u|^{p_{2}-1}u,&(z,t)\in{\mathbb{R}}^{N+k}\times(0,\infty),\\
\displaystyle{u(z,0)=u_{0}(z),\qquad\qquad}&\displaystyle{z\in{\mathbb{R}}^{N+k},}\end{array}\right.

u_{0}\in L^{1}_{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathbb{R}^{N+k})
,
\gamma\in[0,1)
,
k_{1},k_{2}\geq 0
, and
p_{1},p_{2}>1
. The Grushin operator is defined by

\Delta_{\mathcal{G}}=\Delta_{x}+|x|^{2}\Delta_{y},

where
\Delta_{x},\Delta_{y}
denote the classical Laplacian in the variables
x\in\mathbb{R}^{N}
and

, respectively.

In fact, nonlocal nonlinearities play an important role in the analysis of the evolution equations, for example, dissociating the critical Fujita exponent from the one given by scaling computations, which was for the first time observed by Cazenave, Dickstein and Weissler

When

Non-existence results for heat equations with combined pure and memory reaction have been studied by Souplet
[
42
]
, in the case of the Laplace operator, where the author considered the equation

(2)

2.
Preliminaries

In this section, we gather the definitions and results used in our proofs. They are related to tools from the Fractional Calculus and integrodifferential inequalities.

Definition 2.1
.

A function
\mathcal{A}:[a,b]\rightarrow\mathbb{R}
,
-\infty<a<b<\infty

\psi\in L^{1}(a,b)
such that

\mathcal{A}(t)=\mathcal{A}(a)+\int_{a}^{t}\psi(s)\,ds.

AC[a,b]
denotes the space of these functions. Moreover,

AC^{2}[a,b]:=\left\{\varphi:[a,b]\rightarrow\mathbb{R};\,\varphi^{\prime}\in AC[a,b]\right\}.

The following definitions and properties of fractional operators can be found in
[

Definition 2.2

(Riemann-Liouville fractional integral)
.

Let

,

. The Riemann-Liouville left-and right-sided fractional integrals are defined by

(2.1)

I^{\alpha}_{c|t}f(t):=\frac{1}{\Gamma(\alpha)}\int_{c}^{t}(t-s)^{-(1-\alpha)}f(s)\,ds,\quad t>c,\;\alpha\in(0,1),

and

(2.2)

I^{\alpha}_{t|d}f(t):=\frac{1}{\Gamma(\alpha)}\int_{t}^{d}(s-t)^{-(1-\alpha)}f(s)\,ds,\quad t<d,\;\alpha\in(0,1)

where

Definition 2.3

(Riemann-Liouville fractional derivative)
.

Let

,

(2.4)

D^{\alpha}_{t|d}f(t):=-\frac{d}{\mathrm{dt}}I^{1-\alpha}_{t|d}f(t)=-\frac{1}{\Gamma(1-\alpha)}\frac{d}{\mathrm{dt}}\int_{t}^{d}(s-t)^{-\alpha}f(s)\,ds,\quad t<d,\quad\alpha\in(0,1),

D^{\alpha}_{t|d}f(t):=\frac{1}{\Gamma(2-\alpha)}\frac{d^{2}}{\mathrm{dt}^{2}}\int_{t}^{d}(s-t)^{-(\alpha-1)}f(s)\,ds,\quad t<d,\quad\alpha\in(1,2).

Lemma 2.6
.

For
\varepsilon>0
,
A>0
,
c>0
, let

\Theta(z)=c\,e^{-\varepsilon\sqrt{A+|x|^{4}+|y|^{2}}},\qquad z=(x,y)\in\mathbb{R}^{N+k}.

Then

\Delta_{\mathcal{G}}\Theta(z)\geq-\varepsilon[2(N+2)+k]\Theta(z),\qquad\hbox{for all}\,\,z\in\mathbb{R}^{N+k}.

Proof.

Let
z=(x,y)\in\mathbb{R}^{N+k}
, and
\rho(z):=A+|x|^{4}+|y|^{2}
. We have

\partial_{z_{i}}\Theta(z)=-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\rho^{-\frac{1}{2}}\partial_{z_{i}}(\rho)\Theta(z)

and then

\partial_{z_{i}}^{2}\Theta(z)=\frac{\varepsilon}{4}\rho^{-\frac{3}{2}}|\partial_{z_{i}}(\rho)|^{2}\Theta(z)-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\rho^{-\frac{1}{2}}\partial_{z_{i}}^{2}(\rho)\Theta(z)+\frac{\varepsilon^{2}}{4}\rho^{-1}|\partial_{z_{i}}(\rho)|^{2}\Theta(z).

Hence

\Delta_{x}\Theta(z)=\frac{1}{4}\left(\varepsilon\rho^{-\frac{3}{2}}+\varepsilon^{2}\rho^{-1}\right)|\nabla_{x}(\rho)|^{2}\Theta(z)-2\varepsilon(N+2)|x|^{2}\rho^{-\frac{1}{2}}\Theta(z),

and

\Delta_{y}\Theta(z)=\frac{1}{4}\left(\varepsilon\rho^{-\frac{3}{2}}+\varepsilon^{2}\rho^{-1}\right)|\nabla_{y}(\rho)|^{2}\Theta(z)-\varepsilon k\,\rho^{-\frac{1}{2}}\Theta(z).

As
\nabla_{x}(\rho)=4x|x|^{2}
,
\Delta_{x}(\rho)=4(N+2)|x|^{2}
,
\nabla_{y}(\rho)=2y
, and
\Delta_{y}(\rho)=2k
, we conclude that

(2.12)

\begin{split}\Delta_{\mathcal{G}}\Theta(z)&=\Delta_{x}\Theta(z)+|x|^{2}\Delta_{y}\Theta(z)\\
&=\frac{1}{4}\left(\varepsilon\rho^{-\frac{3}{2}}+\varepsilon^{2}\rho^{-1}\right)\left(|\nabla_{x}(\rho)|^{2}+|x|^{2}|\nabla_{y}(\rho)|^{2}\right)\Theta(z)-\varepsilon[2(N+2)+k]|x|^{2}\rho^{-\frac{1}{2}}\Theta(z)\\
&\geq-\varepsilon[2(N+2)+k]|x|^{2}\rho^{-\frac{1}{2}}\Theta(z).\end{split}

Finally, using the fact that

implies
|x|^{2}\rho^{-\frac{1}{2}}\leq 1
, we get

\Delta_{\mathcal{G}}\Theta(z)\geq-\varepsilon[2(N+2)+k]\Theta(z).

This completes the proof.
∎

3.
Non-existence of global solutions

In this section, we present and prove the non-existence results, which are categorized according to the cases
k_{1}=0,\ k_{2}=1
;
k_{1}=1,\ k_{2}=0
; and
k_{1}=k_{2}=1
. These results complete the characterization of the existence of global positive solutions (cf.
[
22
]
) in each respective case. Additional non-existence results are established at the end of the section. Our approach is based on the notion of weak solutions. Throughout this section write
z=(x,y)\in\mathbb{R}^{N+k}
.

Definition 3.1

(Weak solution)
.

Let
u_{0}\in L_{\mathrm{loc}}^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{N+k})
, and
T>0
. We say that

is a
weak solution of the problem (
1.1

and verifies the weak formulation

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N+k}}u(z,\tau)\varphi(z,\tau)\,\mathrm{dz}-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N+k}}u_{0}(z)\varphi(z,0)\,\mathrm{dz}

\displaystyle=k_{1}\Gamma(\alpha)\int_{0}^{T}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N+k}}I^{\alpha}_{0|t}(|u|^{p_{1}-1}u)\varphi(z,t)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}+k_{2}\int_{0}^{T}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N+k}}|u|^{p_{2}-1}u\varphi(z,t)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}

(3.1)

\displaystyle+\int_{0}^{T}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N+k}}u(z,t)\Delta_{\mathcal{G}}\varphi(z,t)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}+\int_{0}^{T}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N+k}}u(z,t)\varphi_{t}(z,t)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt},

for all compactly supported
\varphi\in C^{2,1}(\mathbb{R}^{N+k}\times[0,T])
such that

, where

\Delta_{\mathcal{G}}(wv)=v\Delta_{\mathcal{G}}(w)+\nabla_{x}(w)\cdotp\nabla_{x}(v)+|x|^{2}\nabla_{y}(w)\cdotp\nabla_{y}(v)+w\Delta_{\mathcal{G}}(v).

we get

\displaystyle\int_{\mathcal{B}}u(\tau,z)\varphi_{R}(z)\psi_{0}(\tau,z)\,\mathrm{dz}-\int_{\mathcal{B}}u_{0}(z)\varphi_{R}(z)\psi_{0}(z,0)\,\mathrm{dz}

\displaystyle=k_{1}\Gamma(\alpha)\int_{0}^{\tau}\int_{\mathcal{B}}I^{\alpha}_{0|t}(|u|^{p_{1}-1}u)\varphi_{R}(z)\psi_{0}(z,t)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}+k_{2}\int_{0}^{\tau}\int_{\mathcal{B}}|u|^{p_{2}-1}u\varphi_{R}(z)\psi_{0}(z,t)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}

\displaystyle+\int_{0}^{\tau}\int_{\mathcal{B}}u(z,t)\psi_{0}(z,t)\Delta_{\mathcal{G}}\left(\varphi_{R}(z)\right)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}+2\int_{0}^{\tau}\int_{\mathcal{B}}u(z,t)\nabla_{x}(\varphi_{R}(z))\cdotp\nabla_{x}\left(\psi_{0}(z,t)\right)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}

\displaystyle+2\int_{0}^{\tau}\int_{\mathcal{B}}u(z,t)|x|^{2}\nabla_{y}(\varphi_{R}(z))\cdotp\nabla_{y}\left(\psi_{0}(z,t)\right)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}+\int_{0}^{\tau}\int_{\mathcal{B}}u(z,t)\varphi_{R}(z)\Delta_{\mathcal{G}}\left(\psi_{0}(z,t)\right)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}

\displaystyle+\int_{0}^{\tau}\int_{\mathcal{B}}u(z,t)\varphi_{R}(z)\partial_{t}\psi_{0}(z,t)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}.

By denoting

\mathcal{C}_{1}=\{z=(x,y)\in\mathbb{R}^{N+k};\,\,R\leq|x|\leq 2R,\,|y|\leq 2R^{3}\},

\mathcal{C}_{2}=\{z=(x,y)\in\mathbb{R}^{N+k};\,\,|x|\leq 2R,\,R^{3}\leq|y|\leq 2R^{3}\},

and using

\Delta_{\mathcal{G}}\left(\varphi_{R}(z)\right)=\varphi_{2}(y)\Delta_{x}\left(\varphi_{1}(x)\right)+|x|^{2}\varphi_{1}(x)\Delta_{y}\left(\varphi_{2}(y)\right),

we get

\displaystyle\int_{\mathcal{B}}u(\tau,z)\varphi_{R}(z)\psi_{0}(\tau,z)\,\mathrm{dz}-\int_{\mathcal{B}}u_{0}(z)\varphi_{R}(z)\psi_{0}(z,0)\,\mathrm{dz}

\displaystyle=k_{1}\Gamma(\alpha)\int_{0}^{\tau}\int_{\mathcal{B}}I^{\alpha}_{0|t}(|u|^{p_{1}-1}u)\varphi_{R}(z)\psi_{0}(z,t)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}+k_{2}\int_{0}^{\tau}\int_{\mathcal{B}}|u|^{p_{2}-1}u\varphi_{R}(z)\psi_{0}(z,t)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}

\displaystyle+\int_{0}^{\tau}\int_{\mathcal{C}_{1}}u(z,t)\psi_{0}(z,t)\varphi_{2}(y)\Delta_{x}\left(\varphi_{1}(x)\right)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}+\int_{0}^{\tau}\int_{\mathcal{C}_{2}}u(z,t)\psi_{0}(z,t)|x|^{2}\varphi_{1}(x)\Delta_{y}\left(\varphi_{2}(y)\right)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}

\displaystyle+\int_{0}^{\tau}\int_{\mathcal{C}_{1}}u(z,t)\nabla_{x}(\varphi_{R}(z))\cdotp\nabla_{x}\left(\psi_{0}(z,t)\right)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}+\int_{0}^{\tau}\int_{\mathcal{C}_{2}}u(z,t)|x|^{2}\nabla_{y}(\varphi_{R}(z))\cdotp\nabla_{y}\left(\psi_{0}(z,t)\right)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}

(3.3)

\displaystyle+\int_{0}^{\tau}\int_{\mathcal{B}}u(z,t)\varphi_{R}(z)\Delta_{\mathcal{G}}\left(\psi_{0}(z,t)\right)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}+\int_{0}^{\tau}\int_{\mathcal{B}}u(z,t)\varphi_{R}(z)\partial_{t}\psi_{0}(z,t)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}.

On the other hand,

I_{1}:=\left|\int_{0}^{\tau}\int_{\mathcal{C}_{1}}u(z,t)\psi_{0}(z,t)\varphi_{2}(y)\Delta_{x}\left(\varphi_{1}(x)\right)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}\right|\leq\int_{0}^{\tau}\int_{\mathcal{C}_{1}}|u(z,t)||\psi_{0}(z,t)|\varphi_{2}(y)\left|\Delta_{x}\left(\varphi_{1}(x)\right)\right|\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}.

Note that, as
\Phi\leq 1
and
\Phi\in C^{\infty}
on
\mathcal{C}_{1}
, we can easily see that

\left|\Delta_{x}\varphi_{1}\right|\leq\,C\,{R^{-2}},\qquad\hbox{on}\,\,\mathcal{C}_{1},

and therefore

\displaystyle I_{1}

\displaystyle\leq C\,R^{-2}\int_{0}^{\tau}\int_{\mathcal{C}_{1}}|u(z,t)||\psi_{0}(z,t)|\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}

\displaystyle\leq C\,R^{-2}\tau\sup_{t\in[0,\tau]}\|u(t,\cdotp)\|_{L^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{N+k})}\sup_{t\in[0,\tau]}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N+k}}|\psi_{0}(t,\eta)|\,d\eta,

this implies, using (
3.2
), that

(3.4)

I_{1}\longrightarrow 0,\qquad\hbox{when}\,\,R\rightarrow+\infty.

Similarly, using the fact that

|x|^{2}\left|\Delta_{y}\varphi_{2}\right|\leq\,C\,{R^{-4}},\qquad\hbox{on}\,\,\mathcal{C}_{2},

we infer that

(3.5)

I_{2}:=\int_{0}^{\tau}\int_{\mathcal{C}_{2}}u(z,t)\psi_{0}(z,t)|x|^{2}\varphi_{1}(x)\Delta_{y}\left(\varphi_{2}(y)\right)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}\longrightarrow 0,\qquad\hbox{when}\,\,R\rightarrow+\infty.

In addition,

\displaystyle I_{3}:

\displaystyle=\int_{0}^{\tau}\int_{\mathcal{C}_{1}}u(z,t)\nabla_{x}(\varphi_{R}(z))\cdotp\nabla_{x}\left(\psi_{0}(z,t)\right)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}

\displaystyle\leq\int_{0}^{\tau}\int_{\mathcal{C}_{1}}|u(z,t)|\left|\nabla_{x}(\varphi_{R}(z))\right|\left|\nabla_{x}\left(\psi_{0}(z,t)\right)\right|\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}.

As
\Phi\leq 1
and
\Phi\in C^{\infty}
on
\mathcal{C}
, we can easily see that

\left|\nabla_{x}\varphi_{1}\right|\leq\,C\,{R^{-1}},\qquad\hbox{on}\,\,\mathcal{C}_{1},

and therefore

\displaystyle I_{3}

\displaystyle\leq C\,R^{-1}\int_{0}^{\tau}\int_{\mathcal{C}_{1}}|u(z,t)|\,|\nabla_{x}\psi_{0}(z,t)|\,dz\,dt

\displaystyle\leq C\,R^{-1}\tau\sup_{t\in[0,\tau]}\|u(t,\cdotp)\|_{L^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{N+k})}\sup_{t\in[0,\tau]}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N+k}}|\nabla_{x}\psi_{0}(z,t)|\,dz,

this implies, using (
3.2
), that

(3.6)

I_{3}\longrightarrow 0,\qquad\hbox{when}\,\,R\rightarrow+\infty.

Similarly, using the fact that

|x|^{2}\left|\nabla_{y}\varphi_{2}\right|\leq\,C\,{R^{-1}},\qquad\hbox{on}\,\,\mathcal{C}_{2},

we infer that

(3.7)

Finally, letting

in (
3.3
) and using (

), (

)-(
3.7
) together with Lebesgue’s dominated convergence theorem we conclude the result.
∎

3.1.
The Cauchy problem (
1.1
) without memory

Here, we will consider the case
k_{1}=0,\ k_{2}=1
,

(3.8)

\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\,\,\displaystyle{u_{t}-\Delta_{\mathcal{G}}u=|u|^{p-1}u,}&\displaystyle{(z,t)\in{\mathbb{R}}^{N+k}\times(0,\infty)},\\
\\
\displaystyle{u(z,0)=u_{0}(z),\qquad\qquad}&\displaystyle{z\in{\mathbb{R}}^{N+k},}\end{array}\right.

where
p>1
.

Theorem 3.2
.

Let
u_{0}\in L^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{N+k})
be such that
\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N+k}}u_{0}(z)\,\mathrm{dz}>0
. If

p\leq 1+\frac{2}{N+2k}:=p_{c},

then there is no global nonnegative weak solution to (
3.8
).

Proof.

The proof is by contradiction. Suppose that
u
is a global nonnegative weak
solution to (
3.8
), that is, (
3.1
) holds for all
T\gg 1
, with
k_{1}=0,\ k_{2}=1
. Let us choose

\varphi_{T}(z,t)=\left(\varphi_{1}(x)\right)^{\ell}\left(\varphi_{2}(y)\right)^{\ell}\left(\varphi_{3}(t)\right)^{\ell},\qquad z=(x,y)\in\mathbb{R}^{N+k},\,\,t\geq 0,

with

\varphi_{1}(x):=\Phi\left(\lvert x\rvert/T^{1/2}\right),\quad\varphi_{2}(y):=\Phi\left(\lvert y\rvert/T\right),\quad\varphi_{3}(t):=\eta\left(t/T\right),

where
\ell=2p/(p-1)
, and
\Phi,\eta\in\mathcal{C}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R})
are smooth non-increasing functions
satisfying

\mathbbm{1}_{(-\infty,1]}\leq\Phi\leq\mathbbm{1}_{(-\infty,2]}\quad\hbox{and}\quad\mathbbm{1}_{(-\infty,\frac{1}{2}]}\leq\eta\leq\mathbbm{1}_{(-\infty,1]}.

Then

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}u_{0}(x,y)\left(\varphi_{1}(x)\right)^{\ell}\left(\varphi_{2}(y)\right)^{\ell}\,dx\,dy+\int_{\Omega_{T}}|u(z,t)|^{p}\varphi(z,t)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}

\displaystyle=-\int_{\Omega_{T}}u(z,t)\varphi_{t}(z,t)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}-\int_{\Omega_{T}}u(z,t)\Delta_{\mathcal{G}}\varphi(z,t)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}

(3.9)

\displaystyle=:I_{1}+I_{2},

where

\Omega_{T}:=[0,T]\times\Omega\;\;\text{for}\;\;\Omega=\left\{z=(x,y)\in{\mathbb{R}}^{N+k}\hskip 5.69054pt;\hskip 5.69054pt|x|\leq 2T^{1/2},\,|y|\leq 2T\right\}.

In order to estimate the right-hand side of (
3.1
), we introduce the term

\varphi^{1/p}\varphi^{-1/p}
in
I_{1}
, and we use Young’s inequality to obtain

(3.10)

\displaystyle I_{1}\leqslant\frac{1}{4}\int_{\Omega_{T}}|u(z,t)|^{p}\varphi(z,t)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}+C\int_{\Omega_{T}}\varphi_{1}^{\ell}(x)\varphi_{2}^{\ell}(y)\varphi_{3}^{p^{\prime}}(t)\left|\partial_{t}\varphi_{3}(t)\right|^{p^{\prime}}\mathrm{d}z\,\mathrm{d}t.

To estimate
I_{2}
, notice that

(3.11)

\displaystyle\Delta_{\mathcal{G}}\varphi(z,t)

\displaystyle=\ell(\ell-1)\varphi^{\ell}_{2}(y)\varphi^{\ell}_{3}(t)\varphi^{\ell-2}_{1}(x)|\nabla_{x}\varphi_{1}(x)|^{2}+\ell\varphi^{\ell}_{2}(y)\varphi^{\ell}_{3}(t)\varphi^{\ell-1}_{1}(x)\Delta_{x}\varphi_{1}(x)

\displaystyle{}\quad+\,\ell(\ell-1)|x|^{2}\varphi^{\ell}_{1}(x)\varphi^{\ell}_{3}(t)\varphi^{\ell-2}_{2}(y)|\nabla_{y}\varphi_{2}(y)|^{2}+\ell|x|^{2}\varphi^{\ell}_{1}(x)\varphi^{\ell}_{3}(t)\varphi^{\ell-1}_{2}(y)\Delta_{y}\varphi_{2}(y).

Hence

(3.12)

\displaystyle I_{2}

\displaystyle\leqslant

\displaystyle\frac{1}{4}\int_{\Omega_{T}}|u(z,t)|^{p}\varphi(z,t)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}+C\int_{\Omega_{T}}\varphi_{2}^{\ell}(y)\varphi_{3}^{\ell}(t)\left|\nabla_{x}\varphi_{1}(x)\right|^{2p^{\prime}}\mathrm{d}z\,\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle+\,C\int_{\Omega_{T}}\varphi_{2}^{\ell}(y)\varphi_{3}^{\ell}(t)\varphi_{1}^{p^{\prime}}(x)\left|\Delta_{x}\varphi_{1}(x)\right|^{p^{\prime}}\mathrm{d}z\,\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle+C\int_{\Omega_{T}}|x|^{2p^{\prime}}\varphi_{1}^{\ell}(x)\varphi_{3}^{\ell}(t)\left|\nabla_{y}\varphi_{2}(y)\right|^{2p^{\prime}}\mathrm{d}z\,\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle+\,C\int_{\Omega_{T}}|x|^{2p^{\prime}}\varphi_{1}^{\ell}(x)\varphi_{3}^{\ell}(t)\varphi_{2}^{p^{\prime}}(y)\left|\Delta_{y}\varphi_{2}(y)\right|^{p^{\prime}}\mathrm{d}z\,\mathrm{d}t.

Combining (
3.10
), (
3.12
), and (
3.1
), we arrive at

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}u_{0}(x,y)\left(\varphi_{1}(x)\right)^{\ell}\left(\varphi_{2}(y)\right)^{\ell}\,dx\,dy+\frac{1}{2}\int_{\Omega_{T}}|u(z,t)|^{p}\varphi(z,t)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}

\displaystyle\leq C\int_{\Omega_{T}}\varphi_{1}^{\ell}(x)\varphi_{2}^{\ell}(y)\varphi_{3}^{p^{\prime}}(t)\left|\partial_{t}\varphi_{3}(t)\right|^{p^{\prime}}\mathrm{d}z\,\mathrm{d}t+C\int_{\Omega_{T}}\varphi_{2}^{\ell}(y)\varphi_{3}^{\ell}(t)\left|\nabla_{x}\varphi_{1}(x)\right|^{2p^{\prime}}\mathrm{d}z\,\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle+\,C\int_{\Omega_{T}}\varphi_{2}^{\ell}(y)\varphi_{3}^{\ell}(t)\varphi_{1}^{p^{\prime}}(x)\left|\Delta_{x}\varphi_{1}(x)\right|^{p^{\prime}}\mathrm{d}z\,\mathrm{d}t+C\int_{\Omega_{T}}|x|^{2p^{\prime}}\varphi_{1}^{\ell}(x)\varphi_{3}^{\ell}(t)\left|\nabla_{y}\varphi_{2}(y)\right|^{2p^{\prime}}\mathrm{d}z\,\mathrm{d}t

(3.13)

\displaystyle+\,C\int_{\Omega_{T}}|x|^{2p^{\prime}}\varphi_{1}^{\ell}(x)\varphi_{3}^{\ell}(t)\varphi_{2}^{p^{\prime}}(y)\left|\Delta_{y}\varphi_{2}(y)\right|^{p^{\prime}}\mathrm{d}z\,\mathrm{d}t.

Now, we introduce the scaled variables

\tau_{1}=T^{-1/2}x,\quad\tau_{2}=T^{-1}y,\quad\tau_{3}=T^{-1}t.

We get

(3.14)

\int_{\Omega}u_{0}(x,y)\left(\varphi_{1}(x)\right)^{\ell}\left(\varphi_{2}(y)\right)^{\ell}\,dx\,dy+\frac{1}{2}\int_{\Omega_{T}}|u(z,t)|^{p}\varphi(z,t)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}\leq C\,T^{-p^{\prime}+\frac{N}{2}+k+1},

which implies that

\int_{\Omega}u_{0}(x,y)\left(\varphi_{1}(x)\right)^{\ell}\left(\varphi_{2}(y)\right)^{\ell}\,dx\,dy\leq C\,T^{-\delta},

with

. Since

, we have to distinguish two cases:

\bullet

. Passing to the limit in (

) as

, and using Lebesgue dominated convergence theorem together with

, we get

contradiction.

\bullet
The critical case
p=p_{c}
. Using inequality
(
3.14
) with
T\rightarrow\infty
and taking into account the fact that
p=p_{c}
, we have

(3.16)

u\in L^{p}((0,\infty),L^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{N+k})).

On the other hand, by repeating the same calculation as above by using Hölder’s inequality instead of Young’s inequality, we arrive at

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}u_{0}(x,y)\left(\varphi_{1}(x)\right)^{\ell}\left(\varphi_{2}(y)\right)^{\ell}\,dx\,dy+\int_{\Omega_{T}}|u(z,t)|^{p}\varphi(z,t)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}

\displaystyle\leq\left(\int_{T/2}^{T}\int_{\Omega}|u(z,t)|^{p}\varphi(z,t)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}\right)^{1/p}\left(\int_{\Omega_{T}}\varphi_{1}^{\ell}(x)\varphi_{2}^{\ell}(y)\varphi_{3}^{p^{\prime}}(t)\left|\partial_{t}\varphi_{3}(t)\right|^{p^{\prime}}\mathrm{d}z\,\mathrm{d}t\right)^{1/p^{\prime}}

\displaystyle+\left(\int_{\Sigma_{T}}|u(z,t)|^{p}\varphi(z,t)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}\right)^{1/p}\left(\int_{\Omega_{T}}\varphi_{2}^{\ell}(y)\varphi_{3}^{\ell}(t)\left|\nabla_{x}\varphi_{1}(x)\right|^{2p^{\prime}}\mathrm{d}z\,\mathrm{d}t\right)^{1/p^{\prime}}

\displaystyle+\left(\int_{\Sigma_{T}}|u(z,t)|^{p}\varphi(z,t)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}\right)^{1/p}\left(\int_{\Omega_{T}}\varphi_{2}^{\ell}(y)\varphi_{3}^{\ell}(t)\varphi_{1}^{p^{\prime}}(x)\left|\Delta_{x}\varphi_{1}(x)\right|^{p^{\prime}}\mathrm{d}z\,\mathrm{d}t\right)^{1/p^{\prime}}

\displaystyle+\left(\int_{\Sigma_{T}}|u(z,t)|^{p}\varphi(z,t)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}\right)^{1/p}\left(\int_{\Omega_{T}}|x|^{2p^{\prime}}\varphi_{1}^{\ell}(x)\varphi_{3}^{\ell}(t)\left|\nabla_{y}\varphi_{2}(y)\right|^{2p^{\prime}}\mathrm{d}z\,\mathrm{d}t\right)^{1/p^{\prime}}

\displaystyle+\left(\int_{\Sigma_{T}}|u(z,t)|^{p}\varphi(z,t)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}\right)^{1/p}\left(\int_{\Omega_{T}}|x|^{2p^{\prime}}\varphi_{1}^{\ell}(x)\varphi_{3}^{\ell}(t)\varphi_{2}^{p^{\prime}}(y)\left|\Delta_{y}\varphi_{2}(y)\right|^{p^{\prime}}\mathrm{d}z\,\mathrm{d}t\right)^{1/p^{\prime}}

where

\Sigma_{T}:=[0,T]\times\Sigma\;\;\text{for}\;\;\Sigma=\left\{z=(x,y)\in{\mathbb{R}}^{N+k}\hskip 5.69054pt;\hskip 5.69054ptT^{1/2}\leq|x|\leq 2T^{1/2},\,T\leq|y|\leq 2T\right\}.

Using the same change of variable as above and taking into account the fact that

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}u_{0}(x,y)\left(\varphi_{1}(x)\right)^{\ell}\left(\varphi_{2}(y)\right)^{\ell}\,dx\,dy\leq

\displaystyle C\left(\int_{T/2}^{T}\int_{\Omega}|u(z,t)|^{p}\varphi(z,t)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}\right)^{1/p}

\displaystyle+C\left(\int_{\Sigma_{T}}|u(z,t)|^{p}\varphi(z,t)\,\mathrm{dz}\,\mathrm{dt}\right)^{1/p}.

Recall that

depend on

. Passing to the limit as

, and using (

∎
