23 C.F.R. § 450.202 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:23:450.202
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-450.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 450 (Planning Assistance And Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to States and any other organizations or entities (e.g., MPOs, RTPOs and public transportation operators) that are responsible for satisfying the requirements for transportation plans and programs throughout the State pursuant to 23 U.S.C. 135 and 49 U.S.C. 5304.
