30 U.S.C. § 224 — Payments for oil or gas taken prior to application for lease
Citation: us:usc:30:224
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section224
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 3A (Leases And Prospecting Permits); Subchapter IV (Oil And Gas). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Until the permittee shall apply for lease to the one quarter of the permit area heretofore provided for he shall pay to the United States 20 per centum of the gross value of all oil or gas secured by him from the lands embraced within his permit and sold or otherwise disposed of or held by him for sale or other disposition.
