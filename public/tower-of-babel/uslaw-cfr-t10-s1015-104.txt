10 C.F.R. § 1015.104 — Compromise, waiver, or disposition under other statutes not precluded.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1015.104
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1015.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1015 (Collection Of Claims Owed The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this part precludes DOE from disposing of any claim under statutes and implementing regulations other than subchapter II of chapter 37 of Title 31 of the United States Code (Claims of the United States Government) and the standards in this part. In such cases, the specifically applicable laws and regulations will generally take precedence over this part.
