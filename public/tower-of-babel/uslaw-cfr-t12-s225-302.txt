12 C.F.R. § 225.302 — Notification.
Citation: us:cfr:12:225.302
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-225.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 225 (Bank Holding Companies And Change In Bank Control (Regulation Y)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A banking organization must notify the appropriate Board-designated point of contact about a notification incident through email, telephone, or other similar methods that the Board may prescribe. The Board must receive this notification from the banking organization as soon as possible and no later than 36 hours after the banking organization determines that a notification incident has occurred.
