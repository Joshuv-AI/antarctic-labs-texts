30 C.F.R. § 887.15 — Grant administration requirements and procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:30:887.15
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-887.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 887 (Subsidence Insurance Program Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirements and procedures for grant administration set forth in part 885 of this chapter for reclamation grants to certified States and Indian tribes or in part 886 of this chapter for reclamation grants to uncertified States and Indian tribes must be used for subsidence insurance funds in grants.
