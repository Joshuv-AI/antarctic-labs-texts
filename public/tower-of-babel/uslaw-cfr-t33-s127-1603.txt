33 C.F.R. § 127.1603 — Hotwork.
Citation: us:cfr:33:127.1603
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-127.1603
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 127 (Waterfront Facilities Handling Liquefied Natural Gas And Liquefied Hazardous Gas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each operator of a waterfront facility handling LHG must ensure that no person conducts welding, torch cutting, or other hotwork on the facility, or on a vessel moored to the facility, unless—
(a) The COTP has issued a permit for that hotwork; and
(b) The conditions of the permit are met.
