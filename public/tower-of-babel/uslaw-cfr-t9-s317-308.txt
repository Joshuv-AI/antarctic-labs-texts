9 C.F.R. § 317.308 — Labeling of meat or meat food products with number of servings.
Citation: us:cfr:9:317.308
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-317.308
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 317 (Labeling, Marking Devices, And Containers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The label of any package of a meat or meat food product that bears a representation as to the number of servings contained in such package shall meet the requirements of § 317.2(h)(10).
