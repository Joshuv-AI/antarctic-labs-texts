29 U.S.C. § 158a — Providing facilities for operations of Federal Credit Unions
Citation: us:usc:29:158a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section158a
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 7 (Labor-Management Relations); Subchapter II (National Labor Relations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Provision by an employer of facilities for the operations of a Federal Credit Union on the premises of such employer shall not be deemed to be intimidation, coercion, interference, restraint or discrimination within the provisions of sections 157 and 158 of this title, or acts amendatory thereof.
