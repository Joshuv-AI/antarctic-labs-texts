40 C.F.R. § 455.65 — New source performance standards (NSPS).
Citation: us:cfr:40:455.65
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-455.65
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 455 (Pesticide Chemicals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any new source subject to this subpart which discharges process wastewater pollutants must meet the following standards: There shall be no discharge of process wastewater pollutants.
