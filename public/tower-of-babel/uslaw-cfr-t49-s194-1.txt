49 C.F.R. § 194.1 — Purpose.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 194 (Response Plans For Onshore Oil Pipelines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part contains requirements for oil spill response plans to reduce the environmental impact of oil discharged from onshore oil pipelines.
