43 C.F.R. § 2091.9-2 — -2 Selections by the State of Alaska.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2091.9-2
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2091.9-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2090 (Special Laws And Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The segregation and opening of lands authorized for selection and selected by the State of Alaska under the various statutes granting lands to the State of Alaska are covered by subpart 2627 of this title.
