19 U.S.C. § 1652 — Separability
Citation: us:usc:19:1652
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section1652
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 4 (Tariff Act Of 1930); Part VI (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this chapter, or the application thereof to any person or circumstances, is held invalid, the remainder of the chapter, and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances, shall not be affected thereby.
