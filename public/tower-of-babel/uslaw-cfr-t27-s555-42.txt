27 C.F.R. § 555.42 — License fees.
Citation: us:cfr:27:555.42
Creator: Bureau Of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms, And Explosives, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-555.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 555 (Commerce In Explosives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each applicant shall pay a fee for obtaining a three year license, a separate fee being required for each business premises, as follows:
(1) Manufacturer—$200.
(2) Importer—$200.
(3) Dealer—$200.
(b) Each applicant for a renewal of a license shall pay a fee for a three year license as follows:
(1) Manufacturer—$100.
(2) Importer—$100.
(3) Dealer—$100.
