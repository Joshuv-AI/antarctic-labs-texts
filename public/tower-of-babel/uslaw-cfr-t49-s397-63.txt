49 C.F.R. § 397.63 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:49:397.63
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-397.63
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 397 (Transportation Of Hazardous Materials; Driving And Parking Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart apply to any State or Indian tribe that establishes, maintains, or enforces any routing designations over which NRHM may or may not be transported by motor vehicle. They also apply to any motor carrier that transports or causes to be transported placarded or marked NRHM in commerce.
