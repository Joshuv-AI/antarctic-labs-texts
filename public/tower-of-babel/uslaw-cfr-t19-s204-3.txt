19 C.F.R. § 204.3 — Public hearings.
Citation: us:cfr:19:204.3
Creator: United States International Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-204.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 204 (Investigations Of Effects Of Imports On Agricultural Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A public hearing will be held in connection with each investigation to which this part 204 relates. The Foreign Agricultural Service of the U.S. Department of Agriculture may have a representative or representatives at each hearing who shall have the privilege of examining witnesses.
