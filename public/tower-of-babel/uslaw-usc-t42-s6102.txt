42 U.S.C. § 6102 — Prohibition of discrimination
Citation: us:usc:42:6102
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section6102
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 76 (Age Discrimination In Federally Assisted Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Pursuant to regulations prescribed under section 6103 of this title, and except as provided by section 6103(b) and section 6103(c) of this title, no person in the United States shall, on the basis of age, be excluded from participation in, be denied the benefits of, or be subjected to discrimination under, any program or activity receiving Federal financial assistance.
