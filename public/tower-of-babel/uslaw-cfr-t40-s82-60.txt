40 C.F.R. § 82.60 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:40:82.60
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-82.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 82 (Protection Of Stratospheric Ozone). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this subpart is to implement the requirements of sections 608 and 610 of the Clean Air Act as amended in 1990 on emission reductions and nonessential products.
