46 U.S.C. § 51308 — Uniforms, textbooks, and transportation allowances
Citation: us:usc:46:51308
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section51308
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part B (Merchant Marine Service); Chapter 513 (United States Merchant Marine Academy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Transportation shall provide cadets at the United States Merchant Marine Academy—
(1)
all required uniforms and textbooks; and
(2)
allowances for transportation (including reimbursement of traveling expenses) when traveling under orders as a cadet.
