12 C.F.R. § 1291.43 — Approval of AHP applications.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1291.43
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1291.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1291 (Federal Home Loan Banks' Affordable Housing Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Bank shall approve applications for AHP direct subsidy under its Homeownership Set-Aside Programs in accordance with the Bank's criteria governing the allocation of funds.
