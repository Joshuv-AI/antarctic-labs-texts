16 U.S.C. § 4201 — Statement of purpose
Citation: us:usc:16:4201
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 62 (African Elephant Conservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of this chapter is to perpetuate healthy populations of African elephants.
