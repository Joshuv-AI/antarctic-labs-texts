33 C.F.R. § 175.113 — Launchers.
Citation: us:cfr:33:175.113
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-175.113
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 175 (Equipment Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) When a visual distress signal carried to meet the requirements of § 175.110 requires a launcher to activate, then a launcher approved under 46 CFR 160.028 must also be carried.
