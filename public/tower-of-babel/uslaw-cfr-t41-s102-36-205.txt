41 C.F.R. § 102-36.205 — -36.205 Excess firearms disposal.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-36.205
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-36.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-36 (36—Disposition Of Excess Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless you have specific statutory authority to do otherwise, excess firearms may be transferred only to those Federal agencies authorized to acquire firearms for official use. Firearms not transferred must be destroyed and sold as scrap. For additional guidance on the disposition of firearms refer to part 102-40 of this subchapter.
