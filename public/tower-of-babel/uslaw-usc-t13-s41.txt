13 U.S.C. § 41 — Collection and publication
Citation: us:usc:13:41
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title13-section41
Context: United States Code, Title 13 (Census); Chapter 3 (Collection And Publication Of Statistics); Subchapter I (Cotton). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall collect and publish statistics concerning the—
(1)
amount of cotton ginned;
(2)
quantity of raw cotton consumed in manufacturing establishments of every character;
(3)
quantity of baled cotton on hand;
(4)
number of active consuming cotton spindles;
(5)
number of active spindle hours; and
(6)
quantity of cotton imported and exported, with the country of origin and destination.
