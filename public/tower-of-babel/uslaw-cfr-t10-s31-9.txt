10 C.F.R. § 31.9 — General license to own byproduct material.
Citation: us:cfr:10:31.9
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-31.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 31 (General Domestic Licenses For Byproduct Material). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A general license is hereby issued to own byproduct material without regard to quantity. Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter, a general licensee under this paragraph is not authorized to manufacture, produce, transfer, receive, possess, use, import or export byproduct material, except as authorized in a specific license.
