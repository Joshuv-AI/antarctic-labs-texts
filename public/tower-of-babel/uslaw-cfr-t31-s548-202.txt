31 C.F.R. § 548.202 — Prohibited transactions with respect to the Ministry of Finance of the Republic of Belarus and the Development Bank of the Republic of Belarus (Directive 1).
Citation: us:cfr:31:548.202
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-548.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 548 (Belarus Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following activities by a U.S. person or within the United States are prohibited: all transactions in, provision of financing for, and other dealings in new debt with a maturity of longer than 90 days issued on or after December 2, 2021 by the Ministry of Finance of the Republic of Belarus or the Development Bank of the Republic of Belarus.
