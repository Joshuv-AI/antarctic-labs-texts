31 C.F.R. § 561.314 — Knowingly.
Citation: us:cfr:31:561.314
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-561.314
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 561 (Iranian Financial Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term knowingly, with respect to conduct, a circumstance, or a result, means that a person has actual knowledge, or should have known, of the conduct, the circumstance, or the result.
