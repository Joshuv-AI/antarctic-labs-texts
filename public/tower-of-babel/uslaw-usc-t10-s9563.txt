10 U.S.C. § 9563 — Clothing: replacement when destroyed to prevent contagion
Citation: us:usc:10:9563
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9563
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Procurement); Chapter 965 (Issue Of Serviceable Material To Armed Forces). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Air Force may order a gratuitous issue of clothing to any enlisted member of the Air Force or the Space Force who has had a contagious disease, and to any hospital attendant who attended him while he had that disease, to replace clothing destroyed by order of a medical officer to prevent contagion.
