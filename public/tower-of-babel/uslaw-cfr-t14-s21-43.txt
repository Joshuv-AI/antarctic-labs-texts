14 C.F.R. § 21.43 — Location of manufacturing facilities.
Citation: us:cfr:14:21.43
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-21.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 21 (Certification Procedures For Products And Articles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in § 21.29, the FAA does not issue a type certificate if the manufacturing facilities for the product are located outside of the United States, unless the FAA finds that the location of the manufacturer's facilities places no undue burden on the FAA in administering applicable airworthiness requirements.
