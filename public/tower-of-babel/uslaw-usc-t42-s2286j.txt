42 U.S.C. § 2286j — Procurement of inspector general services
Citation: us:usc:42:2286j
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2286j
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 23 (Development And Control Of Atomic Energy); Subchapter XVII–A (Defense Nuclear Facilities Safety Board). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Within 90 days of December 23, 2011, the Defense Nuclear Facilities Safety Board shall enter into an agreement for inspector general services with the Office of Inspector General for the Nuclear Regulatory Commission for fiscal years 2012 and 2013: Provided, That at the expiration of such agreement, the Defense Nuclear Facilities Safety Board shall procure inspector general services annually thereafter.
