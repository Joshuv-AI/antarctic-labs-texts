18 U.S.C. § 3189 — Place and character of hearing
Citation: us:usc:18:3189
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section3189
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part II (Criminal Procedure); Chapter 209 (Extradition). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Hearings in cases of extradition under treaty stipulation or convention shall be held on land, publicly, and in a room or office easily accessible to the public.
