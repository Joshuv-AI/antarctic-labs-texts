40 C.F.R. § 104.1 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:40:104.1
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-104.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 104 (Public Hearings On Effluent Standards For Toxic Pollutants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part shall be applicable to hearings required by statute to be held in connection with the establishment of toxic pollutant effluent standards under section 307(a) of the Act.
