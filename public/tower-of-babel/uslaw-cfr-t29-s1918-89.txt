29 C.F.R. § 1918.89 — Handling hazardous cargo (See also § 1918.2 and § 1918.99).
Citation: us:cfr:29:1918.89
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1918.89
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1918 (Safety And Health Regulations For Longshoring). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Hazardous cargo shall be slung and secured so that neither the draft nor individual packages can fall because of tipping of the draft or slacking of the supporting gear.
