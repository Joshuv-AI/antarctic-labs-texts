7 U.S.C. § 511p — Delegation of duties by Secretary of Agriculture
Citation: us:usc:7:511p
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section511p
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 21A (Tobacco Inspection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any duties devolving upon the Secretary of Agriculture by virtue of the provisions of this chapter may with like force and effect be executed by such officer or officers, agent or agents, of the Department of Agriculture as the Secretary may designate for the purpose.
