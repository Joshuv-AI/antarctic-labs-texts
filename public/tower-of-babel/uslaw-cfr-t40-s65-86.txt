40 C.F.R. § 65.86 — Monitoring.
Citation: us:cfr:40:65.86
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-65.86
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 65 (Consolidated Federal Air Rule). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The owner or operator of a transfer rack equipped with a closed vent system and control device pursuant to § 65.83(a)(1) or (2) shall monitor the closed vent system and control device as required under the applicable paragraphs specified in § 65.142(c).
