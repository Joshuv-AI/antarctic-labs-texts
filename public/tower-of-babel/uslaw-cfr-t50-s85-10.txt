50 C.F.R. § 85.10 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:50:85.10
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-85.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 85 (Clean Vessel Act Grant Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to establish requirements for state participation in the Clean Vessel Act Grant Program authorized by Section 5604 of the Clean Vessel Act (Public Law 102-587, Subtitle F).
