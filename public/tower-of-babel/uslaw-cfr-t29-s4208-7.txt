29 C.F.R. § 4208.7 — Adjustment of withdrawal liability for subsequent withdrawals.
Citation: us:cfr:29:4208.7
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4208.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4208 (Reduction Or Waiver Of Partial Withdrawal Liability). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The liability of an employer for a partial or complete withdrawal from a plan subsequent to a partial withdrawal from that plan in a prior plan year shall be reduced in accordance with part 4206 of this chapter.
