12 C.F.R. § 1253.1 — Purpose and authority.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1253.1
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1253.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1253 (Prior Approval For Enterprise Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to establish policies and procedures implementing the prior approval authority for Enterprise products, in accordance with section 1321 of the Federal Housing Enterprises Financial Safety and Soundness Act of 1992 (12 U.S.C. 4541), as amended (Safety and Soundness Act).
