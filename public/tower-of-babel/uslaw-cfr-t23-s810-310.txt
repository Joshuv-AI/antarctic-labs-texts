23 C.F.R. § 810.310 — Applicability of other provisions.
Citation: us:cfr:23:810.310
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-810.310
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 810 (Mass Transit And Special Use Highway Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Federal proportional share of the cost of an urban system nonhighway public mass transit project approved under this subpart shall be equal to the Federal share which would have been paid if the project were a highway project as determined under 23 U.S.C. 120(a).
