52 U.S.C. § 21141 — “State” defined
Citation: us:usc:52:21141
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title52-section21141
Context: United States Code, Title 52 (Voting And Elections); Chapter 209 (Election Administration Improvement); Subchapter VII (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter, the term “State” includes the District of Columbia, the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico, Guam, American Samoa, and the United States Virgin Islands.
