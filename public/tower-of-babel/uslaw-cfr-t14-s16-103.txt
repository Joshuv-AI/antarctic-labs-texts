14 C.F.R. § 16.103 — Notice of investigation.
Citation: us:cfr:14:16.103
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-16.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 16 (Rules Of Practice For Federally-Assisted Airport Enforcement Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Following the initiation of an investigation under § 16.101, the FAA sends a notice to the person(s) subject to investigation. The notice will set forth the areas of the agency's concern and the reasons therefor; request a response to the notice within 30 days of the date of service; and inform the respondent that the FAA will, in its discretion, invite good faith efforts to resolve the matter.
