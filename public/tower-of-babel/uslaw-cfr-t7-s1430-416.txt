7 C.F.R. § 1430.416 — Offsets and withholdings.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1430.416
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1430.416
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1430 (Dairy Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

FSA may offset or withhold any amount due to FSA or CCC under this subpart under the provisions of part 3 of this title or any successor regulations, or any other authorities that may allow for collection action of that sort.
