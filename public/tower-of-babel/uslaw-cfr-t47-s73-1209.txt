47 C.F.R. § 73.1209 — References to time.
Citation: us:cfr:47:73.1209
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-73.1209
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 73 (Radio Broadcast Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless specifically designated as “standard (non-advanced)” or “advanced,” all references to time contained in this part, and in license documents and other authorizations issued thereunder shall be understood to mean local time; i.e., the time legally observed in the community.
