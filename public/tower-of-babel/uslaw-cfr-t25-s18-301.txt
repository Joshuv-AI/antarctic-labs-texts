25 C.F.R. § 18.301 — May a tribe create and adopt a single heir rule without adopting a tribal probate code?
Citation: us:cfr:25:18.301
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-18.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 18 (Tribal Probate Codes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. A tribe may create and adopt a single heir rule for intestate succession. The single heir rule may specify a single recipient other than the one specified in 25 U.S.C. 2206(a)(2)(D).
