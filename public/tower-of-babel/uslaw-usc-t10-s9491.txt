10 U.S.C. § 9491 — Status as federally chartered corporation; purposes
Citation: us:usc:10:9491
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9491
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training); Chapter 959 (Civil Air Patrol). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Status.—
(1)
The Civil Air Patrol is a nonprofit corporation that is federally chartered under section 40301 of title 36.
(2)
Except as provided in section 9492(b)(2) of this title, the Civil Air Patrol is not an instrumentality of the Federal Government for any purpose.
(b) Purposes.—
The purposes of the Civil Air Patrol are set forth in section 40302 of title 36.
