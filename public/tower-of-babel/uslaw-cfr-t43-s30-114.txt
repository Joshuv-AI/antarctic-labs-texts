43 C.F.R. § 30.114 — Will I receive notice of the probate proceeding?
Citation: us:cfr:43:30.114
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-30.114
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 30 (Indian Probate Hearings Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the case is designated as a formal probate proceeding, OHA will send a notice of hearing to:
(a) Potential heirs and devisees named in the probate file;
(b) Those creditors whose claims are included in the probate file; and
(c) Other interested parties identified by OHA
