34 C.F.R. § 373.10 — What selection criteria does the Secretary use?
Citation: us:cfr:34:373.10
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-373.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 373 (Rehabilitation National Activities Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary publishes in the Federal Register or includes in the application package the selection criteria for each competition under this program. To evaluate the applications for new grants under this program, the Secretary may use the following:
(a) Selection criteria established under 34 CFR 75.209.
(b) Selection criteria in 34 CFR 75.210.
(c) Any combination of selection criteria from paragraphs (a) and (b) of this section.
