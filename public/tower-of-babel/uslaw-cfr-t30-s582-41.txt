30 C.F.R. § 582.41 — Method of royalty calculation.
Citation: us:cfr:30:582.41
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-582.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 582 (Operations In The Outer Continental Shelf For Minerals Other Than Oil, Gas, And Sulphur). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the event that the provisions of royalty management regulations in part 1206 of chapter XII do not apply to the specific commodities produced under regulations in this part, the lessee shall comply with procedures specified in the leasing notice.
