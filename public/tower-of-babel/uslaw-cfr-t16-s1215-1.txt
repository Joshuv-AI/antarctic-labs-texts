16 C.F.R. § 1215.1 — Scope.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1215 (Safety Standard For Infant Bath Seats). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part 1215 establishes a consumer product safety standard for infant bath seats manufactured or imported on or after December 6, 2010.
