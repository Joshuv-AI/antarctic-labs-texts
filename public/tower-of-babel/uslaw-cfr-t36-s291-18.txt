36 C.F.R. § 291.18 — Modification or cancellation of permits.
Citation: us:cfr:36:291.18
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-291.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 291 (Paleontological Resources Preservation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Authorized Officer may modify a permit, consistent with applicable laws and policies, when:
(a) The Authorized Officer determines that there are management, administrative, or safety reasons to modify a permit; or
(b) A permittee requests a modification in writing.
