12 C.F.R. § 34.24 — Nonfederally chartered commercial banks.
Citation: us:cfr:12:34.24
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-34.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 34 (Real Estate Lending And Appraisals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pursuant to 12 U.S.C. 3803(a), a State chartered commercial bank may make ARM loans in accordance with the provisions of this subpart. For purposes of this section, the term “State” shall have the same meaning as set forth in § 34.2(b).
