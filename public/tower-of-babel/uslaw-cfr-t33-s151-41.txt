33 C.F.R. § 151.41 — Operating requirements for oceangoing ships with IOPP Certificates: Category C and D Oil-like NLSs.
Citation: us:cfr:33:151.41
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-151.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 151 (Vessels Carrying Oil, Noxious Liquid Substances, Garbage, Municipal Or Commercial Waste, And Ballast Water). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The master or person in charge of an oceangoing ship certificated under § 151.37(a) shall ensure that—
(a) The carriage and discharge of the oil-like NLS meets §§ 157.29, 157.31, 157.35, 157.37, 157.41, 157.45, 157.47, and 157.49 of this chapter; and
(b) The oil-like NLS is not discharged unless—
(1) The monitor required by § 151.37(a)(1) is set to detect the oil-like NLS; and
(2) A statement that the monitor has been set to detect the oil-like NLS is entered in the Oil Record Book Part II(Cargo/Ballast Operations), required by § 151.25.
