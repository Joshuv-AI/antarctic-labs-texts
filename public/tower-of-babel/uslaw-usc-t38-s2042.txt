38 U.S.C. § 2042 — Supported housing for veterans participating in compensated work therapies
Citation: us:usc:38:2042
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section2042
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 20 (Benefits For Homeless Veterans); Subchapter V (Housing Assistance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may authorize homeless veterans in the compensated work therapy program to be provided housing through the therapeutic residence program under section 2032 of this title or through grant and per diem providers under subchapter II of this chapter.
