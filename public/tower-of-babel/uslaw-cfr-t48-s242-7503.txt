48 C.F.R. § 242.7503 — 242.7503 Contract clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:242.7503
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-242.7503
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 242 (Contract Administration And Audit Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Use the clause at 252.242-7006, Accounting System Administration, in solicitations and contracts when contemplating—
(a) A cost-reimbursement, incentive type, time-and-materials, or labor-hour contract; or
(b) A contract with progress payments made on the basis of costs incurred by the contractor or on a percentage or stage of completion.
