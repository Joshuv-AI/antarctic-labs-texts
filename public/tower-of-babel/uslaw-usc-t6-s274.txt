6 U.S.C. § 274 — Employee discipline
Citation: us:usc:6:274
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section274
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 1 (Homeland Security Organization); Subchapter IV (Border, Maritime, And Transportation Security); Part E (Citizenship And Immigration Services). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of the Bureau of Citizenship and Immigration Services may, notwithstanding any other provision of law, impose disciplinary action, including termination of employment, pursuant to policies and procedures applicable to employees of the Federal Bureau of Investigation, on any employee of the Bureau of Citizenship and Immigration Services who willfully deceives Congress or agency leadership on any matter.
