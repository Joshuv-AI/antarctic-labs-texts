29 C.F.R. § 1626.19 — Filing of Commission lawsuit.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1626.19
Creator: Equal Employment Opportunity Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1626.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1626 (Procedures—Age Discrimination In Employment Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The right of the Commission to file a civil action under the ADEA is not dependent on the filing of a charge and is not affected by the issuance of a Notice of Dismissal or Termination to any aggrieved person.
