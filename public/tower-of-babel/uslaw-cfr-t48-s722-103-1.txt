48 C.F.R. § 722.103-1 — 722.103-1 Definitions.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 722 (Application Of Labor Laws To Government Acquisition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Compensatory time off means leave equal to overtime worked, which, unless otherwise authorized in a contract or approved by a contracting officer, must be taken not later than the end of the calendar month following that in which the overtime is worked.
