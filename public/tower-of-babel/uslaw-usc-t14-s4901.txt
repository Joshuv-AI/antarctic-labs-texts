14 U.S.C. § 4901 — Requirement for prior authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:14:4901
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section4901
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 49 (Authorizations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Amounts may be appropriated to or for the use of the Coast Guard for the following matters only if the amounts have been authorized by law after December 31, 1976:
(1)
For the operation and support of the Coast Guard, not otherwise provided for.
(2)
For the procurement, construction, renovation, and improvement of aids to navigation, shore facilities, vessels, aircraft, and systems, including equipment related thereto, and for maintenance, rehabilitation, lease, and operation of facilities and equipment.
(3)
For research and development.1 of technologies, materials, and human factors directly related to improving the performance of the Coast Guard.
