33 C.F.R. § 150.850 — How long must a declaration of inspection form be kept?
Citation: us:cfr:33:150.850
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-150.850
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 150 (Deepwater Ports: Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The licensee must keep signed copies of the declaration of inspection forms required by § 150.430 of this part for one month from the date of signature.
