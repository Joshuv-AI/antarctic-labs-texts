43 C.F.R. § 4.905 — What if a due date falls on a day the Department or relevant office is not open for business?
Citation: us:cfr:43:4.905
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4.905
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4 (Department Of The Interior Hearings And Appeals Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a due date under this subpart falls on a day the relevant office is not open for business (such as a weekend, holiday, or shutdown), the due date is the next day the relevant office is open for business.
