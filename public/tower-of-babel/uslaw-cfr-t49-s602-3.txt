49 C.F.R. § 602.3 — Applicability.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 602 (Emergency Relief). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to entities that provide public transportation services and that are impacted by emergencies and major disasters.
