10 U.S.C. § 8583 — Annuity premium to be paid by monthly installments; government reimbursement
Citation: us:usc:10:8583
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8583
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Education And Training); Chapter 857 (Retirement Of Civilian Members Of The Teaching Staffs Of The United States Naval Academy And United States Naval Postgraduate School). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each civilian member shall make a monthly allotment in an amount equal to 10 percent of his monthly basic salary toward the purchase of his deferred annuity policy. For each month the allotment is in force, the pay account of the civilian member shall be credited monthly from appropriations made for this purpose with an additional amount equal to 5 percent of his monthly basic salary.
