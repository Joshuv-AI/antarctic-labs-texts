43 C.F.R. § 3815.5 — Access to stock watering places.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3815.5
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3815.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3810 (Lands And Minerals Subject To Location). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No watering places shall be inclosed, nor proper and lawful access of stock thereto prevented, nor the watering of stock thereat interfered with.
