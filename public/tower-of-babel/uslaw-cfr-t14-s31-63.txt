14 C.F.R. § 31.63 — Safety belts.
Citation: us:cfr:14:31.63
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-31.63
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 31 (Airworthiness Standards: Manned Free Balloons). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) There must be a safety belt, harness, or other restraining means for each occupant, unless the Administrator finds it unnecessary. If installed, the belt, harness, or other restraining means and its supporting structure must meet the strength requirements of subpart C of this part.
(b) This section does not apply to balloons that incorporate a basket or gondola.
