19 C.F.R. § 147.32 — Detail of officers to protect the revenue.
Citation: us:cfr:19:147.32
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-147.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 147 (Trade Fairs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Center director shall detail an officer to act as his representative at the fair and shall station inside the buildings as many additional Custom officers and employees as may be necessary to properly protect the revenue.
