42 C.F.R. § 136a.55 — a.55 Drugs and devices and termination of ectopic pregnancies.
Citation: us:cfr:42:136a.55
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-136a.55
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 136a (PART 136a—INDIAN HEALTH). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Federal funds are available for drugs or devices to prevent implantation of the fertilized ovum, and for medical procedures necessary for the termination of an ectopic pregnancy.
