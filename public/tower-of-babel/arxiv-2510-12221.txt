Stochastic nonlinear wave equation with rougher than white noise
Xue-Mei Li 1,2 and Xianfeng Ren 2

Abstract
We study the singular stochastic wave equation on 𝐓 2 \mathbf{T}^{2} , with a cubic nonlinearity and Gaussian rough ‘Matérn’ forcing (a Fourier multiplier of order α > 0 \alpha>0 applied to space-time white noise) and establish local well-posedness for α < 3 8 \alpha<\tfrac{3}{8} . This extends [ GKO18 ] beyond white noise and strengthens the quadratic-case result [ OO21 ] ( α < 1 2 \alpha<\frac{1}{2} ). Our argument develops new trilinear estimates in Bourgain spaces together with case-specific cubic counting estimates.
