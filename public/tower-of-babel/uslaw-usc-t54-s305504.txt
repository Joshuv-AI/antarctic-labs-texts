54 U.S.C. § 305504 — Matching grants to Committee
Citation: us:usc:54:305504
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section305504
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3055 (National Building Museum). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall provide matching grants to the Committee for its programs related to historic preservation. The Committee shall match the grants in such a manner and with such funds and services as shall be satisfactory to the Secretary, except that not more than $500,000 may be provided to the Committee in any one fiscal year.
