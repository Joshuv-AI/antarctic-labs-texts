37 C.F.R. § 41.102 — Completion of examination.
Citation: us:cfr:37:41.102
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-41.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 41 (Practice Before The Patent Trial And Appeal Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before a contested case is initiated, except as the Board may otherwise authorize, for each involved application and patent:
(a) Examination or reexamination must be completed, and
(b) There must be at least one claim that:
(1) Is patentable but for a judgment in the contested case, and
(2) Would be involved in the contested case.
