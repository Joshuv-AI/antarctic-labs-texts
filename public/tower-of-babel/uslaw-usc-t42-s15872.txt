42 U.S.C. § 15872 — Assessment of geothermal energy potential
Citation: us:usc:42:15872
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section15872
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 149 (National Energy Policy And Programs); Subchapter II (Renewable Energy); Part B (Geothermal Energy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 3 years after August 8, 2005, and thereafter as the availability of data and developments in technology warrants, the Secretary of the Interior, acting through the Director of the United States Geological Survey and in cooperation with the States, shall—
(1)
update the Assessment of Geothermal Resources made during 1978; and
(2)
submit to Congress the updated assessment.
