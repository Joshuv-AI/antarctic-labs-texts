33 U.S.C. § 3804 — Compliance with international law
Citation: us:usc:33:3804
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section3804
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 51 (Clean Hulls); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any action taken under this chapter shall be taken in accordance with treaties to which the United States is a party and other international obligations of the United States.
