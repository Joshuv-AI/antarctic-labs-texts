36 C.F.R. § 9.191 — How does the NPS process my request for reconsideration?
Citation: us:cfr:36:9.191
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-9.191
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 9 (Minerals Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Regional Director will review his or her original decision and, within 90 days after receipt of your appeal, provide you with a written statement reversing, affirming, or modifying that decision, unless the Regional Director notifies you that he or she needs additional time to review the original decision. When issued, that written statement constitutes the Regional Director's final decision on the matter.
