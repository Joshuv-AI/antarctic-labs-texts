17 C.F.R. § 170.4 — Allocation of dues (section 17(b)(6) of the Act).
Citation: us:cfr:17:170.4
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-170.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 170 (Registered Futures Associations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Dues imposed on members of a futures association must be allocated equitably among members and may not be structured in a manner constituting a barrier to entry of any person seeking to engage in commodity-related business activities.
