42 C.F.R. § 460.2 — Basis.
Citation: us:cfr:42:460.2

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-460.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 460 (Programs Of All-Inclusive Care For The Elderly (Pace)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part implements sections 1894, 1905(a), and 1934 of the Act, which authorize the following:
(a) Medicare payments to, and coverage of benefits under, PACE.
(b) The establishment of PACE as a State option under Medicaid to provide for Medicaid payments to, and coverage of benefits under, PACE.
