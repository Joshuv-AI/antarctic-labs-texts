1 U.S.C. § 104 — Numbering of sections; single proposition
Citation: us:usc:1:104
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title1-section104
Context: United States Code, Title 1 (General Provisions); Chapter 2 (Acts And Resolutions; Formalities Of Enactment; Repeals; Sealing Of Instruments). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each section shall be numbered, and shall contain, as nearly as may be, a single proposition of enactment.
