42 U.S.C. § 8285 — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:42:8285
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section8285
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 91 (National Energy Conservation Policy); Subchapter V (Energy Auditor Training And Certification). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the purpose of this subchapter to encourage the training and certification of individuals to conduct energy audits for residential and commercial buildings in order to serve the various private and public needs of the Nation for energy audits.
