38 C.F.R. § 36.4513 — Foreclosure and liquidation.
Citation: us:cfr:38:36.4513
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-36.4513
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 36 (Loan Guaranty). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the event of a foreclosure sale or other liquidation of the security for a loan, the Department of Veterans Affairs shall credit upon the indebtedness the greater of:
(a) The net proceeds of the sale, or
(b) The current market value of the property as determined by the Department of Veterans Affairs, less the costs and expenses of liquidation.
