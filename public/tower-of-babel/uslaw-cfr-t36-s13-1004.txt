36 C.F.R. § 13.1004 — Aircraft use.
Citation: us:cfr:36:13.1004
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-13.1004
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 13 (National Park System Units In Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In extraordinary cases where no reasonable alternative exists, local rural residents who permanently reside in the following exempted community(ies) may use aircraft for access to lands and waters within the park for subsistence purposes in accordance with a permit issued by the Superintendent: Anaktuvuk Pass.
