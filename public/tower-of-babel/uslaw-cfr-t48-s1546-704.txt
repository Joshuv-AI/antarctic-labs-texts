48 C.F.R. § 1546.704 — 1546.704 Authority for use of warranties.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1546.704
Creator: Chapter 15—Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1546.704
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1546 (Quality Assurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Contracting Officer shall ensure that the use of a warranty clause in a contract has the concurrence of the Project Officer.
