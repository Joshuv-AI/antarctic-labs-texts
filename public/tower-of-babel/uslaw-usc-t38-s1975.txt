38 U.S.C. § 1975 — Jurisdiction of District Courts
Citation: us:usc:38:1975
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section1975
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 19 (Insurance); Subchapter III (Servicemembers’ Group Life Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The district courts of the United States shall have original jurisdiction of any civil action or claim against the United States founded upon this subchapter.
