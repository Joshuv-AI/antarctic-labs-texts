15 U.S.C. § 9526 — Publicity
Citation: us:usc:15:9526
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section9526
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 120 (Minority Business Development); Subchapter I (Existing Initiatives); Part B (Minority Business Development Agency Business Center Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In carrying out the MBDA Business Center Program, the Under Secretary shall widely publicize the MBDA Business Center Program, including—
(1)
on the website of the Agency;
(2)
via social media outlets; and
(3)
by sharing information relating to the MBDA Business Center Program with community-based organizations, including interpretation groups where necessary, to communicate in the most common languages spoken by the groups served by those organizations.
