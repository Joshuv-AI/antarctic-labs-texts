10 C.F.R. § 780.6 — Department participation.
Citation: us:cfr:10:780.6
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 780 (Patent Compensation Board Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department shall be a party to all proceedings under this part, and the Office of the General Counsel will represent the Department's interests before the Board.
