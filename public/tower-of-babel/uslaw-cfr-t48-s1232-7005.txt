48 C.F.R. § 1232.7005 — 1232.7005 Contract clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1232.7005
Creator: Chapter 12—Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1232.7005
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1232 (Contract Financing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 1252.232-70, Electronic Submission of Payment Requests, in solicitations and contracts exceeding the micro-purchase threshold, except those for which the contracting officer has directed or approved otherwise under 1232.7002, and those paid with a Governmentwide commercial purchase card.
