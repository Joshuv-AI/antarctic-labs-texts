38 U.S.C. § 5100 — Definitions

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part IV (General Administrative Provisions); Chapter 51 (Claims, Effective Dates, And Payments); Subchapter I (Claims). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter:
(1)
The term “claimant” means any individual applying for, or submitting a claim for, any benefit under the laws administered by the Secretary.
(2)
The term “notice” means a communication issued through means (including electronic means) prescribed by the Secretary.
