32 C.F.R. § 1653.4 — File to be returned after appeal to the President is decided.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1653.4
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1653.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1653 (Appeal To The President). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the appeal to the President has been decided, the file shall be returned as prescribed by the Director of Selective Service.
