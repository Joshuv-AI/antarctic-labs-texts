47 C.F.R. § 96.31 — Aggregation of priority access licenses.
Citation: us:cfr:47:96.31
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-96.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 96 (Citizens Broadband Radio Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Priority Access Licensees may aggregate up to four PAL channels in any License Area at any given time.
(b) The criteria in § 20.22(b) of this chapter will apply in order to attribute partial ownership and other interests for the purpose of applying the aggregation limit in paragraph (a) of this section.
