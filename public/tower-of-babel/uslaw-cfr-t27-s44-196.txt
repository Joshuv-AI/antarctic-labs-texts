27 C.F.R. § 44.196 — To district director of customs for consumption as supplies on commercial vessels and aircraft.
Citation: us:cfr:27:44.196
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-44.196
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 44 (Exportation Of Tobacco Products And Cigarette Papers And Tubes, Without Payment Of Tax, Or With Drawback Of Tax). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where tobacco products, or cigarette papers or tubes are removed from a factory or an export warehouse for consumption as supplies beyond the jurisdiction of the internal revenue laws of the United States, the manufacturer or export warehouse proprietor shall consign the shipment to the district director of customs at the port at which the shipment is to be laden.
