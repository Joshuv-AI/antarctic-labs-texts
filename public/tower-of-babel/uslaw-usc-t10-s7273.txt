10 U.S.C. § 7273 — Distinguished-service medal: award
Citation: us:usc:10:7273
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7273
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 737 (Decorations And Awards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may award a distinguished-service medal of appropriate design and a ribbon, together with a rosette or other device to be worn in place thereof, to a person who, while serving in any capacity with the Army, distinguishes himself by exceptionally meritorious service to the United States in a duty of great responsibility.
