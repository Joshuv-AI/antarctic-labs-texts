42 C.F.R. § 137.100 — May a Self-Governance Tribe retain and spend interest earned on any funds paid under a compact or funding agreement?
Citation: us:cfr:42:137.100
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-137.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 137 (Tribal Self-Governance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, pursuant to section 508(h) of the Act [25 U.S.C. 458aaa-7(h)], a Self-Governance Tribe may retain and spend interest earned on any funds paid under a compact or funding agreement.
