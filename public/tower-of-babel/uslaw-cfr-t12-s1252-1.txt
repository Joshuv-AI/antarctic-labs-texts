12 C.F.R. § 1252.1 — Enterprise portfolio holding criteria.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1252.1
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1252.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1252 (Portfolio Holdings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Enterprises are required to comply with the portfolio holdings criteria set forth in their respective Senior Preferred Stock Purchase Agreements with the Department of the Treasury, as they may be amended from time to time.
