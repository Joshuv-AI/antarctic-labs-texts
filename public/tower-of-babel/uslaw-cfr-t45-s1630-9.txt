45 C.F.R. § 1630.9 — Tax-sheltered annuities, retirement accounts, and penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1630.9
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1630.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1630 (Cost Standards And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No provision contained in this part shall be construed to affect any payment by a recipient on behalf of its employees for the purpose of contributing to or funding a tax-sheltered annuity, retirement account, or pension fund.
