7 C.F.R. § 764.301 — Youth loan uses.
Citation: us:cfr:7:764.301
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-764.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 764 (Direct Loan Making). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Youth loan funds may only be used to finance a modest, income-producing, agriculture-related, educational project while participating in 4-H, FFA, or a similar organization.
