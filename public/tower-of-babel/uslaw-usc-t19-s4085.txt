19 U.S.C. § 4085 — Rate after termination of import relief
Citation: us:usc:19:4085
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section4085
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 26 (Dominican Republic-Central America Free Trade); Subchapter III (Relief From Imports); Part B (Textile And Apparel Safeguard Measures). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When import relief under this part is terminated with respect to an article, the rate of duty on that article shall be the rate that would have been in effect, but for the provision of such relief.
