16 U.S.C. § 410gggg–2 — Authorization of annual appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:410gggg–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section410gggg–2
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LIX–EEE (Homestead National Historical Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of carrying out the suggestions and recommendations of the Secretary of the Interior, the necessary annual appropriations therefor are authorized.
