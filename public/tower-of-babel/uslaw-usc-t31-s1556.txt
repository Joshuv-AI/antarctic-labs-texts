31 U.S.C. § 1556 — Comptroller General: reports on appropriation accounts
Citation: us:usc:31:1556
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section1556
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 15 (Appropriation Accounting); Subchapter IV (Closing Accounts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
In carrying out audit responsibilities, the Comptroller General shall report on operations under this subchapter to—
(1)
the head of the agency concerned;
(2)
the Secretary of the Treasury; and
(3)
the President.
(b)
A report under this section shall include an appraisal of unpaid obligations under fixed appropriation accounts for which the period of availability for obligation has ended.
