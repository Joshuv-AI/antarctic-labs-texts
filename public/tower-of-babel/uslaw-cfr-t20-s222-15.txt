20 C.F.R. § 222.15 — When spouse is living with employee.
Citation: us:cfr:20:222.15
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-222.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 222 (Family Relationships). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A spouse, or widow(er) is living with the employee if—
(a) He or she and the employee are living in the same household; or
(b) The employee is contributing to the support of the spouse or widow(er); or
(c) The employee is under court order to contribute to the support of the spouse or widow(er).
