21 C.F.R. § 660.5 — Specificity.
Citation: us:cfr:21:660.5
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-660.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 660 (Additional Standards For Diagnostic Substances For Laboratory Tests). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each filling of the product shall be specific for antibody to hepatitis B surface antigen, as determined by specificity tests found acceptable by the Director, Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research.
