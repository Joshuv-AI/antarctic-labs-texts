46 C.F.R. § 31.05-5 — -5 Posting the certificate of inspection—TB/ALL.
Citation: us:cfr:46:31.05-5
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-31.05-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 31 (Inspection And Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The certificate of inspection shall be framed under glass and posted in a conspicuous part of the vessel, except that where it is not practicable to so expose the certificate of inspection it shall be carried in the vessel in such manner as authorized by the Officer in Charge, Marine Inspection.
