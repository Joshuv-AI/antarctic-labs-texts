20 C.F.R. § 652.2 — Scope and purpose of the Wagner-Peyser Act Employment Service.
Citation: us:cfr:20:652.2
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-652.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 652 (Establishment And Functioning Of State Employment Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The basic purpose of the ES is to improve the functioning of the nation's labor markets by bringing together individuals who are seeking employment and employers who are seeking workers.
