26 U.S.C. § 860A — Taxation of REMIC’s
Citation: us:usc:26:860A
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section860A
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 1 (Normal Taxes And Surtaxes); Subchapter M (Regulated Investment Companies And Real Estate Investment Trusts); Part IV (Real Estate Mortgage Investment Conduits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) General rule
Except as otherwise provided in this part, a REMIC shall not be subject to taxation under this subtitle (and shall not be treated as a corporation, partnership, or trust for purposes of this subtitle).
(b) Income taxable to holders
The income of any REMIC shall be taxable to the holders of interests in such REMIC as provided in this part.
