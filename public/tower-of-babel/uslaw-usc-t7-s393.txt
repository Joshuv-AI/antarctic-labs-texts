7 U.S.C. § 393 — Sale of pathological and zoological specimens; disposition of moneys
Citation: us:usc:7:393
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section393
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 15 (Bureau Of Animal Industry). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Agriculture is authorized to prepare and sell at cost such pathological and zoological specimens as he may deem of scientific or educational value to scientists or others engaged in the work of hygiene and sanitation: Provided, That all moneys received from the sale of such specimens shall be deposited in the Treasury as miscellaneous receipts.
