29 C.F.R. § 1695.9 — Rescinded guidance.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1695.9
Creator: Equal Employment Opportunity Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1695.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1695 (Guidance Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission shall not cite, use, or rely on guidance documents that are rescinded, except to establish historical facts.
