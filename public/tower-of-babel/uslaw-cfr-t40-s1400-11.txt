40 C.F.R. § 1400.11 — Limitation on dissemination to State and local government officials.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1400.11
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency And Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1400.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1400 (Distribution Of Off-Site Consequence Analysis Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as authorized by this part and by 42 U.S.C. 7412(r)(7)(H)(v)(III), Federal, State, and local government officials, and qualified researchers are prohibited from disseminating OCA information to State and local government officials. Violation of this provision subjects the violator to civil liability as provided in 42 U.S.C. 7413.
