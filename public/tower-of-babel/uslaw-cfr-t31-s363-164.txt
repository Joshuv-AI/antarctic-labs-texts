31 C.F.R. § 363.164 — Is a converted savings bond eligible to be converted back into a definitive bond?
Citation: us:cfr:31:363.164
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-363.164
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 363 (Regulations Governing Securities Held In Treasurydirect). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Once a definitive savings bond has been converted to a book-entry bond, it may not be converted back into a definitive bond.
