41 C.F.R. § 301-51.203 — -51.203 Procedure for canceled or postponed trip.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-51.203
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-51.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-51 (51—Paying Travel Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a trip is canceled or postponed indefinitely, the employee must notify the appropriate agency officials and refund any monies advanced.
