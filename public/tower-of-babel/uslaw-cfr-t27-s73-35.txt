27 C.F.R. § 73.35 — Do I need to keep paper copies of forms I submit to TTB electronically?
Citation: us:cfr:27:73.35
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-73.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 73 (Electronic Signatures; Electronic Submission Of Forms). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this part alters any other regulatory or statutory requirement that records be maintained in paper format. If the regulations in this chapter require you to keep paper copies of certain forms, you must continue to do so unless TTB otherwise authorizes you to maintain electronic copies of these documents through an approved alternate method or procedure.
