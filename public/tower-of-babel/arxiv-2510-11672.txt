Some Properties of Homology and Exactness of Nomura’s Homology Sequences in a Grandis Homological Category
Yaroslav Kopylov, Vadim Leshkov

Abstract
. We consider Lambek’s invariants Ker \Ker and Im \Img for commutative squares in Grandis homological categories. We prove that Nomura’s null sequences exist in such categories and find sufficienct conditions for their exactness. We also prove the coincidence of left and right homology in Grandis homological categories. Key words and phrases: commutative square, Grandis homological category, homology, Nomura’s null sequences, exactness Mathematics Subject Classification 2020: 18G50

Later Leicht

In

In

(Lambek’s morphism) provided that the morphism

In the present article, we prove the existence of sequences (
3

4
) and obtain criteria for their exactness in the framework of Grandis homological categories (see
[
3
,
4
]
).
These categories were introduced as a tool for developing
homological algebra “in a strongly non-abelian setting.” In recent years, Grandis homological categories have found applications in homological algebra and the theory of approximate representations of groups
[
1
,
2
]
.

The structure of the article is as follows.
In Section
2
, we recall the basic definitions concerning semiexact (ex1-categories), ex2-, and homological categories and prove that an ex2-category is homological if and only if the natural morphism from the left homology object to the right homology object is an isomorphism
(a generalization of
[
6

, which is a criterion for a Raikov semi-abelian category to be quasi-abelian). In Section
3
, we recall the construction of Lambek’s morphism
\Lambda
and some related facts. Section
4
is devoted to constructing and investigating Nomura’s null sequences (
3
) and (
4
) in a Grandis homological category; some related assertions are also proved.
