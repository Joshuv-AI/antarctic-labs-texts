48 C.F.R. § 1253.227-3 — 1253.227-3 Patent rights under Government contracts.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1253.227-3
Creator: Chapter 12—Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1253.227-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1253 (Forms). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following form is prescribed as a means for contractors to report inventions made during contract performance, as specified in 1227.305-4: Department of Defense DD Form 882, Report of Inventions and Subcontracts. DD Form 882 can be found at https://www.esd.whs.mil/Directives/forms/.
