7 U.S.C. § 1638d — Applicability
Citation: us:usc:7:1638d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1638d
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 38 (Distribution And Marketing Of Agricultural Products); Subchapter IV (Country Of Origin Labeling). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This subchapter shall apply to the retail sale of a covered commodity beginning September 30, 2008, except for “farm-raised fish” and “wild fish” which shall be September 30, 2004.
