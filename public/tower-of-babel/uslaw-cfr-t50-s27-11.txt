50 C.F.R. § 27.11 — Purpose of regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:50:27.11
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-27.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 27 (Prohibited Acts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part 27 govern those acts by the public which are prohibited at all times except as permitted in this part, part 26, and part 25, subpart D—Permits.
