7 C.F.R. § 4280.13 — Applicant eligibility.
Citation: us:cfr:7:4280.13
Creator: Rural Business-Cooperative Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-4280.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 4280 (Loans And Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applicants that are not delinquent on any Federal debt or otherwise disqualified from participation in these Programs are eligible to apply. An applicant must be eligible under 7 U.S.C. 940c.
