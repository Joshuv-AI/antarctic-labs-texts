41 C.F.R. § 102-71.85 — -71.85 Portable heaters, fans, and other such devices.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-71.85
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-71.85
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-71 (71—Real Property Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Federal agencies are prohibited from operating portable heaters, fans, and other such devices in Government-controlled facilities unless authorized by the Federal agency buildings manager.
