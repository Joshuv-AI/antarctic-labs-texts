18 U.S.C. § 1910 — Nepotism in appointment of receiver or trustee
Citation: us:usc:18:1910
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1910
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 93 (Public Officers And Employees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever, being a judge of any court of the United States, appoints as receiver, or trustee, any person related to such judge by consanguinity, or affinity, within the fourth degree—
Shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than five years, or both.
