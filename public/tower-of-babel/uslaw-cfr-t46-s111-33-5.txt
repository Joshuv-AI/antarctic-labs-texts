46 C.F.R. § 111.33-5 — -5 Installation.
Citation: us:cfr:46:111.33-5
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-111.33-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 111 (Electric Systems—General Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each semiconductor converter system must meet the installation requirements, as appropriate, of—
(a) Sections 4.31.19.2, 4.31.19.7, and 4.31.19.8 of IEEE 45.2-2011 (incorporated by reference; see § 110.10-1 of this subchapter); or
(b) IEC 60092-304:1980 (incorporated by reference; see § 110.10-1 of this subchapter).
