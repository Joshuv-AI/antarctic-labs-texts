43 U.S.C. § 1576 — Interagency cooperation
Citation: us:usc:43:1576
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section1576
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 32A (Colorado River Basin Salinity Control); Subchapter I (Programs Downstream From Imperial Dam). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In carrying out the provisions of this subchapter, the Secretary shall consult and cooperate with the Secretary of State, the Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency, the Secretary of Agriculture, and other affected Federal, State, and local agencies.
