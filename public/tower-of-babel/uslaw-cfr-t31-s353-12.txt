31 C.F.R. § 353.12 — Disposition of excess.
Citation: us:cfr:31:353.12
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-353.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 353 (Regulations Governing Definitive United States Savings Bonds, Series Ee And Hh). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If any person at any time has savings bonds issued during any one calendar year in excess of the prescribed amount, the Bureau of the Fiscal Service reserves the right to take any action that it deems necessary to adjust the excess. Instructions for adjustment of the excess can be obtained by email at savbonds@bpd.treas.gov or by writing to Bureau of the Fiscal Service, Parkersburg, WV 26106-1328.
