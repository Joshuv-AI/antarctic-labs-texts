43 C.F.R. § 49.520 — When will the Federal land manager issue a final assessment of civil penalty?
Citation: us:cfr:43:49.520
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-49.520
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 49 (Paleontological Resources Preservation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Federal land manager will issue a final assessment of civil penalty:
(a) If the person served with a notice of violation and proposed civil penalty does not file a timely objection; or
(b) If the person does file a timely objection that is denied in whole or in part under § 49.515.
