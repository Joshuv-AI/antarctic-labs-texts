18 C.F.R. § 5.7 — Tribal consultation.
Citation: us:cfr:18:5.7
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-5.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 5 (Integrated License Application Process). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A meeting shall be held no later than 30 days following filing of the notification of intent required by § 5.5 between each Indian tribe likely to be affected by the potential license application and the Commission staff if the affected Indian tribe agrees to such meeting.
