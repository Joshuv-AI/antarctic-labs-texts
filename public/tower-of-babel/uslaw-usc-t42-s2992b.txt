42 U.S.C. § 2992b — Administration
Citation: us:usc:42:2992b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2992b
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 34 (Economic Opportunity Program); Subchapter VIII (Native American Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subchapter shall be construed to prohibit interagency funding agreements made between the Administration and other agencies of the Federal Government for the development and implementation of specific grants or projects.
