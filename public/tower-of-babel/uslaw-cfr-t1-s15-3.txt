1 C.F.R. § 15.3 — Staff assistance.
Citation: us:cfr:1:15.3
Creator: Administrative Committee Of The Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-1/section-15.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 1 (General Provisions), Part 15 (Services To Federal Agencies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-23 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The staff of the Office of the Federal Register shall provide informal assistance and advice to officials of the various agencies with respect to general or specific programs of regulatory drafting, procedures, and promulgation practices.
