7 C.F.R. § 1775.23 — Grant servicing.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1775.23
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1775.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1775 (Technical Assistance Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Grants will be serviced in accordance with RUS Guide 1775-1 and subpart E of part 1951 of this title. When grants are terminated for cause, 7 CFR part 11 will be followed.
