38 U.S.C. § 3113 — Vocational rehabilitation for hospitalized members of the Armed Forces and veterans
Citation: us:usc:38:3113
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section3113
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part III (Readjustment And Related Benefits); Chapter 31 (Training And Rehabilitation For Veterans With Service-Connected Disabilities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Services and assistance may be provided under this chapter to a person described in subparagraphs (A)(ii) and (B) of section 3102(1) of this title who is hospitalized pending discharge from active military, naval, air, or space service. In such cases, no subsistence allowance shall be paid.
(b)
Services and assistance may be provided under this chapter to a veteran who is receiving care in a Department of Veterans Affairs hospital, nursing home, or domiciliary facility or in any other hospital or medical facility.
