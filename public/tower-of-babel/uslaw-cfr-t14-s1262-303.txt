14 C.F.R. § 1262.303 — Reply.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1262.303
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1262.303
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1262 (Equal Access To Justice Act In Agency Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within 15 calendar days after service of an answer, the applicant may file a reply. If the reply is based on any alleged facts not already in the record of the proceeding, the applicant shall include with the reply either supporting affidavits or a request for further proceedings under § 1262.306.
