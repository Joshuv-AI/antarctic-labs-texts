Orthogonalization and polarization of Yangians
Wolfgang Bock, Vyacheslav Futorny, Mikhail Neklyudov, Jian Zhang

Abstract
For every family of orthogonal polynomials, we define a new realization of the Yangian of 𝔤 ​ 𝔩 n {\mathfrak{gl}}_{n} . Except in the case of Dickson polynomials, the new realizations do not satisfy the RTT relation. We obtain an analogue of the Christoffel-Darboux formula. Similar construction can be made for any family of functions satisfying certain recurrence relations, for example, q q -Pochhhammer symbols and Bessel functions. Furthermore, using an analogue of the Jordan-Schwinger map, we define the ternary Yangian for a Lie algebra as a flat deformation of the current algebra of certain ternary extension of the given Lie algebra.

Introduction

Yangians were introduced by Drinfeld in 1985 in his seminal paper
[
2
]
following their exploration in the context of the inverse scattering method by Faddeev’s school
[
7
,
9
]
. Yangians constitute a family of quantum groups associated with rational solutions of the classical Yang–Baxter equation. For any simple finite-dimensional Lie algebra
\mathcal{A}
over the field complex numbers, the Yangian
Y(\mathcal{A})
corresponds to a canonical deformation of the universal enveloping algebra
U(\mathcal{A}[x])
for the polynomial current Lie algebra
\mathcal{A}[x]
.

In this paper, we introduce two constructions of families of algebras generalizing the Yangian of
{\mathfrak{gl}}_{n}
. The first procedure can be deduced from the elementary identity-motivating definition of the Yangian as follows.

Let
E:=\left(E_{ij}\right)_{i,j=1}^{n}
be the matrix whose entries are the standard matrix units

\{E_{ij}\}_{i,j=1}^{n}
of
Mat(n)
. Then we have following well known identity:

[(E^{r+1})_{ij},(E^{s})_{kl}]-[(E^{r})_{ij},(E^{s+1})_{kl}]=(E^{r})_{kj}(E^{s})_{il}-(E^{s})_{kj}(E^{r})_{il},i,j=1,\ldots,n,r,s\in\mathbb{N}\cup\{0\}.

(1.1)

Notice that by linearity we can extend it as follows

[((xf)(E))_{ij},(g(E))_{kl}]-[(f(E))_{ij},((xg)(E))_{kl}]=(f(E))_{kj}(g(E))_{il}-(g(E))_{kj}(f(E))_{il},

(1.2)

(
i,j,k,l=1,\ldots,n
) for arbitrary polynomials
f,g\in\mathbb{C}[x]
. Furthermore, the identity (
1.2
) is informally valid for arbitrary power series
f
and
g
under some natural assumptions. Choosing different sequences of functions
\{f_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}}
satisfying recurrence relations (i.e.
q
-Pochhhammer symbols, orthogonal polynomials etc.) we construct from identity (
1.2
) new realizations of Yangian
Y({\mathfrak{gl}}_{n})
or similar algebras. They in general do not satisfy the RTT relation, a notable exception is the case of Dickson polynomials (
[
3
]
) considered in Section
4
, where this method leads to, as long as authors aware, to the new rational solution of inhomogeneous Yang-Baxter equation.
The construction connects the theory of Yangians with the classical theory of orthogonal polynomials. As a result, we deduce an analogue of the Christoffel-Darboux formula based on the new realization of the Yangian.

The second construction presented in Section
6
is an analogue of the Jordan-Schwinger map (
[
1
]
) for the Yangian. In the case of the Jordan-Schwinger map, we start with a basis of
{\mathfrak{gl}}_{n}
(where
n
could be finite or infinite), and
then construct a realization of an arbitrary Lie algebra. Here, instead of the basis of
{\mathfrak{gl}}_{n}
we start with a basis of the Yangian
Y({\mathfrak{gl}}_{n})
. For an arbitrary Lie algebra
\mathcal{A}
of dimension
n
we linearly ¨deform¨ Yangian
Y({\mathfrak{gl}}_{n})
into the algebra
Yt(\mathcal{A})
. The surprising fact is that
Yt(\mathcal{A})
is a flat deformation of the universal enveloping algebra
U(\mathcal{A}^{tern}[x])
(see Theorem
6.15
), where
\mathcal{A}^{tern}
is a certain ternary extension of the initial Lie algebra
\mathcal{A}
.
In Example
6.12
we compute the extension for some low-dimensional algebras which are not necessarily simple. Thus, the algebra
Yt(\mathcal{A})
can be viewed as Yangian

, where
\mathcal{A}^{tern}
is not necessarily semisimple. Note that
Yt(\mathcal{A})
is not a Hopf algebra (unless
\mathcal{A}^{tern}={\mathfrak{gl}}_{m}
, for some

), but nevertheless we still have a notion of the evaluation homomorphism (Proposition

).

New realization of Yangian of
{\mathfrak{gl}}_{n}

Recall that the Yangian
Y({\mathfrak{gl}}_{n})
of
{\mathfrak{gl}}_{n}
is a unital algebra generated by

{t}_{ij}^{(r)}
,
1\leq i,j\leq n
,
r\in\mathbb{N}

with defining relations given by

[t^{(r+1)}_{ij},t^{(s)}_{kl}]-[t^{(r)}_{ij},t^{(s+1)}_{kl}]=t^{(r)}_{kj}t^{(s)}_{il}-t^{(s)}_{kj}t^{(r)}_{il},

(2.1)

where
r,s\in\mathbb{N}
, and

t^{(0)}_{ij}=\delta_{ij}
.

We define a family of algebras
OY({\mathfrak{gl}}_{n},a,b)

for arbitrary complex parameters
a=\{a_{m}\}_{m=0}^{\infty}
,
b=\{b_{m}\}_{m=0}^{\infty}
as follows.

Definition 2.1
.

Let
a=\{a_{m}\}_{m=0}^{\infty},b=\{b_{m}\}_{m=0}^{\infty}
be sequences of complex parameters and
OY({\mathfrak{gl}}_{n},a,b)
be a unital associative algebra over
\mathbb{C}
with generators

\tilde{t}_{ij}^{(r)},1\leq i,j\leq n,r\in\mathbb{N}

and defining relations given by

[\tilde{t}^{(r+1)}_{ij}+a_{r}\tilde{t}^{(r)}_{ij}+b_{r}\tilde{t}^{(r-1)}_{ij},\tilde{t}^{(s)}_{kl}]-[\tilde{t}^{(r)}_{ij},\tilde{t}^{(s+1)}_{kl}+a_{s}\tilde{t}^{(s)}_{kl}+b_{s}\tilde{t}^{(s-1)}_{kl}]=\tilde{t}^{(r)}_{kj}\tilde{t}^{(s)}_{il}-\tilde{t}^{(s)}_{kj}\tilde{t}^{(r)}_{il},

(2.2)

and

\tilde{t}_{ij}^{(0)}=\delta_{ij},\tilde{t}_{ij}^{(-1)}:=0,

where
i,j=1,\ldots,n,r,s\in\mathbb{N}\cup\{0\}
.

Remark 2.2
.

Classical Yangian relations correspond to the case
a_{n}=b_{n}=0

Generating function.

Consider the following generating function:

\tilde{T}_{ij}(u)=\delta_{ij}+\sum\limits_{r=1}^{\infty}\tilde{t}^{(r)}_{ij}u^{-r}

For a sequence
\{c_{n}\}_{n=0}^{\infty}
we define
the shifted sequence

s(c)=\{c_{n+1}\}_{n=0}^{\infty}

and the (pseudo) differential operator

Now we can rewrite (

\left(u+L^{a}_{u}+\frac{1}{u}L^{s(b)}_{u}-v-L^{a}_{v}-\frac{1}{v}L^{s(b)}_{v}\right)[\tilde{T}_{ij}(u),\tilde{T}_{kl}(v)]=\tilde{T}_{kj}(u)\tilde{T}_{il}(v)-\tilde{T}_{kj}(v)\tilde{T}_{il}(u),

(2.3)

where
i,j,k,l=1,\ldots,n
.

Realization.

Let
\{p_{m}\}_{m=0}^{\infty}

(2.4)

(2.5)

2.4

Then applying formula (

In the following we will denote
p_{l}^{m}
,
l=0,\ldots,m-1
the coefficients of polynomial

i.e.

p_{m}=x^{m}+\sum\limits_{l=0}^{m-1}p_{l}^{m}x^{l},m\geq 1.

From now on, unless otherwise explicitly stated, we will assume that
b_{n}\neq 0,n\in\mathbb{N}
, and, consequently, the polynomials

are orthogonal (with respect to some measure
\mu

For each integer

Proposition 4.6

Definition 6.1

Proposition 6.3

(Polarized ternary relation)
.

We have

(u-v)[\widetilde{G}_{ij}(A,u),\widetilde{G}_{kl}(B,v)]=\widetilde{G}_{il}(A,u)\widetilde{G}_{kj}(B,v)-\widetilde{G}_{il}(A,v)\widetilde{G}_{kj}(B,u).

\displaystyle(u-v)[\widetilde{G}_{ij}(A,u),

\displaystyle\widetilde{G}_{kl}(B,v)]=(u-v)\sum\limits_{r,s=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{u^{r}v^{s}}\sum\limits_{p,l=1}^{\infty}<Ae_{i},f_{l}><Be_{k},f_{p}>[t_{jm}^{(r)},t_{lp}^{(s)}]

(6.7)

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{r,s=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{u^{r}v^{s}}\sum\limits_{p,l=1}^{\infty}<Ae_{i},f_{l}><Be_{k},f_{p}>([t_{jm}^{(r+1)},t_{lp}^{(s)}]-[t_{jm}^{(r)},t_{lp}^{(s+1)}])

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{r,s=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{u^{r}v^{s}}\sum\limits_{p,l=1}^{\infty}<Ae_{i},f_{l}><Be_{k},f_{p}>(t^{(r)}_{lm}t^{(s)}_{jp}-t^{(s)}_{lm}t^{(r)}_{jp})
