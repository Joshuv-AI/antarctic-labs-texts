43 C.F.R. § 3602.22 — When will a contract terminate?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3602.22
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3602.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3600 (Mineral Materials Disposal). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Your contract terminates when—
(1) Its term expires;
(2) You have completed production under your contract or permit and any renewal, and completed required reclamation; or
(3) BLM cancels your contract under § 3601.60 et seq. of this part.
(b) You and BLM may, by agreement, terminate the sales contract at any time.
