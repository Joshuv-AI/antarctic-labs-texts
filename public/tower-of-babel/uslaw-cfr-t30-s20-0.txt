30 C.F.R. § 20.0 — Compliance with the requirements necessary for obtaining approval.
Citation: us:cfr:30:20.0
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-20.0
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 20 (Electric Mine Lamps Other Than Standard Cap Lamps). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To receive approval of MSHA for any electric mine lamps other than standard cap lamps a manufacturer must comply with the requirements specified in this part.
