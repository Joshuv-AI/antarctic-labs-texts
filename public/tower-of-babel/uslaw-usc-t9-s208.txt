9 U.S.C. § 208 — Application
Citation: us:usc:9:208
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title9-section208
Context: United States Code, Title 9 (Arbitration); Chapter 2 (Convention On The Recognition And Enforcement Of Foreign Arbitral Awards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Chapter 1 applies to actions and proceedings brought under this chapter to the extent that chapter is not in conflict with this chapter or the Convention as ratified by the United States. This chapter applies to the extent that this chapter is not in conflict with chapter 4.
