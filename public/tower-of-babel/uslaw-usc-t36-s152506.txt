36 U.S.C. § 152506 — Exclusive right to name, seals, emblems, and badges
Citation: us:usc:36:152506
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section152506
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1525 (National Safety Council). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation and its subordinate divisions and regional, State, and local chapters have the exclusive right to use the name “National Safety Council”. The corporation has the exclusive right to use and to allow others to use seals, emblems, and badges the corporation adopts. This section does not affect any vested rights.
