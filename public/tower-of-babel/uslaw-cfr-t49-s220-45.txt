49 C.F.R. § 220.45 — Radio communication shall be complete.
Citation: us:cfr:49:220.45
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-220.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 220 (Railroad Communications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any radio communication which is not fully understood or completed in accordance with the requirements of this part and the operating rules of the railroad, shall not be acted upon and shall be treated as though not sent.
