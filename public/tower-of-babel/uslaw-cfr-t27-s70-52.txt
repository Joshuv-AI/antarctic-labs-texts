27 C.F.R. § 70.52 — Signature presumed authentic.
Citation: us:cfr:27:70.52
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-70.52
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 70 (Procedure And Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An individual's name signed to a return, statement, or other document shall be prima facie evidence for all purposes that the return, statement or other document was actually signed by that individual.
