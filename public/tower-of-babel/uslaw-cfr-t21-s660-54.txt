21 C.F.R. § 660.54 — Potency tests, specificity tests, tests for heterospecific antibodies, and additional tests for nonspecific properties.
Citation: us:cfr:21:660.54
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-660.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 660 (Additional Standards For Diagnostic Substances For Laboratory Tests). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following tests shall be performed using test procedures approved by the Director, Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research:
(a) Potency tests for determining anti-IgG and anti-complement activity.
(b) Specificity tests, tests for heterospecific antibodies, and additional tests for nonspecific properties.
