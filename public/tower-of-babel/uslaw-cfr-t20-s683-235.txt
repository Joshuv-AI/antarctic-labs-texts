20 C.F.R. § 683.235 — May Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act title I funds be spent for construction?
Citation: us:cfr:20:683.235
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-683.235
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 683 (Administrative Provisions Under Title I Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

WIOA title I funds must not be spent on construction, purchase of facilities or buildings, or other capital expenditures for improvements to land or buildings, except with the prior written approval of the Secretary.
