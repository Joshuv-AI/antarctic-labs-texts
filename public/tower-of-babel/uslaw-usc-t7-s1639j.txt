7 U.S.C. § 1639j — Exclusion from Federal preemption
Citation: us:usc:7:1639j
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1639j
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 38 (Distribution And Marketing Of Agricultural Products); Subchapter VI (Labeling Of Certain Food). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subchapter, subchapter V, or any regulation, rule, or requirement promulgated in accordance with this subchapter or subchapter V shall be construed to preempt any remedy created by a State or Federal statutory or common law right.
