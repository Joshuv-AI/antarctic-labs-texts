42 C.F.R. § 88.25 — Reopening of WTC Health Program final decisions.
Citation: us:cfr:42:88.25
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-88.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 88 (World Trade Center Health Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At any time, and without regard to whether new evidence or information is provided or obtained, the Administrator of the WTC Health Program may reopen any final decision made by the WTC Health Program pursuant to the provisions of this part. The Administrator may affirm, vacate, or modify such decision, or take any other action he or she deems appropriate.
