22 C.F.R. § 901.22 — Representative.
Citation: us:cfr:22:901.22
Creator: Foreign Service Grievance Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-901.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 901 (General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Representative means the person(s) identified in writing to the Board as assisting the party or parties in the presentation of the case.
