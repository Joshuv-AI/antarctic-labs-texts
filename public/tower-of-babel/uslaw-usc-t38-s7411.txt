38 U.S.C. § 7411 — Full-time board-certified physicians and dentists: reimbursement of continuing professional education expenses
Citation: us:usc:38:7411
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section7411
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part V (Boards, Administrations, And Services); Chapter 74 (Veterans Health Administration—Personnel); Subchapter I (Appointments). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall reimburse any full-time board-certified physician or dentist appointed under section 7401(1) of this title for expenses incurred, up to $1,000 per year, for continuing professional education.
