36 C.F.R. § 1001.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1001.1
Creator: Presidio Trust
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1001.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1001 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The regulations in this chapter provide for the proper use, management, government and protection of persons, property and natural and cultural resources within the area under the jurisdiction of the Presidio Trust.
(b) The regulations in this chapter will be utilized to fulfill the statutory purposes of the Presidio Trust Act.
