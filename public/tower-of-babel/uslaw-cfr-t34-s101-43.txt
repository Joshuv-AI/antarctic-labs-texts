34 C.F.R. § 101.43 — Reduction of time to file documents.
Citation: us:cfr:34:101.43
Creator: Office For Civil Rights, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-101.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 101 (Practice And Procedure For Hearings Under Part 100 Of This Title). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For good cause, the reviewing authority or the presiding officer, with respect to matters pending before them, may reduce any time limit prescribed by the rules in this part, except as provided by law or in part 100 of this chapter.
