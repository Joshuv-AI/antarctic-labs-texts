42 U.S.C. § 300a–2 — Conduct, etc., of research activities
Citation: us:usc:42:300a–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section300a–2
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter VIII (Population Research And Voluntary Family Planning Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may—
(1)
conduct, and
(2)
make grants to public or nonprofit private entities and enter into contracts with public or private entities and individuals for projects for,
research in the biomedical, contraceptive development, behavioral, and program implementation fields related to family planning and population.
