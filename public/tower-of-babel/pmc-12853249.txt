Recognition and appreciation of our peer reviewers: honoring the 2025 best reviewer awards
Authors: JUNG-EUN CHEON
Published: 2026

The Ultrasonography extends its sincere gratitude to all of our peer reviewers for generously dedicating their time and expertise to uphold the high standards of quality that define our journal. I am especially delighted to recognize a select group of individuals who have demonstrated exceptional commitment through their prompt, thorough, and scholarly reviews. These distinguished reviewers are the recipients of the 2025 Best Reviewer Awards, which are given based on the quality, timeliness, and volume of reviews completed throughout the year. Each awardee will be presented with a certificate and a letter of congratulations. In addition to these exceptional individuals, the Korean Society of Ultrasound in Medicine and the Ultrasonography Editorial Board wish to acknowledge all of our reviewers, who represent some of the most meticulous and dedicated contributors in the field of medical journals.
