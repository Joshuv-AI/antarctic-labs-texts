46 C.F.R. § 59.10-30 — -30 Seal welding.
Citation: us:cfr:46:59.10-30
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-59.10-30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 59 (Repairs To Boilers, Pressure Vessels, And Appurtenances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where leaks occur in riveted joints or connections, they must be carefully investigated to determine the cause. Such leaks may be made tight by seal welding the edge, if accepted by the Officer in Charge, Marine Inspection.
