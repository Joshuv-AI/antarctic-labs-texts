48 C.F.R. § 9.407-5 — 9.407-5 Scope of suspension.
Citation: us:cfr:48:9.407-5
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-9.407-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 9 (Contractor Qualifications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The scope of suspension shall be the same as that for debarment (see 9.406-5), except that the procedures of 9.407-3 shall be used in imposing suspension.
