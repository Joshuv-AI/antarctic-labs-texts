24 C.F.R. § 58.36 — Environmental assessments.
Citation: us:cfr:24:58.36
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-58.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 58 (Environmental Review Procedures For Entities Assuming Hud Environmental Responsibilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a project is not exempt or categorically excluded under §§ 58.34 and 58.35, the responsible entity must prepare an EA in accordance with subpart E of this part. If it is evident without preparing an EA that an EIS is required under § 58.37, the responsible entity should proceed directly to an EIS.
