15 U.S.C. § 3059 — Unfair or deceptive acts or practices
Citation: us:usc:15:3059
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section3059
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 57A (Horseracing Integrity And Safety). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The sale of a covered horse, or of any other horse in anticipation of its future participation in a covered race, shall be considered an unfair or deceptive act or practice in or affecting commerce under section 45(a) of this title if the seller—
(1)
knows or has reason to know the horse has been administered—
(A)
a bisphosphonate prior to the horse’s fourth birthday; or
(B)
any other substance or method the Authority determines has a long-term degrading effect on the soundness of the covered horse; and
(2)
fails to disclose to the buyer the administration of the bisphosphonate or other substance or method described in paragraph (1)(B).
