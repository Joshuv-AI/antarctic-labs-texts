34 C.F.R. § 76.202 — Opportunity for a hearing before a State plan is disapproved.
Citation: us:cfr:34:76.202
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-76.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 76 (State-Administered Formula Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary may disapprove a State plan only after:
(a) Notifying the State;
(b) Offering the State a reasonable opportunity for a hearing; and
(c) Holding the hearing, if requested by the State.
