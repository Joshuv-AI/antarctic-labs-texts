39 C.F.R. § 762.21 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:39:762.21
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-762.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 762 (Disbursement Postal Money Orders). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this subpart prescribe the requirements for endorsement and the conditions for payment of Disbursement Postal Money Orders drawn by the Postal Service.
