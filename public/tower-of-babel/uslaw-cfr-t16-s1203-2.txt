16 C.F.R. § 1203.2 — Purpose and basis.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1203.2
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1203.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1203 (Safety Standard For Bicycle Helmets). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose and basis of this standard is to reduce the likelihood of serious injury and death to bicyclists resulting from impacts to the head, pursuant to 15 U.S.C. 6001-6006.
