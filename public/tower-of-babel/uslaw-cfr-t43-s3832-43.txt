43 C.F.R. § 3832.43 — How may I use a tunnel site?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3832.43
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3832.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3832 (Locating Mining Claims Or Sites). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may use the tunnel site for subsurface access to a lode claim or to explore for and acquire previously unknown lodes, veins, or ledges within the confines of the tunnel site.
