49 U.S.C. § 60501 — Secretary of Energy
Citation: us:usc:49:60501
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section60501
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Chapter 605 (Interstate Commerce Regulation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as provided in section 60502 of this title, the Secretary of Energy has the duties and powers related to the transportation of oil by pipeline that were vested on October 1, 1977, in the Interstate Commerce Commission or the chairman or a member of the Commission.
