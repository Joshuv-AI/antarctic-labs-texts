46 C.F.R. § 5.67 — Physician-patient privilege.
Citation: us:cfr:46:5.67
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-5.67
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 5 (Marine Investigation Regulations—Personnel Action). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the purpose of these proceedings, the physician-patient privilege does not exist between a physician and a respondent.
