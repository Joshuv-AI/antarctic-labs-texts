15 U.S.C. § 1141m — Incontestability
Citation: us:usc:15:1141m
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1141m
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 22 (Trademarks); Subchapter IV (The Madrid Protocol). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The period of continuous use prescribed under section 1065 of this title for a mark covered by an extension of protection issued under this subchapter may begin no earlier than the date on which the Director issues the certificate of the extension of protection under section 1141i of this title, except as provided in section 1141n of this title.
