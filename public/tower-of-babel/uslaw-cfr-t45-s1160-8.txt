45 C.F.R. § 1160.8 — Indemnity agreement.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1160.8
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1160.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1160 (Indemnities Under The Arts And Artifacts Indemnity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In cases where the requirements of §§ 1160.4 and 1160.5 have been met to the satisfaction of the Council, an Indemnity Agreement pledging the full faith and credit of the United States for the agreed value of the exhibition in question may be issued to the indemnitee by the Council, subject to the provisions of § 1160.7.
