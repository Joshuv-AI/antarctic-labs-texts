26 C.F.R. § 301.6064-1 — -1 Signature presumed authentic.
Citation: us:cfr:26:301.6064-1
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-301.6064-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 301 (Procedure And Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An individual's name signed to a return, statement, or other document shall be prima facie evidence for all purposes that the return, statement, or other document was actually signed by him.
