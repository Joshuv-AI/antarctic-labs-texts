28 C.F.R. § 34.101 — Exceptions to applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:28:34.101
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-34.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 34 (Ojjdp Competition And Peer Review Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The assistance and procurement contract situations specified in § 34.2 (c), (d), (e), (f), and (g) of subpart A of this part are considered by OJJDP to be outside the scope of the section 262(d) peer review requirement as set forth in this subpart.
