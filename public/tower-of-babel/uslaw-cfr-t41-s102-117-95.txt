41 C.F.R. § 102-117.95 — -117.95 Agency responsibilities after shipping HHG.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-117.95
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-117.95
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-117 (117—Transportation Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each agency develops an evaluation survey for the employee to complete following the move.
(b) When using GSA's Centralized Household Goods Traffic Management Program (CHAMP), agencies must instruct the employee to complete their portion of the GSA Form 3080, Household Goods Carrier Evaluation Report, using the link provided by the TSP.
