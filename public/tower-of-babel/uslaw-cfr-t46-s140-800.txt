46 C.F.R. § 140.800 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:46:140.800
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-140.800
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 140 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to all towing vessels unless otherwise specified. Certain vessels are also subject to the navigation safety regulations in 33 CFR parts 163 and 164.
