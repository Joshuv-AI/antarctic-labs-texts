30 C.F.R. § 256.70 — Extension of lease by drilling or well reworking operations.
Citation: us:cfr:30:256.70
Creator: Bureau Of Safety And Environmental Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-256.70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 256 (Leasing Of Sulphur Or Oil And Gas In The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term of a lease shall be extended beyond the primary term so long as drilling or well reworking operations are approved by the Secretary according to the conditions set forth in 30 CFR 250.180.
