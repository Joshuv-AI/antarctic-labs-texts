40 C.F.R. § 18.11 — Standards of Conduct and Financial Disclosure.
Citation: us:cfr:40:18.11
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-18.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 18 (Environmental Protection Research Fellowships And Special Research Consultants For Environmental Protection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All individuals appointed to an Environmental Protection Research Fellowship or as a Special Research Consultant shall be subject to the same current standards and disclosure regulations and requirements as Title 5 appointees.
