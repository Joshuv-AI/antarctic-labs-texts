12 C.F.R. § 341.6 — Reports.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 341 (Registration Of Securities Transfer Agents). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every registration or amendment filed under this section shall constitute a report or application within the meaning or sections 17, 17A(c), and 32(a) of the Act.
