45 C.F.R. § 2106.4 — Requests for copies of records.

Creator: Commission Of Fine Arts
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2106 (RULES FOR COMPLIANCE WITH 5 U.S.C. 552a, THE PRIVACY ACT OF 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Rules governing requests for copies of records are the same as those for the granting of access to the records outlined in § 2106.2 of this part (see also § 2106.7 for rules regarding fees).
