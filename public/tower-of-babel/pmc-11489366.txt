Retraction Note: Wilms tumor-suppressing peptide inhibits proliferation and induces apoptosis of Wilms tumor cells in vitro and in vivo
Authors: Wei Zhao, Juan Li, Ping Li, Fei Guo, Pengfei Gao, Junjie Zhang, Zechen Yan, Lei Wang, Da Zhang, Pan Qin, Guoqiang Zhao, Jiaxiang Wang
Published: 2024

Retraction Note: Journal of Cancer Research and Clinical Oncology (2019) 145:2457–2468

10.1007/s00432-019-03003-0

The Editor-in-Chief has retracted this article after concerns were raised about the data reported. In Figure 4, each of the flow cytometry plots for IC80 and IC50 WTSP-treated conditions appear to feature recurring patterns. The Editor-in-Chief no longer has confidence in the reliability of the results or findings of this article.

The authors did respond to correspondence from the Publisher regarding this retraction.
