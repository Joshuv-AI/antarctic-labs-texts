32 C.F.R. § 34.18 — Fee and profit.
Citation: us:cfr:32:34.18
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-34.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 34 (Administrative Requirements For Grants And Agreements With For-Profit Organizations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In accordance with 32 CFR 22.205(b), grants and cooperative agreements shall not:
(a) Provide for the payment of fee or profit to the recipient.
(b) Be used to carry out programs where fee or profit is necessary to achieving program objectives.
