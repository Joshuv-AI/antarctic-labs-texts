14 C.F.R. § 171.251 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:14:171.251
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-171.251
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 171 (Non-Federal Navigation Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart sets forth minimum requirements for the approval and operation of non-Federal Interim Standard Microwave Landing System (ISMLS) facilities that are to be involved in the approval of instrument flight rules and air traffic control procedures related to those facilities.
