18 C.F.R. § 706.403 — Form of statements.
Citation: us:cfr:18:706.403
Creator: Water Resources Council
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-706.403
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 706 (Employee Responsibilities And Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee required to submit a statement of employment and financial interests shall submit that statement in the format prescribed by the Division of Management.
