12 U.S.C. § 4246 — Awards of costs and fees to prevailing plaintiff
Citation: us:usc:12:4246
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section4246
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 43 (Actions Against Persons Committing Bank Fraud Crimes); Subchapter III (Use Of Private Legal Resources). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When the United States, through private counsel retained under this subchapter, prevails in any civil action, the court, in its discretion, may allow the United States reasonable attorney’s fees and other expenses of litigation as part of the costs.
