28 U.S.C. § 568 — Practice of law prohibited
Citation: us:usc:28:568
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section568
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part II (Department Of Justice); Chapter 37 (United States Marshals Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A United States marshal or deputy marshal may not practice law in any court of the United States.
