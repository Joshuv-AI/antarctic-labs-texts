43 U.S.C. § 938 — Lands excepted
Citation: us:usc:43:938
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section938
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 22 (Rights-Of-Way And Other Easements In Public Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Sections 934 to 939 of this title shall not apply to any lands within the limits of any military, park, or Indian reservation, or other lands especially reserved from sale, unless such right of way shall be provided for by treaty-stipulation or by Act of Congress passed prior to March 3, 1875.
