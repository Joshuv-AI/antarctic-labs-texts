32 C.F.R. § 631.12 — Objectives.
Citation: us:cfr:32:631.12
Creator: Department Of The Army
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-631.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 631 (Armed Forces Disciplinary Control Boards And Off-Installation Liaison And Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The primary objectives of off-installation operations are to—
(a) Render assistance and provide information to Service members.
(b) Preserve the safety, and security of service members.
(c) Preserve good order and discipline among Service members and reduce off-installation incidents and offenses.
(d) Maintain effective cooperation with civil authorities, and community leaders.
