31 C.F.R. § 29.311 — Credit only for service performed on or before June 30, 1997.
Citation: us:cfr:31:29.311
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-29.311
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 29 (Federal Benefit Payments Under Certain District Of Columbia Retirement Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Only service performed on or before June 30, 1997, is credited toward Federal Benefit Payments.
