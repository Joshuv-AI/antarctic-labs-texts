33 C.F.R. § 20.801 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:33:20.801
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-20.801
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 20 (Rules Of Practice, Procedure, And Evidence For Formal Administrative Proceedings Of The Coast Guard). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any party may present his or her case or defense by oral, documentary, or demonstrative evidence; submit rebuttal evidence; and conduct any cross-examination that may be necessary for a full and true disclosure of the facts.
