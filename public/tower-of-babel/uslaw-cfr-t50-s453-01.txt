50 C.F.R. § 453.01 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:50:453.01

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 453 (Endangered Species Committee). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes the procedures to be used by the Endangered Species Committee when examining applications for exemption from section 7(a)(2) of the Endangered Species Act of 1973, as amended.
