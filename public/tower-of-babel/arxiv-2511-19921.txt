A power-saving error term in counting C 2 ≀ H C_{2}\wr H extensions of an arbitrary base field parametrized by discriminants
Arijit Chakraborty

Abstract
We study Malle’s conjecture for the group C 2 ≀ H C_{2}\wr H where H H is a permutation group. Malle’s conjecture for this case was proved by Jürgen Klüners in [ 7 ] under mild conditions for H H . In this article, we provide an alternative method to obtain the explicit main term and a power-saving error term for C 2 ≀ H C_{2}\wr H extensions of an arbitrary number field. Furthermore, our method allows us to relax the assumptions for H . H.



1
Introduction

One of the most important problems in arithmetic statistics is to count the number of number field extensions of an arbitrary base field with bounded discriminant, and the Galois group being isomorphic to a fixed permutation group
G
. To be precise, we ask the following question:

Question.
Fix a number field
F
and an algebraic closure
\overline{\mathbb{Q}}
of
\mathbb{Q}
containing
F.
For a field extension
K/F
of degree
n
, let
\widetilde{K}
denote the Galois closure of
K/F.
The Galois group
\mathrm{Gal}(\widetilde{K}/F)
together with the action of permuting the
n
embeddings of
K
into
\overline{\mathbb{Q}}
can be viewed as a transitive permutation subgroup of
S_{n}.
Suppose
G
is a transitive permutation group of order
n.
Fix an embedding
G\hookrightarrow S_{n}.
We introduce the following notations:

\displaystyle\begin{split}\mathcal{F}_{n}(F,\,G)\coloneqq\{K/F\mid[K\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}F]=n,\,\mathrm{Gal}(\widetilde{K}/F)\cong G\}\end{split}

and

\displaystyle\begin{split}\mathcal{F}_{n}(F,\,G,\,X)\coloneqq\{K/F\mid[K\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}F]=n,\,\mathrm{Gal}(\widetilde{K}/F)\cong G,\,|\mathrm{Nm}_{F/\mathbb{Q}}(\mathrm{Disc}({K/F})|\leq X\}.\end{split}

How does
\#\mathcal{F}_{n}(F,\,G,\,X)
grow as
X\to\infty
?

In this article, we will focus on the case where
G\cong C_{2}\wr H
for any transitive permutation group
H
. In 2012, Jürgen Klüners
[
7
]
proved that the counting function
\#\mathcal{F}_{n}(F,\,C_{2}\wr H,\,X)
is asymptotic to
c(F,C_{2}\wr H)X
for some positive constant
c(F,C_{2}\wr H)>0
assuming mild conditions on
H
. We restate the main result of
[
7
]
here.

Theorem 1.1

(Klüners, 2012)
.

Let
H
is a transitive subgroup of
S_{n}
and fix an embedding
H\hookrightarrow S_{n}.
Assume that there exists at least one extension
E
of
F
of degree
n
such that
\mathrm{Gal}(\widetilde{E}/F)\cong H
. Furthermore, assume that the following estimate holds

\displaystyle\#\mathcal{F}_{n}(F,\,H,\,X)=\text{O}_{F,H,\epsilon}(X^{1+\epsilon}).

Then,

\displaystyle\#\mathcal{F}_{2n}(F,\,C_{2}\wr H,\,X)\sim c(F,C_{2}\wr H)X

where the constant
c(F,H)
is given by

\displaystyle c(F,C_{2}\wr H)=\frac{1}{\alpha(H)}\sum_{K\in\mathcal{F}_{n}(F,\,H)}\frac{1}{2^{i(K)}}\cdot\frac{\mathrm{Res}_{s=1}\zeta_{K}(s)}{\zeta_{K}(2)}\cdot\frac{1}{|\mathrm{Nm}_{F/\mathbb{Q}}(\mathrm{Disc}({K/F})|^{2}},

(1.2)

where
\zeta_{K}(s)
denotes the Dedekind zeta function associated with
K
,
i(K)
is the number of pairs of complex conjugate embeddings of
K
in
\overline{\mathbb{Q}}
, and
\alpha(H)
is a positive integer depending only on
H
.

This result was subsequently strengthened by the work of Brandon Alberts, Robert J. Lemke Oliver, Jiuya Wang, and Melanie Matchett Wood
[
1
]
. In particular, Corollary 1.4 of
[
1
]
relaxes the hypothesis on
H
to
\#\mathcal{F}_{n}(F,\,G,\,X)\ll X^{\frac{3}{2}-\delta}
for some
\delta>0.

Once we know the asymptotic behavior of the counting function
\#\mathcal{F}_{n}(F,\,G,\,X)
, a natural question arises: How rapidly does this counting function
\#\mathcal{F}_{n}(F,\,G,\,X)
approach the asymptotic? In other words, can we bound the difference between the actual count and the asymptotic? We refer to this difference as the error term. The method used by Klüners can do slightly better than the main term. Even though the quality of the error term is not mentioned explicitly, an application of a suitable Tauberian theorem would yield an error term of
\text{O}(X^{\frac{5}{6}+\epsilon})
. In this article, we provide an improved error term for this counting function.

Prior to this, in the case
H=C_{2}
(so that
G\cong C_{2}\wr C_{2}\cong D_{4}
), Henri Cohen, Francisco Diaz Y Diaz, and Michel Olivier
[
5
]
proved the asymptotic formula for the counting function
\#\mathcal{F}_{4}(F,\,D_{4},\,X)
obtaining a power saving error term of
\text{O}(X^{\frac{3}{4}+\epsilon}).

Theorem 1.3

(Cohen, Diaz Y Diaz, Olivier; 2001)
.

The number
\mathcal{F}_{4}(\mathbb{Q},\,D_{4},\,X)
of quartic
D_{4}
extensions of
\mathbb{Q}
up to isomorphism is asymptotic to
c(\mathbb{Q},D_{4})X
, i.e.,

\#\mathcal{F}_{4}(\mathbb{Q},\,D_{4},\,X)=c(\mathbb{Q},D_{4})X+\text{O}(X^{\frac{3}{4}+\epsilon}),

with the constant
c(\mathbb{Q},D_{4})
given as follows:

c(\mathbb{Q},D_{4})=\frac{1}{2}\sum_{K\in\mathcal{F}_{2}(\mathbb{Q},\,C_{2})}\frac{2^{-i(K)}}{\mathrm{Disc}(K)^{2}}\cdot\frac{\mathrm{Res}_{s=1}\zeta_{K}(s)}{\zeta_{K}(2)},

where
\zeta_{K}(s)
denotes the Dedekind zeta function associated with
K
, and
i(K)
is the number of pairs of complex conjugate embeddings of
K
in
\overline{\mathbb{Q}}
.

However, in the quartic
D_{4}
case, assuming the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis, one gets an error term of
\text{O}(X^{\frac{1}{2}+\epsilon})
. To this end, in 2024 , Kevin J. McGown and Amanda Tucker
[
11
]
improved the error term in the count of quartic
D_{4}
field extensions without assuming GRH. They proved the following theorem:

Theorem 1.4

(McGown, Tucker; 2024)
.

\displaystyle\#\mathcal{F}_{4}(\mathbb{Q},\,D_{4},\,X)=

\displaystyle c(\mathbb{Q},D_{4})X+\text{O}(X^{\frac{5}{8}+\epsilon}).

In this article, we extend the work of McGown and Tucker to obtain a better power-saving error term in counting
C_{2}\wr H
field extensions over an arbitrary base field
F.
The following is our main theorem that gives power saving error term for the counting function
\#\mathcal{F}_{2n}(F,\,C_{2}\wr H,\,X)
:

Theorem 1.5
.

Let
F
be a number field of degree
m
. Let
H
be a transitive subgroup of
S_{n}
and fix an embedding
H\hookrightarrow S_{n}.
Assume that there exists at least one extension
E
of
F
of degree
n
such that
\mathrm{Gal}(\widetilde{E}/F)\cong H
. Furthermore, assume

\#\mathcal{F}_{n}(F,\,H,\,X)\ll X^{a},

where
a\leq\frac{3}{2}
is a constant.

(i)

If
a\leq\frac{3}{2}-\frac{9mn-1}{2mn(mn+1)}
then we have

\#\mathcal{F}_{2n}(F,\,C_{2}\wr H,\,X)=c(F,C_{2}\wr H)X+\text{O}(X^{1-\frac{2}{mn+1}+\epsilon})+\text{O}(X^{\frac{3}{4}+\epsilon}).

(ii)

If
\frac{3}{2}-\frac{9mn-1}{2mn(mn+1)}<a\leq\frac{3}{2}
then we have

\displaystyle\#\mathcal{F}_{2n}(F,\,C_{2}\wr H,\,X)=c(F,C_{2}\wr H)X+\text{O}(X^{\frac{2}{5}(1+a-\frac{1}{2mn})+\epsilon})+\text{O}(X^{\frac{3}{4}+\epsilon}).

Remark 1.6
.

(i)

Our result proves Conjecture
1.10
with a weaker hypothesis on
H
than Corollary 1.4,
[
1
]
. It only requires
\#\mathcal{F}_{n}(F,\,H,\,X)\ll X^{\frac{3}{2}}
, instead of a power savings from this.

(ii)

This result provides a better power-saving error term than Klüners’s work
[
7
]
. For a given base field
F
and a given permutation group
H
, the best possible scenario is case (i) of this theorem, i.e, when the bound for
\mathcal{F}_{n}(F,\,H,\,X)\ll X^{a}
is such that
a\leq\frac{3}{2}-\frac{9mn-1}{2mn(mn+1)}
. If the upper bound for
\mathcal{F}_{n}(F,\,H,\,X)
is worse, then we will be reduced to case (ii). As a result, it fails to provide a better power-saving error term than the one expected from
[
7
]
for
mn\geq 11.

We provide a recipe to obtain even better power-saving error term for
H
being certain small groups. We demonstrate this by considering the special case
H\cong S_{3}
viewed as a transitive subgroup of
S_{6}
via the regular representation. We prove the following result:

Theorem 1.7
.

We have

\#\mathcal{F}_{12}(\mathbb{Q},\,C_{2}\wr S_{3},\,X)=c(\mathbb{Q},C_{2}\wr S_{3})X+\text{O}(X^{\frac{5}{7}+\epsilon})

where
c(F,C_{2}\wr S_{3})
is given by equation (
1.2
).

For
H\cong C_{3}
viewed as a subgroup of
S_{3}
via the regular representation, we obtained an even better power-saving error term using better bounds for the size of the
2
-torsion subgroup of the class group. In this case, we prove the following theorem:

Theorem 1.8
.

We have

\#\mathcal{F}_{6}(\mathbb{Q},\,C_{2}\wr C_{3},\,X)=c(\mathbb{Q},C_{2}\wr C_{3})X+\text{O}(X^{\frac{2(1+\delta)}{5}+\epsilon}),

where
\delta
is such that
|\mathrm{Cl}_{K}[2]|\ll|\mathrm{Disc}(K)|^{\delta+\epsilon}
for each
K\in\mathcal{F}_{3}(\mathbb{Q},\,C_{3})
. The value of
c(\mathbb{Q},C_{2}\wr C_{3})
is given as follows:

c(\mathbb{Q},C_{2}\wr C_{3})=\sum_{K\in\mathcal{F}_{3}(\mathbb{Q},\,C_{3})}\frac{1}{2^{i(K)}}\cdot\frac{\mathrm{Res}_{s=1}\zeta_{K}(s)}{\zeta_{K}(2)}\cdot\frac{1}{|\mathrm{Disc}(K)|^{2}}.

Remark 1.9
.

Using the value of
\delta=0.2784\dots
given in
[
2
]
, we get the error term in Theorem
1.8
for the group
C_{2}\wr C_{3}
is
\text{O}(X^{0.51136\dots+\epsilon})
, which is much better than the expected error term of
\text{O}(X^{\frac{5}{6}+\epsilon})
from
[
7
]
, and it is also much better than the error term
\text{O}(X^{\frac{3}{4}+\epsilon})
provided by Cohen, Diaz Y Diaz, and Olivier in
[
5
]

for the quartic
D_{4}(\cong C_{2}\wr C_{2})
case. Additionally, if one assumes the
2
-torsion Conjecture, i.e,
\#\mathrm{Cl}_{K}[2]\ll|\mathrm{Disc}(K)|^{\epsilon}
for
\epsilon
arbitrarily small, it would yield an improved error term of
\text{O}(X^{\frac{1}{2}+\epsilon})
.

1.1
Brief historical review: Malle’s conjecture

In
[
9
]
,
[
10
]
, Gunter Malle made a general prediction for the asymptotic growth of the counting function
\#\mathcal{F}_{n}(F,\,G,\,X)
, which leads to the following conjecture

Conjecture 1.10
.

Let
F
be a number field and
G
is a transitive subgroup of
S_{n}.
Then, there exist positive constants
a,\,b,\,c
depending on
F
, and
G
such that

\#\mathcal{F}_{n}(F,\,G,\,X)\sim cX^{a}(\log(X))^{b}.

We introduce the following definition

Definition 1.11
.

Let
G\leq S_{n}
be a transitive subgroup acting on the set
\Omega=\{1,2,\dots,n\}.

1.

For
g\in G
we define the index of
g
to be

\mathrm{\mathrm{ind}}(g)\coloneqq n-\text{number of orbits of }g\text{ in }\Omega.

2.

\mathrm{\mathrm{ind}}(G)\coloneqq\min\{\mathrm{\mathrm{ind}}(g)\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}1\neq g\in G\}.

3.

a(G)\coloneqq\mathrm{\mathrm{ind}}(G)^{-1}.

The constant
a(G)
is known as Malle’s constant. Malle’s predicted value of the constant
a
in Conjecture
1.10
is this
a(G).
Malle also proposed a value of the constant
b
. However, Jürgen Klüners
[
6
]
produced a counterexample demonstrating that the proposed value of
b
cannot hold in general. We also remark that Conjecture
1.10
was known in several cases prior to Malle’s prediction, most notably, David J. Wright
[
12
]
established Conjecture
1.10
for all abelian groups. For a comprehensive historical review of the number field counting problem, we refer the reader to
[
4
]
.

For any group
A

, we denote

Let

.

We use

E
, and

denotes the
m
-torsion subgroup of the class group.

We denote by

.

For an extension of number fields

,

We write

Furthermore, we write

, for some constants
C
and

Counting

-extensions of a number field

Suppose the degree of the base field
F

m=[F\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}\mathbb{Q}].

\#\mathcal{F}_{n}(F,\,H,\,X)\ll X^{a},

a\leq\frac{3}{2}

\mathrm{Gal}(L/K)=C_{2}

\displaystyle\sum_{K\in\mathcal{F}_{n}(F,\,H,\,X^{1/2})}\sum_{L\in\mathcal{F}_{2}(K,\,C_{2},\,X/|\mathrm{Nm}_{F/\mathbb{Q}}(\mathrm{Disc}({K/F})|^{2})}1=

\displaystyle\alpha(H)\#\mathcal{F}_{2n}(F,\,C_{2}\wr H,\,X)+E_{2n}(F,C_{2}\wr H,X),

(3.1)

where
\alpha(H)
denotes the multiplicity of
C_{2}\wr H
-extensions of
F
appearing in this sum and
E_{2n}(F,\,C_{2}\wr H,\,X)
is defined as follows:

\displaystyle E_{2n}(F,\,C_{2}\wr H,\,X)\coloneqq\#\{L/K/F\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}[L\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}K]=2,

\displaystyle\,\mathrm{Gal}(K/F)\cong H,

\displaystyle\mathrm{Gal}(\widetilde{L}/F)\neq C_{2}\wr H,|{\mathrm{Nm}_{F/\mathbb{Q}}(\mathrm{Disc}({L/F})}|\leq X\}.

Jürgen Klüners proved(Theorem 5.2,
[
7
]

E_{2n}(F,\,C_{2}\wr H,\,X)\ll X^{\frac{3}{4}+\epsilon}.

(3.2)

Thus, to obtain the main asymptotic for

it suffices to analyze the left-hand side of equation (

).

We will use Theorem 2 of
[
11
]

Theorem 3.3

(McGown, Tucker; 2024)

Let

be a number field of degree

Then, we have

\displaystyle\begin{split}\sum_{K\in\mathcal{F}_{2}(F,\,C_{2},\,X)}1=&\frac{1}{2^{i(F)}}\frac{\mathrm{Res}_{s=1}\zeta_{F}(s)}{\zeta_{F}(2)}X+\\
&\mathrm{Cl}_{F}[2]\cdot\begin{cases}\text{O}(|\mathrm{Disc}(F)|^{\frac{1}{3}}\log{(|\mathrm{Disc}(F)|)X^{\frac{1}{2}}\log X})&\text{if }m=2\\
\text{O}(|\mathrm{Disc}(F)|^{\frac{1}{4}}(\log|\mathrm{Disc}(F))^{2}X^{\frac{1}{2}}(\log X)^{3})&\text{if }m=3\\
\text{O}_{m}(|\mathrm{Disc}(F)^{\frac{1}{m+1}}X^{1-\frac{2}{m+1}}(\log X)^{m-1})&\text{if }m>3\\
\end{cases}\end{split}

Applying Theorem
3.3
on the left-hand side of equation
(
3.1
)
, we get

\displaystyle\sum_{K\in\mathcal{F}_{n}(F,\,H,\,X^{1/2})}\sum_{L\in\mathcal{F}_{2}(K,\,C_{2},\,X/|\mathrm{Nm}_{F/\mathbb{Q}}(\mathrm{Disc}({K/F})|^{2})}1

\displaystyle=\sum_{K\in\mathcal{F}_{n}(F,\,H,\,X^{1/2})}\frac{1}{2^{i(K)}}\cdot\frac{\mathrm{Res}_{s=1}\zeta_{K}(s)}{\zeta_{K}(2)}\cdot\frac{X}{|\mathrm{Nm}_{F/\mathbb{Q}}(\mathrm{Disc}({K/F})|^{2}}+

\displaystyle\sum_{K\in\mathcal{F}_{n}(F,\,H,\,X^{1/2})}\#\mathrm{Cl}_{K}[2]\cdot\text{O}\left(|\mathrm{Disc}(K)|^{\frac{1}{mn+1}}\cdot\left(\frac{X}{|\mathrm{Nm}_{F/\mathbb{Q}}(\mathrm{Disc}({K/F})|^{2}}\right)^{1-\frac{2}{mn+1}+\epsilon}\right).

(3.4)

We analyze the main term and the error term in (

) separately.

3.1
Sum of main terms

In this section, we study the main term in equation (
3
), i.e, we analyze the sum

\sum_{K\in\mathcal{F}_{n}(F,\,H,\,X^{1/2})}\frac{1}{2^{i(K)}}\cdot\frac{\mathrm{Res}_{s=1}\zeta_{K}(s)}{\zeta_{K}(2)}\cdot\frac{X}{|\mathrm{Nm}_{F/\mathbb{Q}}(\mathrm{Disc}({K/F})|^{2}}.

We will require the following lemma:

Lemma 3.5
.

Suppose
f
and
g
are mappings from
\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}
such that
g
is continuously differentiable with
g^{\prime}(t)\leq 0
for all
t\geq 1
and

\displaystyle\#\{K/F\mid[K\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}F]=k,\mathrm{Gal}(K/F)\cong H,|\mathrm{Nm}_{F/\mathbb{Q}}(\mathrm{Disc}({K/F})|\leq X\}\ll f(X).

Then,

\displaystyle\sum_{K\in\mathcal{F}_{k}(F,\,H,\,Z)\setminus\mathcal{F}_{k}(F,\,H,\,W)}g(|\mathrm{Nm}_{F/\mathbb{Q}}(\mathrm{Disc}({K/F})|)\ll f(Z)|g(Z)|+f(W)|g(W)|-\int_{W}^{Z}f(t)g^{\prime}(t)dt.

In particular, if
k>1
and
W=1
then

\sum_{K\in\mathcal{F}_{k}(F,\,H,\,Z)}g(|\mathrm{Nm}_{F/\mathbb{Q}}(\mathrm{Disc}({K/F})|)\ll f(Z)|g(Z)|-\int_{1}^{Z}f(t)g^{\prime}(t)dt.

Proof.

We have

\displaystyle\sum_{K\in\mathcal{F}_{k}(F,\,H,\,Z)\setminus\mathcal{F}_{k}(F,\,H,\,W)}g(|\mathrm{Nm}_{F/\mathbb{Q}}(\mathrm{Disc}({K/F})|)

\displaystyle=\sum_{W\leq l\leq Z}\#\{K/F\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}[K\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}F]=k,\,\mathrm{Gal}(K/F)\cong H,\,|\mathrm{Nm}_{F/\mathbb{Q}}(\mathrm{Disc}({K/F})|=l\}\cdot g(l)

\displaystyle=\#\mathcal{F}_{k}(F,\,H,\,Z)g(Z)-\#\mathcal{F}_{k}(F,\,H,\,W)g(W)+\int_{W}^{Z}(-g^{\prime}(t))\cdot\#\mathcal{F}_{k}(F,\,H,\,t)dt

\displaystyle\ll f(Z)|g(Z)|+f(W)|g(W)|-\int_{W}^{Z}f(t)g^{\prime}(t)dt\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\text{[Since }g^{\prime}(t)\leq 0]

Note that for any field extension
K/F
with degree greater than one, the absolute norm of the relative discriminant of
K/F
cannot be equal to
1
. Thus, for
W=1
and
k>1
, we get
\#\mathcal{F}_{k}(F,\,H,\,W)=0
. This completes the proof of the second part of the lemma..

∎

Coming back to the analysis of the main term in equation (
3

is greater than
1
, we have
\zeta_{K}(2)\geq 1
. Furthermore, from Lemma 2.1 of

\mathrm{Res}_{s=1}\zeta_{K}(s)\ll_{n}|\mathrm{Disc}(K)|^{\epsilon}.

We also have the following norm-discriminant relation:

\mathrm{Disc}(K)=|\mathrm{Nm}_{F/\mathbb{Q}}(\mathrm{Disc}({K/F})|\cdot\mathrm{Disc}(F)^{n}.

Therefore,
\mathrm{Disc}(K)

differ only by a constant. Using these results and applying Lemma

\displaystyle\sum_{K\in\mathcal{F}_{n}(F,\,H)\setminus\mathcal{F}_{n}(F,H,X^{1/2})}\frac{1}{2^{i(K)}}\cdot\frac{\mathrm{Res}_{s=1}\zeta_{K}(s)}{\zeta_{K}(2)}\cdot\frac{X}{|\mathrm{Nm}_{F/\mathbb{Q}}(\mathrm{Disc}({K/F})|^{2}}

\displaystyle\ll_{\epsilon,n}X\sum_{K\in\mathcal{F}_{n}(F,\,H)\setminus\mathcal{F}_{n}(F,H,X^{1/2})}\frac{|\mathrm{Disc}(K)|^{\epsilon}}{|\mathrm{Nm}_{F/\mathbb{Q}}(\mathrm{Disc}({K/F})|^{2}}

\displaystyle\ll_{\epsilon,n,F}X\sum_{K\in\mathcal{F}_{n}(F,\,H)\setminus\mathcal{F}_{n}(F,H,X^{1/2})}|\mathrm{Nm}_{F/\mathbb{Q}}(\mathrm{Disc}({K/F})|^{-2+\epsilon}

\displaystyle\ll_{\epsilon,n,F}X(X^{\frac{a}{2}}X^{-1+\epsilon}-(-2+\epsilon)\int_{X^{\frac{1}{2}}}^{\infty}t^{a}t^{-3+\epsilon}dt)

\displaystyle\ll_{\epsilon,n,F}X^{\frac{a}{2}+\epsilon}.

Therefore, we get

\displaystyle\sum_{K\in\mathcal{F}_{n}(F,\,H,\,X^{1/2})}\frac{1}{2^{i(K)}}\cdot\frac{\mathrm{Res}_{s=1}\zeta_{K}(s)}{\zeta_{K}(2)}\cdot\frac{X}{|\mathrm{Nm}_{F/\mathbb{Q}}(\mathrm{Disc}({K/F})|^{2}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left(\sum_{K\in\mathcal{F}_{n}(F,\,H)}\frac{1}{2^{i(K)}}\cdot\frac{\mathrm{Res}_{s=1}\zeta_{K}(s)}{\zeta_{K}(2)}\cdot\frac{1}{|\mathrm{Nm}_{F/\mathbb{Q}}(\mathrm{Disc}({K/F})|^{2}}\right)X+\text{O}(X^{\frac{a}{2}+\epsilon})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle c(F,C_{2}\wr H)X+\text{O}(X^{\frac{3}{4}+\epsilon}),

(3.6)

where in the last step we used the assumption
a\leq\frac{3}{2}.

3.2
Sum of error terms

Now we will focus on the error term in equation (
3
), i.e., we will analyze the following sum:

\sum_{K\in\mathcal{F}_{n}(F,H,X^{1/2})}\#\mathrm{Cl}_{K}[2]\cdot|\mathrm{Disc}(K)|^{\frac{1}{mn+1}}\cdot\left(\frac{X}{\mathrm{Nm}_{F/\mathbb{Q}}(\mathrm{Disc}({K/F})|^{2}}\right)^{1-\frac{2}{mn+1}+\epsilon}.

First, we will separate the sum into two parts, namely:

\displaystyle\sum_{K\in\mathcal{F}_{n}(F,\,H,\,Z)}+\sum_{K\in\mathcal{F}_{n}(F,H,X^{1/2})\setminus\mathcal{F}_{n}(F,\,H,\,Z)}

Let us analyze the first part of this sum in detail. In 2017
[
2
]
, Manjul Bhargava, Arul Shankar, Takashi Taniguchi, Frank Thorne, Jacob Tsimerman, and Yongqiang Zhao obtained an upper bound for the size of
2
-torsion subgroup of the class group which we will use. Let us restate the main theorem of
[
2
]
here.

Theorem 3.7

(Bhargava, Shankar, Taniguchi, Thorne, Tsimerman, Zhao; 2020)
.

The size of the
2-
torsion subgroup of the class group of a number field
E
of degree
n
is
\text{O}_{\epsilon}(|\mathrm{Disc}(E)|^{\frac{1}{2}-\delta_{n}+\epsilon}).
If
E
