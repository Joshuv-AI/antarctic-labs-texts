7 C.F.R. § 1410.54 — Performance based upon advice or action of the U.S. Department of Agriculture.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1410.54
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1410.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1410 (Conservation Reserve Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of part 718 of this title relating to performance based upon the action or advice of an authorized representative of the U.S. Department of Agriculture are applicable to this part.
