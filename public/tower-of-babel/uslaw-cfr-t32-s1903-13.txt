32 C.F.R. § 1903.13 — Under the influence while on an Agency installation.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1903.13
Creator: Central Intelligence Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1903.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1903 (Conduct On Agency Installations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Presence on an Agency installation when under the influence of alcohol, a drug, or a controlled substance or a combination thereof to a degree that interferes with, impedes or hinders the performance of the official duties of any government employee, or damages government or personal property is prohibited.
