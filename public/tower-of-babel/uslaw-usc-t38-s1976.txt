38 U.S.C. § 1976 — Effective date
Citation: us:usc:38:1976
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section1976
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 19 (Insurance); Subchapter III (Servicemembers’ Group Life Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The insurance provided for in this subchapter and the deductions and contributions for that purpose shall take effect on the date designated by the Secretary and certified by the Secretary to each Secretary concerned.
