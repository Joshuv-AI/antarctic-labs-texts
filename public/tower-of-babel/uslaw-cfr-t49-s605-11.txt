49 C.F.R. § 605.11 — Exemptions.
Citation: us:cfr:49:605.11
Creator: Federal Transit Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-605.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 605 (School Bus Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A recipient may not engage in school bus operations in competition with private school bus operators unless it demonstrates to the satisfaction of the Administrator that:
(a) It operates a school system in the area to be served and also operates a separate and exclusive school bus program for that school system; and
(b) Private school bus operators are unable to provide adequate transportation that complies with applicable safety standards at reasonable rates.
