5 C.F.R. § 892.302 — Will the Government contribution continue?
Citation: us:cfr:5:892.302
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-892.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 892 (Federal Flexible Benefits Plan: Pre-Tax Payment Of Health Benefits Premiums). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, your employer will still pay the same share of your premium as provided in the Federal Employees Health Benefits Act, and § 890.501 of this chapter. Employee allotments do not count toward the Government's statutory maximum contribution.
