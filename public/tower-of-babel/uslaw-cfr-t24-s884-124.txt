24 C.F.R. § 884.124 — Audit.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 884 (Section 8 Housing Assistance Payments Program, New Construction Set-Aside For Section 515 Rural Rental Housing Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where a non-Federal entity (as defined in 2 CFR 200.69) is the eligible owner of a project, or is a contract administrator under § 884.119 or § 884.120, receiving financial assistance under this part, the audit requirements in 2 CFR part 200, subpart F, shall apply.
