SURFACE TRANSPORTATION BOARD

[Docket No. EP 290 (Sub-No. 4)]


Railroad Cost Recovery Procedures--Productivity Adjustment

AGENCY: Surface Transportation Board.

ACTION: Presentation of the Board's calculation for the change in 
railroad productivity for the 2020-2024 averaging period.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In a decision served on March 31, 2026, the Board proposed to 
adopt 1.015 (1.5% per year) as the measure of average (geometric mean) 
change in railroad productivity for the 2020-2024 (five-year) period. 
The Board's March 31, 2026 decision stated that comments may be filed 
addressing any perceived data and computational errors in the Board's 
calculation. The decision also stated that, unless a further order is 
issued postponing the effective date, the decision will take effect on 
April 18, 2026.

DATES: Comments are due by April 15, 2026.

ADDRESSES: Comments may be filed via e-filing on the Board's website at 
www.stb.gov. Comments must be served on all parties appearing on the 
service list.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Pedro Ramirez at (202) 245-0333. If 
you require accommodation under the Americans with Disabilities Act, 
please call (202) 245-0245.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Additional information is contained in the 
Board's decision, which is available at www.stb.gov under Docket No. EP 
290 (Sub-No. 4).
