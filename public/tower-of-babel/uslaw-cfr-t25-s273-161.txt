25 C.F.R. § 273.161 — What is the maximum decrease in funding allowed?
Citation: us:cfr:25:273.161
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-273.161
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 273 (Education Contracts Under Johnson-O'Malley Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Beginning December 31, 2022 (4 years after the December 31, 2018, date of enactment of the JOM Modernization Act), no contracting party may receive for a fiscal year more than a 10 percent decrease in funding per eligible Indian student from the previous fiscal year.
