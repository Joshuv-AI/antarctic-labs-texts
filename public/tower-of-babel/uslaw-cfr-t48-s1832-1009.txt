48 C.F.R. § 1832.1009 — 1832.1009 Title.

Creator: Chapter 18—National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1832.1009
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1832 (Contract Financing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In accordance with 42 U.S.C. 2465d, NASA shall not take title to launch vehicles under contracts for launch services unless one of the exceptions in the law applies. However, the law does not eliminate NASA's right to take title to other property acquired or produced by the contractor under a contract containing a title provision.
