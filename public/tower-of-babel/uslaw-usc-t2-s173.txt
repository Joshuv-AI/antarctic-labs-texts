2 U.S.C. § 173 — Establishment of Center for the Book
Citation: us:usc:2:173
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section173
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 5 (Library Of Congress). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is hereby established in the Library of Congress a Center for the Book.
The Center shall be under the direction of the Librarian of Congress.
