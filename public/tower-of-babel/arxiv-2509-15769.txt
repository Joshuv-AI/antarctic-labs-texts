Abstract

Introduction

Let

,

, be a space of
2\pi
–periodic functions
\varphi

–periodic functions

Further, let

-periodic functions

with Weyl-Nagy kernels

B_{r,\beta}(t)=\sum\limits_{k=1}^{\infty}k^{-r}\cos\left(kt-\frac{\beta\pi}{2}\right),\quad r>0,\quad\beta\in\mathbb{R},

(5)

of function
\varphi
satisfying the condition

B_{p}:=\left\{\varphi\in L_{p}:\|\varphi\|_{p}\leq 1\right\},\quad 1\leq p\leq\infty.

The classes

[
22
,
23
,

]

If

and
\beta=r,\
then functions of the form (
5

coincide with the known classes
W^{r}_{p}
of
2\pi
-periodic functions

(r-1)
-th order, inclusivly, such that

\beta=r
, then the classes
W^{r}_{\beta,p}
are known Weyl classes
W^{r}_{p}
.

For all

,
r>(\frac{1}{p}-\frac{1}{s})_{+}=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\frac{1}{p}-\frac{1}{s},&p<s,\\
0,&p\geq s,\end{array}\right.
and
\beta\in\mathbb{R}

,
[

). In this paper we consider the case

Let

. By

(7)

where

The quantity

3
Main Results

The following statement allows us to write the principal term of the asymptotic expansion of the quantity
{\cal E}_{n}(W^{r}_{\beta,1})
as
r\rightarrow\infty
and
r/n\rightarrow 0
. This case also fully covers the relation (
24
) as
r\rightarrow\infty
.

Theorem 1
.

Let
r>2,

\beta\in\mathbb{R}
and
n\in\mathbb{N}
. Then the formula

{\cal E}_{n}(W^{r}_{\beta,1})=\frac{1}{n^{r}}\frac{n}{r}\left(\frac{1}{\pi}+\bar{\theta}\left(\frac{1}{r-2}+\frac{r}{n}\right)\right),

(25)

is true. Here
\bar{\theta}=\bar{\theta}_{r,n,\beta}\in\left(-\frac{2}{\pi},\frac{2}{\pi}\right)
.

Proof of Theorem
1
.

For
r>2
from Theorem 2.2 of the work
[
20
]
it follows that

{\cal E}_{n}(W^{r}_{\beta,1})=\frac{1}{\pi}\left(\sum\limits_{k=n}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k^{r}}+\frac{\theta}{n}\sum\limits_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{k}{(k+n)^{r}}\right),

(26)

where
\theta\in[-1,0]
.

Because

\frac{n}{r-1}\frac{1}{n^{r}}=\int\limits_{n}^{\infty}\frac{1}{t^{r}}dt<\sum\limits_{k=n}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k^{r}}<\frac{1}{n^{r}}+\int\limits_{n}^{\infty}\frac{1}{t^{r}}dt=\left(1+\frac{n}{r-1}\right)\frac{1}{n^{r}},

(27)

then

\sum\limits_{k=n}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k^{r}}=\frac{1}{n^{r}}\left(\frac{n}{r-1}+\theta_{1}\right),\quad\theta_{1}\in[0,1].

(28)

Taking into account the estimate (
28
) and the following equalities

\frac{1}{n}\sum\limits_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{k}{(k+n)^{r}}=\frac{1}{n}\sum\limits_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{k+n}{(k+n)^{r}}-\sum\limits_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{(k+n)^{r}}=\frac{1}{n}\sum\limits_{k=n}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k^{r-1}}-\sum\limits_{k=n}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k^{r}},

(29)

we obtain estimates

\frac{1}{n}\sum\limits_{k=n}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k^{r-1}}=\frac{1}{n}\frac{1}{n^{r-1}}\left(\frac{n}{r-2}+\theta_{1}\right)=\frac{1}{n^{r}}\left(\frac{n}{r-2}+\theta_{1}\right),\quad\theta_{1}\in[0,1],

(30)

\frac{1}{n}\sum\limits_{k=n}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k^{r-1}}-\sum\limits_{k=n}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k^{r}}=\frac{1}{n^{r}}\left(\frac{n}{r-2}-\frac{n}{r-1}+\theta_{2}\right)=\frac{1}{n^{r}}\left(\frac{n}{(r-1)(r-2)}+\theta_{2}\right),

(31)

\theta_{2}\in[-1,1]
.

Combining the formulas (
28
)–(
31
) allows you to write

\sum\limits_{k=n}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k^{r}}+\frac{\theta}{n}\sum\limits_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{k}{(k+n)^{r}}=\frac{1}{n^{r}}\left(\frac{n}{r-1}+\theta_{1}\right)+\frac{\theta}{n^{r}}\left(\frac{n}{(r-1)(r-2)}+\theta_{2}\right)=

=\frac{1}{n^{r}}\left(\frac{n}{r-1}+\frac{\theta n}{(r-1)(r-2)}+\theta_{1}+\theta\theta_{2}\right)=\frac{1}{n^{r}}\left(\frac{n}{r-1}\left(1+\frac{\theta}{r-2}\right)+\theta_{3}\right),

(32)

where
\theta_{3}\in[-1,2]
.

Because

\frac{n}{r-1}=\frac{n}{r}-\frac{n}{r(r-1)},

then

\frac{n}{r-1}\left(1+\frac{\theta}{r-2}\right)=\frac{n}{r}-\frac{n}{r(r-1)}+\frac{n\theta}{r(r-2)}-\frac{n\theta}{r(r-1)(r-2)}=\frac{n}{r}(1+A_{n,r}),

(33)

where

A_{n,r}=A_{n,r}(\theta):=-\frac{1}{r-1}+\frac{\theta}{r-2}-\frac{\theta}{(r-1)(r-2)},\quad\theta\in[-1,0].

(34)

Due to (
34
)

A_{n,r}\leq-\frac{1}{r-1}+\frac{1}{(r-1)(r-2)}=-\frac{1}{r-1}+\frac{1}{r-2}-\frac{1}{r-1}\leq\frac{1}{r-2},

A_{n,r}\geq-\frac{1}{r-1}-\frac{1}{r-2}>-\frac{2}{r-2},

and therefore

A_{n,r}=\frac{\theta_{4}}{r-2},\quad\theta_{4}\in(-2,1).

(35)

From formulas (
33
) and (
35
) we obtain

\frac{n}{r-1}\left(1+\frac{\theta}{r-2}\right)=\frac{n}{r}\left(1+\frac{\theta_{4}}{r-2}\right),\quad\theta_{4}\in(-2,1).

(36)

Taking into account (
36
), we have

\frac{1}{n^{r}}\left(\frac{n}{r-1}\left(1+\frac{\theta}{r-2}\right)+\theta_{3}\right)=\frac{1}{n^{r}}\left(\frac{n}{r}\left(1+\frac{\theta_{4}}{r-2}\right)+\theta_{3}\right),

(37)

\theta_{3}\in[-1,2],\ \theta_{4}\in(-2,1).

Therefore, from (
26
), (
32
) and (
37
), it follows that

{\cal E}_{n}(W^{r}_{\beta,1})=\frac{1}{\pi n^{r}}\left(\frac{n}{r}\left(1+\frac{\theta_{4}}{r-2}\right)+\theta_{3}\right),\quad\theta_{3}\in[-1,2],\ \theta_{4}\in(-2,1),

(38)

and, consequently, a somewhat rougher in comparison with (
38
) estimate (
25
) follows.
Theorem
1
is proved.

R_{r,n}=\frac{1}{n\Gamma(r-1)}\int\limits_{0}^{\infty}\frac{t^{r-2}e^{-(n+1)t}}{1-e^{-t}}dt-\frac{1}{\Gamma(r)}\int\limits_{0}^{\infty}\frac{t^{r-1}e^{-(n+1)t}}{1-e^{-t}}dt=
