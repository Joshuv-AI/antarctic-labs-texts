48 C.F.R. § 915.408-70 — 915.408-70 Key personnel clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:915.408-70
Creator: Chapter 9—Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-915.408-70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 915 (Contracting By Negotiation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Contracting Officer shall insert the clause at 952.215-70, Key Personnel, in solicitations and contracts under which successful performance is largely dependent on the expertise of specific key personnel.
