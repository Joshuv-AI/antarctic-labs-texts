18 C.F.R. § 4.24 — Determination of cost.
Citation: us:cfr:18:4.24
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-4.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 4 (Licenses, Permits, Exemptions, And Determination Of Project Costs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission, after receipt of the reports, or after the conclusion of the hearing if one is held, will determine the amounts to be included in the electric plant accounts of the licensee as the cost of the property and the accrued depreciation thereon.
