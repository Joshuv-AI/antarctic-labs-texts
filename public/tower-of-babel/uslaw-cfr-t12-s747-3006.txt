12 C.F.R. § 747.3006 — Conservatorship or liquidation of critically undercapitalized corporate credit union.
Citation: us:cfr:12:747.3006
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-747.3006
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 747 (Administrative Actions, Adjudicative Hearings, Rules Of Practice And Procedure, And Investigations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notwithstanding any other provision of this title, the NCUA may, without any administrative due process, immediately place into conservatorship or liquidation any corporate credit union that has been categorized as critically undercapitalized.
