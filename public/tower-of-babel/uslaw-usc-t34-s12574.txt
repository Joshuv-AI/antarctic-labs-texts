34 U.S.C. § 12574 — Scholarships
Citation: us:usc:34:12574
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section12574
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 121 (Violent Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Subchapter VII (Police Corps And Law Enforcement Officers Training And Education); Part B (Law Enforcement Scholarship Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Period of award
Scholarships awarded under this part shall be for a period of 1 academic year.
(b) Use of scholarships
Each individual awarded a scholarship under this part may use the scholarship for educational expenses at an institution of higher education.
