40 C.F.R. § 144.15 — Prohibition of non-experimental Class V wells for geologic sequestration.
Citation: us:cfr:40:144.15
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-144.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 144 (Underground Injection Control Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The construction, operation or maintenance of any non-experimental Class V geologic sequestration well is prohibited.
