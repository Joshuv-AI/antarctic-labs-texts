7 U.S.C. § 1393 — Allotment of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:7:1393
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1393
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 35 (Agricultural Adjustment Act Of 1938); Subchapter II (Loans, Parity Payments, Consumer Safeguards, Marketing Quotas, And Marketing Certificates); Part F (Miscellaneous Provisions And Appropriations); Subpart ii (Appropriations And Administrative Expenses). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All funds for carrying out the provisions of this chapter shall be available for allotment to bureaus and offices of the Department, and for transfer to such other agencies of the Federal Government, and to such State agencies, as the Secretary may request to cooperate or assist in carrying out the provisions of this chapter.
