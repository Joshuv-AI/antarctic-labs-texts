Quasi-integrability from 𝒫𝒯 {\cal PT} -symmetry
Kumar Abhinav, Partha Guha, Indranil Mukherjee

Abstract
Parity and time-reversal ( 𝒫𝒯 {\cal PT} ) symmetry is shown as the natural cause of quasi-integrability of deformed integrable models. The condition for asymptotic conservation of quasi-conserved charges appear as a direct consequence of the 𝒫𝒯 {\cal PT} -symmetric phase of the system, ensuring definite 𝒫𝒯 {\cal PT} -properties of the corresponding Lax pair as well as that of the anomalous contribution. This construction applies to quasi-deformations of multiple systems such as KdV, NLSE and non-local NLSE.
