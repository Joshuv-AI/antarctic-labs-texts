7 C.F.R. § 1215.10 — Part and subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1215.10
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Miscellaneous Commodities), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1215.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1215 (Popcorn Promotion, Research, And Consumer Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Part means the Popcorn Promotion, Research, and Consumer Information Order and all rules and regulations and supplemental orders issued thereunder, and the term subpart means the Popcorn Promotion, Research, and Consumer Information Order.
