38 U.S.C. § 1121 — Basic entitlement
Citation: us:usc:38:1121
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section1121
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 11 (Compensation For Service-Connected Disability Or Death); Subchapter III (Wartime Death Compensation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The surviving spouse, child or children, and dependent parent or parents of any veteran who died before January 1, 1957 as the result of injury or disease incurred in or aggravated by active military, naval, or air service, in line of duty, during a period of war, shall be entitled to receive compensation at the monthly rates specified in section 1122 of this title.
