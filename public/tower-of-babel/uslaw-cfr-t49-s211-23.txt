49 C.F.R. § 211.23 — Additional public proceedings.
Citation: us:cfr:49:211.23
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-211.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 211 (Rules Of Practice). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator may conduct other public proceedings that he finds necessary or desirable. For example, he may invite interested persons to present oral arguments, participate in conferences, or appear at informal hearings.
