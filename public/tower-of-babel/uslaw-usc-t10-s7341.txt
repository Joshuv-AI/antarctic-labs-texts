10 U.S.C. § 7341 — General rule
Citation: us:usc:10:7341
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7341
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 743 (Retired Grade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
(1)
The retired grade of a regular commissioned officer of the Army who retires other than for physical disability is determined under section 1370 of this title.
(2)
The retired grade of a reserve commissioned officer of the Army who retires other than for physical disability is determined under section 1370a of this title.
(b)
Unless entitled to a higher retired grade under some other provision of law, a Regular or Reserve of the Army not covered by subsection (a) who retires other than for physical disability retires in the regular or reserve grade that the member holds on the date of the member’s retirement.
