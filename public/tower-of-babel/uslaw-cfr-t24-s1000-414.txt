24 C.F.R. § 1000.414 — How is an issuer's financial capacity demonstrated?
Citation: us:cfr:24:1000.414
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-1000.414
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 1000 (Native American Housing Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An issuer must demonstrate its financial capacity to:
(a) Meet its obligations; and
(b) Protect and maintain the viability of housing developed or operated pursuant to the 1937 Act.
