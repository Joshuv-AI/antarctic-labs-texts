27 C.F.R. § 20.54 — Photocopying of permits.
Citation: us:cfr:27:20.54
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-20.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 20 (Distribution And Use Of Denatured Alcohol And Rum). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A permittee may make photocopies of its permit exclusively for the purpose of furnishing proof of authorization to withdraw specially denatured spirits from a distilled spirits plant and other persons authorized under this part to deal in specially denatured spirits.
