7 C.F.R. § 1489.34 — Suspension, termination, and closeout of agreements.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1489.34
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1489.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1489 (Regional Agricultural Promotion Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A program agreement may be suspended or terminated in accordance with the suspension and termination procedures in 2 CFR part 200. If an agreement is terminated, the applicable regulations in 2 CFR part 200 will apply to the closeout of the agreement.
