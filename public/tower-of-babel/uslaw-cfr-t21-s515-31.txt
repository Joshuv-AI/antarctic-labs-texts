21 C.F.R. § 515.31 — Procedures for hearings.
Citation: us:cfr:21:515.31
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-515.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 515 (Medicated Feed Mill License). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Hearings relating to new animal drugs under section 512(m)(3) and (m)(4) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (the act) shall be governed by part 12 of this chapter.
