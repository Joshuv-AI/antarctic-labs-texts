43 C.F.R. § 1812.1-1 — -1 Regulations governing practice before the Department.
Citation: us:cfr:43:1812.1-1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-1812.1-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 1810 (Introduction And General Guidance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every individual who wishes to practice before the Department of the Interior, including the Bureau, must comply with the requirements of part 1 of this title.
