14 C.F.R. § 323.1 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:323.1
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-323.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 323 (Terminations, Suspensions, And Reductions Of Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to certificated air carriers who terminate or suspend service to a point, or in a market, and to all air carriers who terminate, suspend, or reduce service below the level of essential air service under 49 U.S.C. 41731-41742.
