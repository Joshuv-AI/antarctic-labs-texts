47 U.S.C. § 1609 — Severability
Citation: us:usc:47:1609
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title47-section1609
Context: United States Code, Title 47 (Telecommunications); Chapter 15 (Secure And Trusted Communications Networks). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this chapter, or the application of such a provision to any person or circumstance, is held to be unconstitutional, the remaining provisions of this chapter, and the application of such provisions to any person or circumstance, shall not be affected thereby.
