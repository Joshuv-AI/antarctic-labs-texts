28 C.F.R. § 63.8 — Implementation.
Citation: us:cfr:28:63.8
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-63.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 63 (Floodplain Management And Wetland Protection Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies and divisions within the Department of Justice shall amend existing regulations and procedures, as appropriate, to incorporate the policy and procedures set forth in these guidelines. Such amendments will be made within 6 months of final publication of these guidelines.
