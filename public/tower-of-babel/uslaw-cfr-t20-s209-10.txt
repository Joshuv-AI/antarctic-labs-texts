20 C.F.R. § 209.10 — Terminated employers' reports.
Citation: us:cfr:20:209.10
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-209.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 209 (Railroad Employers' Reports And Responsibilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When an employer's status as an employer is terminated, a final report of creditable service and compensation shall be made. The final report shall be submitted to the Board on or before the last day of the month following the final month for which there was compensated service. The report shall be completed as prescribed in § 209.8(a) of this part and shall be marked Final Compensation Report.
