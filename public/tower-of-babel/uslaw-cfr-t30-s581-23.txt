30 C.F.R. § 581.23 — Effective date of leases.
Citation: us:cfr:30:581.23
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-581.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 581 (Leasing Of Minerals Other Than Oil, Gas, And Sulphur In The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Leases issued under the regulations in this part shall be dated and become effective as of the first day of the month following the date leases are signed on behalf of the lessor except that, upon written request, a lease may be dated and become effective as of the first day of the month within which it is signed on behalf of the lessor.
