40 U.S.C. § 11311 — Responsibilities
Citation: us:usc:40:11311
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section11311
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Chapter 113 (Responsibility For Acquisitions Of Information Technology); Subchapter II (Executive Agencies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In fulfilling the responsibilities assigned under chapter 35 of title 44, the head of each executive agency shall comply with this subchapter with respect to the specific matters covered by this subchapter.
