34 U.S.C. § 50109 — Bureau of Justice Assistance
Citation: us:usc:34:50109
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section50109
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 501 (Emergency Federal Law Enforcement Assistance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of the Bureau of Justice Assistance may assist the Attorney General in providing Federal law enforcement assistance under this chapter and in coordinating the activities authorized under this chapter.
