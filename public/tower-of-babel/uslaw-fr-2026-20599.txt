I. Self-Regulatory Organization's Statement of the Terms of Substance 
of the Proposed Rule Change

    Pursuant to the provisions of Section 19(b)(1) under the Act,\3\ 
and Rule 19b-4 thereunder,\4\ the Exchange is filing with the 
Commission a proposed rule change to amend the IEX Equities Fee 
Schedule, pursuant to IEX Rules 15.110(a) and (c), to adopt fees for 
non-display use of two of its proprietary equities market data feeds, 
DEEP and DEEP+. Changes to the Fee Schedule pursuant to this proposal 
are effective upon filing,\5\ and will be operative beginning on 
October 1, 2026.

A. Self-Regulatory Organization's Statement of the Purpose of, and the 
Statutory Basis for, the Proposed Rule Change

1. Purpose
    The Exchange proposes to amend the Market Data Fees section of the 
IEX Equities Fee Schedule (``Fee Schedule'') to adopt separate fees for 
non-display use of its depth of book proprietary market data feeds, 
DEEP and DEEP+. DEEP is an uncompressed data feed \6\ that provides 
aggregated depth of book quotations for all displayed orders resting on 
the Order Book at each price level, and execution information (i.e., 
last sale information) for executions on the Exchange.\7\ DEEP+ is an 
uncompressed data feed that provides order-by-order depth of book 
quotations for all displayed orders resting on the Order Book at each 
price level, and execution information (i.e., last sale information) 
for executions on the Exchange.\8\
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \6\ An uncompressed data feed is a transmission of raw, 
unprocessed data without applying compression techniques.
    \7\ See IEX Rule 11.330(a)(2).
    \8\ See IEX Rule 11.330(a)(3).
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    The Exchange currently charges a single flat monthly fee of $2,500 
for DEEP and $3,500 for DEEP+ for Real-Time \9\ access to each data 
feed, which covers all uses of that data feed, including non-display 
use. The Exchange proposes to retain those fees for access to each data 
feed, which it proposes to rename a ``Real-Time Access Fee'' for 
clarity, and to adopt a separate flat monthly fee of $2,500 for DEEP 
and $3,500 for DEEP+ that would cover all non-display use of the 
respective data feed.\10\ A Data

[[Page 64432]]

Subscriber \11\ that makes any non-display use of a data feed would 
therefore pay both the Access Fee and the single non-display fee for 
that data feed. As proposed, the Exchange would only charge such a Data 
Subscriber once per month for the Access Fee and Non-Display Fee for 
the applicable data feed, and would not charge any additional fees 
based upon how the Data Subscriber uses the data feeds.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \9\ ``Real-Time'' means IEX market data that is accessed, used 
or distributed less than fifteen (15) minutes after it was made 
available by the Exchange. IEX provides only Real-Time IEX market 
data to Data Subscribers. A Data Subscriber may redistribute Real-
Time IEX market data that it receives from the Exchange on a Real-
Time basis to a natural person or entity. Receipt of IEX market data 
on a Real-Time basis by an affiliate of a Data Subscriber is not 
subject to additional Fees beyond those paid by such Data 
Subscriber. ``Delayed IEX Market Data'' is IEX Market Data that is 
accessed, used or distributed at least fifteen (15) minutes after it 
was made available by the Exchange. See IEX Equities Fee Schedule--

    \10\ The Exchange is not proposing to adopt non-display fees for

that the Exchange makes available through third-parties.
    \11\ ``Data Subscriber'' means any natural person or entity that 
receives Real-Time IEX market data either directly from the Exchange 
or from another non-affiliated Data Subscriber via uncontrolled

Market Data by the Data Subscriber. A Data Subscriber must enter

Time IEX market data from an affiliated Data Subscriber is subject 
to the Data Subscriber Agreement of such affiliated Data Subscriber. 
See Fee Schedule, supra note 10.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    In the Exchange's Market Data Policies,\12\ the Exchange defines 
non-display use as accessing, processing, or consuming Real-Time IEX 
Market Data for a purpose other than (i) solely facilitating a display 
of the Real-Time IEX Market Data or (ii) solely internally or 
externally distributing the Real-Time IEX Market Data. Non-display use 
includes, but is not limited to, using Real-Time IEX Market Data in 
operating a trading platform,\13\ automated trading, order routing, 
order management, investment analysis, risk management, surveillance, 
compliance, and portfolio valuation.\14\ A Data Subscriber would not 
incur the proposed non-display fees if it uses the data feeds solely to 
facilitate its own display of Real-Time IEX Market Data or to 
distribute Real-Time IEX Market Data internally or externally.\15\
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    The Exchange understands that Data Subscribers use real-time market 
data on a non-display basis for a range of revenue-generating 
activities, including proprietary and agency trading, automated order 
and quote generation, and the operation of trading platforms. Data 
Subscribers also use real-time market data on a non-display basis for 
functions that do not directly generate revenue, such as risk 
management, surveillance, compliance, order verification, and portfolio 
valuation.
    As proposed, the non-display fees for each data feed would not 
differentiate between the various forms of non-display use that a Data 
Subscriber may use. The proposed fees would be in addition to the 
current fees the Exchange charges a Data Subscriber to gain access to 
the data feeds.\16\ For clarity, the Exchange proposes renaming the 
existing fee a ``Real-Time Access Fee,'' but is not proposing any 
changes to those fees. In addition, the proposed non-display fees for 
the respective data feeds would be the same for each Data Subscriber, 
regardless of whether the Data Subscriber is using the data feed 
internally or distributing it externally in connection with its non-
display use.

    As of August 31, 2026, approximately 87% of current DEEP Data 
Subscribers and 94% of DEEP+ Data Subscribers report non-display use 
and would be subject to the proposed non-display fees.\17\ The Exchange 
believes the proposed fees are reasonable when compared with the fees 
charged by other exchanges for non-display use of comparable market 
data products, notwithstanding that the other exchanges may have 
different fee structures. More specifically, as described in the 
Statutory Basis section, the total monthly amount a Data Subscriber 
would pay IEX for access to and non-display use of DEEP is the same as 
the amount charged by each of the two exchanges that offer a comparable 
aggregated depth of book data feed and which have a similar market 
share as IEX. The total monthly amount payable for access to and non-
display use of DEEP+ is within the range charged by other exchanges for 
comparable order-by-order depth of book data feeds.

their fee schedules for the same reason.\18\

2. Statutory Basis
    IEX believes that the proposed rule change is consistent with the 
provisions of Section 6(b) of the Act \20\ in general and furthers the 
objectives of Section 6(b)(4) of the Act,\21\ in particular, in that it 
is designed to provide for the equitable allocation of reasonable dues, 
fees, and other charges among its Members and other persons using its 
facilities. In addition, the Exchange believes that the proposed fee is 
consistent with the purposes of Section 6(b)(5) \22\ of the Act in that 
it is designed to promote just and equitable principles of trade, to 
foster cooperation and coordination with persons engaged in regulating, 
clearing, settling, processing information with respect to, and 
facilitating transactions in securities, to remove impediments to a 
free and open

[[Page 64433]]

market and national market system, and, in general, to protect 
investors and the public interest, and particularly, is not designed to 
permit unfair discrimination between customers, issuers, brokers, or 
dealers.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \20\ 15 U.S.C. 78f(b).
    \21\ 15 U.S.C. 78f(b)(4).
    \22\ 15 U.S.C. 78f(b)(5).

    The Exchange notes that use of the DEEP and DEEP+ data feeds is 
optional. The Exchange is not required to make its depth of book market 
data feeds available to Members or market data customers for non-
display use, nor is any Member or customer required, either by any 
Exchange rule or the federal securities laws, to subscribe to or 
purchase non-display use of the data feeds.
The Proposed Fees Are Reasonable and Comparable to the Fees Charged by 
Other Exchanges for Non-Display Use of Similar Data Feeds
    Based on publicly available information as of September 1, 2026, 
the Exchange compared the proposed fees to the non-display fees charged 
by other exchanges with market share similar to or lower than IEX's for 
similar data products. IEX's year-to-date market share as of August 31, 
2026 was approximately 3.89%.\23\ Based on usage data reported to IEX 
by current Data Subscribers, approximately 49% of current DEEP 
subscribers who report non-display use also report using the data feed 
in connection with operating a trading platform. Approximately 24% of 
current DEEP+ subscribers who report non-display use also report using 
the data feed in connection with operating a trading platform. 
Accordingly, the Exchange believes that the reasonableness of the 
proposed non-display fees is most appropriately evaluated in comparison 
with the aggregate of the corresponding non-display fees that other 
exchanges charge, rather than against any single component of another 
exchange's non-display fee structure.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \23\ Exchange market share data noted in this rule filing 
represents the percent of executed share volume by the relevant 
exchange compared to market-wide executed share volume in NMS 
securities (see Rule 600(64) of Regulation NMS) as of August 31, 
2026 based on NYSE TAQ (Trade and Quote) data.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    The comparisons below present, for each category of use, the total 
monthly amount a Data Subscriber would pay each exchange for that use 
of the exchange's comparable data feed, comprised of the exchange's 
access or internal distribution fee plus any applicable non-display 
fee. The Exchange has used this approach because the proposed fees are 
all-inclusive, while other exchanges assess non-display charges in 
separate components, and a comparison limited to any single component 
would not reflect what a Data Subscriber actually pays.
    For the reasons set forth below, the Exchange believes that the 
proposed fees are reasonable because they are the same as or within the 
range of non-display fees charged by other exchanges with similar or 
lower market share for similar data feeds.

The Proposed Fees Are Equitably Allocated and Not Designed To Permit 
Unfair Discrimination
    The Exchange believes that its proposed fees for non-display use of 
the DEEP and DEEP+ data feeds are reasonable, fair, equitable, and not 
designed to permit unfair discrimination. The Exchange believes the 
proposed fees are fair and equitable because subscribing to the DEEP 
and DEEP+ data feeds is optional and the fees would apply uniformly to 
all Data Subscribers, irrespective of their relationship with the 
Exchange (i.e., Member, non-Member, etc.) or what type of business they 
operate.
    The Exchange further believes that the proposed fees are equitably 
allocated because a Data Subscriber would incur a non-display fee only 
if it actually makes non-display use of the data feed. A Data 
Subscriber that uses DEEP or DEEP+ solely to facilitate its own display 
of Real-Time IEX Market Data, or solely to distribute the data 
internally or externally, would pay only the Access Fee for that data 
feed and would not incur a non-display fee. Whether a Data Subscriber 
incurs the proposed fees therefore turns on an objective difference in 
how it uses the data, which remains within each Data Subscriber's 
control.
    The Exchange also believes the proposed fees are equitably 
allocated because a Data Subscriber would pay a single non-display fee 
per data feed, regardless of the number of non-display uses it makes of 
that feed or the number of trading platforms it operates using the data 
feed. A Data Subscriber that uses DEEP+ in connection with operating 
several trading platforms, and also for risk management and compliance 
purposes, would pay the same $7,000 per month as a Data Subscriber with 
a single non-display use. Several other exchanges instead assess 
separate non-display fees by category of use, by trading platform, or 
both, with the result that a Data Subscriber with multiple non-display 
uses pays those exchanges more than once for non-display use of the 
same data feed.\45\ The Exchange

[[Page 64436]]

notes that Data Subscribers that operate multiple trading platforms or 
make multiple categories of non-display use would benefit from the 
proposed fee structure relative to the fee structures of those 
exchanges.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \45\ See NYSE American Fee Schedule, supra note 32; NYSE 
National Fee Schedule, supra note 32; EDGX Fee Schedule, supra note 
25; BZX Fee Schedule, supra note 25; and MIAX Pearl Fee Schedule,

---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Moreover, the proposed non-display fees would apply equally to all 
Data Subscribers. The decision to subscribe to the DEEP and DEEP+ data 
feeds or any other market data feed offered by IEX is within the 
control of any particular market participant, and each market 
participant has the ability to choose the market data product (or 
combination of products) best suited to its business objectives. Each 
Data Subscriber would be subject to the same non-display fee for each 
data feed. The proposed fees also do not favor certain categories of 
market participants in a manner that would impose a burden on 
competition because each market participant can select the market data 
product best suited to its needs. Thus, the Exchange believes that the 
proposed non-display fees of $2,500 per month for non-display use of 
DEEP and $3,500 per month for non-display use of DEEP+ is not designed 
to permit unfair discrimination.
    The Exchange believes that adding the definition of ``Non-Display'' 
use to the Fee Schedule is reasonable, equitable, and not designed to 
permit unfair discrimination. The definition does not change the scope 
of what constitutes non-display use, which is already set out in the 
Exchange's Market Data Policies, and it would apply in the same manner 
to every Data Subscriber. Stating the definition in the Fee Schedule 
alongside the fees it applies to allows each Data Subscriber to 
determine from a single document whether a contemplated use would be 
subject to a non-display fee, which the Exchange believes promotes just 
and equitable principles of trade and removes impediments to a free and 
open market by providing clarity about the application of the fees.
    Finally, the Exchange notes that it publicly announced the proposed 
fees in advance of this filing, on July 2, 2026, in order to give Data 
Subscribers notice and an opportunity to evaluate their subscriptions 
before the fees become operative.\46\ The Exchange also notes that a 
Data Subscriber who does not wish to pay the proposed fees has 
alternatives. It may continue to subscribe to DEEP or DEEP+ for display 
or distribution purposes without incurring a non-display fee; it may 
use the Exchange's TOPS data feed; it may receive DEEP or DEEP+ on a 
Delayed basis free of charge; or it may use the consolidated data 
disseminated pursuant to the effective national market system 
plans.\47\

B. Self-Regulatory Organization's Statement on Burden on Competition

    In accordance with Section 6(b)(8) of the Act,\48\ IEX does not 
believe that the proposed rule change will result in any burden on 
competition that is not necessary or appropriate in furtherance of the 
purposes of the Act.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \48\ 15 U.S.C. 78f(b)(8).
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    The Exchange does not believe that the proposed rule change will 
result in any burden on intra-market competition that is not necessary 
or appropriate in furtherance of the purposes of the Act. Non-display 
use of DEEP and DEEP+ is optional. As discussed in the Statutory Basis 
section, each Data Subscriber that makes non-display use of a data feed 
would be subject to the same non-display fee for that data feed, 
irrespective of whether it is a Member \49\ of the Exchange, what type 
of business it operates, or the extent of its activity, and a Data 
Subscriber that does not make non-display use of a data feed would not 
incur the fee at all. Whether the proposed fees apply to a particular 
Data Subscriber is a function of that Data Subscriber's use of the 
data, which remains within its control. Because the proposed fees would 
be assessed once per data feed, they do not place Data Subscribers with 
more extensive non-display operations at a relative disadvantage. As a 
result, the proposed fees also do not favor certain categories of 
market participants in a manner that would impose a burden on 
competition because each market participant can select the market data 
product best suited to its needs. Moreover, the Exchange will continue 
to make access to non-display use of DEEP and DEEP+ available to market 
participants on a fair and impartial basis, and on terms that are not 
designed to permit unfair discrimination, as discussed in the Statutory 
Basis section.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \49\ See Rule 1.160(s).
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Further, as discussed in the Statutory Basis section, the proposed 
fees are the same as, or within the range of, fees charged by other 
exchanges for non-display use of comparable market data products, 
including by exchanges with market share similar to or lower than IEX. 
Thus, IEX does not believe that the proposed fees would operate as a 
barrier to entry, or impose a significant cost burden, on smaller 
Members or Data Subscribers.
    The Exchange also does not believe that the proposed rule change 
will impose any burden on inter-market competition that is not 
necessary or appropriate in furtherance of the purposes of the Act. As 
discussed in the Statutory Basis section, other exchanges charge fees 
for non-display use of comparable market data products that are equal 
to or higher than the fees IEX proposes, including exchanges with lower 
market share than IEX. Market participants are not required to 
subscribe to any market data feed, and are not required to use any data 
feed on a non-display basis. An exchange that overprices non-display 
use of its market data runs the risk that Data Subscribers will decline 
to use the data on a non-display basis or will substitute another 
exchange's data product or consolidated market data, and that 
competitive pressure constrains the Exchange in setting the proposed 
fees. Accordingly, the Exchange does not believe that adopting fees for 
non-display use of DEEP and DEEP+ will harm inter-market competition. 
Moreover, competing exchanges are free to adopt comparable fee 
structures subject to the Commission rule filing process.
