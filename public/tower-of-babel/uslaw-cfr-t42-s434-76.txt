42 C.F.R. § 434.76 — Costs under fiscal agent contracts.
Citation: us:cfr:42:434.76
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-434.76
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 434 (Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Under each contract with a fiscal agent—
(a) The amount paid to the provider of medical services is a medical assistance cost; and
(b) The amount paid to the contractor for performing the agreed-upon functions is an administrative cost.
