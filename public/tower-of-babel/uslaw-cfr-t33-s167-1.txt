33 C.F.R. § 167.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:33:167.1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-167.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 167 (Offshore Traffic Separation Schemes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of the regulations in this part is to establish and designate traffic separation schemes and precautionary areas to provide access routes for vessels proceeding to and from U.S. ports.
