33 C.F.R. § 62.5 — Marking of marine parades and regattas.
Citation: us:cfr:33:62.5
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-62.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 62 (United States Aids To Navigation System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Coast Guard may establish aids to navigation to mark marine parades and regattas which are regulated by the Coast Guard for the purpose of protecting life and property, or to assist in the observance and enforcement of special regulations. For marine parade and regatta regulations, see Part 100 of this chapter.
