47 C.F.R. § 51.713 — Bill-and-keep arrangements.
Citation: us:cfr:47:51.713
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-51.713
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 51 (Interconnection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Bill-and-keep arrangements are those in which carriers exchanging telecommunications traffic do not charge each other for specific transport and/or termination functions or services.
