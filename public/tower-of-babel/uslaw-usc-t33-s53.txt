33 U.S.C. § 53 — Benton Harbor Canal, Michigan
Citation: us:usc:33:53
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section53
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 1 (Navigable Waters Generally); Subchapter II (Waters Declared Nonnavigable: Change Of Name). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Benton Harbor Canal at and above the west line of Ninth Street, in the city of Benton Harbor and State of Michigan, is declared to be not a navigable water of the United States within the meaning of the Constitution and laws of the United States.
The right to alter, amend, or repeal this section is expressly reserved.
