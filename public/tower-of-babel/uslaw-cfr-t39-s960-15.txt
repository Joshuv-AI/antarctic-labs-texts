39 C.F.R. § 960.15 — Reply.
Citation: us:cfr:39:960.15
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-960.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 960 (Equal Access To Justice Act In Postal Service Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within 15 days after service of an answer, the applicant may file a reply. If the reply is based on any alleged facts not already in the record of the proceeding, the applicant shall include with the reply either supporting affidavits or a request for further proceedings under § 960.19.
