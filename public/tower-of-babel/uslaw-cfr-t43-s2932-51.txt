43 C.F.R. § 2932.51 — When can I renew my Special Recreation Permit?
Citation: us:cfr:43:2932.51
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2932.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2930 (Permits For Recreation On Public Lands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We will renew your Special Recreation Permit upon application at the end of its term only if—
(a) It is in good standing;
(b) Consistent with BLM management plans and policies; and
(c) You and all of your affiliates have a satisfactory record of performance.
