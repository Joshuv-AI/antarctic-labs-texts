The strip

A key function is

\xi(s)=\Gamma\left(\frac{s}{2}+1\right)(s-1)\pi^{-s/2}\zeta(s)=\left[\frac{\Gamma\left(\frac{s}{2}+1\right)}{\pi^{s/2}}\right]\ \times\ \left[\zeta(s)(s-1)\ \right]

RH statement is:

“ the analytic continuation of
\zeta(s)
has all non trivial zeros,
s_{0}
,
on
\Re[s]=1/2
( i.e. on critical-line) “.

The equivalence stated in

[
4

is reported here for easy reading:

”The Riemann hypothesis is equivalent to the statement that all local maxima
of
\xi(t)=\xi\left(\frac{1}{2}+i\ t\right)
are positive and all local minima are negative, . . .”
.

This article is structured as follows:

•

In section 2 is shown that the above equivalence is linked to
\frac{\partial\angle[\xi(s)]}{\partial t}
, and,a numerical example is given.

•

In section 3 a similar expression,
\frac{\partial\Im\{\ln[\zeta(s)(s-1)]\}}{\partial t}

\epsilon>0

It is stressed also that this result opens new shorter ways to prove RH.

•

\forall t\ \ with\ \ \xi(t)\neq 0\ \ we\ have\ \ \left(\frac{d\xi(t)}{dt}\right)^{2}-\xi(t)\times\frac{d^{2}\xi(t)}{dt^{2}}>0

.
Where
\xi(t)=\xi(1/2+it)
.

•

In section 5 results are summarized and concluding remarks are given.

•

In appendixes some numerical example, and some analytical development are given.
