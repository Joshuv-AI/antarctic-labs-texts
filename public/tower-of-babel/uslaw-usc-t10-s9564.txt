10 U.S.C. § 9564 — Navy and Marine Corps: camp equipment and transportation; when on shore duty with Air Force
Citation: us:usc:10:9564
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9564
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Procurement); Chapter 965 (Issue Of Serviceable Material To Armed Forces). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

While any detachment of the Navy or Marine Corps is on shore duty in cooperation with troops of the Air Force, the Secretary of the Air Force shall, upon the requisition of the officer of the Navy or Marine Corps in command of the detachment, issue rations and camp equipment, and furnish transportation, to that detachment.
