13 C.F.R. § 121.502 — What size standards are applicable to programs for sales or leases of Government property?
Citation: us:cfr:13:121.502
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-121.502
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 121 (Small Business Size Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Unless otherwise specified in this part—
(1) A concern primarily engaged in manufacturing is small for sales or leases of Government property if it does not exceed 500 employees;
(2) A concern not primarily engaged in manufacturing is small for sales or leases of Government property if it has annual receipts not exceeding $9 million.
(b) Size status for such sales and leases is determined by the primary industry of the applicant business concern.
