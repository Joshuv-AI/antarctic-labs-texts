9 C.F.R. § 104.7 — Product permit.
Citation: us:cfr:9:104.7
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-104.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 104 (Permits For Biological Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A permit shall be numbered and dated.
(b) The purpose for which the product is imported shall be specified on the permit as for Research and Evaluation, Distribution and Sale, or Transit Shipment Only.
(c) A permit shall not be used after the date specified.
