33 C.F.R. § 401.18 — Steering lights.
Citation: us:cfr:33:401.18
Creator: Great Lakes St. Lawrence Seaway Development Corporation, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-401.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 401 (Seaway Regulations And Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every vessel shall be equipped with:
(a) A steering light located on the centerline at or near the stem of the vessel and clearly visible from the helm; or
(b) Two steering lights located at equal distances either side of the centerline at the forepart of the vessel and clearly visible from the bridge along a line parallel to the keel.
