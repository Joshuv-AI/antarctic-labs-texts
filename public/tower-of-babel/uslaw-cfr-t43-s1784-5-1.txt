43 C.F.R. § 1784.5-1 — -1 Functions.
Citation: us:cfr:43:1784.5-1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-1784.5-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 1780 (Cooperative Relations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The function of an advisory committee is solely advisory, and recommendations shall be made only to the authorized representative specified in its charter. Determinations of actions to be taken on the reports and recommendations of a committee shall be made only by the Secretary or the designated Federal officer.
