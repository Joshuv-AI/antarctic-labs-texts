38 U.S.C. § 7692 — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:38:7692
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section7692
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part V (Boards, Administrations, And Services); Chapter 76 (Health Professionals Educational Assistance Program); Subchapter VIII (Specialty Education Loan Repayment Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of the Specialty Education Loan Repayment Program is to assist, through the establishment of an incentive program for certain individuals employed in the Veterans Health Administration, in meeting the staffing needs of the Veterans Health Administration for physicians in medical specialties for which the Secretary determines recruitment or retention of qualified personnel is difficult.
