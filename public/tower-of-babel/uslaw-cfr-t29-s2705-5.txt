29 C.F.R. § 2705.5 — Access to requested information to the individual.
Citation: us:cfr:29:2705.5
Creator: Federal Mine Safety And Health Review Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-2705.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 2705 (Privacy Act Implementation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As soon as practicable after verification of identity the Commission shall disclose to the individual the information contained in the record which pertains to that individual.
