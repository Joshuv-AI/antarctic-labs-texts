49 C.F.R. § 173.459 — Mixing of fissile material packages with non-fissile or fissile-excepted material packages.
Citation: us:cfr:49:173.459
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-173.459
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 173 (Shippers—General Requirements For Shipments And Packagings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Mixing of fissile material packages with other types of Class 7 (radioactive) materials in any conveyance or storage location is authorized only if the TI of any single package does not exceed 10, the CSI of any single package does not exceed 50, and the provisions of §§ 173.441 and 173.457 are satisfied.
