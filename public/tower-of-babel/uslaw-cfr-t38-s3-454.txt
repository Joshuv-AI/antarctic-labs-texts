38 C.F.R. § 3.454 — Apportionment of pension.
Citation: us:cfr:38:3.454
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-3.454
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 3 (Adjudication). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Disability pension. Disability pension will be apportioned to the veteran's spouse or child or children.
(b) Survivors pension. Old-Law survivors pension, section 306 survivors pension, and improved pension will be apportioned to the veteran's child or children.
