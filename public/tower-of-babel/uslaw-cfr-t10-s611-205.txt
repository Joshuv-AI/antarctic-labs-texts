10 C.F.R. § 611.205 — Period of award availability.
Citation: us:cfr:10:611.205
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-611.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 611 (Advanced Technology Vehicles Manufacturer Assistance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An award under section 611.204 shall apply to—
(a) Facilities and equipment placed in service before December 30, 2020; and
(b) Engineering integration costs incurred during the period beginning on December 19, 2007 and ending on December 30, 2020.
