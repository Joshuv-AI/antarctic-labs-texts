10 U.S.C. § 8324 — Officers: creditable service
Citation: us:usc:10:8324
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8324
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 841 (Voluntary Retirement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of this chapter, service as a nurse in the armed forces before April 16, 1947, is considered as commissioned service.
