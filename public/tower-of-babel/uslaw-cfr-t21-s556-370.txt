21 C.F.R. § 556.370 — Lubabegron.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 556 (Tolerances For Residues Of New Animal Drugs In Food). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Acceptable daily intake (ADI). The ADI for total residues of lubabegron is 3 micrograms per kilogram of body weight per day.
(b) Tolerances. The tolerances for lubabegron (marker residue) are:
(1) Cattle. (i) Liver (target tissue): 10 ppb.
(ii) Muscle: 3 ppb.
(iii) Kidney: 20 ppb.
(2) [Reserved]
(c) Related conditions of use. See § 558.330 of this chapter.
