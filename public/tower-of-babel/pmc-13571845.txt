Reviewers
Published: 2026

In celebration of Peer Review Week (September 14-18, 2026) we wish to thank the following reviewers for their generous dedication of time in evaluating manuscripts submitted to JNCI Cancer Spectrum during the past year. Peer reviewers are the lifeblood of any scientific journal. Their careful and expert review is critical for the integrity, clarity, and impact of the journal. Our peer reviewers are not compensated for their time, but evaluate manuscripts for the altruistic purpose of improving cancer care and ensuring that the knowledge published in our pages is spread via open access, without paywalls, and for the greater good.
