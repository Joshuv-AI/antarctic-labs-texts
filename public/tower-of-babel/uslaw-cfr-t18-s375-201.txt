18 C.F.R. § 375.201 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:18:375.201
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-375.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 375 (The Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this subpart is to set forth the Commission procedures for conduct of its official business in accordance with the provisions of 5 U.S.C. 552b. The Commission may waive the provisions set forth in this subpart to the extent authorized by law.
