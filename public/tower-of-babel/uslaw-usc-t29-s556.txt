29 U.S.C. § 556 — Chief clerk; other employees
Citation: us:usc:29:556
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section556
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 12 (Department Of Labor). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There shall be in said department a chief clerk and such other clerical assistants, inspectors, and special agents as may from time to time be provided for by Congress.
