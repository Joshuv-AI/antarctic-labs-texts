40 C.F.R. § 142.310 — How can a person served by the public water system obtain EPA review of a State proposed small system variance?
Citation: us:cfr:40:142.310
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-142.310
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 142 (National Primary Drinking Water Regulations Implementation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Any person served by the public water system may petition the Administrator to object to the granting of a small system variance within 30 days after a State proposes to grant a small system variance for a public water system.
(b) The Administrator must respond to a petition filed by any person served by the public water system and determine whether to object to the small system variance under § 142.311, no later than 60 days after the receipt of the petition.
