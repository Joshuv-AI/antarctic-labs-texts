21 U.S.C. § 465 — Limitations upon entry of poultry products and other materials into official establishments
Citation: us:usc:21:465
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section465
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 10 (Poultry And Poultry Products Inspection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may limit the entry of poultry products and other materials into any official establishment, under such conditions as he may prescribe to assure that allowing the entry of such articles into such inspected establishments will be consistent with the purposes of this chapter.
