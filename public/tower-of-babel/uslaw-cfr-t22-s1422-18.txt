22 C.F.R. § 1422.18 — Challenged ballots.
Citation: us:cfr:22:1422.18
Creator: Foreign Service Labor Relations Board; Federal Labor Relations Authority; General Counsel Of The Federal Labor Relations Authority; And The Foreign Service Impasse Disputes Panel
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1422.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1422 (Representation Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any party or the representative of the Board may challenge, for good cause, the eligibility of any person to participate in the election. The ballots of such challenged persons shall be impounded.
