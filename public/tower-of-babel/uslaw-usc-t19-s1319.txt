19 U.S.C. § 1319 — Duty on coffee imported into Puerto Rico
Citation: us:usc:19:1319
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section1319
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 4 (Tariff Act Of 1930); Part I (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Legislature of Puerto Rico is empowered to impose tariff duties upon coffee imported into Puerto Rico, including coffee grown in a foreign country coming into Puerto Rico from the United States. Such duties shall be collected and accounted for as now provided by law in the case of duties collected in Puerto Rico.
