43 C.F.R. § 8.4 — Blocking out.
Citation: us:cfr:43:8.4
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-8.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 8 (Joint Policies Of The Departments Of The Interior And Of The Army Relative To Reservoir Project Lands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Blocking out will be accomplished in accordance with sound real estate practices, for example, on minor sectional subdivision lines; and normally land will not be acquired to avoid severance damage if the owner will waive such damage.
