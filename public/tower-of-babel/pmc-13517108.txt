Doss, K.M.; Heldman, M.R.; Limaye, A.P. Updates in Cytomegalovirus Prevention and Treatment in Solid Organ Transplantation. Infect. Dis. Clin. N. Am. 2023, 37, e1–e16.

18.Piret, J.; Boivin, G. Clinical development of letermovir and maribavir: Overview of human cytomegalovirus drug resistance. Antivir. Res. 2019, 163, 91–105.

Piret, J.; Boivin, G. Clinical development of letermovir and maribavir: Overview of human cytomegalovirus drug resistance. Antivir. Res. 2019, 163, 91–105.

The authors state that the scientific conclusions are unaffected. This correction was approved by the Academic Editor. The original publication has also been updated.
