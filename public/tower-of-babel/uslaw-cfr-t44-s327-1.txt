44 C.F.R. § 327.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:44:327.1
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-327.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 327 (Policy On Use Of Government-Owned Industrial Plant Equipment By Private Industry (Dmo-10A)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part establishes policy on the use by private industry of Government-owned industrial plant equipment. This policy is necessary to maintain a highly effective and immediately available reserve of such equipment for the emergency preparedness programs of the U.S. Government.
