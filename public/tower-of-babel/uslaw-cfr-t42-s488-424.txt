42 C.F.R. § 488.424 — Directed plan of correction.
Citation: us:cfr:42:488.424
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-488.424
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 488 (Survey, Certification, And Enforcement Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

CMS, the State survey agency, or the temporary manager (with CMS or State approval) may develop a plan of correction and CMS, the State, or the temporary manager require a facility to take action within specified timeframes.
