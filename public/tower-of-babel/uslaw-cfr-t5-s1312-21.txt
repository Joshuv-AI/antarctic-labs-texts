5 C.F.R. § 1312.21 — Purpose and authority.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1312.21
Creator: Office Of Management And Budget
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1312.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1312 (Classification, Downgrading, Declassification And Safeguarding Of National Security Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart sets forth procedures for the receipt, storage, accountability, and transmission of classified information at the Office of Management and Budget. It is issued under the authority of Executive Order 12958, (60 FR 19825, 3 CFR, 1995 Comp., P.333), as implemented by Information Security Oversight Office Directive No 1 (32 CFR part 2001), and is applicable to all OMB employees.
