40 C.F.R. § 414.20 — Applicability; description of the rayon fibers subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:414.20
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-414.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 414 (Organic Chemicals, Plastics, And Synthetic Fibers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to process wastewater discharges resulting from the manufacture of rayon fiber by the viscose process only.
