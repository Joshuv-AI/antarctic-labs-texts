25 U.S.C. § 2016 — Rights of Indian students
Citation: us:usc:25:2016
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section2016
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 22 (Bureau Of Indian Affairs Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall prescribe such rules and regulations as are necessary to ensure the constitutional and civil rights of Indian students attending Bureau-funded schools, including such students’ rights to—
(1)
privacy under the laws of the United States;
(2)
freedom of religion and expression; and
(3)
due process in connection with disciplinary actions, suspensions, and expulsions.
