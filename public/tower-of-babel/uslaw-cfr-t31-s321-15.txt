31 C.F.R. § 321.15 — Liability for losses.
Citation: us:cfr:31:321.15
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-321.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 321 (Payments By Banks And Other Financial Institutions Of Definitive United States Savings Bonds And United States Savings Notes (Freedom Shares)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Under the governing statute, as amended (31 U.S.C. 3126(a)), an agent cannot be relieved of liability for a loss resulting from an erroneous payment unless the Secretary of the Treasury can make a determination that the loss resulted from no fault or negligence on the agent's part.
