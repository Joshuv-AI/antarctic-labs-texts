20 C.F.R. § 226.34 — Divorced spouse regular annuity rate.
Citation: us:cfr:20:226.34
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-226.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 226 (Computing Employee, Spouse, And Divorced Spouse Annuities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regular annuity rate of a divorced spouse is equal to his or her tier I amount. The divorced spouse is not entitled to a tier II benefit.
