26 U.S.C. § 6341 — Expense of levy and sale
Citation: us:usc:26:6341
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section6341
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 64 (Collection); Subchapter D (Seizure Of Property For Collection Of Taxes); Part II (Levy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall determine the expenses to be allowed in all cases of levy and sale.
