48 C.F.R. § 625.7002 — 625.7002 Solicitation provision and contract clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:625.7002
Creator: Chapter 6—Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-625.7002
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 625 (Foreign Acquisition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contracting officers shall include the following provision and clause in all solicitations and contracts exceeding the simplified acquisition threshold, unless a waiver has been granted in accordance with 625.7001(c):
(a) 652.225-70, Arab League Boycott of Israel; and.
(b) 652.225-71, Section 8(a) of the Export Administration Act, as amended.
