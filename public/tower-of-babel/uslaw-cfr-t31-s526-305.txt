31 C.F.R. § 526.305 — Foreign person.
Citation: us:cfr:31:526.305
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-526.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 526 (Hostages And Wrongful Detention Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term foreign person means any citizen or national of a foreign state (including any such individual who is also a citizen or national of the United States, provided such individual does not reside in the United States) or any entity not organized solely under the laws of the United States or existing solely in the United States.
