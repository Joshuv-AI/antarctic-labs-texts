43 C.F.R. § 4710.1 — Land use planning.
Citation: us:cfr:43:4710.1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4710.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4700 (Protection, Management, And Control Of Wild Free-Roaming Horses And Burros). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Management activities affecting wild horses and burros, including the establishment of herd management areas, shall be in accordance with approved land use plans prepared pursuant to part 1600 of this title.
