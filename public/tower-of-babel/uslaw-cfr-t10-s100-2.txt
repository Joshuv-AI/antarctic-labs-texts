10 C.F.R. § 100.2 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:10:100.2
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-100.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 100 (Reactor Site Criteria). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The siting requirements contained in this part apply to applications for site approval for the purpose of constructing and operating stationary power and testing reactors pursuant to the provisions of part 50 or part 52 of this chapter.
