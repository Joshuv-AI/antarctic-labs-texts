15 C.F.R. § 904.210 — Summary decision.
Citation: us:cfr:15:904.210
Creator: National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-904.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 904 (Civil Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Judge may render a summary decision disposing of all or part of the administrative proceeding if:
(a) Jointly requested by every party to the administrative proceeding; and
(b) There is no genuine issue as to any material fact and a party is entitled to summary decision as a matter of law.
