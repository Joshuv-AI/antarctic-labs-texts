46 C.F.R. § 44.01-20 — -20 New and existing vessels; special service.
Citation: us:cfr:46:44.01-20
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-44.01-20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 44 (Special Service Limited Domestic Voyages). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A new vessel marked with load lines for special service on a coastwise or inter-island voyage is a vessel whose keel was laid on or after September 28, 1937. An existing vessel is one whose keel was laid before that date.
