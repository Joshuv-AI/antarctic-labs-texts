8 C.F.R. § 280.6 — Bond to obtain clearance; form.
Citation: us:cfr:8:280.6
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-280.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 280 (Imposition And Collection Of Fines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A bond to obtain clearance of a vessel or aircraft under section 231, 237, 239, 243, 251, 253, 254, 255, 256, 272, or 273 of the Immigration and Nationality Act shall be filed on Form I-310.
