42 U.S.C. § 11333 — Role of Federal Emergency Management Agency
Citation: us:usc:42:11333
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section11333
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 119 (Homeless Assistance); Subchapter III (Federal Emergency Management Food And Shelter Program); Part A (Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The Director shall provide the National Board with administrative support and act as Federal liaison to the National Board.
(b) Specific support activities
The Director shall—
(1)
make available to the National Board, upon request, the services of the legal counsel and Inspector General of the Federal Emergency Management Agency;
(2)
assign clerical personnel to the National Board on a temporary basis; and
(3)
conduct audits of the National Board annually and at such other times as may be appropriate.
