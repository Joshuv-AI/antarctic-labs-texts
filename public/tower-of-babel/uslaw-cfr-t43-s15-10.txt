43 C.F.R. § 15.10 — Operation of watercraft.
Citation: us:cfr:43:15.10
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-15.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 15 (Key Largo Coral Reef Preserve). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No watercraft shall be operated in such a manner as to strike or otherwise cause damage to the natural features of the Preserve. Except in case of emergency endangering life or property, no anchor shall be cast or dragged in such a way as to damage any reef structure.
