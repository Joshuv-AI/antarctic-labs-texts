22 U.S.C. § 286ww — Acceptance of amendments to Articles of Agreement of Fund
Citation: us:usc:22:286ww
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section286ww
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter XV (International Monetary Fund And Bank For Reconstruction And Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States Governor of the Fund may accept the amendments to the Articles of Agreement of the Fund as proposed in resolution 66–2 of the Board of Governors of the Fund.
