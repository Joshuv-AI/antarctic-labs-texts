5 C.F.R. § 1201.12 — Revocation, amendment, or waiver of rules.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1201.12
Creator: Merit Systems Protection Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1201.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1201 (Practices And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board may revoke, amend, or waive any of these regulations. A judge may, for good cause shown, waive a Board regulation unless a statute requires application of the regulation. The judge must give notice of the waiver to all parties, but is not required to give the parties an opportunity to respond.
