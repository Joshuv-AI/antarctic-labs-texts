41 C.F.R. § 304-9.5 — -9.5 Handling duplicate expense compensation.
Citation: us:cfr:41:304-9.5
Creator: Chapter 304—Payment Of Travel Expenses From A Non-Federal Source
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-304-9.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 304-9 (9—Contributions And Awards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If agencies reimburse an employee for expenses that are also paid by a donor, agencies must establish and carry out policy in accordance with 5 U.S.C. 5514 and the Federal Claims Collection Standards (31 CFR parts 900 through 904) to recover any excess amount paid to the employee.
