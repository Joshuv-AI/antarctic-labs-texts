29 C.F.R. § 548.500 — Methods of computation.
Citation: us:cfr:29:548.500
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-548.500
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 548 (Authorization Of Established Basic Rates For Computing Overtime Pay). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The methods of computing overtime pay on the basic rates for piece workers, hourly rated employees, and salaried employees are the same as the methods of computing overtime pay at the regular rate.
