31 C.F.R. § 375.21 — How will the Treasury Department announce the redemption operation results?
Citation: us:cfr:31:375.21
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-375.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 375 (Marketable Treasury Securities Redemption Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We will make the redemption operation results available on our website. For each operation, the results generally will include information such as the amounts offered and accepted and pricing information.
