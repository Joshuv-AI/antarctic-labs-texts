35 U.S.C. § 262 — Joint owners
Citation: us:usc:35:262
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title35-section262
Context: United States Code, Title 35 (Patents); Part III (Patents And Protection Of Patent Rights); Chapter 26 (Ownership And Assignment). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the absence of any agreement to the contrary, each of the joint owners of a patent may make, use, offer to sell, or sell the patented invention within the United States, or import the patented invention into the United States, without the consent of and without accounting to the other owners.
