37 C.F.R. § 211.3 — Mask work fees.
Citation: us:cfr:37:211.3
Creator: U.S. Copyright Office, Library Of Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-211.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 211 (Mask Work Protection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Section 201.3 of this chapter prescribes the fees or charges established by the Register of Copyrights for services relating to mask works.
(b) Section 201.6 of this chapter on the payment and refund of Copyright Office fees shall apply to mask work fees.
