34 C.F.R. § 75.903 — Effective date of termination.
Citation: us:cfr:34:75.903
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-75.903
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 75 (Direct Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Termination is effective on the latest of:
(a) The date of delivery to the grantee of the notice of termination;
(b) The termination date given in the notice of termination; or
(c) The date of a final decision of the Secretary under part 81 of this title.
