10 U.S.C. § 453 — Sale of maps, charts, and navigational publications: prices; use of proceeds

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And General Military Powers); Chapter 22 (National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency); Subchapter II (Maps, Charts, And Geomatics Products). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Prices.—
All maps, charts, and other publications offered for sale by the National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency shall be sold at prices and under regulations that may be prescribed by the Secretary of Defense.
(b) Use of Proceeds To Pay Foreign Licensing Fees.—
(1)
The Secretary of Defense may pay any NGA foreign data acquisition fee out of the proceeds of the sale of maps, charts, and other publications of the Agency, and those proceeds are hereby made available for that purpose.
(2)
In this subsection, the term “NGA foreign data acquisition fee” means any licensing or other fee imposed by a foreign country or international organization for the acquisition or use of data or products by the National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency.
