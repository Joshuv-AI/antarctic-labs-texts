46 C.F.R. § 541.2 — Scope and applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:46:541.2
Creator: Federal Maritime Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-541.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 541 (Demurrage And Detention). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) This part sets forth regulations governing any invoice issued by an ocean common carrier, marine terminal operator, or non-vessel-operating common carrier for the collection of demurrage or detention charges.
(b) This part does not govern the billing relationships among and between ocean common carriers and marine terminal operators.
