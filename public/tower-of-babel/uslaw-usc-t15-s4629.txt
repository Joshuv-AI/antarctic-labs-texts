15 U.S.C. § 4629 — Avoidance of duplication
Citation: us:usc:15:4629
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section4629
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 72 (Semiconductor Research); Subchapter II (Department Of Energy Semiconductor Technology Research Excellence Initiative). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In carrying out the Initiative, the Secretary of Energy shall ensure that unnecessary duplicative research is not performed at the research facilities (including the national laboratories of the Department of Energy) that are participating in the Initiative.
