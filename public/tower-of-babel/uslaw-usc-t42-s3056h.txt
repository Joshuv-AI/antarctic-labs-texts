42 U.S.C. § 3056h — Eligibility for workforce investment activities
Citation: us:usc:42:3056h
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section3056h
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 35 (Programs For Older Americans); Subchapter IX (Community Service Senior Opportunities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Eligible individuals under this subchapter may be considered by local workforce development boards and one-stop operators established under title I of the Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act [29 U.S.C. 3111 et seq.] to satisfy the requirements for receiving services under such title that are applicable to adults.
