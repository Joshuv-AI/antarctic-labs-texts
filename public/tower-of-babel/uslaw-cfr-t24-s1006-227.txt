24 C.F.R. § 1006.227 — Tenant-based or project-based rental assistance.
Citation: us:cfr:24:1006.227
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-1006.227
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 1006 (Native Hawaiian Housing Block Grant Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

NHHBG funds may be used for the provision of tenant-based rental assistance, which may include security deposits and first month's rent, and project-based rental assistance.
(a) Rental assistance must comply with the requirements of this part and be provided to eligible families.
(b) Rental assistance may be provided to eligible families both on and off the Hawaiian Home Lands provided such use is consistent with the applicable appropriations acts governing the use of the NHHBG funds.
