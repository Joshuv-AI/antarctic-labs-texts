29 C.F.R. § 1919.17 — Exemptions from heat treatment.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1919.17
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1919.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1919 (Gear Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Gear made of steel, or gear which contains (as in ball bearings swivels), or is permanently attached to (as with blocks) equipment made of materials which cannot be subjected to heat treatment shall be exempt from the requirements of § 1919.16. Such gear, however, shall be thoroughly examined in the manner described in § 1919.15(c).
