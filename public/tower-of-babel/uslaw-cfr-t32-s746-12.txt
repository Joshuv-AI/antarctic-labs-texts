32 C.F.R. § 746.12 — Litigation.
Citation: us:cfr:32:746.12
Creator: Department Of The Navy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-746.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 746 (Licensing Of Government Inventions In The Custody Of The Department Of The Navy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The property interest in a patent is the right to exclude. It is not the intent of the Government to transfer the property right in a patent when a license is issued pursuant to this part. Accordingly, the right to sue for infringement shall be retained with respect to all licenses so issued by the Government.
