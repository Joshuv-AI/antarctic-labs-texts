5 C.F.R. § 511.609 — Ascertainment of facts.
Citation: us:cfr:5:511.609
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-511.609
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 511 (Classification Under The General Schedule). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The employee, a designated representative, and the agency shall furnish such facts as may be requested by the Office within the time frames specified. The facts shall be in writing when so requested. The Office, in its discretion, may investigate or audit the position. A representative may not participate in OPM on-site audits unless specifically requested to do so by the Office.
