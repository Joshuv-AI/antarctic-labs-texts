42 U.S.C. § 3940 — Reservation of right to repeal, alter, or amend chapter
Citation: us:usc:42:3940
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section3940
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 49 (National Housing Partnerships). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The right to repeal, alter, or amend this chapter at any time is expressly reserved.
