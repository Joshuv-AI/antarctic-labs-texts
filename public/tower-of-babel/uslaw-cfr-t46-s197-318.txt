46 C.F.R. § 197.318 — Gages and timekeeping devices.
Citation: us:cfr:46:197.318
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-197.318
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 197 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A gage indicating diver depth must be at each dive location for surface-supplied dives.
(b) A timekeeping device must be at each dive location.
