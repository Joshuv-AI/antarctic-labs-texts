25 C.F.R. § 2.804 — May I challenge the underlying action that generated the proceeds deposited into my account under this subpart?
Citation: us:cfr:25:2.804
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-2.804
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 2 (Appeals From Administrative Decisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. This subpart is solely for the purpose of challenging the accuracy of the activity within the SOP. If you want to challenge the underlying action that generated the proceeds deposited into your trust account, you must contact the BIA agency responsible for the action.
