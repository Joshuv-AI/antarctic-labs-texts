4 U.S.C. § 102 — Same; by whom administered
Citation: us:usc:4:102
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title4-section102
Context: United States Code, Title 4 (Flag And Seal, Seat Of Government, And The States); Chapter 4 (The States). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Such oath may be administered by any person who, by the law of the State, is authorized to administer the oath of office; and the person so administering such oath shall cause a record or certificate thereof to be made in the same manner, as by the law of the State, he is directed to record or certify the oath of office.
