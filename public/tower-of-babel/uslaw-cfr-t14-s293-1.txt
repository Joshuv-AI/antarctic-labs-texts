14 C.F.R. § 293.1 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:293.1

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 293 (International Passenger Transportation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to air carriers and foreign air carriers providing scheduled transportation of passengers and their baggage in foreign air transportation.
