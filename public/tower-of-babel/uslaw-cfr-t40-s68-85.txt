40 C.F.R. § 68.85 — Hot work permit.
Citation: us:cfr:40:68.85
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-68.85
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 68 (Chemical Accident Prevention Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The owner or operator shall issue a hot work permit for hot work operations conducted on or near a covered process.
(b) The permit shall document that the fire prevention and protection requirements in 29 CFR 1910.252(a) have been implemented prior to beginning the hot work operations; it shall indicate the date(s) authorized for hot work; and identify the object on which hot work is to be performed.
(c) The permit shall be retained for three years after the completion of the hot work operations.
