7 C.F.R. § 502.7 — Gambling.
Citation: us:cfr:7:502.7
Creator: Agricultural Research Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-502.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 502 (Conduct On Beltsville Agriculture Research Center Property, Beltsville, Maryland). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Participating in games for money or other personal property, or the operation of gambling devices, the conduct of a lottery or pool, or the selling or purchasing of numbers tickets, in or on BARC property, is prohibited.
