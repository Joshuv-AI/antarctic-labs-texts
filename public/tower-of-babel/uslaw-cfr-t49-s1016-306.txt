49 C.F.R. § 1016.306 — Settlement.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1016.306
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1016.306
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1016 (Special Procedures Governing The Recovery Of Expenses By Parties To Board Adjudicatory Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The applicant and agency counsel may agree on a proposed settlement of the award before final action on the application, either in connection with a settlement of the underlying proceeding, or after the underlying proceeding has been concluded. If a prevailing party and agency counsel agree on a proposed settlement of an award before an application has been filed, the application shall be filed with the proposed settlement.
