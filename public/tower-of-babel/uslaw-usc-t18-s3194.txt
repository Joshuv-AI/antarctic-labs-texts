18 U.S.C. § 3194 — Transportation of fugitive by receiving agent
Citation: us:usc:18:3194
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section3194
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part II (Criminal Procedure); Chapter 209 (Extradition). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any agent appointed as provided in section 3182 of this title who receives the fugitive into his custody is empowered to transport him to the State or Territory from which he has fled.
