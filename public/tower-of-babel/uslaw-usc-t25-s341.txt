25 U.S.C. § 341 — Power to grant rights-of-way not affected
Citation: us:usc:25:341
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section341
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 9 (Allotment Of Indian Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this act shall be so construed as to affect the right and power of Congress to grant the right of way through any lands granted to an Indian, or a tribe of Indians, for railroads or other highways, or telegraph lines, for the public use, or to condemn such lands to public uses, upon making just compensation.
