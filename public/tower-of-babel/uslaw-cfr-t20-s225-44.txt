20 C.F.R. § 225.44 — When a cost-of-living increase is payable.
Citation: us:cfr:20:225.44
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-225.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 225 (Primary Insurance Amount Determinations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A cost-of-living increase is payable beginning with December of the year for which the increase is due. The increase is paid in the January payment.
