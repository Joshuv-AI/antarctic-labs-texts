PAROLE (shortened from the Fr. parole d’honneur, word of
honour), a military term signifying the engagement given by a
prisoner of war that if released he will not again take up arms
against his captors during the term of engagement or the
war, unless previously relieved of the obligation by exchange.
“Parole” is also used in the same sense as “word” to imply a
watchword or password. The French word, formed from the
Late Lat. paraula, parabola, Gr. παραβολή, story, parable, was
also adopted into English as “parol,” i.e. verbal, oral, by word
of mouth, now only used in the legal term “parol evidence,”
i.e. oral as opposed to documentary evidence.
