1 C.F.R. § 425.1 — Purpose and scope.

Creator: Miscellaneous Agencies

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 1 (General Provisions), Part 425 (President'S Commission On White House Fellowships). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-23 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth the President's Commission on White House Fellowships procedures under the Privacy Act of 1974 as required by 5 U.S.C. 552a(f). Information to applicants regarding the implementation of this Act is contained in the White House Fellowships Application Instructions.
