28 U.S.C. § 2522 — Notice of appeal
Citation: us:usc:28:2522
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section2522
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part VI (Particular Proceedings); Chapter 165 (United States Court Of Federal Claims Procedure). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Review of a decision of the United States Court of Federal Claims shall be obtained by filing a notice of appeal with the clerk of the Court of Federal Claims within the time and in the manner prescribed for appeals to United States courts of appeals from the United States district courts.
