34 U.S.C. § 30502 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:34:30502
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section30502
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 305 (Hate Crimes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this division—
(1)
the term “crime of violence” has the meaning given that term in section 16 of title 18;
(2)
the term “hate crime” has the meaning given that term in section 280003(a) of the Violent Crime Control and Law Enforcement Act of 1994 (Public Law 103–322; 108 Stat. 2096), as amended by this Act;
(3)
the term “local” means a county, city, town, township, parish, village, or other general purpose political subdivision of a State; and
(4)
the term “State” includes the District of Columbia, Puerto Rico, and any other territory or possession of the United States.
