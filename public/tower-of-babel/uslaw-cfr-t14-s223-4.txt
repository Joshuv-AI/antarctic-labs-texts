14 C.F.R. § 223.4 — Transferability of passes.
Citation: us:cfr:14:223.4
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-223.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 223 (Free And Reduced-Rate Transportation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any pass authorizing free or reduced-rate transportation issued by a carrier may be made transferable to the extent specified by the granting carrier.
