5 C.F.R. § 353.208 — Use of paid time off during uniformed service.
Citation: us:cfr:5:353.208
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-353.208
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 353 (Restoration To Duty From Uniformed Service Or Compensable Injury). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee performing service with the uniformed services must be permitted, upon request, to use any accrued annual leave under 5 U.S.C. 6304, military leave under 5 U.S.C. 6323, earned compensatory time off for travel under 5 U.S.C. 5550b, or sick leave under 5 U.S.C. 6307, if appropriate, during such service.
