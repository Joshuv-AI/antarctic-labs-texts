41 C.F.R. § 101-28.203-4 — -28.203-4 Contact point.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-28.203-4
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-28.203-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-28 (28—Storage And Distribution). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The point within the headquarters of a military service or civilian agency to which requests should be forwarded. Coordination necessary with various organizational elements within a military service or civilian agency shall be accomplished by the contact point.
