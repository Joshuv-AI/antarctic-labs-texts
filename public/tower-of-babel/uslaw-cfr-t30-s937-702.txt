30 C.F.R. § 937.702 — Exemption for coal extraction incidental to the extraction of other minerals.
Citation: us:cfr:30:937.702
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-937.702
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 937 (Oregon). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Part 702 of this chapter, Exemption for Coal Extraction Incidental to the Extraction of Other Minerals, shall apply to any person who conducts coal extraction incidental to the extraction of other minerals for purposes of commercial use or sale.
