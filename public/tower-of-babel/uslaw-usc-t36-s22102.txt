36 U.S.C. § 22102 — Purposes
Citation: us:usc:36:22102
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section22102
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 221 (The American Society Of International Law). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purposes of the corporation are—
(1)
to foster the study of international law; and
(2)
to promote the establishment and maintenance of international relations on the basis of law and justice.
