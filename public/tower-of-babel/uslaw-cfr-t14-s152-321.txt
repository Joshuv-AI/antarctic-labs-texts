14 C.F.R. § 152.321 — Notice of delay or acceleration.
Citation: us:cfr:14:152.321
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-152.321
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 152 (Airport Aid Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The sponsor or planning agency shall promptly notify the FAA of each condition or event that may delay or accelerate accomplishment of the project.
(b) In the event that delay is anticipated, the notice required by paragraph (a) of this section must include—
(1) A statement of actions taken or contemplated; and
(2) Any Federal assistance needed.
