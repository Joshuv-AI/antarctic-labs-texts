43 U.S.C. § 127 — Change of boundaries of land districts
Citation: us:usc:43:127
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section127
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 5 (Land Districts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President is authorized to change and reestablish the boundaries of land districts whenever, in his opinion, the public interests will be subserved thereby, without authority to increase the number of land offices or land districts.
