12 C.F.R. § 790.1 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:12:790.1
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-790.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 790 (Description Of Ncua; Requests For Agency Action). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part contains a description of NCUA's organization and the procedures for public requests for action by the Board. Part 790 pertains to the practices of the National Credit Union Administration (NCUA) only and does not apply to credit union operations.
