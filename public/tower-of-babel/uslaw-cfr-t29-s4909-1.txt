29 C.F.R. § 4909.1 — Information Collection Control Numbers.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4909 (Omb Control Numbers For Pbgc Information Collection Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

PBGC regulations that contain information collections requirements without corresponding written or electronic forms, questionnaires, or instructions are displayed in table 1 to this section. They are displayed along with their respective control numbers as assigned by the Office of Management and Budget (OMB) under the Paperwork Reduction Act, 44 U.S.C. 3501 et seq.
