43 C.F.R. § 8365.2-4 — -4 Vehicles.
Citation: us:cfr:43:8365.2-4
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-8365.2-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 8360 (Visitor Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless otherwise authorized, no motor vehicle shall be driven within developed recreation sites or areas except on roads or places provided for this purpose.
