33 C.F.R. § 159.83 — Level indicator.
Citation: us:cfr:33:159.83
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-159.83
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 159 (Marine Sanitation Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each sewage retention device must have a means of indicating when the device is more than 3/4 full by volume.
