41 C.F.R. § 105-70.031 — -70.031 Determining the amount of penalties and assessments.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-70.031
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-70.031
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-70 (70—Implementation Of The Program Fraud Civil Remedies Act Of 1986). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In determining an appropriate amount of civil penalties and assessments, the ALJ and the Authority Head, upon appeal, should evaluate any circumstances presented that mitigate or aggravate the violation and should articulate in their opinions the reasons that support the penalties and assessments they impose.
