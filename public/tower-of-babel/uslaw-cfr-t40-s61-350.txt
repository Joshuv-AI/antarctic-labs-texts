40 C.F.R. § 61.350 — Standards: Delay of repair.
Citation: us:cfr:40:61.350
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-61.350
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 61 (National Emission Standards For Hazardous Air Pollutants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Delay of repair of facilities or units that are subject to the provisions of this subpart will be allowed if the repair is technically impossible without a complete or partial facility or unit shutdown.
(b) Repair of such equipment shall occur before the end of the next facility or unit shutdown.
