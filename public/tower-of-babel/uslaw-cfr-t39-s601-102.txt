39 C.F.R. § 601.102 — Revocation of prior purchasing regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:39:601.102
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-601.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 601 (Purchasing Of Property And Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All previous postal purchasing regulations, including the Postal Contracting Manual, Procurement Manual, the Purchasing Manual (Issues 1, 2 and 3), and procurement handbooks, circulars, and instructions, are revoked and are superseded by the regulations contained in this part.
