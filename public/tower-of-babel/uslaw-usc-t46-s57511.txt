46 U.S.C. § 57511 — Demise charters
Citation: us:usc:46:57511
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section57511
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part F (Government-Owned Merchant Vessels); Chapter 575 (Construction, Charter, And Sale Of Vessels); Subchapter II (Charters). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A charter by the Secretary of Transportation under this chapter shall demise the vessel to the charterer subject to all usual conditions contained in a bareboat charter. The charter shall be for a term the Secretary considers to be in the best interest of the United States Government and the merchant marine.
