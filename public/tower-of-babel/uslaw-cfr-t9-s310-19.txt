9 C.F.R. § 310.19 — Inspection of kidneys.
Citation: us:cfr:9:310.19
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-310.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 310 (Post-Mortem Inspection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee of the establishment shall open the kidney capsule and expose the kidneys of all livestock at the time of slaughter for the purpose of examination by a Program employee.
