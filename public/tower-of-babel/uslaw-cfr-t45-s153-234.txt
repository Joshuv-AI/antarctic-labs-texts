45 C.F.R. § 153.234 — Eligibility under health insurance market rules.
Citation: us:cfr:45:153.234
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-153.234
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 153 (Standards Related To Reinsurance, Risk Corridors, And Hhs Risk Adjustment Under The Affordable Care Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A reinsurance-eligible plan's covered claims costs for an enrollee incurred prior to the application of the following provisions do not count towards either the national reinsurance payment parameters or the State supplemental reinsurance payment parameters: 45 CFR 147.102, 147.104 (subject to 147.145), 147.106 (subject to 147.145), 156.80, and subpart B of part 156.
