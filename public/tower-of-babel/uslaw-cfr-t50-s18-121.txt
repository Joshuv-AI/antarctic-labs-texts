50 C.F.R. § 18.121 — Dates this subpart is in effect.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 18 (Marine Mammals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart is effective from October 19, 2026, through October 19, 2031, for year-round oil and gas exploration, development, production, and associated transportation activities.
