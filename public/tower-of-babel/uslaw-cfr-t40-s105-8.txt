40 C.F.R. § 105.8 — When can I apply for an award?
Citation: us:cfr:40:105.8
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-105.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 105 (Recognition Awards Under The Clean Water Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You can contact your local EPA Regional office for award submission deadline information which may vary for the award categories, or check the Web site at http://www.epa.gov/owm/intnet.htm.
