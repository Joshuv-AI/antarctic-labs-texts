10 U.S.C. § 454 — Exchange of mapping, charting, and geomatics data with foreign countries, international organizations, nongovernmental organizations, and academic institutions
Citation: us:usc:10:454
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section454
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And General Military Powers); Chapter 22 (National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency); Subchapter II (Maps, Charts, And Geomatics Products). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Foreign Countries and International Organizations.—
The Secretary of Defense may authorize the National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency to exchange or furnish mapping, charting, and geomatics data, supplies and services to a foreign country or international organization pursuant to an agreement for the production or exchange of such data.
(b) Nongovernmental Organizations and Academic Institutions.—
The Secretary may authorize the National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency to exchange or furnish mapping, charting, and geomatics data, supplies, and services relating to areas outside of the United States to a nongovernmental organization or an academic institution engaged in geospatial information research or production of such areas pursuant to an agreement for the production or exchange of such data.
