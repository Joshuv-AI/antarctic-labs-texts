24 C.F.R. § 92.65 — Funding sanctions.
Citation: us:cfr:24:92.65
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-92.65
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 92 (Home Investment Partnerships Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Following notice and opportunity for informal consultation, HUD may withhold, reduce or terminate the assistance where any corrective or remedial actions taken under § 92.551 fail to remedy an insular area's performance deficiencies, and the deficiencies are sufficiently substantial, in the judgment of HUD, to warrant sanctions.
