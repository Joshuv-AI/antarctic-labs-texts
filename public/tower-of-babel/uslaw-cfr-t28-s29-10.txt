28 C.F.R. § 29.10 — Owner withdrawal from the program.
Citation: us:cfr:28:29.10
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-29.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 29 (Motor Vehicle Theft Prevention Act Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An owner may withdraw from the program at any time by completely removing the program decal and changing the license plate if necessary. The owner is also encouraged to notify the participating agency in writing of such withdrawal.
