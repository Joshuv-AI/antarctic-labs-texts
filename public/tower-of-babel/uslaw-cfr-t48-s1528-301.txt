48 C.F.R. § 1528.301 — 1528.301 Insurance liability to third persons.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1528.301
Creator: Chapter 15—Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1528.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1528 (Bonds Of Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contracting officers shall insert the clause at 1552.228-70, Insurance Liability to Third Persons, in cost-reimbursement solicitations and contracts, except those for construction and architect-engineer services.
