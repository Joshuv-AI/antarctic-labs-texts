48 C.F.R. § 10.003 — 10.003 Contract clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:10.003
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-10.003
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 10 (Market Research). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.210-1, Market Research, in solicitations and contracts over $7.5 million, other than solicitations and contracts for the acquisition of commercial products or commercial services.
