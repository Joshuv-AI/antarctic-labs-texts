47 C.F.R. § 69.408 — All other customer services expenses in Account 6620.
Citation: us:cfr:47:69.408
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-69.408
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 69 (Access Charges). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All other customer services expenses shall be apportioned among the Interexchange category, the billing and collection category and all access elements based on the combined expenses in §§ 69.404 through 69.407.
