10 U.S.C. § 20252a — Special selection review boards: reference
Citation: us:usc:10:20252a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section20252a
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Space Force); Chapter 2005 (Officers); Subchapter IV (Failure Of Selection For Promotion And Involuntary Separation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Section 628a of this title, relating to the convening of a special selection review board when credible information of an adverse nature was not furnished to a promotion board, applies with respect to persons recommended by a selection board for promotion to a grade at or below the grade of major general in the Space Force.
