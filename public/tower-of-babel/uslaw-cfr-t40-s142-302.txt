40 C.F.R. § 142.302 — Who can issue a small system variance?
Citation: us:cfr:40:142.302
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-142.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 142 (National Primary Drinking Water Regulations Implementation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A small system variance under this subpart may only be issued by either:
(a) A State that is exercising primary enforcement responsibility under Subpart B for public water systems under the State's jurisdiction; or
(b) The Administrator, for a public water system in a State which does not have primary enforcement responsibility.
