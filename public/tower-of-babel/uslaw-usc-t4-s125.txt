4 U.S.C. § 125 — Nonseverability
Citation: us:usc:4:125
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title4-section125
Context: United States Code, Title 4 (Flag And Seal, Seat Of Government, And The States); Chapter 4 (The States). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If a court of competent jurisdiction enters a final judgment on the merits that—
(1)
is based on Federal law;
(2)
is no longer subject to appeal; and
(3)
substantially limits or impairs the essential elements of sections 116 through 126 of this title,
then sections 116 through 126 of this title are invalid and have no legal effect as of the date of entry of such judgment.
