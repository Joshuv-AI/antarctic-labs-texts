Editorial
Authors: Joanne Murphy-Ullrich
Published: 2026

Dear Editorial Board Members,

I am writing to share my decision to retire as Editor-in-Chief of Matrix Biology and Matrix Biology Plus at the end of May. It has been my privilege and honor to serve as Editor (2021) and Editor-in-Chief (since 2022) of the two essential journals of the extracellular matrix field. My retirement has been in the planning process for nearly a year and it was essential that the right person lead the journals going forward. I am most pleased to announce that Sylvie Ricard-Blum will become the next Editor-in-Chief of Matrix Biology and Matrix Biology Plus on June 1, 2026. Dr. Ricard-Blum is well known and highly respected in the matrix community, having served as President of the French Society for Matrix Biology and President of ISMB (2019–2020), as well as Editor of the ISMB newsletter. More recently, she received the ISMB Distinguished Investigator Award in 2025. A link to her important and numerous contributions to the extracellular matrix field can be found in this link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/matrix-biology/about/editorial-board. Importantly, Sylvie has served as Editor of Matrix Biology and Matrix Biology Plus since 2021 with diligence, wisdom, fairness, and a commitment to scientific integrity. Critically, Sylvie has a well-informed and broad perspective of the state of the ECM field, its potential and its challenges, and a vision for its future. I could not retire in good conscience if I did not feel confident in her ability to wisely lead our journals. I hope you will all welcome and support Dr. Ricard-Blum in her new role as Editor-in-Chief.

Like the matrix, we are all connected and it has been a joy to work together as a community of scientists to support this vital field. In collaboration with a wonderful international team of editors (Suneel Apte, Clair Baldock, Sylvie Ricard-Blum, Hiromi Yanagisawa, and Roy Zent), we have tried to guide the journals through a transitional period and instituted necessary standards for scientific rigor and new features such as the Matrix in Medicine series and the Protocols and Methods feature. I have thoroughly enjoyed the interactions with our Editors at our constructive monthly meetings which have provided perspective, new insights, support, and humor! I will miss these meetings. I also owe thanks to Guest Editors of Special Issues published during my tenure. They rose to the task of learning the intricacies of Editorial Manager and oversaw the recruitment and review of important state-of-the-art contributions. Of course, the journals could not run without the greatly appreciated and timely support from our Journal Managers (Sridhar Venkataraman, Nivedha Manoharan) and the Publishers (Lynn Sherrer, Han Xi) who helped the journals address operational concerns.

Most importantly, thank you to our Editorial Board Members. It is your commitment, considerable expertise, and thoughtful reviews that have ensured that the high standard we seek is achieved. The specialized knowledge that you bring to these reviews distinguishes Matrix Biology and Matrix Biology Plus from most journals. Please continue to support our journals by submitting your work for publication and by continuing your commitment to review manuscripts. Your contributions to the advancement of the science of matrix biology cannot be appreciated enough.

Sincere thanks and all the best.
