16 C.F.R. § 1303.4 — Banned hazardous products.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1303.4
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1303.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1303 (Ban Of Lead-Containing Paint And Certain Consumer Products Bearing Lead-Containing Paint). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following consumer products, manufactured after February 27, 1978, unless exempted by § 1303.3, are banned hazardous products (see definitions in § 1303.2):
(a) Paint and other similar surface-coating materials which are “lead-containing paint.”
(b) Toys and other articles intended for use by children that bear “lead-containing paint.”
(c) Furniture articles that bear “lead-containing paint.”
