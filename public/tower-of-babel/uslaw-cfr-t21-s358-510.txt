21 C.F.R. § 358.510 — Corn and callus remover active ingredients.
Citation: us:cfr:21:358.510
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-358.510
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 358 (Miscellaneous External Drug Products For Over-The-Counter Human Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The product consists of any of the following active ingredients within the specified concentrations and in the dosage form established for each ingredient.
(a) Salicylic acid 12 to 40 percent in a plaster vehicle.
(b) Salicylic acid 12 to 17.6 percent in a collodion-like vehicle.
