7 U.S.C. § 79d — Limitation on administrative and supervisory costs
Citation: us:usc:7:79d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section79d
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 3 (Grain Standards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The total administrative and supervisory costs which may be incurred under this chapter for services performed (excluding standardization, compliance, and foreign monitoring activities) for each of the fiscal years 1989 through 2025 1 shall not exceed 30 percent of the total costs for such activities carried out by the Secretary for such year.
