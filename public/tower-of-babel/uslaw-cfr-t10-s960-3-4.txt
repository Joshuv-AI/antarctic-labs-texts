10 C.F.R. § 960.3-4 — -4 Environmental impacts.
Citation: us:cfr:10:960.3-4
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-960.3-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 960 (General Guidelines For The Preliminary Screening Of Potential Sites For A Nuclear Waste Repository). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Environmental impacts shall be considered by the DOE throughout the site characterization, site selection, and repository development process. The DOE shall mitigate significant adverse environmental impacts, to the extent practicable, during site characterization and repository construction, operation, closure, and decommissioning.
