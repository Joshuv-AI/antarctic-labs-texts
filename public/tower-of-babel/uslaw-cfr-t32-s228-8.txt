32 C.F.R. § 228.8 — Prohibition on photographic or electronic recording or transmitting equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:32:228.8
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-228.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 228 (Security Protective Force). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person entering or while on protected property shall bring or possess any kind of photographic, recording or transmitting equipment (including but not limited to cameras, cellular telephones, or recorders), except as specially authorized by the NSA Director of Security or his designee at each Agency facility.
