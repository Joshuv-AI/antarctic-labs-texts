43 C.F.R. § 4.320 — Who may appeal a probate judge's order?
Citation: us:cfr:43:4.320
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4.320
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4 (Department Of The Interior Hearings And Appeals Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any interested party has a right to appeal to the Board if he or she is adversely affected by an order of a probate judge under part 30 of this subtitle:
(a) On a petition for rehearing;
(b) On a petition for reopening;
(c) Regarding purchase of interests in a deceased Indian's estate; or
(d) Regarding added or omitted property; or
(e) Determining that a person for whom a probate proceeding is sought is not deceased.
