2 C.F.R. § 1.100 — Content of this title.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1.100
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1 (About Title 2 Of The Code Of Federal Regulations And Subtitle A). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This title contains:
(a) Office of Management and Budget (OMB) guidance to Federal agencies on government-wide policies for the award and administration of Federal financial assistance; and
(b) Federal agency regulations implementing that OMB guidance.
