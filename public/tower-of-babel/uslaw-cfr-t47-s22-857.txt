47 C.F.R. § 22.857 — Channel plan for commercial aviation air-ground systems.
Citation: us:cfr:47:22.857
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-22.857
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 22 (Public Mobile Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The 849-851 MHz and 894-896 MHz frequency bands are designated for paired nationwide exclusive assignment to the licensee or licensees of systems providing radio telecommunications service, including voice and/or data service, to persons on board aircraft. Air-ground systems operating in these frequency bands are referred to in this part as “commercial aviation” systems.
