29 U.S.C. § 182 — Exemption of Railway Labor Act from subchapter
Citation: us:usc:29:182
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section182
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 7 (Labor-Management Relations); Subchapter III (Conciliation Of Labor Disputes; National Emergencies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of this subchapter shall not be applicable with respect to any matter which is subject to the provisions of the Railway Labor Act [45 U.S.C. 151 et seq.], as amended from time to time.
