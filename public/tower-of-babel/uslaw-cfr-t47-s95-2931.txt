47 C.F.R. § 95.2931 — Permissible use of PLBs and MSLDs.
Citation: us:cfr:47:95.2931
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-95.2931
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 95 (Personal Radio Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) PLBs may be used only for transmission of distress and safety of life communications.
(b) MSLDs may be used only to aid in the location of persons in the water.
