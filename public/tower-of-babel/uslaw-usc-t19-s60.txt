19 U.S.C. § 60 — Penalty for extortion
Citation: us:usc:19:60
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section60
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 1 (Collection Districts, Ports, And Officers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Every officer of the customs who demands or receives any other or greater fee, compensation, or reward than is allowed by law, for performing any duty or service required from him by law, shall be liable to a penalty of $200 for each offense, recoverable to the use of the party aggrieved.
