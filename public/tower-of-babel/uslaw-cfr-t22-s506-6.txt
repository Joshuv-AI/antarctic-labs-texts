22 C.F.R. § 506.6 — Publicizing vacancies.
Citation: us:cfr:22:506.6
Creator: United States Agency For Global Media
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-506.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 506 (Part-Time Career Employment Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When applicants from outside the Federal service are desired, part-time vacancies may be publicized through various recruiting means, such as:
(a) Federal Job Information Centers.
(b) State Employment Offices.
(c) Broadcasting Board of Governors Vacancy Announcements.
(d) College and University Placement Offices.
