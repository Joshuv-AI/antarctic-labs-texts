21 C.F.R. § 516.23 — Timing of requests for MUMS-drug designation.
Citation: us:cfr:21:516.23
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-516.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 516 (New Animal Drugs For Minor Use And Minor Species). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A sponsor may request MUMS-drug designation at any time in the drug development process prior to the submission of an application for either conditional approval or approval of the MUMS drug for which designation is being requested.
