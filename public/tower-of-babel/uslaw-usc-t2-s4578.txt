2 U.S.C. § 4578 — Restriction on payment of dual compensation by Secretary of Senate
Citation: us:usc:2:4578
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section4578
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 45 (Congressional Pay And Benefits); Subchapter III (Senate); Part A (Amount And Type). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Unless otherwise specifically authorized by law, no part of any appropriation disbursed by the Secretary of the Senate shall be available for payment of compensation to any person holding any position, for any period for which such person received compensation for holding any other position, the compensation for which is disbursed by the Secretary of the Senate.
