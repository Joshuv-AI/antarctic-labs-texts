29 C.F.R. § 1912.26 — Approval of agenda.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1912.26
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1912.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1912 (Advisory Committees On Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each meeting of an advisory committee shall be conducted in accordance with an agenda approved by the Assistant Secretary or his representative designated for this purpose. No particular form for the agency is prescribed.
