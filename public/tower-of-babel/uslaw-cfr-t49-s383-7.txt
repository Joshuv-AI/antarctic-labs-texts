49 C.F.R. § 383.7 — Validity of CDL issued by decertified State.
Citation: us:cfr:49:383.7
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-383.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 383 (Commercial Driver'S License Standards; Requirements And Penalties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A CDL issued by a State prior to the date the State is notified by the Administrator, in accordance with the provisions of § 384.405 of this subchapter, that the State is prohibited from issuing CDLs, will remain valid until its stated expiration date.
