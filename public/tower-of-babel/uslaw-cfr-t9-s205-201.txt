9 C.F.R. § 205.201 — System operator.
Citation: us:cfr:9:205.201
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Fair Trade Practices Program), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-205.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 205 (Clear Title—Protection For Purchasers Of Farm Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The system operator can be the Secretary of State of a State, or any designee of the State pursuant to its laws. Note that the provision in subsection (c)(2) for a system refers to operation by the Secretary of State of a State, but the definition in (c)(11) of “Secretary of State” includes “designee of the State.”
