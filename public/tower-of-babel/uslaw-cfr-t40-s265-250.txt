40 C.F.R. § 265.250 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:40:265.250
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-265.250
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 265 (Interim Status Standards For Owners And Operators Of Hazardous Waste Treatment, Storage, And Disposal Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this subpart apply to owners and operators of facilities that treat or store hazardous waste in piles, except as § 265.1 provides otherwise. Alternatively, a pile of hazardous waste may be managed as a landfill under subpart N.
