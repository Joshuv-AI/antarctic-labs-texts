33 C.F.R. § 95.050 — Responsibility for compliance.
Citation: us:cfr:33:95.050
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-95.050
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 95 (Operating A Vessel While Under The Influence Of Alcohol Or A Dangerous Drug). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The marine employer shall exercise due diligence to assure compliance with the applicable provisions of this part.
(b) If the marine employer has reason to believe that an individual is intoxicated, the marine employer shall not allow that individual to stand watch or perform other duties.
