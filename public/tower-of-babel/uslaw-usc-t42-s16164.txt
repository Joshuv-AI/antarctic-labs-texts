42 U.S.C. § 16164 — Cost sharing
Citation: us:usc:42:16164
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section16164
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 149 (National Energy Policy And Programs); Subchapter VIII (Hydrogen). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The costs of carrying out projects and activities under this subchapter shall be shared in accordance with section 16352 of this title.
