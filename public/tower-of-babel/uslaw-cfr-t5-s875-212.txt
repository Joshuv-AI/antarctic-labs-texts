5 C.F.R. § 875.212 — Is there a minimum application age?
Citation: us:cfr:5:875.212
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-875.212
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 875 (Federal Long Term Care Insurance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, there is a minimum application age. You must be at least 18 years old at the time you submit an application for coverage.
