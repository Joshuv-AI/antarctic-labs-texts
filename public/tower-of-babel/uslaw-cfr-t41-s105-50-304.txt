41 C.F.R. § 105-50.304 — -50.304 Services provided through revolving funds.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-50.304
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-50.304
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-50 (50—Provision Of Special Or Technical Services To State And Local Units Of Government). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where the service furnished is of the type which GSA is now billing through revolving funds, reimbursement shall be obtained from State and local governments on the same basis; i.e., the same pricing method, billing forms, and billing support shall be used.
