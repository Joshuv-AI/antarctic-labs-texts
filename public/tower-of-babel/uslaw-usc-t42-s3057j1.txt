42 U.S.C. § 3057j–1 — Distribution of funds among organizations
Citation: us:usc:42:3057j–1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section3057j–1
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 35 (Programs For Older Americans); Subchapter X (Grants For Native Americans); Part B (Native Hawaiian Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Subject to the availability of appropriations to carry out this part, the amount of the grant (if any) made under this part to an organization for fiscal year 1992 and for each subsequent fiscal year shall be not less than the amount of the grant made under this part to the organization for fiscal year 1991.
