30 U.S.C. § 359 — Rules and regulations
Citation: us:usc:30:359
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section359
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 7 (Lease Of Mineral Deposits Within Acquired Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior is authorized to prescribe such rules and regulations as are necessary and appropriate to carry out the purposes of this chapter, which rules and regulations shall be the same as those prescribed under the mineral leasing laws to the extent that they are applicable.
