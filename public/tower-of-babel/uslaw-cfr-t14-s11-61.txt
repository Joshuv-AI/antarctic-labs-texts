14 C.F.R. § 11.61 — May I ask FAA to adopt, amend, or repeal a regulation, or grant relief from the requirements of a current regulation?
Citation: us:cfr:14:11.61
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-11.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 11 (General Rulemaking Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Using a petition for rulemaking, you may ask FAA to add a new regulation to title 14 of the Code of Federal Regulations (14 CFR) or ask FAA to amend or repeal a current regulation in 14 CFR.
(b) Using a petition for exemption, you may ask FAA to grant you relief from current regulations in 14 CFR.
