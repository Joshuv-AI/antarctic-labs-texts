9 C.F.R. § 108.1 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:9:108.1
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-108.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 108 (Facility Requirements For Licensed Establishments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless otherwise authorized by the Administrator, all buildings, appurtenances, and equipment used in the preparation of biological products shall be in compliance with the regulations in this part. Each land area on which such buildings and appurtenances are located shall be identified by an address which shall appear on the establishment license.
