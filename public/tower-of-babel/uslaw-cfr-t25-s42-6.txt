25 C.F.R. § 42.6 — When does due process require a formal disciplinary hearing?
Citation: us:cfr:25:42.6
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-42.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 42 (Student Rights). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless local school policies and procedures provide for less, a formal disciplinary hearing is required before a suspension in excess of 10 days or expulsion.
