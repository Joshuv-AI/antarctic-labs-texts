22 U.S.C. § 1641g — Certification of awards
Citation: us:usc:22:1641g
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1641g
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 21 (Settlement Of International Claims); Subchapter III (Claims Against Bulgaria, Hungary, Rumania, Italy, And The Soviet Union). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commission shall as soon as possible, and in the order of the making of such awards, certify to the Secretary of the Treasury, in terms of United States currency, each award made pursuant to this subchapter.
