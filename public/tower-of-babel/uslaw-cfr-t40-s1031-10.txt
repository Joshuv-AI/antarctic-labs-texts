40 C.F.R. § 1031.10 — State standards and controls.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1031.10
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1031.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1031 (Control Of Air Pollution From Aircraft Engines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No State or political subdivision of a State may adopt or attempt to enforce any aircraft or aircraft engine standard with respect to emissions unless the standard is identical to a standard that applies to aircraft or aircraft engines under this part.
