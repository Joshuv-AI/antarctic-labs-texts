2 C.F.R. § 3700.137 — Who in the Peace Corps may grant an exception to let an excluded person participate in a covered transaction?
Citation: us:cfr:2:3700.137
Creator: Peace Corps
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-3700.137
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 3700 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director of the Peace Corps has the authority to grant an exception to let an excluded person participate in a covered transaction, as provided in the OMB guidance at 2 CFR 180.135.
