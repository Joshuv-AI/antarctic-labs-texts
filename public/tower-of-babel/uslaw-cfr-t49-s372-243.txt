49 C.F.R. § 372.243 — Controlling distances and population data.
Citation: us:cfr:49:372.243
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-372.243
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 372 (Exemptions, Commercial Zones, And Terminal Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the application of § 372.241:
(a) Air-line distances or mileages about corporate limits of municipalities shall be used.
(b) The population of any municipality shall be deemed to be the highest figure shown for that municipality in any decennial census since (and including) the 1940 decennial census.
(c) Contraction of municipal boundaries will not alter the size of commercial zones.
