28 U.S.C. § 1872 — Issues of fact in Supreme Court
Citation: us:usc:28:1872
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1872
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part V (Procedure); Chapter 121 (Juries; Trial By Jury). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In all original actions at law in the Supreme Court against citizens of the United States, issues of fact shall be tried by a jury.
