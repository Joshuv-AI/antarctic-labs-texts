43 C.F.R. § 3512.11 — Once BLM issues me a permit or lease, may I assign or sublease it?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3512.11
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3512.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3500 (Leasing Of Solid Minerals Other Than Coal And Oil Shale). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may assign or sublease your permit or lease in whole or in part to any person, association, or corporation qualified to hold a permit or lease.
