40 U.S.C. § 1305 — Disposition of land acquired by devise
Citation: us:usc:40:1305
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section1305
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Chapter 13 (Public Property). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The General Services Administration may take custody, for disposal as excess property under this subtitle and division C (except sections 3302, 3501(b), 3509, 3906, 4710, and 4711) of subtitle I of title 41, of land acquired by the Federal Government by devise.
