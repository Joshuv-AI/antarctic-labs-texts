37 C.F.R. § 104.3 — Waiver of rules.
Citation: us:cfr:37:104.3
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-104.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 104 (Legal Processes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In extraordinary situations, when the interest of justice requires, the General Counsel may waive or suspend the rules of this part, sua sponte or on petition of an interested party to the Director, subject to such requirements as the General Counsel may impose. Any such petition must be accompanied by a petition fee of $130.00.
