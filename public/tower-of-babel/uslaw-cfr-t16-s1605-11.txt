16 C.F.R. § 1605.11 — Remedies for failure to permit authorized investigations.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1605.11
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1605.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1605 (Investigations, Inspections And Inquiries Pursuant To The Flammable Fabrics Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the event of failure to comply with any investigative process authorized by these rules, the Commission may seek appropriate action pursuant to the authority conferred by the Federal Trade Commission Act, including actions for enforcement, forfeitures, penalities, or criminal sanctions.
