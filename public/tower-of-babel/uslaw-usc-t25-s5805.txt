25 U.S.C. § 5805 — Schools to business incubator pipeline
Citation: us:usc:25:5805
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section5805
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 50 (Native American Business Incubators Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall facilitate the establishment of relationships between eligible applicants receiving funds through the program and educational institutions serving Native American communities, including tribal colleges and universities.
