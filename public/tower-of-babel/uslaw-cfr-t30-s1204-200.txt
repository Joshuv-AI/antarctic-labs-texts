30 C.F.R. § 1204.200 — What is the purpose of this subpart?
Citation: us:cfr:30:1204.200
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1204.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1204 (Alternatives For Marginal Properties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart explains how you as a lessee or designee may obtain accounting and auditing relief for your Federal onshore or OCS lease production from a marginal property. The two types of accounting and auditing relief that you can receive under this subpart are cumulative reports and payment relief (explained in § 1204.202) and other accounting and auditing relief appropriate for your property (explained in § 1204.203).
