43 C.F.R. § 3192.18 — Can I appeal a BLM decision?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3192.18
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3192.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3190 (Delegation Of Authority, Cooperative Agreements And Contracts For Oil And Gas Inspection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any party adversely affected by a BLM decision made under this subpart may appeal the decision in accordance with parts 4 and 1840 of this title.
