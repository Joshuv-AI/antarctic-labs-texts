27 C.F.R. § 28.245 — Shipment to foreign-trade zone.
Citation: us:cfr:27:28.245
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-28.245
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 28 (Exportation Of Alcohol). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where distilled spirits (including specially denatured spirits), wines, or beer, are transferred to a foreign-trade zone for exportation or for storage pending exportation, the shipment shall be consigned to the Zone Operator in care of the customs officer in charge of the zone.
