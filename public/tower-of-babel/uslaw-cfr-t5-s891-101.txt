5 C.F.R. § 891.101 — Relationship to part 890 of this chapter.
Citation: us:cfr:5:891.101
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-891.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 891 (Retired Federal Employees Health Benefits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part does not apply to the Federal Employees Health Benefits Program which is governed by part 890 of this chapter. Part 890 of this chapter does not apply to the Retired Federal Employees Health Benefits Program which is governed by this part.
