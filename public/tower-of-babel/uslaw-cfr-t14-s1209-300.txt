14 C.F.R. § 1209.300 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1209.300
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1209.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1209 (Boards And Committees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart continues in effect the Contract Adjustment Board (hereinafter referred to as “the Board”) to consider and dispose of requests for extraordinary contractual adjustments by contractors of the National Aeronautics and Space Administration (hereinafter referred to as NASA).
