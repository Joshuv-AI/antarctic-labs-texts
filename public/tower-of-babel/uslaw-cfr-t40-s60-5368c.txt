40 C.F.R. § 60.5368c — c What if my state or Tribal plan is not approvable?
Citation: us:cfr:40:60.5368c
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.5368c
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 60 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you do not submit a state or Tribal plan (or a negative declaration letter) by January 22, 2027, or if EPA disapproves your state plan, EPA will develop a Federal plan according to § 60.27a(c) through (f) to implement the emission guidelines contained in this subpart.
