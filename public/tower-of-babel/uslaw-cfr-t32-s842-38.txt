32 C.F.R. § 842.38 — Who are not proper claimants.
Citation: us:cfr:32:842.38
Creator: Department Of The Air Force
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-842.38
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 842 (Administrative Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Governments of foreign nations, their agencies, political subdivisions, or municipalities.
(b) Agencies and nonappropriated fund instrumentalities (NAFIs) of the U.S. Government.
(c) Subrogees of § 842.42(a) and (b).
(d) Inhabitants of foreign countries.
