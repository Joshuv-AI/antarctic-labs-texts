25 C.F.R. § 20.102 — What is the Bureau's policy in providing financial assistance and social services under this part?
Citation: us:cfr:25:20.102
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-20.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 20 (Financial Assistance And Social Services Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Bureau social services programs are a secondary, or residual resource, and must not be used to supplement or supplant other programs.
(b) The Bureau can provide assistance under this part to eligible Indians when comparable financial assistance or social services are either not available or not provided by state, tribal, county, local or other federal agencies.
(c) Bureau financial assistance and social services are subject to annual Congressional appropriations.
