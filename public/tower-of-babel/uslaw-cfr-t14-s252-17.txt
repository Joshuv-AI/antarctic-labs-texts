14 C.F.R. § 252.17 — Enforcement.
Citation: us:cfr:14:252.17
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-252.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 252 (Smoking Aboard Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Air carriers and foreign air carriers shall take such action as is necessary to ensure that smoking by passengers or crew is not permitted where smoking is prohibited by this part, including but not limited to aircraft lavatories.
