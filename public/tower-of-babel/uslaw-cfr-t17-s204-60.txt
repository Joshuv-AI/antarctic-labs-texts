17 C.F.R. § 204.60 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:17:204.60
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-204.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 204 (Rules Relating To Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart provides procedures for the Commission to collect money from a debtor's disposable pay by means of administrative wage garnishment to satisfy a delinquent nontax debt owed to the United States.
