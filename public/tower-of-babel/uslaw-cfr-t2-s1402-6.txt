2 C.F.R. § 1402.6 — Real property.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1402.6
Creator: Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1402.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1402 (Financial Assistance Interior Regulation, Supplementing The Uniform Administrative Requirements, Cost Principles, And Audit Requirements For Federal Awards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Real property has the same meaning as set forth in 2 CFR 200.1, except that the definition in this section also applies to legal ownership interests in land such as easements.
