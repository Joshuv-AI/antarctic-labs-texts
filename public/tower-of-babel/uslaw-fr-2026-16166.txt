    On August 3, 2026, pursuant to Rule 212 of the Commission's Rules 
of Practice and Procedure,\1\ California Independent System Operator 
Corporation; DCR Transmission, L.L.C.; and Joint Participating 
Transmission Owners (PTOs) \2\ each filed motions to hold the show 
cause proceeding in Docket No. EL26-71-000 in abeyance for 90 days. 
Also on August 3, 2026, Six Cities \3\ filed a conditional motion to 
hold the show cause proceeding in Docket No. EL26-71-000 in abeyance 
for 90 days.\4\

    \1\ 18 CFR 385.212 (2025).
    \2\ Joint PTOs include: Southern California Edison Company;

Transmission LLC; Citizens S-Line Transmission LLC; SunZia

    \3\ Six Cities include the Cities of Anaheim, Azusa, Banning,

    \4\ Six Cities state that the motion is conditional because they 
have filed a motion to rescind the show cause proceeding, which if
