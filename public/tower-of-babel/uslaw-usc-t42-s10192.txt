42 U.S.C. § 10192 — Applicability
Citation: us:usc:42:10192
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section10192
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 108 (Nuclear Waste Policy); Subchapter II (Research, Development, And Demonstration Regarding Disposal Of High-Level Radioactive Waste And Spent Nuclear Fuel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of this subchapter are subject to section 10107 of this title and shall not apply to facilities that are used for the disposal of high-level radioactive waste, low-level radioactive waste, transuranic waste, or spent nuclear fuel resulting from atomic energy defense activities.
