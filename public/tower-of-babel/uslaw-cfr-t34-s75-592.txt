34 C.F.R. § 75.592 — Federal evaluation—satisfying requirement for grantee evaluation.
Citation: us:cfr:34:75.592
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-75.592
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 75 (Direct Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a grantee cooperates in a Federal evaluation of a program, the Secretary may determine that the grantee meets the evaluation requirements of the program, including § 75.590.
