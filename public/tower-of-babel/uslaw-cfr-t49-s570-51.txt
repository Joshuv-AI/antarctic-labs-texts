49 C.F.R. § 570.51 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:49:570.51
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-570.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 570 (Vehicle In Use Inspection Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part specifies standards and procedures for the inspection of brake, steering and suspension systems, and tire and wheel assemblies, of motor vehicles in use with a gross vehicle weight rating of more than 10,000 pounds.
