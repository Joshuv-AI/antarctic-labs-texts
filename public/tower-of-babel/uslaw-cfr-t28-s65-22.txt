28 C.F.R. § 65.22 — Exclusions.
Citation: us:cfr:28:65.22
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-65.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 65 (Emergency Federal Law Enforcement Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Excluded from the situations for which this assistance is intended are:
(a) The perceived need for planning or other activities related to crowd control for general public safety projects; and,
(b) A situation requiring the enforcement of laws associated with scheduled public events, including political conventions and sports events.
