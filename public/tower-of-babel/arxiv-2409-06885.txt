Alternative Bell’s states and Teleportation
Juan M. Romero, Emiliano Montoya-González, Oscar Velazquez-Alvarado

Abstract
Bell’s states are among the most useful in quantum computing. These state are an orthonormal base of entagled states with two qubits. We propose alternative bases of entangled states. Some of these states depend on a continuous parameter. We present the quantum circuit and code of these alternative bases. In addition, we study quantum teleportation with these entangled states and present their quantum circuits and codes associated .

\displaystyle A_{0}=\begin{pmatrix}a_{00}&a_{01}\\

a_{32}&a_{33}\end{pmatrix},

(7)

\displaystyle\det A_{i}=a_{i0}a_{i3}-a_{i1}a_{i2}\not=0,\quad a_{ij}\in\mathbb{C},ij=0,1,2,3

\displaystyle a_{10}\ket{00}+a_{11}\ket{01}+a_{12}\ket{10}+a_{13}\ket{11},

\displaystyle\ket{V_{2}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle a_{20}\ket{00}+a_{21}\ket{01}+a_{22}\ket{10}+a_{23}\ket{11},

\displaystyle\ket{V_{3}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle a_{30}\ket{00}+a_{31}\ket{01}+a_{32}\ket{10}+a_{33}\ket{11}.

Notices that to obtain a base of orthonormal enangled states
\ket{V_{j}}
the equation

\displaystyle\braket{V_{i}|V_{j}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle a_{i0}^{*}a_{j0}+a_{i1}^{*}a_{j1}+a_{i2}^{*}a_{j2}+a_{i3}^{*}a_{j3}=\delta_{ij},

must be satisfied. This equation which can be written as

\displaystyle Tr\left(A^{\dagger}_{i}A_{j}\right)=\braket{V_{i}|V_{j}}=\delta_{ij}.

In addition, notice that by using the matrix

\displaystyle T=\begin{pmatrix}a_{00}&a_{01}&a_{02}&a_{03}\\
a_{10}&a_{11}&a_{12}&a_{13}\\
a_{20}&a_{21}&a_{22}&a_{23}\\
a_{30}&a_{31}&a_{32}&a_{3}\\
\end{pmatrix}

(9)

the states

can be written as

\displaystyle\begin{pmatrix}\ket{V_{0}}\\
\ket{V_{1}}\\
\ket{V_{2}}\\

\ket{01}\\
\ket{10}\\
\ket{11}\end{pmatrix},

\displaystyle\ket{V_{i}}=T_{ij}\ket{C_{j}},

Quantum teleportation

The basic teleportation algorithm consider three qubits. The first one is the target qubit, the second one is the Alice qubit and the third one is the Bob qubit.
