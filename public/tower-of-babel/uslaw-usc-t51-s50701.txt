51 U.S.C. § 50701 — Definition of Office
Citation: us:usc:51:50701
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section50701
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 507 (Office Of Space Commerce). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter, the term “Office” means the Office of Space Commerce established in section 50702 of this title.
