25 C.F.R. § 900.222 — What goes into a decision?

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 900 (Contracts Under The Indian Self-Determination And Education Assistance Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A decision shall:
(a) Describe the claim or dispute;
(b) Refer to the relevant terms of the contract;
(c) Set out the factual areas of agreement and disagreement;
(d) Set out the actual decision, based on the facts, and outline the reasoning which supports the decision; and
