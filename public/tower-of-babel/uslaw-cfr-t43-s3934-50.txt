43 C.F.R. § 3934.50 — Bona fide purchasers.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3934.50
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3934.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3930 (Management Of Oil Shale Exploration And Leases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The BLM will not cancel a lease or an interest in a lease of a purchaser if at the time of purchase the purchaser was not aware and could not have reasonably determined from the BLM records the existence of a violation of any of the following:
(a) Federal regulatory requirements;
(b) The Act, as amended; or
(c) Lease terms and conditions.
