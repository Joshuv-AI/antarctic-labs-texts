47 C.F.R. § 74.1290 — Political programming rules applicable to program originating FM booster stations.
Citation: us:cfr:47:74.1290
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-74.1290
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 74 (Experimental Radio, Auxiliary, Special Broadcast And Other Program Distributional Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To the extent a program originating FM booster station originates programming different than that broadcast by its FM primary station, pursuant to the limits set forth in § 74.1201(f)(2), it shall comply with the requirements in §§ 73.1212, 73.1940, 73.1941, 73.1942, 73.1943, and 73.1944 of this chapter.
