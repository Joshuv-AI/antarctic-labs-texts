12 C.F.R. § 749.3 — Vital records center and third-party service providers.
Citation: us:cfr:12:749.3
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-749.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 749 (Vital Records Preservation Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A credit union must maintain, or contract with a third-party service provider to maintain, any equipment or software for its vital records center necessary for the credit union to access its records. If a credit union contracts with a third-party service provider to maintain its records, the credit union must maintain effective oversight of the third-party service provider to ensure the records meet the requirements of this section.
