36 C.F.R. § 18.11 — What general provisions must a lease contain?
Citation: us:cfr:36:18.11
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-18.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 18 (Leasing Of Properties In Park Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All leases entered into under this part must contain terms and conditions that are determined necessary by the Director to assure use of the leased property in a manner consistent with the purposes of the applicable park area as established by law, and where applicable, to assure the preservation of historic property.
