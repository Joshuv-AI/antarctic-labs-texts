36 C.F.R. § 1211.455 — Textbooks and curricular material.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1211.455

Year: 2026

Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in these Title IX regulations shall be interpreted as requiring or prohibiting or abridging in any way the use of particular textbooks or curricular materials.
