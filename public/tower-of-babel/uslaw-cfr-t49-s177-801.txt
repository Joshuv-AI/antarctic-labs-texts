49 C.F.R. § 177.801 — Unacceptable hazardous materials shipments.
Citation: us:cfr:49:177.801
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-177.801
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 177 (Carriage By Public Highway). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person may accept for transportation or transport by motor vehicle a forbidden material or hazardous material that is not prepared in accordance with the requirements of this subchapter.
