25 C.F.R. § 169.126 — May a right-of-way contain a preference consistent with tribal law for employment of tribal members?
Citation: us:cfr:25:169.126
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-169.126
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 169 (Rights-Of-Way Over Indian Land). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A grant of right-of-way over or across Indian land may include a provision, consistent with tribal law, requiring the grantee to give a preference to qualified tribal members, based on their political affiliation with the tribe.
