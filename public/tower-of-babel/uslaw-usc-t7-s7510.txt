7 U.S.C. § 7510 — Commodities not income
Citation: us:usc:7:7510
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section7510
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 102 (Emergency Food Assistance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, commodities distributed under this chapter shall not be considered income or resources for any purposes under any Federal, State, or local law.
