29 C.F.R. § 785.40 — When private automobile is used in travel away from home community.
Citation: us:cfr:29:785.40
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-785.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 785 (Hours Worked). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an employee is offered public transporation but requests permission to drive his car instead, the employer may count as hours worked either the time spent driving the car or the time he would have had to count as hours worked during working hours if the employee had used the public conveyance.
