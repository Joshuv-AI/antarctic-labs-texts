10 C.F.R. § 708.43 — Affirmative duty not to retaliate.
Citation: us:cfr:10:708.43
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-708.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 708 (Doe Contractor Employee Protection Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

DOE contractors will not retaliate against any employee because the employee (or any person acting at the request of the employee) has taken an action listed in § 708.5(a) through (c).
