46 U.S.C. § 80106 — International agreement on derelicts
Citation: us:usc:46:80106
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section80106
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 801 (Wrecks And Salvage). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may make an international agreement with other governments interested in the navigation of the North Atlantic Ocean, providing for the reporting, marking, and removal of dangerous wrecks, derelicts, and other menaces to navigation outside the coast waters of the countries bordering the North Atlantic Ocean.
