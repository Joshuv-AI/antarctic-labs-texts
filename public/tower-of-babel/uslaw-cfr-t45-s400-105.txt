45 C.F.R. § 400.105 — Mandatory services.
Citation: us:cfr:45:400.105
Creator: Office Of Refugee Resettlement, Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-400.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 400 (Refugee Resettlement Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In providing refugee medical assistance to refugees, a State must provide at least the same services in the same manner and to the same extent as under the State's Medicaid program, as delineated in 42 CFR part 440.
