5 C.F.R. § 1636.111 — Notice.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1636.111
Creator: Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1636.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1636 (Enforcement Of Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap In Programs Or Activities Conducted By The Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agency shall make available to employees, applicants, participants, beneficiaries, and other interested persons such information regarding the provisions of this part and its applicability to the programs or activities conducted by the agency, and make such information available to them in such manner as the head of the agency finds necessary to apprise such persons of the protections against discrimination assured them by section 504 and this part.
