5 C.F.R. § 1501.1 — Name.

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1501.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1501 (Operations Of The International Organizations Employees Loyalty Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This Board shall be known as the International Organizations Employees Loyalty Board, and any reference to the “Board” in this part shall mean such International Organizations Employees Loyalty Board.
