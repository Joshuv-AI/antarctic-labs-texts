On some multiple solutions for a p ⁡ ( x ) p(x) -Laplace equation with supercritical growth
Lin Zhao

Abstract




Abstract:
We consider the multiplicity of solutions for the
p(x)
-Laplacian problems involving the supercritical Sobolev growth via Ricceri’s principle. By means of the truncation combining with De Giorgi iteration, we can extend the result about subcritical and critical growth to the supercritical growth and obtain at least three solutions for the
p(x)
Laplacian problem.

Keywords
: Degenerate elliptic equations; a priori estimates; supercritical Sobolev exponent; truncated technique

2020 Mathematics Subject Classification
: 35J70, 35B45, 35J62

Introduction and main result

In this paper we study the following problem

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}-\text{div}(|\nabla u|^{p(x)-2}\nabla u)=\lambda f(x,u)+\mu|u|^{q(x)-2}u,&\text{in}~\Omega,\\
u(x)=0,&\text{on}~\partial\Omega,\\
\end{array}\right.

where
\Omega\subset\mathbb{R}^{N}
is a bounded domain with smooth boundary,
p,q:\overline{\Omega}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}
are continuous and satisfy

\displaystyle 1<p_{-}=\min_{x\in\overline{\Omega}}p(x)\leq p_{+}=\max_{x\in\overline{\Omega}}p(x)<N,

(1.4)

\displaystyle p\in L_{+}^{\infty}(\Omega)=\{p\in L^{\infty}(\Omega)\mid\text{ess}\inf_{\Omega}p(x)\geq 1\},

(1.5)

\displaystyle p_{+}<p^{\ast}(x)=\frac{Np(x)}{N-p(x)},~q\succ p^{\ast},

(1.6)

means
\inf_{\Omega}(q(x)-p^{\ast}(x))>0

\lambda,\mu>0
are nonnegative parameters, and

is a Caratheodory functions satisfying

\displaystyle\sup_{s\in[-M,M]}|f(x,s)|<+\infty,~\forall M>0.

(1.7)

We suppose that the nonlinearity

satisfies the following conditions:

\displaystyle\lim_{|s|\rightarrow+\infty}\frac{f(x,s)}{|s|^{p(x)-1}}=0,~\text{uniformly~in}~x\in\Omega;

(1.8)

\displaystyle\lim_{|s|\rightarrow 0}\frac{f(x,s)}{|s|^{p(x)-1}}=0,~\text{uniformly~ in}~x\in\Omega;

(1.9)

\displaystyle\sup_{u\in W_{0}^{1,p(x)}}\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{u}f(x,s)dsdx>0.

(1.10)
