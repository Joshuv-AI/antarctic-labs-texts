14 C.F.R. § 380.61 — Operation by foreign charter operators.
Citation: us:cfr:14:380.61
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-380.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 380 (Public Charters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each foreign charter operator shall be registered under this subpart and file a prospectus under § 380.25 before organizing groups for transportation on Public Charters that originate in the United States.
(b) Each foreign charter registered under this subpart shall comply with the other provisions of this part directed to charter operators.
