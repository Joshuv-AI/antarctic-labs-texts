46 C.F.R. § 13.115 — Chemical testing requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:46:13.115
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-13.115
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 13 (Certification Of Tank Vessel Personnel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each applicant for an original tank vessel endorsement must provide evidence of having passed a chemical test for dangerous drugs or of qualifying for an exemption from testing in § 16.220 of this subchapter as specified in § 10.225(b)(5) of this subchapter.
