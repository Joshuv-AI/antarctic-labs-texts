5 C.F.R. § 1600.32 — Treatment accorded rollover funds.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1600.32
Creator: Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1600.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1600 (Employee Contribution Elections, Investment Elections, And Automatic Enrollment Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) All funds rolled over to the TSP pursuant to §§ 1600.30 and 1600.31 will be treated as employee contributions.
(b) All funds rolled over to the TSP pursuant to §§ 1600.30 and 1600.31 will be invested in accordance with the participant's investment election on file at the time the rollover is completed.
(c) Funds rolled over to the TSP pursuant to §§ 1600.30 and 1600.31 are not subject to the limits on contributions described in § 1600.22.
