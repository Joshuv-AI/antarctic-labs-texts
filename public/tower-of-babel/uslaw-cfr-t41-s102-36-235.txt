41 C.F.R. § 102-36.235 — -36.235 Money or intangible personal property disposal.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-36.235
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-36.235
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-36 (36—Disposition Of Excess Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Report intangible personal property to GSA. You must not transfer or dispose of this property without prior approval of GSA. Per 31 U.S.C. 324, the Secretary of the Treasury will dispose of money and negotiable instruments such as bonds, notes, or other securities.
