12 C.F.R. § 326.4 — Reports.
Citation: us:cfr:12:326.4
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-326.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 326 (Minimum Security Devices And Procedures And Bank Secrecy Act 1 Compliance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The security officer for each institution shall report at least annually to the institution's board of directors on the implementation, administration, and effectiveness of the security program.
