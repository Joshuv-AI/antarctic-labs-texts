34 C.F.R. § 682.103 — Applicability of subparts.
Citation: us:cfr:34:682.103
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-682.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 682 (Federal Family Education Loan (Ffel) Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Subpart B of this part contains general provisions that are applicable to all participants in the FFEL and Federal GSL programs.
(b) The administration of the FFEL programs by a guaranty agency is subject to subparts C, D, F, and G of this part.
(c) The Federal FFEL and Federal GSL programs are subject to subparts C, F, and G of this part.
(d) Certain requirements applicable to schools under all the FFEL and Federal GSL programs are set forth in subpart F of this part.
