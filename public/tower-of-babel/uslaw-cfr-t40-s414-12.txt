40 C.F.R. § 414.12 — Compliance date for pretreatment standards for existing sources (PSES).
Citation: us:cfr:40:414.12
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-414.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 414 (Organic Chemicals, Plastics, And Synthetic Fibers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All dischargers subject to PSES in this part must comply with the standards by no later than three years after date of promulgation in the Federal Register.
