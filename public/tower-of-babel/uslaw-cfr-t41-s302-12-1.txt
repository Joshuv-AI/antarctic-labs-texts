41 C.F.R. § 302-12.1 — -12.1 Determining use of an RSC.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-12.1
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-12.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-12 (12—Use Of A Relocation Services Company). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies determine whether an employee may use an RSC, choose which RSC they may use, and determine the contract terms to which they will be required to agree.
