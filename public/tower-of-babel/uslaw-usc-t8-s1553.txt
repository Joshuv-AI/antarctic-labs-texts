8 U.S.C. § 1553 — Assistant Commissioners and one District Director; compensation and salary grade
Citation: us:usc:8:1553
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title8-section1553
Context: United States Code, Title 8 (Aliens And Nationality); Chapter 13 (Immigration And Naturalization Service); Subchapter I (Organization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The compensation of the five assistant commissioners and one district director shall be at the rate of grade GS–16.
