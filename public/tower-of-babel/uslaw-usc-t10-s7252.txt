10 U.S.C. § 7252 — Service credit: regular enlisted members; service as an officer to be counted as enlisted service
Citation: us:usc:10:7252
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7252
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 733 (Miscellaneous Rights And Benefits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An enlisted member of the Regular Army is entitled to count active service as an officer in the Army as enlisted service for all purposes.
