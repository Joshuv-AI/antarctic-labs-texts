47 C.F.R. § 68.415 — Hearing aid-compatibility and volume control informal complaints.
Citation: us:cfr:47:68.415
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-68.415
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 68 (Connection Of Terminal Equipment To The Telephone Network). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Persons with complaints under § 68.4 that are not addressed by the states pursuant to § 68.414, and all other complaints regarding rules in this part pertaining to hearing aid compatibility and volume control, may bring informal complaints as described in §§ 68.416 through 68.420. All responsible parties of terminal equipment are subject to the informal complaint provisions specified in this section.
