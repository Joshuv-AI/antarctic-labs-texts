10 C.F.R. § 590.503 — Opinion and order on rehearing.
Citation: us:cfr:10:590.503
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-590.503
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 590 (Administrative Procedures With Respect To The Import And Export Of Natural Gas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon application for rehearing, the Assistant Secretary may grant or deny rehearing or may abrogate or modify the final opinion and order, conditional order, or emergency interim order with or without further proceedings.
