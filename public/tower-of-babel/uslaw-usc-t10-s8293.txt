10 U.S.C. § 8293 — Distinguished-service medal
Citation: us:usc:10:8293
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8293
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 837 (Decorations And Awards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may award a distinguished-service medal of appropriate design and a ribbon, together with a rosette or other device to be worn in place thereof, to any person who, while serving in any capacity with the Navy or the Marine Corps, distinguishes himself by exceptionally meritorious service to the United States in a duty of great responsibility.
