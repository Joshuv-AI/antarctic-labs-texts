46 C.F.R. § 64.69 — Location of the pressure relief device.
Citation: us:cfr:46:64.69
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-64.69
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 64 (Marine Portable Tanks And Cargo Handling Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A pressure relief device must be—
(a) Accessible for inspection and repair before stowage of the tank; and
(b) Attached so that escaping gas does not impinge on the tank or framework.
