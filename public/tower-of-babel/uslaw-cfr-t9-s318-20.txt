9 C.F.R. § 318.20 — Use of animal drugs.
Citation: us:cfr:9:318.20
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-318.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 318 (Entry Into Official Establishments; Reinspection And Preparation Of Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Animal drug residues are permitted in meat and meat food products if such residues are from drugs which have been approved by the Food and Drug Administration and any such drug residues are within tolerance levels approved by the Food and Drug Administration, unless otherwise determined by the Administrator and listed herein.
