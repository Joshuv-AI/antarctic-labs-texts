42 U.S.C. § 3153 — Prohibition on use of funds for attorney’s and consultant’s fees
Citation: us:usc:42:3153
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section3153
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 38 (Public Works And Economic Development); Subchapter II (Grants For Public Works And Economic Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Assistance made available under this subchapter shall not be used directly or indirectly for an attorney’s or consultant’s fee incurred in connection with obtaining grants and contracts under this subchapter.
