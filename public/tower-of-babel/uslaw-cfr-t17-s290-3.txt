17 C.F.R. § 290.3 — Reports with respect to proposed distribution of obligations.
Citation: us:cfr:17:290.3
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 290 (GENERAL RULES AND REGULATIONS PURSUANT TO SECTION 9(a) OF THE EUROPEAN BANK FOR RECONSTRUCTION AND DEVELOPMENT ACT). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The EBRD shall file with the Commission, on or prior to the date on which it sells any of its primary obligations in connection with a distribution of such obligations in the United States, a report containing the information and documents specified in Schedule A of this part. The term “sell” as used in this section and in Schedule A of this part means a completed sale, or a firm committment to sell to an underwriter.
