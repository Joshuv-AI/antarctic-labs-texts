25 U.S.C. § 5137 — Removal of duplicative appraisals
Citation: us:usc:25:5137
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section5137
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 45 (Protection Of Indians And Conservation Of Resources). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other law (including regulations), in making loans under section 5136 of this title, borrowers who are Indian tribes, members of Indian tribes, or tribal corporations shall only be required to obtain 1 appraisal under an appraisal standard recognized as of February 7, 2014, by the Secretary or the Secretary of the Interior.
