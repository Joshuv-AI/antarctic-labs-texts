30 U.S.C. § 553 — Duties of Secretary; surveys, research, etc.; proj­ects
Citation: us:usc:30:553
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section553
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 13 (Control Of Coal-Mine Fires). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior, in order to effectuate the policy declared in section 551 of this title, is hereby authorized—
(a)
to conduct surveys, investigations, and research relating to the causes and extent of outcrop and underground fires in coal formations and the methods for control or extinguishment of such fires; to publish the results of any such surveys, investigations, and researches; and to disseminate information concerning such method; and
(b)
to plan and execute projects for control or extinguishment of fires in coal formations.
