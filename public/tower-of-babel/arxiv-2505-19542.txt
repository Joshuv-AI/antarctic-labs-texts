Mazur’s growth number conjecture and congruences
Anwesh Ray

Abstract
. Motivated by the work of Greenberg–Vatsal [ GV00 ] and Emerton–Pollack–Weston [ EPW06 ] , I investigate the extent to which Mazur’s conjecture on the growth of Selmer ranks in ℤ p \mathbb{Z}_{p} -extensions of an imaginary quadratic field persists under p p -congruences between Galois representations. As a first step, I establish Mazur’s conjecture for certain triples ( E , K , p ) (E,K,p) under explicit hypotheses. Building on this, I prove analogous results for Greenberg Selmer groups attached to modular forms that are congruent mod p p to E E , including all specializations arising from Hida families of fixed tame level. In particular, I show that the Mordell–Weil ranks in non-anticyclotomic ℤ p \mathbb{Z}_{p} -extensions of K K remain bounded for elliptic curves E ′ E^{\prime} such that E ⁡ [ p ] E[p] and E ′ ​ [ p ] E^{\prime}[p] are isomorphic as Galois modules.

1.
Introduction

1.1.
Background and motivation

Let
L
be a number field and
E_{/L}
be an elliptic curve. The group of
L
-rational points on
E
, denoted
E(L)
, forms an abelian group under a natural geometric addition law arising from the chord-tangent construction on the curve. This group is referred to as the Mordell–Weil group of
E
over
L
. A foundational result in the arithmetic of elliptic curves, known as the Mordell–Weil theorem, asserts that
E(L)
is a finitely generated abelian group. Consequently, one may write
E(L)\simeq\mathbb{Z}^{r}\oplus T
, where
T\subseteq E(L)
is a finite abelian subgroup consisting of torsion points, and
r\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\operatorname{rank}E(L)
. This rank is a central arithmetic invariant of the curve and has connections to the behavior of
L
-functions. Understanding how this rank varies in families of number fields is a major theme in modern number theory.

In a seminal development, Mazur
[
Maz72
]
initiated the study of the Iwasawa theory of elliptic curves, which seeks to understand the growth properties of
p
-primary Selmer groups of abelian varieties as one ascends pro-
p
towers of number fields. These Selmer groups interpolate information about the Mordell–Weil group and
p
-primary Tate–Shafarevich group.

Let
\mathbb{Q}_{\mathrm{cyc}}
denote the unique
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-extension of
\mathbb{Q}
and
E_{/\mathbb{Q}}
be an elliptic curve. Kato
[
Kat04
]
and Rohrlich
[
Roh88
]
show that the group
E(\mathbb{Q}_{\mathrm{cyc}})
has finite rank. Let
K
now be an imaginary quadratic field, and let
\mathcal{K}
be a
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-extension of
K
. For each integer
n\geq 0
, denote by
\mathcal{K}_{n}\subset\mathcal{K}
the unique subextension with
[\mathcal{K}_{n}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}K]=p^{n}
, so that one obtains a tower of number fields:

K=\mathcal{K}_{0}\subset\mathcal{K}_{1}\subset\cdots\subset\mathcal{K}_{n}\subset\mathcal{K}_{n+1}\subset\cdots\subset\mathcal{K}=\bigcup_{n\geq 0}\mathcal{K}_{n}.

Among such extensions, a particularly important one is the anticyclotomic
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-extension of
K
, denoted
\mathcal{K}_{\mathrm{ac}}
, characterized by the action of complex conjugation on its Galois group being nontrivial. A distinction is made between two types of pairs
(E,K)
. The pair is said to be exceptional if the elliptic curve
E
has complex multiplication (CM) by an order in the imaginary quadratic field
K
; otherwise, it is termed generic. The sign of the pair
(E,K)
plays a crucial role in formulating growth predictions. In the generic case, this sign is defined as the root number (i.e., the sign of the functional equation) of the Hasse–Weil
L
-function
L(s,E_{/K})
. In the exceptional case, when
E
has CM by
K
, there exists a Hecke character
\varphi
such that
L(s,E/\mathbb{Q})=L(s,\varphi)
, and the sign of
(E,K)
is then defined to be the sign of the functional equation of
L(s,\varphi)
.

In this context, Mazur
[
Maz
, p. 201]
formulated a precise conjecture describing the asymptotic growth of the
p^{\infty}
-Selmer groups over the finite layers
\mathcal{K}_{n}
of a
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-extension
\mathcal{K}
of
K
.

Conjecture 1.1

(Mazur’s Growth Number Conjecture)
.

Let
E/\mathbb{Q}
be an elliptic curve,
K
an imaginary quadratic field, and
p
a prime at which
E
has good ordinary reduction. Suppose
\mathcal{K}/K
is a
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-extension in which all primes of bad reduction for
E
remain finitely decomposed. Then for all sufficiently large
n
, the
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-corank of the
p^{\infty}
-Selmer group satisfies

\operatorname{corank}_{\mathbb{Z}_{p}}\operatorname{Sel}_{p^{\infty}}(E/\mathcal{K}_{n})=cp^{n}+O(1),

where the constant
c
depends on the nature of the extension and the sign of
(E,K)
, and is given explicitly by:

c=\begin{cases}0&\text{if }\mathcal{K}\neq\mathcal{K}_{\mathrm{ac}}\text{ or }(E,K)\text{ has sign }+1,\\
1&\text{if }\mathcal{K}=\mathcal{K}_{\mathrm{ac}},\ (E,K)\text{ is generic, and has sign }-1,\\
2&\text{if }\mathcal{K}=\mathcal{K}_{\mathrm{ac}},\ (E,K)\text{ is exceptional, and has sign }-1.\end{cases}

1.2.
Prior results

I present below a chronological and non-exhaustive list of results related to Conjecture
1.1
.

(i)

The exceptional case of Conjecture (
1.1
) has been extensively studied and is now well understood. A summary of the main results can be found in
[
Gre01
, Section 1, Theorems 1.7 and 1.8]
.

(ii)

Recently, there has been progress on Conjecture
1.1
for elliptic curves
E
of rank at most one. Gajek–Leonard, Hatley, Kundu and Lei
[
GLHKL24
]
show that, under a certain semisimplicity hypothesis (
3.4
) on the structure of the Selmer group over the
\mathbb{Z}_{p}^{2}
-extension of
K
, the conjecture holds.

(iii)

The connection between Mazur’s growth number conjecture and Hilbert’s tenth problem was investigated by Katharina Müller and the author in
[
MR24
]
. There, we established the constancy of Mordell–Weil ranks for elliptic curves in many
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-extensions of imaginary quadratic fields. These results, obtained through techniques from arithmetic statistics, led to the construction of infinite families of number fields over which Hilbert’s tenth problem has a negative solution. Precise statements can be found in Theorems A, B, 4.13, and 4.14 of
loc. cit.
Subsequently, a solution to Hilbert’s tenth problem for number rings was announced by Koymans and Pagano
[
KP24
]
. An independent proof of the same result was later made public by Alpöge, Bhargava, Ho, and Schnidman in
[
ABHS25
]
.

(iv)

Using a formula for due to Disegni
[
Dis17
,
Dis20
]
, Kundu and Lei
[
KL25
]
showed that for elliptic curves
E
of rank
1
satisfying the generalized Heegner hypothesis (
(GHH)
), the Conjecture
1.1
does follow from one divisibility of the Iwasawa main conjecture of
E
over the
\mathbb{Z}_{p}^{2}
-extension of
K
and a hypothesis
\langle z_{\operatorname{Heeg}},z_{\operatorname{Heeg}}\rangle\neq 0
arising from Perrin–Riou’s anticyclotomic
\Lambda
-adic pairing. I refer to Theorem
3.3
for the precise statement of their result. Examples verifying
\langle z_{\operatorname{Heeg}},z_{\operatorname{Heeg}}\rangle\neq 0
have not been computed in
loc. cit.
, however in principle, can be computed via the approach outlined in
[
BcS15
]
.

1.3.
Main results

Let
E
and
E^{\prime}
be elliptic curves over
\mathbb{Q}
with good ordinary reduction at a prime
p
. One says that
E
and
E^{\prime}
are
p
-congruent
if their mod
p
Galois representations
E[p]
and
E^{\prime}[p]
are isomorphic as
\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}/\mathbb{Q})
-modules. In this case, Greenberg and Vatsal
[
GV00
]
demonstrated that the Iwasawa invariants associated to
E
are closely related to those of
E^{\prime}
. Analogous phenomena have been investigated by Emerton, Pollack, and Weston
[
EPW06
]
in the context of
p
-ordinary Hida families of modular forms. The aim of this paper is to investigate the propagation of Mazur’s growth number conjecture under congruences between Galois representations. Let
E/\mathbb{Q}
be an elliptic curve and let
f
be a Hecke eigenform. Suppose
p
is a prime such that the residual Galois representation associated to
f
is isomorphic to the Galois representation on
E[p]
. Assuming that Conjecture
1.1
holds for the triple
(E,K,p)
, I ask: what conclusions, if any, can be drawn about the corresponding triple
(f,K,p)
, particularly concerning the growth of
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-coranks of Selmer groups in various
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-extensions of
K
?

In the special case where
f
has weight
2
, trivial nebentypus, and rational Fourier coefficients, it corresponds to the isogeny class of an elliptic curve
E^{\prime}/\mathbb{Q}
. I consider the situation in which the residual representation is absolutely irreducible and the associated elliptic curve
E^{\prime}
is fixed. In this setting, an isomorphism
E[p]\simeq E^{\prime}[p]
of
\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}/\mathbb{Q})
-modules has significant consequences for Conjecture
1.1
as it pertains to the triple
(E^{\prime},K,p)
. In order for my congruence results to apply, it becomes necessary that the
\mu
-invariant of the Selmer groups vanish in
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-extensions of number fields. The
p
-adic regulator of
E_{/K}
is denoted by
\operatorname{Reg}_{p}(E/K)
. It is conjectured that the
p
-adic regulator is non-zero (see
[
Sch85
]
). Let
\mathcal{R}_{p}(E/K)
represent the normalized regulator, defined as
\mathcal{R}_{p}(E/K)\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\operatorname{Reg}_{p}(E/K)/p
. Given a prime
v
of
K
, denote by
c_{v}(E/K)
the Tamagawa number of
E
over
K_{v}
. Set
\kappa_{v}
to denote the residue field at
v
. Suppose
E
has good reduction at
v
, set
\widetilde{E}(\kappa_{v})
to be the group of
\kappa_{v}
-rational points of the reduction of a regular model of
E
at the prime
v
.

Theorem A

(Proposition
3.7
)
.

Let
E
be an elliptic curve over
\mathbb{Q}
with good ordinary reduction at a prime
p\geq 5
and
K
be an imaginary quadratic number field. Let

be the conductor of

. Assume that the following conditions are satisfied for

(1)

,

(2)

all rational primes dividing

are split in

(3)

\operatorname{corank}_{\mathbb{Z}_{p}}\operatorname{Sel}_{p^{\infty}}(E/K)=1
,

(4)

p\nmid\mathcal{R}_{p}(E/K)

(5)

\Sh(E/K)[p^{\infty}]=0

p\nmid c_{v}(E/K)

of

,

(7)

and
\widetilde{E}(\kappa_{v})[p^{\infty}]=0
for all primes

of

.

(8)

the
2
-variable Iwasawa main conjecture holds (see (
IMC
)),

Let
\mathcal{K}
be a
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-extension of
K
in which the primes of

that lie above

are ramified. Then, the following assertions hold.

(a)

Assume that
\mathcal{K}
is not the anticyclotomic extension

-invariant equal to

)

. Let

2.
Iwasawa theory of Selmer groups and congruences

2.1.
Selmer groups over
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-extensions

This section introduces preliminary notions and fixes standard notation, chosen to be consistent with that of

,

KL25
]

\bar{\mathbb{Q}}

(resp.
\bar{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}

\iota_{\ell}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}\bar{\mathbb{Q}}\hookrightarrow\bar{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}

\iota_{\ell}^{*}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}\operatorname{G}_{\ell}\hookrightarrow\operatorname{G}_{\mathbb{Q}}

\operatorname{G}_{\ell}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\operatorname{Gal}(\bar{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}/\mathbb{Q}_{\ell})

\operatorname{G}_{\mathbb{Q}}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\operatorname{Gal}(\bar{\mathbb{Q}}/\mathbb{Q})

and

be a prime at which
E
has good ordinary reduction. Let
E[p^{n}]
(resp.
E[p^{\infty}]
) be the

torsion (
p
-primary torsion) subgroup of

, viewed as a module over
\operatorname{G}_{\mathbb{Q}}
. Set
T\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\varprojlim_{n}E[p^{n}]
to denote the

-adic Tate module of

. Let

be an imaginary quadratic field in which
p
splits. Write

(resp.

Some of the results stated below will require the stronger assumption that

Let
\Sigma_{0}=\Sigma_{0}(E)
be the set of prime numbers

H^{i}(\mathbb{Q}_{\Sigma}/F,\cdot)\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=H^{i}(\operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb{Q}_{\Sigma}/F),\cdot)\quad\text{and}\quad H^{i}(\mathbb{Q}_{\ell},\cdot)\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=H^{i}(G_{\ell},\cdot).

, denote by

the set of primes

. If

.

Define the

2.2.
Iwasawa invariants of

\mathcal{L}_{a,b}(T_{a,b})=\alpha_{a,b}T+\dots+\text{higher order terms},

where
\alpha_{a,b}\sim\langle z_{\infty},z_{\infty}\rangle_{0}
. Thus, I find that
\alpha_{a,b}\in\mathbb{Z}_{p}^{\times}
and this implies that
I_{a,b}=(T)
. I thus deduce that
\mu_{a,b}=0
and
\lambda_{a,b}=1
and this completes the proof of (1).

Since
\langle z_{\infty},z_{\infty}\rangle_{\infty}\neq 0
, it follows from Theorem
3.3
that Conjecture
1.1
holds for
(E,K,p)
.
∎

Theorem 3.8
.

Let
E
be an elliptic curve over
\mathbb{Q}
with good ordinary reduction at a prime
p\geq 5
and
K
be an imaginary quadratic number field. Assume that
E_{/K}
satisfies the conditions (
(HH)
), (
IMC
) and (
(tEC)
). Let
f
be a
p
-ordinary Hecke eigencuspform with
p\nmid N_{f}
. Assume that
f
satisfies (
(HH)
) and
A_{f}[\varpi]\simeq E[p]\otimes_{\mathbb{F}_{p}}\kappa
as modules over
\operatorname{G}_{\mathbb{Q}}
. Then, for each
(a,b)\neq(0,1)
such that
\mathfrak{p}
and
\bar{\mathfrak{p}}
are totally ramified in
K_{a,b}
, the
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-corank of
\operatorname{Sel}_{\operatorname{Gr}}(A_{f}/K_{a,b})
is finite. In particular, if
A_{f}=E^{\prime}[p^{\infty}]
for some elliptic curve
E^{\prime}
over
\mathbb{Q}
, the rank of
E^{\prime}(K_{a,b})
is finite.

Proof.

For any
(a,b)\in\mathbb{P}^{1}(\mathbb{Z}_{p})
such that
(a,b)\neq(0,1)
, Proposition
3.7
implies that
\operatorname{Sel}_{p^{\infty}}(E/K_{a,b})
is cotorsion over
\Lambda_{a,b}
with
\mu_{a,b}=0
and
\lambda_{a,b}=1
. It then follows from Proposition
2.7
that
\operatorname{Sel}_{p^{\infty}}(A_{f}/K_{a,b})
is cotorsion over
\Lambda_{a,b}
, and consequently the
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-corank of
\operatorname{Sel}_{\operatorname{Gr}}(A_{f}/K_{a,b})
is finite.
∎

.

-modules, one has an isomorphism of the residual representations

). Note that
5

\mathcal{V}

3.2
, the (

3.3.
An application involving Hida families

Let
(E,K,p)
be a triple satisfying the hypotheses of Theorem
3.8
. Let
N
denote the conductor of
E
, and let
\bar{\rho}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}/\mathbb{Q})\to\operatorname{GL}_{2}(\mathbb{F}_{p})
be the residual Galois representation arising from the action on
E[p]
. Assume that
\bar{\rho}
is absolutely irreducible, and let
f_{E}
denote the weight two cuspidal eigenform of trivial nebentypus associated with
E
.

Let
\mathbf{T}_{N}
be the universal reduced ordinary Hecke algebra of tame level
N
,
p
-power level, and arbitrary weight. Let
\Gamma^{\prime}\subset\mathbb{Z}_{p}^{\times}
denote the group of principal units, and define
\Lambda^{\prime}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\mathbb{Z}_{p}\llbracket\Gamma^{\prime}\rrbracket
, the corresponding Iwasawa algebra. After fixing a topological generator of
\Gamma^{\prime}
, I identify
\Lambda^{\prime}
with the formal power series ring
\mathbb{Z}_{p}\llbracket X\rrbracket
. Let
\mathbf{T}_{N}^{\mathrm{new}}
denote the new quotient of
\mathbf{T}_{N}
, and let
\mathfrak{m}\subset\mathbf{T}_{N}
be the maximal ideal corresponding to
\bar{\rho}
. Set
\mathcal{T}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\mathbf{T}_{N,\mathfrak{m}}
to be the localization of
\mathbf{T}_{N}
at
\mathfrak{m}
. A minimal prime ideal
\mathfrak{a}\subset\mathcal{T}
is called a
branch
, and I write
\mathcal{T}(\mathfrak{a})\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\mathcal{T}/\mathfrak{a}
. The scheme
\operatorname{Spec}\mathcal{T}(\mathfrak{a})
is referred to as a
Hida family
. I refer to
\operatorname{Spec}\Lambda^{\prime}
as the
weight space
and to
\pi_{\mathfrak{a}}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}\operatorname{Spec}\mathcal{T}(\mathfrak{a})\to\operatorname{Spec}\Lambda^{\prime}
as the
map to weight space
. This map is finite and surjective. The Hida family
\operatorname{Spec}\mathcal{T}(\mathfrak{a})
is equipped with a Galois representation

\rho_{\mathfrak{a}}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}\operatorname{G}_{\mathbb{Q}}\to\operatorname{GL}_{2}(\mathcal{T}(\mathfrak{a}))

lifting
\bar{\rho}
.

Now let
\operatorname{Spec}\mathcal{T}(\mathfrak{a})
be one of the branches which contains the point corresponding to
f_{E}
. Then for each integer
k>0
with
k\equiv 2\pmod{p-1}
, there exists a classical modular form
f_{k}
of weight
k
lying on
\operatorname{Spec}\mathcal{T}(\mathfrak{a})
, congruent modulo
p
to
f_{E}
. The conclusion of Theorem
3.8
then implies that for each pair
(a,b)\neq(0,1)
such that the primes
\mathfrak{p}
and
\bar{\mathfrak{p}}
are totally ramified in the extension
K_{a,b}
, the
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-corank of the Greenberg Selmer group
\operatorname{Sel}_{\operatorname{Gr}}(A_{f_{k}}/K_{a,b})
is finite.
