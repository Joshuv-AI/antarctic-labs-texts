42 C.F.R. § 137.375 — Are Tribally-owned facilities constructed under section 509 of the Act [25 U.S.C. 458aaa-8] eligible for replacement, maintenance, and improvement funds on the same basis as if title to such property were vested in the United States?
Citation: us:cfr:42:137.375
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-137.375
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 137 (Tribal Self-Governance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, Tribally-owned facilities constructed under section 509 of the Act [25 U.S.C. 458aaa-8] are eligible for replacement, maintenance, and improvement funds on the same basis as if title to such property were vested in the United States.
