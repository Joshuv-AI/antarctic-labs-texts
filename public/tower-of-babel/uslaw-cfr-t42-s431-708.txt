42 C.F.R. § 431.708 — Procedures for applying standards.
Citation: us:cfr:42:431.708
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-431.708
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 431 (State Organization And General Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agency or board must develop and apply appropriate procedures and techniques, including examinations and investigations, for determining if a person meets the licensing standards.
