On the subsystems of certain almost Markov shifts
Wolfgang Krieger

Abstract
. For an aperiodic subshift of finite type Y Y and for a subshift X X with topological entropy less than the topological entropy of Y Y , it was proved in Krieger: On the subsystems of topological Markov chains, Ergodic Theory & dynamical systems 1982 𝟚 \mathbb{2} , 195-202, that the necessary condition on the periodic points of X X and Y Y for the existence of an embedding of X X into Y Y is also sufficient for the existence of an embedding of X X into Y Y . In this note we extend this theorem to a certain class of almost Markov shifts as target shifts.
