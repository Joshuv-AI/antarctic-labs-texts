49 U.S.C. § 10910 — Effect on other statutes and authorities
Citation: us:usc:49:10910
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section10910
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Rail); Chapter 109 (Licensing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in section 10908 or 10909 is intended to affect the traditional police powers of the State to require a rail carrier to comply with State and local environmental, public health, and public safety standards that are not unreasonably burdensome to interstate commerce and do not discriminate against rail carriers.
