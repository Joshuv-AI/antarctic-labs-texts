42 C.F.R. § 35.2 — Compliance with hospital rules.
Citation: us:cfr:42:35.2
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-35.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 35 (Hospital And Station Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All patients and visitors in stations and hospitals of the Service are expected to comply with the rules and instructions issued under the authority of the officer in charge.
