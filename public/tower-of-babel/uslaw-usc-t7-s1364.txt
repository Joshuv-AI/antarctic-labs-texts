7 U.S.C. § 1364 — Compensation of review committee
Citation: us:usc:7:1364
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1364
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 35 (Agricultural Adjustment Act Of 1938); Subchapter II (Loans, Parity Payments, Consumer Safeguards, Marketing Quotas, And Marketing Certificates); Part C (Administrative Provisions); Subpart i (Publication And Review Of Quotas). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The members of the review committee shall receive as compensation for their services the same per diem as that received by the members of the committee utilized for the purposes of chapter 3B of title 16. The members of the review committee shall not be entitled to receive compensation for more than thirty days in any one year.
