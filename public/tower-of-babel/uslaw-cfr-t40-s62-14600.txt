40 C.F.R. § 62.14600 — When must the operator training course be completed?
Citation: us:cfr:40:62.14600
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-62.14600
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 62 (Approval And Promulgation Of State Plans For Designated Facilities And Pollutants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The operator training course must be completed by the later of the two dates specified in paragraphs (a)(1) and (2) of this section.
(1) October 4, 2004.
(2) Six months after an employee assumes responsibility for operating the CISWI unit or assumes responsibility for supervising the operation of the CISWI unit.
(b) You must follow the requirements in § 63.14625 if all qualified operators are temporarily not accessible.
