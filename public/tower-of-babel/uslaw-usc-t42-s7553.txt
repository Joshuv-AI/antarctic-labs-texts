42 U.S.C. § 7553 — Prohibition on production of engines requiring leaded gasoline
Citation: us:usc:42:7553
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section7553
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 85 (Air Pollution Prevention And Control); Subchapter II (Emission Standards For Moving Sources); Part A (Motor Vehicle Emission And Fuel Standards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator shall promulgate regulations applicable to motor vehicle engines and nonroad engines manufactured after model year 1992 that prohibit the manufacture, sale, or introduction into commerce of any engine that requires leaded gasoline.
