18 U.S.C. § 1656 — Conversion or surrender of vessel
Citation: us:usc:18:1656
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1656
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 81 (Piracy And Privateering). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever, being a captain or other officer or mariner of a vessel upon the high seas or on any other waters within the admiralty and maritime jurisdiction of the United States, piratically or feloniously runs away with such vessel, or with any goods or merchandise thereof, to the value of $50 or over; or
Whoever yields up such vessel voluntarily to any pirate—
Shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than ten years, or both.
