13 U.S.C. § 184 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:13:184
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title13-section184
Context: United States Code, Title 13 (Census); Chapter 5 (Censuses); Subchapter IV (Interim Current Data). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of this subchapter—
(1)
the term “local unit of general purpose government” means the government of a county, municipality, township, Indian tribe, Alaskan native village, or other unit of government (other than a State) which is a unit of general government, and
(2)
the term “State” includes the District of Columbia.
