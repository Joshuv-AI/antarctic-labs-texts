20 U.S.C. § 9542 — Commissioner for Education Statistics
Citation: us:usc:20:9542
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section9542
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 76 (Education Research, Statistics, Evaluation, Information, And Dissemination); Subchapter I (Education Sciences Reform); Part C (National Center For Education Statistics). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Statistics Center shall be headed by a Commissioner for Education Statistics (in this part referred to as the “Statistics Commissioner”) who shall be highly qualified and have substantial knowledge of statistical methodologies and activities undertaken by the Statistics Center.
