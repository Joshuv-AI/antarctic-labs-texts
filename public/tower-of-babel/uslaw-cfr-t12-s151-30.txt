12 C.F.R. § 151.30 — What requirements apply to all transactions?
Citation: us:cfr:12:151.30
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-151.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 151 (Recordkeeping And Confirmation Requirements For Securities Transactions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must effect all transactions, including transactions excepted under § 151.20, in a safe and sound manner. You must maintain effective systems of records and controls regarding your customers' securities transactions. These systems must clearly and accurately reflect all appropriate information and provide an adequate basis for an audit.
