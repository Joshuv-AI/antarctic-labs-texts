30 C.F.R. § 795.5 — Grant application procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:30:795.5
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-795.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 795 (Permanent Regulatory Program—Small Operator Assistance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A State intending to administer a Small Operator Assistance Program under a grant from the Office of Surface Mining may submit a grant application to OSM for funding of the program under the procedures of part 735 of this chapter.
