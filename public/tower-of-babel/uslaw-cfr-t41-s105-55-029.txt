41 C.F.R. § 105-55.029 — -55.029 Exception to termination.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-55.029
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-55.029
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-55 (55—Collection Of Claims Owed The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a significant enforcement policy is involved, or recovery of a judgment is a prerequisite to the imposition of administrative sanctions, the General Services Administration may refer debts for litigation even though termination of collection activity may otherwise be appropriate.
