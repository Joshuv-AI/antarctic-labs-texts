31 C.F.R. § 245.10 — Performance of functions of the Commissioner.
Citation: us:cfr:31:245.10
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-245.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 245 (Claims On Account Of Treasury Checks). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commissioner of the Bureau of the Fiscal Services may authorize any officer of the Treasury Department to perform any of his functions under this part and to redelegate such authority within such limits as the Commissioner may prescribe.
