43 C.F.R. § 3473.4 — Suspension of operations, production, and payment obligations.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3473.4
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3473.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3470 (Coal Management Provisions And Limitations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Application by a lessee for relief from any operating and producing requirements of a lease; shall be filed in triplicate in the office of the Mining Supervisor in accordance with 43 CFR part 3480.
(b) The term of any lease shall be extended by adding thereto any period of suspension of all operations and production during such term in accordance with any direction or assent of the Mining Supervisor.
