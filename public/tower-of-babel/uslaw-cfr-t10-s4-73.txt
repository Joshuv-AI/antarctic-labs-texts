10 C.F.R. § 4.73 — Rulings required.
Citation: us:cfr:10:4.73
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-4.73
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 4 (Nondiscrimination In Federally Assisted Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance From The Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each decision of a presiding officer or the Commission shall set forth the rulings on each finding, conclusion, or exception presented, and shall identify the requirement or requirements imposed by or pursuant to this subpart with which it is found that the applicant or recipient has failed to comply.
