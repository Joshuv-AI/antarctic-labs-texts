11 C.F.R. § 111.1 — Scope (52 U.S.C. 30109).
Citation: us:cfr:11:111.1
Creator: Federal Election Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-111.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 111 (COMPLIANCE PROCEDURE (52 U.S.C. 30109, 30107(a))). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These regulations provide procedures for processing possible violations of the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971, as amended (52 U.S.C. 30101, et seq.) and chapters 95 and 96 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1954 (26 U.S.C. 9001, et seq. and 9031 et seq.).
