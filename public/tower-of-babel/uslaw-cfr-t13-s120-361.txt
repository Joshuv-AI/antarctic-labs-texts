13 C.F.R. § 120.361 — Other conditions of eligibility.
Citation: us:cfr:13:120.361
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-120.361
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 120 (Business Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Management and daily operations of the business must be directed by one or more of the veteran owners whose veteran status was used to qualify for the loan.
(b) This direct loan program is available only if private sector financing and guaranteed loans are not available.
(c) A veteran may qualify only once for this program on a direct loan basis.
