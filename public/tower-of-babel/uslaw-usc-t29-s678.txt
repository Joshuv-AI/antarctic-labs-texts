29 U.S.C. § 678 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:29:678
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section678
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 15 (Occupational Safety And Health). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out this chapter for each fiscal year such sums as the Congress shall deem necessary.
