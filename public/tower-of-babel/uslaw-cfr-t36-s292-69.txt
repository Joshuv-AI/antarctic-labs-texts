36 C.F.R. § 292.69 — Concurrent reclamation.
Citation: us:cfr:36:292.69
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-292.69
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 292 (National Recreation Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Plans of operations involving locatable minerals, operating plans involving outstanding mineral rights, and contracts or permits for mineral materials should all provide, to the maximum extent practicable, that reclamation proceed concurrently with the mineral operation.
