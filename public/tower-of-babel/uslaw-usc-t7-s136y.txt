7 U.S.C. § 136y — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:7:136y
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section136y
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 6 (Insecticides And Environmental Pesticide Control); Subchapter II (Environmental Pesticide Control). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out this subchapter (other than section 136u(a) of this title)—
(1)
$83,000,000 for fiscal year 1989, of which not more than $13,735,500 shall be available for research under this subchapter;
(2)
$95,000,000 for fiscal year 1990, of which not more than $14,343,600 shall be available for research under this subchapter; and
(3)
$95,000,000 for fiscal year 1991, of which not more than $14,978,200 shall be available for research under this subchapter.
