9 C.F.R. § 93.409 — Articles accompanying ruminants.
Citation: us:cfr:9:93.409
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-93.409
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 93 (Importation Of Certain Animals, Birds, Fish, And Poultry, And Certain Animal, Bird, And Poultry Products; Requirements For Means Of Conveyance And Shipping Containers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No litter or manure, fodder or other aliment, nor any equipment such as boxes, buckets, ropes, chains, blankets, or other things used for or about ruminants governed by the regulations in this part, shall be landed from any conveyance except under such restrictions as the inspector in charge at the port of entry shall direct.
