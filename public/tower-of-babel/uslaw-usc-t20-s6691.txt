20 U.S.C. § 6691 — Supplement, not supplant
Citation: us:usc:20:6691
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section6691
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 70 (Strengthening And Improvement Of Elementary And Secondary Schools); Subchapter II (Preparing, Training, And Recruiting High-Quality Teachers, Principals, Or Other School Leaders); Part C (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Funds made available under this subchapter shall be used to supplement, and not supplant, non-Federal funds that would otherwise be used for activities authorized under this subchapter.
