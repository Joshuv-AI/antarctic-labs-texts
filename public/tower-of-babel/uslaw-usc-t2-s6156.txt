2 U.S.C. § 6156 — Appointment and compensation of employees by Secretary of Conference of Majority of Senate and Secretary of Conference of Minority of Senate
Citation: us:usc:2:6156
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section6156
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 61 (Senate Leadership); Subchapter IV (Majority And Minority Secretaries, Conferences, And Policy Committees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Effective October 1, 1979, the Secretary of the Conference of the Majority and the Secretary of the Conference of the Minority are each authorized to appoint and fix the compensation of such employees as they deem appropriate: Provided, That the gross compensation paid to such employees shall not exceed $70,000 each fiscal year for each Secretary.
