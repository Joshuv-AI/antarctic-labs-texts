10 U.S.C. § 1523 — Posthumous commissions and warrants: effect on pay and allowances
Citation: us:usc:10:1523
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1523
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 77 (Posthumous Commissions And Warrants). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No person is entitled to any bonus, gratuity, pay, or allowance because of a posthumous commission or warrant.
