5 C.F.R. § 875.411 — May I continue my coverage when I am no longer a qualified relative?
Citation: us:cfr:5:875.411
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-875.411
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 875 (Federal Long Term Care Insurance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you are already enrolled as a qualified relative, you may continue your FLTCIP coverage if you subsequently lose qualified relative status (such as upon divorce), as long as the Carrier receives the required premium when due.
