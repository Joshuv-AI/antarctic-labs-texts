10 U.S.C. § 9015 — Under Secretary of the Air Force
Citation: us:usc:10:9015
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9015
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization); Chapter 903 (Department Of The Air Force). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
There is an Under Secretary of the Air Force, appointed from civilian life by the President, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate.
(b)
The Under Secretary shall perform such duties and exercise such powers as the Secretary of the Air Force may prescribe.
