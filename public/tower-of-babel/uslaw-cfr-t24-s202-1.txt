24 C.F.R. § 202.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:24:202.1
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-202.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 202 (Approval Of Lending Institutions And Mortgagees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part establishes minimum standards and requirements for approval by the Secretary of lenders and mortgagees to participate in the Title I and Title II programs.
