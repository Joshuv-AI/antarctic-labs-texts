35 U.S.C. § 7 — Library
Citation: us:usc:35:7
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title35-section7
Context: United States Code, Title 35 (Patents); Part I (United States Patent And Trademark Office); Chapter 1 (Establishment, Officers And Employees, Functions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director shall maintain a library of scientific and other works and periodicals, both foreign and domestic, in the Patent and Trademark Office to aid the officers in the discharge of their duties.
