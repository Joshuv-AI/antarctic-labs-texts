7 C.F.R. § 3560.615 — Participation with other funding sources.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3560.615
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3560.615
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3560 (Direct Multi-Family Housing Loans And Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Agency encourages the use of other funding sources in conjunction with on-farm labor housing loans. Use of such financing in conjunction with an on-farm labor housing loan is subject to the approval of the Agency and must comply with the requirements of § 3560.66.
