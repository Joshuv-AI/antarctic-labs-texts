5 C.F.R. § 1653.22 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1653.22
Creator: Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1653.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1653 (Court Orders And Legal Processes Affecting Thrift Savings Plan Accounts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Under 5 U.S.C. 8437(e)(3) and 8467(a)(2), the TSP record keeper will honor a court order or other similar process in the nature of a garnishment that is brought to enforce a judgment against a participant for physically, sexually, or emotionally abusing a child.
