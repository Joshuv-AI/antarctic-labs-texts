21 C.F.R. § 1401.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1401.1
Creator: Office Of National Drug Control Policy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1401.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1401 (Public Availability Of Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to prescribe rules, guidelines and procedures to implement the Freedom of Information Act (FOIA), as amended, 5 U.S.C. 552.
