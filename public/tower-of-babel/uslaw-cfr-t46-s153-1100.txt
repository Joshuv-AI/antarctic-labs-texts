46 C.F.R. § 153.1100 — Responsibility of the person in charge.
Citation: us:cfr:46:153.1100
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-153.1100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 153 (Ships Carrying Bulk Liquid, Liquefied Gas, Or Compressed Gas Hazardous Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The person in charge of the ship shall ensure that—
(a) The requirements of §§ 153.1102 through 153.1132 are met; and
(b) The procedures in the approved Procedures and Arrangements Manual are followed.
