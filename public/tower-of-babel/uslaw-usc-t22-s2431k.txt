22 U.S.C. § 2431k — Annual reports to Congress
Citation: us:usc:22:2431k
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2431k
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 32 (Foreign Assistance); Subchapter IV (Debt Reduction For Developing Countries With Tropical Forests). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than April 15 of each year, the President shall prepare and transmit to the Congress an annual report concerning the operation of the Facility for the prior calendar year. Such report shall include—
(1)
a description of the activities undertaken by the Facility during the previous calendar year;
(2)
a description of any Agreement entered into under this subchapter;
(3)
a report on any Funds that have been established under this subchapter and on the operations of such Funds; and
(4)
a description of any grants that have been provided by administering bodies pursuant to Agreements under this subchapter.
