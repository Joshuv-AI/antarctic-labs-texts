48 C.F.R. § 15.205 — 15.205 Issuing solicitations.
Citation: us:cfr:48:15.205
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-15.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 15 (Contracting By Negotiation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The contracting officer shall issue solicitations to potential sources in accordance with the policies and procedures in 5.102, 19.202-4, and part 6.
(b) A master solicitation, as described in 14.203-3, may also be used for negotiated acquisitions.
