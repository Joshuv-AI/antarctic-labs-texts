33 C.F.R. § 334.60 — Cape Cod Bay south of Wellfleet Harbor, Mass.; naval aircraft bombing target area.
Citation: us:cfr:33:334.60
Creator: Corps Of Engineers, Department Of The Army, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-334.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 334 (Danger Zone And Restricted Area Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The danger zone. A circular area with a radius of 1,000 yards having its center on the aircraft bombing target hulk James Longstreet in Cape Cod Bay at latitude 41°49′46″, longitude 70°02′54″.
(b) The regulations. (1) No person or vessel shall enter or remain in the danger zone at any time, except as authorized by the enforcing agency.
(2) This section shall be enforced by the Commandant, First Naval District, and such agencies as he may designate.
