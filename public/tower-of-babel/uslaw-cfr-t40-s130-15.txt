40 C.F.R. § 130.15 — Processing application for Indian tribes.
Citation: us:cfr:40:130.15
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-130.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 130 (Water Quality Planning And Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Regional Administrator shall process an application of an Indian Tribe submitted under § 130.6(d) in a timely manner. He shall promptly notify the Indian Tribe of receipt of the application.
