10 C.F.R. § 34.63 — Records of receipt and transfer of sealed sources.
Citation: us:cfr:10:34.63
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-34.63
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 34 (Licenses For Industrial Radiography And Radiation Safety Requirements For Industrial Radiographic Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each licensee shall maintain records showing the receipts and transfers of sealed sources and devices using DU for shielding and retain each record for 3 years after it is made.
(b) These records must include the date, the name of the individual making the record, radionuclide, number of becquerels (curies) or mass (for DU), and manufacturer, model, and serial number of each sealed source and/or device, as appropriate.
