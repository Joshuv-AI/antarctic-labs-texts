30 C.F.R. § 77.601 — Trailing cables or portable cables; temporary splices.
Citation: us:cfr:30:77.601
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-77.601
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 77 (Mandatory Safety Standards, Surface Coal Mines And Surface Work Areas Of Underground Coal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Temporary splices in trailing cables or portable cables shall be made in a workmanlike manner and shall be mechanically strong and well insulated. Trailing cables or portable cables with exposed wires or splices that heat or spark under load shall not be used.
