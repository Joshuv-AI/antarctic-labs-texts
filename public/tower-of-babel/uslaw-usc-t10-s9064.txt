10 U.S.C. § 9064 — Air Force nurses: Chief; appointment

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization); Chapter 907 (The Air Force). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Position of Chief.—
There is a Chief of the Air Force Nurse Corps.
(b) Chief.—
The Secretary of the Air Force shall appoint the Chief from the officers of the Regular Air Force designated as Air Force nurses whose regular grade is above lieutenant colonel and who are recommended by the Surgeon General. The Chief serves during the pleasure of the Secretary.
