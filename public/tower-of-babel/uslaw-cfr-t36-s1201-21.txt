36 C.F.R. § 1201.21 — May NARA's failure to comply with these regulations be used as a defense to a debt?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1201.21
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1201.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1201 (Collection Of Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No, the failure of NARA to comply with any standard in the FCCS or these regulations will not be available to any debtor as a defense.
