21 C.F.R. § 110.93 — Warehousing and distribution.
Citation: us:cfr:21:110.93
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-110.93
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 110 (Current Good Manufacturing Practice In Manufacturing, Packing, Or Holding Human Food). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Storage and transportation of finished food shall be under conditions that will protect food against physical, chemical, and microbial contamination as well as against deterioration of the food and the container.
