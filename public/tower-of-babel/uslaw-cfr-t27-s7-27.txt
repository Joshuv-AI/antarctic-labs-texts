27 C.F.R. § 7.27 — Presenting certificates of label approval (COLAs) to Government officials.
Citation: us:cfr:27:7.27
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-7.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 7 (Labeling And Advertising Of Malt Beverages). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A certificate holder must present the original or a paper or electronic copy of the appropriate certificate of label approval (COLA) upon the request of any duly authorized representative of the United States Government.
