43 C.F.R. § 3270.11 — What general standards apply to my utilization operations?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3270.11
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3270.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3200 (Geothermal Resource Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Your utilization operations must:
(a) Meet all operational and environmental standards;
(b) Prevent unnecessary impacts on surface and subsurface resources;
(c) Result in the maximum ultimate recovery of geothermal resources;
(d) Result in the beneficial use of geothermal resources, with minimum waste;
(e) Protect public health, safety, and property; and
(f) Comply with the requirements of § 3200.4.
