50 U.S.C. § 3367 — Requirement for efficient use by intelligence community of open-source intelligence
Citation: us:usc:50:3367
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section3367
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 45 (Miscellaneous Intelligence Community Authorities); Subchapter IV (Collection, Analysis, And Sharing Of Intelligence). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of National Intelligence shall ensure that the intelligence community makes efficient and effective use of open-source information and analysis.
