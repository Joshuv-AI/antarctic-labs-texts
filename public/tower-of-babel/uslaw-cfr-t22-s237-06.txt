22 C.F.R. § 237.06 — Transferability of Guarantee; Note Register.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 237 (Ukraine Loan Guarantees Issued Under The Department Of State, Foreign Operations, And Related Programs Appropriations Act Of 2015, And The Support For The Sovereignty, Integrity, Democracy, And Economic Stability Of Ukraine Act Of 2014—Standard Terms And Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Noteholder may assign, transfer or pledge an Eligible Note to any Person. Any such assignment, transfer or pledge shall be effective on the date that the name of the new Noteholder is entered on the Note Register. USAID shall be entitled to treat the Persons in whose names the Eligible Notes are registered as the owners thereof for all purposes of this Guarantee and USAID shall not be affected by notice to the contrary.
