48 U.S.C. § 1408e — Additional powers
Citation: us:usc:48:1408e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1408e
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 7 (Virgin Islands); Subchapter IV (Public Housing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Powers granted in this Act shall be in addition to and not in derogation of any powers granted by other law to or for the benefit or assistance of any public corporate authority or municipality.
