23 C.F.R. § 778.113 — Program termination and regulations sunset.
Citation: us:cfr:23:778.113
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-778.113
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 778 (Pilot Program For Eliminating Duplication Of Environmental Reviews). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In general. The Pilot Program shall terminate December 4, 2027, unless Congress extends the authority under 23 U.S.C. 330.
(b) Sunset. Unless Congress extends the authority for the Pilot Program that sunsets 12 years after the date of enactment, this rule shall expire on December 4, 2027.
