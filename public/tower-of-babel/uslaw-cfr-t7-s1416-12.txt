7 C.F.R. § 1416.12 — Minors.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1416.12
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1416.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1416 (Emergency Agricultural Disaster Assistance Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A minor child is eligible to apply for program benefits under ELAP, LFP, LIP, or TAP if all the eligibility requirements are met and the provision for minor children in part 1400 of this chapter are met.
