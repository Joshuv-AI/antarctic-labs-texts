33 C.F.R. § 118.20 — Obtaining information.
Citation: us:cfr:33:118.20
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-118.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 118 (Bridge Lighting And Other Signals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Persons desiring information concerning the marking of bridges shall address their inquiry to the District Commander having jurisdiction over the area concerned, or to the Commandant.
