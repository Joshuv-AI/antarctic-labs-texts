22 U.S.C. § 3408 — Non-proliferation of nuclear weapons
Citation: us:usc:22:3408
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section3408
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 49 (Support Of Peace Treaty Between Egypt And Israel); Subchapter I (Political, Economic, And Technological Support). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In accordance with the Nuclear Non-Proliferation Act of 1978 [22 U.S.C. 3201 et seq.], the Congress strongly encourages all countries in the Middle East which are not parties to the Treaty on the Non-Proliferation of Nuclear Weapons to become parties to that Treaty.
