6 U.S.C. § 811 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:6:811
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section811
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 2 (National Emergency Management); Subchapter II (Comprehensive Preparedness System); Part E (Authorization Of Appropriations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out this title 1 and the amendments made by this title for the administration and operations of the Agency—
(1)
for fiscal year 2008, an amount equal to the amount appropriated for fiscal year 2007 for administration and operations of the Agency, multiplied by 1.1;
(2)
for fiscal year 2009, an amount equal to the amount described in paragraph (1), multiplied by 1.1; and
(3)
for fiscal year 2010, an amount equal to the amount described in paragraph (2), multiplied by 1.1.
