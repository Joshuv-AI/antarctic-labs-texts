Intertwined Electron Pairing in the Bilayer Two-orbital Kanamori-Hubbard Model: a Unified Picture of Two Superconductivities in La 3 ​ Ni 2 ​ O 7 \mathrm{La_{3}Ni_{2}O_{7}} Preprint: APS/123-QED
Shicong Mo, Yaoyuan Zheng, Wéi Wú

Abstract
The mechanism of pairing in La 3 ​ Ni 2 ​ O 7 \mathrm{La_{3}Ni_{2}O_{7}} bulk and film superconductors remains actively debated. Here, we investigate the bilayer two-orbital Kanamori-Hubbard model for La 3 ​ Ni 2 ​ O 7 \mathrm{La_{3}Ni_{2}O_{7}} using cellular dynamical mean-field theory. We find two intertwined s ± s_{\pm} -wave superconductivities with distinct physical origins under enhanced Hund’s coupling J H J_{H} . We show that when d z 2 d_{z^{2}} orbital is half-filled and its bonding band lies below Fermi level, electron pairing associated to J H J_{H} prevails. As d z 2 d_{z^{2}} orbital is hole-doped, this superconducting state becomes suppressed and a second superconductivity, which is largely insensitive to J H J_{H} but exhibiting a critical reliance on d z 2 d_{z^{2}} - d x 2 − y 2 d_{x^{2}-y^{2}} hybridization V V , arises. These two pairing states exhibit comparable maximum transition temperatures and evolve continuously from one to the other with varying d z 2 d_{z^{2}} doping, resulting a smooth T c T_{c} versus doping evolution. The dependence on various physical parameters of the two superconductivities is studied. Our results present a unified picture reconciling the debate about the role of d z 2 d_{z^{2}} orbital in pairing. We discuss the relevance of our results to recent experiments on superconducting La 3 ​ Ni 2 ​ O 7 \mathrm{La_{3}Ni_{2}O_{7}} films.

Discussion and experimental relevance -
Our results reconcile the apparent paradox that SC emerges in
{\mathrm{La_{3}Ni_{2}O_{7}}}
superconductors both with
Zhou et al. 2025
;
Li et al. 2025c
;
Li et al. 2025a
and without
Ko et al. 2025
;
Wang et al. 2025
a
\gamma
-pocket at Fermi level, as they are now considered representing two distinct dominant pairing states (SC I and SC II).
As revealed by DFT calculations
Geisler et al. 2024
, pristine
{\mathrm{La_{3}Ni_{2}O_{7}}}
films on SLAO substrate host a
\gamma-
band below Fermi level. The
\mathrm{Sr}-
doping, nevertheless, introduces holes and can elevate the
\gamma-
band to form a pocket at Fermi level
Shi et al. 2025a
;
Hu et al. 2025
;
Shi et al. 2025b
;
Yue et al. 2025
. The possible variance of
\mathrm{Sr}-
doping level across film experiments may explain the dichotomy on the presence/absence of
\gamma-
pocket, which eventually lead to the occurrence of two distinct predominant SCs in experiments. Notably, a most recent systematic
\mathrm{Sr-}
doping study in
{\mathrm{La_{3}Ni_{2}O_{7}}}
films
Hao et al. 2025
reports a continuous evolution of
T_{c}
as a function of
\mathrm{Sr-}
contend, yielding a phase diagram remarkably similar to ours (Fig.
3
). A key question then is how
\mathrm{Sr-}
doping can be related to
\delta_{z}
? Holes doped into the system occupy both
{d_{x^{2}-y^{2}}}
and
{d_{z^{2}}}
orbitals
Wú et al. 2024
;
Dong et al. 2024
. Our calculation indicates that holes went to
{d_{x^{2}-y^{2}}}
orbital barely affects SC, whereas those in
{d_{z^{2}}}
orbital govern the transition between SC I and SC II. Hence, experimentally probing the hole content in
{d_{z^{2}}}-
derived bands, as well as the strength of Hund’s coupling
J_{H}

are crucial for clarifying the pairing nature in future
{\mathrm{La_{3}Ni_{2}O_{7}}}
experiments
Wang and Yang 2025
;
Shao et al. 2025
.
How are the normal-state transportation properties
Liu et al.
;
Zhou et al. 2025
impacted by these parameters should also be
investigated in future studies.

Finally, while we consider the SC I pairing identified here is largely related to the“two-component” SC proposed in Ref.
Yang et al. 2023b
, and the SC II state to the Hund scenario proposed in Ref.
Lu et al. 2024
, significant difference can be still present. For instance, Ref.
Yang et al. 2023b
suggests
T_{c}
vanishing continuously as hybridization
V
approaches zero, whereas we observe a sudden drop in
T_{c}
around
V=0.35t
(Fig.
4

{\mathrm{La_{3}Ni_{2}O_{7}}}
superconductors.

Summary-
Using cellular dynamical mean-field theory, we have investigated superconducting states in a BLTO Kanamori-Hubbard model for
{\mathrm{La_{3}Ni_{2}O_{7}}}
on 2D square lattice. Our results reveal two distinct
s_{\pm}
-wave superconducting states, each arising from a different physical origin. The stability of these pairing states against variations in key physical parameters is verified. Our results resolve the ongoing debate concerning the role of
\gamma-
pocket in
{\mathrm{La_{3}Ni_{2}O_{7}}}
superconductivity. Finally, we discuss the relevance of these results to recent experiments.
