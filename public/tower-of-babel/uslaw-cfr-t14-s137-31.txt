14 C.F.R. § 137.31 — Aircraft requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:14:137.31
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-137.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 137 (Agricultural Aircraft Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person may operate an aircraft unless that aircraft—
(a) Meets the requirements of § 137.19(d); and
(b) Is equipped with a suitable and properly installed shoulder harness for use by each pilot.
