25 C.F.R. § 122.8 — Administrative costs for management of the fund.
Citation: us:cfr:25:122.8
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-122.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 122 (Management Of Osage Judgment Funds For Education). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Funds available for expenditures may be used by the Osage Tribal Education Committee in the performance of its duties and responsibilities. Recordkeeping is required and proposed expenditures are to be attached with the August 1 proposed annual budget to the Assistant Secretary or his/her designated representative.
