43 C.F.R. § 8223.0-1 — -1 Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:43:8223.0-1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-8223.0-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 8200 (Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to provide procedures for the management and protection of public lands having natural characteristics that are unusual or that are of scientific or other special interest.
