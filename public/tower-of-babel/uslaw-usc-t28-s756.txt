28 U.S.C. § 756 — Power to appoint
Citation: us:usc:28:756
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section756
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part III (Court Officers And Employees); Chapter 49 (District Courts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whenever a majority of the district judges of any district court cannot agree upon the appointment of any officer of such court, the chief judge shall make such appointment.
