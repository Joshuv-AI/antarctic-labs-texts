7 C.F.R. § 1944.251 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1944.251
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Rural Business-Cooperative Service, And Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1944.251
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1944 (Housing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirements of this subpart augment the requirements of section 802 of the National Affordable Housing Act of 1990 (approved November 28, 1990, Public Law 101-625) (42 U.S.C. 8011), (hereinafter, section 802), as amended by the Housing and Community Development Act of 1992 (Public Law 102-550, approved October 28, 1992), which authorizes the Congregate Housing Services Program (hereinafter, CHSP or Program).
