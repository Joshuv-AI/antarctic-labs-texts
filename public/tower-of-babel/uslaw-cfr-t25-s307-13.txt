25 C.F.R. § 307.13 — Licensee's responsibility.
Citation: us:cfr:25:307.13
Creator: Indian Arts And Crafts Board, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-307.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 307 (Navajo All-Wool Woven Fabrics; Use Of Government Certificate Of Genuineness). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Certificates may be attached only to products which are in the ownership or possession of the licensee. Certificates will be consecutively numbered and records of the allocation of such certificates will be maintained by the Indian Arts and Crafts Board. Each licensee will be held responsible for the proper use of such certificates and of the Government hand seal press furnished to such licensee.
