41 C.F.R. § 304-3.5 — -3.5 Handling direct payment offers.
Citation: us:cfr:41:304-3.5
Creator: Chapter 304—Payment Of Travel Expenses From A Non-Federal Source
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-304-3.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 304-3 (3—Employee Responsibility). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When contacted directly by a non-Federal source offering to pay travel expenses, employees must inform their agency and allow the authorized agency official to determine whether to accept the payment.
