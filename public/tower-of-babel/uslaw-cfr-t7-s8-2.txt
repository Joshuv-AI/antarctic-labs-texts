7 C.F.R. § 8.2 — Delegation of authority.
Citation: us:cfr:7:8.2
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-8.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 8 (4-H Club Name And Emblem). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director of the National Institute of Food and Agriculture, United States Department of Agriculture, may authorize the use of the 4-H Club Name and Emblem in accordance with the regulations in this part.
