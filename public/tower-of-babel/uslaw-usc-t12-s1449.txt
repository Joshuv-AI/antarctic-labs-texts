12 U.S.C. § 1449 — Reservation of right to amend or repeal chapter
Citation: us:usc:12:1449
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section1449
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 11 (Federal Home Loan Banks). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this chapter is expressly reserved.
