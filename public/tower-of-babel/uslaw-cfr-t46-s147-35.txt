46 C.F.R. § 147.35 — Purpose of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:46:147.35
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-147.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 147 (Hazardous Ships' Stores). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes special requirements applicable to particular, named materials. These requirements are in addition to the general requirements in subpart A applicable to those materials.
