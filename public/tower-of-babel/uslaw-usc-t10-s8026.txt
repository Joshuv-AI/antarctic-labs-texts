10 U.S.C. § 8026 — Consultation with Commandant of the Marine Corps on major decisions directly concerning Marine Corps aviation or amphibious force structure and capability
Citation: us:usc:10:8026
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8026
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization); Chapter 803 (Department Of The Navy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Navy shall ensure that the views of the Commandant of the Marine Corps are given appropriate consideration before a major decision is made by an element of the Department of the Navy outside the Marine Corps on a matter that directly concerns Marine Corps aviation or amphibious force structure and capability.
