9 C.F.R. § 88.6 — Violations and penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:9:88.6
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-88.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 88 (Commercial Transportation Of Equines For Slaughter). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Secretary is authorized to assess civil penalties of up to $5,000 per violation of any of the regulations in this part.
(b) Each equine transported in violation of the regulations of this part will be considered a separate violation.
