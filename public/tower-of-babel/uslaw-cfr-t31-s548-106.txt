31 C.F.R. § 548.106 — Delegation of certain authorities of the Secretary of the Treasury.
Citation: us:cfr:31:548.106
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-548.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 548 (Belarus Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any action that the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized to take pursuant to E.O. 13405 of June 16, 2006, E.O. 14038 of August 9, 2021, and any further Executive orders relating to the national emergency declared in E.O. 13405, may be taken by the Director of OFAC or by any other person to whom the Secretary of the Treasury has delegated authority so to act.
