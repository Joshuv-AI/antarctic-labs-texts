18 C.F.R. § 1308.11 — Contractor's request for relief.
Citation: us:cfr:18:1308.11
Creator: Tennessee Valley Authority
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-1308.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 1308 (Contract Disputes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any request for relief which a Contractor believes is due under a contract shall be submitted to the Contracting Officer in writing, in accordance with the terms of the contract, including applicable time limits.
