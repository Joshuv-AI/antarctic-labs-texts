12 C.F.R. § 229.60 — Variation by agreement.
Citation: us:cfr:12:229.60
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-229.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 229 (Availability Of Funds And Collection Of Checks (Regulation Cc)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any provision of § 229.55 may be varied by agreement of the banks involved. No other provision of this subpart may be varied by agreement by any person or persons.
