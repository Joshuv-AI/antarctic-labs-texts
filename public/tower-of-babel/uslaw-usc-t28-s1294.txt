28 U.S.C. § 1294 — Circuits in which decisions reviewable
Citation: us:usc:28:1294
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1294
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part IV (Jurisdiction And Venue); Chapter 83 (Courts Of Appeals). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as provided in sections 1292(c), 1292(d), and 1295 of this title, appeals from reviewable decisions of the district and territorial courts shall be taken to the courts of appeals as follows:
(1)
From a district court of the United States to the court of appeals for the circuit embracing the district;
(2)
From the United States District Court for the District of the Canal Zone, to the Court of Appeals for the Fifth Circuit;
(3)
From the District Court of the Virgin Islands, to the Court of Appeals for the Third Circuit;
(4)
From the District Court of Guam, to the Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit.
