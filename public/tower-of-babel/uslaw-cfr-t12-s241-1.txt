12 C.F.R. § 241.1 — Authority and purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:12:241.1
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-241.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 241 (Securities Holding Companies (Regulation Oo)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Authority. This part is issued by the Board pursuant to section 618 of the Dodd-Frank Wall Street Reform and Consumer Protection Act (12 U.S.C. 1850a).
(b) Purpose. This part establishes the procedures by which a securities holding company may elect to register to be supervised by the Board.
