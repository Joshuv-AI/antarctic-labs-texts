46 U.S.C. § 10701 — Application
Citation: us:usc:46:10701
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section10701
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part G (Merchant Seamen Protection And Relief); Chapter 107 (Effects Of Deceased Seamen). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Except as otherwise specifically provided, this chapter applies to a vessel on a voyage between—
(1)
a port of the United States and a port in a foreign country (except a port in Canada, Mexico, and the West Indies); and
(2)
a port of the United States on the Atlantic Ocean and a port of the United States on the Pacific Ocean.
(b)
This chapter does not apply to a vessel on which a seaman by custom or agreement is entitled to share in the profit or result of a voyage.
(c)
This chapter does not apply to a foreign vessel.
