10 U.S.C. § 8663 — Number in service in time of peace
Citation: us:usc:10:8663
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8663
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 863 (Naval Vessels). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In time of peace, the President may keep in service such vessels of the Navy as are required and keep the rest in reserve.
