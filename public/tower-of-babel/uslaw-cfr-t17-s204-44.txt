17 C.F.R. § 204.44 — Interest, penalties, and administrative costs.
Citation: us:cfr:17:204.44
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-204.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 204 (Rules Relating To Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Charges may be assessed for interest, penalties, and administrative costs in accordance with the Federal Claims Collection Standards, 31 CFR 901.9.
