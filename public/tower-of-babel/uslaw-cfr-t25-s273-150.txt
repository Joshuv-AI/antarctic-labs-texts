25 C.F.R. § 273.150 — Does an existing contracting party need to submit any reports?
Citation: us:cfr:25:273.150
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-273.150
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 273 (Education Contracts Under Johnson-O'Malley Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each existing contracting party must submit an annual report based on the JOM funding received and other contract-related reports as required by the BIE Director.
