25 C.F.R. § 161.802 — How will the Navajo Nation recommend amendments to this part?
Citation: us:cfr:25:161.802
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-161.802
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 161 (Navajo Partitioned Lands Grazing Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Resources Committee will have final authority on behalf of the Navajo Nation to approve amendments to the Navajo Partitioned Lands grazing provisions, upon the recommendation of the Grazing Committee and the Navajo-Hopi Land Commission, and the concurrence of BIA.
