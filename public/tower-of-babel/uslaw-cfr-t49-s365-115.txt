49 C.F.R. § 365.115 — After publication in the FMCSA Register.
Citation: us:cfr:49:365.115
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-365.115
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 365 (Rules Governing Applications For Operating Authority). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Interested persons have 10 days from the date of FMCSA Register publication to file protests. See Subpart B of this part.
(b) If no one opposes the application, the grant published in the FMCSA Register will become effective by issuance of a certificate, permit, or license.
