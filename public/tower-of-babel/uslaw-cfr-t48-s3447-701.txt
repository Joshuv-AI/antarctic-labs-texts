48 C.F.R. § 3447.701 — 3447.701 Foreign travel clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:3447.701
Creator: Chapter 34—Department Of Education Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 3447 (Transportation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer must insert the clause at 3452.247-70 (Foreign travel) in all solicitations and resultant cost-reimbursement contracts where foreign travel is contemplated.
