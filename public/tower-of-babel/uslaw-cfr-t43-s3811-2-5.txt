43 C.F.R. § 3811.2-5 — -5 O and C and Coos Bay Wagon Road lands.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3811.2-5
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3811.2-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3810 (Lands And Minerals Subject To Location). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Revested Oregon and California Railroad and Reconveyed Coos Bay Wagon Road Grant Lands, located in Oregon, are subject to mining locations in accordance with provisions of subpart 3821 of this chapter.
