46 C.F.R. § 134.160 — Freeboard markings.
Citation: us:cfr:46:134.160
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-134.160
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 134 (Added Provisions For Liftboats). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Freeboard markings required by § 174.260 of this subchapter must be both permanently scribed or embossed and painted white or yellow on a dark background.
