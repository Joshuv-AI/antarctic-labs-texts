28 C.F.R. § 8.11 — Interplay of administrative and criminal judicial forfeiture proceedings.
Citation: us:cfr:28:8.11
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-8.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 8 (Forfeiture Authority For Certain Statutes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An administrative forfeiture proceeding pending against seized or restrained property does not bar the Government from alleging that the same property is forfeitable in a criminal case. Notwithstanding the fact that an allegation of forfeiture has been included in a criminal indictment or information, the property may be administratively forfeited in a parallel proceeding.
