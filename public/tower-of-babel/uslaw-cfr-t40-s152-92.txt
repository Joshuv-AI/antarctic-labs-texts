40 C.F.R. § 152.92 — Submission of a new valid study.
Citation: us:cfr:40:152.92
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-152.92
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 152 (Pesticide Registration And Classification Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An applicant may demonstrate compliance for a data requirement by submitting a valid study that has not previously been submitted to the Agency. A study previously submitted to the Agency should not be resubmitted but should be cited in accordance with § 152.93.
