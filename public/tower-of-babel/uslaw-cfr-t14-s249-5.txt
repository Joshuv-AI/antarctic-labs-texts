14 C.F.R. § 249.5 — Storage of records.
Citation: us:cfr:14:249.5
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-249.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 249 (Preservation Of Air Carrier Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each carrier shall provide reasonable protection from damage by fire, floods, and other hazards for records subject to the provisions of this part.
