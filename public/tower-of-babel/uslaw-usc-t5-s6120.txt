5 U.S.C. § 6120 — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:5:6120
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section6120
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart E (Attendance And Leave); Chapter 61 (Hours Of Work); Subchapter II (Flexible And Compressed Work Schedules). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Congress finds that the use of flexible and compressed work schedules has the potential to improve productivity in the Federal Government and provide greater service to the public.
