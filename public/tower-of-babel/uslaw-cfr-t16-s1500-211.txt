16 C.F.R. § 1500.211 — Guaranty.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1500.211
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1500.211
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1500 (Hazardous Substances And Articles: Administration And Enforcement Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the case of the giving of a guaranty or undertaking referred to in section 5(b)(2) of the act, each person signing such guaranty or undertaking, or causing it to be signed, shall be considered to have given it. Each person causing a guaranty or undertaking to be false is chargeable with violations of section 4(d) of the act.
