41 C.F.R. § 302-10.3 — -10.3 Allowances for transporting a mobile home for an employee and immediate family member(s).
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-10.3
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-10.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-10 (10—Allowances For Transportation Of Mobile Homes And Boats Used As A Primary Residence). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Allowances for transporting a mobile home (including mileage when towed by a POV) are in addition to the reimbursement of subsistence expenses, mileage, and transportation expenses for the employee and their immediate family member(s). Allowances under parts 302-5, 302-6, and 302-11 of this chapter will be paid accordingly.
