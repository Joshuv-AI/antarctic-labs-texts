41 C.F.R. § 105-55.005 — -55.005 Form of payment.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-55.005
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-55.005
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-55 (55—Collection Of Claims Owed The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Claims may be paid in the form of money or, when a contractual basis exists, the General Services Administration may demand the return of specific property or the performance of specific services.
