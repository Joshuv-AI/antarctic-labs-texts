10 U.S.C. § 7213 — Corps of Engineers: assignment or transfer of officers to duties involving civil functions
Citation: us:usc:10:7213
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7213
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 723 (Special Appointments, Assignments, Details, And Duties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Officers of the Corps of Engineers may be assigned or transferred to and from duties involving the civil functions of the Corps of Engineers only with the approval of the Secretary of the Army.
