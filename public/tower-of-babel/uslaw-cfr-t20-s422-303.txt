20 C.F.R. § 422.303 — Interest, late payment penalties, and administrative costs of collection.
Citation: us:cfr:20:422.303
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-422.303
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 422 (Organization And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We may charge the debtor with interest, late payment penalties, and our costs of collection on delinquent debts covered by this subpart when authorized by our regulations issued in accordance with the Federal Claims Collection Standards (31 CFR 901.9).
