41 C.F.R. § 105-68.635 — -68.635 May the General Services Administration settle a debarment or suspension action?
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-68.635
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-68.635
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-68 (68—Governmentwide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, we may settle a debarment or suspension action at any time if it is in the best interest of the Federal Government.
