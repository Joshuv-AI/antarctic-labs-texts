Retraction Note: Knockdown of CCNB2 inhibits the tumorigenesis of gastric cancer by regulation of the PI3K/Akt pathway
Authors: Ming Hu, Pengxian Tao, Yongfeng Wang, Chengzhang Zhu, Yuntao Ma, Xiaolong Liu, Hui Cai
Published: 2026

Retraction of: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-89948-4, published online 17 February 2025

The Editors have retracted this Article.

Upon publication, concerns were raised about partially overlapping images between Figures 3F and 6F, inconsistencies in timings described in the methods and Figures 3E, 4A, and 4C, and the follow-up timing for patients shown in Figure 2B. Upon request by the Editors, the Authors provided underlying raw data; however, this data was unable to fully address the concerns.

The Editors have therefore lost confidence in the results presented in the Article.

The Authors did not respond to the Editors’ communication about this retraction.
