7 C.F.R. § 1219.55 — Exemption from assessment.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1219.55
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Miscellaneous Commodities), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1219.55
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1219 (Hass Avocado Promotion, Research, And Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Any sale of Hass avocados for export from the United States is exempt from assessment.
(b) The Board may require persons receiving an exemption from assessments to provide to the Board reports on the disposition of exempt Hass avocados.
