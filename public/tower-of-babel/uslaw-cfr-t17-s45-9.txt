17 C.F.R. § 45.9 — Third-party facilitation of data reporting.
Citation: us:cfr:17:45.9
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-45.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 45 (Swap Data Recordkeeping And Reporting Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Registered entities and reporting counterparties required by this part to report required swap creation data or required swap continuation data, while remaining fully responsible for reporting as required by this part, may contract with third-party service providers to facilitate reporting.
