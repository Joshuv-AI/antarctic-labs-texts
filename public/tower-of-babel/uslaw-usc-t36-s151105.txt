36 U.S.C. § 151105 — Powers
Citation: us:usc:36:151105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section151105
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1511 (National Education Association Of The United States). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation may—
(1)
adopt and amend bylaws;
(2)
adopt and alter a corporate seal;
(3)
acquire, own, lease, encumber, and transfer property to carry out the purposes of the corporation;
(4)
accept and administer a trust for educational purposes;
(5)
borrow money, issue instruments of indebtedness, and secure its obligations by granting security interests in its property; and
(6)
sue and be sued in any court of the United States, or other court of competent jurisdiction.
