10 U.S.C. § 2265 — Access to broadband internet access service for certain members of the armed forces
Citation: us:usc:10:2265
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2265
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Property); Chapter 134 (Miscellaneous Administrative Provisions); Subchapter II (Miscellaneous Administrative Authority). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of a military department may provide, to a member of the armed forces who resides in military unaccompanied housing (as defined in section 2871 of this title) within the United States, broadband internet access service, at no cost to such member.
