33 U.S.C. § 926 — Costs in proceedings brought without reasonable grounds
Citation: us:usc:33:926
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section926
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 18 (Longshore And Harbor Workers’ Compensation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If the court having jurisdiction of proceedings in respect of any claim or compensation order determines that the proceedings in respect of such claim or order have been instituted or continued without reasonable ground, the costs of such proceedings shall be assessed against the party who has so instituted or continued such proceedings.
