25 U.S.C. § 4117 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:25:4117
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section4117
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 43 (Native American Housing Assistance And Self-Determination); Subchapter I (Block Grants And Grant Requirements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated for grants under this subchapter such sums as may be necessary for each of fiscal years 2009 through 2013. This section shall take effect on October 26, 1996.
