DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Foreign-Trade Zones Board

[B-36-2025]


Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) 12; Authorization of Production 
Activity; Avant Technology, Inc.; (Solid-State Drives and Memory 
Modules); Pharr, Texas

    On July 2, 2025, Avant Technology, Inc., submitted a notification 
of proposed production activity to the FTZ Board for its facility 
within FTZ 12, in Pharr, Texas.
    The notification was processed in accordance with the regulations 
of the FTZ Board (15 CFR part 400), including notice in the Federal 
Register inviting public comment (90 FR 30048, July 8, 2025). On July 
31, 2026, the applicant was notified of the FTZ Board's decision that 
the proposed activity has been authorized on a limited basis, subject 
to the FTZ Act and the Board's regulations, including section 400.14, 
and subject to a one-year time period.

    Dated: July 31, 2026.
Elizabeth Whiteman,
Executive Secretary.
