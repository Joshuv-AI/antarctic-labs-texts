46 C.F.R. § 502.407 — Authority of the arbitrator.
Citation: us:cfr:46:502.407
Creator: Federal Maritime Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-502.407
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 502 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An arbitrator to whom a dispute is referred may—
(a) Regulate the course of and conduct arbitral hearings;
(b) Administer oaths and affirmations;
(c) Compel the attendance of witnesses and production of evidence at the hearing under the provisions of 9 U.S.C. 7 only to the extent the Commission is otherwise authorized by law to do so; and
(d) Make awards.
