43 U.S.C. § 126 — Change of location of land offices
Citation: us:usc:43:126
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section126
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 5 (Land Districts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President is authorized to change the location of the land offices in the several land districts established by law, and to relocate the same from time to time at such point in the district as he deems expedient.
