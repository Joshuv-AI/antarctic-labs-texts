Introduction

All groups

The number of

Several researchers have investigated the properties of singular elements in different types of groups. In 1991, Guralnick and L

[

[
12
]

-singular. In 2012, Babai, Guest,
Praeger and Wilson (see
[
2
]
) investigated the proportions of
r
-regular elements in finite classical groups. Brandl and Shi (see
[
3
]
) characterized

{\operatorname{PSL}}(2,q)
by its set of orders of elements.
Denote by

the number of
r
-singluar elements of

. Let
{\mu_{r}}(G):=

denote the proportion of
r
-singular elements
of

.
Let

be the set of all proportions of
r
-singular elements for each prime

that divides
|G|
. In this paper, we use this set to characterize

{\operatorname{PSL}}(2,q)
. Our main result is the following theorem:

Theorem 1.1
.

Let

be a finite group, and

. If
\mu(G)=\mu({\operatorname{PSL}}(2,q))
, then

Remark 2.5 in Section 2 says that
