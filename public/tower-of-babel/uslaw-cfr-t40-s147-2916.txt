40 C.F.R. § 147.2916 — Coverage of permitting requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:40:147.2916
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-147.2916
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 147 (State, Tribal, And Epa-Administered Underground Injection Control Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The owner or operator of a new Class II injection well or any other Class II well required to have a permit in the Osage Mineral Reserve shall comply with the requirements of §§ 147.2903, 147.2907, 147.2918, through 147.2928.
