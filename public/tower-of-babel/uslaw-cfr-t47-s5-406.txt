47 C.F.R. § 5.406 — Responsible party, “stop-buzzer,” and notification requirements, and additional requirements related to safety of the public.
Citation: us:cfr:47:5.406
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-5.406
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 5 (Experimental Radio Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Medical testing licensees must identify a single point of contact responsible for all experiments conducted under the license and must also identify a “stop buzzer” point of contact for all experiments, consistent with subpart E, §§ 5.307 and 5.308.
(b) Medical testing licensees must meet the notification and safety of the public requirements of subpart E, §§ 5.309 and 5.311.
