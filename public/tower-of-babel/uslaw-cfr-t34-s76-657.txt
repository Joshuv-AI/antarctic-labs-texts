34 C.F.R. § 76.657 — Separate classes prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:34:76.657
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-76.657
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 76 (State-Administered Formula Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A subgrantee may not use program funds for classes that are organized separately on the basis of school enrollment or religion of the students if:
(a) The classes are at the same site; and
(b) The classes include students enrolled in public schools and students enrolled in private schools.
