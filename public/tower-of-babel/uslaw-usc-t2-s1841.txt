2 U.S.C. § 1841 — Single per annum gross rates of pay
Citation: us:usc:2:1841
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1841
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 28 (Architect Of The Capitol); Subchapter III (Personnel); Part B (Compensation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whenever the rate of pay of—
(1)
an employee of the Office of the Architect of the Capitol; or
(2)
an employee of the House Restaurant, or of the Senate Restaurant, under the supervision of the Architect of the Capitol as an agent of the House or Senate, respectively, as the case may be;
is fixed or adjusted on or after the effective date of this section, that rate, as so fixed and adjusted, shall be a single per annum gross rate.
