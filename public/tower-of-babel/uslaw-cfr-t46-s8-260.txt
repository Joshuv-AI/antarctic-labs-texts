46 C.F.R. § 8.260 — Revocation of classification society recognition.
Citation: us:cfr:46:8.260
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-8.260
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 8 (Vessel Inspection Alternatives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A recognized classification society which fails to maintain the minimum standards established in this part will be reevaluated and its recognized status revoked if warranted.
