22 U.S.C. § 2023 — Actions and votes of representatives
Citation: us:usc:22:2023
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2023
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 28 (International Atomic Energy Agency Participation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The representatives provided for in section 2021 of this title, when representing the United States in the organs of the Agency, shall, at all times, act in accordance with the instructions of the President, and such representatives shall, in accordance with such instructions, cast any and all votes under the Statute of the International Atomic Energy Agency.
