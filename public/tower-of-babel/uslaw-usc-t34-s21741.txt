34 U.S.C. § 21741 — Training and technical assistance for States
Citation: us:usc:34:21741
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section21741
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 217 (Elder Abuse Prevention And Prosecution); Subchapter IV (Robert Matava Elder Abuse Prosecution Act Of 2017). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General, in consultation with the Secretary of Health and Human Services and in coordination with the Elder Justice Coordinating Council (established under section 1397k of title 42), shall create, compile, evaluate, and disseminate materials and information, and provide the necessary training and technical assistance, to assist States and units of local government in—
(1)
investigating, prosecuting, pursuing, preventing, understanding, and mitigating the impact of—
(A)
physical, sexual, and psychological abuse of elders;
(B)
exploitation of elders, including financial abuse and scams targeting elders; and
(C)
neglect of elders; and
(2)
assessing, addressing, and mitigating the physical and psychological trauma to victims of elder abuse.
