25 U.S.C. § 179 — Driving stock to feed on lands
Citation: us:usc:25:179
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section179
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 5 (Protection Of Indians). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Every person who drives or otherwise conveys any stock of horses, mules, or cattle, to range and feed on any land belonging to any Indian or Indian tribe, without the consent of such tribe, is liable to a penalty of $1 for each animal of such stock. This section shall not apply to Creek lands.
