Uncovering seed vigor responsive miRNA in hybrid wheat and its parents by deep sequencing
Authors: Jie-ru Yue, Yong-jie Liu, Shao-hua Yuan, Hui Sun, Hong-yao Lou, Yan-mei Li, Hao-yu Guo, Zi-han Liu, Feng-ting Zhang, Nuo Zhai, Sheng-quan Zhang, Jian-fang Bai, Li-ping Zhang
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
AbstractBackgroundTwo-line hybrid wheat technology system is one way to harness wheat heterosis both domestically and internationally. Seed vigor is a crucial parameter for assessing seed quality, as enhanced seed vigor can lead to yield increments of over 20% to a certain extent. MicroRNAs (miRNAs) were known to participate in the development and vigor of seed in plants, but its impact on seed vigor in two-line hybrid wheat remains poorly elucidated.ResultsThe hybrid (BS1453/11GF5135) wheat exhibited superiority in seed vigor and anti-aging capacity, compared to its male parent (11GF5135, MP) and female parent (BS1453, FP). We identified four miRNAs associated with seed vigor, all of which are novel miRNAs. The majority of targets of miRNAs were related to ubiquitin ligases, kinases, sucrose synthases and hydrolases, involving in starch and sucrose metabolism, hydrolysis, catalysis, plant hormone signal transduction, and other pathways, which played crucial roles in seed development. Additionally, we also found miR531 was differentially expressed in both male parent and hybrid, and its target gene was a component of the E1 subunit of α-ketoate dehydrogenase complex, which interacted with dihydrolipoamide acetyltransferase (E2) and dihydrolipoyl dehydrogenase (E3). Finally, We established a presumptive interaction model to speculate the relationship of miR531 and seed vigor.ConclusionsThis study analyzed the seed vigor of two-line hybrid wheat, and screened seed vigor-related miRNAs. Meanwhile speculated the genetic relationship of hybrid and parents, in terms of miRNAs. Consequently, the present study provides new insights into the miRNA-mediated gene and protein interaction network that regulates seed vigor. These findings hold significance for enhancing the yield and quality of two-line hybrid wheat, facilitating its future applications.Supplementary InformationThe online version contains supplementary material available at 10.1186/s12864-024-10878-y.Keywords: Two-line hybrid wheat, Seed vigor, MiRNA, RNA-seq

Background

Seed vigor refers to the potential activity level and performance of seeds during germination and emergence. These attributes dictate the ability of seeds to undergo normal and rapid emergence, as well as their development under diverse field conditions (Association of Official Seed Analysis, AOSA). Seed vigor gradually increases during seed development and peaks at physiological maturity stages [1]. Prolonged storage can lead to a reduction in seed vigor, a phenomenon known as seed aging, which is irreversible [2]. Due to the lengthy duration of natural aging, it is difficult to meet the requirements of experimental research. Therefore, artificial aging is usually employed to simulate the natural aging process [3]. Aging affected the enzyme activity, particularly that of antioxidant enzyme in seeds. The antioxidant system plays a crucial role in maintaining a balance of reactive oxygen species (ROS) and cellular homeostasis. The effectiveness of antioxidant enzymes in eliminating free radicals determines the extent of cellular damage [4]. Hydrogen peroxide levels in cells are mainly regulated by antioxidant enzymes such as SOD, peroxidase (POD), ascorbate peroxidase (APX) and catalase (CAT) [5].

miRNAs are endogenous non-coding RNAs with an average length of about 22nt. In plants, miRNAs are mainly regulated through mRNA cleavage or translational repression of target mRNAs. Numerous studies have demonstrated the regulatory role of miRNAs in seed development and vigor in various plant species, including Arabidopsis [6, 7], rice [8–10], maize [11], barley [12] and wheat [13, 14]. For example, miR160 targets auxin response factor (ARF17), which functions at the post-transcriptional level. It is essential for the normal development of Arabidopsis [6]. The miR164-mediated regulation of NAC (NAM, ATAF1/2, CUC2) gene participates in flower and embryo development of vegetative organs in Arabidopsis [15]. miR156 plays a role in rice panicle branche development by targeting OsSPL14, which is significant to increase grain yield [16]. Over-expression of miR397 in rice leads to larger grain size and increased branches on panicle, ultimately boosting rice yield [17]. The up-regulation of miR164c and down-regulation of miR168a in rice have been found to decrease the viability and anti-aging ability, making them less suitable for storage [18]. Differential expression of miR168 and miR817 has been observed in artificially aged seeds, suggesting that their involvement in rice seed vigor [19]. Additionally, as a maternal effector gene, miR167a is involved in embryo and seed development. Normal embryo development and unimpeded seed development were observed in cases whether the seed coat genotype were miR167A+/+ and miR167A+/−. Conversely, defective embryo development, with consequential impacts on seed development, was observed when the seed coat genotype was miR167A−/− [20]. In wheat, 104 miRNAs related to grain filling have been identified, suggesting their crucial role in wheat grain maturation [13]. However, the function of miRNAs on seed vigor in two-line hybrid wheat is still unknown.

Heterosis utilization is considered as an important way to increase yield per unit area of wheat [21], and two-line hybrid wheat is one of the important ways to utilize heterosis [22]. Despite the superior yield potential of hybrid wheat compared to common wheat, the vigor of its seeds directly impacts the quantity of seeds utilized and the ultimate yield [23]. Regrettably, no effective strategy has been developed thus far to enhance the longevity of hybrid wheat seeds. Therefore, comprehending the molecular regulatory mechanism governing seed vigor in hybrid wheat assumes paramount importance. To date, a great deal of efforts have been devoted to discovering new miRNAs, unraveling their roles in various biological processes. However, the research of miRNA in two-line hybrid wheat is still in the blank stage, and no miRNA has been proved to have effect on seed vigor. In order to investigate whether hybrid show an advantage over its parents in seed vigor and the roles of miRNAs play in this process, we measured the physiological indexes of seed vigor in both hybrid and parents before and after aging process. Additionally, we also screened miRNAs related to seed vigor through high-throughput sequencing technology, and predicted the function of their target genes. This study will lay a foundation for further research on the regulation of miRNAs on hybrid wheat seed vigor.

Rusults

Effects of artificial aging on seed vigor of hybrid and parents

To investigate the mechanism of seed vigor in hybrid and parents, artificial aging treatment was performed. Then we measured the germination index parameters of artificially accelerated aging treated seeds, including germination rate (GR), germination potential (GP), germination index (GI) and vigor index (VI). The results showed that the hybrid exhibited a higher GR than its parents (Fig. 1A), and the GP, GI and VI of the hybrid surpassed those of its parents, with the exception of the third and ninth day of aging (Fig. 1B and D). These results indicated that the hybrid possesses greater resilience against the effects of aging. Although artificial aging treatment for three, five, seven and nine days resulted in a decline in the germination index parameters of both hybrid and the parents seeds, the performance of the hybrid seeds exceed than that of the parents (Fig. 1E).

Effects of artificial aging on enzyme activities of hybrid and parents seeds

We conducted an analysis of antioxidate enzyme and α-amylase activities of seeds at different aging time points. Interestingly, we observed that the activities of APX and POD in hybrid initially decreased at A3 days, followed by an increase. With the extension of aging time, the activities of APX and POD in the hybrid surpassed that of its parents (Fig. 2A-B). Similarly, the activity of SOD also decreased at A3 days, except in male parent, and exhibited a significant increase at A9 days (Fig. 2C). In contrast, no obvious differences were observed in the α-amylase and CAT activity between the hybrid and parents seeds (Fig. 2D-E). The results indicated that the hybrid possesses enhanced seed vigor and anti-aging ability compared to its parents.

Isolation and identification of seed vigor associated miRNAs

In order to investigate miRNAs associated with seed vigor and their roles in the genetic relationship between hybrid and its parents, high-throughput sequencing assays were performed on parents and hybrid for unaged and aged nine days seeds. The results showed that unaged female parent (UAFP), male parent (UAMP) and hybrid (UAHybrid), resulted in 40,689,650, 49,670,143, and 41,219,530 raw sequence reads (Table S1), and 4,989,431, 7,355,925, and 4,497,358 unique reads (Table 1), respectively. Similarly, artificially aged female parent (AFP), male parent (AMP), and hybrid (AHybrid) generated 39,407,927, 41,767,554, and 46,021,968 raw sequence reads (Table S1), resulting in 4,175,962, 3,536,794, and 4,999,378 unique reads (Table 1), respectively. Further analysis showed that the majority of miRNAs were 24nt in length, and the miRNAs were identified across all the chromosomes (Fig. S1).

Analysis of differentially expressed miRNAs in wheat seeds

In order to obtain seed vigor related miRNAs in wheat, differential expressed miRNAs analysis were performed. 603, 193 and 180 differentially expressed miRNAs were screened in FP, MP and Hybrid, among which 8, 11 and 3 were known, respectively (Fig. 4A-C, Table S2-4). Notably, four seed vigor-related miRNAs were differentially expressed in both hybrid and its parents, so we conducted an analysis to these miRNAs (Fig. 4D). To elucidate the genetic relationship between hybrid and its parents in terms of seed vigor-related miRNAs, we performed a comparative analysis between hybrid and its parents. It uncovered 35 differentially expressed miRNAs between the Hybrid and FP, as well as 17 miRNAs between the Hybrid and MP (Fig. 4E-F). Furthermore, one known miRNA-miR531 displayed differential expression in both the Hybrid and MP. However, there was no known miRNA differentially expressed in both the Hybrid and FP.

Functional analysis of differentially expressed miRNAs

Based on expression profile analysis, we observed highly significant expression patterns in aging-induced miRNAs of the parents and hybrid (Fig. 5A-B). In order to further understand the roles of miRNAs in seed vigor and their contribution to the genetic relationship between hybrid and its parents, we conducted GO enrichment and KEGG pathway analyses of miRNA target genes in FP/Hybrid and MP/Hybrid groups. The top significantly enriched GO terms are shown in Fig. 5C. Notably, the most obvious seed vigor-related differentially expressed genes (DEGs) of FP and Hybrid were associated with biological processes, such as, response to “tubulin deacetylation”, and “cellular component organization or biogenesis”. In contrast, the enriched GO terms for MP and Hybrid seed vigor-related DEGs included “developmental process involved in reproduction” and “reactive oxygen species biosynthetic process” (Fig. 5C-D).

Furthermore, the KEGG pathway analysis revealed that FP and Hybrid seed vigor-related targets of miRNAs were enriched in “starch and sucrose metabolism”, “brassinosteroid biosynthesis” and “glycerophospholipid metabolism” pathways. In contrast, the MP and Hybrid seed vigor-related targets of miRNAs were primarily enriched in “carbon fixation in photosynthetic organisms”, “ascorbate and aldarate metabolism”, “carbon metabolism” and “plant hormone signal transduction” pathways (Fig. 5E-F).

Analysis of proteins interaction and prediction of regulation pathway for the target of miR531 on seed vigor

In our research, miR531 differentially expressed in both Hybrid and MP seeds. Protein interaction prediction for the target gene of miR531 was performed. There were 24 targets of miR531, and most of them have the activity of aspartic-type endopeptidase, protein serine and threonine kinase. However, the target, TraesCS6A02G036000 exhibited 100% homology to wheat 2-oxoisovalerate dehydrogenase subunit beta1, mitochondrial-like, annotated as involved in metabolic pathways and possessed redox enzyme activity [24]. Given the crucial role of redox enzyme in seed vigor during seed development, this finding holds particular significance. The 2-oxoisovalerate dehydrogenase subunit beta1 is the branched-chain alpha-keto dehydrogenase complex, and it is one of the components of the heterotetramer E1 subunit of α-ketoate dehydrogenase complex, which catalyzes the overall conversion of alpha-keto acids to acyl-CoA and CO2 [25]. Moreover, our investigation unveiled that TraesCS6A02G036000 interacted with dihydrolipoamide acetyltransferase (TraesCS2D02G073400.1, E2) and dihydrolipoyl dehydrogenase (TraesCS1B02G084700.1, E3) (Fig. 6). E2, E3, and pyruvate dehydrogenase (E1) are the principal components of pyruvate dehydrogenase complex (PDHc). PDHc is a group of rate-regulating enzymes that catalyze irreversible oxidative decarboxylation of pyruvate into acetyl coA (Acetyl-CoA). At the same time, PDHc reduces NAD+ to NADH. Notably, defects in PDHc can lead to metabolic disorders and tissue damage [25]. Therefore, we speculated that miR531 might interact with TraesCS6A02G036000 to regulate seed vigor (Fig. 7).

Analysis of expression patterns of seed vigor-related miRNAs and target genes

Nine target genes corresponding to four miRNAs associated with seed vigor were performed qRT-PCR analysis. After nine days aging treatment, the target gene TraesCS5B02G204400 of novel miR-212568-5p possessed ubiquitin-protein transferase activity, and was up-regulated in parents and hybrid (Table S5, Fig. 8C), while TraesCS3D02G013000 and TraesCS1A02G436500 possessed sucrose synthase and catalytic activity, which were up-regulated in parents but down-regulated in hybrid (Table S5, Fig. 8A-B). For novel miR-212117-5p, it had four target genes, which were involved in plant hormone signal transduction and displayed consistent. They were up-regulation in parents but down-regulation in hybrid (Table S5, Fig. 8D-G). Additionally, the target gene TraesCS7A02G006900 of novel miR-19960-3p possessed cysteine peptidyase activity, showing down-regulated in parents and up-regulated in hybrid (Table S5, Fig. 8H). In contrast, the target gene TraesCS5A02G164200 of novel miR-170814-5p, which possessed hydrolase activity, was up-regulated in parents and down-regulated in hybrid (Table S5, Fig. 8I). In this study, we also found a known miRNA-miR531, and its target gene TraesCS6A02G036000 down-regulated in both hybrid and male parent (Fig. 8J). These results showed that the expression patterns of most target genes were opposite to those of the miRNAs, suggesting that miRNAs might interact with its target genes to regulate seed vigor. To verify the function of miRNAs and the reliability of sequencing results, 19 differentially expressed miRNAs were selected randomly for qRT-PCR validation, and correlation analysis was performed with the sequencing data, R2>0.8, indicating a good correlation, it verifying the reliability of sequencing data (Fig. 8K).

Discussion

Hybrid wheat showed heterosis in seed vigor

Heterosis is the increased growth or agronomic performance of heterozygous F1 offspring relative to genetically distinct homozygous parents. It has become one of the most successful strategies for increasing production [26, 27]. The GR, GI and VI of hybrid wheat were significantly higher than those of conventional wheat under the same artificial aging treatment time have been confirmed, in BNS type hybrid wheat [28]. In this study, our findings revealed that the germination index of hybrid seeds consistently surpassed that of their parents counterparts, except for aging nine days (Fig. 1). Additionally, early scholars used the measurement of enzyme activity as an evaluation technique for predicting seed vigor and evaluating seed deterioration [29]. SOD activity of hybrids were higher than that of their parents at four different growth stages in sorghum [30]. In terms of CAT, POD and SOD enzyme, some hybrid cotton varieties had superparental advantages in some developmental stages [31]. Under the same aging conditions, the POD activity of hybrid wheat seeds was higher than that of conventional wheat, and the decrease trend of POD activity was relatively gentle [28]. Our research found that APX and POD exhibiting a parallel trajectory, while the parents seeds displayed varying degrees of decline with prolonged aging, the hybrid seeds manifested a continuous increase after three days of aging, followed by a decrease after nine days of aging (Fig. 2A-B). The SOD increased sharply after nine days of aging, in hybrid (Fig. 2C). These results indicated that the hybrid had higher anti-aging ability, and it exhibited heterosis in seed vigor.

Four novel miRNAs interact with target genes to regulate seed vigor

Recent studies have elucidated the regulation impact of miRNAs on seed development and vigor by interacting with target genes [32]. For instance, miR159, targeting MYB33, MYB101 and two abscisic acid (ABA)-positive regulators were turned to play a vital role in seed germination [33]. miR164c targets PSK5 (Phytosulfokines 5) and PM27 (Os11g44940), to negatively regulate seed vigor, PSK5 was annotated as involved in promotes plant cell differentiation, organogenesis and somatic embryogenesis as well as cell proliferation. PSK5 may interact with RPS27AA, a ubiquitin-related hub gene, to regulate seed vigor of rice [34].

In present study, we screened four seed vigor-related novel miRNAs, basically, they all interact with targets regulating seed vigor. Among these, miR-212568-5p targets TraesCS3D02G013000 and TraesCS5B02G204400, which was annotated as SPS4 (Sucrose-phosphate synthase 4) and PUB33 (U-box domain-containing protein 33), respectively. It has been reported that SPS is a key rate-limiting enzyme that regulated sucrose synthesism, and it played a role in the early stage of seed development. Specifically, It synthesized sucrose from hexose transported into storage tissue through SPS pathway. During the transition from the cell division stage to the storage stage, the highly active SPS promoted the synthesis of sucrose and decreased the ratio of reducing sugar to sucrose, thus promoting the storage process [35]. Additionally, SPS with multiple families have different functions, such as, in Cucumber, CsSPS4 functioned in accelerating sucrose synthesis and export, promoting plant development [36]. In spinach and potato, low temperature can increase the expression of SPS protein and lead to the increase of SPS activity-Vmax [37]. In rice, the SPS8 mutant line can reduce the percentage of ripened grain and the yield decreased by 15–17%, compared with the wild type [38]. In our study, SPS4 was negatively regulated by miR-212568-5p, except in hybrid (Fig. 8B), suggesting it may be involved in seed vigor regulation.

PUB (plant U-box protein) is a U-box ubiquitin ligase (E3), which was reported to regulate hormone signal transduction and resistant to abiotic stress in plants [39]. In Arabidopsis, AtPUB9 can negatively regulate ABA signal and promote seed germination by promoting ABI3 degradation [40]. PUB43 and PUB44 positively regulated ABA signaling and inhibits seed germination [41]. Additionally, PUB30 degraded BKI1 through ubiquitination and promoted seed germination under salt stress [42]. In this study, PUB33 was up-regulated in hybrid and parents, and miR-212568-5p was negatively correlated with it (Fig. 8C), suggesting it may involved in seed vigor regulation.

miR-212117-5p targets TraesCS1D02G099900, which was annotated as TIR1 (Transport inhibitor response 1-like protein). It has been reported that TIR1/AFB, a SCF ubiquitin ligase, which could bind to auxin and promote the degradation of AXR2 and AXR3, release the activity of ARF10 and ARF16, which could maintain the expression of ABI3, thus promoting seed dormancy in Arabidopsis [43]. In Arabidopsis, miR393 inhibited TIR1 expression and negatively regulated seed germination and seedling growth sensitivity to ABA [44]. In Peas, PsTIR1a, PsTIR1b and PsAFB2 were involved in auxin related responses during fruit/seed development [45]. In our study, TIR1 was negatively regulated by miR-212117-5p (Fig. 8G), suggesting it may be involved in seed vigor regulation.

miR-19960-3p targets TraesCS7A02G006900, which was annotated as LOC123157489 (uncharacterized protein), it possessed cysteine peptidyase activity (Table S5). Reserches have shown that cysteine protease degraded seed storage protein and provided nitrogen source for seed germination [46]. The normal activation of cysteine protease was the key to ensure the normal germination of seeds, and the normal growth and development of seedlings [47]. In Populus trichocarpa, PtCP5 was an important factor to mobilize storage proteins, and the correct expression of PtCP5 was necessary for the maturation and germination of pollen and seeds [46]. Additionally, cysteine protease dominated in the germinating endosperm of triticale seeds [48], such as, EP8 was expressed in both developing and germinating triticale seeds [49].The target was down-regulated in parents, while up-regulated in hybrid and it was negatively regulated by miR-19960-3p (Fig. 8H), suggesting it may be involved in seed vigor regulation.

miR-170814-5p targets TraesCS5A02G164200, which was annotated as GELP (GDSL esterase/lipase At2g04570-like), possessed hydrolase activity [50]. Studies have shown that esterase/lipase also played a crucial role in seed germination of oil plants [51]. Such as, in Arabidopsis, the regulation of GDSL lipase expression by GA signal pathway can influence the lipid metabolism of seed oil [52]. In Brassica napus, the over-expression of BnGDSL1 and AtGDSL1 accelerated the degradation of fatty acids, thereby increasing the germination rate of seeds [53]. In this study, GELP was negatively regulated by miR-170814-5p, except in hybrid (Fig. 8I), suggesting it may involved in seed vigor regulation.

To sum up, the targets TIR1 and PUB33 regulate plant hormones (IAA, ABA) through ubiquitination pathway to control seed germination. Plant hormones especially ABA and GA play an important role in seed germination [54]. SPS, GELP, and LOC123157489 are enzymes related to energy storage and supply, SPS promotes the storage process, and GELP, LOC123157489 promote the breakdown of stored substances to support the growth of early seedlings [35]. Therefore, we consider that the four novel miRNAs are related to seed vigor and a hypothesized pathway network of target gene regulated by seed vigor related miRNAs was constructed (Fig. 9).

Hybrid seed vigor may be inherited from the male parent and regulated by miR531

Seed vigor is mainly affected by their own genetic material and environmental factors [55]. Genetic material plays a major role in seed vigor, and it’s passed on to the next generation [56, 57]. In the present study, miR531 was differentially expressed in both hybrid and male parent, while none of miRNA was differentially expressed in both hybrid and female parent. Previous researches have shown that miR531 was specifically expressed in wheat seeds [58], tae-MIR531-P5-2ss11CG20AG targets gibberellin 2-β-dioxygenase (GA2OX1), which may participate in the catabolic metabolism of gibberellin, thereby indirectly regulating the endogenous gibberellin content during seed germination [59]. Additionally, miR531 was involved in signal transduction pathways in rice [60], and it played important roles in drought response and innate immunity in rice, biomass improvement in sugarcane [61], and metabolism in switchgrass [62]. In our study, target gene was negatively regulated by miR531 (Fig. 8J), and it was involved in metabolic pathways and possessed redox enzyme activity. Specifically, the branched-chain alpha-keto dehydrogenase complex, to which 2-oxoisovalerate dehydrogenase subunit beta1 belongs, catalyzes the overall conversion of alpha-keto acids to acyl-CoA and CO2, and this complex contains multiple copies of three enzymatic components: branched-chain alpha-keto acid decarboxylase, lipoamide acyltransferase and lipoamide dehydrogenase [25]. Additionally, the 2-oxoisovalerate dehydrogenase subunit beta1 is correlated with pyruvate dehydrogenase complex (PDHc). As we all know, redox enzyme is an important class of enzymes that work by resisting damage caused by peroxides. SOD dismutes superoxide to H2O2, and inhibits hydroxyl radical production. CAT helps in quenching H2O2 which ultimately protect seeds from its toxicity [63], Spermine (Spm) and Spermidine (Spd) can eliminate excess total hydrogen peroxide by maintaining high antioxidant mechanism, thus improving germination and seedling vitality of tomato varieties [64]. Similarly, the PDHc is one of the most important oxidoreductases in organism. This complex catalyzes the oxidative decarboxylation of pyruvate to acetyl CoA, and connects the aerobic oxidation of sugars with the tricarboxylic acid cycle and oxidative phosphorylation, and plays an important role in the energy metabolism of mitochondrial respiratory chain [65]. Studies have shown that the energy produced during seed eruption was mainly from tricarboxylic acid cycle, which was very important for seed eruption and seedling growth [66]. Therefore, miR531 may interact with TraesCS6A02G036000 to regulate seed vigor. Additionally, we speculate the hybrid seed vigor might be inherited from the male parent, in terms of miRNA.

Conclusions

In conclusion, it is clear that the seed vigor of two-line hybrid wheat surpasses that of its parents and exhibits heterosis. At the same time, a known miRNA associated with seed vigor, miR531, was screened by high-throughput sequencing technology. The function and regulatory network were preliminarily analyzed by protein interaction prediction. We suggest that hybrid seed vigor may be inherited from the male parent and regulated by the interaction of miR531 with the target gene TraesCS6A02G036000. Additionally, four novel miRNAs interact with target genes to regulate seed germination mainly by regulating hormone-ubiquitination pathway and metabolic pathway. Although the mechanism of genes interaction with miRNAs remains to be further clarified, these results provide a new perspective for studying the regulatory mechanism of miRNA-mediated hybrid wheat seed vigor.

Methods

Plant materials, artificial aging treatments

Two-line hybrid wheat (BS1453/11GF5135, hybrid) and its parents (BS1453, female parent), (11GF5135, male parent) were used in this study. All materials were provided by the Institute of hybrid wheat Beijing Academy of Agriculture and Forestry Sciences. Seeds were surface-sterilized by 1% sodium hypochlorite. Healthy hybrid and parents seeds of the same size and at the same maturity level were treated with high temperature (41℃) and RH (100%) for zero, three, five, seven, nine days, respectively [67], for follow-up tests.

Seed germination and enzyme activity assay

Seed germination was tested by rolling paper. The seeds were grown in a 20℃ constant temperature and light incubator. Enzyme activity was measured using kit (Beijing Solarbio Science & Technology Co., Ltd.). Seed germination tests were performed in triplicate. Germination data was calculated according to Zhang et al. (2013) [28].

Construction of small RNA library and high throughput sequencing

miRNA high-throughput sequencing service is provided by Beijing Boyun Huakang Gene Technology Co., LTD. In this experiment, embryos of BS1453, 11GF5135, BS1453/11GF5135 artificially aged for nine days and unaged seeds (CK) were selected to construct a library with three biological replicates per sample.

Prediction and analysis of miRNAs

Raw image data files obtained by high-throughput sequencing was analyzed by CASAVA Base Calling and converted into Raw Data of sequencing sequence. The clean tag, Known miRNAs identification were referred to the method of Bai et al. (2017) [68]. The DEGseq was used to detect the difference of miRNAs and screening | log2 (FoldChange) | > 1 & p value < 0.05 as differentially expressed miRNAs [69]. The psRobot and TargetFinder were used for target gene prediction, and then the intersection of target gene prediction results obtained by the two methods was taken as the result of miRNA target gene prediction. Gene ontology and KEGG pathway analysis of predicted targets were analyzed referred to the method of Bai et al. (2017) [68].

Expression analyses of targets and miRNAs

Total RNA extraction, reverse transcription of gene RNA, and miRNA, qRT-RCR assays for targets and miRNAs were referred to the method of Luo et al. (2022) [70]. Wheat 18 S gene and U6 were used as reference genes for targets and miRNA analysis. The data were calculated according to the method of Luo et al. (2022) [70]. The primers used in this study were designed by Primer premier 5.0 (Primer, CAN, UK) and were listed in Table S6.
