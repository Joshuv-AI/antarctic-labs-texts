21 C.F.R. § 178.3600 — Methyl glucoside-coconut oil ester.
Citation: us:cfr:21:178.3600
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-178.3600
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 178 (Indirect Food Additives: Adjuvants, Production Aids, And Sanitizers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Methyl glucoside-coconut oil ester identified in § 172.816(a) of this chapter may be safely used as a processing aid (filter aid) in the manufacture of starch, including industrial starch-modified complying with § 178.3520, intended for use as a component of articles that contact food.
