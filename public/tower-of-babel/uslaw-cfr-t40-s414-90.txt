40 C.F.R. § 414.90 — Applicability; description of the subcategory of direct discharge point sources that use end-of-pipe biological treatment.
Citation: us:cfr:40:414.90
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-414.90
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 414 (Organic Chemicals, Plastics, And Synthetic Fibers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to the process wastewater discharges resulting from the manufacture of the OCPSF products and product groups defined by § 414.11 from any point source that uses end-of-pipe biological treatment or installs end-of-pipe biological treatment to comply with BPT effluent limitations.
