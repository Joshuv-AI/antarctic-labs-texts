14 C.F.R. § 31.82 — Instructions for Continued Airworthiness.
Citation: us:cfr:14:31.82
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-31.82
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 31 (Airworthiness Standards: Manned Free Balloons). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The applicant must prepare Instructions for Continued Airworthiness in accordance with appendix A to this part that are acceptable to the Administrator. The instructions may be incomplete at type certification if a program exists to ensure their completion prior to delivery of the first balloon or issuance of a standard certificate of airworthiness, whichever occurs later.
