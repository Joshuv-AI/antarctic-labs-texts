Comparison of Hyodo-Kato and de Rham Fargues-Fontaine Cohomology Theories
Kaixing Cao

Abstract
We prove that, for adic étale motives over ℂ p \mathbb{C}_{p} , the vector bundles on the Fargues-Fontaine curve arising from their Hyodo-Kato cohomology coincide with their de Rham-Fargues-Fontaine cohomologies, where the latter provides an overconvergent refinement of crystalline vector bundles, albeit constructed on the generic fiber. This equivalence is established in the setting of symmetric monoidal ∞ \infty -categories and respects the full motivic structure. Furthermore, we enrich both realizations with Galois actions, yielding G ℚ ˘ p G_{\breve{\mathbb{Q}}_{p}} -equivariant solid quasi-coherent sheaves on the Fargues-Fontaine curve; in this equivariant context, the comparison isomorphism becomes canonical. As an application, we show that the de Rham-Fargues-Fontaine cohomology of any smooth quasi-compact rigid analytic variety over ℂ p \mathbb{C}_{p} admits a finite slope-increasing filtration.

1 Introduction

Let
K
be a complete discrete valued field of characteristic zero with perfect residue field
k
of characteristic
p>0
. We fix an algebraic closure
\bar{K}
of
K
and let
C=\hat{\bar{K}}
denote its completion. Then the residue field of
C
, denoted by
\bar{k}
, is an algebraic closure of
k
.

1.1
Background and Motivation

Bhatt, Morrow and Scholze studied various
p
-adic cohomology theories in
[
BMS18
]
. We begin by recalling the geometrization of certain results in
[
BMS18
]
from the perspective of the
Fargues-Fontaine curve

\mathbf{FF}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\mathbf{FF}_{C^{\flat},\mathbb{Q}_{p}}
, where
C^{\flat}
is the tilt of
C
. Let
\mathfrak{X}
be a proper smooth formal scheme over
\mathcal{O}_{C}
. We consider the following cohomology theories:

•

the
p
-adic étale cohomology
\mathrm{R}\boldsymbol{\Gamma}_{\text{\'{e}t}}(\mathfrak{X}_{C},\mathbb{Z}_{p})
defined on the rigid generic fiber
\mathfrak{X}_{C}
;

•

the crystalline cohomology
\mathrm{R}\boldsymbol{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{crys}}(\mathfrak{X}_{\bar{k}}/W(\bar{k}))
defined on the special fiber
\mathfrak{X}_{\bar{k}}
;

•

a new cohomology theory
\mathrm{R}\boldsymbol{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{crys}}(\mathfrak{X}_{C}/B_{\mathrm{dR}}^{+})
defined on the rigid generic fiber.

Some of the comparison results in
[
BMS18
]
yield a modification
\mathcal{E}_{\text{\'{e}t}}\to\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{crys}}
of vector bundles on the Fargues-Fontaine curve, where

\mathcal{E}_{\text{\'{e}t}}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=H^{i}_{\text{\'{e}t}}(\mathfrak{X}_{C},\mathbb{Z}_{p})\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}_{p}}\mathcal{O}_{\mathbf{FF}},\quad\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{crys}}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\mathcal{E}(H^{i}_{\mathrm{crys}}(\mathfrak{X}_{\bar{k}}/W(\bar{k})[1/p]),\varphi)

and the functor
\mathcal{E}
is Fargues-Fontaine’s functor from
\varphi
-modules over
\bar{k}
to vector bundles on
\mathbf{FF}
. More precisely, the crystalline vector bundle
\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{crys}}
is obtained from the étale vector bundle
\mathcal{E}_{\text{\'{e}t}}
by modifying it at the distinguished point
x_{C}
determined by Fontaine’s map
\theta\colon A_{\mathrm{inf}}\to\mathcal{O}_{C}
. This modification at
x_{C}
is prescribed by the crystalline
B_{\mathrm{dR}}^{+}
-cohomology
H^{i}_{\mathrm{crys}}(\mathfrak{X}_{C}/B_{\mathrm{dR}}^{+})
. In other words, the crystalline cohomology can be reconstructed from two cohomology data on the rigid generic fiber
\mathfrak{X}_{C}
: the
p
-adic étale cohomology and the crystalline
B_{\mathrm{dR}}^{+}
-cohomology.

Together with the work of Colmez and Nizioł
[
CN17
,
Niz19
]
, these results motivate the following conjecture, which seeks a direct cohomological construction for
\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{crys}}
on the rigid generic fiber:

Conjecture

(
[
Far19
, Conjecture 1.13]
,
[
Sch19
, Conjecture 6.4]
)
.

Let
\mathbf{RigDA}(C)
be the
\infty
-category of adic étale motives over
C
. There exists a motivic realization functor defined on compact rigid motives

\mathrm{R}\boldsymbol{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{FF}}\colon\mathbf{RigDA}(C)_{\omega}\to\mathbf{Perf}(\mathbf{FF})

sending each smooth quasi-compact rigid analytic variety
X
over
C
to perfect complexes
\mathrm{R}\boldsymbol{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{FF}}(X)
on the Fargues-Fontaine; in particular, each cohomology
\mathcal{H}^{i}_{\mathbf{FF}}(X)
is a vector bundle on the Fargues-Fontaine curve. It is expected to satisfy the following properties:

(1)

the pullback
x_{C}^{*}\mathrm{R}\boldsymbol{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{\mathbf{FF}}}(X)
to the distinguished point is isomorphic to the overconvergent de Rham cohomology
\mathrm{R}\boldsymbol{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{dR}}^{\dagger}(X)
;

(2)

the complete stalk of
\mathrm{R}\boldsymbol{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{FF}}(X)
at
x_{C}
recovers the overconvergent
B_{\mathrm{dR}}^{+}
-cohomology;

(3)

in the case of semistable reduction, it agrees with the vector bundle associated to log-crystalline cohomology on the special fiber.

From now on, we refer to any such cohomology theory as the
Fargues-Fontaine cohomology
.

Remark 1.1
.

(1)

Such a cohomology theory can in fact be extended to the entire category of rigid motives via Clausen and Scholze’s condensed mathematics. This yields a motivic realization functor on the whole category of motives

\mathrm{R}\boldsymbol{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{FF}}\colon\mathbf{RigDA}(C)\to\mathbf{QCoh}(\mathbf{FF})

valued in solid quasi-coherent sheaves on the Fargues-Fontaine curve, in the sense of
[
And21
]
.

(2)

All cohomology theories appearing in the conjecture are overconvergent since the category
\mathbf{RigDA}(C)
serves as a model for mixed motives that allows one to treat non-proper spaces.

1.2
Hyodo-Kato Cohomology on the Rigid Generic Fiber

The part
(3)
of the conjecture asks for a comparison with log-crystalline cohomology, which is defined on the special fiber. However, Colmez and Nizioł, adapting a construction of Beilinson for algebraic varieties in
[
Bei13
]
, introduced a Hyodo-Kato cohomology theory for rigid analytic spaces in
[
CN20
]
. When
X
is a rigid analytic space with semistable model
\mathfrak{X}
over
\mathcal{O}_{C}
, their local-global compatibility result shows that the Hyodo-Kato cohomology
\mathrm{R}\boldsymbol{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{HK}}(X)
is isomorphic, as a
(\varphi,N)
-module, to the log-crystalline cohomology
\mathrm{R}\boldsymbol{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{crys}}(\mathfrak{X}^{0}_{\mathcal{O}_{C}/p}/\mathcal{O}_{\breve{K}}^{0})[1/p]
. This leads to a refinement of part
(3)
of the conjecture:

(3’)

the Fargues-Fontaine cohomology agrees with the overconvergent Hyodo-Kato cohomology.

Since the Fargues-Fontaine cohomology is expected to be motivic, it is natural to formulate a comparison with the motivic (overconvergent) Hyodo-Kato cohomology: fixing a pseudo-uniformizer
\varpi\in\mathcal{O}_{C}
, we have the
\boldsymbol{\varpi}
-Hyodo-Kato realization functor

\mathrm{R}\boldsymbol{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{HK}}^{\varpi}\colon\mathbf{RigDA}(C)\to\mathcal{D}_{(\varphi,N)}(\breve{K}),

(1.1)

where
\breve{K}=\frc W(\bar{k})
. The canonical choice is
\varpi=p
, in which case we simply refer to it as the
Hyodo-Kato realization
, denoted by
\mathrm{R}\boldsymbol{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{HK}}
.

Remark 1.2
.

As observed in
[
BGV25
, Remark 4.18]
and
[
BKV25
, Remark 3.5]
, different choices of pseudo-uniformizers affect only the behaviors of the monodromy operator on the Hyodo-Kato cohomology.

1.3
The De Rham-Fargues-Fontaine Cohomology

The main goal of this paper is to show that the de Rham-Fargues-Fontaine cohomology

\mathrm{R}\boldsymbol{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{FF}}\colon\mathbf{RigDA}(C)\simeq\mathbf{RigDA}(C^{\flat})\to\mathbf{QCoh}(\mathbf{FF})

constructed by Le Bras and Vezzani in
[
LBV23
]
, qualifies as a Fargues-Fontaine cohomology; that is, it satisfies all properties stated in the conjecture above. Here the first equivalence is the motivic tilting equivalence established in
[
Vez19a
]
.

To this end, since Le Bras and Vezzani have already shown that this cohomology theory satisfies parts
(1)
and
(2)
of the conjecture, it remains to prove the comparison with the Hyodo–Kato realization (
1.1
), namely part
(3’)
:

Theorem 1.3

(Theorem
3.7
, Remark
3.8
)
.

We have a monoidal equivalence
\mathrm{R}\boldsymbol{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{FF}}\simeq\mathcal{E}_{N}\circ\mathrm{R}\boldsymbol{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{HK}}^{\varpi}\colon\mathbf{RigDA}(C)\to\mathbf{QCoh}(\mathbf{FF})
in
\mathbf{CAlg}(\mathbf{\boldsymbol{\mathcal{P}}r}^{\mathrm{st}}_{\omega})
. Here
\mathcal{E}_{N}\colon\mathcal{D}_{(\varphi,N)}(\breve{K})\to\mathbf{QCoh}(\mathbf{FF})
extends the Fargues-Fontaine’s functor from
(\varphi,N)
-modules over
\breve{K}
to vector bundles over the Fargues-Fontaine curve.

The proof of Theorem
1.3
involves several crucial aspects. First, one has the key identifications (
[
AGV22
, Theorem 3.7.21, Theorem 3.3.3]
)

\mathbf{RigDA}(C)\simeq\mathbf{RigDA}_{\mathrm{gr}}(C)\simeq\mathbf{Mod}_{\chi{\mathbbm{1}}}(\mathbf{DA}(\bar{k}))

where
\chi{\mathbbm{1}}\simeq{\mathbbm{1}}\oplus{\mathbbm{1}}(-1)[-1]
is the cohomological motive of
\mathbb{G}_{m}
, arising from the fixed pseudo-uniformizer
\varpi\in\mathcal{O}_{C}
. Under this identification, the realization functors become functors from
\mathbf{Mod}_{\chi{\mathbbm{1}}}(\mathbf{DA}(\bar{k}))
to
\mathbf{QCoh}(\mathbf{FF})
. However, the mapping space

\mathrm{Map}(\mathbf{Mod}_{\chi{\mathbbm{1}}}(\mathbf{DA}(\bar{k})),\mathbf{QCoh}(\mathbf{FF}))

is not connected. Fortunately, both realization functors can be compared to the rigid realization functor (Proposition
3.2
and Proposition
3.4
). More precisely, let

\xi\colon\mathbf{DA}(\bar{k})\to\mathbf{RigDA}(C)

be the
Monsky-Washinitzer functor
defined in
[
Vez18
]
(see also (
2.1
)). We have the following comparisons with rigid cohomology

\mathcal{E}_{N}\circ\mathrm{R}\boldsymbol{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{HK}}\circ\xi\simeq\mathcal{E}\circ\mathrm{R}\boldsymbol{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{rig}}\simeq\mathrm{R}\boldsymbol{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{FF}}\circ\xi

in
\mathbf{CAlg}(\mathbf{\boldsymbol{\mathcal{P}}r}^{\mathrm{st}}_{\omega})
. Therefore, these realization functors not only lie in
\mathbf{CAlg}(\mathbf{\boldsymbol{\mathcal{P}}r}^{\mathrm{st}}_{\omega})
, but also in the category
\mathbf{CAlg}(\mathbf{\boldsymbol{\mathcal{P}}r}^{\mathrm{st}}_{\omega})_{\mathbf{DA}(\bar{k})/-}
through their comparisons with the rigid cohomology. The refined mapping space

\mathrm{Map}_{\mathbf{CAlg}(\mathbf{\boldsymbol{\mathcal{P}}r}^{\mathrm{st}}_{\omega})_{\mathbf{DA}(\bar{k})/-}}\left(\mathbf{RigDA}(C),\mathbf{QCoh}(\mathbf{FF})\right),

particularly its set of connected components, can then be analyzed in terms of extension groups of vector bundles over the Fargues-Fontaine curve; see Proposition
3.6
.

Galois Enrichment for Motives and the Uniqueness of Comparisons

Although we have a monoidal equivalence
\mathrm{R}\boldsymbol{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{FF}}\simeq\mathcal{E}_{N}\circ\mathrm{R}\boldsymbol{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{HK}}
in
\mathbf{CAlg}(\mathbf{\boldsymbol{\mathcal{P}}r}^{\mathrm{st}}_{\omega})
obtained via a computation of the relevant mapping space, the equivalence is not canonical. Indeed, the space of such monoidal equivalences is large; see Remark
3.9
. As a result, there is no canonical comparison between these two realization functors.

To obtain the canonical comparison, we must incorporate the Galois action. More precisely, let
G_{\breve{K}}
be the absolute Galois group of
\breve{K}
. Then both realization functors above can be upgraded to take values in the
\infty
-category of
G_{\breve{K}}
-equivariant solid quasi-coherent sheaves on
\mathbf{FF}
. That Hyodo-Kato cohomology naturally yields
G_{\breve{K}}
-equivariant vector bundles on the Fargues-Fontaine is already known from
[
FF18
]
.

We now explain how to equip the de Rham-Fargues-Fontaine cohomology with a
G_{\breve{K}}
-action. This crucial point is to define the
G_{\breve{K}}
-enrichment of adic étale motives over
C
. Let
M
be an adic étale motive over
C
. Then it admits a model
\bar{M}
over
\breve{K}
; that is, there exists an adic étale motive
\bar{M}
over
\breve{K}
such that
M\simeq\bar{M}_{C}
. This fact follows from
[
AGV22
, Theorem 3.7.21]
and
[
BKV25
, Proposition 3.23]
. Thus, the identification
M\simeq\bar{M}_{C}
equips
M
with a natural
G_{\breve{K}}
-action, and hence its de Rham Fargues-Fontaine cohomology inherits a
G_{\breve{K}}
-action. This gives rise to the Galois-refined realization functor

\mathrm{R}\boldsymbol{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{FF}}^{\mathrm{ari}}\colon\mathbf{RigDA}(C)\to\mathbf{QCoh}(\mathbf{FF})^{\htcat G_{\breve{K}}}

of the de Rham-Fargues-Fontaine realization. Here
\mathbf{QCoh}(\mathbf{FF})^{\htcat G_{\breve{K}}}
is the
\infty
-category of
G_{\breve{K}}
-equivariant solid quasi-coherent sheaves on
\mathbf{FF}
; see (
3.5
).

With these enhancements, we obtain a unique comparison equivalence:

Theorem 1.4

(Theorem
3.16
)
.

There is a unique monoidal comparison equivalence
\mathrm{R}\boldsymbol{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{FF}}^{\mathrm{ari}}\simeq\mathcal{E}_{N}^{\mathrm{ari}}\circ\mathrm{R}\boldsymbol{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{HK}}
in
\mathbf{CAlg}(\mathbf{\boldsymbol{\mathcal{P}}r}^{\mathrm{st}}_{\omega})
, which is compatible with the monoidal comparison equivalences relating each of these two realization functors to the realization functor of the rigid cohomology on the special fiber.

A New Filtration for Vector Bundles

As a consequence of these comparison results, we deduce that the de Rham-Fargues-Fontaine realization factors through the weight complex functor (see
[
Sos19
,
Aok20
,
BGV25
]
) on the full subcategory
\mathbf{RigDA}(C)_{\omega}
of compact motives. In particular, this yields a convergent spectral sequence computing the de Rham-Fargues-Fontaine cohomology of compact motives over
C
:

Corollary 1.5

(Corollary
3.11
, Corollary
3.12
)
.

Let
M
be a compact motive (e.g, associated motives of smooth quasi-compact rigid analytic varieties) over
C
. Define

\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{FF}}^{i}(M)\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=H_{-i}\mathrm{R}\boldsymbol{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{\mathrm{FF}}}(M^{\vee}).

Then there is a convergent (cohomological) spectral sequence starting from the first page and degenerates at the second page:

E_{1}^{pq}=\mathcal{H}^{q}_{\mathrm{FF}}(W^{p}M)\Rightarrow\mathcal{H}^{p+q}_{\mathrm{FF}}(M)

where
W^{\bullet}M
is the weight cochain complex of
M
. Moreover, the
i
-th graded piece of the induced finite (increasing) filtration on
\mathcal{H}^{n}_{\mathrm{FF}}(M)
has a slope of
(i+n)/2
and carries a natural
G_{\breve{K}}
-action. In particular, this is a new filtration on the vector bundle
\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{FF}}^{n}(M)
different from the Harder-Narasimhan filtration.

1.4
The Fargues-Fontaine Cohomology via the Décalage Functor

Another version of Fargues-Fontaine cohomology, denoted by

[
LB18
,
Bos23a
]
. Le Bras showed in

To compare this version with the de Rham-Fargues-Fontaine cohomology in a motivic way, we first prove that this Fargues-Fontaine cohomology

, defined by Le Bras and Bosco, is also motivic:

Lemma 1.6

(Proposition
3.19
)
.

The functor sending each smooth dagger space
X
over
C
to a solid quasi-coherent sheaf
\widetilde{\mathrm{R}\Gamma}_{\mathrm{FF}}(X)
, defined
[
Bos23a
, Definition 6.16]
, extends to a functor

\widetilde{\mathrm{R}\boldsymbol{\Gamma}}_{\mathrm{FF}}\colon\mathbf{RigDA}(C)\simeq\mathbf{RigDA}^{\dagger}(C)\to\mathbf{QCoh}(\mathbf{FF})

in
\mathbf{CAlg}(\mathbf{\boldsymbol{\mathcal{P}}r}^{\mathrm{st}}_{\omega})
, where the first equivalence is given in
[
Vez18
, Theorem 4.23]
.

This motivic extension enables a comparison between the two versions of Fargues–Fontaine cohomology via their respective relations to Hyodo–Kato cohomology:

Proposition 1.7

(Proposition
3.20
)
.

There is a (non-canonical) monoidal equivalence
\mathrm{R}\boldsymbol{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{FF}}\simeq\widetilde{\mathrm{R}\boldsymbol{\Gamma}}_{\mathrm{FF}}
in
\mathbf{CAlg}(\mathbf{\boldsymbol{\mathcal{P}}r}^{\mathrm{st}}_{\omega})
.

2.1
Adic Étale Motives

Let

2.2
Rigid Motives on the Fargues-Fontaine Curve

We now recall the spreading-out result for motives on the Fargues-Fontaine curve from
[
