2 C.F.R. § 5800.332 — What methods must I use to pass requirements down to participants at lower tiers with whom I intend to do business?
Citation: us:cfr:2:5800.332
Creator: Election Assistance Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-5800.332
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 5800 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a lower-tier transaction is covered pursuant to § 5800.220, you as a participant must include a term or condition in lower-tier transactions requiring lower-tier participants to comply with Subpart C of the OMB guidance in 2 CFR part 180.
