45 C.F.R. § 508.8 — Application of other regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:45:508.8
Creator: Foreign Claims Settlement Commission Of The United States, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-508.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 508 (Hearings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To the extent they are not inconsistent with the regulations set forth under provisions of this subchapter, the other regulations of the Commission will also be applicable to the claims filed hereunder.
