7 C.F.R. § 3402.22 — Access to peer review information.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3402.22
Creator: National Institute Of Food And Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3402.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3402 (Food And Agricultural Sciences National Needs Graduate And Postgraduate Fellowship Grants Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After final decisions have been announced, HEP will, upon request, inform the PD of the reasons for its decision on an application. Verbatim copies of summary reviews, not including the identity of the reviewers, will be made available to respective PDs upon specific request.
