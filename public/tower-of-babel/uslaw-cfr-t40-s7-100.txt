40 C.F.R. § 7.100 — Intimidation and retaliation prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:40:7.100
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-7.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 7 (Nondiscrimination In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Assistance From The Environmental Protection Agency). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No applicant, recipient, nor other person shall intimidate, threaten, coerce, or discriminate against any individual or group, either:
(a) For the purpose of interfering with any right or privilege guaranteed by the Acts or this part, or
(b) Because the individual has filed a complaint or has testified, assisted or participated in any way in an investigation, proceeding or hearing under this part, or has opposed any practice made unlawful by this regulation.
