40 C.F.R. § 1400.7 — In general.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1400.7
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency And Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1400.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1400 (Distribution Of Off-Site Consequence Analysis Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator shall provide OCA information to government officials as provided in this subpart. Any OCA information provided to government officials shall be accompanied by a copy of the notice prescribed by 42 U.S.C. 7412(r)(7)(H)(vi).
