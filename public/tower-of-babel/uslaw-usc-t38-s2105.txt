38 U.S.C. § 2105 — Nonliability of United States
Citation: us:usc:38:2105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section2105
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 21 (Specially Adapted Housing For Disabled Veterans). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Government of the United States shall have no liability in connection with any housing unit, or necessary land therefor, or adaptation acquired under the provisions of this chapter.
