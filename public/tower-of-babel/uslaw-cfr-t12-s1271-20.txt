12 C.F.R. § 1271.20 — Access to confidential regulatory information.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1271.20
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1271.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1271 (Miscellaneous Federal Home Loan Bank Operations And Authorities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each Bank shall ensure that access to the confidential regulatory information stored at its facility is limited to those with a need to know such information and that employees with access maintain the confidentiality of the confidential regulatory information in accordance with the Bank's own procedures for maintaining the confidentiality of its members' privileged or non-public information.
