48 C.F.R. § 25.404 — 25.404 Least developed countries.
Citation: us:cfr:48:25.404
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-25.404
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 25 (Foreign Acquisition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For acquisitions covered by the WTO GPA, least developed country end products, construction material, and services must be treated as eligible products.
