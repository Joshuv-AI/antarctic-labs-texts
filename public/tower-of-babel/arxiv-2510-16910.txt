Spectrum of weighted composition operators. Part XII. Kamowitz - Scheinberg theorem revisited
Arkady Kitover, Mehmet Orhon

Abstract
. The well-known Kamowitz - Scheinberg theorem states that if U U is an automorphism of a commutative semi-simple Banach algebra and U n ≠ I , n ∈ ℕ U^{n}\neq I,n\in\mathds{N} , then the spectrum of U U contains the unit circle. In this paper we present some results about the spectrum of weighted automorphisms of unital commutative semi-simple Banach algebras that considerably strengthen the statement of the Kamowitz - Scheinberg theorem.

Theorem 2.1
.

Let
A
be a unital semi-simple commutative Banach algebra,
U
be an automorphism of
A
and
w\in A^{-1}
. Let

T=wU
and let
\check{T}
be the associated weighted composition operator on
\check{A}
. Assume that

\;U^{n}\neq I,n\in\mathds{N},

(1)

\;\sum\limits_{n=-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{|\ln{\|(\check{T})^{n}\|}|}{1+n^{2}}<\infty.

(2)

Then
\sigma(T)\supseteq\mathds{T}
.

Proof.

Assume to the contrary that there is an
\alpha\in\mathds{T}
such that
\alpha\not\in\sigma(T)
. Let

be an open disc centered at

\sigma(T)\cap U(\alpha,r)=\emptyset

Condition (1) implies (see

((\lambda I-T)^{-1}f\check{)}=(\lambda I-\check{T})^{-1}\check{f},\;f\in A,\;\lambda\in D(\alpha,r)\setminus\mathds{T}.

(3)

Notice first that for any
g\in A
and

(\lambda I-\check{T})\check{g}(t)=((\lambda I-T)g\check{)}(t).

(4)

Let
t\in\partial A
. Then
((\lambda I-\check{T})(\lambda I-\check{T})^{-1}\check{f})(t)=\check{f}(t)

and in virtue of (
4

(\lambda I-\check{T})((\lambda I-T)^{-1}f\check{)}(t)=((\lambda I-T)(\lambda I-T)^{-1}f\check{)}(t)=\check{f}(t)
. Recall that the operator
\lambda I-\check{T}
is invertible, and therefore (
3
) is proved.

Let
\check{G}
be a continuous functional on
\check{A}
. The formula
G(f)=\check{G}(\check{f})
defines a continuous functional

on
A
. Therefore, in virtue of (

f\in A
and any
\check{G}\in(\check{A})^{\prime}
the function

is analytic in

U(\alpha,r)

Let

and let

\|\check{f_{n}}-g\|_{\check{A}}\rightarrow 0
. It follows from condition (

It follows (

\sigma(S)\supseteq\mathds{T}

Proof.

The factor operator

Theorem 2.4
.

Let
A
be a unital semi-simple commutative Banach algebra,
U
be an automorphism of
A
and
w\in A
. Let

T=wU
and let
\check{T}
be the associated weighted composition operator on
\check{A}
. Assume that

U^{n}\neq I,n\in\mathds{N}

\sigma(\check{T})\subseteq\mathds{T}

contains no
\varphi
-periodic points and that every point of
\partial A

\check{A}

.

Proof.

Consider the operator
S
on
\check{A}
defined by the formula

(Sg)(t)=\check{w}(t)g(\varphi^{-1}(t))
. It is immediate to see that

\|S^{n}\|=\|\check{T}^{n}\|,n\in\mathds{N}
, and therefore
\sigma(S)\subseteq\mathds{T}
. It follows from Lemma 3.6 in
[
7
]
that there is a point
s\in\partial A
such that

|\check{w}_{n}(s)|\leq 1\;\text{and}\;|\check{w}_{n}(\varphi^{-n}(s))|\geq 1,n\in\mathds{N},

(5)

where
\varphi^{n},n\in\mathds{N}
is the
n^{th}
iteration of
\varphi
and

\check{w}_{n}=\check{w}(\check{w}\circ\varphi)\ldots(\check{w}\circ\varphi^{n-1})
.

Let us assume like in the proof of Theorem
1.1
that there exist an
\alpha\in\mathds{T}
and an
r>0
such that
U(\alpha,r)\cap\sigma(T)=\emptyset
. It follows from the formula

R(\lambda,\check{T})=(\lambda I-\check{T})^{-1}=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\sum\limits_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(\check{T})^{n}}{\lambda^{n+1}},&\hbox{if $|\lambda|>1$;}\\
\sum\limits_{n=0}^{\infty}\lambda^{n}(\check{T})^{-(n+1)},&\hbox{if $|\lambda|<1$,}\end{array}\right.

(6)

and from the equality (
3
) that for any
f\in A
the function

R(\lambda,\check{T})\check{f}(s)=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\sum\limits_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(\check{w}_{n}(s)\check{f}(\varphi^{n}(s))}{\lambda^{n+1}},&\hbox{if $|\lambda|>1$;}\\
\sum\limits_{n=0}^{\infty}\lambda^{n}\frac{\check{f}(\varphi^{-(n+1)}(s))}{\check{w}_{n+1}(\varphi^{-(n+1)}(s))},&\hbox{if $|\lambda|<1$}\end{array}\right.

(7)

can be analytically extended on the disc

.

Let
g\in\check{A}
and let

be such that
\|\check{f_{n}}-g\|_{\check{A}}\rightarrow 0
. Inequalities (

is normal in

an therefore the function

is analytic in

. Because

is a peak point for
\check{A}
and it is not
\varphi
-periodic, there is a sequence

such that
\|g_{m}\|=g_{m}(s)=1
and

|g_{m}(\varphi^{i}(s))|<1/m,0<|i|<m
. Applying again inequalities (

R(\lambda,\check{T})g_{m}(s)
is normal in

and therefore the function

continues analytically to

through the arc
\mathds{T}\cap D(\alpha,r)
, a contradiction.
∎

Example 4.3
.

Let
A
be the Banach algebra of all complex-valued continuously differentiable functions on the unit disc

with the norm

\|f\|=\|f\|_{\infty}+\|\partial f/\partial x\|_{\infty}+\|\partial f/\partial y\|_{\infty}.

Let

,

Example 4.4

Let

Example 4.5
.

Let
A
be the Wiener algebra of all the functions on

Let
