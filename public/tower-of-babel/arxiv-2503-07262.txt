Abstract
In this work, we will continue our analysis of some general probe M5 brane solutions from our previous work in A ​ d ​ S 7 × S 4 AdS_{7}\times S^{4} spacetime (appeared in arxiv:2109.08551). These are codimension-2 in A ​ d ​ S 7 AdS_{7} and preserve at least 2 supercharges when the worldvolume 3-form flux field strength is zero. We will turn on the field strength and find that the embedding conditions are modified, excluding certain branes contained in the previous result. The new main result here is very general, so we pick simpler embedding conditions that describe highly symmetric examples that preserve half of the supersymmetry of the 11 dimensions. When the flux field is zero, worldvolumes have A ​ d ​ S 5 × S 1 AdS_{5}\times S^{1} topology. We turn the flux field value non-zero in these examples and analyze how the shape of the worldvolume deforms as supersymmetry is broken by some additional fractions.

From the

The nature or shape of the deformation can be analyzed a bit if we look at the projection conditions given in (

This web can be formed when each of the three component M5 brane worldvolumes start to develop some 2-dimensional ridge-like spike deformations emerging out in the orthogonal directions.
