36 U.S.C. § 22505 — Exclusive right to name
Citation: us:usc:36:22505
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section22505
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 225 (American War Mothers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation and its State, territorial, and local subdivisions have the exclusive right to use the name “American War Mothers”.
