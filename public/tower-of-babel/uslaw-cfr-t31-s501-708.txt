31 C.F.R. § 501.708 — Director's finding of no penalty warranted.
Citation: us:cfr:31:501.708
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-501.708
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 501 (Reporting, Procedures And Penalties Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If after considering any written response to the Prepenalty Notice submitted pursuant to § 501.707 and any other relevant facts, the Director determines that there was no violation or that the violation does not warrant the imposition of a civil monetary penalty, the Director promptly shall notify the respondent in writing of that determination and that no civil monetary penalty pursuant to this subpart will be imposed.
