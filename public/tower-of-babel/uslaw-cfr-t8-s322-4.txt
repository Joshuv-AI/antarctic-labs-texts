8 C.F.R. § 322.4 — Interview.
Citation: us:cfr:8:322.4
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-322.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 322 (Child Born Outside The United States; Requirements For Application For Certificate Of Citizenship). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The U.S. citizen parent and the child must appear in person before a USCIS officer for examination on the application under this section. If the U.S. citizen parent is deceased, the child's U.S. citizen grandparent or U.S. citizen legal guardian who filed the application on the child's behalf must appear.
