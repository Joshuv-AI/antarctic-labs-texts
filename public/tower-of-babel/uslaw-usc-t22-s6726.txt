22 U.S.C. § 6726 — Prohibited acts relating to inspections
Citation: us:usc:22:6726
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section6726
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 75 (Chemical Weapons Convention Implementation); Subchapter II (Inspections). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It shall be unlawful for any person willfully to fail or refuse to permit entry or inspection, or to disrupt, delay, or otherwise impede an inspection, authorized by this chapter.
