7 C.F.R. § 1000.90 — Dates.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1000.90
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Milk), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1000.90
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1000 (General Provisions Of Federal Milk Marketing Orders). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a date required for a payment contained in a Federal milk order falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or national holiday, such payment will be due on the next day that the market administrator's office is open for public business.
