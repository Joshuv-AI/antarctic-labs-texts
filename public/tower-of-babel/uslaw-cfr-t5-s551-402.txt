5 C.F.R. § 551.402 — Agency responsibility.
Citation: us:cfr:5:551.402
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-551.402
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 551 (Pay Administration Under The Fair Labor Standards Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An agency is responsible for exercising appropriate controls to assure that only that work for which it intends to make payment is performed.
(b) An agency shall keep complete and accurate records of all hours worked by its employees.
