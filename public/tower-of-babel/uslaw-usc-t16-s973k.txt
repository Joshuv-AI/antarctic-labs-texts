16 U.S.C. § 973k — Closed Area stowage requirements
Citation: us:usc:16:973k
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section973k
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 16C (South Pacific Tuna Fishing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

At all times while a vessel is in a Closed Area, the fishing gear of the vessel shall be stowed in such a manner as not to be readily available for fishing and in accordance with any requirements established by the Secretary.
