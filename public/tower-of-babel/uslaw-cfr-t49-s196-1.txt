49 C.F.R. § 196.1 — What is the purpose and scope of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:49:196.1
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-196.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 196 (Protection Of Underground Pipelines From Excavation Activity). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes the minimum requirements that excavators must follow to protect underground pipelines from excavation-related damage. It also establishes an enforcement process for violations of these requirements.
