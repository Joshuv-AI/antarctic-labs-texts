41 C.F.R. § 109-40.110-1 — -40.110-1 Small business assistance.
Citation: us:cfr:41:109-40.110-1
Creator: Chapter 109—Department Of Energy Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-109-40.110-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 109-40 (40—Transportation And Traffic Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Consistent with the policies of the Government with respect to small businesses, DOE shall place with small business concerns a fair proportion of the total purchases and contracts for transportation and related services such as packing and crating, loading and unloading, and local drayage.
