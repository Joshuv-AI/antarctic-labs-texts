47 C.F.R. § 73.1530 — Portable test stations [Definition].
Citation: us:cfr:47:73.1530
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-73.1530
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 73 (Radio Broadcast Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A portable test station is one that is moved from place to place for making field strength and ground conductivity measurements, for selecting station transmitter sites, and conducting other specialized propagation tests. Portable test stations are not normally used while in motion, and may not be used for the transmission of programs intended to be received by the public.
