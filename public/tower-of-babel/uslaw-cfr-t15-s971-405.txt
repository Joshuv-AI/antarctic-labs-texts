15 C.F.R. § 971.405 — Breach of international peace and security involving armed conflict.
Citation: us:cfr:15:971.405
Creator: National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-971.405
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 971 (Deep Seabed Mining Regulations For Commercial Recovery Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before issuing or transferring a commercial recovery permit, the Administrator must find that the recovery proposed in the application will not create a situation which may reasonably be expected to lead to a breach of international peace and security involving armed conflict.
