14 U.S.C. § 1947 — Assignment of personnel as instructors
Citation: us:usc:14:1947
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section1947
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 19 (Coast Guard Academy); Subchapter III (Faculty). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commandant may assign any member to appropriate instruction duty at the Academy.
