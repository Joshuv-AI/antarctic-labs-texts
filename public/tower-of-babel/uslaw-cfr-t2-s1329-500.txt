2 C.F.R. § 1329.500 — Who in the Department of Commerce determines that a recipient other than an individual violated the requirements of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:2:1329.500
Creator: Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1329.500
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1329 (Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary of Commerce or designee determines that a recipient other than an individual violated the requirements of this part.
