16 C.F.R. § 1507.8 — Wheel devices.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1507.8
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1507.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1507 (Fireworks Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Drivers in fireworks devices commonly known as “wheels” shall be securely attached to the device so that they will not come loose in transportation, handling, and normal operation. Wheel devices intended to operate in a fixed location shall be designed in such a manner that the axle remains attached to the device during normal operation.
