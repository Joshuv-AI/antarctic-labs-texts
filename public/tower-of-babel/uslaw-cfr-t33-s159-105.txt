33 C.F.R. § 159.105 — Shock test.
Citation: us:cfr:33:159.105
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-159.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 159 (Marine Sanitation Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The device, with liquid retention components, if any, filled with water to half of their volume, must be subjected to 1,000 vertical shocks that are ten times the force of gravity (10g) and have a duration of 20-25 milliseconds measured at the base of the half-sine shock envelope.
