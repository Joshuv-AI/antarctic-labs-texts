20 C.F.R. § 219.38 — Evidence of stepparent or stepchild relationship.
Citation: us:cfr:20:219.38
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-219.38
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 219 (Evidence Required For Payment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the claimant is a stepparent or stepchild of the employee, the Board will ask for the evidence described in § 219.37 or § 219.39 which shows the person's natural or adoptive relationship to the employee's husband, wife, widow, or widower. The Board will also ask for evidence of the husband's, wife's, widow's or widower's marriage to the employee (See §§ 219.30-219.33).
