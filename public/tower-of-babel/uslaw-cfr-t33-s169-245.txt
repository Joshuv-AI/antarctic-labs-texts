33 C.F.R. § 169.245 — What must a ship master do if LRIT equipment is switched off or fails to operate?
Citation: us:cfr:33:169.245
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-169.245
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 169 (Ship Reporting Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If a ship's LRIT equipment is switched off or fails to operate, the ship's master must inform his or her Flag Administration without undue delay.
(b) The master must also make an entry in the ship's logbook that states—
(1) His or her reason for switching the LRIT equipment off, or an entry that the equipment has failed to operate, and
(2) The period during which the LRIT equipment was switched off or non-operational.
