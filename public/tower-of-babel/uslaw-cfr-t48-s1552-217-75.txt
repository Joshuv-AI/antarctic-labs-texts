48 C.F.R. § 1552.217-75 — 1552.217-75 Option to extend the effective period of the contract—time and materials or labor hour contract.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1552.217-75
Creator: Chapter 15—Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1552.217-75
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1552 (Solicitation Provisions And Contract Clauses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As prescribed in 1517.208(f), insert this clause in time and materials or labor hour type contracts when applicable. This clause will be modified to reflect the actual number of option periods for the acquisition. If only one option period is used, modify (c) accordingly.
