29 C.F.R. § 825.114 — Inpatient care.
Citation: us:cfr:29:825.114
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-825.114
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 825 (The Family And Medical Leave Act Of 1993). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Inpatient care means an overnight stay in a hospital, hospice, or residential medical care facility, including any period of incapacity as defined in § 825.113(b), or any subsequent treatment in connection with such inpatient care.
