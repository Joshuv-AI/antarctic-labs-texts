21 C.F.R. § 870.2900 — Patient transducer and electrode cable (including connector).
Citation: us:cfr:21:870.2900
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-870.2900
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 870 (Cardiovascular Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. A patient transducer and electrode cable (including connector) is an electrical conductor used to transmit signals from, or power or excitation signals to, patient-connected electrodes or transducers.
(b) Classification. Class II (performance standards).
