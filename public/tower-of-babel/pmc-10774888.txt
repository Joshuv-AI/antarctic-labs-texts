•US Senator Tammy Duckworth (D-IL) and Congresswoman Ayanna Pressley (D-MA-07) are making a renewed push to raise awareness about pregnancy loss and establish new paid leave benefits for workers nationwide who experience painful, traumatic challenges while seeking to start or grow their families. The ASRM has joined other leading medical organizations in supporting the bicameral “Support Through Loss Act,” which would increase access to resources and patient-centered care while providing 7 days of paid leave for all American workers after they experience many of the challenges that hopeful parents face on the road to parenthood—including miscarriage, an unsuccessful assisted reproductive technology procedure, or a medical diagnosis or event impacting pregnancy or fertility.
