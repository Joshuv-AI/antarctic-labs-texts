15 U.S.C. § 7423 — No additional funds authorized
Citation: us:usc:15:7423
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section7423
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 100A (Cybersecurity Enhancement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No additional funds are authorized to carry out this Act, and the amendments made by this Act. This Act, and the amendments made by this Act, shall be carried out using amounts otherwise authorized or appropriated.
