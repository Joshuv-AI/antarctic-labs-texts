2 C.F.R. § 1120.305 — Requirement for general terms and conditions.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1120.305
Creator: Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1120.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1120 (Award Format For Dod Grants And Cooperative Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each DoD Component must establish at least one set of general terms and conditions. A DoD Component may have more than one set, as it deems appropriate to reflect differences in its award terms and conditions across different programs, classes of recipients, or types of activity.
