40 C.F.R. § 179.45 — Appearance.
Citation: us:cfr:40:179.45
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-179.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 179 (Formal Evidentiary Public Hearing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A party to a hearing may appear in person or by counsel or other representative in the hearing.
(b) The presiding officer may strike a person's right to appear in the hearing for violation of the rules of conduct in § l79.50.
