25 C.F.R. § 518.9 — When will a certificate of self-regulation become effective?
Citation: us:cfr:25:518.9
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-518.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 518 (Self-Regulation Of Class Ii Gaming). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A certificate of self-regulation shall become effective on January 1 of the year following the year in which the Commission determines that a certificate will issue. Petitions will be reviewed in chronological order based on the date of receipt of a complete petition.
