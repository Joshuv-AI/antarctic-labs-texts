25 C.F.R. § 556.8 — Compliance with this part.
Citation: us:cfr:25:556.8
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-556.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 556 (Background Investigations For Primary Management Officials And Key Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All tribal gaming ordinances and ordinance amendments approved by the Chair prior to September 14, 2023 do not need to be amended to comply with this part. All future ordinance submissions, however, must comply.
