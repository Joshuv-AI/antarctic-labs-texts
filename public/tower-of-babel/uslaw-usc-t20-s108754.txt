20 U.S.C. § 1087–54 — Sources of matching funds
Citation: us:usc:20:1087–54
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1087–54
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 28 (Higher Education Resources And Student Assistance); Subchapter IV (Student Assistance); Part C (Federal Work-Study Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this part shall be construed as restricting the source (other than this part) from which the institution may pay its share of the compensation of a student employed under a work-study program covered by an agreement under this part, and such share may be paid to such student in the form of services and equipment (including tuition, room, board, and books) furnished by such institution.
