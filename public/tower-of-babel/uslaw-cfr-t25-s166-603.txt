25 C.F.R. § 166.603 — If cash is submitted as a bond, how is it administered?
Citation: us:cfr:25:166.603
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-166.603
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 166 (Grazing Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If cash is submitted as a bond, we will establish an account in the name of the permittee and retain it.
