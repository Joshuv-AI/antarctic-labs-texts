16 U.S.C. § 655 — Agents to be disinterested
Citation: us:usc:16:655
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section655
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 5 (Protection Of Fur Seals And Other Fur-Bearing Animals). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The persons charged with the management of the seal fisheries in Alaska, and the performance of such other duties as may be assigned to them by the Secretary of the Interior, shall never be interested directly or indirectly in any lease of the right to take seals, nor in any proceeds or profits thereof either as owner, agent, partner, or otherwise.
