21 C.F.R. § 178.3730 — Piperonyl butoxide and pyrethrins as components of bags.
Citation: us:cfr:21:178.3730
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-178.3730
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 178 (Indirect Food Additives: Adjuvants, Production Aids, And Sanitizers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Piperonyl butoxide in combination with pyrethrins may be safely used for insect control on bags that are intended for use in contact with dried feed or dried food in compliance with 40 CFR 180.127 and 40 CFR 180.128.
