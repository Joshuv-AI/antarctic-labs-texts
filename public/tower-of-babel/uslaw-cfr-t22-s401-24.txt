22 C.F.R. § 401.24 — Expenses of proceedings.
Citation: us:cfr:22:401.24
Creator: International Joint Commission, United States And Canada
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-401.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 401 (Rules Of Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The expenses of those participating in any proceeding under this subpart B shall be borne by the participants.
(b) The Commission, after due notice to the participant or participants concerned, may require that any unusual cost or expense to the Commission shall be paid by the person on whose behalf or at whose request such unusual cost or expense has been or will be incurred.
