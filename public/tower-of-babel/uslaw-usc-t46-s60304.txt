46 U.S.C. § 60304 — Presidential suspension of tonnage taxes and light money
Citation: us:usc:46:60304
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section60304
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 603 (Tonnage Taxes And Light Money). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If the President is satisfied that the government of a foreign country does not impose discriminating or countervailing duties to the disadvantage of the United States, the President shall suspend the imposition of special tonnage taxes and light money under sections 60302 and 60303 of this title on vessels of that country.
