12 C.F.R. § 1805.300 — Purposes of financial assistance.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1805.300
Creator: Community Development Financial Institutions Fund, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1805.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1805 (Community Development Financial Institutions Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The CDFI Fund may provide financial assistance through investment instruments described under subpart D of this part. Such financial assistance is intended to increase available capital and enhance the ability of a Recipient to provide Financial Products, Financial Services, and Development Services.
