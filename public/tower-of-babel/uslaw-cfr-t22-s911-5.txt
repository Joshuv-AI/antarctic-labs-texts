22 C.F.R. § 911.5 — Arbitrability of determination.
Citation: us:cfr:22:911.5
Creator: Foreign Service Grievance Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-911.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 911 (Implementation Disputes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Questions that cannot be resolved by the parties as to whether a complaint is subject to this procedure may be referred by either party to the Grievance Board for a threshold determination.
