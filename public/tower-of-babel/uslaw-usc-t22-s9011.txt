22 U.S.C. § 9011 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:22:9011
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section9011
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 97 (International Child Abduction Remedies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated for each fiscal year such sums as may be necessary to carry out the purposes of the Convention and this chapter.
