49 C.F.R. § 1108.10 — Precedent.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1108.10
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1108.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1108 (Arbitration Of Certain Disputes Subject To The Statutory Jurisdiction Of The Surface Transportation Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Decisions rendered by arbitrators pursuant to these rules may be guided by, but need not be bound by, agency precedent. Arbitration decisions shall have no precedential value and may not be relied upon in any manner during subsequent arbitration proceedings conducted under the rules in this part.
