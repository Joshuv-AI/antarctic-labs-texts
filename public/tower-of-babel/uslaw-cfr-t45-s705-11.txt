45 C.F.R. § 705.11 — Penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:45:705.11
Creator: Commission On Civil Rights
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-705.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 705 (MATERIALS AVAILABLE PURSUANT TO 5 U.S.C. 552a). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person who makes a false statement in connection with any request for a record, or in any request for an amendment to a record under this part, is subject to the penalties prescribed in 18 U.S.C. 494 and 495.
