43 C.F.R. § 2450.7 — Right to occupy or settle.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2450.7
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2450.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2450 (Petition-Application Classification System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The filing of a petition-application gives no right to occupy or settle upon the land. A person shall be entitled to the possession and use of land only after his entry, selection, or location has been allowed, or a lease has been issued. Settlement on the land prior to that time constitutes a trespass.
