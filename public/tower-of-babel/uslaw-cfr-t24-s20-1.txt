24 C.F.R. § 20.1 — Establishment of the Office of Hearings and Appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:24:20.1
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-20.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 20 (Office Of Hearings And Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

There is established in the Office of the Secretary the Office of Hearings and Appeals. The Administrative Law Judges and the Administrative Judges within the Office of Hearings and Appeals are appointed by the Secretary of the Department pursuant to the Appointments Clause of the United States Constitution.
