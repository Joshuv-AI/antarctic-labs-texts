Categorical quotients for actions of groupoids on varieties
Ian M. Musson

Abstract
For certain actions of the Weyl groupoid 𝔚 \mathfrak{W} from [ 17 ] on an affine variety X X , geometric properties of the map π : X ⟶ Y = Spec ⁡ 𝒪 ​ ( X ) 𝔚 \pi:X\longrightarrow Y={\operatorname{Spec}\;}\mathcal{O}(X)^{\mathfrak{W}} were studied in [ 13 ] , In this paper we show that if the base field 𝚔 \mathtt{k} is uncountable, the map π \pi is a geometric quotient which is universal in the category of 𝚔 \mathtt{k} -schemes. To do this we adapt a result from [ 12 ] showing that a geometric quotient is universal in the category of 𝚔 \mathtt{k} -schemes, to quotients by groupoids and more generally by equivalence relations. In our approach a key role is played by the closed points and Jacobson schemes.

2.2
The Jacobson condition

By a result of Amitsur
[
1
]
, if

is an uncountable field and

is a

is Jacobson.
A scheme

This is a local property: If

is an open covering, then

is Jacobson if and only if every
Y_{i}
is Jacobson.
If a
\mathtt{k}
-scheme

is Jacobson, then the topology is determined by the induced topology on the
\mathtt{k}
-rational points
Y(\mathtt{k})
. For example if
U_{1},U_{2}\subset Y
are open subschemes such that
U_{1}(\mathtt{k})=U_{2}(\mathtt{k})
, then
U_{1}=U_{2}
. This follows easily from Lemma
2.5
(b).

Lemma 2.4
.

If
\mathtt{k}
is uncountable and
\pi\colon X\to Y
is a geometric quotient, then

is Jacobson.

Proof.

If

is open in
Y
, then by (iv),
\mathcal{O}_{Y}(U)\subseteq\mathcal{O}_{X}(\pi^{-1}(U))
which is a
\mathtt{k}
-algebra of countable dimension, hence a Jacobson algebra. Thus the result follows since the Jacobson property is local.
∎

page 85 and we have
