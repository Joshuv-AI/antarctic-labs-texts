7 C.F.R. § 56.20 — Who may obtain grading service.
Citation: us:cfr:7:56.20
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-56.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 56 (Voluntary Grading Of Shell Eggs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An application for grading service may be made by any interested person, including, but not being limited to any authorized agent of the United States, any State, county, municipality, or common carrier.
