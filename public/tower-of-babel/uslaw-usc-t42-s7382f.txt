42 U.S.C. § 7382f — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:42:7382f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section7382f
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 84 (Department Of Energy); Subchapter XIV (Albert Einstein Distinguished Educator Fellowships). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
There are authorized to be appropriated for the Program $700,000 for fiscal year 1995, and such sums as may be necessary for each of the four succeeding fiscal years.
(b) WERC program
There are authorized to be appropriated for the WERC program under section 7382e of this title such sums as may be necessary for fiscal year 1995 and each of the four succeeding fiscal years.
