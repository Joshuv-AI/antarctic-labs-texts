2 U.S.C. § 1516 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:2:1516
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1516
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 25 (Unfunded Mandates Reform); Subchapter I (Legislative Accountability And Reform). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Congressional Budget Office $4,500,000 for each of the fiscal years 1996, 1997, 1998, 1999, 2000, 2001, and 2002 to carry out the provisions of this subchapter.
