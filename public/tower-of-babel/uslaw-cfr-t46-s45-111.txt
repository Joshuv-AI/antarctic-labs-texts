46 C.F.R. § 45.111 — Strength of bulkheads at ends of superstructures.
Citation: us:cfr:46:45.111
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-45.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 45 (Great Lakes Load Lines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Bulkheads at ends of enclosed superstructures must have sufficient strength to withstand impact of boarding seas.
