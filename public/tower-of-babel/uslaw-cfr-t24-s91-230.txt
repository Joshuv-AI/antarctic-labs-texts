24 C.F.R. § 91.230 — Monitoring.
Citation: us:cfr:24:91.230
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-91.230
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 91 (Consolidated Submissions For Community Planning And Development Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The plan must describe the standards and procedures that the jurisdiction will use to monitor activities carried out in furtherance of the plan and will use to ensure long-term compliance with requirements of the programs involved, including civil rights related program requirements and the comprehensive planning requirements.
