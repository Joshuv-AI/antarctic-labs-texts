25 C.F.R. § 162.344 — Will BIA require an appeal bond for an appeal of a decision on a residential lease document?
Citation: us:cfr:25:162.344
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-162.344
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 162 (Leases And Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

BIA will not require an appeal bond for an appeal of a decision on a residential lease document.
