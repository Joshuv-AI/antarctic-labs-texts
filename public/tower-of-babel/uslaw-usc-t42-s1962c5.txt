42 U.S.C. § 1962c–5 — “State” defined
Citation: us:usc:42:1962c–5
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1962c–5
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 19B (Water Resources Planning); Subchapter III (Financial Assistance To States For Comprehensive Planning Grant Authorizations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of this subchapter the term “State” means a State, the District of Columbia, Puerto Rico, the Virgin Islands or Guam.
