22 C.F.R. § 909.5 — Time limits for compliance.
Citation: us:cfr:22:909.5
Creator: Foreign Service Grievance Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-909.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 909 (Decisionmaking). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Orders of the Board and recommendations which are not rejected in accordance with § 908.2 of this chapter shall be complied with within any time limits for compliance established by the Board's decision, unless the Board extends the time limit on a showing of good cause.
