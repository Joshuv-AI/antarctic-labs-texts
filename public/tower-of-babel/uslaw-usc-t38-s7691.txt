38 U.S.C. § 7691 — Establishment
Citation: us:usc:38:7691
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section7691
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part V (Boards, Administrations, And Services); Chapter 76 (Health Professionals Educational Assistance Program); Subchapter VIII (Specialty Education Loan Repayment Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As part of the Educational Assistance Program, the Secretary may carry out a student loan repayment program under section 5379 of title 5. The program shall be known as the Department of Veterans Affairs Specialty Education Loan Repayment Program (in this chapter referred to as the “Specialty Education Loan Repayment Program”).
