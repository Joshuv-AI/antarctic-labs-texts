40 C.F.R. § 600.316-08 — -08 Multistage manufacture.
Citation: us:cfr:40:600.316-08
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-600.316-08
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 600 (Fuel Economy And Greenhouse Gas Exhaust Emissions Of Motor Vehicles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where more than one person is the manufacturer of a vehicle, the final stage manufacturer (as defined in 49 CFR 529.3) is treated as the vehicle manufacturer for purposes of compliance with this subpart.
