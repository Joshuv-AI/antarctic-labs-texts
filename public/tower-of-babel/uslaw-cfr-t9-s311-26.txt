9 C.F.R. § 311.26 — Emaciation.
Citation: us:cfr:9:311.26
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-311.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 311 (Disposal Of Diseased Or Otherwise Adulterated Carcasses And Parts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Carcasses of livestock too emaciated to produce wholesome meat, and carcasses which show a serous infiltration of muscle tissues, or a serous or mucoid degeneration of the fatty tissue, shall be condemned. A gelatinous change of the fat of the heart and kidneys of well-nourished carcasses and mere leanness shall not be classed as emaciation.
