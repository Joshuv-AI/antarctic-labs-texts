48 C.F.R. § 2301.104 — 2301.104 Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2301.104
Creator: Chapter 23—Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2301.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2301 (Social Security Acquisition Regulation System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The FAR and SSAR apply to all SSA acquisitions as stated in FAR 1.104. Unless specified otherwise, the FAR and SSAR apply to acquisitions within and outside the United States.
