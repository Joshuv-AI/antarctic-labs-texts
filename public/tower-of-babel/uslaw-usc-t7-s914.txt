7 U.S.C. § 914 — Separability
Citation: us:usc:7:914
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section914
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 31 (Rural Electrification And Telephone Service); Subchapter I (Rural Electrification). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this chapter, or the application thereof to any person or circumstances, is held invalid, the remainder of the chapter and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
