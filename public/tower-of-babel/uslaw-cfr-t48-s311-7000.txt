48 C.F.R. § 311.7000 — 311.7000 Defining electronic and information technology requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:48:311.7000
Creator: Chapter 3—Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-311.7000
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 311 (Describing Agency Needs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall ensure that requiring activities specify agency needs for electronic and information technology (EIT) supplies and services, and document market research, document EIT requirements, and identify the applicable Section 508 accessibility standards. See FAR 11.002(f) and HHSAR subpart 339.2.
