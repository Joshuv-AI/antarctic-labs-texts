33 C.F.R. § 95.005 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:33:95.005
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-95.005
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 95 (Operating A Vessel While Under The Influence Of Alcohol Or A Dangerous Drug). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) This part is applicable to a vessel (except those excluded by 46 U.S.C. 2109) operated on waters subject to the jurisdiction of the United States, and to a vessel owned in the United States on the high seas. This includes a foreign vessel operated on waters subject to the jurisdiction of the United States.
(b) This part is also applicable at all times to vessels inspected, or subject to inspection, under Chapter 33 of Title 46 United States Code.
