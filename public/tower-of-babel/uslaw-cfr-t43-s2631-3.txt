43 C.F.R. § 2631.3 — Surveying and conveyance fees.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2631.3
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2631.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2630 (Railroad Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The carrier must pay the cost of the survey of the land, paying also one-half the cost of any segregation survey in accordance with the laws and regulations pertaining to the survey and patenting of railroad lands. (See 43 U.S.C. 881 et seq.; also subpart 1822 of this chapter.)
