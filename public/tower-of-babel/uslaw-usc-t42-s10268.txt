42 U.S.C. § 10268 — Report
Citation: us:usc:42:10268
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section10268
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 108 (Nuclear Waste Policy); Subchapter V (Nuclear Waste Technical Review Board). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Board shall report not less than 2 times per year to Congress and the Secretary its findings, conclusions, and recommendations. The first such report shall be submitted not later than 12 months after December 22, 1987.
