42 C.F.R. § 68b.12 — b.12 What other regulations and statutes apply?

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 68b (PART 68b—NATIONAL INSTITUTES OF HEALTH (NIH) UNDERGRADUATE SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAM REGARDING PROFESSIONS NEEDED BY NATIONAL RESEARCH INSTITUTES (UGSP)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Several other regulations and statutes apply to this part. These include, but are not necessarily limited to:
(a) Debt Collection Act of 1982 (31 U.S.C. 3701 et seq.);
(b) Debt Collection Improvement Act of 1996 (31 U.S.C. 3701 note);
(c) Fair Credit Reporting Act (15 U.S.C. 1681 et seq.);
(d) Federal Debt Collection Procedures Act of 1990 (28 U.S.C. 176); and
(e) Privacy Act of 1974 (5 U.S.C. 552a).
