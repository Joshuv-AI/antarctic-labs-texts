5 C.F.R. § 890.605 — Persons confined on effective date.
Citation: us:cfr:5:890.605
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-890.605
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 890 (Federal Employees Health Benefits Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Benefits may not be limited for persons who, on the effective date of an enrollment under § 890.602, are confined in a hospital or institution.
