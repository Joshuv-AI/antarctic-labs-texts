43 C.F.R. § 3927.20 — Lease size.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3927.20
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3927.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3920 (Oil Shale Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The maximum size of an oil shale lease is 5,760 acres.
