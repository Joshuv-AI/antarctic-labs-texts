40 C.F.R. § 166.45 — Duration of crisis exemption.
Citation: us:cfr:40:166.45
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-166.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 166 (Exemption Of Federal And State Agencies For Use Of Pesticides Under Emergency Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A crisis exemption may be authorized for:
(a) Only as long as is necessary to control the pest or conditions causing the emergency; and
(b) No longer than 15 days, unless an application requesting a specific, quarantine, or public health exemption for this use has been submitted to the Agency.
