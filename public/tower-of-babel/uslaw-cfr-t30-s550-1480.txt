30 C.F.R. § 550.1480 — May the United States criminally prosecute me for violations under Federal oil and gas leases?
Citation: us:cfr:30:550.1480
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-550.1480
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 550 (Oil And Gas And Sulfur Operations In The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you commit an act for which a civil penalty is provided at 30 U.S.C. 1719(d) and § 550.1460(b), the United States may pursue criminal penalties as provided at 30 U.S.C. 1720, in addition to any authority for prosecution under other statutes.
