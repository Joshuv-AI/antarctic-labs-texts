25 U.S.C. § 3746 — Tribal immunity
Citation: us:usc:25:3746
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section3746
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 39 (American Indian Agricultural Resource Management); Subchapter III (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to affect, modify, diminish, or otherwise impair the sovereign immunity from suit enjoyed by Indian tribes.
