7 C.F.R. § 1221.211 — Voting period.

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1221.211
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1221 (Sorghum Promotion, Research, And Information Order). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term voting period means a 4-week period to be announced by the Secretary for voting in the referendum.
