29 C.F.R. § 1630.5 — Limiting, segregating, and classifying.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1630.5
Creator: Equal Employment Opportunity Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1630.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1630 (Regulations To Implement The Equal Employment Provisions Of The Americans With Disabilities Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is unlawful for a covered entity to limit, segregate, or classify a job applicant or employee in a way that adversely affects his or her employment opportunities or status on the basis of disability.
