43 C.F.R. § 3901.20 — Acreage limitations.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3901.20
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3901.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3900 (Oil Shale Management—General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No entity may hold more than 50,000 acres of Federal oil shale leases on public lands and 50,000 acres on acquired lands in any one state. Oil shale lease acreage does not count toward acreage limitations associated with leases for other minerals.
