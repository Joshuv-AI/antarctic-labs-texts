20 U.S.C. § 1655 — Uniform rules of evidence of racial discrimination
Citation: us:usc:20:1655
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1655
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 37 (Assignment Or Transportation Of Students). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The rules of evidence required to prove that State or local authorities are practicing racial discrimination in assigning students to public schools shall be uniform throughout the United States.
