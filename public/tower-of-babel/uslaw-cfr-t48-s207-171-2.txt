48 C.F.R. § 207.171-2 — 207.171-2 Definition.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 207 (Acquisition Planning). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Component, as used in this section, includes subsystems, assemblies, subassemblies, and other major elements of an end item; it does not include elements of relatively small annual acquisition value.
