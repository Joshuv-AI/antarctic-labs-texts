5 C.F.R. § 1315.19 — Regulatory references to OMB Circular A-125.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1315.19
Creator: Office Of Management And Budget
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1315.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1315 (Prompt Payment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part supercedes OMB Circular A-125 (“Prompt Payment”). Until revised to reflect the codification in this part, regulatory references to Circular A-125 shall be construed as referring to this part.
