About resurgence (vozrojdenie):

This term have introduced (defined) by J. Ecalle in 1998 year
[
10
]
.

Consider Euler’s equation

t^{2}(dy/dt)=t-y

(3)

By a
book on ODE we try to solve (
3
) by

y=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}a_{m}t^{m}

We obtaine

y=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}(-1)^{m}m!t^{m+1}

Unfortunately this series divergences for all
t\neq 0
.

But if we consider homogeneous equation

t^{2}(dy/dt)=-y

and solve it, we have

y=ae^{1/t}.

The solution

y=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}(-1)^{m}m!t^{m+1}+ae^{1/t}.

is the resurgence solution of (
3
).

I will call the resurgence the Tauberian resurgence.

There exists also the Abelian resurgence.
