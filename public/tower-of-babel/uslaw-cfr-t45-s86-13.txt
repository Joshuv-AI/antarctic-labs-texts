45 C.F.R. § 86.13 — Military and merchant marine educational institutions.
Citation: us:cfr:45:86.13
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-86.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 86 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Sex In Education Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part does not apply to an educational institution whose primary purpose is the training of individuals for a military service of the United States or for the merchant marine.
