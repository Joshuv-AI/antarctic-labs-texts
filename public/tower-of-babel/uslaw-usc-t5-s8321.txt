5 U.S.C. § 8321 — Liability of accountable employees
Citation: us:usc:5:8321
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section8321
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart G (Insurance And Annuities); Chapter 83 (Retirement); Subchapter II (Forfeiture Of Annuities And Retired Pay). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An accountable employee may not be held responsible for a payment made in violation of this subchapter when the payment made is in due course and without fraud, collusion, or gross negligence.
