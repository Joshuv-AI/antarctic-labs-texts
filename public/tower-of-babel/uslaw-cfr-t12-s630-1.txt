12 C.F.R. § 630.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:12:630.1
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-630.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 630 (Disclosure To Investors In Systemwide And Consolidated Bank Debt Obligations Of The Farm Credit System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth the requirements for preparation and publication by the Farm Credit System (FCS or System) of annual and quarterly reports to investors and potential investors in Systemwide and consolidated bank debt obligations of the System and to other users of the reports in the general public.
