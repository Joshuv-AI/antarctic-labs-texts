24 C.F.R. § 27.119 — Redemption rights.
Citation: us:cfr:24:27.119
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-27.119
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 27 (Nonjudicial Foreclosure Of Multifamily And Single Family Mortgages). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Only for purposes of redemption rights under the Statute, a foreclosure shall be considered completed upon the date and at the time of the foreclosure sale.
