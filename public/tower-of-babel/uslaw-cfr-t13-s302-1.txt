13 C.F.R. § 302.1 — Environment.
Citation: us:cfr:13:302.1
Creator: Economic Development Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-302.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 302 (General Terms And Conditions For Investment Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

EDA will undertake environmental reviews of projects in accordance with the requirements of the National Environmental Policy Act of 1969, as amended (Pub. L. 91-190; 42 U.S.C. 4321 et seq.) (“NEPA”), and all applicable Federal environmental statutes, regulations, and Executive Orders. Depending on the project's location, environmental information concerning specific projects may be obtained from the individual serving as the Environmental Officer for the proposed action.
