36 C.F.R. § 1235.30 — How may records in the National Archives of the United States be used?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1235.30
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1235.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1235 (Transfer Of Records To The National Archives Of The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) NARA will enforce restrictions that are consistent with FOIA (5 U.S.C. 552(b) as amended) for both official use of the records by Federal agencies and research by the public.
(b) NARA regulations in Subchapter C of this chapter apply to Federal agency personnel using transferred records for official Government purposes, and to the public at large.
