25 C.F.R. § 138.4 — Deferment of assessments on lands remaining in Indian ownership.
Citation: us:cfr:25:138.4
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-138.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 138 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In conformity with the act of July 1, 1932 (47 Stat. 564); 25 U.S.C. 386(a) no assessment shall be made on behalf of construction costs against Indian-owned land within the project until the Indian title thereto has been extinguished.
