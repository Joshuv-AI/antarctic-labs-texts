29 C.F.R. § 503.15 — Enforcement.
Citation: us:cfr:29:503.15
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-503.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 503 (Enforcement Of Obligations For Temporary Nonimmigrant Non-Agricultural Workers Described In The Immigration And Nationality Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The investigation, inspection, and law enforcement functions that carry out the provisions of 8 U.S.C. 1184(c), INA section 214(c), 20 CFR part 655, subpart A, or the regulations in this part pertain to the employment of any H-2B worker, any worker in corresponding employment, or any U.S. worker improperly rejected for employment or improperly laid off or displaced.
