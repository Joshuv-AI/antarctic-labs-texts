42 U.S.C. § 16291a — Property interests
Citation: us:usc:42:16291a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section16291a
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 149 (National Energy Policy And Programs); Subchapter IX (Research And Development); Part F (Fossil Energy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

That for all programs funded under Fossil Energy appropriations in this and subsequent Acts, the Secretary may vest fee title or other property interests acquired under projects in any entity, including the United States.
