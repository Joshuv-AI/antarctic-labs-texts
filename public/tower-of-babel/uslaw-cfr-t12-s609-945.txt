12 C.F.R. § 609.945 — Records retention.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 609 (Cyber Risk Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Records stored electronically must be accurate, accessible, and reproducible for later reference.
