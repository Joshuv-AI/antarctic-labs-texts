7 C.F.R. § 917.9 — Fiscal period.
Citation: us:cfr:7:917.9
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-917.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 917 (Fresh Pears And Peaches Grown In California). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Fiscal period is synonymous with fiscal year and means the 12-month period ending on the last day of February of each year, or such other period that may be approved by the Secretary pursuant to recommendations by the committee.
