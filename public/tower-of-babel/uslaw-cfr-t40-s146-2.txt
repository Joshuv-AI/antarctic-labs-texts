40 C.F.R. § 146.2 — Law authorizing these regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:40:146.2
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-146.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 146 (Underground Injection Control Program: Criteria And Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Safe Drinking Water Act, 42 U.S.C. 300f et seq. authorizes these regulations and all other UIC program regulations referenced in 40 CFR part 144. Certain regulations relating to the injection of hazardous waste are also authorized by the Resource Conservation and Recovery Act, 42 U.S.C. 6901 et seq.
