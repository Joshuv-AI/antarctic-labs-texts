46 C.F.R. § 4.03-70 — -70 Tank vessel.
Citation: us:cfr:46:4.03-70
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-4.03-70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 4 (Marine Casualties And Investigations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Tank vessel means a vessel that is constructed or adapted to carry, or that carries, oil, hazardous substances, marine pollutants, or noxious liquid substances, in bulk as cargo or cargo residue.
