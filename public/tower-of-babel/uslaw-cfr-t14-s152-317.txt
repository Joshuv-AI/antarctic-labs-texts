14 C.F.R. § 152.317 — Report of Federal cash transactions.
Citation: us:cfr:14:152.317
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-152.317
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 152 (Airport Aid Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When funds are advanced to a sponsor or planning agency by Treasury check, the sponsor or planning agency shall submit the report form prescribed by the Administrator within 15 working days following the end of the quarter in which check was received.
