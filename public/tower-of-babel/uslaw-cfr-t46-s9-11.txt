46 C.F.R. § 9.11 — Proration of charges.
Citation: us:cfr:46:9.11
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-9.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 9 (Extra Compensation For Overtime Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If services are performed for two or more applicants during one continuous tour of overtime duty, the charge for the extra compensation earned shall be prorated equitably according to the time attributable to the services performed for each applicant.
