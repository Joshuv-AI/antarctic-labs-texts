12 C.F.R. § 614.4080 — Loans and chartered territory—banks for cooperatives.
Citation: us:cfr:12:614.4080
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-614.4080
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 614 (Loan Policies And Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Loans made under title III by banks for cooperatives and agricultural credit banks may be made to eligible domestic parties domiciled within any territory that may be served by Farm Credit institutions under section 1.2 of the Act and to eligible foreign parties without regard to domicile.
