18 C.F.R. § 420.45 — Historical use.
Citation: us:cfr:18:420.45
Creator: Delaware River Basin Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-420.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 420 (Basin Regulations—Water Supply Charges). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person who or which could not for any reason use, take, withdraw or divert waters of the basin from the place in question on March 31, 1971, shall not be entitled to a certificate of entitlement.
