12 C.F.R. § 622.102 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:12:622.102
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-622.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 622 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules in this subpart apply to formal investigations initiated by order of the Board and pertain to the exercise of powers specified in section 5.37 of the Act. These rules do not restrict or in any way affect the authority of the FCA, including but not limited to the powers enumerated in section 5.37 of the Act, to conduct examinations of System institutions.
