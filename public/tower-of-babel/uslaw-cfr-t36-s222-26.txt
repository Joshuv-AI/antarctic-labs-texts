36 C.F.R. § 222.26 — Ex parte communications.
Citation: us:cfr:36:222.26
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-222.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 222 (Range Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Chief of the Forest Service or other Forest Service employee who made the decision being mediated, or his or her designee, shall not discuss mediation with the Appeal Deciding Officer, except to request an extension of time or to communicate the results of mediation.
