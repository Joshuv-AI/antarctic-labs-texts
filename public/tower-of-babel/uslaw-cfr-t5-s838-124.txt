5 C.F.R. § 838.124 — Employees' and retirees' responsibilities.
Citation: us:cfr:5:838.124
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-838.124
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 838 (Court Orders Affecting Retirement Benefits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees and retirees are responsible for—
(a) Raising any objections to the validity of a court order in the appropriate State court; and
(b) Submitting all disputes with former spouses to the appropriate State court for resolution.
