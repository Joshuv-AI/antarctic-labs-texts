9 C.F.R. § 96.12 — Uncertified casings not disinfected in 30 days; disposition.
Citation: us:cfr:9:96.12
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-96.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 96 (Restriction Of Importations Of Foreign Animal Casings Offered For Entry Into The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Foreign animal casings offered for importation without certification shall be disinfected as prescribed in § 96.13 within a period of 30 days after arrival in the United States, subject to the ability of Division inspectors to cover their respective districts. Otherwise such casings shall be exported or destroyed.
