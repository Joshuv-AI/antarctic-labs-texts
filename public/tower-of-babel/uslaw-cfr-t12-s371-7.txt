12 C.F.R. § 371.7 — Enforcement actions.
Citation: us:cfr:12:371.7
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-371.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 371 (Recordkeeping Requirements For Qualified Financial Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Violating the terms or requirements set forth in this part constitutes a violation of a regulation and subjects the records entity to enforcement actions under Section 8 of the Federal Deposit Insurance Act (12 U.S.C. 1818).
