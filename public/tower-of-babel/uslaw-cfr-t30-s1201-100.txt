30 C.F.R. § 1201.100 — Responsibilities of the Director for Office of Natural Resources Revenue.
Citation: us:cfr:30:1201.100
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1201.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1201 (General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director is responsible for the collection of certain rents, royalties, and other payments; for the receipt of sales and production reports; for determining royalty liability; for maintaining accounting records; for any audits of the royalty payments and obligations; and for any and all other functions relating to royalty management on Federal and Indian oil and gas leases.
