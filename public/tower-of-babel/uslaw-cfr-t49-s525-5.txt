49 C.F.R. § 525.5 — Limitation on eligibility.
Citation: us:cfr:49:525.5
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-525.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 525 (Exemptions From Average Fuel Economy Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any manufacturer that manufactures (whether or not in the customs territory of the United States) 10,000 or more passenger automobiles in the second model year preceding an affected model year or in the affected model year is ineligible for an exemption for that affected model year.
