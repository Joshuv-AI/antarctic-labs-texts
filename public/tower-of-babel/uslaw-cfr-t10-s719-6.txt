10 C.F.R. § 719.6 — Are there any types of legal matters not included in the coverage of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:10:719.6
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-719.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 719 (Contractor Legal Management Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Legal matters not covered by this part include:
(a) Matters handled by counsel retained by an insurance carrier, except under retrospective insurance in accordance with § 719.45;
(b) Routine intellectual property law support services; and
(c) Routine workers and unemployment compensation matters.
