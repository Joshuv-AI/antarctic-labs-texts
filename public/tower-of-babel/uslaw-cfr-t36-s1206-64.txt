36 C.F.R. § 1206.64 — What formal notification will I receive, and will it contain other information?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1206.64
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1206.64
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1206 (National Historical Publications And Records Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Successful grant applicants will receive a formal grant award document. The document and attachments specify terms of the grant. NHPRC staff notifies project directors informally of awards and any conditions soon after the Archivist approves the grants.
(b) The grant period begins and ends on the dates specified in the award document.
