46 C.F.R. § 113.20-3 — -3 Connection boxes.
Citation: us:cfr:46:113.20-3
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-113.20-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 113 (Communication And Alarm Systems And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each connection box and each switch enclosure in an automatic sprinkler system must be constructed in accordance with Type 4 or 4X of NEMA 250 or IP 56 of IEC 60529:2013 (both incorporated by reference; see 46 CFR 110.10-1) requirements.
