24 C.F.R. § 902.113 — Incentives for small rural PHAs high-performers.
Citation: us:cfr:24:902.113
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-902.113
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 902 (Public Housing Assessment System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) High performer. PHAs with a weighted average score for all inspections of at least 90 percent of all available points will be considered high performers and will be eligible for benefits as described in § 902.113(b) and § 905.400(l) of this chapter.
(b) Incentives. High performer small rural PHAs under the public housing program will be eligible for the same incentives as high performer PHAs under PHAS as described in § 902.71.
