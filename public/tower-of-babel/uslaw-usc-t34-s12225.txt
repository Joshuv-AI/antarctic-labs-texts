34 U.S.C. § 12225 — Award of grants
Citation: us:usc:34:12225
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section12225
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 121 (Violent Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Subchapter II (Crime Prevention); Part F (Community-Based Justice Grants For Prosecutors). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General shall consider the following facts in awarding grants:
(1)
Demonstrated need and evidence of the ability to provide the services described in the plan required under section 12223 of this title.
(2)
The Attorney General shall attempt, to the extent practicable, to achieve an equitable geographic distribution of grant awards.
