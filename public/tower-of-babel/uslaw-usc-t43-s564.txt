43 U.S.C. § 564 — Reappraisal of town lots for sale
Citation: us:usc:43:564
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section564
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 12 (Reclamation And Irrigation Of Lands By Federal Government); Subchapter XV (Town Sites, Parks, Playgrounds, And School Sites). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior is authorized, whenever he may deem it necessary, to reappraise all unsold lots within town sites on proj­ects under the reclamation Act heretofore or hereafter appraised under the provisions of sections 562 and 563 of this title; and thereafter to proceed with the sale of such town lots in accordance with said sections.
