30 U.S.C. § 293 — Duties of Secretary of the Interior
Citation: us:usc:30:293
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section293
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 4 (Lease Of Gold, Silver, Or Quicksilver Deposits When Title Confirmed By Court Of Private Land Claims). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized to perform any and all acts and to make such rules and regulations as may be necessary and proper for the purpose of carrying this chapter into full force and effect.
