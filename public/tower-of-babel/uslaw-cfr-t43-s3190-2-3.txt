43 C.F.R. § 3190.2-3 — -3 Audit.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3190.2-3
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3190.2-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3190 (Delegation Of Authority, Cooperative Agreements And Contracts For Oil And Gas Inspection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In maintaining financial records relating to the funds received and expended under a delegation of authority, cooperative agreement, or contract, States, Indian tribes and contractors shall comply with generally accepted accounting principles and audit requirements established by the Department of the Interior and Bureau of Land Management.
