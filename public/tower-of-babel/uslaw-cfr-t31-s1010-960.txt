31 C.F.R. § 1010.960 — Disclosure.
Citation: us:cfr:31:1010.960
Creator: Financial Crimes Enforcement Network, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-1010.960
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 1010 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All reports required under this chapter and all records of such reports are specifically exempted from disclosure under section 552 of Title 5, United States Code.
