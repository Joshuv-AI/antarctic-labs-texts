Probing the elusive κ / K 0 ∗ ​ ( 700 ) \kappa/K_{0}^{*}(700) resonance in semileptonic D D decays
Yu-Kuo Hsiao, Yan-Li Wang, Wen-Juan Wei

Abstract
The κ / K 0 ∗ ​ ( 700 ) \kappa/K_{0}^{*}(700) meson remains the most elusive among the light scalar resonances, with its presence in weak decays obscured by limited precision in branching fraction measurements. As a result, the true nature of the κ \kappa remains difficult to explore. Through a partial-wave analysis of the semileptonic decay D + → K − ​ π + ​ e + ​ ν e D^{+}\to K^{-}\pi^{+}e^{+}\nu_{e} , we extract ℬ ( D + → κ ¯ 0 e + ν e , κ ¯ 0 → K − π + ) = ( 2.2 ± 0.1 ) × 10 − 3 {\cal B}(D^{+}\to\bar{\kappa}^{0}e^{+}\nu_{e},\bar{\kappa}^{0}\to K^{-}\pi^{+})=(2.2\pm 0.1)\times 10^{-3} . Previously considered negligible, this contribution is now shown to dominate the observed s-wave branching fraction. This reveals that clear evidence for the κ \kappa in weak decays has long existed, but was misidentified as part of the non-resonant background. The extracted D + → κ ¯ 0 D^{+}\to\bar{\kappa}^{0} form factor, f + ​ ( 0 ) = 0.32 ± 0.01 f^{+}(0)=0.32\pm 0.01 , is significantly smaller than the q ​ q ¯ q\bar{q} prediction of 0.82 ± 0.05 0.82\pm 0.05 , and closely aligns with the q 2 ​ q ¯ 2 q^{2}\bar{q}^{2} expectation of 0.36 ± 0.02 0.36\pm 0.02 . Notably, this finding supports a compact tetraquark interpretation of the κ \kappa meson.
