29 C.F.R. § 4043.5 — How and where to file.
Citation: us:cfr:29:4043.5
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4043.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4043 (Reportable Events And Certain Other Notification Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Reportable event notices required under this part must be filed electronically in accordance with the instructions posted on PBGC's Web site, http://www.pbgc.gov. Filing guidance is provided by the instructions and by subpart A of part 4000 of this chapter.
