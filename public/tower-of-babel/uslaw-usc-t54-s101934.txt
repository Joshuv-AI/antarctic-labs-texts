54 U.S.C. § 101934 — Capital improvements
Citation: us:usc:54:101934
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section101934
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 1019 (Concessions And Commercial Use Authorizations); Subchapter III (Commercial Services Authorization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A person or entity awarded a contract under this subchapter shall receive no leasehold surrender interest, as defined in section 101915, in capital improvements constructed under the terms of the contract.
