Experimental Study and Hydration Mechanism Analysis of Geopolymer Multielement Net Slurry Self-Leveling Slurry Material
Authors: Haotian Jiang, Ruohao Zhang, Chenhua Jin, Jingjing Cao, Wei Lu
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
AbstractIn this project, cement-based composites reinforced with slag powder (abbreviated as SP), steel slag powder (abbreviated as SSP), and desulfurization gypsum (abbreviated as FGD) were used as the research objects, and the preparation, mechanical properties, and strengthening mechanism of the composites were systematically studied. A laser particle analyzer (Malvern Instruments Analysis) was used to determine that the particle sizes of the raw SSP, SP, and FGD materials were concentrated between 5 and 40 μm, indicating that they were fine-grained minerals. SSP and SP are highly active alkaline substances. With the two ratios of 6:3:1 (SP/SSP/FGD) and 7:2:1 (SP/SSP/FGD), the macroscopic mechanical properties and mechanism analysis results revealed that the alkali activator was incorporated into the composite material and that the compressive strength activation effects of the activators from strong to weak were as follows: Ca(OH)2, Na2SiO4, and NaOH. Microscopic scanning revealed that many AFt and C–S–H gels formed after hydration of the cementitious system. After water curing, efflorescence occurred on the surface of the test block, resulting in the formation of many pores inside the cementitious system.

Introduction

For global climate change, the main way to address the greenhouse effect is to reduce CO2 emissions.1,2 Therefore, China’s energy structure is gradually upgrading from fossil energy to the clean use of traditional energy,3 green energy complementarity, and low-carbon energy collaborative development.4,5 Carbon emissions of the global cement industry account for 7.5% of the total anthropogenic carbon dioxide emissions, and their proportion in China is as high as 13.75%.6,7 The China Building Materials Federation estimates that each ton of cement produced releases approximately 517.46 kg of CO2.8 The research group consulted the relevant literature and the report of the association9−11 and counted the annual emissions of CO2 produced by cement production in China in recent years,8,12,13 as shown in Figure 1. Thus, formulating practical emission reduction measures for CO2 produced in the process of cement production and reaction is highly significant for researchers.14−16 Energy conservation and emission reduction are major research directions in addressing environmental problems.17−19

Annual CO2 emission volume.

Research has shown that after a certain amount of alkaline activator (alkaline activator-Aa) is added to slag, steel slag, desulfurization gypsum, or other waste slag, the activator can stimulate the activity of the waste slag under the action of the activator and generate ettringite with high-crystallization water, thereby greatly improving the strength of the system.20,21 Therefore, on the basis of domestic and foreign researchers,22−24 our research group intends to establish a new gelled self-leveling system of geopolymer slag powder, steel slag powder, and desulfurization gypsum in the Nanjing area according to the most compact accumulation theory. China’s domestic floor has more materials for carborundum floors, such as fine stone concrete mixed with steel fiber or epoxy resin floors.25,26 The above floor materials have the disadvantages of high price, high carbon emissions, and environmental pollution. Newly gelled self-leveling methods can better solve the above shortcomings. This manuscript mainly studies the compressive strength, fluidity, and hydration reaction mechanism of a solid waste cementitious system to replace some cement clinkers in the preparation of floor plaster.27−29 Moreover, the treatment of industrial waste residue is an ideal way to reduce CO2 emissions.

Preparation Method for the Oligomer Cementitious System

An electronic scale was used to accurately weigh all types of solid waste and alkaline activator (electronic scale accuracy ±1 g), which was slowly poured into the stirring pot and stirred slowly for 1 min so that the materials were fully and evenly mixed, tap water (water–binder ratio 0.5) was added to the pot, the machine was started, the mixture was stirred at a low speed for 30 s, and the process was stopped for 30 s. The machine was turned at high speed, the mixture was stirred for 30 s, and then stirring was stopped for 90 s. In the first 15 s, the blade and the net slurry on the wall of the pot were scraped into the pot with a scraper. The mixture was then stirred at high speed for 1 min.

Fluidity Test

The slurry fluidity test refers to the measurement and evaluation of the fluidity of the mixed slurry by the macroscopic record measurement method after the gelling system is fully mixed with an appropriate amount of diluent. The main purpose is to evaluate the workability of the paste system itself, and good fluidity guarantees workability and pumping. In addition, poor fluidity reduces the durability of the system during its use.

The test standard for paste fluidity of a solid waste cementitious system refers to GB/T 8077-2012 (“Test method for homogeneity of concrete admixtures”). SP, SSP, and FGD are dissolved in water and added to a stirring pan. The stirred slurry is injected into the truncated cone die, and the maximum diameters of the slurry in the two directions perpendicular to each other through vibration flow on the glass plane are measured after lifting. The average value is the fluidity of the paste. The test results are shown in Figure 7.

Mechanistic Analysis

SEM Analysis

The main hydration products of SSP, SP, and FGD are hydrated calcium silicate (C–S–H gel), calcium hydroxide, and AFt, resulting in gel and microcrystalline structures. The hydration reaction of steel slag powder produces tricalcium silicate (C3S) and dicalcium silicate (C2S), and the generated hydroxide ions react with the ore powder to form calcium sulfoaluminate (AFt) crystals. The hydration reaction of ettringite is produced mainly by calcium aluminosilicate decahydrate (CAH10) and dicalcium aluminate octahydrate (C2AH8), resulting in a slender needle-like and plate-like structure. Calcium hydroxide has a hexagonal plate-like structure, and ettringite (AFt) has a slender needle-like structure. SEM images (Figure 13) revealed that a large amount of hydrated calcium silicate gel was produced in the geopolymer gel system and a secondary hydration reaction between Ca(OH)2 and slag occurred to form hydrated calcium silicate gel (C–S–H). The surface of the unhydrated solid waste particles was wrapped by C–S–H gel, the C–S–H gel filled the holes and gaps of the unhydrated solid waste particles, and the overall structure was relatively dense. However, long-term exposure of calcium hydroxide to air and water vapor results in the formation of a more stable CaCO3 structure, which is a carbonation phenomenon and is common in reinforced concrete structures.

Mechanism Response Analysis

The geopolymer ternary cementitious system is composed of SSP, SP, FGD, alkaline activator, and other powder solid waste particles mixed via a hydration reaction. According to previous chemical composition (e.g., XRD, XRF, etc.) analyses, the system mainly contains Al2O3, SiO, CaO, and Fe2O3. After the hydration reaction, a certain gel system should be formed, such as AFt, AFm, and C–S–H. The alkaline activator + Si (Ca) hydration reaction provides later strength. SEM scanning revealed that after 28 days, the above three kinds of dense crystals formed inside the sample. Among them, steel slag powder generates tricalcium silicate (C3S) and dicalcium silicate (C2S) in the early stage, which increases the compressive strength of the early gel system. The hydration reaction mechanism is shown in formula 10, which is also consistent with the theoretical argument that Al2O3 can accelerate the hydration reaction of the system and provide early strength to the system. A large amount of Aft and C–S–H is formed after the reaction of mineral powder with hydroxide ions, which guarantees the later strength of the system. The early strength of the system increases slowly (the Al2O3 content accounts for less of the total system), but the later strength can reach approximately 35 MPa, and the hydration reaction mechanism is shown in formula 12. After the solid waste particles are optimized and compounded, a geopolymer ternary gelling system is formed under the action of admixtures, such as water and alkaline activator.

Conclusions

This project uses cement-based composite materials reinforced with mineral powder, steel slag powder, and desulfurization gypsum as the research objects. The research group developed self-leveling cementitious materials similar to floor plastering mortar by means of theoretical analysis, test analysis, experimental research, and mechanism analysis. The main conclusions are as follows:

(1)The particle sizes of the SSP, SP, and FGD raw materials are concentrated in the range of 5–40 μm, and the content of the three materials with particle sizes less than 0.038 mm is greater than 50%, which are fine-grade minerals. The specific surface area of FGD is approximately 3.40 m2/g. The specific surface area of SP is approximately 2.89 m2/g. The specific surface area of SSP is approximately 1.97 m2/g. XRF and XRD tests and theoretical analysis revealed that both SSP and SP are highly active alkaline substances.
