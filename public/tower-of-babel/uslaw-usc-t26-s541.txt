26 U.S.C. § 541 — Imposition of personal holding company tax
Citation: us:usc:26:541
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section541
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 1 (Normal Taxes And Surtaxes); Subchapter G (Corporations Used To Avoid Income Tax On Shareholders); Part II (Personal Holding Companies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In addition to other taxes imposed by this chapter, there is hereby imposed for each taxable year on the undistributed personal holding company income (as defined in section 545) of every personal holding company (as defined in section 542) a personal holding company tax equal to 20 percent of the undistributed personal holding company income.
