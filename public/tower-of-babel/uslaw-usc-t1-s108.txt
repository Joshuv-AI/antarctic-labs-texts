1 U.S.C. § 108 — Repeal of repealing act
Citation: us:usc:1:108
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title1-section108
Context: United States Code, Title 1 (General Provisions); Chapter 2 (Acts And Resolutions; Formalities Of Enactment; Repeals; Sealing Of Instruments). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whenever an Act is repealed, which repealed a former Act, such former Act shall not thereby be revived, unless it shall be expressly so provided.
