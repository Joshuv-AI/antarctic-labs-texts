25 U.S.C. § 2509 — Regulations
Citation: us:usc:25:2509
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section2509
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 27 (Tribally Controlled School Grants). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to issue regulations relating to the discharge of duties specifically assigned to the Secretary in this chapter. For all other matters relating to the details of planning, developing, implementing, and evaluating grants under this chapter, the Secretary shall not issue regulations.
