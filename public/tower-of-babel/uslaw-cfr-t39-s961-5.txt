39 C.F.R. § 961.5 — (Rule 5) Effect of filing a petition.
Citation: us:cfr:39:961.5
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-961.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 961 (Debt Collection Act Proceedings Against Current Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After receipt and docketing of the employee's petition for a hearing, further collection activity by the Postal Service must cease as required by section 5 of the Debt Collection Act until the petition is resolved by the Hearing Official.
