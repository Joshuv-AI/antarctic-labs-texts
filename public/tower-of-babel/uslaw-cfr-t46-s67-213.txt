46 C.F.R. § 67.213 — Place of filing and recording.
Citation: us:cfr:46:67.213
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-67.213
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 67 (Documentation Of Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) All instruments submitted for filing and recording must be submitted to the National Vessel Documentation Center.
(b) All instruments are recorded at the National Vessel Documentation Center.
