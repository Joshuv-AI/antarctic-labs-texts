19 U.S.C. § 1606 — Seizure; appraisement
Citation: us:usc:19:1606
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section1606
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 4 (Tariff Act Of 1930); Part V (Enforcement Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The appropriate customs officer shall determine the domestic value, at the time and place of appraisement, of any vessel, vehicle, aircraft, merchandise, or baggage seized under the customs laws.
