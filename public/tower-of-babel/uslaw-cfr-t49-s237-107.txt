49 C.F.R. § 237.107 — Conduct of bridge inspections.
Citation: us:cfr:49:237.107
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-237.107
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 237 (Bridge Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Bridge inspections shall be conducted under the direct supervision of a designated railroad bridge inspector, who shall be responsible for the accuracy of the results and the conformity of the inspection to the bridge management program.
