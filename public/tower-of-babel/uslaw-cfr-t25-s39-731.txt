25 C.F.R. § 39.731 — Can transportation time be used as instruction time for day school students?
Citation: us:cfr:25:39.731
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-39.731
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 39 (The Indian School Equalization Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. Transportation time cannot be used as instruction time for day school students in meeting the minimum required hours for academic funding.
