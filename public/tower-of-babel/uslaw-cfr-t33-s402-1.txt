33 C.F.R. § 402.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:33:402.1
Creator: Great Lakes St. Lawrence Seaway Development Corporation, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-402.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 402 (Tariff Of Tolls). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This regulation prescribes the charges to be assessed for the full or partial transit of the St. Lawrence Seaway between Montreal, Quebec, and Lake Erie.
