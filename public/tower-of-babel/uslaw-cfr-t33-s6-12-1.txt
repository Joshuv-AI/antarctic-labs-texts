33 C.F.R. § 6.12-1 — -1 General supervision and control.
Citation: us:cfr:33:6.12-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-6.12-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 6 (Protection And Security Of Vessels, Harbors, And Waterfront Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Captain of the Port may supervise and control the transportation, handling, loading, discharging, stowage, or storage of hazardous materials on board vessels as covered by the regulations in 49 CFR parts 170-189, 46 CFR parts 150-156, 46 CFR parts 146-148 and the regulations governing tank vessels (46 CFR parts 30-39).
