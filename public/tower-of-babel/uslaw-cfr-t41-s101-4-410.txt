41 C.F.R. § 101-4.410 — -4.410 Comparable facilities.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-4.410
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-4.410
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-4 (4—Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Sex In Education Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A recipient may provide separate toilet, locker room, and shower facilities on the basis of sex, but such facilities provided for students of one sex shall be comparable to such facilities provided for students of the other sex.
