10 C.F.R. § 1008.24 — Criminal penalties—failure to publish a system notice.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1008.24
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1008.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1008 (Records Maintained On Individuals (Privacy Act)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subsection (i)(2) of the Act provides that an agency officer or employee who willfully maintains a system of records without publishing a system notice as required by subsection (e)(4) of the Act shall be guilty of a misdemeanor and fined up to $5,000.
