49 U.S.C. § 41765 — Administrative expenses
Citation: us:usc:49:41765
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section41765
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Air Commerce And Safety); Subpart ii (Economic Regulation); Chapter 417 (Operations Of Carriers); Subchapter III (Regional Air Service Incentive Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In carrying out this subchapter, the Secretary shall use funds made available by appropriations to the Department of Transportation for the purpose of administration, in addition to the proceeds of any fees collected under this subchapter, to cover administrative expenses of the Federal credit instrument program under this subchapter.
