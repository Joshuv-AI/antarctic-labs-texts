15 U.S.C. § 1666e — Notification of credit card issuer by seller of return of goods, etc., by obligor; credit for account of obligor
Citation: us:usc:15:1666e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1666e
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 41 (Consumer Credit Protection); Subchapter I (Consumer Credit Cost Disclosure); Part D (Credit Billing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

With respect to any sales transaction where a credit card has been used to obtain credit, where the seller is a person other than the card issuer, and where the seller accepts or allows a return of the goods or forgiveness of a debit for services which were the subject of such sale, the seller shall promptly transmit to the credit card issuer, a credit statement with respect thereto and the credit card issuer shall credit the account of the obligor for the amount of the transaction.
