7 U.S.C. § 6710 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:7:6710
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section6710
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 96 (Global Climate Change). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary for each of the fiscal years 1991 through 1997, to carry out this chapter.
