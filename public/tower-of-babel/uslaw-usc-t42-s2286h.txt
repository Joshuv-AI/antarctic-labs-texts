42 U.S.C. § 2286h — Contract authority subject to appropriations
Citation: us:usc:42:2286h
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2286h
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 23 (Development And Control Of Atomic Energy); Subchapter XVII–A (Defense Nuclear Facilities Safety Board). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The authority of the Board to enter into contracts under this subchapter is effective only to the extent that appropriations (including transfers of appropriations) are provided in advance for such purpose.
