43 U.S.C. § 152 — Restoration of lands previously withdrawn
Citation: us:usc:43:152
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section152
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 6 (Withdrawal From Settlement, Location, Sale, Or Entry). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Where under the law the Secretary of the Interior is authorized or directed to make restoration of lands previously withdrawn he may also restrict the restoration as prescribed in section 151 of this title.
