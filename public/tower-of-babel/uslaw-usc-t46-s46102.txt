46 U.S.C. § 46102 — Quorum
Citation: us:usc:46:46102
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section46102
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part D (Federal Maritime Commission); Chapter 461 (Federal Maritime Commission). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A vacancy or vacancies in the membership of the Federal Maritime Commission do not impair the power of the Commission to execute its functions. The affirmative vote of a majority of the Commissioners serving on the Commission is required to dispose of any matter before the Commission.
