15 U.S.C. § 317 — Appropriations and estimates
Citation: us:usc:15:317
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section317
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 9 (National Weather Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The appropriations for the support of the National Weather Service shall be made with those of the other bureaus of the Department of Commerce, and it shall be the duty of the Secretary of Commerce to prepare future estimates for the National Weather Service which shall be specially developed and extended in the interests of agriculture.
