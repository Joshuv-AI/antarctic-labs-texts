33 C.F.R. § 179.17 — Penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:33:179.17
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-179.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 179 (Defect Notification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each manufacturer who fails to comply with a provision of 46 U.S.C. 4310 or the regulations in this part, is subject to the penalties as prescribed in 46 U.S.C. 4311.
