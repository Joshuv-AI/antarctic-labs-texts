43 C.F.R. § 8.3 — Easements.
Citation: us:cfr:43:8.3
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-8.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 8 (Joint Policies Of The Departments Of The Interior And Of The Army Relative To Reservoir Project Lands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Easements in lieu of fee title may be taken only for lands that meet all of the following conditions:
(a) Lands lying above the storage pool.
(b) Lands in remote portions of the project area.
(c) Lands determined to be of no substantial value for protection or enhancement of fish and wildlife resources, or for public outdoor recreation.
(d) It is to the financial advantage of the Government to take easements in lieu of fee title.
