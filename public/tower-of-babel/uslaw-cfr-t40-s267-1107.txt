40 C.F.R. § 267.1107 — Can a containment building itself be considered secondary containment?
Citation: us:cfr:40:267.1107
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-267.1107
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 267 (Standards For Owners And Operators Of Hazardous Waste Facilities Operating Under A Standardized Permit). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Containment buildings can serve as secondary containment systems for tanks placed within the building under certain conditions.
(a) A containment building can serve as an external liner system for a tank, provided it meets the requirements of § 267.196(a).
(b) The containment building must also meet the requirements of § 267.195(a), (b)(1) and (2) to be considered an acceptable secondary containment system for a tank.
