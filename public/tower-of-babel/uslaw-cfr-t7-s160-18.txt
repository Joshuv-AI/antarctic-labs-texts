7 C.F.R. § 160.18 — Determining the grade of rosin.
Citation: us:cfr:7:160.18
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-160.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 160 (Regulations And Standards For Naval Stores). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The grade of rosin shall be determined by comparing a representative sample, taken and prepared in accordance with the provisions in this part, with the appropriate standard types. The grade shall be the grade designation of the standard type which the sample equals or excels in color, but below the next higher grade.
