5 C.F.R. § 410.202 — Responsibilities for evaluating training.
Citation: us:cfr:5:410.202
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-410.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 410 (Training). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies must evaluate their training programs annually to determine how well such plans and programs contribute to mission accomplishment and meet organizational performance goals.
