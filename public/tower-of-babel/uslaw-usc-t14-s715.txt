14 U.S.C. § 715 — Oceanographic research
Citation: us:usc:14:715
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section715
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 7 (Cooperation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Coast Guard shall conduct such oceanographic research, use such equipment or instruments, and collect and analyze such oceanographic data, in cooperation with other agencies of the Government, or not, as may be in the national interest.
