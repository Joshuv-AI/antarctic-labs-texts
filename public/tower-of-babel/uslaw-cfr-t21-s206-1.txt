21 C.F.R. § 206.1 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:21:206.1
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-206.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 206 (Imprinting Of Solid Oral Dosage Form Drug Products For Human Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to all solid oral dosage form human drug products, including prescription drug products, over-the-counter drug products, biological drug products, and homeopathic drug products, unless otherwise exempted under § 206.7.
