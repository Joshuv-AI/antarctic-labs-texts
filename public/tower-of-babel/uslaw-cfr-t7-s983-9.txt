7 C.F.R. § 983.9 — Confidential data or information.

Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-983.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 983 (Pistachios Grown In California, Arizona, And New Mexico). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Confidential data or information submitted to the committee consists of data or information constituting a trade secret or disclosure of the trade position, financial condition, or business operations of a particular entity or its customers.
