46 C.F.R. § 42.05-27 — -27 Credential.
Citation: us:cfr:46:42.05-27
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-42.05-27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 42 (Domestic And Foreign Voyages By Sea). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in this subchapter, credential means any or all of the following:
(a) Merchant mariner's document.
(b) Merchant mariner's license.
(c) STCW endorsement.
(d) Certificate of registry.
(e) Merchant mariner credential.
