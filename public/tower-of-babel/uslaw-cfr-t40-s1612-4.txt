40 C.F.R. § 1612.4 — Requests for authentication or certification of records.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1612.4
Creator: Chemical Safety And Hazard Investigation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1612.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1612 (Production Of Records In Legal Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The CSB may authenticate or certify records to facilitate their use as evidence. Requests for certified copies should be made to the General Counsel at least 30 days before the date they will be needed. The CSB may charge a certification fee of $5.00 per document.
