16 U.S.C. § 460l–15 — Lease of facilities and lands to non-Federal public bodies
Citation: us:usc:16:460l–15
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section460l–15
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LXIX (Outdoor Recreation Programs); Part C (Water Resources Projects). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

At projects, the construction of which has commenced or been completed as of July 9, 1965, where non-Federal public bodies agree to administer project land and water areas for recreation and fish and wildlife enhancement purposes and to bear the 1 not less than one-half the costs of operation, maintenance, and replacement of existing facilities serving those purposes, such facilities and appropriate project lands may be leased to non-Federal public bodies.
