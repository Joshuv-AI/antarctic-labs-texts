24 C.F.R. § 982.606 — Congregate housing: Who may reside in congregate housing.
Citation: us:cfr:24:982.606
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-982.606
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 982 (Section 8 Tenant-Based Assistance: Housing Choice Voucher Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An elderly person or a person with disabilities may reside in a congregate housing unit.
(b)(1) If approved by the PHA, a family member or live-in aide may reside with the elderly person or person with disabilities.
(2) The PHA must approve a live-in aide if needed as a reasonable accommodation so that the program is readily accessible to and usable by persons with disabilities in accordance with 24 CFR part 8. See § 982.316 concerning occupancy by a live-in aide.
