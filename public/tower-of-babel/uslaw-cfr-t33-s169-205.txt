33 C.F.R. § 169.205 — What types of ships are required to transmit LRIT information (position reports)?
Citation: us:cfr:33:169.205
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-169.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 169 (Ship Reporting Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following ships, while engaged on an international voyage, are required to transmit position reports:
(a) A passenger ship, including high speed passenger craft.
(b) A cargo ship, including high speed craft, of 300 gross tonnage or more.
(c) A mobile offshore drilling unit while underway and not engaged in drilling operations.
