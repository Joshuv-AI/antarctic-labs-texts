7 C.F.R. § 15.143 — Decision where financial assistance affected.
Citation: us:cfr:7:15.143
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-15.143
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 15 (Nondiscrimination). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary shall make any final decision which provides for the suspension or termination of, or the refusal to grant or continue Federal financial assistance, or the imposition of any other sanction available under the regulations in this part or the Act.
