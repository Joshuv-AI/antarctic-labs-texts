48 C.F.R. § 627.304-1 — 627.304-1 General.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 627 (Patents, Data, And Copyrights). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Procurement Executive is the agency head's designee for the purposes of FAR 27.304-1. Questions regarding fact-finding procedures as specified in FAR 27.304-1(a)(4) shall be referred to A/OPE. Determinations proposed to be issued by the Procurement Executive shall be reviewed by the Office of the Legal Adviser.
