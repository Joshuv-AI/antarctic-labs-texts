16 U.S.C. § 838d — Transmission of non-Federal power
Citation: us:usc:16:838d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section838d
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 12G (Pacific Northwest Federal Transmission System). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator shall make available to all utilities on a fair and nondiscriminatory basis, any capacity in the Federal transmission system which he determines to be in excess of the capacity required to transmit electric power generated or acquired by the United States.
