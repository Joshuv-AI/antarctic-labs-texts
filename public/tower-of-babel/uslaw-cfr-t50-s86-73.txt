50 C.F.R. § 86.73 — How long must I operate and maintain a BIG-funded facility, and who is responsible for the cost of facility operation and maintenance?
Citation: us:cfr:50:86.73
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-86.73
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 86 (Boating Infrastructure Grant Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You must operate and maintain a BIG-funded facility for its authorized purpose for its useful life. See §§ 86.3, 86.43(f), and 86.74.
(b) Catastrophic events may shorten the useful life of a BIG-funded facility. If it is not feasible or is cost-prohibitive to repair or replace the BIG-funded facility, you may ask to revise the grant to reduce the useful-life obligation.
(c) You are responsible for the costs of the operation and maintenance of the BIG-funded facility for its useful life, except as allowed at § 86.14(b).
