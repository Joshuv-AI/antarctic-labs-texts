41 C.F.R. § 101-29.220 — -29.220 Market research and analysis.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-29.220
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-29.220
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-29 (29—Federal Product Descriptions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Market research and analysis is a process used to ascertain and analyze the range and quality of available commercial products to determine whether they meet user needs and to identify the market practices of firms engaged in producing, distributing, and supporting the products.
