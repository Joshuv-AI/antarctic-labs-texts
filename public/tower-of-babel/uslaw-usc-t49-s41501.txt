49 U.S.C. § 41501 — Establishing reasonable prices, classifications, rules, practices, and divisions of joint prices for foreign air transportation
Citation: us:usc:49:41501
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section41501
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Air Commerce And Safety); Subpart ii (Economic Regulation); Chapter 415 (Pricing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Every air carrier and foreign air carrier shall establish, comply with, and enforce—
(1)
reasonable prices, classifications, rules, and practices related to foreign air transportation; and
(2)
for joint prices established for foreign air transportation, reasonable divisions of those prices among the participating air carriers or foreign air carriers without unreasonably discriminating against any of those carriers.
