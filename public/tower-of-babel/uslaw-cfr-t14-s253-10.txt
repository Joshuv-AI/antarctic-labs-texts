14 C.F.R. § 253.10 — Notice of contract of carriage choice-of-forum provisions.
Citation: us:cfr:14:253.10
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-253.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 253 (Notice Of Terms Of Contract Of Carriage). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No carrier may impose any contract of carriage provision containing a choice-of-forum clause that attempts to preclude a passenger, or a person who purchases a ticket for air transportation on behalf of a passenger, from bringing a claim against a carrier in any court of competent jurisdiction, including a court within the jurisdiction of that passenger's residence in the United States (provided that the carrier does business within that jurisdiction).
