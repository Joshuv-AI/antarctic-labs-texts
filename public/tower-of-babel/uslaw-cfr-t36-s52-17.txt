36 C.F.R. § 52.17 — Does this part limit the authority of the Director?
Citation: us:cfr:36:52.17
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-52.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 52 (Visitor Experience Improvements Authority Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this part may be construed as limiting the authority of the Director at any time to determine whether to solicit or award a contract, to cancel a solicitation, or to terminate a contract in accordance with its terms.
