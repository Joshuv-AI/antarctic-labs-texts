46 C.F.R. § 130.250 — Mooring and towing equipment for OSVs of less than 100 gross tons.
Citation: us:cfr:46:130.250
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-130.250
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 130 (Vessel Control, And Miscellaneous Equipment And Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each OSV of less than 100 gross tons must be fitted with mooring and towing equipment meeting the applicable requirements for small passenger vessels in § 184.300 of this chapter.
