20 C.F.R. § 259.4 — Authority to conduct investigations.
Citation: us:cfr:20:259.4
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-259.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 259 (Initial Determinations And Appeals From Initial Determinations With Respect To Employer Status And Employee Status). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In performing his or her responsibilities under § 259.1 or § 259.3, the General Counsel or his or her designee shall have the authority and the power to conduct any investigations he deems necessary. In addition, the General Counsel or his or her designee shall have the power to compel, by subpoena, any person, company, corporation, or other entity to produce any records or other documents pertinent to the matter under consideration.
