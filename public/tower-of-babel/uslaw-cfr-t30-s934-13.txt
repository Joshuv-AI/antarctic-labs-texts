30 C.F.R. § 934.13 — State program provisions set aside.
Citation: us:cfr:30:934.13
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-934.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 934 (North Dakota). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

North Dakota regulation NDAC 69-05.2-27-01 is inconsistent with Federal provisions for permitting and bonding of research projects and is set aside under the provisions of section 505(b) of the Surface Mining Control and Reclamation Act of 1977.
