5 C.F.R. § 297.305 — Denial of requests to amend records.
Citation: us:cfr:5:297.305
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-297.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 297 (Privacy Procedures For Personnel Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If the Office or agency system manager decides not to amend the record in the manner sought, the requester should be notified in writing of the reasons for the denial.
(b) The decision letter should also include the requester's right to appeal the denial and the procedures for appealing the denial to the appropriate official.
