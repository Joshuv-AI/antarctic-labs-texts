41 C.F.R. § 128-1.5002-8 — -1.5002-8 Property custodian (PC).
Citation: us:cfr:41:128-1.5002-8
Creator: Chapter 128—Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-128-1.5002-8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 128-1 (1—Introduction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An individual responsible for the immediate physical custody of all personal property under his control and for providing documentation as required on all actions affecting the personal property within his jurisdiction. The designation as PC may or may not correspond to the individual's official job title.
