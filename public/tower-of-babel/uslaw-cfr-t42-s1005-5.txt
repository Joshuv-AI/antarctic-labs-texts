42 C.F.R. § 1005.5 — Ex parte contacts.
Citation: us:cfr:42:1005.5
Creator: Office Of Inspector General-Health Care, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-1005.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 1005 (Appeals Of Exclusions, Civil Money Penalties And Assessments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No party or person (except employees of the ALJ's office) will communicate in any way with the ALJ on any matter at issue in a case, unless on notice and opportunity for all parties to participate. This provision does not prohibit a person or party from inquiring about the status of a case or asking routine questions concerning administrative functions or procedures.
