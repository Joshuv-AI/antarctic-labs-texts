46 C.F.R. § 308.205 — Reporting casualties and filing claims.
Citation: us:cfr:46:308.205
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-308.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 308 (War Risk Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All casualties occurring after insurance under a binder has attached shall be reported promptly to, and all claim documents filed with “Office of Marine Insurance, Maritime Administration, Department of Transportation.”
