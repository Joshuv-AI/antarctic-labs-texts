49 C.F.R. § 10.73 — Payment of fees.
Citation: us:cfr:49:10.73
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-10.73
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 10 (Maintenance Of And Access To Records Pertaining To Individuals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The fees prescribed in this subpart may be paid by check, draft, or postal money order payable to the Treasury of the United States.
