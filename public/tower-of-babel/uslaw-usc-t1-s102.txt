1 U.S.C. § 102 — Resolving clause
Citation: us:usc:1:102
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title1-section102
Context: United States Code, Title 1 (General Provisions); Chapter 2 (Acts And Resolutions; Formalities Of Enactment; Repeals; Sealing Of Instruments). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The resolving clause of all joint resolutions shall be in the following form: “Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled.”
