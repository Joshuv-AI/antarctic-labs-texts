23 C.F.R. § 636.405 — After developing a short list, can I still establish a competitive range?
Citation: us:cfr:23:636.405
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-636.405
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 636 (Design-Build Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, if you have developed a short list of firms, you may still establish a competitive range. The short list is based on qualifications criteria. The competitive range is based on the rating of technical and price proposals.
