7 C.F.R. § 1210.330 — Policy and objective.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1210.330
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Miscellaneous Commodities), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1210.330
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1210 (Watermelon Research And Promotion Plan). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It shall be the policy of the Board to carry out an effective, continuous, and coordinated program of research, development, advertising, and promotion in order to:
(a) Strengthen watermelons' competitive position in the marketplace,
(b) Maintain and expand existing domestic and foreign markets, and
(c) Develop new or improved markets.
