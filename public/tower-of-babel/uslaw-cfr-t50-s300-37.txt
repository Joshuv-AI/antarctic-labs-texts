50 C.F.R. § 300.37 — Radio monitoring.
Citation: us:cfr:50:300.37
Creator: International Fishing And Related Activities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-300.37
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 300 (International Fisheries Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The international distress frequency, 2.182 mHz, and 156.8 mHz (Channel 16, VHF) shall be monitored continuously from the vessel for the purpose of facilitating communication with the fisheries management, surveillance and enforcement authorities of the Parties.
