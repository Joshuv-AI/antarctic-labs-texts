20 C.F.R. § 680.910 — When may supportive services be provided to participants?
Citation: us:cfr:20:680.910
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-680.910
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 680 (Adult And Dislocated Worker Activities Under Title I Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Supportive services may only be provided to individuals who are:
(1) Participating in career or training services as defined in WIOA secs. 134(c)(2) and (3); and
(2) Unable to obtain supportive services through other programs providing such services.
(b) Supportive services only may be provided when they are necessary to enable individuals to participate in career service or training activities.
