38 C.F.R. § 3.112 — Fractions of one cent.
Citation: us:cfr:38:3.112
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-3.112
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 3 (Adjudication). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In all cases where the amount to be paid under any award involves a fraction of a cent, the fractional part will be excluded.
