46 C.F.R. § 119.720 — Nonmetallic piping materials.
Citation: us:cfr:46:119.720
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-119.720
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 119 (Machinery Installation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nonmetallic piping materials, including nonmetallic flexible hose assemblies, must meet the requirements of § 56.60-25 in subchapter F of this chapter.
