28 C.F.R. § 505.5 — Waiver of fee by Warden.
Citation: us:cfr:28:505.5
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-505.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 505 (Cost Of Incarceration Fee). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Warden may reduce or waive the fee if the inmate establishes that:
(a) He or she is not able and, even with the use of a reasonable installment schedule, is not likely to become able to pay all or part of the fee, or
(b) Imposition of a fee would unduly burden the inmate's dependents.
