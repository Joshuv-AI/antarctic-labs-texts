42 C.F.R. § 436.1003 — beneficiaries overcoming certain conditions of eligibility.
Citation: us:cfr:42:436.1003
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-436.1003
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 436 (Eligibility In Guam, Puerto Rico, And The Virgin Islands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

FFP is available for a temporary period specified in the State plan in expenditures for services provided to beneficiaries who are overcoming certain eligibility conditions, including blindness, disability, continued absence or incapacity of a parent, or unemployment of a parent.
