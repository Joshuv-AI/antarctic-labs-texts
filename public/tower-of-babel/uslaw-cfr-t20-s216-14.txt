20 C.F.R. § 216.14 — Regular non-railroad employment that will not break a current connection.
Citation: us:cfr:20:216.14
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-216.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 216 (Eligibility For An Annuity). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Regular non-railroad employment will not break an employee's current connection if it is performed during the 30-month period described in § 216.13(b), in or after the month the annuity begins, or in the month the employee dies.
