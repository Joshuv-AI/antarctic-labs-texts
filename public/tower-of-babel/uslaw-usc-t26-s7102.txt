26 U.S.C. § 7102 — Single bond in lieu of multiple bonds
Citation: us:usc:26:7102
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section7102
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 73 (Bonds). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In any case in which two or more bonds are required or authorized, the Secretary may provide for the acceptance of a single bond complying with the requirements for which the several bonds are required or authorized.
