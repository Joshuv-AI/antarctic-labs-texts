47 C.F.R. § 500.1 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:47:500.1
Creator: The First Responder Network Authority
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-500.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 500 (Review And Approval Of Fees Proposed By The First Responder Network Authority (Firstnet)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Sections 500.2 through 500.5 of this part implement 47 U.S.C. 1428(c) as codified pursuant to the Middle Class Tax Relief and Job Creation Act of 2012 (Pub. L. 112-96, Title VI, 126 Stat. 256 (codified at 47 U.S.C. 1401 et seq.)) (Act), which requires the National Telecommunications and Information Administration (NTIA) to annually review fees the First Responder Network Authority (FirstNet) proposes to assess pursuant to 47 U.S.C. 1428(a).
