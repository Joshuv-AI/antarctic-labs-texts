10 C.F.R. § 501.124 — Decision and order.
Citation: us:cfr:10:501.124
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-501.124
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 501 (Administrative Procedures And Sanctions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) OFE will issue an order granting or denying the petition for a stay upon consideration of the request and other relevant information received or obtained during the proceeding.
(b) OFE will include in the order a brief written statement setting forth the relevant facts and the basis of the decision, including any appropriate terms and conditions of the stay.
