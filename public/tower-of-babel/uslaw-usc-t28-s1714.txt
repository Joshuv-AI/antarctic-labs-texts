28 U.S.C. § 1714 — Protection against discrimination based on geographic location
Citation: us:usc:28:1714
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1714
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part V (Procedure); Chapter 114 (Class Actions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The court may not approve a proposed settlement that provides for the payment of greater sums to some class members than to others solely on the basis that the class members to whom the greater sums are to be paid are located in closer geographic proximity to the court.
