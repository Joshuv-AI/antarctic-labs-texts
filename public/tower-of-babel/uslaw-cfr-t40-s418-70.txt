40 C.F.R. § 418.70 — Applicability; description of the mixed and blend fertilizer production subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:418.70
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-418.70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 418 (Fertilizer Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges resulting from the production of mixed fertilizer and blend fertilizer.
