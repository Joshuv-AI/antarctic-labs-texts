48 C.F.R. § 1505.202 — 1505.202 Exceptions.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1505.202
Creator: Chapter 15—Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1505.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1505 (Publicizing Contract Actions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Contracting Officer need not submit the notice required by FAR 5.201 when the Contracting Officer determines in writing that the contract is for the services of experts for use in preparing or prosecuting a civil or criminal action under the Superfund Amendments and Reauthorization Act of 1986.
