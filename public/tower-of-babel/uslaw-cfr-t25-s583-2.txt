25 C.F.R. § 583.2 — Who may appeal the approval or disapproval of a management contract or amendment to a management contract?
Citation: us:cfr:25:583.2
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-583.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 583 (Appeals From Approvals Or Disapprovals Of Management Contracts Or Amendments To Management Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Only a party to the management contract or amendment thereto approved or disapproved by the Chair may appeal.
