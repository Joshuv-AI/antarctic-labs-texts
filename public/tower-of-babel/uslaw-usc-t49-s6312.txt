49 U.S.C. § 6312 — Transportation statistics annual report
Citation: us:usc:49:6312
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section6312
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Chapter 63 (Bureau Of Transportation Statistics). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director shall submit to the President and Congress a transportation statistics annual report, which shall include—
(1)
information on the progress of the Director in carrying out the duties described in section 6302(b)(3)(B);
(2)
documentation of the methods used to obtain and ensure the quality of the statistics presented in the report; and
(3)
any recommendations of the Director for improving transportation statistical information.
