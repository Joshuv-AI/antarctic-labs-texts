30 C.F.R. § 14.6 — Issuance of approval.
Citation: us:cfr:30:14.6
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-14.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 14 (Requirements For The Approval Of Flame-Resistant Conveyor Belts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) MSHA will issue an approval or notice of the reasons for denying approval after completing the evaluation and testing provided in this part.
(b) An applicant must not advertise or otherwise represent a conveyor belt as approved until MSHA has issued an approval.
