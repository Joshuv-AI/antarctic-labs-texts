49 C.F.R. § 850.20 — Cause or probable cause determinations from Board investigation.
Citation: us:cfr:49:850.20
Creator: National Transportation Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-850.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 850 (Coast Guard—National Transportation Safety Board Marine Casualty Investigations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After an investigation conducted by the Board under § 850.15, the Board determines cause or probable cause and issues a report of that determination.
