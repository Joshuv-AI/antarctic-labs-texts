4 C.F.R. § 27.3 — The General Counsel.
Citation: us:cfr:4:27.3
Creator: Government Accountability Office
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-4/section-27.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 4 (Accounts), Part 27 (Government Accountability Office Personnel Appeals Board; Organization). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Chair shall select an individual and the Comptroller General shall appoint the individual selected by the Chair to serve as the General Counsel of the Board. The General Counsel, at the request of the Board, shall investigate matters under the jurisdiction of the Board, and otherwise assist the Board in carrying out its functions.
