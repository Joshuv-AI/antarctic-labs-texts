17 C.F.R. § 204.56 — Administrative charges.
Citation: us:cfr:17:204.56
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-204.56
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 204 (Rules Relating To Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To the extent permitted by law, all administrative charges incurred in connection with the referral of the debts for tax refund offset will be assessed on the debt and thus increase the amount of the offset.
