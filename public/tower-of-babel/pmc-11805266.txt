Excision of wound healing

Before any wounds will create, the animals will be put to sleep using the open-mask approach and anesthetic ether. The central dorsal thoracic region of the anesthetized rats will be cleaned of hair and disinfected with 70% alcohol. The entire thickness of the marked area will be removed, resulting in a wound that is about 2 cm. The wound will be exposed to the open air without any clothing. This model will keep track of the time it takes for a wound to contract and close. The level of wound compression will be determined at regular intervals following the improvement of the injury.

Percentage of time for epithelialization and wound contraction

Following the formation of the wound, measurements will be made for a total of 14 days, monitoring the wound margin every 2 days using a millimeter-scale translucent graph paper. Every 2 days, the healed area will be determined by deducting the initial wound area from the region. The percentage of wound contraction will used to depict contraction, and the time it took for epithelialization to occur after full healing will be noted.[1]
