9 C.F.R. § 145.9 — Terminology and classification; hatcheries and dealers.
Citation: us:cfr:9:145.9
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-145.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 145 (National Poultry Improvement Plan For Breeding Poultry). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Participating hatcheries and dealers shall be designated as “National Plan Hatchery” and “National Plan Dealer”, respectively. All Official State Agencies shall be notified by the Service of additions, withdrawals, and changes in classification.
