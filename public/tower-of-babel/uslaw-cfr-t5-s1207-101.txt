5 C.F.R. § 1207.101 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1207.101
Creator: Merit Systems Protection Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1207.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1207 (Enforcement Of Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Disability In Programs Or Activities Conducted By The Merit Systems Protection Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to effectuate section 119 of the Rehabilitation, Comprehensive Services, and Developmental Disabilities Amendments of 1978, which amended section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973 to prohibit discrimination on the basis of disability in programs or activities conducted by Executive agencies or the United States Postal Service.
