2 C.F.R. § 1120.300 — Purpose of general terms and conditions.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1120.300
Creator: Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1120.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1120 (Award Format For Dod Grants And Cooperative Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The general terms and conditions comprise the portion of the award with requirements that apply to a class of awards (e.g., awards under a particular program or type of program activity, such as research or education, or for a class of recipients, such as for-profit entities).
