9 C.F.R. § 351.15 — Reports of violations.
Citation: us:cfr:9:351.15
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-351.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 351 (Certification Of Technical Animal Fats For Export). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Inspectors shall report to the circuit supervisor any apparent violations of the regulations in this part or the Federal Meat Inspection Act or regulations thereunder (subchapter A of this chapter) which occur at certified plants, or elsewhere, within their knowledge. The circuit supervisor shall report such actions to the Administrator through appropriate channels.
