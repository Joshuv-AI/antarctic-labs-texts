40 C.F.R. § 86.1840-01 — -01 Special test procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:40:86.1840-01
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-86.1840-01
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 86 (Control Of Emissions From New And In-Use Highway Vehicles And Engines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Provisions for special test procedures apply as described in 40 CFR 1065.10 and 1066.10. For example, manufacturers must propose a procedure for EPA's review and advance approval for testing and certifying vehicles equipped with periodically regenerating aftertreatment devices, including sufficient documentation and data for EPA to fully evaluate the request.
