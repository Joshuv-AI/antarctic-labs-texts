30 U.S.C. § 1731 — Application of subchapter
Citation: us:usc:30:1731
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section1731
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 29 (Oil And Gas Royalty Management); Subchapter II (States And Indian Tribes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This subchapter shall apply only with respect to oil and gas leases on Federal lands or Indian lands. Nothing in this subchapter shall be construed to apply to any lease on the Outer Continental Shelf.
