ETC Tiger Pipeline, LLC; Notice of Schedule for the Preparation 
of an Environmental Assessment for the Franklin Farms Project

    On July 1, 2026, ETC Tiger Pipeline, LLC (ETC Tiger) filed an 
application in Docket No. CP26-61-000 requesting a Certificate of 
Public Convenience and Necessity pursuant to Section 7c of the Natural 
Gas Act to construct and operate certain natural gas facilities. The 
proposed project, known as the Franklin Farms Project (Project), would 
enable ETC Tiger to provide Entergy Louisiana, LLC with 250,000 
dekatherms per day of firm transportation service mainline capacity and 
1,000,000 dekatherms per day of capacity on a newly constructed lateral 
for a new artificial intelligence data center campus in Richland 
Parish, Louisiana.
    On July 16, 2026, the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission 
(Commission or FERC) issued its Notice of Application for the Project. 
Among other things, that notice alerted agencies issuing federal 
authorizations of the requirement to complete all necessary reviews and 
to reach a final decision on a request for a federal authorization 
within 90 days of the date of issuance of the Commission staff's 
environmental document for the Project.
    This notice identifies Commission staff's intention to prepare an 
environmental assessment (EA) for the Project and the planned schedule 
for the completion of the environmental review.\1\ The EA will be 
issued for a 30-day comment period.

Project Description

    ETC Tiger is proposing to construct and operate the following 
facilities in Richland Parish, Louisiana:
     approximately 13.2 miles of new 36-inch-diameter 
greenfield pipeline (Franklin Farms Lateral);
     approximately 1.75 miles of 20-inch-diameter pipeline 
lateral (Franklin Farms Trio Farm Lateral);
     the Franklin Farms, Bee Bayou, and Trio Farm Delivery 
Meter Stations;
     the Midcontinent Express, Gulf South, and Gulf Crossing 
Receipt Meter Stations and the Gulf Run Bi-directional Meter Station;
     the Franklin Farms Interconnect; and
     three new mainline valves and other related appurtenant 
facilities.

Background

    On August 25, 2026, the Commission issued a Notice of Scoping 
Period Requesting Comments on Environmental Issues for the Proposed 
Franklin Farms Project (Notice of Scoping). The Notice of Scoping was 
sent to affected landowners; federal, state, and local government 
agencies; elected officials; Native American tribes; environmental and 
public interest groups; other interested parties; local libraries, and 
media outlets. To date, the Commission has received comments from the 
Sierra Club, one individual, the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency, 
and Restore Explicit Symmetry To Our Ravaged Earth (RESTORE). The 
primary issues raised by the Sierra Club are concerns regarding 
alternatives, adverse effects on land use, water resources and water 
quality, wetlands, wildlife habitats, migratory birds, endangered 
species, soil erosion, air quality, and noise. The individual filed a 
comment opposed to the project and raised concerns about increased 
energy costs consumers. The U.S. Environmental Protection Agency 
recommended that the environmental document address water quality, 
groundwater, National

[[Page 67831]]

Pollutant Discharge Elimination System permit coverage, ambient air 
conditions and air quality effects, and solid and hazardous waste 
generated. RESTORE filed comments regarding concerns of geological 
hazards impacting the pipeline and related impacts from the new Data 
Center in the project area. All substantive comments will be addressed 
in the EA.
