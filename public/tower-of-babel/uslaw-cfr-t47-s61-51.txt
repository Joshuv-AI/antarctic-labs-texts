47 C.F.R. § 61.51 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:47:61.51
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-61.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 61 (Tariffs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules in this subpart apply to tariffs filed by issuing carriers, with the exception of the informational tariffs filed pursuant to 47 U.S.C. 226(h)(1)(A), unless otherwise noted.
