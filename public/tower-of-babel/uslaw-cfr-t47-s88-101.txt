47 C.F.R. § 88.101 — Transmitter power.
Citation: us:cfr:47:88.101
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-88.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 88 (Uncrewed Aircraft System Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Transmitters operating in the 5030-5091 MHz band must comply with the transmitter output power specified in technical standard RTCA DO-362A (incorporated by reference, see § 88.117) section 2.2.1.6.1 and associated subsections.
