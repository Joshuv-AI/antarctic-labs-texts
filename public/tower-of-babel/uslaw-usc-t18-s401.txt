18 U.S.C. § 401 — Power of court
Citation: us:usc:18:401
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section401
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 21 (Contempts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A court of the United States shall have power to punish by fine or imprisonment, or both, at its discretion, such contempt of its authority, and none other, as—
(1)
Misbehavior of any person in its presence or so near thereto as to obstruct the administration of justice;
(2)
Misbehavior of any of its officers in their official transactions;
(3)
Disobedience or resistance to its lawful writ, process, order, rule, decree, or command.
