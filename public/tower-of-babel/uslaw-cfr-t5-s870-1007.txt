5 C.F.R. § 870.1007 — Termination and conversion.
Citation: us:cfr:5:870.1007
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-870.1007
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 870 (Federal Employees' Group Life Insurance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Insurance under this subpart terminates 12 months after hostage status ends, unless the individual cancels the insurance earlier.
(b) Insured individuals whose coverage terminates are eligible for the 31-day extension of coverage and conversion as set forth in subpart F of this part, unless the individual cancelled the coverage.
