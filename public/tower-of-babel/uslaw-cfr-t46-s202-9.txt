46 C.F.R. § 202.9 — Decisions by the Secretary of Transportation.
Citation: us:cfr:46:202.9
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-202.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 202 (Procedures Relating To Review By Secretary Of Transportation Of Actions By Maritime Subsidy Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Decisions of the Secretary will be reached in accordance with applicable law and the evidence. Upon the determination of a case taken under review by the Secretary, a written decision and opinion which states the Secretary's conclusions and an explanation thereof will be issued.
