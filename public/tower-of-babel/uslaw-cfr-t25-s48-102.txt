25 C.F.R. § 48.102 — With whom may the Director enter into a lease?
Citation: us:cfr:25:48.102
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-48.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 48 (Leases Of Land Or Facilities Of Bureau-Operated Schools And Fundraising Activities At Bureau-Operated Schools). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director or designee may lease to public or private persons or entities who meet the requirements of this part that are applicable to leasing activities.
