28 C.F.R. § 80.9 — No oral opinion.
Citation: us:cfr:28:80.9
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-80.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 80 (Foreign Corrupt Practices Act Opinion Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No oral clearance, release or other statement purporting to limit the enforcement discretion of the Department of Justice may be given. The requesting issuer or domestic concern may rely only upon a written FCPA Opinion letter signed by the Attorney General or his designee.
