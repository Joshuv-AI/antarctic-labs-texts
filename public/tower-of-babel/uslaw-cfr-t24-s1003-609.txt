24 C.F.R. § 1003.609 — Housing counseling.
Citation: us:cfr:24:1003.609
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-1003.609
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 1003 (Community Development Block Grants For Indian Tribes And Alaska Native Villages). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Housing counseling, as defined in 24 CFR 5.100, that is funded with or provided in connection with ICDBG funds must be carried out in accordance with 24 CFR 5.111. Housing counseling conducted in connection with the ICDBG program may only be conducted by individuals who are HUD-certified in accordance with 24 CFR part 214, subpart F.
