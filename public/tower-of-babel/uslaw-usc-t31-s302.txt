31 U.S.C. § 302 — Treasury of the United States
Citation: us:usc:31:302
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section302
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 3 (Department Of The Treasury); Subchapter I (Organization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States Government has a Treasury of the United States. The Treasury is in the Department of the Treasury.
