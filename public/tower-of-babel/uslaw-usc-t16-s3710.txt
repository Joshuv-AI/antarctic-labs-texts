16 U.S.C. § 3710 — Limitation on authority
Citation: us:usc:16:3710
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section3710
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 57 (National Fish And Wildlife Foundation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter authorizes the Foundation to perform any function the exclusive authority for which is provided to the National Park Foundation by subchapter II of chapter 1011 of title 54.
