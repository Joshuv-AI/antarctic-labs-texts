46 C.F.R. § 193.01-1 — -1 General; preemptive effect.
Citation: us:cfr:46:193.01-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-193.01-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 193 (Fire Protection Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The provisions of this part shall apply to all vessels other than non-self-propelled vessels of less than 300 gross tons.
(b) Non-self-propelled vessels of less than 300 gross tons shall not be subject to the provisions of this part, except as provided otherwise by §§ 193.01-5 and 193.50-1.
(c) The regulations in this part have preemptive effect over State or local regulations in the same field.
