29 U.S.C. § 2653 — Encouragement of more generous leave policies
Citation: us:usc:29:2653
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section2653
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 28 (Family And Medical Leave); Subchapter III (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this Act or any amendment made by this Act shall be construed to discourage employers from adopting or retaining leave policies more generous than any policies that comply with the requirements under this Act or any amendment made by this Act.
