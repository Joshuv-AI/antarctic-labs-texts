52 U.S.C. § 20509 — Designation of chief State election official
Citation: us:usc:52:20509
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title52-section20509
Context: United States Code, Title 52 (Voting And Elections); Chapter 205 (National Voter Registration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each State shall designate a State officer or employee as the chief State election official to be responsible for coordination of State responsibilities under this chapter.
