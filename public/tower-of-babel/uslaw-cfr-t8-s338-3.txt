8 C.F.R. § 338.3 — Delivery of certificates.
Citation: us:cfr:8:338.3
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-338.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 338 (Certificate Of Naturalization). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No Certificate of Naturalization will be delivered in any case in which the naturalized person has not surrendered his or her Permanent Resident Card to USCIS. Upon a finding that the card is destroyed or otherwise unavailable, USCIS may waive the surrender of the card and the Certificate of Naturalization shall then be delivered to the naturalized person.
