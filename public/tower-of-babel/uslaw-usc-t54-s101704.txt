54 U.S.C. § 101704 — Reimbursable agreements

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 1017 (Financial Agreements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In General.—
In carrying out work under reimbursable agreements with any State, local, or tribal government, the Secretary, without regard to any provision of law or a regulation—
(1)
may record obligations against accounts receivable from those governments; and
(2)
shall credit amounts received from those governments to the appropriate account.
(b) When Amounts Shall Be Credited.—
Amounts shall be credited within 90 days of the date of the original request by the Service for payment.
