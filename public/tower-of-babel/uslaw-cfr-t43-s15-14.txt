43 C.F.R. § 15.14 — Applicability of laws.
Citation: us:cfr:43:15.14
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-15.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 15 (Key Largo Coral Reef Preserve). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In areas to which this part pertains all Federal Acts shall be enforced insofar as they are applicable, and the laws and regulations of the State of Florida shall be invoked and enforced in accordance with the Act of June 25, 1948 (62 Stat. 686; 18 U.S.C. 13)
