32 C.F.R. § 2402.12 — Disclaimer.
Citation: us:cfr:32:2402.12
Creator: Office Of Science And Technology Policy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-2402.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 2402 (Regulations Implementing The Freedom Of Information Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this part shall be construed to entitle any person, as a right, to any service or to the disclosure of any record to which such person is not entitled under the FOIA.
