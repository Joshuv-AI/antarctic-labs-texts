29 C.F.R. § 452.115 — Distribution of ballots.
Citation: us:cfr:29:452.115
Creator: Office Of Labor-Management Standards, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-452.115
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 452 (General Statement Concerning The Election Provisions Of The Labor-Management Reporting And Disclosure Act Of 1959). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

So long as secrecy of the ballot is maintained, there is no restriction on how the ballots are distributed to the voters. Any method which actually provides each eligible voter with one blank ballot would be in conformance with the law.
