9 C.F.R. § 123.1 — Scope and applicability of rules of practice.
Citation: us:cfr:9:123.1
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-123.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 123 (Rules Of Practice Governing Proceedings Under The Virus-Serum-Toxin Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Uniform Rules of Practice for the Department of Agriculture promulgated in subpart H of part 1, subtitle A, title 7, Code of Federal Regulations, are the Rules of Practice applicable to adjudicatory, administrative proceedings under the Virus-Serum-Toxin Act.
