22 C.F.R. § 204.23 — Payment to A.I.D. of excess amounts received by the lender of any assignee.
Citation: us:cfr:22:204.23
Creator: Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-204.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 204 (Housing Guaranty Standard Terms And Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Lender or Assignee shall, as a result of A.I.D. paying compensation under this Guaranty, receive an excess payment, it shall refund the excess to A.I.D.
