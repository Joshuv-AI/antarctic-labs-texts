33 C.F.R. § 45.2 — Records of enlistment of former service members.
Citation: us:cfr:33:45.2
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-45.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 45 (Enlistment Of Personnel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Former members who have any questions about their service or who need information regarding their service should contact the nearest Coast Guard Recruiting Office or Coast Guard Recruiting Center, 4200 Wilson Boulevard, Suite 450, Arlington, VA 20203.
