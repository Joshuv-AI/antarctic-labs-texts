43 U.S.C. § 984 — Legal subdivisions mostly wet and unfit for cultivation
Citation: us:usc:43:984
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section984
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 23 (Grants Of Swamp And Overflowed Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In making out lists and plats of the lands aforesaid all legal subdivisions, the greater part whereof is wet and unfit for cultivation, shall be included in said lists and plats, but when the greater part of a subdivision is not of that character, the whole of it shall be excluded therefrom.
