24 C.F.R. § 60.122 — Use of Federal funds.
Citation: us:cfr:24:60.122
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-60.122
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 60 (Protection Of Human Subjects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Federal funds administered by a Federal department or agency may not be expended for research involving human subjects unless the requirements of this policy have been satisfied.
