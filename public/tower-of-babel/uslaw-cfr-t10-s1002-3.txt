10 C.F.R. § 1002.3 — Custody of official seal and distinguishing flags.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1002.3
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1002.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1002 (Official Seal And Distinguishing Flag). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary or his designee shall:
(a) Have custody of:
(1) The official seal and prototypes thereof, and masters, molds, dies, and all other means of producing replicas, reproductions, and embossing seals; and
(2) Production, inventory and loan records relating to items specified in paragraph (a)(1) of this section; and
(b) Have custody of distinguishing flags, and be responsible for production, inventory, and loan records thereof.
