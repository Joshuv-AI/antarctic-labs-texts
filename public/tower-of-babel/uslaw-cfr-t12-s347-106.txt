12 C.F.R. § 347.106 — Going concerns.
Citation: us:cfr:12:347.106
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-347.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 347 (International Banking). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Going concerns. If a bank acquires an equity interest in a foreign organization that is a going concern, no more than 5 percent of either the consolidated assets or revenues of the foreign organization may be attributable to activities that are not permissible under § 347.105(b).
