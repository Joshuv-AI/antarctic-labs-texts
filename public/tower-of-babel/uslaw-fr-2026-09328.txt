Regulatory Issue Summary: Combined License Review Performance and 
Reporting

AGENCY: Nuclear Regulatory Commission.

ACTION: Final guide; issuance.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) is issuing 
Regulatory Issue Summary (RIS) 2026-04, ``Regulatory Issue Summary: 
Combined License Review Performance and Reporting.'' This RIS discusses 
the performance and reporting requirements for a combined license 
application review in accordance with the requirements in section 207 
of the Accelerating Deployment of Versatile, Advanced Nuclear for Clean 
Energy Act of 2024 (ADVANCE Act).

DATES: The RIS is available as of May 12, 2026.
