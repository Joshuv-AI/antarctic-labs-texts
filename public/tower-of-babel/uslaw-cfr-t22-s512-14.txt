22 C.F.R. § 512.14 — Suspending or terminating collection action.
Citation: us:cfr:22:512.14
Creator: United States Agency For Global Media
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-512.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 512 (Collection Of Debts Under The Debt Collection Act Of 1982). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The suspension or termination of collection action shall be made in accordance with the standards set forth in part 104 of the Federal Claims Collection Standard (4 CFR part 104).
