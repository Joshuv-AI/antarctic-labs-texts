33 C.F.R. § 67.40-10 — -10 Communication with owner.
Citation: us:cfr:33:67.40-10
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-67.40-10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 67 (Aids To Navigation On Artificial Islands And Fixed Structures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Communication with the owners of private aids to navigation by the District Commander shall be addressed to their usual or last known place of business, or to their local representative, if any. Communication shall be by the method considered appropriate for the circumstances.
