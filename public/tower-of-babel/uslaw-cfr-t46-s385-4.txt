46 C.F.R. § 385.4 — Authority.
Citation: us:cfr:46:385.4
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-385.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 385 (Research And Development Grant And Cooperative Agreements Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The MarAd assistance regulations are issued pursuant to section 204(b) of the Merchant Marine Act, 1936, as amended (46 U.S.C. 1114(b)), and pursuant to delegation of authority by the Secretary of Transportation to the Maritime Administrator, Department of Transportation.
