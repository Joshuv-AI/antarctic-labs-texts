49 C.F.R. § 260.49 — Avoiding defaults.

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-260.49
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 260 (Regulations Governing Loans And Loan Guarantees Under The Railroad Rehabilitation And Improvement Financing Program). Borrowers are encouraged to contact the Administrator prior to the occurrence of an event of default to explore possible avenues for avoiding such an occurrence.
