7 C.F.R. § 501.15 — Exceptions.
Citation: us:cfr:7:501.15
Creator: Agricultural Research Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-501.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 501 (Conduct On U.S. Meat Animal Research Center, Clay Center, Nebraska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator, Agricultural Research Service, may in individual cases make prior, written exceptions to the rules and regulations in this part if he determines it to be not adverse to the public interest.
