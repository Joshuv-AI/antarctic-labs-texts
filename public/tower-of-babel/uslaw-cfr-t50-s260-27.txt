50 C.F.R. § 260.27 — Surrender of license.
Citation: us:cfr:50:260.27
Creator: National Marine Fisheries Service, National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-260.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 260 (Inspection And Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon suspension, revocation and/or termination of the services of a licensed inspector and/or licensed sampler, or in the case of an expired license, the licensee shall surrender their license to the Agency.
