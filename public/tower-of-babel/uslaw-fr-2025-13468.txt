Petition for Exemption

    Docket No.: FAA-2025-1060.
    Petitioner: Jeffrey Jacob.
    Section of 14 CFR Affected: Sec.  65.75(a).
    Description of Relief Sought: Jeffrey Jacob is petitioning for an 
exemption from Title 14 Code of Federal Regulations Sec.  65.75(a) to 
replace the written test for the Airframe and Powerplant (A&P) Mechanic 
certification with an alternative evaluation method due to learning 
differences and test-related anxiety. He proposes a comprehensive oral 
and practical assessment that evaluates understanding and safety 
awareness.
