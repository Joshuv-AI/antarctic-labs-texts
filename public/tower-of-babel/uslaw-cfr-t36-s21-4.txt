36 C.F.R. § 21.4 — Registration of physicians.
Citation: us:cfr:36:21.4
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-21.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 21 (Hot Springs National Park; Bathhouse Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Physicians desiring to prescribe the thermal waters of Hot Springs National Park must first be registered at the office of the Superintendent. Any physician may make application for registration to the Superintendent. To maintain registered status, reapplication is required triannually.
