DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Office of the Secretary


Senior Executive Service; Appointment of Members to the 
Performance Review Board

AGENCY: Office of the Secretary.

ACTION: Appointment of members of the Performance Review Board.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Title 5 U.S.C. 4314(c)(4) provides that Notice of the 
Appointment of the individual to serve as a member of the Performance 
Review Board of the Senior Executive Service shall be published in the 
Federal Register.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Tania Burkley, Chief, Division of 
Executive Resources, Room C2318, U.S. Department of Labor, Frances 
Perkins Building, 200 Constitution Ave, NW, Washington, D.C. 20210, 
telephone: (202) 693-7638.
