45 C.F.R. § 12.4 — Limitations.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 12 (Disposal And Utilization Of Surplus Real Property For Public Health Purposes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Surplus property transferred pursuant to this part will be disposed of on an “as is, where is,” basis without warranty of any kind.
(b) Unless excepted by the General Services Administrator in his assignment, mineral rights will be conveyed together with the surface rights.
