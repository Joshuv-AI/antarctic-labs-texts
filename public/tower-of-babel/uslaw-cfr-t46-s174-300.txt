46 C.F.R. § 174.300 — Specific applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:46:174.300
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-174.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 174 (Special Rules Pertaining To Specific Vessel Types). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to each self-propelled hopper dredge for which a working freeboard assignment is being sought under part 44, subpart C, of this chapter.
