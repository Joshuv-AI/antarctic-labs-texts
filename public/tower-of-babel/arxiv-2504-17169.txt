The critical power of short pulse initial data on the global existence or blowup of smooth solutions to 3-D semilinear Klein-Gordon equations
Shen Jindou, Yin Huicheng 1. Department of Mathematics, Nanjing University, Nanjing 210093, China.2. School of Mathematical Sciences and Mathematical Institute,Nanjing Normal University, Nanjing 210023, China.

Abstract
It is well-known that there are global small data smooth solutions for the 3-D semilinear Klein-Gordon equations □ ​ u + u = F ⁡ ( u , ∂ u ) \square u+u=F(u,{\partial u}) with cubic nonlinearities. However, for the short pulse initial data ( u , ∂ t u ) ​ ( 0 , x ) = ( δ ν + 1 ​ u 0 ​ ( x δ ) , δ ν ​ u 1 ​ ( x δ ) ) (u,\partial_{t}u)(0,x)=({\delta^{\nu+1}}{u_{0}}({\frac{x}{\delta}}),{\delta^{\nu}}{u_{1}}({\frac{x}{\delta}})) with ν ∈ ℝ \nu\in\mathbb{R} and ( u 0 , u 1 ) ∈ C 0 ∞ ​ ( ℝ ) (u_{0},u_{1})\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}) , which are a class of large initial data, we establish that when ν ≤ − 1 2 \nu\leq-\frac{1}{2} , the solution u u can blow up in finite time for some suitable choices of ( u 0 , u 1 ) (u_{0},u_{1}) and cubic nonlinearity F ⁡ ( u , ∂ u ) F(u,{\partial u}) ; when ν > − 1 2 \nu>-\frac{1}{2} , the smooth solution u u exists globally. Therefore, ν = − 1 2 \nu=-\frac{1}{2} is just the critical power corresponding to the global existence or blowup of smooth short pulse solutions for the cubic semilinear Klein-Gordon equations.

Similarly,

(4.9)
