21 C.F.R. § 514.116 — Notice of withdrawal of approval of application.
Citation: us:cfr:21:514.116
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-514.116
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 514 (New Animal Drug Applications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When an approval of an application submitted pursuant to section 512 of the act is withdrawn by the Commissioner, he will give appropriate public notice of such action by publication in the Federal Register.
