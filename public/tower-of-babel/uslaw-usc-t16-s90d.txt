16 U.S.C. § 90d — Distributive share of counties of receipts for schools and roads unaffected
Citation: us:usc:16:90d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section90d
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter X (North Cascades National Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The distributive shares of the respective counties of receipts from the national forests from which the national park and recreation areas are created, as paid under the provisions of section 500 of this title, shall not be affected by the elimination of lands from such national forests by the enactment of this subchapter.
