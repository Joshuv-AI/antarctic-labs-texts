10 C.F.R. § 708.37 — Reimbursement of costs and expenses.
Citation: us:cfr:10:708.37
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-708.37
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 708 (Doe Contractor Employee Protection Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a complaint is denied by a final agency decision, the complainant will not be reimbursed for the costs and expenses incurred in pursuing the complaint.
