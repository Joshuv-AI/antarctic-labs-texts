43 C.F.R. § 15.6 — Markers.
Citation: us:cfr:43:15.6
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-15.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 15 (Key Largo Coral Reef Preserve). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person shall willfully mark, deface or injure in any way, or displace, remove or tamper with any Preserve signs, notices or placards, whether temporary or permanent, or with any monuments, stakes, posts or other boundary markers.
