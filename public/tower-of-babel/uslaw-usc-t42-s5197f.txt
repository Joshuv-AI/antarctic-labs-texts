42 U.S.C. § 5197f — Relation to Atomic Energy Act of 1954
Citation: us:usc:42:5197f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section5197f
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 68 (Disaster Relief); Subchapter IV–B (Emergency Preparedness); Part B (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subchapter shall be construed to alter or modify the provisions of the Atomic Energy Act of 1954 (42 U.S.C. 2011 et seq.).
