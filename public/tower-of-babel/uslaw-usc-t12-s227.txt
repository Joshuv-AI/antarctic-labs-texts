12 U.S.C. § 227 — “Banking Act of 1933”
Citation: us:usc:12:227
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section227
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 3 (Federal Reserve System); Subchapter I (Definitions, Organization, And General Provisions Affecting System). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The short title of the Act of June 16, 1933, ch. 89, 48 Stat. 162, shall be the “Banking Act of 1933.”
