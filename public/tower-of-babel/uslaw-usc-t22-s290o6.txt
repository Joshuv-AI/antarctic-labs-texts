22 U.S.C. § 290o–6 — Effectiveness of Agreement
Citation: us:usc:22:290o–6
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section290o–6
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter XXX (Middle East Development Bank). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Agreement shall have full force and effect in the United States, its territories and possessions, and the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico, upon acceptance of membership by the United States in the Bank and the entry into force of the Agreement.
