Varieties with two smooth blow up structures
Supravat Sarkar

Abstract
We classify smooth projective varieties of Picard rank 2 2 which have two structures of blow-up of projective space along smooth subvarieties of different dimensions. This gives a characterization of the so called quadro-cubic Cremona transformation.



Keywords
: Blow up, simple Cremona map, quadro-cubic transformation
