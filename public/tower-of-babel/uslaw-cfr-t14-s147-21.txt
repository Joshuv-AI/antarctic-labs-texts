14 C.F.R. § 147.21 — Certificate of completion.
Citation: us:cfr:14:147.21
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-147.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 147 (Aviation Maintenance Technician Schools). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each certificated aviation maintenance technician school must provide an authenticated document to each graduating student, indicating the student's date of graduation and curriculum completed.
