32 C.F.R. § 1907.26 — Prohibition on adverse action.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1907.26
Creator: Central Intelligence Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1907.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1907 (Challenges To Classification Of Documents By Authorized Holders Pursuant To Sec. 1.8 Of Executive Order 13526). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agency correspondence to the challenger shall include a notice that CIA will take no adverse action or retribution against the challenger for bringing the classification challenge in good faith.
