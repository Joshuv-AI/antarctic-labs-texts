42 U.S.C. § 12655d — Related programs
Citation: us:usc:42:12655d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section12655d
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 129 (National And Community Service); Subchapter I (National And Community Service State Grant Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An activity administered under the authority of the Secretary of Health and Human Services, that is operated for the same purpose as a program eligible to be carried out under this division, is encouraged to use services available under this division.
