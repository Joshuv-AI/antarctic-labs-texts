40 C.F.R. § 503.42 — General requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:40:503.42
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-503.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 503 (Standards For The Use Or Disposal Of Sewage Sludge). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person shall fire sewage sludge in a sewage sludge incinerator except in compliance with the requirements in this subpart.
