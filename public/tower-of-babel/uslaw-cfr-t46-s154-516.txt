46 C.F.R. § 154.516 — Piping: Hull protection.
Citation: us:cfr:46:154.516
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-154.516
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 154 (Safety Standards For Self-Propelled Vessels Carrying Bulk Liquefied Gases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A vessel's hull must be protected from low temperature liquid leakage by a drip pan, or other means specially approved by the Commandant (CG-ENG), at:
(a) Each piping connection dismantled on a routine basis;
(b) Cargo discharge and loading manifolds; and
(c) Pump seals.
