34 C.F.R. § 675.49 — Procedures and records.
Citation: us:cfr:34:675.49
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-675.49
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 675 (Federal Work-Study Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In administering a Work-Colleges program under this subpart, an institution shall comply with the applicable provisions of 34 CFR part 673 and this part 675.
