24 C.F.R. § 266.632 — Withdrawal of claim.
Citation: us:cfr:24:266.632
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-266.632
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 266 (Housing Finance Agency Risk-Sharing Program For Insured Affordable Multifamily Project Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In case of a default and subsequent filing of claim, the HFA shall determine the form of workout or modification and will inform HUD of the type of mortgage relief determined to be appropriate. If the default is cured after the claim is made but before the initial claim payment is paid by HUD, the HFA may, in writing, withdraw the claim, and insurance will continue as if the default had not occurred.
