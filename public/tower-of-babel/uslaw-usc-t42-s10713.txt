42 U.S.C. § 10713 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:42:10713
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section10713
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 113 (State Justice Institute). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out the purposes of this chapter, $7,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2005, 2006, 2007, and 2008. Amounts appropriated for each such year are to remain available until expended.
