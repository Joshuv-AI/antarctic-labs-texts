21 C.F.R. § 803.13 — Do I need to submit reports in English?
Citation: us:cfr:21:803.13
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-803.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 803 (Medical Device Reporting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. You must submit all reports required by this part in English.
