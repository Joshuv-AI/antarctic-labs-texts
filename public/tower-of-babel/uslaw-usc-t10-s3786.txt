10 U.S.C. § 3786 — Use or release restriction: definition
Citation: us:usc:10:3786
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section3786
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part V (Acquisition); Subpart D (General Contracting Provisions); Chapter 275 (Proprietary Contractor Data And Rights In Technical Data); Subchapter II (Validation Of Proprietary Data Restrictions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this subchapter, the term “use or release restriction”, with respect to technical data delivered to the United States under a contract subject to this section, means a restriction by the contractor or subcontractor on the right of the United States—
(1)
to use such technical data; or
(2)
to release or disclose such technical data to persons outside the Government or permit the use of such technical data by persons outside the Government.
