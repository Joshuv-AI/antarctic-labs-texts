7 U.S.C. § 3336 — Authorization of appropriations; allocation of funds
Citation: us:usc:7:3336
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section3336
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 64 (Agricultural Research, Extension, And Teaching); Subchapter XII (Rangeland Research). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
There are authorized to be appropriated, to implement the provisions of this subchapter—
(1)
$10,000,000 for each of fiscal years 1991 through 2013; and
(2)
$2,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2014 through 2023.
(b)
Funds appropriated under this section shall be allocated by the Secretary to eligible institutions for work to be done as mutually agreed upon between the Secretary and the eligible institution or institutions.
