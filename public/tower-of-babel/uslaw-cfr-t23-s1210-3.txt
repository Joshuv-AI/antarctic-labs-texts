23 C.F.R. § 1210.3 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:23:1210.3
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration And Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-1210.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 1210 (Operation Of Motor Vehicles By Intoxicated Minors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in this part:
(a) Alcohol concentration means either grams of alcohol per 100 milliliters of blood or grams of alcohol per 210 liters of breath.
(b) BAC means either blood or breath alcohol concentration.
(c) Operating a motor vehicle means driving or being in actual physical control of a motor vehicle.
