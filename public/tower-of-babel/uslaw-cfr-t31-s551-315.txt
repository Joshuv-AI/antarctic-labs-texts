31 C.F.R. § 551.315 — Transitional Federal Institutions.
Citation: us:cfr:31:551.315
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-551.315
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 551 (Somalia Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term Transitional Federal Institutions means the Transitional Federal Charter of the Somali Republic adopted in February 2004 and the Somali federal institutions established pursuant to such charter, and includes their agencies, instrumentalities, and controlled entities.
