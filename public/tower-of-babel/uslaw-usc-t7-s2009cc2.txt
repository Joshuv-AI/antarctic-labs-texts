7 U.S.C. § 2009cc–2 — Establishment
Citation: us:usc:7:2009cc–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2009cc–2
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 50 (Agricultural Credit); Subchapter VIII (Rural Business Investment Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In accordance with this subchapter, the Secretary shall establish a Rural Business Investment Program, under which the Secretary may—
(1)
enter into participation agreements with companies granted final approval under section 2009cc–3(e) of this title for the purposes set forth in section 2009cc–1 of this title;
(2)
guarantee the debentures issued by rural business investment companies as provided in section 2009cc–4 of this title; and
(3)
make grants to rural business investment companies, and to other entities, under section 2009cc–7 of this title.
