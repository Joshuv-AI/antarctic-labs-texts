9 C.F.R. § 315.2 — Carcasses and parts passed for cooking; utilization for food purposes after cooking.
Citation: us:cfr:9:315.2
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-315.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 315 (Rendering Or Other Disposal Of Carcasses And Parts Passed For Cooking). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Carcasses and parts passed for cooking may be used for the preparation of meat food products, provided all such carcasses or parts are heated to a temperature not lower than 170 °F. for a period of not less than 30 minutes either before being used in or during the preparation of the finished product.
