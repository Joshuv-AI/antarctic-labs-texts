45 C.F.R. § 2506.21 — May the Corporation's failure to comply with these regulations be used as a defense to a debt?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2506.21
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2506.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2506 (Collection Of Debts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No, the failure of the Corporation to comply with any standard in the FCCS or these regulations will not be available to any debtor as a defense.
