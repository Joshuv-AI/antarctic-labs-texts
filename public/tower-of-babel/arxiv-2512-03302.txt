Lepton number violation from Higgs/ Z Z decays into a pair of right-handed neutrinos
Arindam Das, Takaaki Nomura, Kei Yagyu

Abstract
We explore signatures of Lepton Number Violation (LNV) from decays of the discovered Higgs ( h h ) and Z bosons into a pair of Right-Handed Neutrinos (RHNs). Due to the Majorana nature of RHNs, the final state can be the same-sign dilepton plus jets leading to 2 units of LNV. As a simple but plausible scenario, we investigate such a signal in models with a new U ​ ( 1 ) X U(1)_{X} gauge symmetry which naturally introduces three RHNs for gauge anomaly cancellation, and is spontaneously broken down by a vacuum expectation value of an isospin singlet scalar field ( ϕ \phi ). In this scenario, h h and the Z boson can decay into a pair of RHNs via the h h - ϕ \phi mixing and the Z Z - Z ′ Z^{\prime} mixing with Z ′ Z^{\prime} being a new massive gauge boson, respectively. Estimating the LNV signal and corresponding backgrounds for the ℓ ± ​ ℓ ± ​ 4 ​ j \ell^{\pm}\ell^{\pm}4j final states, we find bounds on the h h - ϕ \phi mixing and the Z Z - Z ′ Z^{\prime} mixing as a function of a mass of RHNs at Higgs factories, e.g., at High-Luminosity LHC with s = \sqrt{s}= 14 TeV, future e − ​ e + e^{-}e^{+} colliders at s = \sqrt{s}= 250 GeV, muon colliders at s = \sqrt{s}= 125 GeV as well as at Z factories, e.g., CEPC and FCC-ee at s = \sqrt{s}= 91.2 GeV. We also discuss limits on the scalar mixing at e − ​ μ + e^{-}\mu^{+} and μ + ​ μ + \mu^{+}\mu^{+} collisions ( μ \mu TRISTAN) with s = \sqrt{s}= 346 GeV/775 GeV and 2 TeV, respectively.

Framework
–
Under the
SU(2)_{L}\otimes U(1)_{Y}\otimes U(1)_{X}
gauge symmetry, the SM quarks, leptons and Higgs fields are charged as follows

\displaystyle q_{L}^{i}\sim\left(2,\frac{1}{6},\frac{x_{H}}{6}+\frac{x_{\Phi}}{3}\right),\penalty\ u_{R}^{i}\sim\left(1,\frac{2}{3},\frac{2}{3}x_{H}+\frac{x_{\Phi}}{3}\right),

\displaystyle d_{R}^{i}\sim\left(1,-\frac{1}{3},-\frac{x_{H}}{3}+\frac{x_{\Phi}}{3}\right),\penalty\ \ell_{L}^{i}\sim\left(2,-\frac{1}{2},-\frac{x_{H}}{2}-x_{\Phi}\right),

where the index

As new fields, we introduce three generations of RHNs

\displaystyle N_{R}^{i}=(1,1,0,-x_{\Phi}),\penalty\ \penalty\ \Phi=(1,1,0,2x_{\Phi}).

(2)

Generally, the kinetic-mixing term between

and

The scalar potential is given by

\displaystyle V

\displaystyle=-\ m_{H}^{2}(H^{\dagger}H)-m_{\Phi}^{2}(\Phi^{\dagger}\Phi)

\displaystyle+\lambda_{H}(H^{\dagger}H)^{2}+\lambda_{\Phi}(\Phi^{\dagger}\Phi)^{2}+\lambda_{\rm mix}(H^{\dagger}H)(\Phi^{\dagger}\Phi)\penalty\ .

(3)

\displaystyle H\ =\ \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{pmatrix}0\\

We consider

After imposing the stationary condition of the scalar potential, we obtain the mass-squared matrix for the scalar fields:

\displaystyle M^{2}=\begin{bmatrix}2\lambda_{H}v^{2}&\lambda_{\rm mix}vv_{\Phi}\\
\lambda_{\rm mix}vv_{\Phi}&2\lambda_{\Phi}v_{\Phi}^{2}\\
\end{bmatrix},

(5)

\displaystyle\begin{bmatrix}\tilde{h}\\

\displaystyle\lambda_{H}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2v^{2}}\left(m_{h}^{2}\cos^{2}\alpha+m_{\phi}^{2}\sin^{2}\alpha\right),

\displaystyle\lambda_{\Phi}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2v_{\Phi}^{2}}\left(m_{h}^{2}\sin^{2}\alpha+m_{\phi}^{2}\cos^{2}\alpha\right),

The mass matrix for the massive neutral gauge bosons is extracted as

\displaystyle M_{ZZ^{\prime}}=\begin{pmatrix}M_{Z}^{2}&-\frac{v^{2}}{2}g_{Z}g_{X}x_{H}\\

\displaystyle M_{Z}^{2}

\displaystyle=\frac{g_{Z}^{2}}{4}v^{2},

(10)

Conclusions
–
In this work, we have investigated LNV signals from the decays of the 125 GeV Higgs boson
h
and
Z
boson into a pair of RHNs (
N
). The decay of
h\to NN
is induced via the mixing with a singlet scalar which induces Majorana masses for the RHNs after the
U(1)_{X}
symmetry breaking. On the other hand, the
Z
boson decays into
NN
via the
Z
-
Z^{\prime}
mixing. We have performed simulation studies at parton level for the LNV signals and BGs at the Higgs/
Z
factories. It has been found that LNV signals via the Higgs decay can be explored for the scalar mixing
\sin^{2}\alpha\gtrsim 10^{-2}(10^{-3})
at the HL-LHC, MCs and ILC (CEPC) for
m_{N}\simeq 40
GeV.
We also have shown that a similar sensitivity can be obtained at
e^{-}\mu^{+}
and
\mu^{+}\mu^{+}
collisions (
\mu
TRISTAN) as those given at the HL-LHC.
For the LNV signal from
Z
decay, we can have sensitivity to test it when
Z
-
Z^{\prime}
mixing is
|\zeta|\gtrsim 10^{-4}
.
Therefore, we have shown that future Higgs and
Z
factories are excellent opportunities to find LNV signals from the decays of Higgs/
Z
providing significant avenues to explore the Majorana nature of neutrinos.
