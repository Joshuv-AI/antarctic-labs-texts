49 U.S.C. § 24709 — International transportation
Citation: us:usc:49:24709
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section24709
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part C (Passenger Transportation); Chapter 247 (Amtrak Route System). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Amtrak may develop and operate international intercity rail passenger transportation between the United States and Canada and between the United States and Mexico. The Secretary of Homeland Security, in cooperation with Amtrak, shall maintain, consistent with the effective enforcement of the immigration and customs laws, en route customs inspection and immigration procedures for international intercity rail passenger transportation that will—
(1)
be convenient for passengers; and
(2)
result in the quickest possible international intercity rail passenger transportation.
