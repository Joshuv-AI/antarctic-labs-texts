14 C.F.R. § 31.57 — Rip cords.
Citation: us:cfr:14:31.57
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-31.57
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 31 (Airworthiness Standards: Manned Free Balloons). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If a rip cord is used for emergency deflation, it must be designed and installed to preclude entanglement.
(b) The force required to operate the rip cord may not be less than 25, or more than 75, pounds.
(c) The end of the rip cord to be operated by the pilot must be colored red.
(d) The rip cord must be long enough to allow an increase of at least 10 percent in the vertical dimension of the envelope.
