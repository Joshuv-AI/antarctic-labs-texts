2 U.S.C. § 6652 — Compensation of employees of Chaplain of Senate
Citation: us:usc:2:6652
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section6652
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 65 (Senate Officers And Administration); Subchapter IV (Chaplain). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Chaplain of the Senate may appoint and fix the compensation of such employees as he deems appropriate, except that the amount which may be paid for any fiscal year as gross compensation for personnel in such Office for any fiscal year shall not exceed $147,000.
