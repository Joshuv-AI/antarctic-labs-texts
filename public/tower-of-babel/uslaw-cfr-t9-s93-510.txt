9 C.F.R. § 93.510 — Quarantine requirements.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 93 (Importation Of Certain Animals, Birds, Fish, And Poultry, And Certain Animal, Bird, And Poultry Products; Requirements For Means Of Conveyance And Shipping Containers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Swine shall be quarantined for not less than 15 days, counting from the date of arrival at the port of entry. During their quarantine, wild swine shall be subject to such inspections, disinfection, blood tests, or other tests as may be required by the Administrator, to determine their freedom from disease and the infection of disease.
