45 C.F.R. § 2553.52 — Under what circumstances may a sponsor remove an RSVP volunteer from service?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2553.52
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2553.52
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2553 (The Retired And Senior Volunteer Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A sponsor may remove an RSVP volunteer from service for cause. Grounds for removal include, but are not limited to: Extensive and unauthorized absences; misconduct; failure to perform assignments and or failure to accept supervision.
(b) The sponsor shall establish appropriate policies on removal from service as well as procedures for appeal.
