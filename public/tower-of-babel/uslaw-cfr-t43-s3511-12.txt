43 C.F.R. § 3511.12 — Are there standard terms and conditions which apply to all leases?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3511.12
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3511.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3500 (Leasing Of Solid Minerals Other Than Coal And Oil Shale). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. BLM will issue your lease on a standard form which will contain several terms and conditions. We will add your rental rate, royalty obligations and any special stipulations to this lease form.
