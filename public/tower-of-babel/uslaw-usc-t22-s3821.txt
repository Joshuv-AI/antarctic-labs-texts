22 U.S.C. § 3821 — Vessels subject to inspection
Citation: us:usc:22:3821
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section3821
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 51 (Panama Canal); Subchapter I (Administration And Regulations); Part 8 (Shipping And Navigation); Subpart ii (Inspection Of Vessels). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

With the exception of private vessels merely transiting the Panama Canal, and of public vessels of all nations, vessels navigating the waters of the Panama Canal shall be subject to an annual inspection of hulls, boilers, machinery, equipment, and passenger accommodations.
