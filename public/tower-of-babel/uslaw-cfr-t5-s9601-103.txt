5 C.F.R. § 9601.103 — Prohibited outside employment.
Citation: us:cfr:5:9601.103
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-9601.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 9601 (Supplemental Standards Of Ethical Conduct For Employees Of The National Credit Union Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No employee may engage in outside employment, with or without compensation, with any credit union, credit union trade group, credit union service organization, or other credit union-related entity, in any capacity.
