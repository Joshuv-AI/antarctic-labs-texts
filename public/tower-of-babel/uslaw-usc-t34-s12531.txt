34 U.S.C. § 12531 — Juvenile anti-drug and anti-gang grants in federally assisted low-income housing
Citation: us:usc:34:12531
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section12531
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 121 (Violent Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Subchapter V (Criminal Street Gangs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Grants authorized in this Act to reduce or prevent juvenile drug and gang-related activity in “public housing” may be used for such purposes in federally assisted, low-income housing.
