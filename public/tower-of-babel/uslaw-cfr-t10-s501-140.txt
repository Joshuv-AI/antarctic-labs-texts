10 C.F.R. § 501.140 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:10:501.140
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-501.140
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 501 (Administrative Procedures And Sanctions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

DOE may issue rulings in accordance with the provisions of this subpart. DOE will publish each ruling in the Federal Register and in 10 CFR part 518. A person is entitled to rely upon a ruling to the extent provided in this subpart.
