17 U.S.C. § 1332 — No retroactive effect
Citation: us:usc:17:1332
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title17-section1332
Context: United States Code, Title 17 (Copyrights); Chapter 13 (Protection Of Original Designs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Protection under this chapter shall not be available for any design that has been made public under section 1310(b) before the effective date of this chapter.
