45 C.F.R. § 99.12 — Time of hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:45:99.12
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-99.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 99 (Procedure For Hearings For The Child Care And Development Fund). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The hearing shall be scheduled not less than 30 days nor more than 90 days after the date of the notice of the hearing furnished to the applicant or Lead Agency, unless otherwise agreed to, in writing, by the parties.
