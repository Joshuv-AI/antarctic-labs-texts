39 C.F.R. § 954.25 — Public information.
Citation: us:cfr:39:954.25
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-954.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 954 (Denial, Suspension, Or Revocaton Of Periodicals Mail Privileges). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Librarian of the Postal Service maintains for public inspection in the Library copies of all initial and final Agency decisions. The Recorder of the Postal Service maintains a complete official record of every proceeding. A person may examine a record upon authorization by the Judicial Officer.
