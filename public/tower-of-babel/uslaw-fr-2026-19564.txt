Petition for Exemption

    Docket No.: FAA-2026-11090
    Petitioner: Air Methods, LLC
    Section(s) of 14 CFR Affected: Sec. Sec.  135.117(a) and 135.117(b)
    Description of Relief Sought: Petitioner seeks an exemption from 
Sec. Sec.  135.117(a) and 135.117(b) so that the pilot in command does 
not have to brief medical personnel before each takeoff of a helicopter 
air ambulance operations if the requirements of Sec. Sec.  135.117(a) 
and 135.117(b) have been incorporated into the petitioner's approved 
training program required by Sec.  135.621(b) and those medical 
personnel have received this training within the preceding 24 calendar 
months.
