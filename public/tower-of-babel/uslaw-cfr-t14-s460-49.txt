14 C.F.R. § 460.49 — Space flight participant waiver of claims against U.S. Government.
Citation: us:cfr:14:460.49
Creator: Commercial Space Transportation, Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-460.49
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 460 (Human Space Flight Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each space flight participant must execute a reciprocal waiver of claims with the Federal Aviation Administration of the Department of Transportation in accordance with the requirements of part 440 of this chapter.
