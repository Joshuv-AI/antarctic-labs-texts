2 C.F.R. § 2900.5 — Federal awarding agency review of risk posed by applicants.
Citation: us:cfr:2:2900.5
Creator: Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-2900.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 2900 (Uniform Administrative Requirements, Cost Principles, And Audit Requirements For Federal Awards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to the guidance set forth in 2 CFR 200.206(b), in evaluating risks of applicants, DOL also considers audits and monitoring reports containing findings and issues of noncompliance or questioned costs, in addition to reports and findings from audits performed under Subpart F—Audit Requirements of 2 CFR 200 or the reports and findings of any other available audits (see 2 CFR 200.206(b)).
