2 U.S.C. § 285c — Law Revision Counsel
Citation: us:usc:2:285c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section285c
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 9A (Office Of Law Revision Counsel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The management, supervision, and administration of the Office are vested in the Law Revision Counsel, who shall be appointed by the Speaker without regard to political affiliation and solely on the basis of fitness to perform the duties of the position. Any person so appointed shall serve at the pleasure of the Speaker.
