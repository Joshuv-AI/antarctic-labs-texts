42 U.S.C. § 12897 — Limitation on selection criteria
Citation: us:usc:42:12897
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section12897
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 130 (National Affordable Housing); Subchapter IV (Hope For Homeownership Of Multifamily And Single Family Homes); Part B (Hope For Homeownership Of Single Family Homes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In establishing criteria for selecting applicants to receive assistance under this part, the Secretary may not establish any selection criterion or criteria that grant or deny such assistance to an applicant (or have the effect of granting or denying assistance) based on the implementation, continuation, or discontinuation of any public policy, regulation, or law of any jurisdiction in which the applicant or project is located.
