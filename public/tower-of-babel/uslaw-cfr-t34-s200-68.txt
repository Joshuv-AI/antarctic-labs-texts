34 C.F.R. § 200.68 — Ombudsman.
Citation: us:cfr:34:200.68
Creator: Office Of Elementary And Secondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-200.68
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 200 (Title I—Improving The Academic Achievement Of The Disadvantaged). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To help ensure equity for eligible private school children, teachers, and other educational personnel, an SEA must designate an ombudsman to monitor and enforce the requirements in §§ 200.62 through 200.67.
