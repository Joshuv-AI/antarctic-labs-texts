20 U.S.C. § 2394 — Voluntary selection and participation
Citation: us:usc:20:2394
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section2394
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 44 (Career And Technical Education); Subchapter II (General Provisions); Part A (Federal Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No funds made available under this chapter shall be used—
(1)
to require any secondary school student to choose or pursue a specific career pathway or program of study; or
(2)
to mandate that any individual participate in a career and technical education program, including a career and technical education program that requires the attainment of a federally funded skill level, standard, or certificate of mastery.
