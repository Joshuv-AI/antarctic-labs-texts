33 C.F.R. § 126.25 — Penalties for handling designated dangerous cargo without permit.
Citation: us:cfr:33:126.25
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-126.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 126 (Handling Of Dangerous Cargo At Waterfront Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Handling, loading, discharging, or transporting any designated dangerous cargo without a permit, as provided under § 126.17, being in force, will subject persons responsible therefore to the civil or criminal penalties provided in 46 U.S.C. 70036.
