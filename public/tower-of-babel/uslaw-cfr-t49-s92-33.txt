49 C.F.R. § 92.33 — Recovery from other payments due a separated employee.
Citation: us:cfr:49:92.33
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-92.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 92 (Recovering Debts To The United States By Salary Offset). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the debt cannot be liquidated by offset from any final payment due the employee as of the date of separation, the DOT operating element shall liquidate the debt by administrative offset pursuant to 31 U.S.C. 3716 from later payments of any kind due the former employee from the United States, where appropriate (see § 92.41(b)(2)(ii)).
