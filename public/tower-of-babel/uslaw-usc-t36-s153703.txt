36 U.S.C. § 153703 — Membership
Citation: us:usc:36:153703
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section153703
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1537 (National Woman’S Relief Corps, Auxiliary To The Grand Army Of The Republic). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as provided in this chapter, eligibility for membership in the corporation and the rights, privileges, and designation of classes of members are as provided in the constitution and bylaws of the corporation. Eligibility for membership is limited to—
(1)
women who are the wives, mothers, daughters, and sisters of Union soldiers, sailors, and marines; and
(2)
other loyal women who have not given aid or comfort to the enemies of the United States of America.
