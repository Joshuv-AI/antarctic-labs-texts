49 C.F.R. § 232.701 — Power brakes; minimum percentage.
Citation: us:cfr:49:232.701
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-232.701
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 232 (Brake System Safety Standards For Freight And Other Non-Passenger Trains And Equipment; End-Of-Train Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

On and after September 1, 1910, on all railroads used in interstate commerce, whenever, as required by the Safety Appliance Act as amended March 2, 1903, any train is operated with power or train brakes, not less than 85 percent of the cars of such train shall have their brakes used and operated by the engineer of the locomotive drawing such train, and all power-brake cars in every such train which are associated together with the 85 percent shall have their brakes so used and operated.
