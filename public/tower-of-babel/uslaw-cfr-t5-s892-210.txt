5 C.F.R. § 892.210 — Does premium conversion change the effective date of an FEHB enrollment, change in enrollment, or cancellation of enrollment?
Citation: us:cfr:5:892.210
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-892.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 892 (Federal Flexible Benefits Plan: Pre-Tax Payment Of Health Benefits Premiums). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. If you are participating in premium conversion, the effective date of an FEHB enrollment, change in enrollment, or cancellation of enrollment is the same effective date as provided in § 890.301 of this chapter.
