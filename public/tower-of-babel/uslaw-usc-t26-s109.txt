26 U.S.C. § 109 — Improvements by lessee on lessor’s property
Citation: us:usc:26:109
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section109
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 1 (Normal Taxes And Surtaxes); Subchapter B (Computation Of Taxable Income); Part III (Items Specifically Excluded From Gross Income). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Gross income does not include income (other than rent) derived by a lessor of real property on the termination of a lease, representing the value of such property attributable to buildings erected or other improvements made by the lessee.
