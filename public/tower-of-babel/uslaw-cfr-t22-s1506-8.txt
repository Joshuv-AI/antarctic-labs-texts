22 C.F.R. § 1506.8 — Will an omission by the Agency in complying with this part serve as a debtor's defense against payment?
Citation: us:cfr:22:1506.8
Creator: African Development Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1506.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1506 (Collection Of Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Failure by USADF to comply with any provision of this Part is not available to a debtor as a defense against payment of a debt.
