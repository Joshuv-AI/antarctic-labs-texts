17 C.F.R. § 300.500 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:17:300.500
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-300.500
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 300 (Rules Of The Securities Investor Protection Corporation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These rules will be applied in determining whether a securities transaction gives rise to a “claim for cash” or a “claim for securities” on the filing date of either a liquidation proceeding pursuant to the Securities Investor Protection Act (hereinafter referred to as “the Act”) or a direct payment procedure pursuant to section 10 of the Act.
