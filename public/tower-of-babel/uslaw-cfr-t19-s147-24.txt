19 C.F.R. § 147.24 — Merchandise subject to licensing.
Citation: us:cfr:19:147.24
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-147.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 147 (Trade Fairs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Merchandise, the importation of which is subject to the licensing regulations of any agency of the U.S. Government, may be entered for a fair only upon the presentation of the required license, or a waiver of such license.
