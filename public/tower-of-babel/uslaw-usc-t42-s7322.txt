42 U.S.C. § 7322 — Congressional review
Citation: us:usc:42:7322
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section7322
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 84 (Department Of Energy); Subchapter VIII (Energy Planning). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Each proposed Plan shall be referred to the appropriate committees in the Senate and the House of Representatives.
(b)
Each such committee shall review the proposed Plan and, if it deems appropriate and necessary, report to the Senate or the House of Representatives legislation regarding such Plan which may contain such alternatives to, modifications of, or additions to the proposed Plan submitted by the President as the committee deems appropriate.
