43 C.F.R. § 404.1 — What is the purpose of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:43:404.1
Creator: Bureau Of Reclamation, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-404.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 404 (Reclamation Rural Water Supply Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to explain how the Reclamation Rural Water Supply Program is implemented. This part describes:
(a) The purpose and priorities of the program;
(b) How to apply for assistance under the program;
(c) How Reclamation will evaluate requests for assistance;
(d) How Reclamation will evaluate an appraisal investigation; and
(e) How Reclamation will evaluate a feasibility study.
