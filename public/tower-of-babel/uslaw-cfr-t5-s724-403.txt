5 C.F.R. § 724.403 — Advisory guidelines.
Citation: us:cfr:5:724.403
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-724.403
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 724 (Implementation Of Title Ii Of The Notification And Federal Employee Antidiscrimination And Retaliation Act Of 2002). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

OPM will issue advisory guidelines to Federal agencies incorporating the best practices identified under § 724.402 that agencies may follow to take appropriate disciplinary actions against employees for conduct that is inconsistent with Federal Antidiscrimination Laws and Whistleblower Laws.
