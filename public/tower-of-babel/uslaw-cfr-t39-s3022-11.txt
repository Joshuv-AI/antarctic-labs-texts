39 C.F.R. § 3022.11 — Service.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3022.11
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3022.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3022 (Rules For Complaints). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person filing a complaint must simultaneously serve a copy of the complaint on the Postal Service at this address: PRCCOMPLAINTS@usps.gov. A person without internet access may contact the Secretary to obtain approval for alternative methods of service.
