48 U.S.C. § 911 — Legislative authorization to create authorities
Citation: us:usc:48:911
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section911
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 4 (Puerto Rico); Subchapter VII (Low Rent Housing Projects And Elimination Of Sub-Standard Housing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Legislature of Puerto Rico may create public corporate authorities to undertake slum clearance and projects to provide dwelling accommodations for families of low income.
