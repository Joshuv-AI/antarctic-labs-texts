45 C.F.R. § 1329.24 — Training and technical assistance to Centers for Independent Living.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1329.24
Creator: Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1329.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1329 (State Independent Living Services And Centers For Independent Living). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator shall reserve between 1.8% and 2% of appropriated funds to provide training and technical assistance to Centers through grants, contracts or cooperative agreements, consistent with section 721(b) of the Act. The training and technical assistance funds shall be administered in accordance with section 721(b) of the Act.
