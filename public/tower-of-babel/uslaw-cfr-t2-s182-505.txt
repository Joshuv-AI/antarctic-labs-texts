2 C.F.R. § 182.505 — How are violations of this part determined for recipients who are individuals?
Citation: us:cfr:2:182.505
Creator: Office Of Management And Budget Government-Wide Guidance For Federal Financial Assistance
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-182.505
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 182 (Government-Wide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A recipient who is an individual is in violation of the requirements of this part if the Federal agency head or their designee determines, in writing, that:
(a) The recipient has violated the requirements of subpart C of this part; or
(b) The recipient is convicted of a criminal drug offense resulting from a violation occurring during the conduct of any award activity.
