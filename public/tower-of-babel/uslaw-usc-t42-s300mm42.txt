42 U.S.C. § 300mm–42 — Administrative arrangement authority
Citation: us:usc:42:300mm–42
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section300mm–42
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter XXXI (World Trade Center Health Program); Part B (Program Of Monitoring, Initial Health Evaluations, And Treatment); Subpart 3 (Payor Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The WTC Program Administrator may enter into arrangements with other government agencies, insurance companies, or other third-party administrators to provide for timely and accurate processing of claims under sections 300mm–22, 300mm–23, 300mm–32, and 300mm–33 of this title.
