34 C.F.R. § 222.10 — How long must a local educational agency retain records?
Citation: us:cfr:34:222.10
Creator: Office Of Elementary And Secondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-222.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 222 (Impact Aid Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An LEA must retain the records described in § 222.9 until the later of—
(a) Three years after the last payment for a fiscal year; or
(b) If the records have been questioned on Federal audit or review, until the question is finally resolved and any necessary adjustments to payments have been made.
