43 C.F.R. § 3927.40 — Effective date of leases.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3927.40
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3927.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3920 (Oil Shale Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Leases are dated and effective the first day of the month following the date the BLM signs it. However, upon receiving a prior written request, the BLM may make the effective date of the lease the first day of the month in which the BLM signs it.
