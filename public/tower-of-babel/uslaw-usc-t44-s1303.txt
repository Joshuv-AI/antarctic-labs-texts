44 U.S.C. § 1303 — American Historical Association: report
Citation: us:usc:44:1303
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section1303
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 13 (Particular Reports And Documents). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In addition to the usual number of the report of the American Historical Association, five thousand five hundred copies shall be printed: one thousand for the Senate, two thousand for the House of Representatives, one thousand five hundred for distribution by the Association and the Smithsonian Institution, and one thousand copies for the use of the Association.
