30 C.F.R. § 1241.50 — What may ONRR do if I violate a statute, regulation, order, or lease term relating to a lease subject to this part?
Citation: us:cfr:30:1241.50
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1241.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1241 (Penalties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If we determine that you have not followed any requirement of a statute, regulation, order, or a term of a lease subject to this part, we may serve you with a NONC explaining:
(a) What the violation is.
(b) How to correct the violation to avoid a civil penalty.
(c) That you have 20 days after the date on which you are served the NONC to correct the violation, unless the NONC specifies a longer period.
