Because thermal energy may allow flux lines to jump from one pinning point to another in response to the driving force of the current and flux-density gradient, there may be an observable decrease of the trapped moment with time. Since any creep will relieve the gradient, the creep gets slower and slower. In other words, the time dependence is logarithmic.
