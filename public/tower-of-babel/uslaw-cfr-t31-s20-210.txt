31 C.F.R. § 20.210 — To whom must I distribute my drug-free workplace statement?
Citation: us:cfr:31:20.210
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-20.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 20 (Governmentwide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must require that a copy of the statement described in § 20.205 be given to each employee who will be engaged in the performance of any Federal award.
