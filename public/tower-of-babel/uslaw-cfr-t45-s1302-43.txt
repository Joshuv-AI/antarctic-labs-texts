45 C.F.R. § 1302.43 — Oral health practices.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1302.43
Creator: Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1302.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1302 (Program Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A program must promote effective oral health hygiene by ensuring all children with teeth are assisted by appropriate staff, or volunteers, if available, in brushing their teeth with toothpaste containing fluoride once daily.
