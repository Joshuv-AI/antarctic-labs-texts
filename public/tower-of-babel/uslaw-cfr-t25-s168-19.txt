25 C.F.R. § 168.19 — Information collection.
Citation: us:cfr:25:168.19
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-168.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 168 (Grazing Regulations For The Hopi Partitioned Lands Area). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The information collection requirement(s) contained in this regulation have been approved by the Office of Management and Budget under 44 U.S.C. 3501 et seq. and assigned clearance number 1076-0027. The information is being collected in order to ascertain eligibility for the issuance of a grazing permit. Response is mandatory in order to obtain a permit.
