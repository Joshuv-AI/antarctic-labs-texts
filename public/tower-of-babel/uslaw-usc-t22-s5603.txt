22 U.S.C. § 5603 — United States export controls
Citation: us:usc:22:5603
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section5603
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 65 (Control And Elimination Of Chemical And Biological Weapons). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President shall—
(1)
use the authorities of the Arms Export Control Act [22 U.S.C. 2751 et seq.] to control the export of those defense articles and defense services, and
(2)
use the authorities of the Export Administration Act of 1979 to control the export of those goods and technology,
that the President determines would assist the government of any foreign country in acquiring the capability to develop, produce, stockpile, deliver, or use chemical or biological weapons.
