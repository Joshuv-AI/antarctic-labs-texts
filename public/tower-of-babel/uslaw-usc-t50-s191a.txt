50 U.S.C. § 191a — Transfer of Secretary of Transportation’s powers to Secretary of Navy when Coast Guard operates as part of Navy
Citation: us:usc:50:191a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section191a
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 12 (Vessels In Territorial Waters Of United States). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When the Coast Guard operates as a part of the Navy pursuant to section 3 1 of title 14, the powers conferred on the Secretary of Transportation by section 191 1 of this title, shall vest in and be exercised by the Secretary of the Navy.
