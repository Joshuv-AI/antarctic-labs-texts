5 C.F.R. § 849.103 — Implementing directives.
Citation: us:cfr:5:849.103
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-849.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 849 (Representative Payees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director may prescribe, in the form he or she deems appropriate, such detailed procedures as are necessary to carry out the purpose of this part.
