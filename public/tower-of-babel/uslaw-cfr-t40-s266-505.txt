40 C.F.R. § 266.505 — Prohibition on sewering hazardous waste pharmaceuticals.
Citation: us:cfr:40:266.505
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-266.505
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 266 (Standards For The Management Of Specific Hazardous Wastes And Specific Types Of Hazardous Waste Management Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All healthcare facilities—including very small quantity generators operating under § 262.14 of this subchapter in lieu of this subpart—and reverse distributors are prohibited from discharging hazardous waste pharmaceuticals to a sewer system that passes through to a publicly-owned treatment works. Healthcare facilities and reverse distributors remain subject to the prohibitions in 40 CFR 403.5(b).
