27 C.F.R. § 555.161 — Engaging in business without a license.
Citation: us:cfr:27:555.161
Creator: Bureau Of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms, And Explosives, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-555.161
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 555 (Commerce In Explosives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person engaging in the business of importing, manufacturing, or dealing in explosive materials without a license issued under the Act, shall be fined not more than $10,000 or imprisoned not more than 10 years, or both.
