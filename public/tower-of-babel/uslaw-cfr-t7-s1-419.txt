7 C.F.R. § 1.419 — Amendment of a sourcing area application.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1.419
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1.419
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1 (Administrative Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The sourcing area applicant may move to amend the sourcing area application with clarifying and technical amendments at any time prior to the Judge's initial determination if there is no hearing, or prior to the close of the hearing if there is a hearing.
