38 U.S.C. § 5905 — Penalty for certain acts
Citation: us:usc:38:5905
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section5905
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part IV (General Administrative Provisions); Chapter 59 (Agents And Attorneys). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever wrongfully withholds from any claimant or beneficiary any part of a benefit or claim allowed and due to the claimant or beneficiary, shall be fined as provided in title 18, or imprisoned not more than one year, or both.
