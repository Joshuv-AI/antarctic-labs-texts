The List-distinguishing chromatic number of graphs containing only small complete bigraphs
Amitayu Banerjee

Abstract
. Let G G be a simple finite graph with maximum degree Δ ⁡ ( G ) \Delta(G) . Collins and Trenk (2006) proved that χ D ​ ( G ) ≤ 2 ​ Δ ​ ( G ) \chi_{D}(G)\leq 2\Delta(G) for any connected graph G G , and that the equality holds if G G is the complete bigraph K Δ ⁡ ( G ) , Δ ⁡ ( G ) K_{\Delta(G),\Delta(G)} or C 6 C_{6} . t

3.2.
Defining a partition

Consider the partition

(1)

Using the methods used in

For the reader’s convenience, we write the details from

For each

Simplifying, we obtain

Considering the summation of both sides of this system of inequalities by letting

A similar argument applies for any vertex
