26 U.S.C. § 115 — Income of States, municipalities, etc.
Citation: us:usc:26:115
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section115
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 1 (Normal Taxes And Surtaxes); Subchapter B (Computation Of Taxable Income); Part III (Items Specifically Excluded From Gross Income). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Gross income does not include—
(1)
income derived from any public utility or the exercise of any essential governmental function and accruing to a State or any political subdivision thereof, or the District of Columbia; or
(2)
income accruing to the government of any possession of the United States, or any political subdivision thereof.
