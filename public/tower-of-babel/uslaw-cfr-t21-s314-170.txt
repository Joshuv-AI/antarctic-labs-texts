21 C.F.R. § 314.170 — Adulteration and misbranding of an approved drug.
Citation: us:cfr:21:314.170
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-314.170
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 314 (Applications For Fda Approval To Market A New Drug). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All drugs, including those the Food and Drug Administration approves under section 505 of the act and this part, are subject to the adulteration and misbranding provisions in sections 501, 502, and 503 of the act. FDA is authorized to regulate approved new drugs by regulations issued through informal rulemaking under sections 501, 502, and 503 of the act.
