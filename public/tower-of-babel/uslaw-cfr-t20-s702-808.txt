20 C.F.R. § 702.808 — What weekly minimum rates apply to compensation for partial disability?
Citation: us:cfr:20:702.808
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-702.808
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 702 (Administration And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

There is no minimum rate for compensation paid for partial disability, whether temporary or permanent.
