22 U.S.C. § 2292a–1 — Appropriated funds; Presidential reports to Committees on Appropriations of the Senate and the House
Citation: us:usc:22:2292a–1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2292a–1
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 32 (Foreign Assistance); Subchapter I (International Development); Part IX (International Disaster Assistance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President shall submit quarterly reports to the Committee on Appropriations of the United States Senate and to the Committee on Appropriations of the House of Representatives on the programing and obligation of funds appropriated for International Disaster Assistance.
