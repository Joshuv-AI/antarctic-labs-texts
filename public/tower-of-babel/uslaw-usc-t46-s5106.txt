46 U.S.C. § 5106 — Load line certificate
Citation: us:usc:46:5106
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section5106
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part C (Load Lines Of Vessels); Chapter 51 (Load Lines). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
On finding that a load line survey of a vessel under this chapter is satisfactory and that the vessel’s load lines are marked correctly, the Secretary shall issue the vessel a load line certificate and deliver it to the owner, master, or individual in charge of the vessel.
(b)
The certificate shall be maintained as required by the Secretary.
