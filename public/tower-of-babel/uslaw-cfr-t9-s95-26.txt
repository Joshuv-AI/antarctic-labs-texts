9 C.F.R. § 95.26 — Blood meal, blood albumin, intestines, and other animal byproducts for industrial use; requirements for unrestricted entry.
Citation: us:cfr:9:95.26
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-95.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 95 (Sanitary Control Of Animal Byproducts (Except Casings), And Hay And Straw, Offered For Entry Into The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Blood meal, blood albumin, bone meal, intestines, or other animal materials intended for use in the industrial arts shall not be imported except subject to handling and treatment in accordance with § 95.27, unless such products originated in and were shipped directly from a region not declared by the Secretary of Agriculture to be infected with foot-and-mouth disease.
