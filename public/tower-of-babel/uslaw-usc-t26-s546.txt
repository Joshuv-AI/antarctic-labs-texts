26 U.S.C. § 546 — Income not placed on annual basis
Citation: us:usc:26:546
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section546
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 1 (Normal Taxes And Surtaxes); Subchapter G (Corporations Used To Avoid Income Tax On Shareholders); Part II (Personal Holding Companies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Section 443(b) (relating to computation of tax on change of annual accounting period) shall not apply in the computation of the personal holding company tax imposed by section 541.
