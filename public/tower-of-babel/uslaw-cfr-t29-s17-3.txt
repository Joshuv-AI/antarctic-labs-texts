29 C.F.R. § 17.3 — What programs and activities of the Department are subject to these regulations?
Citation: us:cfr:29:17.3
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-17.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 17 (Intergovernmental Review Of Department Of Labor Programs And Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary publishes in the Federal Register a list of the Department's programs and activities that are subject to these regulations.
