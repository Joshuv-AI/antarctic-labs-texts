46 U.S.C. § 10708 — Sale of property
Citation: us:usc:46:10708
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section10708
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part G (Merchant Seamen Protection And Relief); Chapter 107 (Effects Of Deceased Seamen). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A district court of the United States may direct the sale of any part of the property of a deceased seaman. Proceeds of the sale shall be held as wages of the seaman are held.
