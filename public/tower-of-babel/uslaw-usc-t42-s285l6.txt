42 U.S.C. § 285l–6 — Methods of controlling certain insect and vermin populations
Citation: us:usc:42:285l–6
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section285l–6
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter III (National Research Institutes); Part C (Specific Provisions Respecting National Research Institutes); Subpart 12 (National Institute Of Environmental Health Sciences). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of the Institute shall conduct or support research to identify or develop methods of controlling insect and vermin populations that transmit to humans diseases that have significant adverse health consequences.
