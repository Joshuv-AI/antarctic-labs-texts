40 C.F.R. § 139.17 — Elevator pits.
Citation: us:cfr:40:139.17
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-139.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 139 (Discharges Incidental To The Normal Operation Of Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The requirements in paragraph (b) of this section apply to the liquid that accumulates in, and is discharged from, the sumps of elevator wells.
(b) The discharge of untreated accumulated water and sediment from any elevator pit is prohibited.
