15 U.S.C. § 6553 — Annual reports
Citation: us:usc:15:6553
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section6553
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 91A (Promoting A Safe Internet For Children). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commission shall submit a report to the Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation of the Senate and the Committee on Energy and Commerce of the House of Representatives not later than March 31 of each year that describes the activities carried out under section 6552 1 of this title by the Commission during the preceding calendar year.
