12 U.S.C. § 1795h — Audit of financial transactions
Citation: us:usc:12:1795h
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section1795h
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 14 (Federal Credit Unions); Subchapter III (Central Liquidity Facility). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Comptroller General of the United States shall audit the Central Liquidity Facility under such rules and regulations as the Comptroller may prescribe.
