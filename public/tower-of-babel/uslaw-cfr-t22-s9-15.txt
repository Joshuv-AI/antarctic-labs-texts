22 C.F.R. § 9.15 — Assistance to the Historian's Office.
Citation: us:cfr:22:9.15
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-9.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 9 (Security Information Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All elements of the Department shall assist the Historian's Office in its preparation of the Foreign Relations of the United States (FRUS) series such as by providing prompt access to and, when possible, declassification of information deemed appropriate for inclusion in the FRUS.
