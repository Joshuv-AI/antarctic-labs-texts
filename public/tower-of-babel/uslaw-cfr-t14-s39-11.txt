14 C.F.R. § 39.11 — What actions do airworthiness directives require?
Citation: us:cfr:14:39.11
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-39.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 39 (Airworthiness Directives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Airworthiness directives specify inspections you must carry out, conditions and limitations you must comply with, and any actions you must take to resolve an unsafe condition.
