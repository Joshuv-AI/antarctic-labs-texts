29 C.F.R. § 1902.6 — Consultation with the National Institute for Occupational Safety and Health.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1902.6
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1902.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1902 (State Plans For The Development And Enforcement Of State Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Assistant Secretary will consult, as appropriate, with the Director of the National Institute for Occupational Safety and Health with regard to plans submitted by the States under this part.
