5 C.F.R. § 330.613 — OPM's role in CTAP.
Citation: us:cfr:5:330.613
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-330.613
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 330 (Recruitment, Selection, And Placement (General)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

OPM has oversight of CTAP and may conduct reviews of agency compliance and require corrective action at any time.
