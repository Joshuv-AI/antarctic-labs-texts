16 U.S.C. § 835h — Acts and regulations by Secretary of the Interior
Citation: us:usc:16:835h
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section835h
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 12D (Columbia Basin Project). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior is authorized to perform any and all acts and to prescribe such regulations as he may deem appropriate to carry out the provisions of sections 835d to 835g of this title.
