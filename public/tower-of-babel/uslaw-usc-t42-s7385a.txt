42 U.S.C. § 7385a — Subrogation of the United States
Citation: us:usc:42:7385a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section7385a
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 84 (Department Of Energy); Subchapter XVI (Energy Employees Occupational Illness Compensation Program); Part C (Treatment, Coordination, And Forfeiture Of Compensation And Benefits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Upon payment of compensation under this subchapter, the United States is subrogated for the amount of the payment to a right or claim that the individual to whom the payment was made may have against any person on account of injuries referred to in section 7385 of this title.
