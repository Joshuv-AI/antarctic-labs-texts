29 U.S.C. § 1906 — Construction; effect on agreements
Citation: us:usc:29:1906
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section1906
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 21 (Helen Keller National Center For Youths And Adults Who Are Deaf-Blind). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter shall not be construed as modifying or affecting any agreement between the Department of Education or any other department or agency of the United States and the Helen Keller Services for the Blind, Incorporated, or any successor to or assignee of such corporation, with respect to the Center.
