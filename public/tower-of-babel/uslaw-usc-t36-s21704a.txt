36 U.S.C. § 21704A — Nondiscrimination
Citation: us:usc:36:21704A
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section21704A
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 217 (The American Legion). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The requirements for holding a staff position in the corporation may not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, or national origin.
