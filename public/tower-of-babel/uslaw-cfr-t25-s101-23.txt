25 C.F.R. § 101.23 — Approval of articles of association and bylaws.
Citation: us:cfr:25:101.23
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-101.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 101 (Loans To Indians From The Revolving Loan Fund). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Articles of association and bylaws of relending organizations and cooperative associations require approval of the Commissioner if they make application for a revolving credit loan.
