11 C.F.R. § 200.5 — Agency considerations.
Citation: us:cfr:11:200.5
Creator: Federal Election Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-200.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 200 (Petitions For Rulemaking). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission's decision on the petition for rulemaking may include, but will not be limited to, the following considerations—
(a) The Commission's statutory authority;
(b) Policy considerations;
(c) The desirability of proceeding on a case-by-case-basis;
(d) The necessity or desirability of statutory revision;
(e) Available agency resources.
