29 C.F.R. § 2704.310 — Payment of award.
Citation: us:cfr:29:2704.310
Creator: Federal Mine Safety And Health Review Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-2704.310
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 2704 (Implementation Of The Equal Access To Justice Act In Commission Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Payment of awards made under the Equal Access to Justice Act by final orders of the Commission or its administrative law judge shall be in accordance with the applicable rules of the Department of Labor.
