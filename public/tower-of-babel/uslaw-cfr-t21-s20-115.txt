21 C.F.R. § 20.115 — Product codes for manufacturing or sales dates.
Citation: us:cfr:21:20.115
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-20.115
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 20 (Public Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Data or information in Food and Drug Administration files which provide a means for deciphering or decoding a manufacturing date or sales date or use date contained on the label or in labeling or otherwise used in connection with a product subject to the jurisdiction of the Food and Drug Administration are available for public disclosure.
