36 U.S.C. § 40704 — Restrictions
Citation: us:usc:36:40704
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section40704
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 407 (Corporation For The Promotion Of Rifle Practice And Firearms Safety); Subchapter I (Corporation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Profit.—
The corporation may not operate for profit.
(b) Use of Amounts Collected.—
Amounts collected under section 40703(3) and (5) of this title, including proceeds from the sale of firearms, ammunition, repair parts, and other supplies, may be used only to support the Civilian Marksmanship Program.
