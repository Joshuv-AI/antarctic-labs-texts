8 C.F.R. § 1241.15 — Lack of jurisdiction to review other country of removal.
Citation: us:cfr:8:1241.15
Creator: Executive Office For Immigration Review, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-1241.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 1241 (Apprehension And Detention Of Aliens Ordered Removed). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The immigration judges and the Board of Immigration Appeals have no jurisdiction to review any determination by officers of the Department of Homeland Security under 8 CFR 241.15.
