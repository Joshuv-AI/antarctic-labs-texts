42 U.S.C. § 291j–5 — Limitation on amounts of loans guaranteed or directly made
Citation: us:usc:42:291j–5
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section291j–5
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter IV (Construction And Modernization Of Hospitals And Other Medical Facilities); Part B (Loan Guarantees And Loans For Modernization And Construction Of Hospitals And Other Medical Facilities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The cumulative total of the principal of the loans outstanding at any time with respect to which guarantees have been issued, or which have been directly made, under this part may not exceed the lesser of—
(1)
such limitations as may be specified in appropriations Acts, or
(2)
in the case of loans covered by allotments for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1971, $500,000,000; for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1972, $1,000,000,000; and for each of the fiscal years ending June 30, 1973, and June 30, 1974, $1,500,000,000.
