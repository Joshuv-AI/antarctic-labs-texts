33 C.F.R. § 80.710 — Charleston Harbor, SC.
Citation: us:cfr:33:80.710
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-80.710
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 80 (Colregs Demarcation Lines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A line formed by the submerged north jetty from the shore to the west end of the north jetty.
(b) A line drawn from across the seaward extremity of the Charleston Harbor Jetties.
(c) A line drawn from the west end of the South Jetty across the South Entrance to Charleston Harbor to shore on a line formed by the submerged south jetty.
