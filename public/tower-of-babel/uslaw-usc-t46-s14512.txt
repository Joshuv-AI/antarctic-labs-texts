46 U.S.C. § 14512 — Standard tonnage measurement
Citation: us:usc:46:14512
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section14512
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part J (Measurement Of Vessels); Chapter 145 (Regulatory Measurement); Subchapter II (Formal Systems). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Secretary shall prescribe regulations for measuring the gross and net tonnages of a vessel under this subchapter. The regulations shall provide for tonnages comparable to the tonnages that could have been assigned under sections 4151 and 4153 of the Revised Statutes of the United States, as sections 4151 and 4153 existed immediately before the enactment of this section.
(b)
On application of the owner or master of a vessel of the United States used in foreign trade, the Secretary may attach an appendix to the vessel’s register stating the measurement of spaces that may be deducted from gross tonnage under laws and regulations of other countries but not under those of the United States.
