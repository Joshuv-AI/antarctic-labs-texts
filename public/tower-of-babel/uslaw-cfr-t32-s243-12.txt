32 C.F.R. § 243.12 — Required records retention.
Citation: us:cfr:32:243.12
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-243.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 243 (Department Of Defense Ratemaking Procedures For Civil Reserve Air Fleet Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The air carrier is required to retain copies of data submitted to support rate determination for a period identified in Subpart 4.7 of the Federal Acquisition Regulation, Contractor Records Retention.
