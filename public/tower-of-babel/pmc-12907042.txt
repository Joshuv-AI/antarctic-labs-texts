Towards the International Conference on Biophysics and Biomedical Sciences: ICBBS 2026
Authors: Sam-Yong Park, Haruki Nakamura
Published: 2025

The International Conference on Biophysics and Biomedical Sciences (ICBBS 2026) is to be held from November 23 (Monday) to November 28 (Saturday), 2026, at BEXCO in Busan, Korea. Jointly hosted by Korea and Japan, this conference will provide a meaningful platform for advancing the frontiers of biophysics and biomedical sciences, bridging communities across East Asia and beyond.

ICBBS 2026 will also serve as the 64th Annual Meeting of the Biophysical Society of Japan (BSJ) and the Federation Meeting of Korean Societies for Biomolecular and Biophysical Sciences (FKSBS), commemorating the anniversary of the normalization of diplomatic relations between Japan and Korea. This historic occasion symbolizes not only scientific collaboration but also the enduring spirit of academic and cultural unity between the two nations. The conference stands as a testament to the shared commitment to peace, innovation, and mutual respect through scientific dialogue.

This meeting will bring together a diverse, global community of researchers, educators, and students working in broad fields including molecular biophysics, structural biology, chemical biology, systems biology, and biomedical engineering. Many of these disciplines are being rapidly reshaped by artificial intelligence, cryo-electron microscopy, single-molecule techniques, and integrative modeling. The program will include keynote lectures, symposia, workshops, and poster sessions, covering the latest breakthroughs in both fundamental and applied biosciences. A special emphasis will be placed on promoting early-career researchers, including dedicated young investigator sessions, career-development symposia, and travel fellowships to support international participation. We believe that fostering the next generation of scientists is essential to the vitality and longevity of our field. In addition to academic excellence, ICBBS 2026 will feature programs to connect academia with industry, highlighting translational research, diagnostics, and therapeutic innovations. Opportunities for networking with biotech and pharmaceutical stakeholders will also be provided, aiming to catalyze real-world impact and collaborative development. Moreover, this conference will be enriched by cultural events, community outreach, and a welcoming environment to build lasting friendships across borders. Busan, a dynamic and vibrant coastal city, offers not only world-class convention facilities but also a unique blend of modern infrastructure and rich cultural heritage. From scenic coastlines to historic temples and culinary delights, we hope participants will enjoy both scientific exchange and memorable cultural experiences.

This special issue is dedicated to introducing several collaborative activities led by Korean and Japanese scientists, highlighting their long-standing efforts to advance biomolecular sciences together. These contributions illustrate the importance of transnational partnerships in solving complex scientific challenges.

We warmly invite you to join us at ICBBS 2026 in Busan. Your presence will make this event even more meaningful, and we sincerely look forward to welcoming you to this inspiring gathering of minds, bridging science and society. Please visit the Web site of ICBBS 2026 at https://www.icbbs2026.org/.
