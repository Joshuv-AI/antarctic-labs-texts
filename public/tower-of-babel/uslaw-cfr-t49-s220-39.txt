49 C.F.R. § 220.39 — Continuous radio monitoring.
Citation: us:cfr:49:220.39
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-220.39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 220 (Railroad Communications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each radio used in a railroad operation shall be turned on to the appropriate channel as designated in § 220.23 and adjusted to receive communications.
