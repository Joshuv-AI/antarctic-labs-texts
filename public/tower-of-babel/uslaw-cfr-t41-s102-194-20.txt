41 C.F.R. § 102-194.20 — -194.20 What is an electronic Standard or Optional form?
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-194.20
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-194.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-194 (194—Standard And Optional Forms Management Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An electronic Standard or Optional form is an officially prescribed set of data residing in an electronic medium that is used to produce a mirror-like image or as near to a mirror-like image as the creation software will allow of the officially prescribed form.
