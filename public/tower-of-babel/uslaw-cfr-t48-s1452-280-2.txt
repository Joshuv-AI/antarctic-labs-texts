48 C.F.R. § 1452.280-2 — 1452.280-2 Notice of Indian Economic Enterprise set-aside.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1452.280-2
Creator: Chapter 14—Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1452.280-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1452 (Solicitation Provisions And Contract Clauses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As prescribed in 1480.503(e)(2), insert the following clause in solicitations and contracts involving Indian Economic Enterprise set-asides. If the solicitation is oral, information substantially identical to that contained in the provision must be given to potential offerors.
