x=a+u\in A_{\mathfrak{l}}+\prod_{\mathfrak{L}\mid\mathfrak{l}}L^{{\rm max}(t_{L}-1,0)}\mathcal{O}_{K,\mathfrak{L}}
, we firstly prove that
\mathfrak{m}_{t}x\subset\mathfrak{m}_{t}
. The product
x\mathfrak{m}_{t}=a\mathfrak{l}A_{\mathfrak{l}}+\left[u\mathfrak{l}A_{\mathfrak{l}}+(a+u)\prod_{\mathfrak{L}\mid\mathfrak{l}}L^{t_{\mathfrak{L}}}\mathcal{O}_{K,\mathfrak{L}}\right]\subset\mathfrak{l}A_{\mathfrak{l}}+\prod_{\mathfrak{L}\mid\mathfrak{l}}L^{t_{\mathfrak{L}}}\mathcal{O}_{K,\mathfrak{L}}=\mathfrak{m}_{t}
.

Conversely, let
x\in(\mathfrak{m}_{t}:\mathfrak{m}_{t})
, one can deduce that as
A_{\mathfrak{l}}
-modules, the multiplication-by-
x
map induces
A_{\mathfrak{l}}
linear map on
\mathfrak{m}_{t}
whose characteristic polynomial is defined over
A_{\mathfrak{l}}
. Hence
x\in\mathcal{O}_{K,\mathfrak{l}}
.

Write
x=(x_{\mathfrak{L}})_{\mathfrak{L}\mid\mathfrak{l}}\in\mathcal{O}_{K,\mathfrak{l}}
, consider the diagonal reduction
\pi:R_{\mathfrak{l},t}\rightarrow\prod_{\mathfrak{L}\mid\mathfrak{l}}\mathcal{O}_{K,\mathfrak{L}}/\mathfrak{L}
, it has kernel
{\rm ker}(\pi)=\mathfrak{m}_{t}
and
R_{\mathfrak{l},t}/\mathfrak{m}_{t}\cong A_{\mathfrak{l}}/\mathfrak{l}A_{\mathfrak{l}}
. As
x\in(\mathfrak{m}_{t}:\mathfrak{m}_{t})
, the multiplication-by-
x
map induces a well-define
A_{\mathfrak{l}}
-linear map
\bar{\lambda}_{x}:R_{\mathfrak{l},t}/\mathfrak{m}_{t}\rightarrow R_{\mathfrak{l},t}/\mathfrak{m}_{t}
. Let
\rho:\mathcal{O}_{K,\mathfrak{l}}\rightarrow\prod_{\mathfrak{L}\mid\mathfrak{l}}\mathcal{O}_{K,\mathfrak{L}}/\mathfrak{L}
and set
\rho(x)=(\bar{x}_{\mathfrak{L}})_{\mathfrak{L}}
, then for any
a\in A_{\mathfrak{l}}
, we have

\bar{\lambda}_{x}(\pi(a))=\pi(xa)=\rho(x)\pi(a)=(\bar{x}_{\mathfrak{L}}\bar{a})_{\mathfrak{L}}\in A_{\mathfrak{l}}/\mathfrak{l}A_{\mathfrak{l}},

thus all
\mathfrak{L}
-components of
(\bar{x}_{\mathfrak{L}})_{\mathfrak{L}}
are equal. Set
n_{\mathfrak{L}}:={\rm max}\{t_{\mathfrak{L}}-1,0\}
and
N:={\rm max}_{\mathfrak{L}\mid\mathfrak{l}}\{n_{\mathfrak{L}}\}
, consider the map
\iota:A_{\mathfrak{l}}/\mathfrak{l}^{N}\rightarrow\prod_{\mathfrak{L}\mid\mathfrak{l}}\mathcal{O}_{K,\mathfrak{L}}/\mathfrak{L}^{n_{\mathfrak{L}}}
induced from the diagonal embedding of
A_{\mathfrak{l}}
into
\prod_{\mathfrak{L}\mid\mathfrak{l}}\mathcal{O}_{K,\mathfrak{L}}
. Because
\bar{x}_{\mathfrak{L}}
are equal for all
\mathfrak{L}\mid\mathfrak{l}
, one can apply Hensel’s lifting lemma to find an element

such that

Thus we have

, the proof is now complete.

Proof.

Let

from Theorem

(i). From CM uniformization (ref. section 2.2) it is well known that there is a 1-1 correspondence between the isomorphism classes of Drinfeld modules CM by

of

, we claim that

It’s clear that

and
\Phi
factors through

. For injectivity of
