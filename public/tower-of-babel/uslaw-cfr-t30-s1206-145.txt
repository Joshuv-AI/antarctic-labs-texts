30 C.F.R. § 1206.145 — What records must I keep in order to support my calculations of royalty under this subpart?
Citation: us:cfr:30:1206.145
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1206.145
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1206 (Product Valuation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you value your gas under this subpart, you must retain all data relevant to the determination of the royalty that you paid. You can find recordkeeping requirements in parts 1207 and 1212 of this chapter.
(a) You must show:
(1) How you calculated the royalty value, including all allowable deductions; and
(2) How you complied with this subpart.
(b) Upon request, you must submit all data to ONRR. You must comply with any such requirement within the time that ONRR specifies.
