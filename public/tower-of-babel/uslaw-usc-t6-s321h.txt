6 U.S.C. § 321h — Use of national private sector networks in emergency response
Citation: us:usc:6:321h
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section321h
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 1 (Homeland Security Organization); Subchapter V (National Emergency Management). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To the maximum extent practicable, the Secretary shall use national private sector networks and infrastructure for emergency response to chemical, biological, radiological, nuclear, or explosive disasters, and other major disasters.
