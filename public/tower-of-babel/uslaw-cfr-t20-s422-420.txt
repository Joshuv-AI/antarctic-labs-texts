20 C.F.R. § 422.420 — May you inspect and copy our records related to the debt?
Citation: us:cfr:20:422.420
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-422.420
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 422 (Organization And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may inspect and copy our records related to the debt. You must notify us of your intention to review our records. After you notify us, we will arrange with you the place and time the records will be available to you. At our discretion, we may send copies of the records to you.
