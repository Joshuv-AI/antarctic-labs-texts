5 C.F.R. § 1600.14 — Effect of election to be covered by BRS.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1600.14
Creator: Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1600.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1600 (Employee Contribution Elections, Investment Elections, And Automatic Enrollment Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If a uniformed service member elects to be covered by BRS, the member may make a contribution election at any time.
(b) [Reserved]
(c) If the member had elected to make TSP contributions while not covered by BRS, the election remains effective until the member makes a new election.
(d) Agency automatic (1%) contributions for all members covered under this section and, if applicable, agency matching contributions attributable to employee contributions must begin at the time set forth in § 1600.19(c).
