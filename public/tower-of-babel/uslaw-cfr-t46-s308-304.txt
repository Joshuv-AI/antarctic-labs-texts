46 C.F.R. § 308.304 — Reporting casualties and filing claims.
Citation: us:cfr:46:308.304
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-308.304
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 308 (War Risk Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All casualties occurring after insurance under a binder has attached shall be reported promptly to, and all claim documents filed with, “Maritime Administration, Attention: Chief, Office of Marine Insurance.”
