45 C.F.R. § 1801.40 — Travel expenses of finalists.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1801.40
Creator: Harry S. Truman Scholarship Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1801.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1801 (Harry S. Truman Scholarship Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Foundation will provide partial funding for intercity round-trip transportation from the finalist's nominating institution to the interview site. The Foundation does not reimburse finalists for lodging, meals, local transportation, or other expenses. The Foundation announces the terms and conditions of support on its website (http://www.truman.gov) and in the Bulletin of Information.
