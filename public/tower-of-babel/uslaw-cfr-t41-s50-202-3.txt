41 C.F.R. § 50-202.3 — -202.3 Learners, student learners, apprentices, and handicapped workers.
Citation: us:cfr:41:50-202.3
Creator: Chapter 50—Public Contracts, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-50-202.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 50-202 (202—Minimum Wage Determinations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Learners, student learners, apprentices, and handicapped workers may be employed at less than the minimum wage prescribed in § 50-202.2 to the same extent such employment is permitted under section 14 of the Fair Labor Standards Act.
