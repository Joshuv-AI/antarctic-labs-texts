5 C.F.R. § 550.901 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:5:550.901
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-550.901
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 550 (Pay Administration (General)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes the regulations required by sections 5545(d) and 5548(b) of title 5, United States Code, for the payment of differentials for duty involving unusual physical hardship or hazard to employees.
