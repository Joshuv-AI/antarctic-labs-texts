12 C.F.R. § 708a.109 — a.109 Other regulatory oversight of methods and procedures of membership vote.
Citation: us:cfr:12:708a.109
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-708a.109
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 708a (PART 708a—BANK CONVERSIONS AND MERGERS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The federal or state regulatory agency that will have jurisdiction over the financial institution after conversion must verify the membership vote and may direct that a new vote be taken, if it disapproves of the methods by which the membership vote was taken or the procedures applicable to the membership vote.
