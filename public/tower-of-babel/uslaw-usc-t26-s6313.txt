26 U.S.C. § 6313 — Fractional parts of a cent
Citation: us:usc:26:6313
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section6313
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 64 (Collection); Subchapter B (Receipt Of Payment). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the payment of any tax imposed by this title, a fractional part of a cent shall be disregarded unless it amounts to one-half cent or more, in which case it shall be increased to 1 cent.
