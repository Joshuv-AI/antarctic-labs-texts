40 C.F.R. § 81.455 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:40:81.455
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-81.455
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 81 (Designation Of Areas For Air Quality Planning Purposes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart identifies interstate transport regions established for national ambient air quality standards pursuant to section 184 or section 176A of the Clean Air Act.
