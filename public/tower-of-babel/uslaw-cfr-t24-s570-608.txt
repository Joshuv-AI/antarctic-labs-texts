24 C.F.R. § 570.608 — Lead-based paint.
Citation: us:cfr:24:570.608
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Community Planning And Development, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-570.608
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 570 (Community Development Block Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Lead-Based Paint Poisoning Prevention Act (42 U.S.C. 4821-4846), the Residential Lead-Based Paint Hazard Reduction Act of 1992 (42 U.S.C. 4851-4856), and implementing regulations at part 35, subparts A, B, J, K, and R of this part apply to activities under this program.
