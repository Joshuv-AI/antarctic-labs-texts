SUMMARY: On August 14, 2025, the Census Bureau published a final rule 
in

[[Page 41777]]

the Federal Register entitled, ``Foreign Trade Regulations (FTR): 
Clarification of Filing Requirements Regarding In-Transit Shipments and 
Other FTR Provisions''. This document referenced incorrect amendatory 
language in the List of Subjects in 15 CFR part 30 section.

DATES: Effective September 15, 2025.

Bureau, 4600 Silver Hill Road, Washington, DC 20233-6010 by email at 
[email protected].
