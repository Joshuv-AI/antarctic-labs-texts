25 C.F.R. § 48.108 — How will the Bureau determine appropriate consideration for a lease?
Citation: us:cfr:25:48.108
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-48.108
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 48 (Leases Of Land Or Facilities Of Bureau-Operated Schools And Fundraising Activities At Bureau-Operated Schools). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Bureau will determine what consideration is appropriate for a lease by considering, at a minimum, the following factors:
(a) Fair market value and the indirect and direct costs of the lease; and
(b) Whether there will be a net financial benefit to the school.
