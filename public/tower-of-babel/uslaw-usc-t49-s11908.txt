49 U.S.C. § 11908 — Relation to other Federal criminal penalties
Citation: us:usc:49:11908
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section11908
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Rail); Chapter 119 (Civil And Criminal Penalties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding section 3571 of title 18, United States Code, the criminal penalties provided for in this chapter are the exclusive criminal penalties for violations of this part.
