15 U.S.C. § 2689 — Prohibited acts
Citation: us:usc:15:2689
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section2689
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 53 (Toxic Substances Control); Subchapter IV (Lead Exposure Reduction). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It shall be unlawful for any person to fail or refuse to comply with a provision of this subchapter or with any rule or order issued under this subchapter.
