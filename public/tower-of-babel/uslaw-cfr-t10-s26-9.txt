10 C.F.R. § 26.9 — Specific exemptions.
Citation: us:cfr:10:26.9
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-26.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 26 (Fitness For Duty Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon application of any interested person or on its own initiative, the Commission may grant such exemptions from the requirements of the regulations in this part as it determines are authorized by law and will not endanger life or property or the common defense and security, and are otherwise in the public interest.
