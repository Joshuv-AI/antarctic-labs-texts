42 U.S.C. § 3939 — Applicability of antitrust laws
Citation: us:usc:42:3939
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section3939
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 49 (National Housing Partnerships). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing contained herein shall affect the applicability of the Federal antitrust laws to the activities of the corporation and the partnership created under this chapter and of the persons participating therein or in partnerships, limited partnerships, or joint ventures with either of them.
