48 C.F.R. § 2509.405-2 — 2509.405-2 Restrictions on subcontracting.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2509.405-2
Creator: Chapter 25—National Science Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2509.405-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2509 (Contractor Qualifications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The NSF Deputy Director may authorize a contracting officer to consent to a subcontract with a contractor debarred, suspended, or proposed for debarment. He or she will prepare a written statement of the compelling reasons for such approval.
