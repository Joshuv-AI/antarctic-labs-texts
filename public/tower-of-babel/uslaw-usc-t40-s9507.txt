40 U.S.C. § 9507 — Control of expenditures
Citation: us:usc:40:9507
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section9507
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part D (Public Buildings, Grounds, And Parks In The District Of Columbia); Chapter 95 (Washington Aqueduct And Other Public Works In The District Of Columbia). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Unless expressly provided for by law, the Secretary of the Army shall direct the expenditure of amounts appropriated for the Washington Aqueduct and for other public works in the District of Columbia.
