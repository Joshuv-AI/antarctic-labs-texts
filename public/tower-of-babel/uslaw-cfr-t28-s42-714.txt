28 C.F.R. § 42.714 — Special benefits.
Citation: us:cfr:28:42.714
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-42.714
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 42 (Nondiscrimination; Equal Employment Opportunity; Policies And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a recipient operating a program or activity provides special benefits to the elderly or to children, such use of age distinctions shall be presumed to be necessary to the normal operation of the program or activity, notwithstanding the provisions of § 42.712.
