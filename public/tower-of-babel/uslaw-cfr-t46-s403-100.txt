46 C.F.R. § 403.100 — Applicability of system of accounts and reports.
Citation: us:cfr:46:403.100
Creator: Coast Guard (Great Lakes Pilotage), Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-403.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 403 (Great Lakes Pilotage Uniform Accounting System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each Association shall keep its books of account, records and memoranda, and make reports to the Director in accordance with the guidelines of the Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP) issued by the Financial Accounting Standards Board. These guidelines are available by writing to the Director, Great Lakes Pilotage at the address listed in § 401.110(a)(9) of this chapter.
