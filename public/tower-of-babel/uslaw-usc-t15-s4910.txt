15 U.S.C. § 4910 — Nonduplication
Citation: us:usc:15:4910
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section4910
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 75 (National Trade Data Bank). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall ensure that information systems created or developed pursuant to this chapter do not unnecessarily duplicate information systems available from other Federal agencies or from the private sector.
