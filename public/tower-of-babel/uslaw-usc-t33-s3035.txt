33 U.S.C. § 3035 — Procurement of personnel
Citation: us:usc:33:3035
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section3035
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 43 (National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration Commissioned Officer Corps); Subchapter II (Appointment And Promotion Of Officers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may take such measures as the Secretary determines necessary in order to obtain recruits for the commissioned officer corps of the Administration, including advertising.
