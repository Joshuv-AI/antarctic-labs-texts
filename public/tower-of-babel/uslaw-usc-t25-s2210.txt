25 U.S.C. § 2210 — Tax exemption
Citation: us:usc:25:2210
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section2210
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 24 (Indian Land Consolidation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All lands or interests in land acquired by the United States for an Indian or Indian tribe under authority of this chapter shall be exempt from Federal, State and local taxation.
