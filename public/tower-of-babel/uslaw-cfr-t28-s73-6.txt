28 C.F.R. § 73.6 — Relation to other statutes.
Citation: us:cfr:28:73.6
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-73.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 73 (Notifications To The Attorney General By Agents Of Foreign Governments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The filing of a notification under this section shall not be deemed compliance with the requirements of the Foreign Agents Registration Act of 1938, as amended, 22 U.S.C. 611, et seq., nor compliance with any other statute.
