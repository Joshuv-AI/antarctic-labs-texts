14 U.S.C. § 524 — Enforcement of coastwise trade laws
Citation: us:usc:14:524
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section524
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 5 (Functions And Powers); Subchapter II (Life Saving And Law Enforcement Authorities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Officers and members of the Coast Guard are authorized to enforce chapter 551 of title 46. The Secretary shall establish a program for these officers and members to enforce that chapter.
