28 U.S.C. § 1824 — Mileage fees under summons as both witness and juror
Citation: us:usc:28:1824
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1824
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part V (Procedure); Chapter 119 (Evidence; Witnesses). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No constructive or double mileage fees shall be allowed by reason of any person being summoned both as a witness and a juror.
