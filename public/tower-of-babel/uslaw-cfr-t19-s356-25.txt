19 C.F.R. § 356.25 — Witnesses.
Citation: us:cfr:19:356.25
Creator: International Trade Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-356.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 356 (Procedures And Rules For Article 10.12 Of The United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Witnesses summoned before the Department shall be paid the same fees and mileage that are paid witnesses in the courts of the United States.
