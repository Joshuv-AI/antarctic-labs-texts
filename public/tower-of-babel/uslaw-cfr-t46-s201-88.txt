46 C.F.R. § 201.88 — Postponement or change of place by presiding officer.
Citation: us:cfr:46:201.88
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-201.88
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 201 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If, in the judgment of the presiding officer, convenience or necessity so requires he may postpone the time or change the place of hearing.
