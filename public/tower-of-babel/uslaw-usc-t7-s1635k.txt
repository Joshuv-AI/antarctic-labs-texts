7 U.S.C. § 1635k — Mandatory reporting of wholesale pork cuts
Citation: us:usc:7:1635k
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1635k
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 38 (Distribution And Marketing Of Agricultural Products); Subchapter II (Livestock Mandatory Reporting); Part C (Swine Reporting). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Reporting
The corporate officers or officially designated representatives of each packer shall report to the Secretary information concerning the price and volume of wholesale pork cuts, as the Secretary determines is necessary and appropriate.
(b) Publication
The Secretary shall publish information reported under subsection (a) as the Secretary determines necessary and appropriate.
