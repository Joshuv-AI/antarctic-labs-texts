5 U.S.C. § 8912 — Jurisdiction of courts
Citation: us:usc:5:8912
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section8912
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart G (Insurance And Annuities); Chapter 89 (Health Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The district courts of the United States have original jurisdiction, concurrent with the United States Court of Federal Claims, of a civil action or claim against the United States founded on this chapter.
