5 U.S.C. § 8444 — Rights of a named individual with an insurable interest
Citation: us:usc:5:8444
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section8444
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart G (Insurance And Annuities); Chapter 84 (Federal Employees’ Retirement System); Subchapter IV (Survivor Annuities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The annuity of a survivor named under section 8420(a) is 55 percent of the reduced annuity of the retired employee or Member determined under paragraph (2) of such section 8420(a). The annuity of the survivor commences on the day after the retired employee or Member dies. This annuity and the right thereto terminate on the last day of the month before the survivor dies.
