28 C.F.R. § 5.302 — Exemptions under sections 3(b) and (c) of the Act.
Citation: us:cfr:28:5.302
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-5.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 5 (Administration And Enforcement Of Foreign Agents Registration Act Of 1938, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The exemptions provided by sections 3(b) and (c) of the Act shall not be available to any person described therein unless he has filed with the Secretary of State a fully executed Notification of Status with a Foreign Government (Form D.S. 394).
