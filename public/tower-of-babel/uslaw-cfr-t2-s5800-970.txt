2 C.F.R. § 5800.970 — Nonprocurement transaction.

Creator: Election Assistance Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-5800.970
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 5800 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

While the Commission treats all payments made to states under 42 U.S.C. 15301, 15302 and 15401 as grants, this part does not apply to grants made to states and political subdivisions therein.
