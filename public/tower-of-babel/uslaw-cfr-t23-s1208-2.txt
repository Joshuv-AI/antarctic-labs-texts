23 C.F.R. § 1208.2 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:23:1208.2
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration And Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-1208.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 1208 (National Minimum Drinking Age). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to clarify the provisions which a State must have incorporated into its laws in order to prevent the withholding of Federal-aid highway funds for noncompliance with the National Minimum Drinking Age.
