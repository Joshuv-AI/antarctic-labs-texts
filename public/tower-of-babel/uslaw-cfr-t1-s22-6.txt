1 C.F.R. § 22.6 — Code designation.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 1 (General Provisions), Part 22 (Preparation Of Notices And Proposed Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-23 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The area of the Code of Federal Regulations directly affected by a proposed regulatory action shall be identified by placing the appropriate CFR citation immediately below the name of the issuing agency. For example:
1 CFR part 22
