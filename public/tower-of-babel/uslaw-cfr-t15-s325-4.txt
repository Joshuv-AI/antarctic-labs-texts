15 C.F.R. § 325.4 — Calculating time periods.
Citation: us:cfr:15:325.4
Creator: International Trade Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-325.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 325 (Export Trade Certificates Of Review). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) When these regulations require action to be taken within a fixed time period, and the last day of the time period falls on a non-working day, the time period shall be extended to the next working day.
(b) The day after an application is deemed submitted shall be deemed the first of the days within which the Secretary must make a determination on the application.
