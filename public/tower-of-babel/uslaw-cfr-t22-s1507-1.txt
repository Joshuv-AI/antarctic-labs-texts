22 C.F.R. § 1507.1 — Purpose.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1507 (Rules Safeguarding Personal Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to set forth the basic policies of the African Development Foundation (“the Foundation” or “ADF”) governing the maintenance of systems of records containing personal information as defined in the Privacy Act of 1974 (5 U.S.C. 552a).
