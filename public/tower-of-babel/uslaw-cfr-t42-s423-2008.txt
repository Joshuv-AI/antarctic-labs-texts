42 C.F.R. § 423.2008 — Parties to the proceedings on a request for an ALJ hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:42:423.2008
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-423.2008
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 423 (Voluntary Medicare Prescription Drug Benefit). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The enrollee (or the enrollee's representative) who filed the request for hearing is the only party to the proceedings on a request for an ALJ hearing.
