40 C.F.R. § 1068.440 — How do I ask EPA to reinstate my suspended certificate?
Citation: us:cfr:40:1068.440
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1068.440
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1068 (General Compliance Provisions For Highway, Stationary, And Nonroad Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Send us a written report asking us to reinstate your suspended certificate. In your report, identify the reason for the SEA failure, propose a remedy, and commit to a date for carrying it out. In your proposed remedy include any quality control measures you propose to keep the problem from happening again.
(b) Give us test data from production engines/equipment showing that engines/equipment in the remedied family comply with all the emission standards that apply.
