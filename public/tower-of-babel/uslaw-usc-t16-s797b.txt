16 U.S.C. § 797b — Duty to keep Congress fully and currently informed
Citation: us:usc:16:797b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section797b
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 12 (Federal Regulation And Development Of Power); Subchapter I (Regulation Of The Development Of Water Power And Resources). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission shall keep the Committee on Energy and Commerce of the United States House of Representatives and the Committee on Energy and Natural Resources of the United States Senate fully and currently informed regarding actions of the Commission with respect to the provisions of Part I of the Federal Power Act [16 U.S.C. 791a et seq.].
