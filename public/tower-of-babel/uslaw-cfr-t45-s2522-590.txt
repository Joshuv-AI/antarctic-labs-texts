45 C.F.R. § 2522.590 — Who develops my performance measures?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2522.590
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2522.590
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2522 (Americorps Participants, Programs, And Applicants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You are responsible for developing your program-specific performance measures through your own internal process.
(b) In addition, AmeriCorps may, in consultation with grantees, establish performance measures that will apply to all AmeriCorps-sponsored programs, which you will be responsible for collecting and meeting.
