42 C.F.R. § 405.920 — Initial determinations.
Citation: us:cfr:42:405.920
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-405.920
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 405 (Federal Health Insurance For The Aged And Disabled). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After a claim is filed with the appropriate contractor in the manner and form described in subpart C of part 424 of this chapter, the contractor must—
(a) Determine if the items and services furnished are covered or otherwise reimbursable under title XVIII of the Act;
(b) Determine any amounts payable and make payment accordingly; and
(c) Notify the parties to the initial determination of the determination in accordance with § 405.921.
