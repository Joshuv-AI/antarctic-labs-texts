20 C.F.R. § 416.919b — b When we will not purchase a consultative examination.
Citation: us:cfr:20:416.919b
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-416.919b
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 416 (Supplemental Security Income For The Aged, Blind, And Disabled). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We will not purchase a consultative examination in situations including, but not limited to, the following situations:
(a) When any issues about your actual performance of substantial gainful activity have not been resolved;
(b) When you do not meet all of the nondisability requirements.
