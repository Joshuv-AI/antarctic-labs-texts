40 C.F.R. § 124.61 — Final environmental impact statement.
Citation: us:cfr:40:124.61
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-124.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 124 (Procedures For Decisionmaking). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No final NPDES permit for a new source shall be issued until at least 30 days after the date of issuance of a final environmental impact statement if one is required under 40 CFR 6.805.
