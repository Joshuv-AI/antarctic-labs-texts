49 U.S.C. § 20903 — Reports not evidence in civil actions for damages
Citation: us:usc:49:20903
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section20903
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Safety); Chapter 209 (Accidents And Incidents). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No part of an accident or incident report filed by a railroad carrier under section 20901 of this title or made by the Secretary of Transportation under section 20902 of this title may be used in a civil action for damages resulting from a matter mentioned in the report.
