12 C.F.R. § 1081.213 — Partial summary disposition.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1081.213
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1081.213
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1081 (Rules Of Practice For Adjudication Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If on a motion for summary disposition under § 1081.212 a decision is not rendered upon the whole case or for all the relief asked and a hearing is necessary, the hearing officer shall issue an order specifying the facts that appear without substantial controversy and directing further proceedings in the action. The facts so specified shall be deemed established.
