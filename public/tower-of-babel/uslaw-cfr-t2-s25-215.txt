2 C.F.R. § 25.215 — Requirements for agency information systems.
Citation: us:cfr:2:25.215
Creator: Office Of Management And Budget Government-Wide Guidance For Federal Financial Assistance
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-25.215
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 25 (Unique Entity Identifier And System For Award Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each Federal agency that awards Federal financial assistance (see § 25.400) must ensure that its information systems are able to both accept and transmit the UEI as the universal identifier for Federal financial assistance applicants and recipients.
