49 C.F.R. § 32.210 — To whom must I distribute my drug-free workplace statement?
Citation: us:cfr:49:32.210
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-32.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 32 (Governmentwide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must require that a copy of the statement described in § 32.205 be given to each employee who will be engaged in the performance of any Federal award.
