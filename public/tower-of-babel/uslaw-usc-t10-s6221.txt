10 U.S.C. § 6221 — Prohibition on international inspections of Department of Energy facilities unless protection of restricted data is certified
Citation: us:usc:10:6221
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section6221
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part VI (Elements Of Department Of Defense And Other Matters); Subpart B (Atomic Energy Defense); Chapter 605 (Safeguards And Security Matters); Subchapter I (Safeguards And Security). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Energy may not allow an inspection of a national security laboratory or nuclear weapons production facility by the International Atomic Energy Agency until the Secretary certifies to Congress that no Restricted Data will be revealed during such inspection.
