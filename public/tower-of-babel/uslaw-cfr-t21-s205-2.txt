21 C.F.R. § 205.2 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:21:205.2
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-205.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 205 (Guidelines For State Licensing Of Wholesale Prescription Drug Distributors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to implement the Prescription Drug Marketing Act of 1987 by providing minimum standards, terms, and conditions for the licensing by State licensing authorities of persons who engage in wholesale distributions in interstate commerce of prescription drugs.
