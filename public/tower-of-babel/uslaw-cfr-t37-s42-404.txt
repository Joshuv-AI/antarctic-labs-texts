37 C.F.R. § 42.404 — Derivation fee.
Citation: us:cfr:37:42.404
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-42.404
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 42 (Trial Practice Before The Patent Trial And Appeal Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A derivation fee set forth in § 42.15(c) must accompany the petition.
(b) No filing date will be accorded to the petition until payment is complete.
