48 C.F.R. § 970.1504-111 — 970.1504-111 The maximum total available fee amount for a contract.
Citation: us:cfr:48:970.1504-111
Creator: Chapter 9—Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-970.1504-111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 970 (Doe Management And Operating Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The maximum total available fee amount for a contract is the sum of the maximum total available fee amounts of the contract's one-year periods.
