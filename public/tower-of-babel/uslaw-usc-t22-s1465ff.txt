22 U.S.C. § 1465ff — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:22:1465ff
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1465ff
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 18 (United States Information And Educational Exchange Programs); Subchapter V–B (Television Broadcasting To Cuba). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this subchapter—
(1)
the term “licensee” has the meaning provided in section 153(c) 1 of title 47;
(2)
the term “incumbent domestic licensee” means a licensee as provided in section 153(c) 1 of title 47 that was broadcasting a television signal as of January 1, 1989;
(3)
the term “objectionable interference” shall be applied in the same manner as such term is applied under regulations of the Federal Communications Commission to other domestic broadcasters; and
(4)
the term “appropriate committees of Congress” includes the Committee on Foreign Affairs and the Committee on Energy and Commerce of the House of Representatives and the Committee on Foreign Relations of the Senate.
