5 C.F.R. § 1201.75 — Taking depositions.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1201.75
Creator: Merit Systems Protection Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1201.75
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1201 (Practices And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Depositions may be taken by any method agreed upon by the parties. The person providing information is subject to penalties for intentional false statements.
