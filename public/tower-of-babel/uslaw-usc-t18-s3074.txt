18 U.S.C. § 3074 — Exception of governmental officials
Citation: us:usc:18:3074
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section3074
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part II (Criminal Procedure); Chapter 204 (Rewards For Information Concerning Terrorist Acts And Espionage). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No officer or employee of any governmental entity who, while in the performance of his or her official duties, furnishes the information described in section 3071 shall be eligible for any monetary reward under this chapter.
