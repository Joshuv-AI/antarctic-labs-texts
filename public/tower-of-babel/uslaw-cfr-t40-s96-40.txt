40 C.F.R. § 96.40 — State trading program budget.
Citation: us:cfr:40:96.40
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-96.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 96 (Nox Budget Trading Program And Cair Nox And So2 Trading Programs For State Implementation Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The State trading program budget allocated by the permitting authority under § 96.42 for a control period will equal the total number of tons of NOX emissions apportioned to the NOX Budget units under § 96.4 in the State for the control period, as determined by the applicable, approved State implementation plan.
