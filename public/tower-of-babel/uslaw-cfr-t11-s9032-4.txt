11 C.F.R. § 9032.4 — Contribution.
Citation: us:cfr:11:9032.4
Creator: Federal Election Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-9032.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 9032 (Definitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of this subchapter, contribution has the same meaning given the term under 52 U.S.C. 30101(8)(A) and 11 CFR part 100, subparts B and C, except as provided at 11 CFR 9034.4(b)(4).
