7 C.F.R. § 1216.19 — Peanut producer organization.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1216.19
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Miscellaneous Commodities), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1216.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1216 (Peanut Promotion, Research, And Information Order). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Peanut producer organization means a state-legislated peanut promotion, research, and education commission or organization. For states without a state-legislated peanut promotion, research, and education commission or organization, “peanut producer organization” means any organization which has the primary purpose of representing peanut producers and has peanut producers as members.
