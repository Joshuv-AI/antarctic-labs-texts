5 C.F.R. § 839.1111 — If I elect to change my retirement coverage under the FERCCA, can I change the election I originally made at retirement for survivor benefits?
Citation: us:cfr:5:839.1111
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-839.1111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 839 (Correction Of Retirement Coverage Errors Under The Federal Erroneous Retirement Coverage Corrections Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Yes, if you elect to change your retirement coverage under the FERCCA, you will have an opportunity to change the election you made for survivor benefits.
(b) If you elect less than the maximum survivor benefit, your spouse's consent is necessary in accordance with § 831.614 or § 842.603(a)(1) of this chapter, as applicable.
