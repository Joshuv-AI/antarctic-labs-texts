31 C.F.R. § 550.306 — Government of Eritrea.
Citation: us:cfr:31:550.306
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-550.306
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 550 (Ethiopia Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term Government of Eritrea means the Government of Eritrea, any political subdivision, agency, or instrumentality thereof, including the Bank of Eritrea, and any person owned, controlled, or directed by, or acting for or on behalf of, the Government of Eritrea.
