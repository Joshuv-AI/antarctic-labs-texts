31 C.F.R. § 355.8 — How can I get a replacement fiscal agency check?
Citation: us:cfr:31:355.8
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-355.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 355 (Regulations Governing Fiscal Agency Checks). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The payor Federal Reserve Bank will issue a replacement fiscal agency check if:
(a) You submit written notice:
(b) The check is unpaid;
(c) We determine that recovery of the original check is unlikely; and
(d) The payee and endorsee, if any, of the check execute the required indemnification agreement.
