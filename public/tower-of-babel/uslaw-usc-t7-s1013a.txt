7 U.S.C. § 1013a — Benefits extended to Puerto Rico and Virgin Islands; “county” defined; payments to Governor or fiscal agent of county
Citation: us:usc:7:1013a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1013a
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 33 (Farm Tenancy); Subchapter III (Land Conservation And Land Utilization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of this subchapter shall extend to Puerto Rico and the Virgin Islands. In the case of Alaska, Puerto Rico, and the Virgin Islands, the term “county” as used in this subchapter may be the entire area, or any subdivision thereof as may be determined by the Secretary, and payments under section 1012 of this title shall be made to the Governor or to the fiscal agent of such subdivision.
