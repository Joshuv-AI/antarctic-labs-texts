20 U.S.C. § 6572 — Agreements and records
Citation: us:usc:20:6572
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section6572
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 70 (Strengthening And Improvement Of Elementary And Secondary Schools); Subchapter I (Improving The Academic Achievement Of The Disadvantaged); Part F (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Agreements
In any case in which a negotiated rulemaking process is established under section 6571(b) of this title, all published proposed regulations shall conform to agreements that result from the rulemaking described in section 6571 of this title unless the Secretary reopens the negotiated rulemaking process.
(b) Records
The Secretary shall ensure that an accurate and reliable record of agreements reached during the negotiations process is maintained.
