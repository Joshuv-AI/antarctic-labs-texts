14 C.F.R. § 296.4 — Joint loading.
Citation: us:cfr:14:296.4
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-296.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 296 (Indirect Air Transportation Of Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this part shall preclude joint loading, meaning the pooling of shipments and their delivery to a direct air carrier for transportation as one shipment, under an agreement between two or more indirect air carriers or foreign indirect air carriers.
