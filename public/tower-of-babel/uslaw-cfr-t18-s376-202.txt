18 C.F.R. § 376.202 — Authority to move Commission offices.
Citation: us:cfr:18:376.202
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-376.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 376 (Organization, Mission, And Functions; Operations During Emergency Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission may provide for removal of its headquarters to any location in the United States for the duration of emergency conditions. Consistent with directives of the Chairman, the Commission officer or employee in charge of a regional office of the Commission may move such office to a new location in the United States for the duration of emergency conditions.
