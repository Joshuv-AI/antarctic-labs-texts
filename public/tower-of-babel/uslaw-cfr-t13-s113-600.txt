13 C.F.R. § 113.600 — Notice of covered programs.
Citation: us:cfr:13:113.600
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-113.600
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 113 (Nondiscrimination In Financial Assistance Programs Of Sba—Effectuation Of Policies Of Federal Government And Sba Administrator). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within 60 days of September 29, 2000, each Federal agency that awards Federal financial assistance shall publish in the Federal Register a notice of the programs covered by these Title IX regulations. Each such Federal agency shall periodically republish the notice of covered programs to reflect changes in covered programs. Copies of this notice also shall be made available upon request to the Federal agency's office that enforces Title IX.
