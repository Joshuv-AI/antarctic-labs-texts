20 U.S.C. § 2568 — Payments to Humphrey Institute and Dirksen Center; procedures applicable
Citation: us:usc:20:2568
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 45 (Career Education And Career Development); Subchapter III (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No payment may be made under sections 2566 to 2569 of this title except upon an application at such time, in such manner, and containing or accompanied by such information as the Secretary may require.
