27 C.F.R. § 5.51 — Requirement for firmly affixed labels.
Citation: us:cfr:27:5.51
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-5.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 5 (Labeling And Advertising Of Distilled Spirits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any label that is not an integral part of the container must be affixed to the container in such a way that it cannot be removed without thorough application of water or other solvents.
