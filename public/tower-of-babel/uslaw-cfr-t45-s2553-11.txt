45 C.F.R. § 2553.11 — What is the Retired and Senior Volunteer Program?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2553.11
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2553.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2553 (The Retired And Senior Volunteer Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Retired and Senior Volunteer Program (RSVP) provides grants to qualified agencies and organizations for the dual purpose of: engaging persons 55 and older in volunteer service to meet critical community needs; and to provide a high quality experience that will enrich the lives of volunteers.
