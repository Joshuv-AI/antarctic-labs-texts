7 C.F.R. § 4285.47 — Limitations.
Citation: us:cfr:7:4285.47
Creator: Rural Business-Cooperative Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-4285.47
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 4285 (Cooperative Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The amount of funds available for the cooperative agreements under this program is limited to the amount appropriated for the fiscal year.
