36 C.F.R. § 51.96 — May the Director amend or extend a concession contract for the purpose of facilitating a transaction?
Citation: us:cfr:36:51.96
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-51.96
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 51 (Concession Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director may not amend or extend a concession contract for the purpose of facilitating an assignment or encumbrance. The Director may not make commitments regarding rates to the public, contract extensions, concession contract terms and conditions, or any other matter, for the purpose of facilitating an assignment or encumbrance.
