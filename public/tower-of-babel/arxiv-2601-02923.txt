Abstract
. Characteristic earlier results were of the form CON ⁡ ( 2 ℵ 0 → [ λ ] n , 2 2 ) {\mathrm{CON}}(2^{\aleph_{0}}\to[\lambda]_{n,2}^{2}) , with 2 ℵ 0 2^{\aleph_{0}} an ex-large cardinal, in the best case the first weakly Mahlo cardinal. Characteristic new results are CON ⁡ ( ( 2 ℵ 0 = ℵ m ) + ℵ ℓ → [ ℵ k ] n , 2 2 ) {\mathrm{CON}}\big((2^{\aleph_{0}}=\aleph_{m})+\aleph_{\ell}\to[\aleph_{k}]_{n,2}^{2}\big) , for suitable k < ℓ < m k<\ell<m . So we improve in three respects: the continuum may be small (e.g. not a weakly Mahlo), we use no large cardinal, and the cardinals λ \lambda involved are < 2 ℵ 0 <2^{\aleph_{0}} after the forcing.
