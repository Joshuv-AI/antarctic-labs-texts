20 C.F.R. § 1002.19 — What activity is protected from employer retaliation by USERRA?
Citation: us:cfr:20:1002.19
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary For Veterans' Employment And Training Service, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-1002.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 1002 (Regulations Under The Uniformed Services Employment And Reemployment Rights Act Of 1994). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employer must not retaliate against an individual by taking any adverse employment action against him or her because the individual has taken an action to enforce a protection afforded any person under USERRA; testified or otherwise made a statement in or in connection with a proceeding under USERRA; assisted or participated in a USERRA investigation: or, exercised a right provided for by USERRA.
