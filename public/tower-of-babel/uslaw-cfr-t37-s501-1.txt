37 C.F.R. § 501.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:37:501.1
Creator: National Institute Of Standards And Technology, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-501.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 501 (Uniform Patent Policy For Rights In Inventions Made By Government Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to provide for the administration of a uniform patent policy for the Government with respect to the rights in inventions made by Government employees and to prescribe rules and regulations for implementing and effectuating such policy.
