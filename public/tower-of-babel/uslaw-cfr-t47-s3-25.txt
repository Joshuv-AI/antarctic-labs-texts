47 C.F.R. § 3.25 — Number of copies.
Citation: us:cfr:47:3.25
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-3.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 3 (Authorization And Administration Of Accounting Authorities In Maritime And Maritime Mobile-Satellite Radio Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

One original and one copy of FCC Form 44, “Application For Certification As An Accounting Authority” will be required. Only applications mailed to the Commission on official, Commission approved application forms will be considered. Applications should be mailed at least 90 days prior to planned commencement of settlement activities to allow time for the Commission to review the application and to allow for the informal public comment period.
