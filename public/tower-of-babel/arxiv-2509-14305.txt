Lemma 2

(Random projection to a surviving parity)

There exists a distribution

. Let

.

For

Notation.

Fix an invertible row transform

Write

Given a column permutation

Edge case.

Proof of Lemma
2

We construct a randomized,

(independent of

1.

Exactly one parity survives

(a)

(b)

Assume

Draw a uniform nonzero

Assumption (Lin-Weight).

There exist constants

(Standard via expansion of the 2-core in random 3-uniform hypergraphs / LDPC-duals; primary references are cited in the main text.)

Bias term
\Delta(A)
(definition & bound).

\Delta(A)\ :=\ \#\{\ \text{survivor-support columns that are forced into }S(\pi)\ \text{because they are coloops of }H^{\prime}_{-1}\ \},

Theorem 1

(Size-aware
\mathrm{AC}^{0}\!{+}\!\log
hardness on Balanced 3XOR)
.

Fix depth
d
and a size bound
s(N)\leq N^{k}
.
There exist absolute constants
c\in(0,1)
and
\alpha_{d}=\alpha_{d}(d)>0
such that for every P-uniform
\mathrm{AC}^{0}\!{+}\!\log
model
M

of size at most

,

\Pr[M(A,b)=\chi(\Phi)]\ \leq\ \min\Big\{1,\ \tfrac{1}{2}\;+\;s(N)\cdot\exp\!\big(-\alpha_{d}\,m^{c/d}\big)\Big\}.

Proof sketch (full proof in Appendix A).

Apply the projection lemma to reduce an AND of many parities to a single surviving parity in expectation.
Choose the switching parameter
p=m^{-c/d}
for a fixed absolute
c\in(0,1)
; after
d
rounds the bottom width is
\mathrm{poly}(1/p)=m^{O(c/d)}
,
and parity has exponentially small correlation
\exp(-\alpha_{d}m^{c/d})
(see the cited switching-lemma references).
The
O(\log N)
advice is length-only and cannot encode instance-specific masks. The factor
s(N)
comes from a union bound
(or standard counting) over substructures after restriction.

Image window (canonical 3SAT slice).

We define the image window
\mathcal{W}
as the class of 3CNFs obtained by replacing each XOR clause
by the canonical block of four 3-clauses (fixed literal order, fixed block order, no auxiliaries).
All statements below are with respect to the uniform distribution on
\mathcal{W}
.

Assume P-uniform depth-
d
models of size
s(N)\leq N^{k}
(fixed

).
Then, for all sufficiently large

,

\Pr[M=\chi]\;\leq\;\tfrac{1}{2}\;+\;s(N)\cdot\exp\!\bigl(-\alpha_{d}\,m^{\,c/d}\bigr),

with absolute constants

and
\alpha_{d}=\alpha_{d}(d)>0
.
Concretely, we instantiate

, which corresponds to the shorthand exponent
\beta_{d}=\tfrac{1}{3d}
used elsewhere.

Each XOR clause is translated into four 3-clauses without auxiliaries (canonical order).

\delta{=}0

On the balanced slice with canonical 4-clause blocking per XOR,

Proof.

Fix the canonical image window
\mathcal{W}

The translation

On the image window

\chi_{\mathrm{SAT}}(R(\Phi))=\chi_{\oplus}(\Phi)

Let

be the uniform distribution on the source slice; since all fibers have size

,
the push-forward

is exactly the uniform distribution on
\mathcal{W}
. Hence
\delta=0
in Total Variation.
∎

By
\mathrm{A2}^{d}
we obtain:

Theorem 2

(Size-aware
\mathrm{AC}^{0}\!{+}\!\log
lower bound for 3SAT in the balanced window)
.

Fix
d
and

. There are constants
\alpha_{d},\beta_{d}>0
as above such that

\Pr_{\Psi\sim R_{\#}D_{\mathrm{bal}}}[M(\Psi)=\chi_{\mathrm{SAT}}(\Psi)]\ \leq\ \min\Big\{1,\ \tfrac{1}{2}\;+\;s(N^{\prime})\cdot\exp\!\big(-\alpha_{d}\,m^{\beta_{d}}\big)\Big\},

where
m
denotes the
preimage
clause count and
N^{\prime}
is the 3SAT encoding length (with
m^{\prime}=4m
clauses and unchanged variables).
Equivalently, writing
\delta(m):=\exp(-\alpha_{d}m^{\beta_{d}})
, the bound is
1/2+s(N^{\prime})\,\delta(m)
(capped at
1
).

Corollary 1

(Reading as a
K_{C}
statement)
.

Let
\delta(m):=\exp(-\alpha_{d}m^{\beta_{d}})
and assume

.
For any
\varepsilon<\tfrac{1}{2}-s(N^{\prime})\,\delta(m)
there is
no

M\in\mathrm{AC}^{0}\!{+}\!\log{}
of size
\leq s(N^{\prime})
with error
\leq\varepsilon
on
R_{\#}D_{\mathrm{bal}}
.
Equivalently: within the
size-capped
class,
K^{3\mathrm{SAT}}_{\mathrm{AC}^{0}\!{+}\!\log{}}(N^{\prime},\varepsilon;R_{\#}D_{\mathrm{bal}})=\infty
.
If, alternatively, one parametrizes by size
s

\mathrm{AC}^{0}\!{+}\!\log{}_{d}[s]

M

K\geq\Omega(\log s(N^{\prime}))
.

Our lower bound targets
\mathrm{AC}^{0}\!{+}\!\log
(depth-

P-uniform circuits with
O(\log N)
advice) on the balanced window.
The co-rank statement relies on standard sparse-hypergraph 2-core phenomena; we use an exponent
c/d
with a fixed absolute
c\in(0,1)

Appendix A collects the parameterized switching setup (including the correlation exponent; we use the Håstad ’87 SICOMP formulation as presented in

Note on constants. Our choice of

Recent work on rank/corank phenomena in sparse binary structures informs our 2-core and co-rank scaling assumptions, including

Our contribution combines a TV-stable reduction lemma (

Our

We presented a size-aware lower bound against P-uniform

Appendix A
Switching parameters, independence, and the correlation exponent

Independence of
\rho
and
\rho^{\prime}
.

The projection
\rho
from the projection lemma and the
p
-random restriction
\rho^{\prime}
used for the switching step are drawn
independently
.
All expectations and tail bounds are conditioned on the (constant-probability) event that exactly one parity survives under
\rho
with
\Omega(m)
free support.
The randomness of
\rho^{\prime}
is fresh and independent of both
\rho
and the draws of
A
and
u
.
The internal choices
\alpha
(survivor) and
\pi
(or
S(\pi)
) are independent of
\rho^{\prime}
.

Lemma 4

(Permutation concentration for free support)
.

Assume the Lin-Weight condition with parameters
\gamma_{0},p_{0}>0
. Let
\pi
be a uniformly random permutation of columns, and let
S(\pi)
be the greedy pivot set for
H^{\prime}_{-1}
exposed in the order
\pi
, with
F(\pi):=[m]\setminus S(\pi)
. Then there is a constant
c_{2}=c_{2}(\gamma_{0},p_{0},t^{\prime})>0
such that

\Pr_{\alpha,A,\pi}\!\left[\,\bigl|\mathrm{supp}(H_{\star})\cap F(\pi)\bigr|\;\geq\;\tfrac{1}{2}\!\Bigl(1-\tfrac{t^{\prime}-1}{m}\Bigr)\gamma_{0}m\,\right]\ \geq\ c_{2}.

Proof idea.
Expose
\pi
via a Doob martingale on
S_{m}
and apply a permutations-based concentration inequality (e.g., exchangeable pairs on
S_{m}
in the sense of Chatterjee, or Efron–Stein on symmetric groups) with bounded increments for the greedy pivot process. The precise value of
c_{2}
is immaterial for our purposes and depends only on
(\gamma_{0},p_{0},t^{\prime})
.

Parameter path and exponent (size-aware; referenced).

Fix depth
d
and choose

p\;=\;m^{-c/d}\qquad\text{for a fixed absolute }c\in(0,1).

After
d
switching rounds we obtain:

•

the number of
live variables
is at most

m\cdot p^{d}\;=\;m^{\,1-c},

•

and the resulting DNF/CNF has
bottom width

W\;=\;\mathrm{poly}(1/p)\;=\;m^{\,O(c/d)}

(for the adopted switching-lemma variant; we follow the cited parametrization verbatim, e.g.
Håstad

[
7
]
,
O’Donnell–Wimmer
notes
[
14
]
,
Katz–Immerman

[
8
]
).

For the surviving parity, which (by the projection lemma) depends on
\Omega(m)
free bits with constant probability, standard parity-vs-width correlation bounds imply

\bigl|\mathrm{corr}(\text{DNF/CNF of width }\leq W,\ \mathrm{Parity})\bigr|\;\leq\;\exp\!\bigl(-\,\alpha_{d}\,m^{\,c/d}\bigr),

where
\alpha_{d}=\alpha_{d}(d)>0
depends only on the depth
d
and the chosen switching-lemma reference.
A union bound over at most
s(N)\leq N^{k}
relevant substructures yields,
for all sufficiently large
m
and
N=\Theta(m\log n)
,

\Pr[M=\chi]\;\leq\;\tfrac{1}{2}\;+\;s(N)\cdot\exp\!\bigl(-\,\alpha_{d}\,m^{\,c/d}\bigr).

Equivalently, set
\beta_{d}:=c/d
(instantiated as
\beta_{d}=1/(3d)
).

Quantitative constants (calibration & convention).

We fix the exponent parameter at
c=\tfrac{1}{3}
, hence
\beta_{d}=\tfrac{1}{3d}
throughout.
For the correlation constant we carry
\alpha_{d}=\alpha_{d}(d)
as a named depth-dependent parameter admitted by the chosen switching-lemma instantiation.
To make all displays checkable, we record a conservative convention in the ancillary artifacts:

\alpha_{d}^{\mathrm{conf}}\ :=\ \frac{\alpha_{0}}{(d+1)^{4}}\qquad\text{with a universal }\alpha_{0}\in(0,1].

All stated inequalities remain valid for any
\alpha_{d}^{\prime}\leq\alpha_{d}^{\mathrm{conf}}
.

Reviewer note.
The
\alpha_{d}
values used here are conservative by design; any improvement only tightens the exponential rate and leaves all size-aware statements intact.
(If a different switching-lemma reference is adopted,
\alpha_{d}^{\mathrm{conf}}
can be tightened accordingly without affecting the statements.)

Notes.

(i) We bound
bottom width
and infer decision-tree depth via switching (up to constants).
(ii) The constants
\alpha_{d}
and the exponent

are taken from the fixed switching-lemma reference named in the main text.
(iii) The
balanced window
refers to the random 3XOR slice with

and affine-uniform RHS-conditioning.
(iv) Empirical per-depth calibration is pending; until then we report the conservative convention

Appendix B
2-core facts for random 3XOR (balanced window)

Fix
\gamma\in(0,1]
and
m=(1+\gamma)n
. For a random 3XOR incidence matrix
A\in\{0,1\}^{m\times n}
,
the 2-core of the associated 3-uniform hypergraph has linear size w.h.p., and the left-kernel
H(A)

has co-rank
t^{\prime}=\Theta(n)
(standard XORSAT window). In particular, for a uniform nonzero

\alpha\in\{0,1\}^{t^{\prime}}
, the survivor
H_{\star}=\alpha^{\top}H(A)
has linear support with constant probability
(
Lin-Weight
assumption), and the number of survivor-support columns that are coloops of
H^{\prime}_{-1}
is
o(m)
w.h.p.
These facts justify that the bias term in our projection analysis is sublinear.

Pittel–Sorkin (k-XORSAT threshold).)
