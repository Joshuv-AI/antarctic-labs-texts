42 C.F.R. § 124.1 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:42:124.1
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-124.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 124 (Medical Facility Construction And Modernization). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations of this subpart are applicable to grants under section 1625 of the Public Health Service Act for construction and modernization projects designed to:
(a) Eliminate or prevent imminent safety hazards as defined by Federal, State or local fire, building, or life safety codes or regulations, or
(b) Avoid noncompliance with State or voluntary licensure or accreditation standards.
