11 U.S.C. § 721 — Authorization to operate business
Citation: us:usc:11:721
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section721
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 7 (Liquidation); Subchapter II (Collection, Liquidation, And Distribution Of The Estate). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The court may authorize the trustee to operate the business of the debtor for a limited period, if such operation is in the best interest of the estate and consistent with the orderly liquidation of the estate.
