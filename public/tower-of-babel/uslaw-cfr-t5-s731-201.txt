5 C.F.R. § 731.201 — Standard.
Citation: us:cfr:5:731.201
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-731.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 731 (Suitability And Fitness). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The standard for a suitability and fitness determination and for a suitability action defined in § 731.203 is that the action will protect the integrity or promote the efficiency of the service.
