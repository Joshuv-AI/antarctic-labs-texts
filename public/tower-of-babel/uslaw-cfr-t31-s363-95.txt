31 C.F.R. § 363.95 — How may I give, and who can receive, a book-entry savings bond as a gift?
Citation: us:cfr:31:363.95
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-363.95
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 363 (Regulations Governing Securities Held In Treasurydirect). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may give a book-entry savings bond as a gift in two ways:
(a) An individual may purchase a book-entry savings bond online as a gift and give it to an individual; or
(b) A person who owns a bond may transfer that bond to another person as a gift with immediate delivery.
