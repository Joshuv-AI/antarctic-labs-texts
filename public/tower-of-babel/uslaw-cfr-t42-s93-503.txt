42 C.F.R. § 93.503 — Filing of the administrative record.
Citation: us:cfr:42:93.503
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-93.503
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 93 (Public Health Service Policies On Research Misconduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) For appeals that are not dismissed under § 93.502(a), ORI will file the administrative record for the appeal.
(b) The ALJ's review will be based on the administrative record.
(c) The parties have no right to supplement the administrative record.
