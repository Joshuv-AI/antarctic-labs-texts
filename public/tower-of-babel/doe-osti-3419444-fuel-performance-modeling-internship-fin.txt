 Project Summary

 • The experiments of interest in this
 study included metallic U-Zr fuel
 rodlets within HT-9 cladding which
 were placed in SS-316 capsules and
 inserted in the Advanced Test Reactor
 (ATR)

 • BISON was used to simulate gap heat
 transfer, mechanical contact, and the
 evolution of the gap/plenum pressure
 with plenum volume, gas temperature,
 and fission gas addition.

1

 BISON Fuel Performance Modeling

 BISON is a Finite Element Method (FEM) nuclear
 fuel performance code. It is a multiphysics fuel
 analysis tool that solves fully-coupled
 thermomechanical problems.

 Metallic Fuel-Specific Models:
 − Basic mechanics for the fuel and cladding
 • Elasticity
 • Plasticity
 • Creep
 • Thermal Conductivity
 − Fission gas release and swelling
 − Material models that are functions of irradiation
 • Creep
 • Thermal Conductivity
 − Gap heat transfer in sodium bond
 − Fuel-cladding chemical interaction (FCCI)
 − Zr redistribution

 AFC 1 and AFC 2 Experiments

 Current Modeling Progress
 • Mesh files produced in Cubit exist for all experiment capsules of interest in
       AFC 1B, 1D, 1F and AFC 2A, 2B, 2E.
 • Input scripts exist for all AFC 2A, 2B, 2E experiment capsules.

 Further Model Specifications
 • The BISON Kinematic contact module and the Coulomb formulation were used
 for thermal and physical contact.

 Future Work
 • Further work is needed on the contact modeling. Possible contact tolerance
 and penalty combinations have been explored and stored with their respective
 output data.
