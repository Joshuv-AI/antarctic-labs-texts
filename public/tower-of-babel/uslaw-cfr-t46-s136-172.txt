46 C.F.R. § 136.172 — Temporary compliance for existing towing vessels.
Citation: us:cfr:46:136.172
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-136.172
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 136 (Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An existing towing vessel subject to this subchapter will remain subject to Coast Guard regulations applicable to the vessel on July 19, 2016 until either July 20, 2018 or the date the vessel obtains a COI, whichever date is earlier.
