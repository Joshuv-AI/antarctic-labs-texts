19 C.F.R. § 145.53 — Firearms and munitions of war.
Citation: us:cfr:19:145.53
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-145.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 145 (Mail Importations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Importations of firearms, munitions of war, and related articles are subject to the import permit requirements and other restrictions set forth in 27 CFR parts 47, 178, 179.
