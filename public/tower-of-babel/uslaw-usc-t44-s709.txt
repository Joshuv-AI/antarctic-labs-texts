44 U.S.C. § 709 — Public and private laws, postal conventions, and treaties
Citation: us:usc:44:709
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section709
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 7 (Congressional Printing And Binding). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of the Government Publishing Office shall print in slip form copies of public and private laws, postal conventions, and treaties, to be charged to the congressional allotment for printing and binding. The Joint Committee on Printing shall control the number and distribution of copies.
