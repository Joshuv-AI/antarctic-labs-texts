42 C.F.R. § 476.76 — Cooperation with health care facilities.
Citation: us:cfr:42:476.76
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-476.76
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 476 (Quality Improvement Organization Review). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before implementation of review, a QIO must make a good faith effort to discuss the QIO's administrative and review procedures with each involved health care facility.
