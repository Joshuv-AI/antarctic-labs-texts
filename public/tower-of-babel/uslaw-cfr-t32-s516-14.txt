32 C.F.R. § 516.14 — Service of process on DA or Secretary of Army.
Citation: us:cfr:32:516.14
Creator: Department Of The Army
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-516.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 516 (Litigation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Chief, Litigation Division, shall accept service of process for Department of the Army or for the Secretary of the Army in his official capacity.
