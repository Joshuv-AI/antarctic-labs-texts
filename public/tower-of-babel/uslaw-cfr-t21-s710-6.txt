21 C.F.R. § 710.6 — Notification of registrant; cosmetic product establishment registration number.
Citation: us:cfr:21:710.6
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-710.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 710 (Voluntary Registration Of Cosmetic Product Establishments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commissioner of Food and Drugs will provide the registrant with a validated copy of Form FD-2511 as evidence of registration. This validated copy will be sent only to the location shown for the registering establishment. A permanent registration number will be assigned to each cosmetic product establishment registered in accordance with the regulations in this part.
