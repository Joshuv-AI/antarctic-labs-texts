36 C.F.R. § 1254.38 — How do I keep documents in order?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1254.38
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1254.38
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1254 (Using Records And Donated Historical Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You must keep unbound documents in the order in which we deliver them to you.
(b) You must not attempt to rearrange documents that appear to be in disorder. Instead, you must refer any suspected problems with the records to the research room attendant.
(c) You may use only one folder at a time.
(d) Remove documents from only one container at a time.
