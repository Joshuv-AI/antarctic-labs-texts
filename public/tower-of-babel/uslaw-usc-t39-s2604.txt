39 U.S.C. § 2604 — Delivery of stolen money to owner
Citation: us:usc:39:2604
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title39-section2604
Context: United States Code, Title 39 (Postal Service); Part III (Modernization And Fiscal Administration); Chapter 26 (Debts And Collection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When the Postal Service is satisfied that money or property in the possession of the Postal Service represents money or property stolen from the mails, or the proceeds thereof, it may deliver it to the person it finds to be the rightful owner.
