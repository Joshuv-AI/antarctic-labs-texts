10 U.S.C. § 240a — Audit of Department of Defense financial statements
Citation: us:usc:10:240a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section240a
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And General Military Powers); Chapter 9A (Audit). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Defense shall ensure that a full audit is performed on the financial statements of the Department of Defense for each fiscal year as required by section 3521(e) of title 31.
