7 C.F.R. § 56.41 — Check grading officially identified product.
Citation: us:cfr:7:56.41
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-56.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 56 (Voluntary Grading Of Shell Eggs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Officially identified shell eggs packed or received in an official plant may be subject to final check grading prior to their shipment. Such product found not to be in compliance with the assigned official grade shall be placed under a retention tag until it is regraded to comply with the grade assigned or until the official identification is removed.
