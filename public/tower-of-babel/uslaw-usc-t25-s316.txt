25 U.S.C. § 316 — Rights of several roads through canyons
Citation: us:usc:25:316
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section316
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 8 (Rights-Of-Way Through Indian Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of section 935 1 of title 43 relating to the rights of several railroads through any canyon, pass, or defile are extended and made applicable to rights of way granted under sections 312 to 318 of this title and to railroad companies obtaining such rights of way.
