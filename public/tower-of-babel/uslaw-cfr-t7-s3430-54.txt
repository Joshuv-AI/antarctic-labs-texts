7 C.F.R. § 3430.54 — Indirect costs.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3430.54
Creator: National Institute Of Food And Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3430.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3430 (Competitive And Noncompetitive Non-Formula Federal Assistance Programs—General Award Administrative Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Indirect cost rates for grants and cooperative agreements shall be determined in accordance with 2 CFR part 200, unless superseded by another authority. Any restriction on indirect costs is to be identified in the request for applications as appropriate. Use of indirect costs as in-kind matching contributions is subject to § 3430.52(b).
