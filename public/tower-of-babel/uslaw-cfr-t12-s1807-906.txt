12 C.F.R. § 1807.906 — CDFI Fund deemed not to control.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1807.906
Creator: Community Development Financial Institutions Fund, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1807.906
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1807 (Capital Magnet Fund). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The CDFI Fund shall not be deemed to control a Recipient by reason of any CMF Award provided under the Act for the purpose of any applicable law.
