16 U.S.C. § 410hhhh–5 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:16:410hhhh–5
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section410hhhh–5
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LIX–FFF (Jimmy Carter National Historical Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purposes of this subchapter—
(1)
the term “preservation district” means the Jimmy Carter National Preservation District established under section 410hhhh–1 of this title;
(2)
the term “historical park” means the Jimmy Carter National Historical Park established under section 410hhhh of this title; and
(3)
the term “Secretary” means the Secretary of the Interior.
