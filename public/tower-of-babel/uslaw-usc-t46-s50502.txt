46 U.S.C. § 50502 — Applicability to receivers, trustees, successors, and assigns
Citation: us:usc:46:50502
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section50502
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part A (General); Chapter 505 (Other General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This subtitle applies to receivers, trustees, successors, and assigns of any person to whom this subtitle applies.
