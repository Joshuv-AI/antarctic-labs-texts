Hermitian K K -theory and Milnor-Witt motivic cohomology over ℤ \mathbb{Z}
Håkon Kolderup, Oliver Röndigs, Paul Arne Østvær

Abstract
The theme of this paper is to compute hermitian K K -groups in terms of the recently developed theory of Milnor-Witt motivic cohomology. Our approach makes use of the very effective slice spectral sequence within the motivic stable homotopy category, which we analyze in detail for base schemes of arithmetic interest. We show a Grothendieck-Riemann-Roch theorem, determine the map between Milnor-Witt and hermitian K K -theory up to degree five for all fields, and compute the hermitian K K -groups and the higher Witt-groups of the ring of integers.

The very effective slice spectral sequence

Let
S
be a qcqs base scheme of finite Krull dimension. The very effective slice filtration on
\mathbf{SH}(S)
filters with respect to the projective line
\mathbb{P}^{1}
, or equivalently the Tate object
T=\mathbb{A}^{1}/(\mathbb{A}^{1}\setminus{0})
, rather than the multiplicative group
\mathbb{G}_{m}
as in Voevodsky’s effective slice filtration
[
83
]
.
Following Spitzweck–Østvær
[
80
]
, let
\mathbf{SH}^{\text{veff}}(S)
denote the smallest full subcategory of
\mathbf{SH}(S)
containing all suspension spectra of smooth finite type
S
-schemes and closed under extensions and homotopy colimits. Unlike Voevodsky’s effective subcategory
\mathbf{SH}(S)^{\text{eff}}
,
\mathbf{SH}^{\text{veff}}(S)
is not triangulated. For
q\in\mathbb{Z}
, define

\mathbf{SH}^{\text{veff}}(S,q)=\Sigma^{2q,q}\mathbf{SH}^{\text{veff}}(S)
.
The full embedding of very effective motivic spectra participates in an adjunction

\tilde{\mathsf{i}}_{q}:\mathbf{SH}^{\text{veff}}(S,q)\rightleftarrows\mathbf{SH}(S):\tilde{\mathsf{r}}_{q}

(2)

Setting
\tilde{{\mathsf{f}}}_{q}=\tilde{\mathsf{i}}_{q}\circ\tilde{\mathsf{r}}_{q}
produces natural cofiber sequences

\displaystyle\tilde{{\mathsf{f}}}_{q+1}\mathsf{E}\to\tilde{{\mathsf{f}}}_{q}\mathsf{E}\to\tilde{\mathsf{s}}_{q}\mathsf{E}

(3)

The term
\tilde{{\mathsf{f}}}_{q}\mathsf{E}
is the
q
-th
very effective cover of
\mathsf{E}

and
\tilde{\mathsf{s}}_{q}\mathsf{E}
is the
q
-th
very effective slice of
\mathsf{E}
.
Applying homotopy to (
3
),
and letting
q

yields an exact couple and the associated very effective spectral sequence

\tilde{E}^{1}_{t,q,w}=\pi_{t,w}\tilde{\mathsf{s}}_{q}\mathsf{E}\Longrightarrow\pi_{t,w}\mathsf{E}

(4)

The corresponding filtration on the target graded group
\pi_{t,w}\mathsf{E}
is given by

\tilde{{\mathsf{f}}}_{q}\pi_{t,w}\mathsf{E}:=\operatorname{im}(\pi_{t,w}\tilde{{\mathsf{f}}}_{q}\mathsf{E}\to\pi_{t,w}\mathsf{E})

(5)

We note that the
r
-th differential takes the form
\tilde{E}^{r}_{p,q,w}\to\tilde{E}^{r}_{p-1,q+r,w}
.

Convergence is one of the merits of the very effective slice filtration

\displaystyle\cdots\to\tilde{{\mathsf{f}}}_{q+1}\mathsf{E}\to\tilde{{\mathsf{f}}}_{q}\mathsf{E}\to\tilde{{\mathsf{f}}}_{q-1}\mathsf{E}\to\cdots

(6)

By the construction in

[

,
the connectivity of
\tilde{{\mathsf{f}}}_{q}\mathsf{E}
increases with

so that the homotopy limit of

) is contractible,

\operatorname{holim}_{q\to\infty}\tilde{{\mathsf{f}}}_{q}\mathsf{E}\simeq\ast

(7)

The above informs us that (
4
) is a conditionally convergent spectral sequence in the sense of

[
19

.
It follows that the very effective slice filtration on motivic homotopy groups
\pi_{\ast,\ast}\mathsf{E}
is complete;
see
[
19

.

Proposition 3.1
.

The very effective slice spectral sequence (
4
) is strongly convergent
over qcqs base schemes of finite Krull dimension.

Proof.

It remains to show that (
6
) is exhaustive and Hausdorff;
see
[
72
, Lemma 3.1]

First we show that

\displaystyle\bigcup_{q\in\mathbb{Z}}\tilde{{\mathsf{f}}}_{q}\pi_{t,w}\mathsf{E}

\displaystyle=\pi_{t,w}\mathsf{E}

(8)

The motivic sphere
S^{t,w}\in\mathbf{SH}^{\text{veff}}(S,q)
whenever
q\leq\min\{t-w,w\}
.
By abuse of notation, we do not distinguish
S^{t,w}
as an object of
\mathbf{SH}^{\text{veff}}(S,q)
or
\mathbf{SH}(S)

Then,
in the said range and with reference to the adjunction (

we have

[S^{t,w},\mathsf{E}]\cong[S^{t,w},\tilde{\mathsf{r}}_{q}\mathsf{E}]\cong[S^{t,w},\tilde{{\mathsf{f}}}_{q}\mathsf{E}]

Hence any element of
\pi_{t,w}\mathsf{E}
is in the image of
\pi_{t,w}\tilde{{\mathsf{f}}}_{q}\mathsf{E}
when
q\leq\min\{t-w,w\}
.

To verify the Hausdorff condition
[
19
, Definition 5.2(ii)]
for (
5
),
we consider an element

\displaystyle x\in\bigcap_{q\in\mathbb{Z}}\tilde{{\mathsf{f}}}_{q}\pi_{t,w}\mathsf{E}

We want to show that

By assumption

factors through

Recall that for

Theorem 10.13
.

The coefficients of
\mathsf{KW}_{\mathbb{Z}}
are given by

\pi_{s+(w)}\mathsf{KW}_{\mathbb{Z}}\cong\begin{cases}\mathbb{Z}&s\equiv 0\mod{4}\\

\pi_{\ast+(\star)}\mathsf{KW}_{\mathbb{Z}}=\mathbb{Z}[\alpha^{\pm 1},\eta^{\pm 1},\xi]/(2\xi,\xi^{2})

Here
\alpha\in\pi_{4+(4)}\mathsf{KW}_{\mathbb{Z}}
is a generator, and
\xi\in\pi_{1+(0)}\mathsf{KW}_{\mathbb{Z}}
is the nonzero element.

Proof.

Theorem
4.9
shows that the second page of the slice spectral sequence for
\mathsf{KQ}_{\mathbb{Z}}
has zero columns when
s\equiv 2,3\mod{4}
.
Furthermore, the single nonzero entry in a column with

is

\mathbb{Z}/2\cong h^{1,2}(\mathbb{Z})/\tau h^{1,1}(\mathbb{Z})

The columns for

Passage along the ring inclusion
\mathbb{Z}\to\mathbb{Q}
and naturality of the slice filtration imply that all potentially nonzero slice differentials originating in a column for
s\equiv 1\mod{4}
vanish.
Hence the slice spectral sequence for
\mathsf{KW}_{\mathbb{Z}}
collapses at the second page.

Comparison along the ring inclusion

and the computationally determined relations

.
∎
