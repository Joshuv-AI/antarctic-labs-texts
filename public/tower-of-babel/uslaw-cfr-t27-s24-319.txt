27 C.F.R. § 24.319 — Carbon dioxide record.
Citation: us:cfr:27:24.319
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-24.319
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 24 (Wine). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A proprietor who uses carbon dioxide in still wine or still hard cider shall maintain a record of the laboratory tests conducted to establish compliance with the limitations prescribed in § 24.245.
