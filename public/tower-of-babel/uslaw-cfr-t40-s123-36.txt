40 C.F.R. § 123.36 — Establishment of technical standards for concentrated animal feeding operations.
Citation: us:cfr:40:123.36
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-123.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 123 (State Program Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the State has not already established technical standards for nutrient management that are consistent with 40 CFR 412.4(c)(2), the Director shall establish such standards by the date specified in § 123.62(e).
