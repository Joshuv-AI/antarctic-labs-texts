10 C.F.R. § 20.1207 — Occupational dose limits for minors.
Citation: us:cfr:10:20.1207
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-20.1207
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 20 (Standards For Protection Against Radiation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The annual occupational dose limits for minors are 10 percent of the annual dose limits specified for adult workers in § 20.1201.
