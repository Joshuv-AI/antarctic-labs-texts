30 C.F.R. § 824.2 — Objectives.
Citation: us:cfr:30:824.2
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-824.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 824 (Special Permanent Program Performance Standards—Mountaintop Removal). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The objectives of this part are to—
(a) Enhance coal recovery;
(b) Reclaim the land to equal or higher postmining use; and
(c) Protect and enhance environmental and other values protected under the Act and this chapter.
