40 C.F.R. § 57.106 — Expiration date.
Citation: us:cfr:40:57.106
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-57.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 57 (Primary Nonferrous Smelter Orders). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each NSO shall state its expiration date. No NSO issued under this regulation shall expire later than January 1, 1988.
