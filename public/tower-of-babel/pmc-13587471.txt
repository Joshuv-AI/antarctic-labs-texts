Correction: Multi‑omics analysis identifies different molecular subtypes with unique outcomes in early-stage poorly differentiated lung adenocarcinoma
Authors: Bing Liu, Wei Tao, Xuantong Zhou, Li‑Di Xu, Yanrui Luo, Xin Yang, Qingjie Min, Miao Huang, Yuge Zhu, Xinrun Cui, Yaqi Wang, Tongyang Gong, Enli Zhang, Yu S Huang, Weizhi Chen, Shi Yan, Nan Wu
Published: 2026

Correction: Mol Cancer 24, 129 (2025)

https://doi.org/10.1186/s12943-025-02333-7

The authors recognize that functional evidence derived from a single cell line, SPC-A1, may be insufficient to firmly establish the observed effects. To further substantiate the key functional observations, the authors have performed additional independent validation experiments in two well-established lung adenocarcinoma cell lines, A549 and PC-9. These new data fully corroborate the principal findings for GINS1 and CPT1C. Importantly, the study’s main conclusions, derived primarily from patient-based multi-omics analyses and validated in external cohorts (TCGA and GSE31210), remain robust and unaffected. The supplementary validation results are provided in Figs. S1 and S2. The article has been updated to include these new data [1].

Figure S1. Effects of GINS1 in A549 and PC-9 cells. (A, B) GINS1 knockdown efficiency was assessed by qRT-PCR in A549 (A) and PC-9 (B) cells (n = 3). ***P < 0.001. (C, D) GINS1 knockdown efficiency was assessed by western blotting in A549 (C) and PC-9 (D) cells. (E, F) Transwell assays were used to assess the effects of GINS1 depletion on migration and invasion in A549 (E) and PC-9 (F) cells (n = 3). Representative images (left) and quantitative analyses (right) are shown. Scale bar, 200 μm. ***P < 0.001. (G, H) Colony formation assay was performed to evaluate the effect of GINS1 in A549 (G) and PC-9 (H) cells (n = 3). ***P < 0.001. (I) CCK-8 assay was performed to evaluate the effect of GINS1 depletion on cell viability of A549 and PC-9 cells (n = 5). ***P < 0.001. (J) Images of xenograft tumors derived from control A549 cells and A549 cells with GINS1 depletion (n = 5). ***P < 0.001.
