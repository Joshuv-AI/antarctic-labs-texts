Cosmic reverberations on a constrained f ⁡ ( Q , T ) f(Q,T) -model of the Universe
Akanksha Singh, Shaily, J. K. Singh, Ertan Güdekli

Abstract
In this paper, we construct an isotropic cosmological model in the f ⁡ ( Q , T ) f(Q,T) theory of gravity in the frame of a flat FLRW spacetime being Q Q the non-metricity tensor and T T the trace of the energy-momentum tensor. The gravity function is taken to be a quadratic equation, f ⁡ ( Q , T ) = ζ ​ Q 2 + γ ​ T f(Q,T)=\zeta Q^{2}+\gamma T , where ζ < 0 \zeta<0 and γ \gamma are the arbitrary constants. We constrain the model parameters α \alpha and H 0 H_{0} using the recent observational datasets: the Hubble dataset (OHD), the P ​ a ​ n ​ t ​ h ​ e ​ o ​ n Pantheon dataset of 1048 1048 points, and the joint dataset (OHD + P ​ a ​ n ​ t ​ h ​ e ​ o ​ n Pantheon ). The universe model transitions from an early deceleration state to an acceleration in late times. This model also provides the ekpyrotic phase of the universe on the redshift z > 12.32 z>12.32 . In this model, the Big Bang is described as a collision of branes, and thus, the Big Bang is not the beginning of time. Before the Big Bang, there is an ekpyrotic phase with the equation of state ω >> 1 \omega>>1 . In late times, the undeviating Hubble measurements reduce the H 0 H_{0} tension in the reconstructed f ⁡ ( Q , T ) f(Q,T) function. Additionally, we study various physical parameters of the model. Finally, our model describes a quintessence dark energy model at later times.
