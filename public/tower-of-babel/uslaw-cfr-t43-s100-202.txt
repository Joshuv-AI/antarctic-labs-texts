43 C.F.R. § 100.202 — Will the Secretary verify the appraiser's qualifications?
Citation: us:cfr:43:100.202
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-100.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 100 (Waiving Departmental Review Of Appraisals And Valuations Of Indian Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary will verify the appraiser's qualifications to determine whether the appraiser meets the requirements of § 100.200.
