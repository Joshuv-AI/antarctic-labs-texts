7 C.F.R. § 1217.16 — Net size or count.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1217.16
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Miscellaneous Commodities), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1217.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1217 (Softwood Lumber Research, Promotion, Consumer Education And Industry Information Order). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Net size or count means the volume of softwood lumber based on its actual dimensions. It is used in certain jurisdictions as the quantity by which the softwood lumber is sold. Net size or count differs from the nominal size or count as it is based on the thickness and width of a board after it has been dried and planed.
