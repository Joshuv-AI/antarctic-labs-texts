7 C.F.R. § 3801.4 — Denials.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3801.4
Creator: World Agricultural Outlook Board, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3801.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3801 (Availability Of Information To The Public). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Economics Agencies FOIA Officer determines that a requested record is exempt from mandatory disclosure and that discretionary release would be improper, the Economics Agencies FOIA Officer shall give written notice of denial in accordance with § 1.8(a) of this title.
