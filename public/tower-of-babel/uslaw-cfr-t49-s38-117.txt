49 C.F.R. § 38.117 — Floors, steps and thresholds.
Citation: us:cfr:49:38.117
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-38.117
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 38 (Americans With Disabilities Act (Ada) Accessibility Specifications For Transportation Vehicles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Floor surfaces on aisles, step treads and areas where wheelchair and mobility aid users are to be accommodated shall be slip-resistant.
(b) All step edges and thresholds shall have a band of color(s) running the full width of the step or threshold which contrasts from the step tread and riser or adjacent floor, either light-on-dark or dark-on-light.
