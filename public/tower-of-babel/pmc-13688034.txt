Advances in Radiation Oncology Top Downloaded Articles of 2025
Authors: Rachel B. Jimenez
Published: 2026

It is my pleasure to highlight the most frequently downloaded articles from Advances in Radiation Oncology in 2025. A review of these publications celebrates the trajectory of our specialty and underscores the kinds of innovations that we, as a field, are most interested in embracing.

Rising to the top of the list this year are biological advances, including the immunologic effects of radiation therapy, the use of radioisotopes, Boswellia serrata as a radioprotectant, and even the thoughtful reconsideration of α/β ratios for select cancer types. In addition to a focus on tumor and normal tissue biology, several articles also focus on the use of tissue sparing, via hydrogel spacers and biologically informed target volumes, to reduce the potential side effects of radiation therapy. Throughout the list, there is a sustained interest in the use of ablative and ultrahypofractionated regimens for a variety of cancers and conditions, cementing these approaches as central to the future of our specialty.

Seeing several 2025 publications rise so quickly, despite a short interval since publication, emphasizes both the growing momentum of our journal and the increased engagement of our readership. I look forward to seeing how the outstanding scholarship reflected in our top 20 list continues to inform our field and secure the attention of our Advances readership in the years to come!
