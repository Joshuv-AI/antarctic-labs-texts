54 U.S.C. § 311101 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:54:311101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section311101
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3111 (Preserve America Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter:
(1) Council.—
The term “Council” means the Advisory Council on Historic Preservation.
(2) Heritage tourism.—
The term “heritage tourism” means the conduct of activities to attract and accommodate visitors to a site or area based on the unique or special aspects of the history, landscape (including trail systems), and culture of the site or area.
(3) Program.—
The term “program” means the Preserve America Program established under section 311102(a).
