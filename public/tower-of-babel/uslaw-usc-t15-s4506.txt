15 U.S.C. § 4506 — Termination
Citation: us:usc:15:4506
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section4506
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 71 (Petroleum Overcharge Distribution And Restitution). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
(1)
Except as provided in subsection (b), the provisions of this chapter (other than section 4504 of this title) shall terminate 90 days after the Secretary—
(A)
determines that all of the restitutionary amounts to which section 4501(a) of this title applies have been collected and disbursed as provided in this chapter; and
(B)
submits to Congress the final report required by section 4505 of this title.
(2)
Such final report shall include the determination (and the justification thereof) described in paragraph (1)(A). Such report shall also be published in the Federal Register.
(b) Exception
The requirements of section 4502(d) 1 of this title shall continue to be applicable to the use of restitutionary amounts received under this chapter as long as such funds remain available.
