14 C.F.R. § 437.65 — Collision avoidance analysis.
Citation: us:cfr:14:437.65
Creator: Commercial Space Transportation, Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-437.65
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 437 (Experimental Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For a permitted flight with a planned maximum altitude greater than 150 kilometers, a permittee must obtain a collision avoidance analysis in accordance with § 450.169 of this chapter.
