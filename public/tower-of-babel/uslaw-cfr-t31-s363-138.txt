31 C.F.R. § 363.138 — Is Treasury liable for the purchase of a zero-percent certificate of indebtedness that is made in error?
Citation: us:cfr:31:363.138
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-363.138
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 363 (Regulations Governing Securities Held In Treasurydirect). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We are not liable for any deposits of funds for the purchase of a zero-percent certificate of indebtedness that are made in error by your financial institution or employer.
