12 U.S.C. § 1706d — Applicability
Citation: us:usc:12:1706d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section1706d
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 13 (National Housing); Subchapter I (Housing Renovation And Modernization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of sections 1703 and 1706c of this title shall be applicable in the several States and Puerto Rico, the District of Columbia, Guam, the Trust Territory of the Pacific Islands, American Samoa, and the Virgin Islands.
