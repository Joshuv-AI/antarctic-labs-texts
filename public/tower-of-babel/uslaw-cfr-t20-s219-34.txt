20 C.F.R. § 219.34 — When evidence that a marriage has ended is required.
Citation: us:cfr:20:219.34
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-219.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 219 (Evidence Required For Payment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Evidence of how a previous marriage ended may be required to determine whether a later marriage is valid. If a widow or widower remarried after the employee's death and that marriage was annulled, evidence of the annulment is required. If the claimant is a divorced spouse or surviving divorced spouse, evidence to prove a final or absolute divorce from the employee may be required.
