40 C.F.R. § 13.7 — Omission not a defense.
Citation: us:cfr:40:13.7
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-13.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 13 (Claims Collection Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Failure by the Administrator to comply with any provision of this regulation is not available to a debtor as a defense against payment of a debt.
