42 C.F.R. § 512.350 — Schedule of home dialysis payment adjustments.
Citation: us:cfr:42:512.350
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-512.350
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 512 (Standard Provisions For Mandatory Innovation Center Models And Specific Provisions For Certain Models). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

CMS adjusts the payments specified in § 512.340 by the Facility HDPA and adjusts the payments specified in § 512.345 by the Clinician HDPA, according to the following schedule:
(a) Calendar year 2021: +3 percent.
(b) Calendar year 2022: +2 percent.
(c) Calendar year 2023: +1 percent.
