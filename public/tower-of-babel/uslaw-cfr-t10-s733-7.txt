10 C.F.R. § 733.7 — Referral to the contracting officer.
Citation: us:cfr:10:733.7
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-733.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 733 (Allegations Of Research Misconduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the DOE Office of the Inspector General declines to investigate an allegation of research misconduct, the DOE Element should forward the allegation to the contracting officer responsible for administration of the contract or financial assistance agreement to which the allegation pertains.
