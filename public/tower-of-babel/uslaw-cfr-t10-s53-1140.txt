10 C.F.R. § 53.1140 — Early site permits.
Citation: us:cfr:10:53.1140
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-53.1140
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 53 (Risk-Informed, Technology-Inclusive Regulatory Framework For Commercial Nuclear Plants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Sections 53.1140 through 53.1188 set out the requirements and procedures applicable to Commission issuance of an early site permit under this part for approval of a site for a commercial nuclear plant separate from the filing of an application for a CP or COL for the facility.
