24 C.F.R. § 3800.10 — Scope of rules.
Citation: us:cfr:24:3800.10
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-3800.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 3800 (Investigations In Consumer Regulatory Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to investigations and investigational proceedings undertaken by the Secretary, or the Secretary's designee, pursuant to the following:
(a) The Interstate Land Sales Full Disclosure Act, 15 U.S.C. 1701 et seq.;
(b) The National Manufactured Housing Construction and Safety Standards Act of 1974, 42 U.S.C. 5401 et seq.; and
(c) The Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act of 1974, 12 U.S.C. 2601 et seq.
