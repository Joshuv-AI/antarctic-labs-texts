19 U.S.C. § 2076 — Advances in foreign countries

Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 10 (Customs Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Section 3648 of the Revised Statutes (31 U.S.C. 529b) 1 shall not apply to payments made for the Bureau of Customs 2 in foreign countries.
