7 C.F.R. § 761.209 — Loan funds for beginning farmers.
Citation: us:cfr:7:761.209
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-761.209
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 761 (Farm Loan Programs; General Program Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each fiscal year, the Agency reserves a portion of direct and guaranteed FO and OL loan funds for beginning farmers in accordance with section 346(b)(2) of the Act.
