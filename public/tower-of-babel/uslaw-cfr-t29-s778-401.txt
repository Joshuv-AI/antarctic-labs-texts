29 C.F.R. § 778.401 — Regulations issued under section 7(g)(3).
Citation: us:cfr:29:778.401
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-778.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 778 (Overtime Compensation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Regulations issued pursuant to section 7(g) (3) of the Act are published as Part 548 of this chapter. Payments made in conformance with these regulations satisfy the overtime pay requirements of the Act.
