20 C.F.R. § 684.850 — What cost principles apply to Indian and Native American funds?
Citation: us:cfr:20:684.850
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-684.850
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 684 (Indian And Native American Programs Under Title I Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The cost principles at 2 CFR part 200, subpart E, Uniform Administrative Requirements, Cost Principles, & Audit Requirements for Federal Awards, and the Department's modifications to 2 CFR part 200, subpart E, at 2 CFR part 2900, apply to INA program grantees.
