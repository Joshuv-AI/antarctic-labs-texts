40 C.F.R. § 158.1710 — Structural and wood-destroying pest claims.
Citation: us:cfr:40:158.1710
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-158.1710
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 158 (Data Requirements For Pesticides). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an application for registration or amended registration requests a labeling claim specific to a structural or wood-destroying pest not identified in §§ 158.1782 through 158.1786, EPA may require submission of product performance data, with testing on that specific pest and subject to specific performance standards, to support those claims for effectiveness.
