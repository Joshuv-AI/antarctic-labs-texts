7 C.F.R. § 1780.63 — Sewage treatment and bulk water sales contracts.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1780.63
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1780.63
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1780 (Water And Waste Loans And Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Owners entering into agreements with private or public parties to treat sewage or supply bulk water shall have written contracts for such service and all such contracts shall be subject to the Agency concurrence. Section 1780.62 should be used as a guide to prepare such contracts.
