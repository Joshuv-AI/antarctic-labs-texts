36 U.S.C. § 150503 — Purposes

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1505 (National Conference Of State Societies, Washington, District Of Columbia). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purposes of the corporation are—
(1)
to promote friendly and cooperative relations between the State and territorial societies in the District of Columbia;
(2)
to foster, participate in, and encourage educational, cultural, charitable, civic, and patriotic programs and activities in the District of Columbia and surrounding communities; and
(3)
to act as contact agent with States for carrying out State and national programs.
