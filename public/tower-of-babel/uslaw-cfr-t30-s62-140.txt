30 C.F.R. § 62.140 — Dual hearing protection level.
Citation: us:cfr:30:62.140
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-62.140
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 62 (Occupational Noise Exposure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If during any work shift a miner's noise exposure exceeds the dual hearing protection level, the mine operator must, in addition to the actions required for noise exposures that exceed the permissible exposure level, provide and ensure the concurrent use of both an ear plug and an ear muff type hearing protector.
