Trends in the Prevalance Gap of Perceived Depressive Symptoms between Cities or Provinces, during 2013–2022
Published: 2023

In 2022, the age-standardized rate of perceived depressive symptoms among individuals aged ≥19 years was the lowest in Sejong-si (4.1%) and the highest in Incheon (8.0%). The prevalance gap of perceived depressive symptoms between the highest and lowest rates increased from 3.8%p in 2021 to 3.9%p in 2022 (Figure 1).

*The prevalence of perceived depressive symptoms: the percentage of individuals (aged ≥19 years) who experienced such extreme sorrow or despair to the extent of withdrawing from daily life for 2 weeks.

†Prevalence rates in Figure 1 were age-standardized using the 2005 projected population.
