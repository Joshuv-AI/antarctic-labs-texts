5 C.F.R. § 1312.37 — Fees.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1312 (Classification, Downgrading, Declassification And Safeguarding Of National Security Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

There will normally be no fees charged for the mandatory review of classified material for declassification under this section.
