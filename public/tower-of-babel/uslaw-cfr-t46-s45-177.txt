46 C.F.R. § 45.177 — Freeboard requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:46:45.177
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-45.177
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 45 (Great Lakes Load Lines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) All barges must have a minimum freeboard of 24 inches (610 mm).
(b) Additionally, open hopper barges must have a combined freeboard plus cargo box coaming height of at least 54 inches (1,372 mm).
