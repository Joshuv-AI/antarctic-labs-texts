22 C.F.R. § 9b.5 — b.5 Temporary Department of State press building passes.
Citation: us:cfr:22:9b.5
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-9b.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 9b (PART 9b—REGULATIONS GOVERNING DEPARTMENT OF STATE PRESS BUILDING PASSES). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A media correspondent or technician who meets all the qualifications stated in §§ 9b.2(a)(1) and 9b.2(a)(2) or §§ 9b.3(a) and 9b.3(b), but does not have regular and substantial assignments in connection with the Department of State may make arrangements with the Office of Press Relations for the issuance of a visitor's pass valid for one day.
