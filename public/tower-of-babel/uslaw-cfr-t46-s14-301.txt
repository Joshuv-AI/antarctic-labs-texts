46 C.F.R. § 14.301 — Paying off of merchant mariner during or after voyage upon which shipping articles are required.
Citation: us:cfr:46:14.301
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-14.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 14 (Shipment And Discharge Of Merchant Mariners). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each master or individual in charge of a vessel when shipping articles are required must complete and sign, and each merchant mariner paid off during or after such a voyage must sign the articles and otherwise comply with the requirements of this subpart. When signed by the master or individual in charge and by the mariner, the articles constitute a release from the duties to which they bound their parties.
