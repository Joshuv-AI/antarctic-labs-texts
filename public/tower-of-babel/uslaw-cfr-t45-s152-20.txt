45 C.F.R. § 152.20 — Prohibitions on pre-existing condition exclusions and waiting periods.
Citation: us:cfr:45:152.20
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-152.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 152 (Pre-Existing Condition Insurance Plan Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Pre-existing condition exclusions. A PCIP must provide all enrollees with health coverage that does not impose any pre-existing condition exclusions (as defined in § 152.2) with respect to such coverage.
(b) Waiting periods. A PCIP may not impose a waiting period with respect to the coverage of services after the effective date of enrollment.
