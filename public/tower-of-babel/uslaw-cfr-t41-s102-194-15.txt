41 C.F.R. § 102-194.15 — -194.15 What is an Optional form?
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-194.15
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-194.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-194 (194—Standard And Optional Forms Management Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An Optional form is approved by GSA for nonmandatory Governmentwide use and is used by two or more agencies. This criteria is the same whether the form resides on paper or purely electronic.
