33 C.F.R. § 187.304 — Titling exclusively in one State.
Citation: us:cfr:33:187.304
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-187.304
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 187 (Vessel Identification System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A State must require that all vessels required to be numbered in the State under 46 U.S.C. Chapter 123 be titled only in that State, if that State issues titles to that class of vessels.
