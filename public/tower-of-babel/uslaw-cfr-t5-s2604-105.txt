5 C.F.R. § 2604.105 — Other rights and services.
Citation: us:cfr:5:2604.105
Creator: Office Of Government Ethics
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-2604.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 2604 (Freedom Of Information Act Rules And Schedule Of Fees For The Production Of Public Financial Disclosure Reports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this part will be construed to entitle any person, as of right, to any service or to the disclosure of any record to which such person is not entitled under the FOIA.
