45 C.F.R. § 5.71 — How does HHS retain FOIA records?
Citation: us:cfr:45:5.71
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-5.71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 5 (Freedom Of Information Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We will preserve records created in administering the Department's Freedom of Information program until disposition is authorized under an applicable General Records Schedule or other records schedule duly approved by the Archivist of the United States.
