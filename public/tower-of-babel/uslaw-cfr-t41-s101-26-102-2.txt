41 C.F.R. § 101-26.102-2 — -26.102-2 Utilization by military agencies.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-26.102-2
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-26.102-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-26 (26—Procurement Sources And Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Military activities shall utilize the buying services of GSA when:
(a) GSA has agreed with the Secretary of Defense, or with the Secretary of a military department in connection with the requirements of that department, to perform such buying services; and
(b) The items involved are not properly obtainable from GSA stock or Federal Supply Schedules.
