7 C.F.R. § 915.25 — Acceptance.
Citation: us:cfr:7:915.25
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-915.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 915 (Avocados Grown In South Florida). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person selected by the Secretary as a member or as an alternate member of the committee shall qualify by filing a written acceptance with the Secretary within ten days after being notified of such selection.
