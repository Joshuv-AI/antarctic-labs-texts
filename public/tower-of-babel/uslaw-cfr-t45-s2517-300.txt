45 C.F.R. § 2517.300 — Who may participate in a community-based service-learning program?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2517.300
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2517.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2517 (Community-Based Service-Learning Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

School-age youth as defined in § 2510.20 of this chapter may participate in a community-based program.
