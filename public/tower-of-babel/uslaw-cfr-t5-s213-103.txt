5 C.F.R. § 213.103 — Publication of excepted appointing authorities in Schedules A, B, C, D, and Policy/Career.
Citation: us:cfr:5:213.103
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-213.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 213 (Excepted Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Schedule A, B, C, D, and Policy/Career appointing authorities available for use by all agencies will be published as regulations in the Federal Register and the Code of Federal Regulations.
(b) Establishment and revocation of Schedule A, B, C, and Policy/Career appointing authorities applicable to a single agency shall be published monthly in the Notices section of the Federal Register.
(c) A consolidated listing of all Schedule A, B, C, and Policy/Career authorities current as of June 30 of each year, with assigned authority numbers, shall be published annually as a notice in the Federal Register.
