10 C.F.R. § 12.108 — Awards against other agencies.
Citation: us:cfr:10:12.108
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-12.108
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 12 (Implementation Of The Equal Access To Justice Act In Agency Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an applicant is entitled to an award because it prevails over another agency of the United States that participates in a proceeding before the Commission and takes a position that is not substantially justified, the award or an appropriate portion of the award shall be made against that agency.
