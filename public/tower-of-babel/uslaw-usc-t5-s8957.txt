5 U.S.C. § 8957 — Coverage of restored survivor or disability annuitants
Citation: us:usc:5:8957
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section8957
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart G (Insurance And Annuities); Chapter 89A (Enhanced Dental Benefits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A surviving spouse, disability annuitant, or surviving child whose annuity is terminated and is later restored, may continue enrollment in a dental benefits plan subject to the terms and conditions prescribed in regulations issued by the Office.
