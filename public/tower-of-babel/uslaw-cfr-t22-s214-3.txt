22 C.F.R. § 214.3 — A.I.D. Advisory Committee Management Officer.
Citation: us:cfr:22:214.3
Creator: Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-214.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 214 (Advisory Committee Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Advisory Committee Management Officer is responsible to the Administrator for the establishment of uniform administrative guidelines and management controls which must be consistent with directives of the Director of the OMB under sections 7 and 10 of the Act.
