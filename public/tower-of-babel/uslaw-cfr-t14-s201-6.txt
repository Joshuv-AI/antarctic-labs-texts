14 C.F.R. § 201.6 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:201.6
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-201.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 201 (Air Carrier Authority Under Subtitle Vii Of Title 49 Of The United States Code). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless the certificate or the order authorizing its issuance shall otherwise provide, such terms, conditions and limitations as are set forth in this part, and as may from time to time be prescribed by the Department, shall apply to the exercise of the privileges granted by each certificate issued under section 41102 or section 41103 of 49 U.S.C. Subtitle VII.
