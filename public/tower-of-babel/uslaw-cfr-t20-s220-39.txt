20 C.F.R. § 220.39 — Disability determination for a surviving divorced spouse or remarried widow(er).
Citation: us:cfr:20:220.39
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-220.39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 220 (Determining Disability). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A surviving divorced spouse or a remarried widow(er) must be found disabled under the Social Security Act in order to qualify for both an annuity under the Railroad Retirement Act and early Medicare based upon disability. Disability determinations for surviving divorced spouses and remarried widow(er)s are governed by the applicable regulations of the Social Security Administration, found at § 404.1577 of this title.
