45 C.F.R. § 2105.44 — When may you ask the Agency for a fee waiver?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2105.44
Creator: Commission Of Fine Arts
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2105.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2105 (Requirements For Compliance With The Freedom Of Information Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You should request a fee waiver when your request is first submitted to the Agency (see § 2105.5).
(b) You may submit a fee waiver request at a later time if the Agency has not yet completed processing your request.
