9 C.F.R. § 557.3 — No fish or fish product to be imported without compliance with applicable regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:9:557.3
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-557.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 557 (Importation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No fish or fish product offered for importation from any foreign country shall be admitted into the United States if it is adulterated or misbranded or does not comply with all the requirements of this subchapter that would apply to it if it were a domestic product.
