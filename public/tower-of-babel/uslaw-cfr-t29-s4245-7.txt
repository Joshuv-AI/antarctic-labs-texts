29 C.F.R. § 4245.7 — Successor plan.
Citation: us:cfr:29:4245.7
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4245.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4245 (Duties Of Plan Sponsor Of An Insolvent Plan). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The plan sponsor of a successor plan created by a partition order under § 4233.14 of this chapter must issue to participants and beneficiaries any notice required under the partition order and is not required to file or issue notices under § 4245.3 or § 4245.5.
