36 U.S.C. § 20503 — Powers
Citation: us:usc:36:20503
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section20503
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 205 (American Chemical Society). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation may—
(1)
adopt a constitution, bylaws, and regulations;
(2)
fill vacancies;
(3)
provide for the election of members and the division of those members into classes;
(4)
receive property, hold the property absolutely or in trust, invest and manage the property, and use the property and income arising from it to carry out the purposes of the corporation; and
(5)
do any other act necessary and proper to carry out the purposes of the corporation.
