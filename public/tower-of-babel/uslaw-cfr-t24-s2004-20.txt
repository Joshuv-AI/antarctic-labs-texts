24 C.F.R. § 2004.20 — General prohibition.
Citation: us:cfr:24:2004.20
Creator: Office Of Inspector General, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-2004.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 2004 (Subpoenas And Production In Response To Subpoenas Or Demands Of Courts Or Other Authorities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No employee may produce official records and information or provide any testimony relating to official information in response to a demand or request without the prior, written approval of the Inspector General or the Counsel.
