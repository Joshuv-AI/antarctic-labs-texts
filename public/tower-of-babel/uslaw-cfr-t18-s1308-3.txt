18 C.F.R. § 1308.3 — Exclusions.
Citation: us:cfr:18:1308.3
Creator: Tennessee Valley Authority
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-1308.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 1308 (Contract Disputes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) This part does not apply to any TVA contract which does not contain a disputes clause.
(b) Except as otherwise specifically provided, this part does not apply to any TVA contract entered into prior to March 1, 1979, or to any dispute relating to such a contract.
