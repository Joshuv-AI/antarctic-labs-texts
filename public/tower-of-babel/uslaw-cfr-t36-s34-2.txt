36 C.F.R. § 34.2 — Applicability and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:36:34.2
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-34.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 34 (El Portal Administrative Site Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The regulations in this part apply to all persons entering, using, visiting, residing on or otherwise within the boundaries of the El Portal Administrative Site. All regulations apply throughout the site, with certain specific exceptions provided for leased lands.
(b) The regulations in this part may be enforced only by persons authorized to enforce the other provisions of this chapter.
