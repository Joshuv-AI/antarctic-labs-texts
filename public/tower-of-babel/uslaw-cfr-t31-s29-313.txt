31 C.F.R. § 29.313 — Federal Benefit Payments are computed based on retirement eligibility as of the separation date and service creditable as of June 30, 1997.
Citation: us:cfr:31:29.313
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-29.313
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 29 (Federal Benefit Payments Under Certain District Of Columbia Retirement Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise provided in this subpart, the amount of Federal Benefit Payments is computed based on retirement eligibility as of the separation date and service creditable as of June 30, 1997.
