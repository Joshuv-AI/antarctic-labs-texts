41 C.F.R. § 105-71.104 — -71.104 Effect on other issuances.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-71.104
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-71.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-71 (71—Uniform Administrative Requirements For Grants And Cooperative Agreements With State And Local Governments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All other grants administration provisions of codified program regulations, program manuals, handbooks and other nonregulatory materials which are inconsistent with this part are superseded, except to the extent they are required by statute, or authorized in accordance with the exception provision in § 105-71.105.
