49 C.F.R. § 231.22 — Operation of track motor cars.
Citation: us:cfr:49:231.22
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-231.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 231 (Railroad Safety Appliance Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

On and after August 1, 1963, it shall be unlawful for any railroad subject to the requirements of the Safety Appliance Acts to operate or permit to be operated on its line track motor cars to pull or haul trailers, push trucks, hand cars, or similar cars or equipment.
