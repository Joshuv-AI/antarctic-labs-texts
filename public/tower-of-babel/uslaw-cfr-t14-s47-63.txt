14 C.F.R. § 47.63 — Application.
Citation: us:cfr:14:47.63
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-47.63
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 47 (Aircraft Registration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A manufacturer or dealer that wishes to obtain a Dealer's Aircraft Registration Certificate must submit—
(a) A Dealer's Aircraft Registration Certificate Application, AC Form 8050-5; and
(b) The fee required by § 47.17.
