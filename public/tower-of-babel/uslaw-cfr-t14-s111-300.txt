14 C.F.R. § 111.300 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:111.300
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-111.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 111 (Pilot Records Database). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to each individual who is employed as a pilot by, or is seeking employment as a pilot with, an operator or entity subject to the applicability of this part, as set forth in § 111.1.
