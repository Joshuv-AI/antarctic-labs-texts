34 C.F.R. § 463.710 — How are infrastructure costs funded?
Citation: us:cfr:34:463.710
Creator: Office Of Career, Technical, And Adult Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-463.710
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 463 (Adult Education And Family Literacy Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Infrastructure costs are funded either through the local funding mechanism described in § 463.715 or through the State funding mechanism described in § 463.730.
