12 U.S.C. § 4519 — Authority to provide for review of regulated entities
Citation: us:usc:12:4519
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section4519
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 46 (Government Sponsored Enterprises); Subchapter I (Supervision And Regulation Of Enterprises); Part A (Financial Safety And Soundness Regulator). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director may, on such terms and conditions as the Director deems appropriate, contract with any entity to conduct a review of the regulated entities.
