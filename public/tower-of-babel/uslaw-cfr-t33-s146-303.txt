33 C.F.R. § 146.303 — Notice and written report of casualties.
Citation: us:cfr:33:146.303
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-146.303
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 146 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The owner, operator, or person in charge of a vessel, including a MODU, engaged in OCS activities must ensure compliance with the notice of casualty requirements in 46 CFR part 4.
