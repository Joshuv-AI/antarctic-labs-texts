40 C.F.R. § 52.14 — State ambient air quality standards.
Citation: us:cfr:40:52.14
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-52.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 52 (Approval And Promulgation Of Implementation Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any ambient air quality standard submitted with a plan which is less stringent than a national standard is not considered part of the plan.
