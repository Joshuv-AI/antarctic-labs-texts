42 U.S.C. § 8854 — Utilization of National Forest System in wood energy development projects
Citation: us:usc:42:8854
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section8854
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 96 (Biomass Energy And Alcohol Fuels); Subchapter III (Rural, Agricultural, And Forestry Biomass Energy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Agriculture may make available the timber resources of the National Forest System, in accordance with appropriate timber appraisal and sale procedures, for use by biomass energy projects.
