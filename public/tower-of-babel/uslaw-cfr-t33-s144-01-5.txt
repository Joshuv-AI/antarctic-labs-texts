33 C.F.R. § 144.01-5 — -5 Location and launching of life floats.
Citation: us:cfr:33:144.01-5
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-144.01-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 144 (Lifesaving Appliances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The life floats shall be distributed in accessible locations and mounted on the outboard sides of the working platform in such a manner as to be readily launched.
