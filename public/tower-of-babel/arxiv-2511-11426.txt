Search for possible signals of space-time non-commutativity from ACT DR6
Vishnu Rajagopal, Puxun Wu

Abstract
The non-commutative geometry offers an effective framework for describing physics at the Planck scale, incorporating generic quantum-gravitational effects through an intrinsic minimal length and the κ \kappa -deformed space-time stands out as a particularly well-developed model based on a Lie-algebraic type non-commutative space-time structure. We investigate the inflationary paradigm and the associated primordial perturbations within the framework of κ \kappa -deformed non-commutative space-time. By constructing a scalar field theory invariant under the κ \kappa -Poincaré algebra, we derive the deformed oscillator algebra for the field modes of the primordial perturbations resulting in the non-trivial scale dependent modification of both the scalar and tensor power spectra. Further, we compute the corresponding κ \kappa -deformed corrections to the scalar spectral index and its running, finding that the κ \kappa -deformation naturally provides a higher value for spectral index which offers a potential resolution to the discrepancy between the values reported by PLANCK and ACT. Finally we perform a comprehensive Bayesian analysis using the latest ACT DR6 data, constraining the κ \kappa -deformed non-commutative length scale to λ = 2.17 − 1.53 + 2.33 × 10 − 30 ​ m \lambda=2.17^{+2.33}_{-1.53}\times 10^{-30}m at the 1 ​ σ 1\sigma confidence level, suggesting the precision cosmology as a powerful probe of quantum gravity phenomenology.
