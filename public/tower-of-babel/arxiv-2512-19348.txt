de Rham affineness of the Nygaard filtered prismatization in positive characteristic
Shubhankar Sahai

Abstract
. Let k k be a perfect ring of characteristic p > 0 p>0 , and let R R be an animated k k -algebra. This note aims to show that the Nygaard filtered prismatization R Nyg R^{\mathrm{Nyg}} of R R is naturally isomorphic, as a stack over k Nyg k^{\mathrm{Nyg}} , to the relative spectrum over k Nyg k^{\mathrm{Nyg}} of the Rees algebra of the Nygaard filtered prismatic cohomology of R R relative to k k . In doing so, we axiomatise the functorial affineness property displayed by the relative Nygaard filtered prismatization, and dub it de Rham affineness after the fundamental example of the functor sending an animated ring to its relative de Rham stack. While we treat this concept as an organising tool for the author’s forthcoming work studying the syntomification of Frobenius liftable schemes [ Sah25c ] , we are able to frame some questions based on a structural theorem of independent interest: a functor to stacks which is de Rham affine often arises via ring stacks through transmutation.

1.1.
Background.

Let
X
be a smooth scheme over
k

\mathrm{dR}_{X/k}
of

-algebra over

The

can also be recovered via the theory of the de Rham stack, which produces, for such

(X/k)^{\mathrm{dR}}
with the property that, as
E_{\infty}
-algebras over
k

\Gamma((X/k)^{\mathrm{dR}},{\mathcal{O}}_{(X/k)^{\mathrm{dR}}})\simeq\mathrm{dR}_{X/k}.

In
[
Mon22
]
, Mondal answers a question of Illusie by showing that when
X
is affine then
\mathrm{dR}_{X/k}
along with its multiplicative structure is enough to
reconstruct
the stack
(X/k)^{\mathrm{dR}}
, in fact showing that the stack is the
spectrum
of the coconnective
E_{\infty}
-ring
\mathrm{dR}_{X/k}
in the sense of
[
MM24
, Construction 1.2]
i.e. as stacks on
k
-algebras one has

(X/k)^{\mathrm{dR}}\simeq\mathrm{Maps}_{\mathrm{CAlg}_{k}}(\mathrm{dR}_{X/k},-)

where
\mathrm{CAlg}_{k}
is the
\infty\text{-category}
of
E_{\infty}
-algebras over
k.

The two viewpoints on de Rham cohomology reflect different structures of the theory, and one can hope to use this result to pass from one side to the other as the forthcoming example shows.

Remark 1.5

Definition 1.6

(De Rham affineness)
.

Let
A
be an animated ring and
\mathrm{ARing}_{A}
be the
\infty\text{-category}
of animated
A
-algebras. Let
S
be a stack with an affine diagonal,
\mathrm{Stacks}_{S}
the
\infty\text{-category}
of stacks over
S
and
\mathrm{DAlg}(S)
be the
\infty\text{-category}
of derived algebras in quasi-coherent sheaves on
S
. Assume that we are given a functor

\mathfrak{X}\colon\mathrm{ARing}_{A}\to\mathrm{Stacks}_{S}.

We will say that
\mathfrak{X}
is
de Rham affine relative to
S
if there is a functor

\mathcal{T}\colon\mathrm{ARing}_{A}\to\mathrm{DAlg}(S)

and a natural in
B\in\mathrm{ARing}_{A}
isomorphism of
S
-stacks

\mathfrak{X}(B)\simeq\underline{\mathrm{Spec}}_{S}(\mathcal{T}(B))

where
\underline{\mathrm{Spec}}_{S}(-)
denotes the relative spectrum of
\mathcal{T}(A)
i.e. the functor corepresented by
\mathcal{T}(A)
over
S.

In particular there is an equivalence of
\mathrm{Stacks}_{S}
-valued functors

\mathfrak{X}(-)\simeq\underline{\mathrm{Spec}}_{S}(\mathcal{T}(-)).

Remark 1.7

(Colimit preserving de Rham affine functors arise via transmutation)

Appendix A
Testing faithful flatness on a constructible stratification

In this appendix we prove some of the faithful flatness lemmas needed in the main body of the note. Fix an animated ring
A
and
f\in\pi_{0}(A).
We are interested in testing
t
-exactness for modules
M\in\mathrm{Mod}(A)
on the ‘constructible’ stratification of the derived scheme
{\mathrm{Spec}}A
given by
{\mathrm{Spec}}A[1/f]
and the underlying topological space of
{\mathrm{Spec}}A/f
. This will be obtained by an analysis of the category of complexes on both these loci. The main results are recorded Corollaries
A.5
and
A.7
.

We begin with some general remarks.

Remark A.1
.

(Recollections on flatness over animated rings) Let
A
be an animated ring and
M\in\mathrm{Mod}_{A}
. Then recall from
[
Lur17
, Definition 7.2.2.10]
that is called flat if
\pi_{0}M
is a flat
\pi_{0}(A)
module and the natural action map
\pi_{*}(A)\otimes_{\pi_{0}(A)}\pi_{0}(M)\to\pi_{*}(M)
is an isomorphism of
\mathbf{Z}
-graded abelian groups (in particular,
M
is connective). By the derived variant of Lazard’s theorem
[
Lur17
, Theorem 7.2.2.15]
, when
M
is already known to be connective, this condition is equivalent to the following two (easier to work with) and equivalent conditions

(1)

The functor
M\otimes_{A}-
is left
t
-exact on
\mathrm{Mod}_{A}
i.e. sends coconnective modules to coconnective modules,

(2)

The functor
M\otimes_{A}-
preserves discrete
A
-modules.

In the sequel we will use flat and left
t
-exact interchangeably for connective modules.

Remark A.2
.

(Recollection on
faithful flatness
over an animated ring.) Following
[
Lur11a
, Definition 5.2]
we say that a module
M
over an animated ring
A
is faithfully flat if it is flat in the sense of Remark
A.1
and
\pi_{0}M
is faithfully flat over
\pi_{0}A
.

Remark A.3
.

(Recollections on local cohomology.)
Let
A
be an animated ring and
I\subset\pi_{0}(A)
a finitely generated ideal. Then recall from
[
Lur11b
, § 4.1]
that the category
\mathrm{Mod}_{A}^{I\text{-}\mathrm{tors}}
of
I
-torsion modules is a colocalizing subcategory of
\mathrm{Mod}_{A}
consisting of those modules whose homotopy groups are endowed with a locally nilpotent action of
I
. The right adjoint
\mathrm{Mod}_{A}^{I\text{-}\mathrm{tors}}\hookrightarrow\mathrm{Mod}_{A}
is denoted by
\Gamma_{I}(-)\colon\mathrm{Mod}_{A}\to\mathrm{Mod}_{A}^{I\text{-}\mathrm{tors}}
and agrees with Grothendieck’s theory of local cohomology in the Noetherian case. We are here interested in the following properties of local cohomology when
I=(f)
is a principal ideal. Fix a module
M\in\mathrm{Mod}_{A}.

(1)

There is a cofiber sequence

\Gamma_{(f)}(M)\to M\to M[1/f]

which is a sequence of coconnective modules whenever
M
is coconnective. Indeed, this follows from
[
Lur11b
, Example 4.1.14.]
and
[
Lur11b
, Proposition 4.1.18.]
) respectively.

(2)

The canonical map
\Gamma_{(f)}(M/\!\!/f)\to M/\!\!/f
is an isomorphism. This can be seen as the cofiber is
(M/\!\!/f)[1/f]
by
(1)
and is clearly
0
since
f
acts both null-homotopically and invertibly on
M.
More conceptually,
M/\!\!/f
has
f
-torsion homotopy groups, and therefore is in
\mathrm{Mod}_{A}^{I\text{-}\mathrm{tors}}
and so
\Gamma_{(f)}(-)
is the identity functor.

(3)

There is an equivalence
\Gamma_{(f)}M=\Gamma_{(f)}(A)\otimes_{A}M
. In particular for any
N\in\mathrm{Mod}_{A}
we have
\Gamma_{(f)}(M)\otimes_{A}N=\Gamma_{(f)}(M\otimes_{A}N)=M\otimes_{A}\Gamma_{(f)}(N)
by
[
Lur11b
, Proposition 4.1.12]
. In particular
\Gamma_{(f)}(M)\otimes_{A}N\in\mathrm{Mod}^{I\text{-}\mathrm{tors}}_{A}
always (see also
[
Lur11b
, Corollary 4.1.7]
for a more general statement).

We will now use the above observations to prove a constructible criterion for faithful flatness. This statement should certainly be well known but we don’t know a reference.

Lemma A.4
.

Let
M\in\mathrm{Mod}_{A}
. Then the following conditions are equivalent

(1)

M\otimes_{A}-
is left
t
-exact,

(2)

M[1/f]\otimes_{A[1/f]}-
and
M/\!\!/f\otimes_{A/\!\!/f}-
are left
t
-exact on
\mathrm{Mod}_{A[1/f]}
and
\mathrm{Mod}_{A/\!\!/f}
respectively

Proof.

The forward direction is immediate since
t
-exactness for connective
E_{\infty}
-rings is a property of the underlying spectra.

So let us prove the other direction.
We will show that if
N\in(\mathrm{Mod}_{A})_{\leq 0}
then
M\otimes_{A}N\in(\mathrm{Mod}_{A})_{\leq 0}
.

Using point
(1)
of Remark
A.3
, there is a cofiber sequence of coconnective
A
-modules

\Gamma_{(f)}(N)\to N\to N[1/f]

Tensoring with
-\otimes_{A}M
we get

\Gamma_{(f)}(N)\otimes_{A}M\to N\otimes_{A}M\to N[1/f]\otimes_{A}M.

(A.1)

Observe that if the left and right terms of
A.1
are coconnective then so is the middle term and we would be done.

First note that because
N[1/f]\otimes_{A}M=N[1/f]\otimes_{A[1/f]}M[1/f]
, we learn that the rightmost term of
A.1
is in fact coconnective by the hypothesis on
M.

We are left to understand
\Gamma_{(f)}(N)\otimes_{A}M
.
For this, first consider the canonical cofiber sequence

M\xrightarrow{f}M\to M/\!\!/f

which we tensor with
\Gamma_{(f)}(N)
to get

\Gamma_{(f)}(N)\otimes_{A}M\xrightarrow{f}\Gamma_{(f)}(N)\otimes_{A}M\to\Gamma_{(f)}(N)\otimes_{A}M/\!\!/f.

(A.2)

Note that the right most term is the cofiber of multiplication by
f
on
\Gamma_{(f)}(N)\otimes_{A}M.

To control the terms in
A.2
, we use the colocalizing properties of local cohomology as explained in Remark
A.3
. Specifically by point
(3)
of Remark
A.3
we have
\Gamma_{(f)}(N)\otimes_{A}M/f=N\otimes_{A}\Gamma_{(f)}(M/\!\!/f)
and by point
(2)
of Remark
A.3
we have
N\otimes_{A}\Gamma_{(f)}(M/\!\!/f)=N\otimes_{A}M/\!\!/f.

Now
N\otimes_{A}M/\!\!/f=N/\!\!/f\otimes_{A/\!\!/f}M/\!\!/f
and since
N/\!\!/f\in(\mathrm{Mod}_{A/\!\!/f})_{\leq 1}
we learn that
N/\!\!/f\otimes_{A/\!\!/f}M/\!\!/f\in(\mathrm{Mod}_{A/\!\!/f})_{\leq 1}
by the
t
-exactness hypothesis on
M/\!\!/f
as an
A/\!\!/f
-module.

All in all

\Gamma_{(f)}(N)\otimes_{A}M/\!\!/f\in(\mathrm{Mod}_{A})_{\leq 1}.

(A.3)

Thus it remains to control the first two terms of
A.2
. Note that by point
(2)
of Remark
A.3
the homotopy groups of
\Gamma_{(f)}(N)\otimes_{A}M
are
(f)
-torsion. Then taking the long exact sequence associated to
A.2
the relevant terms for
i\geq 1
are

\ldots\to 0=\pi_{i+1}(\Gamma_{(f)}(N)\otimes_{A}M/\!\!/f)\to\pi_{i}\Gamma_{(f)}(N)\otimes_{A}M\xrightarrow{f}\pi_{i}\Gamma_{(f)}(N)\otimes_{A}M\to\ldots.

Now since
\pi_{i}\Gamma_{(f)}(N)\otimes_{A}M
is entirely
(f)
-torsion for all
i\in\mathbf{Z}
, we observe that if it is non-zero for
i\geq 1
then
\pi_{i+1}(\Gamma_{(f)}(N)\otimes_{A}M/\!\!/f)\neq 0
which contradicts
A.3
.

∎

Lemma B.2
.

Let

(f)

Appendix C
Colimit preservation and Künneth for derived Nygaard filtered prismatic cohomology

We will prove a Künneth formula for derived Nygaard filtered prismatic cohomology. Our proof follows
[
AB23
, § 3.5]
along with the constructible stratification argument already used in the main text.

In this section we let
(A,I)
be a bounded prism and set
\overline{A}=A/I
. Let
R
and
S
be
p
-completely smooth (discrete)
\overline{A}
-algebras. Our goal is to prove that the canonical morphism of filtered derived algebras over the filtered ring
\mathrm{Fil}_{I}A

\mathrm{KM}\colon\mathrm{Fil}_{\mathrm{Nyg}}F^{*}{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}_{R/A}\widehat{\otimes}_{\mathrm{Fil}_{I}A}\mathrm{Fil}_{\mathrm{Nyg}}F^{*}{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}_{S/A}\to\mathrm{Fil}_{\mathrm{Nyg}}F^{*}{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}_{R\widehat{\otimes}_{\overline{A}}S/A}

is an equivalence.

The functor
\mathrm{Fil}_{\mathrm{Nyg}}F^{*}{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}_{-/A}
is left Kan extended from
p
-completely smooth
\overline{A}
-algebras. Therefore, if
C.5
is an isomorphism, it follows formally from
[
BMS19

that the functor

-complete
\overline{A}

-complete

Follows from Lemma
C.5
.

Lemma C.2
.

The functor which sends a
p
-complete animated
\overline{A}
-algebra
R
to

It suffices to check that the functor which sends a
p
-completely smooth
A

Lemma C.3

-complete animated
\overline{A}
