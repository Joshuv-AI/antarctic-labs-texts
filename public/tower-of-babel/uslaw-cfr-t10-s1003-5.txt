10 C.F.R. § 1003.5 — Extension of time.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1003.5
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1003.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1003 (Office Of Hearings And Appeals Procedural Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a document is required to be filed within a prescribed time, an extension of time to file may be granted by the OHA upon good cause shown.
