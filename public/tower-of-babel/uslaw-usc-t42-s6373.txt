42 U.S.C. § 6373 — Off-highway motor vehicles
Citation: us:usc:42:6373
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section6373
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 77 (Energy Conservation); Subchapter III (Improving Energy Efficiency); Part G (Off-Highway Motor Vehicles). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 1 year after November 9, 1978, the Secretary of Transportation shall complete a study of the energy conservation potential of recreational motor vehicles, including, but not limited to, aircraft and motor boats which are designed for recreational use, and shall submit a report to the President and to the Congress containing the results of such study.
