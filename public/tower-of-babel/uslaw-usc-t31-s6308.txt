31 U.S.C. § 6308 — Use of multiple relationships for different parts of jointly financed projects
Citation: us:usc:31:6308
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section6308
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 63 (Using Procurement Contracts And Grant And Cooperative Agreements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter does not require an executive agency to establish only one relationship between the United States Government and a State, a local government, or other recipient on a jointly financed project involving amounts from more than one program or appropriation when different relationships would otherwise be appropriate for different parts of the project.
