13 C.F.R. § 123.506 — How much can you borrow under the Military Reservist EIDL Program?
Citation: us:cfr:13:123.506
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-123.506
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 123 (Disaster Loan Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You can borrow an amount equal to the substantial economic injury you have suffered or are likely to suffer until normal operations resume as a result of the absence of one or more essential employees called to active service, up to a maximum of $2 million.
