46 C.F.R. § 178.220 — Stability booklet.
Citation: us:cfr:46:178.220
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-178.220
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 178 (Intact Stability And Seaworthiness). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the Commanding Officer, Marine Safety Center determines, in accordance with § 178.210(b), that a vessel must have a stability booklet, the owner or operator must prepare the booklet in accordance with subchapter S of this chapter, and submit it to the Commanding Officer, Marine Safety Center.
