12 U.S.C. § 2208 — Prohibition against use of signed ballots
Citation: us:usc:12:2208
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section2208
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 23 (Farm Credit System); Subchapter IV (Provisions Applicable To Two Or More Classes Of Institutions Of The System); Part D (Activities Of Institutions Of The System). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In any election or merger vote, or other proceeding subject to a vote of the stockholders (or subscribers to the guaranty fund of a bank for cooperatives), conducted by a lending institution of the Farm Credit System, the institution—
(1)
may not use signed ballots; and
(2)
shall implement measures to safeguard the voting process for the protection of the right of stockholders (or subscribers) to a secret ballot.
