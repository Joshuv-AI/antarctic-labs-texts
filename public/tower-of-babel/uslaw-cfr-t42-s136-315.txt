42 C.F.R. § 136.315 — Publication of list of grantees and projects.
Citation: us:cfr:42:136.315
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-136.315
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 136 (Indian Health). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary acting through the Service shall publish annually in the Federal Register a list of organizations receiving grants under this subdivision including for each grantee:
(a) The organization's name and address;
(b) The amount of the grant;
(c) A summary of the project's purposes and its geographic location.
