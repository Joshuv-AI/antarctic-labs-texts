43 C.F.R. § 418.5 — Responsibility for violations.
Citation: us:cfr:43:418.5
Creator: Bureau Of Reclamation, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-418.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 418 (Operating Criteria And Procedures For The Newlands Reclamation Project, Nevada). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Violations of the terms and provisions of this part must be reported immediately to the Bureau. The District or individual water users will be responsible for any shortages to water users occasioned by waste or excess delivery or delivery of water to ineligible land as provided in this part.
