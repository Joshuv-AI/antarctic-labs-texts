42 U.S.C. § 5186 — Emergency public transportation
Citation: us:usc:42:5186
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section5186
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 68 (Disaster Relief); Subchapter IV (Major Disaster Assistance Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President is authorized to provide temporary public transportation service in an area affected by a major disaster to meet emergency needs and to provide transportation to governmental offices, supply centers, stores, post offices, schools, major employment centers, and such other places as may be necessary in order to enable the community to resume its normal pattern of life as soon as possible.
