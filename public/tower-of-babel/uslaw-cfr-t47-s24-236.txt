47 C.F.R. § 24.236 — Field strength limits.
Citation: us:cfr:47:24.236
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-24.236
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 24 (Personal Communications Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The predicted or measured median field strength at any location on the border of the PCS service area shall not exceed 47 dBuV/m unless the parties agree to a higher field strength.
