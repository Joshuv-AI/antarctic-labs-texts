24 C.F.R. § 972.136 — Timing of submission of conversion plans to HUD.
Citation: us:cfr:24:972.136
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-972.136
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 972 (Conversion Of Public Housing To Tenant-Based Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirements of this section are on-going requirements. If the PHA must submit a plan for conversion, it must submit the conversion plan as part of the PHA's Annual Plan, beginning with PHA fiscal years that commence six months after the effective date of HUD's final rule establishing the cost methodology for required conversions.
