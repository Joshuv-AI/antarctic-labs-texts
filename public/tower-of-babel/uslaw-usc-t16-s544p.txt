16 U.S.C. § 544p — Severability
Citation: us:usc:16:544p
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section544p
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 2 (National Forests); Subchapter II (Scenic Areas). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
1 If any provision of sections 544 to 544p of this title or the application thereof to any person, State, Indian tribe, entity, or circumstance is held invalid, neither the remainder of sections 544 to 544p of this title, nor the application of any provisions herein to other persons, States, Indian tribes, entities, or circumstances, shall be affected thereby.
