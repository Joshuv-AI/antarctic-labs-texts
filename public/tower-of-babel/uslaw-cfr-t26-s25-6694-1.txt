26 C.F.R. § 25.6694-1 — -1 Section 6694 penalties applicable to tax return preparer.
Citation: us:cfr:26:25.6694-1
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-25.6694-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 25 (Gift Tax; Gifts Made After December 31, 1954). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In general. For general definitions regarding section 6694 penalties applicable to preparers of gift tax returns or claims for refund, see § 1.6694-1 of this chapter.
(b) Effective/applicability date. Paragraph (a) of this section is applicable to returns and claims for refund filed, and advice provided, after December 31, 2008.
