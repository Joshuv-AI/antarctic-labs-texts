48 C.F.R. § 45.604-1 — 45.604-1 Sales procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:48:45.604-1
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-45.604-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 45 (Government Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Surplus personal property that has completed screening in accordance with 45.602-3(a) shall be sold in accordance with the policy for the sale of surplus personal property contained in the Federal Management Regulation, at part 102-38 (41 CFR part 102-38). Agencies may specify implementing procedures.
