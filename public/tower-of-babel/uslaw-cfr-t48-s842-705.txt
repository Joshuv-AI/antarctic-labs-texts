48 C.F.R. § 842.705 — 842.705 Final indirect cost rates.
Citation: us:cfr:48:842.705
Creator: Chapter 8—Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-842.705
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 842 (Contract Administration And Audit Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except when the quick-closeout procedures described in FAR 42.708 are used, contracting officers shall request contract audits on proposed final indirect cost rates and billing rates for use in cost reimbursement and fixed-price incentive contracts as prescribed in FAR subpart 42.7.
