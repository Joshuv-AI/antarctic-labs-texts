24 C.F.R. § 982.506 — Negotiating rent to owner.
Citation: us:cfr:24:982.506
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-982.506
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 982 (Section 8 Tenant-Based Assistance: Housing Choice Voucher Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The owner and the family negotiate the rent to owner. At the family's request, the PHA must help the family negotiate the rent to owner.
