46 C.F.R. § 56.50-35 — -35 Condensate pumps.
Citation: us:cfr:46:56.50-35
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-56.50-35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 56 (Piping Systems And Appurtenances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Two means must be provided for discharging the condensate from the main condenser, one of which must be independent of the main propelling machinery.
