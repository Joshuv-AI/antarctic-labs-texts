10 C.F.R. § 1040.71 — Discrimination prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1040.71
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1040.71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1040 (Nondiscrimination In Federally Assisted Programs Or Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No handicapped person shall, because a recipient's facilities are inaccessible to or unuseable by handicapped persons, be denied the benefits of, be excluded from participation in, or be subjected to discrimination under any program or activity that receives or benefits from Federal financial assistance from DOE.
