42 C.F.R. § 1008.51 — Exclusivity of OIG advisory opinions.
Citation: us:cfr:42:1008.51
Creator: Office Of Inspector General-Health Care, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-1008.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 1008 (Advisory Opinions By The Oig). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The only method for obtaining a binding advisory opinion regarding any of the subject matters set forth in § 1008.5(a) is through the procedures described in this part. No binding advisory opinion, oral or written, has or may be issued by the OIG regarding the specific matters set forth in § 1008.5(a) except through written opinions issued in accordance with this part.
