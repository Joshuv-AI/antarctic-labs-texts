18 U.S.C. § 2383 — Rebellion or insurrection
Citation: us:usc:18:2383
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section2383
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 115 (Treason, Sedition, And Subversive Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever incites, sets on foot, assists, or engages in any rebellion or insurrection against the authority of the United States or the laws thereof, or gives aid or comfort thereto, shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than ten years, or both; and shall be incapable of holding any office under the United States.
