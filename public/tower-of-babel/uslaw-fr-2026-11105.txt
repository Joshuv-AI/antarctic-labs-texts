DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION

DEPARTMENT OF LABOR


Notice Announcing American History and Civics Education National 
Activities Program Competition; Correction

AGENCY: Department of Education; Department of Labor.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

[[Page 33163]]

SUMMARY: On May 29, 2026, the Department of Education (ED) published in 
the Federal Register a Competition Announcement Notice (CAN) in which 
the Employment and Training Administration at the U.S. Department of 
Labor (DOL) solicited applications in support of the administration of 
the FY 2026 American History and Civics Education National Activities 
(AHC-NA) program, Assistance Listing Number 84.422B, on behalf of ED. 
This notice corrects an error in that CAN.
    The CAN published on May 29, 2026, had two email addresses listed 
for the For Further Information Contact. ED and DOL are correcting the 
FY 2026 AHC-NA CAN by removing the second email address to simplify the 
information contact. All other information in the CAN remains the same.

DATES: Deadline for Transmittal of Applications: July 13, 2026.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Orman Feres. Phone: (202) 453-6921. 
Email: [email protected].
    If you use a telecommunications device for the deaf (TDD) or a text 
telephone (TTY), call the Federal Relay Service (FRS), toll free, at 1-
800-877-8339.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: We are correcting the May 29, 2026, CAN to 
remove the second email address for the information contact as 
described below:

Correction

    In FR Doc. No. 2026-10719, in the Federal Register published on May 
29, 2026 (91 FR 32018), we make the following correction:
    On Page 32018, in the second column, revise the text under FOR 
FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT as follows:
    Orman Feres. Phone: (202) 453-6921. Email: [email protected].
    Program Authority: Section 2233 of the Elementary and Secondary 
Education Act of 1965, as amended (ESEA), 20 U.S.C. 6663.
    Accessible Format: On request to the program contact person listed 
under FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT, individuals with disabilities 
can obtain this document in an accessible format.
    Note: Henry Maklakiewicz signs this notice in furtherance of DOL's 
role in providing support to ED.

Kirsten Baesler,
Assistant Secretary, Office of Elementary and Secondary Education, 
Department of Education.

    In concurrence.
Henry Maklakiewicz,
Assistant Secretary for Employment and Training, Department of Labor.
