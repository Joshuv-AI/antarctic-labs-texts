42 U.S.C. § 5314 — Consultation by Secretary with other Federal departments, etc.
Citation: us:usc:42:5314
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section5314
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 69 (Community Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In carrying out the provisions of this chapter including the issuance of regulations, the Secretary shall consult with other Federal departments and agencies administering Federal grant-in-aid programs.
