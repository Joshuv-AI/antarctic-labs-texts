39 C.F.R. § 957.17 — Public information.
Citation: us:cfr:39:957.17
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-957.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 957 (Debarment And Suspension From Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Postal Service shall maintain for public inspection copies of all findings of fact issued under this Part, and make them available through the Postal Service Web site. The Recorder maintains the complete official record of every proceeding.
