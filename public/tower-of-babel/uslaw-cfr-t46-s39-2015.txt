46 C.F.R. § 39.2015 — Tank barge pressure-vacuum indicating device—B/ALL.
Citation: us:cfr:46:39.2015
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-39.2015
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 39 (Vapor Control Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A fixed pressure-sensing device must be installed as close as practicable to the vessel vapor connection on a tank barge with a vapor collection system. The pressure-sensing device must measure the pressure vacuum in the main vapor collection line and have a pressure indicator located where the cargo transfer is controlled.
