49 U.S.C. § 80108 — Alterations and additions
Citation: us:usc:49:80108
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section80108
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Chapter 801 (Bills Of Lading). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An alteration or addition to a bill of lading after its issuance by a common carrier, without authorization from the carrier in writing or noted on the bill, is void. However, the original terms of the bill are enforceable.
