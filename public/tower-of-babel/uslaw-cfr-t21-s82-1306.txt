21 C.F.R. § 82.1306 — D&C Red No. 6.
Citation: us:cfr:21:82.1306
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-82.1306
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 82 (Listing Of Certified Provisionally Listed Colors And Specifications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The color additive D&C Red No. 6 shall conform in identity and specifications to the requirements of § 74.1306 (a)(1) and (b) of this chapter.
(b) The color additive D&C Red No. 6 may be safely used for coloring drugs such that the combined total of D&C Red No. 6 and D&C Red No. 7 does not exceed 5 milligrams per daily dose of the drug.
