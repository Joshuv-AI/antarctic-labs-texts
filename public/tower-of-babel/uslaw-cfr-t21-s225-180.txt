21 C.F.R. § 225.180 — Labeling.
Citation: us:cfr:21:225.180
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-225.180
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 225 (Current Good Manufacturing Practice For Medicated Feeds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Labels shall be received, handled, and stored in a manner that prevents label mixups and assures that the correct labels are used for the medicated feed. All deliveries of medicated feeds, whether bagged or in bulk, shall be adequately labeled to assure that the feed can be properly used.
