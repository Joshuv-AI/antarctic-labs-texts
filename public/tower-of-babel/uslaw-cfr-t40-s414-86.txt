40 C.F.R. § 414.86 — Pretreatment standards for new sources (PSNS).
Citation: us:cfr:40:414.86
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-414.86
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 414 (Organic Chemicals, Plastics, And Synthetic Fibers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in 40 CFR 403.7 any new source subject to this subpart which introduces pollutants into a publicly owned treatment works must comply with 40 CFR part 403 and achieve discharges in accordance with § 414.111.
