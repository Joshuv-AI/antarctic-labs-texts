18 C.F.R. § 401.107 — Permanent file of requests for Commission records.
Citation: us:cfr:18:401.107
Creator: Delaware River Basin Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-401.107
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 401 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission shall maintain a permanent file of all requests for Commission records and all responses thereto, incuding a list of all records furnished in response to a request. This file is available for public review during working hours.
