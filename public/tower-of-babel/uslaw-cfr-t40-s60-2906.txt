40 C.F.R. § 60.2906 — When must the operator training course be completed?
Citation: us:cfr:40:60.2906
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.2906
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 60 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The operator training course must be completed by the latest of the dates specified in paragraphs (a) through (d) of this section.
(a) Six months after your OSWI unit startup date.
(b) December 29, 2025.
(c) The date before an employee assumes responsibility for operating the OSWI unit or assumes responsibility for supervising the operation of the OSWI unit.
