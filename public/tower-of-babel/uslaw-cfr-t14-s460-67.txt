14 C.F.R. § 460.67 — Training of government astronauts without a safety-critical role.
Citation: us:cfr:14:460.67
Creator: Commercial Space Transportation, Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-460.67
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 460 (Human Space Flight Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An operator must ensure that each government astronaut without a safety-critical role is trained on how to respond to emergency situations, including smoke, fire, loss of cabin pressure, and emergency exit.
