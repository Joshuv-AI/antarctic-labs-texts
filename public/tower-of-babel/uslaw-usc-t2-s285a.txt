2 U.S.C. § 285a — Purpose and policy
Citation: us:usc:2:285a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section285a
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 9A (Office Of Law Revision Counsel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The principal purpose of the Office shall be to develop and keep current an official and positive codification of the laws of the United States. The Office shall maintain impartiality as to issues of legislative policy to be determined by the House.
