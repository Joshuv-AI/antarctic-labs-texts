49 C.F.R. § 198.35 — Grants conditioned on adoption of one-call damage prevention program.
Citation: us:cfr:49:198.35
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-198.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 198 (Regulations For Grants To Aid State Pipeline Safety Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In allocating grants to State agencies under the pipeline safety laws, (49 U.S.C. 60101 et seq.), the Secretary considers whether a State has adopted or is seeking to adopt a one-call damage prevention program in accordance with § 198.37. If a State has not adopted or is not seeking to adopt such program, the State agency may not receive the full reimbursement to which it would otherwise be entitled.
