21 C.F.R. § 1250.62 — Submittal of construction plans.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1250.62
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1250.62
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1250 (Interstate Conveyance Sanitation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Plans for construction or major reconstruction of sanitation facilities at servicing areas shall be submitted to the Commissioner of Food and Drugs for review of the conformity of the proposed facilities with the requirements of this subpart.
