Abstract
. We compute the F F -signature function of the ample cone of any nontrivial ruled surface over ℙ k 1 \mathbb{P}^{1}_{k} where k k is an algebraically closed field of prime characteristic. As an application, we construct a Noetherian F F -finite strongly F F -regular ring of prime characteristic admitting two maximal ideals 𝔫 1 , 𝔫 2 ∈ Spec ⁡ R \mathfrak{n}_{1},\mathfrak{n}_{2}\in\operatorname{Spec}R at which the Hilbert–Kunz multiplicity and F F -signature measure different singularities; that is, e HK ⁡ ( R 𝔫 1 ) < e HK ⁡ ( R 𝔫 2 ) \operatorname{e}_{\operatorname{HK}}(R_{\mathfrak{n}_{1}})<\operatorname{e}_{\operatorname{HK}}(R_{\mathfrak{n}_{2}}) and s ⁡ ( R 𝔫 1 ) < s ⁡ ( R 𝔫 2 ) s(R_{\mathfrak{n}_{1}})<s(R_{\mathfrak{n}_{2}}) . Our calculation of the F F -signature for the Hirzebruch surfaces also corrects an inaccuracy in a preprint by different authors.



1.
Introduction

This note concerns two measures of singularity in prime characteristic: the Hilbert–Kunz multiplicity and the
F
-signature. Let
(R,\mathfrak{m},k)
be a
d
-dimensional
F
-finite Noetherian local
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-algebra. For simplicity in the introduction, we also assume that
R
is a domain with perfect residue field
k=k^{p}
. Recall that the
Hilbert–Kunz multiplicity
of
R
, denoted
\operatorname{e}_{\operatorname{HK}}(R)
, is defined to be the limit

\operatorname{e}_{\operatorname{HK}}(R)=\lim\limits_{e\to\infty}\frac{\ell_{R}(R/\mathfrak{m}^{[p^{e}]})}{p^{ed}}

which was shown to exist in
[
Mon83
]
. The
F
-signature of
R
on the other hand, denoted
s(R)
, asymptotically counts the splittings of
R\hookrightarrow R^{1/p^{e}}
via

s(R)=\lim\limits_{e\to\infty}\frac{\operatorname{frk}_{R}R^{1/p^{e}}}{p^{ed}},

where

\operatorname{frk}_{R}R^{1/p^{e}}=\max\{N\mid\exists R^{1/p^{e}}\twoheadrightarrow R^{\oplus N}\}

is the free rank of the
R
-module
R^{1/p^{e}}
. This latter limit was shown to exist in
[
Tuc12
]
.

Both of these measurements can be thought of as local volumes of a singularity. For example,
\operatorname{e}_{\operatorname{HK}}(R)
and
s(R)
attain their respective minimal or maximal values of one precisely when
R
is regular (see
[
WY00
, Theorem 1.5]
and
[
HL02
]
). An oft-repeated philosophy concerning these invariants is that values closer to one correspond to less severe singularities, a perspective for which there is ample evidence (see e.g.
[
AE08
,
PT18
,
PS20
,
CST21
,
DS22
]
). In the present article we raise a counterpoint to this heuristic, inspired by a question posed to the third named author by T. Polstra regarding whether these invariants ever give contradictory verdicts about when one singularity is milder than another.

Theorem A
.

For every prime integer
p>0
, there exists a Noetherian
F
-finite strongly
F
-regular ring
R
of characteristic
p
with maximal ideals
\mathfrak{n}_{1},\mathfrak{n}_{2}\in\operatorname{Spec}(R)
such that
\operatorname{e}_{\operatorname{HK}}(R_{\mathfrak{n}_{1}})<\operatorname{e}_{\operatorname{HK}}(R_{\mathfrak{n}_{2}})
and
s(R_{\mathfrak{n}_{1}})<s(R_{\mathfrak{n}_{2}})
.

Simply stated, the inequalities above suggest that
R_{\mathfrak{n}_{1}}
is less singular than
R_{\mathfrak{n}_{2}}
according to the Hilbert–Kunz multiplicity, whereas the
F
-signature suggests the opposite. The ring
R
appearing in
A
is given by the tensor product
R_{1}\otimes_{k}R_{2}
where
R_{1}
and
R_{2}
are carefully chosen section rings of the Hirzebruch surface
\mathcal{H}_{a}
with parameter
a
with respect to ample line bundles
\mathcal{L}_{1}
and
\mathcal{L}_{2}
. See
Section

2
for a summary of the toric geometry of
\mathcal{H}_{a}
and for our notational conventions.

The Hilbert–Kunz multiplicity of the section rings of
\mathcal{H}_{a}
has been computed already by Trivedi in
[
Tri16
]
(see
Theorem

2.6
). Values for the
F
-signature were claimed in the preprint
[
HS17
]
, but unfortunately the calculations there contain an error which prevents their usage in our applications. In essence, the formulas of
op. cit.
do not satisfy the transformation rule for
F
-signature under finite maps (see
Remark

2.4
for more details). Therefore, we give a new calculation for the
F
-signature function of the ample cone of
\mathcal{H}_{a}
for
a\geq 2
, while the cases of
a=0
and
a=1
were handled previously in
[
Von12
, Example 4.4.10]
and
[
Lee23
, Appendix A]
respectively. The majority of the paper is dedicated to this calculation in
Section

3
, and our formula (which does satisfy the transformation rule) is summarized below.

Theorem B
.

Let
a\geq 2
and let
\pi:X=\mathcal{H}_{a}\to\mathbb{P}_{k}^{1}
be a ruled surface over
\mathbb{P}^{1}_{k}
where
k
is an algebraically closed field of characteristic
p>0
. Let
D_{1}
be the divisor class of a fiber of
\pi
and
D_{4}
be the divisor class of a section of
\pi
. Let
\mathcal{L}
be an ample line bundle on
X
given by
\mathcal{L}=cD_{1}+dD_{4}
where
c,d\geq 1
. If
R=\bigoplus_{n\in\mathbb{Z}}H^{0}(X,\mathcal{L}^{\otimes n})
then the
F
-signature is given by

s(R)=\begin{cases}\frac{6d^{2}-c^{2}}{6d^{2}(ad+c)}&\text{ if $c\leq d$}\\
\frac{6d^{3}-(2d-c)^{3}}{6cd^{2}(ad+c)}&\text{ if $d\leq c\leq 2d$}\\
\frac{d}{c(ad+c)}&\text{ if $2d\leq c$}.\end{cases}

2.
Preliminaries

2.1.
Properties of the
F
-signature function

In this subsection, we recall how the
F
-signature function is defined on the ample cone of a projective variety and summarize some of its properties. Throughout this subsection we let
X
be a normal projective variety over an algebraically closed field
k
of positive characteristic
p>0
.

Definition 2.1
.

A normal projective variety
X
is said to be
globally
F
-regular
if for any effective Weil divisor
D
on
X
, there exists an integer
e>0
such that map
\mathcal{O}_{X}\to F^{e}_{*}\mathcal{O}_{X}(D)
splits as a map of
\mathcal{O}_{X}
-modules.

Let
\mathcal{L}
be an ample divisor on
X
. Then, we have a section ring
S_{\mathcal{L}}:=\bigoplus_{n\in\mathbb{Z}}H^{0}(X,\mathcal{L}^{\otimes n})
such that
X\cong\mathrm{Proj}(S_{\mathcal{L}})
. By
[
SS10
]
,
X
is globally
F
-regular if and only if
S_{\mathcal{L}}
is strongly
F
-regular. Combined with the equivalence between strong
F
-regularity and positivity of the
F
-signature
[
AL03
, Theorem 0.2]
, we see that
X
is globally
F
-regular if and only if there is an ample divisor
\mathcal{L}
whose section ring has positive
F
-signature. Furthermore, it is proven in
[
SS10
]
that for a globally
F
-regular variety
X
, there is an effective divisor
\Delta
such that
(X,\Delta)
is log-Fano.

Definition 2.2
.

Let
X
be a globally
F
-regular projective variety over
k
and let
\mathcal{L}
be an ample divisor on
X
. Letting
S_{\mathcal{L}}
be the section ring of
\mathcal{L}
as above, the
F
-signature of
\mathcal{L}
is defined to be
s(\mathcal{L}):=s(S_{\mathcal{L}})
.

The
F
-signature function is well-defined on the ample classes and is Lipschitz continuous on the ample cone of
X
. Furthermore, the
F
-signature function extends to
\mathrm{Nef}_{\mathbb{R}}(X)\setminus\{0\}
and is zero if
\mathcal{L}\in\mathrm{Nef}_{\mathbb{R}}(X)
is not big
[
LP24
]
. Additionally, the
F
-signature function enjoys the following transformation rule (see
[
Von12
]
for Veronese subrings and
[
Car22
]
for more general types of extensions).

Theorem 2.3
.

[
Von12
, Theorem 2.6.2]
Let
X
be a projective variety over
k
and
\mathcal{L}
be an ample invertible divisor on
X
. Let
S_{\mathcal{L}}
and
S_{\mathcal{L}^{n}}
denote the section rings with respect to
\mathcal{L}
and
\mathcal{L}^{n}
respectively for
n\in\mathbb{N}
. Then,

s(S_{\mathcal{L}})=n\cdot s(S_{\mathcal{L}^{n}}).

Remark 2.4
.

Theorem

2.3
suggests that if an ample divisor
\mathcal{L}
is a finite sum
a_{1}e_{i_{1}}+\dots+a_{t}e_{i_{t}}
of basis elements of the Néron–Severi group such that each
e_{i_{k}}
is an ample class and the
F
-signature function
s(\mathcal{L})
is a rational function on the coefficients of
e_{i_{k}}
, then
s(\mathcal{L})
must be a homogeneous function of degree
-1
. This demonstrates an issue with the computation of the
F
-signature function on the Hirzebruch surface in
[
HS17
]
(the cases on pages 14 to 16 of
op. cit.
, for instance, do not pass this criterion).
