7 C.F.R. § 3.46 — Offset against tax refunds.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3.46
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3.46
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3 (Debt Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

USDA will take action to effect administrative offset against tax refunds due to debtors under 26 U.S.C. 6402 in accordance with the provisions of 31 U.S.C. 3720A through referral for centralized administrative offset under § 3.44.
