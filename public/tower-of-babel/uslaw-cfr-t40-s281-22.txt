40 C.F.R. § 281.22 — Procedures for adequate enforcement.
Citation: us:cfr:40:281.22
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-281.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 281 (Approval Of State Underground Storage Tank Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A state must submit a description of its compliance monitoring and enforcement procedures, including related state administrative or judicial review procedures.
