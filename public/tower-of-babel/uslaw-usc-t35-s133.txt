35 U.S.C. § 133 — Time for prosecuting application
Citation: us:usc:35:133
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title35-section133
Context: United States Code, Title 35 (Patents); Part II (Patentability Of Inventions And Grant Of Patents); Chapter 12 (Examination Of Application). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Upon failure of the applicant to prosecute the application within six months after any action therein, of which notice has been given or mailed to the applicant, or within such shorter time, not less than thirty days, as fixed by the Director in such action, the application shall be regarded as abandoned by the parties thereto.
