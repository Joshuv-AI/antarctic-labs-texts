33 U.S.C. § 634 — Assistance relating to water supply
Citation: us:usc:33:634
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section634
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 12 (River And Harbor Improvements Generally); Subchapter V (Prosecution Of Work Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may provide assistance to municipalities the water supply of which is adversely affected by construction carried out by the Corps of Engineers.
