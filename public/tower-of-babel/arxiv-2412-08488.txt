Orbital stability of normalized ground states for critical Choquard equation with potential
Jun Wang, Zhaoyang Yin

Abstract
In this paper, we study the existence of ground state standing waves and orbital stability, of prescribed mass, for the nonlinear critical Choquard equation { i ∂ t u + Δ u − V ( x ) u + ( I α ∗ | u | q ) | u | q − 2 u + ( I α ∗ | u | 2 α ∗ ) | u | 2 α ∗ − 2 u = 0 , ( x , t ) ∈ ℝ d × ℝ , u | t = 0 = φ ∈ H 1 ​ ( ℝ d ) , \left\{\begin{array}[]{l}i\partial_{t}u+\Delta u-V(x)u+(I_{\alpha}\ast|u|^{q})|u|^{q-2}u+(I_{\alpha}\ast|u|^{2_{\alpha}^{*}})|u|^{2_{\alpha}^{*}-2}u=0,\ (x,t)\in\mathbb{R}^{d}\times\mathbb{R},\\ \left.u\right|_{t=0}=\varphi\in H^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{d}),\end{array}\right. where I α I_{\alpha} is a Riesz potential of order α ∈ ( 0 , d ) , d ≥ 3 , 2 α ∗ = 2 ​ d − α d − 2 \alpha\in(0,d),\ d\geq 3,\ 2_{\alpha}^{*}=\frac{2d-\alpha}{d-2} is the upper critical exponent due to Hardy-Littlewood-Sobolev inequality, 2 ​ d − α d < q < 2 ​ d − α + 2 d \frac{2d-\alpha}{d}<q<\frac{2d-\alpha+2}{d} . Under appropriate potential conditions, we obtain new Strichartz estimates and construct the new space to get orbital stability of normalized ground state. To our best knowledge, this is the first orbital stability result for this model. Our method is also applicable to other mixed nonlinear equations with potential.

Introduction and main results

In this paper, we consider the following nonlinear critical Choquard equation

i\partial_{t}u+\Delta u-V(x)u+(I_{\alpha}\ast|u|^{q})|u|^{q-2}u+(I_{\alpha}\ast|u|^{2_{\alpha}^{*}})|u|^{2_{\alpha}^{*}-2}u=0,\ (x,t)\in\mathbb{R}^{d}\times\mathbb{R},

where

I_{\alpha}(x)=\frac{\Gamma\left(\frac{d-\alpha}{2}\right)}{\Gamma\left(\frac{\alpha}{2}\right)\pi^{\frac{d}{2}}2^{\alpha}|x|^{d-\alpha}}\triangleq\frac{\bar{C}}{|x|^{d-\alpha}}

If (

[

Our idea is to generalize its results to critical Choquard equation with potential. However, there are two fundamental difficulties. On the one hand, due to the emergence of the potential function, the original operator

To overcome the second difficulty, we use a new equivalent norm to construct space and distance, see Lemma
3.3

3.3
.

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{s^{2}}{2}\left(1+\|V\|_{\frac{d}{2}}\mathcal{S}^{-1}\right)\|\nabla u\|_{2}^{2}-\frac{s^{d(q-2)+\alpha}}{2q}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{d}}(I_{\alpha}\ast|u_{s}|^{q})|u_{s}|^{q}dx

\displaystyle-\frac{s^{2\cdot 2_{\alpha}^{*}}}{2\cdot 2_{\alpha}^{*}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{d}}(I_{\alpha}\ast|u_{s}|^{2_{\alpha}^{*}})|u_{s}|^{2_{\alpha}^{*}}dx.

Note that

∎

Lemma 2.5

\mathrm{(i)}

\mathrm{(ii)}
Let

\displaystyle I\left(y_{n}\right)-I\left(u_{n}\right)

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{a}{a_{n}}-1\right)\left\|\nabla u_{n}\right\|_{2}^{2}-\frac{1}{2q}\left[\left(\frac{a}{a_{n}}\right)^{q}-1\right]\int_{\mathbb{R}^{d}}(I_{\alpha}\ast|u_{n}|^{q})|u_{n}|^{q}dx

(ii) Fixed

\displaystyle\frac{a-\alpha}{a}m(a)+\frac{\alpha}{a}m(a)

Lemma 2.6
.

Let
\left(v_{n}\right)\subset\mathbf{B}_{\rho_{0}}

Indeed, using the Sobolev inequality, we have

\displaystyle I\left(v_{n}\right)

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\|\nabla v_{n}\|_{2}^{2}+\frac{1}{2}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{d}}V(x)v_{n}^{2}dx-\frac{1}{2\cdot 2_{\alpha}^{*}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{d}}(I_{\alpha}\ast|v_{n}|^{2_{\alpha}^{*}})|v_{n}|^{2_{\alpha}^{*}}dx+o_{n}(1)

Lemma 2.7

For any

Lemma 2.8

For any

Proof.

It follows from Lemma

\displaystyle\left\|u_{n}\right\|_{2}^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left\|u_{n}\left(x-y_{n}\right)\right\|_{2}^{2}

\displaystyle\left\|u_{n}\left(x-y_{n}\right)-u_{a}(x)\right\|_{2}^{2}+\left\|u_{a}\right\|_{2}^{2}+o_{n}(1)

By a similar argument,

Now we remember that

Since

by weak limit, we have that

and hence

3
Orbital stability

In this section we focus on the local existence of solutions to the following Cauchy problem

\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}i\partial_{t}u+\Delta u-V(x)u+(I_{\alpha}\ast|u|^{q})|u|^{q-2}u+(I_{\alpha}\ast|u|^{2_{\alpha}^{*}})|u|^{2_{\alpha}^{*}-2}u=0,\\
\left.u\right|_{t=0}=\varphi\in H^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{d}).\end{array}\right.

(3.1)

Next we give the notion of integral equation associated with (

Definition 3.1
.

If
d\geq 3

\frac{2}{m}+\frac{d}{n}=\frac{d}{2},\ p,r\in[2,\infty].

Note that, we will work with two particular admissible pairs

\left(m_{1},n_{1}\right):=\left(2q,\frac{2dq}{dq-2}\right)

Now, we introduce the spaces

Proposition 3.2

Proposition 3.3

1<s,r,q<\infty
be such that
\frac{1}{q}+\frac{1}{r}+\frac{1}{s}+\frac{\alpha}{d}=2
. Then,

\left\|\left(I_{\alpha}*f\right)g\right\|_{r^{\prime}}\leq C(d,s,\alpha)\|f\|_{s}\|g\|_{q},\quad\forall f\in L^{s}(\mathbb{R}^{d}),\forall g\in L^{q}(\mathbb{R}^{d}).

Proof.

Using Hardy-Littlewood-Sobolev inequality for
\frac{1}{t}+\frac{1}{s}+\frac{\alpha}{d}=2
,

\displaystyle\left\|\left(I_{\alpha}*f\right)g\right\|_{r^{\prime}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sup_{\|v\|_{r}=1}\left|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{d}\times\mathbb{R}^{d}}I_{\alpha}(x-y)f(y)g(x)v(x)dydx\right|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle C_{\alpha}\sup_{\|v\|_{r}=1}\left|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{d}\times\mathbb{R}^{d}}\frac{f(y)g(x)v(x)}{|x-y|^{d-\alpha}}dydx\right|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C(d,s,\alpha)\|f\|_{s}\|gv\|_{t}.

By Hölder inequality, we have

\left\|\left(I_{\alpha}*f\right)g\right\|_{r^{\prime}}\leq C(d,s,\alpha)\|f\|_{s}\|gv\|_{t}\leq C(d,s,\alpha)\|f\|_{s}\|g\|_{q}\|v\|_{r}\leq C(d,s,\alpha)\|f\|_{s}\|g\|_{q}

for
\frac{1}{q}+\frac{1}{r}+\frac{1}{s}+\frac{\alpha}{d}=2
.
∎
