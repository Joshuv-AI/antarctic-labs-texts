16 U.S.C. § 1335 — Recovery rights
Citation: us:usc:16:1335
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section1335
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 30 (Wild Horses And Burros: Protection, Management, And Control). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A person claiming ownership of a horse or burro on the public lands shall be entitled to recover it only if recovery is permissible under the branding and estray laws of the State in which the animal is found.
