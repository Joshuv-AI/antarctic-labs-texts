14 C.F.R. § 380.63 — Objections to registration applications.
Citation: us:cfr:14:380.63
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-380.63
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 380 (Public Charters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person objecting to the registration application of a foreign charter operator or to a proposed change in the name or ownership of that operator shall file an objection with the Office of International Aviation, U.S. Air Carrier Licensing/Special Authorities Division, within 28 days after the Department receives the properly completed registration application.
