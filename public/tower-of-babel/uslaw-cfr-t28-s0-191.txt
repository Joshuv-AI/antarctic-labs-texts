28 C.F.R. § 0.191 — Changes which affect the overall structure of the Department.
Citation: us:cfr:28:0.191
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-0.191
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 0 (Organization Of The Department Of Justice). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Changes to the overall structure of the Department include: The establishment, merger or abolishment of Offices, Boards, Divisions, and Bureaus; changes in reporting lines of Offices, Boards, Divisions and Bureaus to the Department; and transfers of major functions between or among Offices, Boards, Divisions and Bureaus.
