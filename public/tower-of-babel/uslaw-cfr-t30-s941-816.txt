30 C.F.R. § 941.816 — Performance standards—surface mining activities.
Citation: us:cfr:30:941.816
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-941.816
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 941 (South Dakota). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Part 816 of this chapter, Permanent Program Performance Standards—Surface Mining Activities, shall apply to any person who conducts surface coal mining and reclamation operations.
