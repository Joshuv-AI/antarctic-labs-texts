12 U.S.C. § 1705 — Allocation of funds
Citation: us:usc:12:1705
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section1705
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 13 (National Housing); Subchapter I (Housing Renovation And Modernization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purposes of carrying out the provisions of this subchapter and subchapters II and III, the President, in his discretion, is authorized to provide such funds or any portion thereof by allotment to the Secretary from any funds that are available, or may hereafter be made available, to the President for emergency purposes.
