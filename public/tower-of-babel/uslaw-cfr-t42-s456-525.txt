42 C.F.R. § 456.525 — Request for renewal of variance.
Citation: us:cfr:42:456.525
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-456.525
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 456 (Utilization Control). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The agency must submit a request for renewal of a variance to the Administrator at least 30 days before the variance expires.
(b) The renewal request must contain the information required under § 456.522.
(c) The renewal request must show, to the Administrator's satisfaction, that the remote facility continues to meet the requirements of §§ 456.521 through 456.523.
