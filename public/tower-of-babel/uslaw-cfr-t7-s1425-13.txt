7 C.F.R. § 1425.13 — Uniform marketing agreement.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1425.13
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1425.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1425 (Cooperative Marketing Associations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A CMA must enter into a uniform marketing agreement with each member who delivers a commodity to a loan pool.
(b) The identification number used by the member to report acreage on applicable farms to FSA must appear on the marketing agreement.
