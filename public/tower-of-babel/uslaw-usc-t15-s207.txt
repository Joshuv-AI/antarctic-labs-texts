15 U.S.C. § 207 — Preparation of standards by Secretary of Commerce
Citation: us:usc:15:207
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section207
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 6 (Weights And Measures And Standard Time); Subchapter III (Standard Gauge For Iron And Steel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Commerce is authorized and required to prepare suitable standards in accordance with section 206 of this title.
