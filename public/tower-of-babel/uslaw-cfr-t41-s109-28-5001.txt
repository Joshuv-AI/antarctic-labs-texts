41 C.F.R. § 109-28.5001 — -28.5001 Definition.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 109-28 (28—Storage And Distribution). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Equipment held for future projects means items being retained, based on approved justifications, for a known future use, or for a potential use in planned projects.
