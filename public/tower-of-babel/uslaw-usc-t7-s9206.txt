7 U.S.C. § 9206 — Effect on other authorities
Citation: us:usc:7:9206
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section9206
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 116 (National Bio And Agro-Defense Facility). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter affects the authority of the Secretary of Agriculture or the Secretary of Homeland Security under any other provision of law or program relating to the protection of food supplies, agriculture, or public health.
