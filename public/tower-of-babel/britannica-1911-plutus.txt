PLUTUS, in Greek mythology, son of Iasion and Demeter,
the personification of wealth (πλοῦτος). According to Aristophanes,
he was blinded by Zeus because he distributed his gifts
without regard to merit. At Thebes there was a statue of Fortune
holding the child Plutus in her arms; at Athens he was
similarly represented in the arms of Peace; at Thespiae he was
represented standing beside Athena the Worker. Elsewhere
he was represented as a boy with a cornu copiae. He is the subject
of one of the extant comedies of Aristophanes, the Plutus.
