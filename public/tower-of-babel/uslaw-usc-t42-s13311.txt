42 U.S.C. § 13311 — Purposes
Citation: us:usc:42:13311
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section13311
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 134 (Energy Policy); Subchapter V (Renewable Energy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purposes of this subchapter are to promote—
(1)
increases in the production and utilization of energy from renewable energy resources;
(2)
further advances of renewable energy technologies; and
(3)
exports of United States renewable energy technologies and services.
