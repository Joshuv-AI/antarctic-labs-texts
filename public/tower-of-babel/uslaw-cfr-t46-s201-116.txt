46 C.F.R. § 201.116 — Time of filing.
Citation: us:cfr:46:201.116
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-201.116
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 201 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any depositions to be offered in evidence shall be filed with the presiding officer not later than the close of the offering party's presentation.
