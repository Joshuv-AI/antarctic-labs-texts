46 C.F.R. § 2.01-20 — -20 Suspension or revocation of certificates of inspection.
Citation: us:cfr:46:2.01-20
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-2.01-20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 2 (Vessel Inspections). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Under the authority if 46 U.S.C. 3313 and 46 U.S.C. 3710, a certificate of inspection issued to a vessel may be suspended or revoked if a vessel is found not to comply with the terms of its certificate or fails to meet a standard required by this chapter.
