21 U.S.C. § 391 — Separability clause
Citation: us:usc:21:391
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section391
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 9 (Federal Food, Drug, And Cosmetic Act); Subchapter X (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this chapter is declared unconstitutional, or the applicability thereof to any person or circumstances is held invalid, the constitutionality of the remainder of the chapter and the applicability thereof to other persons and circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
