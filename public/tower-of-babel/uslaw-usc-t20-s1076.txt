20 U.S.C. § 1076 — Sources of funds
Citation: us:usc:20:1076
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1076
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 28 (Higher Education Resources And Student Assistance); Subchapter IV (Student Assistance); Part B (Federal Family Education Loan Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Loans made by eligible lenders in accordance with this part shall be insurable by the Secretary whether made from funds fully owned by the lender or from funds held by the lender in a trust or similar capacity and available for such loans.
