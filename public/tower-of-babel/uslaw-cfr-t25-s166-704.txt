25 C.F.R. § 166.704 — What will a written notice of a permit violation contain?
Citation: us:cfr:25:166.704
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-166.704
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 166 (Grazing Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The written notice of a permit violation will provide the permittee with ten days from the receipt of the written notice to:
(a) Cure the permit violation and notify us that the violation is cured.
(b) Explain why we should not cancel the permit; or
(c) Request in writing additional time to complete corrective actions. If additional time is granted, we may require that certain corrective actions be taken immediately.
