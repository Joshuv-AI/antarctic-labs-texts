20 C.F.R. § 652.301 — What are wage records for purposes of the Wagner-Peyser Act?
Citation: us:cfr:20:652.301
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-652.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 652 (Establishment And Functioning Of State Employment Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Wage records, for purposes of the Wagner-Peyser Act, are records that contain “wage information” as defined in § 603.2(k) of this chapter. In this part, “State wage records” refers to wage records produced or maintained by a State.
