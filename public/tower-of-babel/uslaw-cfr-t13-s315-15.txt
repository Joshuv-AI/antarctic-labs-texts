13 C.F.R. § 315.15 — Affirmative findings.
Citation: us:cfr:13:315.15
Creator: Economic Development Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-315.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 315 (Trade Adjustment Assistance For Firms). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever the International Trade Commission makes an affirmative finding under section 202(b) of the Trade Act (19 U.S.C. 2252) that increased imports are a substantial cause of serious injury or threat thereof with respect to an industry, EDA will notify the TAACs and provide expedited review of petitions and Adjustment Proposals from Firms within the specified industry.
