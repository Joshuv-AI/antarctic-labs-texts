48 C.F.R. § 1842.7003 — 1842.7003 Emergency medical services and evacuation.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1842.7003
Creator: Chapter 18—National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1842.7003
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1842 (Contract Administration And Audit Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer must insert the clause at 1852.242-78, Emergency Medical Services and Evacuation, in all solicitations and contracts when employees of the contractor are required to travel outside the United States or to remote locations in the United States.
