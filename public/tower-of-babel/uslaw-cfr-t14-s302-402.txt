14 C.F.R. § 302.402 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:14:302.402
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-302.402
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 302 (Rules Of Practice In Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Assistant General Counsel, when used in this subpart, refers to the Assistant General Counsel for Aviation Enforcement and Proceedings.
Complainant refers to the person filing a complaint.
Parties, when used in this subpart, include the Office of the Assistant General Counsel, the respondent, the complainant, and any other person permitted to intervene under § 302.20.
Respondent refers to the person against whom a complaint is filed.
