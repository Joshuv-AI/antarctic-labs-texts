30 C.F.R. § 203.73 — How do suspension volumes apply to natural gas?
Citation: us:cfr:30:203.73
Creator: Bureau Of Safety And Environmental Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-203.73
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 203 (Relief Or Reduction In Royalty Rates). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must measure natural gas production under the royalty-suspension volume as follows: 5.62 thousand cubic feet of natural gas, measured in accordance with 30 CFR part 250, subpart L, equals one barrel of oil equivalent.
