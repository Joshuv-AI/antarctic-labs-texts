9 C.F.R. § 559.1 — Fish and other articles subject to administrative detention.
Citation: us:cfr:9:559.1
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-559.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 559 (Detention, Seizure, Condemnation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of 9 CFR 329.1 through 329.5 governing the administrative detention of carcasses, parts, meat, and meat food products of livestock apply also with respect to the carcasses, parts, and products of fish.
