34 U.S.C. § 11275 — Restrictions on disclosure and transfer
Citation: us:usc:34:11275
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section11275
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 111 (Juvenile Justice And Delinquency Prevention); Subchapter III (Runaway And Homeless Youth); Part F (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Records containing the identity of individual youths pursuant to this chapter may under no circumstances be disclosed or transferred to any individual or to any public or private agency.
