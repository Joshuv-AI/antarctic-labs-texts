Authors: Tianrui Li, Jinmao Xu

ABSTRACT
This paper addresses the non-fragile reliable event-triggered control problem for networked switched systems subject to time-varying delays and actuator failures, with a focus on achieving mixed \documentclass[12pt]{minimal} \usepackage{amsmath} \usepackage{wasysym} \usepackage{amsfonts} \usepackage{amssymb} \usepackage{amsbsy} \usepackage{mathrsfs} \usepackage{upgreek} \setlength{\oddsidemargin}{-69pt} \begin{document}$$H_{\infty }$$\end{document} and passivity performance. Specifically, an event-triggered transmission protocol is adopted, under which the control input is updated piecewise in accordance with the switching intervals. Based on this mechanism, a closed-loop state-feedback switched system is formulated. By exploiting multiple Lyapunov-Krasovskii functionals, a set of sufficient criteria is derived to guarantee the exponential stability of the resultant closed-loop system. Furthermore, it is strictly proved that the system satisfies a prescribed weighted mixed \documentclass[12pt]{minimal} \usepackage{amsmath} \usepackage{wasysym} \usepackage{amsfonts} \usepackage{amssymb} \usepackage{amsbsy} \usepackage{mathrsfs} \usepackage{upgreek} \setlength{\oddsidemargin}{-69pt} \begin{document}$$H_{\infty }$$\end{document}/passivity performance index. Finally, a numerical example is provided to demonstrate the effectiveness and applicability of the proposed control scheme.

Introduction

With the ever-increasing demands of practical systems for robustness performance, switched systems have emerged as a crucial research direction in control theory. Owing to their prominent merits of high flexibility and easy maintainability, switched systems have been widely adopted in various modern engineering systems and technical fields, including robotic systems1, biochemical systems2, and intelligent transportation systems3, aero-engine dynamic models4, as well as the relevant references cited therein. Over the past few decades, switched systems have attracted widespread research attention worldwide, and a wealth of valuable theoretical and application results have been achieved by scholars. For instance, the piecewise Lyapunov functionals are constructed in Yang et al.5, where the stability analysis and performance synthesis problems for switched systems are thoroughly investigated. In6, the nonlinear switched stochastic systems are studied , with the matched and mismatched interval constraints between controllers and subsystems taken into account separately. Wang7 addresses the analysis and synthesis problems of continuous-time and discrete-time switched systems with saturated control inputs, and several novel stability criteria are derived by resorting to the multiple Lyapunov-Krasovskii functionals approach and linear matrix inequality technique.

In practical networked switched systems, actuator failures are almost inevitable due to constrained communication bandwidth and harsh operating conditions. Specifically, the complex coupling between arbitrary subsystem switching and unexpected actuator degradation can easily lead to severe performance deterioration or even system instability6–9, which highlights the urgent necessity of investigating reliable fault-tolerant mechanisms. Against this background, traditional controllers often exhibit extreme fragility to minor implementation uncertainties, thereby threatening the closed-loop stability. Although the authors in10 explored the non-fragile reliable control for switched systems, the comprehensive disturbance attenuation performance was unfortunately overlooked. Furthermore, when subjected to complex external environments, relying solely on a single \documentclass[12pt]{minimal} \usepackage{amsmath} \usepackage{wasysym} \usepackage{amsfonts} \usepackage{amssymb} \usepackage{amsbsy} \usepackage{mathrsfs} \usepackage{upgreek} \setlength{\oddsidemargin}{-69pt} \begin{document}$$H_{\infty }$$\end{document} or passivity control strategy is often inadequate to meet stringent engineering requirements. To be specific, integrating \documentclass[12pt]{minimal} \usepackage{amsmath} \usepackage{wasysym} \usepackage{amsfonts} \usepackage{amssymb} \usepackage{amsbsy} \usepackage{mathrsfs} \usepackage{upgreek} \setlength{\oddsidemargin}{-69pt} \begin{document}$$H_{\infty }$$\end{document} performance with passivity theory not only attenuates external energy-bounded disturbances effectively but also captures the internal energy dissipation characteristics of the system, thereby yielding a more robust overall performance. Consequently, it is of profound significance to address the non-fragile reliable mixed \documentclass[12pt]{minimal} \usepackage{amsmath} \usepackage{wasysym} \usepackage{amsfonts} \usepackage{amssymb} \usepackage{amsbsy} \usepackage{mathrsfs} \usepackage{upgreek} \setlength{\oddsidemargin}{-69pt} \begin{document}$$H_{\infty }$$\end{document} and passivity control problem for networked switched systems subject to actuator failures.

For network-based data transmission, a communication channel is established among sensors, controllers, and actuators11. Nevertheless, it should be pointed out that periodic data sampling and transmission tend to result in unnecessary resource waste12,13. To effectively alleviate communication burdens, various advanced event-triggered schemes have been recently developed. For instance, a deep-Q-learning-based proportional-integral event-triggered fuzzy output control strategy is proposed for variable speed AGVs in14, while an improved dynamic memory-event-triggered scheme is designed for T-S fuzzy systems against actuator attacks in15. Moreover, the event-triggered \documentclass[12pt]{minimal} \usepackage{amsmath} \usepackage{wasysym} \usepackage{amsfonts} \usepackage{amssymb} \usepackage{amsbsy} \usepackage{mathrsfs} \usepackage{upgreek} \setlength{\oddsidemargin}{-69pt} \begin{document}$$H_\infty$$\end{document} control problem for networked control systems with distributed transmission delay is thoroughly discussed in12. In the context of networked switched systems, the problem of networked switched linear systems under event-triggered control is investigated in16. Following this application scenario, the study in17 makes full use of the combined advantages of periodic sampled-data control and event-triggered control techniques. In general, state-dependent switching laws cannot be implemented when state information is unavailable18. In addition, the existence of delay and exogenous disturbance could lead to performance degradation and system instability. In recent years, switched systems subject to time-varying delay and actuator failures have also been the subject of extensive research. For instance, under asynchronous switching, the nonlinear stochastic switched system is investigated in19. In the case of actuator failure, Li et al.20 address the problem of switched systems with unknown parameter. In particular, some delay-dependent exponential stability conditions are obtained in21. However, the aforementioned methods predominantly focus on ideal system environments without accounting for the inevitable occurrence of actuator failures and network-induced constraints. Furthermore, the simultaneous synthesis of mixed \documentclass[12pt]{minimal} \usepackage{amsmath} \usepackage{wasysym} \usepackage{amsfonts} \usepackage{amssymb} \usepackage{amsbsy} \usepackage{mathrsfs} \usepackage{upgreek} \setlength{\oddsidemargin}{-69pt} \begin{document}$$H_\infty$$\end{document} and passivity control within an event-triggered framework for faulty switched systems remains largely unexplored, which constitutes the primary motivation of this paper.

Remark 1

In practical engineering systems, controllers are often subject to uncertainties such as gain variations or implementation errors, which can render them fragile and lead to system instability or performance degradation. Existing methods, such as those in3, typically assume ideal controllers without considering such non-fragile reliability. In contrast, this paper addresses this gap by designing a non- fragile reliable controller, which ensures the stability and prescribed performance level of switched systems even in the presence of controller uncertainties. This distinguishes our work from prior studies and highlights its practical significance in real applications.\documentclass[12pt]{minimal} \usepackage{amsmath} \usepackage{wasysym} \usepackage{amsfonts} \usepackage{amssymb} \usepackage{amsbsy} \usepackage{mathrsfs} \usepackage{upgreek} \setlength{\oddsidemargin}{-69pt} \begin{document}$$\square$$\end{document}

Remark 2

Different from the constant-threshold event-triggered mechanism adopted in22, which applies an identical threshold to all subsystems regardless of their specific modes, the proposed condition in (5) is designed to be mode-dependent. The conventional constant-threshold scheme lacks adaptability to system dynamics and may lead to unnecessary waste of sampling resources. In contrast, the introduced mode-dependent event-triggered mechanism adaptively determines transmission instants based on the current system mode, thereby significantly improving communication resource utilization. Conclusions

This work has conducted an in-depth investigation into the non-fragile reliable mixed \documentclass[12pt]{minimal} \usepackage{amsmath} \usepackage{wasysym} \usepackage{amsfonts} \usepackage{amssymb} \usepackage{amsbsy} \usepackage{mathrsfs} \usepackage{upgreek} \setlength{\oddsidemargin}{-69pt} \begin{document}$$H_\infty$$\end{document} and passive event-triggered control problem for switched systems with time-varying delays and actuator failures. A piecewise non-fragile reliable control input has been designed for the concerned systems by introducing the event-triggered transmission mechanism, on the basis of which a closed-loop state feedback model of the switched system has been further constructed. On the foundation of the multiple Lyapunov functions method and average dwell time approach, a series of sufficient conditions have been derived to ensure that the switched systems with actuator failures and time-varying delays achieve exponential stability, while satisfying the prescribed weighted mixed \documentclass[12pt]{minimal} \usepackage{amsmath} \usepackage{wasysym} \usepackage{amsfonts} \usepackage{amssymb} \usepackage{amsbsy} \usepackage{mathrsfs} \usepackage{upgreek} \setlength{\oddsidemargin}{-69pt} \begin{document}$$H_\infty$$\end{document} and passive performance index at the same time. Finally, a numerical simulation example has been carried out to verify the feasibility and effectiveness of the control strategy put forward in this study. Moreover, it is worth noting that communication delays in practical networked systems are typically stochastic and follow specific probability distributions. Future research directions could involve extending the proposed framework to handle such stochastic delays, for instance, by incorporating techniques from the synchronization of delayed fuzzy neural networks with probabilistic communication delay or developing a delay-kernel-dependent approach for saturated control of linear systems with mixed delays.
