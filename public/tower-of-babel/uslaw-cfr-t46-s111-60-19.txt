46 C.F.R. § 111.60-19 — -19 Cable splices.
Citation: us:cfr:46:111.60-19
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-111.60-19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 111 (Electric Systems—General Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A cable must not be spliced in a hazardous location, except in intrinsically safe systems.
(b) Each cable splice must be made in accordance with Section 6.11 of IEEE 45.8-2016 (incorporated by reference; see § 110.10-1 of this subchapter).
