14 C.F.R. § 5.95 — SMS documentation.
Citation: us:cfr:14:5.95
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-5.95
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 5 (Safety Management Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person required to have an SMS under this part must develop and maintain the following SMS documentation:
(a) Safety policy.
(b) SMS processes and procedures.
