5 C.F.R. § 734.206 — Participation in elections.
Citation: us:cfr:5:734.206
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-734.206
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 734 (Political Activities Of Federal Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee may:
(a) Register and vote in any election;
(b) Act as recorder, watcher, challenger, or similar officer at polling places;
(c) Serve as an election judge or clerk, or in a similar position; and
(d) Drive voters to polling places for a partisan political candidate, partisan political group, or political party.
