33 C.F.R. § 3.55-1 — -1 USCG Southwest District.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 3 (Coast Guard Areas, Districts, Sectors, Marine Inspection Zones, And Captain Of The Port Zones). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The District Office is in Alameda, California.
(b) The USCG Southwest District is comprised of: Arizona; Utah; Nevada; California; and the ocean area bounded by a line from the California-Oregon state line westerly to 40° N. latitude, 150° W. longitude; thence southeasterly to 5° S. latitude, 110° W. longitude; thence northeasterly to the border between Guatemala and Mexico on the Pacific Coast (14°38′ N. latitude, 92°19′ W. longitude).
