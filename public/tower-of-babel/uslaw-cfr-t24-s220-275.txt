24 C.F.R. § 220.275 — Method of paying insurance benefits.
Citation: us:cfr:24:220.275
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-220.275
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 220 (Mortgage Insurance And Insured Improvement Loans For Urban Renewal And Concentrated Development Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the application for insurance benefits is acceptable to the Commissioner, all of the insurance claim shall be paid in cash unless the mortgagee files a written request with the application for payment in debentures. If such a request is made, all of the claim shall be paid by issuing debentures and by making a cash payment adjusting any differences between the total amount of the claim and the amount of the debentures issued.
