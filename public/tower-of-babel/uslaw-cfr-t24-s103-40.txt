24 C.F.R. § 103.40 — Can I change my complaint after it is filed?
Citation: us:cfr:24:103.40
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Equal Opportunity, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-103.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 103 (Fair Housing—Complaint Processing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Yes, you may change your fair housing complaint:
(1) At any time to add or remove people according to the law and the facts; or
(2) To correct other items, such as to add additional information found during the investigation of the complaint.
(b) You must approve any change to your complaint; we will consider the changes made as of the date of your original complaint.
