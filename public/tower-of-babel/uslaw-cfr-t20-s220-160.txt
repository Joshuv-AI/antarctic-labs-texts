20 C.F.R. § 220.160 — How work for a railroad employer affects a disability annuity.
Citation: us:cfr:20:220.160
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-220.160
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 220 (Determining Disability). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A disability annuity is not payable and the annuity must be returned for any month in which the disabled annuitant works for an employer as defined in part 202 of this chapter.
