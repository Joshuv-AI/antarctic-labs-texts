5 C.F.R. § 847.911 — Is an employee who elects to credit NAFI service to qualify for an immediate FERS retirement under subpart H of this part eligible for an FERS annuity supplement under 5 CFR 842 subpart E?
Citation: us:cfr:5:847.911
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-847.911
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 847 (Elections Of Retirement Coverage By Current And Former Employees Of Nonappropriated Fund Instrumentalities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An FERS Annuity Supplement is not payable to a retiree who elects to credit NAFI service under subpart H of this part.
