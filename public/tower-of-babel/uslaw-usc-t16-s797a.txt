16 U.S.C. § 797a — Congressional authorization for permits, licenses, leases, or authorizations for dams, conduits, reservoirs, etc., within national parks or monuments
Citation: us:usc:16:797a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section797a
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 12 (Federal Regulation And Development Of Power); Subchapter I (Regulation Of The Development Of Water Power And Resources). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On and after March 3, 1921, no permit, license, lease, or authorization for dams, conduits, reservoirs, power houses, transmission lines, or other works for storage or carriage of water, or for the development, transmission, or utilization of power within the limits as constituted, March 3, 1921, of any national park or national monument shall be granted or made without specific authority of Congress.
