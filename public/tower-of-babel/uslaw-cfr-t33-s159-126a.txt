33 C.F.R. § 159.126a — a Suspended solids test: Type II devices.
Citation: us:cfr:33:159.126a
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-159.126a
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 159 (Marine Sanitation Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

During the sewage processing test (§ 159.121) 40 effluent samples must be taken at the same time as samples are taken for § 159.126 and they must be analyzed for total suspended solids in accordance with 40 CFR part 136. The arithmetic mean of the total suspended solids in 38 of 40 of these samples must be less than or equal to 150 milligrams per liter.
