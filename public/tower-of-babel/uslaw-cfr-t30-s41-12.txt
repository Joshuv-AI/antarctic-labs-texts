30 C.F.R. § 41.12 — Changes; notification by operator.
Citation: us:cfr:30:41.12
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-41.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 41 (Notification Of Legal Identity). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within 30 days after the occurrence of any change in the information required by § 41.11, the operator of a coal or other mine shall, in writing, notify the appropriate district manager of the Mine Safety and Health Administration in the district in which the mine is located of such change.
