30 C.F.R. § 1227.107 — When will the ONRR Director decide whether to approve a State's delegation proposal?
Citation: us:cfr:30:1227.107
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1227.107
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1227 (Delegation To States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The ONRR Director will decide whether to approve your delegation proposal within 90 days after your delegation proposal is considered complete under § 1227.104. ONRR may extend the 90-day period with your written consent.
