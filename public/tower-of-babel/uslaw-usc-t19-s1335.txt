19 U.S.C. § 1335 — Rules and regulations
Citation: us:usc:19:1335
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 4 (Tariff Act Of 1930); Part II (United States International Trade Commission). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The commission is authorized to adopt such reasonable procedures and rules and regulations as it deems necessary to carry out its functions and duties.
