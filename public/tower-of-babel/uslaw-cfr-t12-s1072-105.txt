12 C.F.R. § 1072.105 — Notice.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1072.105
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1072.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1072 (Enforcement Of Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Disability In Programs And Activities Conducted By The Bureau Of Consumer Financial Protection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Bureau shall make available to all Bureau employees, applicants, participants, beneficiaries, and other interested persons information regarding the provisions of this part and its applicability to the programs or activities conducted by the Bureau in a manner that apprises them of the protections against discrimination provided by § 504 and this regulation.
