41 C.F.R. § 105-74.635 — -74.635 Drug-free workplace.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-74.635
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-74.635
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-74 (74—Governmentwide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Drug-free workplace means a site for the performance of work done in connection with a specific award at which employees of the recipient are prohibited from engaging in the unlawful manufacture, distribution, dispensing, possession, or use of a controlled substance.
