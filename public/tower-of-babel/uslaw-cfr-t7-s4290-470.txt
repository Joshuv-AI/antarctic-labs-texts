7 C.F.R. § 4290.470 — Prior approval of merger, consolidation, or reorganization of RBIC.
Citation: us:cfr:7:4290.470
Creator: Rural Business-Cooperative Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-4290.470
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 4290 (Rural Business Investment Company (“Rbic”) Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may not merge, consolidate, change form of organization (corporation, limited liability company, or limited partnership) or reorganize without the Agency's prior written approval. Any such merger, consolidation, or change of form is subject to § 4290.440.
