31 C.F.R. § 15.737-24 — -24 Proposed findings and conclusions.
Citation: us:cfr:31:15.737-24
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-15.737-24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 15 (Post Employment Conflict Of Interest). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except in cases where the respondent has failed to answer the complaint or where a party has failed to appear at the hearing, the Administrative Law Judge prior to making his/her decision, shall afford the parties a reasonable opportunity to submit proposed findings and conclusions and supporting reasons therefor.
