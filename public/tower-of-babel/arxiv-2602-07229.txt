Abstract
The 3 3 -colorability problem is a well-known NP-complete problem and it remains NP-complete for ( c ​ l ​ a ​ w , d ​ i ​ a ​ m ​ o ​ n ​ d , K 4 ) (claw,diamond,K_{4}) -free graphs. Recently, 3 3 -colorability has been also considered for ( c ​ l ​ a ​ w , N 1 , 1 , 1 ) (claw,N_{1,1,1}) -free graphs. Here, a generalised net N i , j , k N_{i,j,k} is the graph obtained by identifying each vertex of a triangle with an endvertex of one of three vetex-disjont paths of lengths i , j , k i,j,k . We study the class of ( c ​ l ​ a ​ w , d ​ i ​ a ​ m ​ o ​ n ​ d , N i , j , k ) (claw,diamond,N_{i,j,k}) -free graphs for ( i , j , k ) ∈ { ( 1 , 1 , 3 ) , (i,j,k)\in\{(1,1,3), ( 1 , 2 , 2 ) , (1,2,2), ( 2 , 2 , 2 ) } (2,2,2)\} . We show that these graphs are 3 3 -colorable or contain a K 4 K_{4} or belong to some well-defined class of non 3 3 -colorable graphs. Moreover, we prove that there are only finitely many non 3 3 -colorable N 1 , 2 , k N_{1,2,k} -free graphs for any k ≥ 2 k\geq 2 , but there exist infinitely many non 3 3 -colorable N i , j , k N_{i,j,k} -free graphs for any 2 ≤ i ≤ j ≤ k . 2\leq i\leq j\leq k.
