10 U.S.C. § 2199 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:10:2199
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2199
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training And Education); Chapter 111 (Support Of Science, Mathematics, And Engineering Education). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter:
(1)
The term “defense laboratory” means a laboratory operated by the Department of Defense or owned by the Department of Defense and operated by a contractor or a facility of a Defense Agency at which research and development activities are conducted.
(2)
The term “institution of higher education” has the meaning given such term in section 101 of the Higher Education Act of 1965.
(3)
The term “regional center for the transfer of manufacturing technology” means a manufacturing extension center for the transfer of manufacturing technology and best business practices referred to in section 25(b) of the National Institute of Standards and Technology Act (15 U.S.C. 278k).
