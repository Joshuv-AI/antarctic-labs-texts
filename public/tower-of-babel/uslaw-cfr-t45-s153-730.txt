45 C.F.R. § 153.730 — Deadline for submission of data.
Citation: us:cfr:45:153.730
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-153.730
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 153 (Standards Related To Reinsurance, Risk Corridors, And Hhs Risk Adjustment Under The Affordable Care Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A risk adjustment covered plan or a reinsurance-eligible plan in a State in which HHS is operating the risk adjustment or reinsurance program, as applicable, must submit data to be considered for risk adjustment payments and charges and reinsurance payments for the applicable benefit year by April 30 of the year following the applicable benefit year or, if such date is not a business day, the next applicable business day.
