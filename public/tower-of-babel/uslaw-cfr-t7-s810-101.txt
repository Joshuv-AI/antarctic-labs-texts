7 C.F.R. § 810.101 — Grains for which standards are established.
Citation: us:cfr:7:810.101
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Federal Grain Inspection Service, Fair Trade Practices Program), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-810.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 810 (Official United States Standards For Grain). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Grain refers to barley, canola, corn, flaxseed, mixed grain, oats, rye, sorghum, soybeans, sunflower seed, triticale, and wheat. Standards for these food grains, feed grains, and oilseeds are established under the United States Grain Standards Act.
