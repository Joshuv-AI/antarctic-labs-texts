42 U.S.C. § 300b–16 — Authorization of appropriations for newborn screening programs and activities
Citation: us:usc:42:300b–16
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section300b–16
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter IX (Genetic Diseases, Hemophilia Programs, And Sudden Infant Death Syndrome); Part A (Genetic Diseases). There are authorized to be appropriated—
(1)
to carry out sections 300b–8, 300b–9, 300b–10, and 300b–11 of this title, $11,900,000 for each of fiscal years 2015 through 2019; and
(2)
to carry out section 300b–12 of this title, $8,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2015 through 2019.
