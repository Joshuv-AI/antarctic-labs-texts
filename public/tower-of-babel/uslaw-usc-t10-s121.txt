10 U.S.C. § 121 — Regulations
Citation: us:usc:10:121
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And General Military Powers); Chapter 3 (General Powers And Functions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may prescribe regulations to carry out his functions, powers, and duties under this title.
