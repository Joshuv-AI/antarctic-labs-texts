7 C.F.R. § 66.400 — Prohibited act.
Citation: us:cfr:7:66.400
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-66.400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 66 (National Bioengineered Food Disclosure Standard). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is a violation of 7 U.S.C. 1639b for any person to knowingly fail to make a bioengineered food disclosure in accordance with this part.
