44 C.F.R. § 204.2 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:44:204.2
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-204.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 204 (Fire Management Assistance Grant Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part is intended for those individuals responsible for requesting declarations and administering grants under the Fire Management Assistance Grant Program, as well as those applying for assistance under the program.
