43 C.F.R. § 3838.2 — How are SRHA lands different from other Federal lands?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3838.2
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3838.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3838 (Special Procedures For Locating And Recording Mining Claims And Tunnel Sites On Stockraising Homestead Act (Srha) Lands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

SRHA lands are different from other Federal lands in that the United States owns the mineral estate of SRHA lands, but not the surface estate. Patents issued under the SRHA, and Homestead Act entries patented under the SRHA, reserved the mineral estate to the United States along with the right to enter, mine, and remove any reserved minerals that may be present in the mineral estate.
