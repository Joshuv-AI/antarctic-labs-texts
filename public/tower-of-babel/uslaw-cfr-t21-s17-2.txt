21 C.F.R. § 17.2 — Maximum penalty amounts.
Citation: us:cfr:21:17.2
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-17.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 17 (Civil Money Penalties Hearings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The maximum civil money penalties associated with the statutory provisions authorizing civil money penalties under the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act or the Public Health Service Act can be found at 45 CFR part 102. The table of these maximum civil money penalties can be found at 45 CFR 102.3.
