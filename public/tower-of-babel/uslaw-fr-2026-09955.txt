SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Background: The Middle Class Tax Relief and 
Job Creation Act of 2012 (codified at 47 U.S.C. 1401 et seq.) (Act) 
established the FirstNet Authority as an independent authority within 
NTIA. The Act directs the FirstNet Authority to ensure the building, 
deployment, and operation of a nationwide interoperable public safety 
broadband network. The FirstNet Authority Board is responsible for 
making strategic decisions regarding the operations of the FirstNet 
Authority.
