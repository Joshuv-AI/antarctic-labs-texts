30 C.F.R. § 285.903 — What are the requirements for decommissioning FERC-licensed hydrokinetic facilities?
Citation: us:cfr:30:285.903
Creator: Bureau Of Safety And Environmental Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-285.903
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 285 (Renewable Energy And Alternate Uses Of Existing Facilities On The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must comply with the decommissioning requirements in your BOEM-issued lease. If you fail to comply with the decommissioning requirements of your lease then:
(a) BOEM may call for the forfeiture of your bond or other financial assurance;
(b) You remain liable for removal or disposal costs and responsible for accidents or damages that might result from such failure; and
(c) BSEE may take enforcement action under § 285.400.
