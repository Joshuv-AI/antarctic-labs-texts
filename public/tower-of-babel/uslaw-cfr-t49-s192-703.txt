49 C.F.R. § 192.703 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:49:192.703
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-192.703
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 192 (Transportation Of Natural And Other Gas By Pipeline: Minimum Federal Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) No person may operate a segment of pipeline, unless it is maintained in accordance with this subpart.
(b) Each segment of pipeline that becomes unsafe must be replaced, repaired, or removed from service.
(c) Hazardous leaks must be repaired promptly.
