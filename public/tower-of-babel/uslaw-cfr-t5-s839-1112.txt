5 C.F.R. § 839.1112 — If I elect to change my retirement coverage under the FERCCA, can I retroactively revoke the waiver of military retired pay I made at retirement?
Citation: us:cfr:5:839.1112
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026

Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, you may retroactively change your decision regarding waiver of your military retired pay.
