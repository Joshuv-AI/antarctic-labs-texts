10 U.S.C. § 4884 — Industrial mobilization: Board on Mobilization of Industries Essential for Military Preparedness
Citation: us:usc:10:4884
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section4884
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part V (Acquisition); Subpart I (Defense Industrial Base); Chapter 385 (Other Technology Base Policies And Programs); Subchapter IV (Defense Industrial Reserve And Industrial Mobilization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may appoint a nonpartisan Board on Mobilization of Industries Essential for Military Preparedness, and may provide necessary clerical assistance, to organize and coordinate operations under sections 4882 and 4883 of this title.
