42 C.F.R. § 1003.1210 — Amount of penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:42:1003.1210
Creator: Office Of Inspector General-Health Care, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-1003.1210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 1003 (Civil Money Penalties, Assessments And Exclusions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The OIG may impose a penalty of not more than—
(a) $100,000 for each individual violation of § 1003.1200(a) or § 1003.1200(b)(2); and
(b) $10,000 for each day that such information has not been provided in violation of § 1003.1200(b)(1).
