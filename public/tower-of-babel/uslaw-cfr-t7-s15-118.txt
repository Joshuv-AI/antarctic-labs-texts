7 C.F.R. § 15.118 — Cross-examination.
Citation: us:cfr:7:15.118
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-15.118
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 15 (Nondiscrimination). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Cross-examination will be limited to the scope of direct examination and matters at issue in the hearing.
