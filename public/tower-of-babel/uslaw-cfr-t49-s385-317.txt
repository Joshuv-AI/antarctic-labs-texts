49 C.F.R. § 385.317 — Safety audit: Safety fitness determination.
Citation: us:cfr:49:385.317
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-385.317
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 385 (Safety Fitness Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A safety audit will not result in a safety fitness determination. Safety fitness determinations follow completion of a compliance review.
