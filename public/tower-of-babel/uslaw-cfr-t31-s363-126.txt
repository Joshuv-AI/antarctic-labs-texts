31 C.F.R. § 363.126 — Under what circumstances will payment be made?
Citation: us:cfr:31:363.126
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-363.126
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 363 (Regulations Governing Securities Held In Treasurydirect). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We will make payment:
(a) Upon your request for redemption prior to maturity;
(b) When the bond reaches final maturity; and
(c) If a person who becomes entitled to the bond is unable, unwilling or ineligible to open a TreasuryDirect ® account.
