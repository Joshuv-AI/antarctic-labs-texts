16 C.F.R. § 1263.3 — Requirements for consumer products containing button cell or coin batteries.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1263.3
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1263.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1263 (Safety Standard For Button Cell Or Coin Batteries And Consumer Products Containing Such Batteries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each consumer product containing button cell or coin batteries shall comply with ANSI/UL 4200A, Standard for Safety for Products Incorporating Button Batteries or Coin Cell Batteries, approved on August 30, 2023. The Director of the Federal Register approves this incorporation by reference in accordance with 5 U.S.C. 552(a) and 1 CFR part 51.
