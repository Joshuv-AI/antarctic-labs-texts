37 C.F.R. § 1.935 — Initial Office action usually accompanies order for inter partes reexamination.
Citation: us:cfr:37:1.935
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-1.935
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 1 (Rules Of Practice In Patent Cases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The order for inter partes reexamination will usually be accompanied by the initial Office action on the merits of the reexamination.
