50 C.F.R. § 217.162 — Permissible methods of taking.
Citation: us:cfr:50:217.162
Creator: National Marine Fisheries Service, National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-217.162
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 217 (Regulations Governing The Take Of Marine Mammals Incidental To Specified Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Under an LOA issued pursuant to §§ 217.106 of this chapter and 217.166, the holder of the LOA (hereinafter “Hilcorp”) may incidentally, but not intentionally, take marine mammals within the specified geographical region described in § 217.160(b) by harassment associated with the specified activities provided they are in compliance with all terms, conditions, and requirements of the regulations in this subpart and the applicable LOA.
