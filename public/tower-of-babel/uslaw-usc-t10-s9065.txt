10 U.S.C. § 9065 — Commands: territorial organization
Citation: us:usc:10:9065
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9065
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization); Chapter 907 (The Air Force). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Except as otherwise prescribed by law or by the Secretary of Defense, the Air Force shall be divided into such organizations as the Secretary of the Air Force may prescribe.
(b)
For Air Force purposes, the United States, its possessions, and other places in which the Air Force is stationed or is operating, may be divided into such areas as directed by the Secretary. Officers of the Air Force may be assigned to command Air Force activities, installations, and personnel in those areas. In the discharge of the Air Force’s functions or other functions authorized by law, officers so assigned have the duties and powers prescribed by the Secretary.
