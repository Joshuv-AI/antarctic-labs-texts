46 U.S.C. § 55303 — Motor vehicles owned by United States Government personnel
Citation: us:usc:46:55303
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section55303
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part D (Promotional Programs); Chapter 553 (Passenger And Cargo Preferences); Subchapter I (Government Impelled Transportation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other law, privately-owned American shipping services may be used to transport motor vehicles owned by personnel of the United States Government whenever transportation of those vehicles at Government expense is otherwise authorized by law.
