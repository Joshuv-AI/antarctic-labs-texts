20 C.F.R. § 71.6 — Notice of injury or death.
Citation: us:cfr:20:71.6
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-71.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 71 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notwithstanding any of the provisions in part 61 of this chapter, no notice or report of injury or death shall be required for claims filed under this subchapter.
