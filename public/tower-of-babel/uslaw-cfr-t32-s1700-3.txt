32 C.F.R. § 1700.3 — Contact for general information and requests.

Creator: Office Of The Director Of National Intelligence
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1700 (Procedures For Disclosure Of Records Pursuant To The Freedom Of Information Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For general information on this part, to inquire about the FOIA program at ODNI, or to file a FOIA request, a written submission should be sent, either by mail to the Office of the Director of National Intelligence, IRRG Chief, Information Management Office, Washington, DC 20511, or by email to DNI-FOIA@dni.gov. To check on the status of a pending case, an individual may either call the ODNI FOIA Office at (703) 275-1313 or email the ODNI FOIA Office at DNI-FOIA@dni.gov.
