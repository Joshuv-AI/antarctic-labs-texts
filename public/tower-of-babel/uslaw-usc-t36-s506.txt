36 U.S.C. § 506 — Duration of regulations and licenses and publication of regulations
Citation: us:usc:36:506
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section506
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part A (Observances And Ceremonies); Chapter 5 (Presidential Inaugural Ceremonies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Regulations prescribed and licenses authorized under this chapter are effective only during the inaugural period. The regulations shall be published in at least one daily newspaper published in the District of Columbia. A penalty prescribed for violating such a regulation may not be enforced until 5 days after publication.
