44 C.F.R. § 209.5 — Applicant eligibility.
Citation: us:cfr:44:209.5
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-209.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 209 (Supplemental Property Acquisition And Elevation Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following are eligible to apply to the State for a grant:
(a) State and local governments;
(b) Indian tribes or authorized tribal organizations. A tribe may apply either to the State or directly to us; and
(c) Qualified private nonprofit organizations.
