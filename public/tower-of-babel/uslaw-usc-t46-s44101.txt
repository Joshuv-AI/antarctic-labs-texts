46 U.S.C. § 44101 — Application
Citation: us:usc:46:44101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section44101
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part C (Miscellaneous); Chapter 441 (Evidence Of Financial Responsibility For Passenger Transportation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter applies to a vessel that—
(1)
has berth or stateroom accommodations for at least 50 passengers; and
(2)
boards passengers at a port in the United States.
