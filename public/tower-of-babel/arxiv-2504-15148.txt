Uniformly resolvable decompositions of K v K_{v} into 1 1 -factors and odd n n -star factors
Jehyun Lee, Melissa Keranen

Abstract
We consider uniformly resolvable decompositions of K v K_{v} into subgraphs such that each resolution class contains only blocks isomorphic to the same graph. We give a partial solution for the case in which all resolution classes are either K 2 K_{2} or K 1 , n K_{1,n} where n n is odd.

Necessary Conditions

Lemma 2.1
.

Let

Assume that there exists a

By counting the number of edges of

that appear in the factors it follows that

\displaystyle r\frac{v}{2}+s\frac{nv}{n+1}=\frac{v(v-1)}{2},

and hence

\displaystyle(n+1)r+2ns=(n+1)(v-1).

(1)

Let

be the set of
s

K_{1,n}
-factors, and let

be the set of
r
1-factors. Because the factors of

are regular of degree 1, every vertex of

is incident to

(v-1)-r

with degree

and in

with degree 1. Because

\displaystyle x+y=s\textrm{ and }nx+y=v-1-r,

\displaystyle(n+1)(v-1-nx-y)+2n(x+y)=(n+1)(v-1).

This implies

.

Further, replacing

Finally, if

∎

3.1

For a weighted graph

In this section, we construct
