33 U.S.C. § 635 — Coast Guard anchorages
Citation: us:usc:33:635
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section635
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 12 (River And Harbor Improvements Generally); Subchapter V (Prosecution Of Work Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may perform dredging at Federal expense within and adjacent to anchorages established by the Coast Guard pursuant to existing authorities.
