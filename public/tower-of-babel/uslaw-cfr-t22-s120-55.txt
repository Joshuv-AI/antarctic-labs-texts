22 C.F.R. § 120.55 — Access information.
Citation: us:cfr:22:120.55
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-120.55
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 120 (Purpose And Definitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Access information is information that allows access to encrypted technical data subject to this subchapter in an unencrypted form. Examples include decryption keys, network access codes, and passwords.
