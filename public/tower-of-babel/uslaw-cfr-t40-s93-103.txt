40 C.F.R. § 93.103 — Priority.
Citation: us:cfr:40:93.103
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-93.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 93 (Determining Conformity Of Federal Actions To State Or Federal Implementation Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When assisting or approving any action with air quality-related consequences, FHWA and FTA shall give priority to the implementation of those transportation portions of an applicable implementation plan prepared to attain and maintain the NAAQS. This priority shall be consistent with statutory requirements for allocation of funds among States or other jurisdictions.
