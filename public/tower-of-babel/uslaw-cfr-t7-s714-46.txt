7 C.F.R. § 714.46 — Certification for payment.
Citation: us:cfr:7:714.46
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-714.46
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 714 (Refunds Of Penalties Erroneously, Illegally, Or Wrongfully Collected). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An officer or employee of the Department of Agriculture authorized to certify public vouchers for payment shall, for and on behalf of the Secretary of Agriculture, certify to the Secretary of the Treasury of the United States for payment all claims for refund which have been approved.
