22 U.S.C. § 4602 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:22:4602
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section4602
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 56 (United States Institute Of Peace). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this chapter, the term—
(1)
“Institute” means the United States Institute of Peace established by this chapter; and
(2)
“Board” means the Board of Directors of the Institute.
