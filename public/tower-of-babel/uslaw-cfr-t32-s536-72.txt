32 C.F.R. § 536.72 — Finality of settlement.
Citation: us:cfr:32:536.72
Creator: Department Of The Army
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-536.72
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 536 (Claims Against The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A claimant's acceptance of an award, except for an advance payment or a split payment for property damage only, constitutes a release of the United States and its employees from all liability. Where applicable, a release should include the ARNG or the sending State. For further discussion see DA Pam 27-162, paragraph 2-82.
