49 C.F.R. § 273.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:49:273.1
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-273.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 273 (Metrics And Minimum Standards For Intercity Passenger Train Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to establish metrics and minimum standards for measuring the performance and service quality of intercity passenger train operations.
