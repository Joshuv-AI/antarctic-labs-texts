17 C.F.R. § 38.550 — Core Principle 10.
Citation: us:cfr:17:38.550
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-38.550
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 38 (Designated Contract Markets). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The board of trade shall maintain rules and procedures to provide for the recording and safe storage of all identifying trade information in a manner that enables the contract market to use the information:
(a) To assist in the prevention of customer and market abuses; and
(b) To provide evidence of any violations of the rules of the contract market.
