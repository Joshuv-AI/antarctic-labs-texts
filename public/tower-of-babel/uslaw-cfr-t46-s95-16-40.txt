46 C.F.R. § 95.16-40 — -40 Locked spaces.
Citation: us:cfr:46:95.16-40
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-95.16-40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 95 (Fire Protection Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a space or enclosure containing extinguishing agent supply or controls is lockable, a key to the space or enclosure must be in a break glass type box conspicuously located adjacent to the opening.
