20 C.F.R. § 408.1045 — What procedures apply if you request an ALJ hearing?
Citation: us:cfr:20:408.1045
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-408.1045
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 408 (Special Benefits For Certain World War Ii Veterans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) General rules. For purposes of this part, we use the same rules on ALJ hearing procedures that we use in the title XVI program (see §§ 416.1444-416.1461 of this chapter), except as noted in paragraph (b) of this section.
(b) Exceptions. (1) In § 416.1446(b)(1), the last sentence does not apply under this part.
(2) In § 416.1452(a)(1)(i), the words “supplemental security income” are deemed to read “SVB.”
(3) In § 416.1457, the provisions of paragraph (c)(4) do not apply under this part.
