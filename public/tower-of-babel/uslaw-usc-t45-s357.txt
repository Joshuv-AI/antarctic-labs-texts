45 U.S.C. § 357 — Free transportation
Citation: us:usc:45:357
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2024
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title45-section357
Context: United States Code, Title 45 (Railroads); Chapter 11 (Railroad Unemployment Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It shall not be unlawful for carriers to furnish free transportation to employees qualified for benefits or serving waiting periods under this chapter.
