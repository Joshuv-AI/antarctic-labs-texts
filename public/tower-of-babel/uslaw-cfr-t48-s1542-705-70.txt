48 C.F.R. § 1542.705-70 — 1542.705-70 Solicitation and contract clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1542.705-70
Creator: Chapter 15—Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1542.705-70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1542 (Contract Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Contracting Officer shall insert the clause in 1552.242-70, Indirect Costs, in solicitations and contracts where indirect costs apply, unless contracting with an educational institution where there are approved predetermined final indirect cost rates.
