30 C.F.R. § 817.59 — Coal recovery.
Citation: us:cfr:30:817.59
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-817.59
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 817 (Permanent Program Performance Standards—Underground Mining Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Underground mining activities shall be conducted so as to maximize the utilization and conservation of the coal, while utilizing the best technology currently available to maintain environmental integrity, so that reaffecting the land in the future through surface coal mining operations is minimized.
