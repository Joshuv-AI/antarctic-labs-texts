While for other basic Lie superalgebras,

24

In this paper, we study the admissible modules in context of vertex operator superalgebra.
Specially, we consider the Lie superalgebra

We conjecture that

\mathcal{O}
at boundary admissible level
\mathcal{k}

and the irreducible weak modules in the category

,

at admissible level
\mathcal{k}
in the category

Definition 2.1

A vertex superalgebra is a quadruple

\displaystyle Y(\cdot,z):

\displaystyle V\rightarrow(\mbox{End}V)[[z,z^{-1}]]

\displaystyle a\mapsto Y(a,z)=\sum_{n\in\mathbb{Z}}a_{n}z^{-n-1}~~(a_{n}\in\mbox{End}V)

such that

(1) For any
a,b\in V
,
a_{n}b=0
for
n
sufficiently large;

(2)
[D,Y(a,z)]=Y(D(a),z)=\frac{d}{dz}Y(a,z)

;

(3)
Y(\textbf{1},z)=\mbox{Id}_{V}
;

(4)
Y(a,z)\textbf{1}\in V[[z]]

for any
a\in V
;

(5) For
\mathbb{Z}_{2}
-homogeneous elements

\displaystyle z_{0}^{-1}\delta(\frac{z_{1}-z_{2}}{z_{0}})Y(a,z_{1})Y(b,z_{2})-(-1)^{|a||b|}z_{0}^{-1}\delta(\frac{z_{2}-z_{1}}{-z_{0}})Y(b,z_{2})Y(a,z_{1})

\displaystyle=z_{2}^{-1}\delta(\frac{z_{1}-z_{0}}{z_{2}})Y(Y(a,z_{0})b,z_{2}).

is called a

-graded (resp.

-graded) vertex operator superalgebra

(6) Set

(7)
L(-1)=D

(8)

\mathbb{Z}
-graded (resp.

-graded) such that

Proposition 3.9
