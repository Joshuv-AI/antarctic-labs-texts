33 C.F.R. § 158.250 — Standard discharge connection.
Citation: us:cfr:33:158.250
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-158.250
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 158 (Reception Facilities For Oil, Noxious Liquid Substances, And Garbage). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each reception facility that received bilge water containing oily mixtures must have a standard discharge connection that—
(a) Meets § 155.430 of this subchapter; and
(b) Attaches to each hose or pipe that removes bilge water containing oily mixtures from oceangoing ships.
