42 U.S.C. § 4395 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:42:4395
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section4395
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 57 (Environmental Pollution Study). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this chapter.
