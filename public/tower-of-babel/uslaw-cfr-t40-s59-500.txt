40 C.F.R. § 59.500 — What is the purpose of this subpart?
Citation: us:cfr:40:59.500
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-59.500
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 59 (National Volatile Organic Compound Emission Standards For Consumer And Commercial Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart establishes the product-weighted reactivity (PWR) limits regulated entities must meet in order to comply with the national rule for volatile organic compounds (VOC) emitted from aerosol coatings. This subpart also establishes labeling, recordkeeping, and reporting requirements for regulated entities.
