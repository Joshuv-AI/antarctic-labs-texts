47 C.F.R. § 74.605 — Registration of stationary television pickup receive sites.
Citation: us:cfr:47:74.605
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-74.605
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 74 (Experimental Radio, Auxiliary, Special Broadcast And Other Program Distributional Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Licensees of TV pickup stations in the 6875-7125 MHz and 12700-13200 MHz bands shall register their stationary receive sites using the Commission's Universal Licensing System.
