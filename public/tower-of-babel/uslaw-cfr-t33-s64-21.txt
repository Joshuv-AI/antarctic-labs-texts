33 C.F.R. § 64.21 — Marking and notification requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:33:64.21
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-64.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 64 (Marking Of Structures, Sunken Vessels And Other Obstructions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before establishing a structure, the owner or operator shall apply for Coast Guard authorization to mark the structure in accordance with § 66.01-5 of this chapter. The appropriate District Commander will determine the marking requirements.
