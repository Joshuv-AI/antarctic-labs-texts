7 U.S.C. § 508 — Separability
Citation: us:usc:7:508
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section508
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 21 (Tobacco Statistics). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this chapter is declared unconstitutional or the applicability thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the validity of the remainder of said sections and the applicability of such provisions to other persons and circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
