7 C.F.R. § 500.2 — Recording presence.
Citation: us:cfr:7:500.2
Creator: Agricultural Research Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-500.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 500 (National Arboretum). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Admission to the USNA during periods when it is closed to the public will be limited to authorized individuals who may be required to sign the register or display identification documents when requested by the Security Staff, or other authorized individuals.
