45 C.F.R. § 2556.765 — What disciplinary actions are VISTAs subject to for violating restrictions or prohibitions on political activities?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2556.765
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2556.765
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2556 (Volunteers In Service To America). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Violations by a VISTA of any of the prohibitions or restrictions set forth in this subpart may warrant termination for cause, in accordance with proceedings set forth at §§ 2556.420, 2556.425, and 2556.430.
