Introduction

In this paper, let
q=p^{n}
be an odd prime power and
\mathbb{F}_{q}
be the finite field of
q

In recent years, considerable attention has been devoted to the study of the differential properties of functions of the form

F_{r,u}(x)=x^{r}(1+u\chi(x)),

(1)

where
\chi(x)=x^{\frac{q-1}{2}}

\mathbb{F}_{q}

[
16

Definition 1.1
.

Let
F
be a function on
\mathbb{F}_{q}
. Define
\delta_{F}(a,b)
by the number of solutions of
F(x+a)-F(x)=b

a\in\mathbb{F}_{q}^{*}
and

differential uniformity
of
F
is defined by :

\delta_{F}=\displaystyle\max_{a\in\mathbb{F}_{q}^{*},b\in\mathbb{F}_{q}}\delta_{F}(a,b).

A function on
\mathbb{F}_{q}
is said to be
perfect nonlinear (PN)

1

2

almost perfect nonlinear (APN)
.
In
[
15

F_{r,u}
is APN when
r=q-2
and
\chi(u-1)=\chi(u+1)=\chi(u)

p=3

Subsequently, Zeng

[

q\equiv 3\pmod{4}

proving that
F_{q-2,u}
is APN if
\chi(u-1)=\chi(u+1)=-\chi(5u\pm 3)

More recently, several studies have focused on the non-APN case of
F_{q-2,u}

For instance, Xia

[
18

[

Definition 1.2
.

Let
F
be a function on
\mathbb{F}_{q}

\beta_{F}(a,b)

\begin{cases}F(x)-F(y)=b,\\

\beta_{F}=\displaystyle\max_{a,b\in\mathbb{F}_{q}^{*}}\beta_{F}(a,b).

.
By Lemma
2.8

for

Preliminaries

When
F(x)=x^{r}
is a power function, multiplying
\frac{1}{a^{r}}
on the both sides of
b=F(x+a)-F(x)=(x+a)^{d}-x^{d}
, we have

\displaystyle\frac{b}{a^{r}}=\left(\displaystyle\frac{x}{a}+1\right)^{r}-\left(\displaystyle\frac{x}{a}\right)^{r}=(y+1)^{r}-y^{r},

where
y=\displaystyle\frac{x}{a}
. Thus, we have

\delta_{F}(a,b)=\delta_{F}\left(1,\displaystyle\frac{b}{a^{r}}\right),\text{ so }\delta_{F}=\displaystyle\max_{b\in\mathbb{F}_{q}}\delta_{F}(1,b),

(2)

in this case. Notion of
locally-APN
functions was introduced in
[
2
]
, for the even characteristic case. In
[
9
]
, Hu et al generalized it for odd characteristic cases, as follows.

\delta_{F}(1,b)\leq 2\text{ for all }b\in\mathbb{F}_{q}\setminus\mathbb{F}_{p}.

(3)

In our previous paper
[
10
]
, we extend the above definition to functions with the property

\delta_{F}(a,b)=\delta_{F}(1,g_{a}(b))\text{ for all }b\in\mathbb{F}_{p^{n}},

(4)

where
g_{a}
permutes
\mathbb{F}_{q}
for each
a\in\mathbb{F}_{q}^{*}
.

Definition 2.1
.

Let
F
be a function on
\mathbb{F}_{q}
satisfying (
4
) for every
a\in\mathbb{F}_{p^{n}}^{*}
. Then,

is called

if
\delta_{F}(1,b)\leq 2
for all
b\in\mathbb{F}_{q}\setminus\mathbb{F}_{p}
.

Lemma 2.2
.

(Lemma 11 of
[
14
]

Let

and

\displaystyle\delta_{F_{r,u}}(a,b)

\displaystyle=\begin{cases}\delta_{F_{r,u}}\left(1,\frac{b}{a^{r}}\right)&\text{ if }\chi(a)=1,\\

\displaystyle=\begin{cases}\beta_{F_{r,u}}\left(1,\frac{b}{a^{r}}\right)&\text{ if }\chi(a)=1,\\
\beta_{F_{r,u}}\left(1,\frac{b}{(-1)^{r}a^{r}}\right)&\text{ if }\chi(a)=-1.\end{cases}

Lemma 2.8

Let

F_{\frac{3^{k}+1}{2}}

Lemma 3.3

Lemma 3.4

Lemma 3.5

Let

and

Conclusion

In this paper, we show that if
\gcd(q-1,r)\mid 2
and
D_{00}(b)\leq 1
for all
b\in\mathbb{F}_{q}^{*}
, then
F_{r}
is locally-APN with boomerang uniformity at most
2
.
Moreover, it holds that
D_{00}(b)\leq 1
for all
b\in\mathbb{F}_{q}^{*}
for the exponents
r
listed in Table
3
.
We further investigate the differential spectra of
F_{3}
and
F_{\frac{2q-1}{3}}
.
In addition, by analyzing the boomerang spectrum of
F_{2}
when
p=3
, we prove that its boomerang uniformity is equal to
1
,
which contradicts the result of
[
14
]
, where it was claimed that the boomerang uniformity of
F_{2}
equals
2
for sufficiently large
q
.

It should be noted that Table
3
does not exhaust all APN power functions
with
\gcd(r,q-1)=2
.
Preliminary experiments suggest that
D_{00}(b)\leq 1
may also hold for some additional exponents,
which we plan to investigate in future work.
We also plan to further explore how this idea can be extended in future work.
