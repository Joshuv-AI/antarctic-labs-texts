30 C.F.R. § 47.54 — Availability of an MSDS.
Citation: us:cfr:30:47.54
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-47.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 47 (HAZARD COMMUNICATION (HazCom)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The operator must make MSDSs accessible to miners during each work shift for each hazardous chemical to which they may be exposed either—
(a) At each work area where the hazardous chemical is produced or used, or
(b) At an alternative location, provided that the MSDS is readily available to miners in an emergency.
