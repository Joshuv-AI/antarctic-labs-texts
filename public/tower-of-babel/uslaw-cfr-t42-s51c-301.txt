42 C.F.R. § 51c.301 — c.301 Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:42:51c.301
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-51c.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 51c (PART 51c—GRANTS FOR COMMUNITY HEALTH SERVICES). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations of this subpart, in addition to the regulations of subpart A, are applicable to grants awarded pursuant to section 330(d)(1)(A) of the Act for the costs of operation of community health centers which serve medically underserved populations.
