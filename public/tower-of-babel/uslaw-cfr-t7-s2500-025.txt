7 C.F.R. § 2500.025 — Procedures to minimize or eliminate duplication of effort.
Citation: us:cfr:7:2500.025
Creator: Office Of Advocacy And Outreach, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-2500.025
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 2500 (Oao Federal Financial Assistance Programs—General Award Administrative Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

OAO may implement appropriate business processes to minimize or eliminate the awarding of Federal assistance to projects that unnecessarily duplicate activities already being sponsored under other awards, including awards made by other Federal agencies.
