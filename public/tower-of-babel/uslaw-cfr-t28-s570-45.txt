28 C.F.R. § 570.45 — Violation of escorted trip.
Citation: us:cfr:28:570.45
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-570.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 570 (Community Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Staff shall process as an escapee an inmate who absconds from an escorted trip.
(b) Staff may take disciplinary action against an inmate who fails to comply with any of the conditions of the escorted trip.
