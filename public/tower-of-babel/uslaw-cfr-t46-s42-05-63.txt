46 C.F.R. § 42.05-63 — -63 Ship(s) and vessel(s).
Citation: us:cfr:46:42.05-63
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-42.05-63
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 42 (Domestic And Foreign Voyages By Sea). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The terms ship(s) and vessel(s) are interchangeable or synonymous words, and include every description of watercraft, other than a seaplane on the water, used or capable of being used as a means of transportation on water.
