5 C.F.R. § 1655.3 — Information concerning the cost of a loan.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1655.3
Creator: Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1655.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1655 (Loan Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Information concerning the cost of a loan is provided in the booklet TSP Loan Program (available on the TSP Web site, from the participant's personnel office or service, or from the TSP). From this information, a participant can determine the effects of a loan on his or her final account balance and can compare the cost of a loan to that of other sources of financing.
