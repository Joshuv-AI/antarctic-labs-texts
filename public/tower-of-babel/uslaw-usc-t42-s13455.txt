42 U.S.C. § 13455 — Electric drives
Citation: us:usc:42:13455
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section13455
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 134 (Energy Policy); Subchapter IX (Energy And Environment); Part A (Improved Energy Efficiency). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Program
The Secretary shall conduct a 5-year program, in accordance with sections 13541 and 13542 of this title, to increase the efficiency of electric drive technologies, including adjustable speed drives, high speed motors, and high efficiency motors.
(b) Proposals
Within 1 year after October 24, 1992, the Secretary shall solicit proposals for projects under this section.
