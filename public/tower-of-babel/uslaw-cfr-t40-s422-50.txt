40 C.F.R. § 422.50 — Applicability; description of the defluorinated phosphoric acid subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:422.50
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-422.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 422 (Phosphate Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges resulting from the defluorination of phosphoric acid. Wet process phosphoric acid is dehydrated by application of heat and other processing acids such as vacuum and air stripping. The acid is concentrated up to 70-73% P2 O5 in the defluorination process.
