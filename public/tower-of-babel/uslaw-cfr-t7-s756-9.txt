7 C.F.R. § 756.9 — Availability of funds and timing of payments.
Citation: us:cfr:7:756.9
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-756.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 756 (Oriental Fruit Fly Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The total available program funds are $9 million as provided by Public Law 116-6 (the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2019). OFF Program payments will be issued after all applications are received and FSA has approved the application.
