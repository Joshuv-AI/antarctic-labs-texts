4 C.F.R. § 28.66 — Admissibility.
Citation: us:cfr:4:28.66
Creator: Government Accountability Office
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-4/section-28.66
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 4 (Accounts), Part 28 (Government Accountability Office Personnel Appeals Board; Procedures Applicable To Claims Concerning Employment Practices At The Government Accountability Office). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Evidence or testimony may be excluded from consideration by the administrative judge if it is irrelevant, immaterial, unduly repetitious or protected by privilege. The administrative judge is not bound by formal evidentiary rules but may rely on the Federal Rules of Evidence for guidance.
