22 U.S.C. § 1896b — Colombo Plan Council for Technical Cooperation; authorization
Citation: us:usc:22:1896b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1896b
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 24 (Mutual Security Program); Subchapter III (Economic Assistance); Part C (Technical Cooperation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To enable the United States to maintain membership in the Colombo Plan Council for Technical Cooperation, there is authorized to be appropriated from time to time to the Department of State such sums as may be necessary for the payment by the United States of its share of the expenses of the Colombo Plan Council for Technical Cooperation.
