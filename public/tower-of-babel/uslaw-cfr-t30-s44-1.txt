30 C.F.R. § 44.1 — Scope and construction.
Citation: us:cfr:30:44.1
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-44.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 44 (Rules Of Practice For Petitions For Modification Of Mandatory Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The procedures and rules of practice set forth in this part shall govern petitions for modification of mandatory safety standards filed under section 101(c) of the Act.
(b) These rules shall be liberally construed to carry out the purpose of the Act by assuring adequate protection of miners and to secure just and prompt determination of all proceedings consistent with adequate consideration of the issues involved.
