32 C.F.R. § 202.14 — Documenting and reporting activities and expenses.
Citation: us:cfr:32:202.14
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-202.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 202 (Restoration Advisory Boards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The installation at which a RAB is established shall document the activities and meeting minutes and record the administrative expenses associated with the RAB in the information repository at a publicly accessible location. Installations shall use internal department and Military Component-specific reporting mechanisms to submit required information on RAB activities and expenditures.
