29 C.F.R. § 548.404 — Kinds of jobs or employees.
Citation: us:cfr:29:548.404
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-548.404
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 548 (Authorization Of Established Basic Rates For Computing Overtime Pay). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The application should describe or otherwise identify the employees to whom the established basic rate will apply. The individual employees need not be identified by name but may be described in terms of job classification, department, location or other appropriate identifying characteristics.
