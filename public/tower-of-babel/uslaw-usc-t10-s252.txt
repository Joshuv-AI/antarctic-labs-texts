10 U.S.C. § 252 — Use of militia and armed forces to enforce Federal authority
Citation: us:usc:10:252
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section252
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And General Military Powers); Chapter 13 (Insurrection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whenever the President considers that unlawful obstructions, combinations, or assemblages, or rebellion against the authority of the United States, make it impracticable to enforce the laws of the United States in any State by the ordinary course of judicial proceedings, he may call into Federal service such of the militia of any State, and use such of the armed forces, as he considers necessary to enforce those laws or to suppress the rebellion.
