19 U.S.C. § 3811 — Report on impact of trade promotion authority
Citation: us:usc:19:3811
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section3811
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 24 (Bipartisan Trade Promotion Authority). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
Not later than 1 year after August 6, 2002, the International Trade Commission shall report to the Committee on Finance of the Senate and the Committee on Ways and Means of the House of Representatives regarding the economic impact on the United States of the trade agreements described in subsection (b).
(b) Agreements
The trade agreements described in this subsection are the following:
(1)
The United States-Israel Free Trade Agreement.
(2)
The United States-Canada Free Trade Agreement.
(3)
The North American Free Trade Agreement.
(4)
The Uruguay Round Agreements.
(5)
The Tokyo Round of Multilateral Trade Negotiations.
