40 C.F.R. § 155.57 — Registration review decision.
Citation: us:cfr:40:155.57
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-155.57
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 155 (Registration Standards And Registration Review). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A registration review decision is the Agency's determination whether a pesticide meets, or does not meet, the standard for registration in FIFRA.
