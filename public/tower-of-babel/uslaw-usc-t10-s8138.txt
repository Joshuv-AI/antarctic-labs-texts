10 U.S.C. § 8138 — Regular Marine Corps: judge advocates
Citation: us:usc:10:8138
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8138
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 815 (Original Appointments). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

With the approval of the Secretary of the Navy, any regular officer on the active-duty list of the Marine Corps who is qualified under section 827(b) of this title may, upon his application, be designated as a judge advocate.
