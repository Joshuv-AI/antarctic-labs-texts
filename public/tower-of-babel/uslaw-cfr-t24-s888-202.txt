24 C.F.R. § 888.202 — Manner of publication.
Citation: us:cfr:24:888.202
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary For Housing-Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development (Section 8 Housing Assistance Programs, Section 202 Direct Loan Program, Section 202 Supportive Housing For The Elderly Program And Section 811 Supportive Housing For Persons With Disabilities Program)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-888.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 888 (Section 8 Housing Assistance Payments Program—Fair Market Rents And Contract Rent Annual Adjustment Factors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Adjustment Factors will be published in the Federal Register at least annually by Notice. Interim revisions may be published as market conditions indicate. In the case of revised factors applicable only to specific areas, the HUD Field Office will publish a notice appropriate to the limited scope of the revised factors (see § 888.204).
