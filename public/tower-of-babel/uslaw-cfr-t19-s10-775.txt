19 C.F.R. § 10.775 — Packaging and packing materials and containers for retail sale and for shipment.
Citation: us:cfr:19:10.775
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-10.775
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 10 (Articles Conditionally Free, Subject To A Reduced Rate, Etc.). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Packaging materials and containers in which a good is packaged for retail sale and packing materials and containers for shipment are to be disregarded in determining whether a good qualifies as an originating good under § 10.770 of this subpart and General Note 27, HTSUS, except to the extent that the value of such packaging and packing materials and containers may be included in meeting the value-content requirement specified in § 10.770(b) of this subpart.
