Retraction Note: Enhancement of novel Endo-polygalacturonase expression in Rhodotorula mucilaginosa PY18 : insights from mutagenesis and molecular docking
Authors: Nagwa M Abd El-Aziz, Maysa E Moharam, Nora N El-Gamal, Bigad E Khalil
Published: 2025

Retraction: Microbial Cell Factories (2023) 22:252 10.1186/s12934-023-02253

The Editor-in-Chief has retracted this article because it was mistakenly accepted without peer review. Additionally, Fig. 12 PY18 and E-54 lanes appear highly similar, and the authors have not provided the underlying raw data for validation.

Nagwa M. Abd El-Aziz does not agree with this retraction. Maysa E. Moharam, Nora N. El-Gamal and Bigad E. Khalil have not responded to any correspondence from the editor or publisher about this retraction.
