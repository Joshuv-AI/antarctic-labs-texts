19 C.F.R. § 210.41 — Termination of investigation.
Citation: us:cfr:19:210.41
Creator: United States International Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-210.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 210 (Adjudication And Enforcement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in § 210.21 (b)(2), (c), and (d), an order of termination issued by the Commission shall constitute a determination of the Commission under § 210.45(c). The Commission shall publish in the Federal Register notice of each Commission order that terminates an investigation in its entirety.
