26 C.F.R. § 156.6011-1 — -1 General requirement of return, statement, or list.
Citation: us:cfr:26:156.6011-1
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-156.6011-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 156 (Excise Tax On Greenmail). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every person liable for tax under section 5881 of the Code shall file a return with respect to the tax on the form prescribed by the Internal Revenue Service (Form 8725). Each such person shall include therein the information required by the form and the instructions issued with respect thereto.
