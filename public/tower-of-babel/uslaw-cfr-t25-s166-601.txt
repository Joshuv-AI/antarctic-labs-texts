25 C.F.R. § 166.601 — How is the amount of the bond determined?
Citation: us:cfr:25:166.601
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-166.601
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 166 (Grazing Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The amount of the bond for each permit is based on the:
(1) Value of one year's grazing rental payment;
(2) Value of any improvements to be constructed;
(3) Cost of performance of any additional obligations; and
(4) Cost of performance of restoration and reclamation.
(b) Tribal policy made applicable by § 166.100 of this part may establish or waive specific bond requirements for permits.
