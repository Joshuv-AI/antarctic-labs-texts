5 C.F.R. § 330.401 — Restricted positions.
Citation: us:cfr:5:330.401
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-330.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 330 (Recruitment, Selection, And Placement (General)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Under 5 U.S.C. 3310, competitive examinations for the positions of custodian, elevator operator, guard, and messenger (referred to in this subpart as restricted positions) are restricted to preference eligibles as long as a preference eligible is available. For more information on these restricted positions, refer to the OPM Delegated Examining Operations Handbook.
