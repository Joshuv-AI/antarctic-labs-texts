48 C.F.R. § 33.212 — 33.212 Contracting officer's duties upon appeal.
Citation: us:cfr:48:33.212
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-33.212
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 33 (Protests, Disputes, And Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To the extent permitted by any agency procedures controlling contacts with agency BCA personnel, the contracting officer shall provide data, documentation, information, and support as may be required by the agency BCA for use on a pending appeal from the contracting officer's decision.
