18 C.F.R. § 1317.605 — Enforcement procedures.

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-1317.605
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 1317 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Sex In Education Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The investigative, compliance, and enforcement procedural provisions of Title VI of the Civil Rights Act of 1964 (42 U.S.C. 2000d) (“Title VI”) are hereby adopted and applied to these Title IX regulations. These procedures may be found at 18 CFR part 1302.
