15 U.S.C. § 1666g — Tie-in services prohibited for issuance of credit card
Citation: us:usc:15:1666g
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1666g
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 41 (Consumer Credit Protection); Subchapter I (Consumer Credit Cost Disclosure); Part D (Credit Billing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any agreement to the contrary, a card issuer may not require a seller, as a condition to participating in a credit card plan, to open an account with or procure any other service from the card issuer or its subsidiary or agent.
