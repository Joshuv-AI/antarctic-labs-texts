46 C.F.R. § 194.20-11 — -11 Flushing systems.
Citation: us:cfr:46:194.20-11
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-194.20-11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 194 (Handling, Use, And Control Of Explosives And Other Hazardous Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Provision shall be made for flushing away chemical spills.
(b) If a drainage system is installed, it shall be separate from any other drainage system.
