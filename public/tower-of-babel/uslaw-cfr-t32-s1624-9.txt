32 C.F.R. § 1624.9 — Induction into the Armed Forces.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1624.9
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1624.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1624 (Inductions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Registrants in classes 1-A and 1-A-0, who have been ordered for induction and found qualified under standards prescribed by the Secretary of Defense, will be inducted at the MEPS into the Armed Forces.
