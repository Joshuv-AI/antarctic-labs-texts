31 C.F.R. § 235.1 — Scope of regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:31:235.1
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-235.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 235 (Issuance Of Settlement Checks For Forged Checks Drawn On Designated Depositaries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part governs the issuance of settlement checks for checks drawn on designated depositaries of the United States by accountable officers of the United States, that have been negotiated and paid on a forged or unauthorized indorsement.
