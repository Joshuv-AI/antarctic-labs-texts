46 C.F.R. § 90.10-20 — -20 Liftboat.
Citation: us:cfr:46:90.10-20
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-90.10-20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 90 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Liftboat means an offshore supply vessel with moveable legs capable of raising its hull above the surface of the sea.
