27 C.F.R. § 24.68 — Insurance coverage.
Citation: us:cfr:27:24.68
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-24.68
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 24 (Wine). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The remission, abatement, refund, credit, or other relief, of taxes on wine or spirits provided for under this part will be allowed only to the extent that the claimant is not indemnified or recompensed for such tax by any valid claim of insurance or otherwise.
