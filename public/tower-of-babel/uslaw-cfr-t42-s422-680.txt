42 C.F.R. § 422.680 — Witnesses.
Citation: us:cfr:42:422.680
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-422.680
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 422 (Medicare Advantage Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The hearing officer may examine the witnesses.
(b) The parties or their representatives are permitted to examine their witnesses and cross-examine witnesses of other parties.
