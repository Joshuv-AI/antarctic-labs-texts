7 C.F.R. § 3419.7 — Reporting of matching funds.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3419.7
Creator: National Institute Of Food And Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3419.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3419 (Matching Funds Requirement For Agricultural Research And Extension Capacity Funds At 1890 Land-Grant Institutions, Including Central State University, Tuskegee University, And West Virginia State University And At 1862 Land-Grant Institutions In Insular Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Institutions will report all capacity matching funds expended annually using Standard Form (SF) 425, in accordance with 7 CFR 3430.56(a).
