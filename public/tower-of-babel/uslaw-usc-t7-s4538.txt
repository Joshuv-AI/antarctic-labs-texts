7 U.S.C. § 4538 — Additional authority
Citation: us:usc:7:4538
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section4538
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 76 (Dairy Research And Promotion); Subchapter II (Dairy Research Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
No provision of this subchapter shall be construed to preempt or supersede any other program relating to milk or dairy products research organized and operated under the laws of the United States or any State.
(b)
The provisions of this subchapter applicable to the order issued under section 4533(b) of this title shall be applicable to any amendment to the order.
