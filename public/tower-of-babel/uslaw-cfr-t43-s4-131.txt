43 C.F.R. § 4.131 — Appeal and review.
Citation: us:cfr:43:4.131
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4.131
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4 (Department Of The Interior Hearings And Appeals Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any party seeking to appeal or otherwise obtain review of a final order or decision of the ALJ must comply with the statutory or regulatory provisions applicable to the specific type of proceeding involved.
