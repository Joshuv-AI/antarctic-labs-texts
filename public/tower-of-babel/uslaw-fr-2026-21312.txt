Petition for Reconsideration of Action in Rulemaking Proceeding;

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Federal Communications Commission published a document in 
the Federal Register concerning a Petition for Reconsideration 
(Petition) has been filed in the Commission's proceeding by Thomas 
Goode on behalf of the Alliance for Telecommunications Industry 
Solutions. That document contained incorrect dates.

DATES: October 19, 2026.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Jeanne Stockman, Attorney Advisor, 
Public Safety and Homeland Security Bureau at (202) 418-7830 or 
[email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Correction

    In the Federal Register of October 13, 2026 in FR Doc. 2026-20895 
on page 64872, in the third column, correct the DATES caption to read:

DATES: Oppositions to the Petition must be filed on or before October 
28, 2026. Replies to oppositions to the Petition must be filed on or 
before November 9, 2026.
