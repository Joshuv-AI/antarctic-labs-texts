14 C.F.R. § 440.1 — Scope of part.
Citation: us:cfr:14:440.1
Creator: Commercial Space Transportation, Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-440.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 440 (Financial Responsibility). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part establishes financial responsibility and allocation of risk requirements for any launch or reentry authorized by a license or permit issued under this subchapter.
