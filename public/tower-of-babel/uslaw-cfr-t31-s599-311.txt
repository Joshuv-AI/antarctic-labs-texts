31 C.F.R. § 599.311 — Means of production.
Citation: us:cfr:31:599.311
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-599.311
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 599 (Illicit Drug Trade Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term means of production includes any activities or transactions involving any equipment, chemical, product, or material that may be used, directly or indirectly, in the manufacture of illicit drugs or precursor chemicals.
