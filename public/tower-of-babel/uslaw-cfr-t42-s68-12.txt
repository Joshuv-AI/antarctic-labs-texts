42 C.F.R. § 68.12 — How does an individual receive loan repayments beyond the initial applicable contract period?
Citation: us:cfr:42:68.12
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-68.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 68 (NATIONAL INSTITUTES OF HEALTH (NIH) LOAN REPAYMENT PROGRAMS (LRPs)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An individual may apply for a competitive extension contract for at least a one-year period if the individual is engaged in qualifying research and satisfies the eligibility requirements specified under §§ 68.3 and 68.4 for the extension period and has remaining repayable debt as established by the NIH Director.
