5 U.S.C. § 1105 — Administrative procedure
Citation: us:usc:5:1105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section1105
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part II (Civil Service Functions And Responsibilities); Chapter 11 (Office Of Personnel Management). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Subject to section 1103(b) of this title, in the exercise of the functions assigned under this chapter, the Director shall be subject to subsections (b), (c), and (d) of section 553 of this title, notwithstanding subsection (a) of such section 553.
