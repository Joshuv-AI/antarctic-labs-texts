46 U.S.C. § 80303 — Speed of vessel in ice region
Citation: us:usc:46:80303
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section80303
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 803 (Ice And Derelicts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Requirement.—
The master of a vessel of the United States, when ice is reported on or near the vessel’s course, shall proceed at a moderate speed or change the course of the vessel to go well clear of the danger zone.
(b) Civil Penalty.—
A master violating this section is liable to the United States Government for a civil penalty of not more than $500.
