33 C.F.R. § 5.13 — Advancement.
Citation: us:cfr:33:5.13
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-5.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 5 (Coast Guard Auxiliary). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commandant will prescribe the circumstances and qualifications under which members of the Auxiliary may be advanced in offices and programs.
