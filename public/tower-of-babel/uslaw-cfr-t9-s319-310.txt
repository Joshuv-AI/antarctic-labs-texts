9 C.F.R. § 319.310 — Lima beans with ham in sauce, beans with ham in sauce, beans with bacon in sauce, and similar products.
Citation: us:cfr:9:319.310
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-319.310
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 319 (Definitions And Standards Of Identity Or Composition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

“Lima Beans with Ham in Sauce,” “Beans with Ham in Sauce,” “Beans with Bacon in Sauce,” and similar products shall contain not less than 12 percent ham or bacon computed on the weight of the smoked ham or bacon prior to its inclusion with the beans and sauce.
