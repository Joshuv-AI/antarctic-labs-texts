32 C.F.R. § 755.3 — Claims not cognizable.
Citation: us:cfr:32:755.3
Creator: Department Of The Navy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-755.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 755 (Claims For Injuries To Property Under Article 139 Of The Uniform Code Of Military Justice). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following claims are not cognizable under this chapter.
(a) Claims resulting from simple negligence.
(b) Claims of subrogees.
(c) Claims for personal injury or death.
(d) Claims arising from acts or omissions within the scope of employment of the offender.
(e) Claims for reimbursement for damage, loss, or destruction of Government property.
