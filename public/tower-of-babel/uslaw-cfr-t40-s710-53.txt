40 C.F.R. § 710.53 — Recordkeeping requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:40:710.53
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-710.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 710 (Compilation Of The Tsca Chemical Substance Inventory). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each person who is subject to this part must retain records that document any information reported to EPA. Records must be retained for a period of 5 years beginning on the last day of the submission period.
