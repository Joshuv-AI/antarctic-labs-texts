Charmful two-body Ω b \Omega_{b} decays in the light-front quark model
Yan-Li Wang, Yu-Kuo Hsiao, Kai-Lei Wang, Chong-Chung Lih

Abstract
We investigate the singly and doubly charmful two-body Ω b − \Omega_{b}^{-} decays using the light-front quark model. Our findings reveal that most branching fractions calculated in this study, such as ℬ ( Ω b − → Ξ − D 0 , Ξ − D ∗ 0 ) {\cal B}(\Omega_{b}^{-}\to\Xi^{-}D^{0},\Xi^{-}D^{*0}) , can be ten to one hundred times larger than those reported in previous calculations. Additionally, we interpret the ratio ℬ ⁡ ( Ω b − → Ω − ​ J / ψ ) / ℬ ⁡ ( Ω b − → Ω − ​ η c ) ≃ 3.4 {\cal B}(\Omega_{b}^{-}\to\Omega^{-}J/\psi)/{\cal B}(\Omega_{b}^{-}\to\Omega^{-}\eta_{c})\simeq 3.4 within the helicity framework. While the decay involving external W W -boson emission appears to be suppressed by the b → u ​ c ¯ ​ s b\to u\bar{c}s weak transition, it still yields a significant branching fraction. For instance, ℬ ⁡ ( Ω b − → Ξ 0 ​ D s ∗ ⁣ − ) = ( 5.9 − 14.2 ) × 10 − 5 {\cal B}(\Omega_{b}^{-}\to\Xi^{0}D_{s}^{*-})=(5.9-14.2)\times 10^{-5} , ℬ ⁡ ( Ω b − → Ξ ∗ 0 ​ D s − ) = ( 6.7 − 12.1 ) × 10 − 5 {\cal B}(\Omega_{b}^{-}\to\Xi^{*0}D_{s}^{-})=(6.7-12.1)\times 10^{-5} , and ℬ ⁡ ( Ω b − → Ξ ∗ 0 ​ D s ∗ ⁣ − ) = ( 12.8 − 26.7 ) × 10 − 5 {\cal B}(\Omega_{b}^{-}\to\Xi^{*0}D_{s}^{*-})=(12.8-26.7)\times 10^{-5} , with values reaching as large as 10 − 4 10^{-4} . These predictions are well within the experimental reach of LHCb.

In the light-front framework, we write
P=(P^{-},P^{+},P_{\bot})

with
P^{\pm}=P^{0}\pm P^{3}
and
P_{\bot}=(P^{1},P^{2})
.
Similarly,
p_{i}=(p_{i}^{-},p_{i}^{+},p_{i\bot})
, where
i=
1 or 2, and

p_{i}^{\pm}=p_{i}^{0}\pm p_{i}^{3}
,
p_{i\bot}=(p_{i}^{1},p_{i}^{2})
.
Using the identities
P^{+}P^{-}=M^{2}+P_{\bot}^{2}
and
p_{i}^{+}p_{i}^{-}={m_{i}^{2}+p_{i\bot}^{2}}
,
we reduce
P^{-}
and
p_{i}^{-}
, where
(m_{1},m_{2})=(m_{q_{1}},m_{q_{2}}+m_{q_{3}})
.
Thus,
\tilde{P}\equiv(P^{+},P_{\bot})
and
\tilde{p}_{i}\equiv(p_{i}^{+},p_{i\bot})

are applied in the light-front quark model,
with the relations

\displaystyle p^{+}_{1}=(1-x)P^{+}\,,\;p^{+}_{2}=xP^{+}\,,\;

\displaystyle p_{1\bot}=(1-x)P_{\bot}-k_{\bot}\,,\;p_{2\bot}=xP_{\bot}+k_{\bot}\,,

(20)

where
k_{\perp}
is the relative momentum from
\vec{k}=(k_{\perp},k_{z})
,
ensuring
P^{+}=p^{+}_{1}+p^{+}_{2}
and
P_{\bot}=p_{1\bot}+p_{2\bot}
.

By performing the Melosh transformation
[
37
]
,
where
e_{1,2}\equiv\sqrt{m^{2}_{1,2}+\vec{k}^{2}}
and
M_{0}\equiv e_{1}+e_{2}
,
we obtain

\displaystyle x=\frac{e_{2}-k_{z}}{e_{1}+e_{2}}\,,\quad 1-x=\frac{e_{1}+k_{z}}{e_{1}+e_{2}}\,,\quad k_{z}=\frac{xM_{0}}{2}-\frac{m^{2}_{2}+k^{2}_{\perp}}{2xM_{0}}\,,

\displaystyle M_{0}^{2}={m_{1}^{2}+k_{\bot}^{2}\over 1-x}+{m_{2}^{2}+k_{\bot}^{2}\over x}\,,

(21)

with
x
,
1-x
, and
k_{z}
being rewritten. Particularly,

(\bar{P}_{\mu}\gamma^{\mu}-M_{0})u(\bar{P},S_{z})=0
is obtained,
where
\bar{P}\equiv p_{1}+p_{2}
illustrates the internal motions
of
q_{1}
and
q_{[2,3]}
within the baryon
[
38
]
.
Consequently, the wave function is
derived as
[
39
,
40
,
34
,
42
,
41
]

\displaystyle\Psi^{SS_{z}}(\tilde{p}_{1},\tilde{p}_{2},\lambda_{1},\lambda_{2})=\frac{C_{S,A}^{(\alpha)}}{\sqrt{2(p_{1}\cdot\bar{P}+m_{1}M_{0})}}\bar{u}(p_{1},\lambda_{1})\Gamma_{S,A}^{(\alpha)}u(\bar{P},S_{z})\phi(x,k_{\perp})\,,

(22)

with the subscripts
S
and
A
denoting
the scalar and axial-vector combinations of the diquarks.
As the quark-diquark coupling vertex functions in the baryon state,

\Gamma_{S,A}^{(\alpha)}
are derived as
[
43
]

\displaystyle\Gamma_{S}=1\,,\;\Gamma_{A}=-\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\gamma_{5}\epsilon\!\!/\penalty^{*}(p_{2},\lambda_{2})\,,\;\Gamma_{A}^{\alpha}=\epsilon^{*\alpha}(p_{2},\lambda_{2})\,,

(23)

together with
C_{S,A}^{(\alpha)}
the normalization functions,
given by

\displaystyle(C_{S(A)},C_{A}^{\alpha})=\bigg(\frac{3(m_{1}M_{0}+p_{1}\cdot\bar{P})}{3m_{1}M_{0}+p_{1}\cdot\bar{P}+2(p_{1}\cdot p_{2})(p_{2}\cdot\bar{P})/m_{2}^{2}}\,,\;\frac{3m_{2}^{2}M_{0}^{2}}{2m_{2}^{2}M_{0}^{2}+(p_{2}\cdot\bar{P})^{2}}\bigg)\,.

(24)

In Eq. (

),
\phi(x,k_{\perp})
describes the momentum distribution
of constituent quarks
q_{1}
and
q_{[2,3]}
in the bound state,
presented in Gaussian form

,

,

,

,

,

,

\displaystyle\phi(x,k_{\perp})=4\left(\frac{\pi}{\beta^{2}}\right)^{3/4}\sqrt{\frac{dk_{z}}{dx}}\exp\left(\frac{-\vec{k}^{2}}{2\beta^{2}}\right)\,,

where

of the

-

And
[

\displaystyle\times\sum_{\lambda_{2}}{\rm Tr}\{u(\bar{P}^{\prime},S^{\prime}_{z})\bar{u}(\bar{P}^{\prime},S^{\prime}_{z})\left[\bar{\Gamma}^{\prime}_{S(A)}(p\!\!/\penalty_{1}^{\prime}+m_{1}^{\prime})\gamma^{\mu}(p\!\!/\penalty_{1}+m_{1})\Gamma_{S(A)}\right]u(\bar{P},S_{z})\bar{u}(\bar{P},S_{z})(\hat{\Gamma}^{\mu}_{1\over 2})_{i}\}\,,

IV
Discussions and Conclusion

The
\Omega_{b}^{-}\to\Xi^{(*)}
and
\Omega_{b}^{-}\to\Omega^{-}
transition form factors,
denoted by
F_{\Xi^{(*)}}
and
F_{\Omega}
, respectively, have been partially studied
in various models
[
24
,
20
,
23
]
. In this work,
we propose using the light-front quark model to calculate

F_{\Xi}
,
F_{\Xi^{*}}
and
F_{\Omega}
, which are applied to
investigate two-body singly and doubly charmful
\Omega_{b}^{-}
decays.
As a result, the predicted branching fractions span a range from

10^{-4}
to
10^{-8}
, as listed in Table
4
. These values are consistent with
the expected suppression due to the CKM matrix elements. For example,

|V_{cb}V_{cs}^{*}|
and
|V_{cb}V_{ud}^{*}|
correspond to
{\cal B}\simeq 10^{-4}
,
while the more suppressed
|V_{ub}V_{cd}^{*}|
leads to a much smaller
{\cal B}\simeq 10^{-8}
.

It appears that the branching fractions

{\cal B}(\Omega_{b}^{-}\to\Xi^{*-}\eta_{c},\Xi^{*-}J/\psi)
,

{\cal B}(\Omega_{b}^{-}\to\Xi^{*-}\bar{D}^{(*)0})
, and

{\cal B}(\Omega_{b}^{-}\to\Omega^{-}\eta_{c},\Omega^{-}J/\psi)
are consistent with
the results of the previous study
[
4
]
.
However, most of branching fractions in our work exhibit significant deviations.
For instance,
{\cal B}(\Omega_{b}^{-}\to\Xi^{-}D^{(*)0})
is found to be 100 times larger than
the corresponding value reported in Ref.
[
4
]
.
Nevertheless, Ref.
[
4
]
does not
provide details on the form factors, which limits our ability to identify the source of the discrepancy.
On the other hand, the available form factors from Refs.
[
24
,
23
]

can be used for a more direct comparison between our results and those of Ref.
[
4
]
.

Specifically, the dominant
\Omega_{b}^{-}\to\Xi
transition form factors
at
p^{2}=m_{D}^{2}
are given as: i)
(f_{1}^{V},f_{1}^{A})=(0.13,-0.04)
and
ii)
(f_{1}^{V},f_{1}^{A})=(0.23,-0.04)
, from Refs.
[
23
]
and
[
24
]
,
respectively, where in both cases, the value of
f_{1}^{A}
is relatively small.
Using
a_{2}=0.22
in Eq. (
32
), along with the form factors from cases i) and ii),
we obtain
{\cal B}(\Omega_{b}^{-}\to\Xi^{-}D^{0})=0.3\times 10^{-4}
and
0.9\times 10^{-4}
,
respectively. These values are approximately 20 and 60 times larger than
the value given in
[
4
]
.

In our work, the dominant
\Omega_{b}^{-}\to\Xi
form factors at
p^{2}=m_{D}^{2}

are
(f_{1}^{V},f_{1}^{A})=(0.216,0.181)
.
Notably,
f_{1}^{A}
is comparable to
f_{1}^{V}
, resulting in an enhanced
branching fraction of
{\cal B}(\Omega_{b}^{-}\to\Xi^{-}D^{0})=1.4\times 10^{-4}
,
which is larger than the corresponding values from cases i) and ii).
We therefore demonstrate that our estimation of the branching fractions is reasonable,
despite being
10-100
times greater than those reported in Ref.
[
4
]
.
Furthermore, the predicted branching fractions in Refs.
[
24
,
23
]

are found to be consistently larger than those given in Ref.
[
4
]
.

According to Table
4
, there exist certain ratios between branching fractions,
such as
{\cal B}(\Omega_{b}^{-}\to\Xi^{-}D^{*0})/{\cal B}(\Omega_{b}^{-}\to\Xi^{-}D^{0})\simeq 2
.

\displaystyle|H_{\Xi D}|^{2}

\displaystyle\simeq

\displaystyle 2C_{\Xi}[(1-C^{+}_{D})(f_{1}^{V})^{2}+(1-C^{-}_{D})(f_{1}^{A})^{2}]\,,

\displaystyle|H_{\Xi D^{*}}|^{2}

\displaystyle\simeq

\displaystyle 2C_{\Xi}\{[(1-C^{-}_{D^{*}})(f_{1}^{V})^{2}+(1-C^{+}_{D^{*}})(f_{1}^{A})^{2}]

(35)

\displaystyle+2[C_{D^{*}}^{+}(1-C^{-}_{D^{*}})(f_{1}^{V})^{2}+C_{D^{*}}^{-}(1-C^{+}_{D^{*}})(f_{1}^{A})^{2}]\}\,,

with

and

makes
(|H^{V}_{1}|^{2}+|H^{A}_{1}|^{2})/(|H^{V}_{0}|^{2}+|H^{A}_{0}|^{2})\simeq 0.26
.
We hence obtain

\displaystyle{\cal R}_{1}\equiv\frac{{\cal B}(\Omega_{b}^{-}\to\Xi^{-}D^{*0})}{{\cal B}(\Omega_{b}^{-}\to\Xi^{-}D^{0})}\simeq\frac{(m_{D^{*}}f_{D^{*}})^{2}|H_{\Xi D^{*}}|^{2}}{(m_{D}f_{D})^{2}|H_{\Xi D}|^{2}}\simeq 2\,,

(36)
