14 C.F.R. § 1206.802 — General Counsel.

Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1206.802
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1206 (Procedures For Disclosure Of Records Under The Freedom Of Information Act (Foia)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The General Counsel is responsible for the interpretation of 5 U.S.C. 552 and of this part, as well as providing legal guidance with regard to disclosure of Agency records. The General Counsel is also responsible for the handling of appeals and litigation in connection with a request for an Agency record under this part.
