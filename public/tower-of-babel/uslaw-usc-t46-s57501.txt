46 U.S.C. § 57501 — Completion of long-range program
Citation: us:usc:46:57501
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section57501
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part F (Government-Owned Merchant Vessels); Chapter 575 (Construction, Charter, And Sale Of Vessels); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whenever the Secretary of Transportation determines that the objectives and policies declared in sections 50101 and 50102 of this title cannot be fully realized within a reasonable time under title V of the Merchant Marine Act, 1936, and the President approves the determination, the Secretary, in accordance with this chapter, shall complete the long-range program described in section 50102 of this title.
