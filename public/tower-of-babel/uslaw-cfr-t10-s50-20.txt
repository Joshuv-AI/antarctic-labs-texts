10 C.F.R. § 50.20 — Two classes of licenses.
Citation: us:cfr:10:50.20
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-50.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 50 (Domestic Licensing Of Production And Utilization Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Licenses will be issued to named persons applying to the Commission therefor, and will be either class 104 or class 103.
