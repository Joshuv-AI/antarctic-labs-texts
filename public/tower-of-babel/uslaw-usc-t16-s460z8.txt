16 U.S.C. § 460z–8 — Mining restriction
Citation: us:usc:16:460z–8
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section460z–8
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LXXXIV (Oregon Dunes National Recreation Area). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The lands within the recreation area, subject to valid existing rights, are hereby withdrawn from location, entry, and patent under the United States mining laws and from disposition under all laws pertaining to mineral leasing and all amendments thereto.
