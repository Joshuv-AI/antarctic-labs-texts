Some Structures arising from the Farey Fractal
Shai Haran

Abstract
When one study the shape of Farey sequences, that is the shape of the binary planar rooted tree C ( R ) = { ( x , y ) ∈ ℕ × ℕ ∣ gcd ( x , y ) = 1 , x + y ≤ R } , C(R)=\{(x,y)\in\mathbb{N}\times\mathbb{N}\;\mid\;\gcd(x,y)=1,\;x+y\leq R\}, one rediscovers the Zeckendorf expansion of R R as a sum of non-consecutive Fibonacci numbers. With φ \varphi the golden ratio, we notice the subtle difference between identifying φ 2 ⟷ φ + 1 , hence φ n + 1 ⟷ φ n + φ n − 1 , \varphi^{2}\;\longleftrightarrow\;\varphi+1,\quad\text{hence}\quad\varphi^{n+1}\;\longleftrightarrow\;\varphi^{n}+\varphi^{n-1}, obtaining the totally disconnected, locally compact (pro finite) ”Fibonadic numbers”, versus identifying also φ ⟷ ∑ j ≥ 0 φ − 2 ​ j , hence φ n + 1 ⟷ ∑ j ≥ 0 φ n − 2 ​ j , \varphi\;\longleftrightarrow\;\sum_{j\geq 0}\varphi^{-2j},\quad\text{hence}\quad\varphi^{n+1}\;\longleftrightarrow\;\sum_{j\geq 0}\varphi^{\,n-2j}, obtaining the continuum of non-negative real numbers. This work explores new arithmetic and combinatorial structures arising from the interplay between Farey-type graphs, Fibonacci expansions, and operadic constructions. We introduce Fibonadic numbers, defined as an inverse limit under the Zeckendorf shift, equipped with a metric, order, and commutative rig structure. A normalization lemma provides canonical representatives, while quotients of the non-zero Fibonadic numbers under shifts and it’s fundamental domain covering the circle via φ \varphi –values. Levels with associated functions f z f^{z} encode the decomposition of 𝒳 \mathcal{X} into arithmetic layers. This research links number theory, combinatorics, discrete dynamics, operads, fractal geometry and the golden ratio.
