12 U.S.C. § 623 — Voluntary liquidation
Citation: us:usc:12:623
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section623
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 6 (Foreign Banking); Subchapter II (Organization Of Corporations To Do Foreign Banking). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any corporation organized under this subchapter may go into voluntary liquidation and be closed by a vote of its shareholders owning two-thirds of its stock.
