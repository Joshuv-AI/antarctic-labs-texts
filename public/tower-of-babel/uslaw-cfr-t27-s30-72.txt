27 C.F.R. § 30.72 — Recording obscuration by proprietors using the optional method for determination of proof.
Citation: us:cfr:27:30.72
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-30.72
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 30 (Gauging Manual). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any proprietor using the optional method for determination of proof for spirits containing solids of 400 milligrams or less per 100 milligrams as provided in § 30.71 shall record the obscuration so determined on the record of gauge required by 27 CFR part 19.
