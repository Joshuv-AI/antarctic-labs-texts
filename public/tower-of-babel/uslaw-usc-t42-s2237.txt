42 U.S.C. § 2237 — Modification of license
Citation: us:usc:42:2237
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2237
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 23 (Development And Control Of Atomic Energy); Subchapter XV (Judicial Review And Administrative Procedure). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The terms and conditions of all licenses shall be subject to amendment, revision, or modification, by reason of amendments of this chapter or by reason of rules and regulations issued in accordance with the terms of this chapter.
