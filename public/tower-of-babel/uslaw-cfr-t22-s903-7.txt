22 C.F.R. § 903.7 — Acknowledgment.
Citation: us:cfr:22:903.7
Creator: Foreign Service Grievance Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-903.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 903 (Initiation And Documentation Of Cases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each case received shall be acknowledged in writing by the executive secretary of the Board. If in the judgment of the executive secretary additional documentation or information is needed, he or she may request such materials.
