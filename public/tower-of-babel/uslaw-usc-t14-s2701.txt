14 U.S.C. § 2701 — Procurement of personnel
Citation: us:usc:14:2701
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2701
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 27 (Pay, Allowances, Awards, And Other Rights And Benefits); Subchapter I (Personnel Rights And Benefits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Coast Guard may expend operations and support funds for recruiting activities, including advertising and entertainment, to—
(1)
obtain recruits for the Service and cadet applicants; and
(2)
gain support of recruiting objectives from those who may assist in the recruiting effort.
