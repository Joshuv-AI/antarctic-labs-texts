30 C.F.R. § 901.20 — Approval of Alabama abandoned mine land reclamation plan.
Citation: us:cfr:30:901.20
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-901.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 901 (Alabama). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary approved the Alabama abandoned mine land reclamation plan, as submitted on May 29, 1981, and revised on August 13, 1981, effective May 20, 1982. Copies of the plan are available at:
(a) Alabama Department of Industrial Relations, 649 Monroe Street, Montgomery, AL 36131.
(b) Office of Surface Mining Reclamation and Enforcement, Birmingham Field Office, Barber Business Park, 135 Gemini Circle, Homewood, AL 35209.
