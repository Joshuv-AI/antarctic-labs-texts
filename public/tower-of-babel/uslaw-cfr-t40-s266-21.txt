40 C.F.R. § 266.21 — Standards applicable to generators and transporters of materials used in a manner that constitutes disposal.
Citation: us:cfr:40:266.21
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-266.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 266 (Standards For The Management Of Specific Hazardous Wastes And Specific Types Of Hazardous Waste Management Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Generators and transporters of materials that are used in a manner that constitutes disposal are subject to the applicable requirements of parts 262 and 263 of this chapter, and the notification requirement under section 3010 of RCRA.
