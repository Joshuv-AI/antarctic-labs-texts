27 C.F.R. § 73.1 — What does this part do?
Citation: us:cfr:27:73.1
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-73.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 73 (Electronic Signatures; Electronic Submission Of Forms). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) This part provides the conditions under which we will allow you to:
(1) Use electronic signatures or digital signatures executed to electronic forms instead of traditional handwritten signatures executed on paper forms; and
(2) Electronically submit certain forms to TTB or, where applicable, to other agencies.
(b) This part does not require you to submit forms to us electronically.
