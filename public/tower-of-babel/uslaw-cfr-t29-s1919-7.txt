29 C.F.R. § 1919.7 — Voluntary amendment or termination of accreditation.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1919.7
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1919.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1919 (Gear Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The accreditation of any person may be voluntarily amended or terminated upon written request filed with the Assistant Secretary.
