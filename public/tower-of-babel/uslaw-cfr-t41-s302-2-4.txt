41 C.F.R. § 302-2.4 — -2.4 Penalties for violation of service agreement.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-2.4
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-2.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-2 (2—Employee Eligibility Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the employee violates a service agreement (other than for reasons beyond their control and which must be accepted by the agency), the employee will have incurred a debt due to the Government and must reimburse all costs that the agency has paid towards the relocation expenses including withholding tax allowance (WTA) and relocation income tax allowance (RITA).
