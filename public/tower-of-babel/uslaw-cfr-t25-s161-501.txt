25 C.F.R. § 161.501 — When will a permit modification be effective?
Citation: us:cfr:25:161.501
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-161.501
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 161 (Navajo Partitioned Lands Grazing Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

BIA approval of a transfer, assignment, or modification under a permit will be effective immediately, notwithstanding any appeal, which may be filed under part 2 of this title. Copies of approved documents will be provided to the permittee and made available to the Navajo Nation upon request.
