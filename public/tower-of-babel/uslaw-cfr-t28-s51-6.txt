28 C.F.R. § 51.6 — Political subunits.
Citation: us:cfr:28:51.6
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-51.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 51 (Procedures For The Administration Of Section 5 Of The Voting Rights Act Of 1965, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All political subunits within a covered jurisdiction (e.g., counties, cities, school districts) that have not terminated coverage by obtaining the declaratory judgment described in section 4(a) of the Act are subject to the requirements of section 5.
