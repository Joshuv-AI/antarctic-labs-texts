24 C.F.R. § 246.4 — Applicability.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 246 (Local Rent Control). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to all projects with mortgages insured or held by HUD, except those to which subpart C applies.
