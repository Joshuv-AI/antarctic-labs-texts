49 C.F.R. § 382.715 — Authorization to enter information into the Clearinghouse.
Citation: us:cfr:49:382.715
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-382.715
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 382 (Controlled Substances And Alcohol Use And Testing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) C/TPAs. No C/TPA or other service agent may enter information into the Clearinghouse on an employer's behalf unless the employer designates the C/TPA or other service agent.
(b) SAPs. A driver must designate a SAP before that SAP can enter any information about the driver's return-to-duty process into the Clearinghouse.
