27 C.F.R. § 21.106 — Diethyl phthalate.
Citation: us:cfr:27:21.106
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-21.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 21 (Formulas For Denatured Alcohol And Rum). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Refractive index at 25 °C. 1.497 to 1.502.
(b) Color. Colorless.
(c) Odor. Practically odorless.
(d) Solubility. Soluble in 20 parts of 60 percent alcohol.
(e) Specific gravity at 25 °/25 °C. 1.115 to 1.118.
(f) Ester content (as diethyl phthalate). Not less than 99 percent by weight.
