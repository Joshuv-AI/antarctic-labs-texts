24 C.F.R. § 1005.605 — Remittance of Up-Front Loan Guarantee Fee.
Citation: us:cfr:24:1005.605
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-1005.605
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 1005 (Loan Guarantees For Indian Housing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Direct Guarantee Lender shall remit the Up-Front Loan Guarantee Fee to HUD within 15 days after settlement, using the payment system as prescribed by Section 184 Program Guidance. The Direct Guarantee Lender shall provide an account reconciliation of the Up-Front Loan Guarantee Fee in the time and manner as may be prescribed in Section 184 Program Guidance.
