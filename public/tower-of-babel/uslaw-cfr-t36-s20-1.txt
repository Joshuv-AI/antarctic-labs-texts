36 C.F.R. § 20.1 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:36:20.1
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-20.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 20 (Isle Royale National Park; Commercial Fishing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in this part:
(a) Park means Isle Royale National Park.
(b) Permittee includes all persons engaged in commercial fishing from bases in the Park, except those life lessees who were engaged in such occupation at the date of the issuance of their leases.
