7 C.F.R. § 22.103 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:7:22.103
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-22.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 22 (Rural Development Coordination). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of these regulations is to establish the policies, procedures, and responsibilities required by section 603 of the Rural Development Act.
