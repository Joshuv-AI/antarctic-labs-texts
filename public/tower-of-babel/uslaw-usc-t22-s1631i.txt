22 U.S.C. § 1631i — Limitations
Citation: us:usc:22:1631i
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1631i
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 21 (Settlement Of International Claims); Subchapter II (Vesting And Liquidation Of Bulgarian, Hungarian, And Rumanian Property). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No suit may be instituted pursuant to section 1631f(a) of this title after the expiration of one year from the date of vesting of the property in respect of which relief is sought. No return may be made pursuant to section 1631f(b) of this title unless notice of claim has been filed within one year from the date of vesting of the property in respect of which the claim is filed.
