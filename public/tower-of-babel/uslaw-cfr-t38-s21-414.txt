38 C.F.R. § 21.414 — Revision of decision.
Citation: us:cfr:38:21.414
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-21.414
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 21 (Veteran Readiness And Employment And Education). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The revision of a decision on which an action is based is subject to the following regulations:
(a) Clear and unmistakable error, § 3.105(a);
(b) Difference of opinion, § 3.105(b);
(c) Character of discharge, § 3.105(c);
(d) Severance of service-connection, § 3.105(d);
(e) Reduction to less than compensable evaluation, § 3.105(e); (See §§ 21.48, 21.322, and 21.324)
(f) Review of decisions, § 21.416.
