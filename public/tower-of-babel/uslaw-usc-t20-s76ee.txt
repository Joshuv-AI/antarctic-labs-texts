20 U.S.C. § 76ee — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:20:76ee
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section76ee
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 3 (Smithsonian Institution, National Museums And Art Galleries); Subchapter VI (Joseph H. Hirshhorn Museum And Sculpture Garden). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is authorized to be appropriated not to exceed $15,000,000 for the planning and construction of the Joseph H. Hirshhorn Museum and Sculpture Garden, and such additional sums as may be necessary for the maintenance and operation of such museum and sculpture garden.
