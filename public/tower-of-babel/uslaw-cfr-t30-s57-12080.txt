30 C.F.R. § 57.12080 — Bare conductor guards.
Citation: us:cfr:30:57.12080
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-57.12080
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 57 (Safety And Health Standards—Underground Metal And Nonmetal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Trolley wires and bare power conductors shall be guarded at mantrip loading and unloading points, and at shaft stations. Where such trolley wires and bare power conductors are less than 7 feet above the rail, they shall be guarded at all points where persons work or pass regularly beneath.
