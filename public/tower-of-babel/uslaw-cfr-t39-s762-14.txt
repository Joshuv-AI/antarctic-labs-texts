39 C.F.R. § 762.14 — Amounts for which disbursement postal money orders may be issued.
Citation: us:cfr:39:762.14
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-762.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 762 (Disbursement Postal Money Orders). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Disbursement Postal Money Orders may be issued for any amount appropriate to pay Postal Service Obligations. There is no maximum amount above which a Disbursement Postal Money Order may not be issued.
