25 C.F.R. § 81.44 — What documents are sent to the Authorizing Official?
Citation: us:cfr:25:81.44
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-81.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 81 (Secretarial Election Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Chair of the Secretarial Election Board must transmit all documents pertaining to the Secretarial election to the Authorizing Official, including:
(a) The original text of the material voted on;
(b) The Eligible Voters List;
(c) The Registered Voters List;
(d) The Secretarial Election Notice Packet;
(e) Any challenges to the Secretarial election results; and
(f) The Certificate of Results of Election.
