34 C.F.R. § 75.533 — Acquisition of real property; construction.
Citation: us:cfr:34:75.533
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-75.533
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 75 (Direct Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No grantee may use its grant for acquisition of real property or for construction unless specifically permitted by the applicable statutes and regulations.
