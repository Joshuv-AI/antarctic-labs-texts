16 C.F.R. § 1500.125 — Labeling requirements for accompanying literature.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1500.125
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1500.125
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1500 (Hazardous Substances And Articles: Administration And Enforcement Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When any accompanying literature includes or bears any directions for use (by printed word, picture, design, or combination thereof), such placard, pamphlet, booklet, book, sign, or other graphic or visual device shall bear all the information required by section 2(p) of the act (repeated in § 1500.3(b)(14)).
