43 C.F.R. § 3861.2-4 — -4 Supplemental proof of expenditures and improvements.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3861.2-4
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3861.2-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3860 (Mineral Patent Applications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the value of the labor and improvements upon a mineral claim is less than $500 at the time of survey the mineral surveyor may file with the cadastral engineer supplemental proof showing $500 expenditure made prior to the expiration of the period of publication.
