42 C.F.R. § 9.11 — Animal transport.
Citation: us:cfr:42:9.11
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-9.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 9 (Standards Of Care For Chimpanzees Held In The Federally Supported Sanctuary System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The transportation of chimpanzees by surface or air must be in accordance with the requirements set forth in the Animal Welfare Act and Regulations and the International Air Transport Association (IATA) Live Animal Regulations and guidelines, as applicable.
