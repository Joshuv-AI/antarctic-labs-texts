43 C.F.R. § 2569.403 — How do I obtain a copy of the application form?
Citation: us:cfr:43:2569.403
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2569.403
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2560 (Alaska Occupancy And Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The BLM will mail you an application form if you are determined to be an Eligible Individual under § 2569.301. If you do not receive an application in the mail, you can also obtain the form at the BIA, a Realty Service Provider's office, the BLM Public Room, or on the internet at https://www.blm.gov/alaska/2019AKNativeVetsLand.
