7 C.F.R. § 1717.1207 — RUS obligations under loan guarantees.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1717.1207
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1717.1207
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1717 (Post-Loan Policies And Procedures Common To Insured And Guaranteed Electric Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this subpart affects the obligations of RUS under loan guarantee commitments it has made to the Federal Financing Bank or other lenders.
