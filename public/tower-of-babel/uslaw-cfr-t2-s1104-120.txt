2 C.F.R. § 1104.120 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1104.120
Creator: Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1104.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1104 (Implementation Of Governmentwide Guidance For Grants And Cooperative Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) DoD Grant and Agreement Regulations or DoDGARs means the regulations in chapter I, subchapter C of title 32, Code of Federal Regulations, and chapter XI of title 2, Code of Federal Regulations.
(b) Other terms. See part 1108 of the DoDGARs for definitions of other terms used in this part.
