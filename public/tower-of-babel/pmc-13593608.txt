Celebrating new beginnings and leadership
Authors: Luisa N Borrell, Andrew W Arthur, Alissa Moore
Published: 2026

Although the work of Editors is performed behind the scenes, it has a substantial impact on the scientific community. Editorial decisions protect the quality and integrity of the published scientific record, ensure the fairness of the peer review process, and affect the opportunities of authors and reviewers. Similarly, the job of Deputy Editor can often go unnoticed, yet it is integral to the scientific publication process. This role requires good judgment, extensive knowledge of public health, and a strong desire to learn.

We are happy to announce that the Deputy Editors of Preventive Medicine and Preventive Medicine Reports are moving on to new endeavors. First, we would like to thank Dr. David Litaker, MD, PhD, who has retired from his role. We appreciate the time, knowledge, attention to detail, and tremendous effort that Dr. Litaker put into Preventive Medicine and Preventive Medicine Reports. His contributions to the editorial and peer-review processes were crucial to the success of the journals. We miss his insightful comments, rigorous approach to the editorial role, and commitment to the journals. We are also pleased to announce the transition of Dr. Ryan Burns, PhD, to the role of Editor-in-Chief of Preventive Medicine Reports. Dr. Burns has been the Deputy Editor of Preventive Medicine and Preventive Medicine Reports since November 2025 and has been an excellent addition to the team. We are excited to see Dr. Burns lead Preventive Medicine Reports and bring his vision to the role. Although we will miss his input at Preventive Medicine, we look forward to a bright future for Preventive Medicine Reports with Dr. Burns at the helm.

Being an Editor or Deputy Editor is not simply about managing the peer review process. Editors of the highest caliber are able to guide the direction of research, identify new areas of study, and tackle new challenges. Dr. Litaker and Dr. Burns have managed each of these responsibilities masterfully, ensuring that manuscripts are reviewed in a timely manner, navigating the advent of generative artificial intelligence, facilitating the publication of high-quality research, and assisting authors and reviewers throughout the peer review process. Both fostered a respectful atmosphere for the editorial team, valued honesty, and welcomed creative viewpoints and solutions.

Dr. Burns's move to Preventive Medicine Reports will see him leading a periodical that is increasingly important in the field of public health and has been receiving an increasing volume of manuscripts over the past three years. Under Dr. Burns's guidance, Preventive Medicine Reports will continue its mission of disseminating high-quality public health research to scholars, practitioners, policymakers, and communities around the world on an open-access basis.

This is not simply a goodbye; it is a new beginning. The Preventive Medicine editorial team thanks both Deputy Editors for their work and expresses gratitude for their participation and contributions. We wish Dr. Litaker all the best as he embarks on his next adventure and Dr. Burns great success as the new Editor-in-Chief of Preventive Medicine Reports. The editorial team looks forward to a new chapter for Preventive Medicine Reports and is excited about the new opportunities and collaborations it will bring.

Thank you, Dr. Litaker and Dr. Burns. Your hard work, dedication, and leadership have advanced science for the benefit of the global public health workforce and the communities they serve. Congratulations on the next phase of your journey!
