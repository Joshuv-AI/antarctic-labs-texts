45 C.F.R. § 670.9 — Antarctic Conservation Act enforcement exception.
Citation: us:cfr:45:670.9
Creator: National Science Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-670.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 670 (Conservation Of Antarctic Animals And Plants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Paragraphs (a) through (d) of § 670.4 shall not apply to acts carried out by an Antarctic Conservation Act Enforcement Officer (designated pursuant to 45 CFR 672.3) if undertaken as part of the Antarctic Conservation Act Enforcement Officer's official duties.
