22 C.F.R. § 521.15 — Ex Parte contacts.
Citation: us:cfr:22:521.15
Creator: United States Agency For Global Media
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-521.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 521 (Implementation Of The Program Fraud Civil Remedies Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No party or person (except employees of the ALJ's office) shall communicate in any way with the ALJ on any matter at issue in a case, unless on notice and opportunity for all parties to participate. This provision does not prohibit a person or party from inquiring about the status of a case or asking routine questions concerning administrative functions or procedures.
