7 C.F.R. § 322.28 — General requirements; restricted articles.
Citation: us:cfr:7:322.28
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-322.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 322 (Bees, Beekeeping Byproducts, And Beekeeping Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The following articles from any region are restricted articles:
(1) Dead bees of any genus;
(2) Beeswax for beekeeping; and
(3) Honey for bee feed.
(b) Restricted articles may only be imported into or transit the United States in accordance with this part.
