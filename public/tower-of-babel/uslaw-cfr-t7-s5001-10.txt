7 C.F.R. § 5001.10 — Federal Register notices and amendments.
Citation: us:cfr:7:5001.10
Creator: Rural Business-Cooperative Service, Rural Housing Service, And Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-5001.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 5001 (Guaranteed Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Rural Development will issue annual Federal Register notices each year specifying the amount of funds available under this part for OneRD guarantees. Notices may also include the following information applicable to projects specifically funded under a particular notice: Maximum loan amounts, fees, and priority scoring for discretionary points.
