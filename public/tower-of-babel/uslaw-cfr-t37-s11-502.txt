37 C.F.R. § 11.502 — Responsibilities of a subordinate practitioner.
Citation: us:cfr:37:11.502
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-11.502
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 11 (Representation Of Others Before The United States Patent And Trademark Office). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A practitioner is bound by the USPTO Rules of Professional Conduct notwithstanding that the practitioner acted at the direction of another person.
(b) A subordinate practitioner does not violate the USPTO Rules of Professional Conduct if that practitioner acts in accordance with a supervisory practitioner's reasonable resolution of an arguable question of professional duty.
