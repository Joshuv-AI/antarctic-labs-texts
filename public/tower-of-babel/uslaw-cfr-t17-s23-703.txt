17 C.F.R. § 23.703 — Investment of segregated initial margin.
Citation: us:cfr:17:23.703
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-23.703
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 23 (Swap Dealers And Major Swap Participants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The swap dealer or major swap participant and the counterparty may enter into any commercial arrangement, in writing, regarding the investment of Initial Margin segregated pursuant to § 23.701 and the related allocation of gains and losses resulting from such investment.
