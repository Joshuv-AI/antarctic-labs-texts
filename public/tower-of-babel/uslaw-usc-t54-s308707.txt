54 U.S.C. § 308707 — Applicability of other authorities
Citation: us:usc:54:308707
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section308707
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3087 (National Maritime Heritage). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The authorities contained in this chapter shall be in addition to, and shall not be construed to supersede or modify those contained in division A of this subtitle.
