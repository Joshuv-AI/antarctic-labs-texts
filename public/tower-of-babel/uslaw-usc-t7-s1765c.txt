7 U.S.C. § 1765c — Performance of functions in foreign localities
Citation: us:usc:7:1765c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1765c
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 43 (Foreign Market Development); Subchapter II (United States Agricultural Trade Offices). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each United States Agricultural Trade Office shall carry out its functions under section 1765b of this title in the nation where the United States Agricultural Trade Office is located and in such other nations as the Secretary of Agriculture, in consultation with the Secretary of State, may prescribe in order to carry out the purposes of this subchapter.
