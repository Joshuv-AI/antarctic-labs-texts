24 C.F.R. § 232.505 — Application and application fee.
Citation: us:cfr:24:232.505
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-232.505
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 232 (Mortgage Insurance For Nursing Homes, Intermediate Care Facilities, Board And Care Homes, And Assisted Living Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Filing of application. An application for insurance of a fire safety loan for a nursing home, intermediate care facility, assisted living facility or board and care home shall be submitted on an approved HUD form by an approved lender and by the owners of the project to the HUD office.
(b) Application fee. See 24 CFR 200.40(d)(2).
