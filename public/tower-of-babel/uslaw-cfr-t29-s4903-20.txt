29 C.F.R. § 4903.20 — Will PBGC issue a refund if money is erroneously collected on a debt?
Citation: us:cfr:29:4903.20
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4903.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4903 (Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

PBGC will promptly refund to a debtor any amount collected on a debt owed to PBGC when the debt is waived or otherwise found not to be owed to the United States, or as otherwise required by law.
