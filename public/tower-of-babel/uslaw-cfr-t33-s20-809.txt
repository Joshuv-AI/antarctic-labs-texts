33 C.F.R. § 20.809 — Stipulations.
Citation: us:cfr:33:20.809
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-20.809
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 20 (Rules Of Practice, Procedure, And Evidence For Formal Administrative Proceedings Of The Coast Guard). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any party or interested person may stipulate, in writing, at any stage of the proceeding, or orally at the hearing, to any pertinent fact or other matter fairly susceptible of stipulation. A stipulation binds all parties to it.
