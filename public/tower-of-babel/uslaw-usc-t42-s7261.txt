42 U.S.C. § 7261 — Acquisition of copyrights, patents, etc.
Citation: us:usc:42:7261
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section7261
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 84 (Department Of Energy); Subchapter VI (Administrative Provisions); Part C (General Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to acquire any of the following described rights if the property acquired thereby is for use by or for, or useful to, the Department:
(1)
copyrights, patents, and applications for patents, designs, processes, and manufacturing data;
(2)
licenses under copyrights, patents, and applications for patents; and
(3)
releases, before suit is brought, for past infringement of patents or copyrights.
