48 C.F.R. § 249.7001 — 249.7001 Congressional notification on significant contract terminations.
Citation: us:cfr:48:249.7001
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-249.7001
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 249 (Termination Of Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Congressional notification is required for any termination involving a reduction in employment of 100 or more contractor employees. Proposed terminations must be cleared through department/agency liaison offices before release of the termination notice, or any information on the proposed termination, to the contractor. Follow the procedures at PGI 249.7001 for congressional notification and release of information.
