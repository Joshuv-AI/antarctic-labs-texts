14 C.F.R. § 1261.310 — Investigation of claims.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1261.310
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1261.310
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1261 (Processing Of Monetary Claims (General)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The officials designated in § 1261.308 shall conduct such investigation of a claim as deemed appropriate. The officials may request any NASA office or other Federal agency to assist in the investigation.
