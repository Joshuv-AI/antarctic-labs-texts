22 C.F.R. § 134.21 — Filing and service of documents.
Citation: us:cfr:22:134.21
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-134.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 134 (Equal Access To Justice Act; Implementation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any application for an award or other pleading or document related to an application shall be filed and served on all parties to the proceeding in the same manner as other pleadings in the proceeding, except as provided in § 134.12(b) for confidential financial information.
