QFT Realization of Non-Unitary 𝖘 ​ 𝖑 ​ ( 𝟐 , ℂ ) \mathfrak{sl}(2,\mathbb{C}) WRT Invariants and Their Galois Conjugations
Kibok Jeong, Soochang Lee

Abstract
We propose a field theoretic realization of the non-unitary 𝔰 ​ 𝔩 ​ ( 2 , ℂ ) \mathfrak{sl}(2,\mathbb{C}) Witten-Reshetikhin-Turaev Topological Quantum Field Theory(WRT TQFT). The WRT TQFT at the principal root of unity is unitary. It is known to be realized by SU ⁡ ( 2 ) \mathrm{SU}(2) Chern-Simons theory. However, the WRT TQFT at a non-principal root of unity is non-unitary. Its field theoretic realization has remained unclear. We propose that such a non-unitary TQFT arises from the topological twist of the 3-dimensional 𝒩 = 4 \mathcal{N}=4 rank-0 theory constructed by joining multiple T ⁡ [ SU ⁡ ( 2 ) ] T[\mathrm{SU}(2)] theories. We construct its modular matrices and identify them with those of the WRT TQFT, establishing a concrete relation between the parameters, up to a decoupled unitary TQFT.

A.3.1
Odd
k
: integer-spin subcategory

A particularly interesting structure emerges when the level
k
is an odd integer. In this case, the set of simple objects
\{\left|j\right\rangle\}_{j=0}^{k/2}
naturally splits into two sectors: One with integer spins
j\in\{0,1,\dots,(k-1)/2\}
, and the other one with half-integer spins
j\in\{1/2,3/2,\dots,k/2\}
. The fusion rules exhibit a
\mathbb{Z}_{2}
-grading with respect to this decomposition. The key object responsible for this symmetry is the highest-spin representation
\left|k/2\right\rangle
. Because
k
is odd,
k/2
is a half-integer. This simple object is an
invertible line
, or a simple current, meaning that its fusion inverse exists. Specifically, its fusion square is the tensor unit

\left|k/2\right\rangle\otimes\left|k/2\right\rangle\cong\left|0\right\rangle.

(58)

Tensoring any object with this current
\left|k/2\right\rangle
implements the
\mathbb{Z}_{2}
symmetry, mapping integer-spin objects to half-integer spin objects (
\left|j\right\rangle\otimes\left|k/2\right\rangle=\left|k/2-j\right\rangle
) and vice-versa.

We can then perform a procedure known as ‘gauging’ or ‘orbifolding’ this
\mathbb{Z}_{2}
symmetry. This amounts to restricting the category to the objects in the trivial sector of the grading, which are the integer-spin representations. This subcategory is itself a consistent, unitary MTC.

A.3.2
Even
m
: non-trivial Galois conjugation

Recall that the categorical level Galois conjugation of MTC is associated with
\mathrm{Gal}_{C}=\textrm{Gal}(\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{4(k+2)})/\mathbb{Q})
which does not admit an even
m
since it requires
\textrm{gcd}(m,4(k+2))=1
. However, the algebraic level Galois conjugation allows
\textrm{Gal}(\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{k+2})/\mathbb{Q})
transformed theory. This exactly corresponds to the case when
m
is even and therefore
k
is odd. This choice of

is given by:

(59)

There is the crucial identity connects quantum dimensions of two objects which their spins are symmetric respect to

\dim_{x}(\left|k/2-j\right\rangle)=(-1)^{m-1}\dim_{x}(\left|j\right\rangle).

(60)

This relation governs the modular properties of the theory. When
m
is odd,
m-1
is even, and the relation becomes a symmetric

\dim_{x}(\left|k/2-j\right\rangle)=\dim_{x}(\left|j\right\rangle).

(61)

The

implies that

\dim_{x}(\left|k/2-j\right\rangle)=-\dim_{x}(\left|j\right\rangle).

(62)

This is not merely a feature, but a direct cause of degeneracy. This anti-symmetricity reflects a linear dependence between the columns of the
S
matrix itself, forcing its determinant to be zero. The presence of these “ghost” objects, where the direct sum
\left|j\right\rangle\oplus\left|k/2-j\right\rangle
has zero total quantum dimension, enlarges the negligible category
\mathcal{N}
. Fortunately, since

is odd,

is an integer if and only if

is a half-integer. The pairing of objects with opposite-sign quantum dimensions always occurs between the integer-spin and half-integer spin sectors. Therefore, additional quotient by that enlarged part can be understood as a orbifolding the
\mathbb{Z}_{2}
symmetry, restricting only to the integer-spin subcategory.
