7 C.F.R. § 66.11 — Severability.
Citation: us:cfr:7:66.11
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-66.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 66 (National Bioengineered Food Disclosure Standard). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If any provision of this part is declared invalid or the applicability thereof to any person or circumstances is held invalid, the validity of the remainder of this part or the applicability thereof to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
