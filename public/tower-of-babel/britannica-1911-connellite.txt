CONNELLITE, a rare mineral species, a hydrous copper
chloro-sulphate. Cu 15 (Cl,OH) 4 SO 16 ·15H 2 O, crystallizing in
the hexagonal system. It occurs as tufts of very delicate
acicular crystals of a fine blue colour, and is associated with other
copper minerals of secondary origin, such as cuprite and malachite.
Its occurrence in Cornwall was noted by Philip Rashleigh
in 1802, and it was first examined chemically by Arthur Connell
in 1847. Outside Cornwall it has been found only in Namaqualand
in South Africa.
