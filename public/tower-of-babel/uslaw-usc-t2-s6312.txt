2 U.S.C. § 6312 — Clerks to Senators-elect
Citation: us:usc:2:6312
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section6312
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 63 (Senate Members); Subchapter II (Employees, Expenses, And Allowances). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A Senator entitled to receive his own salary may appoint the usual clerical assistants allowed Senators.
