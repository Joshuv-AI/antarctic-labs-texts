19 C.F.R. § 358.101 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:19:358.101
Creator: International Trade Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-358.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 358 (Supplies For Use In Emergency Relief Work). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth the procedures for importation of supplies for use in emergency relief work free of antidumping and countervailing duties, as authorized under section 318(a) of the Act.
