25 C.F.R. § 89.40 — General policy.
Citation: us:cfr:25:89.40
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-89.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 89 (Attorney Contracts With Indian Tribes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In ordinary circumstances, legal services with respect to trust resources are provided for Indian tribe(s):
(a) By private counsel employed by tribes when such tribe is financially able and elects to do so, or
(b) By the United States as trustee through the Office of the Solicitor and/or the Department of Justice.
