25 U.S.C. § 5355 — Eligibility for funds of tribe or tribal organization controlling or managing private schools
Citation: us:usc:25:5355
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section5355
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 46 (Indian Self-Determination And Education Assistance); Subchapter III (Indian Education Assistance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized and directed to provide funds, pursuant to this chapter; the the 1 Act of April 16, 1934 (48 Stat. 596), as amended [25 U.S.C. 5342 et seq.]; or any other authority granted to him to any tribe or tribal organization which controls and manages any previously private school.
