33 C.F.R. § 106.120 — Noncompliance.
Citation: us:cfr:33:106.120
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-106.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 106 (Marine Security: Outer Continental Shelf (Ocs) Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When an OCS facility must temporarily deviate from the requirements of this part, the OCS facility owner or operator must notify the cognizant District Commander, and either suspend operations or request and receive permission from the District Commander to continue operating.
