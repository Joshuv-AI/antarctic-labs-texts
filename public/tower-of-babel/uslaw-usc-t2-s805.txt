2 U.S.C. § 805 — Regional award directors of program; appointment criteria
Citation: us:usc:2:805
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section805
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 19 (Congressional Award Program); Subchapter I (Congressional Award Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Regional award directors may be appointed by the Board, upon recommendation of the Director, for any State or other appropriate geographic area of the United States. The Director shall make such recommendations with respect to a State or geographic area only after soliciting recommendations regarding such appointments from public and private youth organizations within such State or geographic area.
