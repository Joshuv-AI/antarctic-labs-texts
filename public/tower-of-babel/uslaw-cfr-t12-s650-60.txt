12 C.F.R. § 650.60 — Final discharge and release of the receiver.
Citation: us:cfr:12:650.60
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-650.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 650 (Federal Agricultural Mortgage Corporation General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After the receiver has made a final distribution of the assets of the receivership, the receivership shall be terminated, the charter shall be canceled by the Farm Credit Administration Board if such cancellation has not previously occurred, and the receiver shall be finally discharged and released.
