45 C.F.R. § 1610.10 — Compliance.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1610.10
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1610.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1610 (Use Of Non-Lsc Funds; Program Integrity). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to all other compliance and enforcement options, LSC may recover from a recipient's LSC funds an amount not to exceed the amount improperly charged to non-LSC funds, as provided in § 1630.16 of this chapter.
