20 C.F.R. § 701.505 — May an employer stop paying benefits awarded before February 17, 2009 if the employee would now fall within the exclusion?
Citation: us:cfr:20:701.505
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-701.505
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 701 (General; Administering Agency; Definitions And Use Of Terms). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. If an individual was awarded compensation for an injury occurring before February 17, 2009, the employer must still pay all benefits awarded, including disability compensation and medical benefits, even if the employee would be excluded from coverage under the amended exclusion.
