42 U.S.C. § 11713 — Compliance with Budget Act
Citation: us:usc:42:11713
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section11713
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 122 (Native Hawaiian Health Care). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any new spending authority (described in subsection (c)(2)(A) or (B) of section 651 1 of title 2) which is provided under this chapter shall be effective for any fiscal year only to such extent or in such amounts as are provided in appropriation Acts.
