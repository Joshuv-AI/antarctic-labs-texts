λ \lambda -matchability in cubic graphs
Santhosh Raghul, Nishad Kothari

Abstract
A vertex v v of a 2 2 -connected cubic graph G G is λ \lambda -matchable if G G has a spanning subgraph in which v v has degree three whereas every other vertex has degree one, and we let λ ⁡ ( G ) \lambda(G) denote the number of such vertices. Clearly, λ = 0 \lambda=0 for bipartite graphs; ergo, we define λ \lambda -matchable pairs analogously, and we let ρ ⁡ ( G ) \rho(G) denote the number of such pairs. We improve the constant lower bounds on both λ \lambda and ρ \rho established recently by Chen, Lu and Zhang [Discrete Math., 2025] using matching-theoretic invariants arising from the seminal work of Lovász [J. Combin. Theory Ser. B, 1987], and we characterize all of the tight examples. We also solve the problem posed by Chen, Lu and Zhang: characterize 2 2 -connected cubic graphs each of whose vertices is λ \lambda -matchable.
