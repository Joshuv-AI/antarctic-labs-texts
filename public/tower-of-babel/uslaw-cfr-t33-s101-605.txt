33 C.F.R. § 101.605 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:33:101.605
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-101.605
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 101 (Maritime Security: General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) This subpart applies to the owners and operators of U.S.-flagged vessels, facilities, and OCS facilities required to have a security plan under 33 CFR parts 104, 105, and 106.
(b) This subpart does not apply to any foreign-flagged vessels subject to 33 CFR part 104.
