33 C.F.R. § 157.120 — Waiver of required documents.
Citation: us:cfr:33:157.120
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-157.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 157 (Rules For The Protection Of The Marine Environment Relating To Tank Vessels Carrying Oil In Bulk). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Coast Guard waives the requirement for the letter under § 157.116(b), if a U.S. tank vessel engages in a voyage, or under § 157.118(b)(2)(ii), if a foreign tank vessel enters the navigable waters of the United States or transfers cargo at a port or place subject to the jurisdiction of the United States, for the purpose of being inspected under § 157.140.
