Optical coherence tomography angiography findings in ADHD
Authors: Hany Mahmoud, Mohamed Salah Hamed Korishy, Ahmed Ezzat Amin, Islam Awny, Dalia Tohamy
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractBackgroundAttention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder ADHD is a chronic neurodevelopmental disorder that affects both youngs and adults with an impact on the eyes and visual structure and functions.PurposeThe aim of this study is to assess the Optical Coherence Tomography (OCT) Angiography findings in adult patients with ADHD.MethodsThis case-control study included 100 participants, grouped into two groups. The adult ADHD group (A) included 50 cases, and the control group (B) included 50 normal. Both groups were evaluated ophthalmologicaly and imaged by OCT angiography to assess the retinal thickness and vasculature.ResultsBoth groups were matched regarding age, sex, BCVA, IOP, average K, axial L, and SER with no significant difference. The ADHD group showed decreased macular thickness, decreased peripapillary retinal nerve fiber layer thickness, and decreased vascularity in all quadrants compared to the control participants, P < 0.05.ConclusionPatients with ADHD showed decreased macular thickness, peripapillary RNFL and retinal vasculature, which support the ocular affection in patients with ADHD.ContributionThis study contributes to the understanding of retinal changes in patients with ADHD.Clinical trial numberNot applicable.Keywords: ADHD, Retina, Retinal vasculature, OCT, Adults

Introduction

Attention deficit hyperactivity disorder (ADHD) is a chronic neurodevelopmental disorder, which is associated with decreased attention along with hyperactivity [1]. It affects both youngs and adults. It affects 2.5% of the world population. The presentation of ADHD is combined and mixed [2].

However, the exact etiology is unknown, but it is proved to be genetically related with some investigated genetic abnormalities. The exaggeration by environmental factors is also suggested [3, 4].

Many researchers found brain abnormalities in ADHD patients with neuroimaging; it is assumed that there is delayed both brain and cortical maturation in comparison to normal neurotypes [5]. Neurotransmitters dysregulation significantly affect the etiology of ADHD. Dopamine is a central neurotransmitter, with profound effects on the visual and retinal functions, including contrast sensitivity and color perception. The roles of dopamine in the brain and the retina enforce the relation and the affection of the retina in case of ADHD, as the dopaminergic hypothesis claims [6, 7].

Many visual problems were reported in ADHD patients related to depth perception and visual processing. The intimate relationship between the retina and the central nervous system reinforces visual affection, as the retina is considered a part of the CNS [8, 9].

Some studies investigated the macular thickness in young patients with ADHD and others investigated the vascularity either in treated patients or untreated patients. In this study we are trying to investigate larger study group in adult ADHD patients, and assessing both retinal thickness and vascularity.

Optical coherence tomography (OCT) angiography is a non-invasive technique, enabling the investigation of retinal and choroidal architecture, providing high-quality imaging to assess the retinal structure and vasculature [10].

The aim of the study

The study aims to identify both retinal thickness and retinal vascular abnormalities using OCT angiography imaging of adult ADHD patients.

Patients and methods

Calculation of the sample size

Utilizing the G*power statistical program V.3.1.9.4, according to the following data: means ± SDs of the VD of the whole macula 14.67 ± 0.63 and 15.75 ± 0.35 of ADHD and control groups respectively according to results of previous studies making an effect size of 0.55 (2), α = 0.05, and power = 0.80. After calculation, the size of the sample was 50 participants for each study group.

Study design

In this case-control study, 50 adults with ADHD were enrolled. They were compared to 50 normal adults. The study was approved by the Sohag Medical Ethical Committee (Soh-med-24-07-06PD) in accordance with Helsinki declaration and conducted in both the Ophthalmology and Neuropsychiatry Departments of Sohag and Assiut Universities between July 2024 and April 2025.informed written consent was taken from each participant.

The inclusion criteria included adult patients with ADHD, more than 18 years old, diagnosed since childhood, and receiving only methylphenidate treatment for at least six months before the study.

Exclusion criteria included patients with mental retardation, other psychiatry disorders, neurological illnesses, drug abuse, previous head trauma, or alcohol abuse. Regarding the eye patients with cataract, glaucoma, corneal opacity, retinal diseases, or high refractive errors, exclusion criteria were applied. Patients with systemic diseases that could affect the retina or the brain, such as diabetics, were excluded.

All participants, including those with ADHD and normal controls, were evaluated by a psychiatrist. Regarding ocular examination, they were evaluated for BCVA, intraocular pressure, average keratometry K, axial length L, spherical equivalent refraction SER, a detailed examination of both anterior and posterior segments, and OCT angiography.

OCT angiography

OCT angiography; (Optovue, Inc., Freemont, CA) is a non-invasive maneuver used to assess the macular thickness, peripapillary RNFL thickness and vascular density in the macula and peripapillary area. Patients were examined by the same physician with 3 × 3 mm scan area in the macula and 4.5 × 4.5 mm scan area in the disc.

Assessment of ADHD: by.

Wender utah rating scale

The reduced version of this scale consists of 25 items that differentiate ADHD patients from normal. It is a 5-point scale in the range: Not at all -very much.

Statistical analysis

The data analysis was conducted making use of SPSS V.26 (Statistical Package for the Social Sciences). We used descriptive statistics to identify and make a summary of the entire data; percentages and numbers for qualitative data and mean ± standard deviation for quantitative data. We utilized the Mann-Whitney U test or the independent sample t-test in order to detect the association between groups, and significant values were assessed at p < 0.05.

Results

The present study enrolled 100 eyes of 50 participants with ADHD and 100 eyes of 50 controls. Age, sex, intraocular pressure IOP, best corrected visual acuity BCVA, average keratometry K, axial length L, and spherical equivalent refraction SER were matched between both groups with insignificant difference.

The macular thickness was 253.39 ± 10.08 μm in group (A) and 254.68 ± 11.97 μm in group (B) p < 0.001. The foveal thickness was 215.89 ± 8.58 μm in group (A) and 216.95 ± 7.25 μm in group (B) p < 0.001. Regarding the peri-papillary RNFL; it was 98.36 ± 12.25 μm in the patient group (A) and 99.27 ± 9.28 μm in the control group (B) p < 0.001.the values showed significant statistical difference between both groups (Figs. 1 and 2).

Discussion

ADHD is one the commonest neurodevelopmental disorders. it’s characterized by inattention and hyperactivity. Many researchers investigated this disorder and suggested abnormal both brain structure and function; with delayed cortical maturation [1, 3]. The intimate relation between the eye and the central nervous system enforces the ocular affection in cases of ADHD [11]. It was found that, there is an intimate relation between the ADHD and the brain neurotransmitters dysregulation especially Dopamine, which is crucial neurotransmitter [12].

Many previous studies investigated retinal affection in other neurodevelopmental disorders such as schizophrenia, and found that there are changes in the macula thickness and macular vasculature, which could be due to associated developmental abnormality in the retina. Furthermore, chronic toxic degeneration in the macula could occur either due to drug toxicity or neurotransmitters dysregulation [13].

Some studies investigated the relationship between ADHD and the macular thickness and vasculature. The results were variable; some found that there is global thinning and others found areas of thinning and alternative areas of increased thickness and vasculature [14, 15]. This difference could be due to different study groups, ages and duration of medication.

Our results were similar to Sánchez et al. [16] we found that the macular thickness decreases significantly. The macular thinning could be explained by developmental abnormalities with tendency to immaturity of nervous tissues in patients with ADHD. Both drug toxicity and neurotransmitters dysregulation have an influence.

We found global decrease in vascularity either in deep or superficial macular layers. This is similar to Tarakcioglu et al. [17] Ramzi et al. supported that in his meta-analysis [18]. In our study, we studied larger sample size and investigated all parameters.

Li et al. supported the peripapillary RNFL thinning in youngs with ADHD; the thinning was explained by developmental abnormalities [19]. Also Kaymak et al. found the same results in adults [20]. Choi et al. [21] applied theses retinal and RNFL changes in machine learning to screen patients with ADHD. This is similar to our results. The decreased vascular density is associated with decreased blood flow in the optic disc. This occurs also in many other conditions causing decreased vascular density such glaucoma and anterior ischemic optic neuropathy [22].

The medications used in ADHD treatment could have an impact on the macular thickness and macular vascularity. The effect of Methylphenidate, a first line treatment, was investigated and some studies found that, it could increase the macular thickness; in other word, it could normalize the retinal thickness and retinal vasculature. However, this is still uncertain with other studies suggesting the reverse [23].

In our study, however, study group was on regular treatment; the macular thickness and vascularity showed thinning. The effect of medications on the macular thickness and vasculature still need further evaluation, and we have to consider the type and duration of the treatment.

In this study, we evaluated all parameters including macular thickness, macular vascularity in superficial and deep layers, and peripapillary RNFL vascularity in small and large vessels. We considered larger sample size.

Limitations

Small sample size, we need to study more patients to clarify the impact on the retina and retinal vasculature.

Lack of longitudinal studies, we have to follow up patients with ADHD for longer durations.

The treatment duration and the treatment medications should be in consideration as it could affect the retinal thickness and vasculature.

Conclusion

Patients with ADHD showed abnormal macular thickness and retinal vasculature, which supports the ocular affection in patients with ADHD.

Global retinal thinning was found with global vascular thinning, this could be due to developmental abnormalities and drug effect which is uncertain. The medications effects on the retina and retinal vasculature need more evaluation.
