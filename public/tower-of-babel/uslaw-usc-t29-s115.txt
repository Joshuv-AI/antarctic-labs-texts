29 U.S.C. § 115 — Repeal of conflicting acts
Citation: us:usc:29:115
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section115
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 6 (Jurisdiction Of Courts In Matters Affecting Employer And Employee). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All acts and parts of acts in conflict with the provisions of this chapter are repealed.
