10 U.S.C. § 7011 — Organization
Citation: us:usc:10:7011
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7011
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization); Chapter 703 (Department Of The Army). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Department of the Army is separately organized under the Secretary of the Army. It operates under the authority, direction, and control of the Secretary of Defense.
