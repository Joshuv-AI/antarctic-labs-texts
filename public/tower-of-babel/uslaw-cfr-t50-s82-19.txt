50 C.F.R. § 82.19 — Patents and inventions.
Citation: us:cfr:50:82.19
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-82.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 82 (Administrative Procedures For Grants-In-Aid (Marine Mammal Protection Act Of 1972)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Determination of the patent rights in any inventions or discoveries resulting from work under cooperative agreements entered into pursuant to the Act shall be governed by the “Government Patent Policy,” President's Memorandum for Heads of Executive Departments and Agencies, August 23, 1971, and statement of government patent policy as printed in 36 FR 16889.
