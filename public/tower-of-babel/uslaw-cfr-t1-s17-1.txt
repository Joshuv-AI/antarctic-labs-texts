1 C.F.R. § 17.1 — Receipt and processing.
Citation: us:cfr:1:17.1
Creator: Administrative Committee Of The Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-1/section-17.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 1 (General Provisions), Part 17 (Filing For Public Inspection And Publication Schedules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-23 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless special arrangements are made with the Director of the Federal Register, the Office of the Federal Register receives documents only during official working hours. Upon receipt, each document shall be held for confidential processing until it is filed for public inspection.
