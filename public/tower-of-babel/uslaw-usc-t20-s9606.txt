20 U.S.C. § 9606 — Priorities
Citation: us:usc:20:9606
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section9606
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 76 (Education Research, Statistics, Evaluation, Information, And Dissemination); Subchapter II (Educational Technical Assistance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall establish priorities for the regional educational laboratories (established under section 9564 of this title) and comprehensive centers (established under section 9602 of this title) to address, taking onto account the regional assessments conducted under section 9605 of this title and other relevant regional surveys of educational needs, to the extent the Secretary deems appropriate.
