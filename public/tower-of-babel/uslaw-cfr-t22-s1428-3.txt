22 C.F.R. § 1428.3 — Board decision.
Citation: us:cfr:22:1428.3

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1428.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1428 (Enforcement Of Assistant Secretary Standards Of Conduct Decisions And Orders). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A decision and order of the Assistant Secretary shall be enforced unless it is arbitrary and capricious or based upon manifest disregard of the law.
(b) The Board shall issue its decision on the case enforcing, enforcing as modified, refusing to enforce, or remanding the decision and order of the Assistant Secretary.
