16 U.S.C. § 590q — Coverage; “State” defined; short title
Citation: us:usc:16:590q
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section590q
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 3B (Soil Conservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
This chapter shall apply to the States, the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico, Guam, American Samoa, the Northern Mariana Islands, and the Virgin Islands, and, as used in this chapter, the term “State” includes Puerto Rico, Guam, American Samoa, the Northern Mariana Islands, and the Virgin Islands.
(b)
This chapter may be cited as the “Soil Conservation and Domestic Allotment Act”.
