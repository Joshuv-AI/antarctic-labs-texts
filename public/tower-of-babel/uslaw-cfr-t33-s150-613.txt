33 C.F.R. § 150.613 — What are the requirements for a noise monitoring and hearing protection survey?
Citation: us:cfr:33:150.613
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-150.613
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 150 (Deepwater Ports: Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The deepwater port operator must measure noise and provide hearing protection in accordance with 29 CFR 1910.95.
(b) The initial noise survey for a deepwater port must be completed within one year of beginning operations.
