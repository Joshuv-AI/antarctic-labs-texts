45 C.F.R. § 309.165 — What recourse does a Tribe or Tribal organization have to dispute a determination to disallow Tribal IV-D program expenditures?
Citation: us:cfr:45:309.165
Creator: Office Of Child Support Services, Administration Of Families And Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-309.165
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 309 (Tribal Child Support Enforcement (Iv-D) Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a Tribe or Tribal organization disputes a decision to disallow Tribal IV-D program expenditures, the grant appeals procedures outlined in 45 CFR part 16 are applicable.
