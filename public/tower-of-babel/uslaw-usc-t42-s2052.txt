42 U.S.C. § 2052 — Research by Commission
Citation: us:usc:42:2052
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2052
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 23 (Development And Control Of Atomic Energy); Subchapter III (Research). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commission is authorized and directed to conduct, through its own facilities, activities and studies of the types specified in section 2051 of this title.
