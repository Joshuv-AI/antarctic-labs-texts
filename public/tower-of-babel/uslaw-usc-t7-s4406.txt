7 U.S.C. § 4406 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:7:4406
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section4406
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 75 (International Carriage Of Perishable Foodstuffs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary of Agriculture for the fiscal year beginning October 1, 1982, and for each fiscal year thereafter, such sums as are necessary to carry out the provisions of this chapter, but not to exceed $100,000 in any fiscal year.
