48 C.F.R. § 437.204 — 437.204 Guidelines for determining availability of personnel.
Citation: us:cfr:48:437.204
Creator: Chapter 4—Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-437.204
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 437 (Service Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The HCA is authorized to request the use of non-Government evaluators in proposal evaluations. Each decision shall be supported by a written determination in accordance with FAR 37.204.
