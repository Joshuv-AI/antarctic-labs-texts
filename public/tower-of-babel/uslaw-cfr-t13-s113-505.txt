13 C.F.R. § 113.505 — Employment criteria.
Citation: us:cfr:13:113.505
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-113.505
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 113 (Nondiscrimination In Financial Assistance Programs Of Sba—Effectuation Of Policies Of Federal Government And Sba Administrator). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A recipient shall not administer or operate any test or other criterion for any employment opportunity that has a disproportionately adverse effect on persons on the basis of sex unless:
(a) Use of such test or other criterion is shown to predict validly successful performance in the position in question; and
(b) Alternative tests or criteria for such purpose, which do not have such disproportionately adverse effect, are shown to be unavailable.
