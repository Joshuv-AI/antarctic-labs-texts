45 C.F.R. § 2516.510 — What happens if the Corporation rejects a State's application for an allotment grant?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2516.510
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2516.510
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2516 (School-Based Service-Learning Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Corporation rejects a State's application for an allotment grant under § 2516.600(b)(2), the Corporation will—
(a) Promptly notify the State of the reasons for the rejection;
(b) Provide the State with a reasonable opportunity to revise and resubmit the application;
(c) Provide technical assistance, if necessary; and
(d) Promptly reconsider the resubmitted application and make a decision.
