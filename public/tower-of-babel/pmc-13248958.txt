Evolutionary Frontiers in Urology: Expanding Clinical Care Through Devices and Intelligence
Authors: Sung-Jong Eun
Published: 2026

As urology intersects with new technologies and evolving clinical paradigms, researchers are uncovering valuable insights into patient-centered care and advanced diagnostics. Furthermore, following traditional machine learning and deep learning, the recent proliferation of generative artificial intelligence (AI) and large language models continues to profoundly impact our medical reality. To address these shifting dynamics within urology, this issue is published under the theme “Evolutionary Frontiers in Urology: Expanding Clinical Care Through Devices and Intelligence.”

This issue explores the primary applications of digital healthcare technologies, including image and signal recognition, innovative wearable devices, biopsychosocial approaches, and specialized chatbot frameworks for patient management. This issue features papers on the comprehensive application and integration of digital technology in clinical practice. Within the urological field, it reports on diverse technological use cases— from general predictive analytics to advanced deep learning and natural language interactions. First, four papers highlight the necessity of multidimensional approaches in managing urinary tract and voiding conditions. Specifically, evaluating anatomical biomarkers [1] and utilizing implantable wireless stimulators [2] demonstrate how technological intervention can fundamentally expand treatment options for urinary dysfunction. Moreover, ensuring daily clinical feasibility is emphasized through automated devices [3]. Alongside hardware, the critical importance of integrating the patient environment is underscored by a biopsychosocial approach [4], reminding us that comprehensive care must extend beyond pharmacological outcomes to include family dynamics and behavioral adaptation.

As application examples, research on computer vision and neural networks for deep learning-based image analysis continues to establish itself as essential for clinical decision support. For instance, one study quantitatively demonstrates the performance of deep learning in critical organ segmentation under simulated radiation dose constraints [5]. These continuous evolutionary milestones are thoroughly examined in a comprehensive review [6], which categorizes the paradigm shift from mobile-based monitoring platforms and CNN-based diagnostic systems to generative vision transformers and large language models.

Additionally, the clinical applicability of generative AI technology and natural language models, currently the hottest trends in digital health, is proactively discussed. As an investigative framework on this topic, a targeted study [7] traces the transformation of AI-driven conversational agents across the patient journey—ranging from early screening and patient education to clinical decision support and postoperative follow-up. It explicitly highlights how these models are rapidly shifting from basic administrative assistants into reliable clinical partners.

From specialized medical devices and deep learning segmentations to the clinical integration of generative AI models, these recent studies highlight the transformative potential of technology and patient-centric solutions in healthcare. In urology, the convergence of clinical innovation, technical reproducibility, and digital health infrastructure underlines a broader shift to-ward personalized care. As these innovations are progressively embraced, maintaining high diagnostic accuracy, ensuring strict data privacy, and fostering empathic communication will be essential to fully realizing the potential of this new digital ecosystem in urology.
