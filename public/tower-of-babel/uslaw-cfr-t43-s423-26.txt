43 C.F.R. § 423.26 — Public events and gatherings.
Citation: us:cfr:43:423.26
Creator: Bureau Of Reclamation, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-423.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 423 (Public Conduct On Bureau Of Reclamation Facilities, Lands, And Waterbodies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must not conduct public assemblies, meetings, gatherings, demonstrations, parades, and other events without a permit issued pursuant to subpart D of this part 423. Public gatherings that involve the possession or occupancy of Reclamation facilities, lands, and waterbodies are governed by 43 CFR part 429.
