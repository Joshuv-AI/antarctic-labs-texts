36 C.F.R. § 1201.9 — What rights do I have as a debtor?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1201.9
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1201.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1201 (Collection Of Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As a debtor you have several basic rights. You have a right to:
(a) Notice as set forth in these regulations (see § 1201.14);
(b) Inspect the records that NARA has used to determine that you owe a debt (see § 1201.14);
(c) Request review of the debt and possible payment options (see § 1201.17);
(d) Propose a voluntary repayment agreement (see § 1201.19); and/or
(e) Question if the debt is excluded from these regulations (see § 1201.5(b)).
