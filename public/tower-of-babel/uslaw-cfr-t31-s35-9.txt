31 C.F.R. § 35.9 — Compliance with applicable laws.
Citation: us:cfr:31:35.9
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-35.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 35 (Pandemic Relief Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A recipient must comply with all other applicable Federal statutes, regulations, and executive orders, and a recipient shall provide for compliance with the American Rescue Plan Act, this subpart, and any interpretive guidance by other parties in any agreements it enters into with other parties relating to these funds.
