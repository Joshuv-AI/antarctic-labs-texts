Quantum-inspired improved African vultures optimization algorithm for efficient placement of IoT service in edge computing environment
Authors: B. Panjavarnam, N. Kanimozhi, S. Rahmath Nisha, N. Dharini
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
The emergence of 5G networks has led to development of Edge Computing (EC) environments, which offer improved support for applications that rely on the Internet of Things (IoT). However, Edge Nodes (ENs) have limited resources and heterogeneous IoT applications require changing resource requirements, making it difficult to integrate IoT services. During IoT service installation, ensuring QoS performance is also difficult. In this paper, Quantum-inspired Improved African Vultures Optimization Algorithm for Service Placement (QIAVOA-SP) is proposed for achieving efficient positioning of IoT service in EC environment. This QIAVOA-SP utilized the factors of computation load, delay, energy consumption and throughput during optimization such that necessitated service placement is achieved in EC. It formulated and evaluated the fitness function determined using parameters of load balancing, energy consumption, delay and throughput for optimal positioning of IoT services in EC scenario. It incorporated the concept of Quantum-inspired Improved African Vultures (QIAV) as search agents for representing the complex solutions that are essential for placing IoT services in an edge environment. It further used the technique of double hashing for decoding the QIAV, and further incorporated the Taguchi method for studying the parameters of impact. The simulation result of QIAVOA-SP approach confirmed 19.32% better load balancing, 18.98% reduced delay and 16.45% better energy consumption than baseline approaches used for investigation. The statistical analysis of this QIAVOA-SP approach conducted using Friedman test also confirmed its efficacy over the benchmarked approaches.

Introduction

Rapid advent of communication and information technologies resulted in wide deployment of Internet of Things (IoT) devices in diverse fields that includes healthcare systems, smart cities, industry 4.0 and smart agriculture, etc1. Development of fifth Generation (5G) networks wide opened the possibility of utilizing the benefits of advanced IoT applications. Some of the application which utilizes the merits of advanced IoT include online gaming environment, Augmented Reality (AR), Virtual Reality (VR), video conferencing etc2, 3. However, these applications necessitate high real-time data processing with maximized computation power. Moreover, the IoT devices unfortunately possess restricted energy, low memory and least computation power4, 5. In this context, Edge Nodes (ENs) having comparatively a higher resource potentiality and higher computation power is deployed within the range which is closer to IoT devices and at network edge for the objective of satisfying and handling the progress of the demanding situations6, 7. With this capability, the delay sensitive and computation intensive services can be offloaded for deploying the suitable services at the pertinent edge’s nodes. Hence, the IoT devices or the end users can be effectively provided by all Edge Servers (ESs) present in the implementation environment8, 9. In specific, Edge Computing (EC) thus brings the cloud computing power much closer to network edge for improving services’ response time such that Quality of Services (QoS) and Quality of Experience (QoE) is eventually enhanced in the environment10, 11. Moreover, the ENs comprise of different number of services that includes storage, bandwidth, memory, and computation unit. On the other hand, modern applications that utilized IoT devices require numerous resources in a heterogeneous manner12. But deploying more amounts of application services efficiently over suitable ENs always remains a major challenge in EC environment by considering different limitations of resources with satisfied QoE and QoS requirements13.

Motivation

Service Placement Problem (SPP) represents the method of efficient positioning of IoT services to the ENs which is considered as a herculean challenge in EC systems. This service placement includes the steps that maps different servers to the suitable selected ENs by considering efficiency based on load balancing, delay, throughput and energy consumption14. This SPP problem is more complex as they necessitate potential algorithms that determine optimal service placement solutions owing to dynamic and heterogeneous characteristics of edge networks. Thus, novel and enhanced strategies of service placement are needed in EC environment for addressing the issues of SPP such that the objective that targets on better throughput with optimized load balance, delay and energy consumption is achieved15. But accommodating the complete set of comprehensive available IoT services in the ENs becomes highly challenging due to dynamic resource demands of IoT applications on one end, and restricted resource availability and heterogeneity of ENs from other end. Maximized system throughput is achieved in the implementation environment through the assignment of maximized number of services to ENs16. Hence, design and development of a potential strategy of service allocation is essential for guaranteeing maximized throughput. The ESs specifically incur a substantial quantity of energy for executing services which are assigned to them, and for communicating services with devices of IoT. Thus, energy consumption reduction with respect to the ENs is identified as another major concern during the design of the algorithms for attaining effective and efficient service placement17.

On the other hand, some of the IoT applications such as VR, online gaming etc., are delay sensitive. This delay sensitivity in these applications sometimes introduces an interruption which in turn results in QoS and QoE degradation. The delay incurred in these applications happen due to communication and computation of IoT services over ENs18. Moreover, the strategies of IoT service placement should possess the capability of delivering the required services with reduced amount of time for the purpose of guaranteeing maximized QoS and QoE in the edge network. Most of the IoT services ned to be assigned to the ENs which possesses a comparatively a higher computation power or the ENs that are in the closer proximity of the IoT nodes for minimizing the communication delay such that the delay can be significantly minimized in the EC environment19. But the utilization of this IoT service placement strategy may eventually increase the overhead in terms of overhead incurred by all the ENs resulting in degrading the system performance in terms of load balancing. In addition, underutilization of most of existing resources or imbalance overhead may introduce uneven energy consumption from perspectives of the ENs. The process of load balancing guarantees that none of the ENs is never overburdened by comparatively a higher number of service requests incoming to EC environment. This extra degree of services requests to ENs has the probability of introducing a high load of computation over each of the specified ENs20. Thus, load balancing in the EC environment is determined one of the challenging problems which need to be handled with efficacy.

A diversified number of research works have been conducted for designing potential IoT service placement algorithm with respect to edge and fog computing network, but majority of them failed in handling the issues that are related to load balancing, energy consumption, delay, and throughput. Most of the existing IoT service placement mechanisms contributed for EC still possesses the challenge to hence the execution performance of the overall EC unit. This execution performance completely depends on power consumptions of edge consumption units, levels of load balancing and utilization of resources. It also needs to ensure preventing of leakage of privacy associated with IoT devices during service placement. Most of Swarm Intelligent (SI) metaheuristic optimization algorithms involved in placement of IoT service in EC are prone to major limitations of premature convergence, more number of parameters, poor solution diversity and imbalanced trade-off amid exploration and exploitation rate in Search Space (SS). A potential SI metaheuristic optimization-based IoT service placement approach that can prevent premature convergence, achieves better solution quality, guarantees superior solution diversity and confirms balanced deviation between the rate of exploration and exploitation becomes necessary. In specific, Quantum-based SI metaheuristic optimization algorithms-based IoT service placement approach are beneficial due to following reasons such as i) quantum algorithm contributed depending on quantum mechanics principles facilitates capabilities of potential parallel processing which is adaptable to diversified range of applications, the optimization methods when hybridized with quantum, mechanisms is determined to significantly handle the problem of NP-hard complexity with more efficacy and at a rapid exponential degree.

A motivating scenario

In the smart city environment, the IoT devices deployed over the network forms the major integral part which is completely responsible for provisioning an improved QoS. These IoT devices possess computing potentialities at the edges of the networks through a powerful and ideal paradigm named as EC. This EC paradigm facilitates most potential requirements to the features of large scale and distributed IoT network. In this context, the EC units play an indispensable role of hosting the IoT services in EC for execution. This hosting of IoT services in EC offers minimized latency with reduced bandwidth burden. At this juncture, achieving better resource utilization, balancing the load and minimizing the power consumption for improving overall execution performance of EC units is a herculean challenge. While placing IoT services, these parameters are required for guaranteeing required QoS in smart city environment21. In this situation, intelligent metaheuristic SI optimization algorithms based IoT service placement approaches are determined to be highly suitable for exploring optimization factors with different constraints imposed during optimization.

In addition, IoT SPP in edge networks represents a NP-hard problem. At this juncture, Quantum-inspired evolutionary algorithms which adopted the merits of quantum mechanism principles present a class of optimization algorithms those targets on maximizing the performance of the traditional evolutionary algorithms. This category of Quantum-inspired evolutionary algorithms possesses the capability of exploring a larger SS with maximized efficiency which helps in determining possible and better solution related to the problem of complex optimization. These algorithms also possess the capability of more rapid convergence. Moreover, Quantum-inspired African Vulture Optimization Algorithm22 has attracted wide attention for it’s potentially in solving complex optimization problems. The significance of QIAVOA towards the capability of solving different problems of optimization is kept in mind. It is employed in the proposed scheme for handling SPP in EC environment. Inclusion of QIAVOA facilitates optimal solution in SPP in polynomial time with rapid rate of convergence.

Related work

In this section, a comprehensive review of existing IoT services placement methodologies proposed for EC environment over latest years is offered with their merits and limitations.

Zhang et al.23 proposed a methodology which combines ES and SP where placement of services considers present ES positioning, and request rates and prices of diverse services. It focuses on improving profit of ESs in terms of number of servers, relationship amid servers and Base Stations (BSs), and storage and computing capacities of every server. A 2-step method which includes clustering and non-linear programming is proposed. It is seen that propounded scheme offers improved outcomes when compared to standard methods. Ning et al.24 have focussed on enhancing system utility of Pervasive EC (PEC) networks based on server storage capability along with service execution latency. The networks include numerous mobile users who move across servers seeking for resources. It supports dynamic SP by using Lyapunov optimization for decomposing long-term optimization problem into immediate problems. An average stochastic algorithm based on approximation is propounded to estimate the expected utility of the system. It also employs Markov approximation algorithm for determining SP configurations. To deal with storage capacity and execution delay of servers, Dynamic Storage-Stable Service placement (DASS) algorithm is proposed to improve system utility, balancing system stability as well as overhead. To overcome biased decisions, Sample Average Approximation (SAA) algorithm is used to estimate long-term amassed system utility. Time taken by the propounded algorithm is linear to quantity of users. It analyses performance for real trace-driven and synthetic scenarios. It is seen that proposed algorithm offers enhanced performance when compared to existing ones.

Mala et al.25 have investigated load distribution along with positioning of IoT-based applications for reducing violations of Service Level Agreement (SLA) owing to resource limitations and conflicting objectives. A system model with several application replicas placed in various parts of network is designed for distributing load among replicas. A static scenario with sufficient information for SP and load distribution is provided. To deal with computational complexity, a meta-heuristic multi-objective Genetic Algorithm (GA) with initial population which is based on heuristic and random solutions for getting near-optimal solutions is proposed. SP and load distribution are expressed as Mixed-Integer Nonlinear Programming (MINLP) optimization problems for reducing SLA violations. It provides SP mapping with request distribution among nodes. MINLP is converted into Mixed-Integer LP (MILP) problem by using linear solver. The problem involving an objective can be applied to several conflicting objectives, giving priority to time-sensitive applications. The proposed mechanism outdoes other standard methods based on violation of response deadline and contradictory objectives like operational cost in addition to service availability. SP along with resource allocation is mainly complex based on stochastic task arrival, additional latency of migrating services and waiting time incurred by unprocessed tasks. Liu et al.26 have used Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) and have proposed parameterized deep Q networks-based scheme for handling the issues of SP and resource allocation issues. The proposed scheme focuses on reducing total task latency in long term. Through simulations, convergence as well as performance of proposed scheme is assessed. This approach was proposed for handling the tasks that necessitates heterogeneous computation over the nodes of the edge network. It facilitated the option of placing each service to the ENs in advance. It used the merits of parameterized deep Q networks for achieving decisions related to computation resources allocation and joint service placement. This strategy targeted on the process of attaining reduced total latency incurred by the tasks in addition to time consumed for non-processing tasks waiting and service migration. It also handled resource allocation and SPPs with respect to computation by considering the tasks stochastic arrival rates.

Song et al.27 have proposed computational offloading scheme for tasks involving multiple service. Task execution demands co-operation of several services, and every service is crucial. An evaluation metric is designed involving system cost. The proposed method focuses on finding decision to reduce the metric for solving Mobile EC (MEC) problem involving multi-services tasks. Multi-service Task Computing Offload Algorithm (MTCOA) is designed to get optimal solution. It is evident that the algorithm involves reduced computing offloading cost, offering increased resource utilization in contrast to the benchmarked schemes AI-based use Deep Neural Network (DNN) models which are huge in size offer increased accuracy. These models involve more amount of energy. Premsankar and Ghaddar28 proposed seep neural network (DNN)-based energy-efficient positioning models through the design of a multi-period optimization problem. It places services and orders requests by focussing on reduction of overall energy consumed along with latency. It offers a heuristic which considers the influence of positioning services across time periods. Quality is assessed by comparing the solution with problem’s lower bound got by solving Lagrange relaxation of problem. The proposed scheme involves reduced energy and latency by packing services on reduced number of ENs. It was identified from simulation results that the adopted heuristic approaches exhibited better performance in terms of reduced energy utilization over a minimal ENs count. This approach sustained latency of requests to be satisfied with the use of a pre-determined threshold which is same for all the time periods. This DNN model store and transmit the services effectively with the view to reduce energy utilization degree.

In addition, Bey et al.29 have proposed Quantum-inspired particle swarm optimization for efficient IoT service placement (QIPSO-SP) algorithm for EC-based environment. It involves a fitness function which focuses on improving throughput, and reducing the amount of energy consumed, delay and computation load. It uses QIPSO to represent IoT SP which handles assignment of services to ENs and employs double hashing for decoding the particles. It aids in concurrent representation of several states and solutions. It generates population based on particles to ensure uniform service assignment by using hash function. It represents final solution using decoded particles to aid in optimal SP. The proposed scheme involves less energy, delay, and load balancing with improved throughput when compared to benchmarked mechanisms taken for study. Moreover, Zhang et al.30 have proposed a trusted placement scheme for controllers using deployment cost as well as link failure probability. Link failure probability-based reliability model as well as propagation delay model is built. Controller positioning approach involving reduced network cost is designed using controller layout scheme that depends on density-peak clustering. Mobile-based service adaptive positioning scheme is propounded to handle the issues related to prolonged user-observed delays with increased migration costs owing to restrictions of server resources as well as coverage areas as well as dynamic motion of user. Probability Density Function-based mobility model involving sojourn time and user mobility-based service deployment overhead model are built. Quantum Ant Colony algorithm is used for supporting adaptive SP. The propounded controller placement scheme focuses on deployment cost, propagation delay as well as controller reliability. It offers acceptable quantity of controllers with their locations and optimises QoE of users.

Quantum-inspired AVOA with an elite mutation strategy (QEMAVOA)

In QEMAVOA, QC is included in AVOA, thus improving performance by exploiting quanta’s properties. The population is converted into quantum population of vultures and changezb in population is used for determining optimum solution of objective function. Cui et al.31 have augmented Differential Evolution (DE) by incorporating QDCE and QRG schemes that enables it to efficiently handle large optimization problems. QDCE along with QRG from QC are introduced into AVOA. A detailed description of QDCE and QRG schemes is given below.

Simulation results and discussion

Performance of proposed QIAVOA-SP and the benchmarked schemes is analysed using synthetic dataset and resource demand specified in Table 2. The parameters considered for simulation along with the assigned values is presented in Table 3. The complete set of simulation experiments are conducted using Python 3.9 programming language with the support of libraries that includes SciPy, Matplotlib and NumPy. The experiments are conducted in a system with the configuration of an Intel Core i7-7820HQ processor which is execution over 2.9 GHz and 8 GB of RAM. These comprehensive simulations and evaluations are conducted over a 64-bit Ubuntu 20.04 LTS operating system. Simulations are executed with 50 number of generations as the fixed limit for each algorithm.

The configurations considered for evaluating proposed QIAVOA-SP and baseline approaches are the same for providing a non-bias performance investigation process. The algorithms are run for 50–250 iterations, and the results are averaged, and the plots are demonstrated for highlighting the efficacy of proposed scheme over benchmarked approaches used for evaluation. Moreover, the size of the population adopted for proposed QIAVOA-SP and baseline schemes is also equal to 50. Moreover, the process of IoT service placement over ESs is non-pre-emptive, such that each IoT service possesses a priority over the other subsequent tasks in the EC environment.

The quantum-inspired approach is used with justification even when the size of the population is 50 and the number of iterations ranges between 50 and 250 since it plays a vital role in obtaining a balance between exploration and exploitation during complex search process. Even when the count of iterations is relatively lower, the aspect of quantum increases the probability of introducing quantum leap during the process of determining better solutions. In specific, quantum-based optimization algorithms utilizes the merits of QC such as entanglement and superposition such that it explores the solution space in a more significant manner. On the other hand, quantum-inspired algorithms incorporate the capability of exploring the SS with maximized diversity such that potential solutions are identified in the promising regions. It used quantum leap for speeding up the process of faster convergence such that optimal solutions is determined in the SS with a relatively lesser number of iterations. The inclusion of quantum strategy assists in the process of establishing a better balance between the process of exploring diversity solutions and exploiting potential areas of the SS. In the problems of complex optimization, quantum-based methods are beneficial as it explores diverse regions and converge rapidly. These approaches even under limited number of iterations still facilitate a potential merit in identifying a better solution compared to the traditional algorithms. In specific, Quantum-inspired AVOA algorithm enhances the search capabilities of the classical AVOA since the quantum rotating data aids in establishing the balance between the phases of exploitation and diversification which results in determining better solutions. It is more beneficial since the solutions are determined in the complex SS even with limited computational resources such as 50 iterations.

In this section, experimental results of proposed QIAVOA-SP and baseline approaches conducted using throughput are presented with different number of generations. Conclusions

The proposed QIAVOA-SP achieved optimal placement of IoT service in EC environment by adopting the fitness factors of computation load, delay, energy consumption and throughput into account. It incorporated the concept of QIAV as search agents for representing the complex solutions that are essential for placing IoT services in an edge environment. The simulation results confirmed that the proposed QIAVOA-SP scheme with different number of applications (services count set to 25) remarkably minimized the energy consumptions by 13.28%, 15.31%, 18.94% and 20.28%, better than the benchmarked approaches used for investigation. Moreover, the proposed QIAVOA-SP scheme with different number of applications (services count set to 50) also achieved better throughput of 10.76%, 13.29%, 15.68% and 17.85%, better than the benchmarked approaches used for investigation. As the part of future scope of research, the examination towards the process of designing service placement algorithms which could operate in uncertain and dynamic environments can be studied. In addition, some of the recently proposed quantum-inspired multi-objective evolutionary algorithms is adopted for solving the problem of service placement addressed in the paper.
