10 U.S.C. § 18237 — Supervision of construction: compliance with State law
Citation: us:usc:10:18237
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section18237
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part V (Service, Supply, And Procurement); Chapter 1803 (Facilities For Reserve Components). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Any construction, expansion, rehabilitation, or conversion under section 18233(a)(1) of this title may be performed under the supervision of the Chief of Engineers of the Army or the head of such office or agency in the Department of the Navy as the Secretary of the Navy may designate.
(b)
The construction, expansion, rehabilitation, or conversion of facilities in a State under paragraph (2), (3), (4), (5), or (6) of section 18233(a) of this title shall be done according to the laws of that jurisdiction and under the supervision of its officials, subject to the inspection and approval of the Secretary of Defense.
