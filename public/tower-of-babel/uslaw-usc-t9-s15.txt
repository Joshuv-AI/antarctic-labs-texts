9 U.S.C. § 15 — Inapplicability of the Act of State doctrine
Citation: us:usc:9:15
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title9-section15
Context: United States Code, Title 9 (Arbitration); Chapter 1 (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Enforcement of arbitral agreements, confirmation of arbitral awards, and execution upon judgments based on orders confirming such awards shall not be refused on the basis of the Act of State doctrine.
