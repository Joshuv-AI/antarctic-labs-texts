42 C.F.R. § 422.1012 — Fees for services of representatives.
Citation: us:cfr:42:422.1012
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-422.1012
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 422 (Medicare Advantage Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Fees for any services performed on behalf of an affected party by an attorney appointed and qualified in accordance with 422.1008 are not subject to the provisions of section 206 of Title II of the Act, which authorizes the Secretary to specify or limit those fees.
