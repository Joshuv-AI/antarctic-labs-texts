46 C.F.R. § 535.402 — Complete and definite agreements.
Citation: us:cfr:46:535.402
Creator: Federal Maritime Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-535.402
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 535 (Ocean Common Carrier And Marine Terminal Operator Agreements Subject To The Shipping Act Of 1984). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An agreement filed under the Act must be clear and definite in its terms, must embody the complete, present understanding of the parties, and must set forth the specific authorities and conditions under which the parties to the agreement will conduct their operations and regulate the relationships among the agreement members, unless those details are matters specifically enumerated as exempt from the filing requirements of this part.
