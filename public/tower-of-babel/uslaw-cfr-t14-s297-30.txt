14 C.F.R. § 297.30 — Public disclosure of cargo liability insurance.
Citation: us:cfr:14:297.30
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-297.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 297 (Foreign Air Freight Forwarders And Foreign Cooperative Shippers Associations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every foreign air freight forwarder shall give notice in writing to the shipper, when any shipment is accepted, of the limits of its cargo liability insurance, or of the absence of such insurance, and the limits of its liability, if any. The notice shall be included clearly and conspicuously on all of its rate sheets and airwaybills, and on any other documentation that is given to a shipper at the time of acceptance of the shipment.
