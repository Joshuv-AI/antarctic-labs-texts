20 C.F.R. § 227.2 — Initial supplemental annuity rate.
Citation: us:cfr:20:227.2
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-227.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 227 (Computing Supplemental Annuities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The supplemental annuity rate, before reduction for the railroad retirement family maximum or any private pension, is $23 for an employee's first 25 years of service plus $4 for each added year of service up to 30 years. The highest supplemental annuity rate is $43 for an employee with 30 or more years of service.
