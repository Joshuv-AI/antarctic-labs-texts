9 C.F.R. § 96.8 — Uncertified casings; disinfection at seaboard port.
Citation: us:cfr:9:96.8
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-96.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 96 (Restriction Of Importations Of Foreign Animal Casings Offered For Entry Into The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Uncertified foreign animal casings arriving at a seaboard port may be imported into the United States for use as food containers after they have been disinfected under the direct supervision of an APHIS inspector at the seaboard port.
