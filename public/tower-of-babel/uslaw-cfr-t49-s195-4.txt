49 C.F.R. § 195.4 — Compatibility necessary for transportation of hazardous liquids or carbon dioxide.
Citation: us:cfr:49:195.4
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-195.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 195 (Transportation Of Hazardous Liquids By Pipeline). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person may transport any hazardous liquid or carbon dioxide unless the hazardous liquid or carbon dioxide is chemically compatible with both the pipeline, including all components, and any other commodity that it may come into contact with while in the pipeline.
