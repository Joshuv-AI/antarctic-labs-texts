22 C.F.R. § 1500.1 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:22:1500.1
Creator: African Development Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1500.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1500 (Sunshine Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to effectuate the provisions of the Government in the Sunshine Act. These procedures apply to meetings of the Board of Directors of the African Development Foundation.
