16 C.F.R. § 1016.2 — Definition.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1016.2
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1016.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1016 (Policies And Procedures For Information Disclosure And Commission Employee Testimony In Private Litigation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Private litigation refers to any legal proceeding which does not involve the United States government, or any department or agency of the U.S. government, as a party.
