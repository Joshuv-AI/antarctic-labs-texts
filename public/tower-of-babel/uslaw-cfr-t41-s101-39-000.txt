41 C.F.R. § 101-39.000 — -39.000 Scope of part.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-39 (39—Interagency Fleet Management Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes policies governing the establishment and operation of interagency fleet management systems and operating procedures applicable to the General Services Administration (GSA) Interagency Fleet Management System.
