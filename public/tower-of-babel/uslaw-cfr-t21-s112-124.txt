21 C.F.R. § 112.124 — What requirements apply to instruments and controls used to measure, regulate, or record?
Citation: us:cfr:21:112.124
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-112.124
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 112 (Standards For The Growing, Harvesting, Packing, And Holding Of Produce For Human Consumption). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Instruments or controls you use to measure, regulate, or record temperatures, hydrogen-ion concentration (pH), sanitizer efficacy or other conditions, in order to control or prevent the growth of microorganisms of public health significance, must be:
(a) Accurate and precise as necessary and appropriate in keeping with their purpose;
(b) Adequately maintained; and
(c) Adequate in number for their designated uses.
