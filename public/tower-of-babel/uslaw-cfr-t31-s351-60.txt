31 C.F.R. § 351.60 — How are book-entry Series EE savings bonds purchased and held?
Citation: us:cfr:31:351.60
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-351.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 351 (Offering Of United States Savings Bonds, Series Ee). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Book-entry bonds must be purchased and held online through your New Treasury Direct account. We provide instructions for opening an account online at <http://www.fiscal.treasury.gov>.
