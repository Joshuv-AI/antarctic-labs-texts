22 C.F.R. § 228.22 — Air transportation.
Citation: us:cfr:22:228.22
Creator: Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-228.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 228 (Rules For Procurement Of Commodities And Services Financed By Usaid). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Fly America Act, Title 49 of the United States Code, Subtitle VII, part A, subpart I, Chapter 401, 40118—Government-Financed Air Transportation, is applicable to all transportation of commodities subject to this part. Subpart D of 22 CFR part 228 does not apply to this provision.
