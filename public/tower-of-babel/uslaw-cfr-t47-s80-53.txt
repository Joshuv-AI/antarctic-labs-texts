47 C.F.R. § 80.53 — Application for a portable ship station license.
Citation: us:cfr:47:80.53
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-80.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 80 (Stations In The Maritime Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission may grant a license permitting operation of a portable ship station aboard different vessels of the United States.
