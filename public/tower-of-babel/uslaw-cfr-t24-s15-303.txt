24 C.F.R. § 15.303 — Procedure for review of demands for production of material or provision of testimony in any legal proceeding in which the United States is a party.
Citation: us:cfr:24:15.303
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-15.303
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 15 (Public Access To Hud Records Under The Freedom Of Information Act And Testimony And Production Of Information By Hud Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever a demand is made upon the Department or an employee of the Department for the production of material or provision of testimony, the employee shall immediately notify the Appropriate Associate General Counsel or Appropriate Regional Counsel.
