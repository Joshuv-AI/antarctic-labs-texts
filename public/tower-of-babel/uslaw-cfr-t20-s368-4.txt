20 C.F.R. § 368.4 — Concession stands.
Citation: us:cfr:20:368.4
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-368.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 368 (Prohibition Of Cigarette Sales To Minors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Tobacco products may be sold on property occupied and maintained by the Railroad Retirement Board only as authorized by the Railroad Retirement Board or the General Services Administration or other Federal agency. Concession stands may not sell tobacco products to minors.
