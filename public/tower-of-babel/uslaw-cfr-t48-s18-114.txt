48 C.F.R. § 18.114 — 18.114 Contracting with the Small Business Administration (The 8(a) Program).
Citation: us:cfr:48:18.114
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-18.114
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 18 (Emergency Acquisitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contracts may be awarded to the Small Business Administration (SBA) for performance by eligible 8(a) participants on either a sole source or competitive basis. (See Subpart 19.8.)
