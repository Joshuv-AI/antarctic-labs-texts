49 U.S.C. § 47522 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:49:47522
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section47522
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part B (Airport Development And Noise); Chapter 475 (Noise); Subchapter II (National Aviation Noise Policy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this subchapter—
(1)
“air carrier”, “air transportation”, and “United States” have the same meanings given those terms in section 40102(a) of this title.
(2)
“stage 3 noise levels” means the stage 3 noise levels in part 36 of title 14, Code of Federal Regulations, in effect on November 5, 1990.
