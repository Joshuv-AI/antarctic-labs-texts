15 C.F.R. § 17.2 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:15:17.2
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-17.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 17 (Personnel Exchanges Between Federal Laboratories And Non-Federal Entities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The term funding agreement shall have the meaning according to it under 35 U.S.C. 201(b).
(b) The term contractor shall have the meaning according to it under 35 U.S.C. 201(c).
(c) The term Federal laboratory shall have the meaning according to it under 15 U.S.C. 3703(4).
