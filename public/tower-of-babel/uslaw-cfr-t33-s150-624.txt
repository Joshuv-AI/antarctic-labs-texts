33 C.F.R. § 150.624 — What are the requirements for protecting personnel from blood-borne pathogens?
Citation: us:cfr:33:150.624
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-150.624
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 150 (Deepwater Ports: Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Measures for protection from the dangers of blood-borne pathogens must be taken in compliance with 29 CFR 1910.1030.
