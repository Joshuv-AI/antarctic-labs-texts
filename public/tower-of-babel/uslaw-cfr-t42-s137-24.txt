42 C.F.R. § 137.24 — Are there grants available to assist the Indian Tribe to meet the requirements to participate in self-governance?
Citation: us:cfr:42:137.24
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-137.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 137 (Tribal Self-Governance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, any Indian Tribe may apply, as provided in § 137.25, for a grant to assist it to:
(a) Plan to participate in self-governance; and
(b) Negotiate the terms of the compact and funding agreement between the Indian Tribe and Secretary.
