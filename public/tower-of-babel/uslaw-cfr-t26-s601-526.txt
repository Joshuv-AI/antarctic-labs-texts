26 C.F.R. § 601.526 — Revocation of powers of attorney and tax information authorizations.
Citation: us:cfr:26:601.526
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-601.526
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 601 (Statement Of Procedural Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The revocation of the authority of a representative covered by a power of attorney or tax information authorization filed in an office of the Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, and Firearms shall in no case be effective prior to the giving of written notice to the proper official that the authority of such representative has been revoked.
