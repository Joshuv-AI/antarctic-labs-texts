12 C.F.R. § 161.2 — Account.
Citation: us:cfr:12:161.2
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-161.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 161 (Definitions For Regulations Affecting All Savings Associations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term account means any savings account, demand account, certificate account, tax and loan account, note account, United States Treasury general account or United States Treasury time deposit-open account, whether in the form of a deposit or a share, held by an accountholder in a savings association.
