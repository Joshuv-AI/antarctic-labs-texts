31 C.F.R. § 346.11 — Use of power of attorney.
Citation: us:cfr:31:346.11
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-346.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 346 (Regulations Governing United States Individual Retirement Bonds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No designation of an attorney, agent, or other representative to request payment or reissue on behalf of the owner, beneficiary, or other person entitled under § 346.9, other than as provided in these regulations, will be recognized.
