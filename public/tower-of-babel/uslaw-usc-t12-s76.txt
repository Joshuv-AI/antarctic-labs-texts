12 U.S.C. § 76 — President of bank as member of board; chairman of board
Citation: us:usc:12:76
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section76
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 2 (National Banks); Subchapter III (Directors). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The president of the bank shall be a member of the board and shall be the chairman thereof, but the board may designate a director in lieu of the president to be chairman of the board, who shall perform such duties as may be designated by the board.
