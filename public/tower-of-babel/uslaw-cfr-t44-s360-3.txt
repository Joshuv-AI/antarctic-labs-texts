44 C.F.R. § 360.3 — Eligible applicants.
Citation: us:cfr:44:360.3
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-360.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 360 (State Assistance Programs For Training And Education In Comprehensive Emergency Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each of the 50 States, independent commonwealths, and territories is eligible to participate in a State Cooperative Agreement with FEMA. The department, division, or agency of the State government assigned the responsibility for State training in comprehensive emergency management should file the application.
