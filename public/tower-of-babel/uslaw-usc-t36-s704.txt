36 U.S.C. § 704 — Activities
Citation: us:usc:36:704
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section704
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part A (Observances And Ceremonies); Chapter 7 (Federal Participation In Carl Garner Federal Lands Cleanup Day). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In cooperation with appropriate State and local government authorities, each Federal land management agency shall plan for and carry out activities on Carl Garner Federal Lands Cleanup Day that—
(1)
encourage continuing public and private sector cooperation in preserving the beauty and safety of areas within the jurisdiction of the agency;
(2)
increase citizens’ sense of ownership and community pride in those areas;
(3)
reduce litter on Federal lands, along trails and waterways, and within those areas; and
(4)
maintain and improve trails, recreation areas, waterways, and facilities.
