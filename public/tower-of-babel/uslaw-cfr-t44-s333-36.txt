44 C.F.R. § 333.36 — Changes or cancellations of allocation orders.
Citation: us:cfr:44:333.36
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-333.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 333 (Emergency Management Priorities And Allocations System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An allocation order may be changed or cancelled by an official action from FEMA. Notice of such changes or cancellations may be provided directly to persons to whom the order being cancelled or modified applies or constructive notice may be provided by publication in the Federal Register.
