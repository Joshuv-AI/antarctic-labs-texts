33 U.S.C. § 467i — Statutory construction
Citation: us:usc:33:467i
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section467i
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 9 (Protection Of Navigable Waters And Of Harbor And River Improvements Generally); Subchapter VII (Dam Inspection Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subchapter and no action or failure to act under this subchapter shall—
(1)
create any liability in the United States or its officers or employees for the recovery of damages caused by such action or failure to act;
(2)
relieve an owner or operator of a dam of the legal duties, obligations, or liabilities incident to the ownership or operation of the dam; or
(3)
preempt any other Federal or State law.
