7 C.F.R. § 945.110 — Order.
Citation: us:cfr:7:945.110
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-945.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 945 (Irish Potatoes Grown In Certain Designated Counties In Idaho, And Malheur County, Oregon). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Order means Order No. 945, as amended, effective September 1, 1958 (§§ 945.1 through 945.91) regulating the handling of Irish potatoes grown in Malheur County, Oregon, and the counties of Adams, Valley, Lemhi, Clark, and Fremont in the State of Idaho, and all of the counties in Idaho lying south thereof.
