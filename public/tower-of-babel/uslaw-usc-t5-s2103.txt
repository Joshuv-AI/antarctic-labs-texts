5 U.S.C. § 2103 — The excepted service
Citation: us:usc:5:2103
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section2103
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart A (General Provisions); Chapter 21 (Definitions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
For the purpose of this title, the “excepted service” consists of those civil service positions which are not in the competitive service or the Senior Executive Service.
(b)
As used in other Acts of Congress, “unclassified civil service” or “unclassified service” means the “excepted service”.
