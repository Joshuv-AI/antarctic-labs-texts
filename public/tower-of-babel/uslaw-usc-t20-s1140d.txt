20 U.S.C. § 1140d — Rule of construction
Citation: us:usc:20:1140d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1140d
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 28 (Higher Education Resources And Student Assistance); Subchapter VII (Graduate And Postsecondary Improvement Programs); Part D (Programs To Provide Students With Disabilities With A Quality Higher Education); Subpart 1 (Demonstration Projects To Support Postsecondary Faculty, Staff, And Administrators In Educating Students With Disabilities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subpart shall be construed to impose any additional duty, obligation, or responsibility on an institution of higher education or on the institution’s faculty, administrators, or staff than is required under section 794 of title 29 and the Americans with Disabilities Act of 1990 (42 U.S.C. 12101 et seq.).
