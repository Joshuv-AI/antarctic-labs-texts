33 C.F.R. § 80.1350 — Netarts Bay, OR.
Citation: us:cfr:33:80.1350
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-80.1350
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 80 (Colregs Demarcation Lines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A line drawn from the northernmost extremity of the shore on the south side of Netarts Bay north to the opposite shoreline.
