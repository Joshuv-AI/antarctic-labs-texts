34 C.F.R. § 367.2 — Who is eligible for an award?
Citation: us:cfr:34:367.2
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-367.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 367 (Independent Living Services For Older Individuals Who Are Blind). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any designated State agency (DSA) is eligible for an award under this program if the DSA—
(a) Is authorized to provide rehabilitation services to individuals who are blind; and
(b) Submits to and obtains approval from the Secretary of an application that meets the requirements of section 752(h) of the Act and §§ 367.30-367.31.
