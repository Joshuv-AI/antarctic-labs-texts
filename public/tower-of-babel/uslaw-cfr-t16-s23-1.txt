16 C.F.R. § 23.1 — Deception (general).
Citation: us:cfr:16:23.1
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-23.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 23 (Guides For The Jewelry, Precious Metals, And Pewter Industries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is unfair or deceptive to misrepresent the type, kind, grade, quality, quantity, metallic content, size, weight, cut, color, character, treatment, substance, durability, serviceability, origin, price, value, preparation, production, manufacture, distribution, or any other material aspect of an industry product.
