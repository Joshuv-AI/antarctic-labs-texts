24 C.F.R. § 110.15 — Location of posters.
Citation: us:cfr:24:110.15
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Equal Opportunity, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-110.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 110 (Fair Housing Poster). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All fair housing posters shall be prominently displayed so as to be readily apparent to all persons seeking housing accommodations or seeking to engage in residential real estate-related transactions or brokerage services as contemplated by sections 804 through 806 of the Act.
