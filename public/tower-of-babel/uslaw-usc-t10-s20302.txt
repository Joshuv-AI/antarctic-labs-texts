10 U.S.C. § 20302 — Enlisted members: term of enlistment

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Space Force); Chapter 2007 (Enlisted Members). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Term of Original Enlistments.—
The Secretary of the Air Force may accept original enlistments of persons for the duration of their minority or for a period of at least two but not more than eight years in the Space Force.
(b) Term of Reenlistments.—
The Secretary of the Air Force may accept a reenlistment in the Space Force for a period determined in accordance with paragraphs (2), (3), and (4) of section 505(d) of this title.
