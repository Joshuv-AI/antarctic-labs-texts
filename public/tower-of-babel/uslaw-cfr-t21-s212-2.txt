21 C.F.R. § 212.2 — What is current good manufacturing practice for PET drugs?
Citation: us:cfr:21:212.2
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-212.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 212 (Current Good Manufacturing Practice For Positron Emission Tomography Drugs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Current good manufacturing practice for PET drugs is the minimum requirements for the methods to be used in, and the facilities and controls used for, the production, quality assurance, holding, or distribution of PET drugs intended for human use. Current good manufacturing practice is intended to ensure that each PET drug meets the requirements of the act as to safety and has the identity and strength, and meets the quality and purity characteristics, that it is supposed to have.
