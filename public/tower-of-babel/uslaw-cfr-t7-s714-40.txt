7 C.F.R. § 714.40 — Time of filing.
Citation: us:cfr:7:714.40
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-714.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 714 (Refunds Of Penalties Erroneously, Illegally, Or Wrongfully Collected). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Claim shall be filed within 2 years after the date payment was made to the Secretary. The date payment was made shall be deemed to be the date such payment was deposited in the general fund of the Treasury as shown on the certificate of deposit on which such payment was scheduled.
