6 U.S.C. § 521 — Legal status of EOIR

Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 1 (Homeland Security Organization); Subchapter XI (Department Of Justice Divisions); Part A (Executive Office For Immigration Review). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) 1 Existence of EOIR
There is in the Department of Justice the Executive Office for Immigration Review, which shall be subject to the direction and regulation of the Attorney General under section 1103(g) of title 8.
