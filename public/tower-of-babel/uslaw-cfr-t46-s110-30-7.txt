46 C.F.R. § 110.30-7 — -7 Repairs or alterations.
Citation: us:cfr:46:110.30-7
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-110.30-7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 110 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Officer in Charge, Marine Inspection must be notified before—
(a) Alterations or modifications that deviate from approved plans; or
(b) Repairs, alterations, or modifications that affect the safety of the vessel.
