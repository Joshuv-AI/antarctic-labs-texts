48 C.F.R. § 1401.670 — 1401.670 Contracting officers' representatives.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1401.670
Creator: Chapter 14—Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1401.670
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1401 (Department Of The Interior Acquisition Regulation System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a CO elects to appoint an individual to act as an authorized representative in the administration of a contract, the CO must notify the contractor of the COR appointment in writing, and provide the name and contact information of the COR.
