32 C.F.R. § 243.2 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:32:243.2
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-243.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 243 (Department Of Defense Ratemaking Procedures For Civil Reserve Air Fleet Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This section governs all contracts with the Department of Defense where awards to the air carriers, either through individual contracts or teaming arrangements, are commensurate with the relative amount of airlift capability committed to the Civil Reserve Air Fleet (CRAF).
