50 C.F.R. § 28.32 — Penalty provisions concerning fires and timber.
Citation: us:cfr:50:28.32
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-28.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 28 (Enforcement, Penalty, And Procedural Requirements For Violations Of Subchapter C). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Any person violating sections 1855-1856 of the Criminal Code (18 U.S.C. 1855-1856) as they pertain to fires on national wildlife refuge lands of the United States shall be subject to civil action and to the penalty provisions of the law.
(b) Any person violating sections 1852-1853 of the Criminal Code (18 U.S.C. 1852-1853) as they pertain to timber on national wildlife refuge lands of the United States shall be subject to civil action and to the penalty provisions of the law.
