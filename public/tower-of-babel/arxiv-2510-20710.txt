Theorem 6
.

Let
f\in\mathcal{E}S_{\overline{\mathcal{H}}}(k,\lambda,\gamma)

\mathfrak{F}_{c}(f)

Proof.

By making use of the representation of
\mathfrak{F}_{c}(f)
, we arrive

\begin{array}[]{ll}\mathfrak{F}_{c}(f(z))&=\dfrac{1+c}{z^{c}}\int\limits_{0}^{z}t^{c-1}\left(h(t)+\overline{g(t)}\right)dt\\
&\\
&=\dfrac{1+c}{z^{c}}\int\limits_{0}^{z}t^{c-1}\left(t+\Sigma_{n=2}^{\infty}(-1)^{n}\left|a_{n}\right|t^{n}\right)+\overline{\int\limits_{0}^{z}t^{c-1}\left(\Sigma_{n=1}^{\infty}(-1)^{n-1}\left|b_{n}\right|t^{n}\right)}dt\\
&\\
&=z+\Sigma_{n=2}^{\infty}\frac{c+1}{c+n}(-1)^{n}\left|a_{n}\right|z^{n}+\Sigma_{n=1}^{\infty}\overline{\frac{c+1}{c+n}(-1)^{n-1}\left|b_{n}\right|z^{n}}.\end{array}

Hence,

\begin{array}[]{l}\Sigma_{n=2}^{\infty}\binom{n+\lambda-1}{\lambda}\left(\frac{n-\gamma}{1-\gamma}\frac{c+1}{c+n}\left|a_{n}\right|+\frac{n+\gamma}{1-\gamma}\frac{c+1}{c+n}\left|b_{n}\right|\right)\frac{1}{(2n-1)(n-1)!}\\
\\
\leq\Sigma_{n=2}^{\infty}\binom{n+\lambda-1}{\lambda}\left(\frac{n-\gamma}{1-\gamma}\left|a_{n}\right|+\frac{n+\gamma}{1-\gamma}\left|b_{n}\right|\right)\frac{1}{(2n-1)(n-1)!}\\
\\
\leq 1-\frac{1+\gamma}{1-\gamma}\left|b_{1}\right|.\end{array}
