15 C.F.R. § 325.13 — Nonadmissibility in evidence.
Citation: us:cfr:15:325.13
Creator: International Trade Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-325.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 325 (Export Trade Certificates Of Review). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Secretary denies, in whole or in part, an application for a certificate or for an amendment to a certificate, or revokes or amends a certificate, neither the negative determination nor the statement of reasons therefor shall be admissible in evidence in any administrative or judicial proceeding in support of any claim under the antitrust laws.
