41 U.S.C. § 116 — Technical data
Citation: us:usc:41:116
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title41-section116
Context: United States Code, Title 41 (Public Contracts); Chapter 1 (Definitions); Subchapter I (Subtitle Definitions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this subtitle, the term “technical data”—
(1)
means recorded information (regardless of the form or method of the recording) of a scientific or technical nature (including computer software documentation) relating to supplies procured by an agency; but
(2)
does not include computer software or financial, administrative, cost or pricing, or management data or other information incidental to contract administration.
