15 U.S.C. § 2661 — National goal
Citation: us:usc:15:2661
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section2661
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 53 (Toxic Substances Control); Subchapter III (Indoor Radon Abatement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The national long-term goal of the United States with respect to radon levels in buildings is that the air within buildings in the United States should be as free of radon as the ambient air outside of buildings.
