33 C.F.R. § 181.19 — Construction of labels.
Citation: us:cfr:33:181.19
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-181.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 181 (Manufacturer Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each label must be made of material that can withstand exposure to water, oil, salt spray, direct sunlight, heat, cold, and wear expected in normal use of the boat or item of associated equipment without deterioration of legibility.
(b) Each label must be made of material that shows visible traces of the alteration or removal of information on the label.
