48 C.F.R. § 1330.202-2 — 1330.202-2 Impracticality of submission.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1330.202-2
Creator: Chapter 13—Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1330.202-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1330 (Cost Accounting Standards Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The DOC Head of Agency for Procurement is authorized to determine that it is impractical to secure a Disclosure Statement, although submission is required, and to authorize contract award without obtaining the Statement.
