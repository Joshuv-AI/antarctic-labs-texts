16 U.S.C. § 6502 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:16:6502
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section6502
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 84 (Healthy Forest Restoration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter:
(1) Federal land
The term “Federal land” means—
(A)
land of the National Forest System (as defined in section 1609(a) of this title) administered by the Secretary of Agriculture, acting through the Chief of the Forest Service; and
(B)
public lands (as defined in section 1702 of title 43), the surface of which is administered by the Secretary of the Interior, acting through the Director of the Bureau of Land Management.
(2) Indian tribe
The term “Indian tribe” has the meaning given the term in section 5304 of title 25.
