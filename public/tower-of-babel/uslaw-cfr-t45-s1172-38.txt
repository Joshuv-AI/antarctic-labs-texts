45 C.F.R. § 1172.38 — Remedial action by recipients.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1172.38
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1172.38
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1172 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Age In Federally Assisted Programs Or Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where NEH finds a recipient has discriminated on the basis of age, the recipient shall take any remedial action that NEH may require to overcome the effects of discrimination. If another recipient exercises control over the recipient that has discriminated, NEH may require both recipients to take remedial action.
