10 C.F.R. § 110.65 — Settlement and compromise.
Citation: us:cfr:10:110.65
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-110.65
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 110 (Export And Import Of Nuclear Equipment And Material). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At any time after issuance of an order for any enforcement action under this subpart, an agreement may be entered into for settlement of the proceeding or compromise of a penalty. Upon approval by the Commission, or presiding officer if a hearing has been requested, the terms of the settlement or compromise will be embodied in the order disposing of the enforcement action.
