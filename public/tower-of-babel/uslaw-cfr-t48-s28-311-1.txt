48 C.F.R. § 28.311-1 — 28.311-1 Contract clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:28.311-1
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-28.311-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 28 (Bonds And Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In accordance with agency acquisition regulations, the contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.228-7, Insurance—Liability to Third Persons, in solicitations and contracts, other than those for construction contracts and those for architect-engineer services, when a cost-reimbursement contract is contemplated.
