10 U.S.C. § 1436a — Coverage paid up at 30 years and age 70
Citation: us:usc:10:1436a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1436a
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 73 (Annuities Based On Retired Or Retainer Pay); Subchapter I (Retired Serviceman’S Family Protection Plan). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Effective October 1, 2008, a reduction under this subchapter in the retired or retainer pay of a person electing an annuity under this subchapter may not be made for any month after the later of—
(1)
the month that is the 360th month for which that person’s retired or retainer pay is reduced pursuant to such an election; and
(2)
the month during which that person attains 70 years of age.
