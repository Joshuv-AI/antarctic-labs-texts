43 U.S.C. § 1441 — Lease of contiguous public lands for public airports; authority of Secretary of the Interior
Citation: us:usc:43:1441
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section1441
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 30 (Administration Of Public Lands); Subchapter VIII (Public Airports). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior is authorized, in his discretion and under such regulations as he may prescribe, to lease for use as a public airport any contiguous public lands, unreserved and unappropriated, not to exceed two thousand five hundred and sixty acres in area, subject to valid rights in such lands under the public-land laws.
