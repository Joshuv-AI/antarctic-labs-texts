42 U.S.C. § 3056n — Sense of Congress
Citation: us:usc:42:3056n
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section3056n
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 35 (Programs For Older Americans); Subchapter IX (Community Service Senior Opportunities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the sense of Congress that—
(1)
the older American community service employment program described in this subchapter was established with the intent of placing older individuals in community service positions and providing job training; and
(2)
placing older individuals in community service positions strengthens the ability of the individuals to become self-sufficient, provides much-needed support to organizations that benefit from increased civic engagement, and strengthens the communities that are served by such organizations.
