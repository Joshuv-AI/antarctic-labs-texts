36 C.F.R. § 222.25 — Costs.
Citation: us:cfr:36:222.25
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-222.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 222 (Range Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Forest Service shall cover only those costs incurred by its own employees in mediation sessions.
