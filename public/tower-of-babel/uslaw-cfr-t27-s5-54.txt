27 C.F.R. § 5.54 — Visibility of mandatory information.
Citation: us:cfr:27:5.54
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-5.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 5 (Labeling And Advertising Of Distilled Spirits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Mandatory information on a label must be readily visible and may not be covered or obscured in whole or in part. See § 5.62 for rules regarding packaging of containers (including cartons, coverings, and cases). See subpart N of this part for regulations pertaining to advertising materials.
