21 C.F.R. § 350.3 — Definition.
Citation: us:cfr:21:350.3
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-350.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 350 (Antiperspirant Drug Products For Over-The-Counter Human Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in this part:
Antiperspirant. A drug product applied topically that reduces the production of perspiration (sweat) at that site.
