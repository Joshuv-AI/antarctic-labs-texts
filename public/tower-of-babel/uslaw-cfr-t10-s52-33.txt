10 C.F.R. § 52.33 — Duration of renewal.
Citation: us:cfr:10:52.33
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-52.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 52 (Licenses, Certifications, And Approvals For Nuclear Power Plants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each renewal of an early site permit may be for not less than 10, nor more than 20 years, plus any remaining years on the early site permit then in effect before renewal.
