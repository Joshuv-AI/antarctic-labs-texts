48 C.F.R. § 44.000 — 44.000 Scope of part.
Citation: us:cfr:48:44.000
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-44.000
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 44 (Subcontracting Policies And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) This part prescribes policies and procedures for consent to subcontracts or advance notification of subcontracts, and for review, evaluation, and approval of contractors' purchasing systems.
(b) The consent and advance notification requirements of subpart 44.2 are not applicable to prime contracts for commercial products or commercial services acquired pursuant to part 12.
