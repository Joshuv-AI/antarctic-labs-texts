26 C.F.R. § 48.4081-8 — -8 Taxable fuel; measurement.
Citation: us:cfr:26:48.4081-8
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-48.4081-8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 48 (Manufacturers And Retailers Excise Taxes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In general. Volumes of taxable fuel may be measured on the basis of actual volumetric gallons or gallons adjusted to 60 degrees Fahrenheit.
(b) Effective date. This section is applicable January 1, 1994.
