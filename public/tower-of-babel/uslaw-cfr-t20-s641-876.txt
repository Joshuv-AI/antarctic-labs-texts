20 C.F.R. § 641.876 — How will compliance with cost limitations and minimum expenditure levels be determined?
Citation: us:cfr:20:641.876
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-641.876
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 641 (Provisions Governing The Senior Community Service Employment Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department will determine compliance by examining expenditures of SCSEP funds. The cost limitations and minimum expenditure level requirements must be met at the time all such funds have been expended or the period of availability of such funds has expired, whichever comes first.
