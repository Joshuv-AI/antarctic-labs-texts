49 C.F.R. § 195.202 — Compliance with specifications or standards.
Citation: us:cfr:49:195.202
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-195.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 195 (Transportation Of Hazardous Liquids By Pipeline). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each pipeline system must be constructed in accordance with comprehensive written specifications or standards that are consistent with the requirements of this part.
