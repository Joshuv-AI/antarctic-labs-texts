15 C.F.R. § 921.41 — Withdrawal of designation.
Citation: us:cfr:15:921.41
Creator: National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-921.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 921 (National Estuarine Research Reserve System Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Assistant Administrator may withdraw designation of an estuarine area as a National Estuarine Research Reserve pursuant to and in accordance with the procedures of section 312 and 315 of the Act and regulations promulgated thereunder.
