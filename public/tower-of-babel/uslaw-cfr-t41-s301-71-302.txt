41 C.F.R. § 301-71.302 — -71.302 Required data for travel advance accounting system.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-71.302
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-71.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-71 (71—Agency Travel Accountability Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The travel advance accounting system must capture:
(a) Names and Social Security numbers of employees with advances;
(b) Amount of each advance;
(c) Issuance date; and
(d) Reconciliation date for unused advance portions.
