40 C.F.R. § 307.12 — Use of number and gender.
Citation: us:cfr:40:307.12
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-307.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 307 (Comprehensive Environmental Response, Compensation, And Liability Act (Cercla) Claims Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in this part, words in the singular also include the plural and vice versa, and words in the masculine gender also include the feminine, as the case may require.
