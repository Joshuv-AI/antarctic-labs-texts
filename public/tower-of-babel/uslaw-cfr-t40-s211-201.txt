40 C.F.R. § 211.201 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:40:211.201
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-211.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 211 (Product Noise Labeling). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless this regulation states otherwise, the provisions of this subpart apply to all hearing protective devices manufactured after the effective date of this regulation. (See § 211.203(m) for definition of “hearing protective device.”)
