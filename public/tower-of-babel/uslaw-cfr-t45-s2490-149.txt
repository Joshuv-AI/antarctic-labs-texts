45 C.F.R. § 2490.149 — Program accessibility: Discrimination prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:45:2490.149
Creator: James Madison Memorial Fellowship Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2490.149
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2490 (Enforcement Of Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap In Programs Or Activities Conducted By The James Madison Memorial Fellowship Foundation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise provided in § 2490.150, no qualified individual with handicaps shall, because the agency's facilities are inaccessible to or unusable by individuals with handicaps, be denied the benefits of, be excluded from participation in, or otherwise be subjected to discrimination under any program or activity conducted by the agency.
