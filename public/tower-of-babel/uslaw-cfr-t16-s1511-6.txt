16 C.F.R. § 1511.6 — Ribbons, strings, cords, or other attachments.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1511.6
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1511.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1511 (Requirements For Pacifiers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A pacifier shall not be sold or distributed with any ribbon, string, cord, chain, twine, leather, yarn or similar attachments.
