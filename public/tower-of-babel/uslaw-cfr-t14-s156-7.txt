14 C.F.R. § 156.7 — Enforcement of State block grant agreements and other related grant assurances.
Citation: us:cfr:14:156.7
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-156.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 156 (State Block Grant Pilot Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator may take any action, pursuant to the authority of the Airport and Airway Improvement Act of 1982, as amended, to enforce the terms of a State block grant agreement including any terms imposed upon subsequent recipients of State block agreement funds.
