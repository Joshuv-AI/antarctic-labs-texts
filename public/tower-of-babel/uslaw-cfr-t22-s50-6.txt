22 C.F.R. § 50.6 — Registration at the Department of birth abroad.
Citation: us:cfr:22:50.6
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-50.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 50 (Nationality Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the time of war or national emergency, passport agents may be designated to complete consular reports of birth for children born at military facilities which are not under the jurisdiction of a consular office. An officer of the Armed Forces having authority to administer oaths may take applications for registration under this section.
