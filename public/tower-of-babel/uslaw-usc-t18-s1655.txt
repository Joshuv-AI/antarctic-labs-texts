18 U.S.C. § 1655 — Assault on commander as piracy
Citation: us:usc:18:1655
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1655
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 81 (Piracy And Privateering). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever, being a seaman, lays violent hands upon his commander, to hinder and prevent his fighting in defense of his vessel or the goods intrusted to him, is a pirate, and shall be imprisoned for life.
