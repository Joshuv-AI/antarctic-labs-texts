46 U.S.C. § 7316 — Lifeboatmen
Citation: us:usc:46:7316
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section7316
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part E (Merchant Seamen Licenses, Certificates, And Documents); Chapter 73 (Merchant Mariners’ Documents). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To qualify for an endorsement as lifeboatman, an applicant must provide satisfactory proof that the applicant—
(1)
has the service or training required by regulation;
(2)
is qualified professionally as demonstrated by examination; and
(3)
is qualified professionally by actual demonstration.
