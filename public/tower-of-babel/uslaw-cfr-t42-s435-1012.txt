42 C.F.R. § 435.1012 — Requirement for maintenance of optional State supplement expenditures.
Citation: us:cfr:42:435.1012
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-435.1012
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 435 (Eligibility In The States, District Of Columbia, The Northern Mariana Islands, And American Samoa). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) This section applies to States that make optional State supplement payments under section 1616(a) of the Act and mandatory supplement payments under section 212(a) of Pub. L. 93-66.
(b) FFP in Medicaid expenditures is not available during any period in which the State does not have in effect an agreement with the Secretary under section 1618 of the Act to maintain its supplementary payments.
