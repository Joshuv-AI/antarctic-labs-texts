9 C.F.R. § 381.211 — Method of detention; form of detention tag.
Citation: us:cfr:9:381.211
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-381.211
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 381 (Poultry Products Inspection Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An authorized representative of the Secretary shall detain any poultry or other article to be detained under this subpart, by affixing an official “U.S. Detained” tag (FSIS Form 8400-2) to such article.
