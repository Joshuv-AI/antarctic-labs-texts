15 U.S.C. § 3714 — Spending authority
Citation: us:usc:15:3714
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section3714
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 63 (Technology Innovation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No payments shall be made or contracts shall be entered into pursuant to the provisions of this chapter (other than sections 3710a, 3710b, and 3710c of this title) except to such extent or in such amounts as are provided in advance in appropriation Acts.
