48 C.F.R. § 925.701-70 — 925.701-70 Prohibited sources.
Citation: us:cfr:48:925.701-70
Creator: Chapter 9—Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-925.701-70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 925 (Foreign Acquisition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No contract may be awarded to a company owned by an entity controlled by a foreign government if performance of the contract will require access to proscribed information. See subpart 904.71 for additional guidance.
