33 C.F.R. § 147.15 — Extent of safety zones.
Citation: us:cfr:33:147.15
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-147.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 147 (Safety Zones). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A safety zone establishment under this part may extend to a maximum distance of 500 meters around the OCS facility measured from each point on its outer edge or from its construction site, but may not interfere with the use of recognized sea lanes essential to navigation.
