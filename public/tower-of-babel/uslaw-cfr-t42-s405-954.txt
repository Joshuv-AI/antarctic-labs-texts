42 C.F.R. § 405.954 — Redetermination.
Citation: us:cfr:42:405.954
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-405.954
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 405 (Federal Health Insurance For The Aged And Disabled). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon the basis of the evidence of record, the contractor adjudicates the claim(s), and renders a redetermination affirming or reversing, in whole or in part, the initial determination in question.
