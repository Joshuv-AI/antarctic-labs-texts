38 C.F.R. § 21.258 — Cost limitations on approval of self-employment plans.
Citation: us:cfr:38:21.258
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-21.258
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 21 (Veteran Readiness And Employment And Education). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A self-employment plan with an estimated or actual cost of less than $25,000 may be approved by the Veteran Readiness and Employment (VR&E) Officer with jurisdiction. Any self-employment plan with an estimated or actual cost of $25,000 or more must be approved by the Executive Director, VR&E Service.
