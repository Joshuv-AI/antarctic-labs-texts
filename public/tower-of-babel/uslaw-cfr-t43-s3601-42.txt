43 C.F.R. § 3601.42 — What information must I include in my reclamation plan?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3601.42
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3601.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3600 (Mineral Materials Disposal). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If BLM requires you to submit a reclamation plan, it must include:
(a) A statement of the proposed manner and time in which you will complete reclamation of the areas disturbed by your operations;
(b) A map or sketch which delineates the area you will reclaim; and
(c) Such other information as BLM may require.
