48 C.F.R. § 225.7500 — 225.7500 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:48:225.7500
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-225.7500
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 225 (Foreign Acquisition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart provides policies and procedures implementing the Balance of Payments Program. It applies to contracts for the acquisition of—
(a) Supplies for use outside the United States; and
(b) Construction to be performed outside the United States.
