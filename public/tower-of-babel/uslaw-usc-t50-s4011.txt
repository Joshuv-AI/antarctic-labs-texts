50 U.S.C. § 4011 — Inappropriate use of chapter
Citation: us:usc:50:4011
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section4011
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 50 (Servicemembers Civil Relief); Subchapter VI (Administrative Remedies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If a court determines, in any proceeding to enforce a civil right, that any interest, property, or contract has been transferred or acquired with the intent to delay the just enforcement of such right by taking advantage of this chapter, the court shall enter such judgment or make such order as might lawfully be entered or made concerning such transfer or acquisition.
