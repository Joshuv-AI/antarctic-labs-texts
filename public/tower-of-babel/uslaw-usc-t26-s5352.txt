26 U.S.C. § 5352 — Taxpaid wine bottling house
Citation: us:usc:26:5352
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section5352
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 51 (Distilled Spirits, Wines, And Beer); Subchapter F (Bonded And Taxpaid Wine Premises); Part I (Establishment). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person bottling, packaging, or repackaging taxpaid wines shall, before commencing such operations, make application to the Secretary and receive permission to operate. Such premises shall be known as “tax-paid wine bottling houses.”
