25 U.S.C. § 4027 — Savings provision
Citation: us:usc:25:4027
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section4027
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 42 (American Indian Trust Fund Management Reform); Subchapter II (Indian Trust Fund Management Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

By submitting or approving a plan under this subchapter, neither the tribe nor the Secretary shall be deemed to have accepted the account balance as accurate or to have waived any rights regarding such balance and to seek compensation.
