22 C.F.R. § 120.37 — Major defense equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:22:120.37
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-120.37
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 120 (Purpose And Definitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Major defense equipment, pursuant to section 47(6) of the Arms Export Control Act (22 U.S.C. 2794(6)), means any item of significant military equipment on the U.S. Munitions List having a nonrecurring research and development cost of more than $50,000,000 or a total production cost of more than $200,000,000.
