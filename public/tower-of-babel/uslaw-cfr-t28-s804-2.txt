28 C.F.R. § 804.2 — Delegation of authority.
Citation: us:cfr:28:804.2
Creator: Court Services And Offender Supervision Agency For The District Of Columbia
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-804.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 804 (Acceptance Of Gifts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director of CSOSA hereby delegates to the Director of PSA the authority to accept and use gifts in the form of in-kind contributions of space and hospitality to support defendant programs, and of equipment and vocational training services to educate and train defendants in accordance with the requirements of this part. This delegation of authority may not be further delegated.
