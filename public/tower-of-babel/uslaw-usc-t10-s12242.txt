10 U.S.C. § 12242 — Warrant officers: promotion
Citation: us:usc:10:12242
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section12242
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel Generally); Chapter 1207 (Warrant Officers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The promotion of permanent reserve warrant officers not on the warrant officer active-duty list to permanent reserve warrant officer grades shall be governed by such regulations as the Secretary concerned may prescribe.
