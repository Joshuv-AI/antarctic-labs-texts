14 C.F.R. § 91.101 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:91.101
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-91.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 91 (General Operating And Flight Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes flight rules governing the operation of aircraft within the United States and within 12 nautical miles from the coast of the United States.
