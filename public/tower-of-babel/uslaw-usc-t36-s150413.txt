36 U.S.C. § 150413 — Liability for acts of officers and agents
Citation: us:usc:36:150413
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section150413
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1504 (National American Indian Veterans, Incorporated). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation shall be liable for the acts of the officers and agents of the corporation acting within the scope of their authority.
