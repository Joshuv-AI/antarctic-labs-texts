24 C.F.R. § 902.111 — Sanctions for troubled small rural PHAs.
Citation: us:cfr:24:902.111
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-902.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 902 (Public Housing Assessment System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The sanctions for small rural PHAs with troubled public housing programs that remain troubled as required by § 902.108 will be the same as those sanctions for PHAs assessed under PHAS as described in § 902.83.
