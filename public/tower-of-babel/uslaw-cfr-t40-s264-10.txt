40 C.F.R. § 264.10 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:40:264.10
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-264.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 264 (Standards For Owners And Operators Of Hazardous Waste Treatment, Storage, And Disposal Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The regulations in this subpart apply to owners and operators of all hazardous waste facilities, except as provided in § 264.1 and in paragraph (b) of this section.
(b) Section 264.18(b) applies only to facilities subject to regulation under subparts I through O and subpart X of this part.
