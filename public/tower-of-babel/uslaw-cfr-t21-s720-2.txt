21 C.F.R. § 720.2 — Times for filing.
Citation: us:cfr:21:720.2
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-720.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 720 (Voluntary Filing Of Cosmetic Product Ingredient Composition Statements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within 180 days after forms are made available to the industry, Form FDA 2512 should be filed for each cosmetic product being commercially distributed as of the effective date of this part. Form FDA 2512 should be filed within 60 days after the beginning of commercial distribution of any product not covered within the 180-day period.
