48 C.F.R. § 41.206 — 41.206 Interagency agreements.
Citation: us:cfr:48:41.206
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-41.206
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 41 (Acquisition Of Utility Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies shall use interagency agreements (e.g., consolidated purchase, joint use, or cross-service agreements) when acquiring utility service or facilities from other Government agencies and shall comply with the policies and procedures at 17.502-2, The Economy Act.
