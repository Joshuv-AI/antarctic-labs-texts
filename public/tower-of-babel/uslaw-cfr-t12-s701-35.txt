12 C.F.R. § 701.35 — Share, share draft, and share certificate accounts.
Citation: us:cfr:12:701.35
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-701.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 701 (Organization And Operation Of Federal Credit Unions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Federal credit unions may offer share, share draft, and share certificate accounts in accordance with section 107(6) of the Act (12 U.S.C. 1757(6)) and the board of directors may declare dividends on such accounts as provided in section 117 of the Act (12 U.S.C. 1763).
(b) A Federal credit union shall accurately represent the terms and conditions of its share, share draft, and share certificate accounts in all advertising, disclosures, or agreements, whether written or oral
