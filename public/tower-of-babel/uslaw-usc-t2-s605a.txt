2 U.S.C. § 605a — Contracting parity
Citation: us:usc:2:605a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section605a
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 17 (Congressional Budget Office). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In fiscal year 2018 and thereafter, for all contracts for goods and services to which the Congressional Budget Office is a party, the following Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) clauses will apply: FAR 52.232–39 and FAR 52.233–4.
