43 C.F.R. § 14.4 — Publication of petitions.
Citation: us:cfr:43:14.4
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-14.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 14 (Petitions For Rulemaking). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A petition for rulemaking may be published in the Federal Register if the official responsible for acting on the petition determines that public comment may aid in consideration of the petition.
