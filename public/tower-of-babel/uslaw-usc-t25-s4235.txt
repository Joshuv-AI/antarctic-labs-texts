25 U.S.C. § 4235 — Annual allocation
Citation: us:usc:25:4235
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section4235
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 43 (Native American Housing Assistance And Self-Determination); Subchapter VIII (Housing Assistance For Native Hawaiians). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For each fiscal year, the Secretary shall allocate any amounts made available for assistance under this subchapter for the fiscal year, in accordance with the formula established pursuant to section 4236 of this title to the Department of Hawaiian Home Lands if the Department complies with the requirements under this subchapter for a grant under this subchapter.
