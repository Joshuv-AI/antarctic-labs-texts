48 C.F.R. § 207.7003 — 207.7003 Limitation.
Citation: us:cfr:48:207.7003
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-207.7003
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 207 (Acquisition Planning). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For noncompetitive acquisitions, the acquisition of additional quantities is limited to not more than 10 percent of the quantity approved in the justification and approval prepared in accordance with FAR part 6 for the acquisition of the end item.
