42 U.S.C. § 12213 — Severability
Citation: us:usc:42:12213
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section12213
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 126 (Equal Opportunity For Individuals With Disabilities); Subchapter IV (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Should any provision in this chapter be found to be unconstitutional by a court of law, such provision shall be severed from the remainder of the chapter, and such action shall not affect the enforceability of the remaining provisions of the chapter.
