Overpartitions with repeated smallest non-overlined part
Amita Malik, Rishabh Sarma

Abstract
. Inspired by Andrews’ and Bachraoui’s work on partitions with repeated smallest part, we extend the concept to overpartitions.



1.
Introduction

In recent work
[
3
]
, Andrews and Bachraoui consider integer partitions whose smallest part is repeated exactly
k
-times and the remaining parts are distinct, denoted by
\mathrm{spt}k_{d}(n)
, and prove that their generating functions are linear combinations of the
q
-Pochhammer symbols with polynomials in
q
as coefficients.

Let
N,m,n
be non-negative integers. Throughout, we use the following standard notations.

\displaystyle(a)_{N}=(a;q)_{N}

\displaystyle:=\prod_{k=0}^{N-1}(1-aq^{k}),

\displaystyle(a)_{\infty}=(a;q)_{\infty}

\displaystyle:=\lim_{N\rightarrow\infty}(a)_{N}\,\,\text{where}\,\,\lvert q\rvert<1.

The following two results of Andrews and Bachraoui concern the class of partitions described at the beginning.

Theorem 1.1
.

[
3
, Theorem 1]

For any positive integer
k
, we have

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\mathrm{spt}k_{d}(n)q^{n}=P_{k}(q)(-q;q)_{\infty}+(-1)^{k}(q;q)_{k-1},

where

\displaystyle P_{k}(q)=\begin{cases}1&\text{if }k=1,\\
(q^{k-1}-1)P_{k-1}(q)+q^{k-1}&\text{if }k>1.\end{cases}

And, a closed formula for
P_{k}(q)
is given by

Theorem 1.2
.

[
3
, Theorem 2]

For any positive integer
k
, we have

\displaystyle P_{2k-1}(q)=1+2\sum_{j=1}^{k-1}(-1)^{j}q^{j^{2}}(q^{2k-2j};q^{2})_{j},

\displaystyle P_{2k}(q)=-1-2\sum_{j=1}^{k}(-1)^{j}q^{j^{2}}(q^{2k-2j+2};q^{2})_{j-1}.

Subsequently, the authors carry out the same treatment for the same class of partitions but with the distinct parts being incongruent modulo
2
with the smallest part. Finally, special cases of these results lead to new identities and inequalities for partitions into distinct parts.

In this paper, we extend their work to overpartitions. Recall that an overpartition is a partition in which the first occurrence of a number may be overlined
[
5
]
.

Let
\overline{\mathrm{Spt}}k(n)
denote the set of overpartitions of
n
where the smallest non-overlined part, say
s(\pi)
, appears
k
times and every overlined part is bigger than
s(\pi)
. Accordingly, let
\overline{\mathrm{spt}}k(n)
be the cardinality of
\overline{\mathrm{Spt}}k(n)
. Also, let
a_{e}(k,n)
(resp.,
a_{o}(k,n)
) denote the number of partitions
\pi
of
n
in
\overline{\mathrm{Spt}}k(n)
where the number of parts that are greater than
s(\pi)
is even (resp., odd) and let

\overline{\mathrm{spt}}k^{\prime}(n)=a_{e}(k,n)-a_{o}(k,n).

Then, by standard combinatorial arguments, we may write

\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\overline{\mathrm{spt}}k(n)q^{n}=\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}q^{kn}\,\frac{(-q^{n+1};q)_{\infty}}{(q^{n+1};q)_{\infty}},

and

\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\overline{\mathrm{spt}}k^{\prime}(n)q^{n}=\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}q^{kn}\,\frac{(q^{n+1};q)_{\infty}}{(-q^{n+1};q)_{\infty}}.

Now we impose a restriction and define
\overline{\mathrm{Spt}}k_{o}(n)
to be the set of overpartitions of
n
where the smallest non-overlined part, say
s(\pi)
, appears
k
times, every overlined part is bigger than
s(\pi)
and all parts other than
s(\pi)
are incongruent modulo
2
with
s(\pi)
. Accordingly, let
\overline{\mathrm{spt}}k_{o}(n)
be the cardinality of
\overline{\mathrm{Spt}}k_{o}(n)
. Also, let
B_{e}(k,n)
(resp.,
B_{o}(k,n)
) denote the set of overpartitions
\pi
of
n
in
\overline{\mathrm{Spt}}k_{o}(n)
where the number of parts that are greater than
s(\pi)
is even (resp., odd). Accordingly let
b_{e}(k,n)
(resp.,
b_{o}(k,n)
) be the cardinality of
B_{e}(k,n)
(resp.,
B_{o}(k,n)
) and let

(1.1)

\overline{\mathrm{spt}}k_{o}^{\prime}(n)=b_{e}(k,n)-b_{o}(k,n).

Once again, by standard combinatorial arguments, we note that

\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\overline{\mathrm{spt}}k_{o}(n)q^{n}=\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}q^{kn}\,\frac{(-q^{n+1};q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q^{n+1};q^{2})_{\infty}},

and

\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\overline{\mathrm{spt}}k_{o}^{\prime}(n)q^{n}=\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}q^{kn}\,\frac{(q^{n+1};q^{2})_{\infty}}{(-q^{n+1};q^{2})_{\infty}}.

The paper is organized as follows. In Section
2
, we state our main results concerning the generating functions for the overpartition classes described previously in the introduction. Section
3
contains proofs of the results stated in Section
2
. Lastly, we present some corollaries which are of interest in their own right.

2.
Statements of main results

Our first result is a direct analogue of Theorem
1.1

[
3
, Theorem 1]
.

Theorem 2.1
.

For any positive integer
k
, we have

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\overline{\mathrm{spt}}k(n)q^{n}=\overline{P}_{k}(q)\frac{(-q^{2};q)_{\infty}}{(q^{2};q)_{\infty}}+(-1)^{k}\frac{(q;q)_{k-1}}{(-q;q)_{k}},

where

\displaystyle\overline{P}_{k}(q)=\begin{cases}1&\text{if }k=1,\\
\frac{(q^{k-1}-1)\overline{P}_{k-1}(q)+q^{k-1}(1+q)}{1+q^{k}}&\text{if }k>1.\end{cases}

Next, we give a closed formula for
\overline{P}_{k}(q)
as rational functions of
q
, analogous to Theorem
1.2

[
3
, Theorem 2]
.

Theorem 2.2
.

\displaystyle\overline{P}_{k}(q)=\frac{q^{k-1}(1+q)}{1+q^{k}}-\frac{(q;q)_{k-1}}{(-q^{2};q)_{k-1}}\sum\limits_{j=0}^{k-2}(-1)^{j}q^{k-j-2}\frac{(-q;q)_{k-j-2}}{(q;q)_{k-j-2}}.

Theorem 2.3
.

For any positive integer
k
, we have

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\overline{\mathrm{spt}}k^{\prime}(n)q^{n}=\frac{(q;q)_{k-1}}{(-q;q)_{k}}-\frac{(q;q)_{\infty}}{(-q;q)_{\infty}}.

Our next main theorem is an analogue of
[
3
, Theorem 5]
.

Theorem 2.4
.

For any positive integer
k
, we have

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\overline{\mathrm{spt}}k_{o}(n)q^{n}=\overline{V}_{k}(q)\frac{(-q^{4};q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q^{2};q^{2})_{\infty}}+\overline{W}_{k}(q)\frac{(-q;q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q^{3};q^{2})_{\infty}}+2(-q)^{k}\frac{(q^{2};q^{2})_{k-1}}{(-q^{2};q^{2})_{k}},

where

\displaystyle\overline{V}_{k}(q)=\begin{cases}2q&\text{if }k=1,\\
\frac{(q^{2k-1}-q)\overline{V}_{k-1}(q)+2q^{k}(1+q^{2})}{1+q^{2k}}&\text{if }k>1,\end{cases}

and

\displaystyle\overline{W}_{k}(q)=\begin{cases}\frac{q}{1+q^{2}}&\text{if }k=1,\\
\frac{(q^{2k-1}-q)\overline{W}_{k-1}(q)+q^{2k-1}}{1+q^{2k}}&\text{if }k>1.\end{cases}

Now, we give closed formulas for
\overline{V}_{k}(q)
and
\overline{W}_{k}(q)(q)
as rational functions of
q
, in analogy to
[
3
, Theorem 6]
.

Theorem 2.5
.

\displaystyle\overline{V}_{k}(q)

\displaystyle=(1+q^{2})\left(\frac{2q^{k}}{1+q^{2k}}+2q^{k}\frac{(q^{2};q^{2})_{k-1}}{(-q^{2};q^{2})_{k}}\sum\limits_{j=0}^{k-2}(-1)^{j+1}\frac{(-q^{2};q^{2})_{k-j-2}}{(q^{2};q^{2})_{k-j-2}}\right),

\displaystyle\overline{W}_{k}(q)

\displaystyle=\frac{q^{2k-1}}{1+q^{2k}}+q^{2k-2}\frac{(q^{2};q^{2})_{k-1}}{(-q^{2};q^{2})_{k}}\sum\limits_{j=0}^{k-2}\frac{(-1)^{j+1}}{q^{j}}\frac{(-q^{2};q^{2})_{k-j-2}}{(q^{2};q^{2})_{k-j-2}}.

Our last main theorem is an analogue of
[
3
, Theorem 3]
.

Theorem 2.6
.

For any positive integer
k
, we have

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\overline{\mathrm{spt}}k_{o}^{\prime}(n)q^{n}=\overline{T}_{k}(q)\frac{(q;q^{2})_{\infty}}{(-q;q^{2})_{\infty}}+2q^{k}\frac{(q^{2};q^{2})_{k-1}}{(-q^{2};q^{2})_{k}},

where

\displaystyle\overline{T}_{k}(q)=\begin{cases}-\frac{q(1+q)}{1+q^{2}}&\text{if }k=1,\\
\frac{(q-q^{2k-1})\overline{T}_{k-1}(q)-q^{2k-1}(1+q)}{1+q^{2k}}&\text{if }k>1.\end{cases}

Now, we give a closed formula for
\overline{T}_{k}(q)
as rational functions of
q
analogous to
[
3
, Theorem 4]
.

Theorem 2.7
.

\displaystyle\overline{T}_{k}(q)=-\frac{q^{2k-1}(1+q)}{1+q^{2k}}-(1+q)\frac{(q^{2};q^{2})_{k-1}}{(-q^{2};q^{2})_{k}}\sum\limits_{j=0}^{k-2}q^{2k-j-2}\frac{(-q^{2};q^{2})_{k-j-2}}{(q^{2};q^{2})_{k-j-2}}.

3.
Proofs

We begin by recording the
q
-binomial theorem
[
2
,
6
]

(3.1)

\sum_{n\geq 0}\frac{(a;q)_{n}}{(q;q)_{n}}z^{n}=\frac{(az;q)_{\infty}}{(z;q)_{\infty}},

and the following formula of Andrews, Subbarao, and Vidyasagar
[
4
]

(3.2)

\sum_{n\geq 0}\frac{(a;q)_{n}}{(b;q)_{n}}q^{n}=\frac{q(a;q)_{\infty}}{b(b;q)_{\infty}\big(1-\frac{aq}{b}\big)}+\frac{1-\frac{q}{b}}{1-\frac{aq}{b}}

which we use several times in this section.

3.1.
Proof of Theorem
2.1

Proof.

We prove this using the principle of mathematical induction on
k
.

The theorem is true for the case
k=1
as argued below. Letting
a=q
, and
b=-q
in equation (
3.2
), we have

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\overline{\mathrm{spt}}1(n)q^{n}

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}q^{n}\,\frac{(-q^{n+1};q)_{\infty}}{(q^{n+1};q)_{\infty}}

\displaystyle=\frac{(-q;q)_{\infty}}{(q;q)_{\infty}}\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(q;q)_{n}}{(-q;q)_{n}}q^{n}

\displaystyle=\frac{(-q;q)_{\infty}}{(q;q)_{\infty}}\left(-\frac{(q;q)_{\infty}}{(-q;q)_{\infty}(1+q)}+\frac{2}{1+q}-1\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{-1}{1+q}+\frac{2\,(-q;q)_{\infty}}{(1+q)(q;q)_{\infty}}-\frac{(-q;q)_{\infty}}{(q;q)_{\infty}}

\displaystyle=\frac{(-q^{2};q)_{\infty}}{(q^{2};q)_{\infty}}-\frac{1}{1+q}.

Next, let us assume that the theorem holds for
k-1
,
k\geq 2
, and we prove the result for
k
. Setting
\overline{C}_{k}(q):=\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\overline{\mathrm{spt}}k(n)q^{n}
, we have

\displaystyle\overline{C}_{k}(q)

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}q^{kn}\,\frac{(-q^{n+1};q)_{\infty}}{(q^{n+1};q)_{\infty}}

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}q^{(k-1)n}\,\left(\frac{1+q^{n}-1}{1-q^{n}}\right)\,(1-q^{n})\,\frac{(-q^{n+1};q)_{\infty}}{(q^{n+1};q)_{\infty}}

\displaystyle=-\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}q^{(k-1)n}\,\left(\frac{1-q^{n}}{1-q^{n}}\right)\,\frac{(-q^{n+1};q)_{\infty}}{(q^{n+1};q)_{\infty}}

\displaystyle\hskip 14.22636pt+\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}q^{(k-1)n}\,\left(\frac{1+q^{n}}{1-q^{n}}\right)\,(1-q^{n})\,\frac{(-q^{n+1};q)_{\infty}}{(q^{n+1};q)_{\infty}}

\displaystyle=-\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}q^{(k-1)n}\,\frac{(-q^{n+1};q)_{\infty}}{(q^{n+1};q)_{\infty}}+\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}q^{(k-1)n}\,\frac{(-q^{n};q)_{\infty}}{(q^{n};q)_{\infty}}-\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}q^{kn}\,\frac{(-q^{n};q)_{\infty}}{(q^{n};q)_{\infty}}

\displaystyle=-\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}q^{(k-1)n}\,\frac{(-q^{n+1};q)_{\infty}}{(q^{n+1};q)_{\infty}}+q^{k-1}\frac{(-q;q)_{\infty}}{(q;q)_{\infty}}+\sum\limits_{n=2}^{\infty}q^{(k-1)n}\,\frac{(-q^{n};q)_{\infty}}{(q^{n};q)_{\infty}}

\displaystyle\hskip 11.38109pt-q^{k}\frac{(-q;q)_{\infty}}{(q;q)_{\infty}}-\sum\limits_{n=2}^{\infty}q^{kn}\,\frac{(-q^{n};q)_{\infty}}{(q^{n};q)_{\infty}}

\displaystyle=-\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}q^{(k-1)n}\,\frac{(-q^{n+1};q)_{\infty}}{(q^{n+1};q)_{\infty}}+q^{k-1}\frac{(-q;q)_{\infty}}{(q;q)_{\infty}}+q^{k-1}\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}q^{(k-1)n}\,\frac{(-q^{n+1};q)_{\infty}}{(q^{n+1};q)_{\infty}}

\displaystyle\hskip 11.38109pt-q^{k}\frac{(-q;q)_{\infty}}{(q;q)_{\infty}}-q^{k}\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}q^{kn}\,\frac{(-q^{n+1};q)_{\infty}}{(q^{n+1};q)_{\infty}}

\displaystyle=-\left(\overline{P}_{k-1}(q)\frac{(-q^{2};q)_{\infty}}{(q^{2};q)_{\infty}}+(-1)^{k-1}\frac{(q;q)_{k-2}}{(-q;q)_{k-1}}\right)+q^{k-1}\frac{(-q;q)_{\infty}}{(q;q)_{\infty}}

\displaystyle\hskip 14.22636pt+q^{k-1}\left(\overline{P}_{k-1}(q)\frac{(-q^{2};q)_{\infty}}{(q^{2};q)_{\infty}}+(-1)^{k-1}\frac{(q;q)_{k-2}}{(-q;q)_{k-1}}\right)-q^{k}\frac{(-q;q)_{\infty}}{(q;q)_{\infty}}

\displaystyle\hskip 14.22636pt-q^{k}\overline{C}_{k}(q)

\displaystyle=(q^{k-1}-1)\overline{P}_{k-1}(q)\frac{(-q^{2};q)_{\infty}}{(q^{2};q)_{\infty}}+(q^{k-1}-q^{k})\frac{(-q;q)_{\infty}}{(q;q)_{\infty}}

\displaystyle\hskip 14.22636pt+(-1)^{k}(1-q^{k-1})\frac{(q;q)_{k-2}}{(-q;q)_{k-1}}-q^{k}\overline{C}_{k}(q)

\displaystyle=\left((q^{k-1}-1)\overline{P}_{k-1}(q)+\frac{(q^{k-1}-q^{k})(1+q)}{1-q}\right)\frac{(-q^{2};q)_{\infty}}{(q^{2};q)_{\infty}}

\displaystyle\hskip 14.22636pt+(-1)^{k}\frac{(q;q)_{k-1}}{(-q;q)_{k-1}}-q^{k}\overline{C}_{k}(q).

Adding
q^{k}\overline{C}_{k}(q)
on both sides gives us the theorem.
∎

3.2.
Proof of Theorem
2.2

Proof.

Let
(\overline{p}_{k}(q))=(\overline{p}_{k}(q))_{k\geq 1}
be the sequence defined by

\overline{p}_{k}(q)=\frac{q^{k-1}(1+q)}{1+q^{k}}-\frac{(q;q)_{k-1}}{(-q^{2};q)_{k-1}}\sum\limits_{j=0}^{k-2}(-1)^{j}q^{k-j-2}\frac{(-q;q)_{k-j-2}}{(q;q)_{k-j-2}}.

First we note that

\overline{p}_{1}(q)=1,

which is the same as
\overline{P}_{1}(q)
in Theorem
2.1
.

Next we show below that
\overline{p}_{k}(q)
satisfies the same recurrence as
\overline{P}_{k}(q)
given in Theorem
2.1
.

\displaystyle\overline{p}_{k}(q)

\displaystyle=\frac{q^{k-1}(1+q)}{1+q^{k}}-\frac{(q;q)_{k-1}}{(-q^{2};q)_{k-1}}\left(q^{k-2}\frac{(-q;q)_{k-2}}{(q;q)_{k-2}}+\sum_{j=1}^{k-2}(-1)^{j}q^{k-j-2}\frac{(-q;q)_{k-j-2}}{(q;q)_{k-j-2}}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{q^{k-1}(1+q)}{1+q^{k}}-\frac{q^{k-2}(1-q^{k-1})(1+q)}{(1+q^{k-1})(1+q^{k})}+\frac{(q;q)_{k-1}}{(-q^{2};q)_{k-1}}\sum_{m=0}^{k-3}(-1)^{m}q^{k-m-3}\frac{(-q;q)_{k-m-3}}{(q;q)_{k-m-3}}

\displaystyle=\frac{q^{k-1}(1+q)}{1+q^{k}}-\frac{q^{k-2}(1-q^{k-1})(1+q)}{(1+q^{k-1})(1+q^{k})}

\displaystyle\hskip 14.22636pt-\frac{-1+q^{k-1}}{1+q^{k}}\frac{(q;q)_{k-2}}{(-q^{2};q)_{k-2}}\sum_{m=0}^{k-3}(-1)^{m}q^{k-m-3}\frac{(-q;q)_{k-m-3}}{(q;q)_{k-m-3}}

\displaystyle=\frac{q^{k-1}(1+q)}{1+q^{k}}-\frac{q^{k-2}(1-q^{k-1})(1+q)}{(1+q^{k-1})(1+q^{k})}+\frac{-1+q^{k-1}}{1+q^{k}}\left(\overline{p}_{k-1}(q)-\frac{q^{k-2}(1+q)}{1+q^{k-1}}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{q^{k-1}(1+q)+(q^{k-1}-1)\overline{p}_{k-1}(q)}{1+q^{k}}.

This completes the proof of the theorem.

∎

3.3.
Proof of Theorem
2.3

Proof.

We have

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\overline{\mathrm{spt}}k^{\prime}(n)q^{n}

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}q^{kn}\,\frac{(q^{n+1};q)_{\infty}}{(-q^{n+1};q)_{\infty}}

\displaystyle=\frac{(q;q)_{\infty}}{(-q;q)_{\infty}}\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-q;q)_{n}}{(q;q)_{n}}q^{kn}

\displaystyle=\frac{(q;q)_{\infty}}{(-q;q)_{\infty}}\frac{(-q^{k+1};q)_{\infty}}{(q^{k};q)_{\infty}}-\frac{(q;q)_{\infty}}{(-q;q)_{\infty}}

\displaystyle=\frac{(q;q)_{k-1}}{(-q;q)_{k}}-\frac{(q;q)_{\infty}}{(-q;q)_{\infty}},

where in the third step we have used the
q
-binomial theorem in Equation (
3.1
) with
a=-q
, and
z=q^{k}
.
∎

3.4.
Proof of Theorem
2.4

Proof.

We again prove this using mathematical induction on
k
.

The theorem is true for the case
k=1
as argued below. Letting
q\rightarrow q^{2}
,
a=q
and
b=-q
in Equation (
3.2
) for the first sum below and
q\rightarrow q^{2}
,
a=q^{2}
and
b=-q^{2}
in Equation (
3.2
) for the second sum below, we have

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\overline{\mathrm{spt}}1_{o}(n)q^{n}

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}q^{n}\,\frac{(-q^{n+1};q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q^{n+1};q^{2})_{\infty}}

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}q^{2n}\,\frac{(-q^{2n+1};q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q^{2n+1};q^{2})_{\infty}}+\sum\limits_{n=0}^{\infty}q^{2n+1}\,\frac{(-q^{2n+2};q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q^{2n+2};q^{2})_{\infty}}

\displaystyle=\frac{(-q;q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q;q^{2})_{\infty}}\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(q;q^{2})_{n}}{(-q;q^{2})_{n}}q^{2n}+q\frac{(-q^{2};q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q^{2};q^{2})_{\infty}}\sum\limits_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(q^{2};q^{2})_{n}}{(-q^{2};q^{2})_{n}}q^{2n}

\displaystyle=\frac{(-q;q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q;q^{2})_{\infty}}\left(-\frac{q(q;q^{2})_{\infty}}{(-q;q^{2})_{\infty}(1+q^{2})}+\frac{1+q}{1+q^{2}}-1\right)

\displaystyle\hskip 14.22636pt+\frac{q(-q^{2};q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q^{2};q^{2})_{\infty}}\left(-\frac{(q^{2};q^{2})_{\infty}}{(-q^{2};q^{2})_{\infty}(1+q^{2})}+\frac{2}{1+q^{2}}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{-2q}{1+q^{2}}+\frac{2q}{1+q^{2}}\frac{(-q^{2};q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q^{2};q^{2})_{\infty}}+\frac{q(1-q)}{1+q^{2}}\frac{(-q;q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q;q^{2})_{\infty}}

\displaystyle=2q\frac{(-q^{4};q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q^{2};q^{2})_{\infty}}+\frac{q}{1+q^{2}}\frac{(-q;q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q^{3};q^{2})_{\infty}}+\frac{-2q}{1+q^{2}}.

Next, let us assume that the theorem holds for
k-1
. Then setting
\overline{U}_{k}(q):=\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\overline{\mathrm{spt}}k_{o}(n)q^{n}
, we have

\displaystyle\overline{U}_{k}(q)

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}q^{kn}\,\frac{(-q^{n+1};q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q^{n+1};q^{2})_{\infty}}

\displaystyle=q^{k}\,\frac{(-q^{2};q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q^{2};q^{2})_{\infty}}+\sum\limits_{n=2}^{\infty}q^{kn}\,\frac{(-q^{n+1};q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q^{n+1};q^{2})_{\infty}}

\displaystyle=q^{k}\,\frac{(-q^{2};q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q^{2};q^{2})_{\infty}}+\sum\limits_{n=2}^{\infty}q^{(k-1)n}\,\frac{-q+q(1+q^{n-1})}{1-q^{n-1}}\,(1-q^{n-1})\,\frac{(-q^{n+1};q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q^{n+1};q^{2})_{\infty}}

\displaystyle=q^{k}\,\frac{(-q^{2};q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q^{2};q^{2})_{\infty}}-q\sum\limits_{n=2}^{\infty}q^{(k-1)n}\,\frac{(-q^{n+1};q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q^{n+1};q^{2})_{\infty}}+q\sum\limits_{n=2}^{\infty}q^{(k-1)n}\,\frac{(-q^{n-1};q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q^{n-1};q^{2})_{\infty}}

\displaystyle-\sum\limits_{n=2}^{\infty}q^{kn}\,\frac{(-q^{n-1};q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q^{n-1};q^{2})_{\infty}}

\displaystyle=2q^{k}\,\frac{(-q^{2};q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q^{2};q^{2})_{\infty}}-q\overline{U}_{k-1}(q)+q^{2k-1}\overline{U}_{k-1}(q)+q^{2k-1}\frac{(-q;q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q;q^{2})_{\infty}}-q^{2k}\overline{U}_{k}(q)

\displaystyle-q^{2k}\frac{(-q;q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q;q^{2})_{\infty}}

\displaystyle=2q^{k}(1+q^{2})\frac{(-q^{4};q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q^{2};q^{2})_{\infty}}+(q^{2k-1}-q)\Bigg(\overline{V}_{k-1}(q)\frac{(-q^{4};q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q^{2};q^{2})_{\infty}}+\overline{W}_{k-1}(q)\frac{(-q;q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q^{3};q^{2})_{\infty}}

\displaystyle+2(-q)^{k-1}\frac{(q^{2};q^{2})_{k-2}}{(-q^{2};q^{2})_{k-1}}\Bigg)+(q^{2k-1}-q^{2k})\frac{(-q;q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q;q^{2})_{\infty}}-q^{2k}\overline{U}_{k}(q)

\displaystyle=\frac{(-q^{4};q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q^{2};q^{2})_{\infty}}\Big(2q^{k}(1+q^{2})+(q^{2k-1}-q)\overline{V}_{k-1}(q)\Big)

\displaystyle+\frac{(-q;q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q^{3};q^{2})_{\infty}}\Big(q^{2k-1}+(q^{2k-1}-q)\overline{W}_{k-1}(q)\Big)

\displaystyle+2(q^{2k-1}-q)(-q)^{k-1}\frac{(q^{2};q^{2})_{k-2}}{(-q^{2};q^{2})_{k-1}}-q^{2k}\overline{U}_{k}(q).

Adding
q^{2k}\overline{U}_{k}(q)
on both sides gives us our theorem.
∎

3.5.
Proof of Theorem
2.5

Proof.

Let
(\overline{v}_{k}(q))=(\overline{v}_{k}(q))_{k\geq 1}
be the sequence defined by

\overline{v}_{k}(q)=(1+q^{2})\left(\frac{2q^{k}}{1+q^{2k}}+2q^{k}\frac{(q^{2};q^{2})_{k-1}}{(-q^{2};q^{2})_{k}}\sum\limits_{j=0}^{k-2}(-1)^{j+1}\frac{(-q^{2};q^{2})_{k-j-2}}{(q^{2};q^{2})_{k-j-2}}\right).

First we note that

\overline{v}_{1}(q)=2q,

which is the same as
\overline{V}_{1}(q)
in Theorem
2.4
.

Next we show below that
\overline{v}_{k}(q)
satisfies the same recurrence as
\overline{V}_{k}(q)
given in Theorem
2.4
.

\displaystyle\overline{v}_{k}(q)

\displaystyle=\frac{2q^{k}(1+q^{2})}{1+q^{2k}}+\frac{2q^{k}(1+q^{2})(q^{2};q^{2})_{k-1}}{(-q^{2};q^{2})_{k}}\left(\frac{-(-q^{2};q^{2})_{k-2}}{(q^{2};q^{2})_{k-2}}+\sum_{j=1}^{k-2}(-1)^{j+1}\frac{(-q^{2};q^{2})_{k-j-2}}{(q^{2};q^{2})_{k-j-2}}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{2q^{k}(1+q^{2})}{1+q^{2k}}-\frac{2q^{k}(1+q^{2})(1-q^{2k-2})}{(1+q^{2k})(1+q^{2k-2})}

\displaystyle\hskip 14.22636pt+\frac{2q^{k}(1+q^{2})(q^{2};q^{2})_{k-1}}{(-q^{2};q^{2})_{k}}\sum_{j=1}^{k-2}(-1)^{j+1}\frac{(-q^{2};q^{2})_{k-j-2}}{(q^{2};q^{2})_{k-j-2}}

\displaystyle=\frac{2q^{k}(1+q^{2})}{1+q^{2k}}-\frac{2q^{k}(1+q^{2})(1-q^{2k-2})}{(1+q^{2k})(1+q^{2k-2})}

\displaystyle\hskip 14.22636pt+\frac{2q^{k-1}(1+q^{2})(q^{2};q^{2})_{k-2}}{(-q^{2};q^{2})_{k-1}}q\,\frac{1-q^{2k-2}}{1+q^{2k}}\sum_{m=0}^{k-3}(-1)^{m}\frac{(-q^{2};q^{2})_{(k-1)-m-2}}{(q^{2};q^{2})_{(k-1)-m-2}}

\displaystyle=\frac{2q^{k}(1+q^{2})}{1+q^{2k}}-\frac{2q^{k}(1+q^{2})(1-q^{2k-2})}{(1+q^{2k})(1+q^{2k-2})}

\displaystyle\hskip 14.22636pt-q\,\frac{1-q^{2k-2}}{1+q^{2k}}\left(\frac{2q^{k-1}(1+q^{2})(q^{2};q^{2})_{k-2}}{(-q^{2};q^{2})_{k-1}}\sum_{m=0}^{k-3}(-1)^{m+1}\frac{(-q^{2};q^{2})_{(k-1)-m-2}}{(q^{2};q^{2})_{(k-1)-m-2}}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{2q^{k}(1+q^{2})}{1+q^{2k}}-\frac{2q^{k}(1+q^{2})(1-q^{2k-2})}{(1+q^{2k})(1+q^{2k-2})}-\frac{q(1-q^{2k-2})}{1+q^{2k}}\left(\overline{v}_{k-1}(q)-\frac{2q^{k-1}(1+q^{2})}{1+q^{2k-2}}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{2q^{k}(1+q^{2})}{1+q^{2k}}-\frac{2q^{k}(1+q^{2})(1-q^{2k-2})}{(1+q^{2k})(1+q^{2k-2})}+\frac{q^{2k-1}-q}{1+q^{2k}}\,\overline{v}_{k-1}(q)

\displaystyle\hskip 14.22636pt+\frac{2q^{k}(1-q^{2k-2})(1+q^{2})}{(1+q^{2k})(1+q^{2k-2})}

\displaystyle=\frac{(q^{2k-1}-q)\overline{v}_{k-1}(q)+2q^{k}(1+q^{2})}{1+q^{2k}}.

The same can be done to prove the formula for
\overline{W}_{k}(q)
and the steps of the proof follow very closely. We leave it as an exercise to the reader. This proves the theorem.
∎

\overline{A}_{k}(q):=\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\overline{\mathrm{spt}}k_{o}^{\prime}(n)q^{n}

Adding
q^{2k}\overline{A}_{k}(q)
on both sides gives us our theorem.
∎

4.
Applications to overpartitions

The generating function for the number of overpartitions of
n
denoted by
\overline{p}(n)
is given by

\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\overline{p}(n)q^{n}=\frac{(-q;q)_{\infty}}{(q;q)_{\infty}}.

And, the generating functions for the number of overpartitions of
n
into even (resp. odd) parts denoted by
\overline{p}_{e}(n)
(resp.
\overline{p}_{o}(n)
) is respectively given by

\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\overline{p}_{e}(n)q^{n}=\frac{(-q^{2};q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q^{2};q^{2})_{\infty}},

and

\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\overline{p}_{o}(n)q^{n}=\frac{(-q;q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q;q^{2})_{\infty}}.

Using Theorems
2.1
and
2.4
, one can write
\overline{\mathrm{spt}}k(n)
and
\overline{\mathrm{spt}}k_{o}(n)
as linear combinations of certain subclasses of overpartitions. Below are some examples.

For
k=1
, Theorem
2.1
gives

\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\overline{\mathrm{spt}}1(n)q^{n}=\frac{(-q^{2};q)_{\infty}}{(q^{2};q)_{\infty}}-\frac{1}{1+q}.

Rearranging and simplifying leads to

\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\overline{\mathrm{spt}}1(n)q^{n}+\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\overline{\mathrm{spt}}1(n)q^{n+1}+1=\frac{(-q;q)_{\infty}}{(q^{2};q)_{\infty}}.

Now the following result follows immediately.

Corollary 4.1
.

For
n>1
,
\overline{\mathrm{spt}}1(n)+\overline{\mathrm{spt}}1(n-1)
equals the number of overpartitions of
n
with no non-overlined
1
’s.

The cases for higher
k\geq 2
also lead to similar results but we do not pursue them here and for the subsequent spt-functions since they involve multiple combinations of the spt-functions and the corresponding subclass of overpartitions.

Next, for
k=1
, Theorem
2.4
gives

\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\overline{\mathrm{spt}}1_{o}(n)q^{n}=2q\frac{(-q^{4};q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q^{2};q^{2})_{\infty}}+\frac{q}{1+q^{2}}\frac{(-q;q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q^{3};q^{2})_{\infty}}+\frac{-2q}{1+q^{2}}.

Rearranging and simplifying leads to

\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\overline{\mathrm{spt}}1_{o}(n)q^{n}+\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\overline{\mathrm{spt}}1_{o}(n)q^{n+2}+2q=2q\frac{(-q^{2};q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q^{2};q^{2})_{\infty}}+q\frac{(-q;q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q^{3};q^{2})_{\infty}}.

This immediately gives the following result.

Corollary 4.2
.

For
n>2
, we have

\overline{\mathrm{spt}}1_{o}(n)+\overline{\mathrm{spt}}1_{o}(n-2)=2\overline{p}_{e}(n-1)+\overline{p}_{oex}(n-1),

where
\overline{p}_{oex}(n)
denotes the number of overpartitions of
n
into odd parts with no non-overlined
1
’s.

Lastly, to obtain a similar result for the overpartition difference function discussed in (
1.1
), we once again consider the overpartitions of
n
into odd parts with no non-overlined
1
’s enumerated by
\overline{p}_{oex}(n)
. Then we have

\frac{(q;q^{2})_{\infty}}{(-q^{3};q^{2})_{\infty}}=\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\overline{p}_{oex}^{\prime}(n)q^{n},

where
\overline{p}_{oex}^{\prime}(n)
denotes the number of overpartitions counted by
\overline{p}_{oex}(n)
where the number of parts is even minus the number of overpartitions counted by
\overline{p}_{oex}(n)
where the number of parts is odd.

Now, for
k=1
, Theorem
2.6
gives

\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\overline{\mathrm{spt}}1_{o}^{\prime}(n)q^{n}=\frac{2q}{1+q^{2}}+\frac{-q(1+q)}{1+q^{2}}\frac{(q;q^{2})_{\infty}}{(-q;q^{2})_{\infty}}.

Rearranging and simplifying leads to

\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\overline{\mathrm{spt}}1_{o}^{\prime}(n)q^{n}+\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\overline{\mathrm{spt}}1_{o}^{\prime}(n)q^{n+2}=2q-q\frac{(q;q^{2})_{\infty}}{(-q^{3};q^{2})_{\infty}}.

The following result follows immediately.

Corollary 4.3
.

For
n>2
,

\overline{\mathrm{spt}}1_{o}^{\prime}(n)+\overline{\mathrm{spt}}1_{o}^{\prime}(n-2)=-\overline{p}_{oex}^{\prime}(n-1).
