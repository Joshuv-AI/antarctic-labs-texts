42 U.S.C. § 5512 — Small business concerns’ opportunities to participate in programs
Citation: us:usc:42:5512
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section5512
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 71 (Solar Energy); Subchapter I (Heating And Cooling). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In carrying out their functions under this subchapter, all Federal officers and agencies shall take steps to assure that small business concerns will have realistic and adequate opportunities to participate in the programs under this subchapter to the maximum extent possible.
