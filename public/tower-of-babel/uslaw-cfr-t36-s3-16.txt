36 C.F.R. § 3.16 — May I swim or wade in park waters?
Citation: us:cfr:36:3.16
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-3.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 3 (Boating And Water Use Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Swimming or wading is allowed in waters, subject to closures or restrictions designated by the superintendent in accordance with §§ 1.5 and 1.7 of this chapter.
