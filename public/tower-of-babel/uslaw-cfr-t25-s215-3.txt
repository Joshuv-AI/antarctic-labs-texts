25 C.F.R. § 215.3 — Manner and time of royalty payments.
Citation: us:cfr:25:215.3
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-215.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 215 (Lead And Zinc Mining Operations And Leases, Quapaw Agency). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All royalties belonging to the lessor shall be paid to the superintendent of the Quapaw Agency at Miami, Okla., or such other official as the Secretary of the Interior may designate, for the benefit of the lessor, not later than 15 days from the 1st of each month for ore and concentrates sold during the preceding month.
