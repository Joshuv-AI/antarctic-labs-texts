20 C.F.R. § 355.43 — Collection of civil penalties and assessments.
Citation: us:cfr:20:355.43
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-355.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 355 (Regulations Under The Administrative False Claims Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The statutes at 31 U.S.C. 3806 and 3808(b) authorize actions for collection of civil penalties and assessments imposed under this part and specify the procedures for such actions.
