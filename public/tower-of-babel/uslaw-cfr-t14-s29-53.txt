14 C.F.R. § 29.53 — Takeoff: Category A.
Citation: us:cfr:14:29.53
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-29.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 29 (Airworthiness Standards: Transport Category Rotorcraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The takeoff performance must be determined and scheduled so that, if one engine fails at any time after the start of takeoff, the rotorcraft can—
(a) Return to, and stop safely on, the takeoff area; or
(b) Continue the takeoff and climbout, and attain a configuration and airspeed allowing compliance with § 29.67(a)(2).
