40 C.F.R. § 261.176 — Special requirements for ignitable or reactive hazardous secondary material.
Citation: us:cfr:40:261.176
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-261.176
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 261 (Identification And Listing Of Hazardous Waste). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Containers holding ignitable or reactive hazardous secondary material must be located at least 15 meters (50 feet) from the facility's property line.
