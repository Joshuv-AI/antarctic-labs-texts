32 C.F.R. § 935.163 — Unexploded ordnance material.
Citation: us:cfr:32:935.163
Creator: Department Of The Air Force
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-935.163
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 935 (Wake Island Code). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person who discovers any unexploded ordnance material on Wake Island shall refrain from tampering with it and shall immediately report its site to the Commander.
