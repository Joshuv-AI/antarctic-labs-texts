5 C.F.R. § 1650.22 — Accounts of $200 or more.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1650.22
Creator: Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1650.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1650 (Methods Of Withdrawing Funds From The Thrift Savings Plan). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A participant whose account balance is $200 or more must submit a properly completed distribution election to request a post-employment distribution of his or her account balance.
