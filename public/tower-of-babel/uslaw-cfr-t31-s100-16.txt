31 C.F.R. § 100.16 — Exchange of paper and coin to be handled through Federal Reserve banks and branches.
Citation: us:cfr:31:100.16
Creator: Monetary Offices, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-100.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 100 (Exchange Of Paper Currency And Coin). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Other than as provided in this document all transactions including the exchange of paper currency and coin shall be handled through the Federal Reserve banks and branches.
