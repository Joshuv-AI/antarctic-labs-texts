48 C.F.R. § 2825.204 — 2825.204 Evaluating offers of foreign construction material.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2825.204
Creator: Chapter 28—Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2825.204
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2825 (Foreign Acquisition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The HCA, or designee at a level at or above the BPC, is the agency official authorized to make the determination in accordance with FAR 25.204(b) that using a particular domestic construction material would unreasonably increase the cost of the acquisition or would be impracticable.
