18 C.F.R. § 807.3 — Administrative agreements.
Citation: us:cfr:18:807.3
Creator: Susquehanna River Basin Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-807.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 807 (Water Withdrawal Registration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission may complete appropriate administrative agreements or arrangements to carry out this registration requirement through the offices of member jurisdictions. Forms developed by the Commission shall apprise registrants of any such agreements or arrangements, and provide appropriate instructions to complete and submit the form.
