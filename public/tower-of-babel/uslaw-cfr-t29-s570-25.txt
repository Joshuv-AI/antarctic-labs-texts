29 C.F.R. § 570.25 — Effect on laws other than the Federal child labor standards.
Citation: us:cfr:29:570.25
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-570.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 570 (Child Labor Regulations, Orders And Statements Of Interpretation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No provision of this subpart shall under any circumstances justify or be construed to permit noncompliance with the provisions of any other Federal law or of any State law or municipal ordinance establishing higher standards than those established under this subpart.
