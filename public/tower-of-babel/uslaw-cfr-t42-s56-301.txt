42 C.F.R. § 56.301 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:42:56.301
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-56.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 56 (Grants For Migrant Health Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations of this subpart, in addition to the regulations of subpart A of this part, are applicable to grants awarded pursuant to section 319(d)(1)(A) of the Act for the costs of operation of migrant health centers in high impact areas.
