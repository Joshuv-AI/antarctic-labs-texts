25 C.F.R. § 212.34 — Individual tribal assignments excluded.
Citation: us:cfr:25:212.34
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-212.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 212 (Leasing Of Allotted Lands For Mineral Development). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The reference in this part to Indian mineral owners does not include assignments of tribal lands made pursuant to tribal constitutions or ordinances for the use of individual Indians and assignees of such lands.
