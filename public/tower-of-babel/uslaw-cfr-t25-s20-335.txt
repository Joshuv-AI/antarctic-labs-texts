25 C.F.R. § 20.335 — What is the payment standard for Adult Care Assistance?
Citation: us:cfr:25:20.335
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-20.335
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 20 (Financial Assistance And Social Services Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The approved payment for adult care assistance will not exceed the applicable state payment rate for similar care.
