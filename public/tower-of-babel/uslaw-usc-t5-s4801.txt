5 U.S.C. § 4801 — Nonapplicability of chapter 47

Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart C (Employee Performance); Chapter 48 (Agency Personnel Demonstration Project). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.
