25 U.S.C. § 98 — Purchase of supplies without authority
Citation: us:usc:25:98
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section98
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 4 (Performance By United States Of Obligations To Indians); Subchapter I (Purchase Of Supplies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No claims for supplies for Indians, purchased without authority of law, shall be paid out of any appropriation for expenses of the Office of Indian Affairs, or for Indians.
