32 C.F.R. § 1662.19 — The FOIA Exemption 2: Internal personnel rules and practices.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1662.19
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1662.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1662 (Freedom Of Information Act (Foia) Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The FOIA exempts from disclosure records that are related solely to the internal personnel rules and practices of an agency.
