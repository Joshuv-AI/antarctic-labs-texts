25 C.F.R. § 169.6 — What statutory authority will BIA use to act on requests for rights-of-way under this part?
Citation: us:cfr:25:169.6
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-169.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 169 (Rights-Of-Way Over Indian Land). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

BIA will act on requests for rights-of-way using the authority in 25 U.S.C. 323-328, and relying on supplementary authority such as 25 U.S.C. 2218, where appropriate.
