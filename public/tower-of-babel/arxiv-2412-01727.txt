Ruled surfaces and hyper-dual tangent sphere bundle
K. Derkaoui, F. Hathout , M. Bekar and Y. Yayli

Abstract
In this study, we define the unit hyper-dual sphere 𝕊 𝔻 2 \mathbb{S}_{\mathbb{D}_{2}} in hyper-dual vectors 𝔻 2 \mathbb{D}_{2} and we give E-Study map version in 𝔻 2 \mathbb{D}_{2} which prove that 𝕊 𝔻 2 2 \mathbb{S}_{\mathbb{D}_{2}}^{2} is isomorphism to the tangent bundle T ​ 𝕊 𝔻 2 . T\mathbb{S}_{\mathbb{D}}^{2}. Next, we define ruled surfaces in 𝔻 \mathbb{D} , we give its developability condition and a geometric interpretation in ℝ 3 \mathbb{R}^{3} of any curves in 𝔻 2 \mathbb{D}_{2} . Finally, we present a relationship between a ruled surfaces set in ℝ 3 \mathbb{R}^{3} and curves in hyper dual vectors 𝔻 2 \mathbb{D}_{2} . We close each study with examples. Keywords: Hyper-dual vectors, unit hyper-dual sphere, tangent bundle, E-Study map, Ruled surface. MSC(2010): 53A04, 53A05, 53A17, 55R25
