1 C.F.R. § 17.7 — Criteria for deferred schedule.
Citation: us:cfr:1:17.7
Creator: Administrative Committee Of The Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-1/section-17.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 1 (General Provisions), Part 17 (Filing For Public Inspection And Publication Schedules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-23 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A document may be assigned to the deferred schedule under the following conditions:
(1) There are technical problems, unusual or lengthy tables, or illustrations, or the document is of such size as to require extraordinary processing time.
(2) The agency concerned requests a deferred publication date.
(b) The Office of the Federal Register staff will notify the agency if its documents must be assigned to a deferred schedule.
