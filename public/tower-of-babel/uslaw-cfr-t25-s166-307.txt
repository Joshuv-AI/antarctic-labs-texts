25 C.F.R. § 166.307 — Will the grazing capacity be increased if I graze adjacent trust or non-trust rangelands not covered by the permit?
Citation: us:cfr:25:166.307
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-166.307
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 166 (Grazing Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. You will not receive an increase in grazing capacity in the permit if you graze trust or non-trust rangeland in common with the permitted land. Grazing capacity will be established only for Indian land covered by your permit.
