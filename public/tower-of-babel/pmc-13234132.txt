Correction: Effects of different powers of repeated low-level red light on form-deprivation myopia inhibition in guinea pigs
Authors: Ling Huang, Mengyuan Gao, Aobo Ma, Yunyan Wu, Jiarui Fei, Guoying Mu, Jiangping Hou
Published: 2026

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-31458-4, published online 10 December 2025

The original version of this Article contained an error in Figure 1, where “2.0 mW RLRL (N=12)” was given as “12.0 mW RLRL (N=12)”

The original Figure 1 and the accompanying legend appear below.

Experimental flowchart. D, diopter; NC, normal control; FDM, form-deprivation myopia; RLRL, repeated low-level red light; AL, axial length; ChT, choroidal thickness; ChBP, choroidal blood perfusion.

Additionally, the Funding was omitted. The Funding section now reads: “This work was supported by the Natural Science Foundation of Shandong Province (Grant No. ZR2012HM008).”

The original Article has been corrected.
