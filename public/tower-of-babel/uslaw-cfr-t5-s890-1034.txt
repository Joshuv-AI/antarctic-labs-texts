5 C.F.R. § 890.1034 — Counting a period of suspension as part of a subsequent debarment.
Citation: us:cfr:5:890.1034
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-890.1034
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 890 (Federal Employees Health Benefits Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The debarring official may consider the provider's contiguous period of suspension when determining the length of a debarment.
