25 C.F.R. § 307.2 — Certificates of genuineness; by whom affixed.
Citation: us:cfr:25:307.2
Creator: Indian Arts And Crafts Board, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-307.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 307 (Navajo All-Wool Woven Fabrics; Use Of Government Certificate Of Genuineness). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Government certificates of genuineness for Navajo all-wool woven fabrics may be affixed to fabrics meeting the conditions specified in § 307.4 by persons duly authorized to affix such certificates, under license issued by the Indian Arts and Crafts Board.
