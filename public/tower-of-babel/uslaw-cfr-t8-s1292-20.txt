8 C.F.R. § 1292.20 — Roster of recognized organizations and accredited representatives.
Citation: us:cfr:8:1292.20
Creator: Executive Office For Immigration Review, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-1292.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 1292 (Representation And Appearances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Assistant Director for Policy (or the Assistant Director for Policy's delegate) shall maintain a roster of recognized organizations and their accredited representatives. An electronic copy of the roster shall be made available to the public and updated periodically.
