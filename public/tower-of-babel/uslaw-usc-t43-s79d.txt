43 U.S.C. § 79d — Alaska land claimant liable for fees, commissions or purchase money; deposit in Treasury
Citation: us:usc:43:79d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section79d
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 4 (District Land Offices). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No provision of this Act shall relieve any public land claimant from the necessity of making payment of fees, commissions, or purchase money required by law or regulation in connection with an application, selection, location, or lease of public lands in Alaska, and all such payments, when made, shall be covered into the Treasury of the United States.
