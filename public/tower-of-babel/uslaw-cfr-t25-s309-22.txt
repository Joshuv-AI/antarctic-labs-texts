25 C.F.R. § 309.22 — What are examples of painting and other fine art forms that are Indian products?
Citation: us:cfr:25:309.22
Creator: Indian Arts And Crafts Board, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-309.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 309 (Protection Of Indian Arts And Crafts Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Painting and other fine art forms made by an Indian including but, not limited to, works on canvas, photography, sand painting, mural, computer generated art, graphic art, video art work, printmaking, drawing, bronze casting, glasswork, and art forms to be developed in the future, are Indian products.
