44 U.S.C. § 1721 — Exchange of documents by heads of departments
Citation: us:usc:44:1721
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section1721
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 17 (Distribution And Sale Of Public Documents). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Heads of departments may exchange surplus documents for other documents and books required by them, when it is to the advantage of the public service.
