17 C.F.R. § 32.1 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:17:32.1
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-32.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 32 (Regulation Of Commodity Option Transactions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this part shall apply to all commodity option transactions, except for commodity option transactions on a contract of sale of a commodity for future delivery conducted or executed on or subject to the rules of either a designated contract market or a foreign board of trade.
