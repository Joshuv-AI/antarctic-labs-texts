33 C.F.R. § 154.1310 — Purpose and applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:33:154.1310
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-154.1310
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 154 (Facilities Transferring Oil Or Hazardous Material In Bulk). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart establishes oil spill response planning requirements for an owner or operator of a facility that handles, stores, or transports other non-petroleum oils. The requirements of this subpart are intended for use in developing response plans and identifying response resources during the planning process. They are not performance standards.
