45 C.F.R. § 1225.6 — Freedom from reprisal.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1225.6
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1225.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1225 (Member And Volunteer Discrimination Complaint Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Aggrieved parties, their representatives, and witnesses will be free from restraint, interference, coercion, discrimination, or reprisal at any stage in the presentation and processing of a complaint, including the counseling stage described in § 1225.8, or any time thereafter.
