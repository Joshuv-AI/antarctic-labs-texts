46 C.F.R. § 126.180 — Carriage of passengers.
Citation: us:cfr:46:126.180
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-126.180
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 126 (Inspection And Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No passengers as defined by subparagraph (B) of the definition of passenger in 46 U.S.C. 2101 may be carried aboard an OSV except in an emergency.
