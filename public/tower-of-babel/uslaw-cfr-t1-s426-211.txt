1 C.F.R. § 426.211 — Other rights and services.
Citation: us:cfr:1:426.211
Creator: Miscellaneous Agencies
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-1/section-426.211
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 1 (General Provisions), Part 426 (National Commission On Military, National, And Public Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-23 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this subpart shall be construed to entitle any person, as of right, to any service or to the disclosure of any record to which such person is not entitled under the FOIA.
