48 C.F.R. § 23.108-2 — 23.108-2 Chemically-intensive products.

(a) Program. Safer Choice is EPA's label for products that contain safer chemicals. Every chemical, regardless of percentage, in a Safer Choice-certified product is evaluated through EPA's rigorous scientific process and only the safest ingredients are allowed.
(b) Resource. For information on Safer Choice-certified products, see https://www.epa.gov/saferchoice.
