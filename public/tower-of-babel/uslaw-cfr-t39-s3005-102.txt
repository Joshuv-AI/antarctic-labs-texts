39 C.F.R. § 3005.102 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3005.102
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3005.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3005 (Privacy Act Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of this part:
(a) Commission means the Postal Regulatory Commission.
(b) Individual, record, and system of records have the meanings specified in 5 U.S.C. 552a(a).
(c) Day means a calendar day and does not include Saturdays, Sundays, and legal holidays.
