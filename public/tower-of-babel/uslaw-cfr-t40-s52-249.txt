40 C.F.R. § 52.249 — California Heavy-Duty inspection and maintenance program.

Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-52.249
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 52 (Approval And Promulgation Of Implementation Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Approval of the California Heavy-Duty Vehicle Inspection and Maintenance Program, as approved on February 6, 2026 in table 1 of § 52.220a(c), is limited to vehicles registered in the State of California.
