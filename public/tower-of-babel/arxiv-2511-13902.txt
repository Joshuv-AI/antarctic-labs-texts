Groups Having a Character of Maximal Degree
Sara Jensen, Mark L. Lewis

Abstract
Let G G be a group, let d d be a character degree, and let e e be the integer so that | G | = d ⁡ ( d + e ) |G|=d(d+e) . It has been shown when e > 1 e>1 that | G | ≤ e 4 − e 3 |G|\leq e^{4}-e^{3} . In this paper, we consider the groups where | G | = e 4 − e 3 |G|=e^{4}-e^{3} . It is known that e e must be a power of a prime. We classify the groups where e e is a prime and where e e is 4 4 , 9 9 , and 25 25 . In so doing, we find a new nonsolvable Camina pair.

Background

In this section, we provide some background on the results that we need from the literature. First, we mention two transitive Frobenius groups. A nice description of two-transitive Frobenius groups can be found in Section 5 of
[
6
]
. Two-transitive Frobenius groups can be obtained using a near-field. There is an infinite family of near-fields, often called Dickson near-fields, and seven exceptional near-fields. The Dickson near-fields and four of the exceptional near-fields give two-transitive Frobenius groups that are solvable. There are three exceptional near fields that give two-transitive Frobenius groups that are not solvable. These three near fields have orders
11^{2}
,
29^{2}
, and
59^{2}
. In particular, there exist three nonsolvable two-transitive Frobenius groups:
(Z_{11}\times Z_{11})\rtimes{\rm SL}(2,5)
,
(Z_{29}\times Z_{29})\rtimes({\rm SL}(2,5)\times Z_{7})
, and
(Z_{59}\times Z_{59})\rtimes({\rm SL}(2,5)\times Z_{29})
. Another perspective on two-transitive Frobenius groups can be found in Sections 18 and 19 of
[
20
]
.

As we state in the introduction, Isaacs groups are examples of groups that were first studied by Gagola in
[
10
]
. In that paper, Gagola considers a group

that has an irreducible character that vanishes on all but two conjugacy classes of

is a

if

has a Gagola character. Assuming that

must have a unique minimal normal subgroup

and that

will be an elementary abelian

-group for some prime

Under the additional assumption that

is not a
p
-group, Gagola shows that

and if

, then there is a Sylow

-subgroup

of

so that

is the stabilizer of

in

is fully-ramified with-respect to

. In addition, if

is a

-subgroup of

Let

be a normal subgroup of

Gagola groups are a special case of a broader class of groups we will need to consider. Let

It is known that if

is a

Another related concept that we need is of a semi-extraspecial group. Let

If

Now, Camina pairs of the form

When classifying

Now, Verardi proves in Corollary 5.11 of
