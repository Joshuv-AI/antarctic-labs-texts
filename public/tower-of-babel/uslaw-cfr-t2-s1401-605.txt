2 C.F.R. § 1401.605 — How are violations of this part determined for recipients who are individuals?
Citation: us:cfr:2:1401.605
Creator: Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1401.605
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1401 (Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An individual recipient is in violation of the requirements of this part if the Director, PAM determines, in writing, that—
(a) The recipient has violated the requirements of subpart C of this part; or
(b) The recipient is convicted of a criminal drug offense resulting from a violation occurring during the conduct of any award activity.
