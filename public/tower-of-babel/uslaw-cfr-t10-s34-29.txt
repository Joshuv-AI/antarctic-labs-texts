10 C.F.R. § 34.29 — Quarterly inventory.
Citation: us:cfr:10:34.29
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-34.29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 34 (Licenses For Industrial Radiography And Radiation Safety Requirements For Industrial Radiographic Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each licensee shall conduct a quarterly physical inventory to account for all sealed sources and for devices containing depleted uranium received and possessed under this license.
(b) The licensee shall maintain records of the quarterly inventory in accordance with § 34.69.
