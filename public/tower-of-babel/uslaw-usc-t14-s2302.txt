14 U.S.C. § 2302 — Enlistments; term, grade
Citation: us:usc:14:2302
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2302
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 23 (Personnel; Enlisted). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Under regulations prescribed by the Secretary, the Commandant may enlist persons for the duration of their minority or a period of at least two years but not more than six years.
(b)
The Secretary shall prescribe the grades or ratings for persons enlisting in the Regular Coast Guard.
