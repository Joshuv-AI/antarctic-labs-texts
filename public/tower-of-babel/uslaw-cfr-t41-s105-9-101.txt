41 C.F.R. § 105-9.101 — -9.101 Purpose of the Age Discrimination Act of 1975.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-9.101
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-9.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-9 (9—Discrimination Prohibited On The Basis Of Age). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Age Discrimination Act of 1975, as amended, prohibits discrimination on the basis of age in programs or activities receiving Federal financial assistance.
