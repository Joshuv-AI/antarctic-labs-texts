Retraction Note: Optimizing credit card fraud detection with random forests and SMOTE
Authors: P. Sundaravadivel, R. Augustian Isaac, D. Elangovan, D. KrishnaRaj, V. V. Lokesh Rahul, R. Raja
Published: 2025

Retraction of: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-00873-y, published online 22 May 2025

The Editors have retracted this Article.

After publication of this Article concerns were raised regarding the dataset used in the study and the presentation of the results in several figures. Based on the description provided in the Article the source of the data appears unclear. In addition, there are concerns about the inclusion of non-existent references. The Authors did not provide the underlying raw data and their response to the Editors did not resolve the concerns. The Editors have therefore lost confidence in the data, results, and conclusions of this Article.

R. Augustian Isaac, D. Elangovan, D. KrishnaRaj, V. V. Lokesh Rahul, and R. Raja did not respond to the Editors’ communication about this retraction. P. Sundaravadivel responded to the Editors but did not indicate if they agree or disagree with the retraction.
