48 C.F.R. § 2014.407-4 — 2014.407-4 Mistakes after award.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2014.407-4
Creator: Chapter 20—Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2014.407-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2014 (Sealed Bidding). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The cognizant contracting officer is delegated the authority to make determinations concerning mistakes disclosed after award in accordance with FAR 14.407-4. These determinations will be concurred in by legal counsel prior to notification of the contractor.
