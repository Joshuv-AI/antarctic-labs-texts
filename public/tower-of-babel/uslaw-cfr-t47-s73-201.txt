47 C.F.R. § 73.201 — Numerical designation of FM broadcast channels.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 73 (Radio Broadcast Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The FM broadcast band consists of that portion of the radio frequency spectrum between 88 MHz and 108 MHz. It is divided into 100 channels of 200 kHz each.
