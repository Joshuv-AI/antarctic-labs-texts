48 C.F.R. § 1429.303 — 1429.303 Application of State and local taxes to Government contractors and subcontractors.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1429.303
Creator: Chapter 14—Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1429.303
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1429 (Taxes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contractors to be treated as agents of the Government for the purposes set forth in FAR 29.303(a) shall require the written review and approval of the AS/PMB. The HCA shall submit requests for approval through SOL, to the Director, PAM, for further action.
