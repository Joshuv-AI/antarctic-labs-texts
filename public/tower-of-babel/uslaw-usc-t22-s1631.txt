22 U.S.C. § 1631 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:22:1631
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1631
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 21 (Settlement Of International Claims); Subchapter II (Vesting And Liquidation Of Bulgarian, Hungarian, And Rumanian Property). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this subchapter the term—
(1)
“Person” means a natural person, partnership, association, other unincorporated body, corporation, or body politic.
(2)
“Property” means any property, right, or interest.
(3)
“Treaty of peace”, with respect to a country, means the treaty of peace with that country signed at Paris, France, February 10, 1947, which came into force between that country and the United States on September 15, 1947.
