42 C.F.R. § 86.33 — Human subjects; animal welfare.
Citation: us:cfr:42:86.33
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-86.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 86 (Grants For Education Programs In Occupational Safety And Health). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where the application is for training at a non-Federal institution, no award may be made under this subpart unless said institution has complied with:
(a) 45 CFR part 46 pertaining to the protection of human subjects; and
(b) Chapter 1-43 of the Department Grants Administration Manual 2 concerning animal welfare.
