25 C.F.R. § 12.11 — Do I have to follow these regulations?
Citation: us:cfr:25:12.11
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-12.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 12 (Indian Country Law Enforcement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must follow the minimum standards outlined in the regulations in this part if you are part of a BIA or tribal law enforcement program receiving Federal funding or operating under a BIA law enforcement commission.
