25 C.F.R. § 168.3 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:25:168.3
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-168.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 168 (Grazing Regulations For The Hopi Partitioned Lands Area). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These regulations are issued to implement the Secretary's responsibilities mandated by the Settlement Act and subsequent U.S. District Court Judgement filed May 4, 1982, in the case, Hopi Tribe v. Watt, Civ. No. 81-272 PCT-EHC. This portion of the regulations apply only to lands partitioned to the Hopi Tribe within the former Joint Use Area.
