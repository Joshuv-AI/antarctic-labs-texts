7 C.F.R. § 2100.011 — Biofuel feedstock crops.
Citation: us:cfr:7:2100.011
Creator: Office Of Energy And Environmental Policy, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 2100 (Technical Guidelines For The Production Of Regenerative Agricultural Biofuel Feedstocks). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Crops produced in accordance with subparts F and G of this part, where a CI is quantified in accordance with subpart C of this part, are referred to as reduced-CI crops. Crops produced not in accordance with this part are referred to as conventional crops. A farm producer may produce both reduced-CI crops and conventional crops. Reduced-CI crops and conventional crops can be physically mixed. Crops for which a reduced-CI may be quantified include:
(a) Field corn;
(b) Soybeans;
(c) Sorghum; and
(d) Spring canola.
