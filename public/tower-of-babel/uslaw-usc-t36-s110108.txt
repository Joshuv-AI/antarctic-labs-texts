36 U.S.C. § 110108 — Duty to maintain corporate and tax-exempt status
Citation: us:usc:36:110108
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section110108
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1101 (Jewish War Veterans Of The United States Of America, Incorporated). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Corporate Status.—
The corporation shall maintain its status as a corporation incorporated under the laws of New York.
(b) Tax-Exempt Status.—
The corporation shall maintain its status as an organization exempt from taxation under the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (26 U.S.C. 1 et seq.).
