33 C.F.R. § 6.16-3 — -3 Precautions against sabotage.
Citation: us:cfr:33:6.16-3
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-6.16-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 6 (Protection And Security Of Vessels, Harbors, And Waterfront Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The master, owner, agent, or operator of a vessel or waterfront facility shall take all necessary precautions to protect the vessel, waterfront facility, and cargo, including any data, information, network, program, system, or other digital infrastructure thereon or therein, from sabotage.
