29 C.F.R. § 1918.67 — Notifying the ship's officers before using certain equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1918.67
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1918.67
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1918 (Safety And Health Regulations For Longshoring). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The employer shall notify the officer in charge of the vessel before bringing aboard ship internal combustion or electric powered tools, equipment or vehicles.
(b) The employer shall also notify the officer in charge of the vessel before using the ship's electric power for the operation of any electric tools or equipment.
