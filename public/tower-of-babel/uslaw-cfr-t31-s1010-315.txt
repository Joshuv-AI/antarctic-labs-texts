31 C.F.R. § 1010.315 — Exemptions for non-bank financial institutions.
Citation: us:cfr:31:1010.315
Creator: Financial Crimes Enforcement Network, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-1010.315
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 1010 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A non-bank financial institution is not required to file a report otherwise required by § 1010.311 with respect to a transaction in currency between the institution and a commercial bank.
