25 C.F.R. § 900.116 — Are negotiated fixed-price contracts treated the same as cost-reimbursable contracts?
Citation: us:cfr:25:900.116
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior, And Indian Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-900.116
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 900 (Contracts Under The Indian Self-Determination And Education Assistance Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, except that in negotiated fixed-price construction contracts, appropriate clauses shall be negotiated to allocate properly the contract risks between the government and the contractor.
