28 C.F.R. § 523.15 — Camp or farm good time.
Citation: us:cfr:28:523.15
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-523.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 523 (Computation Of Sentence). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An inmate assigned to a farm or camp is automatically awarded extra good time, beginning on the date of commitment to the camp or farm, and continuing as long as the inmate is assigned to the farm or camp, unless the award is disallowed.
