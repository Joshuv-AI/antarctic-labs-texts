42 C.F.R. § 3.210 — Required disclosure of patient safety work product to the Secretary.
Citation: us:cfr:42:3.210
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-3.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 3 (Patient Safety Organizations And Patient Safety Work Product). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notwithstanding any other provision in this part, providers, PSOs, and responsible persons must disclose patient safety work product upon request by the Secretary when the Secretary determines such patient safety work product is needed to investigate or determine compliance or to seek or impose civil money penalties, with respect to this part or the HIPAA Privacy Rule, or to make or support decisions with respect to listing of a PSO.
