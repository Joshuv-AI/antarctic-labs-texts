Constraints on new physics couplings from B ¯ → D ∗ ​ ( D ​ π ) ​ ℓ ​ ν ¯ ℓ {\bar{B}}\to D^{*}\,(D\,\pi)\,\ell\,{\bar{\nu}}_{\ell} angular analysis
*

Abstract
The Belle Collaboration has measured the complete set of angular coefficient functions for the decays B ¯ → D ∗ ​ ( D ​ π ) ​ ℓ ​ ν ¯ ℓ {\bar{B}}\to D^{*}\,(D\,\pi)\,\ell\,{\bar{\nu}}_{\ell} , where ℓ = e , μ \ell=e,\,\mu , in four bins of the variable w = m B 2 + m D ∗ 2 − q 2 2 ​ m B ​ m D ∗ w={m_{B}^{2}+m_{D^{*}}^{2}-q^{2}\over 2\,m_{B}\,m_{D^{*}}} , with q q the momentum of the lepton pair. In SM this measurement is instrumental in determining the hadronic form factors in the B → D ∗ B\to D^{*} matrix elements of the SM weak current, thereby refining the measurement of | V c ​ b | \lvert V_{cb}\lvert . On the other hand, it can be used to assess the impact of possible new physics contributions. I consider an extension of the SM effective Hamiltonian that governs this mode, incorporating the complete set of Lorentz invariant d = 6 d=6 operators compatible with the gauge symmetry of the theory. The measured angular coefficient functions play a pivotal role in constraining the couplings in the generalized effective Hamiltonian.

Using Belle measurement of the angular coefficient functions
[
11

1

[

\Delta w^{(a)}
of the hadronic recoil parameter
w={m_{B}^{2}+m_{D^{*}}^{2}-q^{2}\over 2\,m_{B}\,m_{D^{*}}}
, with
q=p-p^{\prime}
. The binned coefficients are defined as
{\bar{J}}_{i}^{a}=\int_{{\Delta w}^{(a)}}J_{i}(w)dw
, and normalized as
{\hat{J}}_{i}=J_{i}/{\cal N}
, where
{\cal N}
is a normalization factor corresponding, up to a constant, to the integrated width. The values of the coefficients
{\hat{J}}_{i}
in the four bins
{\Delta w}^{(1)}=[1,\,1.15]
,
{\Delta w}^{(2)}=[1.15,\,1.25]
,
{\Delta w}^{(3)}=[1.25,\,1.35]
and
{\Delta w}^{(4)}=[1.35,\,1.5]
can be extracted from Fig. 1 of
[
11
]
. The products
({\hat{J}}_{i}^{a})^{\rm exp}_{\rm int}={\hat{J}}_{i}\cdot(\Delta w)^{a}
are computed. Using the expressions in
[
15
]
and setting
{\cal N}=0.146
to reproduce the measured branching ratio
[
16
]
, we compute the integrals of the coefficient functions for each bin
w
,
({\hat{J}}_{i}^{a})^{\rm th}_{\rm int}
. We impose the condition
({\hat{J}}_{i}^{a})^{\rm th}_{\rm int}\in[({\hat{J}}_{i}^{a})^{\rm exp}_{\rm int}-k\,\sigma_{i}^{a},\,({\hat{J}}_{i}^{a})^{\rm exp}_{\rm int}+k\,\sigma_{i}^{a}]
, where
k
is the number of standard deviations and
\sigma_{i}^{a}
is the error of the Belle result for each
{\hat{J}}_{i}^{a}
multiplied by the bin width. This provides 48 constraints, one for each of the four bins of the 12 angular coefficients. Focusing on the muon channel, we determine the set of parameters
(\epsilon_{V}^{\mu},\,\epsilon_{R}^{\mu},\,\epsilon_{P}^{\mu},\,\epsilon_{T}^{\mu})
that satisfies all constraints within the initial ranges
|\epsilon_{i}^{\mu}|\leq 0.5
for
i=V,\,R,\,P\,,T
(set 1). The smallest value of
k
that satisfies all constraints is
k=2.5
.

The reduced chi-square
\chi^{2}_{red}=\displaystyle\frac{1}{N_{dof}}\sum_{i,\,a}{\left(({\hat{J}}_{i}^{a})^{\rm th}_{\rm int}-({\hat{J}}_{i}^{a})^{\rm exp}_{\rm int}\right)^{2}}/{\left(\sigma_{i}^{a}\right)^{2}}

and

1.8

\epsilon_{V}=\epsilon_{R}=\epsilon_{P}=\epsilon_{T}=0

Summary

We have constrained the NP coefficients in the generalized low-energy Hamiltonian using a measurement of the full set of angular coefficient functions in
\bar{B}\to D^{*}(D\pi)\mu\bar{\nu}_{\mu}
. The possibility that some NP coefficients differ from zero emerges as a preliminary indication based on the data in
[
11
]
. This observation can be further validated once more complete experimental data will be available.
