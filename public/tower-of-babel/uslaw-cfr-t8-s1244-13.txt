8 C.F.R. § 1244.13 — Termination of temporary treatment benefits.
Citation: us:cfr:8:1244.13
Creator: Executive Office For Immigration Review, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-1244.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 1244 (Temporary Protected Status For Nationals Of Designated States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Temporary treatment benefits terminate upon a final determination with respect to the alien's eligibility for Temporary Protected Status.
(b) Temporary treatment benefits terminate, in any case, sixty (60) days after the date that notice is published of the termination of a foreign state's designation under section 244(b)(3) of the Act.
