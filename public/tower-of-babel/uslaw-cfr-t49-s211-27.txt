49 C.F.R. § 211.27 — Publication of adopted rules and withdrawal of notices.
Citation: us:cfr:49:211.27
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-211.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 211 (Rules Of Practice). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever the Administrator adopts a final rule or withdraws an advance notice or notice of proposed rulemaking, the final rule or a notice of withdrawal is published in the Federal Register.
