44 C.F.R. § 208.10 — Other regulations.

Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-208.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 208 (National Urban Search And Rescue Response System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following provisions of title 44 CFR, Chapter I also apply to the program in this part:
(a) Section 206.9, which deals with the non-liability of DHS in certain circumstances.
(b) Section 206.11, which prescribes nondiscrimination in the provision of disaster assistance.
(c) Section 206.14, which deals with criminal and civil penalties.
(d) Section 206.15, which permits recovery of assistance by DHS.
