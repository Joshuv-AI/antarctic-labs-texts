11 C.F.R. § 113.3 — Deposits of funds donated to a Federal or State officeholder (52 U.S.C. 30102(h)).
Citation: us:cfr:11:113.3
Creator: Federal Election Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-113.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 113 (Permitted And Prohibited Uses Of Campaign Accounts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All funds donated to a federal officeholder, or State officeholder who is a candidate for federal office, shall be deposited into one of the following accounts:
(a) An account of the officeholder's principal campaign committee or other authorized committee pursuant to 11 CFR part 103;
(b) An account to which only funds donated to an individual to support his or her activities as a holder of federal office are deposited (including an office account).
