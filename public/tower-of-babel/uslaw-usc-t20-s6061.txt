20 U.S.C. § 6061 — School prayer
Citation: us:usc:20:6061
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section6061
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 68 (National Education Reform); Subchapter X (Miscellaneous); Part A (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No funds authorized to be appropriated under this chapter may be used by any State or local educational agency to adopt policies that prevent voluntary prayer and meditation in public schools.
