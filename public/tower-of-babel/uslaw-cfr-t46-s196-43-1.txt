46 C.F.R. § 196.43-1 — -1 Application.
Citation: us:cfr:46:196.43-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-196.43-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 196 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart apply to all vessels on an international voyage, and all other vessels of 150 gross tons or over in ocean, coastwise, or Great Lakes service.
