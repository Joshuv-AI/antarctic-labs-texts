Retraction: Indocyanine Green Fluorescence-guided Thoracic Duct Sparing Esophagectomy for Corrosive Esophageal Stricture in a Pediatric Patient
Published: 2026

The case report titled “Indocyanine Green Fluorescence-guided Thoracic Duct Sparing Esophagectomy for Corrosive Esophageal Stricture in a Pediatric Patient”, published in Journal of Indian Association of Pediatric Surgeons, on pages 787-788, Volume 30, Issue 6, 2025,[1] is being retracted as the authors identified an unintended but factual critical errors in the article. To maintain the integrity of the scientific record, the authors requested that the article be withdrawn.

Plagiarism, fabrication, unethical or redundant publication violates the editorial policy of Journal of Indian Association of Pediatric Surgeons, which follows best practice guidelines given by the International Committee of Medical Journal Editors (ICMJE) and Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE) mentioned on the Information for Authors and as codified in the signed statements made by the authors regarding the copyright of their work.

This article is being retracted on request of the Authors, Editor-in-Chief and Editorial Board of the journal.
