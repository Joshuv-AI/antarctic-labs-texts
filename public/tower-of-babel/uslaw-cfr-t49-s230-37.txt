49 C.F.R. § 230.37 — Steam test following repairs or alterations.
Citation: us:cfr:49:230.37
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-230.37
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 230 (Steam Locomotive Inspection And Maintenance Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon completion of any repair or alteration, the locomotive owner and/or operator shall conduct a steam test of the boiler with steam pressure raised to between 95 percent and 100 percent of the MAWP. At this time, the boiler shall be inspected to ensure that it is in a safe and suitable condition for service.
