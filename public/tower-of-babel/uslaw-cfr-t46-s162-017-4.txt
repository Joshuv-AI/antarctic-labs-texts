46 C.F.R. § 162.017-4 — -4 Inspections and testing.
Citation: us:cfr:46:162.017-4
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-162.017-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 162 (Engineering Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pressure-vacuum relief valves may be inspected and tested at the plant of the manufacturer. An inspector may conduct such tests and examinations as may be necessary to determine compliance with this specification.
