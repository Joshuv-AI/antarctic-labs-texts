31 U.S.C. § 9302 — Prohibition against surety bonds for United States Government personnel
Citation: us:usc:31:9302
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section9302
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 93 (Sureties And Surety Bonds). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An agency (except a mixed-ownership Government corporation) may not require or obtain a surety bond for a member of the uniformed services or an officer or employee of the United States Government in carrying out official duties. This section does not affect the personal financial liability of the member, officer, or employee.
