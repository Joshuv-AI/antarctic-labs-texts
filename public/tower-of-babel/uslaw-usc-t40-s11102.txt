40 U.S.C. § 11102 — Sense of Congress
Citation: us:usc:40:11102
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section11102
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Chapter 111 (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the sense of Congress that, during the five-year period beginning with 1996, executive agencies should achieve each year through improvements in information resources management by the agency—
(1)
at least a five percent decrease in the cost (in constant fiscal year 1996 dollars) incurred by the agency in operating and maintaining information technology; and
(2)
a five percent increase in the efficiency of the agency operations.
