16 U.S.C. § 160j — Roads accessible to public facilities
Citation: us:usc:16:160j
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section160j
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter XIX (Voyageurs National Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to make provision for such roads within the park as are, or will be, necessary to assure access from present and future State roads to public facilities within the park.
