22 U.S.C. § 8785 — Termination

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 94 (Iran Threat Reduction And Syria Human Rights); Subchapter VI (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The provisions of sections 8721, 8722, 8723, 8725, 8726, 8727, and 8771 of this title, subchapter I, and subchapter III shall terminate on the date that is 30 days after the date on which the President makes the certification described in section 8551(a) of this title.
