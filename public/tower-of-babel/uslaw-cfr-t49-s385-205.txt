49 C.F.R. § 385.205 — Requirements for re-certification.
Citation: us:cfr:49:385.205
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-385.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 385 (Safety Fitness Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An individual who has lost certification to perform a safety audit or investigation, including review, can only be re-certified if they successfully complete the requirements of § 385.203(a) and (b).
