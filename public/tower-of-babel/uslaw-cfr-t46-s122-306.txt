46 C.F.R. § 122.306 — Passengers excluded from operating station.
Citation: us:cfr:46:122.306
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-122.306
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 122 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When practicable the master shall exclude passengers from the operating station of a vessel when the passengers could distract the navigating crew from their responsibilities, or when otherwise directed by the cognizant OCMI.
