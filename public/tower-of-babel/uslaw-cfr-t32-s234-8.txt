32 C.F.R. § 234.8 — Preservation of property.
Citation: us:cfr:32:234.8
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-234.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 234 (Conduct On The Pentagon Reservation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Willfully destroying or damaging private or government property is prohibited. The throwing of articles of any kind from or at buildings or persons, improper disposal of rubbish, and open fires are also prohibited.
