48 C.F.R. § 247.372 — 247.372 DD Form 1654, Evaluation of Transportation Cost Factors.
Citation: us:cfr:48:247.372
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-247.372
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 247 (Transportation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contracting personnel may use the DD Form 1654 to furnish information to the transportation office for development of cost factors for use by the contracting officer in the evaluation of f.o.b. origin offers.
