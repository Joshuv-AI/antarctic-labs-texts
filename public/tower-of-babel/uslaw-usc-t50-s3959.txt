50 U.S.C. § 3959 — Extension of protections to dependents
Citation: us:usc:50:3959
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section3959
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 50 (Servicemembers Civil Relief); Subchapter III (Rent, Installment Contracts, Mortgages, Liens, Assignment, Leases, Communications Service Contracts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Upon application to a court, a dependent of a servicemember is entitled to the protections of this subchapter if the dependent’s ability to comply with a lease, contract, bailment, or other obligation is materially affected by reason of the servicemember’s military service.
