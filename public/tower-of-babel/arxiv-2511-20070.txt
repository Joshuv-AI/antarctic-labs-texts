Proposition 1.1

For a ring

It is clear that if

Now, since it is known that a Dedekind-finite ring may not be right Dischinger, but a Dedekind-finite exchange ring is Dischinger, it is natural to ask which other classes of Dedekind rings are (one-sided) Dischinger.
