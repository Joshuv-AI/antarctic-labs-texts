40 C.F.R. § 370.32 — To whom must I submit the information?
Citation: us:cfr:40:370.32
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-370.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 370 (Hazardous Chemical Reporting: Community Right-To-Know). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You must submit an SDS or a list to the LEPC, SERC, and fire department with jurisdiction over your facility, as provided in § 370.30(a).
(b) You must submit an SDS to the LEPC if requested, as provided in § 370.30(b).
