49 C.F.R. § 1115.7 — Petitions for judicial review; mailing address.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1115.7
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1115.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1115 (Appellate Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Petitions for judicial review of final agency orders may be served on the Board pursuant to 28 U.S.C. 2112(a) and be addressed to “Chief Counsel, Office of Chief Counsel, Surface Transportation Board, Washington, DC 20423.”
