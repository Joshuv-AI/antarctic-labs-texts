24 C.F.R. § 350.3 — Maintenance of Ginnie Mae Securities.
Citation: us:cfr:24:350.3
Creator: Government National Mortgage Association, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-350.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 350 (Book-Entry Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Ginnie Mae Security may be maintained in the form of a Definitive Ginnie Mae Security or a Book-entry Ginnie Mae Security. A Book-entry Ginnie Mae Security shall be maintained in the Book-entry System.
