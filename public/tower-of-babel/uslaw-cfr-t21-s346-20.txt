21 C.F.R. § 346.20 — Keratolytic active ingredients.
Citation: us:cfr:21:346.20
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-346.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 346 (Anorectal Drug Products For Over-The-Counter Human Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The active ingredient of the product consists of any of the following when used within the concentration range established for each ingredient:
(a) Alcloxa 0.2 to 2 percent.
(b) Resorcinol 1 to 3 percent.
