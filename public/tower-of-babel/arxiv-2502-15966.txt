Abstract
We produce formulas for ∑ j = 1 2 n − 2 1 sin s ⁡ ( ( 2 ​ j − 1 ) ​ π 2 n ) \sum_{j=1}^{2^{n-2}}\frac{1}{\sin^{s}\left(\frac{(2j-1)\pi}{2^{n}}\right)} in terms of Generalized Bernoulli and Euler polynomials and use one of the formulas to produce a nice integral representation of the Riemann zeta function.

Generalized Bernoulli Polynomials

The generalized Bernoulli polynomials are defined via the generating function

\left(\frac{t}{e^{t}-1}\right)^{\alpha}e^{xt}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}B_{n}^{(\alpha)}(x)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

The Euler polynomials are defined

\left(\frac{2}{e^{t}+1}\right)e^{xt}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}E_{n}(x)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

We list some known equivalences for future reference.
