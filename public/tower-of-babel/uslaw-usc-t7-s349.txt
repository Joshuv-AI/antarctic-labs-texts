7 U.S.C. § 349 — “State” defined
Citation: us:usc:7:349
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section349
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 13 (Agricultural And Mechanical Colleges); Subchapter IV (Agricultural Extension Work Appropriation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The term “State” means the States of the Union, Puerto Rico, the Virgin Islands, Guam and the Northern Mariana Islands.
