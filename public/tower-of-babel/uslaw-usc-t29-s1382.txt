29 U.S.C. § 1382 — Determination and collection of liability; notification of employer
Citation: us:usc:29:1382
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section1382
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 18 (Employee Retirement Income Security Program); Subchapter III (Plan Termination Insurance); Part 1 (Employer Withdrawals). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When an employer withdraws from a multiemployer plan, the plan sponsor, in accordance with this part, shall—
(1)
determine the amount of the employer’s withdrawal liability,
(2)
notify the employer of the amount of the withdrawal liability, and
(3)
collect the amount of the withdrawal liability from the employer.
