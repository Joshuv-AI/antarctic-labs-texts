24 C.F.R. § 201.43 — Administrative reports and examinations.
Citation: us:cfr:24:201.43
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-201.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 201 (Title I Property Improvement And Manufactured Home Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary may call upon a lender for any reports deemed necessary in connection with the regulations in this part and may inspect the loan files, records, books and accounts of the lender as they pertain to the loans reported for insurance.
