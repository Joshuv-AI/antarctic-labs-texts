21 U.S.C. § 616 — Inspectors of carcasses, etc., meat of which is intended for export; certificates of condition
Citation: us:usc:21:616
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section616
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 12 (Meat Inspection); Subchapter I (Inspection Requirements; Adulteration And Misbranding). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of section 615 of this title the Secretary may appoint inspectors who shall be authorized to give an official certificate stating the condition in which said cattle, sheep, swine, goats, horses, mules, or other equines, and the meat thereof, are found.
