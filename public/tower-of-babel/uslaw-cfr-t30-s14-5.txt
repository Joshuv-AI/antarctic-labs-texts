30 C.F.R. § 14.5 — Test samples.
Citation: us:cfr:30:14.5
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-14.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 14 (Requirements For The Approval Of Flame-Resistant Conveyor Belts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon request by MSHA, the applicant must submit 3 precut, unrolled, flat conveyor belt samples for flame testing. Each sample must be 60 ± 1/4 inches long (152.4 ±0.6 cm) by 9 ± 1/8 inches (22.9 ±0.3 cm) wide.
