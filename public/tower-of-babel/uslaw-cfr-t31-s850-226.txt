31 C.F.R. § 850.226 — Relevant agencies.
Citation: us:cfr:31:850.226
Creator: Office Of Investment Security, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-850.226
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 850 (Provisions Pertaining To U.S. Investments In Certain National Security Technologies And Products In Countries Of Concern). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term relevant agencies means the Departments of State, Defense, Justice, Commerce, Energy, and Homeland Security, the Office of the United States Trade Representative, the Office of Science and Technology Policy, the Office of the Director of National Intelligence, the Office of the National Cyber Director, and any other department, agency, or office the Secretary determines appropriate.
