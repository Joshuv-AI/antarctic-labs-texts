46 U.S.C. § 58104 — Willful violation constitutes breach of contract or charter
Citation: us:usc:46:58104
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section58104
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part G (Restrictions And Penalties); Chapter 581 (Restrictions And Penalties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A willful violation of any provision of sections 58101–58103 of this title constitutes a breach of the contract or charter. On determining that a violation has occurred, the Secretary of Transportation may declare the contract or charter rescinded.
