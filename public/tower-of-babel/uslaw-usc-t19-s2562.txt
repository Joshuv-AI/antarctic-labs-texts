19 U.S.C. § 2562 — Consideration of standards-related activities by an international forum
Citation: us:usc:19:2562
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section2562
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 13 (Trade Agreements Act Of 1979); Subchapter II (Technical Barriers To Trade (Standards)); Part C (Administrative And Judicial Proceedings Regarding Standards-Related Activities); Subpart 2 (Other Proceedings Regarding Certain Standards-Related Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No standards-related activity being engaged in within the United States may be stayed in any judicial or administrative proceeding on the basis that such activity is currently being considered, pursuant to the Agreement, by an international forum.
