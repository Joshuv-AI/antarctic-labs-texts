14 C.F.R. § 404.13 — Rulemaking.
Citation: us:cfr:14:404.13
Creator: Commercial Space Transportation, Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-404.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 404 (Petition And Rulemaking Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The FAA's rulemaking procedures are located in subpart A of part 11 of this title, under the General, Written Comments, and Public Meetings and Other Proceedings headings.
(b) In addition to the rulemaking procedures referenced in paragraph (a) of this section, the provisions of §§ 404.17 and 404.19 also apply.
