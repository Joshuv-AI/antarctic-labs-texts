36 U.S.C. § 21703 — Membership
Citation: us:usc:36:21703
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section21703
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 217 (The American Legion). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An individual is eligible for membership in the corporation only if the individual—
(1)
has served in the Armed Forces of—
(A)
the United States at any time during—
(i)
the period from April 6, 1917, through November 11, 1918; or
(ii)
any time after December 7, 1941; or
(B)
a government associated with the United States during a period or time referred to in subclause (A) of this clause and was a citizen of the United States when the individual entered that service; and
(2)
was honorably discharged or separated from that service or continues to serve honorably during or after that period or time.
