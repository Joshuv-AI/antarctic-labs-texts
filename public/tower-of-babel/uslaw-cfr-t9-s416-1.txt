9 C.F.R. § 416.1 — General rules.
Citation: us:cfr:9:416.1
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-416.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 416 (Sanitation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each official establishment must be operated and maintained in a manner sufficient to prevent the creation of insanitary conditions and to ensure that product is not adulterated.
