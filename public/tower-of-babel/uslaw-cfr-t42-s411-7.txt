42 C.F.R. § 411.7 — Services that must be furnished at public expense under a Federal law or Federal Government contract.
Citation: us:cfr:42:411.7
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-411.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 411 (Exclusions From Medicare And Limitations On Medicare Payment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Basic rule. Except as provided in paragraph (b) of this section, payment may not be made for services that any provider or supplier is obligated to furnish at public expense, in accordance with a law of, or a contract with, the United States.
(b) Exception. Payment may be made for services that a hospital or SNF of the Indian Health Service is obligated to furnish at public expense.
