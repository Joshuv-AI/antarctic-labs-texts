2 U.S.C. § 2131a — Eligibility for placement of statues in National Statuary Hall
Citation: us:usc:2:2131a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section2131a
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 30 (Operation And Maintenance Of Capitol Complex); Subchapter V (Historical Preservation And Fine Arts); Part D (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Eligibility
No statue of any individual may be placed in National Statuary Hall until after the expiration of the 10-year period which begins on the date of the individual’s death.
(b) Exceptions
Subsection (a) does not apply with respect to—
(1)
the statue obtained and placed in National Statuary Hall under this Act; or
(2)
any statue provided and furnished by a State under section 2131 of this title or any replacement statue provided by a State under section 2132 of this title.
