10 U.S.C. § 2138 — Savings provision
Citation: us:usc:10:2138
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2138
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training And Education); Chapter 106 (Educational Assistance For Members Of The Selected Reserve). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A member who entered into an agreement under this chapter before July 1, 1985, shall continue to be eligible for educational assistance in accordance with the terms of such agreement and of this chapter as in effect before such date.
