7 C.F.R. § 1205.314 — Cotton-producing State.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1205.314
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Miscellaneous Commodities), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1205.314
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1205 (Cotton Research And Promotion). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Cotton-producing State means each of the following States and combination of States: Alabama; Arizona; Arkansas; California-Nevada; Florida; Georgia; Kansas; Louisiana; Mississippi; Missouri-Illinois; New Mexico; North Carolina; Oklahoma; South Carolina; Tennessee-Kentucky; Texas; Virginia.
