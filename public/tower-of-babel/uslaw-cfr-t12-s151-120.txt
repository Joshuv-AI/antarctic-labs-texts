12 C.F.R. § 151.120 — May I charge a fee for a notice?
Citation: us:cfr:12:151.120
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-151.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 151 (Recordkeeping And Confirmation Requirements For Securities Transactions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may not charge a fee for providing a notice required under this subpart B, except that you may charge a reasonable fee for the notices provided under §§ 151.100(a), (d), and (e).
