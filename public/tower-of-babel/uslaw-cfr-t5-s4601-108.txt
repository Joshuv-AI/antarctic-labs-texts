5 C.F.R. § 4601.108 — Reporting waste, fraud, abuse, and corruption.
Citation: us:cfr:5:4601.108
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-4601.108
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 4601 (Supplemental Standards Of Ethical Conduct For Employees Of The Department Of Homeland Security). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees shall disclose waste, fraud, abuse, and corruption to appropriate authorities, such as the DHS Office of Inspector General.
