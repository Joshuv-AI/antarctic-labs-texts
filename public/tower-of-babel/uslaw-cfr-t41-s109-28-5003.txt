41 C.F.R. § 109-28.5003 — -28.5003 Records.

Creator: Chapter 109—Department Of Energy Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-109-28.5003
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 109-28 (28—Storage And Distribution). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Records of all EHFFP shall be maintained by the holding organization, including a listing of items with original date of classification as EHFFP; initial justifications for retaining EHFFP; rejustifications for retention; and documentation of reviews made by higher levels of management.
