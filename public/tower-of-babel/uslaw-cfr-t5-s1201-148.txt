5 C.F.R. § 1201.148 — Enforcement of protective orders.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1201.148
Creator: Merit Systems Protection Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1201.148
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1201 (Practices And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A protective order issued by a judge or the Board under this subpart may be enforced in the same manner as provided under subpart F of this part for Board final decisions and orders.
