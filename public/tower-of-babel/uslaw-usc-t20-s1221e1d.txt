20 U.S.C. § 1221e–1d — Use of Council staff and facilities
Citation: us:usc:20:1221e–1d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1221e–1d
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 31 (General Provisions Concerning Education); Subchapter I (Functions Of Department Of Education). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The National Advisory Council on Educational Research and Improvement, the Advisory Council on Education Statistics, and members of such councils may not use any staff, facilities, equipment, supplies, or franking privileges of the councils for activities unrelated to the purposes of the councils.
