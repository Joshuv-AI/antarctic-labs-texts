7 U.S.C. § 6501 — Purposes
Citation: us:usc:7:6501
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section6501
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 94 (Organic Certification). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the purpose of this chapter—
(1)
to establish national standards governing the marketing of certain agricultural products as organically produced products;
(2)
to assure consumers that organically produced products meet a consistent standard; and
(3)
to facilitate interstate commerce in fresh and processed food that is organically produced.
