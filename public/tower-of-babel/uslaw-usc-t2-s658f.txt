2 U.S.C. § 658f — Requests to Congressional Budget Office from Senators
Citation: us:usc:2:658f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section658f
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 17A (Congressional Budget And Fiscal Operations); Subchapter II (Fiscal Procedures); Part B (Federal Mandates). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

At the written request of a Senator, the Director shall, to the extent practicable, prepare an estimate of the direct costs of a Federal intergovernmental mandate contained in an amendment of such Senator.
