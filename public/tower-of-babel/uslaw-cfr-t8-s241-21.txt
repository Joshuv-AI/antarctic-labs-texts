8 C.F.R. § 241.21 — Stay of deportation of excluded alien.
Citation: us:cfr:8:241.21
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-241.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 241 (Apprehension And Detention Of Aliens Ordered Removed). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The district director in charge of the port of arrival may stay the immediate deportation of an excluded alien pursuant to sections 237 (a) and (d) of the Act under such conditions as he or she may prescribe.
