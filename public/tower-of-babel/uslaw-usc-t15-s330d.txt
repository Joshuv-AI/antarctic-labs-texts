15 U.S.C. § 330d — Violation; penalty
Citation: us:usc:15:330d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section330d
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 9A (Weather Modification Activities Or Attempts; Reporting Requirement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person who knowingly and willfully violates section 330a of this title, or any rule issued thereunder, shall upon conviction thereof be fined not more than $10,000.
