54 U.S.C. § 103303 — Performance of duties of commissions
Citation: us:usc:54:103303
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section103303
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 1033 (National Military Parks). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The duties of commissions in charge of national military parks shall be performed under the direction of the Secretary.
