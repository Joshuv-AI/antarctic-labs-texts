50 U.S.C. § 3979 — Review of findings of fact and conclusions of law
Citation: us:usc:50:3979
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section3979
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 50 (Servicemembers Civil Relief); Subchapter IV (Life Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The findings of fact and conclusions of law made by the Secretary of Veterans Affairs in administering this subchapter are subject to review on appeal to the Board of Veterans’ Appeals pursuant to chapter 71 of title 38 and to judicial review only as provided in chapter 72 of such title.
