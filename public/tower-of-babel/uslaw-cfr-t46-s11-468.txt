46 C.F.R. § 11.468 — National officer endorsements for mobile offshore drilling units (MODUs).
Citation: us:cfr:46:11.468
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-11.468
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 11 (Requirements For Officer Endorsements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Officer endorsements for service on MODUs authorize service on units of unlimited tonnage upon ocean waters while on location or while underway, as restricted on the endorsement, except when moving independently under their own power.
