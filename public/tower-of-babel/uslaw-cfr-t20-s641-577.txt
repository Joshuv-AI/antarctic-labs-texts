20 C.F.R. § 641.577 — Is there a limit on community service assignment hours?
Citation: us:cfr:20:641.577
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-641.577
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 641 (Provisions Governing The Senior Community Service Employment Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

While there is no specific limit on the number of hours that may be worked in a community service assignment, a community service assignment must be a part-time position. However, the Department strongly encourages grantees to use 1,300 hours as a benchmark and good practice for monitoring community service hours.
