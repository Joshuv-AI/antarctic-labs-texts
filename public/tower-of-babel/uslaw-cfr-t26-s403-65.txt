26 C.F.R. § 403.65 — Authority for destruction.
Citation: us:cfr:26:403.65
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-403.65
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 403 (Disposition Of Seized Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commissioner or his delegate is authorized to order the destruction of any coin-operated gaming device as defined in I.R.C. section 4462 upon which a tax is imposed by I.R.C. section 4461, after the expiration of three months from the date of consummation of administrative forfeiture under any provision of I.R.C.
