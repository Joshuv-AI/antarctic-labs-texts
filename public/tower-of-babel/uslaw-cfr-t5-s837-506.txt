5 C.F.R. § 837.506 — Computation of redetermined annuity for former employees of nonappropriated fund instrumentalities.
Citation: us:cfr:5:837.506
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-837.506
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 837 (Reemployment Of Annuitants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The redetermined annuity of a former employee of a nonappropriated fund instrumentality who elected CSRS or FERS coverage under 5 CFR part 847, subpart D, is recomputed under 5 CFR part 847, subpart F.
(b) The redetermined annuity of a former employee of a nonappropriated fund instrumentality who elected CSRS or FERS retirement credit under 5 CFR part 847, subpart H, is recomputed under 5 CFR part 847, subpart I.
