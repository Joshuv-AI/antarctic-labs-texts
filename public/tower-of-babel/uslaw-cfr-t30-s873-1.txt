30 C.F.R. § 873.1 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:30:873.1
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-873.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 873 (Future Reclamation Set-Aside Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part provides requirements for the award of grants to States or Indian tribes for the establishment of special trust accounts that will provide funds for coal reclamation purposes after September 30, 1995.
