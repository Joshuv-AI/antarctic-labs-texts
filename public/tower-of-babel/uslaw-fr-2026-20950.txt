Privacy Act of 1974; Matching Program

AGENCY: Office of the Secretary, Department of Defense, (DoD).

ACTION: Notice of a new matching program.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the Privacy Act of 1974, as amended, and 
Office of Management and Budget (OMB) guidance on computer matching, 
the DoD is providing notice of the establishment of a new matching 
program. Pursuant to the Payment Integrity Information Act of 2019 
(PIIA), DoD, including its component Defense Finance and Accounting 
Service (DFAS), is establishing a new matching program consisting of 
the computerized comparison of its system of records for the Military 
Retirement--Annuitant Pay program with the Do Not Pay (DNP) Working 
System, which is administered by Treasury's Bureau of the Fiscal 
Service. This matching program will enable the DoD program to compare 
records maintained in its system of records with records maintained in 
the DNP Working System for the purposes of identifying and preventing 
improper payments.

DATES: Comments will be accepted on or before November 13, 2026. This 
proposed action will be effective the day following the end of the 
comment period unless comments have been received from interested 
members of the public that require modification and republication of 
the notice. This new matching program will be effective through 
September 10, 2029.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The Computer Matching and Privacy Protection 
Act of 1988 amended the Privacy Act of 1974 (5 United States Code 
(U.S.C.) 552a) by establishing procedural safeguards for agencies' use 
of records in computerized matching. The Payment Integrity Information 
Act of 2019 (31 U.S.C. 3351 et seq.) provides the authority to waive 
the requirements for matching agreements in certain cases. Pursuant to 
this authority, the Secretary of the Treasury, after consulting with 
the OMB Director, authorized the issuance of a four-year waiver of the 
requirement for entering into a matching agreement for programs that 
meet the criteria in OMB Memorandum M-25-32. DoD has determined that 
the DNP matching program for the Military Retirement--Annuitant Pay 
(PIIA Program ID: DOD-0044) is eligible for this waiver.
    Participating Agencies: The DoD, through DFAS, will match records 
against the DNP Working System, maintained by the Bureau of the Fiscal 
Service at the U.S. Department of the Treasury.
    Authority for Conducting the Matching Program: The Payment 
Integrity Information Act of 2019 (31 U.S.C. 3351 et seq.) and OMB 
Memorandum M-25-32. The Payment Integrity Information Act of 2019 (31 
U.S.C. 3351 et seq.) establishes the DNP Initiative and requires, for 
the purposes of identifying and preventing improper payments, each 
executive agency to have access to, and use of, the relevant databases 
in DNP to verify payment or award eligibility. Additional applicable 
authorities for this matching program include Executive Order 13520, 
Reducing Improper Payments (74 FR 62201); Executive Order 14249, 
Protecting America's Bank Account Against Fraud, Waste, and Abuse (90 
FR 14011); and OMB Memorandum M-25-32, Preventing Improper Payments and 
Protecting Privacy Through Do Not Pay. Additional information regarding 
the authorities for the collection and maintenance of information for 
each of

[[Page 65188]]

the DoD programs that will conduct matches with the DNP Working System 
are contained within the systems of records notices listed below.
    Purpose(s): The purpose of the matching program is to identify and 
prevent improper payments by verifying through DNP prepayment or pre-
award eligibility.
    Categories of Individuals: Applicants for, or recipients of, 
Federal funds from the Military Retirement--Annuitant Pay program.
    Categories of Records: Data elements to be matched include 
applicant's name, Social Security Number (SSN) or Taxpayer 
Identification Number (TIN), address, date of birth, and payment/
banking information. These data elements include all the records 
involved in the matching program.
    System(s) of Records: The records contained within the DNP Working 
System are maintained in the Treasury system of records, Bureau of the 
Fiscal Service .017--Do Not Pay Payment Verification Records (85 FR 
11776). The DoD records involved in this matching program are 
maintained in the following system of records: T7347b, Defense Military 
Retiree and Annuity Pay System Records (74 FR 696), (90 FR 54312).
