7 C.F.R. § 1740.79 — Servicing of grants, loans and loan/grant combinations.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1740.79
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1740.79
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1740 (Rural Econnectivity Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Awardees must make payments on the loan as required in the note and Award Documents.
(b) Awardees must comply with all terms, conditions, affirmative covenants, and negative covenants contained in the Award Documents.
(c) The sale or lease of any portion of the Awardee's facilities must be approved in writing by RUS prior to initiating the sale or lease.
