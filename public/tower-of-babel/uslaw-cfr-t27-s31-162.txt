27 C.F.R. § 31.162 — Discontinuance of business.
Citation: us:cfr:27:31.162
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-31.162
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 31 (Alcohol Beverage Dealers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a wholesale dealer in liquors who is required, under § 31.160, to file a monthly summary report discontinues business, a monthly summary report marked “Final” must be filed covering transactions through the date of discontinuance.
