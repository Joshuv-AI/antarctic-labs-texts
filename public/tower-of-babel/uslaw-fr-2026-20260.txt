Foreign-Trade Zones Board

Foreign-Trade Zone 27; Withdrawal of Application for Subzone; 
Applied Materials, Inc.; Boylston, Gloucester, Newburyport, and Rowley, 
Massachusetts

    Notice is hereby given of the withdrawal of the application 
submitted by the Massachusetts Port Authority, grantee of Foreign-Trade 
Zone 27, requesting subzone status for the facilities of Applied 
Materials, Inc. in Boylston, Gloucester, Newburyport, and Rowley, 
Massachusetts.
    The application was docketed on April 15, 2026 (91 FR 20973, April 
20, 2026). The withdrawal was requested by the applicant due to 
outstanding issues that need to be addressed. The case has been closed 
without prejudice.

    Dated: September 28, 2026.
Colin Agostisi,
Executive Secretary.
