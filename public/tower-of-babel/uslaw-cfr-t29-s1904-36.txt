29 C.F.R. § 1904.36 — Prohibition against discrimination.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1904.36
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1904.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1904 (Recording And Reporting Occupational Injuries And Illnesses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to § 1904.35, section 11(c) of the OSH Act also prohibits you from discriminating against an employee for reporting a work-related fatality, injury, or illness. That provision of the Act also protects the employee who files a safety and health complaint, asks for access to the part 1904 records, or otherwise exercises any rights afforded by the OSH Act.
