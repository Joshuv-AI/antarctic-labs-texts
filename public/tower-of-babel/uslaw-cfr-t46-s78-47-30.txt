46 C.F.R. § 78.47-30 — -30 Hand portable fire extinguishers.
Citation: us:cfr:46:78.47-30
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 78 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each hand portable fire extinguisher shall be marked with a number and the location where stowed shall be marked with a corresponding number at least 1/2 inch high. Where only one type and size of hand portable fire extinguisher is carried, the numbering may be omitted.
