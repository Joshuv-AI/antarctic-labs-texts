33 C.F.R. § 238.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:33:238.1
Creator: Corps Of Engineers, Department Of The Army, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-238.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 238 (Water Resources Policies And Authorities: Flood Damage Reduction Measures In Urban Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This regulation provides policies and guidance for Corps of Engineers participation in urban flood damage reduction projects and establishes criteria to distinguish between improvements to be accomplished by the Corps under its flood control authorities and storm sewer systems to be accomplished by local interests.
