14 C.F.R. § 398.9 — Load factor standards.
Citation: us:cfr:14:398.9
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-398.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 398 (Guidelines For Individual Determinations Of Basic Essential Air Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The load factor standards used in this part may be raised for individual eligible places under either of the following circumstances:
(a) The place is served by the carrier as part of a linear route; or
(b) It would be in the interest of the community, the carrier, or the general public to raise the load factor standard for that place.
