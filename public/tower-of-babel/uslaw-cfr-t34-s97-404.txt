34 C.F.R. § 97.404 — Research not involving greater than minimal risk.
Citation: us:cfr:34:97.404
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-97.404
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 97 (Protection Of Human Subjects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

ED conducts or funds research in which the IRB finds that no greater than minimal risk to children is presented, only if the IRB finds that adequate provisions are made for soliciting the assent of the children and the permission of their parents or guardians, as set forth in § 97.408.
