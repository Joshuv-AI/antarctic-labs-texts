1.
Statement of main result

It is an open problem in inverse Galois theory whether every finite group occurs as the Galois group of some unramified Galois extension of a quadratic number field.
Here, the expression “unramified extension” is to be understood as including the archimedean primes. The Cohen-Lenstra heuristics and their non-abelian generalizations (
[
15
]
) provide expectations for the asymptotic count of such quadratic fields (when the finite group has been fixed). Even the question whether there are infinitely many such quadratic fields for a given group
G
has been answered only for very particular groups
G
, among them the alternating groups
A_{n}
, see, e.g.,
[
13
]
. See also the predecessor papers
[
4
]
and
[
6
]
for more background on this problem. In this note, we investigate the embeddability of unramified
A_{n}
extensions in unramified
2.A_{n}
extensions (where
2.A_{n}
denotes the unique degree-
2
stem cover of
A_{n}
). Concretely, we show the following.

Theorem 1.1
.

Let
n\geq 4
be such that either
n\equiv 3\bmod 8
is a prime, or
n\equiv 2\bmod 8
and
n-1
is a prime or a square.
Then there exist infinitely many quadratic number fields which possess an unramified Galois extension with Galois group
2.A_{n}
, the double covering group of

Note that the assumptions hold for infinitely many values of

(in particular, for all

, as well as for a positive density subset of all prime numbers

by Dirichlet’s prime number theorem).
In
[
4
]
, the author gave a

[
5

) proof of the analogous assertion for

The underlying Galois-theoretic ideas were however not without merit and could have been put to much better use, which we will demonstrate here in the spirit of “better late than never”.
See also

In the last section, we present an alternative approach, which, in spite of being slightly computation-heavy and at this point only yielding the analogous result for one further open case (
