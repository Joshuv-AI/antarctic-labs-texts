Durrmeyer type operators linked with Boas-Buck type polynomials
Naokant Deo and Sandeep Kumar

Abstract
The present article intends to introduce the sequence of Baskakov-Durrmeyer type operators linked with the generating functions of Boas-Buck type polynomials. After calculating the moments, including the limiting case of central moments of the constructed sequence of operators, in the subsequent sections, we estimate the convergence rate using the Ditzian-Totik modulus of smoothness and some convergence results in Lipchitz-type space.



Keywords
: Boas-Buck polynomials, Modulus of smoothness, Lipschitz-type space, Rate of Convergence.

MSC
: 41A10, 41A25 and 41A30.

For
\sigma>0,
consider a class of functions
C_{\sigma}\left[0,\infty\right)=\{f\in C\left[0,\infty\right):|f(s)|\leq M(1+s^{\sigma})\}

Now, for

\displaystyle\tilde{\mathcal{B}}_{n}\left(f;x\right)=\frac{1}{\mathcal{S}(1).\xi\left(n^{2}x^{2}\mathcal{T}(1)+nx\mathcal{U}(1)+\mathcal{V}(1)\right)}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\frac{\Theta_{j}(nx)}{B(j,n+1)}

This article intends to investigate the convergence properties of the operators (

After making some convergence results based on the modulus of continuity, Lipchitz-type space, and weighted space, we estimates the convergence for the functions of bounded variations.

\displaystyle\left|\tilde{\mathcal{B}}_{n}\left(f;x\right)-f(x)\right|

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\infty}\underline{\mathcal{V}}_{n}(x,s)\left|f(s)-f(x)\right|ds,

\displaystyle\mathcal{K}\left(\int_{0}^{\infty}\underline{\mathcal{V}}_{n}(x,s)\left(\frac{(s-x)^{2}}{s+x}\right)ds\right)^{\frac{r}{2}}

\displaystyle\frac{\mathcal{K}}{x^{\frac{r}{2}}}\left(\tilde{\mathcal{B}}_{n}\left((s-x)^{2};x\right)\right)^{\frac{r}{2}}.
