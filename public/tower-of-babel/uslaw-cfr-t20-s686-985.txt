20 C.F.R. § 686.985 — What Department of Labor equal opportunity and nondiscrimination regulations apply to Job Corps?
Citation: us:cfr:20:686.985
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-686.985
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 686 (The Job Corps Under Title I Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nondiscrimination requirements, procedures, complaint processing, and compliance reviews are governed by, as applicable, provisions of the following Department of Labor regulations:
(a) Regulations implementing sec. 188 of WIOA for programs receiving Federal financial assistance under WIOA found at 29 CFR part 38;
(b) Title 29 CFR part 33 for programs conducted by the Department of Labor; and
(c) Title 41 CFR chapter 60 for entities that have a Federal government contract.
