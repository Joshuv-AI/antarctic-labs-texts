33 U.S.C. § 1971 — Definitions

Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 33A (Marine Debris Research, Prevention, And Reduction); Subchapter III (Genius Prize For Save Our Seas Innovations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this subchapter:
(1) Prize competition
The term “prize competition” means the competition for the award of the Genius Prize for Save Our Seas Innovations established under section 1972 of this title.
(2) Secretary
The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of Commerce.
