21 U.S.C. § 855 — Alternative fine
Citation: us:usc:21:855
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section855
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 13 (Drug Abuse Prevention And Control); Subchapter I (Control And Enforcement); Part D (Offenses And Penalties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In lieu of a fine otherwise authorized by this part, a defendant who derives profits or other proceeds from an offense may be fined not more than twice the gross profits or other proceeds.
