10 U.S.C. § 8212 — Additional regulations for Marine Corps
Citation: us:usc:10:8212
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8212
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 825 (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may prescribe military regulations for the discipline of the Marine Corps.
