6 C.F.R. § 115.66 — Protection of detainees from contact with alleged abusers.
Citation: us:cfr:6:115.66
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security, Office Of The Secretary
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-6/section-115.66
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 6 (Domestic Security), Part 115 (Sexual Abuse And Assault Prevention Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Staff, contractors, and volunteers suspected of perpetrating sexual abuse shall be removed from all duties requiring detainee contact pending the outcome of an investigation.
