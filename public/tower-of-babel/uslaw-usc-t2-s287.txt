2 U.S.C. § 287 — Establishment
Citation: us:usc:2:287
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section287
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 9C (Office Of Parliamentarian Of House Of Representatives). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is hereby established in the House of Representatives an office to be known as the Office of the Parliamentarian, hereinafter in this chapter referred to as the “Office”.
