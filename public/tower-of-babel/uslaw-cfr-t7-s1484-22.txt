7 C.F.R. § 1484.22 — Allocation factors.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1484.22
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1484.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1484 (Programs To Help Develop Foreign Markets For Agricultural Commodities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

CCC determines final funding levels after allocating available funds to approved applications on the basis of criteria that will be fully described in each program year's Cooperator program announcement. Generally, extensions will not be allowable.
