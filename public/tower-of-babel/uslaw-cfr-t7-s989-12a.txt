7 C.F.R. § 989.12a — a Cooperative bargaining association.
Citation: us:cfr:7:989.12a
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-989.12a
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 989 (Raisins Produced From Grapes Grown In California). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Cooperative bargaining association means a nonprofit cooperative association of raisin producers engaged within the area in bargaining with handlers as to price and otherwise arranging for the sale of natural condition raisin of its members.
