44 U.S.C. § 2202 — Ownership of Presidential records
Citation: us:usc:44:2202
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section2202
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 22 (Presidential Records). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States shall reserve and retain complete ownership, possession, and control of Presidential records; and such records shall be administered in accordance with the provisions of this chapter.
