18 C.F.R. § 301.1 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:18:301.1

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 301 (Average System Cost Methodology For Sales From Utilities To Bonneville Power Administration Under Northwest Power Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part apply to the sales of electric power by any Utility to the Bonneville Power Administration (Bonneville) under section 5(c) of the Pacific Northwest Electric Power Planning and Conservation Act (Northwest Power Act). 16 U.S.C. 839c(c).
