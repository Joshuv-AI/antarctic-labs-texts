24 C.F.R. § 906.11 — Eligible purchasers.
Citation: us:cfr:24:906.11
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-906.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 906 (Public Housing Homeownership Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Entities that purchase units from the PHA for resale to low-income families (purchase and resale entities or PREs) and low-income families are eligible to purchase properties made available for sale under a PHA homeownership program.
