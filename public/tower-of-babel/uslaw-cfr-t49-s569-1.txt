49 C.F.R. § 569.1 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:49:569.1
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-569.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 569 (Regrooved Tires). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth the conditions under which regrooved and regroovable tires manufactured or regrooved after the effective date of the regulation may be sold, offered for sale, introduced for sale or delivered for introduction into interstate commerce.
