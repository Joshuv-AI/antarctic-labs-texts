26 U.S.C. § 5111 — Eligibility
Citation: us:usc:26:5111
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section5111
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 51 (Distilled Spirits, Wines, And Beer); Subchapter A (Gallonage And Occupational Taxes); Part II (Miscellaneous Provisions); Subpart B (Nonbeverage Domestic Drawback Claimants). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person using distilled spirits on which the tax has been determined, in the manufacture or production of medicines, medicinal preparations, food products, flavors, flavoring extracts, or perfume, which are unfit for beverage purposes shall be eligible for drawback at the time when such distilled spirits are used in the manufacture of such products as provided for in this subpart.
