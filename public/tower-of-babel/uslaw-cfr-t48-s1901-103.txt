48 C.F.R. § 1901.103 — 1901.103 Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1901.103
Creator: Chapter 19—Broadcasting Board Of Governors
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1901.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1901 (The Broadcasting Board Of Governors Acquisition Regulation System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except where a deviation is specifically authorized in accordance with subpart 1901.4 or otherwise authorized by law, the FAR and the IAAR govern all the Broadcasting Board of Governors acquisitions within the United States.
