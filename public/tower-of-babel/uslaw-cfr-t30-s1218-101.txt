30 C.F.R. § 1218.101 — Royalty and rental remittance (naval petroleum reserves).
Citation: us:cfr:30:1218.101
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1218.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1218 (Collection Of Royalties, Rentals, Bonuses, And Other Monies Due The Federal Government). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Remittance covering payments of royalty or rental on naval petroleum reserves must be accomplished by necessary identification information and sent direct to the Director, Naval Petroleum Reserves in California.
