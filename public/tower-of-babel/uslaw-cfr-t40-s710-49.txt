40 C.F.R. § 710.49 — Failure to report.
Citation: us:cfr:40:710.49
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-710.49
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 710 (Compilation Of The Tsca Chemical Substance Inventory). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If neither the substantiation required under § 710.43(a) or (b)(1), nor the information specified in § 710.43(b)(2)(ii), is submitted to EPA in accordance with the provisions of this subpart, then EPA will deny the confidentiality claim in accordance with the procedures set forth in TSCA section 14(g)(2) and 40 CFR part 2, subpart B.
