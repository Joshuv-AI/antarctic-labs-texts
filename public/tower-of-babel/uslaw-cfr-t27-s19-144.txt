27 C.F.R. § 19.144 — Alternation of distilled spirits plant and volatile fruit-flavor concentrate plant premises.
Citation: us:cfr:27:19.144
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-19.144
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 19 (Distilled Spirits Plants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The proprietor may temporarily extend or curtail the distilled spirits plant premises for alternate use with the premises of a contiguous volatile fruit-flavor concentrate plant. If a proprietor wishes to use all or a portion of the premises alternately as a volatile fruit-flavor concentrate plant or vice versa, the proprietor must comply with the requirements of §§ 18.39 and 18.41 through 18.43 of this chapter.
