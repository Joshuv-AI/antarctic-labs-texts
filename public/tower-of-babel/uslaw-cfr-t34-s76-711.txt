34 C.F.R. § 76.711 — Requesting funds by ALN number.
Citation: us:cfr:34:76.711
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-76.711
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 76 (State-Administered Formula Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a program is listed in the Assistance Listings and assigned an Assistance Listing Number (ALN), a State, when requesting funds under the program, shall identify that program by the ALN.
