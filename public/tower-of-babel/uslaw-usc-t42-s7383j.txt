42 U.S.C. § 7383j — Definition of Restricted Data

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 84 (Department Of Energy); Subchapter XV (Matters Relating To Safeguards, Security, And Counterintelligence). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this subchapter, the term “Restricted Data” has the meaning given that term in section 2014(y) of this title.
