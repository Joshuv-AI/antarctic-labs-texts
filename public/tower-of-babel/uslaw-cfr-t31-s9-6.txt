31 C.F.R. § 9.6 — Confidential information.
Citation: us:cfr:31:9.6
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-9.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 9 (Effects Of Imported Articles On The National Security). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Information submitted in confidence which the Assistant Secretary determines would disclose trade secrets and commercial or financial information obtained from a person and privileged, within the meaning of 5 U.S.C. 552 and 31 CFR part 1, will be accorded confidential treatment. All information submitted in confidence must be on separate pages marked “Business Confidential.”
