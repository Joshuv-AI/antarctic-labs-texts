47 U.S.C. § 361 — Control by Commission; review of decisions
Citation: us:usc:47:361
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title47-section361
Context: United States Code, Title 47 (Telecommunications); Chapter 5 (Wire Or Radio Communication); Subchapter III (Special Provisions Relating To Radio); Part II (Radio Equipment And Radio Operators On Board Ship). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subchapter shall be interpreted as lessening in any degree the control of the Commission over all matters connected with the radio equipment and its operation on shipboard and its decision and determination in regard to the radio requirements, installations, or exemptions from prescribed radio requirements shall be final, subject only to review in accordance with law.
