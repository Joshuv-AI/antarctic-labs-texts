21 U.S.C. § 692 — Inspection extended to reindeer
Citation: us:usc:21:692
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section692
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 12 (Meat Inspection); Subchapter V (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of the meat-inspection law may be extended to the inspection of reindeer.
