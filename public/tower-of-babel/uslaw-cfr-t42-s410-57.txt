42 C.F.R. § 410.57 — Preventive vaccines.
Citation: us:cfr:42:410.57
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-410.57
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 410 (Supplementary Medical Insurance (Smi) Benefits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Medicare Part B pays for the pneumococcal vaccine and its administration.
(b) Medicare Part B pays for the influenza virus vaccine and its administration.
(c) Medicare Part B pays for the COVID-19 vaccine (or monoclonal antibodies used for pre-exposure prophylaxis of COVID-19) and its administration.
(d) Medicare Part B pays for the Hepatitis B vaccine and its administration, as defined in § 410.63(a).
