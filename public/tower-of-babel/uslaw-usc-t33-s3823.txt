33 U.S.C. § 3823 — Other compliance documentation
Citation: us:usc:33:3823
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section3823
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 51 (Clean Hulls); Subchapter II (Implementation Of The Convention). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In addition to the requirements under sections 3821 and 3822 of this title, the Secretary may require vessels to hold other documentation considered necessary to verify compliance with this chapter.
