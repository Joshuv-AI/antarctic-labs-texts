43 C.F.R. § 30.267 — What if I disagree with the probate decision regarding tribal purchase option?
Citation: us:cfr:43:30.267
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-30.267
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 30 (Indian Probate Hearings Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you are an interested party who is adversely affected by the probate decision, you may, within 30 days after the date on which the probate decision was mailed, file with the judge a written petition for rehearing under this part.
