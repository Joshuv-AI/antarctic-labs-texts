7 U.S.C. § 2331 — Copies for public libraries
Citation: us:usc:7:2331
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2331
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 57 (Plant Variety Protection); Subchapter I (Plant Variety Protection Office); Part A (Organization And Publications). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may supply printed copies of descriptions, drawings, and photographs of protected plant varieties to public libraries in the United States which shall maintain such copies for the use of the public.
