5 C.F.R. § 177.108 — Referral to Department of Justice.
Citation: us:cfr:5:177.108
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-177.108
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 177 (Administrative Claims Under The Federal Tort Claims Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When Department of Justice approval or consultation is required, or the advice of the Department of Justice is otherwise to be requested, under § 177.107, the written referral or request will be transmitted to the Department of Justice by the General Counsel of OPM or his or her designee.
