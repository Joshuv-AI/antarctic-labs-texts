16 U.S.C. § 615b — Exportation of timber pulp wood and wood pulp from Alaska
Citation: us:usc:16:615b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section615b
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 4 (Protection Of Timber, And Depredations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Birch timber and pulp wood or wood pulp manufactured from timber in Alaska may be exported therefrom.
