22 U.S.C. § 4507 — Federal control of education prohibited
Citation: us:usc:22:4507
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section4507
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 55 (Research And Training For Eastern Europe And Independent States Of Former Soviet Union). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing contained in this chapter may be construed to authorize any department, agency, officer, or employee of the United States to exercise any direction, supervision, or control over the curriculum, program of instruction or research, administration, or personnel of any educational institution.
