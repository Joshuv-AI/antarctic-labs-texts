2 U.S.C. § 273 — Compensation
Citation: us:usc:2:273
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section273
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 9 (Office Of Legislative Counsel); Subchapter I (Senate). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Legislative Counsel of the Senate shall be paid at an annual rate of compensation of $40,000.
