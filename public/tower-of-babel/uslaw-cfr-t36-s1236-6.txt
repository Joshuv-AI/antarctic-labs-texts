36 C.F.R. § 1236.6 — What are agency responsibilities for electronic records management?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1236.6
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1236.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1236 (Electronic Records Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies must:
(a) Incorporate management of electronic records into the records management activities required by parts 1220-1235 of this subchapter;
(b) Integrate records management and preservation considerations into the design, development, enhancement, and implementation of electronic information systems in accordance with subpart B of this part; and
(c) Appropriately manage electronic records in accordance with subpart C of this part.
