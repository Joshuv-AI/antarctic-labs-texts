40 C.F.R. § 152.84 — When materials must be submitted to the Agency.
Citation: us:cfr:40:152.84
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-152.84
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 152 (Pesticide Registration And Classification Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Information and materials required by this subpart must be submitted at the time of application, unless the application is determined not to be subject to the requirements of this subpart.
