24 C.F.R. § 92.66 — Reallocation.
Citation: us:cfr:24:92.66
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-92.66
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 92 (Home Investment Partnerships Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any HOME funds which are reduced or recaptured from an insular area's allocation and which are not used to increase the allocation amount for one or more of the remaining insular areas as provided in § 92.60 of this part, will be reallocated by HUD to the States in accordance with the requirements in subpart J for reallocating funds initially allocated to a State.
