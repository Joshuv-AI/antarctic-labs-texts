48 C.F.R. § 226.7200 — 226.7200 Scope of subpart.

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-226.7200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 226 (Other Socioeconomic Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart implements section 853 of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2004 (Pub. L. 108-136, 10 U.S.C. 3901 note prec.). Nothing in this subpart supersedes the requirement to use the mandatory sources in FAR part 8 or the small business programs in FAR part 19.
