32 C.F.R. § 552.116 — Privately owned weapons—security.
Citation: us:cfr:32:552.116
Creator: Department Of The Army
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-552.116
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 552 (Regulations Affecting Military Reservations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Privately owned arms and ammunition will be secured in the manner required for military weapons and ammunition but separate from military arms, ammunition, and explosives (AA&E) items.
