16 C.F.R. § 1000.13 — Directives System.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1000.13
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1000.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1000 (Commission Organization And Functions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission maintains a Directives System which contains delegations of authority and descriptions of Commission programs, policies, and procedures. A complete set of directives is available for inspection in the public reading room at Commission headquarters.
