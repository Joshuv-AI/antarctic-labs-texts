23 C.F.R. § 450.302 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:23:450.302
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-450.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 450 (Planning Assistance And Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to organizations and entities responsible for the transportation planning and programming processes in metropolitan planning areas.
