9 C.F.R. § 51.10 — Part 53 of this chapter not applicable.
Citation: us:cfr:9:51.10
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-51.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 51 (Animals Destroyed Because Of Brucellosis). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No claim for indemnity for animals destroyed because of brucellosis shall hereafter be paid under the regulations contained in part 53 of this chapter, but all such claims shall be presented and paid pursuant to and in compliance with regulations contained in this part.
