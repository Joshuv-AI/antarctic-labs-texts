14 C.F.R. § 291.10 — Applications.
Citation: us:cfr:14:291.10
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-291.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 291 (Cargo Operations In Interstate Air Transportation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applications for all-cargo air service certificates shall comply with the provisions of part 201 and subpart B of part 302 of this chapter with regard to filing procedures, and with the provisions of part 204 of this chapter with regard to evidentiary requirements.
