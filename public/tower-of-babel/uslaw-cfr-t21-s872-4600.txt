21 C.F.R. § 872.4600 — Intraoral ligature and wire lock.
Citation: us:cfr:21:872.4600
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-872.4600
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 872 (Dental Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. An intraoral ligature and wire lock is a metal device intended to constrict fractured bone segments in the oral cavity. The bone segments are stabilized by wrapping the ligature (wire) around the fractured bone segments and locking the ends together.
(b) Classification. Class II.
