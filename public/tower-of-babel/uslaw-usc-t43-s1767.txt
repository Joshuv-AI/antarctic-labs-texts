43 U.S.C. § 1767 — Rights-of-way for Federal departments and agencies
Citation: us:usc:43:1767
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section1767
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 35 (Federal Land Policy And Management); Subchapter V (Rights-Of-Way). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Secretary concerned may provide under applicable provisions of this subchapter for the use of any department or agency of the United States a right-of-way over, upon, under or through the land administered by him, subject to such terms and conditions as he may impose.
(b)
Where a right-of-way has been reserved for the use of any department or agency of the United States, the Secretary shall take no action to terminate, or otherwise limit, that use without the consent of the head of such department or agency.
