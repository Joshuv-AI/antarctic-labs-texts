21 C.F.R. § 60.42 — Notice of hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:21:60.42
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-60.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 60 (Patent Term Restoration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Ten days before the hearing, FDA will notify the requesting party, the applicant, and the petitioner, orally or in writing, of the date, time, and location of the hearing. The agency will provide the requesting party, the applicant, and the petitioner with an opportunity to participate as a party in the hearing.
