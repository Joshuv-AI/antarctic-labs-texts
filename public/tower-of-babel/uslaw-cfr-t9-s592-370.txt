9 C.F.R. § 592.370 — Certificate issuance.
Citation: us:cfr:9:592.370
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-592.370
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 592 (Voluntary Inspection Of Egg Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When performing inspection service at locations other than an official establishment, inspection program personnel shall issue a certificate covering each product inspected. An applicant may request issuance of a certificate for each production lot inspected.
