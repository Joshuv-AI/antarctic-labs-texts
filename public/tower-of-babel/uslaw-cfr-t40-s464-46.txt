40 C.F.R. § 464.46 — Pretreatment standards for new sources.
Citation: us:cfr:40:464.46
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-464.46
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 464 (Metal Molding And Casting Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in 40 CFR 403.7, any new source subject to this subpart which introduces pollutants into publicly owned treatment works must comply with 40 CFR part 403 and achieve the following pretreatment standards for new sources.
(a) Casting Quench Operations.
(b) Die Casting Operations.
(c) Melting Furnace Scrubber Operations.
(d) Mold Cooling Operations.
