48 C.F.R. § 5.404 — 5.404 Release of long-range acquisition estimates.
Citation: us:cfr:48:5.404
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-5.404
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 5 (Publicizing Contract Actions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To assist industry planning and to locate additional sources of supply, it may be desirable to publicize estimates of unclassified long-range acquisition requirements. Estimates may be publicized as far in advance as possible.
