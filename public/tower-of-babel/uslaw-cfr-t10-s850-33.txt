10 C.F.R. § 850.33 — Beryllium emergencies.
Citation: us:cfr:10:850.33
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-850.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 850 (Chronic Beryllium Disease Prevention Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The responsible employer must comply with 29 CFR 1910.120(l) for handling beryllium emergencies related to decontamination and decommissioning operations.
(b) The responsible employer must comply with 29 CFR 1910.120(q) for handling beryllium emergencies related to all other operations.
