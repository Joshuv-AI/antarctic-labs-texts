49 U.S.C. § 41906 — Duty to oppose unreasonable prices under the Universal Postal Union Convention
Citation: us:usc:49:41906
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section41906
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Air Commerce And Safety); Subpart ii (Economic Regulation); Chapter 419 (Transportation Of Mail). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of State and the United States Postal Service shall—
(1)
take appropriate action to ensure that the prices paid for transporting mail under the Universal Postal Union Convention are not higher than reasonable prices for transporting mail; and
(2)
oppose any existing or proposed Universal Postal Union price that is higher than a reasonable price for transporting mail.
