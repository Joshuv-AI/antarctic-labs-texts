15 U.S.C. § 6307 — Reporting
Citation: us:usc:15:6307
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section6307
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 89 (Professional Boxing Safety). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 48 business hours after the conclusion of a professional boxing match, the supervising boxing commission shall report the results of such boxing match and any related suspensions to each boxer registry.
