49 C.F.R. § 213.13 — Measuring track not under load.
Citation: us:cfr:49:213.13
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-213.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 213 (Track Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When unloaded track is measured to determine compliance with requirements of this part, the amount of rail movement, if any, that occurs while the track is loaded must be added to the measurements of the unloaded track.
