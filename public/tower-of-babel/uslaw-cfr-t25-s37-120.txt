25 C.F.R. § 37.120 — How does this part affect current geographic attendance boundaries?
Citation: us:cfr:25:37.120
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-37.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 37 (Geographic Boundaries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The currently established geographic attendance boundaries of day schools, on-reservation boarding schools, and peripheral dorms remain in place unless the tribal governing body revises them.
