49 U.S.C. § 28302 — Penalties
Citation: us:usc:49:28302
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section28302
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part E (Miscellaneous); Chapter 283 (Standard Work Day). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A person violating section 28301 of this title shall be fined under title 18, imprisoned not more than one year, or both.
