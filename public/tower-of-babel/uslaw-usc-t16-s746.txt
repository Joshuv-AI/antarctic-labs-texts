16 U.S.C. § 746 — Vessels of Fish and Wildlife Service
Citation: us:usc:16:746
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section746
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 9 (Fish And Wildlife Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Navy is authorized to place the vessels of the United States Fish and Wildlife Service on the same footing with the Navy Department as those of the National Ocean Survey.
