19 C.F.R. § 351.501 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:19:351.501
Creator: International Trade Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-351.501
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 351 (Antidumping And Countervailing Duties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart E set forth rules regarding the identification and measurement of countervailable subsidies. Where this subpart E does not expressly deal with a particular type of alleged subsidy, the Secretary will identify and measure the subsidy, if any, in accordance with the underlying principles of the Act and this subpart E.
