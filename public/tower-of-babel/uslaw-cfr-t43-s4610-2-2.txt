43 C.F.R. § 4610.2-2 — -2 Period of lease.
Citation: us:cfr:43:4610.2-2
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4610.2-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4600 (Leases Of Grazing Land—Pierce Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Leases may be made for such periods as are deemed proper by an authorized officer in promoting a proper land-use program in connection with the public range, not to exceed, however, the 10-year period as limited by the Pierce Act, beginning with the date of the approval of such lease.
