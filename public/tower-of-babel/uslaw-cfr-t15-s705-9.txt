15 C.F.R. § 705.9 — Emergency action.
Citation: us:cfr:15:705.9
Creator: Bureau Of Industry And Security, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-705.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 705 (Effect Of Imported Articles On The National Security). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In emergency situations, or when in the judgment of the Department, national security interests require it, the Department may vary or dispense with any or all of the procedures set forth in § 705.7 of this part.
