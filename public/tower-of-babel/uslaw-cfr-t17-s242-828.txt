17 C.F.R. § 242.828 — Core Principle 11—Conflicts of interest.
Citation: us:cfr:17:242.828
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 242 (Regulations M, Sho, Ats, Ac, Nms, Se, And Sbsr, And Customer Margin Requirements For Security Futures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The security-based swap execution facility shall:
(1) Establish and enforce rules to minimize conflicts of interest in its decision-making process; and
(2) Establish a process for resolving the conflicts of interest.
(b) A security-based swap execution facility shall comply with the requirements of § 242.834.
