42 U.S.C. § 3030n — Distribution to area agencies on aging
Citation: us:usc:42:3030n
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section3030n
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 35 (Programs For Older Americans); Subchapter III (Grants For State And Community Programs On Aging); Part D (Evidence-Based Disease Prevention And Health Promotion Services). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The State agency shall give priority, in carrying out this part, to areas of the State—
(1)
which are medically underserved; and
(2)
in which there are a large number of older individuals who have the greatest economic need for such services.
