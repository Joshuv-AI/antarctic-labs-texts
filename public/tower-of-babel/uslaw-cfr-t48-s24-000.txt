48 C.F.R. § 24.000 — 24.000 Scope of part.
Citation: us:cfr:48:24.000
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-24.000
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 24 (Protection Of Privacy And Freedom Of Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes policies and procedures that apply requirements of the Privacy Act of 1974 (5 U.S.C. 552a) (the Act) and OMB Circular No. A-130, December 12, 1985, to Government contracts and cites the Freedom of Information Act (5 U.S.C. 552, as amended.)
