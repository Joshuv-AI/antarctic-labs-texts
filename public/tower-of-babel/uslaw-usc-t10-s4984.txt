10 U.S.C. § 4984 — Transferability, additional limitations, and definition
Citation: us:usc:10:4984
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section4984
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part V (Acquisition); Subpart I (Defense Industrial Base); Chapter 389 (Loan Guarantee Programs); Subchapter II (Critical Infrastructure Protection Loan Guarantees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The following provisions of subchapter I of this chapter apply to guarantees issued under this subchapter:
(1)
Section 4972, relating to transferability of guarantees.
(2)
Subsections (b) and (c) of section 4973, providing limitations.
(3)
Section 4975(2), providing a definition of the term “cost”.
