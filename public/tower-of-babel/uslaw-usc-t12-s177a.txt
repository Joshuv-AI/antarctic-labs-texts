12 U.S.C. § 177a — Funds available for cost of transporting and redeeming national and Federal Reserve bank notes
Citation: us:usc:12:177a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section177a
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 2 (National Banks); Subchapter XI (Miscellaneous Provisions Regarding United States Bonds In Relation To National Banks). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The cost of transporting and redeeming outstanding national bank notes and Federal Reserve bank notes as may be presented to the Treasurer of the United States for redemption shall be paid from the regular annual appropriation for the Department of the Treasury.
