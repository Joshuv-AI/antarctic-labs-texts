11 C.F.R. § 9032.5 — Matching payment account.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 9032 (Definitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Matching payment account means the Presidential Primary Matching Payment Account established by the Secretary of the Treasury under 26 U.S.C. 9037(a).
