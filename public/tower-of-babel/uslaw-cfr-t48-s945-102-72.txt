48 C.F.R. § 945.102-72 — 945.102-72 Reporting of contractor sensitive property inventory.
Citation: us:cfr:48:945.102-72
Creator: Chapter 9—Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-945.102-72
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 945 (Government Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contractor must develop and maintain a list of personal property items considered sensitive. Sensitive Items List must be approved by the PA/OPMO annually.
