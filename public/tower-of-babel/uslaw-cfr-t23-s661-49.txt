23 C.F.R. § 661.49 — Can TTFBP funds be spent on Interstate, State Highway, County, City, Township, and Toll Road TTF bridges?
Citation: us:cfr:23:661.49
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-661.49
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 661 (Tribal Transportation Facility Bridge Program (Ttfbp)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. Interstate, State Highway, County, City, Township, and Toll Road TTF bridges are eligible for funding as described in § 661.37(b).
