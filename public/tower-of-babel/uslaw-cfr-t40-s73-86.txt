40 C.F.R. § 73.86 — State regulatory autonomy.
Citation: us:cfr:40:73.86
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-73.86
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 73 (Sulfur Dioxide Allowance System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this subpart shall preclude a State or State regulatory authority from providing additional incentives to utilities to encourage investment in any conservation measures or renewable energy generation.
