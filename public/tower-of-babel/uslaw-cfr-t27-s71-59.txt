27 C.F.R. § 71.59 — Application cases.
Citation: us:cfr:27:71.59
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-71.59
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 71 (Rules Of Practice In Permit Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the applicant for a permit desires a hearing, he shall file a request therefor, in writing, with the appropriate TTB officer within fifteen days after receipt of notice of the contemplated disapproval, in whole or in part, of his application.
