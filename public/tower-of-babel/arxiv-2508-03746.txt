Spectral extremal problem of the p p th power of cycles
Xinhui Duan, Lu Lu

Abstract
For a cycle C k C_{k} on k k vertices, its p p -th power, denoted C k p C_{k}^{p} , is the graph obtained by adding edges between all pairs of vertices at distance at most p p in C k C_{k} . Let ex ​ ( n , F ) \textrm{ex}(n,F) and spex ​ ( n , F ) \textrm{spex}(n,F) denote the maximum possible number of edges and the maximum possible spectral radius, respectively, among all n n -vertex F F -free graphs. In this paper, we determine precisely the unique extremal graph achieving ex ​ ( n , C k p ) \textrm{ex}(n,C_{k}^{p}) and spex ​ ( n , C k p ) \textrm{spex}(n,C_{k}^{p}) for sufficiently large n n . AMS Classification : 05C50 Keywords : Spectral radius, Spectral extremal graph, Turán problem.

G
and every vertex of
H
. For positive integers

, the complete

-partite graph

\overline{K_{n_{1}}\cup\cdots\cup K_{n_{r}}}
. The
r
-partite Turán graph on

with
\max\{|n_{i}-n_{j}|\mid 1\leq i,j\leq r\}\leq 1

The study of Turán numbers for

[

[
13

[
8
]
established
\textrm{ex}(n,C_{n}^{2})
in 2022. The classical Mantel Theorem gives
\textrm{ex}(n,C_{3})
, and Füredi and Gunderson
[
5
]
described

for

. The most recent results on

[

[

[

.

Proof of Theorem
1.1

For any positive integer
k
, a graph
G
is called
color-
k
-critical
if there exist
k
suitable edges whose removal decreases its chromatic number, and deleting any
k-1
vertices does not decrease its chromatic number. It is clear from the definition that any
k
edges whose removal decreases
\chi(G)
must form a matching of size
k
. In particular, color-1-critical graphs are just color-critical graphs. In
[
14
]
, Simonovits determined the unique extremal graph for every color-
k
-critical graph.

Lemma 2.1

(
[
14
]
)
.

Let
k\geq 1
and let
H
be a a color-
k
-critical graph with chromatic number
\chi(H)=r+1
. Then
\textrm{EX}(n,H)=\{K_{k-1}\vee T_{n-k+1,r}\}
for sufficiently large
n
.

Lemma 2.2
.

For two integers
k
and
p
with
k\geq 2p+1
, assume
k=s(p+1)+r
for some
s\geq 1
and
0\leq r<p+1
. If
r=0
, then
\chi(C_{k}^{p})=p+1
. If
r\neq 0
, let
r=ms+t
for
m\geq 0
and
1\leq t\leq s
, then
\chi(C_{k}^{p})=p+m+2
and
C_{k}^{p}
is a color-
t
-critical.

Proof.

Assume
V(C_{k}^{p})=\{v_{0},v_{1},\ldots,v_{k-1}\}
such that
v_{0}v_{1}\cdots v_{k-1}v_{0}
forms the cycle
C_{k}
. First, we consider the case
r=0
. Color the vertex
v_{i}
with the color
a
if
i\equiv a\pmod{p}+1
. Let
V_{a}=\{v_{i}\mid i\equiv a\pmod{p+1}\}
be the set of vertices with color
a
. Clearly,
V_{a}
is an independent set. Thus,
\chi(C_{k}^{p})\leq p+1
. On the other hand, if we color
C_{k}^{p}
by
\{0,1,\ldots,p-1\}
, then let
U_{a}
be the set of vertices with color
a
. Since
k=sp+s
, there exists
U_{b}
with
|U_{b}|\geq s+1
. We claim that
U_{b}
cannot be an independent set. Since otherwise, any pair of vertices in
U_{b}
has distance at least
p+1
in
C_{k}
. Thus, there would be at least
(s+1)(p+1)
vertices in
C_{k}
, which is impossible. Hence,
\chi(C_{k}^{p})\geq p+1
. This yields
\chi(C_{k}^{p})=p+1
.

Next, we consider the case
r\neq 0
. Since
k=s(p+1)+r=s(p+1)+ms+t=t(p+m+2)+(s-t)(p+m+1)
, we construct a coloring
f
:
V(C_{k}^{p})\rightarrow\{0,\ldots,p+m+1\}
by

f(v_{i})=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}a,&\text{ if }0\leq i\leq t(p+m+2)-1\text{ and }a\equiv i\pmod{p+m+2},\\[5.69054pt]
b,&\text{ if }t(p+m+2)\leq i\leq k-1\text{ and }b\equiv i-t(p+m+2)\pmod{p+m+1}.\end{array}\right.

For
0\leq a\leq p+m+1
, let
V_{a}
be the set of vertices colored
a
. Clearly,
V_{a}
is an independent set. Therefore,
\chi(C_{k}^{p})\leq p+m+2
. If
C_{k}^{p}
is colored by
\{0,1,\ldots,p+m+1\}
, let
U_{a}
be the set of vertices colored
a
. Since
k=s(p+m+1)+t
with
1\leq t\leq s
, there must be a set
U_{b}
contains
s+1
vertices. Similarly,
U_{b}
could not be an independent set. Hence,
\chi(C_{k}^{p})\geq p+m+2
. This leads to
\chi(C_{k}^{p})=p+m+2
.

Finally, we show that the graph
C_{k}^{p}
is a color-
t
-critical when
r\neq 0
. On the one hand, let

B=\{v_{p+m}v_{p+m+1},\ldots,v_{(t-1)(p+m+2)+(p+m)}v_{t(p+m+2)-1}\}\subseteq E(C_{k}^{p}).

We claim that
\chi(C_{k}^{p}-B)\leq p+m+1<\chi(C_{k}^{p})=p+m+2
. Define a coloring
g
:
V(C_{k}^{p}-B)\rightarrow\mathbb{Z}_{p+m+1}
by setting

g(v_{i})=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}p+m,&\text{ if }0\leq i\leq t(p+m+2)-1\text{ and }i\equiv p+m+1\pmod{p+m+2},\\[5.69054pt]
f(v_{i}),&\text{ otherwise }.\end{array}\right.

It is easy to verify that
g
is a proper coloring and thus
\chi(C_{k}^{p}-B)\leq p+m+1<\chi(C_{k}^{p})=p+m+2
. On the other hand, for any subset
S\subseteq V(C_{k}^{p})
with
t-1
vertices, we claim that
\chi(C_{k}^{p}-S)=\chi(C_{k}^{p})
. Suppose to the contrary that there exists a subset
S\subset C_{k}^{p}
of
t-1
vertices such that
\chi(C_{k}^{p}-S)<\chi(C_{k}^{p})=p+m+2
, where
0<t<s
. Then, if we color the graph
C_{k}^{p}-S
using
p+m+1
colors, there must be at least
s+1
vertices assigned the same color. Therefore, each pair of these vertices has distance at least
p+1
in
C_{k}
. This indicates there are at least
(s+1)(p+1)
vertices, which is impossible since
(s+1)(p+1)>k
. Therefore, the graph
C_{k}^{p}
is a color-
t
-critical.
∎

Proof of Theorem

From Lemma

Lemma 3.3

\lambda(G)\geq\lambda(K_{t-1}\vee T_{n-t+1,p^{\prime}})\geq(1-1/p^{\prime})n.

\displaystyle e(G)

\displaystyle\leq\sum_{1\leq i<j\leq p^{\prime}}|V_{i}||V_{j}|+\sum^{p^{\prime}}_{i=1}e(V_{i})
