19 C.F.R. § 182.122 — Corrected claim or certification of origin by importers.
Citation: us:cfr:19:182.122
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-182.122
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 182 (United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An importer who makes a corrected claim under § 182.11(c) will not be subject to civil or administrative penalties under 19 U.S.C. 1592 for having made an incorrect claim or having submitted an incorrect certification of origin, provided that the corrected claim is promptly and voluntarily made in accordance with § 182.124.
