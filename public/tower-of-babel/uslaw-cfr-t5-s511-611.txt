5 C.F.R. § 511.611 — Cancellation of an employee appeal.
Citation: us:cfr:5:511.611
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-511.611
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 511 (Classification Under The General Schedule). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee's appeal shall be cancelled and the employee so notified in writing in the following circumstances:
(a) On receipt of the employee's written request for cancellation.
(b) On failure to prosecute, when the employee or the designated representative does not furnish requested information, or proceed with the advancement of the appeal.
