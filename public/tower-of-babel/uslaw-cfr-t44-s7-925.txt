44 C.F.R. § 7.925 — Special benefits for children and the elderly.
Citation: us:cfr:44:7.925
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-7.925
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 7 (Nondiscrimination In Federally-Assisted Programs (Fema Reg. 5)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a recipient operating a program or activity provides special benefits to the elderly or to children, such use of age distinctions shall be presumed to be necessary to the normal operation of the program or activity, notwithstanding the provisions of § 7.921.
