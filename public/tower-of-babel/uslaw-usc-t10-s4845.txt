10 U.S.C. § 4845 — Armament retooling and manufacturing
Citation: us:usc:10:4845
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section4845
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part V (Acquisition); Subpart I (Defense Industrial Base); Chapter 384 (Manufacturing Technology). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Army is authorized by chapter 764 of this title to carry out programs for the support of armaments retooling and manufacturing in the national defense industrial and technology base.
