21 C.F.R. § 14.172 — Utilization of an advisory committee at the request of an interested person.
Citation: us:cfr:21:14.172
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-14.172
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 14 (Public Hearing Before A Public Advisory Committee). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any interested person may request, under § 10.30, that a specific matter relating to a particular human prescription drug be submitted to an appropriate advisory committee for a hearing and review and recommendations. The request must demonstrate the importance of the matter and the reasons why it should be submitted for a hearing at that time. The Commissioner may grant or deny the request.
