49 C.F.R. § 229.206 — Design requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:49:229.206
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-229.206
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 229 (Railroad Locomotive Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each locomotive used in occupied service must meet the minimum anti-climber, emergency egress, emergency interior lighting, and interior configuration design requirements set forth in AAR S-580 (incorporated by reference, see § 229.205).
