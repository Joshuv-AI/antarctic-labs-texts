43 C.F.R. § 3936.10 — Inspection of underground and surface operations and facilities.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3936.10
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3936.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3930 (Management Of Oil Shale Exploration And Leases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Operators, licensees, or lessees must allow the BLM, at any time, either day or night, to inspect or investigate underground and surface mining, in situ, or exploration operations to determine compliance with lease or license terms and conditions, compliance with the approved exploration or development plans, and to verify production.
