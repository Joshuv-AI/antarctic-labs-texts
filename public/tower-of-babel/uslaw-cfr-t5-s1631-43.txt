5 C.F.R. § 1631.43 — Enforcement.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1631.43
Creator: Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1631.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1631 (Availability Of Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon the failure of any party to comply with a subpoena, the General Counsel shall request that the Attorney General seek enforcement of the subpoena in the appropriate United States district court.
