6 U.S.C. § 1002 — Homeland Security Science and Technology Advisory Committee
Citation: us:usc:6:1002
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section1002
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 3 (Security And Accountability For Every Port); Subchapter III (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Under Secretary for Science and Technology shall utilize the Homeland Security Science and Technology Advisory Committee, as appropriate, to provide outside expertise in advancing cargo security technology.
