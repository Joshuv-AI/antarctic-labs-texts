5 C.F.R. § 839.611 — What are the time limits for making an election?
Citation: us:cfr:5:839.611
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-839.611
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 839 (Correction Of Retirement Coverage Errors Under The Federal Erroneous Retirement Coverage Corrections Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If your qualifying retirement coverage error was not previously corrected, you have 6 months from the date you receive notice of the error under § 839.302 to make an election.
(b) If your qualifying retirement coverage error was previously corrected, the time limit for making an election expires on September 19, 2002.
