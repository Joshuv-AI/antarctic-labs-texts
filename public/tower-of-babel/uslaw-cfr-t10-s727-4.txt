10 C.F.R. § 727.4 — Is there any expectation of privacy applicable to a DOE computer?
Citation: us:cfr:10:727.4
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-727.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 727 (Consent For Access To Information On Department Of Energy Computers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notwithstanding any other provision of law (including any provision of law enacted by the Electronic Communications Privacy Act of 1986), no user of a DOE computer shall have any expectation of privacy in the use of that DOE computer.
