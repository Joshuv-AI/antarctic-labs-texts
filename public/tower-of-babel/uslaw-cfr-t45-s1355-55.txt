45 C.F.R. § 1355.55 — Review and assessment of CCWIS projects.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1355.55
Creator: Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1355.55
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1355 (General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

ACF will review, assess, and inspect the planning, design, development, installation, operation, and maintenance of each CCWIS project on a continuing basis, in accordance with APD requirements in 45 CFR part 95, subpart F, to determine the extent to which the project meets the requirements in §§ 1355.52, 1355.53, 1355.56, and, if applicable, § 1355.54.
