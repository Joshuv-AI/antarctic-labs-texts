16 U.S.C. § 6577 — Involvement by other agencies and organizations
Citation: us:usc:16:6577
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section6577
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 84 (Healthy Forest Restoration); Subchapter V (Healthy Forests Reserve Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In carrying out this subchapter, the Secretary of Agriculture may consult with—
(1)
nonindustrial private forest landowners;
(2)
other Federal agencies;
(3)
State fish and wildlife agencies;
(4)
State forestry agencies;
(5)
State environmental quality agencies;
(6)
other State conservation agencies; and
(7)
nonprofit conservation organizations.
