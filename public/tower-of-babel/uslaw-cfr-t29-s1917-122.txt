29 C.F.R. § 1917.122 — Employee exits.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1917.122
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1917.122
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1917 (Marine Terminals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Employee exits shall be clearly marked.
(b) If an employee exit is not visible from employees' work stations, directional signs indicating routes to the exit shall be posted.
(c) Exits shall be readily accessible and sufficient in number to provide employees with a convenient means of escape in emergencies. A clear passage to the exit shall be maintained.
(d) The minimum width of any employee exit shall be 28 inches (71.12 cm).
