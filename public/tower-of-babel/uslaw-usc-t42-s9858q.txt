42 U.S.C. § 9858q — Miscellaneous provisions
Citation: us:usc:42:9858q
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section9858q
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 105 (Community Services Programs); Subchapter II–B (Child Care And Development Block Grant). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other law, the value of any child care provided or arranged (or any amount received as payment for such care or reimbursement for costs incurred for such care) under this subchapter shall not be treated as income for purposes of any other Federal or Federally-assisted program that bases eligibility, or the amount of benefits, on need.
