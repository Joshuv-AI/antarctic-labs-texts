43 U.S.C. § 1470 — Appropriations; availability for certain administrative expenses
Citation: us:usc:43:1470
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section1470
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 31 (Department Of The Interior). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Appropriations for field work of the Department of the Interior shall be available for the hire, with or without personal services, of boats, work animals, and animal-drawn and motor-propelled vehicles and equipment.
