44 U.S.C. § 702 — Extra copies of documents and reports
Citation: us:usc:44:702
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section702
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 7 (Congressional Printing And Binding). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Copies in addition to the “usual number” of documents and reports shall be printed promptly when ready for publication, and may be bound in paper or cloth as the Joint Committee on Printing directs.
