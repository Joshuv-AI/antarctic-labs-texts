20 C.F.R. § 349.7 — Time and place to request a review and/or hearing on revised decision.
Citation: us:cfr:20:349.7
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-349.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 349 (Finality Of Decisions Regarding Unemployment And Sickness Insurance Benefits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A party to a revised decision may request, as appropriate, further review of the decision in accordance with the rules set forth in part 320 of this chapter. Further review or a hearing will be held according to the rules set forth in part 320 of this chapter.
