50 U.S.C. § 834 — “Classified information” defined
Citation: us:usc:50:834
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section834
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 23 (Internal Security); Subchapter III (Personnel Security Procedures In National Security Agency). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purposes of this section, the term “classified information” means information which, for reasons of national security, is specifically designated by a United States Government agency for limited or restricted dissemination or distribution.
