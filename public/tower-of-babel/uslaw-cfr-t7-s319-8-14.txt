7 C.F.R. § 319.8-14 — -14 Mexican cotton and covers not otherwise enterable.
Citation: us:cfr:7:319.8-14
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-319.8-14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 319 (Foreign Quarantine Notices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Mexican cotton and covers not enterable under § 319.8-11, § 319.8-12, or § 319.8-13 may be entered in accordance with §§ 319.8-6 through 319.8-10 and §§ 319.8-16 through 319.8-20 insofar as said sections are applicable.
