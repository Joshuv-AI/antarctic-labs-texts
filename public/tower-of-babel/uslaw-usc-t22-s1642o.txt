22 U.S.C. § 1642o — Applicability of administrative provisions of subchapter I
Citation: us:usc:22:1642o
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1642o
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 21 (Settlement Of International Claims); Subchapter IV (Claims Against Czechoslovakia). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To the extent they are not inconsistent with the provisions of this subchapter, the following provisions of subchapter I shall be applicable to this subchapter: Subsections (b), (c), (d), (e), (h), and (j) of section 1623 of this title; subsections (c), (d), (e), and (f) of section 1626 of this title.
