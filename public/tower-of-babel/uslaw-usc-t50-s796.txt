50 U.S.C. § 796 — Effect of subchapter on other criminal laws
Citation: us:usc:50:796
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section796
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 23 (Internal Security); Subchapter I (Control Of Subversive Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The foregoing provisions of this subchapter shall be construed as being in addition to and not in modification of existing criminal statutes.
