Following publication of the original article [1], the authors reported some typesetting errors, whereby the figures in the final published article were placed in the incorrect order. The correct order of figures is given below.

Further to this, in Table 2, “P < 0.001###” needs to be bolded. In Table 3, the underlining of certain entries are missing. The incorrect and correct Table 3 is supplied below. The original article [1] has been updated.
