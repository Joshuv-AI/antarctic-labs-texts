10 U.S.C. § 1219 — Statement of origin of disease or injury: limitations
Citation: us:usc:10:1219
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1219
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 61 (Retirement Or Separation For Physical Disability). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A member of an armed force may not be required to sign a statement relating to the origin, incurrence, or aggravation of a disease or injury that he has. Any such statement against his interests, signed by a member, is invalid.
