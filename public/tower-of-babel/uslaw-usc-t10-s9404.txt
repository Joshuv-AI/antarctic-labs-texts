10 U.S.C. § 9404 — Aviation students: detail of enlisted members of Air Force
Citation: us:usc:10:9404
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9404
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training); Chapter 951 (Training Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Air Force may detail enlisted Regulars of the Air Force, and enlisted Reserves of the Air Force who are on active duty, for training and instruction as aviation students in their respective grades at schools selected by him.
