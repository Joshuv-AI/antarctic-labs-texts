49 C.F.R. § 387.407 — Surety bonds and certificates of insurance.
Citation: us:cfr:49:387.407
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-387.407
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 387 (Minimum Levels Of Financial Responsibility For Motor Carriers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The limits of liability under § 387.405 may be provided by aggregation under the procedures at 49 CFR part 387, subpart C.
(b) Each policy of insurance used in connection with a certificate of insurance filed with the FMCSA shall be amended by attachment of the appropriate endorsement prescribed by the FMCSA (or the Department of Transportation, where applicable).
