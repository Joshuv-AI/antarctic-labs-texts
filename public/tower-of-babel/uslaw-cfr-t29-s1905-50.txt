29 C.F.R. § 1905.50 — Effect of appeal of a hearing examiner's decision.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1905.50
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1905.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1905 (Rules Of Practice For Variances, Limitations, Variations, Tolerances, And Exemptions Under The Williams-Steiger Occupational Safety And Health Act Of 1970). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A hearing examiner's decision under this part shall not be operative pending a decision on appeal by the Assistant Secretary.
