22 U.S.C. § 2211c — Poverty measurement methods
Citation: us:usc:22:2211c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2211c
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 32 (Foreign Assistance); Subchapter I (International Development); Part II (Other Programs); Subpart vi (Development Assistance For Micro, Small, And Medium-Sized Enterprises). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator of the Agency, in consultation with financial intermediaries and other appropriate organizations, should have in place at least 1 method for implementing partners to use to assess poverty levels of their current incoming or prospective clients.
