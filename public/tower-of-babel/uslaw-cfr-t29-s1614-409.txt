29 C.F.R. § 1614.409 — Effect of filing a civil action.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1614.409
Creator: Equal Employment Opportunity Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1614.409
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1614 (Federal Sector Equal Employment Opportunity). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Filing a civil action under § 1614.407 or § 1614.408 shall terminate Commission processing of the appeal. A Commission decision on an appeal issued after a complainant files suit in district court will not be enforceable by the Commission. If private suit is filed subsequent to the filing of an appeal and prior to a final Commission decision, the complainant should notify the Commission in writing.
