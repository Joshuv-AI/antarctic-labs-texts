46 U.S.C. § 6104 — Commercial fishing industry vessel casualty statistics
Citation: us:usc:46:6104
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section6104
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part D (Marine Casualties); Chapter 61 (Reporting Marine Casualties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Secretary shall compile statistics concerning marine casualties from data compiled from insurers of fishing vessels, fish processing vessels, and fish tender vessels.
(b)
A person underwriting primary insurance for a fishing vessel, fish processing vessel, or fish tender vessel shall submit periodically to the Secretary data concerning marine casualties that is required by regulations prescribed by the Secretary.
(c)
After consulting with the insurance industry, the Secretary shall prescribe regulations under this section to gather a statistical base for analyzing vessel risks.
(d)
The Secretary may delegate to a qualified person that has knowledge and experience in the collection of statistical insurance data the authority of the Secretary under this section to compile statistics from insurers.
