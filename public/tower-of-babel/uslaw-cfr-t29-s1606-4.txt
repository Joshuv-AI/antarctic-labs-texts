29 C.F.R. § 1606.4 — The bona fide occupational qualification exception.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1606.4
Creator: Equal Employment Opportunity Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1606.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1606 (Guidelines On Discrimination Because Of National Origin). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The exception stated in section 703(e) of title VII, that national origin may be a bona fide occupational qualification, shall be strictly construed.
