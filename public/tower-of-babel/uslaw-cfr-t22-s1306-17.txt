22 C.F.R. § 1306.17 — Non-waiver of rights by payments.
Citation: us:cfr:22:1306.17
Creator: Millennium Challenge Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1306.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1306 (Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

So long as there are no statutory or contractual provisions to the contrary, no employee payment (of all or a portion of a debt) collected under this part will be interpreted as a waiver of any rights that the employee may have under 5 U.S.C. 5514.
