29 C.F.R. § 870.2 — Amendments to this part.
Citation: us:cfr:29:870.2
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-870.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 870 (Restriction On Garnishment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator may, at any time upon his own motion or upon written request of any interested person setting forth reasonable grounds therefor, amend any rules in this part.
