45 C.F.R. § 2554.47 — What judicial review is available?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2554.47
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2554.47
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2554 (Program Fraud Civil Remedies Act Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

31 U.S.C. 3805 authorizes judicial review by the appropriate United States District Court of any final Corporation decision imposing penalties or assessments, and specifies the procedures for such review. To obtain judicial review, a defendant must file a petition with the appropriate court in a timely manner.
