5 C.F.R. § 550.171 — Authorization of pay for Sunday work.
Citation: us:cfr:5:550.171
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-550.171
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 550 (Pay Administration (General)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An employee is entitled to pay at his or her rate of basic pay plus premium pay at a rate equal to 25 percent of his or her rate of basic pay for each hour of Sunday work (as defined in § 550.103).
(b) An employee is not entitled to Sunday premium pay while engaged in training, except as provided in § 410.402 of this chapter.
