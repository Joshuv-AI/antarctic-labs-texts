7 C.F.R. § 925.45 — Production research and market research and development.
Citation: us:cfr:7:925.45
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-925.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 925 (Grapes Grown In A Designated Area Of Southeastern California). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The committee, with the approval of the Secretary, may establish or provide for the establishment of production research, marketing research and development projects designed to assist, improve or promote the marketing, distribution and consumption or the efficient production of grapes. The expense of such projects shall be paid from funds collected pursuant to this part.
