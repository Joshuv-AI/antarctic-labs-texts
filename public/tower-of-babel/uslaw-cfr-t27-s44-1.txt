27 C.F.R. § 44.1 — Exportation of tobacco products, and cigarette papers and tubes, without payment of tax, or with drawback of tax.
Citation: us:cfr:27:44.1
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-44.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 44 (Exportation Of Tobacco Products And Cigarette Papers And Tubes, Without Payment Of Tax, Or With Drawback Of Tax). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part contains the regulations relating to the exportation (including supplies for vessels and aircraft) of tobacco products and cigarette papers and tubes, without payment of tax; the qualification of, and operations by, export warehouse proprietors; and the allowance of drawback of tax paid on tobacco products, and cigarette papers and tubes exported.
