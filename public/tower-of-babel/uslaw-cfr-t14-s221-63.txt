14 C.F.R. § 221.63 — Other types of governing tariffs.
Citation: us:cfr:14:221.63
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-221.63
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 221 (Tariffs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subject to approval of the Department, carriers may publish other types of governing tariffs not specified in this subpart, such as routing guides.
