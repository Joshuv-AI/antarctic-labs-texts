5 C.F.R. § 2634.805 — Retention.
Citation: us:cfr:5:2634.805
Creator: Office Of Government Ethics
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-2634.805
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 2634 (Executive Branch Financial Disclosure, Qualified Trusts, And Certificates Of Divestiture). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Records of ethics agreements and actions described in this subpart will be maintained by the agency. In addition, copies of such record will be maintained by the Office of Government Ethics with respect to filers whose reports are certified by the Office of Government Ethics.
