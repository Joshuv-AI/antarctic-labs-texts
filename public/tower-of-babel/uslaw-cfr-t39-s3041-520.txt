39 C.F.R. § 3041.520 — Terminations.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3041.520
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3041.520
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3041 (Competitive Negotiated Service Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Postal Service shall file notice of the termination of a negotiated service agreement within 7 days after such termination.
(b) Upon termination, a negotiated service agreement shall be removed automatically from the competitive product list. No negotiated service agreement shall remain on the competitive product list after its termination.
