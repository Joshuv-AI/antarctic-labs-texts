42 C.F.R. § 460.3 — Part D program requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:42:460.3
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-460.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 460 (Programs Of All-Inclusive Care For The Elderly (Pace)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

PACE organizations offering qualified prescription drug coverage and meeting the definition of a Part D plan sponsor, as defined in § 423.4 of this chapter, must abide by all applicable Part D program requirements in part 423 of this chapter.
