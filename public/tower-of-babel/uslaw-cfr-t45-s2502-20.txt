45 C.F.R. § 2502.20 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:45:2502.20
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2502.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2502 (Employee Indemnification Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) This part is applicable to all former and current AmeriCorps employees, including special Government employees.
(b) This part does not apply to volunteers, service members, contractors, or any other individuals who may be affiliated with AmeriCorps, but not employed by the agency.
