20 U.S.C. § 1651 — Prohibition against assignment or transportation of students to overcome racial imbalance
Citation: us:usc:20:1651
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1651
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 37 (Assignment Or Transportation Of Students). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No provision of this Act shall be construed to require the assignment or transportation of students or teachers in order to overcome racial imbalance.
