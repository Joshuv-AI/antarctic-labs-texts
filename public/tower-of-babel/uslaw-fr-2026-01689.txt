DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute; Amended Notice of 
Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Heart, Lung, and Blood Advisory Council, October 29, 2026, 12:00 p.m. 
to October 29, 2026, 04:00 p.m., Claude D. Pepper Building, 31 Center 
Drive, Bethesda, MD 20892 which was published in the Federal Register 
on January 26, 2026, 91 FRN 3210.
    The National Heart, Lung, and Blood Advisory Council open meeting 
is being amended due to a change in the meeting date from October 29, 
2026, to March 25, 2026; meeting format from regular to virtual; and 
the meeting location Claude D. Pepper Building, 31 Center Drive, 
Bethesda, MD 20892 to 6701 Rockledge Drive, Bethesda, MD 20892. The 
meeting is partially Closed to the public.

    Dated: January 26, 2026.
Denise M. Santeufemio,
Supervisory Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee 
Policy.
