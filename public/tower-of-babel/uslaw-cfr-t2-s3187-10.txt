2 C.F.R. § 3187.10 — Rejection for technical deficiency—appeal.
Citation: us:cfr:2:3187.10
Creator: Institute Of Museum And Library Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-3187.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 3187 (Uniform Administrative Requirements, Cost Principles, And Audit Requirements For Federal Awards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An applicant whose application is rejected because of technical deficiency may appeal such rejection in writing to the Director within 10 business days of electronic or postmarked notice of rejection, whichever is earlier.
