43 U.S.C. § 315m–3 — Availability of contributions received
Citation: us:usc:43:315m–3
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section315m–3
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 8A (Grazing Lands); Subchapter I (Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Contributions received by the Secretary of the Interior under section 315h of this title, toward the administration, protection, and improvement of any district shall be additionally available for the leasing of lands under sections 315m–1 to 315m–4 of this title.
