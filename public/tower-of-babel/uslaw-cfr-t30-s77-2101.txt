30 C.F.R. § 77.2101 — Definitions.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 77 (Mandatory Safety Standards, Surface Coal Mines And Surface Work Areas Of Underground Coal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following definitions apply in this subpart—
Responsible person means a person with authority and responsibility to evaluate and update a written safety program for surface mobile equipment.
Surface mobile equipment means wheeled, skid-mounted, track-mounted, or rail-mounted equipment capable of moving or being moved, and any powered equipment that transports people, equipment, or materials, excluding belt conveyors, at surface coal mines and surface work areas of underground coal mines.
