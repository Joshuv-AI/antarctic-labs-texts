44 C.F.R. § 323.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:44:323.1
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-323.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 323 (Guidance On Priority Use Of Resources In Immediate Post Attack Period (Dmo-4)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part:
(a) States the policy of the Federal Government on use of resources in the period immediately following a nuclear attack on the United States;
(b) Provides general guidance for Federal, State, and local government officials on activities to be accorded priority in the use of postattack resources; and
(c) Lists those items essential to national survival in the immediate postattack period.
