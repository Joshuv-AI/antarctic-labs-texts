28 C.F.R. § 32.41 — Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:28:32.41
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-32.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 32 (Public Safety Officers' Death, Disability, And Educational Assistance Benefit Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Consistent with § 32.1, this subpart contains provisions applicable to requests for Hearing Officer determination of claims denied under subpart B, C (including affirmances of negative disability findings described in § 32.27), or D of this part, and of claims remanded (or matters referred) under § 32.54(c).
