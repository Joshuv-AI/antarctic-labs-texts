41 C.F.R. § 102-71.120 — -71.120 Severability.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-71.120
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-71.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-71 (71—Real Property Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All provisions included in this part are separate and severable from one another. If any provision is stayed or determined to be invalid, it is GSA's intention that the remaining provisions will continue in effect.
