49 C.F.R. § 10.85 — Wrongfully obtaining records.
Citation: us:cfr:49:10.85
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-10.85
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 10 (Maintenance Of And Access To Records Pertaining To Individuals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person who knowingly and willfully requests or obtains any record concerning an individual from the Department under false pretenses is guilty of a misdemeanor and fined not more than $5,000 in accordance with 5 U.S.C. 552a(i)(3).
