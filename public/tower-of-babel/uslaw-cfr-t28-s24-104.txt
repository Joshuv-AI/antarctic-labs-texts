28 C.F.R. § 24.104 — Applicability to Department of Justice proceedings.
Citation: us:cfr:28:24.104
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-24.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 24 (Implementation Of The Equal Access To Justice Act In Department Of Justice Administrative Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Act applies to an adversary adjudication pending before the Department at any time between October 1, 1981 and September 30, 1984. This includes proceedings begun before October 1, 1981 if final Department action has not been taken before that date, and proceedings pending on September 30, 1984.
