2 C.F.R. § 1120.400 — Requirement to include a preamble.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1120.400
Creator: Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1120.400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1120 (Award Format For Dod Grants And Cooperative Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each DoD Component must include a preamble as Subdivision A of each set of general terms and conditions it maintains, to provide information to help recipients understand how to use those terms and conditions.
