40 C.F.R. § 246.201-3 — -3 Recommended procedures: Glass, can, and mixed paper separation.
Citation: us:cfr:40:246.201-3
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-246.201-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 246 (Source Separation For Materials Recovery Guidelines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In areas where markets are available, it is recommended that glass, cans, and mixed paper be separated at the source of generation and separately collected for the purpose of recycling.
