45 C.F.R. § 264.77 — How will we determine if a State met its Contingency Fund expenditure requirements?
Citation: us:cfr:45:264.77
Creator: Office Of Family Assistance (Assistance Programs), Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-264.77
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 264 (Other Accountability Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) States receiving contingency funds for a fiscal year must complete the quarterly TANF Financial Report. As part of the fourth quarter's report, a State must complete its annual reconciliation.
(b) The TANF Financial Report and State reporting on expenditures are subject to our review.
