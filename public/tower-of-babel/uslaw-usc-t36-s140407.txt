36 U.S.C. § 140407 — Tax-exempt status required as condition of charter
Citation: us:usc:36:140407
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section140407
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1404 (Military Officers Association Of America). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If the corporation fails to maintain its status as an organization exempt from taxation under the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, the charter granted under this chapter shall terminate.
