18 C.F.R. § 1307.2 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:18:1307.2
Creator: Tennessee Valley Authority
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-1307.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 1307 (Nondiscrimination With Respect To Handicap). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to effectuate section 504 to the end that no otherwise qualified handicapped individual shall, solely by reason of his or her handicap, be excluded from the participation in, be denied the benefits of, or be subjected to discrimination under any program or activity receiving financial assistance from TVA.
