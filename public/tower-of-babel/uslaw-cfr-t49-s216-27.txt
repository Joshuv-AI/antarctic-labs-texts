49 C.F.R. § 216.27 — Reservation of authority and discretion.
Citation: us:cfr:49:216.27
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-216.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 216 (Special Notice And Emergency Order Procedures: Railroad Track, Locomotive And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The FRA may issue Emergency orders concerning track without regard to the procedures prescribed in this subpart whenever the Administrator determines that immediate action is required to assure the public safety.
