30 C.F.R. § 40.4 — Posting at mine.
Citation: us:cfr:30:40.4
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-40.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 40 (Representative Of Miners). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A copy of the information provided the operator pursuant to § 40.3 of this part shall be posted upon receipt by the operator on the mine bulletin board and maintained in a current status.
