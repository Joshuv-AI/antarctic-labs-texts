5 U.S.C. § 8139 — Employees of the District of Columbia
Citation: us:usc:5:8139
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section8139
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart G (Insurance And Annuities); Chapter 81 (Compensation For Work Injuries); Subchapter I (Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Compensation awarded to an employee of the government of the District of Columbia shall be paid in the manner provided by statute for the payment of the general expenses of the government of the District of Columbia.
