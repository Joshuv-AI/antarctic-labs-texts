7 C.F.R. § 1146.210 — Milk for other programs.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1146.210
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Milk), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1146.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1146 (Milk Donation Reimbursement Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Milk sold or donated under other commodity or food assistance programs administered by the United States Department of Agriculture is not eligible for reimbursement under the Milk Donation Reimbursement Program in this part.
