16 C.F.R. § 301.46 — Reference to guaranty by Government prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:16:301.46
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-301.46
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 301 (Rules And Regulations Under Fur Products Labeling Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No representation nor suggestion that a fur or fur product is guaranteed under the act by the Government, or any branch thereof, shall be made in the labeling, invoicing or advertising in connection therewith.
