46 U.S.C. § 7303 — Possession and description of merchant mariners’ documents
Citation: us:usc:46:7303
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section7303
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part E (Merchant Seamen Licenses, Certificates, And Documents); Chapter 73 (Merchant Mariners’ Documents). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A merchant mariner’s document shall be retained by the individual to whom issued. The document shall contain the signature, notations of nationality, age, and physical description, the photograph, and the home address of the individual. In addition, the document shall specify the rate or ratings in which the individual is qualified to serve.
