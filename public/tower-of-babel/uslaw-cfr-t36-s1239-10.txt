36 C.F.R. § 1239.10 — What program assistance does NARA provide?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1239.10
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1239.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1239 (Program Assistance And Inspections). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) NARA publishes handbooks, conducts workshops and other training sessions, and furnishes information and guidance to Federal agencies about the creation of records, their maintenance and use, and their disposition. NARA also may conduct a targeted assistance project in cooperation with an agency to address a serious records management issue in the agency.
(b) Information on NARA handbooks and guidance is available at http://www.archives.gov/records-mgmt/.
(c) Information on NARA training is available at http://www.archives.gov/records-mgmt/training/.
