The Drainless Circumferential Abdominoplasty: A Case Review and Technique Discussion of 59 Consecutive Patients
Authors: David R. Alfonso
Published: 2026

Intro: Drainless abdominoplasty has been well described in the Plastic Surgery Literature, however, there is scant data present regarding the drainless technique for extended or circumferential body contouring procedures. This presentation will review a single surgeon’s experience and technique for the application of this novel approach. Data from 59 consecutive patients over a two-year time period were analyzed to determine complication rates as well as outcomes.

Conclusion: The data reveal a low overall complication rate as well as high patient satisfaction with the procedure. The talk will also review surgical techniques as well as patient selection in order to educate surgeons on the potential applications.
