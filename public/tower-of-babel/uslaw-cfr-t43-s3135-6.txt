43 C.F.R. § 3135.6 — When will my suspension terminate?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3135.6
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3135.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3130 (Oil And Gas Leasing: National Petroleum Reserve, Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Your suspension terminates—
(1) On the first day of the month in which you begin to operate or produce on your lease with BLM approval; or
(2) The date BLM specifies in a written notice to you.
(b) You must notify BLM at least 24 hours before you begin operations or production under paragraph (a)(1) of this section.
