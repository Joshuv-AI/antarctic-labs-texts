14 U.S.C. § 2142 — Regular lieutenants (junior grade); separation for failure of selection for promotion
Citation: us:usc:14:2142
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2142
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 21 (Personnel; Officers); Subchapter II (Discharges; Retirements; Revocation Of Commissions; Separation For Cause). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each officer of the Regular Coast Guard appointed under section 2101 of this title who is serving in the grade of lieutenant (junior grade) and who has failed of selection for promotion to the grade of lieutenant for the second time, shall:
(1)
be honorably discharged on June 30 of the promotion year in which his second failure of selection occurs; or
(2)
if he so requests, be honorably discharged at an earlier date without loss of benefits that would accrue if he were discharged on that date under clause (1); or
(3)
if, on the date specified for his discharge in this section, he is eligible for retirement under any law, be retired on that date.
