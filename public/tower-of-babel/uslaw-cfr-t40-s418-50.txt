40 C.F.R. § 418.50 — Applicability; description of the nitric acid subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:418.50
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-418.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 418 (Fertilizer Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges resulting from production of nitric acid in concentrations up to 68 percent. Discharges from shipping losses are excluded.
