A cyclic scale-invariance universe with negative variable cosmological constant
Nasr Ahmed

Abstract
The AdS vacuum corresponds to a negative cosmological constant Λ \Lambda . While it is well-motivated theoretically, There has always been controversy over its actual existence. The AdS-dS transition is corresponding to a sign switching Λ \Lambda . We have proposed an ansatz for a negative time-varying cosmological constant Λ ⁡ ( H ) \Lambda(H) as a function of Hubble parameter H H , and used it in constructing a periodic model with no future singularity in a scale-invariance gravity. With the proposed Ansatz for negative Λ \Lambda , The model leads to a physically acceptable cosmic description, while we get unphysical parameters with the positive and zero values of Λ \Lambda . The model reveals a Quintom behavior with a sign flipping of cosmic pressure during each period. The non-conventional mechanism of negative Λ \Lambda that are expected to address the late-time acceleration has been revisited.

6
Conclusion

In the context of the scale-invariance modified gravity, we have presented a toy model in cyclic cosmology with negative time-varying cosmological constant and no future singularity. Physically acceptable results have been obtained only for negative
\Lambda
, while positive and vanishing
\Lambda
lead to unphysical features with dominant negative energy densities through each cycle. In order to solve the cosmological equations, we have suggested a new Ansatz for a negative time-dependent
\Lambda(H)
as a function of Hubble parameter
H
. The model exhibits a Quintom behavior with a sign flipping of cosmic pressure during each cycle. Both the kinetic term and scalar potential undergoes a sign change. The time evolution of the scalar and quantum potentials reveal AdS minima separated by a potential barrier with positive energy. The AdS and dS minima both exist in each cosmic cycle. Because the AdS vacuum corresponds to a negative
\Lambda
, the AdS-dS transition is corresponding to a sign switching
\Lambda
.
