29 C.F.R. § 2700.57 — Depositions.

Creator: Federal Mine Safety And Health Review Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-2700.57
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 2700 (Procedural Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Generally. Any party, without leave of the ALJ, may take the testimony of any person, including a party, by deposition upon oral examination or written interrogatories.
(b) Orders for deposition. If the parties are unable to agree, the time, place, and manner of taking depositions shall be governed by order of the ALJ.
