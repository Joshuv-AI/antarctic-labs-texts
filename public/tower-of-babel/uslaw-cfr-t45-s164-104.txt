45 C.F.R. § 164.104 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:45:164.104
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-164.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 164 (Security And Privacy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except as otherwise provided, the standards, requirements, and implementation specifications adopted under this part apply to the following entities:
(1) A health plan.
(2) A health care clearinghouse.
(3) A health care provider who transmits any health information in electronic form in connection with a transaction covered by this subchapter.
(b) Where provided, the standards, requirements, and implementation specifications adopted under this part apply to a business associate.
