40 C.F.R. § 240.205-1 — -1 Requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:40:240.205-1
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-240.205-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 240 (Guidelines For The Thermal Processing Of Solid Wastes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Emissions shall not exceed applicable existing emission standards established by the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (as published in parts 52, 60, 61 and 76 of this chapter) under the authority of the Clean Air Act, as amended, or State or local emission standards effective under that Act, if the latter are more stringent.
