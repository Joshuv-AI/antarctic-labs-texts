34 U.S.C. § 21907 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:34:21907
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section21907
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 219 (Ashanti Alert Communications Network). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is authorized to be appropriated to the Attorney General $3,000,000 to carry out the Ashanti Alert communications network as authorized under this chapter for each of fiscal years 2019 through 2022.
