27 C.F.R. § 22.64 — Return of permits.
Citation: us:cfr:27:22.64
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-22.64
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 22 (Distribution And Use Of Tax-Free Alcohol). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Following the termination, surrender or revocation of a permit, or the issuance of a new or amended permit, caused by a change, the permittee shall
(a) Obtain and destroy all photocopies of the previous permit from its suppliers, and
(b) Return the original of the permit or obsolete permit to the appropriate TTB officer for cancellation.
