20 U.S.C. § 50a — Gellatly art collection; estimates of sums needed for preservation and maintenance
Citation: us:usc:20:50a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section50a
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 3 (Smithsonian Institution, National Museums And Art Galleries); Subchapter I (Charter Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Smithsonian Institution is authorized to include in its estimates of appropriations such sums as may be needful for the preservation and maintenance of the John Gellatly art collection.
