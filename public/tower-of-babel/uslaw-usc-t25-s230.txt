25 U.S.C. § 230 — Depositions by agents touching depredations
Citation: us:usc:25:230
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section230
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 6 (Government Of Indian Country And Reservations); Subchapter I (Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The superintendents, agents, and subagents within their respective districts are authorized and empowered to take depositions of witnesses touching any depredations, within the purview of sections 227, 228 1 and 229 of this title, and to administer oaths to the deponents.
