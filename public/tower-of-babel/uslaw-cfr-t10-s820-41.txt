10 C.F.R. § 820.41 — Compliance order.
Citation: us:cfr:10:820.41
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-820.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 820 (Procedural Rules For Doe Nuclear Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary may issue to any person involved in a DOE nuclear activity a Compliance Order that:
(a) Identifies a situation that violates, potentially violates, or otherwise is inconsistent with the Act, a Nuclear Statute, or a DOE Nuclear Safety Requirement;
(b) Mandates a remedy or other action; and,
(c) States the reasons for the remedy or other action.
