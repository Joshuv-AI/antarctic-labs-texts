10 U.S.C. § 3306 — Encouragement of alternative dispute resolution
Citation: us:usc:10:3306
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section3306
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part V (Acquisition); Subpart C (Contracting Methods And Contract Types); Chapter 241 (Awarding Of Contracts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Federal Acquisition Regulation shall include a provision encouraging the use of alternative dispute resolution techniques to provide informal, expeditious, and inexpensive procedures for an offeror to consider using before filing a protest, prior to the award of a contract, of the exclusion of the offeror from the competitive range (or otherwise from further consideration) for that contract.
