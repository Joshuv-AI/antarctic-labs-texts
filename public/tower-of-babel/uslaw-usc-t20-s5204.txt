20 U.S.C. § 5204 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:20:5204
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section5204
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 63 (Eisenhower Exchange Fellowship Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To provide a permanent endowment for the Eisenhower Exchange Fellowship Program, there are authorized to be appropriated to the Eisenhower Exchange Fellowships Program Trust Fund—
(1)
$5,000,000; and
(2)
the lesser of—
(A)
$2,500,000, or
(B)
an amount equal to contributions to Eisenhower Exchange Fellowships, Incorporated, from private sector sources during the 4-year period beginning on October 24, 1990.
