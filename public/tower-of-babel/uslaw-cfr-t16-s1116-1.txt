16 C.F.R. § 1116.1 — Purpose.

Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1116.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1116 (Reports Submitted Pursuant To Section 37 Of The Consumer Product Safety Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part 1116 is to establish procedures for filing with the Consumer Product Safety Commission (“the Commission”) reports required by section 37 of the Consumer Product Safety Act (CPSA) (15 U.S.C. 2084) and to set forth the Commission's interpretation of the provisions of section 37.
