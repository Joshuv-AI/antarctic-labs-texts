33 C.F.R. § 96.450 — What happens if the Coast Guard disapproves an organization's request to be authorized?
Citation: us:cfr:33:96.450
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-96.450
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 96 (Rules For The Safe Operation Of Vessels And Safety Management Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Coast Guard will write to the organization explaining why it did not meet the criteria for authorization.
(b) The organization may then correct the deficiencies and reapply.
