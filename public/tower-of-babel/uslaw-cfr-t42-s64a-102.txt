42 C.F.R. § 64a.102 — a.102 To whom do these regulations apply?
Citation: us:cfr:42:64a.102
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-64a.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 64a (PART 64a—OBLIGATED SERVICE FOR MENTAL HEALTH TRAINEESHIPS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to any institution which receives a training grant under section 303 of the Public Health Service Act and to any individual who receives a stipend or other trainee allowances under such a grant for any period beginning on or after July 1, 1981, for clincial training in the field of psychology, psychiatry, nursing, or social work, except for training that is of a limited duration or experimental nature.
