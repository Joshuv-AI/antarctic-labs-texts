41 C.F.R. § 301-10.160 — -10.160 Use of extra-fare train service.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-10.160
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-10.160
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-10 (10—Transportation Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees may use extra-fare train service when the agency determines it is more advantageous to the Government or required for security reasons. Such use must be authorized or approved as other than coach class accommodations in accordance with § 301-10.100.
