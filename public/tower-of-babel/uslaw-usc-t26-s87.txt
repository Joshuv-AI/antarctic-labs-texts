26 U.S.C. § 87 — Alcohol and biodiesel fuels credits
Citation: us:usc:26:87
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section87
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 1 (Normal Taxes And Surtaxes); Subchapter B (Computation Of Taxable Income); Part II (Items Specifically Included In Gross Income). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Gross income includes—
(1)
the amount of the alcohol fuel credit determined with respect to the taxpayer for the taxable year under section 40(a),
(2)
the biodiesel fuels credit determined with respect to the taxpayer for the taxable year under section 40A(a), and
(3)
the sustainable aviation fuel credit determined with respect to the taxpayer for the taxable year under section 40B(a).
