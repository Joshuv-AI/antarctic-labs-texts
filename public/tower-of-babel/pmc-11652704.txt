Author Correction: Electrochemical measurement of morphine using a sensor fabricated from the CuS/g-C3N5/Ag nanocomposite
Authors: Hakimeh Teymourinia, Zakyeh Akram, Ali Ramazani, Vahid Amani
Published: 2024

Correction to: Scientific reports 10.1038/s41598-024-78585-y, published online 09 November 2024

In the original version of this Article, Ali Ramezani and Vahid Amani were omitted as corresponding authors. The correct corresponding authors for this Article are Hakimeh Teymourinia, Ali Ramezani and Vahid Amani.

In addition, Hakimeh Teymourinia was incorrectly affiliated with ‘Department of Chemistry, Faculty of Science, University of Zanjan, 45371-38791 Zanjan, Iran’. The correct affiliation is listed below.

Department of Chemistry Education, Farhangian University, P.O. Box 14665-889, Tehran, Iran.

Additionally, the grant number was omitted from the Funding section. The Funding section now reads:

“We would like to thank the Research and Technology Council of Farhangian University for financial support through this investigation under post-doctorate project No 50000/17340/600.”

The original Article has been corrected.
