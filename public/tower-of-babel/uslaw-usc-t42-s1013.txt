42 U.S.C. § 1013 — Appropriations

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 7 (Social Security); Subchapter VIII (Special Benefits For Certain World War Ii Veterans). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are hereby appropriated for fiscal year 2000 and subsequent fiscal years, out of any funds in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, such sums as may be necessary to carry out this subchapter.
