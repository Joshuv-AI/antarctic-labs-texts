Air Plan Approval; Reconsideration and Repeal of Air Plan Partial 
Approval and Partial Disapproval of North Dakota's Regional Haze State 
Implementation Plan for the Second Implementation Period; Correction

AGENCY: Environmental Protection Agency (EPA).

ACTION: Proposed rule; reconsideration of final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On September 9, 2026 the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency 
(EPA or Agency) proposed to repeal a final rule published in the 
Federal Register (FR) on December 2, 2024, partially approving and 
partially disapproving North Dakota's 2022 regional haze State 
Implementation Plan (SIP) submission for the second implementation 
period. The EPA proposed in the same action to approve the portions of 
North Dakota's 2022 SIP submission for the second implementation period 
that were disapproved in the EPA's 2024 partial approval/partial 
disapproval. The ADDRESSES section of the proposed rulemaking contained 
an incorrect Docket Identification Number (Docket ID No.) in the 
Federal Register. This document corrects an inadvertent typographical 
error in the Federal Register. This correction does not result in any 
substantive changes to the proposed rule.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The EPA is correcting the proposed 
rulemaking, Air Plan Approval; Reconsideration and Repeal of Air Plan 
Partial Approval and Partial Disapproval of North Dakota's Regional 
Haze State Implementation Plan for the Second Implementation Period, 
which was published in the Federal Register at 91 FR 57302, September 
9, 2026. Following the publication of this document, the EPA discovered 
there was an inadvertent typographical error in the docket number of 
the proposed rule.
    The EPA is correcting the following error published in the Federal 
Register Document Number (FR Doc.) 2026-18290, which does not change 
the proposed rule to repeal a final rule published in the Federal 
Register (FR) on December 2, 2024. The EPA is correcting the Docket ID 
No. in the document preamble ADDRESSES section to Docket ID No. EPA-
R08-OAR-2023-0495.

Correction

    In the Federal Register of September 9, 2026, in FR Doc. 2026-
18290, appearing at 91 FR 57302 on page 57303, in the first column, 
under ADDRESSES by correcting the Docket ID No. from ``Docket ID No. 
EPA-R08-OAR-2024-0001'' to ``Docket ID No. EPA-R08-OAR-2023-0495''.
