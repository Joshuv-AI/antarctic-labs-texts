33 C.F.R. § 187.5 — What are the purposes of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:33:187.5
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-187.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 187 (Vessel Identification System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purposes of this part are to—
(a) Establish minimum requirements for States electing to participate in VIS;
(b) Prescribe guidelines for State vessel titling systems; and
(c) Explain how to obtain certification of compliance with State guidelines for vessel titling systems for the purpose of conferring preferred status on mortgages, instruments, or agreements under 46 U.S.C. 31322(d).
