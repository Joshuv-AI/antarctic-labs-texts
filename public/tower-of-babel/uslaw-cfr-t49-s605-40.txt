49 C.F.R. § 605.40 — Reports and information.
Citation: us:cfr:49:605.40
Creator: Federal Transit Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-605.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 605 (School Bus Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator may order any recipient or operator for the recipient, to file special or separate reports setting forth information relating to any transportation service rendered by such recipient or operator, in addition to any other reports required by this part.
