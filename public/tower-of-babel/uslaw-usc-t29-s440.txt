29 U.S.C. § 440 — Civil action for enforcement by Secretary; jurisdiction
Citation: us:usc:29:440
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section440
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 11 (Labor-Management Reporting And Disclosure Procedure); Subchapter III (Reporting By Labor Organizations, Officers And Employees Of Labor Organizations, And Employers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whenever it shall appear that any person has violated or is about to violate any of the provisions of this subchapter, the Secretary may bring a civil action for such relief (including injunctions) as may be appropriate. Any such action may be brought in the district court of the United States where the violation occurred or, at the option of the parties, in the United States District Court for the District of Columbia.
