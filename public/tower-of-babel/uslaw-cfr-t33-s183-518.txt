33 C.F.R. § 183.518 — Fuel tank openings.
Citation: us:cfr:33:183.518
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-183.518
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 183 (Boats And Associated Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each opening into the fuel tank must be at or above the topmost surface of the tank.
