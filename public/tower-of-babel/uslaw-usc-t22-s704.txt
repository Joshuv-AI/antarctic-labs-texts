22 U.S.C. § 704 — Immunities of courts and witnesses
Citation: us:usc:22:704
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section704
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 13 (Service Courts Of Friendly Foreign Forces). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Members of any service court of a friendly foreign force lawfully exercising jurisdiction in the United States in relation to members of such force, and any witnesses appearing before such service court, shall enjoy the same immunities and privileges as are enjoyed by members of a court martial of the United States and by witnesses appearing before such a court martial.
