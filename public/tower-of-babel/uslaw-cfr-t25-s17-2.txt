25 C.F.R. § 17.2 — Attorneys.
Citation: us:cfr:25:17.2
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-17.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 17 (Action On Wills Of Osage Indians). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Interested parties may appear in person or by attorneys at law. Attorneys must file written authority to appear for their clients in the proceedings.
