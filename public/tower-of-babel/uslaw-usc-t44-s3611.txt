44 U.S.C. § 3611 — Independent assessment
Citation: us:usc:44:3611
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section3611
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 36 (Management And Promotion Of Electronic Government Services). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator may determine whether FedRAMP may use an independent assessment service to analyze, validate, and attest to the quality and compliance of security assessment materials provided by cloud service providers during the course of a determination of whether to use a cloud computing product or service.
