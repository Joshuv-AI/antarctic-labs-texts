7 C.F.R. § 1260.112 — Federation.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1260.112
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Miscellaneous Commodities), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1260.112
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1260 (Beef Promotion And Research). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Federation means the Beef Industry Council of the National Live Stock and Meat Board, or any successor organization to the Beef Industry Council, which includes as its State affiliates the qualified State beef councils.
