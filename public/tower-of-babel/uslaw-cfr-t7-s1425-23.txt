7 C.F.R. § 1425.23 — Reports.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1425.23
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1425.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1425 (Cooperative Marketing Associations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) CMA's must annually provide CCC a report of all commodity deliveries involved in loans and LDP's by FSA farm number for each member.
(b) When requested by CCC, CMA's must report market gains received on behalf of each member.
