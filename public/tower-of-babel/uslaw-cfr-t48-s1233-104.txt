48 C.F.R. § 1233.104 — 1233.104 Protests to GAO.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1233.104
Creator: Chapter 12—Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1233.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1233 (Protests, Disputes, And Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The protest process at the Government Accountability Office (GAO) may include ADR assistance by GAO. The contracting officer shall, with advice of counsel, explore the possibility of using ADR for all GAO protests.
