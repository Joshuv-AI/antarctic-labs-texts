25 C.F.R. § 292.24 — Can the public review the Secretarial Determination?
Citation: us:cfr:25:292.24
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-292.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 292 (Gaming On Trust Lands Acquired After October 17, 1988). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subject to restrictions on disclosure required by the Freedom of Information Act (5 U.S.C. 552), the Privacy Act (5 U.S.C. 552a), and the Trade Secrets Act (18 U.S.C. 1905), the Secretarial Determination and the supporting documents will be available for review at the local BIA agency or Regional Office having administrative jurisdiction over the land.
