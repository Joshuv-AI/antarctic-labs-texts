48 C.F.R. § 614.407-3 — 614.407-3 Other mistakes disclosed before award.
Citation: us:cfr:48:614.407-3
Creator: Chapter 6—Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-614.407-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 614 (Sealed Bidding). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The authority to make the determinations prescribed in FAR 14.407 is delegated, without power of redelegation, to the head of the contracting activity. In conformance with FAR 14.407-3(f), the head of the contracting activity shall obtain the concurrence of the Office of the Legal Adviser before making any determinations pursuant to this subsection.
