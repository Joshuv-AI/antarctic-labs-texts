7 C.F.R. § 656.1 — Purpose.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 656 (Procedures For The Protection Of Archeological And Historical Properties Encountered In Nrcs-Assisted Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes Natural Resources Conservation Service (NRCS) policy, procedures, and guidelines for the implementation of archeological and historical laws and appropriate executive orders for administering NRCS programs.
