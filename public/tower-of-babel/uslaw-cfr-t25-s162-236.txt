25 C.F.R. § 162.236 — How will a cash bond be administered?
Citation: us:cfr:25:162.236
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-162.236
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 162 (Leases And Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If a cash bond is submitted, we will retain the funds in an account established in the name of the tenant.
(b) We will not pay interest on a cash performance bond.
(c) If the bond is not forfeited under § 162.252(a) of this subpart, we will refund the bond to the tenant upon the expiration or termination of the lease.
