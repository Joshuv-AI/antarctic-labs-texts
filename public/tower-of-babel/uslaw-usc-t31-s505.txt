31 U.S.C. § 505 — Office of Information and Regulatory Affairs
Citation: us:usc:31:505
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section505
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 5 (Office Of Management And Budget); Subchapter I (Organization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Office of Information and Regulatory Affairs, established under section 3503 of title 44, is an office in the Office of Management and Budget.
