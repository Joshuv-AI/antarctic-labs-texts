31 C.F.R. § 589.301 — Arctic offshore projects.
Citation: us:cfr:31:589.301
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-589.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 589 (Ukraine-/Russia-Related Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The term Arctic offshore projects as used in § 589.205 means projects that have the potential to produce oil in areas that—
(1) Involve drilling operations originating offshore, and
(2) Are located above the Arctic Circle.
(b) The term Arctic offshore projects as used in § 589.205 does not apply to horizontal drilling operations originating onshore where such drilling operations extend under the seabed to areas above the Arctic Circle.
