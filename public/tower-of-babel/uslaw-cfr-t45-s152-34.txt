45 C.F.R. § 152.34 — Reallocation of funds.
Citation: us:cfr:45:152.34
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-152.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 152 (Pre-Existing Condition Insurance Plan Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If HHS determines, based on actual and projected enrollment and claims experience, that the PCIP in a given State will not make use of the total estimated funding allocated to that State, HHS may reallocate unused funds to other States, as needed.
