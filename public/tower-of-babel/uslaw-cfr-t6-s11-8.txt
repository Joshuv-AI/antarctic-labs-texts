6 C.F.R. § 11.8 — Suspension or revocation of eligibility for loans and loan guarantees, licenses, permits, or privileges.
Citation: us:cfr:6:11.8
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security, Office Of The Secretary
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-6/section-11.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 6 (Domestic Security), Part 11 (Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The authority to extend financial assistance in the form of a loan, loan guarantee, or loan insurance to any person delinquent on a nontax debt owed to DHS is delegated to the Chief Financial Officer.
