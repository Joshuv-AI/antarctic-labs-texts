20 C.F.R. § 726.103 — Application for authority to self-insure; effect of regulations contained in this part.
Citation: us:cfr:20:726.103
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-726.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 726 (Black Lung Benefits; Requirements For Coal Mine Operator'S Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As appropriate, each of the regulations, interpretations, and requirements contained in this part, including those described in subpart C of this part, are binding upon each applicant under this subpart, and the applicant's consent to be bound by all requirements of the regulations in this part are deemed to be included in and a part of the application, as fully as though written therein.
