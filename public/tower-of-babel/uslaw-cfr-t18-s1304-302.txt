18 C.F.R. § 1304.302 — Vegetation management on flowage easement shoreland.
Citation: us:cfr:18:1304.302
Creator: Tennessee Valley Authority
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-1304.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 1304 (Approval Of Construction In The Tennessee River System And Regulation Of Structures And Other Alterations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Removal, modification, or establishment of vegetation on privately-owned shoreland subject to a TVA flowage easement generally does not require approval by TVA. When reviewing proposals for docks or other obstructions on flowage easement shoreland, TVA shall consider the potential for impacts to sensitive plants or other resources and may establish conditions in its approval of a proposal to avoid or minimize such impacts consistent with applicable laws and executive orders.
