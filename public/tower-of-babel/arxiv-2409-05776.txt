Levi equation and local maximum property
Giuseppe Della Sala and Giuseppe Tomassini

Abstract
. The aim of the paper is to study the level sets of the solutions of Dirichlet problems for the Levi operator on strongly pseudoconvex domains Ω \Omega in ℂ 2 \mathbb{C}^{2} . Such solutions are generically non smooth, and the geometric properties of their level sets are characterized by means of hulls of their intersections with b ​ Ω b\Omega , using as main tool the local maximum property introduced in [ S1 ] . The same techniques are then employed to study the behavior of the complete Levi operator for graphs in ℂ 2 \mathbb{C}^{2} .
