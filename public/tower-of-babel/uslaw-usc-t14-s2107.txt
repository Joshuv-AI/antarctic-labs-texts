14 U.S.C. § 2107 — Selection boards; composition of boards
Citation: us:usc:14:2107
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2107
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 21 (Personnel; Officers); Subchapter I (Appointment And Promotion). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A board convened under section 2106 of this title shall consist of five or more officers on the active duty promotion list who are serving in or above the grade to which the board may recommend officers for promotion. No officer may be a member of two successive boards convened to consider officers of the same grade for promotion.
