36 C.F.R. § 228.46 — Application of other laws and regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:36:228.46
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-228.46
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 228 (Minerals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All mining operations for removal of mineral materials from National Forest lands must meet or exceed applicable Federal standards for the protection of public safety, health, and the environment, and must also meet or exceed State and local standards for the protection of public safety, health, and the environment, to the extent that such standards are not in conflict with Federal purposes and functions.
