7 C.F.R. § 810.1603 — Basis of determination.
Citation: us:cfr:7:810.1603
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Federal Grain Inspection Service, Fair Trade Practices Program), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-810.1603
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 810 (Official United States Standards For Grain). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each determination of class, heat-damaged kernels, damaged kernels, and splits is made on the basis of the grain when free from foreign material. Other determinations not specifically provided for under the general provisions are made on the basis of the grain as a whole.
