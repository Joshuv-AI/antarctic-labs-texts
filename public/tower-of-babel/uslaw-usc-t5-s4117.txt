5 U.S.C. § 4117 — Administration
Citation: us:usc:5:4117
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section4117
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart C (Employee Performance); Chapter 41 (Training). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Office of Personnel Management has the responsibility and authority for effective promotion and coordination of the training programs under this chapter and training operations thereunder. The functions, duties, and responsibilities of the Office under this chapter are subject to supervision and control by the President and review by Congress.
