30 U.S.C. § 957 — Promulgation of regulations
Citation: us:usc:30:957
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section957
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 22 (Mine Safety And Health); Subchapter V (Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary, the Secretary of Health and Human Services, the Commissioner of Social Security, and the Panel are authorized to issue such regulations as each deems appropriate to carry out any provision of this chapter.
