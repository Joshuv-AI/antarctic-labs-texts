33 C.F.R. § 173.25 — Location of certificate of number.
Citation: us:cfr:33:173.25
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-173.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 173 (Vessel Numbering And Casualty And Accident Reporting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person may operate a vessel to which this part applies unless the certificate or lease or rental agreement required by § 173.21 is carried on board in such a manner that it can be handed to a person authorized under § 173.23 to inspect it.
