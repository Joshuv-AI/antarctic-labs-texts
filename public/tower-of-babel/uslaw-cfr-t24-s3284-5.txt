24 C.F.R. § 3284.5 — Amount of fee.
Citation: us:cfr:24:3284.5
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-3284.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 3284 (Manufactured Housing Program Fee). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each manufacturer, as defined in § 3282.7 of this chapter, must pay a fee of $100 per transportable section of each manufactured housing unit that it manufactures under the requirements of part 3280 of this chapter.
