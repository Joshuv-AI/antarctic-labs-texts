36 C.F.R. § 13.962 — Does the Superintendent have other regulatory authority?
Citation: us:cfr:36:13.962
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-13.962
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 13 (National Park System Units In Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this subpart shall limit the authority of the superintendent to restrict or limit uses of an area under other statutory authority.
