30 C.F.R. § 885.11 — Who is eligible for a grant?
Citation: us:cfr:30:885.11
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-885.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 885 (Grants For Certified States And Indian Tribes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You are eligible for grants under this part if:
(a) You are a State or Indian tribe with a reclamation plan approved under part 884 of this chapter; and
(b) You have certified under § 875.13 of this chapter that all known coal problems in your State or on Indian lands in your jurisdiction have been addressed.
