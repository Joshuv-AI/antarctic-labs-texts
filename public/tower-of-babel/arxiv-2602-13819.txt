Funicular Preorders Can Be Prelinearized Without Nonprincipal Ultrafilters over ℕ \mathbb{N}
Azul Fatalini, Luke Serafin

Abstract
It is a consequence of the axiom of choice that every preorder can be extended to a total preorder while respecting the strict preorder relation. We call such an extension a prelinearization of the preorder and study the extent to which the axiom of choice is needed to construct prelinearizations. We isolate the class of funicular preorders, and show that these have prelinearizations in models of 𝖹𝖥 + 𝖣𝖢 \mathsf{ZF+DC} containing no nonprincipal ultrafilters over ω \omega . Funicular preorders include coordinatewise domination on ℝ ω \mathbb{R}^{\omega} , Turing reducibility, and various preorders arising in social choice theory. The relevant models are constructed first using tools from the geometric set theory of Larson and Zapletal, which requires an inaccessible cardinal, and then the inaccessible cardinal is eliminated using methods of amalgamation for Cohen reals.

We identify functions and relations with their graphs, and write

So if

The pointwise image of a set

For

6
Outlook

This work just begins the study of the strength of the existence of prelinearizations of Borel preorders as choice principles, and naturally many open questions remain.

Question 1
.

Let
X
be a Polish space.
For which analytic preorders
\mathrel{\mathchoice{\raisebox{6.35971pt}{\vbox{\halign{\hfil#\hfil\cr$\displaystyle\sqsubset$\cr\vskip 0.55554pt\cr\resizebox{618950}{}{$\displaystyle\sim$}\cr\vskip-1.11108pt\cr}}}}{\raisebox{6.35971pt}{\vbox{\halign{\hfil#\hfil\cr$\textstyle\sqsubset$\cr\vskip 0.55554pt\cr\resizebox{618950}{}{$\textstyle\sim$}\cr\vskip-1.11108pt\cr}}}}{\raisebox{4.79828pt}{\vbox{\halign{\hfil#\hfil\cr$\scriptstyle\sqsubset$\cr\vskip 0.55554pt\cr\resizebox{518825}{}{$\scriptstyle\sim$}\cr\vskip-1.11108pt\cr}}}}{\raisebox{3.59659pt}{\vbox{\halign{\hfil#\hfil\cr$\scriptscriptstyle\sqsubset$\cr\vskip 0.55554pt\cr\resizebox{464215}{}{$\scriptscriptstyle\sim$}\cr\vskip-1.11108pt\cr}}}}}
does the existence of a prelinearization imply the existence of a nonprincipal ultrafilter on
\omega
?
Or a Hamel basis for
\mathbb{R}
over
\mathbb{Q}
?
When does the existence of a prelinearization in a transitive extension
M
of
W
imply the existence of a set of ordinals in
M\setminus W
?

Question 2
.

Is there a combinatorial property about preorders that characterizes funicularity?

Question 3
.

For which Borel ideals
I
over
\omega
is the preorder
\leq_{I}
on
\mathbb{R}^{\omega}
defined for
x,y\in\mathbb{R}^{\omega}
by

x\leq_{I}y\Leftrightarrow\{n<\omega:x(n)>y(n)\}\in I,

funicular?
This is of interest in social choice theory, because such orders arise naturally in the analysis of strengthenings of the Pareto efficiency condition.
