34 C.F.R. § 86.402 — Who may be a party in a hearing under this subpart?
Citation: us:cfr:34:86.402
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-86.402
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 86 (Drug And Alcohol Abuse Prevention). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Only the designated Department official and the IHE that is the subject of the proposed termination or recovery of Federal financial assistance may be parties in a hearing under this subpart.
(b) Except as provided in this subpart, no person or organization other than a party may participate in a hearing under this subpart.
