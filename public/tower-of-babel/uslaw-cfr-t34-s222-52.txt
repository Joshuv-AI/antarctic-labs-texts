34 C.F.R. § 222.52 — What requirements must a local educational agency meet to receive a payment under section 8003(d)?
Citation: us:cfr:34:222.52
Creator: Office Of Elementary And Secondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-222.52
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 222 (Impact Aid Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To receive a payment under section 8003(d), an eligible LEA shall—
(a) State in its application the number of federally connected children with disabilities it claims for a payment under section 8003(d);
(b) Have in effect written IEPs or IFSPs for all federally connected children with disabilities it claims under section 8003(d); and
(c) Meet the requirements of subparts A and C of the regulations in this part.
