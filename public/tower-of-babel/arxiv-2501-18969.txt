B → D ( ∗ ) B\rightarrow D^{(*)} decays from N f = 2 + 1 + 1 N_{f}=2+1+1 highly improved staggered quarks and clover b b -quark in the Fermilab interpretation
Alejandro Vaquero

Abstract
We present an update on the analysis of semileptonic B → D ( ∗ ) B\rightarrow D^{(*)} decays at non-zero recoil. Our computation employs 2 + 1 + 1 2+1+1 FNAL-MILC ensembles with highly improved staggered quark (HISQ) action for sea and light valence quarks, while the bottom quark is treated using the clover action in the Fermilab interpretation. Simulations are performed across several lattice spacings, ranging approximately from ∼ 0.15 \sim 0.15 fm to ∼ 0.06 \sim 0.06 fm, and for various quark masses. We will present an overview of the analysis and show some preliminary results for the form factors.

3.1
2-point function analysis

The
D^{*}
and
B
-meson two-point functions are essential to extract the overlap factors and energy states, which serve as inputs for the ratio fits. The two-point functions are constructed using interpolating operators
\mathcal{O}_{Y_{a}}(\mathbf{p},t)
containing the quantum number of the meson of interest
Y=\{B,D^{*}\}
, where
a=\{d,1S\}
represents the type of smearing (point and Richardson
[
6
,
7
]
),
t
is the time, and
\mathbf{p}
is the spatial momentum.
Inserting a complete set of states between the operators in
\expectationvalue{\mathcal{O}_{Y_a}(\mathbf{p}, t)\mathcal{O}_{Y_b}^\dagger(\mathbf{p}, 0)}
yields the spectral decomposition, which can be used as a model for fits to data:

C_{Y_{a}\rightarrow Y_{b}}(\boldsymbol{p},t)=\sum_{n}\quantity({(-1)^{t-1}})^{n}\sqrt{\frac{Z_{a}^{(n)}(\boldsymbol{p})}{2E_{n}(\boldsymbol{p})}}\Bigl(e^{-E_{n}(\boldsymbol{p})t}+e^{-E_{n}(\boldsymbol{p})(N_{t}-t)}\Bigr)\sqrt{\frac{Z_{b}^{(n)}(\boldsymbol{p})}{2E_{n}(\boldsymbol{p})}}

(5)

where
N_{t}
is the temporal extent of the lattice,
Z^{(n)}

E_{n}
are, respectively, the overlap factor and the energy of the
n
th excited states, and the oscillating factor
(-1)^{t-1}
arises due to the presence of particles with the opposite parity in the staggered discretization for the fermions. Most of the correlators appear in four different configurations depending on the combination of source and sink smearing (
d-d

1S-d

d-1S

1S-1S

Correlators related to collinear momenta
(p_{x},0,0)

D^{(*)}

5
) to the data corresponding to different smearings and momentum polarizations.

3.1.1
Analysis of the systematics

To handle autocorrelations, correlator data were binned and analyzed using jackknife resampling, applying the same fitting routines to each bin. A fully correlated fit was performed by minimizing a
\chi^{2}

[
8
]
, also used in Ref.
[
3
]

The fitting hyper-parameters include the fitting window limits
[t_{\text{min}},t_{\text{max}}]

5

Conclusion

We presented preliminary results from the FNAL-MILC collaboration’s analysis of
B\to D^{(*)}\ell\nu
decays, focusing on form factor extraction.. Using two- and three-point correlation data from
N_{f}=2+1+1
MILC ensembles, we applied systematic uncertainty control and extracted the value of the ratios needed to compute the necessary form factors. The results show stable form factors and a good control over systematic effects. Future work includes continuum and physical mass extrapolations to finalize the analysis, advancing lattice QCD in semileptonic
B
decays and precision flavor physics.
