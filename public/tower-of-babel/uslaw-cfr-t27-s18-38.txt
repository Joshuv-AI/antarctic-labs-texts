27 C.F.R. § 18.38 — Permanent discontinuance.
Citation: us:cfr:27:18.38
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-18.38
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 18 (Production Of Volatile Fruit-Flavor Concentrate). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A proprietor who permanently discontinues the business of a concentrate manufacturer shall, after completion of operations, file an application on form TTB F 5520.3 to cover such discontinuance, giving the date of the discontinuance.
