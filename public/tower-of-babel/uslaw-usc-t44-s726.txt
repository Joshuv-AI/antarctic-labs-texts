44 U.S.C. § 726 — Printing for committees of Congress
Citation: us:usc:44:726
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section726
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 7 (Congressional Printing And Binding). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A committee of Congress may not procure the printing of more than one thousand copies of a hearing, or other document germane thereto, for its use except by simple, concurrent, or joint resolution, as provided by section 703 of this title.
