16 U.S.C. § 3642 — Disposition of property of International Pacific Salmon Fisheries Commission
Citation: us:usc:16:3642
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section3642
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 56A (Pacific Salmon Fishing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of State shall dispose of any United States property held by the International Pacific Salmon Fisheries Commission on the date of its termination in a manner which would further the purposes of this chapter.
