14 C.F.R. § 89.125 — Automatic Dependent Surveillance-Broadcast (ADS-B) Out prohibition.
Citation: us:cfr:14:89.125
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-89.125
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 89 (Remote Identification Of Unmanned Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Automatic Dependent Surveillance-Broadcast (ADS-B) Out equipment cannot be used to comply with the remote identification requirements of this part.
