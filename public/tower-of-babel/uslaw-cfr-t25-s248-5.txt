25 C.F.R. § 248.5 — Damage to Government-owned property.
Citation: us:cfr:25:248.5
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-248.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 248 (Use Of Columbia River Indian In-Lieu Fishing Sites). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Anyone committing any act of depredation, destruction, theft, or misuse of the land, buildings, fences, signs, or other structures which are the property of the United States shall be subject to prosecution under applicable Federal or State law.
