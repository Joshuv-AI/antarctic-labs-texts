49 C.F.R. § 213.301 — Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:49:213.301
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-213.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 213 (Track Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to all track used for the operation of trains at a speed greater than 90 m.p.h. for passenger equipment and greater than 80 m.p.h. for freight equipment.
