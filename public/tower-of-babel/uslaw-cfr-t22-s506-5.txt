22 C.F.R. § 506.5 — Review and evaluation.
Citation: us:cfr:22:506.5
Creator: United States Agency For Global Media
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-506.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 506 (Part-Time Career Employment Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The part-time career employment program will be reviewed through semiannual reports submitted by the Director, Office of Personnel to the Associate Director for Management. Regular employment reports will be used to determine levels of part-time employment.
