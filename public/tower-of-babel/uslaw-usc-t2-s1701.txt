2 U.S.C. § 1701 — National Recording Registry of the Library of Congress
Citation: us:usc:2:1701
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1701
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 27 (Sound Recording Preservation By The Library Of Congress); Subchapter I (National Recording Registry). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Librarian of Congress shall establish the National Recording Registry for the purpose of maintaining and preserving sound recordings that are culturally, historically, or aesthetically significant.
