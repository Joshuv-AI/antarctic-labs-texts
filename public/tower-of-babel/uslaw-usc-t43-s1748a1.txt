43 U.S.C. § 1748a–1 — Request for additional wildfire suppression funds
Citation: us:usc:43:1748a–1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section1748a–1
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 35 (Federal Land Policy And Management); Subchapter III (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If the amount provided for wildfire suppression operations for that fiscal year will be exhausted within 30 calendar days, the Secretary of the Interior or the Secretary of Agriculture (as applicable), in consultation with the Director of the Office of Management and Budget, shall promptly submit a request to Congress for supplemental appropriations.
