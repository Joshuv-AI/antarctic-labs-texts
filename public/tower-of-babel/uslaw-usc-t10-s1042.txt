10 U.S.C. § 1042 — Copy of certificate of service
Citation: us:usc:10:1042
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1042
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 53 (Miscellaneous Rights And Benefits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A fee for a copy of a certificate showing service in the armed forces may not be charged to—
(1)
a person discharged or released from the armed forces honorably or under honorable conditions;
(2)
the next of kin of the person; or
(3)
a legal representative of the person.
