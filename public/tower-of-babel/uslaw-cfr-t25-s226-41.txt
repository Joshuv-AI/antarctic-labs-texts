25 C.F.R. § 226.41 — Accidents to be reported.
Citation: us:cfr:25:226.41
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-226.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 226 (Leasing Of Osage Reservation Lands For Oil And Gas Mining). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Lessee shall make a complete report to the Superintendent of all accidents, fires, or acts of theft and vandalism occurring on the leased premises.
