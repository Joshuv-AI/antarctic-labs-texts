42 C.F.R. § 137.167 — What cost principles must a Self-Governance Tribe follow when participating in self-governance under Title V?
Citation: us:cfr:42:137.167
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-137.167
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 137 (Tribal Self-Governance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Self-Governance Tribe must apply the cost principles of the applicable OMB circular, except as modified by:
(a) Section 106 (k) of the Act [25 U.S.C. 450j-1],
(b) Other provisions of law, or
(c) Any exemptions to applicable OMB circulars subsequently granted by the OMB.
