33 C.F.R. § 83.16 — Action by give-way vessel (Rule 16).
Citation: us:cfr:33:83.16
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-83.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 83 (Navigation Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every vessel which is directed to keep out of the way of another vessel shall, so far as possible, take early and substantial action to keep well clear.
