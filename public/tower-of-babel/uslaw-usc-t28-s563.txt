28 U.S.C. § 563 — Oath of office
Citation: us:usc:28:563
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section563
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part II (Department Of Justice); Chapter 37 (United States Marshals Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director and each United States marshal and law enforcement officer of the Service, before taking office, shall take an oath or affirmation to faithfully execute the duties of that office.
