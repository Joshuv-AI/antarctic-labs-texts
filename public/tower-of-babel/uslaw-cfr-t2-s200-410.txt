2 C.F.R. § 200.410 — Collection of unallowable costs.
Citation: us:cfr:2:200.410
Creator: Office Of Management And Budget Guidance
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-200.410
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 200 (Uniform Administrative Requirements, Cost Principles, And Audit Requirements For Federal Awards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Payments made for costs determined to be unallowable by either the awarding Federal agency, cognizant agency for indirect costs, or pass-through entity must be refunded with interest to the Federal Government. Unless directed by Federal statute or regulation, repayments must be made in accordance with the instructions provided by the Federal agency or pass-through entity that made the allowability determination. See §§ 200.300 through 200.309, and § 200.346.
