14 C.F.R. § 71.41 — Class B airspace.
Citation: us:cfr:14:71.41
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-71.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 71 (Designation Of Class A, B, C, D, And E Airspace Areas; Air Traffic Service Routes; And Reporting Points). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Class B airspace areas listed in subpart B of FAA Order JO 7400.11M (incorporated by reference, see § 71.1) consist of specified airspace within which all aircraft operators are subject to the minimum pilot qualification requirements, operating rules, and aircraft equipment requirements of part 91 of this chapter. Each Class B airspace area designated for an airport in subpart B of FAA Order JO 7400.11M (incorporated by reference, see § 71.1) contains at least one primary airport around which the airspace is designated.
