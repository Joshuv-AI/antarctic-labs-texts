43 C.F.R. § 3603.21 — What reclamation requirements pertain to community pits and common use areas?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3603.21
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3603.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3600 (Mineral Materials Disposal). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Generally, you do not need to perform reclamation after extracting mineral materials from community pits or common use areas. However, you must pay a reclamation fee as provided in § 3603.22.
