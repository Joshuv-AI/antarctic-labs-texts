15 U.S.C. § 1661 — Catalogs and multiple-page advertisements
Citation: us:usc:15:1661
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1661
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 41 (Consumer Credit Protection); Subchapter I (Consumer Credit Cost Disclosure); Part C (Credit Advertising And Limits On Credit Card Fees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purposes of this part, a catalog or other multiple-page advertisement shall be considered a single advertisement if it clearly and conspicuously displays a credit terms table on which the information required to be stated under this part is clearly set forth.
