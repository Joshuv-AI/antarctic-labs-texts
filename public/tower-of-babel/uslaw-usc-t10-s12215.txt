10 U.S.C. § 12215 — Commissioned officers: reserve grade of adjutants general and assistant adjutants general
Citation: us:usc:10:12215
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section12215
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel Generally); Chapter 1205 (Appointment Of Reserve Officers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The adjutant general or an assistant adjutant general of the Army National Guard of a State may, upon being extended Federal recognition, be appointed as a reserve officer of the Army as of the date on which he is so recognized.
(b)
The adjutant general or an assistant adjutant general of the Air National Guard of a State may be appointed in the reserve commissioned grade in which Federal recognition in the Air National Guard is extended to him.
