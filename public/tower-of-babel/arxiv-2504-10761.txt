Mazur’s Growth Number Conjecture in the Rank One Case
Debanjana Kundu, Antonio Lei

Abstract
. Let p ≥ 5 p\geq 5 be a prime number. Let 𝖤 / ℚ \mathsf{E}/\mathbb{Q} be an elliptic curve with good ordinary reduction at p p . Let K K be an imaginary quadratic field where p p splits, and such that the generalized Heegner hypothesis holds. Under mild hypotheses, we show that if the p p -adic height of the Heegner point of 𝖤 \mathsf{E} over K K is non-zero, then Mazur’s conjecture on the growth of Selmer coranks in the ℤ p 2 \mathbb{Z}_{p}^{2} -extension of K K holds.

Let

The Mordell–Weil theorem asserts that when

However, when

For an elliptic curve

]

-extension

However, when

is an imaginary quadratic field, and

\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-extension of

[

.
In

is the

-extension of

Mazur’s Growth Number Conjecture
.

Suppose

is an elliptic curve,

\mathsf{E}

Let

Then for

\operatorname{corank}_{\mathbb{Z}_{p}}\mathrm{Sel}_{p^{\infty}}(\mathsf{E}/\mathcal{K}_{n})=cp^{n}+O(1),

c=\begin{cases}0&\textrm{ if }\mathcal{K}\neq K_{{\mathrm{ac}}}\text{ or }(\mathsf{E},K)\textrm{ has `sign' }+1\\
1&\textrm{ if }\mathcal{K}=K_{{\mathrm{ac}}},(\mathsf{E},K)\textrm{ has `sign' }-1\textrm{ and is `generic'}\\
2&\textrm{ if }\mathcal{K}=K_{{\mathrm{ac}}},(\mathsf{E},K)\textrm{ has `sign' }-1\textrm{ and is `exceptional'}.\end{cases}

A few remarks are in order regarding the conjecture.
The integer

The pair

On the other hand, the pair

The

•

The sign of the functional equation of

It is pertinent to note that the ‘sign’ of

Theorem 3.1

Proof.

Recall from (

Let

After multiplying by a non-zero scalar if necessary, we may assume that

or

We consider the two cases separately.

Case 1:

\Gamma_{a,b}=\frac{\overline{\langle\sigma,\tau\rangle}}{\overline{\langle\sigma^{a}\tau^{b}\rangle}}=\frac{\overline{\langle\sigma,\tau\rangle}}{\overline{\langle\sigma^{\frac{a}{b}}\tau\rangle}}=\overline{\langle\pi_{a,b}(\sigma)\rangle}

. In particular, the Iwasawa algebra

may be identified as the power series ring

. Furthermore,

Therefore, we have

L_{p}(X,Y)\equiv\frac{\partial L_{p}}{\partial Y}(X,0)[(1+X)^{-\frac{a}{b}}-1]+\frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{\partial^{2}L_{p}}{\partial Y^{2}}(X,0)[(1+X)^{-\frac{a}{b}}-1]^{2}+\ldots\pmod{f_{a,b}}

Set
X_{a,b}=\pi_{a,b}(X)
and
\Lambda_{a,b}=\mathbb{Z}_{p}\llbracket X_{a,b}\rrbracket
. Identifying
\mathcal{L}_{a,b}
with
L_{p}(X,Y)\pmod{f_{a,b}}
,
we have the following congruences of elements in

\mathcal{L}_{a,b}\equiv-\frac{a}{b}\cdot\frac{\partial L_{p}}{\partial Y}(X_{a,b},0)\cdot X_{a,b}\pmod{\langle X_{a,b}^{2}\rangle},

since
(1+X)^{-\frac{a}{b}}\equiv 1-\frac{a}{b}\cdot X\pmod{\langle X^{2}\rangle}
.
Taking derivative with respect to

gives

\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}_{a,b}}{\partial X_{a,b}}\Bigg|_{X_{a,b}=0}=-\frac{a}{b}\cdot\frac{\partial L_{p}}{\partial Y}(0,0).

Similar to the previous case, we have
\Lambda_{a,b}=\mathbb{Z}_{p}\llbracket Y_{a,b}\rrbracket
, where

and

We deduce analogously

L_{p}(X,Y)\equiv\frac{\partial L_{p}}{\partial Y}((1+Y)^{-\frac{b}{a}}-1,0)Y+\frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{\partial^{2}L_{p}}{\partial Y^{2}}((1+Y)^{-\frac{b}{a}}-1,0)Y^{2}+\ldots\pmod{f_{a,b}}.

In both cases, we can write

Recall from Theorem
2.5
that the following equality of
\mathbb{Q}_{p}\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}_{p}}\mathbb{Z}_{p}\llbracket X\rrbracket
