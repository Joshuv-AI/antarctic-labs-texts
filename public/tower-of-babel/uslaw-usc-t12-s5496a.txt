12 U.S.C. § 5496a — Annual audits

Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 53 (Wall Street Reform And Consumer Protection); Subchapter V (Bureau Of Consumer Financial Protection); Part A (Bureau Of Consumer Financial Protection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Annual independent audit
The Bureau shall order an annual independent audit of the operations and budget of the Bureau.
(b) Annual GAO audit
The Comptroller General of the United States shall conduct an annual audit of the Bureau’s financial statements in accordance with generally accepted government accounting standards.
