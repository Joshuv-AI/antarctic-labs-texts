28 U.S.C. § 2345 — Prehearing conference
Citation: us:usc:28:2345
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section2345
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part VI (Particular Proceedings); Chapter 158 (Orders Of Federal Agencies; Review). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The court of appeals may hold a prehearing conference or direct a judge of the court to hold a prehearing conference.
