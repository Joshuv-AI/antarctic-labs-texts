30 U.S.C. § 1294 — Penalty
Citation: us:usc:30:1294
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section1294
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 25 (Surface Mining Control And Reclamation); Subchapter VII (Administrative And Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person who shall, except as permitted by law, willfully resist, prevent, impede, or interfere with the Secretary or any of his agents in the performance of duties pursuant to this chapter shall be punished by a fine of not more than $5,000 or by imprisonment for not more than one year, or both.
