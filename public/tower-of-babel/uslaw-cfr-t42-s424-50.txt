42 C.F.R. § 424.50 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:42:424.50
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-424.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 424 (Conditions For Medicare Payment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) This subpart specifies to whom Medicare payment is ordinarily made for different kinds of services.
(b) Subpart E of this part sets forth provisions applicable in special situations.
(c) Subpart F of this part specifies the exceptional circumstances under which payment may be made to an assignee or reassignee.
