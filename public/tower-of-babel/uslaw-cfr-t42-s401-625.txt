42 C.F.R. § 401.625 — Effect of CMS claims collection decisions on appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:42:401.625
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-401.625
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 401 (General Administrative Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any action taken under this subpart regarding the compromise of a claim, or suspension or termination of collection action on a claim, is not an initial determination for purposes of CMS appeal procedures.
