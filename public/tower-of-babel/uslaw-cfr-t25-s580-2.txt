25 C.F.R. § 580.2 — When may the Commission waive its procedural rules governing appellate proceedings before the Commission?
Citation: us:cfr:25:580.2
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-580.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 580 (Rules Of General Application In Appeal Proceedings Before The Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The procedural provisions of parts 580 through 585 of this subchapter may be waived, in whole or in part, to promote the orderly conduct of business on motion to the Commission or on its own motion, if the ends of justice so require and if to do so does not substantially prejudice any party, except that the Commission may not extend the time for filing a notice of appeal.
