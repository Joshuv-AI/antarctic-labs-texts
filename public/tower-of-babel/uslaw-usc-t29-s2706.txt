29 U.S.C. § 2706 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:29:2706
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section2706
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 29 (Workers Technology Skill Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out this chapter such sums as may be necessary for each of the fiscal years 1995 through 1997.
(b) Availability
Amounts appropriated under subsection (a) shall remain available until expended.
