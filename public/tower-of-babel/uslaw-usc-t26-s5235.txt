26 U.S.C. § 5235 — Bottling of alcohol for industrial purposes
Citation: us:usc:26:5235
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section5235
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 51 (Distilled Spirits, Wines, And Beer); Subchapter C (Operation Of Distilled Spirits Plants); Part II (Operations On Bonded Premises); Subpart C (Storage). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Alcohol for industrial purposes may be bottled, labeled, and cased on bonded premises of a distilled spirits plant prior to payment or determination of tax, under such regulations as the Secretary may prescribe.
