ABSTRACT NUMBER: ESOC2026A592 INTRACRANIAL ARTERIAL CALCIFICATION ON COMPUTED TOMOGRAPHY AND RISK OF COGNITIVE IMPAIRMENT OR DEMENTIA: A SYSTEMATIC REVIEW AND META-ANALYSIS
Authors: Ke Li, Oluwaseemo Osomo, Peter Rothwell
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
AbstractBackground and aimsCoronary arterial calcification on CT (CT-CAC) predicts coronary events, but although intracranial arterial calcification on CT brain imaging (CT-IAC) is a frequent finding in older individuals, there is no consensus on its prognostic significance, particularly whether its presence, severity, or site predict cognitive impairment/dementia. We did a systematic review and meta-analysis to assess these associations.MethodsStudies published before 4-Dec-2025 were identified from bibliographic databases, reference lists, and forward or backward screening. Inclusion criteria were: (1) Studies of adults linking CT-IAC/CAC with cognitive impairment/dementia; (2) reporting adjusted effect measures with 95%CI/p-values (or calculable); (3) calcification assessed by CT/CT angiography and cognition by recognised tests or expert evaluation.ResultsSix cross-sectional studies and three longitudinal studies reported CT-IAC and cognition data. CT-IAC presence was weakly associated with cognitive impairment/dementia (three cross-sectional studies – adjusted OR=1.42,0.88-2.28,p=0.15; two longitudinal studies – aOR=1.51,1.03-2.22,p=0.033; all – aOR=1.48,1.10-1.99,p=0.01; Fig. 1). More severe vs. milder CT-IAC was more strongly associated with cognitive impairment/dementia (two cross-sectional studies – aOR=2.29,0.50-10.57,p=0.29; three longitudinal studies – aOR=1.84,1.28-2.65,p=0.001; all – aOR=1.74,1.28-2.36,p=0.0004; Fig. 2), including in two longitudinal cohorts in stroke/TIA patients (aOR=1.73,1.22-2.46,p=0.002). In two longitudinal studies, medial CT-IAC severity was a stronger predictor of dementia (aOR=2.35,1.29-4.28,p=0.005) than intimal CT-IAC (aOR=1.29,0.75-2.23,p=0.36). For CT-CAC, three longitudinal cohorts revealed weak associations with dementia (per SD CT-CAC – adjusted HR=1.15,1.02-1.30,p=0.025).ConclusionsPresence and severity of CT-IAC are both independently associated with dementia, with consistent estimates in cross-sectional and longitudinal studies, driven mainly by medial calcification.Conflict of interestDr Ke (Michael) Li is supported by the Clarendon and COSF Funds.
