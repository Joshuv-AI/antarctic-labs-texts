50 U.S.C. § 167g — Promulgation of rules and regulations
Citation: us:usc:50:167g
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section167g
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 10 (Helium Gas). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to establish and promulgate such rules and regulations, as are consistent with the directions of this chapter and are necessary to carry out the provisions hereof.
