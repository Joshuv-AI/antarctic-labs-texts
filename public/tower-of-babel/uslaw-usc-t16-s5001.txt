16 U.S.C. § 5001 — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:16:5001
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section5001
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 70 (North Pacific Anadromous Stocks Convention). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the purpose of this chapter to implement the Convention for the Conservation of Anadromous Stocks in the North Pacific Ocean, signed in Moscow, February 11, 1992.
