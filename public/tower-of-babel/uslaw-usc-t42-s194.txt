42 U.S.C. § 194 — Quarters for bureau
Citation: us:usc:42:194
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section194
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6 (The Children’S Bureau). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Health and Human Services is directed to furnish sufficient quarters for the work of this bureau at an annual rental not to exceed $2,000.
