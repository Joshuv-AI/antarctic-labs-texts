46 U.S.C. § 4101 — Application
Citation: us:usc:46:4101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section4101
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part B (Inspection And Regulation Of Vessels); Chapter 41 (Uninspected Vessels Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter applies to an uninspected vessel not subject to chapter 45 of this title—
(1)
on the navigable waters of the United States; or
(2)
owned in the United States and operating on the high seas.
