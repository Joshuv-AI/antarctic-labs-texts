37 C.F.R. § 501.10 — Dissemination of this part and of implementing regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:37:501.10
Creator: National Institute Of Standards And Technology, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-501.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 501 (Uniform Patent Policy For Rights In Inventions Made By Government Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each Government agency shall disseminate to its employees the provisions of this part, and any appropriate implementing agency regulations and delegations. Copies of any such regulations shall be sent to the Secretary. If the Secretary identifies an inconsistency between this part and the agency regulations or delegations, the agency, upon being informed by the Secretary of the inconsistency, shall take prompt action to correct it.
