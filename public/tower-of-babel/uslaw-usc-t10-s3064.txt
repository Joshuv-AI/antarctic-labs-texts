10 U.S.C. § 3064 — Applicability of chapter 137 legacy provisions

Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part V (Acquisition); Subpart A (General); Chapter 203 (General Matters). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) General Applicability.—
Any provision of this part that is a chapter 137 legacy provision applies to the procurement by any of the agencies named in section 3063 of this title, for its use or otherwise, of all property (other than land) and all services for which payment is to be made from appropriated funds.
(b) Applicability to Contracts for Installation or Alteration.—
The provisions of this part that are chapter 137 legacy provisions that apply to the procurement of property apply also to contracts for its installation or alteration.
