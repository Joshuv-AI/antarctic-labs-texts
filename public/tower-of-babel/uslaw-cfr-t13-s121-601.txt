13 C.F.R. § 121.601 — What is a small business for purposes of admission to SBA's 8(a) Business Development program?
Citation: us:cfr:13:121.601
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-121.601
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 121 (Small Business Size Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An applicant must not exceed the size standard corresponding to its primary industry classification in order to qualify for admission to SBA's 8(a) Business Development Program.
