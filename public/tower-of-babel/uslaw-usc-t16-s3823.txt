16 U.S.C. § 3823 — Affiliated persons
Citation: us:usc:16:3823
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section3823
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 58 (Erodible Land And Wetland Conservation And Reserve Program); Subchapter III (Wetland Conservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If a person is affected by a reduction in benefits under section 3821 of this title and the affected person is affiliated with other persons for the purpose of receiving the benefits, the benefits of each affiliated person shall be reduced under section 3821 of this title in proportion to the interest held by the affiliated person.
