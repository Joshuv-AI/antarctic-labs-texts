12 C.F.R. § 1265.2 — Mission of the Banks.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1265.2
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1265.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1265 (Core Mission Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The mission of the Banks is to provide to their members' and housing associates financial products and services, including but not limited to advances, that assist and enhance such members' and housing associates financing:
(a) Financing of housing, including single-family and multi-family housing serving consumers at all income levels; and
(b) Community lending.
