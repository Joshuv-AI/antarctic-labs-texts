23 C.F.R. § 771.131 — Emergency action procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:23:771.131
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-771.131
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 771 (Environmental Impact And Related Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Responses to some emergencies and disasters are categorically excluded under § 771.117 for FHWA, § 771.118 for FTA, or § 771.116 for FRA. Otherwise, requests for deviations from the procedures in this part because of emergency circumstances must be referred to the Administration's Headquarters for evaluation and decision after consultation with the CEQ.
