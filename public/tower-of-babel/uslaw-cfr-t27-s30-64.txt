27 C.F.R. § 30.64 — Table 4, showing the fractional part of a gallon per pound at each percent and each tenth percent of proof of spirituous liquor.
Citation: us:cfr:27:30.64
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-30.64
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 30 (Gauging Manual). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This table provides a method for use in ascertaining the wine gallon (at 60 degrees Fahrenheit) and/or proof gallon contents of containers of spirits by multiplying the net weight of the spirits by the fractional part of a gallon per pound shown in the table for spirits of the same proof. Fractional gallons beyond the first decimal will be dropped if less than 0.05 or will be added as 0.1 if 0.05 or more.
