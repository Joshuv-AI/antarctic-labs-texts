7 C.F.R. § 955.5 — Vidalia onion.
Citation: us:cfr:7:955.5
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-955.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 955 (Vidalia Onions Grown In Georgia). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Vidalia onion means all varieties of Allium cepa of the hybrid yellow granex, granex parentage or any other similar variety recommended by the committee and approved by the Secretary, that are grown in the production area.
