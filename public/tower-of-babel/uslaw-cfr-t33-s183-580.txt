33 C.F.R. § 183.580 — Static pressure test for fuel tanks.
Citation: us:cfr:33:183.580
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-183.580
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 183 (Boats And Associated Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A fuel tank is tested by performing the following procedures in the following order:
(a) Fill the tank with air or inert gas to the pressure marked on the tank label under § 183.514(b)(5).
(b) Examine each tank fitting and seam for leaks using a leak detection method other than the pressure drop method.
