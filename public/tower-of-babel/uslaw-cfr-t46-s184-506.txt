46 C.F.R. § 184.506 — Emergency broadcast placard.
Citation: us:cfr:46:184.506
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-184.506
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 184 (Vessel Control And Miscellaneous Systems And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A durable placard must be posted next to all radiotelephone installations with the emergency broadcast instructions and information, specific to the individual vessel.
