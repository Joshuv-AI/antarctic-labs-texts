45 C.F.R. § 2400.56 — Payment of stipend.
Citation: us:cfr:45:2400.56
Creator: James Madison Memorial Fellowship Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2400.56
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2400 (Fellowship Program Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Payment for tuition, required fees, books, room, and board subject to the limitations in §§ 2400.52 through 2400.55 and §§ 2400.59 through 2400.60 will be paid via Electronic Funds Transfer to each Fellow at the beginning of each term of enrollment and upon the Fellow's submission of a completed Payment Request Form which includes the current University bulletin of cost information.
