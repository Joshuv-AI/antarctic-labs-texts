33 U.S.C. § 701c–2 — Acquisition and sale of land
Citation: us:usc:33:701c–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section701c–2
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 15 (Flood Control). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of sections 593 to 595 of this title relating to river and harbor improvements are made applicable to works of flood control heretofore or hereafter authorized.
