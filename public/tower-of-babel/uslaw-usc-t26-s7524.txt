26 U.S.C. § 7524 — Annual notice of tax delinquency
Citation: us:usc:26:7524
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section7524
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 77 (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not less often than annually, the Secretary shall send a written notice to each taxpayer who has a tax delinquent account of the amount of the tax delinquency as of the date of the notice.
