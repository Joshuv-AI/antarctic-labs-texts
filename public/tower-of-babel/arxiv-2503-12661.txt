Extendability of general non-prime K ​ 3 K3 surfaces without Gaussian maps
Purnaprajna Bangere, Jayan Mukherjee

Abstract
. In [ BM24 ] , we introduced a new approach to the question of extendability of projective varieties via degeneration to ribbons. In this article we build on these methods to recover optimal results on the extendability of general non-prime K ​ 3 K3 surfaces, classification of non-prime Mukai varieties and irreducibility of their Hilbert schemes. This involves degenerations of K ​ 3 K3 surfaces to ribbons on embedded Hirzebruch surfaces, called K ​ 3 K3 carpets. We directly give optimal upper bounds to the cohomology of the twisted normal bundle of the K ​ 3 K3 carpets instead of computing coranks of Gaussian maps of the canonical curve sections as in [ CLM94 ] , [ CLM98 ] ). As a result of independent interest, we show such K ​ 3 K3 carpets also appear as degenerations of smoothable simple normal crossings of two Hirzebruch surfaces embedded by arbitrary linear series intersecting along an anticanonical elliptic curve. Such type II degenerations constitute a smooth locus of codimension 6 6 in the Hilbert scheme of K ​ 3 K3 surfaces.

To see the statement on the irreducibility of fibers of

5.
Degeneration of
K3
surfaces to union of Hirzebruch surfaces embedded by arbitrary linear series

In this section we show how
K3
carpets arise as degenerations of snc union of embedded Hirzebruch surfaces intersecting along an anticanonical elliptic curve. This relates our degeneration to that of
[
CLM93
]
. In
[
CLM93
]
, the authors degenerate a prime
K3
surface into a union of scrolls meeting along an elliptic curve and further degenerate such a union into a union of planes whose hyperplane sections are graph curves with corank one Gaussian maps. Similarly, one can think of our degeneration as a degeneration into a union of Hirzebruch surfaces embedded as non-scrolls, but instead of further degenerating to a union of planes, we degenerate into
K3
carpets. To fix some notations, we start with a remark.

Remark 5.1
.

Let
i:Y\hookrightarrow\mathbb{P}^{M}
be an embedding of a smooth variety inside a projective space. Let
D\in|L^{-1}|
be a smooth divisor. Then there is an induced embedding of
j:D\hookrightarrow\mathbb{P}^{M}
. Let
p_{1}
and
p_{2}
denote the Hilbert polynomials of
Y\hookrightarrow\mathbb{P}^{M}
and
D\hookrightarrow\mathbb{P}^{M}
respectively. Let
\mathcal{H}(p_{1},p_{2})
denote the flag Hilbert scheme representing the flag-Hilbert functor as defined in
[
Ser06
]
, Section
4.5.1
or
[
Kle81
]
. Closed points of
\mathcal{H}(p_{1},p_{2})
parameterize pairs
(D^{\prime}\hookrightarrow Y^{\prime}\hookrightarrow\mathbb{P}^{M})
where the subschemes
Y^{\prime}\hookrightarrow\mathbb{P}^{M}
and
D^{\prime}\hookrightarrow\mathbb{P}^{M}
have Hilbert polynomials
p_{1}
and
p_{2}
respectively. Let
\mathcal{D}
denote the Hilbert scheme parameterizing subschemes
(D^{\prime}\hookrightarrow\mathbb{P}^{M})
with Hilbert polynomial
p_{2}
. There is a projection map
p:\mathcal{H}(p_{1},p_{2})\to\mathcal{D}
whose fibre
\mathcal{F}_{D}
at a subscheme
(D\hookrightarrow\mathbb{P}^{M})
consists of all those subschemes
Y^{\prime}\hookrightarrow\mathbb{P}^{N}

p_{1}

denote the union of irreducible components of

correspond to deformations over an irreducible curve of the subvariety

inside
\mathbb{P}^{M}
which keeps

to a smooth
