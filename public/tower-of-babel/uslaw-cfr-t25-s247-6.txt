25 C.F.R. § 247.6 — What will happen if I damage Government-owned property?
Citation: us:cfr:25:247.6
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-247.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 247 (Use Of Columbia River Treaty Fishing Access Sites). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you commit any act of vandalism, depredation, destruction, theft, or misuse of the land, buildings, fences, signs, or other structures that are the property of the United States you will be subject to prosecution under applicable Federal or State law.
