22 U.S.C. § 2683 — Assumption of obligations of operation of broadcasting facilities
Citation: us:usc:22:2683
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2683
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 38 (Department Of State). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Department of State shall assume all obligations of the Reconstruction Finance Corporation covering operations of said facilities, equipment, and appurtenant property outstanding at the date of transfer.
