43 C.F.R. § 3715.9 — What appeal rights do I have?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3715.9
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3715.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3710 (Public Law 167; Act Of July 23, 1955). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you are adversely affected by a BLM decision, order or determination made under this subpart, you may appeal the decision, order or determination to the Interior Board of Land Appeals (IBLA) under the provisions of 43 CFR part 4.
