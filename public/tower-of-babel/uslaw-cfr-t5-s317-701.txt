5 C.F.R. § 317.701 — Agency authority.
Citation: us:cfr:5:317.701
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-317.701
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 317 (Employment In The Senior Executive Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As provided for in §§ 317.702 and 317.703, an agency may reinstate a former SES career appointee without regard to the merit staffing requirements established by OPM in § 317.501(c).
