29 C.F.R. § 1400.735-61 — -61 Notice to and appeal of employee.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1400.735-61
Creator: Federal Mediation And Conciliation Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1400.735-61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1400 (Standards Of Conduct, Responsibilities, And Discipline). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director of Administrative Management will prepare charges and institute proceedings, which in all cases will be in accordance with Civil Service procedures for disciplinary actions against status employees. Such proceedings will include notification to the employee of his appeal rights.
