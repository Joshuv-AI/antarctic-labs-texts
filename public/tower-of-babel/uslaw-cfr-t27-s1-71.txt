27 C.F.R. § 1.71 — Distilled spirits in containers of a capacity of one gallon or less.
Citation: us:cfr:27:1.71
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-1.71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 1 (Basic Permit Requirements Under The Federal Alcohol Administration Act, Nonindustrial Use Of Distilled Spirits And Wine, Bulk Sales And Bottling Of Distilled Spirits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Distilled spirits in containers of a capacity of one wine gallon or less, except anhydrous alcohol and alcohol which may be withdrawn free of tax under the internal revenue laws, will be deemed to be for nonindustrial use.
