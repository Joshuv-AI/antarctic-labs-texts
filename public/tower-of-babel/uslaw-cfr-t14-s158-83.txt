14 C.F.R. § 158.83 — Informal resolution.
Citation: us:cfr:14:158.83
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-158.83
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 158 (Passenger Facility Charges (Pfc'S)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator shall undertake informal resolution with the public agency or any other affected party if, after review under § 158.71, the Administrator cannot determine that PFC revenue is being used for the approved projects in accordance with the terms of the Administrator's approval to impose a PFC for those projects or with 49 U.S.C. 40117.
