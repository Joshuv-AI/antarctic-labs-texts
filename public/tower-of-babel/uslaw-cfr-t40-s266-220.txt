40 C.F.R. § 266.220 — What does a storage and treatment conditional exemption do?
Citation: us:cfr:40:266.220
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-266.220
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 266 (Standards For The Management Of Specific Hazardous Wastes And Specific Types Of Hazardous Waste Management Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The storage and treatment conditional exemption exempts your low-level mixed waste from the regulatory definition of hazardous waste in 40 CFR 261.3 if your waste meets the eligibility criteria in § 266.225 and you meet the conditions in § 266.230.
