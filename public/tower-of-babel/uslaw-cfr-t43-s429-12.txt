43 C.F.R. § 429.12 — Where do I file my application?
Citation: us:cfr:43:429.12
Creator: Bureau Of Reclamation, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-429.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 429 (Use Of Bureau Of Reclamation Land, Facilities, And Waterbodies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

File your completed and signed application, including the $100 nonrefundable application fee, with the Reclamation office having jurisdiction over the land, facility, or waterbody associated with your request. Reclamation office locations may be found on http://www.usbr.gov, the official Reclamation Internet Web site.
