22 C.F.R. § 143.38 — Remedial action by recipient.
Citation: us:cfr:22:143.38
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-143.38
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 143 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Age In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where the agency head finds a recipient has discriminated on the basis of age, the recipient shall take any remedial action that the agency head may require to overcome the effects of the discrimination. If another recipient exercises control over the recipient that has discriminated, the agency head may require both recipients to take remedial action.
