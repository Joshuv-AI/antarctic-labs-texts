33 C.F.R. § 52.28 — Stay of proceedings.
Citation: us:cfr:33:52.28
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-52.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 52 (Board For Correction Of Military Records Of The Coast Guard). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An application to the Board for correction of a military record does not operate as a stay of any proceeding or administrative action taken with respect to or affecting the applicant.
