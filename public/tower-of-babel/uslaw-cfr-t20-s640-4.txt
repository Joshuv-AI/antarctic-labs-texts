20 C.F.R. § 640.4 — Standard for conformity.
Citation: us:cfr:20:640.4
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-640.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 640 (Standard For Benefit Payment Promptness—Unemployment Compensation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A State law will satisfy the requirement of section 303(a)(1), if it contains a provision requiring, or which is construed to require, such methods of administration as will reasonably insure the full payment of unemployment benefits to eligible claimants with the greatest promptness that is administratively feasible.
