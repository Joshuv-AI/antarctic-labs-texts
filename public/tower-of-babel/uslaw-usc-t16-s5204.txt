16 U.S.C. § 5204 — Relationship to State and local law and civil actions
Citation: us:usc:16:5204
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section5204
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 72 (Recreational Hunting Safety). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter does not preempt a State law or local ordinance that provides for civil or criminal penalties for conduct that violates this chapter.
