10 U.S.C. § 12318 — Reserves on active duty: duties; funding
Citation: us:usc:10:12318
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section12318
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel Generally); Chapter 1209 (Active Duty). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
During a period that members of a reserve component are serving on active duty pursuant to an order under section 12302 or 12304 of this title, members of reserve components serving on active duty may perform duties in connection with either such section.
(b)
Funds available for the pay and allowances of Reserves referred to section 12310 of this title shall be available for the pay and allowances of such Reserves who perform duties in connection with section 12302 or 12304 of this title under the authority of subsection (a).
