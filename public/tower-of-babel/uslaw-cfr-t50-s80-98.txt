50 C.F.R. § 80.98 — How must a State fish and wildlife agency include barter in an award and report barter transactions?

(a) A State fish and wildlife agency must identify when barter exchanges are anticipated in the project when applying for, or carrying out, an award. All activities included in a barter transaction are subject to Federal compliance requirements under an award.
