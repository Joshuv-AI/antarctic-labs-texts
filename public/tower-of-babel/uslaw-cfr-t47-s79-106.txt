47 C.F.R. § 79.106 — Audio description and emergency information accessibility requirements for recording devices.
Citation: us:cfr:47:79.106
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-79.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 79 (Accessibility Of Video Programming). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Effective May 26, 2015, all apparatus that is designed to record video programming transmitted simultaneously with sound that is provided by entities subject to §§ 79.2 and 79.3 and is manufactured in the United States or imported for use in the United States, must comply with the provisions of this section except that apparatus must only do so if it is achievable as defined in § 79.105(b)(3).
(b) All apparatus subject to this section must enable the presentation or the pass through of the secondary audio stream, which will facilitate the provision of audio description signals and emergency information (as that term is defined in § 79.2) such that viewers are able to activate and de-activate the audio description as the video programming is played back on a picture screen of any size.
(c) All apparatus subject to this section must comply with the interconnection mechanism requirements in § 79.105(c).
