46 C.F.R. § 109.103 — Requirements of the International Convention for Safety of Life at Sea, 1974.
Citation: us:cfr:46:109.103
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-109.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 109 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No self-propelled unit of more than 500 gross tons may embark on an international voyage unless it is issued the appropriate Convention certificate as described in §§ 107.401 through 107.413 of this subchapter.
