13 C.F.R. § 123.17 — Do other Federal requirements apply?
Citation: us:cfr:13:123.17
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-123.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 123 (Disaster Loan Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As a condition of disbursement, you must be in compliance with certain requirements relating to flood insurance, earthquake hazards, coastal barrier islands, and child support obligations, as set forth in §§ 120.170 through 120.172, 120.174 and 120.175 of this chapter.
