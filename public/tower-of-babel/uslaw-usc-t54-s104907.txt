54 U.S.C. § 104907 — Limitation on extension or establishment of national parks in Wyoming
Citation: us:usc:54:104907
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section104907
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 1049 (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No extension or establishment of national parks in Wyoming may be undertaken except by express authorization of Congress.
