39 C.F.R. § 3010.331 — Proposed findings and conclusions.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3010.331
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3010.331
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3010 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission or the presiding officer may direct the filing of proposed findings and conclusions with a brief statement of the supporting reasons for each proposed finding and conclusion.
