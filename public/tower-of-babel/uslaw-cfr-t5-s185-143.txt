5 C.F.R. § 185.143 — Collection of civil penalties and assessments.
Citation: us:cfr:5:185.143
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-185.143
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 185 (Program Fraud Civil Remedies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Sections 3806 and 3808(b) of title 31, United States Code, authorize actions for collection of civil penalties and assessments imposed under this part and specify the procedures for such actions.
