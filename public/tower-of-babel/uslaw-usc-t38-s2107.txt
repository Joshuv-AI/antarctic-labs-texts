38 U.S.C. § 2107 — Coordination of administration of benefits
Citation: us:usc:38:2107
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section2107
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 21 (Specially Adapted Housing For Disabled Veterans). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall provide for the coordination of the administration of programs to provide specially adapted housing that are administered by the Under Secretary for Health and such programs that are administered by the Under Secretary for Benefits under this chapter, chapter 17, and chapter 31 of this title.
