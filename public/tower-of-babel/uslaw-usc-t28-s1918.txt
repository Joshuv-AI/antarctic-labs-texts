28 U.S.C. § 1918 — District courts; fines, forfeitures and criminal proceedings
Citation: us:usc:28:1918
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1918
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part V (Procedure); Chapter 123 (Fees And Costs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Costs shall be included in any judgment, order, or decree rendered against any person for the violation of an Act of Congress in which a civil fine or forfeiture of property is provided for.
(b)
Whenever any conviction for any offense not capital is obtained in a district court, the court may order that the defendant pay the costs of prosecution.
