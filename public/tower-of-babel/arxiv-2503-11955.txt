A generalization of Zwegers’ multivariable μ \mu -function
G. Shibukawa, S. Tsuchimi

Abstract
We introduce a one parameter deformation of Zwegers’ multivariable μ \mu -function by applying iterations of the q q -Borel summation method, which is also a multivariate analogue of the generalized μ \mu -function introduced by the authors. For this deformed multivariable μ \mu -function, we give some formulas, for example, forward shift formula, translation and 𝔖 N + 1 \mathfrak{S}_{N+1} -symmetry. Further we mention modular formulas for the Zwegers’ original multivariable μ \mu -function.

The
q
-Borel summation method is one of the techniques to construct convergent series solutions from divergent series solutions of
q
-difference equations.
Namely, for a formal series
g(x)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}a_{n}x^{n}
, we define the
q
-Borel transformation
\mathcal{B}_{q}
and the
q
-Laplace transformation

\displaystyle\mathcal{B}_{q}(g)(\xi):=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}a_{n}q^{\frac{n(n-1)}{2}}\xi^{n},\quad\mathcal{L}_{q}(g)(x;\lambda):=\sum_{n\in\mathbb{Z}}\frac{g(\lambda q^{n})}{\theta_{q}(\lambda q^{n}/x)}.

(1.24)

The
q
-Laplace transformation has the following Jackson integral expression:

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{q}(g)(x,\lambda)=\frac{1}{1-q}\int_{0}^{\lambda\infty}\frac{g(t)}{\theta_{q}(t/x)}\ dt_{q},\quad\int_{0}^{\lambda\infty}f(t)\ dt_{q}:=(1-q)\sum_{n\in\mathbb{Z}}^{\infty}f(\lambda q^{n}).

Hence, the extra parameter
\lambda
of
\mathcal{L}_{q}
seems to be the argument parameter of the Jackson integral path.

From a simple calculation, for
n\in\mathbb{Z}
and
\varphi_{n}(x):=x^{n}
, we have

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{q}\circ\mathcal{B}_{q}(\varphi_{n})(x,\lambda)=x^{n}.

(1.25)

We remark that the extra parameter
\lambda
of
\mathcal{L}_{q}
does not appear in the right hand side of (
1.25
).
Then, we see that the image of the composition of the
q
-Borel and
q
-Laplace transformations of a convergent series solution of a
q
-difference equation (operator)

is equal to the original convergent series solution.

Also, for a divergent series solution

of

, by the following operator method

\displaystyle\mathcal{B}_{q}(x^{m}T_{x}^{n}[g])(\xi)

\displaystyle=q^{\frac{m(m-1)}{2}}\xi^{m}T_{\xi}^{m+n}\mathcal{B}_{q}([g])(\xi),

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{q}(\xi^{m}T_{\xi}^{n}[g])(x,\lambda)

\displaystyle=q^{-\frac{m(m-1)}{2}}x^{m}T_{x}^{n-m}\mathcal{L}_{q}([g])(x,\lambda),

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{q}\circ\mathcal{B}_{q}(x^{m}T_{x}^{n}[g])(x,\lambda)

\displaystyle=x^{m}T_{x}^{n}[\mathcal{L}_{q}\circ\mathcal{B}_{q}(g)(x,\lambda)],
