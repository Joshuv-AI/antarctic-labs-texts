24 C.F.R. § 93.355 — Eminent domain.
Citation: us:cfr:24:93.355
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-93.355
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 93 (Housing Trust Fund). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No HTF funds may be used in conjunction with property taken by eminent domain, unless eminent domain is employed only for a public use, except that, public use shall not be construed to include economic development that primarily benefits any private entity.
