10 U.S.C. § 9413 — United States Air Force Institute of Technology: establishment
Citation: us:usc:10:9413
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9413
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training); Chapter 951 (Training Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is in the Department of the Air Force a United States Air Force Institute of Technology, the purposes of which are to perform research and to provide, to members of the Air Force and Space Force (including the reserve components) and civilian employees of such Department, advanced instruction and technical education regarding their duties.
