7 C.F.R. § 3400.13 — Availability of information.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3400.13
Creator: National Institute Of Food And Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3400.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3400 (Special Research Grants Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Information regarding the peer review process will be made available to the extent permitted under the Freedom of Information Act (5 U.S.C. 552), the Privacy Act (5 U.S.C. 552a), and implementing Departmental regulations (part 1 of this title).
