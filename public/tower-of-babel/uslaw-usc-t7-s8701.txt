7 U.S.C. § 8701 — Definition of Secretary
Citation: us:usc:7:8701
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section8701
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 113 (Agricultural Commodity Support Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this Act, the term “Secretary” means the Secretary of Agriculture.
