7 C.F.R. § 1410.57 — Payments not subject to claims.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1410.57
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1410.57
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1410 (Conservation Reserve Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subject to part 3 of this title, any payment or portion thereof due any person under this part will be allowed without regard to questions of title under State law, and without regard to any claim or lien in favor of any creditor, except agencies of the United States Government.
