29 U.S.C. § 796f–5 — “Eligible agency” defined
Citation: us:usc:29:796f–5
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section796f–5
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 16 (Vocational Rehabilitation And Other Rehabilitation Services); Subchapter VII (Independent Living Services And Centers For Independent Living); Part A (Individuals With Significant Disabilities); Subpart 3 (Centers For Independent Living). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this subpart, the term “eligible agency” means a consumer-controlled, community-based, cross-disability, nonresidential private nonprofit agency.
