2 C.F.R. § 1402.103 — What other policies or procedures must non-Federal entities follow?
Citation: us:cfr:2:1402.103
Creator: Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1402.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1402 (Financial Assistance Interior Regulation, Supplementing The Uniform Administrative Requirements, Cost Principles, And Audit Requirements For Federal Awards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Non-Federal entities must follow bureau or office policies and procedures as communicated in notices of funding opportunity (NOFOs) and award terms and conditions. In the event such policies or procedures conflict with 2 CFR part 200 or this part, 2 CFR part 200 or this part will supersede, unless otherwise authorized by Federal statute.
