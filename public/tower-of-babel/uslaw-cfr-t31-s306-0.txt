31 C.F.R. § 306.0 — Applicability of regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:31:306.0
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-306.0
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 306 (General Regulations Governing U.S. Securities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These regulations apply to all U.S. transferable and nontransferable securities, 1 other than U.S. Savings Bonds and U.S. Savings Notes, to the extent specified in these regulations, the offering circulars or special regulations governing such securities.
