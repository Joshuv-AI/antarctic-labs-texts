25 C.F.R. § 170.4 — How does this part affect existing Tribal rights?
Citation: us:cfr:25:170.4
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-170.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 170 (Tribal Transportation Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part does not:
(a) Affect Tribes' sovereign immunity from suit;
(b) Terminate or reduce the trust responsibility of the United States to Tribes or individual Indians;
(c) Require a Tribe to assume a program relating to the TTP; or
(d) Impede awards by other agencies of the United States or a State to Tribes to administer programs under any other law.
