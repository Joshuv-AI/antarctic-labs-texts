28 C.F.R. § 345.64 — Referral of releasable medical data to FPI staff.
Citation: us:cfr:28:345.64
Creator: Federal Prison Industries, Inc., Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-345.64
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 345 (Federal Prison Industries (Fpi) Inmate Work Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The SOI is responsible for ensuring that appropriate releasable information pertaining to an inmate's medical limitation (e.g., back injury) is made available to the FPI staff member who directly supervises the assignment.
