41 C.F.R. § 302-11.300 — -11.300 Limit on how much an agency will reimburse for residence transactions.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-11.300
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-11.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-11 (11—Allowances For Expenses Incurred In Connection With Residence Transactions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies will reimburse no more than:
(a) Ten percent of the actual sales price for the sale of a residence at the old official station; and
(b) Five percent of the actual purchase price of the residence for the purchase of a residence at the new official station.
