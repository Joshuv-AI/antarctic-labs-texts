48 C.F.R. § 536.506 — 536.506 Superintendence by the contractor.
Citation: us:cfr:48:536.506
Creator: Chapter 5—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-536.506
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 536 (Construction And Architect-Engineer Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Insert the clause at 552.236-6, Superintendence by the Contractor, in solicitations and contracts if construction, dismantling, demolition, or removal of improvements is contemplated.
