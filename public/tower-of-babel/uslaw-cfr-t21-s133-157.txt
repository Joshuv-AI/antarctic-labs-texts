21 C.F.R. § 133.157 — Part-skim mozzarella and scamorza cheese.
Citation: us:cfr:21:133.157
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-133.157
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 133 (Cheeses And Related Cheese Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Part-skim mozzarella cheese, part-skim scamorza cheese conforms to the definition and standard of identity as prescribed for mozzarella cheese by § 133.155, except that its milk fat content, calculated on the solids basis, is less than 45 percent but not less than 30 percent.
