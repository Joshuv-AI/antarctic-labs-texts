46 C.F.R. § 541.5 — Failure to include required information.
Citation: us:cfr:46:541.5
Creator: Federal Maritime Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-541.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 541 (Demurrage And Detention). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Failure to include any of the required minimum information in this part in a demurrage or detention invoice eliminates any obligation of the billed party to pay the applicable charge.
