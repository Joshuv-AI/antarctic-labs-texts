Correction to: A TaqMan Assay for the Detection and Monitoring of Potentially Invasive Lasiocampids, With Particular Attention to the Siberian Silk Moth, Dendrolimus sibiricus (Lepidoptera: Lasiocampidae)
Published: 2023

This is a correction to: Don Stewart, Abdelmadjid Djoumad, Dave Holden, Troy Kimoto, Arnaud Capron, Vladimir V Dubatolov, Yuriy B Akhanaev, Maria E Yakimova, Vyacheslav V Martemyanov, Michel Cusson, A TaqMan Assay for the Detection and Monitoring of Potentially Invasive Lasiocampids, With Particular Attention to the Siberian Silk Moth, Dendrolimus sibiricus (Lepidoptera: Lasiocampidae), Journal of Insect Science, Volume 23, Issue 1, January 2023, 5, https://doi.org/10.1093/jisesa/ieac062

There was a corresponding change in the enumeration of affiliations for the author and affiliation lists. These should now read: “[…]Maria E. Yakimova, 5 Vyacheslav V. Martemyanov, 4 and Michel Cusson1,6,[…]

[…] 4 Institute of Systematics and Ecology of Animals, Siberian Branch of the Russian Academy of Sciences, Novosibirsk, Russia, 5 Department of Information Biology, Novosibirsk State University, Novosibirsk, Russia, 6 Département de biochimie, de microbiologie et de bio-informatique, Université Laval, Quebec City, Quebec, Canada[…]” instead of: “[…] Maria E. Yakimova, 4 Vyacheslav V. Martemyanov, 4 and Michel Cusson1,5[…]

[…] 4 Institute of Systematics and Ecology of Animals, Siberian Branch of the Russian Academy of Sciences, Novosibirsk, Russia, 5 Département de biochimie, de microbiologie et de bio-informatique, Université Laval, Quebec City, Quebec, Canada[…]”.

These errors have been corrected in the article.
