42 U.S.C. § 1397n–8 — No Federal funding for credit enhancements
Citation: us:usc:42:1397n–8
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1397n–8
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 7 (Social Security); Subchapter XX (Block Grants And Programs For Social Services And Elder Justice). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No amount made available to carry out this division may be used to provide any insurance, guarantee, or other credit enhancement to a State or local government under which a Federal payment would be made to a State or local government as the result of a State or local government failing to achieve an outcome specified in an agreement.
