2 U.S.C. § 6532 — Death, resignation, or disability of Secretary and Assistant Secretary of Senate; Financial Clerk deemed successor as disbursing officer
Citation: us:usc:2:6532
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section6532
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 65 (Senate Officers And Administration); Subchapter II (Secretary Of The Senate); Part A (Organization And Personnel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For any period during which both the Secretary and the Assistant Secretary of the Senate are unable (because of death, resignation, or disability) to discharge such Secretary’s duties as disbursing officer of the Senate, the Financial Clerk of the Senate shall be deemed to be the successor of such Secretary as disbursing officer.
