25 C.F.R. § 31.6 — Coercion prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:25:31.6
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-31.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 31 (Federal Schools For Indians). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

There shall be no coercion of children in the matter of transfers from one school to another, but voluntary enrollment should be effected through maintenance of Federal Indian schools or programs which suit the needs and interests of the areas in which they are located.
