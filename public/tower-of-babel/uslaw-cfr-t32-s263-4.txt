32 C.F.R. § 263.4 — Registration of vehicles.
Citation: us:cfr:32:263.4
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-263.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 263 (Traffic And Vehicle Control On Certain Defense Mapping Agency Sites). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Newly assigned or employed individuals who intend to operate a privately-owned vehicle at the site shall register it with the Security Police Division within 24 hours after entry on duty.
(b) Temporary registration for a specified period of time will be permitted for temporarily hired, detailed, or assigned personnel; consultants; contractors; visiting dignitaries, etc.
