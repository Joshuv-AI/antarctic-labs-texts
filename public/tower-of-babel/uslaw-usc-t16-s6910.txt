16 U.S.C. § 6910 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:6910
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section6910
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 88 (Western And Central Pacific Fisheries Convention). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary of Commerce such sums as may be necessary to carry out this chapter and to pay the United States’ contribution to the Commission under section 5 of part III of the WCPFC Convention.
