30 C.F.R. § 75.519-1 — -1 Main power circuits; disconnecting switches; locations.
Citation: us:cfr:30:75.519-1
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-75.519-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 75 (Mandatory Safety Standards—Underground Coal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 75.519 requires (a) that a disconnecting switch be installed on the surface at a point within 500 feet of the place where the main power circuit enters the underground area of a mine, and (b) that, in an instance on which a main power circuit enters the underground area through a shaft or borehole, a disconnecting switch be installed underground within 500 feet of the bottom of the shaft or borehole.
