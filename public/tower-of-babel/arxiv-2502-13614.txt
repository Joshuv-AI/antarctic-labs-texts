Exact Results for SYM on Y p , q Y^{p,q} and S 2 × S 2 S^{2}\times S^{2} with Conical Singularities
Lorenzo Ruggeri

Abstract
Starting from a theory on S 3 × S 3 S^{3}\times S^{3} and dimensionally reducing, we compute the full partition function, including flux and instanton contributions, for an 𝒩 = 1 \mathcal{N}=1 theory of vector multiplets and hypermultiplets on five-dimensional toric Sasakian manifolds Y p , q Y^{p,q} . Dimensionally reducing, we obtain the partition function for Pestun-like theories on a class of manifolds whose topology is S 2 × S 2 S^{2}\times S^{2} . Generalizing the procedure starting from branched covers of S 3 × S 3 S^{3}\times S^{3} , we reduce to a theory on Y p , q Y^{p,q} with codimension two twist defects. Exploiting a proposed equivalence with partition functions on spaces with orbifold singularities, our results provide the partition function of an 𝒩 = 2 \mathcal{N}=2 theory on the product of two spindles.

2.1
Toric Sasakian Manifolds

Sasakian manifolds
(M,g)
are defined as Riemannian manifolds whose metric cone
(C(M),\bar{g})
is Kähler

C(M)=M\times\mathbb{R}_{>0},\qquad\bar{g}=dr^{2}+r^{2}g.

(1)

The existence of an effective, holomorphic and Hamiltonian torus action on the Kähler cone leads to the definition of toric Sasakian manifolds. Moreover,
C(M)
can be obtained as a Kähler quotient

C(M)=\mathbb{C}^{m}//U(1)^{m-3},

(2)

where
U(1)^{m-3}
acts with charges

\vec{Q}^{M}_{a}=[Q^{1}_{a},\dots,Q^{m}_{a}],\qquad a=1,\dots,m-3,

(3)

on
\mathbb{C}^{m}
. If the charges satisfy the
m-3
conditions
Q^{1}_{a}+\dots+Q^{m}_{a}=0
, the cone is Calabi-Yau and
M
is said to be Sasaki-Einstein.

Restricting to five-dimensional simply connected toric Sasakian manifolds, the simplest example, with
m=3
, is
S^{5}
whose metric cone is
\mathbb{C}^{3}/\{0\}
. Moving to
m=4
, there are two classes of manifolds,
Y^{p,q}
and
L^{a,b,c}
, whose metric cones are defined by the charges of the
U(1)
in (
2
)

\begin{split}\mathbb{C}^{m}//S^{1}_{Y}=C(Y^{p,q}):\qquad&\vec{Q}^{Y}=[p+q,p-q,-p,-p],\quad p>q>0,\\
\mathbb{C}^{m}//S^{1}_{L}=C(L^{a,b,c}):\qquad&\vec{Q}^{L}=[a,b,-c,-a-b+c],\;\quad a,b,a+b-c>0,\end{split}

(4)

where
\gcd(p,q)=\gcd(a,b)=\gcd(a,c)=\gcd(b,c)=1
. Each component of these four-vectors rotates the phase of each
\mathbb{C}
factor. As the sum of the charges in (
4
) vanishes in both cases, these two families of manifolds are Sasaki-Einstein.

Let us explicitly construct the class of
Y^{p,q}
manifolds starting from

25
]

C(Y^{p,q})=\mu_{S^{1}_{Y}}^{-1}(0)/S^{1}_{Y},

(5)

\mu_{S^{1}_{Y}}=(p+q)|z_{1}|^{2}+(p-q)|z_{2}|^{2}-p|z_{3}|^{2}-p|z_{4}|^{2}.

(6)

To further obtain

from (
5

1

H_{\omega}=\{z_{i}\in\mathbb{C}\,|\,\sum_{i=1}^{4}\omega_{i}|z_{i}|^{2}=1\},

(7)

where

6
Discussion

In this work we computed partition functions of
\mathcal{N}=1
theories on five-dimensional toric Sasakian manifolds
Y^{p,q}
and
L^{a,b,c}
, including previously neglected contributions from gauge configurations with flux. We extended these results to theories with twist defects on
\widehat{Y}^{p,q}_{\boldsymbol{p}}
and
\widehat{L}^{a,b,c}_{\boldsymbol{p}}
. According to the proposal in
[
46
]
, for vector multiplets and hypermultiplets in the adjoint representation, the partition function on these spaces is reproduces that on orbifolds
Y^{p,q}_{\boldsymbol{p}}
and
L^{a,b,c}_{\boldsymbol{p}}
.

We further employed these results to compute the full partition function for an
\mathcal{N}=2
SQFT on a manifold
B
whose topology is that of the product of two spheres, and on the corresponding orbifold. In particular, with the same caveat as above, our results provide the partition function on a class of orbifolds whose topology is that of the product of two spindles.

We computed all these results exploiting a sequence of non-trivial
S^{1}
-fibrations, that is
Y^{p,q}=(S^{3}\times S^{3})/S^{1}_{Y}
(similar for
L^{a,b,c}
) and
B=Y^{p,q}/S^{1}
. Thus, we have found that the equivariant index of a certain complex on
S^{3}\times S^{3}
completely determines the one-loop determinant around both fluxes on
B
. The same holds on orbifolds, starting from the equivariant index on
\widehat{S}^{3}_{\boldsymbol{p}_{A}}\times\widehat{S}^{3}_{\boldsymbol{p}_{B}}
.
