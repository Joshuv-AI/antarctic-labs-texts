40 C.F.R. § 33.410 — Can a recipient be penalized for failing to meet its fair share objectives?
Citation: us:cfr:40:33.410
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-33.410
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 33 (Participation By Disadvantaged Business Enterprises In United States Environmental Protection Agency Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A recipient cannot be penalized, or treated by EPA as being in noncompliance with this subpart, solely because its MBE or WBE participation does not meet its applicable fair share objective. However, EPA may take remedial action under § 33.105 for a recipient's failure to comply with other provisions of this part, including, but not limited to, the good faith efforts requirements described in subpart C of this part.
