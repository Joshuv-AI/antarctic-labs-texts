9 C.F.R. § 560.1 — Cooperation with States and Territories.
Citation: us:cfr:9:560.1
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-560.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 560 (State-Federal, Federal-State Cooperative Agreements; State Designations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions in § 321.1 of this chapter authorizing the Administrator to cooperate with any State (including Puerto Rico) or any organized Territory in developing and administering a meat inspection program for the State or Territory apply with respect to fish and fish products inspection.
