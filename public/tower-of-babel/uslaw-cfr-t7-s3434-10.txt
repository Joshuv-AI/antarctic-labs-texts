7 C.F.R. § 3434.10 — Reporting requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3434.10
Creator: National Institute Of Food And Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3434.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3434 (Hispanic-Serving Agricultural Colleges And Universities Certification Process). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The certification process does not involve any reporting requirements.
(b) Reporting requirements for HSACU programs (e.g., HSACU Endowment Fund) shall be established in separate parts.
