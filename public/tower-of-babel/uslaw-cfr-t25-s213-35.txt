25 C.F.R. § 213.35 — Mines to be timbered properly.
Citation: us:cfr:25:213.35
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-213.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 213 (Leasing Of Restricted Lands Of Members Of Five Civilized Tribes, Oklahoma, For Mining). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In mining operations the lessee shall keep the mine well and sufficiently timbered at all points where necessary, in accordance with good mining practice, and in such manner as may be necessary to the proper preservation of the property leased and safety of workmen.
