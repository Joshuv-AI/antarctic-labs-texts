42 C.F.R. § 3.418 — Exclusivity of penalty.
Citation: us:cfr:42:3.418
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-3.418
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 3 (Patient Safety Organizations And Patient Safety Work Product). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except as otherwise provided by paragraph (b) of this section, a penalty imposed under this part is in addition to any other penalty prescribed by law.
(b) Civil money penalties shall not be imposed both under this part and under the HIPAA Privacy Rule (45 CFR parts 160 and 164).
