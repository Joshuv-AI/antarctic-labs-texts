1 C.F.R. § 21.6 — Notice of expiration of codified material.
Citation: us:cfr:1:21.6
Creator: Administrative Committee Of The Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-1/section-21.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 1 (General Provisions), Part 21 (Preparation Of Documents Subject To Codification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-23 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever a codified regulation expires after a specified period by its own terms or by law, the issuing agency shall submit a notification by document for publication in the Federal Register.
