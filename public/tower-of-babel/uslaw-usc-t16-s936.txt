16 U.S.C. § 936 — Secretary of the Interior; authority to transfer lamprey control projects and act on behalf of United States Section
Citation: us:usc:16:936
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section936
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 15A (Great Lakes Fisheries). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior is authorized, upon the request of the United States Section—
(a)
to transfer to the United States Section any lamprey control project or works under his jurisdiction now existing or now under construction; and
(b)
to act for or on behalf of the United States Section in the exercise of the powers granted by this chapter.
