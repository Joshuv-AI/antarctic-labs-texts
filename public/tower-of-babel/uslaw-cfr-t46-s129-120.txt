46 C.F.R. § 129.120 — Alternative standards.
Citation: us:cfr:46:129.120
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-129.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 129 (Electrical Installations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An OSV of 19.8 meters (65 feet) in length or less may meet the following requirements of the American Yacht and Boat Council Projects, where applicable, instead of § 129.340 of this part:
(1) E-1, Bonding of Direct Current Systems.
(2) E-8, AC Electrical Systems on Boats.
(3) E-9, DC Electrical Systems on Boats.
(b) An OSV with an electrical installation operating at a potential of less than 50 volts may comply with 33 CFR 183.430 instead of § 129.340 of this part.
