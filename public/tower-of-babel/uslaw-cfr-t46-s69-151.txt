46 C.F.R. § 69.151 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:46:69.151
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-69.151
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 69 (Measurement Of Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes measurement requirements for the assignment of either one gross and one net register tonnage or two gross and two net register tonnages to vessels under this subpart.
