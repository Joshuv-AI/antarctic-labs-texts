49 C.F.R. § 1111.7 — Satisfaction of complaint.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1111.7
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1111.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1111 (Complaint And Investigation Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a defendant satisfies a formal complaint, either before or after answering, a statement to that effect signed by the complainant must be filed (original only need be filed), setting forth when and how the complaint has been satisfied. This action should be taken as expeditiously as possible.
