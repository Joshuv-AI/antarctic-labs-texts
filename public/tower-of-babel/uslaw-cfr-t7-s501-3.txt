7 C.F.R. § 501.3 — Preservation of property.
Citation: us:cfr:7:501.3
Creator: Agricultural Research Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-501.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 501 (Conduct On U.S. Meat Animal Research Center, Clay Center, Nebraska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is unlawful to willfully destroy, damage, or remove property or any part thereof. Hunting, fishing, motorcycling, using snowmobiles, and other disturbances or encroachment activities are prohibited except for official purposes.
