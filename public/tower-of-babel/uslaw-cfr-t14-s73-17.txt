14 C.F.R. § 73.17 — Controlling agency.
Citation: us:cfr:14:73.17
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-73.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 73 (Special Use Airspace). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the purposes of this part, the controlling agency is the FAA facility that may authorize transit through or flight within a restricted area in accordance with a joint-use letter issued under § 73.15.
