47 C.F.R. § 27.802 — Permissible communications.
Citation: us:cfr:47:27.802
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-27.802
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 27 (Miscellaneous Wireless Communications Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Licensees in the paired 1392-1395 MHz and 1432-1435 MHz bands and unpaired 1390-1392 MHz band are authorized to provide fixed or mobile service, except aeronautical mobile service, subject to the technical requirements of this subpart.
