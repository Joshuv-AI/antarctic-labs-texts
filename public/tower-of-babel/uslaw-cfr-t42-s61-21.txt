42 C.F.R. § 61.21 — Interest.
Citation: us:cfr:42:61.21
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-61.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 61 (Fellowships). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any interest earned through deposit or investment by the sponsoring institution of funds paid pursuant to the provisions of this subpart shall be paid to the United States as such interest is received by the sponsoring institution.
