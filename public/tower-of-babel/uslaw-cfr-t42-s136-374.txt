42 C.F.R. § 136.374 — Availability of list of recipients.
Citation: us:cfr:42:136.374
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-136.374
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 136 (Indian Health). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The IHS will provide to any person requesting it a list of the recipients of scholarship grants under this subdivision, including the school attended and tribal affiliation of each recipient.
