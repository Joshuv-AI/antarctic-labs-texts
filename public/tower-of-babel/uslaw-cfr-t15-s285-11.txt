15 C.F.R. § 285.11 — Changes to scope of accreditation.
Citation: us:cfr:15:285.11
Creator: National Institute Of Standards And Technology, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-285.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 285 (National Voluntary Laboratory Accreditation Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A laboratory may request in writing changes to its Scope of Accreditation. If the laboratory requests additions to its Scope, it must meet all NVLAP criteria for the additional tests or calibrations, types of tests or calibrations, or standards. The need for an additional on-site assessment and/or proficiency testing will be determined on a case-by-case basis.
