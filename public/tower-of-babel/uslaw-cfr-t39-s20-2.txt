39 C.F.R. § 20.2 — Effective date of the International Mail Manual.
Citation: us:cfr:39:20.2
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-20.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 20 (International Postal Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of the International Mail Manual, issued April 7, 2025, (incorporated by reference, see § 20.1) are applicable with respect to the international mail services of the Postal Service.
