49 C.F.R. § 271.213 — Safety performance evaluation process.
Citation: us:cfr:49:271.213
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-271.213
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 271 (Risk Reduction Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An RRP plan shall describe a railroad's processes for identifying and analyzing its safety culture pursuant to § 271.105(a), monitoring safety performance pursuant to § 271.105(b), and conducting safety assessments pursuant to § 271.105(c).
