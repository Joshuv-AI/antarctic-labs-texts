38 C.F.R. § 21.7170 — Course measurement.
Citation: us:cfr:38:21.7170
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-21.7170
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 21 (Veteran Readiness And Employment And Education). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In administering benefits payable under 38 U.S.C. chapter 30, VA will apply the following sections:
(a) § 21.4270 (except paragraphs (a)(2) and (a)(3) and those portions of paragraph (c) and footnotes dealing with farm cooperative training)—Measurement of courses;
(b) § 21.4272—Collegiate course measurement;
(c) § 21.4273—Collegiate graduate;
(d) § 21.4274—Law courses; and
(e) § 21.4275—Practical training courses; measurement.
