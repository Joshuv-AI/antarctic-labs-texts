Retraction Note: Epidermal growth factor-receptor activation modulates Src-dependent resistance to lapatinib in breast cancer models
Authors: Luigi Formisano, Lucia Nappi, Roberta Rosa, Roberta Marciano, Claudia D’Amato, Valentina D’Amato, Vincenzo Damiano, Lucia Raimondo, Francesca Iommelli, Antonella Scorziello, Giancarlo Troncone, Bianca Maria Veneziani, Sarah J Parsons, Sabino De Placido, Roberto Bianco
Published: 2025

Retraction Note to: Breast Cancer Research 2014, 16:R45 10.1186/bcr3650

The Editor-in-Chief has retracted this article. After publication, concerns were raised about some high image similarities within the article and across publications from the same authors. Specifically:

Fig. 1C, Actin (MDA-MB-361-LR) and Fig. 1C, Akt (JIMT-1) appear to be highly similar.

Fig. 1C, Actin (MDA-MB-361-LR) and Fig. 1C, Akt (JIMT-1) appear to be highly similar.

Fig. 1 C, Actin (JIMT-1) and Fig. 2E, FAK (MDA-MB-361-LR) appear to be highly similar.

Fig. 1 C, Actin (JIMT-1) and Fig. 2E, FAK (MDA-MB-361-LR) appear to be highly similar.

Fig. 1 C, Actin (MDA-MB-361) and Fig. 2E, Actin (MDA-MB-361) appear to be highly similar.

Fig. 1 C, Actin (MDA-MB-361) and Fig. 2E, Actin (MDA-MB-361) appear to be highly similar.

Fig. 1 C, Akt and Fig. 1B, Actin (lanes 5-6-7-8) [1] appear to overlap.

Fig. 1 C, Akt and Fig. 1B, Actin (lanes 5-6-7-8) [1] appear to overlap.

Fig. 2E, FAK (MDA-MB-361-LR) and Fig. 2E, Actin (JIMT-1) appear to be highly similar.

Fig. 2E, FAK (MDA-MB-361-LR) and Fig. 2E, Actin (JIMT-1) appear to be highly similar.

Fig. 3D, Paxillin and Fig. 3D, p130Cad appear to be highly similar.

Fig. 3D, Paxillin and Fig. 3D, p130Cad appear to be highly similar.

Fig. 5 A, pAkt (lane 1) ( MDA-MB-361) and Fig. 5B, pSrc (lane 1)( MDA-MB-361) (vertical and horizontal flipping) appear to overlap.

Fig. 5 A, pAkt (lane 1) ( MDA-MB-361) and Fig. 5B, pSrc (lane 1)( MDA-MB-361) (vertical and horizontal flipping) appear to overlap.

Fig. 3D, FAK and Fig. 2C, Actin (ACHN) [1] appear to be highly similar.

Fig. 3D, FAK and Fig. 2C, Actin (ACHN) [1] appear to be highly similar.

The Authors have stated that the mistakes might have occurred in collecting western blot images for different projects. However, due to the age of the article they could not provide any original raw data. Due to the number of concerns, the Editor-in-Chief no longer has confidence in the presented data.

None of the Authors responded to correspondence from the Publisher about this retraction notice.
