49 C.F.R. § 180.1 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:49:180.1
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-180.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 180 (Continuing Qualification And Maintenance Of Packagings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes requirements pertaining to the maintenance, reconditioning, repair, inspection and testing of packagings, and any other function having an effect on the continuing qualification and use of a packaging under the requirements of this subchapter.
