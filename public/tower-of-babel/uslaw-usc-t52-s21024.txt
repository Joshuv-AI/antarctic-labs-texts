52 U.S.C. § 21024 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:52:21024
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title52-section21024
Context: United States Code, Title 52 (Voting And Elections); Chapter 209 (Election Administration Improvement); Subchapter II (Commission); Part D (Election Assistance); Subpart 2 (Payments To States And Units Of Local Government To Assure Access For Individuals With Disabilities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out the provisions of this subpart the following amounts:
(1)
For fiscal year 2003, $50,000,000.
(2)
For fiscal year 2004, $25,000,000.
(3)
For fiscal year 2005, $25,000,000.
(b) Availability
Any amounts appropriated pursuant to the authority of subsection (a) shall remain available without fiscal year limitation until expended.
