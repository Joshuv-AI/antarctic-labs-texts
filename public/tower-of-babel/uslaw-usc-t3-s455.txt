3 U.S.C. § 455 — Effect of failure to issue regulations
Citation: us:usc:3:455
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title3-section455
Context: United States Code, Title 3 (The President); Chapter 5 (Extension Of Certain Rights And Protections To Presidential Offices); Subchapter III (Administrative And Judicial Dispute Resolution Procedures). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In any proceeding under section 453(1), if the President, or the designee of the President, has not issued a regulation on a matter for which this chapter requires a regulation to be issued, the administrative agency shall apply, to the extent necessary and appropriate, the most relevant substantive executive agency regulation promulgated to implement the statutory provision at issue in the proceeding.
