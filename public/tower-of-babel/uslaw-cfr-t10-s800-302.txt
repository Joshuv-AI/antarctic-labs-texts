10 C.F.R. § 800.302 — Loan limitation.
Citation: us:cfr:10:800.302
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-800.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 800 (Loans For Bid Or Proposal Preparation By Minority Business Enterprises Seeking Doe Contracts And Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary may limit the loan by written notice to the borrower to those amounts, if any, already disbursed under the loan, if the Secretary has determined that the borower has failed to comply with a material term or condition set forth in the loan agreement.
