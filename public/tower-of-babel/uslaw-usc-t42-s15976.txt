42 U.S.C. § 15976 — Electron scrubbing demonstration
Citation: us:usc:42:15976
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section15976
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 149 (National Energy Policy And Programs); Subchapter IV (Coal); Part B (Clean Power Projects). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall use $5,000,000 from amounts appropriated to initiate, through the Chicago Operations Office, a project to demonstrate the viability of high-energy electron scrubbing technology on commercial-scale electrical generation using high-sulfur coal.
