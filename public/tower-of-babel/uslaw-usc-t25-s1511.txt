25 U.S.C. § 1511 — Interest subsidies; rules and regulations
Citation: us:usc:25:1511
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section1511
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 17 (Financing Economic Development Of Indians And Indian Organizations); Subchapter III (Interest Subsidies And Administrative Expenses). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized under such rules and regulations as he may prescribe to pay as an interest subsidy on loans which are guaranteed or insured under the provisions of subchapter II of this chapter amounts which are necessary to reduce the rate payable by the borrower to the rate determined under section 1464 of this title.
