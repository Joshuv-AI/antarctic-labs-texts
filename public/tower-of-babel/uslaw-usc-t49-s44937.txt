49 U.S.C. § 44937 — Prohibition on transferring duties and powers
Citation: us:usc:49:44937
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section44937
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Air Commerce And Safety); Subpart iii (Safety); Chapter 449 (Security); Subchapter II (Administration And Personnel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as specifically provided by law, the Administrator of the Transportation Security Administration may not transfer a duty or power under section 44903(a), (b), (c), or (e), 44906, 44912, 44935, 44936, or 44938(b)(3) of this title to another department, agency, or instrumentality of the United States Government.
