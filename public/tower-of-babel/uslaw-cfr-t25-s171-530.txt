25 C.F.R. § 171.530 — What information must I provide BIA for billing purposes?
Citation: us:cfr:25:171.530
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-171.530
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 171 (Irrigation Operation And Maintenance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We must obtain certain information from you to ensure we can properly bill, collect, deposit, and account for money you owe the United States. At a minimum, this information is:
(a) Your full legal name;
(b) Your correct mailing address; and
(c) Your taxpayer identifying number.
