14 C.F.R. § 29.551 — Auxiliary lifting surfaces.
Citation: us:cfr:14:29.551
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-29.551
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 29 (Airworthiness Standards: Transport Category Rotorcraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each auxiliary lifting surface must be designed to withstand—
(a) The critical flight loads in §§ 29.337 through 29.341, and 29.351;
(b) the applicable ground loads in §§ 29.235, 29.471 through 29.485, 29.493, 29.505, and 29.521; and
(c) Any other critical condition expected in normal operation.
