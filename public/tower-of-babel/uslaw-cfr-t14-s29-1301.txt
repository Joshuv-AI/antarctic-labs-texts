14 C.F.R. § 29.1301 — Function and installation.
Citation: us:cfr:14:29.1301
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-29.1301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 29 (Airworthiness Standards: Transport Category Rotorcraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each item of installed equipment must—
(a) Be of a kind and design appropriate to its intended function;
(b) Be labeled as to its identification, function, or operating limitations, or any applicable combination of these factors;
(c) Be installed according to limitations specified for that equipment; and
(d) Function properly when installed.
