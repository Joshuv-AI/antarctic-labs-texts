49 C.F.R. § 171.19 — Approvals or authorizations issued by the Bureau of Explosives.
Citation: us:cfr:49:171.19
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-171.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 171 (General Information, Regulations, And Definitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Effective December 31, 1998, approvals or authorizations issued by the Bureau of Explosives (BOE), other than those issued under part 179 of this subchapter, are no longer valid.
