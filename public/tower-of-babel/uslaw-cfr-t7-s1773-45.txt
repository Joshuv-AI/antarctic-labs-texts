7 C.F.R. § 1773.45 — Regulatory liabilities.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1773.45
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1773.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1773 (Policy On Audits Of Rus Awardees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The auditor's audit documentation shall support that all regulatory liabilities comply with the requirements of FASB Accounting Standards Codification (ASC) 980 or GASB Statement (GASBS) 62, as appropriate. For electric auditees only, the auditor's audit documentation shall document whether all regulatory liabilities have received RUS approval.
