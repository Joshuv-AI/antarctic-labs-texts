9 C.F.R. § 354.73 — Retention labels.
Citation: us:cfr:9:354.73
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-354.73
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 354 (Voluntary Inspection Of Rabbits And Edible Products Thereof). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An inspector may use such labels, devices, and methods as may be approved by the Administrator for the identification of:
(a) Products which are held for further examination, and
(b) All equipment and utensils which are to be held for proper cleaning.
