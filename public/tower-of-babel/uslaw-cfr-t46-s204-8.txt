46 C.F.R. § 204.8 — Where to file claims.
Citation: us:cfr:46:204.8
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-204.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 204 (Claims Against The Maritime Administration Under The Federal Tort Claims Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Claimants must file claims with the Chief Counsel (MAR-220), Maritime Administration, Department of Transportation, Room 7232, SW, Washington, DC 20590 at the Nassif Building, 7th and D Streets.
