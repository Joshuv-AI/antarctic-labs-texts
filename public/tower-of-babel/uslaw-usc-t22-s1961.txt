22 U.S.C. § 1961 — Economic assistance
Citation: us:usc:22:1961
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1961
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 24A (Middle East Peace And Stability). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President is authorized to cooperate with and assist any nation or group of nations in the general area of the Middle East desiring such assistance in the development of economic strength dedicated to the maintenance of national independence.
