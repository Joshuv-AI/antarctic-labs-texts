39 C.F.R. § 281.4 — Disclaimer.
Citation: us:cfr:39:281.4
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-281.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 281 (Firm Mailings Damaged Or Destroyed Through Transportation Accidents Or Catastrophes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Postal Service will not be liable in damages for any loss occasioned by any failure to notify firm mailers in accordance with this part of damage to or destruction of firm mailings.
