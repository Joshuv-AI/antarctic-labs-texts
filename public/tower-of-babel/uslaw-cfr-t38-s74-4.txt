38 C.F.R. § 74.4 — Who does CVE consider to control a veteran-owned small business?
Citation: us:cfr:38:74.4
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-74.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 74 (Veterans Small Business Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Control is determined in accordance with 13 CFR part 125. However, where 13 CFR part 125 is limited to SDVOSBs, CVE applies the same control criteria to firms seeking verified VOSB status.
