12 U.S.C. § 228 — “Banking Act of 1935”
Citation: us:usc:12:228
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section228
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 3 (Federal Reserve System); Subchapter I (Definitions, Organization, And General Provisions Affecting System). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The act of August 23, 1935, ch. 614, 49 Stat. 684, may be cited as the “Banking Act of 1935.”
