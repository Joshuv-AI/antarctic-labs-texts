43 C.F.R. § 3933.60 — Effective date.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3933.60
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3933.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3930 (Management Of Oil Shale Exploration And Leases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An assignment or sublease takes effect, so far as the United States is concerned, on the first day of the month following the BLM's final approval, or if the assignee requests it in advance, the first day of the month of the approval.
