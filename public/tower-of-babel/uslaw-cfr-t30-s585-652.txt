30 C.F.R. § 585.652 — How long do I have to conduct activities under an approved GAP?
Citation: us:cfr:30:585.652
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-585.652
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 585 (Renewable Energy On The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After BOEM approves your GAP, you have:
(a) For a limited lease, the time period established under § 585.236(a)(2), unless we renew the operations period under §§ 585.425 through 585.429.
(b) For a ROW grant or RUE grant, the time provided in the terms of the grant.
