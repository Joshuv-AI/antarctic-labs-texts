45 C.F.R. § 263.14 — What methodology shall States use to allocate TANF costs?
Citation: us:cfr:45:263.14
Creator: Office Of Family Assistance (Assistance Programs), Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-263.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 263 (Expenditures Of State And Federal Tanf Funds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

States shall use a benefiting program cost allocation methodology consistent with the general requirements of 2 CFR parts 200 and 300, subpart E to allocate TANF costs.
