40 C.F.R. § 428.90 — Applicability; description of the pan, dry digestion, and mechanical reclaimed rubber subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:428.90
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-428.90
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 428 (Rubber Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to process waste water discharges resulting from the production of reclaimed rubber except when produced by the wet digestion process.
