34 U.S.C. § 10591 — Grants authorized
Citation: us:usc:34:10591
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section10591
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 101 (Justice System Improvement); Subchapter XXIX (Grants For Family-Based Substance Abuse Treatment). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General may make grants to States, units of local government, territories, nonprofit organizations, and Indian Tribes to—
(1)
develop, implement, and expand comprehensive and clinically-appropriate family-based substance abuse treatment programs as alternatives to incarceration for nonviolent parent drug offenders; and
(2)
to 1 provide prison-based family treatment programs for incarcerated parents of minor children or pregnant women.
