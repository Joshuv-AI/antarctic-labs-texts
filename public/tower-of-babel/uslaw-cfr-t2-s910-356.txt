2 C.F.R. § 910.356 — Audits.

Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-910.356
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 910 (Uniform Administrative Requirements, Cost Principles, And Audit Requirements For Federal Awards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

See Subpart F of this part (Sections 910.500 through 910.521) for specific DOE regulations which apply to audits of DOE's For-Profit Recipients. For-Profit entities are an exception to the Single Audit requirements contained in Subpart F of 2 CFR 200 and therefore the regulations contained in 2 CFR 910 Subpart F apply instead.
