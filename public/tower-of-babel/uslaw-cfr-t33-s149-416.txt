33 C.F.R. § 149.416 — What are the requirements for a dry chemical fire suppression system?
Citation: us:cfr:33:149.416
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-149.416
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 149 (Deepwater Ports: Design, Construction, And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each natural gas deepwater port must be equipped with a dry chemical system that meets the requirements of § 127.609 of this chapter.
