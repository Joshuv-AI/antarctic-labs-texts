12 C.F.R. § 414.1 — Collection of conference and other fees.
Citation: us:cfr:12:414.1
Creator: Export-Import Bank Of The United States
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-414.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 414 (Conference And Other Fees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Ex-Im Bank may impose and collect reasonable fees to cover the costs of conferences and seminars sponsored by, and publications provided by Ex-Im Bank. Amounts received under the preceding sentence shall be credited to the fund which initially paid for such activities and shall be offset against the expenses of Ex-Im Bank for such activities.
