10 C.F.R. § 1710.105 — Application.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1710.105
Creator: Defense Nuclear Facilities Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1710.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1710 (Federal Employee Salary Offset Procedures For The Collection Of A Debt Owed To The Federal Government). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part are to be followed when:
(a) The Board is owed a debt by an individual currently employed by another federal agency;
(b) The Board is owed a debt by an individual who is a current employee of the Board; or
(c) The Board employs an individual who owes a debt to another federal agency.
