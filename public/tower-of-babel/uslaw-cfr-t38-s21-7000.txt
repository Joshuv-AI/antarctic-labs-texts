38 C.F.R. § 21.7000 — Establishment of educational assistance program.
Citation: us:cfr:38:21.7000
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-21.7000
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 21 (Veteran Readiness And Employment And Education). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Establishment. An educational assistance program for certain veterans and servicemembers is established.
(b) Purpose. The purpose of this program is as stated in 38 U.S.C. 3001.
