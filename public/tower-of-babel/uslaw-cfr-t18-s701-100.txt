18 C.F.R. § 701.100 — Field Directors.
Citation: us:cfr:18:701.100
Creator: Water Resources Council
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-701.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 701 (Council Organization). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Council may employ as professional staff Field Directors who shall be designated as chairmen of committees or groups established by the Council to develop and prepare regional or river basin assessments or plans. Such Field Directors shall perform their official functions at locations established by the Council.
