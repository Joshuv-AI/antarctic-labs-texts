41 C.F.R. § 101-25.001 — -25.001 Scope of part.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-25.001
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-25.001
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-25 (25—General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part provides policies and guidelines pertaining to subject matter in the general area of supply management which is not appropriate for coverage in other parts of this subchapter E.
