49 U.S.C. § 20145 — Report on bridge displacement detection systems
Citation: us:usc:49:20145
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section20145
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Safety); Chapter 201 (General); Subchapter II (Particular Aspects Of Safety). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 18 months after November 2, 1994, the Secretary of Transportation shall transmit to the Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation of the Senate and the Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure of the House of Representatives a report concerning any action that has been taken by the Secretary on railroad bridge displacement detection systems.
