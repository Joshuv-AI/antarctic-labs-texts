7 C.F.R. § 1205.337 — Influencing governmental action.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1205.337
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Miscellaneous Commodities), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1205.337
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1205 (Cotton Research And Promotion). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No funds collected by the Board under this subpart shall in any manner be used for the purpose of influencing governmental policy or action except in recommending to the Secretary amendments to this subpart.
