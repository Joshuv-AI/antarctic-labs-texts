48 C.F.R. § 3052.228-70 — 3052.228-70 Insurance.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 3052 (Solicitation Provisions And Contract Clauses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As prescribed in (HSAR) 48 CFR 3028.310-70 and 3028.311-1, insert a clause substantially the same as follows. The contracting officer may specify additional kinds (e.g., aircraft public and passenger liability, vessel liability) or increased amounts of insurance.
