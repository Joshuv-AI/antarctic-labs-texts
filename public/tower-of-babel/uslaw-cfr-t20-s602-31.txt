20 C.F.R. § 602.31 — Oversight.
Citation: us:cfr:20:602.31
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-602.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 602 (Quality Control In The Federal-State Unemployment Insurance System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department shall review QC operational procedures and samples, and validate QC methodology to ensure uniformity in the administration of the QC program and to ensure compliance with the requirements of this part. The Department shall, for purposes of determining eligibility for grants described in § 602.40, annually review the adequacy of the administration of a State's QC program.
