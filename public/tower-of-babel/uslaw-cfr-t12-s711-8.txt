12 C.F.R. § 711.8 — Enforcement.
Citation: us:cfr:12:711.8
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-711.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 711 (Management Official Interlocks). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in this section, NCUA administers and enforces the Interlocks Act with respect to federally insured credit unions, and may refer any case of a prohibited interlocking relationship involving these entities to the Attorney General of the United States to enforce compliance with the Interlocks Act and this part.
