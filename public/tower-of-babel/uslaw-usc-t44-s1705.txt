44 U.S.C. § 1705 — Printing additional copies for sale to public; regulations
Citation: us:usc:44:1705
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section1705
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 17 (Distribution And Sale Of Public Documents). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of the Government Publishing Office shall print additional copies of a Government publication, not confidential in character, required for sale to the public by the Superintendent of Documents, subject to regulation by the Joint Committee on Printing and without interference with the prompt execution of printing for the Government.
