12 U.S.C. § 1448 — Effect of partial invalidity of chapter
Citation: us:usc:12:1448
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section1448
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 11 (Federal Home Loan Banks). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this chapter, or the application thereof to any person or circumstances, is held invalid, the remainder of the chapter, and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances, shall not be affected thereby.
