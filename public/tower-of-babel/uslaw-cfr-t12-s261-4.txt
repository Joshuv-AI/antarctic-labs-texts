12 C.F.R. § 261.4 — Prohibition against disclosure.
Citation: us:cfr:12:261.4
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-261.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 261 (Rules Regarding Availability Of Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in this part or as otherwise authorized, no officer, employee, or agent of the Board or any Reserve Bank shall disclose or permit the disclosure of any nonpublic information of the Board to any person other than Board or Reserve Bank officers, employees, or agents properly entitled to such information for the performance of official duties.
