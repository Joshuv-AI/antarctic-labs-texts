41 C.F.R. § 102-33.225 — -33.225 Government aircraft report requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-33.225
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-33.225
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-33 (33—Management Of Government Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must report information to GSA on Government aircraft if your agency:
(a) Is an executive agency of the U.S. Government; and
(b) Owns, bails, borrows, loans, leases, rents, charters, or contracts for (or obtains by ISSA) Government aircraft.
