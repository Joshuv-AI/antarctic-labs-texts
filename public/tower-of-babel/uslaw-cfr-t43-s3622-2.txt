43 C.F.R. § 3622.2 — Procedures; permits.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3622.2
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3622.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3620 (Free Use Of Petrified Wood). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No application or permit for free use is required except for specimens over 250 pounds in weight. The authorized officer may issue permits, using the procedures of subpart 3604 of this chapter, for the removal of such specimens if the applicant certifies that they will be displayed to the public in a museum or similar institution.
