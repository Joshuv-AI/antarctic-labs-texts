41 C.F.R. § 105-56.012 — -56.012 Refunds.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-56.012
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-56.012
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-56 (56—Salary Offset For Indebtedness Of Federal Employees To The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) GSA will promptly refund to the employee any amounts offset under these regulations when a debt is waived or otherwise found not owing the United States (unless expressly prohibited by statute or regulation), or GSA is directed by an administrative or judicial order to refund amounts deducted from the employee's current pay or withheld from non-salary payments.
(b) Unless required by Federal law or contract, refunds under this subpart will not bear interest.
