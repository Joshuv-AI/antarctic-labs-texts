25 C.F.R. § 152.19 — Sale by fiduciaries.
Citation: us:cfr:25:152.19
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-152.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 152 (Issuance Of Patents In Fee, Certificates Of Competency, Removal Of Restrictions, And Sale Of Certain Indian Lands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Guardians, conservators, or other fiduciaries appointed by State courts, or by tribal courts operating under approved constitutions or law and order codes, may, upon order of the court, convey with the approval of the Secretary or consent to the conveyance by the Secretary of trust or restricted land belonging to their Indian wards who are minors, non compos mentis or otherwise under legal disability. This section is subject to the exceptions contained in 25 U.S.C. 954(b).
