10 U.S.C. § 520c — Recruiting functions: provision of meals and refreshments
Citation: us:usc:10:520c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section520c
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 31 (Enlistments). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Under regulations prescribed by the Secretary concerned, funds appropriated to the Department of Defense for recruitment of military personnel may be expended for small meals and refreshments during recruiting functions for the following persons:
(1)
Persons who have enlisted under the Delayed Entry Program authorized by section 513 of this title.
(2)
Persons who are objects of armed forces recruiting efforts.
(3)
Persons whose assistance in recruiting efforts of the military departments is determined to be influential by the Secretary concerned.
(4)
Members of the armed forces and Federal employees when attending recruiting functions in accordance with a requirement to do so.
(5)
Other persons whose presence at recruiting functions will contribute to recruiting efforts.
