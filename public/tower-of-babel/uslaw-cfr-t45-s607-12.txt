45 C.F.R. § 607.12 — Interest, penalties, and administrative costs.
Citation: us:cfr:45:607.12
Creator: National Science Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-607.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 607 (Salary Offset). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Charges may be assessed on a debt for interest, penalties, and administrative costs in accordance with 31 U.S.C. 3717 and the Federal Claims Collection Standards, 4 CFR 101.1.
