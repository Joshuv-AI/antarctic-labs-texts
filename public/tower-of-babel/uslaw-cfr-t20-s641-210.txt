20 C.F.R. § 641.210 — What services, in addition to the applicable career services, must SCSEP grantees and sub-recipients provide through the One-Stop delivery system?
Citation: us:cfr:20:641.210
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-641.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 641 (Provisions Governing The Senior Community Service Employment Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to providing career services, as defined at 20 CFR 678.430, SCSEP grantees and sub-recipients must make arrangements through the One-Stop delivery system to provide eligible and ineligible individuals with referrals to WIOA career and training services and access to other activities and programs carried out by other One-Stop partners.
