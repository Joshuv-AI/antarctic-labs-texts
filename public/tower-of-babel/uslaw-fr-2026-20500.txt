South Atlantic Fishery Management Council--Public Meetings

AGENCY: National Marine Fisheries Service (NMFS), National Oceanic and 
Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), Commerce.

ACTION: Notice of a public meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The South Atlantic Fishery Management Council (Council) will 
host a meeting of the Wreckfish Advisory Panel (AP) on November 4 
through November 6, 2026.

DATES: The meeting will be held from 8:30 a.m. until 5 p.m. EDT on 
November 4 and November 5 and from 8:30 a.m. until 12 p.m. on November 
6, 2026.

ADDRESSES: 
    Council address: South Atlantic Fishery Management Council, 4055 
Faber Place Drive, Suite 201, N Charleston, SC 29405.
    Meeting Address: The AP meeting will be held at the Town & Country 
Inn and Suites; 2008 Savannah Hwy., Charleston, SC 29407; phone: (843) 
571-1000. The meeting will also be available via webinar. Registration 
is required. Webinar registration, an online public comment form, and 
briefing book materials will be available 2 weeks prior to the meetings 
at: https://safmc.net/advisory-panel-meetings/.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Christina Curtis, Fishery Social 
Scientist, SAFMC; phone 843/302-8437 or toll free 866/SAFMC-10; FAX 
843/769-4520; email: [email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The Wreckfish AP will receive an update on 
the Council's resilient fisheries project using adaptive implementable 
management to develop a stakeholder driven management strategy for the 
wreckfish fishery. The AP will work with project developers to develop 
management recommendations that are robust to future uncertainly using 
decision support tools that explore various management measures and 
harvest control rules. The AP will also review a draft of the second 
subsequent Wreckfish Individual Transferable Quota Program Review and 
provide input on conclusions and recommendations.

Special Accommodations

    These meetings are physically accessible to people with 
disabilities. Requests for auxiliary aids should be directed to the 
council office (see ADDRESSES) 5 days prior to the meeting.

    Note:  The times and sequence specified in this agenda are 
subject to change.
