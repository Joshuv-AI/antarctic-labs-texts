51 U.S.C. § 60144 — Reimbursement for assistance
Citation: us:usc:51:60144
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section60144
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 601 (Land Remote Sensing Policy); Subchapter V (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator, the Secretary of Defense, and the heads of other United States Government agencies may provide assistance to land remote sensing system operators under the provisions of this chapter. Substantial assistance shall be reimbursed by the operator, except as otherwise provided by law.
