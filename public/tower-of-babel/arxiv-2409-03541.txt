Submodularity of Mutual Information for Multivariate Gaussian Sources with Additive Noise
George Crowley, Iñaki Esnaola

Abstract
. Sensor placement approaches in networks often involve using information-theoretic measures such as entropy and mutual information. We prove that mutual information abides by submodularity and is non-decreasing when considering the mutual information between the states of the network and a subset of k k nodes subjected to additive white Gaussian noise. We prove this under the assumption that the states follow a non-degenerate multivariate Gaussian distribution.

2.
Submodularity

We begin by introducing the definitions of non-decreasing and submodular set functions.

Definition 2

(Definition 2.1
[
2
]
)
.

Given a finite set
\Omega
, a real-valued function
z
on the set of subsets of
\Omega
is called submodular if

(8)

\displaystyle z(\mathcal{A})+z(\mathcal{B})\geq z(\mathcal{A}\cup\mathcal{B})+z(\mathcal{A}\cap\mathcal{B}),\quad\forall\mathcal{A},\mathcal{B}\subseteq\Omega.

We shall often make use of the incremental value of adding element
j
to the set
\mathcal{S}
, let
\rho_{j}(\mathcal{S})=z(\mathcal{S}\cup\{j\})-z(\mathcal{S}).

Proposition 1

(Proposition 2.1
[
2
]
)
.

Each of the following statements is equivalent and defines a submodular set function.

(i)

z(\mathcal{A})+z(\mathcal{B})\geq z(\mathcal{A}\cup\mathcal{B})+z(\mathcal{A}\cap\mathcal{B}),\quad\forall\mathcal{A},\mathcal{B}\subseteq\Omega.

(ii)

\rho_{j}(\mathcal{S})\geq\rho_{j}(\mathcal{T}),\quad\forall\mathcal{S}\subseteq\mathcal{T}\subseteq\Omega,\quad\forall j\in\Omega\setminus\mathcal{T}.

Condition
(ii)
can be re-written as

(9)

\displaystyle z(\mathcal{S}\cup\{j\})-z(\mathcal{S})\geq z(\mathcal{T}\cup\{j\})-z(\mathcal{T}),\quad\forall\mathcal{S}\subseteq\mathcal{T}\subseteq\Omega,\quad\forall j\in\Omega\setminus\mathcal{T}.

Proposition 2

(Proposition 2.2
[
2
]
)
.

Each of the following statements is equivalent and defines a non-decreasing submodular set function.

(i’)

Submodularity:
z(\mathcal{A})+z(\mathcal{B})\geq z(\mathcal{A}\cup\mathcal{B})+z(\mathcal{A}\cap\mathcal{B}),\quad\forall\mathcal{A},\mathcal{B}\subseteq\Omega.

Non-decreasing:
z(\mathcal{A})\leq z(\mathcal{B}),\quad\forall\mathcal{A}\subseteq\mathcal{B}\subseteq\Omega.

\displaystyle{\bf H}_{\{j\}}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}\Big(\begin{pmatrix}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}^{{\sf T}},&{\bf H}_{\Gamma}^{{\sf T}}\end{pmatrix}\left({\bf H}_{\mathcal{T}}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{T}}^{{\sf T}}+\sigma^{2}{\bf I}_{t}\right)^{-1}\begin{pmatrix}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}\\
{\bf H}_{\Gamma}\end{pmatrix}

\displaystyle-{\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}^{{\sf T}}\left({\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}^{{\sf T}}+\sigma^{2}{\bf I}_{s}\right)^{-1}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}\Big)\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\{j\}}^{{\sf T}}\geq 0.

Observe that we can further manipulate the inequality in (

\displaystyle{\bf H}_{\{j\}}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}\Big[\begin{pmatrix}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}^{{\sf T}},&{\bf H}_{\Gamma}^{{\sf T}}\end{pmatrix}\left({\bf H}_{\mathcal{T}}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{T}}^{{\sf T}}+\sigma^{2}{\bf I}_{t}\right)^{-1}\begin{pmatrix}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}\\

{\bf H}_{\Gamma}\end{pmatrix}\Big]\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\{j\}}^{{\sf T}}\geq 0.

It then follows after using (

(34)

\displaystyle{\bf H}_{\{j\}}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{T}}^{{\sf T}}\Bigg[\left({\bf H}_{\mathcal{T}}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{T}}^{{\sf T}}+\sigma^{2}{\bf I}_{t}\right)^{-1}

\displaystyle-\begin{pmatrix}\left({\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}^{{\sf T}}+\sigma^{2}{\bf I}_{s}\right)^{-1}&{\bf 0}\\
{\bf 0}^{{\sf T}}&0*{\bf I}_{\gamma}\end{pmatrix}\Bigg]{\bf H}_{\mathcal{T}}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\{j\}}^{{\sf T}}\geq 0.

The inequality holds if the matrix inside is positive semi-definite, i.e.

(35)

\displaystyle\Bigg(\left({\bf H}_{\mathcal{T}}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{T}}^{{\sf T}}+\sigma^{2}{\bf I}_{t}\right)^{-1}

\displaystyle-\begin{pmatrix}\left({\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}^{{\sf T}}+\sigma^{2}{\bf I}_{s}\right)^{-1}&{\bf 0}\\
{\bf 0}^{{\sf T}}&0*{\bf I}_{\gamma}\end{pmatrix}\Bigg)\succeq 0.

The block form of
{\bf H}_{\mathcal{T}}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{T}}^{{\sf T}}+\sigma^{2}{\bf I}_{t}
can be expressed as

(36)

\displaystyle{\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}\cup\Gamma}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}\cup\Gamma}^{{\sf T}}+\sigma^{2}{\bf I}_{s+\gamma}

\displaystyle=\begin{pmatrix}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}^{{\sf T}}+\sigma^{2}{\bf I}_{s}&{\hbox{cov}}({\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}X^{n},{\bf H}_{\Gamma}X^{n})&\\
\\
\left({\hbox{cov}}({\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}X^{n},{\bf H}_{\Gamma}X^{n})\right)^{{\sf T}}&{\bf H}_{\Gamma}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\Gamma}^{{\sf T}}+\sigma^{2}{\bf I}_{\gamma}\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}{\bf A}&{\bf B}\\
\\
{\bf B}^{{\sf T}}&{\bf C}\end{pmatrix}.

Using Lemma
31
, with
{\bf A}={\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}^{{\sf T}}+\sigma^{2}{\bf I}_{s}
,
{\bf B}
and
{\bf D}
as indicated from (

(37)

\displaystyle\left({\bf H}_{S\cup\Gamma}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}\cup\Gamma}^{{\sf T}}+\sigma^{2}{\bf I}_{s+\gamma}\right)^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}{\bf A}^{-1}&{\bf 0}\\
{\bf 0}&{\bf 0}\end{pmatrix}+\begin{pmatrix}-{\bf A}^{-1}{\bf B}\\
{\bf I}_{\gamma}\end{pmatrix}\left({\bf D}-{\bf B}^{{\sf T}}{\bf A}^{-1}{\bf B}\right)^{-1}\begin{pmatrix}-{\bf B}^{{\sf T}}{\bf A}^{-1},&{\bf I}_{\gamma}\end{pmatrix}.

Inserting equation (

) yields the condition

(38)

\displaystyle\begin{pmatrix}-{\bf A}^{-1}{\bf B}\\
{\bf I}_{\gamma}\end{pmatrix}\left({\bf D}-{\bf B}^{{\sf T}}{\bf A}^{-1}{\bf B}\right)^{-1}\begin{pmatrix}-{\bf B}^{{\sf T}}{\bf A}^{-1},&{\bf I}_{\gamma}\end{pmatrix}\succeq 0.

{\bf A}={\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}^{{\sf T}}+\sigma^{2}{\bf I}_{s}

{\bf A}\succ 0
, and

(39)

\displaystyle\begin{pmatrix}-{\bf A}^{-1}{\bf B}\\

\displaystyle{\bf C}:=\begin{pmatrix}-{\bf A}^{-1}{\bf B}\\

\displaystyle{\bf C}\left({\bf D}-{\bf B}^{{\sf T}}{\bf A}^{-1}{\bf B}\right)^{-1}{\bf C}^{{\sf T}}\succeq 0\iff\left({\bf D}-{\bf B}^{{\sf T}}{\bf A}^{-1}{\bf B}\right)^{-1}\succ 0\iff{\bf D}-{\bf B}^{{\sf T}}{\bf A}^{-1}{\bf B}\succ 0.

Proof of condition (3).

Using the same notation as before, the non-decreasing property states

(42)

\displaystyle z(\mathcal{S})\leq z(\mathcal{T}),\quad\forall\mathcal{S}\subseteq\mathcal{T}\subseteq{\mathcal{V}}.

In our formulation, the non-decreasing property yields as

(43)

\displaystyle\dfrac{1}{2}\log\left(\dfrac{1}{\sigma^{2s}}\Det\left({\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}^{{\sf T}}+\sigma^{2}{\bf I}_{s}\right)\right)\leq\dfrac{1}{2}\log\left(\dfrac{1}{\sigma^{2t}}\Det\left({\bf H}_{\mathcal{T}}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{T}}^{{\sf T}}+\sigma^{2}{\bf I}_{t}\right)\right).

First, let us assume that
\mathcal{S}=\mathcal{T}
, then the equality holds trivially. Hence, we assume that
\mathcal{T}=\mathcal{S}\cup\Gamma
, then using the monotonicity of the logarithm, it follows that

(44)

\displaystyle\dfrac{1}{\sigma^{2s}}\Det\left({\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}^{{\sf T}}+\sigma^{2}{\bf I}_{s}\right)\leq\dfrac{1}{\sigma^{2t}}\Det\left({\bf H}_{\mathcal{T}}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{T}}^{{\sf T}}+\sigma^{2}{\bf I}_{t}\right).

We set the block matrix
{\bf M}
as

(45)

\displaystyle{\bf M}={\bf H}_{\mathcal{T}}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{T}}^{{\sf T}}+\sigma^{2}{\bf I}_{t}

\displaystyle=\begin{pmatrix}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}^{{\sf T}}+\sigma^{2}{\bf I}_{s}&{\hbox{cov}}({\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}X^{n},{\bf H}_{\Gamma}X^{n})\\
\\
\left({\hbox{cov}}({\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}X^{n},{\bf H}_{\Gamma}X^{n})\right)^{{\sf T}}&{\bf H}_{\Gamma}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\Gamma}^{{\sf T}}+\sigma^{2}{\bf I}_{\gamma}\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}{\bf A}&{\bf B}\\
{\bf C}&{\bf D}\end{pmatrix},

then, by Lemma
1
, it follows that

(46)

\displaystyle{\hbox{det}}({\bf M})

\displaystyle={\hbox{det}}({\bf A}){\hbox{det}}({\bf D}-{\bf C}{\bf A}^{-1}{\bf B})

(47)

\displaystyle={\hbox{det}}\left({\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}^{{\sf T}}+\sigma^{2}{\bf I}_{s}\right){\hbox{det}}({\bf D}-{\bf C}{\bf A}^{-1}{\bf B}).

Using (
47
) in (
44
) yields

(48)

\displaystyle\dfrac{1}{\sigma^{2s}}{\hbox{det}}\left({\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}^{{\sf T}}+\sigma^{2}{\bf I}_{s}\right)\leq\dfrac{1}{\sigma^{2t}}{\hbox{det}}\left({\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}^{{\sf T}}+\sigma^{2}{\bf I}_{s}\right){\hbox{det}}({\bf D}-{\bf C}{\bf A}^{-1}{\bf B}).

Since
{\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}^{{\sf T}}+\sigma^{2}{\bf I}_{s}\succ 0\implies{\hbox{det}}\left({\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}^{{\sf T}}+\sigma^{2}{\bf I}_{s}\right)>0
, we can divide this term out of (
48
) such that

(49)

\displaystyle\dfrac{1}{\sigma^{2s}}\leq\dfrac{1}{\sigma^{2t}}{\hbox{det}}({\bf D}-{\bf C}{\bf A}^{-1}{\bf B}),

and hence, using
t=s+\gamma
and fully expanding all the terms, (
49
) can be written as

(50)

\displaystyle{\hbox{det}}\left({\bf H}_{\Gamma}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\Gamma}^{{\sf T}}+\sigma^{2}{\bf I}_{\gamma}-\left({\hbox{cov}}({\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}X^{n},{\bf H}_{\Gamma}X^{n})\right)^{{\sf T}}\left({\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}^{{\sf T}}+\sigma^{2}{\bf I}_{s}\right)^{-1}{\hbox{cov}}({\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}X^{n},{\bf H}_{\Gamma}X^{n})\right)\geq\sigma^{2\gamma}.

Set
{\bf A}=\sigma^{2}{\bf I}_{\gamma}
and
{\bf B}={\bf H}_{\Gamma}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\Gamma}^{{\sf T}}-\left({\hbox{cov}}({\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}X^{n},{\bf H}_{\Gamma}X^{n})\right)^{{\sf T}}\left({\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}^{{\sf T}}+\sigma^{2}{\bf I}_{s}\right)^{-1}{\hbox{cov}}({\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}X^{n},{\bf H}_{\Gamma}X^{n})
. We omit temporarily showing that

, but will invoke Lemma
5
on (

\displaystyle{\hbox{det}}({\bf A}+{\bf B})\geq{\hbox{det}}({\bf A})+{\hbox{det}}({\bf B})\geq\sigma^{2\gamma}.

, we have

(52)

\displaystyle{\hbox{det}}({\bf A}+{\bf B})\geq\sigma^{2\gamma}+{\hbox{det}}({\bf B})\geq\sigma^{2\gamma}\implies{\hbox{det}}({\bf B})\geq 0\iff{\bf B}\succeq 0.

is semi-positive definite. We can write the joint random vector of

\displaystyle\left({\bf H}_{\Gamma}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\Gamma}^{{\sf T}}-{\bf H}_{\Gamma}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}^{{\sf T}}\left({\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}^{{\sf T}}+\sigma^{2}{\bf I}_{s}\right)^{-1}{\bf H}_{\mathcal{S}}\hbox{\boldmath$\Sigma$}{\bf H}_{\Gamma}^{{\sf T}}\right)^{-1}
