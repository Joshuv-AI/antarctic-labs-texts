33 C.F.R. § 150.509 — How must emergency equipment be tested and inspected?
Citation: us:cfr:33:150.509
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-150.509
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 150 (Deepwater Ports: Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All lifesaving, firefighting, and other emergency equipment at a deepwater port must be tested and inspected under this subpart.
