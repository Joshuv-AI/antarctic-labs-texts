2 C.F.R. § 1201.112 — Conflict of interest.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1201.112
Creator: Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1201.112
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1201 (Uniform Administrative Requirements, Cost Principles, And Audit Requirements For Federal Awards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The DOT Component making a financial assistance award must establish conflict of interest policies for Federal awards, including policies from DOT Headquarters. The non-Federal entity must disclose in writing any potential conflict of interest to the DOT Component or pass-through entity in accordance with applicable Federal awarding agency policy.
