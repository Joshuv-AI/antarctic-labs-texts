2 U.S.C. § 6152 — Appointment and compensation of employees by Secretaries for Senate Majority and Minority; gross compensation
Citation: us:usc:2:6152
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section6152
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 61 (Senate Leadership); Subchapter IV (Majority And Minority Secretaries, Conferences, And Policy Committees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Effective October 1, 1977, the Secretary for the Majority and the Secretary for the Minority are each authorized to appoint and fix the compensation of such employees as they deem appropriate: Provided, That the gross compensation paid to such employees shall not exceed $143,200 each fiscal year for each Secretary.
