2 U.S.C. § 1881d — Effective date
Citation: us:usc:2:1881d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1881d
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 28 (Architect Of The Capitol); Subchapter V (Capitol-Flown Flags For Families Of Fallen Heroes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This subchapter shall take effect on May 16, 2016, except that a Capitol-flown flag may not be provided under section 1881a of this title until the regulations issued under section 1881b(a) of this title take effect in accordance with section 1881b(b) of this title.
