49 C.F.R. § 174.82 — General requirements for the handling of placarded rail cars, transport vehicles, freight containers, and bulk packages.
Citation: us:cfr:49:174.82
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-174.82
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 174 (Carriage By Rail). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Unless otherwise specified, this subpart does not apply to the handling of rail cars, transport vehicles, freight containers, or bulk packagings, which contain Division 1.6, combustible liquids, Division 6.1 PG III materials, or Class 9 materials.
(b) A placarded rail car, transport vehicle, freight container, or bulk package may not be transported in a passenger train.
