48 C.F.R. § 32.008 — 32.008 Notification of overpayment.
Citation: us:cfr:48:32.008
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-32.008
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 32 (Contract Financing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the contractor notifies the contracting officer of a duplicate payment or that the Government has otherwise overpaid, the contracting officer shall follow the procedures at 32.604.
