5 U.S.C. § 596 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:5:596
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section596
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part I (The Agencies Generally); Chapter 5 (Administrative Procedure); Subchapter V (Administrative Conference Of The United States). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out this subchapter not more than $3,200,000 for fiscal year 2009, $3,200,000 for fiscal year 2010, and $3,200,000 for fiscal year 2011. Of any amounts appropriated under this section, not more than $2,500 may be made available in each fiscal year for official representation and entertainment expenses for foreign dignitaries.
