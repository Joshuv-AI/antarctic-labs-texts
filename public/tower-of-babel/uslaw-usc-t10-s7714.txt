10 U.S.C. § 7714 — Collection of captured flags, standards, and colors
Citation: us:usc:10:7714
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7714
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Procurement); Chapter 775 (Disposition Of Effects Of Deceased Persons; Captured Flags). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Army shall have sent to him all flags, standards, and colors taken by the Army from enemies of the United States.
