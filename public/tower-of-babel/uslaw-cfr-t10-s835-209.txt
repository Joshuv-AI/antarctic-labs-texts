10 C.F.R. § 835.209 — Concentrations of radioactive material in air.
Citation: us:cfr:10:835.209
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-835.209
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 835 (Occupational Radiation Protection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The derived air concentration (DAC) values given in appendices A and C of this part shall be used in the control of occupational exposures to airborne radioactive material.
(b) The estimation of internal dose shall be based on bioassay data rather than air concentration values unless bioassay data are:
(1) Unavailable;
(2) Inadequate; or
(3) Internal dose estimates based on air concentration values are demonstrated to be as or more accurate.
