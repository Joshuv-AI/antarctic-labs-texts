48 C.F.R. § 613.307-70 — 613.307-70 File folders for purchase orders, delivery orders, blanket purchase agreements, and purchase card transactions.
Citation: us:cfr:48:613.307-70
Creator: Chapter 6—Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-613.307-70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 613 (Simplified Acquisition Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contracting officers shall use Forms DS-1918, Purchase Order File; DS-1919, Delivery Order File; DS-1920, Blanket Purchase Agreement (BPA) File; and DS-3014, Purchase Card Transaction File (Actions Exceeding $3,000 Through $25,000), to record relevant data and document those acquisitions, respectively.
