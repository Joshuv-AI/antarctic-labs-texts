8 C.F.R. § 251.5 — Arrival and departure manifests for crew.
Citation: us:cfr:8:251.5
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-251.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 251 (Arrival And Departure Manifests And Lists: Supporting Documents). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to the electronic manifest transmission requirement applicable to crew members specified in §§ 231.1 and 231.2 of this chapter, the master or commanding officer, or authorized agent, owner, or consignee, of a commercial vessel or commercial aircraft arriving in or departing from the United States must submit arrival and departure manifests in accordance with §§ 251.1, 251.3, and 251.4.
