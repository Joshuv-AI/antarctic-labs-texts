7 C.F.R. § 52.803 — Sample unit size.
Citation: us:cfr:7:52.803
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-52.803
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 52 (Processed Fruits And Vegetables, Processed Products Thereof, And Certain Other Processed Food Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Compliance with requirements for size and the various quality factors is based on the following sample unit sizes for the applicable factor:
(a) Pits, character, and harmless extraneous material—20 ounces of drained cherries.
(b) Size, color, and defects (other than harmless extraneous material)—100 cherries.
