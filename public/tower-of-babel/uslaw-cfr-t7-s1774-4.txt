7 C.F.R. § 1774.4 — Allocation of funds.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1774.4
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1774.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1774 (Special Evaluation Assistance For Rural Communities And Households Program (Search)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary may use not more than four percent of the total amount of funds made available for a fiscal year for water and waste disposal activities for SEARCH grants.
