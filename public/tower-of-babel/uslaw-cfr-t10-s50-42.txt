10 C.F.R. § 50.42 — Additional standard for class 103 licenses.
Citation: us:cfr:10:50.42
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-50.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 50 (Domestic Licensing Of Production And Utilization Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In determining whether a class 103 license will be issued to an applicant, the Commission will, in addition to applying the standards set forth in § 50.40, consider whether the proposed activities will serve a useful purpose proportionate to the quantities of special nuclear material or source material to be utilized.
