11 U.S.C. § 307 — United States trustee
Citation: us:usc:11:307
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section307
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 3 (Case Administration); Subchapter I (Commencement Of A Case). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States trustee may raise and may appear and be heard on any issue in any case or proceeding under this title but may not file a plan pursuant to section 1121(c) of this title.
