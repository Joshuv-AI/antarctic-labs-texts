12 C.F.R. § 625.21 — Filing and service of documents.
Citation: us:cfr:12:625.21
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-625.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 625 (Application For Award Of Fees And Other Expenses Under The Equal Access To Justice Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any application for an award or other pleading or document related to an application shall be filed and served on all parties to the adversary adjudication in the same manner as other pleadings in the adversary adjudication (see §§ 622.18 and 622.19 of this chapter), except as provided in § 625.11(b) of this part for confidential financial information.
