5 C.F.R. § 847.903 — How is the monthly reduction to the retirement annuity computed?
Citation: us:cfr:5:847.903
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-847.903
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 847 (Elections Of Retirement Coverage By Current And Former Employees Of Nonappropriated Fund Instrumentalities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The reduction equals:
(1) The difference in the present value of the immediate annuity with credit for NAFI service and the deferred annuity without credit for NAFI service, divided by
(2) The present value factor for the retiree's attained age (in full years) at the time of retirement.
(b) The reduction computed in paragraph (a) of this section is rounded to the next higher dollar.
