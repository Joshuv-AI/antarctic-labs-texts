A hybrid sparrow optimization Kriging model and its application in geological modeling
Authors: Xiaonan Shi, Yumo Wang, Haoran Wu, Aoqian Wang
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
AbstractWith the proposal of intelligent mines, the demand for drilling is increasing daily. Therefore, it is particularly crucial to gather more geological data by interpolation of limited drilling data for subsequent three-dimensional geological modeling. In this paper, a hybrid sparrow optimization Kriging model (HSSA), in which chaos theory and Levy flight are integrated into the initial population update algorithm of the sparrow algorithm and the location update algorithm of the entrants, is proposed. Next, the golden sine optimization algorithm is introduced into the reconnaissance and early warning mechanism of the sparrow algorithm to further improve the accuracy and local escape ability. By the correlation optimization of the original sparrow algorithm, the speed and accuracy of swarm intelligence optimization are further improved. In addition, the model solves the parameters of the variation function of the ordinary Kriging interpolation and reduces the generation error of the formation data interpolation. The results of relevant experiments show that the hybrid sparrow optimization Kriging model improves the accuracy and convergence speed compared with other swarm intelligence algorithms and that the accuracy of this model is improved by 8.4% compared with the original Kriging interpolation algorithm. Based on the hybrid sparrow optimization Kriging model, we propose a three-dimensional stratigraphic model for the Yangchangwan Coal Mine, which provides further support for mining operations and three-dimensional stratigraphic research in this area. The accuracy and applicability of the hybrid sparrow optimization Kriging model are further explained using a case study with the stratigraphic model data in the Yangchangwan Coal Mine. HSSA with significant potential for applications in industries such as coal mining and geological exploration. In these fields, the efficient acquisition, processing, and modeling of stratigraphic data are critical for enhancing geological interpretation and optimizing operational workflows.Keywords: Kriging, Sparrow optimization algorithm, Drilling, Three-dimensional geological modelingSubject terms: Mathematics and computing, Computer science

Introduction

With the ongoing development of geological and environmental engineering technology and the proposal of intelligent mines, the demand for high-precision mine geology is increasing. As the main raw data of three-dimensional geological modeling, the relevance of drilling is self-evident. However, due to the high cost of drilling, it is impossible to obtain numerous drilling data to construct a fine three-dimensional geological model in actual engineering. Thus, it is particularly important to obtain more geological data by limited drilling data interpolation for subsequent three-dimensional geological modeling. The Kriging method is widely utilized in interpolation algorithms1, and among various Kriging methods, the ordinary Kriging method is more frequently utilized. However, the ordinary Kriging method has some shortcomings in the actual complex geological environment; for example, it is difficult to solve the Kriging equation and to select the appropriate value of parameters.

To address the problem that the Kriging interpolation model parameters commonly employ empirical values, which leads to unstable interpolation accuracy, the crowd intelligence method can be used to select the ideal parameters in the ordinary Kriging interpolation model. The crowd intelligence algorithm is an algorithm for addressing the problem of global optimal solution search by developing the complex behavior of individual and collective populations according to the life law of biological groups2. The crowd intelligence algorithm is widely applied in various fields, including information security3,4 and neural networks5. In recent years, crowd intelligence algorithms have rapidly grown, and several crowd intelligence algorithms have arisen, such as the Gray Wolf Optimizer (GWO)6, Whale Optimization Algorithm (WOA)7, Harris Hawk Optimization (HHO)8 and the sparrow algorithm9. GWO, WOA, and HHO each offer advantages in metaheuristic, with GWO excelling in exploration–exploitation balance, WOA being effective in handling high-dimensional spaces and global optimization, and HHO demonstrating strong convergence speed and adaptive behavior in complex optimization problems. However, most crowd intelligence algorithms may quickly settle to the local optimum rather than the global optimum in the search process, resulting in low accuracy of solution findings. Therefore, many scientists have focused on the advancement of crowd intelligence optimization algorithms10–18. The Sparrow Search Algorithm (SSA) offers fast search speed and great accuracy compared with other search algorithms and has been employed in numerous fields in recent years. However, the SSA also has some limitations, such as the low fraction of global development and limited search breadth. To address this difficulty, Dunqiao Li19 used the reverse opposition-based learning strategy and the Metropolis criterion of the simulated annealing algorithm to increase the convergence speed and greater solution accuracy of the Sparrow method. Jingyu Huang20 combined the tent chaotic map, t-distribution and other optimization algorithms with the sparrow algorithm to improve the convergence accuracy of the algorithm. Although the optimization direction of the SSA mainly focuses on the improvement in the related optimization search ability, the SSA still requires additional investigation in the direction of expanding the development scope, balancing the global development ability, and boosting individual diversification.

For stratum data interpolation modeling, Feng Zhang21 employed the Kriging spatial interpolation method to conduct stratum interpolation and utilized a generalized tri-prism modeling approach to construct a three-dimensional stratum model.

Peixian Li22 established a FLAC3D modeling method, which also applied Kriging spatial interpolation to generate mesh data files needed for modeling. All the aforementioned studies employed Kriging spatial interpolation to accomplish stratum data interpolation; however, there is still potential for optimization of the variogram function of Kriging spatial interpolation. The proposed approach by Fengtao Qu23 utilizes a combination of CGAN and swarm intelligence optimization to effectively optimize data interpolation technology in well log data imputation methods.

Therefore, to address the lack of drilling data and to obtain the optimal parameters of the variogram in the ordinary Kriging interpolation algorithm, this paper utilized the optimization ability of the crowd intelligence algorithm and integrated the sparrow algorithm to obtain the optimal parameters of the variogram in the ordinary Kriging interpolation algorithm. In this paper, we offer the Hybrid Sparrow Optimization Kriging Model (HSSAK). By combining the circle chaotic map, Levy flying, and golden sine optimization technique, the overall development and partial exploration of Sparrow are balanced. For ordinary Kriging interpolation, variogram parameters are obtained to solve the ordinary Kriging interpolation algorithm in the variogram parameters problem so that the formation data interpolation error is reduced. Furthermore, the algorithm is applied to the actual stratum data interpolation processing for the Yangchangwan Coal Mine, and the stratum virtual drilling, three-dimensional stratum map of the study area, which provides further support for mining operations and three-dimensional stratum research in this area, is established. Compared with the actual stratum situation and the stratum model of standard Kriging interpolation, the accuracy and usefulness of the optimization algorithm for virtual drilling interpolation are reviewed.

Experiment

The experimental context of this paper is described as follows: 11th Gen Intel Core i5-1135G7 CPU with 16 GB RAM, Windows 10 operating system, and Visual Studio Code software v1.92.2 (https://code.visualstudio.com/updates/v1_92).

In this study, two experiments are designed to examine the advantages and disadvantages of HSSAK from two dimensions. First, by comparing with other swarm intelligence algorithms in 23 unit test functions, the HSSA algorithm is verified to have better global solving ability and a more stable development speed. The selected benchmark functions for this study are 23 test functions from the Congress on Evolutionary Computation (CEC). These 23 functions, chosen by the CEC, provide a comprehensive evaluation of the strengths and weaknesses of genetic evolutionary algorithms from multiple perspectives. For instance, unimodal functions assess the algorithm’s ability to converge to a single solution, while multimodal functions challenge the algorithm’s capacity to explore the search landscape and avoid being trapped in local optima. Some functions have separable variables that can be optimized independently, whereas others feature tightly coupled variables (non-separable), simulating real-world scenarios where variables interact in complex ways. These functions range from simple to highly complex, ensuring a thorough evaluation of the algorithm’s performance.

In the second experiment, the interpolation simulation is carried out using actual coal seam data, and the actual error range is measured by comparing the ordinary Kriging interpolation, Universal Kriging Interpolation and the optimized Kriging interpolation with the original coal seam data to measure the actual error range and compare the accuracy and practicability of the optimized Kriging interpolation in the practical application process.

As shown in Table 3, for high-dimensional unimodal functions F1–F7, HSSA demonstrated significant advantages in optimization accuracy and convergence speed. Compared to SSA, the average value and standard deviation of HSSA increased by several orders of magnitude, making the analysis results more reliable. Additionally, as illustrated in Figs. 2, 3, 4, HSSA exhibited extremely fast convergence speed; it took only one to five iterations on average to achieve optimal values while other swarm intelligence algorithms required between fifty to sixty iterations. Overall, when compared with other swarm intelligence algorithms’ optimization performance on high-dimensional unimodal functions, HSSA ranked first.

For high-dimensional multimodal test function from F8-F13, the average fitness and standard deviation of HSSA remained ahead of other swarm intelligence algorithms; it achieved a perfect score (100% optimization) for F9 and F11. As depicted in Figs. 4, 5, 6, HSSA still led in terms of optimization ability and stability since it was optimal within an average range of one-to-ten iterations.

For the fixed-dimensional test function, HSSA consistently ranks first in F14–F18 and F21–F23 functions, while Hartman’s Family performs poorly in F19–F20. However, when optimizing the Kriging function, the main performance indicators are based on high-dimensional unimodal test functions. Therefore, Hartman’s Family tends to fall into local optima, which has minimal impact on the error of optimizing the Kriging function. As shown in Figs. 6, 7, 8, 9, for fixed-dimensional test functions, HSSA gradually converges with other swarm intelligence algorithms in terms of convergence speed and accuracy. This is because the large jump float resulting from the Levi flight strategy hindered the maintenance of stability for the corresponding optimal value in this fixed dimension. Such a strategy is beneficial for optimizing the Kriging function for large-scale strati graphic interpolation but does not perform well for small-scale fixed-dimensional algorithms.

Additionally, a comparison of the SSA and the HSSA was conducted to assess their performance and resource consumption using the F1 test function. Under this benchmark, the average runtime of SSA was 1.6036 s, while HSSA achieved a significantly reduced average runtime of 0.1835 s, representing an 88.55% reduction in computational time. In terms of memory consumption, SSA utilized 8356.0 KB, whereas HSSA required 8652.0 KB, indicating an increase of 296 KB. Despite the slight rise in memory usage, the substantial decrease in runtime was primarily achieved by reducing the exponential operations present in the original Sparrow algorithm, thereby minimizing computational overhead.

In summary, HSSA demonstrates an advantageous initialization through cyclic chaotic strategy optimization on the 23 basic test functions. Additionally, the local perturbation of Levy flight and golden sine strategy accelerates convergence speed and enhances optimization accuracy, effectively balancing both local and global search capabilities. HSSA exhibits a remarkable ability to bypass local advantages in order to optimize global development. Based on the results obtained, HSSA exhibits a 43.311% improvement in solution speed and a 7.39% enhancement in accuracy. Furthermore, the method demonstrates an order-of-magnitude increase in stability and a 12.07% improvement in optimization precision when compared to conventional approaches.

Hybrid sparrow optimization Kriging model: experimental comparison

In Experiment 2, the accuracy and usefulness of the optimized Kriging interpolation in practical applications are tested by comparing the difference between the ordinary Kriging, Universal Kriging and the improved Kriging with the original data. Introduction to Yangchangwan Coal Mine

The Yangchangwan Coal Mine is situated within the renowned Ningdong Coalfield, known for its complex geological conditions. The coal seams are uniformly thick, moderately inclined, and exhibit stable deposition characteristics, offering substantial mining value. The mine is primarily located in the Craton Structural Belt, where coal seams are closely associated with geological features such as faults and folds. Overall, the coal seams are stable, making Yangchangwan one of the representative mines in the region.

In terms of topography, the area surrounding the mine is relatively flat, with moderate mine depth and rich surface vegetation. The geological structure is highly representative, making it ideal for advanced mining operations. Currently, Yangchangwan Coal Mine is actively advancing its Intelligent Mines initiative. By integrating cutting-edge technologies such as the Internet of Things (IoT), big data, and cloud computing, the mine is progressively moving towards automation and digitalized management. The combination of digital geological modeling and intelligent mining is not only ensuring efficient and safe production operations but also serving as a model for the digital transformation of the regional coal industry.

Modeling of a three-dimensional stratum

TIN data model

Thomas Peucker31 was the first to introduce the TIN (Triangulated Irregular Network), which has since seen increasingly widespread application in geology. The TIN method approximates topographical surfaces through a series of non-overlapping, non-intersecting triangles, providing a precise representation of terrain features. This approach32 has proven to be a powerful tool for modeling and analyzing complex geological surfaces. These triangular surfaces are usually constructed by triangulation of discrete points in space by Delaunay rules. The data sources include borehole data and topographic and geomorphic data obtained by sensors. The higher the density of triangulation is, the greater the constructed surface model can reflect the real topographic and geomorphic features.

The advantages of the TIN model are that the model construction speed is fast, that the mode construction principle is relatively simple, and that surface models of different precision can be built according to the number of discrete points, which is suitable for some small terrain data quantities and some large terrain data quantities. Compared with the regular grid model, there is no limitation of a more uniform data distribution. The TIN model can be evolved into a TIN data model based on feature lines according to the complexity of the terrain, such as contour lines, fracture lines and other terrain features, which can be well expressed. The topological relationship among points, edges and triangles of the TIN model is relatively simple, the amount of stored data is relatively small, and the access speed is fast. For complex terrain geological bodies, feature lines can be added to construct TIN surface models based on constraints. The established models have high accuracy and are more consistent with the real terrain. The TIN model arbitrarily adjusts the accuracy of the model. When the topography surface fluctuation sharply changes, the TIN model can contain many data points, and the established model can express the fluctuation changes. When the topography surface is relatively flat, the TIN model only needs a small number of data points to express the general topography. The TIN model is quite mature in the field of geological research and has been widely applied in the field of geological modeling, and the accuracy of the constructed model is high. The TIN algorithm is employed to implement 3D strati graphic modeling of the Yangchangwan Coal Mine, clearly illustrating the application scenario of HSSA interpolation.

Conclusions

The purpose of this paper is to address the unreasonable setting of empirical values for variogram parameters or the unstable accuracy of the interpolation results caused by random setting when using ordinary Kriging for borehole interpolation in practical 3D geological modeling applications. This paper proposes a Kriging interpolation algorithm based on a mixed strategy to improve sparrow optimization. The circle chaotic strategy is selected to initialize the sparrow individual, and the chaotic strategy is used to disturb the initial direction of the sparrow individual to make it more diversified. Then, the Levy flight and golden sine algorithm are introduced to balance the proportion of the local solution and global solution so that the HSSA has a better ability to jump out of the local optimum. The effectiveness of the proposed algorithm is verified by comparison with other crowd optimization algorithms based on 23 benchmark test functions. The results show that the proposed algorithm performs well in solving function optimization problems. The final results show that the hybrid optimization sparrow algorithm has obvious advantages in actual Kriging interpolation applications and that the accuracy of this model is improved by 8.4%. In addition, we carried out actual virtual drilling interpolation processing for the drilling part of the Yangchangwan Coal Mine. In the case study of drilling in the Yangchangwan Coal Mine, the accuracy and applicability of the Kriging interpolation algorithm optimized by the mixed strategy sparrow are further explained. In the future development direction, we can focus on the application and research of other fields of the HSSA, increasing testing on datasets from different domains and applying it to practical scenarios in other industries, such as drones and cybersecurity, and put into the practical application in other industrial backgrounds. In addition, we can strengthen the large-scale practical application to verify the actual performance of the hybrid model, or consider the application of optimized ordinary Kriging in other practical fields.
