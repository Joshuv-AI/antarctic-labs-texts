5 C.F.R. § 846.501 — Cancellation upon transfer to FERS.
Citation: us:cfr:5:846.501
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-846.501
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 846 (Federal Employees Retirement System—Elections Of Coverage). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A designation of beneficiary made under § 831.2005 of this chapter is cancelled on the effective date of an election of FERS coverage. Designations of beneficiary under FERS must be made in accordance with § 843.205 of this chapter and apply to an employee's contributions under both CSRS and FERS.
