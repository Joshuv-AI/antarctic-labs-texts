17 C.F.R. § 288.101 — Schedule A. Information required in reports pursuant to § 288.3.
Citation: us:cfr:17:288.101
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 288 (GENERAL RULES AND REGULATIONS PURSUANT TO SECTION 9(a) OF THE AFRICAN DEVELOPMENT BANK ACT). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This schedule specifies the information and documents to be furnished in a report pursuant to Rule 3 (17 CFR 288.3) with respect to a proposed distribution of primary obligations of the Bank. Information not available at the time of filing the report shall be filed as promptly thereafter as possible.
