46 C.F.R. § 185.530 — Responsibilities of licensed individuals.
Citation: us:cfr:46:185.530
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-185.530
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 185 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in the emergency instructions or a station bill required by this subpart exempts any licensed individual from the exercise of good judgment in an emergency situation.
