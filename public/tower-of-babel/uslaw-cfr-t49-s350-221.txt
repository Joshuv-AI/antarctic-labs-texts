49 C.F.R. § 350.221 — MCSAP administration: Availability of funds.
Citation: us:cfr:49:350.221
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-350.221
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 350 (Motor Carrier Safety Assistance Program (Mcsap) And High Priority Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

MCSAP funds obligated to a State will remain available for the Federal fiscal year that the funds are obligated and the next 2 full Federal fiscal years.
