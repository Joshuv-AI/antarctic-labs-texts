48 C.F.R. § 1909.403 — 1909.403 Definitions.

Creator: Chapter 19—Broadcasting Board Of Governors
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1909.403
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1909 (Contractor Qualifications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board Procurement Executive, is designated the “debarring official” and the “suspending official” as defined in FAR 9.403 and is designated as the Board official authorized to make the decisions required in FAR 9.405(a), 9.405-1(b), 9.405-2, 9.406-1(c), and 9.407-1(d).
