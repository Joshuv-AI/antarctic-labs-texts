25 C.F.R. § 81.21 — What are the responsibilities of the Secretarial Election Board in conducting a Secretarial election?
Citation: us:cfr:25:81.21
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-81.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 81 (Secretarial Election Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretarial Election Board conducts the Secretarial election. Except as provided in § 81.43, decisions of the Secretarial Election Board are not subject to administrative appeal.
