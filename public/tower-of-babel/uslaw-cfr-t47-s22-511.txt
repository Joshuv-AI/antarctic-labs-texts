47 C.F.R. § 22.511 — Construction period for the Paging and Radiotelephone Service.
Citation: us:cfr:47:22.511
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-22.511
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 22 (Public Mobile Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The construction period for stations in the Paging and Radiotelephone Service is one year.
