5 C.F.R. § 178.208 — Applicability of general procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:5:178.208
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-178.208
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 178 (Procedures For Settling Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When not in conflict with this subpart, the provisions of subpart A of this part relating to procedures applicable to claims generally are also applicable to the settlement of account of deceased civilian officers and employees.
