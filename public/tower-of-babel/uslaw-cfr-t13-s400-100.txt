13 C.F.R. § 400.100 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:13:400.100
Creator: Emergency Steel Guarantee Loan Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-400.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 400 (Emergency Steel Guarantee Loan Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart describes the Board's authorities and organizational structure, the means and rules by which the Board takes actions, and procedures for public access to Board records.
