30 C.F.R. § 1208.13 — Reporting requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:30:1208.13
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1208.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1208 (Sale Of Federal Royalty Oil). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If ONRR underbills a purchaser under a royalty oil contract because of a payor's underreporting or failure to report on Form MMS-2014 pursuant to § 1210.52 of this chapter, the payor will be liable for payment of such underbilled amounts plus interest if they are unrecoverable from the purchaser or the surety instrument related to the contract.
