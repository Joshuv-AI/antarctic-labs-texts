46 C.F.R. § 24.01-7 — -7 Right of appeal.
Citation: us:cfr:46:24.01-7
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-24.01-7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 24 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person directly affected by a decision or action taken under this subchapter, by or on behalf of the Coast Guard, may appeal therefrom in accordance with subpart 1.03 of this chapter.
