42 U.S.C. § 4370m–9 — Application
Citation: us:usc:42:4370m–9
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section4370m–9
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 55 (National Environmental Policy); Subchapter IV (Federal Permitting Improvement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This subchapter applies to any covered project for which—
(1)
a notice is filed under section 4370m–2(a)(1) of this title; or
(2)
an application or other request for a Federal authorization is pending before a Federal agency 90 days after December 4, 2015.
