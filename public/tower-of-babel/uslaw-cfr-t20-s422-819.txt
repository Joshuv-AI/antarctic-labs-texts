20 C.F.R. § 422.819 — Fees.
Citation: us:cfr:20:422.819
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-422.819
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 422 (Organization And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Federal agencies operating Department of the Treasury-designated debt collection centers are authorized to charge a fee for services rendered regarding referred or transferred debts. The fee may be paid out of amounts collected and may be added to the debt as an administrative cost.
