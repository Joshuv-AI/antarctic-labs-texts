20 C.F.R. § 1010.100 — What is the purpose and scope of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:20:1010.100
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary For Veterans' Employment And Training Service, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-1010.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 1010 (Application Of Priority Of Service For Covered Persons). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Part 1010 contains the Department regulations implementing priority of service for covered persons. Priority of service for covered persons is authorized by section 2(a)(1) of JVA (38 U.S.C. 4215). These regulations fulfill section 605 of the Veterans Benefits, Health Care, and Information Technology Act of 2006, Pub. L. 109-461 (Dec. 22, 2006), which requires the Department to implement priority of service via regulation.
(b) As provided in § 1010.210, this part applies to all qualified job training programs.
