Correction to: “Endogenous Diurnal Patterns of Adrenal and Gonadal Hormones During a 24-Hour Constant Routine After Simulated Shift Work”
Published: 2023

In the above-named article by Kelly MR, Yuen F, Satterfield BC, Auchus RJ, Gaddameedhi S, Van Dongen HPA, and Liu PY (J Endo Society. 2022; 10.1210/jendso/bvac153), there was a typographical error in the supplemental material URL:

In the originally published version of this manuscript, in Reference 29, there was an error in supplemental material URL: “Kelly MR, Yuen F, Satterfield BC, et al. Supplementary materials.

Deposited September 23, 2022. Figshare. https://doi.org/10.6084/m6089.figshare.21200455.” In the corrected article, Reference 29 reads: “Kelly MR, Yuen F, Satterfield BC, et al. Supplementary materials. Deposited September 23, 2022. Figshare. https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.21200455.v1.”

The article has been corrected online.
