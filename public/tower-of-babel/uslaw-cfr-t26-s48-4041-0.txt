26 C.F.R. § 48.4041-0 — -0 Applicability of regulations relating to diesel fuel after December 31, 1993.
Citation: us:cfr:26:48.4041-0
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-48.4041-0
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 48 (Manufacturers And Retailers Excise Taxes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Sections 48.4041-3 through 48.4041-17 do not apply to sales or uses of diesel fuel after December 31, 1993. For rules relating to the diesel fuel tax imposed by section 4041 after that date, see § 48.4082-4.
