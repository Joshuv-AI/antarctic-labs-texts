Expression of concern – Safety and effects of anti-obesity medications on weight loss, cardiometabolic, and psychological outcomes in people living with overweight or obesity: a systematic review and meta-analysis
Authors: The Editors for eClinicalMedicine
Published: 2026

In January, 2025, eClinicalMedicine published the paper Safety and effects of anti-obesity medications on weight loss, cardiometabolic, and psychological outcomes in people living with overweight or obesity: a systematic review and meta-analysis.1

Shortly after that, we received a letter from a reader pointing out some issues regarding the analysis. Based on that the authors confirmed that in several instances, values reported as mean ± SEM were mistakenly interpreted as mean ± SD. The authors are now completing a thorough check of all the data and are working on the re-analysis.

The Editors wish to alert readers to these concerns while we work to resolve this matter; we will provide an update once available.
