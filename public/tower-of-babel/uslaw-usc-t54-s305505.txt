54 U.S.C. § 305505 — Annual report
Citation: us:usc:54:305505
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section305505
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3055 (National Building Museum). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Committee shall submit an annual report to the Secretary and the Administrator of General Services concerning its activities under this chapter and shall provide the Secretary and the Administrator of General Services with such other information as the Secretary may consider necessary or advisable.
