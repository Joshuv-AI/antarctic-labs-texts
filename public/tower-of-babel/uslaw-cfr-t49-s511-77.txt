49 C.F.R. § 511.77 — Restrictions as to former members and employees.
Citation: us:cfr:49:511.77
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-511.77
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 511 (Adjudicative Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The postemployee restrictions applicable to former Administrators and NHTSA employees, as set forth in 18 U.S.C. 207, shall govern the activities of former Administrators and NHTSA employees in matters connected with their former duties and responsibilities.
