43 C.F.R. § 4610.2-3 — -3 Approval of lease; renewal.
Citation: us:cfr:43:4610.2-3
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4610.2-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4600 (Leases Of Grazing Land—Pierce Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Local negotiations for leasing of lands under this act will not be effective until the lease and any renewal thereof has been approved by an authorized officer of the Bureau of Land Management. Upon such approval the lease should be recorded in the land records of the county in which the land is situated.
