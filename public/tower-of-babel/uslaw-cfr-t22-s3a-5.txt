22 C.F.R. § 3a.5 — a.5 Basis for approval or disapproval.
Citation: us:cfr:22:3a.5
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-3a.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 3a (PART 3a—ACCEPTANCE OF EMPLOYMENT FROM FOREIGN GOVERNMENTS BY MEMBERS OF THE UNIFORMED SERVICES). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Decisions by the Director, Bureau of Politico-Military Affairs, under this part shall be based on whether the applicant's proposed employment with a foreign government would adversely affect the foreign relations of the United States, in light of the applicant's official status as a retiree or reservist.
