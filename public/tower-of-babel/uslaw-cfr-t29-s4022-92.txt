29 C.F.R. § 4022.92 — What definitions do I need to know for these rules?
Citation: us:cfr:29:4022.92
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4022.92
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4022 (Benefits Payable In Terminated Single-Employer Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You need to know three definitions from § 4001.2 of this chapter (PBGC, person, and plan) and the following definitions:
“We” means the PBGC.
“You” means the person to whom we may owe benefits at the time of death.
