43 C.F.R. § 1824.11 — How does BLM choose a newspaper in which to publish a notice?
Citation: us:cfr:43:1824.11
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-1824.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 1820 (Application Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

BLM bases its choice of newspapers on their reputation and frequency and level of circulation in the vicinity of the public or private lands involved.
