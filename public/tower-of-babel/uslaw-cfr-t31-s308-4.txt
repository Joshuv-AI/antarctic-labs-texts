31 C.F.R. § 308.4 — Applicable regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:31:308.4
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-308.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 308 (General Regulations Governing Full-Paid Interim Certificates). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as may otherwise be provided, and in so far as applicable, the general regulations of the Treasury Department, as contained in part 306 of this subchapter, as amended or revised, shall apply to full-paid interim certificates.
