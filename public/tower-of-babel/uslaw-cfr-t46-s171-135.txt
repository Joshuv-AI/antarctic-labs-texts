46 C.F.R. § 171.135 — Weather deck drainage on a vessel of 100 gross tons or more.
Citation: us:cfr:46:171.135
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-171.135
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 171 (Special Rules Pertaining To Vessels Carrying Passengers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The weather deck must have freeing ports, open rails, and scuppers, as necessary, to allow rapid clearing of water under all weather conditions.
