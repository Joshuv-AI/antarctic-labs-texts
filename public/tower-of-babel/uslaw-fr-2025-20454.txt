Sunshine Act Meetings

TIME AND DATE: Week of November 17, 2025.

PLACE: Via Teleconference.

STATUS: Open.

Week of November 17, 2025

Tuesday, November 18, 2025.

1:00 p.m. Affirmation Session (Public Meeting) (Tentative), Final 
Rule--Streamlining Select Rules of Practice and Procedure (RIN 3150-
AL54) (Contact: Wesley Held: 301-287-3591)

ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: By a vote of 3-0 on November 18, 2025, the 
Commission determined pursuant to 5 U.S.C. 552b(e)(1) and 10 CFR 9.107

live; via teleconference. Details for joining the teleconference in 
listen only mode at https://www.nrc.gov/pmns/mtg.
