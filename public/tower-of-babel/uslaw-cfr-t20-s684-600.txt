20 C.F.R. § 684.600 — To whom is the Indian and Native American program grantee accountable for the provision of services and the expenditure of Indian and Native American funds?
Citation: us:cfr:20:684.600
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-684.600
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 684 (Indian And Native American Programs Under Title I Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The INA program grantee is responsible to the INA community to be served by INA funds.
(b) The INA program grantee also is responsible to the Department of Labor, which is charged by law with ensuring that all WIOA funds are expended:
(1) According to applicable laws and regulations;
(2) For the benefit of the identified INA client group; and
(3) For the purposes approved in the grantee's plan and signed grant document.
