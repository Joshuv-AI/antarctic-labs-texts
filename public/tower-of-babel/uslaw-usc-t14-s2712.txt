14 U.S.C. § 2712 — Payment for the apprehension of stragglers
Citation: us:usc:14:2712
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2712
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 27 (Pay, Allowances, Awards, And Other Rights And Benefits); Subchapter I (Personnel Rights And Benefits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Coast Guard may offer and pay rewards for the apprehension and delivery of deserters, stragglers, and prisoners.
