12 U.S.C. § 5012 — Effect on other law
Citation: us:usc:12:5012
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section5012
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 50 (Check Truncation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter shall supersede any provision of Federal or State law, including the Uniform Commercial Code, that is inconsistent with this chapter, but only to the extent of the inconsistency.
