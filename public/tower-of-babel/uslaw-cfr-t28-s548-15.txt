28 C.F.R. § 548.15 — Equity.
Citation: us:cfr:28:548.15
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-548.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 548 (Religious Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No one may disparage the religious beliefs of an inmate, nor coerce or harass an inmate to change religious affiliation. Attendance at all religious activities is voluntary and, unless otherwise specifically determined by the Warden, open to all.
