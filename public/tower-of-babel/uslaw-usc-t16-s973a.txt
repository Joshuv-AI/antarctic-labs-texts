16 U.S.C. § 973a — Application to other laws
Citation: us:usc:16:973a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section973a
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 16C (South Pacific Tuna Fishing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The seizure by a Pacific Island Party of a vessel of the United States shall not be determined to be a seizure described in section 1825(a)(4)(C) of this title or section 1972 of title 22 if the seizure is found by the Secretary of State to be in accordance with the provisions of the Treaty.
