42 C.F.R. § 410.35 — X-ray therapy and other radiation therapy services: Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:42:410.35
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-410.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 410 (Supplementary Medical Insurance (Smi) Benefits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Medicare Part B pays for X-ray therapy and other radiation therapy services, including radium therapy and radioactive isotope therapy, and materials and the services of technicians administering the treatment.
