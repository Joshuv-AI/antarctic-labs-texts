41 C.F.R. § 102-36.25 — -36.25 Eligibility.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-36.25
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-36.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-36 (36—Disposition Of Excess Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following are eligible to acquire excess personal property:
(a) Federal agencies (including for authorized use by their contractors, cooperatives, and project grantees).
(b) The Senate.
(c) The House of Representatives.
(d) The Architect of the Capitol and any activities under the Architect's direction.
(e) The DC Government.
(f) Mixed-ownership Government corporations as defined in 31 U.S.C. 9101.
