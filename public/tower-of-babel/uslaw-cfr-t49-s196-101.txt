49 C.F.R. § 196.101 — What is the purpose and scope of this subpart?
Citation: us:cfr:49:196.101
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-196.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 196 (Protection Of Underground Pipelines From Excavation Activity). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes the minimum requirements that excavators must follow to protect pipelines subject to PHMSA or State pipeline safety regulations from excavation-related damage.
