5 C.F.R. § 1206.3 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1206.3
Creator: Merit Systems Protection Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1206.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1206 (Open Meetings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following definitions apply to this part:
(a) Meeting means deliberations of at least two Board members that determine or result in the joint conduct of official Board business.
(b) Member means one of the members of the Merit Systems Protection Board.
