43 C.F.R. § 1822.14 — What if I try to file a required document on the last day of the stated period for filing, but the BLM office where it is to be filed is officially closed all day?
Citation: us:cfr:43:1822.14
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-1822.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 1820 (Application Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

BLM considers the document timely filed if we receive it in the office on the next day it is officially open.
