45 C.F.R. § 95.503 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:45:95.503
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-95.503
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 95 (General Administration—Grant Programs (Public Assistance, Medical Assistance And State Children'S Health Insurance Programs)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to all State agency costs applicable to awards made under titles I, IV-A, IV-B, IV-C, IV-D, IV-E, X, XIV, XVI (AABD), XIX, and XXI, of the Social Security Act, and under the Refugee Act of 1980, title IV, Chapter 2 of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1521 et seq.), and under title V of Pub. L. 96-422, the Refugee Education Assistance Act of 1980.
