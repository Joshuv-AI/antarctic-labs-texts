22 U.S.C. § 3651 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:22:3651
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section3651
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 51 (Panama Canal); Subchapter I (Administration And Regulations); Part 2 (Employees); Subpart ii (Wage And Employment Practices). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this subpart—
(1)
“agency” means—
(A)
the Commission, and
(B)
any other Executive agency or the Smithsonian Institution, to the extent of any election in effect under section 3652(b) of this title;
(2)
“position” means a civilian position in the Commission, or in any other agency if a substantial portion of the duties and responsibilities are performed in the Republic of Panama; and
(3)
“employee” means an individual serving in a position.
