31 C.F.R. § 800.507 — Commencement of investigation.
Citation: us:cfr:31:800.507
Creator: Office Of Investment Security, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-800.507
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 800 (Regulations Pertaining To Certain Investments In The United States By Foreign Persons). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If it is determined that an investigation should be undertaken, such investigation shall commence no later than the end of the review period described in § 800.503.
(b) An official of the Department of the Treasury shall promptly inform the parties to a covered transaction in writing of the commencement of an investigation.
