48 U.S.C. § 915 — Bonds as public debt
Citation: us:usc:48:915
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section915
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 4 (Puerto Rico); Subchapter VII (Low Rent Housing Projects And Elimination Of Sub-Standard Housing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Such bonds shall not be a debt of Puerto Rico or any municipality, and shall not constitute a public indebtedness within the meaning of section 3 of the Act of Congress approved March 2, 1917, entitled “An Act to provide a civil government for Porto Rico, and for other purposes”, as amended.
