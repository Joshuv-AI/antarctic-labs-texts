20 U.S.C. § 7341a — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:20:7341a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section7341a
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 70 (Strengthening And Improvement Of Elementary And Secondary Schools); Subchapter V (Flexibility And Accountability); Part B (Rural Education Initiative). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the purpose of this part to address the unique needs of rural school districts that frequently—
(1)
lack the personnel and resources needed to compete effectively for Federal competitive grants; and
(2)
receive formula grant allocations in amounts too small to be effective in meeting their intended purposes.
