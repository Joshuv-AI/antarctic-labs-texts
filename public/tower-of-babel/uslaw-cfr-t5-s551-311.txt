5 C.F.R. § 551.311 — Subminimum wage.
Citation: us:cfr:5:551.311
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-551.311
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 551 (Pay Administration Under The Fair Labor Standards Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An agency may, if it meets certain criteria published by the Office of Personnel Management, employ certain groups of less than fully productive employees (e.g., handicapped patient workers) at rates less than the minimum wage specified in section 6(a)(1) of the Act.
