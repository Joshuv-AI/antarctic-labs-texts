7 C.F.R. § 31.402 — Cost of samples for wool top grades.
Citation: us:cfr:7:31.402
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-31.402
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 31 (Purchase Of Wool And Wool Top Samples). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Complete set: $42 each, delivered to any destination within the United States and $44 each, delivered to any destination outside the United States.
(b) Individual sample: $3 each, delivered to any destination within the United States and $3.50 each, delivered to any destination outside the United States.
