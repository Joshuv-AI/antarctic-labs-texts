34 U.S.C. § 12262 — Requirements
Citation: us:usc:34:12262
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section12262
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 121 (Violent Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Subchapter II (Crime Prevention); Part G (Family Unity Demonstration Project); Subpart 2 (Family Unity Demonstration Project For Federal Prisoners). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of placing Federal participants in a family unity demonstration project under section 12261 of this title, the Attorney General shall consult with the Secretary of Health and Human Services regarding the development and operation of the project.
