18 C.F.R. § 158.7 — Assignment for oral hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:18:158.7
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-158.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 158 (Accounts, Records, Memoranda And Disposition Of Contested Audit Findings And Proposed Remedies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In case consent to the shortened procedure is not given, or if at any stage of the proceeding prior to the submission of the case to the Commission any party in interest requests a hearing, the proceeding will be assigned for hearing as provided for by subpart E of part 385 of this chapter. The Commission may also in its discretion set the proceeding for hearing on its own motion at any stage thereof.
