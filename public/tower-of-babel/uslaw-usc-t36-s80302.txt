36 U.S.C. § 80302 — Purposes

Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 803 (Girl Scouts Of The United States Of America). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purposes of the corporation are—
(1)
to promote the qualities of truth, loyalty, helpfulness, friendliness, courtesy, purity, kindness, obedience, cheerfulness, thriftiness, and kindred virtues among girls, as a preparation for their responsibilities in the home and for service to the community;
(2)
to direct and coordinate the Girl Scout movement in the United States and territories and possessions of the United States; and
(3)
to fix and maintain standards for the movement that will inspire the rising generation with the highest ideals of character, patriotism, conduct, and attainment.
