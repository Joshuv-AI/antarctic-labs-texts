48 C.F.R. § 34.001 — 34.001 Definition.
Citation: us:cfr:48:34.001
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-34.001
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 34 (Major System Acquisition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Effective competition, as used in this part, is a market condition that exists when two or more contractors, acting independently, actively contend for the Government's business in a manner that ensures that the Government will be offered the lowest cost or price alternative or best technical design meeting its minimum needs.
