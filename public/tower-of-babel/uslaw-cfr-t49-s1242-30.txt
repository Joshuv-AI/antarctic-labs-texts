49 C.F.R. § 1242.30 — Dismantling retired road property and depreciation (accounts XX-17-39, XX-18-39, XX-19-39, 62-17-00, 62-18-00, and 62-19-00).
Citation: us:cfr:49:1242.30
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1242.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1242 (Separation Of Common Operating Expenses Between Freight Service And Passenger Service For Railroads 1). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Separate common expenses in each account for each subactivity (running, switching and other) in proportion to the separation of common repair and maintenance expenses associated with the particular common properties depreciated and/or dismantled.
