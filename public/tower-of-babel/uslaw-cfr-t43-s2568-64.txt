43 C.F.R. § 2568.64 — Are there different requirements for giving an allotment to the estate of a deceased veteran?
Citation: us:cfr:43:2568.64
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2568.64
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2560 (Alaska Occupancy And Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No, the estate of the deceased veteran eligible under § 2568.60 must meet the same requirements for a Native allotment as other living Alaska Native veterans. In addition, a deceased veteran must have been a resident of Alaska at the time of death.
