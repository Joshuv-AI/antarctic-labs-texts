7 U.S.C. § 6920 — Office of Energy Policy and New Uses
Citation: us:usc:7:6920
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section6920
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 98 (Department Of Agriculture Reorganization); Subchapter I (General Reorganization Authorities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall establish for the Department, in the Office of the Secretary, an Office of Energy Policy and New Uses.
