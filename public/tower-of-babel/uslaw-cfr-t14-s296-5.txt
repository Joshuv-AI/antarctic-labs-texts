14 C.F.R. § 296.5 — Agency relationships.
Citation: us:cfr:14:296.5
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-296.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 296 (Indirect Air Transportation Of Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An indirect cargo air carrier may act as agent of a shipper, or of a direct air carrier that has authorized such agency, rather than as an air carrier, if it expressly reserves the option to do so when the shipment is accepted.
