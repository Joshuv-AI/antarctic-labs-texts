19 U.S.C. § 2077 — Advances for enforcement of customs provisions
Citation: us:usc:19:2077
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section2077
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 10 (Customs Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commissioner of Customs,1 with the approval of the Secretary of the Treasury, is authorized to direct the advance of funds by the Fiscal Service, Treasury Department, in connection with the enforcement of the customs laws.
