48 U.S.C. § 1423g — Vacancies
Citation: us:usc:48:1423g
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1423g
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 8A (Guam); Subchapter III (The Legislature). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Vacancies occurring in the legislature shall be filled as the legislature shall provide, except that no person filling a vacancy shall hold office longer than for the remainder of the term for which his predecessor was elected.
