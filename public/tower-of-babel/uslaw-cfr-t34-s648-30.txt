34 C.F.R. § 648.30 — How does the Secretary evaluate an application?
Citation: us:cfr:34:648.30
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-648.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 648 (Graduate Assistance In Areas Of National Need). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Secretary evaluates an application on the basis of the criteria in § 648.31.
(b) The Secretary informs applicants of the maximum possible score for each criterion in the application package or in a notice published in the Federal Register.
