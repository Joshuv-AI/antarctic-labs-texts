22 U.S.C. § 282g — Status, privileges, and immunities of the United States
Citation: us:usc:22:282g
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section282g
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter XI (International Finance Corporation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of article V, section 5(d), and article VI, sections 2 to 9, both inclusive, of the Articles of Agreement of the Corporation shall have full force and effect in the United States and its Territories and possessions upon acceptance of membership by the United States in, and the establishment of, the Corporation.
