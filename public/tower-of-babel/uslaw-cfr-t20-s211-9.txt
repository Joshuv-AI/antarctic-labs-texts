20 C.F.R. § 211.9 — Dismissal allowance.
Citation: us:cfr:20:211.9
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-211.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 211 (Creditable Railroad Compensation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Dismissal allowances paid to an employee under a protective labor agreement that covers the amounts paid for specific periods of time are creditable as compensation under the Railroad Retirement Act, provided the employee has not severed his or her employee-employer relationship.
