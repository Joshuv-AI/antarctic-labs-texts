49 C.F.R. § 601.41 — Petitions for relief.
Citation: us:cfr:49:601.41
Creator: Federal Transit Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-601.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 601 (Organization, Functions, And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the case of a national or regional emergency or disaster, or in anticipation of such a disaster, any FTA grantee or subgrantee may petition the Administrator for temporary relief from the provisions of any policy statement, circular, guidance document or rule.
