34 C.F.R. § 385.44 — What requirement applies to the training of individuals with disabilities?
Citation: us:cfr:34:385.44
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-385.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 385 (Rehabilitation Training). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any grantee or contractor who provides training shall give due regard to the training of individuals with disabilities as part of its effort to increase the number of qualified personnel available to provide rehabilitation services.
