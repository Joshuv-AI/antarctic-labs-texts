5 U.S.C. § 13126 — Reports to Congress
Citation: us:usc:5:13126
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section13126
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part IV (Ethics Requirements); Chapter 131 (Ethics In Government); Subchapter II (Office Of Government Ethics). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director shall, no later than April 30 of each year in which the second session of a Congress begins, submit to the Congress a report containing—
(1)
a summary of the actions taken by the Director during a 2-year period ending on December 31 of the preceding year in order to carry out the Director’s functions and responsibilities under this subchapter; and
(2)
such other information as the Director may consider appropriate.
