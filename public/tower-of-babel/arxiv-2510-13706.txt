Analysis of the strong vertices of hadronic molecules D ​ K DK , D ∗ ​ K D^{*}K , D ​ K ∗ DK^{*} and their bottom analogs
Ze Zhou 1, Guo-Liang Yu 1,2, Zhi-Gang Wang 1,2, Jie Lu 3

Abstract
In this work, we analyze the strong vertices of hadronic molecules D ​ K DK , D ∗ ​ K D^{*}K , D ​ K ∗ DK^{*} and their bottom analogs within the framework of three-point QCD sum rules. The coupling between interpolating currents and low spin particles is considered in the phenomenological side, and the vacuum condensates ⟨ q ¯ ​ q ⟩ , ⟨ g s 2 ​ G ​ G ⟩ , ⟨ q ¯ ​ g s ​ σ ​ G ​ q ⟩ , ⟨ g s 3 ​ G ​ G ​ G ⟩ , ⟨ q ¯ ​ q ⟩ 2 \left\langle\bar{q}q\right\rangle,\left\langle g_{s}^{2}GG\right\rangle,\left\langle\bar{q}g_{s}\sigma Gq\right\rangle,\left\langle g_{s}^{3}GGG\right\rangle,{\left\langle\bar{q}q\right\rangle^{2}} are included in the QCD side. As an application of strong coupling constants, we also obtain the partial decay widths of these states, where Γ T D ​ K → D s ​ π = 10.3 − 2.9 + 3.0 \Gamma_{T_{DK}\rightarrow D_{s}\pi}=10.3_{-2.9}^{+3.0} KeV, Γ T D ∗ ​ K → D s ∗ ​ π = 24.8 − 5.4 + 5.5 \Gamma_{T_{D^{*}K}\rightarrow D_{s}^{*}\pi}=24.8_{-5.4}^{+5.5} KeV, Γ T B ​ K → B ​ K = 101 − 21 + 36 \Gamma_{T_{BK}\rightarrow BK}=101_{-21}^{+36} MeV and Γ T B ∗ ​ K → B ∗ ​ K = 142 − 25 + 52 \Gamma_{T_{B^{*}K}\rightarrow B^{*}K}=142_{-25}^{+52} MeV. It is shown that the results of Γ T D ​ K → D s ​ π \Gamma_{T_{DK}\rightarrow D_{s}\pi} and Γ T D ∗ ​ K → D s ∗ ​ π \Gamma_{T_{D^{*}K}\rightarrow D_{s}^{*}\pi} are compatible with the experimental data of D s ​ 0 ∗ ​ ( 2317 ) D_{s0}^{*}(2317) and D s ​ 1 ​ ( 2460 ) D_{s1}(2460) .

IV
The decay processes of
T_{D^{(*)}K}\to D_{s}^{(*)}\pi
and
T_{B^{(*)}K}\to B^{(*)}K

As an application of these strong coupling constants, we analyzed the decay processes of
T_{D^{(*)}K}\to D_{s}^{(*)}\pi
and
T_{B^{(*)}K}\to B^{(*)}K
with the above predicted results, where the former decay process can be explained by
\eta-\pi
mixing mechanism. The two-body decay width can be expressed as,

\displaystyle\Gamma=\frac{1}{2J+1}\sum\frac{p}{8\pi M_{i}^{2}}|T|^{2}

(28)

with
p=\frac{\sqrt{[M_{i}^{2}-(m_{f1}+m_{f2})^{2}][M_{i}^{2}-(m_{f1}-m_{f2})^{2}]}}{2M_{i}}
. Here,
M_{i}
, and
m_{f1}/m_{f2}
represent the masses of initial and final particles,
J
is the total angular momentum of the initial hadron,
\sum
denotes the summation of all the polarization vectors, and
T
is the scattering amplitude. With Eqs. (
II.1
) and (
28
), the decay widths can be written as,

\displaystyle\Gamma_{T_{DK}D_{s}\pi}=

\displaystyle t_{\eta\pi}^{2}G_{T_{DK}D_{s}\eta}^{2}\frac{\sqrt{\lambda{(m_{T_{DK}}^{2},m_{D_{s}}^{2},m_{\pi}^{2})}}}{16\pi m_{T_{DK}}^{3}(m_{\eta}^{2}-m_{\pi}^{2})^{2}}

\displaystyle\Gamma_{T_{D^{*}K}D_{s}^{*}\pi}=

\displaystyle t_{\eta\pi}^{2}G_{T_{D^{*}K}D_{s}^{*}\eta}^{2}\frac{\sqrt{\lambda{(m_{T_{D^{*}K}}^{2},m_{D_{s}^{*}}^{2},m_{\pi}^{2})}}}{192\pi m_{T_{D^{*}K}}^{5}m_{D_{s}^{*}}^{2}(m_{\eta}^{2}-m_{\pi}^{2})^{2}}

\displaystyle\times[8m_{T_{D^{*}K}}^{2}m_{D_{s}^{*}}^{2}+(m_{T_{D^{*}K}}^{2}+m_{D_{s}^{*}}^{2}-m_{\eta}^{2})^{2}]

\displaystyle\Gamma_{T_{BK}BK}=

\displaystyle G_{T_{BK}BK}^{2}\frac{\sqrt{\lambda{(m_{T_{BK}}^{2},m_{B}^{2},m_{K}^{2})}}}{16\pi m_{T_{BK}}^{3}}

\displaystyle\Gamma_{T_{B^{*}K}B^{*}K}=

\displaystyle G_{T_{B^{*}K}B^{*}K}^{2}\frac{\sqrt{\lambda{(m_{T_{B^{*}K}}^{2},m_{B^{*}}^{2},m_{K}^{2})}}}{{48\pi m_{T_{B^{*}K}}^{3}}}

(29)

\displaystyle\times[\frac{8m_{T_{B^{*}K}}^{2}m_{B^{*}}^{2}+(m_{T_{B^{*}K}}^{2}+m_{B^{*}}^{2}-m_{K}^{2})^{2}}{4m_{T_{B^{*}K}}^{2}m_{B^{*}}^{2}}]

Using the predicted values of the strong coupling constants of method I, we obtain the widths of these above decay channels,

\displaystyle\Gamma_{T_{DK}D_{s}\pi}=10.3_{-2.9}^{+3.0}\;\mathrm{KeV}

\displaystyle\Gamma_{T_{D^{*}K}D_{s}^{*}\pi}=24.8_{-5.4}^{+5.5}\;\mathrm{KeV}

\displaystyle\Gamma_{T_{BK}BK}=101_{-21}^{+36}\;\mathrm{MeV}

\displaystyle\Gamma_{T_{B^{*}K}B^{*}K}=142_{-25}^{+52}\;\mathrm{MeV}

(30)

\eta-{\pi^{0}}

V
Conclusion

In this work, we perform the analysis of strong vertices of hadronic molecules
DK
,
D^{*}K
,
DK^{*}
,
BK
,
B^{*}K
, and
BK^{*}
by using two methods within the framework of three-point QCDSR. According to comparison, the results obtained from these two methods are roughly consistent with each other. However, we sometime can not obtain stable sum rules for method II because the contributions of excited and continuum states of initial particle are neglected. These coupling constants are important input parameters in analyzing the decaying and producing processes of these charm/bottom strange states. Using the predicted coupling constants of method I, we also study the partial decay widths of
T_{D^{(*)}K}\to D_{s}^{(*)}\pi
and
T_{B^{(*)}K}\to B^{(*)}K
.
According to comparison, we find that the experimental data of
D_{s0}^{*}(2317)
and
D_{s1}(2460)
can be well reproduced by our results. Thus, we temporarily support explaining these two states as
DK
,
D^{*}K
hadronic molecules. Certainly, this conclusion needs to be confirmed by more theoretical analysis and experimental data. As for
BK
and
B^{*}K
, we hope our calculating results can provide useful information for searching these hadronic states in the future in experiments.
