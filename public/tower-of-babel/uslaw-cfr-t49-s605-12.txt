49 C.F.R. § 605.12 — Use of project equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:49:605.12
Creator: Federal Transit Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-605.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 605 (School Bus Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No recipient or operator of project equipment shall engage in school bus operations using buses, facilities or equipment funded under 49 U.S.C. chapter 53. A recipient or operator may, however, use such buses, facilities and equipment for the transportation of school students, personnel and equipment in incidental charter bus operations. Such use of project equipment is subject to part 604 of this title.
