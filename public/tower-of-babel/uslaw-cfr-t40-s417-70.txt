40 C.F.R. § 417.70 — Applicability; description of the manufacture of bar soaps subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:417.70
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-417.70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 417 (Soap And Detergent Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges resulting from all operations associated with conversion of neat soap to finished bar soaps, including drying, milling, plodding, stamping and packaging.
