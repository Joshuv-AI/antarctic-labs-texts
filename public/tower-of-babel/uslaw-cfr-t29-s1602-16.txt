29 C.F.R. § 1602.16 — Penalty for making of willfully false statements on report.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1602.16
Creator: Equal Employment Opportunity Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1602.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1602 (Recordkeeping And Reporting Requirements Under Title Vii, The Ada, Gina, And The Pwfa). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The making of willfully false statements on Report EEO-2 is a violation of the U.S. Code, title 18, section 1001, and is punishable by fine or imprisonment as set forth therein.
