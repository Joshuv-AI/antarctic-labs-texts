16 C.F.R. § 1237.2 — Requirements for booster seats.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1237.2
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1237.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1237 (Safety Standard For Booster Seats). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each booster seat must comply with all applicable provisions of ASTM F2640-18, Standard Consumer Safety Specification for Booster Seats (approved on April 1, 2018). The Director of the Federal Register approves this incorporation by reference in accordance with 5 U.S.C. 552(a) and 1 CFR part 51.
