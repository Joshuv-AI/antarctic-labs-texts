22 U.S.C. § 4071g — Cost-of-living adjustments
Citation: us:usc:22:4071g
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section4071g
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 52 (Foreign Service); Subchapter VIII (Foreign Service Retirement And Disability); Part II (Foreign Service Pension System). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Cost-of-living adjustments for annuitants under this System shall be granted under procedures in section 8462 of title 5 in the same manner as such adjustments are made for annuitants referred to in subsection (c)(3)(B)(ii) of such section.
