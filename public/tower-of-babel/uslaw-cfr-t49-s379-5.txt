49 C.F.R. § 379.5 — Protection and storage of records.
Citation: us:cfr:49:379.5
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-379.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 379 (Preservation Of Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The entity shall protect records subject to this part from destruction, deterioration, unauthorized access, modification and/or data corruption.
(b) The entity shall notify the Secretary if prescribed records are substantially destroyed, damaged, accessed and modified without authorization, or otherwise corrupted.
