20 C.F.R. § 350.6 — Garnishment of payments after disbursement.
Citation: us:cfr:20:350.6
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-350.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 350 (Garnishment Of Benefits Paid Under The Railroad Retirement Act, The Railroad Unemployment Insurance Act, And Under Any Other Act Administered By The Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Payments that are covered by 45 U.S.C. 231m or 45 U.S.C. 352(e) and that are made by direct deposit are subject to 31 CFR part 212, Garnishment of Accounts Containing Federal Benefit Payments. This section may be amended only by a rulemaking issued jointly by the Department of the Treasury and the agencies defined as a “benefit agency” in 31 CFR 212.3.
