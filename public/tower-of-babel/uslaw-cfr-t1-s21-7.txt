1 C.F.R. § 21.7 — Titles and subtitles.
Citation: us:cfr:1:21.7
Creator: Administrative Committee Of The Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-1/section-21.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 1 (General Provisions), Part 21 (Preparation Of Documents Subject To Codification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-23 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The major divisions of the Code are titles, each of which brings together broadly related Government functions.
(b) Subtitles may be used to distinguish between materials emanating from an overall agency and the material issued by its various components. Subtitles may also be used to group chapters within a title.
