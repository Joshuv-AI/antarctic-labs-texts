31 U.S.C. § 7301 — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:31:7301
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section7301
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 73 (Administering Block Grants). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the purpose of this chapter to ensure that—
(1)
block grant amounts are allocated for programs of special importance to meet the needs of local governments, residents of local governments, and other eligible entities; and
(2)
all eligible local governments, residents of local governments, and other eligible entities are treated fairly in distributing block grant amounts.
