7 U.S.C. § 138i — Effect of other laws
Citation: us:usc:7:138i
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section138i
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 6A (National Laboratory Accreditation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall alter the authority of the Secretary of Health and Human Services under the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (21 U.S.C. 301 et seq.).
