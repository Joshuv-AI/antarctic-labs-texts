31 C.F.R. § 363.140 — May a zero-percent certificate of indebtedness be pledged or used as collateral?
Citation: us:cfr:31:363.140
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-363.140
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 363 (Regulations Governing Securities Held In Treasurydirect). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A zero-percent certificate of indebtedness may not be pledged or used as collateral for the performance of an obligation.
