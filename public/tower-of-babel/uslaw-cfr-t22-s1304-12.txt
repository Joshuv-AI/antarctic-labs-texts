22 C.F.R. § 1304.12 — Other rights and services.
Citation: us:cfr:22:1304.12
Creator: Millennium Challenge Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1304.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1304 (Production Or Disclosure Of Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this part shall be construed to entitle any person a right to any service or to the disclosure of any record to which such person is not entitled under the FOIA.
