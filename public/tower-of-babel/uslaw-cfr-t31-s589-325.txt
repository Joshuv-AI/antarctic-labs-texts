31 C.F.R. § 589.325 — Metals and mining sector of the Russian Federation economy.
Citation: us:cfr:31:589.325
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-589.325
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 589 (Ukraine-/Russia-Related Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term metals and mining sector of the Russian Federation economy includes any act, process, or industry of extracting, at the surface or underground, ores, coal, precious stones, or any other minerals or geological materials in the Russian Federation, or any act of procuring, processing, manufacturing, or refining such geological materials, or transporting them to, from, or within the Russian Federation.
