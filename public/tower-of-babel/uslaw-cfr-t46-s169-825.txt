46 C.F.R. § 169.825 — Wearing of safety belts.
Citation: us:cfr:46:169.825
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-169.825
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 169 (Sailing School Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The master of each vessel shall ensure that each person wears an approved safety harness when aloft or working topside in heavy weather.
