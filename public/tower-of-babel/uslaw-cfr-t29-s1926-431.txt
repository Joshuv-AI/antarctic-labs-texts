29 C.F.R. § 1926.431 — Maintenance of equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1926.431
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1926.431
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1926 (Safety And Health Regulations For Construction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The employer shall ensure that all wiring components and utilization equipment in hazardous locations are maintained in a dust-tight, dust-ignition-proof, or explosion-proof condition, as appropriate. There shall be no loose or missing screws, gaskets, threaded connections, seals, or other impairments to a tight condition.
