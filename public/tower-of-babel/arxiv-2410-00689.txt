4
Hypothesis: Can memory help improve in-context learning abilities of LLM agents?

Although the context window of LLMS have gotten increasingly larger, catastrophic forgetting remains an issue as we work towards continual learning. One way to remedy the limitations of in-context learning, is store previously seen trajectories in a library. During inference time, the library can be leveraged to select the most relevant trajectories as few-shot prompts for the given task.
