1 C.F.R. § 3.3 — Reproduction and certification of copies of acts and documents.
Citation: us:cfr:1:3.3
Creator: Administrative Committee Of The Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-1/section-3.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 1 (General Provisions), Part 3 (Services To The Public). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-23 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations for the public use of records in the National Archives (36 CFR parts 1252-1258) govern the furnishing of reproductions of acts and documents and certificates of authentication for them. Section 1258.14 of those regulations provides for the advance payment of appropriate fees for reproduction services and for certifying reproductions.
