42 U.S.C. § 3057k — “Native Hawaiian” defined
Citation: us:usc:42:3057k
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section3057k
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 35 (Programs For Older Americans); Subchapter X (Grants For Native Americans); Part B (Native Hawaiian Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of this part, the term “Native Hawaiian” means any individual any of whose ancestors were natives of the area which consists of the Hawaiian Islands prior to 1778.
