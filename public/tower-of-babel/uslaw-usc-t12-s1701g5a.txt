12 U.S.C. § 1701g–5a — Transfer of New Communities Fund assets and liabilities
Citation: us:usc:12:1701g–5a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section1701g–5a
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 13 (National Housing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall transfer all assets and liabilities of the fund established pursuant to section 717 of the Housing and Urban Development Act of 1970, as amended (42 U.S.C. 4518), to the Revolving fund (liquidating programs) established pursuant to title II of the Independent Offices Appropriation Act, 1955, as amended (12 U.S.C. 1701g–5).
