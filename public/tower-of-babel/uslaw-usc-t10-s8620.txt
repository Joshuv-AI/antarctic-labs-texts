10 U.S.C. § 8620 — Gifts for welfare of enlisted members
Citation: us:usc:10:8620
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8620
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 861 (Secretary Of The Navy: Miscellaneous Powers And Duties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Navy may accept gifts for use in providing recreation, amusement, and contentment for enlisted members of the naval service. The fund “Ships’ Stores Profits, Navy” shall be credited with these gifts.
