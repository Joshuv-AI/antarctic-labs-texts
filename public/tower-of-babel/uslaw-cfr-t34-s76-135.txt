34 C.F.R. § 76.135 — Are there any requirements for matching funds?
Citation: us:cfr:34:76.135
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-76.135
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 76 (State-Administered Formula Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary waives all requirements for matching funds for those programs that are consolidated by an Insular Area in a consolidated grant application.
