18 U.S.C. § 1120 — Murder by escaped prisoners
Citation: us:usc:18:1120
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1120
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 51 (Homicide). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Definition.—
In this section, “Federal correctional institution” and “term of life imprisonment” have the meanings stated in section 1118.
(b) Offense and Penalty.—
A person, having escaped from a Federal correctional institution where the person was confined under a sentence for a term of life imprisonment, kills another shall be punished as provided in sections 1111 and 1112.
