Correction: TAB182 aggravates progression of esophageal squamous cell carcinoma by enhancing β-catenin nuclear translocation through FHL2 dependent manner
Authors: Aidi Gao, Zhenzi Su, Zengfu Shang, Chao He, Dongliu Miao, Xiaoqing Li, Shitao Zou, Weiqun Ding, Yue Zhou, Ming Sun, Jundong Zhou
Published: 2025

Correction to: Cell Death & Disease 10.1038/s41419-022-05334-2, published online 26 October 2022

While the first author, Dr. Aidi Gao was conducting a follow-up study, she recently re-checked the original data and figures from this publication and discovered an error in the Figure 1J. Due to a mistake during the image formatting process, the labels for the horizontal and vertical annotations in Figure 1J panels were swapped by Gao. As a result, two IHC images of the same group were used, and one was incorrectly labeled as representing a different marker.
