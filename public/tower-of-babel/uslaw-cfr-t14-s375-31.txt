14 C.F.R. § 375.31 — Demonstration flights of foreign aircraft.
Citation: us:cfr:14:375.31
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-375.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 375 (Navigation Of Foreign Civil Aircraft Within The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Flights of foreign civil aircraft within the United States may be made for the purpose of demonstration of the aircraft or any component thereof (including demonstrations at airshows), provided no persons, cargo or mail are carried for remuneration or hire.
