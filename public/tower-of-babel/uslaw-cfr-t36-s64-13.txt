36 C.F.R. § 64.13 — Performance reports.

Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 64 (Grants And Allocations For Recreation And Conservation Use Of Abandoned Railroad Rights-Of-Way). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Performance reports shall be submitted quarterly for all active projects. The performance reports shall briefly present the following:
(a) The status of the work required under the project scope.
(b) Other pertinent information including, when appropriate, time schedule delays and other similar problems encountered and their expected impact on the project, etc.
