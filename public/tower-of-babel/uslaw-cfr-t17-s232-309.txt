17 C.F.R. § 232.309 — Paper size; binding; sequential numbering; number of copies.
Citation: us:cfr:17:232.309
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-232.309
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 232 (Regulation S-T—General Rules And Regulations For Electronic Filings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Requirements as to paper size, binding, and sequential page numbering shall not apply to electronic format documents.
(b) An electronic format document, submitted in the manner prescribed by the EDGAR Filer Manual, shall satisfy any requirement that more than one copy of such document be filed with or provided to the Commission.
