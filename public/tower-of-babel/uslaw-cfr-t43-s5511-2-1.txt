43 C.F.R. § 5511.2-1 — -1 Free use privilege; cutting by agent.
Citation: us:cfr:43:5511.2-1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-5511.2-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 5510 (Free Use Of Timber). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Free use permits will not be issued where the applicant owns or controls lands having an adequate supply of timber to meet his needs.
