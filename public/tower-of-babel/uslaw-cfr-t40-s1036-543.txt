40 C.F.R. § 1036.543 — Carbon balance error verification.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1036.543
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1036.543
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1036 (Control Of Emissions From New And In-Use Heavy-Duty Highway Engines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The optional carbon balance error verification in 40 CFR 1065.543 compares independent assessments of the flow of carbon through the system (engine plus aftertreatment). This procedure applies for each individual interval in §§ 1036.535(b), (c), and (d), 1036.540, and 1036.545.
