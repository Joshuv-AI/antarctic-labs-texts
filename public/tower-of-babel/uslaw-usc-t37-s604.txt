37 U.S.C. § 604 — Determination of Secretary final
Citation: us:usc:37:604
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title37-section604
Context: United States Code, Title 37 (Pay And Allowances Of The Uniformed Services); Chapter 11 (Payments To Mentally Incompetent Persons). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The determination as to the person authorized to receive a payment under section 602 of this title is final and is not subject to review by an official of the United States or a court.
