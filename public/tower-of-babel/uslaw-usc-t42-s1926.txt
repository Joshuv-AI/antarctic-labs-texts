42 U.S.C. § 1926 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:42:1926
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1926
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 18 (Youth Medals). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It shall also be the duty of the Department of Justice to list in its annual budget request the sum of money necessary to carry out the provisions of this chapter, which sum is authorized in a sum not to exceed $5,000 per annum.
