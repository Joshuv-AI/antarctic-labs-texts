49 C.F.R. § 701.12 — Other rights and services.
Citation: us:cfr:49:701.12
Creator: National Railroad Passenger Corporation (Amtrak)
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 701 (Part 701—AMTRAK FREEDOM OF INFORMATION ACT PROGRAM). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this part shall be construed as entitling any person, as of right, to any service or the disclosure of any record to which such person is not entitled under the FOIA.
