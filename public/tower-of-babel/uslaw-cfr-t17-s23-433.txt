17 C.F.R. § 23.433 — Communications—fair dealing.
Citation: us:cfr:17:23.433
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-23.433
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 23 (Swap Dealers And Major Swap Participants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

With respect to any communication between a swap entity and any counterparty, the swap entity shall communicate in a fair and balanced manner based on principles of fair dealing and good faith.
