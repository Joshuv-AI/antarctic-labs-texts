30 C.F.R. § 5.40 — Fee administration.
Citation: us:cfr:30:5.40
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-5.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 5 (Fees For Testing, Evaluation, And Approval Of Mining Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applicants and approval holders will be billed for all fees, including actual travel expenses, if any, when approval program activities are completed. Invoices will contain specific payment instruction, including the address to mail payments and authorized methods of payment.
