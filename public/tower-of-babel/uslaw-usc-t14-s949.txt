14 U.S.C. § 949 — Telephone installation and charges
Citation: us:usc:14:949
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section949
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 9 (Administration); Subchapter II (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Under regulations prescribed by the Secretary, amounts appropriated to the Department of Homeland Security are available to install, repair, and maintain telephone wiring in residences owned or leased by the United States Government and, if necessary for national defense purposes in other private residences.
