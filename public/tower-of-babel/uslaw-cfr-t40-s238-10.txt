40 C.F.R. § 238.10 — Purpose and applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:40:238.10
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-238.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 238 (Degradable Plastic Ring Carriers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to require that plastic ring carriers be made of degradable materials as described in §§ 238.20 and 238.30. The requirements of this part apply to all processors and importers of plastic ring carriers in the United States as defined in § 238.20.
