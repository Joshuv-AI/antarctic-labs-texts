19 U.S.C. § 2131 — Authorization of appropriation for GATT revision
Citation: us:usc:19:2131
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section2131
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 12 (Trade Act Of 1974); Subchapter I (Negotiating And Other Authority); Part 2 (Other Authority). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated annually such sums as may be necessary for the payment by the United States of its share of the expenses of the Contracting Parties to the General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade. This authorization does not imply approval or disapproval by the Congress of all articles of the General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade.
