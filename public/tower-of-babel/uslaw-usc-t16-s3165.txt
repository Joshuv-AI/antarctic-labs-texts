16 U.S.C. § 3165 — Standards for granting certain authorizations
Citation: us:usc:16:3165
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section3165
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 51 (Alaska National Interest Lands Conservation); Subchapter IV (Transportation And Utility Systems In And Across, And Access Into, Conservation System Units). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In any case in which there is no applicable law with respect to a transportation or utility system, the head of the Federal agency concerned shall, within four months after the date of filing of any final Environmental Impact Statement, make recommendations, for purposes of section 3166(b) of this title, to grant such authorizations as may be necessary to establish such system, in whole or in part, within the conservation system unit concerned if he determines that—
(1)
such system would be compatible with the purposes for which the unit was established; and
(2)
there is no economically feasible and prudent alternative route for the system.
