39 C.F.R. § 775.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:39:775.1
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-775.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 775 (National Environmental Policy Act Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to establish certain procedures that the United States Postal Service (Postal Service) shall use to comply with the National Environmental Policy Act (NEPA) of 1969 (42 U.S.C. 4321 et seq.), as amended.
