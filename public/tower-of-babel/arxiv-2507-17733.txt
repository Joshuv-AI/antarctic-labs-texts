Electron-muon conversion in nuclei and rare decays induced by LFV dark photon
Alexey S. Zhevlakov, Sergey Kuleshov, Valery E. Lyubovitskij, Evgenie O. Oleynik

Abstract
We study lepton flavor violation (LFV) effects occurring in the e − μ e-\mu conversion in nuclei and in the rare radiative decays η ⁡ ( η ′ ) → γ ​ μ ​ e \eta(\eta^{\prime})\to\gamma\mu e of the η \eta and η ′ \eta^{\prime} mesons with a special impact of the sub-GeV mass vector mediator (dark photon). In the case of the lepton conversion we make estimates for running and future experiments operating with electron beams at fixed target. Obtained results are implemented in analysis of the LFV decays η ⁡ ( η ′ ) → γ ​ μ ​ e \eta(\eta^{\prime})\to\gamma\mu e . In particular, we derive the following upper bounds on the branching ratios Br ⁡ ( η → γ ​ μ ​ e ) < 2 × 10 − 7 \mathrm{Br(\eta\to\gamma\mu e)}<2\times 10^{-7} and Br ⁡ ( η ′ → γ ​ μ ​ e ) < 2 × 10 − 8 \mathrm{Br(\eta^{\prime}\to\gamma\mu e)}<2\times 10^{-8} in the framework of massive vector mediator portal.

Introduction

Study of lepton flavor violation (LFV)
is one of the promising directions for searching effects beyond Standard Model (SM).
During last decades certain experimental and theoretical progress has been achieved
in understanding and deeper studying of LFV physics: neutrino oscillations,
lepton conversion in nuclei, rare decays, etc.
In particular, the most stringent upper limit on the branching ratio
of the
\mu\to e\gamma

\mathrm{Br}(\mu\to e\gamma)<3.1\times 10^{-13}

\mathrm{Br}(\mu\to e\gamma)<6\times 10^{-14}

\mathrm{Br}(\mu\to 3e)<10^{-16}

II
Inclusion of dark photon in Quantum Field Formalism

In our paper we follow quantum field formalism allowing to include dark photon
developed in Ref.
Kachanovich:2021eqa
. In particular, phenomenological
Lagrangian involves singlet scalar field
\sigma
, dark photon
A^{\prime}
, and Dirac dark fermion

\chi
. The mass of the dark photon
m_{A^{\prime}}
is generated via the Stueckelberg
mechanism (Stueckelberg portal)
Stueckelberg:1938zz
by extension
of derivative acting on the scalar field
\sigma
to covariant one containing mass
of the
A^{\prime}
. The corresponding model Lagrangian reads
Kachanovich:2021eqa
:

\displaystyle{\cal L}^{\prime}_{\rm DS}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{4}\,{A}_{\mu\nu}^{\prime}{A}^{\prime\mu\nu}\,+\,\frac{m_{A^{\prime}}^{2}}{2}A^{\prime}_{\mu}A^{\prime\mu}

(2)

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle\,\bar{\chi}\,(i\not\!\!D_{\chi}-m_{\chi})\,\chi-\frac{1}{2\xi}\left(\partial_{\mu}A^{\prime\mu}\right)^{2}

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\partial_{\mu}\sigma\partial^{\mu}\sigma-\xi\frac{m_{A^{\prime}}^{2}}{2}\sigma^{2}\,,

where
\xi
is the gauge-fixing parameter.
Note, the interaction of dark photon with charged SM fermions
can include both diagonal and non-diagonal couplings

\displaystyle{\cal L}_{\rm A^{\prime}\psi}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\epsilon eA^{\prime}_{\mu}(x)\,\sum\limits_{i,j=e,\mu,\tau}\,\bar{\psi}_{i}(x)\,\gamma^{\mu}\,G_{ij}\,\psi_{j}(x)\,,

\displaystyle G_{ij}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\delta_{ij}+g^{V}_{ij}+g^{A}_{ij}\,\gamma_{5}\,,

(3)

where
g_{ij}^{V}
and
g_{ik}^{A}
are the vector and axial-vector dimensionless couplings,
respectively;
\epsilon
is the QED-dark photon mixing parameter. Next, we use
the following notations
g_{ll}=e(\epsilon+g^{V}_{ii})
and
g_{lf}=e\epsilon g_{ij}^{A,V}

for diagonal and non-diagonal couplings, respectively.
In our applications we focus the use of
the vector couplings.

III.1
Decay
\mu\to e\gamma

Gauge-invariant set of Feynman diagrams induced by
dark photon mediator and contributing to the

\mu\to e\gamma
decay is shown in Fig.

Corresponding manifestly gauge-invariant leptonic current
in momentum space has the form

\displaystyle J^{\mu}(k_{1},k_{2})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\bar{u}_{e}(k_{2})\Gamma^{\mu}(k_{1},k_{2})u_{\mu}(k_{1})\,,

\displaystyle\Gamma^{\mu}(k_{1},k_{2})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\gamma^{\mu}_{\perp}F^{e\mu}_{1}(q^{2})+\frac{i\sigma^{\mu\nu}q_{\nu}}{m_{\mu}+m_{e}}F^{e\mu}_{2}(q^{2})\,,

(4)

where
\gamma^{\mu}_{\perp}=\gamma^{\mu}-\dfrac{q^{\mu}\not\!q}{q^{2}}
is the
Dirac matrix orthogonal to the photon momentum
q=p_{1}-p_{2}
with

q_{\mu}\,\gamma^{\mu}_{\perp}=0
.

Expression for the form factors
F^{e\mu}_{i}(q^{2})
is given in Appendix.
In the limit
m_{\mu}=m_{e}
and
m_{A^{\prime}}\to 0
the
F^{e\mu}_{2}(0)
is equal to

\dfrac{g_{ll}g_{lf}}{4\pi^{2}}
, which is the LFV analogue of
the Schwinger contribution
\dfrac{\alpha}{2\pi}

to the anomalous magnetic moment of charged fermion with spin
1/2
in QED.

The decay width of the
\mu\to e\gamma
transition reads

\displaystyle\Gamma(\mu\to e\gamma)=(g_{\mu e}g_{ll})^{2}\frac{\alpha}{2}\,m_{\mu}\,\Big(1-\frac{m_{e}}{m_{\mu}}\Big)^{2}\,\Big[F^{e\mu}_{2}(0)\Big]^{2}\,.

(5)

Note, the Dirac form factor
F^{e\mu}_{1}(q^{2})
is finite for
q^{2}\neq 0
,
while it has divergence at
q^{2}=0
. This divergence is removed
by introducing appropriate counterterm in analogy with QED.
Therefore, the renormalized Dirac form factor has the form

F^{e\mu}_{1;r}(q^{2})=F^{e\mu}_{1}(q^{2})-F^{e\mu}_{1}(0)
. It means that

F^{e\mu}_{1;r}(0)=0
, i.e. LFV is absent for real photon emission.
Expression for the
F^{e\mu}_{1;r}(q^{2})
is presented in Appendix
A
.
In the limit
m_{e}=m_{\mu}
and
m_{A^{\prime}}=0
we reproduce the well-known QED result
for the one-loop vertex correction
Peskin:1995ev
.

huge intensity electron beams in fixed target experiments. Unfortunately,

in fixed target experiments it is impossible to get the level of sensitivity achieved

IV.1
Bounds for LFV couplings from rare
\eta
and
\eta^{\prime}
mesons decays

Using obtained results for combination of diagonal and non-diagonal coupling,
we have a possibility to estimate branchings of the LFV decays of
\eta
and
\eta^{\prime}
mesons.
It is planned experimental study of these decays in the
\eta
and
\eta^{\prime}
factory REDTOP
at FermiLab (USA)
REDTOP:2022slw
. The vector mediator is simple model, which can give
bounds for the three-body LFV decay widths of
\eta(\eta^{\prime})\to\gamma\mu e

using existed limits from LFV decay and lepton conversion. In general, such bound should be
weakly changed in dependence
of type of mediator. For other LFV decays, such as two-body decays
\eta\to e\mu
and

\eta^{\prime}\to e\mu
, strict limits from conversion were obtained in
Ref.
Hoferichter:2022mna
.

The LFV decays
\eta(\eta^{\prime})\to\gamma\mu e
are related to the
Dalitz lepton flavor conversing (LFC) decays
\eta(\eta^{\prime})\to\gamma l^{+}l^{-}

because matrix elements of both
types of processes contain anomalous pseudoscalar-vector-vector couplings:

P\gamma A^{\prime}
and
P\gamma\gamma
(the Wess-Zumino-Witten chiral anomaly), respectively.
Nice point is that we can relate
P\gamma A^{\prime}
and
P\gamma\gamma
couplings
using the QED-dark photon mixing. In particular,
starting with phenomenological Lagrangian governing the chiral anomaly

\displaystyle{\cal L}_{P\gamma\gamma}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{e^{2}}{8}\,g_{P\gamma\gamma}\,\epsilon_{\mu\nu\alpha\beta}\,P(x)\,F^{\mu\nu}(x)\,F^{\alpha\beta}(x)\,,

(18)

where
F^{\mu\nu}
is the stress tensor of electromagnetic field,

\epsilon_{\mu\nu\alpha\beta}
is the antisymmetric Levi-Civita tensor,
and
g_{P\gamma\gamma}
is the
P\gamma\gamma
coupling,
we derive phenomenological Lagrangian describing the coupling of pseudoscalar
meson
P
with QED and dark photons:

\displaystyle{\cal L}_{P\gamma A^{\prime}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{e^{2}}{8}\,g_{P\gamma A^{\prime}}\,\epsilon_{\mu\nu\alpha\beta}\,P(x)\,F^{\mu\nu}(x)\,F^{\prime\alpha\beta}(x)\,,

(19)

where
g_{P\gamma A^{\prime}}
is the
P\gamma A^{\prime}
coupling, which for three different scenarios is fixed as:
(1)
g_{P\gamma A^{\prime}}=\epsilon g_{P\gamma\gamma}
for general scenario,
(2)
g_{P\gamma A^{\prime}}=(g_{qq}/e)g_{P\gamma\gamma}
for hadrophilic scenario,
(3)
g_{P\gamma A^{\prime}}=\cfrac{\epsilon e}{16\pi^{2}}\ln(m_{l}^{2}/\mu^{2})g_{P\gamma\gamma}

Gherghetta:2019coi

for leptophilic scenario, where
\mu^{2}
is a renormalization scale,

m_{l}
is mass of lepton propagating in loop.
Note, the decay width of the anomaly process
P\to\gamma\gamma

in terms of coupling
g_{P\gamma\gamma}
reads

\displaystyle\Gamma(P\rightarrow\gamma\gamma)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\pi\,\alpha^{2}}{4}\,g^{2}_{P\gamma\gamma}\,m_{P}^{3}\,,

(20)

where
m_{P}
is the mass of the respective pseudoscalar meson.

P\rightarrow\gamma A^{\prime}
decay width can be written as

\displaystyle\Gamma(P\rightarrow\gamma A^{\prime})=\frac{\epsilon^{2}}{2}\,(1-x_{A^{\prime}P})^{3}\,\Gamma(P\rightarrow\gamma\gamma)\,,

(21)

where
x_{ij}=m_{i}^{2}/m_{j}^{2}
is the ratio of squared masses of
corresponding particles.

With taking into account intermediate
vector mesons
