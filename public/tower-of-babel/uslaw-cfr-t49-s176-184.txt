49 C.F.R. § 176.184 — Class 1 (explosive) materials of Compatibility Group L.
Citation: us:cfr:49:176.184
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-176.184
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 176 (Carriage By Vessel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Class 1 (explosive) materials in compatibility group L may not be handled in a port area without the special permission of, and subject to any special precautions required by, the COTP.
