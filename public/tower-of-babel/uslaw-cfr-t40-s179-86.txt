40 C.F.R. § 179.86 — Time and place of preliminary conference.
Citation: us:cfr:40:179.86
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-179.86
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 179 (Formal Evidentiary Public Hearing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A preliminary conference will commence at the date, time, and place announced in the Notice of Hearing, or as otherwise specified by the Administrator or presiding officer in a subsequent notice. The preliminary conference may not commence until after expiration of the time for filing notices of participation under § 179.42. The presiding officer may specify that two or more such conferences shall be held.
