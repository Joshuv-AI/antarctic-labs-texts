33 C.F.R. § 150.425 — What are the requirements for transferring cargo?
Citation: us:cfr:33:150.425
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-150.425
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 150 (Deepwater Ports: Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Cargo transfer procedures must be outlined in the deepwater port operations manual and must provide:
(a) Oil transfer procedures that accord with § 156.120 of this chapter; and
(b) Natural gas transfer procedures that accord with §§ 127.315, 127.317 and 127.319 of this chapter.
