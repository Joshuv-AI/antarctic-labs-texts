46 U.S.C. § 46108 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:46:46108
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section46108
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part D (Federal Maritime Commission); Chapter 461 (Federal Maritime Commission). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is authorized to be appropriated to the Federal Maritime Commission $32,869,000 for fiscal year 2022, $38,260,000 for fiscal year 2023, $43,720,000 for fiscal year 2024, and $49,200,000 for fiscal year 2025 for the activities of the Commission authorized under this chapter and subtitle IV.
