50 U.S.C. § 3223 — Educational scholarship program
Citation: us:usc:50:3223
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section3223
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 44 (National Security); Subchapter VIII (Education In Support Of National Intelligence); Part C (Additional Education Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The head of a department or agency containing an element of the intelligence community may establish an undergraduate or graduate training program with respect to civilian employees and prospective civilian employees of such element similar in purpose, conditions, content, and administration to the program that the Secretary of Defense is authorized to establish under section 3614 of this title.
