42 U.S.C. § 13333 — Clean coal, waste-to-energy
Citation: us:usc:42:13333
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section13333
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 134 (Energy Policy); Subchapter VI (Coal); Part A (Research, Development, Demonstration, And Commercial Application). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall establish a program of research, development, demonstration, and commercial application with respect to the use of solid waste combined with coal as a fuel source for clean coal combustion technologies. The program shall address—
(1)
the feasibility of cofiring coal and used vehicle tires in fluidized bed combustion units;
(2)
the combined gasification of coal and municipal sludge using integrated gasification combined cycle technology;
(3)
the creation of fuel pellets combining coal and material reclaimed from solid waste;
(4)
the feasibility of cofiring, in fluidized bed combustion units, waste methane from coal mines, including ventilation air, together with coal or coal wastes; and
(5)
other sources of waste and coal mixtures in other applications that the Secretary considers appropriate.
