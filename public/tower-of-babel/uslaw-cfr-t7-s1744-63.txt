7 C.F.R. § 1744.63 — The telephone loan budget.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1744.63
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1744.63
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1744 (Post-Loan Policies And Procedures Common To Guaranteed And Insured Telephone Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the loan is made, RUS provides the borrower a Telephone Loan Budget, RUS Form 493. This budget divides the loan into budget accounts such as “Engineering.” When a contract or other document is approved by RUS, funds are encumbered from the appropriate budget account. See 7 CFR part 1753.
