40 C.F.R. § 246.201-6 — -6 Recommended procedures: Transportation to market.
Citation: us:cfr:40:246.201-6
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-246.201-6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 246 (Source Separation For Materials Recovery Guidelines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Transportation to market may be supplied by the facility or the community generating the waste, by a private hauler, or by the purchaser.
