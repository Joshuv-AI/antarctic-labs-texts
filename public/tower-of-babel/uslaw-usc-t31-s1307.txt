31 U.S.C. § 1307 — Public building construction
Citation: us:usc:31:1307
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section1307
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 13 (Appropriations); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Amounts appropriated to construct public buildings remain available until completion of the work. When a building is completed and outstanding liabilities for the construction are paid, balances remaining shall revert immediately to the Treasury.
