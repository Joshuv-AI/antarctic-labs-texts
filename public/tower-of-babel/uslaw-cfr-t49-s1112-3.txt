49 C.F.R. § 1112.3 — Default for failure to comply with schedule; effect of default.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1112.3
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1112.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1112 (Modified Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a party fails to comply with the schedule for submission of verified statements, or any other requirements established by the modified procedure decision, that party will be deemed to be in default and to have waived any further participation in the proceeding. Thereafter, the proceeding may be disposed of without notice to and without participation by parties in default.
