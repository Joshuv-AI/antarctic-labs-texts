50 C.F.R. § 35.10 — Controlled burning.
Citation: us:cfr:50:35.10
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-35.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 35 (Wilderness Preservation And Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Controlled burning will be permitted on wilderness units when such burning will contribute to the maintenance of the wilderness resource and values in the unit; however, any fire in a wilderness area that poses a threat to resources or facilities outside the unit will be controlled and extinguished.
