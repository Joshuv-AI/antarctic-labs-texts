45 C.F.R. § 102.2 — Applicability date.
Citation: us:cfr:45:102.2
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-102.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 102 (Adjustment Of Civil Monetary Penalties For Inflation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The increased penalty amounts set forth in the right-most column of the table in Section 102.3, “Maximum Adjusted Penalty ($)”, apply to all civil monetary penalties which are assessed after August 1, 2016, including those penalties whose associated violations occurred after November 2, 2015.
