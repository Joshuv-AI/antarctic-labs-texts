42 C.F.R. § 419.94 — Preclusion of administrative and judicial review.
Citation: us:cfr:42:419.94
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-419.94
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 419 (Prospective Payment Systems For Hospital Outpatient Department Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

There is no administrative or judicial review under section 1869 of the Act, section 1878 of the Act, or otherwise of the following:
(a) The determination of whether a rural emergency hospital meets the requirements of this subpart.
(b) The determination of payment amounts under this subpart.
(c) The requirements established by this subpart.
