33 C.F.R. § 401.39 — Preparing mooring lines for passing through.
Citation: us:cfr:33:401.39
Creator: Great Lakes St. Lawrence Seaway Development Corporation, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-401.39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 401 (Seaway Regulations And Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before a vessel enters a lock:
(a) Winches shall be capable of paying out and heaving in at a minimum speed of 46 m per minute; and
(b) The eye of each mooring line shall be passed outward through the fairleads at the side.
