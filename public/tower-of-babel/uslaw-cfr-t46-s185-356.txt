46 C.F.R. § 185.356 — Carriage of hazardous materials.
Citation: us:cfr:46:185.356
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-185.356
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 185 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A vessel that transports a hazardous material, listed in 49 CFR 172.101, in commerce shall ensure the material is handled and transported in accordance with 49 CFR parts 171 and 176.
