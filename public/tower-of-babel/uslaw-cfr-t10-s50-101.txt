10 C.F.R. § 50.101 — Retaking possession of special nuclear material.
Citation: us:cfr:10:50.101
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-50.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 50 (Domestic Licensing Of Production And Utilization Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon revocation of a license, the Commission may immediately cause the retaking of possession of all special nuclear material held by the licensee.
