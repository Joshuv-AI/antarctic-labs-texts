2 U.S.C. § 6621 — Payment for telecommunications equipment and services; definitions
Citation: us:usc:2:6621
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section6621
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 65 (Senate Officers And Administration); Subchapter III (Sergeant At Arms And Doorkeeper Of Senate); Part B (General Powers And Duties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in sections 6621 to 6623 of this title, the term—
(1)
“Sergeant at Arms” means the Sergeant at Arms and Doorkeeper of the United States Senate; and
(2)
“user” means any Senator, Officer of the Senate, Committee, office, or entity provided telephone equipment and services by the Sergeant at Arms.
