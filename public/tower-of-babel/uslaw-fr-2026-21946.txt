SOCIAL SECURITY ADMINISTRATION

[Docket No. SSA-2026-0364]


Privacy Act of 1974; Matching Program

AGENCY: Social Security Administration (SSA).

ACTION: Notice of re-establishment of a matching program.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the provisions of the Privacy Act, as 
amended, this notice announces the re-establishment of a matching 
program with the Department of War (DOW), Defense Manpower Data Center 
(DMDC). Under this matching program, DOW will disclose military 
retirement payment and survivor benefit payment data to SSA. SSA will 
use the military retirement payment and survivor benefit payment data 
to verify information provided by applicants, recipients, deemors 
(i.e., someone other than the eligible individual or eligible spouse 
whose income and resources are considered when determining an 
individual's eligibility for Supplemental Security Income (SSI) and the 
amount of their payment), and beneficiaries of SSI payments and Special 
Veterans Benefits (SVB).

DATES: The deadline to submit comments on the proposed matching program 
is November 27, 2026.
    The matching program will be applicable on November 29, 2026, or 
once a minimum of 30 days after publication of this notice has elapsed, 
whichever is later. The matching program will be in effect for a period 
of 18 months.

PARTICIPATING AGENCIES:
    SSA and DOW.

AUTHORITY FOR CONDUCTING THE MATCHING PROGRAM:
    The matching agreement between SSA and DOW is executed in 
compliance with the Privacy Act of 1974 (5 U.S.C. 552a), as amended by 
the Computer Matching and Privacy Protection Act of 1988, and the 
regulations and guidance promulgated thereunder.

[[Page 67893]]

    Legal authority for the disclosure under the agreement for SSA to 
conduct this matching activity is contained in section 1631(e)(1)(B) 
and (f) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1383(e)(1)(B) and (f)). 
The legal authority for this exchange is sections 806(b) and 
1631(e)(1)(B) and (f) of the Social Security Act (Act) (42 U.S.C. 
1006(b) and 1383(e)(1)(B) and (f)). SSA's legal authority to disclose 
data to DOW is section 1106(a) of the Act (42 U.S.C. 1306(a)) and 
section (b)(3) of the Privacy Act (5 U.S.C. 552a(b)(3)).

PURPOSE(S):
    Under this matching program DOW will disclose military retirement 
payment and survivor benefit payment data to SSA. SSA will use the 
military retirement payment and survivor benefit payment data to verify 
information provided by applicants, recipients, deemors (i.e., someone 
other than the eligible individual or eligible spouse whose income and 
resources are considered when determining an individual's eligibility 
for SSI and the amount of their payment), and beneficiaries of SSI 
payments and SVB.

CATEGORIES OF INDIVIDUALS:
    The individuals whose information is involved in this matching 
program are SSI and SVB applicants, recipients, deemors, and 
beneficiaries including retired members, or their survivors, of the 
Uniformed Services.

CATEGORIES OF RECORDS:
    SSA will provide DOW with a quarterly finder file containing 
approximately 10 million records of individuals for whom SSA requests 
data to administer the SSI and SVB programs. SSA and DOW will use 
Social Security numbers to conduct the match. DOW will provide a 
response file containing miliary retirement payment and survivor 
benefit payment data for matched records.

SYSTEM(S) OF RECORDS:
