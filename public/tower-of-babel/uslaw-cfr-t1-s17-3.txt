1 C.F.R. § 17.3 — Criteria for emergency publication.
Citation: us:cfr:1:17.3
Creator: Administrative Committee Of The Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-1/section-17.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 1 (General Provisions), Part 17 (Filing For Public Inspection And Publication Schedules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-23 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The emergency schedule is designed to provide the fastest possible publication of a document involving the prevention, alleviation, control, or relief of an emergency situation.
