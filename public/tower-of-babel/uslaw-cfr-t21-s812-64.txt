21 C.F.R. § 812.64 — IRB's continuing review.
Citation: us:cfr:21:812.64
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-812.64
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 812 (Investigational Device Exemptions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The IRB shall conduct its continuing review of an investigation in accordance with part 56.
