48 C.F.R. § 3047.305-70 — 3047.305-70 Solicitation provisions.
Citation: us:cfr:48:3047.305-70
Creator: Chapter 30—Department Of Homeland Security, Homeland Security Acquisition Regulation (Hsar)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-3047.305-70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 3047 (Transportation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall insert the following provisions in solicitations, as applicable:
(a) (HSAR) 48 CFR 3052.247-70, F.o.b. Origin Information, with Alternates I or II, as applicable, shall be inserted in accordance with (FAR) 48 CFR 47.305-3(b);
(b) (HSAR) 48 CFR 3052.247-71, F.o.b. Origin Only, shall be inserted in accordance with (FAR) 48 CFR 47.305-3(e); and
(c) (HSAR) 48 CFR 3052.247-72, F.o.b. Destination Only, shall be inserted in accordance with (FAR) 48 CFR 47.305-4(b).
