5 C.F.R. § 9301.15 — Access to the accounting of disclosures from records.
Citation: us:cfr:5:9301.15
Creator: Special Inspector General For Afghanistan Reconstruction
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-9301.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 9301 (Disclosure Of Records And Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Rules governing the granting of access to the accounting of disclosures are the same as those for granting access to the records (including verification of identity) outlined in § 9301.14.
