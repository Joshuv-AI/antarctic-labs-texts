48 C.F.R. § 229.170-3 — 229.170-3 Reports.
Citation: us:cfr:48:229.170-3
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-229.170-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 229 (Taxes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall submit a report to the designated Security Assistance Office when a foreign government or entity imposes tax or customs duties on commodities acquired under contracts or subcontracts meeting the criteria of 229.170-2(b)(1). Follow the procedures at PGI 229.170-3 for submission of reports.
