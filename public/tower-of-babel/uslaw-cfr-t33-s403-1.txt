33 C.F.R. § 403.1 — Purpose of the Joint Tolls Review Board. [Rule 1]
Citation: us:cfr:33:403.1
Creator: Great Lakes St. Lawrence Seaway Development Corporation, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-403.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 403 (Rules Of Procedure Of The Joint Tolls Review Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board shall hear complaints relating to the interpretation of the St. Lawrence Seaway Tariff of Tolls or allegations of unjust discrimination arising out of the operation of the said Tariff and shall conduct such other business as agreed to by the Board (Rule 1).
