43 C.F.R. § 3902.29 — Other parties in interest.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3902.29
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3902.29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3900 (Oil Shale Management—General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If there is more than one party in interest in an application for a lease, include with the application the names of all other parties who hold or will hold any interest in the application or in the lease. All interested parties who wish to hold an interest in a lease must provide to the BLM the information required by this subpart to qualify to hold a lease interest.
