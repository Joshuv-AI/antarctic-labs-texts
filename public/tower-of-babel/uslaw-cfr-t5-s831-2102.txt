5 C.F.R. § 831.2102 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:5:831.2102
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-831.2102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 831 (Retirement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to all agencies with employees occupying positions subject to subchapter III of chapter 83 of title 5, United States Code, the United States Senate, and the United States House of Representatives.
