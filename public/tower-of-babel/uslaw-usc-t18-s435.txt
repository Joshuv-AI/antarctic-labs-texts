18 U.S.C. § 435 — Contracts in excess of specific appropriation
Citation: us:usc:18:435
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section435
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 23 (Contracts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever, being an officer or employee of the United States, knowingly contracts for the erection, repair, or furnishing of any public building, or for any public improvement, to pay a larger amount than the specific sum appropriated for such purpose, shall be fined under this title 1 or imprisoned not more than one year, or both.
