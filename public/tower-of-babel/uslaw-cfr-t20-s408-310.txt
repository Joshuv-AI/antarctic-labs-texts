20 C.F.R. § 408.310 — What makes an application a claim for SVB?
Citation: us:cfr:20:408.310
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-408.310
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 408 (Special Benefits For Certain World War Ii Veterans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To be considered a claim for SVB, an application must generally meet all of the following conditions:
(a) It must be on the prescribed SVB application form (SSA-2000-F6, Application for Special Benefits for World War II Veterans).
(b) It must be completed and filed with SSA as described in § 408.325.
(c) It must be signed by you or by someone who may sign an application for you as described in § 408.315.
(d) You must be alive at the time it is filed.
