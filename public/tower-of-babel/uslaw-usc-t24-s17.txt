24 U.S.C. § 17 — Government of Naval Asylum
Citation: us:usc:24:17
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title24-section17
Context: United States Code, Title 24 (Hospitals And Asylums); Chapter 1 (Navy Hospitals, Army And Navy Hospital, And Hospital Relief For Seamen And Others). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The asylum for disabled and decrepit Navy officers, seamen, and marines shall be governed in accordance with the rules and regulations prescribed by the Secretary of the Navy.
