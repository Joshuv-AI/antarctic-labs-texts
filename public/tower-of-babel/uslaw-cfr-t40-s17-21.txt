40 C.F.R. § 17.21 — Filing and service of documents.
Citation: us:cfr:40:17.21
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-17.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 17 (Implementation Of The Equal Access To Justice Act In Epa Administrative Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An application for an award and any other pleading or document related to the application shall be filed and served on all parties to the proceeding in the same manner as other pleadings in the proceeding.
