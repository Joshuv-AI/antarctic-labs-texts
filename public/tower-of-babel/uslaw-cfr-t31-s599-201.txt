31 C.F.R. § 599.201 — Prohibited transactions.
Citation: us:cfr:31:599.201
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-599.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 599 (Illicit Drug Trade Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All transactions prohibited pursuant to E.O. 14059 of December 15, 2021, or any further Executive orders issued pursuant to the national emergency declared in E.O. 14059 are prohibited pursuant to this part.
