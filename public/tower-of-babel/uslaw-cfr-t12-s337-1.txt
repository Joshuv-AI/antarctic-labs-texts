12 C.F.R. § 337.1 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:12:337.1
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-337.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 337 (Unsafe And Unsound Banking Practices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this part apply to certain banking practices which are likely to have adverse effects on the safety and soundness of insured State nonmember banks or which are likely to result in violations of law, rule, or regulation.
