25 C.F.R. § 26.8 — Where do I go to apply for Job Placement and Training assistance?
Citation: us:cfr:25:26.8
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-26.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 26 (Job Placement And Training Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may apply for Job Placement and Training assistance at the servicing office nearest to your current residence.
