28 C.F.R. § 83.210 — To whom must I distribute my drug-free workplace statement?
Citation: us:cfr:28:83.210
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-83.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 83 (Government-Wide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Grants)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must require that a copy of the statement described in § 83.205 be given to each employee who will be engaged in the performance of any Federal award.
