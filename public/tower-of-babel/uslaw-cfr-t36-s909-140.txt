36 C.F.R. § 909.140 — Employment.
Citation: us:cfr:36:909.140
Creator: Pennsylvania Avenue Development Corporation
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 909 (Enforcement Of Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap In Programs Or Activities Conducted By The Pennsylvania Avenue Development Corporation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No qualified handicapped person shall, on the basis of handicap, be subjected to discrimination in employment under any program or activity conducted by the agency. The definitions, requirements, and procedures of section 501 of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973 (29 U.S.C. 791), as established by the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission in 29 CFR part 1613, shall apply to employment in federally conducted programs or activities.
