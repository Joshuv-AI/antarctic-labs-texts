39 U.S.C. § 3209 — Executive departments to supply information
Citation: us:usc:39:3209
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title39-section3209
Context: United States Code, Title 39 (Postal Service); Part IV (Mail Matter); Chapter 32 (Penalty And Franked Mail). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Persons and governmental organizations authorized to use penalty mail shall supply all information requested by the Postal Service necessary to carry out the provisions of this chapter as soon as practicable after request therefor.
