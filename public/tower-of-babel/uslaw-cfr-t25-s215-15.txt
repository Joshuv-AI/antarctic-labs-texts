25 C.F.R. § 215.15 — Leases to be accompanied by Form D.
Citation: us:cfr:25:215.15
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-215.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 215 (Lead And Zinc Mining Operations And Leases, Quapaw Agency). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Lead and zinc leases should be accompanied, when filed, with application for approval (Form D) 1 made under oath, and said application shall set forth the information therein required.
