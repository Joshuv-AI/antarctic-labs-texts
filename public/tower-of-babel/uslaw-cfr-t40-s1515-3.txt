40 C.F.R. § 1515.3 — Are there any CEQ records that CEQ proactively discloses and for which I do not have to make a request?
Citation: us:cfr:40:1515.3
Creator: Council On Environmental Quality
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1515.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1515 (Freedom Of Information Act Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. The FOIA requires CEQ to proactively disclose—that is, to make certain records available for public inspection online—records that have been or are likely to become the subject of repeated requests. You can find those records, together with an index, at https://www.whitehouse.gov/ceq/foia/.
