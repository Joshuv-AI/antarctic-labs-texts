40 U.S.C. § 8162 — Ailanthus trees prohibited
Citation: us:usc:40:8162
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section8162
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part D (Public Buildings, Grounds, And Parks In The District Of Columbia); Chapter 81 (Administrative); Subchapter IV (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Ailanthus trees shall not be purchased for, or planted in, the public grounds.
