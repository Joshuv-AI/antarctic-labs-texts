22 C.F.R. § 92.91 — Service of documents at request of Congressional committees.
Citation: us:cfr:22:92.91
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-92.91
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 92 (Notarial And Related Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Officers of the Foreign Service have no authority to serve upon persons in their consular districts legal process such as subpoenas or citations in connection with Congressional investigations. All requests for such service should be referred to the Department of State.
