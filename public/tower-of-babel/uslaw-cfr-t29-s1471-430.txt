29 C.F.R. § 1471.430 — How do I check to see if a person is excluded or disqualified?
Citation: us:cfr:29:1471.430
Creator: Federal Mediation And Conciliation Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1471.430
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1471 (Governmentwide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You check to see if a person is excluded or disqualified in two ways:
(a) You as an agency official must check the EPLS when you take any action listed in § 1471.425.
(b) You must review information that a participant gives you, as required by § 1471.335, about its status or the status of the principals of a transaction.
