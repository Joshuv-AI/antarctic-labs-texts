20 U.S.C. § 9109 — Prohibition on use of funds for construction
Citation: us:usc:20:9109
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section9109
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 72 (Museum And Library Services); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No funds appropriated to carry out this chapter, subchapter II of this chapter, or subchapter III of this chapter may be used for construction expenses.
