23 C.F.R. § 636.208 — May I use my existing prequalification procedures with design-build contracts?
Citation: us:cfr:23:636.208
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-636.208
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 636 (Design-Build Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, you may use your existing prequalification procedures for either construction or engineering design firms as a supplement to the procedures in this part.
