A note on the projective representations of p p -solvable and π \pi -separable groups
Mariagrazia Bianchi, Nicola Sambonet

Abstract
Following Gluck and Wolf we complete the Itô–Michler’s Theorem for the projective representations of a p p -solvable or π \pi -separable group, and then we relate the projective irreducible modules of such a group with those of its Sylow p p -subroups and Hall π \pi -subgroups.
