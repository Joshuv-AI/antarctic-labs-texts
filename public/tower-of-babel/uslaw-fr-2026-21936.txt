Proposed Collection; Comment Request

AGENCY: Department of the Navy, Department of Defense (DoD).

ACTION: 60-Day information collection notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In compliance with the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995, the 
Naval Intelligence Activity announces the proposed extension of an 
approved public information collection and seeks public comment on the 
provisions thereof. Comments are invited on: whether the collection of 
information is necessary for the proper performance of the functions of 
the agency, including whether the information shall have practical 
utility; the accuracy of the agency's estimate of the burden of the 
information collection; ways to enhance the quality, utility, and 
clarity of the information to be collected; and ways to minimize the 
burden of the information collection on respondents, including through 
the use of automated collection techniques or other forms of 
information technology.

DATES: Consideration will be given to all comments received by December 
28, 2026.

ADDRESSES: You may submit comments, identified by docket number and 
title, by any of the following methods:

    Mail: Department of Defense, Office of the Director of 
Administration and Management, Oversight and Compliance Directorate,

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: 
    Title; Associated Form; and OMB Number: Navy Insider Threat Report 
Form; OPNAV 5510/423; OMB Control Number 0703-0092.
    Needs and Uses: The Department of the Navy uses Office of the Chief 
of Naval Operations (OPNAV) Form 5510/423, Navy Insider Threat Report, 
to collect standardized information concerning potential insider 
threats in support of the Navy Insider Threat Program. The collection 
supports the Navy's implementation of Executive Order 13587, which 
requires executive branch agencies to establish insider threat programs 
to deter, detect, and mitigate insider threats and unauthorized 
disclosures of classified national security information.
    The form enables the Navy Analytic Hub to receive, assess, 
integrate, and respond to reports of concerning behavior or other 
information that may indicate a potential insider threat. Use of a 
standardized form reduces the need for repeated follow-up 
communications

[[Page 67828]]

and provides a consistent means to document relevant information, 
including submitter information, available information about the 
subject of the report, a narrative description of the concern, and any 
known mitigation actions or previous reporting.
    The form may be completed by individuals reporting observed 
incidents or information within their knowledge, as well as by Navy 
personnel gathering information in support of an insider threat 
inquiry. For purposes of the Paperwork Reduction Act, the collection 
accounts only for burden imposed on contractor respondents. Respondents 
provide only the information available to them; they are not expected 
to provide personal information about a report subject or information 
concerning mitigation measures when they do not have access to that 
information. Navy Analytic Hub personnel may supplement incomplete 
reports using authorized Government sources and coordination with 
appropriate command personnel.
    Information collected through OPNAV Form 5510/423 is used to 
support the Navy Insider Threat Program's mission to prevent, detect, 
deter, and mitigate risks posed by malicious or unwitting insiders.
    Affected Public: Individuals or households.
    Annual Burden Hours: 25.
    Number of Respondents: 100.
    Responses per Respondent: 1.
    Annual Responses: 100.
    Average Burden per Response: 15 minutes.
    Frequency: On Occasion.
