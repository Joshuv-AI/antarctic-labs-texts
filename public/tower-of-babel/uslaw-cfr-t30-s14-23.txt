30 C.F.R. § 14.23 — New technology.
Citation: us:cfr:30:14.23
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-14.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 14 (Requirements For The Approval Of Flame-Resistant Conveyor Belts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

MSHA may approve a conveyor belt that incorporates technology for which the requirements of this part are not applicable if the Agency determines that the conveyor belt is as safe as those which meet the requirements of this part.
