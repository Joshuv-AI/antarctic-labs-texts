33 C.F.R. § 245.60 — Reimbursement for removal costs.
Citation: us:cfr:33:245.60
Creator: Corps Of Engineers, Department Of The Army, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-245.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 245 (Removal Of Wrecks And Other Obstructions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Corps of Engineers will seek reimbursement from the owner, operator, or lessee, if identified, for all removal and disposal costs in excess of the value of the recovered vessel (or other obstruction) and cargo.
