13 C.F.R. § 113.120 — Transfers of property.
Citation: us:cfr:13:113.120
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-113.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 113 (Nondiscrimination In Financial Assistance Programs Of Sba—Effectuation Of Policies Of Federal Government And Sba Administrator). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a recipient sells or otherwise transfers property financed in whole or in part with Federal financial assistance to a transferee that operates any education program or activity, and the Federal share of the fair market value of the property is not upon such sale or transfer properly accounted for to the Federal Government, both the transferor and the transferee shall be deemed to be recipients, subject to the provisions of §§ 113.205 through 113.235(a).
