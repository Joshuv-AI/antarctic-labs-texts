45 C.F.R. § 2556.100 — Which entities are eligible to apply to become VISTA sponsors?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2556.100
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2556.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2556 (Volunteers In Service To America). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following types of entities are eligible to apply to become VISTA sponsors and thereby undertake projects in the U.S. and certain U.S. territories:
(a) Private nonprofit organizations.
(b) Public nonprofit organizations.
(c) State government or state government agencies.
(d) Local government or local government agencies.
(e) Tribal government or tribal government agencies.
