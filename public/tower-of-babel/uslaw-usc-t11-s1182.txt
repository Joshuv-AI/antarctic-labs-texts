11 U.S.C. § 1182 — Definitions

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 11 (Reorganization); Subchapter V (Small Business Debtor Reorganization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this subchapter:
(1) Debtor.—
The term “debtor” means a small business debtor.
(2) Debtor in possession.—
The term “debtor in possession” means the debtor, unless removed as debtor in possession under section 1185(a) of this title.
