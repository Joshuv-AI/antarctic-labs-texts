41 C.F.R. § 302-3.201 — -3.201 Involuntary relocations (i.e., due to reduction in force, cessation, or transfer of work).
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-3.201
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-3.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-3 (3—Relocation Allowance By Specific Type). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an employee is subject to an involuntary transfer (i.e., due to reduction in force, cessation, or transfer of work), the transfer is considered to be in the interest of the Government.
