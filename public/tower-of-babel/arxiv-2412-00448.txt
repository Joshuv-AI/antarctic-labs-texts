Blow up versus scattering below the mass-energy threshold for the focusing NLH with potential
Shuang Ji, Jing Lu

Abstract
. In this paper, we study the blow up and scattering result of the solution to the focusing nonlinear Hartree equation with potential i ∂ t u + Δ u − V u = − ( | ⋅ | − 3 ∗ | u | 2 ) u , ( t , x ) ∈ ℝ × ℝ 5 i\partial_{t}u+\Delta u-Vu=-(|\cdot|^{-3}\ast|u|^{2})u,\qquad(t,x)\in{\mathbb{R}}\times{\mathbb{R}}^{5} in the energy space H 1 ​ ( ℝ 5 ) {H}^{1}({\mathbb{R}}^{5}) below the mass-energy threshold. The potential V V we considered is an extension of Kato potential in some sense. We extend the results of Meng [ 26 ] to nonlinear Hartree equation with potential V V under some conditions. By establishing a Virial-Morawetz estimate and a scattering criteria, we obtain the scattering theory based on the method from Dodson-Murphy [ 11 ] .

Introduction

In this paper, we consider the Cauchy problem of the following nonlinear Hartree equation with potential:

\left\{\begin{aligned} &i\partial_{t}u+\Delta u-Vu=\mu(|\cdot|^{-\gamma}\ast|u|^{2})u,\\
&u(0)=u_{0}\in{H}^{1}({\mathbb{R}}^{d}),\end{aligned}\right.\qquad(t,x)\in{\mathbb{R}}\times{\mathbb{R}}^{d}

)

in (
2.2
) corresponds to
\|f\|_{L^{q}}\lesssim\|f\|_{{{W}}_{V}^{s,p}}
.
∎

Lemma 2.8

(Equivalence of Sobolev spaces)
.

For
d\geqslant 3
, let
V:{\mathbb{R}}^{d}\rightarrow{\mathbb{R}}
satisfy
\mathbf{(H1)}
and
\mathbf{(H2)}
. Then it holds that

\|f\|_{{\dot{W}}_{V}^{s,p}}\sim\|f\|_{{\dot{W}}^{s,p}},\quad\|f\|_{W_{V}^{s,p}}\sim\|f\|_{W^{s,p}},

where
1<p<\frac{3}{s}
and
0\leqslant s\leqslant 2
.

Proof.

Let
0\leqslant a\leqslant 1
. On one hand, using Lemma
2.1
, we have

\displaystyle\|(a+\mathcal{H})f\|_{L^{r}}

\displaystyle\leqslant\|(a-\Delta)f\|_{L^{r}}+\|Vf\|_{L^{r}}

\displaystyle\leqslant\|(a-\Delta)f\|_{L^{r}}+\|V\|_{L^{\frac{d}{2}}}\|f\|_{L^{\frac{dr}{d-2r}}}

\displaystyle\lesssim\|(a-\Delta)f\|_{L^{r}}

and

\displaystyle\|(a-\Delta)f\|_{L^{r}}

\displaystyle\leqslant\|(a+\mathcal{H})f\|_{L^{r}}+\|Vf\|_{L^{r}}

\displaystyle\leqslant\|(a+\mathcal{H})f\|_{L^{r}}+\|V\|_{L^{\frac{d}{2}}}\|f\|_{L^{\frac{dr}{d-2r}}}

\displaystyle\lesssim\|(a+\mathcal{H})f\|_{L^{r}}.

On the other hand, according to the boundness of the imaginary power operator in
[
39
]
, we have

\|(a+\mathcal{H})^{ib}\|_{L^{r}\to L^{r}}\lesssim\langle b\rangle^{\frac{d}{2}}

and

\|(a-\Delta)^{ib}\|_{L^{r}\to L^{r}}\lesssim\langle b\rangle^{\frac{d}{2}}

for any
b\in{\mathbb{R}}
and
1<r<\infty
.

Combining the above two parts, we have

\displaystyle\|(a+\mathcal{H})^{z}f\|_{L^{r}}\lesssim\langle\mathop{\mathrm{Im}}z\rangle^{\frac{d}{2}}\|(a-\Delta)^{z}f\|_{L^{r}},

\displaystyle\|(a-\Delta)^{z}f\|_{L^{r}}\lesssim\langle\mathop{\mathrm{Im}}z\rangle^{\frac{d}{2}}\|(a+\mathcal{H})^{z}f\|_{L^{r}}

for
1<r<\infty
when
\mathop{\mathrm{Re}}z=0
and
1<r<\frac{d}{2}
when
\mathop{\mathrm{Re}}z=1
. By the Stein-Weiss complex interpolation, we obtain the equivalence

\|(a+\mathcal{H})f\|_{L^{r}}\sim\|(a-\Delta)f\|_{L^{r}},~1<r<\frac{d}{2}.

In particular,
a=0
corresponds to
\|f\|_{{\dot{W}}_{V}^{s,p}}
and
a=1
corresponds to
\|f\|_{{{W}}_{V}^{s,p}}
.
∎

Proposition 3.3

(Local well-posedness)
.

Let
V:{\mathbb{R}}^{5}\rightarrow{\mathbb{R}}
satisfy
\mathbf{(H1)}
and
\mathbf{(H2)}
. Then the equation (

NLH
V

) is locally well-posed in
H^{1}
for
(\mu,\gamma,d)=(-1,3,5)
.

Proof.

Our proof is in view of a complete Banach space
(X,d)
, where

\displaystyle X:\{u:\|\langle\nabla_{V}\rangle u\|_{S(L^{2},I)}\leqslant M\}

and

d(u,v):=\|u-v\|_{S(L^{2},I)}.

I=[0,T]
with
T,M>0
will be chosen later. We demonstrate the integral transformation as

\Phi(u(t)):=e^{-it\mathcal{H}}u_{0}+i\int_{0}^{t}e^{-i(t-s)\mathcal{H}}(|\cdot|^{-3}\ast|u|^{2})u(s)ds.

(3.1)

The chief aim is to prove that
\Phi
is a contraction on
(X,d)
.
According to Lemma
2.1
, Lemma
2.2
, and Lemma
3.2
, we have

\displaystyle~\|\langle\nabla_{V}\rangle\Phi(u)\|_{S(L^{2},I)}

\displaystyle\leqslant

\displaystyle~\|e^{-it\mathcal{H}}\langle\nabla_{V}\rangle u_{0}\|_{S(L^{2},I)}+\biggl\|\int_{0}^{t}e^{-i(t-s)\mathcal{H}}\langle\nabla_{V}\rangle(|\cdot|^{-3}\ast|u|^{2})u(s)ds\biggl\|_{S(L^{2},I)}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle~\|\langle\nabla_{V}\rangle u_{0}\|_{L_{x}^{2}({\mathbb{R}}^{5})}+\|\langle\nabla_{V}\rangle(|\cdot|^{-3}\ast|u|^{2})u\|_{L_{t}^{2}L_{x}^{\frac{10}{7}}(I\times{\mathbb{R}}^{5})}

\displaystyle\sim

\displaystyle~\|u_{0}\|_{H_{x}^{1}({\mathbb{R}}^{5})}+\biggl\|\nabla\biggl((|\cdot|^{-3}\ast|u|^{2})u\biggr)\biggr\|_{L_{t}^{2}L_{x}^{\frac{10}{7}}(I\times{\mathbb{R}}^{5})}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle~\|u_{0}\|_{H_{x}^{1}({\mathbb{R}}^{5})}+\biggl\|3\|u\|_{L_{x}^{\frac{10}{3}}({\mathbb{R}}^{5})}^{2}\|u\|_{H_{x}^{1}({\mathbb{R}}^{5})}\biggl\|_{L_{t}^{2}(I)}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle~\|u_{0}\|_{H_{x}^{1}({\mathbb{R}}^{5})}+3T^{\frac{1}{4}}\|u\|_{L_{t}^{2}L_{x}^{\frac{10}{3}}(I\times{\mathbb{R}}^{5})}^{2}\|u\|_{L_{t}^{\infty}H_{x}^{1}(I\times{\mathbb{R}}^{5})}\lesssim\|u_{0}\|_{H_{x}^{1}({\mathbb{R}}^{5})}+3T^{\frac{1}{4}}M^{3}.

Similarly, we have

\displaystyle~d(\Phi(u)-\Phi(v))=\|\Phi(u)-\Phi(v)\|_{S(L^{2},I)}

\displaystyle\leqslant

\displaystyle~\biggl\|\int_{0}^{t}e^{-i(t-s)\mathcal{H}}[(|\cdot|^{-3}\ast|u|^{2})u-(|\cdot|^{-3}\ast|v|^{2})v](s)ds\biggl\|_{S(L^{2},I)}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle~\|(|\cdot|^{-3}\ast|u|^{2})u-(|\cdot|^{-3}\ast|v|^{2})v\|_{S^{\prime}(L^{2},I)}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle~2T^{\frac{1}{4}}\biggl(\|u\|_{L_{t}^{2}L_{x}^{\frac{10}{3}}(I\times{\mathbb{R}}^{5})}^{2}+\|u+v\|_{L_{t}^{2}L_{x}^{\frac{10}{3}}(I\times{\mathbb{R}}^{5})}\|v\|_{L_{t}^{\infty}L_{x}^{2}(I\times{\mathbb{R}}^{5})}\biggr)\|u-v\|_{S(L^{2},I)}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle~6T^{\frac{1}{4}}\|u\|_{S(L^{2},I)}^{2}d(u,v)\lesssim 6T^{\frac{1}{4}}M^{2}d(u,v).

It implies that for any
u,v\in X
, there exists the constant
C>0
independent of
u_{0}
and
T
such that

\|\langle\nabla_{V}\rangle\Phi(u)\|_{S(L^{2},I)}\leqslant C\|u_{0}\|_{H_{x}^{1}({\mathbb{R}}^{5})}+3CT^{\frac{1}{4}}M^{3},

If we choose

and take

sufficiently small, then we have

\|\langle\nabla_{V}\rangle\Phi(u)\|_{S(L^{2},I)}\leqslant M,~d(\Phi(u)-\Phi(v))\leqslant d(u,v).

The proof of Local well-posedness is completed.

Proposition 4.1

(Scattering criteria)
.

For
(\mu,\gamma,d)=(-1,3,5)
in (

NLH
V

), let
V:{\mathbb{R}}^{5}\rightarrow{\mathbb{R}}
satisfy
\mathbf{(H1)}
and
\mathbf{(H2)}

V

\sup_{t}\|u(t)\|_{H^{1}}:=\mathcal{E}<+\infty,

R>0
and

\lim_{t\to\infty}\int_{B(0,R)}|u(t,x)|^{2}dx\leqslant{\varepsilon}^{1+},

(4.2)

H^{1}({\mathbb{R}}^{5})

Proof.

Our proof is divided into four steps.

\mathit{Step\ one}
. In this step, we will prove the Strichartz norm of

||u(t,x)||_{L_{t}^{\infty}L_{x}^{\frac{5}{2}}([T-l,T]\times{\mathbb{R}}^{5})}\lesssim{\varepsilon}^{\frac{1}{4}+}.

(4.3)

According to the assumption (
4.2

such that

\int_{B(0,R)}|u(T_{1},x)|^{2}dx\leqslant{\varepsilon}^{1+}.

(4.4)

Let

\eta_{R}(x)=\left\{\begin{aligned} &1,\qquad&|x|\leqslant\frac{R}{2},\\

with

To establish a connection between

\displaystyle~\Big|\frac{d}{dt}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{5}}|\eta_{R}(x)|^{2}|u(T_{1},x)|^{2}dx\Big|

\displaystyle\leqslant

\displaystyle~\biggl\|\biggl(\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{5}}|\eta_{R}(x)|^{2}|u(T_{1},x)|^{2}dx+\frac{6}{R}||u||_{H_{x}^{1}({\mathbb{R}}^{5})}^{2}|t-T_{1}|\biggl)^{\frac{1}{2}}\biggl\|_{L_{t}^{\infty}([T-l,T])}

\displaystyle\leqslant

\displaystyle~\biggl\|\biggl({\varepsilon}^{1+}+\frac{6}{R}||u||_{H_{x}^{1}({\mathbb{R}}^{5})}^{2}|t-T_{1}|\biggl)^{\frac{1}{2}}\biggl\|_{L_{t}^{\infty}([T-l,T])}\leqslant 2{\varepsilon}^{\frac{1}{2}+},

where

,

.

2.4

\displaystyle||\eta_{R}(x)u(t,x)||_{L_{t}^{\infty}L_{x}^{\frac{5}{2}}([T-l,T]\times{\mathbb{R}}^{5})}

\displaystyle\leqslant

\displaystyle||\eta_{R}(x)u(t,x)||_{L_{t}^{\infty}L_{x}^{2}([T-l,T]\times{\mathbb{R}}^{5})}^{\frac{1}{2}}||\eta_{R}(x)u(t,x)||_{L_{t}^{\infty}L_{x}^{\frac{10}{3}}([T-l,T]\times{\mathbb{R}}^{5})}^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle\leqslant

\displaystyle\sqrt{2}{\varepsilon}^{\frac{1}{4}+}||u(t,x)||_{L_{t}^{\infty}H_{x}^{1}({\mathbb{R}}\times{\mathbb{R}}^{5})}^{\frac{1}{2}}.

On the other hand, we also have

\displaystyle~||(1-\eta_{R}(x))u(t,x)||_{L_{t}^{\infty}L_{x}^{\frac{5}{2}}([T-l,T]\times{\mathbb{R}}^{5})}

\displaystyle\leqslant

5.
Proof of Blow Up

In this section, we will prove blow up result.

Firstly, using Hardy inequality

\int_{0}^{\infty}\biggl[\frac{1}{x}\int_{0}^{x}f(\xi)d\xi\biggr]^{p}dx\leqslant\biggl(\frac{p}{p-1}\biggr)^{p}\int_{0}^{\infty}f^{p}(x)dx,\quad f(x)\geqslant 0,\quad p>1

and the conservation of mass, we have

\displaystyle\|u_{0}\|_{L^{2}({\mathbb{R}}^{5})}^{2}=\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{5}}|u(x,t)|^{2}dx=\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{5}}|x||u|\cdot\frac{|u|}{|x|}dx\leqslant\|xu(t)\|_{L^{2}}\|u(t)\|_{\dot{H}^{1}}.

It is worth mention that if

Thus, before to prove the blow up part in Theorem

) and (
5.12
), can be simplified as the following equation

\displaystyle\frac{d^{2}}{dt^{2}}V_{{\varphi}_{R}}(t)\leqslant-8\delta+o_{R}(1)+C(o_{R}(1)+{\varepsilon})^{\frac{1}{2}}

for all
t\in[0,T]
. Choosing
{\varepsilon}>0
small enough and
R>0
large enough so that

then we have

\frac{d^{2}}{dt^{2}}V_{{\varphi}_{R}}(t)\leqslant-8\delta+4\delta=-4\delta

for all
t\in[0,T]
. It follows that

\displaystyle V_{{\varphi}_{R}}(T)

\displaystyle\leqslant V_{{\varphi}_{R}}(0)+V_{{\varphi}_{R}}^{\prime}(0)T-2\delta T^{2}

\displaystyle\leqslant V_{{\varphi}_{R}}(0)+V_{{\varphi}_{R}}^{\prime}(0)\frac{{\varepsilon}R}{C}-2\delta(\frac{{\varepsilon}R}{C})^{2}

\displaystyle\leqslant o_{R}(1)R^{2}+o_{R}(1)\frac{{\varepsilon}R^{2}}{C}-2\delta(\frac{{\varepsilon}^{2}R^{2}}{C^{2}})

where we have used
