43 C.F.R. § 5511.2-4 — -4 Timber on withdrawn lands.
Citation: us:cfr:43:5511.2-4
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-5511.2-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 5510 (Free Use Of Timber). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Sections 5511.2-1 to 5511.2-5 are inapplicable to timber on withdrawn areas unless the order of withdrawal so permits.
