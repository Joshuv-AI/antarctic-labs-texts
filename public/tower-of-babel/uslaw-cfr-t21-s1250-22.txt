21 C.F.R. § 1250.22 — General requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1250.22
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1250.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1250 (Interstate Conveyance Sanitation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All food and drink served on conveyances shall be clean, wholesome, and free from spoilage, and shall be prepared, stored, handled, and served in accordance with the requirements prescribed in this subpart and § 1240.20 of this chapter.
