2 U.S.C. § 6543 — Designation of reporters
Citation: us:usc:2:6543
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section6543
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 65 (Senate Officers And Administration); Subchapter II (Secretary Of The Senate); Part A (Organization And Personnel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The reporters of debates in the office of the Secretary of the Senate are hereby designated the official reporters of debates of the Senate.
