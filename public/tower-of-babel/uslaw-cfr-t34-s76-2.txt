34 C.F.R. § 76.2 — Exceptions in program regulations to part 76.
Citation: us:cfr:34:76.2
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-76.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 76 (State-Administered Formula Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a program has regulations that are not consistent with part 76, the implementing regulations for that program identify the sections of part 76 that do not apply.
