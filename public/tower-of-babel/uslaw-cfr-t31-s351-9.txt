31 C.F.R. § 351.9 — When will I receive the redemption value of my Series EE savings bonds?
Citation: us:cfr:31:351.9
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-351.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 351 (Offering Of United States Savings Bonds, Series Ee). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You will be paid the redemption value of your definitive bond when you surrender the bond for payment as provided in these regulations and in 31 CFR part 353.
(b) You will be paid the redemption value of your book-entry bond when it reaches final maturity, if you have not redeemed the bond previously.
