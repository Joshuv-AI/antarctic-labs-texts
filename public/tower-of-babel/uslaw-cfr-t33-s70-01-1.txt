33 C.F.R. § 70.01-1 — -1 General provisions.
Citation: us:cfr:33:70.01-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-70.01-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 70 (Interference With Or Damage To Aids To Navigation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person, excluding the Armed Forces, shall obstruct or interfere with any aid to navigation established and maintained by the Coast Guard, or any private aid to navigation established and maintained in accordance with part 64, 66, 67, or 118 of this chapter.
