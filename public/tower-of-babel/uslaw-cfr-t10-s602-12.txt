10 C.F.R. § 602.12 — Cost sharing.
Citation: us:cfr:10:602.12
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-602.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 602 (Epidemiology And Other Health Studies Financial Assistance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Cost sharing is not required, nor will it be considered, as a criterion in the evaluation and selection process unless otherwise provided under § 602.9(d)(5).
