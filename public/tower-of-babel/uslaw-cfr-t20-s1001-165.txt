20 C.F.R. § 1001.165 — When will the uniform national threshold EER be published?
Citation: us:cfr:20:1001.165
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary For Veterans' Employment And Training Service, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-1001.165
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 1001 (Services For Veterans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When practicable, the Veterans' Employment and Training Service (VETS) will publish the uniform national threshold EER for a given program year by the end of December of the calendar year in which that program year ends.
