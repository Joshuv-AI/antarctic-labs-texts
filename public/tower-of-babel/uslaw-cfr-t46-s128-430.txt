46 C.F.R. § 128.430 — Non-integral keel cooler installations.
Citation: us:cfr:46:128.430
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-128.430
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 128 (Marine Engineering: Equipment And Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each hull penetration for a non-integral keel cooler installation must be made through a cofferdam or at a seachest and must be provided with isolation valves fitted as close to the sea inlet as possible.
(b) Each non-integral keel cooler must be protected against damage from debris and grounding by protective guards or by recessing the cooler into the hull.
