41 C.F.R. § 302-3.211 — -3.211 Return eligibility for dependent who turned 21 while overseas.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-3.211
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-3.211
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-3 (3—Relocation Allowance By Specific Type). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A dependent who turned 21 while overseas is entitled to return travel to the place of actual residence at the expense of the Government only if the dependent traveled overseas as a dependent of the employee under their TA, but not beyond the end of the current agreed tour of duty.
