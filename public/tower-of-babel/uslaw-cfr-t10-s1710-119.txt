10 C.F.R. § 1710.119 — Non-waiver of rights.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1710.119
Creator: Defense Nuclear Facilities Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1710.119
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1710 (Federal Employee Salary Offset Procedures For The Collection Of A Debt Owed To The Federal Government). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee's involuntary payment of all or any part of a debt collected under these regulations will not be construed as a waiver of any rights that the employee may have under 5 U.S.C. 5514 or any other provision of contract or law, unless there are statutes or contracts to the contrary.
