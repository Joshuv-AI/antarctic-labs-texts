30 C.F.R. § 556.602 — What is the primary term of my sulfur lease?
Citation: us:cfr:30:556.602
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-556.602
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 556 (Leasing Of Sulfur Or Oil And Gas And Financial Assurance Requirements In The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Your sulfur lease will have a primary term of not more than 10 years, as specified in the lease.
(b) BOEM will announce the primary term prior to the lease sale.
(c) The lease will expire at the end of the primary term unless maintained beyond that term in accordance with the provisions of § 556.603.
