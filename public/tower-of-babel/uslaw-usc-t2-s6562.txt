2 U.S.C. § 6562 — Transfers of funds by Secretary of Senate; approval of Committee on Appropriations
Citation: us:usc:2:6562
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section6562
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 65 (Senate Officers And Administration); Subchapter II (Secretary Of The Senate); Part B (General Powers And Duties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

During any fiscal year (commencing with the fiscal year beginning October 1, 1982) the Secretary of the Senate is authorized to make such transfers between appropriations of funds available for disbursement by him during such year, subject to the approval of the Committee on Appropriations of the Senate.
