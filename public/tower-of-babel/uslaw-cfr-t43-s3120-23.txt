43 C.F.R. § 3120.23 — Lease size.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3120.23
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3120.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3120 (Competitive Leases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Lands may be offered in leasing units of not more than 2,560 acres outside Alaska, or 5,760 acres within Alaska, which may be as nearly compact in form as possible.
