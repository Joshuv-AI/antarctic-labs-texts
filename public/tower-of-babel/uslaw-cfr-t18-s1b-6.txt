18 C.F.R. § 1b.6 — b.6 Preliminary investigations.
Citation: us:cfr:18:1b.6
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-1b.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 1b (PART 1b—RULES RELATING TO INVESTIGATIONS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission or its staff may, in its discretion, initiate a preliminary investigation. In such investigations, no process is issued or testimony compelled. Where it appears from the preliminary investigation that a formal investigation is appropriate, the staff will so recommend to the Commission.
