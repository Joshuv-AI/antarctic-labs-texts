41 U.S.C. § 4708 — Payment of reimbursable indirect costs in cost-type research and development contracts with educational institutions
Citation: us:usc:41:4708
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title41-section4708
Context: United States Code, Title 41 (Public Contracts); Chapter 47 (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A cost-type research and development contract (including a grant) with a university, college, or other educational institution may provide for payment of reimbursable indirect costs on the basis of predetermined fixed-percentage rates applied to the total of the reimbursable direct costs incurred or to an element of the total of the reimbursable direct costs incurred.
