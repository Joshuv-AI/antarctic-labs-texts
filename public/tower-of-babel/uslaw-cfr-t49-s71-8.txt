49 C.F.R. § 71.8 — Mountain zone.
Citation: us:cfr:49:71.8
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-71.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 71 (Standard Time Zone Boundaries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The fourth zone, the mountain standard time zone, includes that part of the United States that is west of the boundary line between the central and mountain standard time zones described in § 71.7 and east of the boundary line between the mountain and Pacific standard time zones described in § 71.9.
