40 C.F.R. § 429.120 — Applicability; description of the sawmills and planing mills subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:429.120
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-429.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 429 (Timber Products Processing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to discharges to waters of the United States and to the introduction of process wastewater pollutants into publicly owned treatment works from the timber products processing procedures that include all or part of the following operations: bark removal (other than hydraulic barking as defined in § 429.11 of this part), sawing, resawing, edging, trimming, planing and machining.
