48 C.F.R. § 1536.521 — 1536.521 Specifications and drawings for construction.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1536.521
Creator: Chapter 15—Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1536.521
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1536 (Construction And Architect-Engineer Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Contracting Officer shall insert the clause at 1552.236-70, Samples and Certificates, in soliciations and contracts when a fixed price construction contract is expected to exceed the simplified acquisition threshold limitation. The clause may be inserted in solicitations and contracts when the contract is expected to be within the simplified acquisition threshold limitation.
