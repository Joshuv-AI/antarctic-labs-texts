28 U.S.C. § 2402 — Jury trial in actions against United States
Citation: us:usc:28:2402
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section2402
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part VI (Particular Proceedings); Chapter 161 (United States As Party Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Subject to chapter 179 of this title, any action against the United States under section 1346 shall be tried by the court without a jury, except that any action against the United States under section 1346(a)(1) shall, at the request of either party to such action, be tried by the court with a jury.
