18 C.F.R. § 390.1 — Electronic registration.
Citation: us:cfr:18:390.1
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-390.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 390 (Electronic Registration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person who wishes to engage in any of the activities listed in § 390.2 must register electronically through the Commission's web site, in compliance with instructions located on the Web site, at http://www.ferc.gov.
