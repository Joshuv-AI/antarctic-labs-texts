24 C.F.R. § 232.630 — Assurance of completion.
Citation: us:cfr:24:232.630
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-232.630
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 232 (Mortgage Insurance For Nursing Homes, Intermediate Care Facilities, Board And Care Homes, And Assisted Living Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the property upon which the fire safety equipment is to be installed is subject to a mortgage insured or held by the Commissioner pursuant to subpart B of this part, the Commissioner may require such assurance of completion of the contract for installation as he may from time to time prescribe.
