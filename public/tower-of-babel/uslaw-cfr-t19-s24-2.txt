19 C.F.R. § 24.2 — Persons authorized to receive Customs collections.
Citation: us:cfr:19:24.2
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-24.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 24 (Customs Financial And Accounting Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Center directors, port directors, CBP cashiers, CBP officers, CBP dock tellers, and such other officers and employees as the Center director or port director will designate will receive Customs collections.
