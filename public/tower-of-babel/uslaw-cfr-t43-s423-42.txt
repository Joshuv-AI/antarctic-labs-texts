43 C.F.R. § 423.42 — Gambling.
Citation: us:cfr:43:423.42
Creator: Bureau Of Reclamation, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-423.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 423 (Public Conduct On Bureau Of Reclamation Facilities, Lands, And Waterbodies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Commercial gambling in any form, or the operation of gambling devices, is prohibited on Reclamation facilities, lands, and waterbodies unless authorized by applicable treaties or Federal, State, and local laws or regulations.
