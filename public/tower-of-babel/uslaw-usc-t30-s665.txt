30 U.S.C. § 665 — Sites for conducting research; availability of personnel and facilities
Citation: us:usc:30:665
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section665
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 18 (Coal Research And Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Research authorized by this chapter may be conducted wherever suitable personnel and facilities are available.
