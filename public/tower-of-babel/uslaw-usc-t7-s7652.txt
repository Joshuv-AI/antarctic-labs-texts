7 U.S.C. § 7652 — Role of Secretary regarding food and agricultural sciences research and extension
Citation: us:usc:7:7652
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section7652
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 103 (Agricultural Research, Extension, And Education Reform); Subchapter III (Miscellaneous Provisions); Part B (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Agriculture shall be the principal official in the executive branch responsible for coordinating all Federal research and extension activities related to food and agricultural sciences.
