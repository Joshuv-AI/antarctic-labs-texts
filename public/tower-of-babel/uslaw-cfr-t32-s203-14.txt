32 C.F.R. § 203.14 — RAB/TRC reporting requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:32:203.14
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-203.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 203 (Technical Assistance For Public Participation (Tapp) In Defense Environmental Restoration Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The community point of contact of the RAB or TRC must submit a report, to be provided to the installation and to DUSD(ES), to enable the Department of Defense to meet DoD reporting requirements to Congress. This report should include a description of the TAPP project, a summary of services and products obtained, and a statement regarding the overall satisfaction of the community member of the RAB or TRC with the quality of service and/or products received.
