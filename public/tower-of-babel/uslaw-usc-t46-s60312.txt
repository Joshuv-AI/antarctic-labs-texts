46 U.S.C. § 60312 — Rights under treaties preserved
Citation: us:usc:46:60312
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section60312
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 603 (Tonnage Taxes And Light Money). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter and chapter 605 of this title do not affect a right or privilege of a foreign country relating to tonnage taxes or other duties on vessels under a law or treaty of the United States.
