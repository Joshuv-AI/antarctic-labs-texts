37 C.F.R. § 351.12 — Closing the record.
Citation: us:cfr:37:351.12
Creator: Copyright Royalty Board, Library Of Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-351.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 351 (Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To close the record of a proceeding, the presiding Judge shall make an announcement that the taking of evidence has concluded.
