21 C.F.R. § 113.5 — Current good manufacturing practice.
Citation: us:cfr:21:113.5
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-113.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 113 (Thermally Processed Low-Acid Foods Packaged In Hermetically Sealed Containers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The criteria in §§ 113.10, 113.40, 113.60, 113.81, 113.83, 113.87, 113.89, and 113.100 shall apply in determining whether the facilities, methods, practices, and controls used by the commercial processor in the manufacture, processing, or packing of low-acid foods in hermetically sealed containers are operated or administered in a manner adequate to protect the public health.
