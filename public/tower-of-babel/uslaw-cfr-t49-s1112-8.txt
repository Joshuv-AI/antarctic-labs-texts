49 C.F.R. § 1112.8 — Verification.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1112 (Modified Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The original of any pleading filed must show the signature, capacity, and seal, if any, of the person administering the oath, and the date thereof.
