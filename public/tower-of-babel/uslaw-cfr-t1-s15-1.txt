1 C.F.R. § 15.1 — Cooperation.
Citation: us:cfr:1:15.1
Creator: Administrative Committee Of The Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-1/section-15.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 1 (General Provisions), Part 15 (Services To Federal Agencies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-23 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director of the Federal Register shall assist each agency in complying with the pertinent publication laws to assure efficient public service in promulgating administrative documents having the effect of legal notice or of law.
