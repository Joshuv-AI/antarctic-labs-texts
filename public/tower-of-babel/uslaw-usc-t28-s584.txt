28 U.S.C. § 584 — Official stations
Citation: us:usc:28:584
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section584
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part II (Department Of Justice); Chapter 39 (United States Trustees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General may determine the official stations of the United States trustees and assistant United States trustees within the regions for which they were appointed.
