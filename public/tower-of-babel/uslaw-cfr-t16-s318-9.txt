16 C.F.R. § 318.9 — Sunset.
Citation: us:cfr:16:318.9
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-318.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 318 (Health Breach Notification Rule). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If new legislation is enacted establishing requirements for notification in the case of a breach of security that apply to entities covered by this part, the provisions of this part shall not apply to breaches of security discovered on or after the effective date of regulations implementing such legislation.
