40 C.F.R. § 141.521 — What updated watershed control requirements must my unfiltered system implement to continue to avoid filtration?
Citation: us:cfr:40:141.521
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-141.521
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 141 (National Primary Drinking Water Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Your system must take any additional steps necessary to minimize the potential for contamination by Cryptosporidium oocysts in the source water. Your system's watershed control program must, for Cryptosporidium:
(a) Identify watershed characteristics and activities which may have an adverse effect on source water quality; and
(b) Monitor the occurrence of activities which may have an adverse effect on source water quality.
