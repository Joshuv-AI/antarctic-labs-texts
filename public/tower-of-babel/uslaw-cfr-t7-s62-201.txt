7 C.F.R. § 62.201 — Availability of service.

Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-62.201

Services under this part are available to applicants, including international and domestic government agencies, private agricultural businesses, and any financially interested person.
