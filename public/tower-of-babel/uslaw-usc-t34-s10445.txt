34 U.S.C. § 10445 — Staff of Office on Violence Against Women
Citation: us:usc:34:10445
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section10445
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 101 (Justice System Improvement); Subchapter XIX (Grants To Combat Violent Crimes Against Women). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General shall ensure that the Director has adequate staff to support the Director in carrying out the Director’s responsibilities under this subchapter.
