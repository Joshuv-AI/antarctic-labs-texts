On Hermite type sampling Kantorovich operators in the settings of mixed norm Spaces
Puja Sonawane, A. Sathish Kumar

Abstract
. In this paper, we analyze the convergence behavior of Hermite-type sampling Kantorovich operators in the context of mixed norm spaces. We prove certain direct approximation theorems, including the uniform convergence theorem, the Voronovskaja-type asymptotic formula, and an estimate of error in the approximation in terms of the modulus of continuity in mixed norm settings. Next, we estimate the rate of convergence of these sampling Kantorovich operators in terms of the modulus of continuity. In addition, we obtain simultaneous approximation results of these sampling Kantorovich operators, including the uniform approximation, the asymptotic formula, and the approximation error in terms of the modulus of continuity in mixed settings. Finally, using cardinal B B -splines, the implementation of differentiable functions has been shown.

Introduction and Preliminaries

The Shannon Sampling Theorem, a fundamental and important result in Fourier analysis, was independently discovered by Whittaker, Kotelnikov, and Shannon. It states that if a function
f
is band-limited to
[-\pi w,\pi w],

w>0,
then
f
can be completely reconstructed by the reconstruction formula

\displaystyle f(x)=\sum_{k=-\infty}^{\infty}f\bigg(\frac{k}{w}\bigg)\frac{\sin\pi(wx-k)}{\pi(wx-k)},\ \ \ \ (x\in{\mathbb{R}}).

(1.1)

The above sampling theorem is used in many areas like information theory, signal and image processing, artificial intelligence, etc., mainly because it can reconstruct a signal without losing the actual information. The sampling and reconstruction problems in the setting of uniform and non-uniform sampling have been analyzed by several researchers, see
[
4
,
5
,
15
,
30
,
32
,
33

35
,
42
]
and the references therein. Further, the above sampling series has been analyzed in the direction of approximation theory point of view. In particular, the rate of convergence and
L^{p}-
approximation behavior of these sampling series (
1.1
) has been discussed in the series of papers, see
[
8
,
9
,
29
,
41
]
and the references therein.

To approximate the functions that are not necessarily band-limited, Butzer and his school initiated the study of generalized sampling operators. These generalized sampling operators play an important role in signal and image processing, since signals are not necessarily band-limited in nature. So, the approximation behavior of these sampling series is of great importance. For
f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R},

w>0,
the generalized sampling operator is defined by (see
[
19
]
)

(S_{w}^{\chi}f)(x)=\sum_{k=-\infty}^{\infty}f\left(\frac{k}{w}\right)\chi(wx-k),\ \ \ \hskip 14.22636pt(x\in\mathbb{R}),

(1.2)

where
\chi:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}
is a suitable kernel satisfying certain assumptions. The direct approximation theorems for the above sampling series were discussed in

Such operators are called Kantorovich sampling operators. For
f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R},w>0,
the sampling Kantorovich operator is defined by

(K_{w}^{\chi}f)(x)=\displaystyle\sum_{k=-\infty}^{\infty}\left(w\int_{\frac{k}{w}}^{\frac{k+1}{w}}f(u)du\right)\chi(wx-k),\ \ \ \hskip 14.22636pt(x\in\mathbb{R}).

(1.3)

These operators are useful in signal processing, since more information is usually available in the neighbourhood of a point than at the point itself. In recent years the direct and inverse approximation theorems have been discussed for the Kantorovich sampling operators in different settings; see

Very recently, Corso

w>0,

(G_{n,w}f)(x)=\sum_{k=-\infty}^{\infty}\left(\sum_{j=0}^{n}f^{(j)}\left(\frac{k}{w}\right)\left(x-\frac{k}{w}\right)^{j}\right)\chi(wx-k),\ \ \ \hskip 14.22636pt(x\in\mathbb{R}).

(1.4)

We note that if

Further, we note that if

\displaystyle f(x)=\sum_{k=-\infty}^{\infty}(f(k\tau)+f^{{}^{\prime}}(k\tau)(x-k\tau))\sin c^{2}\left(\frac{x}{\tau}-k\right),\ \ \ \ \left(\tau=\frac{2}{w}\right)

(1.5)

for every

\sin c

\sin c(u)=\begin{cases}\dfrac{\sin\pi u}{\pi u},&\text{if}\ u\neq 0\\
1,&\text{if}\ u=0.\par\end{cases}

The above reconstruction formula was proved by Jagerman and Fogel in

The advantage of the above sampling formula is that one can reconstruct or approximate the functions with the help of a few sample points over an interval of same length. Further to improve the sampling rate Linden and Abramson modified the above formula in

for a band-limited and continuous function

For every

\displaystyle f(x)=\sum_{k=-\infty}^{\infty}\left(\sum_{j=0}^{n}\dfrac{1}{j!}f_{j}(k\tau)(x-k\tau)^{j}\right)\sin c^{n+1}\left(\frac{x}{\tau}-k\right),\ \ \ \ \left(\tau=\frac{n+1}{w}\right)

(1.6)

f_{j}(x)=\sum_{i=0}^{j}\binom{j}{i}\left(\dfrac{\pi w}{n+1}\right)^{j-i}\Gamma^{j-i}_{n+1}(0)f^{(i)}(x),\ \ \Gamma^{m}_{n+1}(x)=\dfrac{d^{m}}{dx^{m}}\left(\dfrac{1}{\sin c(\frac{x}{\pi})}\right)^{n+1}.

Given
\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y}\in\mathbb{R}^{d}

\displaystyle\boldsymbol{\alpha}!=\prod_{i=1}^{d}(\alpha_{i}!),\ \binom{\boldsymbol{\beta}}{\boldsymbol{\alpha}}=\dfrac{\boldsymbol{\beta}!}{\boldsymbol{\alpha}!(\boldsymbol{\beta}-\boldsymbol{\alpha})!},\ \partial^{\boldsymbol{\alpha}}f=\dfrac{\partial^{|\boldsymbol{\alpha}|}f}{\partial x_{1}^{\alpha_{1}}...\partial x_{d}^{\alpha_{d}}}.

By

(respectively,

) we denote the space of all continuous and bounded (respectively, uniformly continuous and bounded) functions
f:\mathbb{R}^{d}\to\mathbb{R},
endowed with the supremum norm

Similarly,
C_{b}(\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{d})
(respectively,

) we denote the space of all continuous and bounded (respectively, uniformly continuous and bounded) functions
f:\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{d}\to\mathbb{R},
endowed with the supremum norm
\|f\|_{\infty}:=\displaystyle\sup_{\begin{subarray}{c}x\in\mathbb{R}\\
\mathbf{y}\in\mathbb{R}^{d}\end{subarray}}|f(x,\mathbf{y})|.

Let
C^{n}(\mathbb{R}^{d})
be the space of all
n

\partial^{\boldsymbol{\alpha}}f=\dfrac{\partial^{|\boldsymbol{\alpha}|}f}{\partial x_{1}^{\alpha_{1}}...\partial x_{d}^{\alpha_{d}}}\in C(\mathbb{R}^{d})
with
|\boldsymbol{\alpha}|=n.

We define a mixed Lebesgue space as follows. For
1\leq p,q<\infty,
let
L^{p,q}(\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{d})
denote the space of all complex-valued measurable functions
f
defined on
\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{d}
such that

\|f\|_{L^{p,q}}:=\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}}\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{d}}|f(x,\mathbf{y})|^{q}d\mathbf{y}\right)^{p/q}dx\right)^{1/p}<\infty.

Further, let
L^{\infty,\infty}(\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{d})
denote the set of all complex-valued measurable functions on
\mathbb{R}^{d+1}

\|f\|_{L^{\infty,\infty}}:=ess\sup|f|<\infty
.
For
1\leq p,q<\infty
,
\ell^{p,q}(\mathbb{Z}\times\mathbb{Z}^{d})

c=\big(c(k_{1},k_{2})\big)_{(k_{1}\in\mathbb{Z},k_{2}\in\mathbb{Z}^{d})}

\|c\|_{\ell^{p,q}}:=\left(\sum_{k_{1}\in\mathbb{Z}}\left(\sum_{k_{2}\in\mathbb{Z}^{d}}|c(k_{1},k_{2})|^{q}\right)^{p/q}\right)^{1/p}<\infty.

We denote by
\ell^{\infty,\infty}(\mathbb{Z}\times\mathbb{Z}^{d}),
the space of all complex sequences on
\mathbb{Z}^{d+1}

\|c\|_{\ell^{\infty,\infty}}:=\displaystyle\sup_{k\in\mathbb{Z}^{d+1}}|c(k)|<\infty.

We note that
L^{p,p}(\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{d})=L^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{d+1})
and
\ell^{p,p}(\mathbb{Z}\times\mathbb{Z}^{d})=\ell^{p}(\mathbb{Z}^{d+1})
for
1\leq p<\infty.

Let
\chi:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}.
We say that
\chi
is a kernel of order
n
if it satisfies the following conditions.

(i)

\displaystyle\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}\chi(u-k)=1,
for every
u\in\mathbb{R}.

(ii)

The series

\displaystyle\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}|u-k|^{n}|\chi(u-k)|
is uniformly convergent in
\mathbb{R}.

For kernel
\chi
and
\alpha>0,
we define discrete algebraic moments as

m_{\alpha}(\chi,u):=\displaystyle\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}(k-u)^{\alpha}\chi(u-k),\ u\in\mathbb{R}

and the discrete absolute moment by

M_{\alpha}(\chi):=\displaystyle\sup_{u\in\mathbb{R}}\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}|u-k|^{\alpha}|\chi(u-k)|.

Remark 1
.

Note that if
\chi
is a kernel of order
\alpha
, then
\chi
is kernel of order
\beta,
i.e.
M_{\beta}(\chi)<\infty
for every
0\leq\beta\leq\alpha.

Further, we have
\displaystyle\lim_{w\to\infty}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}k\in\mathbb{Z}\\
|u-k|\geq w\end{subarray}}|u-k|^{n}|\chi(u-k)|=0
is uniformly convergent in
\mathbb{R}.

In this paper, we study the approximation behaviour of the Kantorovich-Hermite-type sampling operator in the context of mixed settings.

Definition 1
.

Let
f:\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{d}\to\mathbb{R}
be
n
times differentiable function and
\phi,\psi_{i},\ i=1,...,d,
are kernels of order
n.
For
w>0,
we define the following Kantorovich-Hermite type sampling operators:

\displaystyle K_{n,w}^{\phi,\psi}f(x,\mathbb{y})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\displaystyle\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}\sum_{\mathbf{m}\in\mathbb{Z}^{d}}\phi(wx-k)\ \prod_{i=1}^{d}\psi_{i}(wy_{i}-m_{i})

\displaystyle\times\left(\sum_{l+|\mathbb{j}|\leq n}\dfrac{1}{l!\mathbb{j}!}w^{d+1}\int_{I_{k}^{w}}\int_{I_{\mathbf{m}}^{w}}\dfrac{\partial^{l+|\mathbb{j}|}f(u,\mathbb{v})}{\partial u^{l}\partial\mathbb{v}^{\mathbb{j}}}{(x-u)}^{l}{(\mathbb{y}-\mathbb{v})^{\mathbb{j}}}\ du\ d\mathbb{v}\right),

where
x\in\mathbb{R},\ \mathbb{y}\in\mathbb{R}^{d},\ I_{k}^{w}=\left[\dfrac{k}{w},\dfrac{k+1}{w}\right]\ \text{and}\ I_{\mathbf{m}}^{w}=\left[\dfrac{m_{1}}{w},\dfrac{m_{1}+1}{w}\right]\times...\times\left[\dfrac{m_{d}}{w},\dfrac{m_{d}+1}{w}\right].

We note that if
\phi
and
\psi_{i},

i=1,...,d,
are kernels of order
n,
and all partial derivatives of
f
upto order
n
are bounded, then the sampling operator
K_{n,w}^{\phi,\psi}
is well-defined. Indeed, we obtain

\displaystyle|K_{n,w}^{\phi,\psi}f(x,\mathbf{y})|

\displaystyle\leq\displaystyle\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}\sum_{\mathbf{m}\in\mathbb{Z}^{d}}|\phi(wx-k)|\prod_{i=1}^{d}|\psi_{i}(wy_{i}-m_{i})|w^{d+1}\sum_{l+|\mathbb{j}|\leq n}\dfrac{1}{l!\mathbb{j}!}\|\partial^{l+\mathbb{j}}f\|_{\infty}

\displaystyle\times\int_{I_{k}^{w}}\int_{I_{\mathbf{m}}^{w}}2^{l-1}\left(\left|x-\dfrac{k}{w}\right|^{l}+\left|\dfrac{k}{w}-u\right|^{l}\right)\prod_{i=1}^{d}2^{j_{i}-1}\left(\left|y_{i}-\dfrac{m_{i}}{w}\right|^{j_{i}}+\left|\dfrac{m_{i}}{w}-v_{i}\right|^{j_{i}}\right)du\ d\mathbb{v}

\displaystyle\leq\sum_{l+|\mathbb{j}|\leq n}\dfrac{1}{l!\mathbb{j}!}\dfrac{2^{l+|\mathbf{j}|-(d+1)}}{w^{l+|\mathbf{j}|}}\|\partial^{l+\mathbb{j}}f\|_{\infty}(M_{l}(\phi)+M_{0}(\phi))\prod_{i=1}^{d}(M_{j_{i}}(\psi_{i})+M_{0}(\psi_{i})),

for all
x\in\mathbb{R},\ \mathbb{y}\in\mathbb{R}^{d}.

The structure of the paper is organized as follows. The main aim of this paper is to analyze the direct convergence theorems of Hermite-type sampling Kantorovich operators
K_{n,w}^{\phi,\psi}
in the context of a mixed norm. In Section
2
, we first prove that if
f\in C_{b}^{n}(\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{d}),
then the sampling Kantorovich operators
K_{n,w}^{\phi,\psi}f\in C_{b}^{p}(\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{d}).
Next, we prove the uniform convergence theorem for these sampling operators. Further, we obtain the rate of convergence of
K_{n,w}^{\phi,\psi}
for
f\in C(\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{d}).
The main tool to estimate the rate of convergence is the modulus of continuity. In this result, we have shown that the error in the approximation can be controlled by the modulus of continuity in the mixed norm. Furthermore, we derive the Voronovskaja-type asymptotic formula for
K_{n,w}^{\phi,\psi}.
The Section
3
, deals with the simultaneous approximation results, including the uniform approximation theorem, the asymptotic formula, and the approximation error in terms of the modulus of continuity in mixed setting for these sampling Kantorovich operators
K_{n,w}^{\phi,\psi}.
In the last Section, we show the implementation of differentiable functions by these operators using cardinal
B
-spline kernel.

2.
Direct Approximation Results

In this section, we discuss certain direct approximation results, including uniform convergence theorem, Voronovskaja-type asymptotic formula and an estimate of error in the approximation in terms of the modulus of continuity in the settings of mixed norm.

Proposition 1
.

Let
\phi
and
\psi_{i}\in C_{b}^{p}(\mathbb{R}),\ i=1,...,d
be a kernel of order
n
such that for every
q=1,...,p,\ p\in\mathbb{N},
the series

\displaystyle\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}|u-k|^{n}|\phi^{(q)}(u-k)|\ \text{and}\ \sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}|u-k|^{n}|\psi_{i}^{(q)}(u-k)|,\ \ i=1,...,d

are uniformly convergent in
\mathbb{R}.

Then for any
f\in C_{b}^{n}(\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{d}),
we have
K_{n,w}^{\phi,\psi}f\in C_{b}^{p}(\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{d}).

Proof.

We know that
\phi,\psi_{i},\ i=1,...,d
is a kernel of order
n.
Then using Remark
1
, the following series

\displaystyle\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}|u-k|^{r}|\phi^{(q)}(u-k)|\ and\ \sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}|u-k|^{r}|\psi_{i}^{(q)}(u-k)|,\ \ i=1,...,d

(0)

are uniformly convergent in
\mathbb{R},
for
r=0,1,...,n
and
q=0,1,...,p.

Let us consider the following partial sum
S_{N,w}^{\phi,\psi}
of the Hermite type Kantorovich sampling operators:

\displaystyle S_{N,w}^{\phi,\psi}f(x,\mathbf{y})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\displaystyle\sum\sum_{k+|\mathbf{m}|\leq N}\phi(wx-k)\ \prod_{i=1}^{d}\psi_{i}(wy_{i}-m_{i})

\displaystyle\times\left(\sum_{l+|\mathbb{j}|\leq n}\dfrac{1}{l!\mathbb{j}!}w^{d+1}\int_{I_{k}^{w}}\int_{I_{\mathbf{m}}^{w}}\dfrac{\partial^{l+|\mathbb{j}|}f(u,\mathbb{v})}{\partial u^{l}\partial\mathbb{v}^{\mathbb{j}}}{(x-u)}^{l}{(\mathbb{y}-\mathbb{v})^{\mathbb{j}}}\ du\ d\mathbb{v}\right).

Clearly, we have
S_{N,w}^{\phi,\psi}f\in C_{b}^{p}(\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{d}).

We now show that
K_{n,w}^{\phi,\psi}f\in C_{b}^{p}(\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{d})
for
f\in C_{b}^{n}(\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{d}).

Let
\epsilon>0
be given. We estimate

\displaystyle|K_{n,w}^{\phi,\psi}f(x,\mathbf{y})-S_{N,w}^{\phi,\psi}f(x,\mathbf{y})|

\displaystyle\leq\displaystyle\sum\sum_{k+|\mathbf{m}|>N}|\phi(wx-k)|\prod_{i=1}^{d}|\psi_{i}(wy_{i}-m_{i})|

\displaystyle\times w^{d+1}\int_{I_{k}^{w}}\int_{I_{\mathbf{m}}^{w}}\left|\sum_{l+|\mathbf{j}|\leq n}\dfrac{1}{l!\mathbf{j}!}\dfrac{\partial^{l+|\mathbf{j}|}f(u,\mathbf{v})}{\partial u^{l}\partial\mathbf{v}^{\mathbf{j}}}(x-u)^{l}(\mathbf{y}-\mathbf{v})^{\mathbf{j}}\right|\,du\,d\mathbf{v}.

By applying Jensen’s inequality, we obtain

\displaystyle|K_{n,w}^{\phi,\psi}f(x,\mathbf{y})-S_{N,w}^{\phi,\psi}f(x,\mathbf{y})|

\displaystyle\leq\displaystyle\sum\sum_{k+|\mathbf{m}|>N}|\phi(wx-k)|\prod_{i=1}^{d}|\psi_{i}(wy_{i}-m_{i})|w^{d+1}\sum_{l+|\mathbb{j}|\leq n}\dfrac{1}{l!\mathbb{j}!}\|\partial^{l+\mathbb{j}}f\|_{\infty}

\displaystyle\times\int_{I_{k}^{w}}\int_{I_{\mathbf{m}}^{w}}2^{l-1}\left(\left|x-\dfrac{k}{w}\right|^{l}+\left|\dfrac{k}{w}-u\right|^{l}\right)\prod_{i=1}^{d}2^{j_{i}-1}\left(\left|y_{i}-\dfrac{m_{i}}{w}\right|^{j_{i}}+\left|\dfrac{m_{i}}{w}-v_{i}\right|^{j_{i}}\right)du\ d\mathbb{v}

\displaystyle\leq\displaystyle\sum\sum_{k+|\mathbf{m}|>N}|\phi(wx-k)|\prod_{i=1}^{d}|\psi_{i}(wy_{i}-m_{i})|w^{d+1}\sum_{l+|\mathbb{j}|\leq n}\dfrac{1}{l!\mathbb{j}!}\|\partial^{l+\mathbb{j}}f\|_{\infty}

\displaystyle\times 2^{l-1}\left(\left|x-\dfrac{k}{w}\right|^{l}\dfrac{1}{w}+\dfrac{1}{w^{l+1}}\right)\prod_{i=1}^{d}2^{j_{i}-1}\left(\left|y_{i}-\dfrac{m_{i}}{w}\right|^{j_{i}}\dfrac{1}{w}+\dfrac{1}{w^{j_{i}+1}}\right)

\displaystyle=\sum_{l+|\mathbb{j}|\leq n}\dfrac{1}{l!\mathbb{j}!}\dfrac{2^{l+|\mathbf{j}|-(d+1)}}{w^{l+|\mathbf{j}|}}\|\partial^{l+\mathbb{j}}f\|_{\infty}

\displaystyle\times\sum\sum_{k+|\mathbf{m}|>N}|\phi(wx-k)|\prod_{i=1}^{d}|\psi_{i}(wy_{i}-m_{i})|\left(\left|wx-k\right|^{l}+1\right)\prod_{i=1}^{d}\left(\left|wy_{i}-m_{i}\right|^{j_{i}}+1\right)

\displaystyle\leq\epsilon\sum_{l+|\mathbb{j}|\leq n}\dfrac{1}{l!\mathbb{j}!}\dfrac{2^{l+|\mathbf{j}|-(d+1)}}{w^{l+|\mathbf{j}|}}\|\partial^{l+\mathbb{j}}f\|_{\infty}.

Since
\epsilon>0
is arbitrary, we have
(S_{N,w}^{\phi,\psi}f)
converges uniformly to
K_{n,w}^{\phi,\psi}f.

Hence,
K_{n,w}^{\phi,\psi}f
is continuous on
\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{d}.

We note that
(S_{N,w}^{\phi,\psi}f)^{{}^{\prime}}
is a linear combination of functions of type
(u-x)^{r},(y_{i}-v_{i})^{r},\phi^{(q)}(wu-k),\psi_{i}^{(q)}(wu-k),i=1,...,d,r=0,1,...,n,q=0,1.
Using (
1
) and repeating the same approach, we easily obtain
(S_{N,w}^{\phi,\psi}f)^{{}^{\prime}}
is uniformly convergent. Thus, we get
K_{n,w}^{\phi,\psi}f\in C_{b}^{{}^{\prime}}(\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{d}).

Iterating the above arguments, we have
K_{n,w}^{\phi,\psi}f\in C_{b}^{p}(\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{d}).

This completes the proof.
∎

Next, we prove the following uniform convergence theorem for the Hermite-type Kantorovich sampling operators.

Theorem 1
.

Let
\phi
and
\psi_{i},\ i=1,...,d
be a kernel of order
n.
Suppose that
f\in C_{b}^{n}(\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{d}).
Then, we have

\lim_{w\to\infty}\|K_{n,w}^{\phi,\psi}f-f\|_{\infty}=0.

Proof.

By the definition of the Hermite-type sampling Kantorovich operator, we have

\displaystyle|K_{n,w}^{\phi,\psi}f(x,\mathbf{y})-f(x,\mathbf{y})|

\displaystyle\leq\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}\sum_{\mathbf{m}\in\mathbb{Z}^{d}}|\phi(wx-k)|\prod_{i=1}^{d}|\psi_{i}(wy_{i}-m_{i})|

\displaystyle\times w^{d+1}\int_{I_{k}^{w}}\int_{I_{\mathbf{m}}^{w}}\left|\sum_{l+|\mathbf{j}|\leq n}\dfrac{1}{l!\mathbf{j}!}\dfrac{\partial^{l+|\mathbf{j}|}f(u,\mathbf{v})}{\partial u^{l}\partial\mathbf{v}^{\mathbf{j}}}(x-u)^{l}(\mathbf{y}-\mathbf{v})^{\mathbf{j}}-f(x,\mathbf{y})\right|\,du\,d\mathbf{v}.

By Taylor’s formula, we have

\displaystyle f(x,\mathbb{y})

\displaystyle=\displaystyle\sum_{l+|\mathbb{j}|\leq n-1}\dfrac{1}{l!\mathbb{j}!}\dfrac{\partial^{l+|\mathbb{j}|}f(u,\mathbb{v})}{\partial u^{l}\partial\mathbb{v}^{\mathbb{j}}}{(x-u)}^{l}{(\mathbb{y}-\mathbb{v})^{\mathbb{j}}}

\displaystyle\quad~~~~~~~~~~~+\sum_{l+|\mathbb{j}|=n}\dfrac{1}{l!\mathbb{j}!}\dfrac{\partial^{l+|\mathbb{j}|}f(\zeta_{u,x},\ \zeta_{\mathbb{v},\mathbb{y}})}{\partial u^{l}\partial\mathbb{v}^{\mathbb{j}}}{(x-u)}^{l}{(\mathbb{y}-\mathbb{v})^{\mathbb{j}}},

where
\zeta_{u,x}
lies between
u
and
x,
and
\zeta_{v_{i},y_{i}}
lies between
v_{i}
and
y_{i}
for
i=1,...,d.
Thus, we can write

\displaystyle|K_{n,w}^{\phi,\psi}f(x,\mathbf{y})-f(x,\mathbf{y})|

\displaystyle\leq\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}\sum_{\mathbf{m}\in\mathbb{Z}^{d}}|\phi(wx-k)|\prod_{i=1}^{d}|\psi_{i}(wy_{i}-m_{i})|w^{d+1}\int_{I_{k}^{w}}\int_{I_{\mathbf{m}}^{w}}

\displaystyle\times\left|\sum_{l+|\mathbf{j}|=n}\dfrac{1}{l!\mathbf{j}!}\left(\dfrac{\partial^{l+|\mathbf{j}|}f(u,\mathbf{v})}{\partial u^{l}\partial\mathbf{v}^{\mathbf{j}}}-\dfrac{\partial^{l+|\mathbf{j}|}f(\zeta_{u,x},\zeta_{\mathbf{v},\mathbf{y}})}{\partial u^{l}\partial\mathbf{v}^{\mathbf{j}}}\right)(x-u)^{l}(\mathbf{y}-\mathbf{v})^{\mathbf{j}}\right|\,du\,d\mathbf{v}.

(1)

Then, by applying Jensen’s inequality, we obtain

\displaystyle|K_{n,w}^{\phi,\psi}f(x,\mathbb{y})-f(x,\mathbb{y})|

\displaystyle\leq\displaystyle\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}\sum_{\mathbf{m}\in\mathbb{Z}^{d}}|\phi(wx-k)|\prod_{i=1}^{d}|\psi_{i}(wy_{i}-m_{i})|w^{d+1}\sum_{l+|\mathbb{j}|=n}\dfrac{1}{l!\mathbb{j}!}2\|\partial^{l+\mathbb{j}}f\|_{\infty}

\displaystyle\times\int_{I_{k}^{w}}\int_{I_{\mathbf{m}}^{w}}2^{l-1}\left(\left|x-\dfrac{k}{w}\right|^{l}+\left|\dfrac{k}{w}-u\right|^{l}\right)\prod_{i=1}^{d}2^{j_{i}-1}\left(\left|y_{i}-\dfrac{m_{i}}{w}\right|^{j_{i}}+\left|\dfrac{m_{i}}{w}-v_{i}\right|^{j_{i}}\right)du\ d\mathbb{v}

\displaystyle\leq\displaystyle\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}\sum_{\mathbf{m}\in\mathbb{Z}^{d}}|\phi(wx-k)|\prod_{i=1}^{d}|\psi_{i}(wy_{i}-m_{i})|w^{d+1}\sum_{l+|\mathbb{j}|=n}\dfrac{1}{l!\mathbb{j}!}2\|\partial^{l+\mathbb{j}}f\|_{\infty}

\displaystyle\times 2^{l-1}\left(\left|x-\dfrac{k}{w}\right|^{l}\dfrac{1}{w}+\dfrac{1}{w^{l+1}}\right)\prod_{i=1}^{d}2^{j_{i}-1}\left(\left|y_{i}-\dfrac{m_{i}}{w}\right|^{j_{i}}\dfrac{1}{w}+\dfrac{1}{w^{j_{i}+1}}\right)

\displaystyle\leq\displaystyle\sum_{l+|\mathbb{j}|=n}\dfrac{1}{l!\mathbb{j}!}\dfrac{2^{l+|j|-d}}{w^{l+|j|}}\|\partial^{l+\mathbb{j}}f\|_{\infty}(M_{l}(\phi)+M_{0}(\phi))\prod_{i=1}^{d}(M_{j_{i}}(\psi_{i})+M_{0}(\psi_{i}))

\displaystyle=\dfrac{2^{n-d}}{w^{n}}\displaystyle\sum_{l+|\mathbb{j}|=n}\dfrac{1}{l!\mathbb{j}!}\|\partial^{l+\mathbb{j}}f\|_{\infty}(M_{l}(\phi)+M_{0}(\phi))\prod_{i=1}^{d}(M_{j_{i}}(\psi_{i})+M_{0}(\psi_{i})).

Since
f\in C_{b}^{n}(\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{d})
and
M_{l}(\phi),M_{j_{i}}(\psi_{i}),\ i=1,...,d
are finite, then we get

\lim_{w\to\infty}\|K_{n,w}^{\phi,\psi}f-f\|_{\infty}=0.

Hence, the proof is completed.
∎

2.1.
Rate of Convergence

In this section, we estimate the rate of convergence of the Hermite sampling Kantorovich operators in terms of the modulus of continuity. First, we define the modulus of continuity for
f\in C(\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{d}).

Definition 2
.

For
f\in C(\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{d}),
the modulus of continuity is defined as follows:

\omega(f,\delta,\gamma_{1},...,\gamma_{d}):=\sup_{\begin{subarray}{c}|x-t|<\delta\\
|y_{i}-s_{i}|<\gamma_{i}\\
i=1,...,d\end{subarray}}|f(x,\mathbb{y})-f(t,\mathbb{s})|,\ \ \ (x,\mathbb{y}),(t,\mathbb{s})\in\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{d},

where
\delta,\gamma_{i}>0,
for
i=1,...,d.

We note that
\omega(f,\delta,\gamma_{1},...,\gamma_{d})
satisfies the following properties:

(i)

\omega(f,\delta,\gamma_{1},...,\gamma_{d})\to 0
as
\delta,\gamma_{i}\to 0,\ \text{for}\ i=1,...,d.

(ii)

|f(x,\mathbb{y})-f(t,\mathbb{s})|\leq\omega(f,\delta,\gamma_{1},...,\gamma_{d})\left(1+\dfrac{|x-t|}{\delta}\right)\displaystyle\prod_{i=1}^{d}\left(1+\dfrac{|y_{i}-s_{i}|}{\gamma_{i}}\right).

Theorem 2
.

Let
\phi
and
\psi_{i},\ i=1,...,d
be a kernel of order
n.

(i)

If
f\in C^{n}(\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{d}),
then we have

\|K_{n,w}^{\phi,\psi}f-f\|_{\infty}=o(w^{-n})\ \ \text{as}\ w\to\infty.

(ii)

Suppose that
f\in C^{n}(\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{d})\ \text{and}\ M_{n+1}(\phi),M_{n+1}(\psi_{i})<\infty,\ i=1,...,d.
Then, we have

\displaystyle\|K_{n,w}^{\phi,\psi}f-f\|_{\infty}

\displaystyle\leq\dfrac{2^{n-(d+1)}}{w^{n}}\sum_{l+|\mathbf{j}|=n}\dfrac{1}{l!\mathbf{j}!}\omega\left(\partial^{l+\mathbf{j}}f,\dfrac{1}{w},\dfrac{1}{w},...,\dfrac{1}{w}\right)\left(M_{l+1}(\phi)+2M_{l}(\phi)\right.

\displaystyle+\left.M_{1}(\phi)+2M_{0}(\phi)\right)\prod_{i=1}^{d}\left(M_{j_{i}+1}(\psi_{i})+2M_{j_{i}}(\psi_{i})+M_{1}(\psi_{i})+2M_{0}(\psi_{i})\right).

(iii)

If
f\in C^{n+1}_{b}(\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{d})\ \text{and}\ M_{n+1}(\phi),\ M_{n+1}(\psi_{i})<\infty,\ i=1,...,d,
then we have

\displaystyle\|K_{n,w}^{\phi,\psi}f-f\|_{\infty}

\displaystyle\leq\dfrac{2^{n-d}}{w^{n+1}}\displaystyle\sum_{l+|\mathbb{j}|=n+1}\dfrac{1}{l!\mathbb{j}!}\|\partial^{l+\mathbb{j}}f\|_{\infty}(M_{l}(\phi)+M_{0}(\phi))\prod_{i=1}^{d}(M_{j_{i}}(\psi_{i})+M_{0}(\psi_{i})).

Proof.

First, we prove part (i). From (
2
), we can write

\displaystyle|K_{n,w}^{\phi,\psi}f(x,\mathbf{y})-f(x,\mathbf{y})|

\displaystyle\leq\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}\sum_{\mathbf{m}\in\mathbb{Z}^{d}}|\phi(wx-k)|\prod_{i=1}^{d}|\psi_{i}(wy_{i}-m_{i})|w^{d+1}

\displaystyle\times\int_{I_{k}^{w}}\int_{I_{\mathbf{m}}^{w}}\left|\sum_{l+|\mathbf{j}|=n}\dfrac{1}{l!\mathbf{j}!}\left(\dfrac{\partial^{l+|\mathbf{j}|}f(u,\mathbf{v})}{\partial u^{l}\partial\mathbf{v}^{\mathbf{j}}}-\dfrac{\partial^{l+|\mathbf{j}|}f(\zeta_{u,x},\zeta_{\mathbf{v},\mathbf{y}})}{\partial u^{l}\partial\mathbf{v}^{\mathbf{j}}}\right)(x-u)^{l}(\mathbf{y}-\mathbf{v})^{\mathbf{j}}\right|\,du\,d\mathbf{v}

\displaystyle\leq\displaystyle\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}\sum_{\mathbf{m}\in\mathbb{Z}^{d}}|\phi(wx-k)|\prod_{i=1}^{d}|\psi_{i}(wy_{i}-m_{i})|w^{d+1}\sum_{l+|\mathbb{j}|=n}\dfrac{1}{l!\mathbb{j}!}

\displaystyle\times\int_{I_{k}^{w}}\int_{I_{\mathbf{m}}^{w}}\left|\dfrac{\partial^{l+|\mathbf{j}|}f(u,\mathbf{v})}{\partial u^{l}\partial\mathbf{v}^{\mathbf{j}}}-\dfrac{\partial^{l+|\mathbf{j}|}f(\zeta_{u,x},\zeta_{\mathbf{v},\mathbf{y}})}{\partial u^{l}\partial\mathbf{v}^{\mathbf{j}}}\right|2^{l-1}\left(\left|x-\dfrac{k}{w}\right|^{l}+\left|\dfrac{k}{w}-u\right|^{l}\right)

\displaystyle\times\prod_{i=1}^{d}2^{j_{i}-1}\left(\left|y_{i}-\dfrac{m_{i}}{w}\right|^{j_{i}}+\left|\dfrac{m_{i}}{w}-v_{i}\right|^{j_{i}}\right)du\ d\mathbb{v}

\displaystyle=\dfrac{2^{n-(d+1)}}{w^{n}}\displaystyle\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}\sum_{\mathbf{m}\in\mathbb{Z}^{d}}|\phi(wx-k)|\prod_{i=1}^{d}|\psi_{i}(wy_{i}-m_{i})|w^{d+1}\sum_{l+|\mathbb{j}|=n}\dfrac{1}{l!\mathbb{j}!}

\displaystyle\times\int_{I_{k}^{w}}\int_{I_{\mathbf{m}}^{w}}\left|\dfrac{\partial^{l+|\mathbf{j}|}f(u,\mathbf{v})}{\partial u^{l}\partial\mathbf{v}^{\mathbf{j}}}-\dfrac{\partial^{l+|\mathbf{j}|}f(\zeta_{u,x},\zeta_{\mathbf{v},\mathbf{y}})}{\partial u^{l}\partial\mathbf{v}^{\mathbf{j}}}\right|\left(\left|wx-k\right|^{l}+\left|k-wu\right|^{l}\right)

\displaystyle\times\prod_{i=1}^{d}\left(\left|wy_{i}-m_{i}\right|^{j_{i}}+\left|m_{i}-wv_{i}\right|^{j_{i}}\right)du\ d\mathbb{v}.

Since
\partial^{l+\mathbf{j}}f
is uniform continuous on
\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{d},
for a given
\epsilon>0,\ \exists\ \delta_{l,\mathbb{j}}>0
such that

|\partial^{l+\mathbf{j}}f(u,\mathbf{v})-\partial^{l+\mathbf{j}}f(x,\mathbf{y})|<\epsilon,

whenever
\|(x,\mathbb{y})-(u,\mathbb{v})\|_{\infty}\leq\delta_{l,\mathbb{j}}.
Thus, we obtain

\displaystyle w^{n}|K_{n,w}^{\phi,\psi}f(x,\mathbf{y})-f(x,\mathbf{y})|

\displaystyle\leq 2^{n-(d+1)}\displaystyle\left(\mathop{\sum\sum}_{\begin{subarray}{c}\left\|\left(x,\mathbb{y}\right)-\left(\dfrac{k}{w},\dfrac{\mathbf{m}}{w}\right)\right\|_{\infty}\leq\dfrac{\delta_{l,\mathbb{j}}}{2}\end{subarray}}+\mathop{\sum\sum}_{\begin{subarray}{c}\left\|\left(x,\mathbb{y}\right)-\left(\dfrac{k}{w},\dfrac{\mathbf{m}}{w}\right)\right\|_{\infty}>\dfrac{\delta_{l,\mathbb{j}}}{2}\end{subarray}}\right)

\displaystyle\times|\phi(wx-k)|\prod_{i=1}^{d}|\psi_{i}(wy_{i}-m_{i})|w^{d+1}\sum_{l+|\mathbb{j}|=n}\dfrac{1}{l!\mathbb{j}!}\int_{I_{k}^{w}}\int_{I_{\mathbf{m}}^{w}}\left|\dfrac{\partial^{l+|\mathbf{j}|}f(u,\mathbf{v})}{\partial u^{l}\partial\mathbf{v}^{\mathbf{j}}}-\dfrac{\partial^{l+|\mathbf{j}|}f(\zeta_{u,x},\zeta_{\mathbf{v},\mathbf{y}})}{\partial u^{l}\partial\mathbf{v}^{\mathbf{j}}}\right|

\displaystyle\times\left(\left|wx-k\right|^{l}+\left|k-wu\right|^{l}\right)\prod_{i=1}^{d}\left(\left|wy_{i}-m_{i}\right|^{j_{i}}+\left|m_{i}-wv_{i}\right|^{j_{i}}\right)du\ d\mathbb{v}

We have

\displaystyle\|(\zeta_{u,x},\zeta_{\mathbf{v},\mathbf{y}})-(u,\mathbb{v})\|_{\infty}

\displaystyle\leq\left\|\left(x,\mathbb{y}\right)-\left(\dfrac{k}{w},\dfrac{\mathbf{m}}{w}\right)\right\|_{\infty}+\left\|\left(u,\mathbb{v}\right)-\left(\dfrac{k}{w},\dfrac{\mathbf{m}}{w}\right)\right\|_{\infty}

with

S_{1}\leq\epsilon\ 2^{n-(d+1)}\displaystyle\sum_{l+|\mathbb{j}|=n}\dfrac{1}{l!\mathbb{j}!}(M_{l}(\phi)+M_{0}(\phi))\prod_{i=1}^{d}(M_{j_{i}}(\psi_{i})+M_{0}(\psi_{i})).

Since

is arbitrary, so we obtain

as

Now
S_{2}
is estimated by

\displaystyle S_{2}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle 2^{n-(d+1)}\displaystyle\sum_{l+|\mathbb{j}|=n}\dfrac{1}{l!\mathbb{j}!}2\|\partial^{l+\mathbb{j}}f\|_{\infty}\mathop{\sum\sum}_{\begin{subarray}{c}\left\|\left(x,\mathbb{y}\right)-\left(\dfrac{k}{w},\dfrac{\mathbf{m}}{w}\right)\right\|_{\infty}>\dfrac{\delta_{l,\mathbb{j}}}{2}\end{subarray}}

\displaystyle\times

\displaystyle|\phi(wx-k)|\left(\left|wx-k\right|^{l}+1\right)\prod_{i=1}^{d}|\psi_{i}(wy_{i}-m_{i})|\left(\left|wy_{i}-m_{i}\right|^{j_{i}}+1\right).

Since

for some

This implies that

\left|wx-k\right|>\dfrac{w\delta_{l,\mathbb{j}}}{2}

for some

\displaystyle S_{2}

\displaystyle|K_{n,w}^{\phi,\psi}f(x,\mathbf{y})-f(x,\mathbf{y})|

\displaystyle\leq\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}\sum_{\mathbf{m}\in\mathbb{Z}^{d}}|\phi(wx-k)|\prod_{i=1}^{d}|\psi_{i}(wy_{i}-m_{i})|w^{d+1}\int_{I_{k}^{w}}\int_{I_{\mathbf{m}}^{w}}\sum_{l+|\mathbf{j}|=n}\dfrac{1}{l!\mathbf{j}!}\omega(\partial^{l+\mathbf{j}}f,\delta,\gamma_{1},...,\gamma_{d})

By using Jensen’s inequality and moment conditions, we obtain

\displaystyle|K_{n,w}^{\phi,\psi}f(x,\mathbf{y})-f(x,\mathbf{y})|

\displaystyle|K_{n,w}^{\phi,\psi}f(x,\mathbf{y})-f(x,\mathbf{y})|

In the following theorem, we prove the Voronovskaja-type asymptotic formula for the Kantorovich sampling operators
K_{n,w}^{\phi,\psi}
.

Theorem 3
.

Let
\phi,\psi_{i},\ i=1,...,d
be a kernel of order
n+1
with constant discrete moments
m_{r}(\phi,u),m_{r}(\psi_{i},u),\ r=0,1,...,n+1,\ i=1,...,d
for all
u\in\mathbb{R}
and not null. Then for any
f\in C_{b}^{n+1}(\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{d}),
we have

\displaystyle\displaystyle\lim_{w\to\infty}w^{n+1}(K_{n,w}^{\phi,\psi}f(x,\mathbf{y})-f(x,\mathbf{y}))

\displaystyle=\displaystyle\sum_{l+|\mathbf{j}|=n+1}\dfrac{(-1)^{n}}{l!\mathbf{j}!}\sum_{c=0}^{l}\sum_{\mathbf{d}=0}^{\mathbf{j}}\binom{l}{c}\binom{\mathbf{j}}{\mathbf{d}}\dfrac{m_{c}(\phi)}{l-c+1}\prod_{i=1}^{d}\dfrac{m_{d_{i}}(\psi_{i})}{j_{i}-{d_{i}+1}}\partial^{l+\mathbf{j}}f(x,\mathbf{y}),

for every
(x,\mathbf{y})\in\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{d}.

Proof.

Using Taylor’s formula, we can write

\displaystyle K_{n,w}^{\phi,\psi}f(x,\mathbf{y})-f(x,\mathbf{y})

\displaystyle=-\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}\sum_{\mathbf{m}\in\mathbb{Z}^{d}}\phi(wx-k)\prod_{i=1}^{d}\psi_{i}(wy_{i}-m_{i})w^{d+1}

\displaystyle\times\int_{I_{k}^{w}}\int_{I_{\mathbf{m}}^{w}}\sum_{l+|\mathbf{j}|=n+1}\dfrac{1}{l!\mathbf{j}!}\partial^{l+\mathbf{j}}f(\zeta_{u,x},\zeta_{\mathbf{v},\mathbf{y}})(x-u)^{l}(\mathbf{y}-\mathbf{v})^{\mathbf{j}}\,du\,d\mathbf{v}.

\displaystyle=\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}\sum_{\mathbf{m}\in\mathbb{Z}^{d}}\phi(wx-k)\prod_{i=1}^{d}\psi_{i}(wy_{i}-m_{i})w^{d+1}\int_{I_{k}^{w}}\int_{I_{\mathbf{m}}^{w}}\sum_{l+|\mathbf{j}|=n+1}\dfrac{(-1)^{n}}{l!\mathbf{j}!}\partial^{l+\mathbf{j}}f(\zeta_{u,x},\zeta_{\mathbf{v},\mathbf{y}})

\displaystyle\times\sum_{c=0}^{l}\sum_{\mathbf{d}=0}^{\mathbf{j}}\binom{l}{c}\binom{\mathbf{j}}{\mathbf{d}}\left(\dfrac{k}{w}-x\right)^{c}\left(u-\dfrac{k}{w}\right)^{l-c}\left(\dfrac{\mathbf{m}}{w}-\mathbf{y}\right)^{\mathbf{d}}\left(\mathbf{v}-\dfrac{\mathbf{m}}{w}\right)^{\mathbf{j}-\mathbf{d}}\,du\,d\mathbf{v}.

\displaystyle=\sum_{l+|\mathbf{j}|=n+1}\dfrac{(-1)^{n}}{l!\mathbf{j}!}\sum_{c=0}^{l}\sum_{\mathbf{d}=0}^{\mathbf{j}}\binom{l}{c}\binom{\mathbf{j}}{\mathbf{d}}\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}\sum_{\mathbf{m}\in\mathbb{Z}^{d}}\phi(wx-k)\prod_{i=1}^{d}\psi_{i}(wy_{i}-m_{i})\left(\dfrac{k}{w}-x\right)^{c}

\displaystyle\times\left(\dfrac{\mathbf{m}}{w}-\mathbf{y}\right)^{\mathbf{d}}w^{d+1}\int_{I_{k}^{w}}\int_{I_{\mathbf{m}}^{w}}\partial^{l+\mathbf{j}}f(\zeta_{u,x},\zeta_{\mathbf{v},\mathbf{y}})\left(u-\dfrac{k}{w}\right)^{l-c}\left(\mathbf{v}-\dfrac{\mathbf{m}}{w}\right)^{\mathbf{j}-\mathbf{d}}\,du\,d\mathbf{v}.

We have

\displaystyle w^{n+1}(K_{n,w}^{\phi,\psi}f(x,\mathbf{y})-f(x,\mathbf{y}))

\displaystyle-\displaystyle\sum_{l+|\mathbf{j}|=n+1}\dfrac{(-1)^{n}}{l!\mathbf{j}!}\sum_{c=0}^{l}\sum_{\mathbf{d}=0}^{\mathbf{j}}\binom{l}{c}\binom{\mathbf{j}}{\mathbf{d}}\dfrac{m_{c}(\phi)}{l-c+1}\prod_{i=1}^{d}\dfrac{m_{d_{i}}(\psi_{i})}{j_{i}-{d_{i}+1}}\partial^{l+\mathbf{j}}f(x,\mathbf{y})

\displaystyle=\sum_{l+|\mathbf{j}|=n+1}\dfrac{(-1)^{n}}{l!\mathbf{j}!}\sum_{c=0}^{l}\sum_{\mathbf{d}=0}^{\mathbf{j}}\binom{l}{c}\binom{\mathbf{j}}{\mathbf{d}}\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}\sum_{\mathbf{m}\in\mathbb{Z}^{d}}\phi(wx-k)\prod_{i=1}^{d}\psi_{i}(wy_{i}-m_{i})\left(k-wx\right)^{c}

\displaystyle\times\left(\mathbf{m}-w\mathbf{y}\right)^{\mathbf{d}}w^{d+1}\int_{I_{k}^{w}}\int_{I_{\mathbf{m}}^{w}}\left(\partial^{l+\mathbf{j}}f(\zeta_{u,x},\zeta_{\mathbf{v},\mathbf{y}})-\partial^{l+\mathbf{j}}f(x,\mathbf{y})\right)\left(wu-k\right)^{l-c}\left(w\mathbf{v}-\mathbf{m}\right)^{\mathbf{j}-\mathbf{d}}\,du\,d\mathbf{v}

\displaystyle:=\sum_{l+|\mathbf{j}|=n+1}\dfrac{(-1)^{n}}{l!\mathbf{j}!}\sum_{c=0}^{l}\sum_{\mathbf{d}=0}^{\mathbf{j}}\binom{l}{c}\binom{\mathbf{j}}{\mathbf{d}}S,

where

\displaystyle S

\displaystyle:=

\displaystyle\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}\sum_{\mathbf{m}\in\mathbb{Z}^{d}}\phi(wx-k)\prod_{i=1}^{d}\psi_{i}(wy_{i}-m_{i})\left(k-wx\right)^{c}\left(\mathbf{m}-w\mathbf{y}\right)^{\mathbf{d}}

\displaystyle\times

\displaystyle w^{d+1}\int_{I_{k}^{w}}\int_{I_{\mathbf{m}}^{w}}\left(\partial^{l+\mathbf{j}}f(\zeta_{u,x},\zeta_{\mathbf{v},\mathbf{y}})-\partial^{l+\mathbf{j}}f(x,\mathbf{y})\right)\left(wu-k\right)^{l-c}\left(w\mathbf{v}-\mathbf{m}\right)^{\mathbf{j}-\mathbf{d}}\,du\,d\mathbf{v}.

Now let
\epsilon>0

\displaystyle:=

\displaystyle:=

∎

Lemma 1
.

Let
\phi,\psi_{i}\in C_{b}^{n}(\mathbb{R}),\ i=1,...,d

Proof.

We aim to prove the identity

Now, let us consider the case

\displaystyle\dfrac{\partial K_{n,w}^{\phi,\psi}f(x,\mathbf{y})}{\partial x}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\displaystyle\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}\sum_{\mathbf{m}\in\mathbb{Z}^{d}}w\ \phi^{{}^{\prime}}(wx-k)\ \prod_{i=1}^{d}\psi_{i}(wy_{i}-m_{i})

\displaystyle\times\left(\sum_{l+|\mathbb{j}|\leq n}\dfrac{1}{l!\mathbb{j}!}w^{d+1}\int_{I_{k}^{w}}\int_{I_{\mathbf{m}}^{w}}\dfrac{\partial^{l+|\mathbb{j}|}f(u,\mathbb{v})}{\partial u^{l}\partial\mathbb{v}^{\mathbb{j}}}l{(x-u)}^{l-1}{(\mathbb{y}-\mathbb{v})^{\mathbb{j}}}\ du\ d\mathbb{v}\right)

p.

\dfrac{\partial^{p}K_{n,w}^{\phi,\psi}f(x,\mathbf{y})}{\partial x^{p}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\dfrac{\partial}{\partial x}\left(\dfrac{\partial^{p-1}K_{n,w}^{\phi,\psi}f(x,\mathbf{y})}{\partial x^{p-1}}\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\dfrac{\partial}{\partial x}\left(\displaystyle\sum_{a=0}^{p-1}\binom{p-1}{a}w^{a}K_{n-(p-1-a),w}^{\phi^{(a)},\psi}\dfrac{\partial^{p-1-a}f(x,\mathbf{y})}{\partial x^{p-1-a}}\right)

Conclusions

In this work, the approximation behavior of Hermite-type sampling Kantorovich operators in the context of mixed norm spaces are investigated. The direct approximation theorems have been proved, including including the uniform convergence theorem, the Voronovskaja-type asymptotic formula, and an estimate of error in the approximation in terms of the modulus of continuity in mixed norm settings. The rate of convergence of sampling Kantorovich operators in terms of the modulus of continuity has been studied. The simultaneous approximation results of these sampling Kantorovich operators, including the uniform approximation, the asymptotic formula, and the approximation error in terms of the modulus of continuity in mixed settings have been discussed. The realization of differentiable functions is also demonstrated using appropriate kernel functions.
