The ordering of hypertrees and unicyclic hypergraphs by the traces of 𝒜 α \mathcal{A}_{\alpha} -tensor
Jueru Liu, Lizhu Sun, Changjiang Bu

Abstract
For a real number α ∈ [ 0 , 1 ] \alpha\in[0,1] and a k k -uniform hypergraph ℋ \mathcal{H} , 𝒜 α ​ ( ℋ ) = α ​ 𝒟 ​ ( ℋ ) + ( 1 − α ) ​ 𝒜 ​ ( ℋ ) \mathcal{A}_{\alpha}(\mathcal{H})=\alpha\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{H})+(1-\alpha)\mathcal{A}(\mathcal{H}) is called the 𝒜 α \mathcal{A}_{\alpha} -tensor of ℋ \mathcal{H} , where 𝒟 ⁡ ( ℋ ) \mathcal{D}(\mathcal{H}) and 𝒜 ⁡ ( ℋ ) \mathcal{A}(\mathcal{H}) are the degree tensor and adjacency tensor of ℋ \mathcal{H} , respectively. The sum of the d d -th powers of all eigenvalues of 𝒜 α ​ ( ℋ ) \mathcal{A}_{\alpha}(\mathcal{H}) is called the d d -th order 𝒜 α \mathcal{A}_{\alpha} -spectral moment of ℋ \mathcal{H} , which is equal to the d d -th order trace of 𝒜 α ​ ( ℋ ) \mathcal{A}_{\alpha}(\mathcal{H}) . In this paper, some hypergraphs are ordered lexicographically by their 𝒜 α \mathcal{A}_{\alpha} -spectral moments in non-decreasing order. The first, the second, the last and the second last hypergraphs among all k k -uniform linear unicyclic hypergraphs and hypertrees are characterized, respectively. We give the first and the last hypergraphs among all k k -uniform linear unicyclic hypergraphs with given grith, and characterize the last hypertree among all k k -uniform hypertrees with given diameter. Furthermore, we determine some extreme values of the 𝒜 α \mathcal{A}_{\alpha} -spectral moments for hypertrees and linear unicyclic hypergraphs, respectively.

For a positive integer

.

Let

and
\mathcal{H}
be a
k
-uniform hypergraph with degree sequence

. Then

\displaystyle\mathrm{Tr}_{k+2}(\mathcal{A}_{\alpha}(\mathcal{H}))=

\displaystyle\phi(k+2)+(1-\alpha)^{k+2}\mathrm{Tr}_{k+2}(\mathcal{A}(\mathcal{H}))

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle(k+2)(k-1)^{n-k}k^{k-2}\alpha^{2}(1-\alpha)^{k}\sum\limits_{e\in E(\mathcal{H})}\left(\sum\limits_{\{i,j\}\subset e}d_{i}d_{j}+\sum\limits_{i\in e}d_{i}^{2}\right)

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle(k+2)(k-1)^{n-k-1}\alpha(1-\alpha)^{k+1}\sum\limits_{\mathcal{G}\in\mathcal{K}_{k+1}}\left(\sum\limits_{D\in R(\mathcal{G})}\tau(D)\right)\left(\sum\limits_{i\in V(\mathcal{G})}d_{i}\right),

where
R(\mathcal{G})=\{D|~D\cong D_{f},~f\in\mathcal{F}_{k+2}(\mathcal{G})\}
,
\tau(D)
is the number of rooted spanning trees of the multi-digraph
D
with a specified root.

Proof.

From Theorem
3.3
, we have

\displaystyle\mathrm{Tr}_{k+2}(\mathcal{A}_{\alpha}(\mathcal{H}))=

\displaystyle\phi(k+2)+(1-\alpha)^{k+2}\mathrm{Tr}_{k+2}(\mathcal{A}(\mathcal{H}))

(3.5)

\displaystyle+(k+2)(k-1)^{n}\sum\limits_{H\in\mathcal{V}_{k+1}(\mathcal{H})}\sum\limits_{f\in\mathcal{F}_{k+2}(H)}\frac{\tau(f)\pi_{f}(\mathcal{A}_{\alpha}(\mathcal{H}))}{\prod\limits_{v\in V(f)}d^{+}(v)}.

For
e\in E(\mathcal{H})
, let

be the connected Veblen infragraph obtained by adding multiplicity
k
to the hyperedge
e
.
Then
\mathcal{V}_{k+1}(\mathcal{H})=\{\mathcal{E}_{e}|\ e\in E(\mathcal{H})\}\cup\mathcal{K}_{k+1}
.

For a hyperedge

with

, let
\alpha_{j}
be a permutation of

and
\beta_{j}=i_{j}\cdots i_{j}\in[n]^{k-1}
for

.
The elements in
\mathcal{F}_{k+2}(\mathcal{E}_{e})
have the following two cases.

Case 1.

There are two elements in
f_{1}
corresponding to the same diagonal entry
(\mathcal{A}_{\alpha})_{i_{j}\beta_{j}}
of
\mathcal{A}_{\alpha}(\mathcal{H})
, where
j\in[k]
.
Without loss of generality, we assume

\pi_{f_{1}}(\mathcal{A}_{\alpha}(\mathcal{H}))=\alpha^{2}(1-\alpha)^{k}\left(\frac{1}{(k-1)!}\right)^{k}d_{i_{j}}^{2},~\prod_{v\in V(f_{1})}d_{v}^{+}=3(k-1)^{k}.

Case 2.

There are two elements in
f_{2}
that correspond to the two different diagonal entries
(\mathcal{A}_{\alpha})_{i_{j}\beta_{j}}
and
(\mathcal{A}_{\alpha})_{i_{l}\beta_{l}}
of
\mathcal{A}_{\alpha}(\mathcal{H})
, where

.
Without loss of generality, we assume

f_{2}=(i_{1}\alpha_{1},\ldots,i_{j}\alpha_{j},i_{j}\beta_{j},i_{j+1}\alpha_{j+1},\ldots,i_{l}\alpha_{l},i_{l}\beta_{l},i_{l+1}\alpha_{l+1},\ldots,i_{k}\alpha_{k})\in\mathcal{F}_{k+2}(\mathcal{E}_{e}).

As is known, the number of rooted spanning trees (with a specified root) of a complete digraph with
k

[
2
]
.
The digraphs obtained by removing all loops of

and

are complete digraphs with
k
vertices, then
\tau(f_{1})=\tau(f_{2})=k^{k-2}
.
There are

(resp.

that have the same induced multi-digraph as

(resp.

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{e\in E(\mathcal{H})}\sum\limits_{f\in\mathcal{F}_{k+2}(\mathcal{E}_{e})}\frac{\tau(f)\pi_{f}(\mathcal{A}_{\alpha}(\mathcal{H}))}{\prod\limits_{v\in V(f)}d_{v}^{+}}

(3.6)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(k-1)^{-k}k^{k-2}\alpha^{2}(1-\alpha)^{k}\sum\limits_{e\in E(\mathcal{H})}\left(\sum\limits_{i\in e}d_{i}^{2}+\sum\limits_{\{i,j\}\subset e}d_{i}d_{j}\right).

For a subhypergraph
\mathcal{G}\in\mathcal{K}_{k+1}

\alpha_{j}
be a permutation of

\beta_{j}=i_{j}\cdots i_{j}\in[N]^{k-1}
for

.

. There are

elements in

that have the same induced multi-digraph as

. Then

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{\mathcal{G}\in\mathcal{K}_{k+1}}\sum\limits_{f\in\mathcal{F}_{k+2}(\mathcal{G})}\frac{\tau(f)\pi_{f}(\mathcal{A}_{\alpha}(\mathcal{H}))}{\prod\limits_{v\in V(f)}d_{v}^{+}}

(3.7)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(k-1)^{-k-1}\alpha(1-\alpha)^{k+1}\sum\limits_{\mathcal{G}\in\mathcal{K}_{k+1}}\left(\sum\limits_{i\in V(\mathcal{G})}d_{i}\left(\sum\limits_{D\in R(\mathcal{G})}\tau(D)\right)\right).

is obtained.

4

For a

-uniform hypergraph

Lemma 4.6
.

For a positive integer
k\geq 2
, let

k
-uniform hypergraph
\mathcal{H}
with

e_{1},\ldots,e_{s}

k
-uniform hypergraph

from

to
v

\mathcal{H}^{{}^{\prime}}

\sigma
-transformation of

.

.

Proof.

If

, then

\displaystyle\sum_{v\in V(\mathcal{H})}d_{\mathcal{H}}(v)^{2}-\sum_{v\in V(\mathcal{H}^{{}^{\prime}})}d_{\mathcal{H}^{{}^{\prime}}}(v)^{2}=

\displaystyle d_{\mathcal{H}}(u)^{2}+d_{\mathcal{H}}(v)^{2}-(d_{\mathcal{H}}(u)-s)^{2}-(d_{\mathcal{H}}(v)+s)^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2s(d_{\mathcal{H}}(u)-d_{\mathcal{H}}(v)-s)<0.

From Corollary
3.4

.
∎

5

.

-uniform linear unicyclic hypergraphs with

and

For any

-uniform linear unicyclic hypergraph

with

is a subhypergraph of

.

Theorem 5.11

for any

with equality if and only if

.
Hence,

is the last hypertree in
S_{\alpha}
-order among all
k
-uniform hypertrees with
m
hyperedges.
∎

Theorem 6.21
.

Among all
k
-uniform hypertrees with
m
hyperedges and diameter
d(2\leq d\leq m)
, the last hypertree in
S_{\alpha}
-order is
S_{t,d-t,1,\ldots,1}^{(k)}(t=\lfloor\frac{d}{2}\rfloor)
for
k\geq 2
and

.

Proof.

If
T
is a
k
-uniform hypertree with diameter
d
, then the hyperpath
P_{d}^{(k)}
is a subhypergraph of
T
. Repeating
\sigma
-transformation, any
k
-uniform hypertree
T
with
m
hyperedges and diameter
d
can be changed into a
k
-uniform hypertree
T_{1}
obtained from
P_{d}^{(k)}
by adding
m-d
pendant hyperedges to a non-cored vertex of
P_{d}^{(k)}
. From Lemma
4.6
,
it is known that
T\prec_{\alpha}T_{1}
. By using the inverse third path-sliding transformation repeatedly, we can change
T_{1}
into a
k
-uniform hypertree obtained from
P_{d}^{(k)}
by adding
m-d
pendant hyperedges to the non-cored vertex
v_{\lfloor\frac{d}{2}\rfloor+1}
of
P_{d}^{(k)}
i.e.,
S_{\lfloor\frac{d}{2}\rfloor,d-\lfloor\frac{d}{2}\rfloor,1,\ldots,1}^{(k)}
. From Lemma
4.9
, we know that
T_{1}\prec_{\alpha}S_{\lfloor\frac{d}{2}\rfloor,d-\lfloor\frac{d}{2}\rfloor,1,\ldots,1}^{(k)}
. Hence,
S_{\lfloor\frac{d}{2}\rfloor,d-\lfloor\frac{d}{2}\rfloor,1,\ldots,1}^{(k)}
is the last hypergraph in
S_{\alpha}
-order among all
k
-uniform hypertrees with
m
hyperedges and diameter
d
.
∎

Let
\mathcal{S}=\{S_{\lfloor\frac{d}{2}\rfloor,d-\lfloor\frac{d}{2}\rfloor,1,\ldots,1}^{(k)}|\ d=2,3,\ldots,m\}
.
When
d=2
, it is obvious that
S_{\lfloor\frac{d}{2}\rfloor,d-\lfloor\frac{d}{2}\rfloor,1,\ldots,1}^{(k)}\cong S_{m}^{(k)}
.
The hyperstar
S_{m}^{(k)}
is not only the only
k
-uniform hypertree diameter
2

-order among all
k
-uniform hypertrees with

hyperedges, then that

\mathcal{S}
. From Theorem

, we know that the second last hypertree in

must be the last in

.

Theorem 6.22
.

Among all
k
-uniform hypertrees with
m
hyperedges, the second last hypertree in
S_{\alpha}
-order is the starlike hypertree
S_{1,2,1,\ldots,1}^{(k)}
for
k\geq 2
and
0<\alpha<1
.

Proof.

Note that the
\mathcal{A}_{\alpha}
-spectral moments
\mathrm{Tr}_{0}(\mathcal{A}_{\alpha})
and
\mathrm{Tr}_{1}(\mathcal{A}_{\alpha})
are same for all
k
-uniform hypertrees in
\mathcal{S}\setminus\{S_{m}^{(k)}\}=\{S_{\lfloor\frac{d}{2}\rfloor,d-\lfloor\frac{d}{2}\rfloor,1,\ldots,1}^{(k)}|\ d=3,\ldots,m\}
. And

\displaystyle\mathrm{Tr}_{2}(\mathcal{A}_{\alpha}(S_{\lfloor\frac{d}{2}\rfloor,d-\lfloor\frac{d}{2}\rfloor,1,\ldots,1}^{(k)}))

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(k-1)^{m(k-1)-2}\alpha^{2}\left((m-d+2)^{2}+4(d-2)+(m-d)(k-1)+d(k-2)+2\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(k-1)^{m(k-1)-2}\alpha^{2}\left(d^{2}-(2m+1)d+(m+2)^{2}+m(k-1)-6\right).

Let
f(x)=x^{2}-(2m+1)x+(m+2)^{2}+m(k-1)-6
for
3\leq x\leq m
. We have
f^{{}^{\prime}}(x)=2(x-m)-1\leq 0
for
3\leq x\leq m

f(x)\leq f(3)
for
3\leq x\leq m

\mathrm{Tr}_{2}(\mathcal{A}_{\alpha}(S_{\lfloor\frac{d}{2}\rfloor,d-\lfloor\frac{d}{2}\rfloor,1,\ldots,1}^{(k)}))\leq\mathrm{Tr}_{2}(\mathcal{A}_{\alpha}(S_{1,2,1,\ldots,1}^{(k)}))
for
3\leq d\leq m
with equality if and only if
d=3

S_{\lfloor\frac{d}{2}\rfloor,d-\lfloor\frac{d}{2}\rfloor,1,\ldots,1}^{(k)}\cong S_{1,2,1,\ldots,1}^{(k)}
. It follows that
S_{1,2,1,\ldots,1}^{(k)}
is the last hypergraph in
\mathcal{S}\setminus\{S_{m}^{(k)}\}
.
Hence,
S_{1,2,1,\ldots,1}^{(k)}
is the second last hypergraph in
S_{\alpha}
-order among all
k
-uniform hypertrees with
m
hyperedges.
∎
