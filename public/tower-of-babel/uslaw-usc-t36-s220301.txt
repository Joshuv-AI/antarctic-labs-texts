Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 2203 (United States Capitol Historical Society). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Federal Charter.—
United States Capitol Historical Society (in this chapter, the “corporation”) is a federally chartered corporation.
(b) Place of Incorporation.—
The corporation is declared to be incorporated in the District of Columbia.
(c) Perpetual Existence.—
Except as otherwise provided, the corporation has perpetual existence.
