49 C.F.R. § 384.222 — Violation of out-of-service orders.
Citation: us:cfr:49:384.222
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-384.222
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 384 (State Compliance With Commercial Driver'S License Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The State must have and enforce laws and/or regulations applicable to drivers of CMVs and their employers, as defined in § 383.5 of this subchapter, which meet the minimum requirements of § 383.37(d), Table 4 to § 383.51, and § 383.53(b) of this subchapter.
