54 U.S.C. § 200304 — Statement of estimated requirements
Citation: us:usc:54:200304
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section200304
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 2003 (Land And Water Conservation Fund). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In General.—
There shall be submitted with the annual budget of the United States a comprehensive statement of estimated requirements during the ensuing fiscal year for appropriations from the Fund.
(b) Allocation of Funds.—
Of the total amount made available to the Fund through appropriations or deposited in the Fund under section 105(a)(2)(B) of the Gulf of Mexico Energy Security Act of 2006 (43 U.S.C. 1331 note; Public Law 109–432)—
(1)
not less than 40 percent shall be used for Federal purposes; and
(2)
not less than 40 percent shall be used to provide financial assistance to States.
