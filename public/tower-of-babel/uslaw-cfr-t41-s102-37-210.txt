41 C.F.R. § 102-37.210 — -37.210 Undistributed surplus property proposal to sell.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-37.210
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-37.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-37 (37—Donation Of Surplus Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Your request to sell undistributed surplus property must include:
(1) The proposed sale date;
(2) A listing of the property;
(3) Location of the sale;
(4) Method of sale; and
(5) Proposed advertising to be used.
(b) If the request is approved, GSA will provide the necessary forms and instructions for you to use in conducting the sale.
