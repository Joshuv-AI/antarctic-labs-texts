42 C.F.R. § 84.191 — Chemical cartridge respirators; required components.
Citation: us:cfr:42:84.191
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-84.191
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 84 (Approval Of Respiratory Protective Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each chemical cartridge respirator described in § 84.190 shall, where its design requires, contain the following component parts:
(1) Facepiece, mouthpiece, and noseclip, hood, or helmet;
(2) Cartridge;
(3) Cartridge with filter;
(4) Harness;
(5) Breathing tube; and
(6) Attached blower.
(b) The components of each chemical cartridge respirator shall meet the minimum construction requirements set forth in subpart G of this part.
