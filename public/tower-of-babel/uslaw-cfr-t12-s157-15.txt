12 C.F.R. § 157.15 — Who owns a deposit account?
Citation: us:cfr:12:157.15
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-157.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 157 (Deposits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may treat the holder of record as the account owner, even if you receive contrary notice, until you transfer the account on your records.
