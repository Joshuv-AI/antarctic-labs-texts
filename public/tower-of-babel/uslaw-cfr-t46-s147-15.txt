46 C.F.R. § 147.15 — Hazardous ships' stores permitted on board vessels.
Citation: us:cfr:46:147.15
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-147.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 147 (Hazardous Ships' Stores). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless prohibited under subpart B of this part, any hazardous material may be on board a vessel as ships' stores if the material—
(a) Is labeled according to § 147.30; and
(b) Meets the requirements, if any, in subpart B of this part applicable to the material.
