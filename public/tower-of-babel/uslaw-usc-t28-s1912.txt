28 U.S.C. § 1912 — Damages and costs on affirmance
Citation: us:usc:28:1912
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1912
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part V (Procedure); Chapter 123 (Fees And Costs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Where a judgment is affirmed by the Supreme Court or a court of appeals, the court in its discretion may adjudge to the prevailing party just damages for his delay, and single or double costs.
