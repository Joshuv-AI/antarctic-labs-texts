49 C.F.R. § 29.207 — What happens if the Department takes no action within the 45-day review period (or any extensions thereof)?
Citation: us:cfr:49:29.207
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-29.207
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 29 (Tribal Transportation Self-Governance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The final offer is accepted by operation of law if the Department takes no action within the 45-day review period (or any extensions thereof).
