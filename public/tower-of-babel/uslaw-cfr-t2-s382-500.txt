2 C.F.R. § 382.500 — Who in HHS determines that a recipient other than an individual violated the requirements of this part?

Creator: Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 382 (Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).
