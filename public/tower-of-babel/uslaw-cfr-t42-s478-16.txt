42 C.F.R. § 478.16 — Right to reconsideration.
Citation: us:cfr:42:478.16
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-478.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 478 (Reconsiderations And Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A beneficiary, provider or practitioner who is dissatisfied with a QIO initial denial determination on one of the issues specified in § 478.14 has a right to a reconsideration of that determination by the QIO that made the initial denial determination.
