46 U.S.C. § 51905 — Flags and grave markers
Citation: us:usc:46:51905
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section51905
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part B (Merchant Marine Service); Chapter 519 (Merchant Marine Awards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as authorized under another law, the Secretary of Transportation may issue, at no cost, a flag of the United States and a grave marker to the family or personal representative of a deceased individual who served in the merchant marine of the United States in support of the armed forces of the United States or its allies during a war or national emergency.
