23 C.F.R. § 190.7 — Processing of claims.

Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 190 (Incentive Payments For Controlling Outdoor Advertising On The Interstate System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Audited and approved PR-20 vouchers with form FHWA-1175 shall be forwarded to the regional office for submission to the Finance Division, Washington Headquarters, for payment. The associated strip maps shall be retained with the division office copies of the PR-20 vouchers.
