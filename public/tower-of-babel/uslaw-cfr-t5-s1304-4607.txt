5 C.F.R. § 1304.4607 — Advice to former Government employees.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1304.4607
Creator: Office Of Management And Budget
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1304.4607
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1304 (Post Employment Conflict Of Interest). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Office of General Counsel, OMB, has the responsibility for providing assistance promptly to former Government employees who seek advice on specific problems.
