49 C.F.R. § 299.601 — Responsibility for verification demonstrations and tests.
Citation: us:cfr:49:299.601
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-299.601
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 299 (Texas Central Railroad High-Speed Rail Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The railroad shall comply with the pre-revenue qualification tests and verification requirements set forth in this subpart to demonstrate the overall safety of the system, prior to revenue operations.
