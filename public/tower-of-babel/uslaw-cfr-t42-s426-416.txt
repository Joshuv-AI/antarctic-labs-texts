42 C.F.R. § 426.416 — Role of Medicare Managed Care Organizations (MCOs) and State agencies in the LCD review.
Citation: us:cfr:42:426.416
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-426.416
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 426 (Review Of National Coverage Determinations And Local Coverage Determinations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Medicare MCOs and Medicaid State agencies have no role in the LCD review process. However, once the ALJ has issued its decision, the decision is made available to all Medicare MCOs and State agencies.
