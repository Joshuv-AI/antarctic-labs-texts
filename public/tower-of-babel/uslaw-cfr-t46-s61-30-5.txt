46 C.F.R. § 61.30-5 — -5 Preparation of thermal fluid heater for inspection and test.
Citation: us:cfr:46:61.30-5
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-61.30-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 61 (Periodic Tests And Inspections). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For visual inspection, access plates and manholes must be removed as required by the marine inspector and the heater and combustion chambers must be thoroughly cooled and cleaned.
