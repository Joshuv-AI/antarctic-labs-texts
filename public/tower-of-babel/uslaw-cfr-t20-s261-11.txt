20 C.F.R. § 261.11 — Discretion of the three-member Board to reopen or not to reopen a final decision.
Citation: us:cfr:20:261.11
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-261.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 261 (Administrative Finality). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In any case in which the three-member Board may deem proper, the Board may direct that any decision, which is otherwise subject to reopening under this part, shall not be reopened or direct that any decision, which is otherwise not subject to reopening under this part, shall be reopened.
