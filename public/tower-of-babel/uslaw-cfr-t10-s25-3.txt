10 C.F.R. § 25.3 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:10:25.3
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-25.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 25 (Access Authorization). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part apply to licensees, certificate holders, and others who may require access to classified information related to a license, certificate, an application for a license or certificate, or other activities as the Commission may determine.
