28 C.F.R. § 29.5 — Notification of law enforcement officials.
Citation: us:cfr:28:29.5
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-29.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 29 (Motor Vehicle Theft Prevention Act Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to the actions enumerated in § 29.4(b), as a condition of participating in the program, a State or locality must agree to take reasonable steps to ensure that law enforcement officials under its jurisdiction are familiar with the program and with the conditions under which motor vehicles may be stopped.
