46 U.S.C. § 57520 — Vessel maintenance

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part F (Government-Owned Merchant Vessels); Chapter 575 (Construction, Charter, And Sale Of Vessels); Subchapter II (Charters). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In General.—
A charter under this chapter shall require the charterer, at the charterer’s expense, to—
(1)
keep the chartered vessel in good repair and efficient operating condition; and
(2)
make any repairs required by the Secretary of Transportation.
(b) Inspection.—
The charter shall provide that the Secretary has the right to inspect the vessel at any time to ascertain its condition.
