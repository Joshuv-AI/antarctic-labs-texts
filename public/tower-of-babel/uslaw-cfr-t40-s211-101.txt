40 C.F.R. § 211.101 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:40:211.101
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-211.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 211 (Product Noise Labeling). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of subpart A apply to all products for which regulations are published under part 211 and manufactured after the effective date of this regulation, unless they are made inapplicable by product-specific regulations.
