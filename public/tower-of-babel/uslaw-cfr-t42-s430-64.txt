42 C.F.R. § 430.64 — Suspension of rules.
Citation: us:cfr:42:430.64
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-430.64
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 430 (Grants To States For Medical Assistance Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon notice to all parties, the Administrator or the presiding officer may modify or waive any rule in this subpart upon determination that no party will be unduly prejudiced and the ends of justice will thereby be served.
