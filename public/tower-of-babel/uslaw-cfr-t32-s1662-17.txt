32 C.F.R. § 1662.17 — U.S. District Court action.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1662.17
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1662.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1662 (Freedom Of Information Act (Foia) Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Director of Selective Service, upon review, affirms the denial of the Chief FOIA Officer's determination of items specified in § 1662.9(a), requesters may ask a U.S. District Court to review that denial. See 5 U.S.C. 552(a)(4)(B).
