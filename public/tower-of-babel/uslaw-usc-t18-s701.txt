18 U.S.C. § 701 — Official badges, identification cards, other insignia
Citation: us:usc:18:701
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section701
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 33 (Emblems, Insignia, And Names). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever manufactures, sells, or possesses any badge, identification card, or other insignia, of the design prescribed by the head of any department or agency of the United States for use by any officer or employee thereof, or any colorable imitation thereof, or photographs, prints, or in any other manner makes or executes any engraving, photograph, print, or impression in the likeness of any such badge, identification card, or other insignia, or any colorable imitation thereof, except as authorized under regulations made pursuant to law, shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than six months, or both.
