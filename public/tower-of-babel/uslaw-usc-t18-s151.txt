18 U.S.C. § 151 — Definition
Citation: us:usc:18:151
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section151
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 9 (Bankruptcy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this chapter, the term “debtor” means a debtor concerning whom a petition has been filed under title 11.
