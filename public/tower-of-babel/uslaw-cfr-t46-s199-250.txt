46 C.F.R. § 199.250 — Drills.
Citation: us:cfr:46:199.250
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-199.250
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 199 (Lifesaving Systems For Certain Inspected Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An abandon-ship drill and a fire drill, as described in § 199.180, must be conducted on each passenger vessel at least weekly.
(b) The entire crew does not have to be involved in every drill, but each crewmember must participate in an abandon-ship drill and a fire drill each month.
(c) Passengers and special personnel must be strongly encouraged to attend abandon-ship and fire drills.
