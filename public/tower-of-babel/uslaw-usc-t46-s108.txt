46 U.S.C. § 108 — Fisheries
Citation: us:usc:46:108
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section108
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 1 (Definitions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this title, the term “fisheries” includes processing, storing, transporting (except in foreign commerce), planting, cultivating, catching, taking, or harvesting fish, shellfish, marine animals, pearls, shells, or marine vegetation in the navigable waters of the United States or in the exclusive economic zone.
