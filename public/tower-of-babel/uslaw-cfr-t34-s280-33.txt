34 C.F.R. § 280.33 — How does the Secretary select applications for new grants with funds appropriated in excess of $75 million?
Citation: us:cfr:34:280.33
Creator: Office Of Elementary And Secondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-280.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 280 (Magnet Schools Assistance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In selecting among applicants for funds appropriated for this program in excess of $75 million, the Secretary first identifies those remaining applicants that did not receive funds under this program in the last fiscal year of the previous funding cycle.
(b) The Secretary then awards ten additional points to each applicant identified under paragraph (a) of this section.
