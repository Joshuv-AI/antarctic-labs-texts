NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION

[Docket No. 40-8968-LR; ASLBP No. 26-995-01-MLR-BD01]


NuFuels, Inc.; Establishment of Atomic Safety and Licensing Board

    Pursuant to the Commission's regulations, see, e.g., 10 CFR 2.104, 
2.105, 2.300, 2.309, 2.313, 2.318, 2.321, notice is hereby given that 
an Atomic Safety and Licensing Board (Board) is

[[Page 50896]]

being established to preside over the following proceeding:

NUFUELS, INC. (Crownpoint Uranium Project In-Situ Recovery Facility)

    NuFuels, Inc. (NuFuels) seeks to renew source and byproduct 
materials license number SUA-1580, which authorizes NuFuels to operate 
the Crownpoint Uranium Project In-Situ Recovery Facility in McKinley 
County, New Mexico. If granted, the renewed license would authorize 
NuFuels to operate the facility, which has not been constructed, for 20 
years beyond the date of such approval. In response to a notice filed 
in the Federal Register announcing the opportunity to request a 
hearing, see 91 FR 31,476 (May 27, 2026), the State of New Mexico filed 
a hearing request on July 27, 2026.
    The Board is comprised of the following Administrative Judges:

Michael M. Gibson, Chair, Atomic Safety and Licensing Board Panel, U.S. 
Nuclear Regulatory Commission, Washington, DC 20555-0001
Dr. David A. Smith, Atomic Safety and Licensing Board Panel, U.S. 
Nuclear Regulatory Commission, Washington, DC 20555-0001
Dr. Sue H. Abreu, Atomic Safety and Licensing Board Panel, U.S. Nuclear 
Regulatory Commission, Washington, DC 20555-0001

    All correspondence, documents, and other materials shall be filed 
in accordance with the NRC E-Filing rule. See 10 CFR 2.302.

    Rockville, Maryland.

    Dated: August 3, 2026.
Edward R. Hawkens,
Chief Administrative Judge, Atomic Safety and Licensing Board Panel.
