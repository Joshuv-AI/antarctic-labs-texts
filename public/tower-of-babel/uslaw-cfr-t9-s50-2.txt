9 C.F.R. § 50.2 — Applicability of this subpart; cooperation with States.
Citation: us:cfr:9:50.2
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-50.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 50 (Animals Destroyed Because Of Tuberculosis). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The provisions of this subpart apply to all payments made by the Department for the destruction of animals because of tuberculosis, except as specifically provided in subpart B of this part.
(b) The Administrator cooperates with the proper State authorities in the eradication of tuberculosis and pays Federal indemnities for the destruction of cattle, bison, captive cervids, or swine affected with or exposed to tuberculosis.
