32 C.F.R. § 275.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:32:275.1
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-275.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 275 (Right To Financial Privacy Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to authorize DoD Components to request financial records from a financial institution pursuant to the formal written request procedure authorized by section 1108 of the Act and to set forth the conditions under which such requests may be made.
