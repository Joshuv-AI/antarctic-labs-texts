49 C.F.R. § 220.305 — Use of personal electronic devices.
Citation: us:cfr:49:220.305
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-220.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 220 (Railroad Communications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A railroad operating employee must have each personal electronic device turned off with any earpiece removed from the ear—
(a) When on a moving train;
(b) When any member of the crew is—
(1) On the ground, or
(2) Riding rolling equipment during a switching operation; or
(c) When any railroad employee is assisting in preparation of the train for movement.
