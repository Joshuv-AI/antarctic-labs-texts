29 C.F.R. § 1626.2 — Terms defined in the Age Discrimination in Employment Act of 1967, as amended.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1626.2
Creator: Equal Employment Opportunity Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1626.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1626 (Procedures—Age Discrimination In Employment Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The terms person, employer, employment agency, labor organization, employee, commerce, industry affecting commerce, and State as used herein shall have the meanings set forth in section 11 of the Age Discrimination in Employment Act, as amended.
