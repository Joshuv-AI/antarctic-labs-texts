45 C.F.R. § 1629.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1629.1
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1629.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1629 (Bonding Requirements For Recipients). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part is intended to protect LSC funds by requiring that recipients be bonded or have similar insurance coverage to indemnify recipients against losses resulting from fraudulent or dishonest acts committed by one or more employees, officers, directors, agents, volunteers, and third-party contractors who handle LSC funds.
