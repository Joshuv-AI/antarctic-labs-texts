20 U.S.C. § 1708 — Jurisdiction of district courts
Citation: us:usc:20:1708
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1708
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 39 (Equal Educational Opportunities And Transportation Of Students); Subchapter I (Equal Educational Opportunities); Part 3 (Enforcement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The appropriate district court of the United States shall have and exercise jurisdiction of proceedings instituted under section 1706 of this title.
