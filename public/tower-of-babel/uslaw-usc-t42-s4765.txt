42 U.S.C. § 4765 — Review and audit
Citation: us:usc:42:4765
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section4765
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 62 (Intergovernmental Personnel Program); Subchapter IV (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Office, the head of the Federal agency concerned, and the Comptroller General of the United States, or any of their duly authorized representatives, shall have access, for the purpose of audit and examination, to any books, documents, papers, and records of a grant recipient that are pertinent to the grant received.
