45 C.F.R. § 1637.2 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1637.2
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1637.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1637 (Representation Of Prisoners). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Incarcerated means the involuntary physical restraint of a person who has been arrested for or convicted of a crime.
(b) Federal, State or local prison means any penal facility maintained under governmental authority.
