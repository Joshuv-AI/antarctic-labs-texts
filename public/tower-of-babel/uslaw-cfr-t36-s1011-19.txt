36 C.F.R. § 1011.19 — Will the Presidio Trust issue a refund if money is erroneously collected on a debt?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1011.19
Creator: Presidio Trust
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1011.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1011 (Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Presidio Trust will promptly refund to a debtor any amount collected on a debt when the debt is waived or otherwise found not to be owed to the United States, or as otherwise required by law. Refunds under this part will not bear interest unless required by law.
