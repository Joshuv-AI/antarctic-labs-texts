11 U.S.C. § 1303 — Rights and powers of debtor
Citation: us:usc:11:1303
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section1303
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 13 (Adjustment Of Debts Of An Individual With Regular Income); Subchapter I (Officers, Administration, And The Estate). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Subject to any limitations on a trustee under this chapter, the debtor shall have, exclusive of the trustee, the rights and powers of a trustee under sections 363(b), 363(d), 363(e), 363(f), and 363(l), of this title.
