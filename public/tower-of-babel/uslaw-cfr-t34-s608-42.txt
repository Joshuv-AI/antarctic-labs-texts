34 C.F.R. § 608.42 — Under what conditions does the Secretary terminate a grant?
Citation: us:cfr:34:608.42
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-608.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 608 (Strengthening Historically Black Colleges And Universities Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary terminates any grant under which funds were not expended if an institution loses—
(a) Its accredited status; or
(b) Its legal authority in the State in which it is located—
(1) To be a junior or community college; or
(2) To provide an educational program for which it awards a bachelor's degree.
