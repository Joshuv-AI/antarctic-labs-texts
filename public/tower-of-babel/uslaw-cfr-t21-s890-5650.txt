21 C.F.R. § 890.5650 — Powered inflatable tube massager.
Citation: us:cfr:21:890.5650
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-890.5650
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 890 (Physical Medicine Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. A powered inflatable tube massager is a powered device intended for medical purposes, such as to relieve minor muscle aches and pains and to increase circulation. It simulates kneading and stroking of tissues with the hands by use of an inflatable pressure cuff.
(b) Classification. Class II (performance standards).
