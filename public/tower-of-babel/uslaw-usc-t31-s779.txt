31 U.S.C. § 779 — Use of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:31:779
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section779
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 7 (Government Accountability Office); Subchapter V (Annuities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Annuities and refunds under this subchapter shall be paid by the Comptroller General from appropriations of the Government Accountability Office.
