43 U.S.C. § 646 — Grant extended to New Mexico and Arizona
Citation: us:usc:43:646
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section646
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 14 (Grants Of Desert Lands To States For Reclamation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All the provisions of sections 641, 642 and 643 1 of this title are extended to the States of New Mexico and Arizona, and the said States upon complying with the provisions of said sections shall be entitled to have and receive all of the benefits therein conferred upon the States.
