18 U.S.C. § 1651 — Piracy under law of nations
Citation: us:usc:18:1651
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1651
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 81 (Piracy And Privateering). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever, on the high seas, commits the crime of piracy as defined by the law of nations, and is afterwards brought into or found in the United States, shall be imprisoned for life.
