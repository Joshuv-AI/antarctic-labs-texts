34 U.S.C. § 21510 — Rule of construction
Citation: us:usc:34:21510
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section21510
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 215 (Advanced Notification Of Traveling Sex Offenders). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to limit international information sharing or law enforcement cooperation relating to any person pursuant to any authority of the Department of Justice, the Department of Homeland Security, or any other department or agency.
