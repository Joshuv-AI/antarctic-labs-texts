7 C.F.R. § 2500.021 — Guiding principles.
Citation: us:cfr:7:2500.021
Creator: Office Of Advocacy And Outreach, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-2500.021
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 2500 (Oao Federal Financial Assistance Programs—General Award Administrative Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The guiding principle for Federal assistance proposal review and evaluation is to ensure that each proposal is treated in a consistent and fair manner. After the evaluation process by the review panel, OAO will provide an opportunity for applicant feedback in as timely a manner as possible.
