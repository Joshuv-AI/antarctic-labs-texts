25 C.F.R. § 39.204 — How does OIEP calculate ADM?
Citation: us:cfr:25:39.204
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-39.204
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 39 (The Indian School Equalization Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

OIEP calculates ADM by:
(a) Adding the total enrollment figures from periodic reports received from each Bureau-funded school; and
(b) Dividing the total enrollment for each school by the number of days in the school's reporting period.
