25 U.S.C. § 5126 — Mandatory application of sections 5102 and 5124
Citation: us:usc:25:5126
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section5126
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 45 (Protection Of Indians And Conservation Of Resources). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding section 5125 of this title, sections 5102 and 5124 of this title shall apply to—
(1)
all Indian tribes,
(2)
all lands held in trust by the United States for Indians, and
(3)
all lands owned by Indians that are subject to a restriction imposed by the United States on alienation of the rights of the Indians in the lands.
