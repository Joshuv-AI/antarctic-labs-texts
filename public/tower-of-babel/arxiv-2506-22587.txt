The Piltz divisor Problem in Number Fields Using The Resonance Method Thanks: 2020 Mathematics Subject Classification. 11R42, 11P21, 11N37
Nilmoni Karak, Kamalakshya Mahatab

Abstract
. The Piltz divisor problem is a natural generalization of the classical Dirichlet divisor problem. In this paper, we study this problem over number fields and obtain improved Ω − \Omega- bounds for its error terms. Our approach involves generalizing a Voronoi-type formula due to Soundararajan in the number field setting, and applying a recent result due to the second author.

Introduction

The study of error terms in various counting functions is a central theme in analytic number theory. The Dirichlet divisor problem and the Gauss circle problem are two classical examples that ask to estimate the discrepancy between an arithmetic counting function and its asymptotic approximation.
The error terms of the above said problems are

\Delta(x)=\sum_{n\leq x}d(n)-x\log x-(2\gamma-1)x,\ \ \ \text{and}\ \ \ R(x)=\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}{a^{2}+b^{2}\leq x}\\
a,b\,\in\mathbb{Z}\end{subarray}}1-\pi x,

(1.1)

Lemma 2.3
.

We have

\max_{-1\leq h\leq 1}\Delta_{\mathbb{K}}^{(k)}\left(x^{mk}e^{h}\right)\geq\frac{\alpha^{\frac{1}{mk}}}{\sqrt{\pi}}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\Delta_{\mathbb{K}}^{(k)}\left(x^{mk}e^{u/x}\right)\exp\left(-u^{2}\alpha^{\frac{2}{mk}}\right)\mathrm{d}u+O(x^{mk-1}e^{-x^{2}}).

Proof.

We start with

\displaystyle\frac{\alpha^{\frac{1}{mk}}}{\sqrt{\pi}}\int_{|u|\leq x}\Delta_{\mathbb{K}}^{(k)}\left(x^{mk}e^{u/x}\right)\exp\left(-u^{2}\alpha^{\frac{2}{mk}}\right)\mathrm{d}u

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\max_{-1\leq h\leq 1}\Delta_{\mathbb{K}}^{(k)}\left(x^{mk}e^{h}\right)\frac{\alpha^{\frac{1}{mk}}}{\sqrt{\pi}}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\exp\left(-u^{2}\alpha^{\frac{2}{mk}}\right)\mathrm{d}u

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\max_{-1\leq h\leq 1}\Delta_{\mathbb{K}}^{(k)}\left(x^{mk}e^{h}\right).

Also, using the bound
\Delta_{\mathbb{K}}^{(k)}\left(y\right)=O(y)
, we find

\displaystyle\frac{\alpha^{\frac{1}{mk}}}{\sqrt{\pi}}\int_{x}^{\infty}\Delta_{\mathbb{K}}^{(k)}\left(x^{mk}e^{u/x}\right)\exp\left(-u^{2}\alpha^{\frac{2}{mk}}\right)\mathrm{d}u

\displaystyle\ll\,

\displaystyle x^{mk}\alpha^{\frac{1}{mk}}\int_{x}^{\infty}\exp\left(\frac{u}{x}-u^{2}\alpha^{\frac{2}{mk}}\right)\mathrm{d}u

\displaystyle=\,

\displaystyle x^{mk}\exp\left(\frac{1}{4x^{2}\alpha^{\frac{1}{mk}}}\right)\int_{x\alpha^{\frac{1}{mk}}}\exp\left(-\left(u-\frac{1}{2x\alpha^{\frac{1}{mk}}}\right)^{2}\right)\mathrm{d}u

\displaystyle\ll

\displaystyle\,x^{mk-1}e^{-x^{2}}.

A similar calculation gives

\frac{\alpha^{\frac{1}{mk}}}{\sqrt{\pi}}\int_{-\infty}^{-x}\Delta_{\mathbb{K}}^{(k)}\left(x^{mk}e^{u/x}\right)\exp\left(-u^{2}\alpha^{\frac{2}{mk}}\right)\mathrm{d}u\ll x^{mk-1}e^{-x^{2}}.

Combining all the above estimates, we conclude the proof.
∎

\displaystyle\sum_{n\in\mathcal{M}}f(n)

\displaystyle\geq 2^{\frac{1}{2}(1-mk)}\alpha^{-\frac{mk+1}{2mk}}\prod_{\nu\in\mathcal{I}}(k\nu)^{[\mu_{\nu}\log_{2}\alpha]}\sum_{n\in\mathcal{M}}1

4.

4.1.

Setting

and choosing any

1.1
reproduces the lower bound for the Piltz divisor problem previously proved by the second author
[
8
]
.

4.2.

We consider
\mathbb{K}
to be a normal extension of
\mathbb{Q}
with degree
m\geq 2
. Then the sets
P_{\nu}
is empty for
1\leq\nu<m
. In this case, the Dirichlet densities
\delta_{\nu}=0

1\leq\nu<m

1.1
gives

\Delta_{\mathbb{K}}^{(k)}(x)=\Omega\left((x\log x)^{\frac{mk-1}{2mk}}(\log_{2}x)^{\frac{1}{2}k(mk+1)(mk)^{-2/(mk+1)}-\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{2mk}}(\log_{3}x)^{-\frac{1}{4}-\frac{1}{4mk}}\right).

This result improves upon the bounds previously obtained by Hafner
[
4

and by Girstmair et al.
[
2
]

4.3.

We take
\mathbb{K}
to be a non-normal extension of
\mathbb{Q}
with
[\mathbb{K}:\mathbb{Q}]=3
. Let us choose a field

such that

, where the discriminant

\mathbb{Q}
and its Galois group

L
is a quadratic extension of
\mathbb{K}

\delta_{2}=0

Then for any

\beta=\frac{(3k+1)}{12}k^{(3k-1)/(3k+1)}\left(3^{\frac{3k-1}{3k+1}}+1\right)-\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{6k}.

4.4.

Let
