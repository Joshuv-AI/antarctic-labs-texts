5 C.F.R. § 846.301 — General rules.
Citation: us:cfr:5:846.301
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-846.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 846 (Federal Employees Retirement System—Elections Of Coverage). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An individual who becomes covered by FERS as a result of an election under § 846.201 is subject to the provisions of chapter 84 of title 5, United States Code and parts 841 through 845 of this chapter, except as provided in this part.
(b) Civilian service performed before the effective date of the election under § 846.201 is not creditable under FERS except as provided in this part.
