45 C.F.R. § 153.365 — General oversight requirements for State-operated risk adjustment programs.
Citation: us:cfr:45:153.365
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-153.365
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 153 (Standards Related To Reinsurance, Risk Corridors, And Hhs Risk Adjustment Under The Affordable Care Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a State is operating a risk adjustment program, it must keep an accounting of all receipts and expenditures related to risk adjustment payments and charges and the administration of risk adjustment-related functions and activities for each benefit year.
