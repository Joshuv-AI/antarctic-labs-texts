46 C.F.R. § 46.05-25 — -25 New passenger vessel.
Citation: us:cfr:46:46.05-25
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-46.05-25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 46 (Subdivision Load Lines For Passenger Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A new passenger vessel is a vessel whose keel was laid or was a vessel converted into a passenger vessel on or after May 26, 1965.
