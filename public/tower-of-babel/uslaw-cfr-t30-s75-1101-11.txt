30 C.F.R. § 75.1101-11 — -11 Inspection of water sprinkler systems.
Citation: us:cfr:30:75.1101-11
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-75.1101-11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 75 (Mandatory Safety Standards—Underground Coal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each water sprinkler system shall be examined weekly and a functional test of the complete system shall be conducted at least once each year.
