7 C.F.R. § 927.31 — Powers.
Citation: us:cfr:7:927.31
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-927.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 927 (Pears Grown In Oregon And Washington). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Fresh Pear Committee and the Processed Pear Committee shall have the following powers to exercise each independently:
(a) To administer, as specifically provided in §§ 927.20 to 927.35, the terms and provisions of this subpart:
(b) To make administrative rules and regulations in accordance with, and to effectuate, the terms and provisions of this subpart; and
(c) To receive, investigate, and report to the Secretary complaints of violations of the provisions of this subpart.
