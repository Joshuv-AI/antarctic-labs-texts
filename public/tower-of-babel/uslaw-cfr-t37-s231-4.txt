37 C.F.R. § 231.4 — No new evidence.
Citation: us:cfr:37:231.4
Creator: U.S. Copyright Office, Library Of Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-231.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 231 (Register'S Review). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Evidence that was not previously submitted to the Board as part of written testimony or at a hearing or in response to a specific request for evidence from the Board shall not be submitted as part of a request for review or a response to a request for review.
