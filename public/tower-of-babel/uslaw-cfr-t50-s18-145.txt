50 C.F.R. § 18.145 — Procedure to obtain a Letter of Authorization (LOA).
Citation: us:cfr:50:18.145
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-18.145
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 18 (Marine Mammals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The applicant must submit the request for authorization to the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (Service) Alaska Region Marine Mammals Management Office (MMM), MS 341, 1011 East Tudor Road, Anchorage, Alaska, 99503, or by email at r7mmmregulatory@fws.gov, at least 30 days prior to the start of the planned activity.
(b) The request for an LOA must comply with the requirements set forth in §§ 18.149 through 18.151 and must include the following information:
(1) An operational plan for the activity;
(2) A digital geospatial file of the project footprint, including sound isopleths;
(3) A site-specific marine mammal monitoring and mitigation plan that specifies the procedures to monitor and mitigate the effects of the activities on sea otters; and
(4) When appropriate, a plan of cooperation, which is a documented plan that describes measures to mitigate potential conflicts between planned project activities and subsistence hunting.
