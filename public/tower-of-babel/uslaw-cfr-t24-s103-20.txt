24 C.F.R. § 103.20 — Can someone help me with filing a claim?
Citation: us:cfr:24:103.20
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Equal Opportunity, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-103.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 103 (Fair Housing—Complaint Processing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

HUD's Office of Fair Housing and Equal Opportunity can help you in filing a claim, if you contact them directly. You, or anyone who acts for you, may also ask any HUD office or an organization, individual, or attorney to help you.
