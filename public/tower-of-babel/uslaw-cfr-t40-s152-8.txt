40 C.F.R. § 152.8 — Products that are not pesticides because they are not for use against pests.
Citation: us:cfr:40:152.8
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-152.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 152 (Pesticide Registration And Classification Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A substance or article is not a pesticide, because it is not intended for use against “pests” as defined in § 152.5, if it is:
(a) A fertilizer product not containing a pesticide.
(b) A product intended to force bees from hives for the collection of honey crops.
