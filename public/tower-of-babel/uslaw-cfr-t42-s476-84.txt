42 C.F.R. § 476.84 — Changes as a result of DRG validation.
Citation: us:cfr:42:476.84
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-476.84
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 476 (Quality Improvement Organization Review). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A provider or practitioner may obtain a review by a QIO under part 473 of this chapter for changes in diagnostic and procedural coding that resulted in a change in DRG assignment as a result of QIO validation activities.
