20 U.S.C. § 1161l–1 — Model emergency response policies, procedures, and practices
Citation: us:usc:20:1161l–1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1161l–1
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 28 (Higher Education Resources And Student Assistance); Subchapter IX (Additional Programs); Part L (Student Safety And Campus Emergency Management). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary, in consultation with the Attorney General and the Secretary of Homeland Security, shall continue to—
(1)
advise institutions of higher education on model emergency response policies, procedures, and practices; and
(2)
disseminate information concerning those policies, procedures, and practices.
