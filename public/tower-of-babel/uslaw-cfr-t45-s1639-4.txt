45 C.F.R. § 1639.4 — Permissible representation of eligible clients.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1639.4
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1639.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1639 (Welfare Reform). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Recipients may represent an individual eligible client who is seeking specific relief from a welfare agency.
