On the dichotomy of p p -walk dimensions on metric measure spaces
Meng Yang

Abstract
On a volume doubling metric measure space endowed with a family of p p -energies such that the Poincaré inequality and the cutoff Sobolev inequality with p p -walk dimension β p \beta_{p} hold, for p p in an open interval I ⊆ ( 1 , + ∞ ) I\subseteq(1,+\infty) , we prove the following dichotomy: either β p = p \beta_{p}=p for all p ∈ I p\in I , or β p > p \beta_{p}>p for all p ∈ I p\in I .

Introduction

On many fractals, including the Sierpiński gasket and the Sierpiński carpet, there exists a diffusion with a heat kernel satisfying the following two-sided sub-Gaussian estimates:

\displaystyle\frac{C_{1}}{V(x,t^{1/\beta})}\exp\left(-C_{2}\left(\frac{d(x,y)}{t^{1/\beta}}\right)^{\frac{\beta}{\beta-1}}\right)\leq p_{t}(x,y)\leq\frac{C_{3}}{V(x,t^{1/\beta})}\exp\left(-C_{4}\left(\frac{d(x,y)}{t^{1/\beta}}\right)^{\frac{\beta}{\beta-1}}\right),

where
\beta

\beta=\frac{\log 5}{\log 2}
on the Sierpiński gasket (see
[

]
). For
\beta=2
,

By the standard Dirichlet form theory, a diffusion corresponds to a local regular Dirichlet form (see
[

). The Dirichlet form framework generalizes the classical Dirichlet integral

in
\mathbb{R}^{d}
. For general

-energy

Since

We provide a brief outline of the proof as follows. Firstly, under the volume doubling condition, the Poincaré inequality and the capacity upper bound with
