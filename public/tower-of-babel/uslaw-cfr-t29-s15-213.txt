29 C.F.R. § 15.213 — Are there limits to representatives' fees for claims under this subpart?
Citation: us:cfr:29:15.213
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-15.213
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 15 (Administrative Claims Under The Federal Tort Claims Act And Related Claims Statutes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. No more than 10 percent of the amount in settlement of each individual claim submitted and settled under this subpart shall be paid or delivered to or received by any agent or attorney on account of services rendered in connection with that claim. 31 U.S.C. 3721(i).
