2 U.S.C. § 287d — Expenditures
Citation: us:usc:2:287d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section287d
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 9C (Office Of Parliamentarian Of House Of Representatives). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In accordance with policies and procedures approved by the Speaker, the Parliamentarian may make such expenditures as may be necessary or appropriate for the functioning of the Office.
