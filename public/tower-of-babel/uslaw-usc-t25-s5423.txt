25 U.S.C. § 5423 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:25:5423
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section5423
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 46 (Indian Self-Determination And Education Assistance); Subchapter VII (National Fund For Excellence In American Indian Education). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purposes of this subchapter—
(1)
the term “Bureau funded school” has the meaning given that term in title XI of the Education Amendments of 1978 [25 U.S.C. 2000 et seq.];
(2)
the term “Foundation” means the Foundation established by the Secretary pursuant to section 5421 of this title; and
(3)
the term “Secretary” means the Secretary of the Interior.
