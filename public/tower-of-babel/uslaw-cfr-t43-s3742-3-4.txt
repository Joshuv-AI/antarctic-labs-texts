43 C.F.R. § 3742.3-4 — -4 Proof of publication.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3742.3-4
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3742.3-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3740 (Public Law 585; Multiple Mineral Development). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After the period of newspaper publication has expired, the person requesting publication shall obtain from the office of the newspaper of publication, a sworn statement 1 that the notice was published at the time and in accordance with the requirements under these regulations of this part, and shall file such sworn statement in the office where the Request for Publication was filed.
