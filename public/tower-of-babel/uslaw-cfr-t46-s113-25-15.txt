46 C.F.R. § 113.25-15 — -15 Distribution panels.
Citation: us:cfr:46:113.25-15
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-113.25-15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 113 (Communication And Alarm Systems And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each distribution panel must:
(a) Be watertight;
(b) Need a tool to be opened.
