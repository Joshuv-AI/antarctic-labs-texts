25 C.F.R. § 161.207 — What livestock are authorized to graze?
Citation: us:cfr:25:161.207
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-161.207
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 161 (Navajo Partitioned Lands Grazing Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following livestock are authorized to graze on the Navajo Partitioned Lands: horses, cattle, sheep, goats, mules, burros, donkeys, and llamas.
