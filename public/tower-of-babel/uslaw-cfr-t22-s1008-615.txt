22 C.F.R. § 1008.615 — Conviction.
Citation: us:cfr:22:1008.615
Creator: Inter-American Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1008.615
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1008 (Governmentwide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Conviction means a finding of guilt (including a plea of nolo contendere) or imposition of sentence, or both, by any judicial body charged with the responsibility to determine violations of the Federal or State criminal drug statutes.
