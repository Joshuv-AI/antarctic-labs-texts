2 U.S.C. § 656 — Member User Group
Citation: us:usc:2:656
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section656
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 17A (Congressional Budget And Fiscal Operations); Subchapter II (Fiscal Procedures); Part A (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Speaker of the House of Representatives, after consulting with the Minority Leader of the House, may appoint a Member User Group for the purpose of reviewing budgetary scorekeeping rules and practices of the House and advising the Speaker from time to time on the effect and impact of such rules and practices.
