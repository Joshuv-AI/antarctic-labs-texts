29 C.F.R. § 409.6 — Publication of reports required by this part.
Citation: us:cfr:29:409.6
Creator: Office Of Labor-Management Standards, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-409.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 409 (Reports By Surety Companies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Part 70 of this title shall govern inspection and examination of any report or other document filed as required by this part, and the furnishing by the Office of Labor-Management Standards of copies thereof to any person requesting them.
