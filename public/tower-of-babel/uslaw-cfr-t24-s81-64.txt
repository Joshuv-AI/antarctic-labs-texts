24 C.F.R. § 81.64 — Periodic reports.
Citation: us:cfr:24:81.64
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-81.64
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 81 (The Secretary Of Hud'S Regulation Of The Federal National Mortgage Association (Fannie Mae) And The Federal Home Loan Mortgage Corporation (Freddie Mac)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each GSE shall provide to the Secretary all:
(a) Material distributed to the GSE's Housing Advisory Council;
(b) Press releases;
(c) Investor reports;
(d) Proxy statements;
(e) Seller-servicer guides; and
(f) Other information disclosed by the GSE to entities outside of the GSE, but only where the GSE determines that such information is relevant to the Secretary's regulatory responsibilities.
