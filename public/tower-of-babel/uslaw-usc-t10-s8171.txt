10 U.S.C. § 8171 — Continuation of authority after loss of vessel or aircraft
Citation: us:usc:10:8171
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8171
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 821 (Officers In Command). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If the crew of any naval vessel or naval aircraft are separated from their vessel or aircraft because of its wreck, loss, or destruction, all the command and authority given to the officers of the vessel or aircraft remain in full force until the crew are discharged or reassigned.
