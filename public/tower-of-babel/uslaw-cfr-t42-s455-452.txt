42 C.F.R. § 455.452 — Other State screening methods.
Citation: us:cfr:42:455.452
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-455.452
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 455 (Program Integrity: Medicaid). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this subpart must restrict the State Medicaid agency from establishing provider screening methods in addition to or more stringent than those required by this subpart.
