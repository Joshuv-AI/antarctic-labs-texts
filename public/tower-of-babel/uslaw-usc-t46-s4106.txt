46 U.S.C. § 4106 — Penalties
Citation: us:usc:46:4106
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section4106
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part B (Inspection And Regulation Of Vessels); Chapter 41 (Uninspected Vessels Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If a vessel to which this chapter applies is operated in violation of this chapter or a regulation prescribed under this chapter, the owner, charterer, managing operator, agent, master, and individual in charge are each liable to the United States Government for a civil penalty of not more than $5,000. The vessel also is liable in rem for the penalty.
