18 U.S.C. § 2381 — Treason
Citation: us:usc:18:2381
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section2381
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 115 (Treason, Sedition, And Subversive Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever, owing allegiance to the United States, levies war against them or adheres to their enemies, giving them aid and comfort within the United States or elsewhere, is guilty of treason and shall suffer death, or shall be imprisoned not less than five years and fined under this title but not less than $10,000; and shall be incapable of holding any office under the United States.
