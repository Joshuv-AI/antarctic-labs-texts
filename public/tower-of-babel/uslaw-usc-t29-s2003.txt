29 U.S.C. § 2003 — Notice of protection
Citation: us:usc:29:2003
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section2003
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 22 (Employee Polygraph Protection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall prepare, have printed, and distribute a notice setting forth excerpts from, or summaries of, the pertinent provisions of this chapter. Each employer shall post and maintain such notice in conspicuous places on its premises where notices to employees and applicants to employment are customarily posted.
