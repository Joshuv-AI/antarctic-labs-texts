41 U.S.C. § 8506 — Authorization of appropriations

Context: United States Code, Title 41 (Public Contracts); Chapter 85 (Committee For Purchase From People Who Are Blind Or Severely Disabled). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.
