24 C.F.R. § 15.304 — Consideration of demands for production of material or provision of testimony.
Citation: us:cfr:24:15.304
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-15.304
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 15 (Public Access To Hud Records Under The Freedom Of Information Act And Testimony And Production Of Information By Hud Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Consideration of demands shall be within the province of the attorney representing the United States, who may raise any valid objection to the production of material or provision of testimony in response to the demand.
