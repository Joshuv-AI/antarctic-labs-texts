36 U.S.C. § 21904 — Exclusive right to name
Citation: us:usc:36:21904
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section21904
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 219 (The American National Theater And Academy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation and its State and local branches and subdivisions have the exclusive right to use the name “The American National Theater and Academy”.
