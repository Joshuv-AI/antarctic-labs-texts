42 C.F.R. § 498.52 — Time and place of hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:42:498.52
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-498.52
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 498 (APPEALS PROCEDURES FOR DETERMINATIONS THAT AFFECT PARTICIPATION IN THE MEDICARE PROGRAM AND FOR DETERMINATIONS THAT AFFECT THE PARTICIPATION OF ICFs/IID AND CERTAIN NFs IN THE MEDICAID PROGRAM). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The ALJ fixes a time and place for the hearing and gives the parties written notice at least 10 days before the scheduled date.
(b) The notice informs the parties of the general and specific issues to be resolved at the hearing.
