10 C.F.R. § 1016.25 — Storage, use, processing, transmission and destruction of classified information on computers, computer networks, electronic devices/media and mobile devices.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1016.25
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1016.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1016 (Safeguarding Of Restricted Data By Access Permittees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Storage, use, processing, and transmission of Restricted Data on computers, computer networks, electronic devices/media and mobile devices must be approved by DOE. DOE-approved methods must be used when destroying classified information that is in electronic format.
