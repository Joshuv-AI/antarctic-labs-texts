43 U.S.C. § 568 — Use of reclamation fund for expenses of and disposal of proceeds of sale of town sites
Citation: us:usc:43:568
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section568
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 12 (Reclamation And Irrigation Of Lands By Federal Government); Subchapter XV (Town Sites, Parks, Playgrounds, And School Sites). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Reclamation funds shall be available for the payment of all expenses incurred in executing the provisions of sections 561 to 563 and 566 of this title relating to town sites, and the proceeds of all sales of town sites shall be covered into the reclamation fund.
