45 C.F.R. § 156.963 — Final order not subject to review.
Citation: us:cfr:45:156.963
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-156.963
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 156 (Health Insurance Issuer Standards Under The Affordable Care Act, Including Standards Related To Exchanges). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In an action brought under § 156.961, the validity and appropriateness of the final order imposing a civil money penalty is not subject to review.
