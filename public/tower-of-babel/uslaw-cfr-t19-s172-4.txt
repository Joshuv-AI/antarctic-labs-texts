19 C.F.R. § 172.4 — Demand on surety.
Citation: us:cfr:19:172.4
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-172.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 172 (Claims For Liquidated Damages; Penalties Secured By Bonds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the principal fails to file a petition for relief or fails to comply in the prescribed time with a decision to mitigate a penalty or cancel a claim for liquidated damages issued with regard to a petition for relief, Customs will make a demand for payment on surety. The surety will then have 60 days from the date of the demand to file a petition for relief.
