50 C.F.R. § 81.12 — Contracts.
Citation: us:cfr:50:81.12

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 81 (Conservation Of Endangered And Threatened Species Of Fish, Wildlife, And Plants—Cooperation With The States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The State may use its own regulations in obtaining services providing that they adhere to Federal laws and the requirements provided by Office of Management and Budget Circular A-102. The State is the responsible authority without recourse to the Secretary regarding settlement of contractual issues.
