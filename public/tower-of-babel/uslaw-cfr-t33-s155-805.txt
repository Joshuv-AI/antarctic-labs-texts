33 C.F.R. § 155.805 — Closure devices.
Citation: us:cfr:33:155.805
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-155.805
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 155 (Oil Or Hazardous Material Pollution Prevention Regulations For Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each end of each transfer hose on board which is not connected for the transfer of oil or hazardous material must be blanked off with butterfly valves, wafer-type resilient seated valves, blank flanges, or other means acceptable to the COTP or OCMI.
(b) New, unused hose is exempt from the requirement in paragraph (a) of this section.
