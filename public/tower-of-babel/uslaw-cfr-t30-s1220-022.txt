30 C.F.R. § 1220.022 — Calculation of net profit share payment.
Citation: us:cfr:30:1220.022
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1220.022
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1220 (Accounting Procedures For Determining Net Profit Share Payment For Outer Continental Shelf Oil And Gas Leases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The net profit share payment shall be calculated by multiplying the net profit share base calculated in accordance with § 1220.021 by the net profit share rate. The net profit share payment shall be paid to the United States in accordance with § 1220.031.
