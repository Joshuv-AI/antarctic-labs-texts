49 C.F.R. § 1280.8 — Requests for mandatory review.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1280.8
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1280.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1280 (Handling Of National Security Information And Classified Material). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Because the Board does not itself generate classified documents, any requests made for mandatory review shall be coordinated by the Emergency Coordinator with appropriate officials of the Department or Agency responsible for issuance of the document involved.
