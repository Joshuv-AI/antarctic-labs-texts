42 C.F.R. § 460.158 — Effective date of enrollment.
Citation: us:cfr:42:460.158
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-460.158
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 460 (Programs Of All-Inclusive Care For The Elderly (Pace)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A participant's enrollment in the program is effective on the first day of the calendar month following the date the PACE organization receives the signed enrollment agreement.
