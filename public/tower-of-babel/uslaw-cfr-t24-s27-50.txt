24 C.F.R. § 27.50 — Management and disposition by the Secretary.
Citation: us:cfr:24:27.50
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-27.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 27 (Nonjudicial Foreclosure Of Multifamily And Single Family Mortgages). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the Secretary is the purchaser of the security property, the Secretary shall manage and dispose of it in accordance with section 203 of the Housing and Community Development Amendments of 1978, as amended, 12 U.S.C. 1701z-11, and in accordance with 24 CFR part 290.
