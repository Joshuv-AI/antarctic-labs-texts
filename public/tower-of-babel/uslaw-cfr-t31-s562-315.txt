31 C.F.R. § 562.315 — Copper sector of Iran.
Citation: us:cfr:31:562.315
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-562.315
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 562 (Iranian Sector And Human Rights Abuses Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term copper sector of Iran means the mining, refining, processing, or manufacturing of copper or copper products in Iran.
