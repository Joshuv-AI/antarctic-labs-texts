15 U.S.C. § 8901 — Definition of Administrator
Citation: us:usc:15:8901
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section8901
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 115 (Perfluoroalkyl And Polyfluoroalkyl Substances And Emerging Contaminants). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter, the term “Administrator” means the Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency.
