25 C.F.R. § 1000.1135 — What happens if the Secretary takes no action within the 60-day period (or any extensions thereof)?
Citation: us:cfr:25:1000.1135
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary, Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-1000.1135
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 1000 (Annual Funding Agreements Under The Tribal Self-Government Act Amendments To The Indian Self-Determination And Education Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The final offer is:
(a) Accepted automatically by operation of law for a compact or funding agreement provision except as to its application to a program described under 25 U.S.C. 5363(c); or
(b) Rejected automatically by operation of law with respect to any program described under 25 U.S.C. 5363(c).
