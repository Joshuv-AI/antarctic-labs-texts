42 U.S.C. § 14305 — Information gathering and access
Citation: us:usc:42:14305
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section14305
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 137 (Management Of Rechargeable Batteries And Batteries Containing Mercury); Subchapter I (Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Records and reports
A person who is required to carry out the objectives of this chapter, including—
(1)
a regulated battery manufacturer;
(2)
a rechargeable consumer product manufacturer;
(3)
a mercury-containing battery manufacturer; and
(4)
an authorized agent of a person described in paragraph (1), (2), or (3),
shall establish and maintain such records and report such information as the Administrator may by regulation reasonably require to carry out the objectives of this chapter.
(b) Access and copying
The Administrator or the Administrator’s authorized representative, on presentation of credentials of the Administrator, may at reasonable times have access to and copy any records required to be maintained under subsection (a).
(c) Confidentiality
The Administrator shall maintain the confidentiality of documents and records that contain proprietary information.
