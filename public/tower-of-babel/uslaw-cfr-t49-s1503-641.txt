49 C.F.R. § 1503.641 — Offer of proof.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1503.641
Creator: Transportation Security Administration, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1503.641
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1503 (Investigative And Enforcement Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A party whose evidence has been excluded by a ruling of the ALJ may offer the evidence for the record on appeal.
