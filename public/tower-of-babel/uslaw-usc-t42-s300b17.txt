42 U.S.C. § 300b–17 — Report by Secretary

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section300b–17
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter IX (Genetic Diseases, Hemophilia Programs, And Sudden Infant Death Syndrome); Part A (Genetic Diseases). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(1) In general
The Secretary of Health and Human Services shall—
(A)
not later than 1 year after December 18, 2014, submit to the Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions of the Senate and the Committee on Energy and Commerce of the House of Representatives a report on activities related to—
(i)
newborn screening; and
(ii)
screening children who have or are at risk for heritable disorders; and
(B)
not less than every 2 years, submit to such committees an updated version of such report.
(2) Contents
The report submitted under this section shall contain a description of—
(A)
the ongoing activities under sections 300b–8, 300b–9, and 300b–11 through 300b–14 of this title; and
(B)
the amounts expended on such activities.
