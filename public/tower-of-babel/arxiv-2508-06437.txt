Simple complexes on a flopped curve
Parth Shimpi

Abstract
Studying crepant blow-ups of (compound) du Val singularities, we classify complexes of coherent sheaves which admit no negative self-extensions — such a complex, up to flops and mutation equivalences, must either be (1) a module over a derived–equivalent algebra, or (2) a two–term extension of a coherent sheaf by skyscraper sheaves, or (3) a direct sum of shifts of skyscrapers. This translates into classifications of bricks, spherical objects, stability conditions, and algebraic t-structures in the local derived category; the lists populated by the homological minimal programme turn out exhaustive. We deduce that the Bridgeland stability manifold is connected, and that all basic tilting complexes on the variety (equivalently on the g − g- tame algebras derived-equivalent to it) are related by shifts and iterated mutation.

§ 2 The taxonomy of objects

Let

be the crepant contraction of an irreducible curve

and fix a complex

that is supported on

\Hom^{i}(x,x)\coloneq\Hom(x,x[i])=0
whenever

To prove

equivalence
\text{VdB}=\RHom({N}\oplus{O}_{X},-)

[
DW25
, §2.2]
. It is convenient to suppress

from the notation, making the identifications
\Db X\simeq\Db\Lambda_{0}
and

Notation 2.1

We write

Now we may, up to replacing

in
