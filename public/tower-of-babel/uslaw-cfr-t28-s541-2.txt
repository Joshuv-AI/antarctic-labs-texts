28 C.F.R. § 541.2 — Application.
Citation: us:cfr:28:541.2
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-541.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 541 (Inmate Discipline And Special Housing Units). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This program applies to sentenced and unsentenced inmates in Bureau custody. It also applies to sentenced and unsentenced inmates designated to any prison, institution, or facility in which persons are held in custody by direction of, or under an agreement with, the Bureau of Prisons.
