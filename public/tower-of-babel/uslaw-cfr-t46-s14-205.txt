46 C.F.R. § 14.205 — Production of credentials by merchant mariner signing shipping articles.
Citation: us:cfr:46:14.205
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-14.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 14 (Shipment And Discharge Of Merchant Mariners). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

On engagement for a voyage upon which shipping articles are required, each merchant mariner must provide to the master or individual in charge of the vessel a merchant mariner credential with endorsements required by law for the service the mariner would perform.
