7 C.F.R. § 66.406 — Summary of results.
Citation: us:cfr:7:66.406
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-66.406
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 66 (National Bioengineered Food Disclosure Standard). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If the entity that was the subject of the audit or examination of records or similar activity does not request a hearing in accordance with § 66.404, or at the conclusion of a hearing, AMS will make public the summary of the final results of the investigation.
(b) AMS's decision to make public the summary of the final results constitutes final agency action for purposes of judicial review.
