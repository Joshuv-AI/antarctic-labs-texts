30 C.F.R. § 1241.9 — May I appeal the ALJ's decision?
Citation: us:cfr:30:1241.9
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1241.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1241 (Penalties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you are adversely affected by the ALJ's decision, you may appeal that decision to the IBLA under 43 CFR part 4, subpart E.
