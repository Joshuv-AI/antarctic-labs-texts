36 C.F.R. § 9.62 — When will the NPS grant a temporary access permit?
Citation: us:cfr:36:9.62
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-9.62
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 9 (Minerals Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Regional Director determines that your proposed reconnaissance surveys will not result in surface disturbance, except for minimal disturbance necessary to perform required surveys, the Regional Director will issue you a temporary access permit within 30 days after receipt of a complete application, unless the Regional Director notifies you that additional time is necessary to evaluate or process your application.
