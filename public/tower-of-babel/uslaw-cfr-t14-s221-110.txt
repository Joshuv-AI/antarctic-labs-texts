14 C.F.R. § 221.110 — Department's authority to reject.
Citation: us:cfr:14:221.110
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-221.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 221 (Tariffs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department may reject any tariff which is not consistent with section 41504 of 49 U.S.C. Subtitle VII, with the regulations in this part, or with Department orders.
