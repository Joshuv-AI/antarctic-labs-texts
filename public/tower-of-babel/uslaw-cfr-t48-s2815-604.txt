48 C.F.R. § 2815.604 — 2815.604 Agency points of contact.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2815.604
Creator: Chapter 28—Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2815.604
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2815 (Contracting By Negotiation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each contracting activity shall designate a point of contact for the receipt and handling of unsolicited proposals. Generally, the official designated shall be the BPC or immediate subordinate.
