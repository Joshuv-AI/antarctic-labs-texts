38 U.S.C. § 1812 — Covered birth defects
Citation: us:usc:38:1812
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section1812
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 18 (Benefits For Children Of Vietnam Veterans And Certain Other Veterans); Subchapter II (Children Of Women Vietnam Veterans Born With Certain Birth Defects). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Identification.—
The Secretary shall identify the birth defects of children of women Vietnam veterans that—
(1)
are associated with the service of those veterans in the Republic of Vietnam during the Vietnam era; and
(2)
result in permanent physical or mental disability.
(b) Limitations.—
(1)
The birth defects identified under subsection (a) may not include birth defects resulting from the following:
(A)
A familial disorder.
(B)
A birth-related injury.
(C)
A fetal or neonatal infirmity with well-established causes.
(2)
In any case where affirmative evidence establishes that a covered birth defect of a child of a woman Vietnam veteran results from a cause other than the active military, naval, or air service of that veteran in the Republic of Vietnam during the Vietnam era, no benefits or assistance may be provided the child under this subchapter.
