41 U.S.C. § 6303 — Certain contracts limited to appropriated amounts
Citation: us:usc:41:6303
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title41-section6303
Context: United States Code, Title 41 (Public Contracts); Chapter 63 (General Contract Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A contract to erect, repair, or furnish a public building, or to make any public improvement, shall not be made on terms requiring the Federal Government to pay more than the amount specifically appropriated for the activity covered by the contract.
