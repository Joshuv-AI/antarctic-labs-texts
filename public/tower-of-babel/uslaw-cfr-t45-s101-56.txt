45 C.F.R. § 101.56 — Changes or cancellations of an allocation order.
Citation: us:cfr:45:101.56
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-101.56
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 101 (Health Resources Priorities And Allocations System (Hrpas)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An allocation order may be changed or canceled by an official action of HHS. Notice of such changes or cancellations may be provided directly to persons to whom the order being cancelled or modified applies or constructive notice may be provided by publication in the Federal Register.
