47 C.F.R. § 8.211 — Denial of application.
Citation: us:cfr:47:8.211
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-8.211
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 8 (Part 8— INTERNET TRANSPARENCY FOR CONSUMERS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the CLA is unable to make the findings specified in § 8.209(a), it will deny the application. Notification of the denial to the applicant will include a statement of the reasons for the denial.
