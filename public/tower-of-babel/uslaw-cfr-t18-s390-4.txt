18 C.F.R. § 390.4 — Exemptions.
Citation: us:cfr:18:390.4
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-390.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 390 (Electronic Registration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In instances in which the Commission receives communications from persons who are not registered under this part that relate to docketed proceedings and in which it appears that registration under this part offers no value to the person submitting the communication, the Commission may accept the communication for filing without requiring the person to comply with § 390.1 or § 390.3.
