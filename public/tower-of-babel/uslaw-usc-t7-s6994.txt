7 U.S.C. § 6994 — Notice and opportunity for hearing
Citation: us:usc:7:6994
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section6994
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 98 (Department Of Agriculture Reorganization); Subchapter VIII (National Appeals Division). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 10 working days after an adverse decision is made that affects the participant, the Secretary shall provide the participant with written notice of such adverse decision and the rights available to the participant under this subchapter or other law for the review of such adverse decision.
