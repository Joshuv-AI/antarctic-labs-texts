46 U.S.C. § 42307 — Review of regulations and orders
Citation: us:usc:46:42307
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section42307
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part B (Actions To Address Foreign Practices); Chapter 423 (Foreign Shipping Practices). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A regulation or final order of the Federal Maritime Commission under this chapter is reviewable exclusively in the same forum and in the same manner as provided in section 2342(3)(B) of title 28.
