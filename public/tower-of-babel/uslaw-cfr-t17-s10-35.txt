17 C.F.R. § 10.35 — Permission to state views.
Citation: us:cfr:17:10.35
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-10.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 10 (Rules Of Practice). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person may, in the discretion of the Administrative Law Judge be permitted to file a memorandum or make an oral statement of his views, and the Administrative Law Judge may, in his discretion, accept for the record written communications received from any person.
