22 U.S.C. § 2347d — Training in maritime skills
Citation: us:usc:22:2347d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2347d
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 32 (Foreign Assistance); Subchapter II (Military Assistance And Sales); Part V (International Military Education And Training). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President is encouraged to allocate a portion of the funds made available each fiscal year to carry out this part for use in providing education and training in maritime search and rescue, operation and maintenance of aids to navigation, port security, at-sea law enforcement, international maritime law, and general maritime skills.
