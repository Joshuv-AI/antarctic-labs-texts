11 C.F.R. § 9405.1 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:11:9405.1
Creator: Election Assistance Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-9405.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 9405 (Procedures For Disclosure Of Records Under The Freedom Of Information Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part implement the provisions of the Freedom of Information Act (FOIA), 5 U.S.C. 552, as amended, with respect to the availability of records for inspection and copying.
