54 U.S.C. § 101336 — Contracts for the management of field employee quarters
Citation: us:usc:54:101336
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section101336
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 1013 (Employees); Subchapter III (Housing Improvement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Subject to the appropriation of necessary funds in advance, the Secretary may enter into contracts of any duration for the management, repair, and maintenance of field employee quarters. The contract shall contain terms and conditions that the Secretary considers necessary or appropriate to protect the interests of the United States and ensure that necessary quarters are available to field employees.
