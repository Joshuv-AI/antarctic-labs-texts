49 C.F.R. § 591.3 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:49:591.3

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 591 (Importation Of Vehicles And Equipment Subject To Federal Safety, Bumper And Theft Prevention Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to any person offering a motor vehicle or item of motor vehicle equipment for importation into the United States.
