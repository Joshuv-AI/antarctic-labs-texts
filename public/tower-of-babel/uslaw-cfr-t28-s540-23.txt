28 C.F.R. § 540.23 — Inmate funds received through the mails.
Citation: us:cfr:28:540.23
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-540.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 540 (Contact With Persons In The Community). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided for in part 506 of this chapter, funds enclosed in inmate correspondence are to be rejected. Deposits intended for the inmate's commissary account must be mailed directly to the centralized commissary account (see 28 CFR part 506).
