22 U.S.C. § 287l — Congressional declaration that United Nations take steps to give effect to advisory opinion of International Court of Justice on financial obligations of members
Citation: us:usc:22:287l
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section287l
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter XVI (United Nations Organization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the sense of the Congress that the United Nations should take immediate steps to give effect to the advisory opinion of the International Court of Justice on the financial obligations of members of the United Nations in order to assure prompt payment of all assessments, including assessments to cover the cost of operations to maintain or restore international peace and security.
