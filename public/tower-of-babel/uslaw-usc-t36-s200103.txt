36 U.S.C. § 200103 — Powers
Citation: us:usc:36:200103
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section200103
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 2001 (Society Of American Florists And Ornamental Horticulturists). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation may—
(1)
adopt a constitution and bylaws for the management of its property and the regulation of its affairs; and
(2)
(A)
hold property, in the District of Columbia or elsewhere, necessary to carry out the purposes of the corporation, in an amount not to exceed $1,000,000; and
(B)
hold other property donated or bequeathed in any State or territory of the United States.
