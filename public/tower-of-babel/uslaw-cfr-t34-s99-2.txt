34 C.F.R. § 99.2 — What is the purpose of these regulations?
Citation: us:cfr:34:99.2
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-99.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 99 (Family Educational Rights And Privacy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to set out requirements for the protection of privacy of parents and students under section 444 of the General Education Provisions Act, as amended.
