49 C.F.R. § 372.115 — Commodities that are not exempt under 49 U.S.C. 13506(a)(6).
Citation: us:cfr:49:372.115
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-372.115
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 372 (Exemptions, Commercial Zones, And Terminal Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

49 U.S.C. 13506(a)(6) provides an exemption from regulation for motor vehicles used in carrying ordinary livestock, fish, and unmanufactured agricultural commodities. Certain specific commodities have been statutorily determined to be non-exempt. Administrative Ruling No. 133, which is reproduced below, is a list of those commodities that are non-exempt by statute.
