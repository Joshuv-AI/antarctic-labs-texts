9 C.F.R. § 590.28 — Other inspections.
Citation: us:cfr:9:590.28
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-590.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 590 (Inspection Of Eggs And Egg Products (Egg Products Inspection Act)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Inspection program personnel will make periodic inspections of business premises, facilities, inventories, operations, transport vehicles, and records of egg handlers, and the records of all persons engaged in the business of transporting, shipping, or receiving any eggs or egg products.
