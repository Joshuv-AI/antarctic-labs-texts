25 C.F.R. § 1000.1270 — May a Tribe/Consortium have more than one waiver request pending before the Secretary at the same time?
Citation: us:cfr:25:1000.1270
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary, Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-1000.1270
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 1000 (Annual Funding Agreements Under The Tribal Self-Government Act Amendments To The Indian Self-Determination And Education Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. A Tribe/Consortium may have more than one waiver request pending before the Secretary at the same time, provided that each waiver request affects a different regulatory provision.
