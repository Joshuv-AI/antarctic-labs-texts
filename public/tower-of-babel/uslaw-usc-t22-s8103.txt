22 U.S.C. § 8103 — Severability
Citation: us:usc:22:8103
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section8103
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 88 (Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty—United States Additional Protocol Implementation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this chapter, or the application of such provision to any person or circumstance, is held invalid, the remainder of this chapter, or the application of such provision to persons or circumstances other than those as to which it is held invalid, shall not be affected thereby.
