20 C.F.R. § 362.15 — Agent's or attorney's fee.
Citation: us:cfr:20:362.15
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-362.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 362 (Employees' Personal Property Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Under the terms of the Act, no more than 10 percent of the amount paid in settlement of a claim submitted and settled under this part may be paid or delivered to or received by any agent or attorney on account of services rendered in connection with that claim, any contract to the contrary notwithstanding.
