41 C.F.R. § 101-39.004 — -39.004 Optional operations.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-39.004
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-39.004
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-39 (39—Interagency Fleet Management Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this part shall preclude the establishment or operation of interagency fleet management systems by GSA or by other agencies which are to be operated on the basis of optional use by executive or other agencies under arrangements worked out between the agencies concerned and GSA.
