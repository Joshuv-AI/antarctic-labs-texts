7 C.F.R. § 925.54 — Special purpose shipments.
Citation: us:cfr:7:925.54
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-925.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 925 (Grapes Grown In A Designated Area Of Southeastern California). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Regulations in effect pursuant to § 925.41, § 925.52, or § 925.55 may be modified, suspended, or terminated to facilitate handling of grapes for purposes which may be recommended by the committee and approved by the Secretary.
(b) The committee shall, with the approval of the Secretary, prescribe such rules, regulations, and safeguards as it may deem necessary to prevent grapes handled under the provisions of this section from entering the channels of trade for other than the specific purposes authorized by this section.
