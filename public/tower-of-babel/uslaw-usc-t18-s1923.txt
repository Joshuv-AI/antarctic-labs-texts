18 U.S.C. § 1923 — Fraudulent receipt of payments of missing persons
Citation: us:usc:18:1923
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1923
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 93 (Public Officers And Employees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever obtains or receives any money, check, or allotment under—
(1)
subchapter VII of chapter 55 of title 5; or
(2)
chapter 10 of title 37;
without being entitled thereto, with intent to defraud, shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than one year, or both.
