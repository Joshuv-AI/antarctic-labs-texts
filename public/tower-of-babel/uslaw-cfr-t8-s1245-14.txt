8 C.F.R. § 1245.14 — Adjustment of status of certain health care workers.
Citation: us:cfr:8:1245.14
Creator: Executive Office For Immigration Review, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-1245.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 1245 (Adjustment Of Status To That Of Person Admitted For Permanent Residence). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An alien applying for adjustment of status to perform labor in a health care occupation as described in 8 CFR 1212.15(c) must present evidence at the time he or she applies for adjustment of status, and, if applicable, at the time of the interview on the application, that he or she has a valid certificate issued by the Commission on Graduates of Foreign Nursing Schools or the National Board of Certification in Occupational Therapy.
