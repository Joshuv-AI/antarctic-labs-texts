54 U.S.C. § 303101 — Establishment

Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3031 (Historic Preservation Fund). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To carry out this division (except chapter 3041) and chapter 3121, there is established in the Treasury the Historic Preservation Fund.
