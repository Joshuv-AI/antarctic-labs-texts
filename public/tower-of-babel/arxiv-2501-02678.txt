Abstract
. This article introduces the ( m , n ) (m,n) -seminearring structure, which is a generalization of ( m , n ) (m,n) -semiring. This research aims to develop theories of ( m , n ) (m,n) -seminearring. In particular, the concepts of ( m , n ) (m,n) -seminearring, ( m , n ) (m,n) -subseminearring, ( m , n ) (m,n) -ideal, ( m , n ) (m,n) -seminearring with unity, homomorphism of ( m , n ) (m,n) -seminearrings, construction of factor ( m , n ) (m,n) -seminearring of ( m , n ) (m,n) -seminearring by congruence relation, and some of its exciting properties are given. The method used in this study is to adopt the theory in ( m , n ) (m,n) -semiring.

Algebra structures play a vital role in mathematics, and they have a wide range of applications in many disciplines, such as theoretical physics, computer science, graph theory, cryptography, statistics, economics, and other fields. Let

. Let
\rho
be an equivalence relation on
X

(denoted

\rho(x)=\rho(y)

\rho(x)\cap\rho(y)=\emptyset

Definition 3.7
.

Let

-seminearring and

Lemma 3.8

Let
(R,f,g)
be an
(m,n)
-seminearring,
S\subseteq R
, and
S\neq\emptyset
. Then
(S,f,g)
is an
(m,n)
-subseminearring of
(R,f,g)
if and only if the following two conditions are
satisfied.

(1)

If
a_{1}^{m}\in S
then
f\left(a_{1}^{m}\right)\in S
.

(2)

If
b_{1}^{n}\in S
then
g\left(b_{1}^{n}\right)\in S
.

Proof.

(\Rightarrow)
They are easily seen to be smooth.
(\Leftarrow)
By part (1) and (2),
S
is closed under
f
and
g
. Since
S\subseteq R
and
(R,f,g)
is an
(m,n)
-seminearring,
f
and
g
are associative, and
g
is left distributive with respect to
f
. Thus,
(S,f,g)
is an
(m,n)
-seminearring. Therefore,
(S,f,g)
is an
(m,n)
-subseminearring of
(R,f,g)
.
∎

Theorem 3.9
.

Let
\Delta
be an index set,
(R,f,g)
is an
(m,n)
-seminearring, and
\{(S_{\alpha},f,g)|\alpha\in\Delta\}
is a collection of

-subseminearrings of

(m,n)
-subseminearring of
(R,f,g)
.

Suppose that

. Then
a_{1}^{m},b_{1}^{n}\in S_{\alpha}
for all
\alpha\in\Delta
. Since

is closed under

and

,
f\left(a_{1}^{m}\right),g\left(b_{1}^{n}\right)\in S_{\alpha}
for all
\alpha\in\Delta
. Hence,
f\left(a_{1}^{m}\right),g\left(b_{1}^{n}\right)\in S

\displaystyle g\left(a_{2}^{i},1,a_{i+1}^{n}\right)

\displaystyle=g\left(a_{2}^{i},1,a_{i+1}^{j},a_{j+1}^{n}\right)

\displaystyle=g\left(a_{2}^{i},1,g\left(a_{i+1},1^{n-1}\right),g\left(a_{i+2},1^{n-1}\right),\dotsc,g\left(a_{j},1^{n-1}\right),a_{j+1}^{n}\right)

\displaystyle=g\left(a_{2}^{j},1,a_{j+1}^{n}\right),
