10 U.S.C. § 9435 — Dean of the Faculty
Citation: us:usc:10:9435
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9435
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training); Chapter 953 (United States Air Force Academy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Dean of the Faculty is responsible to the Superintendent for developing and sustaining the curriculum and overseeing the faculty of the Academy. The qualifications, selection procedures, training, pay grade, and retention of the Dean shall be prescribed by the Secretary of the Air Force, except that a person may not be appointed or assigned as Dean unless that person holds the highest academic degree in that person’s academic field. If a person appointed as the Dean is not an officer on active duty, the person shall be appointed as a member of the Senior Executive Service.
(b)
The retirement age of an officer appointed as Dean of the Faculty is that of a permanent professor of the Academy.
