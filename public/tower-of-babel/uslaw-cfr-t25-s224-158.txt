25 C.F.R. § 224.158 — What must the Secretary include in a written notice of reassumption?
Citation: us:cfr:25:224.158
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-224.158
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 224 (Tribal Energy Resource Agreements Under The Indian Tribal Energy Development And Self Determination Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The written notice of reassumption must include:
(a) A description of the authorities the Secretary is reassuming;
(b) The reasons for the determination under § 224.157(b);
(c) The effective date of the reassumption; and
(d) A statement that the decision is a final agency action and is subject to judicial review.
