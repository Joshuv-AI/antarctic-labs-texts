DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute; Amended Notice of 
Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute Advisory Council, October 29, 2025, 
8:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., National Institutes of Health, Claude D. Pepper 
Building, 31 Center Drive, Bethesda, MD, 20894, which was published in 
the Federal Register on September 17, 2025, FR 2025-17998, 90 FRN 
44834.
    The National Heart, Lung, and Blood Advisory Council open meeting 
is being amended due to change of the meeting times. The meeting will 
be held on October 29, 2025, from 9:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. This meeting 
is open to the public.

Denise M. Santeufemio,
Supervisory Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee 
Policy.
