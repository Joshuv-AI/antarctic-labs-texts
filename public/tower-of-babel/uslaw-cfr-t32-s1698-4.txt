32 C.F.R. § 1698.4 — Confidentiality of advisory opinions and requests for advisory opinions.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1698.4
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1698.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1698 (Advisory Opinions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Advisory opinions will be confidential except as provided in § 1698.6. Requests for advisory opinions will be confidential except as provided in § 1698.3.
