40 C.F.R. § 1039.635 — What are the hardship provisions for engine manufacturers?
Citation: us:cfr:40:1039.635
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1039.635
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1039 (Control Of Emissions From New And In-Use Nonroad Compression-Ignition Engines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you qualify for the hardship provisions specified in 40 CFR 1068.245, we may approve a period of delayed compliance for up to one model year total (or two model years total for small-volume manufacturers). If you qualify for the hardship provisions specified in 40 CFR 1068.250 for small-volume manufacturers, we may approve a period of delayed compliance for up to two model years total.
