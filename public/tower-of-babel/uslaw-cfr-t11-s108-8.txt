11 C.F.R. § 108.8 — Exemption for the District of Columbia (52 U.S.C. 30102(g))
Citation: us:cfr:11:108.8
Creator: Federal Election Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-108.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 108 (Filing Copies Of Reports And Statements With State Officers (52 U.S.C. 30113)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any copy of a report required to be filed with the equivalent officer in the District of Columbia shall be deemed to be filed if the original has been filed with the Commission.
