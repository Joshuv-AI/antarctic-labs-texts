12 U.S.C. § 4518a — Limitation on bonuses to executives of Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac
Citation: us:usc:12:4518a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section4518a
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 46 (Government Sponsored Enterprises); Subchapter I (Supervision And Regulation Of Enterprises); Part A (Financial Safety And Soundness Regulator). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision in law, senior executives at the Federal National Mortgage Association and the Federal Home Loan Mortgage Corporation are prohibited from receiving bonuses during any period of conservatorship for those entities on or after April 4, 2012.
