43 C.F.R. § 3834.13 — Will BLM prorate annual maintenance or oil shale fees?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3834.13
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3834.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3834 (Required Fees For Mining Claims Or Sites). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

BLM will not prorate annual maintenance or oil shale fees if you hold a mining claim or site for only part of a year. You must pay the full annual fee even if you hold the claim or site for just one day in an assessment year.
