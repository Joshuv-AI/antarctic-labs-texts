20 C.F.R. § 625.7 — Disaster Unemployment Assistance: Duration.
Citation: us:cfr:20:625.7
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-625.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 625 (Disaster Unemployment Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

DUA shall be payable to an eligible unemployed worker or eligible unemployed self-employed individual for all weeks of unemployment which begin during a Disaster Assistance Period.
