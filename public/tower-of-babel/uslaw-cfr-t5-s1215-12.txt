5 C.F.R. § 1215.12 — Interest, penalties, and administrative costs.

Creator: Merit Systems Protection Board
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1215 (Debt Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Charges may be assessed for interest, penalties, and administrative costs in accordance with the Federal Claims Collection Standards, 4 CFR 102.13. Dated: July 24, 1987.
