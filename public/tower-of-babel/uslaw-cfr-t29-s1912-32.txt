29 C.F.R. § 1912.32 — Presence of OSHA officer or employee.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1912.32
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1912.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1912 (Advisory Committees On Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The meetings of all advisory committees shall be in the presence of an OSHA officer or employee designated for this purpose. Such officer or employee shall be empowered to adjourn any meeting whenever he determines adjournment to be in the public interest.
