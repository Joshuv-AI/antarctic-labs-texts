43 C.F.R. § 3152.3 — Renewal of exploration permit.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3152.3
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3152.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3150 (Onshore Oil And Gas Geophysical Exploration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon application by the permittee and payment of the filing fee for renewal of exploration permit—Alaska, found in the fee schedule in section 3000.12 of this chapter (except where the exploration operations are to be conducted on a leasehold by or on behalf of the lessee), an exploration permit may be renewed for a period not to exceed one year.
