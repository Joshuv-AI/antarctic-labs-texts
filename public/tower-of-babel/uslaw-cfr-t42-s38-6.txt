42 C.F.R. § 38.6 — Nondiscrimination.
Citation: us:cfr:42:38.6
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-38.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 38 (Disaster Assistance For Crisis Counseling And Training). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Attention is called to the requirements of 24 CFR 2205.13 relating to nondiscrimination on the grounds of race. religion, sex, color, age, economic status, or national origin in the provision of disaster assistance.
