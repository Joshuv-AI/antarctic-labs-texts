14 C.F.R. § 156.5 — Project cost allowability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:156.5
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-156.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 156 (State Block Grant Pilot Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A participating State shall not use State block grant funds for reimbursement of project costs that would not be eligible for reimbursement under a project grant administered by the FAA.
(b) A participating State shall not use State block grant funds for reimbursement or funding of administrative costs incurred by the State pursuant to the State block grant program.
