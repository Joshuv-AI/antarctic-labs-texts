22 U.S.C. § 287e–4 — Limitation on United States contributions to peacekeeping operations not authorized by the United Nations Security Council
Citation: us:usc:22:287e–4
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section287e–4
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter XVI (United Nations Organization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

None of the funds authorized to be appropriated or otherwise made available to pay assessed and other expenses of international peacekeeping activities under this subchapter may be made available for an international peacekeeping operation that has not been expressly authorized by the United Nations Security Council.
