34 C.F.R. § 385.2 — Who is eligible for assistance under these programs?
Citation: us:cfr:34:385.2
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-385.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 385 (Rehabilitation Training). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

States and public or private nonprofit agencies and organizations, including Indian tribes and institutions of higher education, are eligible for assistance under the Rehabilitation Training program.
