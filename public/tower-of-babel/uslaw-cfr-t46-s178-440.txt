46 C.F.R. § 178.440 — Drainage of open boats.
Citation: us:cfr:46:178.440
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-178.440
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 178 (Intact Stability And Seaworthiness). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The deck within the hull of an open boat must drain to the bilge. Overboard drainage of the deck is not permitted.
