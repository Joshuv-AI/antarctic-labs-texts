39 C.F.R. § 953.13 — Appeal from initial decision.
Citation: us:cfr:39:953.13
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-953.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 953 (Mailability). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Either party may file exceptions in a brief on appeal to the Judicial Officer within 5 days after receipt of the initial decision unless additional time is granted. A reply brief may be filed within 5 days after the receipt of the appeal brief by the opposing party.
