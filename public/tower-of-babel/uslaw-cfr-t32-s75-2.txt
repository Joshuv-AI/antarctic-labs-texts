32 C.F.R. § 75.2 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:32:75.2
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-75.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 75 (Exceptional Family Member Program (Efmp)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to:
(a) Service members who have family members with special needs as described in this part.
(b) All DoD civilian employees in overseas locations and selectees for overseas positions who have family members with special needs as described in this part.
