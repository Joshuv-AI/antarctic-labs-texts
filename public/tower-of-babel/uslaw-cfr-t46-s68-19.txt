46 C.F.R. § 68.19 — Application by an 883-1 corporation to document a vessel.
Citation: us:cfr:46:68.19
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-68.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 68 (Documentation Of Vessels: Exceptions To Coastwise Qualification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An application by an 883-1 corporation to document a vessel must comply with the applicable requirements in subparts A, D, E, F, G, H, I, K, and L of part 67 of this chapter.
(b) An application by an 883-1 corporation to document a vessel must include a copy of the Certificate of Compliance issued under § 68.7.
