22 C.F.R. § 1003.9 — Mailing lists.
Citation: us:cfr:22:1003.9
Creator: Inter-American Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1003.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1003 (Rules Safeguarding Personal Information In Iaf Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An individual's name and address may not be sold or rented by the Inter-American Foundation unless such action is specifically authorized by law. This section does not require the withholding of names and addresses otherwise permitted to be made public.
