42 C.F.R. § 137.48 — What is the effect of incorporating a Title I provision into a compact or funding agreement?
Citation: us:cfr:42:137.48
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-137.48
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 137 (Tribal Self-Governance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The incorporated Title I provision shall have the same force and effect as if it were set out in full in Title V.
