Abstract
T2K has made improved measurements of three-flavor neutrino mixing with 19.7(16.3) × 10 20 \times 10^{20} protons on target in (anti-)neutrino-enhanced beam modes. A new sample of muon-neutrino events with tagged pions has been added at the far detector, increasing the neutrino-enhanced muon-neutrino sample size by 42.5%. In addition, new samples have been added at the near detector, and significant improvements have been made to the flux and neutrino interaction modeling. T2K data continues to prefer the normal mass ordering and upper octant of sin 2 ⁡ θ 23 \sin^{2}\theta_{23} with a near-maximal value of the charge-parity violating phase with best-fit values in the normal ordering of δ CP \delta_{\scriptscriptstyle\mathrm{CP}} = − 2.18 ​ + 1.22 − 0.47 =-2.18\begin{subarray}{c}+1.22\\ -0.47\end{subarray} , sin 2 ⁡ θ 23 \sin^{2}\theta_{23} = 0.559 ​ + 0.018 − 0.078 =0.559\begin{subarray}{c}+0.018\\ -0.078\end{subarray} and Δ ​ m 32 2 \Delta{}m^{2}_{32} = ( + 2.506 ​ + 0.039 − 0.052 ) × 10 − 3 =(+2.506\begin{subarray}{c}+0.039\\ -0.052\end{subarray})\times 10^{-3} eV 2 .



Introduction—
The T2K experiment
Abe et al. 2011
measures three-flavor neutrino mixing parameters by observing the disappearance of
\nu_{\mu}
(
\bar{\nu}_{\mu}
) and the appearance of
\nu_{e}
(
\bar{\nu}_{e}
) over a distance of 295 km in a narrow-band, predominantly
\nu_{\mu}
or
\bar{\nu}_{\mu}
beam, which peaks at an energy of 0.6 GeV. Data corresponding to 3.6
\times
10
21
protons on target (POT) was analyzed with improvement to the neutrino flux and interaction modeling compared to previous studies
Abe et al. 2023
. New photon- and proton-tagged near detector (ND) samples, as well as a new neutrino-enhanced muon-neutrino sample with tagged pions (
\nu_{\mu}
CC
1\pi^{\pm}

The T2K experiment—
A beam of neutrinos is produced by colliding 30 GeV protons, accelerated by the J-PARC main ring, with a graphite target. These protons interact in the target to produce pions and kaons that are focused into a secondary beam by a system of three magnetic horns and then decay in a 96 m long tunnel. Depending on the direction of the current flowing in the horns, either positively or negatively charged hadrons are focused, producing a neutrino- (
\nu
-mode) or antineutrino-enhanced (
\bar{\nu}
-mode) beam.

Two near detectors are located 280 m away from the graphite target. One is placed on-axis (INGRID)
Abe et al. 2012
and the other at 2.5
∘
off-axis (ND280) with respect to the beam direction. They sample the unoscillated beam by monitoring its direction, intensity and flavor content as well as constraining uncertainties in the neutrino interaction model. The far detector, Super-Kamiokande (SK)
Fukuda et al. 2003
is a 50 kton water-Cherenkov detector located beneath a 1 km rock overburden within the Kamioka mine in Japan. It measures the oscillated neutrino flux 295 km away from its production point at
2.5^{\circ}
off-axis.

The analysis reported in this letter uses all data collected by T2K from January 2010 to February 2020, corresponding to an SK exposure of
19.7\times 10^{20}
POT in
\nu
-mode and
16.3\times 10^{20}
POT in
\bar{\nu}
-mode. The same POT was used in a previous analysis
Abe et al. 2023
.

Neutrino flux prediction—
The neutrino flux Monte Carlo (MC) simulation uses the FLUKA 2011.2
Ahdida et al. 2022
;
Battistoni et al. 2015
interaction model for proton interactions inside the target and GEANT3
Brun et al. 1994
for interactions outside the target. Hadron production in the target is tuned to external datasets
Abgrall et al. 2016a
;
Abgrall et al. 2016b
;
Abgrall et al. 2019
. The flux depends on the beamline conditions and the measured proton beam profile, and the prediction is modified to account for conditions during each operating period.

The resulting flux model
Abe et al. 2013
is used to estimate the unoscillated
\nu
-mode and
\bar{\nu}
-mode fluxes at all detectors, for all contributing neutrino flavors, as well as their correlated uncertainties. INGRID is used to monitor the beam direction and validate the neutrino flux simulation. The uncertainty on the measured beam direction is included in the flux uncertainties used in the oscillation parameter measurement.

This analysis includes new constraints from the NA61/SHINE replica target datasets
Abgrall et al. 2019
, including high-statistics measurements of
\pi^{\pm}
production as well as, for the first time,
K^{\pm}
and proton production measurements. Among other improvements these constraints reduce the
\nu_{\mu}
flux uncertainty to below 4% for energies up to 7 GeV.

Neutrino interaction modeling—
The nominal model prediction was generated with the neutrino event generator NEUT 5.4.0
Hayato and Pickering 2021
. Important improvements were made to the estimated neutrino interaction uncertainty for this analysis with the number of free parameters used to describe this uncertainty increasing by 26 from the previous analysis
Abe et al. 2023
to a total of 75.

Pionless:

New degrees of freedom were included to cover theoretical uncertainties in nuclear effects for Charged-Current (CC) Quasi-Elastic (CCQE), multi-nucleon (2p2h) and hard-scattering processes.

Ad hoc
uncertainties in the low four-momentum transfer response for CCQE interactions were replaced with parameters that vary the impact of Pauli blocking and the effect of nuclear transparency on the inclusive cross section. An effective parameterization of the latter was derived by comparison to the NuWro
Golan et al. 2012
;
Juszczak 2009
implementation of the optical potential
Ankowski et al. 2015
. New uncertainties on the nuclear ground state for CCQE events, which parameterize the shell structure of the missing momentum (
p_{\text{miss}}
) and missing energy (
E_{\text{miss}}
) response in the initial state Spectral Function (SF), were included
Stowell et al. 2017
;
Chakrani et al. 2024
. The Benhar SF
Benhar and Fabrocini 2000
incorporates multi-nucleon knockout in the
E_{\text{miss}}>100
MeV,
p_{\text{miss}}>300
MeV/
c

Bodek and Cai 2019
, a three-momentum-transfer-dependent freedom on the Nucleon Removal Energy (NRE) was added
Dolan et al. 2023
. Additional freedom for 2p2h interactions was included by separating the existing uncertainties by the target nucleon pair type.

Multiple pions:

new uncertainties were developed for multiple pion production in the low hadronic mass region,
1.3<W<2.0~\mathrm{GeV}

Bodek et al. 2021

A new 16% uncertainty on the cross section for pion charge-exchange reactions for

As the new ND selections are sensitive to proton kinematics, an uncertainty was added that varies the fraction of events that include intranuclear nucleon rescattering while keeping the leptonic observables unmodified. The size of this uncertainty (30%) was motivated by a recent analysis of nuclear transparency data

Near detector analysis—
The neutrino flux and interaction models were constrained by fitting the unoscillated CC event spectra at the ND280. Three Time Projection Chambers (TPC)
Abgrall et al. 2011

Amaudruz et al. 2012

Selected CC events were separated into different samples according to the FGD in which the interaction occurred, the beam mode, the muon charge and the reconstructed final-state particle multiplicities. Negatively-charged muon candidates selected in
\nu
-mode were divided into five samples per FGD: 1) at least one photon (CC-photon), 2) no photons, pions or protons (CC0
\pi
0p), 3) no photons or pions and at least one proton (CC0
\pi

\pi^{+}

\bar{\nu}

\pi

\pi
), 3) and all other CC events (CC-Other). Positively charged muon candidates in
\nu

\bar{\nu}
-mode
Abe et al. 2017

Oscillation Analysis—
In the analysis, the PDG parameterization of the PMNS matrix

\sin^{2}\theta_{23}

\delta_{\scriptscriptstyle\mathrm{CP}}

Conclusions—
The T2K collaboration has measured the three-flavor PMNS neutrino oscillation parameters
\Delta{}m^{2}_{32}
,
\sin^{2}\theta_{13}
,
\sin^{2}\theta_{23}
,
\delta_{\scriptscriptstyle\mathrm{CP}}
,
J_{\scriptscriptstyle\mathrm{CP}}
, and the mass ordering, using
3.6\times 10^{21}
POT at SK. The analysis included a new
\nu_{\mu}
CC1
\pi^{\pm}
SK sample, which increased the
\nu
-mode
\mu
-like statistics by 42.5%; a new ND event samples in
\nu
-mode; and significant improvements to the flux and neutrino interaction modeling. The results are consistent with previous analyses
Abe et al. 2023
, showing a weak preference for normal ordering and upper octant of
\sin^{2}\theta_{23}
with best-fit values of
\sin^{2}\theta_{23}=0.559\begin{subarray}{c}+0.018\\
-0.078\end{subarray}
and
\Delta{}m^{2}_{32}

=(2.506\begin{subarray}{c}+0.039\\
-0.052\end{subarray})\times 10^{-3}

\mathrm{e}\mathrm{V}^{2}
. One of the CP conserving values,
\delta_{\scriptscriptstyle\mathrm{CP}}=0
, is excluded at 90% confidence level and a best-fit value of
\delta_{\scriptscriptstyle\mathrm{CP}}=-2.18\begin{subarray}{c}+1.22\\
-0.47\end{subarray}
for normal ordering with the RC applied was found.
