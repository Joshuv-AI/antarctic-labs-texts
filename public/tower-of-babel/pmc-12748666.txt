RETRACTED ARTICLE: Hybrid deep learning model for autism spectrum disorder diagnosis
Authors: D Aarthi, S Kannimuthu
Published: 2025

Retraction of: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-28819-4, published online 29 December 2025

The Editors have retracted and removed this Article. Concerns were raised regarding the use of images of minors from a non-curated dataset. The dataset is reported to contain images of children with autism spectrum disorder (ASD). However, the images appear to have been collected from the internet without any documented clinical history or confirmation of an actual ASD diagnosis. Additionally, there is no documented ethical oversight, or consent of the children included in the dataset or their parents and legal guardians.

In light of these ethical concerns and the reliance on unverifiable data, the Editors and Publisher no longer have confidence in the reliability of this Article.

The Authors disagree with this retraction.
