42 C.F.R. § 460.56 — Procedures for imposing sanctions and civil money penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:42:460.56
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-460.56
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 460 (Programs Of All-Inclusive Care For The Elderly (Pace)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

CMS provides notice and a right to request a hearing according to the procedures set forth in either of the following:
(a) Section 422.756(a) and (b) of this chapter if CMS imposes a suspension of enrollment or payment under § 460.42 or § 460.48(b).
(b) Section 422.756(e)(2)(v) of this chapter if CMS imposes civil money penalties under § 460.46.
