25 C.F.R. § 117.22 — Disbursements to legal guardians.
Citation: us:cfr:25:117.22
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-117.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 117 (Deposit And Expenditure Of Individual Funds Of Members Of The Osage Tribe Of Indians Who Do Not Have Certificates Of Competency). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any disbursement authorized to be made to an Indian by the regulations of this part may, when the Indian is under guardianship, be made by the superintendent to the guardian. All expenditures by a guardian of the funds of his ward must be approved in writing by the court and the superintendent.
