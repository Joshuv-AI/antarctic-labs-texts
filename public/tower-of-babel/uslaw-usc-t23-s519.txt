23 U.S.C. § 519 — Infrastructure development
Citation: us:usc:23:519
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title23-section519
Context: United States Code, Title 23 (Highways); Chapter 5 (Research, Technology, And Education). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Funds made available to carry out this chapter for operational tests of intelligent transportation systems—
(1)
shall be used primarily for the development of intelligent transportation system infrastructure, equipment, and systems; and
(2)
to the maximum extent practicable, shall not be used for the construction of physical surface transportation infrastructure unless the construction is incidental and critically necessary to the implementation of an intelligent transportation system project.
