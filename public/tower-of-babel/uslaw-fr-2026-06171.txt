NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION


Sunshine Act Meetings

    The National Science Board's Committee on Strategy hereby gives 
notice of scheduling a teleconference for the transaction of National 
Science Board business pursuant to the NSF Act and the Government in 
the Sunshine Act.

TIME AND DATE: April 3, 2026, at 11:00 a.m.-12:00 p.m. Eastern.

PLACE: This meeting will be via videoconference through the National 
Science Foundation, 401 Dulaney Street, Alexandria, VA 22314.

STATUS: Closed.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED:  The agenda is: Chair's Opening Remarks about 
the Agenda; Committee discussion of NSF FY28 budget.

CONTACT PERSON FOR MORE INFORMATION: The point of contact for this 
meeting is: Chris Blair, [email protected], 703-292-7000. Meeting 
information and updates may be found at www.nsf.gov/nsb.

Ann E. Bushmiller,
Senior Counsel to the National Science Board.
