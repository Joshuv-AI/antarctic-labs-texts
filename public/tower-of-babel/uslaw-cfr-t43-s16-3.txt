43 C.F.R. § 16.3 — Terms and conditions.
Citation: us:cfr:43:16.3
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-16.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 16 (Conservation Of Helium). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The applicant must agree not to develop wells on Federal land with the principal purpose of recovering the helium component of natural gas unless permission to do so has been expressly granted by the Secretary.
