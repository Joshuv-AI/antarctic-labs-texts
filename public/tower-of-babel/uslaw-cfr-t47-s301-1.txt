47 C.F.R. § 301.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:47:301.1
Creator: National Telecommunications And Information Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-301.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 301 (Relocation Of And Spectrum Sharing By Federal Government Stations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to set forth procedures for the Technical Panel and Dispute Resolution Board as required pursuant to the National Telecommunications and Information Administration Organization Act (hereinafter “NTIA Organization Act”), as amended (47 U.S.C. 923(g)-(l) and 928).
