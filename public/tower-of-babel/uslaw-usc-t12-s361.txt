12 U.S.C. § 361 — Bills receivable, bills of exchange, acceptances; regulations by Board of Governors
Citation: us:usc:12:361
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section361
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 3 (Federal Reserve System); Subchapter IX (Powers And Duties Of Federal Reserve Banks). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The discount and rediscount and the purchase and sale by any Federal reserve bank of any bills receivable and of domestic and foreign bills of exchange, and of acceptances authorized by this chapter, shall be subject to such restrictions, limitations, and regulations as may be imposed by the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System.
