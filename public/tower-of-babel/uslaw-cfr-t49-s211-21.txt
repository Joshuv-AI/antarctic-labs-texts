49 C.F.R. § 211.21 — Consideration of comments received.
Citation: us:cfr:49:211.21
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-211.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 211 (Rules Of Practice). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All timely comments are considered before final action is taken on a rulemaking proposal. Late-filed comments will be considered so far as possible without incurring additional expense or delay.
