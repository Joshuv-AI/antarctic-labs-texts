36 U.S.C. § 31101 — Organization

Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 311 (Boys & Girls Clubs Of America). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Federal Charter.—
Boys & Girls Clubs of America (in this chapter, the “corporation”) is a federally chartered corporation.
(b) Place of Incorporation and Domicile.—
The corporation is declared to be incorporated and domiciled in the District of Columbia.
(c) Perpetual Existence.—
Except as otherwise provided, the corporation has perpetual existence.
