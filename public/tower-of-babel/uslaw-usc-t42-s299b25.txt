42 U.S.C. § 299b–25 — Technical assistance
Citation: us:usc:42:299b–25
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section299b–25
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter VII (Agency For Healthcare Research And Quality); Part C (Patient Safety Improvement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary, acting through the Director, may provide technical assistance to patient safety organizations, including convening annual meetings for patient safety organizations to discuss methodology, communication, data collection, or privacy concerns.
