43 C.F.R. § 2650.0-8 — -8 Waiver.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2650.0-8
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2650.0-8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2650 (Alaska Native Selections). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary may, in his discretion, waive any nonstatutory requirement of these regulations. When the rights of third parties will not be impaired, and when rapid, certain settlement of the claims of Natives will be assisted, minor procedural and technical errors should be waived.
