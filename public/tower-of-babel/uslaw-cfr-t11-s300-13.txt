11 C.F.R. § 300.13 — Reporting (52 U.S.C. 30101 note and 30104(e)).
Citation: us:cfr:11:300.13
Creator: Federal Election Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-300.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 300 (Non-Federal Funds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The national committee of a political party, any national congressional campaign committee of a political party, and any subordinate committee of either, shall report all receipts and disbursements during the reporting period.
