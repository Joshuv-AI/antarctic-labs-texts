31 C.F.R. § 407.14 — Penalties and other law.
Citation: us:cfr:31:407.14
Creator: Secret Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-407.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 407 (Regulations Governing Conduct In The Treasury Building And The Treasury Annex). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whoever shall be found guilty of violating the regulations in this part while on the property is subject to a fine of not more than $50 or imprisonment of not more than 30 days, or both (see 40 U.S.C. 318c). Nothing contained in these regulations shall be construed to abrogate any other Federal laws or regulations of the District of Columbia applicable to the property referred to in § 407.2 and governed by the regulations.
