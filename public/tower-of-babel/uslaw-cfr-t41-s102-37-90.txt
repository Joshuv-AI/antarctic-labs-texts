41 C.F.R. § 102-37.90 — -37.90 State plan nonconformance.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-37.90
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-37.90
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-37 (37—Donation Of Surplus Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a SASP does not operate in accordance with its plan, GSA may withhold allocation and transfer of surplus property until the nonconformance is corrected.
