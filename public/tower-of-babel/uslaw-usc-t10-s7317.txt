10 U.S.C. § 7317 — Thirty years or more: regular enlisted members
Citation: us:usc:10:7317
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7317
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 741 (Retirement For Length Of Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A regular enlisted member of the Army who has at least 30 years of service computed under section 7325 of this title shall be retired upon his request.
