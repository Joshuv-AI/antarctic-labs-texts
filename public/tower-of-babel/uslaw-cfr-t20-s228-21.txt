20 C.F.R. § 228.21 — Entitlement as a spouse or divorced spouse and as a survivor.
Citation: us:cfr:20:228.21
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-228.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 228 (Computation Of Survivor Annuities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an individual is entitled to both a spouse or divorced spouse and survivor annuity, only the larger annuity will be paid. However, if the individual so chooses, he or she may receive the smaller annuity rather than the larger annuity.
