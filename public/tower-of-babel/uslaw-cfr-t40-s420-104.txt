40 C.F.R. § 420.104 — New source performance standards (NSPS).
Citation: us:cfr:40:420.104
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-420.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 420 (Iron And Steel Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The discharge of wastewater pollutants from any new source subject to this subpart shall not exceed the standards set forth below.
(a) Cold rolling mills—(1) Recirculation—single stand.
(2) Recirculation—multiple stands.
(3) Combination.
(4) Direct application—single stand.
(5) Direct application—multiple stands.
(b) Cold worked pipe and tube mills—(1) Using water.
(2) Using oil solutions.
