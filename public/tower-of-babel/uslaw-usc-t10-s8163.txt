10 U.S.C. § 8163 — Naval shipyards
Citation: us:usc:10:8163
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8163
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 821 (Officers In Command). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Commanders of naval shipyards may be selected by the President from officers of the Navy not below the grade of commander.
