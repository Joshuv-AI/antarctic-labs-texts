48 C.F.R. § 1313.106-2-70 — 1313.106-2-70 Evaluation of solicitations.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1313.106-2-70
Creator: Chapter 13—Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1313.106-2-70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1313 (Simplified Acquisition Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All solicitations using simplified acquisition procedures in FAR Part 13 must include provision 1352.213-70, Evaluation Utilizing Simplified Acquisition Procedures, or similar language setting out evaluation criteria.
