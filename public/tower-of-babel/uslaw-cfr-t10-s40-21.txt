10 C.F.R. § 40.21 — General license to receive title to source or byproduct material.
Citation: us:cfr:10:40.21
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-40.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 40 (Domestic Licensing Of Source Material). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A general license is hereby issued authorizing the receipt of title to source or byproduct material, as defined in this part, without regard to quantity. This general license does not authorize any person to receive, possess, deliver, use, or transfer source or byproduct material.
