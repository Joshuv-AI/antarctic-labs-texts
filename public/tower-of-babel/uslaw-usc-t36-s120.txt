36 U.S.C. § 120 — National Defense Transportation Day
Citation: us:usc:36:120
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section120
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part A (Observances And Ceremonies); Chapter 1 (Patriotic And National Observances). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President is requested to issue each year a proclamation—
(1)
designating the third Friday in May as National Defense Transportation Day; and
(2)
urging the people of the United States, including labor, management, users, and investors, in all communities served by the various forms of transportation to observe National Defense Transportation Day by appropriate ceremonies that will give complete recognition to the importance to each community and its people of the transportation system of the United States and the maintenance of the facilities of the system in the most modern state of adequacy to serve the needs of the United States in times of peace and in national defense.
