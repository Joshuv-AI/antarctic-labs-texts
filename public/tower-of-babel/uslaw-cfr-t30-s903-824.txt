30 C.F.R. § 903.824 — Special performance standards—Mountaintop removal.
Citation: us:cfr:30:903.824
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-903.824
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 903 (Arizona). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Part 824 of this chapter, Special Permanent Program Performance Standards—Mountaintop Removal, applies to any person who conducts surface coal mining and reclamation operations constituting mountaintop removal mining.
