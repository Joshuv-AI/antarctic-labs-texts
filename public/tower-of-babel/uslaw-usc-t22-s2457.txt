22 U.S.C. § 2457 — Reports by Board
Citation: us:usc:22:2457
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2457
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 33 (Mutual Educational And Cultural Exchange Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Board shall submit annual reports to the Congress and such other reports to the Congress as it deems appropriate, and shall make reports to the public in the United States and abroad to develop a better understanding of and support for the programs authorized by this chapter.
