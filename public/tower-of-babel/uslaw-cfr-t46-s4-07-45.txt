46 C.F.R. § 4.07-45 — -45 Foreign units of Coast Guard, investigation by.
Citation: us:cfr:46:4.07-45
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-4.07-45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 4 (Marine Casualties And Investigations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Investigations of marine casualties conducted by foreign units of the Coast Guard must be in accordance with the regulations in this part and all actions taken in connection with the investigations of such marine casualties entered in the official log(s) of the vessel(s) concerned.
