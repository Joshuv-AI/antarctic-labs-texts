15 C.F.R. § 971.429 — Special terms, conditions and restrictions.
Citation: us:cfr:15:971.429
Creator: National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-971.429
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 971 (Deep Seabed Mining Regulations For Commercial Recovery Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Although the general criteria and standards to be used in establishing TCRs for a permit are set forth in this part, as referenced in §§ 971.418 through 971.428, the Administrator may impose special TCRs for the conservation of natural resources, protection of the environment, or the safety of life and property at sea when required by differing physical and environmental conditions.
