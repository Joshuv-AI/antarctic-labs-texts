49 C.F.R. § 40.107 — Who may inspect laboratories?
Citation: us:cfr:49:40.107
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-40.107
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 40 (Procedures For Transportation Workplace Drug And Alcohol Testing Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As a laboratory, you must permit an inspection, with or without prior notice, by ODAPC, a DOT agency, or a DOT-regulated employer that contracts with the laboratory for drug testing under the DOT drug testing program, or the designee of such an employer.
