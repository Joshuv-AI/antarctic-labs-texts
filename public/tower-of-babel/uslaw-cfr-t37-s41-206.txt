37 C.F.R. § 41.206 — Common interests in the invention.
Citation: us:cfr:37:41.206
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-41.206
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 41 (Practice Before The Patent Trial And Appeal Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An administrative patent judge may decline to declare, or if already declared the Board may issue judgment in, an interference between an application and another application or patent that are commonly owned.
