31 U.S.C. § 1518 — Adverse personnel actions
Citation: us:usc:31:1518
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section1518
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 15 (Appropriation Accounting); Subchapter II (Apportionment). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An officer or employee of the United States Government or of the District of Columbia government violating section 1517(a) of this title shall be subject to appropriate administrative discipline including, when circumstances warrant, suspension from duty without pay or removal from office.
