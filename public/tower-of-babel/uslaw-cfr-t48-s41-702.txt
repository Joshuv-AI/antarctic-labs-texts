48 C.F.R. § 41.702 — 41.702 Formats for annual utility service review.
Citation: us:cfr:48:41.702
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-41.702
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 41 (Acquisition Of Utility Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Formats for use in conducting annual reviews of the following utility services are available from the address specified at 41.301(a) and may be used at the agency's discretion:
(1) Electric service.
(2) Gas service.
(3) Water and sewage service.
(b) Contracting officers may modify the annual utility service review format as necessary to fully cover the service used.
