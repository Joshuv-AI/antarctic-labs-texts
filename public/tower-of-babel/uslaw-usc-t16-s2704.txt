16 U.S.C. § 2704 — Loan rates and repayment
Citation: us:usc:16:2704
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section2704
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 47 (Small Hydroelectric Power Projects). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Interest
Each loan made pursuant to this chapter shall bear interest at the discount or interest rate used at the time the loan is made for water resources planning projects under section 1962d–17 of title 42. Each such loan shall be for such term, as the Secretary deems appropriate, but not in excess of—
(1)
10 years (in the case of a loan under section 2702 of this title) or
(2)
30 years (in the case of a loan under section 2703 of this title).
(b) Repayments
Amounts repaid on loans made pursuant to this chapter shall be deposited into the United States Treasury as miscellaneous receipts.
