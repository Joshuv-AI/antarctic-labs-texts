16 C.F.R. § 1309.1 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1309.1
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1309.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1309 (Ban Of Crib Bumpers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this rule is to prohibit the sale, offer for sale, 9manufacture for sale, distribution in commerce, or importation into the United States, of any crib bumpers, as defined in part 1309.2, as set forth in the Safety Sleep for Babies Act of 2021 (15 U.S.C. 2057e).
