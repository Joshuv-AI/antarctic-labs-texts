40 C.F.R. § 1516.8 — Will CEQ charge me a fee for a copy of my records?
Citation: us:cfr:40:1516.8
Creator: Council On Environmental Quality
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1516.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1516 (Privacy Act Implementation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you request a copy of CEQ's records about you, CEQ may charge you a fee of no more than 10 cents per page, which you must pay before CEQ provides you with a copy of your records.
