13 C.F.R. § 126.701 — Can the limitations on subcontracting requirements change?
Citation: us:cfr:13:126.701
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-126.701
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 126 (Hubzone Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. The Administrator may change the limitations on subcontracting requirements if the Administrator determines that such action is necessary to reflect conventional industry practices.
