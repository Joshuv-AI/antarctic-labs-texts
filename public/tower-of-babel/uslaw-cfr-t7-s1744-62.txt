7 C.F.R. § 1744.62 — Introduction.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1744 (Post-Loan Policies And Procedures Common To Guaranteed And Insured Telephone Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

RUS is under no obligation to make or approve advances of loan funds unless the borrower is in compliance with all terms and conditions of the loan documents. The borrower shall use funds in its construction fund only to make disbursements approved by RUS.
