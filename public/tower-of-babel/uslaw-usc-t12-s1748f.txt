12 U.S.C. § 1748f — Rules and regulations

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 13 (National Housing); Subchapter VIII (Armed Services Housing Mortgage Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized and directed to make such rules and regulations as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this subchapter.
