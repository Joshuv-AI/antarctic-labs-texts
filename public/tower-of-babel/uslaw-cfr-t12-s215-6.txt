12 C.F.R. § 215.6 — Prohibition on knowingly receiving unauthorized extension of credit.
Citation: us:cfr:12:215.6
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-215.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 215 (Loans To Executive Officers, Directors, And Principal Shareholders Of Member Banks (Regulation O)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No executive officer, director, or principal shareholder of a member bank or any of its affiliates shall knowingly receive (or knowingly permit any of that person's related interests to receive) from a member bank, directly or indirectly, any extension of credit not authorized under this part.
