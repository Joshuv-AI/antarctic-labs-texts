13 C.F.R. § 108.430 — Notification to SBA of transactions that may change ownership or Control.
Citation: us:cfr:13:108.430
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-108.430
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 108 (New Markets Venture Capital (“Nmvc”) Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must promptly notify SBA as soon as you have knowledge of transactions or events that may result in a transfer of Control or ownership of at least 10 percent of your capital. If there is any doubt as to whether a particular transaction or event will result in such a change, report the facts to SBA.
