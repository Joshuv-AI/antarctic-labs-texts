36 U.S.C. § 2107 — National Memorial Cemetery of the Pacific
Citation: us:usc:36:2107
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section2107
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (United States Government Organizations Involved With Observances And Ceremonies); Chapter 21 (American Battle Monuments Commission). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

With the consent of the Secretary of Veterans Affairs, the American Battle Monuments Commission may build works of architecture and art in the National Memorial Cemetery of the Pacific.
