39 C.F.R. § 956.11 — (Rule 11) Effect of Hearing Official's decision.
Citation: us:cfr:39:956.11
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-956.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 956 (Administrative Wage Garnishment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except for any issues reserved for the Department of the Treasury, the Hearing Official's decision shall be the final agency action for the purposes of judicial review under the Administrative Procedure Act (5 U.S.C 701 et. seq.).
