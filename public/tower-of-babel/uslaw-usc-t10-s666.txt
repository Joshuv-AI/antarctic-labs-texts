10 U.S.C. § 666 — Reserve officers not on the active-duty list
Citation: us:usc:10:666
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section666
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 38 (Joint Officer Management). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Defense shall establish personnel policies emphasizing education and experience in joint matters for reserve officers not on the active-duty list. Such policies shall, to the extent practicable for the reserve components, be similar to the policies provided by this chapter.
