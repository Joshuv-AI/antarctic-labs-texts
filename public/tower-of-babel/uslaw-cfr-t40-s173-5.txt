40 C.F.R. § 173.5 — Request for hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:40:173.5
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-173.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 173 (Procedures Governing The Rescission Of State Primary Enforcement Responsibility For Pesticide Use Violations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A State may request a hearing before a Presiding Officer not later than sixty (60) days after receipt of a notice of intent to rescind.
