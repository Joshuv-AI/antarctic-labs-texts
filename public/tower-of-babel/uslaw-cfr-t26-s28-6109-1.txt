26 C.F.R. § 28.6109-1 — -1 Tax return preparers furnishing identifying numbers for returns or claims for refund.

Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-28.6109-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 28 (Imposition Of Tax On Gifts And Bequests From Covered Expatriates). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In general. Each tax return or claim for refund of the section 2801 tax prepared by one or more signing tax return preparers must include the identifying number of the preparer required by § 1.6695-1(b) of this chapter to sign the return or claim for refund in the manner stated in § 1.6109-2 of this chapter.
(b) Applicability date. This section applies to returns and claims for refund filed on or after January 14, 2025.
