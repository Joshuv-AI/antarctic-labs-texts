Trends in the Prevalence of Hypercholesterolemia, 2015–2024 - KNHANES
Authors: Boram Kim, 보람 김
Published: 2026

The prevalence of hypercholesterolemia (among Korean adults aged ≥19 years, age-standardized) increased from 14.8% in 2015 to 23.6% in 2024, an increase of 8.8%p. Compared with 2023, it increased by 2.7%p (3.5%p among males and 2.0%p among females) (Figure 1). In 2024, the prevalence of hypercholesterolemia was highest among both males and females in their 60s (Figure 2).
