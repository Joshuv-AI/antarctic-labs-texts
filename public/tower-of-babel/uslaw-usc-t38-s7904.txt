38 U.S.C. § 7904 — Preferences in awarding financial assistance
Citation: us:usc:38:7904
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section7904
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part V (Boards, Administrations, And Services); Chapter 79 (Information Security Education Assistance Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In awarding financial assistance under this chapter, the Secretary shall give a preference to qualified individuals who are otherwise eligible to receive the financial assistance in the following order of priority:
(1)
Veterans with service-connected disabilities.
(2)
Veterans.
(3)
Persons described in section 4215(a)(1)(B) of this title.
(4)
Individuals who received or are pursuing degrees at institutions designated by the National Security Agency as Centers of Academic Excellence in Cyber Education.
(5)
Citizens of the United States.
