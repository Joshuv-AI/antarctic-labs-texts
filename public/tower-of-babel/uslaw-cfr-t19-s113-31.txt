19 C.F.R. § 113.31 — Same party as principal and surety; attorney in fact.
Citation: us:cfr:19:113.31
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-113.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 113 (Cbp Bonds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Same party as principal and surety. The same person, partnership, or corporation cannot be both principal and surety on a bond.
(b) Attorney in fact for principal or surety. In executing a bond, a person may act as:
(1) Attorney in fact for both principal and surety;
(2) Surety and attorney in fact for the principal; or
(3) Principal and attorney in fact for the surety.
