43 C.F.R. § 34.11 — Enforcement sanctions.
Citation: us:cfr:43:34.11
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-34.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 34 (Requirements For Equal Opportunity During Construction And Operation Of The Alaska Natural Gas Transportation System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of section 17, the President's Decision, and implementing rules, regulations, and orders, as appropriate, will be enforced through:
(a) The issuance of a compliance order by the Federal Inspector pursuant to section 11 of ANGTA; or
(b) The commencement of a civil action for appropriate relief, including a permanent or temporary injunction, or a civil penalty not to exceed $25,000 per day; or
(c) By any other means authorized by law.
