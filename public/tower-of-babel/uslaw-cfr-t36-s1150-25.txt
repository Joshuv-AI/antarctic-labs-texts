36 C.F.R. § 1150.25 — Date of service.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1150.25
Creator: Architectural And Transportation Barriers Compliance Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1150.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1150 (Practice And Procedures For Compliance Hearings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The date of service shall be the day when the matter is deposited in United States mail or is delivered in person, except that the date of service of the initial notice of hearing or opportunity for hearing shall be the date of its delivery, or the date that its attempted delivery is refused.
