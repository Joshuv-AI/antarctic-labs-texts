14 C.F.R. § 33.21 — Engine cooling.
Citation: us:cfr:14:33.21
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-33.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 33 (Airworthiness Standards: Aircraft Engines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Engine design and construction must provide the necessary cooling under conditions in which the airplane is expected to operate.
