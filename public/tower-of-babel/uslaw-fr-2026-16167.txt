    On August 3, 2026, pursuant to Rule 212 of the Commission's Rules 
of Practice and Procedure,\1\ Midcontinent Independent System Operator, 
Inc. (MISO) and the MISO Transmission Owners \2\ filed a motion to hold 
the show cause proceeding in Docket No. EL26-70-000 in abeyance for 90 
days. MISO and the MISO Transmission Owners request that the Commission 
shorten the answer period for the motion to five days, such that 
answers to the motion would be due on or by August 10, 2026.
