7 C.F.R. § 1c.122 — c.122 Use of Federal funds.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1c.122
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1c.122
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1c (PART 1c—PROTECTION OF HUMAN SUBJECTS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Federal funds administered by a Federal department or agency may not be expended for research involving human subjects unless the requirements of this policy have been satisfied.
