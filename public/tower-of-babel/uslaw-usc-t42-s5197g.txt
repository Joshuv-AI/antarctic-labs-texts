42 U.S.C. § 5197g — Federal Bureau of Investigation
Citation: us:usc:42:5197g
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section5197g
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 68 (Disaster Relief); Subchapter IV–B (Emergency Preparedness); Part B (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subchapter shall be construed to authorize investigations of espionage, sabotage, or subversive acts by any persons other than personnel of the Federal Bureau of Investigation.
