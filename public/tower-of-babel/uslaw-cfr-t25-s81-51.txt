25 C.F.R. § 81.51 — How do tribal members circulate a petition to adopt or amend the tribe's governing document?
Citation: us:cfr:25:81.51
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-81.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 81 (Secretarial Election Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Tribal members wishing to circulate a petition to adopt or amend the tribe's governing document may submit the proposed document to the Local Bureau Official for review and comment. The Local Bureau Official may help the petitioners in drafting governing documents, bylaws, charters, amendments and revocations. The Bureau may also explain the Secretarial election process.
