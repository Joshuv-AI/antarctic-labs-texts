15 U.S.C. § 9001 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:15:9001
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section9001
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 116 (Coronavirus Economic Stabilization (Cares Act)); Subchapter I (Keeping American Workers Paid And Employed). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this title— 1
(1)
the terms “Administration” and “Administrator” mean the Small Business Administration and the Administrator thereof, respectively; and
(2)
the term “small business concern” has the meaning given the term in section 632 of this title.
