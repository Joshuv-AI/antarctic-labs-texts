7 C.F.R. § 764.303 — Limitations.
Citation: us:cfr:7:764.303
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-764.303
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 764 (Direct Loan Making). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The applicant must comply with the general limitations established at § 764.102.
(b) The total principal balance owed by the applicant to the Agency on all Youth loans at any one time cannot exceed $10,000.
