7 U.S.C. § 6109 — Savings provision
Citation: us:usc:7:6109
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section6109
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 90 (Mushroom Promotion, Research, And Consumer Information). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter may be construed to preempt or supersede any other program relating to mushroom promotion, research, consumer information, or industry information organized and operated under the laws of the United States or any State.
