40 C.F.R. § 434.83 — Effluent limitations attainable by application of the best available technology economically achievable (BAT).
Citation: us:cfr:40:434.83
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-434.83
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 434 (Coal Mining Point Source Category Bpt, Bat, Bct Limitations And New Source Performance Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in 40 CFR 125.30 through 125.32, any existing western coal mining operation with drainage subject to this subpart must meet the effluent limitations in § 434.82.
