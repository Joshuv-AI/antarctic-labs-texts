36 C.F.R. § 67.11 — Fees for processing certification requests.
Citation: us:cfr:36:67.11
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-67.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 67 (Historic Preservation Certifications Under The Internal Revenue Code). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Fees are charged for reviewing certification requests according to the schedule and instructions provided in public notices in the Federal Register by NPS.
(b) No payment should be made until requested by the NPS. A certification decision will not be issued on an application until the appropriate remittance is received.
(c) Fees are nonrefundable.
