28 U.S.C. § 119 — Puerto Rico
Citation: us:usc:28:119
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section119
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part I (Organization Of Courts); Chapter 5 (District Courts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Puerto Rico constitutes one judicial district.
Court shall be held at Mayaguez, Ponce, and San Juan.
