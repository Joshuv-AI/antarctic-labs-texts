40 C.F.R. § 1700.11 — State petition for review of determinations or standards.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1700.11
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency And Department Of Defense; Uniform National Discharge Standards For Vessels Of The Armed Forces
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1700.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1700 (Uniform National Discharge Standards For Vessels Of The Armed Forces). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Governor of any State may submit a petition requesting that the Administrator and Secretary review a determination of whether a Marine Pollution Control Device is required for any discharge listed in § 1700.4 or § 1700.5, or review a Federal standard of performance for a Marine Pollution Control Device. A State may submit a petition only where there is new, significant information not considered previously by the Administrator and Secretary.
