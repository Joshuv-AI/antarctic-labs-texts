26 U.S.C. § 5612 — Forfeiture of taxpaid distilled spirits remaining on bonded premises
Citation: us:usc:26:5612
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section5612
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 51 (Distilled Spirits, Wines, And Beer); Subchapter J (Penalties, Seizures, And Forfeitures Relating To Liquors); Part I (Penalty, Seizure, And Forfeiture Provisions Applicable To Distilling, Rectifying, And Distilled And Rectified Products). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) General
No distilled spirits on which tax has been paid or determined shall be stored or allowed to remain on the bonded premises of any distilled spirits plant, under the penalty of forfeiture of all spirits so found.
(b) Exceptions
Subsection (a) shall not apply in the case of—
(1)
distilled spirits in the process of prompt removal from bonded premises on payment or determination of the tax; or
(2)
distilled spirits returned to bonded premises in accordance with the provisions of section 5215.
