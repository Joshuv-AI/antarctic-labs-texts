31 C.F.R. § 505.101 — Relation of this part to other laws and regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:31:505.101
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-505.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 505 (Sanctions Penalties Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth standard sanctions penalty regulations applicable to transactions regulated pursuant to other parts codified in this chapter, as well as to economic sanctions programs for which implementation and administration are delegated to OFAC. Substantive prohibitions, definitions, interpretations, and licenses particular to each economic sanctions program are not contained in this part but are set forth in the particular part of this chapter dedicated to that program and OFAC's website, https://ofac.treasury.gov, or, in the case of economic sanctions programs not yet implemented in regulations, in the applicable Executive order or other authority. Violations of certain economic sanctions programs are subject to additional penalties; information about these will be found in the particular part of this chapter dedicated to that program.
