30 C.F.R. § 820.2 — Objective.
Citation: us:cfr:30:820.2
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-820.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 820 (Special Permanent Program Performance Standards—Anthracite Mines In Pennsylvania). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part implements subsection 529(a) of the Act, which requires the Secretary to adopt special performance standards for anthracite mines regulated by special environmental protection performance standards of a State as of the date of enactment of the Act.
