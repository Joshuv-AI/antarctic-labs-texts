40 C.F.R. § 35.6020 — Requirements for both applicants and recipients.
Citation: us:cfr:40:35.6020
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-35.6020
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 35 (State And Local Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applicants and recipients must comply with the applicable requirements of 2 CFR part 1532, “Nonprocurement Debarment and Suspension and of 2 CFR part 1536, “Requirements for Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance).”
