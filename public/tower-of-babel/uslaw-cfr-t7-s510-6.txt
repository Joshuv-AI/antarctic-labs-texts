7 C.F.R. § 510.6 — Appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:7:510.6
Creator: Agricultural Research Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-510.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 510 (Public Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person whose request is denied shall have the right to appeal such denial. Appeals shall be made in accordance with § 1.14 of this title and should be addressed as follows: Administrator, ARS, U.S. Department of Agriculture, Washington, DC 20250.
