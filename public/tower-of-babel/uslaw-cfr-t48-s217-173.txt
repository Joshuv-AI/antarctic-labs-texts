48 C.F.R. § 217.173 — 217.173 Multiyear contracts for military family housing.
Citation: us:cfr:48:217.173
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-217.173
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 217 (Special Contracting Methods). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The head of the agency may enter into multiyear contracts for periods up to 4 years for supplies and services required for management, maintenance, and operation of military family housing and may pay the costs of such contracts for each year from annual appropriations for that year (10 U.S.C. 2829).
