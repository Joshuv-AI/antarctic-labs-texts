26 U.S.C. § 733 — Basis of distributee partner’s interest
Citation: us:usc:26:733
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section733
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 1 (Normal Taxes And Surtaxes); Subchapter K (Partners And Partnerships); Part II (Contributions, Distributions, And Transfers); Subpart B (Distributions By A Partnership). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the case of a distribution by a partnership to a partner other than in liquidation of a partner’s interest, the adjusted basis to such partner of his interest in the partnership shall be reduced (but not below zero) by—
(1)
the amount of any money distributed to such partner, and
(2)
the amount of the basis to such partner of distributed property other than money, as determined under section 732.
