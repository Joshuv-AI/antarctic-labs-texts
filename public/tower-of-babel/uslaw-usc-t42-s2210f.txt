42 U.S.C. § 2210f — Recruitment tools
Citation: us:usc:42:2210f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2210f
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 23 (Development And Control Of Atomic Energy); Subchapter XIII (General Authority Of Commission). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commission may purchase promotional items of nominal value for use in the recruitment of individuals for employment.
