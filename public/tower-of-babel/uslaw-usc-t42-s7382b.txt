42 U.S.C. § 7382b — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:42:7382b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section7382b
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 84 (Department Of Energy); Subchapter XIV (Albert Einstein Distinguished Educator Fellowships). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this subchapter—
(1)
the term “elementary school” has the meaning provided by section 7801 of title 20;
(2)
the term “local educational agency” has the meaning provided by section 7801 of title 20;
(3)
the term “secondary school” has the meaning provided by section 7801 of title 20;
(4)
the term “Secretary” means the Secretary of Energy.
