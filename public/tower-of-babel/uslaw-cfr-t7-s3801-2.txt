7 C.F.R. § 3801.2 — Public inspection, copying, and indexing.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3801.2
Creator: World Agricultural Outlook Board, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3801.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3801 (Availability Of Information To The Public). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

5 U.S.C. 552(a)(2) requires that certain materials be made available for public inspection and copying and that a current index of these materials be published quarterly or otherwise be made available. WAOB does not maintain any materials within the scope of these requirements.
