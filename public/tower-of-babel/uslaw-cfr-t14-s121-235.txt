14 C.F.R. § 121.235 — Fuel valves.
Citation: us:cfr:14:121.235
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-121.235
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 121 (Operating Requirements: Domestic, Flag, And Supplemental Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each fuel valve must—
(a) Comply with § 121.257;
(b) Have positive stops or suitable index provisions in the “on” and “off” positions; and
(c) Be supported so that loads resulting from its operation or from accelerated flight conditions are not transmitted to the lines connected to the valve.
