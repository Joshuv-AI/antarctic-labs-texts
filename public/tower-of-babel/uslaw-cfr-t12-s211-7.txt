12 C.F.R. § 211.7 — Voluntary liquidation of Edge and agreement corporations.

Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-211.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 211 (International Banking Operations (Regulation K)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Prior notice. An Edge or agreement corporation desiring voluntarily to discontinue normal business and dissolve, shall provide the Board with 45 days' prior written notice of its intent to do so.
(b) Waiver of notice period. The Board may waive the 45-day period if it finds that immediate action is required by the circumstances presented.
