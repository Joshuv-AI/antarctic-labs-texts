40 C.F.R. § 18.4 — Establishment of Environmental Protection Research Fellowships.
Citation: us:cfr:40:18.4
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-18.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 18 (Environmental Protection Research Fellowships And Special Research Consultants For Environmental Protection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All Environmental Protection Research fellowships shall be established by the Administrator or designee. In establishing an Environmental Protection Research fellowship, or a series of Environmental Protection Research fellowships, the Administrator shall prescribe in writing the conditions (in addition to those provided in the regulations in this part) under which Environmental Protection Research fellows will be appointed and will hold their fellowships.
