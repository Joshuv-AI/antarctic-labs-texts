15 C.F.R. § 930.111 — OCRM mediation.
Citation: us:cfr:15:930.111
Creator: National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-930.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 930 (Federal Consistency With Approved Coastal Management Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The availability of mediation does not preclude use by the parties of alternative means for resolving their disagreement. In the event a serious disagreement arises, the parties are strongly encouraged to make every effort to resolve the disagreement informally. OCRM shall be available to assist the parties in these efforts.
