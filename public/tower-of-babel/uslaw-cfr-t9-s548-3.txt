9 C.F.R. § 548.3 — Samples of products, water, dyes, chemicals, etc. to be taken for examination.
Citation: us:cfr:9:548.3
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-548.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 548 (Preparation Of Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Samples of products, water, dyes, chemicals, preservatives, spices, or other articles in any official establishment shall be taken, without cost to the Program, for examination, as often as may be deemed necessary for the efficient conduct of the inspection.
