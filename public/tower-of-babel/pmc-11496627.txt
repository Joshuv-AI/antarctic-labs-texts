Author Correction: Generalized roughness of three dimensional (∈, ∈ ∨ q) fuzzy ideals in terms of set‑valued homomorphism
Authors: Shahida Bashir, Rabia Mazhar, Nasreen Kausar, Saziye Yaman, Syed Suleman Ali, Muneeb Ul Hassan Afzal
Published: 2024

Correction to: Scientific Reports10.1038/s41598-024-62207-8, published online 29 May 2024

The original version of this Article contained errors.

In the Abstract,

“The objective of this study is to generalize the roughness of a fuzzy set-in three-dimensional structure by introducing ternary multiplication.”

now reads:

“The objective of this study is to generalize the roughness of a fuzzy set in three-dimensional structure by introducing ternary multiplication.”

Furthermore, the ‘Preliminaries’ section, contained errors.

Under the subheading ’Definition 2.5’.

“A fuzzy ideal η of R is called a semiprime if η(rrr)≥η(r) and prime if η(opq)=η(o) or η(opq)=η(p) or η(opq)=η(q) for all o,p,q,r∈R (2) η(opq)≥η(q)( resp. η(opq)≥η(o) , η(opq)≥η(p));”

now reads:

“A fuzzy ideal η of R is called a semiprime if η(rrr)≥η(r) and prime if η(opq)=η(o) or η(opq)=η(p) or η(opq)=η(q) for all o,p,q,r∈R”

Additionally, ‘Definition 2.7’:

“An (∈,∈∨q) - fuzzy left (resp. right and lateral), the ideal ηofR is defined as for all l,m,n∈R and r,t∈(0,1]"

now reads:

“An (∈,∈∨q) - fuzzy left (resp. right and lateral) the ideal ηofR is defined as for all l,m,n∈R and r,t∈(0,1]”

And, in Definition 2.16, a lower bar was typeset incorrectly. The definition now reads:

“For every x∈R, we define fuzzy subsets S(η)_(l)=∧n∈S(l)η(n) and S(η)¯(l)=∨n∈S(l)η(n).S(η)_ is the lower approximation and S(η)¯ is the upper approximation of η with respect to mapping S. Combination (S(η)_,S(η)¯) is called rough fuzzy subset of R if S(η)_=S(η)¯.

In addition, section ‘Approximations of fuzzy ideals in ternary semirings’ contained errors.

In Proof of ‘Theorem 3.4’,

“For case (i), see Theorem Mark3.”

now reads:

“For case (i), see Theorem 1.”

In Proof of Theorem 3.6,

“Proof is same as Theorem 3.5.”

now reads:

“Proof is same as Theorem 3.4.”

In Proof of Theorem 3.7,

“Because η is the fuzzy semiprime ideal of R, then η is the fuzzy ideal of R. Hence, by Theorem 3.5, S(η) is the fuzzy ideal of R.”

now reads:

“Because η is the fuzzy semiprime ideal of R, then η is the fuzzy ideal of R. Hence, by Theorem 3.4, S(η) is the fuzzy ideal of R.”

Finally, in Proof of Theorem 3.9,

“Because, η is a fuzzy ideal of R, therefore by Theorem 3.5, S(η) is the fuzzy ideal of R, we require for all l, m, n ∈ R”

now reads:

“Because, η is a fuzzy ideal of R, therefore by Theorem 3.4, S(η) is the fuzzy ideal of R, we require for all l, m, n ∈ R”

The original article has been corrected.
