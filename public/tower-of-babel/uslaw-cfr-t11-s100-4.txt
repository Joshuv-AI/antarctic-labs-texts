11 C.F.R. § 100.4 — Federal office (52 U.S.C. 30101(3)).
Citation: us:cfr:11:100.4
Creator: Federal Election Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-100.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 100 (Scope And Definitions (52 U.S.C. 30101)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Federal office means the office of President or Vice President of the United States, Senator or Representative in, or Delegate or Resident Commissioner to, the Congress of the United States.
