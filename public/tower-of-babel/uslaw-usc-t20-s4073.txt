20 U.S.C. § 4073 — Severability
Citation: us:usc:20:4073
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section4073
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 52 (Education For Economic Security); Subchapter VIII (Equal Access). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this subchapter or the application thereof to any person or circumstances is judicially determined to be invalid, the provisions of the remainder of the subchapter and the application to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
