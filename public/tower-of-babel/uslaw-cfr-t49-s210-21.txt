49 C.F.R. § 210.21 — Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:49:210.21
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-210.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 210 (Railroad Noise Emission Compliance Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes the compliance criteria concerning the requirements for inspection and testing of railroad equipment or operations covered by the Standards.
