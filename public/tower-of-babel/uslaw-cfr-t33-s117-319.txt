33 C.F.R. § 117.319 — Oklawaha River.
Citation: us:cfr:33:117.319
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-117.319
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 117 (Drawbridge Operation Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The draw of the Sharpes Ferry (SR 40) bridge, mile 55.1 shall open on signal if at least three hours notice is given.
(b) The draw of the Moss Bluff (SR 464) bridge, mile 66.0, need not open for the passage of vessels.
(c) The draw of the Muclan Farms bridge, mile 63.9, need not open for the passage of vessels.
