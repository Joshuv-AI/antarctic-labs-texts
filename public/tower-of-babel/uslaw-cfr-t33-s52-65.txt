33 C.F.R. § 52.65 — Orders.
Citation: us:cfr:33:52.65
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-52.65
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 52 (Board For Correction Of Military Records Of The Coast Guard). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Board shall issue such orders or directives as may be necessary to carry out a final action.
(b) The Board may ask the Coast Guard to submit a written report to the Board specifying the action taken and the date thereof with respect to any final action.
(c) Unless doing so is likely to nullify the relief granted, copies of the final decision shall be placed in the military record of the applicant.
