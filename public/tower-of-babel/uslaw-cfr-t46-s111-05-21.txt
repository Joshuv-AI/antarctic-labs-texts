46 C.F.R. § 111.05-21 — -21 Ground detection.
Citation: us:cfr:46:111.05-21
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-111.05-21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 111 (Electric Systems—General Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

There must be ground detection for each:
(a) Electric propulsion system;
(b) Ship's service power system;
(c) Lighting system; and
(d) Power or lighting distribution system that is isolated from the ship's service power and lighting system by transformers, motor generator sets, or other devices.
