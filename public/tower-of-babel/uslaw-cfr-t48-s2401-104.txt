48 C.F.R. § 2401.104 — 2401.104 Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2401.104
Creator: Chapter 24—Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2401.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2401 (Federal Acquisition Regulation System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All acquisition of personal property and non-personal services (including construction) by HUD, except as may be otherwise authorized by law, must be accomplished in accordance with the HUDAR and the FAR.
