7 U.S.C. § 1746 — Records and accounts
Citation: us:usc:7:1746
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1746
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 42 (Agricultural Commodity Set-Aside). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commodity Credit Corporation shall keep such records and accounts as may be necessary to show, for each commodity set-aside, the initial and current composition, value (in accordance with section 1742 of this title), current investment, quantity disposed of, method of disposition, and amounts received on disposition.
