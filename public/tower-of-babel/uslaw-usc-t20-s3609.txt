20 U.S.C. § 3609 — Retained rights
Citation: us:usc:20:3609
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section3609
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 49 (Asbestos School Hazard Detection And Control). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as otherwise provided in section 3607 of this title, nothing in this chapter shall—
(1)
affect the right of any party to seek legal redress in connection with the purchase or installation of asbestos materials in schools or any claim of disability or death related to exposure to asbestos in a school setting; or
(2)
affect the rights of any party under any other law.
