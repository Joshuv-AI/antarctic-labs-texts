15 U.S.C. § 1613 — Annual reports to Congress by Bureau
Citation: us:usc:15:1613
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1613
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 41 (Consumer Credit Protection); Subchapter I (Consumer Credit Cost Disclosure); Part A (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each year the Bureau shall make a report to the Congress concerning the administration of its functions under this subchapter, including such recommendations as the Bureau deems necessary or appropriate. In addition, each report of the Bureau shall include its assessment of the extent to which compliance with the requirements imposed under this subchapter is being achieved.
