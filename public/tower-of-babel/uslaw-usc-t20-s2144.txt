20 U.S.C. § 2144 — Authorization of appropriations

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 43 (American Folklife Preservation); Subchapter II (Veterans’ Oral History). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out this subchapter—
(1)
$250,000 for fiscal year 2001; and
(2)
such sums as may be necessary for each succeeding fiscal year.
