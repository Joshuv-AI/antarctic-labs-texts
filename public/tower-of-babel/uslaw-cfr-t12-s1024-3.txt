12 C.F.R. § 1024.3 — E-Sign applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1024.3
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1024.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1024 (Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act (Regulation X)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The disclosures required by this part may be provided in electronic form, subject to compliance with the consumer consent and other applicable provisions of the Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act (E-Sign Act) (15 U.S.C. 7001 et seq.).
