7 C.F.R. § 27.28 — Disposition of samples.
Citation: us:cfr:7:27.28
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-27.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 27 (Cotton Classification Under Cotton Futures Legislation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Samples submitted to a Marketing Services Office shall become the property of the Department and shall be disposed of in accordance with the property regulations of Department when no longer needed for classification or Micronaire determinations.
