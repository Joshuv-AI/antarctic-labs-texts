15 U.S.C. § 1358 — Consent of Congress for interstate compacts; reservation of right to alter, amend, or repeal
Citation: us:usc:15:1358
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1358
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 37 (State Technical Services). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The consent of the Congress is given to any two or more States to enter into agreement or compacts, not in conflict with any law of the United States, for cooperative efforts and mutual assistance and in designating agencies, under section 1357 of this title, for accomplishing the purposes of this chapter.
(b)
The right to alter, amend, or repeal this section, or consent granted by this section, is expressly reserved.
