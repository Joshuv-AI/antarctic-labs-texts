39 U.S.C. § 2402 — Annual report
Citation: us:usc:39:2402
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title39-section2402
Context: United States Code, Title 39 (Postal Service); Part III (Modernization And Fiscal Administration); Chapter 24 (Appropriations And Annual Report). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Postmaster General shall render an annual report to the Board concerning the operations of the Postal Service under this title. Upon approval thereof, or after making such changes as it considers appropriate, the Board shall transmit such reports to the President and the Congress.
