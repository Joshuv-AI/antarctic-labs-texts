43 C.F.R. § 3515.23 — May BLM require me to submit additional information?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3515.23
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3515.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3500 (Leasing Of Solid Minerals Other Than Coal And Oil Shale). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. You must be willing to provide geologic and economic data we need to determine the fair market value of your preference right or lease to be relinquished.
