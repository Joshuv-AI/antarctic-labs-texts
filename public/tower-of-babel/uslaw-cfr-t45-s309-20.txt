45 C.F.R. § 309.20 — Who submits a Tribal IV-D program application and where?
Citation: us:cfr:45:309.20
Creator: Office Of Child Support Services, Administration Of Families And Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-309.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 309 (Tribal Child Support Enforcement (Iv-D) Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The authorized representative of the Tribe or Tribal organization must sign and submit the Tribal IV-D program application.
(b) Applications must be submitted to the Office of Child Support Enforcement, Attention: Federal Office of Child Support Enforcement , with a copy to the appropriate regional office.
