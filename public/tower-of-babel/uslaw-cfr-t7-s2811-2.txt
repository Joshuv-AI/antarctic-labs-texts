7 C.F.R. § 2811.2 — Public inspection and copying.

Creator: Office Of Operations, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-2811.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 2811 (Availability Of Information To The Public). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Background. 5 U.S.C. 552(a)(2) requires that each agency maintain and make available for public inspection and copying certain kinds of records.
(b) Procedure. To gain access to OO records that are available for public inspection, contact the division that maintains them. See Appendix A, List of Addresses, for the location and hours of operation.
