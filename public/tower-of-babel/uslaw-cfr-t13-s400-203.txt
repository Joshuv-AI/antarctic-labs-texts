13 C.F.R. § 400.203 — Guarantee percentage.
Citation: us:cfr:13:400.203
Creator: Emergency Steel Guarantee Loan Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-400.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 400 (Emergency Steel Guarantee Loan Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A guarantee issued by the Board may not exceed 85 percent of the amount of the principal of a loan to a Qualified Steel Company. Subject to the provisions of this part, one or more third parties, public or private, may guarantee repayment of part of the Unguaranteed Portion of a loan guaranteed by the Board.
