31 C.F.R. § 549.509 — Authorization of emergency medical services.
Citation: us:cfr:31:549.509
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-549.509
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 549 (Lebanon Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provision of nonscheduled emergency medical services in the United States to persons whose property and interests in property are blocked pursuant to § 549.201(a) is authorized, provided that all receipt of payment for such services must be specifically licensed.
