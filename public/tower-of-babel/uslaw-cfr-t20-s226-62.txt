20 C.F.R. § 226.62 — Computing average monthly compensation.
Citation: us:cfr:20:226.62
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-226.62
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 226 (Computing Employee, Spouse, And Divorced Spouse Annuities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The employee's average monthly compensation is computed by first determining the employee's highest 60 months of railroad compensation (disregarding compensation in excess of the maximum creditable tier II compensation for that year). The total of the highest 60 months is then divided by 60 to determine the average monthly compensation.
