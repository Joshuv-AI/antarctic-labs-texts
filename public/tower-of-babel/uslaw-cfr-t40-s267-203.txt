40 C.F.R. § 267.203 — What special requirements must I meet for incompatible wastes?
Citation: us:cfr:40:267.203
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-267.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 267 (Standards For Owners And Operators Of Hazardous Waste Facilities Operating Under A Standardized Permit). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You may not place incompatible wastes, or incompatible wastes and materials, in the same tank system, unless you comply with § 267.17(b).
(b) You may not place hazardous waste in a tank system that has not been decontaminated and that previously held an incompatible waste or material, unless you comply with § 267.17(b).
