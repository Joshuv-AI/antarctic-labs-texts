40 C.F.R. § 59.600 — Does this subpart apply for my products?
Citation: us:cfr:40:59.600
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-59.600
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 59 (National Volatile Organic Compound Emission Standards For Consumer And Commercial Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except as provided in § 59.605 and paragraph (b) of this section, the regulations in this subpart F apply for all portable fuel containers (defined in § 59.680) that are manufactured on or after January 1, 2009.
(b) See § 59.602 (a) and (b) to determine how to apply the provisions of this subpart for containers that were manufactured before January 1, 2009.
