42 U.S.C. § 4020 — Dissemination of flood insurance information
Citation: us:usc:42:4020
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section4020
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 50 (National Flood Insurance); Subchapter I (The National Flood Insurance Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator shall from time to time take such action as may be necessary in order to make information and data available to the public, and to any State or local agency or official, with regard to—
(1)
the flood insurance program, its coverage and objectives, and
(2)
estimated and chargeable flood insurance premium rates, including the basis for and differences between such rates in accordance with the provisions of section 4015 of this title.
