5 C.F.R. § 214.203 — Reporting requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:5:214.203
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-214.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 214 (Senior Executive Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies shall report such information as may be requested by OPM relating to positions and employees in the Senior Executive Service.
