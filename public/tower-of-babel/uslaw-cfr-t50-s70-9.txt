50 C.F.R. § 70.9 — Wildlife species management.
Citation: us:cfr:50:70.9
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-70.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 70 (National Fish Hatcheries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The wildlife species management provisions set forth in part 31 of this chapter are equally applicable to national fish hatchery areas, except § 31.15 relating to hunting and fishing, which are treated separately in this part.
