30 C.F.R. § 44.50 — Effect of appeal on initial decision.
Citation: us:cfr:30:44.50
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-44.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 44 (Rules Of Practice For Petitions For Modification Of Mandatory Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in § 44.14(c), a proposed decision and order of an Administrator is not operative pending appeal to an administrative law judge, and a decision of an administrative law judge is not operative pending appeal to the Assistant Secretary.
