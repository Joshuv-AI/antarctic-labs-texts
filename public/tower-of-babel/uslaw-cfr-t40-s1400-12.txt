40 C.F.R. § 1400.12 — Qualified researchers.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1400.12
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency And Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1400.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1400 (Distribution Of Off-Site Consequence Analysis Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator is authorized to provide OCA information, including facility identification, to qualified researchers pursuant to a system developed and implemented under 42 U.S.C. 7412(r)(7)(H)(vii), in consultation with the Attorney General.
