46 C.F.R. § 113.35-1 — -1 Definitions.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 113 (Communication And Alarm Systems And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in this subpart:
(a) Indicator means an instrument in the engine room to receive and acknowledge engine orders; and
(b) Transmitter means an instrument to send engine orders to the engineroom and receive acknowledgement from the engineroom.
