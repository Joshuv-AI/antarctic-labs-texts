Modular forms for GL ⁡ ( r , 𝔽 q ​ [ T ] ) \GL(r,\mathds{F}_{q}[T]) : Hecke operators and growth of expansion coefficients
Ernst-Ulrich Gekeler Universität des Saarlandes gekeler@math.uni-sb.de

Abstract
. We determine the action of the Hecke operators T 𝔭 , i T_{\mathfrak{p},i} on the coefficient forms g 1 , … , g r − 1 , g r = Δ g_{1},\dots,g_{r-1},g_{r}=\Delta , and h h , which together generate the ring of modular forms for GL ⁡ ( r , 𝔽 q ​ [ T ] ) \GL(r,\mathds{F}_{q}[T]) . All these are eigenforms with powers of π \pi as eigenvalues, where π \pi is the monic generator of the prime ideal 𝔭 \mathfrak{p} of 𝔽 q ​ [ T ] \mathds{F}_{q}[T] . We further describe the growth of the t t -expansion coefficients of the discriminant function Δ \Delta . It is such that the product expansion of Δ \Delta as well as the t t -expansion of each modular form converges on the natural fundamental domain for GL ⁡ ( r , 𝔽 q ​ [ T ] ) \GL(r,\mathds{F}_{q}[T]) .

6.1.

Let

\displaystyle\Delta(\boldsymbol{\omega})

\displaystyle={-}(\Delta^{\prime}(\boldsymbol{\omega}^{\prime}))^{q}t^{q-1}(\boldsymbol{\omega})P(t(\boldsymbol{\omega}))^{(q^{r}-1)(q-1)}

\displaystyle=(h^{\prime}(\boldsymbol{\omega}^{\prime}))^{q}t(\boldsymbol{\omega})P(t(\boldsymbol{\omega}))^{q^{r}-1}.

The

6.2.

We first study

\mathbf{F}
. As it vanishes nowhere, its logarithm

\mathbf{F}
(see (
1.2.5
)). Here
\mathbf{k}\in\mathds{N}_{0}^{r}
is a fundamental index,
which means
k_{1}\geq k_{2}\geq\cdots\geq k_{r}=0
. Throughout,
r\geq 2
is fixed. As usual,

\boldsymbol{\omega}=(\omega_{1},\boldsymbol{\omega}^{\prime})
with
\boldsymbol{\omega}^{\prime}\in\mathbf{F}^{\prime}
and
\mathbf{k}=(k_{1},\mathbf{k}^{\prime})

For

t(\boldsymbol{\omega})=(e^{\Lambda_{\boldsymbol{\omega}^{\prime}}}(\omega_{1}))^{-1}=\Big[\omega_{1}\prod_{\mathbf{a}\in A^{r-1}}\Big(1-\frac{\omega_{1}}{\mathbf{a}\boldsymbol{\omega}^{\prime}}\Big)\Big]^{-1},

\mathbf{a}\boldsymbol{\omega}^{\prime}=\sum_{2\leq i\leq r}a_{i}\omega_{i}

The absolute value of the factor

(6.2.2)

\displaystyle\left\lvert 1-\frac{\omega_{1}}{\mathbf{a}\boldsymbol{\omega}^{\prime}}\right\rvert

\displaystyle=1\quad\text{if}\quad\lvert\mathbf{a}\boldsymbol{\omega}^{\prime}\rvert\geq\lvert\omega_{1}\rvert

\displaystyle=\left\lvert\frac{\omega_{1}}{\mathbf{a}\boldsymbol{\omega}^{\prime}}\right\rvert,\quad\text{if}\quad\lvert\mathbf{a}\boldsymbol{\omega}^{\prime}\rvert\leq\lvert\omega_{1}\rvert

(if

).

\displaystyle\lvert t(\boldsymbol{\omega})\rvert

\displaystyle=\lvert\omega_{1}\rvert^{-1}\sideset{}{{}^{\prime}}{\prod}_{\begin{subarray}{c}\mathbf{a}\in A^{r-1}\\
\lvert\mathbf{a}\boldsymbol{\omega}^{\prime}\rvert\leq\lvert\omega_{1}\rvert\end{subarray}}\left\lvert\frac{\mathbf{a}\boldsymbol{\omega}^{\prime}}{\omega_{1}}\right\rvert

\displaystyle\lvert t(\boldsymbol{\omega})\rvert

\displaystyle\leq 1\text{ on }\mathbf{F},\qquad\text{where }\lvert t(\boldsymbol{\omega})\rvert=1\Longleftrightarrow\boldsymbol{\omega}\in\mathbf{F}_{\mathbf{0}},\mathbf{0}=(0,\dots,0).

6.3.

Next we investigate the

The division function is defined by

\lvert d_{\mathbf{u}}(\boldsymbol{\omega})\rvert=\lvert\mathbf{u}\boldsymbol{\omega}\rvert\sideset{}{{}^{\prime}}{\prod}_{\begin{subarray}{c}\mathbf{a}\in A^{r}\\

as in (

6.5.

We are interested in the sizes of coefficients of the polynomial

Next we choose an element

of

\displaystyle d_{\mathbf{u}}^{*}=\gamma^{-1}d_{\mathbf{u}},t^{*}=\gamma t,p_{k}^{*}=\gamma^{{-}k}p_{k},S_{n}^{*}(X)=\prod(1-d_{\mathbf{u}}^{*}X),

6.10.4.

Any representation of

We thus get the following result.

Theorem 6.11

Let
\mathbf{k}=(k_{1},\dots,k_{r})\in\mathds{N}_{0}^{r}

Remarks 6.13
:

(i)

Arguing as in

(ii)

Thanks to the Cauchy integral formula, a complex power series has always the largest possible radius of convergence. In our
situation, the discriminant function (or its root

6.14.

We conclude with analogous but more simple observations on the convergence of the formula
(

;
