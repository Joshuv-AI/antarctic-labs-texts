26 U.S.C. § 8005 — Payment of expenses
Citation: us:usc:26:8005
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section8005
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 91 (Organization And Membership Of The Joint Committee). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The expenses of the Joint Committee shall be paid one-half from the contingent fund of the Senate and one-half from the contingent fund of the House of Representatives, upon vouchers signed by the chairman or the vice chairman.
