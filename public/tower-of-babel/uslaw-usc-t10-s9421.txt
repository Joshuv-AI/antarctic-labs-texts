10 U.S.C. § 9421 — Space Force officer training course requirements
Citation: us:usc:10:9421
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9421
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training); Chapter 951 (Training Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Air Force shall ensure that—
(1)
the initial Space Force officer training course provides foundational instruction in acquisition management, space mission integration, and related disciplines; and
(2)
the curriculum for such course is developed by the Commander of Space Training and Readiness Command, in coordination with the Assistant Secretary of the Air Force for Space Acquisition and Integration and the Director of Acquisition Career Management.
