10 U.S.C. § 8875 — Commissions of auctioneers
Citation: us:usc:10:8875
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8875
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 883 (Prize). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Secretary of the Navy may establish a scale of commissions to be paid to auctioneers employed to make sales of prize property. These commissions are in full satisfaction of expenses as well as services. The scale may in no case allow a commission in excess of—
(1)
½ of 1 percent on any amount exceeding $10,000 on the sale of a vessel; and
(2)
1 percent on any amount exceeding $10,000 on the sale of other prize property.
(b)
If no such scale is established, auctioneers in prize causes shall be paid such compensation as the court considers just under the circumstances of each case.
