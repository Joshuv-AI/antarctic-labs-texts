34 C.F.R. § 463.110 — What are the program-specific requirements in the Unified State Plan for the adult, dislocated worker, and youth programs authorized under Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act title I?
Citation: us:cfr:34:463.110
Creator: Office Of Career, Technical, And Adult Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-463.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 463 (Adult Education And Family Literacy Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The program-specific requirements for the adult, dislocated worker, and youth programs that must be included in the Unified State Plan are described in sec. 102(b)(2)(D) of WIOA. Additional planning requirements may be explained in joint planning guidelines issued by the Secretaries of Labor and Education.
