26 U.S.C. § 5821 — Making tax
Citation: us:usc:26:5821
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section5821
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 53 (Machine Guns, Destructive Devices, And Certain Other Firearms); Subchapter A (Taxes); Part III (Tax On Making Firearms). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Rate
There shall be levied, collected, and paid upon the making of a firearm a tax at the rate of—
(1)
$200 for each firearm made in the case of a machinegun or a destructive device, and
(2)
$0 for any firearm made which is not described in paragraph (1).
(b) By whom paid
The tax imposed by subsection (a) of this section shall be paid by the person making the firearm.
(c) Payment
The tax imposed by subsection (a) of this section shall be payable by the stamp prescribed for payment by the Secretary.
