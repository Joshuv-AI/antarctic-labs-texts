41 C.F.R. § 304-6.4 — -6.4 Due dates for the OGE Form 1353 or SF 326
Citation: us:cfr:41:304-6.4
Creator: Chapter 304—Payment Of Travel Expenses From A Non-Federal Source
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-304-6.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 304-6 (6—Payment Guidelines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Agencies must—
(1) Submit the completed report no later than May 31 for payments received from the preceding October 1 through March 31; and
(2) Submit the completed report no later than November 30 for payments received from the preceding April 1 through September 30; and
(b) Submit the completed report including negative reports, to: Director of the Office of Government Ethics (OGE), 1201 New York Avenue NW, Suite 500, Washington, DC 20005-3917 or at 1353Travel@oge.gov.
