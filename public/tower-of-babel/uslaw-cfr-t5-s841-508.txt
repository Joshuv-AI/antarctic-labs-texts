5 C.F.R. § 841.508 — Effective date.
Citation: us:cfr:5:841.508
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-841.508
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 841 (Federal Employees Retirement System—General Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The employee deductions specified in § 841.503 are effective on the later of the first day of the first pay period beginning in 1987 or the first day an employee is covered by FERS.
