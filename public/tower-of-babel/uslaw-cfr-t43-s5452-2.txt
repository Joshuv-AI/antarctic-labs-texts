43 C.F.R. § 5452.2 — Installment payments.
Citation: us:cfr:43:5452.2
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-5452.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 5450 (Award Of Contract). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For sales of $500 or more the authorized officer may allow payment by installments as provided by § 5461.2 of this chapter.
