21 C.F.R. § 123.5 — Current good manufacturing practice.
Citation: us:cfr:21:123.5
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-123.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 123 (Fish And Fishery Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except as provided by § 117.5(b), parts 110 and 117 of this chapter apply in determining whether the facilities, methods, practices, and controls used to process fish and fishery products are safe, and whether these products have been processed under sanitary conditions.
(b) The purpose of this part is to set forth requirements specific to the processing of fish and fishery products.
