43 C.F.R. § 4300.60 — What must be included in my assignment document?
Citation: us:cfr:43:4300.60
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4300.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4300 (Grazing Administration; Alaska; Reindeer; General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Assignments must contain:
(a) All terms and conditions agreed to by the parties;
(b) A showing under §§ 4300.11 and 4300.12 that the assignee is qualified to hold a permit;
(c) A showing under § 4300.21(a) regarding a reindeer allotment; and
(d) The assignee's statement agreeing to be bound by the provisions of the permit.
