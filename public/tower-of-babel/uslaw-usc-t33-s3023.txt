33 U.S.C. § 3023 — Separation of ensigns found not fully qualified
Citation: us:usc:33:3023
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section3023
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 43 (National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration Commissioned Officer Corps); Subchapter II (Appointment And Promotion Of Officers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If an officer in the grade of ensign is at any time found not fully qualified, the officer shall be separated from the commissioned service.
