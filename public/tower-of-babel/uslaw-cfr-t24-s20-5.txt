24 C.F.R. § 20.5 — Jurisdiction of Office of Hearings and Appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:24:20.5
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-20.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 20 (Office Of Hearings And Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Office of Hearings and Appeals shall, consistent with statute and regulation, have jurisdiction over matters assigned to it by the Secretary or designee. Determinations shall have the finality provided by the applicable statute, regulation, or agreement.
