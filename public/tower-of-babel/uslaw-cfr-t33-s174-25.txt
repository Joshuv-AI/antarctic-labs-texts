33 C.F.R. § 174.25 — Size of certificate of number.
Citation: us:cfr:33:174.25
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-174.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 174 (State Numbering And Casualty Reporting Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each certificate of number must be in hard copy or digital form. Any certificate issued in hard copy under this section must be pocketsized.
