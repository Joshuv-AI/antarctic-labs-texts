46 U.S.C. § 3307 — Frequency of inspection
Citation: us:usc:46:3307
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section3307
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part B (Inspection And Regulation Of Vessels); Chapter 33 (Inspection Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each vessel subject to inspection under this part shall undergo an initial inspection for certification before being put into service. After being put into service—
(1)
each passenger vessel, nautical school vessel, and small passenger vessel allowed to carry more than 12 passengers on a foreign voyage shall be inspected at least once a year; and
(2)
any other vessel shall be inspected at least once every 5 years.
