Abstract
. We consider the Calkin algebra 𝒬 ⁡ ( ℓ 2 ) \mathcal{Q}(\ell_{2}) , i.e., the quotient of the algebra ℬ ⁡ ( ℓ 2 ) \mathcal{B}(\ell_{2}) of all bounded linear operators on the separable Hilbert space ℓ 2 \ell_{2} divided by the ideal 𝒦 ⁡ ( ℓ 2 ) \mathcal{K}(\ell_{2}) of all compact operators on ℓ 2 \ell_{2} . We show that in the Cohen model of set theory ZFC there is no embedding of the product ( c 0 ​ ( 2 ω ) ) ℕ (c_{0}(2^{\omega}))^{\mathbb{N}} of infinitely many copies of the abelian C*-algebra c 0 ​ ( 2 ω ) c_{0}(2^{\omega}) into 𝒬 ⁡ ( ℓ 2 ) \mathcal{Q}(\ell_{2}) (while c 0 ​ ( 2 ω ) c_{0}(2^{\omega}) always embeds into 𝒬 ⁡ ( ℓ 2 ) \mathcal{Q}(\ell_{2}) ). This enlarges the collection of the known examples due to Vaccaro and to McKenney and Vignati of abelian algebras, asymptotic sequence algebras, reduced products and coronas of stabilizations which consistently do not embed into the Calkin algebra. As in the Cohen model the rigidity of quotient structures fails in general, our methods do not rely on these rigidity phenomena as is the case of most examples mentioned above. The results should be considered in the context of the result of Farah, Hirshberg and Vignati which says that consistently all C*-algebras of density up to 2 ω 2^{\omega} do embed into 𝒬 ⁡ ( ℓ 2 ) \mathcal{Q}(\ell_{2}) . In particular, the algebra ( c 0 ​ ( 2 ω ) ) ℕ (c_{0}(2^{\omega}))^{\mathbb{N}} consistently embeds into the Calkin algebra as well.

Theorem 27
.

In the Cohen model there is no embedding of
(c_{0}(2^{\omega}))^{\mathbb{N}}
into
\mathcal{Q}(\ell_{2})
.

Proof.

T\colon(c_{0}(\omega_{2}))^{\mathbb{N}}\rightarrow\mathcal{Q}(\ell_{2})

is an embedding. Let
\chi_{n,\alpha},\chi_{\gamma}\in(c_{0}(\omega_{2}))^{\mathbb{N}}
for

be given by

\chi_{n,\alpha}(m)(\beta)=\begin{cases}&1,\ \text{if}\ (n,\alpha)=(m,\beta)\\
&0,\ \text{otherwise}\end{cases}

and

\chi_{\gamma}(m)(\beta)=\begin{cases}&1,\ \text{if}\ \gamma(m)=\beta\\
&0,\ \text{otherwise}\end{cases}

Clearly
\chi_{n,\alpha},\chi_{\gamma}
are projections and if
\gamma(n)=\alpha
, then
\chi_{n,\alpha}\leq\chi_{\gamma}
.

Since embeddings preserve posets of projections,

and

are projections
in
\mathcal{Q}(\ell_{2})
for

. By

there are projections

in
\mathcal{B}(\ell_{2})

such that
\pi(E_{n,\alpha})=T(\chi_{n,\alpha}),\pi(B_{\gamma})=T(\chi_{\gamma})

(here

so there are disjoint

4.
Reduced products and coronas of stabilizations

Now we will show an application of Theorem

Theorem 28
.

In the Cohen model, if for every
n\in\mathbb{N}
a C*-algebra
\mathcal{A}_{n}
admits a
pairwise orthogonal family of projections of cardinality
2^{\omega}
and
\mathcal{I}

\mathbb{N}

\prod_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\mathcal{A}_{n}/\bigoplus_{\mathcal{I}}\mathcal{A}_{n}

does not embed into
\mathcal{Q}(\ell_{2})
.

Proof.

By Theorem
27
it is enough
to construct an embedding of
(c_{0}(2^{\omega}))^{\mathbb{N}}
into

\prod_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\mathcal{A}_{n}/\bigoplus_{\mathcal{I}}\mathcal{A}_{n}
.
Since
\wp(\mathbb{N})/\mathcal{I}
is infinite, there is a pairwise disjoint family
(B_{m})_{m\in\mathbb{N}}

of subsets of
\mathbb{N}
such that
B_{m}\notin\mathcal{I}
for every
m\in\mathbb{N}
.

For each
n\in\mathbb{N}
let
\{p_{n,\alpha}\}_{\alpha<2^{\omega}}
be a family
of pairwise orthogonal projections in
\mathcal{A}_{n}
. Then
T_{n}\colon c_{0}(2^{\omega})\to\mathcal{A}_{n}

given by
T_{n}((x_{\alpha})_{\alpha<2^{\omega}})=\sum_{\alpha<2^{\omega}}x_{\alpha}p_{k,\alpha}

is an embedding, and so is
T\colon(c_{0}(2^{\omega}))^{\mathbb{N}}\to\prod_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\mathcal{A}_{n}

defined by
T((s_{n})_{n\in\mathbb{N}})=(T_{n}(s_{k(n)}))_{n\in\mathbb{N}}
where
k(n)=m
whenever
n\in B_{m}
.

We will show that
\pi\circ T\colon(c_{0}(2^{\omega}))^{\mathbb{N}}\to\prod_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\mathcal{A}_{n}/\bigoplus_{\mathcal{I}}\mathcal{A}_{n}

also is an embedding, where
\pi\colon\prod_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\mathcal{A}_{n}\to\prod_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\mathcal{A}_{n}/\bigoplus_{\mathcal{I}}\mathcal{A}_{n}

denotes the canonical quotient homomorphism. Suppose it is not.
Then there is a non-zero sequence
(s_{n})_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\in\prod_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\mathcal{A}_{n}
,
s_{n}\in c_{0}(2^{\omega})

such that
\pi\circ T((s_{n})_{n\in\mathbb{N}})=0
i.e.
T((s_{n})_{n\in\mathbb{N}})\in\bigoplus_{\mathcal{I}}\mathcal{A}_{n}
.
But this is impossible, because if
s_{m}\neq 0
, then the sequence
T((s_{n})_{n\in\mathbb{N}})
is
constant and non-zero on indices in
B_{m}\notin\mathcal{I}
.
∎

Taking
\mathcal{I}=\{\{0\}\}
we obtain

Corollary 29
.

In the Cohen model, if for every
n\in\mathbb{N}
a C*-algebra
\mathcal{A}_{n}

admits a pairwise orthogonal family of projections of cardinality

Another consequence of Theorem
28
is the impossibility of
embedding the coronas of the stabilizations of C*-algebras with big families of
pairwise orthogonal projections. For that we will need the following lemma.

Lemma 30
.

For any C*-algebra
\mathcal{A}
there is an embedding of
\mathcal{A}^{\mathbb{N}}/\bigoplus_{Fin}\mathcal{A}
into
\mathcal{Q}(\mathcal{A}\otimes\mathcal{K}(\ell_{2}))

Proof.

First, let us define an embedding
I\colon\bigoplus_{Fin}\mathcal{A}\to\mathcal{A}\otimes\mathcal{K}(\ell_{2})
:

I((a_{n})_{n\in\mathbb{N}}))=\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}}a_{n}\otimes e_{n,n}

where
a_{n}\in\mathcal{A}
and
e_{n,m}\in\mathcal{K}(\ell_{2})
is given by

e_{n,m}((c_{k})_{k\in\mathbb{N}})(l)=\begin{cases}c_{m},&\ \text{if}\ l=n\\
0,&\ \text{otherwise}\end{cases}

for
n,m,l\in\mathbb{N}
and
(c_{n})_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\in\ell_{2}
i.e.
e_{n,m}
is the matrix with entry
1
at position
(n,m)
and
0
at other positions (so called matrix unit).

Now fix
A\in\mathcal{A}^{\mathbb{N}},A=(a_{n})_{n\in\mathbb{N}}
. Consider operators
L_{A},R_{A}\colon\mathcal{A}\otimes\mathcal{K}(\ell_{2})\to\mathcal{A}\otimes\mathcal{K}(\ell_{2})
defined by

\displaystyle L_{A}(x\otimes e_{n,m})=a_{n}x\otimes e_{n,m}

\displaystyle R_{A}(x\otimes e_{n,m})=xa_{m}\otimes e_{n,m}

i.e.
L_{A}
and
R_{A}
are multiplications by diagonal matrices (from the left and right side respectively) with entries
(a_{n})_{n\in\mathbb{N}}
on the diagonal.

It is easy to check that
(L_{A},R_{A})
is a double centralizer of
\mathcal{A}\otimes\mathcal{K}(\ell_{2})
and
I_{\infty}\colon\mathcal{A}^{\mathbb{N}}\to\mathcal{M}(\mathcal{A}\otimes\mathcal{K}(\ell_{2}))
given by
I_{\infty}(A)=(L_{A},R_{A})
is an embedding.
Moreover,
I_{\infty}(A)\in\mathcal{A}\otimes\mathcal{K}(\ell_{2})
if and only if

A\in\bigoplus_{Fin}\mathcal{A}
. Thus,

J\colon\mathcal{A}^{\mathbb{N}}/\bigoplus_{Fin}\mathcal{A}\to\mathcal{Q}(\mathcal{A}\otimes\mathcal{K}(\ell_{2}))

given by
J([A])=[I_{\infty}(A)]
is well-defined and is an embedding.
∎
