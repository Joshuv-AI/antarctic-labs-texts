Restricted Overpartitions and concave compositions: their modularity and asymptotics
Koustav Banerjee, Kathrin Bringmann, Atul Dixit

Abstract
. In this paper we study restricted overpartitions and concave compositions. Using q q -series transformations, we show that their generating functions are related to modular forms, mock theta functions, false theta functions, and mock Maass theta functions. Moreover, we obtain their asymptotic main terms. We also study related rank statistics.

2.3.
The Euler–Maclaurin summation formula

We use a variation of the Euler–Maclaurin summation formula, an important tool for computing asymptotic expansions of infinite sums, studied by Zagier
[
36
, Proposition 3]
for one-dimensional smooth functions and extended by Jennings-Shaffer, Mahlburg, and the second author
[
19
, Theorem 1.2]
for multi-dimensional complex-valued functions. A function
g
, defined over an unbounded domain
D\subset\mathbb{C}
, is of
sufficient decay
if there exists
\varepsilon>0
such that
g(w)\ll|w|^{-1-\varepsilon}
uniformly as
|w|\to\infty
in
D
. For
0\leq\theta<\frac{\pi}{2}
, we define

D_{\theta}:=\{re^{i\alpha}:r\geq 0\ \text{and}\ |\alpha|\leq\theta\}
.

Proposition 2.17
.

Let
0\leq\theta<\frac{\pi}{2}
and
g
be a holomorphic function in a domain containing
D_{\theta}
. Also assume that
g
and all of its derivatives are of sufficient decay. Then, for
a\in\mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}
,
N\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
, and as
z\to 0
uniformly in
D_{\theta}
, we have

\sum_{m\geq 0}g\left((m+a)z\right)=\frac{1}{z}\int_{0}^{\infty}g(w)dw-\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}\frac{B_{n+1}(a)g^{(n)}(0)}{(n+1)!}z^{n}+O\left(z^{N}\right),

where
B_{n}(x)
denotes the
n
-th Bernoulli polynomial.

Next, we consider two-dimensional sums. Throughout we write
\bm{x}=\left(x_{1},x_{2}\right)\in\mathbb{R}^{2}
. A function
f
defined on an unbounded domain
D\subset\mathbb{C}^{2}
is of
sufficient decay
if there exist
\varepsilon_{1},\varepsilon_{2}>0
such that
f(\bm{x})\ll|w_{1}|^{-1-\varepsilon_{1}}|w_{2}|^{-1-\varepsilon_{2}}
uniformly as
|w_{1}|+|w_{2}|\to\infty
in
D
. From
[
19
, Proposition 5.2]
(with
r=2
), we have the following.

Proposition 2.18
.

Let
0\leq\theta<\frac{\pi}{2}
and
f
be a holomorphic function in a domain containing
D_{\theta}\times D_{\theta}
. Also assume that
f
and all of its derivatives are of sufficient decay. Then for
\bm{a}\in\mathbb{R}^{2}_{\geq 0}
,
N\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
, and as

uniformly in
D_{\theta}
, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{m\geq 0}f\left(\left(\bm{m}+\bm{a}\right)z\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{z^{2}}\int_{0}^{\infty}\int_{0}^{\infty}f(\bm{x})dx_{1}dx_{2}-\frac{1}{z}\sum_{n_{1}=0}^{N}\frac{B_{n_{1}+1}\left(a_{1}\right)z^{n_{1}}}{\left(n_{1}+1\right)!}\int_{0}^{\infty}f^{\left(n_{1},0\right)}\left(0,x_{2}\right)dx_{2}

\displaystyle\hskip 28.45274pt-\frac{1}{z}\sum_{n_{2}=0}^{N}\frac{B_{n_{2}+1}\left(a_{2}\right)z^{n_{2}}}{\left(n_{2}+1\right)!}\int_{0}^{\infty}f^{\left(0,n_{2}\right)}\left(x_{1},0\right)dx_{1}

\displaystyle\hskip 28.45274pt+\sum_{n_{1}+n_{2}<N}\frac{B_{n_{1}+1}\left(a_{1}\right)B_{n_{2}+1}\left(a_{2}\right)f^{\left(n_{1},n_{2}\right)}\left(\bm{0}\right)}{\left(n_{1}+1\right)!\left(n_{2}+1\right)!}+O\left(z^{N}\right).
