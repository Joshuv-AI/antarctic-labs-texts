42 C.F.R. § 84.178 — Breath response type, airflow resistance test—PAPR classes HE and PAPR100.
Citation: us:cfr:42:84.178
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-84.178
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 84 (Approval Of Respiratory Protective Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Resistance to airflow will be measured with a breathing machine as described in § 84.88.
(a) Minimum inhalation resistance must be greater than zero mm of water-column height.
(b) Maximum exhalation resistance must be less than 89 mm of water-column height.
