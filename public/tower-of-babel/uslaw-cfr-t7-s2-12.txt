7 C.F.R. § 2.12 — Secretary and general officers not precluded from exercising delegated powers.
Citation: us:cfr:7:2.12
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-2.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 2 (Delegations Of Authority By The Secretary Of Agriculture And General Officers Of The Department). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No delegation of authority by the Secretary or a general officer contained in this part shall preclude the Secretary or general officer from exercising any of the authority so delegated.
