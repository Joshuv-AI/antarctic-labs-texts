25 C.F.R. § 2.606 — May I appeal inaction by a reviewing official on an appeal from a decision?
Citation: us:cfr:25:2.606
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-2.606
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 2 (Appeals From Administrative Decisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Yes. If a reviewing official fails to take action on the appeal within the timeframes established in § 2.505, any interested party may appeal the reviewing official's inaction as provided for in this subpart.
(b) Inaction by the IBIA or by the AS-IA is not subject to appeal under this part.
