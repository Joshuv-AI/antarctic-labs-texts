13 C.F.R. § 126.604 — Who decides if a contract opportunity for HUBZone set-aside competition exists?
Citation: us:cfr:13:126.604
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-126.604
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 126 (Hubzone Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer for the contracting activity determines if a contract opportunity for HUBZone set-aside competition exists.
