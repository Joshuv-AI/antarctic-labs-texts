40 C.F.R. § 440.20 — Applicability; description of the aluminum ore subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:440.20
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-440.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 440 (Ore Mining And Dressing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart B are applicable to discharges from facilities engaged in the mining of bauxite as an aluminum ore.
