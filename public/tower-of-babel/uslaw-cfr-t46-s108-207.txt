46 C.F.R. § 108.207 — Messrooms.
Citation: us:cfr:46:108.207
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-108.207
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 108 (Design And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each messroom that is not adjacent to the galley that serves it must be equipped with a steamtable.
(b) Each messroom must seat the number of persons expected to eat in the messroom at one time.
