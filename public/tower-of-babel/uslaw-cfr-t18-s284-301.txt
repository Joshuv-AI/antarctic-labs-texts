18 C.F.R. § 284.301 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:18:284.301
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-284.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 284 (Certain Sales And Transportation Of Natural Gas Under The Natural Gas Policy Act Of 1978 And Related Authorities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart implements section 5 of the Outer Continental Shelf Land Act (OCSLA) and applies to any jurisdictional interstate natural gas pipeline that holds a certificate under section 7 of the Natural Gas Act (NGA) authorizing the construction and operation of facilities on the Outer Continental Shelf (OCS).
