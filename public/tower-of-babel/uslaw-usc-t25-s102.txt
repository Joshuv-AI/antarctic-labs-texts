25 U.S.C. § 102 — Payment of costs for furnishing coal for Indian Service
Citation: us:usc:25:102
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section102
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 4 (Performance By United States Of Obligations To Indians); Subchapter I (Purchase Of Supplies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The cost of inspection, storage, transportation, and so forth, of coal for the Indian Service shall be paid from the support fund of the school or agency for which the coal is purchased.
