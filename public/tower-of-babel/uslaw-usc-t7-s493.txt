7 U.S.C. § 493 — Enforcement of provisions; prosecution of cases
Citation: us:usc:7:493
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section493
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 20 (Dumping Or Destruction Of Interstate Produce). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Agriculture is authorized and directed to enforce this chapter. It is made the duty of all United States attorneys to prosecute cases arising under this chapter, subject to the supervision and control of the Department of Justice.
