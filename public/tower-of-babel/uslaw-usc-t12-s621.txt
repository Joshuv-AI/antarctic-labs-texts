12 U.S.C. § 621 — Liability of shareholders on unpaid subscriptions; membership of corporation in Federal reserve bank prohibited
Citation: us:usc:12:621
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section621
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 6 (Foreign Banking); Subchapter II (Organization Of Corporations To Do Foreign Banking). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Shareholders in any corporation organized under the provisions of this subchapter shall be liable for the amount of their unpaid stock subscriptions. No such corporation shall become a member of any Federal reserve bank.
