27 C.F.R. § 20.36 — Execution under penalties of perjury.
Citation: us:cfr:27:20.36
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-20.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 20 (Distribution And Use Of Denatured Alcohol And Rum). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) When any form or document prescribed by this part is required to be executed under penalties of perjury, the dealer or user or other authorized person shall:
(1) Insert the declaration “I declare under the penalties of perjury that I have examined this ________ (insert the type of document such as claim, application, statement, report, certificate), including all supporting documents, and to the best of my knowledge and belief, it is true, correct, and complete”; and
(2) Sign the document.
(b) When the required document already bears a perjury declaration, the dealer or user or other authorized person shall sign the document.
