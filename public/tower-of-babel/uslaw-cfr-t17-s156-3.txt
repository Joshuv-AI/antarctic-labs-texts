17 C.F.R. § 156.3 — Contract market program for enforcement.
Citation: us:cfr:17:156.3
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-156.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 156 (Broker Associations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A contract market must, as part of its responsibilities pursuant to the Act and § 1.51, demonstrate effective use of broker association registration information to monitor the trading activity of broker associations and their members for potential abuse and to secure compliance with all other contract market bylaws, rules, regulations and resolutions which may pertain to such associations or their members.
