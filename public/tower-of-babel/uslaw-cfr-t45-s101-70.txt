45 C.F.R. § 101.70 — General provisions.

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-101.70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 101 (Health Resources Priorities And Allocations System (Hrpas)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) HHS may take specific official actions for any reason necessary or appropriate to the enforcement or the administration of the Defense Production Act and other applicable statutes, this part, or an official action. Such actions include Administrative Subpoenas, Demands for Information, and Inspection Authorizations.
(b) Any person who places or receives a rated order or an allocation order must comply with the provisions of this part.
(c) Willful violation of the provisions of title I or section 705 of the DPA and other applicable statutes, this part, or an official action of HHS is a criminal act, punishable as provided in the DPA and other applicable statutes, and as set forth in § 101.74.
