46 C.F.R. § 185.350 — Fueling of vessels using fuel having a flash point of 43.3 °C (110 °F) or lower (such as gasoline).
Citation: us:cfr:46:185.350
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-185.350
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 185 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A vessel must not take on fuel having a flash point of 43.3 °C (110 °F) or lower when passengers are on board.
