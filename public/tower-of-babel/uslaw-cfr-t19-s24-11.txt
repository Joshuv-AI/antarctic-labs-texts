19 C.F.R. § 24.11 — Notice to importer or owner of increased or additional duties, taxes, fees and interest.
Citation: us:cfr:19:24.11
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-24.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 24 (Customs Financial And Accounting Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any increased or additional duties, taxes, fees or interest found due upon liquidation or reliquidation shall be billed to the importer of record, or to the actual owner if the following have been filed with Customs:
(a) A declaration of the actual owner in accordance with section 485(d), Tariff Act of 1930, as amended (19 U.S.C. 1485(d)), and § 141.20 of this chapter; and
(b) A bond on Customs Form 301 in accordance with § 141.20 of this chapter.
