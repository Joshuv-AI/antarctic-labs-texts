14 C.F.R. § 271.2 — Definitions.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 271 (Guidelines For Subsidizing Air Carriers Providing Essential Air Transportation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in this part:
Eligible place means a place in the United States that meets the specified criteria outlined in 49 U.S.C. Chapter 417.
Essential air service is that air transportation which the Department has found to be essential under Subchapter II of Chapter 417 of the Statute.
