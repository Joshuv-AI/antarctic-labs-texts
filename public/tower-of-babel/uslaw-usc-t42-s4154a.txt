42 U.S.C. § 4154a — Standards for design, construction, and alteration of buildings; United States Postal Service
Citation: us:usc:42:4154a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section4154a
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 51 (Design And Construction Of Public Buildings To Accommodate Physically Handicapped). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States Postal Service, in consultation with the Secretary of Health and Human Services, shall prescribe such standards for the design, construction, and alteration of its buildings to insure whenever possible that physically handicapped persons will have ready access to, and use of, such buildings.
