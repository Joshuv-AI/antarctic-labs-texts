38 U.S.C. § 7619 — Expiration of program

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part V (Boards, Administrations, And Services); Chapter 76 (Health Professionals Educational Assistance Program); Subchapter II (Scholarship Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may not furnish scholarships to new participants in the Scholarship Program after December 31, 2033.
