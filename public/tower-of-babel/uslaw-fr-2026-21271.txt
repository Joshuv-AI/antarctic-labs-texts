DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Fine Denier Polyester Staple Fiber From India: Preliminary 
Results and Rescission, in Part of Antidumping Duty Administrative 
Review; 2024-2025

AGENCY: Enforcement and Compliance, International Trade Administration, 
Department of Commerce.
SUMMARY: The U.S. Department of Commerce (Commerce) preliminarily 
determines that The Bombay Dyeing And Manufacturing Company Limited 
(Bombay Dyeing) sold subject merchandise at less than normal value (NV) 
during the period of review (POR), July 1, 2024, through June 30, 2025. 
In addition, we are rescinding the review with respect to Reliance 
Industries Limited (RIL). Interested parties are invited to comment on 
these preliminary results of review.

DATES: Applicable October 19, 2026.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Howard Smith, AD/CVD Operations, 
Office IV, Enforcement and Compliance, International Trade 
Administration, U.S. Department of Commerce, 1401 Constitution Avenue 
NW, Washington, DC 20230; telephone: (202) 482-5193.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Background

    On August 22, 2025, based on timely requests for review, in 
accordance with 19 CFR 351.221(c)(1)(i), Commerce initiated an 
administrative review of the antidumping duty order on fine denier 
polyester staple fiber (fine denier PSF) from India with respect to RIL 
and Bombay Dyeing.\1\ On November 20, 2025, RIL timely withdrew its 
request for an administrative review of its exports of subject 
merchandise during the POR.\2\ On March 6, 2026, Bombay Dyeing notified 
Commerce that it will not participate in this administrative review.\3\

Scope of the Order

    The merchandise subject to the Order is fine denier PSF from India. 
For a complete description of the scope of the Order, see the 
Preliminary Decision Memorandum.

Rescission of Administrative Review, in Part

    Pursuant to 19 CFR 351.213(d)(1), Commerce will rescind an 
administrative review, in whole or in part, if all parties who 
requested the review withdraw their requests within 90 days of the date 
of publication of the notice of initiation of the requested review in 
the Federal Register. As noted above, Commerce received a timely-filed 
withdrawal request from RIL and no other parties requested an 
administrative review of RIL. Therefore, Commerce is rescinding this 
administrative review with respect to RIL, pursuant to 19 CFR 
351.213(d)(1).

Methodology

    Commerce is conducting this review in accordance with section 
751(a) of the Tariff Act of 1930, as amended (the Act). Pursuant to 
sections 776(a) and (b) of the Act, Commerce has preliminarily relied 
upon facts otherwise available with adverse inferences (AFA) to 
determine a dumping margin for Bombay Dyeing. For a full description of 
the methodology underlying the preliminary results of review, see the 
Preliminary Decision Memorandum.

Disclosure

    Normally, Commerce discloses to parties to the proceeding the 
calculations performed in the preliminary results of review within five 
days of any public announcement of the preliminary results or, if there 
is no public announcement, within five days of the date of publication 
of the notice of preliminary results in the Federal Register, in 
accordance with 19 CFR 351.224(b). However, because Commerce 
preliminarily applied total AFA to the individually examined company, 
in accordance with section 776 of the Act, there are no calculations to 
disclose.

Verification

    On February 4, 2026, Auriga Polymers, Inc. and Nan Ya Plastics 
Corporation, America, domestic interested parties, requested that 
Commerce conduct a verification of the information submitted by Bombay 
Dyeing in this review. However, because Bombay Dyeing did not provide 
any of the information requested by Commerce and Commerce preliminarily 
determines that Bombay Dyeing has been uncooperative, it will not 
conduct a verification in this review.

Assessment Rates

    Pursuant to section 751(a)(2)(A) of the Act and 19 CFR 
351.212(b)(1), Commerce will determine, and U.S. Customs and Border 
Protection (CBP) shall assess, antidumping duties on all appropriate 
entries of subject merchandise in accordance with the final results of 
this review.
    Commerce intends to issue rescission instructions to CBP in which 
it instructs CBP to assess antidumping duties on all entries of subject 
merchandise during the POR from RIL at rates equal to the cash deposit 
rate for estimated antidumping duties required at the time of entry, or 
withdrawal from warehouse, for consumption, in accordance with 19

[[Page 66080]]

CFR 351.212(c)(1)(i). Commerce intends to issue these rescission 
instructions to CBP no earlier than 35 days after the date of 
publication of this notice in the Federal Register.
    Commerce intends to issue assessment instructions to CBP regarding 
Bombay Dyeing in which it instructs CBP to assess antidumping duties on 
all entries of subject merchandise during the POR from Bombay Dyeing at 
rates equal to the weighted-average dumping margin determined for 
Bombay Dyeing in the final results of this review.
    If a timely summons is filed at the U.S. Court of International 
Trade, Commerce will direct CBP in its assessment instructions not to 
liquidate relevant entries until the time for parties to file a request 
for a statutory injunction has expired (i.e., within 90 days of 
publication).

Cash Deposit Requirements

    The following deposit requirements will be effective for all 
shipments of the subject merchandise entered, or withdrawn from 
warehouse, for consumption on or after the publication date of the 
final results of this administrative review, as provided by section 
751(a)(2)(C) of the Act: (1) the cash deposit rate for Bombay Dyeing 
will be the cash deposit rate established for Bombay Dyeing in the 
final results of this review (if that rate continues to be based on 
total AFA, the rate will be offset by the lowest export subsidy rate 
determined for any company in the companion countervailing duty 
proceeding, as part of the application of AFA to Bombay Dyeing); \15\ 
(2) for companies previously investigated or reviewed in this 
proceeding that are not covered by this review, the cash deposit rate 
will continue to be the company-specific cash deposit rate determined 
for the company in the most recently completed segment of this 
proceeding that covered the company; (3) if the exporter is not a firm 
covered in this review, or in a prior segment of this proceeding, but 
the manufacturer is, then the cash deposit rate will be the rate 
established for the manufacturer in the most recently completed segment 
of this proceeding that covered the manufacturer; and (4) the cash 
deposit rate for all other manufacturers or exporters will continue to 
be 14.67 percent, the all-others rate established in the less than fair 
value investigation adjusted for export subsidies.\16\ These cash 
deposit requirements, when imposed, shall remain in effect until 
further notice.

Notification to Importers

    This notice also serves as a preliminary reminder to importers of 
their responsibility under 19 CFR 351.402(f)(2) to file a certificate 
regarding the reimbursement of antidumping and/or countervailing duties 
prior to liquidation of the relevant entries during this review period. 
Failure to comply with this requirement could result in Commerce's 
presumption that reimbursement of antidumping and/or countervailing 
duties occurred and the subsequent assessment of double antidumping 
duties, and/or an increase in the amount of antidumping duties by the 
amount of the countervailing duties.
