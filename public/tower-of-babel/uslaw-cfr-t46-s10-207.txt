46 C.F.R. § 10.207 — Identification number.
Citation: us:cfr:46:10.207
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-10.207
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 10 (Merchant Mariner Credential). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For recordkeeping purposes only, a mariner's official MMC identification number is the individual's social security number. However, a unique serial number, called the mariner reference number, and not the social security number, will appear on the credential.
