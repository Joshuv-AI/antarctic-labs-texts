24 C.F.R. § 26.6 — Department representative.
Citation: us:cfr:24:26.6
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-26.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 26 (Hearing Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In each case heard before a hearing officer under this part, the Department shall be represented by attorneys from the Office of General Counsel.
