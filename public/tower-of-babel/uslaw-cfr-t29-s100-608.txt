29 C.F.R. § 100.608 — Subdivision of claims or debts.
Citation: us:cfr:29:100.608
Creator: National Labor Relations Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-100.608
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 100 (Administrative Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A debt may not be subdivided to avoid the monetary ceiling established by 31 U.S.C. 3711(a)(2) and 29 CFR 100.604.
