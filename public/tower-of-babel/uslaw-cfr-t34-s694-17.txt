34 C.F.R. § 694.17 — How does a State determine which State agency will apply for, and administer, a State grant under this program?
Citation: us:cfr:34:694.17
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-694.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 694 (Gaining Early Awareness And Readiness For Undergraduate Programs (Gear Up)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Governor of a State must designate which State agency applies for, and administers, a State grant under GEAR UP.
