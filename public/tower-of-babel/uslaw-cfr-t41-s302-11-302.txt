41 C.F.R. § 302-11.302 — -11.302 Purchase or sale of land in excess of what reasonably relates to the residence site.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-11.302
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-11.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-11 (11—Allowances For Expenses Incurred In Connection With Residence Transactions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When an employee purchases or sells land in excess of what reasonably relates to the residence site, reimbursement will be limited to a pro rata reimbursement of the land reasonably related to the residence site.
