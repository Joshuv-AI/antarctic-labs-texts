20 C.F.R. § 216.72 — What is required for payment of a child's annuity.
Citation: us:cfr:20:216.72
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-216.72
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 216 (Eligibility For An Annuity). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An eligible child of a deceased employee is entitled to an annuity upon applying therefor and submitting any evidence requested by the Board.
