45 C.F.R. § 1206.1-6 — -6 Time and place of termination hearings.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1206.1-6
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1206.1-6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1206 (Grants And Contracts—Suspension And Termination And Denial Of Application For Refunding). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The termination hearing shall be held in Washington, DC, or in the appropriate Service Center or Corporation State Office, at a time and place fixed by the responsible Corporation official unless he determines that for the convenience of the Corporation, or of the parties or their representatives, requires that another place be selected.
