Overview of This Information Collection

    Abstract: ATF's Office of Science and Technology's Forensic 
Services offers a National Firearms Examiner Academy (NFEA) training 
program for entry-level firearms and toolmark examiners. This program 
is designed in part to address the critical law enforcement services 
provided by firearms and toolmark examiners, which all law enforcement 
organizations that perform ballistic and forensic firearms examinations 
require. This program curriculum is designed for qualified applicants 
from state, local, and federal law enforcement agencies, and newly 
appointed ATF firearms and toolmark examiners. To request enrollment in 
the NFEA, individuals in the law enforcement agencies, including ATF, 
must complete the Application for National Firearms Examiner Academy, 
ATF Form 6330.1. Form 6330.1 provides ATF with information necessary to 
determine an applicant's qualifications and whether they may be 
enrolled in the program: a candidate's identifying information, 
information on their qualifications, and whether they have completed 
prerequisites.
    Type of information collection: revising a previously approved 
collection.
    Title of the form/collection: Application for National Firearms

    Agency form number, if any, and the applicable component of the

as the obligation to respond: Affected public: state, local, and tribal 
governments, federal government. Obligation to respond: required to 
obtain a benefit.
    Estimate of the total number of respondents and the amount of time 
estimated for an average respondent to respond: There are an estimated 
50 respondents associated with this collection per year, and it will 
take each respondent an average of approximately 10 minutes (0.167 
hours) per response to complete their responses.
    Estimate of the total annual burden (in hours) associated with the 
collection: The estimated annual public burden associated with this 
collection is 8.53 hours, which is equal to 50 (total respondents) * 1 
(# of responses per respondent) * 0.167 hours.
    Estimate of the total annual other cost burden associated with the 
collection, if applicable: 0; there are no costs.

Revisions to This Information Collection

    As part of this ICR renewal, ATF is making no revisions to this 
information collection, OMB 1140-0049. However, the annual hourly 
burden has decreased because the number of respondents went down from 
75 to 50 over the past three years. This in turn resulted in a 
decreased total annual burden from 15 to 8.35 hours. ATF is making some 
minor edits to the form for plain writing compliance.
