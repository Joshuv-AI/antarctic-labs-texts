25 C.F.R. § 2.808 — To whom must I submit my Objection to the Statement of Performance?
Citation: us:cfr:25:2.808
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-2.808
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 2 (Appeals From Administrative Decisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You must submit your Objection to the Statement of Performance to the decision-maker at: U.S. Department of the Interior, Bureau of Trust Funds Administration, Attn: Director, Office of Trust Analysis and Research, 1849 C Street NW, Washington, DC 20240.
(b) Your submission must comply with the provisions of § 2.214.
