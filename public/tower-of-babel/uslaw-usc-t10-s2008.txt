10 U.S.C. § 2008 — Authority to use funds for certain educational purposes
Citation: us:usc:10:2008
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2008
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training And Education); Chapter 101 (Training Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Funds appropriated to the Department of Defense may be used to carry out construction, as defined in section 7013(3) of the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965, or to carry out section 7008 of such Act, relating to the provision of assistance to certain school facilities under the impact aid program.
