30 U.S.C. § 206 — Noncontiguous coal or phosphate tracts in single lease
Citation: us:usc:30:206
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section206
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 3A (Leases And Prospecting Permits); Subchapter II (Coal). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Where coal or phosphate lands aggregating two thousand five hundred and sixty acres and subject to lease hereunder do not exist as contiguous areas, the Secretary of the Interior is authorized, if, in his opinion the interests of the public and of the lessee will be thereby subserved, to embrace in a single lease noncontiguous tracts which can be operated as a single mine or unit.
