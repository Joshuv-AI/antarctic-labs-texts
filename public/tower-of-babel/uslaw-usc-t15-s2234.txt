15 U.S.C. § 2234 — Encouraging adoption of standards for firefighter health and safety
Citation: us:usc:15:2234
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section2234
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 49 (Fire Prevention And Control). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator shall promote adoption by fire services of national voluntary consensus standards for firefighter health and safety, including such standards for firefighter operations, training, staffing, and fitness, by—
(1)
educating fire services about such standards;
(2)
encouraging the adoption at all levels of government of such standards; and
(3)
making recommendations on other ways in which the Federal Government can promote the adoption of such standards by fire services.
