7 C.F.R. § 1435.500 — General statement.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1435.500
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1435.500
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1435 (Sugar Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart shall be applicable to sugar beet and sugarcane processors throughout the United States who, acting in conjunction with the producers of the sugarcane or sugar beets processed by the processors, reduce sugar production in return for a payment of sugar from CCC when CCC determines that such action will reduce forfeitures of sugar pledged as collateral for a CCC loan.
