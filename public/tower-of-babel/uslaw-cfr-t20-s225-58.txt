20 C.F.R. § 225.58 — Waiver of recomputation.
Citation: us:cfr:20:225.58
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-225.58
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 225 (Primary Insurance Amount Determinations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the employee or the employee's family are disadvantaged in any way by a recomputation of a PIA to consider additional earnings, a request can be made to waive or give up the right to the recomputation. Such a request must be in writing and be made by every entitled family member. A request for waiver of a recomputation applies only to that recomputation for which the request is made.
