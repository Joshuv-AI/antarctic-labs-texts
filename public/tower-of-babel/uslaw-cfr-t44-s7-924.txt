44 C.F.R. § 7.924 — Affirmative action by recipient.
Citation: us:cfr:44:7.924
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-7.924
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 7 (Nondiscrimination In Federally-Assisted Programs (Fema Reg. 5)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Even in the absence of a finding of discrimination, a recipient may take affirmative action to overcome the effects of conditions that resulted in the limited participation in the recipient's program or activity on the basis of age.
