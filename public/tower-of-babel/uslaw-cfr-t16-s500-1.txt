16 C.F.R. § 500.1 — Scope of the regulations of this part.
Citation: us:cfr:16:500.1
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-500.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 500 (Regulations Under Section 4 Of The Fair Packaging And Labeling Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part establish requirements for labeling of consumer commodities as hereinafter defined with respect to identity of the commodity; the name and place of business of the manufacturer, packer, or distributor; the net quantity of contents; and net quantity of servings, uses, or applications represented to be present.
