Generating functions of Stieljes-Widgert polynomials

Definition 2
.

Define the
q
-exponential operator type Rogers-Ramanujan
\mathcal{R}(yD_{q})
by letting

\mathcal{R}(yD_{q})=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}q^{n^{2}}\frac{(yD_{q})^{n}}{(q;q)_{n}}.

Definition 3
.

We define the bivariate Stieljes-Wigert polynomials as

\mathrm{S}^{*}_{n}(x,y;q)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}q^{k^{2}}x^{n-k}y^{k}.

Theorem 1
.

\mathcal{R}(yD_{q})\{x^{n}\}=\mathrm{S}^{*}_{n}(x,y;q).

Proof.

\displaystyle\mathcal{R}(yD_{q})\{x^{n}\}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}q^{k^{2}}\frac{y^{k}}{(q;q)_{k}}D_{q}^{k}\{x^{n}\}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}q^{k^{2}}y^{k}x^{n-k}.

∎

Theorem 2
.

\mathcal{R}(yD_{q})\left\{\frac{1}{(ax;q)_{\infty}}\right\}=\frac{\mathcal{R}_{q}(ay)}{(ax;q)_{\infty}}.

Proof.

\displaystyle\mathcal{R}(yD_{q})\left\{\frac{1}{(ax;q)_{\infty}}\right\}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}q^{k^{2}}\frac{y^{k}}{(q;q)_{k}}D_{q}^{k}\left\{\frac{1}{(ax;q)_{\infty}}\right\}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}q^{k^{2}}\frac{y^{k}}{(q;q)_{k}}\frac{a^{k}}{(ax;q)_{\infty}}

\displaystyle=\frac{\mathcal{R}_{q}(ay)}{(ax;q)_{\infty}}.

∎

Theorem 3
.

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\mathrm{S}^{*}_{n}(x,y;q)\frac{z^{n}}{(q;q)_{n}}=\frac{\mathcal{R}_{q}(zy)}{(zx;q)_{\infty}}.

Proof.

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\mathrm{S}^{*}_{n}(x,y;q)\frac{z^{n}}{(q;q)_{n}}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{\mathcal{R}(yD_{q})\{(zx)^{n}\}}{(q;q)_{n}}

\displaystyle=\mathcal{R}(yD_{q})\left\{\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(zx)^{n}}{(q;q)_{n}}\right\}

\displaystyle=\mathcal{R}(yD_{q})\left\{\frac{1}{(zx;q)_{\infty}}\right\}

\displaystyle=\frac{\mathcal{R}_{q}(zy)}{(zx;q)_{\infty}}.

∎
