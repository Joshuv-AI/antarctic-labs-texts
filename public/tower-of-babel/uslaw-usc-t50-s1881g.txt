50 U.S.C. § 1881g — Savings provision
Citation: us:usc:50:1881g
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section1881g
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 36 (Foreign Intelligence Surveillance); Subchapter VI (Additional Procedures Regarding Certain Persons Outside The United States). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subchapter shall be construed to limit the authority of the Government to seek an order or authorization under, or otherwise engage in any activity that is authorized under, any other subchapter of this chapter.
