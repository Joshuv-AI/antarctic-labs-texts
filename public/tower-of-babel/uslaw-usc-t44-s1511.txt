44 U.S.C. § 1511 — International agreements excluded from provisions of chapter
Citation: us:usc:44:1511
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section1511
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 15 (Federal Register And Code Of Federal Regulations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter does not apply to treaties, conventions, protocols, and other international agreements, or proclamations thereof by the President.
