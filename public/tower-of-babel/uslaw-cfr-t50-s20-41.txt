50 C.F.R. § 20.41 — Prohibited if taken in violation of subpart C.
Citation: us:cfr:50:20.41
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-20.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 20 (Migratory Bird Hunting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person shall at any time, by any means, or in any manner, transport any migratory game bird or part thereof, taken in violation of any provision of subpart C of this part.
