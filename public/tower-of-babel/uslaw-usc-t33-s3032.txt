33 U.S.C. § 3032 — Service credit as deck officer or junior engineer for promotion purposes
Citation: us:usc:33:3032
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section3032
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 43 (National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration Commissioned Officer Corps); Subchapter II (Appointment And Promotion Of Officers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of promotion, there shall be counted in addition to active commissioned service, service as deck officer or junior engineer.
