9 C.F.R. § 3.133 — Separation.
Citation: us:cfr:9:3.133
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-3.133
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 3 (Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Animals housed in the same primary enclosure must be compatible. Animals shall not be housed near animals that interfere with their health or cause them discomfort.
