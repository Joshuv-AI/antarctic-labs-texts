39 C.F.R. § 5.2 — Committee procedure.
Citation: us:cfr:39:5.2
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-5.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 5 (Committees (Article V)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each committee establishes its own rules of procedure, consistent with these bylaws, and meets as provided in its rules. A majority of the members of a committee constitute a quorum.
