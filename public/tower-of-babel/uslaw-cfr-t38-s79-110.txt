38 C.F.R. § 79.110 — Withholding, suspension, deobligation, termination, and recovery of funds by VA.
Citation: us:cfr:38:79.110
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-79.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 79 (Legal Services For Homeless Veterans And Veterans At-Risk For Homelessness Grant Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

VA will enforce this part through such actions as may be appropriate. Appropriate actions include withholding, suspension, deobligation, termination, recovery of funds by VA, and actions in accordance with 2 CFR part 200.
