12 C.F.R. § 611.1290 — Continuation of borrower rights.
Citation: us:cfr:12:611.1290
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-611.1290
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 611 (Organization). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may not require a waiver of contractual borrower rights provisions as a condition of borrowing from and owning equity in the successor institution. Institutions that become other financing institutions on termination must comply with the applicable borrower rights provisions in the Act and part 617 of this chapter.
