21 C.F.R. § 211.142 — Warehousing procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:21:211.142
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-211.142
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 211 (Current Good Manufacturing Practice For Finished Pharmaceuticals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Written procedures describing the warehousing of drug products shall be established and followed. They shall include:
(a) Quarantine of drug products before release by the quality control unit.
(b) Storage of drug products under appropriate conditions of temperature, humidity, and light so that the identity, strength, quality, and purity of the drug products are not affected.
