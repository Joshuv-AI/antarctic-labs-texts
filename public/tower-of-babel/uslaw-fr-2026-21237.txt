SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: 
    Title: Great American Digital Corridors (GADC) Program.
    Background: The Federal Highway Administration (FHWA), in 
partnership with the Office of the Assistant Secretary for Research and 
Technology, administers the Fiscal Year (FY) 2026 Great American 
Digital Corridors (GADC) Program Cooperative Agreement. The GADC 
Program was authorized under Section 11101(c)(1)(D) of the 
Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act (IIJA) (Pub. L. 117-58) to carry 
out the Intelligent Transportation Systems Program pursuant to Title 
23, United States Code (U.S.C.) Sec. Sec.  512-518.
    The purpose of the GADC Program is to support States and eligible 
entities in advancing the deployment of digital infrastructure along 
the Federal-aid highway system, including the Interstate System. The 
program's goals include modernizing the digital infrastructure of the 
Federal-aid highway system, including the Interstate System, to improve 
safety and efficiency, reduce congestion, and optimize freight 
movement. Digital infrastructure supports the modernization of 
transportation systems by enabling the exchange of precise, accurate, 
and reliable digital feeds of work zones, lane closures, and other 
dynamic, real-time operational conditions. By using digital 
infrastructure to bridge the gap between physical infrastructure and 
digital intelligence, infrastructure owner-operators can improve 
safety, efficiency, security, and mobility for transportation workers 
and all road users.
    The program provides competitive cost-reimbursable cooperative 
agreements to eligible entities, including States, local and Tribal 
governments, transit agencies, public toll authorities, Metropolitan 
Planning Organizations (MPOs), other political subdivisions of a State 
or local government, private entities, nonprofit organizations, and

[[Page 66193]]

academic institutions may apply as an eligible entity and lead 
administration of the project, provided they apply as part of 
consortium with one or more other eligible entities, and consortiums of 
eligible entities. The Federal share for GADC projects is 100 percent, 
requiring no non-Federal match. The FHWA announced the availability of 
up to $20 million for fiscal year 2026 funding to support these digital 
infrastructure improvements.
    Respondents: The NOFO announcing up to $20 million of Fiscal Year 
(FY) 2026, funding for GADC cooperative agreement will be available for 
eligible respondents entities, including States, local and Tribal 
governments, transit agencies, public toll authorities, Metropolitan 
Planning Organizations (MPOs), other political subdivisions of a State 
or local government, private entities, nonprofit organizations, and 
academic institutions may apply as an eligible entity and lead 
administration of the project, provided they apply as part of 
consortium with one or more other eligible entities, and consortiums of 
eligible entities. FHWA is expecting approximately 20 applicants to 
apply for the GADC cooperative agreement.
    Frequency: NOFOs and cooperative agreement solicitations may be 
published annually by FHWA but, are subject to the availability of 
funds in appropriations or, any legislation signed into law authorizing 
funds.
    Estimated Average Burden per Response: 10 hours per respondent per 
applicant.
    Estimated Total Annual Burden Hours: It is expected that the 
respondents will complete approximately 20 applications for the 
program, for an estimated total of 200 annual burden hours (10hrs x 20 
= 200hrs).
    Public Comments Invited: You are asked to comment on any aspect of 
this information collection, including: (1) Whether the proposed 
collection is necessary for the FHWA's performance; (2) the accuracy of 
the estimated burdens; (3) ways for the FHWA to enhance the quality, 
usefulness, and clarity of the collected information; and (4) ways that 
the burden could be minimized, including the use of electronic 
technology, without reducing the quality of the collected information. 
The agency will summarize and/or include your comments in the request 
for OMB's clearance of this information collection.
