36 C.F.R. § 327.18 — Commercial activities.
Citation: us:cfr:36:327.18
Creator: Corps Of Engineers, Department Of The Army
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-327.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 327 (Rules And Regulations Governing Public Use Of Water Resource Development Projects Administered By The Chief Of Engineers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The engaging in or solicitation of business on project land or waters without the express written permission of the District Commander is prohibited.
(b) It shall be a violation of this part to refuse to or fail to comply with any terms, clauses or conditions of any lease, license or agreements issued by the District Commander.
