40 C.F.R. § 60.5140 — How do I obtain my operator qualification?
Citation: us:cfr:40:60.5140
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.5140
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 60 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You must obtain operator qualification by completing a training course that satisfies the criteria under § 60.5130(b).
(b) Qualification is valid from the date on which the training course is completed and the operator successfully passes the examination required under § 60.5130(c)(2).
