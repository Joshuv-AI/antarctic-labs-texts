43 C.F.R. § 4750.1 — Private maintenance.
Citation: us:cfr:43:4750.1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4750.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4700 (Protection, Management, And Control Of Wild Free-Roaming Horses And Burros). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The authorized officer shall make available for private maintenance all healthy excess wild horses or burros for which an adoption demand by qualified individuals exists.
