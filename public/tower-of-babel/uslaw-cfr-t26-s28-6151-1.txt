26 C.F.R. § 28.6151-1 — -1 Time and place for paying tax shown on returns.
Citation: us:cfr:26:28.6151-1
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-28.6151-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 28 (Imposition Of Tax On Gifts And Bequests From Covered Expatriates). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In general. The section 2801 tax shown on the return must be paid at the time prescribed in § 28.6071-1 for filing the return, and in the manner prescribed in § 28.6091-1 for filing the return.
(b) Applicability date. This section applies to covered gifts or covered bequests received on or after January 1, 2025.
