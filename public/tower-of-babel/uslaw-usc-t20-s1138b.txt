20 U.S.C. § 1138b — Administrative provisions
Citation: us:usc:20:1138b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1138b
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 28 (Higher Education Resources And Student Assistance); Subchapter VII (Graduate And Postsecondary Improvement Programs); Part B (Fund For The Improvement Of Postsecondary Education). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may appoint, for terms not to exceed 3 years, without regard to the provisions of title 5 governing appointments in the competitive service, not more than 7 technical employees to administer this part who may be paid without regard to the provisions of chapter 51 and subchapter III of chapter 53 of such title relating to classification and General Schedule pay rates.
