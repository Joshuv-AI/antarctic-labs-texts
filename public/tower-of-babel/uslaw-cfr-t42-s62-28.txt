42 C.F.R. § 62.28 — Under what circumstances can the service or payment obligation be canceled, waived or suspended?
Citation: us:cfr:42:62.28
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-62.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 62 (National Health Service Corps Scholarship And Loan Repayment Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A service or payment obligation under the Loan Repayment Program will be canceled or may be waived or suspended as provided in § 62.12 of this part of the regulations.
