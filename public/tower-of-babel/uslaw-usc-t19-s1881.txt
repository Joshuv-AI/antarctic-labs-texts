19 U.S.C. § 1881 — Normal trade relations
Citation: us:usc:19:1881
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section1881
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 7 (Trade Expansion Program); Subchapter II (Trade Agreements); Part VI (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as otherwise provided in this subchapter, in section 1351 of this title, or in section 401(a) of the Tariff Classification Act of 1962, any duty or other import restriction or duty-free treatment proclaimed in carrying out any trade agreement under this subchapter or section 1351 of this title shall apply to products of all foreign countries, whether imported directly or indirectly.
