24 C.F.R. § 10.6 — Initiation of rulemaking.
Citation: us:cfr:24:10.6
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-10.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 10 (Rulemaking: Policy And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Rulemaking proceedings may be initiated on the Secretary's motion, or on the recommendation of a Federal, State, or local government or government agency, or on the petition of any interested person.
