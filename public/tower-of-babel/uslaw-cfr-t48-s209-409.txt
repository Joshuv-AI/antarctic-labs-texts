48 C.F.R. § 209.409 — 209.409 Contract clause.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 209 (Contractor Qualifications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Use the clause at 252.209-7004, Subcontracting with Firms that are Owned or Controlled by the Government of a Country that is a State Sponsor of Terrorism, in solicitations and contracts with a value of $200,000 or more.
