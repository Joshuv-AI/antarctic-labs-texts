48 C.F.R. § 335.072 — 335.072 Key personnel.
Citation: us:cfr:48:335.072
Creator: Chapter 3—Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-335.072
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 335 (Research And Development Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the contracting officer determines that the personnel to be assigned to perform effort on an R&D contract are critical to the success of the R&D effort, or were a critical factor in the award of the contract, then the contracting officer should consider using the key personnel clause at 352.237-75, Key Personnel.
