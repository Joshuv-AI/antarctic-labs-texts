45 C.F.R. § 162.1501 — Enrollment and disenrollment in a health plan transaction.
Citation: us:cfr:45:162.1501
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-162.1501
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 162 (Administrative Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The enrollment and disenrollment in a health plan transaction is the transmission of subscriber enrollment information from the sponsor of the insurance coverage, benefits, or policy, to a health plan to establish or terminate insurance coverage.
