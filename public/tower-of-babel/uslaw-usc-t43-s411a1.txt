43 U.S.C. § 411a–1 — Authorization of appropriations for investigations of feasibility of reclamation projects
Citation: us:usc:43:411a–1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section411a–1
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 12 (Reclamation And Irrigation Of Lands By Federal Government); Subchapter III (Institution And Construction Of Projects). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The sum of $125,000 annually is authorized to be appropriated for cooperative and miscellaneous investigations of the feasibility of reclamation projects.
