32 U.S.C. § 907 — Relationship to State duty
Citation: us:usc:32:907
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title32-section907
Context: United States Code, Title 32 (National Guard); Chapter 9 (Homeland Defense Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed as a limitation on the authority of any unit of the National Guard of a State, when such unit is not in Federal service, to perform functions authorized to be performed by the National Guard by the laws of the State concerned.
