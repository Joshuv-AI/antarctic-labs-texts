22 U.S.C. § 2431j — Consultations with Congress
Citation: us:usc:22:2431j
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2431j
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 32 (Foreign Assistance); Subchapter IV (Debt Reduction For Developing Countries With Tropical Forests). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President shall consult with the appropriate congressional committees on a periodic basis to review the operation of the Facility under this subchapter and the eligibility of countries for benefits from the Facility under this subchapter.
