27 C.F.R. § 479.89 — Transfers to the United States.
Citation: us:cfr:27:479.89
Creator: Bureau Of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms, And Explosives, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-479.89
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 479 (Machine Guns, Destructive Devices, And Certain Other Firearms). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A firearm may be transferred to the United States or any department, independent establishment or agency thereof without payment of the transfer tax. However, the procedures for the transfer of a firearm as provided in § 479.90 shall be followed in a tax-exempt transfer of a firearm under this section, unless the transferor is relieved of such requirement under other provisions of this part.
