36 U.S.C. § 22706 — Exclusive right to name, seals, emblems, and badges
Citation: us:usc:36:22706
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section22706
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 227 (Amvets (American Veterans)). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation and its State, regional, and local subdivisions have the exclusive right to use the name “AMVETS (American Veterans)” and seals, emblems, and badges the corporation adopts.
