7 C.F.R. § 930.6 — District.

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-930.6

District means one of the subdivisions of the production area described in § 930.20(c), or such other subdivisions as may be established pursuant to § 930.21, or any subdivision added pursuant to § 930.52.
