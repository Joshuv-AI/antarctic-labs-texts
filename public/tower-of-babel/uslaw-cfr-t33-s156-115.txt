33 C.F.R. § 156.115 — Person in charge: Limitations.
Citation: us:cfr:33:156.115
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-156.115
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 156 (Oil And Hazardous Material Transfer Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) No person may serve as the person in charge of transfer operations on more than one vessel at a time during transfers between vessels or between two or more vessels and a facility unless authorized by the COTP.
(b) No person may serve as the person in charge of both a vessel and a facility during transfer operations unless authorized by the COTP.
