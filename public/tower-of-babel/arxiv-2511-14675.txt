Abstract
Let 𝐁 {\bf B} be the ring of analytic functions on the Fargues-Fontaine curve Y FF Y_{\rm FF} . We show that adding p p -adic analogs of log ⁡ p \log p and log ⁡ 2 ​ π ​ i \log 2\pi i kills its Galois cohomology in degrees ≥ 1 \geq 1 . The analogous result for 𝐁 dR + {\bf B}_{{\rm dR}}^{+} is folklore. This makes it possible to formulate C dR C_{\rm dR} and C st C_{\rm st} -type conjectures for compact support cohomology of p p -adic analytic varieties.
