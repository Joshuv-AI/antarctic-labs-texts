46 C.F.R. § 10.214 — Security Check.
Citation: us:cfr:46:10.214
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-10.214
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 10 (Merchant Mariner Credential). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Until April 15, 2009, the Coast Guard may conduct a security check on an applicant for an MMC, utilizing the criminal record review discussed in § 10.211 of this part.
