18 U.S.C. § 2 — Principals
Citation: us:usc:18:2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section2
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 1 (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Whoever commits an offense against the United States or aids, abets, counsels, commands, induces or procures its commission, is punishable as a principal.
(b)
Whoever willfully causes an act to be done which if directly performed by him or another would be an offense against the United States, is punishable as a principal.
