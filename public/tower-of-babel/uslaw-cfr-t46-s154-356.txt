46 C.F.R. § 154.356 — Cargo emergency jettisoning piping.
Citation: us:cfr:46:154.356
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-154.356
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 154 (Safety Standards For Self-Propelled Vessels Carrying Bulk Liquefied Gases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Emergency jettisoning piping must:
(a) Meet § 154.355(a);
(b) Be designed to allow cargo discharge without the outer hull steel temperature falling below the minimum temperatures under §§ 154.170 and 154.172; and
(c) Be specially approved by the Commandant (CG-ENG).
