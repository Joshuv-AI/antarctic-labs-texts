12 U.S.C. § 142 — Banks in reserve cities; reserves
Citation: us:usc:12:142
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section142
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 2 (National Banks); Subchapter VIII (Reserve Cities; Lawful Reserves). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

National banking associations located in reserve cities or central reserve cities shall maintain reserves provided for in section 462 of this title for banks so located.
