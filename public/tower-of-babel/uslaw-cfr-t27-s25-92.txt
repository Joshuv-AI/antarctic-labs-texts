27 C.F.R. § 25.92 — Consent of surety.
Citation: us:cfr:27:25.92
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-25.92
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 25 (Beer). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A brewer may change the terms of any bond filed under this part by filing a consent of surety. Consents of surety will be executed on Form 5000.18 by the brewer and the surety on the bond, with the same formality and proof of authorization as required for the execution of a bond.
