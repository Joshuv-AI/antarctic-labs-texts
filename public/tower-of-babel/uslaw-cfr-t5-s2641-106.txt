5 C.F.R. § 2641.106 — Applicability of certain provisions to Vice President.
Citation: us:cfr:5:2641.106
Creator: Office Of Government Ethics
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-2641.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 2641 (Post-Employment Conflict Of Interest Restrictions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subsections 207(d) (relating to restrictions on very senior personnel) and 207(f) (restrictions with regard to foreign entities) of title 18, United States Code, apply to a Vice President, to the same extent as they apply to employees and former employees covered by those provisions. See §§ 2641.205 and 2641.206. There are no other restrictions in 18 U.S.C. 207 applicable to a Vice President.
