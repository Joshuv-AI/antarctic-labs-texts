23 C.F.R. § 1200.42 — Post-grant adjustments.
Citation: us:cfr:23:1200.42
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration And Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-1200.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 1200 (Uniform Procedures For State Highway Safety Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The expiration of a Highway Safety Plan does not affect the ability of NHTSA to disallow costs and recover funds on the basis of a later audit or other review or the State's obligation to return any funds due as a result of later refunds, corrections, or other transactions.
