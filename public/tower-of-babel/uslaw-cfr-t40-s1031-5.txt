40 C.F.R. § 1031.5 — Engines installed on domestic and foreign aircraft.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1031.5
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1031.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1031 (Control Of Air Pollution From Aircraft Engines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary of Transportation shall apply these regulations to aircraft of foreign registry in a manner consistent with obligations assumed by the United States in any treaty, convention or agreement between the United States and any foreign country or foreign countries.
