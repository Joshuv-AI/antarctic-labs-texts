22 U.S.C. § 1643i — Time limitation on completion of affairs of Commission
Citation: us:usc:22:1643i
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1643i
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 21 (Settlement Of International Claims); Subchapter V (Claims Against Cuba And China). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commission shall complete its affairs in connection with the settlement of claims pursuant to this subchapter not later than July 6, 1972.
