46 C.F.R. § 168.15-45 — -45 Heating and cooling.
Citation: us:cfr:46:168.15-45
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-168.15-45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 168 (Civilian Nautical School Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All quarters must be adequately heated and cooled in a manner suitable to the purpose of the space.
