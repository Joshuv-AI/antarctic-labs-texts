16 U.S.C. § 4101 — Congressional statement of purposes
Citation: us:usc:16:4101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section4101
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 61 (Interjurisdictional Fisheries). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purposes of this chapter are—
(1)
to promote and encourage State activities in support of the management of interjurisdictional fishery resources;
(2)
to promote and encourage management of interjurisdictional fishery resources throughout their range; and
(3)
to promote and encourage research in preparation for the implementation of the use of ecosystems and interspecies approaches to the conservation and management of interjurisdictional fishery resources throughout their range.
