27 C.F.R. § 17.186 — Transfer of distilled spirits to other containers.
Citation: us:cfr:27:17.186
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-17.186
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 17 (Drawback On Taxpaid Distilled Spirits Used In Manufacturing Nonbeverage Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A manufacturer may transfer taxpaid distilled spirits from the original package to other containers at any time for the purpose of facilitating the manufacture of products unfit for beverage use. Containers into which distilled spirits have been transferred under this section shall bear a label identifying their contents as taxpaid distilled spirits, and shall be marked with the serial number of the original package from which the spirits were withdrawn.
