30 C.F.R. § 48.32 — Appeals procedures.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 48 (Training And Retraining Of Miners). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The operator, miner, and miners' representative shall have the right of appeal from a decision of the District Manager.
(a) In the event an operator, miner, or miners' representative decides to appeal a decision by the District Manager, such an appeal shall be submitted, in writing, to the Administrator for Coal Mine Safety and Health or the Administrator for Metal and Nonmetal Mine Safety and Health, as appropriate, MSHA, 200 Constitution Avenue NW, Washington, DC 20210, within 30 days of notification of the District Manager's decision.
(b) The Administrator may require additional information from the operator, the miners or their representatives, and the District Manager, if the Administrator determines such information is necessary.
(c) The Administrator shall render a decision on the appeal within 30 days after receipt of the appeal.
