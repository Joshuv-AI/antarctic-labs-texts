Correction: Advanced strategies for detecting acid sphingomyelinase deficiency type B with attenuated phenotypes
Authors: Thomas Villeneuve, Thibaut Jamme, Robin Schwob, Thierry Levade, Grégoire Prévot
Published: 2025

Correction: Orphanet J Rare Dis 20, 252 (2025)

10.1186/s13023-025-03746-9

Following publication of the original article [1], the authors reported an error in the numerical values of Fig. 1 in the published version.

The incorrect version of Fig. 1 was:

The Venn diagram illustrates the overlap between all types of conditions compatible with ASMD among selected patients with a cholesterol risk ratio > 4.5 n = 63 653). The numbers represent the cohort of patients derived from the total population. Individuals with a confirmed diagnosis of ASMD who met four or five diagnostic criteria are represented by grey points. Abbreviations: CT: Computed Tomography; GGOs: Ground-Glass Opacities

The correct Fig. 1 is:

The Venn diagram illustrates the overlap between all types of conditions compatible with ASMD among selected patients with a cholesterol risk ratio > 4.5 n = 63 653). The numbers represent the cohort of patients derived from the total population. Individuals with a confirmed diagnosis of ASMD who met four or five diagnostic criteria are represented by grey points. Abbreviations: CT: Computed Tomography; GGOs: Ground-Glass Opacities

The original article [1] has been updated.
