36 C.F.R. § 1011.15 — How will the Presidio Trust refer debts to private collection contractors?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1011.15
Creator: Presidio Trust
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1011.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1011 (Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Presidio Trust will transfer delinquent debts to the FMS to obtain debt collection services provided by private collection contractors. See § 1011.9 of this part.
