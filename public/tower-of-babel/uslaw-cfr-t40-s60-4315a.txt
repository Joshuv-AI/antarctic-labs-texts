40 C.F.R. § 60.4315a — a What pollutants are regulated by this subpart?
Citation: us:cfr:40:60.4315a
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.4315a
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 60 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The pollutants regulated by this subpart are nitrogen oxide (NOX) and sulfur dioxide (SO2).
