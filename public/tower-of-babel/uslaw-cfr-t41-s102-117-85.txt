41 C.F.R. § 102-117.85 — -117.85 Filing damage claims.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-117.85
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-117.85
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-117 (117—Transportation Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

File a claim for loss or damage to property with the TSP. Refer to 41 CFR part 102-118 for more information regarding statutory time limits to file administrative claims or judicial actions against a TSP.
