15 U.S.C. § 5105 — Coordination
Citation: us:usc:15:5105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section5105
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 77 (Steel And Aluminum Energy Conservation And Technology Competitiveness). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall coordinate the research and development conducted under this chapter with other research and development being conducted by the Department of Energy and other Federal agencies in order to increase efficiency and avoid duplication of effort.
