48 C.F.R. § 4.1007 — 4.1007 Solicitation alternative line item proposal.
Citation: us:cfr:48:4.1007
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-4.1007
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 4 (Administrative And Information Matters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Solicitations should be structured to allow offerors to propose alternative line items (see 4.1008 and 52.212-1(e)). For example, when soliciting certain items using units of measure such as kit, set, or lot, the offeror may not be able to group and deliver all items in a single shipment.
