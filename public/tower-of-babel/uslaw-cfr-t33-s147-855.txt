33 C.F.R. § 147.855 — Blind Faith Semi-Sub Facility Safety Zone.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 147 (Safety Zones). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Description. The Blind Faith Semi-Sub facility is in the deepwater area of the Gulf of America at Mississippi Canyon Block 650. The facility is located at 28°20′29.5279″ N/−88°15′56.4728″ W and the area within 500 meters (1640.4 feet) from each point on the facility structure's outer edge is a safety zone.
(b) Regulation. No vessel may enter or remain in this safety zone except the following:
(1) An attending vessel;
(2) A vessel under 100 feet in length overall not engaged in towing; or
(3) A vessel authorized by the Commander, USCG Heartland District or a designated representative.
