26 C.F.R. § 44.4422-1 — -1 Doing business in violation of Federal or State law.
Citation: us:cfr:26:44.4422-1
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-44.4422-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 44 (Taxes On Wagering; Effective January 1, 1955). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Payment of any special tax within the scope of the regulations in this part in nowise authorizes the carrying on of any business in violation of a law of the United States or the law of any State. The special tax stamp is not a license or permit and affords no protection from prosecution for violation of any Federal or State law. See also section 4906.
