2 U.S.C. § 4581 — Aggregate gross compensation of employee of Senator of State with population under 5,000,000
Citation: us:usc:2:4581
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section4581
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 45 (Congressional Pay And Benefits); Subchapter III (Senate); Part A (Amount And Type). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Notwithstanding the provisions of section 4575(d)(1) of this title, and except as otherwise provided in subparagraph (C) of such subsection (d)(1), the aggregate of gross compensation paid employees in the office of a Senator shall not exceed during each fiscal year $1,012,083 if the population of his State is less than 5,000,000.
(b)
Subsection (a) shall take effect October 1, 1991.
