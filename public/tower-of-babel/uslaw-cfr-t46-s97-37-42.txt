46 C.F.R. § 97.37-42 — -42 Markings for lifesaving appliances, instructions to passengers, and stowage locations.
Citation: us:cfr:46:97.37-42
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-97.37-42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 97 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Lifesaving appliances, instructions to passengers, and stowage locations must be marked in accordance with subchapter W (Lifesaving Appliances and Arrangements) of this chapter.
