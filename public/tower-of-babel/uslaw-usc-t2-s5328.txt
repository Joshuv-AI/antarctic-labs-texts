2 U.S.C. § 5328 — Authority to prescribe regulations
Citation: us:usc:2:5328
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section5328
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 53 (House Of Representatives Members); Subchapter II (Employees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Committee on House Oversight of the House of Representatives shall have authority to prescribe regulations for the carrying out of sections 5327 to 5329 of this title.
