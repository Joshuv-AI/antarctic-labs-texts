34 C.F.R. § 76.201 — A State plan must meet all statutory and regulatory requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:34:76.201
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-76.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 76 (State-Administered Formula Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary approves a State plan if it meets the requirements of the Federal statutes and regulations that apply to the plan.
