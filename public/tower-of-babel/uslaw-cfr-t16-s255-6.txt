16 C.F.R. § 255.6 — Endorsements directed to children.
Citation: us:cfr:16:255.6
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-255.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 255 (Guides Concerning Use Of Endorsements And Testimonials In Advertising). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Endorsements in advertisements addressed to children may be of special concern because of the character of the audience. Practices that would not ordinarily be questioned in advertisements addressed to adults might be questioned in such cases.
