50 U.S.C. § 3366 — Authorities of heads of other departments and agencies
Citation: us:usc:50:3366
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section3366
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 45 (Miscellaneous Intelligence Community Authorities); Subchapter IV (Collection, Analysis, And Sharing Of Intelligence). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the head of any department or agency of the United States is authorized to receive and utilize funds made available to the department or agency by the Director of National Intelligence pursuant to section 3024(d)(2) of this title, as amended by subsection (a), and receive and utilize any system referred to in such section that is made available to such department or agency.
