40 C.F.R. § 444.14 — Effluent limitations attainable by the application of the best conventional pollutant control technology (BCT).
Citation: us:cfr:40:444.14
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-444.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 444 (Waste Combustors Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in 40 CFR 125.30 through 125.32, any existing point source subject to this subpart must achieve the following effluent limitations representing the application of BCT: Limitations for TSS and pH are the same as the corresponding limitation specified in § 444.13.
