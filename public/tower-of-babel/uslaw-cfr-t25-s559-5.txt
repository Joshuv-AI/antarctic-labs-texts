25 C.F.R. § 559.5 — Must a tribe notify the Chair if a facility license is terminated or expires or if a gaming place, facility, or location closes or reopens?
Citation: us:cfr:25:559.5
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-559.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 559 (Facility License Notifications And Submissions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A tribe must notify the Chair within 30 days if a facility license is terminated or expires or if a gaming place, facility, or location closes or reopens. A tribe need not provide a notification of seasonal closures or temporary closures with a duration of less than 180 days.
