28 C.F.R. § 42.308 — Noncompliance.
Citation: us:cfr:28:42.308
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-42.308
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 42 (Nondiscrimination; Equal Employment Opportunity; Policies And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Failure to implement and maintain an equal employment opportunity program as required by these guidelines shall subject recipients of LEAA assistance to the sanctions prescribed by the Safe Streets Act and the equal employment opportunity regulations of the Department of Justice. (See 42 U.S.C. 3757 and 28 CFR 42.207.)
