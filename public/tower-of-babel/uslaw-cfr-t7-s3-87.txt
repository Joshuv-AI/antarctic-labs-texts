7 C.F.R. § 3.87 — Agency regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3.87
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3.87
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3 (Debt Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

USDA agencies may issue regulations or policies not inconsistent with OPM regulations (5 CFR part 550, subpart K) and regulations in this subpart governing the collection of a debt by salary offset.
