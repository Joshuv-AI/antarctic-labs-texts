45 C.F.R. § 1150.44 — Can a judgment I have obtained against the United States be used to satisfy a debt that I owe to the Endowment?
Citation: us:cfr:45:1150.44
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1150.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1150 (Collection Of Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Collection by offset against a judgment obtained by a debtor against the United States will be accomplished in accordance with 31 U.S.C. 3728.
