30 C.F.R. § 550.103 — Where can I find more information about the requirements in this part?
Citation: us:cfr:30:550.103
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-550.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 550 (Oil And Gas And Sulfur Operations In The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

BOEM may issue Notices to Lessees and Operators (NTLs) that clarify or provide more detail about certain regulatory requirements. NTLs may also outline what information you must provide, as required by regulation, in your various submissions to BOEM.
