43 C.F.R. § 1823.11 — What is the authority for BLM issuing a refund of a payment?
Citation: us:cfr:43:1823.11
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-1823.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 1820 (Application Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

BLM can issue you a refund under the authority of section 304(c) of the Federal Land Policy and Management Act, 43 U.S.C. 1734.
