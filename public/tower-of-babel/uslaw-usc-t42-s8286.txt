42 U.S.C. § 8286 — Consensus on factors and data for energy conservation standards
Citation: us:usc:42:8286
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section8286
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 91 (National Energy Conservation Policy); Subchapter VI (Coordination Of Federal Energy Conservation Factors And Data). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Energy shall assure that within 6 months after June 30, 1980, the Secretary of Energy, the Secretary of Housing and Urban Development, the Secretary of Agriculture, the Secretary of Health and Human Services, the Secretary of Defense, the Administrator of the General Services Administration, and the head of any other agency responsible for developing energy conservation standards for new or existing residential, commercial, or agricultural buildings shall reach a consensus regarding factors and data used to develop such standards. This consensus shall apply to, but not be limited to—
(1)
fuel price projections;
(2)
discount rates;
(3)
inflation rates;
(4)
climatic conditions and zones; and
(5)
the cost and energy saving characteristics of construction materials.
