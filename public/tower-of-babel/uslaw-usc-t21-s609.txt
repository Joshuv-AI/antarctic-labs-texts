21 U.S.C. § 609 — Examination of animals and food products thereof, slaughtered and prepared during nighttime
Citation: us:usc:21:609
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section609
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 12 (Meat Inspection); Subchapter I (Inspection Requirements; Adulteration And Misbranding). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall cause an examination and inspection of all amenable species, and the food products thereof, slaughtered and prepared in the establishments hereinbefore described for the purposes of commerce to be made during the nighttime as well as during the daytime when the slaughtering of said amenable species, or the preparation of said food products is conducted during the nighttime.
