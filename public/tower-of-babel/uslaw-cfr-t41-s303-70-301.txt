41 C.F.R. § 303-70.301 — -70.301 Transportation of immediate family member's remains.
Citation: us:cfr:41:303-70.301
Creator: Chapter 303—Payment Of Expenses Connected With The Death Of Certain Employees
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-303-70.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 303-70 (70—Agency Requirements For Payment Of Expenses Connected With The Death Of Certain Employees And Immediate Family Members). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If requested by the employee, agencies must pay to transport the remains of an immediate family member, residing with the employee, who dies while the employee is stationed OCONUS. The remains may be transported to the residence of the immediate family member. The employee may elect an alternate destination, but it must be approved by the agency head or designated representative.
