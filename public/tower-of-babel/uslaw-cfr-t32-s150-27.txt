32 C.F.R. § 150.27 — Recording, photographing, broadcasting, or telecasting of hearings.
Citation: us:cfr:32:150.27
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-150.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 150 (Courts Of Criminal Appeals Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The recording, photographing, broadcasting, or televising of any session of the Court or other activity relating thereto is prohibited unless specifically authorized by the Court.
