7 U.S.C. § 7611 — Standards for Federal funding of agricultural research, extension, and education
Citation: us:usc:7:7611
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section7611
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 103 (Agricultural Research, Extension, And Education Reform); Subchapter I (Priorities, Scope, Review, And Coordination Of Agricultural Research, Extension, And Education). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The Secretary shall ensure that agricultural research, extension, or education activities described in subsection (b) address a concern that—
(1)
is a priority, as determined under section 7612(a) of this title; and
(2)
has national, multistate, or regional significance.
(b) Application
Subsection (a) applies to—
(1)
research activities conducted by the Agricultural Research Service; and
(2)
research, extension, or education activities administered, on a competitive basis, by the National Institute of Food and Agriculture.
