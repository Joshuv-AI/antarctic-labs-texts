50 C.F.R. § 80.92 — How long are funds available for a Federal obligation?
Citation: us:cfr:50:80.92
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-80.92
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 80 (Administrative Requirements, Pittman-Robertson Wildlife Restoration And Dingell-Johnson Sport Fish Restoration Acts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Funds are available for a Federal obligation starting October 1 of the FFY in which they are apportioned and for the number of years indicated in table 1 to § 80.92. Funds not obligated within the required period of availability will revert to the Service and be disbursed as described in the table.
