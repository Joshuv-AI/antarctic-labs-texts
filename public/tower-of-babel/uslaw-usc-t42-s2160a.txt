42 U.S.C. § 2160a — Review of Nuclear Proliferation Assessment Statements
Citation: us:usc:42:2160a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2160a
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 23 (Development And Control Of Atomic Energy); Subchapter X (International Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No court or regulatory body shall have any jurisdiction under any law to compel the performance of or to review the adequacy of the performance of any Nuclear Proliferation Assessment Statement, or any annexes thereto, called for in this Act or in the 1954 Act.
