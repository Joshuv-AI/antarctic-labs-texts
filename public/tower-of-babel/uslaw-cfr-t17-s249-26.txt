17 C.F.R. § 249.26 — Form 26, for notification of the admission to trading of a substituted or additional class of security under Rule 12a-5 (§ 240.12a-5 of this chapter).
Citation: us:cfr:17:249.26
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-249.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 249 (Forms, Securities Exchange Act Of 1934). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This form shall be used by a registered national securities exchange for notification of the admission to trading of a substituted or additional class of security under Rule 12a-5.
