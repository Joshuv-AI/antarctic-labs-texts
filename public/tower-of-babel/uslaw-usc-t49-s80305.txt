49 U.S.C. § 80305 — Availability of certain appropriations
Citation: us:usc:49:80305
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section80305
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Chapter 803 (Contraband). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Appropriations for enforcing customs, narcotics, counterfeiting, or internal revenue laws are available to carry out this chapter.
