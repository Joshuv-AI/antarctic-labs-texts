14 C.F.R. § 253.2 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:253.2
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-253.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 253 (Notice Of Terms Of Contract Of Carriage). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to all scheduled direct air carrier operations in interstate and overseas air transportation. It applies to all contracts with passengers, for those operations, that incorporate terms by reference.
