22 U.S.C. § 1477c — Notification and award of grants
Citation: us:usc:22:1477c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1477c
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 18 (United States Information And Educational Exchange Programs); Subchapter VIII (Appropriations And Other Funds). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Department of State may award grants for overseas public diplomacy programs only if the Committee on Foreign Affairs of the House of Representatives and the Committee on Foreign Relations of the Senate are notified fifteen days in advance of the proposed grant.
