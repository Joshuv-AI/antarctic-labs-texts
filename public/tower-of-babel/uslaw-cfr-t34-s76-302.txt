34 C.F.R. § 76.302 — The notice to the subgrantee.
Citation: us:cfr:34:76.302
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-76.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 76 (State-Administered Formula Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A State shall notify a subgrantee in writing of:
(a) The amount of the subgrant;
(b) The period during which the subgrantee may obligate the funds; and
(c) The Federal requirements that apply to the subgrant.
