14 C.F.R. § 414.5 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:414.5
Creator: Commercial Space Transportation, Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-414.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 414 (Safety Element Approvals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to an applicant that wants to obtain a safety element approval for any of the safety elements defined under this part and to persons granted a safety element approval under this part. Any person eligible under this part may apply to become the holder of a safety element approval.
