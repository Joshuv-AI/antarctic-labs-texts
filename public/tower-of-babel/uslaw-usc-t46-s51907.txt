46 U.S.C. § 51907 — Provision of decorations, medals, and replacements
Citation: us:usc:46:51907
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section51907
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part B (Merchant Marine Service); Chapter 519 (Merchant Marine Awards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Transportation may provide—
(1)
the decorations and medals authorized by this chapter and replacements for those decorations and medals; and
(2)
replacements for decorations and medals issued under a prior law.
