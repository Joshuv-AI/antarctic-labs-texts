46 C.F.R. § 148.235 — Castor beans.
Citation: us:cfr:46:148.235
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-148.235
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 148 (Carriage Of Bulk Solid Materials That Require Special Handling). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) This part applies only to the stowage and transportation of whole castor beans. Castor meal, castor pomace, and castor flakes may not be shipped in bulk.
(b) Persons handling castor beans must wear dust masks and goggles.
(c) Care must be taken to prevent castor bean dust from entering accommodation, control, or service spaces during cargo transfer operations.
