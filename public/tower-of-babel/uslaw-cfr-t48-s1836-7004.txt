48 C.F.R. § 1836.7004 — 1836.7004 NASA solicitation provision and contract clause.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1836 (Construction And Architect-Engineer Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer may insert a clause substantially the same as stated at 1852.236-75, Partnering for Construction Contracts, in solicitations and contracts for construction, when it has been determined that the benefits to be derived from partnering exceed the costs.
