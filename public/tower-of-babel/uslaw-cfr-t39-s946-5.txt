39 C.F.R. § 946.5 — Disposition of property having a value of less than $200.
Citation: us:cfr:39:946.5
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-946.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 946 (Rules Of Procedure Relating To The Disposition Of Stolen Mail Matter And Property Acquired By The Postal Inspection Service For Use As Evidence). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where the owner of property subject to this part is unknown and the Chief Postal Inspector or delegate estimates that fair market value of such property is $200 or less, title to the property vests in the United States Postal Service, subject to the right of the owner to submit a valid claim as provided in § 946.6.
