54 U.S.C. § 308304 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:54:308304
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section308304
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3083 (National Underground Railroad Network To Freedom). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Amounts.—
There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out this chapter $2,500,000 for each fiscal year, of which—
(1)
$2,000,000 shall be used to carry out section 308302 of this title; and
(2)
$500,000 shall be used to carry out section 308303 of this title.
(b) Limitation.—
No amount may be appropriated for the purposes of this chapter except to the Secretary for carrying out the responsibilities of the Secretary as set forth in this chapter.
