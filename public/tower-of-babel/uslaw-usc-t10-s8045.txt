10 U.S.C. § 8045 — Deputy Commandants
Citation: us:usc:10:8045
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8045
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization); Chapter 806 (Headquarters, Marine Corps). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are in the Headquarters, Marine Corps, not more than eight Deputy Commandants, detailed by the Secretary of the Navy from officers on the active-duty list of the Marine Corps.
