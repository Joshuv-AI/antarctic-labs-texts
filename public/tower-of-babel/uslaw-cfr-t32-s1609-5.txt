32 C.F.R. § 1609.5 — Suspension.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1609.5
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1609.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1609 (Uncompensated Personnel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director of Selective Service may suspend from duty any uncompensated person engaged in the administration of the Selective Service Law pending his consideration of the advisability of removing such person.
