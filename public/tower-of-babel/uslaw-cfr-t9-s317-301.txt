9 C.F.R. § 317.301 — Required nutrition labeling of ground or chopped meat products.
Citation: us:cfr:9:317.301
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-317.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 317 (Labeling, Marking Devices, And Containers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Nutrition labels must be provided for all ground or chopped products (livestock species) and hamburger with or without added seasonings (including, but not limited to, ground beef, ground beef patties, ground sirloin, ground pork, and ground lamb) that are intended for human consumption and offered for sale, in accordance with the provisions of § 317.309, except as exempted under § 317.400.
