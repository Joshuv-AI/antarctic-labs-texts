42 U.S.C. § 4952 — Authority to operate VISTA program
Citation: us:usc:42:4952
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section4952
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 66 (Domestic Volunteer Services); Subchapter I (National Volunteer Antipoverty Programs); Part A (Volunteers In Service To America). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This part shall be administered by one of the Assistant Directors appointed pursuant to section 12651e(d)(1)(A) of this title. Such Director may recruit, select, and train persons to serve in full-time volunteer programs consistent with the provisions and to carry out the purpose of this part.
