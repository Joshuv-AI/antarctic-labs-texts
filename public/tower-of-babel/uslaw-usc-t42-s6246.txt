42 U.S.C. § 6246 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:42:6246
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section6246
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 77 (Energy Conservation); Subchapter I (Domestic Supply Availability); Part B (Strategic Petroleum Reserve). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary such sums as are necessary to carry out this part and part D, to remain available until expended.
