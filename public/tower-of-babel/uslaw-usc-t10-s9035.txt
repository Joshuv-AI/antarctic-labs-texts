10 U.S.C. § 9035 — Deputy Chiefs of Staff and Assistant Chiefs of Staff
Citation: us:usc:10:9035
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9035
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization); Chapter 905 (The Air Staff). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Deputy Chiefs of Staff and the Assistant Chiefs of Staff shall be general officers detailed to those positions.
(b)
The Secretary of the Air Force shall prescribe the number of Deputy Chiefs of Staff and Assistant Chiefs of Staff, for a total of not more than eight positions.
