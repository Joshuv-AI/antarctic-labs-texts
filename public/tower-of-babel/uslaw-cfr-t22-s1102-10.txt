22 C.F.R. § 1102.10 — Examination of records.
Citation: us:cfr:22:1102.10
Creator: International Boundary And Water Commission, United States And Mexico, United States Section
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1102.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1102 (Freedom Of Information Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a request to examine records is approved by the FOIA Officer, every reasonable effort will be made to provide facilities for the purpose of such examination. “On the spot” copying will be available if the FOIA Officer decides there will be no interference with ordinary activities or routine business of the section.
