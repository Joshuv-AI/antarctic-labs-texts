10 U.S.C. § 15109 — Other administrative authorities
Citation: us:usc:10:15109
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Promotion And Retention Of Officers On The Reserve Active-Status List); Chapter 1413 (Alternative Promotion Authority For Officers In Designated Competitive Categories). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The following provisions of this title shall apply to officers in competitive categories of officers designated for purposes of this chapter:
(1)
Section 14518, relating to continuation of officers to complete disciplinary action.
(2)
Section 14519, relating to deferment of retirement or separation for medical reasons.
(3)
Section 14704, relating to the selective early removal from the reserve active-status list.
(4)
Section 14705, relating to the selective early retirement of reserve general and flag officers of the Navy and Marine Corps.
