15 U.S.C. § 1268 — Proceedings in name of United States; subpenas
Citation: us:usc:15:1268
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1268
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 30 (Hazardous Substances). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All criminal proceedings and all libel or injunction proceedings for the enforcement, or to restrain violations, of this chapter shall be by and in the name of the United States. Subpenas for witnesses who are required to attend a court of the United States in any district may run into any other district in any such proceeding.
