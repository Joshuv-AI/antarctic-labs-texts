17 C.F.R. § 39.50 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:17:39.50
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-39.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 39 (Derivatives Clearing Organizations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart D apply to any derivatives clearing organization that is registered through the process described in § 39.3(a)(3) of this part or as otherwise provided by order of the Commission.
