7 C.F.R. § 227.43 — Participation of adults.
Citation: us:cfr:7:227.43
Creator: Food And Nutrition Administration, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-227.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 227 (Nutrition Education And Training Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this part shall prohibit a State or local educational agency from making available or distributing to adults education materials, resources, activities or programs authorized by this part.
