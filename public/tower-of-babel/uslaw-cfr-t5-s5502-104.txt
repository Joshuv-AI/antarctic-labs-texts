5 C.F.R. § 5502.104 — Confidentiality of reports.
Citation: us:cfr:5:5502.104
Creator: Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-5502.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 5502 (Supplemental Financial Disclosure Requirements For Employees Of The Department Of Health And Human Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each report filed under this part is confidential and shall not be disclosed to the public, except as provided under § 2634.604(b) of this title.
