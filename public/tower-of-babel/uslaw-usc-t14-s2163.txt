14 U.S.C. § 2163 — Removal of officer from active duty; action by Secretary
Citation: us:usc:14:2163
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2163
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 21 (Personnel; Officers); Subchapter II (Discharges; Retirements; Revocation Of Commissions; Separation For Cause). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may remove an officer from active duty if his removal is recommended by a board of inquiry under section 2159 of this title. The Secretary’s action in such as case is final and conclusive.
