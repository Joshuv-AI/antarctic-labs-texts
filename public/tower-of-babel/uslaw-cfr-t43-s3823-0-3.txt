43 C.F.R. § 3823.0-3 — -3 Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3823.0-3
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3823.0-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3820 (Areas Subject To Special Mining Laws). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart sets forth procedures to be followed by persons wishing to prospect on lands within National Forest Wilderness, and special provisions pertaining to mineral locations and mineral patents within National Forest Wilderness.
