13 C.F.R. § 126.100 — What is the purpose of the HUBZone program?
Citation: us:cfr:13:126.100
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-126.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 126 (Hubzone Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of the HUBZone program is to provide federal contracting assistance for small business concerns located in historically underutilized business zones in an effort to increase employment opportunities, investment, and economic development in such areas.
