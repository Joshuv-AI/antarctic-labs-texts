7 U.S.C. § 2615 — Finding and issuance of plan
Citation: us:usc:7:2615
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2615
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 58 (Potato Research And Promotion). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

After notice and opportunity for hearing, the Secretary shall issue a plan if he finds, and sets forth in such plan, upon the evidence introduced at such hearing, that the issuance of such plan and all the terms and conditions thereof will tend to effectuate the declared policy of this chapter.
