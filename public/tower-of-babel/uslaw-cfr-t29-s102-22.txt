29 C.F.R. § 102.22 — Extension of time for filing.
Citation: us:cfr:29:102.22
Creator: National Labor Relations Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-102.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 102 (Rules And Regulations, Series 8). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon the Regional Director's own motion or upon proper cause shown by any other party, the Regional Director issuing the complaint may by written order extend the time within which the answer must be filed.
