12 C.F.R. § 790.3 — Requests for action.
Citation: us:cfr:12:790.3
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-790.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 790 (Description Of Ncua; Requests For Agency Action). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise provided by NCUA regulation, all applications, requests, and submittals for action by the NCUA shall be in writing and addressed to the appropriate office described in § 790.2. This will usually be one of the Regional Offices. In instances where the appropriate office cannot be determined, requests should be sent to the Office of External Affairs and Communications.
