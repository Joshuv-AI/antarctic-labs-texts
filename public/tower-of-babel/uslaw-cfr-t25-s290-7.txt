25 C.F.R. § 290.7 — Must an Indian tribe have a tribal revenue allocation plan if it is not making per capita payments?
Citation: us:cfr:25:290.7
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-290.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 290 (Tribal Revenue Allocation Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No, if you do not make per capita payments, you do not need to submit a tribal revenue allocation plan.
