Superoptimal continued fractions
Slade Sanderson

Abstract
. Motivated by the optimal continued fractions studied independently by Selenius and Bosma, we define and introduce algorithms producing superoptimal continued fraction expansions of irrationals. These expansions simultaneously provide arbitrarily good rational approximations and converge arbitrarily quickly.
