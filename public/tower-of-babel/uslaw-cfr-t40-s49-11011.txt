40 C.F.R. § 49.11011 — Identification of plan.
Citation: us:cfr:40:49.11011
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-49.11011
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 49 (Indian Country: Air Quality Planning And Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This section and §§ 49.11012 through 49.11040 contain the implementation plan for the Confederated Tribes of the Umatilla Reservation. This plan consists of a combination of Tribal rules and measures and Federal regulations and measures which apply within the Umatilla Reservation.
