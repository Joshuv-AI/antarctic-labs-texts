49 C.F.R. § 178.1000 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:49:178.1000
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-178.1000
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 178 (Specifications For Packagings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) This subpart prescribes requirements for Flexible Bulk Containers (FBCs) intended for the transportation of hazardous materials. FBC standards in this subpart are based on the UN Model Regulations.
(b) Terms used in this subpart are defined in § 171.8 of this subchapter.
