24 C.F.R. § 290.33 — Sale of delinquent mortgages securing subsidized projects.
Citation: us:cfr:24:290.33
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-290.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 290 (Disposition Of Multifamily Projects And Sale Of Hud-Held Multifamily Mortgages). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Delinquent mortgages securing subsidized projects will be sold only if, as part of the sales transaction:
(a) The mortgages are restructured; and
(b) Either FHA mortgage insurance or equivalent protections are provided.
