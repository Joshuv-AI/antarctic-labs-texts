41 C.F.R. § 301-10.180 — -10.180 U.S. flag ship requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-10.180
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-10.180
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-10 (10—Transportation Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When authorized to travel by ship, employees must use a U.S. flag ship when available, unless the mission's necessity requires using a foreign ship. (See 46 U.S.C. 55302.)
