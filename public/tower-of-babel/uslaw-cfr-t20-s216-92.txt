20 C.F.R. § 216.92 — Entitlement as a spouse or divorced spouse and as a survivor.
Citation: us:cfr:20:216.92
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-216.92
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 216 (Eligibility For An Annuity). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an individual is entitled to both a spouse or divorced spouse and survivor annuity, only the larger annuity will be paid. However, if the individual so chooses, he or she can receive the smaller annuity rather than the larger annuity.
