5 C.F.R. § 531.503 — Purpose of quality step increases.
Citation: us:cfr:5:531.503
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-531.503
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 531 (Pay Under The General Schedule). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of quality step increases is to provide appropriate incentives and recognition for excellence in performance by granting faster than normal step increases.
