32 C.F.R. § 1633.4 — Information relating to claims for deferment or exemption.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1633.4
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1633.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1633 (Administration Of Classification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The registrant shall be entitled to present all relevant written information which he believes to be necessary to assist the classifying authority in determining his proper classification; such information may include documents, affidavits, and depositions. The affidavits and depositions shall be as concise and brief as possible.
