10 U.S.C. § 649i — Continuation on active duty: officers in certain military specialties and career tracks
Citation: us:usc:10:649i
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section649i
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 36 (Promotion, Separation, And Involuntary Retirement Of Officers On The Active-Duty List); Subchapter VI (Alternative Promotion Authority For Officers In Designated Competitive Categories). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In addition to continuation on active duty provided for in section 649h of this title, an officer to whom section 637a of this title applies may be continued on active duty in accordance with the provisions of such section 637a.
