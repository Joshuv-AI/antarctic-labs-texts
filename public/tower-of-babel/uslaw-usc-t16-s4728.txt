16 U.S.C. § 4728 — Brown tree snake control program
Citation: us:usc:16:4728
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section4728
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 67 (Aquatic Nuisance Prevention And Control); Subchapter III (Prevention And Control Of Aquatic Nuisance Species Dispersal). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Task Force shall, within the program developed under section 4722(a) of this title, undertake a comprehensive, environmentally sound program in coordination with regional, territorial, State and local entities to control the brown tree snake (Boiga irregularis) in Guam and other areas where the species is established outside of its historic range.
