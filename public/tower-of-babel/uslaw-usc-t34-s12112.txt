34 U.S.C. § 12112 — Report by Attorney General
Citation: us:usc:34:12112
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section12112
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 121 (Violent Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Subchapter I (Prisons); Part A (Violent Offender Incarceration And Truth-In-Sentencing Incentive Grants). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Beginning on October 1, 1996, and each subsequent July 1 thereafter, the Attorney General shall report to the Congress on the implementation of this part, including a report on the eligibility of the States under sections 12103 and 12104 of this title, and the distribution and use of funds under this part.
