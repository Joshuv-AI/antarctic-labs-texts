19 U.S.C. § 1556 — Bonded warehouses; regulations for establishing
Citation: us:usc:19:1556
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section1556
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 4 (Tariff Act Of 1930); Part IV (Transportation In Bond And Warehousing Of Merchandise). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Treasury shall from time to time establish such rules and regulations as may be necessary for the establishment of bonded warehouses and to protect the interests of the Government in the conduct, management, and operation of such warehouses and in the withdrawal of and accounting for merchandise deposited therein.
