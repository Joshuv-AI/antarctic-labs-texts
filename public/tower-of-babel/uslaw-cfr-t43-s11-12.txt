43 C.F.R. § 11.12 — Biennial review of regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:43:11.12
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-11.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 11 (Natural Resource Damage Assessments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations and procedures included within this part shall be reviewed and revised as appropriate 2 years from the effective date of these rules and every second anniversary thereafter.
