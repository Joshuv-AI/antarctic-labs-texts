7 U.S.C. § 1449 — Determination of price support level
Citation: us:usc:7:1449
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1449
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 35A (Price Support Of Agricultural Commodities); Subchapter III (Nonbasic Agricultural Commodities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In determining the level of price support for any nonbasic agricultural commodity under sections 1447 to 1449 of this title, particular consideration shall be given to the levels at which the prices of competing agricultural commodities are being supported.
