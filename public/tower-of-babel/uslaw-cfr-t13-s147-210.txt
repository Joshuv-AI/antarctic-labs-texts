13 C.F.R. § 147.210 — To whom must I distribute my drug-free workplace statement?
Citation: us:cfr:13:147.210
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-147.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 147 (Governmentwide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must require that a copy of the statement described in § 147.205 be given to each employee who will be engaged in the performance of any Federal award.
