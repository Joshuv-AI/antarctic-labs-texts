41 C.F.R. § 301-30.2 — -30.2 Procedure for interrupting or discontinuing TDY travel.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-30.2
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-30.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-30 (30—Employee Emergency Travel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees must contact their travel authorizing/approving official for instructions as soon as possible when needing to interrupt or discontinue TDY.
