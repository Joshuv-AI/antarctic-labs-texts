25 C.F.R. § 248.7 — Liability for condition and use of structures.
Citation: us:cfr:25:248.7
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-248.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 248 (Use Of Columbia River Indian In-Lieu Fishing Sites). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any private structures including drying sheds, tents, tepees, or fishing platforms erected, placed, or maintained on the sites are the sole responsibility of their owners, and all use of such structures shall be at the user's or owner's sole responsibility and risk. Neither the United States nor any officer or employee thereof warrants, makes any representation, or is responsible for the safety or condition of any such structure.
