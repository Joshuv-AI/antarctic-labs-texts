9 C.F.R. § 327.24 — Appeals; how made.
Citation: us:cfr:9:327.24
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-327.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 327 (Imported Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any appeal of a decision or action of any program employee will be made to his/her immediate supervisor having responsibility over the subject matter of the appeal in accordance with 9 CFR 500.9.
