42 U.S.C. § 3020a — Application of other laws; costs of projects under this chapter not treated as income or benefits under other laws
Citation: us:usc:42:3020a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section3020a
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 35 (Programs For Older Americans); Subchapter II (Administration On Aging). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The provisions and requirements of chapter 71 of title 31 shall not apply to the administration of the provisions of this chapter or to the administration of any program or activity under this chapter.
(b)
No part of the costs of any project under any subchapter of this chapter may be treated as income or benefits to any eligible individual (other than any wage or salary to such individual) for the purpose of any other program or provision of Federal or State law.
