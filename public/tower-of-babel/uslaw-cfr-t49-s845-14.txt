49 C.F.R. § 845.14 — Transcript.
Citation: us:cfr:49:845.14
Creator: National Transportation Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-845.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 845 (Rules Of Practice In Transportation: Investigative Hearings; Meetings, Reports, And Petitions For Reconsideration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A verbatim report of the investigative hearing shall be taken. Any interested person may obtain copies of the transcript from the NTSB or from the court reporting firm preparing the transcript upon payment of the fees fixed therefor. (See part 801, subpart G, Fee schedule.)
