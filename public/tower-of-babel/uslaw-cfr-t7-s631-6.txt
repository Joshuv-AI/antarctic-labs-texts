7 C.F.R. § 631.6 — Land eligible for the program.
Citation: us:cfr:7:631.6
Creator: Natural Resources Conservation Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-631.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 631 (Great Plains Conservation Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The program is applicable to:
(a) Privately owned land,
(b) Nonfederally owned public land under private control for the contract period and included in the participant's operating unit, and
(c) Federally owned land, if installation of conservation practices would directly benefit nearby or adjoining privately owned land of persons who maintain and use the Federal land.
