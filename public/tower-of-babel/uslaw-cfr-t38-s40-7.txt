38 C.F.R. § 40.7 — Communicating with State and local officials concerning VA's programs and activities.
Citation: us:cfr:38:40.7
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-40.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 40 (Intergovernmental Review Of Department Of Veterans Affairs Programs And Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary provides notice to directly affected State, areawide, regional, and local entities in a State of proposed Federal financial assistance or direct Federal development if:
(a) The State has not adopted a process under the order; or
(b) The assistance or development involves a program or activity not selected for the State process.
