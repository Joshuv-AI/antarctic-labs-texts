20 U.S.C. § 7841 — Purposes
Citation: us:usc:20:7841
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section7841
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 70 (Strengthening And Improvement Of Elementary And Secondary Schools); Subchapter VIII (General Provisions); Part C (Coordination Of Programs; Consolidated State And Local Plans And Applications). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purposes of this part are—
(1)
to improve teaching and learning by encouraging greater cross-program coordination, planning, and service delivery;
(2)
to provide greater flexibility to State and local authorities through consolidated plans, applications, and reporting; and
(3)
to enhance the integration of programs under this chapter with State and local programs.
