Mixed state topological order: operator algebraic approach
Yoshiko Ogata

Abstract
We study the classification problem of mixed states in two-dimensional quantum spin systems in the operator algebraic framework of quantum statistical mechanics. We associate a braided C ∗ C^{*} -tensor category to each state satisfying a mixed-state version of the approximate Haag duality. We study how this category behaves under decoherence: suppose the state is acted by a finite depth quantum channel. We prove that the braided C ∗ C^{*} -tensor category of the final state is a braided C ∗ C^{*} -tensor subcategory of the initial state.

1.3
Cone von Neumann algebras

Let
\omega
be a state on a
2
-dimensional quantum spin system
{\mathcal{A}}
with a GNS representation
({\mathcal{H}},\pi)
.
For each cone
\Lambda
in
{\mathbb{Z}}^{2}
, we consider the local von Neumann algebra

\pi({\mathcal{A}}_{\Lambda})^{\prime\prime}
.
With a bit of abuse of notation, we say that
\omega
has properly infinite cone algebras
if
\pi({\mathcal{A}}_{\Lambda})^{\prime\prime}
is properly infinite for any cone
\Lambda
.
By the stabilization procedure, namely, by tensoring a pure infinite tensor product state,
we can always move to a state with properly infinite cone algebras:

Lemma 1.2
.

Let
\omega
be a state on a
2
-dimensional quantum spin system
{\mathcal{A}}
.
Let
{\mathcal{B}}
be another two-dimensional quantum spin system and

\psi
a pure infinite tensor product state on
{\mathcal{B}}
.
Then the state
\omega\otimes\psi
on
{\mathcal{A}}\otimes{\mathcal{B}}
has properly infinite cone algebras.

If
({\mathcal{H}},\pi)
,
({\mathcal{H}}_{\psi},\pi_{\psi})
are GNS representations of
\omega
,
\psi
respectively,
then
({\mathcal{H}}\otimes{\mathcal{H}}_{\psi},\pi\otimes\pi_{\psi})
is a GNS representation of

\omega\otimes\psi
.
Because
\psi
is a pure infinite tensor product state, the cone von Neumann algebra associated to
a cone
\Lambda
is
of the form

\pi({\mathcal{A}}_{\Lambda})^{\prime\prime}\bar{\otimes}\pi_{\psi}({\mathcal{B}}_{\Lambda})^{\prime\prime}

with
\pi_{\psi}({\mathcal{B}}_{\Lambda})^{\prime\prime}
a type
{\mathrm{I}}_{\infty}
-von Neumann algebra.
The center of this algebra is

{\mathcal{Z}}\left(\pi({\mathcal{A}}_{\Lambda})^{\prime\prime}\right)\bar{\otimes}{\mathbb{C}}\mathbb{I}

(see IV Corollary 5.11
[
T+02
]
).
Because
\pi_{\psi}({\mathcal{B}}_{\Lambda})^{\prime\prime}
is an infinite factor,
there exists an isometry
v\in\pi_{\psi}({\mathcal{B}}_{\Lambda})^{\prime\prime}
with
vv^{*}\lneq\mathbb{I}
.
Therefore, for any nonzero central projection
z\otimes\mathbb{I}\in{\mathcal{Z}}\left(\pi({\mathcal{A}}_{\Lambda})^{\prime\prime}\right)\bar{\otimes}{\mathbb{C}}\mathbb{I}
,
we have

\displaystyle\begin{split}(z\otimes v)^{*}(z\otimes v)=z\otimes\mathbb{I},\quad(z\otimes v)(z\otimes v)^{*}=z\otimes vv^{*}\lneq z\otimes\mathbb{I}.\end{split}

(1.3)

Because
z\otimes v\in\pi({\mathcal{A}}_{\Lambda})^{\prime\prime}\bar{\otimes}\pi_{\psi}({\mathcal{B}}_{\Lambda})^{\prime\prime}
, this means
z\otimes\mathbb{I}
is infinite
in
\pi({\mathcal{A}}_{\Lambda})^{\prime\prime}\bar{\otimes}\pi_{\psi}({\mathcal{B}}_{\Lambda})^{\prime\prime}
. Hence
\pi({\mathcal{A}}_{\Lambda})^{\prime\prime}\bar{\otimes}\pi_{\psi}({\mathcal{B}}_{\Lambda})^{\prime\prime}

is properly infinite.

\square

Therefore, by tensoring a pure infinite tensor product state, we can always make a
state with properly infinite cone algebras.

.

Let us first recall the definition of approximately-factorizable automorphisms.

Definition 5.1
.

Let
\alpha
be an automorphism of a two-dimensional quantum spin system
{\mathcal{A}}
.
We say that
\alpha
is approximately-factorizable
if the following condition holds.

(i)

For any cone
\Lambda
and
\delta>0
,
there are automorphisms
\alpha_{\Lambda},\tilde{\alpha}_{\Lambda}\in\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits\left({\mathcal{A}}_{\Lambda}\right)

\alpha_{\Lambda^{c}},\tilde{\alpha}_{\Lambda^{c}}\in\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits\left({\mathcal{A}}_{\Lambda^{c}}\right)

and unitaries

such that

\displaystyle\begin{split}&\alpha=\mathop{\mathrm{Ad}}\nolimits\left(v_{\Lambda\delta}\right)\circ\Xi_{\Lambda,\delta}\circ\left(\alpha_{\Lambda}\otimes\alpha_{\Lambda^{c}}\right),\\
&\alpha^{-1}=\mathop{\mathrm{Ad}}\nolimits\left(\tilde{v}_{\Lambda\delta}\right)\circ\tilde{\Xi}_{\Lambda,\delta}\circ\left(\tilde{\alpha}_{\Lambda}\otimes\tilde{\alpha}_{\Lambda^{c}}\right).\end{split}

(5.1)

(ii)

For each
\delta,\delta^{\prime}>0,\varphi\in(0,2\pi)
, there exists a decreasing
function
g_{\varphi,\delta,\delta^{\prime}}(t)
on
{\mathbb{R}}_{\geq 0}

with
\lim_{t\to\infty}g_{\varphi,\delta,\delta^{\prime}}(t)=0
.
For any cone
\Lambda
with
\varphi=|\arg\Lambda|
, there are unitaries

v_{\Lambda,\delta,\delta^{\prime},t}^{\prime},\tilde{v}_{\Lambda,\delta,\delta^{\prime},t}^{\prime}\in{\mathcal{A}}_{\Lambda_{\delta+\delta^{\prime}}-t\bm{e}_{\Lambda}}

satisfying

\displaystyle\left\|v_{\Lambda,\delta}-v_{\Lambda,\delta,\delta^{\prime},t}^{\prime}\right\|,\left\|\tilde{v}_{\Lambda,\delta}-\tilde{v}_{\Lambda,\delta,\delta^{\prime},t}^{\prime}\right\|\leq g_{\varphi,\delta,\delta^{\prime}}(t),

(5.2)

for unitaries
v_{\Lambda\delta},\tilde{v}_{\Lambda\delta}
in (i).

We note that approximately factorizable automorphisms preserve
the approximate Haag duality and the properly infiniteness of cone algebras.

Lemma 5.2
.

Let
\omega
be a state on a two-dimensional quantum spin system
{\mathcal{A}}

with properly infinite cone algebras, satisfying the approximate Haag duality.
Let

[

]

\Lambda

\displaystyle\begin{split}\pi\alpha({\mathcal{A}}_{\Lambda})=\mathop{\mathrm{Ad}}\nolimits\left(\pi(v_{\Lambda\delta})\right)\left(\pi\Xi_{\Lambda\delta}({\mathcal{A}}_{\Lambda})\right)\supset\mathop{\mathrm{Ad}}\nolimits\left(\pi(v_{\Lambda\delta})\right)\left(\pi({\mathcal{A}}_{\Lambda_{-\delta}})\right),\end{split}

(5.3)

and the von Neumann algebra
\pi\alpha({\mathcal{A}}_{\Lambda})^{\prime\prime}
includes

\mathop{\mathrm{Ad}}\nolimits\left(\pi(v_{\Lambda\delta})\right)\left(\pi({\mathcal{A}}_{\Lambda_{-\delta}})^{\prime\prime}\right)
.
Hence
\pi\alpha({\mathcal{A}}_{\Lambda})^{\prime\prime}
is properly infinite,
and
\omega\alpha
has properly infinite cone algebras.

\square

Now we prove Theorem
1.12
.

We denote by
\alpha_{13}
the automorphism

[

the braided

\mathfrak{A}
is denoted by
{\mathcal{U}}(\mathfrak{A})
.
For a state
\omega
on a
C^{*}
-algebra
\mathfrak{A}
, when we write

({\mathcal{H}}_{\omega},\pi_{\omega},\Omega_{\omega})
, it means a GNS triple
of
\omega
.
For two UHF algebras
\mathfrak{A}
,
\mathfrak{B}
, we occasionally denote the sub-algebra

\mathfrak{A}\otimes\mathbb{I}_{\mathfrak{B}}
(resp.
\mathbb{I}_{\mathfrak{A}}\otimes\mathfrak{B}
) of

\mathfrak{A}\otimes\mathfrak{B}
by

\mathfrak{A}
(resp.
\mathfrak{B}
).
With the same spirit, we frequently denote
A\otimes\mathbb{I}_{\mathfrak{B}},\mathbb{I}_{\mathfrak{A}}\otimes B\in\mathfrak{A}\otimes\mathfrak{B}
by
A
,
B
respectively.
We denote by
\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits(\mathfrak{A})
the automorphism group of
\mathfrak{A}
.

For a von Neumann algebra
{\mathcal{M}}
, we denote its center by
Z({\mathcal{M}})
.
We denote by
Z_{{\mathcal{M}}}(x)
the central carrier of
x\in{\mathcal{M}}
in
{\mathcal{M}}
.
For von Neumann algebras
{\mathcal{M}}
,
{\mathcal{N}}
,
{\mathcal{M}}\bar{\otimes}{\mathcal{N}}
is the von Neumann algebra
tensor product of them.

For a Hilbert space
{\mathcal{H}}
,
{\mathcal{B}}({\mathcal{H}})
denotes the algebra of all bounded operators
on
{\mathcal{H}}
. The identity map on a Hilbert space
{\mathcal{H}}
is denoted by
\mathbb{I}_{{\mathcal{H}}}
, but the subscript is occationally
omitted. For a state
\omega
,
\mathbb{I}_{{\mathcal{H}}_{\omega}}

is also denoted by
\mathbb{I}_{\omega}
for simplicity.
For operators
V,x
on a Hilbert space
{\mathcal{H}}
, we set
\mathop{\mathrm{Ad}}\nolimits V(x):=VxV^{*}
.
