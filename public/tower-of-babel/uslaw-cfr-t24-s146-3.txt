24 C.F.R. § 146.3 — Purpose of HUD's age discrimination regulation.
Citation: us:cfr:24:146.3
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Equal Opportunity, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-146.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 146 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Age In Hud Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to state HUD's policies and procedures under the Age Discrimination Act of 1975, consistent with the government-wide age discrimination regulation contained at 45 CFR part 90.
