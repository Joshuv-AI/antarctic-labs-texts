21 C.F.R. § 1210.11 — Sanitary inspection of dairy farms.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1210.11
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1210.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1210 (Regulations Under The Federal Import Milk Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The sanitary conditions of any dairy farm producing milk or cream to be shipped or transported into the United States or to a plant from which milk or cream is to be shipped or transported into the United States must score at least 50 points out of 100 points, according to the methods for scoring as provided by the score card for sanitary inspection of dairy farms in the form prescribed by the Secretary.
