42 U.S.C. § 9706 — Dissemination of information
Citation: us:usc:42:9706
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section9706
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 104 (Nuclear Safety Research, Development, And Demonstration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall assure that full and complete safety-related information resulting from any project or other activity conducted under this chapter is made available in a timely manner to appropriate committees of Congress, Federal, State, and local authorities, relevant segments of private industry, the scientific community, and the public.
