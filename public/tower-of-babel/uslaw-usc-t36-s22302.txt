36 U.S.C. § 22302 — Purposes

Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 223 (American Symphony Orchestra League). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purposes of the corporation are—
(1)
to serve as a coordinating, research, and educational agency and clearinghouse for symphony orchestras to help strengthen the work in their local communities;
(2)
to assist in the formation of new symphony orchestras;
(3)
to encourage and recognize the work of America’s musicians, conductors, and composers, through suitable means; and
(4)
to aid the expansion of the musical and cultural life of the United States through suitable educational and service activities.
