10 C.F.R. § 1017.2 — Applicability.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1017 (Identification And Protection Of Unclassified Controlled Nuclear Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to any person who is or was authorized access to UCNI, requires authorized access to UCNI, or attempts to gain or gains unauthorized access to UCNI.
