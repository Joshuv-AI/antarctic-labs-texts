41 C.F.R. § 102-38.110 — -38.110 Unused or unauthorized retention of sales proceeds.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-38.110
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-38.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-38 (38—Sale Of Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any sales proceeds that are not retained pursuant to the authorities in § 102-38.105 must be deposited as miscellaneous receipts in the U.S. Treasury.
