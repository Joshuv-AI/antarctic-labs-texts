22 U.S.C. § 1631m — Lien, attachment, garnishment, etc., of transferred property
Citation: us:usc:22:1631m
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1631m
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 21 (Settlement Of International Claims); Subchapter II (Vesting And Liquidation Of Bulgarian, Hungarian, And Rumanian Property). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No property conveyed, transferred, assigned, delivered, or paid to the designee of the President under this subchapter, or the net proceeds thereof, shall be liable to lien, attachment, garnishment, trustee process, or execution, or subject to any order or decree of any court, except as provided in this subchapter.
