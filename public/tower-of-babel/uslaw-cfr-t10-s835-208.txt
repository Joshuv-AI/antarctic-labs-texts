10 C.F.R. § 835.208 — Limits for members of the public entering a controlled area.
Citation: us:cfr:10:835.208
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-835.208
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 835 (Occupational Radiation Protection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The total effective dose limit for members of the public exposed to radiation and/or radioactive material during access to a controlled area is 0.1 rem (0.001 Sv) in a year.
