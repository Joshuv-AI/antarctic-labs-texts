25 C.F.R. § 84.005 — Will the Secretary approve contracts or agreements even where such approval is not required under this part?
Citation: us:cfr:25:84.005
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-84.005
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 84 (Encumbrances Of Tribal Land—Contract Approvals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No, the Secretary will not approve contracts or agreements that do not encumber tribal lands for a period of seven or more years. Within thirty days after receipt of final, executed documents, the Secretary will return such contracts and agreements with a statement explaining why Secretarial approval is not required. The provisions of the Act will not apply to those contracts or agreements the Secretary determines are not covered by the Act.
