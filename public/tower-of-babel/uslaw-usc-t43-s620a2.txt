43 U.S.C. § 620a–2 — Establishment of nonexcess irrigable acreage for participating projects
Citation: us:usc:43:620a–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section620a–2
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 12B (Colorado River Storage Project). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall, for the Animas-La Plata, Dolores, Dallas Creek, San Miguel, West Divide, and Seedskadee participating projects of the Colorado River storage project, establish the nonexcess irrigable acreage for which any single ownership may receive project water at one hundred and sixty acres of class 1 land or the equivalent thereof, as determined by the Secretary, in other land classes.
