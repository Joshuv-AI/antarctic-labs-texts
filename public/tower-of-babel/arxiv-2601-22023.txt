Introduction

Substructural logics form a wide family of nonclassical logics that can be roughly defined as those logic systems such that, when presented by means of a Gentzen-style calculus, lack some of the structural rules (exchange, contraction and weakening), which occur in standard sequent systems of classical and intuitionistic logics
Galatos

\mathbf{FL}
of full Lambek calculus, the weakest logic considered in substructural logics, is obtained from the Gentzen system
\mathbf{LJ}
for intuitionistic propositional logic by removing three structural rules. As a very important extension of
\mathbf{FL}
, the sequent calculus
\mathbf{FL_{ew}}
is also obtained from the intuitionistic logic by only deleting the contraction rule. Researchers always call substructural logics over
\mathbf{FL_{ew}}
or logics without contraction rule
Ono

Chang
and fuzzy logics in the sense of Hájek
Hajeka

\mathbf{FL_{ew}}
and the variety of residuated lattices

Let

For any

Let
L_{\mathcal{V}}
and
L_{\mathcal{W}}
be the languages of the varieties

\mathcal{V}
and
\mathcal{W}
, respectively. A
2

preserving arities. Such a
translation extends uniquely to terms, giving rise to a map

defined recursively on variables, constants, and compound terms. This extension
further induces a transformation on sets of equations.

A
2
-contextual translation of
\models_{\mathcal{V}}
into

is a

Theorem 5.1
.

The algebra
\mathbf{C}(\mathbf{F}_{\mathbb{MDRDL^{\prime}}}(1))

n\geq 1

Proof.

Observe that the first part is an adaptation of Theorem 7 of
CPZ2024

For the

is an adaptation of

Assume, as induction hypothesis, that

We show that the statement holds for

Indeed, since both functors
\mathbf{C}
and
\mathbf{F}_{\mathbb{MDRDL^{\prime}}}

preserve coproducts, and

\begin{array}[]{rcl}\mathbf{C}(\mathbf{F}_{\mathbb{MDRDL^{\prime}}}(k+1))&\cong&\mathbf{C}(\mathbf{F}_{\mathbb{MDRDL^{\prime}}}(k))\,+\,\mathbf{C}(\mathbf{F}_{\mathbb{MDRDL^{\prime}}}(1))\\[3.99994pt]

6
Conclusions and further work

The motivation of this paper is to lift the relationship between the categories
\mathbb{RDL}
and
\mathbb{DRDL^{\prime}}
to the categories
\mathbb{MRDL}
and
\mathbb{MDRDL^{\prime}}
. In order to achieve our aim, we first reviewed some of their algebraic results related to monadic residuated lattices and obtained the notion of monadic involutive residuated lattices. The results of Subsection 4.1 established a bijection that maps a monadic residuated distributive lattice into a monadic c-differential residuated distributive lattice that satisfy
\mathbf{CK}
. This map extends to a functor that is a categorical equivalence in Subsection 4.2. Subsection 4.2 showed that there exists a categorical equivalence between the categories
\mathbb{MRDL}
and
\mathbb{MDRDL^{\prime}}
.

The paper is devoted to transferring the balance between the categories
\mathbb{MRDL}
and
\mathbb{MDRDL^{\prime}}
. There is also an open problem that establish the corresponding relation between the categories
\mathbb{MDFL}_{\textmd{e}}
and
\mathbb{MCDFL^{\prime}}_{\textmd{e}}
, whose objects are monadic distributive FL
{}_{\textrm{e}}
-algebras and monadic distributive c-differential FL
{}_{\textrm{e}}
-algebras with a special condition.
The critical point for obtaining this more abroad and more common result is to reconstruct the categorical equivalence between the categories
\mathbb{DFL}_{\textmd{e}}
and
\mathbb{CDFL^{\prime}}_{\textmd{e}}
, because Kalman functor
\mathbf{K}

As an application of the adjunction

we obtained a
a finite, nontrivial

relation of
\mathbb{MDRDL^{\prime}}
into that of
\mathbb{MRDL}
. The key step was an
explicit description of the algebras
