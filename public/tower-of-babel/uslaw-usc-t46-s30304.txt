46 U.S.C. § 30304 — Contributory negligence
Citation: us:usc:46:30304
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section30304
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 303 (Death On The High Seas). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In an action under this chapter, contributory negligence of the decedent is not a bar to recovery. The court shall consider the degree of negligence of the decedent and reduce the recovery accordingly.
