23 C.F.R. § 624.1 — Purpose.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 624 (Interstate System Access). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To prescribe requirements and procedures for State requests for, and FHWA consideration of, changes in access to the Interstate System.
