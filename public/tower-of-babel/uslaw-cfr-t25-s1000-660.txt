25 C.F.R. § 1000.660 — Do Tribes/Consortium need Secretarial approval to redesign BIA programs that the Tribe/Consortium administers under a funding agreement?
Citation: us:cfr:25:1000.660
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary, Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-1000.660
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 1000 (Annual Funding Agreements Under The Tribal Self-Government Act Amendments To The Indian Self-Determination And Education Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No, the Secretary does not have to approve a redesign of a program under the funding agreement, except when the redesign involves:
(a) Programs described in 25 U.S.C. 5363(b)(2) or (c); or
(b) A request to waive a regulation.
