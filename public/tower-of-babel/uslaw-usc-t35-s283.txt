35 U.S.C. § 283 — Injunction
Citation: us:usc:35:283
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title35-section283
Context: United States Code, Title 35 (Patents); Part III (Patents And Protection Of Patent Rights); Chapter 29 (Remedies For Infringement Of Patent, And Other Actions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The several courts having jurisdiction of cases under this title may grant injunctions in accordance with the principles of equity to prevent the violation of any right secured by patent, on such terms as the court deems reasonable.
