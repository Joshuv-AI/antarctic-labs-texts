22 U.S.C. § 286e–1g — Additional increase in quota of United States; condition
Citation: us:usc:22:286e–1g
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section286e–1g
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter XV (International Monetary Fund And Bank For Reconstruction And Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States Governor of the Fund is authorized to consent to an increase in the quota of the United States in the Fund equivalent to 4,202.5 million Special Drawing Rights, limited to such amounts as are appropriated in advance in appropriation Acts.
