48 C.F.R. § 7.500 — 7.500 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:48:7.500
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-7.500
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 7 (Acquisition Planning). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this subpart is to prescribe policies and procedures to ensure that inherently governmental functions are not performed by contractors.
