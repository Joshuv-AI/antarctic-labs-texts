41 C.F.R. § 105-64.401 — -64.401 Can I amend my record?
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-64.401
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-64.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-64 (64—Gsa Privacy Act Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may request to amend your record by writing to the system manager with the proposed amendment. Mark both the envelope and the letter “Privacy Act Request to Amend Record”.
