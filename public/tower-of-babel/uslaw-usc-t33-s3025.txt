33 U.S.C. § 3025 — Length of service for promotion purposes
Citation: us:usc:33:3025
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section3025
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 43 (National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration Commissioned Officer Corps); Subchapter II (Appointment And Promotion Of Officers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) General rule
Each officer shall be assumed to have, for promotion purposes, at least the same length of service as any other officer below that officer on the lineal list.
(b) Exception
Notwithstanding subsection (a), an officer who has lost numbers shall be assumed to have, for promotion purposes, no greater service than the officer next above such officer in such officer’s new position on the lineal list.
