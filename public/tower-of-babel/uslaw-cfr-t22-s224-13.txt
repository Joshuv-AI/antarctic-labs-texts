22 C.F.R. § 224.13 — Parties to the hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:22:224.13
Creator: Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-224.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 224 (Implementation Of The Program Fraud Civil Remedies Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The parties to the hearing shall be the defendant and A.I.D.
(b) Pursuant to 31 U.S.C. 3730(c)(5), a private plaintiff under the False Claims Act may participate in these proceedings to the extent authorized by the provisions of that Act.
