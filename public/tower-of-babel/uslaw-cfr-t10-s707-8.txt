10 C.F.R. § 707.8 — Applicant drug testing.
Citation: us:cfr:10:707.8
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-707.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 707 (Workplace Substance Abuse Programs At Doe Sites). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An applicant for a testing designated position will be tested for the use of illegal drugs before final selection for employment or assignment to such a position. Provisions of this part do not prohibit contractors from conducting drug testing on applicants for employment in any position.
