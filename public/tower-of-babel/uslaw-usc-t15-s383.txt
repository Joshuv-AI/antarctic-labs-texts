15 U.S.C. § 383 — “Net income tax” defined
Citation: us:usc:15:383
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section383
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 10B (State Taxation Of Income From Interstate Commerce); Subchapter I (Net Income Taxes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of this chapter, the term “net income tax” means any tax imposed on, or measured by, net income.
