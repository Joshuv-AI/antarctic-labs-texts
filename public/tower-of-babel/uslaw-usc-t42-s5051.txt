42 U.S.C. § 5051 — Performance of functions by existing departments or offices rather than new departments or offices
Citation: us:usc:42:5051
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section5051
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 66 (Domestic Volunteer Services); Subchapter IV (Administration And Coordination). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In order to assure that existing Federal agencies are used to the fullest extent possible in carrying out the purposes of this chapter, no funds appropriated to carry out this chapter shall be used to establish any new department or office when the intended function is being performed by an existing department or office.
