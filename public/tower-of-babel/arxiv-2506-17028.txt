Optimal Sobolev inequalities of high order with L 2 − L^{2}- remainder
Lorenzo Carletti, Frédéric Robert

Abstract
. We investigate the validity of the optimal higher-order Sobolev inequality H k 2 ​ ( M n ) ↪ L 2 ​ n n − 2 ​ k ​ ( M n ) H_{k}^{2}(M^{n})\hookrightarrow L^{\frac{2n}{n-2k}}(M^{n}) on a closed Riemannian manifold when the remainder term is the L 2 − L^{2}- norm. Unlike the case k = 1 k=1 , the optimal inequality does not hold in general for k > 1 k>1 . We prove conditions for the validity and non-validity that depend on the geometry of the manifold. Our conditions are sharp when k = 2 k=2 and in small dimensions.

be a compact Riemannian manifold of dimension

and let
(X_{\alpha})_{\alpha>0},(Y_{\alpha})_{\alpha>0}:M\times M-\{(x,x)/\,x\in M\}\to\mathbb{R}
measurable such that there exists
a,b\in(0,n]
,
p,q>n
and
C_{X},C_{Y}>0
independent of
\alpha>0
such that

|X_{\alpha}(x,y)|\leq C_{X}\frac{d_{g}(x,y)^{a-n}}{1+\alpha^{p}d_{g}(x,y)^{p}}\hbox{ and }|Y_{\alpha}(x,y)|\leq C_{Y}\frac{d_{g}(x,y)^{b-n}}{1+\alpha^{q}d_{g}(x,y)^{q}}

for all
x,y\in M
,
x\neq y
and
\alpha>0
. We set

Z_{\alpha}(x,y):=\int_{M}X_{\alpha}(x,z)Y_{\alpha}(z,y)\,dv_{g}(z)\hbox{ for all }x,y\in M,\,x\neq y.

This definition makes sense and there exists
C(M,g,a,b,p,q)>0
such that

\bullet
If
a+b<n
, then

|Z_{\alpha}(x,y)|\leq C(M,g,a,b,p,q)C_{X}C_{Y}\frac{d_{g}(x,y)^{a+b-n}}{1+(\alpha d_{g}(x,y))^{\min\{a+q,b+p\}}}

\bullet
If
a+b=n
, then

|Z_{\alpha}(x,y)|\leq C(M,g,a,b,p,q)C_{X}C_{Y}\left\{\begin{array}[]{cc}|\ln(\alpha d_{g}(x,y))|&\hbox{ if }\alpha d_{g}(x,y)<\frac{1}{2}\\
\\
\frac{1}{(\alpha d_{g}(x,y))^{\min\{a+q,b+p\}}}&\hbox{ if }\alpha d_{g}(x,y)\geq\frac{1}{2}\end{array}\right.

\bullet
If
a+b>n
, then

|Z_{\alpha}(x,y)|\leq C(M,g,a,b,p,q)\frac{C_{X}C_{Y}}{\alpha^{a+b-n}}\cdot\frac{1}{1+(\alpha d_{g}(x,y))^{\min\{a+q,b+p\}-a-b+n}}

Moreover, if we have that
|Y_{\alpha}(x,y)|\leq C_{Y}\frac{(\mu+d_{g}(x,y))^{b-n}}{1+\alpha^{q}d_{g}(x,y)^{q}}
for all
x\neq y
and
\alpha>0
, for some
b\in\mathbb{R}
(possibly negative), then, with
a+b<n
we get

|Z_{\alpha}(x,y)|\leq C\frac{1}{1+(\alpha d_{g}(x,y))^{\min\{a+q,b+p\}}}\left\{\begin{array}[]{cc}\mu^{n-\gamma}(\mu+d_{g}(x,y))^{a-n}&\hbox{ if }b<0\\
(\mu+d_{g}(x,y))^{a+b-n}&\hbox{ if }b>0\end{array}\right.

for all
x,y\in M
,
x\neq y
and all
\alpha\geq 1
and
0<\mu\leq 1
.

Proof of Lemma
7.1
:
We take inspiration of the proof in
[
DHR
]
. For
x\neq y
in
M
, we have that

|Z_{\alpha}(x,y)|\leq C_{X}C_{Y}\int_{M}\frac{d_{g}(x,z)^{a-n}}{1+\alpha^{p}d_{g}(x,z)^{p}}\frac{d_{g}(z,y)^{b-n}}{1+\alpha^{q}d_{g}(z,y)^{q}}\,dv_{g}(z),

so the definition makes sense since
a,b>0
. We fix
\delta<\frac{i_{g}}{4}
.

Case 1:
Let us assume that
d_{g}(x,y)\geq 2\delta
. Then, for all
z\in B_{\delta}(x)
, we have that
d_{g}(z,y)\geq d_{g}(x,y)-d_{g}(x,z)\geq\delta
. Arguing similarly on
B_{\delta}(y)
and splitting
M
in three subdomains, we get
C=C(M,g,\delta,a,b,p,q)
such that

\displaystyle|Z_{\alpha}(x,y)|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{X}C_{Y}\left(\int_{B_{\delta}(x)}+\int_{B_{\delta}(y)}+\int_{\{d_{g}(z,y)\geq\delta\hbox{ and }d_{g}(z,x)\geq\delta\}}\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{X}C_{Y}C\left(\frac{1}{\alpha^{a+q}}+\frac{1}{\alpha^{b+p}}+\frac{1}{\alpha^{p+q}}\right)\leq\frac{C_{X}C_{Y}C}{\alpha^{\min\{a+q,b+p\}}}

since
p,q>n
,
0<a,b\leq n
and
\alpha\geq 1
. This proves the Lemma when
d_{g}(x,y)\geq 2\delta
.

Case 2:
We assume that
d_{g}(x,y)<2\delta
. Let
Y_{0}\in\mathbb{R}^{n}
be such that
y=\hbox{exp}_{x}(Y_{0})
. In particular
0<|Y_{0}|<2\delta
. For
z\in M-B_{3\delta}(x)
, we have that
d_{g}(y,z)\geq d_{g}(z,x)-d_{g}(x,y)\geq\delta
. The change of variable
z:=\hbox{exp}_{x}^{g}(Z)
yields

\displaystyle|Z_{\alpha}(x,y)|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{X}C_{Y}\left(\int_{B_{3\delta}(x)}+\int_{M-B_{3\delta}(x)}\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{X}C_{Y}C\left(\int_{B_{3\delta}(0)}\frac{|Z|^{a-n}}{1+\alpha^{p}|Z|^{p}}\cdot\frac{|Z-Y_{0}|^{b-n}}{1+\alpha^{q}|Z-Y_{0}|^{q}}\,dZ+\frac{1}{\alpha^{p+q}}\right)

We set
\Theta_{0}:=\frac{Y_{0}}{|Y_{0}|}
so that
|\Theta_{0}|=1
. The change of variable
Z=|Y_{0}|X
yields

|Z_{\alpha}(x,y)|\\
\leq C_{X}C_{Y}C\left(\int_{B_{\frac{3\delta}{|Y_{0}|}}(0)}\frac{|X|^{a-n}|Y_{0}|^{a+b-n}}{1+\alpha^{p}|Y_{0}|^{p}|X|^{p}}\cdot\frac{|X-\Theta_{0}|^{b-n}}{1+\alpha^{q}|Y_{0}|^{q}|X-\Theta_{0}|^{q}}\,dX+\frac{1}{\alpha^{p+q}}\right)

In order to get the result, one needs to separate the cases
\alpha|Y_{0}|\leq\frac{1}{3}
,
\frac{1}{3}\leq\alpha|Y_{0}|\leq 3
, and
\alpha|Y_{0}|\geq 3
, with the distinction
a+b>n
,
a+b=n
, and
a+b<n
.

7.4.
Step 4: Construction of the Neumann series

We fix
q\geq 1
and an integer
N\geq E\left(\frac{n}{2}\right)+\frac{q}{2}
. For
x,y\in M
,
x\neq y
and
\alpha\geq 1
, as in
[
robert:green
]
, we set

(74)

G_{\alpha}(x,y):=H_{\alpha}(x,y)+\sum_{i=1}^{N}\int_{M}H_{\alpha}^{i}(x,z)H_{\alpha}(z,y)\,dv_{g}(z)+\phi_{\alpha}(x,y)

where
H_{\alpha}
is as in (
71
),
\phi_{\alpha}(x,\cdot)\in C^{2k}(M)
for all
x\in M
and the
H_{\alpha}^{i}
’s are defined recursively as follows:

\left\{\begin{array}[]{cc}H_{\alpha}^{1}(x,y):=-f_{\alpha}(x,y)&\\
H_{\alpha}^{i+1}(x,y)=-\int_{M}H_{\alpha}^{i}(x,z)f_{\alpha}(z,y)\,dv_{g}(z)&\hbox{for all }i\geq 0\end{array}\right.

for all
x,y\in M
,
x\neq y
, where
f_{\alpha}
is as in (
73
) and (
72
). Iterating Lemma
7.1
yields

\hbox{for }i<E\left(\frac{n}{2}\right)\hbox{ or }\{i=E\left(\frac{n}{2}\right),\,n\hbox{ odd}\},\;|H_{\alpha}^{i}(x,y)|\leq C\frac{d_{g}(x,y)^{2i-n}}{1+(\alpha d_{g}(x,y))^{q}}

\hbox{for }\{i=E\left(\frac{n}{2}\right),\,n\hbox{ even}\},\,|H_{\alpha}^{i}(x,y)|\leq C\left\{\begin{array}[]{cc}|\ln(\alpha d_{g}(x,y))|&\hbox{ if }\alpha d_{g}(x,y)<\frac{1}{2}\\
\frac{1}{(\alpha d_{g}(x,y))^{q}}&\hbox{ if }\alpha d_{g}(x,y)\geq\frac{1}{2}\end{array}\right.

(75)

\hbox{for }i>E\left(\frac{n}{2}\right),\;|H_{\alpha}^{i}(x,y)|\leq\frac{C}{\alpha^{2(i-E(n/2))-1}}\cdot\frac{1}{1+(\alpha d_{g}(x,y))^{q}}

for all
x,y\in M
,
x\neq y
and
\alpha\geq 1
.
Noting that for any
\alpha\geq 1
and
x\in M
, we have that
H_{\alpha}^{N+1}(x,\cdot)\in C^{0,\theta}(M)
for some
0<\theta<1
(see for instance
[
DHR
]
), we let
\phi_{\alpha}(x,\cdot)\in C^{2k}(M)
be such that

(76)

\Delta_{g}^{k}\phi_{\alpha}(x,\cdot)+\alpha^{2k}\phi_{\alpha}(x,\cdot)=H_{\alpha}^{N+1}(x,\cdot)\hbox{ in }M.

The standard cancellation in the Neumann series (see for instance
[
robert:gjms
]
) yields

(77)

\Delta_{g}^{k}G_{\alpha}(x,\cdot)+\alpha^{2k}G_{\alpha}(x,\cdot)=\delta_{x}\hbox{ weakly in }M.

Since

7.5.
Step 5: Regularity for
\Delta_{g}^{k}+\alpha^{2k}

Lemma 7.2
.

Let
(h_{\alpha})_{\alpha\geq 1}\in L^{\infty}(M)
be a family of functions. For all
\alpha\geq 1
, let
\varphi_{\alpha}\in H_{k}^{2}(M)
be the variational solution to

(78)

\Delta_{g}^{k}\varphi_{\alpha}+\alpha^{2k}\varphi_{\alpha}=h_{\alpha}\hbox{ in }M.

Assume that there exists
p\in M
,
C_{H}>0
and
q>\frac{n+2k}{2}
such that
|h_{\alpha}(x)|\leq C_{H}(1+(\alpha d_{g}(x,p))^{-q}
for all
x\in M
and
\alpha\geq 1
. Then there exists
C=C(M,g,k)>0
such that for all
0\leq i<2k
, we have that
|\nabla^{i}_{g}\varphi_{\alpha}(x)|_{g}\leq C\cdot C_{H}\alpha^{i-2k}
for all
x\in M
and
\alpha\geq 1
.

Proof of Lemma
7.2
:
Since
q>\frac{n+2k}{2}
, a straightforward estimate yields
\|h_{\alpha}\|_{\frac{2n}{n+2k}}\leq C\cdot C_{H}\alpha^{-\frac{n+2k}{2}}
for all
\alpha\geq 1
. Since
\alpha\geq 1
, the operator
\Delta_{g}^{k}+\alpha^{2k}
is uniformly coercive and then, using Sobolev’s inequality (
1
), we have that

\displaystyle\|\varphi_{\alpha}\|_{H_{k}^{2}}^{2}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\int_{M}\left((\Delta_{g}^{\frac{k}{2}}\varphi_{\alpha})^{2}+\varphi_{\alpha}^{2}\right)\,dv_{g}\leq\int_{M}\left((\Delta_{g}^{\frac{k}{2}}\varphi_{\alpha})^{2}+\alpha^{2k}\varphi_{\alpha}^{2}\right)\,dv_{g}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\int_{M}(\Delta_{g}^{k}\varphi_{\alpha}+\alpha^{2k}\varphi_{\alpha})\varphi_{\alpha}\,dv_{g}=\int_{M}h_{\alpha}\varphi_{\alpha}\,dv_{g}\leq\|h_{\alpha}\|_{\frac{2n}{n+2k}}\|\varphi_{\alpha}\|_{{2^{\star}}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\|h_{\alpha}\|_{\frac{2n}{n+2k}}\|\varphi_{\alpha}\|_{H_{k}^{2}}

and therefore
\|\varphi_{\alpha}\|_{{2^{\star}}}\leq C\|\varphi_{\alpha}\|_{H_{k}^{2}}\leq C\|h_{\alpha}\|_{\frac{2n}{n+2k}}\leq C\cdot C_{H}\alpha^{-\frac{n+2k}{2}}
for all
\alpha\geq 1
. We fix a family
(x_{\alpha})_{\alpha\geq 1}\in M
and we define
\tilde{\varphi}_{\alpha}(X):=\varphi_{\alpha}(\hbox{exp}_{x_{\alpha}}(\alpha^{-1}X))
for all
X\in B_{\alpha i_{g}}(0)\subset\mathbb{R}^{n}
. Equation (
78
) rewrites
\Delta_{g_{\alpha}}^{k}\tilde{\varphi}_{\alpha}+\tilde{\varphi}_{\alpha}=\tilde{h}_{\alpha}(X):=\alpha^{-2k}h_{\alpha}(\hbox{exp}_{x_{\alpha}}(\alpha^{-1}X))
,
where
g_{\alpha}:=\hbox{exp}_{x_{\alpha}}^{\star}g(\alpha^{-1}\cdot)
. We fix
R>0
. We get

\displaystyle\|\tilde{\varphi}_{\alpha}\|_{L^{{2^{\star}}}(B_{R}(0))}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left(\int_{B_{R}(0)}|\tilde{\varphi}_{\alpha}|^{{2^{\star}}}\,dX\right)^{\frac{1}{{2^{\star}}}}\leq C\alpha^{\frac{n-2k}{2}}\left(\int_{B_{R\alpha^{-1}}(x_{\alpha})}|\varphi_{\alpha}|^{{2^{\star}}}\,dv_{g}\right)^{\frac{1}{{2^{\star}}}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\alpha^{\frac{n-2k}{2}}\|\varphi_{\alpha}\|_{{2^{\star}}}\leq C\cdot C_{H}\alpha^{-2k}.

We fix
p>1
large enough. We fix
i\leq 2k-1
. Sobolev’s embedding and elliptic regularity (see
[
ADN
]
or the Appendix in
[
robert:gjms
]
) yield

\displaystyle|\nabla^{i}_{\xi}\tilde{\varphi}_{\alpha}(0)|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\|\tilde{\varphi}_{\alpha}\|_{C^{2k-1}(B(0,R/2))}\leq C\|\tilde{\varphi}_{\alpha}\|_{H_{2k}^{p}(B(0,R/2))}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\left(\|\tilde{h}_{\alpha}\|_{L^{\infty}(B(0,R))}+\|\tilde{\varphi}_{\alpha}\|_{L^{{2^{\star}}}(B(0,R))}\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\cdot C_{H}\left(\alpha^{-\frac{n+2k}{2}}+\alpha^{-2k}\right)\leq C\cdot C_{H}\ \alpha^{-2k}.

Coming back to
\varphi_{\alpha}
, we get that
|\alpha^{-i}\nabla_{g}^{i}\varphi_{\alpha}(x_{\alpha})|\leq C\sum_{j\leq i}|\nabla^{j}_{\xi}\tilde{\varphi}_{\alpha}(0)|C\cdot C_{H}\ \alpha^{-2k}
. This proves the Lemma.∎

7.6.
Step 6: Pointwise estimates and conclusions.

We fix
q>\frac{n+2k}{2}
and we define
N\geq E\left(\frac{n}{2}\right)+\frac{q}{2}
as in the construction. We let
\phi_{\alpha}
be as in (
76
). With the pointwise control (
75
) and Lemma
7.2
, we get that

|\nabla_{y}^{i}\phi_{\alpha}(x,\cdot)|\leq\frac{C\alpha^{i}}{\alpha^{2(N-E(n/2))+1+2k}}\leq\frac{C\alpha^{i}}{\alpha^{q+1+2k}}\leq\frac{C}{\alpha^{q+1}}\hbox{ for all }i<2k.

With (
71
) and the controls (
75
), we get that for all

\left|\int_{M}H_{\alpha}^{i}(x,z)H_{\alpha}(z,y)\,dv_{g}(z)\right|\leq C\frac{d_{g}(x,y)^{2k-n}}{1+(\alpha d_{g}(x,y))^{q}}

for all
x,y\in M
,
x\neq y
. With the definition (
74
), we get that

|G_{\alpha}(x,y)|\leq C\frac{d_{g}(x,y)^{2k-n}}{1+(\alpha d_{g}(x,y))^{q}}+\frac{C}{\alpha^{q}}\leq C\frac{d_{g}(x,y)^{2k-n}}{1+(\alpha d_{g}(x,y))^{q}}.

The upper bound of the derivatives is similar, and we get Theorem
8
.
