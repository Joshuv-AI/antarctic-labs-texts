40 C.F.R. § 261.171 — Condition of containers.
Citation: us:cfr:40:261.171
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-261.171
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 261 (Identification And Listing Of Hazardous Waste). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a container holding hazardous secondary material is not in good condition (e.g., severe rusting, apparent structural defects) or if it begins to leak, the hazardous secondary material must be transferred from this container to a container that is in good condition or managed in some other way that complies with the requirements of this part.
