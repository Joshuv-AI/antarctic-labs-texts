19 C.F.R. § 4.101 — Prohibitions against Customs officers and employees.
Citation: us:cfr:19:4.101
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-4.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 4 (Vessels In Foreign And Domestic Trades). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No Customs officer or employee shall:
(a) Own, in whole or in part, any vessel except a yacht or other pleasure boat;
(b) Act as agent, attorney, or consignee for the owner or owners of any vessel, or of any cargo or lading on board the vessel; or
(c) Import or be concerned directly or indirectly in the importation of any merchandise for sale into the United States
