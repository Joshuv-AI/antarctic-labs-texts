46 U.S.C. § 56312 — Documented defined
Citation: us:usc:46:56312
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section56312
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part E (Control Of Merchant Marine Capabilities); Chapter 563 (Emergency Acquisition Of Vessels). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In sections 56309 through 56311, the term “documented” means, with respect to a vessel, that a certificate of documentation has been issued for the vessel under chapter 121.
