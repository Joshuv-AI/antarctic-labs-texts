27 C.F.R. § 7.56 — Additional information.
Citation: us:cfr:27:7.56
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-7.56
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 7 (Labeling And Advertising Of Malt Beverages). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Information (other than mandatory information) that is truthful, accurate, and specific, and that does not violate subpart F, G, or H of this part, may appear on labels. Such additional information may not conflict with, modify, qualify or restrict mandatory information in any manner.
