48 C.F.R. § 37.115-3 — 37.115-3 Solicitation provision.
Citation: us:cfr:48:37.115-3
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-37.115-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 37 (Service Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall insert the provision at 52.237-10, Identification of Uncompensated Overtime, in all solicitations valued above the simplified acquisition threshold, for professional or technical services to be acquired on the basis of the number of hours to be provided.
