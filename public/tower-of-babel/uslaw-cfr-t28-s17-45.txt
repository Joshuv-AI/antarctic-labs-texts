28 C.F.R. § 17.45 — Need-to-know.
Citation: us:cfr:28:17.45
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-17.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 17 (Classified National Security Information And Access To Classified Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person shall be granted access to specific classified information unless that person has an actual need-to-know that classified information, pursuant to section 2.5 of Executive Order 12968.
