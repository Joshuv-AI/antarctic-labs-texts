25 C.F.R. § 175.310 — How do I pay my bill?
Citation: us:cfr:25:175.310
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-175.310
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 175 (Electric Power Utilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may pay your bill by any of the following methods:
(a) In person at our utility office;
(b) Mail your payment to the address stated on your bill; or
(c) As further provided by the electric utility that serves you.
