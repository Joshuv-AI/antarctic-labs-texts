43 C.F.R. § 3506.13 — What information must I provide to BLM to include in the notice of exploration?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3506.13
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3506.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3500 (Leasing Of Solid Minerals Other Than Coal And Oil Shale). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must include:
(a) Your name and address;
(b) A description of the lands;
(c) The address of the BLM office where your exploration plan will be available for inspection; and
(d) An invitation to the public to participate in the exploration under the license.
