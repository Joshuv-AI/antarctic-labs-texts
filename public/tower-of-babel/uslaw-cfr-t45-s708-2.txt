45 C.F.R. § 708.2 — Policy.
Citation: us:cfr:45:708.2
Creator: Commission On Civil Rights
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-708.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 708 (Collection By Salary Offset From Indebted Current And Former Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is the policy of the Commission to apply the procedures in the regulations in this part uniformly and consistently in the collection of internal debts from its current and former employees.
