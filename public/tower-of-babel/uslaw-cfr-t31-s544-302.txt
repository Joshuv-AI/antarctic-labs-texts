31 C.F.R. § 544.302 — Effective date.
Citation: us:cfr:31:544.302
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-544.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 544 (Weapons Of Mass Destruction Proliferators Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term effective date refers to the effective date of the applicable prohibitions and directives contained in this part as follows:
(a) With respect to a person whose property and interests in property are blocked pursuant to § 544.201(a)(1), 12:01 a.m. eastern daylight time, June 29, 2005; and
(b) With respect to a person whose property and interests in property are blocked pursuant to § 544.201(a)(2), (a)(3), or (a)(4), the earlier of the date of actual or constructive notice of such person's designation.
