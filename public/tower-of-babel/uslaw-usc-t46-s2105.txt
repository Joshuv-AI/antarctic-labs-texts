46 U.S.C. § 2105 — Report
Citation: us:usc:46:2105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section2105
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part A (General Provisions); Chapter 21 (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall provide for the investigation of the operation of this subtitle and of all laws related to marine safety, and shall require that a report be made to the Secretary annually about those matters that may require improvement or amendment.
