28 C.F.R. § 551.117 — Access to legal resources.
Citation: us:cfr:28:551.117
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-551.117
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 551 (Miscellaneous). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Warden shall provide the opportunity for pretrial inmate-attorney visits on a seven-days-a-week basis.
(b) Staff shall provide pretrial inmates with access to legal materials in the institution.
(c) Staff shall allow the pretrial inmate, upon the inmate's request, to telephone the inmate's attorney as often as resources of the institution allow.
