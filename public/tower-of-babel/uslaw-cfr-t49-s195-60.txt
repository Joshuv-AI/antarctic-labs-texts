49 C.F.R. § 195.60 — Operator assistance in investigation.
Citation: us:cfr:49:195.60
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-195.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 195 (Transportation Of Hazardous Liquids By Pipeline). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Department of Transportation investigates an accident, the operator involved shall make available to the representative of the Department all records and information that in any way pertain to the accident, and shall afford all reasonable assistance in the investigation of the accident.
