31 C.F.R. § 13.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:31:13.1
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-13.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 13 (Procedures For Providing Assistance To State And Local Governments In Protecting Foreign Diplomatic Missions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes the procedures governing protective and financial assistance to State and local governments when an extraordinary protective need requires the protection of foreign diplomatic missions as authorized by sections 202 and 208 of Title 3, U.S. Code, as amended and added, respectively, by Pub. L. 94-196 (89 Stat. 1109).
