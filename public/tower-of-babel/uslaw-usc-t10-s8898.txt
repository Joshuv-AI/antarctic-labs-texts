10 U.S.C. § 8898 — Restricted certificate
Citation: us:usc:10:8898
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8898
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 885 (Stay Of Judicial Proceedings). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Navy may restrict a certificate issued under this chapter so that it stays only the taking of testimony of certain witnesses or the production of evidence on certain subjects. The proceedings not stayed may continue.
