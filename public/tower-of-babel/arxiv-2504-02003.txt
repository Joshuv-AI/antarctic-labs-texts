3-handle construction on II 1 factors
David Gao, Srivatsav Kunnawalkam Elayavalli, Gregory Patchell

Abstract
. We find a subtle modification to the construction in [ CIKE23 ] , which yields a drastic simplification of the proof of the existence of two non Gamma non elementarily equivalent II 1 factors.



We introduce in this note a 3-handle construction on II
1
factors, which is directly inspired by and modifies the Chifan-Ioana-Kunnawalkam Elayavalli construction of an exotic non Gamma II
1
factor
[
CIKE23
]
that gave the first concrete example of a pair of non Gamma, non elementarily equivalent II
1
factors. The novelty here is that the 3-handle construction makes the proofs drastically shorter, notably entirely removing two technical challenges of the
[
CIKE23
]
approach: a deformation-rigidity argument to control relative commutants and ultrapower lifting theorems for pairs of independent unitaries. This new construction also yields sharper bounds on the commutation diameter
[
KEP25
]
than previously known.

The construction

For a II
1
factor
N
and
u,v\in\mathcal{U}(N)
, define
\Omega_{1}(N,u)=N*_{\{u\}^{\prime\prime}}\left(\{u\}^{\prime\prime}\mathbin{\bar{\otimes}}L(\mathbb{Z})\right)
and denote the Haar unitary generating the
L(\mathbb{Z})
on the right as
\omega(u)
. Define
\Omega_{2}(N,u,v)=N*_{\{u,v\}^{\prime\prime}}(\{u,v\}^{\prime\prime}\mathbin{\bar{\otimes}}L(\mathbb{Z}))
.
Let
\sigma=(\sigma_{1},\sigma_{2}):\mathbb{N}\rightarrow\mathbb{N}\times\mathbb{N}
be a bijection such that
\sigma_{1}(n)\leq n
, for every
n\in\mathbb{N}
. Assume that
M_{1},\ldots,M_{n}
have been constructed, for some
n\in\mathbb{N}
.
Let
\{(u_{1}^{n,k},u_{2}^{n,k})\}_{k\in\mathbb{N}}\subset\mathcal{U}(M_{n})
be a
\|\cdot\|_{2}
-dense sequence.
We define

M_{n+1}:=\Omega_{2}(\Omega_{1}(\Omega_{1}(M_{n},u_{1}^{\sigma(n)}),u_{2}^{\sigma(n)}),\omega(u_{1}^{\sigma(n)}),\omega(u_{2}^{\sigma(n)})).

Note that
M_{n+1}
is well-defined since
\sigma_{1}(n)\leq n
and thus
(u_{1}^{\sigma(n)},u_{2}^{\sigma(n)})\in\mathcal{U}(M_{n})
. Denote by
M
the inductive limit, where we start with
M_{1}=L(SL_{3}(\mathbb{Z}))
. Note that since unitaries in ultrapowers lift into unitaries in strongly dense subsets, we have for all ultrafilters
U
and any pair of unitaries
u,v\in M^{U}
, there exist diffuse unitaries
w_{1},w_{2},w_{3}\in M^{U}
such that
[u,w_{1}]=[w_{1},w_{2}]=[w_{2},w_{3}]=[w_{3},v]=0
.

Showing that
M
is a non Gamma II
1
factor

Observe that
L(SL_{3}(\mathbb{Z}))\subset M_{n}
satisfies
L(SL_{3}(\mathbb{Z}))^{\prime}\cap M_{n+1}=L(SL_{3}(\mathbb{Z}))^{\prime}\cap M_{n}
. Indeed, by Theorem 1.1 of
[
IPP08
]
, we have that if
L(SL_{3}(\mathbb{Z}))\not\prec_{M_{n+1}}\{\omega(u_{1}^{\sigma(n)}),\omega(u_{2}^{\sigma(n)})\}^{\prime\prime}
then
L(SL_{3}(\mathbb{Z}))^{\prime}\cap M_{n+1}\subset M_{n}
(notice that the other two amalgams in the construction are abelian). This is indeed the case:
L(SL_{3}(\mathbb{Z}))
has Property (T) and the
central point
of this note is that since
\omega(u_{1}^{\sigma(n)})
and
\omega(u_{2}^{\sigma(n)})
are by construction freely independent they generate a copy of the free group factor which has the Haagerup property (see
[
CJ85
]
and Proposition 16.2.3 of
[
AP16
]
). Hence by weak spectral gap of Property (T) factors,
L(SL_{3}(\mathbb{Z}))^{\prime}\cap M^{\mathcal{U}}=\mathbb{C}
.

Proving non elementary equivalence

Let
a,b
be the generator unitaries in
L(\mathbb{F}_{2})
. For any ultrafilter
\mathcal{U}
on a set
I
, it is known that there do not exist diffuse unitaries
w_{1},w_{2},w_{3}\in\mathcal{U}(L(\mathbb{F}_{2})^{\mathcal{U}})
such that
[a,w_{1}]=[w_{1},w_{2}]=[w_{2},w_{3}]=[w_{3},b]=0
. There are now multiple proofs of the above statement. For instance, this follows immediately from repeatedly applying the well known
join lemma
in 1-bounded entropy theory (see Corollary 4.8 and Proposition A.12 of
[
Hay18
]
, also
[
Jun07
]
, see also Fact 2.9 in
[
KEP25
]
); alternatively, one can apply the Jekel-Kunnawalkam Elayavalli upgraded free independence theorem
[
JKE24
]
. Note that the free independence theorem of Houdayer-Ioana
[
HI23
]
does not apply here as it can only handle one level of commutants.

The following theorem follows immediately by combining the above with our 3-handle construction which by design attaches a handle of three sequentially commuting diffuse unitaries on every pair of unitaries in a countable strongly dense set.

Theorem A
.

For any ultrafilters
\mathcal{U},\mathcal{V}
on sets
I,J
respectively,
M^{\mathcal{U}}\ncong L(\mathbb{F}_{2})^{\mathcal{V}}
. Moreover
3\leq\mathfrak{D}(M)\leq 4
where
\mathfrak{D}(M)
denotes the commutation diameter introduced in
[
KEP25
]
.
