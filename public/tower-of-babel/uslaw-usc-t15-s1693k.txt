15 U.S.C. § 1693k — Compulsory use of electronic fund transfers
Citation: us:usc:15:1693k
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1693k
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 41 (Consumer Credit Protection); Subchapter VI (Electronic Fund Transfers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No person may—
(1)
condition the extension of credit to a consumer on such consumer’s repayment by means of preauthorized electronic fund transfers; or
(2)
require a consumer to establish an account for receipt of electronic fund transfers with a particular financial institution as a condition of employment or receipt of a government benefit.
