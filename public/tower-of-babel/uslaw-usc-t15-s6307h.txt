15 U.S.C. § 6307h — Judges and referees
Citation: us:usc:15:6307h
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section6307h
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 89 (Professional Boxing Safety). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No person may arrange, promote, organize, produce, or fight in a professional boxing match unless all referees and judges participating in the match have been certified and approved by the boxing commission responsible for regulating the match in the State where the match is held.
