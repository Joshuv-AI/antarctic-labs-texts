18 U.S.C. § 1834 — Criminal forfeiture
Citation: us:usc:18:1834
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1834
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 90 (Protection Of Trade Secrets). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Forfeiture, destruction, and restitution relating to this chapter shall be subject to section 2323, to the extent provided in that section, in addition to any other similar remedies provided by law.
