48 C.F.R. § 1452.280-1 — 1452.280-1 Notice of Indian Small Business Economic Enterprise set-aside.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1452.280-1
Creator: Chapter 14—Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1452.280-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1452 (Solicitation Provisions And Contract Clauses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As prescribed in 1480.503(e)(1), and in lieu of the requirements of FAR 19.508, insert the following provision in each written solicitation of offers to provide supplies, general services, A-E services, or construction. If the solicitation is oral, information substantially identical to that contained in the provision must be given to potential offerors.
