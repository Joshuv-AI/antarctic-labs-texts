27 C.F.R. § 24.92 — Products in customs custody.
Citation: us:cfr:27:24.92
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-24.92
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 24 (Wine). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Products in customs custody may be conveyed across bonded wine premises subject to the following conditions:
(a) The products are not stored or allowed to remain on bonded wine premises beyond the close of the business day; and
(b) The products in customs custody are kept separate from wine and spirits on bonded wine premises.
