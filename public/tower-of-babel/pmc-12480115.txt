Trends in the Proportion of People Who Eat Out More than Once a Day, 2013–2022
Published: 2024

The proportion of people who eat out more than once a day (among those aged 1 year and over) increased from 25.5% in 2021 to 27.7% in 2022, and was higher in men than that in women (Figure 1). The proportions among those 20s and 30s were higher than other age groups (Figure 2).
