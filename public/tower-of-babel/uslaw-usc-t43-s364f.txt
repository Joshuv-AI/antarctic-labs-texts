43 U.S.C. § 364f — Application to naming of offices or establishments
Citation: us:usc:43:364f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section364f
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 11A (Board On Geographic Names). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed as applying to the naming of the offices or establishments of any Federal agency.
