24 C.F.R. § 248.261 — Agreements implementing plans of action and State strategies.
Citation: us:cfr:24:248.261
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-248.261
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 248 (Prepayment Of Low Income Housing Mortgages). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commissioner is authorized to enter into agreements, including those for the provision of incentives, necessary to implement any plan of action or State strategy approved by the Commissioner under this part.
