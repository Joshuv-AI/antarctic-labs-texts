43 C.F.R. § 3474.6 — Termination of the period of liability.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3474.6
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3474.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3470 (Coal Management Provisions And Limitations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The authorized officer shall not consent to termination of the period of liability under the lease bond unless an acceptable substitute bond has been filed or until all terms and conditions of the lease have been fulfilled.
