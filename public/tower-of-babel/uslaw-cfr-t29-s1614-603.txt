29 C.F.R. § 1614.603 — Voluntary settlement attempts.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1614.603
Creator: Equal Employment Opportunity Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1614.603
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1614 (Federal Sector Equal Employment Opportunity). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each agency shall make reasonable efforts to voluntarily settle complaints of discrimination as early as possible in, and throughout, the administrative processing of complaints, including the pre-complaint counseling stage. Any settlement reached shall be in writing and signed by both parties and shall identify the claims resolved.
