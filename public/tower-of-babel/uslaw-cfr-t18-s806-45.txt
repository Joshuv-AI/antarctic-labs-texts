18 C.F.R. § 806.45 — Appeal of determination.
Citation: us:cfr:18:806.45
Creator: Susquehanna River Basin Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-806.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 806 (Review And Approval Of Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A final determination of the grandfathered quantity by the Executive Director must be appealed to the Commission within 30 days from actual notice of the determination.
(b) The Commission shall appoint a hearing officer to preside over appeals under this section. Hearings shall be governed by the procedures set forth in part 808 of this chapter.
