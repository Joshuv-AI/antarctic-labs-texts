10 U.S.C. § 1095d — TRICARE program: waiver of certain deductibles
Citation: us:usc:10:1095d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1095d
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 55 (Medical And Dental Care). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Waiver Authorized.—
The Secretary of Defense may waive the deductible payable for medical care provided under the TRICARE program to an eligible dependent of—
(1)
a member of a reserve component on active duty pursuant to a call or order to active duty for a period of more than 30 days; or
(2)
a member of the National Guard on full-time National Guard duty pursuant to a call or order to full-time National Guard duty for a period of more than 30 days.
(b) Eligible Dependent.—
As used in this section, the term “eligible dependent” means a dependent described in subparagraph (A), (D), or (I) of section 1072(2) of this title.
