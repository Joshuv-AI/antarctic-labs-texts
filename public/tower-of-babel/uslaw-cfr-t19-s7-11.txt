19 C.F.R. § 7.11 — Guantanamo Bay Naval Station.
Citation: us:cfr:19:7.11
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-7.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 7 (Customs Relations With Insular Possessions And Guantanamo Bay Naval Station). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Articles of foreign origin may enter the area (both land and water) of the Guantanamo Bay Naval Station free of duty, but such articles shall be subject to duty upon their subsequent entry into the United States.
