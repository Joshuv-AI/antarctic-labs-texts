19 C.F.R. § 4.67 — Closed ports or places.
Citation: us:cfr:19:4.67
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-4.67
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 4 (Vessels In Foreign And Domestic Trades). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No foreign vessel shall be granted a clearance or permit to proceed to any port or place from which such vessels are excluded by orders or regulations of the United States Navy Department except with the prior approval of that Department.
