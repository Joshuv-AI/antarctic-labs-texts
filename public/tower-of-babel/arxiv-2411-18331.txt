Chemo - Structural Disorder in the kagomé spin S S = 1/2 systems ZnCu 3 (OH) 6 Cl 2 and YCu 3 (OH) 6 Br 2 [Br x (OH) 1-x ]
Reinhard K. Kremer, Sebastian Bette, Jürgen Nuss, Pascal Puphal

Abstract
By single crystal diffraction we characterize the chemo - structural disorder introduced by Zn - Cu site mixing in the kagomé spin S S - 1/2 systems herbertsmithite ZnCu 3 (OH) 6 Cl 2 and YCu 3 (OH) 6 Br 2 [Br x (OH) 1-x ]. For an untwinned single crystal of herbertsmithite of composition Zn 0.95(1) Cu 2.99(3) O 5.9(1) H 5.8(1) Cl 2 we find substitution by Cu of the Zn atoms in the layers separating the kagomé layers as well as substantial Zn substitution for Cu in the kagomé layers. In YCu 3 (OH) 6 Br 2 [Br x (OH) 1-x ] site mixing disorder is present for intermediate x x . Analogous to the Cl homologous system in crystals with x x = 1/3 disorder is absent and a low-temperature structural transition emerges driven by strong magneto-phonon coupling as a release of frustration. Apart from this structural anomaly we find the physical properties of these crystals unchanged compared to intermediate x x and closely resembling the Cl homologue where long-range magnetic order was observed.
