14 C.F.R. § 1203.601 — Responsibilities.

Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1203.601
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1203 (Information Security Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Authorized officials with Declassification Authority (DCA) may declassify or downgrade information that is subject to the final classification jurisdiction of NASA and shall take such action in accordance with the provisions of this subpart F.
