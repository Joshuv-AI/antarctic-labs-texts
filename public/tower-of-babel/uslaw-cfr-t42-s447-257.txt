42 C.F.R. § 447.257 — FFP: Conditions relating to institutional reimbursement.
Citation: us:cfr:42:447.257
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-447.257
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 447 (Payments For Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

FFP is not available for a State's expenditures for hospital inpatient or long-term care facility services that are in excess of the amounts allowable under this subpart.
