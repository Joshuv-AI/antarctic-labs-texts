43 C.F.R. § 3835.16 — If I am a qualified small miner, and I obtained a waiver in one assessment year, what must I do if I want to pay the maintenance fee for the following assessment year?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3835.16
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3835.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3835 (Waivers From Annual Maintenance Fees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You must perform the required assessment work in the assessment year for which you obtained a waiver from payment of the annual maintenance fee, and file the annual FLPMA document required by the December 30th immediately following the payment of the maintenance fee; and
(b) You must pay the maintenance fee by the proper deadline for the following assessment year.
