30 C.F.R. § 15.22 — Tolerances for performance, wrapper, and specific gravity.
Citation: us:cfr:30:15.22
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-15.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 15 (Requirements For Approval Of Explosives And Sheathed Explosive Units). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The rate of detonation of the explosive shall be within ±15 percent of that specified in the approval.
(b) The weight of wrapper per 100 grams of explosive shall be within ±2 grams of that specified in the approval.
(c) The apparent specific gravity of the explosive shall be within ±7.5 percent of that specified in the approval.
