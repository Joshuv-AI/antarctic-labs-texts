54 U.S.C. § 300320 — Undertaking
Citation: us:usc:54:300320
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section300320
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3003 (Definitions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this division, the term “undertaking” means a project, activity, or program funded in whole or in part under the direct or indirect jurisdiction of a Federal agency, including—
(1)
those carried out by or on behalf of the Federal agency;
(2)
those carried out with Federal financial assistance;
(3)
those requiring a Federal permit, license, or approval; and
(4)
those subject to State or local regulation administered pursuant to a delegation or approval by a Federal agency.
