30 U.S.C. § 171 — Disposal as agricultural lands
Citation: us:usc:30:171
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section171
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 3 (Lands Containing Coal, Oil, Gas, Salts, Asphaltic Materials, Sodium, Sulphur, And Building Stone); Subchapter IX (Disposal Of Alabama Lands As Agricultural Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as otherwise provided in chapter 3A of this title, all public lands within the State of Alabama, whether mineral or otherwise, shall be subject to disposal only as agricultural lands. All lands which had been reported to the General Land Office prior to March 3, 1883, as containing coal and iron shall first be offered at public sale.
