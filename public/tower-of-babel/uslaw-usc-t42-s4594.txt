42 U.S.C. § 4594 — Contract authority in appropriation Acts
Citation: us:usc:42:4594
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section4594
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 60 (Comprehensive Alcohol Abuse And Alcoholism Prevention, Treatment, And Rehabilitation Program); Subchapter V (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The authority of the Secretary to enter into contracts under this chapter shall be effective for any fiscal year only to such extent or in such amounts as are provided in advance by appropriation Acts.
