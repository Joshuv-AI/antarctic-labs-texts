46 C.F.R. § 221.81 — Counsel.
Citation: us:cfr:46:221.81
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-221.81
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 221 (Regulated Transactions Involving Documented Vessels And Other Maritime Interests). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Party has the right to be represented at all stages of the proceeding by counsel. After receiving notification that a Party is represented by counsel, the Hearing Officer will direct all further communications to that counsel.
