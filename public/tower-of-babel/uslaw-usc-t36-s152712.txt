36 U.S.C. § 152712 — Annual report
Citation: us:usc:36:152712
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section152712
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1527 (National Ski Patrol System, Incorporated). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation shall submit an annual report to Congress on the activities of the corporation during the prior calendar year. The report may not be printed as a public document.
