20 U.S.C. § 2506 — Functions of National Advisory Council for Career Education
Citation: us:usc:20:2506
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section2506
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 45 (Career Education And Career Development); Subchapter I (Career Education And Development Programs And Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The National Advisory Council for Career Education established pursuant to section 2612a(g) of this title shall, in addition to its duties under that section, advise the Secretary of Education with respect to the implementation of this subchapter.
