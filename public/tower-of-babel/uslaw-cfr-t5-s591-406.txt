5 C.F.R. § 591.406 — Records and reports.
Citation: us:cfr:5:591.406
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-591.406
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 591 (Allowances And Differentials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

So that the Office of Personnel Management can evaluate agencies' use of this authority and provide the Congress and others with information regarding the use of a nonforeign separate maintenance allowance, each agency shall maintain such records and submit to the Office of Personnel Management reports and data as requested.
