7 C.F.R. § 958.26 — Expenses.
Citation: us:cfr:7:958.26
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-958.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 958 (Onions Grown In Certain Designated Counties In Idaho, And Malheur County, Oregon). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Committee members and alternates when acting on committee business shall be reimbursed for reasonable expenses necessarily incurred by them in the performance of their duties and in the exercise of their powers under this part. However, at its discretion the committee may request the attendance of alternates at any or all meetings notwithstanding the expected or actual presence of the respective members.
