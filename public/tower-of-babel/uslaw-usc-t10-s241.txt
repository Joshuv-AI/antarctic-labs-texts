10 U.S.C. § 241 — Reference to chapters 1003, 1005, and 1007
Citation: us:usc:10:241
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section241
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And General Military Powers); Chapter 11 (Reserve Components). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Provisions of law relating to the reserve components generally, including provisions relating to the organization and administration of the reserve components, are set forth in chapter 1003 (beginning with section 10101), chapter 1005 (beginning with section 10141), and chapter 1007 (beginning with section 10201) of this title.
