45 C.F.R. § 2519.800 — What are the evaluation requirements for Higher Education programs?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2519.800
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2519.800
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2519 (Higher Education Innovative Programs For Community Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The monitoring and evaluation requirements for recipients of grants and subgrants under part 2516 of this chapter, relating to school-based service-learning programs, apply to recipients under this part.
