24 C.F.R. § 241.260 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:24:241.260
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-241.260
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 241 (Supplementary Financing For Insured Project Mortgages). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All of the definitions contained in § 241.1 shall apply to this subpart. In addition, the term contract of insurance, as used in this subpart, means the agreement evidenced by endorsement of the credit instrument by the Commissioner or his duly authorized representative, and includes the provisions of this subpart and of the National Housing Act.
