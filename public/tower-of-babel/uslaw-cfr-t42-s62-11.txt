42 C.F.R. § 62.11 — When can a scholarship program payment obligation be discharged in bankruptcy?
Citation: us:cfr:42:62.11
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-62.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 62 (National Health Service Corps Scholarship And Loan Repayment Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In accordance with section 754(d)(3) of the Act, any payment obligation incurred under § 62.10 may not be discharged in bankruptcy under title XI of the United States Code until 5 years after the date on which the payment obligation is due.
