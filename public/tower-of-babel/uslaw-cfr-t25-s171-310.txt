25 C.F.R. § 171.310 — Can I use water delivered by BIA for livestock purposes?
Citation: us:cfr:25:171.310
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-171.310
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 171 (Irrigation Operation And Maintenance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, if we determine it will not:
(a) Interfere with the operation, maintenance, or rehabilitation of our facilities;
(b) Be detrimental to or jeopardize our facilities;
(c) Adversely affect the water rights or water supply; or
(d) Cause additional costs to us that we do not agree to in writing.
