22 U.S.C. § 449 — American republics
Citation: us:usc:22:449
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section449
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 9 (Foreign Wars, War Materials, And Neutrality); Subchapter II (Neutrality). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This subchapter (except section 452 1 of this title) shall not apply to any American republic engaged in war against a non-American state or states, provided the American republic is not cooperating with a non-American state or states in such war.
