43 C.F.R. § 5463.1 — Time for cutting and removal.
Citation: us:cfr:43:5463.1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-5463.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 5460 (Sales Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Time for cutting and removal of timber or other vegetative resources sold shall not exceed a period of forty-eight months such time for cutting and removal may be extended as provided in 43 CFR part 5470, subpart 5473.
