29 C.F.R. § 520.202 — How do persons who want to apply for a particular certificate find out what is needed?
Citation: us:cfr:29:520.202
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-520.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 520 (Employment Under Special Certificate Of Messengers, Learners (Including Student-Learners), And Apprentices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The application process, terms, conditions and requirements of certificates and other matters are discussed in subparts D and E of this part. Messengers, learners (excluding student-learners), and apprentices are discussed in subpart D of this part and student-learners in subpart E of this part.
