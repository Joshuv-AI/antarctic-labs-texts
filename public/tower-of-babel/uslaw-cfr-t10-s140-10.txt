10 C.F.R. § 140.10 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:10:140.10
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-140.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 140 (Financial Protection Requirements And Indemnity Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to each person who is an applicant for or holder of a license issued under 10 CFR part 50, 53 or 54 to operate a nuclear reactor, or is the applicant for or holder of a combined license issued under 10 CFR part 52, 53, or 54, except licenses held by persons found by the Commission to be Federal agencies or nonprofit educational institutions licensed to conduct educational activities. This subpart also applies to persons licensed to possess and use plutonium in a plutonium processing and fuel fabrication plant.
