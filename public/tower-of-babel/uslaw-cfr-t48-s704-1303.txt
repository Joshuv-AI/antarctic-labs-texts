48 C.F.R. § 704.1303 — Contract clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:704.1303
Creator: Chapter 7—Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-704.1303
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 704 (Administrative And Information Matters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When contract performance requires the contractor—including its employees, volunteers, or subcontractor employees at any tier—to have routine physical access to USAID-controlled facilities or logical access to USAID's information systems, the contracting officer must insert the clause found at FAR 52.204-9 and AIDAR 752.204-72 (“Access to USAID Facilities and USAID's Information Systems”) in the solicitation and contract.
