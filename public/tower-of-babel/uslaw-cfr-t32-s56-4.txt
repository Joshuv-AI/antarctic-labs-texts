32 C.F.R. § 56.4 — Policy.
Citation: us:cfr:32:56.4
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-56.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 56 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap In Programs And Activities Assisted Or Conducted By The Department Of Defense). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is DoD policy that no qualified handicapped person shall be subjected to discrimination on the basis of handicap under any program or activity that receives or benefits from Federal financial assistance disbursed by a DoD Component or under any Federal program or activity that is conducted by a DoD Component. Guidelines for determining actions that discriminate against handicapped persons are prescribed in § 56.8.
