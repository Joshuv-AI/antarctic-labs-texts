48 C.F.R. § 1406.202 — 1406.202 Establishing or maintaining alternative sources.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1406.202
Creator: Chapter 14—Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1406.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1406 (Competition Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

HCAs are authorized to approve the determinations and findings (D&Fs) to establish or maintain an alternative source or sources for supplies or services.
