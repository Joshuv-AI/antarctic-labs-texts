19 C.F.R. § 115.67 — Approval certificate.
Citation: us:cfr:19:115.67
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-115.67
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 115 (Cargo Container And Road Vehicle Certification Pursuant To International Customs Conventions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The holder of the approval certificate shall, before using the vehicle for the carriage of goods under the cover of a TIR Carnet, fill in as may be required on the approval certificate:
(a) The registration number given to the vehicle (item No. 1); or
(b) In the case of a vehicle not subject to registration, particulars of his name and business address (item No. 8). (See Annex 4 of the Convention for model of certificate of approval.)
