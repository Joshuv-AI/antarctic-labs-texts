16 U.S.C. § 7912 — Federal land open to hunting, fishing, and recreational shooting
Citation: us:usc:16:7912
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section7912
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 98 (Sportsmen’S Access To Federal Land); Subchapter II (Sportsmen’S Access To Federal Land). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
Subject to subsection (b), Federal land shall be open to hunting, fishing, and recreational shooting, in accordance with applicable law, unless the Secretary concerned closes an area in accordance with section 7913 of this title.
(b) Effect of part
Nothing in this subchapter opens to hunting, fishing, or recreational shooting any land that is not open to those activities as of March 12, 2019.
