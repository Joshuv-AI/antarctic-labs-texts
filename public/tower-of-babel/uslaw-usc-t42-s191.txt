42 U.S.C. § 191 — Bureau established
Citation: us:usc:42:191
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section191
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6 (The Children’S Bureau). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There shall be established in the Department of Health and Human Services a bureau to be known as the Children’s Bureau.
