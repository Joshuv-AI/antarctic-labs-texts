46 U.S.C. § 31112 — Lien not recognized or created
Citation: us:usc:46:31112
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section31112
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 311 (Suits Involving Public Vessels). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter shall not be construed as recognizing the existence of or as creating a lien against a public vessel of the United States.
