7 C.F.R. § 504.3 — Payment of fees.
Citation: us:cfr:7:504.3
Creator: Agricultural Research Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-504.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 504 (User Fees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Payment of user fees must accompany a culture deposit or request.
(b) Payment shall be made by check, draft, money order, or pay.gov, payable to USDA, National Finance Center.
