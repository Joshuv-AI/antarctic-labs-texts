47 C.F.R. § 95.3201 — Permissible uses.
Citation: us:cfr:47:95.3201
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-95.3201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 95 (Personal Radio Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

C-V2X OBUs may transmit signals to other C-V2X OBUs and to C-V2X Roadside Units (RSUs) authorized under part 90 of this chapter or any licensed commercial mobile radio service station as defined in part 20 of this chapter.
