31 C.F.R. § 544.409 — Credit extended and cards issued by U.S. financial institutions.
Citation: us:cfr:31:544.409
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-544.409
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 544 (Weapons Of Mass Destruction Proliferators Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The prohibition in § 544.201 on dealing in property subject to that section prohibits U.S. financial institutions from performing under any existing credit agreements, including, but not limited to, charge cards, debit cards, or other credit facilities issued by a U.S. financial institution to a person whose property and interests in property are blocked pursuant to § 544.201(a).
