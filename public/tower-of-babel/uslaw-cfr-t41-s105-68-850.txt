41 C.F.R. § 105-68.850 — -68.850 What is the standard of proof in a debarment action?
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-68.850
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-68.850
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-68 (68—Governmentwide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In any debarment action, we must establish the cause for debarment by a preponderance of the evidence.
(b) If the proposed debarment is based upon a conviction or civil judgment, the standard of proof is met.
