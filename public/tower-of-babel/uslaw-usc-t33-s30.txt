33 U.S.C. § 30 — Grand River, Missouri, above Brunswick
Citation: us:usc:33:30
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section30
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 1 (Navigable Waters Generally); Subchapter II (Waters Declared Nonnavigable: Change Of Name). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Grand River in the State of Missouri above the city of Brunswick, in the county of Chariton in said State, is declared to be not a navigable stream and shall be so treated by the Secretary of the Army and by all other authorities.
