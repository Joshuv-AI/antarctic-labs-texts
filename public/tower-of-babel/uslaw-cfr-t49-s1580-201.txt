49 C.F.R. § 1580.201 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1580.201
Creator: Transportation Security Administration, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1580.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1580 (Freight Rail Transportation Security). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to the following:
(1) Each owner/operator described in § 1580.1(a)(1) of this part that transports one or more of the categories and quantities of rail security-sensitive materials.
(2) Each owner/operator described in § 1580.1(a)(2) and (3) of this part.
