5 C.F.R. § 831.1108 — Witnesses.
Citation: us:cfr:5:831.1108
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-831.1108
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 831 (Retirement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Witnesses shall testify under oath or affirmation and shall be subject to cross-examination.
(b) Each party is responsible for securing the attendance of his witnesses. OPM has no power of subpena in these cases.
