49 C.F.R. § 826.8 — Awards against the Federal Aviation Administration.
Citation: us:cfr:49:826.8
Creator: National Transportation Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-826.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 826 (Rules Implementing The Equal Access To Justice Act Of 1980). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When an applicant is entitled to an award because it prevails over an agency of the United States that participates in a proceeding before the Board and takes a position that is not substantially justified, the award shall be made against that agency.
