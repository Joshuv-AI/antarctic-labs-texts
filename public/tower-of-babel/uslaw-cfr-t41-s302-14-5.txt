41 C.F.R. § 302-14.5 — -14.5 Tax consequences of receiving a home marketing incentive payment.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-14.5
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-14.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-14 (14—Home Marketing Incentive Payments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The home marketing incentive payment is considered income. Consequently, the employee will be taxed, and the agency will withhold income and employment taxes, on the home marketing incentive payment. Employees will not, however, receive a withholding tax allowance (WTA) to offset the withholding on the home marketing incentive payment, nor will they receive a relocation income tax allowance (RITA) payment.
