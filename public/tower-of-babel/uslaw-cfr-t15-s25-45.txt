15 C.F.R. § 25.45 — Deposit in Treasury of United States.
Citation: us:cfr:15:25.45
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-25.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 25 (Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All amounts collected pursuant to this part shall be deposited as miscellaneous receipts in the Treasury of the United States, except as provided in 31 U.S.C. 3806(g).
