23 C.F.R. § 230.205 — Supportive services funds obligation.
Citation: us:cfr:23:230.205
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-230.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 230 (External Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Supportive services funds shall be obligated in accordance with the procedures set forth in § 230.117(b) of this part. The point of obligation is defined as that time when the FHWA has approved a detailed work statement for the supportive services.
