49 U.S.C. § 14703 — Enforcement by the Attorney General
Citation: us:usc:49:14703
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section14703
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part B (Motor Carriers, Water Carriers, Brokers, And Freight Forwarders); Chapter 147 (Enforcement; Investigations; Rights; Remedies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General may, and on request of either the Secretary or the Board shall, bring court proceedings—
(1)
to enforce this part or a regulation or order of the Secretary or Board or terms of registration under this part; and
(2)
to prosecute a person violating this part or a regulation or order of the Secretary or Board or term of registration under this part.
