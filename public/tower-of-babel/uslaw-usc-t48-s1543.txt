48 U.S.C. § 1543 — United States citizenship requirement for government officials
Citation: us:usc:48:1543
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1543
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 12 (Virgin Islands [1954]); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All members of the Legislature of the Virgin Islands, the Governor, the Lieutenant Governor, all judges and all officials of the government of the Virgin Islands who report directly to the Governor shall be citizens of the United States.
