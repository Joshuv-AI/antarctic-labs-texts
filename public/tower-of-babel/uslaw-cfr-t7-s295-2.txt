7 C.F.R. § 295.2 — Organizational description.
Citation: us:cfr:7:295.2
Creator: Food And Nutrition Administration, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-295.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 295 (Availability Of Information And Records To The Public). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The description of the central and field organization of FNS is published as a notice in the Federal Register and may be revised from time to time in like manner. Such description contains a listing of FNS headquarters and field organizational units and their functions.
