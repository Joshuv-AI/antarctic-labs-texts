48 C.F.R. § 225.7303-3 — 225.7303-3 Government-to-government agreements.
Citation: us:cfr:48:225.7303-3
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-225.7303-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 225 (Foreign Acquisition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a government-to-government agreement between the United States and a foreign government for the sale, coproduction, or cooperative logistic support of a specifically defined weapon system, major end item, or support item, contains language in conflict with the provisions of this section, the language of the government-to-government agreement prevails.
