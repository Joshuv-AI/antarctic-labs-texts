12 C.F.R. § 1412.4 — Prohibited indemnification payments.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1412.4
Creator: Farm Credit System Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1412.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1412 (Golden Parachute And Indemnification Payments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No System institution shall make or agree to make any prohibited indemnification payment, except as provided in this part.
