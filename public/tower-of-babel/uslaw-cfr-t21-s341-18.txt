21 C.F.R. § 341.18 — Expectorant active ingredient.
Citation: us:cfr:21:341.18
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-341.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 341 (Cold, Cough, Allergy, Bronchodilator, And Antiasthmatic Drug Products For Over-The-Counter Human Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The active ingredient of the product is guaifenesin when used within the dosage limits established in § 341.78(d).
