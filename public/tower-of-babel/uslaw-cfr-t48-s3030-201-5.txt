48 C.F.R. § 3030.201-5 — 3030.201-5 Waiver.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 3030 (Cost Accounting Standards Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The CPO is authorized to waive the applicability of the Cost Accounting Standards (CAS) under (FAR) 48 CFR 30.201-5(b). This authority may not be redelegated.
(c) Waiver requests must conform to (HSAR) 48 CFR 3001.70.
