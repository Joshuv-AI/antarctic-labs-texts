42 U.S.C. § 2210g — Expenses authorized to be paid by the Commission
Citation: us:usc:42:2210g
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2210g
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 23 (Development And Control Of Atomic Energy); Subchapter XIII (General Authority Of Commission). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commission may—
(1)
pay transportation, lodging, and subsistence expenses of employees who—
(A)
assist scientific, professional, administrative, or technical employees of the Commission; and
(B)
are students in good standing at an institution of higher education (as defined in section 1002 of title 20) pursuing courses related to the field in which the students are employed by the Commission; and
(2)
pay the costs of health and medical services furnished, pursuant to an agreement between the Commission and the Department of State, to employees of the Commission and dependents of the employees serving in foreign countries.
