12 C.F.R. § 366.7 — Will the FDIC waive the prohibitions under § 366.3?
Citation: us:cfr:12:366.7
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-366.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 366 (Minimum Standards Of Integrity And Fitness For An Fdic Contractor). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We may waive the prohibitions for entities other than individuals for good cause shown at our discretion when our need to contract for your services outweighs all relevant factors. The statute does not allow us to waive the prohibitions for individuals.
