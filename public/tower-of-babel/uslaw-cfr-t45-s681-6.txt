45 C.F.R. § 681.6 — When may NSF issue a complaint?
Citation: us:cfr:45:681.6
Creator: National Science Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-681.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 681 (Program Fraud Civil Remedies Act Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

NSF may issue a complaint:
(a) If the Attorney General (or designee) approves the referral of the allegations for adjudication; and
(b) In a case of submission of false claims, if the amount of money or the value of property or services demanded or requested in a false claim, or a group of related claims submitted at the same time, does not exceed $150,000.
