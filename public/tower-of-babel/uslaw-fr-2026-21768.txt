DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project No. 15345-001]


Nightfall Renewable Inc.; Notice of Surrender of Preliminary 
Permit

    Take notice that Nightfall Renewables Inc., permittee for the 
proposed Maxwell Pumped Storage Project No. 15345, has requested that 
its preliminary permit be terminated. The permit was issued on October 
11, 2024, and would have expired on September 20, 2028.\1\ The project 
would have been located on lands administered by the U.S. Department of 
the Interior, Bureau of Land Management near the unincorporated town of 
Rosamond in Kern County, California.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ Nightfall Renewables Inc., 189 FERC ] 62,020 (2024).
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    The preliminary permit for Project No. 15345 will remain in effect 
until the close of business, thirty days from the date of this notice. 
But, if the Commission is closed on this day, then the permit remains 
in effect until the close of business on the next day on which the 
Commission is open.\2\ New applications for this site may not be 
submitted until after the permit surrender is effective.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \2\ 18 CFR 385.2007(a)(2) (2026).
