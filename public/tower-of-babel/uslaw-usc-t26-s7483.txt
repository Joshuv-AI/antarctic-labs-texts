26 U.S.C. § 7483 — Notice of appeal
Citation: us:usc:26:7483
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section7483
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 76 (Judicial Proceedings); Subchapter D (Court Review Of Tax Court Decisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Review of a decision of the Tax Court shall be obtained by filing a notice of appeal with the clerk of the Tax Court within 90 days after the decision of the Tax Court is entered. If a timely notice of appeal is filed by one party, any other party may take an appeal by filing a notice of appeal within 120 days after the decision of the Tax Court is entered.
