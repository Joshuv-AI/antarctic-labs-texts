16 C.F.R. § 1105.7 — Limits on compensation.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1105.7
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1105.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1105 (Contributions To Costs Of Participants In Development Of Consumer Product Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission may establish a limit on the total amount of financial compensation to be made to all participants in a particular proceeding and may establish a limit on the total amount of compensation to be made to any one participant in a particular proceeding.
