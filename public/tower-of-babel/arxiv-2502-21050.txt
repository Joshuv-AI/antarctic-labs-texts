Hankel determinants for convolution powers of Motzkin numbers
Ying Wang 1 and Yingrui Zhang 2,∗

Abstract
. We evaluate the Hankel determinants of the convolution powers of Motzkin numbers for r ≤ 27 r\leq 27 by finding shifted periodic continued fractions, which arose in application of Sulanke and Xin’s continued fraction method. We also conjecture some polynomial characterization of these determinants.



Mathematic subject classification
: Primary 15A15; Secondary 05A15, 11B83.

Keywords
: Hankel determinants; Continued fractions; Motzkin numbers.

Suppose the generating function

is the unique solution of a quadratic functional equation which can be written as

\displaystyle F(x)=\frac{x^{d}}{u(x)+x^{k}v(x)F(x)},

(2)

where

are rational power series with nonzero constants,

is a positive integer.
We need the unique decomposition of

with respect to

u(x)=u_{L}(x)+x^{d+2}u_{H}(x)
where
u_{L}(x)
is a polynomial of degree at most

and

is a power series.
Then Propositions 4.1 and 4.2 of
[
22
]
can be summarized as follows.

Proposition 10
.

Let
F(x)
be determined by (
2
). Then the quadratic transformation
\tau(F)
of
F
defined as follows gives close connections
between
H(F)
and
H(\tau(F))
.

i)

If
u(0)\neq 1
, then
\tau(F)=G=u(0)F
is determined by
G(x)=\frac{x^{d}}{u(0)^{-1}u(x)+x^{k}u(0)^{-2}v(x)G(x)}
, and
H_{n}(\tau(F))=u(0)^{n}H_{n}(F(x))
;

ii)

If
u(0)=1
and
k=1
, then
\tau(F)=x^{-1}(G(x)-G(0))
, where
G(x)
is determined by

G(x)=\frac{-v(x)-xu_{L}(x)u_{H}(x)}{u_{L}(x)-x^{d+2}u_{H}(x)-x^{d+1}G(x)},

and we have

H_{n-d-1}(\tau(F))=(-1)^{\binom{d+1}{2}}H_{n}(F(x));

iii)

If
u(0)=1
and

, then
\tau(F)=G
, where
G(x)
is determined by

G(x)=\frac{-x^{k-2}v(x)-u_{L}(x)u_{H}(x)}{u_{L}(x)-x^{d+2}u_{H}(x)-x^{d+2}G(x)},

H_{n-d-1}(\tau(F))=(-1)^{\binom{d+1}{2}}H_{n}(F(x)).
