20 C.F.R. § 411.580 — Can an EN receive payments for milestones or outcome payment months that occur before the beneficiary assigns a ticket to the EN?
Citation: us:cfr:20:411.580
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-411.580
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 411 (The Ticket To Work And Self-Sufficiency Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. An EN (or State VR agency acting as an EN) may be paid only for milestones or outcome payment months that are achieved after the month in which the ticket is assigned to the EN or State VR agency acting as an EN (except as provided for in § 411.536).
