14 C.F.R. § 61.81 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:61.81
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-61.81
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 61 (Certification: Pilots, Flight Instructors, And Ground Instructors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes the requirements for the issuance of student pilot certificates, the conditions under which those certificates are necessary, and the general operating rules and limitations for the holders of those certificates.
