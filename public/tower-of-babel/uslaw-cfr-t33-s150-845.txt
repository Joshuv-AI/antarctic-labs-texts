33 C.F.R. § 150.845 — Personnel records.
Citation: us:cfr:33:150.845
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-150.845
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 150 (Deepwater Ports: Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The licensee must keep documentation on the designation and qualification of the supervisory positions, outlined in the port operations manual, that are responsible for the management of the deepwater port. These records must be kept for the life of the deepwater port.
