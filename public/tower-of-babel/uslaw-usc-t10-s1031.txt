10 U.S.C. § 1031 — Administration of oath
Citation: us:usc:10:1031
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1031
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 53 (Miscellaneous Rights And Benefits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President, the Vice-President, the Secretary of Defense, any commissioned officer, and any other person designated under regulations prescribed by the Secretary of Defense may administer any oath—
(1)
required for the enlistment or appointment of any person in the armed forces; or
(2)
required by law in connection with such an enlistment or appointment.
