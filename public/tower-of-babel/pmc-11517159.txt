Defining, measuring, and reducing low-value care – proposing a new comprehensive definition and framework
Authors: K Achstetter, V Steinbeck, D Panteli, R Busse
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
Abstract Despite intensified efforts to address waste and inefficiencies in healthcare in the last decade, the ability of health systems in Europe to identify and reduce low-value care varies. Through the EU Expert Group on Health System Performance Assessment’s work, a new comprehensive definition was proposed and a framework to distinguish different types of low-value care was developed as a basis for identifying and tackling low-value care in EU Member States. Indicators to measure low-value care as well as measures to reduce it were collected.
