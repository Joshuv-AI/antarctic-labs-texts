1 C.F.R. § 304.27 — Fees.
Citation: us:cfr:1:304.27
Creator: Administrative Conference Of The United States
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-1/section-304.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 1 (General Provisions), Part 304 (Disclosure Of Records Or Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-23 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agency will charge fees for duplication of records under the Privacy Act in the same way in which it charges duplication fees under § 304.9 of subpart A. No search or review fee may be charged for any record under the Privacy Act.
