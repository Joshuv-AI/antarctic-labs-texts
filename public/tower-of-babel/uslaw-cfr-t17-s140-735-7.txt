17 C.F.R. § 140.735-7 — -7 Statutory violations applicable to conduct of Commission members and employees.
Citation: us:cfr:17:140.735-7
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-140.735-7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 140 (Organization, Functions, And Procedures Of The Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A violation of section 2(a)(7), 8 or 9 (c) or (d) of the Commodity Exchange Act, as amended, shall be deemed to be a violation of this subpart as well.
