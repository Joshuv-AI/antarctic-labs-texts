10 U.S.C. § 9622 — Rations: commissioned officers in field
Citation: us:usc:10:9622
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9622
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Procurement); Chapter 969 (Sale Of Serviceable Material). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Commissioned officers of the Air Force and commissioned officers of the Space Force serving in the field may buy rations for their own use, on credit. Amounts due for these purchases shall be reported monthly to the Secretary of the Air Force.
