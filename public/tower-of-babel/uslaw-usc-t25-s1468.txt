25 U.S.C. § 1468 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:25:1468
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 17 (Financing Economic Development Of Indians And Indian Organizations); Subchapter I (Indian Revolving Loan Fund). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is authorized to be appropriated, to provide capital and to restore any impairment of capital for the revolving loan fund $50,000,000 exclusive of prior authorizations and appropriations.
