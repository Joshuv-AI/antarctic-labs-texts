14 C.F.R. § 133.39 — Inspection authority.
Citation: us:cfr:14:133.39
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-133.39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 133 (Rotorcraft External-Load Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each person conducting an operation under this part shall allow the Administrator to make any inspections or tests that he considers necessary to determine compliance with the Federal Aviation Regulations and the Rotorcraft External-Load Operator Certificate.
