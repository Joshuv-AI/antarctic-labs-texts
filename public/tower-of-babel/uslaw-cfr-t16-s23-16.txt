16 C.F.R. § 23.16 — Misuse of the term “properly cut,” etc.
Citation: us:cfr:16:23.16
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-23.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 23 (Guides For The Jewelry, Precious Metals, And Pewter Industries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is unfair or deceptive to use the terms “properly cut,” “proper cut,” “modern cut,” or any representation of similar meaning to describe any diamond that is lopsided, or is so thick or so thin in depth as to detract materially from the brilliance of the stone.
