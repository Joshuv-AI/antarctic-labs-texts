12 C.F.R. § 797.11 — Interest, administrative costs, and penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:12:797.11
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-797.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 797 (Procedures For Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where NCUA is the creditor agency, it shall assess interest, penalties and administrative costs pursuant to 31 U.S.C. 3717 and 31 CFR parts 900 through 904, unless excused in accordance with the FCCS.
