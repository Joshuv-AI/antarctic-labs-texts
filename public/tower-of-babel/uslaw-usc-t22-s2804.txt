22 U.S.C. § 2804 — Establishment of standards and criteria; publication in the Federal Register
Citation: us:usc:22:2804
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2804
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 40 (International Expositions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Secretary of Commerce is hereby authorized and directed to establish and maintain standards, definitions, and criteria which are adequate to carry out the purposes of section 2802(a)(1) and section 2803(a) of this title; and
(b)
Standards, definitions, and criteria established by the Secretary and such revisions in them as he may make from time to time shall be published in the Federal Register.
