36 C.F.R. § 1250.2 — Presumption of Openness and Proactive Disclosures.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1250.2
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1250.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1250 (Nara Records Subject To Foia). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

NARA, consistent with its core mission, has always been committed to providing public access to as many of our records as possible. We therefore continue to affirmatively release and post records, or descriptions of such records, on our Web site at www.archives.gov in the absence of any FOIA request. We proactively identify and make discretionary disclosures of additional records of interest to the public whenever possible.
