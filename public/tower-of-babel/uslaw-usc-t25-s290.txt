25 U.S.C. § 290 — Transportation of pupils under 14 at Government expense
Citation: us:usc:25:290
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section290
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 7 (Education Of Indians). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No Indian pupil under the age of fourteen years shall be transported at Government expense to any Indian school beyond the limits of the State or Territory in which the parents of such child reside or of the adjoining State or Territory.
