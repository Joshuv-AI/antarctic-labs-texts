19 C.F.R. § 210.65 — Certification of the record.
Citation: us:cfr:19:210.65
Creator: United States International Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-210.65
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 210 (Adjudication And Enforcement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the administrative law judge issues an initial determination concerning temporary relief pursuant to § 210.66(a), the administrative law judge shall also certify to the Commission the record upon which the initial determination is based.
