Columbia Gas Transmission, LLC; Notice of Cancellation of Public 
Scoping Session for the Southeast Virginia Energy Storage Project

    On January 13, 2026, the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (FERC 
or Commission) issued a Notice of Scoping Period Requesting Comments on 
Environmental Issues for the Proposed Southeast Virginia Energy Storage 
Project and Notice of Public Scoping Sessions. The notice solicited 
comments on the potential environmental impacts of the planned project 
and announced the time and location of two public scoping sessions 
being held for the environmental proceedings.
    Due to the upcoming winter storm, the Commission staff is 
cancelling the scoping sessions planned for February 3, 2026 at the 
Jessica Ann Moore Community Center, 408 School Street, Waverly, VA. If 
a new session is established and scheduled, the Commission will issue 
another notice advising the date and time.

(Authority: 18 CFR 2.1)
