42 U.S.C. § 7140 — Leasing Liaison Committee; establishment; composition
Citation: us:usc:42:7140
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section7140
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 84 (Department Of Energy); Subchapter II (Establishment Of Department). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is established a Leasing Liaison Committee which shall be composed of an equal number of members appointed by the Secretary and the Secretary of the Interior.
