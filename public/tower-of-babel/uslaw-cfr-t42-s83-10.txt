42 C.F.R. § 83.10 — If a petition satisfies all relevant requirements under § 83.9, does this mean the class will be added to the Cohort?
Citation: us:cfr:42:83.10
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-83.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 83 (Procedures For Designating Classes Of Employees As Members Of The Special Exposure Cohort Under The Energy Employees Occupational Illness Compensation Program Act Of 2000). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Satisfying the informational requirements for a petition does not mean the class will be added to the Cohort. It means the petition will receive a full evaluation by NIOSH, the Board, and HHS, as described under §§ 83.13 through 83.16. The role of the petitioner(s) is to identify classes of employees that should be considered for addition to the Cohort.
