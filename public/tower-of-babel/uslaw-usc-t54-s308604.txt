54 U.S.C. § 308604 — Cooperative agreements and memoranda of understanding
Citation: us:usc:54:308604
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section308604
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3086 (United States African-American Burial Grounds Preservation Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may enter into cooperative agreements and memoranda of understanding with, and provide technical assistance to, the heads of other Federal agencies, States, units of local government, Tribal governments, regional governmental bodies, nonprofit organizations, educational institutions, and private entities—
(1)
to achieve the purposes of this chapter; and
(2)
to ensure effective coordination of the Federal elements and non-Federal elements provided a grant or other assistance under the Program with System units and programs of the Service.
