40 C.F.R. § 142.24 — Administrator's rescission.
Citation: us:cfr:40:142.24
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-142.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 142 (National Primary Drinking Water Regulations Implementation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If, upon notification of a finding by the Administrator under § 142.23, the State takes adequate corrective action before the effective date of the revocation or revised schedule, the Administrator shall rescind the application of his finding to that variance, exemption or schedule.
