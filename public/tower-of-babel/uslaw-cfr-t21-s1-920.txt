21 C.F.R. § 1.920 — What information submitted in a petition requesting a waiver or submitted in comments on such a petition is publicly available?
Citation: us:cfr:21:1.920
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1.920
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1 (General Enforcement Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We will presume that information submitted in a petition requesting a waiver and comments submitted on such a petition does not contain information exempt from public disclosure under part 20 of this chapter and would be made public as part of the docket associated with this request.
