34 U.S.C. § 10595 — Reports
Citation: us:usc:34:10595
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section10595
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 101 (Justice System Improvement); Subchapter XXIX (Grants For Family-Based Substance Abuse Treatment). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An entity that receives a grant under this subchapter during a fiscal year shall submit to the Attorney General, not later than a date specified by the Attorney General, a report that describes and evaluates the effectiveness of that program during such fiscal year that—
(1)
is based on evidence-based data; and
(2)
uses the methods and measurements described in the application of that entity for purposes of evaluating that program.
