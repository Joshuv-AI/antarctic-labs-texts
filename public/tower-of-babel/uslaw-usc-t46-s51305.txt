46 U.S.C. § 51305 — Prohibited basis for appointment
Citation: us:usc:46:51305
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section51305
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part B (Merchant Marine Service); Chapter 513 (United States Merchant Marine Academy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Preference may not be given to an individual for appointment as a cadet at the United States Merchant Marine Academy because one or more members of the individual’s immediate family are alumni of the Academy.
