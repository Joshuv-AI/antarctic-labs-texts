46 C.F.R. § 67.243 — Requirements for instruments subordinating mortgages.
Citation: us:cfr:46:67.243
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-67.243
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 67 (Documentation Of Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An instrument subordinating a mortgage presented for filing and recording must:
(a) Be signed by or on behalf of each mortgagee whose mortgage is being subordinated; and
(b) Recite the following:
(1) The name and address of each mortgagee whose mortgage is being subordinated; and
(2) The name and address of each party holding an interest in the instrument subordinating the mortgage.
