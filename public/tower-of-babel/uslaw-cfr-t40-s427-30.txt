40 C.F.R. § 427.30 — Applicability; description of the asbestos paper (starch binder) subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:427.30
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-427.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 427 (Asbestos Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges resulting from the process in which asbestos, starch binders and other ingredients are used in the manufacture of asbestos paper (starch binder).
