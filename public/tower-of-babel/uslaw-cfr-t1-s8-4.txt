1 C.F.R. § 8.4 — Indexes.
Citation: us:cfr:1:8.4
Creator: Administrative Committee Of The Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-1/section-8.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 1 (General Provisions), Part 8 (Code Of Federal Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-23 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A subject index to the entire Code shall be annually revised and separately published. An agency-prepared index for any individual book may be published with the approval of the Director of the Federal Register.
