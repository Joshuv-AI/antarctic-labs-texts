46 C.F.R. § 294.19 — Nature of award procedure.
Citation: us:cfr:46:294.19
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-294.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 294 (Tanker Security Program (Tsp)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

TSP furthers a public purpose and MARAD does not acquire goods or services through TSP. Therefore, the selection process for awarding TSP Operating Agreements does not constitute an acquisition subject to procurement law or the Federal Acquisition Regulation in title 48 of the Code of Federal Regulations.
