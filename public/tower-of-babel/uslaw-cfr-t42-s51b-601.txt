42 C.F.R. § 51b.601 — b.601 To which programs does this subpart apply?
Citation: us:cfr:42:51b.601
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-51b.601
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 51b (PART 51b—PROJECT GRANTS FOR PREVENTIVE HEALTH SERVICES). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this subpart apply to the award of venereal disease control project grants for research, demonstrations, public information, and education activities which can be applied to achieve improvements in venereal disease prevention and control under section 318(b) of the Act.
