48 C.F.R. § 1325.204 — 1325.204 Evaluating offers of foreign construction material.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1325.204
Creator: Chapter 13—Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1325.204
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1325 (Foreign Acquisition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The designee authorized to specify a percentage higher than the 6 percent that the contracting officer must add to the cost of any foreign construction material proposed for exception from the requirements of the Buy America Act is set forth in CAM 1301.70.
