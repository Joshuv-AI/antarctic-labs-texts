27 C.F.R. § 4.101 — Use of the term “organic.”
Citation: us:cfr:27:4.101
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-4.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 4 (Labeling And Advertising Of Wine). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Use of the term “organic” is optional and is treated as “additional information on labels” under § 4.38(f).
(b) Any use of the term “organic” on a wine label or in advertising of wine must comply with the United States Department of Agriculture's (USDA) National Organic Program rules (7 CFR part 205) as interpreted by the USDA.
(c) This section applies to labels and advertising that use the term “organic” on and after October 21, 2002.
