40 C.F.R. § 436.40 — Applicability; description of the industrial sand subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:436.40
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-436.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 436 (Mineral Mining And Processing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to the mining and the processing of sand and gravel for uses other than construction and fill. These uses include, but are not limited to glassmaking, molding, abrasives, filtration, refractories, and refractory bonding.
