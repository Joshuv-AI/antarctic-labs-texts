2 C.F.R. § 801.137 — Who in the Department of Veterans Affairs may grant an exception to allow an excluded person to participate in a covered transaction?
Citation: us:cfr:2:801.137
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-801.137
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 801 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within the Department of Veterans Affairs, the Secretary of Veterans Affairs, the Under Secretary for Health, the Under Secretary for Benefits, and the Under Secretary for Memorial Affairs each has the authority to grant an exception to allow an excluded person to participate in a covered transaction, as provided in the OMB guidance at 2 CFR 180.135.
