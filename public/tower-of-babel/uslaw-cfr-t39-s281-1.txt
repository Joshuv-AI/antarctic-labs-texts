39 C.F.R. § 281.1 — Notification of firm mailers.
Citation: us:cfr:39:281.1
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-281.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 281 (Firm Mailings Damaged Or Destroyed Through Transportation Accidents Or Catastrophes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever bulk firm mail shipments are involved in transportation accidents or catastrophes, such as train or highway accidents, fire, flood, etc., it will be the responsibility of the sectional center director of customer services at the office of mailing to give known mailers timely notification of the incident and its effect on their mail shipment(s).
