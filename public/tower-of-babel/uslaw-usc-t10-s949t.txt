10 U.S.C. § 949t — Maximum limits
Citation: us:usc:10:949t
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section949t
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 47A (Military Commissions); Subchapter VI (Sentences). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The punishment which a military commission under this chapter may direct for an offense may not exceed such limits as the President or Secretary of Defense may prescribe for that offense.
