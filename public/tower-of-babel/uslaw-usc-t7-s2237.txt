7 U.S.C. § 2237 — Use of field work funds for employment of men with equipment, etc.
Citation: us:usc:7:2237
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2237
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 55 (Department Of Agriculture). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Funds available for field work in the Department of Agriculture shall be available for employment by contract or otherwise of men with equipment, boats, work animals, animal-drawn, and motor-propelled vehicles.
