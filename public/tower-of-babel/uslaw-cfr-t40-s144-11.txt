40 C.F.R. § 144.11 — Prohibition of unauthorized injection.
Citation: us:cfr:40:144.11
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-144.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 144 (Underground Injection Control Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any underground injection, except into a well authorized by rule or except as authorized by permit issued under the UIC program, is prohibited. The construction of any well required to have a permit is prohibited until the permit has been issued.
