15 U.S.C. § 1242 — Introduction, manufacture for introduction, transportation or distribution in interstate commerce; penalty
Citation: us:usc:15:1242
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1242
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 29 (Manufacture, Transportation, Or Distribution Of Switchblade Knives). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever knowingly introduces, or manufactures for introduction, into interstate commerce, or transports or distributes in interstate commerce, any switchblade knife, shall be fined not more than $2,000 or imprisoned not more than five years, or both.
