34 C.F.R. § 35.5 — Investigation of claims.
Citation: us:cfr:34:35.5
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-35.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 35 (Tort Claims Against The Government). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a claim is received, the Department will make such investigation as may be necessary or appropriate for a determination of the validity of the claim.
