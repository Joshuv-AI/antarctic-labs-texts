5 C.F.R. § 843.314 — Amount of survivor annuity where service includes credit for service with a nonappropriated fund instrumentality.
Citation: us:cfr:5:843.314
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-843.314
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 843 (Federal Employees Retirement System—Death Benefits And Employee Refunds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The survivor annuity based on service that includes service with a nonappropriated fund instrumentality made creditable by an election under 5 CFR part 847, subpart D, is computed under 5 CFR part 847, subpart F.
(b) The survivor annuity based on service that includes service with a nonappropriated fund instrumentality made creditable by an election under 5 CFR part 847, subpart H, is computed under 5 CFR part 847, subpart I.
