7 C.F.R. § 29.9233 — When certification will be made.
Citation: us:cfr:7:29.9233
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-29.9233
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 29 (Tobacco Inspection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Certification services for the tobacco shall be made when inspectors are available and such sales will not interfere with scheduled sales on designated auction markets.
