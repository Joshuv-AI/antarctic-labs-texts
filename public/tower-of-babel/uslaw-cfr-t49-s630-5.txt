49 C.F.R. § 630.5 — Failure to report data.
Citation: us:cfr:49:630.5
Creator: Federal Transit Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-630.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 630 (National Transit Database). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Failure to report data in accordance with this part may result in the noncompliant reporting entity being ineligible to receive any funding under 49 U.S.C. chapter 53, directly or indirectly, until such time as a report is filed in accordance with this part.
