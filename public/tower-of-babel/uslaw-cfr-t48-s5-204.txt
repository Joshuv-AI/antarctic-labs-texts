48 C.F.R. § 5.204 — 5.204 Presolicitation notices.
Citation: us:cfr:48:5.204
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-5.204
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 5 (Publicizing Contract Actions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contracting officers must provide access to presolicitation notices through the GPE (see 15.201 and 36.213-2). The contracting officer must synopsize a proposed contract action before issuing any resulting solicitation (see 5.201 and 5.203).
