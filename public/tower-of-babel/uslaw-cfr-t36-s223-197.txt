36 C.F.R. § 223.197 — Civil penalty assessment procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:36:223.197
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-223.197
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 223 (Sale And Disposal Of National Forest System Timber). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Adjudicatory procedures for hearing alleged violations of this Act and its implementing regulations and assessing penalties shall be conducted under the rules of practice governing formal adjudicatory proceedings instituted by the Secretary. Such procedures are found at 7 CFR 1.130, et seq.
