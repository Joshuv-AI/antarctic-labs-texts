30 C.F.R. § 586.111 — How do I submit plans, applications, or notices required by this part?
Citation: us:cfr:30:586.111
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-586.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 586 (Alternate Uses Of Existing Facilities On The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You must submit all plans, applications, or notices required by this part to BOEM at the following address: Office of Renewable Energy Programs, 45600 Woodland Road, Sterling, VA 20166.
(b) Unless otherwise stated, you must submit one paper copy and one electronic copy of all plans, applications, or notices required by this part.
