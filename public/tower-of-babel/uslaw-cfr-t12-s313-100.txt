12 C.F.R. § 313.100 — Refunds.
Citation: us:cfr:12:313.100
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-313.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 313 (Procedures For Collection Of Corporate Debt, Criminal Restitution Debt, And Civil Money Penalty Debt). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If a hearing official determines that a debt is not legally due and owing to the United States, the FDIC shall promptly refund any amount collected by means of administrative wage garnishment.
(b) Unless required by federal law or contract, refunds under this section shall not bear interest.
