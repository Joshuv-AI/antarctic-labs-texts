36 C.F.R. § 902.81 — Payment of fees.
Citation: us:cfr:36:902.81
Creator: Pennsylvania Avenue Development Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-902.81
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 902 (Freedom Of Information Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The fees prescribed in this part may be paid in cash or by check, draft, or postal money order made payable to the Pennsylvania Avenue Development Corporation.
