51 U.S.C. § 40115 — Aviation weather research
Citation: us:usc:51:40115
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section40115
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 401 (Aeronautics); Subchapter II (High Priority Aeronautics Research And Development Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator may carry out a program of collaborative research with the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration on convective weather events, with the goal of significantly improving the reliability of 2-hour to 6-hour aviation weather forecasts.
