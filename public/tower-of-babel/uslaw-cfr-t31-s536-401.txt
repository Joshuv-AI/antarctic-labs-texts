31 C.F.R. § 536.401 — Reference to amended sections.
Citation: us:cfr:31:536.401
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-536.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 536 (Narcotics Trafficking Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise specified, reference to any section of this part or to any regulation, ruling, order, instruction, direction, or license issued pursuant to this part shall be deemed to refer to the same as currently amended.
