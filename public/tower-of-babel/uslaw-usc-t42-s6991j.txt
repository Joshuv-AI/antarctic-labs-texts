42 U.S.C. § 6991j — Use of funds for release prevention and compliance
Citation: us:usc:42:6991j
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section6991j
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 82 (Solid Waste Disposal); Subchapter IX (Regulation Of Underground Storage Tanks). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Funds made available under section 6991m(2)(D) of this title from the Trust Fund may be used to conduct inspections, issue orders, or bring actions under this subchapter—
(1)
by a State, in accordance with a grant or cooperative agreement with the Administrator, of 1 State regulations pertaining to underground storage tanks regulated under this subchapter; and
(2)
by the Administrator, for tanks regulated under this subchapter (including under a State program approved under section 6991c of this title).
