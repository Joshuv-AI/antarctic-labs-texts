12 U.S.C. § 1883 — Insurance rates; report to Congress
Citation: us:usc:12:1883
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section1883
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 19 (Security Measures For Banks And Savings Associations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Federal supervisory agencies shall consult with
(1)
insurers furnishing insurance protection against losses resulting from robberies, burglaries, and larcenies committed against financial institutions referred to in section 1881 of this title, and
(2)
State agencies having supervisory or regulatory responsibilities with respect to such insurers
to determine the feasibility and desirability of premium rate differentials based on the installation, maintenance, and operation of security devices and procedures. The Federal supervisory agencies shall report to the Congress the results of their consultations pursuant to this section not later than two years after July 7, 1968.
