9 C.F.R. § 116.4 — Sterilization and pasteurization records.
Citation: us:cfr:9:116.4
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-116.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 116 (Records And Reports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Records shall be made by means of automatic recording devices or an equivalent accurate and reliable system. Such records shall be identified with the ingredients, equipment, or biological product subjected to sterilization or pasteurization.
