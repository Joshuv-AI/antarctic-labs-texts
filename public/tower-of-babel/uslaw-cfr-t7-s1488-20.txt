7 C.F.R. § 1488.20 — Officials not to benefit.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1488.20
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1488.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1488 (Financing Of Sales Of Agricultural Commodities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No member of or delegate to Congress, or Resident Commissioner, shall be admitted to any share or part of the financing agreement or to any benefit that may arise therefrom, but this provision shall not be construed to extend to the financing agreement if made with a corporation for its general benefit.
