20 C.F.R. § 211.5 — Employee representative compensation.
Citation: us:cfr:20:211.5
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-211.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 211 (Creditable Railroad Compensation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All payments made by a railway labor organization to an individual who is an employee representative as a result of the position or office he occupies with such organization are creditable as compensation, including payments made for services not connected with the representation of employees, except that payments in excess of the annual maximum amount will not be credited.
