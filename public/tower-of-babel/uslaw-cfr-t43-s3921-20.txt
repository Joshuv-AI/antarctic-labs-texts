43 C.F.R. § 3921.20 — Compliance with the National Environmental Policy Act.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3921.20
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3921.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3920 (Oil Shale Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before the BLM will offer a tract for competitive lease sale under subpart 3924, the BLM must prepare a NEPA analysis of the proposed lease area under 40 CFR parts 1500 through 1508 either separately or in conjunction with a land use planning action.
