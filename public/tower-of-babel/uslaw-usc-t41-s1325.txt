41 U.S.C. § 1325 — Annual report
Citation: us:usc:41:1325
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title41-section1325
Context: United States Code, Title 41 (Public Contracts); Chapter 13 (Acquisition Councils); Subchapter III (Federal Acquisition Supply Chain Security). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than December 31 of each year, the Chairperson of the Council shall submit to the appropriate congressional committees and leadership a report on the activities of the Council during the preceding 12-month period.
