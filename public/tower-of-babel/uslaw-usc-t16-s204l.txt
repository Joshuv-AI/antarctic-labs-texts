16 U.S.C. § 204l — Application of Federal Power Act
Citation: us:usc:16:204l
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section204l
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter XXII (Lassen Volcanic National Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of the Federal Power Act [16 U.S.C. 791a et seq.], shall not apply to any lands added to the Lassen Volcanic National Park under the authority of section 204k of this title.
