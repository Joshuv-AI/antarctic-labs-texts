25 C.F.R. § 36.81 — May a homeliving program use support staff or teachers to meet behavioral health staffing requirements?
Citation: us:cfr:25:36.81
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-36.81
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 36 (Minimum Academic Standards For The Basic Education Of Indian Children And National Criteria For Dormitory Situations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No, a homeliving program must not use support staff or teachers to meet behavioral health staffing requirements. The only exception is if the individual support staff employee or teacher has the appropriate behavioral health license or certification.
