41 C.F.R. § 102-2.140 — -2.140 What elements of plain language appear in the FMR?
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-2.140
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-2.140
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-2 (2—Federal Management Regulation System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The FMR is written in a “plain language” regulatory style. This style is easy to read and uses a question and answer format directed at the reader, active voice, shorter sentences, and, where appropriate, personal pronouns.
