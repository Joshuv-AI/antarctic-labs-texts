21 U.S.C. § 64 — Regulations for enforcement
Citation: us:usc:21:64
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section64
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 3 (Filled Milk). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Health and Human Services is authorized and directed to make and enforce such regulations as may in his judgment be necessary to carry out the purposes of this chapter.
