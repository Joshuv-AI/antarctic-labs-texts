18 U.S.C. § 1072 — Concealing escaped prisoner
Citation: us:usc:18:1072
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1072
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 49 (Fugitives From Justice). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever willfully harbors or conceals any prisoner after his escape from the custody of the Attorney General or from a Federal penal or correctional institution, shall be imprisoned not more than three years.
