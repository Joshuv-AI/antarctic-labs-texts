13 C.F.R. § 123.806 — Interference with SBA Disaster Loan Usage.
Citation: us:cfr:13:123.806
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-123.806
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 123 (Disaster Loan Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) State or local government officials must not unlawfully interfere with, impede, or disrupt the otherwise lawful use of SBA Disaster Loan proceeds under this Part in the name of enforcing a preempted State or Local Requirement.
(b) State or Local Requirements are preempted only to the extent that they result in a condition identified by § 123.803.
