Retraction Note: Mathematical modeling and statistical analysis of breast cancer drugs using M-polynomial indices for the physical properties
Authors: Qasem M. Tawhari, Muhammad Naeem, Saba Maqbool, Abdul Rauf, Oladele Oyelakin
Published: 2026

Retraction of: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-07067-6, published 19 August 2025

The Editors have retracted this Article.

After publication, concerns were raised about the existence of certain references cited in this Article to support statements made about cancer treatment and the medical properties of daunorubicin. The Editors were unable to verify the sources of references 31–35 and 37–43. The Editors therefore no longer have confidence in this Article.

The Authors did not respond to correspondence from the Editors regarding this retraction.
