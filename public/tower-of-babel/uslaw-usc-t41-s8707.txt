41 U.S.C. § 8707 — Criminal penalties
Citation: us:usc:41:8707
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title41-section8707
Context: United States Code, Title 41 (Public Contracts); Chapter 87 (Kickbacks). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A person that knowingly and willfully engages in conduct prohibited by section 8702 of this title shall be fined under title 18, imprisoned for not more than 10 years, or both.
