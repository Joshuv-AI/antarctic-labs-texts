46 C.F.R. § 58.25-60 — -60 Non-duplicated hydraulic rudder actuators.
Citation: us:cfr:46:58.25-60
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-58.25-60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 58 (Main And Auxiliary Machinery And Related Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Non-duplicated hydraulic rudder actuators may be installed in the steering gear control systems on vessels of less than 100,000 deadweight tons. These actuators must meet IMO A.467(XII) (incorporated by reference, see § 58.03-1) and be acceptable to the Commanding Officer, Marine Safety Center.
