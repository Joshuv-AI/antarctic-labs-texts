20 C.F.R. § 404.1074 — Farm crew leader who is self-employed.
Citation: us:cfr:20:404.1074
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-404.1074
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 404 (Federal Old-Age, Survivors And Disability Insurance (1950- )). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you are a farm crew leader and are deemed the employer of the workers as described in § 404.1010, we consider you to be engaged in a trade or business. This includes services performed in furnishing workers to perform agricultural labor for others, as well as services performed as a member of the crew.
