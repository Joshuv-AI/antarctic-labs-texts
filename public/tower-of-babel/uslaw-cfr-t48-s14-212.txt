48 C.F.R. § 14.212 — 14.212 Economic purchase quantities (supplies).
Citation: us:cfr:48:14.212
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-14.212
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 14 (Sealed Bidding). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contracting officers shall comply with the economic purchase quantity planning requirements for supplies in subpart 7.2. See 7.203 for instructions regarding use of the provision at 52.207-4, Economic Purchase Quantity—Supplies, and 7.204 for guidance on handling responses to that provision.
