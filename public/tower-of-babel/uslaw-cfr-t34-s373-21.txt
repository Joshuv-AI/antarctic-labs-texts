34 C.F.R. § 373.21 — What are the reporting requirements under this part?
Citation: us:cfr:34:373.21
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-373.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 373 (Rehabilitation National Activities Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In addition to the program and fiscal reporting requirements in 34 CFR 75.720 and 2 CFR 200.327 that are applicable to projects funded under this program, the Secretary may require that recipients of grants under this part submit information determined by the Secretary to be necessary to measure project outcomes and performance, including any data needed to comply with the Government Performance and Results Act.
(b) Specific reporting requirements for competitions will be identified by the Secretary and published in the Federal Register.
