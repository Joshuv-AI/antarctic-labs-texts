49 C.F.R. § 230.83 — Cylinder cocks.
Citation: us:cfr:49:230.83
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-230.83
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 230 (Steam Locomotive Inspection And Maintenance Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each steam locomotive shall be equipped with cylinder cocks which can be operated from the cab of the steam locomotive. All cylinder cocks shall be maintained in a safe and suitable condition for service.
