20 C.F.R. § 616.10 — Reuse of employment and wages.
Citation: us:cfr:20:616.10
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-616.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 616 (Interstate Arrangement For Combining Employment And Wages). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employment and wages which have been used under this arrangement for a determination of benefits which establishes a benefit year shall not thereafter be used by any State as the basis for another monetary determination of benefits.
