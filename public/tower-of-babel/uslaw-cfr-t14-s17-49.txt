14 C.F.R. § 17.49 — Sanctions.
Citation: us:cfr:14:17.49
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-17.49
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 17 (Procedures For Protests And Contract Disputes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If any party or its representative fails to comply with an Order or Directive of the ODRA, the ODRA may enter such orders and take such other actions as it deems necessary and in the interest of justice.
