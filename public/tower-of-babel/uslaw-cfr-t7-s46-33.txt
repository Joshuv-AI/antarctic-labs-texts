7 C.F.R. § 46.33 — Conversion of funds.
Citation: us:cfr:7:46.33
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-46.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 46 (Requirements (Other Than Administrative Procedures) Under The Perishable Agricultural Commodities Act, 1930). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any licensee who collects or receives funds for or on behalf of another person or firm in connection with produce shall not make any use or disposition of such funds in his possession or control that will endanger or impair faithful and prompt payment to the owner or consignor of the produce or to any other person having a financial interest therein.
