Abstract
. In recent years, there has been significant interest in characterizing the induced subgraph obstructions to bounded treewidth and pathwidth. While this has recently been resolved for pathwidth, the case of treewidth remains open, and prior work has reduced the problem to understanding the layered-wheel-like obstructions – graphs that contain large complete minor models with each branching set inducing a path, exclude large walls as induced minors, exclude large complete bipartite graphs as induced minors, and exclude large complete subgraphs. There are various constructions of such graphs, but they are all rather involved. In this paper, we present a simple construction of layered-wheel-like graphs with arbitrarily large treewidth. Three notable features of our construction are: (a) the vertices of degree at least four can be made arbitrarily far apart; (b) the girth can be made arbitrarily large; and (c) every outerstring induced subgraph of the graphs from our construction has treewidth bounded by an absolute constant. In contrast, among several previously known constructions of layered wheels, none achieves (a); at most one satisfies either (b) or (c); and none satisfies both (b) and (c) simultaneously. In particular, this is related to a former conjecture of Trotignon, that every graph with large enough treewidth, excluding large walls and large complete bipartite graphs as induced minors, and large complete subgraphs, must contain an outerstring induced subgraph of large treewidth. Our construction provides the first counterexample to this conjecture that can also be made to have arbitrarily large girth.

We call these obstructions

Our construction is substantially simpler than all previous ones and simultaneously achieves several key properties, no two of which are attained individually by any earlier construction. Explicitly, our main result is as follows.

Theorem 1.4
.

There exist
L,t_{0}\in\mathbb{N}
such that for all
g,k\in\mathbb{N}
, there is a graph

with the following properties.

(i)

There is a linear model in

isomorphic to

. In particular,

(ii)

(iii)

.

(iv)

has girth at least

(v)

No preexisting construction of layered wheels satisfies (

[
22
]
, allows for arbitrarily large girth, and the most recent construction of layered wheels
[

]
(which is also the most complicated one) is the only one that achieves (

Property (

[
23
]
. A

Conjecture 1.5

(Trotignon
[

]
)
.

For all

, there exists

Note that

, we have

\displaystyle Y^{\prime}_{B,>3}

\displaystyle K^{\prime}_{B,\leq 3}

\displaystyle=\{N_{H}^{M}[b]\colon b\in Y^{\prime}_{B,\leq 3}\},

\displaystyle K^{\prime}_{M}

\displaystyle=K^{\prime}\cap M_{H^{\prime}},

for

is a weak weight function on

. To bound the treewidth of

Lemma 5.9

(

Lemma 5.10
.

At least one of the following holds.

(ii)

Suppose instead that case (

Lemma 5.12
.

Let

be as in Corollary

. Then

(i)

K^{\prime\prime}_{\mathcal{N}}\cup N_{H^{\prime\prime}}(K^{\prime\prime}_{\mathcal{D}})
is a

-balanced separator in

, and

(ii)

|K^{\prime\prime}_{\mathcal{N}}\cup N_{H^{\prime\prime}}(K^{\prime\prime}_{\mathcal{D}})|\leq 9(L^{\prime\prime}+1)
.

Proof.

Let
C
be a component of
H^{\prime\prime}\setminus(K^{\prime\prime}_{\mathcal{N}}\cup N_{H^{\prime\prime}}(K^{\prime\prime}_{\mathcal{D}}))
. First suppose that
V(C)\cap K^{\prime\prime}_{\mathcal{D}}\neq\varnothing
, and let
D\in V(C)\cap K^{\prime\prime}_{\mathcal{D}}
. Since
N_{H^{\prime\prime}}(D)\subseteq K^{\prime\prime}_{\mathcal{N}}\cup N_{H^{\prime\prime}}(K^{\prime\prime}_{\mathcal{D}})
, we have
\deg_{H^{\prime\prime}\setminus(K^{\prime\prime}_{\mathcal{N}}\cup N_{H^{\prime\prime}}(K^{\prime\prime}_{\mathcal{D}}))}(D)=0
, and so

. As

is a component of

, it follows that

. Now suppose that
V(C)\cap K^{\prime\prime}_{\mathcal{D}}=\varnothing
. Then
C
is a connected induced subgraph of
H^{\prime\prime}\setminus K^{\prime\prime}
, so in particular, there exists a component
C^{*}
of
H^{\prime\prime}\setminus K^{\prime\prime}
such that

. It follows that

. This proves (

).

To see that (
ii
) holds, we observe that

is obtained from

by removing a subset of its elements and replacing each removed element by at most nine new elements. Since

, the bound follows.

We are ready to translate the balanced separators for

Lemma 5.13
.

Let
K^{*}=K^{\prime}_{S}\cup K^{\prime}_{M}\cup K^{\prime}_{B,\leq 3}\cup K^{\prime\prime}_{\mathcal{N}}\cup N_{H^{\prime\prime}}(K^{\prime\prime}_{\mathcal{D}})
, and let

. Then the following hold.

(i)

is a

-balanced separator in

.

(ii)

|K|\leq 21+9(L^{\prime\prime}+1)

Proof.

The induced subgraph

of
H
, which will be denoted by
F
, has vertex set
\{n\colon\{n\}\in\mathcal{N}\}\cup(\bigcup_{D\in\mathcal{D}}V(D))
. Thus, for every component
C
of
F\setminus(\{n\colon\{n\}\in K^{\prime\prime}_{\mathcal{N}}\cup N_{H^{\prime\prime}}(K^{\prime\prime}_{\mathcal{D}})\})
, there is a corresponding component

of
H^{\prime\prime}
such that

, and so it follows that

. This proves (

).

Since
K^{\prime}_{S}
,
K^{\prime}_{M}
, and

are all subsets of

, they each have size at most

. Every element of
