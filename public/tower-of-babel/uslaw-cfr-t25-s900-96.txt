25 C.F.R. § 900.96 — How can Indian tribes or tribal organizations learn about BIA and IHS excess property?
Citation: us:cfr:25:900.96
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior, And Indian Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-900.96
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 900 (Contracts Under The Indian Self-Determination And Education Assistance Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary shall not less than annually send to Indian tribes and tribal organizations a listing of all excess BIA or IHS personal property before reporting the property to GSA or to any other Federal agency as excess. The listing shall identify the agency official to whom a request for donation shall be submitted.
