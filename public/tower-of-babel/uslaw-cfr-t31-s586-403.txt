31 C.F.R. § 586.403 — Transactions ordinarily incident to a licensed transaction.
Citation: us:cfr:31:586.403
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-586.403
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 586 (Chinese Military-Industrial Complex Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any transaction ordinarily incident to a licensed transaction and necessary to give effect thereto is also authorized.
