37 C.F.R. § 210.11 — Record companies using individual download licenses.
Citation: us:cfr:37:210.11
Creator: U.S. Copyright Office, Library Of Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-210.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 210 (Compulsory License For Making And Distributing Physical And Digital Phonorecords Of Nondramatic Musical Works). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A record company that obtains an individual download license under 17 U.S.C. 115(b)(3) shall provide statements of account and pay royalties as a compulsory licensee in accordance with this subpart.
