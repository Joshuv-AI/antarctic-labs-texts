51 U.S.C. § 51301 — Definitions

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 513 (Space Resource Commercial Exploration And Utilization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter:
(1) Asteroid resource.—
The term “asteroid resource” means a space resource found on or within a single asteroid.
(2) Space resource.—
(A) In general.—
The term “space resource” means an abiotic resource in situ in outer space.
(B) Inclusions.—
The term “space resource” includes water and minerals.
(3) United states citizen.—
The term “United States citizen” has the meaning given the term “citizen of the United States” in section 50902.
