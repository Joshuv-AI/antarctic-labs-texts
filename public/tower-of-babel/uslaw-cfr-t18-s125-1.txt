18 C.F.R. § 125.1 — Promulgation.
Citation: us:cfr:18:125.1
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-125.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 125 (Preservation Of Records Of Public Utilities And Licensees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This Part is prescribed and promulgated as the regulations governing the preservation of records by public utilities subject to the jurisdiction of the Commission and by licensees holding licenses issued by the Commission, to the extent and in the manner set forth therein.
