RETRACTED ARTICLE: Macrogenomic analysis of the previous crops effects on tobacco soil microbiomes
Authors: Xin Liu, Yan Chen, Zhufeng Shi, Zhuli Shi, Te Pu, Yijie Zhang, Feifei He, Xuewei Li, Yu Wang, Jianpeng Jia, Biao He, Peiwen Yang
Published: 2025

The Editors have retracted this Article due to the inability to verify its authorship.

Following publication the Authors contacted the Editors requesting that the paper be withdrawn due to one of the Authors being incorrectly included. The Editors requested information confirming the identities and contributions of all Authors, but the reply provided did not address those.

All Authors disagree with this retraction.
