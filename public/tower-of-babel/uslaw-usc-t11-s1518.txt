11 U.S.C. § 1518 — Subsequent information
Citation: us:usc:11:1518
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section1518
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 15 (Ancillary And Other Cross-Border Cases); Subchapter III (Recognition Of A Foreign Proceeding And Relief). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

From the time of filing the petition for recognition of a foreign proceeding, the foreign representative shall file with the court promptly a notice of change of status concerning—
(1)
any substantial change in the status of such foreign proceeding or the status of the foreign representative’s appointment; and
(2)
any other foreign proceeding regarding the debtor that becomes known to the foreign representative.
