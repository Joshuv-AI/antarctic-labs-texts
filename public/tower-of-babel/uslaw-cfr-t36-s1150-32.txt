36 C.F.R. § 1150.32 — Extension of time or postponement.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1150.32
Creator: Architectural And Transportation Barriers Compliance Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1150.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1150 (Practice And Procedures For Compliance Hearings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Requests for extension of time shall be addressed to the judge and served on all parties and participants. Requests should set forth the reasons for the application.
(b) If made promptly, answers to requests for extension of time are permitted.
(c) The judge may grant the extension upon a showing of good cause by the applicant.
