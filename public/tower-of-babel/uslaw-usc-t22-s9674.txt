22 U.S.C. § 9674 — Applicability of certain provisions of law

Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 103 (Better Utilization Of Investments Leading To Development); Subchapter V (Conditions, Restrictions, And Prohibitions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Subsections (g), (l), (m), and (n) of section 2197 of this title shall apply with respect to the Corporation to the same extent and in the same manner as such subsections applied with respect to the Overseas Private Investment Corporation on the day before October 5, 2018.
