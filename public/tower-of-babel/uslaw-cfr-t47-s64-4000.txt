47 C.F.R. § 64.4000 — Basis and purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:47:64.4000
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-64.4000
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 64 (Miscellaneous Rules Relating To Common Carriers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Basis. The rules in this subpart are issued pursuant to the Communications Act of 1934, as amended.
(b) Purpose. The purpose of these rules is to facilitate the timely and accurate establishment, termination, and billing of customer telephone service accounts.
