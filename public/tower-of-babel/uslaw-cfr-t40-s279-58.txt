40 C.F.R. § 279.58 — Off-site shipments of used oil.
Citation: us:cfr:40:279.58
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-279.58
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 279 (Standards For The Management Of Used Oil). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Used oil processors/re-refiners who initiate shipments of used oil off-site must ship the used oil using a used oil transporter who has obtained an EPA identification number.
