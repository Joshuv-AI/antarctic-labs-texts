7 C.F.R. § 4280.54 — Construction procurement requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:7:4280.54
Creator: Rural Business-Cooperative Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-4280.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 4280 (Loans And Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Construction, including bidding and awarding of contracts, must be conducted in a manner that provides maximum open and free competition.
