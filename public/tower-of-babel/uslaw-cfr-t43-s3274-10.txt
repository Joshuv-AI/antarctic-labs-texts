43 C.F.R. § 3274.10 — Do I need a commercial use permit to start commercial operations?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3274.10
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3274.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3200 (Geothermal Resource Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must have a commercial use permit approved by BLM before you begin commercial operations from a Federal lease, a Federal unit, or a utilization facility.
