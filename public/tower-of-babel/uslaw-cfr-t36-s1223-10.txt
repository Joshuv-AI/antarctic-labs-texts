36 C.F.R. § 1223.10 — What is the purpose of Part 1223?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1223.10
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1223.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1223 (Managing Vital Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Part 1223 specifies policies and procedures needed to establish a program to identify, protect, and manage vital records as part of an agency's continuity of operation plan designed to meet emergency management responsibilities.
