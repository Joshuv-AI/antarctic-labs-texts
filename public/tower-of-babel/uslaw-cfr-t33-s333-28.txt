33 C.F.R. § 333.28 — Comments received on an environmental impact statement.
Citation: us:cfr:33:333.28
Creator: Corps Of Engineers, Department Of The Army, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-333.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 333 (Processing Of Department Of The Army Permits And 33 U.S.C. 408 Permissions, National Environmental Policy Act Implementing Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For permit applications or requests for permissions to be decided at the district level, the District Engineer should consider incoming comments and provide responses in the environmental impact statement when substantive issues are raised. For permit applications or requests for permissions to be decided at a higher authority, the District Engineer shall forward any comment letters together with appropriate responses to the higher authority.
