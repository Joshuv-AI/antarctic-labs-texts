42 C.F.R. § 493.1201 — Condition: Bacteriology.
Citation: us:cfr:42:493.1201
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-493.1201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 493 (Laboratory Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the laboratory provides services in the subspecialty of Bacteriology, the laboratory must meet the requirements specified in §§ 493.1230 through 493.1256, § 493.1261, and §§ 493.1281 through 493.1299.
