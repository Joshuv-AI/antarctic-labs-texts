7 C.F.R. § 65.240 — Retailer.
Citation: us:cfr:7:65.240
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-65.240
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 65 (Country Of Origin Labeling Of Lamb, Chicken, And Goat Meat, Perishable Agricultural Commodities, Macadamia Nuts, Pecans, Peanuts, And Ginseng). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Retailer means any person subject to be licensed as a retailer under the Perishable Agricultural Commodities Act of 1930 (7 U.S.C. 499a(b)).
