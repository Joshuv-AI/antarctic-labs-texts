46 C.F.R. § 177.1010 — Safety glazing materials.
Citation: us:cfr:46:177.1010
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-177.1010
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 177 (Construction And Arrangement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Glass and other glazing material used in windows accessible to passengers and crew must be of material that will not break into dangerous fragments if fractured.
