42 C.F.R. § 137.20 — What is required during the planning phase?
Citation: us:cfr:42:137.20
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-137.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 137 (Tribal Self-Governance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The planning phase must be conducted to the satisfaction of the Indian Tribe and must include:
(a) legal and budgetary research; and
(b) internal Tribal government planning and organizational preparation relating to the administration of health programs.
