12 C.F.R. § 628.44 — Securitization exposures to which the SSFA and gross-up approach do not apply.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 628 (Capital Adequacy Of System Institutions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) General requirement. A System institution must assign a 1,250-percent risk weight to all securitization exposures to which the System institution does not apply the SSFA or the gross up approach under § 628.43.
