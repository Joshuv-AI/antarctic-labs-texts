38 U.S.C. § 1722B — Copayments: waiver of collection of copayments for telehealth and telemedicine visits of veterans
Citation: us:usc:38:1722B
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section1722B
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 17 (Hospital, Nursing Home, Domiciliary, And Medical Care); Subchapter III (Miscellaneous Provisions Relating To Hospital And Nursing Home Care And Medical Treatment Of Veterans). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may waive the imposition or collection of copayments for telehealth and telemedicine visits of veterans under the laws administered by the Secretary.
