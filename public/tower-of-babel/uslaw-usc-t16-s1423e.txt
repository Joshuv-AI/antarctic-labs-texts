16 U.S.C. § 1423e — Votes taken by the United States section on matters before the Commission
Citation: us:usc:16:1423e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section1423e
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 31 (Marine Mammal Protection); Subchapter VI (Polar Bears). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In accordance with paragraph 3 of article 8 of the Agreement, the United States section, made up of commissioners appointed by the President, shall vote on any issue before the United States-Russia Polar Bear Commission only if there is no disagreement between the United States commissioners regarding the vote.
