Probabilistic generalization of Spivey-type relation for degenerate Bell polynomials
Taekyun Kim, Dae San Kim

Abstract
. Following Spivey’s pivotal discovery of a recurrence relation for Bell numbers, significant research has emerged concerning various generalizations of Bell numbers and polynomials. For example, Kim and Kim established a Spivey-type recurrence relation specifically for degenerate Bell and Dowling polynomials. In this paper, we extend this work by deriving a probabilistic generalization of Spivey-type recurrence relations for both degenerate Bell and degenerate r r -Bell polynomials.

11B73; 11B83; 60-08

1.
Introduction

The Stirling number of the second kind
{n\brace k}
enumerates the number of partitiions of a set with
n
objects into
k
nonempty disjoint subsets. The sum of the Stirling numbers of the second kind
{n\brace k}
, called the Bell number and denoted by
\phi_{n}
, counts the total number of partitions of a set with
n
objects. That is,
\phi_{n}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n\brace k}
. The Bell polynomial
\phi_{n}(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n\brace k}x^{k}
is a natural polynomial extension of the Bell number
\phi_{n}
(see
[
1
,
2
,
4
,
9
,
11
]
).

Spivey found the following recurrence relation on the Bell numbers (see
[
11
]
):

\phi_{l+n}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\sum_{m=0}^{l}\binom{l}{m}{n\brace k}k^{l-m}\phi_{m}.

His relation was extended by Gould-Quaintance to the Bell polynomials (see
[
5
]
):

\phi_{l+n}(y)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\sum_{m=0}^{l}\binom{l}{m}{n\brace k}k^{l-m}y^{k}\phi_{m}(y).

Recently, in
[
8
]
, Kim-Kim discovered the following relation for the degenerate Bell polynomials (see (
6
), (
7
), (
8
)):

(1)

\phi_{l+n,\lambda}(y)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\sum_{m=0}^{l}\binom{l}{m}{n\brace k}_{\lambda}(k-n\lambda)_{l-m,\lambda}y^{k}\phi_{m,\lambda}(y).

Assume that
Y
is a random variable whose moment generating function exists in a neighborhood of the origin:

(2)

E\Big[e^{tY}\Big]=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}E\big[Y^{n}\big]\frac{t^{n}}{n!},\quad(|t|<r),

exists for some
r>0
, (see
[
3
]
).

Let
(Y_{j})_{j\geq 1}
be a sequence of mutually independent copies of the random variable
Y
, and let

(3)

S_{k}=Y_{1}+Y_{2}+\cdots+Y_{k},\ (k\geq 1),\ \mathrm{with}\quad S_{0}=0.

The aim of this paper is to generalize the Spivey-type relation for the degenerate Bell polynomials in (
1
) to that for the probabilistic degenerate Bell polynomials associated with
Y
(see (
13
), (
14
)). Indeed, we obtain (see Theorem 2.1):

(4)

\displaystyle\phi_{l+n,\lambda}^{Y}(y)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\sum_{m=0}^{l}\binom{l}{m}\frac{y^{k}}{k!}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=n}\binom{n}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\times E\bigg[(S_{k}-n\lambda)_{l-m,\lambda}\prod_{i=1}^{k}(Y_{i})_{l_{i},\lambda}\bigg]\phi_{m,\lambda}^{Y}(y),

where
l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{k}
are positive integers.

Furthermore, the relation in (
4
) is extended to the probabilistic degenerate
r
-Bell polynomials associated with
Y
(see (
22
)) as follows (see Theorem 2.3):

\displaystyle\phi_{j+n,\lambda}^{(r,Y)}(y)=\sum_{l=0}^{n}\binom{n}{l}(r)_{n-l,\lambda}\sum_{k=0}^{l}\sum_{m=0}^{j}\frac{y^{k}}{k!}\binom{j}{m}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=l}\binom{l}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}

\displaystyle\qquad\times E\bigg[\big(S_{k}-n\lambda)_{j-m,\lambda}\prod_{i=1}^{k}(Y_{i})_{l_{i},\lambda}\bigg]\phi_{m,\lambda}^{(r,Y)}(y).

The degenerate exponentials are defined as

(5)

e_{\lambda}^{x}(t)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(x)_{n,\lambda}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}=(1+\lambda t)^{\frac{x}{\lambda}},\quad e_{\lambda}(t)=e_{\lambda}^{1}(t),\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [8,9,12]),

where

(x)_{0,\lambda}=1,\quad(x)_{n,\lambda}=x(x-\lambda)(x-2\lambda)\cdots\big(x-(n-1)\lambda\big),\ (n\geq 1).

The degenerate Stirling numbers of the second kind are defined by

(6)

(x)_{n,\lambda}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n\brace k}_{\lambda}(x)_{k},\quad(n\geq 0),\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [9]),

where

(x)_{0}=1,\quad(x)_{n}=x(x-1)\cdots(x-n+1),\ (n\geq 1).

The degenerate Bell polynomials are given by

(7)

\phi_{n,\lambda}(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n\brace k}_{\lambda}x^{k},\quad(n\geq 0),\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [9]).

When
x=1,\ \phi_{n,\lambda}=\phi_{n,\lambda}(1)
are called the degenerate Bell numbers. Note that

\lim_{\lambda\rightarrow 0}\phi_{n,\lambda}(x)=\phi_{n}(x),\quad\lim_{\lambda\rightarrow 0}{n\brace k}_{\lambda}={n\brace k}.

From (
7
), we have

(8)

e^{x(e_{\lambda}(t)-1)}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\phi_{n,\lambda}(x)\frac{t^{n}}{n!},\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [9]).

For
r\in\mathbb{N}
and
n\geq 0
, the
r
-Stirling numbers of the second kind are defined by

(9)

(x+r)_{n,\lambda}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n+r\brace k+r}_{r,\lambda}(x)_{k},\quad(n\geq 0),\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [8]).

In view of (
7
), the degenerate
r
-Bell polynomials are given by

(10)

\phi_{n,\lambda}^{(r)}(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n+r\brace k+r}_{r,\lambda}x^{k},\quad(n\geq 0),\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [8]).

When
x=1,\ \phi_{n,\lambda}^{(r)}=\phi_{n,\lambda}^{(r)}(1)
are called the degenerate
r
-Bell numbers.

We note that

(11)

g(x+t)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{g^{(n)}(x)}{n!}t^{n}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{t^{n}D_{x}^{n}}{n!}g(x)=e^{tD_{x}}g(x),\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [7]),

where
D_{x}=\frac{d}{dx}
.

In
[
9
]
, the probabilistic degenerate Stirling numbers of the second kind associated with
Y
are defined by

(12)

\frac{1}{k!}\Big(E\big[e_{\lambda}^{Y}(t)\big]-1\Big)^{k}=\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}{n\brace k}_{Y,\lambda}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

Thus, by (
12
), we get

{n\brace k}_{Y,\lambda}=\frac{1}{k!}\sum_{l=0}^{k}\binom{k}{l}(-1)^{k-l}E\big[(S_{l})_{n,\lambda}\big],\quad(0\leq k\leq n).

In view of (
8
), the probabilistic degenerate Bell polynomials associated with
Y
are defined by

(13)

e^{x\big(E[e_{\lambda}^{Y}(t)]-1\big)}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\phi_{n,\lambda}^{Y}(x)\frac{t^{n}}{n!},\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [9]).

By (
10
) and (
12
), we get

(14)

\phi_{n,\lambda}^{Y}(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n\brace k}_{Y,\lambda}x^{k},\quad(n\geq 0),

and

\phi_{n+1,\lambda}^{Y}(x)=x\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}E\Big[(Y)_{k+1,\lambda}\Big]\phi_{n-k,\lambda}^{Y}(x),\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [9]).

2.
Probabilistic generalization of Spivey-type relation for degenerate Bell polynomials

Let
Y,(Y_{j})_{j\geq 1}
, and
S_{k},\,\,(k\geq 0)
, be as in (
2
) and (
3
).
By (
5
), we get

(15)

\displaystyle e_{\lambda}^{Y}(x+t)

\displaystyle=\big(1+\lambda(x+t)\big)^{\frac{Y}{\lambda}}=(1+\lambda x)^{\frac{Y}{\lambda}}\bigg(1+\frac{\lambda t}{1+\lambda x}\bigg)^{\frac{Y}{\lambda}}

\displaystyle=e_{\lambda}^{Y}(x)e_{\lambda}^{Y}\bigg(\frac{t}{1+\lambda x}\bigg).

From (
11
) and (
15
), we note that

(16)

\displaystyle e^{tD_{x}}e^{y\big(E\big[e_{\lambda}^{Y}(x)\big]-1\big)}

\displaystyle=e^{y\big(E\big[e_{\lambda}^{Y}(x+t)\big]-1\big)}=e^{y\big(E\big[e_{\lambda}^{Y}(x)\big(e_{\lambda}^{Y}\big(\frac{t}{1+\lambda x}\big)-1\big)+e_{\lambda}^{Y}(x)]-1\big)}

\displaystyle=e^{yE\big[e_{\lambda}^{Y}(x)\big(e_{\lambda}^{Y}\big(\frac{t}{1+\lambda x}\big)-1\big)\big]}e^{y\big(E[e_{\lambda}^{Y}(x)]-1\big)}.

Now, we observe that

(17)

\displaystyle e^{yE\big[e_{\lambda}^{Y}(x)\big(e_{\lambda}^{Y}\big(\frac{t}{1+\lambda x}\big)-1\big)\big]}=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{y^{k}}{k!}\bigg(E\bigg[e_{\lambda}^{Y}(x)\bigg(e_{\lambda}^{Y}\bigg(\frac{t}{1+\lambda x}\bigg)-1\bigg)\bigg]\bigg)^{k}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{y^{k}}{k!}E\bigg[e_{\lambda}^{Y_{1}+\cdots+Y_{k}}(x)\bigg(e_{\lambda}^{Y_{1}}\bigg(\frac{t}{1+\lambda x}\bigg)-1\bigg)\cdots\bigg(e_{\lambda}^{Y_{k}}\bigg(\frac{t}{1+\lambda x}\bigg)-1\bigg)\bigg]

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{y^{k}}{k!}\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}\bigg(\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=n}\binom{n}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}E\Big[e_{\lambda}^{S_{k}}(x)(Y_{1})_{l_{1},\lambda}(Y_{2})_{l_{2},\lambda}\cdots(Y_{k})_{l_{k},\lambda}\Big]\bigg)\frac{\big(\frac{t}{1+\lambda x}\big)^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{y^{k}}{k!}\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}\bigg(\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=n}\binom{n}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}E\Big[e_{\lambda}^{S_{k}-n\lambda}(x)(Y_{1})_{l_{1},\lambda}(Y_{2})_{l_{2},\lambda}\cdots(Y_{k})_{l_{k},\lambda}\Big]\bigg)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\bigg(\sum_{k=0}^{n}\frac{y^{k}}{k!}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=n}\binom{n}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}E\Big[e_{\lambda}^{S_{k}-n\lambda}(x)\prod_{i=1}^{k}(Y_{i})_{l_{i},\lambda}\Big]\bigg)\frac{t^{n}}{n!},

where
l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l
are positive integers.

By (
13
), (
16
), and (
17
), we get

(18)

\displaystyle e^{tD_{x}}e^{y\big(E[e_{\lambda}^{Y}(x)]-1\big)}=e^{yE\big[e_{\lambda}^{Y}(x)\big(e_{\lambda}^{Y}\big(\frac{t}{1+\lambda x}\big)-1\big)\big]}e^{y\big(E[e_{\lambda}^{Y}(x)]-1\big)}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{k=0}^{n}\frac{y^{k}}{k!}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=n}\binom{n}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\times\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}E\bigg[(S_{k}-n\lambda)_{j,\lambda}\prod_{i=1}^{k}(Y_{i})_{l_{i},\lambda}\bigg]\frac{x^{j}}{j!}\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\phi_{m,\lambda}^{Y}(y)\frac{x^{m}}{m!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{k=0}^{n}\frac{y^{k}}{k!}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=n}\binom{n}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\times\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}\sum_{m=0}^{l}\binom{l}{m}E\bigg[(S_{k}-n\lambda)_{l-m,\lambda}\prod_{i=1}^{k}(Y_{i})_{l_{i},\lambda}\bigg]\phi_{m,\lambda}^{Y}(y)\frac{x^{l}}{l!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}\sum_{k=0}^{n}\sum_{m=0}^{l}\binom{l}{m}\frac{y^{k}}{k!}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=n}\binom{n}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\times E\bigg[(S_{k}-n\lambda)_{l-m,\lambda}\prod_{i=1}^{k}(Y_{i})_{l_{i},\lambda}\bigg]\phi_{m,\lambda}^{Y}(y)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}\frac{x^{l}}{l!}.

On the other hand, by (
13
), we get

(19)

\displaystyle e^{tD_{x}}e^{y\big(E[e_{\lambda}^{Y}(x)]-1\big)}

\displaystyle=e^{tD_{x}}\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}\phi_{l,\lambda}^{Y}(y)\frac{x^{l}}{l!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}D_{x}^{n}\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}\phi_{l,\lambda}^{Y}(y)\frac{x^{l}}{l!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}\phi_{l+n,\lambda}^{Y}(y)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}\frac{x^{l}}{l!}.

Therefore, by (
18
) and (
19
), we obtain the following theorem.

Theorem 2.1
.

For
n,l\geq 0
, we have

(20)

\displaystyle\phi_{l+n,\lambda}^{Y}(y)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\sum_{m=0}^{l}\binom{l}{m}\frac{y^{k}}{k!}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=n}\binom{n}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}

\displaystyle\times E\bigg[(S_{k}-n\lambda)_{l-m,\lambda}\prod_{i=1}^{k}(Y_{i})_{l_{i},\lambda}\bigg]\phi_{m,\lambda}^{Y}(y),

where
l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{k}
are positive integers.

When
Y=1
, from (
20
) we have the following relation in (
1
):

\displaystyle\phi_{l+n,\lambda}(y)

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\sum_{m=0}^{l}\binom{l}{m}\big(k-n\lambda\big)_{l-m,\lambda}y^{k}\phi_{m,\lambda}(y)

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\quad\times\frac{1}{k!}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=n}\binom{n}{l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{k}}\prod_{i=1}^{k}(1)_{l_{i},\lambda}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\sum_{m=0}^{l}\binom{l}{m}{n\brace k}_{\lambda}(k-n\lambda)_{l-m,\lambda}y^{k}\phi_{m,\lambda}(y).

Here we note that

{n\brace k}_{\lambda}=\frac{1}{k!}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=n}\binom{n}{l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{k}}\prod_{i=1}^{k}(1)_{l_{i},\lambda},

where
l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{k}
are positive integers.

Further, letting
\lambda\rightarrow 0
yields Gould-Quaintance extension for Bell polynomials of Spivey’s recurrence relation for Bell numbers.

\phi_{l+n}(y)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\sum_{m=0}^{l}\binom{l}{m}{n\brace k}k^{l-m}y^{k}\phi_{m}(y),\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [5,7]).

Letting
y=1
in (
20
), we get the following Spivey-like relation for the probabilistic degenerate Bell numbers associated with
Y
.

Corollary 2.2
.

For
n,l\geq 0
, we have

\phi_{l+n,\lambda}^{Y}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\sum_{m=0}^{l}\binom{l}{m}\frac{1}{k!}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=n}\binom{n}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}E\bigg[\big(S_{k}-n\lambda)_{l-m,\lambda}\prod_{i=1}^{k}(Y_{i})_{l_{i},\lambda}\bigg]\phi_{m,\lambda}^{Y},

where
l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{k}
are positive integers.

For
r\in\mathbb{N}
, we define the probabilistic degenerate
r
-Stirling numbers of the second kind associated with
Y
as

(21)

\frac{1}{k!}\Big(E\Big[e_{\lambda}^{Y}(t)\Big]-1\Big)^{k}e_{\lambda}^{r}(t)=\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}{n+r\brace k+r}_{r,\lambda}^{Y}\frac{t^{n}}{n!},\quad(k\geq 0).

When
Y=1
, we have (see (
9
))

\quad{n+r\brace k+r}^{Y}_{r,\lambda}={n+r\brace k+r}_{r,\lambda},\quad(n\geq k\geq 0).

Also, we define the probabilistic degenerate
r
-Bell polynomials associated with
Y
by

(22)

e^{x\big(E\big[e_{\lambda}^{Y}(t)\big]-1\big)}e_{\lambda}^{r}(t)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\phi_{n,\lambda}^{(r,Y)}(x)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

When
x=1,\ \phi_{n,\lambda}^{(r,Y)}=\phi_{n,\lambda}^{(r,Y)}(1)
are called the probabilistic degenerate
r
-Bell numbers associated with
Y
.

By (
8
), (
15
)-(
17
), and (
22
), we get

(23)

\displaystyle e^{tD_{x}}\Big(e^{y\big(E\big[e_{\lambda}^{Y}(x)\big]-1\big)}e_{\lambda}^{r}(x)\Big)=e^{y\big(E\big[e_{\lambda}^{Y}(x+t)\big]-1\big)}e_{\lambda}^{r}(x+t)

\displaystyle=e^{yE\big[e_{\lambda}^{Y}(x)\big(e_{\lambda}^{Y}\big(\frac{t}{1+\lambda x}-1\big)\big]}e_{\lambda}^{r}\bigg(\frac{t}{1+\lambda x}\bigg)e^{y\big(E[e_{\lambda}^{Y}(x)]-1\big)}e_{\lambda}^{r}(x)

\displaystyle=\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}\sum_{k=0}^{l}\frac{y^{k}}{k!}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=l}\binom{l}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}\frac{t^{l}}{l!}E\bigg[e_{\lambda}^{S_{k}-l\lambda}(x)\prod_{i=1}^{k}(Y_{i})_{l_{i},\lambda}\bigg]

\displaystyle\quad\times\sum_{s=0}^{\infty}(r)_{s,\lambda}\frac{t^{s}}{s!}e_{\lambda}^{-s\lambda}(x)\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\phi_{m,\lambda}^{(r,Y)}(y)\frac{x^{m}}{m!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{l=0}^{n}\binom{n}{l}(r)_{n-l,\lambda}\sum_{k=0}^{l}\frac{y^{k}}{k!}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=l}\binom{l}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle\quad\times E\bigg[e_{\lambda}^{S_{k}-l\lambda-(n-l)\lambda}(x)\prod_{i=1}^{k}(Y_{i})_{l_{i},\lambda}\bigg]\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\phi_{m,\lambda}^{(r,Y)}(y)\frac{x^{m}}{m!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{l=0}^{n}\binom{n}{l}(r)_{n-l,\lambda}\sum_{k=0}^{l}\frac{y^{k}}{k!}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=l}\binom{l}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle\quad\times\sum_{a=0}^{\infty}E\bigg[(S_{k}-n\lambda)_{a,\lambda}\prod_{i=1}^{k}(Y_{i})_{l_{i},\lambda}\bigg]\frac{x^{a}}{a!}\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\phi_{m,\lambda}^{(r,Y)}(y)\frac{x^{m}}{m!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{l=0}^{n}\binom{n}{l}(r)_{n-l,\lambda}\sum_{k=0}^{l}\frac{y^{k}}{k!}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=l}\binom{l}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle\quad\times\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\sum_{m=0}^{j}\binom{j}{m}E\bigg[(S_{k}-n\lambda)_{j-m,\lambda}\prod_{i=1}^{k}(Y_{i})_{l_{i},\lambda}\bigg]\phi_{m,\lambda}^{(r,Y)}(y)\frac{x^{j}}{j!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\sum_{m=0}^{j}\sum_{l=0}^{n}\binom{n}{l}(r)_{n-l,\lambda}\binom{j}{m}\sum_{k=0}^{l}\frac{y^{k}}{k}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=l}\binom{l}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}

\displaystyle\quad\times E\bigg[\Big(S_{k}-n\lambda\Big)_{j-m,\lambda}\prod_{i=1}^{k}(Y_{i})_{l_{i},\lambda}\bigg]\phi_{m,\lambda}^{(r,Y)}(y)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}\frac{x^{j}}{j!},

where
l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{k}
are positive integers.

On the other hand, by (
22
), we get

(24)

\displaystyle e^{tD_{x}}\Big(e^{y\big(E\big[e_{\lambda}^{Y}(x)\big]-1\big)}e_{\lambda}^{r}(x)\Big)

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}D_{x}^{n}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\phi_{j,\lambda}^{(r,Y)}(y)\frac{x^{j}}{j!}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\phi_{j+n,\lambda}^{(r,Y)}(y)\frac{t^{n}}{n!]}\frac{x^{j}}{j!}.

Therefore, by (
23
) and (
24
), we obtain the following theorem.

Theorem 2.3
.

For
n,j\geq 0
, we have

\displaystyle\phi_{j+n,\lambda}^{(r,Y)}(y)=\sum_{l=0}^{n}\binom{n}{l}(r)_{n-l,\lambda}\sum_{k=0}^{l}\sum_{m=0}^{j}\frac{y^{k}}{k!}\binom{j}{m}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=l}\binom{l}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}

\displaystyle\qquad\times E\bigg[\big(S_{k}-n\lambda)_{j-m,\lambda}\prod_{i=1}^{k}(Y_{i})_{l_{i},\lambda}\bigg]\phi_{m,\lambda}^{(r,Y)}(y),

where
l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{k}
are positive integers.

When
Y=1
, we have

\displaystyle\phi_{j+n,\lambda}^{(r)}(y)=\sum_{l=0}^{n}\sum_{m=0}^{j}\binom{n}{l}\binom{j}{m}(r)_{n-l,\lambda}\sum_{k=0}^{l}\frac{y^{k}}{k!}

(25)

\displaystyle\qquad\times\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=l}\binom{l}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}\prod_{i=1}^{k}(1)_{l_{i},\lambda}(k-n\lambda)_{j-m,\lambda}\phi_{m,\lambda}^{(r)}(y)

\displaystyle=\sum_{l=0}^{n}\sum_{m=0}^{j}\binom{n}{l}\binom{j}{m}(r)_{n-l,\lambda}\sum_{k=0}^{l}y^{k}{l\brace k}_{\lambda}(k-n\lambda)_{j-m,\lambda}\phi_{m,\lambda}^{(r)}(y)

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\sum_{m=0}^{j}\sum_{l=k}^{n}\binom{n}{l}(r)_{n-l,\lambda}{l\brace k}_{\lambda}\binom{j}{m}y^{k}(k-n\lambda)_{j-m,\lambda}\phi_{m,\lambda}^{(r)}(y).

From (
21
), we note that

(26)

\displaystyle\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}{n+r\brace k+r}_{r,\lambda}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{k!}\big(e_{\lambda}(t)-1\big)^{k}e_{\lambda}^{r}(t)

\displaystyle=\sum_{l=k}^{\infty}{l\brace k}_{\lambda}\frac{t^{l}}{l!}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}(r)_{j,\lambda}\frac{t^{j}}{j!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}\sum_{l=k}^{n}\binom{n}{l}{l\brace k}_{\lambda}(r)_{n-l,\lambda}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

Thus, by comparing the coefficients on both sides of (
26
), we get

(27)

{n+r\brace k+r}_{r,\lambda}=\sum_{l=k}^{n}\binom{n}{l}{l\brace k}_{\lambda}(r)_{n-l,\lambda},\quad(n\geq k\geq 0).

Therefore, by (
25
) and (
27
), we obtain the following corollary.

Corollary 2.4
.

For
j,n\geq 0
, we have

\phi_{j+n,\lambda}^{(r)}(y)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\sum_{m=0}^{j}{n+r\brace k+r}_{r,\lambda}\binom{j}{m}\big(k-n\lambda\big)_{j-m,\lambda}y^{k}\phi_{m,\lambda}^{(r)}(y).

3.
Conclusion

In 2008, Spivey’s discovery of a recurrence relation for the Bell numbers
\phi_{n}
opened a new avenue of research. Since then, significant work has been dedicated to exploring various Spivey-type recurrence relations (see
[
5
,
6
,
7
,
8
,
10
]
). This includes generalizing Spivey’s relation to probabilistic Bell and
r
-Bell polynomials associated with
Y
. Here
Y
is a random variable whose moment generating function exists in a neighborhood of the origin.

In this paper, we further expanded this area by proving Spivey-type recurrence relations for probabilistic degenerate Bell and
r
-Bell polynomials associated with
Y
. These polynomials are degenerate versions of the aforementioned probabilistic Bell and
r
-Bell polynomials associated with
Y

Moving forward, our future research will focus on continuing to investigate degenerate versions and probabilistic extensions of various other special polynomials and numbers.
