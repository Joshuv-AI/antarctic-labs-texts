2 C.F.R. § 2205.201 — Use of grant agreements (including fixed amount awards), cooperative agreements, and contracts.
Citation: us:cfr:2:2205.201
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-2205.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 2205 (Implementation Of And Exemptions To 2 Cfr). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Corporation will determine the appropriate instrument in accordance with its authorities under the national service laws, and in accordance with the Federal Grant and Cooperative Agreement Act (31 U.S.C. 6301-6308), as appropriate.
(b) The Corporation and pass through entities may also provide fixed amount awards in the manner and in the amounts permitted under the national service laws.
