42 U.S.C. § 14954 — No private right of action
Citation: us:usc:42:14954
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section14954
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 143 (Intercountry Adoptions); Subchapter V (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Convention and this chapter shall not be construed to create a private right of action to seek administrative or judicial relief, except to the extent expressly provided in this chapter.
