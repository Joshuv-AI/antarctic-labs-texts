21 C.F.R. § 225.120 — Buildings and grounds.
Citation: us:cfr:21:225.120
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-225.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 225 (Current Good Manufacturing Practice For Medicated Feeds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Buildings used for production of medicated feed shall provide adequate space for equipment, processing, and orderly receipt and storage of medicated feed. Areas shall include access for routine maintenance and cleaning of equipment. Buildings and grounds shall be constructed and maintained in a manner to minimize vermin and pest infestation.
