6 C.F.R. § 115.111 — Zero tolerance of sexual abuse; Prevention of Sexual Assault Coordinator.
Citation: us:cfr:6:115.111
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security, Office Of The Secretary
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-6/section-115.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 6 (Domestic Security), Part 115 (Sexual Abuse And Assault Prevention Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The agency shall have a written policy mandating zero tolerance toward all forms of sexual abuse and outlining the agency's approach to preventing, detecting, and responding to such conduct.
(b) The agency shall employ or designate an upper-level, agency-wide PSA Coordinator with sufficient time and authority to develop, implement, and oversee agency efforts to comply with these standards in all of its holding facilities.
