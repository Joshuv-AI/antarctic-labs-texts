34 C.F.R. § 21.11 — Effect of judicial review of adversary adjudication.
Citation: us:cfr:34:21.11
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-21.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 21 (Equal Access To Justice). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a court reviews the underlying decision of an adversary adjudication covered under this part, an award of fees and other expenses may be made only under 28 U.S.C. 2412 (awards in certain judicial proceedings).
