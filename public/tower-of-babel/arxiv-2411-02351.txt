On Torsion Subgroups of Elliptic Curves over Quartic, Quintic and Sextic Number Fields
Mustafa Umut Kazancıoğlu, Mohammad Sadek

Abstract
. The list of all groups that can appear as torsion subgroups of elliptic curves over number fields of degree d d , d = 4 , 5 , 6 d=4,5,6 , is not completely determined. However, the list of groups Φ ∞ ​ ( d ) \Phi^{\infty}(d) , d = 4 , 5 , 6 d=4,5,6 , that can be realized as torsion subgroups for infinitely many non-isomorphic elliptic curves over these fields are known. We address the question of which torsion subgroups can arise over a given number field of degree d d . In fact, given G ∈ Φ ∞ ​ ( d ) G\in\Phi^{\infty}(d) and a number field K K of degree d d , we give explicit criteria telling whether G G is realized finitely or infinitely often over K K . We also give results on the field with the smallest absolute value of its discriminant such that there exists an elliptic curve with torsion G G . Finally, we give examples of number fields K K of degree d d , d = 4 , 5 , 6 d=4,5,6 , over which the Mordell-Weil rank of elliptic curves with prescribed torsion is bounded from above.

Although for any

. There are three possibilities for

Given

,

occurs as the torsion subgroup
of at most finitely many non-isomorphic elliptic curves over

.

If the genus of

is one, we give a complete description of the number fields of degree

, over which the torsion subgroup of

d

Over the rational field, there exists an elliptic curve with every possible torsion and

6.

It has been believed that an elliptic curve with prescribed torsion over the rational field
can have arbitrary large rank. In fact, for any torsion group of an elliptic curve over the rational field, there exists an elliptic curve with that torsion group and
rank at least
3
, see
[
7
]
. Unlike the rational field, it appears that upper bounds can be found for the Mordell-Weil rank of elliptic curves with prescribed torsion over number fields of higher degree, see
[
3
,
18
]
.

In Theorems
3.1
,
3.2
,
3.4

3.5
