39 C.F.R. § 255.5 — Employment.
Citation: us:cfr:39:255.5
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-255.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 255 (Access Of Persons With Disabilities To Postal Service Programs, Activities, Facilities, And Electronic And Information Technology). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No qualified individual with a disability shall, on the basis of disability, be subjected to discrimination in employment with the Postal Service. The definitions, requirements, and procedures of section 501 of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973, as established by the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission in 29 CFR part 1614 shall apply to employment within the Postal Service.
