7 U.S.C. § 7771 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:7:7771
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section7771
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 104 (Plant Protection); Subchapter IV (Authorization Of Appropriations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated such amounts as may be necessary to carry out this chapter. Except as specifically authorized by law, no part of the money appropriated under this section shall be used to pay indemnities for property injured or destroyed by or at the direction of the Secretary.
