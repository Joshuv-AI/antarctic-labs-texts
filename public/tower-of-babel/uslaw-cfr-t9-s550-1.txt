9 C.F.R. § 550.1 — Records required to be kept.
Citation: us:cfr:9:550.1
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-550.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 550 (Records Required To Be Kept). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirements in 9 CFR 320.1 for records to be kept apply to persons that engage in businesses relating to fish and fish products as they do to persons that engage in businesses relating to the carcasses, parts, or products of other species amenable to the FMIA.
