31 C.F.R. § 576.310 — Government of Iraq.
Citation: us:cfr:31:576.310
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-576.310
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 576 (Iraq Stabilization And Insurgency Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term Government of Iraq means:
(a) Any interim or permanent Iraqi government in authority after June 30, 2004, and any subdivision, agency, or instrumentality thereof; and
(b) Any partnership, association, corporation, or other organization substantially owned or controlled by the foregoing.
