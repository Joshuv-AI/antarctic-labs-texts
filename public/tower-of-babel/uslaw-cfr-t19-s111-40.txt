19 C.F.R. § 111.40 — Protests.
Citation: us:cfr:19:111.40
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-111.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 111 (Customs Brokers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A broker must not act on behalf of any person, or attempt to represent any person, regarding any protest unless he is authorized to do so in accordance with part 174 of this chapter.
