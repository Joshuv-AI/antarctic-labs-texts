27 C.F.R. § 25.232 — Basic permit.
Citation: us:cfr:27:25.232
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-25.232
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 25 (Beer). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A brewer who engages in the business of purchasing beer for resale is required to possess a wholesaler's or importer's basic permit under the provisions of section 3(c) of the Federal Alcohol Administration Act and part 1 of this chapter.
