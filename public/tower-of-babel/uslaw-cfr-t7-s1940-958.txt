7 C.F.R. § 1940.958 — Designated agency.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1940.958
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Rural Business-Cooperative Service, And Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1940.958
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1940 (General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Governor will appoint a State agency to provide the panel and the State coordinator with support for the daily operation of the panel. In addition to providing support, the designated agency is responsible for identifying:
(a) Alternative sources of financial assistance for project preapplications/applications reviewed and ranked by the panel, and
(b) Related activities within the State.
