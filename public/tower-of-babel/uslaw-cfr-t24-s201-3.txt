24 C.F.R. § 201.3 — Applicability of the regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:24:201.3
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-201.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 201 (Title I Property Improvement And Manufactured Home Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part may be amended by the Secretary at any time. Such amendment shall not adversely affect the insurance privileges of a lender on any loan that has been made or for which a loan application has been approved before the effective date of the amendment.
