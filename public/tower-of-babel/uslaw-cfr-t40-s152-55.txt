40 C.F.R. § 152.55 — Where to send applications and correspondence.
Citation: us:cfr:40:152.55
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-152.55
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 152 (Pesticide Registration And Classification Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applications and correspondence relating to registration should be sent to the Office of Pesticide Programs' Document Processing Desk at the appropriate address as set forth in 40 CFR 150.17(a) or (b).
