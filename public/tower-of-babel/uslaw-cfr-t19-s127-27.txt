19 C.F.R. § 127.27 — Conduct of sale.
Citation: us:cfr:19:127.27
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-127.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 127 (General Order, Unclaimed, And Abandoned Merchandise). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Sales may be conducted by the port director, any employee designated by him or by a public auctioneer.
