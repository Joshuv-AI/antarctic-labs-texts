10 U.S.C. § 2125 — Members of the program: exclusion from authorized strengths
Citation: us:usc:10:2125
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2125
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training And Education); Chapter 105 (Armed Forces Health Professions Financial Assistance Programs); Subchapter I (Health Professions Scholarship And Financial Assistance Program For Active Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, members of the program shall not be counted against any prescribed military strengths.
