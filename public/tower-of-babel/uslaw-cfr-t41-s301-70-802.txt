41 C.F.R. § 301-70.802 — -70.802 Inapplicability to travel by the President and Vice President.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-70.802
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-70.802
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-70 (70—Internal Policy And Procedure Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules in this part and §§ 301-10.260 through 301-10.265 of this chapter do not apply to travel on Government aircraft by the President and Vice President or by individuals traveling in support of the President and Vice President.
