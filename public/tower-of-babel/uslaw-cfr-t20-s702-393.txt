20 C.F.R. § 702.393 — Appeals; time limitations.
Citation: us:cfr:20:702.393
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-702.393
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 702 (Administration And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The notice of appeal (see § 702.391) shall be filed with the district director within 30 days of the filing of the decision or order complained of, as defined and described in §§ 802.205 and 802.206 of this title. A petition for review of the decision or order is required to be filed within 30 days after receipt of the Board's acknowledgment of the notice of appeal, as provided in § 802.210 of this title.
