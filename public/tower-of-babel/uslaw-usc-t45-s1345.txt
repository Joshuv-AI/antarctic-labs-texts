45 U.S.C. § 1345 — Effect on contracts
Citation: us:usc:45:1345
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2024
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title45-section1345
Context: United States Code, Title 45 (Railroads); Chapter 22 (Conrail Privatization); Subchapter II (Conrail); Part C (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subchapter shall affect any obligation of the Corporation to carry out its transportation contracts and equipment leases, equipment trusts, and conditional sales agreements, in accordance with their terms.
