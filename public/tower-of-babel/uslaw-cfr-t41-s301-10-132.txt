41 C.F.R. § 301-10.132 — -10.132 U.S. flag air carrier requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-10.132
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-10.132
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-10 (10—Transportation Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Anyone whose air travel is financed by U.S. Government funds must use a U.S. flag air carrier, except as provided in §§ 301-10.134, 301-10.135, and 301-10.136.
