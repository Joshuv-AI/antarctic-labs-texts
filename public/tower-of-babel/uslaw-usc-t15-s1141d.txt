15 U.S.C. § 1141d — Request for extension of protection subsequent to international registration
Citation: us:usc:15:1141d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1141d
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 22 (Trademarks); Subchapter IV (The Madrid Protocol). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The holder of an international registration that is based upon a basic application filed with the United States Patent and Trademark Office or a basic registration granted by the Patent and Trademark Office may request an extension of protection of its international registration by filing such a request—
(1)
directly with the International Bureau; or
(2)
with the United States Patent and Trademark Office for transmittal to the International Bureau, if the request is in such form, and contains such transmittal fee, as may be prescribed by the Director.
