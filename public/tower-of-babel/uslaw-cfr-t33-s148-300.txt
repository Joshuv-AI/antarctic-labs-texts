33 C.F.R. § 148.300 — What does this subpart concern?
Citation: us:cfr:33:148.300
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-148.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 148 (Deepwater Ports: General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart concerns the license for a deepwater port and the procedures for transferring, amending, suspending, reinstating, revoking, and enforcing a license.
