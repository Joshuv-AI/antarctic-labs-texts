29 C.F.R. § 4003.52 — When to file.
Citation: us:cfr:29:4003.52
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4003.52
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4003 (Rules For Administrative Review Of Agency Decisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in §§ 4003.4 and 4003.5, an appeal under this subpart must be filed within 45 days after the date of the initial determination being appealed or, when administrative review includes a procedure in part 4903 of this chapter, by the date that is specified in PBGC's notice of the right to request review.
