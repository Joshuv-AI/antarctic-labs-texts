42 U.S.C. § 18384 — Decadal results
Citation: us:usc:42:18384
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section18384
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 159 (Space Exploration, Technology, And Science); Subchapter VII (Space Science). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

NASA shall take into account the current decadal surveys from the National Academies’ Space Studies Board when submitting the President’s budget request to the Congress.
