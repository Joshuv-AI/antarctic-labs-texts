14 C.F.R. § 29.65 — Climb: All engines operating.
Citation: us:cfr:14:29.65
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-29.65
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 29 (Airworthiness Standards: Transport Category Rotorcraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The steady rate of climb must be determined—
(1) With maximum continuous power;
(2) With the landing gear retracted; and
(3) At Vy for standard sea level conditions and at speeds selected by the applicant for other conditions.
(b) For each Category B rotorcraft except helicopters, the rate of climb determined under paragraph (a) of this section must provide a steady climb gradient of at least 1:6 under standard sea level conditions.
