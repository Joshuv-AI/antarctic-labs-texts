12 C.F.R. § 1410.1 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1410.1
Creator: Farm Credit System Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1410.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1410 (Premiums). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth the rules for:
(a) The calculation of premiums;
(b) The time for payment of the premium required by sections 5.55 and 5.56 of the Farm Credit Act of 1971, as amended;
(c) Interest charges on delinquent payments;
(d) The form and content of certified statements; and,
(e) Documentation supporting certified statements.
