19 U.S.C. § 1649 — Change in designation of customs attachés
Citation: us:usc:19:1649
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section1649
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 4 (Tariff Act Of 1930); Part VI (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On and after June 17, 1930, customs attachés shall be known as “Treasury attachés.”
