36 C.F.R. § 296.12 — Relationship to Section 106 of the National Historic Preservation Act.
Citation: us:cfr:36:296.12
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-296.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 296 (Protection Of Archaeological Resources: Uniform Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Issuance of a permit in accordance with the Act and this part does not constitute an undertaking requiring compliance with section 106 of the Act of October 15, 1966 (16 U.S.C. 470f). However, the mere issuance of such a permit does not excuse the Federal land manager from compliance with section 106 where otherwise required.
