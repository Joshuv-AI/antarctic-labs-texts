25 C.F.R. § 2.701 — May a Local Bureau Official's decision to recognize, or decline to recognize, a Tribal representative be appealed?
Citation: us:cfr:25:2.701
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-2.701
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 2 (Appeals From Administrative Decisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. A written decision by the LBO to recognize or decline to recognize a Tribal representative is appealable.
