42 U.S.C. § 1716 — Presumption of death or detention
Citation: us:usc:42:1716
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1716
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 12 (Compensation For Injury, Death, Or Detention Of Employees Of Contractors With United States Outside United States); Subchapter II (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A determination that an individual is dead or a determination that he has been detained by a hostile force or person may be made on the basis of evidence that he has disappeared under circumstances such as to make such death or detention appear probable.
