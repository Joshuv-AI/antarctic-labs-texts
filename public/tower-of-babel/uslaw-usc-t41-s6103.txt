41 U.S.C. § 6103 — Opening of bids
Citation: us:usc:41:6103
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title41-section6103
Context: United States Code, Title 41 (Public Contracts); Chapter 61 (Advertising). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whenever proposals for supplies have been solicited, the parties responding to the solicitation shall be notified of the time and place of the opening of the bids, and be permitted to be present either in person or by attorney. A record of each bid shall be made at the time and place of the opening of the bids.
