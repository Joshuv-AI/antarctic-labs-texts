29 C.F.R. § 401.3 — Industry affecting commerce.
Citation: us:cfr:29:401.3
Creator: Office Of Labor-Management Standards, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-401.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 401 (Meaning Of Terms Used In This Subchapter). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Industry affecting commerce means any activity, business, or industry in commerce or in which a labor dispute would hinder or obstruct commerce or the free flow of commerce and includes any activity or industry “affecting commerce” within the meaning of the Labor Management Relations Act, 1947, as amended, or the Railway Labor Act, as amended.
