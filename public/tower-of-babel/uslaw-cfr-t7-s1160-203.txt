7 C.F.R. § 1160.203 — Nominee's agreement to serve.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1160.203
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Milk), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1160.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1160 (Fluid Milk Promotion Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each nominee for Board membership must file with the Secretary at the time of nomination a written agreement to serve on the Board if appointed.
