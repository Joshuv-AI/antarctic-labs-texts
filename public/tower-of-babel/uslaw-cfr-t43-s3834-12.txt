43 C.F.R. § 3834.12 — How will BLM know for which mining claims or sites I am paying the fees?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3834.12
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3834.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3834 (Required Fees For Mining Claims Or Sites). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When you pay any fees to BLM, you must include a list of the mining claims or sites that you are paying for by claim name, and by the BLM serial number if BLM has notified you what the serial numbers are.
