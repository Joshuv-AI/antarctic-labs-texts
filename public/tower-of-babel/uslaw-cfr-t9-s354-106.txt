9 C.F.R. § 354.106 — Travel expenses and other charges.
Citation: us:cfr:9:354.106
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-354.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 354 (Voluntary Inspection Of Rabbits And Edible Products Thereof). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Charges are to be made to cover the cost of travel and other expenses incurred by the Service in connection with rendering inspection service. Such charges shall include the costs of transportation, per diem, and any other expenses.
