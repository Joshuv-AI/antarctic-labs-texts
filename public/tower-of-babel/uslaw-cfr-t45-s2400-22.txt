45 C.F.R. § 2400.22 — Application deadline.
Citation: us:cfr:45:2400.22
Creator: James Madison Memorial Fellowship Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2400.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2400 (Fellowship Program Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Completed applications must be received by the Foundation no later than March 1st of each year preceding the start of the academic year for which candidates are applying.
