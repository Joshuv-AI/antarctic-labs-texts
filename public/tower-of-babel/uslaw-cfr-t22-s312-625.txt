22 C.F.R. § 312.625 — Criminal drug statute.
Citation: us:cfr:22:312.625
Creator: Peace Corps
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-312.625
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 312 (Governmentwide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Criminal drug statute means a Federal or non-Federal criminal statute involving the manufacture, distribution, dispensing, use, or possession of any controlled substance.
