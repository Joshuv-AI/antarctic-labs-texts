On approximately ideals: primary and 1-absorbing ideals in descriptive relator spaces
Maram Almahariq

Abstract
. For the approx. ideal 𝒲 \mathcal{W} of the approx. commutative ring ℛ {\mathcal{R}} with unity in a descriptive relator space, after introducing the approx. prime ideal in [ 12 ] , this work demonstrates some special properties of the approx. ideals-specifically, the approx. primary ideal, the approx. semi-primary ideal and the approx. 1-absorbing primary ideal. A set of theorems related to these concepts is presented. Among them is this important result: If 𝒲 \mathcal{W} is an approx. 1-absorbing primary ideal, then r ⁡ ( 𝒲 ) r(\mathcal{W}) is an approx. prime ideal of the approx. ring ℛ {\mathcal{R}} . Furthermore, the relationship between these classes is studied: If 𝒲 \mathcal{W} is an approx. prime ideal of ℛ {\mathcal{R}} , then 𝒲 \mathcal{W} is also an approx. primary ideal. Moreover, it turns out that this is an approx. 1-absorbing primary ideal.
