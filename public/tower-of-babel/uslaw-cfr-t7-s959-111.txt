7 C.F.R. § 959.111 — Reapportionment of Committee membership.
Citation: us:cfr:7:959.111
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-959.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 959 (Onions Grown In South Texas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pursuant to § 959.25, the Committee membership of eight producer members and five handler members and the respective alternates is reapportioned to a single district made up of all counties in the production area.
