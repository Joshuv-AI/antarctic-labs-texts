12 C.F.R. § 792.13 — Can I get the records in different forms or formats?
Citation: us:cfr:12:792.13
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-792.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 792 (Requests For Information Under The Freedom Of Information Act And Privacy Act, And By Subpoena; Security Procedures For Classified Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

NCUA will provide a copy of the record in any form or format requested, such as computer disk, if the record is readily reproducible by us in that form or format, but we will not provide more than one copy of any record.
