31 C.F.R. § 315.51 — Requests for reissue.
Citation: us:cfr:31:315.51
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-315.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 315 (Regulations Governing U.S. Savings Bonds, Series A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, J, And K, And U.S. Savings Notes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A request for reissue of bonds in coownership form during the lifetime of the coowners must be signed by both coowners, except that a request solely to eliminate the name of one coowner may be signed by that coowner only. A bond registered in beneficiary form may be reissued upon the request of the owner, supported by the certified consent of the beneficiary or by proof of his or her death. Fiscal Service forms are available for requesting reissue.
