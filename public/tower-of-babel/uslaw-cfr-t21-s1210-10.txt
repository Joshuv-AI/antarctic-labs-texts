21 C.F.R. § 1210.10 — Availability for examination and inspection.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1210.10
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1210.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1210 (Regulations Under The Federal Import Milk Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Dairy farms and plants from which milk or cream is shipped or transported into the United States shall be open at all reasonable times to authorized agents for necessary examinations and inspections. Failure to permit such examinations and inspections may be considered cause for the suspension or revocation of the permit.
