8 C.F.R. § 1003.39 — Finality of decision.
Citation: us:cfr:8:1003.39
Creator: Executive Office For Immigration Review, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-1003.39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 1003 (Executive Office For Immigration Review). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except when certified to the Board, the decision of the Immigration Judge becomes final upon waiver of appeal or upon expiration of the time to appeal if no appeal is taken whichever occurs first.
