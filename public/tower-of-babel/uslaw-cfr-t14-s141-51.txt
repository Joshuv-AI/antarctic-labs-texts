14 C.F.R. § 141.51 — Applicability.

Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-141.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 141 (Pilot Schools). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes the curriculum and course outline requirements for the issuance of a pilot school certificate or provisional pilot school certificate and ratings.
