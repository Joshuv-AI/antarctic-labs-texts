21 C.F.R. § 1402.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1402.1
Creator: Office Of National Drug Control Policy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1402.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1402 (Mandatory Declassification Review). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Other government agencies, U.S. citizens or permanent resident aliens may request that classified information in files of the Office of National Drug Control Policy (ONDCP) be reviewed for possible declassification and release. This part prescribes the procedures for such review and subsequent release or denial.
