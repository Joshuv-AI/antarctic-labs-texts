7 C.F.R. § 1405.5 — Notice and comment.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1405.5
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1405.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1405 (Loans, Purchases, And Other Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The level of loans, purchases and payments made in accordance with the programs set forth in this chapter shall be determined without regard to the notice and comment provisions of 5 U.S.C. 553.
