Proposed Information Collection Activity; Child Care and 
Development Fund Plan Preprint for States/Territories for Federal 
Fiscal Year (FFY) 2028-2030 (ACF-118) and extension of Child Care and 
Development Fund Plan Preprint for States/Territories for FFY 2025-2027

AGENCY: Office of Child Care, Administration for Children and Families, 
U.S. Department of Health and Human Services.

ACTION: Request for public comments.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Administration for Children and Families (ACF) is 
requesting an extension without changes of the form ACF-118: Child Care 
and Development Fund (CCDF) Plan Preprint for States/Territories for 
FFY 2025-2027 (Office of Management and Budget #: 0970-0114, expiration 
March 31, 2027), and an additional 3-year extension of the form ACF-
118: CCDF Plan Preprint for States/Territories for FFY 2028-2030. There 
are changes requested to the form ACF-118: CCDF Plan Preprint for 
States/Territories for FFY 2028-2030 to reduce burden, streamline 
questions, reduce data entry, and strengthen program integrity.

DATES: Comments due December 22, 2026.

ADDRESSES: In compliance with the requirements of the Paperwork 
Reduction Act of 1995, ACF is soliciting public comment on the specific 
aspects of the information collection described above. You can obtain 
copies of the proposed collection of information and submit comments by 
emailing [email protected]. Identify all requests by the title 
of the information collection.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: 
    Description: The CCDF Plan (the Plan) for states and territories is 
required from each CCDF Lead Agency in accordance with section 658E of 
the Child Care and Development Block Grant Act of 1990 (CCDBG Act), as 
amended, CCDBG Act of 2014 (Pub. L. 113-186), and 42 U.S.C. 9858. The 
Plan, submitted on the ACF-118, is required triennially, and remains in 
effect for 3 years. These Plans are the applications for CCDF funds and 
provide ACF and the public with a description of and assurance about 
the

[[Page 66845]]

states' and territories' CCDF program and child care services.
    At this time, ACF's Office of Child Care (OCC) is proposing an 
extension of the approval of the currently approved CCDF Plan Preprint 
for FFY 2025-2027 to allow states and territories to continue to submit 
amendments through September 30, 2027, as required. There are no 
changes proposed to the FFY 2025-2027 Plan Preprint. In addition, OCC 
is requesting comments on the proposed CCDF Plan Preprint for FFY 2028-
2030. Updates were made to simplify and reduce questions, reduce 
burden, and strengthen program integrity.
    Respondents: State and Territory Lead Agencies.

    Comments: The Department specifically requests comments on (a) 
whether the proposed collection of information is necessary for the 
proper performance of the functions of the agency, including whether 
the information shall have practical utility; (b) the accuracy of the 
agency's estimate of the burden of the proposed collection of 
information; (c) the quality, utility, and clarity of the information 
to be collected; and (d) ways to minimize the burden of the collection 
of information on respondents, including through the use of automated 
collection techniques or other forms of information technology. 
Consideration will be given to comments and suggestions submitted 
within 60 days of this publication.
