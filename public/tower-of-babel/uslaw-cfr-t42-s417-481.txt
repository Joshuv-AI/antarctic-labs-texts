42 C.F.R. § 417.481 — Maintenance of records: Risk HMOs and CMPs.
Citation: us:cfr:42:417.481
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-417.481
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 417 (Health Maintenance Organizations, Competitive Medical Plans, And Health Care Prepayment Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A risk contract must provide that the HMO or CMP agrees to maintain and make available to CMS upon request, books, records, documents, and other evidence of acounting procedures and practices that—
(a) Are sufficient to—
(1) Establish component rates of the ACR for determining additional and supplementary benefits; and
(2) Determine the rates utilized in setting premiums for State insurance agency purposes; and
(b) Include at least any records or financial reports filed with other Federal agencies or State authorities.
