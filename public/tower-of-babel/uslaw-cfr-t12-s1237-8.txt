12 C.F.R. § 1237.8 — Alternate procedures for determination of claims.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1237.8
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1237.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1237 (Conservatorship And Receivership). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Claimants seeking a review of the determination of claims may seek alternative dispute resolution from the Agency as receiver in lieu of a judicial determination. The Director may by order, policy statement, or directive establish alternative dispute resolution procedures for this purpose.
