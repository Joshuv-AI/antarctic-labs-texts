49 U.S.C. § 33117 — Judicial review
Citation: us:usc:49:33117
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section33117
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part C (Information, Standards, And Requirements); Chapter 331 (Theft Prevention). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A person that may be adversely affected by a regulation prescribed under this chapter may obtain judicial review of the regulation under section 32909 of this title. A remedy under this section is in addition to any other remedies provided by law.
