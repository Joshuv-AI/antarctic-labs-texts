34 C.F.R. § 222.4 — How does the Secretary determine when an application is timely filed?
Citation: us:cfr:34:222.4
Creator: Office Of Elementary And Secondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-222.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 222 (Impact Aid Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To be timely filed under § 222.3, an application must be received by the Secretary on or before the applicable filing date.
