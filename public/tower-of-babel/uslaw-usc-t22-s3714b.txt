22 U.S.C. § 3714b — Printing
Citation: us:usc:22:3714b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section3714b
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 51 (Panama Canal); Subchapter I (Administration And Regulations); Part 3 (Funds And Accounts); Subpart i (Funds). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Sections 501 through 517 and 1101 through 1123 of title 44 shall not apply to direct purchase by the Commission for its use of printing, binding, and blank-book work in the Republic of Panama when the Commission determines that such direct purchase is in the best interest of the Government.
(b)
This section shall not affect the Commission’s authority, under chapter 5 of title 44, to operate a field printing plant.
