13 C.F.R. § 127.600 — Who may protest the status of a concern as an EDWOSB or WOSB?
Citation: us:cfr:13:127.600
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-127.600
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 127 (Women-Owned Small Business Federal Contract Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) For sole source procurements. SBA or the contracting officer may protest the proposed awardee's EDWOSB or WOSB status.
(b) For all other EDWOSB or WOSB requirements. An interested party may protest the apparent successful offeror's EDWOSB or WOSB status.
