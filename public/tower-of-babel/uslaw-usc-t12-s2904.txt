12 U.S.C. § 2904 — Report to Congress
Citation: us:usc:12:2904
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section2904
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 30 (Community Reinvestment). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each appropriate Federal financial supervisory agency shall include in its annual report to the Congress a section outlining the actions it has taken to carry out its responsibilities under this chapter.
