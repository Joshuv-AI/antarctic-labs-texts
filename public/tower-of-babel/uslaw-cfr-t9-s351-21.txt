9 C.F.R. § 351.21 — Certification of certain animal fat for export.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 351 (Certification Of Technical Animal Fats For Export). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person receiving inspection service may, if dissatisfied with any decision of an inspector relating to any inspection, file an appeal from such decision or action in accordance with 9 CFR 500.9.
