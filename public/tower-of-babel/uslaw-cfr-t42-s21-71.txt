42 C.F.R. § 21.71 — Applicability and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:42:21.71
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-21.71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 21 (Commissioned Officers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) This subpart applies to officers in the Public Health Service Commissioned Corps. The term “Public Health Service,” hereinafter shall be referred to as Service.
(b) Its provisions pertain to officers of the Service under a call or order to active duty for a period of six months or more.
