25 C.F.R. § 11.432 — Impersonating a public servant.
Citation: us:cfr:25:11.432
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-11.432
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 11 (Courts Of Indian Offenses And Law And Order Code). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person commits a misdemeanor if he or she falsely pretends to hold a position in the public service with purpose to induce another to submit to such pretended official authority or otherwise to act in reliance upon that pretense to his or her prejudice.
