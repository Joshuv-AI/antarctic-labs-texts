46 C.F.R. § 116.1120 — Drainage of cockpit vessels, well deck vessels, and open boats.
Citation: us:cfr:46:116.1120
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-116.1120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 116 (Construction And Arrangement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Drainage of cockpit vessels, well deck vessels, and open boats must meet the applicable requirements of §§ 178.420, 178.430, 178.440, 178.450 in subchapter T of this chapter.
