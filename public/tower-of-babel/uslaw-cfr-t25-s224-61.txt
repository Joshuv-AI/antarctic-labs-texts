25 C.F.R. § 224.61 — What will the Tribe provide to the Secretary after receipt of the Secretary's report on the application consultation meeting?
Citation: us:cfr:25:224.61
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-224.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 224 (Tribal Energy Resource Agreements Under The Indian Tribal Energy Development And Self Determination Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Tribe wishes to proceed with the application, the Tribe must submit a final proposed TERA to the Secretary within 45 days following the date of the Tribe's receipt of the Secretary's report on the application consultation meeting.
