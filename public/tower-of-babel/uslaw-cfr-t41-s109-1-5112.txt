41 C.F.R. § 109-1.5112 — -1.5112 Loss, damage, or destruction of personal property in possession of DOE direct operations.
Citation: us:cfr:41:109-1.5112
Creator: Chapter 109—Department Of Energy Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-109-1.5112
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 109-1 (1—Introduction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

DOE offices shall establish procedures to provide for the reporting, documentation, and investigation of instances of loss, damage, or destruction of personal property including:
(a) Notification to appropriate DOE organizations and law enforcement offices;
(b) Determination of cause or origin;
(c) Liability and responsibility for repair or replacement; and
(d) Actions taken to prevent further loss, damage, or destruction, and to prevent repetition of similar incidents.
