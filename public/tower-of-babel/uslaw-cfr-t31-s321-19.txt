31 C.F.R. § 321.19 — Certification of signatures.
Citation: us:cfr:31:321.19
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-321.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 321 (Payments By Banks And Other Financial Institutions Of Definitive United States Savings Bonds And United States Savings Notes (Freedom Shares)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this subpart shall, to the extent appropriate, apply to losses resulting from payments made in reliance on certifications of signatures by an officer or designated employee of any financial institution authorized to certify requests for payment.
