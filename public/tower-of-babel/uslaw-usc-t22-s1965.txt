22 U.S.C. § 1965 — Expiration
Citation: us:usc:22:1965
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1965
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 24A (Middle East Peace And Stability). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter shall expire when the President shall determine that the peace and security of the nations in the general area of the Middle East are reasonably assured by international conditions created by action of the United Nations or otherwise except that it may be terminated earlier by a concurrent resolution of the two Houses of Congress.
