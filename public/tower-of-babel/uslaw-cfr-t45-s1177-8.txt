45 C.F.R. § 1177.8 — No private rights created.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1177.8
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1177.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1177 (Claims Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The standards in this part do not create any right or benefit, substantive or procedural, enforceable at law or in equity by a party against the United States, its agencies, its officers, or any other person, nor shall NEH's failure to comply with any of the provisions of this part be available to any debtor as a defense.
