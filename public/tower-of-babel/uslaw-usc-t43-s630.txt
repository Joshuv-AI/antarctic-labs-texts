43 U.S.C. § 630 — Disposition by Government of proceeds of land sold
Citation: us:usc:43:630
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section630
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 13 (Federal Lands Included In State Irrigation Districts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All moneys derived by the United States from the sale of public lands referred to in this chapter shall be paid into such funds and applied as provided by law for the disposal of the proceeds from the sale of public lands.
