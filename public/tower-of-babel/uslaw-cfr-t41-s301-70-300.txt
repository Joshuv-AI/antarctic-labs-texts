41 C.F.R. § 301-70.300 — -70.300 Governing policies for payment of miscellaneous expenses.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-70.300
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-70.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-70 (70—Internal Policy And Procedure Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies must establish policies and procedures governing who will determine what types of miscellaneous expenses are appropriate for reimbursement in connection with official travel. Agencies are reminded that payment of miscellaneous expenses should be limited to only those expenses that are necessary and in the interest of the Government.
