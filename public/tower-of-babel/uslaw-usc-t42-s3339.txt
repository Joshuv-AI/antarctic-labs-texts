42 U.S.C. § 3339 — Limitation on amount of grant
Citation: us:usc:42:3339
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section3339
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 41 (Demonstration Cities And Metropolitan Development Program); Subchapter II (Planned Areawide Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Grants made under section 3335 of this title for projects in any one State shall not exceed in the aggregate 15 per centum of the aggregate amount of funds authorized to be appropriated pursuant to section 3336(b) of this title.
