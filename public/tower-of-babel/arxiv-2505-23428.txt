Gaps between quadratic forms
Siddharth Iyer

Abstract
. Let △ \triangle denote the integers represented by the quadratic form x 2 + x ​ y + y 2 x^{2}+xy+y^{2} and □ 2 \raisebox{-2.15277pt}{\scalebox{2}{$\square$}}_{2} denote the numbers represented as a sum of two squares. For a non-zero integer a a , let S ( △ , □ 2 , a ) S(\triangle,\raisebox{-2.15277pt}{\scalebox{2}{$\square$}}_{2},a) be the set of integers n n such that n ∈ △ n\in\triangle , and n + a ∈ □ 2 n+a\in\raisebox{-2.15277pt}{\scalebox{2}{$\square$}}_{2} . We conduct a census of S ( △ , □ 2 , a ) S(\triangle,\raisebox{-2.15277pt}{\scalebox{2}{$\square$}}_{2},a) in short intervals by showing that there exists a constant H a > 0 H_{a}>0 with # S ( △ , □ 2 , a ) ∩ [ x , x + H a ⋅ x 5 / 6 ⋅ log 19 x ] ≥ x 5 / 6 − ε \displaystyle\#S(\triangle,\raisebox{-2.15277pt}{\scalebox{2}{$\square$}}_{2},a)\cap[x,x+H_{a}\cdot x^{5/6}\cdot\log^{19}x]\geq x^{5/6-\varepsilon} for large x x . \displaystyle\mathcal{I}(n)=\sum_{d|n}\chi_{3}(d).

.
Since

\displaystyle a_{j}+\omega\cdot b_{j}\not\sim a_{k}+\omega\cdot b_{k}

\displaystyle W_{j}\cdot\overline{W_{j}}=(a_{j}+\omega\cdot b_{j})\cdot(a_{j}+\bar{\omega}\cdot b_{j})=(a_{j}^{2}+a_{j}\cdot b_{j}+b_{j}^{2}).

Giving us the equation

\displaystyle P((a_{j}+\omega\cdot b_{j})\cdot(a_{j}+\bar{\omega}\cdot b_{j}))=P((a_{j}^{2}+a_{j}\cdot b_{j}+b_{j}^{2})).

Expanding both sides of the above equation gives us an equation comparing integer types.

\displaystyle P((a_{j}+\omega\cdot b_{j}))^{2}=(a_{j}^{2}+a_{j}\cdot b_{j}+b_{j}^{2})^{2},

so that

. Considering the

\displaystyle R_{2}(n)\geq 6\cdot\mathcal{I}(n)

\displaystyle P((a+\omega\cdot b)\cdot(a+\bar{\omega}b))=P(n)=n^{2},
