43 C.F.R. § 2883.11 — Who may not hold a grant or TUP?
Citation: us:cfr:43:2883.11
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2883.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2880 (Rights-Of-Way Under The Mineral Leasing Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Aliens may not acquire or hold any direct or indirect interest in grants or TUPs, except that they may own or control stock in corporations holding grants or TUPs if the laws of their country do not deny similar or like privileges to citizens of the United States.
