9 C.F.R. § 71.17 — Interstate movement of dead poultry or other animals prohibited in same car with live poultry or other animals.
Citation: us:cfr:9:71.17
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-71.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 71 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No dead poultry or other animals shall be offered or accepted for transportation or transported in the same car with live poultry or other animals from the original point of shipment in any State or Territory or the District of Columbia to or through any other State, Territory, or the District of Columbia.
