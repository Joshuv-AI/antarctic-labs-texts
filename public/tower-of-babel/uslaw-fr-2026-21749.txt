LEGAL SERVICES CORPORATION


Sunshine Act Meetings

TIME AND DATE: The Legal Services Corporation (LSC) Board of Directors 
and its Finance and Audit committees will meet on Friday, October 30, 
2026. The Finance Committee meeting will begin at 9:00 a.m. PT, 
followed by the Audit Committee. The Board of Directors meeting will 
begin at 1:45 p.m. PT. Each meeting will continue until the conclusion 
of its agenda.

PLACE: Public Notice of Hybrid Meeting.
    LSC will conduct its October 30, 2026, meetings at Davis Wright 
Tremaine LLP, 920 5th Ave #3300, Seattle, WA 98104, and virtually via 
videoconference.
    Public Observation: Unless otherwise noted herein, the meetings 
will be open to public observation via LSC's YouTube channel: https://www.youtube.com/@LegalServicesCorp/streams or in person at Davis Wright 
Tremaine. Members of the public wishing to observe the open meetings in 
person must register in advance by sending an email to [email protected] 
no later than 5:00 p.m. Pacific Time on Tuesday, October 27, 2026.

STATUS: Open, except as noted below.
    Finance Committee--Open, except that, upon a vote of the Board of 
Directors, the meeting may be closed to the public to discuss a policy 
regarding unrestricted funds and reserves, an investment policy, and 
next steps for the investment advisor RFP.
    Audit Committee--Open, except that, upon a vote of the Board of 
Directors, the meeting may be closed to the public to discuss follow-up 
work by the Office of Compliance and Enforcement relating to open 
Office of Inspector General investigations and to discuss grantee 
oversight activities.
    Board of Directors--Open, except that, upon a vote of the Board of 
Directors, the meeting may be closed to the public to receive briefings 
from Management and the Inspector General and to consider and act on 
potential and pending litigation involving LSC as well as a list of 
prospective Leaders Council and Emerging Leaders Council members.
    Any portion of the closed sessions consisting solely of briefings 
does not fall within the Sunshine Act's definition of the term 
``meeting'' and, therefore, the requirements of the Sunshine Act do not 
apply to such portions of the closed session.
    A verbatim written transcript will be made of the closed sessions 
of the Finance Committee, Audit Committee, and Board of Directors. The 
transcript of any portions of the closed sessions falling within the 
relevant provisions of the Government in the Sunshine Act, 5 U.S.C. 
552b(c)(6), (7), (9) and (10), will not be available for public 
inspection. A copy of the General Counsel's certification that, in his 
opinion, the closing is authorized by law will be available upon 
request.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED: 

Meeting Schedule

Friday, October 30, 2026

Start Time (all PT)
1. Finance Committee Meeting
9:00 a.m. PT
    a. Matters to be discussed include LSC's preliminary financial 
results for fiscal year 2026; the status of LSC's fiscal year 2027 
appropriation; proposed fiscal year 2027 management and grant oversight 
budget; fiscal year 2028 budget request; and considering and acting on 
a policy approving unrestricted funds and reserves, a policy regarding 
investment, and funding of operating reserve.
2. Audit Committee Meeting
10:45 a.m. PT
    a. Matters to be discussed include an update on the scope and plan 
for LSC's forthcoming annual financial audit; a briefing by the Office 
of Inspector General; a briefing on LSC's efforts to ensure that LSC 
employees and grantees act ethically and safeguard LSC funds; a 
management update regarding risk management; and follow-up by the 
Office of Compliance and Enforcement on referrals by the Office of 
Inspector General regarding audit reports and annual financial 
statement audits of grantees.
3. Board of Directors Meeting
1:45 p.m. PT
    a. Matters to be discussed include Chairman's Report; Members' 
Reports; President's Report; Inspector General's Report; the fall 2027 
Board meeting location; and reports from the Governance and Performance 
Review, Operations and Regulations, Delivery of Legal Services, 
Institutional Advancement, Finance, and Audit committees.
