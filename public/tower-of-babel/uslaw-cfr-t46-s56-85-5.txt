46 C.F.R. § 56.85-5 — -5 Heating and cooling method.
Citation: us:cfr:46:56.85-5
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-56.85-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 56 (Piping Systems And Appurtenances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Heat treatment may be accomplished by a suitable heating method that will provide the desired heating and cooling rates, the required metal temperature, metal temperature uniformity, and temperature control.
