22 C.F.R. § 16.14 — Reconsideration of a grievance.
Citation: us:cfr:22:16.14
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-16.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 16 (Foreign Service Grievance System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A grievant whose grievance is found not to be meritorious by the Board may obtain reconsideration by the Board only upon presenting newly discovered or previously unavailable material evidence not previously considered by the Board and then only upon approval of the Board.
