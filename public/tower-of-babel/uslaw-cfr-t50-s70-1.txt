50 C.F.R. § 70.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:50:70.1
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-70.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 70 (National Fish Hatcheries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All national fish hatchery areas are maintained for the fundamental purpose of the propagation and distribution of fish and other aquatic animal life and managed for the protection of all species of wildlife.
