5 C.F.R. § 752.202 — Standard for action.
Citation: us:cfr:5:752.202
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-752.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 752 (Adverse Actions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An agency may take action under this subpart for such cause as will promote the efficiency of the service as set forth in 5 U.S.C. 7503(a).
(b) An agency may not take a suspension against an employee on the basis of any reason prohibited by 5 U.S.C. 2302.
