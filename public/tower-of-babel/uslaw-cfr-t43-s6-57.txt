43 C.F.R. § 6.57 — Evaluation Committee.
Citation: us:cfr:43:6.57
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-6.57
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 6 (Patent Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At the request of the Solicitor, an Evaluation Committee will be appointed by the Secretary to recommend royalty rates with respect to any patents or inventions for which royalties may be charged.
