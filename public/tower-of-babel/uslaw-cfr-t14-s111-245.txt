14 C.F.R. § 111.245 — Special rules for protected records.
Citation: us:cfr:14:111.245
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-111.245
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 111 (Pilot Records Database). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person may report any pilot record for inclusion in the PRD that was reported by any individual as part of any approved Voluntary Safety Reporting Program for which the FAA has designated reported information as protected in accordance with part 193 of this chapter.
