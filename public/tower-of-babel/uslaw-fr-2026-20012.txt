DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

Office of Foreign Assets Control

31 CFR Part 591


Publication of Venezuela Sanctions Regulations Web General 
License 5W

AGENCY: Office of Foreign Assets Control, Treasury.

ACTION: Publication of a web general license.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of the Treasury's Office of Foreign Assets 
Control (OFAC) is publishing a general license (GL) issued pursuant to 
the Venezuela Sanctions Regulations: GL 5W, which was previously made 
available on OFAC's website.

DATES: GL 5W was issued on May 4, 2026. See SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION 
for additional relevant dates.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:  OFAC: Assistant Director for 
Regulatory Affairs, 202-622-4855; or https://ofac.treasury.gov/contact-ofac.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Electronic Availability

    This document and additional information concerning OFAC are 
available on OFAC's website: https://ofac.treasury.gov/.

Background

    On May 4, 2026, OFAC issued GL 5W to authorize certain transactions 
otherwise prohibited by the Venezuela Sanctions Regulations, 31 CFR 
part 591. GL 5W replaced and superseded GL 5V. This GL was made 
available on OFAC's website (https://ofac.treasury.gov) when it was 
issued. The text of this GL is provided below.

OFFICE OF FOREIGN ASSETS CONTROL

Venezuela Sanctions Regulations

31 CFR Part 591

GENERAL LICENSE NO. 5W

Authorizing Certain Transactions Related to the Petr[oacute]leos de 
Venezuela, S.A. 2020 8.5 Percent Bond On or After June 19, 2026

    (a) Except as provided in paragraph (b) of this general license, on 
or after June 19, 2026, all transactions related to, the provision of 
financing for, and other dealings in the Petr[oacute]leos de Venezuela, 
S.A. 2020 8.5 Percent Bond that would be prohibited by subsection 
l(a)(iii) of Executive Order (E.O.) 13835 of May 21, 2018, as amended 
by E.O. 13857 of January 25, 2019, and incorporated into the Venezuela 
Sanctions Regulations, 31 CFR part 591 (the VSR), are authorized.
    (b) This general license does not authorize any transactions or 
activities otherwise prohibited by the VSR, or any other part of 31 CFR 
chapter V.
    (c) Effective May 4, 2026, General License No. 5V, dated March 19, 
2026, is replaced and superseded in its entirety by this General 
License No. 5W.

Bradley T. Smith,
Director, Office of Foreign Assets Control.
