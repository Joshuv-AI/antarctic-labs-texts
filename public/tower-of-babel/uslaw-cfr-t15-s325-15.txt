15 C.F.R. § 325.15 — Relinquishing a certificate.
Citation: us:cfr:15:325.15
Creator: International Trade Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-325.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 325 (Export Trade Certificates Of Review). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A certificate holder may relinquish a certificate at any time through written notice to the Secretary. The certificate will cease to be effective on the day the Secretary receives the notice.
