12 C.F.R. § 604.415 — Open meetings.
Citation: us:cfr:12:604.415
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-604.415
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 604 (Farm Credit Administration Board Meetings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every meeting and portion of a meeting of the Board shall be open to public observation unless the Board determines that such meeting or portion of a meeting will involve the discussion of matters which are within one or more of the exemptive provisions listed in § 604.420 of this part, and that the public interest is not served by the discussion of such matters in an open meeting.
