36 U.S.C. § 140302 — Purposes

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1403 (The Military Chaplains Association Of The United States Of America). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purposes of the corporation are—
(1)
to safeguard and strengthen the forces of faith and morality of our Nation;
(2)
to perpetuate and deepen the bonds of understanding and friendship of our military service;
(3)
to preserve our spiritual influence and interest in all members and veterans of the Armed Forces;
(4)
to uphold the Constitution of the United States; and
(5)
to promote justice, peace, and good will.
