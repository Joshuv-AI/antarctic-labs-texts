10 C.F.R. § 1705.11 — Exemptions.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1705.11
Creator: Defense Nuclear Facilities Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1705.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1705 (Privacy Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pursuant to 5 U.S.C. 552a(k), the Board has determined that system of records DNFSB-3, “Drug Testing Program Records,” is partially exempt from 5 U.S.C. 552(a)(c)(3), (d), (e)(1), (e)(4)(G), (H), (I), and (f). The exemption pertains to portions of these records which would identify persons supplying information on drug abuse by Board employees or contractors.
