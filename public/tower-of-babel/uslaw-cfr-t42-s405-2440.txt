42 C.F.R. § 405.2440 — Conditions for reinstatement after termination by CMS.
Citation: us:cfr:42:405.2440
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-405.2440
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 405 (Federal Health Insurance For The Aged And Disabled). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When CMS has terminated an agreement with a FQHC, CMS does not enter into another agreement with the FQHC to participate in the Medicare program unless CMS—
(a) Finds that the reason for the termination no longer exists; and
(b) Is assured that the reason for the termination of the prior agreement will not recur.
