24 C.F.R. § 792.203 — Application of amounts recovered.
Citation: us:cfr:24:792.203
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Housing And Urban Development (Housing Assistance Programs And Public And Indian Housing Programs)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-792.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 792 (Public Housing Agency Section 8 Fraud Recoveries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The PHA may only use the amount of the recovery it is authorized to retain in support of the section 8 program in which the fraud occurred.
(b) The remaining balance of the recovery proceeds (i.e., the portion of recovery the PHA is not authorized to retain) must be applied as directed by HUD.
