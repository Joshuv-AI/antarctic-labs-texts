Safety Zone; Cumberland River, Hendersonville, TN

AGENCY: Coast Guard, Department of Homeland Security.

ACTION: Notice of proposed rulemaking.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Coast Guard is proposing to establish a temporary safety 
zone for navigable waters on the Cumberland River from mile marker 
221.7 to 222. The safety zone is needed to protect personnel, vessels, 
and the marine environment from potential hazards associated with an 
overwater fireworks display. This proposed rulemaking would prohibit 
persons and vessels from being in the safety zone unless specifically 
authorized by the Captain of the Port, Ohio Valley, or their designated 
representative.

II. Background and Authority

    On July 17, 2026, an organization notified the Coast Guard that 
they will be sponsoring an event where fireworks will be launched from 
a barge on the Cumberland River Mile Marker 221.7-222 near Sanders 
Ferry Park. The event will begin with a morning fireworks display on 
October 29, 2026, from approximately 7:30 a.m. to 8 a.m., and will 
conclude with a second fireworks display on October 31, 2026, from 
approximately 5 p.m. through 5:15 p.m. The safety zone will be enforced 
for a period of time before and after the fireworks displays to ensure 
safety during staging and at the end of the fireworks. The safety zone 
will be enforced from 6 a.m. to 9:30 a.m. on October 29, 2026, and from 
4 p.m. to 6:30 p.m. on October 30, 2026. This event and associated 
fireworks displays are expected to draw a large number of spectator 
craft.
    The Captain of the Port, Ohio Valley (COTP) is issuing safety zone 
under the authority in 46 U.S.C. 70034. The COTP has determined that 
potential hazards with the fireworks displays include the launch of 
explosive projectiles, falling debris, and potential for vessel 
collisions. The purpose of this rulemaking is to protect event 
spectators and transiting vessels before, during, and after the 
scheduled fireworks displays.

III. Discussion of the Rule

    This proposed rule would establish a safety zone from 6:30 a.m. on 
October 29, 2026, until 6:30 p.m. on October 31, 2026. The safety zone 
would only be enforced from 6:30 a.m. to 9 a.m. on October 29, 2026, 
and from 4 p.m. until 6:30 p.m. on October 31, 2026. The safety zone 
would cover all navigable waters on the Cumberland River from mile 
marker 221.7 to 222. No vessel or person would be permitted to enter 
the safety zone without obtaining permission from the COTP or their 
designated representative. The regulatory text we are proposing appears 
at the end of this document.

IV. Regulatory Analyses

A. Impact on Small Entities

    The Regulatory Flexibility Act of 1980, 5 U.S.C. 601-612, as 
amended, requires Federal agencies to consider the potential impact of 
regulations on small entities during rulemaking. The term ``small 
entities'' comprises small businesses, not-for-profit organizations 
that are independently owned and operated and are not dominant in their 
fields, and governmental jurisdictions with populations of less than 
50,000. The Coast Guard certifies under 5 U.S.C. 605(b) that this 
proposed rule would not have a significant economic impact on a 
substantial number of small entities for the following reasons.
    Although vessel traffic would not be able to transit around this 
regulated area, the regulation would only impact a small area for 
approximately 2.5 hours each day. The Coast Guard would issue a 
Broadcast Notice to Marines via VHF FM marine channel 16, which would 
allow small entities to adjust their transit plans, and the rule allows 
vessels to request permission to enter the regulated area from the 
COTP.
    If you think that your business, organization, or governmental 
jurisdiction qualifies as a small entity and that this proposed rule 
would have a significant economic impact on it, please submit a comment 
(see ADDRESSES) explaining why you think it qualifies and how and to 
what degree this proposed rule would economically affect it.
    Under section 213(a) of the Small Business Regulatory Enforcement 
Fairness Act of 1996 (Pub. L. 104-121), if this proposed rule would 
affect your small business, organization, or governmental jurisdiction 
and you have questions, contact the person listed in the FOR FURTHER 
INFORMATION CONTACT section. Small businesses may send comments to the 
Small Business and Agriculture Regulatory Enforcement Ombudsman and the

Sec.  165.T899-1075  Safety Zone; Cumberland River, Hendersonville, TN

    (a) Location.
    This safety zone applies to the following regulated area: all 
waters of the Cumberland River from mile marker 221.7 to 222.
    (b) Definitions.
    As used in this section, designated representative means a Coast 
Guard Patrol Commander, including a Coast Guard coxswain, petty 
officer, or other officer operating a Coast Guard vessel and a Federal, 
State, and local officer designated by or assisting the COTP, Ohio 
Valley in the enforcement of the regulated area.
    (c) Regulations. (1) Under the general safety zone regulations in 
subpart C of this part, you may not enter the safety zone described in 
paragraph (a) of this section unless authorized by the COTP or the 
COTP's designated representative.
    (2) To seek permission to enter, contact the COTP or the COTP's 
representative on VHF-FM channel 16 or by telephone at (502) 779-5444. 
Those in the safety zone must comply with all lawful orders or 
directions given to them by the COTP or the COTP's designated 
representative.
    (d) Enforcement period. This section will be enforced on October 
29, 2026, from 6:30 a.m. to 9 a.m., and October 31, 2026, from 4 p.m. 
to 6:30 p.m.
