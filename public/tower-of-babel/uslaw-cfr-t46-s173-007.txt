46 C.F.R. § 173.007 — Location of the hook load.
Citation: us:cfr:46:173.007
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-173.007
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 173 (Special Rules Pertaining To Vessel Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When doing the calculations required in this subpart, the hook load must be considered to be located at the head of the crane.
