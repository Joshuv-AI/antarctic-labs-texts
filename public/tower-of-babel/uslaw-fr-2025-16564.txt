DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project No. 6411-004]


Valley Falls Associates; Albany Engineering Corporation; Notice 
of Transfer of Exemption

    1. On January 15, 2025, pursuant to 18 CFR 4.106(i), Albany 
Engineering Corporation, on behalf of Valley Falls Associates, the 
transferee for the 2,500-kilowatt James Thompson Hydroelectric Project 
No. 6411, filed a letter notifying the Federal Energy Regulatory 
Commission (Commission) that the exemption was transferred from Valley 
Falls Associates to Albany Engineering Corporation. The James Thompson 
Hydroelectric Project No. 6411 was originally issued an exemption on 
March 5, 1984.\1\ The project is located on the Hoosic River in the 
Towns of Pittstown and Schaghticoke, Rensselaer County, New York. The 
transfer of an exemption does not require Commission approval.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ James Thompson and Co., Inc., 26 FERC ] 62,193 (1984) (Order 
Granting Exemption from Licensing of a Small Hydroelectric Project 
of 5 Megawatts or Less).
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    2. Albany Engineering Corporation is now the exemptee of the James 
Thompson Hydroelectric Project No. 6411. All correspondence regarding 
the project should be directed to Wendy Jo Carey, Albany Engineering 
Corporation, 5 Washington Square, Albany, NY 12205, Email: 
[email protected].
