38 U.S.C. § 8156 — Coordination with health services development activities carried out under the National Health Planning and Resources Development Act of 1974
Citation: us:usc:38:8156
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section8156
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part VI (Acquisition And Disposition Of Property); Chapter 81 (Acquisition And Operation Of Hospital And Domiciliary Facilities; Procurement And Supply; Enhanced-Use Leases Of Real Property); Subchapter IV (Sharing Of Medical Facilities, Equipment, And Information). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary and the Secretary of Health and Human Services shall, to the maximum extent practicable, coordinate programs carried out under this subchapter and programs carried out under part F of title XVI of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 300t et seq.).
