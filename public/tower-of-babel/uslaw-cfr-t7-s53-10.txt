7 C.F.R. § 53.10 — When request for service deemed made.
Citation: us:cfr:7:53.10
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-53.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 53 (Livestock (Grading, Certification, And Standards)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A request for service under the regulations shall be deemed to be made when received by a market news office. Records showing the date and time of the request shall be made and kept in such office.
