40 C.F.R. § 40.120 — Publication of EPA research objectives.
Citation: us:cfr:40:40.120
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-40.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 40 (Research And Demonstration Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Office of Research and Development of EPA publishes a statement of research objectives and priorities annually in a document entitled “Office of Research and Development—Program Guide.” This document may be obtained from either the Office of Research and Development, RD-674, or the Grants Administration Division, PM-216, U.S. Environmental Protection Agency, Washington, DC 20460.
