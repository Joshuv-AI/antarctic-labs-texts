10 U.S.C. § 948h — Who may convene military commissions
Citation: us:usc:10:948h
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section948h
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 47A (Military Commissions); Subchapter II (Composition Of Military Commissions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Military commissions under this chapter may be convened by the Secretary of Defense or by any officer or official of the United States designated by the Secretary for that purpose.
