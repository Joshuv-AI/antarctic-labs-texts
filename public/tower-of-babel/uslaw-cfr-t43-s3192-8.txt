43 C.F.R. § 3192.8 — May I subcontract activities in the agreement?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3192.8
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3192.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3190 (Delegation Of Authority, Cooperative Agreements And Contracts For Oil And Gas Inspection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must obtain BLM's written approval before you subcontract any activities in the agreement with the exception of financial audits of program funds that are required by the Single Audit Act of 1984 (31 U.S.C. 7501 et seq.).
