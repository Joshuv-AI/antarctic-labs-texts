7 U.S.C. § 2285 — Budget prohibitions
Citation: us:usc:7:2285
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2285
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 55A (Department Of Agriculture Advisory Committees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No advisory committee may expend funds in excess of its estimated annual operating costs by more than 10 per centum or $500, whichever is greater, until it provides the Secretary with an explanation of the need for the additional expenditure and the Secretary approves such additional expenditure.
