7 U.S.C. § 1768 — Foreign Operations Administration unaffected
Citation: us:usc:7:1768
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1768
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 43 (Foreign Market Development); Subchapter III (Representation Allowances, Regulations, General Provisions, And Authorization For Appropriations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to affect personnel employed by or funds available to the Foreign Operations Administration or programs conducted under its authorities.
