8 C.F.R. § 1240.38 — Fingerprinting of excluded aliens.
Citation: us:cfr:8:1240.38
Creator: Executive Office For Immigration Review, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-1240.38
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 1240 (Proceedings To Determine Removability Of Aliens In The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every alien 14 years of age or older who is excluded from admission to the United States by an immigration judge shall be fingerprinted, unless during the preceding year he or she has been fingerprinted at an American consular office.
