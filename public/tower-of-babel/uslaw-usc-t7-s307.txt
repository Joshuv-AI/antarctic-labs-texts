7 U.S.C. § 307 — Fees for locating land scrip
Citation: us:usc:7:307
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section307
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 13 (Agricultural And Mechanical Colleges); Subchapter I (College-Aid Land Appropriation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The land officers shall receive the same fees for locating land scrip issued under the provisions of this subchapter as was on July 2, 1862, allowed for the location of military bounty land warrants under laws existing at that time: Provided, That their maximum compensation shall not be thereby increased.
