12 U.S.C. § 2905 — Regulations
Citation: us:usc:12:2905
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section2905
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 30 (Community Reinvestment). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Regulations to carry out the purposes of this chapter shall be published by each appropriate Federal financial supervisory agency, except that the Comptroller of the Currency shall prescribe regulations applicable to savings associations and the Board of Governors shall prescribe regulations applicable to insured State member banks, bank holding companies and savings and loan holding companies,,1 and shall take effect no later than 390 days after October 12, 1977.
