52 U.S.C. § 20101 — Congressional declaration of purpose
Citation: us:usc:52:20101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title52-section20101
Context: United States Code, Title 52 (Voting And Elections); Chapter 201 (Voting Accessibility For The Elderly And Handicapped). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the intention of Congress in enacting this chapter to promote the fundamental right to vote by improving access for handicapped and elderly individuals to registration facilities and polling places for Federal elections.
