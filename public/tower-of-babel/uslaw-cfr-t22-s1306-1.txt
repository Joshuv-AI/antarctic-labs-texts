22 C.F.R. § 1306.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:22:1306.1
Creator: Millennium Challenge Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1306.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1306 (Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part prescribe the procedures to be used by the Millennium Challenge Corporation (MCC) in the collection and/or disposal of non-tax debts owed to MCC and to the United States.
