On the Narrow 2-Class Field Tower of Some Real Quadratic Number Fields: Lengths Heuristics Follow-Up
Elliot Benjamin, Mohamed Mahmoud Chems-Eddin

Abstract
. In this article we continue the investigation of the length of the narrow 2 2 -class field tower of real quadratic number fields k \mathrm{k} whose discriminants are not a sum of two squares and for which their 2 2 -class groups are elementary of order 4 4 . Letting G \mathrm{G} equal the Galois group of the second Hilbert narrow 2 2 -class field over k \mathrm{k} , and [ G i ] [\mathrm{G}_{i}] denote the lower central series of G \mathrm{G} , we give heuristic evidence that the length of the narrow 2 2 -class field tower of k \mathrm{k} is equal to 2 2 when G / G 3 \mathrm{G}/\mathrm{G}_{3} is of type 64.150 64.150 (in the tables of Hall and Senior). We also give the formulation of the relevant unit groups of the narrow Hilbert 2 2 -class field for these fields.
