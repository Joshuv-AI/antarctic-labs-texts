30 C.F.R. § 250.421 — What are the casing and cementing requirements by type of casing string?
Citation: us:cfr:30:250.421
Creator: Bureau Of Safety And Environmental Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-250.421
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 250 (Oil And Gas And Sulphur Operations In The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The table in this section identifies specific design, setting, and cementing requirements for casing strings and liners. For the purposes of subpart D, the casing strings in order of normal installation are as follows: drive or structural, conductor, surface, intermediate, and production casings (including liners). The District Manager may approve or prescribe other casing and cementing requirements where appropriate.
