7 C.F.R. § 755.11 — Maintaining records.
Citation: us:cfr:7:755.11
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-755.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 755 (Reimbursement Transportation Cost Payment Program For Geographically Disadvantaged Farmers And Ranchers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Persons applying for payment under this part must maintain records and accounts to document all eligibility requirements specified in this part. Such records and accounts must be retained for 3 years after the date of payment to the producer under this part.
