16 U.S.C. § 822 — Reservation of right to alter or repeal chapter
Citation: us:usc:16:822
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section822
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 12 (Federal Regulation And Development Of Power); Subchapter I (Regulation Of The Development Of Water Power And Resources). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this chapter is expressly reserved; but no such alteration, amendment, or repeal shall affect any license theretofore issued under the provisions of this chapter or the rights of any licensee thereunder.
