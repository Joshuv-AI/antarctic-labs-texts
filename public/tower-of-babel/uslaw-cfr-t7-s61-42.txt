7 C.F.R. § 61.42 — Information on sampling to be kept confidential.
Citation: us:cfr:7:61.42
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-61.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 61 (Cottonseed Sold Or Offered For Sale For Crushing Purposes (Inspection, Sampling And Certification)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every person licensed under the act as a sampler of cottonseed shall keep confidential all information secured by him relative to shipments of cottonseed sampled by him. He shall not disclose such information to any person except an authorized representative of the Department.
