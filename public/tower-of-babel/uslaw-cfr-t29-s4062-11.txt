29 C.F.R. § 4062.11 — Computation of time.
Citation: us:cfr:29:4062.11
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4062.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4062 (Liability For Termination Of Single-Employer Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The PBGC applies the rules in subpart D of part 4000 of this chapter to compute any time period under this part. However, for purposes of determining the amount of an interest charge under § 4062.7, the rule in § 4000.43(a) of this chapter governing periods ending on weekends or Federal holidays does not apply.
