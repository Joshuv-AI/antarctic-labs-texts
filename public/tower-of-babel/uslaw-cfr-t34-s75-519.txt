34 C.F.R. § 75.519 — Dual compensation of staff.
Citation: us:cfr:34:75.519
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-75.519
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 75 (Direct Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A grant may not use its grant to pay a project staff member for time or work for which that staff member is compensated from some other source of funds, consistent with the cost principles described in 2 CFR part 200.
