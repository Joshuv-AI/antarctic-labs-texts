48 C.F.R. § 1501.104 — 1501.104 Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1501.104
Creator: Chapter 15—Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1501.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1501 (General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The FAR (48 CFR chapter 1) and the EPAAR (48 CFR chapter 15) apply to all EPA acquisitions as defined in part 2 of the FAR, except where expressly excluded.
