Correction: The blood lipidome fatty acid profile predicts the disease risk and clinical phenotypes of Alzheimer’s disease: associations from two prospective cohort studies
Authors: Wen-Zheng Liu, Liang-Yu Huang, Song Chi, Ya-Hui Ma, Chen-Chen Tan, Lan Tan, Wei Xu
Published: 2026

Correction to: Translational Psychiatry 10.1038/s41398-025-03526-w, published online 06 October 2025

In accordance with the ADNI Data Use Agreement (DUA), the Alzheimer’s Disease Metabolomics Consortium is included in the author list. The consortium appears after the Alzheimer’s Disease Neuroimaging Initiative and before Xu Wei (corresponding author).

In addition, the following statement has been added to the Funding section:

“ADNI Data collection and sharing for this project was funded by the Alzheimer’s Disease Metabolomics Consortium (National Institute on Aging R01AG046171, RF1AG051550, and 3U01AG024904-09S4).” The original article has been corrected.
