34 C.F.R. § 675.31 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:34:675.31
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-675.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 675 (Federal Work-Study Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of the Job Location and Development program is to expand off-campus job opportunities for students who are enrolled in eligible institutions of higher education and want jobs, regardless of their financial need, and to encourage students to participate in community service activities.
