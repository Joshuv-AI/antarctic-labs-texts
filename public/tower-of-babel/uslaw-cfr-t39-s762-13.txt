39 C.F.R. § 762.13 — Issuance.
Citation: us:cfr:39:762.13
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-762.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 762 (Disbursement Postal Money Orders). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Disbursement Postal Money Orders are issued solely by Postal Data Centers and solely for the purpose of paying Postal Service obligations. Accordingly, Disbursement Postal Money Orders may be issued in lieu of U.S. Treasury checks.
