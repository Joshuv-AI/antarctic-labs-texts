40 C.F.R. § 455.61 — Special definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:40:455.61
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-455.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 455 (Pesticide Chemicals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Process wastewater, for this subpart, means all wastewater except for sanitary water and those wastewaters excluded from the applicability of the rule in § 455.60.
