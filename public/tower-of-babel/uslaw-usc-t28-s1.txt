28 U.S.C. § 1 — Number of justices; quorum
Citation: us:usc:28:1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part I (Organization Of Courts); Chapter 1 (Supreme Court). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Supreme Court of the United States shall consist of a Chief Justice of the United States and eight associate justices, any six of whom shall constitute a quorum.
