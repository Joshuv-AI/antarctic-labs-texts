43 C.F.R. § 4750.3-1 — -1 Application for private maintenance of wild horses and burros.
Citation: us:cfr:43:4750.3-1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4750.3-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4700 (Protection, Management, And Control Of Wild Free-Roaming Horses And Burros). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An individual applying for a wild horse or burro shall file an application with the Bureau of Land Management on a form approved by the Director.
