17 C.F.R. § 10.36 — Commission review of rulings.
Citation: us:cfr:17:10.36
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-10.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 10 (Rules Of Practice). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Interlocutory review by the Commission of a ruling as to matters within the scope of § 10.33, § 10.34 or § 10.35 may be sought in accordance with the procedures set forth in § 10.101 of these rules without certification by the Administrative Law Judge.
