23 C.F.R. § 230.119 — Monitoring of supportive services.
Citation: us:cfr:23:230.119
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-230.119
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 230 (External Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Supportive services procured by a State highway agency shall be monitored by both the State highway agency and the division office.
