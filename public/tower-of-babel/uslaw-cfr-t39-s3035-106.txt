39 C.F.R. § 3035.106 — Sufficiency of information.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3035.106
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3035.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3035 (Regulation Of Rates For Competitive Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If, after review of the information submitted pursuant to this part, the Commission determines additional information is necessary to enable it to evaluate whether competitive products will be in compliance with 39 U.S.C. 3633(a), it may, in its discretion, require the Postal Service to provide additional information as deemed necessary.
