49 C.F.R. § 562.7 — Lighting and marking requirements for new agricultural equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:49:562.7
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-562.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 562 (Lighting And Marking Of Agricultural Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

New agricultural equipment that may be operated on a public road must meet the lighting and marking standards set forth in ANSI/ASAE 279.14 JUL2008, “Lighting and Marking of Agricultural Equipment on Highways” (incorporated by reference, see § 562.11).
