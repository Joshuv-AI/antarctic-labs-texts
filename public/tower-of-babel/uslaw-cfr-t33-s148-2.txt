33 C.F.R. § 148.2 — Who is responsible for implementing this subchapter?
Citation: us:cfr:33:148.2
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-148.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 148 (Deepwater Ports: General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless otherwise specified, the owner of a deepwater port must ensure that the requirements of this subchapter are carried out at that port.
