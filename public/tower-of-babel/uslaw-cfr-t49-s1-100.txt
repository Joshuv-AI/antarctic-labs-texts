49 C.F.R. § 1.100 — The Saint Lawrence Seaway Development Corporation.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1.100
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1 (Organization And Delegation Of Powers And Duties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Is responsible for the development, operation, and maintenance of that part of the Saint Lawrence Seaway within the territorial limits of the United States.
