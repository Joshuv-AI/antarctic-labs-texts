40 C.F.R. § 197.38 — Are the Individual Protection and Ground Water Protection Standards Severable?
Citation: us:cfr:40:197.38
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-197.38
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 197 (Public Health And Environmental Radiation Protection Standards For Yucca Mountain, Nevada). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. The individual protection and ground water protection standards are severable.
