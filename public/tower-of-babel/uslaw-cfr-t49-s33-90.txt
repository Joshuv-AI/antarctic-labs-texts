49 C.F.R. § 33.90 — Protection against claims.
Citation: us:cfr:49:33.90
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-33.90
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 33 (Transportation Priorities And Allocation System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person shall not be held liable for damages or penalties for any act or failure to act resulting directly or indirectly from compliance with any provision of this part, or an official action, notwithstanding that such provision or action shall subsequently be declared invalid by judicial or other competent authority.
