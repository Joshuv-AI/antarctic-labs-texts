42 C.F.R. § 52.4 — How to apply for a grant.
Citation: us:cfr:42:52.4
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-52.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 52 (Grants For Research Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each institution interested in applying for a grant under this part must submit an application at such time and in such form and manner as the Secretary may prescribe.
