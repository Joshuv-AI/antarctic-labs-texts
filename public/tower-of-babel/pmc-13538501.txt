Retraction Note: FTIR based assessment of microplastic contamination in soil water and insect ecosystems reveals environmental and ecological risks
Authors: S Rathikannu, Sneha Gautam, Suneel Kumar Joshi, Praveena Katharine, K E Mithra, P Banusaranya, V M Amudhavarshini, R Gayatri, Chang-Hoi Ho
Published: 2026

Retraction of: Scientific Reports https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-14507-w, published online 05 August 2025

The Editors have retracted this Article.

Following publication, concerns were raised regarding inadequate reporting of the FTIR methodology, particularly the polymer identification criteria, use of standards for method validation, and quality control details. Additionally, the spectra data presented in Figures 4, 5, and 6 do not contain peaks consistent with the reported identification of polyethylene, polystyrene, and polypropylene. The Authors provided raw data and additional information about the experimental reporting, however, they did not adequately clarify how the reported polymer types were identified and quantified. The Authors further confirmed that polymer standards were not used during the study to reliably identify and validate the reported presence of microplastics. The Editors have therefore lost confidence in the claims of this Article.

Sneha Gautam agrees with this retraction. S. Rathikannu, Suneel Kumar Joshi, Praveena Katharine, K. E. Mithra, P. Banusaranya, V. M. Amudhavarshini, R. Gayatri, and Chang-Hoi Ho have not responded to correspondence regarding this retraction.
