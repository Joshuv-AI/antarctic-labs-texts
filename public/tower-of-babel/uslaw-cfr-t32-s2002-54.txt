32 C.F.R. § 2002.54 — Misuse of CUI.
Citation: us:cfr:32:2002.54
Creator: Information Security Oversight Office, National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-2002.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 2002 (Controlled Unclassified Information (Cui)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The CUI SAO must establish agency processes and criteria for reporting and investigating misuse of CUI.
(b) The CUI EA reports findings on any incident involving misuse of CUI to the offending agency's CUI SAO or CUI Program manager for action, as appropriate.
