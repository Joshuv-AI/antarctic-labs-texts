29 C.F.R. § 1611.10 — Disclosure of record to person other than the individual to whom it pertains.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1611.10
Creator: Equal Employment Opportunity Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1611.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1611 (Privacy Act Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission shall not disclose any record which is contained in a system of records it maintains, by any means of communication to any person or to another agency, except pursuant to a written request by, or with the prior written consent of the individual to whom the record pertains, unless the disclosure is authorized by one or more provisions of 5 U.S.C. 552a(b).
