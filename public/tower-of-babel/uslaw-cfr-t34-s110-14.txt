34 C.F.R. § 110.14 — Burden of proof.
Citation: us:cfr:34:110.14
Creator: Office For Civil Rights, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-110.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 110 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Age In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The burden of proving that an age distinction or other action falls within the exceptions outlined in §§ 110.12 and 110.13 is on the recipient of Federal financial assistance.
