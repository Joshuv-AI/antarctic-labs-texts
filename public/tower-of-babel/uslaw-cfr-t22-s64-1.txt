22 C.F.R. § 64.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:22:64.1
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-64.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 64 (Participation By Federal Employees In Cultural Exchange Programs Of Foreign Countries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth the procedures for the application for approval of a cultural exchange program of a foreign government, so that Federal employees may participate in such program; the grant and termination of such approval; and related procedures.
