42 U.S.C. § 1591d — Powers as cumulative and additional
Citation: us:usc:42:1591d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1591d
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 9 (Housing Of Persons Engaged In National Defense); Subchapter VIII (Critical Defense Housing Areas). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as may be otherwise expressly provided in this Act, all powers and authorities conferred by this Act shall be cumulative and additional to and not in derogation of any powers and authorities otherwise existing.
