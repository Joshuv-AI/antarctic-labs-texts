27 C.F.R. § 29.57 — Failure to register; penalty.
Citation: us:cfr:27:29.57
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-29.57
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 29 (Stills And Miscellaneous Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person having possession, custody, or control of any still or distilling apparatus set up who fails to register the still or distilling apparatus is subject to a fine of not more than $10,000 or imprisonment of not more than 5 years, or both, and the still or distilling apparatus is forfeitable to the Government.
