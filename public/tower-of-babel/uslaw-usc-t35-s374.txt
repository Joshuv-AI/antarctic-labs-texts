35 U.S.C. § 374 — Publication of international application
Citation: us:usc:35:374
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title35-section374
Context: United States Code, Title 35 (Patents); Part IV (Patent Cooperation Treaty); Chapter 37 (National Stage). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The publication under the treaty defined in section 351(a), of an international application designating the United States shall be deemed a publication under section 122(b), except as provided in section 154(d).
