16 C.F.R. § 1200.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1200.1
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1200.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1200 (Definition Of Children'S Product Under The Consumer Product Safety Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part provides guidance on the definition of children's product and the factors the Commission will consider when making determinations regarding children's products as set forth under 15 U.S.C. 2052(2).
