33 U.S.C. § 3844 — Additional authority of the Administrator
Citation: us:usc:33:3844
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section3844
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 51 (Clean Hulls); Subchapter III (Prohibitions And Enforcement Authority). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator, in consultation with the Secretary, may establish, as necessary, terms and conditions regarding the removal and disposal of antifouling systems prohibited or restricted under this chapter.
