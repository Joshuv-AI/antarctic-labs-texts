25 U.S.C. § 2108 — Tribal right to develop mineral resources
Citation: us:usc:25:2108
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section2108
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 23 (Development Of Tribal Mineral Resources). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall impair any right of an Indian tribe organized under section 16 or 17 of the Act of June 18, 1934 (48 Stat. 987), as amended [25 U.S.C. 5123, 5124], to develop their mineral resources as may be provided in any constitution or charter adopted by such tribe pursuant to that Act [25 U.S.C. 5101 et seq.].
