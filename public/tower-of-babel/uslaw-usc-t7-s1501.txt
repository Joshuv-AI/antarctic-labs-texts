7 U.S.C. § 1501 — Short title and application of other provisions
Citation: us:usc:7:1501
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1501
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 36 (Crop Insurance); Subchapter I (Federal Crop Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This subchapter may be cited as the “Federal Crop Insurance Act”. Except as otherwise expressly provided the provisions in titles I to IV, inclusive, shall not apply with respect to this subchapter, and the term “Act” wherever it appears in such titles shall not be construed to include this subchapter.
