43 U.S.C. § 2634 — Appropriations to carry out sections 2632 and 2633
Citation: us:usc:43:2634
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section2634
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 44 (Oregon And California Railroad And Coos Bay Wagon Road Grant Lands); Subchapter III (Administrative Jurisdiction). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of carrying out the provisions of sections 2632 and 2633 of this title there are authorized to be appropriated such sums as the Congress may from time to time determine to be necessary.
