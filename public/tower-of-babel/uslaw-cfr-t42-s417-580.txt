42 C.F.R. § 417.580 — Basis and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:42:417.580
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-417.580
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 417 (Health Maintenance Organizations, Competitive Medical Plans, And Health Care Prepayment Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Basis. This subpart implements those portions of section 1876 (a), (e), and (g) of the Act that pertain to the amount CMS pays an organization for its Medicare enrollees who are enrolled on a risk basis.
(b) Scope. This subpart sets forth—
(1) Method of payment;
(2) Procedures for determining the HMO's or CMP's payment rate; and
(3) Procedures for determining the additional benefits (and their value) the HMO or CMP must provide to its Medicare enrollees.
