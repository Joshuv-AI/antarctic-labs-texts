42 U.S.C. § 13201 — “Secretary” defined
Citation: us:usc:42:13201
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section13201
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 134 (Energy Policy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of this Act, the term “Secretary” means the Secretary of Energy.
