2 C.F.R. § 1138.515 — Use and disposition of real property, equipment, supplies, and federally owned property under subawards.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1138.515
Creator: Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1138.515
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1138 (Requirements Related To Subawards: General Award Terms And Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Requirement. A DoD Component's general terms and conditions must specify the requirements concerning use and disposition of real property, equipment, supplies, and federally owned property that recipients must include in subawards.
(b) Award terms and conditions. A DoD Component's general terms and conditions must use the wording appendix E to this part provides for Sections D through F of SUB Article V.
