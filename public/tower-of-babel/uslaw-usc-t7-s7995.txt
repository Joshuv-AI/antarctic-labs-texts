7 U.S.C. § 7995 — Assignment of payments
Citation: us:usc:7:7995
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section7995
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 106 (Commodity Programs); Subchapter VI (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of section 590h(g) of title 16, relating to assignment of payments, shall apply to payments made under the authority of this Act. The producer making the assignment, or the assignee, shall provide the Secretary with notice, in such manner as the Secretary may require, of any assignment made under this section.
