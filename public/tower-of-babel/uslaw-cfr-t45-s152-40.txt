45 C.F.R. § 152.40 — Relation to State laws.
Citation: us:cfr:45:152.40
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-152.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 152 (Pre-Existing Condition Insurance Plan Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The standards established under this section shall supersede any State law or regulation, other than State licensing laws or State laws relating to plan solvency, with respect to PCIPs which are established in accordance with this section.
