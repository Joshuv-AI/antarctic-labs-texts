48 C.F.R. § 1401.405 — 1401.405 Deviations pertaining to treaties and executive agreements.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1401.405
Creator: Chapter 14—Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1401.405
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1401 (Department Of The Interior Acquisition Regulation System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Director, PAM, is responsible for transmitting to the FAR Secretariat the information required in FAR 1.405(d).
(b) For deviations not authorized by FAR 1.405(b) or (c), the Director, PAM, shall process the request for deviation through the FAR Secretariat.
(c) Deviations authorized or requested under paragraph (d) or (e) of this section shall be submitted by the HCA to the Director, PAM for further action.
