30 U.S.C. § 702 — “Qualified applicant” defined
Citation: us:usc:30:702
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section702
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 20 (Conveyances To Occupants Of Unpatented Mining Claims). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purposes of this chapter a qualified applicant is a residential occupant-owner, as of October 23, 1962, of valuable improvements in an unpatented mining claim which constitute for him a principal place of residence and which he and his predecessors in interest were in possession of for not less than seven years prior to July 23, 1962.
