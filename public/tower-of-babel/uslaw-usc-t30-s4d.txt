30 U.S.C. § 4d — Plants, machinery, and equipment
Citation: us:usc:30:4d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section4d
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 1 (United States Bureau Of Mines). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States Bureau of Mines is further authorized, under the general direction of the Secretary of the Interior, to erect such plants, construct and purchase such machinery and equipment, and to take such other steps as it may deem necessary and proper to effectuate the purposes of section 4c of this title.
