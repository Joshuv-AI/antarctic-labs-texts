Regularity of paths of stochastic measures


Abstract
Random functions μ ⁡ ( x ) \mu(x) , generated by values of stochastic measures are considered. The Besov regularity of the continuous paths of μ ⁡ ( x ) \mu(x) , x ∈ [ 0 , 1 ] d x\in[0,1]^{d} is proved. Fourier series expansion of μ ⁡ ( x ) \mu(x) , x ∈ [ 0 , 2 ​ π ] x\in[0,2\pi] is obtained. These results are proved under weaker conditions than similar results in previous papers.

Introduction

In this paper, we consider the properties of paths of processes generated by values of stochastic measures (SMs). SM is a stochastic set function that is
\sigma
-additive in probability, see exact definition and examples in subsection
2.1

Besov regularity of paths of SMs was studied in
[
15
]
and
[
16
]
, Fourier series expansions of paths of SMs were obtained in
[
17
]
. Some important results in these papers were obtained under the following condition for SM
\mu
defined on measurable space
({\sf{X}},\mathcal{B})
.

Assumption A 1
.

There exists a real-valued finite measure
{\sf{m}}

on
({\sf{X}},\mathcal{B})
with the following property: if a measurable function
g:{\sf{X}}\to\mathbb{R}
be such that
\int_{{\sf{X}}}g^{2}\,d{\sf{m}}<+\infty
then

is integrable w. r. t.
\mu

{\sf{X}}
.

In this paper, we prove that some statements from
[
16
]
and
[
17
]
remain valid if we assume the following condition instead of A
1
.

Assumption A 2
.

There exists a real-valued finite measure
{\sf{m}}

on
({\sf{X}},\mathcal{B})
with the following property: if a measurable function
g:{\sf{X}}\to\mathbb{R}
is such that for all
\lambda\in\mathbb{R},\ \lambda>0

\int_{{\sf{X}}}2^{\lambda|g|}\,d{\sf{m}}<+\infty
then

is integrable w. r. t.
\mu
on
{\sf{X}}

Obviously, condition A

Our proofs are very similar to respective proofs in
[
16
]
and
[
17
]
with one essential change – now we use Lemma
2
from the present paper. In the previous publications, the statement of Lemma

The rest of the paper is organised as follows. In Section

2.2
Besov spaces

These classical functional Banach spaces found many applications in mathematical physics, function theory, and functional analysis. We recall the definition of Besov spaces following
[
10
]
.

For functions
f\in{\sf{L}}_{p}([0,1]^{d})={\sf{L}}_{p}([0,1]^{d},{\sf{m}}_{L})
we set

\|f\|_{B^{\alpha}_{p,q}([0,1]^{d})}=\|f\|_{{\sf{L}}_{p}([0,1]^{d})}+\Bigl(\int_{0}^{1}(\omega_{p}(f,r))^{q}r^{-\alpha q-1}\,dr\Bigr)^{1/q},

where
\omega_{p}
denotes the
{\sf{L}}_{p}
-modulus of continuity,

\displaystyle\omega_{p}(f,r)=\sup_{|h|\leq r}\Bigl(\int_{I_{h}}|f(x+h)-f(x)|^{p}\,dx\Bigr)^{1/p},

\displaystyle I_{h}=\{x\in[0,1]^{d}:\ x+h\in[0,1]^{d}\},

|h|
denotes the Euclidean norm in
\mathbb{R}^{d}
. Then

B^{\alpha}_{p,q}([0,1]^{d})=\{f\in{\sf{L}}_{p}([0,1]^{d}):\ \|f\|_{B^{\alpha}_{p,q}([0,1]^{d})}<+\infty\},

and
\|\cdot\|_{B^{\alpha}_{p,q}([0,1]^{d})}
is a norm in this space.

Besov regularity of trajectories of random processes is well-studied, see, for example,
[
5
]
,
[
7
]
,
[
8
]
. For Besov–Orlicz spaces, similar results are obtained in
[
14
]
,
[
23
]
. Besov regularity and continuity of paths of multidimensional integral w.r.t. SMs are considered in
[
13
]
.

\displaystyle{\sf{P}}^{\prime}\{\lambda|\eta(x,\omega^{\prime})|\geq 1\}\leq{\textrm{Var}}\,(\lambda|\eta(x,\omega^{\prime})|)=\lambda^{2}\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}f^{2}_{k}(x)\leq\lambda^{2}C_{f}.

\int_{\sf{X}}2^{\lambda_{0}\zeta(x,\omega^{\prime})}\,d{\sf{m}}(x)<+\infty\quad{\sf{P}}^{\prime}-\textrm{a.\penalty\ s.}

{\sf{P}}^{\prime}

For all

Firstly, consider the case

Let
\mu

\mathcal{B}([0,2\pi])
. Consider the random process
\mu(t)=\mu((0,t])
,

Assume that paths of the process
\mu(t)
are Riemann integrable on

\mu(t)
for each fixed

\displaystyle\xi_{k}=\frac{1}{\pi}\int_{[0,2\pi]}\mu(s)\cos ks\,ds,\quad\eta_{k}=\frac{1}{\pi}\int_{[0,2\pi]}\mu(s)\sin ks\,ds,

(14)

\displaystyle\mu(t)\sim\frac{\xi_{0}}{2}+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}(\xi_{k}\cos kt+\eta_{k}\sin kt).

(15)

Set

S_{n}(t)=\frac{\xi_{0}}{2}+\sum_{k=1}^{n}(\xi_{k}\cos kt+\eta_{k}\sin kt).

Applying the integration by parts formula for integrals in (
14

[
17
, Lemma 1]
or
[
19
, Lemma 2.7]
), we get

\begin{split}\xi_{k}=-\frac{1}{k\pi}\int_{(0,2\pi]}\sin ks\,d\mu,\quad\eta_{k}=\frac{1}{k\pi}\int_{(0,2\pi]}(\cos ks-1)\,d\mu,\quad k\geq 1,\\
\xi_{0}=2\mu((0,2\pi])-\frac{1}{\pi}\int_{(0,2\pi]}s\,d\mu,\end{split}

(16)

and these integrals are defined for any SM
\mu
without the assuming Riemann integrability.

In the sequel, we will assume that
\xi_{k}
and
\eta_{k}
are defined by (
16
), an consider the Fourier series for arbitrary SM
\mu
on
\mathcal{B}([0,2\pi])

Theorem 2
.

If Assumption A
2

{\sf{P}}\bigl[S_{n}(t)\ \textrm{converges }{\sf{m}}_{L}\textrm{-a.s.\ on\ }[0,2\pi]\bigr]=1.

Proof.

Applying Lemma
2
for

f_{k}=\dfrac{\sin kt}{\pi k}\quad\textrm{and}\quad f_{k}=\dfrac{\cos kt-1}{\pi k},

using (
16
), we get
\sum_{k}(\xi_{k}^{2}+\eta_{k}^{2})<\infty
a. s. Applying the famous Carleson’s theorem (see

]

Further, we prove that, under some assumptions,

Representations of processes in the form of random series began with the well-known Paley–Wiener expansion of the Wiener process. A similar representation of the fractional Brownian motion was obtained in
[

The following statement gives a generalization of the Dirichlet – Jordan theorem about Fourier series expansion of functions of bounded variation, see, for example,
[

Theorem 3
.

Let Assumption A
2
holds, and paths of the process
\mu(t)
,

S_{n}(t)\stackrel{{\scriptstyle{\sf P}}}{{\to}}\mu(t)
and

S_{n}(0)=S_{n}(2\pi)\stackrel{{\scriptstyle{\sf P}}}{{\to}}\mu(2\pi)/2
as

Proof.

Without loss of generality, we may assume that
\mu((0,2\pi])=0
, and the periodic continuation of

\mu(t)
to
\mathbb{R}
is continuous. Otherwise, we can consider the SM

\widetilde{\mu}({\sf A})=\mu({\sf A})-{\sf{m}}_{L}({\sf A})\dfrac{\mu((0,2\pi])}{2\pi}.

Set

\displaystyle S_{n}^{*}(t)=\frac{1}{2}(S_{n-1}(t)+S_{n}(t))=S_{n-1}(t)+\frac{1}{2}(\xi_{n}\cos nt+\eta_{n}\sin nt),

\displaystyle\varphi_{t}(s)=\frac{1}{2}(\mu(t+s)+\mu(t-s)-2\mu(t))=\frac{1}{2}\bigl(\mu((t,t+s])-\mu((t-s,t])\bigr).

From Theorem (II.10.1)

\displaystyle|S_{n}^{*}(t)-\mu(t)|\leq\frac{1}{\pi}\int_{h}^{\pi}\frac{|\varphi_{t}(s)-\varphi_{t}(s+h)|}{s}\,ds+h\int_{h}^{\pi}\frac{|\varphi_{t}(s)|}{s^{2}}\,ds+\frac{2}{h}\int_{0}^{2h}{|\varphi_{t}(s)|}\,ds+o(1)

\displaystyle:=I_{1}+I_{2}+I_{3}+o(1).

Continuity of

as

\displaystyle 2\pi I_{1}=\int_{h}^{\pi}\frac{|\mu((t+s,t+s+h])-\mu((t-s-h,t-s])|}{s}\,ds

\displaystyle\leq\Bigl(\int_{h}^{\pi}\frac{h^{1/2}}{s^{3/2}}\,ds\Bigr)^{\frac{2}{3}}\Bigl(\int_{h}^{\pi}\frac{|\mu((t+s,t+s+h])-\mu((t-s-h,t-s])|^{3}}{h}\,ds\Bigr)^{\frac{1}{3}}.
