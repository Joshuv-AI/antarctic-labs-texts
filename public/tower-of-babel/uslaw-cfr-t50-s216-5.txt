50 C.F.R. § 216.5 — Payment of penalty.
Citation: us:cfr:50:216.5
Creator: National Marine Fisheries Service, National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-216.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 216 (Regulations Governing The Taking And Importing Of Marine Mammals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The respondent shall have 30 days from receipt of the final assessment decision within which to pay the penalty assessed. Upon a failure to pay the penalty, the Secretary may request the Attorney General to institute a civil action in the appropriate United States District Court to collect the penalty.
