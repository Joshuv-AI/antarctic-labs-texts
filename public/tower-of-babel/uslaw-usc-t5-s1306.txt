5 U.S.C. § 1306 — Oaths to witnesses
Citation: us:usc:5:1306
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section1306
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part II (Civil Service Functions And Responsibilities); Chapter 13 (Special Authority). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of the Office of Personnel Management and authorized representatives of the Director may administer oaths to witnesses in matters pending before the Office.
