7 C.F.R. § 2500.044 — Indirect costs.
Citation: us:cfr:7:2500.044
Creator: Office Of Advocacy And Outreach, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-2500.044
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 2500 (Oao Federal Financial Assistance Programs—General Award Administrative Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Indirect cost rates for grants and cooperative agreements shall be determined in accordance with the applicable assistance regulations and cost principles, unless superseded by another authority.
