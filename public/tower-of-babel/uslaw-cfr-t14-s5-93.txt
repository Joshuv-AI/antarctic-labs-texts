14 C.F.R. § 5.93 — Safety communication.
Citation: us:cfr:14:5.93
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-5.93
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 5 (Safety Management Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person required to have an SMS under this part must develop and maintain means for communicating safety information that, at a minimum:
(a) Ensures that employees are aware of the SMS policies, processes, and tools that are relevant to their responsibilities.
(b) Conveys hazard information relevant to the employee's responsibilities.
(c) Explains why safety actions have been taken.
(d) Explains why safety procedures are introduced or changed.
