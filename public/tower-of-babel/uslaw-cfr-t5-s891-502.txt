5 C.F.R. § 891.502 — Standards for carrier of uniform plan.
Citation: us:cfr:5:891.502
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-891.502
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 891 (Retired Federal Employees Health Benefits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the most recent year for which data are available, the carrier of the uniform plan shall have made at least 1 percent of all group health insurance benefit payments in the United States. If the carrier is an insurance company, it must be licensed to issue group health insurance in all the States of the United States and the District of Columbia.
