Homotopy similarity of maps. Compositions
S. S. Podkorytov

Abstract
We describe the behaviour of the homotopy similarity relations and finite-order invariants under the function [ X , Y ] → [ X , Z ] [X,Y]\to[X,Z] induced by a map Y → Z Y\to Z strongly r r -similar to the constant map.
