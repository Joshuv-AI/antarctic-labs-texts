34 C.F.R. § 648.61 — How must the academic department supervise the training of fellows?
Citation: us:cfr:34:648.61
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-648.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 648 (Graduate Assistance In Areas Of National Need). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The institution shall provide to fellows at least one academic year of supervised training in instruction at the graduate or undergraduate level at the schedule of at least one-half-time teaching assistant.
