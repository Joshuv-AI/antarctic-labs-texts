36 C.F.R. § 21.2 — Penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:36:21.2
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-21.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 21 (Hot Springs National Park; Bathhouse Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person convicted of violating any provision of the regulations contained in this part, or as the same may be amended or supplemented, shall be punished by a fine not exceeding $100 and shall be adjudged to pay all costs of the proceedings.
