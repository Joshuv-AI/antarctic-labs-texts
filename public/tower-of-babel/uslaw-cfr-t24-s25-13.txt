24 C.F.R. § 25.13 — Notifying GNMA of withdrawal actions.
Citation: us:cfr:24:25.13
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-25.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 25 (Mortgagee Review Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the Board issues a notice of violation that could lead to withdrawal of a mortgagee's approval, or is notified by GNMA of an action that could lead to withdrawal of GNMA approval, the Board shall proceed in accordance with 12 U.S.C. 1708(d).
