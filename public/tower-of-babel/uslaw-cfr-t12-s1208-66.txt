12 C.F.R. § 1208.66 — Hearing official.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1208.66
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1208.66
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1208 (Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A hearing official may be any qualified individual, as determined by FHFA, including an administrative law judge.
