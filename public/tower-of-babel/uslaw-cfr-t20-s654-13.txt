20 C.F.R. § 654.13 — Determination of areas of substantial unemployment.
Citation: us:cfr:20:654.13
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-654.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 654 (Special Responsibilities Of The Employment Service System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An area of substantial unemployment, for purposes of Executive Order 10582, shall be any area classified as a labor surplus area at § 654.5 of this part pursuant to the procedures set forth at subpart A of this part.
