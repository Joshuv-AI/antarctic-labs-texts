Message by the President, Indian Society of Anaesthesiologists (ISA) National
Authors: Naveen Malhotra
Published: 2026

Respected Seniors and Dear ISAians,

Indian Journal of Anaesthesia (IJA) has steadily grown over the years and has become an important part of our academic life. It provides a platform for anaesthesiologists from across the country to share their research, clinical experiences, and ideas. The growth of the journal is also a reflection of the increasing academic contribution of our speciality.

The re-introduction of zonal supplements is a very welcome step. There is a great deal of good academic work being done in different parts of the country, often within individual institutions. A zonal supplement gives our colleagues an opportunity to bring some of this work together and share it with a wider audience. It also allows us to learn from the experiences and practices of colleagues working in different regions.

I would like to congratulate the editorial team, authors, and reviewers for the successful publication of the Northern Supplement, which was the first step in this initiative. I am happy to see the Central Supplement following it and carrying the initiative forward. The issue brings together contributions from the region and highlights the interest and effort being put into clinical work, research, and teaching by our colleagues.

These supplements will also give young anaesthesiologists and researchers an opportunity to come forward and share their work. Providing a platform to present and publish their ideas and research will help build confidence, encourage academic engagement, and strengthen the culture of research within our speciality.

My sincere thanks to the Editor-in-Chief of the IJA, the Zonal Editorial Board, reviewers, and all the contributors for their time and effort in putting this supplement together. Congratulations to the entire team, and my best wishes for the success of the Central Supplement. I hope the IJA continues to bring anaesthesiologists from across the country together and provides a platform to share their work and experiences.

You all are requested to actively promote ISA Panchrakshak (every anaesthesiologist trains five lay persons five life-saving skills), Breathe Green-Project Oxygen, Walk-esthesia, and ISA Wellness Car. Do contribute in establishing Centres of Excellence and join Special Interest Groups of CISA.

My best wishes to all the contributors and readers.
