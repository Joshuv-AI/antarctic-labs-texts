10 C.F.R. § 770.10 — When must a person or entity, who wishes to contest a DOE denial of request for indemnification of a claim, begin legal action?
Citation: us:cfr:10:770.10
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-770.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 770 (Transfer Of Real Property At Defense Nuclear Facilities For Economic Development). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If DOE denies the claim, DOE must provide the person or entity with a notice of final denial of the claim by DOE by certified or registered mail. The person or entity must begin legal action within six months after the date of mailing.
