10 C.F.R. § 436.40 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:10:436.40
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-436.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 436 (Federal Energy Management And Planning Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart provides guidance to promote the procurement of energy efficient products by Federal agencies and promote procurement practices which facilitate the procurement of energy efficient products, consistent with the requirements in section 553 of the National Energy Conservation Policy Act. (42 U.S.C. 8259b)
