49 C.F.R. § 564.3 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:49:564.3
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-564.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 564 (Replaceable Light Source And Sealed Beam Headlamp Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to replaceable light sources used as original equipment, and standardized sealed beam headlamp units used as original equipment and replacement equipment in motor vehicle headlighting systems.
