40 C.F.R. § 1030.5 — State standards and controls.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1030.5
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1030.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1030 (Control Of Greenhouse Gas Emissions From Engines Installed On Airplanes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No State or political subdivision of a State may adopt or attempt to enforce any airplane or aircraft engine standard with respect to emissions unless the standard is identical to a standard that applies to airplanes under this part.
