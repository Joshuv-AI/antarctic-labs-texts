18 C.F.R. § 1306.4 — Rent after acquisition.
Citation: us:cfr:18:1306.4
Creator: Tennessee Valley Authority
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-1306.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 1306 (Relocation Assistance And Real Property Acquisition Policies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If TVA rents real property acquired by it to the former owner or former tenant, the amount of rent shall not exceed the fair rental value on a short-term basis.
