17 C.F.R. § 249b.101 — b.101 Form TA-W, notice of withdrawal from registration as transfer agent.
Citation: us:cfr:17:249b.101
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-249b.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 249b (PART 249b—FURTHER FORMS, SECURITIES EXCHANGE ACT OF 1934). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This form shall be used for withdrawing, pursuant to section 17A of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, the registration of transfer agents registered with the Commission.
