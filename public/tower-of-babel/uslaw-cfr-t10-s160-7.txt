10 C.F.R. § 160.7 — Effective date of prohibition on designated locations.
Citation: us:cfr:10:160.7
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-160.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 160 (Trespassing On Commission Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The prohibitions in §§ 160.3 and 160.4 shall take effect as to any facility, installation or real property on publication in the Federal Register of the notice designating the facility, installation or real property and posting in accordance with § 160.6.
