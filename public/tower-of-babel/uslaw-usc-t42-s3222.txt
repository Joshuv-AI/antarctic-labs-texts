42 U.S.C. § 3222 — Savings clause
Citation: us:usc:42:3222
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section3222
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 38 (Public Works And Economic Development); Subchapter VI (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To the extent that any portion of grants made under this chapter are used for an economic development project that involves remediation, the remediation shall be conducted in compliance with all applicable Federal, State, and local laws and standards.
