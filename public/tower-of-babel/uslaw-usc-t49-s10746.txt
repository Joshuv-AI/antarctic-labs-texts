49 U.S.C. § 10746 — Demurrage charges
Citation: us:usc:49:10746
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section10746
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Rail); Chapter 107 (Rates); Subchapter III (Limitations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A rail carrier providing transportation subject to the jurisdiction of the Board under this part shall compute demurrage charges, and establish rules related to those charges, in a way that fulfills the national needs related to—
(1)
freight car use and distribution; and
(2)
maintenance of an adequate supply of freight cars to be available for transportation of property.
