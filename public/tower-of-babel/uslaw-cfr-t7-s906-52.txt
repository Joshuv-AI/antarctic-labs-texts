7 C.F.R. § 906.52 — Compliance.
Citation: us:cfr:7:906.52
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-906.52
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 906 (Oranges And Grapefruit Grown In Lower Rio Grande Valley In Texas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in this subpart, no handler shall handle fruit, the handling of which has been prohibited by the Secretary in accordance with provisions of this subpart, or the rules and regulations issued thereunder, and no handler shall handle fruit except in conformity to the provisions of this part.
