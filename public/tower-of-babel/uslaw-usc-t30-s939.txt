30 U.S.C. § 939 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:30:939
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section939
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 22 (Mine Safety And Health); Subchapter IV (Black Lung Benefits); Part C (Claims For Benefits After December 31, 1973). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary of Labor such sums as may be necessary to carry out his responsibilities under this subchapter. Such sums shall remain available until expended.
