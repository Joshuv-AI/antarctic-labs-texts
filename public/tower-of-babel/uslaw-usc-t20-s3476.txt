20 U.S.C. § 3476 — Regional and field offices
Citation: us:usc:20:3476
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section3476
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 48 (Department Of Education); Subchapter IV (Administrative Provisions); Part B (General Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to establish, alter, discontinue, or maintain such regional or other field offices as the Secretary may find necessary or appropriate to perform functions of the Secretary or the Department.
