On the semi-finite vector bundles with connection over Kähler manifolds
Sanjay Amrutiya, Indranil Biswas

Abstract
. Let X X be a compact connected Kähler manifold. We consider the category 𝒞 EC ​ ( X ) \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X) of flat holomorphic connections ( E , ∇ E ) (E,\,\nabla^{E}) over X X satisfying the condition that the underlying holomorphic vector bundle E E admits a filtration of holomorphic subbundles preserved by the connection ∇ E \nabla^{E} such that the monodromy of the induced connection on each successive quotient has finite image. The category 𝒞 EC ​ ( X ) \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X) , equipped with the neutral fiber functor that sends any object ( E , ∇ E ) (E,\,\nabla^{E}) to the fiber E x 0 E_{x_{0}} , where x 0 ∈ X x_{0}\,\in\,X is a fixed point, defines a neutral Tannakian category over ℂ \mathbb{C} . Let ϖ EC ​ ( X , x 0 ) \varpi^{\mathrm{EC}}(X,\,x_{0}) denote the affine group scheme corresponding to this neutral Tannakian category 𝒞 EC ​ ( X ) \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X) . Let π EN ​ ( X , x 0 ) \pi^{\mathrm{EN}}(X,\,x_{0}) be an extension of the Nori fundamental group scheme over ℂ \mathbb{C} [ 8 ] . We show that π EN ​ ( X , x 0 ) \pi^{\mathrm{EN}}(X,\,x_{0}) is a closed subgroup scheme of ϖ EC ​ ( X , x 0 ) \varpi^{\mathrm{EC}}(X,\,x_{0}) . Finally, we discuss an example illustrating that if X X is not Kähler, then the natural homomorphism π EN ​ ( X , x 0 ) ⟶ ϖ EC ​ ( X , x 0 ) \pi^{\mathrm{EN}}(X,\,x_{0})\,\longrightarrow\,\varpi^{\mathrm{EC}}(X,\,x_{0}) might fail to be an embedding.

1.
Introduction

Tannaka duality has played a key role in the development of various generalizations
of fundamental groups, designed to capture the geometry of schemes and complex
manifolds (see
[
3
]
for the Tannakian category). A seminal example of such a
construction is the foundational work of M. Nori
[
7
]
.

Recall that a vector bundle
E
over a scheme
X
is said to be finite if there are
two distinct polynomials
f
and
g
with non-negative integer coefficients such that

f(E)
is isomorphic to
g(E)
(see Definition
2.1
).
Over the field of complex numbers, it follows from
[
7
]
that a vector bundle

E
is finite if and only if it is given by a representation of the fundamental
group
\pi_{1}(X)
that factors through a finite group.

Let
X
be a compact connected Kähler manifold, and let
E
be a holomorphic vector
bundle over
X
. Using the results from
[
4
]
and
[
9
]
, it is shown in

[
1
]
that
E
admits a flat holomorphic connection with finite monodromy group
if and only if
E
is finite vector bundle over
X
.

Otabe
[
8
]
introduced a broader category than that of finite bundles in
characteristic zero. He calls a vector bundle
E
over
X
to be semi-finite
if it admits a filtration of subbundles such that each successive quotient is a finite bundle
(see Definition
2.2
). The category of semi-finite bundles over
X
,
equipped with the neutral fiber functor, forms a neutral Tannakian category over the base field
k
.
The corresponding affine group scheme is called the extended Nori fundamental group
scheme and it is denoted by
\pi^{\mathrm{EN}}(X,\,x)
.

C. Simpson
[
9
]
considered the category of all flat holomorphic connections

(E,\,\nabla^{E})
over a compact connected Kähler manifold
X
.
This category is equipped with the operators of direct sum, tensor product, and
dualization, and it is a neutral Tannakian category with the usual fiber functor given
by a base point
x_{0}
(see
[
9
, p. 70]
). The resulting fundamental group scheme
over
\mathbb{C}
is denoted by
\varpi(X,\,x_{0})
, which represents a pro-algebraic completion
of the topological fundamental group.

We consider the category
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
of all flat holomorphic connections
(E,\,\nabla^{E})
over

X
, where the underlying holomorphic vector bundle
E
admits a filtration of subbundles
preserved by the connection
\nabla^{E}
, such that the induced connection on each
successive quotient has finite monodromy (see Section
2
). The category

\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
, equipped with the neutral fiber functor that sends any object
(E,\,\nabla^{E})

to the fiber
E_{x_{0}}
, defines a neutral Tannakian category over
\mathbb{C}
.
Let
\varpi^{\mathrm{EC}}(X,\,x_{0})
denote the affine group scheme corresponding to
this Tannakian category. There is a natural faithfully flat homomorphism:

\varpi(X,\,x_{0})\ \longrightarrow\ \varpi^{\mathrm{EC}}(X,\,x_{0})

of affine group schemes over
\mathbb{C}
(see Theorem
2.9
).

Next, we define a forgetful functor

\xi\,:\,\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)\,\longrightarrow\,\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EN}}(X)

that sends any pair
(E,\,\nabla^{E})
to the underlying vector bundle
E
. This induces
a morphism of affine group schemes over
\mathbb{C}
:

\xi^{*}\ :\ \pi^{\mathrm{EN}}(X,\,x_{0})\ \longrightarrow\ \varpi^{\mathrm{EC}}(X,\,x_{0})\,.

We prove the following result (see Theorem
2.10
):

Theorem 1.1
.

The induced morphism

\xi^{*}\ :\ \pi^{{\rm EN}}(X,\,x_{0})\ \longrightarrow\ \varpi^{\rm EC}(X,\,x_{0})

is a closed immersion.

Finally, we discuss an example illustrating that if
X
is not Kähler, the above theorem
does not hold in general.

2.
Semi–finite bundles and flat connections

Let
X
be a compact connected Kähler manifold. The holomorphic tangent and cotangent bundles
of
X
will respectively be denoted by
TX
and
\Omega_{X}^{1}
.

Let
f(t)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}a_{k}t^{k}\,\in\,\mathbb{N}[t]
be a polynomial
whose coefficients are nonnegative integers. For any holomorphic vector bundle

E
over
X
, define the holomorphic vector bundle

f(E)\ :=\ \bigoplus_{k=0}^{n}(E^{\otimes k})^{\oplus a_{k}},

where
E^{\otimes 0}
denotes the trivial line bundle
{\mathcal{O}}_{X}
.

Definition 2.1

(
[
7
]
)
.

A holomorphic vector bundle
E
over
X
is said to be finite if there are two distinct polynomials
f
and
g
,
whose coefficients are nonnegative integers, such that
f(E)
is holomorphically isomorphic to
g(E)
.

A flat connection
\nabla
on a holomorphic vector bundle
E
on
X
is called a holomorphic flat connection
if the sheaf of flat sections of
E
for
\nabla
is contained in the sheaf of holomorphic sections.

A holomorphic vector bundle
E
on
X
is finite if and only if it admits a flat
holomorphic connection for which the monodromy group is finite
[
7
]
(see also

[
1
]
).

Definition 2.2

(
[
8
]
)
.

A holomorphic vector bundle
E
on
X
is called
semi–finite
if it has a filtration of holomorphic subbundles

E\,=\,E_{0}\,\supset\,E_{1}\,\supset\,E_{2}\,\supset\,\cdots\,\supset\,E_{n+1}\,=\,0

such that
E_{i}/E_{i+1}
is a finite bundle, for all
0\,\leq\,i\,\leq\,n
.

Semi–finite vector bundles form a Tannakian category
[
8
, Proposition 2.14]
.
Let
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EN}}(X)
be the Tannakian category of semi–finite vector
bundles over
X
. Fix a base point
x_{0}\,\in\,X
. Then, we get a neutral fiber functor on

\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EN}}(X)
that sends any
E
to its
fiber
E_{x_{0}}
. The corresponding affine group scheme is denoted by
\pi^{\mathrm{EN}}(X,\,x_{0})

(see
[
8
]
for more details).

2.1.
Flat connections

Let
\mathbf{Conn}(X)
be the category of all pairs
(E,\,\nabla^{E})
, where
E
is a
holomorphic vector bundle on
X
and
\nabla^{E}\,:\,E\,\longrightarrow\,E\otimes_{\mathcal{O}_{X}}\Omega_{X}^{1}
is a flat holomorphic connection on
E
.
A morphism
f\,:\,(E,\,\nabla^{E})\,\longrightarrow\,(F,\,\nabla^{F})
in
\mathbf{Conn}(X)

is a morphism of vector bundles
f_{0}\,:\,E\,\longrightarrow\,F
that intertwines
\nabla^{E}

and
\nabla^{F}
, meaning

\nabla^{F}\circ f_{0}\ =\ (f_{0}\otimes{\rm Id}_{\Omega_{X}^{1}})\circ\nabla^{E}.

One can perform the usual operations between the objects in
\mathbf{Conn}(X)
as follows:
Take two objects
(E,\,\nabla^{E})
and
(F,\,\nabla^{F})
in
\mathbf{Conn}(X)
.

•

Direct Sum:
\nabla^{E\oplus F}\,:\,E\oplus F\,\longrightarrow\,(E\oplus F)\otimes_{\mathcal{O}_{X}}\Omega_{X}^{1}
given by
\nabla^{E\oplus F}(s\oplus t)\,=\,\nabla^{E}(s)+\nabla^{F}(t)
, where

s
and
t
are locally defied holomorphic sections of
E
and
F
respectively.

•

Tensor Product:
\nabla^{E\otimes F}\,:\,E\otimes F\,\longrightarrow\,(E\otimes F)\otimes_{\mathcal{O}_{X}}\Omega_{X}^{1}
given by
\nabla^{E\otimes F}(s\otimes t)\,=\,\nabla^{E}(s)\otimes t+s\otimes\nabla^{F}(t)
.

•

Dual:
\nabla^{E^{*}}\,:\,E^{*}\,\longrightarrow\,E^{*}\otimes_{\mathcal{O}_{X}}\Omega_{X}^{1}
given by

\nabla^{E^{*}}(\sigma)(s)=d(\sigma(s))-\sigma(\nabla^{E}(s))
, where
\sigma
(respectively,
s
) is a locally
defined holomorphic section of
E^{*}
(respectively,
E
).

Note that the category
\mathbf{Conn}(X)
is an abelian
\mathbb{C}
-linear rigid tensor category with the
above operations. Fix a base point
x_{0}\,\in\,X
. The category
\mathbf{Conn}(X)
, equipped with the
faithful fiber functor
T_{x_{0}}\,:\,\mathbf{Conn}(X)\,\longrightarrow\,\mathrm{Vec}(\mathbb{C})

that sends any object
(E,\,\nabla^{E})
to the fiber
E_{x_{0}}
, defines a neutral Tannakian category
over
\mathbb{C}
. By Tannaka duality, we get a pro-algebraic affine group scheme, which we shall denote
by

(2.1)

\varpi(X,\,x_{0}).

See
[
9
]
for other equivalent description of
\varpi(X,\,x_{0})
.

Let
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
be the full subcategory of
\mathbf{Conn}(X)
consisting of all objects

(E,\,\nabla^{E})
satisfying the the following condition:

•

The holomorphic vector bundle
E
admits a filtration of holomorphic subbundles

E\,=\,E_{0}\,\supseteq\,E_{1}\,\supseteq E_{2}\,\supseteq\,\cdots\,\supseteq\,E_{n}\,\supseteq\,E_{n+1}\,=\,0

such that
\nabla^{E}(E_{i})\,\subseteq\,E_{i}\otimes_{\mathcal{O}_{X}}\Omega_{X}^{1}
for all

1\,\leq\,i\,\leq\,n
, and the flat holomorphic connection on each quotient

E_{i}/E_{i+1}
,
0\,\leq\,i\,\leq\,n
, induced by
\nabla^{E}
has finite monodromy group.

Lemma 2.3
.

Let
0\,\longrightarrow\,(E_{1},\,\nabla^{E_{1}})\,\stackrel{{\scriptstyle f}}{{\longrightarrow}}\,(E,\,\nabla^{E})\,\stackrel{{\scriptstyle g}}{{\longrightarrow}}\,(E_{2},\,\nabla^{E_{2}})\,\longrightarrow\,0

be an exact sequence in
\mathbf{Conn}(X)
, meaning the underlying sequence of holomorphic
vector bundles
is exact with
\nabla^{E}\big|_{E_{1}}\,=\,\nabla^{E_{1}}
and
\nabla^{E_{2}}
coincides
with the connection on
E_{2}
induced by
\nabla^{E}
.
If
(E_{1},\,\nabla^{E_{1}})
and
(E_{2},\nabla^{E_{2}})
are objects of
\mathrm{\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)}
,
then
(E,\,\nabla^{E})
is also an object of
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
.

Proof.

Assume that
(E_{1},\,\nabla^{E_{1}})
and
(E_{2},\,\nabla^{E_{2}})
are objects of
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
. Let

E_{1}\,=\,V_{0}\,\supseteq\,V_{1}\,\supseteq\,\cdots\,\supseteq\,V_{m}\,\supseteq\,V_{m+1}\,=\,0

be a filtration of holomorphic subbundles satisfying the condition in the definition of

\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
. Let

E_{2}\,=\,W_{0}\,\supseteq\,W_{1}\,\supseteq\,\cdots\,\supseteq\,W_{n}\,\supseteq\,W_{n+1}\,=\,0

be a filtration of holomorphic subbundles satisfying the condition in the definition of

\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
. Consider the following filtration of holomorphic subbundles of
E
:

E\,\supseteq\,g^{-1}_{0}(W_{1})\,\supseteq\,\cdots\,\supseteq\,g^{-1}_{0}(W_{n})\,\supseteq\,f_{0}(V_{1})\,\supseteq\,\cdots\,\supseteq\,f_{0}(V_{m})\,\supseteq\,0.

It clearly satisfies the condition in the definition of
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
. Hence,

(E,\,\nabla^{E})
is an object of
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
.
∎

Lemma 2.4
.

Let
f\,:\,(E,\,\nabla^{E})\,\longrightarrow\,(F,\,\nabla^{F})
be an epimorphism in

\mathbf{Conn}(X)
. If
(E,\,\nabla^{E})
is an object of
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
, then
(F,\,\nabla^{F})

is also an object of
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
.

Proof.

Since
(E,\,\nabla^{E})
is an object of
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
, there exists a filtration

(2.2)

E\,=\,E_{0}\,\supseteq\,E_{1}\,\supseteq\,E_{2}\,\supseteq\,\cdots\,\supseteq\,E_{n}\,\supseteq\,E_{n+1}\,=\,0

with
\nabla^{E}(E_{i})\,\subseteq\,E_{i}\otimes_{\mathcal{O}_{X}}\Omega_{X}^{1}
for all
1\,\leq\,i\,\leq\,n
, and the flat holomorphic connection on each quotient

E_{i}/E_{i+1}
,
0\,\leq\,i\,\leq\,n
, induced by
\nabla^{E}
has finite monodromy group.
Note that
\ker(f_{0})
equipped with the flat holomorphic connection
\nabla^{\ker(f_{0})}

induced by
\nabla^{E}
is an object of
\mathbf{Conn}(X)
. Since
f
is an epimorphism in

\mathbf{Conn}(X)
, the connection
\nabla^{F}
coincides with the connection on

E/\ker(f_{0})\,\simeq\,F
induced by
\nabla^{E}
. Therefore, the filtration (
2.2
)
yields a filtration

F\,=\,f_{0}(E_{0})\,\supseteq\,f_{0}(E_{1})\,\supseteq\,f_{0}(E_{2})\,\supseteq\,\cdots\,\supseteq\,f_{0}(E_{m})\,\supseteq\,f_{0}(E_{m+1})\,=\,0

with
\nabla^{F}(f_{0}(E_{i}))\,\subseteq\,f_{0}(E_{i})\otimes_{\mathcal{O}_{X}}\Omega_{X}^{1}
, and the flat
holomorphic connection on each quotient
f_{0}(E_{i})/f_{0}(E_{i+1})
,
0\,\leq\,i\,\leq\,m
, induced by
\nabla^{F}
has finite monodromy group.
∎

Lemma 2.5
.

For any object
(E,\,\nabla^{E})
of
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
, the dual vector bundle
E^{*}
equipped with the
connection induced by
\nabla^{E}
is also an object of
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
.

Proof.

Let

E\,=\,E_{0}\,\supseteq\,E_{1}\,\supseteq\,E_{2}\,\supseteq\,\cdots\,\supseteq\,E_{n}\,\supseteq\,E_{n+1}\,=\,0

be a filtration of holomorphic subbundles such that
\nabla^{E}(E_{i})\,\subseteq\,E_{i}\otimes_{\mathcal{O}_{X}}\Omega_{X}^{1}
for all
1\,\leq\,i\,\leq\,n
, and the flat holomorphic connection on each quotient

E_{i}/E_{i+1}
,
0\,\leq\,i\,\leq\,n
, induced by
\nabla^{E}
has finite monodromy group.
Consider the dual filtration of
E^{*}
given by the following surjections:

E^{*}\,=\,E^{*}_{0}\,\longrightarrow\,E^{*}_{1}\,\longrightarrow\,\cdots\,\longrightarrow\,E^{*}_{n}\,\longrightarrow\,E^{*}_{n+1}\,=\,0.

It is preserved by the connection on
E^{*}
induced by
\nabla^{E}
. The monodromy of
a dual connection is the dual representation of the fundamental group. Hence,
E^{*}
equipped
with the connection induced by
\nabla^{E}
is an object of
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
.
∎

Corollary 2.6
.

Let
f\,:\,(E,\,\nabla^{E})\,\longrightarrow\,(F,\,\nabla^{F})
be an epimorphism in

\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
. Then
\ker(f_{0})
equipped with the connection induced by
\nabla^{E}
is
also an object of
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
.

Proof.

Consider the dual
E^{*}\,\longrightarrow\,\ker(f_{0})^{*}
of the inclusion map

\ker(f_{0})\,\hookrightarrow\,E
. The connection of
E^{*}
induced by
\nabla^{E}
produces a
connection of
\ker(f_{0})^{*}
. Indeed, this follows immediately from the fact that the epimorphism

f_{0}
is connection preserving. The vector bundle
E^{*}
equipped with the connection induced by

\nabla^{E}
is an object of
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
(see Lemma
2.5
). Therefore, from Lemma
2.4
,
it follows that
\ker(f_{0})^{*}
equipped with the above connection is an object of
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
.
Hence, from Lemma
2.5
, it follows that
\ker(f_{0})
equipped with the connection induced
by
\nabla^{E}
is an object of
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
.
∎

Proposition 2.7
.

The full subcategory
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
of
\mathbf{Conn}(X)
is a
\mathbb{C}
-linear rigid tensor subcategory.

Proof.

We will show that
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
is a tensor category. Take two objects
(E,\,\nabla^{E})
and

(F,\,\nabla^{F})
in
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
. We need to show that
E\otimes F
together with the induced
connection
\nabla^{E\otimes F})
is an object of
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
.
Since
(E,\,\nabla^{E})
is an object in
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
, there exist a filtration

(2.3)

E\,=\,E_{0}\,\supseteq\,E_{1}\,\supseteq\,E_{2}\,\supseteq\,\cdots\,\supseteq\,E_{n}\,\supseteq\,E_{n+1}\,=\,0

with
\nabla^{E}(E_{i})\,\subseteq\,E_{i}\otimes_{\mathcal{O}_{X}}\Omega_{X}^{1}
, and the flat holomorphic
connection on each quotient
E_{i}/E_{i+1}
,
0\,\leq\,i\,\leq\,n
, induced by
\nabla^{E}

has finite monodromy group. Tensoring (
2.3
) with
F
, we get a filtration

E\otimes F\,=\,E_{0}\otimes F\,\supseteq\,E_{1}\otimes F\,\supseteq\,\cdots\,\supseteq\,E_{n}\otimes F\,\supseteq\,E_{n+1}\otimes F\,=\,0

with
(E_{i}\otimes F)/(E_{i+1}\otimes F)\,\simeq\,(E_{i}/E_{i+1})\otimes F
and it is compatible
with the induced connection
\nabla^{E\otimes F}
. By Lemma
2.3
, the
category
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
is closed under taking extension in
\mathbf{Conn}(X)
. Consequently, it follows
that
(E\otimes F,\,\nabla^{E\otimes F})
is an object of
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
.

In Lemma
2.5
, it was shown that
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
is closed under taking duals. This completes the proof.
∎

Proposition 2.8
.

The full subcategory
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
of
\mathbf{Conn}(X)
is an abelian subcategory.

Proof.

Let
f\,:\,(E,\,\nabla^{E})\longrightarrow(F,\,\nabla^{F})
be a morphism in
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
.
Then, the image of
f_{0}
, denoted by
\mathrm{Im}(f_{0})
, with the flat holomorphic connection

\nabla^{\mathrm{Im}(f_{0})}
induced by
\nabla^{F}
is a subobject of
(F,\,\nabla^{F})
in

\mathbf{Conn}(X)
. Hence, we have an epimorphism

(2.4)

f\,:\,(E,\,\nabla^{E})\longrightarrow(\mathrm{Im}(f_{0}),\,\nabla^{\mathrm{Im}(f_{0})})

in
\mathbf{Conn}(X)
. By Lemma
2.4
, it follows that

(\mathrm{Im}(f_{0}),\,\nabla^{\mathrm{Im}(f_{0})})
is an object of
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
. Again from
Lemma
2.4
, we can conclude that
\operatorname{coker}(f_{0})
equipped with the connection
induced by
\nabla^{F}
is an object of
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
.

Since
(\mathrm{Im}(f_{0}),\,\nabla^{\mathrm{Im}(f_{0})})
is an object of
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
,
the morphism in (
2.4
) is an epimorphism in
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
. Hence, using Corollary

2.6
, it follows that
\ker(f_{0})
equipped with the connection induced by
\nabla^{E}

is an object of
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
.
∎

Theorem 2.9
.

The category
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
, equipped with the faithful fiber functor
T_{x_{0}}
that sends any object

(E,\,\nabla^{E})
to the fiber
E_{x_{0}}
, defines a neutral Tannakian category
over
\mathbb{C}
. Denoting by
\varpi^{\mathrm{EC}}(X,\,x_{0})
the affine group scheme corresponding
to the neutral Tannakian category
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
, there is a natural faithfully flat homomorphism

\varpi(X,\,x_{0})\ \longrightarrow\ \varpi^{\mathrm{EC}}(X,\,x_{0})

of affine group schemes over
\mathbb{C}
(see (
2.1
) for
\varpi(X,\,x_{0})
).

Proof.

The theorem follows from Proposition
2.7
, Proposition
2.8

and
[
3
, Proposition 2.21]
.
∎

Let
\xi\,:\,\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)\,\longrightarrow\,\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EN}}(X)
be the forgetful functor
that sends any pair
(E,\,\nabla^{E})
to
E
. Then, we get an induced morphism

(2.5)

\xi^{*}\ :\ \pi^{\mathrm{EN}}(X,\,x_{0})\ \longrightarrow\ \varpi^{\mathrm{EC}}(X,\,x_{0})

of affine group scheme over
\mathbb{C}
.

Theorem 2.10
.

The morphism
\xi^{*}
in (
2.5
) of affine group schemes over
\mathbb{C}
is a closed immersion.

Proof.

Take a semi-finite vector bundle
E
over
X
. Let

(2.6)

E\,=\,E_{0}\,\supseteq\,E_{1}\,\supseteq\,E_{2}\,\supseteq\,\cdots\,\supseteq\,E_{\ell}\,\supseteq\,E_{\ell+1}\,=\,0

be a filtration of holomorphic subbundles such that the quotient
E_{i}/E_{i+1}
is a finite vector
bundle for all
0\,\leq\,i\,\leq\,\ell
. Therefore, each
E_{i}/E_{i+1}
admits a flat holomorphic
connection whose monodromy is a finite group
[
1
, Theorem 2.3]
. In particular,
E_{i}/E_{i+1}

is polystable and
c_{j}(E_{i}/E_{i+1})\,=\,0
for all
j\,\geq\,1
. In fact,
E_{i}/E_{i+1}
admits a
unique flat unitary connection and this connection has finite monodromy (see
[
1
]
).

Since each successive quotient
E_{i}/E_{i+1}
in (
2.6
) is polystable and
c_{j}(E_{i}/E_{i+1})\,=\,0

for all
j\,\geq\,1
, by
[
9
, Corollary 3.10 and its following remark]
, the vector bundle
E
admits
a unique flat holomorphic connection — denote it by
\nabla^{E}
— such that the following hold:

•

\nabla^{E}(E_{i})\,\subseteq\,E_{i}\otimes_{\mathcal{O}_{X}}\Omega_{X}^{1}
for every

1\,\leq\,i\,\leq\,\ell
, and

•

the flat holomorphic connection on
E_{i}/E_{i+1}
induced by
\nabla^{E}
coincides with the
unique unitary flat connection on
E_{i}/E_{i+1}
, for all
0\,\leq\,i\,\leq\,\ell
.

Hence
(E,\,\nabla^{E})
is an object of
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(X)
. The theorem follows from this, using

[
3
, Proposition 2.21]
.
∎

Example

Let
M
be a compact complex manifold. Then,
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EC}}(M)
and
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{EN}}(M)

can be defined as before, and both form neutral Tannakian categories, once a base point
x_{0}
is chosen. We have the homomorphism

\xi^{*}\,:\,\pi^{\mathrm{EN}}(M,\,x_{0})\,\longrightarrow\,\varpi^{\mathrm{EC}}(M,\,x_{0})

as in (
2.5
) constructed using the forgetful map
(E,\,\nabla^{E})\,\longmapsto\,E
.

However, if
M
is not Kähler, then Theorem
2.10
is not true in general.
To construct an example, let
T\,=\,\mathbb{C}/(\mathbb{Z}+\tau\mathbb{Z})
be an elliptic curve,
where
\tau\,\in\,\mathbb{C}
with
\mathrm{Im}(\tau)\,>\,0
. Take

M\,=\,\mathbb{S}^{2m+1}\times\mathbb{S}^{2n+1},\ \ \ m,\,n\,\geq\,1,

with a complex structure such that we have a
holomorphic principal
T
–bundle

M\ \longrightarrow\ \mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^{m}\times\mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^{n}

(see

5
]
for the construction of such a holomorphic principal
T
–bundle).

Then,
M
is not Kähler because
H^{2}(M,\,{\mathbb{R}})\,=\,0
.
It is known that
h^{1,0}(M)\,=\,1
(see
[
5
, p. 232]
or
[
2
]
).
Let
\eta
be a non-zero element in

. Then,
\eta
gives an

E

{\mathcal{O}}_{M}

.
Since

E

nontrivial flat holomorphic connection. This show that the morphism
\xi^{*}

M
