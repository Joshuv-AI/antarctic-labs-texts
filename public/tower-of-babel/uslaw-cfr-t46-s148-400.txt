46 C.F.R. § 148.400 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:46:148.400
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-148.400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 148 (Carriage Of Bulk Solid Materials That Require Special Handling). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless stated otherwise, the requirements of this subpart apply only to the shipment or loading of materials, listed in Table 148.10 of this part, for which Table 148.10 contains a reference to a section or paragraph of this subpart.
