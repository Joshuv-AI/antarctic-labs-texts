39 C.F.R. § 958.16 — Transcript of proceedings.
Citation: us:cfr:39:958.16
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-958.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 958 (Hazardous Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Testimony and argument at oral hearings shall be reported verbatim, unless the Presiding Officer orders otherwise. Transcripts or copies of the proceedings may be obtained by the parties at such rates as may be fixed by contract between the reporter and the Postal Service.
