10 U.S.C. § 929 — Art. 129. Burglary; unlawful entry
Citation: us:usc:10:929
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section929
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 47 (Uniform Code Of Military Justice); Subchapter X (Punitive Articles). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Burglary.—
Any person subject to this chapter who, with intent to commit an offense under this chapter, breaks and enters the building or structure of another shall be punished as a court-martial may direct.
(b) Unlawful Entry.—
Any person subject to this chapter who unlawfully enters—
(1)
the real property of another; or
(2)
the personal property of another which amounts to a structure usually used for habitation or storage;
shall be punished as a court-martial may direct.
