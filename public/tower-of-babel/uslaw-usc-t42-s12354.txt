42 U.S.C. § 12354 — Evaluation
Citation: us:usc:42:12354
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section12354
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 127 (Coordinated Services For Children, Youth, And Families); Subchapter I (Establishment Of Administration And Awarding Of Grants For Programs); Part C (National Clearinghouse). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commissioner shall, through grants or contracts awarded or entered into with independent auditors, conduct evaluations and related activities, of family resource and support programs, including—
(1)
evaluations of on-going programs;
(2)
process evaluations focusing on implementation strategies; and
(3)
the development of simple evaluation models for use by local family resource and support programs.
