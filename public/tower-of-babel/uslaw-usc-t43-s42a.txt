43 U.S.C. § 42a — Use of receipts from sale of maps for map printing and distribution
Citation: us:usc:43:42a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section42a
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 2 (United States Geological Survey). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In fiscal year 1984 and thereafter, all receipts from the sale of maps sold or stored by the United States Geological Survey shall be available for map printing and distribution to supplement funds otherwise available, to remain available until expended.
