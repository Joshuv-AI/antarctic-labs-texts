21 C.F.R. § 812.62 — IRB approval.
Citation: us:cfr:21:812.62
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-812.62
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 812 (Investigational Device Exemptions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An IRB shall review and have authority to approve, require modifications in (to secure approval), or disapprove all investigations covered by this part.
(b) If no IRB exists or if FDA finds that an IRB's review is inadequate, a sponsor may submit an application to FDA.
