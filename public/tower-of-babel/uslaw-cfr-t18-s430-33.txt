18 C.F.R. § 430.33 — Duration.
Citation: us:cfr:18:430.33
Creator: Delaware River Basin Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-430.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 430 (Ground Water Protection Area: Pennsylvania). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The delineation and declaration of the Southeastern Pennsylvania Ground Water Protected Area made pursuant to this regulation, and the requirements established hereby, shall continue until terminated by specific action of the Commission.
