49 C.F.R. § 625.3 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:49:625.3
Creator: Federal Transit Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-625.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 625 (Transit Asset Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to all recipients and subrecipients of Federal financial assistance under 49 U.S.C. Chapter 53 that own, operate, or manage capital assets used for providing public transportation.
