28 C.F.R. § 20.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:28:20.1
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-20.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 20 (Criminal Justice Information Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is the purpose of these regulations to assure that criminal history record information wherever it appears is collected, stored, and disseminated in a manner to ensure the accuracy, completeness, currency, integrity, and security of such information and to protect individual privacy.
