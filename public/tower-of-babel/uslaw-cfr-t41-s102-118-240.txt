41 C.F.R. § 102-118.240 — -118.240 Appeals of a CBCA audit decision.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-118.240
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-118.240
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-118 (118—Transportation Payment And Audit). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A ruling by the CBCA is the final administrative remedy available and the TSP has no statutory right of appeal. This subpart governs administrative actions only and does not affect any of the TSP's rights. A TSP may still pursue a legal remedy through the courts.
