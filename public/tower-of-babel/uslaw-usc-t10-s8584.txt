10 U.S.C. § 8584 — Age of retirement
Citation: us:usc:10:8584
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8584
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Education And Training); Chapter 857 (Retirement Of Civilian Members Of The Teaching Staffs Of The United States Naval Academy And United States Naval Postgraduate School). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A civilian member may be retired at any time after his sixty-fifth birthday, and shall be retired by June 30 following that birthday. However, in any special case the Secretary of the Navy may defer the retirement of a member until a date not later than the member’s seventieth birthday.
