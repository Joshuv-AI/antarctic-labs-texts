48 C.F.R. § 1808.870 — 1808.870 Contract clause.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1808 (Required Sources Of Supplies And Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 1852.208-81, Restrictions on Printing and Duplicating, in solicitations and contracts where there is a requirement for any printing, and/or any duplicating/copying in excess of that described in paragraph (c) of the clause.
