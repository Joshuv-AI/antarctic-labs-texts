20 C.F.R. § 702.811 — What weekly minimum rates apply to death benefits?
Citation: us:cfr:20:702.811
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-702.811
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 702 (Administration And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The average weekly wage used to compute death benefits is the greater of—
(1) The deceased employee's average weekly wages; or
(2) The national average weekly wage in effect at the time of the employee's death.
(b) The weekly minimum rate does not apply to death benefits.
