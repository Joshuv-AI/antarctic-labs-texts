11 U.S.C. § 1190 — Contents of plan
Citation: us:usc:11:1190
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section1190
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 11 (Reorganization); Subchapter V (Small Business Debtor Reorganization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A plan filed under this subchapter—
(1)
shall include—
(A)
a brief history of the business operations of the debtor;
(B)
a liquidation analysis; and
(C)
projections with respect to the ability of the debtor to make payments under the proposed plan of reorganization;
(2)
shall provide for the submission of all or such portion of the future earnings or other future income of the debtor to the supervision and control of the trustee as is necessary for the execution of the plan; and
(3)
notwithstanding section 1123(b)(5) of this title, may modify the rights of the holder of a claim secured only by a security interest in real property that is the principal residence of the debtor if the new value received in connection with the granting of the security interest was—
(A)
not used primarily to acquire the real property; and
(B)
used primarily in connection with the small business of the debtor.
