12 U.S.C. § 3025 — Examination and audit
Citation: us:usc:12:3025
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section3025
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 31 (National Consumer Cooperative Bank); Subchapter I (Establishment And Operation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Farm Credit Administration and the Government Accountability Office are hereby authorized and directed to examine and audit the Bank. Reports regarding such examinations and audits shall be promptly forwarded to both Houses of the Congress. The Bank shall reimburse the Farm Credit Administration for the costs of any examination or audit conducted by the Farm Credit Administration.
