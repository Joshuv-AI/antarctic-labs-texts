2 C.F.R. § 1329.300 — Whom in the Department of Commerce does a recipient who is an individual notify about a criminal drug conviction?
Citation: us:cfr:2:1329.300
Creator: Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1329.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1329 (Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A recipient who is an individual and is required under 2 CFR 182.300(b) to notify Federal agencies about a conviction for a criminal drug offense must notify each Department of Commerce office from which it currently has an award.
