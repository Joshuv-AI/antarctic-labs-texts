18 U.S.C. § 1367 — Interference with the operation of a satellite
Citation: us:usc:18:1367
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1367
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 65 (Malicious Mischief). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Whoever, without the authority of the satellite operator, intentionally or maliciously interferes with the authorized operation of a communications or weather satellite or obstructs or hinders any satellite transmission shall be fined in accordance with this title or imprisoned not more than ten years or both.
(b)
This section does not prohibit any lawfully authorized investigative, protective, or intelligence activity of a law enforcement agency or of an intelligence agency of the United States.
