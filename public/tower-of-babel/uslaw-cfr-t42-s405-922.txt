42 C.F.R. § 405.922 — Time frame for processing initial determinations.
Citation: us:cfr:42:405.922
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-405.922
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 405 (Federal Health Insurance For The Aged And Disabled). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contractor issues initial determinations on clean claims within 30 calendar days of receipt if they are submitted by or on behalf of the beneficiary who received the items and/or services; otherwise, interest must be paid at the rate specified at 31 U.S.C. 3902(a) for the period beginning on the day after the required payment date and ending on the date payment is made.
