26 U.S.C. § 6064 — Signature presumed authentic
Citation: us:usc:26:6064
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section6064
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 61 (Information And Returns); Subchapter A (Returns And Records); Part IV (Signing And Verifying Of Returns And Other Documents). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The fact that an individual’s name is signed to a return, statement, or other document shall be prima facie evidence for all purposes that the return, statement, or other document was actually signed by him.
