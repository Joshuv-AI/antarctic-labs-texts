Correction: Kawai et al. Effect of Isoflavone on Muscle Atrophy in Ovariectomized Mice. Nutrients 2024, 16, 3295
Authors: Sayaka Kawai, Takuro Okamura, Chihiro Munekawa, Yuka Hasegawa, Ayaka Kobayashi, Hanako Nakajima, Saori Majima, Naoko Nakanishi, Ryoichi Sasano, Masahide Hamaguchi, Michiaki Fukui
Published: 2026

Error in Figure

In the original publication [1], there was a mistake in Figure 2 as published. Specifically, the GAPDH panel in Figure 2f was incorrect. In addition, the original raw image corresponding to the published GAPDH blot could not be retrieved. Accordingly, Figure 2 has been replaced with a corrected version, in which panel (f) has been updated using Western blot images obtained from a repeated experiment performed with the same stored frozen samples, together with updated quantification. The authors state that the scientific conclusions are unaffected. This correction was approved by the Academic Editor. The original publication has also been updated.
