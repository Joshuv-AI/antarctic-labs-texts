34 U.S.C. § 50111 — Issuance of rules
Citation: us:usc:34:50111
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section50111
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 501 (Emergency Federal Law Enforcement Assistance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General, after consultation with appropriate members of the law enforcement community and with State and local officials, shall issue rules to carry out this chapter.
