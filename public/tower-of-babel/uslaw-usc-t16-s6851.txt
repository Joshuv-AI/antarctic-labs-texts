16 U.S.C. § 6851 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:16:6851
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section6851
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 87A (Modernizing Access To Public Land). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter:
(1) Federal land
The term “Federal land” means any land managed by a Federal land management agency.
(2) Federal land management agency
The term “Federal land management agency” means—
(A)
the Bureau of Reclamation;
(B)
the National Park Service;
(C)
the Bureau of Land Management;
(D)
the United States Fish and Wildlife Service;
(E)
the Forest Service; and
(F)
the Corps of Engineers.
(3) Road or trail
The term “road or trail” means a road or trail designated by 1 or more of the Secretaries for public use.
(4) Secretaries
The term “Secretaries” means—
(A)
the Secretary of Agriculture, acting through the Chief of the Forest Service;
(B)
the Secretary of the Interior; and
(C)
the Secretary of the Army, acting through the Assistant Secretary of the Army for Civil Works.
