20 C.F.R. § 416.982 — Blindness under a State plan.
Citation: us:cfr:20:416.982
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-416.982
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 416 (Supplemental Security Income For The Aged, Blind, And Disabled). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We shall also consider you blind for the purposes of payment of supplemental security income benefits if—
(a) You were found to be blind as defined under a State plan approved under title X or title XVI of the Social Security Act, as in effect for October 1972;
(b) You received aid under the State plan because of your blindness for the month of December 1973; and
(c) You continue to be blind as defined under the State plan.
