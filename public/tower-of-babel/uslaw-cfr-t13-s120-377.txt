13 C.F.R. § 120.377 — Use of proceeds.
Citation: us:cfr:13:120.377
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-120.377
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 120 (Business Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The loan proceeds shall not be used for debt refinancing. Only a manufacturing concern may use loan proceeds for working capital.
