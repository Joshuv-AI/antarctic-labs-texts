50 C.F.R. § 27.61 — Destruction or removal of property.
Citation: us:cfr:50:27.61
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-27.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 27 (Prohibited Acts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The destruction, injury, defacement, disturbance, or the unauthorized removal of any public property including natural objects or private property on or from any national wildlife refuge is prohibited.
