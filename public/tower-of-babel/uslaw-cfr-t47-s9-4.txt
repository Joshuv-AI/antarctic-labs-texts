47 C.F.R. § 9.4 — Obligation to transmit 911 calls.
Citation: us:cfr:47:9.4
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-9.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 9 (911 Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All telecommunications carriers shall transmit all 911 calls to a PSAP, to a designated statewide default answering point, or to an appropriate local emergency authority as set forth in § 9.5.
