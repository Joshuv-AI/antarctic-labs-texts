5 C.F.R. § 213.3199 — Temporary organizations.
Citation: us:cfr:5:213.3199
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-213.3199
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 213 (Excepted Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Positions on the staffs of temporary organizations, as defined in 5 U.S.C. 3161(a). Appointments may not exceed 3 years, but temporary organizations may extend the appointments for 2 additional years if the conditions for extension are related to the completion of the study or project.
