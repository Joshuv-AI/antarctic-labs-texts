40 U.S.C. § 3117 — Exclusion of certain property by stipulation of Attorney General
Citation: us:usc:40:3117
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section3117
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part A (General); Chapter 31 (General); Subchapter II (Acquiring Land). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In any condemnation proceeding brought by or on behalf of the Federal Government, the Attorney General may stipulate or agree on behalf of the Government to exclude any part of the property, or any interest in the property, taken by or on behalf of the Government by a declaration of taking or otherwise.
