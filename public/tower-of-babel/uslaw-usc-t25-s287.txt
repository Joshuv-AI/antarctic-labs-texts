25 U.S.C. § 287 — Taking child to school in another State without written consent
Citation: us:usc:25:287
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section287
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 7 (Education Of Indians). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No Indian child shall be taken from any school in any State or Territory to a school in any other State against its will or without the written consent of its parents.
