46 C.F.R. § 111.33-11 — -11 Propulsion systems.
Citation: us:cfr:46:111.33-11
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-111.33-11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 111 (Electric Systems—General Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each power semiconductor converter system in a propulsion system must meet sections 4-8-5/5.17.8 and 4-8-5/5.17.9 of ABS Marine Vessel Rules (incorporated by reference; see § 110.10-1 of this subchapter), except that each one for mobile offshore drilling units must meet the requirements in section 6-1-7/12 of ABS MOU Rules (incorporated by reference; see § 110.10-1 of this subchapter).
