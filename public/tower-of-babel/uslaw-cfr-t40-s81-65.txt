40 C.F.R. § 81.65 — Joplin (Missouri)-Northeast Oklahoma Interstate Air Quality Control Region.

Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-81.65
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 81 (Designation Of Areas For Air Quality Planning Purposes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Joplin (Missouri)-Northeast Oklahoma Interstate Air Quality Control Region, designated on December 8, 1970, and consisting of the counties of Barton, Jasper, McDonald, and Newton in the State of Missouri and Craig, Delaware, and Ottawa in the State of Oklahoma, is revoked effective upon publication.
