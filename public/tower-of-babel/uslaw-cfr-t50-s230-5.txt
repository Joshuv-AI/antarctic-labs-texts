50 C.F.R. § 230.5 — Licenses for aboriginal subsistence whaling.
Citation: us:cfr:50:230.5
Creator: National Marine Fisheries Service, National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-230.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 230 (Whaling Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A license is hereby issued to whaling captains identified by the relevant Native American whaling organization.
(b) The Assistant Administrator may suspend the license of any whaling captain who fails to comply with the regulations in this part.
