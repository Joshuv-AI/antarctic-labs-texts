32 C.F.R. § 1609.1 — Uncompensated positions.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1609.1
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1609.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1609 (Uncompensated Personnel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Members of local boards, district appeal boards, and all other persons volunteering their services to assist in the administration of the Selective Service Law shall be uncompensated. No person serving without compensation shall accept remuneration from any source for services rendered in connection with Selective Service matters.
