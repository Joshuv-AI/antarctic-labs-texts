22 C.F.R. § 513.420 — Scope of suspension.
Citation: us:cfr:22:513.420
Creator: United States Agency For Global Media
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-513.420
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 513 (Government Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement) And Governmentwide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Grants)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The scope of a suspension is the same as the scope of a debarment (see § 513.325), except that the procedures of §§ 513.410 through 513.413 shall be used in imposing a suspension.
