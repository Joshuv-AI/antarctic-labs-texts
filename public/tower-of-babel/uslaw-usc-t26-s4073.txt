26 U.S.C. § 4073 — Exemptions
Citation: us:usc:26:4073
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section4073
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 32 (Manufacturers Excise Taxes); Subchapter A (Automotive And Related Items); Part II (Tires). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The tax imposed by section 4071 shall not apply to tires sold for the exclusive use of the Department of Defense or the Coast Guard.
