40 C.F.R. § 52.1395 — Smoke management plan.
Citation: us:cfr:40:52.1395
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-52.1395
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 52 (Approval And Promulgation Of Implementation Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department considers smoke management techniques for agriculture and forestry management burning purposes as set forth in 40 CFR 51.308(d)(3)(v)(E). The Department considers the visibility impact of smoke when developing, issuing, or conditioning permits and when making dispersion forecast recommendations through the implementation of Title 17, Chapter 8, subchapter 6, ARM, Open Burning.
