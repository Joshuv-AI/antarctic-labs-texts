48 C.F.R. § 32.608-1 — 32.608-1 Interest charges.
Citation: us:cfr:48:32.608-1
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-32.608-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 32 (Contract Financing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless specified otherwise in the clause at 52.232-17, Interest, interest charges shall apply to any contract debt unpaid after 30 days from the issuance of a demand unless—
(a) The contract is a kind excluded under 32.611; or
(b) The contract or debt has been exempted from interest charges under agency procedures.
