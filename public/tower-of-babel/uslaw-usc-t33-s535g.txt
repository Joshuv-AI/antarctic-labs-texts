33 U.S.C. § 535g — Federal navigable waters and commerce jurisdiction unaffected
Citation: us:usc:33:535g
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section535g
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 11 (Bridges Over Navigable Waters); Subchapter IV (International Bridges). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subchapter shall be construed to affect, impair, or diminish any right, power, or jurisdiction of the United States over or in regard to any navigable water or any interstate or foreign commerce.
