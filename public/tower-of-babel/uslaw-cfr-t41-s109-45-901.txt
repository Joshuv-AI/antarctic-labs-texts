41 C.F.R. § 109-45.901 — -45.901 Authority to abandon or destroy.
Citation: us:cfr:41:109-45.901
Creator: Chapter 109—Department Of Energy Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-109-45.901
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 109-45 (45—Sale, Abandonment, Or Destruction Of Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Personal property in the possession of DOE offices or designated contractors may be abandoned or destroyed provided that a written determination has been made by the OPMO/PA that property has no commercial value or the estimated cost of its continued care and handling would exceed the estimated proceeds from its sale.
