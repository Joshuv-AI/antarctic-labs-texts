29 C.F.R. § 452.133 — Election of delegates not members of the labor organization.
Citation: us:cfr:29:452.133
Creator: Office Of Labor-Management Standards, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-452.133
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 452 (General Statement Concerning The Election Provisions Of The Labor-Management Reporting And Disclosure Act Of 1959). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A labor organization's constitution and bylaws may authorize the election of delegates who are not members of the subordinate labor organization they represent, provided the members of the subordinate organization are also eligible to be candidates.
