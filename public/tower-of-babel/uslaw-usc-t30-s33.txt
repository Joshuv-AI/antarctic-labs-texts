30 U.S.C. § 33 — Existing rights
Citation: us:usc:30:33
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section33
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 2 (Mineral Lands And Regulations In General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All patents for mining claims upon veins or lodes issued prior to May 10, 1872, shall convey all the rights and privileges conferred by sections 21, 22 to 24, 26 to 28, 29, 30, 33 to 48, 50 to 52, 71 to 76 of this title and section 661 of title 43 where no adverse rights existed on the 10th day of May, 1872.
