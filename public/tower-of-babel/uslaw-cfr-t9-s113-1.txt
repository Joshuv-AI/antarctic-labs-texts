9 C.F.R. § 113.1 — Compliance.
Citation: us:cfr:9:113.1
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-113.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 113 (Standard Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part apply to each serial or subserial of a licensed biological product manufactured in a licensed establishment and to each serial or subserial of a biological product in each shipment imported for distribution and sale.
