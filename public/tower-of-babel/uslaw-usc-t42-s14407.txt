42 U.S.C. § 14407 — Application to District of Columbia
Citation: us:usc:42:14407
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section14407
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 138 (Assisted Suicide Funding Restriction). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of this chapter, the term “funds appropriated by Congress” includes funds appropriated to the District of Columbia pursuant to an authorization of appropriations under title V of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act and the term “Federal government” includes the government of the District of Columbia.
