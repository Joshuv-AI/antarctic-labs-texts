42 C.F.R. § 51c.401 — c.401 Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:42:51c.401
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-51c.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 51c (PART 51c—GRANTS FOR COMMUNITY HEALTH SERVICES). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations of this subpart, in addition to the regulations of subpart A are applicable to grants awarded pursuant to section 330(d)(1)(B) of the Act for the costs of operation of projects which provide health services to medically underserved populations.
