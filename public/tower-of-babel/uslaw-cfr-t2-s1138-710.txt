2 C.F.R. § 1138.710 — Financial reporting requirements for subawards.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1138.710
Creator: Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1138.710
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1138 (Requirements Related To Subawards: General Award Terms And Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Requirement. A DoD Component's general terms and conditions must specify financial reporting requirements for subawards.
(b) Award terms and conditions. A DoD Component's general terms and conditions must use the wording appendix G to this part provides for Section C of SUB Article VII to specify the financial reporting requirements that recipients must include in their subawards.
