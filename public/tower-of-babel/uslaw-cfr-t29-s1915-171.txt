29 C.F.R. § 1915.171 — Scope and application of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1915.171
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1915.171
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1915 (Occupational Safety And Health Standards For Shipyard Employment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The standards contained in this subpart shall apply to ship repairing and shipbuilding and shall not apply to shipbreaking.
