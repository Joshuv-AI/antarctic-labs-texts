10 U.S.C. § 8777 — Quarters: Nurse Corps officers; assignment in hospitals
Citation: us:usc:10:8777
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8777
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 877 (Quarters, Utilities, And Services). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Under such regulations as the Secretary of the Navy prescribes, officers in the Nurse Corps may be assigned quarters in naval hospitals.
