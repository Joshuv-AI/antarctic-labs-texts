7 C.F.R. § 2100.001 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:7:2100.001
Creator: Office Of Energy And Environmental Policy, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-2100.001
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 2100 (Technical Guidelines For The Production Of Regenerative Agricultural Biofuel Feedstocks). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to establish technical guidelines for quantifying, reporting, and verifying the carbon intensity of agricultural biofuel feedstock commodity crops grown in the United States.
