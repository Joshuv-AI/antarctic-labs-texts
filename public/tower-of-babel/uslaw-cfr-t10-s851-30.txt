10 C.F.R. § 851.30 — Consideration of variances.
Citation: us:cfr:10:851.30
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-851.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 851 (Worker Safety And Health Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Variances shall be granted by the Under Secretary after considering the recommendation of the EHSS Director. The authority to grant a variance cannot be delegated.
(b) The application must satisfy the requirements for applications specified in § 851.31.
