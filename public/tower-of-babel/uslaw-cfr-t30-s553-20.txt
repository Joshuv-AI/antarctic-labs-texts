30 C.F.R. § 553.20 — What methods may I use to demonstrate OSFR?
Citation: us:cfr:30:553.20
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-553.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 553 (Oil Spill Financial Responsibility For Offshore Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As the designated applicant, you may satisfy your OSFR requirements by using one or a combination of the following methods to demonstrate OSFR:
(a) Self-insurance under §§ 553.21 through 553.28;
(b) Insurance under § 553.29;
(c) An indemnity under § 553.30;
(d) A surety bond under § 553.31; or
(e) An alternative method the Director approves under § 553.32.
