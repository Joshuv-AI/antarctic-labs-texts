29 C.F.R. § 1691.2 — Exchange of information.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1691.2
Creator: Equal Employment Opportunity Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1691.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1691 (Procedures For Complaints Of Employment Discrimination Filed Against Recipients Of Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

EEOC and agencies shall share any information relating to the employment policies and practices of recipients of Federal financial assistance that may assist each office in carrying out its responsibilities. Such information shall include, but not necessarily be limited to, affirmative action programs, annual employment reports, complaints, investigative files, conciliation or compliance agreements, and compliance review reports and files.
