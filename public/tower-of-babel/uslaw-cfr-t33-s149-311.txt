33 C.F.R. § 149.311 — What are the launching and recovery requirements for lifeboats?
Citation: us:cfr:33:149.311
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-149.311
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 149 (Deepwater Ports: Design, Construction, And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each lifeboat launched by falls must have a launching and recovery system that complies with 46 CFR 108.555.
(b) Each free-fall lifeboat must have a launching and recovery system that complies with 46 CFR 108.557.
