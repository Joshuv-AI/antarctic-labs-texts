14 C.F.R. § 29.605 — Fabrication methods.
Citation: us:cfr:14:29.605
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-29.605
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 29 (Airworthiness Standards: Transport Category Rotorcraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The methods of fabrication used must produce consistently sound structures. If a fabrication process (such as gluing, spot welding, or heat-treating) requires close control to reach this objective, the process must be performed according to an approved process specification.
(b) Each new aircraft fabrication method must be substantiated by a test program.
