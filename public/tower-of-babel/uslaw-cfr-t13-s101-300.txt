13 C.F.R. § 101.300 — What is the Inspector General's authority to conduct audits, investigations, and inspections?
Citation: us:cfr:13:101.300
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-101.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 101 (Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Inspector General Act of 1978, as amended (5 U.S.C. App. 3) authorizes SBA's Inspector General to provide policy direction for, and to conduct, supervise, and coordinate such audits, investigations, and inspections relating to the programs and operations of SBA as appears necessary or desirable.
