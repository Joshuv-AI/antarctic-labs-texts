7 U.S.C. § 328 — Power to amend, repeal, etc., reserved
Citation: us:usc:7:328
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section328
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 13 (Agricultural And Mechanical Colleges); Subchapter II (College-Aid Annual Appropriation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Congress may at any time amend, suspend, or repeal any or all of the provisions of this subchapter.
