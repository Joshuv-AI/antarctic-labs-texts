49 C.F.R. § 392.71 — Radar detectors; use and/or possession.
Citation: us:cfr:49:392.71
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-392.71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 392 (Driving Of Commercial Motor Vehicles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) No driver shall use a radar detector in a commercial motor vehicle, or operate a commercial motor vehicle that is equipped with or contains any radar detector.
(b) No motor carrier shall require or permit a driver to violate paragraph (a) of this section.
