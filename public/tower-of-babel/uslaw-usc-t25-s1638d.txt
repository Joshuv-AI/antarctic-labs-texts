25 U.S.C. § 1638d — Credit to appropriations of money collected for meals at Indian Health Service facilities
Citation: us:usc:25:1638d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section1638d
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 18 (Indian Health Care); Subchapter III (Health Facilities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Money before, on, and after September 30, 1994, collected for meals served at Indian Health Service facilities will be credited to the appropriations from which the services were furnished and shall be credited to the appropriation when received.
