43 C.F.R. § 4.416 — Appeals of wildfire management decisions.
Citation: us:cfr:43:4.416
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4.416
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4 (Department Of The Interior Hearings And Appeals Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board must decide an appeal of a BLM decision under 43 CFR 4190.1 and 5003.1(b) within 180 days after the notice of appeal was filed. The Board may issue an expedited briefing schedule to meet this deadline. If the Board does not rule on the appeal within 180 days after the notice of appeal was filed, BLM's decision will be deemed final for the Department.
