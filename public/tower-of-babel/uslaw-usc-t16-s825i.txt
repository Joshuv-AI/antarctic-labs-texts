16 U.S.C. § 825i — Appointment of officers and employees; compensation
Citation: us:usc:16:825i
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section825i
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 12 (Federal Regulation And Development Of Power); Subchapter III (Licensees And Public Utilities; Procedural And Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commission is authorized to appoint and fix the compensation of such officers, attorneys, examiners, and experts as may be necessary for carrying out its functions under this chapter; and the Commission may, subject to civil-service laws, appoint such other officers and employees as are necessary for carrying out such functions and fix their salaries in accordance with chapter 51 and subchapter III of chapter 53 of title 5.
