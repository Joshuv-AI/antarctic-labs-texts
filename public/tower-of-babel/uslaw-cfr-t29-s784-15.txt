29 C.F.R. § 784.15 — “State.”
Citation: us:cfr:29:784.15
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-784.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 784 (Provisions Of The Fair Labor Standards Act Applicable To Fishing And Operations On Aquatic Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in the Act, “State” means “any State of the United States or the District of Columbia or any Territory or possession of the United States” (Act, section 3(c)). The application of this definition in determining questions of “coverage under the Act's definition of “commerce” and “produced” (see §§ 784.12, 784.13) is discussed in part 776 of this chapter, dealing with general coverage.
