MORA, JOSÉ (1638–1725), Spanish sculptor, was a pupil of Alonzo Cano. He died in Granada in 1725 and was buried in

the Albaicin church. His work can be usefully studied in the
eight statues in the Capella del Cardenal in the Cordova Cathedral
and in the figures of St Bruno and St Joseph in the Cartuja
near Granada.

See B. Haendeke, Studien zur Geschichte der spanischen Plastik (Strassburg, 1900).
