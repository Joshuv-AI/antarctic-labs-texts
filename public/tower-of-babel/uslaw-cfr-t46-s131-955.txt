46 C.F.R. § 131.955 — Display of merchant mariner credential.
Citation: us:cfr:46:131.955
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-131.955
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 131 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each officer on a vessel must conspicuously display his or her license or officer endorsements as required by 46 U.S.C. 7110.
