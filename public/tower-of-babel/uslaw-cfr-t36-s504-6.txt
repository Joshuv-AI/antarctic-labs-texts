36 C.F.R. § 504.6 — Gambling.
Citation: us:cfr:36:504.6
Creator: Smithsonian Institution
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-504.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 504 (Rules And Regulations Governing Smithsonian Institution Buildings And Grounds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Participating in games for money or other personal property or the operation of gambling devices, the conduct of a lottery or pool, or the selling or purchasing of numbers tickets in or on the premises is prohibited.
