25 C.F.R. § 2.712 — May the AS-IA take jurisdiction over the appeal?
Citation: us:cfr:25:2.712
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-2.712
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 2 (Appeals From Administrative Decisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. The AS-IA may take jurisdiction over the appeal at any time before the reviewing official issues a final decision.
