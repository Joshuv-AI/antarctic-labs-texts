46 C.F.R. § 327.8 — Court action.
Citation: us:cfr:46:327.8
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-327.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 327 (Seamen'S Claims; Administrative Action And Litigation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No seamen, their surviving dependents and beneficiaries, or their legal representatives may institute a court action unless such claim has been prepared and filed in accordance with §§ 327.4 and 327.5 and was administratively disallowed in accordance with § 327.6 or § 327.7.
