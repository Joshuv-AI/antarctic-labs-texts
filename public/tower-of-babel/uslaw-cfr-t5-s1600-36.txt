5 C.F.R. § 1600.36 — Matching contributions.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1600.36
Creator: Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1600.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1600 (Employee Contribution Elections, Investment Elections, And Automatic Enrollment Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A participant is not entitled to keep the matching contributions and their associated earnings that are attributable to refunded default employee contributions.
(b) The matching contributions and associated earnings attributable to refunded default employee contributions shall be forfeited to the TSP and used to offset administrative expenses.
