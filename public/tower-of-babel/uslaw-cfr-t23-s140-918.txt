23 C.F.R. § 140.918 — Maintenance and extended construction.
Citation: us:cfr:23:140.918
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-140.918
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 140 (Reimbursement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The cost of maintenance and extended construction is reimbursable to the extent provided for in 23 CFR 646.216(f)(4), and where included in the State-Railroad Agreement or otherwise approved by the State and FHWA.
