7 C.F.R. § 1404.3 — Payments which may be assigned.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1404.3
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1404.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1404 (Assignment Of Payments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise provided in this part or in individual program regulations, contracts and agreements entered into by FSA or CCC, any payment due a person from FSA or CCC may be assigned.
