5 C.F.R. § 7101.103 — Standard for accomplishing disqualification; disqualifying financial interest.
Citation: us:cfr:5:7101.103
Creator: National Labor Relations Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-7101.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 7101 (Supplemental Standards Of Ethical Conduct For Employees Of The National Labor Relations Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An NLRB employee who is required, in accordance with 5 CFR 2635.402(c), to disqualify himself from participation in a particular matter to which he has been assigned shall, notwithstanding the guidance in 5 CFR 2635.402(c) (1) and (2), provide written notice of disqualification to his or her supervisor upon determining that he or she will not participate in the matter.
