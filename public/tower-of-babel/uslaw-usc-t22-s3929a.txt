22 U.S.C. § 3929a — Abolishment of Inspector General of Department of State and Foreign Service
Citation: us:usc:22:3929a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section3929a
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 52 (Foreign Service); Subchapter II (Management Of Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding section 3929 of this title, the Inspector General of the Department of State and the Foreign Service is hereby abolished.
