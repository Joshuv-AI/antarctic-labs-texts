24 C.F.R. § 41.2 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:24:41.2
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-41.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 41 (Policies And Procedures For The Enforcement Of Standards And Requirements For Accessibility By The Physically Handicapped). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in this part, the term Secretary means the Secretary of Housing and Urban Development, or to the extent of any delegation of authority by the Secretary to act under this part, any other Department Official to whom authority has been delegated.
