42 U.S.C. § 1437aaa–6 — Relationship to other homeownership opportunities
Citation: us:usc:42:1437aaa–6
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1437aaa–6
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 8 (Low-Income Housing); Subchapter II–A (Hope For Public Housing Homeownership). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The program authorized under this subchapter shall be in addition to any other public housing homeownership and management opportunities, including opportunities under section 1437c(h) 1 of this title.
