47 C.F.R. § 1.423 — Oral argument and other proceedings.
Citation: us:cfr:47:1.423
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-1.423
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 1 (Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In any rulemaking where the Commission determines that an oral argument, hearing or any other type of proceeding is warranted, notice of the time, place and nature of such proceeding will be published in the Federal Register.
