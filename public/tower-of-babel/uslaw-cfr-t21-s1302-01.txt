21 C.F.R. § 1302.01 — Scope of part 1302.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1302.01
Creator: Drug Enforcement Administration, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1302.01
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1302 (Labeling And Packaging Requirements For Controlled Substances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Requirements governing the labeling and packaging of controlled substances pursuant to sections 1305 and 1008(d) of the Act (21 U.S.C. 825 and 958(d)) are set forth generally by those sections and specifically by the sections of this part.
