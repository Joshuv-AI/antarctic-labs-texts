20 U.S.C. § 9532 — Commissioner for Education Research
Citation: us:usc:20:9532
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section9532
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 76 (Education Research, Statistics, Evaluation, Information, And Dissemination); Subchapter I (Education Sciences Reform); Part B (National Center For Education Research). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Research Center shall be headed by a Commissioner for Education Research (in this part referred to as the “Research Commissioner”) who shall have substantial knowledge of the activities of the Research Center, including a high level of expertise in the fields of research and research management.
