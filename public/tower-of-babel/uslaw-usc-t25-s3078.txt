25 U.S.C. § 3078 — Regulations
Citation: us:usc:25:3078
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section3078
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 32B (Safeguard Tribal Objects Of Patrimony). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 1 year after December 21, 2022, the Secretary, in consultation with the Secretary of State, the Secretary of Homeland Security, and the Attorney General, and after consultation with Indian Tribes and Native Hawaiian organizations, shall promulgate rules and regulations to carry out this chapter.
