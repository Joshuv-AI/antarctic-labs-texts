40 C.F.R. § 791.85 — Availability of final Agency order.
Citation: us:cfr:40:791.85
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-791.85
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 791 (Data Reimbursement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The final Agency order shall be available to the public for inspection and copying pursuant to 5 U.S.C. 552(a)(2), subject to necessary confidentiality restrictions.
