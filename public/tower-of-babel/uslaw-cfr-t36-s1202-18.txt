36 C.F.R. § 1202.18 — How does NARA collect information about individuals?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1202.18
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1202.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1202 (Regulations Implementing The Privacy Act Of 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any information that is used in making a determination about your rights, benefits, or privileges under NARA programs is collected directly from you—the subject individual—to the greatest extent possible.
