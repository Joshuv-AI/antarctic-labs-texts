The mRNA and protein datasets after cold stress of red tilapia
Authors: Lanmei Wang, Haoran Yang, Herbert Brightmore Munyaradzia, Wenbin Zhu, Zai-jie Dong
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
The cold stress during overwintering is considered the bottleneck of red tilapia industry. In this study, the water temperature (WT) was reduced by 2 °C per day from 20 °C to 8 °C in the cold (C) group. Then transcriptome of brain(B), gill(G), liver(L) and skin(S) tissues and proteome of G, L and S tissues were performed in C and Normal (N) (WT: 20 °C) group. 24 transcriptomes were completed, and 168.8 Gb data were obtained, with more than 5.89 Gb clean data of each sample. A total of 30499 annotation results were obtained with 3199, 4697, 4393, and 3382 differentially expressed mRNAs in NB_vs_CB, NG_vs_CG, NL_vs_CL, NS_vs_CS. 18 DIA proteomes were performed, and 6341 proteins were obtained with 178, 500 and 166 differentially expressed proteins in NG_vs_CG, NL_vs_CL, NS_vs_CS. Our datasets can be reused for key genes and proteins identification, omics joint analysis and regulatory mechanism analysis of low temperature or cold stress in fish, which will help understanding the regulatory mechanism and facilitate the molecular selective breeding of cold-resistant varieties of fish.

Background & Summary

The red tilapia (Oreochromis spp.) is an excellent cross breed between mutant red mozambique tilapia (O. mossambicus) with other tilapia species, such as nile tilapia (O. niloticus)1. It is an important economic fish in the world2. However, the skin color differentiation in genetic breeding and cold stress during the overwintering period are the bottleneck problems limiting the development of commercial red tilapia cultures1,3–5. We explained previously the genetic molecular mechanism of pigmentation differentiation in red tilapia1,4–10 and obtained a new aquaculture variety red tilapia “Zhongheng No. 1” (GS-01-002-2022) with stable and consistent skin color in china. The red tilapia faces the problems of stunted growth, skin color darkening and high mortality as a consequence of cold stress11,12, and it is usually transferred into greenhouse during overwintering in some regions13. Recently, we found that acute cold stress significantly affected the blood biochemical indices, immuno-antioxidant function, liver and gill histology and intestinal microorganisms of red tilapia (Under submission review). To better understand the regulatory mechanism of cold stress in fish, we are interested in the transcriptional mRNAs and protein regulation after cold stress in red tilapia.

Protein, encoded by mRNA, is the main undertakers of life activities, and the transcriptome refers to all mRNAs that a cell can transcribe in a narrow sense. Red tilapia has a wide water temperature adaptation range and can tolerate low temperatures of 8 °C, with a critical temperature of 7 °C. When the water temperature drops to 6.5 °C, red tilapia may go into shock and die at 6 °C14. In this study, the water temperature in the cold stress group was decreased by 2 °C per day from 20 °C to 8 °C, then transcriptome of brain, gill, liver and skin tissues and mass spectrometry (MS) proteome sequencing of gill, liver and skin tissues were employed to obtain the mRNA and protein datasets in cold stress (8 °C) group and normal (20 °C) group respectively during overwintering.

The datasets of this study can be reused by other researchers for key genes and proteins identification, omics joint analysis and regulatory mechanism analysis of low temperature or cold stress in fish, which will provide valuable and novel insights on the understanding of cold stress and facilitate the molecular selective breeding of new cold-resistant varieties of fish in the future.

Methods

Fish

The red tilapia used in the study was a new aquaculture variety “Zhongheng No. 1” (GS-01-002-2022) with genetically stable and consistent pink skin color. It is obtained from the original parents of Malaysia red tilapia population with pink skin color and no red and black spots after five generation mass selection by Freshwater Fisheries Research Center, Chinese Academy of Fishery Sciences. The breeding target traits were skin color and body weight. It is now one of the dominant aquaculture species in China due to its consistent skin color, fast growth, and stable genetic traits under various culture conditions. The fish were kept in indoor conical fiberglass tanks (diameter 150 cm × depth 120 cm) and acclimated in a flow-through water system for overwintering.

Experiment and sampling

Eighty fish (initial weight 72.71 ± 1.32 g) were randomly divided into two groups (each group 40 fish) for cold stress (C) and control (Normal) group (N) respectively. In the control group, the water temperature was maintained at 20 °C, which is basically consistent to the overwintering water temperature indoor or greenhouse of red tilapia in local area. The water temperature in the cold stress group was decreased by 2 °C per day from 20 °C to 8 °C using temperature controllers (SUNSUN, Zhejiang, China). If the water temperature could not be lowered naturally, we added crushed ice to help cool it down. Following a one-day of water temperature maintenance at 8 °C, fish from both groups were sampled. In the period of the experiment, the same commercial fish feed (Tong Wei, Chengdu, China) was used and the fish was fed twice a day until the day before sampling. Aeration was supplied to each tank 24 h per day and photoperiod was 12D:12 L. During the experiment, the swimming and feeding behaviours of the red tilapia were observed and recorded every day.

At the end of the experiment, six fish were randomly selected and sampled from C and N goup, respectively. The brain (B), dorsal skin (S), liver (L) and gill (G) tissues of three fish were excised and snap-frozen in liquid nitrogen and stored at −80 °C until processed.

Transcriptome

RNA isolation and qualification

Total RNA was extracted from brain, skin, liver and gill tissues using the RNAsimple Total RNA Kit (DP190211) of Tiangen (Biotech Co. Ltd., Beijing, China). RNA degradation and contamination was monitored on 1% agarose gels. RNA purity, RNA concentration and RNA integrity were assessed15,16, respectively. And only high quality samples were used to construct the sequencing library. The sample names are CB-4, CB-5, CB-6, CG-1, CG-2, CG-3, CL-1, CL-2, CL-3, CS-1, CS-2, CS-3 for cold stress group and NB-4, NB-5, NB-6, NG-1, NG-2, NG-3, NL-4, NL-5, NL-6, NS-1, NS-2, NS-3 for control (Normal) group, respectively.

Library preparation for transcriptome sequencing

24 cDNA Sequencing libraries were constructed using NEB Next® Ultra™ RNA Library Prep Kit for Illumina. As described by Wang et al. (2023), after mRNA purification, first and second strand cDNA synthesis, the library fragments were purified with 150-200 bp cDNA fragments15. Then PCR was performed to enrich the cDNA library and library quality was assessed15.

The clustering of the index-coded samples was performed on a cBot Cluster Generation System using TruSeq PE Cluster Kit v3-cBot-HS (Illumina). Then the library preparations were sequenced on an Illumina Nova seq6000 platform and 150 bp paired-end reads were generated.

Data analysis

The quality control of raw data (raw reads) was performed with Fastp software (v0.20.0)17, with parameter setting: -q 5 -n 5. The clean reads were aligned to the reference genome using HISAT2 (v2.1.0)18, with parameter setting: --novel-splicesite-outfile XX.ss. The Reference genome version: Oreochromis_niloticus. O_niloticus_UMD_NMBU. Reference genome website: http://ftp.ensembl.org/pub/current_fasta/oreochromis_niloticus/dna/. Then StringTie (stringtie-2.1.3b.Linux_x86_64)19, with parameter setting: -f 0.2 -a 30 -c 5 -j 5, was used to assemble transcripts, then StringTie was used to fuse all sample reconstruction results, and the optimized transcript structure annotation file was obtained, and the parameter was set as follows: --merge. StringTie was used again to quantify gene expression according to the annotation file after gene structure optimization and compared with the obtained bam file, and the parameter was set: -e -p 4.

Differential expression analysis of the cold stress and control groups was performed using the DESeq2 (v1.26.0) R package20. Genes with an adjusted P-value < 0.05 were assigned as differentially expressed. FDR (false discovery rate) <0.05 & Fold Change≥2 was set as the threshold for significantly differential expression in this study. Perl, with module of use Text::NSP::Measures::2D::Fisher::right, was used to count the number of differential genes annotated to each database and to map.

Quantitative real-time-PCR (qRT-PCR) validation

RNA samples from the brain (B), dorsal skin (S), liver (L) and gill (G) tissues of three fishes used for the RNA-seq were analyzed by qRT-PCR. As described by Wang et al. (2023)15, qRT-PCR was performed on a CFX96 Real-Time PCR System (Bio-Rad, Hercules, CA, USA). The final volume of each PCR reaction was 25 μL. The relative mRNA expression levels were normalized to β-actin and calculated using the 2−ΔΔCt method21. The primer sequences used in qRT-PCR were shown in Supplementary Table S1.

Proteomics

Protein extraction and quantification

Add 5 uL protease inhibitor and 5 uL PMSF (Phenylmethanesulfonyl fluoride) to every 500ul pre-cooled Lysis Buffer and store on ice for use, and 200 mg of animal tissue (CG-4, CG-5, CG-6, NG-4, NG-5, NG-6, CS-4, CS-5, CS-6, NS-4, NS-5, NS-6, CL-4, CL-5, CL-6, NL-1, NL-2, NL-3) was crushed in liquid nitrogen. The crushed sample was added to 500ul pre-cooled Lysis Buffer and lysis was performed every 5 minutes at 4 °C for 30 min, then centrifuge at 14000 rpm for 30 min at 4 °C. The protein concentration was determined with bicinchoninic acid (BCA) method. The concentration of the sample must be ≥ 1 μg/μL before data-independent acquisition DIA unlabeled test can be performed. 5 μg total protein was removed from the samples for western blotting, staining and decolorization to check the protein content.

Mass spectrometry (MS) detection

500 μg whole cell lysates were subsequently reduced with 5 mM dithiothreitol DTT (Thermo Scientific, 20291) for one hour at 37° and alkylated with 10 mM iodoacetamide (Sigma, A3221) for 45 min in the dark at room temperature. Protein was precipitated with methanol/chloroform, then washed with 1 ml methanol, and resolved with 8 M urea. Digestion was performed with endoproteinase LysC (Wako, 129-02541) for 2 h and with trypsin (Promega, V511X) overnight, both at a 1:50 enzyme-to-protein ratio at room temperature. Digested samples were acidified with formicacid (FA; Sigma, F0507) to achieve a final volumetric concentration of 1% (final pH of 2-3), and centrifuged at 1,500 g for 15 min to clear precipitated urea from peptide lysates. Samples were desalted on C18 SepPak columns (Waters, WAT054925) and lyophilized using a SpeedVac apparatus.

After reconstituted with 0.1% formic acid, 25ug proteome samples were separated using an EASY-nLC 1200 system with the same gradient, and the analytical column (180 mm × 100 μm) in-house packed with C18-AQ 1.9 μm C18 resin (Dr. Maisch GmbH, Germany) with a gradient of 5-28% solvent B (0.1% FA/80% ACN) in 45 min, at a flow rate of 250 nL/min. MS data acquisition in DIA mode was performed on Orbitrap Fusion Lumos using 30 variable windows covering a mass range of 350-1500 m/z. The resolution was set to 60,000 for MS1 and 15,000 for MS2. The AGC was 3e6 in MS1 and 5e5 in MS2, with a maximum injection time of 100 ms in MS1 and auto in MS2. NCE was set to 28%.

In the analysis of data, search parameters of DIA-NN (version 1.8.1)22 were set as follows: precursor FDR 1%; mass accuracy at MS1 and MS2 set to 5 ppm and 15 ppm for liquid chromatograph-mass spectrometer LC-MS data; scan window set to 0; isotopologues and membrane bio-reactor MBR turned on; protein inference at gene level; heuristic protein inference enabled; quantification strategy set to Robust LC (high precision); neural network classifier double-pass mode; cross-run normalization off. The main search settings were the same with additional settings for in silico library generation as follows: Trypsin/P with maximum 1 missed cleavage; protein N-terminal M excision on; Carbamidomethyl on C as fixed modification; no variable modification; peptide length from 7 to 30; precursor charge 1–4; precursor m/z from 300 to 1300; fragment m/z from 300 to 1800.

Bioinformatics analysis

MS data need to be searched for protein database. The used database in this study: Oreochromis_niloticus.O_niloticus_UMD_NMBU.pep.all.fa (75555 protein sequence) and the link website: https://ftp.ensembl.org/pub/release-109/fasta/oreochromis_niloticus/pep/. All identified protein were compared with databases including the Nr (NCBI non-redundant protein sequences), SwissProt (A manually annotated and reviewed protein sequence database), KEGG (Kyoto Encyclopedia of Genes and Genomes), KOG (euKaryotic Orthologous Groups), COG (Cluster of Orthologous Groups of protein), GO (Gene Ontology) database and Pfam (Protein family) for functional annotation. The basic steps are as follows: compare the identified proteins by BLAST (version: 2.2.26) (blastp, evalue ≤ 1e-5), and then filter the BLAST results: the comparison result with the lowest e-values and the highest score is selected as the final annotation result. Then the relative quantitative value of each protein was obtained according to the quantitative information of the peptides contained in each protein. Perl was used to count the number of proteins annotated to each database with built-in function. PCA analysis is performed using the R (V3.6.2) prin_comp_data function.

For protein difference analysis, the relative quantitative value of each protein in the samples of the two comparison groups was tested by T-test, and the corresponding p-value was calculated as a significance indicator. When Fold Change (FC) ≥ 1.2 and p-value ≤ 0.05, protein expression was up-regulated; when FC ≤ 0.833 and p-value ≤ 0.05, protein expression was down-regulated. FC represents the ratio of expression between two groups, and p value is the significance of differential expression. Correction of the P-value of significance will obtain the FDR value of false discovery rate, and controlling FDR less than a certain threshold can reduce the false positive rate of differentially expressed proteins. Use R (V3.6.2) ggplot2 program package to draw volcano map; R (V3.6.2) pheatmap package was used to make differential protein clustering heatmap, and the clustering method was hierarchical cluster.

Technical Validation

Confirming cold stress status of red tilapia

There was no mortality during the period of experiment. It was observed that some of the red tilapia in the cold stress group did not swim much at the bottom of the tank and ceased feeding when the water temperature was below 10 °C. At the end of the experiment, the histological morphology of fish liver, skin and gill was different between the C and N group. Some of the serum indices and immune antioxidant index of fish liver and gill tissue in the C group changed compared to that in the N group. The results are submitting for publication.
