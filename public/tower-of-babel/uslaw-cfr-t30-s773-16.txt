30 C.F.R. § 773.16 — Performance bond submittal.
Citation: us:cfr:30:773.16
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-773.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 773 (Requirements For Permits And Permit Processing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the regulatory authority decides to approve the application, it shall require that the applicant file the performance bond or provide other equivalent guarantee before the permit is issued, in accordance with the provisions of subchapter J of this chapter.
