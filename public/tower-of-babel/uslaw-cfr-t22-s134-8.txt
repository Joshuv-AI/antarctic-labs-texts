22 C.F.R. § 134.8 — Official authorized to take final action under the Act.
Citation: us:cfr:22:134.8
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-134.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 134 (Equal Access To Justice Act; Implementation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department of State official who renders the final agency decision in a covered proceeding is authorized to take final action on matters pertaining to the Equal Access to Justice Act as applied to the proceeding.
