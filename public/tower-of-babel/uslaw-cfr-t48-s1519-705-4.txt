48 C.F.R. § 1519.705-4 — 1519.705-4 Reviewing the subcontracting plan.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1519.705-4
Creator: Chapter 15—Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1519.705-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1519 (Small Business Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In determining the acceptability of a proposed subcontracting plan, the contracting officer shall obtain advice and recommendations from the Office of Small and Disadvantaged Business Utilization, which shall in turn coordinate review by the Small Business Administration Procurement Center Representative (if any).
