10 C.F.R. § 1015.404 — Exception to termination.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1015.404
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1015.404
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1015 (Collection Of Claims Owed The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a significant enforcement policy is involved, or recovery of a judgment is a prerequisite to the imposition of administrative sanctions, DOE may refer debts for litigation even though termination of collection activity may otherwise be appropriate.
