48 C.F.R. § 215.570 — 215.570 Solicitation provision.
Citation: us:cfr:48:215.570
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-215.570
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 215 (Contracting By Negotiation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Use the provision at 252.215-7016, Notification to Offerors—Postaward Debriefings, in competitive negotiated solicitations for contract awards valued at $15 million or more, including solicitations using FAR part 12 procedures for the acquisition of commercial products and commercial services.
