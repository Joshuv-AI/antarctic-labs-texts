Correction: Resveratrol synergizes with cisplatin to suppress osteosarcoma U2-OS cells via up-regulating Cx43 and inducing ROS-dependent apoptosis
Authors: Hong-Bo Xing, Hao-Yu Li, Gui-Zhou Zheng, Qi-Hao Zhang, Lei Zhai, Xiang Wu, Shi-Xin Du, Xue-Dong Li
Published: 2026

Correction: Discover Oncology (2025) 16:2218 https://doi.org/10.1007/s12672-025-04313-1

In the original publication of this article [1], E cadherin protein band panel in Figure 3F has been published incorrectly. The incorrect and correct figure are shown in this correction article (Fig. 3).

Res and CDDP synergistically inhibit migration and invasion of U2-OS cells. A, B Migration of U2-OS cells treated with Res, CDDP, and/or their combination (wound-healing assay). C, D Invasion of U2-OS cells treated with Res, CDDP, and/or their combination (Transwell assay). E–G mRNA and protein expression levels of E-cadherin, matrix metalloproteinase (MMP)−2, and MMP-9, analyzed by qRT-PCR and Western blotting. Data are presented as mean ± SEM from three independent experiments (n = 3).
