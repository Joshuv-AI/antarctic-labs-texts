16 U.S.C. § 7201 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:16:7201
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section7201
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 91 (National Landscape Conservation System). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter:
(1) Secretary
The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of the Interior.
(2) System
The term “system” means the National Landscape Conservation System established by section 7202(a) of this title.
