45 C.F.R. § 63.17 — Amount of award.
Citation: us:cfr:45:63.17
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-63.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 63 (Grant Programs Administered By The Office Of The Assistant Secretary For Planning And Evaluation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Federal assistance shall be provided only to meet allowable costs incurred by the award recipient in carrying out an approved project in accordance with the authorizing legislation and the regulations of this part.
