WOAD, a herbaceous plant, known botanically as Isatis
tinctoria (natural order Cruciferae), which occurs sporadically
in England in fields, on banks and chalk-pits. The erect branched
Stem, 1 to 3 ft. in height, bears sessile leaves and terminal clusters
of small yellow flowers; the brown pendulous pods are 2 in. long.
The ancient Britons stained themselves with this plant.
It is still cultivated in Lincolnshire.
