40 U.S.C. § 15307 — Tribal participation
Citation: us:usc:40:15307
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section15307
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Chapter 153 (Regional Commissions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Governments of Indian tribes in the region of the Southwest Border Regional Commission shall be allowed to participate in matters before that Commission in the same manner and to the same extent as State agencies and instrumentalities in the region.
