24 C.F.R. § 574.605 — Applicability of uniform administrative requirements, cost principles, and audit requirements for Federal awards.
Citation: us:cfr:24:574.605
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Community Planning And Development, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-574.605
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 574 (Housing Opportunities For Persons With Aids). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of 2 CFR part 200, “Uniform Administrative Requirements, Cost Principles, and Audit Requirements for Federal Awards”, apply to HOPWA grants.
