40 C.F.R. § 51.906 — Redesignation to nonattainment following initial designations for the 8-hour NAAQS.
Citation: us:cfr:40:51.906
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-51.906
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 51 (Requirements For Preparation, Adoption, And Submittal Of Implementation Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For any area that is initially designated attainment or unclassifiable for the 8-hour NAAQS and that is subsequently redesignated to nonattainment for the 8-hour ozone NAAQS, any absolute, fixed date applicable in connection with the requirements of this part is extended by a period of time equal to the length of time between the effective date of the initial designation for the 8-hour NAAQS and the effective date of redesignation, except as otherwise provided in this subpart.
