43 C.F.R. § 1824.14 — Does the claimant or applicant pay for an error by the printer of the paper in which the notice appears?
Citation: us:cfr:43:1824.14
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-1824.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 1820 (Application Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. The claimant or applicant is not responsible for costs involved in correcting an error by the printer.
