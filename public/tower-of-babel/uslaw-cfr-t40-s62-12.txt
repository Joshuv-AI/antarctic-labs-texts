40 C.F.R. § 62.12 — Availability of applicable plans.
Citation: us:cfr:40:62.12
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-62.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 62 (Approval And Promulgation Of State Plans For Designated Facilities And Pollutants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Copies of the applicable plans will be available for public inspection at the following locations:
(a) EPA Docket Center, Room 3334, WJC West Building, 1301 Constitution Avenue NW, Washington, DC.
(b) The applicable EPA Regional office, at the address listed in § 62.10.
