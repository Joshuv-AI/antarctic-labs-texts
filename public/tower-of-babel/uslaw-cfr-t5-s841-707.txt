5 C.F.R. § 841.707 — COLA's affecting computation of survivor supplements.
Citation: us:cfr:5:841.707
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-841.707
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 841 (Federal Employees Retirement System—General Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of computing the assumed CSRS annuity under § 843.308 of this chapter, the assumed CSRS annuity includes COLA's computed under CSRS rules.
