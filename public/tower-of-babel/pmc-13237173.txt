Correction: Q-CaDD: accelerating in silico methodologies with quantum computation and machine learning for Epidermal growth factor receptor
Authors: Lohitaksh Badarala
Published: 2026

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-026-44978-4, published online 21 March 2026

The original version of this Article contained errors.

In the Abstract, the sentence

“To support early-stage drug discovery targeting EGFR, we introduce Q-CaDD (Quantum-enhanced Computer-aided Drug Design), a hybrid quantum–classical computational framework.”

now reads:

“To support early-stage drug discovery targeting EGFR, this work introduces Q-CaDD (Quantum-enhanced Computer-aided Drug Design), a hybrid quantum–classical computational framework. Q-CaDD integrates quantum machine learning (QML), classical models, molecular docking, and multi-stage ligand filtering.”

In addition, the hybrid model’s AUC-ROC accuracy value was wrong.

As a result,

“The hybrid ensemble achieved an AUC-ROC of 0.83, demonstrating improved generalisation relative to standalone classical models under the same evaluation setting and competitive performance with respect to reported benchmark results.”

now reads:

“The hybrid ensemble achieved an AUC-ROC of ~ 0.76, demonstrating improved generalisation relative to standalone classical models under the same evaluation setting and competitive performance with respect to reported benchmark results.”

Furthermore, in the Background section of the Main Body, under the subheading ‘Application’, Clause (i) of the Motivation Statement was incorrect.

As a result, the sentence

“This endpoint was selected for three reasons: (i) NR-AR is one of the twelve gold-standard endpoints from the Tox21 Data Challenge and is one of the better-annoted endpoints, characterized by its high signal-to-noise ratio and robust annotation7,15; (ii) it provides a controlled and well-defined test case for evaluating model behavior under class imbalance; and (iii) it allows the contribution of the quantum-enhanced component to be isolated as a proof-of-concept.”

now reads:

“This endpoint was selected for three reasons: (i) NR-AR is one of the twelve gold-standard endpoints from the Tox21 Data Challenge and represents one of the more challenging assays to model, characterized by a low active rate of less than 5% and a high class imbalance between active and inactive compounds (ii) it provides a controlled and well-defined test case for evaluating model behavior under class imbalance; and (iii) it allows the contribution of the quantum-enhanced component to be isolated as a proof-of-concept.”

Lastly, the hyperlink included in the Data Availability Statement has been corrected and now reads:

“Data and Code used in conducting this research and constructing all stages of the framework can be found in the following GitHub repository: https://github.com/LohitakshB/Q-CaDD_public.”

The original Article has been corrected.
