31 C.F.R. § 570.407 — Payments from blocked accounts to satisfy obligations prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:31:570.407
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-570.407
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 570 (Libyan Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pursuant to § 570.201, no debits may be made to a blocked account to pay obligations to U.S. persons or other persons, except as authorized by or pursuant to this part.
