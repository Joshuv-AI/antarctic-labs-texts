10 U.S.C. § 7629 — Proceeds: disposition
Citation: us:usc:10:7629
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7629
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Procurement); Chapter 769 (Sale Of Serviceable Material). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The proceeds of sales of the following shall be paid into the Treasury to the credit of the appropriation out of which they were purchased, and are available for the purposes of that appropriation:
(1)
Exterior articles of uniform sold under section 7621 of this title.
(2)
Supplies and military publications sold to educational institutions under section 7627 of this title.
(3)
Fuel, oil, other supplies, and services for aircraft of a foreign military or air attache sold under section 7626 of this title.
