42 U.S.C. § 2323 — Basis of appraisal
Citation: us:usc:42:2323
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2323
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 24 (Disposal Of Atomic Energy Communities); Subchapter II (Lots, Appraisals, And Prices). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except for lots sold pursuant to the provisions of section 2347(a) of this title, the appraised value shall be the current fair market value of the Government’s interest in the property.
