33 C.F.R. § 175.19 — Stowage.
Citation: us:cfr:33:175.19
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-175.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 175 (Equipment Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) No person may use a recreational boat unless each wearable PFD required by § 175.15 is readily accessible.
(b) No person may use a recreational boat unless each throwable PFD required by § 175.15 is immediately available.
