AGENCY: Bureau of Labor Statistics, Department of Labor.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This is a correction of a notice published in the Federal 
Register of April 23, 2026, which contained an incorrect subject 
heading.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Correction

    In the Federal Register of April 23, 2026 in FR Doc. 2026-07872, at 
91 FR 21854, the subject heading incorrectly referenced Consumer 
Expenditure Surveys. The collection is the Contingent Work Supplement 
to the Current Population Survey.
