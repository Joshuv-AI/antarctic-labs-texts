15 C.F.R. § 950.1 — Scope and purpose.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 950 (Environmental Data And Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part describes the Environmental Data and Information Service (EDIS), a major program element of the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration, and EDIS management of environmental data and information.
