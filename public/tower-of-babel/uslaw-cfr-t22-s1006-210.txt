22 C.F.R. § 1006.210 — Which nonprocurement transactions are covered transactions?
Citation: us:cfr:22:1006.210
Creator: Inter-American Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1006.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1006 (Governmentwide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All nonprocurement transactions, as defined in § 1006.970, are covered transactions unless listed in § 1006.215. (See appendix to this part.)
