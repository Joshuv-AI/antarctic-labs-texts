12 C.F.R. § 1239.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1239.1
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1239.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1239 (Responsibilities Of Boards Of Directors, Corporate Practices, And Corporate Governance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

FHFA is responsible for supervising and ensuring the safety and soundness of the regulated entities. In furtherance of those responsibilities, this part sets forth minimum standards with respect to responsibilities of boards of directors, corporate practices, and corporate governance matters of the regulated entities.
