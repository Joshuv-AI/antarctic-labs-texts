44 U.S.C. § 3101 — Records management by agency heads; general duties
Citation: us:usc:44:3101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section3101
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 31 (Records Management By Federal Agencies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The head of each Federal agency shall make and preserve records containing adequate and proper documentation of the organization, functions, policies, decisions, procedures, and essential transactions of the agency and designed to furnish the information necessary to protect the legal and financial rights of the Government and of persons directly affected by the agency’s activities.
