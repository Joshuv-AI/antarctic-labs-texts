45 C.F.R. § 1302.100 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1302.100
Creator: Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1302.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1302 (Program Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A program must provide management and a process of ongoing monitoring and continuous improvement for achieving program goals that ensures child safety and the delivery of effective, high-quality program services.
