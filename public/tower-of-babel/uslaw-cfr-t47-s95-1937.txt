47 C.F.R. § 95.1937 — Station inspection.
Citation: us:cfr:47:95.1937
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-95.1937
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 95 (Personal Radio Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon request by an authorized Commission representative, the 218-219 MHz Service system licensee must make any component CTS available for inspection.
