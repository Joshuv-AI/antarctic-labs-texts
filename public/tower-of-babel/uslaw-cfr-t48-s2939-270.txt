48 C.F.R. § 2939.270 — 2939.270 Contract clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2939.270
Creator: Chapter 29—Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2939.270
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2939 (Acquisition Of Information Technology). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contracting officers shall insert the clause at DOLAR 2952.239-70, Section 508 Requirements, in all solicitations and contracts for the acquisition of Information and Communication Technology (ICT) to be used by the DOL.
