40 C.F.R. § 142.4 — State and local authority.
Citation: us:cfr:40:142.4
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-142.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 142 (National Primary Drinking Water Regulations Implementation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this part shall diminish any authority of a State or political subdivision to adopt or enforce any law or regulation respecting drinking water regulations or public water systems, but no such law or regulation shall relieve any person of any requirements otherwise applicable under this part.
