16 C.F.R. § 1061.10 — Information on affected parties.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1061.10
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1061.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1061 (Applications For Exemption From Preemption). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An application for an exemption from preemption shall include a statement which identifies in general terms, parties potentially affected by the State or local requirement, especially small businesses, including manufacturers, distributors, retailers, consumers, and consumer groups.
