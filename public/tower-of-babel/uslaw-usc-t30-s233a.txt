30 U.S.C. § 233a — Permits or leases of certain lands in Okla­homa; retention of royalties
Citation: us:usc:30:233a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section233a
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 3A (Leases And Prospecting Permits); Subchapter IV (Oil And Gas). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior is directed to retain in his custody until otherwise directed by law the 12½ per centum and other royalties heretofore or hereafter received by him in pursuance of section 233 1 of this title.
