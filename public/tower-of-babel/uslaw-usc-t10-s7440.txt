10 U.S.C. § 7440 — Quartermaster
Citation: us:usc:10:7440
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7440
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training); Chapter 753 (United States Military Academy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Army shall detail a commissioned officer of the Army as quartermaster for the Corps of Cadets. The quartermaster shall—
(1)
buy and issue all supplies for the cadets;
(2)
buy and issue all provisions for the mess; and
(3)
supervise the mess.
