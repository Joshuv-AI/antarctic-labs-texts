14 C.F.R. § 11.3 — What is an advance notice of proposed rulemaking?
Citation: us:cfr:14:11.3
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-11.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 11 (General Rulemaking Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An advance notice of proposed rulemaking (ANPRM) tells the public that FAA is considering an area for rulemaking and requests written comments on the appropriate scope of the rulemaking or on specific topics. An advance notice of proposed rulemaking may or may not include the text of potential changes to a regulation.
