54 U.S.C. § 101115 — Corporate succession and powers and duties acting as trustee; personal liability for malfeasance
Citation: us:usc:54:101115
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section101115
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 1011 (Donations); Subchapter II (National Park Foundation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Perpetual Succession.—
The National Park Foundation shall have perpetual succession.
(b) Powers and Duties of Trustee.—
The National Park Foundation shall have all the usual powers and obligations of a corporation acting as a trustee, including the power to sue and to be sued in its own name.
(c) Personal Liability of Board Members.—
The members of the Board shall not be personally liable, except for malfeasance.
