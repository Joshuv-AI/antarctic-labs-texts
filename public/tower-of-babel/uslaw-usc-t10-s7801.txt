10 U.S.C. § 7801 — Definition
Citation: us:usc:10:7801
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7801
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Procurement); Chapter 781 (Military Claims). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter, the term “settle” means consider, ascertain, adjust, determine, and dispose of a claim, whether by full or partial allowance or by disallowance.
