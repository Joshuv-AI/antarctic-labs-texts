6 C.F.R. § 158.652 — Compensatory time-off for religious observance.
Citation: us:cfr:6:158.652
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security, Office Of The Secretary
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-6/section-158.652
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 6 (Domestic Security), Part 158 (Cybersecurity Talent Management System (Ctms)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In alignment with salary administration under § 158.622 of this part and work scheduling under § 158.705, the Department provides compensatory time-off for religious observance to a DHS-CS employee under this section and in accordance with 5 U.S.C. 5550a and 5 CFR 550, subpart J.
