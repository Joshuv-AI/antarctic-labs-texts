16 U.S.C. § 837g–1 — Construction of additional facilities by Secretary of Energy for mutually beneficial power sales between Pacific Northwest and California; contribution of funds by non-Federal entities
Citation: us:usc:16:837g–1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section837g–1
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 12F (Pacific Northwest Consumer Power Preference; Reciprocal Priority In Other Regions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding the provisions of section 837g of this title, the Secretary of Energy is authorized to construct or participate in the construction of such additional facilities as he deems necessary to allow mutually beneficial power sales between the Pacific Northwest and California and to accept funds contributed by non-Federal entities for that purpose.
