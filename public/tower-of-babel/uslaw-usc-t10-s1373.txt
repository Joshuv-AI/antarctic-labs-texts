10 U.S.C. § 1373 — Higher grade for later physical disability: retired officers recalled to active duty
Citation: us:usc:10:1373
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1373
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 69 (Retired Grade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Unless entitled to a higher retired grade under some other provision of law, a member of an armed force whose retired pay is computed under section 1402(d) or 1402a(d) of this title is entitled, upon his release from active duty, to the grade equivalent to the grade or rank upon which his retired pay is based under that section.
