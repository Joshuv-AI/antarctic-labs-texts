7 C.F.R. § 54.1016 — Advance information concerning service rendered.
Citation: us:cfr:7:54.1016
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-54.1016
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 54 (Meats, Prepared Meats, And Meat Products (Grading, Certification, And Standards)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon request of any applicant, all or any part of the contents of any report issued to the applicant under the regulations, or other notification concerning the determination of compliance of equipment or utensils for such applicant may be transmitted by facsimile transmission to the applicant, or to any person designated by the applicant at the applicant's expense.
