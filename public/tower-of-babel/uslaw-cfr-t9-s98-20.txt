9 C.F.R. § 98.20 — Embryos refused entry.
Citation: us:cfr:9:98.20
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-98.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 98 (Importation Of Certain Animal Embryos And Animal Semen). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If any embryos are determined to be ineligible for importation into the United States upon arrival at the port of entry, the importer must remove the embryos from the United States within 30 days, or the embryos will be destroyed.
