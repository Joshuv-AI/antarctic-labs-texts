8 C.F.R. § 1003.66 — Changes in information or status.
Citation: us:cfr:8:1003.66
Creator: Executive Office For Immigration Review, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-1003.66
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 1003 (Executive Office For Immigration Review). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All providers with a pending application or currently on the List must notify the Director in writing within ten business days if:
(a) The provider's contact information has changed;
(b) Any specific limitations in providing pro bono legal services under § 1003.63(b)(4), (c)(2), or (d)(2) have changed; or
(c) The provider is no longer eligible under § 1003.62.
