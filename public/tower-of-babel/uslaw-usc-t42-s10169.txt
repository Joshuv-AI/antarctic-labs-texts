42 U.S.C. § 10169 — Financial assistance
Citation: us:usc:42:10169
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section10169
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 108 (Nuclear Waste Policy); Subchapter I (Disposal And Storage Of High-Level Radioactive Waste, Spent Nuclear Fuel, And Low-Level Radioactive Waste); Part C (Monitored Retrievable Storage). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of section 10136(c) or 10138(b) of this title with respect to grants, technical assistance, and other financial assistance shall apply to the State, to affected Indian tribes and to affected units of local government in the case of a monitored retrievable storage facility in the same manner as for a repository.
