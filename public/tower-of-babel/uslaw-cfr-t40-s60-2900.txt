40 C.F.R. § 60.2900 — When must I submit my waste management plan?
Citation: us:cfr:40:60.2900
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.2900
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 60 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must submit a waste management plan prior to commencing construction, reconstruction, or modification.
