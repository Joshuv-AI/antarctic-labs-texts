25 C.F.R. § 256.12 — Who administers the program?
Citation: us:cfr:25:256.12
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-256.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 256 (Housing Improvement Program (Hip)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The HIP is administered by a servicing housing office operated by either a tribe (under a Pub. L. 93-638 contract or a self-governance annual funding agreement) or BIA.
