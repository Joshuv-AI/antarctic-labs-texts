17 C.F.R. § 38.711 — Warning letters.
Citation: us:cfr:17:38.711
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-38.711
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 38 (Designated Contract Markets). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where a rule violation is found to have occurred, no more than one warning letter may be issued per rolling 12-month period for the same violation.
