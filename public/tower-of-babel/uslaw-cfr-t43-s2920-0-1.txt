43 C.F.R. § 2920.0-1 — -1 Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2920.0-1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2920.0-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2920 (Leases, Permits And Easements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of the regulations in this part is to establish procedures for the orderly and timely processing of proposals for non-Federal use of the public lands. The procedural and informational requirements set by these regulations vary in relation to the nature of the anticipated use.
