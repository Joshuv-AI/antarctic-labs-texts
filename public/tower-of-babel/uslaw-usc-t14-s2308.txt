14 U.S.C. § 2308 — Recall to active duty during war or national emergency
Citation: us:usc:14:2308
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2308
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 23 (Personnel; Enlisted). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In times of war or national emergency, the Commandant may order any enlisted member on the retired list to active duty.
