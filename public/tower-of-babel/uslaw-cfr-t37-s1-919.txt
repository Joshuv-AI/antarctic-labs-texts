37 C.F.R. § 1.919 — Filing date of request for inter partes reexamination.
Citation: us:cfr:37:1.919
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-1.919
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 1 (Rules Of Practice In Patent Cases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The filing date of a request for inter partes reexamination is the date on which the request satisfies all the requirements for the request set forth in § 1.915.
(b) If the request is not granted a filing date, the request will be placed in the patent file as a citation of prior art if it complies with the requirements of § 1.501.
