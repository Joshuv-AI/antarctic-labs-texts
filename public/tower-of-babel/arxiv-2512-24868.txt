Correlating Resonant Di-Higgs and Tri-Higgs Production to H → V ​ V H\to VV in the 2HDM Preprint: ZU-TH 89/25

Abstract
The observation of resonant di-Higgs production, which would strongly suggest the existence of a new heavy neutral scalar H H , has been searched for extensively at the LHC. In the two-Higgs-doublet model (2HDM) with m H ≫ m h m_{H}\gg m_{h} , where h h is the Higgs boson of mass 125 GeV observed at the LHC, we show that a direct correlation between Br ( H → h ​ h ) (H\to hh) and Br ( H → V ​ V ) (H\to VV) , with V = Z , W V=Z,W , emerges that depends only on m H m_{H} (and m V m_{V} ). In particular, for heavy scalar masses between 500 GeV and 1 TeV, we find that Br( H → h ​ h H\to hh )/ Br( OPEN H → Z ​ Z ) ≈ 9.5 H\to ZZ)\approx 9.5 . The origin of this prediction is most transparent in the Higgs basis, where the term in the scalar potential proportional to ℋ 1 † ​ ℋ 1 ​ ℋ 1 † ​ ℋ 2 \mathcal{H}_{1}^{\dagger}\mathcal{H}_{1}\mathcal{H}_{1}^{\dagger}\mathcal{H}_{2} (and its hermitian conjugate) generates the leading contributions to the H ​ h ​ h Hhh and H ​ h ​ h ​ h Hhhh couplings in the decoupling limit of the 2HDM. Moreover, the latter coupling governs the resonant prompt tri-Higgs production via H → h ​ h ​ h H\to hhh , which is also directly correlated to H → h ​ h H\to hh and ( H → V ​ V ) (H\to VV) , and can yield rates large enough to be measured at the High-Luminosity LHC.

\displaystyle=

VI
Conclusions

In this letter, we derive simple relations among the decay widths of the heavier CP-even Higgs boson of the 2HDM (denoted by
H
) into
hh
,
ZZ
,
WW
and
hhh
which hold for
m_{H}\gg v
, i.e. close to the Higgs alignment limit in the decoupling regime. In particular, we find that for
500
GeV
<m_{H}<
1 TeV,
\Gamma(H\to hh)/\Gamma(H\to ZZ)\approx 9.5
and
0.01\lesssim\Gamma(H\to hhh)/\Gamma(H\to ZZ)\lesssim 0.1
. These relations can be used as simple tests of the 2HDM if a new scalar particle is discovered in one of these decay modes. Furthermore, by taking into account the current experimental LHC bounds on
H\to ZZ
,
H\to hh
,
H\to t\bar{t}
and
A\to t\bar{t}
, we find that
\sigma(pp\to H\to hhh)
can be close to around
0.1
fb at
\sqrt{s}=14
TeV, thereby suggesting that resonant prompt triple Higgs production in a 2HDM can be observable at the HL-LHC.
