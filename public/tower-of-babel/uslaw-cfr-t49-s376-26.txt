49 C.F.R. § 376.26 — Exemption for leases between authorized carriers and their agents.
Citation: us:cfr:49:376.26
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-376.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 376 (Lease And Interchange Of Vehicles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The leasing regulations set forth in § 376.12(e) through (l) do not apply to leases between authorized carriers and their agents.
