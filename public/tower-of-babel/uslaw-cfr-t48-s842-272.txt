48 C.F.R. § 842.272 — 842.272 Contract clause for Government construction contract administration.
Citation: us:cfr:48:842.272
Creator: Chapter 8—Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-842.272
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 842 (Contract Administration And Audit Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 852.242-70, Government Construction Contract Administration, in solicitations and contracts for construction expected to exceed the micro-purchase threshold, when contract administration is delegated.
