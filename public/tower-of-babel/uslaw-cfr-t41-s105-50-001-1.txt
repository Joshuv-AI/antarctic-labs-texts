41 C.F.R. § 105-50.001-1 — -50.001-1 State.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-50.001-1
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-50.001-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-50 (50—Provision Of Special Or Technical Services To State And Local Units Of Government). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

State means any of the several States of the United States, the District of Columbia, Puerto Rico, any territory or possession of the United States, or any agency or instrumentality of a State, but does not include the governments of the political subdivisions of the State.
