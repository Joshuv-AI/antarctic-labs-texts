45 C.F.R. § 2524.20 — What are the guidelines for program development assistance and training grants?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2524.20
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2524.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2524 (Americorps Technical Assistance And Other Special Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Eligibility. States, Federal agencies, Indian tribes, public or private nonprofit agencies, institutions of higher education, for-profit businesses, and individuals may apply for assistance under this section.
(b) Duration. A grant made under this section will be for a term of up to one year and is renewable.
(c) Application requirements. Eligible applicants must comply with the requirements specified in the Corporation's application package.
