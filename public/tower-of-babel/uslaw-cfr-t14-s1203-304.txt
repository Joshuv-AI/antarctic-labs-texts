14 C.F.R. § 1203.304 — Internal effect.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1203.304
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1203.304
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1203 (Information Security Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The effect of security protection on program progess and cost and on other functional activities of NASA should be considered. Impeditive effects and added costs inherent in a security classification must be assessed and weighed against the detrimental effects on the national security interests which would result from failure to classify.
