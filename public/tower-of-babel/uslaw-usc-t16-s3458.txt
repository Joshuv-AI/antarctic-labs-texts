16 U.S.C. § 3458 — Limitation on assistance
Citation: us:usc:16:3458
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 54 (Resource Conservation); Subchapter V (Resource Conservation And Development Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In carrying out this subchapter, the Secretary shall provide technical assistance and financial assistance with respect to not more than 450 active designated areas.
