10 U.S.C. § 278 — Nonpreemption of other law
Citation: us:usc:10:278
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section278
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And General Military Powers); Chapter 15 (Military Support For Civilian Law Enforcement Agencies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to limit the authority of the executive branch in the use of military personnel or equipment for civilian law enforcement purposes beyond that provided by law before December 1, 1981.
