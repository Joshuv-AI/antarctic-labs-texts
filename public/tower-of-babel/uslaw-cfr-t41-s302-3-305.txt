41 C.F.R. § 302-3.305 — -3.305 Time limit to begin travel and transportation upon separation.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-3.305
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-3.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-3 (3—Relocation Allowance By Specific Type). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All travel and transportation of household goods must begin no later than six months after:
(a) The date of separation; or
(b) The date of death of the employee who died before separation.
