50 C.F.R. § 29.100 — How will the Service process my application?
Citation: us:cfr:50:29.100
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-29.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 29 (Land Use Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We will conduct initial review of your application to determine if all information is complete. Once your information is complete, we will begin formal review.
