27 U.S.C. § 216 — Preemption
Citation: us:usc:27:216
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title27-section216
Context: United States Code, Title 27 (Intoxicating Liquors); Chapter 8 (Federal Alcohol Administration Act); Subchapter II (Alcoholic Beverage Labeling). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No statement relating to alcoholic beverages and health, other than the statement required by section 215 of this title, shall be required under State law to be placed on any container of an alcoholic beverage, or on any box, carton, or other package, irrespective of the material from which made, that contains such a container.
