40 U.S.C. § 521 — Policies and methods
Citation: us:usc:40:521
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section521
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Chapter 5 (Property Management); Subchapter II (Use Of Property). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Subject to section 523 of this title, in order to minimize expenditures for property, the Administrator of General Services shall—
(1)
prescribe policies and methods to promote the maximum use of excess property by executive agencies; and
(2)
provide for the transfer of excess property—
(A)
among federal agencies; and
(B)
to the organizations specified in section 321(c)(2) of this title.
