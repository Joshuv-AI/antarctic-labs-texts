42 U.S.C. § 4741 — Declaration of purpose
Citation: us:usc:42:4741
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section4741
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 62 (Intergovernmental Personnel Program); Subchapter III (Training And Developing State And Local Employees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of this subchapter is to strengthen the training and development of State and local government employees and officials, particularly in professional, administrative, and technical fields.
