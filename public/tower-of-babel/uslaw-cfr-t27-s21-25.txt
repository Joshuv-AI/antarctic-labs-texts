27 C.F.R. § 21.25 — Formula No. 35.
Citation: us:cfr:27:21.25
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-21.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 21 (Formulas For Denatured Alcohol And Rum). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Formula. To every 100 gallons of alcohol of not less than 185 proof add:
29.75 gallons of ethyl acetate having an ester content of 100 percent by weight or the equivalent thereof not to exceed 35 gallons of ethyl acetate with an ester content of not less than 85 percent by weight.
