9 C.F.R. § 312.9 — Official detention marks and devices.
Citation: us:cfr:9:312.9
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-312.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 312 (Official Marks, Devices And Certificates). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The official mark for articles and livestock detained under part 329 of this subchapter shall be the designation “U.S. Detained” and the official device for applying such mark shall be the official “U.S. Detained” tag (FSIS Form 8400-2) as prescribed in § 329.2 of this subchapter.
