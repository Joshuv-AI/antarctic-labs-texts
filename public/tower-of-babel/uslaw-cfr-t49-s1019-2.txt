49 C.F.R. § 1019.2 — Interpretation and advisory service.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1019.2
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1019.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1019 (Regulations Governing Conduct Of Surface Transportation Board Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Board's Chief Counsel shall be the Board's Designated Agency Ethics Official (DAEO).
(b) By June 30 of each year, the DAEO shall report to the Board on the operation of the Board's ethics program with any recommendations that the DAEO deems advisable.
