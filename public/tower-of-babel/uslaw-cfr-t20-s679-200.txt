20 C.F.R. § 679.200 — What is the purpose of requiring States to identify regions?
Citation: us:cfr:20:679.200
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-679.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 679 (Statewide And Local Governance Of The Workforce Development System Under Title I Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of identifying regions is to align workforce development activities and resources with larger regional economic development areas and available resources to provide coordinated and efficient services to both job seekers and employers.
