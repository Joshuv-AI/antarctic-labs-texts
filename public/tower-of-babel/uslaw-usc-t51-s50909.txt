51 U.S.C. § 50909 — Prohibition, suspension, and end of launches, operation of launch sites and reentry sites, and reentries
Citation: us:usc:51:50909
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section50909
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 509 (Commercial Space Launch Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) General Authority.—
The Secretary of Transportation may prohibit, suspend, or end immediately the launch of a launch vehicle or the operation of a launch site or reentry site, or reentry of a reentry vehicle, licensed under this chapter if the Secretary decides the launch or operation or reentry is detrimental to the public health and safety, the safety of property, or a national security or foreign policy interest of the United States.
(b) Effective Periods of Orders.—
An order under this section takes effect immediately and remains in effect during a review under section 50912 of this title.
