29 C.F.R. § 32.7 — Designation of responsible employee.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 32 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).
