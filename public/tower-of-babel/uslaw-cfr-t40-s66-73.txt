40 C.F.R. § 66.73 — Petition for reconsideration and procedure for hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:40:66.73
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-66.73
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 66 (Assessment And Collection Of Noncompliance Penalties By Epa). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within forty-five days of receipt of a notice under § 66.72(b) (2) a source owner or operator may petition for reconsideration in the form and manner provided in § 66.52. The petition shall be evaluated as provided in § 66.53 and any hearing shall be held in conformity with § 66.54.
