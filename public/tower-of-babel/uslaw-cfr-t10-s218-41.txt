10 C.F.R. § 218.41 — Violations.
Citation: us:cfr:10:218.41
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-218.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 218 (Standby Mandatory International Oil Allocation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any practice that circumvents, contravenes or results in the circumvention or contravention of the requirements of any provision of this part 218 or any order issued pursuant thereto is a violation of the DOE regulations stated in this part and is unlawful.
