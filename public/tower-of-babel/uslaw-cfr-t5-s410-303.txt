5 C.F.R. § 410.303 — Employee responsibilities.
Citation: us:cfr:5:410.303
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-410.303
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 410 (Training). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees are responsible for self-development, for successfully completing and applying authorized training, and for fulfilling continued service agreements. In addition, they share with their agencies the responsibility to identify training needed to improve individual and organizational performance and identify methods to meet those needs, effectively and efficiently.
