15 U.S.C. § 7605 — Content of advertisements and other representations
Citation: us:usc:15:7605
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section7605
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 102 (Fairness To Contact Lens Consumers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person that engages in the manufacture, processing, assembly, sale, offering for sale, or distribution of contact lenses may not represent, by advertisement, sales presentation, or otherwise, that contact lenses may be obtained without a prescription.
