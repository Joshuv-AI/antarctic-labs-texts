33 U.S.C. § 894b — Rights of the victim of a sexual assault
Citation: us:usc:33:894b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section894b
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 17 (National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration); Subchapter VI (Sexual Harassment And Assault Prevention). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A victim of a sexual assault covered by the comprehensive policy developed under section 894a(a) of this title has the right to be reasonably protected from the accused.
