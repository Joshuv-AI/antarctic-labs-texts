32 C.F.R. § 1656.9 — Alternative service worker's responsibilities.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1656.9
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1656.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1656 (Alternative Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A registrant classified in Class 1-W is required to comply with all orders issued under this part.
(b) A registrant classified in Class 1-W is liable to perform 24 months of creditable time toward completion of Alternative Service, unless released earlier by the Director.
