50 U.S.C. § 1844 — Authorization during time of war
Citation: us:usc:50:1844
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section1844
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 36 (Foreign Intelligence Surveillance); Subchapter III (Pen Registers And Trap And Trace Devices For Foreign Intelligence Purposes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the President, through the Attorney General, may authorize the use of a pen register or trap and trace device without a court order under this subchapter to acquire foreign intelligence information for a period not to exceed 15 calendar days following a declaration of war by Congress.
