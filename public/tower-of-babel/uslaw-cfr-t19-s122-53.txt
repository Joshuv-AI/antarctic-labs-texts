19 C.F.R. § 122.53 — Aircraft of foreign registry chartered or leased to U.S. air carriers.
Citation: us:cfr:19:122.53
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-122.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 122 (Air Commerce Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Aircraft of foreign registry leased or chartered to a U.S. air carrier, while being operated by the U.S. air carrier under the provisions of the Federal Aviation Administration regulations (14 CFR 121.153), shall be treated as U.S. registered aircraft for purposes of this subpart.
