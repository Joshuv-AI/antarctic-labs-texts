4
Conclussion.

We have constructed a one-parameter family, called the
\Sigma^{\alpha}
-spaces, which are models of metric manifolds
(M,g)
such that they change
the signature from Lorentz to Riemann, when traversing a hypersurface
\Sigma

called a singular. These spaces are of geometric interest in themselves but
they also have applications in cosmology.

Our
\Sigma^{\alpha}
-spaces spaces (
\alpha>0
) are characterized by inducing
a Riemannian metric on
\Sigma
, and that around each point

,
there exists a local expression of

such that the
function

extends differently, and
their differential does not vanish at

. When
\alpha=1

is smooth,
we obtain the type-change model proposed by Kossowski in the foundational
paper
[
9
]
where it is shown that these metrics admit a local
representation of the form

around each singular point. But it is used without proof, that for
r=1/2
the
function

is differentiable and this is a far from trivial fact. We
have proven it for all

family of
\Sigma^{\alpha}
-spaces, and has allowed us to prove that in the

around each singular point.
