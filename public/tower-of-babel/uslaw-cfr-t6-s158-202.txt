6 C.F.R. § 158.202 — Cybersecurity Service (DHS-CS).
Citation: us:cfr:6:158.202
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security, Office Of The Secretary
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-6/section-158.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 6 (Domestic Security), Part 158 (Cybersecurity Talent Management System (Ctms)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary or designee establishes and manages the DHS-CS to enhance the cybersecurity of the Nation through the most effective execution of the DHS cybersecurity mission.
