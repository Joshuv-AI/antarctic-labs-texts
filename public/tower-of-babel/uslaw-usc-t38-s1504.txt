38 U.S.C. § 1504 — Persons heretofore having a pensionable status
Citation: us:usc:38:1504
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section1504
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 15 (Pension For Non-Service-Connected Disability Or Death Or For Service); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The pension benefits of subchapters II and III of this chapter shall, notwithstanding the service requirements of such subchapters, be granted to persons heretofore recognized by law as having a pensionable status.
