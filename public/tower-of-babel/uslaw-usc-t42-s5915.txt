42 U.S.C. § 5915 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:42:5915
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section5915
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 74 (Nonnuclear Energy Research And Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There may be appropriated to the Secretary to carry out the purposes of this chapter such sums as may be authorized in annual authorization Acts.
