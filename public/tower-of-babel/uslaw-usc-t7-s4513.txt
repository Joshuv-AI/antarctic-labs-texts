7 U.S.C. § 4513 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:7:4513
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section4513
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 76 (Dairy Research And Promotion); Subchapter I (Dairy Promotion Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are hereby authorized to be appropriated such funds as are necessary to carry out the provisions of this subchapter. The funds so appropriated shall not be available for payment of the expenses or expenditures of the Board in administering any provisions of any order issued under the terms of this subchapter.
