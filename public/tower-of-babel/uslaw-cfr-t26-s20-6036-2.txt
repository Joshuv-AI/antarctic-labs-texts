26 C.F.R. § 20.6036-2 — -2 Notice of qualification as executor of estate of decedent dying after 1970.
Citation: us:cfr:26:20.6036-2
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-20.6036-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 20 (Estate Tax; Estates Of Decedents Dying After August 16, 1954). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the case of the estate of a decedent dying after December 31, 1970, no special notice of qualification as executor of an estate is required to be filed. The requirement of section 6036 for notification of qualification as executor of an estate shall be satisfied by the filing of the estate tax return required by section 6018 and the regulations thereunder.
