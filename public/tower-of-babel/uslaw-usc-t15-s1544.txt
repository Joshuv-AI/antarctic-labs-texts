15 U.S.C. § 1544 — Promotion of tourist travel
Citation: us:usc:15:1544
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1544
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 40 (Department Of Commerce). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Commerce shall encourage, promote, and develop travel within the United States, including any Commonwealth, territory, and possession thereof, through activities which are in the public interest and which do not compete with activities of any State, city, or private agency.
