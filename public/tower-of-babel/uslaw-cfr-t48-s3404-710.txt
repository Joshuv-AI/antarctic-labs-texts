48 C.F.R. § 3404.710 — 3404.710 Contracting officer records management responsibilities.
Citation: us:cfr:48:3404.710
Creator: Chapter 34—Department Of Education Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-3404.710
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 3404 (Administrative And Information Matters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon notification from the contractor of any unlawful or accidental removal, defacing, alteration, or destruction of Federal records, including all forms of mutilation, the contracting officer must notify the requiring activity, the Department Records Officer, and the HCA within one business day.
