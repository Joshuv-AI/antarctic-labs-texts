47 C.F.R. § 88.25 — Limitations on NNA communications.
Citation: us:cfr:47:88.25
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-88.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 88 (Uncrewed Aircraft System Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Transmissions over an NNA assignment are limited to CNPC.
(b) UAS ground stations may be fixed or mobile, but mobile ground stations cannot be used while in motion or at locations other than those approved under the applicable frequency assignment.
