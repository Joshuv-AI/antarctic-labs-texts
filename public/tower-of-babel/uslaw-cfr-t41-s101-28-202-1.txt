41 C.F.R. § 101-28.202-1 — -28.202-1 Request for services.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-28.202-1
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-28.202-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-28 (28—Storage And Distribution). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Requests for storage and warehousing services shall be in accordance with the procedures set forth in the GSA/DOD cross-servicing agreement. Arrangements incident to the furnishing of services, specific limitations, terms, and conditions shall be agreed to directly by the activities concerned.
