29 C.F.R. § 1602.38 — Applicability of State or local law.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1602.38
Creator: Equal Employment Opportunity Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1602.38
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1602 (Recordkeeping And Reporting Requirements Under Title Vii, The Ada, Gina, And The Pwfa). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirements imposed by the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission in these regulations, subparts I and J, supersede any provisions of State or local law which may conflict with them.
