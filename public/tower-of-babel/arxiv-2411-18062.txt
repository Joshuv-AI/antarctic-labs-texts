Note on WAdS 3 black holes in extended BHT gravity
Davood Mahdavian Yekta

Abstract
In this letter, we study a holographic diffeomorphism invariant higher-derivative extension of Bergshoeff-Hohm-Townsend (BHT) cosmological gravity in the context of Wald’s formalism. We calculate the entropy, mass and angular momentum of warped anti-de Sitter (WAdS 3 ) black holes in ghost-free BHT massive gravity and its extension using the covariant phase space method. We also compute the central charges of the dual boundary conformal field theories (CFT) from the thermodynamics method.

I
Introduction

Though the holographic nature of quantum gravity was originally suggested for black hole (BH) entropy
[
1
,
2
,
3
]
, the AdS/CFT correspondence
[
4
]
provides the first explicit example of how holography can be realized in string theory. It is an important tool to learn about the physical properties of various quantum field theories in the strongly coupled non-perturbative regime, namely, perturbative string theory on asymptotically locally AdS
d+1
backgrounds and conventional
d
-dimensional quantum field theories at their conformal fixed points
[
5
,
6
]
. It is well known that perturbative quantum gravity in four spacetime dimensions suffers from the problem of nonrenormalizability. This may be cured by going to lower dimensions. Three-dimensional (3D) gravity is an interesting playground to study problems in quantum gravity. 3D gravity with or without cosmological constant is described by a Chern-Simons (CS) gauge theory
[
7
,
8
]
.

Though 3D gravity with a cosmological constant has no local degrees of freedom, there are global degrees of freedom, that is the theory admits Banados-Teitelboim-Zanelli (BTZ) BH solutions
[
9
]
. The first geometric modification of cosmological 3D gravity is topologically massive gravity (TMG) which includes an odd-parity gravitational CS term
[
10
,
11
]
, while the BHT gravity is a higher-curvature parity-preserving modification (named also new massive gravity (NMG))
[
12
]
. The latter theory describes a massive graviton with the same dynamics of a Fierz-Pauli theory at the linearized level, but demanding unitarity of the bulk theory spoils the unitarity of the boundary theory
[
13
]
. Other gravitational extensions of new massive gravity (ENMG) have been proposed in
[
14
,
15
,
16
]
and with Maxwell field in
[
17
,
18
,
19
]
. However, a bulk/boundary unitary theory in AdS
3
background has been introduced in
[
20
]
as minimal massive gravity (MMG)
[
20
]
. These models also admit WAdS
3
BH as another solution to the equations of motion
[
21
]
.

In general, the conserved charges of BHs such as mass and angular momentum are expressed as integrals at spatial infinity; in the case of asymptotically-flat spacetimes they are denoted by Arnowitt-Deser-Misner (ADM) formula
[
22
]
while for AdS backgrounds they are defined by extended Abbott-Deser-Tekin (ADT) method
[
23
,
24
]
in terms of an integral over the metric perturbation near spatial infinity. However, the ADT method does not yield satisfactory results for the higher curvature gravities like NMG or ENMG in the case of WAdS geometries. There is a background independent method proposed by Wald
[
25
,
26
]
to calculate the conserved charges using Noether charge and symplectic potential on the covariant phase space. One of our main interests is to calculate the conserved charges of WAdS
3
BHs in NMG and ENMG using Wald’s formalism. Calculations for the BTZ BHs could be found in
[
27
,
28
,
29
]
.

According to AdS/CFT, one may expect that for each sector of 3D gravity with asymptotically AdS
3
geometry, there exists a dual 2D CFT. It would be of interest to study properties of 2D CFTs from different aspects such as BH thermodynamics, asymptotic symmetry group (ASG), CS formalism, and so on. From holographic point of view, the ASG of AdS
3
spaces is generated by two copies of the 2D conformal Virasoro algebra with central charges
c_{R}=c_{L}={3\ell\over 2G}
, where
\ell
is the AdS
3
radius and
G
is the 3D Newton’s constant
[
30
]
. The ASG has been applied for different 3D gravity theories in
[
31
,
32
,
33
]
and for WAdS
3
backgrounds in
[
34
,
35
,
36
,
37
]
. However, it is not clear if the same correspondence could be set up in a theory with more general higher derivatives, such as ENMG for WAdS
3
BHs. This is our next motivation in this paper to compute the central charges from an approach rather than ASG.

In the context of BH holography, the central charges of dual CFTs can be computed from thermodynamics of the outer and inner horizons, the so-called thermodynamics method
[
38
]
. For a diffeomorphism-invariant theory of gravity, when the temperatures and entropies of outer and inner horizons satisfy
T^{+}\!S^{+}\!\!=\!\!T^{-}\!S^{-}
, then

c_{L}=c_{R}=6\,\frac{d}{dN}\Big(\frac{S^{+}S^{-}}{4\pi^{2}}\Big),

(1)

where
N
is the conserved charge of BH in the gravity sector
[
39
,
40
]
. This methodology has been used for higher-dimensional BHs in
[
40
,
41
]
and for 3D ones in
[
42
,
43
,
44
]
. We will examine this conjecture for solutions of ENMG theory, particularly WAdS
3
. In this regard, we need the physical conserved charges of the corresponding BHs which were cited in the first motivation.

In this letter, we initially review the first order formalism based on the covariant phase space method in sec.
II
and derive integral expressions for the conserved charges independent of the background geometry. We also show taht the entropy is a conserved charge associated to vinishing Killing vector
\xi\!=\!{\partial_{t}}\!+\!\Omega_{\mathcal{H}}{\partial_{\phi}}
on the balck hole horizon. In sec.
III
, we compute the conserved charges for the WAdS
3
BH in NMG theory for the first time using this method. The generalization for both BTZ and WAdS
3
BHs in ENMG theory is extensively studied in sec.
IV
as well. We will obtain the central charges of the dual CFTs from thermodynamics method in sec.
V
and show that our results are consisitent with literature. We conclude this letter in the last section.

II
Conserved charges in Wald formalism

The first law of BH mechanics for stationary BHs can be derived from a diffeomorphism-invariant Lagrangian
[
25
,
26
]
.
For a BH with Killing vector
\xi
, vanishing on the Killing horizon, the conserved charges are well defined at spatial infinity. Also the BH entropy is the integral of the diffeomorphism Noether charge associated with
\xi
. The variation of
n
-form Lagrangian
L
w.r.t the fields
\Phi
is given by

\delta L=E_{\Phi}\delta\Phi+d\Theta(\Phi,\delta\Phi),

(2)

where
E_{\Phi}
is the field equations, i.e.
E_{\Phi}\!=\!0
and
\Theta
is the symplectic potential. The
(n-1)
-form symplectic current is

\omega(\Phi,\delta\Phi_{1},\delta\Phi_{2})\!=\!\delta_{1}\Theta(\Phi,\delta_{2}\Phi)\!-\!\delta_{2}\Theta(\Phi,\delta_{1}\Phi)
and the symplectic form over a Cauchy surface
\Sigma
is

\Omega(\Phi,\delta\Phi_{1},\delta\Phi_{2})=\int_{\Sigma}\omega(\Phi,\delta\Phi_{1},\delta\Phi_{2}).

(3)

For a given
\xi
, the diffeomorphism invariance of
L
implies
\delta_{\xi}L\!=\!\mathscr{L}_{\xi}L=d\imath_{\xi}L
where
\imath_{\xi}L
is the interior product
\xi\!\cdot\!L
and
\delta_{\xi}\Phi\!\!=\!\!\mathscr{L}_{\xi}\Phi
is the Lie derivative. We can associate a Noether current to each vector
\xi
as
J_{\xi}\!=\!\Theta(\Phi,\mathscr{L}_{\xi}\Phi)\!-\!\imath_{\xi}L

such that from (
2
) we obtain

dJ_{\xi}=d\Theta(\Phi,\mathscr{L}_{\xi}\Phi)-d\imath_{\xi}L=-E_{\Phi}\mathscr{L}_{\xi}\Phi.

(4)

Whenever the equations of motion are satisfied, i.e.
E_{\Phi}\!=\!0
,
J_{\xi}
is closed and

J_{\xi}=dQ_{\xi},

(5)

where
(n-2)
-form
Q_{\xi}
is the Noether charge associated to
\xi
and its integral over
\Sigma
is called the “
conserved charge
”.
The first law of BH mechanics can be deduced by considering the variation of
J_{\xi}
with respect to the dynamical field
\delta\Phi
, i.e.

\delta J_{\xi}=\delta\Theta(\Phi,\mathscr{L}_{\xi}\Phi)-\imath_{\xi}\delta L\,,

(6)

where from (
2
) and
E_{\Phi}\!=\!0
, the second term becomes

\imath_{\xi}\delta L\!=\!\mathscr{L}_{\xi}\Theta(\Phi,\delta\Phi)-d\imath_{\xi}\Theta(\Phi,\delta\Phi)
. Thus,

\delta J_{\xi}=\delta\Theta(\Phi,\mathscr{L}_{\xi}\Phi)-\mathscr{L}_{\xi}\Theta(\Phi,\delta\Phi)+d\imath_{\xi}\Theta(\Phi,\delta\Phi)\,.

(7)

The variation of Hamiltonian
H_{\xi}
conjugate to
\xi
is defined by
\delta H_{\xi}\!=\!\int_{\Sigma}\omega(\Phi,\delta\Phi,\mathscr{L}_{\xi}\Phi)
. From (
7
), one can obtain

\omega(\Phi,\!\delta\Phi,\mathscr{L}_{\xi}\Phi)\!=\!\delta J_{\xi}\!-\!d\imath_{\xi}\Theta(\Phi,\!\delta\Phi)
, then using (
5
)

\delta H_{\xi}=\int_{\Sigma}\delta dQ_{\xi}-d\imath_{\xi}\Theta=\oint_{\partial\Sigma}\delta Q_{\xi}-\imath_{\xi}\Theta,

(8)

where
\partial\Sigma
is the boundary of Cauchy surface.
At the linearized level
\omega(\Phi,\delta\Phi,\mathscr{L}_{\xi}\Phi)=0
, which implies
\delta H_{\xi}=0
and consequently

\oint_{\partial\Sigma}\delta Q_{\xi}-\imath_{\xi}\Theta=0.

(9)

Suppose that
\xi
, a Killing vector vanishing on
\Sigma
, is defined by
\xi={\partial_{t}}+\Omega_{\mathcal{H}}{\partial_{\phi}}
where
\Omega_{\mathcal{H}}
denotes the angular velocity of the horizon and hereafter
{\partial_{t}}=\partial/{\partial t}
and
{\partial_{\phi}}=\partial/{\partial\phi}
. Now, if
\xi
vanishes on a bifurcation surface
\mathcal{H}
and the hypersurface
\Sigma
has its outer boundary at spatial infinity and interior boundary at
\mathcal{H}
, then

\int_{\mathcal{H}}\delta Q_{\xi}=\int_{\infty}\delta Q_{\xi}-\imath_{\xi}\Theta=\int_{\infty}\delta\chi_{\xi}.

(10)

Nonetheless, if the theory admits a suitable definition of “canonical energy”
\mathcal{E}
and “canonical angular momentum”
\mathcal{J}
, the variations of these quantities should be given by

\delta\mathcal{E}=\int_{\infty}\delta\chi_{\xi}\left[\partial_{t}\right],\quad\delta\mathcal{J}=-\int_{\infty}\delta\chi_{\xi}\left[\partial_{\phi}\right].

(11)

Therefore, the variations of mass and angular momentum of a stationary rotating BH are defined by

\delta\mathcal{M}\!=\!\frac{1}{8\pi G}\!\int_{\infty}\!\delta\chi_{\xi}\left[{\partial_{t}}\right],\quad\delta\mathcal{J}\!=\!-\frac{1}{8\pi G}\!\int_{\infty}\!\delta\chi_{\xi}\left[{\partial_{\phi}}\right].

(12)

Comparing Eqs. (
10
) and (
11
) with the first law of thermodynamics, i.e.
T_{\mathcal{H}}\delta S=\delta{\mathcal{E}}-\Omega_{\mathcal{H}}\delta{\mathcal{J}},

the entropy of BH is also given by

S=\frac{2\pi}{\kappa}\int_{\mathcal{H}}Q_{\xi},

(13)

where
\kappa
is the surface gravity of the unperturbed BH. (For more details one can see e.g.
[
27
]
)

III
New Massive Gravity

NMG theory is described by the following action
[
12
]

\!\!\!\!\!I\!=\!\frac{1}{2\kappa}\!\int\!d^{3}x\sqrt{-g}\left[\sigma R\!-\!2\Lambda_{0}\!-\!\frac{1}{m^{2}}\!\Big(R_{\mu\nu}R^{\mu\nu}\!-\!\frac{3}{8}R^{2}\Big)\right]\!\!,

(14)

where
\kappa\!=\!8\pi G_{3}
,
\Lambda_{0}
is a cosmological constant and
m
is a mass parameter. Its CS Lagrangian is as follows
[
45
]

\!\!\!\!L\!=\!-\sigma e\cdot R+\frac{\Lambda_{0}}{6}e\cdot e\times e-\frac{1}{m^{2}}\!\left[h\!\cdot\!De\!+\!f\!\cdot\!\big(R\!+\!\frac{1}{2}e\!\times\!f\big)\!\right]\!\!,

(15)

where
\sigma\!=\!\pm 1
is a sign parameter and
\{e^{a},\omega^{a},f^{a},h^{a}\}
are 1-form dynamical fields (
a=0,1,2
).
R\!=\!d\omega+\frac{1}{2}\omega\times\omega
and
De\!=\!de+\omega\times e
are curvature 2-form and Lorentz covariant torsion, respectively. The field equations are

\displaystyle-\sigma R+\frac{\Lambda_{0}}{2}e\times e-\frac{1}{m^{2}}\big(Dh+\frac{1}{2}\,f\times f\big)=0,

(16a)

\displaystyle Df-m^{2}(e\times h)=0,

(16b)

\displaystyle R+e\times f=0,

(16c)

\displaystyle De=0.

(16d)

The 2-form symplectic potential from (
2
) is given by

\Theta=-\sigma\delta\omega\cdot e-\frac{1}{m^{2}}\left(\delta\omega\cdot f+\delta e\cdot h\right),

(17)

hence, the Noether current is

\displaystyle J_{\xi}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle dQ_{\xi}=\Theta(\Phi,\mathscr{L}_{\xi}\Phi)-i_{\xi}L

(18)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle d\left(-\sigma i_{\xi}\omega\cdot e-\frac{1}{m^{2}}\left(i_{\xi}\omega\cdot f+i_{\xi}e\cdot h\right)\right),

and the corresponding Noether charge becomes

Q=-\sigma i_{\xi}\omega\cdot e-\frac{1}{m^{2}}\left(i_{\xi}\omega\cdot f+i_{\xi}e\cdot h\right).

(19)

The variation of conserved charge from (
10
) yields

\displaystyle\delta\chi_{\xi}

\displaystyle\!\!\!=\!\!\!

\displaystyle-\sigma(i_{\xi}\omega\cdot\delta e+i_{\xi}e\cdot\delta\omega)

(20)

\displaystyle\!\!\!-\!\!\!

\displaystyle\frac{1}{m^{2}}\left(i_{\xi}\omega\cdot\delta f+i_{\xi}f\cdot\delta\omega+i_{\xi}e\cdot\delta h+i_{\xi}h\cdot\delta e\right).

For example, the mass and angular momentum of the BTZ BH have found in
[
27
]
as

M=\frac{r_{+}^{2}+r_{-}^{2}}{8G\ell^{2}}\Big(1-\frac{1}{\tilde{m}^{2}}\Big),\quad J=\frac{r_{+}r_{-}}{4G\ell}\Big(1-\frac{1}{\tilde{m}^{2}}\Big),

(21)

hereafter
r_{+}(r_{-})
refers to the outer (inner) horizon and
\tilde{m}^{2}\!=\!2m^{2}\ell^{2}
. However, the conserved charges of WAdS
3
BH have not been considered in
[
27
]
. Thus, in the following subsection we will investigate this case.

III.1
Conserved charges of WAdS
3
BH in NMG

The WAdS
3
geometry is a fibration of real line over AdS
2
with a constant warped factor which is not asymptotically AdS
3

[

,
21
,
47
,
48
]
. This breaks the

isometry group of AdS

\frac{ds^{2}}{\ell^{2}}=-F^{2}dt^{2}+\frac{dr^{2}}{4F^{2}K^{2}}+K^{2}\left(d\phi+Ndt\right)^{2},

(22)

where the metric functions
F(r),\,K(r)
, and
N(r)
are

\displaystyle\!\!\!\!F^{2}

\displaystyle\!=\!

\displaystyle\frac{(\nu^{2}\!+\!3)(r\!-\!r_{+})(r\!-\!r_{-})}{4K^{2}},\,N\!\!=\!\!\frac{2\nu r\!-\!\sqrt{(\nu^{2}\!+\!3)r_{+}r_{-}}}{2K^{2}},

\displaystyle\!\!\!\!K^{2}

\displaystyle\!\!=\!

\displaystyle\frac{r}{4}\!\left[3(\nu^{2}\!-\!1)\!+\!(\nu^{2}\!+\!3)(r_{+}\!+\!r_{-})\!-\!4\nu\sqrt{(\nu^{2}\!+\!3)r_{+}r_{-}}\right]\!.

\nu
is a dimensionless parameter in the warped factor. The metric (

) is a solution of Eqs. (
16a
)-(
16d
) if

\nu^{2}=\frac{\tilde{m}^{2}+3}{20},\quad\Lambda_{0}=\frac{\tilde{m}^{4}-234\,\tilde{m}^{2}+189}{200\,\tilde{m}^{2}\ell^{2}}.

(23)

The 1-form frame fields and spin connections compatible with (
22
) are given as follows

\displaystyle\begin{split}&e^{0}=\ell F\,dt,\,\,\,e^{1}=\frac{\ell\,dr}{2FK},\,\,\,e^{2}=\ell K(d\phi+N\,dt),&\\
&\omega^{0}\!=\!W_{1}e^{0}\!+\!W_{2}e^{2}\!,\,\,\,\omega^{1}\!=\!W_{3}e^{1}\!,\,\,\,\omega^{2}\!=\!W_{4}e^{2}\!+\!W_{5}e^{0}\!,\,\,\,&\end{split}

(24)

where the functions
W_{i}
(

\!\!\!\!\!\!W_{1}\!=\!W_{3}\!=\!-W_{4}\!=\!\frac{K^{2}N^{\prime}}{\ell},\,\,W_{2}\!=\!\!\frac{2FK^{\prime}}{\ell},\,\,W_{5}\!=\!\!\frac{2KF^{\prime}}{\ell}.

The auxiliary fields
f^{a}
and

are found from Eqs. (

) and (

) as

\displaystyle\begin{split}&\!\!\!\!\!\!f^{0}\!=\!U_{1}e^{0}+U_{2}e^{2},\,\,f^{1}\!=\!U_{3}e^{1},\,\,f^{2}\!=\!U_{4}e^{2}+U_{5}e^{0},&\\
&\!\!\!\!\!\!h^{0}\!=\!V_{1}e^{0}+V_{2}e^{2},\,\,h^{1}\!=\!V_{3}e^{1},\,\,h^{2}\!=\!V_{4}e^{2}+V_{5}e^{0},&\end{split}

(26)

with some lengthy exppressions for coefficient functions
U_{i}
and

that are given in the supplementary material for this letter. To calculate the charge variations (
12
) we need the interior products. For variation
{\partial_{t}}
we have

\displaystyle\begin{split}&\imath_{\xi}e^{0}=\ell F\,,\quad\imath_{\xi}e^{2}=\ell KN,&\\
&\imath_{\xi}\omega^{0}=\ell FW_{1}\!+\!\ell KNW_{2}\,,\,\,\imath_{\xi}\omega^{2}\!=\!\ell KNW_{4}\!+\!FW_{5},&\\
&\imath_{\xi}f^{0}=\ell FU_{1}\!+\!\ell KNU_{2},\,\,\imath_{\xi}f^{2}\!=\!\ell KNU_{4}\!+\!FU_{5},&\\
&\imath_{\xi}h^{0}=\ell FV_{1}\!+\!\ell KNV_{2},\,\,\imath_{\xi}h^{2}\!=\!\ell KNV_{4}\!+\!FV_{5},&\end{split}

(27)

and those for

\displaystyle\begin{split}&\imath_{\xi}e^{2}=\ell K,\,\,\imath_{\xi}\omega^{0}=\ell KW_{2},\,\,\imath_{\xi}\omega^{2}=\ell KW_{4},&\\

(28)

For variations at spatial boundaries on the Cauchy surface

\displaystyle\begin{split}&\delta e^{0}=0,\quad\delta e^{2}=\ell\delta(K)d\phi\,,&\\

Then, the

\displaystyle\!\!\!\!\delta\chi_{\xi}\left[{\partial_{t}}\right]\!=\!\alpha\,\nu\Big(\!(\delta r_{+}\!+\!\delta r_{-})\!-\!\tfrac{(\nu^{2}+3)}{2\nu}\tfrac{(r_{+}\delta r_{-}\!+r_{-}\delta r_{+})}{\sqrt{r_{+}r_{-}(\nu^{2}\!+\!3)}}\!\Big)d\phi,

\displaystyle\!=\!

\displaystyle\!\!\!J

\displaystyle\!=\!

IV
Extended New Massive Gravity

ENMG is a higher order curvature deformation of BHT gravity from holographic
c
-theorem in the context of AdS/CFT correspondence
[
14
]
. This theory is described by the following action

\displaystyle I

\displaystyle\!=\!

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2\kappa}\int d^{3}x\,\sqrt{-g}\Big[\sigma R-2\Lambda_{0}-\frac{1}{m^{2}}\Big(R_{\mu\nu}R^{\mu\nu}-\frac{3}{8}R^{2}\Big)

(32)

\displaystyle\!\!\!-\!\!\!

\displaystyle\frac{2}{3m^{4}}\Big(R_{\mu}^{\nu}R_{\nu}^{\rho}R_{\rho}^{\mu}-\frac{9}{8}RR_{\mu\nu}R^{\mu\nu}+\frac{17}{64}R^{3}\Big)\Big].

This action can also be found in the infinitesimal curvature expansion of a Born-Infeld-like action
[
15
]

\displaystyle S\!=\!\frac{2m^{2}}{\kappa}\!\int\!d^{3}x\Big[\sqrt{-|g_{\mu\nu}\!+\!\frac{\sigma}{m^{2}}\!G_{\mu\nu}|}\!-\!(1\!+\!\frac{\Lambda}{2m^{2}})\sqrt{-g}\Big]\!,

up to the corresponding order. The CS Lagrangian in the methodology of extended gravities
[
45
]
is given by

\displaystyle\!\!\!\!L

\displaystyle\!=\!

\displaystyle-\sigma\,e\cdot R-\frac{\Lambda_{0}}{6}\,e\cdot e\times e+\frac{1}{2m^{2}}e\cdot f\times f

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{m^{4}}\left[h\cdot De-\frac{1}{6}f\cdot f\times f+k\cdot\left(R+e\times f\right)\right]\!,

where

is a new auxiliary field in addition to the previous set. The ENMG fields equations are

\displaystyle\!\!\!\!\!\!\!\!\!\!\!-\sigma R-\frac{\Lambda_{0}}{2}e\times e\!+\!\frac{1}{2m^{2}}f\times f\!-\!\frac{1}{m^{4}}\big(Dh\!+\!f\!\times\!k\big)\!=\!0,

(34a)

\displaystyle Dk+e\times h+\sigma m^{4}De=0,

(34b)

\displaystyle e\times k-\frac{1}{2}f\times f-m^{2}e\times f=0,

(34c)

\displaystyle R+e\times f=0,

(34d)

\displaystyle De=0,

(34e)

and the symplectic potential is as follows

\Theta=-\sigma\delta\omega\cdot e-\tfrac{1}{m^{4}}\left(\delta e\cdot h+\delta\omega\cdot k\right).

(35)

Then, the Noether current becomes

which is associated to the Noether charge

Therefore, the charge variation (

yields

\displaystyle\!\!\!\!\!\!\delta\chi_{\xi}

\displaystyle\!=\!

\displaystyle-\sigma(i_{\xi}\omega\cdot\delta e+i_{\xi}e\cdot\delta\omega)

(37)

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{m^{4}}\!\left(i_{\xi}e\cdot\delta h\!+\!i_{\xi}h\cdot\delta e\!+\!i_{\xi}\omega\cdot\delta k\!+\!i_{\xi}k\cdot\delta\omega\right)\!.

IV.1
Conserved charges of BTZ BH in ENMG

The most well-known locally AdS
3
solution of all 3D gravities is the BTZ BH
[
9
]
with line element

ds^{2}=-F^{2}dt^{2}+F^{-2}{dr^{2}}+r^{2}\left(d\phi+Ndt\right)^{2},

(38)

where the metric functions
F(r)
and
N(r)
are

F^{2}\!=\!{(r^{2}\!-\!r_{+}^{2})(r^{2}\!-\!r_{-}^{2})}/{r^{2}\ell^{2}},\quad N\!=\!-{r_{+}r_{-}}/{\ell\,r^{2}},

(39)

and
\ell
is the radius of AdS
3
space. From (
38
) and Eq. (
34e
) the frame fileds and spin connections are

\displaystyle e^{0}=F\,dt\,,\quad e^{1}=\frac{dr}{F}\,,\quad e^{2}=r(d\phi+N\,dt),

VI
Conclusion

Our aim in this letter was devoted to investigate two related concepts for WAdS
3
and BTZ BHs in two diffeomorphism-invariant theories in 3D gravity, the so-called NMG and ENMG. In the former, we compute the masses and angular momenta of these BHs by employing the first order Wald formalism so that the variations of these charges are satisfied with the first law of the BH thermodynamics. In the latter, we compute the central charges of the CFT duals to these gravity models through the thermodynamics method of BH/CFT correspondence. In fact without knowing the explicit forms of BHs, the thermodynamics method could give consistent results with the ones obtained from ASG analysis or Clement’s approach.

In the context of CS formalism, we have described these 3D gravities with the Lagrangians consisting of frame fields, spin connections and some extra auxiliary fields. The conserved charges were calculated by integrating some charge variations
\delta\chi_{\xi}
over the boundary of Cauchy surface which are defined in terms of some symplectic potential
\Theta
and conserved Noether charge
Q_{\xi}
. In addition, we computed the entropy of BHs in Wald formalism by integrating the Noether charge over a biforcation horizon
\mathcal{H}
with killing vector
\xi\!=\!\partial_{t}\!+\!\Omega_{\mathcal{H}}\partial_{\phi}
.

Beside the quantities on the event horizon, we observed that thermodynamic quantities on the inner Cauchy horizon of all solutions satisfy the first law and Smarr relation as well. With explicit computations we showed that the product of BH entropies of the inner and outer horizons is independent of the mass which is compatible with the universality of diffeomorphism-invariant theories. On the other hand, we naively found that the thermodynamic quantities fulfill the condition
T^{+}S^{+}=T^{-}S^{-}
and therefore using the thermodynamic method lead to equal right- and left-moving central charges of CFT duals for BTZ and WAdS
3
BHs in ENMG theories.
