45 C.F.R. § 2400.48 — Fellows' participation in the Summer Institute.
Citation: us:cfr:45:2400.48
Creator: James Madison Memorial Fellowship Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2400.48
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2400 (Fellowship Program Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each fellow is required as part of his or her fellowship to attend the Institute (if it is offered), normally during the summer following the Fellow's commencement of graduate study under a fellowship.
