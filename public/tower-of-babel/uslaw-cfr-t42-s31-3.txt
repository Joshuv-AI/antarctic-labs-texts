42 C.F.R. § 31.3 — Use of Service facilities.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 31 (Medical Care For Certain Personnel Of The Coast Guard, National Ocean Survey, Public Health Service, And Former Lighthouse Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise provided in §§ 31.3 to 31.10, the persons specified in § 31.2 shall be entitled to medical, surgical, and dental treatment and hospitalization only at medical relief stations and by designated physicians and designated dentists, and the cost of services procured elsewhere shall not be borne by the Service.
