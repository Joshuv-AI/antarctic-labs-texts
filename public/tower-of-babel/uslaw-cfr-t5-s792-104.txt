5 C.F.R. § 792.104 — Responsibilities of the Office of Personnel Management.
Citation: us:cfr:5:792.104
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-792.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 792 (Federal Employees' Health, Counseling, And Work/Life Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

OPM shall provide overall leadership for the Government-wide alcoholism and drug abuse program in cooperation with the Secretary of Health and Human Services. To accomplish this, OPM shall develop and issue policy and program guidance, provide technical assistance to agencies, and determine the overall effectiveness of the Government-wide program, as well as those programs at individual agencies, based on program information required of agencies.
