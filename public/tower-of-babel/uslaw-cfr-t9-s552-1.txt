9 C.F.R. § 552.1 — Affixing stamps and marking products for export; issuance of export certificates; clearance of vessels and transportation.
Citation: us:cfr:9:552.1
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-552.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 552 (Exports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The manner of affixing stamps and marking products for export is that prescribed in § 322.1(a) of this chapter.
(b) The requirements for the issuance of export certificates are as prescribed in § 322.2 of this chapter.
(c) The requirements for clearing vessels and other transportation vehicles are set out in § 322.4 of this chapter.
