11 C.F.R. § 9039.1 — Retention of books and records.
Citation: us:cfr:11:9039.1
Creator: Federal Election Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-9039.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 9039 (Review And Investigation Authority). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The candidate and his or her authorized committee(s) shall keep all books, records and other information required under 11 CFR 9033.11, 9034.2 and part 9036 for a period of three years pursuant to 11 CFR 102.9(c) and shall furnish such books, records and information to the Commission on request.
