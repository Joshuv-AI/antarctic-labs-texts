42 C.F.R. § 408.28 — Increased premiums due to the income-related monthly adjustment amount (IRMAA).
Citation: us:cfr:42:408.28
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-408.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 408 (Premiums For Supplementary Medical Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Beginning January 1, 2007, Medicare beneficiaries must pay an income-related monthly adjustment amount in addition to the Part B (SMI) standard monthly premium, plus any applicable increase for late enrollment or reenrollment, if the beneficiary's modified adjusted gross income exceeds the threshold amounts specified in 20 CFR 418.1115.
