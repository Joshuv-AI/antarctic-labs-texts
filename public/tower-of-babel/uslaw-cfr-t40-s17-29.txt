40 C.F.R. § 17.29 — Payment of award.
Citation: us:cfr:40:17.29
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-17.29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 17 (Implementation Of The Equal Access To Justice Act In Epa Administrative Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An applicant seeking payment of an award shall submit a copy of the final decision granting the award to the Office of Financial Management for Processing. A statement that review of the underlying decision is not being sought in the United States courts or that the process for seeking review of the award has been completed must also be included.
