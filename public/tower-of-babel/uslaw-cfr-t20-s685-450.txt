20 C.F.R. § 685.450 — What is the Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act administrative cost limit for National Farmworker Jobs Program grants?
Citation: us:cfr:20:685.450
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-685.450
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 685 (National Farmworker Jobs Program Under Title I Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Under § 683.205(b) of this chapter, limits on administrative costs for programs operated under subtitle D of WIOA title I will be identified in the grant or contract award document. Administrative costs will not exceed 15 percent of total grantee funding.
