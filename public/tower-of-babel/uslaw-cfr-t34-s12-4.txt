34 C.F.R. § 12.4 — How does the Secretary provide notice of availability of surplus Federal real property?
Citation: us:cfr:34:12.4
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-12.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 12 (Disposal And Utilization Of Surplus Federal Real Property For Educational Purposes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary notifies potential applicants of the availability of surplus Federal real property for transfer for educational uses in accordance with 41 CFR 101-47.308-4.
