12 C.F.R. § 1015.10 — Actions by states.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1015.10
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1015.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1015 (Mortgage Assistance Relief Services (Regulation O)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any attorney general or other officer of a state authorized by the state to bring an action under this part may do so pursuant to section 626(b) of the 2009 Omnibus Appropriations Act, Public Law 111-8, section 626, 123 Stat. 524 (Mar. 11, 2009), as amended by Public Law 111-24, section 511, 123 Stat. 1734 (May 22, 2009), and as amended by Public Law 111-203, section 1097, 124 Stat. 2102 (July 21, 2010).
