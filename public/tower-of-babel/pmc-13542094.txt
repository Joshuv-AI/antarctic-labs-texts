Author Correction - with Update (U+C): Miniature and versatile genome regulation TnpB-ωRNA toolkits facilitate cancer immunotherapy
Authors: Junlin Lu, Jiajian Lai, Liang Cheng, Hengji Zhan, Kaiwen Jie, Chu Liu, Lin Huang, Meifeng Cen, Sen Liu, Zehua Chen, Qiang Zhang, Jianhui Zhang, Jingdie Wu, Bolin Pan, Siting Chen, Jianmei Zhong, Baoqing He, Hui Li, Xu Chen, Tianxin Lin
Published: 2026

Correction to: Nature Communications https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-71327-w, published online 1 April 2026

In the version of this article initially published, due to file preparation errors, the Source Data of the enTnpBa-seq5 group in Fig. 4f were identical to those of enTnpBa-seq5 group in Fig, 4j, while the Source Data of NT group and dTnpB-VPR group in Supplementary Fig. 1g were identical to those of NT group and V1 group in Supplementary Fig.1i. In the Source Data, the cycle threshold CT values in Fig. 4f–j and Supplementary Fig. 7a were submitted incorrectly. Upon reanalysis of the qPCR results, several fold-change values were found to be incorrectly listed in Fig. 4f (seq5 group), Fig. 4i (seq2 group) and Fig. 4j (CasMINIa-seq10 group). The corresponding bar plots are accurate, with all values presented to two decimal places in the original version. The H-scores of IFNγ and IL15 in Supplementary Fig. 7b were duplicated when preparing the Source Data table. The associated figures were reliable and generated from the correct original data. Figure 4j, Supplementary Fig. 1g and the Source Data are now updated in the HTML and PDF versions of the article. For comparison, the original figures are available as Supplementary Information accompanying this amendment.
