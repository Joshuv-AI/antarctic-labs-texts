42 C.F.R. § 485.701 — Basis and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:42:485.701
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-485.701
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 485 (Conditions Of Participation: Specialized Providers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart implements section 1861(p)(4) of the Act, which—
(a) Defines outpatient physical therapy and speech pathology services;
(b) Imposes requirements with respect to adequate program, facilities, policies, staffing, and clinical records; and
(c) Authorizes the Secretary to establish by regulation other health and safety requirements.
