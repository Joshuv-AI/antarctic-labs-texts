Exploring the metabolic landscape of pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma cells using genome-scale metabolic modeling
Authors: Mohammad Mazharul Islam, Andrea Goertzen, Pankaj K Singh, Rajib Saha
Published: 2023

Main text

(iScience 25, 104483; June 17, 2022)

In the original published version of this article, the authors reported three more genes to be essential than in the final model predictions, which is an error in the model simulations at the early manuscript preparation stage and was mistakenly included in the final version of the manuscript. The authors also listed SLC2A1 as an essential gene instead of SLC16A1, which is the correct essential gene. The authors have now revised the list of essential genes and the relevant text in the “potential drug repurposing” subsection in the “results and discussion” section of the manuscript. All authors agree to the correction and apologize for any inconvenience or confusion caused. The authors acknowledge Aadam Basrai for his help during the correction.
