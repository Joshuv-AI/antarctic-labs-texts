29 U.S.C. § 1323 — Plan fiduciaries
Citation: us:usc:29:1323
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section1323
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 18 (Employee Retirement Income Security Program); Subchapter III (Plan Termination Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter, a fiduciary of a plan to which section 1321 of this title applies is not in violation of the fiduciary’s duties as a result of any act or of any withholding of action required by this subchapter.
