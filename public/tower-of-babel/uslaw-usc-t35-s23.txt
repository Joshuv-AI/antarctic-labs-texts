35 U.S.C. § 23 — Testimony in Patent and Trademark Office cases
Citation: us:usc:35:23
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title35-section23
Context: United States Code, Title 35 (Patents); Part I (United States Patent And Trademark Office); Chapter 2 (Proceedings In The Patent And Trademark Office). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director may establish rules for taking affidavits and depositions required in cases in the Patent and Trademark Office. Any officer authorized by law to take depositions to be used in the courts of the United States, or of the State where he resides, may take such affidavits and depositions.
