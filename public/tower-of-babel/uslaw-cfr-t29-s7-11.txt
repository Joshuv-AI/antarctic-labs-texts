29 C.F.R. § 7.11 — Right to counsel.
Citation: us:cfr:29:7.11
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-7.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 7 (Practice Before The Administrative Review Board With Regard To Federal And Federally Assisted Construction Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each interested person or party shall have the right to appear in person or by or with counsel or other qualified representative in any proceeding before the Board.
