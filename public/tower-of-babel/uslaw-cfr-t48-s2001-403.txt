48 C.F.R. § 2001.403 — 2001.403 Individual deviations.

Creator: Chapter 20—Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2001.403
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2001 (Nuclear Regulatory Commission Acquisition Regulation System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In individual cases, deviations from either the FAR or the NRCAR will be authorized only when essential to effect only one contracting action or where special circumstances make the deviations clearly in the best interest of the Government. Individual deviations must be authorized in advance by the Director, Division of Contracts and Property Management.
