5 C.F.R. § 837.305 — Lump-sum credit not reduced.
Citation: us:cfr:5:837.305
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-837.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 837 (Reemployment Of Annuitants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When annuity continues during the period of reemployment, and the reemployment is subject to annuity offset under the provisions of § 837.303 of this subpart, or any similar provision of law or regulation, the amount of an annuitant's lump-sum credit to the Fund shall not be reduced by the amount of annuity allocable to the period of reemployment.
