46 C.F.R. § 308.203 — Amount insured under interim binder.
Citation: us:cfr:46:308.203
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-308.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 308 (War Risk Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The amount insured shall be the amount stated in the application, but not in excess of $750 per gross ton of the vessel.
