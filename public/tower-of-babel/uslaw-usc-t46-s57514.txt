46 U.S.C. § 57514 — Qualifications of bidders

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part F (Government-Owned Merchant Vessels); Chapter 575 (Construction, Charter, And Sale Of Vessels); Subchapter II (Charters). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Considerations.—
In deciding whether to award a charter to a bidder, the Secretary of Transportation shall consider—
(1)
the bidder’s financial resources, credit standing, and practical experience in operating vessels; and
(2)
other factors a prudent business person would consider in entering into a transaction involving a large capital investment.
(b) Disqualifications.—
The Secretary may not charter a vessel to a person appearing to lack sufficient capital, credit, and experience to operate the vessel successfully over the period covered by the charter.
