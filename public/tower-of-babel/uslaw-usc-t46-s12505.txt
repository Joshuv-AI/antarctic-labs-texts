46 U.S.C. § 12505 — Fees
Citation: us:usc:46:12505
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section12505
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part H (Identification Of Vessels); Chapter 125 (Vessel Identification System). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Secretary of Transportation may charge a fee under section 9701 of title 31 for providing information to or requesting information from the vessel identification system, except to—
(1)
an agency; or
(2)
a State making information available to the Secretary under section 12503(a) of this title.
(b)
The Secretary shall deposit amounts transferred or collected under this section in the general fund of the Treasury as proprietary receipts of the Secretary and ascribed to the vessel identification system.
