20 C.F.R. § 345.306 — Availability of information.
Citation: us:cfr:20:345.306
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-345.306
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 345 (Employers' Contributions And Contribution Reports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon request of an employer subject to this part, the Board will make available to such employer any information that is necessary to verify the accuracy of its rate of contribution, as determined by the Board, including information necessary to verify the accuracy of the data maintained by the Board in the employer's individual employer record.
