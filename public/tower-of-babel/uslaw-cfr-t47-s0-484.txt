47 C.F.R. § 0.484 — Amateur radio operator examinations.
Citation: us:cfr:47:0.484
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-0.484
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 0 (Commission Organization). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Generally, examinations for amateur radio operation licenses shall be administered at locations and times specified by volunteer examiners. (See § 97.509). When the FCC conducts examinations for amateur radio operator licenses, they shall take place at locations and times designated by the FCC.
