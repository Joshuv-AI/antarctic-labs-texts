40 C.F.R. § 13.40 — Stay of offset.
Citation: us:cfr:40:13.40
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-13.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 13 (Claims Collection Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the debtor timely notifies the EPA that he or she is exercising the right described in § 13.38(a) and timely submits evidence in accordance with § 13.38(b), any notice to the IRS will be stayed until the issuance of a written decision which sustains or amends its original determination.
