12 C.F.R. § 192.20 — Acquiring another insured depository institution upon conversion.
Citation: us:cfr:12:192.20
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-192.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 192 (Conversions From Mutual To Stock Form). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a savings association converts to stock form, it may acquire for cash or stock another insured depository institution that is already in the stock form of ownership.
