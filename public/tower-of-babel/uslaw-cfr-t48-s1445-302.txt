48 C.F.R. § 1445.302 — 1445.302 Contracts with foreign governments or international organizations.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1445.302
Creator: Chapter 14—Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1445 (Government Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The HCA, after coordinating with the cognizant PMO, shall establish procedures to recover use costs when foreign governments or international organizations request use of Government production and research property
