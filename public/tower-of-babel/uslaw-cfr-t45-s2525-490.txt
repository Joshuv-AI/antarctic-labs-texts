45 C.F.R. § 2525.490 — Is a recipient of a transferred education award eligible for the payment of accrued interest for their own student loans?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2525.490
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2525.490
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2525 (National Service Trust). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. The transfer of an education award does not convey eligibility for payment of accrued interest under subpart E of this part.
