49 C.F.R. § 1103.13 — Attempts to exert political or personal influence on the Board are prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1103.13
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1103.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1103 (Practitioners). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) It is unethical for a practitioner to attempt to influence the judgment of the Board by threats of political or personal reprisal.
(b) Marked attention and unusual hospitality on the part of a practitioner to a Board Member, administrative law judge, or other representative of the Board, which is unwarranted by the personal relationship of the parties, is subject to misconstruction of motive and should be avoided.
