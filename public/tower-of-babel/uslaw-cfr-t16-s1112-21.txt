16 C.F.R. § 1112.21 — May a third party conformity assessment body use testing methods other than those specified in the relevant CPSC rule or test method?
Citation: us:cfr:16:1112.21
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1112.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1112 (Requirements Pertaining To Third Party Conformity Assessment Bodies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the CPSC has specified a test method, a third party conformity assessment body must use that test method for any tests conducted for purposes of section 14 of the CPSA.
