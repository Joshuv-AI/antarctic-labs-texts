7 C.F.R. § 2811.6 — Fee schedule.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 2811 (Availability Of Information To The Public). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Department regulations provide for a schedule of reasonable standard charges for document search and duplication. See 7 CFR 1.2(b). Fees to be charged are set forth in 7 CFR part 1, subpart A, appendix A.
