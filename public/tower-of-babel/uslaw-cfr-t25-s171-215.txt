25 C.F.R. § 171.215 — What if the elevation of my farm unit is too high to receive irrigation water?
Citation: us:cfr:25:171.215
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-171.215
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 171 (Irrigation Operation And Maintenance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) We will not change our service ditch level to provide service to you.
(b) You may install, operate, and maintain your own facilities, at your cost, to provide service to your land:
(1) From a delivery point we designate; and
(2) In accordance with specifications we approve.
