19 U.S.C. § 4333 — Training
Citation: us:usc:19:4333
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section4333
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 28 (Trade Facilitation And Trade Enforcement); Subchapter II (Import Health And Safety). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commissioner shall ensure that personnel of U.S. Customs and Border Protection assigned to United States ports of entry are trained to effectively administer the provisions of this subchapter and to otherwise assist in ensuring the safety of merchandise imported into the United States and the expeditious entry of such merchandise.
