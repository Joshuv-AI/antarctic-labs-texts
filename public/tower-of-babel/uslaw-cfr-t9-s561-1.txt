9 C.F.R. § 561.1 — Rules of practice governing inspection actions.
Citation: us:cfr:9:561.1
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-561.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 561 (Rules Of Practice). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules of practice in part 500 of this chapter, governing inspection actions taken by FSIS with respect to establishments and products, apply to actions taken with respect to fish slaughter, fish processing, fish, and fish products regulated under this subchapter.
