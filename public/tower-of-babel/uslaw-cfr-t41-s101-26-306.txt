41 C.F.R. § 101-26.306 — -26.306 Planned requisitioning for GSA stock items.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-26.306
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-26.306
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-26 (26—Procurement Sources And Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In preparing requisitions for GSA stock items, agencies shall follow schedules or cyclical plans for replenishment of stocks so as to reduce the number of repetitive requisitions required while adjusting ordering frequency to comply with the economic order quantity principle. (See § 101-27.102.)
