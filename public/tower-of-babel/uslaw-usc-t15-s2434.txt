15 U.S.C. § 2434 — Internal productivity
Citation: us:usc:15:2434
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section2434
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 51 (National Productivity And Quality Of Working Life); Subchapter III (Federal Agency Coordination And Liaison With Center). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each department, agency, and independent establishment of the Federal Government shall identify, develop, initiate, and support appropriate programs, systems, procedures, policies, and techniques to improve the productivity of such departments and agencies, including the implementation, where desirable, of specific programs recommended, supported, or implemented by the Center.
