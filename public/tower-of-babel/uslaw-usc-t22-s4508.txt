22 U.S.C. § 4508 — Allocation of funds
Citation: us:usc:22:4508
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section4508
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 55 (Research And Training For Eastern Europe And Independent States Of Former Soviet Union). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Of the funds authorized to be appropriated by section 102(1) of this Act—
(1)
up to $5,000,000 for the fiscal year 1984 shall be available to carry out this chapter; and
(2)
$5,000,000 for the fiscal year 1985 shall be available only to carry out this chapter.
