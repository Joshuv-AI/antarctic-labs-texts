46 C.F.R. § 531.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:46:531.1
Creator: Federal Maritime Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-531.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 531 (Nvocc Service Arrangements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to facilitate NVOCC Service Arrangements (“NSAs”) as they are exempt from the otherwise applicable provisions of the Shipping Act of 1984 (“the Act”).
