15 C.F.R. § 995.16 — Term of certification.
Citation: us:cfr:15:995.16
Creator: National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-995.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 995 (Certification Requirements For Distributors Of Noaa Hydrographic Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term of certification is five years from the date of issuance. At the end of the certification term, the process must be re-certified by the distributor or value added distributor submitting a request for certification as described in § 995.10. It may also be revoked prior to the end of the term under the conditions described in § 995.15.
