The B + → K + ​ ν ​ ν ¯ B^{+}\to K^{+}\nu\bar{\nu} decay as a search for the QCD axion Preprint: LAPTH-036/25
Merna Abumusabh, Giulio Dujany, Diego Guadagnoli, Axel Iohner, Claudio Toni

Abstract
We reinterpret the B + → K + ​ ν ​ ν ¯ B^{+}\to K^{+}\nu\bar{\nu} measurement as a probe of the decay B + → K + ​ a B^{+}\to K^{+}a , where a a denotes an axion or axion-like particle. This is possible in the kinematic regions where the di-neutrino invariant mass squared, q 2 q^{2} , can be identified with the assumed m a 2 m_{a}^{2} . While q 2 q^{2} is reconstructed only in the Hadronic Tag Analysis (HTA), it is not directly accessible in the Inclusive Tag Analysis (ITA), which provides much larger statistics and hence higher sensitivity. In ITA, q 2 q^{2} is proxied by a reconstructed variable, q rec 2 q^{2}_{\text{rec}} , usually modeled through Belle II simulations. We show that the mapping between q 2 q^{2} and q rec 2 q^{2}_{\text{rec}} can be accurately derived from kinematic arguments alone—without relying on internal experimental inputs. Using this relation and publicly available efficiencies, we obtain the strongest existing bounds on the coupling-rescaled Peccei-Quinn scale, | ( F V ) s ​ b | ≥ 0.9 × 10 9 |(F_{V})_{sb}|\geq 0.9\times 10^{9} GeV, improving the latest bound in the literature by an order of magnitude. We further show that the bound depends only marginally on the assumed value of the di-neutrino signal strength μ ν ​ ν ¯ \mu_{\nu\bar{\nu}} , whether fixed or floated. This establishes B + → K + ​ ν ​ ν ¯ B^{+}\to K^{+}\nu\bar{\nu} as a double probe—of new short-distance physics in the B + → K + ​ ν ​ ν ¯ B^{+}\to K^{+}\nu\bar{\nu} amplitude, and of new light, elusive particles produced via B + → K + ​ a B^{+}\to K^{+}a —the two probes working independently to an excellent approximation.

In conclusion, we reinterpret the
B^{+}\to K^{+}\nu\bar{\nu}
measurement as a probe of
B^{+}\to K^{+}a
, with
a
an axion (
m_{a}\simeq 0
) or ALP (
m_{a}>0
). We derive analytically the mapping between the measured
q^{2}_{\text{rec}}
and the true
q^{2}
, allowing the Inclusive Tag Analysis to constrain axion emission without relying on internal simulations. Using public efficiencies, we set the strongest existing limit on the Peccei–Quinn scale rescaled by the fundamental axion coupling relevant to our channel.
The result is largely independent of the assumed
\mu_{\nu\bar{\nu}}
, making
B^{+}\to K^{+}\nu\bar{\nu}
a dual probe of short-distance new physics in the
B^{+}\to K^{+}\nu\bar{\nu}
amplitude
and
of the production of an undetected axion. This approach can be generalized to
B^{+}\to K^{*+}\nu\bar{\nu}
—and to yet other channels—as soon as Belle II measurements become available.
