17 C.F.R. § 242.505 — Exclusion for news media.
Citation: us:cfr:17:242.505
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-242.505
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 242 (Regulations M, Sho, Ats, Ac, Nms, Se, And Sbsr, And Customer Margin Requirements For Security Futures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No provision of this Regulation AC shall apply to any person who:
(a) Is the publisher of any bona fide newspaper, news magazine or business or financial publication of general and regular circulation; and
(b) Is not registered or required to be registered with the Commission as a broker or dealer or investment adviser.
