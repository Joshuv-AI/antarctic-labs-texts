28 C.F.R. § 42.715 — Burden of proof regarding exceptions.
Citation: us:cfr:28:42.715
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-42.715
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 42 (Nondiscrimination; Equal Employment Opportunity; Policies And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The burden of proving that an age distinction or other action falls within the exceptions described in § 42.712 and § 42.713 is on the recipient. This allocation of the burden of proof applies in proceedings by the Department to enforce the Act.
