9 C.F.R. § 316.6 — Products not to be removed from official establishments unless marked in accordance with the regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:9:316.6
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-316.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 316 (Marking Products And Their Containers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person shall remove or cause to be removed from an official establishment any products which the regulations in this subchapter require to be marked in any way unless they are clearly and legibly marked in compliance with such regulations.
