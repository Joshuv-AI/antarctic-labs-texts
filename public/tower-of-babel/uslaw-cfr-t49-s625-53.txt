49 C.F.R. § 625.53 — Recordkeeping for transit asset management.
Citation: us:cfr:49:625.53
Creator: Federal Transit Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-625.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 625 (Transit Asset Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) At all times, each provider must maintain records and documents that support, and set forth in full, its TAM plan.
(b) A provider must make its TAM plan, any supporting records or documents performance targets, investment strategies, and the annual condition assessment report available to a State and Metropolitan Planning Organization that provides funding to the provider to aid in the planning process.
