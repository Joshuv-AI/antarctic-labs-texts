24 U.S.C. § 15 — Superintendence of Navy hospitals
Citation: us:usc:24:15
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title24-section15
Context: United States Code, Title 24 (Hospitals And Asylums); Chapter 1 (Navy Hospitals, Army And Navy Hospital, And Hospital Relief For Seamen And Others). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Navy shall have the general charge and superintendence of Navy hospitals.
