42 C.F.R. § 52e.3 — e.3 Who is eligible to apply?
Citation: us:cfr:42:52e.3
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-52e.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 52e (PART 52e—NATIONAL HEART, LUNG, AND BLOOD INSTITUTE GRANTS FOR PREVENTION AND CONTROL PROJECTS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To be eligible for a grant under this part, an applicant must be a public or nonprofit private agency or institution.
