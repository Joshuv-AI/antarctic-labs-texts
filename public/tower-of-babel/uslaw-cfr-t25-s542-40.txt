25 C.F.R. § 542.40 — What is a Tier C gaming operation?
Citation: us:cfr:25:542.40
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-542.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 542 (Minimum Internal Control Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Tier C gaming operation is one with annual gross gaming revenues of more than $15 million.
