48 C.F.R. § 47.104-5 — 47.104-5 Citation of Government rate tenders.
Citation: us:cfr:48:47.104-5
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-47.104-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 47 (Transportation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When 49 U.S.C. 10721 and 13712 rates apply, transportation offices or contractors, as appropriate, shall identify the applicable Government rate tender by endorsement on bills of lading.
