9 C.F.R. § 559.2 — Articles or fish subject to judicial seizure and condemnation.
Citation: us:cfr:9:559.2
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-559.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 559 (Detention, Seizure, Condemnation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of 9 CFR 329.6 through 329.8 governing the judicial seizure and condemnation of carcasses, parts, meat, and meat food products of livestock apply also with respect to the carcasses, parts, and products of fish.
