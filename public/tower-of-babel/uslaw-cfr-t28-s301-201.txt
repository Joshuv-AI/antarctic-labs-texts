28 C.F.R. § 301.201 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:28:301.201
Creator: Federal Prison Industries, Inc., Department Of Justice
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 301 (Inmate Accident Compensation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Lost-time wages shall be available only for inmates based at Bureau of Prisons institutions.
