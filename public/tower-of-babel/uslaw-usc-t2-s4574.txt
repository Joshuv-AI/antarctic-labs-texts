2 U.S.C. § 4574 — Limit on rate of compensation of Senate officers and employees
Citation: us:usc:2:4574
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section4574
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 45 (Congressional Pay And Benefits); Subchapter III (Senate); Part A (Amount And Type). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No officer or employee of the Senate shall receive pay for any services performed by him at any rate higher than that provided for the office or employment to which he has been regularly appointed.
