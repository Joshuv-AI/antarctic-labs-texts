45 C.F.R. § 2521.50 — If I am an Indian Tribe, to what extent may I use tribal funds towards my share of costs?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2521.50
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2521.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2521 (Eligible Americorps Subtitle C Program Applicants And Types Of Grants Available For Award). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you are an Indian Tribe that receives tribal funds through Public Law 93-638 (the Indian Self-Determination and Education Assistance Act), those funds are considered non-Federal and you may use them towards your share of costs, including member support costs.
