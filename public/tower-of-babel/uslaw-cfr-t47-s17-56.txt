47 C.F.R. § 17.56 — Maintenance of lighting equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:47:17.56
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-17.56
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 17 (Construction, Marking, And Lighting Of Antenna Structures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Replacing or repairing of lights, automatic indicators or automatic control or alarm systems shall be accomplished as soon as practicable.
