14 C.F.R. § 133.51 — Airworthiness certification.
Citation: us:cfr:14:133.51
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-133.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 133 (Rotorcraft External-Load Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Rotorcraft External-Load Operator Certificate is a current and valid airworthiness certificate for each rotorcraft type certificated under part 27 or 29 of this chapter (or their predecessor parts) and listed by registration number on a list attached to the certificate, when the rotorcraft is being used in operations conducted under this part.
