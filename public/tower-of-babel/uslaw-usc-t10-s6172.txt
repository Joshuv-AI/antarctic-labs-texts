10 U.S.C. § 6172 — Classification of defense environmental cleanup as capital asset projects or operations activities
Citation: us:usc:10:6172
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section6172
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part VI (Elements Of Department Of Defense And Other Matters); Subpart B (Atomic Energy Defense); Chapter 604 (Defense Environmental Cleanup Matters); Subchapter I (Defense Environmental Cleanup). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Assistant Secretary of Energy for Environmental Management, in consultation with other appropriate officials of the Department of Energy, shall establish requirements for the classification of defense environmental cleanup projects as capital asset projects or operations activities.
