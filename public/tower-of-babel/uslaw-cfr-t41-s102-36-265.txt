41 C.F.R. § 102-36.265 — -36.265 Transferring or exchanging excess medical shelf-life items with other Federal agencies.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-36.265
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-36.265
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-36 (36—Disposition Of Excess Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may transfer or exchange excess medical shelf-life items held for national emergency purposes with any other Federal agency for other medical materials or supplies, without GSA approval and without regard to part 102-39 of this subchapter. You and the transferee agency will agree to the terms and prices. You may credit any proceeds derived from such transactions to your agency's current applicable appropriation and use the funds only for the purchase of medical materials or supplies for national emergency purposes.
