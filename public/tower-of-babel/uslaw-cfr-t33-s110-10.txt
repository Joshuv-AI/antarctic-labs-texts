33 C.F.R. § 110.10 — Portsmouth Harbor, New Hampshire, north of Newcastle Island.
Citation: us:cfr:33:110.10
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-110.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 110 (Anchorage Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

From the northern most point of Goat Island to latitude 43°04′25″ N, longitude 070°43′37″ W; thence 089°30′ for 1025 yards; thence 120° for 285 yards, thence 213° to the shoreline of Newcastle Island, thence along the shoreline of Newcastle Island and across the breakwater to Goat Island and to the point of beginning.
