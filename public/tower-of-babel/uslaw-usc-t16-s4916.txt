16 U.S.C. § 4916 — Relationship to State law
Citation: us:usc:16:4916
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section4916
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 69 (Wild Exotic Bird Conservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter may be construed as precluding the regulation under State law of the sale, transfer, or possession of exotic birds if such regulation—
(1)
does not authorize any sale, transfer, or possession of exotic birds that is prohibited under this chapter; and
(2)
is consistent with the international obligations of the United States.
