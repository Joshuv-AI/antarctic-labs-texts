39 C.F.R. § 953.11 — Proposed findings of fact and conclusions of law.
Citation: us:cfr:39:953.11
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-953.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 953 (Mailability). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Proposed findings of fact and conclusions of law shall be submitted orally or in writing at the conclusion of the hearing, or otherwise, as ordered by the presiding officer.
