23 C.F.R. § 140.607 — Construction.
Citation: us:cfr:23:140.607
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-140.607
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 140 (Reimbursement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Construction shall be supervised by the SHA in the same manner as for regularly financed Federal-aid projects. The FHWA will make construction inspections and reports.
