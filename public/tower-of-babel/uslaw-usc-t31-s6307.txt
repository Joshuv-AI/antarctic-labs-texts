31 U.S.C. § 6307 — Interpretative guidelines and exemptions
Citation: us:usc:31:6307
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section6307
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 63 (Using Procurement Contracts And Grant And Cooperative Agreements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of the Office of Management and Budget may—
(1)
issue supplementary interpretative guidelines to promote consistent and efficient use of procurement contracts, grant agreements, and cooperative agreements; and
(2)
exempt a transaction or program of an executive agency from this chapter.
