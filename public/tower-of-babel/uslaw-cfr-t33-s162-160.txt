33 C.F.R. § 162.160 — Vermilion, Lorain, Cleveland, Fairport, Ashtabula, and Conneaut Harbors, Ohio.
Citation: us:cfr:33:162.160
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-162.160
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 162 (Inland Waterways Navigation Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In Vermilion Harbor, no vessel may exceed 6 miles per hour.
(b) In Lorain, Cleveland, Fairport, Ashtabula, and Conneaut Harbors, no vessel may exceed 6 miles per hour, except in the outer harbors, where no vessel may exceed 10 miles per hour.
