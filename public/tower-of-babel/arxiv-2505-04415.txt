Regularity of the variance in quenched CLT for random intermittent dynamical systems
Davor Dragičević, Juho Leppänen

Abstract
We study random dynamical systems composed of Liverani–Saussol–Vaienti maps with varying parameters, without any mixing assumptions on the base space of random dynamics. We establish a quenched central limit theorem and identify conditions under which the associated limit variance varies continuously and differentiably with respect to perturbations of the random dynamics. Our arguments rely on recent results on statistical stability and linear response for random intermittent maps established in Dragičević et al. (J. Lond. Math. Soc. 111 (2025), e70150).



Keywords
: linear response; random dynamical systems; intermittent maps; variance.

Introduction

In their seminal paper
[
31
]
, Liverani, Saussol and Vaienti introduced a now famous class of intermittent maps (called LSV maps) on the unit interval.
Trajectories of these nonuniformly expanding maps behave chaotically on a large part of
the state space, but when they visit small neighborhoods of the neutral fixed point at the origin, it
takes a long time before they return to the chaotic regime. We stress that the study of statistical properties of such maps turned out to be a challenging problem that resulted in the development of new techniques. For example, it turned out that such maps exhibit only polynomial decay of correlations, while uniformly expanding (or hyperbolic) maps possess exponential decay of correlations. We refer to
[
1
]
for a detailed survey.

In recent years, there has been a growing interest in the study of statistical properties of nonstationary (or nonautonomous) dynamical systems formed by sequential or random compositions of LSV maps with varying parameters. Notable contributions to their study include estimates on memory loss and decay of correlations (see, for example,
[
2
,
5
,
6
,
26
,
27
,
28
]
), various limit laws (see
[
3
,
4
,
11
,
24
,
32
,
33
,
36
,
37
]
and references therein) and linear response
[
8
,

]
.

In this paper, we first establish the quenched central limit theorem (CLT) for random compositions of LSV maps
with varying parameters. Previous results on the quenched CLT in this setting include
[

,
33
]
for
i.i.d. compositions

[

for ergodic compositions. Our theorem (see Theorem

We deduce the quenched CLT from an abstract result

which, to our knowledge, has not previously been applied in this context. This result can be seen as a natural development in the recent line of research devoted to quenched limit theorems for broad classes of random dynamical systems that avoid any mixing assumptions for the base system (see

Our main objective is to study the regularity of the limit variance in Theorem

Theorem 3
.

Let
\Phi\colon\Omega\times[0,1]\to\mathbb{R}
be a measurable map such that
\varphi_{\omega}:=\Phi(\omega,\cdot)\in C^{1}[0,1]
for
\omega\in\Omega
and that

(5)

\omega\mapsto\|\varphi_{\omega}\|_{C^{1}}\in L^{2}(\Omega,\mathcal{F},\mathbb{P}).

Moreover, assume that one of the following two conditions holds:

(i)

\alpha<1/2
.

(ii)

\alpha\geq 1/2
and,
for
x\in[0,1]
and
{\mathbb{P}}
-a.e.
\omega\in\Omega
,

(6)

|\varphi_{\omega}(x)|\leq C_{\omega}x^{\beta}\quad\text{and}\quad\int_{0}^{1}\varphi_{\omega}h_{\omega}\,dm=0

hold with
\beta>2\alpha-1
, where
\omega\mapsto C_{\omega}\in L^{2}(\Omega,\mathcal{F},{\mathbb{P}})
.

Then, for
\mathbb{P}
-a.e.
\omega\in\Omega
,

\frac{\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}(\varphi_{\sigma^{i}\omega}\circ T_{\omega}^{i}-\int_{0}^{1}\varphi_{\sigma^{i}\omega}\,d\mu_{\sigma^{i}\omega})}{\sqrt{n}}\to_{d}\mathcal{N}(0,\Sigma^{2})\quad\text{on $([0,1],\mu_{\omega})$,}

where

\Sigma^{2}:=\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}\varphi_{\omega.0}^{2}h_{\omega}\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)+2\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}(\varphi_{\omega,0}h_{\omega})\cdot(\varphi_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}\circ T_{\omega}^{n})\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega),

and
\varphi_{\omega,0}:=\varphi_{\omega}-\int_{0}^{1}\varphi_{\omega}h_{\omega}\,dm
. Here
\mathcal{N}(0,\Sigma^{2})
denotes the normal distribution with mean
0
and variance
\Sigma^{2}
provided that
\Sigma^{2}>0
, and the unit mass in
0
in the case
\Sigma^{2}=0
.

Proof.

First, we assume that (i) holds.
We will verify the assumptions of
[
25
, Theorem 2.3.]
with

(i.e., when there is no inducing involved) and with the fiberwise centered observable.
Note that
\varphi_{\omega,0}\in C^{1}[0,1]
for
\omega\in\Omega
.
Since

\left|\int_{0}^{1}\varphi_{\omega}\,d\mu_{\omega}\right|=\left|\int_{0}^{1}\varphi_{\omega}h_{\omega}\,dm\right|\leq\int_{0}^{1}|\varphi_{\omega}|h_{\omega}\,dm\leq\|\varphi_{\omega}\|_{C^{1}}\int_{0}^{1}h_{\omega}\,dm=\|\varphi_{\omega}\|_{C^{1}},

we have that
\|\varphi_{\omega,0}\|_{C^{1}}\leq 2\|\varphi_{\omega}\|_{C^{1}}
, and thus from (
5
) we get that

(7)

\omega\mapsto\|\varphi_{\omega,0}\|_{C^{1}}\in L^{2}(\Omega,\mathcal{F},\mathbb{P}).

Observing that
(\mathbb{E}_{\mu_{\omega}}|\varphi_{\omega,0}|^{2})^{1/2}\leq\|\varphi_{\omega,0}\|_{C^{1}}
, we see that (
7
) implies
[
25

Next, we aim to verify that
[
25
, Theorem 2.3 (i)]
is true. Using (
2
) we have that

(8)

\begin{split}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left|\mathbb{E}_{\mu_{\omega}}(\varphi_{\omega,0}\cdot(\varphi_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}\circ T_{\omega}^{n}))\right|&=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left|\int_{0}^{1}\varphi_{\omega,0}h_{\omega}(\varphi_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}\circ T_{\omega}^{n})\,dm\right|\\

&\leq C_{\alpha}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}n^{-1/\alpha+1}\|\varphi_{\omega,0}\|_{C^{1}}\cdot\|h_{\omega}\|_{L^{1}(m)}\cdot\|\varphi_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}\|_{C^{1}}\\

Consequently, using Hölder’s inequality and that

preserves
\mathbb{P}
, we have that

\begin{split}\mathbb{E}_{\mathbb{P}}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left|\mathbb{E}_{\mu_{\omega}}(\varphi_{\omega,0}\cdot(\varphi_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}\circ T_{\omega}^{n}))\right|&\leq C_{\alpha}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}n^{-1/\alpha+1}\mathbb{E}_{\mathbb{P}}(\|\varphi_{\omega,0}\|_{C^{1}}\cdot\|\varphi_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}\|_{C^{1}})\\

which is finite due to (

(ii) is new in the setting of random dynamics.
For a single LSV map
T_{\alpha}
with parameter

1/2<\alpha<1
and absolutely continuous invariant probability measure
\mu
, Gouëzel
[
22
]

proved that
n^{-1/2}\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}\varphi\circ T_{\alpha}^{n}

converges weakly to a normal distribution on
([0,1],\mu)
, provided that
\varphi
is Hölder continuous
with
\int\varphi\,d\mu=0
and

|\varphi(x)|\leq Cx^{\beta}
holds for
\beta>\alpha-1/2
. A corresponding weak invariance principle was proved by Dedecker
and Merlevède
[
13
]
.

3
Continuity of the variance

We are interested in studying the regularity of the variance
\Sigma^{2}
in Theorem
3
under suitable perturbations of random dynamics.

Let us start by introducing the appropriate setup. We take
\varepsilon_{0}>0
and measurable maps
\beta\colon\Omega\to(0,1)
and
\delta\colon\Omega\to(0,1)
such that

\alpha:=\esssup_{\omega}\beta(\omega)+\varepsilon_{0}<\frac{1}{2}\quad\text{and}\quad\essinf_{\omega}\beta(\omega)-\varepsilon_{0}>0.

For
\omega\in\Omega
and
\varepsilon\in(-\varepsilon_{0},\varepsilon_{0})
let
T_{\omega,\varepsilon}
be an LSV map with parameter
\beta(\omega)+\varepsilon\delta(\omega)
. We will write
T_{\omega}
instead of
T_{\omega,0}
. By
\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}
we will denote the transfer operator associated with
T_{\omega,\varepsilon}
, again writing
\mathcal{L}_{\omega}
instead of
\mathcal{L}_{\omega,0}
.
By
(\mu_{\omega,\varepsilon})_{\omega\in\Omega}
we will denote the random a.c.i.m associated with the cocycle
(T_{\omega,\varepsilon})_{\omega\in\Omega}
. We will write
\mu_{\omega}
instead of
\mu_{\omega,0}
. By
h_{\omega,\varepsilon}\in\mathcal{C}_{*}\cap\mathcal{C}_{2}
, we denote the density of
\mu_{\omega,\varepsilon}
(again writing
h_{\omega}
instead of
h_{\omega,0}
). We need the following consequence of
[
17
, Theorem 13]
.

Proposition 5
.

There exists
C_{\alpha}>0
such that for
\mathbb{P}
-a-e.
\omega\in\Omega
,

\|h_{\omega,\varepsilon}-h_{\omega}\|_{L^{1}(m)}\leq C_{\alpha}|\varepsilon|,\quad\varepsilon\in(-\varepsilon_{0},\varepsilon_{0}).

Let
F\colon\Omega\times[0,1]\to\mathbb{R}
be a measurable map such that
F(\omega,\cdot)\in C^{1}[0,1]
for
\omega\in\Omega
and

(11)

\esssup_{\omega\in\Omega}\|F(\omega,\cdot)\|_{C^{1}}<+\infty.

In the sequel, we will denote
F_{\omega}:=F(\omega,\cdot)
. Set

f_{\omega,\varepsilon}:=F_{\omega}-\int_{0}^{1}F_{\omega}\,d_{\mu_{\omega,\varepsilon}},\quad\omega\in\Omega,\ \varepsilon\in(-\varepsilon_{0},\varepsilon_{0}).

Moreover, let

(12)

\Sigma_{\varepsilon}^{2}:=\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{2}\,d_{\mu_{\omega,\varepsilon}}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)+2\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,\varepsilon}\cdot(f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}\circ T_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n})\,d\mu_{\omega,\varepsilon}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega).

By Theorem
3
,
\Sigma_{\varepsilon}^{2}
is finite for
\varepsilon\in(-\varepsilon_{0},\varepsilon_{0})
.
Then, we have the following theorem.

Theorem 6
.

The map
\varepsilon\mapsto\Sigma_{\varepsilon}^{2}
is continuous in
0
.

Proof.

We will first show that

(13)

\lim_{\varepsilon\to 0}\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{2}\,d\mu_{\omega,\varepsilon}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)=\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,0}^{2}\,d\mu_{\omega}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega).

To this end, observe that

\begin{split}\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{2}\,d\mu_{\omega,\varepsilon}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)-\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,0}^{2}\,d\mu_{\omega}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)&=\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}(f_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{2}-f_{\omega,0}^{2})\,d\mu_{\omega,\varepsilon}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)\\
&\phantom{=}+\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,0}^{2}(h_{\omega,\varepsilon}-h_{\omega})\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega).\end{split}

Observe that for
\mathbb{P}
-a.e.
\omega\in\Omega
,

(14)

f_{\omega,\varepsilon}-f_{\omega,0}=\int_{0}^{1}F_{\omega}(h_{\omega}-h_{\omega,\varepsilon})\,dm,

and consequently

|f_{\omega,\varepsilon}-f_{\omega,0}|\leq\|F_{\omega}\|_{C^{0}}\|h_{\omega,\varepsilon}-h_{\omega,0}\|_{L^{1}(m)}\leq C_{\alpha,F}|\varepsilon|,

where in the second inequality we used Proposition
5
and (
11
). On the other hand,

\|f_{\omega,\varepsilon}+f_{\omega,0}\|_{C^{0}}\leq 4\|F_{\omega}\|_{C^{0}}.

Consequently,

\|f_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{2}-f_{\omega,0}^{2}\|_{C^{0}}\leq C_{\alpha,F}|\varepsilon|,

for
\mathbb{P}
-a.e.
\omega\in\Omega
. This implies that

\begin{split}\left|\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}(f_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{2}-f_{\omega,0}^{2})\,d\mu_{\omega,\varepsilon}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)\right|&\leq\int_{\Omega}\|f_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{2}-f_{\omega,0}^{2}\|_{C^{0}}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)\leq C_{\alpha,F}|\varepsilon|,\end{split}

yielding

\lim_{\varepsilon\to 0}\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}(f_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{2}-f_{\omega,0}^{2})\,d\mu_{\omega,\varepsilon}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)=0.

Similarly,
|f_{\omega,0}^{2}|\leq 4\|F_{\omega}\|_{C^{0}}^{2}
and thus using Proposition
5
and (
11
) we have

\lim_{\varepsilon\to 0}\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,0}^{2}(h_{\omega,\varepsilon}-h_{\omega})\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)=0,

since

\begin{split}\left|\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,0}^{2}(h_{\omega,\varepsilon}-h_{\omega})\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)\right|\leq\int_{\Omega}\|f_{\omega,0}^{2}\|_{C^{0}}\cdot\|h_{\omega,\varepsilon}-h_{\omega}\|_{L^{1}(m)}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)\leq C_{\alpha,F}|\varepsilon|.\end{split}

Therefore, we conclude that (
13
) holds.

Next, we observe that

\begin{split}\left|\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,\varepsilon}\cdot(f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}\circ T_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n})\,d\mu_{\omega,\varepsilon}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)\right|&=\left|\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}(f_{\omega,\varepsilon}h_{\omega,\varepsilon})f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)\right|\\
&\leq\int_{\Omega}\|\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}(f_{\omega,\varepsilon}h_{\omega,\varepsilon})\|_{L^{1}(m)}\cdot\|f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}\|_{C^{0}}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)\\
&\leq C_{\alpha}n^{-1/\alpha+1}\left(\esssup\|F_{\omega}\|_{C^{1}}\right)^{2},\end{split}

where we used (
2
) and (
11
). Since the series
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}n^{-1/\alpha+1}
is summable, in order to prove our theorem, it remains to show that for each
n\in\mathbb{N}
, the map

\varepsilon\mapsto\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,\varepsilon}\cdot(f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}\circ T_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n})\,d\mu_{\omega,\varepsilon}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)=\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}(f_{\omega,\varepsilon}h_{\omega,\varepsilon})f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)

is continuous in
0
. To this end, we first observe that

\begin{split}&\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}(f_{\omega,\varepsilon}h_{\omega,\varepsilon})f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)-\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega}^{n}(f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega})f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)\\
&=\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega}^{n}(f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega})(f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}-f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0})\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)+\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}(\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}-\mathcal{L}_{\omega}^{n})(f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega})f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)\\
&\phantom{=}+\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}(f_{\omega,\varepsilon}h_{\omega,\varepsilon}-f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega})f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)\\
&=\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}(\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}-\mathcal{L}_{\omega}^{n})(f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega})f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)+\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}(f_{\omega,\varepsilon}h_{\omega,\varepsilon}-f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega})f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega),\end{split}

since
f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}-f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}
depends only on
\varepsilon
and
\omega
(see (
14
)) and

\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega}^{n}(f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega})\,dm=\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega}\,dm=0.

Next,

\begin{split}&\left|\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}(f_{\omega,\varepsilon}h_{\omega,\varepsilon}-f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega})f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)\right|\\
&\leq\int_{\Omega}\|\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}(f_{\omega,\varepsilon}h_{\omega,\varepsilon}-f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega})\|_{L^{1}(m)}\cdot\|f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}\|_{C^{0}}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)\\
&\leq\int_{\Omega}\|f_{\omega,\varepsilon}h_{\omega,\varepsilon}-f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega}\|_{L^{1}(m)}\cdot\|f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}\|_{C^{0}}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)\\
&\leq 2\esssup_{\omega\in\Omega}\|F_{\omega}\|_{C^{0}}\int_{\Omega}\|f_{\omega,\varepsilon}h_{\omega,\varepsilon}-f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega}\|_{L^{1}(m)}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega).\end{split}

Moreover,

\begin{split}\|f_{\omega,\varepsilon}h_{\omega,\varepsilon}-f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega}\|_{L^{1}(m)}&\leq\|f_{\omega,\varepsilon}\|_{C^{0}}\|h_{\omega,\varepsilon}-h_{\omega}\|_{L^{1}(m)}+|f_{\omega,\varepsilon}-f_{\omega,0}|\\
&\leq C_{\alpha,F}|\varepsilon|,\end{split}

where we used Proposition
5
. This together with (
11
) implies that

\lim_{\varepsilon\to 0}\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}(f_{\omega,\varepsilon}h_{\omega,\varepsilon}-f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega})f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)=0.

Finally, we have that

\begin{split}&\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}(\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}-\mathcal{L}_{\omega}^{n})(f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega})f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)\\
&=\sum_{j=1}^{n}\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\sigma^{j}\omega,\varepsilon}^{n-j}(\mathcal{L}_{\sigma^{j-1}\omega,\varepsilon}-\mathcal{L}_{\sigma^{j-1}\omega})\mathcal{L}_{\omega}^{j-1}(f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega})f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega).\end{split}

Therefore,

\begin{split}&\left|\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}(\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}-\mathcal{L}_{\omega}^{n})(f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega})f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)\right|\\
&\leq\sum_{j=1}^{n}\int_{\Omega}\|\mathcal{L}_{\sigma^{j}\omega,\varepsilon}^{n-j}(\mathcal{L}_{\sigma^{j-1}\omega,\varepsilon}-\mathcal{L}_{\sigma^{j-1}\omega})\mathcal{L}_{\omega}^{j-1}(f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega})\|_{L^{1}(m)}\cdot\|f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}\|_{C^{0}}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)\\
&\leq\sum_{j=1}^{n}\int_{\Omega}\|(\mathcal{L}_{\sigma^{j-1}\omega,\varepsilon}-\mathcal{L}_{\sigma^{j-1}\omega})\mathcal{L}_{\omega}^{j-1}(f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega})\|_{L^{1}(m)}\cdot\|f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}\|_{C^{0}}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)\\
&\leq 2\left(\esssup_{\omega\in\Omega}\|F_{\omega}\|_{C^{0}}\right)\sum_{j=1}^{n}\int_{\Omega}\|(\mathcal{L}_{\sigma^{j-1}\omega,\varepsilon}-\mathcal{L}_{\sigma^{j-1}\omega})\mathcal{L}_{\omega}^{j-1}(f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega})\|_{L^{1}(m)}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega).\end{split}

In addition, we have

(\mathcal{L}_{\sigma^{j-1}\omega,\varepsilon}-\mathcal{L}_{\sigma^{j-1}\omega})\mathcal{L}_{\omega}^{j-1}(f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega})=-\int_{\beta(\sigma^{j-1}\omega)}^{\beta(\sigma^{j-1}\omega)+\varepsilon\delta(\sigma^{j-1}\omega)}(X_{\gamma}N_{\gamma}(\mathcal{L}_{\omega}^{j-1}(f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega})))^{\prime}\,d\gamma,

since
\partial_{\gamma}\mathcal{L}_{\gamma}(\phi)=-(X_{\gamma}N_{\gamma}(\phi))^{\prime}
(see
[
9
, p.865]
). It follows from
[
17
, Proposition 9]
and (
1
) that, for
\mathbb{P}
-a-e.
\omega\in\Omega
we have
f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega}=\psi_{1}-\psi_{2}
for some
\psi_{i}=\psi_{i}(\omega)\in\mathcal{C}_{*}\cap C^{1}(0,1]
. Moreover,
\|\psi_{i}\|_{L^{1}}\leq K
for
i=1,2
, where
K
does not depend on
\omega
.
Hence,

\mathcal{L}_{\omega}^{j-1}(f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega})=\mathcal{L}_{\omega}^{j-1}(\psi_{1})-\mathcal{L}_{\omega}^{j-1}(\psi_{2}),\quad\mathcal{L}_{\omega}^{j-1}(\psi_{i})\in\mathcal{C}_{*}\cap C^{1}(0,1],\ i=1,2.

Consequently

\displaystyle\|(X_{\gamma}N_{\gamma}(\mathcal{L}_{\omega}^{j-1}(f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega})))^{\prime}\|_{L^{1}(m)}\leq\sum_{i=1,2}\|(X_{\gamma}N_{\gamma}(\mathcal{L}_{\omega}^{j-1}(\psi_{i})))^{\prime}\|_{L^{1}(m)}\leq\tilde{K},

where the last inequality is a consequence of bounds on
X_{\gamma}
and
X_{\gamma}^{\prime}
in
[
9
, (2.3) and (2.4)]
, the invariance of
{\mathcal{C}}_{*}\cap C^{1}(0,1]
with respect to
N_{\gamma}

and
{\mathcal{L}}_{\gamma}
(see
[
17
, Proposition 5]
), and the
observation (see
[
17
, Remark 1]
) that

(15)

\displaystyle\phi\in{\mathcal{C}}_{*}\cap C^{1}(0,1]\implies|\phi^{\prime}(x)|\leq(\alpha+1)x^{-1}\phi(x)\quad\forall x\in(0,1].

This gives that

\int_{\Omega}\|(\mathcal{L}_{\sigma^{j-1}\omega,\varepsilon}-\mathcal{L}_{\sigma^{j-1}\omega})\mathcal{L}_{\omega}^{j-1}(f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega})\|_{L^{1}(m)}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)\leq C|\varepsilon|,

for some
C>0
independent on
\varepsilon
. Thus,

\lim_{\varepsilon\to 0}\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}(\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}-\mathcal{L}_{\omega}^{n})(f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega})f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)=0.

This completes the proof of the theorem.
∎

4
Differentiability of the variance

Take
\beta\colon\Omega\to(0,1)
,
\delta\colon\Omega\to[0,1)
be measurable maps and
\underline{\alpha}>0
such that
0<\underline{\alpha}<\alpha<\frac{1}{2}
,

0<\underline{\alpha}\leq\essinf_{\omega\in\Omega}\beta(\omega)-\varepsilon_{0}\quad\text{and}\quad\esssup_{\omega\in\Omega}\beta(\omega)+\varepsilon_{0}\leq\alpha.

Let
T_{\omega,\varepsilon}
be the LSV map with parameter
\beta(\omega)+\varepsilon\delta(\omega)
for every
\omega\in\Omega
and
\varepsilon\in(-\varepsilon_{0},\varepsilon_{0})
. By
(\mu_{\omega,\varepsilon})_{\omega\in\Omega}
we will denote the random a.c.i.m that corresponds to the cocycle
(T_{\omega,\varepsilon})_{\omega\in\Omega}
, where
h_{\omega,\varepsilon}
is the density of
\mu_{\omega,\varepsilon}
. As in the previous section, we will drop the subscript
\varepsilon
when
\varepsilon=0
.

We will use the following result, which is a sharper version of
[
17
, Theorem 14]
. Its proof is given in Appendix B.

Theorem 7
.

For
\mathbb{P}
-a.e.
\omega\in\Omega
, there exists
C=C_{\alpha,\underline{\alpha}}>0
such that for
\varepsilon
sufficiently close to
0
,

(16)

\|\varepsilon^{-1}(h_{\omega,\varepsilon}-h_{\omega})-\hat{h}_{\omega}\|_{L^{1}(m)}\leq C|\varepsilon|^{1-2\alpha},

where

(17)

\hat{h}_{\omega}:=-\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}\delta(\sigma^{-(i+1)}\omega)\mathcal{L}_{\sigma^{-i}\omega}^{i}\left(X_{\beta(\sigma^{-(i+1)}\omega)}N_{\beta(\sigma^{-(i+1)}\omega)}(h_{\sigma^{-(i+1)}\omega})\right)^{\prime}\in L^{1}(m).

Moreover,

(18)

\esssup_{\omega\in\Omega}\|\hat{h}_{\omega}\|_{L^{1}(m)}<+\infty.

Remark 8
.

In Theorem
7
we show for
\mathbb{P}
-a.e.
\omega\in\Omega
, the map
(-\varepsilon_{0},\varepsilon_{0})\ni\varepsilon\mapsto h_{\omega,\varepsilon}\in L^{1}(m)
is differentiable in
\varepsilon=0
. On the other hand, in
[
17
, Theorem 14]
a weaker statement is proved: for
\mathbb{P}
-a.e.
\omega\in\Omega
and
\psi\in L^{\infty}(m)
, the map
\varepsilon\mapsto\int_{0}^{1}\psi h_{\omega,\varepsilon}\,dm
is differentiable in
\varepsilon=0
. For other quenched linear response results for different classes of random dynamics, we refer to
[
12
,
16
,
19
,
20
,
34
]
.

Let
F\colon\Omega\times[0,1]\to\mathbb{R}
be a measurable map such that
F(\omega,\cdot)\in C^{2}[0,1]
for
\omega\in\Omega
and that

(19)

\esssup_{\omega\in\Omega}\|F(\omega,\cdot)\|_{C^{2}}<+\infty.

Let
f_{\omega,\varepsilon}
and
\Sigma_{\varepsilon}^{2}
be as in the previous section. Then we have the following theorem.

Theorem 9
.

Let
\alpha<\frac{1}{5}
. Then,
the map
\varepsilon\mapsto\Sigma_{\varepsilon}^{2}
is differentiable in
0
.

Remark 10
.

We note that the proof of Theorem
9
yields

\begin{split}\frac{d}{d\varepsilon}\Sigma_{\varepsilon}^{2}\big\rvert_{\varepsilon=0}&=\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,0}^{2}\hat{h}_{\omega}\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)-2\int_{\Omega}\left(\int_{0}^{1}F_{\omega}\hat{h}_{\omega}\,dm\right)\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,0}\,d\mu_{\omega}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)\\
&\phantom{=}+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega}^{n}(-L(\omega,F_{\omega})h_{\omega}+f_{\omega,0}\hat{h}_{\omega})f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)\\
&\phantom{=}+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}\int_{\Omega}\delta(\sigma^{j}\omega)\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\sigma^{j+1}\omega}^{n-j-1}\left(\partial_{\gamma}\mathcal{L}_{\beta(\sigma^{j}\omega)}(\mathcal{L}_{\omega}^{j}(f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega}))\right)f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega),\end{split}

where
L(\omega,F_{\omega}):=\int_{0}^{1}F_{\omega}\hat{h}_{\omega}\,dm
.
The proof of Theorem
9
shows that this formula for
the derivative of

\Sigma_{\varepsilon}^{2}
is well-defined
under the assumption
\alpha<1/5
. In particular, Lemma
18
ensures the summability

(20)

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}|\mathcal{L}_{\sigma^{j+1}\omega}^{n-j-1}\left(\partial_{\gamma}\mathcal{L}_{\beta(\sigma^{j}\omega)}(\mathcal{L}_{\omega}^{j}(f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega}))\right)|\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)<\infty.

We do not know whether (
20
) remains valid beyond
the range
\alpha<1/5
.

Remark 11
.

For a special class of observables as in
Theorem
3
(ii), we can can weaken the constraint on

\alpha
to
\alpha<1/4+\delta
for suitable
\delta>0
.
See Theorem
21
for a precise statement.

The proof of Theorem
9
will be established in a series of auxiliary results.

4.1
Differentiability of the first term in (
12
)

Lemma 12
.

The map

(21)

\varepsilon\to\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{2}\,d\mu_{\omega,\varepsilon}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)

is differentiable in
\varepsilon=0
.

Proof.

Writing

\begin{split}&\varepsilon^{-1}\left(\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{2}\,d\mu_{\omega,\varepsilon}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)-\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,0}^{2}\,d\mu_{\omega}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)\right)\\
&=\varepsilon^{-1}\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}(f_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{2}-f_{\omega,0}^{2})\,d\mu_{\omega,\varepsilon}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)+\varepsilon^{-1}\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,0}^{2}(h_{\omega,\varepsilon}-h_{\omega})\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega),\end{split}

it follows from (
16
) that

\lim_{\varepsilon\to 0}\frac{1}{\varepsilon}\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,0}^{2}(h_{\omega,\varepsilon}-h_{\omega,0})\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)=\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,0}^{2}\hat{h}_{\omega}\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega),

since

\begin{split}&\left|\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,0}^{2}(\varepsilon^{-1}(h_{\omega,\varepsilon}-h_{\omega,0}))\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)-\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,0}^{2}\hat{h}_{\omega}\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)\right|\\
&\leq\int_{\Omega}\|f_{\omega,0}^{2}\|_{C^{0}}\cdot\|\varepsilon^{-1}(h_{\omega,\varepsilon}-h_{\omega,0})-\hat{h}_{\omega}\|_{L^{1}(m)}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)\leq C_{\alpha,\underline{\alpha},F}|\varepsilon|^{1-2\alpha},\end{split}

for
\varepsilon
sufficiently close to
0
.
Next, we note that

\lim_{\varepsilon\to 0}\frac{1}{\varepsilon}\left(\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}(f_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{2}-f_{\omega,0}^{2})\,d\mu_{\omega,\varepsilon}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)-\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}(f_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{2}-f_{\omega,0}^{2})\,d\mu_{\omega}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)\right)=0,

as

\begin{split}&\left|\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}(f_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{2}-f_{\omega,0}^{2})\,d\mu_{\omega,\varepsilon}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)-\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}(f_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{2}-f_{\omega,0}^{2})\,d\mu_{\omega}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)\right|\\
&\leq\int_{\Omega}\|f_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{2}-f_{\omega,0}^{2}\|_{C^{0}}\|h_{\omega,\varepsilon}-h_{\omega}\|_{L^{1}(m)}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)\\
&\leq C_{\alpha,F}|\varepsilon|^{2},\end{split}

for some
C_{\alpha,F}>0
where we used (
14
) and Proposition
5
.
Finally,

\lim_{\varepsilon\to 0}\frac{1}{\varepsilon}\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}(f_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{2}-f_{\omega,0}^{2})\,d\mu_{\omega}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)=-2\int_{\Omega}\left(\int_{0}^{1}F_{\omega}\hat{h}_{\omega}\,dm\right)\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,0}\,d\mu_{\omega}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega),

since

\begin{split}&\left\|\varepsilon^{-1}(f_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{2}-f_{\omega,0}^{2})+2\left(\int_{0}^{1}F_{\omega}\hat{h}_{\omega}\,dm\right)f_{\omega,0}\right\|_{C^{0}}\\
&\leq\left|\varepsilon^{-1}(f_{\omega,\varepsilon}-f_{\omega,0})+\int_{0}^{1}F_{\omega}\hat{h}_{\omega}\,dm\right|\cdot\|f_{\omega,\varepsilon}+f_{\omega,0}\|_{C^{0}}+\left|\int_{0}^{1}F_{\omega}\hat{h}_{\omega}\,dm\right|\cdot|f_{\omega,\varepsilon}-f_{\omega,0}|\\
&\leq C_{\alpha,\underline{\alpha},F}|\varepsilon|^{1-2\alpha}+C_{\alpha,F}|\varepsilon|,\end{split}

for
\varepsilon
sufficiently close to
0
where we used (
14
), Proposition
5
and Theorem
7
.
Thus, the map in (
21
) is differentiable in
\varepsilon=0
.
∎

4.2
The differentiability of the second term in (
12
)

Next, we have consider the second term in (
12
):

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}(f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}\circ T_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n})f_{\omega,\varepsilon}\,d\mu_{\omega,\varepsilon}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}(f_{\omega,\varepsilon}h_{\omega,\varepsilon})f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega).

Let

C_{n}(\varepsilon):=\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}(f_{\omega,\varepsilon}h_{\omega,\varepsilon})f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega),

and

D_{n}(\varepsilon):=\frac{C_{n}(\varepsilon)-C_{n}(0)}{\varepsilon}.

Observe that

|C_{n}(\varepsilon)|\leq\int_{\Omega}\|\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}(f_{\omega,\varepsilon}h_{\omega,\varepsilon})\|_{L^{1}(m)}\cdot\|f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}\|_{C^{0}}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)\leq C_{\alpha,F}n^{-1/\alpha+1}.

Consequently,

|D_{n}(\varepsilon)|\leq C_{\alpha,F}|\varepsilon|^{-1}n^{-1/\alpha+1}.

Let us now take
\gamma\in(0,\frac{1}{2})
such that

(22)

\gamma(1/\alpha-2)>1.

We note that this is possible since
\alpha<\frac{1}{4}
.
Then, we have

\left|\sum_{n\geq|\varepsilon|^{-\gamma}}D_{n}(\varepsilon)\right|\leq C|\varepsilon|^{\gamma(1/\alpha-2)-1},

where
C>0
is not dependent on
\varepsilon
.
Hence, due to (

) we have

(23)

\lim_{\varepsilon\to 0}\sum_{n\geq|\varepsilon|^{-\gamma}}D_{n}(\varepsilon)=0.

Next, note that

(24)

\begin{split}D_{n}(\varepsilon)&=\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega}^{n}(f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega})(\varepsilon^{-1}(f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}-f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}))\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)\\
&\phantom{=}+\frac{1}{\varepsilon}\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}(\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}-\mathcal{L}_{\omega}^{n})(f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega})f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)\\
&\phantom{=}+\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}(\varepsilon^{-1}(f_{\omega,\varepsilon}h_{\omega,\varepsilon}-f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega}))f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)\\
&=\frac{1}{\varepsilon}\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}(\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}-\mathcal{L}_{\omega}^{n})(f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega})f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)\\
&\phantom{=}+\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}(\varepsilon^{-1}(f_{\omega,\varepsilon}h_{\omega,\varepsilon}-f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega}))f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)\\
&=:D_{n}^{1}(\varepsilon)+D_{n}^{2}(\varepsilon),\end{split}

since as in the proof of Theorem
6
we have

\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega}^{n}(f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega})(\varepsilon^{-1}(f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}-f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}))\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)=0.

We first focus on
D_{n}^{2}(\varepsilon)
. Taking into account (

\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}(f_{\omega,\varepsilon}h_{\omega,\varepsilon}-f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega})\,dm=\int_{0}^{1}(f_{\omega,\varepsilon}h_{\omega,\varepsilon}-f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega})\,dm=0,

we have

D_{n}^{2}(\varepsilon)=\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}(\varepsilon^{-1}(f_{\omega,\varepsilon}h_{\omega,\varepsilon}-f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega}))f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega).

Let

\bar{D}_{n}^{2}(\varepsilon):=\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}(-L(\omega,F_{\omega})h_{\omega}+f_{\omega,0}\hat{h}_{\omega})f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega),

Lemma 13
.

We have

|D_{n}^{2}(\varepsilon)-\bar{D}_{n}^{2}(\varepsilon)|\leq C_{\alpha,\underline{\alpha},F}|\varepsilon|^{1-2\alpha},

for
\varepsilon
sufficiently close to
0
.

Proof.

Note that

\begin{split}&|D_{n}^{2}(\varepsilon)-\bar{D}_{n}^{2}(\varepsilon)|\\
&\leq\int_{\Omega}\|\varepsilon^{-1}(f_{\omega,\varepsilon}-f_{\omega,0})h_{\omega,\varepsilon}+L(\omega,F_{\omega})h_{\omega}+f_{\omega,0}(\varepsilon^{-1}(h_{\omega,\varepsilon}-h_{\omega})-\hat{h}_{\omega})\|_{L^{1}(m)}\cdot\|f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}\|_{C^{0}}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)\\
&\leq C_{F}\int_{\Omega}\|\varepsilon^{-1}(f_{\omega,\varepsilon}-f_{\omega,0})h_{\omega,\varepsilon}+L(\omega,F_{\omega})h_{\omega}+f_{\omega,0}(\varepsilon^{-1}(h_{\omega,\varepsilon}-h_{\omega})-\hat{h}_{\omega})\|_{L^{1}(m)}\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega).\end{split}

Moreover,

\begin{split}\|\varepsilon^{-1}(f_{\omega,\varepsilon}-f_{\omega,0})h_{\omega,\varepsilon}+L(\omega,F_{\omega})h_{\omega}\|_{L^{1}(m)}&\leq\|\varepsilon^{-1}(f_{\omega,\varepsilon}-f_{\omega,0})h_{\omega,\varepsilon}+L(\omega,F_{\omega})h_{\omega,\varepsilon}\|_{L^{1}(m)}\\
&\phantom{\leq}+|L(\omega,F_{\omega})|\|h_{\omega,\varepsilon}-h_{\omega}\|_{L^{1}(m)}\\

&\phantom{=}+|L(\omega,F_{\omega})|\|h_{\omega,\varepsilon}-h_{\omega}\|_{L^{1}(m)}\\
&\leq C_{\alpha,\underline{\alpha},F}|\varepsilon|^{1-2\alpha}+C_{\alpha,F}|\varepsilon|\\
&\leq C_{\alpha,\underline{\alpha},F}|\varepsilon|^{1-2\alpha},\end{split}

for
\mathbb{P}
-a.e.
\omega\in\Omega
and
\varepsilon
sufficiently close to

, where we used Proposition

Similarly,

\begin{split}\|f_{\omega,0}(\varepsilon^{-1}(h_{\omega,\varepsilon}-h_{\omega})-\hat{h}_{\omega})\|_{L^{1}(m)}&\leq\|f_{\omega,0}\|_{C^{0}}\cdot\|\varepsilon^{-1}(h_{\omega,\varepsilon}-h_{\omega})-\hat{h}_{\omega}\|_{L^{1}(m)}\\
&\leq C_{\alpha,\underline{\alpha},F}|\varepsilon|^{1-2\alpha},\end{split}

for
\mathbb{P}
-a.e.
\omega\in\Omega
and
\varepsilon
sufficiently close to
0
. The conclusion of the lemma follows readily from the above estimates.
∎

By Lemma

We write

Lemma 14
.

We have

|\bar{D}_{n,2}^{2}(\varepsilon)|\leq C_{\alpha,F}(n^{-1/\gamma}+n^{-1/\alpha+1}),\quad n<|\varepsilon|^{-\gamma}.

Proof.

Note that

\|\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}h_{\omega}-h_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}\|_{L^{1}(m)}=\|\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}(h_{\omega}-h_{\omega,\varepsilon})\|_{L^{1}(m)}\leq C_{\alpha}n^{-1/\alpha+1}.

Then, using that

\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}(h_{\omega,\varepsilon})f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}\,dm=\int_{0}^{1}h_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}\,dm=0,

we have

\begin{split}&\left|\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}(h_{\omega})f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}\,dm\right|\\
&\leq\left|\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}(h_{\omega})(f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}-f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0})\,dm\right|+\left|\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}(h_{\omega})f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}\,dm\right|\\
&=\left|\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}(h_{\omega})(f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}-f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0})\,dm\right|+\left|\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}(h_{\omega}-h_{\omega,\varepsilon})f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}\,dm\right|\\
&\leq\|\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}h_{\omega}\|_{L^{1}(m)}\cdot\left|f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}-f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}\right|+\|\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}(h_{\omega}-h_{\omega,\varepsilon})\|_{L^{1}(m)}\cdot\|f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}\|_{C^{0}}\\
&\leq\left|f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}-f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}\right|+\|\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}(h_{\omega}-h_{\omega,\varepsilon})\|_{L^{1}(m)}\cdot\|f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}\|_{C^{0}}\\
&\leq C_{\alpha,F}|\varepsilon|+C_{\alpha,F}n^{-1/\alpha+1},\end{split}

which yields the desired conclusion.
∎

Lemma 15
.

We have

|\bar{D}_{n,1}^{2}(\varepsilon)|\leq C_{\alpha,\underline{\alpha},F}(n^{1-\frac{1}{2\alpha}}+n^{-1/\gamma}),\quad n<|\varepsilon|^{-\gamma}.

Proof.

By using Theorem
7
we have

(27)

\begin{split}\left|\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}(f_{\omega,0}\hat{h}_{\omega})f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}\,dm\right|&\leq\left|\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}(f_{\omega,0}(\hat{h}_{\omega}-r^{-1}(h_{\omega,r}-h_{\omega})))f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}\,dm\right|\\
&\phantom{\leq}+\left|\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}(f_{\omega,0}r^{-1}(h_{\omega,r}-h_{\omega}))f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}\,dm\right|\\
&\leq\|\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}(f_{\omega,0}(\hat{h}_{\omega}-r^{-1}(h_{\omega,r}-h_{\omega})))\|_{L^{1}(m)}\cdot\|f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}\|_{C^{0}}\\
&\phantom{\leq}+\left|\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}(f_{\omega,0}r^{-1}(h_{\omega,r}-h_{\omega}))f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}\,dm\right|\\
&\leq C_{\alpha,\underline{\alpha},F}r^{1-2\alpha}+\left|\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}(f_{\omega,0}r^{-1}(h_{\omega,r}-h_{\omega}))f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}\,dm\right|,\end{split}

for

Next,

\begin{split}&\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}(f_{\omega,0}r^{-1}(h_{\omega,r}-h_{\omega}))f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}\,dm\\
&=\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}\left(r^{-1}\left(f_{\omega,0}-\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega,r}\,dm\right)h_{\omega,r}\right)f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}\,dm\\
&\phantom{=}+\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}\left(r^{-1}\left(\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega,r}\,dm\right)h_{\omega,r}\right)f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}\,dm\\
&\phantom{=}-\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}\left(r^{-1}\left(f_{\omega,0}-\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega}\,dm\right)h_{\omega}\right)f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}\,dm\\
&\phantom{=}-\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}\left(r^{-1}\left(\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega}\,dm\right)h_{\omega}\right)f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}\,dm.\end{split}

Since

\int_{0}^{1}\left(f_{\omega,0}-\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega,r}\,dm\right)h_{\omega,r}\,dm=0,

we have using (
2
) that

(28)

\begin{split}&\left|\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}\left(r^{-1}\left(f_{\omega,0}-\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega,r}\,dm\right)h_{\omega,r}\right)f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}\,dm\right|\\
&\leq C_{F}r^{-1}\left\|\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}\left(\left(f_{\omega,0}-\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega,r}\,dm\right)h_{\omega,r}\right)\right\|_{L^{1}(m)}\\

In the same manner,

(29)

\left|\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}\left(r^{-1}\left(f_{\omega,0}-\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega}\,dm\right)h_{\omega}\right)f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}\,dm\right|\leq C_{\alpha,F}r^{-1}n^{-1/\alpha+1}.

Moreover,

\begin{split}&\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}\left(r^{-1}\left(\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega,r}\,dm\right)h_{\omega,r}\right)f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}\,dm\\

Observe that

(30)

\begin{split}&\left|\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}\left(r^{-1}\left(\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega,r}\,dm\right)(h_{\omega,r}-h_{\omega})\right)f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}\,dm\right|\\

In addition,

\begin{split}&\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}\left(r^{-1}\left(\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega,r}\,dm-\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,0}h_{\omega}\,dm\right)h_{\omega}\right)f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}\,dm\\

By Theorem

(31)

Furthermore,

(32)

\begin{split}&\left|\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,0}\hat{h}_{\omega}\,dm\int_{0}^{1}\mathcal{L}_{\omega,\varepsilon}^{n}(h_{\omega})f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}\,dm-\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,0}\hat{h}_{\omega}\,dm\int_{0}^{1}h_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}\,dm\right|\\

and thus (taking into account (

\left|\bar{D}_{n,1}^{2}(\varepsilon)-\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}f_{\omega,0}\hat{h}_{\omega}\,dm\int_{0}^{1}h_{\sigma^{n}\omega,\varepsilon}f_{\sigma^{n}\omega,0}\,dm\,d\mathbb{P}(\omega)\right|\leq C_{\alpha,\underline{\alpha},F}(r^{1-2\alpha}+r^{-1}n^{-1/\alpha+1}),

for

. In addition, since

Lemma 22
.

Let

b_{2}

Proof.

We recall the construction from

\begin{split}|\psi_{1}^{\prime}(x)|&\leq(\|\varphi^{\prime}\|_{\infty}+|\lambda|)h(x)+(\varphi(x)+\lambda x+A)|h^{\prime}(x)|\\
&\leq a(\|\varphi^{\prime}\|_{\infty}+|\lambda|)x^{-\alpha}+\frac{b_{1}}{x}(\varphi(x)+\lambda x+A)h(x)\\

provided that
