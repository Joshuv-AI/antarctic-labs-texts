49 C.F.R. § 1016.104 — Decisionmaking authority.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1016.104
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1016.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1016 (Special Procedures Governing The Recovery Of Expenses By Parties To Board Adjudicatory Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless otherwise ordered by the Board in a particular proceeding, each application for an award under this part shall be assigned for decision to the official or decisionmaking body that entered the decision in the adversary adjudication. That official or decisionmaking body is referred to in this part as the “adjudicative officer.”
