48 C.F.R. § 9904.417-40 — 9904.417-40 Fundamental requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:48:9904.417-40
Creator: Chapter 99—Cost Accounting Standards Board, Office Of Federal Procurement Policy, Office Of Management And Budget
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-9904.417-40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 9904 (Cost Accounting Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The cost of money applicable to the investment in tangible and intangible capital assets being constructed, fabricated, or developed for a contractor's own use shall be included in the capitalized acquisition cost of such assets.
