Published: 2025

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-13103-2, published online 25 July 2025.

The original version of this Article contained errors.

The name of author E. L. Tchinda Ngounou was incorrectly given as E. L. T. Ngounou.

In addition, Affiliation 1 was incorrectly given as ‘College of Science Engineering & Technology, School of Science, Department of Physics, Eureka Building-Science Campus, UNESCO-UNISA Africa Chair in Nanosciences & Nanotechnology, University of South Africa, Pretoria, South Africa. The correct affiliation is listed below:

UNESCO-UNISA Africa Chair in Nanosciences & Nanotechnology, College of Graduate Studies, University of South Africa, Muckleneuk Ridge, PO Box 392, Pretoria, South Africa.

The original Article and accompanying Supplementary Information file have been corrected.
