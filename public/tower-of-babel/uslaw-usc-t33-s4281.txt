33 U.S.C. § 4281 — Strategy for improving post-consumer materials management and water management
Citation: us:usc:33:4281
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section4281
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 55 (Save Our Seas 2.0); Subchapter III (Improving Domestic Infrastructure To Prevent Marine Debris). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
Not later than 1 year after December 18, 2020, the EPA Administrator shall, in consultation with stakeholders, develop a strategy to improve post-consumer materials management and infrastructure for the purpose of reducing plastic waste and other post-consumer materials in waterways and oceans.
(b) Release
On development of the strategy under subsection (a), the EPA Administrator shall—
(1)
distribute the strategy to States; and
(2)
make the strategy publicly available, including for use by—
(A)
for-profit private entities involved in post-consumer materials management; and
(B)
other nongovernmental entities.
