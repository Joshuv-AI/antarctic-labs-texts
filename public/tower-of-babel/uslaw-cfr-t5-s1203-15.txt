5 C.F.R. § 1203.15 — Review of regulations on the Board's own motion.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1203.15
Creator: Merit Systems Protection Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1203.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1203 (Procedures For Review Of Rules And Regulations Of The Office Of Personnel Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board may, from time to time, review a regulation on its own motion under 5 U.S.C. 1204(f)(1)(A). When it does so, it will publish notice of the review in the Federal Register.
