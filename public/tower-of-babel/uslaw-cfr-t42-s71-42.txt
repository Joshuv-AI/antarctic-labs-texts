42 C.F.R. § 71.42 — Disinfection of imports.
Citation: us:cfr:42:71.42
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-71.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 71 (Foreign Quarantine). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the cargo manifest of a carrier lists articles which may require disinfection under the provisions of this part, the Director shall disinfect them on board or request the appropriate customs officer to keep the articles separated from the other cargo pending appropriate disposition.
