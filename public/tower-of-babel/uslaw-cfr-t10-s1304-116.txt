10 C.F.R. § 1304.116 — Mailing lists.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1304.116
Creator: Nuclear Waste Technical Review Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1304.116
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1304 (Privacy Act Of 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board shall not sell or rent an individual's name and/or address unless such action is specifically authorized by law. This section shall not be construed to require the withholding of names and addresses otherwise permitted to be made public.
