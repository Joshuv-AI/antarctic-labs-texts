35 U.S.C. § 281 — Remedy for infringement of patent
Citation: us:usc:35:281
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title35-section281
Context: United States Code, Title 35 (Patents); Part III (Patents And Protection Of Patent Rights); Chapter 29 (Remedies For Infringement Of Patent, And Other Actions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A patentee shall have remedy by civil action for infringement of his patent.
