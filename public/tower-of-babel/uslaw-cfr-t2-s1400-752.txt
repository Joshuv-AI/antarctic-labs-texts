2 C.F.R. § 1400.752 — When does a contested suspension action include a fact-finding proceeding?
Citation: us:cfr:2:1400.752
Creator: Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1400.752
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1400 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Fact-finding to resolve genuine disputes over facts material to the suspension occurs where the conditions listed in 2 CFR 180.735(b) are satisfied.
(b) The fact-finding official for DOI suspension proceedings is the DOI Debarment Program Director, unless the Suspending and Debarring Official designates another DOI official to serve as the fact-finding official.
