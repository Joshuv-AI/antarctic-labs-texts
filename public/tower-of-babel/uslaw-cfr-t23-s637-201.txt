23 C.F.R. § 637.201 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:23:637.201
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-637.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 637 (Construction Inspection And Approval). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To prescribe policies, procedures, and guidelines to assure the quality of materials and construction in all Federal-aid highway projects on the National Highway System.
