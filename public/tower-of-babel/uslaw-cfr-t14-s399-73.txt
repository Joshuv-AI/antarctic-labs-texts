14 C.F.R. § 399.73 — Definition of small business for Regulatory Flexibility Act.
Citation: us:cfr:14:399.73
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-399.73
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 399 (Statements Of General Policy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the purposes of the Department's implementation of chapter 6 of title 5, United States Code (Regulatory Flexibility Act), a direct air carrier or foreign air carrier is a small business if it provides air transportation only with small aircraft as defined in § 298.2 of this chapter (up to 60 seats/18,000 pound payload capacity).
