43 C.F.R. § 30.146 — What property is subject to claims?
Citation: us:cfr:43:30.146
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-30.146
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 30 (Indian Probate Hearings Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as prohibited by law, all intangible trust personalty of a decedent on hand or accrued at the date of death may be used for the payment of claims, including:
(a) IIM account balances;
(b) Bonds;
(c) Unpaid judgments; and
(d) Accounts receivable.
