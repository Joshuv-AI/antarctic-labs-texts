26 U.S.C. § 5602 — Penalty for tax fraud by distiller
Citation: us:usc:26:5602
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section5602
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 51 (Distilled Spirits, Wines, And Beer); Subchapter J (Penalties, Seizures, And Forfeitures Relating To Liquors); Part I (Penalty, Seizure, And Forfeiture Provisions Applicable To Distilling, Rectifying, And Distilled And Rectified Products). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whenever any person engaged in or carrying on the business of a distiller defrauds, attempts to defraud, or engages in such business with intent to defraud the United States of the tax on the spirits distilled by him, or of any part thereof, he shall be fined not more than $10,000, or imprisoned not more than 5 years, or both. No discontinuance or nolle prosequi of any prosecution under this section shall be allowed without the permission in writing of the Attorney General.
