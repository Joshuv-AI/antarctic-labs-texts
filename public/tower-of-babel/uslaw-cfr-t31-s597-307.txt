31 C.F.R. § 597.307 — Financial institution.
Citation: us:cfr:31:597.307
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-597.307
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 597 (Foreign Terrorist Organizations Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term financial institution shall have the definition given that term in 31 U.S.C. 5312(a)(2) as from time to time amended, notwithstanding the definition of that term in 31 CFR part 103.
