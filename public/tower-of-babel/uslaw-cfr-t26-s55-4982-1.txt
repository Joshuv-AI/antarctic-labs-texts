26 C.F.R. § 55.4982-1 — -1 Imposition of excise tax on undistributed income of regulated investment companies.

Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-55.4982-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 55 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 4982 imposes an excise tax on a regulated investment company in the amount of four percent of the excess, if any, of the required distribution for a calendar year over the distributed amount for such calendar year. Section 4982 applies only to calendar years beginning after December 31, 1986.
