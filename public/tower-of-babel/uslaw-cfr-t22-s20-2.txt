22 C.F.R. § 20.2 — Funding.
Citation: us:cfr:22:20.2
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-20.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 20 (Benefits For Certain Former Spouses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Benefits under this part are paid from the Fund maintained by the Secretary of the Treasury pursuant to section 802 of the Act but are not authorized to be paid except to the extent provided therefor. Appropriations for such Fund are authorized by section 821(a) of the Act.
