5 C.F.R. § 316.914 — Special provisions for Department of Defense.

Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 316 (Temporary And Term Employment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart does not apply to the Department of Defense during the period that section 1106 of Public Law 114-328, as amended by section 1116 of Public Law 118-31, (see 10 U.S.C. note prec. 1580) or that any applicable successor statute, is effective.
