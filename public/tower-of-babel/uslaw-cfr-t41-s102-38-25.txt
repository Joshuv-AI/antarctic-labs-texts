41 C.F.R. § 102-38.25 — -38.25 Notification of a Federal requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-38.25
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-38.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-38 (38—Sale Of Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Federal agencies have first claim to excess or surplus personal property reported to GSA. When a bona fide need for the property exists and is expressed by a Federal agency, you or the holding agency must make the property available for transfer to the maximum extent practicable and prior to transfer of title to the property.
