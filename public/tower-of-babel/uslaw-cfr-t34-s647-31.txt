34 C.F.R. § 647.31 — What are unallowable costs?
Citation: us:cfr:34:647.31
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-647.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 647 (Ronald E. Mcnair Postbaccalaureate Achievement Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Costs that may not be charged against a grant under this program include the following:
(a) Payment of tuition, stipends, test preparation and fees or any other form of student financial support to staff or participants not expressly allowed under § 647.30.
(b) Construction, renovation, and remodeling of any facilities.
