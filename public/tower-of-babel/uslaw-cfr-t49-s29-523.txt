49 C.F.R. § 29.523 — What technical assistance is available to a Tribe from the Department?
Citation: us:cfr:49:29.523
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-29.523
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 29 (Tribal Transportation Self-Governance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon the written request of a Tribe, and to the extent feasible, the Department will provide technical assistance, including periodic program reviews, to assist a Tribe improve its performance in carrying out the Program.
