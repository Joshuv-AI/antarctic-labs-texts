46 C.F.R. § 147.90 — Refrigerants.
Citation: us:cfr:46:147.90
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-147.90
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 147 (Hazardous Ships' Stores). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Only refrigerants listed in ANSI/ASHRAE 34-78 may be carried as ships' stores.
(b) Refrigerants contained in a vessel's operating system are not considered as being carried as ship's stores.
