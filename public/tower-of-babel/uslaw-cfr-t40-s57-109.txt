40 C.F.R. § 57.109 — Maintenance of pay.
Citation: us:cfr:40:57.109
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-57.109
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 57 (Primary Nonferrous Smelter Orders). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator will not approve or issue an NSO for any smelter unless he has approved or promulgated SIP provisions which are applicable to the smelter and which satisfy the requirements of section 110(a)(6) of the Clean Air Act.
