46 U.S.C. § 30705 — Seaworthiness
Citation: us:usc:46:30705
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section30705
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 307 (Liability Of Water Carriers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Prohibition.—
A carrier may not insert in a bill of lading or shipping document a provision lessening or avoiding its obligation to exercise due diligence to—
(1)
make the vessel seaworthy; and
(2)
properly man, equip, and supply the vessel.
(b) Voidness.—
A provision described in subsection (a) is void.
