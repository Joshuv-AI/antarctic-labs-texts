18 C.F.R. § 806.13 — Submission of application.
Citation: us:cfr:18:806.13
Creator: Susquehanna River Basin Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-806.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 806 (Review And Approval Of Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Sponsors of projects subject to review and approval of the Commission under § 806.4, § 806.5 or § 806.6, or project sponsors seeking renewal of an existing approval of the Commission, shall submit an application and applicable fee to the Commission, in accordance with this subpart.
