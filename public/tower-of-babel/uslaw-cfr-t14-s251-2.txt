14 C.F.R. § 251.2 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:251.2
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-251.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 251 (Carriage Of Musical Instruments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to U.S. certificated air carriers, U.S. commuter air carriers, air taxis, and U.S. indirect carriers that operate passenger service to, from, or within the United States.
