    Abstract: 30 CFR part 778 implements the requirement in section 
701(28) of the Surface Mining Control and Reclamation Act of 1977 
(SMCRA), which grants an exemption from the requirements of SMCRA to 
operators extracting not more than 16\2/3\ percentage tonnage of coal 
incidental to the extraction of other minerals. This information will 
be used by the regulatory authorities to make that determination.
    Title of Collection: Exemption for Coal Extraction Incidental to
