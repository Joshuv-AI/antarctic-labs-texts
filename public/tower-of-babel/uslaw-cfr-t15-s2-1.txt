15 C.F.R. § 2.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:15:2.1
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-2.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 2 (Procedures For Handling And Settlement Of Claims Under The Federal Tort Claims Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The purpose of this part is to delegate authority to settle or deny claims under the Federal Tort Claims Act (in part, 28 U.S.C. 2671-2680) as amended by Pub. L. 89-506, 80 Stat. 306, and to establish procedures for the administrative adjudication of such claims accruing on or after January 18, 1967.
