48 C.F.R. § 504.1303 — 504.1303 Contract clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:504.1303
Creator: Chapter 5—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-504.1303
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 504 (Administrative Matters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Insert the clause at 552.204-9, Personal Identity Verification Requirements, in solicitations and contracts when it is determined that contractor employees will require access to federally controlled facilities or information systems to perform contract requirements.
