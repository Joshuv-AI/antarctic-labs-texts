41 U.S.C. § 4102 — Authorities or responsibilities not affected
Citation: us:usc:41:4102
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title41-section4102
Context: United States Code, Title 41 (Public Contracts); Chapter 41 (Task And Delivery Order Contracts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter does not modify or supersede, and is not intended to impair or restrict, authorities or responsibilities under sections 1101 to 1104 of title 40.
