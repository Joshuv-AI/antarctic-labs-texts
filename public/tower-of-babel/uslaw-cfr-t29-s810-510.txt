29 C.F.R. § 810.510 — Notice to a producer that a verification of compliance with labor value content requirements has been initiated.
Citation: us:cfr:29:810.510
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-810.510
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 810 (High-Wage Components Of The Labor Value Content Requirements Under The United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement Implementation Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

CBP will notify a producer that a verification of LVC compliance has been initiated, including whether the verification concerns the high-wage components of the producer's LVC certification. This notification applies to verifications of compliance with the LVC referred to the Administrator by CBP, as well as verifications the Administrator has initiated with CBP.
