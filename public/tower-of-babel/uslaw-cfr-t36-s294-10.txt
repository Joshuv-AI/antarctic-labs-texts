36 C.F.R. § 294.10 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:36:294.10
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-294.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 294 (Special Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of these administrative procedures is to set forth a process for State-specific rulemaking to address the management of inventoried roadless areas in areas where the Secretary determines that regulatory direction is appropriate based on a petition from the affected Governor.
