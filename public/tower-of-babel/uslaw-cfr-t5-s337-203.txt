5 C.F.R. § 337.203 — Public notice requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:5:337.203
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-337.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 337 (Examining System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies must comply with public notice requirements as prescribed in 5 U.S.C. 3327 and 3330, and subpart A of part 330 of this chapter with respect to any position that an agency seeks to fill using direct-hire authority.
