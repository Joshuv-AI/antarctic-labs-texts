33 U.S.C. § 668 — “Hydraulic mining” and “mining by hydraulic process” defined
Citation: us:usc:33:668
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section668
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 14 (California Debris Commission). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purposes of this chapter “hydraulic mining” and “mining by the hydraulic process” are declared to have the meaning and application given to said terms in the State of California.
