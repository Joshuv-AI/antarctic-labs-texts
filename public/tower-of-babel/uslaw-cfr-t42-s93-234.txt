42 C.F.R. § 93.234 — Research misconduct.
Citation: us:cfr:42:93.234
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-93.234
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 93 (Public Health Service Policies On Research Misconduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Research misconduct means fabrication, falsification, or plagiarism in proposing, performing, or reviewing research, or in reporting research results. Research misconduct does not include honest error or differences of opinion.
