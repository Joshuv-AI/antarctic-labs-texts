41 C.F.R. § 301-11.16 — -11.16 Lodging tax reimbursement.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-11.16
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-11.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-11 (11—Subsistence Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) For CONUS and non-foreign OCONUS locations, lodging taxes paid by the employee are reimbursable as a miscellaneous travel expense limited to the taxes on reimbursable lodging costs.
(b) For foreign areas, separate claims for lodging taxes are not allowed because lodging taxes have not been removed from foreign per diem rates established by the Department of State.
