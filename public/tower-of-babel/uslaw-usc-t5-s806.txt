5 U.S.C. § 806 — Applicability; severability
Citation: us:usc:5:806
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section806
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part I (The Agencies Generally); Chapter 8 (Congressional Review Of Agency Rulemaking). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
This chapter shall apply notwithstanding any other provision of law.
(b)
If any provision of this chapter or the application of any provision of this chapter to any person or circumstance, is held invalid, the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances, and the remainder of this chapter, shall not be affected thereby.
