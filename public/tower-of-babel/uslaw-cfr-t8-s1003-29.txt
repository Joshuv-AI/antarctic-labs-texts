8 C.F.R. § 1003.29 — Continuances.
Citation: us:cfr:8:1003.29
Creator: Executive Office For Immigration Review, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-1003.29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 1003 (Executive Office For Immigration Review). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The immigration judge may grant a motion for continuance for good cause shown, provided that nothing in this section shall authorize a continuance that causes the adjudication of an asylum application to exceed 180 days in the absence of exceptional circumstances, consistent with section 208(d)(5)(A)(iii) of the Act and § 1003.10(b).
