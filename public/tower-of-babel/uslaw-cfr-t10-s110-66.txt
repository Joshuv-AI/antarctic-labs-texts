10 C.F.R. § 110.66 — Enforcement hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:10:110.66
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-110.66
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 110 (Export And Import Of Nuclear Equipment And Material). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If the licensee demands a hearing, the Commission will issue an order specifying the time and place.
(b) A hearing pursuant to this subpart will be conducted under the procedures in subpart G of part 2 of this chapter.
