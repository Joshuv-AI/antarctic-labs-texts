13 C.F.R. § 400.106 — Ex parte communications.
Citation: us:cfr:13:400.106
Creator: Emergency Steel Guarantee Loan Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-400.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 400 (Emergency Steel Guarantee Loan Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Oral or written communication, not on the public record, between any member of the Board and any party or parties interested in any matter pending before the Board concerning the substance of that matter is prohibited.
