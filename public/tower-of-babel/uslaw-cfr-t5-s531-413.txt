5 C.F.R. § 531.413 — Reports and evaluation of within-grade increase authority.
Citation: us:cfr:5:531.413
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-531.413
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 531 (Pay Under The General Schedule). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Reports. The Office of Personnel Management may require agencies to maintain records and report on the use of the authority to grant or withhold within-grade increases.
(b) Evaluation. The Office of Personnel Management may evaluate an agency's use of the authority to grant or withhold within-grade increases. An agency shall take any corrective action required by the Office.
