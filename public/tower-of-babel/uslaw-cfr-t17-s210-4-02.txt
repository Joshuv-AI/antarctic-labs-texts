17 C.F.R. § 210.4-02 — -02 Items not material.
Citation: us:cfr:17:210.4-02
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-210.4-02
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 210 (Form And Content Of And Requirements For Financial Statements, Securities Act Of 1933, Securities Exchange Act Of 1934, Investment Company Act Of 1940, Investment Advisers Act Of 1940, And Energy Policy And Conservation Act Of 1975). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the amount which would otherwise be required to be shown with respect to any item is not material, it need not be separately set forth. The combination of insignificant amounts is permitted.
