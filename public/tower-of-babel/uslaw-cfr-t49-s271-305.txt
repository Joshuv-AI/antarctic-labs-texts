49 C.F.R. § 271.305 — Reopened review.
Citation: us:cfr:49:271.305
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-271.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 271 (Risk Reduction Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Following approval of an RRP plan or an amendment to such a plan, FRA may reopen review of the plan or amendment, in whole or in part, for cause stated.
