32 C.F.R. § 239.3 — Policy.
Citation: us:cfr:32:239.3
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-239.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 239 (Homeowners Assistance Program—Application Processing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is DoD policy, in implementing section 3374 of title 42, United States Code, as amended by section 1001 of the ARRA (Pub. L. 111-5), that those eligible (see section 239.6 of this part) to participate in the HAP and Expanded HAP are treated fairly and receive available benefit as quickly as practicable.
