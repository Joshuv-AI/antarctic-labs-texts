14 C.F.R. § 27.659 — Mass balance.
Citation: us:cfr:14:27.659
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-27.659
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 27 (Airworthiness Standards: Normal Category Rotorcraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The rotors and blades must be mass balanced as necessary to—
(1) Prevent excessive vibration; and
(2) Prevent flutter at any speed up to the maximum forward speed.
(b) The structural integrity of the mass balance installation must be substantiated.
