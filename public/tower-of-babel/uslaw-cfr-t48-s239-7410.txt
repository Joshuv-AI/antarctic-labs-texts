48 C.F.R. § 239.7410 — 239.7410 Cancellation and termination.
Citation: us:cfr:48:239.7410
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-239.7410
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 239 (Acquisition Of Information Technology). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a)(1) Cancellation is stopping a requirement after placing of an order but before service starts.
(2) Termination is stopping a requirement after placing an order and after service starts.
(b) Determine cancellation or termination charges under the provisions of the applicable tariff or agreement/contract.
