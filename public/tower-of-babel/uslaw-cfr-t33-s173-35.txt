33 C.F.R. § 173.35 — Coast Guard validation sticker.
Citation: us:cfr:33:173.35
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-173.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 173 (Vessel Numbering And Casualty And Accident Reporting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person may operate a vessel except a vessel exempted in § 173.13 that has a number issued by the Coast Guard unless it has the validation sticker issued with the certificate of number displayed within 6 inches of the number.
