8 U.S.C. § 1644 — Communication between State and local government agencies and Immigration and Naturalization Service
Citation: us:usc:8:1644
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title8-section1644
Context: United States Code, Title 8 (Aliens And Nationality); Chapter 14 (Restricting Welfare And Public Benefits For Aliens); Subchapter IV (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of Federal, State, or local law, no State or local government entity may be prohibited, or in any way restricted, from sending to or receiving from the Immigration and Naturalization Service information regarding the immigration status, lawful or unlawful, of an alien in the United States.
