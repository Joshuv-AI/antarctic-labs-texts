31 C.F.R. § 558.206 — Exempt transactions.
Citation: us:cfr:31:558.206
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-558.206
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 558 (South Sudan Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The prohibitions contained in this part do not apply to any transactions that are exempt pursuant to section 203(b) of the International Emergency Economic Powers Act (50 U.S.C. 1702(b)).
