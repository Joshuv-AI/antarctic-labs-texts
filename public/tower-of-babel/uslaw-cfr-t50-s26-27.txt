50 C.F.R. § 26.27 — Exception for entry on designated routes of travel.
Citation: us:cfr:50:26.27
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-26.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 26 (Public Entry And Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A permit is not required to enter, travel on, and exit from any national wildlife refuge on public waters and roads, and such roads, trails, footpaths, walkways, or other routes and areas which are designated for public use under the provisions of this subchapter C.
