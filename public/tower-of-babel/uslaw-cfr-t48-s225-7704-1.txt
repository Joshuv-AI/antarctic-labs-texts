48 C.F.R. § 225.7704-1 — 225.7704-1 Applicability of trade agreements.
Citation: us:cfr:48:225.7704-1
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-225.7704-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 225 (Foreign Acquisition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As authorized by the United States Trade Representative, the Secretary of Defense has waived the prohibition in section 302(a) of the Trade Agreements Act (see subpart 225.4) for acquisitions by DoD, and by GSA on behalf of DoD, of products and services from SC/CASA states in direct support of operations in Afghanistan.
