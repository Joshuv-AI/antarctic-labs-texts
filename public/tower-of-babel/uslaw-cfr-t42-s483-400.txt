42 C.F.R. § 483.400 — Basis and purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:42:483.400
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-483.400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 483 (Requirements For States And Long Term Care Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart implements section 1905 (c) and (d) of the Act which gives the Secretary authority to prescribe regulations for intermediate care facility services in facilities for individuals with intellectual disabilities or persons with related conditions.
