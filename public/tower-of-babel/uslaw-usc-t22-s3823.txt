22 U.S.C. § 3823 — Regulations governing inspection
Citation: us:usc:22:3823
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section3823
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 51 (Panama Canal); Subchapter I (Administration And Regulations); Part 8 (Shipping And Navigation); Subpart ii (Inspection Of Vessels). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commission shall prescribe, and from time to time may amend, regulations concerning the inspection of vessels conforming as nearly as practicable to the laws and regulations governing marine inspection by the United States Coast Guard.
