15 U.S.C. § 3615 — Nonexclusion of other statutory rights and remedies
Citation: us:usc:15:3615
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section3615
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 62 (Condominium And Cooperative Conversion Protection And Abuse Relief). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The rights and remedies provided by this chapter shall be in addition to any and all other rights and remedies that may exist under Federal or State law.
