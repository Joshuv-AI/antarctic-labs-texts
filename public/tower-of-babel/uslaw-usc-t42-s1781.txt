42 U.S.C. § 1781 — Preschool programs
Citation: us:usc:42:1781
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1781
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 13A (Child Nutrition). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may extend the benefits of all school feeding programs conducted and supervised by the Department of Agriculture to include preschool programs operated as part of the school system.
