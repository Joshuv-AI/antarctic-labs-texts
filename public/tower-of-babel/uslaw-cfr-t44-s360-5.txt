44 C.F.R. § 360.5 — General provisions for State Cooperative Agreement.
Citation: us:cfr:44:360.5
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-360.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 360 (State Assistance Programs For Training And Education In Comprehensive Emergency Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The legal funding instrument for the State Assistance Program for Training and Education FEMA is the State Cooperative Agreement. All States will be required to comply with FEMA General Provisions for the State Cooperative Agreement. The General Provisions for the State Cooperative Agreement will be provided to the States as part of the Request for Application package. The General Provisions will become part of the Cooperative Agreement.
