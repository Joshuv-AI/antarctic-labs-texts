22 C.F.R. § 237.14 — Arbitration.

Creator: Agency For International Development
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 237 (Ukraine Loan Guarantees Issued Under The Department Of State, Foreign Operations, And Related Programs Appropriations Act Of 2015, And The Support For The Sovereignty, Integrity, Democracy, And Economic Stability Of Ukraine Act Of 2014—Standard Terms And Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any controversy or claim between USAID and any Noteholder arising out of this Guarantee shall be settled by arbitration to be held in Washington, DC in accordance with the then prevailing rules of the American Arbitration Association, and judgment on the award rendered by the arbitrators may be entered in any court of competent jurisdiction.
