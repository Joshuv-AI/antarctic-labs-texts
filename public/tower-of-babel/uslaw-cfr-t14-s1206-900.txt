14 C.F.R. § 1206.900 — FOIA offices and electronic libraries.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1206.900
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1206.900
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1206 (Procedures For Disclosure Of Records Under The Freedom Of Information Act (Foia)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) NASA Headquarters and each NASA Center have a FOIA Electronic Library on the Internet. The Electronic library addresses are located on the NASA FOIA homepage http://www.hq.nasa.gov/office/pao/FOIA/agency/
(b) In addition, a requester may submit a FOIA request electronically. The addresses are located on the NASA FOIA homepage under each Center link.
