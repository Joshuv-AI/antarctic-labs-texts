41 C.F.R. § 50-205.6 — -205.6 Complaints.
Citation: us:cfr:41:50-205.6
Creator: Chapter 50—Public Contracts, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-50-205.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 50-205 (205—Enforcement Of Safety And Health Standards By State Officers And Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a complaint of alleged safety and health violations by an employer apparently subject to the Act is filed with a State agency, that agency shall transmit a copy of the complaint to the cooperating office of the Department of Labor within 5 days from the receipt of the complaint. All complaints shall be considered confidential and shall not be disclosed to any employer without the consent of the complainant.
