42 C.F.R. § 82.33 — How will NIOSH inform the public of changes to the scientific elements underlying the dose reconstruction process?
Citation: us:cfr:42:82.33
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-82.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 82 (Methods For Conducting Dose Reconstruction Under The Energy Employees Occupational Illness Compensation Program Act Of 2000). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) NIOSH will publish a notice in the Federal Register informing the public of changes and the rationale for the changes. This notice will also provide a summary of the recommendations and comments received from the Advisory Board and the public, as well as responses to the comments.
(b) NIOSH may take into account other factors and employ other procedures than those specified in this subpart, if circumstances arise that require NIOSH to implement a change more immediately than the procedures in this subpart allow.
