7 U.S.C. § 2147 — Inspection by legally constituted law enforcement agencies
Citation: us:usc:7:2147
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2147
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 54 (Transportation, Sale, And Handling Of Certain Animals). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall promulgate rules and regulations requiring dealers, exhibitors, research facilities, and operators of auction sales subject to section 2142 of this title to permit inspection of their animals and records at reasonable hours upon request by legally constituted law enforcement agencies in search of lost animals.
