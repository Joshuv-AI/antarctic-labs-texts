48 C.F.R. § 32.502-1 — 32.502-1 Use of customary progress payments.
Citation: us:cfr:48:32.502-1
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-32.502-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 32 (Contract Financing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer may use a Progress Payments clause in solicitations and contracts, in accordance with this subpart. The contracting officer must reject as nonresponsive bids conditioned on progress payments when the solicitation did not provide for progress payments.
