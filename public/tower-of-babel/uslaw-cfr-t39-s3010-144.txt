39 C.F.R. § 3010.144 — Limitation of participation by investigative or prosecuting officers.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3010.144
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3010.144
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3010 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No officer, employee, or agent of the Commission who participates in a proceeding before the Commission as an attorney or witness or who actively participates in the preparation of evidence or argument presented by such persons, shall participate or advise as to the intermediate decision or Commission decision in that proceeding.
