40 C.F.R. § 49.10409 — Permits to operate.

Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-49.10409
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 49 (Indian Country: Air Quality Planning And Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Permits to operate are required for sources not subject to 40 CFR Part 71 in accordance with the requirements of § 49.139.
