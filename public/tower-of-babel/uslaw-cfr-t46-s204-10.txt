46 C.F.R. § 204.10 — Attorney's fees.
Citation: us:cfr:46:204.10
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-204.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 204 (Claims Against The Maritime Administration Under The Federal Tort Claims Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Attorney's fees for any claim settled under this part are limited to not more than twenty percent of the amount paid in settlement.
