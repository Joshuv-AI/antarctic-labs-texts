20 C.F.R. § 429.105 — What happens when you receive my claim?
Citation: us:cfr:20:429.105
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-429.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 429 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When we receive your claim, we will investigate to determine its validity. After our investigation, we will forward your claim to the SSA Claims Officer with our recommendation as to whether your claim should be fully or partially allowed or denied.
