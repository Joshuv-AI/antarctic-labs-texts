33 C.F.R. § 150.705 — What are the requirements for maintaining and inspecting aids to navigation?
Citation: us:cfr:33:150.705
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-150.705
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 150 (Deepwater Ports: Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) All aids to navigation must be maintained in proper operating condition at all times.
(b) The Coast Guard may inspect all aids to navigation at any time without notice.
