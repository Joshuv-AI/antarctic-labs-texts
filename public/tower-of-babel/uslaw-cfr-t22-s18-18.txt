22 C.F.R. § 18.18 — Proposed findings and conclusions.
Citation: us:cfr:22:18.18
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-18.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 18 (Regulations Concerning Post Employment Conflict Of Interest). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except in cases where the respondent has failed to answer the complaint or where a party has failed to appear at the hearing, the hearing examiner, prior to making his/her decision, shall afford the parties a reasonable opportunity to submit proposed findings and conclusions and supporting reasons therefor.
