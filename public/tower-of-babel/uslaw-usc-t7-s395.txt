7 U.S.C. § 395 — Fees for rabies diagnoses; disposition of moneys
Citation: us:usc:7:395
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section395
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 15 (Bureau Of Animal Industry). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Fees shall be charged for all diagnoses in connection with rabies, except those performed for agencies of the United States Government, in such amounts as the Secretary shall prescribe, and such fees shall be covered into the Treasury as miscellaneous receipts.
