7 C.F.R. § 1944.528 — Preapplication submission deadline.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1944.528
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Rural Business-Cooperative Service, And Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1944.528
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1944 (Housing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Dates governing the review and selection of TSA grant preapplications will be published annually in the Federal Register. Preapplications received after that time will not be considered for funding. For use of fiscal year 1979 funds, the deadline for submission of preapplications will be 45 calendar days from date of publication of final regulations.
