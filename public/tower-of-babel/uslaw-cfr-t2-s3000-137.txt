2 C.F.R. § 3000.137 — Who in the Department of Homeland Security may grant an exception to let an excluded person participate in a covered transaction?
Citation: us:cfr:2:3000.137
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-3000.137
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 3000 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within the Department of Homeland Security, the Secretary of Homeland Security has delegated the authority to grant an exception to let an excluded person participate in a covered transaction to the Head of the Contracting Activity for each DHS component as provided in the OMB guidance at 2 CFR 180.135.
