1 C.F.R. § 19.5 — Proclamations of treaties excluded.
Citation: us:cfr:1:19.5
Creator: Administrative Committee Of The Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-1/section-19.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 1 (General Provisions), Part 19 (Executive Orders And Presidential Proclamations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-23 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Consonant with the provisions of chapter 15 of title 44 of the United States Code (44 U.S.C. 1511), nothing in these regulations shall be construed to apply to treaties, conventions, protocols, or other international agreements, or proclamations thereof by the President.
