41 C.F.R. § 105-68.455 — -68.455 What may I do if a lower tier participant fails to disclose the information required under § 105-68.355 to the next higher tier?
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-68.455
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-68.455
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-68 (68—Governmentwide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you as an agency official determine that a lower tier participant failed to disclose information, as required by § 105-68.355, at the time it entered into a covered transaction with a participant at the next higher tier, you may pursue any remedies available to you, including the initiation of a suspension or debarment action.
