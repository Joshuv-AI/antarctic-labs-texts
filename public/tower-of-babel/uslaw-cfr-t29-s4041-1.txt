29 C.F.R. § 4041.1 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:29:4041.1
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4041.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4041 (Termination Of Single-Employer Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth the rules and procedures for terminating a single-employer plan in a standard or distress termination under section 4041 of ERISA, the exclusive means of voluntarily terminating a plan.
