46 C.F.R. § 173.001 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:46:173.001
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-173.001
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 173 (Special Rules Pertaining To Vessel Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each vessel that is engaged in one of the following activities must comply with the applicable provisions of this part:
(a) Lifting.
(b) Training (schoolship).
(c) Oceanographic research.
(d) Towing.
