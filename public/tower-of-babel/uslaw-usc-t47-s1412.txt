47 U.S.C. § 1412 — Flexible use of narrowband spectrum
Citation: us:usc:47:1412
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title47-section1412
Context: United States Code, Title 47 (Telecommunications); Chapter 13 (Public Safety Communications And Electromagnetic Spectrum Auctions); Subchapter I (Reallocation Of Public Safety Spectrum). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commission may allow the narrowband spectrum to be used in a flexible manner, including usage for public safety broadband communications, subject to such technical and interference protection measures as the Commission may require.
