48 C.F.R. § 1326.5 — 1326.5 Suspension of payments, termination of contract and debarment and suspension actions.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1326.5
Creator: Chapter 13—Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1326.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1326 (Other Socioeconomic Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The designee authorized to waive a determination to suspend contract payments, terminate a contract for default, or debar or suspend a contractor for Drug-Free Workplace violations, is set forth in CAM 1301.70. This authority may not be delegated.
