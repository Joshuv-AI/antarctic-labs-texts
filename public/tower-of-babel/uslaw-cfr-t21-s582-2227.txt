21 C.F.R. § 582.2227 — Calcium silicate.
Citation: us:cfr:21:582.2227
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-582.2227
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 582 (Substances Generally Recognized As Safe). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Product. Calcium silicate.
(b) Tolerance. 2 percent and 5 percent.
(c) Limitations, restrictions, or explanation. This substance is generally recognized as safe when used at levels not exceeding 2 percent in table salt and 5 percent in baking powder in accordance with good manufacturing or feeding practice.
