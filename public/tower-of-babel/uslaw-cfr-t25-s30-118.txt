25 C.F.R. § 30.118 — When should the tribal governing body or school board request technical assistance?
Citation: us:cfr:25:30.118
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-30.118
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 30 (Standards, Assessments, And Accountability System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A tribal governing body or school board may request technical assistance in writing at any time. A tribal governing body or school board is welcomed and encouraged to request technical assistance before formally notifying the Secretary of its intention to waive the requirements established by the Secretary in order to maximize the time available for technical assistance.
