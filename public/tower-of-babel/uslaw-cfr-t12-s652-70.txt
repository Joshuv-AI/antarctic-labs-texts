12 C.F.R. § 652.70 — Risk-based capital level.
Citation: us:cfr:12:652.70
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-652.70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 652 (Federal Agricultural Mortgage Corporation Funding And Fiscal Affairs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The risk-based capital level is the sum of the following amounts:
(a) Credit and interest rate risk. The amount of risk-based capital determined by the risk-based capital test under § 652.65.
(b) Management and operations risk. Thirty (30) percent of the amount of risk-based capital determined by the risk-based capital test in § 652.65.
