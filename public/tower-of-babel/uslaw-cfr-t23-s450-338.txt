23 C.F.R. § 450.338 — Applicability of NEPA to metropolitan transportation plans and programs.
Citation: us:cfr:23:450.338
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-450.338
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 450 (Planning Assistance And Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any decision by the Secretary concerning a metropolitan transportation plan or TIP developed through the processes provided for in 23 U.S.C. 134, 49 U.S.C. 5303, and this subpart shall not be considered to be a Federal action subject to review under the National Environmental Policy Act of 1969 (42 U.S.C. 4321 et seq.).
