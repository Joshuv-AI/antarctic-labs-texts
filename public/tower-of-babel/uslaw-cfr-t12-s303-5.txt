12 C.F.R. § 303.5 — Effect of Community Reinvestment Act performance on filings.
Citation: us:cfr:12:303.5
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-303.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 303 (Filing Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Among other factors, the FDIC takes into account the record of performance under the Community Reinvestment Act (CRA) of each applicant in considering a filing for approval of:
(a) The establishment of a domestic branch;
(b) The relocation of the bank's main office or a domestic branch;
(c) The relocation of an insured branch of a foreign bank;
(d) A transaction subject to the Bank Merger Act; and
(e) Deposit insurance.
