7 C.F.R. § 550.112 — Administrative supervision.
Citation: us:cfr:7:550.112
Creator: Agricultural Research Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-550.112
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 550 (General Administrative Policy For Non-Assistance Cooperative Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Cooperator is responsible for employer/employee relations such as personnel, performance, and time management issues. The Cooperator is solely responsible for the administrative supervision of its employees, even when its employees are working in Agency facilities.
