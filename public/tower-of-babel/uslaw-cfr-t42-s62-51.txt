42 C.F.R. § 62.51 — What is the scope and purpose of the State Loan Repayment Program?
Citation: us:cfr:42:62.51
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-62.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 62 (National Health Service Corps Scholarship And Loan Repayment Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations of this subpart apply to the award of grants authorized by section 338H of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 254q-1) to support the establishment of State programs similar to the National Health Service Corps Loan Repayment Program authorized by section 338B of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 2541-1). The purpose of this program is to improve the delivery of health services in medically underserved areas.
