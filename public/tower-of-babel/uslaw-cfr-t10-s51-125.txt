10 C.F.R. § 51.125 — Responsible official.
Citation: us:cfr:10:51.125
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-51.125
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 51 (Environmental Protection Regulations For Domestic Licensing And Related Regulatory Functions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Executive Director for Operations shall be responsible for overall review of NRC NEPA compliance, except for matters under the jurisdiction of a presiding officer, administrative judge, administrative law judge, Atomic Safety and Licensing Board, or the Commission acting as a collegial body.
