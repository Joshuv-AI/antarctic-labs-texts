    Abstract: More than 100 national parks contract with private 
businesses to provide lodging, food, tours, boating, whitewater 
rafting, and other visitor services. These concessions generate over $1 
billion annually and support more than 25,000 peak-season jobs.
    Regulations at 36 CFR part 51 implement Title IV of the National 
Parks Omnibus Management Act of 1998 (54 U.S.C. 101911 et seq.), which 
establishes the authority and requirements for soliciting, awarding, 
and administering NPS concession contracts. The law requires the 
Secretary of the Interior to award contracts through a competitive 
selection process to the best-qualified offeror, with simplified 
procedures for small, individually owned concessions.
    For this purpose of this submission, we are requesting an extension 
of the currently approved forms associated with the evaluation and 
administration of NPS concessions proposals and contracts.
    Concessioner Annual Financial Reports are used to verify franchise 
fee calculations, evaluate a concessioner's financial performance, and 
monitor compliance with contractual requirements.

 Form 10-356 Concessioner Annual Financial Report
 Form 10-356A Concessioner Annual Financial Report (For 
Concessioners with Gross Receipts Less than $500,000)
 Form 10-356B Concessioner Annual Financial Report (Special 
Accounts and Utility Add-ons)

    Forms for Concession Opportunity Proposals are used to objectively 
review and evaluate proposals, compare offerors, and identify the 
proposal best suited for long term, sustainable concession operations.

 Forms 10-357A and 10-357B (Business Organization Information)
 Form 10-358 (Business History Information)
 Forms 10-359A and 10-359B (Financial Projections--Large and 
Small Concessions)

    Additional narrative submissions that allow the NPS to evaluate 
proposals and administer concession contracts include credit reports 
and financial statement, Offeror's Transmittal Letter, Certificate of 
Business Entity Offeror, appeals, capital improvement requests, 
construction reports, applications to sell or transfer operations, and 
recordkeeping documentation.
