22 C.F.R. § 172.7 — Procedure in the event of an adverse ruling.
Citation: us:cfr:22:172.7
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-172.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 172 (Service Of Process; Production Or Disclosure Of Official Information In Response To Court Orders, Subpoenas, Notices Of Depositions, Requests For Admissions, Interrogatories, Or Similar Requests Or Demands In Connection With Federal Or State Litigation; Expert Testimony). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the court or other judicial or quasi-judicial authority declines to stay the effect of the demand in response to a request made pursuant to § 172.6, or if the court or other authority rules that the demand must be complied with irrespective of the Department's instructions not to produce the material or disclose the information sought, the employee upon whom the demand has been made shall respectfully decline to comply with the demand, citing this part and United States ex rel. Touhy v. Ragen, 340 U.S. 462 (1951).
