38 C.F.R. § 21.5150 — State approving agencies.
Citation: us:cfr:38:21.5150
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-21.5150
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 21 (Veteran Readiness And Employment And Education). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In administering chapter 32, title 38, United States Code, VA will apply the provisions of the following sections:
(a) Section 21.4150 (except par. (e))—Designation;
(b) Section 21.4151—Cooperation;
(c) Section 21.4152—Control by agencies of the United States;
(d) Section 21.4153—Reimbursement of expenses;
(e) Section 21.4154—Report of activities;
(f) Section 21.4155—Evaluations of State approving agency performance.
