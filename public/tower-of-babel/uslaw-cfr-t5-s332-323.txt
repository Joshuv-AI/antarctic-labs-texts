5 C.F.R. § 332.323 — Employees separated during probation.
Citation: us:cfr:5:332.323
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-332.323
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 332 (Recruitment And Selection Through Competitive Examination). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee who is separated (voluntarily or involuntarily) without delinquency or misconduct during his probationary period is entitled to have his name restored to the register of eligibles from which he was appointed, if he applies for restoration while the register is still in use.
