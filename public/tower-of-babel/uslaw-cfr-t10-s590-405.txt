10 C.F.R. § 590.405 — Transferability.
Citation: us:cfr:10:590.405
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-590.405
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 590 (Administrative Procedures With Respect To The Import And Export Of Natural Gas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Authorizations by the Assistant Secretary to import or export natural gas shall not be transferable or assignable, unless specifically authorized by the Assistant Secretary.
