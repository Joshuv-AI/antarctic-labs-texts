25 C.F.R. § 83.56 — Can a petitioner withdraw its re-petition request?
Citation: us:cfr:25:83.56
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-83.56
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 83 (Procedures For Federal Acknowledgment Of Indian Tribes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A petitioner can withdraw its re-petition request at any point in the process and re-submit the request at a later date within the five-year time limit applicable to the petitioner under § 83.49. Upon re-submission, the re-petition request will lose its original place in line and be considered after other re-petition requests awaiting review.
