43 U.S.C. § 620i — Expenditures; units excepted from soil survey and land classification requirements
Citation: us:usc:43:620i
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section620i
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 12B (Colorado River Storage Project). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Expenditures for the Flaming Gorge, Glen Canyon, Wayne N. Aspinall, and Navajo initial units of the Colorado River storage project may be made without regard to the soil survey and land classification requirements of section 390a 1 of this title.
