18 C.F.R. § 284.265 — Cost recovery by interstate pipeline.
Citation: us:cfr:18:284.265
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-284.265
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 284 (Certain Sales And Transportation Of Natural Gas Under The Natural Gas Policy Act Of 1978 And Related Authorities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except as provided in paragraph (b), an interstate pipeline that provides emergency natural gas, whether from its system supply or by special purchase, must directly assign the emergency gas costs to the recipient.
(b) If an interstate pipeline cannot identify individual recipients, the interstate pipeline must roll the emergency gas costs into its general system supply costs.
