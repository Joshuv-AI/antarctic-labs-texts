16 C.F.R. § 1702.11 — Product specifications.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1702.11
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1702.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1702 (Petitions For Exemptions From Poison Prevention Packaging Act Requirements; Petition Procedures And Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each petition for an exemption shall include:
(a) A complete quantitative formula for the product, including inert ingredients, diluents, and solvents. (Petitioners should refer to § 1702.6 for information regarding trade secrets.)
(b) A listing of all physical forms or dosage forms (whichever is appropriate) in which the product is available.
