20 C.F.R. § 30.516 — Under what circumstances would recovery of an overpayment defeat the purpose of the Act?
Citation: us:cfr:20:30.516
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-30.516
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 30 (Claims For Compensation Under The Energy Employees Occupational Illness Compensation Program Act Of 2000, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Recovery of an overpayment will defeat the purpose of the Act if such recovery would cause hardship to the recipient because:
(a) The recipient from whom OWCP seeks recovery needs substantially all of his or her current income to meet current ordinary and necessary living expenses; and
(b) The recipient's assets do not exceed two months' expenditures as determined by OWCP using the Bureau of Labor Statistics Consumer Expenditure Survey tables.
