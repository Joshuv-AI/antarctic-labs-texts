36 U.S.C. § 190102 — Purposes
Citation: us:usc:36:190102
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section190102
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1901 (Reserve Officers Association Of The United States). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purposes of the corporation are to support and promote the development and execution of a military policy for the United States that will provide adequate national security.
