48 C.F.R. § 247.270-3 — 247.270-3 Evaluation of bids and proposals.
Citation: us:cfr:48:247.270-3
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-247.270-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 247 (Transportation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As a minimum, require that offers include—
(a) Tonnage or commodity rates that apply to the bulk of the cargo worked under normal conditions;
(b) Labor-hour rates that apply to services not covered by commodity rates, or to work performed under hardship conditions; and
(c) Rates for equipment rental.
