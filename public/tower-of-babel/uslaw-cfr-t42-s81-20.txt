42 C.F.R. § 81.20 — Required use of NIOSH-IREP.
Citation: us:cfr:42:81.20
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-81.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 81 (Guidelines For Determining Probability Of Causation Under The Energy Employees Occupational Illness Compensation Program Act Of 2000). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) NIOSH-IREP is an interactive software program for estimating probability of causation for covered employees seeking compensation for cancer under EEOICPA, other than as members of the Special Exposure Cohort seeking compensation for a specified cancer.
(b) DOL is required to use NIOSH-IREP to estimate probability of causation for all cancers, as identified under §§ 81.21 and 81.23.
