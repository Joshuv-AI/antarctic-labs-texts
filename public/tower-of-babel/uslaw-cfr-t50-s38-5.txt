50 C.F.R. § 38.5 — Emergency authority.
Citation: us:cfr:50:38.5
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-38.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 38 (Midway Atoll National Wildlife Refuge). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

During the imminence and duration of any emergency, the Regional Director may perform any lawful acts necessary to protect life and property on Midway Atoll National Wildlife Refuge.
