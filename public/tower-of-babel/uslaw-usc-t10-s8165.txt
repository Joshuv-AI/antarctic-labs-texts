10 U.S.C. § 8165 — Staff corps officers: limitation on power to command
Citation: us:usc:10:8165
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8165
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 821 (Officers In Command). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An officer in a staff corps may command only such activities as are appropriate to his corps.
