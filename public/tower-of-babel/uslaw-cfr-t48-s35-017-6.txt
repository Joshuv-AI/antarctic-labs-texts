48 C.F.R. § 35.017-6 — 35.017-6 Master list of FFRDC's.
Citation: us:cfr:48:35.017-6
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-35.017-6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 35 (Research And Development Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The National Science Foundation (NSF) maintains a master Government list of FFRDC's. Primary sponsors will provide information on each FFRDC, including sponsoring agreements, mission statements, funding data, and type of R&D being performed, to the NSF upon its request for such information.
