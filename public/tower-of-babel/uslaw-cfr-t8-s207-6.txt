8 C.F.R. § 207.6 — Control over approved refugee numbers.
Citation: us:cfr:8:207.6
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-207.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 207 (Admission Of Refugees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Current numerical accounting of approved refugees is maintained for each special group designated by the President. As refugee status is authorized for each applicant, the total count is reduced correspondingly from the appropriate group so that information is readily available to indicate how many refugee numbers remain available for issuance.
