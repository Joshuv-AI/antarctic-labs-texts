31 C.F.R. § 355.12 — What requirements apply if the check is payable to two or more persons?
Citation: us:cfr:31:355.12
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-355.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 355 (Regulations Governing Fiscal Agency Checks). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the fiscal agency check is payable to two or more persons, the requirements of this part apply to all designated payees.
