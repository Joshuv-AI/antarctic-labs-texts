25 C.F.R. § 166.608 — What types of insurance may be required?
Citation: us:cfr:25:166.608
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-166.608
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 166 (Grazing Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We may require liability or casualty insurance (such as for fire, hazard, or flood), depending upon the activity conducted under the permit.
