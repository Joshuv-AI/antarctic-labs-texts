7 C.F.R. § 654.31 — Performing operation and maintenance.
Citation: us:cfr:7:654.31
Creator: Natural Resources Conservation Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-654.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 654 (Operation And Maintenance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The method of performing O&M is to be at the option of the land user. The NRCS, working through districts, will furnish information and technical assistance as needed and requested to the extent NRCS resources permit.
