7 C.F.R. § 1783.5 — What are the eligibility criteria for grant recipients?
Citation: us:cfr:7:1783.5
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1783.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1783 (Revolving Funds For Financing Water And Wastewater Projects (Revolving Fund Program)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The applicant must be a private entity.
(b) The applicant must be organized as a non-profit entity.
(c) The applicant must have the legal capacity and lawful authority to perform the obligations of a grantee under this part.
(d) The applicant must have sufficient expertise and experience in making and servicing loans to assure the likelihood that the objectives of this part can be achieved.
