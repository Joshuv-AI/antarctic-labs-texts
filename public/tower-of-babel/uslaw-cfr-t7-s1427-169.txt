7 C.F.R. § 1427.169 — Fees, charges, and interest.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1427.169
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1427.169
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1427 (Cotton). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A producer must pay a non-refundable loan service fee at a rate determined by CCC.
(b) Interest which accrues for a loan will be determined under part 1405 of this chapter.
