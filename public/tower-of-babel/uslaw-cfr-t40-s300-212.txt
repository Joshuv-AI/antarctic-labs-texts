40 C.F.R. § 300.212 — Area response drills.
Citation: us:cfr:40:300.212
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-300.212
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 300 (National Oil And Hazardous Substances Pollution Contingency Plan). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The OSC periodically shall conduct drills of removal capability (including fish and wildlife response capability), without prior notice, in areas for which ACPs are required by § 300.210(c) and under relevant tank vessel and facility response plans.
