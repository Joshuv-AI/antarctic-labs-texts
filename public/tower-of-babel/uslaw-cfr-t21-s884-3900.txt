21 C.F.R. § 884.3900 — Vaginal stent.
Citation: us:cfr:21:884.3900
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-884.3900
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 884 (Obstetrical And Gynecological Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. A vaginal stent is a device used to enlarge the vagina by stretching, or to support the vagina and to hold a skin graft after reconstructive surgery.
(b) Classification. Class II (performance standards).
