37 U.S.C. § 413 — Chairman and Vice Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff
Citation: us:usc:37:413
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title37-section413
Context: United States Code, Title 37 (Pay And Allowances Of The Uniformed Services); Chapter 7 (Allowances Other Than Travel And Transportation Allowances). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Chairman and Vice Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff are entitled to the allowances provided by law for the Chief of Staff of the Army.
