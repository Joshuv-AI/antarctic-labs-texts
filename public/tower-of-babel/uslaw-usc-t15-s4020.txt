15 U.S.C. § 4020 — Rules and regulations
Citation: us:usc:15:4020
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section4020
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 66 (Promotion Of Export Trade); Subchapter II (Export Trade Certificates Of Review). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary, with the concurrence of the Attorney General, shall promulgate such rules and regulations as are necessary to carry out the purposes of this chapter.
