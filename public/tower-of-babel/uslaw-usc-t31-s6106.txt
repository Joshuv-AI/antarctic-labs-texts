31 U.S.C. § 6106 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:31:6106
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section6106
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 61 (Program Information). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

After October 1, 1983, there may be appropriated to the Administrator such sums as may be necessary to carry out the responsibilities of this chapter.
