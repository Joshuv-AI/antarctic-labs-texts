48 C.F.R. § 237.102-73 — 237.102-73 Prohibition on contracts for services of senior mentors.
Citation: us:cfr:48:237.102-73
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-237.102-73
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 237 (Service Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

DoD is prohibited from entering into contracts for the services of senior mentors. See PGI 237.102-73 for references to DoD policy and implementation guidance.
