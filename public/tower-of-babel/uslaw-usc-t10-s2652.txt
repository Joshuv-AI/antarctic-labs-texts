10 U.S.C. § 2652 — Prohibition on charge of certain tariffs on aircraft traveling through channel routes
Citation: us:usc:10:2652
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2652
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Property); Chapter 157 (Transportation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States Transportation Command may not charge a tariff by reason of the use by a military service of an aircraft of that military service on a route designated by the United States Transportation Command as a channel route.
