40 C.F.R. § 1045.655 — What special provisions apply for installing and removing altitude kits?
Citation: us:cfr:40:1045.655
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1045.655
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1045 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An action for the purpose of installing or modifying altitude kits and performing other changes to compensate for changing altitude is not considered a prohibited act under 40 CFR 1068.101(b) as long as it is done consistent with the manufacturer's instructions.
