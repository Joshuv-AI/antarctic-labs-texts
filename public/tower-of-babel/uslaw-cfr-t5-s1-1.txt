5 C.F.R. § 1.1 — Positions and employees affected by the rules in this subchapter.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1.1
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1 (Coverage And Definitions (Rule I)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules in this subchapter shall apply to all positions in the competitive service and to all incumbents of such positions. Except as expressly provided in the rule concerned, the rules in this subchapter shall not apply to positions and employees in the excepted service.
