47 C.F.R. § 96.65 — Spectrum access system administrator fees.
Citation: us:cfr:47:96.65
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-96.65
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 96 (Citizens Broadband Radio Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An SAS Administrator may charge Citizens Broadband Radio Service users a reasonable fee for provision of the services set forth in subpart F of this part.
(b) The Commission, upon request, will review the fees and can require changes to those fees if they are found to be unreasonable.
