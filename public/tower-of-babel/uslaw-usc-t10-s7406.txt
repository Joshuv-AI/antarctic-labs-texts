10 U.S.C. § 7406 — Service schools: leaves of absence for instructors
Citation: us:usc:10:7406
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7406
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training); Chapter 751 (Training Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The officer in charge of an Army service school may grant a leave of absence for the period of the suspension of the ordinary academic studies, without reduction of pay or allowances, to any officer on duty exclusively as an instructor at the school.
