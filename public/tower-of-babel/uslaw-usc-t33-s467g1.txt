33 U.S.C. § 467g–1 — Dam safety training
Citation: us:usc:33:467g–1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section467g–1
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 9 (Protection Of Navigable Waters And Of Harbor And River Improvements Generally); Subchapter VII (Dam Inspection Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

At the request of any State that has or intends to develop a State dam safety program, the Administrator shall provide training for State dam safety staff and inspectors.
