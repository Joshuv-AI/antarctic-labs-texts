40 C.F.R. § 267.1 — What are the purpose, scope and applicability of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:40:267.1
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-267.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 267 (Standards For Owners And Operators Of Hazardous Waste Facilities Operating Under A Standardized Permit). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The purpose of this part is to establish minimum national standards which define the acceptable management of hazardous waste under a 40 CFR part 270, subpart J standardized permit.
(b) This part applies to owners and operators of facilities who treat or store hazardous waste under a 40 CFR part 270, subpart J standardized permit, except as provided otherwise in 40 CFR part 261, subpart A, or 40 CFR 264.1(f) and (g).
