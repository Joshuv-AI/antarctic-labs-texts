29 C.F.R. § 1621.2 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1621.2
Creator: Equal Employment Opportunity Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1621.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1621 (Procedures—The Equal Pay Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of this part, the term the Act shall mean the Equal Pay Act the Commission shall mean the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission or any of its designated representatives.
