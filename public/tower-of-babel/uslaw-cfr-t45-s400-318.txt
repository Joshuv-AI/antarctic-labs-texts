45 C.F.R. § 400.318 — Eligible grantees.
Citation: us:cfr:45:400.318
Creator: Office Of Refugee Resettlement, Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-400.318
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 400 (Refugee Resettlement Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Eligible grantees are those agencies of State governments which are responsible for the refugee program under 45 CFR 400.5 in States containing counties which qualify for targeted assistance awards. The use of targeted assistance funds for services to Cuban and Haitian entrants is limited to States which have an approved State plan under the Cuban/Haitian Entrant Program (CHEP).
