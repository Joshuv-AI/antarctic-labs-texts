30 C.F.R. § 42.20 — Schedule of fees.
Citation: us:cfr:30:42.20
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-42.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 42 (National Mine Health And Safety Academy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Tuition fees will be computed on the basis of the cost to the Government for the Academy to conduct the course, as determined by the Superintendent of the Academy.
(b) The tuition fee for each course will be stated in the course announcement and will be reassessed on an annual basis.
