39 C.F.R. § 955.24 — (Rule 24) Motion for reconsideration.
Citation: us:cfr:39:955.24
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-955.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 955 (Rules Of Practice Before The Postal Service Board Of Contract Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A motion for reconsideration must be filed within 30 days of the moving party's receipt of the Board's decision. A motion for reconsideration must set forth the specific grounds for reconsideration.
