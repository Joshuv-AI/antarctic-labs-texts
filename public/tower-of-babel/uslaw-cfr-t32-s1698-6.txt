32 C.F.R. § 1698.6 — Issuance of advisory opinions.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1698.6
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1698.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1698 (Advisory Opinions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A copy of the advisory opinion will be furnished, without charge, to the requester therefor and to the individual to whom it pertains. A copy of an advisory opinion will be furnished, without charge, to any Federal, state, or municipal governmental agency upon request.
