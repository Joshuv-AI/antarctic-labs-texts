5 C.F.R. § 870.905 — Withholdings.
Citation: us:cfr:5:870.905
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-870.905
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 870 (Federal Employees' Group Life Insurance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Premium withholdings for assigned insurance are withheld from the salary, annuity, or compensation of the insured individual, as provided in subpart D of this part.
