32 C.F.R. § 269.5 — Application of increase to violations.
Citation: us:cfr:32:269.5
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-269.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 269 (Civil Monetary Penalty Inflation Adjustment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any increase in a civil monetary penalty under this part must apply only to civil monetary penalties, including those whose associated violation predated such increase, which are assessed after the date the increase takes effect (i.e., July 1, 2016).
