19 U.S.C. § 2705 — Impact study by Secretary of Labor
Citation: us:usc:19:2705
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section2705
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 15 (Caribbean Basin Economic Recovery). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Labor, in consulation 1 with other appropriate Federal agencies, shall undertake a continuing review and analysis of the impact which the implementation of the provisions of this chapter have with respect to United States labor; and shall make an annual written report to Congress on the results of such review and analysis.
