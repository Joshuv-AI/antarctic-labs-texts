9 C.F.R. § 329.9 — Criminal offenses.
Citation: us:cfr:9:329.9
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-329.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 329 (Detention; Seizure And Condemnation; Criminal Offenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Act contains criminal provisions with respect to numerous offenses specified in the Act, including but not limited to bribery of Program employees, receipt of gifts by Program employees, and forcible assaults on, or other interference with, Program employees while engaged in, or on account of, the performance of their official duties under the Act.
