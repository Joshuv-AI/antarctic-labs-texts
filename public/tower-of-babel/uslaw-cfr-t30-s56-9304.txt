30 C.F.R. § 56.9304 — Unstable ground.
Citation: us:cfr:30:56.9304
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-56.9304
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 56 (Safety And Health Standards—Surface Metal And Nonmetal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Dumping locations shall be visually inspected prior to work commencing and as ground conditions warrant.
(b) Where there is evidence that the ground at a dumping location may fail to support the mobile equipment, loads shall be dumped a safe distance back from the edge of the unstable area of the bank.
