Diffusive charge transport in the gapped 1D Hubbard model at all finite temperatures
J. M. P. Carmelo, P. D. Sacramento

Abstract
Studies relying on hydrodynamic theory and Kardar-Parisi-Zhang (KPZ) scaling have found that in the one-dimensional Hubbard model spin and charge transport are for all temperatures T > 0 T>0 anomalous superdiffusive at zero magnetic field, h = 0 h=0 , and zero chemical potential, μ = 0 \mu=0 , respectively. However, this contradicts recent exact results that at very low temperature charge transport rather is normal diffusive. In this Letter we identify the mechanisms that control the different types of temperature dependence of the h = 0 h=0 spin and μ = 0 \mu=0 charge transport and find that the latter is normal diffusive for all finite temperatures T > 0 T>0 , in contrast to the hydrodynamic theory and KPZ scaling predictions.

VI - Einstein relation and the charge dc conductivity

According to the Einstein relation, the charge dc conductivity
\sigma_{\eta}(T)

is related to the charge diffusion constant
D_{\eta}(T)
as

\sigma_{\eta}(T)=\chi_{\eta}(T)D_{\eta}(T)
. Here
\chi_{\eta}(T)
is the uniform charge susceptibility.

By combining the expressions given in Eq. (3) of the Letter with Eq. (54) of Ref.
Carmelo_25
, we find that
at the
h=\mu=0
point the charge dc conductivity
\sigma_{\eta}(T)
can be written for all finite temperatures
T>0
as,

\sigma_{\eta}(T)={L\,\langle|j_{\eta,\pm 1/2}|^{2}\rangle_{m_{sz},T}\over 8v_{\eta{\rm LR}}\,f_{\eta}(T)}={L\,\langle|j_{\eta,\pm 1/2}|^{2}\rangle_{0,T}\over 8v_{\eta{\rm LR}}\,f_{\eta}(T)}\,.

(41)

Here
\langle|j_{\eta,\pm 1/2}|^{2}\rangle_{m_{sz},T}
is defined in Eq. (4) of the Letter,

m_{sz}=0
at the
h=\mu=0
point,
v_{\eta{\rm LR}}
is the charge Lieb-Robinson velocity
Carmelo_25
,
and
f_{\eta}(T)
is the second derivative of the
free energy density with respect to
m_{\eta z}
at
m_{\eta z}=0
.

In the
k_{B}T/\Delta_{\eta}\ll 1
regime where
\Delta_{\eta}
is the Mott-Hubbard gap, the
charge carriers are
\eta
-spin triplet pairs
Carmelo_24
. They correspond to two unpaired physical

\eta
-spins with the same projection
+1/2
or
-1/2
and the two
\tau
-holes of which that describe their
translational degrees of freedom have
\tau
-band momentum values that differ by
2\pi/L
.
The static mass
M_{\eta}
and transport mass
M_{t}
of the
\eta
-spin triplet pairs, the expressions
of which are known
Carmelo_25
;
Carmelo_24
, are related as

.

The expression of the dc charge conductivity, Eq. (
41
),
simplifies in the

Carmelo_25

Carmelo_24

This though does not apply

\displaystyle\sigma_{\eta}(\omega)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 0\hskip 5.69046pt{\rm for}\hskip 5.69046pt\omega<\Delta_{\eta}\hskip 5.69046pt{\rm and}\hskip 5.69046ptT=0
