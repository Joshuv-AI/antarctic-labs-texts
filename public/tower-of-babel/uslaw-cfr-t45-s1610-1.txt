45 C.F.R. § 1610.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1610.1
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1610.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1610 (Use Of Non-Lsc Funds; Program Integrity). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part is designed to implement restrictions and requirements on the use of non-LSC funds by LSC recipients and to set requirements for each LSC recipient to maintain program integrity with respect to any organization that engages in LSC-restricted activities.
