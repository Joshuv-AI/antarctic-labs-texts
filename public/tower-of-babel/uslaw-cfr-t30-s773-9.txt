30 C.F.R. § 773.9 — Review of applicant and operator information.
Citation: us:cfr:30:773.9
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-773.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 773 (Requirements For Permits And Permit Processing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) We, the regulatory authority, will rely upon the information that you, the applicant, are required to submit under § 778.11 of this subchapter, information from AVS, and any other available information, to review your and your operator's organizational structure and ownership or control relationships.
(b) We must conduct the review required under paragraph (a) of this section before making a permit eligibility determination under § 773.12 of this part.
