Random uncertain motor parameters identification combining fourth-order moment and trust region
Authors: Wengui Mao, Congcong Liao, Jie Guo, Xuemei Wu, Jianhua Li
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
In random uncertain motor parameter identification field, there is low identification efficiency and ill-conditioned data coming from the second iteration involved in the uncertainty propagation and surrogate model between the motor parameters and the performance response. In this paper, the fourth-order moment method and trust region model management technology are combined to reduce the dependence of the surrogate model accuracy and improve the computational efficiency. In the framework, the inner layer calculates the cumulative probability under different motor performance response thresholds based on the fourth-order moment method, and obtain the probability density function of the calculated motor performance response. The outer layer transforms the random uncertain motor parameters identification into a deterministic optimization problem by minimizing the probability distribution between the calculated and the measured motor performance response. In the outer layer optimization, the trust region model management technology is used to divide the search interval of the entire parameter to be identified into a series of trust regions, construct a surrogate model on the trust region to calculate the motor performance response, and continuously update the trust region by comparing the response residuals between the calculated and measured motor performance response, so that the motor parameters to be identified continue to approach the interval where true values are located. At the same time, the genetic intelligent technology is introduced to further reduce the calculation cost. Finally, the probability distribution of random uncertain motor parameters is obtained according to the identified mean, standard deviation, skewness and kurtosis coefficients. The numerical results in the example verify that the random uncertain motor parameter identification can be effectively achieved by the proposed method.

Introduction

With the continuous development of advanced manufacturing technology, the permanent magnet synchronous motors are widely used in power transmission systems1,2. However, it is rather difficult or even impossible to directly measure permanent magnet synchronous motor parameters in some complex application environments due to technical or economical limitations. Under this context, the motor parameter identification technology, aiming at estimating motor parameters via measurable motor performance response, has obtained increasing attentions in recent years. Most of the existing motor parameter identification technology is mostly a traditional deterministic problem3,4, where the concerned motor parameters are always treated as unambiguous values. However, due to material internal defects in the raw material5 or generated during the manufacturing or service6, measurement inaccuracy and incomplete knowledge, it is widely acknowledged that the uncertainty in motor is ubiquitous. It is necessary to develop reliable uncertain parameter identification methods.

Monte Carlo simulation (MCS)7 is a general method to solve stochastic problems. The method is simple, and the results are subject to the large number theorem, that is, the simulation results approach the theoretical solution of the problem as the sample points increase. Therefore, the method requires a large number of sampling points to continuously reanalyze the original problem to obtain the sample points of random response, so as to obtain high-precision numerical statistical results, but it leads to low computational efficiency and high computational cost. Many scholars continue to improve sampling techniques, such as the genetic intelligent technology8,9. In order to improve the sampling efficiency, the calculation efficiency is improved under the premise of ensuring the calculation accuracy. Due to the huge amount of calculation of MCS, it is not suitable for practical engineering applications. So, the perturbation method10–13 based on small parameters, in which, the random variable is expanded by Taylor series at its mean value and the first-order term is retained, ignoring the second-order small quantity, continues to develop and gradually matures. Liu et al.12 used the first-order second-moment uncertainty analysis to calculate the probability distribution response, and identified the probability distribution of unknown parameters through minimizing the calculated and measured response based on the generation mapping genetic algorithmic. Ji et al.13 identify the mean and variance of the input variables combining the dimension reduction integral method and the SOP optimization method to improve the calculation efficiency. But the perturbation method ignores the statistical moments above the second order and does not take into account the actual distribution form. A commonly encountered difficulty in calculating the motor performance response is that it is often unable to provide a clear mapping relationship between motor parameters and performance response. The random uncertain motor parameters identification based on the above optimization algorithm will result in low identification accuracy and poor computational efficiency due to the accuracy of the surrogate model14 between motor parameters and performance response coming from a large number of time-consuming finite element simulation. Thus, a more efficient computing method needs to be developed together.

In this study, a new identification method combined fourth-order moment method15,16 together with trust region management technology17,18 and polynomial surrogate-based optimization19,20 is suggested to address the random uncertain motor parameter identification problem. In the inner layer of this method, the fourth-order moment method is used to calculate different cumulative distribution function (CDF) to obtain the corresponding probability distribution function (PDF). The outer layer introduces trust region management technology to deal with the problem that the accuracy of the global surrogate model is difficult to guarantee due to the large search interval, and the number of time-consuming finite element simulation calculations needs to be carried out. At the same time, genetic intelligent technology21 is introduced to reduce the computational cost. The layout of this paper is as follows. Firstly, the improved method on the random uncertain motor parameter identification problem is described in detail in "Random uncertain motor parameter identification problem description". The identification process is simply shown in "Random uncertain motor parameter identification process". The example in "Numerical examples" is used to verify the proposed method. Finally, "Conclusion" summarizes the conclusions throughout this research.

Numerical examples

Performance simulation and validation

The motor performance analysis involves equivalent magnetic circuit analysis, finite element numerical calculation and multi-physical field cross-coupling simulation. It is difficult to establish a high-precision mapping relationship between motor parameters and performance response through numerical analysis algorithms. The finite element method (FEM) based on ANSYS Maxwell26 is often used to calculate the motor performance response. Its calculation accuracy and cost are directly related to the grid. In engineering practice, in order to reduce the calculation cost, it is considered that the FEM is feasible when the relative error between the calculation and real result is less than 5%.In this section, a motor model coming from the literature27 are provided to verify the feasibility of FEM based on ANSYS Maxwell introduced for numerical calculation. The torque, induced electromotive force and cogging torque are calculated, as shown in Fig. 5, the peak values of torque, induced electromotive force and cogging torque are 279.50 N· m, 1.86 N. m and 420.44 V, respectively. Compared with the literature27, as shown in the Table 1, the maximum relative error is only 0.72%, which proves that the finite element method is feasible and lays a foundation for the subsequent construction of polynomial surrogate model.

Conclusion

In this paper, the uncertain motor parameters identification method combined the fourth-order moment with trust region management technology is studied to dealt with the accuracy of the surrogate model and improve the accuracy and efficiency of identification. In the inner layer, the fourth-order motor statistical characteristic parameters are used to calculate the probability density of the motor performance response. In the outer layer, the optimal solution of uncertain motor parameters is searched by coupling agent model optimization and trust region management technology. The advantages of the improved method are summarized below:(1) The fourth-order moment is used to calculate the probability density of the motor performance response, and the cumulative probability under the threshold can be solved sequentially to obtain a complete and discrete motor performance response PDF without secondary iteration, which is suitable for models with high nonlinearity and implicit performance functions.(2) The maximum entropy probability density distribution is constructed based on the fourth-order moment, and the uncertain parameters identification problem is transformed into the deterministic identification problem to the statistical characteristics of the mean, standard deviation, skewness coefficient and kurtosis coefficient.(3) The trust region management technology is induced to improve the identification efficiency and accuracy through constructing a surrogate model in the trust region and drawing the trust region closer to the region where the real value is located.
