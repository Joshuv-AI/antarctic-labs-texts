10 C.F.R. § 780.33 — Hearing and decision.
Citation: us:cfr:10:780.33
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-780.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 780 (Patent Compensation Board Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If any party requests a hearing, the Board will proceed with a hearing and decision. If a hearing is not requested, the Board shall on the basis of the record prepare and issue its decision.
