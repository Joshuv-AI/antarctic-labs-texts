46 C.F.R. § 151.03-35 — -35 Limiting draft.
Citation: us:cfr:46:151.03-35
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-151.03-35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 151 (Barges Carrying Bulk Liquid Hazardous Material Cargoes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Maximum allowable draft to which a barge may be loaded. Limiting draft is a function of hull type and cargo specific gravity. A barge may be assigned different limiting drafts for different hull types or within one hull type for different specific gravities.
