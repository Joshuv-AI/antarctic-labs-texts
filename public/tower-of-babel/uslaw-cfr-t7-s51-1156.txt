7 C.F.R. § 51.1156 — Firm.
Citation: us:cfr:7:51.1156
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-51.1156
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 51 (Fresh Fruits, Vegetables, And Other Products (Inspection, Certification, And Standards)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Firm as applied to common oranges and tangelos means that the fruit is not soft, or noticeably wilted or flabby; as applied to oranges of the Mandarin group (Satsumas, King, Mandarin), “firm” means that the fruit is not extremely puffy, although the skin may be slightly loose.
