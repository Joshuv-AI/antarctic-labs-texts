5 C.F.R. § 315.402 — Tenure on reinstatement.
Citation: us:cfr:5:315.402
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-315.402
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 315 (Career And Career-Conditional Employment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except as provided in paragraph (b) of this section, a person who is reinstated becomes a career-conditional employee.
(b) A person who is reinstated becomes a career employee when he has completed the service requirement for career tenure or is excepted from it by § 315.201(c).
