5 C.F.R. § 894.104 — Who makes enrollment decisions and reconsiderations?
Citation: us:cfr:5:894.104
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-894.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 894 (Federal Employees Dental And Vision Insurance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator makes enrollment decisions and the Administrator reviews requests for reconsideration of an enrollment decision. The Administrator's initial enrollment decision denying enrollment or an opportunity to change coverage must be in writing and must inform you about your right to reconsideration.
