31 C.F.R. § 359.6 — When may I redeem my Series I bond?
Citation: us:cfr:31:359.6
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-359.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 359 (Offering Of United States Savings Bonds, Series I). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Bonds issued on December 1, 2002, or earlier. You may redeem your Series I savings bond issued on January 1, 2003, or earlier, at any time after six months from its issue date.
(b) Bonds issued on February 1, 2003, or thereafter. You may redeem your Series I savings bond issued on February 1, 2003, or thereafter, at any time after 12 months from its issue date.
