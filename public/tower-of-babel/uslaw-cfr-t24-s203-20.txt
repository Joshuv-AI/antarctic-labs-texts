24 C.F.R. § 203.20 — Agreed interest rate.
Citation: us:cfr:24:203.20
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-203.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 203 (Single Family Mortgage Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The mortgage shall bear interest at the rate agreed upon by the mortgagee and the mortgagor.
(b) Interest shall be payable in monthly installments on the principal amount of the mortgage outstanding on the due date of each installment.
