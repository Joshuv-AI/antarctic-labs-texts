31 C.F.R. § 560.408 — Importation into and release from a bonded warehouse or foreign trade zone.
Citation: us:cfr:31:560.408
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-560.408
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 560 (Iranian Transactions And Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The prohibitions in § 560.201 apply to importation into a bonded warehouse or a foreign trade zone of the United States.
