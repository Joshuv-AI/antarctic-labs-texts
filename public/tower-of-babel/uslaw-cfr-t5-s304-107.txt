5 C.F.R. § 304.107 — Reports.
Citation: us:cfr:5:304.107
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-304.107
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 304 (Expert And Consultant Appointments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As required by 5 U.S.C. 3109(e), each agency shall report to the Office of Personnel Management on an annual basis:
(a) The number of days the agency employed each paid expert or consultant; and
(b) The total amount the agency paid each expert or consultant so employed. (Do not include payments for travel and related expenses.)
