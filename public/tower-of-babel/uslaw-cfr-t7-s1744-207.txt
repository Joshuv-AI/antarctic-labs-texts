7 C.F.R. § 1744.207 — Investment not to jeopardize loan security.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1744.207
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1744.207
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1744 (Post-Loan Policies And Procedures Common To Guaranteed And Insured Telephone Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A borrower shall not make a qualified investment or a rural development investment which jeopardizes:
(a) The security of loans made or guaranteed by RUS; or
(b) The borrower's ability to repay such loans under the terms and conditions as agreed.
