22 U.S.C. § 290k–1 — Governor and Alternate Governor
Citation: us:usc:22:290k–1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section290k–1
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter XXVI (Multilateral Investment Guarantee Agency). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Governor and Alternate Governor of the Bank, appointed under section 286a of this title, shall serve as Governor and Alternate Governor, respectively, of the Agency.
