7 U.S.C. § 906a — Use of funds outside the United States or its territories prohibited
Citation: us:usc:7:906a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section906a
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 31 (Rural Electrification And Telephone Service); Subchapter I (Rural Electrification). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No funds provided under the Rural Electrification Act of 1936, as amended [7 U.S.C. 901 et seq.], shall be used outside the United States or any of its territories.
