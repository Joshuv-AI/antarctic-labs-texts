47 C.F.R. § 54.1518 — Appropriate uses of transitional mobile support.
Citation: us:cfr:47:54.1518
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-54.1518
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 54 (Universal Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Recipients of Uniendo a Puerto Rico and Connect USVI transitional mobile support shall use the support to improve the redundancy and resiliency of facilities for 4G LTE or better technologies to help ensure continuity of service by preventing or withstanding damage from disasters, including the maintenance of backup power systems for such networks.
