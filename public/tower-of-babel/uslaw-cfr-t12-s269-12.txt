12 C.F.R. § 269.12 — Amendment.
Citation: us:cfr:12:269.12
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-269.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 269 (Policy On Labor Relations For The Federal Reserve Banks). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This policy may be amended upon appropriate legal notice to all Federal Reserve Banks and labor organizations recognized, or seeking recognition, at any such Bank under this Policy. In no instance shall an amendment be applied retroactively.
