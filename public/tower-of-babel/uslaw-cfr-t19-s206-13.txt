19 C.F.R. § 206.13 — Who may file a petition.
Citation: us:cfr:19:206.13
Creator: United States International Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-206.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 206 (Investigations Relating To Global And Bilateral Safeguard Actions, Market Disruption, Trade Diversion, And Review Of Relief Actions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A petition under this subpart B may be filed by an entity, including a trade association, firm, certified or recognized union, or group of workers, that is representative of a domestic industry producing an article like or directly competitive with a foreign article that is allegedly being imported into the United States in such increased quantities as to be a substantial cause of serious injury, or the threat thereof, to such domestic industry.
