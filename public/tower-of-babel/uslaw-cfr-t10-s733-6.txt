10 C.F.R. § 733.6 — Consultation with the DOE Office of the Inspector General.
Citation: us:cfr:10:733.6
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-733.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 733 (Allegations Of Research Misconduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon receipt of an allegation of research misconduct, the DOE Element shall consult with the DOE Office of the Inspector General which will determine whether that office will elect to investigate the allegation.
