46 C.F.R. § 201.42 — Subscription, authentication of documents.
Citation: us:cfr:46:201.42
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-201.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 201 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Documents filed shall be subscribed: (1) By the person or persons filing same, (2) by an officer thereof if it be a corporation, (3) by an officer or employee if it be a government instrumentality, or (4) by an attorney or other person having authority with respect thereto.
(b) Documents submitted pursuant to stipulation of counsel where no sponsoring witness will be used must be verified.
