25 C.F.R. § 307.12 — Certificates, dating, and signing thereof.
Citation: us:cfr:25:307.12
Creator: Indian Arts And Crafts Board, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-307.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 307 (Navajo All-Wool Woven Fabrics; Use Of Government Certificate Of Genuineness). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the certificate is first affixed the lower of the two spaces provided for the purpose shall be signed by the licensee. In the event the ultimate retailer of any fabric so marked is not the person who originally attached the certificate, that ultimate retailer may sign the upper of the two spaces provided for the purpose and detach the original signature.
