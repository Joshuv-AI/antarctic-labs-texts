47 C.F.R. § 30.3 — Eligibility.
Citation: us:cfr:47:30.3
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-30.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 30 (Upper Microwave Flexible Use Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any entity who meets the technical, financial, character, and citizenship qualifications that the Commission may require in accordance with such Act, other than those precluded by section 310 of the Communications Act of 1934, as amended, 47 U.S.C. 310, is eligible to hold a license under this part.
