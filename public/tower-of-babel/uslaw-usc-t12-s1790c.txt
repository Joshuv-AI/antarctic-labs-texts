12 U.S.C. § 1790c — Reward for information leading to recoveries or civil penalties
Citation: us:usc:12:1790c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section1790c
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 14 (Federal Credit Unions); Subchapter II (Share Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Board may pay rewards in connection with an offense affecting an insured credit union, under the same circumstances and subject to the same limitations that a Federal banking agency may pay rewards under section 1831j of this title in connection with an offense affecting a depository institution insured by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation.
