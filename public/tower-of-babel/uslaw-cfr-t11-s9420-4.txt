11 C.F.R. § 9420.4 — Program accessibility: Discrimination prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:11:9420.4
Creator: Election Assistance Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-9420.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 9420 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap In Programs Or Activities Conducted By The U.S. Election Assistance Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise provided in 11 CFR 9420.6 and 11 CFR 9420.7, no qualified handicapped person shall be denied the benefits of, be excluded from participation in, or otherwise be subjected to discrimination under any program or activity conducted by the Commission because its facilities are inaccessible to or unusable by handicapped persons.
