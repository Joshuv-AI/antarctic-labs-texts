48 C.F.R. § 970.2670-1 — 970.2670-1 Requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:48:970.2670-1
Creator: Chapter 9—Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-970.2670-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 970 (Doe Management And Operating Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The goal requirements of section 3021 of the Energy Policy Act of 1992, and the attendant reporting requirements shall be included in the subcontracting plan for the management and operating contract and shall apply to the annual dollar obligations specifically provided to the contractor for competitively awarded subcontracts that fulfill Energy Policy Act requirements.
