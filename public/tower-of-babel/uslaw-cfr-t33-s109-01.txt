33 C.F.R. § 109.01 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:33:109.01
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-109.01
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 109 (General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of the rules and regulations in this subchapter is to implement certain laws and set forth the requirements for anchorage areas.
