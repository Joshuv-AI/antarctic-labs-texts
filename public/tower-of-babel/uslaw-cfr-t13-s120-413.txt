13 C.F.R. § 120.413 — Advertisement of relationship with SBA.
Citation: us:cfr:13:120.413
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-120.413
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 120 (Business Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Lender may refer in its advertising to its participation with SBA. The advertising may not:
(a) State or imply that the Lender, or any of its Borrowers, has or will receive preferential treatment from SBA;
(b) Be false or misleading; or
(c) Make use of SBA's seal.
