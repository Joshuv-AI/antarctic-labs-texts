24 C.F.R. § 207.252e — e Method of payment of mortgage insurance premiums.
Citation: us:cfr:24:207.252e
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-207.252e
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 207 (Multifamily Housing Mortgage Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the cases that the Commissioner deems appropriate, the Commissioner may require, by means of instructions communicated to all affected mortgagees, that mortgage insurance premiums be remitted electronically.
