45 C.F.R. § 32.10 — Refunds.
Citation: us:cfr:45:32.10
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-32.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 32 (Administrative Wage Garnishment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If the hearing official, pursuant to a hearing under this part, determines that a debt is not legally due and owing to the United States, the Secretary shall promptly refund any amount collected by means of administrative wage garnishment.
(b) Unless required by Federal law or contract, refunds under this part shall not bear interest.
