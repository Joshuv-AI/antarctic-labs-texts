14 C.F.R. § 33.23 — Engine mounting attachments and structure.
Citation: us:cfr:14:33.23
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-33.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 33 (Airworthiness Standards: Aircraft Engines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The maximum allowable limit and ultimate loads for engine mounting attachments and related engine structure must be specified.
(b) The engine mounting attachments and related engine structure must be able to withstand—
(1) The specified limit loads without permanent deformation; and
(2) The specified ultimate loads without failure, but may exhibit permanent deformation.
