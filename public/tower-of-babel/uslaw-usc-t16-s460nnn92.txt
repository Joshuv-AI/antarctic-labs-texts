16 U.S.C. § 460nnn–92 — Release from wilderness study area status
Citation: us:usc:16:460nnn–92
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section460nnn–92
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter CXXV (Steens Mountain Cooperative Management And Protection Area); Part E (Establishment Of Wildlands Juniper Management Area). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Federal lands included in the Wildlands Juniper Management Area established under section 460nnn–91 of this title are no longer subject to the requirement of section 1782(c) of title 43 pertaining to managing the lands so as not to impair the suitability of the lands for preservation as wilderness.
