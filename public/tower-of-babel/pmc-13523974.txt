Human Vaccines & Immunotherapeutics values the dedicated work of Reviewers and Editors who contribute to the high quality of published work, a fair review process and rapid turnaround times.

We thank all of our Reviewers for their time, commitment, and hard work.

In the current HV&I Reviewer Highlight, we are pleased to recognize Verónica Córdoba Sánchez and her service to the scientific community.

Dr Córdoba Sánchez is a Colombian researcher specialized in health psychology and behavioral interventions. She currently serves as a faculty member at Universidad EAFIT and Universidad Cooperativa de Colombia. She completed her Ph.D. in Psychology at Universidad de San Buenaventura, a Master’s degree in Health Psychology Research at Universidad Autónoma de Barcelona, and a Bachelor’s degree in Psychology at Universidad de Antioquia.

In her doctoral dissertation, Dr Córdoba Sánchez developed and tested a protocol to reduce HPV vaccine hesitancy in Colombian girls. She has published numerous research papers in national and international journals (including JCO Global Oncology), focusing primarily on cervical cancer prevention. Additionally, she has participated in international conferences, reviewed research articles for various international journals, and mentored several young researchers in behavioral medicine within the Master’s in Behavioral Studies program at Universidad EAFIT.
