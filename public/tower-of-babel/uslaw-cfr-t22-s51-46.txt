22 C.F.R. § 51.46 — Return or retention of evidence of U.S. citizenship or non-citizen nationality.
Citation: us:cfr:22:51.46
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-51.46
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 51 (Passports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department will generally return to the applicant evidence submitted in connection with an application for a passport. The Department may, however, retain evidence when it deems it necessary for anti-fraud or law enforcement or other similar purposes.
