46 C.F.R. § 386.15 — Distribution of handbills.
Citation: us:cfr:46:386.15
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-386.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 386 (Regulations Governing Public Buildings And Grounds At The United States Merchant Marine Academy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The distribution of materials such as pamphlets, handbills and flyers, and the displaying of placards or posting of materials on bulletin boards or elsewhere in or on Academy property shall be coordinated with the Head, Department of Public Safety and Security, of the Academy so as not to impede Academy employees in the performance of their duties or Midshipmen activities.
