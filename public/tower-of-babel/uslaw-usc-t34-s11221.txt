34 U.S.C. § 11221 — Authority for program
Citation: us:usc:34:11221
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section11221
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 111 (Juvenile Justice And Delinquency Prevention); Subchapter III (Runaway And Homeless Youth); Part B (Transitional Living Grant Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to make grants and to provide technical assistance to public and nonprofit private entities to establish and operate transitional living youth projects for homeless youth.
