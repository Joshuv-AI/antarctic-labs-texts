16 U.S.C. § 459h–5 — Beach erosion control and hurricane protection; study and formulation of plans; activities by Chief of Engineers, Department of Army
Citation: us:usc:16:459h–5
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section459h–5
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LXIII (National Seashore Recreational Areas). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior and the Secretary of the Army may cooperate in the study and formulation of plans for beach erosion control and hurricane protection of the seashore. Any such protective works or spoil deposit activities undertaken by the Chief of Engineers, Department of the Army, shall be carried out within the seashore in accordance with a plan that is acceptable to the Secretary of the Interior and that is consistent with the purposes of sections 459h to 459h–10 of this title.
