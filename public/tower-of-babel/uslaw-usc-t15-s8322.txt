15 U.S.C. § 8322 — Authority of FERC
Citation: us:usc:15:8322
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section8322
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 109 (Wall Street Transparency And Accountability); Subchapter I (Regulation Of Over-The-Counter Swaps Markets); Part B (Regulation Of Swap Markets). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in the Wall Street Transparency and Accountability Act of 2010 or the amendments to the Commodity Exchange Act [7 U.S.C. 1 et seq.] made by such Act shall limit or affect any statutory enforcement authority of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission pursuant to section 824v of title 16 and section 717c–1 of this title that existed prior to July 21, 2010.
