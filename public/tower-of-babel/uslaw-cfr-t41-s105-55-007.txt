41 C.F.R. § 105-55.007 — -55.007 Required administrative proceedings.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-55.007
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-55.007
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-55 (55—Collection Of Claims Owed The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The General Services Administration is not required to omit, foreclose, or duplicate administrative proceedings required by contract or other laws or regulations.
