5 C.F.R. § 892.204 — How do I waive participation in premium conversion before the benefit first becomes effective?
Citation: us:cfr:5:892.204
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-892.204
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 892 (Federal Flexible Benefits Plan: Pre-Tax Payment Of Health Benefits Premiums). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must file a waiver form by the date set by your employing office, but not later than the day before the effective date of coverage. The waiver form is available from your employing office.
