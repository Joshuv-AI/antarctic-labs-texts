48 C.F.R. § 51.107 — 51.107 Contract clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:51.107
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-51.107
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 51 (Use Of Government Sources By Contractors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.251-1, Government Supply Sources, in solicitations and contracts when the contracting officer authorizes the contractor to acquire supplies or services from a Government supply source.
