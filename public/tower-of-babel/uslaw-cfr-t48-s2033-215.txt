48 C.F.R. § 2033.215 — 2033.215 Contract clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2033.215
Creator: Chapter 20—Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2033.215
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2033 (Protests, Disputes, And Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall use the clause at FAR 52.233-1, Disputes, with its Alternate I, where continued performance is vital to national security, the public health and safety, critical and major agency programs, or other essential supplies or services whose timely reprocurement from other sources would be impractical.
