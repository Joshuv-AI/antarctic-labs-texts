Correction: Development of a diagnostic model for ovarian cancer based on machine learning algorithms and functional analysis of key biomarker SOX17
Authors: Xueyan Geng, Maopeng Yin, Hongxi Zhao, Zeyu Zhang, Shichao Liu, Yingjie Liu, Shoucai Zhang, Yongyuan Liang, Li Song, Guixi Zheng
Published: 2026

Correction: J Ovarian Res 18, 237 (2025)

https://doi.org/10.1186/s13048-025-01809-w

Following publication of the original article [1], In the Methods section of the abstract, the first line currently reads “°C,” which appears to be a typographical error. It should actually be “OC”. The updated statement is given below and the changes have been highlighted in bold typeface.

Incorrect:

We acquired RNA sequencing data of 426 tissues (352 °C tumor and 74 adjacent non-tumor) from the Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO) repository. Following rigorous batch effect correction and normalization procedures, DEGs were screened between tumor and non-tumor specimens.

Correct:

We acquired RNA sequencing data of 426 tissues (352 OC tumor and 74 adjacent non-tumor) from the Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO) repository. Following rigorous batch effect correction and normalization procedures, DEGs were screened between tumor and non-tumor specimens.

The original article [1] has been corrected.
