On a Class of Berndt-type Integrals and Related Barnes Multiple Zeta Functions
Xiang Chen a, , Ce Xu a, and Jianing Zhou b a School of Mathematics and Statistics, Anhui Normal University, Wuhu 241002, P.R.C. b School of Mathematics and Computational Science, Xiangtan University, Xiangtan 411105, P.R.C.

Abstract
This paper investigates a class of special Berndt-type integral calculations where the integrand contains only hyperbolic cosine functions. The research approach proceeds as follows: Firstly, through contour integration methods, we transform the integral into a Ramanujan-type hyperbolic infinite series. Subsequently, we introduce a θ \theta -parameterized auxiliary function and apply the residue theorem from complex analysis to successfully simplify mixed-type denominators combining hyperbolic cosine and sine terms into a normalized Ramanujan-type hyperbolic infinite series with denominators containing only single hyperbolic function terms. For these simplified hyperbolic infinite series, we combine properties of Jacobi elliptic functions with composite analytical techniques involving Fourier series expansion and Maclaurin series expansion. This ultimately yields an explicit expression as a rational polynomial combination of Γ ⁡ ( 1 / 4 ) \Gamma(1/4) and π − 1 / 2 \pi^{-1/2} . Notably, this work establishes a connection between the integral and Barnes multiple zeta functions, providing a novel research pathway for solving related problems.



Keywords:
Berndt-type integral, Hyperbolic functions, Contour integration, Jacobi elliptic functions, Barnes multiple zeta functions.

Recently, Xu and Zhao

\displaystyle\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\infty}\displaystyle\frac{\sin(nx)}{x(\cos x+\cosh x)}\mathrm{d}x=\displaystyle\frac{\pi}{4}\quad(n\in\mathbb{N}).

and

[

[

utilized the method of contour integration to study integrals analogous to (

In this paper, we focus on Berndt-type integrals of the form:

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\infty}\displaystyle\frac{x^{s}\mathrm{d}x}{\left[\cosh(2x)-\cos(2x)\right]\left[\cosh x-\cos x\right]}\quad(\Re(s)\geq 4).

(1.8)

Here, we focus on Berndt-type integrals with integrands consisting purely of (hyperbolic) cosine functions, where each integrand is expressed as a product of two specific terms: (i) the sum of a cosine and a hyperbolic cosine function, and (ii) their difference. Through the application of contour integration and series expansions of Jacobi elliptic functions, we establish structural theorems and obtain explicit evaluations for these Berndt-type integrals. Specifically, we shall prove the following results
(also see Theorem
5.1

\Gamma=\Gamma(1/4)
, for

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\infty}\displaystyle\frac{x^{4m-3}\mathrm{d}x}{\left[\cosh(2x)-\cos(2x)\right]\left[\cosh x-\cos x\right]}\in\mathbb{Q}\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma^{8m-8}}{\pi^{2m-2}}+\displaystyle\frac{\mathbb{Q}}{\sqrt{2}}\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma^{8m-6}}{\pi^{(4m-3)/2}}\\
+\mathbb{Q}\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma^{8m-4}}{\pi^{2m-1}}+\displaystyle\frac{\mathbb{Q}}{\sqrt{2}}\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma^{8m-2}}{\pi^{(4m+1)/2}}+\mathbb{Q}\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma^{8m}}{\pi^{2m+2}}.

(1.9)

Additionally, we present the specific coefficients preceding each term and establish explicit relationships between the Berndt-type integrals and Barnes multiple zeta functions. In addition, we further clarify their connection with Barnes multiple zeta functions, thereby providing new closed-form evaluations and insightful perspectives (see Theorem
6.2

3
Berndt-Type Integrals via Hyperbolic Series

In the present section, we delve into establishing rigorous and precise correspondences between Berndt-type integrals and Ramanujan-type hyperbolic series, leveraging the powerful tool of contour integration techniques. Our approach reveals deep connections between these apparently distinct mathematical objects.

Theorem 3.1
.

For any positive integer

\displaystyle\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\infty}\displaystyle\frac{x^{p}\,\mathrm{d}x}{[\cosh(2x)-\cos(2x)][\cosh x-\cos x]}-i^{p+1}\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\infty}\displaystyle\frac{x^{p}\,\mathrm{d}x}{[\cosh(2x)-\cos(2x)][\cosh x-\cos x]}

\displaystyle=\displaystyle\frac{1}{2^{p+3}}i(1+i)^{p+1}\pi^{p+1}\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\displaystyle\frac{(-1)^{n-1}(2n-1)^{p}}{\sinh^{2}((2n-1)\pi/2)\cosh((2n-1)\pi/2)}

\displaystyle\quad-\displaystyle\frac{1}{4}((1+i))^{p-1}\pi^{p+1}\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\displaystyle\frac{(-1)^{n}n^{p}}{\sinh(n\pi)\cosh^{2}(n\pi)}-\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}(1+i)^{p-1}\pi^{p+1}\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\displaystyle\frac{(-1)^{n}n^{p}}{\sinh^{3}(n\pi)}

\displaystyle\quad+\displaystyle\frac{1}{4}p((1+i))^{p-1}\pi^{p}\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\displaystyle\frac{(-1)^{n}n^{p-1}}{\sinh^{2}(n\pi)\cosh(n\pi)}.

(3.29)

Proof.

,

\displaystyle\quad+(-1)^{m}\displaystyle\frac{1}{2^{6m+3}}\left(-{S}_{4m-4}^{\prime}+{S}_{4m-4}+{A}_{4m-4}+{A}_{4m-2}^{\prime}\right)\displaystyle\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma^{8m-2}}{\pi^{2m+1/2}}

\displaystyle\quad+(-1)^{m-1}\displaystyle\frac{1}{2^{6m+7}}\left[(4m-12){R}_{4m-6}+\text{R}_{4m-6}^{\prime\prime}\right](4m-6)!\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma^{8m}}{\pi^{2m+2}}.

(5.91)

In particular, from this, the coefficients
q_{i,m}
can be explicitly identified as

\displaystyle q_{1,m}

\displaystyle=(-1)^{m-1}\displaystyle\frac{1}{2^{6m}}(-8m^{2}+14m-6)(4m-6)!{R}_{4m-6},

(5.92)

\displaystyle q_{2,m}

\displaystyle=(-1)^{m}\displaystyle\frac{1}{2^{6m}}(4m-3)\left({A}_{4m-4}+{S}_{4m-4}\right),

(5.93)

\displaystyle q_{3,m}

\displaystyle=(-1)^{m-1}\displaystyle\frac{1}{2^{6m+2}}{P}_{4m-3},

(5.94)

\displaystyle q_{4,m}

\displaystyle=(-1)^{m}\displaystyle\frac{1}{2^{6m+3}}\left(-{S}_{4m-4}^{\prime}+{S}_{4m-4}+{A}_{4m-4}+{A}_{4m-2}^{\prime}\right),

(5.95)

\displaystyle q_{5,m}

\displaystyle=(-1)^{m-1}\displaystyle\frac{1}{2^{6m+7}}\left[(4m-12){R}_{4m-6}+{R}_{4m-6}^{\prime\prime}\right](4m-6)!.

(5.96)

Thus, the proof of this theorem is complete.
∎

Berndt-type Integrals via Barnes Multiple Zeta Functions

In the present section, we assess the Barnes multiple zeta function by making use of outcomes from Berndt-type integrals.
Bradshaw and Vignat
[
4
]
offered integral representations for these functions, thereby retrieving a previously established result
[
12
, Eq. (3.2)]

Proposition 6.1
.

Let
\Re(s)>N
,
\Re(\omega)>0
, and
\Re(a_{j})>0
for
j=1,\ldots,N
. Then

\zeta_{N}(s,\omega|a_{1},\ldots,a_{N})=\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(s)}\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\infty}u^{s-1}e^{-\omega u}\prod_{j=1}^{N}\displaystyle\frac{1}{(1-e^{-a_{j}u})}\mathrm{d}u.

The alternating form satisfies the following property:

\tilde{\zeta}_{N}(s,\omega|a_{1},\ldots,a_{N})=\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(s)}\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\infty}u^{s-1}e^{-\omega u}\prod_{j=1}^{N}\displaystyle\frac{1}{(1+e^{-a_{j}u})}\mathrm{d}u.

We can now employ the results on Berndt-type integrals established in the previous section to derive the following structural theorem for Barnes multiple zeta functions.

Theorem 6.2
.

, we have

\Gamma(4m-2)\zeta_{4}(4m-2,3|\mathbf{c}_{4},\boldsymbol{\sigma}_{4})\in\mathbb{Q}\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma^{8m-8}}{\pi^{2m-2}}+\displaystyle\frac{\mathbb{Q}}{\sqrt{2}}\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma^{8m-6}}{\pi^{2m-3/2}}+\mathbb{Q}\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma^{8m-4}}{\pi^{2m-1}}+\displaystyle\frac{\mathbb{Q}}{\sqrt{2}}\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma^{8m-2}}{\pi^{2m+1/2}}+\mathbb{Q}\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma^{8m}}{\pi^{2m+2}},
