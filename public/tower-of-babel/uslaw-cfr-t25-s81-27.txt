25 C.F.R. § 81.27 — Must I re-register if I have already registered for a tribal or Secretarial election?
Citation: us:cfr:25:81.27
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-81.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 81 (Secretarial Election Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. A Secretarial election is a Federal election and you must register for each Secretarial election.
