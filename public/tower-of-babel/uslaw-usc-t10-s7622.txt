10 U.S.C. § 7622 — Rations: commissioned officers in field
Citation: us:usc:10:7622
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7622
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Procurement); Chapter 769 (Sale Of Serviceable Material). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Commissioned officers of the Army serving in the field may buy rations for their own use, on credit, from any officer designated by the Secretary of the Army. Amounts due for these purchases shall be reported monthly to the officer of the Army designated by the Secretary.
