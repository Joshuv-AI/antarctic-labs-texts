28 C.F.R. § 551.162 — Smoking generally prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:28:551.162
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-551.162
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 551 (Miscellaneous). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Smoking is generally prohibited in and on the grounds of Bureau institutions and offices, with the following two exceptions:
(a) Smoking is permitted as part of an authorized inmate religious activity; and
(b) For Bureau staff and official visitors, smoking is permitted only in smoking areas designated by the Warden.
