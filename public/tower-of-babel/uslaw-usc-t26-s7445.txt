26 U.S.C. § 7445 — Offices
Citation: us:usc:26:7445
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section7445
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 76 (Judicial Proceedings); Subchapter C (The Tax Court); Part I (Organization And Jurisdiction). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The principal office of the Tax Court shall be in the District of Columbia, but the Tax Court or any of its divisions may sit at any place within the United States.
