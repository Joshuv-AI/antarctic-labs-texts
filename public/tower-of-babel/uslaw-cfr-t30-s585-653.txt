30 C.F.R. § 585.653 — What other reports or notices must I submit to BOEM under my approved GAP?
Citation: us:cfr:30:585.653
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-585.653
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 585 (Renewable Energy On The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must prepare and submit to BOEM annually a report that summarizes the findings from any activities you conduct under your approved GAP and the results of those activities. BOEM will protect the information from public disclosure as provided in § 585.114.
