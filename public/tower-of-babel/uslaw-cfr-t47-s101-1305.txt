47 C.F.R. § 101.1305 — Private internal service.
Citation: us:cfr:47:101.1305
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-101.1305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 101 (Fixed Microwave Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A private internal service is a service where entities utilize frequencies purely for internal business purposes or public safety communications and not on a for-hire or for-profit basis.
