Logics with the axiom of convergence: complexity with a small number of variables in the language (extended version) The paper was prepared by participants of the Internship Program for Employees and Postgraduate Students of Russian Educational and Scientific Institutions at the HSE University, Faculty of Mathematics, based on data obtained during the internship period. It is an extended version of the abstracts submitted to the conference Smirnov Readings [ 15 ] .
M. Rybakov, M. Shcherbakov

Abstract
It is known that many modal and superintuitionistic logics are PSPACE \mathrm{PSPACE} -hard in languages with a small number of variables; however, questions about the complexity of similar fragments of many logics obtained by adding various axioms to “standard” ones remain unexplored. We investigate the complexity of fragments of modal logics obtained by adding an axiom requiring the convergence of the accessibility relation in Kripke frames: 𝐒𝟒 ​ .2 \mathbf{S4.2} , 𝐊𝟒 ​ .2 \mathbf{K4.2} , 𝐆𝐫𝐳 ​ .2 \mathbf{Grz.2} , and 𝐆𝐋 ​ .2 \mathbf{GL.2} . The main result is that 𝐒𝟒 ​ .2 \mathbf{S4.2} and 𝐆𝐫𝐳 ​ .2 \mathbf{Grz.2} are PSPACE \mathrm{PSPACE} -complete in a language with two variables, while 𝐊𝟒 ​ .2 \mathbf{K4.2} and 𝐆𝐋 ​ .2 \mathbf{GL.2} are PSPACE \mathrm{PSPACE} -complete in a language with one variable. The obtained results are extended to infinite classes of logics.

Here, some clarifications are needed.
In first-order language, i.e., over the logic
\mathbf{QCl}
, there are similar conditions:

\begin{array}[]{lcl}\bm{conv}&=&\forall x,y,z\,(xRy\wedge xRz\phantom{{}\wedge y\neq z}\to\exists u\,(yRu\wedge zRu));\\
\bm{dir}&=&\forall x,y,z\,(xRy\wedge xRz\wedge y\neq z\to\exists u\,(yRu\wedge zRu)).\end{array}

These are called the conditions of convergence and directedness, respectively.
We will actually be interested in the directedness condition, which coincides with the convergence condition in the case of reflexivity.

Over intuitionistic logic
\mathbf{Int}
, the condition of convergence (or directedness) is described by the weak law of excluded middle:

\begin{array}[]{lcl}\bm{wem}&=&\neg p\vee\neg\neg p.\end{array}

Over modal logics
\mathbf{S4}
and
\mathbf{Grz}
, the condition of convergence (or directedness) is described by the Geach axiom

\begin{array}[]{lcl}\bm{ga}&=&\Diamond\Box p\to\Box\Diamond p.\end{array}

Over modal logics
\mathbf{K4}
and
\mathbf{GL}
, the directedness condition differs from the convergence condition and is described by the formula

\begin{array}[]{lcl}\bm{dir}&=&\Diamond(p\wedge\Box q)\to\Box(p\vee\Diamond q).\end{array}

During the research, the following general results were obtained.

Theorem 1

Every modal logic between
\mathbf{K}
and
\mathbf{Grz.2}
is
\mathrm{PSPACE}
-hard in a language with two propositional variables.

Theorem 2

Evry modal logic between
\mathbf{K}
and
\mathbf{GL.2}
is
\mathrm{PSPACE}
-hard in a language with one propositional variable.

The first theorem generalizes the observation made about the two-variable fragments of

\mathbf{Grz.2}
