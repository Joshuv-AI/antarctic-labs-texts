43 C.F.R. § 4.740 — What will a preliminary decision include?
Citation: us:cfr:43:4.740
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4.740
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4 (Department Of The Interior Hearings And Appeals Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the presiding officer issues a preliminary decision, the decision will include each heir's name, birth date, relationship to the decedent, and share of the estate, or a statement that the decedent died without heirs.
