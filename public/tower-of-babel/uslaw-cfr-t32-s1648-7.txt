32 C.F.R. § 1648.7 — Procedures upon transfer for classification.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1648.7
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1648.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1648 (Classification By Local Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A board to which a registrant is transferred for classification shall classify the registrant in the same manner it would classify a registrant assigned to it. When the classification has been decided by the transfer board, the file will be returned to the local board of jurisdiction in the manner prescribed by the Director.
