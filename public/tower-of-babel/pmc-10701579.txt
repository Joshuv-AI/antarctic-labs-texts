Thank you to our 2023 reviewers
Published: 2023

The editors and editorial staff at MTO wish to express our sincerest thank you to all the reviewers who volunteered their time and expertise this past year. With your help, we were able to maintain and even improve on our high scientific and ethical standards as well as the turn-around time for publications. As we move forward into a new year and with a new name, Molecular Therapy Oncology, and an expanded scope, we will continue to remain indebted to your service for helping make the journal as strong as possible. In the new year, we look to increase our reach and impact by accepting more types of publications including commentaries, editorials, and meeting summaries. We encourage you to consider submitting such “front matter” in addition to your scientific reports.

Timothy Cripe, Editor-in-Chief
