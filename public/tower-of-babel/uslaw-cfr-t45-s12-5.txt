45 C.F.R. § 12.5 — Awards.
Citation: us:cfr:45:12.5
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-12.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 12 (Disposal And Utilization Of Surplus Real Property For Public Health Purposes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where there is more than one applicant for the same property, it will be awarded to the applicant having a program of utilization which provides, in the opinion of the Department, the greatest public benefit. Where the property will serve more than one program, it will be apportioned to fit the needs of as many programs as is practicable.
