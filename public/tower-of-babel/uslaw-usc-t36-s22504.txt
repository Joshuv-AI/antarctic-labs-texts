36 U.S.C. § 22504 — Powers
Citation: us:usc:36:22504
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section22504
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 225 (American War Mothers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation may—
(1)
adopt a constitution, bylaws, and regulations;
(2)
adopt and alter a corporate seal;
(3)
adopt emblems and badges;
(4)
establish and maintain offices to conduct its activities;
(5)
establish State, territorial, and local subdivisions;
(6)
acquire, own, lease, encumber, and transfer property as necessary to carry out the purposes of the corporation, subject to section 22506(b) of this title;
(7)
publish a magazine and other publications;
(8)
sue and be sued; and
(9)
do any other act necessary and proper to carry out its purposes.
