25 U.S.C. § 3417 — Assignment of Federal personnel to State Indian economic development programs
Citation: us:usc:25:3417
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section3417
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 36 (Indian Employment, Training And Related Services). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any State with an economic development program targeted to Indian tribes shall be eligible to receive, at no cost to the State, such Federal personnel assignments as the Secretary, in accordance with the applicable provisions of the Intergovernmental Personnel Act of 1970 [42 U.S.C. 4701 et seq.], may deem appropriate to help ensure the success of such program.
