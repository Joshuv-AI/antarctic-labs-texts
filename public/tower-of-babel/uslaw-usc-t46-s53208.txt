46 U.S.C. § 53208 — Regulatory relief
Citation: us:usc:46:53208
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section53208
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part C (Financial Assistance Programs); Chapter 532 (Cable Security Fleet). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The telecommunications and other electronic equipment on an existing vessel that is redocumented under the laws of the United States for operation under an Operating Agreement under this chapter shall be deemed to satisfy all Federal Communication Commission equipment certification requirements, if—
(1)
such equipment complies with all applicable international agreements and associated guidelines as determined by the country in which the vessel was documented immediately before becoming documented under the laws of the United States;
(2)
that country has not been identified by the Secretary of the Department in which the Coast Guard is operating as inadequately enforcing international regulations as to that vessel; and
(3)
at the end of its useful life, such equipment shall be replaced with equipment that meets Federal Communication Commission equipment certification standards.
