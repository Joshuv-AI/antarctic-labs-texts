17 C.F.R. § 38.706 — Denial of charges and right to hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:17:38.706
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-38.706
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 38 (Designated Contract Markets). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In every instance where a respondent has requested a hearing on a charge that is denied, or on a sanction set by the disciplinary panel, the respondent must be given an opportunity for a hearing in accordance with the requirements of § 38.707 of this part.
