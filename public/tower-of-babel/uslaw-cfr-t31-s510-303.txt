31 C.F.R. § 510.303 — Correspondent account.
Citation: us:cfr:31:510.303
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-510.303
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 510 (North Korea Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term correspondent account means an account established by a U.S. financial institution for a foreign financial institution to receive deposits from, or to make payments on behalf of, the foreign financial institution, or to handle other financial transactions related to such foreign financial institution.
