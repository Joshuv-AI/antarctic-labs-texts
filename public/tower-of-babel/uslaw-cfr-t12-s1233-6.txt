12 C.F.R. § 1233.6 — Supervisory action.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1233.6
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1233.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1233 (Reporting Of Fraudulent Financial Instruments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Failure by a regulated entity to comply with this part may subject the regulated entity or the board members, officers, or employees thereof to supervisory action by FHFA, including but not limited to, cease-and-desist proceedings and civil money penalties.
