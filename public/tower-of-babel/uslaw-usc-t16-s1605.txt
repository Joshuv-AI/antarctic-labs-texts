16 U.S.C. § 1605 — Protection, use and management of renewable resources on non-Federal lands; utilization of Assessment, surveys and Program by Secretary to assist States, etc.
Citation: us:usc:16:1605
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section1605
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 36 (Forest And Rangeland Renewable Resources Planning); Subchapter I (Planning). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may utilize the Assessment, resource surveys, and Program prepared pursuant to this subchapter to assist States and other organizations in proposing the planning for the protection, use, and management of renewable resources on non-Federal land.
