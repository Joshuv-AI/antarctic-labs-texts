48 C.F.R. § 928.103-3 — 928.103-3 Payment bonds.
Citation: us:cfr:48:928.103-3
Creator: Chapter 9—Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-928.103-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 928 (Bonds And Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A determination that is in the best interest of the Government to require payment bonds in connection with other than construction contracts may be made by the contracting officer on individual acquisitions.
