18 C.F.R. § 12.60 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:18:12.60
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-12.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 12 (Safety Of Water Power Projects And Project Works). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The licensee of any dam or other project work classified as having a high or significant hazard potential, as defined in § 12.3(b)(13)(i) and (ii), is required to submit an Owner's Dam Safety Program to the Regional Engineer.
