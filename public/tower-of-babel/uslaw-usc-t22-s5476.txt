22 U.S.C. § 5476 — Notifications to Congress regarding assistance
Citation: us:usc:22:5476
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section5476
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 63 (Support For East European Democracy (Seed)); Subchapter VI (Reports To Congress). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Section 634A of the Foreign Assistance Act of 1961 (22 U.S.C. 2394–1; relating to reprogramming notifications) applies with respect to obligations of funds made available under that Act [22 U.S.C. 2151 et seq.] to carry out this chapter, notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter.
