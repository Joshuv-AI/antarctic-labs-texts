12 C.F.R. § 615.5353 — Relation to other actions.
Citation: us:cfr:12:615.5353
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-615.5353
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 615 (Funding And Fiscal Affairs, Loan Policies And Operations, And Funding Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In lieu of, or in addition to, the procedures in this subpart, the required minimum capital ratios for an institution may be established or revised through a written agreement or cease and desist proceedings under part C of title V of the Act, or as a condition for approval of an application.
