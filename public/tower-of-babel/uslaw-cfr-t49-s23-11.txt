49 C.F.R. § 23.11 — What compliance and enforcement provisions are used under this part?
Citation: us:cfr:49:23.11
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-23.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 23 (Participation Of Disadvantaged Business Enterprise In Airport Concessions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The compliance and enforcement provisions of part 26 (§§ 26.101 and 26.105 through 26.109) apply to this part in the same way that they apply to FAA recipients and programs under part 26.
