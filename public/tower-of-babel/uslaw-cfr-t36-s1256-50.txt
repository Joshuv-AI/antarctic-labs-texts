36 C.F.R. § 1256.50 — Information exempted from disclosure by statute.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1256.50
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1256.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1256 (Access To Records And Donated Historical Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In accordance with 5 U.S.C. 552(b)(3), NARA withholds records containing information that is specifically exempted from disclosure by statute when that statute:
(a) Requires withholding information from the public, leaving no discretion; or
(b) Establishes particular criteria for withholding or refers to particular types of matters to be withheld.
