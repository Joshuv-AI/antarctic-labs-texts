34 U.S.C. § 21305 — Grant increase
Citation: us:usc:34:21305
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section21305
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 213 (Rape Survivor Child Custody). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The amount of the increase provided to a State under the covered formula grants under this chapter shall be equal to not more than 10 percent of the average of the total amount of funding provided to the State under the covered formula grants under the 3 most recent awards to the State.
