25 C.F.R. § 47.7 — What are the expenditure limitations for Bureau-operated schools?
Citation: us:cfr:25:47.7
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-47.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 47 (Uniform Direct Funding And Support For Bureau-Operated Schools). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each Bureau-operated school must spend all allotted funds in accordance with applicable Federal regulations and local education financial plans. If a Bureau-operated school and OIEP region or Agency support services staff disagree over expenditures, the Bureau-operated school must appeal to the Director for a decision.
