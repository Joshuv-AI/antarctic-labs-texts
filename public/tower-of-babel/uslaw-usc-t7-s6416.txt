7 U.S.C. § 6416 — Independent evaluation of programs
Citation: us:usc:7:6416
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section6416
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 93 (Processor-Funded Milk Promotion Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Review and evaluation
The Comptroller General of the United States shall review and evaluate the order to—
(1)
determine the effectiveness of the promotion program conducted under this chapter on fluid milk sales;
(2)
determine if the assessments for the program have been passed back to milk producers by fluid milk processors; and
(3)
make recommendations for future funding and assessment levels for the program.
(b) Report to Congress
The Comptroller General shall submit a report to the Committee on Agriculture of the House of Representatives and the Committee on Agriculture, Nutrition, and Forestry of the Senate on the valuations made under this section no later than January 1, 1995.
