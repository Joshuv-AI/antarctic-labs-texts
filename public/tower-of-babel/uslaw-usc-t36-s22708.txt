36 U.S.C. § 22708 — Headquarters and principal place of business
Citation: us:usc:36:22708
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section22708
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 227 (Amvets (American Veterans)). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The headquarters and principal place of business of the corporation shall be in Maryland. However, the activities of the corporation are not confined to Maryland but may be conducted throughout the States, territories, and possessions of the United States.
