7 C.F.R. § 701.127 — Maximum ECP payments per person or legal entity.
Citation: us:cfr:7:701.127
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-701.127
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 701 (Emergency Conservation Program, Emergency Forest Restoration Program, And Certain Related Programs Previously Administered Under This Part). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A person or legal entity, as defined in part 1400 of this title, is limited to a maximum ECP cost-share of $500,000 per person or legal entity, per natural disaster.
(b) The Secretary may waive the maximum limitations described in paragraph (a) of this section to the maximum extent allowed by law.
