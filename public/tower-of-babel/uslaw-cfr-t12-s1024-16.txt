12 C.F.R. § 1024.16 — Title companies.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1024.16
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1024.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1024 (Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act (Regulation X)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No seller of property that will be purchased with the assistance of a federally related mortgage loan shall violate section 9 of RESPA (12 U.S.C. 2608). Section 1024.2 defines “required use” of a provider of a settlement service.
