7 C.F.R. § 4290.330 — Guarantee fee.
Citation: us:cfr:7:4290.330
Creator: Rural Business-Cooperative Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-4290.330
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 4290 (Rural Business Investment Company (“Rbic”) Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In cases of Leveraged Applications, the Applicant must pay to the Agency an issuance fee for each grant or debenture guarantee. The Agency may charge such fees as the Agency considers appropriate, so long as those fees are proportionally equal for each rural business investment company, with respect to any guarantee or grant issued under this subchapter.
