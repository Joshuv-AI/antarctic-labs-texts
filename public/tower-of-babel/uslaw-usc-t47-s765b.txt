47 U.S.C. § 765b — Elimination of procurement preferences
Citation: us:usc:47:765b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title47-section765b
Context: United States Code, Title 47 (Telecommunications); Chapter 6 (Communications Satellite System); Subchapter VI (Communications Competition And Privatization); Part C (Deregulation And Other Statutory Changes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subchapter or the Communications Act of 1934 [47 U.S.C. 151 et seq.] shall be construed to authorize or require any preference, in Federal Government procurement of telecommunications services, for the satellite space segment provided by INTELSAT, Inmarsat, or any successor entity or separated entity.
