5 C.F.R. § 338.601 — Prohibition of maximum-age requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:5:338.601
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-338.601
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 338 (Qualification Requirements (General)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A maximum-age requirement may not be applied in either competitive or noncompetitive examinations for positions in the competitive service except as provided by:
(a) Section 3307 of title 5, United States Code; or
(b) Public Law 93-259 which authorizes OPM to establish a maximum-age requirement after determining that age is an occupational qualification necessary to the performance of the duties of the position.
