34 C.F.R. § 5.13 — Preservation of agency records.
Citation: us:cfr:34:5.13
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-5.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 5 (Availability Of Information To The Public). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department does not destroy agency records that are the subject of a pending FOIA request, appeal, or lawsuit.
