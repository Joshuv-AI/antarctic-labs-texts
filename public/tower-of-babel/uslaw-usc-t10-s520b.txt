10 U.S.C. § 520b — Applicants for enlistment: authority to use funds for the issue of authorized articles
Citation: us:usc:10:520b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section520b
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 31 (Enlistments). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Funds appropriated to the Department of Defense may be used for the issue of authorized articles to applicants for enlistment.
