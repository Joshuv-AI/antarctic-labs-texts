43 U.S.C. § 1654 — Antitrust laws
Citation: us:usc:43:1654
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section1654
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 34 (Trans-Alaska Pipeline). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The grant of a right-of-way, permit, lease, or other authorization pursuant to this chapter shall grant no immunity from the operation of the Federal anti-trust laws.
