25 U.S.C. § 1482 — Premium charges; deposits in Indian Loan Guaranty and Insurance Fund
Citation: us:usc:25:1482
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section1482
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 17 (Financing Economic Development Of Indians And Indian Organizations); Subchapter II (Loan Guaranty And Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall fix such premium charges for the insurance and guarantee of loans as are in his judgment adequate to cover expenses and probable losses, and deposit receipts from such charges in the Indian Loan Guaranty and Insurance Fund established pursuant to section 1497(a) of this title.
