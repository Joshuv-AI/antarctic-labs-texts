14 C.F.R. § 107.2 — Applicability of certification procedures for products and articles.
Citation: us:cfr:14:107.2
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-107.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 107 (Small Unmanned Aircraft Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of part 21 of this chapter do not apply to small unmanned aircraft systems operated under this part unless the small unmanned aircraft system will operate over human beings in accordance with § 107.140.
