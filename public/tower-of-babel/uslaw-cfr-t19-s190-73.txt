19 C.F.R. § 190.73 — Electronic proof of exportation.
Citation: us:cfr:19:190.73
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-190.73
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 190 (Modernized Drawback). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Records kept through an electronic export system of the United States Government may be presented as actual proof of exportation only if CBP has officially approved the use of that electronic export system as proof of compliance for drawback claims. Official approval will be published as a general notice in the Customs Bulletin.
