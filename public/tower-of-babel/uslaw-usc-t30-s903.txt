30 U.S.C. § 903 — Field offices
Citation: us:usc:30:903
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section903
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 22 (Mine Safety And Health); Subchapter IV (Black Lung Benefits); Part A (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Secretary of Labor shall establish and operate such field offices as may be necessary to assist miners and survivors of miners in the filing and processing of claims under this subchapter. Such field offices shall, to the extent feasible, be reasonably accessible to such miners and survivors. The Secretary, in connection with the establishment and operation of field offices, may enter into arrangements with other Federal departments and agencies, and with State agencies, for the use of existing facilities operated by such departments and agencies. Where the establishment of separate facilities is not feasible the Secretary may enter into such arrangements as he deems necessary with the heads of Federal departments, agencies, and instrumentalities and with State agencies for the use of existing facilities and personnel under their control.
(b)
There are authorized to be appropriated for the purposes of subsection (a) such sums as may be necessary.
