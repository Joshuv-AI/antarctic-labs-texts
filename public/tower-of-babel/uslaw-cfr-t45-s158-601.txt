45 C.F.R. § 158.601 — General rule regarding the imposition of civil penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:45:158.601
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-158.601
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 158 (Issuer Use Of Premium Revenue: Reporting And Rebate Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If any issuer fails to comply with the requirements of this part, civil penalties, as described in this subpart, may be imposed.
