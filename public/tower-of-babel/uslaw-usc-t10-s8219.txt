10 U.S.C. § 8219 — Medical Department: composition
Citation: us:usc:10:8219
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8219
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 825 (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Medical Corps and Dental Corps, and such other staff corps as the Secretary of the Navy may establish under section 8090(b) of this title and designate to be in the Medical Department of the Navy, are in the Medical Department of the Navy.
