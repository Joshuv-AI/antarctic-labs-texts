5 C.F.R. § 9801.107 — Other rights and services.
Citation: us:cfr:5:9801.107
Creator: Council Of The Inspectors General On Integrity And Efficiency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-9801.107
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 9801 (Privacy Act Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this part shall be construed to entitle any person, as of right, to any service or to the disclosure of any record to which such person is not entitled under the Privacy Act.
