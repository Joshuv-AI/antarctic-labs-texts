29 U.S.C. § 2506 — Liaison role of Department of Labor
Citation: us:usc:29:2506
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section2506
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 27 (Women In Apprenticeship And Nontraditional Occupations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Department of Labor shall serve as a liaison among employers, labor unions, and community-based organizations. The liaison role may include—
(1)
coordination of employers, labor unions, and community-based organizations with respect to technical assistance provided under section 2503(a) of this title;
(2)
conducting regular assessment meetings with representatives of employers, labor unions, and community-based organizations with respect to such technical assistance; and
(3)
seeking the input of employers and labor unions with respect to strategies and recommendations for improving such technical assistance.
