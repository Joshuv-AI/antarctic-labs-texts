49 C.F.R. § 543.9 — Duration of exemption.
Citation: us:cfr:49:543.9
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-543.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 543 (Exemption From Vehicle Theft Prevention Standard). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each exemption under this part continues in effect unless it is modified or terminated under § 543.10, or the manufacturer ceases production of the exempted line.
