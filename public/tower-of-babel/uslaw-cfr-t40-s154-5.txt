40 C.F.R. § 154.5 — Burden of persuasion in determinations under this part.
Citation: us:cfr:40:154.5
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-154.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 154 (Special Review Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In making determinations under this part the Administrator shall be guided by the principle that the burden of persuasion that a pesticide product is entitled to registration or continued registration for any particular use or under any particular set of terms and conditions of registration is always on the proponent(s) of registration.
