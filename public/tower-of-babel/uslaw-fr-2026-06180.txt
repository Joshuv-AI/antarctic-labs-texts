ELECTION ASSISTANCE COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meetings

AGENCY: Election Assistance Commission.

ACTION: Sunshine Act Notice; notice of public meeting agenda.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Public Meeting: U.S. Election Assistance Commission Local 
Leadership Council 2026 Annual Meeting.

DATES: Tuesday, April 14, 2026, 9:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. CT and 
Wednesday, April 15, 2026, 9:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. CT. The public 
registration deadline for the event is on or before April 10, 2026. 
Written comments should be received no later than 24 hours prior to the 
start of the meeting.

ADDRESSES: The Drake, 140 East Walton Place, Chicago, IL 60611.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Kristen Muthig, Telephone: (202) 897-
9285, Email: [email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: 
    Purpose: In accordance with the Government in the Sunshine Act 
(Sunshine Act), Public Law 94-409, as amended (5 U.S.C. 552b), the U.S. 
Election Assistance Commission (EAC) will hold its annual meeting of 
the EAC Local Leadership Council (LLC) to conduct regular business, and 
discuss agency updates and upcoming programs.
    Agenda: The EAC's LLC will hold its 2026 Annual Meeting to conduct 
regular business, and discuss agency updates and upcoming programs.
    Registration is required to attend. Please register by April 10, 
2026, on the event page at: eac.gov/events/2026/04/14/2026-eac-local-leadership-council-annual-meeting.
    Public comments on the meeting must be submitted in writing on 
regulations.gov (docket ID: EAC-2026-0100). Comments must be received 
no later than 24 hours prior to the start of the meeting.
    Background: The LLC was established in June 2021 under agency 
authority pursuant to and in accordance with the provisions of the 
Federal Advisory Committee Act, as amended (5 U.S.C. App. 2). The 
Advisory Committee is governed by the Federal Advisory Committee Act, 
which sets forth standards for the formation and use of advisory 
committees. The Advisory Committee advises the EAC on how best to 
fulfill the EAC's statutory duties set forth in 52 U.S.C. 20922 as well 
as such other matters as the EAC determines. It shall provide a 
relevant and comprehensive source of expert, unbiased analysis and 
recommendations to the EAC on local election administration topics.
    The LLC consists of 100 members. The EAC appoints two members from 
each state after soliciting nominations from each state's election 
official professional association. Upon appointment, Advisory Committee 
members must be serving or have previously served in a leadership role 
in a state election official professional association.
    The full agenda will be posted in advance on the EAC website: 
eac.gov/events/2026/04/14/2026-eac-local-leadership-council-annual-meeting.
    Status: This meeting will be open to the public.
