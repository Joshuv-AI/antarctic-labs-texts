10 U.S.C. § 3701 — Definitions

Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part V (Acquisition); Subpart D (General Contracting Provisions); Chapter 271 (Truthful Cost Or Pricing Data (Truth In Negotiations)). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter:
(1) Cost or pricing data.—
The term “cost or pricing data” means all facts that, as of the date of agreement on the price of a contract (or the price of a contract modification), or, if applicable consistent with section 3706(a)(2) of this title, another date agreed upon between the parties, a prudent buyer or seller would reasonably expect to affect price negotiations significantly. Such term does not include information that is judgmental, but does include the factual information from which a judgment was derived.
(2) Subcontract.—
The term “subcontract” includes a transfer of commercial products or commercial services between divisions, subsidiaries, or affiliates of a contractor or a subcontractor.
