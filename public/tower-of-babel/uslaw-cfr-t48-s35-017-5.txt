48 C.F.R. § 35.017-5 — 35.017-5 Terminating FFRDC.
Citation: us:cfr:48:35.017-5
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-35.017-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 35 (Research And Development Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a sponsor's need for the FFRDC no longer exists, the sponsorship may be transferred to one or more Government agencies, if appropriately justified. If the FFRDC is not transferred to another Government agency, it shall be phased out.
