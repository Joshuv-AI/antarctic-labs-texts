12 U.S.C. § 1714 — Taxation
Citation: us:usc:12:1714
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section1714
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 13 (National Housing); Subchapter II (Mortgage Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subchapter shall be construed to exempt any real property acquired and held by the Secretary under this subchapter from taxation by any State or political subdivision thereof, to the same extent, according to its value, as other real property is taxed.
