49 C.F.R. § 655.51 — Compliance with testing procedures requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:49:655.51
Creator: Federal Transit Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-655.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 655 (Prevention Of Alcohol Misuse And Prohibited Drug Use In Transit Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The drug and alcohol testing procedures in 49 CFR Part 40 apply to employers covered by this part, and must be read together with this part, unless expressly provided otherwise in this part.
