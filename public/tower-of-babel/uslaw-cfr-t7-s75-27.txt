7 C.F.R. § 75.27 — How to file an appeal.
Citation: us:cfr:7:75.27
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-75.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 75 (Provisions For Inspection And Certification Of Quality Of Agricultural And Vegetable Seeds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any request for an appeal inspection may be made orally or in writing to the Federal Seed Laboratory. If made orally, written confirmation is required. The applicant shall clearly state the reasons for requesting the appeal service. The original and all available copies of the certificate shall be returned to the appeal inspector assigned to make the appeal inspection.
