Powerfully embedded subgroups of extensions of powerful pro- p p groups and its applications to automorphisms of finite groups
Sathasivam Kalithasan, Tony N. Mavely, Viji Z. Thomas

Abstract
One of the aims of this paper is to obtain structural results showing that powerful subgroups are abundant in pro- p p groups admitting certain powerful quotients. In particular, we obtain an analogue of Baer’s theorem for powerful pro- p p groups, namely that the powerfulness of H / Z n − 1 ​ ( H ) H/Z_{n-1}(H) implies that the n n th terms of both the lower p p -series and the lower central series of H H are powerfully embedded in H H . As a consequence, we obtain that if H H is a finitely generated pro- p p group and H / Z n ​ ( H ) H/Z_{n}(H) is a p p -adic analytic pro- p p group for some positive integer n n , then H H is a p p -adic analytic pro- p p group. We also study crossed squares of powerful p p -groups, establishing that if μ : M → G \mu:M\to G is a crossed module with M M a finite powerful p p -group and G G a finite p p -group, and if μ ⁡ ( M ) \mu(M) is powerfully embedded in G G , then both M ⊗ G M\otimes G and M ⊗ p G M\otimes^{p}G are powerful. Generalizing the commutator and the Frattini subgroups via the action of automorphisms, we study relative autocommutator and the generalized Frattini subgroups L A ​ ( G ) L_{A}(G) and Φ A ​ ( G ) \Phi_{A}(G) , respectively, for subgroups A A with Inn ⁡ ( G ) ⩽ A ⩽ Aut ⁡ ( G ) \operatorname{Inn}(G)\leqslant A\leqslant\operatorname{Aut}(G) . When G G is a finite powerful p p -group and A A is chosen from Aut Φ ⁡ ( G ) \operatorname{Aut}_{\Phi}(G) , Aut c ⁡ ( G ) \operatorname{Aut}_{c}(G) , or IA ⁡ ( G ) \operatorname{IA}(G) , we show that Inn ⁡ ( G ) \operatorname{Inn}(G) is powerfully embedded in A A . As a consequence, both L A ​ ( G ) L_{A}(G) and Φ A ​ ( G ) \Phi_{A}(G) are powerful subgroups of G G .
