The inter-universal Teichmüller theory and new Diophantine results over rational numbers. II
Zhong-Peng Zhou

Abstract
In the present paper, we continue our research on the generalized Fermat equation x r + y s = z t x^{r}+y^{s}=z^{t} with signature ( r , s , t ) (r,s,t) , where r , s , t ≥ 2 r,s,t\geq 2 are positive integers such that 1 r + 1 s + 1 t < 1 \frac{1}{r}+\frac{1}{s}+\frac{1}{t}<1 . All known positive primitive solutions for the generalized Fermat equation when 1 r + 1 s + 1 t < 1 \frac{1}{r}+\frac{1}{s}+\frac{1}{t}<1 are related to the Catalan solutions 1 n + 2 3 = 3 2 1^{n}+2^{3}=3^{2} and nine non-Catalan solutions. By applying inter-universal Teichmüller theory and its slight modification in the case of elliptic curves over rational numbers, we deduce that the generalized Fermat equation x r + y s = z t x^{r}+y^{s}=z^{t} has no non-trivial primitive solution except for those related to the Catalan solutions and nine non-Catalan solutions mentioned above, when ( r , s , t ) (r,s,t) is not a permutation of the following signatures: • ( 4 , 5 , n ) (4,5,n) , ( 4 , 7 , n ) (4,7,n) , ( 5 , 6 , n ) (5,6,n) , with 7 ≤ n ≤ 303 7\leq n\leq 303 . • ( 2 , 3 , n ) (2,3,n) , ( 3 , 4 , n ) (3,4,n) , ( 3 , 8 , n ) (3,8,n) , ( 3 , 10 , n ) (3,10,n) , with 11 ≤ n ≤ 109 11\leq n\leq 109 or n ∈ { 113,121 } n\in\{113,121\} . • ( 3 , 5 , n ) (3,5,n) , with 7 ≤ n ≤ 3677 7\leq n\leq 3677 ; ( 3 , 7 , n ) (3,7,n) , ( 3 , 11 , n ) (3,11,n) , with 11 ≤ n ≤ 667 11\leq n\leq 667 . • ( 3 , m , n ) (3,m,n) , with 13 ≤ m ≤ 17 13\leq m\leq 17 , m < n ≤ 29 m<n\leq 29 ; ( 2 , m , n ) (2,m,n) , with m ≥ 5 m\geq 5 , n ≥ 7 n\geq 7 . As a corollary, to solve the generalized Fermat equation x r + y s = z t x^{r}+y^{s}=z^{t} with exponents r , s , t ≥ 4 r,s,t\geq 4 , we are left with 244 244 signatures ( r , s , t ) (r,s,t) up to permutation; to solve the Beal conjecture, we are left with 2446 2446 signatures ( r , s , t ) (r,s,t) up to permutation.
