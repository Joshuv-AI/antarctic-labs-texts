Towards a Unified Framework for Bioperations on e ⋆ e^{\star} -Open Sets in Topological Spaces
G. Saravanakumar, M. Arun

Abstract
In this paper, we introduce the concept of e [ γ , γ ′ ] ⋆ e^{\star}_{[\gamma,\gamma^{\prime}]} -open sets in topological spaces and examine their properties in detail. Additionally, we propose a new class of functions, termed ( e [ γ , γ ′ ] ⋆ , e [ β , β ′ ] ⋆ ) (e^{\star}_{[\gamma,\gamma^{\prime}]},\ e^{\star}_{[\beta,\beta^{\prime}]}) -continuous functions, and explore their fundamental characteristics.
