14 U.S.C. § 2132 — Designation of officers with particular expertise in military justice or healthcare
Citation: us:usc:14:2132
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2132
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 21 (Personnel; Officers); Subchapter I (Appointment And Promotion). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Secretary Designation.—
The Secretary may designate a limited number of officers of the Coast Guard as having particular expertise in—
(1)
military justice; or
(2)
healthcare.
(b) Promotion and Grade.—
An individual designated under this section—
(1)
shall not be included on the active duty promotion list;
(2)
shall be promoted under section 2126; and
(3)
may not be promoted to a grade higher than captain.
