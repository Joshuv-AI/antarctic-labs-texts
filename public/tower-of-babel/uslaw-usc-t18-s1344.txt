18 U.S.C. § 1344 — Bank fraud
Citation: us:usc:18:1344
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1344
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 63 (Mail Fraud And Other Fraud Offenses). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever knowingly executes, or attempts to execute, a scheme or artifice—
(1)
to defraud a financial institution; or
(2)
to obtain any of the moneys, funds, credits, assets, securities, or other property owned by, or under the custody or control of, a financial institution, by means of false or fraudulent pretenses, representations, or promises;
shall be fined not more than $1,000,000 or imprisoned not more than 30 years, or both.
