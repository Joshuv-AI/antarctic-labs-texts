49 C.F.R. § 1560.201 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1560.201
Creator: Transportation Security Administration, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1560.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1560 (Secure Flight Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to individuals who believe they have been improperly or unfairly delayed or prohibited from boarding an aircraft or entering a sterile area as a result of the Secure Flight program.
