9 C.F.R. § 305.3 — Sanitation and adequate facilities.
Citation: us:cfr:9:305.3
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-305.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 305 (Official Numbers; Inauguration Of Inspection; Withdrawal Of Inspection; Reports Of Violation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Inspection shall not be inaugurated if an establishment is not in a sanitary condition nor unless the establishment agrees to maintain a sanitary condition and provides adequate facilities for conducting such inspection.
