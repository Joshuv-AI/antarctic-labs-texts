    On November 6, 2025, the Commission issued an order in Docket Nos. 
EL26-8-000, EL26-9-000, EL26-10-000, EL26-11-000, EL26-12-000, EL26-13-
000, EL26-14-000, EL26-15-000, EL26-16-000, EL26-17-000, EL26-18-000, 
EL26-19-000, EL26-20-000 and EL26-21-000 pursuant to section 206 of the 
Federal Power Act (FPA), 16 U.S.C. 824e, instituting an investigation 
to determine whether the above-captioned entities' respective Rate 
Schedules are unjust, unreasonable, unduly discriminatory or 
preferential, or otherwise unlawful. Armstrong Power, LLC, 193 FERC ] 
61,108 (2025).
    As established in Armstrong Power LLC, the refund effective date in 
date in Docket Nos. EL26-8-000, EL26-9-000, EL26-10-000, EL26-11-000, 
EL26-12-000, EL26-13-000, EL26-14-000, EL26-15-000, EL26-16-000, EL26-
17-000, EL26-18-000, EL26-19-000, EL26-20-000 and EL26-21-000, pursuant 
to section 206(b) of the FPA, is November 6, 2025.
    Any interested person desiring to be heard in Docket Nos. EL26-8-
000, EL26-9-000, EL26-10-000, EL26-11-000, EL26-12-000, EL26-13-000, 
EL26-14-000, EL26-15-000, EL26-16-000, EL26-17-000, EL26-18-000, EL26-
19-000, EL26-20-000 and EL26-21-000 must file a notice of intervention 
or motion to intervene, as appropriate, with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, in accordance with Rule 214 of the Commission's 
Rules of Practice and Procedure, 18 CFR 385.214 (2025), within 21 days 
of the date of issuance of the order.
    The Commission provides all interested persons an opportunity to 
view and/or print the contents of this document via the internet 
through the Commission's Home Page (http://www.ferc.gov) using the 
``eLibrary'' link.
