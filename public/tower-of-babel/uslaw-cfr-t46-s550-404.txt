46 C.F.R. § 550.404 — Amendment or dismissal of petitions.
Citation: us:cfr:46:550.404
Creator: Federal Maritime Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-550.404
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 550 (Regulations To Adjust Or Meet Conditions Unfavorable To Shipping In The Foreign Trade Of The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon the failure of a petitioner to comply with the provisions of this part, the petitioner will be notified by the Secretary and afforded reasonable opportunity to amend its petition. Failure to timely amend the petition may result in its dismissal. For good cause shown additional time for amendment may be granted.
