42 U.S.C. § 7144a — Establishment of security, counterintelligence, and intelligence policies
Citation: us:usc:42:7144a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section7144a
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 84 (Department Of Energy); Subchapter II (Establishment Of Department). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall be responsible for developing and promulgating the security, counterintelligence, and intelligence policies of the Department. The Secretary may use the immediate staff of the Secretary to assist in developing and promulgating those policies.
