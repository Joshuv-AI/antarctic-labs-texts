46 C.F.R. § 108.404 — Selection of fire detection system.
Citation: us:cfr:46:108.404
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-108.404
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 108 (Design And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If a fire detector is in a space, it must provide effective detection of fires most likely to occur in the space.
(b) The fire detection system must be designed to minimize false alarms.
