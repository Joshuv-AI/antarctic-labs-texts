2 U.S.C. § 7 — Time of election
Citation: us:usc:2:7
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section7
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 1 (Election Of Senators And Representatives). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Tuesday next after the 1st Monday in November, in every even numbered year, is established as the day for the election, in each of the States and Territories of the United States, of Representatives and Delegates to the Congress commencing on the 3d day of January next thereafter.
