33 U.S.C. § 1281b — Availability of Farmers Home Administration funds for non-Federal share
Citation: us:usc:33:1281b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section1281b
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 26 (Water Pollution Prevention And Control); Subchapter II (Grants For Construction Of Treatment Works). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, Federal assistance made available by the Farmers Home Administration to any political subdivision of a State may be used to provide the non-Federal share of the cost of any construction project carried out under section 1281 of this title.
