46 C.F.R. § 108.133 — Hull superstructure, structural bulkheads, decks, and deckhouses.
Citation: us:cfr:46:108.133
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-108.133
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 108 (Design And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each hull superstructure, structural bulkhead, deck, and deckhouse must be made of steel or an equivalent material.
