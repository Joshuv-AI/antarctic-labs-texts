42 C.F.R. § 65.9 — Additional conditions.
Citation: us:cfr:42:65.9
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-65.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 65 (National Institute Of Environmental Health Sciences Hazardous Waste Worker Training). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director may with respect to any award impose additional conditions prior to, or at the time of, any award when, in the Director's judgment, such conditions are necessary to assure the carrying out of the purposes of the award, the interest of the public health, or the conservation of funds awarded.
