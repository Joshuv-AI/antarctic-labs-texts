31 C.F.R. § 20.510 — What actions will the Federal Government take against a recipient determined to have violated this part?
Citation: us:cfr:31:20.510
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-20.510
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 20 (Governmentwide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a recipient is determined to have violated this part, as described in § 20.500 or § 20.505, the Department of the Treasury may take one or more of the following actions—
(a) Suspension of payments under the award;
(b) Suspension or termination of the award; and
(c) Suspension or debarment of the recipient under 22 CFR part 19, for a period not to exceed five years.
