48 U.S.C. § 1428c — Accounting procedures
Citation: us:usc:48:1428c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1428c
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 8A (Guam); Subchapter VI (Guam Development Fund). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The plan provided for in section 1428a of this title shall set forth such fiscal control and fund accounting procedures as may be necessary to assure proper disbursement, repayment, and accounting for such funds.
