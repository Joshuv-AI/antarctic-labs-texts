34 C.F.R. § 21.55 — Final decision if the Secretary does not review.
Citation: us:cfr:34:21.55
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-21.55
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 21 (Equal Access To Justice). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Secretary takes no action under § 21.54—
(a) The adjudicative officer's initial decision on the application becomes the Department's final decision 60 days after it is issued by the adjudicative officer; or
(b) The CRRA's decision on the application becomes the Department's final decision 60 days after it is issued by the CRRA.
