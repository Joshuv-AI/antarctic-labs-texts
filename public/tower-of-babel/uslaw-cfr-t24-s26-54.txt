24 C.F.R. § 26.54 — Judicial review.
Citation: us:cfr:24:26.54
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-26.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 26 (Hearing Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Judicial review shall be available in accordance with applicable statutory procedures and the procedures of the appropriate federal court.
