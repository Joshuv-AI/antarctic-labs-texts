43 U.S.C. § 421f — Existing loan contracts; negotiation by Secretary of amendments
Citation: us:usc:43:421f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section421f
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 12 (Reclamation And Irrigation Of Lands By Federal Government); Subchapter III (Institution And Construction Of Projects). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is hereby authorized to negotiate amendments to existing water service and irrigation distribution system loan contracts to conform said contracts to the provisions of sections 421a to 421h of this title.
