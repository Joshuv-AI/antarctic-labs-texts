49 C.F.R. § 365.119 — Opposed applications.
Citation: us:cfr:49:365.119
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-365.119
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 365 (Rules Governing Applications For Operating Authority). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the application is opposed, opposing parties are required to send a copy of their protest to the applicant and to FMCSA. All protests must include statements made under oath (verified statements). There are no personal appearances or formal hearings.
