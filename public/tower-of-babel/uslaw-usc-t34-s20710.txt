34 U.S.C. § 20710 — Education and outreach to trafficking survivors
Citation: us:usc:34:20710
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section20710
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 207 (Combating Domestic Trafficking In Persons). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General shall make available, on the website of the Office of Juvenile Justice and Delinquency Prevention, a database for trafficking victim advocates, crisis hotline personnel, foster parents, law enforcement personnel, and crime survivors that contains information on—
(1)
counseling and hotline resources;
(2)
housing resources;
(3)
legal assistance; and
(4)
other services for trafficking survivors.
