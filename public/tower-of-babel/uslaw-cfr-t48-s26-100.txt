48 C.F.R. § 26.100 — 26.100 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:48:26.100
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-26.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 26 (Other Socioeconomic Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart implements 25 U.S.C. 1544, which provides an incentive to prime contractors that use Indian organizations and Indian-owned economic enterprises as subcontractors.
