29 C.F.R. § 1977.2 — Purpose of this part.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1977.2
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1977.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1977 (Discrimination Against Employees Exercising Rights Under The Williams-Steiger Occupational Safety And Health Act Of 1970). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to make available in one place interpretations of the various provisions of section 11(c) of the Act which will guide the Secretary of Labor in the performance of his duties thereunder unless and until otherwise directed by authoritative decisions of the courts, or concluding, upon reexamination of an interpretation, that it is incorrect.
