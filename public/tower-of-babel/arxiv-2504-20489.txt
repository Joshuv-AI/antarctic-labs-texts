Sign Convention for 𝑨 ∞ \boldsymbol{A_{\infty}} -Operations in Bott–Morse Case
Kaoru ONO

Abstract
We describe the sign and orientation issue appearing the filtered A ∞ A_{\infty} -formulae in Lagrangian Floer theory using de Rham model in Bott–Morse setting. After giving the definition of filtered A ∞ A_{\infty} -operations in a Fukaya category, we verify the filtered A ∞ A_{\infty} -formulae.

Preliminaries

We use the convention on orientation on the fiber product (in the sense of Kuranishi structure) as in
[
5
, Section 8.2]

Let
p\colon M\to N
be a fiber bundle with oriented relative tangent bundle.
Restrict the fiber bundle to an open subset

Then we give an orientation on

using the isomorphism

TM=p^{*}TN\oplus T_{\rm fiber}M

is the relative tangent bundle.
Then our convention of the integration along fibers of

is

\int_{U}\alpha\wedge p_{!}\beta=\int_{p^{-1}(U)}p^{*}\alpha\wedge\beta,

where
\alpha\in\Omega^{*}(U)
and
\beta\in\Omega^{*}\bigl(p^{-1}(U)\bigr)

Reversing the orientation of

induces reversing of the orientation of
p^{-1}(U)
, hence the push-forward
p_{!}\beta
does not depend on
the choice of the orientation of

Therefore, for a proper submersion

with the oriented relative tangent bundle, the integration along fibers

p_{!}\colon\ \Omega^{k}(M)\to\Omega^{k-\dim M+\dim N}(N)

is well defined.

We have the following properties.

Proposition 2.1

(1)

Using them, we find the following.

Corollary 2.2

(q\circ p)_{!}(p^{*}\theta\wedge\beta)=q_{!}(\theta\wedge p_{!}\beta)

(base change)
.
