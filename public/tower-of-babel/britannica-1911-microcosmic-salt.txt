MICROCOSMIC SALT, or ammonium sodium hydrogen
or tho phosphate, NH 4 NaHPO 4 .4H 2 O, so named by the alchemists because it is contained in the decomposing urine of man (the “microcosm”). It is interesting historically as being the raw material from which Brand prepared phosphorus, whence it is also called “salt of phosphorus.” It may be obtained in large transparent crystals from a mixture of solutions of sal-ammoniac
and disodium phosphate, or by saturating a solution
of monosodium phosphate with ammonia. When heated to
redness, it leaves a transparent glass of sodium metaphosphate,
NaPO 3 , which like borax dissolves most metallic oxides, with
formation of glasses that often exhibit characteristic colours,
and which may be used in the qualitative analysis of substances.
