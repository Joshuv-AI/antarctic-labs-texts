\displaystyle\,\frac{G_{F}}{\sqrt{2}}\biggl\{V^{*}_{cb}V_{cq}\biggl[C_{1}(\mu)O_{1}^{c}(\mu)+C_{2}(\mu)O_{2}^{c}(\mu)\biggr]-V^{*}_{tb}V_{tq}\biggl[\sum_{i=3}^{10}C_{i}(\mu)O_{i}(\mu)\biggr]\biggr\}\;,

\displaystyle{\begin{array}[]{ll}\displaystyle O_{3}\,=\,(\bar{q}_{\alpha}b_{\alpha})_{V-A}\sum_{q^{\prime}}(\bar{q}^{\prime}_{\beta}q^{\prime}_{\beta})_{V-A}\;,&\displaystyle O_{4}\,=\,(\bar{q}_{\alpha}b_{\beta})_{V-A}\sum_{q^{\prime}}(\bar{q}^{\prime}_{\beta}q^{\prime}_{\alpha})_{V-A}\;,\\

Before proceeding, a short overview on the mixing of

-mesons

Generally speaking, based on the assumptions of two-quark structure,

\displaystyle\left(\begin{array}[]{c}|f_{0}(500)\rangle\\

\displaystyle\left(\begin{array}[]{cc}\cos{\varphi}&-\sin{\varphi}\\

\displaystyle\left(\begin{array}[]{c}|f_{0}(1370)\rangle\\

\displaystyle\left(\begin{array}[]{cc}\cos{\varphi^{\prime}}&-\sin{\varphi^{\prime}}\\

Finally, we turn to analyze CPAs of the
B^{0}\to\eta_{c}f_{0}
decays in the iPQCD
approach at NLO accuracy. For the CPAs in these considered
B^{0}
decays, the
B^{0}-\bar{B}^{0}
mixing effects should be taken into account. As is well known, the CPA
of the
B^{0}(\bar{B}^{0})\to\eta_{c}f_{0}
decays is time-dependent and can be defined
as

\displaystyle A_{\rm CP}

\displaystyle\equiv

\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma\left(\bar{B}^{0}(\Delta t)\to f_{\rm CP}\right)-\Gamma\left(B^{0}(\Delta t)\to f_{\rm CP}\right)}{\Gamma\left(\bar{B}^{0}(\Delta t)\to f_{\rm CP}\right)+\Gamma\left(B^{0}(\Delta t)\to f_{\rm CP}\right)}

(124)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle A_{\rm CP}^{\rm dir}\cos(\Delta m\Delta t)+A_{\rm CP}^{\rm mix}\sin(\Delta m\Delta t)\;,

where
\Delta m
is the mass difference between the two
B^{0}
mass eigenstates,

\Delta t=t_{\rm CP}-t_{tag}
is the time difference between the tagged

B^{0}
(
\bar{B}^{0}
) and the accompanying
\bar{B}^{0}
(
B^{0}
) with opposite

b
flavor decaying to the final CP-eigenstate
f_{\rm CP}
at the time
t_{\rm CP}
.
The direct and mixing-induced CPAs
A_{\rm CP}^{\rm dir}
and
A_{\rm CP}^{\rm mix}

can be written as

\displaystyle A_{\rm CP}^{\rm dir}

\displaystyle\equiv

\displaystyle\frac{\left|\lambda_{\rm CP}^{d,s}\right|^{2}-1}{1+\left|\lambda_{\rm CP}^{d,s}\right|^{2}},\qquad A_{\rm CP}^{\rm mix}\equiv\frac{2{\rm Im}(\lambda_{\rm CP}^{d,s})}{1+\left|\lambda_{\rm CP}^{d,s}\right|^{2}},

(125)

where the
CP
-violating parameters
\lambda_{\rm CP}^{d,s}
can be read as

\displaystyle\lambda_{\rm CP}^{d}

\displaystyle\equiv

\displaystyle\eta_{f}\;\frac{V_{tb}^{*}V_{td}}{V_{tb}V_{td}^{*}}\cdot\frac{\langle f_{\rm CP}|H_{\rm eff}|\bar{B}_{d}^{0}\rangle}{\langle f_{\rm CP}|H_{\rm eff}|B_{d}^{0}\rangle},\qquad\lambda_{\rm CP}^{s}\equiv\eta_{f}\;\frac{V_{tb}^{*}V_{ts}}{V_{tb}V_{ts}^{*}}\cdot\frac{\langle f_{\rm CP}|H_{\rm eff}|\bar{B}_{s}^{0}\rangle}{\langle f_{\rm CP}|H_{\rm eff}|B_{s}^{0}\rangle},

(126)

with
CP
-eigenvalue of the final states
\eta_{f}=+1
. Notice that,
for the
B_{s}^{0}
-meson decays, due to the presence of a non-negligible

\Delta\Gamma_{s}
, a non-zero ratio
(\Delta\Gamma/\Gamma)_{B_{s}^{0}}

is expected in the standard model
Beneke:1998sy
;
Fernandez:2006qx
.
Thus, for
B_{s}^{0}\to\eta_{c}f_{0}
decays, the
\Delta\Gamma_{s}
-induced
CPA
A_{\rm CP}^{\Delta\Gamma_{s}}
can be defined as follows
Fernandez:2006qx
:

\displaystyle A_{\rm CP}^{\Delta\Gamma_{s}}

\displaystyle\equiv

\displaystyle\frac{2{\rm Re}(\lambda_{\rm CP}^{s})}{1+\left|\lambda_{\rm CP}^{s}\right|^{2}}.

(127)

The above three CPAs in
B_{s}^{0}
-meson decays shown in Eqs. (
125
)
and (
127
) satisfy the following relation,

\displaystyle|A_{\rm CP}^{\rm dir}|^{2}+|A_{\rm CP}^{\rm mix}|^{2}+|A_{\rm CP}^{\Delta\Gamma_{s}}|^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 1\;.

(128)

IV
Conclusions and summary

We have studied the
B^{0}\to\eta_{c}f_{0}
decays in the iPQCD approach by including
the currently known NLO corrections. Here,
f_{0}
denotes the scalar mesons
f_{0}(500,980,1370,1500)
under the assumptions of two-quark structure, in which
f_{0}(500)(f_{0}(1370))
and
f_{0}(980)(f_{0}(1500))
have a mixing with angle
\varphi(\varphi^{\prime})

in the quark-flavor basis. We calculated the
CP
-averaged BRs and CPAs of

B^{0}\to\eta_{c}f_{0}
and also derived the
B^{0}\to\eta_{c}f_{0}(\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-}/K^{+}K^{-})

BRs under the narrow-width approximation at
|\varphi|=25^{\circ}
and
|\varphi^{\prime}|=146^{\circ}
for experimental tests in the future. In summary, we found that:

(1)

the numerical results of
{\rm BR}(B_{s}^{0}\to\eta_{c}f_{0}(980)(\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-}))=(2.87^{+1.38}_{-1.29})\times 10^{-4}
and
{\rm BR}(B_{d}^{0}\to\eta_{c}f_{0}(500)(\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-}))/{\rm BR}(B_{s}^{0}\to\eta_{c}f_{0}(980)(\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-}))=(12^{+8}_{-7})\%
in the iPQCD formalism are consistent with the available
predictions and measurements within uncertainties.

(2)

the large
B_{s}^{0}\to\eta_{c}f_{0}(1500)(\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-}/K^{+}K^{-})
BRs in the order of
10^{-6}\sim 10^{-4}

are accessible in the near-future LHCb and Belle-II experiments. The relevant tests and verifications could
provide useful information for identifying
f_{0}(1500)
as a primary or fragmented scalar glueball potentially.

(3)

the large
A_{\rm CP}^{\rm dir}(B_{d}^{0}\to\eta_{c}f_{0}(1370,1500))=(23.88^{+33.91}_{-13.68})\times 10^{-2}

and
A_{\rm CP}^{\rm dir}(B_{s}^{0}\to\eta_{c}f_{0}(1370,1500))=(-1.49^{+0.91}_{-2.43})\times 10^{-2}
imply
the important penguin contributions from the
B^{0}\to\eta_{c}f_{0}(1370,1500)
decays in
S1
, which will
be confronted with future experiments to further understand the QCD dynamics of
f_{0}(1370,1500)
.

(4)

the ratios
R_{d,s}[f_{0}(980)/f_{0}(500)]
and
R_{d,s}[f_{0}(1500)/f_{0}(1370)]
might provide the best ways
to explore and constrain the magnitudes of
\varphi
and
\varphi^{\prime}
in a clean manner correspondingly.

The hard functions

of quark and gluon propagators in the hard kernel. The explicit expressions are displayed

\displaystyle h_{fe}(x_{1},x_{3},b_{1},b_{3})=

\displaystyle K_{0}\biggl(\sqrt{x_{1}x_{3}(1-r_{2}^{2})}m_{B^{0}}b_{1}\biggl)\biggl[\theta(b_{1}-b_{3})K_{0}\biggl(\sqrt{x_{3}(1-r_{2}^{2})}m_{B^{0}}b_{1}\biggl)

(129)

\displaystyle\cdot I_{0}\biggl(\sqrt{x_{3}(1-r_{2}^{2})}m_{B^{0}}b_{3}\biggl)+\theta(b_{3}-b_{1})K_{0}\biggl(\sqrt{x_{3}(1-r_{2}^{2})}m_{B^{0}}b_{3}\biggl)

\displaystyle\cdot I_{0}\biggl(\sqrt{x_{3}(1-r_{2}^{2})}m_{B^{0}}b_{1}\biggl)\biggl]S_{t}(x_{3}),

\displaystyle h_{nfe}(x_{1},x_{2},x_{3},b_{1},b_{2})=

\displaystyle\biggl\{\theta(b_{2}-b_{1})I_{0}(m_{B^{0}}\sqrt{x_{1}x_{3}(1-r_{2}^{2})}b_{1})K_{0}(m_{B^{0}}\sqrt{x_{1}x_{3}(1-r_{2}^{2})}b_{2})\hskip 14.22636pt

\displaystyle+\ (b_{1}\leftrightarrow b_{2})\biggl\}\cdot\biggl(\begin{array}[]{cc}K_{0}(m_{B^{0}}F_{(1)}b_{2}),&{\rm for}\;\;F_{(1)}^{2}>0\\
\frac{\pi i}{2}H_{0}^{(1)}(m_{B^{0}}\sqrt{|F_{(1)}|^{2}}b_{2}),&{\rm for}\;\;F_{(1)}^{2}<0\end{array}\biggl),

where

is the Bessel function,
K_{0}
and

are the modified Bessel functions.
The

\displaystyle F_{(1)}^{2}=(x_{2}-x_{1})((x_{3}-x_{2})r_{2}^{2}-x_{3})+r_{c}^{2}.

(133)

with

E_{i}(t)

\displaystyle E_{fe}(t)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\alpha_{s}(t)\cdot\exp{[-S_{ab}(t)]}\cdot S_{t}(x)\;,

(134)

\displaystyle E_{nfe}(t)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\alpha_{s}(t)\cdot\exp{[-S_{cd}(t)]}\;,

(135)

in which the jet function

with

Cheng:2005ye

and
