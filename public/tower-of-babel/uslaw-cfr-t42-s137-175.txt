42 C.F.R. § 137.175 — Is a Self-Governance Tribe required to maintain a recordkeeping system?
Citation: us:cfr:42:137.175
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-137.175
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 137 (Tribal Self-Governance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. Tribes are required to maintain records and provide Federal agency access to those records as provided in § 137.177.
