10 U.S.C. § 3132 — Availability of appropriations for procurement of technical military equipment and supplies
Citation: us:usc:10:3132
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section3132
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part V (Acquisition); Subpart A (General); Chapter 207 (Budgeting And Appropriations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Funds appropriated to the Department of Defense for the procurement of technical military equipment and supplies remain available until spent.
