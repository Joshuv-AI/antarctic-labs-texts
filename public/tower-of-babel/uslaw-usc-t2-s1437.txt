2 U.S.C. § 1437 — Sense of Senate regarding adoption of simplified and streamlined acquisition procedures for Senate acquisitions
Citation: us:usc:2:1437
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1437
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 24 (Congressional Accountability); Subchapter V (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the sense of the Senate that the Committee on Rules and Administration of the Senate should review the rules applicable to purchases by Senate offices to determine whether they are consistent with the acquisition simplification and streamlining laws enacted in the Federal Acquisition Streamlining Act of 1994 (Public Law 103–355).
