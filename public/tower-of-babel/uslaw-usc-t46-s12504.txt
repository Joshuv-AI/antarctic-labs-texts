46 U.S.C. § 12504 — Information available from the system
Citation: us:usc:46:12504
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section12504
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part H (Identification Of Vessels); Chapter 125 (Vessel Identification System). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For law enforcement or other purposes and under conditions prescribed by the Secretary of Transportation, the Secretary—
(1)
shall make available information in the vessel identification system to a State making information available under section 12503(a) of this title; and
(2)
may make available information in the vessel identification system to others.
