41 C.F.R. § 301-52.8 — -52.8 Notification of claim errors.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-52.8
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-52.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-52 (52—Claiming Reimbursement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After the employee submits a travel claim, the agency must notify the employee in seven working days of any errors that would prevent payment within 30 calendar days after submission.
