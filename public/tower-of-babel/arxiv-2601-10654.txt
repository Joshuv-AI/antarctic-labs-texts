Abstract
We show that there exists a completely bounded (c.b. in short) homomorphism u u from a C ∗ C^{*} -algebra C C with the lifting property (in short LP) into a QWEP von Neumann algebra N N that is not strongly similar to a ∗ * -homomorphism, i.e. the similarities that “orthogonalize” u u (which exist since u u is c.b.) cannot belong to the von Neumann algebra N N . Moreover, the map u u does not admit any c.b. lifting up into the WEP C ∗ C^{*} -algebra of which N N is a quotient. We can take C = C ∗ ​ ( 𝔽 ) C=C^{*}(\mathbb{F}) (full C ∗ C^{*} -algebra) where 𝔽 \mathbb{F} is any nonabelian free group and N = B ⁡ ( H ) ​ ⊗ ¯ ​ M N=B(H)\bar{\otimes}M where M M is the von Neumann algebra generated by the reduced C ∗ C^{*} -algebra of 𝔽 \mathbb{F} .
