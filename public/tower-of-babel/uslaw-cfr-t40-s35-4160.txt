40 C.F.R. § 35.4160 — What does EPA do if more than one group applies for a TAG at the same site?
Citation: us:cfr:40:35.4160
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-35.4160
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 35 (State And Local Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When multiple groups apply, EPA will rank each applicant relative to other applicants using the criteria in § 35.4155.
