2 C.F.R. § 1882.120 — Are any of my Federal assistance awards exempt from this part?
Citation: us:cfr:2:1882.120
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1882.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1882 (Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part does not apply to any award for which the Assistant Administrator for Procurement determines that the application of this part would be inconsistent with the international obligations of the United States or the laws or regulations of a foreign government.
