42 U.S.C. § 11294 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:42:11294
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section11294
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 118 (Alzheimer’S Disease And Related Dementias Research); Subchapter V (Educational Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
To carry out sections 11291 and 11293 of this title, there are authorized to be appropriated $1,000,000 for each of the fiscal years 1988 through 1991.
(b)
There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary for each of the fiscal years 1992 through 1996, to carry out section 11292 of this title.
