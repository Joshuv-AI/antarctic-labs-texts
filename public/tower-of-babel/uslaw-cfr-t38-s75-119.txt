38 C.F.R. § 75.119 — Finality of Secretary determination.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 75 (Information Security Matters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).
