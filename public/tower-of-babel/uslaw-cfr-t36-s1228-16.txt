36 C.F.R. § 1228.16 — When must agencies retrieve records that have been loaned?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1228.16
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1228.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1228 (Loan Of Permanent And Unscheduled Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An agency must contact the recipient of loaned permanent or unscheduled records 30 days prior to the expiration of the loan period (as stated in the loan agreement) to arrange for the return of the records. If the agency extends the duration of the loan, it must notify NARA (see § 1228.12(b)) in writing, specifying the reason for the extension and providing the new expiration date of the loan.
