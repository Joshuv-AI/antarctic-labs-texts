42 C.F.R. § 65a.10 — a.10 For what purposes may grant funds be spent?
Citation: us:cfr:42:65a.10
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-65a.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 65a (PART 65a—NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH SCIENCES HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCES BASIC RESEARCH AND TRAINING GRANTS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A grantee shall spend funds it receives under this part solely in accordance with the approved application and budget, the regulations of this part, the terms and conditions of the award, and the applicable cost principles prescribed in 2 CFR parts 200 and 300, subpart E.
