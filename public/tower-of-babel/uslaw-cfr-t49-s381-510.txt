49 C.F.R. § 381.510 — May the FMCSA end a pilot program before its scheduled completion date?
Citation: us:cfr:49:381.510
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-381.510
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 381 (Waivers, Exemptions, And Pilot Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The FMCSA will immediately terminate a pilot program if there is reason to believe the program is not achieving a level of safety that is at least equivalent to the level of safety that would be achieved by complying with the regulations.
