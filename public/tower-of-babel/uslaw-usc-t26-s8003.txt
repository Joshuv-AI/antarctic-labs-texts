26 U.S.C. § 8003 — Election of chairman and vice chairman
Citation: us:usc:26:8003
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section8003
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 91 (Organization And Membership Of The Joint Committee). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Joint Committee shall elect a chairman and vice chairman from among its members.
