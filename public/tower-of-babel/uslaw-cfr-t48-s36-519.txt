48 C.F.R. § 36.519 — 36.519 Organization and direction of the work.
Citation: us:cfr:48:36.519
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-36.519
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 36 (Construction And Architect-Engineer Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.236-19, Organization and Direction of the Work, in solicitations and contracts when a cost-reimbursement construction contract is contemplated.
