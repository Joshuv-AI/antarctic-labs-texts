42 C.F.R. § 486.110 — Condition for coverage: Inspection of equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:42:486.110
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-486.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 486 (Conditions For Coverage Of Specialized Services Furnished By Suppliers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Inspections of all X-ray equipment and shielding are made by qualified individuals at intervals not greater than every 24 months.
(a) Standard—qualified inspectors. Inspections are made at least every 24 months by a radiation health specialist who is on the staff of or approved by an appropriate State or local government agency.
(b) Standard—records of inspection and scope of inspection. The supplier maintains records of current inspections which include the extent to which equipment and shielding are in compliance with the safety standards outlined in § 486.108.
