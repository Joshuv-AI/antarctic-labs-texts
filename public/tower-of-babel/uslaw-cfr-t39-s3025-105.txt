39 C.F.R. § 3025.105 — Issuance of Commission views.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3025.105
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3025.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3025 (Procedures Related To Commission Views Submitted To The Secretary Of State). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Commission will review timely filed comments responding to a Commission solicitation pursuant to § 3025.103(a) prior to submitting its views to the Secretary of State.
(b) After Commission views are developed, the Commission shall post Commission views in the applicable docket established pursuant to § 3025.103(a) and submit Commission views to the Secretary of State pursuant to 39 U.S.C. 407(c)(1).
