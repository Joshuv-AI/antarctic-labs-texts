46 C.F.R. § 109.209 — Appliances for watertight integrity.
Citation: us:cfr:46:109.209
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-109.209
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 109 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Before getting underway, the master or person in charge shall insure that each appliance for watertight integrity is closed and watertight.
(b) If existing conditions warrant, the master or person in charge may permit appliances for watertight integrity to be open while afloat.
