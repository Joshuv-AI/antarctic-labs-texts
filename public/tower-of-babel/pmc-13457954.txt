Knowledge enhanced framework for managing electricity generation and consumption in micro smart grids using Heronian mean MCDM approach
Authors: Zeeshan Ali, Chiranjibe Jana, Hamza Zafar, Jungeun Kim, Nikola Ivković
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
AbstractWith globalization and outsourcing trends, new and modern industrial enterprises frequently rely on a wide network of suppliers to construct sophisticated electricity generation and consumption. To maintain efficient production planning and scheduling, integration, and coping with the wide range of data from electricity generation to consumption management in a micro-smart grid has become increasingly critical. To manage the above dilemma, first, we develop circular intuitionistic uncertain linguistic models with basic laws. Secondly, we design four different types of techniques based on the above model, called the “arithmetic Heronian mean”, “weighted arithmetic Heronian mean”, “geometric Heronian mean”, and “weighted geometric Heronian mean” models, with a few basic properties. Moreover, for the evaluation of the electricity generation and consumption management in a micro-smart grid, we construct a valuable and dominant model of the “multi-attribute border approximation area comparison” technique for derived approaches. Additionally, we discuss case studies for the proposed theory, which covers the usage of electricity generation and consumption in a micro-smart-grid for node classification, scenario classification, and link prediction. Finally, our solution will be ranked with the ranking models of prevailing models for analyzing the supremacy and efficiency of the derived approaches.Keywords: Circular intuitionistic uncertain linguistic models, Electricity generation and consumption, Heronian mean operators, Multi-attribute decision-making models, Micro-smart grid technologiesSubject terms: Energy science and technology, Engineering, Mathematics and computing

Introduction

Electricity generation and consumption management in micro smart grids play an important and significant role in different real-world frameworks. The increase in the demand for electricity leads us towards maximizing the generation of electricity from natural resources and trying to minimize unnecessary usage. To get electricity from natural resources, an advanced sustainable energy system is required. For this, the Micro smart grid (MSG) is a good choice to be used, because the MSG combines different energy sources and applies them in advanced technologies to manage electricity in a specific area. The technique of MSG focuses on different aspects of electricity from generation to consumption. The key component of MSG is to generate energy from both renewable and non-renewable sources and store the leftovers for future usage. In short words, the effective and reliable electricity management in micro smart grids depends on the intelligent forecasting and optimization, real-time monitoring control, and smart coordination of loads, storage, and generation. It also focuses on the load and deeply monitors the flow through sensors and smart meters. It is flexible to use with a main grid or individually, which highlights the reliability of MSG.

Decision-making1 plays an important role in everyday life to achieve a favorable and desired outcome among a group of outcomes. The key point to solving the problem is to know which point in decision-making leads you toward accuracy and vice versa. That’s why decision-making is a vital area for researchers that has been focused on for a long time. Initially, it was easy to decide on a crisp set because it contained only two possibilities, either 0 or 1. But when it came to an intermediate situation, the crisp set failed. For example, the probability of rain is 70 percent, so we are not sure about rain, and we can’t assign 0 (not raining) or 1 (raining) to this problem. To address these types of real-world problems, Zadeh2 invented the idea of the fuzzy set (FS) in 1965. FS deals with the problems of uncertainty and allocates a degree of membership to an element, which shows the positive influence of the concerned element. While FS deals with the uncertainty through membership, which only provides information on the element having a positive influence on the decision-makers, it is limited to dealing with the element having a negative influence or hesitation. To tackle this, the idea of intuitionistic FS (IFS) was introduced by Atanassov3,4. In IFS, the non-membership term was introduced for assigning values to the element having a negative influence. IFS contains both the membership and non-membership terms, and the sum of these two terms always lies between 0 and 1. IFS tackles the uncertainty in a more general way by assigning value to that negatively influenced element.

The procedure and system of the circular IFS (Cr-IFS) were derived by Atanassov5 in 2020. The system of Cr-IFS plays a vital role, especially when uncertainty and ambiguity are present. The Cr-IFS contained three different functions, like the truth function, falsity function, and radius function, which are independent terms. The condition of truth and falsity functions are the same as IFSs, but the radius term has different characteristics. The procedure of the Cr-IFS is a modified version of all existing techniques. The real-world problems mostly contain natural language and are not specified by numerical values, so to address such problems, a linguistic variable and linguistic term set (LS) were proposed by Zadeh6 in 1975. Kaur et al.7 used the technique of fuzzy framework and evaluated the analytical hierarchical process. In 2008, Herrera et al.8 invented the fuzzy LS (FLS) by integrating the FS with LS to cope with complex data. Further, Xu9 generalized the LS to uncertain LS (ULS) in 2004, which described the human opinions very precisely and efficiently. In 2012, the valuable and dominant system of intuitionistic ULS (IULS) was deliberated by Liu and Jin10, which contained the uncertain linguistic variables with intuitionistic fuzzy numbers.

Literature review

Designing a knowledge-improved technique to support electricity generation and consumption management in a micro-smart-grid is an important and very famous project in the context of the smart grid framework, which is increasing the sustainability and energy efficiency. Many scholars have worked on it because of their characteristics and usage in real-world environments. For instance, Mohammadi et al.11 derived the micro-smart-grid for the consumption of electricity generation with the help of multi-objective uzzy optimization. Furthermore, Fabrizio et al.12 designed the micro-smart-grid for the valuation and monitoring of the renewable sources exploitation in an agro-industrial site. Moreover, Kim et al.13 presented the intelligent micro energy grid in the 5G era for the assessment of the testbeds, platforms, business cases, and next-generation applications. In 2023, Mohammadi et al.14 derived the hybrid model of the micro-smart-grid for the derivation of the production and consumption of energy based on fuzzy multi-objective optimization models.

The procedure of the simple decision-making technique plays a vital and efficient role in the valuation of the best and worst decision among some information. Many types of decision-making models are developed for the assessment of real-world problems. For instance, decision-making systems, decision-making systems with multi-attribute or criteria, and also with group decision-making analysis are very famous. These models are used for the valuation of real-world problems. Different Multi-attribute decision-making (MADM) models are used to solve decision-making problems properly, and every model has their importance, but the Multi-Attribute Border Approximation Area Comparison (MABAC) model was invented by Pamucar and Cirovic15 in 2015. The MABAC model is a hybrid decision-making model, which is also used for the same purpose as the MADM model, but the MABAC model can reduce the ambiguity and problems because of the “border approximation area”. The MABAC model also uses the aggregation operators and similarity measures at the same time. Different types of hybrid techniques have been developed; for instance, Dutta et al.16 derived the TOPSIS technique and TODIM technique, which were developed based on similarity measures and aggregation operators, but not both.

The hybrid decision-making technique has been developed based on different types of aggregation operators. This manuscript aims to choose the following existing techniques for the construction of the hybrid models. Therefore, the simple version of the Heronian mean (HM) operator was deliberated and designed by Sykora17, which is a simple shape of the operators without parameters. Furthermore, the generalized HM (GHM) operators were invented by Sykora18, which are very effective and efficient operators because of the parameters, which are involved in the construction of the aggregation operators. These operators are very valuable and dominant, but not enough to cope with awkward and complex real-world problems. Therefore, this study also chooses the existing technique of the Bonferroni mean (BM) operator, which was established by Bonferroni19 in 1950. The BM operator is also very valuable and effective for the aggregation of the data, compared to simple averaging operators. The geometric BM (GBM) operator was invented by Zhu20 for hesitant fuzzy frameworks. The GBM operators are more powerful compared to simple BM operators because of their structure and parameters.

Using the HM operator, GHM operator, BM operator, and GBM operators has received valuable attention from different scholars. Many scholars have used these operators for the construction of the MABAC models. For instance, Zhao et al.21 invented the MABAC mode for IFSs. Further, Liu and Chen22 derived the HM operators for IFSs. Zhang et al.23 diagnosed the HM operators for some normal IFSs. Additionally, Yu24 developed the GHM operators for IFSs. Further, Liu et al.25 presented the HM operators for IULS. Moreover, Xu and Yager26 derived the BM operators for IFSs. The GBM operators for IFS were developed by Xia et al.27. Liu et al.28 determined the BM operators for IFSs based on Dombi norms. He et al.29 established the BM operators for IFSs based on interaction information. The GBM operators for IFSs were developed by Zhou and He30. In 2015, the power Einstein operators for IULS were developed by Liu and Shi31. Further, Wang and Wang32 derived the simple aggregation model for IULSs in 2015. Moreover, Jin et al.33 stated the generalized Hamacher operators for IULSs. Additionally, the BM operators for IULS were developed by Liu and Zhang34 in 2019.

What is the main objective of this study?

The main objective of this study is to integrate the model of circular intuitionistic fuzzy framework and the structure of the uncertain linguistic variables to develop the idea of circular IULS (Cr-IULS), which is the modified version of the many existing models. For instance, FSs, IFSs, ULS, IULS, Cr-IFSs, and their possible combinations. The main problems are that sometimes the decision-maker provides the opinions of human beings in the linguistic variables shape, these types of problems are evaluated with the help of ULSs, but no relation with IFSs. Similarly, sometime decision-maker gives the truth and falsity information, which is handled with the help of IFSs, but not possible for ULSs. The main problem is that when an expert provides information in the shape of truth, falsity, radius, and uncertain linguistic variables, then the existing techniques are not able to cope with it. Therefore, this study aims to develop the main technique of Cr-IULS with a hybrid structure of the MABAC model based on the Heronian operator and Bonferroni operator. Because the study above leads us to the point that there are limitations in the traditional models when decision-makers solve decision-making problems. Some of them are described as: (i) The construction of new ideas from the existing domain is very challenging; (ii) Creating new operational laws for the invented definition is also challenging; (iii) The modification of the existing model for the new idea is difficult; and (iv) Fitting the modified model to the real-life application is involved. To consider these challenges, this study aims on the development of the novel idea for the circular IULS (Cr-IULS) that can solve the above problems and challenges. The considered idea is developed by integrating the basic concept of Cr-IFS and ULS to cope with vague data. For this new idea, this study aims to modify the MABAC model and HM operator to overcome such challenges. Many existing techniques have now become the special cases of our new model, such as FS, IFS, Cr-IFS, ULS, IULS, and their integrations. The modification of the MABAC model for this new idea enables us to get the best alternative from the set of alternatives. From the above discussion, this study notices that the new model can overcome all the limitations faced by the traditional models as well as those faced by existing frameworks. Because the derived structure of Cr-IULS is more valuable than the existing model, it is compared to the existing model to evaluate the real-world challenges. The Cr-IULS is the generalization of the following models: for instance, FS, IFS, LS, ULS, intuitionistic LS, IULS, Cr-IFSs, and their integration. Our generalization can solve complex problems, but in the case of existing models are not. The upgradation of the MABAC model and HM operators for the invented technique is also the generalization of many existing models and operators. Some of them are demonstrated; for instance, the HM operator, GHM operator, BM operator, GBM operator, MABAC model, arithmetic operator, geometric operator, and so on.

Electricity generation and consumption management in a micro smart grid

Micro smart grid (MSG) is an advanced and complete system to manage the generation and consumption of electricity. MSG uses an advanced monitoring system to combine different energy sources, such as wind, solar, and hydropower, to manage electricity. Although MSG manages electricity generation and consumption due to the irregular nature of renewable energy and changes that occur in the consumption area, it needs an appropriate decision-making system to address these complexities and make the system error-free. This decision-making system gives strength to MSG to maintain the generation and consumption of electricity in that specific area. So, for this, we have five alternatives to make an appropriate and optimal strategy for MSG to manage the generation and consumption of electricity. The alternatives are described one by one.

Grid diesel with load management (GDLM)

In GDLM, a diesel generator is put in combination with the main grid. Also, a load prioritization system is placed that systematically manages the electricity according to various loads. The main grid works as the primary source and supplies electricity in normal conditions when it is available. The diesel generator works as an emergency supplier, and it works when the grid is not functional. The load prioritization system controls the load and gives importance to critical loads, such as hospitals, and schools on less important loads, such as signals, park lights, etc. It is highly reliable and can easily integrate with other systems. It minimizes the unnecessary use of diesel due to the load prioritization system. The disadvantages of GDLM are carbon emissions and fuel dependency.

Grid solar storage energy (GSSE)

The GSSE is the combination of solar energy, which is the primary renewable energy source during daytime, battery storage, which is used to store the excess energy produced during daytime, and a connection to the main grid, which provides backup when solar energy and storage are not functional. Solar energy reduces the dependency on the main grid and also minimizes electricity bills. The battery storage provides backup during nighttime and also when there is cloudy weather. The main advantage of GSSE is to maximize clean energy and minimize carbon emissions. It also provides an uninterested supply of electricity due to battery storage. The disadvantages of GSSE are the high initial cost required to get solar panels and batteries, and it depends on the weather.

Renewable diesel hybrid system (RDHS)

RDHS combines solar and wind power, which are renewable energy sources, with a diesel generator. It creates a total grid system for managing the electricity. It is mainly designed for such areas where the grid access is not sufficient. This system contains both solar panels and wind power, which increases the chance of getting maximum energy during the daytime because the solar system takes energy from the sun, while the wind power takes it from the wind. The diesel generator is used as a backup when both the solar and wind power are not functional or do not provide sufficient electricity. The advantages of RDHS are independence and reliability. The consumption of diesel is quite low due to renewable energy sources. The disadvantages are environmental impact, diesel dependencies, and high maintenance costs.

Intelligent renewable energy system (IRES)

The IRES combines the artificial intelligence energy system with renewable energy sources. It maximizes production and efficiency while minimizing the misuse of electricity. The combination of solar panels with wind turbines is used in IRSE to get maximum energy. An energy storage system is used to store the energy and will be used when it is required. A special artificial intelligence-based system is used to predict load and maximize the generation. IRSE contains an advanced energy management system that helps to make real-time decisions on energy generation and consumption. The advantages of this system are load shifting, using less fuel, and the detection of issues on time before failure. The limitations are data dependencies and complex structure.

Smart solar demand response system (SSDRS)

The SSDRS is the combination of a solar energy system with a battery to store the surplus energy, in addition to a demand response system. SSDRS not only focuses on generating energy but also minimizes the use of electricity in peak hours using smart control. The demand response system is used to adjust the loads and power usage according to the demand of the specific areas. The advantages of this system are load shifting, which minimizes the stress on the grid, is highly efficient, and reduces energy bills. The disadvantages are the requirement of smart devices and a limited backup capacity. The geometrical interpretation of the considered alternatives is listed in Fig. 3.

Conclusion

After the evaluation of the development of the proposed model, illustrative examples, and comparative analysis, we concluded that the globalization and outsourcing trends are new and modern industrial enterprises frequently rely on a wide network of suppliers to construct sophisticated electricity generation and consumption. To manage the above dilemma, this study developed the circular intuitionistic uncertain linguistic models with basic laws. Furthermore, to maintain efficient production planning and scheduling, integration, and coping with the wide range of data from electricity generation to consumption management in a micro-smart grid has become increasingly critical. This study designed four different types of techniques based on the above model, called the “arithmetic Heronian mean”, “weighted arithmetic Heronian mean”, “geometric Heronian mean”, and “weighted geometric Heronian mean” models, with a few basic properties. Moreover, for the evaluation of the electricity generation and consumption management in a micro-smart grid, this study constructed a valuable and dominant model of the “MABAC” technique for derived approaches. Additionally, this study also discussed case studies for the proposed theory, which covered the usage of electricity generation and consumption in a micro-smart-grid for node classification, scenario classification, and link prediction. At last, the proposed solution is compared with the ranking models of prevailing models for analyzing the supremacy and efficiency of the derived approaches.

The derived model is very strong and effective, but in some real-world scenarios, it does not work properly. For instance, when an expert provides a group of truth, falsity, radius, and uncertain linguistic variables, then the proposed model is not able to evaluate the considered data. For instance, experts need to develop the model of circular intuitionistic hesitant uncertain linguistic sets and their extensions.

In the upcoming times, we aim to extend this idea for the Circular Pythagorean uncertain linguistic term set in the future because it will be the generalization of our proposed work. We will also modify different aggregation operators, such as the Aczel-Alsina aggregation operator, Frank aggregation operator, and many more, for this exciting work.
