21 C.F.R. § 864.8950 — Russell viper venom reagent.
Citation: us:cfr:21:864.8950
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-864.8950
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 864 (Hematology And Pathology Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. Russell viper venom reagent is a device used to determine the cause of an increase in the prothrombin time.
(b) Classification. Class I (general controls).
