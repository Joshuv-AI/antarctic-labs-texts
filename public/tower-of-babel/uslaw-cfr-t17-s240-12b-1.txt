17 C.F.R. § 240.12b-1 — b-1 Scope of regulation.
Citation: us:cfr:17:240.12b-1
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-240.12b-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 240 (General Rules And Regulations, Securities Exchange Act Of 1934). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules contained in this regulation shall govern all registration statements pursuant to sections 12(b) and 12(g) of the Act and all reports filed pursuant to sections 13 and 15(d) of the Act, including all amendments to such statements and reports, except that any provision in a form covering the same subject matter as any such rule shall be controlling.
