40 C.F.R. § 1850.32 — Definitions.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1850 (Availability Of Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) For purposes of this subpart, the terms individual, maintain, record, and system of records shall have the meanings set forth in 5 U.S.C. 552a(a).
(b) Working days are business days and do not include Saturday, Sunday, or federal holidays.
