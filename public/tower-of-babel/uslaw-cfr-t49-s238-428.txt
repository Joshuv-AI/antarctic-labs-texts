49 C.F.R. § 238.428 — Overheat sensors.
Citation: us:cfr:49:238.428
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-238.428
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 238 (Passenger Equipment Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Overheat sensors for each wheelset journal bearing shall be provided. The sensors may be placed either onboard the equipment or at reasonable intervals along the railroad's right-of-way.
