42 U.S.C. § 4769 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:42:4769
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section4769
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 62 (Intergovernmental Personnel Program); Subchapter IV (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated, without fiscal year limitation, such sums as may be necessary to carry out the programs authorized by this chapter.
