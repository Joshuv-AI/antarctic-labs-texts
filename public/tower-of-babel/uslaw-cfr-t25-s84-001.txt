25 C.F.R. § 84.001 — What is the purpose of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:25:84.001
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-84.001
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 84 (Encumbrances Of Tribal Land—Contract Approvals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to implement the provisions of the Indian Tribal Economic Development and Contract Encouragement Act of 2000, Public Law 106-179, which amends section 2103 of the Revised Statutes, found at 25 U.S.C. 81.
