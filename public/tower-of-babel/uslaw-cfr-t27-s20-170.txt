27 C.F.R. § 20.170 — Physical inventory.
Citation: us:cfr:27:20.170
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-20.170
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 20 (Distribution And Use Of Denatured Alcohol And Rum). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Once in each calendar year and when requested by an appropriate TTB officer, each permittee shall perform and record a physical inventory of each formulation of new and recovered specially denatured spirits.
