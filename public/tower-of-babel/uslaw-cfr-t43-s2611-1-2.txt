43 C.F.R. § 2611.1-2 — -2 Determination of suitability and availability of lands.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2611.1-2
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2611.1-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2610 (Carey Act Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The authorized officer shall evaluate the suitability and availability of the lands for agricultural development under the Carey Act utilizing the criteria and procedures in part 2400 of this title.
