16 U.S.C. § 5305 — Acceptance and use of donations
Citation: us:usc:16:5305
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section5305
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 73 (Rhinoceros And Tiger Conservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may accept and use donations to provide assistance under section 5304 of this title. Amounts received by the Secretary in the form of donations shall be transferred to the Secretary of the Treasury for deposit into the Fund.
