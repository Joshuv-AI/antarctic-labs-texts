29 C.F.R. § 100.502 — Application.
Citation: us:cfr:29:100.502
Creator: National Labor Relations Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-100.502
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 100 (Administrative Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This regulation (§§ 100.501-100.570) applies to all programs or activities conducted by the agency, except for programs or activities conducted outside the United States that do not involve individuals with handicaps in the United States.
