16 U.S.C. § 559a — Reward for information leading to arrest and conviction for violating laws and regulations
Citation: us:usc:16:559a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section559a
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 3 (Forests; Forest Service; Reforestation; Management); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Agriculture may pay rewards from appropriations available for the protection and management of the national forests, under such regulations as he may prescribe, for information leading to the arrest and conviction for violation of the laws and regulations relating to fires in or near national forests, or for the unlawful taking of, or injury to, Government property.
