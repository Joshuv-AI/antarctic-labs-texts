22 U.S.C. § 290k–5 — Applicability of Bretton Woods Agreements Act
Citation: us:usc:22:290k–5
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section290k–5
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter XXVI (Multilateral Investment Guarantee Agency). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of section 286b of this title shall apply with respect to the Agency to the same extent as with respect to the Bank and the International Monetary Fund.
