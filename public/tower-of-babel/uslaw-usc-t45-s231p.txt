45 U.S.C. § 231p — Free transportation
Citation: us:usc:45:231p
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2024
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title45-section231p
Context: United States Code, Title 45 (Railroads); Chapter 9 (Retirement Of Railroad Employees); Subchapter IV (Railroad Retirement Act Of 1974). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It shall not be unlawful for carriers by railroad subject to this subchapter to furnish free transportation to individuals receiving annuities under this subchapter in the same manner as such transportation is furnished to employees in their service.
