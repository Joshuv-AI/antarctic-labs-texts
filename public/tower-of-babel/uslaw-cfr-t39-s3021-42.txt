39 C.F.R. § 3021.42 — Due date for Postal Service response.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3021.42
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3021.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3021 (Rules For Appeals Of Postal Service Determinations To Close Or Consolidate Post Offices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The statement or brief of the Postal Service, and of any other participant opposing return of the matter for further consideration, shall be filed not more than 14 days after the date for filing of petitioner's statement.
