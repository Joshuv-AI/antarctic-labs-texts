5 C.F.R. § 919.105 — Does this part apply to me?
Citation: us:cfr:5:919.105
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-919.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 919 (Governmentwide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Portions of this part (see table at § 919.25(b)) apply to you if you are a(n)—
(a) Person who has been, is, or may reasonably be expected to be, a participant or principal in a covered transaction;
(b) Respondent (a person against whom the OPM has initiated a debarment or suspension action);
(c) OPM debarring or suspending official; or
(d) OPM official who is authorized to enter into covered transactions with non-Federal parties.
