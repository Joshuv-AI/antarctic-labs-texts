29 C.F.R. § 4003.7 — Exhaustion of administrative remedies.
Citation: us:cfr:29:4003.7
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4003.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4003 (Rules For Administrative Review Of Agency Decisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in § 4003.22(b), a person aggrieved by an initial determination of PBGC covered by this part, other than an initial determination subject to reconsideration that is issued by a Department Director, has not exhausted his or her administrative remedies until he or she has filed a request for reconsideration under subpart C of this part or an appeal under subpart D of this part, whichever is applicable, and a decision granting or denying the relief requested has been issued.
