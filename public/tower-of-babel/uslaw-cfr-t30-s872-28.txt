30 C.F.R. § 872.28 — Are there any restrictions on how you may use minimum program make up funds?
Citation: us:cfr:30:872.28
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-872.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 872 (Moneys Available To Eligible States And Indian Tribes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. You may only use minimum program make up funds for:
(a) Priority 1 and 2 coal reclamation under sections 403(a)(1) and (2) of SMCRA;
(b) Priority 3 reclamation that is part of Priority 1 or 2 coal reclamation under sections 403(a)(1) or (2) of SMCRA and § 874.13 of this chapter;
