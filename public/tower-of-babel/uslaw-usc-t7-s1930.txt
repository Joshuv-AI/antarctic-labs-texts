7 U.S.C. § 1930 — Continued availability of appropriated funds for direct real estate loans to farmers and ranchers
Citation: us:usc:7:1930
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1930
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 50 (Agricultural Credit); Subchapter I (Real Estate Loans). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Funds appropriated for the purpose of making direct real estate loans to farmers and ranchers under this subchapter shall remain available until expended.
