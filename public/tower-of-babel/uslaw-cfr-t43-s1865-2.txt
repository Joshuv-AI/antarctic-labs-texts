43 C.F.R. § 1865.2 — Issuance of corrected patent or document of conveyance.
Citation: us:cfr:43:1865.2
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-1865.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 1860 (Conveyances, Disclaimers And Correction Documents). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon the authorized officer's determination that all of the requirements of the Act for issuance of a corrected patent or document of conveyance have been met, the authorized officer shall issue a corrected patent or document of conveyance.
