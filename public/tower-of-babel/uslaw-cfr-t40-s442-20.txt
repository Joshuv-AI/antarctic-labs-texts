40 C.F.R. § 442.20 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:40:442.20
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-442.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 442 (Transportation Equipment Cleaning Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to discharges resulting from the cleaning of rail tank cars which have been used to transport chemical or petroleum cargos.
