42 C.F.R. § 136.111 — Interest.
Citation: us:cfr:42:136.111
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-136.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 136 (Indian Health). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Tribes and Tribal organizations shall not be held accountable for interest earned on grant funds, pending disbursement by such organization.
