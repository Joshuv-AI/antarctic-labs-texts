One step at a time: How so ever baby, it may be
Authors: Ashish Jain
Published: 2023

It is with immense honor and enthusiasm that I extend my heartfelt gratitude for entrusting me with the responsibility of serving as the Secretary of our esteemed Periodontal Society. As I step into this role, I am deeply humbled by the opportunity to contribute to our collective mission of advancing periodontal health and fostering innovation in our field.

First and foremost, I extend my sincere appreciation to my predecessors for their dedicated service and commitment in steering our society toward excellence. Their hard work has paved the way for the remarkable advancements we have achieved, setting a high standard for us to uphold and build upon.

As we bask in the past, the beacon of the future needs to be aligned with the era gone-by so as to keep the track focused and, more importantly relevant. Before we take this next step, let us pause in the moment to dwell on what we have missed, if any, in our journey so far and if so, the bigger question is, “Can we catch up.” A pause in the moment again, to dwell, has “Perio – The specialty” – slipped in being a choice for our students and if so, the bigger question is, “Can we climb up.” A pause in the moment once more, to dwell, has implants in the Perio curriculum disintegrated, and if so, the bigger question is, “Can we integrate it.”

Over the past year or so, we at Indian Society of Periodontology (ISP) have very consciously accepted that the probable pointer for “all these misses” has been that “we missed the bus of implants.” While our colleagues in other specialties rode the “Implant bus” occupying the front seats, we let it go and were satisfied with the last few ones remaining. But not anymore.

As we navigate this transformative phase, I invite each member of our society to actively engage, contribute, and lead. Let us leverage our collective expertise, share best practices, and explore innovative approaches that push the boundaries of integrating implants into periodontal care. For the starters, ISP has initiated a special and exclusive “Research Grant Call” for dissertation topics on dental implants. The call for proposals is aimed at supporting individual MDS students who are pursuing their postgraduation in the specialty of periodontology and intend to work on “oral implant-related research work” during their MDS course. The scheme proposes to support 25 scholarships in total. The quantum of support shall be based on the scientific merit of the proposal. The amount of support provided to the MDS scholar shall range from providing or covering the cost of hardware to funding the entire cost of the study. We appeal to all our worthy colleagues in academics to please encourage your 1st-year MDS students to submit a proposal and let us all take this first step collectively.

I hope in the next editorial we will have more steps to share and we all will take them in tandem. As the newly appointed secretary, I am deeply honored to serve alongside an exceptional team of colleagues who are committed to advancing our field and fostering the growth of our society. Allow me to express my heartfelt congratulations to our newly appointed president, Dr. D. Gopalakrishnan, whose dedication and vision will undoubtedly steer us towards greater heights. Your leadership will undoubtedly inspire us all, as we navigate the challenges and opportunities ahead.

In addition, I extend a warm welcome to our esteemed editor, Dr. Baljeet Singh, whose expertise and diligence will be pivotal in maintaining the high standards of our publications. Your contributions will undoubtedly enrich the academic discourse in our society.

To our dedicated office-bearers and executive committee members, I extend my gratitude for your willingness to serve our society. Your diverse perspectives and unwavering commitment will be instrumental in guiding our initiatives and fostering a thriving community.

Thank you once again, for entrusting me with the responsibilities of the Secretary, and I look forward to a productive and fulfilling tenure ahead.

Warm regards,
