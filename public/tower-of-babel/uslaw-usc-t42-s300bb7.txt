42 U.S.C. § 300bb–7 — Enforcement
Citation: us:usc:42:300bb–7
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section300bb–7
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter XX (Requirements For Certain Group Health Plans For Certain State And Local Employees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any individual who is aggrieved by the failure of a State, political subdivision, or agency or instrumentality thereof, to comply with the requirements of this subchapter may bring an action for appropriate equitable relief.
