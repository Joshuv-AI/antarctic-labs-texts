20 C.F.R. § 222.55 — When a stepchild is dependent.
Citation: us:cfr:20:222.55
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-222.55
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 222 (Family Relationships). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee's stepchild, as described in § 222.35, is considered dependent on the employee if the stepchild receiving at least one-half of his or her support from the employee at one of the times shown in § 222.51.
