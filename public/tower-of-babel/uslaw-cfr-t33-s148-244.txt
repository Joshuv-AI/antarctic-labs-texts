33 C.F.R. § 148.244 — Who must represent the parties at a formal hearing?
Citation: us:cfr:33:148.244
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-148.244
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 148 (Deepwater Ports: General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) All organizations that are parties to the proceeding must be represented by an attorney. Individuals may represent themselves.
(b) Any attorney representing a party to the proceeding must file a notice of appearance according to § 20.301(b) of this chapter.
(c) Each attorney must be in good standing and licensed to practice before a court of the United States or the highest court of any State, territory, or possession of the United States.
