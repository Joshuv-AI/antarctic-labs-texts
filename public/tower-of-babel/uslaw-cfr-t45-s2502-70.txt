45 C.F.R. § 2502.70 — What must an AmeriCorps employee do if served with process or pleadings that includes a covered claim?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2502.70
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2502.70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2502 (Employee Indemnification Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An AmeriCorps employee who is named as a defendant (or the personal representative of the AmeriCorps employee's estate) in a legal proceeding that includes a covered claim and who wishes to seek indemnification must promptly notify their supervisor, who then promptly notifies the Office of General Counsel. Former employees must directly notify the Office of General Counsel.
