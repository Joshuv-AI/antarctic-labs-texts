7 U.S.C. § 2219 — Salaries; how paid
Citation: us:usc:7:2219
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2219
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 55 (Department Of Agriculture). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Agriculture is authorized and directed to pay the salary of each employee from the roll of the bureau, independent division, or office in which the employee is working, and no other.
