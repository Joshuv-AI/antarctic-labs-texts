48 C.F.R. § 1313.302-1-70 — 1313.302-1-70 Non-commercial purchase orders.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1313.302-1-70
Creator: Chapter 13—Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1313.302-1-70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1313 (Simplified Acquisition Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Insert provision 1352.213-71, Instructions for Submitting Quotations under the Simplified Acquisition Threshold—Non-Commercial, or similar language in all solicitations for non-commercial purchase orders under the simplified acquisition threshold. The contracting officer shall indicate whether electronic submissions of quotations will be accepted. Paragraph (b)(4) of provision 1352.213-71 may be tailored based on the evaluation factors.
