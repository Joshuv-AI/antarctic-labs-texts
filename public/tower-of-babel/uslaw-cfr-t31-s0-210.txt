31 C.F.R. § 0.210 — Cooperation with official inquiries.
Citation: us:cfr:31:0.210
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-0.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 0 (Department Of The Treasury Employee Rules Of Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees directed by competent Department or other federal authority to provide oral or written responses to questions, or to provide documents and other materials concerning matters of official interest, shall timely respond fully, truthfully, and, when required, under oath.
