42 U.S.C. § 11361 — Housing affordability strategy
Citation: us:usc:42:11361
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section11361
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 119 (Homeless Assistance); Subchapter IV (Housing Assistance); Part A (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Assistance may be made under this subchapter only if the grantee certifies that it is following—
(1)
a consolidated plan which has been approved by the Secretary in accordance with section 12705 of this title (referred to in such section as a “comprehensive housing affordability strategy”), or
(2)
a comprehensive homeless assistance plan which was approved by the Secretary during the 180-day period beginning on November 28, 1990, or during such longer period as may be prescribed by the Secretary in any case for good cause.
