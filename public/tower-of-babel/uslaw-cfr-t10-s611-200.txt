10 C.F.R. § 611.200 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:10:611.200
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-611.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 611 (Advanced Technology Vehicles Manufacturer Assistance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart sets forth the policies and procedures applicable to the award and administration of grants by DOE for advanced technology vehicle manufacturing facilities as authorized by section 136(b) of the Energy Independence and Security Act (Pub. L. 110-140).
