46 U.S.C. § 503 — Notice of seizure
Citation: us:usc:46:503
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section503
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 5 (Other General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When a forfeiture of a vessel or cargo accrues, the official of the United States Government required to give notice of the seizure of the vessel or cargo shall include in the notice, if they are known to that official, the name and the place of residence of the owner or consignee at the time of the seizure.
