12 C.F.R. § 1022.141 — Reasonable charges for certain disclosures.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1022.141
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1022.141
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1022 (Fair Credit Reporting (Regulation V)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pursuant to section 612(f) of the FCRA, 15 U.S.C. 1681j(f), the charge imposed by a consumer reporting agency for a disclosure to the consumer pursuant to section 609 of the FCRA, 15 U.S.C. 1681g, shall not exceed the maximum allowable charge set by the Bureau.
