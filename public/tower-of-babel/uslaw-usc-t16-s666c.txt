16 U.S.C. § 666c — Applicability to Tennessee Valley Authority
Citation: us:usc:16:666c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section666c
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 5A (Protection And Conservation Of Wildlife); Subchapter I (Game, Fur-Bearing Animals, And Fish). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of this Act shall not apply to the Tennessee Valley Authority.
