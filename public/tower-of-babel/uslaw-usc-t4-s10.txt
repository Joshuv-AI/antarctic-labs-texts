4 U.S.C. § 10 — Modification of rules and customs by President
Citation: us:usc:4:10
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title4-section10
Context: United States Code, Title 4 (Flag And Seal, Seat Of Government, And The States); Chapter 1 (The Flag). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any rule or custom pertaining to the display of the flag of the United States of America, set forth herein, may be altered, modified, or repealed, or additional rules with respect thereto may be prescribed, by the Commander in Chief of the Armed Forces of the United States, whenever he deems it to be appropriate or desirable; and any such alteration or additional rule shall be set forth in a proclamation.
