48 C.F.R. § 23.000 — 23.000 Scope of part.
Citation: us:cfr:48:23.000
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-23.000
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 23 (Environment, Sustainable Acquisition, And Material Safety). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes acquisition policies and procedures supporting the Government's program to protect and improve the quality of the environment, to foster markets for sustainable products and services, and to ensure proper handling and notification of hazardous materials.
