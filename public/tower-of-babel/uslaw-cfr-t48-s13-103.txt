48 C.F.R. § 13.103 — 13.103 Use of standing price quotations.
Citation: us:cfr:48:13.103
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-13.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 13 (Simplified Acquisition Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Authorized individuals do not have to obtain individual quotations for each purchase. Standing price quotations may be used if—
(a) The pricing information is current; and
(b) The Government obtains the benefit of maximum discounts before award.
