43 C.F.R. § 3861.6-1 — -1 Payment of charges of the public survey office.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3861.6-1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3861.6-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3860 (Mineral Patent Applications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

With regard to the platting of the claim and other office work in the Bureau of Land Management office, including the preparation of the copies of the plat and field notes to be furnished the claimant, that office will make an estimate of the cost thereof, which amount the claimant will deposit with it to be passed to the credit of the fund created by “Deposits by Individuals for Surveying Public Lands.”
