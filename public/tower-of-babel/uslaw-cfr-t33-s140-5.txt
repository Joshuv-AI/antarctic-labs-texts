33 C.F.R. § 140.5 — Exemptions during construction.
Citation: us:cfr:33:140.5
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-140.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 140 (General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Officer in Charge, Marine Inspection, may exempt any unit under construction from any requirements of this subchapter that would be impracticable or unreasonable to apply during construction or erection of the unit.
