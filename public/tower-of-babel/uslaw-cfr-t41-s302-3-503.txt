41 C.F.R. § 302-3.503 — -3.503 Requirements for SES separation-relocation travel.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-3.503
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-3.503
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-3 (3—Relocation Allowance By Specific Type). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before issuing payment for separation-relocation travel, agencies must establish timeframes for employees to submit a request for authorization and approval of relocation expenses. Travel advances for separation relocation may not be authorized.
