43 C.F.R. § 2883.14 — What happens to my grant or TUP if I die?
Citation: us:cfr:43:2883.14
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2883.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2880 (Rights-Of-Way Under The Mineral Leasing Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If a grant or TUP holder dies, any inheritable interest in the grant or TUP will be distributed under State law.
(b) If the distributee of a grant or TUP is not qualified to hold a grant or TUP under § 2883.10 of this subpart, BLM will recognize the distributee as grant or TUP holder and allow the distributee to hold its interest in the grant or TUP for up to two years. During that period, the distributee must either become qualified or divest itself of the interest.
