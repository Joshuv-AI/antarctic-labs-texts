2 C.F.R. § 176.40 — Procedure.
Citation: us:cfr:2:176.40
Creator: Office Of Management And Budget Government-Wide Guidance For Federal Financial Assistance
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-176.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 176 (Award Terms For Assistance Agreements That Include Funds Under The American Recovery And Reinvestment Act Of 2009, Public Law 111-5). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The award official shall insert the standard award term in this subpart in all awards funded in whole or in part with Recovery Act funds, except for those that are classified, awarded to individuals, or awarded under mandatory and entitlement programs, except as specifically required by OMB, or expressly exempted from the reporting requirement in the Recovery Act.
