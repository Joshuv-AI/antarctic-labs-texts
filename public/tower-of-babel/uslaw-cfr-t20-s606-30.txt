20 C.F.R. § 606.30 — Interest rates on advances.
Citation: us:cfr:20:606.30
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-606.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 606 (Tax Credits Under The Federal Unemployment Tax Act; Advances Under Title Xii Of The Social Security Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Advances made to States pursuant to title XII of the Social Security Act shall be subject to interest payable on the due dates specified in § 606.31. 1 The interest rate for each calendar year will be 10 percent or, if less, the rate determined by the Secretary of the Treasury and announced to the States by the Department.
