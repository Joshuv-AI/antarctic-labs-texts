29 C.F.R. § 2700.36 — Contents of complaint.
Citation: us:cfr:29:2700.36
Creator: Federal Mine Safety And Health Review Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-2700.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 2700 (Procedural Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A complaint for compensation shall include:
(a) A short and plain statement of the facts giving rise to the claim, including the period for which compensation is claimed;
(b) The total amount of the compensation claimed, if known; and
(c) A legible copy of any pertinent order of withdrawal or, if a legible copy is not available, the text of the order.
