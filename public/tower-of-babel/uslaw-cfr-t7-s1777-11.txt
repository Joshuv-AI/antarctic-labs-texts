7 C.F.R. § 1777.11 — Public infrastructure—Making, processing, and servicing loans and grants.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1777.11
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1777.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1777 (Water And Waste Facility Loans And Grants To Alleviate Health Risks). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless specifically modified by this part, loans and grants will be made and processed in accordance with 7 CFR 1780, “Water and Waste Loans and Grants” and serviced in accordance with 7 CFR 1782, “Servicing of Water and Waste Programs.”
