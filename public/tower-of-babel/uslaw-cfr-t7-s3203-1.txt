7 C.F.R. § 3203.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3203.1
Creator: Office Of Procurement And Property Management, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3203.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3203 (Guidelines For The Transfer Of Excess Computers Or Other Technical Equipment Pursuant To Section 14220 Of The 2008 Farm Bill). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth the procedures to be utilized by USDA when transferring excess USDA computers or other technical equipment to an organization for the purposes of distribution to a city, town, or local government entity in a rural area as authorized by 7 U.S.C. 2206b.
