16 C.F.R. § 1118.8 — General or special orders seeking information.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1118.8
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1118.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1118 (Investigations, Inspections And Inquiries Under The Consumer Product Safety Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission may require by the issuance of general or special orders any person or firm to submit in writing any reports and answers to questions as the Commission may prescribe. The reports or answers shall be made under oath, and shall be filed within the time prescribed by the Commission. Procedures regarding compliance with general or special orders and motions to limit or quash such orders are provided for in § 1118.9.
