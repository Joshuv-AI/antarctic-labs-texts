31 U.S.C. § 6404 — Agency requirements
Citation: us:usc:31:6404
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section6404
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 64 (Data Standards For Grant Reporting). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 1 year after the date on which guidance is issued or updated under subsection (b) or (c), respectively, of section 6403, the head of each agency shall—
(1)
ensure that all of the Federal awards that the agency issues use data standards for all future information collection requests; and
(2)
amend existing information collection requests under chapter 35 of title 44 (commonly known as the “Paperwork Reduction Act”) to comply with the data standards established under section 6402 of this chapter, in accordance with the guidance issued by the Secretary and the Director under section 6403 of this chapter.
