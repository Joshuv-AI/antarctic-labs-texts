40 U.S.C. § 11301 — Responsibility of Director
Citation: us:usc:40:11301
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section11301
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Chapter 113 (Responsibility For Acquisitions Of Information Technology); Subchapter I (Director Of Office Of Management And Budget). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In fulfilling the responsibility to administer the functions assigned under chapter 35 of title 44, the Director of the Office of Management and Budget shall comply with this chapter with respect to the specific matters covered by this chapter.
