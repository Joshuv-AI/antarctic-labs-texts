39 C.F.R. § 953.14 — Final Agency decision.
Citation: us:cfr:39:953.14
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-953.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 953 (Mailability). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Judicial Officer shall render a final Agency decision. The decision shall be served upon the parties and the postal official having custody of any mail detained pursuant to the determination or ruling.
