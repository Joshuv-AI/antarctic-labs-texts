34 U.S.C. § 12403 — Application requirements
Citation: us:usc:34:12403
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section12403
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 121 (Violent Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Subchapter III (Violence Against Women); Part F (National Stalker And Domestic Violence Reduction). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An application for a grant under this part shall be submitted in such form and manner, and contain such information, as the Attorney General may prescribe. In addition, applications shall include documentation showing—
(1)
the need for grant funds and that State or local funding, as the case may be, does not already cover these operations;
(2)
intended use of the grant funds, including a plan of action to increase record input; and
(3)
an estimate of expected results from the use of the grant funds.
