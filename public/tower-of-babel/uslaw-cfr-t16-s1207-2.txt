16 C.F.R. § 1207.2 — Effective date.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1207.2
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1207.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1207 (Safety Standard For Swimming Pool Slides). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part 1207 shall become effective July 17, 1976. All swimming pool slides manufactured after that date must meet the requirements of this part 1207.
