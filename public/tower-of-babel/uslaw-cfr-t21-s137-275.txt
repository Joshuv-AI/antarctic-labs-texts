21 C.F.R. § 137.275 — Yellow corn meal.
Citation: us:cfr:21:137.275
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-137.275
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 137 (Cereal Flours And Related Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yellow corn meal conforms to the definition and standard of identity prescribed by § 137.250 for white corn meal except that cleaned yellow corn is used instead of cleaned white corn.
