25 C.F.R. § 48.111 — How does a lessee pay the Bureau-operated school under a lease?
Citation: us:cfr:25:48.111
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-48.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 48 (Leases Of Land Or Facilities Of Bureau-Operated Schools And Fundraising Activities At Bureau-Operated Schools). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A lessee must pay consideration and any late payment charges due under the lease to the Bureau by certified check, money order, or electronic funds transfer made out to the Bureau and containing identifying information as provided for in the lease.
