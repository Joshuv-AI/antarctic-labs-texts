10 U.S.C. § 3793 — Copyrights, patents, designs, etc.; acquisition
Citation: us:usc:10:3793
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section3793
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part V (Acquisition); Subpart D (General Contracting Provisions); Chapter 275 (Proprietary Contractor Data And Rights In Technical Data); Subchapter III (Other Provisions Relating To Proprietary Contractor Data And Rights In Technical Data). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Funds appropriated for a military department available for making or procuring supplies may be used to acquire any of the following if the acquisition relates to supplies or processes produced or used by or for, or useful to, that department:
(1)
Copyrights, patents, and applications for patents.
(2)
Licenses under copyrights, patents, and applications for patents.
(3)
Design and process data, technical data, and computer software.
(4)
Releases for past infringement of patents or copyrights or for unauthorized use of technical data or computer software.
