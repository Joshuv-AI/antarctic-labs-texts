5 C.F.R. § 576.201 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:5:576.201
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-576.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 576 (Voluntary Separation Incentive Payments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

‘Employment’ means employment with the Government of the United States, including employment under a personal services contract (or other direct contract) with the United States Government (other than an entity in the legislative branch) unless employed pursuant to § 576.203(a).
