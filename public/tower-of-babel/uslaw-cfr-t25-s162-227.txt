25 C.F.R. § 162.227 — What form of rent payment can be accepted under an agricultural lease?
Citation: us:cfr:25:162.227
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-162.227
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 162 (Leases And Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) When rent payments are made directly to the Indian landowners, the form of payment must be acceptable to the Indian landowners.
(b) Payments made to us may be delivered in person or by mail. We will not accept cash, foreign currency, or third-party checks. We will accept:
(1) Personal or business checks drawn on the account of the tenant;
(2) Money orders;
(3) Cashier's checks;
(4) Certified checks; or
(5) Electronic funds transfer payments.
