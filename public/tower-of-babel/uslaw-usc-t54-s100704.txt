54 U.S.C. § 100704 — Inventory and monitoring program
Citation: us:usc:54:100704
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section100704
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 1007 (Resource Management); Subchapter I (System Resource Inventory And Management). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall undertake a program of inventory and monitoring of System resources to establish baseline information and to provide information on the long-term trends in the condition of System resources. The monitoring program shall be developed in cooperation with other Federal monitoring and information collection efforts to ensure a cost-effective approach.
