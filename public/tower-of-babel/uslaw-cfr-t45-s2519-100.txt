45 C.F.R. § 2519.100 — What is the purpose of the Higher Education programs?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2519.100
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2519.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2519 (Higher Education Innovative Programs For Community Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of the higher education innovative programs for community service is to expand participation in community service by supporting high-quality, sustainable community service programs carried out through institutions of higher education, acting as civic institutions helping to meet the educational, public safety, human, and environmental needs of the communities in which the programs operate.
