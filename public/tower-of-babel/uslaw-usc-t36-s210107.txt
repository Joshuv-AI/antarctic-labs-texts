36 U.S.C. § 210107 — Nonapplication of audit requirements
Citation: us:usc:36:210107
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section210107
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 2101 (Theodore Roosevelt Association). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The audit requirements of section 10101 of this title do not apply to the corporation.
