40 C.F.R. § 63.9480 — What is the purpose of this subpart?
Citation: us:cfr:40:63.9480
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-63.9480
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 63 (National Emission Standards For Hazardous Air Pollutants For Source Categories). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart establishes national emission standards for hazardous air pollutants (NESHAP) for friction materials manufacturing facilities that use a solvent-based process. This subpart also establishes requirements to demonstrate initial and continuous compliance with all applicable emission limitations in this subpart.
