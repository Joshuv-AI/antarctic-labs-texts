10 U.S.C. § 2169 — School of Nursing: establishment
Citation: us:usc:10:2169
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2169
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training And Education); Chapter 108 (Department Of Defense Schools). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Establishment Authorized.—
The Secretary of Defense may establish a School of Nursing.
(b) Degree Granting Authority.—
The School of Nursing may include a program that awards a bachelor of science in nursing.
(c) Phased Development.—
The Secretary of Defense may develop the School of Nursing in phases as determined appropriate by the Secretary.
