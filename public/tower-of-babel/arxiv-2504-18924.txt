Slow ferromagnetic fluctuations in the kagome metal Sc 3 Mn 3 Al 7 Si 5 revealed by 27 Al NMR
Qing-Ping Ding, Charles Taylor, Yongbin Lee, Charuni Dissanayake, Vireshwar Mishra, Dang Khoa Le, Manh-Huong Phan, Yasuyuki Nakajima, Yuji Furukawa

Abstract
Static and dynamical magnetic and electronic properties of the kagome metal Sc 3 Mn 3 Al 7 Si 5 have been investigated by 27 Al nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR) measurements. The temperature dependence of Knight shift ( K K ) shows a similar temperature dependence of the DC magnetic susceptibility χ \chi except for the low-temperature region below ∼ \sim 50 K where K K is almost constant while χ \chi keeps increasing, which suggests that the increase in χ \chi at low temperatures is not intrinsic. 27 Al spin-lattice relaxation rate divided by temperature ( 1 / T 1 ​ T 1/T_{1}T ) is found to be constant, confirming the metallic state of Sc 3 Mn 3 Al 7 Si 5 from a microscopic point of view. Based on a Korringa ratio analysis using the T 1 T_{1} and K K data, ferromagnetic spin fluctuations are found to dominate in Sc 3 Mn 3 Al 7 Si 5 . These fluctuations are suggested to be very slow with frequencies on the order of kilohertz or lower.
