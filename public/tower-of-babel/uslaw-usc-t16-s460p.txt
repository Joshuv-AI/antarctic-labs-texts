16 U.S.C. § 460p — Establishment
Citation: us:usc:16:460p
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section460p
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LXXIV (Spruce Knob-Seneca Rocks National Recreation Area). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In order to provide for the public outdoor recreation use and enjoyment thereof by the people of the United States, the Secretary of Agriculture shall establish the Spruce Knob-Seneca Rocks National Recreation Area in the State of West Virginia.
