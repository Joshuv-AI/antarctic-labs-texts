15 C.F.R. § 280.325 — Cumulative listing of recordal information.
Citation: us:cfr:15:280.325
Creator: National Institute Of Standards And Technology, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-280.325
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 280 (Fastener Quality). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director, USPTO, shall maintain a record of the names, current addresses, and legal entities of all recorded manufacturers and their recorded insignia.
