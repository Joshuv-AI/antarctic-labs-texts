28 C.F.R. § 42.735 — Judicial review.
Citation: us:cfr:28:42.735
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-42.735
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 42 (Nondiscrimination; Equal Employment Opportunity; Policies And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A final decision of the Department in an administrative proceeding pursuant to § 42.733(b)(1)(i)(A) is subject to judicial review as provided in section 306 of the Act, 42 U.S.C. 6105.
