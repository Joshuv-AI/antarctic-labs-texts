41 C.F.R. § 60-30.31 — -30.31 Expedited hearings—when appropriate.
Citation: us:cfr:41:60-30.31
Creator: Chapter 60—Office Of Federal Contract Compliance Programs, Equal Employment Opportunity, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-60-30.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 60-30 (30—Rules Of Practice For Administrative Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Expedited hearings may be used, inter alia, when a contractor or subcontractor has violated a conciliation agreement; has not adopted and implemented an acceptable affirmative action program; has refused to give access to or to supply records or other information as required by the equal opportunity clause; or has refused to allow an on-site compliance review to be conducted.
