46 C.F.R. § 13.106 — Requirement to hold an MMC.
Citation: us:cfr:46:13.106
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-13.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 13 (Certification Of Tank Vessel Personnel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An applicant for any endorsement in this part must also meet the requirements for the MMC on which the endorsement would appear. These requirements are set out in part 10 of this subchapter.
