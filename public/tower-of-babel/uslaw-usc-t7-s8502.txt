7 U.S.C. § 8502 — Sense of Congress regarding need for improved and better coordinated Federal policy for brown tree snake introduction, control, and eradication
Citation: us:usc:7:8502
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section8502
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 111 (Brown Tree Snake Control And Eradication). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the sense of Congress that there exists a need for improved and better coordinated control, interdiction, research, and eradication of the brown tree snake on the part of the United States and other interested parties.
