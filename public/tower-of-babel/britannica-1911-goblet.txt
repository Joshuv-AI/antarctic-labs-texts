GOBLET, a large type of drinking-vessel, particularly one
shaped like a cup, without handles, and mounted on a shank
with a foot. The word is derived from the O. Fr. gobelet, diminutive
of gobel, gobeau, which Skeat takes to be formed from Low
Lat. cupellus, cup, diminutive of cupa, tub, cask (see Drinking-Vessels Drinking Vessels).
