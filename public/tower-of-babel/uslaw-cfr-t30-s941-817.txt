30 C.F.R. § 941.817 — Performance standards—underground mining activities.
Citation: us:cfr:30:941.817
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-941.817
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 941 (South Dakota). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Part 817 of this chapter, Permanent Program Performance Standards—Underground Mining Activities, shall apply to any person who conducts underground mining operations.
