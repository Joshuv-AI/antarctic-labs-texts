18 U.S.C. § 1863 — Trespass on national forest lands
Citation: us:usc:18:1863
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1863
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 91 (Public Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever, without lawful authority or permission, goes upon any national-forest land while it is closed to the public pursuant to lawful regulation of the Secretary of Agriculture, shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than six months, or both.
