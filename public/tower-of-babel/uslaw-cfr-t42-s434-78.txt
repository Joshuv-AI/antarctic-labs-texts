42 C.F.R. § 434.78 — Right to reconsideration of disallowance.
Citation: us:cfr:42:434.78
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-434.78
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 434 (Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Medicaid agency dissatisfied with a disallowance of FFP under this subpart may request and will be granted reconsideration in accordance with 45 CFR part 16.
