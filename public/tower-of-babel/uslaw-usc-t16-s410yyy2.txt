16 U.S.C. § 410yyy–2 — Administration, protection, and development
Citation: us:usc:16:410yyy–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section410yyy–2
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LIX–WW (Ocmulgee Mounds National Historical Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The administration, protection, and development of the Ocmulgee National Monument 1 shall be under the supervision of the Secretary of the Interior subject to the provisions of the Act entitled “An Act to establish a National Park Service, and for other purposes”, approved August 25, 1916,2 as amended.
