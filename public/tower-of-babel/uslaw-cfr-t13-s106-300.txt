13 C.F.R. § 106.300 — Fee Based SBA-Sponsored Activity.
Citation: us:cfr:13:106.300
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-106.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 106 (Cosponsorships, Fee And Non-Fee Based Sba-Sponsored Activities And Gifts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator (or designee), after consultation with the General Counsel (or designee), may provide assistance for the benefit of small business through Fee-Based SBA-Sponsored Activities pursuant to section 4(h) of the Small Business Act.
