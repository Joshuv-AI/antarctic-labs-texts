7 C.F.R. § 765.451 — Continuation of FLP debt and transfer of security.
Citation: us:cfr:7:765.451
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-765.451
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 765 (Direct Loan Servicing—Regular). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Individuals who are liable. Following the death of a borrower, the Agency will continue the loan with any individual who is liable for the indebtedness provided that the individual complies with the obligations of the loan and security instruments.
(b) Individuals who are not liable. The Agency will continue the loan with a person who is not liable for the indebtedness in accordance with subpart I of this part.
