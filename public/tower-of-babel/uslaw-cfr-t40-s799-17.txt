40 C.F.R. § 799.17 — Effects of non-compliance.
Citation: us:cfr:40:799.17
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-799.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 799 (Identification Of Specific Chemical Substance And Mixture Testing Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person who fails or refuses to comply with any aspect of this part or part 790 is in violation of section 15 of TSCA. EPA will treat violations of Good Laboratory Practice Standards as indicated in § 792.17 of this chapter.
