Study of Nucleon and Δ \Delta resonances from a Systematic Analysis of K ∗ ​ Σ K^{\ast}\Sigma Photoproduction
Jun Shi, Bing-Song Zou

Abstract
A systematic analysis of the K ∗ ​ Σ K^{\ast}\Sigma photoproduction off proton is performed with all the available differential cross section data. We carry out a strategy different from the previous studies of these reactions, that instead of fixing the parameters of the added resonances from PDG or pentaquark models, we add the resonance with a specific J P J^{P} and leave its parameters to be determined from the experimental data. When adding only one resonance, the best result is to add one N ⁡ ( 3 / 2 − ) N(3/2^{-}) resonance around 2097 2097 MeV, which substantially reduce the χ 2 \chi^{2} per degree of freedom to 1.35 1.35 . There are two best solutions when adding two resonances, one is to add one N ∗ ​ ( 3 / 2 − ) N^{\ast}(3/2^{-}) and one N ∗ ​ ( 7 / 2 − ) N^{\ast}(7/2^{-}) , the other is to add one N ∗ ​ ( 3 / 2 − ) N^{\ast}(3/2^{-}) and one Δ ∗ ​ ( 7 / 2 − ) \Delta^{\ast}(7/2^{-}) , leading the χ 2 \chi^{2} per degree of freedom to be 1.10 1.10 and 1.09 1.09 , respectively. The mass values of the N ∗ ​ ( 3 / 2 − ) N^{\ast}(3/2^{-}) in the two-resonance solutions are both near 2070 2070 MeV. Our solutions indicate that the N ⁡ ( 3 / 2 − ) N(3/2^{-}) resonance around 2080 2080 MeV is strongly coupled with the K ∗ ​ Σ K^{\ast}\Sigma final state and support the molecular picture of N ⁡ ( 2080 , 3 / 2 − ) N(2080,3/2^{-}) .
