39 U.S.C. § 2603 — Settlement of claims for damages caused by the Postal Service
Citation: us:usc:39:2603
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title39-section2603
Context: United States Code, Title 39 (Postal Service); Part III (Modernization And Fiscal Administration); Chapter 26 (Debts And Collection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When the Postal Service finds a claim for damage to persons or property resulting from the operation of the Postal Service to be a proper charge against the United States, and it is not cognizable under section 2672 of title 28, it may adjust and settle the claim.
