43 C.F.R. § 3179.82 — Subsequent well tests for an existing completion.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3179.82
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3179.82
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3170 (Onshore Oil And Gas Production). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

During well tests subsequent to the initial completion or recompletion, the operator may flare gas royalty free under § 3179.41(b)(3) for no more than 24 hours, unless the BLM approves or requires a longer period. The operator must submit any such request using a Sundry Notice.
