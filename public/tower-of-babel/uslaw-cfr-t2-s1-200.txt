2 C.F.R. § 1.200 — Purpose of chapters I and II.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1.200
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1 (About Title 2 Of The Code Of Federal Regulations And Subtitle A). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Chapters I and II of subtitle A provide OMB guidance to Federal agencies that helps ensure consistent and uniform government-wide policies and procedures for the management of the agencies' Federal financial assistance.
