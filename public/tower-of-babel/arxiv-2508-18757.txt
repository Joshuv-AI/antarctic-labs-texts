Structure of leptonic Yukawa couplings in the Zee model
Chuan-Hung Chen

Abstract
The radiative neutrino mass matrix m ν m^{\nu} in the Zee model depends on leptonic Yukawa couplings F F to a singlet scalar and Y ℓ Y^{\ell} to a new Higgs doublet. Leveraging the skew-symmetric structure of F F , we derive a unique identity linking F F and m ν m^{\nu} that is explicitly independent of Y ℓ Y^{\ell} . This relation implies that five entries of Y ℓ Y^{\ell} can, in principle, be determined directly from m ν m^{\nu} and F F , while the remaining four can be selected based on phenomenological assumptions. As an illustration, we apply this framework to the two-zero texture B ​ 2 B2 , highlighting its enhancement of the muon g − 2 g-2 .

II
Leptonic Yukawa couplings and radiative neutrino mass matrix

In this section, we briefly review the relevant interactions in the Zee model. To radiatively generate Majorana neutrino masses without introducing additional exotic fermions, the standard model (SM) is extended to include a second Higgs doublet,
H_{2}
, and a charged scalar singlet,
\chi^{+}
. Lepton number is explicitly violated by the trilinear term
H^{T}_{1}i\tau_{2}H_{2}\chi^{-}
in the scalar potential, where
\tau_{2}
is the second Pauli matrix. Based on the
SU(2)_{L}\times U(1)_{Y}

-{\cal L}\supset\bar{L}\left(y^{\ell}_{1}H_{1}+y^{\ell}_{2}H_{2}\right)\ell_{R}+L^{T}Ci\tau_{2}FL\chi^{+}+\mu H^{T}_{1}i\tau_{2}H_{2}\chi^{-}+H.c.\,,

(2)

where

are leptonic Yukawa coupling matrices,

is the charge-conjugation operator, and
F
is a skew-symmetric Yukawa matrix. In the Zee model,

and

must not be aligned in order to fit the observed neutrino oscillation data. Since quark couplings play no role in this context, we do not discuss them further.

The Higgs flavor basis

III
Element-wise correlations among

,

, and

The leptonic Yukawa couplings

and

Scanning the parameter space yields eigenvalues and eigenvectors of

contains more parameters than oscillation data can determine, it is convenient to work with a reduced parameter set for studying lepton-flavor phenomenology. This requires clarifying the role of

in shaping

and cLFV processes within the Zee model before imposing further assumptions.

From the symmetry property of
m^{\nu}
and
F
, we derive analytic formulations for

in terms of
f_{ij}
,
m_{\ell}
, and
m^{\nu}_{ij}
, and identify which of its entries are insensitive to neutrino oscillation parameters. These results provide a systematic basis for applications to lepton phenomenology, especially when supplemented with suitable assumptions or approximations.

III.1
The unique relation between
F
and
m^{\nu}

Before presenting the relationships of
Y^{\ell}_{ij}
in terms of
m^{\nu}_{ij}
,
m_{\ell}
, and
f_{ij}

in the Zee model. From the skew-symmetric matrix

u^{T}m^{\nu}u=-\kappa\,u^{T}(F^{T}\;m_{\ell}\;Y^{\ell}+Y^{\ell T}\;m_{\ell}\;F)u=0\,.

(7)

An analogous application of the identity can be found in models with a singlet charged scalar

f^{2}_{23}m^{\nu}_{11}+f^{2}_{13}m^{\nu}_{22}+f^{2}_{12}m^{\nu}_{33}-2f_{13}f_{23}m^{\nu}_{12}-2f_{12}f_{32}m^{\nu}_{13}-2f_{21}f_{31}m^{\nu}_{23}=0\,,

(8)

explicitly. We stress that Eq. (
7
) or Eq. (
8

This relation fixes one element of

and

Summary

We analyzed the relationships between the leptonic Yukawa matrices
F
and
Y^{\ell}
and the one-loop-induced neutrino mass matrix in the Zee model. We demonstrate a unique relation arising from the skew-symmetric nature of
F
and the neutrino mass matrix, independent of
Y^{\ell}
. As a consequence, four entries of
Y^{\ell}
cannot be determined from the neutrino mass matrix and
F
alone.

We identify
Y^{\ell}_{11}
,
Y^{\ell}_{12}
,
Y^{\ell}_{13}
, and
Y^{\ell}_{21}
as viable candidates for these undetermined entries, with the first three multiplied by
m_{e}
. In the limit
m_{e}\to 0
, these couplings become irrelevant to neutrino oscillation parameters; however, phenomenological constraints, particularly from muonium-antimuonium oscillation, require
Y^{\ell}_{12,21}
to be suppressed. In principle, all other Yukawa couplings
Y^{\ell}_{ij}
can be constrained by charged lepton-flavor violation processes. Imposing
Y^{\ell}_{11}=Y^{\ell}_{13}=0
forbids the tree-level decays
\mu\to 3e
and
\tau\to(3e,\mu e^{-}e^{+})
. Among the remaining five couplings,
Y^{\ell}_{22}
,
Y^{\ell}_{23}
,
Y^{\ell}_{31}
,
Y^{\ell}_{32}
, and
Y^{\ell}_{33}
, additional ones may be set to zero, depending on the phenomenological scenario under study. As an illustration, we apply the framework to the two-zero texture
B2
neutrino mass matrix. When fitted to neutrino oscillation data, the predicted
\Delta a_{\mu}
is close to the
1\sigma
upper value of current measurements, and the BRs for
\tau\to(e\mu^{-}\mu^{+},3\mu)
are within the reach of the projected sensitivities at Belle II.
