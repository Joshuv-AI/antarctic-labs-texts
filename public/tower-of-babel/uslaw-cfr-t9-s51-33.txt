9 C.F.R. § 51.33 — Multiple indemnity payments.
Citation: us:cfr:9:51.33
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-51.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 51 (Animals Destroyed Because Of Brucellosis). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

APHIS has indemnity programs for several other livestock diseases. However, if a claim is paid for indemnity for animals destroyed because of brucellosis, no other claims for indemnity will be paid for the same animals.
