17 C.F.R. § 141.12 — Interest, penalties, and administrative costs.
Citation: us:cfr:17:141.12
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-141.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 141 (Salary Offset). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Charges may be assessed for interest, penalties, and administrative costs in accordance with the Federal Claims Collection Standards, 4 CFR 102.13.
