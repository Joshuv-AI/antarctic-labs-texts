10 C.F.R. § 60.78 — Material control and accounting records and reports.

Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-60.78
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 60 (Disposal Of High-Level Radioactive Wastes In Geologic Repositories). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

DOE shall implement a program of material control and accounting (and accidental criticality reporting) that is the same as that specified in §§ 72.72, 72.74, 72.76, and 72.78 of this chapter.
