48 C.F.R. § 46.303 — 46.303 Cost-reimbursement supply contracts.
Citation: us:cfr:48:46.303
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-46.303
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 46 (Quality Assurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.246-3, Inspection of Supplies—Cost-Reimbursement, in solicitations and contracts for supplies, or services that involve the furnishing of supplies, when a cost-reimbursement contract is contemplated.
