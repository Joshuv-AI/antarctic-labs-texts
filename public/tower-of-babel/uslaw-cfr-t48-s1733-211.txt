48 C.F.R. § 1733.211 — 1733.211 Contracting officer's decision.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1733.211
Creator: Chapter 17—Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1733.211
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1733 (Protests, Disputes, And Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The written decision required by FAR 33.211(a)(4) shall include, in the paragraph listed under FAR 33.211(a)(4)(v), specific reference to the Interior Board of Contract Appeals, 801 North Quincy Street, Arlington, VA 22203, and its procedures under 43 CFR part 4. The IBCA optional small claims (expedited) procedures and accelerated procedures under 43 CFR 4.113 shall also be referenced as required by the FAR.
