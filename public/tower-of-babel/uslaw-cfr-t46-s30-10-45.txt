46 C.F.R. § 30.10-45 — -45 Ocean—TB/O.
Citation: us:cfr:46:30.10-45
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-30.10-45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 30 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Under this designation shall be included all tank vessels normally navigating the waters of any ocean or the Gulf of America more than 20 nautical miles offshore.
