BWTP model for hadrons p T p_{\rm T} -spectra and elliptic flow v 2 v_{2} in AA collisions at the LHC
Smbat Grigoryan

Abstract
A generalization of the phenomenological BWTP model, recently proposed by the author for the hadrons transverse momentum ( p T p_{\textrm{T}} ) spectra measured at the LHC, is developed to also describe the hadrons p T p_{\textrm{T}} -differential elliptic flow coefficients v 2 v_{2} in identical nuclei (AA) collisions of different centralities. The model describes well the available data on p T p_{\textrm{T}} -spectra and v 2 v_{2} for any p T p_{\textrm{T}} of various particles, from pions to charmonia, in Pb–Pb at s NN = \sqrt{s_{\mathrm{NN}}}= 2.76 and 5.02 TeV, and in Xe–Xe at s NN = \sqrt{s_{\mathrm{NN}}}= 5.44 TeV. Also, predictions for OO collisions at s NN = \sqrt{s_{\mathrm{NN}}}= 5.36 TeV are given. While the model is mainly targeting the LHC energies, it also works at much lower RHIC energies.

VII
Conclusions

Thus, a more general and parameterized version of BWTPM
G21
is presented, which has much fewer free parameters, but successfully describes not only the midrapidity
p_{\textrm{T}}
-spectra, but also elliptic flow
v_{2}
of various hadrons with arbitrary
p_{\textrm{T}}
in AA collisions for any centrality at the LHC energies. It is shown that the model also works at much lower RHIC energies. For all considered particles, from pions to charmonia, BWTPM allows us to calculate easily the midrapidity
p_{\textrm{T}}
-spectra and
v_{2}
for new AA collisions with any A and centrality at high energies. Predictions are given for the OO collisions carried out recently at the LHC. To calculate the
p_{\textrm{T}}
-spectra or
v_{2}
for new particles, not considered here, one needs some data (e.g.,
p_{\textrm{T}}
-spectrum for one centrality or
\langle p_{\textrm{T}}\rangle
versus centrality) to fix at least two particle-type dependent parameters
f_{BW}
and
f_{2}
.

BWTPM can be efficiently used by experimenters to quickly check new data on hadron
p_{\textrm{T}}
-spectra and
v_{2}
, and by theorists to test their more general and sophisticated theories and models of hadron production in AA collisions. Similarly, the simple parameterization for the charged-particle multiplicity density at midrapidity in AA or
pp
collisions presented in Sec.
III
can also be used.
