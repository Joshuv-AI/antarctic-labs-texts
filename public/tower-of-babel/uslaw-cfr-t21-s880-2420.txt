21 C.F.R. § 880.2420 — Electronic monitor for gravity flow infusion systems.
Citation: us:cfr:21:880.2420
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-880.2420
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 880 (General Hospital And Personal Use Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. An electronic monitor for gravity flow infusion systems is a device used to monitor the amount of fluid being infused into a patient. The device consists of an electronic transducer and equipment for signal amplification, conditioning, and display.
(b) Classification. Class II (performance standards).
