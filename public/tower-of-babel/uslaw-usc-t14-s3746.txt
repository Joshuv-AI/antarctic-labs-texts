14 U.S.C. § 3746 — Promotion; acceptance; oath of office
Citation: us:usc:14:3746
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section3746
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 37 (Coast Guard Reserve); Subchapter II (Personnel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
A Reserve officer who has been appointed under this subchapter is considered to have accepted the appointment unless delivery thereof cannot be effected.
(b)
A Reserve officer who has served continuously since taking the oath of office prescribed in section 3331 of title 5, is not required to take a new oath of office upon appointment in a higher grade.
