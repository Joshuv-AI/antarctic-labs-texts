42 U.S.C. § 285d–2 — Research and training
Citation: us:usc:42:285d–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section285d–2
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter III (National Research Institutes); Part C (Specific Provisions Respecting National Research Institutes); Subpart 4 (National Institute Of Arthritis And Musculoskeletal And Skin Diseases). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of the Institute shall—
(1)
carry out programs of support for research and training (other than training for which Ruth L. Kirschstein National Research Service Awards may be made under section 288 of this title) in the diagnosis, prevention, and treatment of arthritis and musculoskeletal and skin diseases, including support for training in medical schools, graduate clinical training, graduate training in epidemiology, epidemiology studies, clinical trials, and interdisciplinary research programs; and
(2)
establish programs of evaluation, planning, and dissemination of knowledge related to such research and training.
