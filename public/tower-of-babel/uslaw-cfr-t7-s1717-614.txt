7 C.F.R. § 1717.614 — Notification of rate changes.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1717.614
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1717.614
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1717 (Post-Loan Policies And Procedures Common To Insured And Guaranteed Electric Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a distribution borrower is required by its loan documents to notify RUS in writing of proposed changes in electric rates more than 30 days prior to the effective date of such rates, the required notification period shall be 30 days. Moreover, such notification shall be required only upon the request of RUS.
