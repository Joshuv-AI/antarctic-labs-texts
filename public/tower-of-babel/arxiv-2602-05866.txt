Remarks on relative categoricity
Anand Pillay

Abstract
We make some elementary observations about relative categoricity and the Gaifman property. Precise definitions will be given below, but roughly speaking T T will be a complete theory in a countable language L L with a distinguished unary predicate P P . T T is relatively categorical if any isomorphism between the P P -parts of two models of T T lifts to an isomorphism between the two models. It was conjectured that if T T is relatively categorical then any model of T P T^{P} (the relativization of T T to P P ) is the P P -part of a model of T T (the Gaifman property). This remains open. Restricting the definition of relative categoricity to countable models of T T , gives the notion of relative ( ω , ω ) (\omega,\omega) -categoricity. And restricting to models whose P P -part is countable gives the notion of relative ω \omega -categoricity. We observe that if T T is relatively ( ω , ω ) (\omega,\omega) -categorical then any model of T P T^{P} of cardinality ≤ ℵ 1 \leq\aleph_{1} is the P P -part of a model of T T . If, in addition, every model of T T is “almost internal” (or “ 1 1 -coanalyzable”) in the P P -part, we show that T T is relatively categorical and has the Gaifman property.
