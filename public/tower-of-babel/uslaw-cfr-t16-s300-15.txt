16 C.F.R. § 300.15 — Labeling of containers or packaging of wool products.
Citation: us:cfr:16:300.15
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-300.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 300 (Rules And Regulations Under The Wool Products Labeling Act Of 1939). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When wool products are marketed and delivered in a package which is intended to remain unbroken and intact until after delivery to the ultimate consumer, each wool product in the package, except hosiery, and the package shall be labeled with the required information. If the package is transparent to the extent it allows for a clear reading of the required information on the wool product, the package is not required to be labeled.
