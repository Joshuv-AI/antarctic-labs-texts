18 C.F.R. § 35.40 — Ancillary services.
Citation: us:cfr:18:35.40
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-35.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 35 (Filing Of Rate Schedules And Tariffs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Seller may make sales of ancillary services at market-based rates only if it has been authorized by the Commission and only in specific geographic markets as the Commission has authorized.
