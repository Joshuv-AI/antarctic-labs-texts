34 U.S.C. § 10612 — Prohibition of participation by violent offenders
Citation: us:usc:34:10612
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section10612
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 101 (Justice System Improvement); Subchapter XXX (Drug Courts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General shall—
(1)
issue regulations or guidelines to ensure that the programs authorized in this subchapter do not permit participation by violent offenders; and
(2)
immediately suspend funding for any grant under this subchapter, pending compliance, if the Attorney General finds that violent offenders are participating in any program funded under this subchapter.
