10 C.F.R. § 719.35 — When must the contractor provide a copy of an executed settlement agreement?
Citation: us:cfr:10:719.35
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-719.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 719 (Contractor Legal Management Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A contractor must provide a copy of an executed settlement agreement within seven (7) days of execution.
