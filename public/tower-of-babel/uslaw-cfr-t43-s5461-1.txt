43 C.F.R. § 5461.1 — Payment in advance of cutting or removal.
Citation: us:cfr:43:5461.1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-5461.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 5460 (Sales Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in §§ 5451.2 and 5451.4 no part of any timber or other vegetative resources sold may be cut or removed unless advance payment has been made as provided in the contract.
