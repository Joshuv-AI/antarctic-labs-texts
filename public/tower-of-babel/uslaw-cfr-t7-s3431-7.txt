7 C.F.R. § 3431.7 — Notification and use of designated veterinarian shortage situations.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3431.7
Creator: National Institute Of Food And Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3431.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3431 (Veterinary Medicine Loan Repayment Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary will publish the designated veterinarian shortage situations on the Agency's Web site and will use the designated veterinarian shortage situations to solicit VMLRP loan repayment applications from individual veterinarians in accordance with subpart B of this part.
