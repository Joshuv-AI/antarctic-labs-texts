49 U.S.C. § 32501 — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:49:32501
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section32501
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part C (Information, Standards, And Requirements); Chapter 325 (Bumper Standards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of this chapter is to reduce economic loss resulting from damage to passenger motor vehicles involved in motor vehicle accidents by providing for the maintenance and enforcement of bumper standards.
