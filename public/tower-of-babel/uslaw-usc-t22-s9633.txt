22 U.S.C. § 9633 — Maximum contingent liability
Citation: us:usc:22:9633
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section9633
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 103 (Better Utilization Of Investments Leading To Development); Subchapter III (Administrative And General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The maximum contingent liability of the Corporation outstanding at any one time shall not exceed in the aggregate $205,000,000,000.
(b) Rule of construction
The maximum contingent liability shall apply to all extension of liability by the Corporation regardless of the authority cited thereto.
