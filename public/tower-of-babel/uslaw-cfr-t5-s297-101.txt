5 C.F.R. § 297.101 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:5:297.101
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-297.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 297 (Privacy Procedures For Personnel Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth the regulations of the U.S. Office of Personnel Management (the Office) to govern the maintenance, protection, disclosure, and amendment of records within the systems of records as defned by the Privacy Act of 1974 (5 U.S.C. 552a), Public Law 93-579.
