42 C.F.R. § 431.711 — Compliance with standards.
Citation: us:cfr:42:431.711
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-431.711
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 431 (State Organization And General Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agency or board must establish and carry out procedures to insure that licensed administrators comply with the standards in this subpart when they serve as nursing home administrators.
