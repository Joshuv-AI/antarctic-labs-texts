26 U.S.C. § 4423 — Inspection of books
Citation: us:usc:26:4423
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section4423
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 35 (Taxes On Wagering); Subchapter C (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding section 7605(b), the books of account of any person liable for tax under this chapter may be examined and inspected as frequently as may be needful to the enforcement of this chapter.
