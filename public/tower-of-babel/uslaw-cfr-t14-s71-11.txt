14 C.F.R. § 71.11 — Air Traffic Service (ATS) routes.

Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 71 (Designation Of Class A, B, C, D, And E Airspace Areas; Air Traffic Service Routes; And Reporting Points). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless otherwise specified, the following apply:
(a) An Air Traffic Service (ATS) route is based on a centerline that extends from one navigation aid, fix, or intersection, to another navigation aid, fix, or intersection (or through several navigation aids, fixes, or intersections) specified for that route.
(b) An ATS route does not include the airspace of a prohibited area.
