2 C.F.R. § 3001.500 — Who in DHS determines that a recipient other than an individual violated the requirements of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:2:3001.500
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-3001.500
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 3001 (Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary of Homeland Security, or his or her official designee, will make the determination that a recipient other than an individual violated the requirements of this part.
