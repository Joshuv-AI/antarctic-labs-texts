7 C.F.R. § 760.20 — Payments to manufacturers of dairy products.
Citation: us:cfr:7:760.20
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-760.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 760 (Indemnity Payment Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An indemnity payment may be made to the affected manufacturer who is determined by the Deputy Administrator to be in compliance with all the terms and conditions of this subpart in the amount of the fair market value of the product removed from the commercial market because of pesticide residues, less any amount the manufacturer receives for the product in the form of salvage.
