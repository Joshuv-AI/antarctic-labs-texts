12 C.F.R. § 352.4 — Nondiscrimination in any program or activity conducted by the FDIC.
Citation: us:cfr:12:352.4
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-352.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 352 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Disability). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In accordance with section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act, no qualified individual with a disability shall, solely by reason of his or her disability, be excluded from participation in, be denied the benefits of, or be subjected to discrimination in any program or activity conducted by the FDIC.
