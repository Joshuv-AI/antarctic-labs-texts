25 C.F.R. § 103.41 — What happens if a lender violates provisions of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:25:103.41
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-103.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 103 (Loan Guaranty, Insurance, And Interest Subsidy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to reducing or eliminating payment on a specific claim for loss, BIA may either temporarily suspend, or permanently bar, a lender from making or acquiring loans under the Program if the lender repeatedly fails to abide by the requirements of this part, or if the lender significantly violates the requirements of this part on any single occasion.
