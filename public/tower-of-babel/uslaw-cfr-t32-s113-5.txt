32 C.F.R. § 113.5 — Responsibilities.
Citation: us:cfr:32:113.5
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-113.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 113 (Indebtedness Procedures Of Military Personnel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Under Secretary of Defense for Personnel and Readiness shall monitor compliance with this part.
(b) The Under Secretary of Defense (Comptroller) shall ensure Defense Finance and Accounting Service (DFAS) implementation of this part.
(c) The Heads of the DoD Components shall ensure compliance with this part.
