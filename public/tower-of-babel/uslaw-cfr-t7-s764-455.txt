7 C.F.R. § 764.455 — Potential training vendors.
Citation: us:cfr:7:764.455
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-764.455
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 764 (Direct Loan Making). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Agency will contract for training services with State or private providers of financial management training services.
