12 C.F.R. § 712.6 — What activities and services are prohibited for CUSOs?
Citation: us:cfr:12:712.6
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-712.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 712 (CREDIT UNION SERVICE ORGANIZATIONS (CUSOs)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

General. CUSOs must not acquire control of, either directly or indirectly, another depository financial institution, nor invest in shares, stocks, or obligations of an insurance company, trade association, liquidity facility or similar organization, corporation, or association.
