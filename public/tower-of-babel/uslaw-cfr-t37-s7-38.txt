37 C.F.R. § 7.38 — Notice to holder of extension of protection.
Citation: us:cfr:37:7.38
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-7.38
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 7 (Rules Of Practice In Filings Pursuant To The Protocol Relating To The Madrid Agreement Concerning The International Registration Of Marks). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The registration certificate for an extension of protection to the United States includes a notice of the requirement for filing the affidavit or declaration of use or excusable nonuse under section 71 of the Act. However, the affidavit or declaration must be filed within the time period required by section 71 of the Act regardless of whether this notice is received.
