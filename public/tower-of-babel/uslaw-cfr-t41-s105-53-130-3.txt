41 C.F.R. § 105-53.130-3 — -53.130-3 Office of the Executive Secretariat.

Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-53 (53—Statement Of Organization And Functions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Office of the Executive Secretariat, headed by the Director of the Executive Secretariat, is responsible for policy coordination, correspondence control, and various administrative tasks in support of the Administrator and Deputy Administrator.
