41 C.F.R. § 105-9.110 — -9.110 Special benefits for children and the elderly.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-9.110
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-9.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-9 (9—Discrimination Prohibited On The Basis Of Age). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a recipient's program or activity provides special benefits to the elderly or to children, such use of age distinctions is presumed to be necessary to the normal operation of the program or activity.
