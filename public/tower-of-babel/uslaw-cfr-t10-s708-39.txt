10 C.F.R. § 708.39 — The Contract Disputes Act.
Citation: us:cfr:10:708.39
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-708.39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 708 (Doe Contractor Employee Protection Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A final agency decision and order issued pursuant to this regulation is not considered a claim by the government against a contractor or “a decision by the contracting officer” under sections 6 and 7 of the Contract Disputes Act (41 U.S.C. 605 and 41 U.S.C. 606).
