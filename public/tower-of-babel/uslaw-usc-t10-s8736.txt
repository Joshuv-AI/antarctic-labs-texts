10 U.S.C. § 8736 — Regulations
Citation: us:usc:10:8736
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8736
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 869 (Naval Petroleum Reserves). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Secretary may prescribe regulations and take any proper action to accomplish the purposes of this chapter.
(b)
All statements, reports, and representations required by the regulations shall be under oath, unless otherwise specified, and in such form as the Secretary requires.
