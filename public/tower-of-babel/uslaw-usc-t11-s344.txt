11 U.S.C. § 344 — Self-incrimination; immunity
Citation: us:usc:11:344
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section344
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 3 (Case Administration); Subchapter III (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Immunity for persons required to submit to examination, to testify, or to provide information in a case under this title may be granted under part V of title 18.
