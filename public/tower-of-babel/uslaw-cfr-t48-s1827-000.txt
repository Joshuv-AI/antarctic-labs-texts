48 C.F.R. § 1827.000 — 1827.000 Scope of part.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1827.000
Creator: Chapter 18—National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1827.000
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1827 (Patents, Data, And Copyrights). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes NASA policies, procedures, and contract clauses pertaining to patents, data, and copyrights. The provisions of FAR Part 27 apply to NASA acquisitions unless specifically excepted in this part.
