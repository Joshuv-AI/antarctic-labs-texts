29 C.F.R. § 17.13 — May the Secretary waive any provision of these regulations?
Citation: us:cfr:29:17.13
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-17.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 17 (Intergovernmental Review Of Department Of Labor Programs And Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In an emergency, the Secretary may waive any provision of these regulations.
