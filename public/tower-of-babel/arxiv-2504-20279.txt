Strong Gelfand Pairs of the symplectic group 𝐒𝐩 4 ​ ( q CLOSE \boldsymbol{\rm Sp}_{4}(q ) where q q is even
Stephen P. Humphries, Joseph E. Marrow

Abstract
. A strong Gelfand pair ( G , H ) (G,H) is a finite group G G together with a subgroup H H such that every irreducible character of H H induces to a multiplicity-free character of G G . We classify the strong Gelfand pairs of the symplectic groups Sp 4 ​ ( q ) {\rm Sp}_{4}(q) for even q q . Keywords : Strong Gelfand pair, symplectic group, irreducible character, multiplicity one subgroup.

1.
Introduction

For a finite group
G
we let
\hat{G}
denote the set of irreducible characters of
G
. Then a
multiplicity-free character of
G
is a character
\chi
of
G
such that for
\psi\in\hat{G}
, we have
\langle\chi,\psi\rangle\leq 1
. Here only complex characters are considered.

A
Gelfand pair

(G,H)
is a finite group
G
together with a subgroup
H
such that the trivial character of
H
induces a multiplicity-free character of

G
. The importance of Gelfand pairs is indicated by six equivalent conditions as given in
[
3
,
4
]
and
[
1
]
,
[
18
]
.

A
strong Gelfand pair

(G,H)
is a finite group
G
and a subgroup
H\leq G
such that for every
\psi\in\hat{H}
the induced character
\psi\uparrow G
is multiplicity free.
We will call
H
a
strong Gelfand subgroup of
G
in this situation.
Equivalently,
(G,H)
is a strong Gelfand pair if and only if the Schur ring determined by the
H
-classes

g^{H}=\{g^{h}:h\in H\},g\in G
, is commutative
[
4
,
7
,
13
]
. Here our convention is:
g^{h}=h^{-1}gh
. Note that
(G,G)
is always a strong Gelfand pair.

In this paper we continue our investigation of strong Gelfand pairs of groups that are close to being simple; in
[
3
,
10
,
1
]
we found all such pairs for

G={\rm SL}(2,p^{n}),n\geq 1,

p
a prime, and the symmetric groups. We refer to
[
3
,
10
,
4
]
for necessary background.

This paper will consider strong Gelfand pairs for the symplectic groups Sp
{}_{4}(2^{n})
as their irreducible characters are known
[
9
]
. In contrast, the irreducible characters of
{\rm Sp}_{2k}(q),k>2,
are not understood, where
q
is a prime power. The groups Sp
{}_{4}(2^{n}),n>1,
are simple and the representation theory of these groups is considered in
[
6
]
.
The main result of this paper is to determine the strong Gelfand pairs of
{\rm Sp}_{4}(2^{n})
:

Theorem 1.1
.

The only strong Gelfand pair
(Sp
{}_{4}(2^{n}),H),n\geq 2,
is where
H={\rm Sp}_{4}(2^{n})
