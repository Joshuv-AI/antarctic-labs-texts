43 U.S.C. § 971b — Sale of restored Alaskan lands; preference rights; consent of Federal agency
Citation: us:usc:43:971b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section971b
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 22 (Rights-Of-Way And Other Easements In Public Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may sell such restored lands for not less than their appraised value, giving an appropriate preference right to the holders of adjoining claims or entries and to owners of adjoining private lands. If such lands are under the jurisdiction of a Federal department or agency other than the Department of the Interior, any sale thereof shall be made only with the consent of such department or agency.
