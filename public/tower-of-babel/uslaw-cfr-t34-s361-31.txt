34 C.F.R. § 361.31 — Cooperative agreements with private nonprofit organizations.
Citation: us:cfr:34:361.31
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-361.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 361 (State Vocational Rehabilitation Services Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The vocational rehabilitation services portion of the Unified or Combined State Plan must describe the manner in which cooperative agreements with private nonprofit vocational rehabilitation service providers will be established.
