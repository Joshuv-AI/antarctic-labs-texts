21 C.F.R. § 882.1420 — Electroencephalogram (EEG) signal spectrum analyzer.
Citation: us:cfr:21:882.1420
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-882.1420
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 882 (Neurological Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. An electroencephalogram (EEG) signal spectrum analyzer is a device used to display the frequency content or power spectral density of the electroencephalogram (EEG) signal.
(b) Classification. Class I (general controls).
