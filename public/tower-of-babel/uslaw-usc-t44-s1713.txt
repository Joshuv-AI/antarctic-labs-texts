44 U.S.C. § 1713 — Documents to be delivered to the Executive Mansion
Citation: us:usc:44:1713
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section1713
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 17 (Distribution And Sale Of Public Documents). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of the Government Publishing Office shall deliver to the Executive Mansion two copies of each document, bill, and resolution as soon as printed and ready for distribution.
