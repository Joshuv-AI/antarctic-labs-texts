25 C.F.R. § 169.303 — What happens if BIA denies a right-of-way document?
Citation: us:cfr:25:169.303
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-169.303
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 169 (Rights-Of-Way Over Indian Land). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If we deny the right-of-way grant, renewal, amendment, assignment, or mortgage, we will notify the parties immediately and advise the landowners and the applicant of their right to appeal the decision under part 2 of this chapter.
