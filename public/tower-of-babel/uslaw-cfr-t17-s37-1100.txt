17 C.F.R. § 37.1100 — Core Principle 11—Antitrust considerations.
Citation: us:cfr:17:37.1100
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-37.1100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 37 (Swap Execution Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless necessary or appropriate to achieve the purposes of the Act, the swap execution facility shall not:
(a) Adopt any rules or take any actions that result in any unreasonable restraint of trade; or
(b) Impose any material anticompetitive burden on trading or clearing.
