Cohomologous symplectic forms with different Gromov widths
Shengzhen Ning

Abstract
We study McDuff-Salamon’s Problem 46 in [ MS17 ] by showing that there exist closed manifolds of dimension ≥ 6 \geq 6 admitting cohomologous symplectic forms with different Gromov widths. The examples are motivated by Ruan’s early example ( [ Rua94 ] ) of deformation inequivalent symplectic forms in dimension 6 6 distinguished by Gromov-Witten invariants. To find cohomologous symplectic forms and compare their Gromov width, we make use of Li-Liu’s theorem of symplectic cone for manifolds with b 2 + = 1 b_{2}^{+}=1 and Biran’s ball packing theorem in dimension 4 4 . Along the way, we also show that these cohomologous symplectic forms can have distinct first Chern classes, which answers another question by Salamon in [ Sal13 ] .

The Gromov width of a

Problem 1.1

Is there a closed manifold

Another problem related to cohomologous symplectic forms on a fixed closed manifold, raised in

\tilde{\phi}([\omega^{\prime}])(E_{i})=\tilde{\phi}([\omega^{\prime}])\cdot\tilde{\phi}(\text{PD}(E_{i}^{\prime}))=[\omega^{\prime}]\cdot\text{PD}(E_{i}^{\prime})=\omega^{\prime}(E_{i}^{\prime})>0.

Hence, the discussion after Theorem
2.2
implies
\tilde{\phi}([\omega^{\prime}])\in\mathcal{C}_{K_{+}}\cup\mathcal{C}_{K_{-}}
so that there will be some symplectic form
\omega_{X}
(different from the initial
\omega
) on
X
in this class.

Now, let us choose a symplectic form
\omega_{S^{2}}
on
S^{2}
with sufficiently large area and compare the Gromov widths of the cohomologous symplectic forms
\omega_{X}\oplus\omega_{S^{2}}
with
\tilde{f}^{*}(\omega^{\prime}\oplus\omega_{S^{2}})
. By Corollary
2.4
,

w_{G}(X,\omega_{X})=\sqrt{[\omega_{X}]^{2}}=\sqrt{[\tilde{\phi}([\omega^{\prime}])]^{2}}=\sqrt{[\omega^{\prime}]^{2}}.

Since
\omega_{S^{2}}
has large enough area, we see that

w_{G}(X\times S^{2},\omega_{X}\oplus\omega_{S^{2}})\geq\sqrt{[\omega^{\prime}]^{2}}.

On the other hand, by Theorem
2.8
and Proposition
2.10
, we also have

w_{G}(X\times S^{2},\tilde{f}^{*}(\omega^{\prime}\oplus\omega_{S^{2}}))=w_{G}(X^{\prime}\times S^{2},\omega^{\prime}\oplus\omega_{S^{2}})\leq(\omega^{\prime}\oplus\omega_{S^{2}})(A)=\omega^{\prime}(A)<\sqrt{[\omega^{\prime}]^{2}}.

Therefore, they have different Gromov widths.

Fianlly, if these two cohomologous symplectic forms
\omega_{X}\oplus\omega_{S^{2}}
with
\tilde{f}^{*}(\omega^{\prime}\oplus\omega_{S^{2}})
have the same first Chern classes, then
\tilde{\phi}(K_{\omega^{\prime}})=K_{\omega_{X}}
. Recall that by Proposition
2.9
, we may also assume
[\omega^{\prime}]\cdot K_{\omega^{\prime}}<0
. Thus,

[\omega_{X}]\cdot K_{\omega_{X}}=\tilde{\phi}([\omega^{\prime}])\cdot\tilde{\phi}(K_{\omega^{\prime}})=[\omega^{\prime}]\cdot K_{\omega^{\prime}}<0.

This will contradict with Liu-Ohta-Ono’s Theorem
[
Liu96
,
OO96
]
which states that the existence of such symplectic form
\omega_{X}
implies the manifold
X
is diffeomorphic to rational or ruled manifold. So their first Chern classes are also different.
∎

Note that it has been observed in
[
Li08
, Proposition 4.10]
that
\mathcal{C}_{X}=\mathcal{P}
when
X=K3
. Indeed, every positive class even has a hyperKähler representative. Therefore, in the above proof, if we take
(X^{\prime},\omega^{\prime})
to be any homotopy
K3
with
K_{\omega^{\prime}}\neq 0
and consider the diffeomorphism
f:X\times S^{2}\rightarrow X^{\prime}\times S^{2}
, there will be some symplectic form
\omega_{X}\oplus\omega_{S^{2}}
on
X\times S^{2}
cohomologous to
f^{*}(\omega^{\prime}\oplus\omega_{S^{2}})
. However, the Chern class for any symplectic form on K3 must vanishi. As a result,
c_{1}(\omega_{X}\oplus\omega_{S^{2}})
belongs to the subspace
\mathbb{Z}[\text{PD}(X)]\subseteq H^{2}(X\times S^{2};\mathbb{Z})
while
c_{1}(f^{*}(\omega^{\prime}\oplus\omega_{S^{2}}))
not. They must be different.

Finally, we remark that one can easily obtain examples of cohomologous symplectic forms with different Gromov widths and first Chern classes in higher dimensions by taking more products. For instance, consider
(X\times S^{2})\times(S^{2})^{k}
with cohomologous symplectic forms
\omega_{X}\oplus\omega_{S^{2}}\oplus(\omega_{S^{2}})^{k}
and
\tilde{f}^{*}(\omega^{\prime}\oplus\omega_{S^{2}})\oplus(\omega_{S^{2}})^{k}
.
