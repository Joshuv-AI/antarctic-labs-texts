28 C.F.R. § 36.213 — Relationship of subpart B to subparts C and D of this part.
Citation: us:cfr:28:36.213
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-36.213
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 36 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Disability By Public Accommodations And In Commercial Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subpart B of this part sets forth the general principles of nondiscrimination applicable to all entities subject to this part. Subparts C and D of this part provide guidance on the application of the statute to specific situations. The specific provisions, including the limitations on those provisions, control over the general provisions in circumstances where both specific and general provisions apply.
