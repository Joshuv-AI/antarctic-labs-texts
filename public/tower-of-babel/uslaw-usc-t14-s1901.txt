14 U.S.C. § 1901 — Administration of Academy
Citation: us:usc:14:1901
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section1901
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 19 (Coast Guard Academy); Subchapter I (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The immediate government and military command of the Coast Guard Academy shall be in the Superintendent of the Academy, subject to the direction of the Commandant under the general supervision of the Secretary. The Commandant may select a superintendent from the active list of the Coast Guard who shall serve in the pleasure of the Commandant.
