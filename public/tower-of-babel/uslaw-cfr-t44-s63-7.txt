44 C.F.R. § 63.7 — Amount of coverage and deductible on effective date of condemnation or certification.
Citation: us:cfr:44:63.7
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-63.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 63 (IMPLEMENTATION OF SECTION 1306(c) OF THE NATIONAL FLOOD INSURANCE ACT OF 1968). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The amount of building coverage and the deductible applicable to a claim for benefits under section 1306(c) of the Act are what was in effect on the date of condemnation or the date of application for certification.
