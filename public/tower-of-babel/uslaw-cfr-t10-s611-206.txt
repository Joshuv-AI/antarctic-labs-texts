10 C.F.R. § 611.206 — Existing facilities.
Citation: us:cfr:10:611.206
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-611.206
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 611 (Advanced Technology Vehicles Manufacturer Assistance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary shall, in making awards to those manufacturers that have existing facilities, give priority to those facilities that are oldest or have been in existence for at least 20 years. Such facilities can currently be sitting idle.
