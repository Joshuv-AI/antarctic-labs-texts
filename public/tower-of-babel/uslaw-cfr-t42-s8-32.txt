42 C.F.R. § 8.32 — Administrative record.
Citation: us:cfr:42:8.32
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-8.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 8 (Medications For The Treatment Of Opioid Use Disorder). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The administrative record of review consists of the review file; other submissions by the parties; transcripts or other records of any meetings, conference calls, or oral presentation; evidence submitted at the oral presentation; and orders and other documents issued by the reviewing and presiding officials.
