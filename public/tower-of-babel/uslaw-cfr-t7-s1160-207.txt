7 C.F.R. § 1160.207 — Compensation and reimbursement.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1160.207
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Milk), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1160.207
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1160 (Fluid Milk Promotion Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The members of the Board and trustees, if any, named under § 1160.502, shall serve without compensation but shall be reimbursed for necessary and reasonable expenses incurred by them in the performance of their duties under this subpart.
