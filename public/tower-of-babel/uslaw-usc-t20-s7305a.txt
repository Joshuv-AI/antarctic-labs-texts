20 U.S.C. § 7305a — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:20:7305a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section7305a
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 70 (Strengthening And Improvement Of Elementary And Secondary Schools); Subchapter V (Flexibility And Accountability); Part A (Funding Transferability For State And Local Educational Agencies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of this part is to allow States and local educational agencies the flexibility to target Federal funds to the programs and activities that most effectively address the unique needs of States and localities.
