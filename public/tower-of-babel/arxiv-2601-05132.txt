Topological classification of certain nonorientable 4-manifolds with cyclic fundamental group of order 2 mod 4.
Rafael Torres

Abstract




2020 Mathematics Subject Classification
Primary 57K40

Abstract
: We show that the classification up to homeomorphism of closed topological nonorientable 4-manifolds with fundamental group of order 2 due to Hambleton-Kreck-Teichner can be used to classify a large set of such 4-manifolds with cyclic fundamental group of order 2p for every odd
p>1
. This is done through a simple cut-and-paste construction, and classical and modified surgery theory are used only through results of Hambleton-Kreck-Teichner and Khan. It is plausible that this set comprises all closed topological nonorientable 4-manifolds with
\pi_{1}=\mathbb{Z}/2p
. We collect several interesting questions whose answers would guarantee a complete classification.

1.
Introduction.

Hambleton-Kreck-Teichner obtained a classification up to homeomorphism of closed topological nonorientable 4-manifolds with fundamental group of order two in
[
6
, Theorems 1 and 2]
, where they also provided an explicit list of examples that realize every homeomorphsim class
[
6
, Theorem 3]
. They showed that any homeomorphism class of such a 4-manifold
X
is determined by

•

its Euler characteristic
\chi(X)
,

•

the Stiefel-Whitney number
w_{1}(X)^{4}

•

its Kirby-Siebenmann invariant
\Ks(X)
and

•

a
\mathbb{Z}/8
-valued Arf invariant
\Ar(X,\Phi)
for a primitive
\TopPin^{c}
-structure
(X,\Phi_{X})
.

The purpose of this paper is to extend Hambleton-Kreck-Teichner’s results to 4-manifolds with cyclic fundamental group of order
2p
for any odd
p>1
. We begin by recalling a construction introduced in
[

The aforementioned work of Hambleton-Kreck-Teichner and work of Ruberman-Stern
[

show that a closed topological nonorientable 4-manifold with fundamental group of order 2 admits a smooth structure if and only if its Kirby-Siebenmann invariant vanishes; see

Theorem D
.

A closed topological nonorientable 4-manifold that is contained in Collection

admits a smooth structure if and only if its Kirby-Siebenmann invariant vanishes.

The results presented so far raise the following questions.

Question 1
.

Is Collection
B
a complete list of representatives of all homeomorphism classes of closed nonorientable topological 4-manifolds with cyclic fundamental group of order
2p
for odd
p>1
?

Question 2
.

Does the set of invariants

\{\chi,w_{1}^{4},\Ks,\Ar\}

classify closed topological nonorientable 4-manifolds with cyclic fundamental group of order
2p
for odd
p>1
up to homeomorphism?

We obtain the following partial answers to these questions. An affirmative answer is obtained if one considers stable homeomorphism classes instead. Recall that two topological 4-manifolds
M
and
M^{\prime}
are stably homeomorphic if there are integers
k
and
k^{\prime}
such that the connected sums
M\mathbin{\#}k(S^{2}\times S^{2})
and
M^{\prime}\mathbin{\#}k^{\prime}(S^{2}\times S^{2})
are homeomorphic.

Theorem E
.

The 4-manifolds

R^{1}_{2p,1}\mathbin{\#}{\mathbb{C}\mkern-0.5mu\mathrm{P}}^{2}
,
R^{1}_{2p,1}\mathbin{\#}2\cdot{\mathbb{C}\mkern-0.5mu\mathrm{P}}^{2}
,
A_{2p,1}\mathbin{\#}{\mathbb{C}\mkern-0.5mu\mathrm{P}}^{2}
,
A_{2p,1}\mathbin{\#}2\cdot{\mathbb{C}\mkern-0.5mu\mathrm{P}}^{2}
,
\ast R^{1}_{2p,1}\mathbin{\#}{\mathbb{C}\mkern-0.5mu\mathrm{P}}^{2}
,
\ast R^{1}_{2p,1}\mathbin{\#}2\cdot{\mathbb{C}\mkern-0.5mu\mathrm{P}}^{2}
,
\ast A_{2p,1}\mathbin{\#}{\mathbb{C}\mkern-0.5mu\mathrm{P}}^{2}
,
\ast A_{2p,1}\mathbin{\#}2\cdot{\mathbb{C}\mkern-0.5mu\mathrm{P}}^{2}
,

A_{2p,1}
,
\ast A_{2p,1}
,
R^{r}_{2p,1}
,
\ast R^{r}_{2p,1}
,
B_{2p,1}
and
\ast B_{2p,1}

for
1\leq r\leq 4
realize all 20 stable homeomorphism classes of closed topological nonorientable 4-manifolds with finite cyclic fundamental group of order
2p
for odd
p>1
.

The proof of Theorem
E
is given in Section
4.3
. In it, we make use of work of Debray
[
3
, Main Theorem]
, where the author computed the number of stable homeomorphism classes of closed topological nonorientable 4-manifolds with finite fundamental group of order 2 mod 4 under a mild hypothesis by using the modified surgery theory developed by Kreck
[

]
.

A partial answer to Question
1
and Question
2
is obtained by coupling these theorems with a cancellation result of Khan
[
9
]
.

Theorem F
.

Let
M_{2p}
be a closed topological nonorientable 4-manifold with cyclic fundamental group of order 2p for
p>1
odd.

\bullet
The connected sum
M_{2p}\mathbin{\#}2\cdot(S^{2}\times S^{2})
is homeomorphic to exactly one 4-manifold in Collection
B
.

\bullet
There is a homeomorphism

M_{2p}\mathbin{\#}2\cdot(S^{2}\times S^{2})\rightarrow X_{2p}\mathbin{\#}2\cdot(S^{2}\times S^{2}),

where
X_{2p}
is obtained through Construction
A
from an initial data 4-manifold
X
with Euler characteristic
\chi(M_{2p})=\chi(X)
, Stiefel-Whitney number
w_{1}(M_{2p})^{4}=w_{1}(X)^{4}
, Kirby-Siebenmann invariant
\Ks(M_{2p})=\Ks(X)
and Arf invariant
\Arf(M_{2p},\Phi_{M_{2p}})=\pm\Arf(X,\Phi_{X})
for some primitive
\TopPin^{c}
-structures
(M_{2p},\Phi_{M_{2p}})
and
(X,\Phi_{X})
.

The simplicity of the techniques used in this paper only takes one so far. The missing step to obtain a complete classification is to address the cancellation problem for
S^{2}\times S^{2}
factors as in the statement of Theorem
F
, a well-known hard algebraic problem
[
6
,
9
]
. An approach to this is to study the stable equivalence classes dictated by the surgery obstruction group
l_{5}^{s}(\mathbb{Z}/2p,-)
as done by Hambleton-Kreck-Teichner
[
6
]
. This is a problem well worth studying and would yield a proof of the following conjecture.

Conjecture G
.

Theorem
F
holds for
M_{2p}
without any additional copies of
S^{2}\times S^{2}
.
