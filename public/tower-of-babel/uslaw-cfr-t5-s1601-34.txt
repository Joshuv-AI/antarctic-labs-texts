5 C.F.R. § 1601.34 — Error correction.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1601.34
Creator: Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1601.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1601 (Participants' Choices Of Tsp Funds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Errors in processing investment elections and fund reallocation or fund transfer requests, or errors that otherwise cause money to be invested in the wrong investment fund, will be corrected in accordance with the error correction regulations found at 5 CFR part 1605.
