21 C.F.R. § 888.3730 — Toe joint phalangeal (hemi-toe) polymer prosthesis.

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-888.3730
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 888 (Orthopedic Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. A toe joint phalangeal (hemi-toe) polymer prosthesis is a device made of silicone elastomer intended to be implanted to replace the base of the proximal phalanx of the toe.
(b) Classification. Class II.
