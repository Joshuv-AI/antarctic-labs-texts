7 C.F.R. § 1412.63 — Contract or application liability.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1412.63
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1412.63
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1412 (Agriculture Risk Coverage And Price Loss Coverage). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All producers who signed an ARC or PLC Program contract made according to this part are jointly and severally liable for contract or application violations and resulting repayments and penalties.
