22 U.S.C. § 1358 — Rights of third countries
Citation: us:usc:22:1358
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1358
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 15 (The Republic Of The Philippines); Subchapter IV (General Provisions Relating To Trade Relations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The benefits granted by subchapters I to IV of this chapter, and by the executive agreement provided for in subchapter III, to the Philippines, Philippine articles or products, and Philippine citizens, shall not, by reason of any provision of any existing treaty or agreement with any third country, be extended to such country or its products, citizens, or subjects.
