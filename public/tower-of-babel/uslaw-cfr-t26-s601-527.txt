26 C.F.R. § 601.527 — Other provisions applied to representation in alcohol, tobacco, and firearms activities.
Citation: us:cfr:26:601.527
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-601.527
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 601 (Statement Of Procedural Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of paragraph (b) of § 601.505, and of §§ 601.506 through 601.508 of this subpart, as applicable, shall be followed in offices of the Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, and Firearms.
