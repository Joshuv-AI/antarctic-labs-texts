Gauge origami on broken lines
Sergej Monavari

Abstract
. In analogy to Nekrasov’s theory of gauge origami on intersecting branes, we introduce the gauge origami moduli space on broken lines. We realize this moduli space as a Quot scheme parametrising zero-dimensional quotients of a torsion sheaf on two intersecting affine lines, and describe it as a moduli space of quiver representations. We construct a virtual fundamental class and virtual structure sheaf, by which we define K K -theoretic invariants. We compute its associated partition function for all ranks, and show that it reproduces the generating series of equivariant χ y \chi_{y} -genus when the moduli space is smooth. Finally, we relate our partition function with the virtual invariants of the Quot schemes of the affine plane and Nekrasov’s partition function.

Theorem 3.6

The gauge origami partition function

\mathfrak{e}(-T^{\vir}_{\bn}),\qquad\lvert\bn\rvert=n.

By the definition of the operator

\mathfrak{e}(-T^{\vir}_{\bn})=A(t)\prod_{(i,\alpha)\neq(j,\beta)}\frac{\prod_{\mu_{ij,\alpha\beta}}(1-w_{i\alpha}^{-1}w_{j\beta}t^{\mu_{ij,\alpha\beta}})}{\prod_{\nu_{ij,\alpha\beta}}(1-w_{i\alpha}^{-1}w_{j\beta}t^{\nu_{ij,\alpha\beta}})},

4.3.
Example:
\overline{r}=(0,1)
and
n=1

We conclude by working out explicitly the relations among the gauge origami moduli space, the Quot scheme and the moduli space of framed sheaves in the easiest case of
\overline{r}=(0,1)
and
n=1
. We believe this guiding example will help the reader to go through the notation and the correct
\mathbf{T}
-equivariant structure of the sheaves involved in the general construction.

The relevant moduli spaces (and their virtual dimensions) in this setting are

\displaystyle\mathcal{M}_{(0,1),1}\cong\Hilb^{1}{\mathbb{A}}^{1}\cong{\mathbb{A}}^{1},

\displaystyle\vdim(\mathcal{M}_{(0,1),1})=0,

\displaystyle\Quot_{{\mathbb{A}}^{2}}(\oO^{1},1)\cong\Hilb^{1}{\mathbb{A}}^{2}\cong{\mathbb{A}}^{2},

\displaystyle\vdim(\Quot_{{\mathbb{A}}^{2}}(\oO^{1},1))=1,

\displaystyle{\mathcal{M}}^{\rm nc}_{1,1}\cong\Hilb^{1}{\mathbb{A}}^{2}\cong{\mathbb{A}}^{2},

\displaystyle\dim({\mathcal{M}}^{\rm nc}_{1,1})=2,

\displaystyle{\mathcal{M}}^{\rm fr}_{1,1}\cong\Hilb^{1}{\mathbb{A}}^{2}\cong{\mathbb{A}}^{2}.

\displaystyle\dim({\mathcal{M}}^{\rm fr}_{1,1})=2.

While the last three moduli spaces are isomorphic, the virtual structure on the Quot scheme does not coincide with its smooth structure, since its virtual dimension (i.e. the dimension of its virtual fundamental class) is smaller. Consider the chain of inclusions

\displaystyle p\in\mathcal{M}_{(0,1),1}\hookrightarrow\Quot_{{\mathbb{A}}^{2}}(\oO^{1},1)\hookrightarrow{\mathcal{M}}^{\rm nc}_{1,1}\cong{\mathcal{M}}^{\rm fr}_{1,1},

where
p
is the unique
\mathbf{T}
-fixed point of all the four moduli spaces, corresponding to the quotient
[\iota_{2,*}\oO_{{\mathbb{A}}^{1}}\twoheadrightarrow\oO_{0}]
.

We summarise the (weight decomposition of the) fibers at the point
p
of the various locally free sheaves used in constructing the virtual fundamental classes

\displaystyle{\mathcal{V}}|_{p}=\Lambda^{2}{\mathbb{C}}^{2}\otimes\operatorname{End}(V)\oplus{\mathbb{C}}\otimes\operatorname{Hom}(W_{2},V)=t_{1}^{-1}t_{2}^{-1}+t_{2}^{-1},

\displaystyle\widetilde{{\mathcal{V}}}|_{p}=\Lambda^{2}{\mathbb{C}}^{2}\otimes\operatorname{End}(V)=t_{1}^{-1}t_{2}^{-1},

\displaystyle{\mathcal{I}}^{[1]}|_{p}={\mathbb{C}}\otimes\operatorname{Hom}(W_{2},V)=t_{2}^{-1},

\displaystyle{\mathcal{F}}|_{p}^{*}\cdot t_{1}^{-1}t_{2}^{-1}=\operatorname{Hom}(W_{2},V)\cdot t_{1}^{-1}t_{2}^{-1}=t_{1}^{-1}t_{2}^{-1}.

The (virtual) tangent spaces are therefore

\displaystyle T_{{\mathcal{M}_{(0,1),1}}}^{\vir}|_{p}

\displaystyle=t_{1}^{-1}-t_{1}^{-1}t_{2}^{-1},

\displaystyle T_{{\Quot_{{\mathbb{A}}^{2}}(\oO^{1},1)}}^{\vir}|_{p}

\displaystyle=t_{1}^{-1}+t_{2}^{-1}-t_{1}^{-1}t_{2}^{-1},

\displaystyle T_{{\mathcal{M}}^{\rm fr}_{1,1}}|_{p}

\displaystyle=t_{1}^{-1}+t_{2}^{-1}.

Applying the localisation formula in
K
-theory, we obtain

\displaystyle\chi\left(\mathcal{M}_{(0,1),1},\oO^{\vir}_{\mathcal{M}_{(0,1),1}}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{1-t_{1}t_{2}}{1-t_{1}},

\displaystyle\chi\left(\Quot_{{\mathbb{A}}^{2}}(\oO^{1},1),\oO^{\vir}_{\Quot_{{\mathbb{A}}^{2}}(\oO^{1},1)}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{1-t_{1}t_{2}}{(1-t_{1})(1-t_{2})},

\displaystyle\chi\left(\mathcal{M}^{\rm fr}_{1,1},\oO_{\mathcal{M}^{\rm fr}_{1,1}}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{(1-t_{1})(1-t_{2})},

in complete agreement with the pushforward formulas of (
4.5
),
Corollary

4.3
and
Corollary

4.5
.
