49 C.F.R. § 228.335 — Electronic recordkeeping.
Citation: us:cfr:49:228.335
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-228.335
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 228 (Passenger Train Employee Hours Of Service; Recordkeeping And Reporting; Sleeping Quarters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each railroad shall keep records as required by § 228.323 either—
(1) On paper forms provided by the railroad, or
(2) By electronic means that conform with the requirements of subpart D of this part.
(b) Records required to be kept shall be made available to the Federal Railroad Administration as provided by 49 U.S.C. 20107.
