40 U.S.C. § 546 — Contractor inventories
Citation: us:usc:40:546
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section546
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Chapter 5 (Property Management); Subchapter III (Disposing Of Property). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Subject to regulations of the Administrator of General Services, an executive agency may authorize a contractor or subcontractor with the agency to retain or dispose of contractor inventory.
