36 C.F.R. § 262.4 — Audit of expenditures.
Citation: us:cfr:36:262.4
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-262.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 262 (Law Enforcement Support Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Chief of the Forest Service shall, through appropriate directives to agency personnel, assure the accountability of all funds spent in carrying out the provisions of this subpart and safeguard the identity of those wishing to remain anonymous.
