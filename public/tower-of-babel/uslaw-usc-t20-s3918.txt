20 U.S.C. § 3918 — Approval of proposals
Citation: us:usc:20:3918
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section3918
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 52 (Education For Economic Security); Subchapter I (National Science Foundation Science And Engineering Education). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Foundation shall adopt approval procedures designed to assure that awards are made on the basis of the scientific and educational merit as determined by the peer review process. To the maximum extent possible, the Foundation shall assure that there is an equitable distribution of resources with respect to institutions and geographical areas.
