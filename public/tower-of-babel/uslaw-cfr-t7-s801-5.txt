7 C.F.R. § 801.5 — Tolerance for diverter-type mechanical samplers.
Citation: us:cfr:7:801.5
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Federal Grain Inspection Service, Fair Trade Practices Program), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-801.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 801 (Official Performance Requirements For Grain Inspection Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The maintenance tolerance for diverter-type mechanical samplers (primary, or primary and secondary in combination) used in performing official inspection services shall be ±10 percent, mean deviation from standard sampling device using corn or the same type of grain that the system will be used to sample.
