49 C.F.R. § 1103.31 — Responsibility for litigation.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1103.31
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1103.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1103 (Practitioners). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The practitioner bears the responsibility for advising as to questionable transactions, bringing questionable proceedings, or urging questionable defenses. Client's instructions cannot be used as an excuse for questionable practices.
