22 U.S.C. § 2431b — Establishment of Facility
Citation: us:usc:22:2431b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2431b
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 32 (Foreign Assistance); Subchapter IV (Debt Reduction For Developing Countries With Tropical Forests). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is established in the Department of the Treasury an entity to be known as the “Conservation Facility” for the purpose of providing for the administration of debt reduction in accordance with this subchapter.
