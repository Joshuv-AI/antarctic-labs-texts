43 C.F.R. § 2711.4-1 — -1 Grazing improvements.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2711.4-1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2711.4-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2710 (Sales: Federal Land Policy And Management Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No public lands in a grazing lease or permit may be conveyed until the provisions of part 4100 of this title concerning compensation for any authorized grazing improvements have been met.
