38 U.S.C. § 3601 — Definition of emergency situation
Citation: us:usc:38:3601
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section3601
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part III (Readjustment And Related Benefits); Chapter 36 (Administration Of Educational Benefits); Subchapter I (Emergency Situations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter, the term “emergency situation” means a situation that—
(1)
the President declares is an emergency; and
(2)
the Secretary determines is an emergency for purposes of the laws administered by the Secretary.
