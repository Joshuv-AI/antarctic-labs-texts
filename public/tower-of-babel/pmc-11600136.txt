ERA Registry Figure of the month Heterogeneity of kidney replacement therapy incidence across Europe
Authors: Vianda S Stel, Alberto Ortiz, Anneke Kramer
Published: 2024

Incidence of kidney replacement therapy per million population in 2021, by country.

⁎ Data on paediatric patients is not included, ^ Data include dialysis patients only.

Source: Boerstra et al. CKJ 2024, https://doi.org/10.1093/ckj/sfad281, Fig. 2 left panel. Figure was slightly adapted to show differences at the level of the country and made no distinction in colors between countries providing individual or aggregated data to the ERA Registry.

Explanation: Substantial differences exist in the number of patients starting kidney replacement therapy (KRT) per million population (pmp) within Europe in 2021, varying from 53 pmp in Ukraine to 283 pmp in Cyprus. Since most patients started with dialysis and only a few started with a preemptive kidney transplantation, this figure consists mainly of dialysis patients.
