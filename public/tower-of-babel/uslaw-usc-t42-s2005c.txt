42 U.S.C. § 2005c — Payments
Citation: us:usc:42:2005c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2005c
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 22 (Indian Hospitals And Health Facilities); Subchapter II (Construction Of Health Facilities And Community Hospitals). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Surgeon General shall make payments under section 2005 of this title in advance or by way of reimbursement and in such installments consistent with construction progress, as he may determine.
