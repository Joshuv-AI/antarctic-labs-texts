14 C.F.R. § 249.8 — Premature loss or destruction of records.
Citation: us:cfr:14:249.8
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-249.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 249 (Preservation Of Air Carrier Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If records are destroyed or lost before the expiration of the prescribed retention period, a statement shall be prepared and submitted to the Director, Office of Airline Information, which lists, as accurately as possible, the unavailable records and describes the circumstances under which they became unavailable.
