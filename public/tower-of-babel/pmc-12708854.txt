Correction: Relaxation of selective constraint on the sweet-taste receptor gene TAS1R2 in lorisiform primates
Authors: Qinyuan Ji, Min Hou, Muhammad Shoaib Akhtar, Takashi Hayakawa, Yasuka Toda, Akihiro Itoigawa, Hiroo Imai, Takafumi Ishida, Amanda D. Melin, Shoji Kawamura
Published: 2025

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-23648-x, published online 06 November 2025

The original version of this Article contained errors, where data in Figure 1 was incorrect. The label Strepsirrhini was drawn to encompass Lemuriformes and only a portion of Lorisiformes. In order to reflect the text accurately, the label Strepsirrhini should have fully encompassed both infraorders Lemuriformes and Lorisiformes. In addition, the species name ‘Northern lesser galago’ was incorrectly written as ‘Lesser galago.’
