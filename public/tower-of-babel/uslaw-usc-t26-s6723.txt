26 U.S.C. § 6723 — Failure to comply with other information reporting requirements
Citation: us:usc:26:6723
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section6723
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 68 (Additions To The Tax, Additional Amounts, And Assessable Penalties); Subchapter B (Assessable Penalties); Part II (Failure To Comply With Certain Information Reporting Requirements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the case of a failure by any person to comply with a specified information reporting requirement on or before the time prescribed therefor, such person shall pay a penalty of $50 for each such failure, but the total amount imposed on such person for all such failures during any calendar year shall not exceed $100,000.
