42 U.S.C. § 1324 — “Governor” defined
Citation: us:usc:42:1324
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1324
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 7 (Social Security); Subchapter XII (Advances To State Unemployment Funds). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When used in this subchapter, the term “Governor” includes the Mayor of the District of Columbia.
