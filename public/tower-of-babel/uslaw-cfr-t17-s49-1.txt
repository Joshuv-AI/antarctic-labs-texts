17 C.F.R. § 49.1 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:17:49.1
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-49.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 49 (Swap Data Repositories). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this part apply to any swap data repository as defined under Section 1a(48) of the Act which is registered or is required to register as such with the Commission pursuant to Section 21(a) of the Act.
