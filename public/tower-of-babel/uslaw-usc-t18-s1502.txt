18 U.S.C. § 1502 — Resistance to extradition agent
Citation: us:usc:18:1502
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1502
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 73 (Obstruction Of Justice). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever knowingly and willfully obstructs, resists, or opposes an extradition agent of the United States in the execution of his duties, shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than one year, or both.
