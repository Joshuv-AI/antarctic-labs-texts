22 U.S.C. § 2665c — Thomas R. Pickering Foreign Affairs Fellowship Program
Citation: us:usc:22:2665c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2665c
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 38 (Department Of State). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Authorization
There shall be in the Department a Thomas R. Pickering Foreign Affairs Fellowship Program.
(b) Purpose
The program required by subsection (a) shall continue to provide financial assistance, mentoring, and professional development opportunities to graduate students who commit to pursuing careers in the Foreign Service of the United States.
(c) Administration
The program required by subsection (a) shall be administered by the Department, consistent with existing law and regulations.
