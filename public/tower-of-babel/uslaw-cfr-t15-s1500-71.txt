15 C.F.R. § 1500.71 — Books and records.
Citation: us:cfr:15:1500.71
Creator: Office Of The Under-Secretary For Economic Affairs, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-1500.71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 1500 (Concrete Masonry Research, Education, And Promotion). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each manufacturer subject to this Order shall maintain and make available for inspection by the Secretary such books and records as are necessary to carry out the provisions of this Order, including such records as are necessary to verify any reports required. Such records shall be retained for at least 7 years beyond the fiscal period of their applicability.
