28 C.F.R. § 44.102 — Computation of time.
Citation: us:cfr:28:44.102
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-44.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 44 (Unfair Immigration-Related Employment Practices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a time period specified in this part ends on a day when the Federal Government in Washington, DC is closed (such as on weekends and Federal holidays, or due to a closure for all or part of a business day), the time period shall be extended until the next full day that the Federal Government in Washington, DC is open.
