31 C.F.R. § 5.15 — How will Treasury entities refer Treasury debts to private collection agencies?
Citation: us:cfr:31:5.15
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-5.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 5 (Treasury Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Treasury entities will transfer delinquent Treasury debts to the Financial Management Service to obtain debt collection services provided by private collection agencies. See § 5.9 of this part.
