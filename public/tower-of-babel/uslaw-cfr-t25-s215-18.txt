25 C.F.R. § 215.18 — Term of leases.
Citation: us:cfr:25:215.18
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-215.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 215 (Lead And Zinc Mining Operations And Leases, Quapaw Agency). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term of lead and zinc mining leases executed pursuant to acts of Congress and under the regulations in this part shall be for such period of time as may be determined in each case by the Secretary of the Interior, but in no case shall a lease be made to extend beyond the restriction or trust period on the lands covered by such lease.
