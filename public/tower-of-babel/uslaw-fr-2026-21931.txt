I. Introduction

A. Overview of the Exemptive Order

    On September 18, 2026, Cboe Clear U.S., LLC (``CCUS''), a clearing 
agency now temporarily registered with the Securities and Exchange 
Commission (``Commission'') pursuant to Rule 17Ab2-1(c) \1\ under the 
Securities Exchange Act of 1934 \2\ (``Exchange Act'') for a period of 
18 months,\3\ filed an application with the Commission 
(``Application'') \4\ to obtain an exemption pursuant to section 
36(a)(1) \5\ of the Exchange Act, in accordance with the procedures set 
forth in Exchange Act Rule 0-12.\6\ Specifically, CCUS requested 
exemptive relief on behalf of broker-dealers that are CCUS clearing 
members (``Clearing Members'') from the limitation in Item 13 and Note 
F of Exhibit A to Rule 15c3-3 \7\ that confines the Item 13 debit to 
the amount of margin required and on deposit with the Options Clearing 
Corporation (``OCC'') for all option contracts written or purchased in 
customer accounts. This would afford Clearing Members the same 
treatment for margin required and on deposit at CCUS for purposes of 
the reserve computation requirements under Item 13 and Note F of Rule 
15c3-3a as is currently afforded to margin required and on deposit with 
the OCC.\8\

B. Overview of Rule 15c3-3 and Rule 15c3-3a

    Rule 15c3-3, the broker-dealer customer protection rule, requires, 
among other things, broker-dealers that hold customer cash and 
securities (``carrying broker-dealers'') to compute the net amount of 
cash owed to customers under a formula in Rule 15c3-3a (``customer 
reserve computation'').\12\ Generally, carrying broker-dealers must 
perform this customer reserve computation and make any required deposit 
into special reserve bank account daily or weekly. The rule also 
requires a carrying broker-dealer to perform a PAB reserve computation 
for the proprietary securities and cash it holds for other broker-
dealers, known as proprietary accounts of broker-dealers (``PAB 
accounts'').\13\ Item 13 of the customer reserve computation in Rule 
15c3-3a includes as a debit in the computation the amount of margin 
required and on deposit with OCC for all option contracts written or 
purchased in customer accounts. Note F to Item 13 states that Item 13 
must include the amount of margin required and on deposit with the OCC 
to the extent such margin is represented by cash, proprietary qualified 
securities and letters of credit collateralized by customers' 
securities.\14\ Under that framework, the permitted debit is based on 
the margin amount posted by the broker-dealer to OCC that is calculated 
by OCC across all of the broker-dealer's customers with listed options 
(i.e., on an omnibus basis).
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \12\ 17 CFR 240.15c3-3.
    \13\ Broker-dealers use the formula in Rule 15c3-3a for the

15c3-3a. For the purposes of this order, references to ``accounts,''

C. Request for Exemptive Relief From CCUS

    In its Application, CCUS requested, pursuant to section 36(a)(1) of 
the Exchange Act, and in accordance with the procedures set forth in 
Rule 0-12, that the Commission issue an order exempting its Clearing 
Members from the limitation in Item 13 and Note F of Rule 15c3-3a that 
confines the Item 13 debit to the amount of margin required and on 
deposit with OCC for option contracts written or purchased in customer 
accounts. CCUS also requested that the exemptive relief apply with 
respect to the PAB reserve computation.\15\ In effect, CCUS is seeking 
comparable treatment for its Clearing Members under the customer 
reserve computation with respect to the margin that is required and on 
deposit at CCUS as has been afforded broker-dealers that are clearing 
members of OCC.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \15\ See Application, at 3-4.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    In support of its request, CCUS stated that when the Commission 
adopted Item 13, and Note F thereto, OCC was the only clearing agency 
registered with the Commission to provide central counterparty services 
for securities options. CCUS stated that it does not

[[Page 67882]]

believe that the Exchange Act requires or warrants different treatment 
for margin held on similar terms for security option products at 
different registered clearing agencies. Further, CCUS stated that 
section 17A(a)(2) of the Exchange Act \16\ directs the Commission, with 
due regard for the maintenance of fair competition among clearing 
agencies, to facilitate a national system for the prompt and accurate 
clearance and settlement of securities transactions and to assure equal 
regulation under the Exchange Act of registered clearing agencies. 
Consequently, CCUS stated that reading Item 13 to exclude margin held 
at a second registered options clearing agency because it did not exist 
when the rule was amended would be inconsistent with this statutory 
mandate.\17\
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \16\ 15 U.S.C. 78q-1(a)(2).
    \17\ See Application at 4.

Item 15 \19\ of the customer reserve computation do not name any 
particular clearing agency, and do not distinguish between registered 
clearing agencies.\20\ CCUS stated that where the Commission 
anticipated that more than one clearing agency could hold margin, it 
framed the customer reserve computation treatment of such margin by 
reference to a registered category rather than name a specific existing 
clearing agency.\21\ Consequently, CCUS stated that permitting broker-
dealers to treat margin held at other registered clearing agencies 
would treat functionally equivalent margin on equivalent terms.\22\

IV. Discussion

A. Statement of Relief

    Section 36(a)(1) of the Exchange Act authorizes the Commission to 
conditionally or unconditionally exempt any person, security, or 
transaction, or any class or classes of persons, securities, or 
transactions, from certain provisions of the Exchange Act or certain 
rules or regulations thereunder, by rule, regulation, or order, to the 
extent that such exemption is necessary or appropriate in the public 
interest, and is consistent with the protection of investors.\26\ As 
described below, the Commission finds that the conditional exemptive 
relief granted herein to permit Clearing Members to include an Item 13 
debit for margin required and on deposit with CCUS in the customer and 
PAB reserve computations is appropriate in the public interest, and is 
consistent with the protection of investors.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \26\ 15 U.S.C. 78mm(a)(1).
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    In particular, it is appropriate to permit Clearing Members to 
include an Item 13 debit in the customer and PAB reserve computations 
for margin required and on deposit at CCUS for customer binary options. 
Without the availability of an Item 13 debit, increases in the amounts 
of required customer margin as a result of the clearance of binary 
options at CCUS would result in corresponding increases in the need for 
Clearing Members to use their own cash and securities to meet CCUS 
customer margin requirements. This relief also may avoid potential 
increases in a Clearing Member's required deposits in its special 
reserve bank accounts (because there is no permitted Item 13 debit for 
CCUS to offset credits).\27\ This relief would also help to reduce any 
capital constraints and inefficiencies for Clearing Members, which may 
affect their liquidity and reduce their willingness to intermediate 
customer access to cleared binary options at CCUS. Additionally, it is 
in the public interest to permit an Item 13 debit to help promote 
competition in the development of a cleared securities market for 
trading binary security options of the type anticipated to be cleared 
by CCUS and facilitate access to the central clearing and settlement of 
securities options.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \27\ See section I.B. (discussing customer reserve computation 
and special reserve bank account).
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Further, the Commission adopted amendments to Rule 15c3-3a to add 
the Item 13 debit in 1977, when the OCC was the only clearing agency 
registered with the Commission that provided central counterparty 
services for options contracts.\28\ The rule's specific reference to 
OCC therefore identifies the single qualifying clearing agency that 
existed at that time. However, circumstances have changed. CCUS has 
been approved as a second clearing agency for options.\29\ Providing 
this relief will help ensure that the same margin treatment under the 
Item 13 debit applies to both CCUS and OCC, and to their broker-dealer 
members, thereby facilitating fair competition among Commission-
registered options clearing agencies which is in the public interest. 
Finally, permitting the Item 13 debit for CCUS will support a broker-
dealer's customer facilitation activity in securities options

[[Page 67883]]

clearing, in light of the increased number of securities clearing 
agencies.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \28\ See Uniform Net Capital Rule and Customer Protection Rule; 
Final Rule, Exchange Act Release No. 13565 (May 23, 1977), 42 FR 
27221 (May 27, 1977).
    \29\ See CCUS Temporary Registration Order, supra note 3.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    The conditions in this order, discussed below in section II.B, are 
designed to permit the Item 13 debit only in circumstances that provide 
protections to customers consistent with the customer protection 
objectives of Rules 15c3-3 and 15c3-3a and the current approach for 
margin required and on deposit at OCC. For example, Rule 15c3-3 
requires broker-dealers to take steps to safeguard customer assets by 
segregating their securities and cash from the carrying broker-dealer's 
proprietary business activities.\30\ The conditions in this order are 
designed to ensure that customer margin is appropriately segregated and 
that a broker-dealer cannot use customer cash and securities to finance 
its proprietary business activities. Further, consistent with the 
existing Item 13 debit for margin required and on deposit at OCC, the 
conditions of this order explicitly limit the exemption to customer 
margin required and deposit with CCUS with respect to customer 
positions in binary options. These conditions help reduce the 
likelihood of certain risks that may undermine the customer protection 
objectives of Rule 15c3-3, including that broker-dealers may not 
appropriately safeguard customer cash and securities or may improperly 
include debits in their customer reserve computation.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \30\ See section I.B. of this order.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    The one comment letter that the Commission received on the Notice 
of an Application generally supported the request for an exemption,\31\ 
but recommended that the Commission impose additional conditions in 
connection with granting the request. Specifically, the commenter 
pointed to the conditions contained in Note G to the Item 14 debit 
related to margin required and on deposit at a registered clearing 
agency for security futures products and futures contracts, as well as 
Note H to the Item 15 debt related to margin required and on deposit as 
a registered clearing agency for transactions in Treasury securities 
and recommended that the Commission impose the conditions therein on 
CCUS in connection with the requested exemption to the extent that such 
conditions ``fit'' binary options.\32\ The Commission has considered 
the comment letter, and is declining to incorporate the suggested 
conditions. The conditions proposed by CCUS, and outlined below, are 
robust and reasonably designed to protect investors.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \31\ See letter from Shawn SN, at 2.
    \32\ Id. at 2-3 and 11-15.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Accordingly, after careful consideration of the Application and the 
comment received, the Commission is issuing, pursuant to section 
36(a)(1) of the Exchange Act, conditional exemptive relief to exempt 
Clearing Members from the limitation in Item 13 and Note F of Rule 
15c3-3a that confines the Item 13 debit to the amount of margin 
required and on deposit with the OCC, and as such, Clearing Members may 
include a debit in Item 13 of their customer and PAB reserve 
computations for margin required and on deposit with CCUS for all 
binary options contracts written or purchased in customer accounts, 
subject to the conditions discussed below.

B. Discussion of Conditions

    The first condition provides that CCUS must be registered with the 
Commission as a clearing agency in accordance with section 17A(b) \33\ 
of the Exchange Act and Rule 17ab2-1 \34\ thereunder. This condition is 
designed to ensure that the relief provided in this order remains 
consistent with the customer protection objectives of Rule 15c3-3, and 
specifically those provided by the limitations in Item 13 and Note F of 
Exhibit A to Rule 15c3-3, by ensuring that CCUS operates subject to the 
same regulatory framework as the OCC, which is a registered clearing 
agency.\35\
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \33\ 15 U.S.C. 78q-1.
    \34\ 17 CFR 240.17ab2-1.
    \35\ A list of registered clearing agencies is available at: 
https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/self-regulatory-organization-rulemaking.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    The second condition requires that the relief provided by this 
order will apply solely to margin required and on deposit with CCUS 
with respect to customer positions in binary options. This condition is 
designed to ensure that the relief provided is limited to only margin 
applicable to options transactions in customer accounts, the same type 
of transactions covered under Item 13 of Rule 15c3-3a. This condition 
also will ensure that margin required and on deposit at CCUS as a 
result of a Clearing Member's proprietary positions may not be included 
as a debit in the reserve computations. Therefore, this condition is 
designed to effectuate a fundamental aspect of Rule 15c3-3 that a 
broker-dealer cannot use customer cash or securities to finance its 
proprietary business activities.
    The third condition provides that the binary options that CCUS 
clears will be margined by CCUS consistent with the requirements of 
Rule 17Ad-22(e) and Clearing Members will collect such margin from 
their customers, with any proposed changes to CCUS's margin system 
subject to Commission review, and, as appropriate, approval pursuant to 
section 19(b) of the Exchange Act and Rule 19b-4 thereunder. CCUS has 
stated that the binary options will be fully margined at the clearing 
agency level.\36\ This condition ensures that the Clearing Members hold 
sufficient customer collateral to cover the required margin amounts at 
CCUS, and that proposed changes to CCUS's margin system would need to 
be filed with the Commission through a rule filing under section 19(b) 
of the Exchange Act. The purpose of this condition is to help ensure 
that the exemption provided under this order is only available when 
regulatory requirements are appropriately followed.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \36\ Broker-dealers are also subject to margin requirements in 
rules promulgated by the Federal Reserve (12 CFR 220.1, et seq.) and 
SROs (see, e.g., FINRA Rule 4210 and Cboe Rule 10.3).
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    The fourth condition requires that, for the purposes of Rule 15c3-
3a, CCUS will maintain margin required and on deposit with CCUS for 
binary options contracts written or purchased in customer accounts 
separately and independently from margin required and on deposit for 
proprietary positions of Clearing Members. This condition will increase 
customer protection by ensuring that customer margin is clearly 
identifiable and segregated from margin related to the Clearing 
Member's proprietary positions.\37\ In doing so, this condition, 
similar to the second condition, is designed to further effectuate the 
fundamental aspect of Rule 15c3-3 that prohibits a broker-dealer from 
using customer cash or securities to finance its proprietary business 
activities.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \37\ See, e.g., OCC By-Laws, Article 1 (Definitions) (defining 
the term ``customers' account'' in respect of a Clearing Member to 
mean ``an account of the Clearing Member on the records of the 
Corporation which is confined to confirmed trades cleared and 
positions carried by the Clearing Member on behalf of its securities 
customers . . .'').
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    The fifth condition limits the scope of relief provided by this 
order to the same conditions set forth in Note F to Item 13 of Rule 
15c3-3a that apply to margin required and on deposit with OCC for all 
option contracts written or purchased in customer accounts. 
Specifically, Note F to Item 13 states that Item 13 must include the 
amount of margin required and on deposit with the OCC to the extent 
such margin is represented by cash, proprietary qualified securities 
and letters of credit collateralized by customers' securities. This 
condition is thus designed to protect investors by ensuring that

[[Page 67884]]

Clearing Members can only include the Item 13 debit where they deposit 
with CCUS the full amount of margin required for all binary options 
contracts written or purchased in customer accounts. Further, this 
condition will ensure the permitted debit under Item 13 will remain 
limited to the same scope of assets that are permissible to use as 
collateral delivered to the OCC under Note F to Rule 15c3-3a, to remain 
consistent with established customer protections under the rule.
    The sixth condition states that the rules of CCUS must have been 
amended, as necessary to reflect the conditions of this order. This 
condition is designed to require that Clearing Members may only include 
an Item 13 debit in their reserve computations pursuant to this order 
where the rules of CCUS have been amended to conform to the conditions 
of this exemptive order. This will also ensure that the rules of CCUS 
that are amended in accordance with the conditions of this order 
adequately implement the customer protection objectives of Rule 15c3-3 
and Rule 15c3-3a.
    For the reasons discussed above, the Commission finds that granting 
Clearing Members conditional exemptive relief from the limitation in 
Item 13 and Note F to Rule 15c3-3a that confines the Item 13 debit to 
the amount of margin required and on deposit with the OCC for all 
option contracts written or purchased in customer accounts is 
appropriate in the public interest, and consistent with the protection 
of investors.

III. Conclusion

    Accordingly, it is hereby ordered pursuant to section 36(a)(1) of 
the Exchange Act, Clearing Members are exempt from the limitation in 
Item 13 and Note F of Rule 15c3-3a that confines the Item 13 debit to 
the amount of margin required and on deposit with the OCC for all 
option contracts written or purchased in customer accounts, and as 
such, Clearing Members may include a debit in Item 13 of their customer 
and PAB reserve computations for margin required and on deposit with 
CCUS for all binary options contracts written or purchased in customer 
accounts, subject to the following conditions:
    1. Registration: CCUS is registered with the Commission as a 
clearing agency in accordance with section 17A(b) \38\ of the Exchange 
Act and Rule 17ab2-1 \39\ thereunder.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \38\ 15 U.S.C. 78q-1.
    \39\ 17 CFR 240.17ab2-1.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    2. Eligibility Limitation: The relief will apply solely to margin 
required and on deposit with CCUS with respect to customer positions in 
binary options.
    3. Margined Customer Positions: The binary options that CCUS clears 
will be margined by CCUS consistent with the requirements of Rule 17Ad-
22(e) \40\ and Clearing Members will collect such margin from their 
customers, with any proposed changes to CCUS's margin system subject to 
Commission review, and, as appropriate, approval pursuant to section 
19(b) \41\ of the Exchange Act and Rule 19b-4 \42\ thereunder.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \40\ 17 CFR 240.17ad-22(e).
    \41\ 15 U.S.C. 78s.
    \42\ 17 CFR 240.19b-4.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    4. Segregation of Customer Margin: For purposes of Rule 15c3-3a, 
CCUS will maintain margin required and on deposit with CCUS for binary 
options contracts written or purchased in customer accounts separately 
and independently from margin required and on deposit for proprietary 
positions of Clearing Members.
    5. The relief would be subject to the same conditions set forth in 
Note F to Item 13 of Rule 15c3-3a that apply to margin required and on 
deposit with OCC for all option contracts written or purchased in 
customer accounts.
    6. The rules of CCUS must have been amended to reflect the 
conditions of this order.
