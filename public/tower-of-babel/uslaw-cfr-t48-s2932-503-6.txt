48 C.F.R. § 2932.503-6 — 2932.503-6 Suspension or reduction of payments.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2932.503-6
Creator: Chapter 29—Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2932.503-6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2932 (Contract Financing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any action of a contracting officer under FAR 32.503-6 requires approval in advance from the HCA. Upon receipt of approval from the HCA, the contracting officer shall request the contract finance office to suspend or reduce payments.
