2 C.F.R. § 1532.1500 — If I am reinstated, when will my name be removed from the EPLS?
Citation: us:cfr:2:1532.1500
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1532.1500
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1532 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If your eligibility for procurement and nonprocurement participation is restored under the CAA or CWA, whether by decision, appeal, or by administrative agreement, the EPA will remove your name and that of the violating facility from the EPLS, generally within 5 working days of your reinstatement.
