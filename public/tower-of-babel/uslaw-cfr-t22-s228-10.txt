22 C.F.R. § 228.10 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:22:228.10
Creator: Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-228.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 228 (Rules For Procurement Of Commodities And Services Financed By Usaid). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Sections 228.11 through 228.19 set forth the rules governing the eligible source of commodities and nationality of commodity and service suppliers for USAID Federal share financing under prime and subawards. These rules may be waived in accordance with the provisions in subpart D of this part.
