42 U.S.C. § 11047 — Exemption
Citation: us:usc:42:11047
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section11047
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 116 (Emergency Planning And Community Right-To-Know); Subchapter III (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as provided in section 11004 of this title, this chapter does not apply to the transportation, including the storage incident to such transportation, of any substance or chemical subject to the requirements of this chapter, including the transportation and distribution of natural gas.
