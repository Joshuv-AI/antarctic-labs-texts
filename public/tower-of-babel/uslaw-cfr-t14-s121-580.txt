14 C.F.R. § 121.580 — Prohibition on interference with crewmembers.
Citation: us:cfr:14:121.580
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-121.580
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 121 (Operating Requirements: Domestic, Flag, And Supplemental Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person may assault, threaten, intimidate, or interfere with a crewmember in the performance of the crewmember's duties aboard an aircraft being operated under this part.
