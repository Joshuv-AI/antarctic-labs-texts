32 C.F.R. § 111.4 — Policy.
Citation: us:cfr:32:111.4
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 111 (Transitional Compensation For Abused Dependents). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The DoD will make monthly TC payments and provide other benefits described in this part for spouses or dependents of Service members who meet the eligibility requirements of 10 U.S.C. 1059 and this part.
