20 C.F.R. § 682.360 — What rapid response, layoff aversion, or other information will States be required to report to the Employment and Training Administration?
Citation: us:cfr:20:682.360
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-682.360
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 682 (Statewide Activities Under Title I Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Where a WIOA individual record exists for an individual served under programs reporting through the WIOA individual record, States must report information regarding the receipt of services under this subpart for such an individual. This information must be reported in the WIOA individual record.
(b) States must comply with these requirements as explained in guidance issued by the Department of Labor.
