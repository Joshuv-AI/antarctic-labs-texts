12 C.F.R. § 1026.30 — Limitation on rates.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1026.30
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1026.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1026 (Truth In Lending (Regulation Z)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A creditor shall include in any consumer credit contract secured by a dwelling and subject to the Act and this part the maximum interest rate that may be imposed during the term of the obligation when:
(a) In the case of closed-end credit, the annual percentage rate may increase after consummation, or
(b) In the case of open-end credit, the annual percentage rate may increase during the plan.
