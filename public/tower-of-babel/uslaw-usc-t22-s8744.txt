22 U.S.C. § 8744 — Rule of construction
Citation: us:usc:22:8744
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section8744
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 94 (Iran Threat Reduction And Syria Human Rights); Subchapter III (Sanctions With Respect To Iran’S Revolutionary Guard Corps). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subchapter shall be construed to limit the authority of the President to designate foreign persons for the imposition of sanctions pursuant to the International Emergency Economic Powers Act (50 U.S.C. 1701 et seq.).
