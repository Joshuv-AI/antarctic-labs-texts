Area functional and majorant series estimates in the class of bounded functions in the disk Journal: Journal of Mathematical Analysis and Applications
R.S. Khasyanov

Abstract
In this article, the new inequalities for the weighted sums of coefficients in the class of bounded functions in the disk are obtained. We develop the methods of I.R. Kayumov and S. Ponnusamy, using E. Reich’s theorem on the majorization of subordinate functions. The sharp estimates for the area of the image of the disk of radius r r under the action of the function which is expanded into a lacunary series of standard form are obtained. Under significantly lower than in [ 18 ] restrictions on the initial coefficient, the estimates for the Bohr–Bombieri function of the Hadamard convolution operator are proved. Using the example of the differentiation operator, it is shown that in some cases the new method for calculating the lower bound for the Bohr radius of the Hadamard operator with a fixed initial coefficient is more effective than the known one.

2
Majorization of subordinate functions

To estimate the functionals from the Section
1
we will use theorems on the majorization of subordinate functions. Let us first recall the definition of subordinate functions:

Definition 1
.

We say that the analytic in
\mathbb{D}
function
f(z)
is subordinated to the analytic in
\mathbb{D}
function
g(z)
and write
f\prec g
if there exists an analytic in
\mathbb{D}
function
\omega(z)
such that
|\omega(z)|\leq 1,z\in\mathbb{D},

\omega(0)=0
and

f(z)=g(\omega(z)),\quad z\in\mathbb{D}.

Note that if
g
is univalent function then

f\prec g\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad f(0)=g(0)\>\>\text{and}\>\>f(\mathbb{D})\subset g(\mathbb{D}).

In 1925, J. E. Littlewood proved
[
21
]
the theorem on the majorization of norms in Lebesgue spaces of subordinate functions:

Theorem B
.

Let
f\prec g
in
\mathbb{D}.
Then for
p\geq 0,

\int_{0}^{2\pi}|f(re^{i\theta})|^{p}d\theta\leq\int_{0}^{2\pi}|g(re^{i\theta})|^{p}d\theta,\quad 0\leq r\leq 1.

For
p=2
, it follows from this theorem and from Parseval’s equality that
if
f(z)=\sum_{n\geq 0}a_{n}z^{n},\>\>g(z)=\sum_{n\geq 0}b_{n}z^{n}
and
f\prec g
in
\mathbb{D},
then

\sum_{n\geq 0}|a_{n}|^{2}r^{2n}\leq\sum_{n\geq 0}|b_{n}|^{2}r^{2n},\quad 0\leq r\leq 1.

In 1951, G. Goluzin obtained a weighted analogue of this inequality:

Theorem C

(
[
16
]
)
.

Let
f(z)=\sum_{n\geq 0}a_{n}z^{n},\>g(z)=\sum_{n\geq 0}b_{n}z^{n}
and
f\prec g
in
\mathbb{D}.
Then for any non-increasing sequence
\lambda_{n}\geq 0,

\sum_{n\geq 1}\lambda_{n}|a_{n}|^{2}\leq\sum_{n\geq 1}\lambda_{n}|b_{n}|^{2}.

From this theorem Goluzin obtained inequalities for the areas of the images of analytic functions, as well as the inequality for the norms of derivatives in the space
L^{2}
.
The following statement follows from Theorem C:

Proposition 1
.

Let
c_{n}>0,\>n\geq 1,\>

f(z)=\sum_{n\geq 0}a_{n}z^{n},\>g(z)=\sum_{n\geq 0}b_{n}z^{n}
and
f\prec g
in
\mathbb{D}.
Then

\sum_{n\geq 1}c_{n}|a_{n}|^{2}r^{2n}\leq\sum_{n\geq 1}c_{n}|a_{n}|^{2}r^{2n},\quad r^{2}\leq\inf_{n\geq 1}\dfrac{c_{n}}{c_{n+1}}.

Let us fix the sequence
c_{n}>0,\>n\geq 1.
We define the function
G(r)
as follows:

G(r):=\sup_{f\prec g}\dfrac{\sum_{n\geq 1}c_{n}|a_{n}|^{2}r^{2n}}{\sum_{n\geq 1}c_{n}|b_{n}|^{2}r^{2n}},

where
f(z)=\sum_{n\geq 0}a_{n}z^{n},\>g(z)=\sum_{n\geq 0}b_{n}z^{n}.

The following statement generalizes theorem from [
[
25
]
, P. 260–261] (we obtain its proof in a similar way):

Theorem 1
.

Let

and

Then

Proof of Theorem
1
.

The sum
\sum_{n\geq 1}c_{n}\left|a_{n}\right|^{2}r^{2n}
can be expressed in the following form:

\left[c_{s}r^{2s}\sum_{n=1}^{s-1}|a_{n}|^{2}+\sum_{n\geq s}c_{n}\left|a_{n}\right|^{2}r^{2n}\right]-\left[c_{s}r^{2s}\sum_{n=1}^{s-1}\left|a_{n}\right|^{2}-\sum_{n=1}^{s-1}c_{n}\left|a_{n}\right|^{2}r^{2n}\right]\\
\\
=\sum_{n\geq 1}\lambda_{n}^{(s)}\left|a_{n}\right|^{2}-\sum_{n=1}^{s-1}\left(c_{s}r^{2s}-c_{n}r^{2n}\right)\left|a_{n}\right|^{2},

where

\lambda_{n}^{(s)}=\begin{cases}c_{s}r^{2s},\quad 1\leq n\leq s-1,\\
c_{n}r^{2n},\quad n\geq s.\end{cases}

Similarly,

\sum_{n\geq 1}c_{n}\left|b_{n}\right|^{2}r^{2n}=\sum_{n\geq 1}\lambda_{n}^{(s)}\left|b_{n}\right|^{2}-\sum_{n=1}^{s-1}\left(c_{s}r^{2s}-c_{n}r^{2n}\right)\left|b_{n}\right|^{2}.

By the conditions of theorem,
\inf_{n\geq s-1}\dfrac{c_{n}}{c_{n+1}}\leq r^{2}\leq\inf_{n\geq s}\dfrac{c_{n}}{c_{n+1}},
therefore the sequence
(\lambda_{n}^{(s)})_{n\geq 1}
is non-increasing, so we can use Theorem C:

\sum_{n\geq 1}\lambda_{n}^{(s)}\left|a_{n}\right|^{2}\leq\sum_{n\geq 1}\lambda_{n}^{(s)}\left|b_{n}\right|^{2}.

Thus,

\sum_{n\geq 1}c_{n}(\left|a_{n}\right|^{2}-|b_{n}|^{2})r^{2n}

\displaystyle\leq\sum_{n=1}^{s-1}\left(c_{s}r^{2s}-c_{n}r^{2n}\right)\left|b_{n}\right|^{2}-\sum_{n=1}^{s-1}\left(c_{s}r^{2s}-c_{n}r^{2n}\right)\left|a_{n}\right|^{2}\leq\sum_{n=1}^{s-1}\left(c_{s}r^{2s}-c_{n}r^{2n}\right)\left|b_{n}\right|^{2}

\displaystyle=\left(c_{s}r^{2s-2}-1\right)\sum_{n=1}^{s-1}c_{n}\left|b_{n}\right|^{2}r^{2n}-c_{s}r^{2s-2}\sum_{n=1}^{s-1}\left(c_{n}r^{2n}-r^{2}\right)\left|b_{n}\right|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\left(c_{s}r^{2s-2}-1\right)\sum_{n\geq 1}c_{n}|b_{n}|^{2}r^{2n}.

The last inequality follows from the condition (
1
). Thus,
\dfrac{\sum_{n\geq 1}c_{n}|a_{n}|^{2}r^{2n}}{\sum_{n\geq 1}c_{n}|b_{n}|^{2}r^{2n}}\leq c_{s}r^{2s-2}
for all
f
and
g
such that
f\prec g.
Passing to the supremum in this inequality, we obtain
G(r)\leq c_{s}r^{2s-2}.

∎

3
Area functional estimates

Using Theorem
1
for
c_{n}=n,\>n\geq 1,
E. Reich obtained the following corollary from it. Let
f(0)=0,
then

S_{r}f\leq\pi sr^{2s}\|f\|_{\infty}^{2},\quad\dfrac{s-1}{s}\leq r^{2}\leq\dfrac{s}{s+1}.

This fact becomes obvious if we notice that if
\|f\|_{\infty}\leq 1
and
f(0)=0,
then
f(z)\prec z
in the disk
\mathbb{D}.
We generalize this statement for lacunary series of the form
\sum_{n\geq 0}a_{mn+p}z^{mn+p},\>m\geq p\geq 0,
and obtain an interesting corollary for the standard case
m=1,\>p=0.

Theorem 2
.

Let
s\in\mathbb{N}_{0},\>m\geq p\geq 0,\>f(z)=\sum_{n\geq 0}a_{mn+p}z^{mn+p}
and
r\geq 0,
then

S_{r}f\leq\pi(ms+p)r^{2(ms+p)}\|f\|_{\infty}^{2},\quad\dfrac{m(s-1)+p}{ms+p}\leq r^{2m}\leq\dfrac{ms+p}{m(s+1)+p}.

The functions
f(z)=Cz^{ms+p},\>C\in\mathbb{C},
are extremal.

Corollary 1
.

Let
s\in\mathbb{N}
and
f(z)=\sum_{n\geq 0}a_{n}z^{n},
then

S_{r}f\leq\pi sr^{2s}\|f\|_{\infty}^{2},\quad\dfrac{s-1}{s}\leq r^{2}\leq\dfrac{s}{s+1}.

(2)

All extremal functions are of the form
f(z)=Cz^{s},\>C\in\mathbb{C}.

The last inequality seems very simple and classical. It would be interesting to obtain its analogues in other classes of analytic functions.

Corollary 2
.

Let
f(z)=\sum_{n\geq 0}a_{n}z^{n},
then

S_{r}f\leq\pi\|f\|_{\infty}^{2},\quad 0\leq r\leq 1/\sqrt[6]{3}.

The number
1/\sqrt[6]{3}
is the best possible. Extremal functions are of the form
f(z)=Cz^{3},\>C\in\mathbb{C}.

The inequality from Corollary
2
is an analogue of Bohr’s inequality (Theorem A) for the area functional.

Proof of Corollary
2

Let

h(x)=\sqrt{\dfrac{x}{x+1}},\>x\geq 1.

It follows from the inequality (

S_{r}f\leq\pi\|f\|^{2}_{\infty},\quad r\leq\sup_{s\in\mathbb{N}}\{g(s+1):\>g(i)\geq h(i),\>1\leq i\leq s-1\>\>\text{and}\>\>g(s)>h(s)\}.

It is easy to check that
g(x)=h(x)\Longleftrightarrow x=y=2.29317...,\>

g(x)>h(x),\>1<x<y,
and
g(x)<h(x),\>y<x<\infty.
Thus, the supremum is attained at
s=2.

∎

4

h^{\prime}_{x}(x,b)\leq p+\dfrac{m}{m+p}\phi_{s}\Big(\dfrac{(s-1)m+p}{sm+p}\Big)=h^{\prime}_{x}\Big(x,\dfrac{(s-1)m+p}{sm+p}\Big),\quad b\in I_{s},\>s\geq 2.

Hence,

\displaystyle\Big(h(x,b)-h\Big(x,\dfrac{(s-1)m+p}{sm+p}\Big)\Big)^{\prime}_{x}\leq 0,\quad 0\leq x\leq 1,\>b\in I_{s}

\displaystyle\Longrightarrow\quad h(x,b)-h\Big(x,\dfrac{(s-1)m+p}{sm+p}\Big)\leq h(0,b)-h\Big(0,\dfrac{(s-1)m+p}{sm+p}\Big),\quad 0\leq x\leq 1,\>b\in I_{s},

which is exactly (
3
). Thus, the theorem is proved.
∎

4
Weighted majorant series estimates

In this Section we will give new estimates for the weighted majorant series of the form

\sum_{n\geq m}c_{n}|a_{n}|r^{n},\quad c_{n}>0\>(n\geq 1),\>m\in\mathbb{N}_{0}.

The obtained result will be applied to the problem on the Bohr radius of the differentiation operator.
To do this, we will use the concepts of the Bohr radius of a pair of operators and related concepts introduced in the work
[
18
]
. Let us recall these definitions.

Let
D
be an open disk with center at zero or an interval with center at zero. Let us denote by

\mathcal{H}(D)
the set of all functions of the form
f(z)=\sum_{n\geq 0}a_{n}z^{n},
where the series converges in
D
. Let

\mathcal{H}_{m}(D):=\{f\in\mathcal{H}(D):\>f(0)=f^{\prime}(0)=...=f^{(m-1)}(0)=0\}.

Definition 2
.

Let
m\in\mathbb{N}_{0},\>t,s_{1},s_{2}\geq 0
and

T_{1}:\mathcal{H}_{m}(t\mathbb{D})\rightarrow\mathcal{H}(\mathbb{D}),\hskip 14.22636ptT_{2}:\mathcal{H}_{m}(-s_{1},s_{1})\rightarrow\mathcal{H}(-s_{2},s_{2})

are linear operators. Supremum of the set of numbers
R\in[0,\infty)
such that

\|T_{1}f\|_{\infty}\leq 1\Longrightarrow|T_{2}M_{r}f|\leq 1,\quad 0\leq r\leq R,

(5)

will be called the Bohr radius of a pair
T_{1}
and
T_{2}
and denoted by
R_{T_{1}\rightarrow T_{2}}.
If
T_{1}=T_{2}=T,
then we write
R_{T}
.

The condition (
5
) is equivalent to the inequality

|T_{2}M_{r}f|\leq\|T_{1}f\|_{\infty}.

Let us denote by
id_{m}
the identity operator defined in the space
\mathcal{H}_{m}(\mathbb{D}).
From Bohr’s theorem it follows that
R_{id_{0}}=1/3.
In 1962, E. Bombieri proved
[
10
]
that
R_{id_{1}}=1/\sqrt{2}.
The problem of calculating
R_{id_{m}}
remains open. It was discussed in
[
24
]
and
[
9
]

Definition 3
.

The function

m_{T_{1}\rightarrow T_{2}}(r):=\sup_{f:\|T_{1}f\|_{\infty}\neq 0}\dfrac{|T_{2}M_{r}f|}{\|T_{1}f\|_{\infty}}

will be called the Bohr-Bombieri function of the operators
T_{1}
and
T_{2}.
If the operators
T_{1}
and
T_{2}
coincide and are equal to
T
, then we write
m_{T}(r).

It follows from the Bohr theorem that
m_{id_{0}}(r)=1,\>0\leq r\leq 1/3.
In 1962, E. Bombieri and D. Ricci proved
[
10
]
that for
r\in[1/3,1/\sqrt{2}],

m_{id_{0}}(r)=\dfrac{3-\sqrt{8(1-r^{2})}}{r}.

For
r\in[1/\sqrt{2},1),
the problem of calculating the Bohr-Bombieri function for the operator
id_{0}
remains open. Profound results related to this problem were obtained by E. Bombieri and J. Bourgain in 2004 (see
[
9
]
).

Definition 4
.

Let us consider the functions of the form
f(z)=\sum_{n\geq m}a_{n}z^{n}.
We fix the modulus of the initial coefficient
a:=|a_{m}|
.
The supremum of the set of the numbers
r
such that for all functions of the form
f(z)=\sum_{n\geq m}a_{n}z^{n},\>|a_{m}|=a,
the condition (
5
) is satisfied
will be called the Bohr radius of a pair of operators
T_{1}
and
T_{2}
with the initial coefficient
a
and denoted by

R_{T_{1}\rightarrow T_{2}}(a).
We similarly define the Bohr–Bombieri function of a pair of operators with an initial coefficient:

m_{T_{1}\rightarrow T_{2}}(r,a):=\sup\dfrac{|T_{2}M_{r}f|}{\|T_{1}f\|_{\infty}},

where the supremum is taken over all functions
f(z)=\sum_{n\geq m}a_{n}z^{n},
such that

\|T_{1}f\|_{\infty}\neq 0
and
|a_{m}|=a.

For example (see (
[
10
]
),

R_{id_{0}}(a)=\dfrac{1}{1+2a},\quad 1/2<a\leq 1.

The above concepts are convenient for formulating certain theorems and allow us to give them a unified proof. In addition, they can be used to formulate statements when the Bohr radius is unknown (see Theorems 6-9 of the work
[
18
]
). For a number of examples of previously calculated Bohr radii and Bohr–Bombieri functions, see
[
18
]
.

Definition 5
.

Let
f(z)=\sum_{n\geq m}a_{n}z^{n},\>m\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
and let
h(z)=\sum_{n\geq m}c_{n}z^{n}
converges in the disk of the radius
s>0.
The operator

A_{h}f(z)=(h\ast f)(z):=\sum_{n\geq m}c_{n}a_{n}z^{n},

acting from
\mathcal{H}_{m}(t\mathbb{D}),\>t>0,
to
\mathcal{H}_{m}(st\mathbb{D}),

is called the Hadamard convolution operator (see, for example,
[
27
]
). We will call the function
h
a convolution function.

Let
m\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
and
h(z)=\sum_{n\geq m}c_{n}z^{n}.
Let
S_{m,l}
be a shift operator defined in the space
\mathcal{H}_{m}(D),
namely

S_{m,l}f(z):=z^{l}f(z),\quad f\in\mathcal{H}_{m}(D).

We will consider operators of the form

A^{m,l}_{h}f:=S_{m,l}(h\ast f)=\sum_{n\geq m}c_{n}a_{n}z^{n+l},\quad f(z)=\sum_{n\geq m}a_{n}z^{n}.

In particular, examples of such operators are the differentiation operators defined in
\mathcal{H}_{m}(\mathbb{D}),
that is

\partial^{m}f(z):=\dfrac{d^{m}}{dz^{m}}f(z)=m!\cdot S_{m,-m}\Big(\dfrac{z^{m}}{(1-z)^{m+1}}\ast f(z)\Big),\quad\partial:=\partial^{1},

(6)

and the integration operator acting from

\mathcal{H}_{0}(\mathbb{D})
to
\mathcal{H}_{1}(\mathbb{D})
:

\int_{0}^{z}f(\zeta)d\zeta=S_{0,1}\Big(\dfrac{-\log(1-z)}{z}\ast f(z)\Big).

Let us formulate the main result of
[
18
]
(more precisely, its special case):

Theorem D
.

Let
h(z)=\sum_{n\geq m}c_{n}z^{n},\>c_{n}>0.
If
\dfrac{r}{a}\leq\inf_{n\geq m+1}\dfrac{c_{n}}{c_{n+1}}
and
a>r,
then

m_{id_{m}\rightarrow A^{m,l}_{h}}(r,a)=r^{m+l}c_{m}a+(1/a-a)a^{-m}r^{l}(h(ar)-c_{m}(ar)^{m}).

The main goal of this Section is to give estimates for
m_{id\rightarrow A^{m,l}_{h}}(r,a)
where
r
and
a
such that
\dfrac{r}{a}>\inf_{n\geq m+1}\dfrac{c_{n}}{c_{n+1}}
and
a>r.

Theorem 3
.

Let
s\geq 2,\>h(z)=\sum_{n\geq m}c_{n}z^{n},\>\>c_{n}>0\>(n\geq m+1),\>a>r
and

r/a\leq c_{n+m}(r/a)^{n}\leq c_{m+s}(r/a)^{s},\quad 1\leq n<s.

(7)

If

\inf_{n\geq s+m-1}\dfrac{c_{n}}{c_{n+1}}\leq\dfrac{r}{a}\leq\inf_{n\geq s+m}\dfrac{c_{n}}{c_{n+1}},

(8)

then

(1/a-a)a^{-m}r^{l}(h(ar)-c_{m}(ar)^{m})\leq m_{id\rightarrow A^{m,l}_{h}}(r,a)-r^{m+l}c_{m}a\\
\\
\leq\Big(\dfrac{c_{m+s}}{c_{m+1}}\Big(\dfrac{r}{a}\Big)^{s-1}\Big)^{1/2}(1/a-a)a^{-m}r^{l}(h(ar)-c_{m}(ar)^{m}).

For
s=2
and
c_{m+1}=1
, the condition (
7
) is unnecessary.

Let us first prove the following lemma:

Lemma 1
.

Let
s\geq 2,\>m\geq 0,\>h(z)=\sum_{n\geq m}c_{n}z^{n},\>c_{n}>0,\>n\geq m+1,

\inf_{n\geq s+m-1}\dfrac{c_{n}}{c_{n+1}}\leq x^{2}\leq\inf_{n\geq s+m}\dfrac{c_{n}}{c_{n+1}}
and

x^{2}\leq c_{n+m}x^{2n}\leq c_{m+s}x^{2s},\quad 1\leq n<s.

(9)

Let
f(z)=\sum_{n\geq m}a_{n}z^{n},\>\|f\|_{\infty}\leq 1,\>a=|a_{m}|.
Then

\sum_{n\geq m+1}c_{n}|a_{n}|^{2}x^{2n}\leq\dfrac{c_{m+s}}{c_{m+1}}x^{2(s-1)}(1/a-a)^{2}a^{-2m}(h((ax)^{2})-c_{m}(ax)^{2m}).

For
s=2
and
c_{m+1}=1
, the condition (
9
) is unnecessary.

Proof.

By Schwarz lemma
\Big|\dfrac{f(z)}{z^{m}}\Big|\leq 1,z\in\mathbb{D},
therefore

\dfrac{f(z)}{z^{m}}\prec\dfrac{z+a}{1+az},
that is

\sum_{n\geq 0}a_{n+m}z^{n}\prec a+\Big(a-\dfrac{1}{a}\Big)\sum_{n\geq 1}(-az)^{n}.

Applying Theorem
1

to these functions and weights
\Big(\dfrac{c_{n+m}}{c_{m+1}}\Big)_{n\geq 1},
we obtain the statement of the lemma.
∎

Proof of Theorem
3
.

To obtain the lower bound it is enough to consider the function
f(z)=\dfrac{z+a}{1+az}.
Let us give the upper estimate for the sum
A_{h}^{m,l}M_{r}f
using the Cauchy–Bunyakovsky inequality. We choose
\rho=\sqrt{r/a}
(this imposes the restriction
a>r
):

r^{-l}A_{h}^{m,l}M_{r}f=\sum_{n\geq m}c_{n}|a_{n}|r^{n}\leq c_{m}ar^{m}+\Big(\sum_{n\geq m+1}c_{n}|a_{n}|^{2}\rho^{2n}\Big)^{1/2}\Big({\sum_{n\geq m+1}c_{n}(r/\rho)^{2n}}\Big)^{1/2}.

Using Lemma 1 we conclude that if

\inf_{n\geq s+m-1}\dfrac{c_{n}}{c_{n+1}}\leq\rho^{2}=\dfrac{r}{a}\leq\inf_{n\geq s+m}\dfrac{c_{n}}{c_{n+1}}

and

\rho^{2}\leq c_{n+m}\rho^{2n}\leq c_{m+s}\rho^{2s},\quad 1\leq n<s,

then

c_{m}ar^{m}+\Big(\sum_{n\geq m+1}c_{n}|a_{n}|^{2}\rho^{2n}\Big)^{1/2}\Big({\sum_{n\geq m+1}c_{n}(r/\rho)^{2n}}\Big)^{1/2}\\
\\
\leq c_{m}ar^{m}+(c_{s+m}\rho^{2(s-1)})^{1/2}(1/a-a)a^{-m}\big({h((a\rho)^{2})-c_{m}(a\rho)^{2m}}\big)^{1/2}\cdot\\
\\
\cdot\big({(h((r/\rho)^{2})-c_{m}(r/\rho)^{2m}}\big)^{1/2}.

Thus, we proved the upper bound for
A_{h}^{m,l}M_{r}f.

∎

In the work
[
15
]
it was shown that
R_{id\rightarrow\partial}=1-\sqrt{2/3}.
From the proof of this theorem we can obtain the following estimates for the Bohr radius with initial coefficient:

1-\sqrt{\dfrac{1+a}{2+a}}\leq R_{id_{1}\rightarrow\partial}(a)\leq\dfrac{1}{a}\Big(1-\sqrt{\dfrac{1+a}{1+2a}}\Big),\quad 0\leq a<1.

This fact was obtained using the standard estimate
|a_{n}|\leq 1-a^{2},\>n\geq 1,
which follows from the Schwarz–Pick inequality and the symmetrization of the analytic function (see, for example,
[
18
]
): let
\|f\|_{\infty}\leq 1,
then

M_{r}f^{\prime}=\sum_{n\geq 1}n|a_{n}|r^{n-1}\leq a+\Big(\dfrac{1}{(1-r)^{2}}-1\Big)(1-a^{2}).

The last expression is not greater than one if
r\leq 1-\sqrt{\dfrac{1+a}{2+a}},
which proves the required lower estimate. The upper bound can be obtained by considering the functions
f_{a}(z)=\dfrac{z-a}{1-az}
. Note that problems on the Bohr radius of the differentiation operator and related problems were also discussed in the work
[
5
]
.

In the work
[

]
for some
a
the sharp estimate for the Bohr radius of the differentiation operator with a fixed initial coefficient
a
was found, namely the following theorem was proved:

Theorem E
.

Let
m\geq 1.
If

\Big(1-\dfrac{2a^{2}}{m+2}\Big)^{m+1}\leq\dfrac{1+a}{1+2a},\quad 0<a<1,

(10)

then

R_{id_{m}\rightarrow\partial^{m}/m!}(a)=r_{m}(a):=\dfrac{1}{a}\Big(1-\sqrt[m+1]{\dfrac{1+a}{1+2a}}\Big).

In other words, if
f\in\mathcal{H}_{m}
and
\Big|\dfrac{f^{(m)}(0)}{m!}\Big|=a,
then

M_{r}f^{(m)}\leq m!\|f\|_{\infty},\quad r\leq r_{m}(a),

and the number
r_{m}(a)
is the best possible.

In particular, if
a\in(a_{0},1],
where
a_{0}=0.429782...
is the root of the equation
\Big(1-\dfrac{2}{3}a^{2}\Big)^{2}=\dfrac{1+a}{1+2a},\>0<a<1,

then

R_{id_{1}\rightarrow\partial}(a)=\dfrac{1}{a}\Big(1-\sqrt{\dfrac{1+a}{1+2a}}\Big).

Using theorem
3
, we obtain the new lower estimates for the value
R_{id_{1}\rightarrow\partial}(a)
for all
a\in[0,1]

Theorem 4
.

If
a\in[0,0.429782...],

then

\dfrac{as}{1+s}\leq R_{id_{1}\rightarrow\partial}(a)\leq\dfrac{1}{a}\Big(1-\sqrt{\dfrac{1+a}{1+2a}}\Big),

where

is the root of the equation

\Big(\dfrac{[s]+1}{2}\Big(\dfrac{s}{s+1}\Big)^{[s]-1}\Big)^{1/2}\Big(\dfrac{1}{(1-a^{2}\frac{s}{s+1})^{2}}-1\Big)=\dfrac{a}{1+a}.

(11)

Proof.

Let us first prove that the root of the equation (
11
) exists and is unique. Let us denote

g(t):=a+\Big(\dfrac{[t]+1}{2}\Big(\dfrac{t}{t+1}\Big)^{[t]-1}\Big)^{1/2}(1/a-a)\Big(\dfrac{1}{(1-a^{2}\frac{t}{t+1})^{2}}-1\Big).

It is evident that the function
g
is monotonic on every interval
[n,n+1),
since the function
\frac{t}{t+1}
is monotonic for

Let us show that
g
is a continuous function. Let

Then

\lim_{t\rightarrow p-}g(t)=a+\Big(\dfrac{p}{2}\Big(\dfrac{p}{p+1}\Big)^{p-2}\Big)^{1/2}(1/a-a)\Big(\dfrac{1}{(1-a^{2}\frac{p}{p+1})^{2}}-1\Big)\\
=a+\Big(\dfrac{p+1}{2}\Big(\dfrac{p}{p+1}\Big)^{p-1}\Big)^{1/2}(1/a-a)\Big(\dfrac{1}{(1-a^{2}\frac{p}{p+1})^{2}}-1\Big)=\lim_{t\rightarrow p+}g(t).

Since the function

Moreover,

Therefore, there is only number

Now we obtain the lower estimate for

Recall the following:

\partial f=S_{1,-1}(h\ast f),
