29 C.F.R. § 1635.7 — Retaliation.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1635.7
Creator: Equal Employment Opportunity Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1635.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1635 (Genetic Information Nondiscrimination Act Of 2008). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A covered entity may not discriminate against any individual because such individual has opposed any act or practice made unlawful by this title or because such individual made a charge, testified, assisted, or participated in any manner in an investigation, proceeding, or hearing under this title.
