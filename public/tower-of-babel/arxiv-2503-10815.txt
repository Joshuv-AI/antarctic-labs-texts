1.2.
Motivation and related work

Let

be a metric space. In studying geometry of

by a real-valued metric. Based on the different ways of expressing Hausdorff distance

Definition 4.1

(Geometric distance between metric spaces)
.

Let Met be a (sub)category of metric spaces and consider metric spaces
X,Y,Z\in\textrm{Met}
. Let

\textstyle E^{Z}(X,Y):=\{\textrm{simultaneous embeddings}~(I,J)~|~I:X\hookrightarrow Z,~J:Y\hookrightarrow Z\}\subset Z^{X}\times Z^{Y}

and
\mathcal{G}\subset E(X,Y):=\bigcup_{Z\in\textrm{Met}}E^{Z}(X,Y)\subset\bigcup_{Z\in\textrm{Met}}Z^{X}\times Z^{Y}

denoting Hausdorff distance between subsets of
Z

(H,\mathcal{G})
-geometric distance
(within Met) between

and

(4.1)

\displaystyle\textstyle d_{H,\mathcal{G}}(X,Y):=\inf\left\{d_{H}^{Z}(I(X),J(Y)):(I,J)\in\mathcal{G}\cap E^{Z}(X,Y),~Z\in\textrm{Met}\right\},

which is the smallest Hausdorff distance between simultaneous embeddings of

and

.
