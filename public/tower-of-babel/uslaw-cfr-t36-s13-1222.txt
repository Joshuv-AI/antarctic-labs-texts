36 C.F.R. § 13.1222 — Camping.
Citation: us:cfr:36:13.1222
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-13.1222
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 13 (National Park System Units In Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Camping is prohibited in all areas of the BCDA except within the Brooks Camp Campground and other designated areas.
(b) Camping in Brooks Camp Campground for more than a total of 7 nights during the month of July is prohibited.
(c) Exceeding a group size limit of 6 persons per site in the Brooks Camp Campground while in operation as a designated fee area is prohibited.
