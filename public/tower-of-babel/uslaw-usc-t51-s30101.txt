51 U.S.C. § 30101 — Prior authorization of appropriations required
Citation: us:usc:51:30101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section30101
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 301 (Appropriations, Budgets, And Accounting). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding the provisions of any other law, no appropriation may be made to the Administration unless previously authorized by legislation enacted by Congress.
