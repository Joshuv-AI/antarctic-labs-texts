29 C.F.R. § 1471.635 — May the Federal Mediation and Conciliation Service settle a debarment or suspension action?
Citation: us:cfr:29:1471.635
Creator: Federal Mediation And Conciliation Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1471.635
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1471 (Governmentwide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, we may settle a debarment or suspension action at any time if it is in the best interest of the Federal Government.
