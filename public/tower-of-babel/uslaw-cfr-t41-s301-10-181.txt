41 C.F.R. § 301-10.181 — -10.181 Liability for improper foreign ship use.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-10.181
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-10.181
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-10 (10—Transportation Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees are required to travel by U.S. flag ship for the entire trip, unless the agency specifically authorizes use of a foreign ship. Any costs resulting from improper or unauthorized use of a foreign ship are the employee's responsibility.
