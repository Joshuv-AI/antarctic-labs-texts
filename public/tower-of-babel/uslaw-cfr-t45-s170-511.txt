45 C.F.R. § 170.511 — Authorization scope for ONC-ATL status.
Citation: us:cfr:45:170.511
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-170.511
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 170 (Health Information Technology Standards, Implementation Specifications, And Certification Criteria And Certification Programs For Health Information Technology). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applicants may seek authorization from the National Coordinator to perform the testing of Health IT Modules to a portion of a certification criterion, one certification criterion, or many or all certification criteria adopted by the Secretary under subpart C of this part.
