27 C.F.R. § 19.95 — Issuance of operating permits.
Citation: us:cfr:27:19.95
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-19.95
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 19 (Distilled Spirits Plants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

TTB will issue only one operating permit for a distilled spirits plant. The permit will designate the operations that are authorized at the plant. The proprietor must post the permit at the distilled spirits plant and have it available for inspection by appropriate TTB officers.
