10 U.S.C. § 2644 — Control of transportation systems in time of war
Citation: us:usc:10:2644
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2644
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Property); Chapter 157 (Transportation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In time of war, the President, through the Secretary of Defense, may take possession and assume control of all or part of any system of transportation to transport troops, war material, and equipment, or for other purposes related to the emergency. So far as necessary, he may use the system to the exclusion of other traffic.
