22 C.F.R. § 91.1 — Answering inquiries regarding tariff acts and customs regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:22:91.1
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-91.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 91 (Import Controls). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In replying to inquiries received from exporters, travelers, or other interested parties, concerning tariff acts or customs regulations, consular officers shall refrain from giving, or appearing to give, decisions pertaining to matters upon which hey are not competent to pass.
