30 C.F.R. § 582.42 — Payments.
Citation: us:cfr:30:582.42
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-582.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 582 (Operations In The Outer Continental Shelf For Minerals Other Than Oil, Gas, And Sulphur). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Rentals, royalties, and other payments due the Federal Government on leases for OCS minerals shall be paid and reports submitted by the payor for a lease in accordance with § 581.26.
