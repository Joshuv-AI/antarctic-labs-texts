33 U.S.C. § 2504 — Guidance
Citation: us:usc:33:2504
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section2504
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 38 (Dumping Of Medical Waste By Public Vessels). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 3 months after November 18, 1988, the Secretary of Defense and the head of each affected agency, in consultation with the Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency, shall each issue guidance for public vessels under the jurisdiction of their agency regarding implementation of section 2503 of this title.
