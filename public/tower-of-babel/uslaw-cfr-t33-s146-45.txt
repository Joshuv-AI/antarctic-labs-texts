33 C.F.R. § 146.45 — Pollution incidents.
Citation: us:cfr:33:146.45
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-146.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 146 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Oil pollution incidents involving an OCS facility are reported in accordance with §§ 135.305 and 135.307 of this chapter. Additional provisions concerning liability and compensation because of oil pollution are contained in Subchapter M of this chapter.
