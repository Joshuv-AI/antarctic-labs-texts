18 U.S.C. § 3169 — Federal Judicial Center
Citation: us:usc:18:3169
Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part II (Criminal Procedure); Chapter 208 (Speedy Trial). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Federal Judicial Center shall advise and consult with the planning groups and the district courts in connection with their duties under this chapter.
