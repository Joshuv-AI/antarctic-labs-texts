On behalf of the Brazilian Society of Knee Surgery (SBCJ), myself, Riccardo Gobbi, and Dr. Guilherme Abreu are very proud to be Guest Editors of this special edition of the Video Journal of Sports Medicine (VJSM).

The SBCJ was founded in 1983 with the aim of stimulating scientific study and research, improving practice and disseminating progress in the specialty. On the occasion of celebrating 40 years of our society, we promoted this edition containing only videos sent by our members, increasing the visibility of knee surgery in Brazil. This project arose from the society's partnership with VJSM and Orthopaedic Journal of Sports Medicine journals, signed in 2022, as part of a strategy to increase the society's international presence.

Currently, SBCJ has 2122 members and 74 registered knee surgery training services throughout Brazil, a significant growth from a small group of friends interested in knee pathologies who founded it 40 years ago. This last decade was marked by an increase in the participation of Brazilian researchers in conferences around the world, publications in renowned journals, and a consequent important expansion of our international projection.

The SBCJ has been working tirelessly to keep Brazilian surgeons up to date, with access to the rapid advances that the area has seen in recent decades.

Therefore, in this timely moment of celebration, we present 16 videos of techniques and surgeries focused on sports medicine. As highlights of the edition, we mention step-by-step methods for harvesting tendon grafts to expand the surgeons’ arsenal, innovative techniques for multiligament reconstructions with the creation of fewer bone tunnels facilitating these complex surgeries, reconstruction options for the always challenging injuries of the medial collateral ligament, a very didactic standardization of anterior tibial tuberosity osteotomy, and a technical option for physeal-sparing anterior cruciate ligament reconstruction in children.

We hope this edition will be of value to all knee surgeons around the world, and we thank all members who contributed by sending videos. We congratulate the authors who had their videos published, for their effort and dedication in creating very informative and current content.

Thank you and enjoy this special edition!

Riccardo Gomes Gobbi, MD, PhD São Paulo, Brazil Guilherme Moreira de Abreu e Silva, MD, MSc, PhD São Paulo, Brazil
