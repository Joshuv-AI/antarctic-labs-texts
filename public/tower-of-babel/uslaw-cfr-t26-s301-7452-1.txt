26 C.F.R. § 301.7452-1 — -1 Representation of parties.
Citation: us:cfr:26:301.7452-1
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-301.7452-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 301 (Procedure And Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commissioner shall be represented by the Chief Counsel for the Internal Revenue Service in the same manner before the Tax Court as he has heretofore been represented in proceedings before such Court. The taxpayer shall continue to be represented in accordance with the rules of practice prescribed by the Court.
