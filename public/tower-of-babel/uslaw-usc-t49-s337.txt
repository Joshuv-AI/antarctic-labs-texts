49 U.S.C. § 337 — Budget request for the Director of Intelligence and Security
Citation: us:usc:49:337
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section337
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Chapter 3 (General Duties And Powers); Subchapter II (Administrative). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The annual budget the Secretary of Transportation submits shall include a specific request for the Office of the Director of Intelligence and Security. In deciding on the budget request for the Office, the Secretary shall consider recommendations in the annual report submitted under section 44938(a) of this title.
