43 C.F.R. § 2912.2 — Renewal of leases.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2912.2
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2912.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2910 (Leases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A lessee with a privilege of renewal must notify the authorized officer at least 180 days before the end of the lease period that it will exercise the privilege.
