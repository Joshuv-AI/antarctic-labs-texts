Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 98 (Sportsmen’S Access To Federal Land); Subchapter III (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this title 1 or the amendments made by this title 1 provides a preference to hunting, fishing, or recreational shooting over any other use of Federal land or water.
