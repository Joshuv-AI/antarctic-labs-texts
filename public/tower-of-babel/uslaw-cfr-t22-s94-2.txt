22 C.F.R. § 94.2 — Designation of Central Authority.
Citation: us:cfr:22:94.2
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-94.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 94 (International Child Abduction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Office of Children's Issues in the Bureau of Consular Affairs is designated as the U.S. Central Authority to discharge the duties which are imposed by the Convention and the International Child Abduction Remedies Act upon such authorities.
