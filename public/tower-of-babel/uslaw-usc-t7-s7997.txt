7 U.S.C. § 7997 — Tracking of benefits
Citation: us:usc:7:7997
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section7997
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 106 (Commodity Programs); Subchapter VI (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As soon as practicable after May 13, 2002, the Secretary shall establish procedures to track the benefits provided, directly or indirectly, to individuals and entities under titles I and II and the amendments made by those titles.
