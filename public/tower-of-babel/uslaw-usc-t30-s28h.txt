30 U.S.C. § 28h — Co-ownership
Citation: us:usc:30:28h
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section28h
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 2 (Mineral Lands And Regulations In General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The co-ownership provisions of the Mining Law of 1872 (30 U.S.C. 28) 1 shall remain in effect, except that in applying such provisions, the annual claim maintenance fee required under this Act shall, where applicable, replace applicable assessment requirements and expenditures.
