Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1545 (Navy Wives Clubs Of America). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Federal Charter.—
Navy Wives Clubs of America (in this chapter, the “corporation”), incorporated in California, is a federally chartered corporation.
(b) Expiration of Charter.—
If the corporation does not comply with any provision of this chapter, the charter granted by this chapter expires.
