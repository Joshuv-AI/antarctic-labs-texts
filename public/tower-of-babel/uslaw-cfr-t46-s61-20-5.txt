46 C.F.R. § 61.20-5 — -5 Drydock examination.
Citation: us:cfr:46:61.20-5
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-61.20-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 61 (Periodic Tests And Inspections). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) When any vessel is drydocked, examination must be made of the propeller, stern bushing, sea connection, and fastenings if deemed necessary by the marine inspector.
(b) Sea chests, sea valves, sea strainers, and valves for the emergency bilge suction must be opened up for examination every 5 years at the time of drydocking.
