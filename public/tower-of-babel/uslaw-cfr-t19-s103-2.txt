19 C.F.R. § 103.2 — Department of Homeland Security Freedom of Information Act procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:19:103.2
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-103.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 103 (Availability Of Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Inorder to process requests for documents/information and appeals under the Freedom of Information Act (FOIA), as amended (5 U.S.C. 552), CBP applies the Department of Homeland Security FOIA regulations in 6 CFR part 5, subpart A.
