11 U.S.C. § 1164 — Right to be heard
Citation: us:usc:11:1164
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section1164
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 11 (Reorganization); Subchapter IV (Railroad Reorganization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Board, the Department of Transportation, and any State or local commission having regulatory jurisdiction over the debtor may raise and may appear and be heard on any issue in a case under this chapter, but may not appeal from any judgment, order, or decree entered in the case.
