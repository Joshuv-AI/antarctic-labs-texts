33 U.S.C. § 3073 — Medical and dental care
Citation: us:usc:33:3073
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section3073
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 43 (National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration Commissioned Officer Corps); Subchapter V (Rights And Benefits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may provide medical and dental care, including care in private facilities, for personnel of the Administration entitled to that care by law or regulation.
