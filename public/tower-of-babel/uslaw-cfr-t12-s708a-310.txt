12 C.F.R. § 708a.310 — a.310 Limits on compensation of officials.
Citation: us:cfr:12:708a.310
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-708a.310
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 708a (PART 708a—BANK CONVERSIONS AND MERGERS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No director or senior management official of an insured credit union may receive any economic benefit in connection with the merger of a credit union other than reasonable compensation and other benefits paid in the ordinary course of business.
