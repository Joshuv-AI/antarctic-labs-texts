44 C.F.R. § 75.3 — Burden of proof.
Citation: us:cfr:44:75.3
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-75.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 75 (Exemption Of State-Owned Properties Under Self-Insurance Plan). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In any application made by a State to the Administrator for certification of its self-insurance plan, the burden of proof shall rest upon the State making application to establish that its policy of self-insurance is adequate and equals or exceeds the standards provided in this part.
