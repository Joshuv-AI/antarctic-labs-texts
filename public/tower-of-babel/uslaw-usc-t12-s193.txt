12 U.S.C. § 193 — Notice to present claims
Citation: us:usc:12:193
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section193
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 2 (National Banks); Subchapter XIII (Receivership). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Comptroller shall, upon appointing a receiver, cause notice to be given, by advertisement in such newspapers as he may direct, for three consecutive months, calling on all persons who may have claims against such association to present the same, and to make legal proof thereof.
