25 U.S.C. § 2815 — State, tribal, and local law enforcement cooperation
Citation: us:usc:25:2815
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section2815
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 30 (Indian Law Enforcement Reform). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General may provide technical and other assistance to State, tribal, and local governments that enter into cooperative agreements, including agreements relating to mutual aid, hot pursuit of suspects, and cross-deputization for the purposes of—
(1)
improving law enforcement effectiveness;
(2)
reducing crime in Indian country and nearby communities; and
(3)
developing successful cooperative relationships that effectively combat crime in Indian country and nearby communities.
