5 C.F.R. § 1204.15 — Records of other agencies.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1204.15
Creator: Merit Systems Protection Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1204.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1204 (Availability Of Official Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Requests for Board records that were created by another agency may, in appropriate circumstances, be referred to that agency for discussion or processing. In these instances, the Board will notify the requester.
