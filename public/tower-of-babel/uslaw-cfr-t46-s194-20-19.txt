46 C.F.R. § 194.20-19 — -19 Piping and electrical requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:46:194.20-19
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-194.20-19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 194 (Handling, Use, And Control Of Explosives And Other Hazardous Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Piping, electrical equipment, and wiring shall not be installed within or pass through a chemical storeroom except as required for the chemical storeroom itself.
(b) The electrical installation shall be in accordance with the applicable requirements of Subchapter J (Electrical Engineering) of this chapter for Class I, Division 1, Group C hazardous locations.
