Multiplicative Congruences for Andrews’s Even Parts Below Odd Parts Function and Related Infinite Products
F. G. Garvan, Connor Morrow

Abstract
. We prove multiplicative congruences mod 2 12 2^{12} for George Andrews’s partition function, ℰ ​ 𝒪 ¯ ​ ( n ) \overline{\mathcal{EO}}(n) , the number of partitions of n n in which every even part is less than each odd part and only the largest even part occurs an odd number of times. We find analogous congruences for more general infinite products. These congruences are obtained using Fricke involutions and Newman’s approach to half integer weight Hecke operators on eta quotients, and were inspired by Atkin’s multiplicative congruences for the partition function.

For

A subgroup

We say a function

has a Fourier expansion of the form

If in (iii),

, then we say

has a pole at the cusp

. For brevity we say

is on

\Gamma^{\prime}
.
We also say

We will mainly be consider modular functions on

6.
Conclusion and Some Problems

This paper first arose by considering analogs of Atkin’s multiplicative
congruences for the partition function. We observed that both the
crank parity function and the Andrews’s
\overline{\mathcal{EO}}
-function
appear in Ramanujan’s identities for third order mock theta functions.
See Watson
[
22
, p.63]
:

\displaystyle f(q)+4\phi(-q)

\displaystyle=\vartheta_{4}(0,q)\prod_{n=1}^{\infty}(1+q^{n})^{-1},

\displaystyle\nu(q)+q\omega(q^{2})

\displaystyle=\tfrac{1}{2}q^{-\tfrac{1}{4}}\vartheta_{2}(0,q)\prod_{n=1}^{\infty}(1+q^{2n}).

The infinite products on the right sides are the generating functions
for the crank parity and
\overline{\mathcal{EO}}
-functions:

\displaystyle\vartheta_{4}(0,q)\prod_{n=1}^{\infty}(1+q^{n})^{-1}

\displaystyle=\frac{E(q)^{3}}{E(q^{2})^{2}}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(M_{e}(n)-M_{o}(n))q^{n},

\displaystyle\tfrac{1}{2}q^{-\tfrac{1}{4}}\vartheta_{2}(0,q)\prod_{n=1}^{\infty}(1+q^{2n})

\displaystyle=\frac{E(q^{4})^{3}}{E(q^{2})^{2}}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\overline{\mathcal{EO}}({n})q^{n}.

The corresponding eta-quotients are related by the Fricke transformation

\tau\mapsto\frac{-1}{4\tau}
, which is connected with the transformation
in Theorem
2.3
with
p=2
. The functions
f(q)
,
\psi(q)
,

\nu(q)
and
\omega(q)
are third order mock theta functions.
Watson
[
22
, p.75]
found a similar transformation connecting
f(q)

and
\omega(q)
. This was exploited by Zwegers
[
25
]
in his completion
of these third mock theta functions to harmonic Maass forms. In a subsequent
paper we obtain congruences mod powers of
2
for the coefficients
of
\omega(q)
.

We conclude by posing some problems.

(1)

In this paper we have restricted to half-integer weight
eta-quotients
B_{r,s,p}(\tau)
where
\Gamma_{0}(p)
has genus
0
.
We pose the problem for more general eta-quotients.

(2)

The congruences in Theorem
1.5
arise because the function

only has a pole at
0
. It would be interesting
to consider when

has poles at both

(3)

We obtained Theorem
1.4
from the congruence

\mu(s_{\ell})\equiv\ell\,\genfrac{(}{)}{}{}{\ell}{3}\pmod{32},

which in turn came from the following:

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\mu(n)q^{n}

\displaystyle=\frac{E_{2}^{*}(q)}{B(q)}=\frac{E(q)^{5}}{E(q^{2})^{2}}+32\,q\,\frac{E(q^{2})E(q^{4})^{8}}{E(q)^{3}E(q^{2})^{4}}

\displaystyle\equiv\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}n>0\\
n\text{odd}\end{subarray}}\genfrac{(}{)}{}{}{n}{3}\,n\,q^{\frac{n^{2}-1}{24}}\pmod{32}.

Find analogous results for more general
\mu(n)
which will
lead to congruences for the eigenvalues
\lambda_{r,s,p,\ell}

in Theorem
1.5
.
