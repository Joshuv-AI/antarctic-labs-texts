46 C.F.R. § 50.10-35 — -35 Constructed.
Citation: us:cfr:46:50.10-35
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-50.10-35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 50 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term constructed means the keel has been laid or, for vessels with no keel, assembly of at least 50 tons or 1% of the estimated mass of all structural material, whichever is less, has been completed.
