23 C.F.R. § 626.3 — Policy.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 626 (Pavement Policy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pavement shall be designed to accommodate current and predicted traffic needs in a safe, durable, and cost effective manner.
