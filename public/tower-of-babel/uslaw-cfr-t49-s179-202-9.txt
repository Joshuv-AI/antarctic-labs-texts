49 C.F.R. § 179.202-9 — -9 Top fittings protection.
Citation: us:cfr:49:179.202-9
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-179.202-9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 179 (Specifications For Tank Cars). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The tank car tank must be equipped with top fittings protection conforming to AAR Specifications for Tank Cars, appendix E paragraph 10.2.1 (IBR, see § 171.7 of this subchapter).
