24 C.F.R. § 30.20 — Ethical violations by HUD employees.
Citation: us:cfr:24:30.20
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-30.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 30 (Civil Money Penalties: Certain Prohibited Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) General. The General Counsel, or his or her designee, may initiate a civil money penalty action against HUD employees who improperly disclose information pursuant to section 103 of the HUD Reform Act of 1989 (42 U.S.C. 3537a(c)) and 24 CFR part 4, subpart B.
(b) Maximum penalty. The maximum penalty is $25,132 for each violation.
