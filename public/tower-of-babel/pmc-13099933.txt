Effect of opioid sparing anesthesia with esketamine on PONV in laparoscopic sleeve gastrectomy randomized trial
Authors: Kai-rui Shi, Min Zhao, Ke Liu, Da-peng Yu, Bing-yuan Zhang, Hua Hong, Lin Jiang, Chun Yang
Published: 2026

Main text

(iScience 28, 113253, September 19, 2025)

The authors requested a textual correction in Table 2 of the published article; under the section “Anesthetics and analgesics,” the median (interquartile range [IQR]) for sufentanil’s total consumption was corrected to 78 (65–90) for the OSA group and 168 (160–195) for the control group. While the median values were correct, the IQR values in this table were inadvertently transposed during data entry. This change does not alter the data analysis or the article conclusion, and the authors apologize for the mistake.
