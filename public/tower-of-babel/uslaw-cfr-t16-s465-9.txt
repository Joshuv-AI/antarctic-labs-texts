16 C.F.R. § 465.9 — Severability.
Citation: us:cfr:16:465.9
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-465.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 465 (Rule On The Use Of Consumer Reviews And Testimonials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this part are separate and severable from one another. If any provision is stayed or determined to be invalid, the remaining provisions will continue in effect.
