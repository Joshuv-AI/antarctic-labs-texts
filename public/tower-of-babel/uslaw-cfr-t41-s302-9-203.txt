41 C.F.R. § 302-9.203 — -9.203 Retaining a POV at a post of duty after conditions change to make use of the POV no longer in the best interest of the Government, and transporting it at Government expense from the post of duty at a later date.

Agencies will pay the transportation costs not to exceed the cost of transporting the POV to the authorized destination, provided all other conditions are met for transporting a POV.
