7 C.F.R. § 624.11 — Waivers.
Citation: us:cfr:7:624.11
Creator: Natural Resources Conservation Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-624.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 624 (Emergency Watershed Protection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To the extent allowed by law, the NRCS Deputy Chief for Programs may waive any provision of these regulations when the agency makes a written determination that such waiver is in the best interest of the Federal government.
