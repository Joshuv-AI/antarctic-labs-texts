2 U.S.C. § 282c — Expenditures
Citation: us:usc:2:282c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section282c
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 9 (Office Of Legislative Counsel); Subchapter II (House Of Representatives); Part II (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In accordance with policies and procedures approved by the Speaker, the Legislative Counsel may make such expenditures as may be necessary or appropriate for the functioning of the Office.
