30 C.F.R. § 828.2 — Objectives.
Citation: us:cfr:30:828.2
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-828.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 828 (Special Permanent Program Performance Standards—In Situ Processing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part is intended to ensure that all in situ processing activities are conducted in a manner which preserves and enhances environmental values in accordance with the Act. This part provides additional performance, reclamation and design standards to reflect the nature of in situ processing.
