50 C.F.R. § 81.9 — Assurances.
Citation: us:cfr:50:81.9
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-81.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 81 (Conservation Of Endangered And Threatened Species Of Fish, Wildlife, And Plants—Cooperation With The States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The State must assure and certify that it will comply with all applicable Federal laws, regulations, and requirements as they relate to the application, acceptance, and use of Federal funds for projects under the Act in accordance with Office of Management and Budget Circular A-102.
