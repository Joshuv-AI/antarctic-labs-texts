22 C.F.R. § 1504.1 — Cross-references to employee ethical conduct standards and financial disclosure regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:22:1504.1
Creator: African Development Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1504.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1504 (Employee Responsibilities And Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Directors and other employees of the African Development Foundation are subject to the Standards of Ethical Conduct for Employees of the Executive Branch at 5 CFR part 2635, and the executive branch financial disclosure regulations at 5 CFR part 2634.
