15 C.F.R. § 25.11 — Referral of complaint and answer to the ALJ.

Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 25 (Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon receipt of an answer, the reviewing official shall file the complaint and answer with the ALJ.
