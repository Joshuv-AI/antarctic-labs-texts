43 C.F.R. § 3834.14 — May I obtain a waiver from these fees?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3834.14
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3834.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3834 (Required Fees For Mining Claims Or Sites). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) No waivers are available for the initial maintenance fee or the annual $550 oil shale fee.
(b) You may request a waiver from annual maintenance fees under certain circumstances. See part 3835 of this chapter.
