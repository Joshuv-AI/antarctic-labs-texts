44 U.S.C. § 2502 — Vacancies
Citation: us:usc:44:2502
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section2502
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 25 (National Historical Publications And Records Commission). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A person appointed to fill a vacancy in the membership of the Commission shall be appointed only for the unexpired term of the member whom he succeeds, and his appointment shall be made in the same manner as the appointment of his predecessor.
