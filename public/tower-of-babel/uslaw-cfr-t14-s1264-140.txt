14 C.F.R. § 1264.140 — Stay pending appeal.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1264.140
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1264.140
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1264 (Implementation Of The Program Fraud Civil Penalties Act Of 1986). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An initial decision is stayed automatically pending disposition of a motion for reconsideration or of an appeal to the authority head.
(b) No administrative stay is available following a final decision of the authority head.
