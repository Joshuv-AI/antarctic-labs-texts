50 C.F.R. § 19.4 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:50:19.4
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-19.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 19 (Airborne Hunting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to definitions contained in part 10 of this subchapter, and unless the context otherwise requires, in this part 19:
Harass means to disturb, worry, molest, rally, concentrate, harry, chase, drive, herd or torment.
