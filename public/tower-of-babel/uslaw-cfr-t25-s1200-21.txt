25 C.F.R. § 1200.21 — How may a tribe appeal denials under this part?
Citation: us:cfr:25:1200.21
Creator: Office Of The Special Trustee For American Indians, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-1200.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 1200 (American Indian Trust Fund Management Reform Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If we deny a request or do not approve an application within 90 days of a request, the tribe may address any problems that we identify and resubmit a revised request, seek technical assistance, or appeal the denial under 43 CFR part 4.
