14 C.F.R. § 298.35 — Limitations on carriage of mail.
Citation: us:cfr:14:298.35
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-298.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 298 (Exemptions For Air Taxi And Commuter Air Carrier Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An air taxi operator or commuter air carrier is not authorized to carry mail except pursuant to contract with the U.S. Postal Service entered into pursuant to section 5402 of the Postal Reorganization Act (39 U.S.C. 5402).
