45 C.F.R. § 153.265 — Restrictions on use of reinsurance funds for administrative expenses.
Citation: us:cfr:45:153.265
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-153.265
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 153 (Standards Related To Reinsurance, Risk Corridors, And Hhs Risk Adjustment Under The Affordable Care Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A State that establishes a reinsurance program must ensure that its applicable reinsurance entity does not use any funds for the support of reinsurance operations, including any reinsurance contributions provided under the national contribution rate for administrative expenses, for any of the following purposes:
(a) Staff retreats;
(b) Promotional giveaways;
(c) Excessive executive compensation; or
(d) Promotion of Federal or State legislative or regulatory modifications.
