Vanishing properties of Fourier coefficients of holomorphic η \eta -quotients
Kathrin Bringmann, Guoniu Han, Bernhard Heim, Ben Kane

Abstract
. In this paper, we study vanishing of Fourier coefficients of holomorphic η \eta -quotients. We investigate examples of two different types: the first one involves integral weight CM newforms, while the second one involves half-integral weight η \eta -quotients associated with sums of squares and Hurwitz class numbers.

If
