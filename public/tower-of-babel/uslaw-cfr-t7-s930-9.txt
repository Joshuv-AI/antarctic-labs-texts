7 C.F.R. § 930.9 — Grower.
Citation: us:cfr:7:930.9
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-930.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 930 (Tart Cherries Grown In The States Of Michigan, New York, Pennsylvania, Oregon, Utah, Washington, And Wisconsin). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Grower is synonymous with producer and means any person who produces cherries to be marketed in canned, frozen, or other processed form and who has a proprietary interest therein: Provided that, the term grower shall not include a person who produces cherries to be marketed exclusively for the fresh market in an unpitted condition.
