10 C.F.R. § 71.12 — Specific exemptions.
Citation: us:cfr:10:71.12
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-71.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 71 (Packaging And Transportation Of Radioactive Material). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

On application of any interested person or on its own initiative, the Commission may grant any exemption from the requirements of the regulations in this part that it determines is authorized by law and will not endanger life or property nor the common defense and security.
