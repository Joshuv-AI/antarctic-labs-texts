36 C.F.R. § 702.5 — Gambling.
Citation: us:cfr:36:702.5
Creator: Library Of Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-702.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 702 (Conduct On Library Premises). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Participation in any illegal gambling, such as the operation of gambling devices, the conduct of an illegal pool or lottery, or the unauthorized sale or purchase of numbers or lottery tickets, on the premises is prohibited.
