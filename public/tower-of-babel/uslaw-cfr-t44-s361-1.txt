44 C.F.R. § 361.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:44:361.1
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-361.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 361 (National Earthquake Hazards Reduction Assistance To State And Local Governments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes the policies to be followed by the Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA) and States in the administration of FEMA's earthquake hazards reduction assistance program, and establishes the criteria for cost-sharing.
