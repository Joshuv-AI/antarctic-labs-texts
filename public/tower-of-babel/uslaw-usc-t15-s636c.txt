15 U.S.C. § 636c — Age of applicant for disaster loans
Citation: us:usc:15:636c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section636c
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 14A (Aid To Small Business). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the administration of any Federal disaster loan program under the authority of section 636a 1 of this title, section 4452 1 of title 42, or section 233 of Public Law 91–606, the age of any adult loan applicant shall not be considered in determining whether such loan should be made or the amount of such loan.
