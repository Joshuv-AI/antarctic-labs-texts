10 U.S.C. § 12401 — Army and Air National Guard of the United States: status
Citation: us:usc:10:12401
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section12401
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel Generally); Chapter 1211 (National Guard Members In Federal Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Members of the Army National Guard of the United States and the Air National Guard of the United States are not in active Federal service except when ordered thereto under law.
