46 C.F.R. § 54.01-17 — -17 Pressure vessel for human occupancy (PVHO).
Citation: us:cfr:46:54.01-17
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-54.01-17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 54 (Pressure Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pressure vessels for human occupancy (PVHOs) must meet the requirements of subpart B (Commercial Diving Operations) of part 197 of this chapter.
