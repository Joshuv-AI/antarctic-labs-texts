49 C.F.R. § 398.7 — Inspection and maintenance of motor vehicles.
Citation: us:cfr:49:398.7
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-398.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 398 (Transportation Of Migrant Workers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every motor carrier shall systematically inspect and maintain or cause to be systematically maintained, all motor vehicles and their accessories subject to its control, to ensure that such motor vehicles and accessories are in safe and proper operating condition.
