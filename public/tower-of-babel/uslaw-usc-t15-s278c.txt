15 U.S.C. § 278c — Acquisition of land for field sites
Citation: us:usc:15:278c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section278c
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 7 (National Institute Of Standards And Technology). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To the extent that funds are specifically appropriated therefor, the Secretary of Commerce is authorized to acquire land for such field sites as are necessary for the proper and efficient conduct of the activities authorized herein.
