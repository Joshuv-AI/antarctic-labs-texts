33 U.S.C. § 891e–1 — Shipyards located outside of the United States
Citation: us:usc:33:891e–1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section891e–1
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 17 (National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration); Subchapter III (Noaa Fleet Modernization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On and after December 26, 2007, none of the funds made available in this Act or any other Act may be used for the construction, repair (other than emergency repair), overhaul, conversion, or modernization of vessels for the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration in shipyards located outside of the United States.
