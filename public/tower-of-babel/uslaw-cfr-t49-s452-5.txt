49 C.F.R. § 452.5 — Examinations made in conjunction with other inspections.
Citation: us:cfr:49:452.5
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-452.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 452 (Examination Of Containers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Periodic examinations may be made in conjunction with or as part of routine change-of-custody inspections, or in any other manner convenient to the owner so long as the examinations conform to the requirements of § 452.3.
