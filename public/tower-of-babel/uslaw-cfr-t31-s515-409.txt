31 C.F.R. § 515.409 — Certain payments to a designated foreign country and nationals through third countries.
Citation: us:cfr:31:515.409
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-515.409
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 515 (Cuban Assets Control Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 515.201 prohibits any request or authorization made by or on behalf of a bank or other person within the United States to a bank or other person outside of the United States as a result of which request or authorization such latter bank or person makes a payment or transfer of credit either directly or indirectly to a designated national.
