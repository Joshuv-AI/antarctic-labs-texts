46 C.F.R. § 183.540 — Elevators.
Citation: us:cfr:46:183.540
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-183.540
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 183 (Electrical Installation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each elevator on a vessel must meet the requirements of ANSI A 17.1 (incorporated by reference; see 46 CFR 175.600) or other standard specified by the Commandant.
