22 U.S.C. § 4205 — Retention of papers of American vessels until payment of demands and wages
Citation: us:usc:22:4205
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section4205
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 52 (Foreign Service); Subchapter XIV (Powers, Duties And Liabilities Of Consular Officers Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All consular officers are authorized and required to retain in their possession all the papers of vessels of the United States, which shall be deposited with them as directed by law, till payment shall be made of all demands and wages on account of such vessels.
