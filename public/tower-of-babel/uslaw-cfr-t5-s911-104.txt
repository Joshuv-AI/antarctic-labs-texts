5 C.F.R. § 911.104 — Procedures for requesting an indemnification agreement.
Citation: us:cfr:5:911.104
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-911.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 911 (Procedures For States And Localities To Request Indemnification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When requesting an indemnification agreement, the State or locality must—
(a) Certify that on December 4, 1985, the State or locality had in effect a law that prohibited or had the effect of prohibiting the disclosure of criminal history record information to OPM;
(b) Attach a copy of the law to the request for an indemnification agreement;
(c) Notify OPM, at the address below, of its eligibility for an indemnification agreement.
