12 C.F.R. § 336.9 — Finality of determination.
Citation: us:cfr:12:336.9
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-336.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 336 (Fdic Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any determination made by the FDIC pursuant to this part shall be at the FDIC's sole discretion and shall not be subject to further review.
