48 C.F.R. § 847.306-70 — 847.306-70 Records of claims.
Citation: us:cfr:48:847.306-70
Creator: Chapter 8—Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-847.306-70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 847 (Transportation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When contracting for transportation, and consistent with FAR 15.304, contracting officers should consider using offerors' record of claims involving loss or damage as an evaluation factor or subfactor.
